<h1 id="lubin-tate-description-of-local-reciprocity">Lubin–Tate description of local reciprocity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Local Artin map](local-artin-map.md)

With the [arithmetic Frobenius](frobenius-automorphism.md) normalization, a chosen [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$ and [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md) give these actions on the [Lubin–Tate tower](lubin-tate-tower.md) and the [maximal unramified extension](maximal-unramified-extension.md). The two fields are linearly disjoint and their compositum is the [maximal abelian extension](maximal-abelian-extension.md). The inverse on units is essential: using geometric Frobenius would invert the entire [Local Artin map](local-artin-map.md). The finite kernels on a compositum of residue degree $d$ and torsion level $r$ are $\pi^{d\mathbb Z}(1+\pi^r\mathcal O_K)$. They are a basis of finite-index open subgroups, yielding the [profinite completion](profinite-completion.md) of $K^\times$ as the abelian [Galois group](galois-group.md).
