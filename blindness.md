# Blindness

↑ **Parent:** [Sensory loss](sensory-loss.md)

A category of severe visual impairment or absent [vision](vision.md), defined differently by different functional or clinical criteria. Congenital, early and later-onset cases can differ in residual [vision](vision.md) and experience; none should be presumed to possess a universal enhancement of other senses.
