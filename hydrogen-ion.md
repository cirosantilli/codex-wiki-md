# Hydrogen ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_ion)

In [acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry-split.md), [hydrogen ion](hydrogen-ion.md) usually refers to positively charged hydrogen, represented by $\mathrm H^+$. In [water](water.md), this notation describes solvated species, commonly represented by [hydronium](hydronium.md), rather than an isolated bare [proton](proton.md). The [hydrogen ion](hydrogen-ion.md) [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) defines [pH](ph.md).
