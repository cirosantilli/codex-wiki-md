# Holomorphic map from the complex plane to a one-dimensional complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Complex torus](complex-torus.md)

Every [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C/\Lambda$ is constant or surjective. It lifts to an entire function $F:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$. If $F$ is nonconstant, the [Little Picard theorem](little-picard-theorem.md) says it omits at most one point, while every fiber of $\mathbb C\to\mathbb C/\Lambda$ is an infinite lattice coset; hence every point of the torus has a preimage under $f$.
