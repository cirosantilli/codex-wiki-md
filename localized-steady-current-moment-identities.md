# Localized steady-current moment identities

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

A localized steady [electric current density](current-density.md) satisfies $\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$. Integrate the divergences of $x_i\mathbf j$ and $x_ix_k\mathbf j$ over a region enclosing its support; all boundary fluxes vanish. The first identity removes the monopole term from the magnetic vector potential expansion. The antisymmetric first moment is equivalent to the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $\frac12\int\mathbf x\times\mathbf j\,dV$.
