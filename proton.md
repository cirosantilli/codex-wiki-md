# Proton

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleon](nucleon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton)

The proton is the positively charged spin-one-half baryon found in atomic nuclei. Its mass is approximately $1836$ times the [electron](electron.md) mass.
