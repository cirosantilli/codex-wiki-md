# Signal-to-noise ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Signal](signal.md)

A [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) compares a chosen measure of a [signal](signal.md) with the corresponding [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) measure. For a constant measured [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ and a [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) voltage $v_n$, an amplitude ratio is $|V|/v_n$; the corresponding power ratio under equal [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) is its square. State the convention and measurement [signal bandwidth](signal-bandwidth.md), since changing either changes the numerical ratio.
