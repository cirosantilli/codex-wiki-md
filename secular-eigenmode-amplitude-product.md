# Secular eigenmode amplitude product

↑ **Parent:** [Secular eigenmode](secular-eigenmode.md)

For a real two-planet [Laplace-Lagrange secular matrix](laplace-lagrange-secular-matrix.md) with negative off-diagonal entries, the upper [secular eigenmode](secular-eigenmode.md) has anti-aligned apsides and the lower has aligned apsides. Their positive planet-1 to planet-2 amplitude ratios obey $r_pr_m=A_{12}/A_{21}$. Under the conventional [angular momentum deficit](angular-momentum-deficit.md) normalization this is $L_2/L_1$.
