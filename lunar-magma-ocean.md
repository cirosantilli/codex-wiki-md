# Lunar magma ocean

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar mantle](lunar-mantle.md)

An early extensively molten silicate reservoir in the [Moon](moon.md). The flotation model forms an [anorthosite](anorthosite.md)-rich crust by accumulation of buoyant [plagioclase](plagioclase.md), leaving differentiated cumulates and incompatible-element-rich residual liquid beneath. The distribution of late [KREEP](kreep.md)-rich material and the survival of hemispheric chemical gradients are important constraints on proposed explanations of the [lunar crustal dichotomy](lunar-crustal-dichotomy.md).
