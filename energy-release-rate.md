# Energy release rate

↑ **Parent:** [Fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md)

The decrease in potential energy per increment of crack [area](surface-area.md) under specified loading constraints. Its critical value $G_c$ is a crack-growth threshold, not energy stored per specimen [volume](volume.md).
