# Symmetry rule for extremal length

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal length](extremal-length.md)

If a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) or [anticonformal map](anticonformal-map.md) $T$ is an [involution](involution.md) preserving a curve family, its [extremal length](extremal-length.md) can be computed using invariant [conformal metrics](conformal-metric.md). Replace any density by $\rho_s=(\rho+T^*\rho)/2$. Every curve and its image have length at least $L_\rho$, hence $L_{\rho_s}\geq L_\rho$. Convexity of the square and [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) give $A_{\rho_s}\leq A_\rho$, proving the assertion. For reflection in the real axis, invariant means $\rho(z)=\rho(\bar z)$.[Proposition 4.3.8, printed pages 52–53: restriction of the supremum to symmetric metrics](conformal-maps-and-geometry-by-dmitry-beliaev-2015-lecture-notes.md)
