# Gauss continued-fraction map

↑ **Parent:** [Continued fraction](continued-fraction.md)

The map $T(x)=1/x-\lfloor1/x\rfloor$ on $(0,1]$, with $T(0)=0$. Its digit $N(x)=\lfloor1/x\rfloor$ gives the successive partial quotients $N(T^j x)$ of a [continued fraction](continued-fraction.md). This is distinct from the geometric [Gauss map](gauss-map.md) assigning surface normals.
