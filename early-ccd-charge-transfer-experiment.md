# Early CCD charge-transfer experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md)

[George Elwood Smith](george-elwood-smith.md) reports fabricating [MOS capacitors](mos-capacitor.md), thermally generating the first packet, and clocking it to an output that pushed it into the substrate for [electric current](electric-current.md) detection. A later integrated eight-bit device demonstrated imaging. Dark or reversed-clock control traces are not specified; feasibility alone does not calibrate [charge-transfer efficiency](charge-transfer-efficiency.md).[pp. 2309–2310, Figs. 7–9, prototype and imager](nobel-lecture-the-invention-and-early-history-of-the-ccd-by-george-elwood-smith.md)

A discriminating repeat would compare identical cycles with and without initial [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md), then suppress or reverse the transfer sequence while retaining output switching. An [electric current](electric-current.md) transient synchronized with gate switching can include [capacitor](capacitor.md) feedthrough; only the difference tied to initial [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and the expected transfer delay tests packet transport. These are proposed controls, not controls documented in the lecture. For an optical version, a blocked exposure estimates [dark current](dark-current-physics.md) and [read noise](read-noise.md); a spatially isolated illuminated [optical detector pixel](optical-detector-pixel.md) exposes any [deferred charge in CCD readout](deferred-charge-in-ccd-readout.md) trail. [Photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md) persists even with ideal transfer.
