# Gaussian infrared limit of positive four-dimensional quartic coupling

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop quartic scalar beta function](one-loop-quartic-scalar-beta-function.md)

For a positive weak [quartic scalar field theory](quartic-interaction.md) coupling in four dimensions, the one-loop [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) is $\beta=a\lambda^2$, $a=3/(16\pi^2)>0$. Integration gives the displayed inverse-coupling law, so $\lambda$ tends to zero logarithmically as $\mu\to0$ and grows toward the ultraviolet. The interacting [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) of $4-\epsilon$ dimensions merges with the [Gaussian fixed point](gaussian-fixed-point.md) at $\epsilon=0$. This describes the quartic direction in the massless or critically tuned infrared regime, not disappearance of every finite-energy interaction in a massive theory. The ultraviolet [Landau pole](landau-pole.md) signals breakdown of that perturbative extrapolation; one loop alone does not prove a nonperturbative triviality theorem.
