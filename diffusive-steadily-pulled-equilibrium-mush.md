# Diffusive steadily pulled equilibrium mush

↑ **Parent:** [Directional solidification](directional-solidification.md)

For a linear imposed [temperature](temperature.md) $T=T_E+Gz$, local [liquidus](liquidus.md) equilibrium gives $C=-(T_E+Gz)/m$ in the [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md). With equal densities, pure solid, pulling velocity $-V$, and diffusion only in liquid volume fraction $q=1-\phi$, steady solute flux is $-VqC-DqC_z=-VC_0$. The exterior concentration is $C_0+(C_i-C_0)e^{-V(z-h_0)/D}$. [Marginal equilibrium at a mush-liquid boundary](marginal-equilibrium-at-a-mush-liquid-boundary.md) gives $C_i-C_0=GD/(mV)$ and hence the displayed thickness. In the mush, $q[C+(D/V)C_z]=C_0$, so $\phi=(h_0-z)/(mC_0/G+h_0-z)$. A positive thickness requires the thermal interval to exceed the diffusive length $D/V$.
