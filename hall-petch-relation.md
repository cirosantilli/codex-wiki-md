# Hall-Petch relation

↑ **Parent:** [Grain boundary strengthening](grain-boundary-strengthening.md)

The empirical scaling $\sigma_y=\sigma_0+k_y d^{-1/2}$ of yield [stress](stress.md) with average [grain size](grain-size.md) in its applicable regime. It is not a universal law for arbitrarily small grains or high-temperature deformation.
