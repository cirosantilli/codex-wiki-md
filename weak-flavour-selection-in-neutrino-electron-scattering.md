# Weak flavour selection in neutrino-electron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

The [weak charged current](charged-current.md) pairs each neutrino with its corresponding charged lepton. Thus elastic muon-neutrino/electron scattering has only tree-level neutral-current exchange, while electron-neutrino and electron-antineutrino scattering also have crossed and annihilation charged-current diagrams. A contact term pairing a muon neutrino directly with an electron would violate this Standard Model flavour assignment.
