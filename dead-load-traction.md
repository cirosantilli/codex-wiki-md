# Dead-load traction

↑ **Parent:** [Dead loading](dead-loading.md)

A [dead-load traction](dead-load-traction.md) is prescribed [force](force-split.md) per unit reference [surface area](surface-area.md), with its vector held fixed as the body deforms. Its [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) is $T_RN=t_R$. A follower load instead depends on the current deformation, for example through a current [normal](normal-distribution.md) or current area.
