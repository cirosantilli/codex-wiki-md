# Pair detection correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic atom antibunching](fermionic-atom-antibunching.md)

The [pair detection correlation](pair-detection-correlation.md) divides joint detection probability by the product of single detection probabilities. The baseline $g^{(2)}=1$ means independent detection; $g^{(2)}<1$ means suppressed pairs. Its value depends on the prepared [quantum state](quantum-state.md) and detector resolution, so exclusion alone does not require every experimental curve to reach zero.
