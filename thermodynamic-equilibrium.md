# Thermodynamic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic_equilibrium)

A [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) is in thermodynamic equilibrium when its macroscopic state has no spontaneous tendency to change through any exchange allowed by its constraints. For an [isolated system](isolated-system.md), stable [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) maximizes [entropy](entropy.md) over the accessible states at fixed total [energy](energy.md) and conserved material inventories. A wall can forbid an exchange, so always specify what can cross or move. Thermal, mechanical and chemical conditions answer different questions about that same constrained state. [4 Classical Thermodynamics](statistical-physics-by-david-tong/4-classical-thermodynamics.md)

For example, two compartments separated by a rigid, impermeable wall that transmits [heat](heat.md) can reach [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) while retaining different [pressures](pressure.md) and compositions. Allowing an unloaded piston to move adds a [mechanical equilibrium](mechanical-equilibrium.md) condition. Allowing one [chemical species](chemical-species.md) to cross adds [chemical potential equality for particle exchange](chemical-potential-equality-for-particle-exchange.md); allowing a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) adds its [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) condition. A fixed barrier can therefore support a constrained [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) that ceases to be an equilibrium when the barrier is removed.

Uniformity is not the definition of [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md). An [isothermal atmosphere](isothermal-atmosphere.md) in [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) can have a common [temperature](temperature.md) while its [pressure](pressure.md) and [mass density](density.md) vary with height: the [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) balances [gravity](gravity.md). In an external field, the exchange condition must include the relevant potential [energy](energy.md); for charged matter this leads to the [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md). Relativistic gravitational corrections to [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) are described separately by the [Tolman temperature law](tolman-ehrenfest-relation.md).

A [steady state](steady-state.md) only says that the macroscopic description is constant in time. A bar maintained between hot and cold [thermal reservoirs](heat-bath.md) has steady [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) and produces [entropy](entropy.md), so it is not in [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md). Even matching [temperatures](temperature.md) at one perfect-contact interface can coexist with [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) through the surrounding material; matching those two local values does not establish equilibrium of the whole apparatus. See [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) for that spatial transport problem.

The useful simplification is that equilibrium conditions predict an endpoint without computing every collision or flow during relaxation. The [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) supplies the conserved quantities, and the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) supplies the direction of change. For a system held at fixed [temperature](temperature.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), the corresponding criterion is minimization of [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md); at fixed [temperature](temperature.md) and [pressure](pressure.md), it is minimization of [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md), with the material and exchange constraints still imposed.
