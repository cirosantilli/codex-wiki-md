# Linearizable Robin scattering for defocusing NLS

↑ **Parent:** [Defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation](defocusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md)

For the [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md) $q_x(0,t)=cq(0,t)$ with real $c$, the time [Lax pair](lax-pair.md) has a diagonal-conjugation symmetry under $k\mapsto-k$. It implies $A(-k)=A(k)$ and $B(-k)=-h(k)B(k)$. Together with the [half-line NLS global relation](half-line-nls-global-relation.md), this eliminates unknown boundary scattering from an equivalent [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md) determined by the initial data and $c$.
