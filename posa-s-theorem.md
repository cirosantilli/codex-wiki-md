<h1 id="posa-s-theorem">Pósa's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pósa's_theorem)

[Pósa's theorem](posa-s-theorem.md) gives a sufficient degree-sequence condition for a [Hamiltonian cycle](hamilton-cycle.md) in a finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md). [Longest-path rotation](longest-path-rotation.md) is a technique used in proving Hamiltonicity results; it is not itself the degree-sequence theorem.
