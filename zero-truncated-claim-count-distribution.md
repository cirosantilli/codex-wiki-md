# Zero-truncated claim-count distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Truncated distribution](truncated-distribution.md)

For a nonnegative integer claim count $N$ with $p=\mathbb P(N=0)<1$, the conditional law given $N>0$ has masses $\mathbb P(N=n)/(1-p)$ for $n\ge1$. Its [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) is $(G_N(z)-p)/(1-p)$. Truncation at zero is meaningful only when the positive-count event has positive probability.
