# Physics normalization of anyon diagrams

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion tree](anyon-fusion-tree.md)

The convention in which the [anyon fusion trees](anyon-fusion-tree.md) form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) and a splitting vertex followed by its adjoint fusion vertex gives the identity on the total-charge strand. A closed normalized state has norm one. It differs from the [isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams](isotopy-normalization-of-anyon-diagrams.md), in which an unknotted closed charge-$a$ strand evaluates to $d_a$.
