# Zero-relative-velocity mass shedding

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

When matter leaves a moving body with zero instantaneous relative [velocity](velocity.md), it carries the body's current [momentum](momentum.md) per unit mass but creates no additional recoil term. A short-interval [momentum](momentum.md) balance including the newly released material gives $q\dot v=F_{\rm ext}$ for the remaining body's mass $q(t)$. The expression $\frac d{dt}(qv)=F_{\rm ext}$ omits the outgoing momentum flux. Nonzero relative ejection [velocity](velocity.md) instead produces the thrust term of the [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md).
