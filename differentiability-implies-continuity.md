# Differentiability implies continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiable function](differentiable-function.md)

If a real [function](function-split.md) has a finite [derivative](derivative.md) at $x$, its [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) tends to $f'(x)$ and is bounded for sufficiently small nonzero increments. Multiplying it by the increment shows $f(x+h)-f(x)\to0$, proving [continuity](continuous-function.md) at $x$. The converse fails: the [absolute value function](absolute-value.md) is continuous but not differentiable at zero.
