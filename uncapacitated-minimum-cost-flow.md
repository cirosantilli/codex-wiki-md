# Uncapacitated minimum-cost flow

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-cost flow problem](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md)

An uncapacitated [minimum-cost flow](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md) has nonnegative edge flows without finite upper capacities. The [flow balances](flow-balance.md) prescribe net supply at each vertex. For the [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence-matrix.md) convention with tail $+1$ and head $-1$, vertex [network dual potentials](network-dual-potential.md) have [network reduced costs](network-reduced-cost.md) $c_{ij}-\pi_i+\pi_j$. Nonnegative [network reduced costs](network-reduced-cost.md) and [complementary slackness](complementary-slackness.md) certify a feasible optimum. A feasible negative-cost directed cycle makes the problem unbounded below.
