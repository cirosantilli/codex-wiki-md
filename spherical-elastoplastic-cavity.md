# Spherical elastoplastic cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect plasticity](perfect-plasticity.md)

A spherical cavity of fixed reference radius $a$ in an infinite solid with [perfect plasticity](perfect-plasticity.md), the [Tresca yield criterion](tresca-yield-criterion.md), and zero far-field [stress](stress.md) first yields under internal [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P_c$. In the plastic region $a<r<s$, radial [force balance](force-balance.md) and $\tau_{\theta\theta}-\tau_{rr}=2\tau_Y$ give $\tau_{rr}=-P+4\tau_Y\log(r/a)$. Matching to the outer [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) solution, where $\tau_{rr}(s)=-P_c$, gives the displayed plastic-front radius. These are small-[strain](strain.md) reference-geometry formulae.
