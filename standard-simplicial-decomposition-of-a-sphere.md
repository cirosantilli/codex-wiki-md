# Standard simplicial decomposition of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere](regular-antipodal-triangulation-of-a-sphere.md)

The boundary [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) of the [cross-polytope](cross-polytope.md) has vertices $\pm e_i$, and its faces choose at most one vertex from each opposite pair. Radial projection identifies its [geometric realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) with the round [sphere](sphere.md) $S^n$. It is a [regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere](regular-antipodal-triangulation-of-a-sphere.md) and is useful as a target for signed vertex labels: a vertex map is a [simplicial map](simplicial-map.md) precisely when no edge receives opposite labels.
