# Intersection chains

↑ **Parent:** [Allowable singular simplex](allowable-singular-simplex.md)

The group $IS_i^{\bar p}(X)$ consists of finite [singular chains](singular-chain.md) whose nonzero simplices are [allowable singular simplices](allowable-singular-simplex.md) and whose boundary, after cancellation, also consists of allowable simplices. Since $\partial^2=0$, these groups form a [chain complex](chain-complex.md). Its homology is [intersection homology](intersection-homology.md).
