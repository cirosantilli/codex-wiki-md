<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

During a short pulse, detuning is negligible relative to $\Omega$. Rotation through angle $\Omega\Delta t=\pi/2$ about $+x$ takes the initial north-pole [Bloch vector](../../../../../bloch-vector.md) to

$$
\boxed{\mathbf R_1=(0,-1,0).}
$$

The corresponding state is $(|1\rangle-i|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$, since $U_x=\exp(-i\pi\sigma_x/4)$. The [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) of this equatorial superposition is defined relative to the oscillator that will supply the second pulse.
