<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With the coupling off, $\mathbf W=\delta\hat z$. Precession during the dark interval gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf R_2(T)=(\sin\delta T,-\cos\delta T,0).}
$$

The populations remain $p_1=p_2=1/2$ during the dark interval; only their relative [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) changes. For $T=2\pi/\delta$, the vector makes one complete equatorial circuit and returns to $(0,-1,0)$.
