<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $g=\hbar\Omega/2$, with [Rabi frequency](../../../../../rabi-frequency.md) $\Omega$, for the internal coupling and use normalized oscillator states. The [motional carrier transition](../../../../../motional-carrier-transition.md) [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) is the Gaussian [characteristic function](../../../../../characteristic-function-split.md),

$$
M_{00}=g\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{\sqrt\pi l_0}
\exp(-x^2/l_0^2-ikx)
=\boxed{g\exp(-k^2l_0^2/4)=g e^{-\eta^2/2}.}
$$

In the [Lamb-Dicke regime](../../../../../lamb-dicke-regime.md), $M_{00}=g[1-\eta^2/2+O(\eta^4)]$. The diagonal internal-energy terms cannot connect $g$ to $e$.

If the two unnormalized printed functions are used verbatim, the literal ground-state integral is instead $g\sqrt2\exp(-k^2l_0^2/2)$. This is not a [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) between normalized [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) of the stated trap; even its zero-recoil value is incorrectly $\sqrt2g$.
