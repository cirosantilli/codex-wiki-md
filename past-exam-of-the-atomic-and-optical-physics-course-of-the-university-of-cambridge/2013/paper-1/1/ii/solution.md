<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [harmonic-oscillator recoil matrix element](../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-recoil-matrix-element.md), using $\psi_1=\sqrt2(x/l_0)\psi_0$ and differentiating the Gaussian transform with respect to $k$,

$$
M_{01}=\frac{\sqrt2g}{l_0}\,i\frac{d}{dk}e^{-k^2l_0^2/4}
=\boxed{-i\frac{gkl_0}{\sqrt2}e^{-k^2l_0^2/4}
=-ig\eta e^{-\eta^2/2}.}
$$

Consequently $|M_{01}|^2/|M_{00}|^2=\eta^2$. Reversing the travelling-wave phase changes the sign of the imaginary amplitude. For small $\eta$, the sideband is weaker by $\eta$ in amplitude and $\eta^2$ in [probability](../../../../../probability.md).

With the printed unnormalized Gaussians, the literal integral would be $-2igkl_0\exp(-k^2l_0^2/2)$. It is distinguished from the physical normalized-oscillator result rather than silently treating inconsistent wavefunctions as normalized.
