The [Lamb shift](../../../../../lamb-shift.md) is a radiative displacement of atomic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) levels beyond the values from the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) in a [Coulomb potential](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md). In [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md), the $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ levels are degenerate for a point [Coulomb potential](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) in the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md), yet the $2S_{1/2}$ level lies higher by roughly a gigahertz. This is distinct from the [fine structure](../../../../../fine-structure.md) splitting between different $J$ levels and from nuclear-spin [hyperfine structure](../../../../../hyperfine-structure.md).

[Electron self-energy](../../../../../electron-self-energy.md) in the quantized [electromagnetic field](../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) is the dominant contribution to the familiar [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) splitting; [vacuum polarization](../../../../../vacuum-polarization.md) contributes too. [Renormalization](../../../../../renormalization.md) separates the physical [electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md) from its divergent free-electron [self-energy](../../../../../self-energy.md). A useful leading-log scale for a hydrogenic $nS$ level is

$$
\Delta E_{nS}\sim\frac{\alpha(Z\alpha)^4}{\pi n^3}m_ec^2
\ln[(Z\alpha)^{-2}],
$$

not an exact numerical formula. The enhanced sensitivity of S states follows from their nonzero [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) at the nucleus; leading short-distance corrections act like contact perturbations. A precision prediction also includes finite nuclear size and [recoil](../../../../../recoil.md), and must consistently separate those effects from the radiative contribution.[§III A, equations (7)–(8), one-loop self-energy and vacuum-polarization scale; introduction, separation of radiative, recoil and nuclear effects](../../../../../theory-of-the-lamb-shift-in-hydrogen-and-light-hydrogen-like-ions-by-vladimir-yerokhin-and-others.md)

The original [Lamb-Retherford experiment](../../../../../lamb-retherford-experiment.md) used a beam of [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) atoms, electron-impact excitation into metastable $2S$, and microwave-induced transfer into short-lived $2P$ states. Those atoms decayed before reaching the detector. Metastable atoms surviving to a metal target ejected electrons, so a drop in the measured current signalled a resonance. Varying the microwave excitation and [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) established the displacement from the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) expectation. **Microwave quenching converted a small [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) difference into a measurable loss of a long-lived population**, avoiding optical Doppler widths that obscure the splitting.[1947, pp. 241–243, metastable beam, target-current readout and microwave resonance](../../../../../fine-structure-of-the-hydrogen-atom-by-a-microwave-method-by-willis-eugene-lamb-jr-and-robert-curtis-retherford.md)
