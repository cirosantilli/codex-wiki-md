<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Paul trap](../../../../../paul-trap.md) uses a time-dependent electric quadrupole field to provide dynamic confinement, avoiding the impossibility of trapping a charge in a stable three-dimensional static electrostatic minimum. Its dynamics follows a [Mathieu equation](../../../../../mathieu-equation.md): stable drive parameters produce slow [Paul trap secular motion](../../../../../paul-trap-secular-motion.md) with superposed fast [Paul trap micromotion](../../../../../paul-trap-micromotion.md). If $\mathbf E_{\rm rf}$ denotes the peak oscillating field, the effective [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) for small [Paul trap micromotion](../../../../../paul-trap-micromotion.md) is

$$
\boxed{U_{\rm eff}=Q\Phi_{\rm dc}+\frac{Q^2|\mathbf E_{\rm rf}|^2}{4m\Omega_{\rm rf}^2}.}
$$

The [radiofrequency pseudopotential of a Paul trap](../../../../../radiofrequency-pseudopotential-of-a-paul-trap.md) is an averaged mechanical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), not a static electrostatic potential. [Doppler cooling](../../../../../doppler-cooling.md) and, where needed, [resolved sideband cooling](../../../../../resolved-sideband-cooling.md) reduce [Paul trap secular motion](../../../../../paul-trap-secular-motion.md); the [Lamb-Dicke regime](../../../../../lamb-dicke-regime.md) suppresses higher motional sidebands.[Sections III–IV, confinement, internal-state control and quantized ion motion](../../../../../nobel-lecture-superposition-entanglement-and-raising-schrodinger%E2%80%99s-cat-by-david-j-wineland.md)

Two long-lived internal states encode a [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md), for example hyperfine states, while an optical metastable transition provides another option. [Optical pumping](../../../../../optical-pumping.md) initializes it. Resonant or [Raman transition](../../../../../raman-transition.md) pulses make coherent single-qubit rotations, and state-dependent [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) distinguishes a bright state from a dark shelved state. Shared quantized [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) let one ion's internal state influence another, providing entangling gates in the proposal of [J. I. Cirac](../../../../../j-i-cirac.md) and [P. Zoller](../../../../../p-zoller.md); the motional bus must be returned to its intended state rather than left carrying unwanted information.[1995, pp. 4091–4093, shared motion and conditional gates](../../../../../quantum-computations-with-cold-trapped-ions-by-j-i-cirac-and-p-zoller.md)

A concrete early experiment demonstrated a [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) between the internal hyperfine and motional degrees of freedom of a **single** trapped ion. This was an internal–motion two-qubit gate, not yet a gate between two separate ions.[1995, pp. 4714–4717, encoding and gate demonstration](../../../../../demonstration-of-a-fundamental-quantum-logic-gate-by-christopher-monroe-and-others.md)

Challenges include electric-field-noise heating of the motional bus, excess [Paul trap micromotion](../../../../../paul-trap-micromotion.md) from stray fields, magnetic and laser phase noise, off-resonant spontaneous scattering, imperfect preparation and readout, and unwanted excitation of spectator modes. Scaling to many ions adds mode crowding, individual-addressing crosstalk and transport requirements. Cooling and [quantum error correction](../../../../../quantum-error-correction-split.md) must remove [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) without erasing stored qubits. **Long storage coherence is necessary but not sufficient: gates and measurements must also be accurate and reproducible.** The original gate experiment explicitly identified laser, field, trap-drive and motional [decoherence](../../../../../quantum-decoherence.md) limitations.[p. 4716, decoherence sources and limits to extended computations](../../../../../demonstration-of-a-fundamental-quantum-logic-gate-by-christopher-monroe-and-others.md)
