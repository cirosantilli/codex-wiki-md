<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two-level Doppler limit, $k_BT_D=\hbar\Gamma/2$, balances velocity-dependent scattering friction with [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) diffusion. Multilevel atoms have additional, slower internal dynamics. Counterpropagating beams with [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) linear polarizations create a spatially varying [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), giving different position-dependent light shifts for ground-state magnetic sublevels. A moving atom climbs a light-shift hill, loses [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and is optically pumped into a lower-energy sublevel: this repeated [Sisyphus cooling](../../../../../sisyphus-cooling.md) cycle dissipates motion through emitted light.

[Optical pumping](../../../../../optical-pumping.md) responds with a finite delay. Its rate, rather than only the excited-state linewidth, sets the low-velocity response scale. In the opposite-circular-polarization arrangement, motion instead produces unequal sublevel populations and unbalanced [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md). Both mechanisms can cool below the two-level Doppler [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); diffusion heating still balances the cooling, and recoil matters at the lowest temperatures.[1989, pages 2023–2025, two polarization-gradient mechanisms and pumping timescale](../../../../../laser-cooling-below-the-doppler-limit-by-polarization-gradients-simple-theoretical-models-by-jean-dalibard-and-claude-cohen-tannoudji.md)

**Ground-state substructure, [optical pumping](../../../../../optical-pumping.md) and [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) gradients enable the extra cooling.** Magnetic fields that disturb the required sublevel dynamics can suppress it. [Optical molasses](../../../../../optical-molasses.md) damps [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) but does not by itself provide position-restoring confinement.
