**Sound propagation.** In the 1997 experiment, a focused laser beam abruptly modified the trapping potential of a dilute condensate, producing a local excitation. A rapid sequence of nondestructive [phase-contrast imaging](../../../../../phase-contrast-imaging.md) measurements followed the resulting [density](../../../../../density.md) motion. Measuring propagation [speed](../../../../../speed.md) at different densities tested the sound-like collective excitation spectrum, rather than individual free-particle flight.[1997, primary abstract and author publication record; Physical Review Letters 79, 549–552, with 1998 erratum](../../../../../propagation-of-sound-in-a-bose-einstein-condensate-by-m-r-andrews-and-others.md) For a weakly interacting uniform condensate, the [Bogoliubov spectrum](../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) is

$$
\epsilon(p)=\sqrt{\frac{p^2}{2m}\left(\frac{p^2}{2m}+2gn\right)},
\qquad c_s=\lim_{p\to0}\frac{\epsilon(p)}p=\sqrt{\frac{gn}{m}}.
$$

[Density](../../../../../density.md) dependence and trapped-geometry averaging matter when comparing a measured pulse [speed](../../../../../speed.md) with this local uniform formula. A strictly [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) would have no nonzero interaction-supported sound [speed](../../../../../speed.md).

**Moving-obstacle dissipation.** In the 1999 experiment, a blue-detuned focused beam repelled atoms and acted as an obstacle swept through a sodium condensate. Heating was inferred from the [condensate fraction](../../../../../condensate-fraction.md) after stirring and re-equilibration. The heating rose markedly near $1.6\,\mathrm{mm\,s^{-1}}$, below the quoted peak sound [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $6.2\,\mathrm{mm\,s^{-1}}$.[pages 2502–2504, obstacle method, heating onset and comparison with sound](../../../../../evidence-for-a-critical-velocity-in-a-bose-einstein-condensed-gas-by-c-raman-and-others.md) Landau's criterion $v_L=\inf_p\epsilon(p)/p$ motivates a dissipation threshold, but [density](../../../../../density.md) variation, flow acceleration around the obstacle and [vortex](../../../../../phase-vortex.md) nucleation make the observed threshold different from simply substituting the peak sound [speed](../../../../../speed.md). **Together these experiments probe a collective excitation spectrum and the onset of dissipative flow; condensation alone is not a complete demonstration of [superfluidity](../../../../../superfluidity.md).**
