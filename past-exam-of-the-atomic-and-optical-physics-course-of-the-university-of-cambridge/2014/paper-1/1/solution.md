For hydrogen, the leading characteristic scales are

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm gross}\sim\alpha^2m_ec^2,\qquad
E_{\rm fine}\sim\alpha^4m_ec^2,\qquad
E_{\rm hf}\sim\alpha^4\frac{m_e^2}{m_p}c^2.}
$$

Order-one [spin](../../../../spin.md) and nuclear magnetic factors, and state-dependent factors, are suppressed here. [Gross atomic structure](../../../../gross-atomic-structure.md) is Coulomb binding. [Fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md) includes relativistic kinetic and spin-orbit effects, with [Thomas precession](../../../../thomas-precession.md) affecting the spin-orbit coefficient. [Hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) couples electronic motion and [spin](../../../../spin.md) to the [nuclear magnetic moment](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-moment.md), whose inverse nuclear [mass](../../../../mass.md) makes it much weaker. The contact [spin](../../../../spin.md) interaction can remain in an s state where orbital spin-orbit coupling vanishes.

For [positronium](../../../../positronium.md), the [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) is $m_e/2$. Its gross levels and separation scale are

$$
\boxed{E_n=-\frac{\alpha^2m_ec^2}{4n^2}\simeq-\frac{6.80\,\mathrm{eV}}{n^2},
\qquad a_{\rm Ps}=2a_0.}
$$

Thus the mean ground-state [electron](../../../../electron.md)–[Positron](../../../../positron.md) separation is twice the infinite-proton-mass hydrogen value: both means have the same factor $3/2$ multiplying their respective Bohr radii. Retaining [proton](../../../../proton.md) recoil makes the ratio $2m_p/(m_p+m_e)$, very slightly below two. Separation is not the distance of either [positronium](../../../../positronium.md) constituent from its [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md).

Interpret the [spin](../../../../spin.md) operators in units of $\hbar$, as required by the printed Zeeman term, so $A$ has units of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Two [spin](../../../../spin.md) halves give **four eigenstates**. At zero field, total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $F=0,1$ and projection $m_F$ label the singlet and triplet. At high field relative to the hyperfine scale, the individual projections $m_{s,e},m_{s,p}=\pm1/2$ become approximately good [quantum numbers](../../../../quantum-number.md). Total projection remains exact at every field, but $F^2$ does not for the two mixed states.

Choose the quantization axis along $\mathbf B$. The aligned-spin states $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$ and $|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$ both have $E=A/4$ because their Zeeman shifts cancel. In the other product-state sector,

$$
H_0=\begin{pmatrix}-A/4+2\mu_BB&A/2\\A/2&-A/4-2\mu_BB\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\boxed{E_\pm=-\frac A4\pm\frac12\sqrt{A^2+16\mu_B^2B^2}.}
$$

For physical [positronium](../../../../positronium.md) $A>0$. At $B=0$ there is a triplet at $A/4$ and a singlet at $-3A/4$. At large positive field the mixed states approach $|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle$ and $|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle$, with energies $-A/4+2\mu_BB$ and $-A/4-2\mu_BB$ respectively, while the aligned pair remains degenerate at $A/4$.

![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-atomic-and-optical-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2014/paper-1-aop-positronium-levels.png)

**[Figure 1](#1/image-four-positronium-spin-states-an-exactly-degenerate-aligned-pair-and-two-mixed-branches). Four positronium spin states: an exactly degenerate aligned pair and two mixed branches**.

The dimensionless mixing parameter is $x=4\mu_BB/A$. A crossover estimate is $x\sim1$, rather than a sharp transition. The scale $A\sim\alpha^4m_ec^2$ already gives a field of a few tesla. Using the physical hyperfine interval $A/h\simeq203.39\,\mathrm{GHz}$ gives

$$
\boxed{B_\times\sim\frac{A}{4\mu_B}\simeq3.63\,\mathrm T.}
$$

The numerical hyperfine interval is external physical input, not a number supplied by the symbolic [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md).[arXiv:1310.6923, abstract, measured interval approximately 203.39 GHz](../../../../new-precision-measurement-of-hyperfine-splitting-of-positronium-by-a-ishida-and-others.md) The high-field limit here still presumes that orbital distortion is negligible compared with Coulomb binding.
