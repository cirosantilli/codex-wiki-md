↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**[Laser cooling](../../../../../laser-cooling.md) removes atomic [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) through a velocity-dependent optical [force](../../../../../force-split.md), with scattered [photons](../../../../../photon.md) carrying away [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md).** For two counterpropagating beams detuned below resonance, motion towards one beam shifts that light closer to resonance, so the atom preferentially absorbs [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) opposite its [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). At small [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) the net [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is $F\simeq-\gamma_v v$ with $\gamma_v>0$. Three orthogonal beam pairs form [optical molasses](../../../../../optical-molasses.md). [Spontaneous emission](../../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) has zero mean recoil in a symmetric distribution, but its [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) variance is nonzero: random absorption and emission cause heating. The cooling and heating balance sets a finite [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).

For a weakly saturated two-level transition, write $\delta=\omega_L-\omega_0<0$ and let $\Gamma$ be the excited-state population decay rate. A useful one-axis [Doppler cooling](../../../../../doppler-cooling.md) expression is

$$
k_BT(\delta)=\frac{\hbar\Gamma}{4}\frac{1+(2\delta/\Gamma)^2}{-2\delta/\Gamma},\qquad
\boxed{\delta_{\rm opt}=-\Gamma/2,\quad T_D=\frac{\hbar\Gamma}{2k_B}.}
$$

This limit belongs to the weak-intensity [Doppler cooling](../../../../../doppler-cooling.md) model, with [recoil energy](../../../../../recoil-energy.md) small compared with $\hbar\Gamma$. Experimentally, released sodium molasses clouds were probed by time of flight; the unusually small expansion demonstrated [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) below that limit. This distinguishes an observed failure of a two-level cooling model from a failure of [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).[pp. 729–732, Doppler limit, optical molasses, and release/time-of-flight thermometry](../../../../../nobel-lecture-laser-cooling-and-trapping-of-neutral-atoms-by-william-d-phillips.md)

Multilevel atoms permit [polarization-gradient cooling](../../../../../polarization-gradient-cooling.md): spatially varying light [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) produces different [light shifts](../../../../../ac-stark-shift.md) of ground-state sublevels. In [Sisyphus cooling](../../../../../sisyphus-cooling.md), atoms climb a light-shift potential and are preferentially optically pumped near its top into a lower-energy sublevel, repeatedly losing [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). The [optical pumping](../../../../../optical-pumping.md) and emitted light supply dissipation. This can reach below the [Doppler cooling limit](../../../../../doppler-cooling-limit.md); the recoil scale

$$
E_R=\frac{(\hbar k)^2}{2m},\qquad T_R=E_R/k_B
$$

then becomes important. The definition of a recoil [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) sometimes differs by a factor of two, so the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) definition is the unambiguous comparison. Recoil is not a universal lower bound for every cooling protocol: suitable dark-state or [resolved sideband cooling](../../../../../resolved-sideband-cooling.md) schemes can suppress scattering near the desired motional state. [Resolved sideband cooling](../../../../../resolved-sideband-cooling.md) preferentially excites a transition lowering the vibrational [quantum number](../../../../../quantum-number.md), followed by decay that on average preserves it in the [Lamb-Dicke regime](../../../../../lamb-dicke-regime.md).[Section IV, cooling of quantized ion motion](../../../../../nobel-lecture-superposition-entanglement-and-raising-schrodinger%E2%80%99s-cat-by-david-j-wineland.md)

[Optical molasses](../../../../../optical-molasses.md) supplies damping rather than a position-restoring [force](../../../../../force-split.md). A [magneto-optical trap](../../../../../magneto-optical-trap.md) adds a magnetic-field [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) and appropriately chosen circular [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md); the position-dependent [Zeeman splitting](../../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) gives a restoring [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) imbalance as well as damping. In real alkali atoms, repumping light returns population that would otherwise accumulate in an uncoupled hyperfine level. Magnetic and [optical dipole traps](../../../../../optical-dipole-trap.md) can subsequently hold the cold sample without continuous resonant scattering.

**Applications include long-interrogation [atomic clocks](../../../../../atomic-clock.md), precision [spectroscopy](../../../../../spectroscopy.md), [atom interferometry](../../../../../atom-interferometry.md), and preparation for [quantum simulation](../../../../../quantum-simulation.md) and [Bose-Einstein condensation](../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md).** Cooling reduces motion-induced [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) shifts and allows longer coherent interrogation; deeper [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) commonly needs subsequent [evaporative cooling of atoms](../../../../../evaporative-cooling-atomic-physics.md). Finite capture [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), available transitions, recoil heating, stray [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), and loss or heating from unwanted scattering constrain a real experiment.
