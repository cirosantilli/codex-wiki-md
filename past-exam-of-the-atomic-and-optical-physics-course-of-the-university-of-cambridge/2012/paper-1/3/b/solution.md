<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**A [quantum computing](../../../../../quantum-computing.md) apparatus needs a scalable [quantum register](../../../../../quantum-register.md) of controllable [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md), reproducible initialization, a [universal quantum gate set](../../../../../universal-quantum-gate-set.md), sufficiently long [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md), and selective readout.** A [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md) is a two-dimensional state space; several [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md) occupy a [tensor product](../../../../../tensor-product.md) space and can develop [entanglement](../../../../../entangled-state.md). [Quantum state preparation](../../../../../quantum-state-preparation.md) must establish a known low-entropy input and supply fresh [ancillas](../../../../../quantum-ancilla.md) during [quantum error correction](../../../../../quantum-error-correction-split.md). A [universal quantum gate set](../../../../../universal-quantum-gate-set.md) may use arbitrary single-qubit [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) with an entangling two-qubit gate such as [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md). [Quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) and control fidelity must suffice for the intended circuit, and measurement must recover chosen output [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) without uncontrolled disturbance of other [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md). These are the five [DiVincenzo criteria](../../../../../divincenzo-criteria.md).[Section III, requirements 1–5](../../../../../the-physical-implementation-of-quantum-computation-by-david-p-divincenzo.md)

An atomic implementation can encode [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md) in two long-lived internal levels, initialize them by [optical pumping](../../../../../optical-pumping.md), drive coherent transitions with [laser](../../../../../laser-split.md) or microwave pulses, couple selected [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md) through a shared interaction, and distinguish states by state-dependent fluorescence. For trapped ions, a shared vibrational mode can mediate entangling gates, so motional control and cooling are part of the computational apparatus. The motion is a resource only while uncontrolled heating does not erase the required [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md).

The computational resource is coherent control of amplitudes and [entanglement](../../../../../entangled-state.md), with [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) making useful outcomes likely. An $n$-qubit [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) has $2^n$ basis amplitudes, but a single measurement does **not** reveal those amplitudes or all possible answers. Algorithms exploit structure rather than simply reading out parallel classical computations. The [Shor algorithm](../../../../../shor-s-algorithm.md) gives factoring with polynomial resource scaling in the input bit length, compared with the best known classical algorithms' superpolynomial scaling; an exponential classical lower bound has not been proved.[Abstract, polynomial running time in the number of input digits](../../../../../polynomial-time-algorithms-for-prime-factorization-and-discrete-logarithms-on-a-quantum-computer-by-peter-w-shor.md) [Grover's algorithm](../../../../../grover-s-algorithm.md) uses order $\sqrt M$ oracle queries for unstructured search among $M$ possibilities, a quadratic query improvement.[Abstract and algorithmic query scaling](../../../../../a-fast-quantum-mechanical-algorithm-for-database-search-by-lov-grover.md) [Quantum simulation](../../../../../quantum-simulation.md) is a further motivation because interacting quantum systems can require exponentially many classical amplitudes for a direct generic representation.

The main challenges are [quantum decoherence](../../../../../quantum-decoherence.md), fluctuating control fields, imperfect gates, leakage out of the [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md) subspace, readout errors, crosstalk, and the difficulty of scaling connectivity and calibration. Faster control can introduce extra errors, while stronger isolation can make preparation and readout harder. [Quantum error correction](../../../../../quantum-error-correction-split.md) encodes logical information redundantly in several physical [qubits](../../../../../qubit.md) and extracts error syndromes without measuring the unknown logical state itself. [Fault-tolerant quantum computation](../../../../../fault-tolerant-quantum-computation.md) prevents the correction apparatus and gates from spreading a few faults into uncorrectable logical errors. It requires error rates below an architecture- and noise-dependent threshold and substantial resources; there is no universal numerical threshold for all implementations. **Advantages are task-dependent, while reliable scaling requires both coherent hardware and error control.**
