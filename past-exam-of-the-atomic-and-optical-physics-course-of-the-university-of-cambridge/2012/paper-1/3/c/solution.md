<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**[Evaporative cooling of atoms](../../../../../evaporative-cooling-atomic-physics.md) selectively removes atoms with unusually high total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), then [elastic collisions](../../../../../elastic-collision.md) rethermalize those remaining.** Loss of particles alone is insufficient: the lost atoms must carry more than the mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per particle if the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is to fall. The process is open and dissipative, so it can increase the [phase-space density of an atomic gas](../../../../../phase-space-density-of-an-atomic-gas.md) in the remaining sample while reducing its atom number. As the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) falls, the cutoff must be lowered to keep replenishing a useful escaping tail; a fixed high cutoff eventually gives negligible evaporation.

An experimental sequence first loads laser-cooled atoms into a conservative trap and suppresses resonant-light heating. In a trap using [magnetic trapping of neutral atoms](../../../../../magnetic-trapping-of-neutral-atoms.md), a [radiofrequency evaporation knife](../../../../../radiofrequency-evaporation-knife.md) drives a [Zeeman splitting](../../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) transition from a trapped weak-field-seeking state to an untrapped state on a surface where the splitting matches the radiofrequency [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Only atoms energetic enough to reach that surface escape. Lowering the radiofrequency cutoff progressively removes less energetic atoms. Alternatively, lowering an [optical dipole trap](../../../../../optical-dipole-trap.md)'s depth permits energetic atoms to spill out. This changes confinement as well as the escape cutoff, so it can reduce the collision rate. Radiofrequency-selective evaporation and the need for favourable elastic-collision and background-loss rates are documented in the original condensate experiments.[pp. 880–882, elastic/background collisions, magnetic confinement, and radiofrequency evaporation](../../../../../nobel-lecture-bose-einstein-condensation-in-a-dilute-gas-the-first-70-years-and-some-recent-experiments-by-eric-a-cornell-and-carl-wieman.md)

The [elastic collision](../../../../../elastic-collision.md) rate $\Gamma_{\rm el}\sim n\sigma_{\rm el}\bar v$ must be large enough for [elastic rethermalization of a trapped atomic gas](../../../../../elastic-rethermalization-of-a-trapped-atomic-gas.md) throughout evaporation, and much faster than unwanted loss and heating. A clean vacuum reduces background collisions; suitable trapping states suppress inelastic spin-changing loss, and [density](../../../../../density.md) must not make [three-body recombination](../../../../../three-body-recombination.md) dominant. The cutoff ramp should allow equilibration without taking so long that background losses dominate. A large [evaporation truncation parameter](../../../../../evaporation-truncation-parameter.md) $\eta=\epsilon_{\rm cut}/(k_BT)$ selects energetically valuable atoms, but an excessively large value suppresses their escape rate. A very small value discards too many useful low-energy atoms. If [elastic collisions](../../../../../elastic-collision.md) become more frequent as evaporation proceeds, the resulting [runaway evaporative cooling](../../../../../runaway-evaporative-cooling.md) can improve efficiency; it is not automatic in a weakening optical trap.

The [energy selectivity for evaporation to increase phase-space density](../../../../../energy-selectivity-for-evaporation-to-increase-phase-space-density.md) can be made precise in a classical three-dimensional harmonic trap with _fixed [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md)_. [Equipartition](../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) gives $E=3Nk_BT$. If each escaping atom removes mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\epsilon_{\rm ev}$, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) balance is $dE=\epsilon_{\rm ev}\,dN$ with $dN<0$, so

$$
\alpha_{\rm ev}\equiv\frac{d\ln T}{d\ln N}=\frac{\epsilon_{\rm ev}/(k_BT)-3}{3}.
$$

The peak [harmonic-trap phase-space density](../../../../../harmonic-trap-phase-space-density.md) is $\mathcal D=N(\hbar\bar\omega/(k_BT))^3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\ln\mathcal D}{d\ln N}=4-\frac{\epsilon_{\rm ev}}{k_BT}.}
$$

Cooling requires $\epsilon_{\rm ev}>3k_BT$; increasing $\mathcal D$ despite atom loss requires the stronger condition $\epsilon_{\rm ev}>4k_BT$. These inequalities assume rapid thermalization and no work from changing trap [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md). They distinguish lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) from genuinely improved [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md). Eventually [quantum statistics](../../../../../quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md) and inelastic processes require a refinement of this classical estimate. **Efficient evaporation combines energy-selective loss, rapid elastic redistribution, and low unwanted loss; it can bring a precooled gas to [Bose-Einstein condensation](../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md).**
