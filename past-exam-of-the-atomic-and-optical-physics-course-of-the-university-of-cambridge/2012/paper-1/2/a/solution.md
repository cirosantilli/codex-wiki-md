<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Following the [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) through the first two gates gives $(|1\rangle+e^{i\alpha}|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$. After the last [Hadamard gate](../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) it is

$$
|\Psi_f\rangle=e^{i\alpha/2}\left[\cos(\alpha/2)|1\rangle-i\sin(\alpha/2)|2\rangle\right].
$$

Using the [qubit polarization](../../../../../qubit-polarization.md) convention defined above,

$$
\boxed{\vec P_f=(0,-\sin\alpha,\cos\alpha),\qquad p_2=\sin^2(\alpha/2).}
$$

The output remains pure and has unit [Bloch vector](../../../../../bloch-vector.md) length. The population oscillation is the [Ramsey interferometry](../../../../../ramsey-interferometry.md) fringe: the first gate supplies [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md), and the last turns the acquired [relative quantum phase](../../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md) into a measurable population difference.
