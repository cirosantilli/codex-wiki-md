<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For an unpolarized ensemble $\rho_i=I/2$. Every [unitary gate](../../../../../quantum-logic-gate.md) satisfies $U(I/2)U^\dagger=I/2$, so

$$
\boxed{\rho_f=\tfrac12I,\qquad \vec P_f=(0,0,0),\qquad p_1=p_2=\tfrac12.}
$$

The [purity](../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) remains $1/2$. Equal populations here come with zero [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md), unlike the state in (b). There is **no [Ramsey interferometry](../../../../../ramsey-interferometry.md) fringe**: the two orthogonal components of any equal-weight ensemble decomposition give cancelling population responses. [Quantum state preparation](../../../../../quantum-state-preparation.md) that reduces the ensemble [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) is needed for the usual polarized clock interrogation.
