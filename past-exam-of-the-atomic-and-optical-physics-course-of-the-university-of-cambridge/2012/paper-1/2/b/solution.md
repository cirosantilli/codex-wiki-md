<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first [Hadamard gate](../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) maps the prepared [superposition](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) $|+\rangle=(|1\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$ to $|1\rangle$. The [phase gate](../../../../../phase-gate.md) leaves that state unchanged, and the last [Hadamard gate](../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) returns it to $|+\rangle$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|\Psi_f\rangle=|+\rangle,\qquad \vec P_f=(1,0,0),\qquad p_2=\tfrac12.}
$$

This is a fully polarized [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md), not an unpolarized ensemble. Its [Bloch vector](../../../../../bloch-vector.md) lies along the [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) axis of the combined operation. There is **no [Ramsey interferometry](../../../../../ramsey-interferometry.md) fringe as $\alpha$ varies**, because the state during phase accumulation occupies only one basis level. [Quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) in the _final_ state alone does not guarantee phase sensitivity to the specified sequence.
