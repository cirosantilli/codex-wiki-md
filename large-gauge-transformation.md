# Large gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [U(1) gauge symmetry](u-1-gauge-symmetry.md)

A large gauge transformation does not approach the identity at the relevant spatial or asymptotic boundary. Although it leaves the local field strength unchanged, its boundary charge can act nontrivially on physical states and constrain soft limits through a [Ward identity](ward-identity.md).
