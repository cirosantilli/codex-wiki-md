# Uniform-density stellar model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

A uniform-density spherical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) model with zero surface pressure has $P(r)=2\pi G\rho^2(R^2-r^2)/3$, $M=4\pi\rho R^3/3$ and gravitational energy $-3GM^2/(5R)$. A [perfect gas](ideal-gas.md) can have this mechanical structure with varying [temperature](temperature.md) and entropy; the imposed equilibrium density does not require incompressible perturbations.
