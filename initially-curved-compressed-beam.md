# Initially curved compressed beam

↑ **Parent:** [Euler's critical load](euler-s-critical-load.md)

For a pin-ended slender beam with unstressed profile $y_0$, [bending moment](bending-moment.md) depends on [curvature](curvature.md) change: $M=EI(y-y_0)$. An axial dead load gives $EI(y-y_0)=-Fy$ in the small-slope model.
