<h1 id="isopropyl-beta-d-1-thiogalactopyranoside">Isopropyl β-D-1-thiogalactopyranoside</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lac operon](lac-operon.md)

[IPTG](isopropyl-beta-d-1-thiogalactopyranoside.md) is a nonmetabolizable lactose analogue used as an inducer of the [lac operon](lac-operon.md). Binding changes [LacI](laci.md)'s affinity for the [lac operator](lac-operator.md); it supplies a way to change repressor availability without assuming that cytoplasmic transport is instantaneous.
