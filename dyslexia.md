# Dyslexia

↑ **Parent:** [Reading disorder](reading-disorder.md)

A developmental pattern centred on difficulties in accurate or fluent word [reading](reading.md) and [spelling](spelling.md). It is not synonymous with every comprehension problem, nor necessarily an isolated phonological deficit.
