# Chemostat

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemostat)

A chemostat maintains a well-mixed culture at constant volume by feeding fresh nutrient solution and removing the same volume of culture. Its [dilution rate](dilution-rate.md) is the volumetric flow divided by the culture volume.
