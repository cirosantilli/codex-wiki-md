# Tension

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force.md)

Tension is a pulling [force](force.md) transmitted along a taut string, rope, or similar body. For an ideal uniform string with [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$, constant tension $T$ sets the small transverse [wave](wave.md) speed $c=\sqrt{T/\mu}$.
