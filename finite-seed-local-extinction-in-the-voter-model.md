# Finite-seed local extinction in the voter model

↑ **Parent:** [Voter-model duality](voter-model-duality.md)

If the initial one-spin set $F$ is finite on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), [voter-model duality](voter-model-duality.md) gives $P(\xi_t(x)=1)=\sum_{z\in F}p_t(x,z)\to0$. The [transition probabilities](transition-probability.md) tend to zero on this infinite [lattice](lattice.md); their [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) also proves the [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) by [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md). A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) on any finite set of observation sites then proves [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) to the all-zero point mass in the [product topology](product-topology.md).
