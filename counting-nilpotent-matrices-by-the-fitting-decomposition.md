# Counting nilpotent matrices by the Fitting decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent matrix](nilpotent-matrix.md)

There are $q^{n^2-n}$ [nilpotent matrices](nilpotent-matrix.md) of size n over $F_q$. Every matrix has a unique invariant direct sum on which it is nilpotent and invertible. Counting those ordered decompositions gives $q^{n^2}/|GL_n(q)|=\sum_{k=0}^n a_k/|GL_k(q)|$. Subtraction at n and n−1 yields $a_n=q^{n^2-n}$. Translation by the identity counts [unipotent matrices](unipotent-matrix.md).
