# Quadratic geodesic first integral

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-two Killing tensor](rank-two-killing-tensor.md)

A quadratic [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) in the fibre variables of the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) is a first integral of the [geodesic Hamiltonian](geodesic-hamiltonian.md) exactly when its symmetric lowered coefficients form a [rank-two Killing tensor](rank-two-killing-tensor.md). For $v=g^{-1}p$, the [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) is $\{K,H\}=\nabla_{(a}K_{bc)}v^av^bv^c$, so the equivalence follows by comparing cubic coefficients at every point.
