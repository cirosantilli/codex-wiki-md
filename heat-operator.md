# Heat operator

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For the sign convention $\Delta=\operatorname{div}\nabla$, the heat operator is the [differential operator](differential-operator.md) $L=\partial_t-\Delta$. The [heat equation](heat-equation.md) is $Lu=0$. If $P=-\Delta$ is the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md), the same operator is $\partial_t+P$. A [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md) is a fundamental solution with the initial [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md).
