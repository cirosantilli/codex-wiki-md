# Implicit midpoint rule

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit Runge-Kutta method](implicit-runge-kutta-method.md)

The implicit midpoint rule advances an autonomous ordinary differential equation by

$$
y_{n+1}=y_n+h f\left(\frac{y_n+y_{n+1}}2\right).
$$

It is a second-order, [A-stable](a-stability.md), [time-symmetric numerical method](time-symmetric-numerical-method.md).

The [implicit midpoint rule](implicit-midpoint-rule.md) is the [midpoint method](midpoint-method.md) with $y_{n+1}=y_n+h f(t_n+h/2,(y_n+y_{n+1})/2)$. It is a one-stage [implicit Runge-Kutta method](implicit-runge-kutta-method.md).
