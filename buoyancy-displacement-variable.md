# Buoyancy displacement variable

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy perturbation](buoyancy-perturbation.md)

In a radially stratified [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md), one can normalize the scalar [buoyancy perturbation](buoyancy-perturbation.md) by writing $b_x=-N^2\theta$ and $D_t\theta=u_x+\xi\nabla^2\theta$. With this convention $\theta$ has dimensions of length, rather than [temperature](temperature.md); $\xi$ is [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md).
