# Inextendible embedded surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth surface](smooth-surface.md)

An embedded [smooth surface](smooth-surface.md) $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ is inextendible when every connected smooth surface $\widetilde S\subset\mathbb R^3$ containing $S$ is equal to $S$.
