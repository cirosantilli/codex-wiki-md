# Beam mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic cooling](stochastic-cooling.md)

[Beam mixing](beam-mixing.md) changes sample membership between turns through revolution-time differences. Repeatedly correlated samples and thermal [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) limit [stochastic cooling](stochastic-cooling.md).[Section 5, p.295, Mixing and thermal noise, Eqs.(3)–(4)](stochastic-cooling-and-the-accumulation-of-antiprotons-by-simon-van-der-meer.md)

Fresh samples help across turns; losing pick-up–kicker correlation hurts. Useful [feedback gain](feedback-gain.md) therefore depends on [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md), delay, beam dynamics and [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md).
