# Peptidoglycan

↑ **Parent:** [Cell wall](cell-wall.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peptidoglycan)

[Peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md) is a mesh of linked [sugar](sugar.md) chains cross-linked by short chains of [amino acids](amino-acid.md) in the [cell walls](cell-wall.md) of most [bacteria](bacteria.md). Its absence from the [cell walls](cell-wall.md) of [archaea](archaea.md) helps distinguish envelope chemistry without confusing either [cell wall](cell-wall.md) with a [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md). See [OpenStax Microbiology, 3.3: cell walls and peptidoglycan](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells).
