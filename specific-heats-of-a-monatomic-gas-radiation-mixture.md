# Specific heats of a monatomic gas-radiation mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic exponents of a monatomic gas-radiation mixture](adiabatic-exponents-of-a-monatomic-gas-radiation-mixture.md)

For fixed-composition monatomic [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) plus equilibrium [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md), let $\mathcal R=k/(\mu H)$ and $\beta=P_g/P$. The mass-specific heats are $c_P=\mathcal R(32-24\beta-3\beta^2)/(2\beta^2)$ and $c_V=\mathcal R(24-21\beta)/(2\beta)$. The [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md) is $\Gamma_1/\beta$, not generally any one of the [stellar adiabatic exponents](stellar-adiabatic-exponent.md). Differentiate at constant total pressure rather than at constant $\beta$.
