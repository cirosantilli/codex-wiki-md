# Product of finite probability distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Finite probability distribution](finite-probability-distribution.md)

Given two [finite probability distributions](finite-probability-distribution.md), assign the product of their [probability masses](probability-mass.md) to each [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md). The support is the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of the two positive-mass supports. Distributivity gives $\sum_{x,y}p_1(x)p_2(y)=(\sum_xp_1(x))(\sum_yp_2(y))=1$. This is the joint distribution for independent coordinate outcomes; it is a construction from the marginals, not a claim that arbitrary jointly observed coordinates are independent.
