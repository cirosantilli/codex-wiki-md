# Lebesgue invariance of the doubling map

↑ **Parent:** [Dyadic transformation](dyadic-transformation.md)

The [doubling map](dyadic-transformation.md) preserves normalized [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) on the unit circle. Each point has exactly two preimages, $x/2$ and $(x+1)/2$ modulo one, and each inverse branch scales lengths by $1/2$.
