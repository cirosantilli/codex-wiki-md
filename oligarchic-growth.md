# Oligarchic growth

↑ **Parent:** [Core accretion](core-accretion.md)

A stage in which several neighboring [planetary embryos](planetary-embryo.md) dominate stirring of surrounding [planetesimals](planetesimal.md) and grow in a self-regulated manner. Larger embryos heat their feeding populations more strongly, weakening [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md) and preventing unlimited [runaway planetesimal growth](runaway-planetesimal-growth.md). A small number of embryos coexist with many much smaller bodies.
