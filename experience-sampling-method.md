# Experience sampling method

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological research methods](psychological-research-methods.md)

Collecting repeated reports about ongoing experience in ordinary life. Frequent reports reduce some retrospective demands, but are still measurements with selection, response and reactivity issues. An observed association is not automatically a randomized causal effect.
