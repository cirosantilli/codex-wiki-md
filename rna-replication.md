# RNA replication

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

Copying an RNA sequence into RNA using complementary base pairing. It is distinct from [transcription](transcription-biology.md) of a DNA template and from [reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md) into DNA.
