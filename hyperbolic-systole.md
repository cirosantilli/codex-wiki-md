# Hyperbolic systole

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

The [infimum](infimum.md) of nonconstant closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) lengths in curvature $-1$, with value $+\infty$ if there are none. On a [closed surface](closed-surface.md) with a [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) this is a positive attained minimum, and a minimizing [geodesic](geodesic.md) is simple. For fixed closed [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g\geq2$, a common positive lower bound gives relative compactness in the unmarked [moduli space of Riemann surfaces](moduli-space-of-riemann-surfaces.md) by [Mumford's compactness theorem](mumford-s-compactness-theorem.md).
