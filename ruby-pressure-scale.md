# Ruby pressure scale

↑ **Parent:** [Diamond anvil cell](diamond-anvil-cell.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_pressure_scale)

The [ruby pressure scale](ruby-pressure-scale.md) converts a shift of a [ruby](ruby.md) [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) [spectral line](spectral-line.md) into [pressure](pressure-split.md). Excitation [light](light.md) produces a [fluorescence emission spectrum](fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md); fitting its $R_1$ line gives a [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda_{R_1}$. The reference $\lambda_{R_1,0}$ must be measured or assigned at specified reference [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md). The difference $\Delta\lambda$ is an observable; interpreting it as [pressure](pressure-split.md) requires a [pressure calibration](pressure-calibration.md) and suitable [stress](stress.md) conditions.

The original 1972 report found sharp $R_1,R_2$ emission shifting toward lower [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) as [pressure](pressure-split.md) increased; it tentatively associated broadening with nonhydrostatic conditions.[Abstract: direction of R-line shifts and tentative nonhydrostatic interpretation](pressure-measurement-made-by-the-utilization-of-ruby-sharp-line-luminescence-by-richard-a-forman-and-others.md) In use, excitation rejection, a calibrated [wavelength](wavelength.md) axis and a fitted background matter because leakage or background can move an apparent peak. A brighter trace improves counting statistics but does not alone establish a correct [pressure](pressure-split.md) scale.
