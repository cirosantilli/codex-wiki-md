# Specific kinetic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)

[Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per unit [mass](mass.md). For an acceleration field $\mathbf a$, its change along an actual trajectory is $\Delta(|\mathbf v|^2/2)=\int\mathbf a\cdot d\mathbf r$, by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md). This differs by a mass factor from the work integral of a physical force.
