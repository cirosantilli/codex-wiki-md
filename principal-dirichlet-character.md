# Principal Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

The [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md) modulo $N$ is one on integers coprime to $N$ and zero on all other integers. Its [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md) is $L(\chi_0,s)=\zeta(s)\prod_{p\mid N}(1-p^{-s})$, so it has a simple [pole](pole.md) at one. For $N>1$ it is induced from the character of modulus one and is not a [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md).
