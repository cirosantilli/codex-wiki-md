# Weyl spinor bilinear exchange identity

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)

For odd [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md), $(\chi\psi)=\chi^\alpha\psi_\alpha$ is symmetric under exchanging the two spinors: the Grassmann sign cancels the sign of the antisymmetric epsilon contraction. With $\epsilon^{12}=1$, lower components $\chi=(a,b)$ and $\psi=(c,d)$ give $(\chi\psi)=bc-ad$. Commuting numerical spinors instead give an antisymmetric contraction.
