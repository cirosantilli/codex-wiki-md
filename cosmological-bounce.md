# Cosmological bounce

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

A smooth [cosmological bounce](cosmological-bounce.md) is a positive minimum of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md): contraction changes to expansion, with $a(t_b)>0$, $\dot a(t_b)=0$ and $\ddot a(t_b)>0$ in the nondegenerate case. The [closed slicing of de Sitter spacetime](closed-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md) gives $a=H_\Lambda^{-1}\cosh[H_\Lambda(t-t_b)]$, whose minimum is $H_\Lambda^{-1}$. The [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) with zero separate [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) requires $\varrho+3p<0$ there.
