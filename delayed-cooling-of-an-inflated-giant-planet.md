# Delayed cooling of an inflated giant planet

↑ **Parent:** [Hot-Jupiter radius inflation](hot-jupiter-radius-inflation.md)

Delayed cooling preserves a young giant planet's large radius by reducing the escape of internal [energy](energy.md). Enhanced atmospheric [opacity](opacity.md), an irradiation-maintained outer radiative blanket, and composition gradients that inhibit [convection](convection.md) can slow [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md). These mechanisms retain existing heat rather than supplying a new deep heat source.
