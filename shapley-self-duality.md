# Shapley self-duality

↑ **Parent:** [Shapley value](shapley-value.md)

Complementing predecessor sets within $N\setminus\{i\}$ preserves their [Shapley value](shapley-value.md) weights, since the factorials $|S|!$ and $(n-|S|-1)!$ interchange. For the [dual coalitional game](dual-coalitional-game.md), the corresponding [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) equals the original contribution at that complementary set. Hence the values coincide. The same bijection gives $\phi_i(v)=\sum_S w(S)(v(N\setminus S)-v(S))$.
