# Small-argument expansion of the hyperbolic tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic tangent](hyperbolic-tangent.md)

The [taylor series](taylor-series.md) at zero begins

$$
\tanh x=x-\frac{x^3}{3}+O(x^5),
$$

so $\tanh x\sim x$ as $x\to0$.
