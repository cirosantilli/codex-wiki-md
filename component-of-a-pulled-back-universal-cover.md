# Component of a pulled-back universal cover

↑ **Parent:** [Universal cover](universal-cover.md)

For a locally path-connected subspace $A\subseteq X$, a selected component of the inverse image in a [universal cover](universal-cover.md) covers the corresponding component $A_0$ of $A$. Its covering [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $\ker(\pi_1(A,a_0)\to\pi_1(X,a_0))$; surjectivity onto all of $A$ additionally requires $A$ to be path connected.
