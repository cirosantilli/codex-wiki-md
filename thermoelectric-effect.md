# Thermoelectric effect

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermoelectric_effect)

A [thermoelectric effect](thermoelectric-effect.md) couples heat flow and electrical transport. Temperature differences can generate an electromotive force, while [electric currents](electric-current.md) can transport heat. These effects concern nonequilibrium transport and must be distinguished from equilibrium contact potentials.
