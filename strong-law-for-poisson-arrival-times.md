# Strong law for Poisson arrival times

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson process](poisson-process.md)

For a homogeneous [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of rate $\lambda>0$, $N(t)/t\to\lambda$ almost surely: unit-time increments are independent Poisson variables and the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) applies, then monotonicity fills the intervals between integers. At its $n$th point $Y_n$, simplicity gives $N(Y_n)=n$. Consequently $Y_n/n\to1/\lambda$. Rate and mean interarrival time are reciprocals.
