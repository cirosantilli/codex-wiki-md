# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1994

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

For pioneering [neutron scattering](neutron-scattering.md) techniques for studying [condensed matter](condensed-matter.md), [Bertram Neville Brockhouse](bertram-neville-brockhouse.md) received one half for developing [neutron spectroscopy](neutron-spectroscopy.md), while [Clifford Glenwood Shull](clifford-glenwood-shull.md) received one half for developing [neutron diffraction](neutron-diffraction.md).[Individual official 1994 summary: equal halves; Brockhouse neutron spectroscopy and Shull neutron diffraction are distinct contributions.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1994-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
