# Highest posterior density interval

↑ **Parent:** [Credible interval](credible-interval.md)

A [highest posterior density interval](highest-posterior-density-interval.md) is a [credible interval](credible-interval.md) whose included points have at least as high [posterior density](posterior-density.md) as excluded points, apart from boundary and null-set choices. It is a [highest density region](highest-density-region.md) of prescribed [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md) that happens to be an interval. For a continuous unimodal posterior with two interior endpoints, the endpoint densities agree. A monotone posterior on a half-line instead starts at a support boundary, as in a [Pareto highest density interval](pareto-highest-density-interval.md). For a multimodal [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md), the highest-density region can be disconnected and cannot always be represented by a single interval. Density-level regions use a specified parameter coordinate and reference measure; they are not generally preserved by nonlinear reparametrization.
