# Influence of a variable

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete derivative of a Boolean function](discrete-derivative-of-a-boolean-function.md)

For the $\{-1,1\}$-valued convention on the unbiased [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md), the influence of coordinate $i$ is $\operatorname{Inf}_i(f)=\lVert D_i f\rVert_2^2$, where $D_i f$ is the [discrete derivative of a Boolean function](discrete-derivative-of-a-boolean-function.md). It equals the [probability](probability.md) that flipping coordinate $i$ changes $f$. For the $\{0,1\}$-valued convention, the same flip probability is instead $4\lVert D_i f\rVert_2^2$, since a nonzero discrete derivative has magnitude $1/2$.
