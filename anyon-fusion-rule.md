# Anyon fusion rule

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon](anyon.md)

An [anyon fusion rule](anyon-fusion-rule.md) specifies which total [anyon](anyon.md) charges $c$ can result from combining charges $a,b$. The nonnegative integers $N^c_{ab}$ are [anyon fusion multiplicities](anyon-fusion-multiplicity.md). The [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) is the identity for this product, and $N^1_{ab}=\delta_{b,\bar a}$ identifies the [antianyon](antianyon.md).
