# Optimal energy amplification of a linear system

↑ **Parent:** [Transient growth](transient-growth.md)

For the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) $A(t)=e^{Lt}$, maximizing the energy ratio over nonzero initial conditions gives

$$
G(t)=\max_{x_0\ne0}\frac{x_0^*A(t)^*A(t)x_0}{x_0^*x_0}=\|A(t)\|_2^2=\sigma_{\max}(A(t))^2.
$$

The [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) achieves its maximum at an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $A^*A$ with largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), equivalently a [right singular vector](right-singular-vector.md) of $A$. Such an optimal disturbance is generally not an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $L$.
