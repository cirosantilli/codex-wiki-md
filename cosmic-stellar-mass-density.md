# Cosmic stellar mass density

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy stellar mass function](galaxy-stellar-mass-function.md)

The [cosmic stellar mass density](cosmic-stellar-mass-density.md) is the mass moment of a [galaxy stellar mass function](galaxy-stellar-mass-function.md) in a stated volume convention. With a comoving mass function it is mass per [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md), while proper density is larger by $(1+z)^3$. Comparing it to a critical or matter density requires keeping the epoch and volume convention explicit.
