# Most probable rupture force under a linear ramp

↑ **Parent:** [Survival under a linear force ramp](survival-under-a-linear-force-ramp.md)

Differentiate the logarithm of the rupture-force [probability density](probability-density.md) $k(F)p(F)/f$. Its interior stationary point satisfies $e^{F/F_0}=f/(k_0F_0)$. The physical domain is $F\geq0$, giving the displayed [mode](mode-statistics.md). This differs from the [median rupture force under a linear ramp](median-rupture-force-under-a-linear-ramp.md); replacing one by the other shifts a fitted intercept and can bias the inferred zero-force rate.
