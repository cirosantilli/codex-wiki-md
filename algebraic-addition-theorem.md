# Algebraic addition theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)

A [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) $f$ has an [algebraic addition theorem](algebraic-addition-theorem.md) if some nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $P\in\mathbb C[U,V,W]$ satisfies $P(f(z),f(w),f(z+w))=0$ wherever the three values are finite. For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f$ of degree $d>0$, take roots $z_i$ of $f(z)-U$ and $w_j$ of $f(w)-V$; the product $\prod_{i,j}(W-f(z_i+w_j))$ has [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) coefficients in $U,V$ by the [Fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials](fundamental-theorem-of-symmetric-polynomials.md), is monic in $W$, and supplies an addition relation. An [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) also has such a relation, by finite [algebraic extension](algebraic-extension.md) of its [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) and the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) addition formula.
