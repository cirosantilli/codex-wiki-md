# Signature transition from an ellipsoid to an elliptic cylinder and a one-sheet hyperboloid

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

After an [orthogonal diagonalization](orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md), the sign of the third coefficient classifies the displayed [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md). If $C>0$, it is an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) with semiaxes $A^{-1/2},B^{-1/2},C^{-1/2}$. If $C=0$, it is an elliptic cylinder parallel to the $w$ axis, with elliptical transverse section. If $C<0$, it is a [one-sheet hyperboloid](one-sheet-hyperboloid.md) with axis $w$ and waist ellipse $Au^2+Bv^2=1$. The continuous limit $C\to0$ sends the associated axial length scale to infinity. Repeated positive eigenvalues make the corresponding principal-axis choices nonunique within their eigenspace, without changing the surface.
