# Sphere rolling in a symmetric groove

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling acceleration with rotational inertia](rolling-acceleration-with-rotational-inertia.md)

A [sphere](sphere.md) of [radius](radius.md) $a$ touching both faces of a symmetric groove of opening angle $\phi$ rotates instantaneously about the line joining its contacts. The centre is a distance $h=a\sin(\phi/2)$ from this line, so [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) means $v=h\omega$. For a homogeneous [sphere](sphere.md), $K=mv^2/2+I\omega^2/2=mv^2[1+2/(5\sin^2(\phi/2))]/2$. Differentiating this [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) with respect to distance down an incline gives the displayed [acceleration](acceleration.md). Both contact [forces](force-split.md) do no work for fixed faces and no slip.
