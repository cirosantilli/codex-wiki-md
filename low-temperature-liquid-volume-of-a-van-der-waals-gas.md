# Low-temperature liquid volume of a van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals liquid-gas coexistence](van-der-waals-liquid-gas-coexistence.md)

For one mole, liquid–vapour [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) requires equal [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). At $\tau=RTb/a\ll1$, the saturation pressure is exponentially small. Neglecting it in the dense-phase [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) gives $RTV_l^2-aV_l+ab=0$. The root near the excluded volume is

$$
V_l\simeq\frac{a}{2RT}\left(1-\sqrt{1-4RTb/a}\right)
=\frac{2b}{1+\sqrt{1-4\tau}}
=b(1+\tau+2\tau^2+\cdots).
$$

This is an asymptotic low-temperature result, not an exact coexistence volume at every subcritical temperature. Away from that limit, solve equal pressure together with the [Maxwell equal-area construction](maxwell-construction.md).
