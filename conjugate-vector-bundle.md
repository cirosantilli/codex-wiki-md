# Conjugate vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md)

The conjugate bundle has the same underlying real bundle and the opposite complex scalar action. Its transitions are the complex conjugates of the original transitions. For a holomorphic bundle on $X$, these transitions are smooth and generally antiholomorphic on $X$; it is not automatically a holomorphic bundle there. It is naturally holomorphic over the conjugate [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) instead.
