# Quasineutrality

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

On spatial scales much larger than the [Debye length](debye-length.md), a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) may have nearly cancelling positive and negative [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) densities while still carrying an [electric current](electric-current.md) and having a nonzero [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Quasineutrality is an approximation on appropriate slow macroscopic scales, not an assertion that each fluid element has exactly zero charge or that charge separation can be ignored at every boundary.
