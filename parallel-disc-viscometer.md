# Parallel-disc viscometer

↑ **Parent:** [Couette flow](couette-flow.md)

In the thin-gap, laminar approximation, a rotating disc of radius $a$ separated by $d$ from a stationary disc shears a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md). The resisting [torque](torque.md) is $\pi\eta\omega a^4/(2d)$, neglecting edge effects and secondary flows. This gives a way of inferring [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) from a measured [torque](torque.md).
