# Finite prime-intersection representation of a radical

↑ **Parent:** [Radical of an ideal](radical-of-an-ideal.md)

In a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), every [radical of an ideal](radical-of-an-ideal.md) is an intersection of finitely many [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md). A maximal counterexample [ideal](ideal.md) $J$ cannot be prime. Choose $a,b\notin J$ with $ab\in J$; the strictly larger [ideals](ideal.md) $J+(a)$ and $J+(b)$ satisfy the result, and $\sqrt J=\sqrt{J+(a)}\cap\sqrt{J+(b)}$. This contradicts maximality. The unit [ideal](ideal.md) uses an empty intersection.
