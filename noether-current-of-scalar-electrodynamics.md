# Noether current of scalar electrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md)

For $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+iqA_\mu$, the constant phase transformation $\delta\phi=-iq\epsilon\phi$ has the displayed [Noether current](noether-current.md). Varying the [gauge field](gauge-field.md) gives $\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=J^\nu$. In this convention $J^\mu=iq(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*)-2q^2A^\mu|\phi|^2$. The matter equations imply $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$; the integrated [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) additionally requires zero boundary flux. Local phase invariance gives a [Noether second theorem](noether-second-theorem.md) identity, rather than independent physical charges for arbitrary functions.[§1.3.4, phase current; §§6.1.1 and 6.3.2, gauge symmetry and scalar coupling; signs here use the explicitly stated $D_\mu$ convention](quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
