# Absolute continuity of measures

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)

A measure $\nu$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$, written $\nu\ll\mu$, when every $\mu$-null measurable set is also $\nu$-null. This is distinct from the regularity property of functions called [absolute continuity](absolutely-continuous-function.md).
