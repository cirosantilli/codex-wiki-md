# Boojum

↑ **Parent:** [Topological defect](topological-defect.md)

A defect localized at a boundary of an ordered medium. A spherical inclusion with degenerate [planar anchoring](planar-anchoring.md) in a [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md) can have a bipolar texture with two surface boojums, unlike a bulk point-defect pair.
