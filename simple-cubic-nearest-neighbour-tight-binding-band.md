# Simple-cubic nearest-neighbour tight-binding band

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal tight-binding approximation](orthogonal-tight-binding-approximation.md)

For one orthonormal localized orbital per simple-cubic site and real equal nearest-neighbour [hopping integral](hopping-integral.md) $t$, the six neighbours give the displayed [energy band](energy-band.md). Each cosine ranges from minus one to one, so the positive bandwidth is $12|t|$, independently of the sign convention for $t$. Near the zone centre, $E=E_0+6t-ta^2k^2+O(k^4)$ and the [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) is $-\hbar^2/(2ta^2)$. It is positive there for $t<0$; for $t>0$ the zone centre is a maximum instead.
