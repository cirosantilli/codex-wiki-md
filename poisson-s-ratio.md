↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson's_ratio)

Poisson's ratio is minus the transverse strain divided by longitudinal strain under uniaxial loading in isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md).
