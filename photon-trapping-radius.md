# Photon-trapping radius

↑ **Parent:** [Photon trapping in an accretion flow](photon-trapping-in-an-accretion-flow.md)

The photon-trapping radius is where the outward [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) time equals the inward [inflow time](inflow-time.md). Radiation generated inside it is predominantly advected toward the accretor.
