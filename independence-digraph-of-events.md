# Independence digraph of events

↑ **Parent:** [Dependency graph of events](dependency-graph-of-events.md)

For events $A_i$, a directed [graph](graph-split.md) is an independence digraph if $A_i$ is independent of the [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) generated by the events indexed by its non-outneighbors, excluding $i$. The direction records which joint independence condition is available. A cyclically oriented triangle imposes pairwise independence; it does not impose independence of all three events together.
