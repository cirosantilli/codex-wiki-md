# Chain-growth propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)

In the one-site linear model, [chain-growth propagation](chain-growth-propagation.md) changes $(C,A,S)$ by $(0,0,1)$: one [monomer molecule](monomer-molecule.md) is consumed, one [monomeric unit](monomeric-unit.md) is incorporated, and the [polymerization-active site](polymerization-active-site.md) is regenerated. Thus longer chains do not imply more active sites. Repeated [chain-growth propagation](chain-growth-propagation.md) can increase $S$ substantially with $A$ unchanged. This is a material count; it supplies no universal time dependence or empirical [chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md) formula.
