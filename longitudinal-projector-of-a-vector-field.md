# Longitudinal projector of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson propagator](gauge-boson-propagator.md)

At a nonzero Euclidean [momentum](momentum.md) $k$, the [longitudinal projector of a vector field](longitudinal-projector-of-a-vector-field.md) is $P_L^{ab}=k^ak^b/k^2$. Its Lorentzian mixed-index form is $(P_L)^a{}_b=p^ap_b/p^2$ for non-null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). Together with the [transverse projector of a vector field](transverse-projector-of-a-vector-field.md), it obeys $P_T+P_L=I$, $P_TP_L=0$, $P_T^2=P_T$, and $P_L^2=P_L$. This formula is not a pointwise decomposition at null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md).
