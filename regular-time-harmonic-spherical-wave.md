# Regular time-harmonic spherical wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For a [spherically symmetric function](spherically-symmetric-function.md), $v=ru$ reduces the radial [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) to $v_{tt}=c^2v_{rr}$. A time-harmonic separated solution has radial numerator $C\sin(kr)+D\cos(kr)$, with $k=\omega/c$. Finiteness at the origin excludes $D$, and the prescribed finite origin value fixes $C$. This is a standing combination of incoming and outgoing waves. A purely outgoing point-source wave instead has a $1/r$ singularity and must be specified by its source strength, not by a finite value at the origin.
