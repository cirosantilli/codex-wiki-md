# Nuclear-recoil kinematics of WIMP scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md)

For nonrelativistic [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) of a [WIMP](weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) of [mass](mass.md) $m_\chi$ from a stationary nucleus of [mass](mass.md) $m_N$, the [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) is $\mu_{\chi N}=m_\chi m_N/(m_\chi+m_N)$. In the centre-of-momentum description, a [scattering angle](scattering-angle.md) $\vartheta$ gives [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer squared $q^2=2\mu_{\chi N}^2v^2(1-\cos\vartheta)$. The target [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $E_R=q^2/(2m_N)$, giving the displayed maximum at backscattering. In SI units, using [masses](mass.md) in kilograms and [velocity](velocity.md) in metres per second gives joules. Low recoil [energy](energy-split.md) and a falling recoil spectrum make threshold and response [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) important for [dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md).
