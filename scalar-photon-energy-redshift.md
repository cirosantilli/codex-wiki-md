# Scalar photon energy redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar cosmological perturbation](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md)

For the [Newtonian gauge in cosmology](newtonian-gauge.md) with potentials $\psi,\phi$, photon comoving momentum $\epsilon$ obeys $d\ln\epsilon/d\eta=\phi'-\mathbf e\cdot\nabla\psi=-d\psi/d\eta+\phi'+\psi'$. The homogeneous physical-energy redshift is separated by $E=\epsilon/a$. The total derivative follows the unperturbed photon path at linear order.
