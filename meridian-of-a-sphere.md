# Meridian of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

A [great circle](great-circle.md) passing through a chosen pair of antipodal poles, or the pole-to-pole half of that circle at fixed longitude. In [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), a fixed azimuth selects such a half-circle. Meridians are special cases of a [meridian of a surface of revolution](meridian-of-a-surface-of-revolution.md) and are [geodesics](geodesic.md) of the [sphere](sphere.md).
