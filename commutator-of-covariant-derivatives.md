# Commutator of covariant derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md)

For a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md), the [commutator](commutator.md) of coordinate [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) vanishes on a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) and acts on a [vector field](vector-field.md) through the displayed [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md). On a [covector field](one-form.md) it instead gives $[\nabla_\alpha,\nabla_\beta]A_\mu=-R^\nu{}_{\mu\alpha\beta}A_\nu$. The [curvature commutator is a tensor derivation](curvature-commutator-is-a-tensor-derivation.md), so each index of a general [tensor](tensor.md) contributes independently with the corresponding sign. Including the lower derivative index in the second [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) is essential for this identity.
