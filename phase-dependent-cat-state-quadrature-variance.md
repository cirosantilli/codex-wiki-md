# Phase-dependent cat-state quadrature variance

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger cat state](schrodinger-cat-state.md)

For real $\alpha$, put $s=e^{-2\alpha^2}$ in $N(|\alpha\rangle+e^{i\phi}|-\alpha\rangle)$. Its mean second quadrature is $-\alpha s\sin\phi/(1+s\cos\phi)$, which is generally nonzero. Subtracting its square from the second moment gives the displayed [variance](variance-split.md) and squeezing precisely when $\cos\phi>-s$, for nonzero finite amplitude. A useful counterexample to confusing moment and [variance](variance-split.md) is $\phi=\pi/2$.
