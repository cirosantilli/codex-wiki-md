# Exchange interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchange_interaction)

The [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) is an effective interaction between [spins](spin.md) originating in the quantum mechanics of [electrons](electron.md). Its sign convention must accompany any formula. For the illustrative classical fixed-length [spin](spin.md) pair used here, write

$$
E=J\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=JS^2\cos\theta.
$$

With this plus-sign convention, $J>0$ favors antiparallel [spins](spin.md): $E(\pi)=-JS^2$, compared with $E(0)=+JS^2$. The [energy](energy-split.md) difference is $2JS^2$. If instead the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is written with an overall minus sign, the same physical preference has a negative coupling. This prevents a sign convention in the [Ising model](ising-model.md) from being mistaken for a disagreement about the ordering.
