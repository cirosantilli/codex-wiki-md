# Effective equation of state of an oscillating scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)

Suppose a homogeneous scalar field oscillates much faster than the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) in a monomial potential $V(\phi)=\lambda|\phi|^n$. The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) averaged over one oscillation gives $\langle\dot\phi^2\rangle=n\langle V\rangle$, and hence

$$
\langle w\rangle=\frac{\langle P\rangle}{\langle\rho\rangle}=\frac{n-2}{n+2},
\qquad
\rho\propto a^{-6n/(n+2)}.
$$

A quadratic minimum therefore behaves like [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), whereas a quartic minimum behaves like [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md).
