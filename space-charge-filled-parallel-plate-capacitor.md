# Space-charge-filled parallel-plate capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md)

Between large plates at prescribed [electric potentials](electric-potential.md) and separation $d$, fixed uniform [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$ gives $V(x)=V_1+(V_2-V_1)x/d+\rho x(d-x)/(2\epsilon_0)$ by [Poisson's equation](poisson-s-equation.md). The signed [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is linear. Its magnitude can have an interior V-shaped minimum, so a sketch of signed field alone need not answer a request for field magnitude.
