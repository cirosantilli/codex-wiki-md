# Ambient cardinal bound on the constructible reals

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible power set](constructible-power-set.md)

In a [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) universe, the [constructible universe theorem](constructible-universe-theorem.md) supplies a [bijection](bijection.md) inside $L$ between $\omega_1^L$ and its reals. The same graph is a [bijection](bijection.md) externally. Each [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) below $\omega_1^L$ has a countability witness in $L$, hence also externally, proving $\omega_1^L\leq\omega_1$ without assuming [cardinal](cardinal-number-split.md) absoluteness. Therefore the constructible reals have ambient [cardinality](cardinality.md) at most $\aleph_1$. If the ambient continuum is larger, some subset of $\omega$ is not constructible. The [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) $\omega_1^L$ may be externally [countable](countable-set.md), so the argument is an upper bound and not a claim of equality.
