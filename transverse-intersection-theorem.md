# Transverse intersection theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md)

If embedded submanifolds $A,B\subseteq M$ meet transversely, then $A\cap B$ is an embedded submanifold of dimension $\dim A+\dim B-\dim M$.

The hypothesis is [transversality](transversality-of-a-map-to-a-submanifold.md): tangent spaces to the two submanifolds span the ambient tangent space at each intersection point.
