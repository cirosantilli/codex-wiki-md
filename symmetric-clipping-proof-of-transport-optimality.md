# Symmetric clipping proof of transport optimality

↑ **Parent:** [Strong c-monotonicity](strong-c-monotonicity.md)

For a nonnegative cost, simultaneous symmetric clipping preserves feasibility of a pair of [Kantorovich potentials](kantorovich-potential.md). On their equality set the clipped sums are nonnegative and increase to the cost. Their bounded integrals are fixed by the marginals, so [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) proves optimality without subtracting undefined infinite marginal integrals.
