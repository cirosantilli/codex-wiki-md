# Ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md)

For a vertical [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in a homogeneous [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md), a horizontal [normal mode](normal-mode.md) with growth rate $s$ and vertical [Alfvén frequency](alfven-frequency.md) $\omega_a=kv_{Az}$ satisfies

$$
(s^2+\omega_a^2)(s^2+\omega_a^2-2q\Omega^2)+4\Omega^2s^2=0.
$$

The two roots for $s^2$ are real. For $q>0$, a growing magnetic mode exists precisely when $0<\omega_a^2<2q\Omega^2$. [Magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) is strong enough to couple radially displaced elements, yet weak enough to allow their separation to increase. [Ogilvie's lecture on magnetorotational instability](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/gio10/dad14.pdf) derives this relation and its finite-thickness boundary conditions.
