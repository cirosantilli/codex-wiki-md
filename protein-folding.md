# Protein folding

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_folding)

[Protein folding](protein-folding.md) is the process by which a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) acquires an organized spatial arrangement. The interactions of its [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) with one another and the surroundings favor some arrangements over others. [Protein folding](protein-folding.md) can bring chemically useful groups together at an [active site](active-site.md) without changing their order along the [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md). Its importance is practical: knowing a sequence alone does not tell us whether a sample has the structure needed for function. See [Cooper: acquiring functional protein structure](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9843/).

The [ribonuclease A refolding experiment](ribonuclease-a-refolding-experiment.md) showed that an isolated [protein](protein.md) could recover its native properties without a pre-existing folded template under suitable laboratory conditions. Anfinsen interpreted this through a thermodynamic hypothesis: the native arrangement minimizes the whole system's [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) in the appropriate environment. His Nobel lecture was delivered on 11 December 1972 and published in Science in 1973. The specified environment is part of the claim, not a disposable qualification. See [Anfinsen, Principles that Govern the Folding of Protein Chains, Science 181, 223–230 (1973): Nobel lecture, especially p. 223](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~amittal/1973_Anfinsen_Science.pdf).

This does not imply that every [protein](protein.md) rapidly folds unaided. [Molecular chaperones](chaperone-protein.md) can assist [protein folding](protein-folding.md) and reduce [protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md); required partners, surroundings and time still matter. Goloubinoff and colleagues' 1989 reconstitution experiments demonstrated assistance from two [molecular chaperones](chaperone-protein.md) and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) in recovering an active [enzyme](enzyme.md) from unfolded chains. This is experimental evidence that assistance can matter even when the chains already have their specified sequences. See [Goloubinoff, Christeller, Gatenby and Lorimer, 1989: chaperone-assisted reconstitution of an active enzyme](https://www.nature.com/articles/342884a0).
