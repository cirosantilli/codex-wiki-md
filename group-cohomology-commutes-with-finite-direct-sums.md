# Group cohomology commutes with finite direct sums

↑ **Parent:** [Projective-resolution definition of group cohomology](projective-resolution-definition-of-group-cohomology.md)

For $\mathbb ZG$-modules $M_1,M_2$,

$$
H^n(G,M_1\oplus M_2)\cong H^n(G,M_1)\oplus H^n(G,M_2),
$$

because the [Hom functor](hom-functor.md) into a finite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) splits degree by degree and taking [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) preserves that splitting.
