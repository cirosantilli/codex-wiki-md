# Sheaf of smooth differential forms

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

On a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), $\mathcal A^k(U)$ is the space of smooth complex-valued degree-$k$ [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) on $U$. Restriction of forms supplies the [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) maps. Smooth [partitions of unity](partition-of-unity.md) make this a [fine sheaf](fine-sheaf.md).
