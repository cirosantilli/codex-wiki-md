# Leftmost derivation

↑ **Parent:** [Grammar derivation](grammar-derivation.md)

A [grammar derivation](grammar-derivation.md) that always expands the leftmost remaining [nonterminal symbol](nonterminal-symbol.md). In a [context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md), each [parse tree](parse-tree.md) specifies exactly one [leftmost derivation](leftmost-derivation.md). Two distinct [leftmost derivations](leftmost-derivation.md) of the same [word](string.md) therefore prove that the [context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md) is an [ambiguous grammar](ambiguous-grammar.md).
