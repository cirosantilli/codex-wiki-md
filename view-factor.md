# View factor

↑ **Parent:** [Net radiative heat exchange](net-radiative-heat-exchange.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/View_factor)

The [view factor](view-factor.md) $F_{i\to j}$ is the fraction of uniformly distributed, diffuse outgoing [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) from surface $i$ that directly reaches surface $j$. It depends on geometry, including obstruction, rather than on [temperature](temperature-split.md) or [emissivity](emissivity.md). All destinations in a closed enclosure have fractions summing to one. For a convex body fully enclosed by walls, $F_{\rm body\to walls}=1$; the reverse [view factor](view-factor.md) is generally smaller. Reciprocity gives $A_iF_{i\to j}=A_jF_{j\to i}$. Two unobstructed spheres with outer [radius](radius.md) ten times the inner [radius](radius.md) have $F_{\rm inner\to outer}=1$ and $F_{\rm outer\to inner}=1/100$. Do not multiply both directions by an assumed identical [view factor](view-factor.md).[§19.4, equations 19.4 and following reciprocity relation; §19.4.1, concentric spheres](thermodynamics-and-propulsion.md)
