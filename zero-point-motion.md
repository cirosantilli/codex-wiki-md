# Zero-point motion

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md)

[Zero-point motion](zero-point-motion.md) is the nonzero quantum fluctuation of position and momentum in a system's [ground state](ground-state.md). For a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), the [ground state](ground-state.md) has [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega/2$ and nonzero position and momentum variances even at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md). It does not represent a thermal mixture and need not carry [entropy](entropy-split.md). The large [zero-point motion](zero-point-motion.md) of light [helium](helium.md) atoms helps explain why [helium](helium.md) can remain a [liquid](liquid.md) at low [pressure](pressure-split.md) even as [temperature](temperature-split.md) approaches [absolute zero](absolute-zero.md).
