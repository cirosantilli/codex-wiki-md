# Baryonic Tully-Fisher relation

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md)

An empirical scaling between a disk [galaxy](galaxy-split.md)'s total baryonic [mass](mass.md) and its characteristic flat rotation speed. The ideal isolated deep-[MOND](modified-newtonian-dynamics.md) limit predicts $v_f^4=GM_ba_0$. Observational tests must specify the speed estimator, distances, gas masses and stellar [mass-to-light ratios](mass-to-light-ratio.md); the empirical relation alone does not determine a unique theory of gravity.
