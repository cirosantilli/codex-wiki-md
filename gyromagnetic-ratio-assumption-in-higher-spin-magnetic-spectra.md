# Gyromagnetic-ratio assumption in higher-spin magnetic spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md)

The scalar and Dirac magnetic [spectra](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) do not determine every higher-spin [spectrum](spectrum-topology.md). Extrapolating to $E^2=m^2c^4+p_z^2c^2+|e|\hbar|B|c(2n+1-2m_s)$ assumes [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) two. For a spin-one field with that coupling the lowest aligned mode becomes unstable once its squared [frequency](frequency.md) turns negative; spin alone does not imply this coupling.
