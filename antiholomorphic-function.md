# Antiholomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiholomorphic_function)

A complex-valued function is antiholomorphic when its complex conjugate is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). In one complex dimension it locally depends on $\overline z$ and satisfies $\partial_zf=0$. A nonconstant antiholomorphic map reverses the orientation induced by the complex coordinates. Thus complex conjugating a holomorphic sphere map reverses its [topological degree](topological-degree.md) while preserving its Dirichlet energy.
