# Replicator equation

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary game theory](evolutionary-game-theory.md)

In a symmetric [matrix](matrix.md) game, a strategy frequency grows in proportion to its excess [payoff](payoff.md) above the current [mean](expected-value.md). Summing the displayed equations gives zero, preserving the [probability simplex](probability-simplex.md). For two strategies and [payoff](payoff.md) difference $\Delta w(x)$, it reduces to $\dot x=x(1-x)\Delta w(x)$.
