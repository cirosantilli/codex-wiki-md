<h1 id="kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermochemistry">Kirchhoff's law of thermochemistry</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemistry](thermochemistry.md)

At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md), the [temperature](temperature-split.md) [derivative](derivative.md) of a reaction or transformation [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) is the corresponding difference in [heat capacity at constant pressure](heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md):

$$
\left(\frac{\partial\Delta H}{\partial T}\right)_p=\Delta C_p,\qquad
\Delta H(T_2)=\Delta H(T_1)+\int_{T_1}^{T_2}\Delta C_p(T)\,dT.
$$

Use a consistent molar or mass basis and retain each phase's range of stability when applying the relation. A latent enthalpy evaluated along a pressure-dependent [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) can also have a pressure-variation contribution; the fixed-pressure [derivative](derivative.md) must not automatically be identified with that full [derivative](derivative.md).
