# Density-slope satellite torque

↑ **Parent:** [One-sided impulse torque on a disk](one-sided-impulse-torque-on-a-disk.md)

A local odd [mass density](density.md) slope $\Sigma(x)-\Sigma(-x)=2\Sigma_0\beta x/r_0$ changes the cancelling one-sided [torques](torque.md) into this net [torque](torque.md). A local outer cutoff $L$ adds the factor $1-(H/L)^2$. Positive slope transfers net [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) from satellite to disk. An infinite linear [mass density](density.md) law is only a formal local extension, since it cannot remain nonnegative on both sides.
