# Zinc-copper displacement experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

An inspected university demonstration makes the paired changes visible. The 2021 Chemical Demonstrations Booklet hosted by Edith Cowan University describes two cut square [zinc](zinc.md) strips, each immersed in a separate glass [beaker](beaker-laboratory-equipment.md). One contains blue [copper(II) sulfate](copper-ii-sulfate.md) [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), the other colourless [zinc sulfate](zinc-sulfate.md) [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). The published readouts are the appearance of a brown [copper](copper.md) deposit on the first strip and fading of its blue [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md); after thirty minutes more deposit is reported. The comparison strip in [zinc sulfate](zinc-sulfate.md) shows no visible change. The documented preparation is cutting the strips; surface polishing is not specified.[Chemical Demonstrations Booklet (2021), demonstration 16, printed p. 32, “Equipment”, “Preparation”, “Observations” and Figures 1–3; p. 33, Figure 4](chemical-demonstrations-booklet-by-magdalena-wajrak-and-tim-harrison.md)

The reported interpretation is conversion of dissolved $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ to metallic [copper](copper.md), accompanied by conversion of metallic [zinc](zinc.md) to dissolved $\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}$. The [sulfate](sulfate.md) [anions](anion.md) balance bulk [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and cancel from the net [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md). The comparison shows that merely immersing a [zinc](zinc.md) strip in a [sulfate](sulfate.md) [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) does not reproduce the visible coating.[Demonstration 16, printed pp. 32–33: zinc-sulfate comparison and zinc/copper-sulfate equation](chemical-demonstrations-booklet-by-magdalena-wajrak-and-tim-harrison.md)

Appearance is evidence for the proposed conversion, rather than a quantitative measurement of the two-[electron](electron.md) ratio. Fading alone could also reflect dilution; deposit colour alone does not independently establish its composition. A stronger follow-up would compare the [copper](copper.md) content of the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) before and after, identify the solid, and check dissolved [zinc](zinc.md). These are proposed analytical checks, not results reported in the booklet. Comparable starting [chemical solutions](solution-chemistry.md), surface condition, immersion and observation [time](time-in-physics.md) help interpret a comparison; an unchanged-looking strip does not rule out slow or invisible [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md).

The two-[electron](electron.md) interpretation comes from the species identities and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) balance, supported by the paired observations. It does not imply that the coating's [mass](mass.md) equals the [mass](mass.md) of [zinc](zinc.md) consumed: the same [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of different [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) generally have different [molar masses](molar-mass.md). Nor does it resolve microscopic surface sites or reaction pathways. The same net [redox](redox.md) can be organized into separated compartments in a [Daniell cell](daniell-cell.md) to obtain electrical [work](work.md).
