# Highest-weight representation

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A highest-weight representation is generated by a [weight vector](weight-vector.md) killed by every positive-root subspace.

The generating vector has a highest [Weight](weight-representation-theory.md) relative to the chosen positive root system.
