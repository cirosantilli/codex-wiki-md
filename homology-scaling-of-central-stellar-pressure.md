# Homology scaling of central stellar pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar homology](stellar-homology.md)

In [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md), a fixed dimensionless density profile gives $\rho_c=C_\rho M/R^3$ through the mass integral. Integrating the [stellar hydrostatic equation](hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) through the same profile gives $P_c=C_PGM^2/R^4$ when surface pressure is negligible. The constants depend on the shape, rather than the mass and radius. For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md), $T_c=(C_P/C_\rho)\mu GM/(\mathcal R R)$.
