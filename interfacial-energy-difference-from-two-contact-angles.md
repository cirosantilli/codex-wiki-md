# Interfacial-energy difference from two contact angles

↑ **Parent:** [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md)

Subtracting [Young's equations](young-s-equation.md) for two liquids on the same solid eliminates the unknown solid–vapour [interfacial energy](interfacial-energy.md). The result assumes equilibrium angles and an unchanged solid surface. Swelling, contamination or [contact-angle hysteresis](contact-angle-hysteresis.md) can invalidate this cancellation as a comparison of equilibrium material properties.
