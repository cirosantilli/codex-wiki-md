# Reverse SLE derivative bound above the space-filling threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Reverse SLE derivative martingale](reverse-sle-derivative-martingale.md)

If $\kappa>8$, then for every $0<\alpha<(1-8/\kappa)/2$ and fixed $T$, there is an almost surely finite random $C$ such that

$$
|h_T'(x+iy)|\leq Cy^{-1+\alpha}
$$

for $|x|\leq1$ and $0<y\leq1$. The proof combines the derivative martingale, [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md), a dyadic lattice, the [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md), and the [Koebe distortion theorem](koebe-distortion-theorem.md).
