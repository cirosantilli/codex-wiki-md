<h1 id="pick-s-theorem">Pick's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice point](lattice-point.md)

For a simple [polygon](polygon.md) whose vertices are [lattice points](lattice-point.md) in $\mathbb Z^2$, its [area](surface-area.md) is $A=I+B/2-1$, where $I$ counts interior [lattice points](lattice-point.md) and $B$ counts boundary [lattice points](lattice-point.md).
