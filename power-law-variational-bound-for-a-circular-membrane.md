# Power-law variational bound for a circular membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Radial vibrations of a circular membrane](radial-vibrations-of-a-circular-membrane.md)

For a fixed circular [elastic membrane](elastic-membrane.md), the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md) applied to $f(r)=1-(r/a)^p$, $p>0$, gives $\lambda_1\leq(p+3+2/p)/a^2$. The numerator of its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) is $p/2$, and the denominator is $a^2p^2/[2(p+1)(p+2)]$. Minimization gives $p=\sqrt2$, $\lambda_1\leq(3+2\sqrt2)/a^2$, and the displayed upper bound for the ordinary [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu_1=c\sqrt{\lambda_1}/(2\pi)$. Every $p>0$ has finite radial Dirichlet energy, which is the required variational admissibility condition.
