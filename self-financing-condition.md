# Self-financing condition

↑ **Parent:** [Self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md)

For holdings with value $V_t=\phi_tS_t+\psi_tB_t$, the condition says that all gains and losses arise from the held assets, with no external cash transfer. The stochastic-integrability and admissibility requirements on the holdings are part of a valid trading strategy. Differentiating the value identity alone does not establish self-financing, since changes of holdings can contribute additional terms.
