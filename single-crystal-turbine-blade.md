# Single-crystal turbine blade

↑ **Parent:** [Nickel-based superalloy](nickel-based-superalloy.md)

A turbine blade cast as one crystal to remove high-temperature [grain boundary](grain-boundary.md) sliding and [grain-boundary cavitation](grain-boundary-cavitation.md) pathways. This does not eliminate [dislocations](dislocation.md) or all sources of [creep](creep-deformation.md).
