# Massive chiral superstring supersymmetry multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [First massive level of a chiral RNS sector](first-massive-level-of-a-chiral-rns-sector.md)

At the first massive chiral level, 128 NS bosons and 128 R fermions furnish a massive multiplet with 16 real supercharges and no central charge. Its rest-frame [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) algebra has eight fermionic creation operators, producing $2^8=256$ states. The massive little-group representations are $44+84$ for the bosons and a 128-dimensional vector-spinor for the fermions.
