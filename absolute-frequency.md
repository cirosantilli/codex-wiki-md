# Absolute frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md)

The [absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md) is the generally complex [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) at the physically selected [absolute wavenumber](absolute-wavenumber.md). Its [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) is the fixed-position exponential [growth rate](growth-rate.md) under the convention $e^{-i\omega t}$. Marginal zero exponential growth may still carry an algebraic prefactor.
