# Disk (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_(mathematics))

A planar [Euclidean disk](disk-mathematics.md) consists of the points within a fixed radius of a centre, with either the open or closed boundary convention. The closed disk centered at the origin is $D_R=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:|x|\leq R\}$ and has [area](surface-area.md) $\pi R^2$. It is the two-dimensional closed Euclidean ball; the general [closed disc](closed-disc.md) may have any dimension.
