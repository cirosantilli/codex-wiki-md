# Log-determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

On [positive-definite matrices](positive-definite-matrix.md), the log-determinant has differential $d\log\det A=\operatorname{Tr}(A^{-1}dA)$ and is a [strictly concave function](strictly-concave-function.md).
