# Vanishing of global sections of the once-twisted projective cotangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent Euler sequence in homogeneous coordinates](cotangent-euler-sequence-in-homogeneous-coordinates.md)

Dualize the [Euler sequence on complex projective space](euler-sequence-on-complex-projective-space.md) and tensor by the [hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md) to obtain $0\to\Omega^1(1)\to\mathcal O^{n+1}\to\mathcal O(1)\to0$. On global sections the last map sends $(c_i)$ to $\sum_i c_iX_i$, an isomorphism onto the linear forms. Left exactness of [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) therefore gives the asserted vanishing. No higher-cohomology assumption is needed for this final kernel calculation.
