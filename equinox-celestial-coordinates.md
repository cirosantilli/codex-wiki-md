# Equinox (celestial coordinates)

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial coordinate system](equatorial-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equinox_(celestial_coordinates))

The [reference equinox](equinox-celestial-coordinates.md) is the direction conventionally used as the origin of [right ascension](right-ascension.md): the northward intersection of the [Sun](sun.md)'s annual path with the reference [celestial equator](celestial-equator.md). Its mean or true convention and reference date specify which direction is intended. Here the [reference equinox](equinox-celestial-coordinates.md) is a direction on the [celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md), rather than the instant of a seasonal crossing. [US Naval Observatory: Equinox and mean versus true reference directions](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/asa_glossary).
