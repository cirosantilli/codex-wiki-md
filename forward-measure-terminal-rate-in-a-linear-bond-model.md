# Forward-measure terminal rate in a linear bond model

↑ **Parent:** [Linear bond pricing in a bounded short-rate diffusion](linear-bond-pricing-in-a-bounded-short-rate-diffusion.md)

For the bounded rate diffusion of [linear bond pricing in a bounded short-rate diffusion](linear-bond-pricing-in-a-bounded-short-rate-diffusion.md), $D_te^{-(T-t)}r_t$ is a bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md) with terminal value $D_Tr_T$. Dividing its [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) by $D_tP(t,T)$ through the [Bayes formula for conditional expectation](bayes-formula-for-conditional-expectation.md) gives the displayed forward-measure [expectation](expected-value.md). It lies between zero and one.
