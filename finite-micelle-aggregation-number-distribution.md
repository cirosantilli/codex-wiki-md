# Finite-micelle aggregation-number distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-action model of micellization](mass-action-model-of-micellization.md)

Here $\mu$ is the common [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) per [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) and $\mu_\alpha^0$ the standard [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) in an aggregate of size $\alpha$. A minimum of the aggregate free-energy balance at a finite size produces a micellar population around that size, in addition to monomers. Translational [entropy](entropy-split.md) leaves a small aggregate population below the nominal [critical micelle concentration](critical-micelle-concentration.md); a preferred finite size is not supplied by the mass-action equation alone.
