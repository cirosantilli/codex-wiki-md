# Strict monomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md)

A [morphism](morphism.md) $m:A\to B$ is strict when every $g:C\to B$ satisfying $hm=km\Rightarrow hg=kg$ for all parallel arrows $h,k$ out of $B$ factors uniquely through $m$. Every [regular monomorphism](regular-monomorphism.md) is strict by its [equalizer](equaliser.md) property. A strict [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) is strong: in a lifting square with an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md), cancellation of the [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) makes the bottom arrow admissible, and its unique factorization gives the diagonal.
