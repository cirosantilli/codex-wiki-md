# Rotating torsion-stabilized inverted pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion pendulum](torsion-pendulum.md)

A rigid pendulum tilted from the upward vertical and driven at fixed azimuthal [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\Omega$ has effective potential $\mu\theta^2/2+mga\cos\theta-ma^2\Omega^2\sin^2\theta/2$. With $\mu=2mga$, its upright [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) loses [linear stability](linear-stability.md) when $\Omega^2$ passes $g/a$. At equality the leading restoring [torque](torque.md) is cubic and the potential has a positive quartic minimum. This gives a stable [quartic oscillator](quartic-oscillator.md) with zero infinitesimal harmonic [frequency](frequency.md), rather than a freely drifting neutral state.
