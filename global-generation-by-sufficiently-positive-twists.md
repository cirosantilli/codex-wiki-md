# Global generation by sufficiently positive twists

↑ **Parent:** [Globally generated sheaf](globally-generated-sheaf.md)

For an [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md) $L$ on a [noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md) $X$, every [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $F$ becomes a [globally generated sheaf](globally-generated-sheaf.md) after tensoring with $L^{\otimes n}$ for all sufficiently large $n$. Finitely many [global sections](global-section.md) suffice by [finite global generation on a quasi-compact scheme](finite-global-generation-on-a-quasi-compact-scheme.md). This is the global-generation characterization of an [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md).[Stacks Project, Proposition 28.27.13, characterizations of an ample invertible sheaf](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01PR)

For a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) of [projective space](projective-space-split.md), take $L=\iota^*\mathcal O(1)$. One way to see why twisting helps is to choose finitely many module generators on each standard [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md). Multiplication by a sufficiently high power of its homogeneous coordinate extends each generator to a [global section](global-section.md) of a twist. Choose one exponent for all generators. On the original chart that coordinate is invertible, so these extended sections still generate; doing this for every chart proves global generation. Tensoring back by the inverse [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md) gives a surjection from a finite [direct sum of sheaves](direct-sum-of-sheaves.md) of negative twists onto $F$.
