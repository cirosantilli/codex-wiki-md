# Loop gain

↑ **Parent:** [Operational-amplifier negative feedback](operational-amplifier-negative-feedback.md)

The [loop gain](loop-gain.md) is the product of the forward amplifier response and the return-network fraction, with the summing-junction minus sign kept explicit. Closed-loop poles are determined by $1+L=0$ in this convention. Large low-frequency magnitude controls gain error, while frequency-dependent phase affects stability.
