# Free action of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

A [group action](group-action.md) is free when only the identity fixes any given point, or equivalently every [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) is trivial. The trivial group acts freely on every space. A nontrivial [group](group-split.md) acting freely must act by a fixed-point-free map for each nonidentity element; the [fixed-point property of even-dimensional complex projective space](fixed-point-property-of-even-dimensional-complex-projective-space.md) obstructs such actions. For a [finite group](finite-group.md) acting freely on a Hausdorff [manifold](topological-manifold.md), the quotient map is a [covering map](covering-space.md).
