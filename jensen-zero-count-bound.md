# Jensen zero-count bound

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md)

Every zero in the smaller disk contributes at least $\log(R/r)$ in [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md). Bounding the outer circle average by its maximum proves the estimate. With $R=2r$, an exponential $O(r\log r)$ maximum bound gives an $O(r\log r)$ zero count.
