# Marginal contribution

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

A player's [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) to a [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) is the increase in its value when that player joins. In a [convex cooperative game](convex-cooperative-game.md), [marginal contributions](marginal-contribution.md) increase with the preceding [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md). Averaging contributions across player orderings gives the [Shapley value](shapley-value.md).
