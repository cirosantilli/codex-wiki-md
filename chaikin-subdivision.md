# Chaikin subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision curve](subdivision-curve.md)

Iteratively replace every polygon edge by its quarter and three-quarter points, joining the resulting points in order. On an infinite or appropriately treated closed uniform control sequence, the limit is a quadratic [B-spline](b-spline.md) curve. For equally spaced abscissae, its centered [Chaikin basis function](chaikin-basis-function.md) has support of width three original grid intervals and is $C^1$, generally not $C^2$. Finite open polygons need an endpoint convention.
