# Questionnaire

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)

A structured set of items used to obtain reports or judgments. Item wording, response options, respondent perspective and scoring determine what the resulting measurements mean. [Content validity](content-validity.md), [measurement reliability](measurement-reliability.md) and [construct validity](construct-validity.md) require evidence beyond a convenient scoring rule.
