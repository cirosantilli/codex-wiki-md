# Electric and magnetic boundaries of the surface code

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon condensation at a boundary](anyon-condensation-at-a-boundary.md)

An electric boundary of the [surface code](surface-code.md) condenses electric anyons, while a magnetic boundary condenses magnetic anyons. A local boundary Pauli can create a single condensed excitation because its string may end at that boundary.
