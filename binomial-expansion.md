# Binomial expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md)

The binomial expansion expresses $(a+b)^n$ as $\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk a^{n-k}b^k$ for a nonnegative integer $n$. The [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient-split.md) give the number of ways to choose which $k$ factors supply $b$.
