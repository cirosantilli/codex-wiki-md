# Molar absorption coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Beer-Lambert law](beer-lambert-law.md)

The decadic [molar absorption coefficient](molar-absorption-coefficient.md) specifies [absorbance](absorbance.md) per geometric [length](length.md) and [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md). With $l$ in centimetres and $c$ in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, $\varepsilon$ has units $\mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}\,cm^{-1}}$; its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is $\mathrm{m^2\,mol^{-1}}$.[IUPAC Compendium of Chemical Terminology, fifth edition (Gold Book) by International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry; Beer–Lambert law, entry B00626: coefficient name and conventional and SI units](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/beer-lambert-law.md)

The units cancel in $\varepsilon lc$. Since $1\,\mathrm L=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$, one $\mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}\,cm^{-1}}$ equals $0.1\,\mathrm{m^2\,mol^{-1}}$. Specify the [wavelength](wavelength.md) and [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) conditions when giving a [molar absorption coefficient](molar-absorption-coefficient.md): changing the absorbing [chemical species](chemical-species.md) can change it even at fixed total [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md).
