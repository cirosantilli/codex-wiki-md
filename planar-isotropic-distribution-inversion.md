# Planar isotropic distribution inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Planar isotropic stellar distribution function](planar-isotropic-stellar-distribution-function.md)

The [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) associated with a [planar isotropic distribution](planar-isotropic-stellar-distribution-function.md) is $S(\Phi)=2\pi\int_\Phi^\infty F(E)dE$. Differentiation gives $F(E)=-(2\pi)^{-1}dS/d\Phi|_{\Phi=E}$. A vanishing upper-limit boundary value recovers the original integral; monotonic decrease of $S(\Phi)$ ensures a nonnegative distribution.
