# Stroke volume

↑ **Parent:** [Cardiac output](cardiac-output.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroke_volume)

[Stroke volume](stroke-volume.md) is the volume expelled by a ventricle in one contraction. In a time-dependent [ventricular elastance](ventricular-elastance.md) model, if end-ejection [pressure](pressure-split.md) is $p$ and systolic compliance is $C_s$, the remaining ventricular volume is $V_0+C_sp$. Subtracting that from the end-filling volume gives the ejected volume.
