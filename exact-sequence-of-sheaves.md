# Exact sequence of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md)

An [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) of [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) is a sequence of sheaf homomorphisms for which the image of each map equals the kernel of the next. Exactness can be checked on every [stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md). Exactness of the associated sequences of sections on every open set is a stronger condition: a surjective sheaf homomorphism need only admit lifts locally.
