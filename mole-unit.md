# Mole (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mole_(unit))

The mole is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) base unit of [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). One mole contains exactly $6.02214076\times10^{23}$ specified entities, as fixed by the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md). It counts entities rather than a fixed [mass](mass.md) or volume. [BIPM, SI base unit: mole](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/mole)
