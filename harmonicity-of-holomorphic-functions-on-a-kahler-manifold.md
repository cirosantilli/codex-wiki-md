<h1 id="harmonicity-of-holomorphic-functions-on-a-kahler-manifold">Harmonicity of holomorphic functions on a Kähler manifold</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md)

The [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md) $\Delta_d^{\mathrm H}=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}$ implies that every [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). On functions, $\bar\partial u=0$ and $\bar\partial^*u=0$ by degree, so both terms of the [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) vanish. Compactness is unnecessary. Changing the sign convention for the scalar Laplacian does not change this conclusion.
