# Unicyclic graph

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle in a graph](cycle-in-a-graph.md)

A [unicyclic graph](unicyclic-graph.md) is a [connected graph](connected-graph.md) with exactly one [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md). Deleting the [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) of that [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) leaves [trees](tree-graph-theory-split.md), each meeting it in exactly one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Otherwise a surviving [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md), or a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) between two [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of the original [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md), would give another [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md). Thus a finite [unicyclic graph](unicyclic-graph.md) is a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) with [rooted trees](rooted-tree.md) attached at its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
