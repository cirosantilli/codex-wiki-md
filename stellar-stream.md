# Stellar stream

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar halo](stellar-halo.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_stream)

A [stellar stream](stellar-stream.md) is a correlated collection of [stars](star.md) commonly originating in the disruption of a bound progenitor. [Phase mixing](phase-mixing.md) can erase spatial contrast while leaving coherence in orbital integrals. Such fossil debris constrains a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md)'s past accretion, although not every stream is from a galaxy: star clusters also produce streams.
