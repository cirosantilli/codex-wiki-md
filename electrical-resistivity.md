# Electrical resistivity

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)

Electrical resistivity is the inverse of scalar [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) in an isotropic Ohmic medium. In its rest frame, $\mathbf E=\rho_{\rm el}\mathbf j$, so the [electric field](electric-field.md) does work per unit volume $\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E=\rho_{\rm el}|\mathbf j|^2$. In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), finite conductivity gives [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta=c^2/(4\pi\sigma)$; neglecting this diffusion is a distinct approximation from neglecting electron inertia in [Hall magnetohydrodynamics](hall-magnetohydrodynamics.md).
