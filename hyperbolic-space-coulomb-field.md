# Hyperbolic-space Coulomb field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

In static time times round three-dimensional [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md) of curvature radius $R$, a geodesic sphere has area $4\pi R^2\sinh^2(\xi/R)$. The [Gauss law](gauss-s-law.md) therefore gives the displayed radial field. It approaches the flat-space inverse-square field near the charge and falls exponentially at large geodesic distance. The curvature changes the area of a sphere, not the conserved flux.
