# Radial drift of solids in a gas disk

↑ **Parent:** [Sub-Keplerian rotation](sub-keplerian-rotation.md)

For test particles with linear [drag](drag-physics.md), negligible radial gas flow and negligible dust feedback, [Sub-Keplerian rotation](sub-keplerian-rotation.md) removes solid-particle [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The displayed inward [velocity](velocity.md) uses [Stokes number](stokes-number.md) $\mathrm{St}=\Omega_Kt_s$ and the [pressure-gradient parameter of a protoplanetary disk](pressure-gradient-parameter-of-a-protoplanetary-disk.md). Drift is fastest at $\mathrm{St}=1$; the physical particle size at that point depends on the local gas and drag law.
