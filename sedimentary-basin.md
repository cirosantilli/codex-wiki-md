# Sedimentary basin

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentology](sedimentology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_basin)

A region of long-term accommodation and accumulation of [sediments](sediment.md), commonly associated with subsidence. Its [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md) depends on geometry and the [geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) between fill and surrounding [rock](rock-geology.md).
