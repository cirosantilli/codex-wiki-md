# Linearized coordinate gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

An infinitesimal coordinate change generated by $\xi$ changes a metric perturbation about [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) by $h_{\mu\nu}\mapsto h_{\mu\nu}-\partial_\mu\xi_\nu-\partial_\nu\xi_\mu$, in the passive sign convention. For a passive coordinate change $x^\mu\mapsto x^\mu+\xi^\mu$, the first-order change is minus the [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) of the background metric, equivalently the pullback by the inverse flow. The [gauge invariance of the linearized Riemann tensor](gauge-invariance-of-the-linearized-riemann-tensor.md) follows because the added terms have commuting third derivatives, which cancel in the curvature antisymmetrizations. Gauge choices alter the metric components while preserving linearized tidal effects.
