# Screening misclassification model

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden Markov model](hidden-markov-model.md)

In a [screening misclassification model](screening-misclassification-model.md), true disease states are latent and screen results are emissions. A cancer-free state may emit a positive result with a [false positive rate](false-positive-rate.md), while a perfectly sensitive pre-clinical state always emits a positive result. Conditional independence of emissions given latent states is an additional model assumption, not a consequence of a marginal false-positive rate.
