# Trace computation of Specht module dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md)

Right multiplication by $e_t=h_t/H_\lambda$ on the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) is an [idempotent](idempotent.md) with image $Ae_t$. Every diagonal entry in the [permutation](permutation.md) basis is the coefficient of the identity in $e_t$, namely $1/H_\lambda$. Thus its rank equals its trace, giving $\dim S^\lambda=n!/H_\lambda$. Triangular standard-tableau ideals and the [Robinson–Schensted correspondence](robinson-schensted-correspondence.md) identify this dimension with the standard-tableau count.
