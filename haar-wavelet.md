# Haar wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal wavelet](orthonormal-wavelet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haar_wavelet)

The Haar wavelet has one [vanishing moment](vanishing-moment.md) and [compact support](compact-support.md) of unit length. Its [scaling function](scaling-function.md) is $\chi_{[0,1)}$, and its refinement coefficients are $h_0=h_1=1/\sqrt2$. The approximation spaces consist of functions constant on dyadic cells. Its discontinuity makes it effective for jump localization but limits smooth-function approximation.
