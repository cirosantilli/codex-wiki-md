# Schwinger parameterization

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}A>0$ and $\operatorname{Re}v>0$, rescaling the defining [gamma function](gamma-function.md) integral proves this representation, with compatible complex-power branches. It turns denominator powers into exponential factors, making [Gaussian integrals](gaussian-integral.md) possible. Combining two parameters by their sum and ratio gives the usual [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md) identity. Outside its convergence domain a further continuation or pole prescription is needed.
