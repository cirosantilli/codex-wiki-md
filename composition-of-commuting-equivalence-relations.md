# Composition of commuting equivalence relations

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

Using the left-to-right convention for [composition of relations](composition-of-relations.md), two [equivalence relations](equivalence-relation.md) $R,S$ have an equivalence-relation composite exactly when $R\circ S=S\circ R$. Its inverse is $S\circ R$. Commutation gives $(R\circ S)^2=R^2\circ S^2=R\circ S$, proving transitivity. Every equivalence relation containing $R,S$ contains their two-step composite, so this is their least common extension.
