# Pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

A pulse is a localized disturbance that passes a fixed location during a bounded or effectively bounded time interval. A [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) pulse may be written $y(x,t)=f(x-ct)$ with localized $f$. Its width and peak [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) describe its extent and size; a [wave packet](wave-packet.md) additionally emphasizes a carrier and an envelope.
