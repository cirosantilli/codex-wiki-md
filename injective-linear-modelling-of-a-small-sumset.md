# Injective linear modelling of a small sumset

↑ **Parent:** [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field](freiman-ruzsa-theorem-over-a-finite-field.md)

For a finite subset $B\subseteq\mathbb F_2^N$ containing zero, choose $m=\lceil\log_2(2|12B|)\rceil$ and a uniformly random [linear map](linear-map.md) $L:\mathbb F_2^N\to\mathbb F_2^m$. Each fixed nonzero vector has zero image with [probability](probability.md) $2^{-m}$. The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) gives a map whose [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) meets $12B$ only at zero. It is injective on $6B$, and $2^m<4|12B|$. The [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md) makes the image of $B$ have density at least $1/(4C^{12})$ if $|B+B|\le C|B|$.
