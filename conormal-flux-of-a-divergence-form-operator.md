# Conormal flux of a divergence-form operator

↑ **Parent:** [Weak formulation](weak-formulation.md)

For sufficiently regular coefficients and a [function](function-split.md) $u$, this [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) quantity is the outward flux appearing when a divergence-form principal operator is integrated by parts. Its zero condition is the natural [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) for the corresponding [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md). With only [bounded](boundedness.md) coefficients and a Sobolev solution, the [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) can encode that natural condition without asserting that a [pointwise](pointwise-convergence.md) [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [derivative](derivative.md) exists.
