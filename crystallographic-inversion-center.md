# Crystallographic inversion center

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic point group](crystallographic-point-group.md)

A point $\mathbf r_0$ about which inversion maps each atomic species onto equivalent sites of that species, possibly after lattice translations. An inversion center need not coincide with an atom.
