# Softened Toomre dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre's stability criterion](toomre-s-stability-criterion.md)

Linear axisymmetric compressive modes of a [barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk](barotropic-closure-of-a-razor-thin-disk.md) combine radial epicyclic restoration, softened [self-gravity](self-gravity.md) and [pressure](pressure.md). The [off-plane razor-thin Poisson kernel](off-plane-razor-thin-poisson-kernel.md) supplies the exponential gravity reduction. Here $k$ is the nonnegative radial [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) magnitude. The full linear system also has an [axisymmetric geostrophic mode](axisymmetric-geostrophic-mode.md).
