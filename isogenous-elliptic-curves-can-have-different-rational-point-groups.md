# Isogenous elliptic curves can have different rational point groups

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

[Elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md) isogenous over a [finite field](finite-field.md) have the same number of rational points: the isogeny intertwines Frobenius, and multiplicativity of degree gives equal degrees for $1-\pi$. Their groups need not be isomorphic. Over $\mathbb F_7$, $y^2=x^3-x$ and $y^2=x^3+4x$ are two-isogenous and have groups $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z$, respectively.
