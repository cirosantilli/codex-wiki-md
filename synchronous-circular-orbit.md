# Synchronous circular orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md)

A satellite remaining above a fixed point of a uniformly rotating spherical planet must have a prograde equatorial [circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md) with angular speed $2\pi/T$. Balancing gravitational acceleration and centripetal acceleration gives the displayed radius and speed $v=2\pi r/T$. The satellite's own mass cancels. A physically realizable external orbit additionally requires this radius to exceed the planet's radius.
