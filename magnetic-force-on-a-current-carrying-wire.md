# Magnetic force on a current-carrying wire

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

A wire carrying current $I$ in a magnetic field experiences

$$
d\mathbf F=I\,d\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf B.
$$

This follows by summing the magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) over the moving charge carriers.
