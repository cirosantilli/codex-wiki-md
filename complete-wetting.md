# Complete wetting

↑ **Parent:** [Wetting](wetting.md)

A liquid spreads macroscopically over a solid with zero macroscopic equilibrium [contact angle](contact-angle.md). In the simple interfacial-energy model the threshold is $\gamma_{SV}-\gamma_{SL}=\gamma_{LV}$, or zero [spreading coefficient](spreading-coefficient.md); positive coefficient favors a spreading film. Extrapolating a concentration-dependent liquid-vapour [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) to this threshold assumes the other interfacial energies remain fixed and no intervening adsorption or micellization behavior changes the trend.
