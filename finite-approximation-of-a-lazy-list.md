# Finite approximation of a lazy list

↑ **Parent:** [Partial list](partial-list.md)

A finite [list prefix](prefix-of-a-list.md) followed by bottom records only the constructors known so far. The chain $\bot,\ x_1:\bot,\ x_1:x_2:\bot,\ldots$ approximates an infinite [lazy list](lazy-list.md). A [Scott continuous map](scott-continuity.md) is determined on the limit by its values on these approximations. This justifies extending an identity proved on finite [partial lists](partial-list.md) to definable operations on infinite [lists](list-abstract-data-type.md); equality merely on finite terminating [lists](list-abstract-data-type.md) does not provide the same premise.
