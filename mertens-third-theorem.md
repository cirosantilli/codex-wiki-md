# Mertens third theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens' theorems](mertens-theorems.md)

There is a constant $C>0$ such that

$$
\prod_{p\leq x}\left(1-\frac1p\right)^{-1}
=C\log x+O(1).
$$

Taking logarithms reduces the result to the [Mertens second theorem](mertens-second-theorem.md), because the terms of order $p^{-2}$ and smaller form an absolutely convergent series.
