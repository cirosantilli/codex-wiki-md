# Fovea

↑ **Parent:** [Retina](retina.md)

A central retinal region specialized for detailed vision, with a dense cone mosaic and a rod-free central region. Receptor density, optical blur and downstream sampling jointly constrain spatial resolution; visual sensitivity is not uniform across the [retina](retina.md).
