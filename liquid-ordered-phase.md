# Liquid-ordered phase

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A [liquid-ordered phase](liquid-ordered-phase.md) combines relatively ordered lipid chains with lateral molecular mobility. It is distinct from a solid gel and can coexist with a [liquid-disordered phase](liquid-disordered-phase.md) in suitable mixtures.
