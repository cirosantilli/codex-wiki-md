# Photonic GHZ preparation by fourfold coincidence

↑ **Parent:** [Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger state](greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md)

A [postselection](postselection.md) scheme in which two [polarization-entangled photon pairs](polarization-entangled-photon-pair.md) pass through [polarizing beam splitters](polarizing-beam-splitter.md), a [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) and a nonpolarizing [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md). One photon at each of a trigger and three output detectors selects a [GHZ state](greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md) in the three output polarizations, provided [optical mode matching](optical-mode-matching.md) removes distinguishable pair labels. All four detections are needed for the selected event; the trigger alone does not guarantee the three other output occupations.
