# Disc-shaped nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Classical nucleation theory](classical-nucleation-theory.md)

A cylindrical model nucleus of [diameter](diameter.md) $d$ and height $h$. Its [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is $\pi d^2h\Delta g/4+\pi d^2\gamma_f/2+\pi dh\gamma_e$, where $\Delta g$ is the signed bulk free-energy change per [volume](volume.md).
