# Reflux

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflux)

[Reflux](reflux.md) is condensed [liquid](liquid.md) returned toward the heated part of a [distillation](distillation.md) apparatus. In a [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md), descending [reflux](reflux.md) contacts rising [vapor](vapor.md). Returning more condensate also changes the amount available for collection, so enrichment and product recovery must be considered together.[Introduction, reflux paragraph; Intended Learning Outcomes, reflux-ratio dependence](distillation-by-hong-kong-university-of-science-and-technology.md)
