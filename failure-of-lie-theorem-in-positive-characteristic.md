# Failure of Lie theorem in positive characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Lie's theorem](lie-s-theorem.md)

In a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, take a [basis](basis.md) $e_0,\ldots,e_{p-1}$, and let $xe_j=e_{j-1}$ with indices modulo $p$, while $ye_j=je_j$. Then $[x,y]=x$, so $kx+ky$ is a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md), but the distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $y$ force any common [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) to be a coordinate vector, and $x$ cyclically permutes those vectors. There is consequently no common [eigenvector](eigenvector.md), even over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md).
