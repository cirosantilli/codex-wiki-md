# Success probability of positive quantum spectral filtering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum spectral filtering](quantum-spectral-filtering.md)

For $0\leq A<I$ and normalized $|b\rangle=\sum_j\beta_j|u_j\rangle$, filtering by $h(\lambda)=\lambda$ succeeds with

$$
P=\sum_j|\beta_j|^2\lambda_j^2\geq\lambda_{\min}^2.
$$

The inequality follows by replacing each nonnegative $\lambda_j^2$ by its minimum and using $\sum_j|\beta_j|^2=1$. Equality holds exactly when all occupied [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) belong to the minimum-[eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). If $\lambda_{\min}=0$, success is positive exactly for inputs outside the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $A$.
