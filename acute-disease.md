# Acute disease

↑ **Parent:** [Disease](disease.md)

A [disease](disease.md) with a relatively rapid onset or short course, in contrast to a [chronic disease](chronic-disease.md). Ancient authors used their own classifications; their boundaries need not match modern ones.
