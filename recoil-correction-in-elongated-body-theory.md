# Recoil correction in elongated-body theory

↑ **Parent:** [Lighthill elongated-body theory](lighthill-elongated-body-theory.md)

A deforming free swimmer must satisfy total linear and angular [momentum](momentum.md) balances. The unknown lateral translation and yaw are its recoil correction. If the reference translation is the true [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md), the prescribed deformation must first have its mass-weighted mean removed. Otherwise the reference translation differs from actual [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) displacement. The body [mass](mass.md) and its hydrodynamic [added mass](added-mass.md) contribute with the same sign to its effective inertial coefficient.
