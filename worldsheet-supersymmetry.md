# Worldsheet supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Spinning string](spinning-string.md)

Local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) on a [string worldsheet](worldsheet.md) relates embedding coordinates to their [worldsheet Majorana fermion](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) partners. Fixing this fermionic gauge symmetry introduces commuting [superconformal ghosts](superconformal-ghost.md).
