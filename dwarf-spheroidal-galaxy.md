# Dwarf spheroidal galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Dwarf galaxy](dwarf-galaxy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarf_spheroidal_galaxy)

A [dwarf spheroidal galaxy](dwarf-spheroidal-galaxy.md) is a faint, diffuse, largely pressure-supported [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) with little gas. A large dynamical [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) commonly indicates [dark matter](dark-matter.md), subject to assumptions of equilibrium, member selection, and binary-star corrections. Its stellar light and total mass need not share one density profile.
