# Tiny objects are closed under finite products

↑ **Parent:** [Tiny object](tiny-object.md)

In a [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md), exponentiation satisfies $(-)^{A\times B}\cong((-)^B)^A$. If the two exponential functors have [right adjoints](adjoint-functors.md), their composite has the composite of those right adjoints in reverse order. Therefore a product of [tiny objects](tiny-object.md) is tiny. The [terminal object](terminal-object.md) is tiny since exponentiation by it is the identity, covering the empty product.
