# Hadamard transform

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard_transform)

The normalized Hadamard transform maps a vector indexed by binary strings through entries $2^{-n/2}(-1)^{x\cdot y}$. It is its own inverse and an orthogonal transform. The [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) is the $n=1$ quantum case, and the [Hadamard basis](hadamard-basis.md) is the image of its computational basis. The [Walsh-Hadamard transform](walsh-hadamard-transform.md) gives the same tensor-power construction.
