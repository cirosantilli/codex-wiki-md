# Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md)

Over the [finite field](finite-field.md) $K=\mathbb F_{q^2}$, a [sesquilinear form](sesquilinear-form.md) relative to $a\mapsto a^q$ is linear in its first argument and conjugate-linear in its second. It is Hermitian when $B(w,v)=\overline{B(v,w)}$. Its diagonal values belong to $\mathbb F_q$; it is nondegenerate when $B(v,w)=0$ for every $w$ forces $v=0$. This definition applies in characteristic two as well.
