# Completion of a normed space

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md)

The completion of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) $X$ is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) containing an isometric dense copy of $X$. Construct it from [Cauchy sequences](cauchy-sequence.md) modulo [sequences](sequence.md) whose difference tends to zero; define the [norm](norm.md) by $\|[(x_n)]\|=\lim_n\|x_n\|$. Equivalently, the canonical evaluation map into the [bidual space](bidual-of-a-normed-space.md) is an isometry by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md), and the closure of its image supplies a completion.

A [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is precisely a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) in which every [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) converges in [norm](norm.md) to an element of the same space. [Completion of a normed space](completion-of-a-normed-space.md) fills missing limits without changing distances between existing elements. For example, $C([0,1])$ with the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) completes to [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) on the interval with [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). The bounded [integral](integral-split.md) functional extends uniquely to that [completion of a normed space](completion-of-a-normed-space.md): define $F(f)=\lim_kF(f_k)$ for any approximating [sequence](sequence.md) of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md). Its bound makes this independent of the approximation. The unbounded [point evaluation](point-evaluation-functional.md) has no continuous extension agreeing with its values on all [continuous functions](continuous-function.md).[Richard Melrose, Functional Analysis, chapter 1, sections 5–6: completion and continuous extension](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-102-introduction-to-functional-analysis-spring-2021/3d4cc88026d44a01f936cd6a0aa995cb_MIT18_102s20_lec_FA.pdf)
