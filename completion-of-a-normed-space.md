# Completion of a normed space

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)

The completion of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) $X$ is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) containing an isometric dense copy of $X$. Construct it from [Cauchy sequences](cauchy-sequence.md) modulo [sequences](sequence.md) whose difference tends to zero; define the [norm](norm.md) by $\|[(x_n)]\|=\lim_n\|x_n\|$. Equivalently, the canonical evaluation map into the [bidual space](bidual-of-a-normed-space.md) is an isometry by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md), and the closure of its image supplies a completion.
