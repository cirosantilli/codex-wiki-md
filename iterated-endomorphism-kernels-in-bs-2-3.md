<h1 id="iterated-endomorphism-kernels-in-bs-2-3">Iterated endomorphism kernels in BS(2,3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Baumslag-Solitar group](baumslag-solitar-group.md)

For $G=\langle a,t\mid ta^2t^{-1}=a^3\rangle$, the surjective endomorphism $\theta(a)=a^2$, $\theta(t)=t$ has nontrivial kernel by [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md). The [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is generated by $a_i=t^iat^{-i}$ for $i\in\mathbb Z$. For $j>i$, its defining relations give $a_j^{2^{j-i}}=a_i^{3^{j-i}}$. Hence $\theta^k([a_i,a_j])=1$ whenever $k\ge j-i$. The union of the increasing kernels is normal, and the images of all $a_i$ commute in its quotient, proving the first inclusion. The second follows from [finite residual contains surjective endomorphism kernels](finite-residual-contains-surjective-endomorphism-kernels.md) applied to every iterate.
