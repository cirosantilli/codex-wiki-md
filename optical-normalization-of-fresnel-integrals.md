# Optical normalization of Fresnel integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md)

For the optical convention of [Fresnel integrals](fresnel-integral.md), $C(w)=\int_0^w\cos(\pi u^2/2)du$ and $S(w)=\int_0^w\sin(\pi u^2/2)du$. Both functions are odd and tend to $1/2$ as $w\to+\infty$. If the unscaled convention uses phase $t^2$, multiply its argument by $\sqrt{\pi/2}$ and its value by $\sqrt{2/\pi}$ to obtain this convention. An aperture factor with phase $\pi x^2/(\lambda R)$ uses $u=x\sqrt{2/(\lambda R)}$. Stating the convention prevents missing factors of $\pi$ or $\sqrt2$ in [diffraction](diffraction.md) calculations.
