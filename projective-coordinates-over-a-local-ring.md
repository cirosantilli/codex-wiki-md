# Projective coordinates over a local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Projective space](projective-space-split.md)

For a [local ring](local-ring.md) $A$, every map from $\operatorname{Spec}A$ into [projective space](projective-space-split.md) lands in one standard chart: the inverse image of a chart containing the closed point is the whole spectrum. This gives [homogeneous coordinates](homogeneous-coordinate.md) with at least one unit entry, unique up to a common [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). For a nonlocal ring, coordinates may instead merely generate the unit ideal, and general maps can involve a nontrivial [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md) quotient.
