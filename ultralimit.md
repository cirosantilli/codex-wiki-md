# Ultralimit

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultralimit)

A point $x$ is the limit of a map $f:I\to X$ along an ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ on $I$ when $f^{-1}(V)\in\mathcal U$ for every [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $V$ of $x$. Every ultrafilter on a [compact space](compact-space.md) has a limit, and that limit is unique in a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md).
