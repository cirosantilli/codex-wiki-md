**[Preregistration](../../../../preregistration.md) creates a time-stamped record of research questions and planned methods before the relevant results are examined.** Specify the design, recruitment and stopping rules, outcome definitions, exclusions, principal tests, model choices and any conditional decision rules. For existing data, disclose prior access and what was already known. A sufficiently precise record helps readers distinguish prior predictions from choices prompted by the results.[Definition; When Can You Preregister?; FAQ on existing data](../../../../preregistration-by-center-for-open-science.md)

It does not prohibit useful changes or new discoveries: report departures, their timing and reasons, and distinguish [exploratory analysis](../../../../exploratory-analysis.md) from [confirmatory analysis](../../../../confirmatory-analysis.md). A vague or selectively followed registration does not solve selective reporting, and [preregistration](../../../../preregistration.md) cannot repair a poor measure or inadequate [design of experiments](../../../../design-of-experiments-split.md). A [registered report](../../../../registered-report.md) adds pre-result peer review and conditional publication acceptance, which are not implied by registration alone.
