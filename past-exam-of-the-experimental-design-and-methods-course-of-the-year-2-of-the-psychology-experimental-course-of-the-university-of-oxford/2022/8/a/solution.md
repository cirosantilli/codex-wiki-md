**[Bootstrapping](../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) estimates sampling uncertainty by repeating an analysis on resampled data.** In the ordinary nonparametric [bootstrap](../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md), draw observations with replacement from the empirical sample, retaining its size, and recalculate the statistic many times. The resulting distribution can estimate a [standard error](../../../../standard-error.md), assess bias or form a justified [bootstrap confidence interval](../../../../bootstrap-confidence-interval.md). It is useful when an analytic sampling distribution is difficult to obtain.

The resampling unit must match the design: keep paired measurements together, and resample independent clusters when observations are clustered. Resampling individual ratings as if they were independent lecturers would misrepresent uncertainty. The ordinary [bootstrap](../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) assumes the sample and chosen resampling scheme adequately represent the relevant data-generating variation; it does not repair [selection bias](../../../../selection-bias.md), guarantee accurate inference from a tiny sample, or succeed for every statistic. Specify the interval method and computational settings, since a simple percentile interval is not universally appropriate.
