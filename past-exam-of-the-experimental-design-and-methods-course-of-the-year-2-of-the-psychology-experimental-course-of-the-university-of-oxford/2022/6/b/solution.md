<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Make the development process inspectable and separate exploratory decisions from independent confirmation.** Create a [preregistration](../../../../preregistration.md) for the validation stage before examining its outcome-relevant data, specifying recruitment, stopping rules, exclusions, missing-data treatment, item scoring, estimator, factor structure and criteria for evaluating it. The exploratory stage can also have a recorded protocol, including a planned [parallel analysis](../../../../parallel-analysis.md) rule; label data-driven item revisions and model changes as exploratory rather than disguising them as prior predictions. Document deviations and their reasons.[What is Preregistration?; planned versus unplanned work; FAQ on detail and prior data knowledge](../../../../preregistration-by-center-for-open-science.md)

Keep an immutable copy of the collected data, record cleaning steps, and provide a data dictionary, final item wording, scoring instructions and executable analysis code. Record software and package versions, random seeds for simulations or [bootstrap](../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) calculations, and the steps needed to regenerate tables from the permitted data. These support [computational reproducibility](../../../../computational-reproducibility.md); an independently collected validation study or [scientific replication](../../../../scientific-replication.md) addresses a different question.

Share appropriately de-identified data where consent and re-identification risk permit, with controlled access or a clear restriction statement where they do not. Protect lecturer and rater identities and disclose the pilot's [convenience sampling](../../../../convenience-sampling.md) and all item-selection steps. Report inconclusive and unfavorable findings as well as favorable ones. A [registered report](../../../../registered-report.md) can obtain peer review of the validation plan before its results are known, reducing outcome-dependent publication incentives.[Details and Workflow: Stage 1 review, in-principle acceptance, Stage 2 reporting](../../../../registered-reports-by-center-for-open-science.md)
