Use **[logistic regression](../../../../logistic-regression.md)** when the [response variable](../../../../response-variable.md) is binary, for example whether a lecturer missed a deadline. It relates the event's [log odds](../../../../log-odds.md) to one or more predictors, producing fitted [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) between zero and one. Exponentiating a coefficient gives an [odds ratio](../../../../odds-ratio.md) for a specified predictor change, conditional on the other model terms. An [odds ratio](../../../../odds-ratio.md) is not generally a ratio of probabilities; the corresponding probability change depends on the starting probability and other predictors.

Report the outcome coding, estimated associations with [confidence intervals](../../../../confidence-interval.md), and relevant model checks. Sparse events, [complete separation](../../../../complete-separation.md) and dependent observations can make ordinary estimates or inferential procedures unreliable; repeated observations may need a [generalized linear mixed model](../../../../generalized-linear-mixed-model.md). For prediction, evaluate calibration and discrimination on data not used to tune the model. An adjusted association in an [observational study](../../../../observational-study.md) does not automatically identify a causal effect.
