The two [independent variables](../../../../independent-variable.md) are social-cue congruence, with congruent and incongruent levels, and cue-to-response delay, with short ($250$ ms) and long ($750$ ms) levels. The [response variable](../../../../response-variable.md) is reproduction [reaction time](../../../../reaction-time.md), measured in seconds. Thus the known treatment structure is **a $2\times2$ [factorial design](../../../../factorial-design.md)**, potentially estimating two [main effects](../../../../main-effect.md) and their [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md).
