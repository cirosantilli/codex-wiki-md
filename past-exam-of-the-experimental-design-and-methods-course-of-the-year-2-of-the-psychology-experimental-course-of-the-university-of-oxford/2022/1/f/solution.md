**TODO: an empirical Results section cannot be completed without the analysis and descriptive output.** It must identify the actual [factorial design](../../../../factorial-design.md), outcome scale and fitted model; summarize the four condition [means](../../../../expected-value-split.md) with appropriately labeled [standard deviations](../../../../standard-deviation.md) or [standard errors](../../../../standard-error.md); and report the congruence, delay and [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) tests with their actual [F-test](../../../../f-test.md) statistics, [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md), [p-values](../../../../p-value.md), [effect sizes](../../../../effect-size.md) and relevant [confidence intervals](../../../../confidence-interval.md).

Interpret an [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) as evidence that the cue-congruence difference varies with delay, not merely as evidence that one separate test was significant and another was not. Any [simple-effects analysis](../../../../simple-effects-analysis.md) must identify its contrasts and [multiple testing](../../../../multiple-hypothesis-testing.md) treatment. If the analysis used transformed [reaction times](../../../../reaction-time.md), distinguish the model's scale from raw-second summaries. A nonsignificant result does not establish equality or absence of a theoretically meaningful effect. These are reporting requirements, not placeholders for invented results.
