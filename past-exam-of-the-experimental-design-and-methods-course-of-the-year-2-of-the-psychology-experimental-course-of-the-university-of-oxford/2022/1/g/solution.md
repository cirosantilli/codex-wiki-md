**TODO: whether additional analyses are needed depends on the unavailable results and prespecified questions.** An estimated congruence-by-delay [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) can be clarified by the cue-congruence [contrast](../../../../contrast-statistics.md) at each delay, with [confidence intervals](../../../../confidence-interval.md) and justified [multiple testing](../../../../multiple-hypothesis-testing.md) control. A direct [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) test addresses whether those contrasts differ; comparing their separate significance labels does not.

Even without a significant [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md), an adequately powered, prespecified [contrast](../../../../contrast-statistics.md) may address a distinct scientific question; indiscriminate testing until something is significant is not justified. Inspect [regression diagnostics](../../../../regression-diagnostics.md) and the treatment of dependent trials where appropriate. Without the output, one cannot decide that any particular follow-up is required or conclude that none is necessary.
