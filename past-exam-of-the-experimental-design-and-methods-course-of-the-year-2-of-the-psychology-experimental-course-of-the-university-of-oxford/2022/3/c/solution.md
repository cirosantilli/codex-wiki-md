<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use a **[Student t-test](../../../../student-s-t-test.md)** to assess a mean or mean difference relative to its [standard error](../../../../standard-error.md). A one-sample version compares a [population mean](../../../../population-mean.md) with a specified reference. An independent two-sample version compares two separate groups, such as groups assigned different instructions; [Welch's t-test](../../../../welch-s-t-test.md) permits unequal group [variances](../../../../variance-split.md). A [paired t-test](../../../../paired-t-test.md) uses within-pair differences, such as each participant's before/after scores, rather than pretending those measurements are independent.[Handbook Section 1.3.5.3, paired/unpaired data and equal/unequal variance cases](../../../../two-sample-t-test-for-equal-means-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)

The relevant [normality assumption](../../../../normality-assumption.md) concerns within-group errors for the independent-groups model or the distribution of within-pair differences for the paired model. Independent participants or pairs remain essential. Report the estimated difference and its [confidence interval](../../../../confidence-interval.md) together with the test result. A small [p-value](../../../../p-value.md) indicates incompatibility with the specified [null hypothesis](../../../../null-hypothesis.md) under the model; it does not measure the size of the difference, the probability that the [null hypothesis](../../../../null-hypothesis.md) is true, or whether the difference matters practically.
