Use **[one-way analysis of variance](../../../../one-way-analysis-of-variance.md)** to compare a quantitative [response variable](../../../../response-variable.md) across levels of one categorical factor; for example, compare task accuracy across three independently assigned teaching methods. The ordinary independent-groups procedure tests the [null hypothesis](../../../../null-hypothesis.md) that all group [population means](../../../../population-mean.md) are equal. Its [F-test](../../../../f-test.md) compares variation associated with the group factor against within-group error variation; a significant result indicates that not all [population means](../../../../population-mean.md) are equal, without identifying which groups differ.[Handbook Section 3.2.3.1, Description and Model](../../../../one-way-anova-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)

The classical [one-way normal linear model](../../../../one-way-normal-linear-model.md) assumes independent observations and within-group errors approximately following a [normal distribution](../../../../normal-distribution.md) with a common [variance](../../../../variance-split.md). Repeated observations on the same people require a different dependence structure; pronounced unequal [variances](../../../../variance-split.md) may call for [Welch's one-way analysis of variance](../../../../welch-s-one-way-analysis-of-variance.md). Planned [contrasts](../../../../contrast-statistics.md) or appropriately controlled follow-up comparisons identify scientifically relevant differences. Report the [means](../../../../expected-value-split.md), differences and [confidence intervals](../../../../confidence-interval.md), not just significance. Causal interpretation depends on the [design of experiments](../../../../design-of-experiments-split.md), particularly [randomization](../../../../randomization.md), rather than on the [F-test](../../../../f-test.md) alone.
