**[Research ethics](../../../../research-ethics.md) governs how a worthwhile question is investigated while respecting those involved.** The [British Psychological Society](../../../../british-psychological-society.md)'s framework emphasizes respect for autonomy, privacy and dignity, scientific integrity, social responsibility, and maximizing benefit while minimizing harm. Seek appropriate independent ethical review before recruitment and assess risks in the actual study context.[2021, Sections 2–3 and 13: principles, risk and ethics review](../../../../code-of-human-research-ethics-by-british-psychological-society.md)

Give understandable information, seek voluntary [informed consent](../../../../informed-consent.md), explain the practical limits of withdrawal, and avoid pressure arising from a teacher–student or employer–employee relationship. Limit identifiable information, control access and distinguish confidential storage from genuine anonymity. Any justified withholding or [research deception](../../../../research-deception.md) needs proportionate review and appropriate [research debriefing](../../../../research-debriefing.md); debriefing does not undo every possible harm. Explain relevant procedures and offer questions and support consistent with the study's risks.[2021, Section 4: valid consent; informing participants and ongoing consent](../../../../code-of-human-research-ethics-by-british-psychological-society.md)

In the lecturer [questionnaire](../../../../questionnaire.md) study, make participation independent of employment evaluation and prevent identifiable responses reaching supervisors. Ethical reporting also requires honest analysis, disclosure of limitations and proportionate data sharing: [open science](../../../../open-science.md) does not require exposing confidential participant records.
