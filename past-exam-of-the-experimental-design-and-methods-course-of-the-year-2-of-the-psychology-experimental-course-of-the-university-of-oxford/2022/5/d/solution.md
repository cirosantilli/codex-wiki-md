**TODO: directions, magnitudes and inferential conclusions require the unavailable coefficient table, coding and model specification.** For illustration of interpretation only, let $D$ and $S$ be binary indicators whose reference levels are explicitly defined. If an ordinary linear model includes both factors and their [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md), its conditional mean has the form

$$
\operatorname E(Y\mid D,S)=\beta_0+\beta_DD+\beta_SS+\beta_{DS}DS.
$$

Then the distance difference is $\beta_D$ at the reference size and $\beta_D+\beta_{DS}$ at the other size; the [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) is the difference between those differences. If the [interaction](../../../../interaction-statistics.md) is omitted, the model assumes a common distance difference across sizes. These are conditional model meanings, not a claim that the missing analysis used this model or that either predictor had an observed effect. The uncertainty of a coefficient sum also involves their [covariance](../../../../covariance.md), not merely their separate [p-values](../../../../p-value.md).
