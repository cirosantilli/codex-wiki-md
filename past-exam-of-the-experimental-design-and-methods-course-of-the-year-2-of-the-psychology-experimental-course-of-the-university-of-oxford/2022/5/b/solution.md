**TODO: the model's explained-variance value is absent.** For an ordinary least-squares [linear regression](../../../../linear-regression-split.md) with an [intercept](../../../../regression-intercept.md), the [coefficient of determination](../../../../coefficient-of-determination.md) is $R^2=1-SS_{\mathrm{residual}}/SS_{\mathrm{total}}$. Its value multiplied by one hundred is the corresponding percentage of sample variation explained by that fitted model. Read the actual reported $R^2$ and distinguish it from the [adjusted coefficient of determination](../../../../adjusted-coefficient-of-determination.md); neither is a verified prediction-performance estimate on new data. Without the missing summary or sums of squares, no numerical percentage can be recovered.
