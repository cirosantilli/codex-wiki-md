# 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the experimental design and methods course of the year 2 of the psychology (experimental) course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions and source-qualified methods for the Experimental Psychology paper _Experimental design and methods_, dated 25 April 2022. Source release: [Oxford Experimental Psychology examination release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `a16816w1.pdf`.

The separate supplementary data sheet is absent from the held release. Consequently all eight parts of Question 1, parts (a)–(b) of Question 2, and all six parts of Question 5 retain explicit TODOs; conditional explanations at these leaves do not constitute recovered empirical answers. All available theory parts, including every option in the choice questions, are answered.
