# Interaction potential

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

A [function](function-split.md) assigning energy to the separation of two particles. The common symmetric case is an [even function](even-function.md), $W(z)=W(-z)$. Its average over pairs defines an [interaction energy](interaction-energy.md). If the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is bounded, the [gradient](gradient.md) is globally [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) and grows at most linearly; its [convolution](convolution.md) with a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) therefore needs only a finite first [absolute moment](absolute-moment.md), even though the potential itself can require a second [absolute moment](absolute-moment.md).
