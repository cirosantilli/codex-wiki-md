# Prepaid forward contract

↑ **Parent:** [Forward contract](forward-contract.md)

A prepaid forward pays for delivery of an asset at a future time entirely in advance. In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) with deterministic [continuous dividend yield](continuous-dividend-yield.md) $q$, one share delivered at $T$ costs the displayed amount at $t$. Reinvesting the dividends on $e^{-q(T-t)}$ shares replicates one terminal share. An ordinary [forward contract](forward-contract.md) instead has no initial payment and a delivery payment fixed in advance; at constant [interest rate](interest-rate.md) $r$ its fair delivery price is $S_te^{(r-q)(T-t)}$.
