# Brownian bridge covariance kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance kernel](covariance-kernel.md)

The covariance kernel of a standard [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md) on $[0,1]$ is $c(s,t)=\min(s,t)-st$. Its integral operator has eigenfunctions $\sqrt2\sin(k\pi t)$ and eigenvalues $(k\pi)^{-2}$ for $k\geq1$.
