# Convective instability of a square-root exponential atmospheric profile

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root exponential atmospheric profile](square-root-exponential-atmospheric-profile.md)

For a homogeneous [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) of constant [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma>1$, the [Schwarzschild criterion](schwarzschild-criterion.md) applied to the [square-root exponential atmospheric profile](square-root-exponential-atmospheric-profile.md) gives

$$
\nabla=\frac{d\log T}{d\log P}=\frac{2\sqrt{T-T_0}}{\alpha T}>\nabla_{\rm ad}=\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}.
$$

For $T_0>0$ and $\alpha>0$, put $s=\sqrt{T-T_0}$ and $A=\nabla_{\rm ad}$. The [quadratic inequality](quadratic-inequality.md) $A\alpha s^2-2s+A\alpha T_0<0$ has solutions precisely when $A\alpha\sqrt{T_0}<1$, between $s_\pm=[1\pm\sqrt{1-(A\alpha)^2T_0}]/(A\alpha)$. This is a local instability of the imposed [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md); actual [convection](convection.md) adjusts that gradient and the prescribed profile need not survive.
