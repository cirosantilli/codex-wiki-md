# Eigenvalue multiplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

Geometric multiplicity is the dimension of an [eigenspace](eigenspace.md); algebraic multiplicity is its multiplicity as a root of the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md). For a real symmetric [matrix](matrix.md) they coincide because it has an orthonormal eigenbasis.
