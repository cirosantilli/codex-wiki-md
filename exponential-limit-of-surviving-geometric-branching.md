# Exponential limit of surviving geometric branching

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric-offspring critical branching process](geometric-offspring-critical-branching-process.md)

For the stated critical geometric offspring law, the conditional upper tail is exactly $[n/(n+1)]^{\lfloor nx\rfloor}$ for $x\geq0$. Its logarithm tends to $-x$, proving an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) limit of rate one without a transform continuity theorem. The unconditional mass at zero tends to one, so survival conditioning changes the relevant limiting population scale.
