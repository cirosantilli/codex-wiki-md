# Plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Curve](curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_curve)

A [plane curve](plane-curve.md) is a [curve](curve.md) contained in a [plane](plane.md). In [algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md), one studies [plane curves](plane-curve.md) defined by [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) equations. General [plane curves](plane-curve.md), such as an [Archimedean spiral](archimedean-spiral.md), need not be [algebraic curves](algebraic-curve.md).
