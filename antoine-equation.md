# Antoine equation

↑ **Parent:** [Vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_equation)

The Antoine equation is an empirical [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) fit whose coefficients depend on the pressure and temperature units and on a stated validity interval. Its slope is $d\ln p_{\rm sat}/dT=\ln(10)B/(T+C)^2$. In the [ideal-vapor Clausius-Clapeyron approximation](ideal-vapor-clausius-clapeyron-approximation.md), this implies [latent heat](latent-heat.md) $L=R_v\ln(10)B[T/(T+C)]^2$. An accurate pressure fit need not give an equally accurate derivative; extrapolation to the [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md) cannot replace the full [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md).
