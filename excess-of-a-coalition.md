# Excess of a coalition

↑ **Parent:** [Coalition (game theory)](coalition-game-theory.md)

The excess measures a [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md)'s complaint against an allocation: a positive excess means its own attainable value exceeds its assigned total. The [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md) requires every excess to be nonpositive at an efficient allocation. The [nucleolus](nucleolus.md) lexicographically minimizes the decreasingly sorted excess vector over [imputations](imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md).
