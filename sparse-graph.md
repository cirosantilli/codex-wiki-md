# Sparse graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A family of [graphs](graph-split.md) is sparse when its [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count grows substantially more slowly than the square of its [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) count; a common convention is that $m=O(n)$, where $n$ and $m$ count [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). State the intended asymptotic convention explicitly. An [adjacency list](adjacency-list.md) then uses fewer storage entries than an [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix.md).
