# Rational canonical form under an affine change

↑ **Parent:** [Rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md)

Let $U=aT+bI$ on a nonzero finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over a [field](field.md), with $a\ne0$. An invariant block for $T$ is also invariant for $U$. If $v,Tv,\ldots,T^{d-1}v$ is its [cyclic vector](cyclic-vector.md) basis, then $v,Uv,\ldots,U^{d-1}v$ is another basis: the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) gives a triangular change-of-basis matrix with diagonal $1,a,\ldots,a^{d-1}$. The monic polynomial $g(t)=a^df((t-b)/a)$ annihilates this block, and its degree is $d$, so it is the block's [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md). In the new cyclic basis the block is $C(g)$.

The substitution preserves divisibility of the monic [invariant factors of a linear operator](invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md). Apply it to every block to obtain the [rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md) of $U$. If $a=0$, then $U=bI$ is a [scalar matrix](scalar-matrix.md) instead: all invariant factors are $t-b$, one for each dimension. This exceptional case cannot be obtained by dividing by $a$.
