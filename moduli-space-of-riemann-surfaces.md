# Moduli space of Riemann surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Moduli space](moduli-space.md)

The [moduli space](moduli-space.md) of unmarked [compact](compact-space.md) [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md). For $g\geq2$ it is the quotient $\mathcal T_g/\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$ of [Teichmüller space](teichmuller-space.md) by the [mapping class group](mapping-class-group.md), with its quotient topology. The thick subsets are [compact](compact-space.md) by [Mumford's compactness theorem](mumford-s-compactness-theorem.md).
