# Powerful number

↑ **Parent:** [Integer](integer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powerful_number)

A positive integer is squarefull when every [prime](prime-number-split.md) dividing it also divides it to at least the second power. Thus $n$ is not squarefull as soon as some prime $p$ satisfies $p\mid n$ but $p^2\nmid n$.
