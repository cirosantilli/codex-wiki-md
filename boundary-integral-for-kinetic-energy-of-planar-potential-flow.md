# Boundary integral for kinetic energy of planar potential flow

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For a smooth constant-density exterior [potential flow](potential-flow.md) satisfying the [finite-energy condition for planar exterior potential flow](finite-energy-condition-for-planar-exterior-potential-flow.md), use $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$ and the planar [streamfunction](stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. Since $\nabla^2\phi=0$, $\nabla\cdot(\phi\nabla\phi)=|\mathbf u|^2$. Apply the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to a truncated exterior region. The outer term vanishes; on a counterclockwise body boundary the body's outward normal satisfies $\partial_n\phi\,ds=d\psi$, while the fluid's outward normal points the opposite way. This gives the displayed [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per unit span. The formula yields the [added mass of a circular cylinder](added-mass-of-a-circular-cylinder.md) directly from its surface values of $\phi$ and $\psi$.
