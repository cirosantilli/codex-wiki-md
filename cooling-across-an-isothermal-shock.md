# Cooling across an isothermal shock

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal shock](isothermal-shock.md)

The [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) is equal on both sides of an isothermal perfect-gas shock. The energy removed per unit area per unit time is $j(u_1^2-u_2^2)/2$, with $j>0$ the normal mass flux. Net cooling therefore selects compression shocks; an expansion jump requires heating.
