# Baroclinic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-geostrophic vertical mode](quasi-geostrophic-vertical-mode.md)

A baroclinic mode has nontrivial vertical shear and at least one change of vertical structure through the fluid depth. Higher [quasi-geostrophic vertical modes](quasi-geostrophic-vertical-mode.md) have smaller gravity-wave speeds and [Rossby deformation radii](rossby-deformation-radius.md).
