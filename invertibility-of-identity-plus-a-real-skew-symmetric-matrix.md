# Invertibility of identity plus a real skew-symmetric matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md)

**Proof.** If $(I+A)x=0$, the [quadratic-form criterion for a skew-symmetric matrix](quadratic-form-criterion-for-a-skew-symmetric-matrix.md) gives $0=x^\top(I+A)x=\|x\|^2$, so $x=0$. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) makes the square [matrix](matrix.md) $I+A$ invertible. More generally $tI+A$ is invertible for every nonzero real $t$, since its [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) would imply $t\|x\|^2=0$. Equivalently, the only possible real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of a real [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) is zero.
