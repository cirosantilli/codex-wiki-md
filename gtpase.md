# GTPase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An enzyme hydrolysing [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) to [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md) and phosphate. Many act as nucleotide-controlled switches: hydrolysis changes the state, and nucleotide exchange restores a GTP-bound population for another cycle.
