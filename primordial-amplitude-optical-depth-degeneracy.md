<h1 id="primordial-amplitude-optical-depth-degeneracy">Primordial-amplitude–optical-depth degeneracy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Reionization damping of cosmic microwave background temperature anisotropy](reionization-damping-of-cosmic-microwave-background-temperature-anisotropy.md)

The primary small-scale [Cosmic microwave background power spectrum](cosmic-microwave-background-power-spectrum.md) measures the amplitude $A_s e^{-2\tau_{\rm re}}$, because [reionization](reionization.md) damps each temperature amplitude once. The unaffected large-scale temperature modes are few and limited by [cosmic variance](cosmic-variance.md). Polarization and gravitational lensing provide additional information that partly breaks this degeneracy.
