# Dislocation climb

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

Motion of an edge component perpendicular to its glide plane by absorbing or emitting [point defects](point-defect.md). Unlike conservative [dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md), it changes the extra-plane content locally and is coupled to defect transport. Irradiation can drive climb by creating nonequilibrium [vacancies](vacancy.md) and [self-interstitials](self-interstitial.md).
