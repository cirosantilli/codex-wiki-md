# Infectious compartment

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

The [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md) consists of people currently able to transmit [infection](infection.md). In the closed [SIR model](sir-model.md), $I(t)$ is their count and $i(t)=I(t)/N$ their [fraction](fraction.md) of the fixed total $N$. The [SIR model](sir-model.md) has no separate delay between acquiring [infection](infection.md) and entering the [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md); the [SEIR model](seir-model.md) adds such a stage.
