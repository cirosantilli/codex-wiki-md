# Cloud optical depth at fixed liquid-water path

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud albedo](cloud-albedo.md)

For spherical liquid droplets in the geometric-optics regime, the extinction efficiency is approximately2. Comparing projected extinction area with liquid-water mass gives the displayed relation. At fixed [liquid-water path](liquid-water-path.md), smaller effective radius means larger optical depth, the basis of the [Twomey effect](twomey-effect.md). Absorption spectra, phase and cloud adjustments can invalidate a simple inference from this optical-depth change to net climate forcing.
