# Real linear equivalence of divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Real Cartier divisor](real-cartier-divisor.md)

Real linear equivalence means that $D-E$ is a finite real combination of [principal Cartier divisors](principal-cartier-divisor.md). Rational linear equivalence uses rational coefficients instead. Both imply [numerical equivalence of divisors](numerical-equivalence-of-divisors.md).
