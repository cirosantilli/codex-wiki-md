# Magnetic field between circular capacitor plates

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement current](displacement-current.md)

For a circular air-filled [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) with voltage phasor $V_0e^{-i\omega t}$, uniform gap $d$ and radius $a$, the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) on a circular loop of radius $s<a$ gives the displayed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The enclosed [displacement current](displacement-current.md) is proportional to $s^2$, whereas the circulation is $2\pi sB_\phi$. Consequently its amplitude grows linearly from zero on the axis. This assumes negligible [fringing fields](fringing-field.md) and a [quasistatic approximation](quasistatic-approximation-electromagnetism.md) $\omega a/c\ll1$.
