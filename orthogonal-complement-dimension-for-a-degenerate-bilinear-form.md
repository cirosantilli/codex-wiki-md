# Orthogonal complement dimension for a degenerate bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal complement for a bilinear form](orthogonal-complement-for-a-bilinear-form.md)

For a [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) $B$ on a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$, let $\Phi:V\to V^*$ send $u$ to $B(u,\cdot)$. Under the [bidual evaluation map](bidual-evaluation-map.md), $U^\perp$ is the [annihilator of a vector subspace](annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) $\operatorname{im}(\Phi|_U)$, so $\dim U^\perp=\dim V-\operatorname{rank}(\Phi|_U)$. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) on $U$ now gives the displayed equality. In particular the sum of dimensions is at least $\dim V$. If $U\cap U^\perp=0$, the [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md) forces $V=U\oplus U^\perp$. The exact formula records the extra dimensions contributed by the left radical $\ker\Phi$, and does not assume an [inner product](inner-product-split.md).
