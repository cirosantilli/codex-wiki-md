# Universal norm of units in a Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md)

These are units that lie in every finite-layer norm image, using the p-adic closures of the unit groups. Compactness makes this intersection the image of the norm inverse limit. Local and global universal norm subgroups need not coincide. In the cyclotomic main-conjecture proof, the finite defect of global units from universal norms is related by [class field theory](class-field-theory.md) to invariants of the unramified module.
