# Affine test schemes determine scheme morphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md)

Restricting the [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) to [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) gives a [fully faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md) [functor](functor-split.md) from [schemes](scheme.md) to covariant set-valued [functors](functor-split.md) on [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md). Given a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md), apply it to the inclusions of an [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md) of $X$. [Naturality](naturality.md) on [affine open subschemes](affine-open-subscheme.md) of overlaps makes the resulting maps to $Y$ agree, so they glue. For an arbitrary map from an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) to $X$, cover its inverse images of these charts by [affine open subschemes](affine-open-subscheme.md) and apply [naturality](naturality.md) again. This proves that the glued map induces the prescribed [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md), and uniqueness follows from the original cover. In particular, the restricted [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) determines the [scheme](scheme.md) up to an [isomorphism of schemes](isomorphism-of-schemes.md).
