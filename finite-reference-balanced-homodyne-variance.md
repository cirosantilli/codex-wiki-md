# Finite-reference balanced homodyne variance

↑ **Parent:** [Balanced homodyne detection](balanced-homodyne-detection.md)

For the convention of [balanced homodyne detection](balanced-homodyne-detection.md), assume independent signal and coherent reference states and ideal detectors. Write $b=\beta+\delta b$, with $\delta b$ in vacuum. The current difference splits into $2|\beta|X_\vartheta$ and $i(a^\dagger\delta b-a\delta b^\dagger)$. Cross terms have zero expectation, and the second term has variance $\langle a^\dagger a\rangle$. Consequently the exact variance is $4|\beta|^2\operatorname{Var}X_\vartheta+\langle a^\dagger a\rangle$. Division by $4|\beta|^2$ approaches the signal [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) variance when the photon-number correction is negligible.
