# Peierls circuit bound for one-independent percolation

↑ **Parent:** [One-independent bond percolation](one-independent-bond-percolation.md)

If each bond has closed probability at most $q$ in [one-independent bond percolation](one-independent-bond-percolation.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), a set of $\ell$ bonds contains a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) of size at least $\ell/4$: divide horizontal bonds by the parity of their left endpoint and vertical bonds by the parity of their lower endpoint. Each of the four classes is a matching. Mutual independence in the largest class proves the circuit bound. There are at most $4\ell3^\ell$ dual circuits of length $\ell$ surrounding the origin. For sufficiently small $q$, the sum of these circuit probabilities is less than one, so the origin has a positive chance of belonging to an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md).
