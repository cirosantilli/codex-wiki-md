# Free filter

↑ **Parent:** [Filter (set theory)](filter-set-theory.md)

A free [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) $I$ has empty intersection of all its members. This is equivalent to containing every cofinite subset of $I$: for each $i$ some filter member omits $i$, so upward closure gives $I\setminus\{i\}$, and finite intersections give every cofinite set. A proper free filter on an infinite set contains no finite set.
