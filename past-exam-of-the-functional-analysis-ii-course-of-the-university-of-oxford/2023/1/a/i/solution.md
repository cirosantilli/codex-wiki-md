In a real [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), [weak convergence in a Hilbert space](../../../../../weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md) means

$$
\boxed{x_n\rightharpoonup x\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\langle x_n,z\rangle\longrightarrow\langle x,z\rangle\ \text{for every }z\in X.}
$$

By the [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md), every [continuous linear functional](../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) on $X$ has the form $u\mapsto\langle u,z\rangle$. Thus this is exactly [weak convergence](../../../../../weak-convergence.md), defined by convergence under every [continuous linear functional](../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md).
