# Tensile failure criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Brittle fracture](brittle-fracture.md)

In the simplest strength-cutoff model, opening begins when the least compressive [effective stress](effective-stress.md) reaches minus the [tensile strength](tensile-strength.md): $\sigma'_3=-T_0$ with compression positive. In [Mohr's circle](mohr-s-circle.md), this is contact with the vertical line $\sigma'_n=-T_0$ at $\tau=0$. The criterion represents an opening mechanism and differs from frictional [shear failure](shear-failure.md).
