# Thermal penetration depth

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal diffusion wave](thermal-diffusion-wave.md)

For the no-side-loss [thermal diffusion wave](thermal-diffusion-wave.md), the [thermal penetration depth](thermal-penetration-depth.md) $\delta=1/a=\sqrt{2\alpha/\omega}=\sqrt{\alpha P/\pi}$ is the distance over which the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) falls by $e^{-1}$ and the [wave phase](phase-waves.md) lags by one [radian](radian.md). It is a periodic counterpart of the [thermal diffusion length](thermal-diffusion-length.md), with its numerical factor fixed by this definition. A shorter period probes a shallower region; a longer period increases sensitivity to the far-end [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md).
