# Isometric evaluation embedding into continuous functions on a compact dual ball

↑ **Parent:** [Space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md)

For a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) $X$, take $K=B_{X^*}$ with the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md). [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) makes it compact Hausdorff, and each displayed evaluation function is continuous. Its supremum norm is $\|x\|$ by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md), giving a linear [isometric embedding](isometric-embedding.md) into $C(K)$.
