# Complex coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Complex plane](complex-plane.md)

Represent Cartesian coordinates $(x,y)$ in the plane by the [complex number](complex-number.md) $z=x+iy$, whose [real part](real-part.md) and [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) recover them. Multiplication by $e^{i\theta}$ rotates the coordinates through angle $\theta$, so a rotating-frame calculation becomes a complex exponential change of variable.
