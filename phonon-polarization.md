# Phonon polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

The displacement eigenvector of a lattice [normal mode](normal-mode.md), including relative amplitudes and phases for the atoms in a primitive cell. Longitudinal and transverse describe its orientation relative to the [phonon](phonon.md) wavevector in simple isotropic cases.
