# Softened isothermal lensing potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md)

This circular lensing potential has reduced deflection $\alpha(r)=\theta_Er/\sqrt{r^2+\theta_c^2}$, mean [lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md) $\bar\kappa=\theta_E/\sqrt{r^2+\theta_c^2}$ and local convergence $\kappa=\theta_E(\theta_c^2+r^2/2)/(r^2+\theta_c^2)^{3/2}$. Its central convergence is finite for $\theta_c>0$. As the core shrinks to zero, the deflection tends to that of a [singular isothermal sphere lens](singular-isothermal-sphere-lens.md). This softening of the projected lensing potential must not be confused with replacing a three-dimensional density by a different cored profile.
