# Polychromatic coloring of a hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A [polychromatic coloring of a hypergraph](polychromatic-coloring-of-a-hypergraph.md) assigns $k$ colors to vertices so that every hyperedge contains all $k$ colors. It is stronger than merely requiring each hyperedge to be nonmonochromatic. Independently random colors and the [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md) can construct such colorings when edges are sufficiently large relative to their overlap degrees.
