# Countable additivity

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)

For pairwise disjoint measurable sets $E_n$, countable additivity means

$$
\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}E_n\right)
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mu(E_n).
$$
