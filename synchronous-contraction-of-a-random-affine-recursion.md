# Synchronous contraction of a random affine recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Random affine recursion](random-affine-recursion.md)

Drive two copies of a [random affine recursion](random-affine-recursion.md) with the same coefficient pairs, independent of their initial [coupling](coupling.md). Then $X_n-Y_n=A_n(X_{n-1}-Y_{n-1})$. If $\rho=\mathbb E|A_1|<1$ and $\mathbb E|X_0-Y_0|<\infty$, [independence of random variables](independent-random-variables.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E|X_n-Y_n|=\rho^n\mathbb E|X_0-Y_0|\longrightarrow0.
$$

This proves [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md) and hence [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md) of the difference. If one copy starts in a [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md), [Slutsky's theorem](slutsky-theorem.md) shows that the other copy converges to that distribution. The argument compares paths before taking their one-time [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md); it does not require the paths ever to become exactly equal.
