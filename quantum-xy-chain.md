# Quantum XY chain

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)

A one-dimensional quantum [spin](spin.md) model with transverse [XY exchange interactions](xy-exchange-interaction.md); $J>0$ is the ferromagnetic sign in the displayed convention. Unlike the classical [XY model](xy-model.md), its site variables are noncommuting [spin operators](spin-operator.md) in a fixed [irreducible spin representation](irreducible-spin-representation.md). With [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) and $L\ge2$, $H=-\tfrac J2\sum_n(S_n^+S_{n+1}^-+S_n^-S_{n+1}^+)$. It preserves total $S^z$ and the associated global [U(1)](circle-group.md) [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md).
