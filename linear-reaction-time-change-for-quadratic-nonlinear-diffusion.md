# Linear-reaction time change for quadratic nonlinear diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional Barenblatt solution with diffusivity proportional to concentration](two-dimensional-barenblatt-solution-with-diffusivity-proportional-to-concentration.md)

The substitution

$$
C(x,t)=e^{at}G(x,\tau(t)),
\qquad
\tau(t)=
\begin{cases}
(e^{at}-1)/a,&a\ne0,\\
t,&a=0,
\end{cases}
$$

reduces $C_t=k\nabla\cdot(C\nabla C)+aC$ to  
$G_\tau=k\nabla\cdot(G\nabla G)$.
