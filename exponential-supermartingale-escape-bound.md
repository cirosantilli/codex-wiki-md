# Exponential-supermartingale escape bound

↑ **Parent:** [Optional sampling theorem for a supermartingale](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md)

If $e^{-\theta N_{t\wedge\sigma_K}}$ is a nonnegative [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), with $\sigma_K$ the first time $N_t\leq K$, the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) yields $\mathbb P_N(\sigma_K<\infty)\leq e^{-\theta(N-K)}$. Such a bound can prove escape of a backlog independently of heuristic [fluid models](fluid-model.md).
