# Leaf of a regular foliation

↑ **Parent:** [Foliation](foliation.md)

A leaf is a maximal connected [integral manifold](integral-manifold.md) of the smooth constant-rank [integrable distribution](integrable-distribution.md) defining a regular [foliation](foliation.md). It carries its intrinsic manifold topology and is immersed in the ambient manifold. It need not be embedded or closed. Local [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md) coordinates make each leaf segment a plaque with constant transverse coordinates; [global confinement to an immersed leaf](global-confinement-to-an-immersed-leaf.md) follows by joining such plaques along tangent curves.
