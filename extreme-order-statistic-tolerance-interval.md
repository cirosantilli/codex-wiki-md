# Extreme-order-statistic tolerance interval

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence interval](confidence-interval.md)

The sample minimum and maximum enclose random population content given by the [uniformized sample range](uniformized-sample-range.md). The exact failure probability for content at least $1-\varepsilon$ is $(1-\varepsilon)^{n-1}[1+(n-1)\varepsilon]$. For small $\varepsilon$, setting $n\varepsilon\approx\vartheta$ gives the displayed sample-size approximation at confidence $1-\delta$. The positive solution is unique for $0<\delta<1$, since $(1+\vartheta)e^{-\vartheta}$ decreases from one to zero. A tolerance interval concerns population content, rather than a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) for a fixed parameter; the exact inequality should select the final integer size.
