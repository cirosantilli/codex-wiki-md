# Healing length

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md)

The [healing length](healing-length.md) of a repulsive homogeneous condensate is the length at which density-gradient [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and interaction [energy](energy.md) balance. The displayed convention sets the dimensionless [Laplacian](laplacian.md) coefficient to one in the stationary [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md). A hard-wall [number density](number-density.md) profile has width $\sqrt2\ell_h$; definitions without the factor $\sqrt2$ occur, so the equation and convention must be specified.
