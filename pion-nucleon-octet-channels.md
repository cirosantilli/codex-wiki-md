# Pion-nucleon octet channels

↑ **Parent:** [Meson-baryon scattering](meson-baryon-scattering.md)

For an initial [pion](pion.md)-[nucleon](nucleon.md) state and outgoing [meson octet](meson-octet.md)-[baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) pair, conservation of [baryon number](baryon-number.md) and [strangeness](strangeness.md) allows $\pi N$, $\eta_8N$, $K\Lambda$, and $K\Sigma$, subject to [electric charge](electric-charge.md) and production thresholds. Total [isospin](isospin.md) is $1/2$ or $3/2$; the $\eta_8N$ and $K\Lambda$ channels contain only $1/2$.
