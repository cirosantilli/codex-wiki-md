# Quadratic resolvent field of a cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Galois group of an irreducible cubic](galois-group-of-an-irreducible-cubic.md)

If a separable cubic has [polynomial discriminant](polynomial-discriminant.md) $\Delta$, then adjoining $\sqrt\Delta$ gives the subfield fixed by the alternating part of its Galois group. When the cubic Galois group is $S_3$, this is its unique quadratic subfield.
