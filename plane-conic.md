# Plane conic

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

A degree-two [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md), allowing singular and nonreduced cases. In characteristic different from two, write its equation as $z^{\mathsf T}Mz$ with $M$ a symmetric [matrix](matrix.md). It is smooth exactly when $M$ has [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) three. Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), rank two gives two distinct [projective lines](projective-line.md), while rank one gives a double line.
