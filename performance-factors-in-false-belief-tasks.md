# Performance factors in false-belief tasks

↑ **Parent:** [False-belief task](false-belief-task.md)

[Language](language.md) interpretation, [memory](memory-in-animals.md), [inhibitory control](inhibitory-control.md), response generation and other demands affecting the observed answer. Lowering concurrent demands while preserving the mistaken-belief contrast can reveal successful performance earlier than a standard task. The modified procedure still needs controls against cueing and simplified nonmental strategies.[PNAS 113, 13360–13365; Experiments 1–4 and picture-book apparatus](two-and-a-half-year-olds-succeed-at-a-traditional-false-belief-task-with-reduced-processing-demands-by-peipei-setoh-and-others.md)
