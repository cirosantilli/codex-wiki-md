# Character generating series of symmetric powers

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric power](symmetric-power.md)

For [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_d$ of a finite-order linear action, its [symmetric power](symmetric-power.md) characters satisfy $\sum_{n\geq0}\chi_{S^nV}t^n=\prod_j(1-\lambda_jt)^{-1}$. The denominator is the alternating generating [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of its [exterior power](exterior-power.md) characters.
