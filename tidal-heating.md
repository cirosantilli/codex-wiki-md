# Tidal heating

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_heating)

Tidal heating converts mechanical energy of tidal deformation into internal heat through dissipation. Sustained eccentricity or obliquity can maintain the deformation and heat an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) interior.
