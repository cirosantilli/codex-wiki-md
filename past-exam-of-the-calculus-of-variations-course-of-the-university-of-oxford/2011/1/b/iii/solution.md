<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If an admissible $u$ attained the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md) zero, each nonnegative term of its integrand would vanish [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md). In particular $|u|^{2011}=0$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), so $u=0$ in its [Sobolev space](../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md) and its [weak derivative](../../../../../weak-derivative.md) is zero. But then the remaining term is $(-64)^2=4096$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), giving $I(u)=4096$, a contradiction. Thus **the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md) is zero but no global minimizer exists**. The oscillatory sequence has no convergence of derivatives compatible with substitution into this nonconvex energy.
