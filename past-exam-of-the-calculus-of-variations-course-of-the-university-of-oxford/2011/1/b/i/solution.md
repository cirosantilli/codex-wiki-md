↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [global minimizer](../../../../../global-minimum.md) is an admissible function $\bar u$ for which

$$
\boxed{I(\bar u)\leq I(v)\quad\text{for every }v\in\mathcal A.}
$$

Equivalently it attains the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md) over the whole admissible class. Its endpoint traces must equal the prescribed boundary values; comparing only with nearby functions would define a local rather than a global notion.
