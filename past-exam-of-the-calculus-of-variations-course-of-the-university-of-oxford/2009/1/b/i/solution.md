<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An admissible $\bar u$ is a [global minimizer](../../../../../global-minimizer.md) if

$$
\boxed{I(\bar u)\leq I(v)\quad\text{for every }v\in\mathcal A.}
$$

In particular it attains the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md); no closeness condition on competing admissible functions is imposed.
