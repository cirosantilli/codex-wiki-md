<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If an admissible $u$ attained the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md), nonnegativity and $I(u)=0$ would force $u^4=0$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), so its [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) Sobolev representative would be identically zero. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) would then vanish [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), but the other integrand term would equal $16^4>0$. This is impossible. Equivalently, zero energy would simultaneously require $u=0$ and $|u'|=2$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{The infimum zero is not attained}.}
$$
