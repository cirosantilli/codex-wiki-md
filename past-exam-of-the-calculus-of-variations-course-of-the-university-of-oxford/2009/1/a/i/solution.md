A [convex function](../../../../../convex-function.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{F((1-t)x+ty)\leq(1-t)F(x)+tF(y)\quad(x,y\in\mathbb R^n,\ 0\leq t\leq1).}
$$

Thus its value on every [line segment](../../../../../line-segment.md) lies below the chord joining the endpoint values.
