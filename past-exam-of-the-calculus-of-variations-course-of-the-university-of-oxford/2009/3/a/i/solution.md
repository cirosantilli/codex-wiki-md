A [weak local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../weak-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) is an admissible $\bar u$ for which some $\rho>0$ makes $I(v)\geq I(\bar u)$ whenever $v\in\mathcal A$ and $\|v-\bar u\|_{W^{1,\infty}}<\rho$. Both the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) and its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) are required to be close.
