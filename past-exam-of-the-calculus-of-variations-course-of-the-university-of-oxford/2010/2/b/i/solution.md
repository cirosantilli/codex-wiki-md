↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Here $P=2$, $Q=-2\lambda^2$ and the [Jacobi equation](../../../../../jacobi-equation.md) is $w''+\lambda^2w=0$. With $w(0)=0$ and $w'(0)=1$, its solution is $w(x)=\sin(|\lambda|x)/|\lambda|$ for $\lambda\ne0$, and $w(x)=x$ for $\lambda=0$. Its first positive zero is $\pi/|\lambda|$ when $\lambda\ne0$.

If $\lambda^2<\pi^2/b^2$, no [conjugate point](../../../../../conjugate-point.md) occurs in $(0,b]$. The [Jacobi positivity criterion](../../../../../jacobi-positivity-criterion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\delta^2I_\lambda(0)(\varphi,\varphi)=2\int_0^b(\varphi'^2-\lambda^2\varphi^2)dx>0\quad(\varphi\ne0).}
$$

Since $I_\lambda$ itself is this [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) divided by two, $I_\lambda(u)>0=I_\lambda(0)$ for every nonzero admissible $u$. Thus **zero is the unique [global minimizer](../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**. This argument establishes the result before using the sharp [Poincaré inequality](../../../../../poincare-inequality.md).
