<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use $u_A(x)=A\sin(\pi x/b)$, which is admissible for every real $A$. Direct integration gives

$$
I_\lambda(u_A)=\frac{A^2b}{2}\left(\frac{\pi^2}{b^2}-\lambda^2\right).
$$

The coefficient is strictly negative, so the energy tends to minus infinity as $|A|\to\infty$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\inf_{u\in\mathcal A}I_\lambda(u)=-\infty.}
$$
