<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A convenient form of the [Tonelli existence theorem](../../../../../tonelli-existence-theorem.md) is: on a finite interval with prescribed endpoint values, if a continuous integrand $F(x,z,v)$ is [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md) in $v$ and satisfies $F(x,z,v)\geq c|v|^p-C$ for some $p>1$, then, provided a finite-energy admissible competitor exists, its integral attains a minimum in the corresponding $W^{1,p}$ class. The lower bound may more generally subtract an integrable function of $x$. Compact one-dimensional [Sobolev embedding](../../../../../failure-of-first-order-sobolev-embedding-into-linfinity-in-two-dimensions.md) controls $z$, while [convexity](../../../../../convex-function.md) gives [weak lower semicontinuity](../../../../../weak-lower-semicontinuity.md) in the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md).

Here $p=4/3$ and $F_\varepsilon=(z^4-x)^2+\varepsilon|v|^{4/3}$ is continuous, [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md) in $v$, and bounded below by $\varepsilon|v|^{4/3}$. The competitor $u(x)=x$ has finite energy. Thus for every $\varepsilon>0$ the [Tonelli existence theorem](../../../../../tonelli-existence-theorem.md) gives an admissible minimizer in $W^{1,4/3}(0,1)$.

Any finite-energy member of the larger stated $W^{1,1}$ class necessarily has [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) in $L^{4/3}$ and bounded function values by [absolute continuity](../../../../../absolutely-continuous-function.md); it therefore belongs to $W^{1,4/3}$. All other competitors have infinite energy. Hence **the same function $u_\varepsilon$ is a [global minimizer](../../../../../global-minimizer.md) in the full stated class**.
