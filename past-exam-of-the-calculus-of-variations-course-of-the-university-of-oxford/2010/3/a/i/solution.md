<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function $u_*(x)=x^{1/4}$ has [weak derivative](../../../../../weak-derivative.md) $u_*'(x)=\tfrac14x^{-3/4}\in L^1(0,1)$ and the required endpoint values, so it belongs to the admissible [Sobolev space](../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md). Its fourth power is $x$, and hence

$$
\boxed{I_0(u_*)=0.}
$$

Since the integrand $(u^4-x)^2$ is nonnegative, zero is a global lower bound. Thus **$u_*$ is a [global minimizer](../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) does not belong to $L^{4/3}$, since its $4/3$ power is proportional to $x^{-1}$; positive regularization therefore needs different competitors.
