↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Zero is a strict strong local minimizer, and therefore also a strict weak local minimizer.** Here $I(0)=-2/2010$, and

$$
I(u)-I(0)=\int_0^1u'^2\,dx-\frac2{2010}\int_0^1(\cosh u-1)\,dx.
$$

For $|u|\leq1$, [Taylor's theorem](../../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) gives $\cosh u-1\leq\tfrac12\cosh(1)u^2$. Also $u(0)=0$ and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) give $\int_0^1u^2\,dx\leq\tfrac12\int_0^1u'^2\,dx$. Therefore

$$
I(u)-I(0)\geq\left(1-\frac{\cosh1}{4020}\right)\int_0^1u'^2\,dx.
$$

The coefficient is positive. Thus every nonzero admissible $u$ with $\|u\|_\infty\leq1$ has strictly greater energy, proving that zero is a strict [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md).
