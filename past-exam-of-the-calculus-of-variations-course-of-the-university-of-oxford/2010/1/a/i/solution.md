A [weak local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../weak-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) is an admissible $\bar u$ for which there is $\rho>0$ such that

$$
\boxed{I(v)\geq I(\bar u)\quad\text{whenever }v\in\mathcal A,\ \|v-\bar u\|_{W^{1,\infty}}<\rho.}
$$

The [Sobolev norm](../../../../../sobolev-norm.md) controls both the function and its first [weak derivative](../../../../../weak-derivative.md). The boundary values remain fixed because both functions are admissible.
