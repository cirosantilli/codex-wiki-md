# Hilbert class field of Q of square root minus six

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

The [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) of $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-6})$ has order two, and

$$
H_K=K(\sqrt2)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt{-3}).
$$

This quadratic extension of $K$ is unramified at every finite prime and therefore has the degree required of the [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md).
