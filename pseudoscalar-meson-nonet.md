# Pseudoscalar meson nonet

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md)

In the [quark model](quark-model.md), the light-flavour triplet and its antiquark conjugate give an octet and a singlet. A colour-singlet pair with orbital angular momentum zero and total spin zero has parity minus one, giving a pseudoscalar nonet. Its six outer weights are charged [pions](pion.md) and four [kaons](kaon.md). At the centre the octet contains the neutral pion and octet eta direction; the additional singlet direction gives a third state at the same weight.
