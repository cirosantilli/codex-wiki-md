<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [state function](../../../../state-function.md) is determined by the equilibrium state, rather than the path used to reach it. Its differential is exact and its change around a closed [thermodynamic cycle](../../../../thermodynamic-cycle.md) is zero. [Internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md), [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) and [enthalpy](../../../../enthalpy-split.md) are examples; transferred [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) and [work](../../../../work.md) are not [state functions](../../../../state-function.md).

For slow expansion in contact with a large [thermal reservoir](../../../../heat-bath.md), the fixed [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) of that reservoir maintains an [isothermal process](../../../../isothermal-process.md). For a fixed amount of [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) and [enthalpy](../../../../enthalpy-split.md) depend only on [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), so

$$
\boxed{T=\mathrm{constant},\qquad U=\mathrm{constant},\qquad H=\mathrm{constant}.}
$$

The [ideal gas law](../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) also gives $pV=\mathrm{constant}$. For a reversible expansion the absorbed [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) equals the [work](../../../../work.md) done by the gas, while its [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) increases by $n_{\rm mol}R\ln(V_2/V_1)$; constant [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) does not mean zero [heat transfer](../../../../heat-transfer.md).
