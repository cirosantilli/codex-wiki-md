<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For steady insulated flow through a porous plug, neglect changes of bulk [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and gravitational [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md), and allow no shaft [work](../../../../work.md). Each amount of gas brings flow [work](../../../../work.md) $p_1V_1$ upstream and delivers $p_2V_2$ downstream. The steady [energy conservation](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) balance is

$$
U_1+p_1V_1=U_2+p_2V_2,\qquad \boxed{H_1=H_2.}
$$

Thus [enthalpy](../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is conserved in this [Joule-Thomson effect](../../../../joule-thomson-effect.md). For an [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), $H=H(T)$, so $T_1=T_2$ and $U_1=U_2$ as well. The [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) increases:

$$
\boxed{\Delta S=n_{\rm mol}R\ln\frac{p_1}{p_2}>0.}
$$

The porous-plug process is irreversible despite being slow and insulated; it is not the reversible expansion of the preceding part.
