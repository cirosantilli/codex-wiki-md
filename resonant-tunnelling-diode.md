# Resonant tunnelling diode

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)

A biased double-barrier structure whose intermediate quasi-bound levels control transmission from reservoirs. Bias-dependent alignment can produce [negative differential resistance](negative-differential-resistance.md) when a resonance moves out of the available injection window.
