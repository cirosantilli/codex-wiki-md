# Sagitta momentum uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md)

For a short circular track of chord length $L$, [sagitta](sagitta.md) $s$ and fixed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $B$, $p=|q|BL^2/(8s)$. Differentiating this inverse relation gives $\sigma(p)/p=\sigma(s)/s$. A fixed spatial [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md) therefore makes the relative [momentum](momentum-split.md) uncertainty grow linearly with [momentum](momentum-split.md).
