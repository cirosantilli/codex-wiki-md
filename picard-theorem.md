# Picard theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Entire function](entire-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picard_theorem)

The Picard theorems concern omitted values of [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md). The [Little Picard theorem](little-picard-theorem.md) permits at most one omitted value for a nonconstant [entire function](entire-function.md); the [Great Picard theorem](great-picard-theorem.md) gives infinitely many occurrences of all but at most one value near an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md).
