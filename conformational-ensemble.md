# Conformational ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Protein structure](protein-structure.md)

A [conformational ensemble](conformational-ensemble.md) is a distribution over molecular conformations under specified conditions. Different measurements average different functions of that distribution; a single mean structure can conceal rare states important for binding or activity.
