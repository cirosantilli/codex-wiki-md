# Oxatitanacyclobutane

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic compound](organometallic-compound.md)

A four-membered [titanium](titanium.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md)–[carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) intermediate in [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) olefination. Fragmentation yields a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) double bond and a [titanium](titanium.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) product.
