# Partial dislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

A [dislocation](dislocation.md) whose [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) is not a full translation of the perfect crystal, so its glide can leave a planar [stacking fault](stacking-fault.md). Vector conservation and the actual slip plane constrain reactions and dissociation.
