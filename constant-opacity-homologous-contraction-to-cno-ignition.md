# Constant-opacity homologous contraction to CNO ignition

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar homology](stellar-homology.md)

For radiative, gas-pressure-supported homologous stars with constant [opacity](opacity.md), write $\rho=M b/(4\pi R^3)$, $P=GM^2p/(4\pi R^4)$ and $T=\mu GMp/(\mathcal R Rb)$. The [radiative diffusion in a star](radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) equation contains the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) coefficient $D=3\kappa_0\mathcal R^4L/(64\pi^2ac\mu^4G^4M^3)$. A common fixed profile fixes $D$, so $L\propto M^3$ and the [luminosity](luminosity.md) of a fixed-mass member is constant. [Gravitational energy generation in a homologously contracting ideal-gas star](gravitational-energy-generation-in-a-homologously-contracting-ideal-gas-star.md) gives $\dot R=-2ELR^2/(3GM^2)$ for a positive profile constant $E$. Thus $R^{-1}=R_0^{-1}+2ELt/(3GM^2)$. For [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) heating $\epsilon\propto\rho T^{16}$, the [stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) [luminosity](luminosity.md) scales as $M^{18}R^{-19}$. Equating this with $M^3$ gives $R_{\rm MS}\propto M^{15/19}$. Neglecting $R_0^{-1}$, the arrival time is $t_{\rm MS}\propto M^{-34/19}$, hence a coeval cluster's newly arriving [mass](mass.md) scales as $t^{-19/34}$. All comparisons hold [stellar composition](stellar-chemical-abundance.md), [opacity](opacity.md) normalization and [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) profiles fixed.
