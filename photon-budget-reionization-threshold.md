# Photon-budget reionization threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Reionization](reionization.md)

If each collapsed baryon supplies $N_\gamma$ effective ionizing photons and $N_{\rm req}$ photons per cosmic baryon are required, the threshold collapsed fraction is $f_{\rm coll}=N_{\rm req}/N_\gamma$. The [Press-Schechter formalism](press-schechter-formalism.md) gives $\operatorname{erfc}[\delta_{\rm sc}/(\sqrt2\sigma(M_{\min},z))]=f_{\rm coll}$. Escape losses and recombinations must either be incorporated in the effective yield/requirement or counted separately, not twice.
