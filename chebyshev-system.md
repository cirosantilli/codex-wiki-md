# Chebyshev system

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

A Chebyshev system on a nondegenerate real interval is a family of $n+1$ real [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) such that every [linear combination](linear-combination.md) with coefficients not all zero has at most $n$ distinct zeros. The condition entails [linear independence](linear-independence.md) and unique interpolation at any $n+1$ distinct points. It is a zero-counting hypothesis, not a requirement to count multiplicities.
