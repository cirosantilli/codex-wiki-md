# Rotatory power

↑ **Parent:** [Circular birefringence](circular-birefringence.md)

The [rotatory power](rotatory-power.md) is the rotation of transmitted [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) per unit path length, in $\mathrm{rad\,m^{-1}}$. With equally transmitted circular eigenmodes $(\mathbf e_1\mp i\mathbf e_2)/\sqrt2$ and time convention $e^{-i\omega t}$, their accumulated [phase difference](phase-difference.md) gives $\theta=(\phi_+-\phi_-)/2$. For uniform [circular birefringence](circular-birefringence.md), $\rho=\pi(n_+-n_-)/\lambda_0$. Generic linear [birefringence](birefringence.md) need not admit one rotation angle common to all incident polarization directions. In a weak longitudinal [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md), $\rho=VB_\parallel$, with signed [Verdet constant](verdet-constant.md) $V$.
