# Crystallographic point symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallography](crystallography.md)

A symmetry operation of a crystal with a fixed point, such as a rotation, reflection, inversion or rotoinversion. Its translational part can be removed by an origin choice. A nontrivial fractional screw advance or glide translation cannot be removed in that way.
