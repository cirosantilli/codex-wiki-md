# Artificial gravity in a rotating ring

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

At radius $a$ in a ring rotating at rate $\omega$, a walker with signed tangential [speed](speed.md) $u$ relative to the ring has inertial [speed](speed.md) $a\omega+u$. The normal reaction per [mass](mass.md) is $(a\omega+u)^2/a$; the [curvature](curvature.md) of the relative path matters as well as the [centrifugal force](centrifugal-force.md) and the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md).
