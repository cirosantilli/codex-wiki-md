# Parameter domain

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric equation](parametric-equation.md)

The [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) $U$ used by a [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) $\mathbf r$ is its parameter domain. A [parametrized surface](parametrized-surface.md) may require several such domains, and duplicated images must be accounted for in a [surface integral](surface-integral.md).
