<h1 id="mobius-scaling-generated-by-translations-and-reciprocal-inversion">Möbius scaling generated by translations and reciprocal inversion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

Let $T_b(z)=z+b$ and $I(z)=1/z$ on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). For $s\ne0$, direct composition gives the displayed identity. Every nonzero complex scaling $z\mapsto\lambda z$ is obtained by choosing $s^2=-\lambda$. Since translations, scalings and reciprocal inversion generate the [Möbius group](mobius-group.md), translations and reciprocal inversion alone also generate it.
