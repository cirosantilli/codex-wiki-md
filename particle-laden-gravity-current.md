# Particle-laden gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-current box model](gravity-current-box-model.md)

A particle-laden gravity current is driven by the excess density of suspended particles. Deposition decreases its [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md), so a finite-volume current can approach a finite runout length.
