# Pure-polarization preparation with two quarter-wave plates

↑ **Parent:** [Quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md)

Two rotatable ideal [quarter-wave plates](quarter-wave-plate.md) can transform a fixed [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) into any [pure polarization](pure-polarization.md) represented by a [Jones vector](jones-vector.md). Use time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, $Q(\theta)=R(-\theta)\operatorname{diag}(1,i)R(\theta)$ and input $J_L=(1,i)^T/\sqrt2$. Every target [polarization ellipse](polarization-ellipse.md) can be written, up to a common phase, as $R(-\beta)(\cos\eta,i\sin\eta)^T$, with signed ellipticity angle $-\pi/4\leq\eta\leq\pi/4$. The first [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) at $\theta_1=\beta+\eta+\pi/4$ produces [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) at $\beta+\eta$. The second, at $\theta_2=\beta$, therefore produces the required target. The two angles supply the two independent parameters of pure [polarization](polarization-waves.md).
