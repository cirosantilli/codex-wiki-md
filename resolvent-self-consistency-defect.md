# Resolvent self-consistency defect

↑ **Parent:** [Stieltjes matrix resolvent](stieltjes-matrix-resolvent.md)

For a zero-diagonal real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), put $q_i=x_i^T(X^{(i)}-zI)^{-1}x_i$ and $g_N=N^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(X-zI)^{-1}$. The [Schur complement formula for a diagonal resolvent entry](schur-complement-formula-for-a-diagonal-resolvent-entry.md) gives $g_N=-N^{-1}\sum_i(z+q_i)^{-1}$. Subtracting the comparison value $-(z+g_N)^{-1}$ gives $\varepsilon_N=N^{-1}\sum_i(q_i-g_N)/[(z+g_N)(z+q_i)]$. Upper-half-plane positivity and the [principal minor resolvent trace bound](principal-minor-resolvent-trace-bound.md) control this defect by the average of $|q_i-g_N^{(i)}|$ plus a trace correction.
