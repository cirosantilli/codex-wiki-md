# Normal series of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Subnormal series](subnormal-series.md)

A normal series is a chain $1=G_0\le G_1\le\cdots\le G_r=G$ whose terms are all [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md) of the ambient group. A [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md) only requires each term normal in the next. Confusing these conditions would incorrectly require every term of a composition series to be normal in the full group.
