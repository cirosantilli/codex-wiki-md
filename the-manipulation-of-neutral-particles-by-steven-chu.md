# The manipulation of neutral particles by Steven Chu

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Steven Chu](work-by-steven-chu.md)

By [Steven Chu](steven-chu.md).

Online text: [https://cdn.journals.aps.org/files/RevModPhys.70.685.pdf](https://cdn.journals.aps.org/files/RevModPhys.70.685.pdf)

Published version of a Nobel lecture, solely credited to [Steven Chu](steven-chu.md), in Reviews of Modern Physics 70, pp. 685–706, July 1998. Journal publication is distinct from the 8 December 1997 lecture delivery. The historical release-and-recapture account includes a retrospective correction for cloud geometry.

Cited passages: Steven Chu, The manipulation of neutral particles, 1998 publication of his Nobel lecture, pp. 687–690, Figs. 3–4 and footnote 5: sodium source, precooling, vacuum apparatus, beam geometry and fluorescence detection; Steven Chu, The manipulation of neutral particles, p. 688, release-and-recapture paragraph: measurement sequence and retrospective account of the geometry correction.
