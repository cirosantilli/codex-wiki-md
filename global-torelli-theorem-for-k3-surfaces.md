# Global Torelli theorem for K3 surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

An integral Hodge isometry between complex [K3 surfaces](k3-surface.md) which carries some [Kähler class](kahler-class.md) to a Kähler class is the pullback of a unique [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of the surfaces. A Hodge isometry without that cone condition need not itself be geometric: reflection in an effective $(-2)$-curve reverses its class and cannot be induced by an isomorphism.
