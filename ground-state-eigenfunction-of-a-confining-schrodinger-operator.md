<h1 id="ground-state-eigenfunction-of-a-confining-schrodinger-operator">Ground-state eigenfunction of a confining Schrödinger operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Confining-potential energy space](confining-potential-energy-space.md)

The minimum of

$$
\|\nabla u\|_2^2+\|V^{1/2}u\|_2^2
$$

subject to $\|u\|_2=1$ is attained because $\Sigma_V$ embeds compactly into $L^2$. Replacing a minimizer by its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) does not increase its energy. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) therefore produces a nonnegative eigenfunction of $-\Delta+V$ for its lowest eigenvalue.
