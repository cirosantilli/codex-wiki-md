# Shear-driven viscous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

A thin dense [gravity current](gravity-current.md) on a rigid no-slip substrate can be carried downstream by imposed upper [shear stress](shear-stress.md) $\tau$. For density contrast $\Delta\rho$, [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives $\mathbf q=Ah^2\mathbf e_x-Dh^3\nabla h$, where $A=\tau/(2\mu)$ and $D=\Delta\rho g/(3\mu)$. The [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives the displayed equation. Besides small slopes and reduced inertia, the hydrostatic reduction requires $\tau/(\Delta\rho gL)\ll1$ to keep shear-related normal stress small. Finite zero-thickness fronts are formal outer profiles and need a separate local description when their slopes are large.
