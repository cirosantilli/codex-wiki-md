# Support inclusion under rank-one dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-one dephasing](rank-one-dephasing.md)

If $p_i=\langle\psi_i|\rho|\psi_i\rangle=0$, positivity gives $\sqrt\rho|\psi_i\rangle=0$, so the corresponding basis vector is in $\ker\rho$. Therefore $\ker\Delta(\rho)\subseteq\ker\rho$, and $\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\Delta(\rho)$. This makes the [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md) of $\rho$ relative to its [rank-one dephasing](rank-one-dephasing.md) finite.
