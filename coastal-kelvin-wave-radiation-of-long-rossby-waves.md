# Coastal Kelvin-wave radiation of long Rossby waves

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin wave](kelvin-wave.md)

A slowly varying coastal [Kelvin wave](kelvin-wave.md) can force long [Rossby waves](rossby-wave.md) at the same absolute [frequency](frequency.md) and alongshore [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). In the regime $k^2+\ell^2\ll L_d^{-2}$, the offshore [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is westward with magnitude $\beta L_d^2$. With coastal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) magnitude $c$, matching gives $|k|=c|\ell|/(\beta L_d^2)$. The zonal long-wave condition therefore additionally requires $|\ell|\ll\beta L_d/c$; $|\ell|L_d\ll1$ alone is insufficient. This mechanism transmits coastal [thermocline](thermocline.md) anomalies into an ocean basin over a much slower timescale than coastal propagation.
