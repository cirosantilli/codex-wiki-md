# Multiplicity of an irreducible representation

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md)

In a completely reducible [group representation](group-representation.md) $V=\bigoplus_\lambda m_\lambda V_\lambda$, the nonnegative integer $m_\lambda$ counts the copies of the corresponding [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). For complex [representations](group-representation.md) of a finite [group](group-split.md), [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) gives $m_\lambda=\langle\chi_\lambda,\chi_V\rangle$.
