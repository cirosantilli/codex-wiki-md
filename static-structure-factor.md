# Static structure factor

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic structure factor](dynamic-structure-factor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Static_structure_factor)

The static structure factor is the equal-time spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of a [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md). Depending on convention it is obtained either by integrating the [dynamic structure factor](dynamic-structure-factor.md) over frequency or by taking an appropriate zero-frequency limit.
