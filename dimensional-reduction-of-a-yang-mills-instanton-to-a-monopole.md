# Dimensional reduction of a Yang-Mills instanton to a monopole

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

If a four-dimensional Euclidean connection is independent of one coordinate and that connection component is renamed $\Phi$, the self-dual Yang-Mills equation reduces to the three-dimensional [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md) equation $B_i=\pm D_i\Phi$.
