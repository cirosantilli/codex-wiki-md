# Power-set axiom at limit ranks

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md)

For a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) $\delta$, the structure $(V_\delta,\in)$ satisfies the [Axiom of power set](axiom-of-power-set.md). For $x\in V_\beta$ with $\beta<\delta$, transitivity gives $x\subseteq V_\beta$, so $\mathcal P(x)\subseteq V_{\beta+1}$ and $\mathcal P(x)\in V_{\beta+2}\subseteq V_\delta$. Every external subset is therefore present and the external [power set](power-set.md) is also the internal one.
