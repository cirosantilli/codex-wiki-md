# Heralded remote preparation of an arbitrary qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Remote state preparation](remote-state-preparation.md)

For a target [pure state](pure-state.md) $|\psi\rangle$ of a [qubit](qubit.md), choose its orthogonal complement so that

$$
|\Psi^-\rangle=\frac{|\psi\rangle|\psi^\perp\rangle-|\psi^\perp\rangle|\psi\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Measuring the sender's half of this [singlet state](singlet-state.md) in that basis leaves the receiver in $|\psi\rangle$ when the sender obtains $|\psi^\perp\rangle$. The outcome has [probability](probability.md) one half. One classical success flag heralds the exact preparation without conveying the target's classical description.
