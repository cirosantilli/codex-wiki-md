# Restriction of range

↑ **Parent:** [Selection bias](selection-bias.md)

Limiting the observed variation in a variable through selection, such as studying only a narrow stratum of senior executives. It can change measured [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) and limits the settings to which an observed relationship can generalize. Its effect is not universally a simple attenuation factor.
