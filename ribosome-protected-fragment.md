# Ribosome-protected fragment

↑ **Parent:** [Ribosome profiling](ribosome-profiling.md)

An [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) fragment protected from digestion by a [ribosome](ribosome.md) in the assayed preparation. Length, genomic position and frame periodicity help distinguish productive [biological translation](translation-biology.md) from other protected [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md).
