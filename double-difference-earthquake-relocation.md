# Double-difference earthquake relocation

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake location by arrival times](earthquake-location-by-arrival-times.md)

Differences between arrival times of nearby [earthquakes](earthquake.md) at a common station largely cancel common propagation errors. Comparing observed and predicted differences constrains relative source separation and origin-time differences. Closely similar waveforms permit precise differential picks by [cross-correlation](cross-correlation.md). Absolute position still needs an accurate model or external anchor.
