# Vertex centroid

↑ **Parent:** [Centroid](centroid.md)

The arithmetic mean of a finite nonempty collection of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), treating each vertex as an equal point mass. For a [polygon](polygon.md), this need not equal the uniform-area [centroid](centroid.md) of its interior. A [translation](translation-geometry.md) or [linear map](linear-map.md) commutes with this average; consequently rotating all vertices about their vertex centroid leaves that centroid fixed.
