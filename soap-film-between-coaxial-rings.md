# Soap film between coaxial rings

↑ **Parent:** [Soap film pressure balance](soap-film-pressure-balance.md)

Two equal circular rings of [radius](radius.md) $R$ in parallel [planes](plane.md) at $z=\pm L$ provide a boundary for an open film. In the ideal axisymmetric model, a stationary [connected](connected-space.md) film has the [catenoid](catenoid.md) profile

$$
r(z)=a\cosh(z/a),\qquad R=a\cosh(L/a).
$$

The [catenoid existence threshold for equal rings](catenoid-existence-threshold-for-equal-rings.md) explains why this equation can have two solutions, one solution, or none. The cancellation of [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) holds for both solutions whenever they exist; the [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) equation alone does not select a [stable minimal surface](stable-minimal-surface.md).

**[Figure 4](#image-a-catenoid-spanning-equal-coaxial-rings-and-two-stationary-profiles-at-the-same-separation). A catenoid spanning equal coaxial rings and two stationary profiles at the same separation**.

This ideal geometry uses $R=1$ and $L/R=0.60$. The two profiles have different waist [radii](radius.md) $a$, although both have $H=0$. For either profile the [surface area](surface-area.md) follows by [integration](integral.md):

$$
A_{\mathrm{cat}}=2\pi a^2\left(U+\sinh U\cosh U\right),\qquad U=L/a.
$$

At this illustrated separation both profiles have greater [surface area](surface-area.md) than the two disconnected disks, whose total is $2\pi R^2$. This comparison disproves a claim of global area minimization in a class allowing disconnected competitors; it does not determine the sign of the [second variation](second-variation.md) for small variations of a [connected](connected-space.md) film.

A reported experiment used coaxial wire rings of [radius](radius.md) $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, drawn from a [surfactant](surfactant.md) solution and slowly separated until collapse. The solution contained [tetradecyltrimethylammonium bromide](tetradecyltrimethylammonium-bromide.md) in deionized [water](water.md), with [glycerol](glycerol.md) added to vary [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md). [Fluorescein](fluorescein.md) enabled [fluorescence](fluorescence.md) imaging under cyan LED arrays; a Phantom V641 camera recorded 4,000 or 5,600 frames per second. Extracted contours gave neck [radius](radius.md), attachment [angle](angle.md), and inferred [surface area](surface-area.md). They showed neck narrowing and eventual breakup, rather than indefinite persistence of a stationary [catenoid](catenoid.md). Comparing solutions across [viscosities](dynamic-viscosity.md) helped distinguish liquid dissipation from the surrounding air's [inertia](inertia.md), but the ring displacement beyond the last stable state was not precisely controlled. These are collapse observations, not direct measurements of equilibrium $H$. [Geometry of Catenoidal Soap Film Collapse Induced by Boundary Deformation, §II, manuscript pp. 3–4, Figs. 1–3: apparatus, contour readouts, viscosity comparison, and displacement limitation](geometry-of-catenoidal-soap-film-collapse-induced-by-boundary-deformation-by-raymond-e-goldstein-and-others.md)

A proposed classroom measurement would instead hold two equal rings coaxial on a translation stage, leave both sides open to the same [gas](gas.md), and photograph the settled silhouette beside a scale. Fit $r(z)=a\cosh(z/a)$ and inspect fit residuals before inferring cancellation of the [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md). Repeat at several separations and with the camera rotated about the axis to test [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md). Keep solution composition, ring wetting, and elapsed drainage time comparable, and shield the film from drafts. Film weight, drainage, evaporation, [surfactant](surfactant.md) gradients, and rupture can invalidate the ideal [soap film pressure balance](soap-film-pressure-balance.md). A silhouette fit alone neither measures the [pressure](pressure.md) jump nor tests all possible perturbations of a [stable minimal surface](stable-minimal-surface.md).
