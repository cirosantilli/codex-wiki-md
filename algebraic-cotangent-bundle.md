# Algebraic cotangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md)

For a smooth [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) of dimension $r$ over a field, the [sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md) is locally free of rank $r$ and defines its algebraic cotangent bundle. Its dual is the [tangent sheaf of a scheme](tangent-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). For a smooth hypersurface of degree $d$ in $\mathbb P^n$, the [Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials](conormal-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md) is the short exact sequence $0\to\mathcal O_X(-d)\to\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^n}|_X\to\Omega^1_X\to0$.
