# Autonoetic consciousness

↑ **Parent:** [Consciousness](consciousness.md)

Awareness of oneself as the subject of an event located in personal subjective time. In [Endel Tulving](endel-tulving.md)'s account it characterizes [episodic memory](episodic-memory.md), as distinct from knowing facts without reliving their acquisition. An operational test of event information is not automatically a test of this phenomenology.[1985, definition of autonoetic awareness and memory-system distinctions](memory-and-consciousness-by-endel-tulving.md)
