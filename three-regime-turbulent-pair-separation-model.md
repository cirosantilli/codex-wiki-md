# Three-regime turbulent pair-separation model

↑ **Parent:** [Richardson pair dispersion](richardson-pair-dispersion.md)

For positive initial separation below the [Kolmogorov length scale](kolmogorov-length-scale.md), a phenomenological pair-dispersion model has smooth-flow exponential stretching, inertial-range [Richardson pair dispersion](richardson-pair-dispersion.md), and [diffusive large-scale pair dispersion](diffusive-large-scale-pair-dispersion.md) beyond the [integral scale of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md). A positive [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md) is needed for the first regime, scale locality and initial-memory loss for the second, and integrable relative-velocity [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) for the third. The inertial-range crossing time is of order $L^{2/3}\epsilon^{-1/3}$, one outer [eddy turnover time](eddy-turnover-time.md).
