# Delft electron-spin Bell test

↑ **Parent:** [Bell test](bell-test.md)

The [Bell test](bell-test.md) reported by [Bas Hensen](bas-hensen.md) and collaborators used electron spins separated by $1.3\,\mathrm{km}$, efficient spin readout and rapid random measurement settings. Those features addressed detection and locality loopholes together. Its result tests [Bell local causality](bell-local-causality.md) with the stated experimental assumptions, rather than ruling out every possible hidden-variable theory.[2015 abstract: spin separation, efficient readout and basis-choice timing](experimental-loophole-free-violation-of-a-bell-inequality-by-bas-hensen-and-others.md)
