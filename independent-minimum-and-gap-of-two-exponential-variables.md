# Independent minimum and gap of two exponential variables

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

For two independent rate-$\lambda>0$ [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md), set $U=\min(X,Y)$ and $V=\max(X,Y)$. Summing the two ordering branches gives [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) $2\lambda^2e^{-\lambda(2u+d)}$ for $(U,d=V-U)$ with $u,d>0$. It factors into independent exponential [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md). Thus $V$ is distributed as $X+Y/2$, with [expected value](expected-value.md) $3/(2\lambda)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $5/(4\lambda^2)$. This spacing result is the order-statistic manifestation of the [memorylessness of the exponential distribution](memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md).
