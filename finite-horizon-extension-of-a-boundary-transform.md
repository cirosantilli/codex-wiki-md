# Finite-horizon extension of a boundary transform

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-time spectral boundary transform](finite-time-spectral-boundary-transform.md)

In a [Fokas method](fokas-method.md) boundary [integral](integral.md) over $\partial D_+$, where $\operatorname{Re}\omega<0$ inside, the contribution of replacing $G(k,t)$ by $G(k,T)$ is an [integral](integral.md) of $e^{ikx}\int_t^T e^{\omega(k)(s-t)}g(s)\,ds$ times an [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) coefficient. For $x>0$ it vanishes by the [Cauchy integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) and the exponential decay in the upper domain. The transformed amplitude is then independent of the observation time, which simplifies differentiation of the [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md). Temporal inversion at $0<t<T$ recovers the full boundary value rather than the half-value of a truncated transform at its endpoint.
