# Shortest distance between skew lines

↑ **Parent:** [Skew lines](skew-lines.md)

For lines $a_1+t m_1$ and $a_2+u m_2$ with nonparallel direction vectors, their shortest separation is the absolute [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of $a_2-a_1$ onto their common normal $m_1\times m_2$. Components in the span of $m_1,m_2$ can be cancelled by choosing $t,u$, while the normal component cannot. The same formula gives zero for intersecting nonparallel lines. Parallel lines instead have separation $|(a_2-a_1)\times m_1|/|m_1|$.
