# Electronic and lattice heat-capacity crossover in a monovalent metal

↑ **Parent:** [Debye heat capacity](debye-heat-capacity.md)

For a monovalent three-dimensional [free electron gas](free-electron-model.md), the spin-summed molar [density of states](density-of-states.md) is $g(E_F)=3N_A/(2E_F)$. Combining the shell estimate $C_{el}=3k_B^2g(E_F)T$ with $C_{la}=(12\pi^4/5)R(T/\theta_D)^3$ gives the displayed nonzero crossover. The controlled [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md) replaces $3$ by $\pi^2/3$ and multiplies $T_*^2$ by $\pi^2/9$. A [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) alone does not determine the DOS in an unspecified band structure.
