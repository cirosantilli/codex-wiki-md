# External inductance of a thin two-wire line

↑ **Parent:** [Self-inductance](self-inductance.md)

Two long round wires of radius $a_w\ll d$ carrying opposite [electric currents](electric-current.md) form a loop with the displayed leading external [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) per unit length. Integrating their summed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) across a connecting ribbon gives the logarithm; magnetic-energy integration gives the same leading value. The zero-radius limit diverges. Uniform direct current inside two nonmagnetic round wires contributes another $\mu_0/(4\pi)$, while an ideal surface current has no interior contribution.
