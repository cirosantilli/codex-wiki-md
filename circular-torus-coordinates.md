# Circular torus coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md)

For a [ring torus](ring-torus.md) of major radius $A>0$, [circular torus coordinates](circular-torus-coordinates.md) use distance $r$ and angle $\alpha$ in the circular cross-section, and azimuth $\beta$ around the central axis. The coordinate [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) are the three partial [derivatives](derivative.md) of the displayed position. They are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md), with [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) $1,r,A+r\sin\alpha$. For $0<r<A$ their ordered [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $r(A+r\sin\alpha)>0$, so $dV=r(A+r\sin\alpha)\,dr\,d\alpha\,d\beta$. At $r=0$ the cross-section angle degenerates. These are tubular coordinates for a circular torus, rather than the confocal coordinate convention also called toroidal coordinates.
