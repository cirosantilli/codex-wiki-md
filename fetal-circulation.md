# Fetal circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Blood circulation](blood-circulation.md)

The fetal vascular arrangement, with placental [gas exchange](gas-exchange.md) and shunts including the ductus venosus, foramen ovale and ductus arteriosus. It differs from established postnatal pulmonary circulation.
