# Crossed-channel lightest-breather exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon breather fusion amplitude](sine-gordon-breather-fusion-amplitude.md)

At the closest physical-strip [pole](pole.md) of $S_{2,1}$, the difference of the analytically continued external momenta has squared [mass](mass.md) $m_1^2$. Using $m_2=2m_1\cos a$ makes the identity immediate. Thus the exchanged [t-channel](scattering-t-channel.md) one-particle state is the lightest [Sine-Gordon breather](sine-gordon-breather.md), not a new member of the spectrum.
