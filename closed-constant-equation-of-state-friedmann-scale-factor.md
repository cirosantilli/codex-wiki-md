# Closed constant-equation-of-state Friedmann scale factor

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For a closed [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) normalized to $kc^2=1$, constant equation-of-state parameter $w$, and [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $\tau$, let $q=(1+3w)/2$. The [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) and [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) give $\mathcal H'=-q(1+\mathcal H^2)$, where $\mathcal H=a'/a$. For $q\ne0$ this integrates to the stated scale factor on any interval where the sine is positive, with $\alpha^{2q}=8\pi G\rho_0/3$. For $w=-1/3$ the separate solution is $a=Ae^{H_0\tau}$ with $H_0^2+1=8\pi G\rho_0/3$; the sine-power formula is inapplicable.
