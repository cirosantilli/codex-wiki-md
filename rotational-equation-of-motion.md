# Rotational equation of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

The net external [torque](torque.md) about an inertial origin equals the time derivative of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). For rotation about a fixed axis with constant [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md), its axial component reduces to $I\dot\omega=\tau$.
