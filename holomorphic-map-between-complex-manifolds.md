# Holomorphic map between complex manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A map between [complex manifolds](complex-manifold.md) is holomorphic when its local coordinate expression has holomorphic scalar components. For a smooth map, this is equivalent to its differential being complex-linear at every point. These maps compose by the chain rule. For one-dimensional manifolds this reduces to a [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) between [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md); the general definition also applies to multiplication and inversion on a [complex Lie group](complex-lie-group.md).
