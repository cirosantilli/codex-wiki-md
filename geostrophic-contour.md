# Geostrophic contour

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor–Proudman theorem](taylor-proudman-theorem.md)

In nearly columnar flow between rigid boundaries, a geostrophic contour is a contour of column height divided by the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md). If relative [vorticity](vorticity.md) is negligible, conservation of [shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md) constrains leading [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) to these contours. Motion across them stretches or squashes columns, generating relative [vorticity](vorticity.md); for constant $f$, the contours are simply contours of $H$.
