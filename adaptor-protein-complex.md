# Adaptor protein complex

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)

An [adaptor protein complex](adaptor-protein-complex.md) couples membrane identity and cargo recognition to coat assembly. Coincident recognition of a membrane determinant, a cargo motif and coat partners helps restrict productive assembly to the appropriate site.
