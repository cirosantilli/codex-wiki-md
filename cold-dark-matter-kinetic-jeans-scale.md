# Cold-dark-matter kinetic Jeans scale

↑ **Parent:** [Collisionless Jeans length](collisionless-jeans-length.md)

An instantaneous kinetic suppression estimate replaces [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) by the [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) of [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md). Nonrelativistic particles in thermal contact with [photons](photon.md) have $\sigma\propto a^{-1/2}$; after [kinetic decoupling](kinetic-decoupling.md), their momenta redshift and $\sigma\propto a^{-1}$. A background dynamical estimate using total density is then constant during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) and decreases as $a^{-1/2}$ during [matter domination](matter-domination.md). Using only the species' own matter density instead makes the self-gravitating [collisionless Jeans length](collisionless-jeans-length.md) decrease as $a^{-1/2}$ throughout the decoupled nonrelativistic era. Neither instantaneous estimate is the accumulated distance traveled by [collisionless free streaming](free-streaming.md).
