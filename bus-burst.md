# Bus burst

↑ **Parent:** [Bus transaction](bus-transaction.md)

A group of related payload transfers, often to consecutive addresses, arranged under one transfer sequence. A [bus burst](bus-burst.md) can amortize setup and [bus arbitration](bus-arbitration.md) overhead; a longer period of exclusive [bus ownership](bus-ownership.md) can also make another initiator wait longer. The exact overhead and permitted handover boundaries depend on the protocol.[AMBA Specification, revision 2.0, section 1.4 and section 3.11.4: burst operation and early burst termination](amba-specification-rev-2-0-by-arm-limited.md)
