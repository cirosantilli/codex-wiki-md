# Associated graded ring of a regular local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md)

For a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md) of [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) $e$, a minimal maximal-[ideal](ideal.md) generating set induces the displayed polynomial-algebra isomorphism. It is surjective in every degree. A nonzero homogeneous kernel relation would bound the cumulative Hilbert function by $O(t^{e-1})$, contradicting the [prime-chain lower bound for local length](prime-chain-lower-bound-for-local-length.md) at $\dim R=e$. The [graded ring](graded-ring.md) is a domain; the [Krull intersection theorem](krull-intersection-theorem.md) then shows the original ring is a domain by multiplying nonzero initial forms.
