# Solid harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_harmonic)

A [solid harmonic](solid-harmonic.md) extends a [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) to a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in space with either the regular radial power $r^n$ or the irregular radial power $r^{-n-1}$. In three dimensions these are the two separated radial solutions of the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) at angular degree $n$.
