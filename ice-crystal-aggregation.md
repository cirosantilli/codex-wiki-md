# Ice-crystal aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric precipitation](atmospheric-precipitation.md)

Ice particles collide and stick to form a larger aggregate, such as a snowflake. Temperature, habit and collision speed affect sticking. This differs from vapour deposition onto ice and from [riming](riming.md), in which collected supercooled liquid freezes on the ice particle.
