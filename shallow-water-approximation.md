# Shallow-water approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

The shallow-water approximation applies when a fluid layer's vertical scale is much smaller than its horizontal scale. Vertical acceleration is then negligible to leading order, pressure is [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md), and the horizontal velocity can be represented by a depth average.
