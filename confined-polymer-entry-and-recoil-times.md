# Confined-polymer entry and recoil times

↑ **Parent:** [Insertion barrier of a charged polymer](insertion-barrier-of-a-charged-polymer.md)

With friction $\gamma(x)=\zeta_\ell x$, inward [force](force-split.md) $qEx$ gives constant entry [speed](speed.md) after capture. With the field off, constant outward [entropic force](entropic-force.md) gives $x\dot x=-f/\zeta_\ell$ and a quadratic recoil time. Exterior drag, adsorption and the short final end region are omitted. These ideal transport times alone do not establish steady size separation through a whole uniform channel.
