# Hyperedge

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A hyperedge is a member of the edge family of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md). In an $r$-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) it is an $r$-element subset of the [vertex set](vertex-set.md). For $r=2$ it is an ordinary [edge of a graph](edge-of-a-graph.md). A simple [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) has no repeated hyperedges; a [multihypergraph](multihypergraph.md) records multiplicities.
