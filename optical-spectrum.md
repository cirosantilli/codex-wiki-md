# Optical spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

An optical spectrum describes the distribution of optical radiation over [wavelength](wavelength.md) or [frequency](frequency.md). A [spectrograph](spectrograph.md) records it by separating wavelengths, while a [photometric passband](photometric-passband.md) integrates a chosen part.
