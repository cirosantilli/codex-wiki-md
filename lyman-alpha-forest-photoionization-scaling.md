# Lyman-alpha forest photoionization scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md)

In optically thin, almost fully ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) in [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md), $n_{\rm HI}\Gamma=n_en_p\alpha(T)$ and $n_e\simeq n_p\simeq n_H$. Thus the neutral fraction is approximately $\alpha(T)n_H/\Gamma$, while the neutral density is proportional to $n_H^2$. At fixed overdensity, cosmic dilution gives $n_H\propto(1+z)^3$, hence $n_{\rm HI}\propto\alpha(T)(1+z)^6/\Gamma$. Gas density, temperature, ionizing radiation and path length are all required to infer a baryon density from [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md).
