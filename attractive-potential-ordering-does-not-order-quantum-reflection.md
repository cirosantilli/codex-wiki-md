# Attractive potential ordering does not order quantum reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

For a square well of width $L$, exterior [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ and interior [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q>k$, matching the [wavefunction](wave-function.md) and its [derivative](derivative.md) gives the [reflection probability](quantum-reflection-probability.md) $R=C\sin^2(qL)/(1+C\sin^2(qL))$, where $C=(q^2-k^2)^2/(4k^2q^2)$. For example $q=3k$ and $kL=\pi/2$ give $R=16/25$, whereas zero potential gives $R=0$. Moreover deeper wells at $qL\in\pi\mathbb Z$ have zero reflection by [resonant transmission through a square well](resonant-transmission-through-a-square-well.md). Reflection therefore cannot be ordered solely by pointwise ordering of attractive potentials.
