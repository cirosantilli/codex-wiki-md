# Lambda Calculus and Types examination paper of the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) Finals paper dated Thursday 4 June 2015, 2:30 pm–4:00 pm, Trinity Term 2015. The released [examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains the three-page original as `2015-lambda.pdf`. Questions 1–2 occupy printed p. 2 and Question 3 printed p. 3. The local sources in the Oxford examinations repository are `pdf/computer-science/finals/lambda-calculus-and-types/2015.pdf` and `tex/computer-science/finals/lambda-calculus-and-types/2015.tex`. Question 2(d)'s literal claim is false: its proposed [typing judgment](typing-judgment.md) holds precisely when $B=A\Rightarrow C$ for some [simple type](simple-type.md) $C$.
