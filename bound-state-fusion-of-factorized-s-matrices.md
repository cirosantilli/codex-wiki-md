# Bound-state fusion of factorized S-matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md)

For an equal-mass bound pair $C=(AA)$ at fusion [rapidity](rapidity.md) $iu$, scatter a third particle off its two constituents with shifted [rapidities](rapidity.md) and project onto the bound-state residue. Scalar amplitudes give the displayed product. With internal indices, bound-state coupling tensors perform the projection. The [Yang-Baxter equation](yang-baxter-equation.md) makes different scattering orders consistent; factorization eliminates independent many-body scattering contributions.
