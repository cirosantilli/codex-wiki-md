# Dwell time (quantum mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

For a stationary one-dimensional [scattering state](scattering-state.md) with incident [probability current](probability-current.md) $j_{\rm in}>0$, the dwell time in a region $\Omega$ is its integrated [probability density](probability-density.md) divided by incident flux. It measures occupancy per incoming rate, not a classical trajectory or a uniquely defined tunnelling traversal time.
