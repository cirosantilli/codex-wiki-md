# Cosine integral

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric integral](trigonometric-integral.md)

For real $x>0$, its small-argument expansion is $\operatorname{Ci}(x)=\gamma_E+\log x+O(x^2)$. Integrating $\operatorname{Ci}'(x)=\cos x/x$ separates the logarithm from the regular correction. Its conventional constant is the [Euler--Mascheroni constant](euler-s-constant.md); the primary series normalization is [https://dlmf.nist.gov/6.6.E6](https://dlmf.nist.gov/6.6.E6) .
