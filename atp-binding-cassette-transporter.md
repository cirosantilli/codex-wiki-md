# ATP-binding cassette transporter

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

An [ATP-binding cassette transporter](atp-binding-cassette-transporter.md) couples nucleotide-binding-domain activity to transmembrane transport. For an exporter, productive substrate movement must be measured separately from substrate binding and [ATP hydrolysis](atp-hydrolysis.md).
