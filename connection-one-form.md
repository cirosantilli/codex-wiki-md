# Connection one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

In a local frame $e$, a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) is written $\nabla e=Ae$, where $A$ is a matrix-valued one-form. Under a change of frame $e'=eg$, it transforms as $A'=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg$.
