# Evaluation homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md)

For an element $a$ of a commutative $R$-algebra, evaluation on a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) is the ring homomorphism

$$
R[X]\longrightarrow R[a],\qquad f\longmapsto f(a).
$$
