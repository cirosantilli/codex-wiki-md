# Amplitude of an oscillatory integral

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md)

An oscillatory integral amplitude multiplies $e^{i\Phi}$ in an [oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md) with [phase function](phase-function.md) $\Phi$. A finite [symbol class](symbol-class.md) order controls its frequency growth and the [order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md) obtained by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md).
