# Two-photon vertex of a scalar field-strength interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Ward identity](ward-identity.md)

The interaction $\lambda\Phi F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}/4$ gives the displayed on-shell [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md), up to an overall phase, for a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) decaying to two [photons](photon.md). Substituting either [photon](photon.md) [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) for its [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md) makes the expression vanish, verifying its [Ward identity](ward-identity.md). In the parent [rest frame](rest-frame.md), summing the two physical transverse [photon polarizations](photon-polarization.md) gives $\sum|\mathcal A|^2=|\lambda|^2M^4/2$. Including the [identical-particle factor in a final-state phase-space integral](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) gives $\Gamma=|\lambda|^2M^3/(64\pi)$.
