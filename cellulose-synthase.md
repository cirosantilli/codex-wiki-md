# Cellulose synthase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[cellulose synthase](cellulose-synthase.md) polymerizes [glucose](glucose.md) into [cellulose](cellulose.md) using an activated donor. Membrane-associated systems can couple chain elongation to extrusion through a membrane channel.
