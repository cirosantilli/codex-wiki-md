# Ordovician

↑ **Parent:** [Geological time scale](geological-time-scale.md)

A geological period preceding the [Silurian](silurian.md). Its late interval includes a major [mass extinction](mass-extinction.md) associated with glaciation, changes in [eustatic sea level change](eustatic-sea-level-change.md) and later marine oxygen stress.
