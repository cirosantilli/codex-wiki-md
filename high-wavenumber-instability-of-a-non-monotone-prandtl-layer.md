# High-wavenumber instability of a non-monotone Prandtl layer

↑ **Parent:** [Prandtl critical layer at a stationary shear profile](prandtl-critical-layer-at-a-stationary-shear-profile.md)

A [stationary point](stationary-point.md) in a non-monotone [velocity profile](velocity-profile.md) can support a [quarter-power critical layer](prandtl-critical-layer-at-a-stationary-shear-profile.md) whose phase-speed correction has positive imaginary part. The resulting [normal mode](normal-mode.md) grows at a rate proportional to the square root of the [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). Such unbounded high-frequency amplification demonstrates [linear instability](linear-instability.md) of the frozen layer; nonlinear evolution and precise well-posedness consequences require separate analysis.
