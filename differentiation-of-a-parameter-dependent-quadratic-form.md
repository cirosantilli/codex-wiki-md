# Differentiation of a parameter-dependent quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md)

Apply the [product rule](product-rule.md) to the scalar [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) and transpose its last scalar term: $x^TA\,dx=(dx)^TA^Tx$. Only the symmetric part of $A$ contributes to its value or its derivative with respect to $x$. A varying matrix also contributes the independent term $x^T(dA)x$. No assumption that $A$ is symmetric is needed. Repeating this rule and the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) computes mixed derivatives when vector and matrix arguments depend on separate parameters.
