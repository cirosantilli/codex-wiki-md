# Random copolymer

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical copolymer](statistical-copolymer.md)

A [copolymer](copolymer.md) with an ideal sequence in which the probability of a monomeric unit is independent of neighbouring units. A real copolymer made by concurrent radical reactions can have sequence correlations; a feed ratio does not by itself establish incorporated composition or independent-unit statistics.
