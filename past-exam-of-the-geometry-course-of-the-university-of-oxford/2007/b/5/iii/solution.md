<h1 id="b/5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $P_5=[u:v:w]$ in the same normalized coordinates. Its coordinates are all nonzero, because the coordinate lines join pairs of the first three points. Evaluation at $P_5$ gives the additional linear equation $avw+bwu+cuv=0$ on the two-dimensional coefficient plane $a+b+c=0$. It is not identically zero there: that would imply $vw=wu=uv$, hence $u=v=w$ and $P_5=P_4$, contradicting the hypothesis. Its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is therefore one-dimensional. Thus **there is exactly one [projective conic](../../../../../projective-conic.md) through all five points**. If it were reducible, $P_5$ would lie on a line joining two of the other points, contrary to no three being collinear. Hence **the unique [conic](../../../../../conic-section.md) is irreducible**.
