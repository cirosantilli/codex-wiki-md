<h1 id="b/8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Compactness makes the continuous function $|f|$ attain a maximum. Apply the preceding argument in a chart at a maximizing point to make $f$ constant on a neighborhood. The permitted [identity theorem](../../../../../identity-theorem.md) and connectedness then make it constant on all of $S$. Hence **every [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) from a compact connected [Riemann surface](../../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md) to $\mathbb C$ is constant**.
