<h1 id="b/8/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Apply Riemann-Roch at $D=K$ and use $\ell(K)=g$ and $\ell(0)=1$:

$$
g-1=\deg K+1-g.
$$

Rearranging gives **$\deg K=2g-2$**. This holds for every [canonical divisor](../../../../../canonical-divisor.md), since any two differ by a [principal divisor](../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) of degree zero.
