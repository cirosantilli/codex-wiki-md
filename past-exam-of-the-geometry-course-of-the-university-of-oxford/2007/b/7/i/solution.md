<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With local indices $e_p$, the required specialization is

$$
\boxed{2g(C)-2=-2d+\sum_{p\in C}(e_p-1).}
$$

Only the finitely many points of index greater than one contribute. Their images are the [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md) on the target.
