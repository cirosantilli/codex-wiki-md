<h1 id="b/7/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For degree two every [ramification point](../../../../../ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) has index two, so contributes exactly one. The formula gives **$2g+2$ [ramification points](../../../../../ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md)**. No two can share an image: that would give a fiber of total local degree at least four. Hence **there are also $2g+2$ distinct [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md)** on the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md).
