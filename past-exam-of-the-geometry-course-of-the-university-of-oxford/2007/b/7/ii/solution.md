<h1 id="b/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $d>1$, the map $z\mapsto z^d$ has local index $d$ at zero and at infinity; at infinity use coordinates $w=1/z$ in source and target, giving again $w\mapsto w^d$. At every nonzero finite point its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is nonzero, so there are no further [ramification points](../../../../../ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md). Thus the ramification sum is $2(d-1)$ and the formula gives $-2=-2d+2(d-1)$, as required for the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md). For $d=1$ the map is the identity and has no ramification, giving the same equality separately.
