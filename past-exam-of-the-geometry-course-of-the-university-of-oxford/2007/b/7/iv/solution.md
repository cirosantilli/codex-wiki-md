<h1 id="b/7/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The ramification sum equals $2g-2+2d$. Since $g\geq0$ and $d>1$, it is at least $2d-2>0$. Thus **some point has [analytic ramification index](../../../../../ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) greater than one**.
