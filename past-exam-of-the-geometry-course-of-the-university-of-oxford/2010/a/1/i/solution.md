<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) says that every [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) connected [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) without boundary is [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to exactly one of the following: the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md), an [orientable surface](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) obtained as the [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $g\geq1$ [tori](../../../../../torus.md), or a [nonorientable surface](../../../../../non-orientable-surface.md) obtained as the [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $k\geq1$ real [projective planes](../../../../../projective-plane.md). The integers $g$ and $k$ are unique, and the orientable and nonorientable families do not overlap. Including $g=0$ makes the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) the genus-zero member of the orientable family.

Their [Euler characteristics](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) are $2-2g$ and $2-k$, respectively. Thus **[orientability](../../../../../orientability.md) and the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) determine the closed connected [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md)**. If boundaries are allowed, one additionally specifies the number of boundary circles and removes that many open discs from a closed model.
