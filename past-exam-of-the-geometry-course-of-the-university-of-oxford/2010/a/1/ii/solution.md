↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a finite polygonal [CW complex](../../../../../cw-complex.md) decomposition of a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md), with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges and $F$ faces after all identifications,

$$
\boxed{\chi=V-E+F.}
$$

This is the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md), independent of the chosen subdivision. In a polygonal presentation, count [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) of vertices and paired edges, rather than their number before gluing.
