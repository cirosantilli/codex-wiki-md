<h1 id="a/1/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a square with boundary word $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, identifying opposite sides with matching labels and arrows. All four vertices become one, the four sides form two edge classes, and there is one face. Therefore the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md) has

$$
\boxed{\chi(T)=1-2+1=0.}
$$
