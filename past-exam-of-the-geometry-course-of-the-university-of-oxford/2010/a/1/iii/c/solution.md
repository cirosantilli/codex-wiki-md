<h1 id="a/1/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A square with boundary word $aba^{-1}b$ gives the [Klein bottle](../../../../../../klein-bottle.md). The side identifications produce one vertex, two edges and one face. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\chi(K)=1-2+1=0.}
$$

Unlike the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md), this [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is nonorientable; its equal [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) alone does not distinguish them.
