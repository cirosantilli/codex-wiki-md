<h1 id="a/1/iii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Glue two triangular discs along their boundaries to obtain a [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md). The resulting [CW complex](../../../../../../cw-complex.md) decomposition has three vertices, three edges and two faces, giving

$$
\boxed{\chi(S^2)=3-3+2=2.}
$$

The two triangles represent the two closed hemispheres, with an equator subdivided into three edges.
