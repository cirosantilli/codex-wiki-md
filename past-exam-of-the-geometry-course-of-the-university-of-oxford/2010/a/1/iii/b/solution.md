↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A polygonal model of the real [projective plane](../../../../../../projective-plane.md) is a bigon with boundary word $aa$, with the equally labelled arrows identified. Its two vertices become one, its sides form one edge, and it has one face. Thus

$$
\boxed{\chi(P)=1-1+1=1.}
$$

Equivalently, glue antipodal boundary points of a disc.
