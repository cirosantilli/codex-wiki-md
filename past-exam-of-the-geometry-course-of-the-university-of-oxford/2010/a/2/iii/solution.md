<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiating the arc-length identity gives $f'f''+h'h''=0$. Multiply by $f'$ and use $(f')^2+(h')^2=1$ to obtain

$$
(f'h''-f''h')h'=f'h'h''-f''(h')^2=-f''\bigl((f')^2+(h')^2\bigr)=-f''.
$$

This derivation does not divide by $h'$, so it remains valid where $h'=0$. Inserting the [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) and [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) computed above gives the [Gaussian curvature of a surface of revolution](../../../../../gaussian-curvature-of-a-surface-of-revolution.md):

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{(f'h''-f''h')fh'}{f^2}=-\frac{f''}{f}.}
$$
