<h1 id="a/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a regular [parametric surface](../../../../../parametric-surface.md) $r(u,v)$, set

$$
E=r_u\cdot r_u,\quad F=r_u\cdot r_v,\quad G=r_v\cdot r_v,\qquad N=\frac{r_u\times r_v}{\|r_u\times r_v\|}.
$$

The [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is the induced [Riemannian metric](../../../../../riemannian-metric-split.md)

$$
\mathrm I=E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2.
$$

The [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md), for this choice of [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md), is

$$
\mathrm{II}=e\,du^2+2f_2\,du\,dv+g_2\,dv^2,\quad e=r_{uu}\cdot N,\quad f_2=r_{uv}\cdot N,\quad g_2=r_{vv}\cdot N.
$$

The [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is the product of the [principal curvatures](../../../../../principal-curvature.md), equivalently the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of the [shape operator](../../../../../shape-operator.md):

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{eg_2-f_2^2}{EG-F^2}.}
$$

Reversing the normal reverses the [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) and both [principal curvatures](../../../../../principal-curvature.md), while leaving $K$ unchanged.
