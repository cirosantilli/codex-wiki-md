<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For curves with no common local component at $p$,

$$
\boxed{I_p(C,D)=1\iff C,D\text{ are both smooth at }p\text{ and have distinct tangent lines}.}
$$

In local affine coordinates, their defining equations have linearly independent gradients exactly in this case. These independent linear terms form local coordinates, so the local intersection quotient has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. Conversely, if either equation lacks a nonzero linear term or the two linear terms are dependent, the local intersection quotient cannot have [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. This is [transverse intersection of plane curves](../../../../../transverse-intersection-of-plane-curves.md) of two smooth plane curves.
