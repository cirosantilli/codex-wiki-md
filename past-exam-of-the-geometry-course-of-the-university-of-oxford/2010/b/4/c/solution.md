<h1 id="b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $u=(1,1,1)$ and $v=(1,\alpha,\beta)$. The vector representing $r_{23}$ satisfies

$$
(\alpha\beta-1,\alpha(\beta-1),\beta(\alpha-1))=\alpha\beta\,u-v.
$$

Consequently the three representative vectors are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md), so **$r_{12},r_{13},r_{23}$ are collinear** in the [projective plane](../../../../../projective-plane.md). Equivalently their three-by-three [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) vanishes. This is the two-line configuration of [Pappus's hexagon theorem](../../../../../pappus-s-hexagon-theorem.md).
