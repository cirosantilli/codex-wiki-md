↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a regular parametrization $r(u,v)$, the [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is the induced metric

$$
\boxed{ds^2=E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2,\quad E=r_u\cdot r_u,\ F=r_u\cdot r_v,\ G=r_v\cdot r_v.}
$$

It measures the lengths and angles of tangent vectors. A [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) is a curve whose unit tangent has zero covariant derivative along itself. For an arc-length parametrized [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) curve in Euclidean three-space, this means its acceleration is normal to the [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md), or $\gamma''\cdot r_u=\gamma''\cdot r_v=0$.
