<h1 id="a/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a nonconstant [holomorphic map](../../../../../holomorphic-map.md) of degree $d$ between compact connected [Riemann surfaces](../../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md), the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{2-2g_X=d(2-2g_Y)-\sum_{p\in X}(e_p-1).}
$$

Here $e_p$ is the [local degree](../../../../../local-degree-of-a-continuous-map.md), characterized by local coordinates in which the map is $z\mapsto z^{e_p}$. The sum is finite and its terms are nonnegative; a [ramification point](../../../../../ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) has $e_p>1$.
