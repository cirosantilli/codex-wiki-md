↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For a degree-two map to the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../riemann-sphere.md), the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives $2-2g=4-R$. Again each ramification index is two, so the number of [ramification points](../../../../../ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) equals $R$. Thus

$$
\boxed{2g+2\text{ ramification points}.}
$$
