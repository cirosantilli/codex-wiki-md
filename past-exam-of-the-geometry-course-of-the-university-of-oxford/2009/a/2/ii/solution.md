<h1 id="a/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $g_X=0$, the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives $2=d(2-2g_Y)-R$, where $R\geq0$. If $g_Y\geq1$, the right side is nonpositive, a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{g_Y=0.}
$$
