The [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) states that every compact connected [topological surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) without boundary is [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to exactly one of the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md), a [connected sum of surfaces](../../../../../connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) consisting of $g\geq1$ [tori](../../../../../torus.md), or a connected sum of $h\geq1$ real [projective planes](../../../../../projective-plane.md). The [torus](../../../../../torus.md) family is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) and the projective-plane family is nonorientable; their respective genera are unique. Including $g=0$ makes the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) the [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md)-zero [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) case. If boundaries are allowed, the number of removed open discs is an additional invariant.
