<h1 id="a/1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A square model of the [torus](../../../../../torus.md) has one vertex, two edges and one face, so its [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) is zero. The connected-sum formula gives

$$
\boxed{\chi(\#^gT^2)=g\cdot0-2(g-1)=2-2g.}
$$

The real [projective plane](../../../../../projective-plane.md) has a bigon model with one vertex, one edge and one face, hence characteristic one. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi(\#^h\mathbb{RP}^2)=h-2(h-1)=2-h.}
$$
