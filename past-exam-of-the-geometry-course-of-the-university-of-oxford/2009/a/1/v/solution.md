<h1 id="a/1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Take $M=T^2$, $N=\mathbb{RP}^2\#\mathbb{RP}^2$ and $S=\mathbb{RP}^2$. The first is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md), while $N$, the [Klein bottle](../../../../../klein-bottle.md), is not, so $M$ and $N$ are not [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md).

Both $M\#S$ and $N\#S$ are nonorientable and have [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $-1$. By the [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md), both are homeomorphic to the connected sum of three projective planes. Hence

$$
\boxed{M\#S\cong N\#S,\qquad M\not\cong N.}
$$

This is [failure of cancellation for connected sums of surfaces](../../../../../failure-of-cancellation-for-connected-sums-of-surfaces.md).
