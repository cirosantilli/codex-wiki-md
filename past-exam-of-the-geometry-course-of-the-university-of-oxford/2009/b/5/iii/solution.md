<h1 id="b/5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

On the nonsingular cubic, $x^3+y^3+z^3=-3\lambda xyz$. Substituting this into the Hessian equation leaves $-4(\lambda^3+1)xyz=0$. Since $\lambda^3+1\ne0$, a flex has a zero coordinate. The remaining cubic equation then gives the nine points

$$
\boxed{[0:1:-\omega^k],\quad[1:0:-\omega^k],\quad[1:-\omega^k:0],\qquad k=0,1,2,}
$$

where $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$. All are distinct, smooth, and lie on both the cubic and its [Hessian curve](../../../../../hessian-curve-of-a-plane-curve.md), so they are exactly the [inflection points](../../../../../inflection-point.md). In particular the nine flexes are independent of $\lambda$ within the nonsingular family. This is the [flex configuration of the Hesse pencil](../../../../../flex-configuration-of-the-hesse-pencil.md).
