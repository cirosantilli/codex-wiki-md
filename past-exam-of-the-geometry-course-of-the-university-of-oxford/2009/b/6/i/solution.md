<h1 id="b/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) is a finite formal integer sum $D=\sum_pn_pp$. Its degree is $\sum_pn_p$. The [Riemann-Roch space](../../../../../riemann-roch-space.md) $L(D)$ consists of zero and the meromorphic functions $h$ with $(h)+D\geq0$, and $\ell(D)=\dim L(D)$. The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) states

$$
\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D+1-g,
$$

where $K$ is a [canonical divisor](../../../../../canonical-divisor.md), of degree $2g-2$. In [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) one, $\deg(K-D)=-n<0$ for $\deg D=n>0$. A negative-degree [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) has no nonzero section, since a [principal divisor](../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) has degree zero and an [effective divisor](../../../../../effective-cartier-divisor.md) has nonnegative degree. Hence

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)=n\quad(g=1,\ n>0).}
$$

A [nonsingular plane cubic](../../../../../nonsingular-plane-cubic.md) has [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) one by the [genus-degree formula](../../../../../genus-degree-formula.md). Therefore $\ell(p+q)=2$, and there is a nonconstant $f\in L(p+q)$. It has at most simple poles at $p,q$ and no other poles. It cannot have no poles, since a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on a compact [Riemann surface](../../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md) is constant. It cannot have just one simple pole either, by the allowed fact, or by $\ell(p)=\ell(q)=1$. Thus **$f$ has simple poles at both $p$ and $q$, and no others**.
