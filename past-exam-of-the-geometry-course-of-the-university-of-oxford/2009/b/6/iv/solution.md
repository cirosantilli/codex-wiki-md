<h1 id="b/6/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every degree-three monomial evaluated at $(1,f,g)$ is $f^ag^b$ with $a+b\leq3$. Its possible pole orders at $p,q,r$ are at most $a$, $a+b$, $b$, respectively, and hence at most three each. Therefore $Q(1,f,g)\in L(3p+3q+3r)$.

The [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of homogeneous degree-three polynomials in three variables has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $\binom52=10$. The target [Riemann-Roch space](../../../../../riemann-roch-space.md) has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) nine, because its [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) has degree nine and [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) is one. Thus the evaluation [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
Q\longmapsto Q(1,f,g)
$$

has nontrivial kernel. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{there is a nonzero homogeneous cubic }Q\text{ with }Q(1,f,g)\equiv0.}
$$
