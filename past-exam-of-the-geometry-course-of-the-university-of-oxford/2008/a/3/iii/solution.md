<h1 id="a/3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $R$ be reflection in the plane. Choose a point of the regular intersection curve and a [unit tangent vector](../../../../../unit-tangent-vector.md) $w$ to it. Both the point and $w$ are fixed by $R$. The unique local [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) with these initial data is carried by the [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) $R$ to another [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) with the same initial data. Uniqueness therefore gives $R\gamma=\gamma$. Its image lies in the fixed-point set of the reflection, namely the plane, hence locally in the intersection curve. Thus **the intersection curve is a [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md)**, when parametrized by arclength.
