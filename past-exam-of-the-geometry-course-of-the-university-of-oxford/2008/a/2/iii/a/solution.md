<h1 id="a/2/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $b=\gamma'+va'$. Then $r_u=b$ and $r_v=a$, so

$$
\boxed{I=|\gamma'+va'|^2du^2+2(\gamma'+va')\cdot a\,du\,dv+|a|^2dv^2.}
$$

This is the induced metric of the [ruled surface](../../../../../../ruled-surface.md) wherever $b\times a\ne0$.
