<h1 id="a/2/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [ruled surface](../../../../../../ruled-surface.md), $r_{vv}=0$, so $N=0$. Also

$$
M=\frac{(b\times a)\cdot a'}{|b\times a|}=\frac{\gamma'\cdot(a\times a')}{|b\times a|},\qquad EG-F^2=|b\times a|^2.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{K=-\frac{[\gamma'\cdot(a\times a')]^2}{|b\times a|^4}.}
$$

The denominator is positive at every regular point, proving that zero [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is equivalent to the vanishing of the stated scalar triple product. In particular the [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) of a regular [ruled surface](../../../../../../ruled-surface.md) is always nonpositive.
