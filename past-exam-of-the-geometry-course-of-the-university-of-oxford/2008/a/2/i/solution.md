<h1 id="a/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a regular parametrization choose the [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md) $n=(r_u\times r_v)/|r_u\times r_v|$. The [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is the squared length induced on tangent vectors:

$$
I=E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2,\qquad E=r_u\cdot r_u,\quad F=r_u\cdot r_v,\quad G=r_v\cdot r_v.
$$

The [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) records their normal bending:

$$
II=L\,du^2+2M\,du\,dv+N\,dv^2,\qquad L=n\cdot r_{uu},\quad M=n\cdot r_{uv},\quad N=n\cdot r_{vv}.
$$

The [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{LN-M^2}{EG-F^2}.}
$$

Regularity ensures $EG-F^2>0$.
