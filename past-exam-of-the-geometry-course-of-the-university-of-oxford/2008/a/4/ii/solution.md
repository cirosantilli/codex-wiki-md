<h1 id="a/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md), choose the inward normal so that the [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) is $I/a$. Both principal curvatures are $1/a$, giving **$K=1/a^2$**; the outward choice reverses both signs and gives the same product.

The octant has area $4\pi a^2/8=\pi a^2/2$, so its curvature integral is $\pi/2$. Its sides are great-circle geodesics, hence have zero [geodesic curvature](../../../../../geodesic-curvature.md). Each interior angle is $\pi/2$, so each exterior angle is also $\pi/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_DK\,dA+\int_{\partial D}\kappa_g\,ds+\sum_i\theta_i=\frac\pi2+0+\frac{3\pi}2=2\pi,}
$$

verifying the polygon formula directly.
