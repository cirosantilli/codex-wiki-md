<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) says that every compact connected [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) without boundary is homeomorphic either to the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) with $g\geq0$ handles, with [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $2-2g$, or to a [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $k\geq1$ real projective planes, with [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $2-k$. The first family is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) and the second is nonorientable; the displayed invariants uniquely determine the type. With boundary one additionally removes a uniquely determined number of open disks.

Number the vertices of the fourteen-gon $v_0,\ldots,v_{13}$ in boundary order. The printed edge identifications imply

$$
v_0\sim v_5\sim v_2\sim v_3\sim v_4\sim v_1\sim v_6\sim v_{13}\sim v_7\sim v_{12}\sim v_9\sim v_{10}\sim v_{11}\sim v_8\sim v_0.
$$

Thus there is one vertex, seven edges and one face in the quotient. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\chi(S)=1-7+1=-5.}
$$

The two occurrences of $a_1$ have the same sign, as do those of $a_5$, so the [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) on the polygon cannot extend consistently across those paired edges. Thus **the [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is nonorientable**, and classification identifies it as a [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of seven projective planes. The inverse on the first occurrence of $a_4$ and the positive second occurrence have been retained in the vertex calculation.
