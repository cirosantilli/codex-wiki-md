<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [Riemann surface](../../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md) is a connected Hausdorff second-countable space equipped with complex coordinate charts whose changes of coordinate are holomorphic with holomorphic inverses. Such charts give it the structure of a one-dimensional [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md).

A holomorphic change of coordinate has nonzero derivative. In real coordinates its derivative matrix has determinant $|f'(z)|^2>0$ by the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md). Thus every overlap preserves the standard [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md). These chart orientations fit together, proving **every [Riemann surface](../../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md) is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md)**.
