<h1 id="b/8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If a nonidentity holomorphic [automorphism](../../../../../automorphism.md) had more than $2g+2$ fixed points, choose that many distinct ones and apply the function constructed above. The zero-and-pole count would give $f=f\circ\varphi$, and the local reciprocal coordinate would force $\varphi$ to be the identity, a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\#\operatorname{Fix}(\varphi)\leq2g+2\quad\text{for every nonidentity holomorphic automorphism}.}
$$
