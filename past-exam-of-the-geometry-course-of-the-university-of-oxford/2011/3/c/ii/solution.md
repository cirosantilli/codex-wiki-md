<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [surface of revolution](../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md),

$$
\mathbf r_u=(f'\cos v,f'\sin v,1),\qquad
\mathbf r_v=(-f\sin v,f\cos v,0).
$$

The mixed [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) vanishes, giving

$$
\boxed{I=(1+f'(u)^2)du^2+f(u)^2dv^2.}
$$

Taking $f(u)=\cosh u$ makes both coefficients $\cosh^2u$, as used in the parent calculation.
