<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate to get

$$
\mathbf r_u=(a\cos v,a\sin v,0),\qquad
\mathbf r_v=(-au\sin v,au\cos v,1).
$$

Their squared lengths are $a^2$ and $a^2u^2+1$, and their [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is zero. Hence the [helicoid](../../../../../helicoid.md) has [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md)

$$
\boxed{I=a^2du^2+(a^2u^2+1)dv^2.}
$$
