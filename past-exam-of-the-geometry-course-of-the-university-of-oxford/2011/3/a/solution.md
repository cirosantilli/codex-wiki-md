<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a regular parametrization $\mathbf r(u,v)$, the [first fundamental form](../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is the induced [Riemannian metric](../../../../riemannian-metric-split.md)

$$
I=d\mathbf r\cdot d\mathbf r=E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2,
\quad E=\mathbf r_u\cdot\mathbf r_u,\quad F=\mathbf r_u\cdot\mathbf r_v,
\quad G=\mathbf r_v\cdot\mathbf r_v.
$$

It measures squared lengths of [tangent vectors](../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) and therefore lengths of [curves](../../../../curve.md). In the plane put $x=\rho\cos\theta$, $y=\rho\sin\theta$. The derivatives are orthogonal, of lengths one and $\rho$, so

$$
\boxed{dx^2+dy^2=d\rho^2+\rho^2d\theta^2.}
$$

These [polar coordinates](../../../../polar-coordinates.md) are regular for $\rho>0$.
