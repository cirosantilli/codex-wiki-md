<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On the parameter, the two transformations are $S(\lambda)=1/\lambda$ and $T(\lambda)=1-\lambda$. Their compositions yield

$$
\lambda,\quad S\lambda=1/\lambda,\quad T\lambda=1-\lambda,
\quad TS\lambda=1-1/\lambda,\quad ST\lambda=1/(1-\lambda),
\quad STS\lambda=\lambda/(\lambda-1).
$$

Every step lifts to an invertible [projective transformation](../../../../projective-linear-transformation.md) of the corresponding cubic [curves](../../../../curve.md). Therefore **all six parameter values give isomorphic [curves](../../../../curve.md)**. Some values coincide for special $\lambda$; this does not affect the conclusion.
