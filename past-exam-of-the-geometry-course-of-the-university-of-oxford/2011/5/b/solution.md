<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An appropriate [projective transformation](../../../../projective-linear-transformation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\tau_\lambda[x:y:z]=[z-x:iy:z].}
$$

It fixes $[0:1:0]$ and has an invertible representing matrix. For $X=z-x$, $Y=iy$, $Z=z$,

$$
X(X-Z)(X-(1-\lambda)Z)
=(z-x)(-x)(\lambda z-x)
=-x(x-z)(x-\lambda z)=Y^2Z.
$$

Hence its restriction is an isomorphism $C_\lambda\to C_{1-\lambda}$.
