<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The simplicial intersection conditions prohibit loops and multiple edges between the same pair of vertices, so the one-skeleton is a [simple graph](../../../../../simple-graph.md) and

$$
E\leq\binom V2.
$$

Using the preceding identity gives $V^2-7V+6\chi\geq0$. A closed [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) has $\chi\leq2$ and a simplicial [triangulation](../../../../../triangulation.md) has $V\geq4$. The smaller root is at most three, so this inequality forces $V$ to lie above the larger root:

$$
\boxed{V\geq\frac{7+\sqrt{49-24\chi}}2.}
$$

For completeness, fewer than four vertices could give at most one triangle under the intersection rules, which cannot form a closed [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md).
