<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Count incidences of triangular faces with edges. Every triangle contributes three and every edge of a closed [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) occurs in two triangles, hence $3F=2E$. Substituting $F=2E/3$ into the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) gives

$$
\chi=V-E+\frac{2E}{3}=V-\frac E3,
\qquad \boxed{E=3(V-\chi).}
$$
