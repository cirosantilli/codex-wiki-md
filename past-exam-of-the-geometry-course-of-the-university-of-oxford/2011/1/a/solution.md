<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [CW complex](../../../../cw-complex.md) with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges and $F$ faces,

$$
\boxed{\chi=V-E+F.}
$$

This [Euler characteristic](../../../../euler-characteristic.md) is unchanged by subdividing an edge, which adds one vertex and one edge, or subdividing a face by an edge, which adds one edge and one face. Common refinements therefore give the same value for different subdivisions of the same [surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md).
