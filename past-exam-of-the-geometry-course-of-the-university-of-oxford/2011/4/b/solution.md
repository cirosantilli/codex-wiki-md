<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Represent each point by a nonzero column $v_i$. The source's [cross-ratio](../../../../cross-ratio.md) is

$$
\frac{\det(v_1,v_3)\det(v_2,v_4)}{\det(v_1,v_4)\det(v_2,v_3)}.
$$

Rescaling representatives cancels between numerator and denominator. If the [projective transformation](../../../../projective-linear-transformation.md) is represented by $A\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb C)$, then $\det(Av_i,Av_j)=\det(A)\det(v_i,v_j)$. Two factors of $\det(A)$ occur in both numerator and denominator and cancel. Hence **the [cross-ratio](../../../../cross-ratio.md) is invariant under every [projective transformation](../../../../projective-linear-transformation.md)**.
