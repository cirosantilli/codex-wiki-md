<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplication by two on the [complex torus](../../../../../complex-torus.md) is a holomorphic unramified covering

$$
[2]:T\to T,\qquad[z]\mapsto[2z].
$$

Its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $(\tfrac12\Lambda)/\Lambda$, of order four; its derivative is the nonzero constant two. Therefore $\deg[2]=4$. Composition with the degree-two [Weierstrass elliptic function](../../../../../weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\deg\bigl([z]\mapsto\wp(2z)\bigr)=4\cdot2=8.}
$$

It is well-defined because changing $z$ by $\omega\in\Lambda$ changes $2z$ by the period $2\omega$.
