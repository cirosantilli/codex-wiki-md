<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonconstant [holomorphic map](../../../../holomorphic-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ between compact connected [Riemann surfaces](../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md), let $n$ be its degree and $e_p$ its local degree at $p$. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi(X)=n\chi(Y)-\sum_{p\in X}(e_p-1).}
$$

Equivalently, $2g_X-2=n(2g_Y-2)+\sum_p(e_p-1)$. Only finitely many terms are nonzero; their sum is the total ramification. In local coordinates the map has the form $w=z^{e_p}$ after suitable coordinate changes.
