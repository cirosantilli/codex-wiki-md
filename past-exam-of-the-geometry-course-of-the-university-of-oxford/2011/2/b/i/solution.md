<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\Lambda=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2$ and $T=\mathbb C/\Lambda$. Its fundamental parallelogram with opposite sides identified is a [torus](../../../../../torus.md), so $\chi(T)=0$. The [Weierstrass elliptic function](../../../../../weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) is even and doubly periodic. One way to verify the latter from its series is to differentiate: the locally normally convergent derivative series is

$$
\wp'(z)=-2\sum_{\omega\in\Lambda}(z-\omega)^{-3},
$$

which is absolutely convergent away from the [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) and periodic by reindexing. Thus $\wp(z+\omega_j)-\wp(z)$ is constant. Evaluating at $z=-\omega_j/2$ and using evenness shows that this constant is zero for each generating period.

Therefore $[z]\mapsto\wp(z)$ descends to a [meromorphic function](../../../../../meromorphic-function.md) on $T$, with one double pole at $[0]$. Viewed as a map to the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../riemann-sphere.md), a pole is a holomorphic point in the target coordinate $1/w$. The map is consequently holomorphic everywhere and

$$
\boxed{T\longrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^1,\qquad[z]\longmapsto\wp(z),\qquad\deg\wp=2.}
$$
