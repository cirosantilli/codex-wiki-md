<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Given $f$, choose the [hyperplane divisors](../../../../hyperplane-divisor.md) as in (a). The ratios $f_Q$ form a [linear subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $L(D)$ by (d), and its [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) equals $md+1-g=\ell(D)$ by (b). A [linear subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) of a finite-dimensional [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) with the same [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is the whole space. Hence $f=f_Q$ for some homogeneous [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $Q$ of degree $m$, giving

$$
\boxed{f([x:y:z])=\frac{Q(x,y,z)}{\prod_{i=1}^m(a_ix+b_iy+c_iz)}.}
$$

The zero function corresponds to $Q=0$. Thus every allowed [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md) is a ratio of homogeneous [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) of equal degree, with a denominator that can be chosen as a product of linear forms.
