<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [genus-degree formula](../../../genus-degree-formula.md) for a nonsingular plane [curve](../../../curve.md) gives

$$
\boxed{g=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2.}
$$

A [divisor](../../../divisor.md) is a finite formal sum $D=\sum_pn_p[p]$ with integer coefficients; its degree is $\deg D=\sum_pn_p$. An effective [divisor](../../../divisor.md) has all $n_p\geq0$. A [hyperplane divisor](../../../hyperplane-divisor.md) is the intersection with a projective line not containing the [curve](../../../curve.md), counted with local intersection multiplicities. Its degree is $d$.

Define $L(D)=\{0\}\cup\{f\text{ meromorphic}:(f)+D\geq0\}$ and $\ell(D)=\dim_{\mathbb C}L(D)$. For a [canonical divisor](../../../canonical-divisor.md) $K$, the [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D+1-g.}
$$

Here $(f)$ records zero orders positively and pole orders negatively, and $\deg K=2g-2$.
