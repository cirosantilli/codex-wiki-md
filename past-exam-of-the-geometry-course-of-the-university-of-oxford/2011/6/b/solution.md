<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [divisor](../../../../divisor.md) $D=\sum_iH_i$ has degree $md$. The hypothesis gives $\deg(K-D)=2g-2-md<0$. A [divisor](../../../../divisor.md) of negative degree has no nonzero section: if $h\in L(K-D)\setminus\{0\}$, then $(h)+K-D$ would be effective, but its degree would be negative because the [divisor](../../../../divisor.md) of a [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md) has degree zero. Thus $\ell(K-D)=0$. The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)=md+1-g=md+\frac{d(3-d)}2.}
$$
