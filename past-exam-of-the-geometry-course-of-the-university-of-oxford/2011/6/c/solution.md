<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The monomials $x^iy^jz^k$ with nonnegative integers $i+j+k=m$ form a [basis](../../../../basis.md) for the [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) of homogeneous [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree $m$. For each $i=0,\ldots,m$, there are $m-i+1$ choices of $(j,k)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\dim V_m=\sum_{i=0}^m(m-i+1)=\binom{m+2}{2}
=\frac{(m+1)(m+2)}2.}
$$
