# Electromagnetic gauge symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md)

The unbroken local [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) associated with [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md). Its [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) is the massless [photon](photon.md). The neutral [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) vacuum preserves this subgroup while the W and Z acquire masses.
