# Cellular cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular cochain complex](cellular-cochain-complex.md)

The [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the [cellular cochain complex](cellular-cochain-complex.md) naturally agrees with singular cohomology. A [CW complex](cw-complex.md) with no odd cells has zero cellular differentials and free integral cohomology on its even cells, although its [cup product](cup-product.md) still requires a separate computation.
