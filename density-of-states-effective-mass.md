# Density-of-states effective mass

↑ **Parent:** [Effective density of states](effective-density-of-states.md)

A scalar [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) chosen to reproduce carrier state counting near a band edge. For $g_v$ equivalent ellipsoidal conduction valleys, $m_{\mathrm{DOS}}^{3/2}=g_v(m_lm_t^2)^{1/2}$ when the displayed factor two accounts for [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md). The [valley degeneracy](valley-degeneracy.md) is then already included and must not be multiplied in again. A conductivity mass weights inverse curvatures and is generally different; for an isotropically averaged set of ellipsoids, $3/m_{\mathrm{cond}}=1/m_l+2/m_t$.
