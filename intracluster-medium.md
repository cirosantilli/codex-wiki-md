# Intracluster medium

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intracluster_medium)

The [intracluster medium](intracluster-medium.md) is diffuse hot plasma between the [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) of a [galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md). Its X-ray radiation includes [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) and atomic line emission. Measured [temperature](temperature.md) and density profiles supply a [cluster hydrostatic mass estimator](cluster-hydrostatic-mass-estimator.md) when approximately thermal [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) holds. The medium supplies much of a cluster's baryonic mass, but this need not equal its total gravitating mass.
