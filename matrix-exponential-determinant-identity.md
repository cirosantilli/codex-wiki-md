# Matrix exponential determinant identity

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

The [determinant](determinant.md) is a smooth [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md) from the real [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) to $\mathbb R^\times$. Its derivative at the identity is the [trace](matrix-trace.md), and the flow-defined exponential on $\mathbb R^\times$ is the ordinary scalar exponential. [Naturality of the Lie group exponential](naturality-of-the-lie-group-exponential.md) therefore proves the identity without diagonalizability assumptions. The series $\sum_{k\geq0}t^kA^k/k!$ is the flow-defined [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) because it solves $E'=EA$, $E(0)=I$, and has inverse $E(-t)$.
