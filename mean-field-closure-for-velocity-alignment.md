# Mean-field closure for velocity alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity alignment](velocity-alignment.md)

Neglect of pair correlations closes the first marginal equation of the [dissipative Liouville equation](dissipative-liouville-equation.md). For unit-normalized [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md), the acceleration is $(N-1)\mathbf L[p_1]$. With number-normalized $f=Np_1$, it is $(1-1/N)\mathbf L[f]$. The usual [mean-field alignment equation](mean-field-alignment-equation.md) neglects this relative $1/N$ correction. Independence at one time does not imply that interactions preserve independence.
