# Coadjoint representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group action](lie-group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coadjoint_representation)

The coadjoint action of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) on the [dual space](dual-space.md) of its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is $(\operatorname{Ad}_g^*\eta)(X)=\eta(\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}X)$. The inverse in this formula makes it a left [group action](group-action.md).
