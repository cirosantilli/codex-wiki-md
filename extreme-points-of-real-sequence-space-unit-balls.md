# Extreme points of real sequence-space unit balls

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme point](extreme-point.md)

For the real [l-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md), every coordinate of an [extreme point](extreme-point.md) must be an endpoint of $[-1,1]$; any slack coordinate permits opposite perturbations. For the real [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md), a unit vector with two nonzero coordinates permits a transfer of mass between them, so only signed coordinate vectors are extreme. For the real [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md), the [parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md) makes every unit vector extreme. In all three cases interior points of the unit ball are nonextreme.
