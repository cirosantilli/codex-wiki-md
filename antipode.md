# Antipode

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

The convolution inverse $S$ of the identity on a [bialgebra](bialgebra.md): $m(S\otimes1)\Delta=j\varepsilon=m(1\otimes S)\Delta$. Existence makes the [bialgebra](bialgebra.md) a [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md); $S$ reverses multiplication.
