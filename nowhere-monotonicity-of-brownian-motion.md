# Nowhere monotonicity of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

Almost surely, no restriction of a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) path to an interval of positive length is a [monotone function](monotonic-function.md). On a fixed interval, monotonicity forces the independent centered [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) given by every dyadic subdivision's increments to have the same sign. The probability is at most $2^{1-2^m}$ for a subdivision into $2^m$ pieces. A countable union over rational intervals gives the simultaneous pathwise assertion.
