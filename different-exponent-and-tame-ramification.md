# Different exponent and tame ramification

↑ **Parent:** [Different exponent](different-exponent.md)

For a finite extension of [number fields](number-field.md), the [different exponent](different-exponent.md) satisfies $d_{\mathfrak P}\geq e_{\mathfrak P}-1$, with equality exactly when the extension at $\mathfrak P$ is [tamely ramified](tamely-ramified-extension.md). The finite residue fields are perfect, so tameness is equivalent to the residue characteristic not dividing the [ramification index](ramification-index.md). Thus unramified primes have exponent zero and wildly ramified primes have exponent at least $e_{\mathfrak P}$. No [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) hypothesis is needed.
