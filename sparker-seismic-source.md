# Sparker seismic source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic source](seismic-source.md)

A marine acoustic source using a rapid underwater electrical discharge and its expanding bubble to generate a pulse. Its energy and spectrum can be chosen to investigate shallow sub-bottom structure; bubble behaviour and [seismic wavelet](seismic-wavelet.md) shape affect resolution.
