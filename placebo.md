# Placebo

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

A comparator intended to lack a specified active treatment ingredient. Its credibility, expectations and contact still require assessment; a pill [placebo](placebo.md) is not automatically a contact-matched control for psychotherapy.
