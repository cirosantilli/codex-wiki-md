# Subcritical magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

Subcritical magnetoconvection means finite-amplitude [convection](convection.md) on the stable side of the linear conductive-state threshold. At large [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md), [flux expulsion](flux-expulsion.md) can reduce interior magnetic inhibition enough to sustain a nonlinear convective cell even when infinitesimal disturbances decay in the unredistributed imposed field. Stable subcritical states can produce hysteresis and [magnetic flux separation](magnetic-flux-separation.md). A schematic saturated [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md) $\dot A=\mu A+c|A|^2A-d|A|^4A$, $c,d>0$, has an unstable lower branch and stable upper radial branch for $-c^2/(4d)<\mu<0$, coexisting with stable $A=0$. This illustrates bistability; it is not a derivation of coefficients for all magnetoconvection geometries.
