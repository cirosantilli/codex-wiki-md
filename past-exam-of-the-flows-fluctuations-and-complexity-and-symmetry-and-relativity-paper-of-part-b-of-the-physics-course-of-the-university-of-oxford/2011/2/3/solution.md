<h1 id="2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A parcel at fixed $z$ spends [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) $a/u(z)$ in the light. With no [thermal conduction](../../../../thermal-conduction.md) or [thermal expansion](../../../../thermal-expansion.md), its energy balance is $\rho c\,dT/dt=q_R$. Equivalently the steady [advection equation](../../../../transport-equation-split.md) is $\rho c\,u(z)T_x=q_R(z)$ with $T(0,z)=T_0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{T(a,z)=T_0+\frac{aF_0k}{c\,u(z)}e^{-k\rho z}
=T_0+\frac{2\mu aF_0k}{cf}\frac{e^{-k\rho z}}{z(b-z)}.}
$$

The [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) makes residence [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) diverge as either wall is approached. The resulting unbounded [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) is a failure of the approximations, rather than a physically steady wall prediction. Even small [thermal conductivity](../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) transfers heat away from these layers; conduction into real perspex adds another heat sink. [Thermal expansion](../../../../thermal-expansion.md) can alter [mass density](../../../../density.md), create [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md)-driven motion and change the background profile, while temperature-dependent [dynamic viscosity](../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) changes the assumed residence [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md). With exactly insulating walls, finite fluid [thermal conductivity](../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) still regularizes the transverse profile; an entrance region and, sufficiently near a stagnant wall, longitudinal conduction also matter.
