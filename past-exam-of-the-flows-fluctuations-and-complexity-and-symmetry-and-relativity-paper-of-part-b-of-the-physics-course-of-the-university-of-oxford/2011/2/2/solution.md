Here $k$ is a [mass absorption coefficient](../../../../mass-absorption-coefficient.md), because $k\rho$ has units of inverse length. The [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../beer-lambert-law.md) is $dF/dz=-k\rho F$, giving $F(z)=F_0e^{-k\rho z}$. A horizontal slab of area $A$ and thickness $dz$ absorbs [power](../../../../power.md) $A[F(z)-F(z+dz)]$. Dividing by its volume and taking the limit gives

$$
\boxed{q_R(z)=-\frac{dF}{dz}=F_0k\rho e^{-k\rho z}\quad\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}}.}
$$
