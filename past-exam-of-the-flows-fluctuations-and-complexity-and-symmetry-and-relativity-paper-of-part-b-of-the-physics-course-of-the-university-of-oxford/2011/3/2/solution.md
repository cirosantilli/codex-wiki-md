<h1 id="3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

At an [equilibrium point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) of the [Rössler system](../../../../rossler-system.md), $y=-z$, $x=az$, and $az^2-cz+a=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{z_\pm=\frac{c\pm\sqrt{c^2-4a^2}}{2a},\qquad
(x_\pm,y_\pm,z_\pm)=(az_\pm,-z_\pm,z_\pm).}
$$

There are two distinct real [equilibrium points](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) only if $c>2a$; they merge if $c=2a$ and there are none if $c<2a$. Positivity of $a,c$ alone does not guarantee two. Under the later limits, the nearer one is $(x_-,y_-,z_-)\simeq(a^2/c,-a/c,a/c)$, while $(x_+,y_+,z_+)\simeq(c,-c/a,c/a)$.
