An [equilibrium point of a dynamical system](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a state $\mathbf x_*$ with $\mathbf f(\mathbf x_*)=0$, so the trajectory stays there. In a planar [phase portrait](../../../../phase-portrait.md), an [unstable spiral](../../../../unstable-spiral.md) has a complex-conjugate pair of [Jacobian matrix](../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) with positive real part: nearby trajectories rotate while moving outward. An [attractor](../../../../attractor.md) is an invariant set approached by trajectories from a neighborhood, its basin of attraction. It can be an [equilibrium point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), a [limit cycle](../../../../limit-cycle.md), or a more complicated set; outward spiralling near one [equilibrium point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) does not preclude attraction to a larger set.
