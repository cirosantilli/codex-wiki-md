↑ **Parent:** [Comment](../comment.md)

The orientation-dependent results in the official notes show that the measured [spring constant](../../../../../spring-constant.md) is **not a Lorentz scalar**. A [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) changes both the simultaneous extension and the measured three-force; their ratio depends on the orientation. This does not make the spring intrinsically anisotropic in its own [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md), or require a preferred inertial frame. It is a quasistatic effective stiffness comparison between frames; an extended real spring also has finite internal signal speeds.
