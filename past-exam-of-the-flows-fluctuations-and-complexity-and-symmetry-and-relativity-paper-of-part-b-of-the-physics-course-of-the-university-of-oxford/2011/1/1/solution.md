<h1 id="1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Set $\partial_t\mathbf u=0$ and $\nu=0$ in the [Navier-Stokes equation](../../../../navier-stokes-equation.md). Dotting the resulting [Euler equation](../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) with a displacement $d\mathbf r$ tangent to a [streamline](../../../../streamline.md) gives

$$
d\left(\frac{|\mathbf u|^2}{2}\right)+\frac{dp}{\rho}+g\,dz=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\frac{|\mathbf u|^2}{2}+\frac p\rho+gz=B.}
$$

Here [mass density](../../../../density.md) $\rho$ is constant, and $B$ is constant along each [streamline](../../../../streamline.md). The terms are [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) per unit mass, the [pressure work](../../../../pressure-work.md) per unit mass available from pushing a parcel through the flow, and [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) per unit mass. Their sum is conserved mechanical energy per unit mass; $p/\rho$ is not an additional independently conserved parcel energy. Different [streamlines](../../../../streamline.md) can have different $B$ unless, for example, the flow is also [irrotational](../../../../irrotational-vector-field.md) in a connected region.
