**Covered by the official notes:** comparison with a 200 keV neutrino mass (PDF p. 12; handwritten p. 7).[PDF p. 12; handwritten p. 7: comparison with a 200 keV neutrino mass](../../../../symmetry-and-relativity-2011-exam-solution-notes.md)

The notes stop at “no change”; the experimental implication is that this is a poorly conditioned [neutrino mass](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) measurement. In [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md), the corrections to the rest-frame energy and momentum enter through the square of the daughter [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md), while the [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) boosts both the massless value and its small correction. A measured laboratory energy or angle must therefore resolve a very small change against beam-energy spread, decay-angle uncertainty and detector resolution. **No visible change at the quoted precision does not establish zero neutrino mass.**
