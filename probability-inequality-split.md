# Probability inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

[Probability inequalities](probability-inequality-split.md) bound [event](event.md) [probabilities](probability.md) from limited information, such as [moments](moment.md), without requiring a complete [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). [Markov's inequality](markov-s-inequality.md) uses a nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md)'s [expected value](expected-value-split.md); [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-s-inequality.md) uses a [variance](variance-split.md) to bound deviations from a [mean](expected-value-split.md). The attraction is a guarantee that applies to many possible [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md). Their bounds can be conservative for a particular model, and distributions attaining equality show when the available information really cannot give more.
