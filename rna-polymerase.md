# RNA polymerase

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription (biology)](transcription-biology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_polymerase)

An [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) used in [transcription](transcription-biology.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme.md) that synthesizes [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) from a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) template. Unlike a replicating [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md), it can start a new strand without a pre-existing paired strand end; initiation still requires the appropriate [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) signals and associated machinery. See [Alberts et al.: RNA polymerase and transcription initiation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26887/).
