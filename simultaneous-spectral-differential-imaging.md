# Simultaneous spectral differential imaging

↑ **Parent:** [High-contrast imaging](high-contrast-imaging.md)

Simultaneous spectral differential imaging compares images in nearby bands, rescaled so stellar [speckle patterns](speckle-pattern.md) approximately coincide. A companion has a different positional or spectral response. Simultaneity reduces temporal variation but does not remove chromatic and non-common-path errors.
