# Heat-current relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Heat current](heat-current.md)

A [heat-current relaxation time](heat-current-relaxation-time.md) measures the decay of a departure from equilibrium carrying heat. It need not equal the [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md) or the time between arbitrary scattering events. In an isotropic degenerate-electron estimate, $K_e=c_ev_F^2\tau_Q/3$. Small-angle inelastic [electron-phonon scattering](electron-phonon-scattering.md) can remove an excitation energy of order $k_BT$ while barely changing the electron's velocity direction, thus relaxing heat more efficiently than charge flow.
