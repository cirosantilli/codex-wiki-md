<h1 id="quantum-depletion-of-neel-order">Quantum depletion of Néel order</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Néel order parameter](neel-order-parameter.md)

For a [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md), the ground state is empty of the diagonal [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md) but contains the original spin bosons. If $a_k=u_k\alpha_k-v_k\alpha_{-k}^\dagger$, their [occupation number](occupation-number.md) is $v_k^2$. The [staggered magnetization](staggered-magnetization.md) is reduced from $S$ to $S-N^{-1}\sum_kv_k^2$. For the one-dimensional nearest-neighbor [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md), $v_k^2=(|\sin k|^{-1}-1)/2$ and the integral diverges logarithmically. This invalidates finite ordered magnetization within the approximation; it is not itself a determination of the exact spectral gap.
