# Thylacine

↑ **Parent:** [Dasyuromorphia](dasyuromorphia.md)

An extinct predatory [marsupial](marsupial.md). Its superficial resemblance to canids illustrates [convergent evolution](convergent-evolution.md), not membership in [Carnivora](carnivora.md).
