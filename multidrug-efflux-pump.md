# Multidrug efflux pump

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

A [multidrug efflux pump](multidrug-efflux-pump.md) exports multiple chemical substrates using a coupled source of [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Broad recognition can arise from overlapping binding sites and conformational flexibility, but binding alone does not establish productive transport.
