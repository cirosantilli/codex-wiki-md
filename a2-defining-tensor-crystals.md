# A2 defining tensor crystals

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of crystals](tensor-product-of-crystals.md)

For the defining representation of the [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_3$, the [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) is $1\xrightarrow{1}2\xrightarrow{2}3$, with weights $\varepsilon_i$ restricted to trace-zero diagonal matrices. Its dual is $\bar3\xrightarrow{2}\bar2\xrightarrow{1}\bar1$, with weights $-\varepsilon_i$. Using the [crystal tensor-product rule](crystal-tensor-product-rule.md) with lowering on the first factor when $\varphi_i(a)>\varepsilon_i(b)$, the tensor square has highest vertices $1\otimes1$ and $1\otimes2$, of [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $2\omega_1,\omega_2$, generating components of six and three vertices. The defining tensor dual has highest vertices $1\otimes\bar3$ and $1\otimes\bar1$, generating components of eight and one vertices and weights $\omega_1+\omega_2,0$. These encode the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) plus [exterior square](exterior-square.md), and the trace-free endomorphisms plus scalar identity, respectively. A crystal vertex labelled by a tensor pair need not represent that same undeformed ordinary simple tensor; the actual invariant vector is $\sum_i e_i\otimes e_i^*$.
