# Deoxynucleoside triphosphate

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)

A [deoxynucleoside triphosphate](deoxynucleoside-triphosphate.md) is a [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) containing [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md) and three [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups. [DNA polymerases](dna-polymerase.md) use these substrates to extend [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md); the four standard substrate types carry [adenine](adenine.md), [cytosine](cytosine.md), [guanine](guanine.md) or [thymine](thymine.md).
