# Spatial root splitting for the dispersive Stokes resolvent

↑ **Parent:** [Linear dispersive Stokes equation](linear-dispersive-stokes-equation.md)

For a time [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) parameter with $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, the spatial [characteristic roots](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) satisfy $r^3+r+p=0$. Exactly one root $r_-$ has negative [real part](real-part.md) and two have positive [real part](real-part.md). Indeed an imaginary root would force $p=-i(k-k^3)$ to be imaginary. The count is therefore constant on the connected right half-plane; for real $p>0$, the cubic has one negative real root and a conjugate pair with positive real parts. Repeated roots would satisfy $3r^2+1=0$, which again puts $p$ on the imaginary axis. Thus $r_-(p)$ is a nonzero [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) in the right half-plane. Only $e^{r_-x}$ decays at positive spatial infinity.
