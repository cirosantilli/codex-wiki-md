# Deep logarithm subgroup of a formal group

↑ **Parent:** [Formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md)

Let $F$ be a [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) over the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) of a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$, with $v_K(\pi)=1$ and $e=v_K(p)$. If $m>e/(p-1)$, its [formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md) is an isomorphism $F(\pi^m\mathcal O_K)\to(\pi^m\mathcal O_K,+)$. Indeed, its scaled series is $U+\pi R(U)$ with $R$ integral and convergent on $\mathcal O_K$; solving by contraction produces the [formal group exponential](formal-group-exponential.md). The subgroup has index $q^{m-1}$ in $F(\pi\mathcal O_K)$, where $q$ is the residue-field order.
