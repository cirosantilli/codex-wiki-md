<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The given [Doob L2 maximal inequality](../../../../doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E[(M_t^*)^2]\leq4\mathbb E|M_t|^2<4\,2^{-2n}=2^{-2(n-1)}.
$$

Apply [Markov's inequality](../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the nonnegative variable $(M_t^*)^2$, at the threshold printed in the question:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\bigl((M_t^*)^2\geq2^{-(n-1)}\bigr)
\leq\frac{2^{-2(n-1)}}{2^{-(n-1)}}=2^{-(n-1)}.}
$$

The corresponding threshold for $M_t^*$ itself is $2^{-(n-1)/2}$.
