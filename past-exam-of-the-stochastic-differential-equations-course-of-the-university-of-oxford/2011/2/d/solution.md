<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For simple predictable integrands, the [Itô integral](../../../../ito-integral.md) is a continuous square-integrable martingale and the [Itô isometry](../../../../ito-isometry.md) gives

$$
\left\|\int_0^t(H_s-K_s)\,dW_s\right\|_2^2=\mathbb E\int_0^t|H_s-K_s|^2ds.
$$

Approximate a general square-integrable predictable integrand by simple ones. The resulting integral martingales are Cauchy in terminal $L^2$ norm. [Completeness of continuous square-integrable martingales](../../../../completeness-of-continuous-square-integrable-martingales.md) guarantees a unique continuous martingale limit, independent of the approximation, which defines the integral on the completed integrand space.
