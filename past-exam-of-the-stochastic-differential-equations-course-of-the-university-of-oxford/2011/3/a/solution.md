<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The bounded integrand makes $X_s=\int_0^sY_u\,dW_u$ a square-integrable martingale, with [quadratic variation](../../../../quadratic-variation.md)

$$
\langle X\rangle_s=\int_0^sY_u^2du\leq C^2t\qquad(s\leq t).
$$

Thus

$$
\mathbb E\exp\bigl(\tfrac12\langle X\rangle_t\bigr)\leq e^{C^2t/2}<\infty.
$$

[Novikov's condition](../../../../novikov-s-condition.md) applies to the [stochastic exponential](../../../../doleans-dade-exponential.md) $Z_s=\exp(X_s-\langle X\rangle_s/2)$. Hence **$Z$ is a true martingale with $\mathbb E Z_s=Z_0=1$**. It is strictly positive because its exponent is finite almost surely.
