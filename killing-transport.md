# Killing transport

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

The [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) and the [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) transport a Killing field and its antisymmetric derivative along any curve. Here $L_{ab}=\nabla_b\xi_a$ and the curvature convention is the one in [curvature-sign convention in Killing derivative identities](curvature-sign-convention-in-killing-derivative-identities.md). In a parallel frame the equations form a homogeneous linear system of ordinary differential equations. Initial value zero has unique zero continuation. Thus on a connected [pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md), not just on a Riemannian manifold, the field is determined by its value and first derivative at one point.
