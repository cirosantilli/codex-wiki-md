# Spectral majorization under a unital quantum channel

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue mixing matrix of a unital quantum channel](eigenvalue-mixing-matrix-of-a-unital-quantum-channel.md)

A [unital quantum channel](unital-quantum-channel.md) mixes its input spectrum with a [doubly stochastic matrix](doubly-stochastic-matrix.md), so its output spectrum satisfies the [majorization](majorization.md) relation with its input spectrum. The reverse inequality is generally false: a [pure state](pure-state.md) can be sent to a maximally mixed state. The direction expresses the fact that unital noise can make eigenvalues more uniform. Equality of the two spectra is possible, for example under a unitary channel.
