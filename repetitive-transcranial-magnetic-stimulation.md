# Repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation

↑ **Parent:** [Transcranial magnetic stimulation](transcranial-magnetic-stimulation.md)

Repeated magnetic stimulation used to perturb cortical activity or investigate changes after a stimulation intervention. The physiological effect and network spread depend on site, state and protocol; a nominally inhibitory manipulation does not guarantee a purely local reduction.
