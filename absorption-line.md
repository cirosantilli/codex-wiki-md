# Absorption line

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)

An [absorption line](absorption-line.md) is a deficit of observed spectral [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) relative to the surrounding continuum. In a photosphere with [temperature](temperature.md) falling outward, increased line [opacity](opacity.md) makes photons emerge from cooler layers than the continuum. For an absorption-dominated [LTE](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) source, the [Planck function](planck-function.md) then supplies a smaller line [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md). A foreground layer with source below the incident continuum gives another direct transfer description.
