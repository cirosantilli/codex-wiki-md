# Characteristic three

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md)

A [field](field.md) has characteristic three when $1+1+1=0$. The binomial identity then becomes $(a+b)^3=a^3+b^3$, and $X^3-1=(X-1)^3$. Thus a [cyclic shift operator](cyclic-shift-operator.md) on three coordinates has a repeated-root [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) in this characteristic and cannot be diagonalizable.
