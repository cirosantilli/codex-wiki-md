# Wolff-Kishner reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Carbonyl reduction](carbonyl-reduction.md)

Conversion of an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) or [ketone](ketone.md) [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) into a methylene through hydrazone formation and base-promoted [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) extrusion. The strongly basic, often heated conditions must be compatible with the other functional groups.
