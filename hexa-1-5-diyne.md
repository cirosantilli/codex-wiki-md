<h1 id="hexa-1-5-diyne">Hexa-1,5-diyne</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alkyne](alkyne.md)

The [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) $\mathrm{HC\equiv C-CH_2-CH_2-C\equiv CH}$. Its triple-bond fragments are linear, but the entire [carbon](carbon.md) skeleton need not be. The extended anti conformation has an [inversion centre](inversion-centre.md) at the central bond midpoint and belongs to the [C2h molecular point group](c2h-molecular-point-group.md).
