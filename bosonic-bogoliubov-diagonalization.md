# Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

For real even coefficients with $A_k>|B_k|$, the quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md)

$$
H=\sum_k\left[A_ka_k^\dagger a_k+\frac{B_k}{2}(a_ka_{-k}+a_k^\dagger a_{-k}^\dagger)\right]
$$

is diagonalized by the [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) $a_k=\cosh\theta_k\,\alpha_k-\sinh\theta_k\,\alpha_{-k}^\dagger$, with $\tanh2\theta_k=B_k/A_k$. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) follow from $\cosh^2\theta_k-\sinh^2\theta_k=1$. The diagonal form is $\sum_k[\omega_k\alpha_k^\dagger\alpha_k+(\omega_k-A_k)/2]$, with $\omega_k=\sqrt{A_k^2-B_k^2}$. The original-mode vacuum depletion is $\sinh^2\theta_k=(A_k/\omega_k-1)/2$. At $A_k=|B_k|$, the exact zero mode requires separate infrared treatment.
