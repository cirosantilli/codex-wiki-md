# Optical pupil

↑ **Parent:** [Aperture stop](aperture-stop.md)

An [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) is an image of the [aperture stop](aperture-stop.md). The [entrance pupil](entrance-pupil.md) is its image viewed through optics before the stop; the exit [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) is its image through the optics after it. [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) reimaging maps the telescope aperture onto devices such as a [deformable mirror](deformable-mirror.md).
