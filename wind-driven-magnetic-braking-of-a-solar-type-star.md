# Wind-driven magnetic braking of a solar-type star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

A magnetized [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) extracts angular momentum through matter transport and [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md). The [Alfvén radius](alfven-radius.md) supplies an effective lever arm larger than the stellar radius, with geometry factors absorbed into an averaged lever arm. [Dynamo action](dynamo-action.md) couples the torque to rotation by supplying the magnetic field. For a simplified radial wind of fixed speed, $R_A^2$ scales as $B_*^2R_*^4/(\mu_0\dot M_wv_w)$, so unsaturated $B_*\propto\Omega$ gives a torque proportional to $-\Omega^3$. Magnetic topology and wind thermodynamics can alter this scaling.
