# Discrete antiderivative

↑ **Parent:** [Forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md)

A discrete antiderivative of $f$ is a function $F$ satisfying $\Delta F=f$. [Pascal's identity](pascal-s-rule.md) gives

$$
\Delta\binom nr=\binom n{r-1}.
$$
