# Skew Hilbert basis theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem](noncommutative-hilbert-basis-theorem.md)

For an [automorphism](automorphism.md) $\sigma$ of a [left Noetherian ring](left-noetherian-ring.md) $A$, the [skew polynomial ring of an automorphism](skew-polynomial-ring-of-an-automorphism.md) $A[t;\sigma]$ is left [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md). For a [left ideal](left-ideal.md) $L$, the normalized leading-coefficient ideals $I_n=\{\sigma^{-n}(a_n):\sum_{j=0}^n a_jt^j\in L\}$ form an ascending chain of [left ideals](left-ideal.md) of $A$. Choose finite generating lifts through the stabilization degree. Every element of $L$ has its leading coefficient cancelled by a suitable left multiple of one of these lifts, and induction on degree proves finite generation. Passing to the [skew Laurent polynomial ring](skew-laurent-polynomial-ring.md) preserves the property by clearing negative powers. The right-sided statement follows by applying the argument to the [opposite ring](opposite-ring.md).
