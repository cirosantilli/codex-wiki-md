<h1 id="goodstein-s-theorem">Goodstein's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Goodstein sequence](goodstein-sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodstein's_theorem)

Every [Goodstein sequence](goodstein-sequence.md) reaches zero. Before zero, the [ordinal rank of a Goodstein term](ordinal-rank-of-a-goodstein-term.md) of each step is strictly smaller than the preceding one. An infinite [sequence](sequence.md) would contradict [well-foundedness](well-founded-relation.md) of the [ordinals](ordinal.md) below [epsilon zero](epsilon-zero.md).
