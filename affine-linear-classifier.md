# Affine linear classifier

↑ **Parent:** [Classifier](classifier.md)

An [affine linear classifier](affine-linear-classifier.md) assigns binary labels according to the sign of an [affine function](affine-function.md). Its boundary is an [affine hyperplane](affine-hyperplane.md), and its positive region is a [closed half-space](closed-half-space.md) under the displayed convention. On $\mathbb R^d$ the full class has [VC dimension](vc-dimension.md) $d+1$: arbitrary signs interpolate on $d+1$ [affinely independent](affine-independence.md) points, while the [intersecting convex hull partition theorem](radon-s-theorem.md) prevents shattering any $d+2$ points. Requiring $b=0$ gives the homogeneous case and a different [VC dimension](vc-dimension.md).
