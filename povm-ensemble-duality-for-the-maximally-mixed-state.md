<h1 id="povm-ensemble-duality-for-the-maximally-mixed-state">POVM–ensemble duality for the maximally mixed state</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator-valued measure](positive-operator-valued-measure.md)

A [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) $\{E_i\}$ in dimension $d$ corresponds to a [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md) with $p_i=\operatorname{Tr}(E_i)/d$ and $\rho_i=E_i/\operatorname{Tr}(E_i)$, omitting zero effects. Its average is the [maximally mixed state](maximally-mixed-state.md) $I/d$. Conversely any ensemble with that average defines a POVM by $E_i=dp_i\rho_i$. These transformations are inverse. A [pure POVM](pure-positive-operator-valued-measure.md) corresponds exactly to a pure-state ensemble decomposition of $I/d$.
