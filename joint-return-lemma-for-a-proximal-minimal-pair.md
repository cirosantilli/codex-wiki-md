# Joint return lemma for a proximal minimal pair

↑ **Parent:** [Proximality](proximality.md)

For a compact [metric space](metric-space.md) with a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $T$, suppose $y$ is a [minimal point](minimal-point.md) and $x,y$ are [proximal](proximality.md). Every [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ of $y$ admits arbitrarily large $n$ with $T^nx,T^ny\in U$. First shrink $U$ to an open [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md), then choose an open $V$ with $y\in V$ and $\overline V\subseteq U$. Minimality and [compactness](compact-space.md) give a finite cover of the [orbit closure](orbit-closure.md) of $y$ by $T^{-j}V$, $0\leq j\leq J$. [Uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) of these finitely many iterates turns a sufficiently close [proximal](proximality.md) encounter into a common visit to $U$ after at most $J$ more steps. If the [proximal](proximality.md) encounters occur only at bounded times, a zero distance at some time makes the two future [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) coincide, and minimality then gives arbitrarily late common visits. Thus injectivity is not needed for this general lemma.
