# Jacobi cubic product identity

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi triple product](jacobi-triple-product.md)

The odd [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) modulo four has $\chi_4(2r+1)=(-1)^r$. The series $B=\tfrac12\sum_n n\chi_4(n)q^{n^2/8}$ is therefore $q^{1/8}$ times the displayed sum. The [character theta proof of the eta product](character-theta-proof-of-the-eta-product.md) gives $B=\eta^3$, proving the identity.
