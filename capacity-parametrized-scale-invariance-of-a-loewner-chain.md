# Capacity-parametrized scale invariance of a Loewner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md)

For a [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) with [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md) $2t$, scale invariance means $(\lambda^{-1}K_{\lambda^2t})_{t\geq0}$ has the original law for every $\lambda>0$. Its mapping-out maps are $\lambda^{-1}g_{\lambda^2t}(\lambda z)$ and its [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md) is $\lambda^{-1}U_{\lambda^2t}$. This follows by differentiating the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md). [Brownian scaling](brownian-scaling.md) gives this property for [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md).
