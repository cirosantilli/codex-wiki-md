# Free actions on even-dimensional spheres have order at most two

↑ **Parent:** [Free group action](free-group-action.md)

For every nonidentity element of a [free group action](free-group-action.md) on $S^{2r}$, its action map has no fixed point and hence degree minus one by [fixed-point-free sphere maps are homotopic to the antipodal map](fixed-point-free-sphere-maps-are-homotopic-to-the-antipodal-map.md). Degree of homeomorphisms defines a homomorphism into $\{1,-1\}$, whose kernel is trivial, since no nonidentity action map can have degree one. Thus the acting group has order at most two. For $S^0$, freeness on its two points gives the same conclusion directly.
