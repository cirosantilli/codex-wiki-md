# Metal coating thickness relative to skin depth

↑ **Parent:** [Skin effect](skin-effect.md)

A coating substantially thinner than the [skin depth](skin-depth.md) does not screen the substrate as a bulk conductor would. Thickness of a few [skin depths](skin-depth.md) suppresses the field reaching the substrate exponentially. A unique required thickness additionally needs an allowed residual field or loss criterion.
