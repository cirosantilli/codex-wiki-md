# Finite-codimensional weak-star dense dual subspace is norming

↑ **Parent:** [Norming subspace of a dual space](norming-subspace-of-a-dual-space.md)

A norm-closed finite-codimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $Y\subseteq X^*$ that is weak-star dense is a [norming subspace](norming-subspace-of-a-dual-space.md). Its annihilator $F=Y^\perp\subseteq X^{**}$ is finite-dimensional and disjoint from $JX$. The positive distance $d$ of the unit sphere from $F$ gives a triple-dual [linear functional](linear-functional.md) vanishing on $F$ and taking a value at least $d$ at a unit vector. Apply the [finite-dimensional interpolation form of Goldstine's theorem](finite-dimensional-interpolation-form-of-goldstine-s-theorem.md) to the [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X^*$, then normalize and take a supremum to obtain norming constant $d$.
