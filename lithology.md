# Lithology

↑ **Parent:** [Petrology](petrology.md)

The observable physical and compositional character of [rock](rock-geology.md), including its constituent [minerals](mineral.md), texture and grain size. Different [lithologies](lithology.md) can have distinct [spectral reflectance](spectral-reflectance.md), [density](density.md) and [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md), making geological mapping possible through several independent measurements.
