# Zero-momentum renormalized mass

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

In a specified scalar-field normalisation, one can define a renormalized squared mass by $R=\widetilde\Gamma^{(2)}(0)$. Its value depends on the renormalization prescription and is not automatically the pole mass in a theory with momentum-dependent [self-energy](self-energy.md). A reference propagator $(p^2+R)^{-1}$ and [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md) then organise perturbation theory around that prescription.
