# Finite-record Fourier covariance bound

↑ **Parent:** [Periodogram](periodogram.md)

For a centered stationary real [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) sequence, let $A_T$ and $B_T$ be its cosine and sine coefficients with normalization $1/\sqrt{\pi T}$ at a nonzero Fourier frequency. Grouping the double [covariance](covariance.md) sum by lag and extending each lag sum to a whole record leaves at most $2k$ omitted bounded products. The full cosine-sine sum is zero, giving the bound. If $\sum k|\gamma_k|<\infty$, the two coefficients become asymptotically uncorrelated, and their limiting joint [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) consequently has [independent](independent-random-variables.md) components.
