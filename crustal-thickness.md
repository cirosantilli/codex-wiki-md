# Crustal thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary crust](planetary-crust.md)

The distance from a body's surface to its [crust-mantle boundary](mohorovicic-discontinuity.md), under a stated geometric convention. It differs from [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) thickness and [effective elastic thickness](effective-elastic-thickness.md). Converting a vertical column thickness to a layer thickness normal to a dipping interface requires its geometry.
