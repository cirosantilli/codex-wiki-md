# Dividend

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dividend)

A [dividend](dividend.md) is a payment to the holder of a financial asset in addition to changes in its quoted price. Under a [continuous dividend yield](continuous-dividend-yield.md) $\delta$, one stock unit produces cash $\delta S_tdt$, so its total gains are $dS_t+\delta S_tdt$. A [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) must include this cash in its budget equation.
