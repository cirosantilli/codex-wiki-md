# Rate-zonal centrifugation

↑ **Parent:** [Density-gradient centrifugation](density-gradient-centrifugation.md)

[Rate-zonal centrifugation](rate-zonal-centrifugation.md) resolves an initially narrow sample zone by differences in [sedimentation coefficient](sedimentation-coefficient.md) during a finite interval. Its bands remain transient: extending the interval can carry material into a [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md). Thus a [density gradient](density-gradient.md) need not imply an equilibrium [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md) measurement.[Types of Density Gradients, Rate Zonal Centrifugation and Table 1: migration rates before equilibrium or complete sedimentation](https://www.beckman.com/resources/technologies/centrifugation/density-gradient-centrifugation/)
