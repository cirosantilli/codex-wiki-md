# Angle between vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Dot product](dot-product.md)

For nonzero real [vectors](vector.md) in a [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), their [angle](angle.md) $\theta\in[0,\pi]$ is determined by the displayed [dot product](dot-product.md) formula. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) puts the quotient in $[-1,1]$, so the inverse cosine is defined. Perpendicular [vectors](vector.md) have angle $\pi/2$, parallel vectors pointing the same way have angle zero, and those pointing in opposite directions have angle $\pi$. A zero vector has no uniquely defined direction and hence no angle by this definition.
