# Cartesian theory

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A Cartesian theory uses a many-sorted [first-order signature](first-order-signature.md) and sequents between [Cartesian formulas](cartesian-formula.md). The fragment contains equality, atomic relations, truth, finite conjunction and existential quantification with provably unique witnesses. It has a [Cartesian syntactic category](cartesian-syntactic-category.md) with finite limits, whose [finite-limit-preserving functors](finite-limit-preserving-functor.md) classify its internal models. Arbitrary existential quantification, disjunction and falsity are not added as finite-limit constructors.
