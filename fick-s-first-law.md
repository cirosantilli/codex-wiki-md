<h1 id="fick-s-first-law">Fick's first law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)

[Fick's laws](fick-s-laws.md) describe diffusion through a flux law and [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md). The first law says that [diffusive flux](diffusive-flux.md) points down the concentration [gradient](gradient.md):

$$
\mathbf J=-D(C)\nabla C.
$$

Combining it with local conservation gives  
$C_t=\nabla\cdot(D(C)\nabla C)$.
