# Fixed points of a damped first-order exterior resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Damped first-order resonant complex eccentricity](damped-first-order-resonant-complex-eccentricity.md)

For positive locally constant $A,C$, supplement [damped first-order resonant complex eccentricity](damped-first-order-resonant-complex-eccentricity.md) by $\dot a/a=-2(p+1)Ce\sin\phi-4A/5$. Balancing the two dissipative rates gives $e_f^2=2/[5(p+1)]$, $\sin\phi_f=-Ae_f/C$ and $Bx_f=-(C/e_f)\cos\phi_f$. Two fixed points exist when $A<C\sqrt{5(p+1)/2}$. The negative-cosine branch has $x_f>0$ and is stable in this leading model: a phase increase raises the positive resonant [torque](torque.md), raises [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md), lowers [mean motion](mean-motion.md), and reverses the phase drift. The positive-cosine branch has the opposite feedback.

For a coupled verification put $z=u+iv$. The [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) at either fixed point is $\lambda^3+2A\lambda^2+[A^2+B^2x_f^2-2B(p+1)Cu_f]\lambda-4AB(p+1)Cu_f$. For $u_f<0$ its positive coefficients satisfy the cubic [Routh-Hurwitz stability criterion](routh-hurwitz-stability-criterion.md), since the remaining inequality reduces to $2A(A^2+B^2x_f^2)>0$. For $u_f>0$ its negative constant term forces a positive real root. At the equality threshold the equilibria merge and an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is zero. This is local stability for the frozen smooth-coefficient approximation, not a conclusion for arbitrary rapidly varying dissipation laws.
