# Kernel squares in an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

In a commutative diagram of two kernel rows, a monic arrow between the final objects makes the square between their kernels and middle objects a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md). The kernel [universal property](universal-property.md) factors any [categorical cone](cone-over-a-diagram.md) uniquely. Conversely, if the square between middle and final objects is a pullback, the induced arrow between their [kernels in a category](kernel-in-a-category.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), since both kernels have the same [universal property](universal-property.md) after pullback.
