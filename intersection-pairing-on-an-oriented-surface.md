# Intersection pairing on an oriented surface

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

Represent one-dimensional [homology classes](homology-class.md) on a closed oriented [topological surface](topological-surface.md) by transverse oriented curves and sum their signed crossing points. This gives a [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) that is skew-symmetric. A symplectic handle basis has matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&I_g\\-I_g&0\end{pmatrix}$, which is unimodular. In particular, a class detected with intersection $1$ or $-1$ by another integral class is a [primitive homology class](primitive-homology-class.md): the detecting homomorphism splits its rank-one subgroup.
