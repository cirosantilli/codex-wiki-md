<h1 id="extreme-points-of-the-dual-unit-ball-of-c-k">Extreme points of the dual unit ball of C(K)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Banach–Stone theorem](banach-stone-theorem.md)

For complex $C(K)$ on a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md), the [extreme points](extreme-point.md) of its dual unit ball are precisely unimodular multiples of [Dirac measures](dirac-measure.md). A measure whose [variation measure](variation-measure.md) has mass on two disjoint sets splits as a nontrivial [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of normalized restrictions and is not extreme. This description is the key to the [Banach–Stone theorem](banach-stone-theorem.md).
