# Isothermal-galaxy momentum-balance shell threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum-driven outflow](momentum-driven-outflow.md)

A swept gas shell of fraction $f$ at radius $R$ in a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) of one-dimensional dispersion $\sigma$ has mass $2f\sigma^2R/G$. Interior non-gas gravity supplies weight $4f(1-f)\sigma^4/G$. Fully intercepted single-scattering Eddington [momentum](momentum-split.md) overcomes it when $M_{\rm BH}>f(1-f)\sigma^4\sigma_T/(\pi m_pG^2)$. This neglects shell [self-gravity](self-gravity.md), central-hole gravity and stored outward [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md); weaker coupling raises the required [luminosity](luminosity.md).
