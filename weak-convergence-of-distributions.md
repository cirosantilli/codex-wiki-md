# Weak convergence of distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution (mathematical analysis)](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md)

A sequence of distributions $u_j$ converges weakly to $u$ when $\langle u_j,\varphi\rangle\to\langle u,\varphi\rangle$ for every [test function](test-function.md) $\varphi$.
