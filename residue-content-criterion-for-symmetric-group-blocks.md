# Residue-content criterion for symmetric-group blocks

↑ **Parent:** [Block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md)

For shapes of the same size, equal residue-content multiplicities are equivalent to equal [cores of a partition](core-of-a-partition.md) at modulus $p$. Removing a rim $p$-hook subtracts one of each residue. With the number of [beta numbers](beta-number-of-a-partition.md) divisible by $p$, the packed-runner charges are $q_r=c_r-c_{r+1}$, so the multiplicities recover the core. [Symmetric polynomials](symmetric-polynomial.md) in [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](jucys-murphy-element.md) act by their evaluations on these residues, and generate the modular center. Equality of its [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) is precisely the block-equivalence criterion for [simple modules](irreducible-module.md).
