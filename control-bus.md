# Control bus

↑ **Parent:** [Shared computer bus](shared-computer-bus.md)

The [computer bus](computer-bus.md) paths specifying operations and coordinating progress, including requests and acknowledgments. Some control travels from initiator to target and some returns. In the diagram, [bus arbitration](bus-arbitration.md) requests and grants are shown separately from transfer control to distinguish choosing an initiator from executing its [bus transaction](bus-transaction.md).[CS152 lecture 21, slides 22 and 33–36: control signals, sequencing, request and grant](cs152-computer-architecture-and-engineering-fall-1999/lecture-21-buses-and-i-and-o.md)
