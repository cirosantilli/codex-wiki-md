# Central commutator has no torsion from an abelian Sylow subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant character](determinant-character.md)

If a [Sylow p-subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) $P$ is abelian, a hypothetical central subgroup $Q\leq G'$ of order $p$ has a nontrivial linear character extending to $P$. Inducing this extension to $G$ gives a character of degree prime to $p$, hence an irreducible constituent of such degree. On $Q$ its [determinant character](determinant-character.md) is a nontrivial power of the chosen linear character, but every determinant character is trivial on $G'$. This contradiction proves the displayed restriction.
