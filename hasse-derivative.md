# Hasse derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse_derivative)

The Hasse derivatives of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) are the coefficients in $f(x+t)=\sum_{\ell\geq0}f^{[\ell]}(x)t^\ell$. A point $a$ has [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) at least $m$ exactly when $f^{[\ell]}(a)=0$ for $0\leq\ell<m$. Unlike iterated [formal derivatives](formal-derivative.md) divided by [factorials](factorial.md), this definition works in positive [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md), where factorials can vanish. For example, in characteristic two $(x+1)^2$ has zero first [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md) but nonzero second Hasse derivative.
