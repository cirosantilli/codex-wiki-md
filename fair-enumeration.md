# Fair enumeration

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

A [fair enumeration](fair-enumeration.md) emits every required occurrence after finitely many computation steps. The inputs can be infinite; completeness does not mean waiting until they have all been exhausted. If two [proper lazy lists](proper-lazy-list.md) alternate their next available entries, every occurrence reaches the output after finitely many swaps. In contrast, a nested [list comprehension](list-comprehension.md) can remain forever in its first infinite inner [list](list-abstract-data-type.md). Fairness concerns input occurrences, even when different occurrences happen to have equal values.
