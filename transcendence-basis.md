# Transcendence basis

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic independence](algebraic-independence.md)

A transcendence basis of a [field extension](field-extension.md) $L/k$ is an algebraically independent set $T$ such that $L/k(T)$ is algebraic. Its cardinality is the [transcendence degree](transcendence-degree.md). For finitely generated extensions it is finite, and the remaining algebraic extension is finite.
