# Central charge of reparameterization ghosts

↑ **Parent:** [bc system](bc-system.md)

The [worldsheet ghost fields](worldsheet-ghost-field.md) form a [bc system](bc-system.md) with [conformal weights](conformal-weight.md) $2$ and $-1$. Setting $J=2$ in $c=-2(6J^2-6J+1)$ gives [central charge](central-charge.md) $-26$ in each chirality. The [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) of $D$ free embedding bosons cancels at $D=26$.
