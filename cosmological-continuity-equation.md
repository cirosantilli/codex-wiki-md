# Cosmological continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

In [natural units](natural-units.md), conservation of the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) for a homogeneous [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) in an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) gives the displayed equation for its [energy density](energy-density.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). It is equivalent to $d(\rho a^3)=-P\,d(a^3)$. For a constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w=P/\rho$, separation gives $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$. If $\rho$ instead denotes [mass density](density.md), the pressure term is $P/c^2$.

This is local [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md); it does not in general define a globally conserved total [energy](energy-split.md) for an expanding universe. The [adiabatic work in a comoving volume](adiabatic-work-in-a-comoving-volume.md) makes this distinction explicit.
