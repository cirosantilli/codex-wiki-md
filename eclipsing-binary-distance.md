# Eclipsing-binary distance

↑ **Parent:** [Eclipsing binary](eclipsing-binary.md)

For a detached double-lined binary, [radial velocities](radial-velocity.md) supply an orbital length scale times $\sin i$, while eclipse geometry supplies inclination and relative radii. Together they determine physical [stellar radii](stellar-radius.md). Comparing them with angular radii from a calibrated surface-brightness/colour relation gives distance. The orbital scale is geometric, but the angular-radius [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md), [extinction](extinction.md) and third light add astrophysical assumptions.[Nature 495, 76–79; eight late-type systems](an-eclipsing-binary-distance-to-the-large-magellanic-cloud-accurate-to-two-per-cent-by-g-pietrzynski-and-others.md)
