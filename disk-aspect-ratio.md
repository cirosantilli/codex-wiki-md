# Disk aspect ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Disk scale height](disk-scale-height.md)

The aspect ratio of an [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md) is its vertical thickness scale divided by cylindrical radius. A [thin disk](thin-disk.md) has $H/r\ll1$. In gas-supported vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) with harmonic external gravity $-\Omega_z^2z$, $H/r\sim c_s/(r\Omega_z)$, where $\Omega_z$ is the [vertical epicyclic frequency](vertical-epicyclic-frequency.md) and $c_s$ is the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md). In a centrally dominated [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md), $\Omega_z=\Omega$, so this compares [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) with orbital speed. If disk self-gravity supplies the vertical restoring force, the relation $H\sim c_s/\Omega$ requires replacement. A polytropic free-surface height and an isothermal scale height differ by model-dependent constants even though their aspect ratios have the same basic role.
