# Quantum state exclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state discrimination](quantum-state-discrimination.md)

A task in which a [quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md) outputs a label of a preparation that can be ruled out. If preparations are $\rho_j$ and the outcome labelled $j$ has effect $E_j$, error-free exclusion requires $\operatorname{Tr}(E_j\rho_j)=0$ for every $j$, together with $E_j\geq0$ and $\sum_jE_j=I$. A family admitting a complete error-free exclusion measurement consists of [antidistinguishable quantum states](antidistinguishable-quantum-states.md). Exclusion need not identify the actual preparation.
