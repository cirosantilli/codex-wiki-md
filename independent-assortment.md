# Independent assortment

↑ **Parent:** [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md)

[Independent assortment](independent-assortment.md) between two diallelic [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) means that the transmitted [allele](allele.md) at one [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) is independent of that at the other, conditional on the parental states. In the ordinary two-locus linkage model this has [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) $1/2$, with each of four combinations equally likely for a parent [heterozygous](heterozygosity.md) at both [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md).
