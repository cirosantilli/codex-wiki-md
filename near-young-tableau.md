# Near Young tableau

↑ **Parent:** [Young tableau](young-tableau.md)

A near Young tableau fills a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) with distinct entries from a totally ordered alphabet, increasing along rows and down columns. Its entries need not be the initial interval $1,\ldots,n$. It is the natural intermediate object for [row insertion](row-insertion.md) of a new entry.
