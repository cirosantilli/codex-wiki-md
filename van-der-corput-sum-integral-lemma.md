# Van der Corput sum-integral lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential sum](exponential-sum.md)

For a real $C^1$ phase with continuous [monotone](monotonic-function.md) derivative and $|f'|\le\delta<1$, the sum-integral discrepancy has the displayed uniform bound. Periodization and the [Dirichlet-Jordan convergence theorem](dirichlet-jordan-convergence-theorem.md) express it as symmetric nonzero [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives endpoint terms and reciprocal-derivative variations. Monotonicity bounds their total variation by $\sum_{h\ne0}2\delta/(h^2-\delta^2)=O((1-\delta)^{-1})$. Pairing the leading $1/h$ endpoint terms reduces them to the uniformly bounded sine series. The exclusion of integer nonzero frequencies is essential.
