# Thermal occupation of a quantum well

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum well](quantum-well.md)

For one nondegenerate thermalized particle, excited-state occupation relative to the [ground state](ground-state.md) is set by the excitation gap, not by the absolute [energy](energy-split.md) origin. In an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) of width $L$, the first gap is $3\pi^2\hbar^2/(2mL^2)$. Predominant ground-state occupation requires this gap to be much larger than $k_BT$; an explicit tolerated occupation gives a quantitative [temperature](temperature-split.md) criterion. State [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) adds its statistical weight.
