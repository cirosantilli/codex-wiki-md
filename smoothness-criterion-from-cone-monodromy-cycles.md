# Smoothness criterion from cone-monodromy cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Orbifold fundamental group](orbifold-fundamental-group.md)

Over a cone point of order $m$, a cycle of length $\ell$ in the [monodromy](monodromy.md) on sheets has ramification degree $\ell$ and residual cone angle $2\pi\ell/m$. The lifted inherited [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) is smooth precisely when every such cycle has length $m$. Equivalently the subgroup defining the cover meets no conjugate of a nonidentity power of a cone generator.
