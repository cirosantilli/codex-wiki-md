# Bubble-chamber track readout

↑ **Parent:** [Bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md)

[Stereoscopy](stereoscopy.md) enables three-dimensional [track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md).[How it works, views](how-it-works-bubble-chamber-cern-teaching-archive.md) Calibrated [camera](camera.md) positions and optical geometry are needed before a photographed arc becomes a physical [radius of curvature](radius-of-curvature.md). [Neutral particles](neutral-particle.md) do not directly leave the same continuous [ionization](ionization.md) trail as [charged particles](charged-particle.md); an interaction or decay can instead reveal them through charged secondary tracks. A [photon](photon.md)'s inferred path between interactions is not a photographed luminous line.

In a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), with negligible [stopping power](stopping-power.md) and [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) over the fitted segment, the transverse projection is a [circle](circle.md). The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) turns transverse [momentum](momentum-split.md) at angular rate $v_\perp/r$, giving

$$
\frac{p_\perp v_\perp}{r}=|q|v_\perp B,\qquad
p_\perp=|q|Br.
$$

Here $r$ is measured in the plane perpendicular to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The relation remains valid with relativistic [physical momentum](physical-momentum.md); it does not require $p=mv$. Thus curvature determines [magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md), not [mass](mass.md) or [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) alone. For chosen $|q|=e$, $B=1.5\,\mathrm T$ and $r=0.40\,\mathrm m$, $p_\perp\simeq0.180\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$. This is an invented example, not a fit to a historical photograph. Reconstructing a three-dimensional [helix](helix.md) also determines the component parallel to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); the transverse [radius of curvature](radius-of-curvature.md) alone does not. Determining the sign of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) additionally requires the field direction and the direction of travel, as in the [charge-sign ambiguity of a curved track](charge-sign-ambiguity-of-a-curved-track.md).
