# Faddeev-Popov operator

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md)

For a gauge condition $\chi[A]=0$, the linearization of $\chi[A+D\omega]$ in the gauge parameter is the Faddeev-Popov operator. Its determinant is represented by [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](faddeev-popov-ghost.md).
