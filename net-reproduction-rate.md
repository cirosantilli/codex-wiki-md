# Net reproduction rate

↑ **Parent:** [Age-structured population equation](age-structured-population-equation.md)

The mean lifetime number of offspring is the birth rate integrated against the probability of surviving to each age. For a continuous age model it is $\int_0^\infty b(a)\exp[-\int_0^a\mu(s)ds]da$. The [Euler-Lotka equation](euler-lotka-equation.md) compares this with one to locate positive population growth.
