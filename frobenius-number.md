# Frobenius number

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

For [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $a_1,\ldots,a_k$ with [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) $1$, and with every $a_i>1$, the largest [integer](integer-split.md) not expressible as a nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md) [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of the $a_i$. For two [coprime](coprime-integers.md) generators $a,b$, it equals $ab-a-b$.
