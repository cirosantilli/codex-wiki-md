# Escape speed, circular speed, and propulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Escape velocity](escape-velocity.md)

For a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md), [gravity](gravity.md) supplies [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md), giving $v_c^2/r=\mu/r^2$. Thus

$$
v_c=\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}},\qquad v_{\mathrm{esc}}=\sqrt2\,v_c,\qquad \varepsilon_c=-\frac{\mu}{2r}.
$$

A body in a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) is bound despite its continuous [free fall](free-fall.md). At the same $r$, escape needs twice its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per unit [mass](mass.md). An instantaneous forward tangential [velocity](velocity.md) increment from the [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) requires $\Delta v=(\sqrt2-1)v_c$, rather than the full [escape speed](escape-velocity.md) from rest. In the synthetic example this is exactly $2(\sqrt2-1)\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx0.828427\,\mathrm{m/s}$.[§13.4, “Circular Orbits” and “Energy in Circular Orbits”: centripetal balance and negative total energy](university-physics-volume-1-by-william-moebs-and-others/13-4-satellite-orbits-and-energy.md)

[Escape speed](escape-velocity.md) is a coasting threshold, not a required continuously maintained [speed](speed.md) or a measure of [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md). A powered vehicle can start at rest and acquire [energy](energy.md) gradually. The [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) relates an ideal [velocity](velocity.md) increment to [rocket exhaust speed](rocket-exhaust-speed.md) and [rocket mass ratio](rocket-mass-ratio.md); [rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md), [drag](drag-physics.md), and the timing and direction of propulsion affect a real ascent. Rotation also changes the relation between surface-relative [velocity](velocity.md) and the [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) used in the calculation.

Reaching another body is a finite-destination [spacecraft trajectory](spacecraft-trajectory.md) problem. In the synthetic radial example, a launch at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ reaches $r=4\,\mathrm m$ while remaining bound. A spacecraft can similarly encounter the [Moon](moon.md) on a bound geocentric transfer; actual planning must include the [Moon](moon.md)'s [gravity](gravity.md), target motion, arrival [velocity](velocity.md), and any propulsion needed for capture. Escaping [Earth](earth.md) alone does not imply escaping the [Sun](sun.md), because the relevant [reference frame](reference-frame.md) and attracting [mass](mass.md) have changed. The isolated formula describes one gravitational well; a full mission requires more than its scalar threshold.
