# Two-layer Sverdrup interior

↑ **Parent:** [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md)

For a uniform normalized upper-layer [wind stress curl](wind-stress-curl.md) $W$ and no lower-layer forcing, a uniform local [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md) is

$$
\bar\psi_1=Vx,\quad\bar\psi_2=0,\quad V=W/\beta.
$$

The upper layer flows meridionally and the lower layer rests. Although the relative [vorticity](vorticity.md) vanishes, the [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) has the zonal gradients $\bar q_{1,x}=-FV$, $\bar q_{2,x}=FV$. These are essential for the perturbation stability and do not vanish merely because the background velocity is uniform.
