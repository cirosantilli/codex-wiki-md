# Epsilon expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epsilon_expansion)

The epsilon expansion computes critical quantities perturbatively in $\epsilon=d_c-d$ below an [upper critical dimension](upper-critical-dimension.md) $d_c$. For scalar quartic theory, $d_c=4$ and the [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) coupling is of order $\epsilon$.
