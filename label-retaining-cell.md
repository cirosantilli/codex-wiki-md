# Label-retaining cell

↑ **Parent:** [Pulse-chase analysis](pulse-chase-analysis.md)

A cell retaining a dilution-sensitive label after a chase. Retention can reflect slow division, [terminal differentiation](terminal-differentiation.md) or assay properties; it is not by itself a functional stem-cell test.
