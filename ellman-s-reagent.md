↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellman's_reagent)

[Ellman's reagent](ellman-s-reagent.md) is 5,5'-dithiobis(2-nitrobenzoic acid), abbreviated [DTNB](ellman-s-reagent.md), a [disulfide](disulfide.md) used to quantify accessible [thiols](thiol.md). Reaction releases the colored TNB species measured by [absorbance](absorbance.md) at [wavelength](wavelength.md) $412\,\mathrm{nm}$. The [disulfide bond](disulfide-bond.md) experiment above explains why a [thiol](thiol.md) measurement can become an indirect bridge count only after controlled [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md).
