# Simple wave in magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Simple wave](simple-wave.md)

An ideal-MHD simple wave traces a one-parameter state-space curve whose tangent is a right [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the characteristic matrix. Its parameter obeys $q_t+v(q)q_x=0$ and its characteristic speed obeys the [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md). [Nonlinear Alfvén waves](nonlinear-alfven-wave.md) are a linearly degenerate example.
