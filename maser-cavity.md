# Maser cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Ammonia maser](ammonia-maser.md)

The maser cavity stores a resonant [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) so that an arriving [molecule](molecule-split.md) encounters a field left by earlier [molecules](molecule-split.md). An output coupling lets part of that stored [energy](energy-split.md) reach a detector. The same coupling also removes [energy](energy-split.md), so increasing extraction can prevent startup. The cavity provides feedback; the selected [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) supplies the useful internal [energy](energy-split.md).

Let $U$ be stored field [energy](energy-split.md), $\omega=2\pi\nu_0$, and $\kappa=\kappa_{\mathrm{int}}+\kappa_{\mathrm{out}}$ the total passive energy decay rate. Internal dissipation and output extraction contribute $\kappa_{\mathrm{int}}U$ and $\kappa_{\mathrm{out}}U$ to lost [power](power.md). With this energy convention, the loaded [quality factor](q-factor.md) is

$$
Q_{\mathrm L}=\frac{\omega U}{\kappa U}=\frac{\omega}{\kappa},
\qquad
\frac{1}{Q_{\mathrm L}}=\frac{1}{Q_{\mathrm{int}}}+\frac{1}{Q_{\mathrm{out}}}.
$$

The field amplitude decays at $\kappa/2$; confusing amplitude and [energy](energy-split.md) decay introduces a factor of two into a [maser oscillation threshold](maser-oscillation-threshold.md) calculation.
