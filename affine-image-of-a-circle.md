# Affine image of a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

Let a radius-$R$ [circle](circle.md) have center $c\in\mathbb R^2$, and apply the real [affine map](affine-map.md) $y=Mx+b$. If $M$ is invertible, substitute $x-c=M^{-1}(y-Mc-b)$ into $|x-c|^2=R^2$ to obtain the displayed [ellipse](ellipse.md). Its center is $Mc+b$ and its semiaxes are $R$ times the two [singular values](singular-value.md) of $M$, as follows from the [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md). A rotation or reflection preserves both semiaxes and hence gives another circle. If $M$ has rank one, the image is a segment; rank zero gives one point. This real-plane description also handles maps that stretch real and imaginary coordinates differently and are not holomorphic complex maps.
