# Local quadratic galaxy bias

↑ **Parent:** [Bias expansion](bias-expansion.md)

The simplest nonlinear local bias model is

$$
\delta_g=b_1\delta+\frac{b_2}{2}(\delta^2-\langle\delta^2\rangle).
$$

For Gaussian matter fluctuations, its leading galaxy three-point function is proportional to $b_1^2b_2$.
