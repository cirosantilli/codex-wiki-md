<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an integrable or approximately integrable background [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md), [action-angle variables](../../../../action-angle-variables.md) turn an otherwise complicated [stellar orbit](../../../../stellar-orbit.md) into $\dot{\mathbf J}=0$ and $\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}=\boldsymbol\Omega(\mathbf J)$. This has two useful consequences. First, a phase-mixed equilibrium can be represented directly as $f_0(\mathbf J)$: the [action variables](../../../../action-variable.md) label the unperturbed [orbits](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), while [angle variables](../../../../angle-variable.md) describe position along them. Second, the angles are periodic, so [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md) reduce unperturbed transport to multiplication by $i\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\Omega$ and make [orbital resonances](../../../../orbital-resonance.md) explicit. The [action variables](../../../../action-variable.md) also separate rapid orbital motion from slow secular changes. These advantages apply on regular orbital regions; a globally chaotic background need not admit global [action-angle variables](../../../../action-angle-variables.md).

The transformation is canonical, preserving the [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) measure and the stated total [mass](../../../../mass.md) $M=\int f\,d^3\theta\,d^3J$. The collisionless [Vlasov equation](../../../../vlasov-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\partial_tf+\{f,H\}_{\theta,J}=\partial_tf+\frac{\partial H}{\partial\mathbf J}\cdot\frac{\partial f}{\partial\boldsymbol\theta}-\frac{\partial H}{\partial\boldsymbol\theta}\cdot\frac{\partial f}{\partial\mathbf J}=0.}
$$

Here $H$ is the single-star specific [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md); the normalization of $f$ by [mass](../../../../mass.md) changes neither the [Poisson bracket](../../../../poisson-bracket.md) nor the transport equation.
