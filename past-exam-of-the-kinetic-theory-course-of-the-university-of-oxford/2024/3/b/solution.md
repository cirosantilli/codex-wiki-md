<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $H_0$ and $f_0$ depend only on the [action variables](../../../../action-variable.md), $\{f_0,H_0\}=0$. Drop the quadratic term $\{\delta f,\delta\Phi^{\mathrm{tot}}\}$ from the [Vlasov equation](../../../../vlasov-equation.md) in [a](../a.md). Defining the orbital [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) vector by $\boldsymbol\Omega(\mathbf J)=\partial H_0/\partial\mathbf J$, the linearized equation is

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\delta f+\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}\delta f-\frac{\partial f_0}{\partial\mathbf J}\cdot\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}\delta\Phi^{\mathrm{tot}}=0.}
$$

The sign of the driving term follows from the second term in the canonical [Poisson bracket](../../../../poisson-bracket.md), because the background has no angle dependence.
