<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The long-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) limit in [d](../d.md) is **[resonant heating](../../../../resonant-heating.md)**. Most electrons screen the slowly varying spatial field. The noise nevertheless excites collective [Langmuir waves](../../../../langmuir-wave.md), whose sharp temporal spectrum lies near $\omega=\pm\omega_{pe}$. Electrons with $v\simeq\omega/k$ remain in phase with a wave and exchange [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) with it. A stable tail absorbs net wave [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) through [Landau damping](../../../../landau-damping.md). The heating is initially deposited in narrow tail regions of [velocity space](../../../../velocity-space.md), even though the volume-integrated rate equals that in [c](../c.md).

The short-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) limit in [c](../c.md) is **[stochastic heating](../../../../stochastic-heating.md)** in the terminology intended here. With $\epsilon\simeq1$, the imposed [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is essentially unscreened. Its temporally uncorrelated kicks cause a random walk of electron [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), so $\langle(\Delta v)^2\rangle=2D_0\Delta t$. Electrons throughout the thermal population contribute to the resulting [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) increase; no narrow collective wave peak selects a small tail population.

Both mechanisms are driven by [white noise](../../../../white-noise.md), and the formal condition $\omega=kv$ enters the stationary [quasilinear diffusion](../../../../quasilinear-diffusion.md) calculation in both cases. The physical distinction is the sharp collective [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) in [d](../d.md) versus the broad direct random forcing in [c](../c.md), rather than the mere presence of randomness. In collisionless theory this heating is an increase of the averaged [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md); subsequent [collisions](../../../../collision.md) can establish [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md).
