<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [action-angle variables](../../../../action-angle-variables.md) $(\boldsymbol\theta,\mathbf J)$ are [canonical coordinates](../../../../canonical-variables.md), with $[\theta_a,J_b]=\delta_{ab}$. In the convention $\dot g=\partial_tg+[g,H]$, their [Poisson bracket](../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is

$$
\boxed{[g,h]=\sum_{a=1}^3\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial\theta_a}\frac{\partial h}{\partial J_a}-\frac{\partial g}{\partial J_a}\frac{\partial h}{\partial\theta_a}\right)
=\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}g\cdot\partial_{\mathbf J}h-\partial_{\mathbf J}g\cdot\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}h.}
$$

In particular, [Hamilton's equations](../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) read $\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}=\partial_{\mathbf J}H$ and $\dot{\mathbf J}=-\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}H$, fixing the sign needed below.
