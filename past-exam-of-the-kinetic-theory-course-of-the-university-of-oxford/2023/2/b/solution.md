<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

There is **no [Landau damping](../../../../landau-damping.md)** of this branch within the completely degenerate collisionless model. Its [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) obeys $\omega/k>V_e>V_i$, while each parallel [marginal distribution](../../../../marginal-distribution.md) vanishes outside $|u|\leq V_\alpha$. There are no particles at the resonant [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), and $F_\alpha'(\omega/k)=0$. The [Landau contour](../../../../landau-contour.md) consequently contributes no imaginary part to the [dielectric function](../../../../dielectric-function.md); a real root is allowed with $\boxed{\gamma=0}$.

If a small nonzero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) supplied a tail beyond the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md), the conclusion would change: the resonant derivative would generally be nonzero. For the convention $p=\gamma-i\omega$ and a simple weakly damped root, expanding the continued [dielectric function](../../../../dielectric-function.md) would give $\gamma=-\epsilon_I(\omega,k)/\partial_\omega\epsilon_R(\omega,k)$. This conditional formula is not needed to manufacture [damping](../../../../damping.md) for the exact cutoff. Neither collisional attenuation nor nonlinear evolution is included in the given [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md).
