<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Retain $K=k^2\lambda_{\mathrm{De}}^2$ while making the same narrow-[velocity plateau](../../../../velocity-plateau.md) expansion as in [d](../d.md). With $u=v_1+\delta a$, the factor that varies rapidly across the plateau is $(v_2-u)/(u-v_1)=(1-a)/a$. The other factors vary slowly and may be evaluated at $u=v_1$, giving

$$
\frac{v_1^2}{v_0^2-v_1^2}\frac{1-a}{a}=e^K.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\mathrm a}\approx k\left[v_1+\frac{v_2-v_1}{1+e^{k^2\lambda_{\mathrm{De}}^2}(v_0^2-v_1^2)/v_1^2}\right].}
$$

At $K\ll1$, the denominator tends to $v_0^2/v_1^2$, recovering [d](../d.md). At $K\gg1$,

$$
u_{\mathrm a}-v_1\approx\delta\frac{v_1^2}{v_0^2-v_1^2}e^{-K},
$$

recovering the exponentially close approach to the plateau edge in [c](../c.md). The [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) remains strictly inside the [velocity plateau](../../../../velocity-plateau.md) for every finite $k$.

As an asymptotic expansion, the formula takes $\delta\to0$ at fixed $0<v_1/v_0<1$. If the plateau also approaches the outer edge, so that $v_0-v_1$ is comparable to $\delta$, the factors treated as slowly varying need not be slowly varying. In that additional limiting regime, use the exact [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) in [b](../b.md) instead.
