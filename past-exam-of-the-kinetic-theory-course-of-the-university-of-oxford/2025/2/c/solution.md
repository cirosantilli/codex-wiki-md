<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $K=k^2\lambda_{\mathrm{De}}^2\gg1$, the logarithm in [b](../b.md) must be large and positive. In each allowed interval this happens near a denominator zero, approached from the undamped side:

$$
u_-=-v_0-O(e^{-K}),\qquad u_{\mathrm{a}}=v_1+O(e^{-K}),\qquad u_+=V+O(e^{-K}).
$$

The constants hidden by $O(e^{-K})$ have [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) units. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\omega_-\sim-kv_0,\qquad \omega_{\mathrm{a}}\sim kv_1,\qquad \omega_+\sim k(v_0+v_2-v_1).}
$$

The two outer modes have [phase velocities](../../../../phase-velocity.md) just outside the occupied range and therefore have no resonant particles. The middle [electron acoustic wave](../../../../electron-acoustic-wave.md) lies just inside the lower edge of the [velocity plateau](../../../../velocity-plateau.md), where $f_0'=0$. All three escape [Landau damping](../../../../landau-damping.md) in this collisionless model. The stated neglect of ion response remains necessary for the middle mode as well.
