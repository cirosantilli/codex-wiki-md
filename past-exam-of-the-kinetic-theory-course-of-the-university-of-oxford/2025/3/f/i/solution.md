<h1 id="3/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $k_R=0$, the detuning is $\omega_{m0}=m(\Omega_\varphi-\Omega_{\mathrm p})$. The [Gaussian resonance broadening](../../../../../gaussian-resonance-broadening.md) factor selects the [corotation resonance](../../../../../corotation-resonance.md), where the star's azimuthal [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) matches the [pattern speed](../../../../../pattern-speed.md). The derivative driving the flux is just $m\partial f_0/\partial J_\varphi$. In fact this contribution alone has the form

$$
\Delta F_0\big|_{k_R=0}=\partial_{J_\varphi}\left[\frac{\sqrt{8\pi^5}\,m^2}{\Gamma}\mathcal R_\Gamma(\omega_{m0})\int_0^\infty|u_{m0}|^2\partial_{J_\varphi}f_0\,dJ_R\right].
$$

A single [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) harmonic changes the [action variables](../../../../../action-variable.md) in the direction $(m,k_R)$, because $\dot{\mathbf J}=-i\mathbf k\delta\phi_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\theta}$ plus its conjugate. For this harmonic, $\Delta J_R=0$: stars change [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and shift their [epicyclic guiding center](../../../../../epicyclic-guiding-center.md) without a leading-order increase of random radial motion. This is [stellar radial migration](../../../../../stellar-radial-migration.md) driven by the [corotation resonance](../../../../../corotation-resonance.md). The resonance condition explains the absence of leading heating: $\Delta E\simeq\Omega_\varphi\Delta J_\varphi=\Omega_{\mathrm p}\Delta J_\varphi$ at the [corotation resonance](../../../../../corotation-resonance.md).
