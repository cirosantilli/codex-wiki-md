<h1 id="3/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $k_R=\pm1$, the selected [orbital resonances](../../../../../orbital-resonance.md) satisfy

$$
m(\Omega_\varphi-\Omega_{\mathrm p})\pm\Omega_R=0.
$$

For near-circular [stellar orbits](../../../../../stellar-orbit.md), $\Omega_R$ is the [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md). Thus $k_R=-1$ is the [inner Lindblad resonance](../../../../../inner-lindblad-resonance.md), $m(\Omega_\varphi-\Omega_{\mathrm p})=\Omega_R$, and $k_R=+1$ is the [outer Lindblad resonance](../../../../../outer-lindblad-resonance.md), $m(\Omega_\varphi-\Omega_{\mathrm p})=-\Omega_R$.

The flux is driven by $m\partial_{J_\varphi}f_0\pm\partial_{J_R}f_0$, and each resonant harmonic exchanges both [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and [radial action](../../../../../radial-action.md) in the ratio

$$
\boxed{\Delta J_R=\frac{k_R}{m}\Delta J_\varphi.}
$$

These terms can therefore produce [galactic disk heating](../../../../../galactic-disk-heating.md) as well as [angular momentum transport](../../../../../angular-momentum-transport.md). In the usual absorbing response, stars losing [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) at the [inner Lindblad resonance](../../../../../inner-lindblad-resonance.md) gain [radial action](../../../../../radial-action.md), and stars gaining [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) at the [outer Lindblad resonance](../../../../../outer-lindblad-resonance.md) also gain [radial action](../../../../../radial-action.md). The actual direction of flux follows from the displayed derivatives of $f_0$; it is not fixed by the resonance condition alone. A finite pulse replaces exact [Lindblad resonance](../../../../../lindblad-resonance.md) selection by a band of detunings of width of order $1/\tau$.
