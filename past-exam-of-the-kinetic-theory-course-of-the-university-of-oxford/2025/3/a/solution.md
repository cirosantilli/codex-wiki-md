<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a regular [integrable Hamiltonian system](../../../../integrable-hamiltonian-system.md), [action-angle variables](../../../../action-angle-variables.md) offer two related simplifications.

- Unperturbed [Hamilton's equations](../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) are $\dot{\mathbf J}=0$ and $\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}=\boldsymbol\Omega(\mathbf J)$. Complicated [stellar orbits](../../../../stellar-orbit.md) become uniform motion on an [invariant torus](../../../../invariant-torus.md), and the unperturbed transport operator becomes $\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\partial_{\boldsymbol\theta}$. A [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md) in the angles then makes [orbital resonances](../../../../orbital-resonance.md) explicit through $\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\Omega$.
- A phase-mixed equilibrium [galactic distribution function](../../../../galactic-distribution-function.md) depends on the [action variables](../../../../action-variable.md) rather than the fast angles, consistently with [Jeans theorem](../../../../jeans-theorem.md). Its slow evolution, including [quasilinear diffusion in action space](../../../../quasilinear-diffusion-in-action-space.md), can therefore be described by an angle average in two [action variables](../../../../action-variable.md) rather than by resolving every [stellar orbit](../../../../stellar-orbit.md) in position and [velocity](../../../../velocity.md).

These advantages assume regular or approximately integrable mean motion; globally defined [action-angle variables](../../../../action-angle-variables.md) need not exist in chaotic regions.
