# Pseudo-unitarity of the spinor Lorentz representation

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

The spinor Lorentz matrices preserve the indefinite form defined by $\gamma^0$: $S^\dagger\gamma^0S=\gamma^0$. This follows from the gamma-matrix adjoint identities for infinitesimal generators and extends to finite connected transformations. Consequently the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) transforms with $S^{-1}$, even though a boost matrix is not ordinarily unitary.
