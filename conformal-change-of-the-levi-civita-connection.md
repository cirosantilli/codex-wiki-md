# Conformal change of the Levi-Civita connection

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md)

For [metric tensors](metric-tensor.md) related by $\widetilde g=e^{2\omega}g$, differentiating the rescaled [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) in the [Christoffel symbol](christoffel-symbol.md) formula gives the displayed difference, where $\omega_a=\partial_a\omega$ and the index on $\omega^a$ is raised with $g$. Consequently $\widetilde\nabla_VV=\nabla_VV+2V(\omega)V-g(V,V)\operatorname{grad}_g\omega$. The last term vanishes on a [null vector](null-vector.md), proving [conformal preservation of null geodesic paths](conformal-preservation-of-null-geodesic-paths.md); for a non-null [geodesic](geodesic.md) its orthogonal component generally changes the path.
