# Libration

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libration)

[Libration](libration.md) is the apparent rocking of a body as seen by an observer. For the [Moon](moon.md), longitudinal [libration](libration.md) arises because [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) makes its orbital angular speed vary while its spin is much more uniform: the direction to [Earth](earth.md) advances ahead of, then falls behind, the mean facing direction. The [Moon](moon.md)'s tilted geometry also causes latitudinal [libration](libration.md); small actual rotational oscillations provide further corrections. Thus lunar [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) holds in the mean, rather than requiring exact instantaneous alignment.
