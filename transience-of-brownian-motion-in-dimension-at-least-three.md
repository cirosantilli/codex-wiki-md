# Transience of Brownian motion in dimension at least three

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

Brownian motion in $\mathbb R^d$ is transient for $d\geq3$: its distance from the origin tends to infinity almost surely. In dimension three, the positive local martingale $|B_t|^{-1}$ and [Brownian scaling](brownian-scaling.md) give a short proof.

It is a dimension-dependent long-time property of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
