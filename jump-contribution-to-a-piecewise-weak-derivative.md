# Jump contribution to a piecewise weak derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md)

For a [function](function-split.md) smooth on either side of a joining point $a$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) on the two [intervals](interval-mathematics.md) adds a Dirac mass whose coefficient is the right [trace](trace-operator.md) minus the left [trace](trace-operator.md). If the one-sided [derivatives](derivative.md) are [locally integrable](locally-integrable-function.md), a first [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) can be represented by a [locally integrable function](locally-integrable-function.md) across the join only when the jump vanishes. [Derivative](derivative.md) jumps themselves do not obstruct first-order Sobolev membership: a [continuous](continuous-function.md) piecewise [Lipschitz function](lipschitz-continuity.md) still has a [bounded](boundedness.md) weak first [derivative](derivative.md).
