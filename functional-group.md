# Functional group

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

A [functional group](functional-group.md) is an identifiable group of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) associated with characteristic [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md). In [step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md), $A$ and $B$ name compatible reactive groups, not necessarily entire [monomers](monomer.md) or particular [chemical elements](chemical-element.md).
