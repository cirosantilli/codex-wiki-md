# Nuclear pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

Correlations that favor pairs of like [nucleons](nucleon.md) coupled to zero total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in the simplest spherical picture. They help explain why an unpaired [nucleon](nucleon.md) often dominates an odd nucleus's [spin](spin.md), and why neighboring [nuclear separation energies](nuclear-separation-energy.md) show an odd-even staggering. [Nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) belongs to the residual-interaction physics beyond merely filling independent [energy levels](energy-level.md). Mixing different occupations and deformation can also be necessary; the closed-core picture is most useful near suitable [nuclear shell closures](nuclear-shell-closure.md).
