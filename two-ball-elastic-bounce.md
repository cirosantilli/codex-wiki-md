# Two-ball elastic bounce

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

In the separated-impact approximation, an upper ball of [mass](mass.md) $m_1$ falling at [speed](speed.md) $u$ meets a lower ball of [mass](mass.md) $m_2$ after the latter rebounds elastically from a fixed floor. Their outgoing upward [velocities](velocity.md) are $(3m_2-m_1)u/(m_1+m_2)$ and $(m_2-3m_1)u/(m_1+m_2)$. These follow from [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) and reversal of relative [velocity](velocity.md). The upper ball receives a fraction $r(3-r)^2/(1+r)^3$ of the initial [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), where $r=m_1/m_2$; subtracting this fraction from unity gives $(1-3r)^2/(1+r)^3$. Hence complete transfer occurs at $r=1/3$, whereas a very light upper ball rebounds to nine times the common drop height.
