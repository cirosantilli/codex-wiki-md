# Complete elliptic integral of the second kind

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic integral of the second kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-second-kind.md)

For $0\leq k\leq1$, define $E(k)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\sqrt{1-k^2\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta$. An [ellipse](ellipse.md) with [semi-major and semi-minor axes](semi-major-and-semi-minor-axes.md) $a\geq b>0$ and [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) $e=\sqrt{1-b^2/a^2}$ has perimeter $4aE(e)$. Differentiating its parametrization gives the speed $a\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\theta}$, and fourfold symmetry reduces the full [arc length](arc-length.md) to a quadrant. The [binomial series](binomial-series.md) gives $E(k)=(\pi/2)(1-k^2/4-3k^4/64+O(k^6))$ near zero.
