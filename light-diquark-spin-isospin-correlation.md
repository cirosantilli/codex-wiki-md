# Light-diquark spin-isospin correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli constraint on three-quark flavour multiplets](pauli-constraint-on-three-quark-flavour-multiplets.md)

Two light [quarks](quark.md) in a symmetric spatial state and the antisymmetric colour pair inside a [three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md) require symmetric spin-flavour symmetry. Their [isospin](isospin.md) singlet and spin singlet are both antisymmetric, while their respective triplets are symmetric. Thus the allowed pairs have $(I,S_{ud})=(0,0)$ or $(1,1)$; opposite exchange parities are excluded. Coupling an isospin-zero spin-one-half [quark](quark.md) gives an $I=0,J=1/2$ [baryon](baryon.md) or $I=1,J=1/2,3/2$ [baryons](baryon.md).
