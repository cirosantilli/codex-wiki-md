# Fast-scan cyclic voltammetry

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophysiology](electrophysiology.md)

An electrochemical approach measuring rapid changes in oxidation/reduction currents. Chemical identification, calibration and electroactive interferents matter when interpreting a signal as a particular [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md).
