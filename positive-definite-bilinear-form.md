# Positive-definite bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

A symmetric real [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is positive-definite when $B(v,v)>0$ for every nonzero vector. It defines an [inner product](inner-product.md) and the associated [norm](norm.md) $\sqrt{B(v,v)}$. The complex [Killing form](killing-form.md) is not positive-definite on the entire complex [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md); its restriction to the [Euclidean subspace of a Cartan subalgebra](euclidean-subspace-of-a-cartan-subalgebra.md) is a real positive-definite form.
