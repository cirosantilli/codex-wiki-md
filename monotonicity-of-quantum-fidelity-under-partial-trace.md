# Monotonicity of quantum fidelity under partial trace

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

Discarding a subsystem cannot decrease unsquared [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md): $F(\rho_A,\sigma_A)\geq F(\rho_{AB},\sigma_{AB})$. Maximizing purifications of the joint states are also candidates for purifications of the marginals, with the discarded subsystem included in the reference. [Uhlmann's theorem](uhlmann-s-theorem.md) then proves the inequality. Information loss makes the two states at least as similar.
