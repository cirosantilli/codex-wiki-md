# Symmetric Biot stress

↑ **Parent:** [Work-conjugate stress and strain](work-conjugate-stress-and-strain.md)

Use the material-first [nominal stress](nominal-stress-tensor.md) $P_{Ii}$ and $F=RU$. The [symmetric Biot stress](symmetric-biot-stress.md) conjugate to the [Biot strain tensor](biot-strain-tensor.md) is $T^{(1)}=\operatorname{sym}(PR)$. To prove it, set $M=P^T$ and differentiate $F=RU$. Conservation of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) makes $MF^T$ symmetric, so the contraction of $R^TM$ with $R^T\dot R\,U$ is zero: it is a contraction of a symmetric [tensor](tensor.md) with a skew [tensor](tensor.md) after cyclically moving $U$. Thus $M:\dot F=\operatorname{sym}(R^TM):\dot U=T^{(1)}:\dot E^{(1)}$. No assumption that $PR$ itself is symmetric is needed.
