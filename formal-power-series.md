# Formal power series

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative ring](commutative-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_power_series)

The [formal power series ring](formal-power-series.md) $R[[T]]$ consists of all expressions $\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$ with $a_n\in R$. Addition is coefficientwise, while multiplication uses the [Cauchy product](cauchy-product.md); no analytic convergence is required.
