# Poisson maximal concentration bound

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson process](poisson-process.md)

For a unit-rate [Poisson process](poisson-process.md), $h(\eta)=(1+\eta)\log(1+\eta)-\eta$ gives the displayed maximal deviation bound for $L,\eta>0$. [Poisson exponential martingales](poisson-exponential-martingale.md) and the [Ville inequality](ville-inequality.md) control upper and lower deviations; the upper-tail rate $h$ also bounds the lower-tail probability. Since $h(0)=0$ and $h'(\eta)=\log(1+\eta)>0$, the rate is strictly positive for every positive deviation level.
