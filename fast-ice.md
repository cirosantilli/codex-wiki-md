# Fast ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_ice)

[Sea ice](sea-ice.md) anchored to a coast, grounded feature or other fixed support is fast ice. It can be smooth or deformed, young or old. Currents can transport an [oil spill under sea ice](oil-spill-under-sea-ice.md) beneath it even though the ice itself remains stationary.
