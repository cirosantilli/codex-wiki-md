# Superconducting weak link

↑ **Parent:** [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md)

A [superconducting weak link](superconducting-weak-link.md) is a connection between regions with [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) whose coupling is weak enough that a substantial difference of [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) can develop across the connection. An insulating tunnel barrier is one realization; a narrow conducting constriction can also couple the regions. The [current-phase relation of a superconducting weak link](current-phase-relation-of-a-superconducting-weak-link.md) depends on the connection. A sinusoid is the leading tunnel-junction approximation, not a universal law for every [superconducting weak link](superconducting-weak-link.md).
