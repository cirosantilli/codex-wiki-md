# Evaporation-limited hemispherical imbibition

↑ **Parent:** [Hemispherical capillary imbibition](hemispherical-capillary-imbibition.md)

When a hemispherical wetted front loses liquid at fixed volumetric flux per unit area $F_e$, equilibrium balances inlet flow with $2\pi R^2F_e$. The displayed radius exceeds $R_s$ and is stable because inlet flow decreases while evaporating area increases with radius. [Porosity](porosity.md) affects the adjustment time but cancels from the equilibrium radius.
