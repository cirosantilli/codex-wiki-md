# Slips-of-action test

↑ **Parent:** [Outcome devaluation](outcome-devaluation.md)

A speeded test requiring responses associated with still-valued outcomes and withholding responses associated with devalued outcomes. Response errors can reveal deficient use of outcome knowledge, but also require controls for memory retention, comprehension and inhibitory control.[American Journal of Psychiatry 168, 718–726; Method, instructed outcome devaluation and slips-of-action test](disruption-in-the-balance-between-goal-directed-behavior-and-habit-learning-in-obsessive-compulsive-disorder-by-claire-m-gillan-and-others.md)
