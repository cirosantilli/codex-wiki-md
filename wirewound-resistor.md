# Wirewound resistor

↑ **Parent:** [Resistor](resistor.md)

A [wirewound resistor](wirewound-resistor.md) uses resistive metal wire wound on an insulating former. Suitable alloys and heat-resistant construction provide stable [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and substantial [electrical dissipation](joule-heating.md); parasitic [inductance](inductance.md) and interturn [capacitance](capacitance.md) limit the frequency range over which it behaves as a pure [resistor](resistor.md).
