# Domain determined by a finite segment under a radial characteristic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

For a first-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) with the displayed projected [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) field, label the initial segment by $r=y(0)\in[0,c]$. The [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) are $x=a-(a-b)e^s$, $y=re^s$. They are the branches of straight lines through $(a,0)$ on the side $x<a$. The inverse coordinates are

$$
e^s=\frac{a-x}{a-b},\qquad r=\frac{(a-b)y}{a-x}.
$$

They are unique exactly in the displayed sector. The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $-(a-b)e^{2s}\ne0$, so the initial segment is noncharacteristic and its transported data determine a unique classical solution on the sector's interior whenever the characteristic equation for the dependent variable has a unique solution. The bounding rays inherit data from the two endpoints. The point $(a,0)$ is approached only at infinite parameter time, and characteristics on the opposite side cannot reach the initial segment.
