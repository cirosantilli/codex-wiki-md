# Local radiative equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative equilibrium](radiative-equilibrium.md)

A material element is in [local radiative equilibrium](local-radiative-equilibrium.md) when its absorbed and emitted radiative powers balance, so radiation causes no net local heating. In a non-scattering [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) in [local thermodynamic equilibrium](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), this reduces to equality of the angular mean [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) $J$ and the [Planck function](planck-function.md) $B$. Integrating the [radiative transfer equation](radiative-transfer-equation.md) over [solid angle](solid-angle.md) then shows that the [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) is constant through a plane-parallel atmosphere. The radiation can remain anisotropic; equilibrium constrains its angular mean, not every ray separately.
