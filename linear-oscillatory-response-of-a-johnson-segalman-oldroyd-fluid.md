<h1 id="linear-oscillatory-response-of-a-johnson-segalman-oldroyd-fluid">Linear oscillatory response of a Johnson--Segalman--Oldroyd fluid</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Johnson--Segalman--Oldroyd model](johnson-segalman-oldroyd-model.md)

In small-amplitude oscillatory shear,

$$
G'=\frac{\eta\omega^2(\lambda_1-\lambda_2)}{1+\omega^2\lambda_1^2},
\qquad
G''=\frac{\eta\omega(1+\omega^2\lambda_1\lambda_2)}{1+\omega^2\lambda_1^2}.
$$
