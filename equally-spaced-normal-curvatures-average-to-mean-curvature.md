# Equally spaced normal curvatures average to mean curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Euler formula for normal curvature](euler-formula-for-normal-curvature.md)

For directions spaced in the same rotational sense, [Euler formula for normal curvature](euler-formula-for-normal-curvature.md) gives $k_n(\theta)=H+(k_1-k_2)\cos(2\theta)/2$. The sum of the $m$ equally spaced complex exponentials $e^{2i\theta+2\pi ij/m}$ is zero when $m\geq2$, proving the formula. Consecutive unsigned angles alone do not imply this arrangement: they permit directions to double back.
