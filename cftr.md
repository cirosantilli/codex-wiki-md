# CFTR

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)

An ATP-regulated anion channel whose phosphorylation-dependent activity contributes to epithelial chloride transport. Its activation downstream of cyclic AMP is important in cholera-associated secretion.
