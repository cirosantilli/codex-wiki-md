# Finite length of a commutative Artinian ring

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian commutative ring is Noetherian theorem](artinian-commutative-ring-is-noetherian-theorem.md)

A commutative [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md) has finite [composition length](composition-length.md) as a [module](module-mathematics.md) over itself. It has finitely many maximal [ideals](ideal.md), and its [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) $J$ is nilpotent. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) makes $R/J$ a finite product of [fields](field.md); each [Artinian module](artinian-module.md) $J^i/J^{i+1}$ has finite-dimensional components over those [fields](field.md). Adding their lengths along the finite radical filtration proves the assertion, and in particular the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md).
