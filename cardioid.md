# Cardioid

↑ **Parent:** [Polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardioid)

A cardioid is an epicycloid with one cusp; a standard polar equation is $r=a(1+\cos\theta)$.
