# Relaxed graph-area functional

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

For a [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) function with $Du=\nabla u\,dx+D^su$, relaxed graph area is $A(u)=\int\sqrt{1+|\nabla u|^2}\,dx+|D^su|(\Omega)$. Its dual [supremum](supremum.md) uses the coupled constraint $\varphi_0^2+|\varphi|^2\leq1$ in the pairing $\int(\varphi_0+u\operatorname{div}\varphi)$. It is convex and [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md) in $L^1$. Independent bounds on the two test fields define a different [functional](functional.md).
