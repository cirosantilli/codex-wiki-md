# Logarithmic change of variable in a Sturm-Liouville problem

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Euler equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md)

For a [Cauchy-Euler equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md), logarithmic coordinates turn scale changes in $x>0$ into translations in $t$. With $Y(t)=y(e^t)$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $xy'=Y'$ and $x^2y''=Y''-Y'$. Consequently the [Sturm-Liouville operator](sturm-liouville-operator.md) $-x^2y''-xy'-cy$ becomes $-Y''-cY$. Its natural [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) has measure $dx/x=dt$, so this change of variable also preserves the norm when the transformed problem uses ordinary integration in $t$. On $1<x<e^\ell$ with [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the orthonormal [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are $\sqrt{2/\ell}\sin(n\pi\log x/\ell)$ and the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $(n\pi/\ell)^2-c$, for $n\geq1$.
