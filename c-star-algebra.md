# C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebra)

A C-star algebra is a complex Banach algebra with an involution satisfying $\|a^*a\|=\|a\|^2$.
