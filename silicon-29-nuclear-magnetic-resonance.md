# Silicon-29 nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) of the spin-one-half isotope $^{29}\mathrm{Si}$. Equivalent [silicon](silicon.md) sites give the same isotropic [chemical shift](chemical-shift.md), while condensation and [silanol](silanol.md) substitution can distinguish environments. Line heights alone are not quantitative populations.
