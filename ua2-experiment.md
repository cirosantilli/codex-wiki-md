# UA2 experiment

↑ **Parent:** [W and Z discovery experiments](w-and-z-discovery-experiments.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UA2_experiment)

The 1983 [UA2 experiment](ua2-experiment.md) [Z boson](z-boson.md) paper reports eight candidate [electron](electron.md) pairs, including radiative decays, at $\sqrt{s}=550\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Four events entered its [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) determination. The published result was $91.9\pm1.3\pm1.4\,\mathrm{GeV}/c^2$, the second uncertainty systematic. These are that paper's reported numbers, not inputs fitted to the illustration below.[Physics Letters B 129 (1983), pp.130–140, abstract: eight candidates, four-event mass determination and stated uncertainties](evidence-for-z0-%E2%86%92-e-plus-e-minus-at-the-cern-proton-antiproton-collider-by-p-bagnaia.md)
