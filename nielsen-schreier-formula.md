<h1 id="nielsen-schreier-formula">Nielsen–Schreier formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nielsen–Schreier theorem](nielsen-schreier-theorem.md)

If $H$ has finite index $d$ in the free group $F_n$, then

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(H)=1+d(n-1).}
$$

Indeed, the $d$-sheeted [covering graph](covering-graph.md) of the rose with $n$ petals has $d$ vertices and $dn$ edges, so its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) has rank $dn-d+1$.
