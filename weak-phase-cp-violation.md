# Weak phase (CP violation)

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

A [weak phase](weak-phase-cp-violation.md) is a phase in a coupling or [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) that reverses sign in the [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) conjugate process. A physically relevant difference must survive allowed field rephasings; a complex coupling alone does not prove [CP violation](cp-violation.md).
