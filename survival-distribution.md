# Survival distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Survival time](survival-time.md)

A survival distribution is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of an event or failure time, commonly summarized by its [survivor function](survival-function.md), [hazard function](hazard-function.md) or [cumulative hazard function](cumulative-hazard-function.md). It may contain atoms or a positive probability of an event never occurring; an ordinary density-based hazard therefore need not describe every survival distribution.
