# Siberian Traps

↑ **Parent:** [Large igneous province](large-igneous-province.md)

A large volcanic and intrusive province associated in time with the end-[Permian](permian.md) crisis. Gas release and interactions with surrounding sediments provide mechanisms for linked warming, ocean chemical change and physiological stress during [mass extinction](mass-extinction.md).
