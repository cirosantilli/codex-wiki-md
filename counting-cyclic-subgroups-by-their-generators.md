# Counting cyclic subgroups by their generators

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md)

In a finite [group](group-split.md), a [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) of order $m$ has exactly $\varphi(m)$ generators, where $\varphi$ is the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md). Each element generates exactly one [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md), so dividing the number of elements of order $m$ by $\varphi(m)$ counts these subgroups. Include the identity subgroup using $m=1$ and $\varphi(1)=1$. In a [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md), element counts come from [cycle type](cycle-type.md) and [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) is the [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md) of cycle lengths.
