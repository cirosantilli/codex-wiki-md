# Quantum Hall plateau

↑ **Parent:** [Integer quantum Hall effect](integer-quantum-hall-effect.md)

The [degeneracy of a Landau level](degeneracy-of-a-landau-level.md) is $eBA/h$ per resolved internal branch in [area](surface-area.md) $A$. The [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) $f=n_sh/(eB)$ varies continuously. Additional [localized eigenstates](localized-eigenstate.md) need not carry bulk transport, allowing an integer plateau over an interval of $f$; a [mobility gap](mobility-gap.md) need not be an empty [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md).[p. 405, gaps and localized tails](quantized-hall-conductance-in-a-two-dimensional-periodic-potential-by-d-j-thouless-and-others.md)

![](quantum-hall-plateaus-and-tensor-inversion.png)

**[Figure 9](#image-chosen-quantum-hall-staircase-and-longitudinal-response). Chosen quantum Hall staircase and longitudinal response**.

The plot is an original phenomenological construction, not measured data or a calculation of [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md). It stipulates dimensionless transverse [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) $k(f)=1+\tfrac12\sum_{m=1}^5[1+\tanh((f-m-1/2)/w)]$ and longitudinal [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) $s(f)=a\sum_{m=1}^5\operatorname{sech}^2((f-m-1/2)/w)$, with chosen $w=0.06$, $a=0.08$. Both are measured in units $e^2/h$. The [quantum Hall conductivity tensor inversion](quantum-hall-conductivity-tensor-inversion.md) produces $|\rho_{yx}|/R_K=k/(s^2+k^2)$ and $\rho_{xx}/R_K=s/(s^2+k^2)$. Regions near $k=N$, $s=0$ yield plateaus near $R_K/N$; the model alone does not explain their microscopic robustness. Each step represents one resolved branch, without assuming unresolved [spin](spin.md) or valley multiplicities.
