# Leray-Helmholtz projection

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)

The Leray-Helmholtz projection $P_\sigma$ is the orthogonal projection of square-integrable vector fields onto divergence-free vector fields. Applying it to an incompressible momentum equation removes the pressure gradient.
