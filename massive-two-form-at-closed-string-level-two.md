# Massive two-form at closed-string level two

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic string mass spectrum](bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md)

Each level-two chiral sector of the critical [closed string](closed-string.md) is the [symmetric traceless square](symmetric-trace-free-square-of-the-defining-orthogonal-representation.md) $S^2_0V$ of the massive [little group](little-group.md) vector space. The [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of its two sectors contains the [exterior square](exterior-square.md) $\Lambda^2V$: the equivariant map $(S,T)\mapsto ST-TS$ is onto the antisymmetric matrices. Compact-group complete reducibility realizes this quotient as a subrepresentation. It describes a [massive two-form field](massive-two-form-field.md) with $M^2=8\pi T=4/\alpha'$.
