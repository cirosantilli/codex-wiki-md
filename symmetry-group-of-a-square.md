# Symmetry group of a square

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

Take $r$ as a counterclockwise quarter-turn and $s$ as reflection across the horizontal axis. The eight [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md) of a square are $e,r,r^2,r^3,s,rs,r^2s,r^3s$. The last four are reflections across the horizontal axis, the diagonal $y=x$, the vertical axis and the diagonal $y=-x$, respectively. This is a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order eight; the subscript convention $D_4$ counts polygon vertices, while some authors use $D_8$ for the same [group](group-split.md).
