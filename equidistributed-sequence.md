# Equidistributed sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic theory](ergodic-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equidistributed_sequence)

A sequence $(x_n)$ in $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ is equidistributed if the limiting frequency in every half-open interval equals that interval's length. Equivalently, its empirical probability measures converge to normalized [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) when tested against every continuous function.
