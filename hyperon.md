# Hyperon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

A [baryon](baryon.md) containing one or more [strange quarks](strange-quark.md) and no heavier [quark](quark.md). The light [hyperon](hyperon.md) multiplets include lambda, sigma, xi and omega baryons. Strong and [electromagnetic interactions](electromagnetic-interaction.md) conserve [strangeness](strangeness.md), whereas a [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) can change it; a [photon](photon.md) in the final state does not by itself make a [hyperon](hyperon.md) decay electromagnetic.
