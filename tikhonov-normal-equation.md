# Tikhonov normal equation

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md)

The unique minimizer of $\|Au-f\|^2+\alpha\|u\|^2$ satisfies

$$
(A^*A+\alpha I)u=A^*f.
$$
