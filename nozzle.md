# Nozzle

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nozzle)

A [nozzle](nozzle.md) shapes a passage to control the speed and direction of a [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md). In [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md), converging-diverging passages can accelerate a [gas](gas.md) through a [sonic flow](sonic-flow.md) throat into [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md).
