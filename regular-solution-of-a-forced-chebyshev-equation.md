# Regular solution of a forced Chebyshev equation

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md)

For noninteger real $\gamma$, expand a smooth forcing as $F=\sum_{n\geq0}F_nT_n$ in [Chebyshev polynomials](chebyshev-polynomial.md). Because $(1-x^2)T_n''-xT_n'=-n^2T_n$, division of each coefficient by $\gamma^2-n^2$ gives the regular solution. The coefficients use the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) with weight $(1-x^2)^{-1/2}$. Choosing the same endpoint domain as the regular [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) of the [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md) is essential: finite values alone also allow homogeneous solutions $\cos(\gamma\arccos x)$ and $\sin(\gamma\arccos x)$, which generally have divergent endpoint derivatives. Setting $x=\cos\theta$ converts the regular endpoint condition into the [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) $Z_\theta(0)=Z_\theta(\pi)=0$. The homogeneous equation then has no nonzero solution when $\gamma$ is noninteger, proving uniqueness in this domain.
