# Wirtinger derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirtinger_derivatives)

For $z=x+iy$, the Wirtinger derivatives are $\partial_z=(\partial_x-i\partial_y)/2$ and $\partial_{\bar z}=(\partial_x+i\partial_y)/2$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) become $\partial_{\bar z}F=0$ for a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $F$, and $4\partial_z\partial_{\bar z}=\Delta$. Thus $u_z$ is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) whenever $u$ is a twice continuously [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md).
