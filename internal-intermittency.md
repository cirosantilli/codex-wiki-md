# Internal intermittency

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

[Internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md) is the fluctuating concentration of gradients, transfer and [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) inside turbulent fluid. It is distinct from an interface between turbulent and non-turbulent regions intermittently passing an observer. Its scale dependence is reflected in non-Gaussian increments and coarse-grained [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) [moments](moment.md).
