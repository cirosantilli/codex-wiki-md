# Wien bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

An alternating-current [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) comparing a parallel [resistor](resistor.md)-[capacitor](capacitor.md) arm with a series [resistor](resistor.md)-[capacitor](capacitor.md) arm. Let the upper-left arm have [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md) $Z_1=R_1/(1+i\omega R_1C_1)$, the lower-left arm $Z_2=R_2+1/(i\omega C_2)$, and the upper-right and lower-right arms have [electrical resistances](electrical-resistance.md) $R_3,R_4$. For input between the upper and lower nodes and an unloaded difference measurement between the arm junctions, [voltage divider](voltage-divider.md) balance requires $Z_2/Z_1=R_4/R_3$. Separating real and imaginary parts gives

$$
\omega^2R_1R_2C_1C_2=1,\qquad
\frac{R_4}{R_3}=\frac{R_2}{R_1}+\frac{C_1}{C_2}.
$$

The balance [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is positive for positive components. Equal $R_1=R_2=R$ and $C_1=C_2=C$ require $\omega=1/(RC)$ and $R_4=2R_3$. Reversing which reactive arm is upper changes the divider ratios, so the connections must always be specified.
