# Microsome

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

A [microsome](microsome.md) is a closed [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) compartment produced during disruption of a [biological tissue](tissue-biology.md); [rough endoplasmic reticulum](rough-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)-derived [microsomes](microsome.md) provide a sealed compartment for [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) experiments.
