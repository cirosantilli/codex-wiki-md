<h1 id="cesaro-summation">Cesàro summation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cesàro_summation)

A [series](series-mathematics.md) is Cesàro summable to $L$ when the [Cesaro means](cesaro-mean.md) of its [partial sums](partial-sum.md) converge to $L$. This is a summation method for series, distinct from a mean of the original terms. The [Cesaro theorem for convergent sequences](cesaro-theorem-for-convergent-sequences.md) ensures that every ordinarily convergent [series](series-mathematics.md) has the same Cesàro sum. The alternating series $1-1+1-\cdots$ has Cesàro sum $1/2$ without an ordinary sum.
