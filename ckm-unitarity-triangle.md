# CKM unitarity triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

Orthogonality of two CKM rows or columns makes the three complex products form a closed triangle. Its area is $|J|/2$, where $J$ is the Jarlskog invariant. Normalizing the displayed column-orthogonality relation gives vertices $0,1,\bar\rho+i\bar\eta$, with $\bar\rho+i\bar\eta=-V_{ud}V_{ub}^*/(V_{cd}V_{cb}^*)$. A collapsed triangle has zero weak rephasing-invariant CP violation for nondegenerate quark masses.
