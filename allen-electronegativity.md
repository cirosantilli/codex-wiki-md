# Allen electronegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md)

A scale proportional to the average spectroscopic one-electron binding energy of a free ground-state atom’s [valence electrons](valence-electron.md). For an s,p atom, $\chi=C(n_s\varepsilon_s+n_p\varepsilon_p)/(n_s+n_p)$, with positive binding energies and a scale constant $C$. It distinguishes s and p binding rather than assigning both one Slater charge.[1989, spectroscopic definition](electronegativity-is-the-average-one-electron-energy-of-the-valence-shell-electrons-in-ground-state-free-atoms-by-leland-c-allen.md)
