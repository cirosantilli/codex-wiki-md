# Minimizers under strictly increasing transformations

↑ **Parent:** [Argmin](argmin.md)

If $\phi$ is [strictly increasing](strictly-increasing-function.md) on the range of $f$, then $f(x)\le f(y)$ if and only if $\phi(f(x))\le\phi(f(y))$. Quantifying over every $y$ proves that $f$ and $\phi\circ f$ have the same [argmin](argmin.md), whether or not a minimum is attained. Squaring therefore preserves the [argmin](argmin.md) of a nonnegative objective, while squaring a signed objective need not preserve its [argmin](argmin.md).
