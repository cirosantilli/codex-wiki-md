<h1 id="equatorial-inertia-gravity-wave">Equatorial inertia--gravity wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial wave](equatorial-wave.md)

An equatorial inertia--gravity wave is a high-frequency equatorially trapped mode restored jointly by [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) and the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md). Its dispersion relation has eastward and westward branches lying outside the low-frequency [equatorial Rossby wave](equatorial-rossby-wave.md) branch.
