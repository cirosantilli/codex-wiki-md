# Centralizer of a regular permutation subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Regular permutation subgroup](regular-permutation-subgroup.md)

Identify a regular orbit with the [group](group-split.md) using a chosen base point. A [permutation](permutation.md) commuting with all left translations satisfies $c(x)=xc(1)$. Thus the [centralizer](centralizer.md) consists of right translations. Defining $R_g(x)=xg^{-1}$ makes $R_gR_h=R_{gh}$ and gives an isomorphism to $G$, rather than an unmentioned reversal of multiplication.
