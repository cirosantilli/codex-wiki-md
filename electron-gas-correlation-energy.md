# Electron-gas correlation energy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)

The exact ground-state [energy](energy-split.md) minus the minimum Hartree-Fock [energy](energy-split.md) for the same Hamiltonian and variational constraints. It is nonpositive when compared with the unrestricted variational minimum. Correlations beyond a single determinant can substantially change phase boundaries at low density.
