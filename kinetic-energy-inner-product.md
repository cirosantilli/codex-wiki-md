# Kinetic-energy inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

For a constant reference density, fluid perturbation [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is proportional to $\|u\|^2/2$ under the displayed $L^2$ [inner product](inner-product.md). An [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) for perturbation evolution must be defined relative to this inner product, including [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and the projection onto [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md).
