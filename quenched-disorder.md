# Quenched disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)

[Quenched disorder](quenched-disorder.md) is randomness held fixed while the other degrees of freedom equilibrate. For a sample with couplings $J$, first compute its [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z_J$ and [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) $-k_BT\log Z_J$, then average over samples. One must not silently replace $\mathbb E_J\log Z_J$ by $\log\mathbb E_J Z_J$.[§4.2, pp. 83–86, averaging the free energy after computing each fixed-disorder partition function](out-of-equilibrium-dynamics-of-complex-systems-by-leticia-fernanda-cugliandolo.md)
