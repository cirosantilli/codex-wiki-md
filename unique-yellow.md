# Unique yellow

↑ **Parent:** [Unique hue](unique-hue.md)

A yellow judged neither reddish nor greenish. A [wavelength](wavelength.md) or matching setting can operationalize the judgment under controlled conditions; shifts in that setting do not by themselves identify the neural locus of a change.
