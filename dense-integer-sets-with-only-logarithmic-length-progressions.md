# Dense integer sets with only logarithmic-length progressions

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic method](probabilistic-method.md)

For any fixed density strictly below one, random subsets of an integer interval can have that density yet avoid [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) longer than a constant times the logarithm of the interval length. A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) over possible progressions bounds their occurrence, while concentration of the random cardinality supplies a dense realization. Passing to a subset gives an exact prescribed cardinality.
