<h1 id="quartic-complex-scalar-vertex-in-the-wess-zumino-model">Quartic complex-scalar vertex in the Wess–Zumino model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric relation between quartic and Yukawa couplings](supersymmetric-relation-between-quartic-and-yukawa-couplings.md)

In a canonical [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md) with cubic superpotential $g\Phi^3/3$, auxiliary elimination gives a complex-scalar quartic interaction with two legs of each field type. With all momenta incoming and the displayed coefficient, its [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md) is $-i(2!2!)|g|^2$. For $\varphi=(A+iB)/\sqrt2$, the same quartic [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) is $|g|^2(A^2+B^2)^2/4$, with real-field rules $-6i|g|^2$ for four identical legs and $-2i|g|^2$ for two of each. Factorials depend on how the coefficient is defined, not on a new physical coupling.
