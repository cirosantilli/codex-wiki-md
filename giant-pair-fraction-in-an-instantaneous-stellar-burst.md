# Giant-pair fraction in an instantaneous stellar burst

↑ **Parent:** [Coeval binary population](coeval-binary-population.md)

In an instantaneous burst all binary components have a fixed common age. If the initial component masses are independent, the [probability](probability.md) of two [red giants](red-giant.md) is the square of the individual giant [probability](probability.md) at that age. This need not equal the age-averaged pair fraction in a continuously forming population.
