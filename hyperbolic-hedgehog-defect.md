# Hyperbolic hedgehog defect

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic hedgehog defect](nematic-hedgehog-defect.md)

A point-defect texture that can compensate the radial boundary condition of a sphere with [homeotropic anchoring](homeotropic-anchoring.md), giving a dipolar particle–defect configuration in a uniform far field. It carries the opposite hedgehog charge to the radial particle boundary under a consistent convention.
