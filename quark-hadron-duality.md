# Quark-hadron duality

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

[Quark-hadron duality](quark-hadron-duality.md) is the correspondence, in suitable inclusive or averaged observables, between a quark-level description and a sum over hadronic states. [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md) supports perturbative short-distance production, but sharp resonances and threshold regions can invalidate a pointwise partonic approximation. Inclusive predictions can have both radiative and nonperturbative corrections.
