# Even elliptic functions are rational in the Weierstrass function

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) is the quotient map from the complex torus by $z\mapsto-z$. An even [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) descends through this map; its even Laurent series at each branch point makes the descended function meromorphic there. A [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) is rational, so $f=Q(\wp)$.
