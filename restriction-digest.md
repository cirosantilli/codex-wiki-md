# Restriction digest

↑ **Parent:** [Restriction enzyme](restriction-enzyme.md)

A [restriction digest](restriction-digest.md) is cleavage of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) using a [restriction enzyme](restriction-enzyme.md). Complete cutting at all specified sites and partial cutting can give different fragment patterns.
