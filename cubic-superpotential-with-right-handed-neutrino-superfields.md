# Cubic superpotential with right-handed-neutrino superfields

↑ **Parent:** [MSSM superpotential](mssm-superpotential.md)

For the [MSSM superfield representations](mssm-superfield-representations.md) augmented by singlet [right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md) [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) $N_i$, the homogeneous cubic [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) [superpotential](superpotential.md) contains the four Yukawa structures $QH_uU$, $QH_dD$, $LH_dE$, $LH_uN$ and the additional structures $LLE$, $LQD$, $UDD$, $NH_dH_u$, $NNN$. Weak-doublet pairs contract with the antisymmetric two-index tensor; $UDD$ contracts with the color three-index tensor. Consequently the $LLE$ coefficient is antisymmetric in its two $L$ indices, the $UDD$ coefficient in its two $D$ indices, and the $NNN$ coefficient symmetric. With $L(N)=-1$, the last five structures violate [baryon number](baryon-number.md) or [lepton number](lepton-number.md), whereas the first four conserve both. Singlet fields must not be omitted when classifying a general cubic [superpotential](superpotential.md).
