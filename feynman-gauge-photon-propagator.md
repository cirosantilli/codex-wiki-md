# Feynman-gauge photon propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson propagator](gauge-boson-propagator.md)

At $\xi=1$, the Euclidean photon propagator is $D_{\mu\nu}(k)=\delta_{\mu\nu}/k^2$. Its Lorentz-index contraction simplifies loop numerators in [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md).
