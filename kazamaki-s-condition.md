<h1 id="kazamaki-s-condition">Kazamaki's condition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Doléans-Dade exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazamaki's_condition)

This is an exponential-moment criterion for the [stochastic exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md) to be a true [martingale](martingale-split.md). In the infinite-horizon stopped-moment form, a zero-starting convergent [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) satisfying $\sup_T\mathbb Ee^{M_T/2}<\infty$ has a [uniformly integrable martingale](uniformly-integrable-martingale.md) $\mathcal E(M)$. The [half-threshold for the exponential-martingale Hölder bound](half-threshold-for-the-exponential-martingale-holder-bound.md) first controls strict scalings. The [terminal scaling inequality for stochastic exponentials](terminal-scaling-inequality-for-stochastic-exponentials.md) and [terminal expectation criterion for a nonnegative local martingale](terminal-expectation-criterion-for-a-nonnegative-local-martingale.md) then include the endpoint scaling.
