# Hilbert-Schmidt norm

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md)

The Hilbert-Schmidt norm of a [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) is the square root of the displayed sum. The value is independent of the [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), by the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md). For a finite [matrix](matrix.md), it is the [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md).
