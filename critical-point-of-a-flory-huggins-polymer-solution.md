<h1 id="critical-point-of-a-flory-huggins-polymer-solution">Critical point of a Flory–Huggins polymer solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

For composition-independent $\chi$, the [spinodal](spinodal.md) of [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) is $2\chi=1/(N\phi)+1/(1-\phi)$. At its [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md), a second condition $-1/(N\phi^2)+1/(1-\phi)^2=0$ gives $1-\phi_c=\sqrt N\phi_c$, proving the displayed values. Hence $\phi_c\sim N^{-1/2}$ and $\chi_c-1/2\sim N^{-1/2}$. Under $\chi=C/T$, the [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md) is $\Theta=2C$ and $T_c=\Theta/(1+N^{-1/2})^2$. Thus $T_c$ approaches a constant while $\Theta-T_c$ scales as $N^{-1/2}$. Converting $\chi_c$ to a temperature always requires a specified or inferred $\chi(T)$ law.
