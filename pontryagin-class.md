# Pontryagin class

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Weil homomorphism](chern-weil-homomorphism.md)

For a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$, its Pontryagin classes are the even-index [Chern classes](chern-class.md) of its complexification with the displayed alternating signs. They lie in integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of degrees $4k$ and obey a total-class product formula under [direct sums](direct-sum.md). If a metric [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) has curvature matrix $R$, its first real representative is $p_1(E)=-\operatorname{tr}(R\wedge R)/(8\pi^2)$. The [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) makes this representative closed, and [Chern-Weil connection transgression](chern-weil-connection-transgression.md) makes its [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class independent of the connection. This normalization gives degree-four [A-hat form](a-hat-form.md) $-p_1/24$.
