# Linear birefringence

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

[Linear birefringence](linear-birefringence.md) means that two orthogonal [linear polarizations](linear-polarization.md) have different [refractive indices](refractive-index.md). Their different propagation phases produce [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md). In a lossless [waveplate](waveplate.md), with fast-axis angle $\theta$ and slow-minus-fast retardation $\delta$, the [Jones matrix](jones-matrix.md), up to a common phase, is

$$
J_\delta(\theta)=R(-\theta)\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&e^{i\delta}\end{pmatrix}R(\theta),\qquad R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\-\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},
$$

using time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$. [Circular birefringence](circular-birefringence.md) instead has circular eigenpolarizations.
