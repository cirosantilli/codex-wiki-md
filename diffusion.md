# Diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

Diffusion is the spreading of heat or material by microscopic random motion. A [concentration](concentration.md) gradient produces flux down that gradient; with uniform [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, conservation gives the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) $C_t=D\nabla^2C$. In a moving carrier, [advection](advection.md) must also be included. Physical diffusion is a transport mechanism, distinct from the general probabilistic class of [Markov diffusion](markov-diffusion.md).
