# Beth number

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal arithmetic](cardinal-arithmetic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_number)

The [cardinal numbers](cardinal-number.md) defined by $\beth_0=\aleph_0$, $\beth_{\alpha+1}=2^{\beth_\alpha}$, and $\beth_\lambda=\sup_{\alpha<\lambda}\beth_\alpha$ at [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md) are the beth numbers. In particular $\beth_1=|\mathcal P(\omega)|$, the [cardinality](cardinality.md) of the continuum. This notation distinguishes iterated [power sets](power-set.md) from the enumeration by [Aleph numbers](aleph-number.md) of well-orderable infinite [cardinal numbers](cardinal-number.md).
