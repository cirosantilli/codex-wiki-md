# Like-charge attraction between conductors

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic induction](electrostatic-induction.md)

Conductors with net charges of the same sign can attract because their internal charge distributions respond to one another. A point-charge calculation misses this [electrostatic induction](electrostatic-induction.md) contribution. Exact conducting-sphere results are given in [Electrostatics of Two Charged Conducting Spheres by John Lekner](electrostatics-of-two-charged-conducting-spheres-by-john-lekner.md).[Introduction](electrostatics-of-two-charged-conducting-spheres-by-john-lekner.md)
