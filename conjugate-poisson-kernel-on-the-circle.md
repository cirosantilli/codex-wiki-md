# Conjugate Poisson kernel on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel on the circle](poisson-kernel-on-the-circle.md)

The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives $Q_r(t)=2\sum_{n\ge1}r^n\sin nt$. Its [convolution](convolution.md) multiplier is $-i\operatorname{sgn}(n)r^{|n|}$ for nonzero frequencies and zero at frequency zero. Since the series is uniformly absolutely convergent for $r<1$, these identities hold for every [Lebesgue integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) by termwise integration. If all negative [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) vanish, $Q_r*f=-i(P_r*f-\widehat f(0))$.
