# Algebraic K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)

[Algebraic K-theory](algebraic-k-theory.md) studies invariants built from [projective modules](projective-module.md) and their automorphisms over [rings](ring.md). Its lowest [groups](group-split.md) record the additive projective-module classes and stable invertible [matrices](matrix.md), respectively. It differs from [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md), although vector-bundle clutching and module patching have parallel forms.
