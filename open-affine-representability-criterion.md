# Open affine representability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md)

A [Zariski sheaf](zariski-sheaf.md) $F$ is a [functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) if it has a set of [open subfunctors](open-subfunctor.md) represented by [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) whose pullbacks cover every test [scheme](scheme.md). The overlaps are [open subschemes](open-subscheme.md) in both charts, and their identifications satisfy the compatibility on triple overlaps. [Gluing of schemes along open subschemes](gluing-of-schemes-along-open-subschemes.md) produces a representing [scheme](scheme.md); the [Zariski sheaf](zariski-sheaf.md) condition identifies its [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) with $F$. Conversely, every [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md) of a [scheme](scheme.md) supplies such a presentation. A family of local charts covers in this sense without requiring every global element to belong to a single chart.
