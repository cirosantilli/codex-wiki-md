# Curvature commutator is a tensor derivation

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

For a torsion-free [linear connection on a manifold](affine-connection.md), the commutator of two [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) obeys the tensor-product [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md): mixed first-derivative terms cancel after antisymmetrizing. It vanishes on [scalar fields](scalar-field.md), so $\Delta_{ab}(fQ)=f\Delta_{ab}Q$; its action is therefore pointwise tensorial. Its vector action defines the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md). Applying the commutator to a scalar contraction proves that every covariant tensor index contributes minus the corresponding curvature action, while every contravariant index contributes plus. Torsion-free does not by itself imply [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md).
