# Half-line drift reflection kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion heat-kernel solution](advection-diffusion-heat-kernel-solution.md)

The homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) kernel for $u_t=u_{xx}+\alpha u_x$ on $x>0$. Here $H(r,t)=e^{-r^2/(4t)}/\sqrt{4\pi t}$ is the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md). The exponential reflection weight makes $K_\alpha(0,y,t)=0$. Gaussian decay makes its initial-data integral convergent even when the exponentially weighted initial data are not integrable separately.
