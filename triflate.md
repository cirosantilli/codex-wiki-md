# Triflate

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

[Triflate](triflate.md) is $\mathrm{CF_3SO_3^-}$, the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) of triflic acid. Its delocalized charge makes it a good leaving group in many substitution reactions.
