# Coded experimental variable

↑ **Parent:** [Response surface methodology](response-surface-methodology.md)

A coded experimental variable rescales a physical setting $\xi_j$ around its center $c_j$, dividing by a chosen positive half-range $h_j$. The endpoints $c_j\pm h_j$ become $\pm1$, giving dimensionless [factorial design](factorial-design.md) coordinates. The choice of scale affects the metric used for [steepest ascent in response surface methodology](steepest-ascent-in-response-surface-methodology.md).
