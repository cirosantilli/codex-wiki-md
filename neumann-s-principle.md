<h1 id="neumann-s-principle">Neumann's principle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Point group](point-group.md)

A physical property [tensor](tensor.md) must be invariant under every symmetry operation of the material's [point group](point-group.md). For a polar rank-$r$ [tensor](tensor.md), $T_{i_1\ldots i_r}=R_{i_1j_1}\cdots R_{i_rj_r}T_{j_1\ldots j_r}$. Taking $R=-I$ proves that an odd-rank polar [tensor](tensor.md) vanishes in a centrosymmetric material. A susceptibility of order $n$ has rank $n+1$, so local bulk even-order electric-dipole response is forbidden there. The name refers to [Franz Ernst Neumann](franz-ernst-neumann.md).
