# Meromorphic continuation of the Riemann zeta function to the right half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For $\Re s>0$,

$$
\zeta(s)=\frac{s}{s-1}
-s\int_1^\infty\frac{\{u\}}{u^{s+1}}\,du.
$$

The integral defines a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) in that half-plane, so this continues $\zeta$ meromorphically across $\Re s=1$, with a simple pole of residue one at $s=1$.
