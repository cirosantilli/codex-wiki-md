# Kinetochore

↑ **Parent:** [Centromere](centromere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetochore)

A [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) is a [protein](protein-split.md) assembly at a [centromere](centromere.md) that attaches a [chromosome](chromosome.md) to [microtubules](microtubule.md) of the [spindle apparatus](spindle-apparatus.md). Each replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) has sister [kinetochores](kinetochore.md); their attachment and subsequent separation help distribute the copied material. A [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) is therefore the attachment machinery, while the [centromere](centromere.md) is its [chromosome](chromosome.md) site.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §11.1, Prometaphase I and Prometaphase II](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-process-of-meiosis.md)

The [kinetochore ablation experiment](kinetochore-ablation-experiment.md) tests this mechanical role directly. Counting [kinetochores](kinetochore.md) as independent [chromosomes](chromosome.md) before sister separation would confuse duplicated attachment machinery with [ploidy](ploidy.md).
