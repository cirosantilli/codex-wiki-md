# Schwarzschild marginally bound capture

↑ **Parent:** [Effective potential for timelike Schwarzschild geodesics](effective-potential-for-timelike-schwarzschild-geodesics.md)

A [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) with unit specific [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) can plunge from infinity into a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) only if its specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) satisfies $|h|<4M$. The limiting case $|h|=4M$ asymptotes to the unstable orbit at $r=4M$. A geodesic already inside the angular-momentum barrier can cross the [event horizon](event-horizon.md) with larger $|h|$, so the origin-at-infinity condition matters.
