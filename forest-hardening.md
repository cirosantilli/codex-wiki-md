# Forest hardening

↑ **Parent:** [Work hardening](work-hardening.md)

Resistance to [dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md) produced by intersections with other dislocations. Multiple active [slip systems](slip-system.md) can form junctions and increase the density of obstacles. This is one mechanism of [work hardening](work-hardening.md).
