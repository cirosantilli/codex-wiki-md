# Cauchy derivative convergence bound

↑ **Parent:** [Locally uniform convergence of holomorphic functions](locally-uniform-convergence-of-holomorphic-functions.md)

For $0<r<R$, suppose $f_n,f$ are holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed radius-$R$ disc. Subtract their [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) expressions on that circle. Its length is $2\pi R$, and the distance from an interior point with modulus at most $r$ to the circle is at least $R-r$. The [estimation lemma](estimation-lemma.md) gives the displayed bound. Uniform convergence on that circle therefore implies uniform convergence of each fixed-order derivative on the smaller disc. The positive separation from the contour is essential for a uniform bound.
