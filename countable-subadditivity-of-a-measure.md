# Countable subadditivity of a measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)

For measurable [sets](set-split.md) $E_n$, a [measure](measure.md) satisfies

$$
\mu\left(\bigcup_{n\ge1}E_n\right)\le\sum_{n\ge1}\mu(E_n).
$$

Replace $E_n$ by the disjoint [sets](set-split.md) $E_n\setminus\bigcup_{j<n}E_j$, use [countable additivity](countable-additivity.md), and then monotonicity. The same property is an axiom for an [outer measure](outer-measure.md) on arbitrary [sets](set-split.md).
