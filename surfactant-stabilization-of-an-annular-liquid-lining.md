# Surfactant stabilization of an annular liquid lining

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary instability of an annular liquid lining](capillary-instability-of-an-annular-liquid-lining.md)

For [insoluble surfactant](insoluble-surfactant.md) with $\sigma=\sigma_0-A\Gamma$ and positive base tension, let $q=k^2a^2$, $\lambda=h_0^3(\sigma_0-A\Gamma_0)/(3\mu a^4)$ and $\alpha=h_0A\Gamma_0/(\mu a^2)$. The coupled film-height and advected-[surfactant](surfactant.md) amplitudes give the displayed growth equation. The unstable band remains $0<q<1$. Weak [surfactant](surfactant.md) elasticity gives $\beta_{\max}=\lambda/4-3\alpha/8+O(\alpha^2/\lambda)$; strong elasticity gives $\beta_{\max}\sim\lambda/16$. The [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) reduces tangential surface motion; strong elasticity reduces lubrication mobility to the value for an effectively immobile interface.
