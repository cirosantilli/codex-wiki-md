# Fourier sine inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier sine transform](fourier-sine-transform.md)

For sufficiently regular integrable functions on a half-line, [Fourier sine transform](fourier-sine-transform.md) inversion recovers the odd extension at continuity points. A zero endpoint value is required when demanding continuity at the endpoint.
