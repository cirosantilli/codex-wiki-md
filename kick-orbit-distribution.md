# Kick-orbit distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity kick on a circular Kepler orbit](velocity-kick-on-a-circular-kepler-orbit.md)

Uniform planar kick angle $\theta$ gives $c=\cos\theta$ with probability density $1/(\pi\sqrt{1-c^2})$. This maps onto a one-dimensional curve in [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md)–[semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) space. The two radial-kick signs share that curve but have opposite tangential components of the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md). The resulting density is enhanced near tangential-kick endpoints.
