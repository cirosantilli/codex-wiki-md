# Spin component variance

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The uncertainty of a spin projection is measured by its variance. In a maximally polarized [spin coherent state](spin-coherent-state.md), the longitudinal variance is zero and each transverse variance is $S/2$ in units $\hbar=1$. The transverse fluctuations do not imply finite [magnon](magnon.md) occupation in an isotropic ferromagnet's polarized vacuum.
