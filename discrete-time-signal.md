# Discrete-time signal

↑ **Parent:** [Signal](signal.md)

A discrete-time signal assigns a value to each of a discrete collection of times, often indexed by the [integers](integer.md). Regular sampling at rate $f_s$ gives $x[n]=x(n/f_s)$, so a sinusoid with normalized frequency $f$ cycles per sample has physical [frequency](frequency.md) $ff_s$. Discreteness of time does not itself require the amplitudes to be quantized; rounding the values is an additional operation. The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) gives conditions for recovering a band-limited continuous [signal](signal.md) from its samples.
