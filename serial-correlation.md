# Serial correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Pearson correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md)

Responses observed on the same process or individual at different times may have nonzero [correlation coefficients](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md). Such serial correlation can arise from a shared [random effect](random-effect.md) or from history-dependent conditional means. A scalar [dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md) adjustment alone does not represent this temporal dependence.
