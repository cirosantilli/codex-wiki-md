# Maximum degree of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Degree (graph theory)](degree-graph-theory.md)

The maximum degree $\Delta(G)$ is the greatest degree of a vertex of $G$.

It is the maximum of the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) over all vertices.
