# Anyon balancing identity

↑ **Parent:** [Topological spin](topological-spin.md)

In a ribbon [anyon](anyon.md) theory, twisting a composite charge $c\in a\times b$ is equivalent to twisting each input and applying their full [anyon monodromy](anyon-monodromy.md). The [topological spin](topological-spin.md) obeys $\theta_1=1$ and $\theta_{\bar a}=\theta_a$; the latter is the ribbon duality convention, with no complex conjugation of the scalar.
