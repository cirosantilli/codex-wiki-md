# Fay solution

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md)

The Fay solution is a periodic solution of the negative-flux [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md) for $Z>0$. Its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) has exponentially decreasing coefficients at each positive $\delta Z$. Expanding $1/\sinh(ns)=2\sum_{j\ge0}e^{-(2j+1)ns}$ and summing the sine series gives

$$
q=2\delta\sin\theta\sum_{j\ge0}\frac1{\cosh((2j+1)s)-\cos\theta},\qquad s=\delta Z.
$$

For $s\gg1$, the first harmonic gives $q\sim4\delta e^{-s}\sin\theta$. The [Fay shock-layer asymptotics](fay-shock-layer-asymptotics.md) describe its opposite, small-$s$ regime.
