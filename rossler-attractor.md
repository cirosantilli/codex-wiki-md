<h1 id="rossler-attractor">Rössler attractor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rössler system](rossler-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rössler_attractor)

A chaotic [attractor](attractor.md) of a [Rössler system](rossler-system.md) for suitable parameter values. Local expansion near an [unstable spiral](unstable-spiral.md) combines with excursions in the third coordinate and global return; the existence of a local expanding direction by itself does not prove a chaotic [attractor](attractor.md).
