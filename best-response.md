# Best response

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_response)

A best response is a strategy that maximizes a player's [expected payoff](expected-value.md) when the other players' strategies are held fixed.
