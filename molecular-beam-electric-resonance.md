# Molecular beam electric resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Dipole evidence for bond polarization](dipole-evidence-for-bond-polarization.md)

The apparatus and dipole inference are described under [dipole evidence for bond polarization](dipole-evidence-for-bond-polarization.md). The [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) supplies a general starting model for rotational [quantum states](quantum-state.md); [rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md) measures their transition [frequencies](frequency.md), while [molecular beam electric resonance](molecular-beam-electric-resonance.md) can select and analyze states through their [Stark effects](stark-effect.md).

An actual implementation gives substance to the apparatus description above. In [Johannes Jacobus van Vaals](johannes-jacobus-van-vaals.md)'s experiments, heated samples expanded with [argon](argon.md) through a nozzle, then [molecular beam skimmers](molecular-beam-skimmer.md) formed the [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md). Nonuniform [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) selected and analyzed [quantum states](quantum-state.md); parallel plates housed the [microwave](microwave.md) interaction. A [surface ionization detector](surface-ionization-detector.md) supplied [electric current](electric-current.md) to an [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md) and a [lock-in amplifier](lock-in-amplifier.md). Resonant transfer into a rejected [quantum state](quantum-state.md) reduced the detected flux. [Phase locking](phase-locking.md) stabilized the [microwave](microwave.md) [frequencies](frequency.md); an absorber suppressed reflections that could distort [spectral lines](spectral-line.md). The thesis reports preliminary [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) components for gaseous [potassium cyanide](potassium-cyanide.md) from measurements in nonzero [electric fields](electric-field-split.md). Such inference uses the [Stark effect](stark-effect.md), rather than measuring [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md). The source notes restrictions from [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) and [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) transit time; its preliminary dipole report is not a full calibration record. These are documented features of this apparatus, not proposed controls for the earlier [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) experiment.[chapter I §2, printed pp. 11–14: source, A/C/B fields, detector, phase locking, absorber and limitations; chapter III, printed p. 49, reference note 29: preliminary dipole components measured in nonzero fields](high-resolution-molecular-beam-study-of-alkali-cyanides-by-johannes-jacobus-van-vaals.md)
