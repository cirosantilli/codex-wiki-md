# Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

The stable quadratic spectrum is $E_k=\sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2nV_k)}$. If $V_k\to V_0>0$, its low-momentum form is $\hbar|k|\sqrt{nV_0/m}$, a linear sound branch. For contact repulsion the [healing length](healing-length.md) separates the [phonon](phonon.md) and particle regimes. General momentum-dependent interactions can change the higher-energy shape.
