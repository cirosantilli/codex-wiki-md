# Tangent galvanometer

↑ **Parent:** [Galvanometer](galvanometer.md)

A current-measuring coil and compass arranged so that the coil's central [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) is perpendicular to the horizontal terrestrial field. With unchanged coil geometry and terrestrial field, the [electric current](electric-current.md) ratio is the ratio of the tangents of the needle deflections. A small needle and controlled alignment make the central-field approximation useful.
