# Boundary compatibility in the self-adjoint Fredholm alternative

↑ **Parent:** [Fredholm alternative for an elliptic Dirichlet problem](fredholm-alternative-for-an-elliptic-dirichlet-problem.md)

For real $c$ and $L=\Delta+c$ on a bounded regular domain, let $N$ be the zero-boundary kernel. The problem $Lu=f$, $u=\psi$ on the boundary is solvable exactly when $\int_\Omega fz+\int_{\partial\Omega}\psi\partial_\nu z=0$ for every $z\in N$. The sign follows from [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md). After subtracting a boundary extension of $\psi$, this is the usual orthogonality of the forcing to the [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) Dirichlet realization. All solutions then differ by an element of $N$.
