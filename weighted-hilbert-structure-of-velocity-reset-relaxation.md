# Weighted Hilbert structure of velocity-reset relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized velocity-reset collision operator](normalized-velocity-reset-collision-operator.md)

For $M>0$ with [integral](integral.md) one, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) makes the mass functional bounded on the weighted [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). The vector $M$ has [norm](norm.md) one, so the velocity-reset projection is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto its span. Consequently $L=\Pi-I$ is bounded and [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md). This Hilbert geometry differs from the norm-one positive reset projection on phase-space $L^1$.
