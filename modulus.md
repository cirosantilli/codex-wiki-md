# Modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

The modulus of a [complex number](complex-number.md) $z=x+iy$ is $|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$.

For $z=x+iy$, its modulus is $|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$, the complex case of [absolute value](absolute-value.md).
