# Actin patch

↑ **Parent:** [Actin filament](actin-filament.md)

A localized [actin](actin.md) assembly in [budding yeast](budding-yeast.md), commonly associated with [endocytosis](endocytosis.md). The lifetime of a visible patch and the lifetime of an individual constituent [actin filament](actin-filament.md) are different measurements.
