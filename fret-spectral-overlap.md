# FRET spectral overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Förster distance](forster-distance.md)

[FRET spectral overlap](fret-spectral-overlap.md) quantifies overlap between the [FRET donor](fret-donor.md)'s emission and the [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md)'s absorption as functions of [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda$. With normalized donor emission $f_D$ and acceptor molar absorption coefficient $\varepsilon_A$, one convention is

$$
\int_0^\infty f_D(\lambda)\,d\lambda=1,\qquad J=\int_0^\infty f_D(\lambda)\varepsilon_A(\lambda)\lambda^4\,d\lambda.
$$

Matching two emission colors is insufficient: the receiving absorption matters.[§3.1, equation 17 and Figure 19: normalized donor emission and acceptor absorption overlap](advanced-fluorescence-microscopy-techniques-frap-flip-flap-fret-and-flim-by-hellen-c-ishikawa-ankerhold-and-others.md)
