<h1 id="frechet-derivative-of-a-negative-matrix-power">Fréchet derivative of a negative matrix power</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md)

For a positive integer $m$, the negative [matrix power](matrix-power.md) is differentiable on the open [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). The [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) gives $(A+H)^{-1}=A^{-1}-A^{-1}HA^{-1}+O(\|H\|^2)$ locally around any invertible matrix. Applying the [product rule](product-rule.md) to the $m$ factors of this inverse gives the displayed [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md). Each summand chooses one inverse factor to differentiate while keeping the others in order.

The factor order matters. Only when $A$ and $H$ commute does the sum simplify to $-mA^{-(m+1)}H$. This is a useful example of why scalar differentiation rules for powers cannot be transferred to matrices without accounting for noncommutativity.
