# Lower limit topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_limit_topology)

The lower limit topology on $\mathbb R$ has [basis of a topology](basis-of-a-topology.md) $\{[a,b):a<b\}$. Every such nonempty interval contains a rational, so this [topological space](topological-space.md) is separable. Its topology is finer than the usual Euclidean topology; the one-sided basic neighbourhoods allow its square to have an uncountable discrete antidiagonal.
