# Euphotic depth

↑ **Parent:** [Euphotic zone](euphotic-zone.md)

The depth of a chosen light threshold, commonly 1% of just-below-surface [photosynthetically active radiation](photosynthetically-active-radiation.md). It depends on spectral attenuation and is not a universal species-specific compensation depth.
