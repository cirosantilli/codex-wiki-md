# Bedload transport model with sediment inertia

↑ **Parent:** [Bedload transport](bedload-transport.md)

Keeping sediment acceleration while neglecting water storage and a small gravity-friction residual gives a reduced model for the bed elevation $s$, water speed $u$ and sediment speed $v$. A prescribed water flux $q$ gives $h=q/u$; integrated water momentum gives $s=C-F^2u^2/2-q/u$. Around the normalized uniform state $h=u=q=1$, let $(1-v_0)^2=\kappa v_0$, $0<v_0<1$. A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) of wavenumber $k$ has growth-rate real part

$$
-\frac{2(1-v_0)v_0k^2}{(1-F^2)([2(1-v_0)+\kappa]^2+k^2v_0^2)}.
$$

Thus nonuniform modes decay for subcritical [Froude number](froude-number.md) and grow for supercritical [Froude number](froude-number.md). The spatially constant mode is neutral, and the critical value needs terms neglected in this reduction. In a general uniform state the sign change is at the actual mean-flow [Froude number](froude-number.md) equal to one, rather than at a velocity-scale parameter unrelated to that state.
