# Vertex renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

Multiplicative adjustment of an interaction vertex to cancel its [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md). In [scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md), write the one-[photon](photon.md) two-[scalar field](scalar-field.md) coefficient as $Z_1e$ and the scalar kinetic coefficient as $Z_2$. The [scalar QED Ward identity](scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md) gives $Z_1=Z_2$ in a symmetry-preserving [renormalization scheme](renormalization-scheme.md).
