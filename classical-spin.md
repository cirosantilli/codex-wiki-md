# Classical spin

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A classical spin is a fixed-length [vector](vector.md) representing the orientation of a local [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). A classical limit of a large quantum [spin](spin.md) replaces its components by this vector and minimizes the resulting [energy](energy-split.md). Its length is conventionally $S$ at leading order, whereas the exact quantum squared-spin [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $S(S+1)$ in units $\hbar=1$.
