# Chebyshev theta function

↑ **Parent:** [Primorial](primorial.md)

The Chebyshev theta function is one of the two [Chebyshev functions](chebyshev-function.md) and is the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of the [primorial](primorial.md):

$$
\vartheta(x)=\sum_{p\leq x}\log p
=\log\prod_{p\leq x}p,
$$

where both the sum and the product range over [prime numbers](prime-number.md).
