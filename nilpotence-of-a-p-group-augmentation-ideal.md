# Nilpotence of a p-group augmentation ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md)

In characteristic $p$, the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md) $I$ of the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of a finite $p$-group is nilpotent. Choose a central element $z$ of order $p$ and put $t=z-1$, so $t^p=0$. The quotient by $t$ is the group algebra of the smaller quotient group. Induction gives $I^m\subseteq t kP$ for some $m$, hence $I^{mp}=0$. For a normal $p$-subgroup $P$ of $G$, conjugation stability gives $(I kG)^m=I^m kG$, proving nilpotence of the kernel of $kG\to k(G/P)$.
