# Sharp two-sided Gaussian tail bound

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian tail bound](gaussian-tail-bound.md)

For $G$ with the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) and $t\geq0$, $\mathbb P(|G|>t)=2(1-\Phi(t))\leq e^{-t^2/2}$. The difference has derivative $e^{-t^2/2}(\sqrt{2/\pi}-t)$, starts at zero, and tends to zero, proving its nonnegativity.
