# Esaki tunnel-diode experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md)

[Leo Esaki](leo-esaki.md)'s 1958 report describes alloy-fabricated [germanium](germanium.md) [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md), with [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) concentration $1.6\times10^{19}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}$ and [donor impurity](donor-impurity.md) concentration approximately $10^{19}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}$. Its readout was the [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md): a forward peak occurred at $0.035\pm0.005\,\mathrm V$. The report says specimens could be reproduced; above $0.3\,\mathrm V$ the forward branch approximately followed the ordinary [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md).[New Phenomenon in Narrow Germanium p-n Junctions, p. 603, opening paragraph and Fig. 1; opening reproduced on slide 2 of the Trinity College Dublin lecture PDF](new-phenomenon-in-narrow-germanium-p-n-junctions-by-leo-esaki.md)

In his 1973 lecture, [Leo Esaki](leo-esaki.md) recalls an earlier junction whose strong room-temperature reverse conduction preceded a negative-slope forward segment revealed by cooling. Its estimated zero-bias width was about $200$ [ångströms](angstrom.md) ($20\,\mathrm{nm}$), supported by [capacitance](capacitance.md) measurements. Increasing [semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md) further made the negative slope visible across the measured [temperatures](temperature-split.md), including room temperature. Thus preparation and [temperature](temperature-split.md) variation were experimental controls, not merely changes to a theoretical diagram. The low onset voltage argued against avalanche production of [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md); the [energy band](energy-band.md) explanation connected the forward decline to loss of available final states.[Long Journey into Tunneling, section II, printed pp. 120–122, Figs. 3–5; inspected institutional mirror at kapitza.ras.ru](long-journey-into-tunneling-by-leo-esaki.md)

The historical readouts were junction [electric current](electric-current.md) versus applied [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and a separate [capacitance](capacitance.md) check; preparation and [temperature](temperature-split.md) were varied. The cited descriptions do not identify a meter model or give a complete wiring diagram.

For a modern repetition, measure junction voltage directly while stepping the supply and recording [electric current](electric-current.md), repeat both sweep directions, and compare temperatures and differently doped specimens. These are proposed controls, not a reconstruction of the historical apparatus. Lead [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), [Joule heating](joule-heating.md), contact quality and voltage resolution can obscure the result. The [tunnel-diode load-line stability](tunnel-diode-load-line-stability.md) calculation also predicts that an external [resistor](resistor.md) can make a negative-slope [operating point](operating-point.md) inaccessible in a slow sweep. A jump in terminal readings then need not mean the underlying [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md) is discontinuous.
