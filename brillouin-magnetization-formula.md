# Brillouin magnetization formula

↑ **Parent:** [Paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md)

For independent distinguishable moments with angular [momentum](momentum-split.md) $J$, define $y=g\mu_BB/(k_BT)$. The one-site [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $Z=\sinh[(J+1/2)y]/\sinh(y/2)$. Its logarithmic derivative gives $\langle\mu_z\rangle=g\mu_B[(J+1/2)\coth((J+1/2)y)-\tfrac12\coth(y/2)]$. At $J=1/2$ this reduces to $(g\mu_B/2)\tanh(y/2)$.
