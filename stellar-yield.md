# Stellar yield

↑ **Parent:** [Instantaneous recycling approximation](instantaneous-recycling-approximation.md)

The stellar yield is the newly synthesized metal mass returned to gas per unit mass locked into long-lived stars, under a specified recycling convention and [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md).
