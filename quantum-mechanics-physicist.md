## Daniel Gottesman

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics physicist](quantum-mechanics-physicist.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Physicist](physicist.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Gottesman)

[Physicist](physicist.md) whose work includes the stabilizer formalism for [quantum error correction](quantum-error-correction.md). His [The Heisenberg Representation of Quantum Computers by Daniel Gottesman](physics-bibliography.md#the-heisenberg-representation-of-quantum-computers-by-daniel-gottesman) develops the [Pauli group](quantum-circuit.md#pauli-group) description of [Clifford circuits](quantum-circuit.md#clifford-circuit). [Verified institutional profile](https://quics.umd.edu/people/daniel-gottesman).

<h2 id="erwin-schrodinger">Erwin Schrödinger</h2>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics physicist](quantum-mechanics-physicist.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Physicist](physicist.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger)

A [physicist](physicist.md) who developed [wave mechanics](quantum-mechanics.md#wave-mechanics). In 1926, [Erwin Schrödinger](#erwin-schrodinger) made the energies of [chemical atoms](physics.md#atom-physics) an [eigenvalue](linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) problem, rather than prescribing a separate quantization rule for each classical [orbit](dynamical-systems.md#orbit-dynamical-system). He shared the [Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933](nobel-prize-in-physics.md#nobel-prize-in-physics-in-1933) with [Paul Dirac](#paul-dirac).[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Zurich period and 1926 wave-equation work; joint 1933 award; birth and death dates](physics-bibliography.md#nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/erwin-schrodinger-biographical)

The [Schrödinger equation](mathematical-physics.md#schrodinger-equation) turns a proposed [Hamiltonian operator](quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) into predictions for a [wavefunction](wave-function.md) and its evolution. [Boundary conditions](differential-equation.md#boundary-condition) can select discrete [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue.md), while [quantum superposition](quantum-system.md#quantum-superposition) makes their relative [quantum phases](quantum-system.md#quantum-phase) observable through [interference](physical-optics.md#interference-wave-propagation). The [infinite square well](quantum-mechanics.md#infinite-square-well) gives a transparent worked example; a [hydrogen atom](mathematical-physics.md#hydrogen-atom) supplies a physical application with a different [potential energy](energy.md#potential-energy).

The [Davisson–Germer experiment](quantum-scattering.md#davisson-germer-experiment) gives a complementary experimental route into [matter waves](wave-function.md#matter-wave): an accelerated [electron](physics.md#electron) beam scattered from a [nickel](chemical-substance.md#nickel) crystal produces direction-dependent collected [electric current](electric-charge.md#electric-current). Its apparatus and angular scans make the wave prediction testable. Interpreting a [wavefunction](wave-function.md) as a [probability amplitude](wave-function.md#probability-amplitude) also requires the [Born rule](wave-function.md#born-rule); that statistical interpretation deserves its own history alongside [Erwin Schrödinger](#erwin-schrodinger)'s dynamics.
