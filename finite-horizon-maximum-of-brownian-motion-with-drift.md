# Finite-horizon maximum of Brownian motion with drift

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

For $a>0$ and $t>0$, the distribution function is $\Phi((a-\nu t)/\sqrt t)-e^{2a\nu}\Phi((-a-\nu t)/\sqrt t)$. The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives the zero-drift terminal density below the barrier as $\phi_t(y)-\phi_t(2a-y)$ for $y<a$. Weight it by $e^{\nu y-\nu^2t/2}$ using the [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) and complete squares in the two Gaussian integrals.
