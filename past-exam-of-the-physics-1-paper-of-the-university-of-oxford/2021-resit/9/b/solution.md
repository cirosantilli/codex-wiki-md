<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Retain the unchanged-[angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) assumption of the preceding part. The initial [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) has radius $r_o=6400+500=6900\,\mathrm{km}$, and the desired [pericentre distance](../../../../pericentre-distance.md) is $r_p=6400+100=6500\,\mathrm{km}$. Using $r_p=r_o/(1+e)$ gives

$$
e=\frac{6900}{6500}-1=\frac4{65}.
$$

The required fractional increase in initial [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta K}{K_0}=e^2=\frac{16}{4225}
\simeq0.00378698=0.378698\%.}
$$

Here $\Delta E=\Delta K>0$ because the instantaneous kick does not change [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md). Since the initial total [orbital energy](../../../../orbital-energy.md) is $E_0=-K_0$, an alternative convention for the requested fractional [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) change gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta E}{|E_0|}=+0.00378698,\qquad
\frac{\Delta E}{E_0}=-0.00378698.}
$$

The total [orbital energy](../../../../orbital-energy.md) becomes less negative. Explicitly specifying its denominator avoids interpreting a positive [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) input as a decrease of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
