<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume the station's [mass](../../../../mass.md) remains fixed through an instantaneous kick, with subsequent motion governed only by the Earth's [Newtonian gravitational field](../../../../newtonian-gravitational-field.md). Put $\kappa=GM_E$, and work per unit station [mass](../../../../mass.md). The initial [circular orbit](../../../../circular-orbit.md) has [specific angular momentum](../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $j$ and [specific orbital energy](../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) $\varepsilon_0$ given by

$$
v_0^2=\frac{\kappa}{r_o},\qquad
j^2=\kappa r_o,\qquad
\varepsilon_0=-\frac{\kappa}{2r_o}.
$$

Let $\delta$ denote the fractional increase of [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). The position and [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) are unchanged during the kick, so

$$
\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0+\delta\frac{\kappa}{2r_o}
=-\frac{\kappa(1-\delta)}{2r_o}.
$$

Unchanged [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) at unchanged position fixes the tangential [velocity](../../../../velocity.md); the added [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) must be radial. Thus this is a radial kick, not a purely tangential speed increase. The new [orbital eccentricity](../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) follows from

$$
e^2=1+\frac{2\varepsilon j^2}{\kappa^2}=\delta.
$$

At either radial [turning point](../../../../turning-point.md), the [Kepler radial energy equation](../../../../kepler-radial-energy-equation.md) gives $\varepsilon=j^2/(2r^2)-\kappa/r$. Its two roots, equivalently the [periapsis](../../../../periapsis.md) and [apoapsis](../../../../apoapsis.md) of the [elliptic orbit](../../../../elliptic-orbit.md), are

$$
\boxed{r_p=\frac{r_o}{1+\sqrt\delta},\qquad
r_a=\frac{r_o}{1-\sqrt\delta}.}
$$

For $\delta=0.01$, $e=0.1$ and

$$
\boxed{r_p=\frac{10}{11}r_o,\qquad r_a=\frac{10}{9}r_o.}
$$

An inward or outward kick gives the same pair of radial [turning points](../../../../turning-point.md), although it places the station at a different phase of the [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). These two finite positive radii require $0\leq\delta<1$.
