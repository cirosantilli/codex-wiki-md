<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Interpret the percentage relative to the classical [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md), so that $K_{\rm rel}=1.02K_{\rm cl}$. Set $\beta=v/c$ and use the [Lorentz factor](../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}$. The [relativistic kinetic energy](../../../relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) and classical [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) give

$$
(\gamma-1)mc^2=1.02\left(\frac12mv^2\right),
\qquad \gamma-1=0.51\beta^2.
$$

For the positive nonzero [speed](../../../speed.md), insert $\beta^2=1-\gamma^{-2}$ and cancel $\gamma-1$:

$$
\frac{\gamma^2}{\gamma+1}=0.51,
\qquad \gamma^2-0.51\gamma-0.51=0,
\qquad \gamma=\frac{0.51+\sqrt{2.3001}}2\simeq1.01330403.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{v=c\sqrt{1-\gamma^{-2}}\simeq0.161513c
\simeq4.8420\times10^7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},}
\qquad
\boxed{E=\gamma mc^2\simeq950.479\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

Here $mc^2=938\,\mathrm{MeV}$ is the [proton](../../../proton.md)'s [rest energy](../../../rest-energy.md); the resulting [relativistic kinetic energy](../../../relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) is $12.479\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The zero-[speed](../../../speed.md) equality before cancellation has no meaningful percentage excess because both [kinetic energies](../../../kinetic-energy.md) then vanish.
