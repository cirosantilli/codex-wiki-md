<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

**Invariant and collision.** For a particle of [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$, [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md) gives [relativistic energy](../../../relativistic-energy.md) and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md)

$$
E=\gamma mc^2,\qquad \mathbf p=\gamma m\mathbf v,
\qquad \gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}.
$$

Therefore the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{E^2-p^2c^2=\gamma^2m^2c^4(1-v^2/c^2)=m^2c^4.}
$$

It is the squared [Minkowski norm](../../../minkowski-norm.md) of the [four-momentum](../../../four-momentum.md), multiplied by $c^2$, and hence is preserved by every [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Explicitly a boost with [speed](../../../speed.md) $U$ along $x$ gives $E'=\Gamma(E-Up_x)$, $p'_x=\Gamma(p_x-UE/c^2)$, with $p'_y=p_y$, $p'_z=p_z$. Expanding $E'^2-c^2p_x'^2$ gives $E^2-c^2p_x^2$ because $\Gamma^2(1-U^2/c^2)=1$. Spatial rotations preserve $p^2$, completing the invariance argument for all [inertial frames](../../../inertial-frame.md).

For the incoming moving particle, $v=3c/5$ and $\gamma=5/4$. The total laboratory [energy](../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) before the [perfectly inelastic collision](../../../perfectly-inelastic-collision.md) are

$$
E_i=\left(\frac54+2\right)m_0c^2=\frac{13}{4}m_0c^2,
\qquad p_i=\frac54m_0\frac{3c}{5}=\frac34m_0c.
$$

[Four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) identifies these with the final particle's [energy](../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md). Its [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{m'=\frac{\sqrt{E_i^2-p_i^2c^2}}{c^2}
=\frac{m_0}{4}\sqrt{13^2-3^2}=\sqrt{10}\,m_0.}
$$

The result exceeds the sum $3m_0$ of the incoming [rest masses](../../../invariant-mass.md); some initial [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) has become internal [rest energy](../../../rest-energy.md). Since $pc^2/E=v$ for a massive particle,

$$
\boxed{v'=\frac{p_ic^2}{E_i}=\frac3{13}c,\qquad
\gamma'=\frac{13}{4\sqrt{10}}.}
$$

**Two-body decay.** Work first in the parent's rest frame, denoted by a star. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) requires equal and opposite daughter [momenta](../../../momentum-split.md). Their equal [rest masses](../../../invariant-mass.md) give equal [energies](../../../energy-split.md):

$$
E_* =\frac{m'c^2}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{10}}2m_0c^2,
\qquad
p_*c=\sqrt{E_*^2-\left(\frac{m_0c^2}{2}\right)^2}
=\frac32m_0c^2.
$$

Thus their rest-frame [velocities](../../../velocity.md) are $v_{*\pm}=\pm3c/\sqrt{10}$. Applying the [relativistic velocity-addition formula](../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) with boost $v'=3c/13$ gives the two laboratory [velocities](../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{v_\pm}{c}
=\frac{3/13\pm3/\sqrt{10}}{1\pm9/(13\sqrt{10})}.}
$$

Their laboratory [energies](../../../energy-split.md) follow directly from the [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md) of [four-momentum](../../../four-momentum.md):

$$
\boxed{E_\pm=\gamma'(E_*\pm v'p_*)
=\left(\frac{13}{8}\pm\frac{9}{8\sqrt{10}}\right)m_0c^2.}
$$

The corresponding signed laboratory [momenta](../../../momentum-split.md) are

$$
p_\pm=\left(\frac38\pm\frac{39}{8\sqrt{10}}\right)m_0c.
$$

Their sums recover $E_i$ and $p_i$, and each satisfies $E_\pm^2-p_\pm^2c^2=(m_0c^2/2)^2$. One daughter moves forwards and the other backwards in the laboratory; both are collinear with the original flight axis. Numerically, $v_+\simeq0.967615c$, $v_-\simeq-0.919138c$, $E_+\simeq1.980756m_0c^2$ and $E_-\simeq1.269244m_0c^2$.
