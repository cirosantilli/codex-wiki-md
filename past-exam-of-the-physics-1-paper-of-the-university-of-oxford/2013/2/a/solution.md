<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md) $q_j$ is absent explicitly from the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md): $\partial L/\partial q_j=0$, even though $\dot q_j$ may occur. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_j}\right)=0,\qquad p_j=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_j}=\text{constant}.}
$$

Thus a continuous coordinate-translation symmetry gives a conserved [conjugate momentum](../../../../canonical-momentum.md). The conserved quantity need not be ordinary linear [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md): an absent azimuthal angle can give conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md). The derivative is taken holding the other coordinates and [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) fixed.
