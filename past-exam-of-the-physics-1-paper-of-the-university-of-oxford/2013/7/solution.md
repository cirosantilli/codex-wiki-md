<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The [alpha particle](../../../alpha-particle.md) has [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) $2e$ and acquires [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) $K=2e(20\,\mathrm{kV})=40\,\mathrm{keV}$. For [Coulomb head-on closest approach with target recoil](../../../coulomb-head-on-closest-approach-with-target-recoil.md), the initially stationary [beryllium](../../../beryllium.md) nucleus is free to recoil. Approximating the nuclear [masses](../../../mass.md) by $4u$ and $9u$, the initial relative-motion [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
K_* = \frac{m_{\rm Be}}{m_\alpha+m_{\rm Be}}K=\frac9{13}(40\,\mathrm{keV})=27.6923\,\mathrm{keV}.
$$

At closest separation the relative radial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) vanishes, although the [centre of mass](../../../center-of-mass.md) continues moving. [Conservation of energy](../../../conservation-of-energy.md) for the relative motion gives

$$
\frac{(2e)(4e)}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r_{\min}}=K_*,\qquad
\boxed{r_{\min}=\frac{8e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0K_*}\simeq4.16\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Using $e^2/(4\pi\varepsilon_0)=1.43996\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}$ gives $r_{\min}\simeq416\,\mathrm{fm}$. If the nucleus were externally held fixed, the relevant [energy](../../../energy-split.md) would be $K$, yielding $288\,\mathrm{fm}$ instead. Initially stationary is not the same as constrained stationary; the given proton and neutron counts supply the recoil correction. Nuclear sizes and strong interactions are negligible at this separation.
