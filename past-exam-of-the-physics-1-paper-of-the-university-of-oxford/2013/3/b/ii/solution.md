<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $m\gg M$, retain the ratio $E/m$ until a further limit is specified. Since $E\ge M$, the leading expression from [a](../../a.md) is

$$
\boxed{E'\simeq\frac{Em}{m+2E}=\frac{E}{1+2E/m}.}
$$

For an infinitely heavy target at fixed incident [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), $E/m\to0$ and $\boxed{E'\to E}$: the projectile reverses with negligible target recoil [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). If the incident particle is ultrarelativistic even relative to the target, $E\gg m\gg M$, the same leading expression instead gives $E'\sim m/2$. The mass hierarchy alone does not justify replacing $E/m$ by zero.
