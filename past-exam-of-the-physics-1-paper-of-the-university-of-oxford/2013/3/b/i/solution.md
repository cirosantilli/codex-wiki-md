<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $M\gg m$ and $Em\gg M^2$, one also has $E\gg M^2/m\gg M$. The denominator in [a](../../a.md) is then dominated by $2Em$, and the numerator by $EM^2$:

$$
\boxed{E'\sim\frac{M^2}{2m}.}
$$

This residual total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is still much larger than the incident particle's [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) $M$, but is a small fraction of its incident [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E$. The target acquires most of the available [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in this high-energy, heavy-projectile limit.
