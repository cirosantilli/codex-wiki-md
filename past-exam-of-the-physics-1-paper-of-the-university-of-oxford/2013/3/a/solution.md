<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [relativistic head-on elastic collision with a stationary target](../../../../relativistic-head-on-elastic-collision-with-a-stationary-target.md), use $c=1$ as stipulated, so [masses](../../../../mass.md) are measured in [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) units. The incoming [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is $p=\sqrt{E^2-M^2}$ and the invariant squared total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
s=(E+m)^2-p^2=M^2+m^2+2mE.
$$

The [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) moves at $\beta_*=p/(E+m)$, with $\gamma_*=(E+m)/\sqrt s$. In this frame the incident particle has

$$
E_M^*=\frac{M^2+mE}{\sqrt s},\qquad p_M^*=\gamma_*(p-\beta_*E)=\frac{mp}{\sqrt s}.
$$

A nontrivial head-on [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md) reverses $p_M^*$ and preserves $E_M^*$. Boosting back to the [laboratory frame](../../../../laboratory-frame.md) gives

$$
E'=\gamma_*(E_M^*-\beta_*p_M^*)=\frac{(E+m)(M^2+mE)-m(E^2-M^2)}s,
$$



$$
\boxed{E'=\frac{2mM^2+E(m^2+M^2)}{2Em+m^2+M^2}.}
$$

The no-interaction forward branch instead leaves $E'=E$; the requested formula describes the actual one-dimensional encounter and recoil.
