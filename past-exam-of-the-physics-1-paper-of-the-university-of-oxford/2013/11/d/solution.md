<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Setting $1/r\to0$ in [b](../b.md) gives the asymptotic [speed](../../../../speed.md)

$$
\boxed{v_\infty=\sqrt{22^2-\frac{2(1.32712\times10^{11})}{1.5\times10^9}}\simeq17.52\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The radicand is positive, so the ideal probe has positive total [mechanical energy](../../../../mechanical-energy.md) and escapes the [Sun](../../../../sun.md). The later dust-cloud model takes this limiting [speed](../../../../speed.md) as its initial [speed](../../../../speed.md).
