<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mu=GM_*$ and use plane polar coordinates in the orbital plane. The Newtonian [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) is $U=-\mu M_p/r$, so

$$
\boxed{L=\frac{M_p}{2}(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2)+\frac{GM_*M_p}{r}.}
$$

A [central force](../../../../central-force.md) keeps the orbit in a plane. The star is treated as stationary, appropriate when $M_p\ll M_*$; otherwise the two-body reduction uses the [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) and the relative separation.
