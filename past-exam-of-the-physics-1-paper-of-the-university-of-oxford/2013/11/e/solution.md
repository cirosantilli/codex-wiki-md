<h1 id="11/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For [constant-area capture of stationary dust](../../../../constant-area-capture-of-stationary-dust.md), let $x=0$ at cloud entry, $M(0)=M_p$, $v(0)=v_\infty$, and define $k=\rho A$ with fixed collecting area $A$. Every swept volume $A\,dx$ adds stationary [mass](../../../../mass.md), so

$$
\frac{dM}{dt}=kv,\qquad M=M_p+kx.
$$

There is no external longitudinal [force](../../../../force-split.md), and the incoming dust has zero [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) in the cloud frame. The [variable-mass system](../../../../variable-mass-system.md) therefore satisfies $d(Mv)/dt=0$, rather than $M\dot v=0$. Using $Mv=M_pv_\infty$ gives

$$
M\dot M=kM_pv_\infty,\qquad M^2=M_p^2+2kM_pv_\infty t.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{M(t)=M_p\sqrt{1+\frac{2\rho Av_\infty t}{M_p}},\qquad v(t)=\frac{v_\infty}{\sqrt{1+2\rho Av_\infty t/M_p}},}
$$



$$
\boxed{x(t)=\frac{M_p}{\rho A}\left[\sqrt{1+\frac{2\rho Av_\infty t}{M_p}}-1\right].}
$$

The [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) decreases during sticking: $dK/dt=-\tfrac12\dot M v^2$. The lost [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) becomes internal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Radiation, gravity, sputtering and changes in area are excluded by the ideal complete-collection model. The zero-density limit recovers $M=M_p$, $v=v_\infty$ and $x=v_\infty t$.
