<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Retain the source's idealized solar radial trajectory. Use $\mu_\odot\simeq1.32712\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{km^3\,s^{-2}}$, $r_i=1.5\times10^9\,\mathrm{km}$ and $v_i=22\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. At $r=1.8\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{km}$,

$$
\boxed{v=\sqrt{22^2+2(1.32712\times10^{11})\left(\frac1{1.8\times10^{10}}-\frac1{1.5\times10^9}\right)}\simeq17.94\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The [speed](../../../../speed.md) has decreased because the probe gains [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) while moving away from the [Sun](../../../../sun.md). This is the supplied simplified model, rather than a reconstruction of [Voyager 1](../../../../voyager-1.md)'s actual three-dimensional trajectory or planetary encounters.
