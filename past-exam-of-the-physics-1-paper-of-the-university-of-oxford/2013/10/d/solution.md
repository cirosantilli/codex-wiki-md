<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [velocity-addition formula](../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) combines the ship's station-frame [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) and the capsule's ship-frame [velocity](../../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{v_c}{c}=\frac{0.85+0.9}{1+0.85(0.9)}=\frac{350}{353}\simeq0.9915014.}
$$

For the distance convention in [i](../c/i.md), station-frame arrival takes $D/v_c=(353/350)\,\mathrm{day}=1.008571\,\mathrm{day}$. Transforming its two endpoint events back to the ship frame gives $\gamma_s[D/v_c-0.85D/c]$, agreeing with [i](../c/i.md). The capsule remains below the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md) in either frame.
