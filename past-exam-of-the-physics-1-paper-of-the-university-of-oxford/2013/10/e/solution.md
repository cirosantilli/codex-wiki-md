<h1 id="10/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The capsule continues inertially at $v_c/c=350/353$ while the ship reduces its station-frame [speed](../../../../speed.md) to $0.7c$. Transform the ship's new [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) into the capsule frame:

$$
\boxed{\frac{v_{s/c}}c=\frac{0.7-350/353}{1-0.7(350/353)}=-\frac{343}{360}\simeq-0.952778.}
$$

Thus its [speed](../../../../speed.md) is $0.952778c$, and the negative sign means it recedes behind the capsule along the chosen axis. The same transformation compares the subsequent local inertial [velocities](../../../../velocity.md); the details of the intervening deceleration are not needed.
