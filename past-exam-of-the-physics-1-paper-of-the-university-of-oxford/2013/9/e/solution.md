<h1 id="9/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $\theta=\theta_e+\delta$ at the stable [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). Since $M\sin\theta_e=m$, linearization of [d](../d.md) gives

$$
(M+m)R\ddot\delta+Mg\cos\theta_e\,\delta=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\omega_0=\sqrt{\frac{Mg\cos\theta_e}{(M+m)R}}=\sqrt{\frac{g\sqrt{M^2-m^2}}{(M+m)R}},\qquad f_0=\frac{\omega_0}{2\pi}.}
$$

As $m\to0$, the result approaches $\sqrt{g/R}$, the [simple pendulum](../../../../simple-pendulum-split.md) value. As $m\uparrow M$, the restoring curvature tends to zero and this small-amplitude [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) tends to zero; the endpoint itself is not a stable quadratic minimum.
