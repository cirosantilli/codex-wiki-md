<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) from [a](../a.md) is $2MR^2\ddot\theta+MgR\sin\theta=0$. Linearizing about $\theta=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\rm fixed}=\sqrt{\frac{g}{2R}},\qquad f_{\rm fixed}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{g}{2R}},\qquad T_{\rm fixed}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2R}{g}}.}
$$

Both [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) and ordinary [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) are given to distinguish the two conventions. The stable position places the extra [mass](../../../../mass.md) below the pivot.
