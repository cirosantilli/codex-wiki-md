<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Treat the asteroid as a [point mass](../../../point-mass.md) with negligible influence on the planet. Outside a spherical planet, [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) gives the specific [gravitational potential](../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $-GM/r$. The conserved specific [energy](../../../energy-split.md) and specific [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) are

$$
\mathcal E=\frac12v_0^2,\qquad h=dv_0.
$$

At the [periapsis](../../../periapsis.md) $r_p$, the radial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) vanishes, so [conservation of energy](../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\frac12v_0^2=\frac{d^2v_0^2}{2r_p^2}-\frac{GM}{r_p},\qquad
\boxed{d^2=r_p^2+\frac{2GMr_p}{v_0^2}.}
$$

The right-hand side increases with $r_p>0$. The grazing threshold $r_p=R$ is therefore the [grazing impact parameter in gravitational focusing](../../../grazing-impact-parameter-in-gravitational-focusing.md) relation. For $d>R$ its equivalent speed is

$$
\boxed{v_{\rm graze}=\sqrt{\frac{2GMR}{d^2-R^2}},\qquad
\text{strict avoidance requires }v_0>v_{\rm graze}.}
$$

The threshold is customarily called the minimum avoiding [speed](../../../speed.md), with tangency counted as its limiting boundary. If any contact with the surface counts as a hit, it is an infimum, not an attained minimum. For $d\le R$, **no finite incoming speed avoids contact**: [gravitational focusing](../../../gravitational-focusing.md) bends the path inward, and even its infinite-speed straight-line limit has closest approach $d\le R$. The finite radius of the asteroid has been neglected.
