<h1 id="8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A totally [inelastic collision](../../../../inelastic-collision.md) in the sticking sense has zero final relative [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), so $q=0$. Both [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) are the conserved [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) [velocity](../../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v'_1=\mathbf v'_2=\frac{M_1u_1}{M_1+M_2}\widehat{\mathbf x}.}
$$

Thus the bodies move together along the original incident direction; the moving projectile has $\theta_1=0$. The common outgoing state is stationary in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). The lost translational [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
\Delta K=\frac12\frac{M_1M_2}{M_1+M_2}u_1^2,
$$

which can become internal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). As explained in part [8](../../8.md), setting only the normal [coefficient of restitution](../../../../coefficient-of-restitution.md) to zero in a smooth [oblique collision](../../../../oblique-collision.md) does **not** force sticking: tangential relative motion can remain.
