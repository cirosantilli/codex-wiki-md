<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take rightward translation as positive and counterclockwise [angular acceleration](../../../../../angular-acceleration.md) $\alpha$ as positive. Let $f$ denote signed ground [friction](../../../../../friction.md), positive to the right. The source's lower-tangent winding makes the [tension](../../../../../tension.md) exert counterclockwise [torque](../../../../../torque.md) $Tr$. For a horizontal pull, translation and rotation obey

$$
Ma=T+f,\qquad I\alpha=Tr+Rf,\qquad a=-R\alpha.
$$

The final relation is the [rolling without slipping](../../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) constraint. Eliminating $f$ and $\alpha$ gives

$$
\boxed{a=\frac{TR(R-r)}{I+MR^2}>0,\qquad
f=-T\frac{I+MrR}{I+MR^2}.}
$$

Thus the requested magnitude of [static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) is

$$
\boxed{F=|f|=T\frac{I+MrR}{I+MR^2}.}
$$

It acts leftward. Released from rest, the spool accelerates **rightward, toward the pulling hand**, and rotates clockwise. There is no contradiction between leftward [friction](../../../../../friction.md) and rightward motion: [static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) prevents slipping of the contact point, and the horizontal [tension](../../../../../tension.md) is larger than $|f|$ because $r<R$.
