<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Keep the signs of part [i](../i.md). The [tension](../../../../../tension.md) has horizontal component $T\cos\theta$ but still has counterclockwise [torque](../../../../../torque.md) $Tr$, since it is tangent to the axle. Thus

$$
Ma=T\cos\theta+f,\qquad I\alpha=Tr+Rf,\qquad a=-R\alpha.
$$

Solving these equations gives the [critical pulling angle of a rolling spool](../../../../../critical-pulling-angle-of-a-rolling-spool.md) law

$$
\boxed{a=\frac{TR(R\cos\theta-r)}{I+MR^2},\qquad
f=-T\frac{I\cos\theta+MrR}{I+MR^2}.}
$$

The zero-acceleration angle is therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta_c=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac rR\right).}
$$

At this angle the line of action of the pull passes through the ground contact, so its [torque](../../../../../torque.md) about that point vanishes. A spool initially at rest stays at rest while the contact constraints hold; one already rolling has zero [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md), rather than being brought to rest.

For equilibrium the vertical [normal force](../../../../../normal-force.md) is $N=Mg-T\sin\theta_c$ and $|f|=T\cos\theta_c$. The [static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) inequality $|f|\le\mu N$ becomes

$$
T(\cos\theta_c+\mu\sin\theta_c)\le\mu Mg.
$$

Hence, for $0<r<R$,

$$
\boxed{T_{\max}=\frac{\mu Mg}{\cos\theta_c+\mu\sin\theta_c}
=\frac{\mu MgR}{r+\mu\sqrt{R^2-r^2}}.}
$$

At this limit the [normal force](../../../../../normal-force.md) is still nonnegative; for finite $\mu$ and positive $r$ it is positive. For $\theta_c<\theta\le\pi/2$, the numerator of $a$ is negative. Released from rest, the spool rolls leftward, **away from the pulling hand**, with counterclockwise rotation, provided the assumed [rolling without slipping](../../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) and ground contact remain possible. The nonzero [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) is not obtained by assuming that [friction](../../../../../friction.md) simply opposes the centre's motion.
