<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Put $\beta=v/c$ and $\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}$, the common [Lorentz factor](../../../lorentz-factor.md). The initial total [relativistic energy](../../../relativistic-energy.md) and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) are

$$
E=\gamma mc^2+\gamma\frac m2c^2=\frac32\gamma mc^2,\qquad
P_x=\gamma mv-\gamma\frac m2v=\frac12\gamma mv.
$$

Assume that coalescence emits no additional radiation. By [four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), the final [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) $m_f$ is the [rest mass of a coalescing relativistic collision](../../../rest-mass-of-a-coalescing-relativistic-collision.md):

$$
m_f^2c^4=E^2-c^2P_x^2,
\qquad
\boxed{m_f=\frac{\gamma m}{2}\sqrt{9-\beta^2}
=\frac m2\sqrt{\frac{9-\beta^2}{1-\beta^2}}.}
$$

The final [velocity](../../../velocity.md) is $V=c^2P_x/E=v/3$, the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) [velocity](../../../velocity.md).

For $v\ll c$,

$$
\boxed{m_f=\frac32m+\frac23m\frac{v^2}{c^2}+O(v^4/c^4).}
$$

The leading term is the classical sum of [masses](../../../mass.md). The first correction is the internal [energy](../../../energy-split.md) retained after sticking, divided by $c^2$: the initial classical [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $3mv^2/4$, while the common-motion [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $\tfrac12(3m/2)(v/3)^2=mv^2/12$. Their difference is $2mv^2/3$, consistent with [mass-energy equivalence](../../../mass-energy-equivalence.md).
