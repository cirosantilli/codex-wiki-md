<h1 id="10/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Initially $r_i=l/2$, $\dot r_i=0$ and $\dot\theta_i=v_0/r_i$, so the conserved [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $J=mlv_0/2$. For $v_0\ne0$, the [effective potential](../../../../../effective-potential.md) contains the [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md)

$$
U_J(r)\sim\frac{J^2}{2mr^2}\longrightarrow+\infty\quad(r\to0^+).
$$

The finite conserved [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) therefore forbids the hole from being reached:

$$
\boxed{v_0\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ r(t)>0\ \text{throughout the ideal motion}.}
$$

The exceptional case $v_0=0$ has no [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md). Then $J=0$ and $\ddot r=-Mg/(M+m)$; starting at rest at $l/2$, the tabletop [mass](../../../../../mass.md) reaches the hole after $t=\sqrt{(M+m)l/(Mg)}$. The model ends at that event. Thus the usual nonzero initial tangential [speed](../../../../../speed.md) assumption matters.
