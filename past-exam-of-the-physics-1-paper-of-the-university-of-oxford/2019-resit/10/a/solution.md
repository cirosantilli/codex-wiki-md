<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [massless inextensible string](../../../../massless-inextensible-string.md) has hanging length $y=l-r$, measured downward from the table. Thus $\dot y=-\dot r$. In [polar coordinates](../../../../polar-coordinates.md), the tabletop [mass](../../../../mass.md) has squared [speed](../../../../speed.md) $\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2$. Adding the two [kinetic energies](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and setting the hanging [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) to $-Mgy$ gives

$$
T=\frac12(M+m)\dot r^2+\frac12mr^2\dot\theta^2,\qquad
V=-Mg(l-r),
$$

and the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{L=T-V=\frac12(M+m)\dot r^2+\frac12mr^2\dot\theta^2+Mg(l-r).}
$$

The [canonical momenta](../../../../canonical-momentum.md) are $p_r=(M+m)\dot r$ and $p_\theta=mr^2\dot\theta$. Performing the [Legendre transform](../../../../convex-conjugate.md) gives the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H=p_r\dot r+p_\theta\dot\theta-L
=\frac{p_r^2}{2(M+m)}+\frac{p_\theta^2}{2mr^2}-Mg(l-r).}
$$

It equals the total mechanical [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and is conserved because $L$ has no explicit [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) dependence. Also $\theta$ is a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so $J=p_\theta=mr^2\dot\theta$ is conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) about the vertical through the hole. The table and the string guide supply external [forces](../../../../force-split.md); conservation of the system's total linear [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) does not follow. These are the two independent generic integrals needed for the reduced [tabletop mass coupled to a hanging mass](../../../../tabletop-mass-coupled-to-a-hanging-mass.md).
