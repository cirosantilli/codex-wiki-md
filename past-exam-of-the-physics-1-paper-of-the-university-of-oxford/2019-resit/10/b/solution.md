<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The radial and angular [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
(M+m)\ddot r=mr\dot\theta^2-Mg,\qquad
\frac d{dt}(mr^2\dot\theta)=0.
$$

Writing the conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) as

$$
\boxed{J=mr^2\dot\theta,}
$$

we obtain

$$
\boxed{(M+m)\ddot r=\frac{J^2}{mr^3}-Mg.}
$$

A positive constant radius requires zero radial [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md). Hence the [circular motion](../../../../circular-motion.md) has

$$
\boxed{r_0=\left(\frac{J^2}{mMg}\right)^{1/3},}
$$

provided $J\ne0$ and $0<r_0<l$. Its angular [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is $\dot\theta_0=J/(mr_0^2)$. No positive circular radius exists for $J=0$ with $M,g>0$.

For later use, the conserved radial [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\mathcal H=\frac12(M+m)\dot r^2+U_J(r),\qquad
\boxed{U_J(r)=\frac{J^2}{2mr^2}+Mgr-Mgl.}
$$

The [effective potential](../../../../effective-potential.md) has $U_J''(r)=3J^2/(mr^4)>0$, so its stationary radius is a stable minimum. The [tension](../../../../tension.md) remains positive in the ideal allowed region: the hanging [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives $T_s=M(g+\ddot r)=Mm(g+r\dot\theta^2)/(M+m)>0$.
