<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The dimensions of [mass density](../../../density.md), [time](../../../time-in-physics.md) and [energy](../../../energy-split.md) are $\mathsf M\mathsf L^{-3}$, $\mathsf T$ and $\mathsf M\mathsf L^2\mathsf T^{-2}$. Matching powers in the proposed [dimensional analysis](../../../dimensional-analysis.md) gives

$$
\mathsf L=\mathsf M^{\alpha+\gamma}\mathsf L^{-3\alpha+2\gamma}\mathsf T^{\beta-2\gamma},\qquad
\alpha+\gamma=0,\quad -3\alpha+2\gamma=1,\quad \beta-2\gamma=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\alpha=-\frac15,\qquad\beta=\frac25,\qquad\gamma=\frac15,\qquad R=C\left(\frac{Et^2}{\rho}\right)^{1/5}.}
$$

This is the [Sedov-Taylor blast wave](../../../sedov-taylor-blast-wave.md) scaling for a fixed released [energy](../../../energy-split.md). [Dimensional analysis](../../../dimensional-analysis.md) cannot determine the dimensionless $C$; that requires the fluid equations and thermodynamic assumptions. The assumed list of controlling parameters excludes, for example, an independent ambient sound speed or a continuing energy supply.
