# 4

↑ **Parent:** [2015](../2015-split.md)

Use a circular [Earth](../../earth.md) orbit of radius $a$ about the [Sun](../../sun.md), neglect Earth's rotation, the other planets and atmospheric [drag](../../drag-physics.md), and treat the launch as an initial impulse followed by unpowered flight of constant [mass](../../mass.md). Define

$$
v_E=\sqrt{\frac{GM_\odot}{a}},\qquad v_{e,E}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM_E}{R_E}}.
$$

In the [patched conic approximation](../../patched-conic-approximation.md), a surface launch at [speed](../../speed.md) $u$ leaves Earth's gravitational field with Earth-relative [hyperbolic excess speed](../../hyperbolic-excess-speed.md) $w$, where

$$
u^2=v_{e,E}^2+w^2.
$$

Thus the necessary solar-orbit change must be supplied in addition to escaping Earth's gravity; simply adding the two escape speeds is incorrect. The asymptotic departure direction can be chosen to minimize $w$. Representative values are $v_E\simeq29.78\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ and $v_{e,E}\simeq11.186\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$.[Orbital parameters: mean orbital velocity 29.78 km/s; bulk parameters: escape velocity 11.186 km/s](../../earth-fact-sheet-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)
