<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Work in the parent's [rest frame](../../../rest-frame.md), with $M_A>0$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
E_B+E_C=M_Ac^2,\qquad \mathbf p_B=-\mathbf p_C,
$$

so both daughter [momenta](../../../momentum-split.md) have magnitude $p$. Their [relativistic energy-momentum relations](../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) give $E_B^2-p^2c^2=M_B^2c^4$ and $E_C^2-p^2c^2=M_C^2c^4$. Subtracting these two relations and using the total [energy](../../../energy-split.md) yields

$$
\boxed{E_B=\frac{M_A^2+M_B^2-M_C^2}{2M_A}c^2,\qquad
E_C=\frac{M_A^2+M_C^2-M_B^2}{2M_A}c^2.}
$$

Substitution back gives the [relativistic two-body decay](../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md) momentum:

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{c}{2M_A}\sqrt{\bigl[M_A^2-(M_B+M_C)^2\bigr]\bigl[M_A^2-(M_B-M_C)^2\bigr]},\qquad
\mathbf p_B=p\mathbf n,\quad\mathbf p_C=-p\mathbf n.}
$$

The unit [vector](../../../vector.md) $\mathbf n$ is not fixed by [four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) alone. The expression under the square root is the [Källén function](../../../kallen-function.md) $\kappa(M_A^2,M_B^2,M_C^2)$. The physical [two-body decay threshold](../../../two-body-decay-threshold.md) is $M_A\geq M_B+M_C$; for massive daughters equality gives zero outgoing [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md). For two massless daughters, each has [energy](../../../energy-split.md) $M_Ac^2/2$ and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude $M_Ac/2$.
