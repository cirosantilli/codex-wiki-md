Take $M$ as the constant [mass](../../mass.md) of the sled and rider when snow is immediately removed. Assume the falling snow has zero horizontal [velocity](../../velocity.md) in the ice frame, and its arrival rate $\sigma$ is the specified constant [mass](../../mass.md) flux onto the sled. In a [snow-collecting sled](../../snow-collecting-sled.md), horizontal [momentum flux](../../momentum-flux.md), including the departing snow, determines the change in sled [momentum](../../momentum-split.md).
