<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Rough ground, smooth cylinder contacts.** Let $N$ be either upper-to-lower normal [force](../../../../../force-split.md), and $f_g$ the magnitude of the inward ground [friction](../../../../../friction.md) [force](../../../../../force-split.md) on each lower cylinder. Pure rolling on the ground requires $|\alpha|=u/R$. The [torque](../../../../../torque.md) equation about a lower cylinder's centre is

$$
f_gR=(\beta mR^2)\frac uR,\qquad f_g=\beta mu.
$$

The outward translation equation is $mu=N/2-f_g$, giving $N=2m(1+\beta)u$. The top cylinder's vertical equation is $mw=mg-\sqrt3N$. Using $u=\sqrt3w$ yields

$$
(7+6\beta)w=g,
\qquad
\boxed{a_{\mathrm{top,down}}=\frac{g}{7+6\beta}.}
$$

For uniform solid cylinders, $\beta=1/2$ and $a_{\mathrm{top,down}}=g/10$. The left lower cylinder rotates counterclockwise while rolling left; the right rotates clockwise while rolling right.

The assumed no-slip ground contact is possible if its [coefficient of static friction](../../../../../coefficient-of-static-friction.md) satisfies $\mu_g\geq\beta\sqrt3/(10+9\beta)$ at release. This follows from $f_g=\beta m\sqrt3w$ and ground normal [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $N_g=mg+\sqrt3N/2=mw(10+9\beta)$.
