<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The lines of centres from either lower cylinder to the upper cylinder make $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. Let $N_L,N_R$ be the upward [normal forces](../../../../normal-force.md) on the top cylinder from the left and right cylinders, and let $Q$ be the lower cylinders' mutual normal [force](../../../../force-split.md). While all three contacts remain closed, the triangular configuration translates horizontally with common [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) $a$; frictionless normals exert no [torques](../../../../torque.md) about the cylinder centres.

For the top cylinder, [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
\frac{N_L-N_R}{2}=ma,\qquad\frac{\sqrt3}{2}(N_L+N_R)=mg.
$$

Thus $N_L=m(g/\sqrt3+a)$ and $N_R=m(g/\sqrt3-a)$. The right lower cylinder has horizontal equation $N_R/2+Q=ma$, so

$$
Q=\frac m2\left(3a-\frac g{\sqrt3}\right).
$$

A contact may push but cannot pull: $N_L,N_R,Q\geq0$. The limiting inequalities are therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{g}{3\sqrt3}\leq a\leq\frac g{\sqrt3}.}
$$

At the lower bound, $Q=0$ and the lower pair are just losing compression; at the upper bound, $N_R=0$ and the upper-right contact is just losing compression. All three pairwise normal [forces](../../../../force-split.md) are strictly positive only inside the open interval. The ground normal [forces](../../../../force-split.md) are positive throughout this range. The applied horizontal [force](../../../../force-split.md) on the left cylinder is $F=3ma$, as follows from the whole system's horizontal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) balance.
