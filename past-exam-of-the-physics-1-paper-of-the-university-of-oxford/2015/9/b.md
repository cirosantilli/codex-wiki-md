Assume the cylinders are released from rest. In each case, “there is friction” is interpreted as sufficient [static friction](../../../static-friction.md) for [rolling without slipping](../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) at the rough contacts. Without a coefficient or a no-slip assumption, sliding cases would not have a unique specified acceleration. On release the two lower cylinders separate; their mutual contact must not be retained as a rigid constraint.

Let the top cylinder's initial downward [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) be $w$, and each lower cylinder's outward horizontal [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) be $u$. The top cylinder initially has no horizontal [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md). Differentiating the fixed upper-to-lower centre separation twice at the initial zero [velocity](../../../velocity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u=\sqrt3\,w.}
$$

By reflection symmetry the top cylinder has zero [angular acceleration](../../../angular-acceleration.md), while the lower cylinders rotate in opposite senses.
