<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The photon starts at $x'_E=\gamma L$ and travels left to the runner at the fixed [position](../../../../../position.md) $x'=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{d'_\gamma=\gamma L,\qquad \Delta t'_\gamma=\frac{\gamma L}{c}.}
$$

This distance is not the simultaneous contracted tunnel length $L/\gamma$, because the runner is not at the entrance when the photon is emitted in $S'$. Adding the entrance-to-runner gap from part (ii) to $L/\gamma$ indeed gives $\gamma L$.
