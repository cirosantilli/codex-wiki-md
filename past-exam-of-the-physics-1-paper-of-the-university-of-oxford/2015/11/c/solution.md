<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The photon propagates along $-x$ in the tunnel frame and has [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda_0$ and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_x=-E_\gamma/c$. A [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) into the approaching runner's frame gives

$$
E'_\gamma=\gamma(E_\gamma-vp_x)=\gamma(1+\beta)E_\gamma.
$$

Since $E'_\gamma=hc/\lambda'$, the approaching-source [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lambda'=\frac{\lambda_0}{\gamma(1+\beta)}=\lambda_0\sqrt{\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}}.}
$$

It is a blueshift. With $q=\lambda'/\lambda_0=500.0/589.1$, solving $q^2=(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)$ gives

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac{1-q^2}{1+q^2}=\frac{589.1^2-500.0^2}{589.1^2+500.0^2}\simeq0.16253,
\qquad v\simeq4.873\times10^7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

This shift depends on the relative [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) along the light ray, not on the tunnel length or the location of detection.
