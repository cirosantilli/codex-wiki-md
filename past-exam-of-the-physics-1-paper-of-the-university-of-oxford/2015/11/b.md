Let $S'$ be the runner's [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md), with origin at the entry event. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is

$$
x'=\gamma(x-vt),\qquad t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\qquad\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}.
$$

The emission event $E=(L,0)$ has coordinates $x'_E=\gamma L$, $t'_E=-\gamma vL/c^2$. It is earlier than entry in $S'$: entry and far-end emission are simultaneous only in the tunnel frame. This is [relativity of simultaneity](../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md), not an extra delay of the light source.
