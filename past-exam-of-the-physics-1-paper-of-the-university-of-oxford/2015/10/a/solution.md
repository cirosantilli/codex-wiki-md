<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the pivot's prescribed horizontal [position](../../../../position.md) be $x(t)$ and measure $\theta$ from the downward vertical. The bob's coordinates are $X=x+l\sin\theta$ and $Y=-l\cos\theta$. Therefore

$$
\dot X^2+\dot Y^2=\dot x^2+2l\dot x\dot\theta\cos\theta+l^2\dot\theta^2.
$$

With zero [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) at pivot height, the bob's [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac m2\bigl(\dot x^2+2l\dot x\dot\theta\cos\theta+l^2\dot\theta^2\bigr)+mgl\cos\theta.}
$$

Here $x(t)$ is a prescribed input, not yet a free coordinate. Terms depending only on that prescribed [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) function can be dropped without changing the $\theta$ equation. The moving block's own [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) becomes relevant when its motion is free in part (d).
