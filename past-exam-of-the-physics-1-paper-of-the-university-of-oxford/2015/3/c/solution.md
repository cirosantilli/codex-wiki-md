<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The snow remains aboard, so the moving [mass](../../../../mass.md) grows as $M+\sigma t$. Incoming snow contributes no horizontal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), and there is no horizontal external [force](../../../../force-split.md). Therefore

$$
\frac d{dt}\bigl[(M+\sigma t)v\bigr]=0,
\qquad
\boxed{v_C(t)=\frac{Mv_0}{M+\sigma t}.}
$$

The combined sled–snow system follows a [variable-mass system](../../../../variable-mass-system.md) balance; setting $(M+\sigma t)\dot v=0$ would omit the incoming [mass](../../../../mass.md) term.
