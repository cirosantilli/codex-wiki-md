<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Snow now leaves with zero horizontal [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) in the ice frame, the same as its incoming horizontal [velocity](../../../../velocity.md). The net horizontal [momentum flux](../../../../momentum-flux.md) is zero:

$$
M\dot v=0,\qquad\boxed{v_B(t)=v_0.}
$$

Any forward [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) given to the snow on capture is returned to the sled while sweeping it off. “Perpendicular” specifies a different outgoing [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) here than in case A; changing the reference frame without transforming that [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) would miss this difference.
