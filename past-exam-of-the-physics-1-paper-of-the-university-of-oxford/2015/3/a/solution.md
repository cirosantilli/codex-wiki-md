<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Snow leaves perpendicular to the motion in the rider's frame, so its outgoing horizontal [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) in the ice frame is $v$. During $dt$, incoming snow brings zero horizontal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and outgoing snow carries $\sigma v\,dt$. Thus

$$
M\dot v=-\sigma v,
\qquad
\boxed{v_A(t)=v_0e^{-\sigma t/M}.}
$$

Although the sled does not retain snow, it must continually supply the snow's departing horizontal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md).
