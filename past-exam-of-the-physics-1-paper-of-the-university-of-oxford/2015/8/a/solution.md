<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [central force](../../../../central-force.md) confines nonradial motion to a [plane](../../../../plane.md). In [plane polar coordinates](../../../../plane-polar-coordinates.md), the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) for $V(r)=\beta r^k$ is

$$
\mathcal L=\frac12m(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2)-\beta r^k.
$$

The [azimuth](../../../../azimuth.md) $\phi$ is a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so $L=mr^2\dot\phi$ is conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md). The Lagrangian has no explicit [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) dependence, giving conserved [energy](../../../../energy-split.md)

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12m\dot r^2+\frac{L^2}{2mr^2}+\beta r^k=\frac12m\dot r^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r),\qquad
V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)=\frac{L^2}{2mr^2}+\beta r^k.}
$$

At fixed $L$, the radial [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) is $m\ddot r=-V_{\mathrm{eff}}'(r)$. The [effective potential](../../../../effective-potential.md) packages the transverse [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) as a centrifugal term alongside the actual [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md). It gives a one-dimensional radial problem without introducing an extra physical interaction.
