<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [circular orbit](../../../../circular-orbit.md) of radius $r_0>0$ is a stationary point of the [effective potential](../../../../effective-potential.md):

$$
0=-\frac{L^2}{mr_0^3}+\beta k r_0^{k-1}.
$$

For $L\ne0$, $\beta k>0$ and $k\ne-2$, this gives

$$
\boxed{r_0=\left(\frac{L^2}{m\beta k}\right)^{1/(k+2)}.}
$$

The condition $\beta k>0$ makes the radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) attractive. At $k=-2$, the equation instead requires the fine tuning $L^2/m=-2\beta$; then the centrifugal and potential terms cancel at every radius and there is no isolated restoring minimum. If that equality fails, there is no positive-radius [circular orbit](../../../../circular-orbit.md) for this special power.
