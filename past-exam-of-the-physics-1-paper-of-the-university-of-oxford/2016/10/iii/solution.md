<h1 id="10/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the outer tangent point, the [vis-viva equation](../../../../vis-viva-equation.md) gives $v_a=\sqrt{\mu/r_1}\sqrt{2r_0/(r_0+r_1)}$. This is below the outer [circular Kepler orbit](../../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md)'s speed $v_1=\sqrt{\mu/r_1}$. The second impulse is therefore also prograde:

$$
\boxed{\Delta v_2=\sqrt{\frac\mu{r_1}}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{2r_0}{r_0+r_1}}\right).}
$$

It raises the [pericentre](../../../../periapsis.md) to the outer radius, completing the circularization.
