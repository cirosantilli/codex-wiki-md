<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The assumed orbital [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $E=-\mu m/(2a)$. At a point a distance $r$ from the planet,

$$
\frac12mv_e^2-\frac{\mu m}r=-\frac{\mu m}{2a},\qquad\boxed{v_e^2=\mu\left(\frac2r-\frac1a\right).}
$$

This is the [vis-viva equation](../../../../vis-viva-equation.md). The [semi-major axis](../../../../semi-major-axis.md) and radius are measured from the gravitational focus where the planet lies.
