<h1 id="10/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [Hohmann transfer orbit](../../../../hohmann-transfer-orbit.md), the inner and outer circular radii are the ellipse's [pericentre](../../../../periapsis.md) and [apocentre](../../../../apoapsis.md) distances, so $a=(r_0+r_1)/2$. The [vis-viva equation](../../../../vis-viva-equation.md) gives the transfer speed at the inner tangent point,

$$
v_p=\sqrt{\frac\mu{r_0}}\sqrt{\frac{2r_1}{r_0+r_1}}.
$$

Both old and new [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) are tangential there, so the required first impulse is prograde, with

$$
\boxed{\Delta v_1=\sqrt{\frac\mu{r_0}}\left(\sqrt{\frac{2r_1}{r_0+r_1}}-1\right).}
$$
