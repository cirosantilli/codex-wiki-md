<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

Put $\mu=GM$. Balancing [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) with the required [centripetal force](../../../centripetal-force.md) gives $mv_0^2/r_0=\mu m/r_0^2$, hence

$$
\boxed{v_0=\sqrt{\frac\mu{r_0}},\qquad E_0=\frac12mv_0^2-\frac{\mu m}{r_0}=-\frac{\mu m}{2r_0}.}
$$

The [circular Kepler orbit](../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md) is stable against small radial perturbations: its effective radial potential is $U_{\mathrm{eff}}=J^2/(2mr^2)-\mu m/r$, and at the circular value $J^2=\mu m^2r_0$, $U_{\mathrm{eff}}''(r_0)=\mu m/r_0^3>0$. Here $J$ is the conserved [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md).
