<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) is an [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) in which the system's total [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) is zero. For nonrelativistic particles with total [mass](../../../mass.md) $M_T$ and total [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathbf P$, it moves with [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf V=\mathbf P/M_T$ relative to the original frame.

Under a [Galilean boost](../../../galilean-transformation.md) by a constant [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf U$, the total [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) transforms as

$$
K'=\sum_i\frac{m_i}{2}|\mathbf v_i-\mathbf U|^2=K-\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf P+\frac12M_TU^2.
$$

For an isolated collision, [momentum conservation](../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $\Delta\mathbf P=0$, and nonrelativistic [mass](../../../mass.md) conservation gives $\Delta M_T=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta K'=\Delta K.}
$$

An [elastic collision](../../../elastic-collision.md) with $\Delta K=0$ in one [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) has $\Delta K'=0$ in every [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md). Individual particles' [kinetic energies](../../../kinetic-energy.md) change under the boost, but the total energy difference between the final and initial states does not. This is the [Galilean invariance of collision elasticity](../../../galilean-invariance-of-collision-elasticity.md).
