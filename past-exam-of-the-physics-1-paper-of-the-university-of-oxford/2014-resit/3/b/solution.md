<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) fixes the outgoing system's [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) to the same $2E/c^2$. In the rest frame of particle 3, let particle 4 have [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_4'$ and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p_4'$. Each outgoing particle has [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) $M_2$, so

$$
4E^2=(M_2c^2+E_4')^2-c^2|\mathbf p_4'|^2
=2M_2^2c^4+2M_2c^2E_4'.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{E_4'=\frac{2E^2}{M_2c^2}-M_2c^2.}
$$

Here $E_4'$ is the total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), including rest energy. At the production threshold $E=M_2c^2$, it reduces to $M_2c^2$, as it should. Production is kinematically possible only for $E\ge M_2c^2$. The initial [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) $M_1$ drops out because the conserved total [centre-of-mass energy](../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md), rather than the identity of the incoming particles, determines this outgoing relative motion.
