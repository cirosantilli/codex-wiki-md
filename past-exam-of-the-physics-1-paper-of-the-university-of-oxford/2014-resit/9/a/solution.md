<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $P=m_pc^2$ denote the proton rest energy and $W=220\,\mathrm{GeV}$ the desired particle's rest energy. For an antiproton of total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_{\bar p}$ striking a stationary proton, the incoming squared [centre-of-mass energy](../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md) is

$$
s=(E_{\bar p}+P)^2-c^2p_{\bar p}^2=2P^2+2PE_{\bar p}.
$$

For production of the stated single particle, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) requires $s=W^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{E_{\bar p}^{\rm fixed}=\frac{W^2-2P^2}{2P}\simeq25.7912\,\mathrm{TeV}.}
$$

The numerical value uses $P=0.938272\,\mathrm{GeV}$. It is a **total** incident [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the corresponding [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $E_{\bar p}^{\rm fixed}-P\simeq25.7902\,\mathrm{TeV}$. Much of the laboratory [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) supplies motion of the outgoing system rather than its [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md).

For equal and opposite beam [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), the laboratory is the [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). Each beam has the same total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and their sum is $W$, giving

$$
\boxed{E_{\bar p}^{\rm collider}=\frac W2=110\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
K_{\bar p}^{\rm collider}=110-P\simeq109.062\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

These are the [fixed-target production threshold](../../../../fixed-target-production-threshold.md) and collider kinematic comparison for the specified final state. Extra final particles or restrictions from conserved quantum numbers would require a separately specified reaction.
