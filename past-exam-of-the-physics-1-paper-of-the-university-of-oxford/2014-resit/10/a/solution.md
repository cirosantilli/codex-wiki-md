<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Conservation of angular momentum](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) means that the total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) of a system about a chosen fixed origin in an [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) stays constant. Its dynamical condition is

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathbf J}{dt}=\boldsymbol\tau_{\rm ext}=0.}
$$

Internal torques cancel for the complete isolated mechanical system. If only the component of external [torque](../../../../torque.md) about an axis vanishes, only that [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) component is necessarily conserved. The skater's upright spin uses this axial statement: vertical weight and support provide negligible vertical-axis torque, and frictional torque is neglected. The skater need not conserve [rotational kinetic energy](../../../../rotational-kinetic-energy.md) while changing the internal configuration.
