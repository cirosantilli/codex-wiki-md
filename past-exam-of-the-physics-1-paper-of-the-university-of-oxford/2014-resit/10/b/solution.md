<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The uniform cylindrical torso contributes $I_c=Mr^2/2=50(0.20)^2/2=1.00\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. Adding both arm [point masses](../../../../point-mass.md) about the same vertical axis gives

$$
I_i=1+2(7)(0.70)^2=7.86\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2},\qquad
I_f=1+2(7)(0.30)^2=2.26\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}.
$$

With initial [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_i=2\pi\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$, [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $I_i\omega_i=I_f\omega_f$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_f=2\pi\frac{7.86}{2.26}=21.8521\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

This corresponds to $3.47788$ rotations per second; it is important not to confuse that [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) with the requested [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md).
