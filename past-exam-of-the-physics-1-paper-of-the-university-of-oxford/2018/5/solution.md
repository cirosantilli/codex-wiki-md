<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For a [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md) with $S'$ moving at $+v$ along $x$ relative to $S$,

$$
x'=\gamma(x-vt),\qquad y'=y,\qquad
t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\qquad
\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}.
$$

Differentiate along the object's trajectory and divide the spatial differentials by $dt'$. Using $u_x=dx/dt$ and $u_y=dy/dt$ derives the [relativistic velocity-addition formula](../../../velocity-addition-formula.md):

$$
\boxed{u'_x=\frac{u_x-v}{1-u_xv/c^2},\qquad
u'_y=\frac{u_y}{\gamma(1-u_xv/c^2)}.}
$$

In the rest frame of ship A, take $v=c/2$ and the Earth-frame [velocity](../../../velocity.md) of B as $(0,-c/2)$. Here $\gamma=2/\sqrt3$ and the common denominator is one, so

$$
u'_{Bx}=-\frac c2,\qquad u'_{By}=-\frac{\sqrt3}{4}c.
$$

The required relative [speed](../../../speed.md) is the magnitude of this two-component [velocity](../../../velocity.md), not either component separately:

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf u'_B|=c\sqrt{\frac14+\frac3{16}}=\frac{\sqrt7}{4}c\simeq0.6614c.}
$$

The inverse [Lorentz factor](../../../lorentz-factor.md) check is $\gamma_{AB}=\gamma_A\gamma_B(1-\mathbf u_A\cdot\mathbf u_B/c^2)=4/3$, which gives the same relative [speed](../../../speed.md).
