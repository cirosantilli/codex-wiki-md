<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $X$ increase along the handle, with zero at the centre of the circular head. The ring has its [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) at $X=0$. The rod occupies $a\leq X\leq a+l$, so its [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) is at $a+l/2$. The mass-weighted mean is therefore

$$
\boxed{X_{\mathrm{CM}}=\frac{M_l(a+l/2)}{M_a+M_l}.}
$$

This uses the source diagram's rim-to-end handle length $l$, not a rod beginning at the head's centre.
