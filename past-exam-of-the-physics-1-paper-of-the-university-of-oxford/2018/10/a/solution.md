<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $x$ positive downward and put zero gravitational [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) at the spring's support. The mass is then a distance $l+x$ below that reference, so $V_g=-mg(l+x)$. [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) gives the elastic [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) $kx^2/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{L=\frac12m\dot x^2-\frac12kx^2+mg(l+x).}
$$

The constant term $mgl$ fixes the requested reference but does not affect the motion. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(m\dot x)-(-kx+mg)=0,
\qquad
\boxed{m\ddot x=mg-kx.}
$$

This is exactly [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) with gravity downward and spring [force](../../../../force-split.md) $kx$ upward for positive extension. The static extension is $x_0=mg/k$.
