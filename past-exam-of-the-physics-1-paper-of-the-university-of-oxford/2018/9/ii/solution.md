<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $0<T\ll Mc^2$, the ratio in [9](../../9.md) tends to $1/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{\theta\longrightarrow45^\circ,\qquad 2\theta\longrightarrow90^\circ.}
$$

This recovers the nonrelativistic equal-mass [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md) result: [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $\mathbf v_i=\mathbf v_1+\mathbf v_2$, while conservation of [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) gives $v_i^2=v_1^2+v_2^2$. Subtracting the squared momentum relation leaves $\mathbf v_1\cdot\mathbf v_2=0$. Exactly at $T=0$ there are no outgoing nonzero velocities defining such angles; the result is the limit through positive [kinetic energies](../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
