<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

A [Lorentz invariant](../../../lorentz-invariance.md) has the same value when the same physical system is described in any inertial frame. The components of its [energy-momentum four-vector](../../../four-momentum.md) change, but their [Minkowski norm](../../../minkowski-norm.md) does not. In particular the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{E^2-c^2|\mathbf p|^2=M^2c^4,}
$$

where $M$ is the [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md). Individual total [energy](../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) are frame-dependent; the combination on the left is invariant.

For the fixed-target annihilation, the initial total laboratory [energy](../../../energy-split.md) is $E+Mc^2$ and its total [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) is the incident particle's $\mathbf p$, with $c^2p^2=E^2-M^2c^4$. The [centre-of-mass energy](../../../center-of-mass-energy.md) available for new-particle creation is therefore

$$
\boxed{W=\sqrt{(E+Mc^2)^2-c^2p^2}
=\sqrt{2Mc^2(E+Mc^2)}.}
$$

In the [centre-of-mass frame](../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) the total [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) vanishes, so $W$ can supply the new particles' total [rest energy](../../../rest-energy.md) at threshold. One does not subtract the annihilated particles' original [rest energy](../../../rest-energy.md). The excess laboratory [energy](../../../energy-split.md) beyond $W$ is associated with motion of the whole final system in the [laboratory frame](../../../laboratory-frame.md).

For the subsequent [elastic collision](../../../elastic-collision.md), the equal and opposite transverse components of final [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) imply equal final momentum magnitudes, and hence equal final [energies](../../../energy-split.md), for the two identical particles. By [conservation of relativistic energy](../../../conservation-of-relativistic-energy.md), each has

$$
E_f=Mc^2+\frac T2.
$$

Their final momentum magnitude $p_f$ and the incident momentum magnitude $p_i$ obey

$$
c^2p_f^2=TMc^2+\frac{T^2}{4},\qquad
c^2p_i^2=T^2+2TMc^2.
$$

Longitudinal [momentum conservation](../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $p_i=2p_f\cos\theta$. It follows that the [equal-angle relativistic elastic scattering](../../../equal-angle-relativistic-elastic-scattering.md) relation is

$$
\boxed{\cos^2\theta=\frac{p_i^2}{4p_f^2}
=\frac{T+2Mc^2}{T+4Mc^2}.}
$$

This derivation assumes nonzero incident [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) and the stated symmetric outgoing paths. The angle between those paths is $2\theta$, which is evaluated in the two following parts.
