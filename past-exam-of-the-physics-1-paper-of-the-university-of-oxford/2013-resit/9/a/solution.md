<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a frame moving at [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $V\hat{\mathbf x}$, put $\Gamma=(1-V^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) of the [energy-momentum four-vector](../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{E'=\Gamma(E-Vp_x),\quad p'_x=\Gamma(p_x-VE/c^2),\quad p'_y=p_y,\quad p'_z=p_z.}
$$

Expansion gives

$$
E'^2-c^2p_x'^2=\Gamma^2\left(1-\frac{V^2}{c^2}\right)(E^2-c^2p_x^2)=E^2-c^2p_x^2.
$$

The transverse components are unchanged, hence $\boxed{E'^2-c^2|\mathbf p'|^2=E^2-c^2|\mathbf p|^2}$. For a particle of [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$ this invariant is $m^2c^4$; the same algebra also applies to the total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) of a system.
