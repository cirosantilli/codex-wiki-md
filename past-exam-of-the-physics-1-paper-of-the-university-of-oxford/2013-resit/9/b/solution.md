<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $p$ be the incident [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and $q_1,q_2$ the outgoing magnitudes in the [laboratory frame](../../../../laboratory-frame.md). Transverse [conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $(q_1-q_2)\sin\theta=0$. For the nondegenerate symmetric scattering, $q_1=q_2=q$, and the two outgoing [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) are equal, $\epsilon=(E+E_0)/2$. Then

$$
p=2q\cos\theta,\quad p^2c^2=E^2-E_0^2,\quad q^2c^2=\frac{(E+E_0)^2}{4}-E_0^2.
$$

Combining these [mass shell](../../../../mass-shell.md) relations yields

$$
\boxed{\cos^2\theta=\frac{E+E_0}{E+3E_0},\qquad \tan^2\theta=\frac{2E_0}{E+E_0}.}
$$

Here $E$ includes the incident electron's [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md). As $E\downarrow E_0$ the limiting angle is $45^\circ$, consistent with the nonrelativistic equal-mass [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md); at large $E/E_0$ the outgoing tracks become forward directed.
