<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\theta=30^\circ$, the result in [b](../b.md) gives $1/3=2E_0/(E+E_0)$, hence $E=5E_0$. Acceleration from rest supplies only the change in [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md):

$$
e|\Delta V|=E-E_0=4E_0,\qquad \boxed{|\Delta V|=\frac{4m_ec^2}{e}\simeq2.044\,\mathrm{MV}.}
$$

The accelerating [electric potential difference](../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) is a magnitude; its sign depends on which electrode is taken as the reference, since the electron has negative [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md).
