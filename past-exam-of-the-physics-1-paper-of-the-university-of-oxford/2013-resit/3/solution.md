<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Use the angle $\theta$ from the vertical as the [generalized coordinate](../../../generalized-coordinate.md). For a uniform ladder, its [centre of mass](../../../center-of-mass.md) is $(l\sin\theta,l\cos\theta)$, so $v_G^2=l^2\dot\theta^2$. Its central [moment of inertia](../../../moment-of-inertia.md) is $I_G=m(2l)^2/12=ml^2/3$. With ideal contact constraints that do no [work](../../../work.md), the [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md), [conjugate momentum](../../../canonical-momentum.md), and [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
T=\tfrac12ml^2\dot\theta^2+\tfrac12I_G\dot\theta^2=\tfrac23ml^2\dot\theta^2,\quad U=mgl\cos\theta,
$$



$$
\boxed{L=\tfrac23ml^2\dot\theta^2-mgl\cos\theta,\qquad p_\theta=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot\theta}=\tfrac43ml^2\dot\theta,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\ddot\theta=\frac{3g}{4l}\sin\theta.}
$$

Release from rest at $\theta_0$ also gives $\dot\theta^2=3g(\cos\theta_0-\cos\theta)/(2l)$ by [conservation of energy](../../../conservation-of-energy.md). These results hold while both contacts persist. The ideal wall reaction is normal to the wall; an unspecified tangential wall friction would require an additional generalized [force](../../../force-split.md) and would prevent determination from the supplied data alone.
