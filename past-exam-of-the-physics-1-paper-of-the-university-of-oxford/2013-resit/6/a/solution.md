<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The external [work](../../../../work.md) equals the change in [relativistic kinetic energy](../../../../relativistic-kinetic-energy.md), $W=m_ec^2(\gamma_f-\gamma_i)$. With $m_ec^2=511\,\mathrm{keV}$,

$$
\boxed{W_a=511\left(\frac1{\sqrt{1-0.11^2}}-\frac1{\sqrt{1-0.10^2}}\right)\mathrm{keV}\simeq0.5456\,\mathrm{keV}\simeq8.742\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm J.}
$$

The [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md) cancels between endpoints. At these [speeds](../../../../speed.md), the nonrelativistic estimate $\tfrac12m_e c^2(0.11^2-0.10^2)=0.53655\,\mathrm{keV}$ is close but slightly smaller.
