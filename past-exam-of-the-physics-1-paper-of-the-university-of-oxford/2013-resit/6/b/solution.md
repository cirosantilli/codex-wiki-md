<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Applying the same [work-energy theorem](../../../../work-energy-theorem.md) at the higher pair of [speeds](../../../../speed.md) gives

$$
\boxed{W_b=511\left(\frac1{\sqrt{1-0.99^2}}-\frac1{\sqrt{1-0.98^2}}\right)\mathrm{keV}\simeq1055\,\mathrm{keV}\simeq1.689\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm J.}
$$

The identical increment $0.01c$ now needs about two thousand times as much [work](../../../../work.md). The growth follows from the rapidly increasing [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) near the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md).
