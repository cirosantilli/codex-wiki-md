<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Let $\dot M=500\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$ and $V=10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Incoming material has zero horizontal [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md), while incorporation requires horizontal [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) $V\,dM$. Thus the engine supplies [force](../../../force-split.md) $F=V\dot M$, and its [power](../../../power.md) is

$$
\boxed{F=5000\,\mathrm N,\qquad P=FV=\dot M V^2=50\,\mathrm{kW}.}
$$

No duration or loading distance is given, so a single total [work](../../../work.md) cannot be assigned. Over an interval $t$ in seconds,

$$
\boxed{W=50{,}000t\,\mathrm J,\qquad \Delta K_{\rm horizontal}=\tfrac12\dot M V^2t=25{,}000t\,\mathrm J.}
$$

The remaining $25{,}000t\,\mathrm J$ becomes internal [energy](../../../energy-split.md) in the [inelastic collision](../../../inelastic-collision.md) that brings the falling material to the wagon's horizontal [velocity](../../../velocity.md). Vertical falling [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md), if any, is a separate contribution to dissipation; it does not alter this horizontal [work](../../../work.md) balance. The source alternates between stone and coal; the calculation depends only on the given loading rate.
