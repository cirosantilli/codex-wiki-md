<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [Lorentz factor](../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma=E/(m_\mu c^2)=20000/106=188.6792$ and $\beta=\sqrt{1-\gamma^{-2}}$. Traversal of the height $H$ takes [proper time](../../../proper-time.md) $H/(\beta\gamma c)$. Using the [exponential decay](../../../exponential-decay.md) law with the given [muon lifetime](../../../muon-lifetime.md),

$$
\boxed{P=\exp\left[-\frac{H}{c\tau_0\sqrt{\gamma^2-1}}\right]\simeq0.8180.}
$$

The laboratory flight lasts about $83.39\,\mu\mathrm s$, while the elapsed [proper time](../../../proper-time.md) is about $0.4420\,\mu\mathrm s$. This is the probability of surviving decay to the surface, with atmospheric [energy](../../../energy-split.md) loss and detector efficiency neglected.
