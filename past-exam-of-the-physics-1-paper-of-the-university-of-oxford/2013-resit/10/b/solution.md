<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a tangential launch, conserved specific [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $j=Rv$. At the outer turning point the radial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) vanishes but the tangential [speed](../../../../speed.md) is $j/r_0$. Thus

$$
\frac12v^2-\frac\mu R=\frac{R^2v^2}{2r_0^2}-\frac\mu{r_0}.
$$

Using [a](../a.md) eliminates the [gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) difference and gives

$$
\boxed{v=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt{1-R^2/r_0^2}}=v_0\left[1+\frac{R^2}{2r_0^2}+O\left(\frac{R^4}{r_0^4}\right)\right].}
$$

The expression printed in the source is the leading correction for $r_0\gg R$, rather than an exact identity. Equivalently, $v^2=2\mu r_0/[R(r_0+R)]$, so the launch [speed](../../../../speed.md) exceeds the circular value and the surface is the [periapsis](../../../../periapsis.md) of the nonintersecting ideal orbit.
