<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

About a chosen origin $O$, a particle has [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times\mathbf p$. A body has $\mathbf L=\sum_i\mathbf r_i\times\mathbf p_i$, or the corresponding integral for a continuous [mass](../../../../mass.md) distribution. For a fixed origin in an [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md),

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathbf L}{dt}=\boldsymbol\tau_{\rm external};\qquad\boldsymbol\tau_{\rm external}=0\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathbf L=\text{constant}.}
$$

Internal central pair [forces](../../../../force-split.md) cancel in the total [torque](../../../../torque.md). About a fixed origin, a [central force](../../../../central-force.md) conserves a particle's [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md); zero net [force](../../../../force-split.md) alone is insufficient if an external couple remains.
