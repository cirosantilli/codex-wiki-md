<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\mu=GM$. At the turning point of a radial launch the [speed](../../../../speed.md) vanishes, so [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\frac12v_0^2-\frac\mu R=-\frac\mu{r_0},\qquad
\boxed{r_0=\left(\frac1R-\frac{v_0^2}{2GM}\right)^{-1}=\frac{R}{1-v_0^2/v_{\rm esc}^2},\quad v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}.}
$$

This result is exact within the exterior Newtonian [gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). The condition $r_0\gg R$ means $v_0$ is just below the [escape velocity](../../../../escape-velocity.md): $1-v_0^2/v_{\rm esc}^2\ll1$. Replacing gravity by a constant would be inappropriate in this regime.
