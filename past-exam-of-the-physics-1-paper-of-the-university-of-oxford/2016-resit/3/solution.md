<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In [spherical coordinates](../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md), the [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=\frac m2\left(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+r^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2\right)-V(r).
$$

A [central force](../../../central-force.md) exerts zero [torque](../../../torque.md), so the [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) vector is constant and the orbit lies in a fixed plane. Choose that plane as $\theta=\pi/2$; then $\ell=mr^2\dot\phi$ is conserved. Substituting this relation into the [energy](../../../energy-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E=\frac m2\dot r^2+V_{\rm eff}(r),\qquad V_{\rm eff}(r)=V(r)+\frac{\ell^2}{2mr^2}.}
$$

The [effective potential](../../../effective-potential.md) reduces radial motion to a one-dimensional problem. Allowed radii have $E\geq V_{\rm eff}$, radial turning points satisfy equality, and a [circular orbit](../../../circular-orbit.md) occurs at $V_{\rm eff}'=0$. A strict local minimum gives stable radial oscillations. Eliminating $\dot\phi$ directly inside the original [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) would require a [Routhian](../../../routhian.md); the energy derivation avoids giving the centrifugal term the wrong sign.
