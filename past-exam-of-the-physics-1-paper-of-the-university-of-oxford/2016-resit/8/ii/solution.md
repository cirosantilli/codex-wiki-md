<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With clockwise rotation taken positive and $v_0=b\omega_0$, the total [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{K_0=\frac M2v_0^2+\frac I2\omega_0^2=\frac M2(a^2+b^2)\omega_0^2.}
$$

[Angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) needs an origin. Its spin about the centre is $I\omega_0$, in the clockwise direction. About a fixed point in the horizontal rail plane, spin and orbital contributions add to

$$
\boxed{L_{\rm rail}=I\omega_0+Mbv_0=M(a^2+b^2)\omega_0.}
$$

Here the drum centre is height $b$ above that plane. The signed clockwise convention fixes the displayed signs; the associated vector points perpendicular to the vertical plane of motion.
