<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

First work in the receiver's [rest frame](../../../rest-frame.md). If a source recedes at [speed](../../../speed.md) $\beta c$, two successive emissions separated by source [proper time](../../../proper-time.md) $T_0$ occur $\gamma T_0$ apart in this frame. During that interval the source retreats by $\beta c\gamma T_0$, so the arrival interval is $T_R=\gamma T_0(1+\beta)$. The longitudinal [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{\nu_R}{\nu_0}=\sqrt{\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}},\qquad
\frac{\lambda_R}{\lambda_0}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}}.}
$$

A negative $\beta$ describes approach and gives a blueshift.

Interpret $633\,\mathrm{nm}$ as the laser's rest-frame wavelength and the stated detector [velocity](../../../velocity.md) as measured in the initial laboratory frame. Choose $+x$ from source to sensor, with detector [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $-0.2c$. The required relative recession [speed](../../../speed.md) follows from

$$
q=(1000/633)^2,\qquad\beta_{\rm rec}=\frac{q-1}{q+1}=0.4278687\ldots.
$$

By [relativistic velocity addition](../../../velocity-addition-formula.md), the source [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $\beta_Sc$ in the laboratory obeys $(\beta_S+0.2)/(1+0.2\beta_S)=-\beta_{\rm rec}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta_S=-\frac{\beta_{\rm rec}+0.2}{1+0.2\beta_{\rm rec}}=-0.578375\ldots.}
$$

The laser must move **away from the sensor at about $0.5784c$ in that laboratory frame**. Although the sensor moves toward the source's initial location, the retreating source produces the required net redshift. The frame qualification matters: a [velocity](../../../velocity.md) permanently fixed relative to the moving source would specify the Doppler factor already, rather than leave it adjustable.
