<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Let $\mathbf w=\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_2$ be the [relative velocity](../../../relative-velocity.md) and $\mathbf V=(M_1\mathbf v_1+M_2\mathbf v_2)/(M_1+M_2)$ the [centre-of-mass velocity](../../../center-of-mass-velocity.md). Inverting gives

$$
\mathbf v_1=\mathbf V+\frac{M_2}{M_1+M_2}\mathbf w,\qquad
\mathbf v_2=\mathbf V-\frac{M_1}{M_1+M_2}\mathbf w.
$$

Substitute into the sum of the two [kinetic energies](../../../kinetic-energy.md). The cross terms cancel, while the coefficient of $|\mathbf w|^2/2$ is the [reduced mass](../../../reduced-mass.md) $\mu=M_1M_2/(M_1+M_2)$. Hence

$$
T=\frac12(M_1+M_2)|\mathbf V|^2+\frac12\mu|\mathbf w|^2.
$$

The first term is translation of the [centre of mass](../../../center-of-mass.md); the second is the [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) measured in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). For an isolated [perfectly inelastic collision](../../../perfectly-inelastic-collision.md), the bodies finish with a common [velocity](../../../velocity.md), while their total [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) and thus $\mathbf V$ are unchanged. The [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) converted to internal forms is therefore

$$
\boxed{T_i-T_f=\frac12\mu v^2,}
$$

where $v$ is their initial relative [speed](../../../speed.md). Total [energy](../../../energy-split.md) remains conserved; it is the translational-plus-relative [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) that decreases.
