<h1 id="9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md) is absent from the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) itself: $\partial L/\partial q_i=0$. Its [conjugate momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) $p_i=\partial L/\partial\dot q_i$ therefore obeys $\dot p_i=0$. In the rolling-wedge calculation, the cyclic horizontal translation gives conservation of the system's horizontal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md).
