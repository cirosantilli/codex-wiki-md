↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [holonomic constraint](../../../../../holonomic-constraint.md) can be expressed as $f(q_1,\ldots,q_n,t)=0$, rather than as a genuinely nonintegrable relation among velocities. A fixed rod length is an example. Rolling along a single straight incline also gives an integrable relation $s+R\theta=\text{constant}$ with the sign convention used below; arbitrary rolling in more dimensions need not be holonomic.
