<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) $L(q_i,\dot q_i,t)$ expressed in independent [generalized coordinates](../../../../generalized-coordinate.md), stationary [action](../../../../action.md) gives the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_i}=0.}
$$

Ideal constraints can either be incorporated into the independent coordinates or treated using [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md), as in [solution](../c/solution.md).
