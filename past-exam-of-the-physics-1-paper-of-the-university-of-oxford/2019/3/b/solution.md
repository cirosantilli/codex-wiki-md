<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The parallel axis through the centre is the rod's own long axis, with zero [moment of inertia](../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) in the thin-rod approximation. By the [parallel axis theorem](../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md), the displaced parallel axis has

$$
\boxed{I_D=0+MD^2=MD^2.}
$$

Equivalently, every [mass](../../../../mass.md) element is the same perpendicular distance $D$ from that axis, so the defining integral immediately gives this result.
