<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a boost along $+x$ at [speed](../../../../speed.md) $v$, the [energy-momentum four-vector](../../../../four-momentum.md) transforms as

$$
\boxed{E'=\gamma(E-vp_x),\quad p_x'=\gamma(p_x-vE/c^2),\quad p_y'=p_y,\quad p_z'=p_z.}
$$

The total four-vector is the sum over the particles. Write $E=\sum_iE_i$ and $\mathbf P=\sum_i\mathbf p_i$. A centre-of-momentum [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md) exists when this total is timelike, with $\mathbf V_{\rm CM}=c^2\mathbf P/E$. Boost along $\mathbf P$ and use $P=EV_{\rm CM}/c^2$:

$$
E_{\rm CM}=\gamma_{\rm CM}(E-V_{\rm CM}P)=E/\gamma_{\rm CM}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\gamma_{\rm CM}=\frac{\sum_iE_i}{E_{\rm CM}}=\frac1{\sqrt{1-V_{\rm CM}^2/c^2}}.}
$$

The timelike qualification is needed: exactly parallel [photons](../../../../photon.md) alone have null total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) and no such [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md).
