<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the squared total [energy-momentum four-vector](../../../../four-momentum.md), expressed in energy-squared units. With the [proton](../../../../proton.md) initially at rest,

$$
W^2=(m_pc^2+E_\gamma)^2-E_\gamma^2=m_p^2c^4+2m_pc^2E_\gamma.
$$

At the [reaction threshold](../../../../reaction-threshold.md), the outgoing [proton](../../../../proton.md) and [pion](../../../../pion.md) have no relative [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), so $W=(m_p+m_\pi)c^2$. Solving gives

$$
\boxed{E_\gamma^{\rm th}=m_\pi c^2\left(1+\frac{m_\pi}{2m_p}\right)=144.715\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

The numerical value uses the supplied $938$ and $135\,\mathrm{MeV}$ [rest energies](../../../../rest-energy.md). The threshold exceeds the [pion](../../../../pion.md)'s [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md) because the outgoing system must carry the incident [photon](../../../../photon.md)'s nonzero laboratory [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md).
