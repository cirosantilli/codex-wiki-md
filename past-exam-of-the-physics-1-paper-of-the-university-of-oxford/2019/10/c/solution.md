<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At threshold, the [pion](../../../../pion.md) is at rest in the final [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), which moves in the laboratory with

$$
\beta=\frac{E_\gamma^{\rm th}}{m_pc^2+E_\gamma^{\rm th}}=0.1336592\ldots,\qquad
\gamma=\frac{m_pc^2+E_\gamma^{\rm th}}{(m_p+m_\pi)c^2}=1.0090539\ldots.
$$

In the [pion](../../../../pion.md)'s [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md), its [two-photon decay](../../../../two-photon-decay.md) produces opposite [photons](../../../../photon.md) of equal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $m_\pi c^2/2$. The specified forward and backward directions remain collinear under this boost, so

$$
E_\pm=\frac{m_\pi c^2}{2}\gamma(1\pm\beta).
$$

At threshold, $\gamma(1+\beta)=(m_p+m_\pi)/m_p$ and $\gamma(1-\beta)=m_p/(m_p+m_\pi)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{E_+=77.2148\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad E_-=59.0075\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

The forward [photon](../../../../photon.md) is the more energetic one. Their product remains $(m_\pi c^2/2)^2$, as required by the invariant [mass](../../../../mass.md) of an oppositely directed [photon](../../../../photon.md) pair, while their sum equals the laboratory [pion](../../../../pion.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
