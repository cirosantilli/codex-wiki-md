<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the positive coordinate from $O$ toward $S$. The [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) of the remaining material has coordinate

$$
x_X=\frac{m(0)-(m/4)(a/2)}{3m/4}=-\frac a6.
$$

Thus $SX=a+a/6=7a/6$. If $\phi$ is the small angular displacement from the stable hanging orientation, gravity gives [torque](../../../../torque.md) $-(3m/4)g(7a/6)\sin\phi$ about the suspension point. The [physical pendulum](../../../../physical-pendulum.md) equation is

$$
I_S\ddot\phi=-\frac{7mga}{8}\sin\phi.
$$

Using the [small-angle approximation](../../../../small-angle-approximation.md) $\sin\phi\simeq\phi$ gives [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\frac{(3m/4)g(7a/6)}{45ma^2/32}
=\frac{28g}{45a},\qquad \omega=\sqrt{\frac{28g}{45a}}.}
$$

Both the reduced [mass](../../../../mass.md) and displaced [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) matter; changing only the [moment of inertia](../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) would give an incorrect [frequency](../../../../frequency.md).
