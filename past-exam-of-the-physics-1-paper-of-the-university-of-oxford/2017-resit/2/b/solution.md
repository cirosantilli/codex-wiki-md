<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A radial outward kick has no [angular impulse](../../../../angular-impulse.md) about the centre. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta L=0,\qquad \Delta E=\frac12m\delta^2>0.}
$$

The initial position is on the outward-moving portion of a bound [elliptic Kepler orbit](../../../../elliptic-orbit.md), between [pericentre](../../../../periapsis.md) and [apocentre](../../../../apoapsis.md), rather than at either turning point. The [eccentricity](../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) is $e=\delta/v_c$ and the [semilatus rectum](../../../../semilatus-rectum.md) remains $r_0$. The [orbital energy](../../../../orbital-energy.md) change is second order: it vanishes to first order in the impulse but is positive for any nonzero kick.
