<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a small opposing tangential kick,

$$
\Delta E=-m v_c\delta+\frac12m\delta^2<0,\qquad \Delta L=-m r_0\delta<0.
$$

The new tangential [speed](../../../../speed.md) is less than the [circular orbital speed](../../../../circular-orbital-speed.md), so the kick point is the [apocentre](../../../../apoapsis.md) of an [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md). **The orbit falls inward from the original circle.** The signs assume $0<\delta\ll v_c$; a large velocity reversal is outside the stated small-impulse regime.
