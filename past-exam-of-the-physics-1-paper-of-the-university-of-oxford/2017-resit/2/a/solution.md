<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Adding tangential [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) along the existing motion increases both [orbital energy](../../../../orbital-energy.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md):

$$
\Delta E=m v_c\delta+\frac12m\delta^2>0,\qquad \Delta L=m r_0\delta>0.
$$

For a sufficiently small kick the [Kepler orbit](../../../../kepler-orbit.md) remains an [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md). At the kick point its radial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) vanishes and its tangential [speed](../../../../speed.md) exceeds the [circular orbital speed](../../../../circular-orbital-speed.md), so that point becomes the [pericentre](../../../../periapsis.md). **The ellipse extends farther outward than the original circle.**
