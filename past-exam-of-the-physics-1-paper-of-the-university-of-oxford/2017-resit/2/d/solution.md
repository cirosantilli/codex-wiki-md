The inward radial kick also leaves [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) unchanged and increases [orbital energy](../../../../orbital-energy.md) by $m\delta^2/2$. Its [eccentricity](../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) and [semi-major axis](../../../../semi-major-axis.md) equal those in case $b$, but the orientation of its [eccentricity vector](../../../../eccentricity-vector.md) is reflected about the initial radius. The satellite initially moves inward on its [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md), toward [pericentre](../../../../periapsis.md). **Neither radial kick decreases the orbital energy.**
