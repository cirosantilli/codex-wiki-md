<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Belinda remains in one [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) with $v=5c/13$, so the [time dilation](../../../../time-dilation.md) factor is $\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}=12/13$. Her elapsed [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) is

$$
\boxed{\tau_B=\frac{12}{13}T=\frac{204}{35}\,\mathrm{yr}\simeq5.8286\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

The reunion event is shared by both twins, making their clock readings directly comparable without any convention for synchronizing separated moving clocks.
