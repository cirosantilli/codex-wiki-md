<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Alison reaches the station at $t_1=1/(5/13)=13/5$ years. Her return [worldline](../../../../world-line.md) is

$$
x_A=1-\frac{12}{13}\left(t-\frac{13}{5}\right)=\frac{17}{5}-\frac{12t}{13},
$$

while Belinda's is $x_B=-5t/13$. Equating them gives

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{221}{35}\,\mathrm{yr}\simeq6.3143\,\mathrm{yr},\qquad
x_{\rm meeting}=-\frac{17}{7}\,\mathrm{ly}.}
$$

This is the elapsed Earth-frame time, measurable by synchronized clocks in that [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md). A signal announcing the reunion reaches a clock physically on Earth later; its light-travel delay is not part of the requested frame-time comparison.
