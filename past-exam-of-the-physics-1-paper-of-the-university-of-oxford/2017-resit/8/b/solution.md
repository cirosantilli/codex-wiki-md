<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrating the [rocket equation](../../../../rocket-equation.md) with $v(0)=v_0$ gives

$$
\boxed{v(t)=v_0+gt-u\log\frac{m_0}{m_0-\alpha t}.}
$$

This formula holds during the constant-flow burn, while $m_0-\alpha t>0$ and fuel remains. The negative logarithmic term represents the slowing due to upward [rocket thrust](../../../../rocket-thrust.md).
