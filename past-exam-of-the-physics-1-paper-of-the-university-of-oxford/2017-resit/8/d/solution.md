<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The specified [rocket fuel fraction](../../../../rocket-fuel-fraction.md) gives $m(T)=2m_0/3$, so $\alpha T=m_0/3$. Imposing $v(T)=0$ yields

$$
T=\frac{u\log(3/2)-v_0}{g}.
$$

Then $h(T)=0$ fixes

$$
h_0=(v_0-u)T+\frac12gT^2+2uT\log(3/2).
$$

Numerically,

$$
\boxed{T\simeq60.07\,\mathrm s,\qquad h_0\simeq1.159\times10^3\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The associated constant [mass loss rate](../../../../mass-loss-rate.md) is $\alpha=m_0/(3T)\simeq88.78\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$. The initial upward [thrust acceleration](../../../../thrust-acceleration.md) already exceeds $g$ and increases as [mass](../../../../mass.md) falls, so the downward [speed](../../../../speed.md) decreases monotonically to zero. This calculation assumes constant lunar [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), constant relative [rocket exhaust speed](../../../../rocket-exhaust-speed.md), and negligible [drag](../../../../drag-physics.md).
