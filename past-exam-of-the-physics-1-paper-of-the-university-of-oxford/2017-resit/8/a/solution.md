<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose downward [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $v$ positive. The remaining rocket [mass](../../../../mass.md) is $m(t)=m_0-\alpha t$, and the upward [rocket thrust](../../../../rocket-thrust.md) has magnitude $\alpha u$, where $u>0$ is the relative [rocket exhaust speed](../../../../rocket-exhaust-speed.md). The [rocket equation](../../../../rocket-equation.md) including gravity is

$$
m\dot v=mg-\alpha u,\qquad
\boxed{\dot v=g-\frac{\alpha u}{m_0-\alpha t}.}
$$

This follows by applying [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) to the rocket and its just-expelled [rocket propellant](../../../../rocket-propellant.md), with gravity supplying the external [impulse](../../../../impulse.md). Applying $d(mv)/dt=mg$ to the rocket alone would omit the exhaust [momentum flux](../../../../momentum-flux.md).
