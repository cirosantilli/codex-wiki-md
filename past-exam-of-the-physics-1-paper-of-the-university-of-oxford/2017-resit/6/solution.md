<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Write $\omega$ for the imposed constant [angular velocity](../../../angular-velocity.md). In inertial polar coordinates, the bead has $v^2=\dot r^2+\omega^2r^2$. Its constrained [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
L=\frac m2(\dot r^2+\omega^2r^2),\qquad
\ddot r=\omega^2r.
$$

The reduced [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit time dependence, so its [Lagrangian energy function](../../../lagrangian-energy-function.md) is conserved:

$$
\boxed{J=\dot r\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot r}-L
=\frac m2(\dot r^2-\omega^2r^2).}
$$

This is the [Jacobi integral](../../../jacobi-integral.md) $J=E-\omega L_z$, since the bead's actual inertial [energy](../../../energy-split.md) and axial [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) are

$$
E=\frac m2(\dot r^2+\omega^2r^2),\qquad L_z=m\omega r^2.
$$

The motor maintaining the stick's [angular velocity](../../../angular-velocity.md) supplies [torque](../../../torque.md) and does [work](../../../work.md). Indeed $\dot E=2m\omega^2r\dot r=\omega\dot L_z$, generally nonzero. A time-independent reduced [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) therefore need not imply conservation of the bead's inertial [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) when its imposed constraint is moving.
