<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For equal [masses](../../../../mass.md) $m$, the [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) is $m/2$. The [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) of their [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) cannot be removed by an isolated [collision](../../../../collision.md), since total [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is fixed. Therefore the maximum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) available for conversion to internal forms is the initial relative [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md),

$$
\boxed{K_{\rm available}=\frac m4|\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_2|^2.}
$$

It is attained by a [perfectly inelastic collision](../../../../perfectly-inelastic-collision.md) in which the final relative [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is zero. This concerns the convertible initial [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), not an arbitrary pre-existing internal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) reservoir.
