<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

In the [pion](../../../pion.md) [rest frame](../../../rest-frame.md), [momentum conservation](../../../momentum-conservation.md) makes the [muon](../../../muon.md) and [neutrino](../../../neutrino.md) momenta opposite, with common magnitude $p$. For a massless [neutrino](../../../neutrino.md), $E_\nu=pc$; [energy conservation](../../../conservation-of-energy.md) and the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) give

$$
E_\mu+pc=m_\pi c^2,\qquad E_\mu^2-p^2c^2=m_\mu^2c^4.
$$

Substitute $pc=m_\pi c^2-E_\mu$ into the second equation. The $E_\mu^2$ terms cancel, leaving

$$
\boxed{E_\mu=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_\mu^2}{2m_\pi}c^2.}
$$

For completeness, the complementary [relativistic two-body decay](../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md) energy is $E_\nu=(m_\pi^2-m_\mu^2)c^2/(2m_\pi)$. The result is a total [energy](../../../energy-split.md), including the [muon](../../../muon.md) [rest energy](../../../rest-energy.md), rather than its [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md).
