<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Write the [electron](../../../electron.md) [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) as $-e$, with $e>0$ the [elementary charge](../../../elementary-charge.md), and choose $\mathbf B=B\hat{\mathbf z}$ and initial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $u\hat{\mathbf x}$. In the nonrelativistic approximation, [Newton's second law](../../../newton-s-second-law.md) with the magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md) gives

$$
m_e\dot v_x=-eBv_y,\qquad m_e\dot v_y=eBv_x,\qquad \dot v_z=0.
$$

The magnetic [force](../../../force-split.md) is perpendicular to the [velocity](../../../velocity.md), so it does no [work](../../../work.md): $d(m_e|\mathbf v|^2/2)/dt=0$. Setting $\omega_c=eB/m_e$, the solution with $x(0)=y(0)=0$ is

$$
v_x=u\cos\omega_ct,\qquad v_y=u\sin\omega_ct,
\qquad x=\frac u{\omega_c}\sin\omega_ct,\qquad
y=\frac u{\omega_c}(1-\cos\omega_ct).
$$

Consequently $x^2+(y-r)^2=r^2$, where $r=u/\omega_c$: this is a [cyclotron orbit](../../../gyromotion.md). Its [frequency](../../../frequency.md) and [cyclotron radius](../../../cyclotron-radius.md) are

$$
\boxed{f=\frac{eB}{2\pi m_e},\qquad r=\frac{m_eu}{eB},\qquad
u=\sqrt{\frac{2eV}{m_e}}.}
$$

The accelerating [voltage](../../../voltage.md) changes $u$ and $r$ but cancels out of $f$. Using $m_e=9.1094\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $e=1.6022\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$ gives

$$
B=\frac{2\pi m_e(2.5\times10^9\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})}e
\simeq0.0893\,\mathrm T,\qquad
u\simeq1.876\times10^7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},
\qquad\boxed{r\simeq1.19\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Only the magnitude of the [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is determined; its direction is perpendicular to the orbital plane, with the sense of rotation fixed by the negative [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md). The claimed voltage independence is a nonrelativistic result: exactly, $\gamma=1+eV/(m_ec^2)$ and $f=eB/(2\pi\gamma m_e)$. At $1\,\mathrm{kV}$ the correction is about $0.2\%$. The stated model also neglects the [energy](../../../energy-split.md) lost to emitted [electromagnetic radiation](../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md).
