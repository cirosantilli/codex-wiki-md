<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A $90^\circ$ [gravitational scattering angle](../../../../gravitational-scattering-angle.md) has $\cot(\phi/2)=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{b=\frac{GM_J}{v_0^2}
=\frac{(6.6743\times10^{-11})(2\times10^{27})}{(10^4)^2}\,\mathrm m
\simeq1.33\times10^9\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The denominator uses the squared [speed](../../../../speed.md) in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, not its numerical value in $\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. From [b](../b.md), the corresponding [periapsis distance](../../../../pericentre-distance.md) is $r_{\min}=(\sqrt2-1)b\simeq5.53\times10^8\,\mathrm m$; this differs substantially from the [impact parameter](../../../../impact-parameter.md) because of gravitational focusing.
