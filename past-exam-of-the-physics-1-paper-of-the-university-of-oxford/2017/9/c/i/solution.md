<h1 id="9/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the incident [photon energies](../../../../../photon-energy.md) be $E$ and $\varepsilon$, meeting at angle $\psi$ in the [laboratory frame](../../../../../laboratory-frame.md). Add their [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md); because each is null, the squared total invariant [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\mathcal S=(E+\varepsilon)^2-c^2|\mathbf p_1+\mathbf p_2|^2
=2E\varepsilon(1-\cos\psi).
$$

For [photon-photon pair production](../../../../../photon-photon-pair-production.md), its threshold value is $(2m_ec^2)^2$: the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) have zero relative [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). The minimum possible $E$ occurs for a head-on collision, $\psi=\pi$, so

$$
4E\varepsilon=4m_e^2c^4,
\qquad\boxed{E_{\min}=\frac{(m_ec^2)^2}{\varepsilon}.}
$$

With $m_ec^2\simeq0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $\varepsilon=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}$,

$$
\boxed{E_{\min}\simeq2.61\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{eV}
=261\,\mathrm{TeV}\simeq41.8\,\mathrm J.}
$$

The quoted background [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md) is used directly, without substituting a different characteristic value of the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md). A less opposed collision has a higher threshold; parallel [photons](../../../../../photon.md) cannot produce this massive pair.
