<h1 id="9/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the threshold found in [i](../i.md), both products are stationary in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), so they have the same [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in the [laboratory frame](../../../../../laboratory-frame.md). Take the energetic incident [photon](../../../../../photon.md) along $+x$. The total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $E+\varepsilon$ and total [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is $(E-\varepsilon)/c$, so the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) moves at

$$
\boxed{\mathbf V=c\frac{E-\varepsilon}{E+\varepsilon}\hat{\mathbf x}.}
$$

For the numerical threshold,

$$
\frac Vc=1-\frac{2\varepsilon}{E+\varepsilon}
\simeq1-7.66\times10^{-18},\qquad
\gamma_{\rm CM}=\frac{E+\varepsilon}{2m_ec^2}\simeq2.56\times10^8.
$$

This is extremely close to, but strictly below, the [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md). Each product has [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $(E+\varepsilon)/2$ in the [laboratory frame](../../../../../laboratory-frame.md). Above threshold the same ratio of total [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) to total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) determines the motion of the pair's [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), but the individual [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) need not have a common [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md).
