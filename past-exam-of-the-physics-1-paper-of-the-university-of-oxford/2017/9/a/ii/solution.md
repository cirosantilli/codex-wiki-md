<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose particle 1's incoming [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) positive and particle 2's negative. The final particle is stationary, so [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) requires zero total incoming [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md):

$$
m\gamma_1v_1-2m\gamma_2\frac c3=0,\qquad
\gamma_2=\frac3{2\sqrt2}.
$$

It follows that $\gamma_1\beta_1=1/\sqrt2$. Squaring gives $\beta_1^2/(1-\beta_1^2)=1/2$, hence

$$
\boxed{v_1=\frac c{\sqrt3},\qquad \gamma_1=\sqrt{\frac32}.}
$$

The initial total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) becomes the final [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md). Therefore

$$
Mc^2=mc^2\gamma_1+2mc^2\gamma_2,
\qquad\boxed{M=\frac{3+\sqrt3}{\sqrt2}\,m\simeq3.346m.}
$$

The extra [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is the incoming [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) retained as internal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of the merged particle: $(M-3m)c^2$. It does not violate [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) or [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md); the sum of the separate [rest masses](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is not a conserved quantity in an [inelastic collision](../../../../../inelastic-collision.md).
