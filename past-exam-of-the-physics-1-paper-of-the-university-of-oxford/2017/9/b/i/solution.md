<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $S'$ move at $+v$ along $x$ relative to $S$. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), from primed to unprimed components, is

$$
E=\gamma(E'+vp_x'),\qquad
p_x=\gamma\left(p_x'+\frac{vE'}{c^2}\right),\qquad
p_y=p_y',\qquad p_z=p_z'.
$$

The inverse equations have $v$ replaced by $-v$ and the primes interchanged. A [photon](../../../../../photon.md) making angle $\theta'$ with $+x$ in $S'$ has $p_x'=(E'/c)\cos\theta'$. Thus its general [Doppler factor](../../../../../doppler-factor.md) is

$$
D=\frac E{E'}=\gamma(1+\beta\cos\theta').
$$

The requested blueshifted expression assumes propagation parallel to the boost, $\theta'=0$:

$$
\boxed{D=\gamma(1+\beta)=\sqrt{\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}}.}
$$

The propagation-direction assumption matters: for an oppositely travelling [photon](../../../../../photon.md), $D=\gamma(1-\beta)$, the reciprocal of the displayed factor. Relative frame [speed](../../../../../speed.md) alone does not select the longitudinal [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md).
