<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Choose zero [potential energy](../../../potential-energy.md) at the equilibrium position. For the [simple harmonic oscillator](../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), the [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [elastic potential energy](../../../elastic-energy.md) give the [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md)

$$
L=T-V=\frac12m\dot x^2-\frac12kx^2.
$$

Its [canonical momentum](../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $p=\partial L/\partial\dot x=m\dot x$. The [Legendre transform](../../../convex-conjugate.md) defining the [Hamiltonian](../../../hamiltonian.md) consequently gives

$$
\boxed{H(x,p)=p\dot x-L=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\frac12kx^2=T+V.}
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $m\ddot x+kx=0$. Thus along the motion

$$
\frac{dH}{dt}=m\dot x\ddot x+kx\dot x
=\dot x(m\ddot x+kx)=0.
$$

This proves [conservation of energy](../../../conservation-of-energy.md). Equivalently, the [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit time dependence, so $dH/dt=-\partial L/\partial t=0$. The [Lagrangian](../../../lagrangian.md) itself need not be constant: the [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../potential-energy.md) exchange periodically.
