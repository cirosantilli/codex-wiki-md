↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [special relativity](../../../../special-relativity-split.md) expression for [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is $p=\gamma mv$, with [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Equal [speeds](../../../../speed.md) imply equal [Lorentz factors](../../../../lorentz-factor.md), so the [pion](../../../../pion.md) and [proton](../../../../proton.md) [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) are in the ratio of their [rest masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md):

$$
\boxed{p_\pi=p_p\frac{m_\pi}{m_p}=400\frac{0.14}{0.94}
\simeq59.6\,\mathrm{GeV}/c.}
$$

The common dimensionless product is $\gamma v/c=p_p/(m_pc)=400/0.94\simeq425.53$, giving $\gamma\simeq425.53$ and $v/c\simeq0.99999724$.
