<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the object be at rest in $S'$, with [proper length](../../../../proper-length.md) $L_0=x'_2-x'_1$. Measuring its length in $S$ requires two endpoint [events](../../../../event.md) simultaneous in $S$, so $\Delta t=0$. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) then gives

$$
L_0=\Delta x'=\gamma(\Delta x-v\Delta t)=\gamma L,
\qquad
\boxed{L=\frac{L_0}{\gamma}=L_0\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}.}
$$

This is [length contraction](../../../../length-contraction.md). The simultaneous endpoint [events](../../../../event.md) in $S$ are not simultaneous in $S'$; that [relativity of simultaneity](../../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md) is essential to the measurement.

For the two rockets, take the first rocket's Earth-frame [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) as $+c/2$ and the second's as $-c/2$. The [velocity-addition formula](../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) gives the second rocket's [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) in the first rocket's [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md):

$$
u=\frac{-c/2-c/2}{1-(-c/2)(c/2)/c^2}=-\frac45c.
$$

Its [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma_{\mathrm{rel}}=5/3$, and therefore its measured length is

$$
\boxed{L_{\mathrm{other}}=\frac35L_0.}
$$

Here “appear” is interpreted as a length measured using simultaneous endpoint positions in the observer's [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md). A photograph, involving unequal light travel times, is a different measurement.
