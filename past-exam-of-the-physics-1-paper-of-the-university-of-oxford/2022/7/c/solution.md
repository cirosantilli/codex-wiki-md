<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the incident and scattered [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) as $\varepsilon=hc/\lambda$ and $\varepsilon'=hc/\lambda'$. [Conservation of relativistic energy](../../../../conservation-of-relativistic-energy.md) gives the recoiling [electron](../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_e=mc^2+\varepsilon-\varepsilon'$. By [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md), its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is the difference of the two [photon](../../../../photon.md) [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md); hence

$$
p_e^2c^2=\varepsilon^2+\varepsilon'^2-2\varepsilon\varepsilon'\cos\theta.
$$

Insert these into the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) for the [electron](../../../../electron.md):

$$
(mc^2+\varepsilon-\varepsilon')^2
=m^2c^4+\varepsilon^2+\varepsilon'^2-2\varepsilon\varepsilon'\cos\theta.
$$

Expanding and cancelling terms gives

$$
mc^2(\varepsilon-\varepsilon')=\varepsilon\varepsilon'(1-\cos\theta),\qquad
\frac1{\varepsilon'}-\frac1\varepsilon=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{mc^2}.
$$

Since $1/\varepsilon=\lambda/(hc)$, the [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) formula follows:

$$
\boxed{\lambda'=\lambda+\frac{h}{mc}(1-\cos\theta).}
$$

The scale $h/(mc)$ is the [electron](../../../../electron.md) [Compton wavelength](../../../../compton-wavelength.md). At $\theta=0$, $\lambda'=\lambda$: there is no [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) or [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer in this ideal forward limit, so the [electron](../../../../electron.md) remains at rest. At $\theta=\pi$, the [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) shift is maximal:

$$
\boxed{\Delta\lambda_{\max}=\frac{2h}{mc},\qquad
\varepsilon'_{\min}=\frac{\varepsilon}{1+2\varepsilon/(mc^2)}.}
$$

The [electron](../../../../electron.md) gains its largest [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), $\varepsilon-\varepsilon'_{\min}$, and recoils forward while the [photon](../../../../photon.md) reverses direction.
