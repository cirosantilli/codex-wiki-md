<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Align the spatial axes and make the origins coincide at $t=t'=0$. For an [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) moving at [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $+v$ along $x$, the [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is

$$
\boxed{x'=\gamma(x-vt),\quad t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\quad y'=y,\quad z'=z,
\qquad\gamma=\frac1{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}.}
$$

The inverse [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) follows by interchanging primed and unprimed coordinates and replacing $v$ by $-v$.
