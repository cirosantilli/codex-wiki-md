<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use upward [generalized coordinates](../../../../generalized-coordinate.md). Let $h$ be the displacement of the lower [pulley](../../../../pulley.md) and $z$ that of the $4m$ [mass](../../../../mass.md). The two inextensible [mechanical strings](../../../../mechanical-string.md) impose

$$
x+y-2h=0,\qquad z+h=0,
\qquad h=\frac{x+y}{2},\qquad z=-\frac{x+y}{2}.
$$

For ideal massless [pulleys](../../../../pulley.md) and [mechanical strings](../../../../mechanical-string.md), only the three [masses](../../../../mass.md) contribute [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
K&=\frac12(4m)\left(\frac{\dot x+\dot y}{2}\right)^2
+\frac12(5m)\dot x^2+\frac12(3m)\dot y^2\\
&=m(3\dot x^2+\dot x\dot y+2\dot y^2).
\end{aligned}
$$

Up to a constant, their [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) is

$$
U=4mgz+5mgx+3mgy=mg(3x+y).
$$

The [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) of this [compound Atwood machine](../../../../compound-atwood-machine.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=K-U=m(3\dot x^2+\dot x\dot y+2\dot y^2)-mg(3x+y).}
$$
