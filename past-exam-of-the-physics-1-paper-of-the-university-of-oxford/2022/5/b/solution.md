<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A constant displacement proportional to $(1,-3)$ leaves the [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) unchanged and changes $3x+y$ by zero. It is therefore a continuous symmetry of the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md). The [generalized momentum](../../../../generalized-momentum.md) components are

$$
p_x=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot x}=m(6\dot x+\dot y),\qquad
p_y=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot y}=m(\dot x+4\dot y).
$$

[Noether's theorem](../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives the conserved quantity

$$
\boxed{P=p_x-3p_y=m(3\dot x-11\dot y)=\text{constant}.}
$$

A direct check using the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
6\ddot x+\ddot y=-3g,\qquad \ddot x+4\ddot y=-g
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3\ddot x-11\ddot y=0.
$$

The normalization of a symmetry generator is arbitrary. One can take the transformation parameter to have [length](../../../../length.md) units and $(K_x,K_y)=(1,-3)$ dimensionless, giving $P$ the units of [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). Alternatively, for the paper's convention with dimensionless parameter and [length](../../../../length.md)-valued $K_i$, use $(K_x,K_y)=(\ell,-3\ell)$; the conserved quantity is then $\ell P$. The conserved content is identical.
