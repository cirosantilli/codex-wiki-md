<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $a$ be the [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) downhill and $F$ the uphill [static friction](../../../../static-friction.md). Translation and rotation about the [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) obey

$$
ma=mg\sin\theta-F,\qquad I\alpha=Fr.
$$

The [rolling without slipping](../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) constraint is $a=r\alpha$. For the coin's [moment of inertia](../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) $I=mr^2/2$, this gives $F=ma/2$, so

$$
\boxed{a=\frac23g\sin\theta,\qquad F=\frac13mg\sin\theta.}
$$

The normal [force](../../../../force-split.md) is $N=mg\cos\theta$. [Static friction](../../../../static-friction.md) can enforce [rolling without slipping](../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) only if $F\le\mu N$, giving

$$
\frac13mg\sin\theta\le\mu mg\cos\theta,
\qquad
\boxed{\theta_{\max}=\arctan(3\mu).}
$$

The [static friction](../../../../static-friction.md) acts uphill to provide the [torque](../../../../torque.md); it is not automatically equal to $\mu N$, attaining that limiting value only at the threshold. For finite $\mu\ge0$ the result lies between $0$ and $\pi/2$.
