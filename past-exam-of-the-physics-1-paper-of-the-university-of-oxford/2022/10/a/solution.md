<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an [equal-speed elastic impact on a free rod](../../../../equal-speed-elastic-impact-on-a-free-rod.md), let $h$ be the signed distance of the impact point from the rod's [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md), $I=ml^2/12$, and $\mathcal J$ the horizontal [impulse](../../../../impulse.md) delivered to the rod. For a smooth impact the [impulse](../../../../impulse.md) is perpendicular to the rod. If the ball's signed final [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is $u$, the rod's [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is $V$ and its [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md) is $\omega$, [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) balance and angular [impulse](../../../../impulse.md) give

$$
m(v_0-u)=\mathcal J=mV,\qquad I\omega=\mathcal J h.
$$

Both objects have positive final translational [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) for a nonzero impact away from the centre. More explicitly, restitution in an [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md) gives $V+\omega h-u=v_0$, and hence

$$
V=\frac{2v_0}{2+mh^2/I},\qquad
u=\frac{(mh^2/I)v_0}{2+mh^2/I}\ge0.
$$

Equal final [speeds](../../../../speed.md) thus require $u=V=v_0/2$. One can also see that the alternative $u=-V$ would violate $u+V=v_0$.

By conservation of [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in the [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md),

$$
\frac12mv_0^2=\frac12m\left(\frac{v_0}{2}\right)^2
+\frac12m\left(\frac{v_0}{2}\right)^2+\frac12I\omega^2.
$$

Thus $I\omega^2=mv_0^2/2$. Since $I\omega=mVh=mv_0h/2$, elimination of $\omega$ gives

$$
\boxed{h^2=\frac{2I}{m}=\frac{l^2}{6},\qquad |h|=\frac l{\sqrt6}.}
$$

Either side of the [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) works; the nearer end is a distance $l(1/2-1/\sqrt6)$ away. The final [angular speed](../../../../angular-speed.md) is $|\omega|=\sqrt6\,v_0/l$. These values also satisfy the restitution condition because the struck point's [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is $V+\omega h=3v_0/2$, reversing the initial relative contact [velocity](../../../../velocity.md).
