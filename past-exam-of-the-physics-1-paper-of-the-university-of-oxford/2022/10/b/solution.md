<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The free rod has the same [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$ and length $l$ as in part (a). There is no pivot. Put the rod's initial [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) at $y=0$ and the impact end at $y=l/2$. At impact the combined [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) is

$$
y_C=\frac{m(0)+m(l/2)}{2m}=\frac l4.
$$

[Conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives the combined [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $V_C=v_0/2$. Evaluate [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) about this combined [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md); the incident ball's perpendicular lever arm is $l/4$, so its initial magnitude is $mv_0l/4$.

After the [perfectly inelastic collision](../../../../perfectly-inelastic-collision.md), the [parallel axis theorem](../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md) gives the combined [moment of inertia](../../../../moment-of-inertia.md)

$$
I_C=\left(\frac{ml^2}{12}+m\frac{l^2}{16}\right)+m\frac{l^2}{16}
=\frac{5ml^2}{24}.
$$

With negligible external angular [impulse](../../../../impulse.md), [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{|\omega|=\frac{mv_0l/4}{5ml^2/24}=\frac{6v_0}{5l}.}
$$

Its sense is that of the incident ball's [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), clockwise in the source diagram. As a consistency check, the final [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
K_f=\frac12(2m)\left(\frac{v_0}{2}\right)^2
+\frac12\frac{5ml^2}{24}\left(\frac{6v_0}{5l}\right)^2
=\frac25mv_0^2.
$$

Thus $mv_0^2/10$, or one fifth of the initial [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), is dissipated in sticking. Treating the rod's original centre as a fixed pivot would give an incorrect [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md).
