<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define $f(r)>0$ as the magnitude of the inward [central force](../../../../central-force.md), so $\mathbf F=-f(r)\widehat{\mathbf r}$ and $V'(r)=f(r)$. The conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $J=mr^2\dot\theta$. Eliminating $\dot\theta$ from the [mechanical energy](../../../../mechanical-energy.md) yields

$$
\boxed{V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)=V(r)+\frac{J^2}{2mr^2},\qquad
E=\frac12m\dot r^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r).}
$$

The added term in the [effective potential](../../../../effective-potential.md) is the [centrifugal barrier](../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md). If instead $f$ denotes the signed radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) component, the convention becomes $V'=-f$.
