<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Orient the $x$ axis along the displacement from the earlier event to the later one, so $\Delta x=d=1000\,\mathrm m$ and $\Delta t=10^{-6}\,\mathrm s$. For a parallel [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md), [simultaneity](../../../simultaneity.md) in the second [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) requires

$$
\Delta t'=\gamma\left(\Delta t-\frac{v\Delta x}{c^2}\right)=0,
\qquad
\boxed{v=\frac{c^2\Delta t}{d}=8.99\times10^7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\simeq0.300c.}
$$

The second [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) moves towards the later event's location. The positive value $d^2-c^2\Delta t^2$ establishes [spacelike separation](../../../spacelike-separation.md), which permits this change of [simultaneity](../../../simultaneity.md). For a general [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf V$, the condition is $\mathbf V\cdot\Delta\mathbf r=c^2\Delta t$: only its component along the separation is fixed at the displayed value, and transverse components are allowed provided $|\mathbf V|<c$. Thus the parallel [velocity](../../../velocity.md) has the smallest possible [speed](../../../speed.md).

A physical [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) must have $|v|<c$, giving

$$
\boxed{|\Delta t|<\frac dc=3.336\,\mu\mathrm s.}
$$

**There is no attained maximum:** $d/c$ is the [supremum](../../../supremum.md). For the concrete boundary case $\Delta t=1000\,\mathrm m/c$, the events have [null separation](../../../null-separation.md) and the formula would require $v=c$; no [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) can make them simultaneous.
