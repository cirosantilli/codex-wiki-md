<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Neglecting the planets' [masses](../../../mass.md) compared with the [Sun](../../../sun.md)'s [mass](../../../mass.md), [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) supplies the [centripetal acceleration](../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) of each [circular orbit](../../../circular-orbit.md):

$$
\frac{GM_\odot}{r^2}=\frac{4\pi^2r}{T^2}.
$$

Thus [Kepler's third law](../../../kepler-s-third-law.md), calibrated by the [Earth](../../../earth.md)'s [orbital period](../../../orbital-period.md) of one year and [orbital radius](../../../orbital-radius.md) of one [astronomical unit](../../../astronomical-unit.md), gives

$$
\frac{T}{1\,\mathrm{yr}}=\left(\frac{r}{1\,\mathrm{AU}}\right)^{3/2},\qquad
\boxed{T_J=5.2^{3/2}=11.86\,\mathrm{yr},\quad T_S=9.5^{3/2}=29.28\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

Both [orbits](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) are taken to have the same direction. Their relative [angular speed](../../../angular-speed.md) is $2\pi(1/T_J-1/T_S)$, so the [Sun](../../../sun.md)-centred interval between successive [conjunctions](../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) is the [synodic period](../../../synodic-period.md)

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm syn}=\frac{1}{1/T_J-1/T_S}=19.93\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

Using the supplied rounded [orbital radii](../../../orbital-radius.md) explains the small difference from the commonly quoted approximately twenty-year interval.

From the [Earth](../../../earth.md), the two lines of sight are displaced differently because [Jupiter](../../../jupiter.md) and [Saturn](../../../saturn.md) are at different [distances](../../../distance.md). The [Earth](../../../earth.md)'s annual [motion](../../../motion.md) along its [orbit](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) therefore shifts the dates of apparent [conjunctions](../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) by amounts of order months. The broad recurrence remains about twenty years, but successive apparent [conjunctions](../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) need not be equally spaced: [apparent retrograde motion](../../../apparent-retrograde-motion.md) can produce a group of three [conjunctions](../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) separated by months during one encounter. The [Earth](../../../earth.md)'s daily rotation adds a much smaller change of viewpoint. This is a geometrical comparison in the stipulated coplanar model; daylight and the horizon can prevent a particular [conjunction](../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) from being visible at a chosen surface location.
