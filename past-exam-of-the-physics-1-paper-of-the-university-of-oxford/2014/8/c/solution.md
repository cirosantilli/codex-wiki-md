<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Neglecting exhaust flight time, no [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is stored in transit and

$$
(m_A+M-q)v_A+(m_B+q)v_B=0.
$$

Since the velocities have opposite signs, equal nonzero [speeds](../../../../speed.md) imply equality of the instantaneous [masses](../../../../mass.md). For $m_B=2m_A$ and $M=5m_A$, this occurs at $6m_A-q=2m_A+q$, or $q=2m_A$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{v_s=u_0\ln\frac{6m_A}{4m_A}=u_0\ln\frac32.}
$$

The common-speed event occurs after $40\%$ of A's original fuel has been transferred, rather than when either rocket has its final [mass](../../../../mass.md). At this event the rearward exhaust still overtakes B: $v_A-u_0=-0.5945u_0<v_B=-0.4055u_0$. Thus the interception restriction in [b](../b.md) does not invalidate this event even though extending complete ballistic capture over the much later burn would need separate checking.
