<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose positive x along the rocket's motion. If its instantaneous total [mass](../../../../mass.md) is $\mu$ and it ejects a positive amount $d\eta=-d\mu$, the exhaust [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) in the original [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) is $v-u_0$ to first order. [Conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) over that increment gives

$$
\mu v=(\mu-d\eta)(v+dv)+d\eta(v-u_0),\qquad \mu\,dv=u_0d\eta=-u_0d\mu.
$$

Integrating the [rocket equation](../../../../rocket-equation.md) from $\mu=m+M$ to $\mu=m$ gives

$$
\boxed{v_f=u_0\ln\frac{m+M}{m}.}
$$

The constant exhaust [speed](../../../../speed.md) is relative to the rocket, not the initial frame. [Kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) of rocket plus exhaust is not conserved because stored fuel [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is released. The calculation is Newtonian and requires resulting [speeds](../../../../speed.md) small compared with the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md).
