<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Choose the smaller disc's initial sense as positive. For either uniform [disk](../../../disk-mathematics.md), the axial [moment of inertia](../../../moment-of-inertia.md) is $I=\tfrac12mr^2$, so $I_1=I_2/2$. The signed initial [angular velocities](../../../angular-velocity.md) are $\Omega_1=2\omega_2$ and $\Omega_2=-\omega_2$, giving total axial [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) zero. The frictionless axle supplies no axial [torque](../../../torque.md), and the pins' negligible [mass](../../../mass.md) supplies no extra stored [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md). Thus

$$
I_1\Omega'_1+I_2\Omega'_2=0,\qquad
\frac12I_1(\Omega'_1)^2+\frac12I_2(\Omega'_2)^2=\frac12I_1(2\omega_2)^2+\frac12I_2\omega_2^2.
$$

These equations allow the original state or its simultaneous reversal. A nontrivial elastic pin impact selects reversal of the relative contact [velocity](../../../velocity.md), rather than continued interpenetration. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Omega'_1=-2\omega_2=-\omega_1,\qquad \Omega'_2=+\omega_2.}
$$

The requested magnitudes are unchanged: $|\Omega'_1|=\omega_1$ and $|\Omega'_2|=\omega_2$. The intersecting pin paths make the impact possible; no additional lever arm is needed once axial [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) and [energy](../../../energy-split.md) conservation have been imposed.
