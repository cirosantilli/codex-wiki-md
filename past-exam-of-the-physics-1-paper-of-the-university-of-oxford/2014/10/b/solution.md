<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For fixed-target [electron](../../../../electron.md)-[Positron](../../../../positron.md) formation, set $m_1=m_2=m_e$ in [a](../a.md) and solve for the incident total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
\boxed{E_{e^+}=\frac{m_\rho^2c^4-2m_e^2c^4}{2m_ec^2}=\frac{(768\,\mathrm{MeV})^2}{2(0.511\,\mathrm{MeV})}-0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}\simeq5.7713\times10^5\,\mathrm{MeV}=577.1\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

The incident [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is smaller by another $m_ec^2$, a negligible relative change here. Most of the fixed-target [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) becomes motion of the final [rho meson](../../../../rho-meson.md) in the laboratory.

With equal and opposite incoming [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) is the laboratory. Each incoming particle has the same total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), and there is no final translational [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) at formation:

$$
\boxed{E_{e^+}=E_{e^-}=\frac{m_\rho c^2}{2}=384\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

This large contrast illustrates the invariant-energy advantage of colliding beams. The given $768\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$ resonance [mass](../../../../mass.md) is used rather than replacing it with a modern tabulated value.
