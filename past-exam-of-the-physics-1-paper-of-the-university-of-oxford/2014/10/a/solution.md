<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The incident particle obeys the [energy–momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) $E^2=p^2c^2+m_1^2c^4$. The stationary target contributes [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $m_2c^2$ and zero [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). A single product inherits the total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), so its [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) satisfies

$$
M^2c^4=(E+m_2c^2)^2-p^2c^2=m_1^2c^4+m_2^2c^4+2Em_2c^2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{M=\sqrt{m_1^2+m_2^2+\frac{2m_2E}{c^2}}.}
$$

Here $E$ is the incident total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), including rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In a one-particle final state, exact production of a specified [mass](../../../../mass.md) fixes the incident [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); there is no adjustable final relative motion to absorb excess [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). A resonance with a finite [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) relaxes that ideal exact equality over its line shape.
