<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For equal [masses](../../../../mass.md), let the incoming [laboratory frame](../../../../laboratory-frame.md) [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) be $\mathbf v$ and the outgoing [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) be $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$. [Conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) and [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) give

$$
\mathbf v=\mathbf a+\mathbf b,\qquad v^2=a^2+b^2.
$$

Squaring the first equation and subtracting the second yields $2\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=0,\qquad \text{outgoing directions differ by }90^\circ.}
$$

The stated oblique [collision](../../../../collision.md) ensures both outgoing [speeds](../../../../speed.md) are nonzero. In the degenerate head-on case one particle can stop, and a direction cannot be assigned to its zero [velocity](../../../../velocity.md).
