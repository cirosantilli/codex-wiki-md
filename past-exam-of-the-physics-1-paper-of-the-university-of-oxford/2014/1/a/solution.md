<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use nonrelativistic [mechanics](../../../../mechanics.md) and let $\mathbf V=(m_1\mathbf v_1+m_2\mathbf v_2)/(m_1+m_2)$ be the constant [centre-of-mass velocity](../../../../center-of-mass-velocity.md). In the [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), [conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $m_1\mathbf u_1+m_2\mathbf u_2=0$ before and after the [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md). Thus $\mathbf u_2=-(m_1/m_2)\mathbf u_1$, and the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
T_*=\frac12m_1u_1^2+\frac12m_2u_2^2=\frac12m_1\left(1+\frac{m_1}{m_2}\right)u_1^2.
$$

The decomposition $T_{\rm lab}=T_*+\tfrac12(m_1+m_2)V^2$ and constant [centre-of-mass velocity](../../../../center-of-mass-velocity.md) show that the [elastic collision](../../../../elastic-collision.md) preserves $T_*$, so the positive constant multiplying $u_1^2$ forces that [speed](../../../../speed.md) to remain unchanged. The same [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) relation fixes the second [speed](../../../../speed.md):

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf u'_1|=|\mathbf u_1|,\qquad |\mathbf u'_2|=|\mathbf u_2|.}
$$

Only their directions can change; the two [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) remain antiparallel in this frame. This derivation does not require a head-on [collision](../../../../collision.md).
