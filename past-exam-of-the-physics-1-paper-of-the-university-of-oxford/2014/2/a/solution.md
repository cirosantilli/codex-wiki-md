<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $x$ be the signed distance from the [Earth](../../../../earth.md)'s centre along the polar tunnel, with $x(0)=r_0$ and $\dot x(0)=0$. The [shell theorem](../../../../spherical-shell-theorem.md) leaves only the enclosed [mass](../../../../mass.md) $4\pi\rho|x|^3/3$ contributing to the [gravitational field](../../../../gravitational-field.md). Consequently

$$
\ddot x=-\frac{4\pi G\rho}{3}x=-\frac{g}{r_0}x,\qquad g=\frac{4\pi G\rho r_0}{3}.
$$

This is [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with $\omega=\sqrt{g/r_0}$ and $x=r_0\cos(\omega t)$. Crossing the centre takes a quarter-period; reaching the opposite surface takes a half-period:

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm cross}=\pi\sqrt{\frac{r_0}{g}}=\sqrt{\frac{3\pi}{4G\rho}}.}
$$

The body's [mass](../../../../mass.md) cancels. The polar diameter lies on the rotation axis, so the tunnel motion has no additional [centrifugal force](../../../../centrifugal-force.md) or [Coriolis force](../../../../coriolis-force.md) from the [Earth](../../../../earth.md)'s rotation. A realistic radial density profile would change the harmonic equation.
