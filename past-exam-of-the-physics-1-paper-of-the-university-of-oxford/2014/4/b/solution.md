The number of [degrees of freedom](../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) is the number of independent coordinates needed to specify an allowed configuration locally. For $N$ unconstrained point particles in three dimensions it is $3N$; $k$ independent regular [holonomic constraints](../../../../holonomic-constraint.md) reduce it to $3N-k$. Redundant constraints must not be counted twice, and nonholonomic velocity restrictions require separate care.
