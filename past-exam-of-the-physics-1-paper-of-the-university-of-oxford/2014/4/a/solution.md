[Generalized coordinates](../../../../generalized-coordinate.md) are variables $q_1,\ldots,q_n$ that specify the allowed instantaneous configuration of a [mechanical system](../../../../mechanical-system.md). They need not be Cartesian positions: a rigid pendulum's [angle](../../../../angle.md) is a suitable single coordinate. With constraints solved, the particle positions are functions $\mathbf r_i(q_1,\ldots,q_n,t)$.
