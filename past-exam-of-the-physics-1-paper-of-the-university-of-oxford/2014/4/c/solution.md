<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [holonomic constraint](../../../../holonomic-constraint.md) can be written as an equation $f(q_1,\ldots,q_n,t)=0$ involving coordinates and possibly time, without an irreducible dependence on [velocities](../../../../velocity.md). For example a particle confined to a fixed sphere obeys $x^2+y^2+z^2-a^2=0$. A general [rolling without slipping](../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) constraint in three dimensions need not be integrable into such a coordinate equation.
