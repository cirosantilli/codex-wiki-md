<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Choose the $M$ ball's initial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) as $+v$; the $3M$ ball then has [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $-v$. Both have [Lorentz factor](../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Their total [relativistic energy](../../../relativistic-energy.md) and signed [relativistic momentum](../../../relativistic-momentum.md) are

$$
E_i=4\gamma Mc^2,\qquad p_i=\gamma Mv-3\gamma Mv=-2\gamma Mv.
$$

The laboratory frame is not the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), despite the equal and opposite [speeds](../../../speed.md). [Four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) and the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) give the [invariant mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) $M_f$ of the combined incoming system:

$$
M_f^2c^4=E_i^2-c^2p_i^2
=\gamma^2M^2c^4(16-4\beta^2),\qquad \beta=\frac vc.
$$

The [relativistic collision mass threshold](../../../relativistic-collision-mass-threshold.md) for a final object of [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) $5M$ is $M_f\geq5M$. At the threshold,

$$
\frac{16-4\beta^2}{1-\beta^2}=25
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 21\beta^2=9,
\qquad
\boxed{v_{\min}=c\sqrt{\frac37}\simeq0.65465\,c.}
$$

At equality, the outgoing object has laboratory [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $V=c^2p_i/E_i=-v/2$, directed towards the initially heavier ball's motion. Its nonzero final [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) is why setting $4\gamma Mc^2=5Mc^2$ would give the wrong threshold.

For the literal [rest mass of a coalescing relativistic collision](../../../rest-mass-of-a-coalescing-relativistic-collision.md), if there is exactly one outgoing object of exactly fixed [rest mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) $5M$ and no radiation or other products, [four-momentum conservation](../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) selects the equality above, rather than allowing every larger $v$. Speeds above this threshold require a larger final [invariant mass](../../../invariant-mass.md) or additional outgoing [energy](../../../energy-split.md) carriers.
