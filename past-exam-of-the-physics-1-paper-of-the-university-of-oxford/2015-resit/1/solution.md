<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a [sphere](../../../sphere.md) with uniform [mass density](../../../density.md), $M=(4\pi/3)\rho a^3$. Its [moment of inertia of a uniform solid sphere](../../../moment-of-inertia-of-a-uniform-solid-sphere.md) is $I=2Ma^2/5$, and rotation about a diameter gives [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) magnitude $L=I\omega$. The spheres have the same [angular frequency](../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega$, so

$$
\frac{M_A}{M_B}=\frac{\rho_A}{\rho_B}\left(\frac{a_A}{a_B}\right)^3=\frac14\,2^3=2,
\qquad
\boxed{\frac{L_A}{L_B}=\frac{I_A}{I_B}=\frac{M_A}{M_B}\left(\frac{a_A}{a_B}\right)^2=8.}
$$

The [mass](../../../mass.md) grows by a factor of two, and the extra squared lever arm supplies a further factor of four. Equal [angular frequencies](../../../angular-frequency.md) alone do not mean equal [angular momenta](../../../angular-momentum.md).
