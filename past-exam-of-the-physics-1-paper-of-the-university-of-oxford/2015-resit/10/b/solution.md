<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [azimuth](../../../../azimuth.md) $\phi$ is a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), since $\partial\mathcal L/\partial\phi=0$. Its [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives conservation of the vertical component of [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md):

$$
\boxed{h=p_\phi=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot\phi}=ml^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi=\text{constant}.}
$$

This $h$ is the actual [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), not [specific angular momentum](../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md). The Lagrangian also has no explicit [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) dependence. The conserved [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{E=\dot\theta\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot\theta}+\dot\phi\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot\phi}-\mathcal L
=\frac12ml^2(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\dot\phi^2)-mgl\cos\theta.}
$$

Along solutions, $\dot E=-\partial\mathcal L/\partial t=0$. These are the conserved quantities associated with rotational [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) about the vertical and [time-translation symmetry](../../../../time-translation-symmetry.md), respectively.
