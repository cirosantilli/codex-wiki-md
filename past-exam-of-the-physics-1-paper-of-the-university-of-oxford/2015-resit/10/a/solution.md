<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [spherical coordinates](../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) about the fixed pivot, with fixed rod [length](../../../../length.md) $l$, [polar angle](../../../../polar-angle.md) $\theta$ measured from the downward vertical, and [azimuth](../../../../azimuth.md) $\phi$. The bob's [position](../../../../position.md) is

$$
\mathbf r=l(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\ \sin\theta\sin\phi,\ -\cos\theta)
$$

when the Cartesian $z$ axis points upward. Differentiation gives [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $T=ml^2(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\dot\phi^2)/2$. Taking zero [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) at pivot height gives $V=-mgl\cos\theta$. The [spherical pendulum](../../../../spherical-pendulum.md)'s [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=T-V=\frac12ml^2(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\dot\phi^2)+mgl\cos\theta.}
$$

The rod is light, rigid and of fixed [length](../../../../length.md); pivot [friction](../../../../friction.md) and air [drag](../../../../drag-physics.md) are neglected. A rigid rod, unlike a string, may sustain either tensile or compressive constraint [force](../../../../force-split.md).
