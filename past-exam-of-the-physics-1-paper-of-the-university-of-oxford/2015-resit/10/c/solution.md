<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The $\theta$ [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) reads

$$
\frac d{dt}(ml^2\dot\theta)-\left(ml^2\sin\theta\cos\theta\dot\phi^2-mgl\sin\theta\right)=0.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\ddot\theta=\sin\theta\cos\theta\dot\phi^2-\frac gl\sin\theta.}
$$

The first term reflects the coupling to azimuthal motion; the second is the gravitational restoring contribution for an [angle](../../../../angle.md) measured from the downward vertical.
