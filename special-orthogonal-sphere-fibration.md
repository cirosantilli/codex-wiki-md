# Special orthogonal sphere fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

The last-column map $p(A)=Ae_{s+1}$ has fiber $SO(s)$, included as $\operatorname{diag}(B,1)$. Local completion of a unit vector by the [Gram-Schmidt process](gram-schmidt-process.md) gives local [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) charts. Consequently it has the [homotopy lifting property](homotopy-lifting-property.md) and the [long exact sequence of homotopy groups of a fibration](long-exact-sequence-of-homotopy-groups-of-a-fibration.md).
