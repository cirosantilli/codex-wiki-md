# Impurity band

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

An [energy band](energy-band.md) formed when localized [impurity levels](impurity-level.md) hybridize. For [hydrogenic donor states](hydrogenic-donor-state.md), a typical donor separation $n^{-1/3}$ gives an exponentially small [hopping integral](hopping-integral.md) and hence an exponentially narrow [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) when $n^{-1/3}\gg a_b$. An idealized half-filled band with two [spin](spin.md) states per donor has spin-summed [density of states](density-of-states.md) of order $2n/W$ per volume; its [Pauli paramagnetism](pauli-paramagnetism.md) is $\chi\sim\mu_0(g\mu_B/2)^2(2n/W)$. That expression requires $k_BT\ll W$ and does not by itself describe localization or strong [Coulomb interactions](coulomb-interaction.md).
