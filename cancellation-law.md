# Cancellation law

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)

Left cancellation means $ax=ay\Rightarrow x=y$; right cancellation means $xa=ya\Rightarrow x=y$. A [group](group-split.md) has both laws, since multiplication by an [inverse element](inverse-element.md) cancels the common factor.
