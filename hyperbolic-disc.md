# Hyperbolic disc

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md)

A hyperbolic disc consists of points within a specified [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md) of its centre. In the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md), the disc centred at zero with radius $\rho$ has Euclidean radius $\tanh(\rho/2)$; an [isometry](isometry.md) transports this description to any other centre.
