# Gravitational radiation from a rotating triaxial body

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md)

For a rigid body rotating around its third principal axis, the anisotropic part of its [second mass moment tensor](second-mass-moment-tensor.md) oscillates at angular frequency $2\Omega$. The [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md) gives the displayed luminosity. Equal transverse principal moments remove this rotating quadrupole and its leading [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md) emission.
