# Inertia of a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

The inertia of a real symmetric bilinear form is the triple giving the numbers of positive, negative, and zero squares in a diagonalization. [Sylvester's law of inertia](sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md) makes it independent of the chosen basis.
