# Double-negation introduction

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From $P$, temporarily assume $\neg P$. [Negation elimination rule](negation-elimination-rule.md) gives [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md); discharge that temporary assumption by [negation introduction](negation-introduction-rule.md) to obtain $\neg\neg P$. This is valid in [Intuitionistic propositional logic](intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md), unlike unrestricted [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md).
