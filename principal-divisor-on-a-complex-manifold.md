# Principal divisor on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md)

The [divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) of a nonzero global [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) records its zero and pole orders along irreducible analytic hypersurfaces. It is called principal. Its [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) is trivial: choosing the same global [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) as the local equation on every open set gives identity transition functions. In particular, two hyperplanes in [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) have isomorphic associated bundles, since the quotient of their homogeneous linear equations is a global [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) with their difference as divisor.
