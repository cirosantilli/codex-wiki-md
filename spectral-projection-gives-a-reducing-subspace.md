# Spectral projection gives a reducing subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral measure of a normal operator](spectral-measure-of-a-normal-operator.md)

For a bounded [normal operator](normal-operator.md) $T$ with [spectral measure of a normal operator](spectral-measure-of-a-normal-operator.md) $P$, any projection $P(E)$ commutes with $T$ and $T^*$ by the [Borel functional calculus for a normal operator](borel-functional-calculus-for-a-normal-operator.md). Its range is a closed reducing subspace. If the spectrum has two points, choose disjoint nonempty relative open sets around them. [Full support of a faithful spectral measure](full-support-of-a-faithful-spectral-measure.md) makes both projections nonzero and their product zero, so either range is nonzero and proper, in particular an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md).
