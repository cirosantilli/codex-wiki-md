# Molar mass constant

↑ **Parent:** [Molar mass](molar-mass.md)

The [molar mass constant](molar-mass-constant.md) converts [relative atomic mass](relative-atomic-mass.md) or [relative molecular mass](relative-molecular-mass.md) into [molar mass](molar-mass.md). It is approximately $1\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) numerical value is determined experimentally: the exact [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) multiplies an experimentally determined [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md) in kilograms. Treating $M_{\rm u}$ as exactly $1\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ is therefore an excellent elementary-calculation approximation, rather than a defining equality of the modern [SI](international-system-of-units.md).[§2.3.1, The mole, printed p. 131: carbon-12 molar mass no longer exact; M\_u = N\_A m\_u and M(X) = A\_r(X) M\_u](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
