# Gaussian quadratic exponential moment

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential moment of a Gaussian linear functional](exponential-moment-of-a-gaussian-linear-functional.md)

For a real standard [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md) $Z$, a real symmetric matrix $Q$, and $I-2Q$ strictly positive,

$$
\mathbb E e^{b^TZ+Z^TQZ}=\det(I-2Q)^{-1/2}\exp\left[\frac12b^T(I-2Q)^{-1}b\right].
$$

Diagonalize $Q$ and complete the square in each coordinate. Failure of strict positivity makes the positive exponential integral divergent. The same formula extends to a self-adjoint trace-class quadratic form of an isonormal [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md), with square-summable linear coefficients and a [Fredholm determinant](fredholm-determinant.md). A linear-plus-quadratic [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md) functional is reduced to this form by its covariance square root.
