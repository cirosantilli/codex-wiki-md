# Effective density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

The [effective density of states](effective-density-of-states.md) of a parabolic semiconductor band is its [density of states](density-of-states.md) integrated against a thermal Boltzmann factor measured from its edge. For one spin-degenerate valley,

$$
N_c=2\left(\frac{m_e^*k_BT}{2\pi\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2},\qquad
N_v=2\left(\frac{m_h^*k_BT}{2\pi\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

These quantities have units of number per volume, unlike the energy-resolved [density of states](density-of-states.md). In a nondegenerate [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), $n=N_c e^{-(E_c-\mu)/(k_BT)}$ and $p=N_v e^{-(\mu-E_v)/(k_BT)}$; multiple equivalent valleys add their contributions.
