# Relative entropy of entanglement

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement monotone](entanglement-monotone.md)

The relative entropy of entanglement is the smallest [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md) from a [density operator](density-matrix.md) to a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md), with the separable set specified for the relevant division into parties. [Nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy](nonnegativity-of-quantum-relative-entropy.md) implies $E_R\geq0$. For a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md), choosing $\sigma=\rho$ gives $E_R=0$. For finite-dimensional bipartite states, compactness of the separable set and lower semicontinuity of the relative entropy give the converse. The support convention is $D(\rho\|\sigma)=+\infty$ unless $\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma$.
