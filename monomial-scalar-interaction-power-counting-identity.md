# Monomial scalar interaction power-counting identity

↑ **Parent:** [Superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md)

For a connected scalar graph with nonderivative interaction $-\lambda\phi^n$, $E$ external legs and $V$ vertices, $nV=2I+E$ and $L=I-V+1$. Since $[\phi]=(d-2)/2$ and $[\lambda]=d-n(d-2)/2$, the [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is

$$
\Delta=dL-2I=d-\frac{d-2}{2}E-[\lambda]V.
$$

The graph contributes at order $\lambda^V$. This is simultaneous large-loop-momentum counting for an amputated graph; it does not test subdivergences or guarantee that the predicted pole coefficient is nonzero.
