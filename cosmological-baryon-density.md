# Cosmological baryon density

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md)

The [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) is the mass density in baryonic matter, including hydrogen and helium. Up to nuclear binding-energy and composition corrections it is the proton mass times the baryon number density. The [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) links that number density to the [photon number density](photon-number-density.md). Larger baryon density increases nuclear reaction rates and permits the [deuterium bottleneck](deuterium-bottleneck.md) to end earlier.
