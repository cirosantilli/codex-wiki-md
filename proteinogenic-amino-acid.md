# Proteinogenic amino acid

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha-amino acid](alpha-amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteinogenic_amino_acid)

A [proteinogenic amino acid](proteinogenic-amino-acid.md) is incorporated into [proteins](protein.md) during [translation](translation-geometry.md). The conventional twenty include [glycine](glycine.md), [alanine](alanine.md), [methionine](methionine.md) and [proline](proline.md); they are a subset of [amino acids](amino-acid.md), not a definition of the whole family.
