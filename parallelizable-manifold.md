# Parallelizable manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallelizable_manifold)

A parallelizable manifold is a smooth $n$-manifold whose tangent bundle is trivial, equivalently one admitting $n$ smooth vector fields that form a basis of every tangent space. Every [Lie group](lie-group.md) is parallelizable because a basis at the identity extends to a global frame by left translation.
