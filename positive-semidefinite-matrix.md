# Positive semidefinite matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Definite matrix](definite-matrix.md)

A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix $A$ is positive semidefinite when $x^\dagger Ax\geq0$ for every [vector](vector.md) $x$, equivalently when all its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are nonnegative.
