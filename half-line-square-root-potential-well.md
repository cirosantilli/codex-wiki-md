# Half-line square-root potential well

↑ **Parent:** [Potential well](potential-well.md)

A confining [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) on $x>0$ with $a>0$, potential $a\sqrt x$ and a [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) at zero has natural length $\ell=(\hbar^2/(2ma))^{2/5}$ and [energy](energy-split.md) $E_*=(\hbar^2a^4/(2m))^{1/5}$. Rescaling $x=\ell y$ gives $H/E_*=-d^2/dy^2+\sqrt y$, so every [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) scales as $E_*$. The potential [derivative](derivative.md) diverges at the wall even though the potential itself is finite there.
