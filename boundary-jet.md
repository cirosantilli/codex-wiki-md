# Boundary jet

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy data](cauchy-data.md)

The boundary jet through order $m-1$ is the collection of traces $\partial^\alpha u|_{\partial\Omega}$ with $|\alpha|\le m-1$. Vanishing of the whole jet is stronger than vanishing of one [derivative](derivative.md). On a smooth [hypersurface](hypersurface.md), zero normal Cauchy [derivatives](derivative.md) through order $m-1$, together with their tangential [derivatives](derivative.md), give a zero full boundary jet.
