# Universal coefficient theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_coefficient_theorem)

The [universal coefficient theorem](universal-coefficient-theorem.md) relates homology or cohomology with different coefficient groups through tensor and Tor, or Hom and Ext, respectively. The [universal coefficient theorem for homology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-homology.md) is its tensor-Tor sequence; the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) is its Hom-Ext sequence.
