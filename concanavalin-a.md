# Concanavalin A

↑ **Parent:** [Lectin](lectin.md)

A [lectin](lectin.md) used to label accessible carbohydrate-containing surfaces, including [yeast](yeast.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) walls. A pre-growth pulse marks old surface material; a new unlabelled bud can identify newly deposited wall.
