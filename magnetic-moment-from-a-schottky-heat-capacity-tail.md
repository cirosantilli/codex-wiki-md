# Magnetic moment from a Schottky heat-capacity tail

↑ **Parent:** [Independent symmetric three-level system](independent-symmetric-three-level-system.md)

For one three-level unit with energies $-\mu B,0,\mu B$, the high-temperature [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $C\simeq2\mu^2B^2/(3k_BT^2)$. A measured coefficient $C=a/T^2$ therefore gives the displayed magnetic moment. If the [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) instead represents $N$ independent units, replace $a$ by $a/N$; the normalization must be established before extracting a single-unit moment.
