# Joint coprimality

↑ **Parent:** [Coprime integers](coprime-integers.md)

A list of [integers](integer-split.md) $a_1,\ldots,a_r$, with $r\ge2$, has [joint coprimality](joint-coprimality.md) when its [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) is one; define that [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) by repeated application of the two-input operation. For [positive integers](positive-integer.md), no [prime factor](prime-factor.md) may divide every entry. Thus $6,10,15$ have [joint coprimality](joint-coprimality.md) but fail [pairwise coprimality](pairwise-coprimality.md). For two entries, the conditions coincide.
