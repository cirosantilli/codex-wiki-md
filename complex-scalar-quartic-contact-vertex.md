# Complex scalar quartic contact vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md)

For the connected tree [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) of one particle and one [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) of a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md), the [Dyson series](dyson-series.md) inserts $-i\lambda\phi^{\dagger 2}\phi^2/4$. There are $2!$ assignments of the particle and antiparticle external attachments to each pair of identical field factors, giving $2!2!=4$ and hence the displayed [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md). With [relativistic normalization of a one-particle state](relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md), integrating the vertex position gives $i\mathcal T(2\pi)^4\delta^4(p_1+q_1-p_2-q_2)$ with $\mathcal T=-\lambda$. [Vacuum diagrams](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md) and one-particle [self-energy](self-energy.md) insertions are separate disconnected contributions; [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) eliminates the single-vertex vacuum and tadpole contractions.
