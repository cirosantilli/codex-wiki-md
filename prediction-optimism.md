# Prediction optimism

↑ **Parent:** [Training error](training-error.md)

Prediction optimism is the difference between expected squared prediction error on new independent responses at the training covariates and expected [training error](training-error.md). Reusing responses to fit and assess a model tends to make training error too favorable. The [covariance formula for prediction optimism](covariance-formula-for-prediction-optimism.md) measures this reuse without assuming a linear fitting rule.
