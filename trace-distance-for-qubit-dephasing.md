# Trace distance for qubit dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Trace distance](trace-distance.md)

For the pure [qubit](qubit.md) $\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$, subtracting the output of [dephasing channel](dephasing-channel.md) leaves a Hermitian matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal modulus $|\alpha\beta|\,|1-c|$. Its two [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are that number and its negative, giving the displayed [trace distance](trace-distance.md). For real $c=1-2p$, this is $2p|\alpha\beta|$.
