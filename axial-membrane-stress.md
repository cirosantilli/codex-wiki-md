# Axial membrane stress

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-walled pressure vessel](thin-walled-pressure-vessel.md)

The longitudinal membrane [stress](stress.md) carried by a shell. A thin cylindrical tube supporting its closed-end pressure load has $\sigma_z=Pr/(2t)$; external end loading or an open end changes that balance.
