# Complement of a total Sigma-1 function graph

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-1 formula](sigma-1-formula.md)

If a total single-valued numerical [function](function-split.md) has a Sigma-1 graph, the complement of that graph is also Sigma-1. An input-output pair is outside the graph exactly when some different output is in the graph. Totality and uniqueness are essential to that equivalence. Stage-comparison separation of enumerations of the graph and its complement then gives [weak definability of a numerical function](weak-definability-of-a-numerical-function.md) in a bounded-complete [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md).
