# Schur-Zassenhaus theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hall subgroup](hall-subgroup.md)

A finite [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) whose order is coprime to its index has a [group complement](complement-of-a-normal-subgroup.md): if $N\triangleleft G$ is a [Hall subgroup](hall-subgroup.md), then $G=NH$ and $N\cap H=1$ for some subgroup $H$. Complement conjugacy holds when either $N$ or $G/N$ is solvable; the existence assertion does not require that extra hypothesis.
