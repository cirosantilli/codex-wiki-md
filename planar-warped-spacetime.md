# Planar warped spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

For positive smooth $A$, this [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md) has flat $(t,x,y)$ slices scaled by $A(z)^2$. Translation in each slice coordinate gives a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md). An [orthonormal coframe](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) is $e^0=A\,dt$, $e^1=A\,dx$, $e^2=A\,dy$, $e^3=dz$. Its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) and [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) are controlled by $a=A'/A$ and $q=A''/A=a'+a^2$.
