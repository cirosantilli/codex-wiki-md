# Three-wave mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

An interaction among three [waves](wave-split.md) mediated by a second-order [nonlinear optical susceptibility](nonlinear-optical-susceptibility.md). In [sum-frequency generation](sum-frequency-generation.md), two input [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) satisfy $\omega_3=\omega_1+\omega_2$. The same coupling permits reverse conversion. [Phase matching](phase-matching.md) determines whether successive generated contributions reinforce; the [Manley-Rowe relations](manley-rowe-relations.md) express the associated lossless exchange constraints.
