# Completeness of the bounded-variation space

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

A [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) $(u_n)$ in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) converges in $L^1$ to $u$. For fixed sufficiently large $n$, [lower semicontinuity](lower-semicontinuity.md) gives $|D(u_n-u)|(\Omega)\le\liminf_m|D(u_n-u_m)|(\Omega)$. The $L^1$ [norm](norm.md) of the same difference converges, so the small full-norm Cauchy bound passes to $u_n-u$. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) then puts $u$ in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) and proves convergence in its full [norm](norm.md). This argument turns completeness of $L^1$ and [lower semicontinuity](lower-semicontinuity.md) of variation into completeness of the combined [norm](norm.md).
