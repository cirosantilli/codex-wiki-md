# Planetary ice shell

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

A planetary ice shell is a solid water-rich layer overlying an ocean or deeper planetary interior. Its thickness can be controlled by [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md), basal heat flux, surface radiation, and solid-state [mantle convection](mantle-convection.md).
