# Finite intersection property

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_intersection_property)

A family of sets has the finite intersection property when every finite subfamily has nonempty intersection. A [topological space](topological-space.md) is compact exactly when every family of closed subsets with the finite intersection property has nonempty total intersection.
