# Polar decomposition of a vector measure

↑ **Parent:** [Vector Radon measure](vector-radon-measure.md)

Each component of a finite [vector measure](vector-measure.md) is absolutely continuous with respect to its variation [measure](measure.md). The [Radon-Nikodym theorem](radon-nikodym-theorem.md) gives a vector density $\sigma$. Taking the [variation measure](variation-measure.md) of this representation gives $|\mu|=|\sigma||\mu|$, hence the unit-length property. For a BV [derivative](derivative.md) this separates the magnitude of variation from its local direction.
