# Proof stress

↑ **Parent:** [Yield point](yield-point.md)

An offset [proof stress](proof-stress.md) is defined by the intersection of a loading curve with a line parallel to its initial [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) response but shifted by a specified [engineering strain](engineering-strain.md). A 0.2 percent convention uses the line $\sigma=E(\varepsilon-0.002)$, with [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) $E$.
