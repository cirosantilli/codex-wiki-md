# Luminous efficacy

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminous_efficacy)

The [luminous efficacy](luminous-efficacy.md) of radiation is [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) divided by total [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md), in $\mathrm{lm\,W^{-1}}$, for the specified visual conditions. For nonzero [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md), it is a spectral [weighted mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) of [spectral luminous efficacy](spectral-luminous-efficacy.md). Specify the denominator: a lamp's [luminous efficacy](luminous-efficacy.md) relative to electrical input [power](power.md) also includes conversion losses and is a different ratio.
