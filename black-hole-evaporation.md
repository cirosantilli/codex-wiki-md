# Black-hole evaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md)

[Black-hole evaporation](black-hole-evaporation.md) is mass loss through [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md). For a large isolated [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), the leading mass dependence is $dM/dt\propto-M^{-2}$ and the evaporation timescale is proportional to $M^3$. This semiclassical scaling cannot determine the endpoint when the curvature reaches quantum-gravitational scales.
