# Module of smooth sections

↑ **Parent:** [Section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

The smooth [sections of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) form a [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $C^\infty(M)$ by pointwise multiplication. A [vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md) over the identity induces a $C^\infty(M)$-linear map between these modules. [Bundle morphisms from maps of smooth sections](bundle-morphisms-from-maps-of-smooth-sections.md) gives the converse, without assuming the bundle has a global frame.
