<h1 id="myers-s-theorem">Myers's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myers's_theorem)

For a [connected](connected-space.md) [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of dimension $n\geq2$, assume [geodesic completeness](geodesic-completeness.md) and $\operatorname{Ric}\geq(n-1)\kappa g$ for a constant $\kappa>0$. The [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) then has diameter at most $\pi/\sqrt\kappa$; the manifold is [compact](compact-space.md) and its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is finite. The dimension restriction excludes the vacuous one-dimensional Ricci bound.
