# Angular separation

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomy](astronomy-split.md)

The angle between two lines of sight. For a small transverse physical separation $s$ at distance $D$, $\theta\simeq s/D$ in radians. At cosmological distances $D$ is the appropriate [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md).
