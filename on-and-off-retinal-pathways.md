# ON and OFF retinal pathways

↑ **Parent:** [Retinal bipolar cell](retinal-bipolar-cell.md)

Parallel retinal pathways emphasizing increments and decrements in stimulation. Their sign differences depend on synaptic transformations of [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) output; an ON pathway is not simply a [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) that depolarizes to [light](light.md).
