# Direct sum of modules

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

The direct sum of a family of [modules](module-mathematics.md) over the same [ring](ring.md) consists of all tuples $(m_j)$ with $m_j\in M_j$ and only finitely many nonzero coordinates, with coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication. For two [submodules](submodule.md) $N,P\subseteq M$, an internal direct sum $M=N\oplus P$ means $M=N+P$ and $N\cap P=\{0\}$. Equivalently, every element of $M$ is uniquely $n+p$: equality of two such expressions puts their difference in $N\cap P$. The projection $n+p\mapsto p$ is a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) with [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $N$, image $P$, and square equal to itself.
