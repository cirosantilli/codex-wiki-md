<h1 id="mobius-invariance-of-on-shell-closed-string-vertex-integrals">Möbius invariance of on-shell closed-string vertex integrals</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Koba-Nielsen factor](koba-nielsen-factor.md)

Under $z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d)$ with $ad-bc=1$, the coordinate measure contributes $|cz_i+d|^{-4}$ at insertion $i$. The [Koba-Nielsen factor](koba-nielsen-factor.md) contributes $|cz_i+d|^{-\alpha'\sum_{j\ne i}p_i\cdot p_j}$. [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) makes this exponent $\alpha'p_i^2$, so the factors cancel precisely on the displayed [tachyon](tachyon.md) mass shell. The correlator alone is covariant; its product with all the coordinate measures is invariant.
