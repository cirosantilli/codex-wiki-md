# Peak and integrated resonance width dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

At fixed entrance and exit [partial decay widths](partial-decay-width.md), a resonance's peak [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) is proportional to their product divided by the square of its total width. Integrating a narrow Lorentzian over energy supplies one factor of the total width, leaving an inverse first power. Extracting a width from a peak ratio therefore differs from extracting it from an integrated ratio.
