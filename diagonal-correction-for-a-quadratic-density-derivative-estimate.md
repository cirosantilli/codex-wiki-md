# Diagonal correction for a quadratic density derivative estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic density derivative functional](quadratic-density-derivative-functional.md)

For $A(v)=\int L^{\prime\prime}(t)L^{\prime\prime}(t+v)\,dt$, this [U-statistic](u-statistic.md) has [expectation](expected-value.md) $\|L_b*f^{\prime\prime}\|_2^2$. It removes the diagonal term $\|L^{\prime\prime}\|_2^2/(nb^5)$ from the integral of a squared [second derivative kernel density estimator](second-derivative-kernel-density-estimator.md), but leaves smoothing [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md).
