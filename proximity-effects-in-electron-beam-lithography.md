# Proximity effects in electron-beam lithography

↑ **Parent:** [Electron beam lithography](electron-beam-lithography.md)

Unintended exposure near a written feature caused by [electron](electron.md) scattering in the resist and substrate. The deposited-energy distribution is broader than the incident probe, requiring dose or geometry correction for dense and isolated patterns.
