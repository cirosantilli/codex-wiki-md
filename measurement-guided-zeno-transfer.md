# Measurement-guided Zeno transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Zeno effect](quantum-zeno-effect.md)

Successively measure in bases whose first vector rotates from $|0\rangle$ to $|1\rangle$ in $N$ equal angular steps. The probability to remain on that branch throughout is the displayed product of squared overlaps. Without postselection, the final target probability is $[1+\cos^N(\pi/N)]/2$. Both approach one for an initial $|0\rangle$, but a general input loses its relative phase between the two measurement branches; the process does not implement a coherent [Pauli X gate](pauli-x-gate.md) on unknown inputs.
