# Layer reverberation

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

Repeated [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) and conversion between a layer surface and its lower interface produces additional delayed phases. Their travel times and amplitude depend on layer thickness, wave [speeds](speed.md), impedance contrasts, attenuation and source spectrum.
