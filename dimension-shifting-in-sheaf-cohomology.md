# Dimension shifting in sheaf cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md)

From a [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md) $0\to K_{j+1}\to G_j\to K_j\to0$, the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) gives the displayed [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) whenever $H^q(X,G_j)=H^{q+1}(X,G_j)=0$. Repeating it along a [locally free resolution](locally-free-resolution.md) shifts a fixed group to successively higher degrees. If all relevant [kernel sheaves](kernel-sheaf.md) are [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) and there is a uniform vanishing bound for their [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md), sufficiently many shifts force the original group to vanish. The [cohomological dimension bound from an affine cover](cohomological-dimension-bound-from-an-affine-cover.md) supplies such a bound for a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) of [projective space](projective-space-split.md).
