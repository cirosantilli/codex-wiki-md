# Weak boundedness implies norm boundedness

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md)

A subset of a normed space is norm bounded if it is bounded under every continuous linear functional. Apply the [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) on the complete dual space to the evaluation family $f\mapsto f(x)$. [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) identifies the evaluation norm with $\|x\|$, even when the original normed space is not complete. As a consequence, two norms with exactly the same continuous dual as sets must be equivalent: apply this criterion to either norm's unit ball in the other norm.
