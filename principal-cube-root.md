# Principal cube root

↑ **Parent:** [Complex exponentiation](complex-exponentiation.md)

The principal cube root is $z^{1/3}=\exp(\operatorname{Log}z/3)$ using the [principal complex logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md). In the right half-plane, its [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) lies between $-\pi/6$ and $\pi/6$. Consequently the roots of $r^3=-z$ consist of one root with negative [real part](real-part.md), $-z^{1/3}$, and two with positive [real part](real-part.md), $z^{1/3}e^{\pm i\pi/3}$.
