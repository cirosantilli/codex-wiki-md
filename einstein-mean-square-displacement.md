# Einstein mean-square displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md)

For one Cartesian direction in an [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md), $\langle x^2\rangle=\hbar[2\langle n\rangle+1]/(2m\omega_E)$. Substituting the thermal oscillator occupation proves the displayed result. At $T\gg T_E$, [equipartition](equipartition-theorem.md) gives $\langle x^2\rangle=k_BT/(m\omega_E^2)=\hbar^2T/(mk_BT_E^2)$. The total displacement variance of an isotropic atom is three times the one-coordinate result, a distinction that matters in applying a melting threshold.
