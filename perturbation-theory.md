# Perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

Perturbation theory develops approximate solutions in a small parameter around a simpler reference problem. A power-series [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) gives equations for successive correction coefficients; a [matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md) handles cases where one expansion is not uniformly valid.
