# Pulsed-field optical magnetostriction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md)

A $3\times1.5\times1.5\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ [single crystal](single-crystal.md) of [gadolinium antimonide](gadolinium-antimonide.md) carried a $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ [fibre Bragg grating](fiber-bragg-grating.md) cyanoacrylate-bonded along its $a$ axis. The pulsed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) paralleled that axis. Broadband light, a circulator and a spectrometer resolved reflected-wavelength shifts at $5.4\,\mathrm K$. Pre-pulse readings measured noise; low fibre [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) reduced thermal background. A residual post-pulse shift indicated possible heating. Spectral distortion checked bond uniformity, while the fibre's smaller cross-section limited loading; bonding was not assumed force-free. Longitudinal [strain](strain.md) showed a kink near $32.6\,\mathrm T$.[PDF pp. 2–4, Figs. 1–2](high-resolution-magnetostriction-measurements-in-pulsed-magnetic-fields-using-fibre-bragg-gratings-by-ramzy-daou-and-others.md)

The distinction between readout and inference is essential. The [fibre Bragg grating](fiber-bragg-grating.md) senses a change of reflected [wavelength](wavelength.md); interpreting this as the specimen's [strain](strain.md) requires good strain transfer and a controlled [temperature](temperature-split.md). A useful independent control would compare a bonded sensor with a nearby unloaded sensor through the same pulse. Another would vary bond stiffness or specimen cross-section to test loading. These are proposed checks, not additional procedures claimed for the experiment above. The pulsed magnet's winding details were not specified in the inspected account.

This experiment measures one longitudinal component of [strain](strain.md). Establishing the first-order [volume](volume.md) change would require three orthogonal normal components under the same conditions. GdSb also has its own [antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md) and field-dependent magnetic structure: this measured curve does not establish the simple saturated [isotropic](isotropy.md) law. Its role here is to make the measurement chain and its limitations concrete.
