# Entrainment coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md)

The entrainment coefficient is the dimensionless constant in the [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md), so the mean speed at which ambient fluid crosses a plume edge is $\alpha$ times the plume's characteristic axial speed.
