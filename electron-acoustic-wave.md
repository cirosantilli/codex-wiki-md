# Electron acoustic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma oscillation](plasma-oscillation.md)

An electron-supported electrostatic branch with approximately linear small-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega\simeq ku_a$. A [velocity plateau](velocity-plateau.md) can support such a completely undamped mode even when ion response is neglected. A single [Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md) electron population generally damps the corresponding acoustic response strongly. Distinct electron populations provide another setting for electron acoustic behavior, with different assumptions and damping properties.
