# Equilibrium peeling force of an adsorbed Gaussian chain

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer adsorption](polymer-adsorption.md)

Let an adsorbed [Gaussian chain](gaussian-chain.md) gain [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $\mu>0$ per bound [monomer](monomer.md). A pulled segment of $n$ [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md) steps spanning height $z$ has elastic [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $3k_BTz^2/(2nb^2)$, giving $F=-(N-n)\mu+3k_BTz^2/(2nb^2)$. Minimizing over $n$ yields $n=(z/b)\sqrt{3k_BT/(2\mu)}$ and $F_{\min}=-N\mu+z\sqrt{6k_BT\mu}/b$. Its height [derivative](derivative.md) is the constant peeling [force](force-split.md) displayed above. The plateau lasts while $0<n<N$ and the [Gaussian chain](gaussian-chain.md) approximation remains valid. In the slab model $F_{\mathrm{ads}}=Nk_BT[(b/\xi)^2-\epsilon b/\xi]$, optimization gives $\mu=k_BT\epsilon^2/4$ and $f=\sqrt{3/2}\,\epsilon k_BT/b$.
