# Gene tree

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md)

A [phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md) describing the ancestry of sampled gene copies or a genomic region. It need not match a [species tree](species-tree.md), because lineage sorting, duplication or [introgression](introgression.md) can produce a different history.
