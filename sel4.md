# seL4

↑ **Parent:** [Microkernel](microkernel.md)

A [microkernel](microkernel.md) using [security capabilities](capability-computer-security.md) to control access to [operating-system kernel](kernel-operating-system.md) objects, including [IPC endpoints](ipc-endpoint.md) and [software threads](thread-computing.md). An [IPC endpoint](ipc-endpoint.md) connects cooperating [user-space services](user-space-service.md); its [security capabilities](capability-computer-security.md) are distinct from the service-defined handle `9` in the [microkernel file-read example](microkernel-file-read-example.md). [Capabilities tutorial, Background: What is a capability?](capabilities-tutorial-by-sel4-project.md)
