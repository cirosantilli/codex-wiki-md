<h1 id="fabry-perot-interferometer">Fabry–Pérot interferometer</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

A Fabry–Pérot interferometer uses multiple reflections between two nearly parallel surfaces. Its transmission peaks select narrow [wavelength](wavelength.md) intervals; scanning the spacing or incidence angle can build an imaging spectral cube.
