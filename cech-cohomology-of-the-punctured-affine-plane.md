<h1 id="cech-cohomology-of-the-punctured-affine-plane">Čech cohomology of the punctured affine plane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md)

The two affine opens $D(x),D(y)$ cover the [punctured affine plane](punctured-affine-plane.md). Their intersection has ring $k[x^{\pm1},y^{\pm1}]$. The [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) in degree one is the quotient by $k[x^{\pm1},y]+k[x,y^{\pm1}]$. Its basis consists precisely of the Laurent monomials whose two exponents are negative, so this [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is infinite-dimensional. Global functions, by contrast, form $k[x,y]$.
