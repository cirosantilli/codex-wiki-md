# Carnot cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnot_cycle)

A Carnot cycle consists of two reversible [isothermal changes](isothermal-process.md), one at each reservoir temperature, joined by two reversible [adiabatic processes](adiabatic-process.md). It attains the maximum [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md) permitted between those reservoirs, $1-T_c/T_h$.
