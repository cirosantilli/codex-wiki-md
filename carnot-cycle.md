# Carnot cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnot_cycle)

A Carnot cycle realizes the reversible bound in [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md). Its four steps for an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) working substance are:

- Reversible [isothermal expansion](isothermal-expansion.md) at $T_h$, absorbing $Q_h$ and increasing [entropy](entropy-split.md) by $\Delta S$.
- Reversible [adiabatic expansion](adiabatic-expansion.md), doing [work](work.md) while the [temperature](temperature-split.md) falls from $T_h$ to $T_c$ and [entropy](entropy-split.md) stays fixed.
- Reversible [isothermal compression](isothermal-compression.md) at $T_c$, rejecting $Q_c$ and decreasing [entropy](entropy-split.md) by $\Delta S$.
- Reversible [adiabatic compression](adiabatic-compression.md), requiring [work](work.md) to restore $T_h$ at fixed [entropy](entropy-split.md).

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) versus [entropy](entropy-split.md) plot in the [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md) figure is therefore a rectangle traversed clockwise. Because $\delta Q_{\rm rev}=T\,dS$, its width is $\Delta S=Q_h/T_h$. For the illustrated engine, $\Delta S=5/3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$: the upper step absorbs $600\times5/3=1000\,\mathrm J$, the lower step rejects $300\times5/3=500\,\mathrm J$, and the enclosed area is the $500\,\mathrm J$ net [work](work.md). Geometry displays the [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md) without solving the gas motion. The two vertical steps are [isentropic processes](isentropic-process.md), not constant-[temperature](temperature-split.md) steps. [MIT, Four steps of a Carnot cycle](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node24.html) [MIT, Temperature-entropy diagrams](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node46.html)

Reversible [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) is a limit of vanishing [temperature](temperature-split.md) differences; ordinary finite-rate transfer introduces [entropy production](entropy-production.md). The [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md) is a performance benchmark rather than a guarantee of useful [power](power.md). Running it backward gives the ideal operation of a [refrigerator](refrigerator.md) or [heat pump](heat-pump.md), with [work](work.md) supplied to move [heat](heat-split.md) toward the hotter [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md). [MIT, Reversed cycles and refrigeration](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node25.html)
