# Vibrational relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

Loss of excess vibrational excitation to other molecular degrees of freedom or to the surroundings. It can occur within one electronic state without [photon emission](photon-emission.md), reducing the energy available to subsequent [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md).
