# Ghost coupling in the Abelian Higgs model

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear R-xi gauge in polar Higgs variables](nonlinear-r-xi-gauge-in-polar-higgs-variables.md)

With the specified scalar-dependent [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md), the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) depends on the radial [Higgs mode](higgs-mode.md) even though the [gauge group](gauge-group.md) is Abelian. Besides a ghost [mass](mass.md) squared $\xi e^2f^2$, the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) contains $-2\xi e^2fh\bar cc-\xi e^2h^2\bar cc$. These [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) do not decouple for generic $\xi$; their interactions vanish in the $\xi\to0$ [Landau gauge](landau-gauge-for-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) limit. Free ghosts in pure Abelian [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) rely on a field-independent determinant, rather than merely on commutativity of the [gauge group](gauge-group.md).
