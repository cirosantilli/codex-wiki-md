# Coordinate avoidance from a nonzero permanent

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial Nullstellensatz](combinatorial-nullstellensatz.md)

If an $n$ by $n$ matrix $A$ over a field has nonzero [permanent](permanent-mathematics.md), $b\in F^n$, and every $S_i\subseteq F$ has two elements, then some $x\in\prod_iS_i$ makes every coordinate of $Ax-b$ nonzero. Apply the [Combinatorial Nullstellensatz](combinatorial-nullstellensatz.md) to

$$
\prod_{i=1}^n((Ax)_i-b_i),
$$

whose coefficient of $x_1\cdots x_n$ is $\operatorname{perm}A$.
