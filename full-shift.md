# Full shift

↑ **Parent:** [Symbolic dynamics](symbolic-dynamics.md)

The two-sided [full shift](full-shift.md) on a finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) $[k]$ consists of all [functions](function-split.md) $z:\mathbb Z\to[k]$, with the [product topology](product-topology.md) and the [left shift](left-shift.md). It is a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md). A [compatible metric](compatible-metric.md) is

$$
d(z,w)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}2^{-|j|-2}\mathbf1_{\{z(j)\ne w(j)\}}.
$$

Agreement on increasingly large finite coordinate sets is equivalent to convergence in this topology. The [metric](metric.md) above is compatible but is not invariant under the [left shift](left-shift.md).
