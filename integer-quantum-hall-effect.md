# Integer quantum Hall effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md)

On an ideal integer plateau, the magnitude of transverse [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) is $Ne^2/h$, where $N$ is a positive integer, while the longitudinal [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) vanishes. The measured transverse [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) is then $h/(Ne^2)$. Both statements matter: inverting a [conductivity tensor](conductivity-tensor.md) mixes its longitudinal and transverse components, as the [quantum Hall conductivity tensor inversion](quantum-hall-conductivity-tensor-inversion.md) shows.
