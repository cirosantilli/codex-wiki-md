# Successive quotients of principal-unit groups

↑ **Parent:** [Higher principal-unit group](higher-principal-unit-group.md)

For $r\geq1$, the map $1+\pi^r a\mapsto\bar a$ is a surjective group homomorphism to the additive [residue field](residue-field.md) with kernel $U_{r+1}$. The extra product term is divisible by $\pi^{r+1}$. Thus each quotient has cardinality $|k|$, a useful count when splitting the [unit group](unit-group.md) by roots of unity.
