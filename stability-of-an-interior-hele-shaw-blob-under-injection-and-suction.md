# Stability of an interior Hele-Shaw blob under injection and suction

↑ **Parent:** [Hele-Shaw flow](hele-shaw-flow.md)

For viscous fluid inside a circular [fluid free boundary](fluid-free-boundary.md) between parallel plates of separation $h$, the depth-averaged [Hele-Shaw flow](hele-shaw-flow.md) satisfies $\bar u=-K\nabla p$, $K=h^2/(12\mu)$. With volume injection rate $Q$, $\bar u_r=Q/(2\pi hr)$ and $R^2=R_0^2+Qt/(\pi h)$. Neglecting [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) and exterior viscosity, pressure is constant at the interface. Perturb its radius by $a_n\cos n\theta$ and solve the interior [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) with a regular pressure term proportional to $r^n\cos n\theta$. The linearized pressure boundary condition gives $\delta p(R)=a_nQ/(2\pi hKR)$, hence velocity perturbation $-na_nQ/(2\pi hR^2)$. The displaced base velocity adds another $-a_nQ/(2\pi hR^2)$, proving the displayed growth law. Injection stabilizes the circular blob; suction destabilizes it. An exact radial solution can therefore exist while giving a fragile experimental prediction. These signs concern viscous fluid inside the blob, rather than the opposite geometry of a low-viscosity injected bubble.
