# Two-stage stopping under linear drag and dry friction

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md)

For a body of mass $M$ initially at speed $U$, suppose the total resistance is [linear drag](linear-drag.md) $\lambda u$ until speed $u_*>0$, followed by [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) $\mu Mg$ until rest. The first stage solves $M\dot u=-\lambda u$, giving duration $(M/\lambda)\log(U/u_*)$ and distance $(M/\lambda)(U-u_*)$. Constant deceleration in the second stage gives duration $u_*/(\mu g)$ and distance $u_*^2/(2\mu g)$. The stopping distance is finite because the switch removes the infinite exponential stopping time of pure linear drag.
