# Tidal acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md)

The relative acceleration of neighboring freely falling bodies is measured by [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md), comparing their infinitesimal separation $\xi$ in a [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) frame along a reference [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) with tangent $U$. With $R^a{}_{bcd}$ defined by the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) commutator, it has the displayed form. In the [Newtonian limit](newtonian-limit.md) the corresponding [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) is the spatial gradient of the [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). A single freely falling body can remove local gravitational acceleration by its frame choice, whereas relative tidal acceleration measures [curvature](curvature.md).
