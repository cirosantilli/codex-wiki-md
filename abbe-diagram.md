# Abbe diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Abbe number](abbe-number.md)

An Abbe diagram plots [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n_d$ against [Abbe number](abbe-number.md) $V_d$ to show available [optical glass](optical-glass.md) families. Many diagrams put increasing $V_d$ to the left. Ordinary [crown glasses](crown-glass-optics.md) have larger $V_d$ than ordinary [flint glasses](flint-glass.md), while modern compositions occupy a broader region.
