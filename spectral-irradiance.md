# Spectral irradiance

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiance](irradiance.md)

Incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) per unit area and unit [wavelength](wavelength.md) or [frequency](frequency.md). Integrating $E_\lambda$ over a wavelength interval gives the contribution to [irradiance](irradiance.md) in that interval. Conversion between $E_\lambda$ and $E_\nu$ requires the absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) associated with $\nu=c/\lambda$, as for [spectral radiance](spectral-radiance.md).
