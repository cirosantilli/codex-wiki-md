# Irregularity of an algebraic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic surface](algebraic-surface.md)

The irregularity of a smooth projective complex surface is

$$
q(X)=h^1(X,\mathcal O_X)=h^0(X,\Omega_X^1).
$$
