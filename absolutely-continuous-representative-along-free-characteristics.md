# Absolutely continuous representative along free characteristics

↑ **Parent:** [Free transport equation](free-transport-equation.md)

A [distributional weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $\partial_tf+v\cdot\nabla_xf=g$, with locally integrable $f,g$, becomes $\partial_tF=G$ after the shear $F(t,x,v)=f(t,x+tv,v)$. For almost every characteristic label, there is an [absolutely continuous function](absolutely-continuous-function.md) representing $F$, and its increments are the [integrals](integral.md) of $G$ for every pair of times. Arbitrary modifications on time slices can destroy this every-time identity without changing the distributional solution.
