# Bipartite binary quadratic optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Binary quadratic optimization](binary-quadratic-optimization.md)

For a real rectangular [matrix](matrix.md) $S$, maximize $p^TSq$ over separate sign choices for $p$ and $q$. With $x=(p,q)$, the symmetric block [matrix](matrix.md) $A=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}0&S\\S^T&0\end{pmatrix}$ satisfies $x^TAx=p^TSq$. Its zero diagonal blocks allow the [Krivine rounding scheme](krivine-rounding-scheme.md) to linearize the cross-block expected objective.
