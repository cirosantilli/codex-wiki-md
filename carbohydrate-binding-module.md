# Carbohydrate-binding module

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)

A [carbohydrate-binding module](carbohydrate-binding-module.md) targets an associated [protein](protein-split.md) to a carbohydrate substrate. Surface enrichment can help insoluble-substrate catalysis, but binding alone does not establish productive access.
