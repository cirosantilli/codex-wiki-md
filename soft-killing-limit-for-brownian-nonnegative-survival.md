# Soft killing limit for Brownian nonnegative survival

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kac formula with a bounded potential](feynman-kac-formula-with-a-bounded-potential.md)

Let $\phi$ be continuous, nonnegative, zero on $[0,\infty)$, and strictly positive on $(-\infty,0)$. By continuity of Brownian paths, its occupation integral vanishes exactly on nonnegative paths. [Dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives the displayed limit. The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) makes it $1-2\mathbb P_x(B_t\leq0)$ for $x\geq0$ and zero for $x<0$. In particular the limit at $x=0$ is zero for every positive time. The choice $\phi(x)=\min(1,\max(0,-x))$ is a bounded soft killing potential.
