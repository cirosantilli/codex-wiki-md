<h1 id="nonlinear-alfven-wave">Nonlinear Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

A finite-amplitude ideal-MHD Alfvén wave can rotate the transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) at constant magnitude, with constant [mass density](density.md), [pressure](pressure.md) and longitudinal components. Its transverse velocity obeys $\mathbf u_\perp=\mathbf C-s\mathbf B_\perp/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$ and its speed is constant along the wave. The constant [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) prevents compressive acceleration.
