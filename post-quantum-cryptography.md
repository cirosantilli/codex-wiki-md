# Post-quantum cryptography

↑ **Parent:** [Cryptography](cryptography.md)

[Cryptography](cryptography.md) intended to remain secure against adversaries with quantum computers. It includes classical algorithms based on problems other than the [integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) and [discrete logarithm](discrete-logarithm-problem.md) problems efficiently treated by [Shor's algorithm](shor-s-algorithm.md). A candidate's security still depends on its precise construction, parameters and known attacks; the label alone is not a proof. It is distinct from requiring quantum hardware for [quantum key distribution](quantum-key-distribution.md).
