# Sharpness of the linear growth bound for integrable observables

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth bound for integrable observables](linear-growth-bound-for-integrable-observables.md)

For every $0<a<1$, choose $1<p<1/a$. On the [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md) over independent uniform coordinates in $(0,1)$, the observable $f(x)=x_0^{-1/p}$ is integrable, but $\mu(f(T^nx)>n^a)=n^{-ap}$. The events are independent and their probabilities have divergent sum, so the [Borel-Cantelli lemma](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) implies that $f(T^nx)/n^a>1$ infinitely often [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). No exponent below one gives a universal bound for all integrable observables.
