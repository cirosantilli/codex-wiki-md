# Buoyancy-driven turbulent density diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Eddy diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md)

In a narrow tube with unstable mean density gradient $q=\partial_z\bar\rho>0$, width-limited turbulent eddies have velocity scale $u_*\sim W\sqrt{gq/\rho_0}$. A mixing-length diffusivity gives downward density flux $F_\rho=KW^2\sqrt{g/\rho_0}\,q^{3/2}$ per unit area. With upward coordinate $z$, conservation is $\bar\rho_t=\partial_zF_\rho$ and $q_t=KW^2\sqrt{g/\rho_0}\,\partial_z^2(q^{3/2})$. The closure presupposes established high-[Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) turbulence and requires boundary fluxes and finite available potential energy to be respected.
