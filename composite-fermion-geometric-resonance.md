# Composite-fermion geometric resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Composite fermion](composite-fermion.md)

A fully spin-polarized sea of [composite fermions](composite-fermion.md) has a [Fermi wavevector](fermi-wavevector.md) $\sqrt{4\pi n_s}$. Near half filling its semiclassical orbit [radius](radius.md) depends on the residual effective field, not the full applied field. Matching an orbit diameter to a patterned [length](length.md) can produce transport [resonances](resonance.md) outside an incompressible fractional Hall state. Multiple pattern periods and random/unpatterned controls test that geometric interpretation. Density inhomogeneity and a soft potential can shift or broaden the response.[pp.3598–3600, Eq.(1) and Figs.1–3](composite-fermions-in-periodic-and-random-antidot-lattices-by-j-h-smet-and-others.md)
