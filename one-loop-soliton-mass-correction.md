# One-loop soliton mass correction

↑ **Parent:** [Semiclassical soliton mass](semiclassical-soliton-mass.md)

In a common finite-volume [regularization in quantum field theory](regularization-in-quantum-field-theory.md), the oscillator contribution is $\Delta M_{\rm osc}=\tfrac12\sum_n(\Omega_n-\Omega_n^{(0)})$, including every discrete and continuum-derived mode and treating translation as a [collective coordinate](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md). Physical frequencies are $\Omega_n=m\omega_n$ when coordinates are measured in units of $m^{-1}$. A [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md) is added only afterwards. The vacuum subtraction removes the extensive [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) but can leave an [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md).
