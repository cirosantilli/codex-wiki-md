# Relative speed retention in an oblique collision

↑ **Parent:** [Coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md)

For a two-particle [collision](collision.md) conserving total [momentum](momentum-split.md), the outgoing [speeds](speed.md) in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) are multiplied by the same $q$. The relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is multiplied by $q^2$. For a smooth [oblique collision](oblique-collision.md) with normal [coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md) $e_n$ and incident relative [velocity](velocity.md) inclined by $\chi$ to the contact normal, unchanged tangential relative motion gives $q^2=e_n^2\cos^2\chi+\sin^2\chi$. Thus $q=e_n$ is not a general oblique-impact identity: $e_n=1/2$ and $\chi=\pi/4$ give $q=\sqrt{5/8}$. Sticking requires all relative motion to vanish, not merely its normal component.
