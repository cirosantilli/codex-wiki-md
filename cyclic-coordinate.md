# Cyclic coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_coordinate)

A generalized coordinate absent from the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is cyclic. Its conjugate [generalized momentum](generalized-momentum.md) is conserved by the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md).
