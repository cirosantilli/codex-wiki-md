# Free monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_monoid)

The free monoid on an alphabet consists of all finite words in its letters, including the empty word, under concatenation. Distinct words represent distinct elements.
