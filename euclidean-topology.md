# Euclidean topology

↑ **Parent:** [Metric topology](metric-topology.md)

The Euclidean topology on $\mathbb R^n$ is the [metric topology](metric-topology.md) induced by the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md). A [subset](subset.md) is [open](open-set.md) exactly when each of its points has an [open ball](open-ball.md) contained in it. It agrees with the finite [product topology](product-topology.md) on $n$ copies of the [real line](real-line.md): the inequalities $\|x\|_\infty\leq\|x\|_2\leq\sqrt n\|x\|_\infty$ put a coordinate box inside every sufficiently small ball and a ball inside every sufficiently small coordinate box. The case $n=0$ is the one-point [topological space](topological-space.md).
