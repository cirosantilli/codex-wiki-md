# Displaced-oscillator Franck-Condon progression

↑ **Parent:** [Franck-Condon factor](franck-condon-factor.md)

For equal-frequency harmonic ground and excited molecular potentials displaced by $\Delta Q$, the low-temperature vibrational overlap has the Poisson weights in the title, with $S=M\omega_v(\Delta Q)^2/(2\hbar)$. Optical lines occur at $E_{00}+\nu\hbar\omega_v$, apart from broadening and slowly varying electronic or optical prefactors. The normalized weights sum to one and have mean vibrational excitation $S$. Unequal frequencies, several vibrations, hot initial states and intermolecular coupling modify this simple [Franck-Condon principle](franck-condon-principle.md) model.
