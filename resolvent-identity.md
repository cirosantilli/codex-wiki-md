# Resolvent identity

↑ **Parent:** [Resolvent of an element](resolvent-of-an-element.md)

For two points $z,w$ in the resolvent set of an operator $A$,

$$
R(z,A)-R(w,A)=(w-z)R(z,A)R(w,A).
$$

In particular, the [resolvent operator](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) is differentiable and $\partial_zR(z,A)=-R(z,A)^2$.
