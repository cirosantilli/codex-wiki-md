# Negative-square exclusion under full sphere regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Regularity of a J-holomorphic curve](regularity-of-a-j-holomorphic-curve.md)

If every nonconstant [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md) under consideration is regular, no such sphere in a four-dimensional target has class $A$ with $A^2\leq-2$. For a [simple J-holomorphic sphere](simple-j-holomorphic-sphere.md), the [adjunction inequality for a simple J-holomorphic sphere](adjunction-inequality-for-a-simple-j-holomorphic-sphere.md) makes the predicted moduli dimension negative. A degree-$m$ multiple cover of class $B$ has $B^2\leq-1$ and $c_1(B)\leq1$, so its predicted real dimension is at most $2m-2$. The [rational covering maps of the projective line](rational-covering-maps-of-the-projective-line.md) already form a family of dimension $4m-4>2m-2$, contradicting regularity. The full regularity hypothesis matters: multiple covers of exceptional minus-one spheres violate the conclusion under a simple-only regularity assumption.
