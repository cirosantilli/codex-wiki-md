# Flag manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_manifold)

For $0<d_1<\cdots<d_m<n$, a flag is a chain of complex subspaces $L_1\subset\cdots\subset L_m\subset V$ of the given dimensions. Successively choosing the quotient subspaces gives complex dimension $\sum_{i=1}^m(d_i-d_{i-1})(n-d_i)$ with $d_0=0$. Adapted bases identify it with a homogeneous space of the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md), with stabilizer an appropriate block upper-triangular subgroup. The [twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md) uses $F_{1,2}(\mathbb C^4)$.
