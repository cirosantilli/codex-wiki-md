# Congruence lattice proof of the prime sum of two squares

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of two squares theorem](sum-of-two-squares-theorem.md)

If $u^2\equiv-1\pmod p$, this lattice has basis $(1,u),(0,p)$ and covolume $p$. The [Minkowski convex body theorem](minkowski-s-theorem.md) applied to a disk of squared radius strictly between $4p/\pi$ and $2p$ gives a nonzero lattice vector with $0<a^2+b^2<2p$. The congruence makes its squared length divisible by $p$, forcing $a^2+b^2=p$. The strict radius bounds avoid assuming the critical-volume form of Minkowski's theorem.
