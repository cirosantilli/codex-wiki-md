# Sill of a semivariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Semivariogram](semivariogram.md)

The sill is the large-distance level of a [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) when that limit exists. For [covariance](covariance.md) tending to zero, the sill is the total marginal [variance](variance-split.md); a nugget plus a structured component has total sill $\tau^2+\sigma^2$. A nonzero constant [covariance](covariance.md) component is invisible to the [semivariogram](semivariogram.md).
