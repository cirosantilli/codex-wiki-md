# Gaussian conditional variance from precision

↑ **Parent:** [Precision matrix](precision-matrix.md)

For a nonsingular [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with [precision matrix](precision-matrix.md) $\Omega$, fixing all coordinates except $X_i$ leaves a density proportional to $\exp[-\Omega_{ii}(x_i-m_i)^2/2]$ after completing the square. Thus its [conditional variance](conditional-variance.md) is the reciprocal diagonal precision entry. This is conditioning on every other coordinate, not the marginal [variance](variance-split.md) $(\Omega^{-1})_{ii}$.
