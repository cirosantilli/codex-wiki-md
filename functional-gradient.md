# Functional gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient](gradient.md)

For a differentiable real-valued functional $F$ on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $\mathcal H$, its functional gradient satisfies $DF(g)[h]=\langle\nabla_{\mathcal H}F(g),h\rangle_{\mathcal H}$ for every $h\in\mathcal H$. A bounded linear derivative has a unique such representative by the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md). The [inner product](inner-product-split.md) matters: a formal variational derivative need not equal the functional gradient in another [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). For $F(g)=\frac12\sum_i(y_i-g(x_i))^2$ in a [Reproducing kernel Hilbert space](reproducing-kernel-hilbert-space.md), the [reproducing property](reproducing-property.md) gives $\nabla_{\mathcal H}F(g)=\sum_i(g(x_i)-y_i)k(x_i,\cdot)$. Substitution in the defining [inner product](inner-product-split.md) proves this formula directly.
