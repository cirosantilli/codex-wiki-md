# Relic cooling across muon annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md)

A relativistic relic that decouples between pion and muon annihilation keeps $aT_H$ constant. The coupled bath initially contains photons, electron-positron pairs, muon-antimuon pairs and three neutrino-antineutrino species, with entropy degrees of freedom $57/4$. After muon annihilation this is $43/4$, giving the displayed temperature ratio. After [neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md), electron-positron annihilation heats photons by $(11/4)^{1/3}$, so the final relic-to-photon ratio becomes $(172/627)^{1/3}$. This counting assumes the species are relativistic when counted and treats annihilations and decoupling as sharp equilibrium transitions.
