<h1 id="essential-closedness-of-mumford-shah-jump-sets">Essential closedness of Mumford–Shah jump sets</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md)

A [minimizer](global-minimizer.md) of the [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md) with bounded [image signal](image-signal.md) data has an essentially closed jump set: replacing $J_u$ by its relative closure adds no $(n-1)$-dimensional [measure](measure.md). Its complement supports a Sobolev representative. This regularity theorem connects a relaxed [SBV space](special-bounded-variation-space.md) [minimizer](global-minimizer.md) to the classical closed-edge formulation; it is not a property of every special bounded-variation function.
