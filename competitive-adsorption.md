# Competitive adsorption

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md)

[Competitive adsorption](competitive-adsorption.md) occurs when different [chemical species](chemical-species.md) seek the same [adsorption sites](adsorption-site.md). For intact [molecules](molecule-split.md) $A$ and $B$, retain identical [adsorption sites](adsorption-site.md) and no interactions, but replace the vacant fraction by $1-\theta_A-\theta_B$. The same equilibrium balance gives

$$
\theta_A=\frac{b_AP_A}{1+b_AP_A+b_BP_B},\qquad
\theta_B=\frac{b_BP_B}{1+b_AP_A+b_BP_B}.
$$

Here $P_A$ and $P_B$ are [partial pressures](partial-pressure.md), not total [pressure](pressure-split.md). Increasing $P_B$ can reduce $\theta_A$ at unchanged $P_A$: replacing $P_A$ by total [pressure](pressure-split.md) in a single-species [Langmuir isotherm](langmuir-adsorption-model.md) loses this competition.
