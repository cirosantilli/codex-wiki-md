# H-field

↑ **Parent:** [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md)

The H-field is the three-form field strength of a [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md). It obeys the Bianchi identity $dH=0$ and contributes quadratically to the leading string background equations.
