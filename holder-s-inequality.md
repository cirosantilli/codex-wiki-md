<h1 id="holder-s-inequality">Hölder's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lp norm](lp-norm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hölder's_inequality)

For conjugate exponents $p,q\in[1,\infty]$, [Hölder's inequality](holder-s-inequality.md) states $\lVert fg\rVert_1\leq\lVert f\rVert_p\lVert g\rVert_q$. For finite vectors, the case $p=1$, $q=\infty$ is $|x^Ty|\leq\lVert x\rVert_1\lVert y\rVert_\infty$.
