# Uniform high-degree vanishing on a smooth projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md)

For a fixed [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), one degree bound works for all [divisors on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md), rather than just powers of one ample divisor. Choose a point $P$. The complement of $P$ is affine, so the surjective transition maps on the finite-dimensional groups $H^1(C,\mathcal F(nP))$ eventually kill every class. The [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) gives $h^0(\mathcal O(A))\ge\deg A+1-g$. Every divisor of sufficiently large degree is therefore linearly equivalent to $n_0P+E$ with $E$ effective. The point-supported quotient for adding $E$ transfers vanishing to that divisor. Torsion is first removed so that the relevant inclusions are injections of [locally free sheaves](locally-free-sheaf.md).
