# Agarose gel electrophoresis

↑ **Parent:** [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md)

[Agarose gel electrophoresis](agarose-gel-electrophoresis.md) uses a porous [agarose](agarose.md) [polymer](polymer-split.md) network. For linear double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) within a suitable separation range, shorter fragments generally move farther in a fixed run. Raising the [agarose](agarose.md) concentration changes the mesh and the range of sizes that can be resolved.[Step 1, Selection and gel preparation; Recommended percentage of agarose gels for separation of DNA fragments](five-main-nucleic-acid-gel-electrophoresis-steps-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md) A compact circular [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) need not migrate like linear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) of the same length: geometry affects passage through the mesh.[Effects of conformation, Figure 1: nicked circular, linear and supercoiled forms of the same DNA](five-considerations-for-the-nucleic-acid-gel-electrophoresis-process-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)

A concrete laboratory example is Addgene's documented [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) separation protocol. Its standard 1% gel contains 1 g of [agarose](agarose.md) in 100 mL of [TAE buffer](tae-buffer.md). Dissolved [agarose](agarose.md) sets in a casting tray around a comb; removing the comb leaves sample wells. The gel is covered with the same [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) in a box connected to a voltage source. Loading buffer contains [glycerol](glycerol.md) to increase sample [density](density.md) and a tracking dye to show run progress. A [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md) occupies a separate lane beside the samples. With wells near the negative [cathode](cathode.md), [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) migrates toward the positive [anode](anode.md). After separation, bound stain makes the [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) visible by [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) under a [UV transilluminator](uv-transilluminator.md). The [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md) supplies the size reference; the tracking dye monitors migration but does not identify sample [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).[Equipment; Pouring a Standard 1% Agarose Gel, steps 1–7; Loading Samples and Running an Agarose Gel, steps 1–9; Analyzing Your Gel](agarose-gel-electrophoresis-by-addgene.md)

In the manufacturer's controlled comparison, the same [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md) runs in 1%, 2%, and 3% [agarose](agarose.md) gels with the same run time and other stated conditions. The changed [gel band](gel-band.md) spacing shows why a calibration belongs to its particular gel conditions, rather than establishing a universal distance law.[Step 1, Figure 3 and caption: same DNA ladder at three agarose percentages under the same conditions, including run time](five-main-nucleic-acid-gel-electrophoresis-steps-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)

In interpreting an unknown sample, a blank lane tests for detectable background or carryover in that blank, while the [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md) checks migration and detection of its known fragments. An empty sample lane cannot by itself distinguish absent [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from unsuccessful loading or insufficient detection. These controls answer different questions; neither makes matching migration a proof of matching [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md).
