# Weak-field photon-orbit validity

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized Schwarzschild metric](linearized-schwarzschild-metric.md)

The first-order null-orbit equation $(u')^2=r_S^2E^2/h^2-u^2(1-u)$ formally has a circular solution at $u=2/3$, with $|h/E|=3\sqrt3r_S/2$. Its second-order equation gives a perturbation $\delta u''=\delta u$, hence instability. However, $u=2/3$ is outside the weak-field expansion. The exact [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has a corresponding unstable [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md), but that physical result needs the exact geometry; extrapolating the linearized metric is not a controlled proof.
