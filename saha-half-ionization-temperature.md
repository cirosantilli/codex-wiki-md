# Saha half-ionization temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination temperature](recombination-temperature.md)

With the rounded [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md) coefficient, the condition $X_e=1/2$ gives the displayed equation for $y=\mathcal B/T$. Its low-temperature root is about $42$, hence $T\simeq0.32$ eV for [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) binding energy $13.6$ eV. Dropping the slowly varying logarithm gives the rough leading-log estimate $T\sim0.4$ eV. This equilibrium convention is distinct from [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md) and from a full kinetic recombination calculation.
