# Harmonic power of a square wave

↑ **Parent:** [Square wave](square-wave.md)

A zero-mean [square wave](square-wave.md) of [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $G$ has total [mean-square signal power](signal-power.md) $G^2$. Each positive odd [Fourier harmonic](fourier-harmonic.md) and its negative-frequency partner contribute $8G^2/(\pi^2r^2)$, by its exact [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md). The fundamental pair therefore accounts for $8/\pi^2$ of the total. The sum of contributions above harmonic index $R$ decays on the order of $R^{-1}$, although the discontinuous waveform still requires arbitrarily high harmonics for exact reconstruction.
