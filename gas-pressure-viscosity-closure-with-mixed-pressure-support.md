# Gas-pressure viscosity closure with mixed pressure support

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha disk](alpha-disk.md)

Let $\mathcal R=k/(\mu_m m_H)$, take constant [opacity](opacity.md) $\kappa$, and prescribe the [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu=\alpha p_g/\Omega=\alpha\mathcal R\rho T/\Omega$ in a [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md). The pressure supporting the disk may contain both [gas pressure](gas-pressure.md) and [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md). Combining [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) and [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) gives $\partial_m^2T^4+A\Omega T=0$ in the [vertical mass coordinate of a disk](vertical-mass-coordinate-of-a-disk.md), where $A=27\alpha\kappa\mathcal R/(16\sigma)$. Set $\zeta=2m/\Sigma$, $T=T_*t(\zeta)$ and $T_*^3=A\Omega\Sigma^2/4$. With a [radiative-zero disk surface](radiative-zero-disk-surface.md), $t$ satisfies $(t^4)''+t=0$, $t'(0)=t(1)=0$. Writing $I_t=\int_0^1t\,d\zeta$, the [density-weighted mean kinematic viscosity](density-weighted-mean-kinematic-viscosity.md) is $\bar\nu=\alpha\mathcal R T_*I_t/\Omega$, giving the displayed formula. This calculation does not assume gas-pressure domination: the closure depends on gas pressure while hydrostatic support can be mixed.
