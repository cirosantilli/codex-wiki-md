# Long-root subsystem

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The roots of maximal length in a [root system](root-system.md) form a root subsystem. A chamber for the original system lies in a unique chamber for this long-root subsystem, because its interior avoids every long-root reflecting hyperplane.
