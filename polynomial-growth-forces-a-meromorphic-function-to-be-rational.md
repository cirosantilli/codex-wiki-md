# Polynomial growth forces a meromorphic function to be rational

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)

A [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the plane with $|f(z)|\leq C|z|^n$ outside a disc has no poles there and only finitely many poles inside. Subtract their finite [principal parts](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) to obtain an [entire function](entire-function.md). For $n\geq0$, [Cauchy estimates](cauchy-estimate.md) make every derivative of order above $n$ vanish, so the remainder is a polynomial. For $n<0$ the remainder tends to zero at infinity and is zero by [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md). Adding back the principal parts gives a [rational function](rational-function.md) in either case.
