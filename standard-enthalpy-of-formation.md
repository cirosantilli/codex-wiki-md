# Standard enthalpy of formation

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemistry](thermochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_enthalpy_of_formation)

The [standard enthalpy of formation](standard-enthalpy-of-formation.md) is the [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) change for forming one [mole](mole-unit.md) of a specified [chemical substance](chemical-substance.md) from its [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) in their reference states at the stated [temperature](temperature.md). For [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) at $298.15\,\mathrm K$, those reactant states are [solid](solid.md) [sodium](sodium.md) and diatomic gaseous [chlorine](chlorine.md). [Standard state](standard-state.md) specifies conditions; it does not by itself select a [temperature](temperature.md).
