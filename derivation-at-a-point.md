# Derivation at a point

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent vector](tangent-vector.md)

At $p$ on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), a derivation at that point is a real-linear map $v:C_p^\infty(M)\to\mathbb R$ on [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) of smooth functions with $v(fh)=f(p)v(h)+h(p)v(f)$. It defines a [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md). The local factorization $f-f(p)=\sum_i(x^i-x^i(p))f_i$ gives $v(f)=\sum_i v(x^i)\partial_i f(p)$, so the coordinate derivations form a basis of the [tangent space](tangent-space.md).
