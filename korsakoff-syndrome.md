# Korsakoff syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Diencephalic amnesia](diencephalic-amnesia.md)

A severe amnesic syndrome associated with thiamine deficiency, often but not exclusively in the context of alcohol misuse. Its eponym refers to [Sergei Korsakoff](sergei-korsakoff.md). Co-occurring damage varies across patients, making comparison with non-amnesic pathological controls valuable.
