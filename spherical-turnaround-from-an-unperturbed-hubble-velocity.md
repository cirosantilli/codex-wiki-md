# Spherical turnaround from an unperturbed Hubble velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md)

In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), give an initially spherical, pressureless region total mass $M$ and excess mass $0<\delta M\ll M$, while holding its initial expansion velocity at the unperturbed [Hubble flow](hubble-flow.md) value at physical radius $r_0$. Before [shell crossing](shell-crossing.md), the outer shell has $\dot r_0^2=2G(M-\delta M)/r_0$ and conserved [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) $-G\delta M/r_0$. The [shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) and [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) give $\dot r^2=2GM/r-2G\delta M/r_0$, so its [cosmological turnaround](cosmological-turnaround.md) radius is $Mr_0/\delta M$. With $A=2GM$, $B=2G\delta M/r_0$ and $\epsilon=\delta M/M$, the elapsed [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md) is exactly

$$
\tau=\frac{A}{B^{3/2}}\left[\frac\pi2-\arcsin\sqrt\epsilon+\sqrt{\epsilon(1-\epsilon)}\right].
$$

Replacing the square bracket by $\pi/2$ gives the displayed approximation, with relative overestimate $4\epsilon^{3/2}/(3\pi)+O(\epsilon^{5/2})$. Holding the initial expansion velocity fixed is an essential condition: a pure growing-mode [linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) has an initial peculiar velocity as well as an excess mass, changing the binding energy at the same order.
