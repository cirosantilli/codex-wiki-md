# Dust orbit released from a circular parent ring

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md)

If release imparts no velocity kick, a grain's initial [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) under reduced stellar attraction has $q=r_b$, $Q=r_b/(1-2\beta)$, and $e=\beta/(1-\beta)$ for $0\leq\beta<1/2$. Its [pericentre distance](pericentre-distance.md) remains at the [dust birth ring](dust-birth-ring.md), while its [apocentre distance](apocentre-distance.md) grows with the [radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md).
