# Folium of Descartes

↑ **Parent:** [Affine algebraic curve](affine-algebraic-curve.md)

For nonzero real $a$, the [folium of Descartes](folium-of-descartes.md) is the [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) $x^3+y^3=3axy$, with [rational parametrization of an algebraic curve](rational-parametrization-of-an-algebraic-curve.md)

$$
x=\frac{3at}{1+t^3},\qquad y=\frac{3at^2}{1+t^3},\qquad t\ne-1.
$$

It is symmetric about $y=x$ and has a node at the origin, with the coordinate axes as tangents. For $a>0$, $0\leq t<\infty$ traces a loop in the first quadrant, returning to the origin as $t\to\infty$. Its enclosed [area](surface-area.md), by [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md), is

$$
\frac12\int_0^\infty(xy'-yx')\,dt
=\frac{9a^2}{2}\int_0^\infty\frac{t^2\,dt}{(1+t^3)^2}=\frac{3a^2}{2}.
$$

The other branches approach the [asymptote](asymptote.md) $x+y+a=0$ as $t\to-1$. Negative $a$ rotates the positive-$a$ picture through $\pi$.
