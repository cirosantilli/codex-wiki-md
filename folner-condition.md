<h1 id="folner-condition">Følner condition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) $G$ with finite generating set $S$ satisfies the Følner condition when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a nonempty finite subset $F\subseteq G$ such that

$$
\frac{|sF\mathbin\triangle F|}{|F|}<\varepsilon
\qquad(s\in S).
$$

The condition is independent of the finite generating set.
