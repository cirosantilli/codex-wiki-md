# Complex conjugation of differential-form type

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md)

[Complex conjugation](complex-conjugate.md) interchanges the bidegrees of complex differential forms:

$$
\overline{\Omega^{p,q}(X)}=\Omega^{q,p}(X).
$$

It also interchanges the two type components of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md), so $\bar\partial\bar\alpha=\overline{\partial\alpha}$ and $\partial\bar\alpha=\overline{\bar\partial\alpha}$.
