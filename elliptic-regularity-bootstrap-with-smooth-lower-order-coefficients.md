# Elliptic regularity bootstrap with smooth lower-order coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md)

Once an $L^2$ distributional solution of $\Delta u=\operatorname{div}(bu)+cu+f$ gains $H^1$ regularity, expand the divergence term. Smooth multiplication makes the right side belong to $H^{m-1}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ whenever $u\in H^m_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Interior Poisson regularity gives $H^{m+1}_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Iteration and the [Sobolev embedding theorem](sobolev-embedding-theorem.md) produce a smooth representative.
