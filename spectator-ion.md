# Spectator ion

↑ **Parent:** [Net ionic equation](net-ionic-equation.md)

A [spectator ion](spectator-ion.md) cancels from the chosen [net ionic equation](net-ionic-equation.md) because its net inventory is unchanged. This label does not make it physically irrelevant: dissolved [spectator ions](spectator-ion.md) contribute to [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md), which affects [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md). Their [concentrations](concentration.md) can also change during dilution even though their [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) remains fixed.
