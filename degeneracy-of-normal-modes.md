# Degeneracy of normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Distinct linearly independent [normal modes](normal-mode.md) are degenerate if they have the same [eigenfrequency](eigenfrequency.md). For $K\mathbf u=\omega^2M\mathbf u$, their [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) span a common [eigenspace](eigenspace.md); linear combinations in that space are also modes with the same [frequency](frequency.md). A [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) preserving both quadratic [energies](energy-split.md) maps this space into itself. Higher-dimensional irreducible [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) representations can enforce degeneracy, while perturbations breaking that [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) can split it. Degeneracies can also occur accidentally without such [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) protection.
