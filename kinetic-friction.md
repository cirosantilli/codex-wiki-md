# Kinetic friction

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md)

The Coulomb sliding-friction model gives a tangential [force](force.md) of magnitude $\mu N$, opposite the relative slip velocity at contact, where $N$ is the [normal force](normal-force.md). The transition to [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) occurs when contact slip vanishes; the sliding law is then replaced by the appropriate static contact condition.
