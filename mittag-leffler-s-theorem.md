<h1 id="mittag-leffler-s-theorem">Mittag-Leffler's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mittag-Leffler's_theorem)

[Mittag-Leffler's theorem](mittag-leffler-s-theorem.md) prescribes the [principal parts](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md). Given an [open subset](open-set.md) $D$ of the complex plane, a subset $A$ having no accumulation point in $D$, and a finite sum of negative powers $P_a(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{m_a}c_{a,j}(z-a)^{-j}$ for each $a\in A$, there is a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on $D$ with exactly these [principal parts](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) and no other [poles](pole.md). The difference of any two such [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md) is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on $D$, because their [principal parts](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) cancel at every prescribed [pole](pole.md).
