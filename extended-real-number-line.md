# Extended real number line

↑ **Parent:** [Real number](real-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_real_number_line)

The extended real numbers adjoin $-\infty$ and $+\infty$ to $\mathbb R$. They preserve the [total order](total-order.md) but leave some arithmetic expressions, such as $+\infty-\infty$, undefined.
