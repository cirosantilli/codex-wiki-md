# Exponential void probabilities characterize a Poisson random measure

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md)

A [simple locally finite point measure](simple-locally-finite-point-measure.md) on $[0,\infty)$ with void [probability](probability.md) $e^{-\lambda(A)}$ on finite unions of bounded intervals is a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) with [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) intensity. Void-event intersections factor, hence the occupation indicators on disjoint cells are independent. The [dyadic occupancy approximation of a simple point measure](dyadic-occupancy-approximation-of-a-simple-point-measure.md) is binomial with rare-event [probability](probability.md) $1-e^{-2^{-n}}$ and converges to a Poisson count of mean the interval length. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) survives joint convergence for disjoint intervals. Extension of the mean [measure](measure.md) and the [Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure](laplace-functional-of-a-poisson-random-measure.md) to Borel functions identifies all Borel counts, not just intervals.
