# Directrix of a conic

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

A [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) is the fixed line in its focus-directrix description: distance to the [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) is a constant multiple of distance to this line. For a [parabola](parabola.md) the multiple is one. The [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) belongs to the geometry of the [conic section](conic-section.md), rather than being a tangent or an asymptote.
