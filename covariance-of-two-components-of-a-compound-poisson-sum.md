# Covariance of two components of a compound Poisson sum

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

Let $N\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\lambda)$ be [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $(Y_i,Z_i)$ with finite second [moments](moment.md). Conditioning on $N$ gives [covariance](covariance.md) $N\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Z_1)$ and [conditional expectations](conditional-expectation.md) $N\mathbb EY_1,N\mathbb EZ_1$. [Law of total covariance](law-of-total-covariance.md) and $\operatorname{Var}N=\lambda$ yield the formula. For per-claim [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md) payouts $Y=\min(X,M)$ and $Z=(X-M)_+$, it becomes $\lambda M\mathbb E[(X-M)_+]$. The insurer and reinsurer aggregates share their claim count and are generally dependent.
