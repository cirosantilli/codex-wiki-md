# Brackett series

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen spectral series](hydrogen-spectral-series.md)

The [Brackett series](brackett-series.md) consists of hydrogen [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) with final [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) four and initial [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) greater than four. The [Rydberg formula](rydberg-formula.md) gives $1/\lambda=R_H(1/16-1/n^2)$. Its infrared [wavelengths](wavelength.md) make these [recombination lines](recombination-line.md) less susceptible to [interstellar extinction](interstellar-extinction.md) than optical [Balmer series](balmer-series.md) lines; quantitative use requires the appropriate [effective recombination coefficient](effective-recombination-coefficient.md).
