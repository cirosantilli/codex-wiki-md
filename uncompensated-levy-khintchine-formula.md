<h1 id="uncompensated-levy-khintchine-formula">Uncompensated Lévy–Khintchine formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md)

If a [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md) satisfies $\int_{|y|\le1}|y|K(dy)<\infty$, the jump integral in the [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md) can be written without compensation: $\mathbb E e^{iuX_t}=e^{t\psi(u)}$, with the displayed exponent. Its integral is absolutely convergent because $|e^{iuy}-1|\le|u||y|$ near zero and is at most $2$ elsewhere. If the compensated formula uses drift $\gamma$, the uncompensated drift is $b=\gamma-\int_{|y|\le1}yK(dy)$. The Gaussian coefficient is still $\sigma^2\ge0$.
