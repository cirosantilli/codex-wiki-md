# Ballistic electron transport

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

Electronic propagation through a device region with few momentum-randomizing scattering events. The [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) should exceed the relevant trajectory length. Boundary specularity and aperture geometry can matter separately from the bulk mobility; [electron phase coherence](electron-phase-coherence.md) is an additional condition for interference.
