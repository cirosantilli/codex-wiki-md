# Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment

↑ **Parent:** [Youth anxiety treatment](youth-anxiety-treatment.md)

Caregivers may facilitate access and practice, receive information, change [accommodation](family-accommodation.md), or receive treatment for their own difficulties. These roles imply different mechanisms and should be compared against the same adequate [child](child.md) treatment when testing augmentation. Parent-only treatment and adding parent sessions to [child](child.md) treatment answer different questions.[Child and Adolescent Mental Health 24, 242–250; randomised parent-session allocation, primary remission and follow-up](a-randomized-controlled-trial-of-internet-delivered-cognitive-behaviour-therapy-for-adolescent-anxiety-disorders-in-a-routine-clinical-care-setting-with-and-without-parent-sessions-by-polly-waite-and-others.md)
