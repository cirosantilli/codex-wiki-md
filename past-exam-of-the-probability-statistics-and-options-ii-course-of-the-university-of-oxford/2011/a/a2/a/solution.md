<h1 id="a/a2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $f$ over $F$ is an extension field in which $f$ factors into linear factors and which is generated over $F$ by those roots. The generation requirement excludes unnecessary extra elements.

For existence, induct on the degree. An irreducible factor $p$ of $f$ gives a field $F_1=F[X]/(p)$, since an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) generates a maximal ideal in the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) ring over a field. The class $\alpha$ of $X$ is a root of $p$ and hence of $f$. By the [factor theorem](../../../../../factor-theorem.md), $f=(X-\alpha)g$ over $F_1$, with $\deg g=\deg f-1$. Induction gives a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $g$ over $F_1$. It contains all roots of $f$; taking the subfield generated by these roots over $F$ gives exactly a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $f$. The degree-one case is immediate. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{Every positive-degree polynomial over a field has a splitting field}.}
$$
