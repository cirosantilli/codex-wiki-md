The [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) of order six has two different [composition series](../../../../../composition-series.md):

$$
\boxed{1\lhd C_2\lhd C_6,\qquad1\lhd C_3\lhd C_6.}
$$

Their factor lists are $C_2,C_3$ and $C_3,C_2$ respectively; normality follows from commutativity.

The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](../../../../../jordan-holder-theorem.md) states that any two [composition series](../../../../../composition-series.md) of a group have the same length and the same multiset of composition factors up to isomorphism. Equivalently, the factors of one series can be reordered to match those of the other by group isomorphisms. The actual intermediate subgroups need not be the same.
