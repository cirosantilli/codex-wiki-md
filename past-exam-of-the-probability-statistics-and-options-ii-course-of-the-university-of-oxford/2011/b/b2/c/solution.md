<h1 id="b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $C_n=\langle g\rangle$ and take $H=\langle g^p\rangle$. Since $n=mp$, the order of $g^p$ is $m$, so $H\cong C_m$. All subgroups of a [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) are normal. The quotient is generated by $gH$ and has order $n/m=p$, giving

$$
\boxed{C_n/H\cong C_p.}
$$

A group of prime order has no nontrivial proper [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md). By the [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) correspondence theorem, no [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) lies strictly between $H$ and $C_n$. Thus $H$ is a maximal proper [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md).
