↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A square residue modulo $p$ is a congruence class represented by $x^2$ for some integer $x$. For the final all-primes solvability statement, zero is included as a square. The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) distinguishes the zero class from the nonzero square and nonsquare classes:

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=\begin{cases}0,&p\mid a,\\1,&p\nmid a\text{ and }a\equiv x^2\pmod p\text{ for some }x,\\-1,&a\text{ is not a square modulo }p.\end{cases}}
$$

When the term [quadratic residue](../../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) is reserved for nonzero classes, the zero case must be stated separately, as in this definition.
