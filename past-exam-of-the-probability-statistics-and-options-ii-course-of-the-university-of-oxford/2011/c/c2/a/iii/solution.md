↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For distinct odd primes $p,q$, [quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac pq\right)\left(\frac qp\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)/4}.}
$$

The usual supplementary laws are

$$
\left(\frac{-1}p\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2},\qquad
\left(\frac2p\right)=(-1)^{(p^2-1)/8}.
$$

The first supplement also follows immediately by inserting $a=-1$ into [Euler's criterion](../../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md).
