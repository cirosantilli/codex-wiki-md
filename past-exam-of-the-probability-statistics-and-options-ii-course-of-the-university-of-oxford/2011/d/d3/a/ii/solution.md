↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Let $k$ be the indicator of the positive integers. It is measurable, bounded by one and zero [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), so its [Lebesgue integral](../../../../../../lebesgue-integral.md) on $(0,\infty)$ is zero. Yet $k(n)=1$ for every positive integer $n$, so it does not tend to zero at infinity. [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md) is unchanged by modifications on a [null set](../../../../../../null-set.md), whereas a pointwise limit can change.
