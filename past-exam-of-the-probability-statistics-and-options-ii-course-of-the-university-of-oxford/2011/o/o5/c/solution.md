↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\mathrm{RSS}=\|Y-X\widehat\theta\|^2$. The usual unbiased error-[variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) estimator is

$$
\boxed{s^2=\frac{\mathrm{RSS}}{n-p}.}
$$

Indeed the residual is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of $\epsilon$ onto the $(n-p)$-dimensional complement of the design column space, giving $\mathrm{RSS}/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_{n-p}$ and expected RSS equal to $(n-p)\sigma^2$. The [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) instead divides RSS by $n$; the unbiased divisor above is the one used for the exact t-interval in part (d).
