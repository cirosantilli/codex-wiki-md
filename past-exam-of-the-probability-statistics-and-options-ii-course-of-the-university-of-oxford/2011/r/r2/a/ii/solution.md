<h1 id="r/r2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take a [complete graph](../../../../../../complete-graph.md) on vertices $a,b,c,d$ and add a fifth [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $v$ joined only to $a$ and $b$. The minimum degree is two, which is less than $5/2$. The only nonadjacent pairs are $(v,c)$ and $(v,d)$, and each has degree sum $2+3=5$. Thus Ore's condition holds even though the minimum-degree condition fails. For example,

$$
\boxed{v,a,c,d,b,v}
$$

is a [Hamiltonian cycle](../../../../../../hamilton-cycle.md) in this [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md).
