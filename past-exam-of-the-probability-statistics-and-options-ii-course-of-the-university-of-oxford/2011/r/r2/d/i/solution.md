↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Root the [tree](../../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) at any [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) and order vertices so every parent precedes its children. The root has $k$ colour choices. Each of the other $n-1$ vertices has exactly one earlier adjacent [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md), its parent, and therefore exactly $k-1$ choices. There are no other restrictions because a [tree](../../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) has no cycles and no extra edges between those branches. Multiplication yields

$$
\boxed{P_G(k)=k(k-1)^{n-1}.}
$$

The formula also includes the one-[vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) [tree](../../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md).
