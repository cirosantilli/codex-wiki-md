<h1 id="p/p3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The best [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) minimizes the weighted squared error among all polynomials of degree at most $n$:

$$
\boxed{\|u-p_n\|\leq\|u-q\|\quad\text{for every }q\in\Pi_n.}
$$

The given [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is positive definite on continuous functions despite the weight vanishing at zero: a continuous nonzero function is nonzero on an interval containing nonzero points, so its weighted square integral is strictly positive.
