<h1 id="p/p3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For odd $u$, define $q(x)=-p_n(-x)$. It is another [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $\Pi_n$. Changing variables $x\mapsto-x$, and using evenness of the weight $x^2$, gives $\|u-q\|=\|u-p_n\|$. Thus $q$ is also a best [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Uniqueness from part (b) gives $p_n(x)=-p_n(-x)$, proving

$$
\boxed{p_n\text{ is odd}.}
$$

Equivalently, the even component of a candidate is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to the odd residual and can only increase the squared error.
