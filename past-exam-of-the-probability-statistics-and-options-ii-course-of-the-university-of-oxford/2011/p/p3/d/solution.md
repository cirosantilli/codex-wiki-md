↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By part (c), the best degree-at-most-two [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is odd, so it must be $p_2(x)=bx$. [Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $x$ gives

$$
b=\frac{(x^{2011},x)}{(x,x)}
=\frac{\int_{-1}^1x^{2014}\,dx}{\int_{-1}^1x^4\,dx}
=\frac{2/2015}{2/5}=\frac1{403}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_2(x)=\frac{x}{403}.}
$$

Its error is automatically [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to the even [basis](../../../../../basis.md) functions $1,x^2$ by parity, and the chosen coefficient makes it [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $x$, so part (b) verifies optimality.
