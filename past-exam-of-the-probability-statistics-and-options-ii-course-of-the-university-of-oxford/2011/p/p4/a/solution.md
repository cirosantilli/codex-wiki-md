<h1 id="p/p4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For nonzero $w$, the [Householder transformation](../../../../../householder-transformation.md) is

$$
\boxed{H(w)=I-2\frac{ww^T}{w^Tw}.}
$$

The rank-one [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $P=ww^T/(w^Tw)$ is symmetric and satisfies $P^2=P$. Consequently $H^T=H$ and $H^TH=(I-2P)^2=I-4P+4P^2=I$. Thus the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is symmetric and [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md); geometrically it reflects the component along $w$ and fixes its perpendicular hyperplane.
