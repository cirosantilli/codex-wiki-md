<h1 id="m/m4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $t\geq0$, the geometric survival [probability](../../../../../probability.md) gives

$$
\Pr(T_k>t)=\Pr(S_1>kt)=\left(1-\frac1k\right)^{\lfloor kt\rfloor}\longrightarrow e^{-t}.
$$

For $t<0$, the distribution functions are zero, as is the limiting [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) function. Since that limiting distribution function is continuous everywhere, these limits prove

$$
\boxed{T_k\xrightarrow{d}\operatorname{Exp}(1).}
$$

The parameter is rate one, so the limiting mean is one, consistent with $\mathbb E(S_1/k)=1$.
