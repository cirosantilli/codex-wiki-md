<h1 id="m/m4/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The event $T\geq5$ is the same as $2^T\geq32$. Apply [Markov's inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $2^T$ and use part (iv):

$$
\boxed{\Pr(T\geq5)\leq\frac{7/6}{32}=\frac7{192}\approx0.03646<0.05.}
$$

The strict numerical comparison is enough even though the probabilistic inequality itself is non-strict.
