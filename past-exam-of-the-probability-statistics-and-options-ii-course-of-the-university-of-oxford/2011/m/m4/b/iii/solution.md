[Markov's inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) states that for a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Z$ and $a>0$,

$$
\boxed{\Pr(Z\geq a)\leq\frac{\mathbb EZ}{a}.}
$$

It follows immediately from $Z\geq a\mathbf1_{\{Z\geq a\}}$ and monotonicity of [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md). A finite [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) makes the bound informative.
