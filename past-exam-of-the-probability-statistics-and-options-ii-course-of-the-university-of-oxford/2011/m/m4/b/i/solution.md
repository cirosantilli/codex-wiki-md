<h1 id="m/m4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the analytic factor around zero by the [binomial series](../../../../../../binomial-series.md):

$$
(14-5z)^{-1/2}=\frac1{\sqrt{14}}\sum_{j=0}^\infty
\frac{\binom{2j}{j}}{4^j}\left(\frac5{14}\right)^jz^j.
$$

Every coefficient is nonnegative. Adding $2/3$ to the constant coefficient gives probabilities $p_j$ with sum $g(1)=2/3+1/3=1$. Define $\Pr(T=j)=p_j$ for $j\geq0$. This is a nonnegative integer-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) and its [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is exactly $g$. The power series has radius $14/5>1$, so the evaluations and derivatives used below are finite.
