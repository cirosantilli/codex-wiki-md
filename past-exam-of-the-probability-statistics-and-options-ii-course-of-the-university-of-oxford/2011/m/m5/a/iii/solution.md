<h1 id="m/m5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A state is a [recurrent state](../../../../../../recurrent-state.md) if the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of a return at some positive time, starting there, is one. It is a [transient state](../../../../../../transient-state.md) if that return [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is less than one. It is an [absorbing state](../../../../../../absorbing-state.md) if its one-step [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of remaining there is one. An [absorbing state](../../../../../../absorbing-state.md) is recurrent, since every subsequent time is a return.
