At $(-1,0)$ the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is $(1/2,0)^T$. The [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) is therefore the [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md)

$$
\boxed{Df(-1,0)(h,k)=\frac h2,\qquad J_f(-1,0)=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
