The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) at the specified point is

$$
\boxed{D(f\circ g)(x)=Df(g(x))\circ Dg(x).}
$$

In standard coordinates this becomes

$$
\boxed{J_{f\circ g}(x)=J_f(g(x))J_g(x).}
$$

The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) dimensions are respectively $k\times n$, $k\times m$, and $m\times n$, [matching](../../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) the composition of the two linear derivative maps.
