↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $j$-th [partial derivative](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is the [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) limit

$$
\partial_jf(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+he_j)-f(x)}h,
$$

when it exists. The [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) is a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $Df(x):\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that

$$
f(x+h)=f(x)+Df(x)h+o(\|h\|).
$$

The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is $J_f(x)=(\partial_jf_i(x))_{i,j}$. When the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) exists, all partial derivatives exist and $J_f(x)$ is its [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) in the standard bases; its $j$-th column is $Df(x)e_j$. Existence of partial derivatives alone does not guarantee total differentiability.
