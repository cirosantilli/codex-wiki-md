<h1 id="s/s2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Generate [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [standard normal](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) variables $Z_1,Z_2$ and put $\boxed{Y=Z_1/Z_2}$. The event $Z_2=0$ has probability zero. The ratio calculation in M4 with equal [standard deviations](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) gives [density](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $1/[\pi(1+y^2)]$, exactly the required $t$ distribution with one [degree](../../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) of freedom. The equivalent form $Z_1/|Z_2|$ has the same law by symmetry.
