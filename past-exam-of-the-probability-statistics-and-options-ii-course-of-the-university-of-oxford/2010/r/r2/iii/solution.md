<h1 id="r/r2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A label appears in the code exactly its [degree](../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) minus one times, as proved in (i). It is a [leaf of a graph](../../../../../leaf-of-a-graph.md) exactly when it never appears. Therefore

$$
\boxed{l=n-\#\{\text{distinct labels appearing in the code}\}.}
$$

For example the code in (ii) uses three distinct labels, so its ten-[vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) has seven [leaves of a graph](../../../../../leaf-of-a-graph.md).
