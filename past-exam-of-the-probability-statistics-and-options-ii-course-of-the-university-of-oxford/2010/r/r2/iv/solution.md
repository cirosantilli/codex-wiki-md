<h1 id="r/r2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $n=1$ there are no [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). Every finite [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) with at least two [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) has a [leaf of a graph](../../../../../leaf-of-a-graph.md): an endpoint of a longest simple path cannot have another neighbor, since that would either create a cycle or extend the path. Delete that [leaf of a graph](../../../../../leaf-of-a-graph.md) and its sole [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). The remaining graph is still a [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md), so [induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) gives $n-2$ remaining [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) and $\boxed{n-1}$ original [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). Alternatively the decoding construction in (i) inserts exactly $n-2$ [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) and then the final [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md).
