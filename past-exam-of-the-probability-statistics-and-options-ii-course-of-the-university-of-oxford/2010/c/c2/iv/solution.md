<h1 id="c/c2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Each nonzero [quadratic residue](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) is attained twice as $x^2$ for $1\le x\le p-1$. Therefore twice the sum of those [residues](../../../../../residue.md) is $\sum_{x=1}^{p-1}x^2$, which is zero modulo $p$ by (ii), since $p-1>2$. As two is invertible modulo $p$, the sum of the [residues](../../../../../residue.md) is zero. The sum of all nonzero [residues](../../../../../residue.md) is $p(p-1)/2\equiv0$, so subtracting gives the same result for the nonresidues. Hence $\boxed{\text{both indicated sums are }0\pmod p}$.
