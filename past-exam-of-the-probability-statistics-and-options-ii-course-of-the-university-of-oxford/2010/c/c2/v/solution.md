↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

By (ii), failure of divisibility is equivalent to $q-1\mid30$. The positive divisors of $30$ are $1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30$; adding one gives $2,3,4,6,7,11,16,31$. Retaining only [primes](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) yields

$$
\boxed{q\in\{2,3,7,11,31\}.}
$$
