<h1 id="a/a2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For $\alpha\ne0$, [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) in the multiplicative [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of order $q^m-1$ gives $\alpha^{q^m-1}=1$; at zero the desired [identity](../../../../../identity-element.md) is immediate. Thus all elements satisfy $\alpha^{q^m}-\alpha=0$.

For an irreducible [degree](../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md)-$m$ [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $f\in F[X]$, the quotient $L=F[X]/(f)$ is a [field](../../../../../field.md) of [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $m$, hence has $q^m$ elements by part 1. Its element $\alpha=[X]$ therefore satisfies $\alpha^{q^m}-\alpha=0$. The evaluation homomorphism $F[X]\to L$ has kernel $(f)$, so

$$
\boxed{f(X)\mid X^{q^m}-X.}
$$
