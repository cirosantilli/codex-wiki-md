<h1 id="a/a2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Write $q=p^r$, where $p$ is the [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md). In a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $X^{q^m}-X$ over $F$, let $K$ be its set of roots. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of this [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $-1$, so all $q^m$ roots are distinct. The [Frobenius](../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) [power](../../../../../power-function-of-a-statistical-test.md) is additive and multiplicative: $(x+y)^{q^m}=x^{q^m}+y^{q^m}$ and $(xy)^{q^m}=x^{q^m}y^{q^m}$. Thus roots are closed under addition, negatives and multiplication; for a nonzero root, $(x^{-1})^{q^m}=x^{-1}$. They include $0,1$ and form a [field](../../../../../field.md).

Every $a\in F$ satisfies $a^q=a$, by [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) on $F^\times$ and separately at zero. Iterating gives $a^{q^m}=a$, so $F\subseteq K$. The constructed [field](../../../../../field.md) therefore contains $F$ and has exactly $\boxed{q^m\text{ elements}}$.
