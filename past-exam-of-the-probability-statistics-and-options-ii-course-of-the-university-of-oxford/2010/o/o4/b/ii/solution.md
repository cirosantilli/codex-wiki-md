↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) normal [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) is normal, with [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) adding when the coefficients are $1,-1$. Applying the joint central limit approximation from (i) gives

$$
\boxed{\hat p-\hat q\approx N\left(p-q,\frac{p(1-p)}{2M}+\frac{q(1-q)}{2N}\right).}
$$

Equivalently the difference minus $p-q$, divided by the displayed [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md), converges in distribution to a [standard normal](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) under the same regular large-sample regime.
