<h1 id="o/o4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Under the null put $p=q=r$. The [likelihood](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) becomes the same two binomial constants times $r^{m+n}(1-r)^{2M+2N-m-n}$. Maximization gives the pooled estimate

$$
\boxed{\hat r=\frac{m+n}{2(M+N)}.}
$$

Under an interior null, (ii) has zero mean and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $r(1-r)(1/(2M)+1/(2N))$. [Statistical consistency](../../../../../../consistency-statistics.md) of $\hat r$ and [Slutsky's theorem](../../../../../../slutsky-theorem.md) permit replacement of $r$ by $\hat r$ in the denominator, giving the stated $Z\Rightarrow N(0,1)$. For a two-sided level-$\alpha$ test, reject when $\boxed{|Z|>\Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)}$. The large-sample approximation requires both expected counts of each type to grow; if all observed alleles are identical the stated denominator degenerates and this approximation is not applicable.
