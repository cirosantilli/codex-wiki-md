↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Conditioning on the initial state gives

$$
\boxed{P(X_n=i)=\sum_{j\in S}\lambda_j(P^n)_{ji}=(\lambda P^n)_i.}
$$

The row-vector convention is important: the initial distribution multiplies the [transition matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) on the left.
