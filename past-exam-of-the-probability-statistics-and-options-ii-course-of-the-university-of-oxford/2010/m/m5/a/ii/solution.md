↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) is $P=(p_{ij})_{i,j\in S}$, where $p_{ij}=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)$. It is [row-stochastic](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md): $p_{ij}\ge0$ and $\sum_jp_{ij}=1$ for each $i$. Initial states of zero probability can be specified by the chain's transition mechanism, so the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) describes the chain from any starting state.
