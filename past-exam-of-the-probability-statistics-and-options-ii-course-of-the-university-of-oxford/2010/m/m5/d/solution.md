↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If $\lambda P=\lambda$, [induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) gives $\lambda P^n=\lambda$ for all $n\ge0$. By (c), the marginal distribution at time $n$ is consequently $\boxed{\lambda}$. Such a probability vector is a [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md).
