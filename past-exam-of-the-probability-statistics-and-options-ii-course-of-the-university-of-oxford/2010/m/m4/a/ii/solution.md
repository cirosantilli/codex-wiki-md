<h1 id="m/m4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Integrate the [joint density](../../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) in $u$. Symmetry gives $f(-u)g(-uv)=f(u)g(uv)$, so the integrand $|u|f(u)g(uv)$ is even. Hence

$$
\boxed{f_V(v)=\int_{\mathbb R}|u|f(u)g(uv)du=2\int_0^\infty uf(u)g(uv)du.}
$$

[Tonelli's theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) justifies marginalization for these nonnegative [densities](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md).
