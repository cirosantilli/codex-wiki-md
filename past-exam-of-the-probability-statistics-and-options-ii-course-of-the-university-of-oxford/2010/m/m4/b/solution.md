<h1 id="m/m4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use $U=Y$, $V=XY^2$, with [inverse](../../../../../inverse-element.md) $y=u$, $x=v/u^2$. The Jacobian is $u^{-2}$, and the transformed support is $0<u<1$, $0<v<u^2$. Thus, for $0<v<1$,

$$
f_V(v)=\int_{\sqrt v}^1u^{-2}du=\frac1{\sqrt v}-1.
$$

Therefore $\boxed{f_V(v)=(v^{-1/2}-1)\mathbf1_{(0,1)}(v)}$. The integral over its support is $2-1=1$, checking normalization; there are no endpoint atoms.
