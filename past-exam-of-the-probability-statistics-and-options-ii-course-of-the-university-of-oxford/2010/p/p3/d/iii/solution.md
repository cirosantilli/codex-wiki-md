<h1 id="p/p3/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The decomposition $x=P_nx+(x-P_nx)$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) by (i). Therefore $\|x\|^2=\|P_nx\|^2+\|x-P_nx\|^2$. Substitute (ii) and rearrange to obtain

$$
\boxed{\|x-P_nx\|^2=\|x\|^2-\sum_{k=1}^n|\langle x,e_k\rangle|^2.}
$$

In particular nonnegativity of the left side gives the finite [Bessel inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md).
