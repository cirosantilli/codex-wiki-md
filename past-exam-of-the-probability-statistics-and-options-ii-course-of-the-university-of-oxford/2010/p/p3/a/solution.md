A [best approximation](../../../../../best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md) to $x$ from $U_n$ is an element $u_*\in U_n$ satisfying $\boxed{\|x-u_*\|=\inf_{u\in U_n}\|x-u\|}$. This is a [minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md) over the specified approximation subspace, not over all of $H$.
