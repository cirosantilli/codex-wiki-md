The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) states that if nonnegative measurable functions $h_n$ increase [almost everywhere](../../../almost-everywhere.md) to $h$, then $\int h=\lim_n\int h_n$, allowing infinity. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) states that if measurable $h_n\to h$ [almost everywhere](../../../almost-everywhere.md) and $|h_n|\le g$ for an [integrable](../../../integrability.md) $g$, then $h$ is [integrable](../../../integrability.md) and $\int h_n\to\int h$.
