<h1 id="e/e3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The inclusion of $X$ into $\mathbb R$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), so a [connected component](../../../../../../connected-component.md) $K$ of $X$ is an [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) in $\mathbb R$. For $x\in K$, openness of $X$ gives $\varepsilon>0$ with $(x-\varepsilon,x+\varepsilon)\subseteq X$. This [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) and meets $K$, so their union is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) and maximality forces the [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) into $K$. Thus $K$ is open in $\mathbb R$. A nonempty open [connected subset](../../../../../../connected-subset.md) of the line is an [open interval](../../../../../../open-interval.md), allowing an infinite endpoint or the whole line. Hence **every component is an [open interval](../../../../../../open-interval.md)**.
