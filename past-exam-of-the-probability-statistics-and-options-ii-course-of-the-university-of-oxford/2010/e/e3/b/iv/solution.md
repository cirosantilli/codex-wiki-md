<h1 id="e/e3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The components cover $X$ and are pairwise disjoint by (b)(ii); by (iii) they are [open intervals](../../../../../../open-interval.md). Each nonempty [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) contains a [rational number](../../../../../../rational-number.md). Enumerate the rationals and assign to each [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) the first rational it contains. Disjointness makes these assigned rationals distinct, giving an injection from the set of components into $\mathbb N$. Thus $\boxed{X\text{ is a countable union of pairwise disjoint open intervals}}$, with the empty union for $X=\varnothing$.
