<h1 id="e/e3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $K_a$ be the union of all [connected subsets](../../../../../../connected-subset.md) containing $a$. This collection is nonempty because it includes $\{a\}$. Any two of its members meet at $a$, so (a)(iii) makes their full union [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md). It contains $a$ and by its definition contains every [connected subset](../../../../../../connected-subset.md) through $a$. Thus

$$
\boxed{K_a=\bigcup\{S\subseteq X:S\text{ connected},\ a\in S\}}
$$

has exactly the required largest-set property.
