<h1 id="e/e3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $K_a\cap K_b\ne\varnothing$, their union is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) by (a)(iii). It contains $a$, so maximality gives $K_a\cup K_b\subseteq K_a$ and hence $K_b\subseteq K_a$. Interchanging the roles gives the reverse inclusion. Thus the two components coincide; otherwise they are disjoint.
