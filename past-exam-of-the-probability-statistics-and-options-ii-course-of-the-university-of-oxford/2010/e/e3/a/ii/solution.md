↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [connected subsets](../../../../../../connected-subset.md) of the [real line](../../../../../../real-line.md) are exactly the [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), including singletons, unbounded [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) and the empty set. Equivalently, whenever $x<y$ belong to the set, every $z$ with $x<z<y$ also belongs to it. Endpoints may or may not be included.
