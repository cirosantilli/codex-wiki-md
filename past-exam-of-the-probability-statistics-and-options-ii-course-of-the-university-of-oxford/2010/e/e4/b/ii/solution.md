A [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) is [complete](../../../../../../completeness.md) if every [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) of its points converges to a point of that same space. The requirement on the location of the limit distinguishes, for example, $\mathbb R$ from its incomplete subspace $\mathbb Q$.
