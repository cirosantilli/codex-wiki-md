↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Yes.** A [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) in the [supremum norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) is [Cauchy](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) in the given [complete](../../../../../../completeness.md) space of [bounded](../../../../../../boundedness.md) functions, so it has a [uniform limit](../../../../../../uniform-limit.md) there. A [uniform limit](../../../../../../uniform-limit.md) of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md): at a point, use two uniform errors smaller than $\varepsilon/3$ and [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of one sufficiently late term for the remaining error. Thus the limit is in $C([0,1])$, proving [completeness](../../../../../../completeness.md). Equivalently this is a [closed subspace](../../../../../../closed-subspace.md) of the given [complete](../../../../../../completeness.md) space.
