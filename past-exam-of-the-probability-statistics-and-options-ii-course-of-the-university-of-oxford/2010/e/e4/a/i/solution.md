↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A subset $K$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) if every cover of $K$ by sets open in the ambient space has a finite subcover. Equivalently, every relatively open cover of the subspace $K$ has a finite subcover. These agree because relatively [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) are intersections with ambient [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md).
