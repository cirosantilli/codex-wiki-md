↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A subset $B$ of a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) is [bounded](../../../../../../boundedness.md) if it lies in a ball of finite radius: there exist a point $x_0$ and $R<\infty$ such that $d(x,x_0)\le R$ for every $x\in B$. Equivalently its [diameter](../../../../../../diameter.md) $\sup_{x,y\in B}d(x,y)$ is finite, with the empty set [bounded](../../../../../../boundedness.md) by convention.
