<h1 id="e/e4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

By the Heine–Borel theorem a subset of $\mathbb R$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) exactly when it is closed and [bounded](../../../../../../boundedness.md). Consequently the nonempty [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) are exactly $\boxed{[a,b]\quad(-\infty<a\le b<\infty)}$. This includes the degenerate [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[a,a]$. The empty [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) as well; nonclosed [bounded](../../../../../../boundedness.md) [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) and all unbounded [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) fail the criterion.
