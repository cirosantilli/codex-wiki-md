<h1 id="f/f2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A $C^1$ embedded [hypersurface](../../../../../hypersurface.md) $M\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is locally carried by a $C^1$ coordinate diffeomorphism to an open subset of $\mathbb R^{n-1}\times\{0\}$. Equivalently, near each of its points it has the form $F^{-1}(0)$ for a $C^1$ function with nonzero [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) there.

The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) theorem states that if a [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) function $g$ has a local extremum on such a [hypersurface](../../../../../hypersurface.md) at $x_0$, then $dg(x_0)$ annihilates the [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md). In a regular local level-set description this is

$$
\boxed{\nabla g(x_0)=\lambda\nabla F(x_0)}
$$

for some real $\lambda$. The regularity condition $\nabla F(x_0)\ne0$ is essential; it is what identifies the [normal line](../../../../../normal-line.md) and permits the local constraint chart.
