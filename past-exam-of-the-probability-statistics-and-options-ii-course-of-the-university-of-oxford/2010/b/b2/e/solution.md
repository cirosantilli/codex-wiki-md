<h1 id="b/b2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

There are $p^n$ choices for the translation. Choose the columns of an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) successively: the first is any nonzero column, the second avoids its span, and at stage $j+1$ one avoids a $j$-dimensional span of size $p^j$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{|G|=p^n\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(p^n-p^j).}
$$

The successive [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md) conditions are exactly the criterion that the columns form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md).
