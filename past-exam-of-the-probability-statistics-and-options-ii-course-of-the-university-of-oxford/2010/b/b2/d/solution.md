↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Every pair factors as $(A,b)=(I,b)(A,0)$, and a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) which is also a translation fixes zero and has linear part $I$, hence is the [identity](../../../../../identity-element.md). Thus $G=NH$ and $N\cap H=\{1\}$. The [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) formula in (c) says that the action needed for reconstruction is simply the usual [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) action $A:t\mapsto At$ on the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $F^n$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G\cong F^n\rtimes\operatorname{GL}_n(F).}
$$
