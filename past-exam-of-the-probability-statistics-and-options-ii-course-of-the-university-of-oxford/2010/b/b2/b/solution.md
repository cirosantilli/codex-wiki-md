<h1 id="b/b2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Compose the second map first. Direct substitution gives

$$
\boxed{(A,b)(A',b')=(AA',Ab'+b).}
$$

The result is affine because the product [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is invertible. The [identity](../../../../../identity-element.md) is $(I,0)$ and the [inverse](../../../../../inverse-element.md) is $(A^{-1},-A^{-1}b)$. [Associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) follows from composition of maps, or by expanding this product three times. Distinct pairs give distinct maps, since evaluating at zero gives $b$ and evaluating the difference on [basis vectors](../../../../../basis-vector.md) gives all columns of $A$. Thus these pairs faithfully describe a [group](../../../../../group-split.md).
