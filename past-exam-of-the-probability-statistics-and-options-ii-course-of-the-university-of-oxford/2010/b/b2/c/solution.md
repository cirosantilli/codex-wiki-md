↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The map $A\mapsto(A,0)$ is an [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), so its image is the required copy of $\operatorname{GL}_n(F)$. Translations are the [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $N=\{(I,b):b\in F^n\}$, isomorphic to the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of $F^n$. For any affine map,

$$
\boxed{(A,b)(I,t)(A,b)^{-1}=(I,At).}
$$

Thus [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) preserves translations, proving $N\trianglelefteq G$.
