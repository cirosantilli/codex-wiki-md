[Gauss's lemma](../../../../../gauss-s-lemma-number-theory.md) for [quadratic residues](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) states: if $p\nmid a$ and $m$ is the number of least positive [residue classes](../../../../../residue-class.md) among $a,2a,\ldots,((p-1)/2)a$ that exceed $p/2$, then $\boxed{(a/p)=(-1)^m}$.
