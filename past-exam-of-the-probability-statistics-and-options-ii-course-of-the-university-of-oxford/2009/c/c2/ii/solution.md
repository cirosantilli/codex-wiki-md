↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Euler's criterion](../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) states that for $p\nmid a$,

$$
\boxed{a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p.}
$$

For a multiple of $p$, both the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) and that positive [power](../../../../../power-function-of-a-statistical-test.md) vanish modulo $p$, so the congruence extends to that case as well.
