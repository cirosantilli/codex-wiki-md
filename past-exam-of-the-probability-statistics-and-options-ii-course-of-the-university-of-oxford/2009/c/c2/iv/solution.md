<h1 id="c/c2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $a=2$ the displayed multiples are $2,4,\ldots,p-1$, already least positive [residue classes](../../../../../residue-class.md). Those greater than $p/2$ correspond to $j>p/4$. Thus their number is $m=(p-1)/2-\lfloor p/4\rfloor$. For $p\equiv1,3,5,7\pmod8$, its [parity](../../../../../parity.md) is respectively even, odd, odd, even. [Gauss's lemma](../../../../../gauss-s-lemma-number-theory.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac2p\right)=\begin{cases}1,&p\equiv1,7\pmod8,\\-1,&p\equiv3,5\pmod8.\end{cases}}
$$
