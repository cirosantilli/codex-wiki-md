↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

[Euler's criterion](../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) gives $(-1/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}$. The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is multiplicative, so multiplying this sign by the result of (iv) yields

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{-2}p\right)=\begin{cases}1,&p\equiv1,3\pmod8,\\-1,&p\equiv5,7\pmod8.\end{cases}}
$$
