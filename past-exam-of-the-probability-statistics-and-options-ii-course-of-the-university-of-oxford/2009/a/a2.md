↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic](../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) is the least positive integer $r$ with $r1_K=0$, if it exists, and zero otherwise. The [prime](../../../prime-number-split.md) subfield is the smallest subfield containing $1_K$; in [characteristic](../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$ it is $F=\{0,1,\ldots,(p-1)1_K\}\cong\mathbb F_p$. Every [field automorphism](../../../field-automorphism.md) fixes $1_K$ and addition, so it fixes every element of this [prime](../../../prime-number-split.md) subfield.
