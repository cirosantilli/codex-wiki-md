<h1 id="a/a2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take $0<m<n$, the intended positive-exponent range. If $\vartheta^m(\lambda)=\lambda$, then $\vartheta^m$ fixes both $F$ and its [field](../../../../../field.md) generator, so it fixes every element of $K=F(\lambda)$. Every element would then be a root of $X^{p^m}-X$. But that [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $p^m<p^n=|K|$, impossible for a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md). Thus $\boxed{\vartheta^m(\lambda)\ne\lambda\quad(0<m<n)}$. The source writes only $m<n$; zero must be excluded, since $\vartheta^0(\lambda)=\lambda$.
