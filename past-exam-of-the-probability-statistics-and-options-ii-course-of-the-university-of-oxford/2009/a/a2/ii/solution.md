<h1 id="a/a2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define $\vartheta(x)=x^p$ on all of $K$. The binomial coefficients between the endpoints are divisible by $p$, so $(x+y)^p=x^p+y^p$; also $(xy)^p=x^py^p$ and $1^p=1$. Thus this is a [field](../../../../../field.md) homomorphism. Its kernel is zero, and an [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) self-map of the finite set $K$ is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md). Hence $\boxed{\vartheta\text{ is an automorphism}}$, the [Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md), with the required image of $\lambda$. It fixes $F$ by the preceding [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md)-subfield argument.
