↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $K=F(\lambda)$ and the extension is finite, the element $\lambda$ is algebraic. Its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md), and evaluation gives an isomorphism $F[X]/(m_\lambda)\cong F[\lambda]=F(\lambda)=K$. If its [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) is $d$, the powers $1,\lambda,\ldots,\lambda^{d-1}$ form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md). Comparing with $[K:F]=n$ gives $\boxed{\deg m_\lambda=n}$.
