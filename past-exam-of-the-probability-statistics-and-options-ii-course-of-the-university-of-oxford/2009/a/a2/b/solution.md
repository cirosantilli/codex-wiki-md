The [finite field](../../../../../finite-field.md) $K$ is a finite-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over its [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) subfield $F$ of size $p$. A [basis](../../../../../basis.md) has some positive length $n$, and every element is uniquely specified by $n$ coefficients in $F$. There are $p$ choices per coefficient, so $\boxed{|K|=p^n}$ and $[K:F]=n$.
