<h1 id="a/a2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The additive sequence $0,1,2\cdot1,\ldots$ must repeat because $K$ is finite, so the [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) is positive. If its least positive value were composite, $r=ab$ with $1<a,b<r$, then $(a1_K)(b1_K)=r1_K=0$ while neither factor would be zero. A [field](../../../../../field.md) has no zero divisors, giving a contradiction. Hence $\boxed{\operatorname{char}K=p\text{ is prime}}$.
