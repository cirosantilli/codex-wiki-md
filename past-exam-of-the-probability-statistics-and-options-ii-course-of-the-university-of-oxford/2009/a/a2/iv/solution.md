<h1 id="a/a2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For nonzero $x\in K$, [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) in $K^\times$ gives $x^{p^n-1}=1$; zero satisfies $x^{p^n}=x$ too. Thus $\vartheta^n$ is the [identity](../../../../../identity-element.md), and (iii) excludes every smaller positive order. Hence $\boxed{|\langle\vartheta\rangle|=n}$.

Any [field automorphism](../../../../../field-automorphism.md) of $K$ fixes $F$ and is determined by its value on $\lambda$. That value must be a root of the [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md)-$n$ minimal [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), so there are at most $n$ automorphisms. The $n$ powers of $\vartheta$ already attain this bound. Consequently $\boxed{\operatorname{Aut}(K)=\langle\vartheta\rangle\cong C_n}$.
