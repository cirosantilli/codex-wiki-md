↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $Z_p=\sum_x\tilde p(x)$ and $Z_q=\sum_x\tilde q(x)$, both finite and positive as required for normalization. A sufficient envelope condition is $\boxed{M\ge\sup_{x\in\Omega}\tilde p(x)/\tilde q(x)}$ with finite $M>0$. Use [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) proposals and [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) uniforms on each trial. This guarantees an acceptance probability in $[0,1]$ for every state. Choose the smallest certified upper bound on that ratio, since increasing $M$ decreases the overall acceptance rate. The ratio must be [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md) for this particular proposal to admit such an envelope.
