↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [complete](../../../../../completeness.md) [matching](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) from $V_1$ to $V_2$ is a set of [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) with no shared endpoints that contains an [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) incident to every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of $V_1$. The theorem in (i) gives the necessary and sufficient condition

$$
\boxed{|N(A)|\ge|A|\quad\text{for every }A\subseteq V_1.}
$$

The intended setting is a finite left [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) set; no condition that every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of $V_2$ be used is imposed.
