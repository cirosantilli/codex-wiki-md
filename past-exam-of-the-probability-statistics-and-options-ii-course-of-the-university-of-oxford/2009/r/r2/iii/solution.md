<h1 id="r/r2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Form a [bipartite graph](../../../../../bipartite-graph-split.md) with left labels $1,\ldots,m$, right set $E$, and an [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) $i\!\mathbin{-}\!e$ exactly when $e\in S_i$. A [matching](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) covering all left labels is precisely a choice of distinct representatives. Its neighborhood condition is

$$
\boxed{\left|\bigcup_{i\in I}S_i\right|\ge|I|\quad\text{for every }I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}.}
$$

Thus this is necessary and sufficient. The underlying set $E$ may be infinite; only the number of sets to be represented is finite.
