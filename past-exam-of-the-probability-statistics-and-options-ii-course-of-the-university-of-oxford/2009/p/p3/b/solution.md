<h1 id="p/p3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplying the [Vandermonde matrix](../../../../../vandermonde-matrix.md) $A_n$ by a coefficient vector $(c_0,\ldots,c_n)^T$ evaluates the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $\sum_jc_jx^j$ at all nodes. If the result is zero, part (a)'s uniqueness for the zero interpolation data forces every coefficient to be zero. Thus the square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has trivial kernel and is invertible. Equivalently the interpolation map is a linear [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) between two spaces of [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $n+1$.
