<h1 id="p/p4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $x\in S_v$, then $v^Tx=0$ and $Px=0$, so $\boxed{H_vx=x}$. For arbitrary $x$, $v^TP=v^T$, giving

$$
\boxed{v^TH_vx=v^Tx-2v^TPx=-v^Tx.}
$$

Thus the Householder map fixes the hyperplane [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $v$ and reverses the component in its normal direction: it is a reflection, not merely an arbitrary [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) map.
