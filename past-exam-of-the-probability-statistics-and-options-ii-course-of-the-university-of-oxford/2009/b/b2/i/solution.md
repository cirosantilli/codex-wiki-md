A left action is a map $(g,\omega)\mapsto g\omega$ satisfying $1\omega=\omega$ and $(gh)\omega=g(h\omega)$. The [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of $\omega$ is $G\omega=\{g\omega:g\in G\}$. The action is [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) when there is exactly one [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) on the nonempty acted-on set, equivalently when any point can be moved to any other by a [group](../../../../../group-split.md) element. An action is equivalently a homomorphism from $G$ to the permutation [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of the set.
