↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The moment generating function is $\boxed{M_X(\theta)=\mathbb E[e^{\theta X}]}$ for real $\theta$ where this [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite. Existence on an [open interval](../../../../../open-interval.md) about zero determines the distribution and permits differentiation there to obtain moments, when the usual local integrability conditions hold.
