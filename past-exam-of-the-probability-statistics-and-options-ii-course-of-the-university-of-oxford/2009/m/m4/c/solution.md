<h1 id="m/m4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The interarrival times are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and identically distributed with law $\operatorname{Exponential}(\lambda)$. In particular any finite subcollection of the first $r$ has [joint density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) $\lambda^r\exp(-\lambda\sum_{j=1}^rx_j)$ on $x_j>0$. Direct integration gives

$$
\boxed{M_{X_1}(\theta)=\int_0^\infty\lambda e^{-(\lambda-\theta)x}dx=\frac\lambda{\lambda-\theta}\quad(\theta<\lambda).}
$$
