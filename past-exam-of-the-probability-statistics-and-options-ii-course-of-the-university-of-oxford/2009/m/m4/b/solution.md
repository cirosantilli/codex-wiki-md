A [Poisson process](../../../../../poisson-process-split.md) of rate $\lambda>0$ is a counting process with $N_0=0$, [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) stationary increments, and $N_t-N_s\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda(t-s))$ for $0\le s<t$. It has nondecreasing right-[continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) integer-valued paths with [unit jumps](../../../../../counting-process.md) almost surely. The [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) increment requirement distinguishes the process from merely having Poisson marginals.
