<h1 id="m/m5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $i\notin A$, condition on the first step. The [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md) makes the chance of eventual entry after reaching $j$ equal to $h_j$, including $h_j=1$ when $j\in A$. Total probability gives

$$
\boxed{h_i=\sum_{j\in S}p_{ij}h_j\quad(i\notin A).}
$$

The countable sum is justified by its nonnegative terms. This first-step [identity](../../../../../../identity-element.md) alone need not uniquely determine a [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md) on an infinite state space; one must choose the minimal nonnegative solution or supply the appropriate boundary behavior.
