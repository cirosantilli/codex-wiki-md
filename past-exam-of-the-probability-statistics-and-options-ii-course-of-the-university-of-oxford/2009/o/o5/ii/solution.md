↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With one parameter fitted to the grouped cells, the reference [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) are $10-1-1=8$. The table already gives a deviance of $8.29$ at $0.93$, so the minimized deviance is no larger than that; its direct grouped [minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md) is about $7.76$. Either is below the supplied critical value $15.51$. Therefore **do not reject the fitted grouped log-series model at level five percent**. The estimate being profiled in this test is the grouped-[likelihood](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) estimate described above, not the different full-data score estimate.
