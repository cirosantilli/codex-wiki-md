↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Unbiasedness as an estimator of the parameter means $DX\theta=\theta$ for every $\theta\in\mathbb R^p$, rather than just one particular value. Thus $(DX-I_p)\theta=0$ for every vector, forcing $\boxed{DX=I_p}$. Conversely that [identity](../../../../../identity-element.md) plainly suffices for unbiasedness.
