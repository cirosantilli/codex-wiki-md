The multinomial [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md) is a parameter-[independent](../../../independent-random-variables.md) factor times $\prod_i\pi_i^{n_i}$. Maximize its logarithm subject to $\sum\pi_i=1$: the multiplier equations give $n_i/\pi_i=c$, and summation makes $c=n$. Thus $\hat\pi_i=n_i/n$. Positivity of all counts makes this the unique interior [maximum](../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) by strict concavity.

The [likelihood ratio](../../../likelihood-ratio.md) compares the fitted null [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md) with this saturated [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md). Taking minus twice its logarithm gives $\Lambda(\hat\theta)=2\sum_i n_i\log[n_i/(n\pi_i(\hat\theta))]$. Under regularity and large expected counts, compare it to $\chi^2_{k-2}$ for one fitted parameter: the saturated probabilities have $k-1$ free coordinates and the null has one. If the parameter is fixed at $\theta_0$, use $\Lambda(\theta_0)$ and $\chi^2_{k-1}$. Reject for a value above the chosen upper [quantile](../../../quantile-function.md).

For the ungrouped [logarithmic-series](../../../logarithmic-distribution.md) observations write $n=1188$ and $S=\sum_rX_r=6968$. The log [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md) is

$$
\ell(\theta)=-n\log[-\log(1-\theta)]+S\log\theta-\sum_r\log X_r,\qquad0<\theta<1.
$$

Setting its [derivative](../../../derivative.md) to zero gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{-\hat\theta_{\rm full}}{(1-\hat\theta_{\rm full})\log(1-\hat\theta_{\rm full})}=\frac{6968}{1188}.}
$$

Bisection of this monotone mean equation, or [Newton's method](../../../newton-root-finding-iteration.md) with a valid bracket, obtains $\hat\theta_{\rm full}\approx0.9442280$.

The supplied table instead uses ten grouped cells, with the last probability $\pi_{\ge10}(\theta)=1-\sum_{i=1}^9\pi_i(\theta)$. Its [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md) is determined by the cell counts and is not the ungrouped [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md) just differentiated. From its listed values, the [minimum](../../../local-minimum.md) deviance occurs near $\boxed{\hat\theta_{\rm grouped}\approx0.93}$; direct minimization of this grouped deviance gives about $0.9333714$. The supplied two forms of the estimate therefore concern different likelihoods. They must not be identified: the full-data score equation gives about $0.94423$, while the grouped table points near $0.93$. The following requested tests use the table's grouped [likelihood](../../../likelihood-function.md), with ten cells. The source values and totals are preserved unchanged in TeX.
