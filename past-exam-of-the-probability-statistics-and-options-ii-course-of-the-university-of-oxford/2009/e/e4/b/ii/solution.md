<h1 id="e/e4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $X$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md), its [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) image under $q$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) by (a)(ii). Conversely suppose $X/R$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) and $A\subseteq X$ is [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md). By (i) both $A$ and its complement are saturated. The quotient definition then makes $q(A)$ open, and its complement $q(X\setminus A)$ open too. Thus $q(A)$ is [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) in the [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) quotient, so is empty or the whole quotient. Pulling back gives $A=\varnothing$ or $X$. By the [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) criterion $X$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md). Hence $\boxed{X\text{ connected}\iff X/R\text{ connected}}$.
