<h1 id="e/e4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $A$ be [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) and $C$ an [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md). The intersections $C\cap A$ and $C\setminus A$ are complementary relatively open subsets of $C$. [Connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) of $C$ means one must be empty. Thus every [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) is entirely in $A$ or entirely outside it, proving $\boxed{q^{-1}(q(A))=A}$. This is precisely saturation.
