A subset is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) if it has no decomposition into two disjoint nonempty sets both relatively open in it. Equivalently its only relatively [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) subsets are itself and the empty set. [Connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) here refers to the subset's induced topology.
