<h1 id="e/e4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The set $X/R$ consists of the [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md), with canonical surjection $q:x\mapsto[x]$. The [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) declares $V\subseteq X/R$ open exactly when $q^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. This makes $q$ [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and is the finest topology on the quotient set doing so.
