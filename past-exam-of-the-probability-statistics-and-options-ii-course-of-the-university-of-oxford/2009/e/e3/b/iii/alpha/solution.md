<h1 id="e/e3/b/iii/alpha/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

If [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) holds everywhere, it holds at $a$. Conversely, if it holds at $a$, it holds at every other point automatically: the singleton neighborhood from (ii) maps entirely to the single value at that point. Thus [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) everywhere in $d_a$ is equivalent to [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$ in $d_a$.
