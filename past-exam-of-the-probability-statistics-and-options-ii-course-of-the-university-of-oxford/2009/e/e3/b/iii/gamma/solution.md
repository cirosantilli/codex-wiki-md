↑ **Parent:** [Γ](../gamma.md)

The same equality of balls gives the reverse implication: [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$ in $d$ implies [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$ in $d_a$. The latter implies [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) everywhere by the first part. These implications [complete](../../../../../../../completeness.md) the equivalence of all three printed conditions.
