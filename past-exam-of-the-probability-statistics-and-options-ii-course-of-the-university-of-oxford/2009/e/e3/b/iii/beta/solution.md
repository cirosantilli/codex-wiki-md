<h1 id="e/e3/b/iii/beta/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Β](../beta.md)

For every $r>0$ one has $B_{d_a}(a,r)=B_d(a,r)$ because $d_a(a,x)=d(a,x)$. The epsilon–delta definition at $a$ is therefore identical for the two domain metrics. In particular [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$ in $d_a$ implies [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) there in $d$.
