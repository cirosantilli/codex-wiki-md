<h1 id="t/2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The dual is

$$
\boxed{D:\ \min_{y\ge0}\mathbf1^\mathsf Ty\quad\text{subject to }A^\mathsf Ty\ge\mathbf1.}
$$

For positive payoffs and an optimal solution $y_*$, [strong duality](../../../../../../strong-duality.md) gives $\mathbf1^\mathsf Ty_*=1/v$. Normalizing gives Player I's optimal row strategy $p_*=y_*/(\mathbf1^\mathsf Ty_*)$. Its expected payoff against every column is at least $v$. Thus the primal represents Player II's minimization of worst loss and the dual represents Player I's maximization of guaranteed gain.
