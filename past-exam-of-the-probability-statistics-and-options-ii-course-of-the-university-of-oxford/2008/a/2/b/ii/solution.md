↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes.** Let $g$ send the first [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md) to the second and write $d=\det g\ne0$. In an adapted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of the target [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md), scale its first vector by $d^{-1}$ and fix the others. This defines a [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md)-preserving map $h$ with determinant $d^{-1}$. Then $hg$ still sends the first [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md) to the second and has determinant one. Thus $\operatorname{SL}(n,K)$ is [transitive](../../../../../../transitive-group-action.md) as well.
