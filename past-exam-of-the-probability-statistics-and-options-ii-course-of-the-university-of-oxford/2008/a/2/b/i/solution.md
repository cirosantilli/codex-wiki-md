<h1 id="a/2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) for the first subspace of a [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md), extend it to a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) for the second, and continue until obtaining a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of the whole [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md). Do the same for the target [flag](../../../../../../flag-linear-algebra.md). The unique [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) sending the first adapted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) to the second is invertible and sends every flagged subspace to its counterpart. Hence the action is **[transitive](../../../../../../transitive-group-action.md)**.
