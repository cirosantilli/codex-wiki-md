<h1 id="k/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an analytic complex potential $f$ whose singularities lie outside $|z|=a$, the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) theorem adds the reflected potential

$$
F(z)=f(z)+\overline{f(a^2/\bar z)}.
$$

The added term is analytic outside the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) wherever the reflected original is regular; in particular no new exterior singularities occur when $f$ is regular inside the [circle](../../../../../circle.md). On $|z|=a$, the relation $a^2/\bar z=z$ makes $F=f+\bar f$ real. Thus its imaginary part, the stream function, is constant on the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) and the normal velocity vanishes. This proves the theorem, with any harmless additive constant left free. Its usual decay condition at infinity selects the surrounding flow inherited from $f$.

Write $a_1=z_1$ for the [dipole](../../../../../planar-potential-dipole.md) position to distinguish it from the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) radius, now one. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{F(z)=\frac m{z-a_1}+\frac{\bar m\,z}{1-\bar a_1z},\qquad|a_1|>1.}
$$

The image singularity is at $1/\bar a_1$, strictly inside the solid [circle](../../../../../circle.md). This exterior potential has the original [dipole](../../../../../planar-potential-dipole.md) and an impermeable [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) boundary.
