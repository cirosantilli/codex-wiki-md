<h1 id="k/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On the line $y=-x$ one has $\overline{iz}=z$. Hence the reflected term in the printed formula is $\overline{w(z)}$, and the total is real on the line. This proves that its stream function is constant there. The nested [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) is important: it makes the added term analytic as a function of $z$.

For the [planar potential dipole](../../../../../planar-potential-dipole.md) its image contribution is

$$
\overline{\frac m{\overline{iz}-a}}=\frac{\bar m}{iz-\bar a}=-\frac{i\bar m}{z+i\bar a}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{F(z)=\frac m{z-a}-\frac{i\bar m}{z+i\bar a}.}
$$

The image is the reflected point $-i\bar a$, which lies in the opposite half-plane when the original [dipole](../../../../../planar-potential-dipole.md) lies strictly inside the fluid half-plane. The wall itself is a limiting boundary, not an admissible position for the singularity.
