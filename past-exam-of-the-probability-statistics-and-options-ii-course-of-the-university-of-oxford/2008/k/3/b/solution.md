<h1 id="k/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Across a straight impermeable wall the reflected potential is obtained by conjugating the potential after reflecting its argument. For the real axis the operation is $f(z)\mapsto\overline{f(\bar z)}$; the sum is real on that axis. For the imaginary axis it is $f(z)\mapsto\overline{f(-\bar z)}$. Applying both commuting reflections to the [dipole](../../../../../planar-potential-dipole.md) produces

$$
\boxed{F(z)=\frac m{z-a}+\frac{\bar m}{z-\bar a}-\frac{\bar m}{z+\bar a}-\frac m{z+a},\qquad a=z_1.}
$$

The other three poles are outside the first quadrant. The sum is invariant under each reflected-[conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) operation, hence real on each boundary axis. Both walls are therefore streamlines.
