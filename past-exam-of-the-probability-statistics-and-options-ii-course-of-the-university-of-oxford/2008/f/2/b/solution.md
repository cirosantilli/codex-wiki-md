<h1 id="f/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Define $F(x)=x_1^2/a^2+x_2^2/b^2+x_3^2/c^2-1$. Its [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $2(x_1/a^2,x_2/b^2,x_3/c^2)$. It cannot vanish on $F^{-1}(0)$, because the only possible zero is the origin and that point has $F=-1$. The regular level-set theorem therefore makes the ellipsoid a two-dimensional [embedded submanifold](../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md). Its [normal space](../../../../../normal-space.md) is

$$
\boxed{N_xM=\mathbb R\,(x_1/a^2,x_2/b^2,x_3/c^2),}
$$

and the [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) is the [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) complement of that line.
