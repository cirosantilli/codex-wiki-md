↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A two-dimensional [embedded submanifold](../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) is a subset which near every point can be straightened by a smooth local coordinate change in the ambient space to an open piece of the coordinate plane $\mathbb R^2\times\{0\}$. Equivalently locally it has a regular smooth parametrization of rank two, with the induced local topology.

A [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) at $x$ is the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $\gamma'(0)$ of a smooth curve in the [submanifold](../../../../../submanifold.md) with $\gamma(0)=x$. These [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) form the two-dimensional [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) $T_xM$. A [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is a vector [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to every [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md). Its space is $(T_xM)^\perp$, which has $\boxed{\dim (T_xM)^\perp=1}$ in the three-dimensional ambient Euclidean space.
