<h1 id="p/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $q(x)=a+bx$. [Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of $x^2-q$ to $1,x$ gives

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\log3&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}\binom ab=\binom4{26/3}.
$$

The entries are obtained by integrating $1/x,1,x,x^2$ over the given [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). Subtracting the second equation divided by two from the first gives $(\log3-1)a=-1/3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{q(x)=-\frac1{3(\log3-1)}+\left(\frac{13}6+\frac1{6(\log3-1)}\right)x.}
$$

The [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md) is positive definite, with determinant $4(\log3-1)>0$, so this [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) is the unique [best approximation](../../../../../best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md).
