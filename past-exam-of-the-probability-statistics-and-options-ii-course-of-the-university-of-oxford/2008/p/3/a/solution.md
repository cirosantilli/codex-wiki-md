↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define $\boxed{\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle}}$. Positive definiteness gives nonnegativity and separation of zero, and bilinearity gives absolute homogeneity. [Cauchy](../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md)–Schwarz follows by minimizing the nonnegative quadratic $\|u-tv\|^2$ in real $t$. It then gives $\|u+v\|^2\le(\|u\|+\|v\|)^2$, establishing the triangle inequality. Thus this is a norm.
