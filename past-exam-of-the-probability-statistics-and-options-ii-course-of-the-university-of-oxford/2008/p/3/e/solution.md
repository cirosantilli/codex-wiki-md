<h1 id="p/3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The best-approximation characterization gives $\langle f-q,q\rangle=0$, since $q\in\Pi_k$. Consequently

$$
\|f\|^2=\|(f-q)+q\|^2=\|f-q\|^2+\|q\|^2\ge\|q\|^2.
$$

Taking nonnegative square roots proves $\boxed{\|q\|\le\|f\|}$. Thus an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) cannot increase the norm.
