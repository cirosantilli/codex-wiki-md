<h1 id="p/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first column is five times $(4,0,3,0)^\mathsf T/5$. Its [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) companion in the first-and-third-coordinate plane is $(3,0,-4,0)^\mathsf T/5$, and the second column has positive residual seven times that vector. The remaining coordinate directions can therefore be read off immediately rather than performing a general four-dimensional orthogonalization. One obtains

$$
\boxed{Q=\begin{pmatrix}4/5&3/5&0&0\\0&0&1&0\\3/5&-4/5&0&0\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad R=\begin{pmatrix}5&1&5&2\\0&7&10&-11\\0&0&9&6\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The columns of $Q$ are visibly [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md). Taking their inner products with each column of the given [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) gives the displayed $R$, which also verifies $QR=A$ and positive diagonal entries.
