<h1 id="s/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $L$ be the number of index tables, including the zero table. If distinct feasible states $A,B$ differ by an index table, then $P(A,B)=1/L=P(B,A)$, using the sign-reversal [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) for the reverse move. If they do not differ by an index table, both probabilities are zero. Since $\pi$ is uniform,

$$
\boxed{\pi(A)P(A,B)=\pi(B)P(B,A).}
$$

For equal states [detailed balance](../../../../../detailed-balance.md) is tautological. Thus the chain is reversible and its [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) is stationary.
