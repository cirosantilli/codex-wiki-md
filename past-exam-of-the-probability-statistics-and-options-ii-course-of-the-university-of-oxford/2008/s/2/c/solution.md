<h1 id="s/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A finite [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) [aperiodic Markov chain](../../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md) converges to its unique [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) from every initial state. Finiteness holds because nonnegative entries with fixed finite row sums are [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md). The given connecting-sequence property makes the chain [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md): each feasible step in such a sequence has positive proposal probability and is accepted. The all-zero index table occurs with probability $1/L>0$ and gives a self-loop at every state, establishing [aperiodicity](../../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md). Reversibility supplied the stationary uniform law in (b). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Pr(X_t=x)\longrightarrow\pi(x)\quad\text{for every state }x.}
$$

The conclusion also holds when the feasible state space is a singleton.
