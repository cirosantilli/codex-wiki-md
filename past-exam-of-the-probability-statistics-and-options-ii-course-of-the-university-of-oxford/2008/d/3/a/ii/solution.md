**No.** Take $f_n=\mathbf1_{[n,n+1]}$ on the [real line](../../../../../../real-line.md). At every fixed point it eventually vanishes, so $f=0$, while $\int f_n=1$ for every $n$. Thus the limit of the integrals is one and the integral of the pointwise limit is zero. Nonnegativity alone cannot replace monotonicity or an [integrable](../../../../../../integrability.md) domination hypothesis.
