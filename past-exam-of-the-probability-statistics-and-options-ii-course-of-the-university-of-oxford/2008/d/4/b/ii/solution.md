↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No.** On each [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[k\pi,(k+1)\pi]$, for $k\ge1$,

$$
\int_{k\pi}^{(k+1)\pi}\frac{|\sin x|}{x}\,dx\ge\frac1{(k+1)\pi}\int_{k\pi}^{(k+1)\pi}|\sin x|\,dx=\frac2{(k+1)\pi}.
$$

The resulting harmonic lower bound diverges. Therefore $\sin x/x\notin L^1(0,\infty)$. Its convergent improper integral does not make it [Lebesgue integrable](../../../../../../lebesgue-integrable-function.md); its positive and negative parts both have infinite integral.
