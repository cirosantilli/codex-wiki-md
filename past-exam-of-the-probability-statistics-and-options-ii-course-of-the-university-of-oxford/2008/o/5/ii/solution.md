<h1 id="o/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The variables $(m-1)S_X^2/\sigma^2$ and $(n-1)S_Y^2/\sigma^2$ are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) chi-squared variables with $m-1$ and $n-1$ [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md). [Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) follows because the two whole samples are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). Their sum is chi-squared with the sum of the [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md), so

$$
\boxed{\frac{(m+n-2)S^2}{\sigma^2}\sim\chi^2_{m+n-2},\qquad d=\frac{m+n-2}{\sigma^2}.}
$$

Within each normal sample the [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md) is [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of its [sample variance](../../../../../sample-variance.md), and [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the samples makes the pair of means jointly [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the pair of [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md). Hence this pooled chi-squared variable is [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the standardized mean difference, which is the [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) required for the Student pivot.
