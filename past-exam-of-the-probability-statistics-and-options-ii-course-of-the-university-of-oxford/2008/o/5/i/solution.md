↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) makes a difference of the two normal sample means normal, with means subtracting and [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) adding. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline X-\overline Y\sim N\left(\mu_X-\mu_Y,\ \sigma^2\left(\frac1m+\frac1n\right)\right).}
$$
