↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

As established above, $Q'_3\cong K_{4,4}$. Select any three [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) in each [parity](../../../../../parity.md) class; all nine [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) between the two triples are present, giving a $K_{3,3}$ subgraph. A [planar graph](../../../../../planar-graph-split.md) has only planar subgraphs, whereas $K_{3,3}$ is nonplanar by the permitted result. Therefore $\boxed{Q'_3\text{ is nonplanar}}$.
