↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Opposite [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) differ in all three bits. Thus the four added [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) are

$$
\boxed{000\!-!111,\quad001\!-!110,\quad010\!-!101,\quad011\!-!100.}
$$

They form a [perfect matching](../../../../../perfect-matching.md) and raise the [degree](../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) of every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) by one.
