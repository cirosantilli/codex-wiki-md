↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every cube [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) has [degree](../../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) three, so $Q_3$ is **not Eulerian**; it even has eight odd-[degree](../../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md), so has no open [Eulerian trail](../../../../../../eulerian-trail.md). After the four diagonals are added every [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) has [degree](../../../../../../degree-graph-theory.md) four, and the [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md) remains [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md). Thus $Q'_3$ **is Eulerian**, by the circuit criterion proved above.
