↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**No.** At any cube [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) its three incident faces are pairwise adjacent along the three incident [edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). They would therefore require three different colours. Equivalently the dual of the planar cube embedding contains a triangle, which obstructs a face two-colouring.
