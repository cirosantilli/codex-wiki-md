↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Both are bipartite.** Partition the [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) by [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) of the number of one bits. A cube [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) changes one bit and an interior diagonal changes three bits, so both types join opposite [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) classes. In fact $Q'_3$ is $K_{4,4}$: from any [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) its three cube neighbors and its opposite [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) exhaust the four [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of opposite [parity](../../../../../../parity.md).
