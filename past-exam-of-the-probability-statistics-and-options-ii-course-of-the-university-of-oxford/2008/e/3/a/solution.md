<h1 id="e/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The distance function $x\mapsto d(x,c)$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) because the triangle inequality gives $|d(x,c)-d(y,c)|\le d(x,y)$. Hence $\overline B(c,r)=\{x:d(x,c)\le r\}$ is the [inverse image](../../../../../preimage.md) of the closed [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $(-\infty,r]$, so is closed. Since it contains $B(c,r)$, it also contains the [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) of that [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cl}B(c,r)\subseteq\overline B(c,r).}
$$
