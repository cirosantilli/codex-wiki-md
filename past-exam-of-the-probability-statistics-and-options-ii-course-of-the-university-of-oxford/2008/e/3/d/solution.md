<h1 id="e/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let the radius be $r>0$. One inclusion follows from (a). For the reverse, if $\|x-c\|\le r$, set $x_n=c+(1-1/n)(x-c)$ for $n\ge2$. Then $\|x_n-c\|<r$ and $x_n\to x$, so every closed-ball point is in the [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) of the [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cl}B(c,r)=\overline B(c,r)\quad(r>0).}
$$

The positive-radius convention matters: if radius zero is allowed, the [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) is empty whereas the [closed ball](../../../../../closed-ball.md) is $\{c\}$, so the asserted equality fails at that degenerate radius.
