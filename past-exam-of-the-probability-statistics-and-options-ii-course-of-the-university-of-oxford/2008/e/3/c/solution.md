<h1 id="e/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The induced-[metric](../../../../../metric.md) balls are intersections with the corresponding ambient balls. On the horizontal arm the distance from the centre is $|x-1/2|$; on the vertical arm it is $\max(1/2,y)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{B_E=((0,1)\times\{0\}),\qquad\overline B_E=([0,1]\times\{0\})\cup(\{0\}\times[0,1/2]).}
$$

The [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) relative to $E$ of the [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) is $[0,1]\times\{0\}$. Every point $(0,y)$ with $y>0$ has positive distance $y$ from that horizontal arm, so none of those points is in the [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md). In particular $(0,1/4)$ lies in the [closed ball](../../../../../closed-ball.md) but not in the [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) of the [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md), proving **strict inclusion**.
