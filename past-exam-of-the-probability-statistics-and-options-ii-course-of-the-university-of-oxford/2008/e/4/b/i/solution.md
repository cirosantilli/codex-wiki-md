<h1 id="e/4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) subsets of a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) are closed. Regard every $K_n$ as a closed subset of the [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) set $K_1$. Any finite intersection equals the member with largest index and is therefore nonempty. The [finite intersection property](../../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md) for closed subsets of a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) space implies

$$
\boxed{\bigcap_{n\ge1}K_n\ne\varnothing.}
$$

Equivalently, an empty intersection would give an open cover of $K_1$ by their complements, with a finite subcover contradicting nested nonemptiness.
