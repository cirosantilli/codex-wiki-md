<h1 id="e/4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose no such $n_0$ exists. Nestedness then makes every $K_n\setminus U$ nonempty. These are nested [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) sets: $X\setminus U$ is closed and $K_n$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). By (i) their intersection is nonempty. But that intersection is $(\bigcap_nK_n)\setminus U$, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus $K_{n_0}\subseteq U$ for some $n_0$, and nestedness gives $\boxed{K_n\subseteq U\text{ for all }n\ge n_0}$ immediately.
