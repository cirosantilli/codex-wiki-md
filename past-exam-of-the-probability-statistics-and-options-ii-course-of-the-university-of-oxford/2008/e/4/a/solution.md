↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the usual [real line](../../../../../real-line.md) take $\boxed{F_n=[n,\infty)}$. Each set is nonempty and closed, and $F_{n+1}\subseteq F_n$. No real number lies in them all, because the positive integers are unbounded. Thus their intersection is empty. These sets are not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md).
