↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the Probability, statistics and options II course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [Mathematics release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), supplied bundle `Part A 2007 11-1.pdf`, pages 309–327. This nineteen-page Mathematics and Statistics paper is dated 30 June 2011. All 23 questions and all 86 printed leaf parts are addressed below, including a counterexample and explicit correction for the false algorithmic assertion in S2(a).

The department's [answer-release policy](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/undergraduate-courses/examinations-assessments) excludes ordinary odd years. Bounded exact-paper checks found no [matching](../matching-graph-theory-split.md) advertisement of worked answers. No source part is skipped.
