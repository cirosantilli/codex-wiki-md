<h1 id="b/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A primitive seventh [root of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) $\zeta_7$ generates all roots. Its [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) is the permitted irreducible [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) $1+X+\cdots+X^6$. Hence the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) has

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta_7):\mathbb Q]=6.}
$$
