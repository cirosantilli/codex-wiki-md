↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The sixth [roots of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) all belong to $\mathbb Q(\zeta_6)$, where $\zeta_6=(1+\sqrt{-3})/2$. Its [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) is $X^2-X+1$, which has no rational root. Thus the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ and

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=2.}
$$
