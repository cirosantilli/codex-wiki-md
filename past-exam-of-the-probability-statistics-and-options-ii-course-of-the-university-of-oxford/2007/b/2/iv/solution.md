<h1 id="b/2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $X^3+X+1$ has no root in $\mathbb F_2$, so it is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md). The [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) of $\mathbb F_2[X]$ by the [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) it generates is therefore a [field](../../../../../field.md) with eight elements. The seven nonzero elements form a [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of order seven, so each satisfies $x^7=1$ by [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md). Thus this [field](../../../../../field.md) splits $X^7-1$. A root different from one has order seven; it cannot lie in $\mathbb F_2$ and generates this degree-three [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md), since three is prime and the [tower law](../../../../../tower-law.md) applies. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb F_2]=3.}
$$
