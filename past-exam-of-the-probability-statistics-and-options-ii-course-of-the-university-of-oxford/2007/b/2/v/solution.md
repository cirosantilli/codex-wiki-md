In [characteristic two](../../../../../characteristic-two.md), the [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) gives $X^8-1=(X-1)^8$. The only root is already in $\mathbb F_2$, so the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is the original [field](../../../../../field.md), despite the repeated root:

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb F_2]=1.}
$$
