↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The second part of the [Gershgorin circle theorem](../../../../../gershgorin-circle-theorem.md) states that if a union of $k$ of the discs is disjoint from every remaining disc, that union contains exactly $k$ [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), counted with [algebraic multiplicity](../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md). In particular, a single isolated disc contains precisely one [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Connected components of the full union therefore carry the corresponding disc counts.
