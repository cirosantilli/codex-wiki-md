<h1 id="p/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Gershgorin discs](../../../../../gershgorin-disc.md) of $A^{\mathsf T}$ have radii $1/4,q,\ldots,q,\epsilon$. They too are disjoint and have one [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) each. The disc centered at $n$ therefore improves the final bound to

$$
\boxed{n-\epsilon\le\lambda_n\le n+\epsilon.}
$$

It selects the same [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) as before because all other discs lie a fixed positive distance from the disc centered at $n$.
