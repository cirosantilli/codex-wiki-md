<h1 id="t/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At the current dual prices $(1,1)$, the new product has [reduced cost](../../../../../reduced-cost.md) $5-(1+2)=2>0$, so it can increase profit. Its transformed resource column is

$$
B^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}3&-5\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-7\\3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Increasing $x_4$ by $t$ changes the old [basic variables](../../../../../basic-variable.md) to $x_1=9+7t$ and $x_3=3-3t$. Thus $x_3$ leaves at $t=1$, giving

$$
\boxed{(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=(16,0,0,1),\qquad z_{\max}=53.}
$$

For a certificate, take dual prices $(1/3,7/3)$. They price the four products at $3,17/3,26/3,5$, respectively, each at least its profit. Their resource total is $33/3+18(7/3)=53$, equal to the achieved profit. [Weak duality](../../../../../weak-duality.md) proves optimality, and the profit has increased by two.
