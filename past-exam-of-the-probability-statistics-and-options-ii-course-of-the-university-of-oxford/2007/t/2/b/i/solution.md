↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For this tableau sign convention, a negative coefficient in the objective row means the corresponding nonbasic [variable](../../../../../../variable-mathematics.md) can increase profit. Initially $x_3$ has the most negative coefficient. The [simplex ratio test](../../../../../../simplex-ratio-test.md) gives $33/5$ in row one and $18/3=6$ in row two, so row two leaves to preserve feasibility. Next $x_1$ is the only column with a negative objective coefficient. The ratios are $3/(1/3)=9$ and $6/(1/3)=18$, so row one leaves. All resulting [basic variables](../../../../../../basic-variable.md) remain nonnegative.
