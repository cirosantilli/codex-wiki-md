<h1 id="t/2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the third profit coefficient be $c_3$. For the same [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), the resource dual prices solve $2y_1+y_2=3$ and $5y_1+3y_2=c_3$, giving

$$
y_1=9-c_3,\qquad y_2=2c_3-15.
$$

Dual feasibility requires $y_1,y_2\ge0$ and $3y_1+2y_2\ge4$. These inequalities are $c_3\le9$, $c_3\ge15/2$ and $c_3\ge7$, respectively. Hence

$$
\boxed{15/2\le c_3\le9.}
$$

The production [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is unchanged because the resource constraints are unchanged. Equality at a boundary can allow additional optimal solutions.
