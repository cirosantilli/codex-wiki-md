The [change of variables formula](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) for a [Lebesgue integrable function](../../../../../lebesgue-integrable-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_{T(U)}f(u,v)\,du\,dv
=\int_U f(T(x,y))\,|\det DT(x,y)|\,dx\,dy.}
$$

The absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) accounts for area regardless of orientation. The formula also holds for nonnegative measurable functions with possibly infinite [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md).
