A [coordinate transformation](../../../../../smooth-transition-map.md) is a continuously differentiable [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) from the [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U$ onto an open subset $T(U)\subset\mathbb R^2$, with continuously differentiable inverse. Equivalently, it is a $C^1$ [diffeomorphism](../../../../../diffeomorphism.md); its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) never vanishes. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) supplies the local inverse, while global [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md) is an additional requirement.
