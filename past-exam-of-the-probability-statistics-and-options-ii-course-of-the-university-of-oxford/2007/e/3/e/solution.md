↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**False.** Let $X$ be a two-point set with the [indiscrete topology](../../../../../indiscrete-topology.md), let $Y$ be a one-point space, and let $f$ be the unique map. The space $X$ is finite and hence [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md); its only nonempty open subset is all of $X$, so it is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md). It is not [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), whereas $Y$ is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) and $f$ is a continuous [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md).
