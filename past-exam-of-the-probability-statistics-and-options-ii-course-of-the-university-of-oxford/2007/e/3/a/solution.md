<h1 id="e/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True.** If open sets cover $Y$, their [inverse images](../../../../../preimage.md) under the continuous [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) $f$ cover $X$. [Compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) of $X$ gives a finite subcover; [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) then makes the corresponding finite family cover $Y$. This proves the [compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) of a [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md).
