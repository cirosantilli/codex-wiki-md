↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**True.** The [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) assumption makes $\{y\}$ closed in $Y$. Its [inverse image](../../../../../preimage.md) under the [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f$ is closed in the [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) $X$, and a [closed subset of a compact space](../../../../../closed-subset-of-a-compact-space.md) is compact. This is the [compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) of the fiber with its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md).
