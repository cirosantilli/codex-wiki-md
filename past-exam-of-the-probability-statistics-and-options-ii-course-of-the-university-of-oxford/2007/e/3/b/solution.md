↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**True.** A separation of $Y$ into two disjoint nonempty open subsets would pull back under the continuous [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) $f$ to a separation of $X$. The [inverse images](../../../../../preimage.md) are nonempty by [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md), contradicting [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) of $X$. Thus a [continuous image of a connected space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-connected-space.md) is connected.
