A [topological space](../../../../topological-space.md) is [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) if every open cover has a finite subcover. It is [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) if it cannot be written as the union of two disjoint nonempty open subsets. It is [Hausdorff](../../../../hausdorff-space.md) if every two distinct points have disjoint open neighborhoods. These definitions apply to the space itself and, with the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md), to its subsets.
