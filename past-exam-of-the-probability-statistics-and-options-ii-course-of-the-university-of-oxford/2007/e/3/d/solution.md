↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**False.** Take $X=[0,1]$, $Y=S^1$ and the [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) $f(t)=e^{2\pi it}$. The interval is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), and the circle is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). But $f^{-1}(1)=\{0,1\}$ is a disconnected two-point subspace. Thus a continuous [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) need not have connected fibers.
