<h1 id="g/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parametrize the [surface of revolution](../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md) by

$$
\mathbf r(x,\theta)=(x,y(x)\cos\theta,y(x)\sin\theta),\qquad0\le\theta\le2\pi.
$$

The magnitude of the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) $\mathbf r_x\times\mathbf r_\theta$ is $y\sqrt{1+y'^2}$. Integrating the [surface area element](../../../../../surface-area-element.md) over $\theta$ gives

$$
\boxed{J[y]=2\pi\int_{x_1}^{x_2}y(x)\sqrt{1+y'(x)^2}\,dx.}
$$

Positivity of $y$ removes an otherwise necessary absolute value in the area element.
