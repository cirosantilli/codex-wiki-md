# Digit sum

↑ **Parent:** [Positional notation](positional-notation.md)

The digit sum of a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) in a given base is the sum of its [numerical digits](numerical-digit.md). In base $b$, replacing each power $b^j$ by $1$ shows that the [integer](integer-split.md) and its digit sum are congruent [modulo](modulo-operation.md) $b-1$.
