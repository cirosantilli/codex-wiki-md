# Algebraic independence

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_independence)

Algebraic independence means the absence of a nonzero polynomial relation over the base [field](field.md). It extends the distinction between algebraic and transcendental elements to finite tuples.
