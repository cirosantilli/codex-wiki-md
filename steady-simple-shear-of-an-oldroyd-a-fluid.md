# Steady simple shear of an Oldroyd-A fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Oldroyd-A model](oldroyd-a-model.md)

For constant [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md) rate $s$, the nonpressure stress in the component-first gradient convention $L_{12}=s$ is

$$
\sigma^d=\begin{pmatrix}0&\mu s&0\\\mu s&-2(\mu-\mu_r)\tau s^2&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the first [normal-stress difference](normal-stress-difference.md) is $N_1=2(\mu-\mu_r)\tau s^2$, while the second is $N_2=-N_1$. Adding the incompressibility [pressure](pressure.md) changes neither difference.
