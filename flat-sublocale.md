# Flat sublocale

↑ **Parent:** [Sublocale](sublocale.md)

A [sublocale](sublocale.md) is flat when its [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) preserves finite joins, including $0$. Equivalently its fixed elements are closed under ambient finite joins. In particular every flat sublocale is a [dense sublocale](dense-sublocale.md).
