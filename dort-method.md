# DORT method

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal operator](time-reversal-operator.md)

The DORT method uses [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of a [time reversal operator](time-reversal-operator.md) to focus on individual [point scatterers](point-scatterer.md). A separate nonzero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) corresponds to each scatterer in the ideal weak-scattering, well-resolved limit; coherent overlap and [multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md) can change this correspondence.
