# Spatial resolution element

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction-limited resolution](diffraction-limited-resolution.md)

A [spatial resolution element](spatial-resolution-element.md) is one independently resolved angular or spatial scale under a specified resolution criterion. For an ideal telescope of [diameter](diameter.md) $D$, its angular scale is of order $\lambda/D$ and its angular area of order $(\lambda/D)^2$. A [photodetector](photodetector.md) [detector pixel](optical-detector-pixel.md) or [spaxel](spaxel.md) can be smaller than a resolution element without adding independent spatial information.
