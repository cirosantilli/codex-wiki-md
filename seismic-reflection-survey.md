# Seismic reflection survey

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection](seismic-reflection.md)

A controlled-source survey recording reflected [elastic waves](elastic-wave.md) to infer subsurface interfaces and their properties. [Travel times](travel-time.md) constrain geometry through a [velocity model](velocity-model.md); amplitudes also depend on source, propagation and impedance contrasts.
