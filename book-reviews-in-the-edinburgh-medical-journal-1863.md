# Book Reviews in the Edinburgh Medical Journal (1863)

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography-split.md)

[https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5277139/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5277139/)

January 1863 review, pp. 644–650, of the 1862 Researches on the Nature and Treatment of Diabetes. The review records competing theories of hepatic sugar formation; these are evidence about period beliefs, not all modern conclusions.
