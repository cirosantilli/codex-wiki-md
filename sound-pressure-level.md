# Sound pressure level

↑ **Parent:** [Sound pressure](sound-pressure.md)

The logarithmic level of root-mean-square [sound pressure](sound-pressure.md) relative to a reference, conventionally $20\,\mu\mathrm{Pa}$ in air. A pressure ratio $r$ changes level by $20\log_{10}r$ decibels. The factor twenty follows from the square dependence of [acoustic intensity](acoustic-energy-flux.md) on pressure in a fixed medium, combined with the power-level convention $10\log_{10}$.
