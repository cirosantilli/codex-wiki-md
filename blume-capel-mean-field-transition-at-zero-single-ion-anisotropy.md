# Blume-Capel mean-field transition at zero single-ion anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Blume–Capel model](blume-capel-model.md)

For spins $s_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ with ferromagnetic nearest-neighbour exchange and no single-ion anisotropy, a homogeneous [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) gives

$$
\frac{F_{\rm MF}}N=\frac{zJ}{2}S^2-\frac1\beta\log[1+2\cosh\beta(zJS+B)].
$$

The [self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md) is $S=2\sinh h/(1+2\cosh h)$ with $h=\beta(zJS+B)$. Its small-$h$ expansion is $2h/3-h^3/9+O(h^5)$. Hence $k_BT_c=2zJ/3$ and, with $t=T/T_c-1$, the nonzero branches satisfy $S^2=(8/3)(-t)+O(t^2)$. The [critical exponent](critical-exponent.md) is the mean-field value $1/2$, not an exact two-dimensional exponent. At zero source the finite-system equilibrium magnetization is zero by [spin inversion symmetry](spin-inversion-symmetry.md); the broken branches describe the thermodynamic limit with a phase selected by a vanishing source.
