# Gray stability theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Contact structure](contact-structure.md)

A smooth interval family of [contact forms](contact-form.md) on a compact manifold without boundary is carried back to its initial [contact distribution](contact-distribution.md) by a [smooth isotopy](smooth-isotopy.md). The pullback of each form is a positive smooth multiple of the initial form. Solve for the [horizontal generator of contact stability](horizontal-generator-of-contact-stability.md) and integrate the resulting scalar equation for the [contact conformal factor along an isotopy](contact-conformal-factor-along-an-isotopy.md).
