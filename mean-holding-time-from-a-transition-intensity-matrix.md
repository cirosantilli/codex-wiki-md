# Mean holding time from a transition intensity matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Holding time](holding-time.md)

A state's [holding time](holding-time.md) in a time-homogeneous [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is exponentially distributed with rate $-q_{rr}>0$. Its mean is $-1/q_{rr}$; the [probability](probability.md) that its next jump is to state $s$ is $q_{rs}/(-q_{rr})$. An absorbing state has infinite holding time.
