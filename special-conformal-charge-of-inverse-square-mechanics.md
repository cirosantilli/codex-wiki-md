# Special conformal charge of inverse-square mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal mechanics](conformal-mechanics.md)

For the extended canonical variables with $\{t,E\}=-1$ and $\{x,p\}=1$, the charge $K=t^2E-txp+x^2/2$ generates a special conformal transformation. Its time derivative is $2te(E-H)$, so it is conserved on the [constraint surface](constraint-surface.md). With a time-dependent parameter $\beta$, the multiplier varies as $\delta e=-2te\beta$ and the action variation is $\dot\beta K-d(\beta x^2/2)/d\lambda$.
