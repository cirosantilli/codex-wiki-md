# Operator norm duality

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)

For bounded operators between [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md), write the norm as the supremum of $|\langle Ax,y\rangle|$ over unit vectors $x,y$. Moving the operator to the other slot proves equality of its norm with that of its [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md). Applied to the exponential matrix, this makes the primal and dual [analytic large sieve inequalities](exponential-sum-large-sieve.md) equivalent.
