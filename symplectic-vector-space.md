# Symplectic vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_vector_space)

A [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) is a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) equipped with a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) $\omega$ that is an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md): $\omega(v,v)=0$ for every [vector](vector.md) $v$. This implies $\omega(v,w)=-\omega(w,v)$. When the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is two, that latter equation alone does not imply the alternating condition. A [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) of finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) has even [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md): splitting off a [hyperbolic pair](hyperbolic-pair.md) leaves a smaller [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), and repeating gives a decomposition into two-dimensional planes.
