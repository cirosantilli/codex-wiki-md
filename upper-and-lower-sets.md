# Upper and lower sets

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_and_lower_sets)

In a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) $P$, an upper [set](set-split.md) $U$ satisfies $x\in U$ and $x\leq y\Rightarrow y\in U$; a lower [set](set-split.md) $L$ satisfies $x\in L$ and $y\leq x\Rightarrow y\in L$. The [complement of a set](complement-of-a-set.md) exchanges the two conditions: if $x\notin U$ and $y\leq x$, then $y\in U$ would force $x\in U$, so $P\setminus U$ is lower. In a [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) ordered by inclusion, these are the [up-set](up-set.md) and [down-set](down-set.md) conditions.
