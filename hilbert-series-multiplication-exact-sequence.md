# Hilbert series multiplication exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Serre theorem](hilbert-serre-theorem.md)

For a homogeneous element $x$ of degree $e>0$ acting on a finite [graded module](graded-module.md), set $N=(0:_Mx)$ and $C=M/xM$. The multiplication exact sequence $0\to N(-e)\to M(-e)\to M\to C\to0$ and additivity of component lengths give $(1-t^e)P_M=P_C-t^eP_N$. This is the inductive step in the [Hilbert-Serre theorem](hilbert-serre-theorem.md); the [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) term must be kept unless multiplication by $x$ is injective.
