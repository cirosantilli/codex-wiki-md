# Trace form on scalar-plus-skew-Hermitian two-by-two matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian matrix representation of Minkowski four-vectors](hermitian-matrix-representation-of-minkowski-four-vectors.md)

The real matrix space defined by $C+C^*=\operatorname{tr}(C)I$ consists exactly of $C=aI+i\mathbf v\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, with $a$ and the three components of $\mathbf v$ real. The [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) have $\operatorname{tr}(\sigma_i\sigma_j)=2\delta_{ij}$, giving the displayed real symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) of signature $(3,1)$. The real isomorphism $C\mapsto aI-\mathbf v\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ to [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) preserves this form when the latter have the polarized negative-determinant form $[\operatorname{tr}(AB)-\operatorname{tr}A\operatorname{tr}B]/2$.
