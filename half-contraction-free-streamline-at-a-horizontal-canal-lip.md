# Half-contraction free streamline at a horizontal canal lip

↑ **Parent:** [Hodograph plane](hodograph-plane.md)

A semi-infinite horizontal plate at unit height and a floor at zero give a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) strip of height $a$ and an upper unit [semicircle](semicircle.md) in the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) $q$. Their matching [half-plane](half-plane.md) maps give $(1+e^{\pi w/a})^{-1}=((q-1)/(q+1))^2$. Along the [fluid free boundary](fluid-free-boundary.md) put $q=e^{-i\theta}$, $-\pi<\theta<0$. Then $dz/d\theta=-(a/\pi)e^{i\theta}\cot(\theta/2)$ and $y=1-(a/\pi)(\theta+\pi+\sin\theta)$. Its downstream limit is $1-a$, which must equal the downstream depth $a$. Thus the contraction is exactly one half.
