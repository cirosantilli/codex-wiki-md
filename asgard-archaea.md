# Asgard archaea

↑ **Parent:** [Archaea](archaea.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asgard_archaea)

[Asgard archaea](asgard-archaea.md) are an archaeal group important in studies of [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) origins. Molecular comparisons connect their [genes](gene.md) to the nuclear lineage of [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md), rather than making modern [Asgard archaea](asgard-archaea.md) the direct ancestors of modern [animals](animal.md) or [plants](plant.md). See the original analysis cited under [two-domain system](two-domain-system.md).
