# Block idempotents centralize a normal p-subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer morphism](brauer-morphism.md)

For a normal [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) $P\triangleleft G$, every [central idempotent](central-idempotent.md) $e$ of $kG$ satisfies $e=\operatorname{Br}_P(e)\in kC_G(P)$. Indeed a nonfixed conjugation orbit sum maps to zero in $k(G/P)$, so $e-\operatorname{Br}_P(e)$ belongs to the [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md) $J(kP)kG$. Normality makes the Brauer image a [central idempotent](central-idempotent.md) of $kG$, and two central idempotents congruent modulo a nilpotent ideal coincide. Consequently the [blocks of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) of $kG$ correspond to $G$-orbits of blocks of $kC_G(P)$.
