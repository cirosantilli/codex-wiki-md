# Local spin in an azimuthal power-law flow

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity-split.md)

Away from the axis, the [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) formula for [curl](curl.md) gives $r^{-1}\partial_r(rF_\theta)=2(1-n)r^{-2n}$ when $F_\theta=r^{1-2n}$. Its sign changes at $n=1$, even though the tangential [velocity](velocity.md) stays positive. Thus local [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), one half of [vorticity](vorticity-split.md), can oppose orbital circulation. For $n=1$, the field is a [line vortex](line-vortex.md) with zero ordinary [curl](curl.md) off the axis and circulation $2\pi$ around it. [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) applies on annuli excluding the axis with both boundary components included; it cannot be applied to a spanning disc containing the field singularity.
