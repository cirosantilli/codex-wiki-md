# Velocity moments of an isotropic Mestel disk

↑ **Parent:** [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md)

The planar [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md) has $F(E)\propto e^{-E/v_0^2}$, giving independent centered [Gaussian distributions](normal-distribution.md) for $v_R,v_\phi$ with [velocity dispersions](velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma_R=\sigma_\phi=v_0$. Its [streaming velocity](streaming-velocity.md) is zero. A [maximally prograde stellar distribution](maximally-prograde-stellar-distribution.md) instead has mean $\sqrt{2/\pi}v_0$ and azimuthal [variance](variance-split.md) $v_0^2(1-2/\pi)$, while all second raw moments remain unchanged.
