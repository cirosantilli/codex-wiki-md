# Approximate mean limit of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

An [approximate mean limit of a function](approximate-mean-limit-of-a-function.md) is a value satisfying the displayed condition. Functions in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) have such finite limits outside an [approximate discontinuity set](approximate-discontinuity-set.md) up to negligible sets in the appropriate [measure](measure.md). Their [approximate jump points](approximate-jump-point.md) instead have two distinct half-ball limits.
