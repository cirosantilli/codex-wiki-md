# Separable polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_polynomial)

A polynomial over a field is separable when it has no repeated root in a splitting field. Equivalently, it is coprime to its [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md).
