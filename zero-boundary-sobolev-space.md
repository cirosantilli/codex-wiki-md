# Zero-boundary Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md)

The zero-boundary Sobolev space $H_0^1(U)$ is the closure of the [space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md) $C_c^\infty(U)$ in $H^1(U)$. Under standard regularity assumptions its elements have zero boundary trace.
