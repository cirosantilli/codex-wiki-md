# Coleman norm-fixed lift

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md)

Every [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) $\overline f\in\mathbb F_p[[T]]^\times$ has a unique [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md)-fixed lift to $\mathbb Z_p[[T]]^\times$. Choose any lift $f$ and use $Nf\equiv f\pmod p$. The consecutive quotients of the iterates are $N^n(Nf/f)\in1+p^{n+1}R$ by [Coleman norm contraction](coleman-norm-contraction.md), so they converge coefficientwise uniformly in the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md). Their limit is norm-fixed by continuity. Two such lifts have norm-fixed quotient in $1+pR$ and therefore coincide. Uniqueness also makes the lifting map multiplicative and independent of the initial lift.
