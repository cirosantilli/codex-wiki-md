# Helium flash

↑ **Parent:** [Core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helium_flash)

A helium flash is a thermally unstable ignition of [helium](helium.md) in an electron-degenerate stellar core. A nondegenerate intermediate-mass core can expand and regulate ignition, so a usual isolated star of five [solar masses](solar-mass.md) begins [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md) without this flash.
