# Brownian representation of half-plane capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

If $\tau$ is the first exit time of planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) from $\mathbb H\setminus A$, then

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(A)=\lim_{y\to\infty}y\,\mathbb E_{iy}[\operatorname{Im}B_\tau].
$$

It follows by applying the [optional sampling theorem for a supermartingale](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) to the harmonic function $\operatorname{Im}(z-g_A(z))$.
