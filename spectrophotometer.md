# Spectrophotometer

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrophotometry](spectrophotometry.md)

A [spectrophotometer](spectrophotometer.md) measures [radiant power](radiant-flux.md) in selected [wavelength](wavelength.md) bands. Comparing a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) in a [cuvette](cuvette.md) with a [reference blank](reference-blank.md) gives [transmittance](transmittance.md) and [absorbance](absorbance.md).
