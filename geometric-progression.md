# Geometric progression

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_progression)

A geometric progression is a [sequence](sequence.md) whose successive terms have a constant ratio: $a_n=ar^n$ for constants $a$ and $r$.
