# Projective transitivity of a trinomial linearized polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized polynomial over a finite field](linearized-polynomial-over-a-finite-field.md)

For $d\geq2$, nonzero roots define projective-point coordinates $s=X^{q-1}$, satisfying $s^N+s+T=0$, where $N=(q^d-1)/(q-1)$. This [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is irreducible over $\mathbb F_q(T)$. After fixing one root $s$, the remaining coordinates satisfy $R(Y,s)=(Y^N-s^N)/(Y-s)+1$. Substitute $z=Y/s$ and $h=1/s$: the equation becomes $h^{N-1}+(z^N-1)/(z-1)=0$. At a nontrivial $N$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md) the second term has a simple zero, so the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is Eisenstein over the [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md)'s [rational function field](rational-function-field.md). This proves irreducibility of $R$ and hence two-transitivity on projective points.
