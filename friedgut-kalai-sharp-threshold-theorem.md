# Friedgut-Kalai sharp threshold theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of Boolean functions](analysis-of-boolean-functions.md)

There is an absolute $C>0$ such that any nontrivial [transitive increasing event](transitive-increasing-event.md) on $n\geq2$ independent bits satisfies $\mu_q(A)>1-\varepsilon$ if $\mu_p(A)>\varepsilon$, $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, and the displayed bound holds with $p<q<1$. A uniform weighted [Kahn-Kalai-Linial theorem](kahn-kalai-linial-theorem.md) gives one pivotal probability at least $c\mu_r(A)(1-\mu_r(A))\log(n)/n$. Transitivity makes the [total influence](total-influence.md) at least $c\mu_r(A)(1-\mu_r(A))\log n$. The [Margulis–Russo formula](margulis-russo-formula.md) identifies this with the derivative of $\mu_r(A)$. Integration of the derivative of its log odds proves the threshold bound.
