# Constant-fraction mushy-layer similarity solution

↑ **Parent:** [Mushy layer](mushy-layer.md)

For a diffusion-controlled [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) of constant [solid fraction](solid-fraction.md) $\phi$, a sharp moving edge $h=2\lambda\sqrt{\kappa t}$ reduces the thermal [heat equation](heat-equation.md) to $\theta''+2\eta\theta'=0$ and the exterior solute [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) to $\mathcal C''+2Le\eta\mathcal C'=0$, where $Le=\kappa/D$ is the [Lewis number](lewis-number.md). Wall values select an [error function](error-function.md) in the mush; far-field decay selects [complementary error functions](complementary-error-function.md) in the liquid. Continuity fixes their amplitudes in terms of the interfacial [temperature](temperature-split.md) and $\lambda$. The [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md), solute conservation and [marginal equilibrium at a mush-liquid boundary](marginal-equilibrium-at-a-mush-liquid-boundary.md) give three algebraic/transcendental equations for these two constants and $\phi$. Derivation follows by $\eta_t=-\eta/(2t)$ and $\eta_z=1/(2\sqrt{\kappa t})$, followed by one integration $Y'=Ke^{-a\eta^2}$. Dividing interface equations can discard degenerate branches, so the undivided equations must be retained when a denominator vanishes. Physical admissibility additionally requires $\lambda>0$ and $0\leq\phi\leq1$.
