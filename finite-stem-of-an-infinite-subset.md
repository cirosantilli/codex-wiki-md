# Finite stem of an infinite subset

↑ **Parent:** [Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers](space-of-infinite-subsets-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

A [finite stem](finite-stem-of-an-infinite-subset.md) is a finite initial segment $s$ of an infinite [subset](subset.md) of $\mathbb N$. The associated [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) in the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md) is $[s]=\{X:s\text{ is an initial segment of }X\}$. For an infinite tail $A$ above $\max s$, the [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $[s,A]=\{s\cup B:B\in[A]^\omega\}$ is basic in the [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md). [Finite stems](finite-stem-of-an-infinite-subset.md) allow a fusion argument to fix initial choices while successively thinning the remaining infinite tail.
