# Spherical geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_geometry)

[Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md) studies distances and angles intrinsically on a [sphere](sphere.md). On the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md), the shorter [great circle](great-circle.md) arc between non-antipodal points with unit position [vectors](vector.md) $u,v$ has length $\arccos(u\cdot v)$.
