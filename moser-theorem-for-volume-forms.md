# Moser theorem for volume forms

↑ **Parent:** [Moser's trick](moser-s-trick.md)

On a [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) without boundary, two positive [volume forms](volume-form.md) for the same [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) are related by an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) exactly when their total integrals agree. A positive interpolation and [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) give the [smooth isotopy](smooth-isotopy.md) realizing the equality.
