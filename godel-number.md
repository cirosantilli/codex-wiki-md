<h1 id="godel-number">Gödel number</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel numbering](godel-numbering.md)

A [natural number](natural-number.md) coding a finite syntactic object under a fixed [Gödel numbering](godel-numbering.md). For program descriptions it records a [function in intension](function-in-intension.md); different codes may denote the same [function in extension](function-in-extension.md).
