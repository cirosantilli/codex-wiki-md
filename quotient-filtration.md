# Quotient filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md)

Together with the [subspace filtration](subspace-filtration.md), it gives [exactness of associated graded modules for induced filtrations](exactness-of-associated-graded-modules-for-induced-filtrations.md).
