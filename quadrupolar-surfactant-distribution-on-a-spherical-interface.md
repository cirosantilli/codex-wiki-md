# Quadrupolar surfactant distribution on a spherical interface

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of insoluble surfactant on a moving interface](conservation-of-insoluble-surfactant-on-a-moving-interface.md)

For radius $a$ and tangential velocity $\mathbf u_s=\mathbf I_s\mathbf A\mathbf x$ with symmetric traceless $\mathbf A$, the [surface divergence](surface-divergence.md) is $-3\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf A\mathbf n$. At small surface [Péclet number](peclet-number.md) $a^2|\mathbf A|/D_s$, neglecting advection of the concentration perturbation gives

$$
C-C_0=\frac{C_0a^2}{2D_s}\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf A\mathbf n.
$$

The result follows because this quadratic is a degree-two [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) with [surface Laplacian](surface-laplacian.md) $-6/a^2$ times itself and zero spherical mean.
