# Bishop volume comparison with a positive-curvature model

↑ **Parent:** [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md)

For a complete connected $n$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with $\operatorname{Ric}\geq(n-1)kg$, $k>0$, put $D=\pi/\sqrt k$ and

$$
s_k(t)=\frac{\sin(\sqrt k\,t)}{\sqrt k},\qquad v_k(r)=\omega_{n-1}\int_0^r s_k(t)^{n-1}\,dt.
$$

Here $\omega_{n-1}$ is the area of the unit $(n-1)$-sphere, and $v_k(r)$ is the [Riemannian volume](riemannian-volume.md) of a radius-$r$ ball in the round curvature-$k$ [sphere](sphere.md). The [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md) asserts that $V_p(r)/v_k(r)$ is nonincreasing for $0<r<D$, tends to one at zero, and is at most one. In [geodesic polar coordinates](geodesic-polar-coordinates.md), the radial volume density before the cut time is at most $s_k(t)^{n-1}$. The bound uses trace [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md); with [normalized Ricci curvature](normalized-ricci-curvature.md) it reads $\overline{\operatorname{Ric}}\geq kg$.
