# Ideal sheaf of a point on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Structure sheaf of a complex manifold](structure-sheaf-of-a-complex-manifold.md)

This sheaf consists of [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) vanishing at $p$ on opens containing $p$, and all [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) on opens not containing $p$. Evaluation gives $0\to\mathcal I_p\to\mathcal O_M\to\mathbb C_p\to0$, with a [skyscraper sheaf](skyscraper-sheaf.md) as quotient. On an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), $H^0(\mathcal I_p)=0$, $H^1(\mathcal I_p)\cong\mathbb C$, and all higher cohomology vanishes.
