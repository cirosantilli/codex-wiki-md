# Polypeptide

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)

A [polypeptide](polypeptide.md) is a chain of [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) linked by [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). The term describes chain chemistry; calling a chain a [polypeptide](polypeptide.md) alone does not establish that it is folded or functional. The [gene expression](gene-expression.md) example N–[methionine](methionine.md)–[alanine](alanine.md)–C contains two [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) and one [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md); the twelve-residue example under [protein](protein.md) contains eleven [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). See [IUPAC-IUB: amino-acid symbols and representation of residues in chains](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/AminoAcid/A1416.html).
