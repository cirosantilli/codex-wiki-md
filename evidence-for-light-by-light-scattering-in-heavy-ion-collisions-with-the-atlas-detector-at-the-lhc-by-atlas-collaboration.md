# Evidence for light-by-light scattering in heavy-ion collisions with the ATLAS detector at the LHC by ATLAS Collaboration

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by ATLAS Collaboration](work-by-atlas-collaboration.md)

[https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.01625](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.01625)

The [ATLAS Collaboration](atlas-collaboration.md) reports the [ATLAS evidence for light-by-light scattering in 2017](atlas-evidence-for-light-by-light-scattering-in-2017.md) from [ultra-peripheral collisions](ultra-peripheral-collision.md) of [lead](lead.md) ions. The paper states the event selection and background estimate; its abstract gives the candidate count and the fiducial [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md).
