# Abel map of an algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md)

The [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md) sends an effective [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ to its [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $\mathcal O_C(D)$. Its scheme fibre over a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$ is the [projective space](projective-space-split.md) of one-dimensional subspaces of $H^0(C,L)$. This follows in families: a relative effective [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) in the fixed class determines a nowhere fibre-zero section of $L$ up to a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) from the parameter space, equivalently a line subbundle of $H^0(C,L)\otimes\mathcal O_T$. Thus the scheme fibre is smooth, not merely reduced on its points. The dimensions can nevertheless jump, so the [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md) need not be a smooth morphism.
