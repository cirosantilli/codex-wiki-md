# Trace dual of a fractional ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-dual lattice](trace-dual-lattice.md)

For a nonzero [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md) $\mathfrak b$ of a [number field](number-field.md), its [trace-dual lattice](trace-dual-lattice.md) is the [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md)

$$
\mathfrak b^\vee=\{z\in K:\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(z\mathfrak b)\subseteq\mathbb Z\}=\mathfrak D_{K/\mathbb Q}^{-1}\mathfrak b^{-1}.
$$

The equality follows from the definition of the [inverse different](inverse-different.md) and invertibility of [fractional ideals](fractional-ideal.md). Under the positive metric on the [Minkowski embedding of a number field](minkowski-embedding-of-a-number-field.md), the Euclidean [dual lattice](dual-lattice.md) of $j(\mathfrak b)$ is $\overline{j(\mathfrak b^\vee)}$: coordinatewise [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) converts the positive [inner product](inner-product.md) into the [trace pairing](trace-pairing.md). This assertion does not require that conjugation preserve the number field itself.
