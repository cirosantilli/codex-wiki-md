# Downsizing in galaxy formation

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md)

Downsizing denotes the tendency for the characteristic mass of actively star-forming galaxies to decrease with time, together with related evidence that more massive quiescent systems formed their stars earlier or over shorter intervals. It does not assert that their [dark-matter haloes](dark-matter-halo.md) assembled earlier: stellar formation and halo or stellar assembly are separate histories.
