# Laziness invariance of branching extinction

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson extinction fixed point](galton-watson-extinction-fixed-point.md)

For a [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md), replacing an individual's reproduction by a one-descendant uneventful step with probability $q<1$ changes its offspring [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) to the displayed mixture. Its fixed-point equation reduces to $G(z)=z$, so the smallest fixed point and hence the [extinction probability of a branching process](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md) are unchanged. The mean is $q+(1-q)G'(1)$, which also preserves the subcritical/critical/supercritical classification. The excluded case $q=1$ never reproduces or dies and need not share the extinction probability.
