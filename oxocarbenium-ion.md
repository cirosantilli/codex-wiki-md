# Oxocarbenium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Carbocation](carbocation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxocarbenium_ion)

An [oxocarbenium ion](oxocarbenium-ion.md) is a cationic species stabilized by adjacent oxygen donation, commonly represented by carbon-cation and oxonium resonance contributors. Reversible acetal opening can expose a locally planar carbon for attack from either face.
