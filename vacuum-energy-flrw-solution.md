# Vacuum-energy FLRW solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

The [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p=-\varrho$ makes the [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) imply constant [energy density](energy-density.md) $\varrho_0$. For $\varrho_0>0$, put $H_\Lambda^2=8\pi G\varrho_0/3$. Solving both the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) and [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives

$$
a=Ae^{H_\Lambda t}+Be^{-H_\Lambda t},\qquad 4H_\Lambda^2AB=k.
$$

Up to shifts of [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md) and time reversal, the positive [scale factors](scale-factor-cosmology.md) are $Ae^{H_\Lambda t}$ for $k=0$, $H_\Lambda^{-1}\cosh(H_\Lambda t)$ for $k=1$, and $H_\Lambda^{-1}\sinh(H_\Lambda t)$ on $t>0$ for $k=-1$. They are the flat, closed and open slicings of [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md). The open zero is a coordinate boundary with finite [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) $12H_\Lambda^2$ and finite [Kretschmann scalar](kretschmann-scalar.md) $24H_\Lambda^4$.

For the expanding flat branch, the [cosmological event horizon](cosmological-event-horizon.md) has [comoving radial distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) $\int_t^\infty ds/a(s)=1/[H_\Lambda a(t)]$ and [proper distance in cosmology](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) $H_\Lambda^{-1}$. An event on the horizon reaches the central [comoving observer](comoving-observer.md) only at infinite reception time; events beyond it never do.
