# Barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk

↑ **Parent:** [Razor-thin disk approximation](razor-thin-disk-approximation.md)

A two-dimensional disk equation with [pressure](pressure.md) acceleration $-\Sigma^{-1}\nabla P$ requires an equation of state for vertically integrated [pressure](pressure.md) $P$ in terms of [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma$. A [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) closure is $P=P(\Sigma)$, with linear enthalpy perturbation $h'=c_s^2\Sigma'/\Sigma_0$. Merely giving $P=P(\rho)$ for a volume density does not close the two-dimensional equations unless a vertical conversion $\rho(\Sigma)$ is specified. Positive $c_s^2$ gives ordinary [pressure](pressure.md) restoration of an [inertial-acoustic wave](inertial-acoustic-wave.md).
