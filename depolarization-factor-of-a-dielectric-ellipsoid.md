# Depolarization factor of a dielectric ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Depolarization field](depolarization-field.md)

For a homogeneous linear isotropic [dielectric](dielectric.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md), $P_i=\epsilon_0(\epsilon_r-1)E_{{\rm in},i}$. Combining this with the [depolarization field](depolarization-field.md) and the imposed uniform [electric field](electric-field-split.md) gives $E_{{\rm in},i}=E_{0,i}/[1+N_i(\epsilon_r-1)]$. A [sphere](sphere.md) has $N_i=1/3$. An infinite slab has $N=1$ normal to its faces and $N=0$ in its plane. A very long circular rod has $N=0$ along its axis and $N=1/2$ transverse to it. Slab and rod statements are limiting geometries, with end effects neglected; a finite cylinder does not generally have an exactly uniform internal [electric field](electric-field-split.md).
