# Razor-thin fluid disk dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Spiral density wave](spiral-density-wave.md)

For tightly wound perturbations in a locally uniform, razor-thin [gas](gas.md) disk, [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and rotating-fluid [momentum](momentum-split.md) equations couple the surface-density perturbation to radial and azimuthal velocities. The sheet potential is $\delta\Phi=-2\pi G\delta\Sigma/|k|$. Eliminating these perturbations gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) supplies the [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) term, the sound-speed term resists short waves, and [self-gravity](self-gravity.md) supplies the negative term. For an axisymmetric mode $n=0$, use $\omega^2$ directly; the [pattern speed](pattern-speed.md) $\omega/n$ is undefined.
