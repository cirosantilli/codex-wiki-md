# Optically thick medium

↑ **Parent:** [Optical depth](optical-depth.md)

An optically thick medium has [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $\tau\gg1$ along the relevant path. Photons undergo many interactions before escaping. [Radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) applies under suitable near-isotropy conditions, but a large scattering depth alone does not guarantee thermalization when true absorption is weak.
