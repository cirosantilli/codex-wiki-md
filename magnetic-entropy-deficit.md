# Magnetic entropy deficit

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic entropy](magnetic-entropy.md)

The [magnetic entropy deficit](magnetic-entropy-deficit.md) is the [entropy](entropy-split.md) increase still available to independent moments as their field [polarization](polarization-waves.md) is removed. For a spin-$J$ multiplet, $s_{\max}=k_B\ln(2J+1)$ and the high-temperature deficit is $k_BJ(J+1)[g\mu_BB/(k_BT)]^2/6$. Isothermal [magnetization](magnetization.md) creates this deficit while rejecting heat to a bath. Subsequent reversible demagnetization can absorb [entropy](entropy-split.md) from a cooling load. The deficit, not the initial absolute [magnetic entropy](magnetic-entropy.md), measures that capacity.
