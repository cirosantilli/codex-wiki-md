# Isotropic transverse covariance spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Hankel transform](hankel-transform.md)

For the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) convention $C(\mathbf s)=\int\Phi(\boldsymbol\kappa)e^{i\boldsymbol\kappa\cdot\mathbf s}d^2\kappa/(2\pi)^2$, angular integration of an isotropic [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md) gives the displayed [Hankel transform](hankel-transform.md). A purely radial Hankel transform without the prefactor corresponds to defining its spectrum as $\Phi/(2\pi)$.
