# Strictly increasing concave function

↑ **Parent:** [Concave function](concave-function.md)

Let a differentiable [concave function](concave-function.md) on all of $\mathbb R^d$ be strictly increasing in each coordinate separately. Every coordinate derivative is strictly positive. Indeed it is nonnegative by monotonicity and nonincreasing along its coordinate by [concavity](concave-function.md); a zero value would force zero derivative on the remaining positive ray and hence a constant segment. The global-domain assumption matters at endpoints of restricted domains.
