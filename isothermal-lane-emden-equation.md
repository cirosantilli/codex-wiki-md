# Isothermal Lane-Emden equation

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md)

For an [isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md), put $\rho=\rho_ce^{-\Psi}$ and $r=a\xi$ with $a^2=K/(4\pi G\rho_c)$. The equilibrium equation becomes $\xi^{-2}(\xi^2\Psi')'=e^{-\Psi}$. Finite central density and smooth [spherical symmetry](spherical-symmetry.md) require $\Psi(0)=\Psi'(0)=0$. The regular expansion is $\Psi=\xi^2/6-\xi^4/120+\xi^6/1890+O(\xi^8)$. The enclosed mass is $M=4\pi\rho_ca^3\xi^2\Psi'$, following by integrating the differential equation.
