# Damped free drift of an iceberg

↑ **Parent:** [Iceberg free drift](iceberg-free-drift.md)

For a constant wind force $\mathbf F$, still water, and linear water [drag force](drag-physics.md) $-m\gamma\mathbf v$, let $J\mathbf v=\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf v$. The [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) $f$ gives equilibrium $\mathbf v_\infty=(\gamma\mathbf F-fJ\mathbf F)/[m(\gamma^2+f^2)]$. The deviation evolves as $e^{-\gamma t}R(-ft)(\mathbf v(0)-\mathbf v_\infty)$, a damped [inertial oscillation](inertial-oscillation.md). Actual drag is commonly quadratic, so this is a linear illustrative model rather than a universal iceberg speed law.
