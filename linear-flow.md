# Linear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Flow map](flow-map.md)

For a constant real square [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, the [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md) $\dot x=Ax$ has the [linear flow](linear-flow.md) $\varphi_t(x)=e^{tA}x$, where $e^{tA}$ is the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md). The identity $e^{(t+s)A}=e^{tA}e^{sA}$ gives the [flow map](flow-map.md) composition law, and $e^{-tA}$ reverses the evolution. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is $e^{t\operatorname{tr}A}$, so the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) gives the instantaneous fractional rate of [volume](volume.md) change, as illustrated in [trace and local volume change](trace-and-local-volume-change.md). A zero trace preserves volume even when the flow stretches one direction and contracts another.
