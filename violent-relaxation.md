# Violent relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

[Violent relaxation](violent-relaxation.md) is rapid redistribution of the orbital energies of collisionless matter in a time-dependent collective gravitational potential. It can occur during collapse or a [major galaxy merger](major-galaxy-merger.md). It differs from collisional two-body relaxation and from [phase mixing](phase-mixing.md) in a stationary potential; it does not imply perfect final thermal equilibrium.
