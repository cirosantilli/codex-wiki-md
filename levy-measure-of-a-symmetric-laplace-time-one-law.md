<h1 id="levy-measure-of-a-symmetric-laplace-time-one-law">Lévy measure of a symmetric Laplace time-one law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md)

For $\alpha>0$, the [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\mathbb E e^{iuX_t}=(1+u^2/\alpha^2)^{-t}$ has zero Gaussian coefficient, zero uncompensated drift, and the displayed [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md). Indeed, the symmetric jump integral is $2\int_0^\infty(\cos(uy)-1)e^{-\alpha y}dy/y$. Differentiating in $u$ gives $-2u/(\alpha^2+u^2)$, and integrating back from zero gives $-\log(1+u^2/\alpha^2)$. The time-one law is the [Laplace distribution](laplace-distribution.md) of rate $\alpha$. The jump activity is infinite, since $K$ has infinite mass near zero, but the jump variation is finite because $\int(1\wedge|y|)K(dy)<\infty$.
