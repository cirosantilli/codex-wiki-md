# Last surviving characteristic of a settling fan

↑ **Parent:** [Curved settling fronts within a rarefaction fan](curved-settling-fronts-within-a-rarefaction-fan.md)

If both [shocks](shock-wave.md) meet inside a [settling rarefaction fan](settling-rarefaction-fan.md), $t_c=(\sqrt A+\sqrt D)^2/u_s$ and the last characteristic has concentration $\phi_*=\sqrt A/(\sqrt A+\sqrt D)$. It runs from the initial internal interface to the final deposit point. This construction requires $b\leq\phi_*\leq a$; otherwise a front exits the fan before final deposition.
