# Heisenberg nilmanifold as a torus mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping torus](mapping-torus.md)

The quotient of the [real Heisenberg group](heisenberg-group.md) by the [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md) is the mapping torus of the torus automorphism

$$
f:S^1\times S^1\to S^1\times S^1,
\qquad f(z,w)=(zw,w).
$$

Its universal cover is the contractible real Heisenberg group, so its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is the integer Heisenberg group.
