# Open-circuit voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical measurement](electrical-measurement.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-circuit_voltage)

The terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) with zero external [electric current](electric-current.md), when a finite value exists. A reading from a finite-input-[electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) approximates it subject to [voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md).
