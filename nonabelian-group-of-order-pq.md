# Nonabelian group of order pq

↑ **Parent:** [Sylow theorems](sylow-theorems.md)

Let $p>q$ be [prime numbers](prime-number.md). A nonabelian group of order $pq$ has a unique Sylow $p$-subgroup and exactly $p$ Sylow $q$-subgroups. Hence the Sylow count satisfies $p\equiv1\pmod q$, so $q\mid p-1$.
