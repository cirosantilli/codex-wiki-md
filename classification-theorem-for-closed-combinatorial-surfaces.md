# Classification theorem for closed combinatorial surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of compact surfaces](classification-of-compact-surfaces.md)

Every [closed combinatorial surface](closed-combinatorial-surface.md) has [geometric realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to exactly one member of the following list: the [sphere](sphere.md) $S^2$; the [connected sum of surfaces](connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) of $g\geq1$ [tori](torus.md); or the [connected sum of surfaces](connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) of $k\geq1$ [real projective planes](real-projective-plane.md). Every member admits a finite [triangulation](triangulation.md). The [orientable](orientable-surface.md) family has [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $2-2g$, including $g=0$ for the [sphere](sphere.md); the [nonorientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md) family has [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $2-k$. Thus [orientability](orientability.md) and [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) determine the [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) type. The first two nonorientable cases are the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) and the [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md). For a finite disconnected [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) satisfying the same local conditions, the unordered list of its [connected components](connected-component.md)' types determines its type.
