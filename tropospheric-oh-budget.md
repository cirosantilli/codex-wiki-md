# Tropospheric OH budget

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

A local or box-model balance between hydroxyl-radical production and effective losses. For fixed production $P$ and pseudo-first-order total loss frequency $K$, $[\mathrm{OH}]=P/K$. Additional hydrogen, [carbon monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md) or [methane](methane.md) consumption can lower [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) if production and other terms stay fixed; [radical](radical-chemistry.md) recycling and spatial transport can alter that simple result.
