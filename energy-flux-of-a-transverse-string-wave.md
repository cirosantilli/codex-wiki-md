# Energy flux of a transverse string wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

The small-slope [energy density](energy-density.md) is $\mathcal E=(\rho y_t^2+Ty_x^2)/2$ and [power](power.md) flux is $P=-Ty_xy_t$. The [wave](wave-split.md) equation gives $\partial_t\mathcal E+\partial_xP=0$. For a right-going [wave](wave-split.md), $P=c\mathcal E$; a harmonic [wave](wave-split.md) has mean flux $\rho c A^2\omega^2/2$.
