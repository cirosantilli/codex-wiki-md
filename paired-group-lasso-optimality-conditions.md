# Paired group Lasso optimality conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Paired group Lasso for Gaussian graph estimation](paired-group-lasso-for-gaussian-graph-estimation.md)

For each unordered pair, $g=(\Theta_{jk},\Theta_{kj})$ and $c=(X_j^{\mathsf T}r_k/n,X_k^{\mathsf T}r_j/n)$ with centred columns and [regression residuals](regression-residual.md). The [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) of the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) is the unit ball at zero and $g/\|g\|_2$ otherwise. These [KKT conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) are necessary and sufficient for the convex paired objective and explain joint edge deletion.
