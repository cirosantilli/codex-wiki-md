# Sphere theorem for three-manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

If an orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) has nonzero $\pi_2$, it contains an embedded essential two-sphere. Consequently a closed orientable [irreducible three-manifold](irreducible-three-manifold.md) with infinite fundamental group is [aspherical](aspherical-space.md): its simply connected noncompact universal cover has zero second homotopy and top ordinary homology, hence is acyclic and contractible by the [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) and [Whitehead theorem](whitehead-theorem.md).
