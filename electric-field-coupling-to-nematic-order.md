# Electric-field coupling to nematic order

↑ **Parent:** [Landau-de Gennes free energy](landau-de-gennes-free-energy.md)

The leading scalar coupling of an [electric field](electric-field.md) to the [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) is quadratic in the field and linear in the tensor. For [uniaxial nematic order](uniaxial-nematic-order.md), it is $-3\chi\lambda E^2(\cos^2\vartheta-1/3)/2$. When $\chi\lambda>0$, minimization aligns the [nematic director](nematic-director.md) with the field axis and adds $-\chi E^2\lambda$ to the scalar [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md).
