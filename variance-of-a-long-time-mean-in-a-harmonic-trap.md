# Variance of a long-time mean in a harmonic trap

↑ **Parent:** [Overdamped harmonic-trap power spectrum](overdamped-harmonic-trap-power-spectrum.md)

For stationary covariance $C(t)=(k_BT/k)e^{-|t|/\tau_k}$, the [mean](expected-value-split.md) over duration $\mathcal T$ has [variance](variance-split.md) $2(k_BT/k)[\tau_k/\mathcal T-(\tau_k/\mathcal T)^2(1-e^{-\mathcal T/\tau_k})]$. At $\mathcal T\gg\tau_k$ this becomes $2(k_BT/k)\tau_k/\mathcal T$. Closely sampled positions remain correlated and cannot be counted as independent.
