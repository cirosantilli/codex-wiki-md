<h1 id="labelled-version-of-kruskal-s-tree-theorem">Labelled version of Kruskal's tree theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal's tree theorem](kruskal-s-tree-theorem.md)

Finite [rooted trees](rooted-tree.md) labelled in a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md) are a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md) under [label-monotone tree embedding](label-monotone-tree-embedding.md). A [minimal bad sequence](minimal-bad-sequence.md) argument makes the collection of proper rooted subtrees a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md); [Higman lemma](higman-s-lemma.md) then compares their child lists while the root labels are compared in the label order.
