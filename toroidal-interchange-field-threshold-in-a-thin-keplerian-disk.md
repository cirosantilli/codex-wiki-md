# Toroidal interchange field threshold in a thin Keplerian disk

↑ **Parent:** [Michael criterion for axisymmetric toroidal-field interchange](michael-criterion-for-axisymmetric-toroidal-field-interchange.md)

For $B_\phi\propto r^p$, constant [density](density.md) and Keplerian $\kappa^2=\Omega^2$, the [Michael criterion for axisymmetric toroidal-field interchange](michael-criterion-for-axisymmetric-toroidal-field-interchange.md) becomes $\Omega^2-2(p-1)v_A^2/r^2$. Instability therefore requires $p>1$ and $v_A^2>r^2\Omega^2/[2(p-1)]$. For a field varying on the radial scale $r$, this is an orbital-speed field, much stronger than a gas-pressure-scale field with $v_A\lesssim c_s\sim H\Omega$ in a [thin disk](thin-disk.md). Steep radial field gradients require the exact derivative criterion rather than this scale estimate. The instability is powered by the toroidal field distribution and is distinct from weak vertical-field [magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md).
