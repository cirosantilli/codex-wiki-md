<h1 id="fluctuation-supported-membrane-particle-gap">Fluctuation-supported membrane--particle gap</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md)

Thermal membrane undulations generate [Helfrich repulsion](helfrich-repulsion.md) from a nearby particle. For a sphere of radius $R$ tightly enclosed by a high-tension membrane,

$$
\delta\simeq\left(\frac{(k_BT)^2R}{64k_c\sigma}\right)^{1/3}.
$$
