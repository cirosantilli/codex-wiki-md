# Cofinality of an increasing ordinal supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Cofinality](cofinality.md)

If $\alpha$ is a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) and $(\gamma_\xi)_{\xi<\alpha}$ is strictly increasing, then $\operatorname{cf}(\sup_{\xi<\alpha}\gamma_\xi)=\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)$. A cofinal subsequence of the index order gives one inequality. Conversely, a cofinal family in the supremum determines a cofinal family of indices by choosing an index past each of its values. This also explains the [cofinality](cofinality.md) of limit-indexed [aleph numbers](aleph-number.md).
