# Prequantization

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric quantization](geometric-quantization.md)

[Prequantization](prequantization.md) realizes a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) using a Hermitian [line bundle](line-bundle.md) with connection whose curvature is $i\omega/\hbar$ in the convention $\iota_{X_f}\omega=df$. Its existence requires integral symplectic periods. On sections, the [Kostant-Souriau prequantum operator](kostant-souriau-prequantum-operator.md) represents every classical observable and its [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md), but the resulting states depend on the full phase space. A [polarization in geometric quantization](polarization-in-geometric-quantization.md) removes this excess of variables.
