# Diffuse X-ray background

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray astronomy](x-ray-astronomy.md)

[Diffuse X-ray background](diffuse-x-ray-background.md) is [X-ray](x-ray.md) emission spread over the instrument's observed sky rather than assigned to a single resolved source. Diffuse appearance at finite [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md) does not prove emission by a continuous medium: many unresolved [point-like astronomical sources](point-like-astronomical-source.md) can also give a smooth signal. Instrumental background is a separate contribution, even though both enter measured counts.
