# Polar area element

↑ **Parent:** [Polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md)

For [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) $x=r\cos\theta$, $y=r\sin\theta$ with $r\geq0$, the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $r$. The [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) therefore gives the [area element](area-element-of-a-surface.md) $dA=r\,dr\,d\theta$. Integrating over the radial interval $r_1(\theta)\leq r\leq r_2(\theta)$ gives $[r_2(\theta)^2-r_1(\theta)^2]/2$ per unit angle. Bounds must describe the intended region without counting it more than once.
