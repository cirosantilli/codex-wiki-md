# Equality condition in the complex triangle inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md)

For nonzero [complex numbers](complex-number.md) $x,y$, equality $|x+y|=|x|+|y|$ holds exactly when $x/y$ is a positive real number, equivalently when the two numbers have the same [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) modulo $2\pi$.
