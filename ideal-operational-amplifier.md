# Ideal operational amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

An [ideal operational amplifier](ideal-operational-amplifier.md) has infinite [open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md) and [input impedance](input-impedance.md), zero [output impedance](output-impedance.md), unlimited response speed and zero [input offset voltage](input-offset-voltage.md) and [input bias current](input-bias-current.md). Its input currents vanish. In a stable linear circuit with [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) and a finite output, its differential input voltage tends to zero as the [open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md) tends to infinity. Thus $i_+=i_-=0$ and $v_+=v_-$ are the familiar conditional circuit rules. The equality of the input voltages cannot be imposed on an open-loop, unstable or saturated circuit.
