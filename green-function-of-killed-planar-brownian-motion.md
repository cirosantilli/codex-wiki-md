# Green function of killed planar Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

The density of expected [Brownian occupation time](brownian-occupation-time.md) before exit from a planar domain. For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) with [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) $\tfrac12\Delta$, it obeys $-\tfrac12\Delta_wG_D(z,w)=\delta_z(w)$ and has singularity $-\pi^{-1}\log|w-z|$. A [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) preserves this [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md): the squared derivative in the [conformal Brownian clock](conformal-brownian-clock.md) cancels the area [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md).
