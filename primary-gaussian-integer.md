# Primary Gaussian integer

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian integer](gaussian-integer.md)

An odd [Gaussian integer](gaussian-integer.md) is primary when it satisfies the displayed congruence. The four units have distinct images in $(\mathbb Z[i]/(1+i)^3)^\times$, a group of order four; hence every odd ideal has a unique primary generator. Indeed $v_{1+i}(-2)=2$ and $v_{1+i}(i-1)=v_{1+i}(-i-1)=1$.
