# Minimum-period offset of a physical pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

With a pivot at distance a from the centre of mass, the [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md) and small-angle restoring torque give $T^2=(4\pi^2/g)[a+I_{\rm CM}/(Ma)]$. Differentiation, or the arithmetic–geometric mean inequality, minimizes the period at the radius of gyration $\sqrt{I_{\rm CM}/M}$. The minimum is $T_{\min}=2\pi\sqrt{2a_{\min}/g}$. The allowed pivot locations must include this point; a constraint keeping the pivot outside the body can move the physical optimum to a boundary.
