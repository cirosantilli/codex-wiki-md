# Magma (algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magma_(algebra))

An [algebraic magma](magma-algebra.md) is a [set](set-split.md) equipped with a [binary operation](binary-operation.md). No [associative property](associative-property.md), [commutativity](commutativity.md), or [identity element](identity-element.md) is required. A [semigroup](semigroup.md) adds the [associative property](associative-property.md); a [monoid](monoid.md) also adds an [identity element](identity-element.md).
