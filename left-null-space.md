# Left null space

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md)

For a real [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, its [left null space](left-null-space.md) consists of the [vectors](vector.md) $w$ with $w^TA=0$, or equivalently $A^Tw=0$. It is the [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the [matrix transpose](transpose.md), and is the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of the [column space](column-space.md). The [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) $Ax=b$ is solvable exactly when $w^Tb=0$ for every such $w$.
