# Pressure tapping

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure coefficient](pressure-coefficient.md)

A small flush opening communicating the local surface [static pressure](static-pressure.md) through tubing to a [manometer](manometer.md) or [pressure transducer](pressure-transducer.md). An upstream-facing [Pitot tube](pitot-tube.md) instead brings incident flow to rest to measure [stagnation pressure](stagnation-pressure.md).
