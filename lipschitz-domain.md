# Lipschitz domain

↑ **Parent:** [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipschitz_domain)

A Lipschitz domain is an open connected subset of [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) whose boundary is locally the graph of a [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) function after rotating and translating coordinates. Locally the domain lies on one side of that graph. This permits corners but excludes boundary cusps that cannot be represented with a finite Lipschitz constant. Bounded Lipschitz domains are a standard setting for [Sobolev spaces](sobolev-space-split.md) and the [Poincaré inequality for total variation](poincare-inequality-for-total-variation.md).
