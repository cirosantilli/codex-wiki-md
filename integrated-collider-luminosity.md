# Integrated collider luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [Collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md)

The time integral of the incident-beam overlap flux, with inverse-area units. For a fixed production [cross section](cross-section-physics.md), the mean produced event count is $N=\mathcal L_{\rm int}\sigma$. A selected count includes efficiency and acceptance, often integrated over final-state kinematics. This is distinct from astrophysical radiative [luminosity](luminosity.md), which has power units.
