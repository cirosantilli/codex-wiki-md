# Polarimeter analyzer

↑ **Parent:** [Polarimeter](polarimeter.md)

A [polarimeter analyzer](polarimeter-analyzer.md) is the rotatable final [polarizer](polarizer.md). Comparing its transmission maximum or extinction minimum with the corresponding blank reading measures the sample's [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md).
