# Weight-four modular forms on Gamma 0 3

↑ **Parent:** [Oldform by argument dilation](oldform-by-argument-dilation.md)

The group $\Gamma_0(3)$ has index four. The [dimension bound for modular forms on a finite-index subgroup](dimension-bound-for-modular-forms-on-a-finite-index-subgroup.md) gives dimension at most two in weight four. The [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md) $E_4(z)$ and its [oldform by argument dilation](oldform-by-argument-dilation.md) $E_4(3z)$ are independent, since their constant coefficients agree while their $q$ coefficients are $240$ and zero.
