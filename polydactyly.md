# Polydactyly

↑ **Parent:** [Anatomical digit](anatomical-digit.md)

A condition with additional [anatomical digits](anatomical-digit.md) relative to the relevant standard pattern. In early digit-bearing [vertebrates](vertebrate.md), counts above five show that the modern pentadactyl ground plan cannot simply be projected onto the earliest [limbs](vertebrate-limb.md).
