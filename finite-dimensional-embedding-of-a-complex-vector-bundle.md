# Finite-dimensional embedding of a complex vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of complex vector bundles by a Grassmannian](classification-of-complex-vector-bundles-by-a-grassmannian.md)

For finitely many orthonormal [local trivializations](local-trivialization.md) $t_i$ and a subordinate [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) $\rho_i$, the displayed map embeds a rank-k [complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md) isometrically in $X\times\mathbb C^{kr}$. Outside the support of each $\rho_i$ its component is zero. Its continuous image planes give a finite-dimensional [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md) map and identify the original bundle with a tautological pullback.
