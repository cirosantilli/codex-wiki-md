# BABAR time-dependent CP violation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

BABAR used asymmetric [electron](electron.md)-[Positron](positron.md) collisions near the [Upsilon meson](upsilon-meson.md) $4S$ resonance to prepare neutral [B meson](b-meson.md) pairs. It reconstructed one decay into [charmonium](charmonium.md) plus a [kaon](kaon.md), and used [flavour tagging](flavor-tagging.md) on the other [B meson](b-meson.md) with charged [leptons](lepton.md) or [kaons](kaon.md). [Silicon](silicon.md) tracking reconstructed [decay vertices](decay-vertex.md) for the decay-time difference. Self-tagging decays calibrated mistags and time resolution; charged decays provided a control. The tagged time distributions established [CP violation](cp-violation.md), limited by backgrounds, mistags and vertex alignment.[PDF pp. 4–6, Eq. (1), controls, tagging, vertices and systematics](observation-of-cp-violation-in-the-b0-meson-system-by-babar-collaboration.md)

The [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) converts vertex separation to $\Delta t\simeq\Delta z/(\beta_{\rm boost}\gamma_{\rm boost}c)$ at leading order. Unequal beam energies thus make a time difference measurable as a distance.[p. 1023, Section IV, Fig. 5](nobel-lecture-cp-violation-and-flavor-mixing-by-makoto-kobayashi.md)
