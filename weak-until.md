# Weak until

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal operator](temporal-operator.md)

In [linear temporal logic](linear-temporal-logic.md), $\alpha\mathbin{\mathrm W}\beta$ means $(\alpha\mathbin{\mathrm U}\beta)\lor G\alpha$. Thus it permits the left operand to hold forever without the right operand ever becoming true, unlike [strong until](strong-until.md).
