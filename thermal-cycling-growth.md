# Thermal cycling growth

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear materials](nuclear-materials.md)

Permanent dimensional change accumulated over [temperature](temperature-split.md) cycles. [anisotropic thermal expansion](anisotropic-thermal-expansion.md) can create mismatch [stresses](stress.md) in a [polycrystalline material](polycrystalline-material.md), but noncancelling irreversible accommodation is needed for net growth; a freely expanding, uniformly heated elastic [single crystal](single-crystal.md) returns to its original dimensions.
