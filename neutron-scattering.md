# Neutron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron](neutron-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_scattering)

[Neutron scattering](neutron-scattering.md) measures how a specimen changes the directions and [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) of incident [neutrons](neutron-split.md). [Neutron diffraction](neutron-diffraction.md) uses the [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) pattern to investigate [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) and [magnetic order](magnetic-order.md); [inelastic neutron scattering](inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) uses [energy](energy-split.md) transfer to investigate excitations such as [phonons](phonon.md) and [magnons](magnon.md). The two measurements ask complementary questions: what arrangement repeats in space, and what disturbances can propagate through it?

The 1994 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) recognized [Clifford Glenwood Shull](clifford-glenwood-shull.md)'s diffraction work and [Bertram Neville Brockhouse](bertram-neville-brockhouse.md)'s spectroscopy.[1994 award: diffraction and spectroscopy](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1994-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

Nuclear scattering couples to [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md); magnetic scattering couples the [neutron](neutron-split.md)'s [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) to electronic [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The latter underlies both [magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md) and [magnon](magnon.md) spectroscopy. An [atomic number](atomic-number.md) is not a nuclear neutron scattering weight: the relevant coefficient in the elementary coherent model is the [nuclear neutron scattering length](nuclear-neutron-scattering-length.md).
