# Heat current

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

A [heat current](heat-current.md) is a rate of [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) across a specified boundary. Its sign requires a chosen direction; unlike [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md), it is not a rate per unit [area](surface-area.md).
