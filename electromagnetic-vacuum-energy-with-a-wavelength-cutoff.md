# Electromagnetic vacuum energy with a wavelength cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md)

Counting two transverse [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) and [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) $\hbar ck/2$ per mode gives $u=\hbar ck_{\max}^4/(8\pi^2)=2\pi^2\hbar c/\lambda_{\min}^4$. This is a bare cutoff-dependent mode sum, not a classical incident radiation bath or a renormalization-independent absolute observable.
