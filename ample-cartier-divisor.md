# Ample Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Positivity of divisors](positivity-of-divisors.md)

A [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) is ample when its associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md). The [Nakai–Moishezon criterion](nakai-moishezon-criterion.md) and [Kleiman's criterion](kleiman-s-criterion.md) characterize this condition numerically.
