# Photoemission momentum conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy](angle-resolved-photoemission-spectroscopy.md)

For emission angle $\theta$ measured from the surface normal, a free outgoing [photoelectron](photoelectron.md) has parallel [wavevector](wavevector.md) $k_\parallel=\sqrt{2m_eE_{\rm kin}}\sin\theta/\hbar$. Translation symmetry parallel to a clean periodic surface conserves this component modulo a surface [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector, after accounting for photon momentum when needed. With initial [binding energy](binding-energy.md) $E_B$ below the [Fermi level](fermi-level.md), its kinetic energy immediately outside the sample is $E_{\rm kin}=h\nu-\phi_s-E_B$, where $\phi_s$ is the sample [work function](work-function.md). This sample-vacuum energy is distinct from the analyzer-referenced energy when contact potentials are present. Perpendicular crystal momentum generally needs a final-state model, whereas a strictly two-dimensional band has no perpendicular dispersion to reconstruct.
