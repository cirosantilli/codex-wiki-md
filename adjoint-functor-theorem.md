# Adjoint functor theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

A theorem giving sufficient conditions for a [functor](functor.md) to have an [adjoint functor](adjoint-functors.md). The [general adjoint functor theorem](freyd-general-adjoint-functor-theorem.md) uses a [complete category](complete-category.md), [locally small categories](locally-small-category.md), preservation of [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) and a [solution-set condition](solution-set-condition.md). The [Special adjoint functor theorem](special-adjoint-functor-theorem.md) obtains that condition from a [well-powered](well-powered-category.md) source and a [small cogenerating family](cogenerating-set.md). Precise hypotheses matter in both forms.
