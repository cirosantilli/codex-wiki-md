# Statistical fluctuation

↑ **Parent:** [Probability](probability.md)

A statistical fluctuation is variation between repeated random outcomes or samples. Its scale can be quantified by [variance](variance-split.md) or [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). For independent signal counts with a Poisson mean proportional to deposited [energy](energy-split.md), the absolute fluctuation grows as the square root of [energy](energy-split.md) and its relative size decreases as the inverse square root.
