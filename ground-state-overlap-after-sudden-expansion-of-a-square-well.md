# Ground-state overlap after sudden expansion of a square well

↑ **Parent:** [Sudden approximation](sudden-approximation.md)

For an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) initially on $[-a,a]$, a normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) is $a^{-1/2}\cos(\pi x/(2a))$. If the endpoints change instantaneously to $\pm\eta a$, with $\eta>1$, the [sudden approximation](sudden-approximation.md) leaves the [wave function](wave-function.md) unchanged, extended by zero outside the old well. Its overlap with the new normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) $(\eta a)^{-1/2}\cos(\pi x/(2\eta a))$ is $4\eta^{3/2}\cos(\pi/(2\eta))/[\pi(\eta^2-1)]$. The [Born rule](born-rule.md) squares this [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md); for $\eta=2$ the result is $64/(9\pi^2)$, and the limit as $\eta\downarrow1$ is $1$.
