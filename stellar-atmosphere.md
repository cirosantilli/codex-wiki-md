# Stellar atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_atmosphere)

A [stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md) is the outer region that determines the escaping spectrum and matches the interior [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md) to its radiation boundary conditions. A [photosphere](photosphere.md) is the layer near unit [optical depth](optical-depth.md), not a zero-temperature boundary. The inward intensity at the outer surface vanishes when there is no external illumination.
