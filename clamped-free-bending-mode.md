<h1 id="clamped-free-bending-mode">Clamped--free bending mode</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bending fluctuations of a clamped filament](thermal-bending-fluctuations-of-a-clamped-filament.md)

The bending modes of a filament clamped at one end and force-free and torque-free at the other obey $\cos\alpha_n\cosh\alpha_n=-1$. The slowest mode has $\alpha_1\simeq1.875$.
