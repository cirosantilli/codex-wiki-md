# Two-dimensional cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

For planar vectors $u=(u_x,u_y)$ and $v=(v_x,v_y)$, the scalar $u\times v=u_xv_y-u_yv_x$ is the [determinant](determinant.md) of their coordinate columns. It equals the perpendicular component of the three-dimensional [cross product](cross-product.md) after embedding the plane in three dimensions. Its sign records orientation, and its absolute value is twice the [area](surface-area.md) of the [triangle](triangle.md) spanned by $u,v$.
