# Displacement current density

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement current](displacement-current.md)

The time derivative of the [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) enters the macroscopic [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) alongside free [electric current density](current-density.md). In vacuum, $\mathbf J_D=\epsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E$. It is not transport of free charges through the vacuum gap of a [capacitor](capacitor.md); its [surface integral](surface-integral.md) nevertheless supplies the term needed for compatibility with the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md).
