# Superconducting flux quantum

↑ **Parent:** [Fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md)

The magnitude of one [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) unit for an electron-pair [order parameter](order-parameter.md), whose [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) has magnitude $2e$. The enclosing contour must satisfy the flux condition in [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md); it is not a statement that every finite area inside the material contains an integer number of these units.
