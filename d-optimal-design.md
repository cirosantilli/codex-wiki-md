# D-optimal design

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal experimental design](optimal-experimental-design.md)

A D-optimal design maximizes the [determinant](determinant.md) of the [information matrix of an experimental design](information-matrix-of-an-experimental-design.md), equivalently minimizing the [determinant](determinant.md) of the [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) of a fixed-size [least-squares estimator](ordinary-least-squares-estimators.md). It minimizes the volume of a fixed-level confidence ellipsoid for the model coefficients under the usual [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) model.
