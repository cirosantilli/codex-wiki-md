# Acyclicity of a finite Dynkin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md)

The underlying graph of a finite [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) is a forest. Normalize every [simple root](simple-root.md) to length one. Each bonded pair has inner product at most $-1/2$, and every other distinct pair has nonpositive inner product. A cycle with $m$ vertices would make the squared norm of their sum at most $m-m=0$, contradicting their [linear independence](linear-independence.md) in an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md). Multiple bonds denote root-length information, not graph-theoretic parallel-edge cycles.
