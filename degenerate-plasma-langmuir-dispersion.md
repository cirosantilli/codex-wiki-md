# Degenerate-plasma Langmuir dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir wave](langmuir-wave.md)

The long-[wavelength](wavelength.md) root of the [degenerate-plasma dielectric response](degenerate-plasma-dielectric-response.md). Expanding its [logarithm](logarithm.md) at large $s=\omega/(kv_F)$ gives $\epsilon=1-\omega_p^2/\omega^2-3\omega_p^2k^2v_F^2/(5\omega^4)+\cdots$. The occupied [Fermi sphere](fermi-sphere.md) has $\langle v_\parallel^2\rangle=v_F^2/5$, accounting for the dispersive coefficient. The static electron response instead defines the [Thomas-Fermi screening length](thomas-fermi-screening-length.md) $v_F/(\sqrt3\omega_p)$.
