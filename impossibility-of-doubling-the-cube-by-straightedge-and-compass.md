# Impossibility of doubling the cube by straightedge and compass

↑ **Parent:** [Straightedge and compass construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md)

Doubling the volume of a unit cube requires constructing its new side length $\sqrt[3]2$. Its minimal polynomial $X^3-2$ is irreducible by [Eisenstein's criterion](eisenstein-criterion.md) at two, so this length has degree three. Every [constructible number](constructible-number.md) belongs to a finite tower of quadratic extensions, whose total degree is a power of two. The [tower law for field extensions](tower-law-for-field-extensions.md) would force the degree three to divide that power of two, a contradiction. Thus no such [straightedge and compass construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md) exists.
