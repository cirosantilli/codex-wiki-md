# Clathrin-mediated endocytosis

↑ **Parent:** [Endocytosis](endocytosis.md)

[clathrin-mediated endocytosis](clathrin-mediated-endocytosis.md) internalizes selected surface material through assembly of adaptors and a [clathrin](clathrin.md) coat, membrane deformation, scission and subsequent coat release. Counts of coated puncta alone do not distinguish initiation, successful internalization and stalled intermediates.
