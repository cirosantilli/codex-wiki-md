# Pinned capillary-wave correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary height-difference correlation](capillary-height-difference-correlation.md)

Adding positive quadratic confinement $t\int h^2/2$ to the membrane energy changes its height spectrum to $k_BT/(\sigma q^2+t)$. The continuous height-shift [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) is explicitly broken. [Connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) decay on the finite length $\xi$ and the height-difference [variance](variance-split.md) saturates for every internal dimension, although its saturation value can depend on a microscopic cutoff. A negative confinement coefficient gives an unstable quadratic model rather than pinning.
