# Temporary variable

↑ **Parent:** [Intermediate representation](intermediate-representation.md)

A fresh internal name introduced during [compilation](compilation.md) to hold an intermediate result. Its lifetime and uses can be exposed to [instruction selection](instruction-selection.md) and allocation of [processor registers](processor-register.md); it need not correspond to a variable named in the source.
