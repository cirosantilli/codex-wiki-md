# Quadratic subfields of a multiquadratic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Multiquadratic field](multiquadratic-field.md)

In characteristic different from two, a field generated by finitely many nonzero square roots is the splitting field of a collection of separable quadratic polynomials. Its [Galois group](galois-group.md) embeds into the sign-vector group $(\mathbb Z/2)^n$. Every index-two subgroup is the kernel of a nontrivial sign character. Coordinate sign characters span the dual of this subgroup of $(\mathbb Z/2)^n$, by extending a linear functional to the ambient vector space. A product of the corresponding roots transforms by that character and generates precisely the quadratic fixed field.
