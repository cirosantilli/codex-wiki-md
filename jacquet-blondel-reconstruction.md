# Jacquet-Blondel reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

In a head-on lepton-proton collision with the proton along $+z$, hadronic [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $y_h=\sum_h(E_h-p_{zh})/(2E_e)$ and $Q_h^2=p_{T,h}^2/(1-y_h)$. With $s\simeq4E_eE_p$, the reconstructed [Bjorken scaling variable](bjorken-scaling-variable.md) is $x_h=Q_h^2/(sy_h)$. This is useful for [charged current](charged-current.md) events whose final [neutrino](neutrino.md) escapes. Initial-state radiation and unmeasured forward hadrons spoil the ideal closure.
