# Spin separation at a smooth quantum Hall edge

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md)

For locally linear [electron](electron.md) [confinement](confinement.md) [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $U(y)$, two [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md) branches cross the same [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) at positions differing by the [energy](energy-split.md) splitting divided by $|U'|$. The expression neglects self-consistent [electrostatic screening](electrostatic-screening.md) and [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) enhancement. If the quoted slope is an electrostatic [voltage](voltage.md) gradient instead of an electron-energy gradient, its magnitude must be multiplied by the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). A screened [compressible quantum Hall edge strip](compressible-quantum-hall-edge-strip.md) is not a single sharp crossing with an imposed bare slope.
