# Theta-weighted instanton sectors

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

For integer [instanton number](instanton-number.md) $k$, a topological angle can weight each Euclidean gauge-field sector by $e^{ik\vartheta}$ in a stated convention. The sector sum is then $2\pi$-periodic in $\vartheta$. In temporal gauge, the same integer describes a change in the vacuum winding or [Chern-Simons number](chern-simons-number-of-a-gauge-field.md), with compatible orientation conventions. Classical instanton action fixes exponential suppression, while determinants and collective-coordinate integrals supply prefactors.
