# Virasoro zero-mode shift

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

Shifting the zero mode changes the algebra's central representative:

$$
[\mathcal L_m,\mathcal L_n]=(m-n)\mathcal L_{m+n}
+\left(\frac c{12}m(m^2-1)+2am\right)\delta_{m+n,0}.
$$

The linear term follows from replacing $L_{m+n}$ by $\mathcal L_{m+n}+a\delta_{m+n,0}$. It changes the zero-mode convention, not the cubic coefficient or [central charge](central-charge.md). This distinction separates the intercept from the [Virasoro central extension](virasoro-central-extension.md).
