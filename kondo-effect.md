# Kondo effect

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondo_effect)

Antiferromagnetic exchange between a localized magnetic moment and conduction [electrons](electron.md) produces strong low-energy correlations and screening of the moment. In a dilute magnetic-impurity metal, a resistance minimum and a low-temperature logarithmic increase are characteristic signatures over an appropriate temperature range. The strong-coupling low-temperature regime cannot be obtained by extending the logarithm indefinitely.
