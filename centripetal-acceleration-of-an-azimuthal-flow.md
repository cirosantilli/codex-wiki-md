# Centripetal acceleration of an azimuthal flow

↑ **Parent:** [Convective acceleration](convective-acceleration.md)

For a [velocity field](velocity-field.md) $\mathbf u=U(r)e_\phi$ in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), the [convective acceleration](convective-acceleration.md) is $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=-U(r)^2(e_r+\cot\theta\,e_\theta)/r$, away from the polar axis. Each streamline is a [circle](circle.md) of cylindrical radius $r\sin\theta$, so this is [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md) of magnitude $U^2/(r\sin\theta)$ directed toward the polar axis. The terms containing $U'(r)$ cancel because a particle on such a streamline stays at fixed $r$ and $\theta$.
