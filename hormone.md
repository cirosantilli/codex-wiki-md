# Hormone

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormone)

A [hormone](hormone.md) is a chemical messenger released by [biological cells](biological-cell.md) that regulates target [biological cells](biological-cell.md) bearing suitable [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md). In endocrine signaling, [hormones](hormone.md) travel to distant targets through the bloodstream. The extract-transfer experiment under [second messenger](second-messenger.md) isolates part of this [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md) from intact [biological cells](biological-cell.md). [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 9.1: hormonal signaling](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/9-1-signaling-molecules-and-cellular-receptors)
