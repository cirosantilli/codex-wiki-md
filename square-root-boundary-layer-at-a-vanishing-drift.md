# Square-root boundary layer at a vanishing drift

↑ **Parent:** [Matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md)

In $\varepsilon y''+2xy'-2xy=0$, the drift vanishes at the endpoint $x=0$. Balancing diffusion against drift gives the [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md) coordinate $z=x/\sqrt\varepsilon$. The leading layer equation is $Y''+2zY'=0$, with a bounded [error function](error-function.md) profile. A regular outer first-order calculation alone cannot impose both endpoint values.
