# Two-valued topos

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary topos](elementary-topos.md)

A nondegenerate [elementary topos](elementary-topos.md) is two-valued when its [terminal object](terminal-object.md) has exactly the empty and whole subobjects. This is a global-truth property, distinct from being a [Boolean topos](boolean-topos.md), which requires complements for all subobjects of all objects.
