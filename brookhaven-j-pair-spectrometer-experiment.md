# Brookhaven J pair-spectrometer experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Richter-Ting independent J/psi discovery experiments](richter-ting-independent-j-psi-discovery-experiments.md)

Brookhaven's [proton](proton.md) beam struck stationary [beryllium](beryllium.md). A two-arm magnetic pair spectrometer measured [electron](electron.md)–[Positron](positron.md) [momenta](momentum-split.md) and directions. [Cherenkov detectors](cherenkov-detector.md), tracking chambers and shower counters rejected the much larger [hadron](hadron.md) background. Calibration used an [electron](electron.md) beam; timing and track matching rejected [accidental coincidences](accidental-coincidence.md). The pair [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) distribution showed a narrow excess. Reducing magnet currents left its reconstructed position unchanged; changing target thickness and reversing spectrometer polarity supplied further checks. The unresolved intrinsic width did not mean a zero [decay width](decay-width.md).[Experimental Observation of a Heavy Particle J, pp. 1404–1405, Fig. 1 and checks 1, 2 and 7: target, spectrometer, calibration and stability tests](experimental-observation-of-a-heavy-particle-j-by-j-j-aubert.md)

The logic of the magnet-current check is especially useful. A real state should retain its reconstructed [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) when a calibrated change of [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) makes its daughters follow different accepted paths. A feature tied to one detector location or one setting need not do so. This checks a possible instrumental origin; it does not by itself determine the state's [quark](quark.md) composition.
