# Aleph-one-Suslin decomposition into analytic sets

↑ **Parent:** [Kappa-Suslin set](kappa-suslin-set.md)

Every $\aleph_1$-Suslin set is a union of $\aleph_1$ [analytic sets](analytic-set.md). Every countable sequence of countable ordinals is bounded below $\omega_1$, and after restricting all labels below one countable ordinal the first-coordinate space can be recoded by $\omega$.
