# Uniform lowest-price auction equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Lowest-price auction](lowest-price-auction.md)

For $n\geq2$ risk-neutral bidders with independent [uniform distributions](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[0,1]$, the [lowest-price auction](lowest-price-auction.md) has a symmetric [Bayesian Nash equilibrium](bayesian-nash-equilibrium.md) $b(v)=(n-1)v$. A deviation bidding $(n-1)z$, with $0\leq z\leq1$, wins with probability $z^{n-1}$ and has expected payment $(n-1)z^n/n$. Its expected profit is $vz^{n-1}-(n-1)z^n/n$, whose derivative is $(n-1)z^{n-2}(v-z)$, proving optimality at $z=v$. Bids above $n-1$ give the same outcome as $z=1$. The seller receives $(n-1)$ times the minimum of $n$ independent uniform valuations, with expected revenue $(n-1)/(n+1)$.
