# Delta-system

↑ **Parent:** [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md)

A family of sets is a Delta-system with root $r$ when any two distinct members intersect exactly in $r$. The members themselves need not be finite, although the usual [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md) applies to uncountable families of finite sets.
