# Non-degenerate probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) is non-degenerate when it is not a point mass. This condition excludes constant limits in limit theorems and in the [maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md) of a [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md).
