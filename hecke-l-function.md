# Hecke L-function

↑ **Parent:** [L-function](l-function.md)

The [L-function](l-function.md) of a [Hecke character](hecke-character.md) is initially the displayed [Euler product](euler-product.md) over prime ideals away from its conductor. The equivalent [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) sums $\psi(\mathfrak a)N\mathfrak a^{-s}$ over integral ideals prime to the conductor. For a [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) with its endomorphisms defined over the base, its [Hasse-Weil L-function](hasse-weil-l-function.md) factors into the two conjugate Hecke L-functions.
