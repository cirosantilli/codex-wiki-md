# Most likely radius in an isotropic power-law trap

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function for an ultrarelativistic gas in a power-law trap](canonical-partition-function-for-an-ultrarelativistic-gas-in-a-power-law-trap.md)

The radial density is proportional to $r^2e^{-r^{2n}/(V^{2n/3}k_BT)}$, whose mode is

$$
r=V^{1/3}\left(\frac{k_BT}{n}\right)^{1/(2n)}.
$$
