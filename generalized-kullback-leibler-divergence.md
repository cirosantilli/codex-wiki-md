<h1 id="generalized-kullback-leibler-divergence">Generalized Kullback–Leibler divergence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For nonnegative densities of possibly different total masses, this divergence includes the mass correction $-x+y$ as well as the logarithmic term. It is the [Bregman distance](bregman-divergence.md) of the [maximum-entropy regularization functional](maximum-entropy-regularization-functional.md) at a positive reference $y$. Convexity of $s\log s-s$ proves nonnegativity. For equal masses the correction integrates to zero, recovering the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md); for [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md) both masses are one. Use the usual extended-value conventions at zero reference density.
