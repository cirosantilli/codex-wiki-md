# Characteristic solution of a scalar conservation law

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)

Before [characteristic crossing](characteristic-crossing.md), the [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) $u_t+f(u)_x=0$ has solution $u(t,X_t(\xi))=u_0(\xi)$ with $X_t(\xi)=\xi+t f'(u_0(\xi))$. When this map is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md), the solution is $u_0\circ X_t^{-1}$. Its spatial derivative is $u_0'(\xi)/(1+t f''(u_0(\xi))u_0'(\xi))$.
