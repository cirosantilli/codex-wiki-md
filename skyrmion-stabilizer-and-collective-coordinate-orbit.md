# Skyrmion stabilizer and collective-coordinate orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md)

For a centered static [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) $U_0$, the effective rotation-isorotation group is $G=SO(3)_{\rm space}\times SO(3)_{\rm iso}$. Its [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) $H$ consists of pairs obeying $A U_0(R^{-1}x)A^\dagger=U_0(x)$. Thus distinct rigid orientations form the [homogeneous space](homogeneous-space.md) $G/H$, by the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md). The unit hedgehog has diagonal $SO(3)$ stabilizer and three orientation coordinates; the toroidal two-Skyrmion has a one-dimensional continuous stabilizer and five; the tetrahedral and cubic cases have discrete stabilizers and six. Adding three translations gives zero-mode orbit dimensions 6, 8, 9, 9. These are symmetry-generated low-energy coordinates, not a claim that non-Bogomolny multi-Skyrmion solutions possess an exact flat moduli space of arbitrary separations.
