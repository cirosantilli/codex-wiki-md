# Eomaia scansoria

↑ **Parent:** [Eutheria](eutheria.md)

An approximately 125-million-year-old Early [Cretaceous](cretaceous.md) mammal commonly analysed as a stem [eutherian](eutheria.md). Its informative skeleton combines [eutherian](eutheria.md) affinities, [epipubic bones](epipubic-bone.md) and evidence of climbing adaptations. [Nature 416, pp. 816–822; Eomaia skeleton and stem-eutherian character combination](the-earliest-known-eutherian-mammal-by-qiang-ji-and-others.md)
