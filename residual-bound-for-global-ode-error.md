# Residual bound for global ODE error

↑ **Parent:** [Global discretization error](global-discretization-error.md)

For a differentiable approximation $\eta$, put $d=\eta'-f(t,\eta)$. If $f$ is [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant $L$ on the relevant region, the exact solution's error satisfies the displayed bound by [Gronwall's inequality](gronwall-inequality.md). Thus a bound on the residual over the whole interval supplies an a posteriori [global error](global-discretization-error.md) estimate. A small estimated defect at a single step does not furnish this bound by itself.
