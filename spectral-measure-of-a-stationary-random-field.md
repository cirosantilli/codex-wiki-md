# Spectral measure of a stationary random field

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md)

A second-order stationary complex random field on the line has uncentered correlation $C(\zeta)=\mathbb E[E(z+\zeta)\overline{E(z)}]$. When this correlation is continuous, its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is a finite positive measure $F$ characterized by $C(\zeta)=\int e^{i\nu\zeta}F(d\nu)$. Its total mass is $\mathbb E|E(z)|^2$. If it has a density, $S(\nu)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int C(\zeta)e^{-i\nu\zeta}d\zeta$. A constant mean $m$ contributes $|m|^2\delta_0$; the remaining measure is the covariance spectrum. This uncentered convention is useful for [coherent and diffuse wave fields](coherent-and-diffuse-wave-fields.md).
