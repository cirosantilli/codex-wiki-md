# Mukhanov-Sasaki variable

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md)

For single-field adiabatic inflation, the Mukhanov-Sasaki variable rescales the [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) by $z=a\sqrt{2\epsilon}M_{\rm Pl}$, where $M_{\rm Pl}$ is the [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md). Its quadratic bulk [action](action.md) is $\tfrac12\int[(v')^2-(\nabla v)^2+(z''/z)v^2]$, after a boundary term is removed. A [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) mode obeys $v_k''+(k^2-z''/z)v_k=0$ and has canonical [Wronskian normalization](wronskian-normalization.md). The mass scale and sound-speed factors depend on the underlying scalar theory; this definition assumes the canonical single-field action and positive slow-roll kinetic coefficient.
