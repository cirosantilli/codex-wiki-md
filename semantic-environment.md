# Semantic environment

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)

A mapping from identifiers to their semantic values. A [programming closure](closure-computer-programming.md) retains its defining [semantic environment](semantic-environment.md); extending it for parameters preserves [lexical scope](lexical-scope.md) and lets those parameters shadow outer bindings.
