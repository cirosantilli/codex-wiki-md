# Power-core truncation of a prime-core tower

↑ **Parent:** [Core tower of a partition](core-tower-of-a-partition.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, integer $k\geq1$, and $\gamma=C_{p^k}(\lambda)$, taking the $p^k$-[core of a partition](core-of-a-partition.md) preserves the entries of the [core tower of a partition](core-tower-of-a-partition.md) at levels $0,\ldots,k-1$ and makes every level at least $k$ empty. Also the [weight of a partition](weight-of-a-partition.md) $w_{p^k}(\lambda)$ equals the total size at level $k$ of the [quotient tower of a partition](quotient-tower-of-a-partition.md).

For the first claim, a removable [rim hook](rim-hook.md) of length $p^k$ is a bead move by $p^k$ on the [abacus of a partition](abacus-of-a-partition.md). It preserves the numbers of beads on the $p$ runners and hence the $p$-core. Under the [abacus divisible-hook correspondence](hooks-divisible-by-the-abacus-modulus.md), it becomes removal of a $p^{k-1}$-hook in one quotient component; iteration preserves the cores at the next $k-1$ levels. After all $p^k$-hooks are removed, every component at quotient level $k$ is empty, so every higher core level is empty as well. The weight assertion follows from [iterated quotient equals a power quotient up to permutation](iterated-quotient-equals-a-power-quotient-up-to-permutation.md) and the size formula for the [quotient of a partition](quotient-of-a-partition.md).
