# Magnetohydrodynamic energy principle

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For a static smooth [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) equilibrium with conservative external forces and surface terms eliminated by the [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), the linear displacement equation is $\rho\ddot{\boldsymbol\xi}=\mathcal F\boldsymbol\xi$, where the force operator is a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) in the appropriate integral pairing. Its restoring [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $\delta W=-\tfrac12\int\boldsymbol\xi^*\cdot\mathcal F\boldsymbol\xi\,dV$ is real. Multiplying the [normal mode](normal-mode.md) equation by the [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) of the [displacement](displacement.md) and integrating gives $\omega^2\int\rho|\boldsymbol\xi|^2dV=2\delta W$. Hence squared frequencies are real, and a negative restoring form gives an unstable mode through the variational [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md), subject to the operator domain and spectral hypotheses. Background [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) adds terms to this argument; the static hypothesis is essential.
