# Antarctic bottom water

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctic_bottom_water)

Antarctic bottom water is a dense southern-origin water mass that ventilates much of the abyssal ocean. Cooling and [brine rejection](brine-rejection.md) form dense shelf water; its descent and mixing with adjacent waters contribute to Antarctic bottom water production. Shelf production is geographically concentrated and does not mean that every [polynya](polynya.md) exports water to the abyss.
