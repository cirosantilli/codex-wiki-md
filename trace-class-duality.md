# Trace-class duality

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md)

The dual of the [trace-class operators](trace-class-operator.md), and also of the normed space of [finite-rank operators](finite-rank-operator.md) with the [trace norm](trace-norm.md), is isometrically the space of [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md). The pairing is $F_B(T)=\operatorname{Tr}(BT)$. For $R_{x,y}z=\langle z,y\rangle x$, one has $\|R_{x,y}\|_1=\|x\|\|y\|$ and $F_B(R_{x,y})=\langle Bx,y\rangle$, using an [inner product](inner-product.md) linear in its first argument. A bounded functional on these [rank-one operators](rank-one-operator.md) gives a bounded [sesquilinear form](sesquilinear-form.md) and hence a unique $B$ by the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md). The [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md) proves $\|F_B\|=\|B\|$ and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) for the [trace norm](trace-norm.md).
