↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by A. F. Andersen](work-by-a-f-andersen.md)

By [A. F. Andersen](a-f-andersen.md). Published in Mathematisk-fysiske Meddelelser, volume I, number 4, Det Kgl. Danske Videnskabernes Selskab. The scanned title page names the author and gives Copenhagen and 1918.

Online text: [https://gymarkiv.sdu.dk/MFM/kdvs/mfm%201-9/mfm-1-4.pdf](https://gymarkiv.sdu.dk/MFM/kdvs/mfm%201-9/mfm-1-4.pdf)

Cited passages: §1, printed p. 3, introduces orders of [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md) and cites [Ernesto Cesàro](ernesto-cesaro.md)'s 1890 publication in Bulletin des Sciences Mathématiques, series 2, volume 14, p. 114; §2, pp. 6–7, discusses the multiplication theorem and its role in developing summability theory. The original 1890 paper was not inspected here; the historical citation refers to Andersen's account.
