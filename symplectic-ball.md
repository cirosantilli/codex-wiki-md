# Symplectic ball

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

A symplectic ball is a Euclidean ball in $(\mathbb R^{2n},\omega_0)$, or its image under a [symplectic embedding](symplectic-embedding.md).
