# Conjugation module of a group ring

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

The [group ring](group-ring.md) $\mathbb ZG$ becomes a $\mathbb ZG$-module under the [conjugation action](conjugation-action.md) $g\cdot x=gxg^{-1}$. Its basis splits into [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md), and the span of the class of $x$ is the permutation module on $G/C_G(x)$.
