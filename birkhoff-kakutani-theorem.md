# Birkhoff-Kakutani theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant group metric](left-invariant-group-metric.md)

A Hausdorff [topological group](topological-group-split.md) admits a compatible [left-invariant group metric](left-invariant-group-metric.md) exactly when it has a countable [neighbourhood basis](neighbourhood-basis.md) at the [identity element](identity-element.md). Choose symmetric [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of the [identity element](identity-element.md) $U_n$ with $U_{n+1}^3\subset U_n$ and trivial intersection. Assign a step in $U_n$ cost $2^{-n}$, and take the infimum of total costs of chains joining two points. The [dyadic product estimate for group neighbourhoods](dyadic-product-estimate-for-group-neighbourhoods.md) makes this a genuine compatible [metric](metric.md). The Hausdorff assumption separates distinct points; without it the same construction can give only a pseudometric.
