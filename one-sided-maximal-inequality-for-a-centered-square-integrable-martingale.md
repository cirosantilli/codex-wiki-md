# One-sided maximal inequality for a centered square-integrable martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Doob maximal inequality for a nonnegative submartingale](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md)

For a centered square-integrable [martingale](martingale-split.md) with $v=\mathbb E M_n^2$ and $\lambda>0$, apply the [Doob maximal inequality for a nonnegative submartingale](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md) to $(M_k+c)^2$ for $c\geq0$. [Conditional Jensen inequality](conditional-jensen-inequality.md) gives the [submartingale](submartingale.md) property, and crossing $\lambda$ forces its square above $(\lambda+c)^2$. The bound $(v+c^2)/(\lambda+c)^2$ is minimized by $c=v/\lambda$. This extends the [Cantelli inequality](cantelli-inequality.md) from one [random variable](random-variable-split.md) to a [martingale](martingale-split.md) maximum, without requiring [independent increments](independent-increments.md).
