# Call-price decay and moment threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Power payoff static call representation](power-payoff-static-call-representation.md)

For $C(K)=\mathbb E(S-K)_+$ and $\mathbb ES<\infty$, split the static call integral at a fixed positive strike. The first moment bounds the integrand near zero; polynomial call decay controls the integral at infinity for $0<\varepsilon<\delta$. The endpoint can fail, as shown by a [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) with survival exponent $1+\delta$.
