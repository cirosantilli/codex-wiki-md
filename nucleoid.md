# Nucleoid

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleoid)

A [nucleoid](nucleoid.md) is the region occupied by [chromosome](chromosome.md) [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in a [prokaryotic](prokaryote.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). It has no surrounding [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md), but its [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is organized rather than freely spread at random. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.2: the nucleoid](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-2-prokaryotic-cells).
