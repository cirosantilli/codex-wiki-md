# Elastic energy velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md)

For a real branch polarization $\mathbf a$ and complex displacement amplitude $U$, the cycle averages are $\langle F_j\rangle=\omega|U|^2c_{ijkl}a_i a_k k_l/2$ and $\langle W\rangle=\rho\omega^2|U|^2|\mathbf a|^2/2$. Differentiating the [acoustic tensor](acoustic-tensor.md) [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) equation with respect to $k_j$ gives their ratio as the [group velocity](group-velocity.md). This identifies the [energy flux](energy-flux.md) direction with the normal to the [elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md) in a lossless nondispersive elastic medium.
