# Stork enamine reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A [Stork enamine reaction](stork-enamine-reaction.md) uses a carbonyl compound's [enamine](enamine.md) as a carbon nucleophile, then hydrolyses the resulting [iminium ion](iminium.md) to restore the carbonyl. It enables alkylation or conjugate addition without retaining the amine in the product.
