# Hydrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrometer)

A floating [density](density.md) instrument. At equilibrium, [Archimedes' principle](archimedes-principle.md) gives $\rho V_{\rm displaced}=m$ for fixed instrument [mass](mass.md) $m$, so it displaces more volume in a less dense [liquid](liquid.md). A graduated narrow stem converts this volume change into a visible immersion reading. Calibration specifies a [temperature](temperature-split.md) and a reading convention for the [meniscus](meniscus-liquid.md); a sample's other dissolved substances can affect a sugar-calibrated scale.
