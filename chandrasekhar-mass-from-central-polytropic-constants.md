# Chandrasekhar mass from central polytropic constants

↑ **Parent:** [Chandrasekhar limit](chandrasekhar-limit.md)

An ultrarelativistic [degenerate electron gas](degenerate-electron-gas.md) has $P=K\rho^{4/3}$, so it forms an index-three [stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md). Insert $P_c=c_3GM^2/R^4$ and $\rho_c=3a_3M/(4\pi R^3)$ into that equation of state: the radius cancels, leaving the displayed mass. For fully ionized helium, $n_e=\rho/(2m_p)$ and $K=(3/(2\pi))^{1/3}hc/(16m_p^{4/3})$. This is the ideal limiting [Chandrasekhar mass](chandrasekhar-limit.md), rather than a claim that every ordinary [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) has that mass.
