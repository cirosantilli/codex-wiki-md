# Hesse pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesse_pencil)

This pencil consists of projective cubic curves generated by $x^3+y^3+z^3$ and $xyz$. The displayed finite parameter gives a nonsingular cubic exactly when $\lambda^3\ne-1$; the parameter at infinity gives the triangle $xyz=0$. Multiplying the three gradient equations proves the finite singularity criterion. The [Hessian curve](hessian-curve-of-a-plane-curve.md) of a member has equation $\lambda^2(x^3+y^3+z^3)-(\lambda^3+4)xyz=0$, so the Hessian stays in the same pencil.
