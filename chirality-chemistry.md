# Chirality (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chirality_(chemistry))

[Chirality](chirality-chemistry.md) means that a [molecule](molecule-split.md) cannot be superposed on its mirror image. A [carbon](carbon.md) attached to four different groups is a common source of [chirality](chirality-chemistry.md); the definition concerns the whole [molecule](molecule-split.md), rather than requiring this particular structural feature.

The test is spatial: apply a [translation](translation-geometry.md) and a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) to the entire [molecule](molecule-split.md) until corresponding [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) coincide, keeping their identities and [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md). A mirror operation is a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md); physically turning a model is a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). They are different operations. If no such superposition exists, the two forms are [enantiomers](enantiomer.md); if one succeeds, the [molecule](molecule-split.md) is [achiral](achirality.md). A different-looking flat drawing alone establishes neither conclusion.

For a simple [molecule](molecule-split.md) with exactly one tetrahedral [stereocenter](stereocenter.md) and four constitutionally different [substituents](substituent.md), the mirror forms cannot superpose. Compare entire attached groups, including paths around a ring, rather than just the first [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). Several tetrahedral [stereocenters](stereocenter.md) require a whole-[molecule](molecule-split.md) check: a [meso compound](meso-compound.md) can be [achiral](achirality.md). Conversely, [axial chirality](axial-chirality.md) needs no tetrahedral [stereocenter](stereocenter.md).[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §5.2: whole-molecule chirality, four different groups and equivalent ring paths](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/5-2-the-reason-for-handedness-in-molecules-chirality.md)

![](tetrahedral-enantiomer-mirror-geometry.png)

**[Figure 1](#image-spatial-mirror-pair-with-four-distinct-substituents). Spatial mirror pair with four distinct substituents**. Ideal [tetrahedral molecular geometry](tetrahedral-molecular-geometry.md) with distinct [substituents](substituent.md) A, B, C and D. The two models use the same viewing direction and the [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) $x\mapsto-x$. Their opposite signed [determinants](determinant.md) exclude superposition by any label-preserving [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). The letters do not denote [R and S configuration](r-and-s-configuration.md) priorities.

Why care about this distinction? Two [enantiomers](enantiomer.md) can fit differently into an already [chiral molecule](chiral-molecule.md), just as two opposite hands fit differently into one glove. Thus a [protein](protein-split.md)'s spatial arrangement can distinguish a mirror pair even when an [achiral](achirality.md) measurement cannot. [Polarimetry](polarimetry.md) provides a different kind of test: it measures a sample's net [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md), which depends on the populations of its [enantiomers](enantiomer.md).
