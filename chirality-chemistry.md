# Chirality (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chirality_(chemistry))

[Chirality](chirality-chemistry.md) means that a [molecule](molecule.md) cannot be superposed on its mirror image. A [carbon](carbon.md) attached to four different groups is a common source of [chirality](chirality-chemistry.md); the definition concerns the whole [molecule](molecule.md), rather than requiring this particular structural feature.
