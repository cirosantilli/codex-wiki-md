# Ideal magnetohydrodynamic energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For a fixed [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), total [energy density](energy-density.md) is $\mathcal E=\rho(u^2/2+\Phi)+p/(\gamma-1)+B^2/(2\mu_0)$. Its flux combines advected kinetic/potential energy, [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) and the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md). Dotting the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md) with [velocity](velocity.md), adding the adiabatic [internal energy](internal-energy.md) equation and the [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) equation cancels the magnetic work. The [pressure](pressure.md) terms combine into $-\nabla\cdot(p\mathbf u)$, proving the conservation law. A time-dependent imposed potential instead contributes $\rho\partial_t\Phi$.
