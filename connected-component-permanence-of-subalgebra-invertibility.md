# Connected-component permanence of subalgebra invertibility

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum in a closed unital subalgebra](spectrum-in-a-closed-unital-subalgebra.md)

For a closed unital subalgebra $A\subseteq B$ with common identity, its [group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra](group-of-invertible-elements-of-a-banach-algebra.md) is relatively open in $A\cap G(B)$ by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md). It is relatively closed because inversion in $B$ is continuous and $A$ is closed. Consequently each [connected component](connected-component.md) of $A\cap G(B)$ either consists entirely of elements invertible in $A$, or entirely of elements whose inverses do not belong to $A$.
