# Separable decaying line-plume similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Unsteady Boussinesq triangular-profile line-plume balances](unsteady-boussinesq-triangular-profile-line-plume-balances.md)

The [unsteady Boussinesq triangular-profile line-plume balances](unsteady-boussinesq-triangular-profile-line-plume-balances.md) admit a buoyant [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) $Q=\alpha\rho_0z^2/\tau$, $M=(2/3)\alpha\rho_0z^3/\tau^2$ and $F=(2/3)\alpha\rho_0z^3/\tau^3$, with $\tau=t+t_*>0$. It reconstructs $b=\alpha z$, $W=z/\tau$ and reduced gravity $z/\tau^2$. Origins can be shifted, but the zero-time unshifted solution is singular. Source and initial data are required to select the physical member and region of validity.
