# Barnett rotation magnetometry experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Barnett effect](barnett-effect.md)

The 2015 experiment used an air-driven nonmagnetic rotor containing polycrystalline [gadolinium](gadolinium.md), with [magnetic shielding](magnetic-shielding.md) and an external [fluxgate magnetometer](fluxgate-magnetometer.md). Air drive reduced motor backgrounds. Measurements at 297–305 K were above the [Curie temperature](curie-temperature.md), at rotation [frequencies](frequency.md) up to 1.5 kHz. Reversing airflow reversed rotation and the magnetic signal; an empty capsule showed no speed-dependent signal. The readout measured stray [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); geometry converted it to [magnetization](magnetization.md). Sample [temperature](temperature-split.md) was checked immediately after stopping. Heating and the conversion model limit inference.[§§II–III, Figs. 1–2, note 22](barnett-effect-in-paramagnetic-states-by-masao-ono-and-others.md)

Original functional layout, with simplified geometry. The arrows independently explain the sign expected for $\gamma<0$: positive axial [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), negative axial [magnetization](magnetization.md), and positive axial stray [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at an equatorial sensor. The air line indicates drive function. Dimensions and components follow the inspected experiment; this is neither its original figure nor measured data.

An additional analysis check follows from rotation [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). For signed sensor output $S$, separate

$$
S_{\rm odd}(\Omega)=\frac{S(+\Omega)-S(-\Omega)}2,\qquad
S_{\rm even}(\Omega)=\frac{S(+\Omega)+S(-\Omega)}2.
$$

An ideal linear [Barnett effect](barnett-effect.md) appears in the odd component; a fixed offset appears in the even component. This is a proposed diagnostic, not a claim about the paper's processing. A background correlated with direction can also be odd: reversal alone is insufficient. Empty-rotor checks, zero-speed readings, calibration of sensor polarity, and controlled [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes test different errors. Equatorial stray-field sign must also be converted to [magnetization](magnetization.md) sign: for an axial [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), the [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) at its equator points opposite to that [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The apparatus diagram makes this otherwise easy-to-miss sign change visible.
