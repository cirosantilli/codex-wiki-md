# Subdivision difference scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision mask](subdivision-mask.md)

For backward differences $\Delta P_i=P_i-P_{i-1}$, a [scalar](scalar.md) stationary arity-$a$ [subdivision mask](subdivision-mask.md) has symbol $M(z)=\sum_iM_i z^i$. The identity $(1-z)M(z)=B(z)(1-z^a)$ gives the displayed intertwining whenever the quotient is a finite [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md). Iterating supplies higher-difference masks. The coefficients $a^{r\ell}\Delta^rP_i^\ell$ are candidate $r$th-derivative controls. A convergent [derivative](derivative.md) scheme, proved by contraction of its first differences, establishes corresponding smoothness of the original limit under the standard consistent parameterization.
