# Random measurement error

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement error](measurement-error.md)

A component of [measurement error](measurement-error.md) fluctuating between repetitions under specified conditions, as distinguished from a shared [systematic error](systematic-error.md). Independent zero-mean fluctuations of [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$ give a sample-mean [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2/N$; correlated fluctuations need not follow this averaging law.
