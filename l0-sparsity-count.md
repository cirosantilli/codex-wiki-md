# L0 sparsity count

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse vector](sparse-vector.md)

The number of nonzero coordinates of a finite [vector](vector.md) is its L0 sparsity count. It vanishes only at zero, but is unchanged under multiplication by a nonzero scalar, so it is not a [norm](norm.md). Minimizing it under linear measurement constraints finds a [sparse vector](sparse-vector.md) with the smallest possible [support of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md). [Sparse injectivity](sparse-injectivity.md) of order $s$ guarantees unique recovery of every [sparse vector](sparse-vector.md) with at most $s$ nonzero coordinates by this objective.
