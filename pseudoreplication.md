# Pseudoreplication

↑ **Parent:** [Clustered data](clustered-data.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoreplication)

[Pseudoreplication](pseudoreplication.md) treats dependent measurements from the same [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md) as if they were independent [treatment](treatment.md) [replicates](replicate.md). It exaggerates the effective [sample size](sample-size.md) and commonly makes [standard errors](standard-error.md) too small. In the pot example of [replication in experimental design](replication-in-experimental-design.md), counting twelve [leaves](leaf.md) per group as twelve independent [treatment](treatment.md) [replicates](replicate.md) ignores the four containing pots. The [variance of nested measurements](variance-of-nested-measurements.md) quantifies the resulting understatement of uncertainty. [Nested measurements](nested-measurements.md) themselves are useful; [pseudoreplication](pseudoreplication.md) is the error of using them as independent [replicates](replicate.md) for the wrong level of inference.[Pseudoreplication and the Design of Ecological Field Experiments, p. 190, definition, and p. 201, “Multiple samples per experimental unit”](pseudoreplication-and-the-design-of-ecological-field-experiments-by-stuart-h-hurlbert.md)
