# Rational maximal torus

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal algebraic torus](maximal-algebraic-torus.md)

A [maximal algebraic torus](maximal-algebraic-torus.md) $T$ is rational for $F$ when $F(T)=T$. Rationality does not require every point of $T$ to be fixed: the finite group $T^F$ is its rational-point group. Similarly a rational [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) or [parabolic subgroup](parabolic-subgroup.md) means an $F$-stable algebraic subgroup.
