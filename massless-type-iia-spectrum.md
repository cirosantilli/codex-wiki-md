# Massless type IIA spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Type IIA superstring theory](type-iia-superstring-theory.md)

The bosons are the metric, two-form, [dilaton](dilaton.md), RR one-form and RR three-form, with physical dimensions $35+28+1+8+56=128$. Two opposite-chirality [gravitinos](gravitino.md) and two [dilatinos](dilatino.md) supply $2(56+8)=128$ fermionic degrees of freedom. They form the ten-dimensional [type IIA supergravity](type-iia-supergravity.md) multiplet. Counts refer to on-shell polarizations, not raw field components.
