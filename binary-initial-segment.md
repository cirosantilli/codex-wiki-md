# Binary initial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Binary order on the discrete cube](binary-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md)

An [initial segment](initial-segment.md) of the [binary order on the discrete cube](binary-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md) contains the vertices whose integer encodings are $0,\ldots,m-1$. It is a [down-set](down-set.md), so its internal edges and two-dimensional [cube faces](face-of-the-boolean-hypercube.md) can be counted at their unique upper vertices by $\sum_{j<m}\operatorname{wt}(j)$ and $\sum_{j<m}\binom{\operatorname{wt}(j)}2$.
