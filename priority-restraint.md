# Priority restraint

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-injury priority construction](finite-injury-priority-construction.md)

A [priority restraint](priority-restraint.md) protects finitely many oracle answers by requiring lower-priority enumerations to avoid positions below a numerical bound. Higher-priority action can cancel the [priority restraint](priority-restraint.md) by initializing its owner. After the last such injury, the protected computation is permanent.
