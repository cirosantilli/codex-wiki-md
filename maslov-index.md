# Maslov index

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian Grassmannian](lagrangian-grassmannian.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maslov_index)

The Maslov index assigns an [integer](integer.md) to a loop of [Lagrangian subspaces](lagrangian-subspace.md). Under $\operatorname{LGr}(\mathbb R^{2n})\cong U(n)/O(n)$, it is the degree of the map induced by $A\mapsto\det(A)^2$.
