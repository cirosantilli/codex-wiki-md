# Constant-quota harvested logistic growth

↑ **Parent:** [Logistic growth equation](logistic-growth-equation.md)

For a fixed catch rate $h$, natural logistic growth can replace the catch at two abundances if $0<h<rK/4$. The upper [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $N_+=K[1+\sqrt{1-4h/(rK)}]/2$ attracts, and the lower $N_-$ is an unstable survival threshold. They meet in a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $h=rK/4$, $N=K/2$. Above this catch every positive population declines to zero. Below it a population initially under $N_-$ also becomes extinct. A constant catch is stopped at extinction to avoid negative abundances; it is distinct from per-capita harvesting proportional to $N$.
