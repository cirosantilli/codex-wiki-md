# Hypercontractive weighted influence bound

↑ **Parent:** [Influence of a variable](influence-of-a-variable.md)

For a zero-one [Boolean function](boolean-function.md), the flip-probability [influence](influence-of-a-variable.md) is $\beta_i=4\sum_{S\ni i}\widehat f(S)^2$. Apply [Beckner's inequality](beckner-s-inequality.md) with noise parameter $\sqrt\delta$ to the [discrete derivative of a Boolean function](discrete-derivative-of-a-boolean-function.md), whose nonzero magnitude is $1/2$. Its $L^{1+\delta}$ norm squared is $\beta_i^{2/(1+\delta)}/4$. Summing over coordinates gives the displayed weighted [Fourier weight](fourier-weight.md) bound, a useful intermediate step in influence lower bounds.
