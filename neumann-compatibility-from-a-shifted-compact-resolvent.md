# Neumann compatibility from a shifted compact resolvent

↑ **Parent:** [Fredholm alternative](fredholm-alternative.md)

For a symmetric uniformly elliptic divergence-form operator with zero conormal flux and zero reaction, [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) constructs the shifted inverse $K:L^2\to H^1$. The compact [Rellich-Kondrachov theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) embedding makes it compact on $L^2$, and symmetry makes it self-adjoint. If the homogeneous kernel consists of constants, then $\ker(I-K)$ is the constant line and $K1=1$. The [Fredholm alternative](fredholm-alternative.md) makes solvability equivalent to $Kf$ being orthogonal to constants, or $\langle Kf,1\rangle=\langle f,K1\rangle=\int f=0$. The [weak solution](weak-solution.md) is unique up to an additive constant. For a disconnected domain the identical argument gives one compatibility condition per connected component.
