# Passive acoustic impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Surface acoustic impedance](surface-acoustic-impedance.md)

With [pressure](pressure.md) amplitude $P=ZV$ and [velocity](velocity.md) $V$ directed into a boundary, the mean absorbed [acoustic energy flux](acoustic-energy-flux.md) is $\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}(PV^*)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}Z|V|^2$. A passive boundary cannot supply net energy, so $\operatorname{Re}Z\geq0$ at a real frequency. Its reflection from a propagating [acoustic plane wave](acoustic-plane-wave.md) has magnitude at most one. A pole continued to a complex incidence angle or frequency must be interpreted separately from real-frequency passive scattering.
