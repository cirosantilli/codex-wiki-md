# Iroshnikov-Kraichnan spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md)

The [Iroshnikov-Kraichnan spectrum](iroshnikov-kraichnan-spectrum.md) phenomenology assumes balanced, isotropic local [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md) with $\delta z_l\ll v_A$, decorrelated counterpropagating encounters, $\tau_A=l/v_A$ and $\tau_{\rm nl}=l/\delta z_l$. A [weak wave cascade](weak-wave-cascade.md) takes $\tau_{\rm cas}\sim\tau_{\rm nl}^2/\tau_A=v_Al/\delta z_l^2$. Constant [energy flux](energy-flux.md) gives $\delta z_l^4\sim\epsilon v_Al$ and the displayed shell-integrated [turbulent energy spectrum](turbulent-energy-spectrum.md). Isotropy is an additional assumption, not a consequence of a strong guide field.
