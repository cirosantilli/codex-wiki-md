# Subitizing

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical cognition](numerical-cognition.md)

Rapid exact recognition of a small quantity without serial [counting](counting.md). [Subitizing](subitizing.md) small collections does not imply direct exact representation of arbitrarily large [numbers](number.md) or knowledge of the symbolic [counting](counting.md) system.
