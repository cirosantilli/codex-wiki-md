# Maxwell torque conservation in an axisymmetric wind

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic angular-momentum invariant](magnetohydrodynamic-angular-momentum-invariant.md)

In a steady [axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind](axisymmetric-magnetohydrodynamic-wind.md), axisymmetric [pressure](pressure.md) and [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md) exert no azimuthal [torque](torque.md). The azimuthal momentum equation is $\rho\mathbf u_p\cdot\nabla(su_\phi)=\mu_0^{-1}\mathbf B_p\cdot\nabla(sB_\phi)$. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and the [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) condition imply $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F_J=0$. With [magnetohydrodynamic mass loading](magnetohydrodynamic-mass-loading.md) $\rho\mathbf u_p=\kappa\mathbf B_p$, the transported [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) per unit poloidal [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) is $\ell=\kappa s u_\phi-sB_\phi/\mu_0$. It is constant on connected regular [poloidal flux surfaces](axisymmetric-magnetic-flux-surface.md). The usual specific [magnetohydrodynamic angular-momentum invariant](magnetohydrodynamic-angular-momentum-invariant.md) is $L=\ell/\kappa$, provided $\kappa\ne0$; confusing these two normalizations loses a factor of mass loading in the wind [torque](torque.md).
