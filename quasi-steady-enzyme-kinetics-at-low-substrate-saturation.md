# Quasi-steady enzyme kinetics at low substrate saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md)

A [quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) for [enzyme-substrate complexes](enzyme-substrate-complex.md) can remain useful when total enzyme and initial substrate concentrations are comparable. The relevant parameter can involve the sum $s_0+K_m$, rather than $s_0$ alone. In a [mechanism-based inhibition](mechanism-based-inhibition.md) model with $s_0/K_m=O(\varepsilon)$, bounded intermediate decay rates and an inactivation rate of order $\varepsilon$, the fast complexes adjust in a layer of scaled width $O(\varepsilon)$. On bounded slow-time intervals the main complex follows the substrate, the later complex and accumulated inactivated enzyme remain $O(\varepsilon)$, and the substrate has leading exponential depletion. An outer algebraic complex concentration does not satisfy the unbound initial condition inside that initial layer.
