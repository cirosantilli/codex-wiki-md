# Angular relation for two-photon electron-positron annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

When an electron of Lorentz factor $\gamma>1$ annihilates with a stationary equal-mass positron, transverse [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) makes the [photons](photon.md)' transverse components opposite. Eliminating their positive frequencies gives the displayed relation for noncollinear [photon](photon.md) angles to the incoming electron. The exactly collinear forward/backward configuration is physically allowed but makes the quotient $0/0$; its undivided cross-multiplied identity remains valid. The angle relation thus requires nondegeneracy or an appropriate limit, rather than an assertion that every allowed angle pair defines the quotient.
