# Restriction of a module sheaf to a closed subvariety

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md)

The restriction meant here is the [pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md) to the closed subvariety, including tensoring with its structure sheaf. On an [affine chart](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md), tensoring $M$ with $A/I$ gives $M/IM$. This distinction matters because restricting only the underlying topological sheaf would retain the ambient ring action.
