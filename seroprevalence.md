# Seroprevalence

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)

[Seroprevalence](seroprevalence.md) is the proportion of a specified population with a positive result for a specified antibody assay. Its biological interpretation depends on assay [diagnostic sensitivity](diagnostic-sensitivity.md), [diagnostic specificity](diagnostic-specificity.md) and the persistence and origin of the measured [antibodies](antibody.md). It is not itself the incidence rate of new infection.
