# Nondispersive wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md)

A nondispersive wave has constant [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) on a propagation branch. For $\omega=ck$, its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is also $c$. A [frequency](frequency.md) relation with a nonzero offset, such as $\omega=ck+b$, has constant [group velocity](group-velocity.md) but a [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c+b/k$ depending on [wavenumber](wavenumber.md); constant [group velocity](group-velocity.md) alone is therefore not sufficient for nondispersion.
