<h1 id="limiting-ma-1-innovations-coefficient">Limiting MA(1) innovations coefficient</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-sample innovations of an MA(1) process](finite-sample-innovations-of-an-ma-1-process.md)

For an [MA(1)](moving-average-process-of-order-one.md) process, the recursion $\lambda_t=\theta/(1+\theta^2-\theta\lambda_{t-1})$ has candidate limits $\theta$ and $1/\theta$. The limit in $[-1,1]$ is $\theta$ when $|\theta|\le1$ and $1/\theta$ otherwise. The limiting innovation [variance](variance-split.md) is $\sigma^2\max(1,\theta^2)$, expressing the same [covariance](covariance.md) law through an invertible reciprocal representation.
