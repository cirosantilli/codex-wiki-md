# Stationary spectrum of a linear fluctuating interface

↑ **Parent:** [Linear fluctuating interface](linear-fluctuating-interface.md)

For a nonzero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) satisfying $dh_q=-\alpha q^2h_q\,dt+\sigma\,dW_q$ with $\alpha>0$, the [explicit Ornstein-Uhlenbeck solution](explicit-ornstein-uhlenbeck-solution.md) and [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) give the stationary [second moment](second-moment.md) $\sigma^2/(2\alpha q^2)$. The prefactor depends on the normalization of the mode noise, while the inverse-square dependence follows from diffusive relaxation.
