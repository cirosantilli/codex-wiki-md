# Orthogonality of final functors and discrete fibrations

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete fibration](discrete-fibration.md)

Given a commutative square whose left functor is [final](final-functor.md) and whose right functor is a [discrete fibration](discrete-fibration.md), there is a unique diagonal functor filling the square. To construct its value on an object, choose an object of the relevant connected [comma category](comma-category.md) and lift the resulting arrow; uniqueness of lifts makes the answer constant over that connected category.
