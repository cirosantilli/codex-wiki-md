# Line search

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A [line search](line-search.md) chooses a positive [step size](step-size.md) $\alpha$ along a search [vector](vector.md) $s$, updating $x$ to $x+\alpha s$. For a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) $f$, a [descent direction](descent-direction.md) makes sufficiently short steps decrease the [objective function](objective-function.md); an [Armijo condition](armijo-condition.md) quantifies the decrease, while the [Wolfe conditions](wolfe-conditions.md) also prevent accepting an unnecessarily short step.
