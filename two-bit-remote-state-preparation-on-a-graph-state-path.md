# Two-bit remote state preparation on a graph-state path

↑ **Parent:** [Remote state preparation](remote-state-preparation.md)

Alice holds the first two qubits and Bob the third of a [three-vertex graph-state wire](three-vertex-graph-state-wire.md). Alice measures at $\alpha$ and $(-1)^r\beta$, with outcomes $r,s$, and sends these two bits. Bob applies $Z^rX^s$, the inverse of the known [Pauli frame](pauli-frame.md) $X^sZ^r$, obtaining $U(\beta)U(\alpha)|+\rangle$ up to [global phase](global-phase.md). Bob need not know the angles. Every branch succeeds, with branch probability $1/4$, and no postselection is needed. Alice's measurements and Bob's corrections constitute [local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) on the previously shared entangled resource.
