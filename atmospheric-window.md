# Atmospheric window

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_window)

An atmospheric window is a wavelength interval of relatively high atmospheric transmission. [Infrared photometric bands](infrared-photometric-band.md) are largely chosen to lie in such windows.
