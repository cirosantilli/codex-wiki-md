# Continuous dependence on forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem-split.md)

Solutions depend continuously on their forcing when convergence of the forcing in a specified [norm](norm.md) implies convergence of solutions in a specified solution [norm](norm.md), with [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) fixed. A uniform estimate $\|u-v\|_X\leq C\|f-g\|_Y$ is a stronger quantitative form, namely [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) of the solution map. The [monotonicity energy estimate for a semilinear Dirichlet problem](monotonicity-energy-estimate-for-a-semilinear-dirichlet-problem.md) is an example.
