# Leave-one-out identity for empirical distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Type (information theory)](type-information-theory.md)

Given $n+1$ observations, let $\widehat q_{n,i}$ be the [empirical distribution](type-information-theory.md) of the $n$ observations remaining after deleting observation $i$. Their average is the [empirical distribution](type-information-theory.md) $\widehat p_{n+1}$ of the full sample. For each symbol, its [indicator function](indicator-function.md) for a particular observation appears in exactly $n$ deletion samples. Summing the empirical counts and dividing by $n(n+1)$ therefore gives its full-sample frequency. This identity is deterministic and needs neither [independence](independent-random-variables.md) nor identical distributions.
