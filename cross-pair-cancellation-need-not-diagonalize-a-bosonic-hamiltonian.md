# Cross-pair cancellation need not diagonalize a bosonic Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md)

For a two-mode quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), a transformation can remove $AB+A^\dagger B^\dagger$ while leaving $A^{\dagger2}$ or $B^{\dagger2}$ terms. Such terms create quanta from the proposed vacuum, so their absence must also be checked before identifying that vacuum with an [energy](energy-split.md) [eigenstate](eigenstate.md). Coordinate coupling $kxy$ contains both pair and number-conserving terms and supplies an explicit example.
