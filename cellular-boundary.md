# Cellular boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular cycle](cellular-cycle.md)

A [cellular chain](cellular-chain.md) that is the boundary of a chain one dimension higher. The chain-complex identity $d_nd_{n+1}=0$ makes every cellular boundary a [cellular cycle](cellular-cycle.md); their quotient is [cellular homology](cellular-chain-complex.md).
