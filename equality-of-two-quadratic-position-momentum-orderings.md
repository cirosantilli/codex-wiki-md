# Equality of two quadratic position-momentum orderings

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md)

On a common invariant domain for the operator products, let the [position operator](position-operator.md) and [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md) obey $[\widehat x,\widehat p]=i\hbar I$. Then $\widehat x\widehat p=\widehat p\widehat x+i\hbar I$, so both displayed expressions equal $(\widehat p\widehat x)^2+i\hbar\widehat p\widehat x$. In position representation both act on a smooth function $f$ as $-\hbar^2(x^2f''+2xf')$. This particular equality does not imply that arbitrary classical reorderings remain equivalent in [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md).
