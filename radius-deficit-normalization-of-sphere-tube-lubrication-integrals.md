# Radius-deficit normalization of sphere-tube lubrication integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication resistance integrals for a nearly occluding sphere](lubrication-resistance-integrals-for-a-nearly-occluding-sphere.md)

When the tube radius is $a$ and the sphere radius is $a(1-\epsilon)$, the minimum gap is $a\epsilon$ and its leading neck profile is $h=a\epsilon+z^2/(2a)$. Substitution $z=a\sqrt{2\epsilon}\,t$ in $I_n^*=a^{n-1}\int h^{-n}dz$ gives the displayed coefficients. Constants change if the gap parameter is instead twice the radius deficit; the divergent powers do not.
