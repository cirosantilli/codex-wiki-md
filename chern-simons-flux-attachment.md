<h1 id="chern-simons-flux-attachment">Chern--Simons flux attachment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [K-matrix](k-matrix.md)

In an Abelian Chern--Simons theory, the equation of motion ties a quasiparticle's gauge charge $q$ to gauge flux $\Phi=2\pi\hbar K^{-1}q$. A quasiparticle of charge $q'$ encircling it therefore acquires the [Aharonov--Bohm phase](aharonov-bohm-effect.md) $2\pi q'^TK^{-1}q$.
