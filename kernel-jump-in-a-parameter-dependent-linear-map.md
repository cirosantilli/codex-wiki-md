# Kernel jump in a parameter-dependent linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md)

For a matrix whose entries depend on a parameter, a nonzero maximal minor certifies a fixed [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) away from its zeros. At a zero, other minors must still be checked: one vanishing minor does not itself imply a rank drop. Once the [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) is determined, the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives the dimension of the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). Exceptional parameters can then be solved separately to exhibit the additional [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) directions.
