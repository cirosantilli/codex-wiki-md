# Lyot stop

↑ **Parent:** [Lyot coronagraph](lyot-coronagraph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyot_stop)

A [Lyot stop](lyot-stop.md) is a mask in the reimaged [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) after a [coronagraphic focal plane mask](coronagraphic-focal-plane-mask.md). For an unobstructed circular [entrance pupil](entrance-pupil.md), it usually transmits a smaller concentric disk. In a classical [Lyot coronagraph](lyot-coronagraph.md), much of the residual stellar field lies near the [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) boundary; the [Lyot stop](lyot-stop.md) rejects that field before the final image is formed. It also rejects useful off-axis [light](light.md), so choosing its opening involves a [coronagraph throughput](coronagraph-throughput.md) tradeoff. [Sivaramakrishnan et al., 2001, §2.1](https://ao.jpl.nasa.gov/Publications/Technical/2001ApJ.sivaramakrishnan.AO.pdf).
