# Flux-side correlation function

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

A time correlation $C_{fs}(t)=\langle\delta(s_0)\dot s_0\Theta(s_t)\rangle$ correlating signed initial flux through a [dividing surface](dividing-surface.md) with product-side occupation at a later time. Dividing by reactant population gives a rate on a plateau after microscopic recrossing and before macroscopic equilibration.
