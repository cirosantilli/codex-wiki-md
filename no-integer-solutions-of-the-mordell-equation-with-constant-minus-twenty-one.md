# No integer solutions of the Mordell equation with constant minus twenty-one

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group of the imaginary quadratic field of discriminant minus eighty-four](ideal-class-group-of-the-imaginary-quadratic-field-of-discriminant-minus-eighty-four.md)

Parity and valuations at 3 and 7 make the ideals $(y+\sqrt{-21})$ and $(y-\sqrt{-21})$ coprime in any hypothetical solution. Their product is a cube, so the first is an ideal cube. The [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) has exponent two, forcing its cube root ideal to be principal. Cubing $a+b\sqrt{-21}$ would then give coefficient $3b(a^2-7b^2)$ on $\sqrt{-21}$, which cannot equal one. This proves the nonexistence of integer solutions without assuming unique element factorization.
