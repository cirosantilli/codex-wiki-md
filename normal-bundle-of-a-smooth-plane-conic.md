# Normal bundle of a smooth plane conic

↑ **Parent:** [Normal bundle](normal-bundle.md)

Embed a [smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md) in $\mathbf P^3$ through a plane. The plane equation and conic equation form a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) of degrees one and two, so its [normal sheaf](normal-sheaf.md) is $\mathcal O_C(1)\oplus\mathcal O_C(2)$. Under $C\cong\mathbf P^1$, hyperplane degree is two, hence this [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) is $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(2)\oplus\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(4)$. The normal exact sequence splits as well because $H^1(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(2))=0$.
