# Isostatic ridge elevation in a plate model

↑ **Parent:** [Isostasy](isostasy.md)

A cold linear plate profile has excess column [mass](mass.md) $\rho_m\alpha\Delta T a/2$ relative to hot [mantle](planetary-mantle.md). Equal [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) below the plates is restored by replacing part of the rock column with water. The displayed elevation follows to first order in [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md); its denominator is the rock-minus-water [density](density.md) difference, not the rock [density](density.md) alone.
