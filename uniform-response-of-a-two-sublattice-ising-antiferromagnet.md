# Uniform response of a two-sublattice Ising antiferromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Two-sublattice Ising mean-field approximation](two-sublattice-ising-mean-field-approximation.md)

On the stable zero-field branch $M_B=-M_W=M$, put $a=\beta(1-M^2)$. Differentiating the [two-sublattice Ising mean-field approximation](two-sublattice-ising-mean-field-approximation.md) with respect to the uniform field gives $\chi_B=a(1-qJ\chi_W)$ and $\chi_W=a(1-qJ\chi_B)$. Thus $\chi_+=a/(1+qJa)$ and $\partial M_-/\partial h=0$. The true staggered [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md), $\partial M_-/\partial g=a/(1-qJa)$, is a different response and diverges at the continuous transition. The factor $1-M^2$ is essential: the uniform response vanishes when the spins saturate at zero temperature.
