# Classification of compact symplectic surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic area](symplectic-area.md)

Two [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) [symplectic surfaces](symplectic-surface.md) are [symplectomorphic](symplectomorphism.md) exactly when their [genus of a surface](genus-of-a-surface.md) and [symplectic area](symplectic-area.md) agree. The sufficiency follows from [Moser theorem](moser-s-trick.md) applied to a positive interpolation of their [symplectic forms](symplectic-form.md) after an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md).
