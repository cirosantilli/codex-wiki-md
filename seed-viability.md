# Seed viability

↑ **Parent:** [Germination](germination.md)

The capacity of a living [seed](seed.md) to produce a developing [plant](plant.md) under suitable conditions. A [seed](seed.md) with [seed dormancy](seed-dormancy.md) can retain [seed viability](seed-viability.md). In an ideal counting exercise, if $g$ of $N$ [seeds](seed.md) complete [germination](germination.md), at least $g$ were living at the start. The other $N-g$ are unresolved: a failed [germination assay](germination-assay.md) alone cannot separate dead [seeds](seed.md), [seed dormancy](seed-dormancy.md) and unsuitable conditions.
