# Transconductance

↑ **Parent:** [Small-signal model](small-signal-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transconductance)

The change of output [electric current](electric-current.md) per change of input [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), evaluated at an [operating point](operating-point.md) with the other terminal conditions specified. In a [small-signal model](small-signal-model.md), $i_{\rm out}=g_m v_{\rm in}$ describes this contribution; [transconductance](transconductance.md) has the dimensions of [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md).
