# One-session treatment of specific phobia

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

A concentrated intervention combining approach practice and [learning](learning.md) about a phobic stimulus. A youth [randomised](randomization.md) trial compared this package with education-support and waiting list. Package efficacy is distinct from proof that one session is the optimal dose for all presentations.[Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology 77, 504–516; 196 youths, exposure, education-support and waiting-list conditions](one-session-treatment-of-specific-phobias-in-youth-a-randomized-clinical-trial-in-the-united-states-and-sweden-by-thomas-h-ollendick-and-others.md)
