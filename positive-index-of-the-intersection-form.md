# Positive index of the intersection form

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

For a closed oriented [smooth four-manifold](smooth-four-manifold.md), $b_2^+$ is the dimension of a maximal positive-definite subspace of its real [intersection form](intersection-form.md). A [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) forces $b_2^+\geq1$, because its [cohomology class](cohomology-class.md) has positive square. Reversing orientation exchanges the positive and negative indices.
