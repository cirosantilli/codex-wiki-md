# Ground temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature-split.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) within soil or rock beneath the ground surface. Its time dependence can be governed by [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md), fluid transport and internal heating; a homogeneous conduction-only approximation gives a [heat equation](heat-equation.md).
