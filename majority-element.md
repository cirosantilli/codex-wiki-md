# Majority element

↑ **Parent:** [Array (data structure)](array-data-structure.md)

An entry occurring strictly more than half the time in a finite [array](array-data-structure.md) or [sequence](sequence.md). At most one distinct value can satisfy this condition: two such occurrence counts would sum to more than the input length. A [majority element](majority-element.md) need not be the smallest, largest or most recently observed entry.
