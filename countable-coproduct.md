# Countable coproduct

↑ **Parent:** [Coproduct](coproduct.md)

A countable coproduct is indexed by the natural numbers. In the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md) it is the disjoint union $\coprod_{n\in\mathbb N}A_n$.
