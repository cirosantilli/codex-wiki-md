# Turning-point enhancement of wave amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Airy scaling at a variable-speed wave turning point](airy-scaling-at-a-variable-speed-wave-turning-point.md)

A simple ray turning point has outer [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) proportional to $|Y-Y_s|^{-1/4}$. At the [Airy scaling at a variable-speed wave turning point](airy-scaling-at-a-variable-speed-wave-turning-point.md), $|Y-Y_s|=O(\varepsilon^{2/3})$, this gives an inner wavefield of size $O(\varepsilon^{-1/6})$ when the incident action normalization is order one. The enhancement is finite for nonzero $\varepsilon$ and is resolved by the [Airy function](airy-function.md).
