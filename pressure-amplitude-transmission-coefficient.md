# Pressure-amplitude transmission coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission coefficient](transmission-coefficient.md)

The transmitted-to-incident compressional [pressure](pressure-split.md) or normal-stress amplitude ratio. At normal incidence, continuity of normal stress gives $t_p=1+r_p$, with [pressure-amplitude reflection coefficient](pressure-amplitude-reflection-coefficient.md) $r_p$. The transmitted [energy](energy-split.md) fraction is $(Z_1/Z_2)t_p^2$, not $t_p^2$ alone.
