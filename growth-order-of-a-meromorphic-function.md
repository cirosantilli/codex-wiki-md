# Growth order of a meromorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna characteristic](nevanlinna-characteristic.md)

The growth order of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) is the upper power exponent of its [Nevanlinna characteristic](nevanlinna-characteristic.md), with constants assigned order zero. It is different from the local signed order at a zero or [pole](pole.md). A [Nevanlinna integrated counting function](nevanlinna-integrated-counting-function.md) has an analogous growth order. The [Nevanlinna first main theorem](nevanlinna-first-main-theorem.md) bounds every counting order by the function order.
