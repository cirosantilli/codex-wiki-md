# Line plume

↑ **Parent:** [Pure plume](pure-plume.md)

A line plume is invariant along one horizontal direction, so its integral fluxes are measured per unit span. A wall line plume entrains through one exposed edge and has width proportional to distance from its [plume virtual origin](plume-virtual-origin.md).
