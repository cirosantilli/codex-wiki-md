# Random affine recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)

A random affine recursion updates a real [random variable](random-variable-split.md) by $X_n=A_nX_{n-1}+B_n$. Assume the coefficient pairs $(A_n,B_n)$ are [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), independent of the initial value. The two coordinates within a pair may be dependent. Fresh coefficients make the recursion a time-homogeneous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md): its next [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) depends on the current value alone.

The competition between the random multiplier and the fresh input determines whether initial conditions are forgotten. A [synchronous coupling](synchronous-coupling.md) makes this particularly visible by using the same coefficient sequence for two initial values.
