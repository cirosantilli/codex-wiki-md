# Group (periodic table)

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic table](periodic-table.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_(periodic_table))

A [periodic group](group-periodic-table.md) is a vertical column of the [periodic table](periodic-table.md). For example, [fluorine](fluorine.md) and [chlorine](chlorine.md) are in [periodic group](group-periodic-table.md) 17, with seven [valence electrons](valence-electron.md) in each neutral [ground state](ground-state.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). A shared outer [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md) supports similarities in [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md), rather than equality of every property.
