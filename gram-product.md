# Gram product

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

A real [matrix](matrix.md) product giving pairwise [dot products](dot-product.md) of columns, for $T^\top T$, or rows, for $TT^\top$. It is a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). The [kernel of a Gram product](kernel-of-a-gram-product.md) is determined by the corresponding factor, which makes these products useful for converting a zero [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) into an exact [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) condition.
