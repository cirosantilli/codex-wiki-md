# Stellar evaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Star cluster](star-cluster.md)

[Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md) redistributes stellar energies and feeds stars above the escape threshold. The escaping population removes mass and generally nonnegative energy at infinity. Radius evolution and external tidal limits must be considered before inferring the change in cluster density.
