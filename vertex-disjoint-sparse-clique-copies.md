# Vertex-disjoint sparse clique copies

↑ **Parent:** [Expected subgraph count in the Erdős-Rényi model](expected-subgraph-count-in-the-erdos-renyi-model.md)

For $p=n^{-2/3}\log n$, the expected number of pairs of $K_4$ copies sharing a vertex is

$$
O(n^7p^{12}+n^6p^{11}+n^5p^9)=o(1).
$$

Thus, with probability tending to one, all the $K_4$ copies are vertex-disjoint. Together with [sparse clique-count concentration](sparse-clique-count-concentration.md), this gives arbitrarily many vertex-disjoint copies with probability tending to one.
