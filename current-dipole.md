# Current dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Current density](current-density.md)

A [current dipole](current-dipole.md) idealizes a localized impressed [electric current density](current-density.md) by the displayed [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) distribution. Its moment $\mathbf Q$ has units of current times length. In a homogeneous [volume conductor](volume-conductor.md) of conductivity $\sigma$, its primary [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is $\Psi(\mathbf r;\mathbf r_0)=\mathbf Q\cdot(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0)/(4\pi\sigma|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|^3)$.
