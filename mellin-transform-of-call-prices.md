# Mellin transform of call prices

↑ **Parent:** [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md)

For $C(k)=\mathbb E(e^\xi-e^k)^+$ and $\operatorname{Re}z>1$ in the [exponential moment](exponential-moment.md) domain, the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) satisfies $\mathbb Ee^{z\xi}=\int_{\mathbb R}C(k)z(z-1)e^{(z-1)k}dk$.
