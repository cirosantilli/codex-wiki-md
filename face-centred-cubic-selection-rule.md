# Face-centred cubic selection rule

↑ **Parent:** [Face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md)

For equivalent scatterers on the four conventional fcc sites, the centering factor is $1+e^{i\pi(h+k)}+e^{i\pi(h+l)}+e^{i\pi(k+l)}$. It is four when the integer indices are all even or all odd, and zero otherwise. An additional [atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) can impose further extinctions. This rule concerns conventional cubic indexing.
