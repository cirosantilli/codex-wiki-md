# Spatial optical soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

A beam with a transverse profile sustained by a balance between [diffraction](diffraction.md) and a nonlinear change of [refractive index](refractive-index.md). A one-transverse-dimensional focusing cubic model supports a stationary bright profile after its propagation [wave phase](phase-waves.md) is removed. Existence in that model does not establish stability in two transverse dimensions or in a saturating, absorbing material.
