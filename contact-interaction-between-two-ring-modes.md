# Contact interaction between two ring modes

↑ **Parent:** [Contact interaction (quantum mechanics)](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md)

Two distinct normalized [plane waves](plane-wave.md) on a periodic one-dimensional ring of length $L$ give the displayed projected [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md). [Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) excludes pair conversion between these two modes. Four mixed operator orderings give the coefficient of $n_1n_2$. At fixed [particle number](particle-number.md) $N$, the [energy](energy-split.md) is $g[N(N-1)+2n_1(N-n_1)]/(2L)$. For repulsive coupling, its minima are the two endpoint [Fock states](fock-state.md) with all particles in one mode; a one-particle energy difference selects the lower mode. This ground-state statement concerns the two-mode projection.
