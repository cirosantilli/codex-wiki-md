# Score factorization implies parameter orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal statistical parameters](orthogonal-statistical-parameters.md)

In a regular common-support [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) with [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $a(\lambda,y)e^{\lambda t(y;\psi)}$, the [score function](informant-function.md) in $\psi$ is $\lambda t_\psi$. Its zero [expectation](expected-value.md) gives $\mathbb E t_\psi=0$ for $\lambda\ne0$. The mixed [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) derivative is $t_\psi$, so the cross [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) block vanishes. At zero the same conclusion follows by continuity when the regular derivatives extend there. This proves [parameter orthogonality](orthogonal-statistical-parameters.md); it does not establish exact independence of fitted parameters.
