# Quadratic density derivative functional

↑ **Parent:** [Density estimation](density-estimation.md)

The squared [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) [derivative](derivative.md) measures roughness and appears in optimal smoothing constants. For $r=2$, its [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) along a smooth compactly supported perturbation $a$ is $2\int f^{\prime\prime}a^{\prime\prime}$. When [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) is valid twice, this equals $2\int f^{(4)}a$. Thus regular root-$n$ estimation requires substantially more regularity than merely estimating a smooth [probability density function](probability-density-function.md).
