# Mod-two cohomology ring of the antipodal two-sphere mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring of the antipodal two-sphere mapping torus](cohomology-ring-of-the-antipodal-two-sphere-mapping-torus.md)

Modulo two the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) acts trivially on fiber [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). The [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md) supplies a class $y$ restricting to the generator of $H^2(S^2;\mathbb F_2)$. The [Leray-Hirsch theorem](leray-hirsch-theorem.md) with classes $1,y$ gives a basis $1,t,y,ty$, so $ty\ne0$. Pullback from the circle gives $t^2=0$, while dimension gives $y^2=0$. This [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) agrees with that of $S^1\times S^2$, although the original manifold is nonorientable.
