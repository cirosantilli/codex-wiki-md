<h1 id="turan-normal-order-theorem-for-distinct-prime-divisors">Turán normal-order theorem for distinct prime divisors</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Normal order of an arithmetic function](normal-order-of-an-arithmetic-function.md)

The [prime omega function](prime-omega-function.md) has normal order $\log\log n$. The elementary second-moment estimate

$$
\sum_{n\leq x}\bigl(\omega(n)-\log\log x\bigr)^2
\ll x\log\log x
$$

already proves concentration on every scale larger than $\sqrt{\log\log x}$.
