# Row and column spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Row_and_column_spaces)

The [row space](row-space.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) is the span of its rows, and its [column space](column-space.md) is the span of its columns. Both have dimension equal to the [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md), though they lie in different ambient coordinate spaces for a rectangular matrix.
