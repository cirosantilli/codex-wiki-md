# Spectral gap for powers of a positive line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Positive holomorphic line bundle](positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

For a positive Hermitian [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) on compact $X$, using its curvature as the [Kähler form](kahler-form.md), a linear lower bound in $k$ for the full [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) on positive antiholomorphic degrees rules out harmonic forms for sufficiently large $k$. The [Dolbeault theorem](dolbeault-theorem.md) and [Dolbeault Hodge decomposition](dolbeault-hodge-decomposition-on-a-compact-hermitian-manifold.md) then imply $H^{q>0}(X,L^k)=0$.
