# Recursive call

↑ **Parent:** [Recursion (computer science)](recursion-computer-science.md)

An invocation made by a [recursive procedure](recursive-procedure.md) of itself, directly or through another procedure. Each invocation has its own arguments and local [variables](variable-mathematics.md); a returning invocation supplies an [output](output-computing.md) to its caller.
