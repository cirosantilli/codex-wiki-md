# Touschek effect

↑ **Parent:** [Beam lifetime](beam-lifetime.md)

Intra-bunch Coulomb scattering can transfer transverse momentum into longitudinal momentum changes large enough to exceed a storage ring's accepted momentum range. This produces particle losses and a density-dependent contribution to the [beam lifetime](beam-lifetime.md).
