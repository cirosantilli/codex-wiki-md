# Two-layer Couette flow

↑ **Parent:** [Couette flow](couette-flow.md)

In two-layer Couette flow, each Newtonian-fluid layer has a linear velocity profile. Continuity of velocity and [shear stress](shear-stress.md) at the interface makes the velocity drop across each layer proportional to its thickness divided by its [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md).
