# Critical cone

↑ **Parent:** [Constrained optimization](constrained-optimization.md)

The critical cone consists of linearized feasible directions along which the [objective function](objective-function.md) has zero first-order change. For constraints $c_i\geq0$, its directions satisfy $Dc_i\,d\geq0$ at active constraints and $\nabla f^Td=0$; equality constraints impose zero linearized residual. Under the usual regularity conditions, second-order [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) test the [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) on this cone. Positive active multipliers force their corresponding directional residuals to vanish.
