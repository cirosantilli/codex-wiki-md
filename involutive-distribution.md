# Involutive distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution (differential geometry)](distribution-differential-geometry.md)

A [smooth distribution](distribution-differential-geometry.md) is involutive if its local smooth sections are closed under the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md). The [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md) identifies this algebraic condition with being an [integrable distribution](integrable-distribution.md).
