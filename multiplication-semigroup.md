# Multiplication semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md)

A nonnegative continuous multiplier $k$ defines a [contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md) on the [space of continuous functions vanishing at infinity](space-of-continuous-functions-vanishing-at-infinity.md) and on $L^2(\mu)$ by $P_tf=e^{-tk}f$. [Strong continuity](strong-continuity.md) follows from [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on [compact sets](compact-space.md) and small tails in $C_0$, or the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) in $L^2$. The multiplier itself may be unbounded.
