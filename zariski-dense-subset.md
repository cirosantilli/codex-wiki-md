# Zariski dense subset

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md)

A subset of an algebraic variety is Zariski dense when its closure in the [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md) is the whole variety. For an affine variety, this is equivalent to saying that every regular function vanishing on the subset vanishes on the entire variety.
