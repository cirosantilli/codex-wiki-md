# Disc diagram spur

↑ **Parent:** [Disc diagram](disc-diagram.md)

A spur is an exposed degree-one vertex and its incident edge in a [disc diagram](disc-diagram.md). The exterior boundary runs out along that edge and immediately back. Thus a spur in the interior of a boundary side contradicts that side being a reduced combinatorial path or a [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md). A diagram with no two-cells may be a tree and have spurs rather than shells.
