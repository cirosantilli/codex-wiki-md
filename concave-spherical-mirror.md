# Concave spherical mirror

↑ **Parent:** [Mirror](mirror.md)

A [concave spherical mirror](concave-spherical-mirror.md) reflects at the inside of a spherical [surface](topological-surface-split.md) of [radius of curvature](radius-of-curvature.md) $R$. For incident [light rays](light-ray.md) near its [optical axis](optical-axis.md), the [law of reflection](law-of-reflection.md) gives paraxial [focal length](focal-length.md) $f=R/2$. Rays farther from the [optical axis](optical-axis.md) have different axial crossing positions, causing [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md); one must specify a criterion to define the best full-aperture focus.
