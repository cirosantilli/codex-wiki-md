# Quantum operator ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md)

Products of noncommuting [linear operators](linear-operator.md) depend on their order. For position and [momentum](momentum-split.md), $xp-px=i\hbar$; substituting classical commuting expressions into a product can therefore lose an additive quantum term. For example, expansion of the oscillator product $a^\dagger a$ includes a [commutator](commutator.md) contribution $-1/2$, leading to the correct [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) in $H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)$.
