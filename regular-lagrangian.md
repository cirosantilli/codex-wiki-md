# Regular Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre transform in mechanics](legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md)

A [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is regular when its velocity [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is invertible. The [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) then makes the fiber derivative $p_i=L_{v^i}$ locally invertible, allowing a local [Legendre transform in mechanics](legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) to a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). If that fiber derivative is globally invertible, the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is called a [hyperregular Lagrangian](hyperregular-lagrangian.md). Regularity alone does not assert global invertibility, and singular [Lagrangians](lagrangian.md) may give constrained rather than ordinary unconstrained [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md).
