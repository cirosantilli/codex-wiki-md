# Lag of a time series

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

A [lag of a time series](lag-of-a-time-series.md) is a separation between observation indices. In an equally spaced [time series](time-series-split.md), lag $h=3$ compares observations three intervals apart: for hourly readings this is three hours, while for daily readings it is three days. Positive and negative lags describe the same separation in opposite directions. The [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) and [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) of a real [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) are even functions of lag.
