# Low-dimensional SU3 tensor representations

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md)

The six smallest complex [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) of [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) have [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(2,0),(0,2),(1,1)$ and [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) $1,3,3,6,6,8$. Their [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) row lengths are $(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,2),(2,1)$. They are realized by scalars, defining [vectors](vector.md), [covectors](covector.md) (equivalently [antisymmetric tensors](antisymmetric-tensor.md) of degree two), upper and lower [symmetric tensors](symmetric-tensor.md) of degree two, and mixed [tensors](tensor.md) $T^i{}_j$ with zero [trace](matrix-trace.md), respectively. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) is $\dim(p,q)=(p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2)/2$. For $p+q\geq3$ this is at least ten, proving that the list exhausts the six smallest [representations](group-representation.md).
