# Air resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Drag (physics)](drag-physics.md)

The [drag force](drag-physics.md) exerted by air on a body moving relative to it. Neglecting it is an approximation whose accuracy depends on the body, its [speed](speed.md) and the ambient [gas](gas.md).
