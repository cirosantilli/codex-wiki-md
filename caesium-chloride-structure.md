# Caesium chloride structure

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

A binary [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) with one species at the corners of a primitive cubic [unit cell](unit-cell.md) and the other at its body centre. Each species has [coordination number](coordination-number.md) eight. Distinct chemical labels mean that the body-centre translation is not a [translation symmetry](translational-symmetry.md); its [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) is primitive cubic, not body-centred cubic.
