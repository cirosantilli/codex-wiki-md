# Torque transport of energy in a steady accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md)

For a steady [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) with inward [accretion rate](accretion-rate.md) $\dot M$ and vanishing inner [torque](torque.md), the outward angular-momentum torque is $\mathcal G=\dot M(\sqrt{GMr}-\sqrt{GMr_{\rm in}})$. It transports [energy](energy.md) outward at rate $\Omega\mathcal G$. The binding-energy acquisition rate between radii $b$ and $a$ is $B_{a,b}=GM\dot M(1/a-1/b)/2$. Integrating the vertical [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) and then the annulus gives

$$
D_{a,b}=GM\dot M\left[\frac32\left(\frac1a-\frac1b\right)-\sqrt{r_{\rm in}}(a^{-3/2}-b^{-3/2})\right].
$$

Since $\Omega\mathcal G=GM\dot M[r^{-1}-\sqrt{r_{\rm in}}r^{-3/2}]$, subtraction proves the displayed energy balance. Far from the inner edge $D_{a,b}\simeq3B_{a,b}$, whereas the boundary torque powers vanish for the complete disk and $D=B=GM\dot M/(2r_{\rm in})$. Local dissipation is therefore not supplied solely by local changes in orbital binding energy.
