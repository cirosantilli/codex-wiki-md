# Finite-dimensional complex Lorentz representations

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral decomposition of the complex Lorentz algebra](chiral-decomposition-of-the-complex-lorentz-algebra.md)

The complexified [Lorentz algebra](lorentz-algebra.md) splits into two commuting $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ factors. Extending two [homogeneous polynomial representations of SU2](homogeneous-polynomial-representation-of-su2.md) therefore gives every finite-dimensional irreducible complex [group representation](group-representation.md) of the [Lorentz spinor double cover](lorentz-spinor-double-cover.md). The kernel sign permits descent precisely when $j_L+j_R$ is an integer. These field [group representations](group-representation.md) are generally nonunitary for a positive-definite inner product; infinite-dimensional unitary Lorentz [group representations](group-representation.md) belong to a different construction.
