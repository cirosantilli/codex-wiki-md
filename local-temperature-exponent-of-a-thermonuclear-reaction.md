# Local temperature exponent of a thermonuclear reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Thermonuclear reaction rate](thermonuclear-reaction-rate.md)

If a charged-particle rate coefficient has $\lambda\propto\eta^2e^{-\eta}$ with $\eta\propto T^{-1/3}$, its logarithmic [temperature](temperature.md) derivative is $(\eta-2)/3$. This is a local slope evaluated at fixed composition. It increases as [temperature](temperature.md) falls, so extrapolating a single power law across a large [temperature](temperature.md) range can be inaccurate.
