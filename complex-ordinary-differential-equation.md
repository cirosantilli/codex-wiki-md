# Complex ordinary differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A first-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) with complex independent and dependent variables is solved by a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on an ordinary domain, and its [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) can have [branch points](branch-point.md). A [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) right-hand side gives local existence and uniqueness by the integral [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md) used in the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](picard-lindelof-theorem.md).
