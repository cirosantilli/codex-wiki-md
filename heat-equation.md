# Heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_equation)

The heat equation

$$
u_t=D\nabla^2u,
\qquad D>0,
$$

models diffusion with constant diffusivity $D$.
