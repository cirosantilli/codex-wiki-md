# Gravitational dielectric function

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

For a homogeneous collisionless stellar model with its mean field subtracted, the response to an imposed [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) is $\widetilde\Phi^{\mathrm{tot}}_{\mathbf k}=\widetilde\Phi^{\mathrm{ext}}_{\mathbf k}/\epsilon_{\mathbf k}$, where

$$
\epsilon_{\mathbf k}(\omega)=1+\frac{4\pi G}{k^2}\int d^3v\,\frac{\mathbf k\cdot\partial_{\mathbf v}f_0}{\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v-\omega}.
$$

The [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) $f_0$ is mass-normalized, and $\mathbf k\ne0$. Causal [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) uses the [Landau contour](landau-contour.md) wherever the equilibrium permits it. Zeros describe collective [Landau modes](landau-mode.md). This response function is a gravitational analogue of the electrostatic [dielectric function](dielectric-function.md); its susceptibility sign reflects attraction.
