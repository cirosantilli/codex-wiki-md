# Rectangular section second moment of area

↑ **Parent:** [Second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md)

For a rectangular cross-section of horizontal width $a$ and vertical thickness $b$, the [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md) about its horizontal [centroid](centroid.md) axis is $I=a\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}z^2\,dz=ab^3/12$. Thus increasing vertical thickness is especially effective at reducing the [curvature](curvature.md) caused by a given [bending moment](bending-moment.md).
