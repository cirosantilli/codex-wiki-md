# Semisimple Lie ideal has a commuting ideal complement

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) which is an ideal in a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has the displayed direct complement. The restriction of the [Killing form](killing-form.md) of $M$ to $L$ equals the [Killing form](killing-form.md) of $L$: the induced adjoint maps of elements of $L$ on $M/L$ vanish. This restriction is nondegenerate by the [Cartan criterion for semisimplicity](cartan-criterion-for-semisimplicity.md). Invariance makes its orthogonal complement an ideal. Nondegeneracy on $L$ gives a direct vector-space sum with that complement, and the bracket of the two ideals lies in their zero intersection, so they commute. The ambient algebra need not itself be semisimple.
