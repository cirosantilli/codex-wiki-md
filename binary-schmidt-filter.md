# Binary Schmidt filter

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement concentration](entanglement-concentration.md)

For a pure state with two descending squared [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) $\lambda_0\ge\lambda_1$, this [quantum instrument](quantum-instrument.md) acts in one party's [Schmidt basis](schmidt-basis.md). The first outcome balances the two Schmidt amplitudes when $\tan\theta=\sqrt{\lambda_1/\lambda_0}$, with probability $2\lambda_0\lambda_1$. This is not the optimal unrestricted success probability $2\lambda_1$ in [single-copy entanglement concentration](single-copy-entanglement-concentration.md). An embedding in a larger subsystem requires a [POVM on the occupied support](povm-on-the-occupied-support.md) or a full-space completion.
