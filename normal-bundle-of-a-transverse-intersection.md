# Normal bundle of a transverse intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Normal bundle](normal-bundle.md)

For a [transverse intersection](transverse-intersection.md), the quotient map $TM|_L\to\nu_1|_L\oplus\nu_2|_L$ is onto and has kernel $TL$. This gives the displayed isomorphism of actual [normal bundles](normal-bundle.md). [Complex structures](complex-structure.md) on the two [normal bundles](normal-bundle.md) therefore induce a [complex structure](complex-structure.md) on the intersection's [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md).
