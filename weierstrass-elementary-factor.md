# Weierstrass elementary factor

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite product](infinite-product.md)

The Weierstrass elementary factor has a simple zero at $w=1$ and no other zeros. Its logarithmic series begins at degree $p+1$: $\log E_p(w)=-\sum_{\nu\geq p+1}w^\nu/\nu$ for $|w|<1$. Raising the factor order $p$ makes the tail arbitrarily small on a fixed compact set and enables [infinite product convergence from logarithmic tails](infinite-product-convergence-from-logarithmic-tails.md) for unrestricted discrete zero data.
