# Virtually abelian groups are amenable

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

Every [abelian group](abelian-group.md) is amenable, and amenability is preserved by finite extensions. Hence every group with an abelian [finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md) is an [amenable group](amenable-group.md).
