# Layered convection

↑ **Parent:** [Double-diffusive convection](double-diffusive-convection.md)

Layered convection consists of mixed convecting layers separated by more weakly transporting interfaces. In a giant planet, composition gradients can support such interfaces and reduce heat loss, contributing to [delayed cooling of an inflated giant planet](delayed-cooling-of-an-inflated-giant-planet.md).
