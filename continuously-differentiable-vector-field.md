# Continuously differentiable vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) on an open subset of Euclidean space whose component [functions](function-split.md) have continuous first [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md). Its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) is continuous; this is a standard regularity assumption for the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) and [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md).
