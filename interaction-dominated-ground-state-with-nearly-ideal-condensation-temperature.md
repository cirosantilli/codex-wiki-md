# Interaction-dominated ground state with nearly ideal condensation temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

In a box of size $L$, confinement [energy](energy-split.md) scales as $\hbar^2/(mL^2)$, transition [temperature](temperature-split.md) as that scale times $N^{2/3}$, and mean interaction [energy](energy-split.md) as $\hbar^2Na/(mL^3)$. The displayed window therefore makes interaction [energy](energy-split.md) large compared with confinement but small compared with $k_BT_c$. It simultaneously implies dilute-gas parameter $na^3\ll1$. Ground-state interaction dominance does not require a large relative shift of the condensation [temperature](temperature-split.md).
