# Spectral kernel expansion of a reversible chain

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md)

For a finite [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) satisfying [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) with $\pi$, its [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) is a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) for $\langle f,g\rangle_\pi=\sum_x\pi(x)f(x)g(x)$. For a real [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) $(f_j)$ with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $(\lambda_j)$,

$$
\frac{P^t(x,y)}{\pi(y)}=\sum_j\lambda_j^t f_j(x)f_j(y).
$$

Indeed, apply the [spectral decomposition](spectral-decomposition.md) of $P^t$ to the function $\mathbf1_{\{y\}}/\pi(y)$, whose coefficient against $f_j$ is $f_j(y)$. For $t=0$, every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) multiplier is interpreted as one.
