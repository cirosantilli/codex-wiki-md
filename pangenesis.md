# Pangenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Heredity](heredity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangenesis)

[Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md)'s proposed material account of [heredity](heredity.md), in which contributions from parts of the organism could be represented in reproduction. This is distinct from his mechanism of [natural selection](natural-selection.md), which does not depend on the truth of [pangenesis](pangenesis.md).
