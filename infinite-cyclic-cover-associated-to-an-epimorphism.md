# Infinite cyclic cover associated to an epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

The connected [covering space](covering-space.md) corresponding to $\ker\alpha$ has [deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md) $\mathbb Z$. Choosing a deck generator makes its integral [homology](homology-split.md) a [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}]$.
