# Frobenius monoidal functor

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal functor](monoidal-functor.md)

A [lax monoidal functor](monoidal-functor.md) $\varphi$ and [opmonoidal functor](opmonoidal-functor.md) $\psi$ on the same underlying [functor](functor.md), satisfying $\psi_{A\otimes B,C}\varphi_{A,B\otimes C}=(\varphi_{A,B}\otimes1)(1\otimes\psi_{B,C})$ and $\psi_{A,B\otimes C}\varphi_{A\otimes B,C}=(1\otimes\varphi_{B,C})(\psi_{A,B}\otimes1)$ in coherent notation. It transports [dual pairs in a monoidal category](dual-pair-in-a-monoidal-category.md) and [Frobenius monoids](frobenius-monoid.md).
