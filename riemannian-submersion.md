# Riemannian submersion

↑ **Parent:** [Submersion](submersion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_submersion)

A surjective [submersion](submersion.md) between [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) is Riemannian when its derivative is a [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) from the orthogonal complement of its kernel onto the target [tangent space](tangent-space.md). Its vertical space is the kernel and its horizontal space is the orthogonal complement.
