# Displacement energy (symplectic geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Hofer metric](hofer-metric.md)

The displacement energy of a subset of a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) is the infimum of the energy norms of compactly supported [Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms](hamiltonian-isotopy.md) moving it off itself. The infimum is $+\infty$ if no such map exists. One must use the same chosen norm convention in both this definition and any energy lower bound. A compact set can have zero displacement energy even when every nonidentity map has positive norm.
