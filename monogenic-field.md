# Monogenic field

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind factorization theorem](dedekind-factorization-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monogenic_field)

A number field $K$ is monogenic when its ring of integers has a power integral basis, meaning $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\theta]$ for some algebraic integer $\theta$.
