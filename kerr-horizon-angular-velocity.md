# Kerr horizon angular velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)

The outer [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) of a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) is generated by $k+\Omega_Hm$, with the stationary [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $k$ normalized at infinity and the axial generator $m$ having $2\pi$-periodic orbits. In [ingoing Kerr coordinates](ingoing-kerr-coordinates.md) its generators satisfy $d\chi/dv=\Omega_H$. This is the horizon's angular velocity relative to the nonrotating asymptotic stationary frame.
