# Cotype 2 extrapolation on finite-dimensional linfinity

↑ **Parent:** [Cotype 2](cotype-2.md)

If a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $Y$ has [cotype 2](cotype-2.md) constant $C$, every [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:\ell_\infty^N\to Y$ satisfies $\pi_2(T)\le3^{1/4}C\pi_4(T)$ and $\pi_2(T)\le4\sqrt3 C^2\|T\|$, uniformly in $N$. The first bound follows from [Pietsch factorization theorem](pietsch-factorization-theorem.md) and the fourth moment bound for [Rademacher sums](rademacher-sum.md). For the second, 2-summing domination gives a weighted coordinate $L^2$ norm; clipping coordinates at a variable threshold yields $\pi_4(T)\le2\sqrt{\|T\|\pi_2(T)}$, which absorbs the first bound.
