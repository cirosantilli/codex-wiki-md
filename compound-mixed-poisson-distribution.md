# Compound mixed Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

A compound mixed Poisson distribution is a [random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md) whose count has a [Poisson mixture](poisson-mixture.md) law: conditional on a nonnegative random intensity $\Lambda$, $N$ has [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\Lambda$. The claim sizes are [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), independent of the intensity and count mechanism. If $M_X$ is their [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md), then $M_S(t)=\mathbb E\exp(\Lambda(M_X(t)-1))$ wherever finite. A [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) intensity of shape $r$ and rate $\alpha$ gives a [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md) count with shape $r$ and success probability $\alpha/(\alpha+1)$. Independent intensities with common gamma rate add their shapes by [additivity of independent gamma distributions with a common rate](additivity-of-independent-gamma-distributions-with-a-common-rate.md).
