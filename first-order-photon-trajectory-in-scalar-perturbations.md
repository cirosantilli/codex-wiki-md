# First-order photon trajectory in scalar perturbations

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal optical metric for scalar perturbations](conformal-optical-metric-for-scalar-perturbations.md)

For reception at the origin with unit propagation direction $e$, the [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) condition fixes $v(\eta_0)=(1+2\Psi_0)e$. The perturbative spatial acceleration is $x^{i\prime\prime}=2e^i d\Psi/d\eta-2(\delta^{ij}-e^ie^j)\partial_j\Psi$. Its integrated trajectory is $x^i=e^i(\eta-\eta_0)+2e^i\int_{\eta_0}^{\eta}\Psi\,d\eta^{\prime}-2\int_{\eta_0}^{\eta}(\eta-\eta^{\prime})(\delta^{ij}-e^ie^j)\partial_j\Psi\,d\eta^{\prime}$. Potentials in these already first-order integrals can be evaluated on the straight background ray.
