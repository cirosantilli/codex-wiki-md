# Rapid artery-ventricle pressure equilibration

↑ **Parent:** [Ventricular elastance](ventricular-elastance.md)

If an open aortic valve equilibrates pressure much faster than peripheral outflow, the arteries and ventricle share a common pressure while their combined blood volume is conserved during the short adjustment. Let arterial [vascular compliance](vascular-compliance.md) be $C_a$ and systolic [ventricular elastance](ventricular-elastance.md) be $E_s$. Equating the initial and final arterial-plus-ventricular stored volumes gives the displayed pressure. Subsequent peripheral drainage sees the combined compliance $C_a+1/E_s$; during filling a closed aortic valve removes the ventricular contribution.
