# Hovering rocket mass flow

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md)

For constant gravity and downward [rocket exhaust speed](rocket-exhaust-speed.md) $u$, hovering requires [rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md) $u\alpha=Mg$ with $\alpha=-\dot M$. Hence $M=M_0e^{-gt/u}$ and the required [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) decays exponentially. A nonzero [rocket dry mass](rocket-dry-mass.md) ends the hover at finite time, even though this mass equation tends to zero only asymptotically.
