# Thermal ionization of excitons

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton binding energy](exciton-binding-energy.md)

Thermal [energy](energy-split.md) comparable with an [exciton binding energy](exciton-binding-energy.md) promotes dissociation and often washes out narrow bound-exciton resonances. Comparing $k_BT$ with binding [energy](energy-split.md) is a scale estimate, not an exact disappearance criterion: densities, screening, oscillator strength and linewidths also matter.
