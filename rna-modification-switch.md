# RNA modification switch

↑ **Parent:** [RNA secondary structure](rna-secondary-structure.md)

A regulatory mechanism in which a chemical modification changes [RNA secondary structure](rna-secondary-structure.md) and thereby exposes or occludes a functional element. Modification dependence of binding alone does not distinguish this mechanism from direct chemical recognition.[Abstract and Figure 1](n6-methyladenosine-dependent-rna-structural-switches-regulate-rna-protein-interactions-by-nian-liu-and-others.md)
