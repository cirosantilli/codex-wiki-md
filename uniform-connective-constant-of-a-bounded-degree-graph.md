# Uniform connective constant of a bounded-degree graph

↑ **Parent:** [Connective constant](connective-constant.md)

For an infinite connected [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md) with a uniform degree bound, let $\sigma_n(v)$ count its length-$n$ [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md) from $v$. The suprema satisfy $\sigma_{m+n}\leq\sigma_m\sigma_n$, so the [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) applied to their logarithms gives a finite [connective constant](connective-constant.md). Unlike the usual rooted definition on a [vertex-transitive graph](vertex-transitive-graph.md), this definition explicitly takes the supremum over roots.
