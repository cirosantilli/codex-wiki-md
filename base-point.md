# Base point

↑ **Parent:** [Based space](based-space.md)

A [base point](base-point.md) is a distinguished point $x_0$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$, making $(X,x_0)$ a [based space](based-space.md). A [based loop](based-loop.md) starts and ends at that point, and a map of [based spaces](based-space.md) must preserve the distinguished points.
