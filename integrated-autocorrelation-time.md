# Integrated autocorrelation time

↑ **Parent:** [Effective sample size of a Markov chain](effective-sample-size-of-a-markov-chain.md)

For a stationary [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) and a scalar observable with summable [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) $\rho(k)$, the asymptotic variance of its sample mean is $\operatorname{Var}(h)\tau_{\rm int}/n$. Thus the [effective sample size of a Markov chain](effective-sample-size-of-a-markov-chain.md) is approximately $n/\tau_{\rm int}$. Different observables can have very different autocorrelation times.
