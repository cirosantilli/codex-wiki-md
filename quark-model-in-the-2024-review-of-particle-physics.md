# Quark Model in the 2024 Review of Particle Physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

Official review: [https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-quark-model.pdf](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-quark-model.pdf)

Section 15.3 discusses [mesons](meson.md); section 15.5.2, printed p. 19, explains that adding the [charm quark](charm-quark.md) gives flavour $SU(4)$, which is strongly broken by the large [charm quark](charm-quark.md) [mass](mass.md) but remains useful for classification. This supports distinguishing an approximate flavour symmetry from the existence of a four-flavour [quark model](quark-model.md).
