# Pasting ordered stopping times

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping-time sigma-algebra](stopping-time-sigma-algebra.md)

If $S\leq T$ are [stopping times](stopping-time.md) and $A\in\mathcal F_S$, then $U=S\mathbf1_A+T\mathbf1_{A^c}$ is a [stopping time](stopping-time.md). The key identity is

$$
A^c\cap\{T\leq t\}=\{T\leq t\}\setminus(A\cap\{S\leq t\}).
$$

This uses the ordering $S\leq T$: without it, $A$ need not be known when $T$ occurs. Such pastings test the [martingale](martingale-split.md) identity through [expectations](expected-value.md) at [bounded stopping times](bounded-stopping-time.md).
