# Polynomial vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The real [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $k$, together with the [zero polynomial](zero-polynomial.md), form a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $k+1$ with [basis](basis.md) $1,x,\ldots,x^k$. [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md) spaces of lower degree are nested [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md).
