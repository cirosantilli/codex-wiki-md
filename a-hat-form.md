# A-hat form

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Weil homomorphism](chern-weil-homomorphism.md)

For the [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) matrix $R$ of a metric [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) on a real oriented [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), the A-hat form is the even [differential form](differential-form-split.md) defined by the displayed finite-degree [power series](power-series.md), with square root having constant term one. The expression is independent of the choice of orthonormal frame. Equivalently, for formal paired curvature [Chern roots](chern-root.md) $\pm x_j$, it is $\prod_j(x_j/2)/\sinh(x_j/2)$. The [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) makes it closed, and changing the connection changes it by an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md) via [Chern-Weil connection transgression](chern-weil-connection-transgression.md). Its degree-four part is $-p_1/24$, where $p_1=-\operatorname{tr}(R\wedge R)/(8\pi^2)$ is the first [Pontryagin class](pontryagin-class.md) form.
