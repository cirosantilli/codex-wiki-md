# Rossby-wave downstream development

↑ **Parent:** [Shorter Rossby-wave propagation inside a stationary long wave](shorter-rossby-wave-propagation-inside-a-stationary-long-wave.md)

A wave packet can transfer energy downstream faster than individual phase features move, allowing successive downstream disturbances to develop while upstream ones weaken. In the simple uniform-current barotropic model with zero meridional wavenumber, the zonal group speed is $U+\beta/k^2$ while phase speed is $U-\beta/k^2$. Linear superposition and interference are not direct nonlinear eddy scattering. Real weather development also involves baroclinic energy conversion and wave–mean-flow feedback, which require additional dynamics.
