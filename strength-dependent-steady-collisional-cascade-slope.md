# Strength-dependent steady collisional-cascade slope

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md)

With size-independent collision speed and [catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md) $Q_D^*\propto D^b$, the [catastrophic planetesimal collision rate](catastrophic-planetesimal-collision-rate.md) scales as $R\propto D^{3-\alpha+b(1-\alpha)/3}$. Multiplying by [mass per logarithmic size bin](mass-per-logarithmic-size-bin.md) gives processed mass proportional to $D^{7-2\alpha+b(1-\alpha)/3}$. [Constant mass flux in a collisional cascade](constant-mass-flux-in-a-collisional-cascade.md) sets the exponent to zero, yielding the displayed differential-number index when $b\ne-6$. This is the zero-velocity-exponent case of a [velocity-dependent collisional cascade](velocity-dependent-collisional-cascade.md).
