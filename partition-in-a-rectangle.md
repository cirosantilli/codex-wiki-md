# Partition in a rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Integer partition](integer-partition.md)

An [integer partition](integer-partition.md) whose [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) has at most $h$ rows and at most $w$ cells per row. Zeros may pad the list of row lengths. The [partition interpretation of Gaussian binomial coefficients](partition-interpretation-of-gaussian-binomial-coefficients.md) counts these diagrams by their number of cells.
