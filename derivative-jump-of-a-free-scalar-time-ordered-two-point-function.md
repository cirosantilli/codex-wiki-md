# Derivative jump of a free scalar time-ordered two-point function

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar Feynman propagator pole prescription](scalar-feynman-propagator-pole-prescription.md)

At fixed spatial [momentum](momentum.md) with mode [energy](energy.md) $E>0$, $g_E(t)=e^{-iE|t|}/(2E)$ is continuous, with derivatives $-i/2$ and $+i/2$ at zero from opposite sides. Hence its second [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) has a $-i\delta(t)$ term, while away from zero $g''_E+E^2g_E=0$. Therefore $(\partial_t^2+E^2)g_E=-i\delta(t)$ and, after the spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md), $(\Box+m^2)\Delta_F=-i\delta^4$. This checks the propagator numerator, contour signs and equal-time [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) independently of residue calculation.
