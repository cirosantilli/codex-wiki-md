# Haldane prior

↑ **Parent:** [Improper prior](improper-prior.md)

The Haldane prior has formal [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) parameters $(0,0)$. It is an [improper prior](improper-prior.md), with infinite mass at both endpoints of $(0,1)$. For a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) likelihood with $s$ successes and $f$ failures, its posterior is $\operatorname{Beta}(s,f)$ only when $s,f>0$. A density flat in the log odds induces this kernel by the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $d\log[p/(1-p)]/dp=1/[p(1-p)]$.
