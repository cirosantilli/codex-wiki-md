# Inner-boundary selection of isothermal Bondi flow

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal Bondi accretion](isothermal-bondi-accretion.md)

For steady [Isothermal Bondi accretion](isothermal-bondi-accretion.md) from gas at rest at infinity, define $\lambda=\dot M c_s^3/(4\pi G^2M^2\rho_\infty)$, $x=r/r_s$ and $y=u/c_s>0$, where $r_s=GM/(2c_s^2)$. The [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md) and [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) give $y^2/2-\ln y=2\ln x+2/x-\ln(4\lambda)$. The left side has minimum $1/2$ at $y=1$, and the radius-dependent part on the right has minimum two at $x=1$. Existence over all positive radii therefore requires the displayed bound. Equality permits the smooth accelerating-inward [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) and gives the usual [isothermal Bondi accretion rate](isothermal-bondi-accretion-rate.md). Smaller positive rates have an everywhere-subsonic reservoir-connected branch, and zero rate gives a hydrostatic atmosphere. An absorbing inner boundary inside the sonic radius selects the critical inflow; steady isothermal equations and the outer density alone do not specify that inner condition.
