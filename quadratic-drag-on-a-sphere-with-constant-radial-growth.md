# Quadratic drag on a sphere with constant radial growth

↑ **Parent:** [Surface-fed growth of an accreting sphere](surface-fed-growth-of-an-accreting-sphere.md)

For [surface-fed growth of an accreting sphere](surface-fed-growth-of-an-accreting-sphere.md) with $\mu=\dot r>0$, stationary incoming material, and [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) $F_D=C_D\rho_a\pi r^2v^2/2$, the [variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md) obeys $\dot v+3\mu v/r=g-3C_D\rho_av^2/(8\rho r)$. For constant positive parameters, $r\sim\mu t$ and the late balance gives the displayed [speed](speed.md). The omitted acceleration and accretion terms are both $O(t^{-1/2})$, consistent with the balance. Thus this growing sphere has unbounded [speed](speed.md) with vanishing [acceleration](acceleration.md), rather than a finite [terminal velocity](terminal-velocity.md). This model has a different growth law from the existing [accreting raindrop with quadratic drag](accreting-raindrop-with-quadratic-drag.md), whose growth rate is proportional to speed.
