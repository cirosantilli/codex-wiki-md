# Dedekind completion

↑ **Parent:** [Order completeness](order-completeness.md)

The completion of a [total order](total-order.md) by its nonprincipal proper cuts, supplying least upper bounds. For a dense order it contains the original order densely, and preserves the interval countable chain condition and separability or nonseparability.
