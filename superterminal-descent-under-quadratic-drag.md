# Superterminal descent under quadratic drag

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md)

Let downward [velocity](velocity.md) $v$ obey $\dot v=a-bv^2$, with constants $a,b>0$, [terminal velocity](terminal-velocity.md) $v_t=\sqrt{a/b}$ and initial [speed](speed.md) $u>v_t$. Gravity minus [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) can supply the constant downward [force](force-split.md) represented by $a$. Since $\dot z=v$, the equation for $v^2$ is linear in depth: $d(v^2)/dz=-2b(v^2-v_t^2)$, giving the displayed decay. Exact [terminal velocity](terminal-velocity.md) is approached only asymptotically. A practical tolerance $v\leq(1+\epsilon)v_t$ is attained at $z_\epsilon=(2b)^{-1}\ln[(u^2/v_t^2-1)/(2\epsilon+\epsilon^2)]$, provided the initial excess exceeds that tolerance. Its [time](time-in-physics.md)-domain form is $v=v_t\coth(bv_tt+\chi)$ with $\chi=\operatorname{arctanh}(v_t/u)$, in contrast to the [hyperbolic tangent](hyperbolic-tangent.md) for [descent from rest under quadratic drag](descent-from-rest-under-quadratic-drag.md).
