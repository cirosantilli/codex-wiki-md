# World tube

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime](spacetime.md)

The region of [spacetime](spacetime.md) occupied by an extended object through its history. Different simultaneous slices intersect a moving tube in different endpoint events, explaining why [length contraction](length-contraction.md) compares different sections rather than successive physical compressions.
