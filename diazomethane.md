# Diazomethane

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

$\mathrm{CH_2N_2}$, with a linear C–N–N skeleton and a planar carbon environment. Important [resonance contributors](resonance-structure.md) are $\mathrm{H_2C=N^+=N^-}$ and $\mathrm{H_2C^--N^+\equiv N}$.
