# Dynamical surface density from vertical stellar motions

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical Jeans equation](vertical-jeans-equation.md)

The [vertical Jeans equation](vertical-jeans-equation.md) determines $K_z=\partial_z\Phi$ from a tracer's density and [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md), with a correction for the tilted [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md). Integrating the axisymmetric [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) gives

$$
2\pi G\Sigma_{\rm dyn}(<|z|)=K_z(z)+\int_0^z\frac1R\partial_R(R\partial_R\Phi)\,dz'
$$

for $z>0$ and reflection symmetry. Subtracting independently estimated stellar and gas [surface densities](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) constrains any additional gravitating component; a [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) also contributes, so an excess is not automatically a dark disk.
