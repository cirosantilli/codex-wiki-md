# Galvanometer

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

A [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) detects [electric current](electric-current.md) through a mechanical response, such as the deflection of a pointer.[Analog and Digital Meters: current-driven pointer response](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/10-4-electrical-measuring-instruments.md)

A sensitive [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) makes a small [electric current](electric-current.md) visible; a calibrated deflection also estimates its magnitude. A center-zero scale distinguishes both signs of [electric current](electric-current.md). The [moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md) rotates a coil in an imposed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), whereas a [moving-magnet galvanometer](moving-magnet-galvanometer.md) rotates a magnetic needle in the coil's [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The spring-balanced model below describes the [moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md), not every instrument called a [galvanometer](galvanometer.md).
