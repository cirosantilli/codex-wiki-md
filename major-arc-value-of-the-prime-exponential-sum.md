# Major-arc value of the prime exponential sum

↑ **Parent:** [Siegel–Walfisz theorem](siegel-walfisz-theorem.md)

For $(a,q)=1$ and $q\le\log^A N$, apply the [Siegel–Walfisz theorem](siegel-walfisz-theorem.md) to every reduced residue class with error exponent $A+B+2$. The main terms sum to $N c_q(a)/\varphi(q)$, where the [Ramanujan sum](ramanujan-sum.md) satisfies $c_q(a)=\mu(q)$ for coprime $a,q$. Nonunits with nonzero [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md) are powers of primes dividing $q$ and contribute at most $\omega(q)\log N$, which is absorbed by the error. For arbitrary $a$, the correct main term is $N c_q(a)/\varphi(q)$; the reduced numerator hypothesis cannot be omitted.
