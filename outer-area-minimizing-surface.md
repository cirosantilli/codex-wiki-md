# Outer area-minimizing surface

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal surface](minimal-surface.md)

An [outer area-minimizing surface](outer-area-minimizing-surface.md) is a closed surface in a Riemannian initial-data slice whose area is no greater than that of any enclosing competitor. This is a variational property, not a consequence of containment alone. It is important when choosing the horizon-area quantity in the [Riemannian Penrose inequality](riemannian-penrose-inequality.md) and when examining an [apparent-horizon area comparison](apparent-horizon-area-comparison.md).
