# Perpendicular rovibrational band

↑ **Parent:** [Rovibrational spectroscopy](rovibrational-spectroscopy.md)

A symmetric-top vibrational band whose transition dipole is perpendicular to the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) axis, with $\Delta K=\pm1$. Unresolved Q subbranches can create a regular sequence controlled mainly by $A-B$.
