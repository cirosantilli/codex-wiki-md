# Discriminant ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Trace pairing](trace-pairing.md)

For the integral closure $S$ of a [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md) $R$ in a finite separable extension, the discriminant ideal is locally generated by

$$
\det\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\alpha_i\alpha_j)\bigr),
$$

where $(\alpha_i)$ is a local $R$-basis of $S$. Intrinsically it is the image of $(\det_R S)^{\otimes2}$ under the determinant of the [trace pairing](trace-pairing.md).
