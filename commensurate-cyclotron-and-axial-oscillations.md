# Commensurate cyclotron and axial oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform magnetic field with axial harmonic confinement](uniform-magnetic-field-with-axial-harmonic-confinement.md)

A rational ratio of axial and [cyclotron frequencies](cyclotron-frequency.md) makes a nondegenerate combined orbit close after an integer number of turns. For ratio $p/q$ in lowest terms it closes after $q$ transverse periods and $p$ axial periods. An integer ratio is a sufficient special case. An irrational ratio instead gives [quasiperiodic motion](quasiperiodic-motion.md) when both component amplitudes are nonzero.
