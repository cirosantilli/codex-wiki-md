# Displacement reflection from an interface between two inviscid elastic liquids

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic impedance](acoustic-impedance.md)

For a P-wave incident at angle $\theta$ from a liquid $(\rho,c,\lambda)$ onto $(\rho',c',\lambda')$, with transmitted angle $\theta'$ obeying [Snell law for elastic and acoustic waves](snell-law-for-elastic-and-acoustic-waves.md),

$$
R=\frac{\lambda'\sin2\theta-\lambda\sin2\theta'}
{\lambda'\sin2\theta+\lambda\sin2\theta'}.
$$

No reflection is equivalent to $\rho'c'\cos\theta=\rho c\cos\theta'$.
