# Cloud droplet effective radius

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud-droplet size distribution](cloud-droplet-size-distribution.md)

The ratio of the third to the second radius moment determines projected extinction area per liquid mass. For size-independent extinction efficiency $Q$, the local [particle extinction coefficient](particle-extinction-coefficient.md) is $3Qw/(4\rho_wr_e)$. It is not generally the mean radius or the mean-volume radius. In [adiabatic cloud droplet moments](adiabatic-cloud-droplet-moments.md), the shape factor gives $r_e=r_v/k^{1/3}$.
