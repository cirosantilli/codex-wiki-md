# Ginzburg-Landau parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md)

In the isotropic single-component model, $\kappa<1/\sqrt2$ gives the type-I regime and $\kappa>1/\sqrt2$ the type-II regime; the interface-energy distinction belongs to the model, rather than a purely geometric test of core overlap.[p.975, interface energy and the type-II regime](nobel-lecture-type-ii-superconductors-and-the-vortex-lattice-by-alexei-alexeyevich-abrikosov.md)
