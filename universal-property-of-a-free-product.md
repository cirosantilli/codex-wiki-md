# Universal property of a free product

↑ **Parent:** [Free product](free-product.md)

Given [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) $f_A:A\to H$ and $f_B:B\to H$, there is exactly one [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $A*B\to H$ extending both. Its value on an alternating normal form is the product of the factor-map values. Thus the [free product](free-product.md) is the coproduct in the [category of groups](category-of-groups.md).
