# Local flattening of a Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

A [smooth bump function](smooth-bump-function.md) supported in a chosen coordinate neighborhood and equal to one near a chosen point allows a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) to be replaced locally by a flat one. The displayed convex combination stays positive definite and glues smoothly to the original metric outside the support. This is a local modification of the metric, not a claim that the original metric is flat.
