# Linearized Boussinesq equations

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq equations](boussinesq-equations.md)

The linearized Boussinesq equations describe small velocity, pressure, and buoyancy perturbations about a motionless, stably stratified reference state. With [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$, [mass diffusivity](mass-diffusivity.md) $\kappa$, and [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$, they are

$$
(\partial_t-\nu\nabla^2)\mathbf u=-\frac1{\rho_0}\nabla p+b\mathbf e_z,
\qquad
(\partial_t-\kappa\nabla^2)b=-N^2w,
\qquad
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u=0.
$$
