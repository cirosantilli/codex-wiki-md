# Fermionic atom antibunching

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

The 2007 paper [Comparison of the Hanbury Brown-Twiss effect for bosons and fermions by T. Jeltes and others](comparison-of-the-hanbury-brown-twiss-effect-for-bosons-and-fermions-by-t-jeltes-and-others.md) compares polarized [helium-3](helium-3.md) [fermions](fermion.md) with [helium-4](helium-4.md) [bosons](boson.md). The [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) occupy a [metastable atomic level](metastable-atomic-level.md). Switching off [magnetic trapping of neutral atoms](magnetic-trapping-of-neutral-atoms.md) releases them onto a [microchannel plate detector](microchannel-plate-detector.md) with a delay-line anode, 63 cm below. Position and arrival-time readouts give three-dimensional pair separations by [time-of-flight atom detection](time-of-flight-atom-detection.md). About 1000 clouds per [isotope](isotope.md) supply [histograms](histogram.md); normalization removes the broad cloud shape.

The [pair detection correlation](pair-detection-correlation.md) dips below one for [helium-3](helium-3.md) and rises above one for [helium-4](helium-4.md). The same apparatus, matched [temperatures](temperature.md) near $0.5\,\mu\mathrm K$, nearly identical trapping potentials, negligible interactions, and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) gradient checks of [spin](spin.md) polarization support an exchange-statistics interpretation. Finite detector resolution suppresses contrast; trap switch-off and resolution uncertainties limit quantitative agreement. A diverging [laser](laser.md) lens deepens the dip, but its aberrations preclude a precise lens-model test. This detects exchange correlations, rather than measuring occupation of one selected [spin orbital](spin-orbital.md).[pp. 3–7, Methods, Figs. 1–3; supplementary pp. 14–16](comparison-of-the-hanbury-brown-twiss-effect-for-bosons-and-fermions-by-t-jeltes-and-others.md)
