# Lorentzian reality structure on twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor Hermitian form](twistor-hermitian-form.md)

Real compactified Minkowski points are the two-dimensional [totally isotropic subspaces](totally-isotropic-subspace.md) for the signature-$(2,2)$ [twistor Hermitian form](twistor-hermitian-form.md). In a graph chart this is the usual Hermitian reality condition on $x^{AA'}$. Their [twistor lines](twistor-line.md) lie in [projective null twistor space](projective-null-twistor-space.md). On the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md) the same real form is the projectivized null cone of a real quadratic form of signature $(2,4)$.
