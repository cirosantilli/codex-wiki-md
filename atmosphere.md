# Atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmosphere)

A gravitationally retained gaseous envelope of a [planet](planet.md). Its [pressure](pressure-split.md), composition and [temperature](temperature-split.md) determine its [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) and circulation. A pure saturated [water](water.md) [atmosphere](atmosphere.md) follows the [saturation pressure](vapor-pressure.md) along a [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) when condensate is locally available.
