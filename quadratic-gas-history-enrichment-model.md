# Quadratic gas-history enrichment model

↑ **Parent:** [Retained-ejecta chemical evolution](retained-ejecta-chemical-evolution.md)

Let $S=s/s_\infty$, let the [interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md) have mass $g=s_\infty S(1-S)$, and retain fraction $f=S$ of returned material. For $0\leq\beta<1$ put $q=(2-\beta)/(1-\beta)$. The [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) for the metal mass is $S^{1/(1-\beta)}/(1-S)$. A finite initial [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) removes the homogeneous solution and gives the displayed expression, equivalently $Z=y\sum_{n\geq1}S^n/(q+n)$ for $0\leq S<1$. The increasing initial gas mass requires pristine [galactic gas inflow](galactic-gas-inflow.md): gas accounting gives $dM_{\rm in}/ds=q(1-S)$. A fixed [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) makes the birth [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) proportional to $ds/dZ$ when enrichment is monotone.
