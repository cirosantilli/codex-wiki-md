# Liquid-disordered phase

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A [liquid-disordered phase](liquid-disordered-phase.md) has relatively disordered lipid chains and lateral molecular mobility. Whether it coexists with a [liquid-ordered phase](liquid-ordered-phase.md) depends on mixture composition and [temperature](temperature-split.md).
