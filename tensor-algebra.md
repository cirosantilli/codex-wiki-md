# Tensor algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product](tensor-product.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_algebra)

The tensor algebra of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$ is the [graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

$$
T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}V^{\otimes n},
$$

with multiplication given by concatenation of tensors.
