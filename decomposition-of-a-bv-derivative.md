# Decomposition of a BV derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Structure theorem for functions of bounded variation](structure-theorem-for-functions-of-bounded-variation.md)

The [Lebesgue decomposition theorem](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md) separates the absolutely continuous part from the singular part. BV fine structure then splits the latter into the [jump part of a BV derivative](jump-part-of-a-bv-derivative.md) and the [Cantor part of a BV derivative](cantor-part-of-a-bounded-variation-derivative.md). The density of the absolutely continuous part is the almost-everywhere [approximate gradient](approximate-gradient.md). These three mutually singular components distinguish smooth variation, surface discontinuities and diffuse singular variation.
