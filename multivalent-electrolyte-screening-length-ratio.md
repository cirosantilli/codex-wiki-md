# Multivalent-electrolyte screening-length ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Debye–Hückel screening length](debye-huckel-screening-length.md)

For fully dissociated [electrolytes](electrolyte.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [solvent](solvent.md) [permittivity](permittivity-split.md), linear screening theory gives $\lambda_D^{-2}\propto\sum_i c_i z_i^2=2I$. A divalent symmetric salt of formula [concentration](concentration.md) $c$ has $I=4c$, not $c$ or $2c$. [ion](ion.md) association and nonideal concentrated-solution effects limit this comparison.
