# Electron temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature-split.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) parameter describing a thermal electron [velocity](velocity.md) distribution. Electron-impact excitation coefficients depend on it through both the speed distribution and excitation thresholds. In a dilute plasma, electron and ion temperatures need not coincide.
