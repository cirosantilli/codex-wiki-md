# Binomial coefficient congruence across a prime row

↑ **Parent:** [Prime-row binomial coefficient divisibility](prime-row-binomial-coefficient-divisibility.md)

In polynomial [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic.md), [prime-row binomial coefficient divisibility](prime-row-binomial-coefficient-divisibility.md) gives $(1+X)^p\equiv1+X^p$. Multiply by $(1+X)^k$ and compare coefficients of $X^r$ for $r<p$; the $X^p$ term cannot contribute.
