# Alluvium

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentary deposit](sedimentary-deposit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluvium)

Loose [sediments](sediment.md) transported and deposited by flowing water, such as channel or floodplain material. Their strength, saturation and thickness affect how a buried [fault](geological-fault.md) ruptures the surface.
