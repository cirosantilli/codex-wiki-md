# Canonical bundle of a regular projective complete intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md)

For regular homogeneous equations of degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ in $\mathbb P^n$, the [normal bundle of a regular zero locus](normal-bundle-of-a-regular-zero-locus.md) is $\bigoplus_i\mathcal O(d_i)|_V$. The [Euler sequence](euler-sequence.md) gives $K_{\mathbb P^n}=\mathcal O(-n-1)$, hence $K_V=\mathcal O(\sum_i d_i-n-1)|_V$. Equations with unreduced multiplicities do not have this conclusion for the underlying reduced submanifold.
