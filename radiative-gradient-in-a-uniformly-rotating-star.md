# Radiative gradient in a uniformly rotating star

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly rotating stellar hydrostatic equilibrium](uniformly-rotating-stellar-hydrostatic-equilibrium.md)

The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) and [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) give $\int_S\nabla\phi\cdot d\mathbf S=4\pi Gm-2\Omega^2V$ on a bounding [equipotential surface](equipotential-surface.md). [Equipotential luminosity conservation with stellar circulation](equipotential-luminosity-conservation-with-stellar-circulation.md) then fixes $f=L/(4\pi Gm-2\Omega^2V)$. Combining $dT/d\phi=-f/\chi$, $dP/d\phi=-\rho$ and [radiative conductivity](radiative-conductivity.md) gives the displayed logarithmic temperature-pressure gradient. Both appearances of mass mean the enclosed mass $m$; only on the outer boundary can both be replaced by the total mass.
