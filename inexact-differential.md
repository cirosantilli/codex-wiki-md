# Inexact differential

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

A [differential one-form](one-form.md) on a specified domain is inexact if it is not the differential of a single-valued [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) there. For $P\,dx+Q\,dy$, unequal cross derivatives $P_y\ne Q_x$ prove inexactness locally. Equality is sufficient on a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) plane domain under the usual smoothness hypotheses, but holes can obstruct a global potential. The form $42y\,dx+x\,dy$ has cross derivatives $42$ and $1$ and counterclockwise unit-square integral $-41$, so it is inexact. A [conservative vector field](conservative-vector-field.md) has an [exact differential](exact-differential.md) as its work form.
