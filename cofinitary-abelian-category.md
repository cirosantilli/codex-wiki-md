# Cofinitary abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

A complete [abelian category](abelian-category.md) is cofinitary when its [opposite category](opposite-category.md) is a [finitary abelian category](finitary-abelian-category.md). Thus a cone of epimorphisms over an inverse directed diagram induces an epimorphism from its vertex to the limit.
