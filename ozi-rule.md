# OZI rule

↑ **Parent:** [Strong interaction](strong-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OZI_rule)

[Hadronic](hadron.md) processes with disconnected quark-flavour lines are often suppressed relative to connected [quark](quark.md) rearrangement. A predominantly strange [phi meson](phi-meson.md) can decay to [kaons](kaon.md) through light-pair creation while its original strange lines survive, whereas a purely pionic final state requires strange-pair annihilation. The rule is an approximate dynamical pattern, not an exact conservation law.
