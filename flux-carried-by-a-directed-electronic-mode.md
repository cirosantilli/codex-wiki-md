# Flux carried by a directed electronic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Landauer formula](landauer-formula.md)

For one directed spin-resolved channel, the density of longitudinal states per unit length and wave number is $1/(2\pi)$. Since $v=\hbar^{-1}dE/dk$, velocity times density per energy is $1/h$. A reservoir occupation imbalance therefore contributes particle flux $\int\delta f(E)dE/h$; in zero-temperature [linear response](linear-response-theory.md) it is $\delta\mu/h$. Multiplying by charge and transmission gives the Landauer current per [mode](mode-statistics.md).
