# Greenhouse gas

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse_gas)

A greenhouse gas absorbs and emits planetary thermal radiation and therefore contributes to the [greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md). Its climatic impact depends on absorption spectrum, concentration, lifetime and chemical effects. [Carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), [methane](methane.md) and water vapour are important examples.
