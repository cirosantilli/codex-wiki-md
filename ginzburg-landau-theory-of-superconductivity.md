# Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

[Vitaly Lazarevich Ginzburg](vitaly-lazarevich-ginzburg.md) and [Lev Landau](lev-davidovich-landau.md) applied an [order parameter](order-parameter.md) description to [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) in 1950. A spatially varying complex amplitude extends the fixed-amplitude description of the [London equations](london-equations.md). Their initial charge assumption preceded the later identification of the universal pair charge.[pp.983–984, complex order parameter and the charge question](nobel-lecture-on-superconductivity-and-superfluidity-what-i-have-and-have-not-managed-to-do-as-well-as-on-the-physical-minimum-at-the-beginning-of-the-xxi-century-by-vitaly-lazarevich-ginzburg.md)

Consider the following isotropic, local SI [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) model near a continuous [superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md):

$$
F[\psi,\mathbf A]=\int d^3x\left[
\alpha|\psi|^2+\frac{\beta}{2}|\psi|^4+
\frac{|(-i\hbar\nabla-q\mathbf A)\psi|^2}{2m_*}+
\frac{|\mathbf B|^2}{2\mu_0}\right],\qquad
\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A.
$$

Here $\beta>0$, $m_*>0$ is a phenomenological mass parameter, and $q=-2e$ fixes the electron-pair convention. The uniform superconducting minimum for $\alpha<0$ has $|\psi_0|^2=-\alpha/\beta$. The gradient term penalizes changes of the amplitude and the gauge-invariant [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md); the magnetic term penalizes [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) penetration. The [gauge-coupling sign in Ginzburg-Landau superconductivity](gauge-coupling-sign-in-ginzburg-landau-superconductivity.md) keeps the [electric current](electric-current.md) and field equation consistent. This model omits material anisotropy, microscopic nonlocality and critical fluctuations.
