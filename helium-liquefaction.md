# Helium liquefaction

↑ **Parent:** [Cryogenics](cryogenics.md)

[Helium liquefaction](helium-liquefaction.md) converts [helium](helium.md) [gas](gas.md) into a collectable [liquid](liquid.md). It creates a bath for investigating material properties at very low [temperatures](temperature-split.md), including [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md). The central question is how the incoming [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) compares with the [specific enthalpies](specific-enthalpy.md) of the outgoing [liquid](liquid.md) and [vapor](vapor.md). The [liquefaction enthalpy budget](liquefaction-enthalpy-budget.md) makes that comparison explicit; a [temperature](temperature-split.md) decrease by itself does not specify a liquid yield.

In a [Joule-Thomson effect](joule-thomson-effect.md) cooling stage, a throttling [pressure](pressure-split.md) drop approximately preserves [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) under the assumptions of the [steady-flow energy balance](steady-flow-energy-balance.md). For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) depends only on [temperature](temperature-split.md): with positive [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md), equal [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) means equal [temperature](temperature-split.md). Thus its [Joule-Thomson coefficient](joule-thomson-coefficient.md) is zero. An [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) also supplies no [liquid](liquid.md) branch. Cooling by throttling depends on [real gas](real-gas.md) properties; expansion is not an automatic source of refrigeration.

For a local drop $dp<0$, $dT=\mu_{\rm JT}dp$ cools only where the [Joule-Thomson coefficient](joule-thomson-coefficient.md) is positive. Precooling must bring the [real gas](real-gas.md) into a useful cooling region, with enough cooling to overcome unwanted [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). A finite expansion can cross a [Joule-Thomson inversion curve](joule-thomson-inversion-curve.md); its final state requires the full [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) balance, including [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) if [liquid](liquid.md) forms. [Kamerlingh Onnes's 1908 helium liquefaction experiment](kamerlingh-onnes-s-1908-helium-liquefaction-experiment.md) combined external precooling with regeneration to reach that regime. The [Joule-Thomson effect](joule-thomson-effect.md) article develops the throttling identities and their assumptions.
