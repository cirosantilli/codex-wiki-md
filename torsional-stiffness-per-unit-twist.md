# Torsional stiffness per unit twist

↑ **Parent:** [Torsional stiffness](torsional-stiffness.md)

For a uniform rod of length $L$ and twist per unit length $\tau$, the end-rotation [angle](angle.md) is $\varphi=L\tau$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $K_\tau=LK_\varphi$, where $K_\varphi$ is the [torsional stiffness](torsional-stiffness.md). If a source defines a normalized [torque](torque.md) $M=N/(2\pi)$, its derivative is $dM/d\tau=K_\tau/(2\pi)$.
