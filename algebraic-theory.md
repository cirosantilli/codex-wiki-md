# Algebraic theory

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

A finitary algebraic theory specifies sorts, finite-arity operations and equations between terms. Its internal models can be interpreted in any category with finite products. Finitely presented set-based models generate arbitrary models under filtered colimits and determine its [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md).
