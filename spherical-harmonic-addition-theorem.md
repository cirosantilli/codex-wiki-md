# Spherical harmonic addition theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

For orthonormal complex [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md), $P_\ell(\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\hat{\mathbf m})=4\pi\sum_{m=-\ell}^{\ell}Y_{\ell m}(\hat{\mathbf n})Y_{\ell m}^*(\hat{\mathbf m})/(2\ell+1)$. This converts an angle between two directions into a separable sum. Together with [Orthogonality of Legendre polynomials](orthogonality-of-legendre-polynomials.md), it projects rotationally invariant scattering kernels onto angular multipoles.
