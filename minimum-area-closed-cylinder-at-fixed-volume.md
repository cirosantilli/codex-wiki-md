# Minimum-area closed cylinder at fixed volume

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)

For radius $r>0$ and height $h>0$, volume is $\pi r^2h$ and area including both ends is $2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$. The [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) equations give $h=2r$. Substituting the constraint gives the one-variable function $2\pi r^2+2V/r$, which tends to infinity at both ends and has its only stationary point at $r^3=V/(2\pi)$. This proves the stationary cylinder is the unique global minimizer.
