# Primitive causality

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic quantum field theory](algebraic-quantum-field-theory.md)

[Primitive causality](primitive-causality.md) is a dynamical completeness condition: the state restricted to a suitable initial spacetime region determines its expectations throughout that region's domain of dependence. Algebraically one requires the later domain-of-dependence observable algebra to be contained in the initial algebra, under the identifications supplied by the dynamics. A local [time-slice axiom](time-slice-axiom.md) gives this property for a neighbourhood of a Cauchy slice. Knowledge of the full restricted state, including correlations, is required; knowing only mean initial fields is insufficient.
