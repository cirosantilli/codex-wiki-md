# Observation

↑ **Parent:** [Science](science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation)

A disciplined record or judgment made through sensory or instrumental interaction with an object or event. An [observation](observation.md) has conditions of production and interpretation; it is distinct from a deliberate experimental intervention.
