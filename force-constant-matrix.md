# Force constant matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

The [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) of [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at a mechanical equilibrium, expressed in specified displacement coordinates. It determines restoring forces to first order. Combining it with the [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) gives the [dynamical matrix](dynamical-matrix.md) whose [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are squared mode [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md).
