# Projected Chern connection

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

If $S$ is a holomorphic subbundle of a [Hermitian vector bundle](hermitian-vector-bundle.md) $E$, its induced [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) is $D_S=\pi_SD_E$ on sections of $S$. [Orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) preserves [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md), and holomorphicity of $S$ preserves the required $(0,1)$ operator. Uniqueness of the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) therefore identifies the projected connection.
