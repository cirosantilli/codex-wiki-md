# Countable basis support lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Free module](free-module.md)

A [submodule](submodule.md) $N$ of a [free module](free-module.md) with [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) $E$, generated by a [countable set](countable-set.md), is contained in the [submodule](submodule.md) generated by some [countable](countable-set.md) subset $E'\subseteq E$. Take $E'$ to be the union of the finite [coordinate supports in a free module](coordinate-support-in-a-free-module.md) of a countable generating family for $N$. This union is [countable](countable-set.md), and every generator belongs to $\langle E'\rangle$, which proves the inclusion. Combining this with [basis partition gives a module direct sum](basis-partition-gives-a-module-direct-sum.md) places $N$ inside a countably generated [direct summand](direct-summand.md).
