# Pauli correlator

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md)

A Pauli correlator is the expectation value of a tensor product of [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md), such as $\langle X\otimes Z\rangle$. It measures a specified correlation between qubit observables.
