# Polygonal presentation of a punctured surface

↑ **Parent:** [Polygonal schema](polygonal-schema.md)

Start with a [polygonal schema](polygonal-schema.md) whose outer boundary has $m$ sides, and remove a triangular disc strictly inside its polygon. Cut the resulting [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) along an arc from an outer vertex to an inner vertex. The cut space is a [closed disc](closed-disc.md) with $m+5$ marked sides: the original $m$ outer sides, two banks of the cut, and three inner sides. Parametrize it by a [convex polygon](convex-polygon.md) with that many sides. A boundary [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) carrying the marked vertices in cyclic order extends over the disc by radial extension after identifying both discs with a standard [closed disc](closed-disc.md).

Transport the original side identifications to this polygon and identify the two cut banks, leaving the three inner sides unpaired. The [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) is the original [surface](topological-surface-split.md) with an open triangular disc removed. In boundary-word notation, if $w$ is the original word, the new word is $w\,c\,d\,e\,f\,c^{-1}$, with $d,e,f$ unpaired. The [torus](torus.md) has $w=aba^{-1}b^{-1}$; a square model of the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) has $w=abab$, pairing antipodal points of the square's boundary.
