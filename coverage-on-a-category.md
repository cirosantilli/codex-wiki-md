# Coverage on a category

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md)

A coverage specifies covering families on a [small category](small-category.md), stable under refinement along arrows: for a covering family into $C$ and $f:B\to C$, there is a covering family into $B$ whose composites with $f$ factor through the original family. Passing to generated sieves and imposing maximality and local transitivity gives its associated [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md), with the same [sheaves on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md). One can equivalently specify the saturated covering sieves directly. Rigidity refers to these covering sieves, so it is unchanged by replacing a family presentation by its generated topology.
