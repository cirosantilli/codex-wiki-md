# Prime ideal avoiding a multiplicative subset

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal](prime-ideal.md)

In a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ disjoint from a nonempty [multiplicative subset](multiplicatively-closed-set.md) $S$ extends to an ideal $P$ maximal among those disjoint from $S$, by the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md). It is proper. If $ab\in P$ but neither factor belongs to $P$, both $P+(a)$ and $P+(b)$ meet $S$. Multiplying such representatives puts an element of $S$ in $P$, a contradiction. Thus $P$ is a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). For a general [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) the same argument follows after applying the [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to disjoint ideals; the union of a chain is disjoint from $S$.
