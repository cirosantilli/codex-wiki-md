# Chiral protection of a fermion mass

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry protection of a small mass](symmetry-protection-of-a-small-mass.md)

A fermion mass that violates an otherwise restored chiral symmetry is multiplicatively rather than additively renormalized in perturbation theory: corrections vanish with the symmetry-breaking mass or coupling. A chiral gauge representation can forbid a bare mass altogether until a [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) and [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) make a gauge-invariant term possible. A scalar-partner bilinear may respect all those ordinary symmetries and therefore needs separate [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) protection.
