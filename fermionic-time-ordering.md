# Fermionic time ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Time ordering](time-ordering.md)

For two odd [fermionic operators](fermionic-operator.md), [time ordering](time-ordering.md) contributes a minus sign whenever the chronological rearrangement exchanges them. For several fields the sign is the [sign of a permutation](sign-of-a-permutation.md) restricted to the odd fields. This convention makes the free [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md) a [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) of the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) with its contact term fixed by the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md).
