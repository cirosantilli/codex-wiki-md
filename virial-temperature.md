# Virial temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

The [virial temperature](virial-temperature.md) characterizes the thermal energy of gas supported in a gravitational potential. It is of order $\mu m_pGM/(k_BR)$, with a structural coefficient determined by the density profile. For a uniform self-gravitating gas sphere the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $k_BT=GM\mu m_p/(5R)$; a conventional halo circular-speed definition often uses $k_BT\simeq\mu m_pv_c^2/2$.
