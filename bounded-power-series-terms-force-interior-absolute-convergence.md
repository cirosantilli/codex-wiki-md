# Bounded power-series terms force interior absolute convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md)

If the terms of a [power series](power-series.md) at a nonzero point $z$ are bounded by $M$, then $|a_nw^n|\le M(|w|/|z|)^n$. A convergent [geometric series](geometric-series.md) and the [comparison test for series](comparison-test-for-series.md) give [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) at every point strictly inside that circle. Consequently terms at every point outside the [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) must be unbounded. This conclusion is stronger than merely saying that those terms fail to tend to zero.
