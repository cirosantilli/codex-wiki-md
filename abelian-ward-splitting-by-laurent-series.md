# Abelian Ward splitting by Laurent series

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose-Ward correspondence](penrose-ward-correspondence.md)

On a [twistor line](twistor-line.md) use $\pi_{0'}=\zeta,\pi_{1'}=1$ and $\omega^A=x^{A0'}\zeta+x^{A1'}$. For a degree-zero [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) patching logarithm, its [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) splits as $h_0=-\sum_{n\ge0}f_n\zeta^n$, $h_1=\sum_{n<0}f_n\zeta^n$. The [twistor incidence relation](twistor-incidence-relation.md) implies $\partial_{A0'}f_n=\partial_{A1'}f_{n-1}$. Hence $(-\partial_{A0'}+\zeta\partial_{A1'})h_\alpha=\partial_{A1'}f_{-1}$ on both patches. The [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) is $A_{A0'}=-\partial_{A1'}f_{-1}$, $A_{A1'}=0$. Exponentiating the two additive factors produces the linewise [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) splitting of $e^f$.
