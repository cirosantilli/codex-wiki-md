# Electroweak precision test with the W, top and Higgs masses

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)

In an on-shell convention, the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) relates the [W boson](w-boson.md) [mass](mass.md) to measured electroweak inputs through

$$
m_W^2\left(1-\frac{m_W^2}{m_Z^2}\right)=\frac{\pi\alpha}{\sqrt2G_F(1-\Delta r)}.
$$

The [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) in $\Delta r$ depend on the [top quark](top-quark.md) and [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) masses. The leading heavy-top contribution to the electroweak rho parameter is $\Delta\rho\simeq3G_Fm_t^2/(8\sqrt2\pi^2)$, reflecting the large mass splitting in the top-bottom weak doublet. The Higgs dependence in the precision corrections is mainly logarithmic over the relevant range. Direct measurements of $m_t$ and $m_H$ therefore allow an indirect prediction of $m_W$; comparing it with a direct measurement tests loops as well as tree-level [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md). Any tension must include input uncertainties, experimental systematics and higher-order corrections.[CDF electroweak physics: relation between W, top and Higgs mass measurements](https://www-cdf.lbl.gov/cdf_ewk/cdf_ewk.html)
