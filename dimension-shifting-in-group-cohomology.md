# Dimension shifting in group cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

Embed a [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ over the [group ring](group-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[G]$ in a module $I$ with vanishing positive [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md), and write $M'=I/M$. The connecting map in the long exact [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md) sequence of $0\to M\to I\to M'\to0$ is an isomorphism $H^r(G,M')\to H^{r+1}(G,M)$ for $r\geq1$, since the groups involving $I$ on both sides vanish. For a finite [group](group-split.md) and a module $I$ with vanishing [Tate cohomology of a finite group](tate-cohomology-of-a-finite-group.md) in every degree, the same argument shifts every Tate degree. Repeating this construction transports computations to a convenient resolution.
