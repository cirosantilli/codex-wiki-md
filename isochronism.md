# Isochronism

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillation](oscillation.md)

Equality of repeated time intervals; for an oscillator, independence of its [oscillation period](period-of-an-oscillation.md) from amplitude in the stated regime. Ideal [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) is isochronous. A [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md) has this property only approximately at small amplitude.
