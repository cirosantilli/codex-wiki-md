# Adjoints of inverse image on power sets

↑ **Parent:** [Galois connection](galois-connection.md)

For a [function](function-split.md) $p:A\to B$, order each [power set](power-set.md) by inclusion. The [inverse image](preimage.md) map $p^*(V)=p^{-1}(V)$ has a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) $\exists_p(U)=p(U)$ and a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) $\forall_p(U)=\{b\in B:p^{-1}(\{b\})\subseteq U\}$. The two [adjunctions](adjoint-functors.md) follow from $p(U)\subseteq V\iff U\subseteq p^{-1}(V)$ and $p^{-1}(V)\subseteq U\iff V\subseteq\forall_p(U)$. Empty [fibers](fiber-of-a-function.md) satisfy the second condition vacuously, so points outside the image of $p$ belong to $\forall_p(U)$ for every $U$.
