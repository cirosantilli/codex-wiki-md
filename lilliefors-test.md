# Lilliefors test

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov-Smirnov test](kolmogorov-smirnov-test.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilliefors_test)

The [Lilliefors test](lilliefors-test.md) calibrates the [Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic](kolmogorov-smirnov-statistic.md) against a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) whose [mean](expected-value-split.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) are estimated from the tested observations. In the original procedure, $s^2$ is the [sample variance](sample-variance.md) with denominator $n-1$, and $\Phi$ is the [standard normal distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md). Its reference law includes this fitting step. The original paper's opening procedure and Table 1 explain the changed calibration.[pp. 399-400: opening procedure and Table 1 of the original article; inspected mirrored text](on-the-kolmogorov-smirnov-test-for-normality-with-mean-and-variance-unknown-by-hubert-w-lilliefors.md)
