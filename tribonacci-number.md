# Tribonacci number

↑ **Parent:** [Integer sequence](integer-sequence.md)

Terms of a sequence satisfying the third-order [linear recurrence](linear-recurrence-relation.md) $T_{n+3}=T_n+T_{n+1}+T_{n+2}$. Initial values vary by convention; some counting problems specify $T_1=T_2=T_3=1$. To study [divisibility](divisibility-split.md) by $m$, retain three consecutive [remainders](remainder.md) [modulo](modulo-operation.md) $m$, a deterministic state with at most $m^3$ possibilities.
