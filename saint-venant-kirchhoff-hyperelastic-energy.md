<h1 id="saint-venant-kirchhoff-hyperelastic-energy">Saint Venant–Kirchhoff hyperelastic energy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

This objective [isotropic](isotropy.md) constitutive model uses the [Green-Lagrange strain tensor](green-lagrange-strain-tensor.md) $E=(A^{\mathsf T}A-I)/2$. It reproduces [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) at small strain. Under simple shear $A=[(1,q,0)^{\mathsf T},e_2,e_3]$, its longitudinal [nominal stress](nominal-stress-tensor.md) is $(\lambda+2\mu)q^2/2$. Nonuniform finite shear therefore generates longitudinal acceleration, even though the corresponding infinitesimal transverse mode is uncoupled.
