# Genus of a smooth projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md)

For a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) with $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=k$, its genus is $\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$, equivalently $\dim_kH^0(C,\Omega^1_C)$ by [Serre duality](serre-duality.md). It equals the [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) $1-\chi(\mathcal O_C)$. Without the condition on constants, the general [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) instead uses $\chi(\mathcal O_C)=h^0(\mathcal O_C)-h^1(\mathcal O_C)$.
