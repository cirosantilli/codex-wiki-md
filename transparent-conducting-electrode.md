# Transparent conducting electrode

↑ **Parent:** [Thin film](thin-film.md)

A conducting coating or patterned network designed to transmit a specified optical band while carrying current. Its [transmittance](transmittance.md), [sheet resistance](sheet-resistance.md), thickness, [optical haze](optical-haze.md), substrate reference, pattern geometry and mechanical durability must be specified together. A material name alone does not establish that every processed film meets a device specification.
