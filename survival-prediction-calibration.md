# Survival prediction calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)

Calibration compares predicted survival [probabilities](probability.md) with observed survival experience at relevant horizons or over time. Censoring must be handled, for example by suitable survival estimates or inverse-[probability](probability.md) weighting. Calibration should be assessed on validated predictions, because the fitting sample can hide optimism from variable selection and tuning. It is distinct from the risk-ranking information measured by a [concordance index](concordance-index.md).
