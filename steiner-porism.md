# Steiner porism

↑ **Parent:** [Steiner chain](steiner-chain.md)

If a simple [Steiner chain](steiner-chain.md) closes after $n\geq3$ [circles](circle.md), rotating the starting [circle](circle.md) between concentric boundaries gives another closed $n$-[circle](circle.md) chain. Applying a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) proves the corresponding statement for arbitrary boundary [circles](circle.md). Construction must stay in the same intervening family and proceed without reversing the chosen direction. Merely choosing arbitrary [circles](circle.md) tangent to the preceding [circle](circle.md) permits backtracking and does not assert closure.
