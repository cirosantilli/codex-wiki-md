# Omitted value

↑ **Parent:** [Picard theorem](picard-theorem.md)

An [omitted value](omitted-value.md) of a [function](function-split.md) $f:D\to Y$ is a point $w\in Y$ outside its [image](image-of-a-function.md): the equation $f(z)=w$ has no solution in $D$. Both $D$ and $Y$ matter. The [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) on the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) has one [omitted value](omitted-value.md) in $\mathbb C$, namely zero, and two [omitted values](omitted-value.md) when regarded as a map into the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), namely zero and infinity. Restricting $D$ can create additional [omitted values](omitted-value.md).
