# Fractional entropy of a relativistically decoupled species

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal decoupling in cosmology](thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md)

If a species and the remaining bath share a temperature at decoupling, their initial entropy ratio is the ratio of their entropy-weighted degrees of freedom. Subsequent conservation of each sector's comoving [entropy](entropy.md) keeps that fraction constant, even when the bath heats relative to the relic. For a two-state neutrino species leaving an ideal Standard Model bath, the fraction is $(7/4)/(106.75+7/4)\simeq1.61\%$.
