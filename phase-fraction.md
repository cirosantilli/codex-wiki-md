# Phase fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md)

A phase fraction is the proportion of a mixture occupying a specified [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md). State the basis: by [mass](mass.md), $x_\beta=m_\beta/\sum_\alpha m_\alpha$; by [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), divide the occupied [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) by the total [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). These fractions need not agree when the [mass densities](density.md) differ. The [latent heat](latent-heat.md) [heating curve](heating-curve.md) uses the [liquid](liquid.md) fraction by [mass](mass.md), whereas [liquid fraction](liquid-fraction.md) in a [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) is defined by [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
