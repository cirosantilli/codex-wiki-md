# NP (complexity)

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial time](polynomial-time.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP_(complexity))

$\mathbf{NP}$ is the class of [decision problems](decision-problem.md) whose positive instances have polynomial-length [certificates](certificate-complexity.md) verifiable in [polynomial time](polynomial-time.md). Equivalently, it is polynomial time on a [nondeterministic computation](nondeterministic-turing-machine.md).
