# Morphism of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf (mathematics)](sheaf-mathematics.md)

A morphism of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) assigns a map $\mathcal F(U)\to\mathcal G(U)$ on every [open set](open-set.md), commuting with all restriction maps. For [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md), [sheaves of rings](sheaf-of-rings.md) or [sheaves of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md), the maps must also preserve the relevant algebraic structure. It induces maps on every [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md), and equality of sheaf morphisms can be checked there.
