# Critical-density bound on the sum of relic neutrino masses

↑ **Parent:** [Critical-density mass of a thermal relic neutrino](critical-density-mass-of-a-thermal-relic-neutrino.md)

For three standard thermal relic mass eigenstates, each neutrino-antineutrino pair has [number density](number-density.md) $3n_\gamma/11$ after pair annihilation. Their rest-mass density is this common per-species abundance times the mass sum. Requiring it not to exceed the near-critical total density gives the displayed conservative bound. Dividing instead by the total abundance over three species bounds the mean mass. With $T_{\gamma0}=2.725$ K and $H_0=72$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$ the instantaneous-decoupling estimate is about $49$ eV/$c^2$ for the sum, conditional on standard abundances and nonnegative other energy components.
