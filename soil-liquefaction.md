# Soil liquefaction

↑ **Parent:** [Geology](geology.md)

Loss of strength in suitable water-saturated granular material when rising [pore fluid pressure](pore-fluid-pressure.md) reduces the effective contact load, often during cyclic earthquake shaking. It is distinct from the condensation of a [gas](gas.md).
