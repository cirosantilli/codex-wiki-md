# Functional complementation

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)

[Functional complementation](functional-complementation.md) is restoration of a specified function or [phenotype](phenotype.md) by introducing a [gene](gene.md) into a deficient recipient. In the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md), the restored property is growth without supplied [methionine](methionine.md), under the assay conditions. Rescue need not restore every property of the original [genotype](genotype.md).
