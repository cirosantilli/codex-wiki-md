# Band-pass filter

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter](linear-filter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band-pass_filter)

A band-pass filter preferentially transmits signals within a range of [frequencies](frequency.md) and attenuates sufficiently lower and higher [frequencies](frequency.md). Taking the output voltage across the [resistor](resistor.md) in a [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) gives one example: the [resonant series RLC response](resonant-series-rlc-response.md) selects a band around $1/\sqrt{LC}$. [Feynman lectures, II.22.7: resonant frequency filters](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_22.html#Ch22-S7).
