# Abel-Anderson-Wheatley zero-sound experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Zero sound](zero-sound.md)

In 1966, [William Russell Abel](william-russell-abel.md), [Ansel Cochran Anderson](ansel-cochran-anderson.md) and [John Charles Wheatley](john-charles-wheatley.md) sent [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md) through bulk liquid [helium-3](helium-3.md) at $0.32\ \mathrm{atm}$ and frequencies $15.4$ and $45.5\ \mathrm{MHz}$. A [quartz](quartz.md) delay line and receiving [acoustic transducer](acoustic-transducer.md) bounded a $0.138\ \mathrm{mm}$ liquid gap; shielding reduced electrical pickup. Powdered [cerium magnesium nitrate](cerium-magnesium-nitrate.md) provided magnetic thermometry during a slow warm-up. Pulse amplitudes measured [acoustic attenuation](acoustic-attenuation.md); [phase difference](phase-difference.md) comparison measured velocity. Calibration pulses checked gain; early received cycles avoided multiple reflections. On cooling through a maximum, [acoustic attenuation](acoustic-attenuation.md) changed from $\alpha\propto\omega^2/T^{*2}$ to $\alpha\propto T^{*2}$ with a velocity shift supporting [zero sound](zero-sound.md); $T^*$ is their magnetic [temperature](temperature-split.md) scale. Weak [acoustic attenuation](acoustic-attenuation.md) was harder to resolve.[pp.74–75, Fig.1 and readout/control paragraphs](propagation-of-zero-sound-in-liquid-he3-at-low-temperatures-by-william-russell-abel-and-others.md)

This 1966 test followed [Lev Landau](lev-davidovich-landau.md)'s 1962 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md).[1962 award to Landau for condensed-matter theory](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1962-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

The experiment concerned normal liquid [helium-3](helium-3.md), distinct from the later discovery of [superfluid](superfluid.md) [helium-3](helium-3.md).[pp.3–4, normal-liquid sound and later superfluid work](john-c-wheatley-by-robert-e-ecke-and-others.md) Landau's original papers are theoretical calculations, not reports of an apparatus.
