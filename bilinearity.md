# Bilinearity

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear map](bilinear-map.md)

A map $B:V\times W\to Z$ between [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) is bilinear when it is [linear](linearity.md) in either argument with the other held fixed. A scalar-valued [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is the case $V=W$ and $Z$ is the scalar field. Checking [bilinearity](bilinearity.md) is one of the algebraic steps in verifying a real [inner product](inner-product.md); being a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) and having positive squared [norm](norm.md) impose further conditions.
