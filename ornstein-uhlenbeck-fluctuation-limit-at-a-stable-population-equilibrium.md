# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck fluctuation limit at a stable population equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Linear noise approximation](linear-noise-approximation.md)

For a unit-jump population chain with one-step probabilities $\alpha k/n$ and $\beta k^2/n^2$, its equilibrium density is $u_0=\alpha/\beta$. Speed time by $n$ and center and scale the population as $(k-nu_0)/\sqrt n$. The accelerated conditional drift is $-\alpha x-\beta x^2/\sqrt n$, and the second moment is $2\alpha^2/\beta+3\alpha x/\sqrt n+\beta x^2/n$. Their compact-set limits give the displayed [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md). If $2\alpha^2<\beta$, the invalid-probability threshold stays a fixed positive density distance above equilibrium. Before that threshold, the generator of $x^2$ is at most one because its drift contribution is nonpositive. The stopped [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md) bound proves [compact containment](compact-containment.md) and makes the probability of hitting that threshold on a fixed accelerated horizon tend to zero. This supplies the global hypotheses of the [diffusion approximation theorem](diffusion-approximation-theorem.md).
