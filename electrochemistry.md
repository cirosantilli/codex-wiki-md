# Electrochemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

Chemical systems whose reactions, transport or equilibrium couple to [electric charges](electric-charge.md) and [electric potential](electric-potential.md). An [electrolyte](electrolyte.md) carries mobile [ions](ion.md); a [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) can approximate their equilibrium distributions.
