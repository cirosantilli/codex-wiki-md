# Profile dynamic programming

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md)

A [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md) method that advances a boundary across a structured object and records only the information needed on that boundary. For tilings by pieces of bounded height, a [bit mask](bit-mask.md) recording occupied cells in a few frontier rows can determine every legal continuation. Its [state space](state-space.md) is typically exponential in the width but independent of the total height.
