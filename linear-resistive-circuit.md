# Linear resistive circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)

A steady [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) described by [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md), [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md), [Kirchhoff's voltage law](kirchhoff-cycle-law.md), and ideal [independent electrical sources](independent-electrical-source.md) or linear [dependent electrical sources](dependent-electrical-source.md). Fixed sources make the terminal response an [affine function](affine-function.md) when the circuit equations admit a unique voltage for each prescribed terminal [electric current](electric-current.md).
