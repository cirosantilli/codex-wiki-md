# Partial agonist

↑ **Parent:** [Agonist](agonist.md)

An [agonist](agonist.md) whose efficacy produces less than the maximum response of a higher-efficacy [agonist](agonist.md) under specified conditions. High [receptor reserve](receptor-reserve.md) can conceal partial efficacy.
