# Finite population mean

↑ **Parent:** [Probability sampling](probability-sampling.md)

The [finite population mean](finite-population-mean.md) for $N\geq1$ is the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) of all fixed values in the target population. Under [simple random sampling](simple-random-sampling.md), the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of it.
