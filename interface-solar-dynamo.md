# Interface solar dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md)

An interface solar dynamo separates the region of strong differential rotation from the region of poloidal-field regeneration, often placing shear near the [tachocline](tachocline.md) and an [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md) in the convection zone. Magnetic transport across the interface couples the two regions. The separation can provide toroidal-flux storage in a relatively stable layer, but growth, period and migration depend on the transport, diffusivity contrast, and signs of the regeneration and shear coefficients.
