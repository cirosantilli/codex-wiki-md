# Polynomial evaluation map on a finite point set

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of a bounded-total-degree polynomial space](dimension-of-a-bounded-total-degree-polynomial-space.md)

Evaluation of bounded-degree [multivariate polynomials](multivariate-polynomial.md) on a finite set defines a [linear map](linear-map.md) $E_P:f\mapsto(f(p))_{p\in P}$. Its rank is at most $|P|$ and its kernel is the space of polynomials vanishing on $P$. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $\dim\ker E_P\ge\binom{d+n}{n}-|P|$ for degree at most $d$ in $n$ variables. Dependent point constraints only enlarge the kernel.
