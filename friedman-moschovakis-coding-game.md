<h1 id="friedman-moschovakis-coding-game">Friedman–Moschovakis coding game</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Friedman–Moschovakis coding lemma](friedman-moschovakis-coding-lemma.md)

For $A\subseteq\lambda$, the Friedman–Moschovakis coding game asks the players to present coherent local codes for initial segments $A\cap\xi$ while challenging each other at larger ordinals. The diagonal and boundedness argument rules out a winning strategy for Player I; a winning strategy for Player II determines at most one set $A$. Coding strategies by reals therefore gives a surjection from the [Baire space of sequences](baire-space-of-sequences.md) onto $\mathcal P(\lambda)$.
