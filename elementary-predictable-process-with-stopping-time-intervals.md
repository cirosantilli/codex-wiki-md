# Elementary predictable process with stopping-time intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md)

An elementary [predictable process](predictable-process.md) may use finitely many ordered [stopping times](stopping-time.md) $\tau_j$, with bounded $\mathcal F_{\tau_j}$-measurable coefficients $h_j$ on $(\tau_j,\tau_{j+1}]$. Its elementary [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) is $\sum_jh_j(X_{t\wedge\tau_{j+1}}-X_{t\wedge\tau_j})$. The stopped indicators are left-continuous and adapted, so the process is [predictable](predictable-process.md); common refinement shows the sum is independent of its representation. Deterministic endpoints recover the usual [simple predictable process](simple-predictable-process.md). These integrands give the precise good-integrator test in the [Bichteler-Dellacherie theorem](bichteler-dellacherie-theorem.md).
