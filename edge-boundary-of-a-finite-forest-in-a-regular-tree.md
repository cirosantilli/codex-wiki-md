# Edge boundary of a finite forest in a regular tree

↑ **Parent:** [Regular tree](regular-tree.md)

For a nonempty finite [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) set $A$ in a degree-$k$ [regular tree](regular-tree.md), its induced [forest](forest.md) has $|A|-c(A)$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), where $c(A)$ counts its components. Summing degrees and subtracting twice the internal [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count gives the displayed number of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) with exactly one endpoint in $A$. In particular it is at least $(k-2)|A|+2$.
