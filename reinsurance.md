# Reinsurance

↑ **Parent:** [Actuarial statistics](actuarial-statistics-split.md)

Reinsurance transfers part of an insurer’s claim liability to another insurer. If aggregate claims are $S$, a retained payout $g(S)$ with $0\leq g(S)\leq S$ leaves the reinsurer with $S-g(S)$. [Quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md) retains a fixed fraction, whereas [aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md) retains losses only up to a fixed aggregate threshold.
