# Holomorphic subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

A holomorphic subbundle of a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is a constant-rank [vector subbundle](vector-subbundle.md) which is locally spanned by a holomorphic linearly independent family of sections and whose inclusion is a [holomorphic bundle map](holomorphic-bundle-map.md). It has an induced [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) structure. A varying-rank coherent subsheaf is not generally a subbundle.
