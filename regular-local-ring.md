# Regular local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Local ring](local-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_local_ring)

A Noetherian local ring $(R,\mathfrak m)$ is regular when the minimal number of generators of its [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) equals its [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md). Every regular local ring is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) and a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md).
