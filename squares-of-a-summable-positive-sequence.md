# Squares of a summable positive sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md)

If $a_n\geq0$ and $\sum a_n<\infty$, then $a_n\to0$, so eventually $a_n^2\leq a_n$ and $\sum a_n^2$ converges.
