# Relative molecular mass

↑ **Parent:** [Molar mass](molar-mass.md)

The [relative molecular mass](relative-molecular-mass.md) is the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) ratio of a specified mean [molecule](molecule-split.md) [mass](mass.md) to the [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md), written $m_{\rm u}$ when treated as a [physical quantity](physical-quantity.md):

$$
M_{\rm r}=\frac{\bar m_{\rm molecule}}{m_{\rm u}},\qquad M=M_{\rm r}M_{\rm u}.
$$

Thus a [relative molecular mass](relative-molecular-mass.md) of approximately $18.015$ and a [molar mass](molar-mass.md) of approximately $18.015\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ describe related quantities with different units. The conversion uses the [molar mass constant](molar-mass-constant.md), rather than identifying a [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) number with a [mass](mass.md) per [mole](mole-unit.md).
