# Levi-Civita connection under conformal rescaling

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal rescaling of a Riemannian metric](conformal-rescaling-of-a-riemannian-metric.md)

For $\widetilde g=e^{2\sigma}g$, the displayed correction is a symmetric tensor, so adding it to $\nabla$ gives a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md). The two extra metric-compatibility pairings sum to $2d\sigma(X)g(Y,Z)$; this is exactly the derivative of the conformal factor. Uniqueness of the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) proves the formula. It also yields the [critical-point criterion for equal conformal connections](critical-point-criterion-for-equal-conformal-connections.md).
