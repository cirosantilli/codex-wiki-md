# Two-ball packing obstruction in the projective plane

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic ball chart in complex projective space](symplectic-ball-chart-in-complex-projective-space.md)

Two disjoint [symplectic balls](symplectic-ball.md) of radii $r_1,r_2$ embedding into the unit four-ball must satisfy the displayed inequality. Extend their transported standard [complex structures](complex-structure.md) on smaller concentric balls to a [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) on the projective plane. A degree-one [J-holomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) through both centres has area $\pi$. The [Monotonicity theorem for a J-holomorphic curve](monotonicity-theorem-for-a-j-holomorphic-curve.md) bounds its area in each smaller ball below by $\pi\rho_i^2$. Disjointness allows these bounds to be added; letting $\rho_i$ increase to $r_i$ proves the result.
