# Tension-dependent wrapping phase diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-particle wrapping by a fluid membrane](spherical-particle-wrapping-by-a-fluid-membrane.md)

For positive [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md), the bound-cap [energy](energy-split.md) is convex in indentation. Its constrained [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) is the unconstrained value $(\bar w-2)/\bar\sigma$ clipped to $[0,2]$. No wrapping occurs at $\bar w\leq2$, partial wrapping at $2<\bar w<2+2\bar\sigma$, and full wrapping at $\bar w\geq2+2\bar\sigma$. At zero tension the expression is linear, with all indentations degenerate at $\bar w=2$. These phase boundaries belong to the specified cap model and can move when free-membrane and neck energies are included.
