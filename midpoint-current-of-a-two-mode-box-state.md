# Midpoint current of a two-mode box state

↑ **Parent:** [Equal superposition of the first two box states](equal-superposition-of-the-first-two-box-states.md)

For the [equal superposition of the first two box states](equal-superposition-of-the-first-two-box-states.md) with zero initial relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md), put $\Omega=(E_2-E_1)/\hbar$. The [probability](probability.md) in the left half is $P_{\mathrm L}=1/2+4\cos(\Omega t)/(3\pi)$. The [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and zero wall [probability current](probability-current.md) imply

$$
j(L/2,t)=-P_{\mathrm L}'(t)=\frac{2\pi\hbar}{mL^2}\sin\Omega t.
$$

Direct substitution of $\psi(L/2,t)=L^{-1/2}e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}$ and $\partial_x\psi(L/2,t)=-2\pi L^{-3/2}e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}$ into the [probability current](probability-current.md) verifies the sign. Positive current means probability leaves the left half. The [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md) controls transport even though each component has stationary [probability density](probability-density.md).
