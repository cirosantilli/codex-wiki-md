# Hydrodynamic interaction of a Hookean dumbbell

↑ **Parent:** [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md)

For identical no-slip [spheres](sphere.md) of radius $a$ linked by a [zero-rest-length spring](zero-rest-length-spring.md), with $R\gg a$ and $\zeta=6\pi\mu a$, the leading [Oseen tensor](oseen-tensor.md) interaction reduces their relative spring relaxation rate by $3a/(2R)$. The midpoint follows an imposed incompressible [linear velocity field](linear-velocity-field.md). Add the two bead [force balance](force-balance.md) equations to prove midpoint advection; subtract them and use $\mathsf G(\mathbf R)\mathbf R=\mathbf R/(4\pi\mu R)$ to obtain the displayed equation. This deterministic far-field model omits bead inertia, thermal forcing, connector drag and near-contact lubrication.
