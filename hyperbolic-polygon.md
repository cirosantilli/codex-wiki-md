# Hyperbolic polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md)

A [geodesic](geodesic.md) polygon in the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) is bounded by finitely many [geodesic](geodesic.md) segments. For curvature $-1$, its [area](surface-area.md) is its angle defect, as given by [hyperbolic polygon area](hyperbolic-polygon-area.md). Pairing equally long edges produces [hyperbolic surfaces](hyperbolic-surface.md), with smoothness at glued vertices determined by their total angles.
