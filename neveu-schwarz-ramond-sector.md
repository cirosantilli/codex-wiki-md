<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-ramond-sector">Neveu–Schwarz–Ramond sector</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md)

A [closed string](closed-string.md) sector with left-moving [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) excitations and right-moving [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) excitations. Its spacetime states are [fermions](fermion.md). The [string intercepts](normal-ordering-constant-of-a-string.md) differ, so the pair of oscillator vacua is not level matched: $N_L-1/2=N_R$. After the supersymmetric [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), the lowest allowed states have $(N_L,N_R)=(1/2,0)$ and are a massless [gravitino](gravitino.md) and [dilatino](dilatino.md).
