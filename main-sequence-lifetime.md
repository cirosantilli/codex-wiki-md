# Main-sequence lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Main sequence](main-sequence.md)

The time a [star](star.md) spends with core hydrogen [nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md) as its principal energy source. If the usable fuel fraction $f$, conversion efficiency $\epsilon$, and composition are comparable, the [main-sequence lifetime](main-sequence-lifetime.md) scales as $M/L$. With a local [mass-luminosity relation](mass-luminosity-relation.md) $L\propto M^\alpha$, the lifetime scales as $M^{1-\alpha}$. This is a scaling estimate: convective core size and changing [luminosity](luminosity.md) affect detailed ages.
