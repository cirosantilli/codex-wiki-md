# Local conservation of electromagnetic momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md)

With the momentum-flux sign convention used for the [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md), Maxwell's equations imply

$$
\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\sigma
+\partial_t\mathbf g=-\mathbf f_{\rm L},
$$

where $\mathbf g$ is the [electromagnetic momentum density](electromagnetic-momentum-density.md) and $\mathbf f_{\rm L}$ is the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) exerted by the field on matter. Thus total field-plus-matter momentum is locally conserved.
