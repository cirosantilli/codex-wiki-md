# Nonpenetration constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

For a body lying above an obstacle with height $f$, its normal displacement graph $w$ must obey the displayed inequality. Equality allows contact and strict inequality means separation. This is a unilateral [mechanical constraint](constraint-mechanics.md): motion away from the obstacle is allowed, but motion through it is forbidden. In nonadhesive frictionless contact the associated [normal contact force](normal-contact-force.md) can push the body outwards but cannot pull it into the obstacle.
