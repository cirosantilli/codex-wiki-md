# Key renewal theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Renewal process](renewal-process.md)

For positive nonarithmetic interarrival times with law $K$ and mean $m\in(0,\infty]$, let $U=\sum_{j\geq0}K^{*j}$ be their [renewal measure](renewal-measure.md). If $g$ is [directly Riemann integrable](direct-riemann-integrability.md), then $\int_{[0,t]}g(t-x)U(dx)\to m^{-1}\int_0^\infty g(x)\,dx$, with $1/\infty=0$. Thus a locally bounded solution of $z=g+z*K$ has this limit through its [renewal representation](renewal-representation.md). Arithmetic laws require the corresponding lattice version.
