# M-theory torus duality

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory](m-theory.md)

[M-theory](m-theory.md) on a two-torus and [type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md) on a circle describe the same nine-dimensional physics. Torus shape supplies the [axion-dilaton](axion-dilaton.md); torus area supplies the circle-radius modulus after matching metrics. An [M2-brane](m2-brane.md) on a one-cycle gives a [(p,q) string](p-q-string.md); an [M5-brane](m5-brane.md) on the whole torus gives a [D3-brane](d3-brane.md). Matching brane charges and tensions establishes the dictionary in [The Power of M Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9510086).
