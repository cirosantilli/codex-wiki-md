# Inradius

↑ **Parent:** [Incircle of a triangle](incircle-of-a-triangle.md)

The inradius is the [radius](radius.md) of an [incircle](incircle-of-a-triangle.md). For a [triangle](triangle.md) of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ and [semiperimeter](semiperimeter.md) $s$, it is $r=A/s$: joining the [incenter](incenter.md) to the three vertices partitions the [triangle](triangle.md) into three [triangles](triangle.md) with common altitude $r$.
