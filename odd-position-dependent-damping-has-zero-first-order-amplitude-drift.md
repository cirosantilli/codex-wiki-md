# Odd position-dependent damping has zero first-order amplitude drift

↑ **Parent:** [Averaged amplitude for position-dependent damping](averaged-amplitude-for-position-dependent-damping.md)

If $f$ is odd, its average $\langle f(A\cos\psi)\sin^2\psi\rangle$ vanishes under a half-period shift. Hence the [averaged amplitude for position-dependent damping](averaged-amplitude-for-position-dependent-damping.md) is constant at first order. Odd-power damping creates only even harmonics when multiplied by the leading oscillator velocity; these do not resonate with the fundamental harmonic. Higher-order drift is still possible.
