# Unknotting crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Crossing change](crossing-change.md)

An unknotting crossing is a crossing whose [crossing change](crossing-change.md) converts a [knot](knot.md) into the [unknot](unknot.md). It gives a genus-one [knot cobordism](knot-cobordism.md) to the [unknot](unknot.md), and hence [slice genus](slice-genus.md) at most one after capping with a disk in $B^4$.
