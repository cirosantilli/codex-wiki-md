# Mole fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical species](chemical-species.md)

The mole fraction of species $i$ is $x_i=N_i/\sum_jN_j$, where $N_i$ is its amount in moles. It is dimensionless and equals the [volume mixing ratio](volume-mixing-ratio.md) for an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) mixture.
