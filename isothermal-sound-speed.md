# Isothermal sound speed

↑ **Parent:** [Speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md)

The isothermal sound speed is $a_i^2=(\partial p/\partial\rho)_T$. For an ideal gas it is $a_i^2=p/\rho=\mathcal R T/\mu$.
