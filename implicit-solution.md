# Implicit solution

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A solution specified by a relation $F(x,y)=C$, instead of by an explicit graph $y=f(x)$. Differentiating the relation verifies its [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md). Where $F_y\ne0$, the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) supplies a local graph; other points may require a different parameterization.
