# Hoar frost

↑ **Parent:** [Frost](frost.md)

An ice-crystal deposit formed directly from atmospheric water vapour on a sufficiently cold surface. It is distinguished from [frozen dew](frozen-dew.md), in which liquid water first condenses and subsequently freezes.[Depositional frost and frozen dew](dew-and-frost-development-by-jeff-haby.md)
