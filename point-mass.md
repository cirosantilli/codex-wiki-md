# Point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Mass](mass.md)

A point mass idealizes a localized body's mass density as $\rho(\mathbf x,t)=m\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf z(t))$, using the [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md). Its [second mass moment tensor](second-mass-moment-tensor.md) is $I_{ij}=mz_i z_j$. The approximation ignores its internal size and structure.
