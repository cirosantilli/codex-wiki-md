# Colloidal glass

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A [colloidal glass](colloidal-glass.md) is a disordered dense particle assembly with structural relaxation too slow on the observation timescale. Crowding, repulsion or short-range attraction can impede rearrangement. Kinetic arrest must be distinguished from an equilibrium [phase transition](phase-transition.md) to a [crystal](crystal.md).
