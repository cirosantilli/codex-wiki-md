# Unitarization of a compact-group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary representation](unitary-representation.md)

For a compact group with normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), averaging any positive-definite Hermitian form,

$$
\langle v,w\rangle_G
=\int_G\langle\rho(g)v,\rho(g)w\rangle\,dg,
$$

produces an invariant positive-definite Hermitian form.
