# Dispersion symmetry elimination of a boundary trace

↑ **Parent:** [Fokas method](fokas-method.md)

To eliminate an unknown boundary transform by a [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) symmetry, evaluate the [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) at $\nu(k)$ only where the spatial transform remains analytic. Substitute the resulting trace identity into the contour representation. An unwanted transform vanishes only if its transformed argument and exponential admit a valid [contour deformation](contour-deformation.md) and decay estimate. For constant drift, $\nu(k)=i\alpha-k$ maps the upper domain $\operatorname{Re}(k^2-i\alpha k)<0$ into the lower transform half-plane.
