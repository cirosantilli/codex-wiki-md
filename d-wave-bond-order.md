# D-wave bond order

↑ **Parent:** [D-wave superconductivity](d-wave-superconductivity.md)

For nearest-neighbor spin-singlet pairing on a square lattice, opposite signs of the pairing amplitude on the two bond directions give the displayed momentum-dependent [superconducting gap function](superconducting-gap-function.md). It changes sign under a quarter-turn and has nodes on the zone diagonals. This sign structure is distinct from a nonnegative gap whose magnitude is merely anisotropic. A real pure $d_{x^2-y^2}$ pattern does not by itself require broken time reversal.
