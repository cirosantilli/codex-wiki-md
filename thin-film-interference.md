# Thin-film interference

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin-film_interference)

[Thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md) combines coherent [light](light.md) contributions reflected or transmitted at the two surfaces of a layer. For a film of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n$, thickness $d$ and internal angle $\theta_t$, the round-trip [optical path difference](optical-path-difference.md) is $2nd\cos\theta_t$. The relative reflection [phase shift](phase-shift.md) must also be included. For a nonabsorbing higher-index film surrounded by air, one of the two leading reflected contributions has a $\pi$ [phase shift](phase-shift.md) and the other does not. Reflection maxima therefore satisfy $2nd\cos\theta_t=(m+1/2)\lambda_0$. A smooth reflection spectrum filters many visible [wavelengths](wavelength.md) at once; varying thickness and angle produce spatially varying mixed colours.
