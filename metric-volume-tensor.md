# Metric volume tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Volume form](volume-form.md)

On an oriented $d$-dimensional [pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md), the metric volume tensor is the totally antisymmetric [tensor](tensor.md) with $\epsilon_{1\ldots d}=\sqrt{|\det g|}$ in positively oriented coordinates. It supplies the components of the [volume form](volume-form.md). It differs from the coordinate [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md), whose entries are just $0,1,-1$. Raising indices with a [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md) can change signs; the metric, dimension and orientation must be specified.
