# Centered Dirichlet drift-diffusion energy identity

↑ **Parent:** [Energy method](energy-method.md)

For the centered second difference and centered first difference on a finite interval with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the diffusion [matrix](matrix.md) is symmetric negative definite and the drift [matrix](matrix.md) is [skew-symmetric](skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Discrete [summation by parts](abel-s-summation-formula.md) gives the displayed mesh-weighted identity. It proves contraction in the discrete [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) without needing periodic modes, positive stencil coefficients or simultaneous diagonalization.
