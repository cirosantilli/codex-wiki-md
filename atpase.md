# ATPase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATPase)

An enzyme catalysing [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) hydrolysis. The isolated $F_1$ head of [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) is a rotary ATPase; ATPase activity by itself does not imply proton pumping.
