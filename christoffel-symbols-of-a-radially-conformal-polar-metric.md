# Christoffel symbols of a radially conformal polar metric

↑ **Parent:** [Christoffel symbol](christoffel-symbol.md)

For the displayed [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), its inverse has components $g^{rr}=e^{-2\chi}$ and $g^{\theta\theta}=e^{-2\chi}/r^2$. The nonzero second-kind [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) are $\Gamma^r{}_{rr}=\chi'$, $\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta}=-r-r^2\chi'$ and $\Gamma^\theta{}_{r\theta}=\Gamma^\theta{}_{\theta r}=1/r+\chi'$. They follow by differentiating the two diagonal metric entries and raising the first index of the first-kind connection coefficients. [Polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) require treating $r=0$ separately.
