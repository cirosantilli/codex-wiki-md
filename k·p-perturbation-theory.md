# k·p perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)

For a local periodic scalar potential, writing a Bloch wave as $e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}u_{n\mathbf k}$ gives the displayed cell-periodic Hamiltonian before additional spin-orbit terms. Momentum matrix elements mix zone-centre bands when k is varied. Perturbatively eliminating remote bands changes the [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md); a multiband retained subspace describes degeneracy, nonparabolicity and coupled hole bands. Spin-orbit coupling and the chosen reference wavevector must be included consistently.
