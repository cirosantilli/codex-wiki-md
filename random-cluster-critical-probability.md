# Random-cluster critical probability

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md)

The threshold for the origin to have an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) under a consistently chosen infinite-volume [random-cluster measure](random-cluster-model.md), conventionally the wired measure: $p_c(q)=\inf\{p:\theta^{\mathrm w}(p,q)>0\}$. The definition alone makes no assertion about percolation exactly at the threshold.
