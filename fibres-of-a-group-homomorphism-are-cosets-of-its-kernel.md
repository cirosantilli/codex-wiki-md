# Fibres of a group homomorphism are cosets of its kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md)

For a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\varphi:G\to H$, two inputs give the same output exactly when $\varphi(h^{-1}g)=e_H$. Consequently the [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md) over an output $\varphi(h)$ is the left [coset](coset.md) $h\ker\varphi$. These [cosets](coset.md) identify precisely the inputs indistinguishable to $\varphi$, which is the mechanism behind the [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md).
