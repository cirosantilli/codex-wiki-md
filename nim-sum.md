# Nim-sum

↑ **Parent:** [Nim](nim.md)

The bitwise [exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) of a list of nonnegative [integers](integer-split.md). For heap sizes in ordinary [Nim](nim.md), a zero nim-sum characterizes a [losing position](losing-position.md) under the [normal-play convention](normal-play-convention.md).
