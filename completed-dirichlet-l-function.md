# Completed Dirichlet L-function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

For a primitive nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md), the theta Mellin integral makes this an [entire function](entire-function.md) satisfying $\Lambda(s,\chi)=\varepsilon_\chi\Lambda(1-s,\overline\chi)$. The gamma [poles](pole.md) cancel the [trivial zeros of a Dirichlet L-function](trivial-zero-of-a-dirichlet-l-function.md). For the [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md) whose [conductor of a Dirichlet character](conductor-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) is one the completion is [meromorphic](meromorphic-function.md), with [poles](pole.md) at zero and one.
