# Galactic fountain

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic outflow](galactic-outflow.md)

Gas lifted out of a galactic disk can remain bound and return rather than escape into the [intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md). This recycling carries metals and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) between the disk and its surroundings and delays the availability of gas for subsequent [star formation](star-formation.md).
