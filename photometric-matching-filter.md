# Photometric matching filter

↑ **Parent:** [Photometer](photometer.md)

A [photometric matching filter](photometric-matching-filter.md) shapes a [photodiode](photodiode.md)'s combined [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md) toward the [photopic luminous efficiency function](photopic-luminous-efficiency-function.md). Matching is imperfect, so [photometric calibration](photometric-calibration.md) must retain spectral information. [Realization of the candela](realization-of-the-candela.md)
