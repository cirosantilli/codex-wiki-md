# Prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication isomorphism of a formal group law](multiplication-isomorphism-of-a-formal-group-law.md)

Let $R$ be a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) of residue [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, with [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m$, and let $F$ be a one-dimensional [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) over $R$. Its multiplication series is $[n]_F(T)=nT+O(T^2)$. If $p\nmid n$, the [invertible morphism criterion for formal group laws](invertible-morphism-criterion-for-formal-group-laws.md) constructs its inverse with coefficients in $R$. Both [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) converge on $\mathfrak m$, since their coefficients are integral and the powers of an element of $\mathfrak m$ tend to zero. Their compositions are the identity there. Thus multiplication by $n$ is bijective, including when $p=2$ and $n$ is odd. Every finite-order element of $F(\mathfrak m)$ consequently has [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) equal to a [prime power](prime-power.md) with prime $p$.
