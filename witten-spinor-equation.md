# Witten spinor equation

↑ **Parent:** [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md)

This elliptic equation on a spacelike [spin](spin.md) hypersurface uses the restriction of the spacetime [spin connection](spin-connection.md), including [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md). The spinor tends to a prescribed constant at spatial infinity. It removes the negative derivative square in the divergence of the [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md). It is weaker than the spacetime [Killing spinor](killing-spinor.md) equation; generic positive-energy initial data need not admit any [Killing spinor](killing-spinor.md).
