# Torsion-free divisible Abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Divisible group](divisible-group.md)

A divisible [Abelian group](abelian-group.md) is torsion-free precisely when division by each nonzero integer is unique. Defining rational scalar multiplication by $n((m/n)a)=ma$ identifies these groups with [vector spaces over the rational numbers](vector-space-over-the-rational-numbers.md), including the zero space.
