# Coordinate sphere intersection basis

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection pairing](intersection-pairing.md)

In the product $(S^{2d})^{2\ell}$, for $d,\ell\ge1$, vary the factors indexed by an $\ell$-element subset $I$ and fix points in the other factors. Orient the resulting [submanifold](submanifold.md) $S_I$ in increasing factor order. These [homology classes](homology-class.md) form an integral middle-dimensional basis by the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md). Complementary subsets give a transverse intersection at one point, with positive sign since all factor dimensions are even. For noncomplementary subsets some factor is fixed in both representatives; moving that point makes them disjoint. In particular every basis class has zero [self-intersection number](self-intersection-number.md). Complementary pairs give hyperbolic blocks in the [intersection form](intersection-form.md).
