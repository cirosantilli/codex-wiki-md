# Marsden dual functional

↑ **Parent:** [Marsden identity](marsden-identity.md)

The Marsden dual functionals extract coefficients in a [B-spline](b-spline.md) expansion. If $\psi_i$ is the knot polynomial in the [Marsden identity](marsden-identity.md), then on polynomials of degree at most $k-1$ one such functional is

$$
\lambda_i(p)=\frac1{(k-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(-1)^j\psi_i^{(k-1-j)}(x)p^{(j)}(x),
$$

and the right-hand side is independent of $x$.
