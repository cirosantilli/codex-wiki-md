# Discrete Helmholtz resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md)

On a square Dirichlet grid with spacing $h=1/J$ and interior indices $1,\ldots,J-1$, the [five-point Laplacian](five-point-laplacian.md) is diagonalized by products of the sine modes used in the [discrete sine transform](discrete-sine-transform.md). The discrete equation $(\Delta_h+\lambda I)U=F$ is nonsingular exactly when $\lambda$ is not one of the displayed positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $-\Delta_h$, with $1\leq p,q\leq J-1$. Values below the first resonance are sufficient but not necessary for invertibility.
