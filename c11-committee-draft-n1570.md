# C11 committee draft N1570

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

[https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/www/docs/n1570.pdf](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/www/docs/n1570.pdf)

The 12 April 2011 committee draft specifies [C integer division](c-integer-division.md) in section 6.5.5, especially paragraphs 5–6 and footnote 105. It fixes the truncation and quotient–remainder relationship used when verifying [signed power-of-two division by biasing](signed-power-of-two-division-by-biasing.md).
