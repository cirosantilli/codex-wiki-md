# Linear growth of shifted quadratic spline interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline interpolation operator norm](b-spline-interpolation-operator-norm.md)

For the degree-two [Cardinal B-spline](cardinal-b-spline.md) basis on knots $1,\ldots,n+3$, sampling at $x_i=i+2$ gives the upper-bidiagonal [B-spline collocation matrix](b-spline-collocation-matrix.md) $A=(I+S)/2$, where $S$ is the one-step upper shift. Since $S^n=0$, $A^{-1}=2\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}(-S)^r$ and its maximum absolute row sum is $2n$. The [B-spline interpolation operator norm](b-spline-interpolation-operator-norm.md) estimate with the order-three stability constant $d_3=3$ gives the displayed linear upper and lower bounds. The lower bound proves failure of uniform boundedness.
