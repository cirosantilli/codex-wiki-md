# Logarithmic mean

↑ **Parent:** [Mean (mathematics)](mean-mathematics.md)

For distinct [positive real numbers](positive-real-number.md) $a,b$, their logarithmic mean is $L(a,b)=(b-a)/(\log b-\log a)$, with continuous extension $L(a,a)=a$.
