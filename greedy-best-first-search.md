# Greedy best-first search

↑ **Parent:** [Best-first search](best-first-search.md)

[Best-first search](best-first-search.md) using only a [heuristic function](heuristic-function.md) estimating remaining goal cost, without adding accumulated [path cost](path-cost.md). A comparator supplying the same total ordering of estimates suffices to choose expansions even if numeric estimates are unavailable. In general it need not be a [complete search algorithm](complete-search-algorithm.md) or an [optimal search algorithm](optimal-search-algorithm.md); particular stronger information or finite-[graph](graph-split.md) policies can supply additional guarantees.
