# Divergence of a curl

↑ **Parent:** [Vector calculus identity](vector-calculus-identity.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable [vector field](vector-field.md) in [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md), expand the [curl](curl.md) and then its [divergence](divergence.md). Every mixed [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) occurs twice with opposite signs, proving the identity. In [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md) this forces the right side of the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) to be [divergence-free](solenoidal-vector-field.md), yielding local [charge conservation](charge-conservation.md).
