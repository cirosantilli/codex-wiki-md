# Gradient of a Moreau envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Moreau envelope](moreau-envelope.md)

For proper [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md) [convex functions](convex-function.md), the [Moreau envelope](moreau-envelope.md) has gradient $\nabla f_\tau=(I-\operatorname{prox}_{\tau f})/\tau$. This follows by [subgradient inversion under convex conjugacy](subgradient-inversion-under-convex-conjugacy.md) and the quadratic conjugate. Firm nonexpansiveness of the proximal residual makes the gradient $1/\tau$-Lipschitz continuous.
