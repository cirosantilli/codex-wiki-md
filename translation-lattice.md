# Translation lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Translation subgroup](translation-subgroup.md)

A translation lattice is the additive group generated by linearly independent vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ in a Euclidean space. It defines a [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md) of [translations](translation-geometry.md). A full-rank translation lattice has a compact parallelepiped as a fundamental region and therefore a [cocompact group action](cocompact-group-action.md). This geometric usage is distinct from an order-theoretic lattice.
