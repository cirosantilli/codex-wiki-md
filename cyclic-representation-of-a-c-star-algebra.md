# Cyclic representation of a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a Banach algebra](representation-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

A representation of a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is a star-homomorphism $\pi:A\to\mathcal B(H)$. It is cyclic if some [vector](vector.md) $\xi$ has dense orbit span as displayed; the orbit $\pi(A)\xi$ is itself a [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md). For a unital representation, a unit cyclic [vector](vector.md) defines a [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) by $a\mapsto\langle\pi(a)\xi,\xi\rangle$. Conversely, the [Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md) realizes every state this way. Cyclicity means density under the action of the whole algebra, not necessarily under powers of a single chosen element.
