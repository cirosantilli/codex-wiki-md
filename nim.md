# Nim

↑ **Parent:** [Impartial game](impartial-game.md)

An [impartial game](impartial-game.md) played with heaps of counters: a move removes a positive number of counters from exactly one heap. Under the [normal-play convention](normal-play-convention.md), a position is losing exactly when the bitwise [exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) of its heap sizes is zero.
