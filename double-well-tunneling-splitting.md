# Double-well tunneling splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)

A symmetric two-well system has nearly localized left/right states coupled by a positive [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) magnitude $\Delta=\hbar Ke^{-S_I/\hbar}$. Its effective [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $E_{\rm well}I-\Delta\sigma_x$, so the superposition with an [even](even-function.md) spatial [wavefunction](wave-function.md) is lower, and the level splitting is $2\Delta$. The [dilute instanton gas](dilute-instanton-gas.md) reproduces these two exponents through even/odd crossing sums.
