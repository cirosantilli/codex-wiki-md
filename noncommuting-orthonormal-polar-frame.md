# Noncommuting orthonormal polar frame

↑ **Parent:** [Commuting coordinate basis](commuting-coordinate-basis.md)

On the punctured Euclidean plane, the orthonormal polar frame is

$$
\widehat r=\partial_r,
\qquad
\widehat\theta=\frac1r\partial_\theta.
$$

Its bracket is $[\widehat r,\widehat\theta]=-\widehat\theta/r$, so it is not a [coordinate basis](coordinate-basis.md) even though $(\partial_r,\partial_\theta)$ is one.
