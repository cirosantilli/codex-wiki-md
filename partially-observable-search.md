# Partially observable search

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

A [search problem](search-problem.md) in which the available observations need not identify the current physical state. The controller must choose the same action for physical states that its observed history cannot distinguish. A [belief state](belief-state.md) summarizes those possibilities; [contingent planning](contingent-planning.md) selects future actions according to subsequent observations.
