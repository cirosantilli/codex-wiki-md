<h1 id="velocity-ellipsoid-alignment-in-a-stackel-potential">Velocity-ellipsoid alignment in a Stäckel potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

A regular phase-mixed distribution $F(H,p_\phi,I)$ in an axisymmetric [Stäckel potential](stackel-potential.md) is even separately in $v_\lambda$ and $v_\mu$, because these [integrals](integral.md) depend on those components only through squares. Odd [integration](integral.md) then gives all three mixed [second moments](second-moment.md) zero, even if the $p_\phi$ dependence produces azimuthal streaming. Bare stationarity alone is insufficient in degenerate resonant potentials with additional [integrals](integral.md). For example the isotropic harmonic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) has the extra conserved $K=v_xv_y+\omega^2xy$; a positive Gaussian $\exp(-aH+\epsilon K)$ with $a>|\epsilon|>0$ is a steady tracer and has nonzero $\langle v_xv_y\rangle$. Its harmonic [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) is a [Stäckel potential](stackel-potential.md) in these spheroidal coordinates. This tracer need not itself source that harmonic [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). The alignment claim thus needs the phase-mixed three-integral hypothesis, not merely the weak form of [Jeans theorem](jeans-theorem.md).
