# Exterior magnetic field of a wire with an eccentric cylindrical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md)

A uniform [electric current density](current-density.md) outside a parallel cylindrical cavity can be constructed by the [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md): add a full cylinder carrying that density and a displaced cylinder carrying its negative. At an exterior point, each contribution is the external [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) of its own net [electric current](electric-current.md). The combined [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) generally lacks [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) even though [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) fixes its [line integral](line-integral.md) around the whole wire.
