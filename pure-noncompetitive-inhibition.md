# Pure noncompetitive inhibition

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed inhibition](mixed-inhibition.md)

Set $K_i=K_i'$, so that $\alpha=\alpha'$. The [reversible enzyme inhibition](reversible-enzyme-inhibition.md) equation reduces to

$$
v=\frac{1}{\alpha}\frac{V_{\max}s}{K_M+s},\qquad
V_{\max,\rm app}=\frac{V_{\max}}{\alpha},\quad K_{M,\rm app}=K_M.
$$

At the chosen $I=30\,\mathrm{\mu M}$, every [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) is one quarter of its uninhibited value. The plateau falls to $25\,\mathrm{\mu M\,min^{-1}}$, but half that plateau still occurs at $s=20\,\mathrm{\mu M}$. Increasing [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) cannot restore the uninhibited plateau. An unchanged apparent [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md) here expresses equality of the two binding effects, rather than an absence of inhibitor binding to the [enzyme-substrate complex](enzyme-substrate-complex.md).
