# Weitz-Scheffer reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

The [Weitz-Scheffer epoxidation](weitz-scheffer-reaction.md) of an electron-poor alkene uses hydroperoxide addition followed by intramolecular closure and O–O bond cleavage. A planar achiral enone in an achiral environment can give a racemic epoxide pair.
