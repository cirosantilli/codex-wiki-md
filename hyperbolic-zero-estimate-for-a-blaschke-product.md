# Hyperbolic zero estimate for a Blaschke product

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md)

Use curvature-minus-one [Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare disc](hyperbolic-distance-in-the-poincare-disc.md). Each [Blaschke factor](blaschke-factor.md) has modulus $\tanh(\rho(w,a)/2)=(1-t)/(1+t)$, with $t=e^{-\rho(w,a)}$. For $0\le t<1$, differentiating shows $\log[(1+t)/(1-t)]\ge2t$. Sum this logarithmic inequality over the zero factors and pass to the [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md) by [locally uniform convergence](locally-uniform-convergence.md). At a prescribed zero, its modulus vanishes and the bound is immediate. All sums count [multiplicities](multiplicity-mathematics.md).
