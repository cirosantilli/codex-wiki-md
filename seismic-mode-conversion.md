# Seismic mode conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

At a material interface, incident P or SV [energy](energy-split.md) can scatter into both P and SV modes, subject to conserved horizontal slowness and [traction](traction.md)/[displacement](displacement.md) conditions. SH is decoupled in a planar [isotropic](isotropy.md) model; some conversions vanish at symmetry directions such as normal incidence.
