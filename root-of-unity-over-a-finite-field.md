# Root of unity over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity](root-of-unity.md)

These roots are taken in an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md), not merely in $\mathbb F_q$. If the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is $p$ and $n=p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$, then $X^n-1=(X^m-1)^{p^a}$; hence there are $m$ distinct roots. They form a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) by the [finite multiplicative subgroup of a field is cyclic](finite-multiplicative-subgroup-of-a-field-is-cyclic.md) theorem. In particular they have order $n$ when $\gcd(n,q)=1$.
