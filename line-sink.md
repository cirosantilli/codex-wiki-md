# Line sink

↑ **Parent:** [Point source](point-source.md)

A [line sink](line-sink.md) is a planar [line source](line-source.md) with negative [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md): fluid moves radially inward towards its location. For withdrawal rate $S>0$, its [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) is $-S\log(z-b)/(2\pi)$.
