# Sobolev function with zero weak gradient

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md)

On a connected open set, a [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) function with zero [weak gradient](weak-gradient.md) is constant almost everywhere. Local [mollification](mollification.md) gives constant smooth representatives on interior balls; overlaps and connectedness identify their constants. Without connectedness, the constant may differ between components. This explains the constant ambiguity in a [Neumann Poisson problem](neumann-poisson-problem.md).
