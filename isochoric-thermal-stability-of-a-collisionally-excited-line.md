# Isochoric thermal stability of a collisionally excited line

↑ **Parent:** [Critical density of an atomic transition](critical-density-of-an-atomic-transition.md)

At fixed [density](density.md) below critical [density](density.md), optically thin two-level cooling scales as $\mathcal C\propto T^{-1/2}e^{-\theta/T}$, with $\theta=\Delta E/k_B$. Under [temperature](temperature-split.md)-independent heating, $d\ln\mathcal C/d\ln T=-1/2+\theta/T$ gives restoring thermal balance for $T<2\theta$ and instability for $T>2\theta$. This boundary assumes fixed ionic abundance and does not describe a constant-[pressure](pressure-split.md) perturbation.
