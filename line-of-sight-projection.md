# Line-of-sight projection

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomy](astronomy-split.md)

The map from a three-dimensional astronomical distribution to its observed sky position or integrated surface signal. For a scalar volume emissivity $j(\mathbf x)$ in simple transparent geometry, [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) is proportional to $\int j\,dl$ along the ray. Reconstructing the spatial distribution requires additional geometry or symmetry assumptions; a projected surface distribution is not a unique three-dimensional [density](density.md).
