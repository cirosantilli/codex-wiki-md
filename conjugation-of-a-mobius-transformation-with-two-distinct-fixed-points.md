<h1 id="conjugation-of-a-mobius-transformation-with-two-distinct-fixed-points">Conjugation of a Möbius transformation with two distinct fixed points</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point of a Möbius transformation](fixed-point-of-a-mobius-transformation.md)

For $f(z)=(az+b)/(cz+d)$ with distinct finite [fixed points of a Möbius transformation](fixed-point-of-a-mobius-transformation.md) $\alpha,\beta$, the fixed-point equation gives $f(z)-\alpha=(a-c\alpha)(z-\alpha)/(cz+d)$. Dividing the two identities gives $k=(a-c\alpha)/(a-c\beta)$, which is nonzero because $(a-c\alpha)(a-c\beta)=ad-bc\ne0$. The identities extend over the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). This conjugates the transformation to a dilation.
