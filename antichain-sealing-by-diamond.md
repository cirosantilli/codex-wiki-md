# Antichain sealing by diamond

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary diamond principle](stationary-diamond-principle.md)

At a correctly guessing limit stage of a normal tree construction, make every new level node extend a member of the guessed maximal [tree antichain](tree-antichain.md). It follows that the antichain has no later member. This produces a [Suslin tree](suslin-tree.md) from the [stationary diamond principle](stationary-diamond-principle.md).
