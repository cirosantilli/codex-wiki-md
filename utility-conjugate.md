# Utility conjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md)

For an increasing concave utility on positive wealth, its [utility conjugate](utility-conjugate.md) uses the indicated supremum and is a [convex function](convex-function.md) of the positive price variable. If $F(x)=-U(x)$ on positive $x$, extended by infinity elsewhere, then $\widehat U(y)=F^*(-y)$. Under the [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md) its optimizer is the [inverse marginal utility](inverse-marginal-utility.md), and its [derivative](derivative.md) is the negative of that optimizer. The sign convention is part of the definition.
