# Prismatic-channel shallow water equations

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

In a channel whose transverse width $b(z)$ does not depend on downstream position, the wetted area is $A(h)=\int_0^h b(z)\,dz$ and the integrated [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) force divided by density is $P(h)=g\int_0^h(h-z)b(z)\,dz$. With cross-sectionally uniform velocity, [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) give the displayed [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md). Since $P'(h)=gA(h)$, the two [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) are $u\pm c$ with $c^2=gA/A'$. For $b(z)=Cz^p$, $c^2=gh/(p+1)$ and the [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md) are $u\pm2(p+1)c$.
