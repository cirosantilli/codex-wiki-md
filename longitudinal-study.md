# Longitudinal study

↑ **Parent:** [Observational study](observational-study.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_study)

A [longitudinal study](longitudinal-study.md) observes the same individuals repeatedly. Within-person changes distinguish temporal development from persistent differences between people, although calendar-period changes, selective dropout and [measurement error](measurement-error.md) still require attention. A [random-intercept linear mixed model](random-intercept-linear-mixed-model.md) can represent persistent individual differences and correlated repeated measurements.
