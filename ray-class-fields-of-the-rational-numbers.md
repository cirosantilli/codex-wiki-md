# Ray class fields of the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Ray class field](ray-class-field.md)

For a positive integer $m$, the finite [ray class group](ray-class-group.md) of $\mathbb Q$ is $(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times/\langle-1\rangle$: the [unit group](unit-group.md) permits either sign of a rational generator. Its [ray class field](ray-class-field.md) is the maximal real subfield of the [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$. Including the real place requires positive principal generators and gives the full group $(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times$ and full [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md). Thus modulus $(5)$ gives $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$, while $(5)\infty$ gives $\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)$. The [Artin reciprocity map](artin-reciprocity-law.md) sends a prime $\ell\nmid m$ to the exponent $\ell$, taken modulo $\{\pm1\}$ in the finite case. Ramification at real places is possible only when those places are included in the [modulus of a number field](modulus-of-a-number-field.md).
