# Error norm

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

A [norm](norm.md) used to quantify numerical error on a grid, such as the maximum norm or a mesh-weighted root-mean-square norm. Convergence studies must compare the same norm at a fixed physical time.
