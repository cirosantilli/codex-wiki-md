# Local Lorentz invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md)

Local Lorentz invariance requires the outcome of a local experiment, expressed in its freely falling apparatus, to be independent of the apparatus's velocity and orientation. In the [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md) it applies to nongravitational experiments; the [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) also includes local gravitational experiments. It is distinct from [local position invariance](local-position-invariance.md).
