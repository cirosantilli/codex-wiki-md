# Adaptive introgression

↑ **Parent:** [Introgression](introgression.md)

Transfer of inherited variation between lineages through [hybridization](hybridization-biology.md) and subsequent backcrossing, followed by a selective benefit in the recipient. Shared ancestry at a region and evidence of its benefit are separate requirements.
