# Quantum concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md)

The translational concentration scale for a nonrelativistic particle in three dimensions, per internal state. It is the inverse cube of the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md). A classical component with number density $n$ and external one-particle energy $U$ has [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) $\mu=k_BT\ln(n/n_Q)+U$. An additional internal degeneracy must be included explicitly; it is not hidden in this definition. The dilute classical condition is $n/n_Q\ll1$ for each occupied internal component.
