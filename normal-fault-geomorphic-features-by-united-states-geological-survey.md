# Normal-fault geomorphic features by United States Geological Survey

↑ **Parent:** [Geology bibliography](geology-bibliography.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by United States Geological Survey](work-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)

[https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/1991/report.pdf](https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/1991/report.pdf)

Reusable locator for Table 1, “Geomorphic features associated with active faults”, in the primary mapping report _Late Quaternary Faulting, Death Valley–Furnace Creek Fault System, California-Nevada_, USGS Bulletin 1991. The normal-slip column lists scarps, faceted spurs, oversteepened mountain-front bases and rejuvenated valley floors. The official publication record confirms the report identity and 1991 publication year: [https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b1991](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b1991) .
