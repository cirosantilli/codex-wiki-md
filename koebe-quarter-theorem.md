# Koebe quarter theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Koebe distortion theorem](koebe-distortion-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koebe_quarter_theorem)

If $f$ is a [univalent function](univalent-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), then $B(f(0),|f'(0)|/4)\subseteq f(\mathbb D)$. The constant $1/4$ is sharp, as shown by the [Koebe function](koebe-function.md). This gives useful comparisons between the [derivative](derivative.md) of a [conformal map](conformal-map.md), its [conformal radius](conformal-radius.md), and its distance to the [domain boundary](boundary-of-a-domain.md).
