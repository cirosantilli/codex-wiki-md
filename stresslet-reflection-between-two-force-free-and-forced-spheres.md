# Stresslet reflection between two force-free and forced spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic interaction](hydrodynamic-interaction.md)

Consider two widely separated rigid spheres of equal radius $a$, with separation $R\mathbf n$, in [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md). Apply a [force](force.md) $6\pi\mu a\mathbf U_0$ to the first [sphere](sphere.md) and let the second be [force-free](force-free.md) and [torque-free](torque-free.md). The second [sphere](sphere.md) samples the [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) of the first [sphere](sphere.md)'s [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md), $\mathbf E=3a(\mathbf U_0\cdot\mathbf n)(\mathbf I-3\mathbf n\mathbf n)/(4R^2)$. Its leading disturbance is the [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) of a [sphere in a uniform straining Stokes flow](sphere-in-a-uniform-straining-stokes-flow.md). Evaluating that disturbance back at the first [sphere](sphere.md) gives

$$
\Delta\mathbf U=-\frac{15}{4}\left(\frac aR\right)^4(\mathbf U_0\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n.
$$

The [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md) decreases at fixed [force](force.md), consistently with the increase of resistance caused by an additional rigid inclusion.
