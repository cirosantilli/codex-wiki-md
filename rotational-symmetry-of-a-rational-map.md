# Rotational symmetry of a rational map

↑ **Parent:** [Rational map (complex analysis)](rational-map-complex-analysis.md)

Rotations of the domain and target [Riemann spheres](riemann-sphere.md) act as [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) represented by [SU(2) matrices](su-2-matrix.md). A combined symmetry requires the equivariance identity $R(gz)=hR(z)$ for the corresponding transformations. Symmetry of the [Wronskian of a rational map](wronskian-of-a-rational-map.md) alone is insufficient: it tests the ramification directions, not the entire map. Target rotations preserve the [angular Jacobian of a rational map](angular-jacobian-of-a-rational-map.md), so an equivariant map has a domain-invariant angular density.
