# Well-mixed flow reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Residence time distribution](residence-time-distribution.md)

A continuous reactor with uniform contents. At steady state for first-order [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md), $a_{\rm out}/a_{\rm in}=1/(1+\lambda V/Q)$; young material can leave before the mean [residence time](residence-time.md).
