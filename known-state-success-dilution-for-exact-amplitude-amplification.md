# Known-state success dilution for exact amplitude amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Exact amplitude amplification](exact-amplitude-amplification.md)

For known good probability $p=\sin^2\theta$, choose $j=\lceil\pi/(4\theta)-1/2\rceil$. A known reversible preparation of one state with good probability $p_*$ permits $j$ ordinary [amplitude amplification](amplitude-amplification.md) iterations to reach the good subspace exactly. With ancillary marking access, prepare a flag with one-probability $q=p_*/p$ and define joint success as good data and flag one. Reflection about the prepared product state is known, while the joint phase test requires a controlled marking oracle or a computed Boolean output. This changes the success projector and enlarges the state space; it does not evade the [obstruction to uniform probability lowering by a unitary](obstruction-to-uniform-probability-lowering-by-a-unitary.md). Query costs of preparation, its inverse and marking must all be counted.
