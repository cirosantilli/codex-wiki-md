# Geodesics on a circular cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic flatness of a circular cylinder](intrinsic-flatness-of-a-circular-cylinder.md)

In coordinates $(\theta,z)$ on a [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) of radius $a$, the [induced metric](induced-metric.md) is $a^2d\theta^2+dz^2$. Its coefficients are constant, so the [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) vanish and the affinely parametrized [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) is $\theta''=z''=0$. The nonconstant solutions are generators when $c=0$, circular parallels when $d=0$, and helices when both are nonzero. Unrolling the cylinder with coordinate $u=a\theta$ makes all these curves straight lines in the Euclidean metric. Unit-speed parametrization additionally requires $a^2c^2+d^2=1$.
