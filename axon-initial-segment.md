# Axon initial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Axon](axon.md)

The axon initial segment is the specialized proximal region of an [axon](axon.md) near the [soma](soma-biology.md). In many [neurons](neuron.md), its [voltage-gated ion channels](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md) make it the site where an [action potential](action-potential.md) begins.
