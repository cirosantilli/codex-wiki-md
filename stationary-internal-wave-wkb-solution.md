# Stationary internal-wave WKB solution

↑ **Parent:** [Scorer parameter](scorer-parameter.md)

For a stationary [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md), the [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) gives $m^2=N^2/U^2-U''/U-k^2$. In a propagating interval with slowly varying $m$, the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) has amplitude proportional to $m^{-1/2}$ and phase derivative $m$. The conditions $|m'|\ll m^2$ and $|m''|\ll |m|^3$ fail at a regular turning level. With $U>0$, the upward branch has $m>0$ and negative [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md); laboratory [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $U(k^2,km)/(k^2+m^2)$ when the curvature term is negligible.
