# Mycielski apex

↑ **Parent:** [Mycielskian](mycielskian.md)

The [Mycielski apex](mycielski-apex.md) is the added [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) adjacent to every [Mycielski shadow vertex](mycielski-shadow-vertex.md) and to no original [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Its [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) is precisely $\{u_1,\ldots,u_n\}$.
