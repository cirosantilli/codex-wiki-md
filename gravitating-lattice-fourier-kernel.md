# Gravitating lattice Fourier kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite gravitating particle lattice](infinite-gravitating-particle-lattice.md)

The discrete normal modes of an [infinite gravitating particle lattice](infinite-gravitating-particle-lattice.md) involve $F(\xi)=2\sum_{l=1}^\infty(1-\cos(l\xi))/l^3$. It is even, nonnegative and $2\pi$-periodic. Its derivative is a positive reciprocal-square sine series for $0<\xi<\pi$, so its maxima occur exactly at odd multiples of $\pi$, with $F(\pi)=(7/2)\zeta(3)$.
