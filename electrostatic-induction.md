# Electrostatic induction

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge.md)

[Electrostatic induction](electrostatic-induction.md) is redistribution of [electric charge](electric-charge.md) in response to an external charged object. An isolated [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) can acquire positive and negative surface regions while its total [electric charge](electric-charge.md) stays fixed; see the contact control in [electric charge](electric-charge.md).
