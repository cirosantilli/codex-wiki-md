<h1 id="banach-stone-theorem">Banach–Stone theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach–Stone_theorem)

A surjective linear [isometry](isometry.md) $U:C(K)\to C(L)$ between compact Hausdorff function spaces has the form $(Uf)(s)=u(s)f(h(s))$, where $h:L\to K$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) and $u$ is continuous with modulus one. Thus the [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) structure of $C(K)$ determines $K$ up to [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md).
