<h1 id="equality-in-chebyshev-s-inequality">Equality in Chebyshev's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-s-inequality.md)

For $a\geq\sigma>0$, assign [probability](probability.md) $\sigma^2/(2a^2)$ to each of $\mu-a$ and $\mu+a$, and the remaining [probability](probability.md) $1-\sigma^2/a^2$ to $\mu$. This [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) has [expected value](expected-value.md) $\mu$, [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, and [tail probability](tail-probability.md) $\mathbb P(|X-\mu|\geq a)=\sigma^2/a^2$. Thus [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-s-inequality.md) is sharp when only these two [moments](moment.md) are supplied. For example, $\mu=0$, $\sigma=1$, $a=2$ gives masses $1/8$, $3/4$, $1/8$. Replacing $\geq$ with $>$ would exclude the two extreme points in this example.
