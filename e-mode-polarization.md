# E-mode polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background polarization](cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md)

The parity-even part of linear sky polarization can be represented by a scalar potential $P_E$ through $Q\pm iU=\eth_{\pm}^2(P_E\pm iP_B)$. It is naturally separated from [B-mode polarization](b-mode-polarization.md) by the parity of its spherical multipoles. Linear [scalar cosmological perturbations](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md) generate E modes; the potential has an unobservable low-multipole kernel.
