# Unramified extensions remain unramified under base change

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

For non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md), let $L/K$ be an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of degree $f$, and let $E/K$ be finite. The degree-$f$ [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of $E$ has a [residue field](residue-field.md) containing the degree-$f$ residue extension of $K$. Lift an integral residue generator of $L$ into that field by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). This embeds $L$ into an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of $E$, so $LE/E$ is an [unramified](unramified-extension.md) subextension. Applying this argument to each [completion of a number field at a prime ideal](completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime-ideal.md) proves the finite-prime assertion for [number fields](number-field.md), even when $E/K$ is ramified.
