# Strip density of a spherical system

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical density projection](spherical-density-projection.md)

The strip density is the mass per unit projected Cartesian coordinate $x$: $S(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Sigma(\sqrt{x^2+y^2})\,dy$. It is even in $x$. For $x\geq0$, changing variables to projected radius gives $S(x)=2\int_x^\infty\Sigma(R)R/\sqrt{R^2-x^2}\,dR$. Alternatively integrate the three-dimensional [mass density](density.md) over the plane of coordinates $y,z$ to obtain the displayed expression. Consequently $S'(x)=-2\pi x\rho(x)$ for $x>0$.
