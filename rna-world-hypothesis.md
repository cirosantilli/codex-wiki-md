# RNA world hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

The hypothesis proposes an early hereditary system in which [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) or closely related molecules carried sequence information and performed important [catalytic](catalysis.md) functions. Modern [ribozymes](ribozyme.md), [self-splicing introns](self-splicing-intron.md) and the RNA-centred [ribosome](ribosome.md) demonstrate relevant capacities; they do not by themselves prove a complete origin-of-life pathway. Nucleotide availability, copying fidelity, strand separation, energy supply and chemical stability remain separate constraints.
