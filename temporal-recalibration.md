# Temporal recalibration

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal binding window](temporal-binding-window.md)

An experience-dependent change in the intersensory timing relation judged simultaneous or used in binding. Exposure to a repeated audiovisual lag can shift later timing judgments. Transfer to a different task and recovery tests help distinguish calibration from a task-specific response strategy.
