<h1 id="so-4-vector-higgs-gauge-boson-spectrum">SO(4) vector-Higgs gauge-boson spectrum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

A real four-vector vacuum $\Phi_0=ve_4$ leaves the rotations of its perpendicular three-space unbroken. In a Hermitian generator basis split into two commuting SU(2) triples with $(T^a+T^{a+3})e_4=0$, define $A^{a,\pm}=(A^a\pm A^{a+3})/\sqrt2$. For scalar kinetic term $|D\Phi|^2$ and canonical vector kinetic terms, its vacuum value is $g^2v^2\sum_a(A^{a,-})^2/2$. Comparison with $m^2A^2/2$ gives three massless and three mass-$|gv|$ [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md).

The factor multiplying the scalar kinetic term matters: replacing it by $|D\Phi|^2/2$ at fixed numerical $v$ changes the massive value to $|gv|/\sqrt2$. The unbroken global subgroup is SO(3), rather than its simply connected SU(2) cover. The [Spin(4) double cover](spin-4-double-cover.md) accounts for that central identification.
