# Associated subgroup of an HNN extension

↑ **Parent:** [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md)

The associated subgroups are the source and target $A,B\le G$ of the [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) used to define an [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md). They determine its [pinches in an HNN extension](pinch-in-an-hnn-extension.md), its coset transversals, and the possible cancellations of adjacent inverse [stable letters](stable-letter.md).
