# Orientation reversal of surface curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md)

Replacing the chosen [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $N$ by $-N$ replaces the [shape operator](shape-operator.md) $S$ by $-S$. On a surface, [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) $K=\det S$ is unchanged, while [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) $H=\operatorname{tr}(S)/2$ changes sign.
