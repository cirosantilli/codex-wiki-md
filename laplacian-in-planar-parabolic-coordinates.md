# Laplacian in planar parabolic coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic polar coordinates](parabolic-polar-coordinates.md)

For [planar parabolic coordinates](parabolic-polar-coordinates.md) and $g(u,v)=f(x(u,v),y(u,v))$, the [multivariable chain rule](multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives $g_u=uf_x+vf_y$ and $g_v=-vf_x+uf_y$. Differentiating once more and adding cancels the first [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) and the [mixed partial derivatives](mixed-partial-derivative.md):

$$
g_{uu}+g_{vv}=(u^2+v^2)(f_{xx}+f_{yy}).
$$

Consequently, on every local coordinate patch away from the origin, $f$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) if and only if $g$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) changes by a positive scale factor, so solutions of the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) are preserved. The restriction away from the origin is needed to divide by the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md).
