# Mean free path through randomly oriented disc absorbers

↑ **Parent:** [Mean free path](mean-free-path.md)

For an unclustered population of fully covering circular disc absorbers, the inverse [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) is proper number density times mean projected area. A comoving number density must first be multiplied by $(1+z)^3$. The incidence then constrains the proper radius; covering factor and multiplicity of detectable lines can change the inference.
