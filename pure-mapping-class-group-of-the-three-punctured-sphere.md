# Pure mapping class group of the three-punctured sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Pure mapping class group](pure-mapping-class-group.md)

The pure mapping class group of the three-punctured sphere is trivial. The three simple arcs joining distinct puncture pairs form an ideal triangulation; a pure homeomorphism can be isotoped to fix these arcs, and the [Alexander trick](alexander-trick.md) on the two complementary discs finishes the isotopy to the identity.
