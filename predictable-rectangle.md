# Predictable rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md)

A predictable rectangle has the displayed form for $0\leq s<t$. Its indicator is a left-continuous [adapted process](adapted-process.md), and these rectangles generate the [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md) on positive times. If the time-zero slice is included, also use $B\times\{0\}$ with $B\in\mathcal F_0$.
