# Tietze transformations

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tietze_transformations)

A Tietze transformation changes a [group presentation](group-presentation.md) without changing the presented [group](group-split.md), by adding or removing a consequence of the relators, or by adding or eliminating a generator together with a defining equation expressing it in the others. For example a generator $t$ defined by $t=xsx^{-1}$ can be eliminated by substitution in every remaining relator.
