# Subspace quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Quiver](quiver.md)

This [quiver](quiver.md) has $r$ source vertices, each with one arrow to a common sink. Its [path algebra](path-algebra.md) has the vertex [idempotents](idempotent.md) and the arrows as basis. After extracting source kernels from a representation, its remaining arrows identify the source spaces with subspaces of the sink.
