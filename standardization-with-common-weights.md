# Standardization with common weights

↑ **Parent:** [Outcome standardization](outcome-standardization.md)

Let $p_{gs}$ be a group-specific [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) in stratum $s$, and let $w_s\ge0$ with $\sum_s w_s=1$. Average each group's [conditional probabilities](conditional-probability.md) using the same weights. Then

$$
p_1^{(w)}-p_0^{(w)}=\sum_s w_s(p_{1s}-p_{0s}).
$$

This is a descriptive comparison under a common covariate composition. Its difference from a crude contrast need not be a [causal effect](causal-effect.md). Causal [outcome standardization](outcome-standardization.md) additionally requires its identification assumptions.
