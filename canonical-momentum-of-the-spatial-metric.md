# Canonical momentum of the spatial metric

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity](hamiltonian-formulation-of-general-relativity.md)

With the convention $K_{ij}=(\dot h_{ij}-D_iN_j-D_jN_i)/(2N)$ for [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md), differentiation of the gravitational Lagrangian gives $\pi^{ij}=\sqrt h(K^{ij}-Kh^{ij})$ in units $16\pi G=1$. It is a weight-one [tensor density](tensor-density.md). In three spatial dimensions, $\pi=-2\sqrt hK$, so

$$
K^{ij}=h^{-1/2}(\pi^{ij}-\tfrac12\pi h^{ij}).
$$

The [lapse function](lapse-function.md) and [shift vector](shift-vector.md) have no time derivatives in this action and consequently have vanishing conjugate momenta. Changing the sign convention for extrinsic curvature changes the corresponding momentum relation; it must not be silently combined with this formula.
