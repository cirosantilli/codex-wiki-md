# Domain-wall scaling coordinate of an Ising chain

↑ **Parent:** [Exact zero-field Ising-chain decimation](exact-zero-field-ising-chain-decimation.md)

A reversed bond costs $2J$, so its [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md) $w=e^{-2J/(k_BT)}$ is a natural thermal coordinate near the zero-temperature chain fixed point. Exact length-two decimation gives $w'\simeq2w$, and the [One-dimensional Ising correlation function](one-dimensional-ising-correlation-function.md) gives $\xi/a=-1/\log\tanh K\sim1/(2w)$. This is a [length](length.md) exponent one with respect to $w$. The [correlation length](correlation-length.md) instead diverges exponentially in inverse physical [temperature](temperature-split.md); claiming a conventional finite power $T^{-1}$ would confuse scaling coordinates. Choosing $e^{-K}$ gives multiplier $\sqrt2$ and power two in that alternative coordinate, the same physical [length](length.md).
