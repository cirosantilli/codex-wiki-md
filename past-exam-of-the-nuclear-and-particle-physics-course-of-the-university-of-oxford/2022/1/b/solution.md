<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) is a [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md), with [intrinsic parity](../../../../intrinsic-parity.md) $-1$. At rest, $P|\pm\rangle=-|\pm\rangle$. Combining this with the specified [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) gives

$$
\boxed{C|+\rangle=-|+\rangle,\qquad C|-\rangle=+|-\rangle.}
$$

Equivalently, in the [exchange-positive CP convention for neutral kaon mixing](../../../../exchange-positive-cp-convention-for-neutral-kaon-mixing.md) fixed by the question, $C|K^0\rangle=-|\bar K^0\rangle$ and $C|\bar K^0\rangle=-|K^0\rangle$. This sign is a convention for the flavor states; the specified [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) and physical predictions must be kept consistent with it.

For two spin-zero [pions](../../../../pion.md), conservation of total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) fixes the relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) to zero. The resulting $\pi^+\pi^-$ or $\pi^0\pi^0$ state has [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) $+1$. The lowest [partial waves](../../../../partial-wave.md) of $\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ and $3\pi^0$ have [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) $-1$. Thus the dominant nonleptonic assignments in the [CP symmetry](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) approximation are

$$
\boxed{|K_S\rangle\simeq|+\rangle\to2\pi,\qquad
|K_L\rangle\simeq|-\rangle\to3\pi.}
$$

The two-[pion](../../../../pion.md) mode has substantially more available [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and larger [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md); the three-[pion](../../../../pion.md) mode has an additional final particle and lies closer to its [decay threshold](../../../../decay-threshold.md). This helps explain why the [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md)-odd state has the longer [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md). The actual [mean lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) also depend on the [decay amplitudes](../../../../decay-amplitude.md) and on semileptonic channels, so counting final particles alone is not a quantitative prediction.

The middle panel of the figure in part (a) shows $\bar s\to\bar uW^+$, $W^+\to u\bar d$, with the $d$ as a [spectator quark](../../../../spectator-quark.md). The outgoing $d\bar u$ and $u\bar d$ form $\pi^-$ and $\pi^+$. In the bottom panel, an additional [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) produces $d\bar d$, supplying the neutral [pion](../../../../pion.md) component for $\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$. The charge-conjugate diagrams supply the other component of each of the [kaon CP eigenstates](../../../../kaon-cp-eigenstates.md). The drawn [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) splitting represents nonperturbative [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md), rather than a quantitatively reliable perturbative prediction at the [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) scale.

These multiplicities describe the dominant low-[orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) modes, not an absolute rule for every three-[pion](../../../../pion.md) configuration. In $\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$, odd [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) values of the charged pair and of its motion relative to $\pi^0$ can couple to total zero and give [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) $+1$, but are strongly suppressed near [decay threshold](../../../../decay-threshold.md). Physical $K_L\to2\pi$ also occurs through [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md).
