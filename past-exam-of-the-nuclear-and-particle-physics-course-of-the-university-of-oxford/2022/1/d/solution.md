<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a narrow unstable state, the effective [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) has the complex [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $m_j-i\Gamma_j/2$. With [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md) and $\Gamma_j=1/\tau_j$, its [Schrödinger equation](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives

$$
A_j(t)=A_j(0)e^{-im_jt}e^{-t/(2\tau_j)},\qquad j=S,L.
$$

The first factor is the ordinary [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) phase; squaring the second gives the [exponential decay](../../../../exponential-decay.md) of the survival [probability](../../../../probability.md), $e^{-t/\tau_j}$. This assumes the approximately constant [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md) regime; exact very early and very late behavior need not be exponential.

Writing the surviving state as $A_S(t)|K_S\rangle+A_L(t)|K_L\rangle$, invert the two [kaon CP eigenstates](../../../../kaon-cp-eigenstates.md) definitions:

$$
\boxed{A_{K^0}(t)=\frac{A_S(t)+A_L(t)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
A_{\bar K^0}(t)=\frac{A_S(t)-A_L(t)}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Here $t$ is the [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md). For a relativistic beam of fixed [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma$, substitute $t=t_{\mathrm{lab}}/\gamma$; in ordinary units the [mass](../../../../mass.md) phase is $m_jc^2t/\hbar$.
