<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Measured half-integer [spin](../../../../spin.md) and fermionic behavior rule out a simple [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) pair: two spin-$1/2$ constituents have integer total [spin](../../../../spin.md), and adding integer [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) does not change that. Their measured [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md) $B=1$, inferred through production and conservation bookkeeping, corresponds to $n_q-n_{\bar q}=3$. Their pattern of [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) and [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) then agrees with three light [valence quarks](../../../../valence-quark.md).

[Baryon magnetic moments](../../../../baryon-magnetic-moment.md) provide a more detailed check. The additive [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) gives, for example,

$$
\mu_p=\frac43\mu_u-\frac13\mu_d,\qquad
\mu_n=\frac43\mu_d-\frac13\mu_u.
$$

Approximately equal up and down constituent [masses](../../../../mass.md) give $\mu_u\simeq-2\mu_d$, hence $\mu_p/\mu_n\simeq-3/2$, close to the measured ratio of about $-1.46$.

These observations support three **valence** [quarks](../../../../quark.md); they do not prove the absence of extra [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) pairs. For instance, $qqqq\bar q$ also has $B=1$ and can have half-integer [spin](../../../../spin.md). Ordinary [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) [baryons](../../../../baryon.md) also contain sea pairs and [gluons](../../../../gluon.md). The stronger interpretation that the measurements exclude every [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) component is therefore false.
