<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Both central [baryons](../../../../baryon.md) have [valence quarks](../../../../valence-quark.md) $uds$, [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md) $1$, [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) $-1$ and [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) projection zero. Their total [isospins](../../../../isospin.md) differ:

$$
\boxed{\Sigma^0:\ I=1,\ I_z=0;\qquad
\Lambda^0:\ I=0,\ I_z=0.}
$$

The $ud$ flavor part is symmetric for the [Sigma baryon](../../../../sigma-baryon.md) and antisymmetric for the [Lambda baryon](../../../../lambda-baryon.md). In the usual [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) coupling, the associated $ud$ pair has [spin](../../../../spin.md) one for $\Sigma^0$ and [spin](../../../../spin.md) zero for $\Lambda^0$, with the strange [quark](../../../../quark.md) giving total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $1/2$ in both cases. Distinct total [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) makes these orthogonal states despite their coincident position in the [weight diagram](../../../../weight-diagram.md).
