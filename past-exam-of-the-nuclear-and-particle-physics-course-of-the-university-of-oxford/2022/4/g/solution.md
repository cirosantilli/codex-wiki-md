<h1 id="4/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

A [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) spin-zero [meson](../../../../meson.md) is a [color singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md) $q\bar q$ with $L=0$ and constituent [spin](../../../../spin.md) zero, hence a [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md). The nine flavor combinations form $\mathbf3\otimes\bar{\mathbf3}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$. Here $B=0$, so the vertical coordinate $B+S$ equals [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md). The six peripheral weights in the right panel of the figure in part (a) are

| [Meson](../../../../meson.md) | [Valence quarks](../../../../valence-quark.md) | $I_z$ | $B+S$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Pion](../../../../pion.md) $\pi^+$ | $u\bar d$ | $1$ | $0$ |
| [Pion](../../../../pion.md) $\pi^-$ | $d\bar u$ | $-1$ | $0$ |
| [Kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^+$ | $u\bar s$ | $1/2$ | $1$ |
| [Kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^0$ | $d\bar s$ | $-1/2$ | $1$ |
| [Kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^-$ | $s\bar u$ | $-1/2$ | $-1$ |
| [Neutral antikaon](../../../../neutral-antikaon.md) $\bar K^0$ | $s\bar d$ | $1/2$ | $-1$ |

At the center, $u\bar u,d\bar d,s\bar s$ all have $I_z=S=0$. A useful orthonormal flavor basis is

$$
\boxed{|\pi^0\rangle=\frac{|u\bar u\rangle-|d\bar d\rangle}{\sqrt2},\qquad
|\eta_8\rangle=\frac{|u\bar u\rangle+|d\bar d\rangle-2|s\bar s\rangle}{\sqrt6},\qquad
|\eta_1\rangle=\frac{|u\bar u\rangle+|d\bar d\rangle+|s\bar s\rangle}{\sqrt3}.}
$$

The [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md) has $I=1$; $\eta_8$ and $\eta_1$ have $I=0$. The [Eta octet state](../../../../eta-octet-state.md) belongs to the octet, while $\eta_1$ is the flavor singlet. The physical [eta and eta prime mesons](../../../../eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md) are predominantly mixtures of these two isoscalar basis states,

$$
|\eta\rangle=\cos\theta\,|\eta_8\rangle-\sin\theta\,|\eta_1\rangle,\qquad
|\eta'\rangle=\sin\theta\,|\eta_8\rangle+\cos\theta\,|\eta_1\rangle.
$$

Unequal [quark masses](../../../../quark-mass.md) and the [chiral anomaly](../../../../chiral-anomaly.md) prevent exact flavor symmetry and a degenerate nonet. The three central physical species are consequently $\pi^0,\eta,\eta'$, rather than three separately observable pure diagonal flavor pairs. Small isospin-breaking admixtures are neglected here.
