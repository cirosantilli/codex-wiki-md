<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Write the charge in units of $e$ as $Q=aI_z+b(B+S)$. The [proton](../../../../proton.md) and [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) have $(I_z,B+S)=(1/2,1)$ and $(-1/2,1)$, so their measured charges imply $1=a/2+b$ and $0=-a/2+b$. Thus $a=1$, $b=1/2$:

$$
\boxed{Q=I_z+\frac{B+S}{2}.}
$$

This is the [Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula](../../../../gell-mann-nishijima-formula.md), with the full [flavor hypercharge](../../../../flavor-hypercharge.md) $Y=B+S$. Applying the [quark](../../../../quark.md) [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md) $1/3$ and the [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) and [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) assignments from part (a),

$$
\boxed{Q_u=\frac12+\frac16=\frac23,\quad
Q_d=-\frac12+\frac16=-\frac13,\quad
Q_s=0+\frac{1/3-1}{2}=-\frac13.}
$$

Physical [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) are these numbers multiplied by the positive elementary charge $e$.
