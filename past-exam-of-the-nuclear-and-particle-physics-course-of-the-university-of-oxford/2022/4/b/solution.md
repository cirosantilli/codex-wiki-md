<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The diagram refers to [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md), $L=0$, [color singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md) [baryons](../../../../baryon.md). Their spatial [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) is symmetric and their color [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) is antisymmetric. The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) therefore requires a symmetric combined spin-and-flavor [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md). One cannot independently attach every three-spin coupling to each flavor assignment.

For three spin-$1/2$ particles, there are formally two ways to couple to total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $1/2$. In the [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) these are tied to the corresponding mixed-symmetry flavor functions. Their required symmetric combination yields one physical spin-$1/2$ flavor octet, not two independent species at each peripheral weight. In [spin-flavor symmetry](../../../../spin-flavour-symmetry.md) notation,

$$
\mathbf{56}_{SU(6)}=(\mathbf{10},\mathbf4)\oplus(\mathbf8,\mathbf2)
$$

under flavor $SU(3)$ and spin $SU(2)$. Thus there is one peripheral species in the [baryon octet](../../../../baryon-octet.md), each still possessing its two ordinary spin projections. The two abstract flavor-octet copies in $\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3$ do not double the physical [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) octet.

At the dotted positions, the flavor content would be $uuu$, $ddd$ or $sss$. These flavor [wavefunctions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) are completely symmetric. The spin [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) must then also be completely symmetric, which means total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $3/2$, not $1/2$. The corresponding [baryon decuplet](../../../../baryon-decuplet.md) states are $\Delta^{++}$, $\Delta^-$ and $\Omega^-$. This exclusion is for the stated [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) spin-$1/2$ multiplet; it is not an exclusion of all excited [baryons](../../../../baryon.md) at those weights.
