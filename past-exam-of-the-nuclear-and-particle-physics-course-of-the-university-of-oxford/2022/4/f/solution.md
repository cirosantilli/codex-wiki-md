<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The observed dominant two-body transition is $\boxed{\Sigma^0\to\Lambda^0\gamma}$. Both [baryons](../../../../baryon.md) have [valence quarks](../../../../valence-quark.md) $uds$ and the same [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md); an [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) transition is therefore possible. Their [mass](../../../../mass.md) difference is only about $77\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$, below the lightest [pion](../../../../pion.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md), so no additional on-shell strongly interacting particle fits. A [photon](../../../../photon.md) is neutral and massless, and can carry the required [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) through a [magnetic dipole transition](../../../../magnetic-dipole-transition.md) between the two positive-[parity](../../../../parity.md) spin-$1/2$ states. A single [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) would violate [lepton number](../../../../lepton-number.md) and would not give the required fermion parity.

At rest, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $p_\Lambda=p_\Sigma-k$ and $k^2=0$. Thus

$$
M_\Lambda^2c^4=M_\Sigma^2c^4-2M_\Sigma c^2E_\gamma,
\qquad
\boxed{E_\gamma=\frac{M_\Sigma^2-M_\Lambda^2}{2M_\Sigma}c^2.}
$$

The result is slightly below $(M_\Sigma-M_\Lambda)c^2$ because the [Lambda baryon](../../../../lambda-baryon.md) recoils; rounded [masses](../../../../mass.md) give about $74.5\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

The channels $n\pi^0$ and $p\pi^-$ are actually kinematically open: their total [rest energies](../../../../rest-energy.md) are about $1075$ and $1078\,\mathrm{MeV}$, below $M_\Sigma c^2\simeq1193\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Their suppression is therefore **not** a [decay threshold](../../../../decay-threshold.md) prohibition. Each changes [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) from $-1$ to zero and so requires a [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md). The much faster allowed electromagnetic transition makes such weak [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) extremely small. “Not observed” should be understood as overwhelming electromagnetic competition, not an exact ban in the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md). The photon identification likewise uses the known low-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spectrum and dominant transition mechanism, not neutrality alone.
