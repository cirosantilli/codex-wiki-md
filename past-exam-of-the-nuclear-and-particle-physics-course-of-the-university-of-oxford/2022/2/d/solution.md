<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) organize the perturbative calculation of the [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) in [Fermi's golden rule](../../../../fermi-s-golden-rule.md). Each external line labels an incoming or outgoing particle state; each internal line contributes a [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) for a generally off-shell intermediate field; and each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) contributes a coupling and the appropriate [spin](../../../../spin.md), [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and internal-symmetry structure. All indistinguishable allowed diagrams must be summed as [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) before taking the absolute square, so their [interference](../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) contributes to the rate.

In the [radiative pion decay](../../../../radiative-pion-decay.md) diagram in part (b), solid arrowed lines are [fermions](../../../../fermion.md); the wavy line joining the hadronic and leptonic vertices is a [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) [propagator](../../../../propagator.md); and the wavy outgoing line is an external [photon](../../../../photon.md). Arrows track fermion number, so an outgoing [antimuon](../../../../antimuon.md) has an arrow towards its production vertex. Each vertex conserves [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md); an internal [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) need not satisfy the on-shell [energy–momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md). A closed [loop Feynman diagram](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) additionally requires an integration over an internal [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md).

The final [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md), conservation [Dirac delta functions](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md), and the initial-state normalization or incident [flux](../../../../flux.md) turn the squared [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) into a [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md) or [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md). A diagram supplies the dynamics; it is not itself the final [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md).
