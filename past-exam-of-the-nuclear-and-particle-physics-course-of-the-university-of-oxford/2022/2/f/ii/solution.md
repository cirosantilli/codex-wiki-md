<h1 id="2/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Only $t$-channel [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) exchange contributes at leading order. A [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) would connect $\nu_\mu$ to $\mu^-$, rather than to $e^-$, and therefore does not supply this elastic channel. With the assumptions and normalization of part (i),

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\mathrm{ii}}=\sigma_*\left[(x-\tfrac12)^2+\frac{x^2}{3}\right].}
$$

For the illustrative value $x=0.231$, the dimensionless factors in parts (i) and (ii) are $0.5521$ and $0.09015$. The electron-flavor reaction is therefore about $6.1$ times as large in this limit. This uses the [weak flavour selection in neutrino-electron scattering](../../../../../weak-flavour-selection-in-neutrino-electron-scattering.md), not a hypothetical flavor-changing neutral vertex.
