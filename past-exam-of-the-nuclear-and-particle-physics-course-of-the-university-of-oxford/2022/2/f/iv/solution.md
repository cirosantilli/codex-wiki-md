<h1 id="2/f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

This is the charge-and-parity-conjugate reaction of part (i). There are $t$-channel [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) and crossed [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) contributions, with all fermion arrows reversed relative to part (i). For matched unpolarized [kinematics](../../../../../kinematics.md) and negligible [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md),

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\mathrm{iv}}=\sigma_{\mathrm{i}}.}
$$

Combining all four results in the regime specified in part (i),

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\mathrm{i}}:\sigma_{\mathrm{ii}}:\sigma_{\mathrm{iii}}:\sigma_{\mathrm{iv}}
\simeq6.12:1:2.57:6.12.}
$$

In particular, conjugating both initial particles restores the part-(i) rate; conjugating the [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) alone does not. Initial [spin polarization](../../../../../spin-polarization.md) and energies near a weak-boson [mass](../../../../../mass.md) require a separate comparison.
