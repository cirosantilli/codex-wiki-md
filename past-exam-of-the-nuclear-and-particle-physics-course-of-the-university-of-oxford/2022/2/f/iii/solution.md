<h1 id="2/f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Both $t$-channel [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) exchange and $s$-channel annihilation $e^-\bar\nu_e\to W^{-*}\to e^-\bar\nu_e$ occur. Replacing the incident [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) by an [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) interchanges the two chiral angular weights. In the same contact-interaction regime,

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\mathrm{iii}}=\sigma_*\left[x^2+\frac{(x+\tfrac12)^2}{3}\right].}
$$

At $x=0.231$ the factor is $0.2315$, between parts (i) and (ii). The comparison is energy dependent outside this regime: near $s=m_W^2$, the annihilation channel has a [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) resonance, and the low-[energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) formula is inapplicable.
