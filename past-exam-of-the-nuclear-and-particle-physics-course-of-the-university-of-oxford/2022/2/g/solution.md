<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

**The decay is allowed in the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md).** Begin with ordinary [muon decay](../../../../muon-decay.md), $\mu^-\to\nu_\mu W^{-*}$, $W^{-*}\to e^-\bar\nu_e$. The outgoing [electron](../../../../electron.md) can emit a virtual [photon](../../../../photon.md) that converts to $e^-e^+$. The bottom panel of the figure in part (b) shows one representative diagram. Radiation from other charged lines and exchange of the identical final [electrons](../../../../electron.md) complete the [decay amplitude](../../../../decay-amplitude.md).

The final total [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is $-1$. Its electron-family [lepton number](../../../../lepton-number.md) is $1+1-1-1=0$, and its muon-family [lepton number](../../../../lepton-number.md) is $1$, matching the initial [muon](../../../../muon.md). The extra pair therefore violates no relevant [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) selection rule. It is also kinematically allowed since $m_\mu>3m_e$. The virtual pair costs two electromagnetic vertices: the [decay amplitude](../../../../decay-amplitude.md) is order $G_Fe^2$, and the [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md) is suppressed by roughly two powers of the [fine-structure constant](../../../../fine-structure-constant.md), with additional [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) and logarithmic factors.
