<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a three-body leptonic decay through the [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md), neglecting daughter [masses](../../../../mass.md) and [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md), [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) gives

$$
\Gamma(\ell^-\to\ell^{\prime-}\bar\nu_{\ell'}\nu_\ell)
=\frac{G_F^2m_\ell^5}{192\pi^3}
$$

in [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md). Therefore scaling the [muon decay](../../../../muon-decay.md) rate by $m_\tau^5/m_\mu^5$ predicts only one leptonic channel, not the total [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md).

The [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) has two allowed charged-lepton channels, $e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\tau$ and $\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu\nu_\tau$, as well as hadronic channels from $W^{-*}\to d\bar u,s\bar u$. Each allowed [quark](../../../../quark.md) channel has three [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) choices and its [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) factor. Charm production is kinematically inaccessible in a physical hadronic final state at this [mass](../../../../mass.md). Treating the inclusive hadronic channels by a simple [quark-hadron duality](../../../../quark-hadron-duality.md) estimate,

$$
N_{\mathrm{eff}}\simeq2+3(|V_{ud}|^2+|V_{us}|^2)\simeq5.
$$

Thus, using $m_\mu\simeq105.7\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$ and $m_\tau\simeq1777\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$,

$$
\boxed{\tau_\tau\simeq\frac{2.2\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm s}{5}
\left(\frac{105.7}{1777}\right)^5\simeq3.3\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

Finite daughter [masses](../../../../mass.md), hadronic [decay thresholds](../../../../decay-threshold.md) and resonances, [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md), and electroweak [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md) refine this estimate. The threefold [color multiplicity in a decay width](../../../../color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md) and extra decay channels are the main correction to naive fifth-power scaling.
