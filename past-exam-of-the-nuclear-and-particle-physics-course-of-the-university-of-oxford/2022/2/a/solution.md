<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [Fermi's golden rule](../../../../fermi-s-golden-rule.md),

$$
\Gamma=\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}|M|^2\rho,
$$

$\Gamma$ is a transition [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md), measured in inverse time; $\hbar$ is the [reduced Planck constant](../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md); $M=\langle f|H_{\mathrm{int}}|i\rangle$ is the transition [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) of the interaction part of the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md); and $\rho$ is the final [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) per unit [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), evaluated subject to [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md). Final [spin](../../../../spin.md) and other discrete labels must be summed, with initial labels averaged when the preparation requires it. If $M$ varies across the final [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md), $|M|^2\rho$ denotes the corresponding integral rather than a product of constants.

A convention warning: many [quantum field theory](../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md) texts use $\Gamma$ for an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md). That convention is $\Gamma_{\mathrm{width}}=\hbar\Gamma_{\mathrm{rate}}$; the formula as printed uses the rate convention.
