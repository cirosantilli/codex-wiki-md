<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $M_P$ and $M_D$ denote neutral atomic [rest masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md) for the appropriate parent and daughter, and initially neglect electronic [binding energy](../../../../binding-energy.md) corrections and daughter excitation. The [Q values](../../../../q-value.md) are

$$
\boxed{Q_{\beta^-}=(M_P-M_D)c^2,\qquad
Q_{\beta^+}=(M_P-M_D-2m_e)c^2,\qquad
Q_{\mathrm{EC}}=(M_P-M_D)c^2.}
$$

For [beta-minus decay](../../../../beta-minus-decay.md), the daughter [nucleus](../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) has one extra positive charge. Its neutral atom contains one more [electron](../../../../electron.md) than the neutral parent, cancelling the emitted [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) in the atomic accounting. For [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md), the neutral parent contains one more [electron](../../../../electron.md) than the neutral daughter; together with the emitted [Positron](../../../../positron.md), this gives the $2m_e$ subtraction. Thus [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md) requires an atomic [mass](../../../../mass.md) difference exceeding $2m_ec^2$, whereas [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) need not.

For [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md), the captured bound [electron](../../../../electron.md) is already included in $M_P$. Capture from a shell leaving an atomic hole of excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_{\mathrm{hole}}$, and a nuclear excited state of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_x$, gives approximately

$$
E_\nu=Q_{\mathrm{EC}}-E_x-E_{\mathrm{hole}}-T_{\mathrm{recoil}}.
$$

The hole's [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) later appears as atomic radiation or [Auger electrons](../../../../auger-electron.md). Subtract $E_x$ for either [beta decay](../../../../beta-decay.md) as well. Exact endpoints also depend on daughter ionization and small atomic [binding energy](../../../../binding-energy.md) changes; the boxed expressions are the conventional leading atomic-[mass](../../../../mass.md) formulas.
