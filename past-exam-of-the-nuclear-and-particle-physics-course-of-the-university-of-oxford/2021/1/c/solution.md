<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The ideal two-shower event fraction is $B_e^2$, approximately $4\%$ in simple channel counting or $3.18\%$ using the measured [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) electronic [branching fraction](../../../../branching-fraction.md).** The selected [particle decay](../../../../particle-decay.md) chain is

$$
\tau^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\tau,\qquad
\tau^+\to e^+\nu_e\bar\nu_\tau.
$$

The [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../positron.md) each make a contained [electromagnetic shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md); [muons](../../../../muon.md) penetrate, and generic [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) do not deposit all their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) as a contained [electromagnetic shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md).

To estimate $B_e$, use [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) and neglect final [masses](../../../../mass.md) and [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections. The electronic and muonic [decay widths](../../../../decay-width.md) each carry weight one. The accessible inclusive hadronic channels have weights $3|V_{ud}|^2$ and $3|V_{us}|^2$, where three is the [color multiplicity in a decay width](../../../../color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md). The [charm quark](../../../../charm-quark.md) is inaccessible in physical [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) hadronic decays. With $|V_{ud}|^2+|V_{us}|^2\simeq1$, the total weight is $1+1+3=5$, giving

$$
B_e\simeq\frac15,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{N_{\rm two\ showers}}{N_{\tau^+\tau^-}}=B_e^2\simeq\frac1{25}.}
$$

The 2021 measured value $B_e=0.1782$ instead gives $B_e^2=0.0317552$.[Tau listing, p. 6, mode 5: electronic branching fraction $(17.82\pm0.04)\%$](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2021.md) The difference reflects effects omitted by the simple free-channel estimate, especially the hadronic contribution to the total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md).

There are two [tau leptons](../../../../tau-particle.md) per produced pair and two per selected pair, so the fraction of all produced [tau leptons](../../../../tau-particle.md) belonging to these two-shower events is also $B_e^2$. The unrestricted fraction of individual [tau leptons](../../../../tau-particle.md) decaying electronically is $B_e$; these are different selections. A real high-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) shower cut gives an additional acceptance factor: $N_{\rm selected}/N_{\rm pairs}=B_e^2\varepsilon_{ee}$, which cannot be computed without the cut and detector response. No factor of two is needed for the unique $e^+e^-$ assignment.
