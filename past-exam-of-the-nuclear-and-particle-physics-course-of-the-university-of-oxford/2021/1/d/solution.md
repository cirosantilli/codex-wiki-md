<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**For a purely [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) continuum with five active [quark flavors](../../../../quark-flavor.md), the usual estimate is $N_{e^+e^-}/N_{\rm had}=3/11$.** The unspecified [particle resonance](../../../../particle-resonance.md) and the word “total” require qualifications before this can be treated as a unique physical prediction.

In [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md), neglecting final [masses](../../../../mass.md), single-[photon](../../../../photon.md) annihilation gives

$$
\sigma(\mu^+\mu^-\to e^+e^-)=\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{3s},\qquad
\sigma(\mu^+\mu^-\to q_f\bar q_f)=3Q_f^2\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{3s}.
$$

Here $\alpha$ is the [fine-structure constant](../../../../fine-structure-constant.md), $Q_f$ the [quark](../../../../quark.md) [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) in elementary-charge units, and the factor three counts orthogonal colour final states. Consequently the inverse [hadronic R ratio](../../../../hadronic-r-ratio.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_{e^+e^-}}{N_{\rm had}}=\frac1{3\sum_{f\ {\rm active}}Q_f^2}.}
$$

With $u,d,s,c,b$ accessible, the denominator is $3[2(2/3)^2+3(1/3)^2]=11/3$. The corresponding values for $u,d,s$ and $u,d,s,c$ are $1/2$ and $3/10$. Inclusive [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections increase the hadronic denominator, approximately by $1+\alpha_s/\pi$ away from thresholds and [particle resonances](../../../../particle-resonance.md). These are annihilation ratios before selection and [detection efficiency](../../../../detection-efficiency.md) corrections, not ratios of uncorrected detector counts.

A future [muon-collider Higgs resonance](../../../../muon-collider-higgs-resonance.md) is a plausible reading of part (c), since its narrow [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) makes a detuning of a few $\mathrm{MeV}$ relevant. Even then, two further assumptions are necessary. First, being a few $\mathrm{MeV}$ below the [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) peak does not eliminate it: for detuning $\Delta=\sqrt{s}-m_H$, the ideal narrow [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md) retains the relative factor

$$
\frac{\sigma_H(\Delta)}{\sigma_H(0)}\simeq
\frac{\Gamma_H^2}{4\Delta^2+\Gamma_H^2}.
$$

For $\Gamma_H=4\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $\Delta=-4\,\mathrm{MeV}$ this is $1/5$, not zero. Resonant hadronic production can still change the requested denominator substantially.

Second, at $\sqrt{s}\simeq125\,\mathrm{GeV}$ the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) continuum is not negligible. A more complete annihilation estimate including both [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) uses

$$
\sigma_f=\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{3s}N_c^fF_f(s),\qquad
F_f=Q_f^2-2Q_fv_\mu v_f\operatorname{Re}\chi
+(v_\mu^2+a_\mu^2)(v_f^2+a_f^2)|\chi|^2,
$$



$$
v_f=T_3^f-2Q_fs_W^2,\quad a_f=T_3^f,\quad
\chi=\frac{s}{4s_W^2(1-s_W^2)(s-m_Z^2+i m_Z\Gamma_Z)}.
$$

Here $s_W^2$ is the squared sine of the [weak mixing angle](../../../../weinberg-angle.md), $T_3^f$ is [weak isospin](../../../../weak-isospin.md), and $N_c^f$ is one for a [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) and three for a [quark](../../../../quark.md). Thus the continuum ratio is $F_e/[3(2F_u+3F_d)]$, rather than necessarily $3/11$. For example, $s_W^2=0.231$, $m_Z=91.1876\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $\Gamma_Z=2.4952\,\mathrm{GeV}$ give approximately $0.102$ at $125\,\mathrm{GeV}$ before [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections.

If the unspecified [particle resonance](../../../../particle-resonance.md) instead means the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md), detuning by a few $\mathrm{MeV}$ is tiny compared with its $2.5\,\mathrm{GeV}$ [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md). The resonant ratio remains approximately $\Gamma(Z\to e^+e^-)/\Gamma(Z\to\mathrm{hadrons})=1/20.767\simeq0.0482$.[Z listing, p. 9: hadronic-to-single-lepton partial-width ratio $20.767\pm0.025$](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2021.md) This is a concrete counterexample to treating any slight downward detuning as permission to discard the [particle resonance](../../../../particle-resonance.md). The source supplies neither its identity nor the detuning and selection needed for a unique all-process ratio. The boxed continuum formula is the charge-counting answer under its stated approximation; a literal total must also include remaining resonant and other accepted hadronic channels.
