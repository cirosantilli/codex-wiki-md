<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**All three interactions respect [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md); [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) is conserved by the [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) and the [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md), but can change in the [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md).** These statements concern charges and net numbers, rather than an unchanging inventory of particles.

A [quark](../../../../quark.md) transforms in the fundamental [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) of the [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md) $SU(3)_C$. The [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) can change an individual [quark](../../../../quark.md)'s colour by exchanging a [gluon](../../../../gluon.md), which itself carries [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md). For example, the colour flow $q_r\to q_b+g_{r\bar b}$ is allowed: demanding that the outgoing [quark](../../../../quark.md) remain red would be incorrect. At each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) the full colour flow is conserved. The colourless [photon](../../../../photon.md), [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md), and [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) instead couple to a [quark](../../../../quark.md) with an identity factor $\delta_{ab}$ in colour space. A [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) can therefore change $d_r$ to $u_r$, but cannot turn $d_r$ into $u_b$ without another coloured participant. External isolated [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) are [colour singlets](../../../../colour-singlet.md); [confinement](../../../../confinement.md) prevents direct observation of a free coloured state.

For each [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) $f$, define the [net quark flavor number](../../../../net-quark-flavor-number.md) $F_f=N_f-N_{\bar f}$. The [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) and [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) preserve every $F_f$: pair creation $g\to s\bar s$, for example, changes neither net [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) nor any other $F_f$. Their [quark](../../../../quark.md) couplings are diagonal in [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md). The [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) contains $\bar u_i\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma^5)V_{ij}d_j$, where $V$ is the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), and changes these net numbers. [Weak neutral currents](../../../../neutral-current.md) are diagonal in [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md); [flavor-changing neutral currents](../../../../flavor-changing-neutral-current.md) can occur through higher-order [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md).

The observed hierarchy of [mean lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) and [decay channels](../../../../decay-channel.md) supplies evidence: associated strange-particle production through the [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) preserves net [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md), whereas a [kaon](../../../../kaon.md)'s [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) decay to [pions](../../../../pion.md) changes it. The [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) process $\pi^0\to\gamma\gamma$ has zero net [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) before and after annihilation. [Neutrino oscillations](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) show that individual [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) family numbers are not exact conserved quantities. Within the usual effectively massless-[neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) approximation, [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) decays such as $\mu^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\mu$ preserve family numbers only when the emitted [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) are counted.

Evidence for three colours includes the [hadronic R ratio](../../../../hadronic-r-ratio.md) $R=3\sum_fQ_f^2$, the colour multiplicities in [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) [decay widths](../../../../decay-width.md), and [three-jet events](../../../../three-jet-event.md) revealing [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) radiation. These test [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) and its colour structure together; they are not direct measurements of a named red or blue charge on a free [quark](../../../../quark.md).
