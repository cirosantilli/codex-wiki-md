<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The labels X and Y cannot be uniquely assigned from the surviving text: their [decay vertices](../../../../decay-vertex.md) and daughter tracks were contained in the missing figure.** The available [conservation laws](../../../../conservation-law.md) nevertheless specify how the assignment would be obtained.

The initial $\pi^-p$ state has $(Q,B,S)=(0,1,0)$, with $S$ denoting [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md). At a primary [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) vertex all three numbers must be reproduced. A final [Lambda baryon](../../../../lambda-baryon.md) or [Sigma baryon](../../../../sigma-baryon.md) has $B=1$, $S=-1$ and therefore needs accompanying $S=+1$, for example a neutral [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^0=d\bar s$ or a positive [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^+=u\bar s$, with the remaining [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) balanced. At later [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) [decay vertices](../../../../decay-vertex.md), [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) need not be conserved; it would be wrong to enforce it there.

A displaced $p\pi^-$ pair with the appropriate [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) identifies $\Lambda^0\to p\pi^-$. A displaced $\pi^+\pi^-$ pair can identify $K_S^0\to\pi^+\pi^-$ after an [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) and kinematic check. For a candidate [radiative Sigma-to-Lambda transition](../../../../radiative-sigma-to-lambda-transition.md), the neutral parent satisfies

$$
p_\Sigma=p_\Lambda+p_\gamma,\qquad
p_\Lambda=p_p+p_{\pi^-},\qquad
p_\Sigma^2=m_\Sigma^2,\quad p_\gamma^2=0
$$

in [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md). Its [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in the parent rest frame is $(m_\Sigma^2-m_\Lambda^2)/(2m_\Sigma)$. With known charged [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md), each unobserved neutral [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is the difference between the incoming and visible outgoing [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) at its vertex; its inferred direction must agree with the dashed segment.

This information cannot establish which dashed segment was called X. For a concrete ambiguity, both

$$
\pi^-p\to K^0\Lambda^0,\qquad
\pi^-p\to K^0\Sigma^0\to K^0\Lambda^0\gamma
$$

obey the primary [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md), and [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) constraints and can yield a neutral [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) decay plus a displaced [Lambda baryon](../../../../lambda-baryon.md) decay. Their distinct neutral segments and the extra [photon](../../../../photon.md) would be resolved by the missing topology and kinematics. The supplied possible [Sigma baryon](../../../../sigma-baryon.md) decay alone is insufficient to label X and Y. A labelled answer is therefore a source gap, rather than a completed identification.
