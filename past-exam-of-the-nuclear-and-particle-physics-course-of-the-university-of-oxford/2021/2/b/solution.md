<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The [magnetic rigidity](../../../../magnetic-rigidity.md) gives the [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), the bending direction gives the sign of [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), and the calorimetric [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) then gives the [mass](../../../../mass.md).** For planar tracks perpendicular to a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) and circular motion give

$$
p=|q|Br,\qquad
p\,[\mathrm{GeV}/c]=0.2998\,|q/e|\,B\,[\mathrm T]r\,[\mathrm m].
$$

Trace each track away from its production or [decay vertex](../../../../decay-vertex.md). With the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) into the page, a positive track bends counterclockwise and a negative track bends clockwise. For example, a positive particle initially travelling left accelerates downwards. The known incoming negative [pion](../../../../pion.md) fixes the sign convention experimentally. A radius alone does not distinguish [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) signs.

For a calibrated total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E$, the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{m^2c^4=E^2-p^2c^2.}
$$

If the calorimeter instead supplies only [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $T$, solve $(T+mc^2)^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{mc^2=\frac{p^2c^2-T^2}{2T}.}
$$

Compare with candidate [pion](../../../../pion.md), [kaon](../../../../kaon.md), and [proton](../../../../proton.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md), together with the [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) sign and shower behavior. An [electron](../../../../electron.md) makes an [electromagnetic shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md); a [muon](../../../../muon.md) is penetrating; a [hadron](../../../../hadron.md) generally requires a hadronic calorimeter and its own calibration. If either [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) escapes or the response is uncalibrated, simply substituting a deposited signal for $E$ need not identify the particle correctly. The absent figure prevents attaching identities to individual drawn tracks, but the requested identification procedure does not require its geometry.
