<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**In ordinary perturbative [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) reactions all three interactions conserve [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md), and total [lepton number](../../../../lepton-number.md).** The last two are accidental conservation laws of these perturbative processes, not exact nonperturbative statements: [electroweak sphalerons](../../../../sphaleron.md) violate $B+L$ while preserving [baryon-minus-lepton number](../../../../baryon-minus-lepton-number.md). Total [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) is respected throughout.

The [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) and [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) also conserve each [net quark flavor number](../../../../net-quark-flavor-number.md), including [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md), [charm](../../../../charm-quantum-number.md) and bottom flavour. The [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) can change them through the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). [Isospin](../../../../isospin.md) is an approximate [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) symmetry in the small light-[quark](../../../../quark.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) difference limit; the different [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) of $u$ and $d$ break it in the [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md). Approximate [flavor symmetry](../../../../flavor-symmetry.md) is distinct from exact net-flavour-number conservation by those interactions.

[Parity conservation](../../../../parity-conservation.md) and [charge conjugation symmetry](../../../../charge-conjugation-symmetry.md) hold for the [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) and, assuming a negligible strong CP parameter, the [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md). The [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) violates both, and also violates [CP symmetry](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) in suitable processes. [CPT symmetry](../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) holds under the usual local [Lorentz invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [quantum field theory](../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md) assumptions. Individual [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) family numbers are conserved in the conventional massless-[neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) approximation but not in [neutrino oscillations](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md); an assertion of exact family conservation in all observed reactions would therefore be false.
