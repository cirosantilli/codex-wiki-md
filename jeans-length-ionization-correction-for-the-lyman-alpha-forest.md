# Jeans-length ionization correction for the Lyman-alpha forest

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha forest photoionization scaling](lyman-alpha-forest-photoionization-scaling.md)

Assume optically thin [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) and an absorber depth of order its local [Jeans length](jeans-length.md), $L\propto(f_gT/n_H)^{1/2}$, where $f_g$ is the local gas fraction. Then $N_{\rm HI}\simeq\alpha(T)n_H^2L/\Gamma\propto[\alpha(T)/\Gamma](f_gT)^{1/2}n_H^{3/2}$. Solving for $n_H$ and substituting in $N_H=n_HL$ gives the displayed correction. Thus the ionized mass integral weights the [neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution](neutral-hydrogen-column-density-distribution.md) by $N_{\rm HI}^{1/3}$ rather than $N_{\rm HI}$. This model does not apply without modification to optically thick systems or arbitrary unconfined gas.
