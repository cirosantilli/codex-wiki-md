# Open matter-dominated Friedmann solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

A negatively curved universe with [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and no dark energy expands forever. With $a_0=1$ and $0<\Omega_{m,0}<1$, its [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) is $a=\Omega_{m,0}(\cosh\eta-1)/[2(1-\Omega_{m,0})]$ and its [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md) is $t=\Omega_{m,0}(\sinh\eta-\eta)/[2H_0(1-\Omega_{m,0})^{3/2}]$. The parameter is [conformal time](conformal-time.md) scaled by $\sqrt{-K}$ and measured from the [Big Bang](big-bang.md).
