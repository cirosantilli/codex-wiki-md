# General Relativity by David Tong

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by David Tong](work-by-david-tong.md)

[https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/gr.pdf](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/gr.pdf)

The lecture notes by [David Tong](david-tong.md) derive [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md) in section 4.6.2 and classify [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) in sections 6.2.1–6.2.5. Section 6.2 distinguishes the central [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) from regular horizons, including the extremal limit.
