# Four color theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph](planar-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_color_theorem)

Every finite [planar graph](planar-graph.md) has [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) at most four. This stronger theorem is distinct from the elementary [five colour theorem](five-color-theorem.md); the latter's [Kempe chain](kempe-chain.md) proof does not prove this assertion.
