# Rough Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

For a metric vector bundle connection over a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the rough Laplacian is the formally nonnegative connection operator

$$
\nabla^*\nabla=-\sum_i\bigl(\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}-\nabla_{\nabla_{e_i}e_i}\bigr),
$$

where $e_i$ is a local orthonormal frame. The correction term makes the expression independent of the orthonormal frame. On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md), integration by parts gives $\langle\nabla^*\nabla s,s\rangle_{L^2}=\|\nabla s\|_{L^2}^2$.
