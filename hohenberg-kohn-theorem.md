# Hohenberg-Kohn theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

Ground-state density-potential uniqueness and a density variational principle for electrons with fixed interaction. The elementary uniqueness proof assumes nondegenerate [ground states](ground-state.md) and fixes the potential only up to a constant.
