# Protein phosphatase 1

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Protein phosphatase 1](protein-phosphatase-1.md) catalyses protein dephosphorylation in regulated complexes. Association with targeting partners matters: effects on one substrate do not imply indiscriminate dephosphorylation of all cellular proteins.
