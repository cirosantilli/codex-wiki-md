# Symplectic area

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic surface](symplectic-surface.md)

The symplectic area of a [compact](compact-space.md) [symplectic surface](symplectic-surface.md) $(\Sigma,\omega)$ is $\int_\Sigma\omega$, using the [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) determined by the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md).
