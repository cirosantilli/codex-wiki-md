# Proximality

↑ **Parent:** [Topological dynamics](topological-dynamics.md)

In a compact [metric space](metric-space.md) with a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $T$, points $x,y$ are [proximal](proximality.md) if

$$
\inf_{n\geq0}d(T^n x,T^n y)=0.
$$

This property is independent of the [compatible metric](compatible-metric.md). If $T$ is injective and $x\ne y$, arbitrarily small [proximal](proximality.md) distances must occur at arbitrarily large times, since each finite collection of distances is strictly positive. [Proximal](proximality.md) points need not be equal or have dense [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md).
