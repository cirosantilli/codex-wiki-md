<h1 id="buhlmann-straub-credibility-estimate">Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann–Straub model](buhlmann-straub-model.md)

For total exposure $W=\sum_jm_j$ and weighted average $\overline X_w=\sum_jm_jX_j/W$, the best affine [mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md) estimate of the conditional mean is $Z\overline X_w+(1-Z)m_0$, where $Z=Wa/(Wa+v)$. If the coefficients sum to $B$, their error is $a(1-B)^2+v\sum_jb_j^2/m_j$. [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) minimizes the latter term at $b_j=B m_j/W$, and a one-dimensional quadratic minimization then gives $B=Z$.
