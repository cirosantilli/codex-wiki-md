# Stable matching in a bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory.md)

Give each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) a strict ordering of its [neighbours of a vertex](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md), all preferred to being unmatched. A [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) is stable if no unused [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) has both endpoints preferring each other to their current partners, with an unmatched endpoint willing to accept any neighbour. The [Gale-Shapley algorithm](gale-shapley-algorithm.md) produces a stable matching even when the two parts have unequal sizes and some pairs are unacceptable.
