# Contact-line pinning

↑ **Parent:** [Contact line](contact-line.md)

A [contact line](contact-line.md) is pinned when its position remains fixed while the surrounding drop shape or [contact angle](contact-angle.md) changes. Surface heterogeneity or deposited material can sustain such a state over a range of angles. [Evaporation](evaporation.md) from a pinned drop then requires compensating liquid motion and can generate outward solute transport associated with the [coffee-ring effect](coffee-ring-effect.md). A model with a freely receding line and fixed [contact angle](contact-angle.md) has a different [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) and need not predict the same flow direction.
