# Polynomial invariant ring

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant theory](invariant-theory.md)

For a group acting linearly on $W$, act on the [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) by $(g\cdot f)(w)=f(g^{-1}w)$. The [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md) is the fixed subalgebra $\mathbb C[W]^G$. It inherits the degree grading. Even over the complex numbers it need not be a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md), as the [quadratic cone invariant ring](quadratic-cone-invariant-ring.md) demonstrates.
