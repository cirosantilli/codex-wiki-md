# Slow-roll normalization for exponential inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential-potential power-law inflation](exponential-potential-power-law-inflation.md)

For $V=V_0e^{-A\phi/m_{\rm pl}}$, neglecting acceleration of the field and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives $3H\dot\phi=AV/m_{\rm pl}$ and $H^2=8\pi V/(3m_{\rm pl}^2)$. Integration yields the displayed field with $a\propto(t+b)^{16\pi/A^2}$. The exact flat scalar-dominated solution has the same power but $C^2=A^4V_0/[2m_{\rm pl}^2(48\pi-A^2)]$. The two normalizations agree at leading slow-roll order. Inflation requires $A^2<16\pi$; the stronger slow-roll conditions require $A^2/(8\pi)\ll1$.
