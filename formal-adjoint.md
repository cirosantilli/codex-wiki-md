# Formal adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

The formal adjoint of a differential operator $L$ is characterized by moving $L$ between factors under an integral by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), while discarding boundary terms for compactly supported functions.
