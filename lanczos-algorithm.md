# Lanczos algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Arnoldi iteration](arnoldi-iteration.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$, the leading block $Q_k^TAQ_k$ of the [Arnoldi decomposition](arnoldi-decomposition.md) is both symmetric and an [upper Hessenberg matrix](upper-hessenberg-matrix.md), hence a [tridiagonal matrix](tridiagonal-matrix.md). The resulting three-term recurrence is the Lanczos algorithm. If $A$ is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md), its compression is too, since $y^TQ_k^TAQ_ky=(Q_ky)^TA(Q_ky)>0$ for nonzero $y$.
