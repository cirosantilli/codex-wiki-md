# Isentropic compressibility

↑ **Parent:** [Compressibility](compressibility.md)

Isentropic compressibility is the [compressibility](compressibility.md) at fixed [entropy](entropy-split.md), particle number and composition. The conventional name adiabatic compressibility refers to a reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md); an irreversible [adiabatic compression](adiabatic-compression.md) need not keep [entropy](entropy-split.md) fixed. For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), it is $\kappa_S=1/(\gamma P)$, where $\gamma=C_P/C_V$ is the [heat capacity ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md). It describes a steeper local pressure response than [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md) when $\gamma>1$.
