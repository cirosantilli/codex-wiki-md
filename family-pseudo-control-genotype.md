# Family pseudo-control genotype

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission disequilibrium test](transmission-disequilibrium-test.md)

Combine the untransmitted maternal and paternal alleles into a hypothetical control [genotype](genotype.md), paired with the child's transmitted genotype. Even when two heterozygous parents have a heterozygous child and parent-specific phase is ambiguous, the combined untransmitted genotype is unambiguously heterozygous. Such pseudo-controls are matched family controls, not independent random individuals; [Hardy-Weinberg principle](hardy-weinberg-principle.md) is therefore not guaranteed merely by this construction.
