# Area transformation of a nonlinear acoustic simple wave

↑ **Parent:** [Simple wave](simple-wave.md)

For positive smooth tube area $A$, the equation $q_Z-qq_\theta+q(\log A)'/2=0$ becomes $Q_\zeta-QQ_\theta=0$ under the displayed transformation. Indeed, $g'/g=-A'/(2A)$ cancels geometrical spreading and $\zeta'=g$ makes the remaining nonlinear and transport coefficients equal. Starting with $q(\theta,Z_0)=f(\theta)$ gives $Q=f(\xi)$ and $\theta=\xi-f(\xi)\zeta$; [characteristic crossing](characteristic-crossing.md) first occurs when $\zeta\sup f'=1$, if that supremum is positive.
