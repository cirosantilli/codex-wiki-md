# Waterspout

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterspout)

A rotating atmospheric column over water, with visible mist and sometimes surface spray. Fair-weather waterspouts and thunderstorm-associated tornadic waterspouts have distinct formation settings.[Two categories](what-is-a-waterspout-by-noaa.md)
