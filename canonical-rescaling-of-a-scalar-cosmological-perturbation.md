# Canonical rescaling of a scalar cosmological perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Field theory in cosmology](field-theory-in-cosmology.md)

Neglecting [metric perturbations](linearized-gravity.md), a free massive [scalar field](scalar-field.md) fluctuation obeys $\delta\chi''+2\mathcal H\delta\chi'-\nabla^2\delta\chi+a^2m^2\delta\chi=0$. Rescaling it by the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) removes the conformal-time friction and gives $v''-\nabla^2v+(a^2m^2-a''/a)v=0$. Growth of $v$ need not mean growth of the physical fluctuation $v/a$.
