# Promoter escape

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription (biology)](transcription-biology.md)

The transition from initiation at a [promoter](promoter-genetics.md) to productive elongation by [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md). Initial short [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) products may be released before escape; retention or loss of a [sigma factor](sigma-factor.md) is not by itself a universal marker of escape.
