# Session-by-session outcome monitoring

↑ **Parent:** [Routine outcome monitoring](routine-outcome-monitoring.md)

Obtaining outcome measures at successive attended sessions. It can supply a last available score when a planned final contact is missed, while differing endpoint timing and people who never enter treatment remain important limitations.[Behaviour Research and Therapy 47, 910–920; Doncaster and Newham prospective service cohorts, sessional monitoring and ten-month follow-up](improving-access-to-psychological-therapy-initial-evaluation-of-two-uk-demonstration-sites-by-david-m-clark-and-others.md)
