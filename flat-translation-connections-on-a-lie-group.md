# Flat translation connections on a Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Left and right translation on a Lie group](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md)

Declare a global left-invariant, respectively right-invariant, frame parallel to obtain two flat [affine connections](affine-connection.md) on a [Lie group](lie-group.md). For [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md), their derivatives are as displayed and their [torsion tensors](torsion-tensor.md) are $-[X,Y]$ and $+[X,Y]$. The tensors vanish on Abelian groups; two genuinely torsionful connections are not asserted there.
