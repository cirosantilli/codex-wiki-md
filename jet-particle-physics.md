# Jet (particle physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_(particle_physics))

A collimated collection of final hadrons associated with an energetic parton and its radiation and hadronization. A precise perturbative prediction requires an [infrared-safe observable](infrared-safe-observable.md) that specifies how particles are grouped into jets.
