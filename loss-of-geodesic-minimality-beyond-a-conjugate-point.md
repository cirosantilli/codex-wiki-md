# Loss of geodesic minimality beyond a conjugate point

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate-point criterion for the Riemannian index form](conjugate-point-criterion-for-the-riemannian-index-form.md)

Let a nonzero [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) $J$ vanish at times zero and $a$. On a longer [geodesic](geodesic.md) segment ending at $b>a$, extend it by zero to a continuous piecewise smooth field $Z$. Then $I(Z,Z)=0$. Choose an endpoint-vanishing field $P$ with $P(a)=-D_tJ(a)$; [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $I(Z,P)=-|D_tJ(a)|^2<0$. Thus $I(Z+\varepsilon P,Z+\varepsilon P)<0$ for small positive $\varepsilon$. Smooth approximation preserves negativity. The [second variation of geodesic energy](second-variation-of-geodesic-energy.md) supplies a shorter path, so a [minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md) cannot extend beyond its first [conjugate point](conjugate-point.md).
