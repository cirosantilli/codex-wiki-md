# Phantom map of spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Map of topological spectra](map-of-topological-spectra.md)

A [phantom map of spectra](phantom-map-of-spectra.md) is a stable map $p:D\to E$ with $pu=0$ for every map $u:F\to D$ from a [finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md). Such a map can be nonzero even though it induces zero on all [represented homology theories](represented-homology-theory.md) on [based spaces](based-space.md).
