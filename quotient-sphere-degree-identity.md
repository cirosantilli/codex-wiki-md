# Quotient-sphere degree identity

↑ **Parent:** [Relative mapping degree](relative-mapping-degree.md)

Collapsing the boundary identifies the top [relative homology](relative-homology.md) of a disk pair with the top [reduced homology](reduced-homology.md) of its quotient [sphere](sphere.md). Together with the boundary [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md), this shows that the induced quotient map and the boundary map have the same [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md), using the corresponding orientations.
