# Hermitian part of a matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md)

The Hermitian part of a square [matrix](matrix.md) is half its sum with its [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md). It is a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) and satisfies $\operatorname{Re}(x^*Ax)=x^*Hx$. Its largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is the [numerical abscissa](euclidean-logarithmic-norm.md), controlling instantaneous growth of the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) under $x'=Ax$.
