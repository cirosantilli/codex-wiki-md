# Atkinson efficiency advantage from extended expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Atkinson cycle](atkinson-cycle.md)

For the same initial state, compression ratio and constant-volume [heat](heat-split.md) input, the ideal [Atkinson cycle](atkinson-cycle.md) adds net [work](work.md) during the extended expansion and subsequent constant-pressure return. The extra [work](work.md) is the [integral](integral-split.md) of $p_{\rm expansion}-p_1$ over the added volume, which is positive. Equivalently, [concavity](concave-function.md) of $x^{1/\gamma}$ gives $\gamma(r_p^{1/\gamma}-1)<r_p-1$ when $r_p>1$ and $\gamma>1$, so its efficiency exceeds the corresponding [Otto cycle](otto-cycle.md) efficiency. The comparison fixes these shared conditions rather than asserting universal superiority under arbitrary engine constraints.
