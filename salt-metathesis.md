# Salt metathesis

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

Exchange of ionic partners, often used to replace a [metal](metal.md) halide by an [anionic ligand](anionic-ligand.md) while an alkali-metal halide forms. Atom and charge balance distinguish it from an oxidative-addition reaction.
