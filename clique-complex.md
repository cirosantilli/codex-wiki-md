# Clique complex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clique_complex)

A simplicial complex is flag when every finite set of pairwise adjacent vertices spans a simplex.

Conversely, every flag complex is the [clique complex](clique-complex.md) of its one-skeleton, so these two descriptions specify the same class of complexes.
