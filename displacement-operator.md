# Displacement operator

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displacement_operator)

The displacement operator translates oscillator [phase space](phase-space.md). It is [unitary](unitary-connection.md) and obeys $D(\alpha)^\dagger aD(\alpha)=a+\alpha$ and $D(\alpha)|0\rangle=|\alpha\rangle$, a [coherent state](coherent-state.md). The [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) with a scalar [commutator](commutator.md) gives

$$
D(\alpha)=e^{-|\alpha|^2/2}e^{\alpha a^\dagger}e^{-\alpha^*a},\qquad
D(\alpha)D(\beta)=e^{(\alpha\beta^*-\alpha^*\beta)/2}D(\alpha+\beta).
$$

The conjugation on $\alpha^*$ ensures that the exponent is anti-Hermitian for arbitrary complex displacement. For real $\alpha=\sqrt{m\omega/(2\hbar)}x_0$, this becomes the spatial translation $e^{-ix_0p/\hbar}$.
