# Symmetric exponential excitation pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md)

For $b>0$, the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of $f$ is $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)e^{ixt}dt=2b/(b^2+x^2)$. A weak interaction $D_0f(t)\cos\omega t$ between [energy levels](energy-level.md) of gap $\hbar\omega_0$ gives final [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) $-idb\{[b^2+(\omega_0-\omega)^2]^{-1}+[b^2+(\omega_0+\omega)^2]^{-1}\}/\hbar$, where $d$ is the transition [matrix element](matrix-element.md). Only when $b,|\omega_0-\omega|\ll\omega_0$ does the single resonant lobe give an approximately squared [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) excitation profile. Its [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) is $2b\sqrt{\sqrt2-1}$ in [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md).
