# Spin-flavour symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Quark model](quark-model.md)

For three light flavours and two spin states, an approximate $SU(6)$ [spin-flavour symmetry](spin-flavour-symmetry.md) acts on the six-dimensional single-quark spin-flavour space. A spatial ground-state three-quark [colour singlet](colour-singlet.md) requires the symmetric representation of dimension $56$. Under [flavour symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) and ordinary spin rotations, it decomposes as $(\mathbf{10},\mathbf4)\oplus(\mathbf8,\mathbf2)$: the [baryon decuplet](baryon-decuplet.md) has spin $3/2$ and the [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) spin $1/2$. The product of spin and flavour factors can require a correlated sum, rather than a single unentangled product.
