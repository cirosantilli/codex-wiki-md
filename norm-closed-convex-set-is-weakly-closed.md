# Norm-closed convex set is weakly closed

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence in a Hilbert space](weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md)

A norm-closed convex subset of a Banach space contains every weak limit of its sequences. Apply [Mazur lemma](mazur-s-lemma.md) to obtain norm-convergent convex combinations that remain in the set.
