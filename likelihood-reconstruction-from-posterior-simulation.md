# Likelihood reconstruction from posterior simulation

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian statistics](bayesian-statistics.md)

For data $x$, a positive [prior distribution](prior-probability.md) density $\pi$ and [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) $\ell$, [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) gives posterior density $h(\theta\mid x)=\pi(\theta)\ell(\theta)/m(x)$. Thus $h/\pi$ reconstructs the likelihood up to a constant. Estimate the posterior density from simulation, divide by the known prior density and maximize over the search region. A [posterior mode](maximum-a-posteriori-estimate.md) alone instead estimates a [maximum a posteriori estimate](maximum-a-posteriori-estimate.md); it estimates a likelihood maximizer only when the prior is constant there. Prior support must include the likelihood maximizer of interest.
