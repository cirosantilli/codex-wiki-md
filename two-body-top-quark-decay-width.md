# Two-body top-quark decay width

↑ **Parent:** [Top-quark decay before hadronization](top-quark-decay-before-hadronization.md)

Here $x=m_W^2/m_t^2$, the [bottom quark](bottom-quark.md) [mass](mass.md) is neglected, and the [W boson](w-boson.md) is on shell. The [weak charged current](charged-current.md) gives the spin-averaged squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) $g_W^2|V_{tb}|^2(m_t^2-m_W^2)(m_t^2+2m_W^2)/(4m_W^2)$. The [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) has $p^*=(m_t^2-m_W^2)/(2m_t)$; multiplying by $p^*/(8\pi m_t^2)$ gives the displayed [decay width](decay-width.md). Its longitudinal-vector contribution explains the large-$m_t$ cubic growth, and its threshold factor makes the width vanish as $m_t\downarrow m_W$. The formula requires $m_t>m_W$ and receives finite-mass and [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md).
