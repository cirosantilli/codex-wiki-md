# Nonlinear R-xi gauge in polar Higgs variables

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-dependent gauge fixing](scalar-dependent-gauge-fixing.md)

For the [polar parametrization of the Abelian Higgs field](polar-parametrization-of-the-abelian-higgs-field.md) with $D=\partial+ieA$, adding $-H^2/(2\xi)$ cancels quadratic vector-phase mixing. The cross terms reduce, modulo a [total derivative](total-derivative.md), to $-2e(1+h/f)\pi A^\mu\partial_\mu h$; interactions remain. The [Faddeev-Popov operator](faddeev-popov-operator.md) is proportional to $\Box+\xi e^2(f+h)^2$, so the [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) couple to $h$. The vector [propagator](propagator.md) falls as $k^{-2}$ at fixed finite $\xi$, but the polar kinetic and nonlinear [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) vertices include terms of [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) above four. Manifest polynomial [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) instead uses Cartesian scalar variables with conventional [R-xi gauge](r-xi-gauge.md).
