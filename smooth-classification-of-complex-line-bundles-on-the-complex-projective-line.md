# Smooth classification of complex line bundles on the complex projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md)

Every smooth complex line bundle on $\mathbb{CP}^1$ is isomorphic to exactly one twisting bundle $\mathcal O(k)$. Trivializing on the two standard affine charts leaves a transition function on $\mathbb C^*$; its [winding number](winding-number.md) determines $k$, and a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) removes the zero-winding factor.
