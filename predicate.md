# Predicate

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)

A [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) expressing whether its argument satisfies a property. Membership in a [set](set-split.md) is represented by its characteristic predicate. Returning a [function](function-split.md) lets a [tree fold](tree-fold.md) return a predicate that subsequently checks membership of a [binary search tree](binary-search-tree.md).
