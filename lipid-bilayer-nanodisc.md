# Lipid bilayer nanodisc

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A [lipid bilayer nanodisc](lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) is a small bilayer patch stabilized at its edge, often by a surrounding scaffold. It permits controlled lipid composition and study of membrane-associated [proteins](protein-split.md), while lacking the two separately sealed aqueous compartments of a closed [lipid vesicle](lipid-vesicle.md).
