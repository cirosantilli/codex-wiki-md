# Radiative envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar convective stability](stellar-convective-stability.md)

A radiative envelope transports most of its energy by [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md). Its stratified entropy profile gives a different rapid mass-loss response from a [convective envelope](convective-envelope.md).
