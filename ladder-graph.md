# Ladder graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A [ladder graph](ladder-graph.md) is the [Cartesian product of graphs](cartesian-product-of-graphs.md) of a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) with the two-vertex [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_2$. Its two parallel rails are joined by a rung at each position. The [doubly infinite ladder graph](doubly-infinite-ladder-graph.md) uses an infinite rail indexed by $\mathbb Z$.
