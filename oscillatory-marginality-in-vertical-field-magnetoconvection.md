# Oscillatory marginality in vertical-field magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](vertical-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md)

A nonzero imaginary root of the [vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](vertical-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md) requires $0<\zeta<1$ and $Q>\zeta(1+\sigma)s^2/[\sigma(1-\zeta)\pi^2]$. Its marginal curve is $R^{(o)}=Cs^3/k^2+DQ\pi^2s/k^2$, with $C=(\sigma+\zeta)(1+\zeta)/\sigma$ and $D=\zeta(\sigma+\zeta)/(1+\sigma)$. A formal minimum of that algebraic curve is a physical oscillatory threshold only if the frequency squared there is positive.
