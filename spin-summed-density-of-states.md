# Spin-summed density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

For a spin-degenerate band, the total [density of states](density-of-states.md) includes both spin species and is twice the [single-spin density of states](single-spin-density-of-states.md). Per lattice site, $\nu(\epsilon)=2N^{-1}\sum_k\delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_k)$. Using a separate explicit spin sum then requires a compensating factor one half when converting momentum sums. The normalization matters in the [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md).
