# Ky Fan maximum principle

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of the largest eigenvalues](sum-of-the-largest-eigenvalues.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $X$ and $1\leq k\leq n$,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^k\lambda_i(X)
=\max_{Q^TQ=I_k}\operatorname{tr}(Q^TXQ).
$$

Indeed $Y=QQ^T$ is a rank-$k$ [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) in the [fantope](fantope.md), and projecting onto the [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of the largest [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) attains the [support function](support-function.md) maximum. Equivalently, the largest [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) of a compression to a $k$-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) is the [sum of the largest eigenvalues](sum-of-the-largest-eigenvalues.md).
