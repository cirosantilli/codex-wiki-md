<h1 id="su-1-1">SU(1,1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Indefinite special unitary group](indefinite-special-unitary-group.md)

Matrices $\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\\beta^*&\alpha^*\end{pmatrix}$ with the displayed constraint form a real three-dimensional noncompact [Lie group](lie-group.md). A real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) basis is $m_1=\sigma_x/2$, $m_2=\sigma_y/2$, $m_3=i\sigma_z/2$, with $[m_1,m_2]=m_3$, $[m_2,m_3]=-m_1$, and $[m_3,m_1]=-m_2$.
