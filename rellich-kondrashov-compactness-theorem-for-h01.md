# Rellich-Kondrashov compactness theorem for H01

↑ **Parent:** [Rellich-Kondrachov theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md)

This is the zero-boundary special case of the [Rellich-Kondrachov theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md). For every bounded open $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^d$, the inclusion $H_0^1(\Omega)\hookrightarrow L^2(\Omega)$ is compact.
