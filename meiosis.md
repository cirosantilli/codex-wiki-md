# Meiosis

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiosis)

[Meiosis](meiosis.md) is the chromosome-reduction process producing gametes from diploid cells. [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) chooses between parental copies, while [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) can produce new combinations at multiple [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md).
