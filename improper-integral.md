# Improper integral

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann integral](riemann-integral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improper_integral)

An improper integral is defined by taking a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) when an integration endpoint is infinite or the integrand is unbounded. Its convergence must be established before ordinary integral manipulations are applied.
