# Frobenius reciprocity for subobjects

↑ **Parent:** [Image factorization](image-factorization.md)

In a [category](category-split.md) with [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) constructions and [image factorizations](image-factorization.md), direct image $\exists_f(A')=\operatorname{im}(A'\hookrightarrow A\xrightarrow{f}B)$ is a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to inverse image on [subobjects](subobject.md). Frobenius reciprocity is $\exists_f(A'\cap f^*B')=\exists_f(A')\cap B'$. It holds for all such subobjects exactly when [strong epimorphisms](strong-epimorphism.md) are stable under pullback along [monomorphisms](monomorphism.md). For sufficiency pull the strong part of the image factorization back along the mono into its intersection with $B'$; for necessity take $A'=A$ and $f$ strong, whose image is the whole codomain.
