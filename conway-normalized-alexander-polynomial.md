# Conway-normalized Alexander polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Conway polynomial (knot theory)](conway-polynomial-knot-theory.md)

The symmetric [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md) normalized by the [Conway polynomial](conway-polynomial-knot-theory.md) is uniquely fixed for a [knot](knot.md). Its sign matters in the [signed Jones evaluation at minus one](signed-jones-evaluation-at-minus-one.md), whereas the [knot determinant](knot-determinant.md) retains only the absolute value.
