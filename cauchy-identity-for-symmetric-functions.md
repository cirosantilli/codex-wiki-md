# Cauchy identity for symmetric functions

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric function](symmetric-function.md)

Expand each factor as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) indexed by a nonnegative [matrix](matrix.md). The [RSK correspondence](robinson-schensted-knuth-correspondence.md) preserves its row and column contents, changing the sum to pairs of [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md) of the same shape and proving the displayed formal identity. Its kernel is also $\sum_\lambda h_\lambda(x)m_\lambda(y)=\sum_\lambda p_\lambda(x)p_\lambda(y)/z_\lambda$. These are dual-basis kernels for the [Hall inner product of symmetric functions](hall-inner-product-of-symmetric-functions.md), so the Schur expansion proves orthonormality. Applying the [symmetric-function involution](symmetric-function-involution.md) in one alphabet gives the dual identity $\prod_{i,j}(1+x_iy_j)=\sum_\lambda s_\lambda(x)s_{\lambda'}(y)$.
