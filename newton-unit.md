# Newton (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_(unit))

The [newton](newton-unit.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [force](force.md):

$$
1\,\mathrm N=1\,\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

This combines the units of [mass](mass.md) and [acceleration](acceleration.md) in [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md). [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md).
