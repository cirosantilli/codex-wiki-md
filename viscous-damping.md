# Viscous damping

↑ **Parent:** [Damping](damping.md)

[Viscous damping](viscous-damping.md) means a [force](force.md) proportional to, and opposite to, [velocity](velocity.md): $F_{\mathrm d}=-b\dot x$ with $b\geq0$. Its coefficient has units $\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$, equivalently $\mathrm{N\,s\,m^{-1}}$. The name describes a [force](force.md) law; [eddy-current damping](eddy-current-damping.md) can approximate the same law without a viscous fluid. Constant $m$, $b$ and $k$ produce a [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md), allowing [superposition](superposition-principle.md) of responses. [Kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) with approximately constant magnitude and [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) obey different laws, so their decay need not have the [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) or critical threshold derived here.
