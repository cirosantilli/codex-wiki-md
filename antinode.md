# Antinode

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

An antinode is a position of maximum oscillation [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) in a [standing wave](standing-wave.md). For $u_n=A\sin(n\pi x/L)\cos(\omega_nt+\varphi)$, the [antinodes](antinode.md) occur at

$$
x_j=\frac{(2j+1)L}{2n},\qquad j=0,1,\ldots,n-1,
$$

where the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is $|A|$. They sit halfway between consecutive [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md). For the first [normal mode](normal-mode.md), the sole [antinode](antinode.md) is $x=L/2$. It reaches zero [displacement](displacement.md) at a quarter [oscillation period](period-of-an-oscillation.md) while moving fastest, which illustrates why an [antinode](antinode.md) is defined by oscillation [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) rather than instantaneous height.
