# Constant time

↑ **Parent:** [Time complexity](time-complexity.md)

A [time complexity](time-complexity.md) bounded by $O(1)$ independently of input size, under a stated computational cost model. Fixed-size machine arithmetic may be constant time, whereas [arbitrary-precision arithmetic](arbitrary-precision-arithmetic.md) depends on operand length.
