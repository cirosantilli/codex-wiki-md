# Paschen-Back effect

↑ **Parent:** [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md)

In the Paschen-Back effect, the external [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) interaction becomes large compared with the [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) that holds $\mathbf L$ and $\mathbf S$ coupled. Then $J$ ceases to be a good approximate [quantum number](quantum-number.md); $m_L,m_S$ become more useful, with leading electronic shifts $\Delta E\simeq\mu_BB(m_L+g_sm_S)$. In the ideal complete-decoupling limit an [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md) preserves $m_S$, so its [spin](spin.md) contribution cancels between the levels and orbital triplet spacing can reappear. Intermediate fields require mixing the coupled states. This is a change of coupling regime, distinct from the weak-field [anomalous Zeeman effect](anomalous-zeeman-effect.md); still larger fields require additional terms beyond this linear model.
