# Mountain belt

↑ **Parent:** [Mountain building](mountain-building.md)

An elongated region affected by [mountain building](mountain-building.md), commonly containing related [thrust belts](thrust-belt.md), [metamorphic belts](metamorphic-belt.md) and deformed [sedimentary successions](sedimentary-succession.md). Restoring its age and internal structure across separated continental blocks can test [continental reconstruction](continental-reconstruction.md).
