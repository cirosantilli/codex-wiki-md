# Syndetic near returns of an almost periodic observable

↑ **Parent:** [Almost periodic observable](almost-periodic-observable.md)

If the forward orbit of $f$ under an [isometry](isometry.md) $U$ is totally bounded, choose a finite net whose centers are $U^{n_j}f$ and let $M=\max_jn_j$. For every sufficiently large $n$, some center is within $\varepsilon$ of $U^nf$, so cancellation gives $n-n_j\in R_\varepsilon$. Thus $R_\varepsilon$ is a [syndetic set](syndetic-set.md). Telescoping also gives $\|U^{kn}f-f\|_2\leq k\|U^nf-f\|_2$.
