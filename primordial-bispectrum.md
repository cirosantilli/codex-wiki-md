# Primordial bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial non-Gaussianity](primordial-non-gaussianity.md)

Statistical homogeneity defines the primordial bispectrum by

$$
\langle\phi_{\mathbf k_1}\phi_{\mathbf k_2}\phi_{\mathbf k_3}\rangle
=(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2+\mathbf k_3)
B(k_1,k_2,k_3).
$$

A [bispectrum](bispectrum.md) is a Fourier-space third-order statistic; primordial perturbations supply the cosmological field in this instance.
