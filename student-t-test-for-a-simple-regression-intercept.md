# Student t test for a simple-regression intercept

↑ **Parent:** [Regression intercept](regression-intercept.md)

For a [Gaussian linear model](normal-linear-model.md) with an intercept and one explanatory variable, $n>2$ and $S_{xx}>0$, the [regression intercept](regression-intercept.md) estimator is normal with variance $\sigma^2(1/n+\bar x^2/S_{xx})$. The residual variance estimate $s^2=\operatorname{SSE}/(n-2)$ is independent of the coefficient estimators and $(n-2)s^2/\sigma^2$ has a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $n-2$ degrees of freedom. Consequently the displayed statistic has a [Student t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md) under $\alpha=\alpha_0$, and a two-sided level-$\eta$ test rejects for $|T|>t_{n-2,1-\eta/2}$.
