# Euclidean orthonormal frame

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal structure on a real vector bundle](orthogonal-structure-on-a-real-vector-bundle.md)

This is a frame of a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) with a positive-definite [fiber metric](fiber-metric.md) for which the basis vectors are orthonormal. The [Gram-Schmidt process](gram-schmidt-process.md) applied to any smooth local frame produces such frames smoothly. They are distinguished from Lorentzian orthonormal frames, whose metric has mixed signature.
