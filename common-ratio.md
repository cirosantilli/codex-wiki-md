# Common ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric progression](geometric-progression.md)

The common ratio of a [geometric progression](geometric-progression.md) is the multiplier $r$ in $a_{n+1}=ra_n$. Whenever $a_n\ne0$, it can be recovered as $a_{n+1}/a_n$. For $a\ne0$ and $r=0$, the first step gives zero and all subsequent terms remain zero, so those later quotients are undefined even though the recurrence remains valid. For the all-zero [sequence](sequence.md), every multiplier gives the same progression, so it does not determine a unique common ratio.
