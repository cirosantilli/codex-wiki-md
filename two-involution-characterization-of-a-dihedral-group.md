# Two-involution characterization of a dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized dihedral group](generalized-dihedral-group.md)

A [group](group-split.md) generated by two [involutions](involution.md) is dihedral in the extended convention allowing $C_2$ and the [infinite dihedral group](infinite-dihedral-group.md). If $a\ne b$, put $x=ab$, $y=b$; then $yxy^{-1}=x^{-1}$. Conversely, from the inversion relation, $y$ and $xy$ square to the identity and generate. If $xy=e$, the group is $C_2$ and the same nonidentity element is used twice. Exact order two, rather than merely a square equal to the identity, requires this exceptional case. This convention extends the usual finite [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) definition.
