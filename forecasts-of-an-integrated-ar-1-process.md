<h1 id="forecasts-of-an-integrated-ar-1-process">Forecasts of an integrated AR(1) process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Recursive forecasts of an autoregressive process](recursive-forecasts-of-an-autoregressive-process.md)

If the increments of $W$ have mean $\mu$ and centered increments satisfy a causal [autoregressive process](autoregressive-model.md) of order one with $|\phi|<1$, sum their recursive forecasts to obtain the displayed expression. For fixed forecast origin, $\widehat W_{T,k}/k\to\mu$. One must observe the last increment, requiring $T\ge2$ or a known initial level $W_0$; a single observed level without an initial-level model does not identify that increment.
