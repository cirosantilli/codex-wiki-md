# Reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_transcription_polymerase_chain_reaction)

[RT-PCR](reverse-transcription-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) combines [reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md) with [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md). The RT names the [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md)-to-[DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) step, not real-time observation. [RT-PCR](reverse-transcription-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) can have an endpoint readout. A matched preparation omitting [reverse transcriptase](reverse-transcriptase.md) tests whether contaminating [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) could account for amplification; it answers a different question from a no-template reaction.[One-step vs. Two-step RT-qPCR and Controls for RT-qPCR: minus-reverse-transcription control](basic-principles-of-rt-qpcr-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
