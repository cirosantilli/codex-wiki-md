# Infinitely many primes congruent to one modulo six

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)

Assume that $x^2\equiv-3\pmod p$ with $p>3$ implies $p\equiv1\pmod6$. For a finite list of primes in that residue class, let $P$ be their product. Every prime divisor of $4P^2+3$ is greater than three and satisfies that quadratic [modular congruence](modular-congruence.md), hence has residue one modulo six. None is listed because $4P^2+3\equiv3$ modulo a listed prime.
