# Weakly nonlinear rotating-convection roll stability

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rotating-rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

A saturated [convection roll](convection-roll.md) is not stable merely because its single-amplitude cubic coefficient is positive. Perturbations include phase sidebands, transverse bending, differently oriented [convection rolls](convection-roll.md), oscillatory modes and large-scale [Eulerian mean flow](eulerian-mean-flow.md). Their growth rates follow from the corresponding enlarged [amplitude equations](amplitude-equation.md) or the full [linearization](linearization.md) about the [convection roll](convection-roll.md). [Küppers–Lortz instability](kuppers-lortz-instability.md) and [small-angle instability of rotating convection rolls](small-angle-instability-of-rotating-convection-rolls.md) are distinct orientation-dependent mechanisms.
