# New Carbaalanes Containing Clusters of Aluminum and Carbon Atoms by Werner Uhl and others

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Werner Uhl](work-by-werner-uhl.md)

[https://doi.org/10.1021/om000244s](https://doi.org/10.1021/om000244s)

Primary study in Organometallics, volume 19, pages 4536–4543. Its abstract identifies the open [carbaalane](carbaalane.md) with two $C=CHPh$ groups and reports their [hydroalumination](hydroalumination.md) during conversion to a capped aluminium cube. This establishes that the unsaturated groups are real, but the abstract alone does not establish which [electron counting](electron-counting.md) convention the examination intended.
