# Free-slip convection neutral curve

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

For nonrotating convection under the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md) with fixed-temperature impermeable [stress-free boundary conditions](stress-free-boundary-condition.md), a marginal vertical mode $\sin(n\pi z)$ has neutral [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) $(k^2+n^2\pi^2)^3/k^2$. The minimum is at $n=1$, $k=\pi/\sqrt2$, and $R_c=27\pi^4/4$. These follow by eliminating the [temperature](temperature.md) from $(D^2-k^2)^2w=Rk^2\theta$ and $(D^2-k^2)\theta=-w$, then minimizing over $k$.
