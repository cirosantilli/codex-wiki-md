# Moving-conductor Ohm law

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

In the nonrelativistic scalar-conductivity approximation, neglecting Hall and other nonideal electromotive effects, $\mathbf J=\sigma(\mathbf E+\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)$. The ideal finite-current limit gives $\mathbf E=-\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$ and hence the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md).
