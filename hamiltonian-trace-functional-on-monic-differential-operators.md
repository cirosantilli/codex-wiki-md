# Hamiltonian trace functional on monic differential operators

↑ **Parent:** [Adler trace](adler-trace.md)

For a fixed [formal pseudodifferential operator](formal-pseudodifferential-operator.md) $P=\sum_j p_j\partial^j$ and a monic [differential operator](differential-operator.md) $L=\partial^m+\sum_{l=0}^{m-1}u_l\partial^l$, cyclicity of the [Adler trace](adler-trace.md) gives $l_P(L)=\operatorname{Tr}(PL)$ with $c=\int p_{-m-1}\,dx$ and $a_l=\operatorname{res}(\partial^lP)=\sum_{k=0}^l\binom lk p_{k-l-1}^{(k)}$. The [function](function-split.md) is affine in the variable [coefficients](coefficient.md); its [variational derivative](variational-derivative.md) with respect to $u_l$ is $a_l$.
