# Normal form theorem for an amalgamated free product

↑ **Parent:** [Bass-Serre theory](bass-serre-theory.md)

In $A*_CB$, an alternating product of elements of $A\setminus C$ and $B\setminus C$ is nonidentity. More generally, choosing coset representatives gives a unique reduced normal form.

This is the amalgamated case of [normal form for free groups and free product of groups](normal-form-for-free-groups-and-free-product-of-groups.md).
