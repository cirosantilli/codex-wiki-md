# Einstein-Cartan theory

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein-Cartan_theory)

An independent [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) and metric-compatible [spin connection](spin-connection.md) allow [spin](spin.md) density to source a [torsion tensor](torsion-tensor.md). The connection equation from the [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md) is algebraic in torsion. In the absence of connection-dependent matter it fixes the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). For a minimally coupled [Dirac field](dirac-field.md), eliminating the connection generates a local four-fermion interaction. Thus its equivalent torsion-free second-order [action](action.md) includes this contact term; simply dropping it defines a different matter theory.
