# Dwell time in a semi-infinite potential step

↑ **Parent:** [Dwell time (quantum mechanics)](dwell-time-quantum-mechanics.md)

For $0<E<V_0$, set $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. Continuity at the [potential step](step-potential.md) gives the evanescent amplitude $c=2k/(k+i\kappa)$ for unit incident amplitude. Integrating $|c|^2e^{-2\kappa x}$ over $x>0$ gives $|c|^2/(2\kappa)$. Dividing by the incident [probability current](probability-current.md) $\hbar k/m$ proves the displayed [dwell time](dwell-time-quantum-mechanics.md).
