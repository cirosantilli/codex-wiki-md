# Poisson superposition of insurance portfolios

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate claims model](aggregate-claims-model.md)

Independent claim [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md) of rates $\lambda_i$ merge into a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of rate $\lambda=\sum_i\lambda_i$, by the [Superposition theorem for Poisson point processes](superposition-theorem-for-poisson-point-processes.md). The merged claim law is a [mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md) of the individual claim laws with weights $\lambda_i/\lambda$. Equivalently, multiplication of the individual compound-Poisson transforms produces $\exp(\lambda[\sum_i(\lambda_i/\lambda)M_i(r)-1])$.
