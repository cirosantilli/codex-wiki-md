# Pluto

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluto)

A dwarf [planet](planet.md) in the outer Solar System. Its slow rotation and finite [atmosphere](atmosphere.md) make it an illustrative setting for comparing [planetary vorticity](planetary-vorticity.md) gradients, [linear drag](linear-drag.md) and basin geometry in simplified circulation models.
