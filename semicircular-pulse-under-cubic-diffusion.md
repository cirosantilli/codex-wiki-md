# Semicircular pulse under cubic diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Planar volume-conserving nonlinear-diffusion similarity](planar-volume-conserving-nonlinear-diffusion-similarity.md)

The source-type [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) of $h_t=D(h^2h_x)_x$ with whole-line conserved area $\mathcal V$ is $h=H(t)[1-x^2/L(t)^2]_+^{1/2}$, where $L=2(\mathcal V/\pi)^{1/2}(Dt)^{1/4}$ and $H=(\mathcal V/\pi)^{1/2}(Dt)^{-1/4}$. Its [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) vanishes at the finite front, while the velocity tends to $\dot L=L/(4t)$. A reflecting half-line uses twice its prescribed area in these formulas.
