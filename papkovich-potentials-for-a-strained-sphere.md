# Papkovich potentials for a strained sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere in a uniform straining Stokes flow](sphere-in-a-uniform-straining-stokes-flow.md)

With symmetric traceless strain $\mathbf E$, these harmonic potentials solve the decaying disturbance around a stationary sphere in the convention $\mathbf u=\boldsymbol\Phi-\tfrac12\nabla(\mathbf x\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi+\chi)$ and $p=-\mu\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi$. The vector potential supplies the leading [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md); the scalar degree-two potential corrects no slip at finite radius. Matching the independent $\mathbf E\mathbf x$ and $(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf E\mathbf x)\mathbf x$ structures fixes both coefficients.
