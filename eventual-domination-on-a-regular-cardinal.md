# Eventual domination on a regular cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Eventual domination](eventual-domination.md)

For $f,g:\kappa\to\kappa$, write $f<^*g$ when there is $\gamma<\kappa$ such that $f(\delta)<g(\delta)$ whenever $\gamma<\delta<\kappa$. For an infinite [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md), suprema of fewer than $\kappa$ [ordinals](ordinal.md) below $\kappa$ remain below it, permitting the [long chain under eventual domination](long-chain-under-eventual-domination.md) construction.
