# Measuring rod

↑ **Parent:** [Length](length.md)

A material standard used to compare spatial separations. In [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md), a stationary equilibrium rod defines a [proper length](proper-length.md); measurements in another [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) require simultaneous endpoints in that frame. Geometry alone does not establish that arbitrary matter forms a stable length standard.
