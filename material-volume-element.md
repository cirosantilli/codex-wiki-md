# Material volume element

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid element](fluid-element.md)

A [material volume element](material-volume-element.md) contains the same fluid particles as it moves and deforms. If its edges transform with the local [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md), its volume changes at a rate equal to the trace of that gradient, giving $D(\delta V)/Dt=(\nabla\cdot\mathbf u)\delta V$. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) states $D(\rho\delta V)/Dt=0$, not that its volume is always fixed. These two identities give the material form of the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md), $D\rho/Dt=-\rho\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$.
