# Selectivity filter

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

A [selectivity filter](selectivity-filter.md) is a channel-pore region that preferentially stabilizes permeating ions through geometry and chemical interactions. Selectivity balances dehydration and coordination energies rather than following pore diameter alone.
