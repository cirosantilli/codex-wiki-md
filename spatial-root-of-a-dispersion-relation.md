# Spatial root of a dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md)

For fixed complex frequency $\omega$, a spatial root is a complex [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ satisfying the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). Analytic continuation of these branches from a causal temporal contour is essential in the [Briggs-Bers criterion](briggs-bers-criterion.md); their collision alone does not decide whether a [spatial pinch point](spatial-pinch-point.md) occurs.
