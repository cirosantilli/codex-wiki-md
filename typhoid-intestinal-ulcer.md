# Typhoid intestinal ulcer

↑ **Parent:** [Typhoid fever](typhoid-fever.md)

An ulcer arising with destruction of intestinal lymphoid [tissue](tissue-biology.md) in [typhoid fever](typhoid-fever.md). The classical larger lesion follows the longitudinal shape of a [Peyer's patch](peyer-s-patch.md).
