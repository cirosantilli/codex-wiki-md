# Geothermal gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

The [geothermal gradient](geothermal-gradient.md) is the rate at which [temperature](temperature-split.md) varies with depth. For steady one-dimensional [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) with constant [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) $k_T$, the magnitude of upward [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) is $q=k_T\,dT/dz$.
