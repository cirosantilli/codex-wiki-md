The [official notes, PDF p. 19, handwritten p. 12](https://www-thphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/people/AndreiStarinets/TUTORIALS_NOTES_GR/gr-cosmology-exam-paper-2011-solutions.pdf#page=19) integrate the radial [null geodesic](../../../../../null-geodesic.md), express the radial coordinate in terms of [comoving distance](../../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md), and give its maximum value in that coordinate chart. The maximum radial coordinate is not the total path length around a closed spatial slice.
