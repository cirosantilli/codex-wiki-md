↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 1](../unlettered-1.md)

The [official notes, PDF pp. 17–18, handwritten pp. 10–11](https://www-thphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/people/AndreiStarinets/TUTORIALS_NOTES_GR/gr-cosmology-exam-paper-2011-solutions.pdf#page=17) give the [matter density](../../../../../matter-density.md), curvature and [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) scalings and the requested logarithmic sketch. For the closed model the curvature contribution to the [Friedmann equations](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is negative; the sketched curvature magnitude must be read with that sign restored.
