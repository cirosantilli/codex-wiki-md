<h1 id="section-vi/6/unlettered-1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 1](../unlettered-1.md)

Let $a$ be a stable equilibrium separation: $V'(a)=0$, $K=V''(a)>0$. With bond extension $\delta=x-a$, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is

$$
V(a+\delta)=V(a)+\tfrac12K\delta^2+\tfrac16V'''(a)\delta^3+\cdots.
$$

The [harmonic approximation](../../../../../harmonic-approximation.md) applies when the typical thermal and zero-point bond fluctuations satisfy $|\delta|\ll a$ and the higher terms are negligible, for example $|V'''(a)\delta|\ll3K$ and $|V''''(a)\delta^2|\ll12K$. The relevant small quantity is the relative displacement of neighbouring atoms. **Small bond excursions about a stable minimum give an amplitude-independent [spring constant](../../../../../spring-constant.md) $K=V''(a)$.** Large thermal excursions, bond breaking or appreciable [anharmonicity](../../../../../anharmonicity.md) invalidate the approximation.
