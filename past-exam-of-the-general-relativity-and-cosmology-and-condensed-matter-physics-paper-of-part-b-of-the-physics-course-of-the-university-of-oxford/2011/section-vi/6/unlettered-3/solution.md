<h1 id="section-vi/6/unlettered-3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 3](../unlettered-3.md)

For the [Lennard-Jones potential](../../../../../lennard-jones-potential.md),

$$
V'(x)=\frac{24\epsilon}{x}\left[\left(\frac\sigma x\right)^6-2\left(\frac\sigma x\right)^{12}\right].
$$

Its minimum and harmonic [spring constant](../../../../../spring-constant.md) are

$$
a=2^{1/6}\sigma,\qquad
K=4\epsilon\left(\frac{156\sigma^{12}}{a^{14}}-\frac{42\sigma^6}{a^8}\right)=\frac{72\epsilon}{a^2}.
$$

Using the [monatomic-chain dispersion relation](../../../../../monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md),

$$
\boxed{v=\sqrt{\frac{72\epsilon}{m}},\qquad
\omega_{\max}=2\sqrt{\frac Km}=\frac2{2^{1/6}\sigma}\sqrt{\frac{72\epsilon}{m}},\qquad f_{\max}=\frac{\omega_{\max}}{2\pi}.}
$$

The maximum is at $|k|=\pi/a$. Taking the [argon](../../../../../argon.md) atomic mass as $m\simeq39.95\,\mathrm u=6.63\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $\epsilon=1.602\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm J$ gives

$$
a\simeq0.382\,\mathrm{nm},\qquad K\simeq0.792\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}},
$$



$$
\boxed{v\simeq1.32\times10^3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad\omega_{\max}\simeq6.91\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad f_{\max}\simeq1.10\,\mathrm{THz}.}
$$

These are the stipulated one-dimensional nearest-neighbour model estimates; a three-dimensional [argon](../../../../../argon.md) crystal has additional neighbours and [phonon polarizations](../../../../../phonon-polarization.md).
