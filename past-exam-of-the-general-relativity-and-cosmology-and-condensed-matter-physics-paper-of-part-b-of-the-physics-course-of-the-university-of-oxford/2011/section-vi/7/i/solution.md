<h1 id="section-vi/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $u=V_{10}<0$ and

$$
E_X=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2}{2m_ea^2}.
$$

At $X=(\pi/a,0)$ the two degenerate [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) have momenta $(\pi/a,0)$ and $(-\pi/a,0)$ and coupling $u$. Thus

$$
\begin{vmatrix}E_X-E&u\\u&E_X-E\end{vmatrix}=0,\qquad
\boxed{E_{X,1}=E_X+u,\quad E_{X,2}=E_X-u.}
$$

Their symmetric and antisymmetric combinations are the lower and upper states respectively. The local [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) is $2|u|$.
