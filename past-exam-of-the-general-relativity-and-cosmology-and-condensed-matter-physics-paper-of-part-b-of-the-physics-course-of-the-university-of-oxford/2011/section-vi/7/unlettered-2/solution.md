<h1 id="section-vi/7/unlettered-2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 2](../unlettered-2.md)

There are two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md); a spin-degenerate [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) accommodates exactly that number. A [semiconductor](../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) results when the first band is full and does not overlap the second. In the leading two-wave/four-wave approximation, compare the first-band maximum at $M$ with the second-band minimum at $X$:

$$
\Delta_{\rm ind}=E_{X,2}-E_{M,1}
=(E_X-u)-(2E_X+w+2u)=-E_X-w-3u.
$$

This is an [indirect band gap](../../../../../indirect-band-gap.md); a nonzero local splitting at $X$ alone is insufficient. At the onset,

$$
\boxed{V_{10,\rm crit}=-\frac{E_X+V_{11}}3.}
$$

With $a=0.3\,\mathrm{nm}$, $\hbar^2/(2m_e)=0.03810\,\mathrm{eV\,nm^2}$ gives $E_X\simeq4.178\,\mathrm{eV}$, hence

$$
\boxed{V_{10,\rm crit}\simeq-1.326\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

For $V_{10}$ more negative than this value the estimated [indirect band gap](../../../../../indirect-band-gap.md) is positive; at equality the bands just touch. This is the onset estimate within the source's [nearly-free electron model](../../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md) truncation, not an exact critical potential after coupling all [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md).
