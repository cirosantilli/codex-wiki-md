<h1 id="section-vi/5/unlettered-3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 3](../unlettered-3.md)

Use the [scattering vector](../../../../../scattering-vector.md) $\mathbf q=\mathbf k_{\rm in}-\mathbf k_{\rm out}$; reversing its sign conjugates the following amplitudes without changing the intensities. In the [kinematic approximation](../../../../../kinematic-approximation.md), the [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) of atom $s$ is

$$
f_s(\mathbf q)=\int n_s(\mathbf u)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf u}\,d^3u.
$$

Atoms at $\mathbf R+\mathbf d_s$ therefore give the [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md)

$$
\boxed{A(\mathbf q)=A_e(\mathbf q)\underbrace{\sum_s f_s(\mathbf q)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf d_s}}_{F(\mathbf q)}\underbrace{\sum_{\mathbf R}e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf R}}_{S_L(\mathbf q)}.}
$$

Here $A_e$ is the single-electron amplitude; $F$ is the [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md) [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md), and $S_L$ is the geometrical lattice sum. If the crystal has $N_i$ repeats along $\mathbf a_i$, the latter factor is a product of [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md), whose magnitudes contain

$$
\left|\frac{\sin(N_i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf a_i/2)}{\sin(\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf a_i/2)}\right|.
$$

The sharp constructive peaks occur when every $\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf a_i=2\pi h_i$. Thus **a permitted reflection has wavevector change equal to a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md)**, with $F(\mathbf G)\ne0$ and the additional elastic condition $|\mathbf k_{\rm out}|=|\mathbf k_{\rm in}|$. A finite crystal broadens these peaks; it does not impose an exact delta-function selection on every scattered photon.
