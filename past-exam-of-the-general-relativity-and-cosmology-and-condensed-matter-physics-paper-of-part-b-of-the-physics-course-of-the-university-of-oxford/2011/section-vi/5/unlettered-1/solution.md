<h1 id="section-vi/5/unlettered-1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 1](../unlettered-1.md)

For the mutually orthogonal direct [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) $\mathbf a_i$, define the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) basis by

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf b_j=2\pi\delta_{ij},\qquad \mathbf b_i=\frac{2\pi\mathbf a_i}{|\mathbf a_i|^2}.}
$$

Thus $\mathbf G=\sum_i h_i\mathbf b_i$, with integer $h_i$, satisfies $\exp(i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R)=1$ for every [lattice vector](../../../../../lattice-vector.md) $\mathbf R$.
