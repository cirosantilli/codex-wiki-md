<h1 id="section-vi/5/unlettered-5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 5](../unlettered-5.md)

In the electron-count estimate $f_{\rm Ga}\simeq31$, $f_{\rm As}\simeq33$, the [structure factors](../../../../../structure-factor.md) are $F_{111}=4(31-33i)$ and $F_{200}=4(31-33)$. Thus a single-directed-reflection estimate is

$$
\frac{|F_{111}|^2}{|F_{200}|^2}=\frac{31^2+33^2}{(31-33)^2}=512.5.
$$

A [powder X-ray diffraction](../../../../../powder-x-ray-diffraction.md) peak collects all equivalent reflections. The [powder diffraction multiplicities](../../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md) are eight for $\{111\}$ and six for $\{200\}$, so the multiplicity-and-electron-count estimate is

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_{\{111\}}}{I_{\{200\}}}\simeq\frac86\frac{31^2+33^2}{(31-33)^2}=6.83\times10^2.}
$$

At $(200)$ the two sublattice amplitudes are opposite in phase and nearly cancel; at $(111)$ their relative phase is a quarter cycle, so this cancellation does not occur. In [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md), the two [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) are identical and the [diamond cubic structure](../../../../../diamond-cubic-structure.md) has **an exactly forbidden $(200)$ reflection**, while $(111)$ remains allowed in the [kinematic approximation](../../../../../kinematic-approximation.md).

The numerical ratio neglects the angular dependence of the [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md), the [Lorentz-polarization factor](../../../../../lorentz-polarization-factor.md) and thermal-motion corrections. No wavelength or measurement geometry is supplied to refine those factors. This is therefore a stated structural estimate, rather than a unique precision ratio of measured peak areas.
