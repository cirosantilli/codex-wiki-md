<h1 id="section-vi/5/unlettered-4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unlettered 4](../unlettered-4.md)

In the conventional cubic [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md), the four [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) positions contribute

$$
S_{\rm FCC}=1+e^{i\pi(k+l)}+e^{i\pi(h+l)}+e^{i\pi(h+k)}.
$$

The two-atom [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) gives the [zinc blende structure factor](../../../../../zinc-blende-structure-factor.md)

$$
\boxed{F_{hkl}=S_{\rm FCC}\left[f_{\rm Ga}+f_{\rm As}e^{i\pi(h+k+l)/2}\right].}
$$

Mixed-parity [Miller indices](../../../../../miller-index.md) have $S_{\rm FCC}=0$. With all indices even or all odd, $S_{\rm FCC}=4$. For real [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md),

$$
|F_{hkl}|^2=16\begin{cases}
f_{\rm Ga}^2+f_{\rm As}^2,&h,k,l\text{ all odd},\\
(f_{\rm Ga}+f_{\rm As})^2,&h,k,l\text{ all even},\ h+k+l=0\pmod4,\\
(f_{\rm Ga}-f_{\rm As})^2,&h,k,l\text{ all even},\ h+k+l=2\pmod4.
\end{cases}
$$

Hence **all same-parity reflections are generally permitted in GaAs**, although the last class is weak when the two [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) nearly coincide. These are the structural selection rules; a particular experiment must also satisfy the [elastic Bragg scattering condition](../../../../../elastic-bragg-scattering-condition.md).
