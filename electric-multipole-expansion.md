# Electric multipole expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)

For a localized charge distribution, the far [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is an inverse-radius expansion whose first terms are the total charge monopole, the electric dipole moment, and the electric quadrupole moment.
