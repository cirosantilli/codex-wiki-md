# Cyclotomic character of a finite cyclotomic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

For a primitive $m$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md) $\zeta_m$, every [Galois automorphism](automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md) sends $\zeta_m$ to a unique $\zeta_m^a$ with $a$ invertible modulo $m$. The assignment $\sigma\mapsto a$ is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md), since composition multiplies the exponents, and is injective because $\zeta_m$ generates the [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md). The [irreducibility of cyclotomic polynomials](irreducibility-of-cyclotomic-polynomials.md) gives degree $\varphi(m)$, equal to the order of the target, so the map is an isomorphism. The [cyclotomic character](cyclotomic-character.md) on an [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md) factors through this finite quotient and generally has a nontrivial [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md).
