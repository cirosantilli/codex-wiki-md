# Gaussian sample limsup at logarithmic scale

↑ **Parent:** [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md)

For independent [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md) $X_n$, the displayed [limit superior](limit-superior.md) equals two [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). The [Mills ratio](mills-ratio.md) asymptotic gives, for $a>0$,

$$
\mathbb P(X_n^2>a\log n)\sim\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi a}}\frac{n^{-a/2}}{\sqrt{\log n}}.
$$

The series converges for $a>2$ and diverges for $0<a\leq2$. The two [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md), applied at countably many thresholds approaching two, give the result. In particular, the value two is also a threshold exceeded infinitely often, despite being the almost-sure [limit superior](limit-superior.md).
