# Rational structure on an algebraic group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)

A rational structure over $\mathbb F_q$ is a model over that [finite field](finite-field.md) together with its identification after extension to $\overline{\mathbb F}_q$. It determines a [Frobenius endomorphism of an algebraic group](frobenius-endomorphism-of-an-algebraic-group.md), whose fixed points are the rational points. Conjugating a standard Frobenius by an [algebraic group](algebraic-group.md) automorphism transports the rational structure, even if the automorphism is not itself rational for the original structure.
