# Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$, its Laplace transform is

$$
\psi_X(s)=\mathbb E[e^{-sX}],
\qquad s\geq0.
$$
