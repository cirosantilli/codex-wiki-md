# Rational elliptic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Pencil of plane cubics](pencil-of-plane-cubics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_elliptic_surface)

Blowing up the nine base points of a general [pencil of plane cubics](pencil-of-plane-cubics.md) produces the rational elliptic surface

$$
E(1)=\{([z],[s:t])\in\operatorname{Bl}_B\mathbb{CP}^2\times\mathbb{CP}^1:sF_0(z)+tF_1(z)=0\}.
$$
