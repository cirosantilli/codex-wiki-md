# Weak local minimizer of a variational integral

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

For a fixed-endpoint integral on $W^{1,\infty}(a,b)$, an admissible function $u$ is a weak local minimizer if $I(v)\geq I(u)$ whenever the [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md) $\|v-u\|_{W^{1,\infty}}$ is sufficiently small. Both function and derivative must be close. “Weak” here labels the variational neighborhood convention, not the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md).
