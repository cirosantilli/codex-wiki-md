# Emission measure

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md)

For a hydrogen-dominated fully ionized [plasma](plasma-physics.md), the line-of-sight [emission measure](emission-measure.md) is $\mathrm{EM}=\int n_e^2\,dl$. Its units distinguish it from a volume-integrated [emission measure](emission-measure.md) $\int n_e^2dV$. Thermal free-free [optical depth](optical-depth.md) is proportional to $\mathrm{EM}\,T^{-3/2}\nu^{-2}\bar g_{\rm ff}$ in the radio limit. Inferring [density](density.md) also requires depth, ionization and clumping assumptions.[chapter 4, equations (4.57)–(4.58)](essential-radio-astronomy-by-james-j-condon-and-scott-m-ransom.md)
