# Capillary entry pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md)

The threshold pressure difference required for a nonwetting fluid to invade a connected pore throat. In a simple cylindrical throat of radius $r$, the magnitude is approximately $2\gamma|\cos\theta|/r$, using [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) $\gamma$ and contact angle $\theta$. A sealing [caprock](caprock.md) can fail to retain a buoyant phase if its pressure exceeds this threshold or if connected fractures bypass the fine pore network.
