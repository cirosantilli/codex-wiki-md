# Prime field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)

The prime field of a field $K$ is its smallest [subfield](subfield.md). It is isomorphic to $\mathbb Q$ when $K$ has characteristic zero and to $\mathbb F_p$ when $K$ has characteristic $p>0$.
