# Codimension-two bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

A codimension-two bifurcation requires two independent parameter conditions to hold simultaneously. Simultaneous stationary and [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) thresholds require both kinds of critical mode in the reduced [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md). A crossing of modes at different [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) is different from a zero-frequency double-zero degeneration of one fixed-wave-number cubic.
