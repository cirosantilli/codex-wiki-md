# Homotopy group

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_group)

The $n$th homotopy group consists of based homotopy classes of maps $S^n\to X$. It is a group for $n\geq1$ and an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) for $n\geq2$.
