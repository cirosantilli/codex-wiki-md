# Coreflexive pair

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)

A coreflexive pair $f,g:B\rightrightarrows A$ has a common retraction $r:A\to B$ with $rf=rg=1_B$. It is the dual of a [reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md). Both $f$ and $g$ are [split monomorphisms](split-monomorphism.md).
