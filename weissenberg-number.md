# Weissenberg number

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weissenberg_number)

The Weissenberg number measures deformation accumulated over a [viscoelastic relaxation time](viscoelastic-relaxation-time.md), often as the product of that time and a [shear rate](shear-rate.md) or an extension rate. Its precise rate convention should be stated. Unlike a [Deborah number](deborah-number.md) based on externally imposed temporal variation, it can remain nonzero in a steady flow. A weak-flow constitutive expansion generally requires both slow temporal change and small deformation over the memory time.
