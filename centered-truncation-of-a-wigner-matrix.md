# Centered truncation of a Wigner matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner matrix](wigner-matrix.md)

Truncate the unscaled entries of a [Wigner matrix](wigner-matrix.md) at a fixed threshold and subtract their [expected values](expected-value.md). The difference between original and truncated entries is $N^{-1/2}(Z_C-\mathbb EZ_C)$ with $Z_C=Y\mathbf1_{|Y|\geq C}$, so its [variance](variance-split.md) is bounded by $N^{-1}\mathbb E[Y^2\mathbf1_{|Y|\geq C}]$. The centering term cannot be discarded for an asymmetric entry law.
