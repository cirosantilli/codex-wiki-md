# Nonnormal eigenvalues do not bound a quadratic quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md)

The real [matrix](matrix.md) $B_M=\begin{pmatrix}1&M\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has both [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) equal to one, whereas $x^TB_Mx/(x^Tx)=1+M/2$ for $x=(1,1)^T$. Hence no universal constant bounds this quotient by the maximum [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of every real [matrix](matrix.md) with real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
