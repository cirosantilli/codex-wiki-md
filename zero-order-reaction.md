# Zero-order reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Order of reaction](order-of-reaction.md)

A [zero-order reaction](zero-order-reaction.md) has a [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) independent of the relevant [reactant](reactant.md) [concentration](concentration.md) over the range where its [rate law](rate-law.md) applies. For $\mathrm A\longrightarrow\mathrm B$, $[\mathrm A]=A_0-kt$ is valid only before depletion at $t=A_0/k$. The constant-consumption model must stop or change there.
