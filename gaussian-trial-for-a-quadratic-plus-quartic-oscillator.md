# Gaussian trial for a quadratic-plus-quartic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian variational bounds for a quartic oscillator](gaussian-variational-bounds-for-a-quartic-oscillator.md)

For $H=-d^2/dx^2+x^2+\epsilon x^4$, use the normalized real [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) $\psi_\alpha=(\alpha/\pi)^{1/4}e^{-\alpha x^2/2}$, $\alpha>0$. The [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) gives $\langle x^2\rangle=1/(2\alpha)$ and $\langle x^4\rangle=3/(4\alpha^2)$, while the kinetic contribution is $\alpha/2$. Thus the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) is the displayed expression. For positive $\epsilon$, its stationary width satisfies $\alpha^3-\alpha-3\epsilon=0$. At $\epsilon=2$ the unique positive solution is $\alpha=2$, giving $E_0\leq13/8$ by the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md). For $\epsilon<0$, taking $\alpha\downarrow0$ makes the negative quartic contribution dominate, so the energy is unbounded below even within this normalized family. The positive-coupling bound and negative-coupling instability concern different signs of the same variational expression.
