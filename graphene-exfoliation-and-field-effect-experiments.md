# Graphene exfoliation and field-effect experiments

↑ **Parent:** [Graphene](graphene.md)

[Andre Geim](andre-geim.md), [Konstantin Novoselov](konstantin-novoselov.md) and collaborators mechanically cleaved [graphite](graphite.md), identified thin flakes, and fabricated multi-terminal [Hall bars](hall-bar.md) on oxidized [silicon](silicon.md). A substrate gate changed the [carrier density](carrier-density.md); longitudinal [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and the [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md) supplied different checks. The 2004 report included one-, two- and three-layer devices and described a few-layer semimetal interpretation. Layer identification and electrical control were separate experimental achievements.[pp.1–2 and Figs.1–2: preparation, thickness selection and electrical measurements](electric-field-effect-in-atomically-thin-carbon-films-by-konstantin-novoselov-and-others.md)

The 2005 monolayer study used an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md), [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) and [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md) to select monolayers. It then measured [Shubnikov-de Haas oscillations](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md) as [temperature](temperature-split.md), gate voltage and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) varied. Their damping inferred a density-dependent [cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md); comparison with oscillation frequency tested a linear [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). A comparison with two-layer devices also distinguished their [quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md) sequences. These are complementary tests of the monolayer model, rather than consequences of finding a thin-looking flake alone.[pp.1–3, Figs.2–4: monolayer selection, oscillation damping and bilayer comparison](two-dimensional-gas-of-massless-dirac-fermions-in-graphene-by-konstantin-novoselov-and-others.md)
