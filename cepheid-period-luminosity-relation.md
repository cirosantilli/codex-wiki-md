# Cepheid period-luminosity relation

↑ **Parent:** [Cepheid variable](cepheid-variable.md)

The pulsation period of a [Cepheid variable](cepheid-variable.md) is correlated with its [absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md) in a specified photometric band. Calibrating the relation with stars of known distance allows an observed period and corrected [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md) to give a distance. Extinction and metallicity corrections matter for a precise [cosmic distance ladder](cosmic-distance-ladder.md).
