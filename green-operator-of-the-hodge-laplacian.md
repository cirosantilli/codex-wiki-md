# Green operator of the Hodge Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md)

On a compact oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the Green operator $G$ is the inverse of the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) on the orthogonal complement of harmonic forms and is zero on harmonic forms. If $H$ is harmonic projection, then

$$
\Delta G=G\Delta=1-H.
$$

It commutes with every differential operator that commutes with $\Delta$.
