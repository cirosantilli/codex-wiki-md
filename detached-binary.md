# Detached binary

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

A detached binary has both components lying inside their respective [Roche lobes](roche-lobe.md), so neither currently transfers matter by [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md). A detached system may still exchange matter through a [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md).
