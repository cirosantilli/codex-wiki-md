# Higgs-electron coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

The physical [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) interaction $\mathcal L\supset-(m_e/v)h\bar ee$ after [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md). It is distinguished from the unbroken-phase electron [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) $y_e=\sqrt2m_e/v$. This small coefficient explains the [electron Yukawa suppression of Higgs production](electron-yukawa-suppression-of-higgs-production.md) in direct electron–positron annihilation.
