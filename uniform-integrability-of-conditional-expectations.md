# Uniform integrability of conditional expectations

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md)

For one [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $Y$, the family $Z_{\mathcal G}=\mathbb E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$, over arbitrary [sigma-algebras](sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal G$, has [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md). On $A=\{|Z_{\mathcal G}|>K\}$, the defining property of [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) gives $\mathbb E[|Z_{\mathcal G}|\mathbf1_A]\leq\mathbb E[|Y|\mathbf1_A]$, while $\mathbb P(A)\leq\mathbb E|Y|/K$ by the [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md). The [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $Y$ has uniformly small absolute integrals over [events](event.md) of sufficiently small probability.
