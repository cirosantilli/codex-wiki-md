# Non-acceleration theorem for quasi-geostrophic waves

↑ **Parent:** [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md)

Steady conservative small-amplitude waves have divergence-free [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md) by [wave activity](wave-activity.md) conservation. In a balanced mean state with homogeneous impermeable boundaries and no independently forced [residual mean circulation](residual-mean-circulation.md), this gives zero residual circulation and zero mean acceleration. The [Eulerian mean](eulerian-mean-flow.md) circulation can nevertheless be nonzero because the residual circulation includes an eddy-induced correction. Wave dissipation, time-dependent wave activity or boundary forcing can invalidate the conclusion.
