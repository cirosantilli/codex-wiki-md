# Explicit multistep methods cannot be A-stable

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)

For an explicit [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md), the highest-degree coefficient of $\rho(w)-z\sigma(w)$ is fixed while at least one lower coefficient grows unboundedly with negative real $z$. If all roots stayed in the [unit disk](unit-disk.md), the [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) of those roots would keep every monic coefficient bounded. This contradiction proves that no nontrivial consistent explicit method is [A-stable](a-stability.md).
