# Early-type galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

A morphological grouping of [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md) and [lenticular galaxies](lenticular-galaxy.md). “Early” is a historical classification name, not a direct statement that a galaxy is young. Dynamical comparisons use a stated structural family and [stellar population](stellar-population.md).
