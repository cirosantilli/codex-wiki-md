# Quaternion

↑ **Parent:** [Division algebra](division-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternion)

A quaternion is $a+bi+cj+dk$ with real coefficients, where $i^2=j^2=k^2=ijk=-1$. They form a noncommutative real [division algebra](division-algebra.md). The norm squared is $a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$, and every nonzero quaternion has inverse its conjugate divided by that squared norm. The unit quaternions form the [Lie group](lie-group.md) $S^3$.
