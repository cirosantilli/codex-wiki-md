# Planck function

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md)

The [Planck function](planck-function.md) is the equilibrium spectral specific intensity of a [blackbody](blackbody.md), given by [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md). Per unit wavelength it is

$$
B_\lambda(T)=\frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\frac1{e^{hc/(\lambda k_BT)}-1}.
$$

It can also be written per unit frequency, with $B_\nu\,d\nu=-B_\lambda\,d\lambda$ under the corresponding change of variable.
