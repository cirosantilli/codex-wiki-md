# Zariski tangent space of a Hilbert scheme

↑ **Parent:** [First-order embedded deformation](first-order-embedded-deformation.md)

For a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) $Z\subseteq X$ with [ideal sheaf](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) $\mathcal I$, one has $T_{[Z]}\operatorname{Hilb}(X)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_X}(\mathcal I,i_*\mathcal O_Z)\cong H^0(Z,\mathcal N_{Z/X})$. Locally a flat deformation ideal $J\subseteq A[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$ satisfies $J\cap\varepsilon A=\varepsilon I$. Lifting $f\in I$ to $f+\varepsilon g\in J$ gives a well-defined map $f\mapsto g\bmod I$ killing $I^2$. Conversely the graph condition $g\bmod I=\phi(f)$ defines an ideal with flat quotient by the [flatness criterion over dual numbers](flatness-criterion-over-dual-numbers.md). These operations commute with restriction and are inverse, giving the global identification without a smoothness assumption.
