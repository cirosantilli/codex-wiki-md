# Scale invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scale_invariance)

A law or statistical object is scale invariant if its form is unchanged by rescaling lengths, with any specified scaling weight for the fields. A dimensionless field's three-dimensional nonzero-mode [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md) obeys $P(\lambda k)=\lambda^{-3}P(k)$ when its statistics have this scaling. The meaning of invariance must specify whether zero modes and regulators are being transformed.
