# Reflexive-pair groupoid formula in a preadditive category

↑ **Parent:** [Internal groupoid](internal-groupoid.md)

For a [reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md) $f,g:A\rightrightarrows B$ with section $r$ in a [preadditive category](preadditive-category.md), each hom-set diagram defines a [groupoid](groupoid.md). Arrows $a,b:C\to A$ compose when $ga=fb$, by $b\circ a=a+b-rga$. Identity at $x:C\to B$ is $rx$, and the inverse of $a$ is $rfa+rga-a$. Bilinearity proves the endpoint, identity and associativity laws and compatibility with precomposition in $C$. In the hom-set formulation this does not assume that the composable-arrow pullback exists.
