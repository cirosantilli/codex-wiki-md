# Palladium-catalysed hydrodehalogenation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Replacement of an organic halide by [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) through a [palladium](palladium.md) catalytic cycle. [oxidative addition](oxidative-addition.md), [hydride](hydride.md) delivery and [reductive elimination](reductive-elimination.md) can preserve the [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md) of an alkenyl fragment.
