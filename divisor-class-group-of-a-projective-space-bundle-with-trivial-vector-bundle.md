# Divisor class group of a projective-space bundle with trivial vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Localization sequence for the divisor class group](localization-sequence-for-the-divisor-class-group.md)

For a Noetherian integral scheme $X$ that is regular in codimension one,

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(X\times\mathbb P^n)\cong\operatorname{Cl}(X)\oplus\mathbb Z[H],
$$

where $H=X\times\mathbb P^{n-1}$ is a [hyperplane divisor](hyperplane-divisor.md). Restriction to $X\times\mathbb A^n$ gives the first summand, while restriction to the generic projective-space fiber detects the coefficient of $H$.
