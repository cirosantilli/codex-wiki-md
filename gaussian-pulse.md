# Gaussian pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pulse](optical-pulse.md)

A Gaussian pulse has a field or intensity envelope specified by a Gaussian function; the distinction must be stated. For the displayed intensity, the temporal [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) is $2\sqrt{2\ln2}\,\sigma_t$. A [transform-limited pulse](transform-limited-pulse.md) with this shape has intensity spectral and temporal widths satisfying $\Delta\nu\Delta t=2\ln2/\pi$. A quadratic spectral phase can lengthen the pulse without changing its spectral intensity.
