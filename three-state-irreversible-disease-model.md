# Three-state irreversible disease model

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time multi-state model](continuous-time-multi-state-model.md)

A sequential [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) has transitions $1\to2\to3$ with rates $\lambda,\nu$ and an [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md) $3$. Its probability of ever leaving state $1$ by time $t$ is $1-e^{-\lambda t}$; its probability of occupying state $2$ is $\lambda(e^{-\lambda t}-e^{-\nu t})/(\nu-\lambda)$ for unequal rates.
