# Exchangeable random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchangeable_random_variables)

A family of [random variables](random-variable-split.md) is exchangeable if its [joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md) is unchanged by finite permutations of its coordinates. [Independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) are exchangeable, but exchangeable [random variables](random-variable-split.md) need not be [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md).
