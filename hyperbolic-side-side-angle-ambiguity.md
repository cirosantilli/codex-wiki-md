# Hyperbolic side-side-angle ambiguity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md)

Two corresponding side lengths and an equal nonincluded angle need not determine a [hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md). Set $A=\pi/6$, $\cosh a=3/2$, $\cosh b=2$. The [hyperbolic law of cosines](hyperbolic-law-of-cosines.md) has two distinct positive solutions $c=\ln(3\pm\sqrt2)$. The two triangles share $a,b,A$ but have different third sides. An included angle instead determines the third side by the cosine rule.
