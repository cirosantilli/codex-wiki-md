# Parity-dependent boundary condition of the Jordan-Wigner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md)

For $S_n^+=\prod_{m<n}(1-2n_m)c_n$ and periodic [spins](spin.md), the end exchange term carries the global [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) $P=(-1)^{N_f}$. Replacing the end bond by a uniform hopping expression requires $c_{N+1}=-Pc_1$: even parity gives antiperiodic [fermions](fermion.md), odd parity periodic [fermions](fermion.md). Each resulting quadratic spectrum must be restricted to states of the parity used in its [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md).
