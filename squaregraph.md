# Squaregraph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squaregraph)

A [squaregraph](squaregraph.md) has a plane embedding in which every bounded face has four sides and every interior vertex has degree at least four. Its outer face may have more than four sides, so this definition differs from a [planar quadrangulation](planar-quadrangulation.md) requiring every face to have degree four.
