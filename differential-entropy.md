# Differential entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_entropy)

The [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $-\mathbb E_q[\log q(X)]$ when this [expected value](expected-value.md) is defined. Unlike discrete [information entropy](information-entropy.md), it can be negative and depends on the coordinates and reference [measure](measure.md). It is the entropy term in the [evidence lower bound](evidence-lower-bound.md) for continuous [variational inference](variational-inference.md).
