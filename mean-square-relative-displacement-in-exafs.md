# Mean-square relative displacement in EXAFS

↑ **Parent:** [Extended X-ray absorption fine structure](extended-x-ray-absorption-fine-structure.md)

The bond-direction [variance](variance-split.md) of relative atomic displacement. A Gaussian distance distribution damps the coherent round-trip phase by $e^{-2k^2\sigma^2}$. Correlated atomic motion and static bond disorder contribute, so this is not generally a single atom's mean-square displacement about its lattice site.
