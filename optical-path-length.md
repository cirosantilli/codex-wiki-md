# Optical path length

↑ **Parent:** [Wavefront](wavefront.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_path_length)

The optical path length along a ray is the integral of its [refractive index](refractive-index.md) over geometric length. A difference $h$ in optical path length produces a phase difference $2\pi h/\lambda$, where $\lambda$ is the vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md).
