# Cross-intersection bound from cube separation

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md)

If two nonempty [set families](set-family.md) on $m\geq1$ coordinates have every cross-intersection greater than an integer $w\geq0$, their density product obeys the displayed bound. Complementing the second [set family](set-family.md) separates it from the first by [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md) greater than $w$. [Harper inequality](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md) bounds the first [set family](set-family.md)'s radius-$w$ neighbourhood from below. If $S_m(a-1)<|\mathcal F|\leq S_m(a)$, disjointness yields $|\mathcal F||\mathcal G|\leq S_m(a)S_m(m-a-w)$. The [binary entropy function](binary-entropy-function.md) tail estimate bounds these two factors by Gaussian tails; their deficits from the middle rank sum to at least $w$, proving the claim.
