# p-adic metric

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md)

The [p-adic metric](p-adic-metric.md) on the [P-adic numbers](p-adic-number.md) is induced by the [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md). Its [ultrametric inequality](ultrametric-inequality.md) is $d_p(x,z)\leq\max(d_p(x,y),d_p(y,z))$. On [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md), [digit agreement and p-adic distance](digit-agreement-and-p-adic-distance.md) expresses metric precision as shared low-order digits.
