# Inverse beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_beta_decay)

In the reaction displayed here, an [electron antineutrino](electron-antineutrino.md) interacts with a [proton](proton.md) to produce a [Positron](positron.md) and a [neutron](neutron-split.md). It turns an otherwise hard-to-observe incident particle into two products with different detection signatures. A prompt [Positron](positron.md) signal followed by [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) can identify a candidate much more selectively than a single energy deposit. [Inverse beta decay](inverse-beta-decay.md) is a [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md); the incident [electron antineutrino](electron-antineutrino.md) is detected through its reaction products.
