# Topological sector

↑ **Parent:** [Topological charge](topological-charge.md)

A connected component, or homotopy class, of admissible field configurations with fixed asymptotic conditions. Distinct [topological sectors](topological-sector.md) cannot be joined by a continuous deformation within that configuration space. A nonzero [topological charge](topological-charge.md) labels a sector, but a stable finite-size energy minimum also depends on the energy functional.
