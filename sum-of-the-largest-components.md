# Sum of the largest components

↑ **Parent:** [Support function](support-function.md)

The sum of the $k$ largest components of $x\in\mathbb R^n$ is the [support function](support-function.md) of the [capped simplex](capped-simplex.md): $\sum_{i=1}^k x_{[i]}=\max\{x^Tv:0\leq v\leq1,\ \sum_i v_i=k\}$.
