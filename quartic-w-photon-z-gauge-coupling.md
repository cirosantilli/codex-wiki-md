# Quartic W-photon-Z gauge coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge boson self-interaction](gauge-boson-self-interaction.md)

The non-Abelian electroweak gauge kinetic term contains a $W^+W^-W^3W^3$ interaction. Substituting $W^3=\sin\theta_W A+\cos\theta_W Z$ gives a mixed four-gauge-boson vertex with coupling magnitude $g^2\sin\theta_W\cos\theta_W=eg\cos\theta_W$. It can emit both a [photon](photon.md) and a [Z boson](z-boson.md) at one vertex. A one-loop flavour-changing [quark](quark.md) graph with two quark-W vertices and this quartic vertex consequently has three vertices despite having four external particles.
