# Partial pooling

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical Bayesian model](hierarchical-bayesian-model.md)

[Partial pooling](partial-pooling.md) estimates related local parameters jointly through shared [hyperparameters](hyperparameter.md). Each local [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) balances its own [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) against a group [prior distribution](prior-probability.md), with stronger shrinkage when its data are weak or the group variation is small. It lies between forcing all local parameters to be equal and estimating them independently. [Random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md) is an important application.
