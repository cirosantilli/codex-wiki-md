# Logarithmic coefficient bound in Vaughan identity

↑ **Parent:** [Vaughan's identity](vaughan-s-identity.md)

The [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md) is nonnegative and satisfies $\sum_{d\mid m}\Lambda(d)=\log m$. Therefore the middle short-divisor coefficient in the [Vaughan identity](vaughan-s-identity.md) has the displayed pointwise bound. Grouping its long-divisor term as $\mu_{>U}(m)b(n)$, with $b(n)=\sum_{d\mid n,d>V}\Lambda(d)$, gives $0\le b(n)\le\log n$. This grouping permits bounded-coefficient [Type II sums](type-ii-sum.md) after division by $\log N$.
