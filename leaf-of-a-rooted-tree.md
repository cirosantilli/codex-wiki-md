# Leaf of a rooted tree

↑ **Parent:** [Rooted tree](rooted-tree.md)

A node with no [children in a rooted tree](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). This depends on the chosen root: the root of a nontrivial [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) can have undirected [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) one while still having a child and therefore not being a leaf in the rooted sense. In a single-node [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md), the root is a leaf. The distinction matters when counting the complement of the [internal vertices of a rooted tree](internal-vertex-of-a-rooted-tree.md).
