# Vanishing-viscosity limit

↑ **Parent:** [Damped-driven Navier-Stokes equation](damped-driven-navier-stokes-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanishing-viscosity_limit)

A vanishing-viscosity limit studies solutions as the viscosity coefficient tends to zero. Uniform estimates and compactness can make the viscous term disappear weakly while retaining enough strong convergence to pass nonlinear transport terms to an inviscid equation.
