# Photon-gluon colour orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Color average](color-average.md)

In distinct-flavour [quark](quark.md) annihilation, the one-[photon](photon.md) exchange [color charge](color-charge.md) tensor is $\delta_{ji}\delta_{kl}$, whereas the one-[gluon](gluon.md) tensor is $\sum_AT^A_{ji}T^A_{kl}$. Their inner product after the external [color charge](color-charge.md) sum is $\sum_A\operatorname{tr}(T^A)\operatorname{tr}(T^A)=0$, since the fundamental [gauge generators](gauge-generator.md) of $SU(3)$ are traceless. Thus the averaged squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) has no leading electromagnetic–strong interference term for this process. Fixed external [color charge](color-charge.md) components, or processes with different exchange pairings, need not share this cancellation.
