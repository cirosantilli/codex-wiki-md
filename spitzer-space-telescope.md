# Spitzer Space Telescope

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric observatory](exoplanet-atmospheric-observatory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spitzer_Space_Telescope)

The Spitzer Space Telescope provided infrared observations of [exoplanet secondary eclipses](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md) and [exoplanet thermal phase curves](exoplanet-thermal-phase-curve.md). Its original mission included infrared spectroscopy; its warm mission retained the 3.6 and 4.5 micrometre imaging bands.
