# Radiocarbon calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Radiocarbon dating](radiocarbon-dating.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiocarbon_calibration)

[Radiocarbon calibration](radiocarbon-calibration.md) converts conventional [radiocarbon ages](radiocarbon-age.md) into calendar-age information using independently dated material and models of changes in reservoir isotope ratios. Conventional ages use the historical $5568$-year [half-life](half-life.md), whereas the physical [carbon-14](carbon-14.md) [half-life](half-life.md) is about $5730$ years.[Half-life conventions, changing atmospheric ratios and tree-ring calibration](radiocarbon-calibration-by-oxford-radiocarbon-accelerator-unit.md)
