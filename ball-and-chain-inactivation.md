# Ball-and-chain inactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball-and-chain_inactivation)

A tethered intracellular particle blocks an open [ion channel](ion-channel.md) from the cytoplasmic side. In classical Shaker-type channels the particle is N-terminal: removing it suppresses fast inactivation, while cytoplasmic application of a corresponding peptide can restore block. The pore-blocking mechanism differs from ordinary activation-gate closure.
