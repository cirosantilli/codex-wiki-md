# Dissipation range

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The [dissipation range](dissipation-range.md) contains scales at which [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) appreciably affects [velocity gradients](velocity-gradient.md) and converts [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) into heat. In a high-Reynolds-number three-dimensional cascade its characteristic length and time are the [Kolmogorov microscales](kolmogorov-microscales.md). Intense vortex cores can be intermittent within this range.
