# Chiral conformal-gauge BRST nilpotence is on shell

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet ghost action](worldsheet-ghost-action.md)

In [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md), let the odd [left-acting BRST differential](left-acting-brst-differential.md) act by $sX=c^+\partial_+X+c^-\partial_-X$ and $sc^\pm=c^\pm\partial_\pm c^\pm$. The [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) gives the displayed expression for $s^2X$. It vanishes using the ghost [equations of motion](equation-of-motion.md) $\partial_-c^+=\partial_+c^-=0$, but it is not zero for arbitrary off-shell fields. For independent [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) $\xi,\rho$, take $c^+=\xi$, $c^-=\rho\sigma^+$ and $X=\sigma^-$; then $s^2X=-\xi\rho\ne0$. Each ghost itself satisfies $s^2c^\pm=0$ algebraically. With the eliminated-antighost transformation $sb_{\pm\pm}=i\theta_{\pm\pm}/\alpha'$ and $s\theta_{\pm\pm}=0$ on the solution space, the antighosts are nilpotent too. This on-shell closure is compatible with an off-shell total-derivative variation of the gauge-fixed action. A full off-shell [BRST quantization](brst-quantization.md) uses the complete gauge transformation and, where required, auxiliary fields rather than silently dropping mixed ghost derivatives.
