# Trace reduction in a ramified local extension

↑ **Parent:** [Field trace](field-trace.md)

For an [integral element](integral-element.md) of a finite extension of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md), reduce the multiplication endomorphism on $\mathcal O_L$ modulo a base [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). Its filtration by the powers of the maximal ideal has $e$ successive quotients, each the [residue field](residue-field.md) $\kappa_L$. Multiplication by $a$ induces multiplication by $\bar a$ on each quotient. Additivity of the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) through the filtration proves the formula. If the [residue characteristic](residue-characteristic.md) divides the [ramification index](ramification-index.md), the trace of every integral element lies in the base maximal ideal; a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) is not required.
