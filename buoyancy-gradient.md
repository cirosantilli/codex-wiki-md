# Buoyancy gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

The spatial [gradient](gradient.md) of [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) determines the buoyancy anomaly produced by a small parcel [displacement](displacement.md): $b'=-\boldsymbol\xi\cdot\nabla\bar b$. Its vertical component is the squared [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) in a stably stratified [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md). A horizontal component enters [thermal wind](thermal-wind.md) and the propagation of a [Boundary Rossby wave](boundary-rossby-wave.md).
