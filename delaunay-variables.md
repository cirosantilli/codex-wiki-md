# Delaunay variables

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

For a bound noncollision [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with gravitational parameter $\mu$, the canonical actions per unit mass are $\mathcal L=\sqrt{\mu a}$, $\mathcal G=\mathcal L\sqrt{1-e^2}$ and $\mathcal H=\mathcal G\cos i$. Their angles are [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md) $\ell$, [argument of periapsis](argument-of-periapsis.md) $\omega$ and [longitude of ascending node](longitude-of-ascending-node.md) $\Omega$. The [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $-\mu^2/(2\mathcal L^2)$, so only $\ell$ advances in the unperturbed problem. In the prograde spherical-action chart, $J_r=\mathcal L-\mathcal G$, $J_\theta=\mathcal G-\mathcal H$, $J_\phi=\mathcal H$; the type-two [canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md) is generated by $F_2=(\mathcal L-\mathcal G)w_r+(\mathcal G-\mathcal H)w_\theta+\mathcal H w_\phi$. Retrograde spherical actions have $J_\theta=\mathcal G-|\mathcal H|$, so the absolute value cannot be dropped globally. Circular and coplanar limits are singular angle charts, not singular orbits.
