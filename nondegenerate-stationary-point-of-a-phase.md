# Nondegenerate stationary point of a phase

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

A [stationary point](stationary-point.md) $x_0$ of a [smooth](smooth-function.md) phase $\phi$ is nondegenerate when its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) at $x_0$ is an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md). In one dimension, this condition is $\phi''(x_0)\ne0$.
