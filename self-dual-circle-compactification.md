# Self-dual circle compactification

↑ **Parent:** [T-duality](t-duality.md)

A circle compactification is self-dual when the radius is unchanged by the [T-duality](t-duality.md) map $R\mapsto\alpha'/R$. Momentum and winding then enter the [bosonic string mass spectrum](bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md) with equal weights. Special charge sectors can become massless at this radius.
