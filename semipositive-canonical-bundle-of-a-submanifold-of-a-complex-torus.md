# Semipositive canonical bundle of a submanifold of a complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Complex torus](complex-torus.md)

The tangent bundle of a [complex torus](complex-torus.md) is holomorphically trivial with a flat [Hermitian metric](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md). Apply the [curvature formula for a holomorphic subbundle](curvature-formula-for-a-holomorphic-subbundle.md) to $TV\subset TM|_V$: its determinant dual $K_V$ has semipositive curvature. For a closed smooth hypersurface, [adjunction for a smooth submanifold](adjunction-for-a-smooth-submanifold.md) identifies $K_V$ with the restriction of its [divisor line bundle](divisor-line-bundle.md). Thus a positive ambient line bundle remains positive on the hypersurface after tensoring by any nonnegative power of the restricted divisor bundle.
