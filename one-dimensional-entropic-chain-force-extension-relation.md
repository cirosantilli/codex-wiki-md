# One-dimensional entropic chain force-extension relation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

For $N$ independent links pointing left or right with length $a$, fixed signed extension $L$ gives $N_R=(N+L/a)/2$ and $N_L=(N-L/a)/2$. The multiplicity is $\binom N{N_L}$ when these counts are nonnegative integers. Leading [Stirling's approximation](stirling-formula.md) gives $\mathcal F=(k_BT/(2a))\ln(N_R/N_L)$ as the externally applied [tension](tension.md) conjugate to $L$. Its small-extension limit is $k_BT L/(Na^2)$. At fixed tension, the exact one-link [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $2\cosh(\beta\mathcal Fa)$ and the mean extension is $Na\tanh(\beta\mathcal Fa)$, confirming the same inverse relation without using Stirling at the full-extension endpoint.
