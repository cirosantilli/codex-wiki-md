# Permutation character of a coset action

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation representation](permutation-representation.md)

For a subgroup $H\leq G$, the permutation character of the [coset action](coset-action.md) on $G/H$ is the induced trivial character

$$
\pi_{G/H}=1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G,
$$

and

$$
\pi_{G/H}(g)
=|\{xH:gxH=xH\}|
=\frac1{|H|}|\{x\in G:x^{-1}gx\in H\}|.
$$
