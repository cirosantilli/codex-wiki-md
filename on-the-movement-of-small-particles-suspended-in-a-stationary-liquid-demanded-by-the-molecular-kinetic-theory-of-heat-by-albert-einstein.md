# On the Movement of Small Particles Suspended in a Stationary Liquid Demanded by the Molecular-Kinetic Theory of Heat by Albert Einstein

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Albert Einstein](work-by-albert-einstein.md)

Primary paper, in the English translation collected in Investigations on the Theory of the Brownian Movement: [https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/lectures/Rotman_Summer_School_2013/Einstein_1905_docs/Einstein_Brownian_English.pdf](https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/lectures/Rotman_Summer_School_2013/Einstein_1905_docs/Einstein_Brownian_English.pdf)

Sections 3–5 connect [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) drift and [diffusion](diffusion.md), the spherical-particle [drag coefficient](drag-coefficient.md), and mean-squared displacement. The linked scan includes the 1926 translation reprinted in the 1956 Dover edition; the research paper itself is dated 1905.
