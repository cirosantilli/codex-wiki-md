# Dirac action

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

The free Dirac action is

$$
S_D=\int d^4x\,\bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-m)\psi.
$$

Its Euler-Lagrange equations are the Dirac equation and its adjoint.
