# Negative resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_resistance)

A negative ratio of terminal voltage to entering [electric current](electric-current.md), or a negative differential slope in a more general terminal relation. A linear [dependent electrical source](dependent-electrical-source.md) can produce a negative [Thévenin resistance](thevenin-resistance.md) without any of the network's ordinary [resistors](resistor.md) having a negative value.
