# lin-4 microRNA

↑ **Parent:** [MicroRNA](microrna.md)

A [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) [microRNA](microrna.md) regulating [developmental timing](developmental-timing.md) through targets including [lin-14](lin-14.md). It demonstrates developmental use of a small [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) rather than only defence against foreign sequences.
