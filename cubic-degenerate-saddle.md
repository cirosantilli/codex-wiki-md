# Cubic degenerate saddle

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate critical point](degenerate-critical-point.md)

At the origin the [gradient](gradient.md) vanishes and the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(0,2)$, which is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). Nevertheless, $F(t,0)=t^3$ takes both signs arbitrarily close to the origin. Thus the origin is a [saddle point of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md). This example shows why a zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) prevents the ordinary [second-derivative test](second-derivative-test.md) from establishing a [local minimum](local-minimum.md).
