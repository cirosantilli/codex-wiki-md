# Birth-death master equation

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death process](birth-death-process.md)

For birth rates $b_n$, death rates $d_n$, and state probabilities $P_n$,

$$
\dot P_n=b_{n-1}P_{n-1}+d_{n+1}P_{n+1}-(b_n+d_n)P_n.
$$
