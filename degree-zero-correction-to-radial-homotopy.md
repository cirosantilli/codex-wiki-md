# Degree-zero correction to radial homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Radial homotopy operator](radial-homotopy-operator.md)

For radial contraction on a star-shaped domain, the homotopy formula is the identity in positive form degrees because pullback by the constant map vanishes there. On functions it is $h_1df=f-f(0)$. A nonzero constant is a counterexample to the uncorrected degree-zero identity. The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) is therefore an exactness assertion in positive degrees, while closed degree-zero forms are locally constant.
