# Linkage isomerism

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

[Linkage isomerism](linkage-isomerism.md) occurs when complexes have the same composition but an [ambidentate ligand](ambidentate-ligand.md) attaches through different atoms. N-bound and O-bound [nitrite](nitrite.md) provide the classical example. This is a difference in attachment connectivity, rather than the different spatial arrangements of unchanged connectivity in [stereoisomers](stereoisomer.md).
