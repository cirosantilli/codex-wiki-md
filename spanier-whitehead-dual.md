# Spanier-Whitehead dual

↑ **Parent:** [Finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md)

The [Spanier-Whitehead dual](spanier-whitehead-dual.md) of a [finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md) $A$ is $A^\vee=F(A,\mathbb S)$. It is finite, and evaluation and coevaluation give the natural [Spanier-Whitehead duality](spanier-whitehead-duality.md) adjunction.
