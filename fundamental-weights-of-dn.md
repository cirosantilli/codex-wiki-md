# Fundamental weights of Dn

↑ **Parent:** [Dn root system](dn-root-system.md)

With [simple roots](simple-root.md) $\alpha_i=\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_{i+1}$ for $i<n$ and $\alpha_n=\varepsilon_{n-1}+\varepsilon_n$, the [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) are $\omega_i=\varepsilon_1+\cdots+\varepsilon_i$ for $i\leq n-2$, followed by $\omega_{n-1}=(\varepsilon_1+\cdots+\varepsilon_{n-1}-\varepsilon_n)/2$ and $\omega_n=(\varepsilon_1+\cdots+\varepsilon_n)/2$. All roots have squared length two, so their pairings with [coroots](coroot.md) verify the dual-basis identities. The last two weights are the half-spin highest weights; for $n=2$, the defining highest weight is $\omega_1+\omega_2$.
