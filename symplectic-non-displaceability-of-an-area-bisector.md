# Symplectic non-displaceability of an area bisector

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian displacement (symplectic geometry)](lagrangian-displacement-symplectic-geometry.md)

A simple closed curve dividing a symplectic two-sphere into two regions of equal area cannot be displaced by a [symplectic isotopy](symplectic-isotopy.md). Any disjoint image would lie in one complementary disc, yet it would still have to bound a disc of half the total area.
