# Open addressing

↑ **Parent:** [Hash table](hash-table.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_addressing)

A [hash table](hash-table.md) collision-handling method storing at most one live record per [array](array-data-structure.md) slot. A [hash function](hash-function.md) chooses a starting slot, and a probe rule chooses further slots. Unlike [separate chaining](separate-chaining.md), records occupy the slots themselves.
