# Sqube

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)

A number $p^2q^3$ for distinct [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md) $p,q$. The ordering of the two [primes](prime-number-split.md) matters because their exponents differ. [Unique prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) identifies the ordered pair from the resulting [integer](integer-split.md).
