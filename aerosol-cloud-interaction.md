# Aerosol-cloud interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

A change in [cloud](cloud.md) microphysics or [cloud](cloud.md) properties caused by [aerosol](aerosol.md), including droplet activation, precipitation changes and adjustments of [water](water.md) content or coverage. The [Twomey effect](twomey-effect.md) isolates a fixed-water-content optical response; it does not by itself establish the sign or magnitude of every [cloud](cloud.md) adjustment.
