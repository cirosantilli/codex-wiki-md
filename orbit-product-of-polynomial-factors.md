# Orbit product of polynomial factors

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

A [finite group](finite-group.md) permutes the associate classes of irreducible [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) factors of an invariant. The product of one representative from each orbit transforms by a scalar [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of the group. If that [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is trivial, the product is invariant. Its transitive factor orbit makes it prime in the invariant ring: an invariant [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) divisible by one orbit factor is divisible by all of them.
