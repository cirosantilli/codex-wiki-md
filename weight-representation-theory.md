# Weight (representation theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_(representation_theory))

A weight of a representation is a simultaneous eigenvalue functional for a commuting Cartan subalgebra. In general algebra representations it can be expressed as a one-dimensional character of the acting commutative algebra. A [weight vector](weight-vector.md) is an eigenvector carrying that functional; a [weight space](weight-space.md) is the corresponding eigenspace, and [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) is its dimension.
