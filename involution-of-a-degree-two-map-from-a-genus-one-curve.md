# Involution of a degree-two map from a genus one curve

↑ **Parent:** [Abel-Jacobi map of a genus-one curve](abel-jacobi-map-of-a-genus-one-curve.md)

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two, a degree-two map from a smooth [genus one curve](genus-one-curve.md) to the [projective line](projective-line.md) has a deck involution. After choosing an origin, all its fiber [divisors on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) are linearly equivalent and have the same group sum $s$, by the [Abel-Jacobi map of a genus-one curve](abel-jacobi-map-of-a-genus-one-curve.md). The involution is consequently $p\mapsto s-p$, including its fixed ramification points. Composing two such involutions gives a [translation on an elliptic curve](translation-on-an-elliptic-curve.md).
