# Reflexive space

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflexive_space)

A locally convex [topological vector space](topological-vector-space-split.md) is reflexive when its canonical evaluation map to its continuous bidual is an isomorphism with the specified strong dual topologies. For a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), this reduces to surjectivity of the canonical map and gives a [reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md).
