# Polar-wave deformation-radius identifiability

↑ **Parent:** [Polar gamma-plane Rossby wave](polar-gamma-plane-rossby-wave.md)

For a [polar gamma-plane Rossby wave](polar-gamma-plane-rossby-wave.md), the drift fixes $\alpha^2=K^2+L_d^{-2}$. It does not separately fix radial [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $K$ and [Rossby deformation radius](rossby-deformation-radius.md) $L_d$. Scaling an eigenfunction changes its amplitude without changing either wavenumber, so a meander amplitude cannot supply the missing radial wavelength. A radial boundary condition or a separately justified envelope approximation is needed to determine $L_d$.
