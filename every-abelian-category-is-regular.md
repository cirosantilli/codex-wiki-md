# Every abelian category is regular

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

An abelian category has finite limits, and every morphism factors through its image as an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism. Every epimorphism is the cokernel of its kernel, hence a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md), and epimorphisms in an abelian category are stable under pullback. Therefore every abelian category is a [regular category](regular-category.md).
