# FRET sensitized emission

↑ **Parent:** [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md)

[FRET sensitized emission](fret-sensitized-emission.md) is [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md) [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) caused by transfer from an excited [FRET donor](fret-donor.md). Excite predominantly the [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) and collect both emission bands. Reduced [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) emission and increased [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md) emission are useful together, but an acceptor-to-donor emission [ratio](ratio.md) is not automatically the [FRET efficiency](fret-efficiency.md): brightness, label abundance and channel collection also enter.[§3.4.2: sensitized emission and dependence on optical correction and label concentrations](advanced-fluorescence-microscopy-techniques-frap-flip-flap-fret-and-flim-by-hellen-c-ishikawa-ankerhold-and-others.md)
