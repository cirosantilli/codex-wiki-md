# Ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Division (mathematics)](division-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratio)

A ratio compares two quantities $a,b$, written $a:b$ and corresponding to [division](division-mathematics.md) $a/b$ when $b\ne0$. Equal [ratios](ratio.md) of corresponding [lengths](length.md) characterize the scaling of [similar triangles](similar-triangle.md).

For example, $6:4$ and $3:2$ specify the same [ratio](ratio.md) because $6/4=3/2$. Multiplying both entries by the same nonzero scale preserves the [quotient](quotient.md): $(ca)/(cb)=a/b$. This is useful when comparing mixtures or scaling a recipe; doubling both ingredient amounts preserves their [ratio](ratio.md), while adding the same amount to both generally does not. For quantities with the same physical units, their [ratio](ratio.md) is [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md), connecting this elementary calculation with [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md).
