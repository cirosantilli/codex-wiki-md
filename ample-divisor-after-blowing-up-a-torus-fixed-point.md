# Ample divisor after blowing up a torus-fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Toric blowup at a torus-fixed point](toric-blowup-at-a-torus-fixed-point.md)

For an ample invariant divisor $D$ and the blowup $\pi$ of a torus-fixed smooth point in dimension at least two, $c\pi^*D-E$ is ample for sufficiently large integers $c$. In the subdivided cone, its local characters are $cm_\sigma+u_i$, where $u_i$ is dual to the omitted ray generator. The new strict inequalities have gap one on the omitted ray and grow linearly with $c$ elsewhere, proving the claim by the [toric ampleness criterion](toric-ampleness-criterion.md).
