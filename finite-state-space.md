# Finite state space

↑ **Parent:** [State space](state-space.md)

A finite set of possible complete states of a system. Its probability distribution is a finite vector. A [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain-split.md) on such a space has a matrix generator with nonnegative off-diagonal rates and zero column sums in the column-vector convention.
