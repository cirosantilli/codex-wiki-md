# Poisson moderate deviation principle

↑ **Parent:** [Moderate deviation principle](moderate-deviation-principle.md)

For a [Poisson random variable](poisson-distribution.md) $S_L$ of mean $L\lambda>0$ and $0<\beta<1$, the scaled [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $\lambda L^{1-\beta}(e^{\theta L^{(\beta-1)/2}}-1-\theta L^{(\beta-1)/2})$. Its [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) tends to $\lambda\theta^2/2$ for every real $\theta$. The [Gärtner–Ellis theorem](gartner-ellis-theorem.md) applies because the limit is finite and differentiable everywhere. Its [Legendre-Fenchel transform](convex-conjugate.md) is the displayed quadratic [good rate function](good-rate-function.md).
