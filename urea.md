# Urea

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urea)

Urea is the [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) with [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) $\mathrm{CH}_4\mathrm N_2\mathrm O$ and structure $(\mathrm{NH}_2)_2\mathrm{CO}$. See the [NIST Chemistry WebBook, urea: formula and structure](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C57136&Mask=20). Concentrated urea is used to promote [protein denaturation](denaturation-biochemistry.md) in the [ribonuclease A refolding experiment](ribonuclease-a-refolding-experiment.md).
