# Reduced temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Critical exponent](critical-exponent.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_temperature)

The reduced temperature is a dimensionless distance from a critical temperature. Multiplying it by a nonzero constant does not change any [critical exponent](critical-exponent.md).
