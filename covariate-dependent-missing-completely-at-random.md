# Covariate-dependent missing completely at random

↑ **Parent:** [Missing-data mechanism](missing-data-mechanism.md)

Missingness depends only on fully observed [covariates](covariate.md) $X$, not on the response after conditioning on them. A responder-only marginal estimate may still be biased when response rates and outcome probabilities both vary across [covariate](covariate.md) strata. [MAR standardization over a fully observed covariate](mar-standardization-over-a-fully-observed-covariate.md) weights within-stratum respondent estimates using [covariate](covariate.md) frequencies from the whole target cohort.
