# Curvaton mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Curvaton](curvaton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curvaton_mechanism)

In the curvaton mechanism, the curvaton's relative energy density grows after inflation and its initially isocurvature fluctuation becomes an adiabatic curvature fluctuation when the curvaton decays.
