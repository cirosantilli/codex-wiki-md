# Bromwich inversion formula

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

Under suitable regularity and growth conditions, a function with [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) $F$ is recovered by the inverse contour integral

$$
f(t)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\gamma-i\infty}^{\gamma+i\infty}e^{st}F(s)\,ds,
$$

where the vertical line lies to the right of every singularity of $F$.
