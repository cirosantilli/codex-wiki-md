# G-factor (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-factor_(physics))

A dimensionless factor relating a system's [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) to its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), with a specified charge and mass convention. Electron [spin](spin.md), orbital motion, and a combined atomic level have different [g-factors](g-factor-physics.md); the convention must be stated before comparing them.
