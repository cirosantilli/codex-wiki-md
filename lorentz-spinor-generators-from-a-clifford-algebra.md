# Lorentz-spinor generators from a Clifford algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

In a real-generator convention, $M^{\rho\sigma}=\frac14[\gamma^\rho,\gamma^\sigma]$ obeys $[M^{\rho\sigma},\gamma^\mu]=g^{\sigma\mu}\gamma^\rho-g^{\rho\mu}\gamma^\sigma$. Applying this identity to the commutator of two gamma matrices proves the [Lorentz algebra](lorentz-algebra.md) relations. With the matching vector generators and real antisymmetric parameters, $S=\exp(\frac12\Omega_{\rho\sigma}M^{\rho\sigma})$ obeys $S^{-1}\gamma^\mu S=\Lambda^\mu{}_{\nu}\gamma^\nu$. A convention using Hermitian Lorentz generators instead must consistently insert factors of $i$.
