# One-loop quartic scalar beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic interaction](quartic-interaction.md)

With interaction $\lambda\phi^4/4!$ for one real scalar, the three one-loop four-point bubble channels give the four-dimensional [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) $\beta(\lambda)=3\lambda^2/(16\pi^2)+O(\lambda^3)$. Its positive sign for $\lambda>0$ excludes [asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md) and produces a perturbative [Landau pole](landau-pole.md) on extrapolation.
