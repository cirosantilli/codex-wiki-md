# Cubic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial equation](polynomial-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_equation)

A cubic equation is a [polynomial equation](polynomial-equation.md) of [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) three, $ax^3+bx^2+cx+d=0$, with $a\ne0$. Its three complex [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md), counted with [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md), need not all have physical significance in an application; a model often selects a branch by continuity from a known limit.
