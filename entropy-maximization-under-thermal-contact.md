# Entropy maximization under thermal contact

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md)

If two otherwise isolated systems freely exchange energy while $E_1+E_2$ remains fixed, their total entropy is

$$
S_{\mathrm{tot}}(E_1)=S_1(E_1)+S_2(E-E_1).
$$

At an interior maximum, $\partial S_1/\partial E_1=\partial S_2/\partial E_2$. Since [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) satisfies $1/T=\partial S/\partial E$, the systems have equal temperatures at [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md).
