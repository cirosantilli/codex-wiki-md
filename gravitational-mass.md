# Gravitational mass

↑ **Parent:** [Mass](mass.md)

Passive gravitational [mass](mass.md) is the coefficient in $\mathbf F=m_{\rm g}\mathbf g$ for an imposed [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). Active gravitational [mass](mass.md) instead measures a body's contribution to the source of that field in [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md). Universality of the ratio of passive gravitational [mass](mass.md) to [inertial mass](inertial-mass.md) is the Newtonian content of the [weak equivalence principle](weak-equivalence-principle.md). In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), the source is the full [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md), rather than a single scalar gravitational [mass](mass.md).
