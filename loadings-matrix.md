# Loadings matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Principal component loading](principal-component-loading.md)

In [principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md), the [matrix](matrix.md) whose columns are the chosen orthonormal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of a [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), ordered by decreasing [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Under this convention the [principal component scores](principal-component-score.md) of centered data are $Z=X_cV$. Some literature instead scales the vectors by square roots of eigenvalues; the convention should be stated explicitly.
