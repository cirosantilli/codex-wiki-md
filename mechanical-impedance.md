# Mechanical impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_impedance)

[Mechanical impedance](mechanical-impedance.md) is the ratio of a driving [force](force-split.md) [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) to the resulting [velocity](velocity.md) [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) at a specified point and in a specified direction. Its units are $\mathrm{N\,s\,m^{-1}}$. The direction of the [force](force-split.md), the time convention and whether one is describing a travelling component or a total field must be stated. A positive real travelling-wave [mechanical impedance](mechanical-impedance.md) transfers mean [power](power.md) $\langle P\rangle=Z|v|^2/2$ when $|v|$ is the peak [velocity](velocity.md) [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md).
