# Poly-infinite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md)

A poly-infinite cyclic group has a finite subnormal series all of whose nontrivial factors are [infinite cyclic groups](infinite-cyclic-group.md). Intersecting its series with a subgroup again gives trivial or infinite cyclic factors; repetitions may be deleted. Splicing a kernel series with the preimage of a quotient series proves closure under extensions. Such groups are [finitely presented groups](finitely-presented-group.md): induct on the series, split the last extension by an infinite cyclic quotient, and use [finite presentation of a semidirect product](finite-presentation-of-a-semidirect-product.md).
