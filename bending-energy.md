# Bending energy

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic energy](elastic-energy.md)

The part of an [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md)'s [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) that penalizes departure of its [bending strain of a rod](bending-strain-of-a-rod.md) $u$ from the intrinsic value $\hat u$. Here $B$ is the [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) and $S$ is reference [arc length](arc-length.md). For an [inextensible rod](inextensible-rod.md), the same formula can be written using spatial [curvature](curvature.md) and current [arc length](arc-length.md).
