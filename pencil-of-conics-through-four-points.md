# Pencil of conics through four points

↑ **Parent:** [Projective conic](projective-conic.md)

Four [projective points](projective-point.md) in [general linear position](general-linear-position.md) in the complex projective plane impose four independent linear conditions on the six coefficients of a [quadratic polynomial](quadratic-polynomial.md). To see independence, put them at the three coordinate points and $[1:1:1]$ using a [projective frame](projective-frame.md). The remaining equations are $Axy+Bxz+Cyz=0$ with $A+B+C=0$, a two-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Projectivizing its nonzero elements gives a pencil. It has nonsingular bases, for example the forms with coefficient triples $(1,1,-2)$ and $(1,-2,1)$, because the determinant of the symmetric coefficient matrix is $ABC/4$. Its three singular members are exactly the three pairs of lines obtained by partitioning the four base points into two pairs.
