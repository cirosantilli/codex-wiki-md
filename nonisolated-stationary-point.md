# Nonisolated stationary point

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

A [stationary point](stationary-point.md) is nonisolated if every neighbourhood contains other [stationary points](stationary-point.md). For a twice [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md), a regular smooth curve of [stationary points](stationary-point.md) through a point forces its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) to annihilate the curve's nonzero tangent: differentiate the vanishing [gradient](gradient.md) along the curve. Thus the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) has a zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) there, but that alone need not determine whether the point is a [local minimum](local-minimum.md) or [local maximum](local-maximum.md).
