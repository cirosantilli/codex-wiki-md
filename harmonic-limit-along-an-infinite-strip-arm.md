# Harmonic limit along an infinite strip arm

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded harmonic representation with almost sure Brownian exit](bounded-harmonic-representation-with-almost-sure-brownian-exit.md)

Suppose an unbounded domain contains the upper strip $(-1,1)\times(1,\infty)$ with its two vertical sides on the boundary. A bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) with constant values $a$ on the right side and $b$ on the left side, and with valid [bounded harmonic representation with almost sure Brownian exit](bounded-harmonic-representation-with-almost-sure-brownian-exit.md), satisfies $u(x,y)\to a(1+x)/2+b(1-x)/2$ as $y\to\infty$. The horizontal [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md) is finite; on that finite random interval the vertical Brownian path has a finite minimum. Consequently reaching the bottom before a side has probability tending to zero. The remaining two exit probabilities are the one-dimensional interval probabilities.
