# Schumacher compression

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schumacher_compression)

Schumacher compression is quantum source coding into the typical subspace of $\rho^{\otimes n}$. Asymptotically reliable compression is possible at every qubit rate above the [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) $S(\rho)$.
