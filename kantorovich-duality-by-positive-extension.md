# Kantorovich duality by positive extension

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich duality theorem](kantorovich-duality-theorem.md)

For compact metric spaces and a continuous cost, define the marginal functional $\ell(f(x)+g(y))=\int f\,dP+\int g\,dQ$. Its majorant envelope $p(h)=\inf_{s\geq h}\ell(s)$ is finite and sublinear. The dual value is $-p(-c)$. Assign that value to the cost direction and use the dominated [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) to obtain a [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) on all continuous functions of two variables. The [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) turns it into an optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md) with the prescribed marginals.
