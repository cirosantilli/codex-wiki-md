# Nonlinear stability at the Ricker flip threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Survival-augmented Ricker map](survival-augmented-ricker-map.md)

At the upper multiplier-$-1$ threshold of $x\mapsto sx+kxe^{-\gamma x}$, set $u=\gamma(x-x_*)$. The second iterate is $u-2(s^2-s/2+1/6)u^3+O(u^4)$. Since its cubic damping coefficient is positive for $0\leq s<1$, this endpoint is locally [asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md) despite the inconclusive linear multiplier.
