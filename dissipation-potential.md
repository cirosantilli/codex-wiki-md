# Dissipation potential

↑ **Parent:** [Internal variable](internal-variable.md)

A force-dependent potential specifies internal-variable rates by differentiation. Convexity with a minimum at zero force can ensure nonnegative force-rate dissipation. The name alone does not ensure the [Clausius-Duhem inequality](clausius-duhem-inequality.md) for an arbitrary potential.
