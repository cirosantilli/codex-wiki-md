# Geopotential

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopotential)

The [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) per unit [mass](mass.md), including the centrifugal contribution when using the effective gravity of a rotating planet. In a local atmosphere with approximately constant effective [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md), $\Phi=gz$ up to a constant. Its horizontal [gradient](gradient.md) on an [isobaric surface](isobaric-surface.md) drives the balanced wind.
