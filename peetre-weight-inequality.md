# Peetre weight inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Japanese bracket](japanese-bracket.md)

For real $s$ and vectors $\xi,\eta$, the displayed bound follows from $\langle a+b\rangle\le\sqrt2\langle a\rangle\langle b\rangle$, applied in the opposite direction when $s<0$. One can take $C_s=2^{|s|/2}$. Combined with [Young's convolution inequality](young-s-convolution-inequality.md), it proves [Sobolev multiplication by a smooth cutoff](sobolev-multiplication-by-a-smooth-cutoff.md) for all real orders: the rapidly decreasing transform of the cutoff absorbs the extra weight. The negative-order case is particularly useful when a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) is not initially a function.
