# Stationary drift saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md)

For an adapted [stationary process](stationary-process.md) with the displayed bounds, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) and stationarity give $\int_0^t(\mathbb E[Z_sg_s]+\lambda)ds=0$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) makes each integrand nonnegative, so equality holds almost everywhere. Then $\mathbb E(g_s+\lambda Z_s)^2\le0$, proving $g=-\lambda Z$ for almost every time and outcome. Thus the process solves the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation](ornstein-uhlenbeck-stochastic-differential-equation.md) and its initial value, rather than stationarity alone, determines its coupling with W.
