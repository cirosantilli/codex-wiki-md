# Radiation-era adiabatic potentials with free-streaming neutrinos

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar neutrino anisotropic stress](scalar-neutrino-anisotropic-stress.md)

For regular [adiabatic initial conditions](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) on [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md) during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), the unweighted [neutrino Boltzmann hierarchy](neutrino-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) gives $\Theta_1=\psi k\eta/2$ and $\Theta_2=\psi(k\eta)^2/6$. With $f_\nu=\bar\rho_\nu/(\bar\rho_\nu+\bar\rho_\gamma)$ and neutrinos the only source of [scalar anisotropic stress](scalar-anisotropic-stress.md), the trace-free [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) gives $\phi=(1+2f_\nu/5)\psi$. For the sign convention $\mathcal R=-\phi-\psi/2$,

$$
\psi=-\frac{10}{15+4f_\nu}\mathcal R,\qquad\phi=-\frac{10+4f_\nu}{15+4f_\nu}\mathcal R.
$$
