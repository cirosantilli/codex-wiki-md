# Search direction

↑ **Parent:** [Line search](line-search.md)

A search direction is the nonzero [vector](vector.md) $p_j$ used in the displayed optimization update. In a [line search](line-search.md) with positive [step size](step-size.md), its orientation matters: a [descent direction](descent-direction.md) has negative [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) of the [objective function](objective-function.md). Minimization over a full line instead permits either sign of $\alpha_j$.
