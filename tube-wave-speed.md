# Tube wave speed

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-area law of a collapsible tube](pressure-area-law-of-a-collapsible-tube.md)

Linearizing area conservation and the axial momentum equation of a [collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md) gives waves travelling relative to the fluid at speeds $\pm c$, where the displayed relation uses the area derivative at fixed external pressure. For $p=P_0+\rho c_0^2(A/A_0)^2/2$, it gives $c=c_0A/A_0$.
