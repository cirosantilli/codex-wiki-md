# Inositol trisphosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Second messenger](second-messenger.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inositol_trisphosphate)

In [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md), [inositol trisphosphate](inositol-trisphosphate.md) usually denotes inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate, a small [second messenger](second-messenger.md) that can release stored [calcium](calcium.md) into the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md). It is distinct from the [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) sensing the extracellular [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) and from the [ion channel](ion-channel.md) carrying the eventual membrane [electric current](electric-current.md). The intracellular-injection experiment under [metabotropic receptor](metabotropic-receptor.md) illustrates that distinction.
