# Interior of the copositive cone

↑ **Parent:** [Copositive cone](copositive-cone.md)

The [interior](interior-topology.md) consists exactly of [strictly copositive matrices](strictly-copositive-matrix.md). If $x^TAx$ has positive minimum $\delta$ on the [compact](compact-space.md) nonnegative [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md), perturbations of [operator norm](operator-norm.md) less than $\delta$ preserve this positive lower bound there. Conversely, if a unit nonnegative [vector](vector.md) has zero [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md), $A-\varepsilon I$ leaves the [copositive cone](copositive-cone.md) for every $\varepsilon>0$. Thus such a [matrix](matrix.md) is not [interior](interior-topology.md). The [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md) is a convenient [interior](interior-topology.md) point.
