<h1 id="dual-robinson-schensted-knuth-correspondence">Dual Robinson–Schensted–Knuth correspondence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Robinson–Schensted correspondence](robinson-schensted-correspondence.md)

For a finite $0$-$1$ matrix, list its occupied pairs by increasing top entry and decreasing bottom entry within a top-entry tie. Perform ordinary semistandard [row insertion](row-insertion.md) on the bottom entries and record top entries in the new cells. The result is a [bijection](bijection.md) with same-shape tableaux $T,U$ for which $T$ and $U^t$ are [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md). The types of $T$ and $U$ are respectively the column-sum and row-sum vectors of the matrix.
