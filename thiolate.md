# Thiolate

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A [thiolate](thiolate.md) is $\mathrm{RS^-}$, the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) of a [thiol](thiol.md). Its polarizable [sulfur](sulfur.md) is often effective in [conjugate addition](conjugate-addition.md). [protonation](protonation.md) of the resulting [carbon](carbon.md) anion can regenerate [thiolate](thiolate.md) from another [thiol](thiol.md) molecule.
