<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The limit near 121.8 nm lies about $hc/(121.8\ \mathrm{nm})\simeq10.18$ eV above its lower term. Subtracting from the approximately $11.88$ eV ground-state [ionization energy](../../../../ionization-energy.md) puts that lower term near $1.70$ eV: the $3d\,{}^2D_{3/2,5/2}$ pair. The first upper levels are the already identified $4p\,{}^2P_{1/2,3/2}$ pair. Their allowed decays are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\lambda&\text{emission assignment}\\\hline
849.8\ \mathrm{nm}&4p\,{}^2P_{3/2}\to3d\,{}^2D_{3/2}\\
854.2\ \mathrm{nm}&4p\,{}^2P_{3/2}\to3d\,{}^2D_{5/2}\\
866.2\ \mathrm{nm}&4p\,{}^2P_{1/2}\to3d\,{}^2D_{3/2}
\end{array}}
$$

The fourth possible pairing, $P_{1/2}\to D_{5/2}$, has $|\Delta J|=2$ and is forbidden as an [electric dipole transition](../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md). The three lines therefore arise from **[doublet terms](../../../../doublet-term.md)**, not a spin-triplet term: “triplet series” counts three [spectral lines](../../../../spectral-line.md).

For example, the first two [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) together with the $4p_{3/2}$ [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) give $E(3d_{3/2})\simeq1.693$ eV and $E(3d_{5/2})\simeq1.700$ eV. The longer 854.2 nm [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) from the same upper level places its lower D level higher. The 866.2 nm transition independently returns the lower D [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) within the rounding precision. Increasing the upper [principal quantum number](../../../../principal-quantum-number.md) gives $np\,{}^2P\to3d\,{}^2D$ triplets with two nearby lower-term ionization limits near 121.8 nm. The arrows are included in [b](../b.md); their emission direction is **P to D**, not the reverse. The independent tabulation confirms the D-term position and ordering.[3d entries at13650.20 and13710.89 inverse centimetres](../../../../energy-levels-of-singly-ionized-calcium-ca-ii-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
