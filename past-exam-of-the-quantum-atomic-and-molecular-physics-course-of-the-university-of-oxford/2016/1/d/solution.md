<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the regime of [weak-field Zeeman splitting](../../../../weak-field-zeeman-splitting.md), $J,m_J$ remain good labels and $\Delta E=\mu_B g_Jm_JB$. For the 393.4 nm line, $J_u=3/2$ in $4p\,{}^2P_{3/2}$ and $J_l=1/2$ in $4s\,{}^2S_{1/2}$. The [Landé g-factors](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) are $g_u=4/3$ and $g_l\simeq2$. Apply $\Delta m_J=0,\pm1$ to the four upper and two lower sublevels. The allowed pairs $(m_u,m_l)$ are

$$
(3/2,1/2),\ (1/2,1/2),\ (-1/2,1/2),\
(1/2,-1/2),\ (-1/2,-1/2),\ (-3/2,-1/2).
$$

Their [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) displacements in units of $\mu_BB/h$ are respectively $1,-1/3,-5/3,5/3,1/3,-1$, so none coincide. Looking perpendicular to the field permits both the $\pi$ radiation from $q=0$ transitions and the $\sigma$ radiation from $q=\pm1$ transitions. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{six distinct components: two }\pi\text{ and four }\sigma.}
$$

A linear [polarizer](../../../../polarizer.md) parallel to the field isolates the two $\pi$ components; a transverse [polarizer](../../../../polarizer.md) selects the four $\sigma$ components. Without [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) selection both sets are present. This count assumes a field small compared with [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md) coupling and neglects isotope-dependent [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md); the strong-field [Paschen-Back effect](../../../../paschen-back-effect.md) or a resolved nonzero-spin isotope would require a different model. The spectral spacings are unnecessary for the count, but the distinct shifts verify that counting allowed transitions does not inadvertently count degenerate lines twice.
