<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the second isotope, consecutive gap differences are approximately $56.4$ MHz, but the smallest gap is about $1.5$ times this value. The higher-$F$ values for the five gaps are therefore $3/2,5/2,7/2,9/2,11/2$. The six levels run from $F=1/2$ to $11/2$. The electronic state is unchanged, so $J=5/2$ as in [d](../d.md); the endpoints give

$$
\boxed{I_{56}=3,\qquad \frac{|A_{56}|}{h}\simeq56.41\ \mathrm{MHz}.}
$$

For example, fitting $\nu_F$ to $F|A|/h$ with the same equal-weight convention gives $56.408$ MHz. The nearly constant gap differences are compatible with the same leading model of the [magnetic-dipole hyperfine interaction](../../../../magnetic-dipole-hyperfine-interaction.md) and the printed rounding.

Within an unchanged electronic state, and neglecting isotope-dependent changes of the electronic [wave function](../../../../wave-function-split.md) and [hyperfine anomaly](../../../../hyperfine-anomaly.md), $A\propto g_I$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac{g_I(^{56}\mathrm{Mn})}{g_I(^{55}\mathrm{Mn})}\right|
\simeq\frac{56.41}{72.42}=0.779,}
$$

or the reciprocal magnitude is about $1.284$. This is a ratio of **[nuclear g-factors](../../../../nuclear-g-factor.md)**, not directly a ratio of [nuclear magnetic moments](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-moment.md): $\mu_I=g_I I\mu_N$, so the moment-magnitude ratio would additionally contain $I_{56}/I_{55}=3/(5/2)$ and is about $0.935$.

The positive transition [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) do not fix either coupling sign. If a signed ratio is required, equal signs must be supplied as an additional assumption or determined by an ordering-sensitive measurement. No absolute [nuclear g-factor](../../../../nuclear-g-factor.md) is inferred without a calibrated electronic field coefficient. The second ladder in [b](../b.md) illustrates the inferred half-integer $F$ sequence without turning that sign ambiguity into a falsely measured ordering.
