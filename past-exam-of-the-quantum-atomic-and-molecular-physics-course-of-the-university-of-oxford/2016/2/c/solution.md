<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Within a fixed isolated electronic $J$ multiplet, the electronic field at the [nucleus](../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) is a vector operator. [Rotational symmetry](../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) and the [Wigner-Eckart theorem](../../../../wigner-eckart-theorem.md) imply that its projection into this multiplet is proportional to $\mathbf J$:

$$
P_J\mathbf B_eP_J=b_J\mathbf J.
$$

Its detailed orbital, spin-dipolar and contact contributions are included in the reduced coefficient $b_J$. Consequently the projected [magnetic-dipole hyperfine interaction](../../../../magnetic-dipole-hyperfine-interaction.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm hfs}=A\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J,\qquad A=-g_I\mu_Nb_J,}
$$

in the same dimensionless angular-momentum convention as [a](../a.md). The proportionality is a statement about [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md) **within** the chosen electronic manifold, not an identity for the [electron](../../../../electron.md) field operator on the entire atomic Hilbert space.

Define $\mathbf F=\mathbf I+\mathbf J$. In zero external field the [quantum states](../../../../quantum-state.md) obtained by [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) are labelled $|\gamma J,I;F,m_F\rangle$, where $\gamma$ specifies the [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md) and other fixed labels. The good quantum numbers include $I,J,F,m_F$, with $F=|I-J|,\ldots,I+J$ and $m_F=-F,\ldots,F$. In a description using [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md) the fixed electronic labels can also include $L,S$. The [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) depend on $F$ but not $m_F$:

$$
E_F=\frac A2[F(F+1)-I(I+1)-J(J+1)].
$$

This neglects coupling to other electronic manifolds, [electric-quadrupole hyperfine interactions](../../../../electric-quadrupole-hyperfine-interaction.md) and the [Zeeman effect](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) from external fields. Those additions can shift the intervals or change which quantum numbers remain useful.
