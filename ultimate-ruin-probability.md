# Ultimate ruin probability

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

The probability that the insurance surplus in a [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md) becomes negative at some finite time, starting from capital $u\ge0$. Ultimate survival has probability $1-\psi(u)$. A finite-horizon ruin probability instead restricts the crossing time to a specified time interval.
