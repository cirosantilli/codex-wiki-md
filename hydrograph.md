# Hydrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Discharge](discharge.md)

A [hydrograph](hydrograph.md) records [discharge](discharge.md) against time at a fixed location. A nonlinear [kinematic wave](kinematic-wave.md) can steepen an upstream increase into a sharp front; longitudinal [diffusion](diffusion.md) or additional storage rounds the resulting rise.
