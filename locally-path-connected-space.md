# Locally path-connected space

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

Every [locally path-connected space](locally-path-connected-space.md) is a [locally connected space](locally-connected-space.md), but the converse can fail.

A space is locally path-connected when every point has a neighbourhood basis of path-connected open sets. Its path components are open.
