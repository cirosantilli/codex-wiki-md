# Flow definition of the Lie derivative of a tensor field

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

For the [local flow](local-flow.md) $\phi_t$ of a smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$, pull a [tensor field](tensor-field.md) $T$ back to the fixed base point before differentiating. Pullback uses $d\phi_t^{-1}$ on vector factors and the dual of $d\phi_t$ on covector factors. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\mathcal L_Xf=Xf$. In coordinates, $d\phi_t^{-1}Y(\phi_t(x))=Y+t(dY\,X-dX\,Y)+O(t^2)$, so $\mathcal L_XY=[X,Y]$. Differentiation of pullback preserves [tensor contractions](tensor-contraction.md) and obeys the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) for [tensor products](tensor-product.md); hence this is the [tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md) defining the [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md).
