# Face-on frequency shift of a circular Schwarzschild source

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike circular Schwarzschild geodesic](timelike-circular-schwarzschild-geodesic.md)

A source on a [timelike circular Schwarzschild geodesic](timelike-circular-schwarzschild-geodesic.md) at radius $r>3GM/c^2$ emits light to a static observer at infinity on the orbital symmetry axis. Such a ray has zero conserved axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The locally measured photon [energy](energy-split.md) is the contraction of its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) with the observer's [four-velocity](four-velocity.md). Comparing the emitter with the observer at infinity therefore gives $\nu_{\rm em}=U^t\nu_\infty$, where $U^t=[1-3GM/(c^2r)]^{-1/2}$, proving the formula.

Equivalently the [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) factor $\sqrt{1-2GM/(c^2r)}$ multiplies the transverse [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md) factor $1/\gamma$ measured by a local static observer. For $R_S=2GM/c^2\ll r$,

$$
\nu_{\rm em}=\nu_\infty\left[1+\frac{3R_S}{4r}+O\!\left(\frac{R_S^2}{r^2}\right)\right].
$$

The coefficient contains both gravitational and orbital time dilation. It assumes an observer at infinity on the symmetry axis; other viewing directions generally introduce a first-order longitudinal [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md).
