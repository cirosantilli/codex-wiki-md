# Finite sum of simple submodules

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple module](semisimple-module.md)

A finite sum of [simple modules](irreducible-module.md) inside one [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md). Inductively, if $M$ is the sum already retained, then $M\cap S_j$ is a [submodule](submodule.md) of the [simple module](irreducible-module.md) $S_j$, hence zero or $S_j$. In the first case append $S_j$ as a [direct summand](direct-summand.md); in the second it adds nothing. This gives a [direct sum of modules](direct-sum-of-modules.md) selected from the original family.
