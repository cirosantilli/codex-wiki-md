# Dense model theorem for a multiplicative test family

↑ **Parent:** [Transference principle in additive combinatorics](transference-principle-in-additive-combinatorics.md)

Let $\mathcal F$ be a closed, convex, symmetric family of functions into $[-1,1]$ that contains the constant function $1$, and suppose its [dual test-function norm](dual-test-function-norm.md) is submultiplicative under pointwise products. If a nonnegative majorant $\nu$ has average at most one and $\|\nu-1\|_{\mathcal F}$ is exponentially small in $1/\varepsilon$, then every $0\leq g\leq\nu$ has a dense model $0\leq\widetilde g\leq1$ satisfying

$$
\|g-\widetilde g\|_{\mathcal F}\leq\varepsilon.
$$

The proof separates $g$ from the convex set of dense models, then approximates the positive part of the separating test by a polynomial. Submultiplicativity controls every power in that polynomial.
