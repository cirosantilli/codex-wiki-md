# Cotangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotangent_bundle)

The cotangent bundle has the canonical one-form $\lambda_{(q,p)}(v)=p(d\pi(v))$ and the canonical symplectic form $d\lambda$ up to a conventional sign. Its zero section is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md).
