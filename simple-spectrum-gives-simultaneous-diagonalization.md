# Simple spectrum gives simultaneous diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md)

Suppose $D$ has $n$ distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) over a field and $C$ commutes with $D$. Each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $D$ is one-dimensional. Since [commuting maps preserve eigenspaces](commuting-maps-preserve-eigenspaces.md), $C$ acts as a scalar on each of them. The [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of $D$ give a common [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md) and hence [simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md). For real matrices, the result gives a real [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md) only if the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $D$ are real; otherwise it applies over $\mathbb C$.
