# Field with nine elements

↑ **Parent:** [Finite field](finite-field.md)

The [finite field](finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_9$ can be represented as $\mathbb F_3[X]/(X^2+1)$, since the quadratic has no root modulo three. Its elements are $a+b\beta$ for $a,b\in\mathbb F_3$, with $\beta^2=-1$. Multiplication reduces to $(a+b\beta)(c+d\beta)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)\beta$ modulo three.
