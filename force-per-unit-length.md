# Force per unit length

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A [force per unit length](force-per-unit-length.md) describes a distributed force on a filament and has units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. For a charged filament with [linear charge density](linear-charge-density.md) $\lambda$ and longitudinal current $I$, the external [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) is $\mathbf f=\lambda\mathbf E+I\widehat{\mathbf t}\times\mathbf B$. Length, density and force must all refer to the same measurement frame. An infinite uniform filament generally has a finite force density but no finite total force.
