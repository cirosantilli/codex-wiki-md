# Four-handle

↑ **Parent:** [Handle attachment](handle-attachment.md)

A four-handle is an index-four [handle](handle.md). In a four-dimensional [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md) it is a four-ball attached along its whole boundary, a [three-sphere](three-sphere.md). It caps a spherical boundary component left by earlier [handle attachments](handle-attachment.md).
