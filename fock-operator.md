# Fock operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

The self-consistent one-electron operator obtained by varying a [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md). It contains a local [hartree potential](hartree-potential.md) and a nonlocal exchange action. Its occupied [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) define a stationary determinant; its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are Lagrange multipliers and are not by themselves the total many-electron [energy](energy-split.md).
