# Conformal map of a vertical half-strip by sine

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

The [sine](sine.md) maps $\{-\pi/2<\operatorname{Re}z<\pi/2,\ \operatorname{Im}z>0\}$ bijectively onto the upper half-plane: its imaginary part is $\cos(\operatorname{Re}z)\sinh(\operatorname{Im}z)>0$, and the equality of two sine values forces either equal arguments modulo $2\pi$ or arguments summing to an odd multiple of $\pi$. Only equality is possible in this domain. For surjectivity, solve $t^2-2iwt-1=0$ for $t=e^{iz}$. When $\operatorname{Im}w>0$, exactly one root has modulus below one and it has positive real part. The inverse $z=-i\log t$ therefore belongs to the half-strip. Composing with $(w-i)/(w+i)$ gives a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) onto the [unit disc](unit-disc.md).
