# Specht modules as minimal left ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

In the complex symmetric-group algebra, normalize a [Young symmetrizer](young-symmetrizer.md) by $e_t=h_t/H_\lambda$. The [row-column collision lemma](row-column-collision-lemma.md) proves $e_tAe_t=\mathbb Ce_t$, so $e_t$ is a [primitive idempotent](primitive-idempotent.md) and $Ae_t$ is an irreducible left module. Different shapes are separated by the vanishing corner $h_\lambda A h_\mu=0$ for $\lambda>\mu$. Counting [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) then proves that these modules exhaust the [simple modules](irreducible-module.md).
