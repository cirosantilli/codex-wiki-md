# First moment of area

↑ **Parent:** [Centroid](centroid.md)

For a planar region $R$ of finite positive [area](surface-area.md) $A$, its first moments of area about the coordinate axes are the displayed [double integrals](double-integral.md), provided they exist. Its [centroid](centroid.md) is $(\overline x,\overline y)=(M_y/A,M_x/A)$. This follows by applying the definition $\overline{\mathbf r}=A^{-1}\iint_R\mathbf r\,dA$ to each component. The subscript indicates the axis about which the moment is taken: the first moment about the $x$-axis weights by $y$, not $x$.
