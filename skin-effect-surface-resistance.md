# Skin-effect surface resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Skin effect](skin-effect.md)

In the normal skin-effect regime of a good conductor, $\delta=\sqrt{2/(\mu\sigma\omega)}$ and the surface resistance is $R_s=1/(\sigma\delta)=\sqrt{\mu\omega/(2\sigma)}$. For identical geometries, resistance ratios depend on the square root of [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) divided by conductivity.
