# Basic radiance

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of etendue](conservation-of-etendue.md)

[Basic radiance](basic-radiance.md) is [radiance](radiance.md) divided by the square of the [refractive index](refractive-index.md). For [radiance](radiance.md) $L$ at a fixed [wavelength](wavelength.md),

$$
d\Phi=\frac{L}{n^2}\,dG.
$$

Lossless propagation conserves [reduced radiance](basic-radiance.md) along each [light ray](light-ray.md); $L$ itself is conserved only when the endpoint [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) agree. A larger [irradiance](irradiance.md) at a smaller image can result from admitting more directions without increasing [reduced radiance](basic-radiance.md). Uniform [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md) can lower [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) while leaving the occupied geometric [etendue](etendue.md) unchanged; an opaque [aperture](aperture.md) removes part of that occupied set.
