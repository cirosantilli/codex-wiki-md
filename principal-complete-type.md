# Principal complete type

↑ **Parent:** [Isolated type](isolated-type.md)

A [principal complete type](principal-complete-type.md) is isolated by one [first-order consistent](first-order-consistency.md) [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md): modulo the [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md), that [logical formula](logical-formula.md) implies every member of the [partial type](partial-type.md). For [complete types](complete-type.md) this is exactly an [isolated type](isolated-type.md). The qualifier complete distinguishes this notion from [partial types](partial-type.md).
