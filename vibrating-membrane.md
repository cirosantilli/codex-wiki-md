# Vibrating membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

A physical sheet under uniform isotropic tension per unit length T and with [mass](mass.md) per unit area $\rho$ has small transverse displacement satisfying $\partial_t^2\psi=c^2\nabla^2\psi$, where $c^2=T/\rho$. Fixed edges impose zero displacement. This mechanical membrane model neglects bending stiffness, large slopes and [dissipation](dissipation.md).
