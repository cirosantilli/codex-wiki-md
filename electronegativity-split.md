# Electronegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronegativity)

[Electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) describes a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md)'s tendency to attract [electron density](electron-density.md) towards itself in a [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md). It helps answer a useful question: when two different [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) share [electrons](electron.md), which end of the [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) tends to be richer in [electron density](electron-density.md)? For [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md), the answer is [chlorine](chlorine.md): the [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md) is a [polar covalent bond](polar-covalent-bond.md), conventionally written $\mathrm H^{\delta+}-\mathrm{Cl}^{\delta-}$. The symbols indicate [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md), not isolated [ions](ion.md) carrying whole elementary [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md).[§7.2, “Pure vs. Polar Covalent Bonds”, HCl example](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

[Electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) is a comparison scheme, not the number of [electrons](electron.md) on a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). A [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md) depends on how the [electron density](electron-density.md) is partitioned; [formal charge](formal-charge.md) instead follows an integer allocation in a [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md). Neither is numerically equal to [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md). Nor is [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) identical to [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md), which concerns adding an [electron](electron.md) to an isolated species. Different [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) scales use different measured inputs and conventions; compare numbers only after specifying the scale.[electronegativity](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/electronegativity.md)

For a concrete comparison, the Royal Society of Chemistry lists [Pauling electronegativities](pauling-electronegativity.md) $\chi_{\rm P}(\mathrm H)=2.20$ and $\chi_{\rm P}(\mathrm{Cl})=3.16$. Their difference is $0.96$, so the [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) rule predicts [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md) towards [chlorine](chlorine.md) in [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md). An isolated [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) has identical ends and zero [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) difference. These examples explain a direction of unequal sharing, not a quantitative [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md).[Hydrogen — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/hydrogen-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)[Chlorine — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/chlorine-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)

**[Figure 1](#electronegativity-scales-hydrogen-chlorine). Pauling electronegativity and Mulliken electronegativity for hydrogen and chlorine: separate scales, separate units; the Mulliken electronegativity values are calculated from the stated ground-state inputs**.

A larger [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) difference often suggests stronger [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md), but there is no universal numerical boundary where a [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md) suddenly becomes [ionic bonding](ionic-bonding-split.md). Tables of cutoffs are classification aids with exceptions. [Hydrogen fluoride](hydrogen-fluoride.md), for example, is described by a [polar covalent bond](polar-covalent-bond.md) even when its [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) difference exceeds a textbook's nominal ionic threshold. The actual [electron density](electron-density.md), physical setting, and [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)'s structure matter.[§7.2, “Electronegativity and Bond Type”, general-guide warning and HF exception](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

The distinction from [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) is equally useful: an [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) measures the cost of removing an [electron](electron.md) from a specified isolated species, whereas [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) compares attraction for [electron density](electron-density.md) in a bonded environment. Neither the removal cost alone nor the attachment release alone supplies a unique bonded-atom [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md). A scale combines selected measured inputs with a model and a convention.[E01990: multiple definitions; Mulliken average versus Pauling bond-energy differences](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/electronegativity.md)

For an original relative comparison, stipulate fictitious bonded sites $A,B,C$ on one chosen [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) scale:

$$
\chi(A)=\chi_0,\qquad \chi(B)=\chi_0+\Delta\chi,\qquad
\chi(C)=\chi_0+2\Delta\chi,\qquad \Delta\chi>0.
$$

The [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) heuristic predicts [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md) towards $B$ in $A-B$, towards $C$ in $B-C$, and towards $C$ in $A-C$. Shifting $\chi_0$ leaves every comparison unchanged. Although the $A-C$ difference is twice the $A-B$ difference, this does not make its [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md) or [bond dipole](bond-dipole.md) twice as large: those require additional information about [electron density](electron-density.md) and [bond length](bond-length.md). These symbols describe a stipulated ordering, not a table of measured [chemical elements](chemical-element.md).

Keep the chemical context attached to that ordering. A [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) in a [molecule](molecule-split.md) has neighbours and an [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md); an isolated-atom [energy](energy-split.md) scale and a bonded-atom comparison are different constructions. The [chemical environment dependence of electronegativity](chemical-environment-dependence-of-electronegativity.md) explains why a table is a useful approximation rather than an immutable property of every site. Furthermore, an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can induce [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md) even between identical [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), through [electric polarizability](electric-polarizability.md); equal tabulated [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) does not forbid that response.
