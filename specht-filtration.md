# Specht filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

A [Specht filtration](specht-filtration.md) is a finite chain of [submodules](submodule.md) whose successive quotients are [Specht modules](specht-module.md). In positive [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) it need not split and is not a [composition series](composition-series.md), because its Specht factors may themselves be reducible. [Dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) and ordinary lifted [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) can still be added along the filtration.
