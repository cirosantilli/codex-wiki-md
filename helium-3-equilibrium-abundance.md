# Helium-3 equilibrium abundance

↑ **Parent:** [Effective proton-proton reaction network](effective-proton-proton-reaction-network.md)

With $A=\lambda_{33}$, $B=\lambda_{34}n_4$ and $C=\lambda_{11}n_1^2/2$, [helium-3](helium-3.md) evolves as $\dot n_3=C-An_3^2-Bn_3$ after [deuterium](deuterium.md) elimination. The positive root is its unique attracting equilibrium for a fixed background. In the pp-I limit it reduces to $n_1\sqrt{\lambda_{11}/(2\lambda_{33})}$, making equilibrium abundance strongly [temperature](temperature.md) dependent.
