# Closed nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md)

This [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) determines the [closed sublocale](closed-sublocale.md) complementary to the open determined by $a$. It is the least [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) taking $0$ to $a$. In the [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) of [nuclei on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md), $c(a)$ and the [open nucleus](open-nucleus.md) $o(a)$ are complements.
