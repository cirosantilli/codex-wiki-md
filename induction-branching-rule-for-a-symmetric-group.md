# Induction branching rule for a symmetric group

↑ **Parent:** [Restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md)

In [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), inducing a [Specht module](specht-module.md) from $\Sigma_{n-1}$ to $\Sigma_n$ gives one copy of each [Specht module](specht-module.md) obtained by adding one box in a valid Young-diagram position. By [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md), its multiplicity in the induced [module](module-mathematics.md) equals the multiplicity of $S^\lambda$ in the restriction of that larger [Specht module](specht-module.md). The [restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md) says this multiplicity is one exactly when removing one corner gives $\lambda$, and zero otherwise. Semisimplicity in [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md) turns these multiplicities into the displayed direct-sum decomposition.
