# Second coefficient bound for normalized univalent functions

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized univalent function](normalized-univalent-function.md)

The [odd square-root transform of a normalized univalent function](odd-square-root-transform-of-a-normalized-univalent-function.md) has reciprocal exterior expansion $1/h(1/\zeta)=\zeta-(a_2/2)\zeta^{-1}+\cdots$. Its first negative Laurent coefficient has modulus at most one by the [area theorem for univalent functions](area-theorem-conformal-mapping.md), proving the bound. If $w$ is omitted by $f$, the normalized map $wf/(w-f)$ has second coefficient $a_2+1/w$. Applying the bound twice gives $|1/w|\le4$, the [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md).
