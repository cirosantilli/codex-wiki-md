<h1 id="crossover-from-gaussian-to-thomas-fermi-condensate">Crossover from Gaussian to Thomas–Fermi condensate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md)

Repulsive trapped condensates change smoothly from a nearly ideal [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) for $\chi\ll1$ to a broad [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md) for $\chi\gg1$. Comparing the [Gaussian interaction energy of a trapped condensate](gaussian-interaction-energy-of-a-trapped-condensate.md) with the total ideal [energy](energy-split.md) gives $E_I/E_0=2\chi/(3\sqrt{2\pi})$. Thus $N\sim a_0/a$ is a crossover scale, not a sharply defined critical particle number. Total interaction [energy](energy-split.md) must be compared with total ideal [energy](energy-split.md); comparing it with one oscillator quantum confuses total and per-particle scales.
