# Inviscid core

↑ **Parent:** [Euler limit](euler-limit.md)

An inviscid core is the part of a high-[Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) flow outside thin wall or shear layers, where [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) is negligible at the retained asymptotic order. It obeys the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md). Its tangential velocity need not satisfy no slip at a material wall; adjoining [viscous boundary layers](viscous-boundary-layer.md) supply that condition and can influence the core through displacement and pressure matching.
