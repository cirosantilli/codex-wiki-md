# Causal time series

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

A causal time series can be expressed using present and past innovations, without future innovations. The [autoregressive process of order one](autoregressive-process-of-order-one.md) with $|\alpha|<1$ has the mean-square convergent causal representation $Y_t=\sum_{j\ge0}\alpha^j\varepsilon_{t-j}$.
