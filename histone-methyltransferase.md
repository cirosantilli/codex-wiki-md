# Histone methyltransferase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that adds methyl groups to specified [histone](histone.md) residues. Its presence alone does not specify repression, because distinct modified sites can have different regulatory roles.
