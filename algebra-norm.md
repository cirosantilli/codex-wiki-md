# Algebra norm

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

An algebra norm is a norm satisfying $\lVert ab\rVert\leq\lVert a\rVert\lVert b\rVert$. Its completion is a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) in which the original algebra embeds densely.
