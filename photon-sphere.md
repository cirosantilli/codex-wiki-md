# Photon sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Circular null geodesic](circular-null-geodesic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon_sphere)

A photon sphere is a spherical set of circular [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md). In [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) it occurs at $r=3M$ and is unstable.
