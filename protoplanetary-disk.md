# Protoplanetary disk

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk-split.md)

A protoplanetary disk is a gas-and-solid [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk-split.md) around a young star in which [planet formation](planet-formation.md) occurs. Its evolution combines [accretion](accretion.md), radiation, gas dynamics and the growth of solid material. A [minimum-mass solar nebula](minimum-mass-solar-nebula.md) is one reconstruction of the material needed to form the Solar System.
