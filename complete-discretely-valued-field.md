# Complete discretely valued field

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md)

A [field](field.md) with a surjective discrete [valuation](valuation.md), complete for the associated non-Archimedean metric. Its [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md), and its [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) is generated by a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). [Completeness](completeness.md) allows successive congruence solutions to converge to actual roots, which underlies the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). Non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md) are examples; [completeness](completeness.md) alone does not require the [residue field](residue-field.md) to be finite.
