# Heat-kernel density change for a Brownian bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md)

The [Brownian transition semigroup](brownian-transition-semigroup.md) identity proves that this positive density process has mean one for $t<1$. The [backward heat equation](backward-heat-equation.md) and [Itô's formula](ito-s-lemma.md) give $dM_t=M_t(y-B_t)(1-t)^{-1}dB_t$. On every fixed horizon $T<1$, [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) under $dQ=M_TdP$ makes $B_t-\int_0^t(y-B_s)(1-s)^{-1}ds$ [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). Thus the original coordinate acquires the bridge [drift coefficient](drift-coefficient.md) toward $y$ at time one, without defining an equivalent endpoint density at the singular time.
