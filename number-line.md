# Number line

↑ **Parent:** [Real number](real-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_line)

A number line represents [real numbers](real-number.md) as points on a line, with zero as origin and a chosen positive direction and unit scale. [Addition](addition.md) becomes a shift and [multiplication](multiplication.md) becomes a rescaling about zero.
