# Extension of scalars

↑ **Parent:** [Change of rings](change-of-rings.md)

For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to A$, tensoring an $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) with the $(A,R)$-[bimodule](bimodule.md) $A$ gives an $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md). This functor is left adjoint to [restriction of scalars](restriction-of-scalars.md).
