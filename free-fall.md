# Free fall

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_fall)

Free fall is motion under [gravity](gravity.md) alone: there is no supporting [normal force](normal-force.md), engine thrust or appreciable [drag force](drag-physics.md). In the [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md) model, a [test particle](test-particle.md) of [mass](mass.md) $m$ obeys $m\ddot{\mathbf r}=m\mathbf g(\mathbf r)$, hence $\ddot{\mathbf r}=\mathbf g(\mathbf r)$. The [mass](mass.md) cancels. Bodies released at the same [position](position.md) with the same [velocity](velocity.md) follow the same path in this model, even if their [gravitational weights](weight-physics.md) differ. [NASA Glenn: free-falling objects and the cancellation of mass](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/free-falling-objects/)

A dropped stone is one example; a coasting satellite is another. Free fall does not require motion straight down or decreasing distance to the attracting body. An ideal [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) has constant radius while its [velocity](velocity.md) continually turns under [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md). The [circular orbit as continuous free fall](circular-orbit-as-continuous-free-fall.md) connects the familiar drop with orbital motion.

Near a surface, treating the [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) as constant gives a useful first prediction. Over larger heights, the [inverse-square law](inverse-square-law.md) changes the acceleration during the fall. [Radial free fall from rest](radial-free-fall-from-rest.md) uses [conservation of mechanical energy](conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md) to account for that change exactly, within the specified [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). The contrast matters: the simplest formula is an approximation to a physical model, rather than a law with the same numerical $g$ everywhere.

History also makes the idealization tangible. Galileo's _Two New Sciences_ (1638), Third Day, Theorem II, derives distance proportional to the square of time for uniformly accelerated motion from rest; its first corollary gives successive distances in the ratios $1,3,5,7,\ldots$ over equal time intervals. This is a kinematic statement, predating the gravitational [inverse-square law](inverse-square-law.md). [Galileo, Two New Sciences, Third Day, Theorem II and Corollary I; Crew and de Salvio's 1914 translation, pp. 174–176](https://galileoandeinstein.phys.virginia.edu/tns_draft/tns_160to243.html)

On Apollo 15's final lunar surface excursion, David Scott released a hammer and a feather together. The near-[vacuum](vacuum.md) removed the large difference in [drag force](drag-physics.md) familiar on Earth. NASA's report describes their arrival together within the accuracy of the release; this demonstration illustrates the [free fall](free-fall.md) prediction without establishing infinite experimental precision. [NASA's original footage and Joe Allen's account in the Apollo 15 Preliminary Science Report, p. 2-11](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-apollo-15-hammer-feather-drop/)
