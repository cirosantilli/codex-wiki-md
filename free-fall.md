# Free fall

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_fall)

Motion under a [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) without other forces. The Newtonian radial [free-fall speed](free-fall-speed.md) from rest at infinity is $v=\sqrt{2GM/r}$.
