# Edwards-Anderson model

↑ **Parent:** [Spin glass](spin-glass.md)

A common Ising version of the [Edwards-Anderson model](edwards-anderson-model.md) places [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) on a [lattice](lattice.md) with short-range, fixed random interactions $J_{ij}$. The displayed sum counts each interacting pair once; positive $J_{ij}$ favors equal [spins](spin.md), negative $J_{ij}$ favors opposite [spins](spin.md). Different samples retain different couplings. [Quenched disorder](quenched-disorder.md) describes this fixed-sample averaging.[§III, equation (1), nearest-neighbour random-coupling model; the pair-counting and field conventions here are stated explicitly](nobel-lecture-multiple-equilibria-by-giorgio-parisi.md)
