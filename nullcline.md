# Nullcline

↑ **Parent:** [Phase portrait](phase-portrait.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullcline)

A nullcline of a planar [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md) is a set where one component of its [vector field](vector-field.md) vanishes, typically drawn as a curve. Intersections of the two component nullclines are [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). The sign of each component on either side helps determine the directions in a [phase portrait](phase-portrait.md).
