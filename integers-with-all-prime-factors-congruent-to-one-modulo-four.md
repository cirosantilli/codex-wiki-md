# Integers with all prime factors congruent to one modulo four

↑ **Parent:** [Sieve theory](sieve-theory.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) in this set has no [prime factor](prime-factor.md) equal to two or congruent to three modulo four. Its [prime factors](prime-factor.md) all lie in the [residue class](residue-class.md) one modulo four. For example $5\cdot13=65$ belongs, while $3^2=9$ does not, even though $9$ itself is congruent to one modulo four. A [half-dimensional interval sieve](half-dimensional-interval-sieve.md) bounds the count in an interval of length $z$ by $O(z/\sqrt{\log z})$.
