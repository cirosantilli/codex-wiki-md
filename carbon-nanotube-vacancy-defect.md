# Carbon nanotube vacancy defect

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md)

A missing carbon atom or group of atoms in a [carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md) wall. Local reconstruction can produce a 5–9 monovacancy or 5–8–5 divacancy ring inventory; larger vacancies can interrupt a shell.
