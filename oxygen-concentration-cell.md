# Oxygen concentration cell

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration cell](concentration-cell.md)

A cell comparing [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) across an oxygen-ion-conducting [electrolyte](electrolyte.md). With oxide-ion conduction and four [electrons](electron.md) transferred per [oxygen](oxygen.md) molecule, $E=RT\ln(p_2/p_1)/(4F)$ for consistently ordered terminals. Reference [pressure](pressure-split.md), interface equilibrium and electronic leakage must be controlled.
