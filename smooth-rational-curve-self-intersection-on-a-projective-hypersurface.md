# Smooth rational-curve self-intersection on a projective hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md)

For a degree-$d$ smooth surface $X\subset\mathbb P^3$ and a smooth rational curve $C$ of degree $a$, the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) is $K_X=\mathcal O_X(d-4)$. The [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) gives $(K_X+C)\cdot C=-2$, hence $C^2=-2-(d-4)a$. In particular a line has self-intersection $2-d$.
