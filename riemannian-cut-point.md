# Riemannian cut point

↑ **Parent:** [Cut locus](cut-locus.md)

For a unit-speed [geodesic](geodesic.md) $\gamma(t)=\exp_p(tv)$, a finite cut time $c(v)$ is the last time to which its segment from $p$ minimizes [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md). Its endpoint is the Riemannian cut point along that geodesic. It lies in the [cut locus](cut-locus.md); it need not be a [cut point](cut-point.md) in the topological sense. The segment up to the cut time still minimizes by continuity of distance.
