# Conditional expectation preserving a distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

For a real [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $X$, a [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) $Y=\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ with the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) as $X$ must equal $X$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). No second moment is needed. For any real $c$, the nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $D_c=|X-c|-\operatorname{sign}(Y-c)(X-c)$ has [expected value](expected-value.md) zero, by conditioning and equality of the two absolute moments. Thus $Y>c$ implies $X\geq c$, $Y<c$ implies $X\leq c$, and $Y=c$ implies $X=c$, outside a null set. Using a [countable](countable-set.md) family of rational $c$ separates any unequal real values and proves the claim. Equality of the probabilities of $X=c$ and $Y=c$ also gives equality of their zero sets and hence equality of the two sign functions for every fixed $c$.
