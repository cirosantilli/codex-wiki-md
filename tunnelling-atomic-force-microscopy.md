# Tunnelling atomic force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

Current-sensitive [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md) using a conducting tip and a low-current amplifier. A steady conductivity map requires a complete electrical path through the contacted specimen region, even when force-based topography can be recorded without it.
