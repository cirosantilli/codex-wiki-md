# Visual contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Receptive field](receptive-field.md)

For the toy models here, [visual contrast](visual-contrast.md) means brightness relative to a positive uniform background $L_0$: $C(x,y)=(L(x,y)-L_0)/L_0$. Positive values mean brighter than background, negative values darker, and zero unchanged. This convention separates spatial structure from the background level. A [sinusoid](sinusoid.md) $C=a\cos(\cdots)$ with $0\leq a\leq1$ keeps $L=L_0(1+C)$ nonnegative. Different experimental conventions for [visual contrast](visual-contrast.md) require their own definitions; the toy values below do not specify a historical stimulus intensity.
