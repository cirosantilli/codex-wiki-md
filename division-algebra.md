# Division algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)

A division algebra over a [field](field.md) $k$ is a [division ring](division-ring.md) equipped with a unital [algebra](algebra-split.md) structure over $k$, with $k$ mapping into its [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md). Every nonzero element is invertible; it need not be finite-dimensional over $k$.
