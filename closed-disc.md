# Closed disc

↑ **Parent:** [Topological disc](topological-disc.md)

A closed $n$-disc is a space homeomorphic to $D^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:|x|\leq1\}$; its boundary is the sphere $S^{n-1}$.
