<h1 id="fejer-s-theorem">Fejér's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fejér's_theorem)

The [Cesàro means](cesaro-mean.md) of the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of a continuous periodic function converge uniformly to that function. The [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) is nonnegative with total mass one after normalization, and its mass outside every fixed neighborhood of zero tends to zero. Uniform [continuity](continuous-function.md) controls the near contribution, while the vanishing tail controls the rest. For an $L^1$ function the same positive-kernel argument gives an $L^1$ contraction, which is useful even when pointwise behavior is unavailable.
