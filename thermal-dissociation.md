# Thermal dissociation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

Thermal dissociation is temperature-driven breaking of a molecule into simpler fragments. The [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) abundance depends on pressure as well as temperature: reactions increasing particle number are generally favored at low pressure. In an [exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md), reduced [water](water.md) on a very hot dayside does not by itself establish a low planetary oxygen abundance.
