# Training data

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning theory](statistical-learning-theory.md)

The observations used to construct a [classifier](classifier.md) or other fitted rule. For a random fitted [classifier](classifier.md), its [conditional misclassification risk](conditional-misclassification-risk.md) conditions on these observations and evaluates an independent test pair. Auxiliary randomization used in fitting belongs to the conditioning information as well.
