# Small data global regularity for wave maps

↑ **Parent:** [Wave map](wave-map.md)

Smooth compatible data sufficiently small in high weighted [Sobolev norms](sobolev-norm.md) relative to a constant map produce global smooth [wave maps](wave-map.md) in three and four spatial dimensions. In four dimensions, derivative decay $(1+t)^{-3/2}$ is time-integrable and closes [commuted wave energy](commuted-wave-energy.md) estimates. In three dimensions the weaker $(1+t)^{-1}$ decay requires the cancellation of [null forms for wave equations](null-form-for-wave-equations.md), exploited by the [vector field method for wave equations](vector-field-method-for-wave-equations.md). These classical localized-data statements do not assert global regularity from small supercritical energy alone.
