# Engel lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Engel's theorem](engel-s-theorem.md)

A finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) in which every representing endomorphism is [nilpotent](nilpotent-linear-map.md) has a nonzero vector annihilated by the entire Lie algebra. Applying this to the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is the key step in [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md).
