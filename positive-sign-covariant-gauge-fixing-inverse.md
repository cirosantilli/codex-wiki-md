# Positive-sign covariant gauge-fixing inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant gauge](covariant-gauge.md)

For fixed $\xi\ne0$, the Lorentzian quadratic density $-F^2/4+(\partial\cdot A)^2/(2\xi)$ has momentum kernel $K^{ab}=-p^2\eta^{ab}+(1+1/\xi)p^ap^b$. At $p^2\ne0$ its inverse, defined by $K^{ac}G_{cb}=\delta^a_b$, is $G_{ab}=-\eta_{ab}/p^2+(1+\xi)p_ap_b/(p^2)^2$. A vacuum [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md) is $iG$ with the appropriate [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md). The usual parameter in the negative-sign [covariant gauge](covariant-gauge.md) term is $\alpha=-\xi$, so [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md) is $\xi=-1$.
