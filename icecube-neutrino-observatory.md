# IceCube Neutrino Observatory

↑ **Parent:** [Cherenkov detector](cherenkov-detector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IceCube_Neutrino_Observatory)

[IceCube](icecube-neutrino-observatory.md) turns Antarctic [ice](ice.md) into a [Cherenkov detector](cherenkov-detector.md): $5160$ [digital optical modules](digital-optical-module.md) on $86$ strings span depths of $1450$–$2450\,\mathrm m$. Hot-water drilling allowed deployment before refreezing. [Neutrino](neutrino.md) interactions produce [charged particles](charged-particle.md); their [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md), rather than the incoming [neutrino](neutrino.md), supplies the optical readout.[Sections 1.1.1, 5.1: array and deployment](the-icecube-neutrino-observatory-instrumentation-and-online-systems-by-icecube-collaboration.md)

[Neutrino astronomy](neutrino-astronomy.md) asks where the incoming [neutrino](neutrino.md) originated. A [Cherenkov detector](cherenkov-detector.md) asks how its reaction products make measurable light. Connecting these questions requires [track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md), [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md) and background tests. An astronomical messenger and the light carrying information inside the apparatus are different stages of one inference.
