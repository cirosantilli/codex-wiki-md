# Finite normalization model of a one-variable function field

↑ **Parent:** [Normalization of an algebraic curve](normalization-of-an-algebraic-curve-split.md)

For an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$ and a finitely generated [field extension](field-extension.md) $K/k$ of [transcendence degree](transcendence-degree.md) one, choose $t$ with $K/k(t)$ finite. The [integral closures](integral-closure.md) of the two standard coordinate rings of the [projective line](projective-line.md) in $K$ are finite modules and glue to a finite [normalization of an algebraic curve](normalization-of-an-algebraic-curve-split.md) of the projective line. A one-dimensional normal local ring is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md); over the perfect field $k$ the resulting projective curve is smooth. Extension of rational maps gives uniqueness of this smooth projective model.
