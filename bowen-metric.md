# Bowen metric

↑ **Parent:** [Topological entropy](topological-entropy.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) on a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md), the Bowen metric compares the first $n$ points of two forward [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md). Its radius-$\epsilon$ ball is $B_n(x,\epsilon)=\{y:d_n(x,y)<\epsilon\}$. If $s_n(\epsilon)$ is the largest cardinality of an $\epsilon$-[separated set](separated-subset-of-a-metric-space.md) in this [metric](metric.md), then [topological entropy](topological-entropy.md) is $\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log s_n(\epsilon)$. The [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) of the identity between two [compatible metrics](compatible-metric.md) gives the comparison of separated-set counts needed for independence of the original [metric](metric.md).
