# Vacuum ambiguity in a nonstationary spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory in curved spacetime](quantum-field-theory-in-curved-spacetime.md)

Without a preferred timelike Killing flow there is no canonical positive-frequency splitting. Different choices are related by a [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) and generally define different particle notions and vacuum states.
