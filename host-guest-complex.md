<h1 id="host-guest-complex">Host–guest complex</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

A molecular association in which a host provides a binding site or cavity for a guest. Its [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) includes solvent and entropy contributions, not only direct host–guest attraction.
