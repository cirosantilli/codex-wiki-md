# Marine magnetic stripe

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anomaly](magnetic-anomaly.md)

A lineated pattern associated with differently magnetized crust formed at a [spreading ridge](spreading-ridge.md) during changes of field polarity. The age-distance relation is set by spreading, while the measured anomaly is a spatially filtered field of the magnetized material.
