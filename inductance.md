# Inductance

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductance)

[Inductance](inductance.md) relates [electric currents](electric-current.md) to the [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) they produce. For a single linear coil with fixed geometry and magnetic response, its [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) $L$ is the constant in

$$
\Lambda=LI.
$$

Here $\Lambda$ is [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) from the coil's own [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$. The [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [inductance](inductance.md) is the [henry](henry-unit.md). Knowing [inductance](inductance.md) lets us predict the induced [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) when [electric current](electric-current.md) changes, and the [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) stored by an [inductor](inductor.md). [OpenStax University Physics 2, §14.2: self-inductance and inductors](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/14-2-self-inductance-and-inductors).

For example, a coil of [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) $0.5\,\mathrm H$ carrying [electric current](electric-current.md) $3\,\mathrm A$ has [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) $1.5\,\mathrm{Wb}$. If its $100$ turns link the same [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), each turn has [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) $0.015\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Keeping the geometry and magnetic response fixed while doubling [electric current](electric-current.md) doubles [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) and quadruples the [inductor](inductor.md)'s [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md).

The comparison with [capacitance](capacitance.md) is useful: a linear [capacitor](capacitor.md) has $Q=CV$ and stores [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) $CV^2/2$ in its [electric field](electric-field.md), whereas a linear [inductor](inductor.md) has $\Lambda=LI$ and stores [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) $LI^2/2$ in its [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). The [RLC circuit](rlc-circuit.md) brings both forms of [energy](energy.md) storage together.
