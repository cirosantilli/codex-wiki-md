<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [BCS theory](../../../../../bcs-theory.md), pairing mixes an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) state with a hole in its opposite-momentum partner. For a uniform spin-singlet gap, the uniform [Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian](../../../../../bogoliubov-de-gennes-hamiltonian.md) gives the momentum-pair eigenproblem

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\xi_{\mathbf k}&\Delta\\\Delta^*&-\xi_{\mathbf k}\end{pmatrix}
\binom{u_{\mathbf k}}{v_{\mathbf k}}=E_{\mathbf k}\binom{u_{\mathbf k}}{v_{\mathbf k}},\qquad
\boxed{E_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\xi_{\mathbf k}^2+|\Delta|^2}.}
$$

Here $\xi_{\mathbf k}=\varepsilon_{\mathbf k}-\mu$. A [fermionic Bogoliubov quasiparticle](../../../../../fermionic-bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) is a coherent particle–hole combination, with

$$
|u_{\mathbf k}|^2=\frac12\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\mathbf k}}{E_{\mathbf k}}\right),\qquad
|v_{\mathbf k}|^2=\frac12\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\mathbf k}}{E_{\mathbf k}}\right).
$$

Far above the [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md) the excitation is mostly particle-like; far below it is mostly hole-like; at $\xi_{\mathbf k}=0$ the two weights agree. The transformation preserves the fermionic [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). Its mean-field particle-number mixing does not mean that the full physical theory violates charge conservation.

For a fully gapped isotropic state, the minimum single-quasiparticle [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $|\Delta|$, while creating a pair of [quasiparticles](../../../../../quasiparticle.md) costs at least $2|\Delta|$. This produces suppressed low-temperature thermal populations, an activated electronic [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) and a gap feature in superconducting tunneling. Gap nodes in an unconventional state instead allow low-energy excitations, changing the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence. These fermionic excitations are distinct from collective phase and amplitude oscillations of the paired condensate.
