<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A neutral superfluid's broken global $U(1)$ phase symmetry produces a [Goldstone mode](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). Coupling phase to density fluctuations generally gives a long-wavelength [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) with $E(p)\simeq cp$, rather than the quadratic dispersion of a noninteracting condensate. In a dilute interacting Bose gas, the [Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion](../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) is

$$
E(p)=\sqrt{\varepsilon_p(\varepsilon_p+2gn)},\qquad
\varepsilon_p=\frac{p^2}{2m},\qquad c=\sqrt{\frac{gn}{m}}.
$$

For a heavy weak obstacle moving at speed $v$, emitting an excitation of [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p$ changes the available [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by $E(p)-\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf p$. The [Landau criterion](../../../../../landau-criterion.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{v_L=\inf_{p>0}\frac{E(p)}p.}
$$

The dilute-gas spectrum has $v_L=c$. In liquid helium, a [roton](../../../../../roton.md) minimum can make the minimum ratio smaller than the sound speed; the slope of a line from the origin tangent to the full dispersion determines the kinematic bound. A neutral paired Fermi superfluid also has a fermionic pair-breaking bound of order $\Delta/p_F$ in weak coupling. The smallest relevant branch bound must be considered. Actual flow can fail earlier through vortex nucleation, surface defects or other instabilities: the excitation-energy criterion is not a complete dynamical guarantee of dissipation-free flow.
