<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a neutral condensate with [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md) $\psi=\sqrt{n_s}e^{i\phi}$, the [superfluid velocity](../../../../../superfluid-velocity.md) away from singularities is $\mathbf v_s=\hbar\nabla\phi/m$. Single-valuedness around a closed contour gives $\oint\nabla\phi\cdot d\mathbf l=2\pi n$, hence [quantization of circulation](../../../../../quantized-circulation.md):

$$
\boxed{\oint\mathbf v_s\cdot d\mathbf l=n\frac hm.}
$$

The [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is that of the condensed object: an atom for a bosonic atomic superfluid, or a pair for a paired-fermion condensate. A nonzero winding cannot be continuously removed while the [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md) remains nonzero throughout a spanning region. A vortex allows its phase to wind while its amplitude vanishes in a core.

For a charged condensate, minimal coupling gives $m\mathbf v_s+q\mathbf A=\hbar\nabla\phi$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Phi+\frac mq\oint\mathbf v_s\cdot d\mathbf l=n\frac hq.}
$$

Using $\mathbf j_s=n_sq\mathbf v_s$ and $\lambda_L^2=m/(\mu_0n_sq^2)$ gives the [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](../../../../../fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md) relation $\Phi+\mu_0\lambda_L^2\oint\mathbf j_s\cdot d\mathbf l=nh/q$. The signs of oriented flux and winding depend on the signed pair charge; the magnitude of the elementary superconducting flux quantum is **$\Phi_0=h/(2e)$**. Flux alone is quantized when the current term is negligible on a contour deep in the bulk. A thin ring or a contour near a core can instead require the full fluxoid.

In a neutral superfluid, vortex flow carries [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). Rotation can favor vortices because equilibrium minimizes the rotating-frame quantity $E-\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf L$. The core size is set by a coherence or healing scale. In a type-II superconductor, an [Abrikosov vortex](../../../../../abrikosov-vortex.md) carries quantized [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) and circulating supercurrent; its core scale is the [coherence length](../../../../../optical-coherence-length.md) and its magnetic screening scale is the penetration depth. Between the lower and upper critical fields, a vortex lattice can be favored. [Flux pinning](../../../../../flux-pinning.md) affects how that structure responds to current. Type-I bulk superconductors instead favor macroscopic normal/superconducting separation rather than the usual stable dilute Abrikosov-vortex regime.
