A [Josephson junction](../../../../../josephson-junction.md) converts a relative condensate phase into a measurable current. For superconductors the relevant coherent object is a [Cooper pair](../../../../../cooper-pair.md), with electric charge $q=-2e$. Its [Gauge-invariant Josephson phase](../../../../../gauge-invariant-josephson-phase.md) is

$$
\delta=\phi_2-\phi_1-\frac q\hbar\int_1^2\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf l.
$$

With a consistent directed-current convention, the weak-link relation is $I_s=I_c\sin\delta$. A voltage $V=U_2-U_1$ gives $\dot\delta=2eV/\hbar$, so a constant voltage produces the [AC Josephson effect](../../../../../ac-josephson-effect.md) at [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) $2e|V|/h$. For neutral atom reservoirs the phase evolution instead follows their chemical-potential difference, $\hbar\dot\delta=\mu_1-\mu_2$ with the displayed phase labeling. The current transfers atoms rather than electric charge, but still probes coherent matter-wave amplitudes.

A [DC SQUID](../../../../../dc-squid.md) provides two coherent paths around a superconducting loop. Neglect loop self-inductance, take equal junction critical currents $I_0$, and let $\Phi$ be the enclosed flux. The relative junction phase differs by $2\pi\Phi/\Phi_0$, with [superconducting flux quantum](../../../../../superconducting-flux-quantum.md) $\Phi_0=h/(2e)$. Adding the two sinusoidal currents and maximizing over their common phase gives

$$
\boxed{I_{c,\rm loop}(\Phi)=2I_0\left|\cos\frac{\pi\Phi}{\Phi_0}\right|.}
$$

For unequal junctions the corresponding result is $\sqrt{I_1^2+I_2^2+2I_1I_2\cos(2\pi\Phi/\Phi_0)}$. Finite inductance and junction asymmetry alter the ideal modulation, so a measured minimum need not be zero. Flux changes a phase difference between the paths; the detector reads the resulting critical-current or voltage modulation. The [Jaklevic Josephson interference experiment](../../../../../jaklevic-josephson-interference-experiment.md) established superconducting [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) experimentally.[1964 primary report](../../../../../quantum-interference-effects-in-josephson-tunneling-by-r-c-jaklevic-and-others.md)

A [bosonic Josephson junction](../../../../../bosonic-josephson-junction.md) uses two weakly linked Bose-Einstein condensates. In the [Albiez double-well Josephson experiment](../../../../../albiez-double-well-josephson-experiment.md), the population distribution was measured in situ and the relative phase inferred from [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) fringes. The apparatus therefore tests both number transfer and phase coherence, rather than interpreting a density oscillation alone as a phase measurement. Interactions make the dynamics nonlinear: large imbalance can produce self-trapping instead of simple tunneling oscillations.[2005 abstract: double-well implementation, density measurement, phase from fringes and nonlinear self-trapping](../../../../../direct-observation-of-tunneling-and-nonlinear-self-trapping-in-a-single-bosonic-josephson-junction-by-michael-albiez-and-others.md)

The usefulness is interferometric sensitivity: a small phase perturbation can change a measurable current. It requires a coherent weak link, controlled phase noise and the appropriate particle's phase evolution; a generic insulating barrier between incoherent reservoirs is not already a matter-wave interferometer.
