<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the condensate [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md) as $\Psi=|\Psi|e^{i\theta}$. Away from [vortex](../../../../../phase-vortex.md) cores, single-valuedness around a closed loop gives $\oint\nabla\theta\cdot d\mathbf l=2\pi n$. For neutral condensed objects of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M$, $\mathbf v_s=\hbar\nabla\theta/M$, hence the [quantized circulation](../../../../../quantized-circulation.md)

$$
\boxed{\oint\mathbf v_s\cdot d\mathbf l=n\frac hM.}
$$

Here $M$ is the atomic [mass](../../../../../mass.md) for a simple bosonic atomic condensate, or the pair [mass](../../../../../mass.md) for a paired-fermion condensate. A nonzero winding can surround a [vortex](../../../../../phase-vortex.md) core where the order-parameter amplitude falls to zero. The result concerns collective condensate phase winding, not separate orbit quantization of every constituent particle.

For charge $q$ per condensed object, the gauge-invariant relation is $M\mathbf v_s=\hbar\nabla\theta-q\mathbf A$. Integrating gives

$$
M\oint\mathbf v_s\cdot d\mathbf l+q\Phi=nh,
\qquad \Phi+\frac Mq\oint\mathbf v_s\cdot d\mathbf l=\frac{nh}{q}.
$$

This is [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](../../../../../fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md). With Cooper-pair charge magnitude $2e$, the flux quantum's magnitude is

$$
\boxed{\Phi_0=\frac h{2e}.}
$$

The orientation fixes its sign. A loop taken deep in a thick superconductor has negligible superflow and therefore encloses an integer flux quantum. In a thin loop or one with appreciable screening current, it is the [fluxoid](../../../../../fluxoid.md), including the circulation term, that is quantized; the [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) alone need not be exactly an integer multiple.
