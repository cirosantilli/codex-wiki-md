<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A neutral [superfluid](../../../../../superfluid.md) can select a phase of its complex [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md), breaking a global $U(1)$ symmetry. A spatially uniform change of that phase costs no [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Slowly varying changes therefore produce a gapless [Goldstone mode](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). For a dilute weakly repulsive [Bose gas](../../../../../bose-gas.md), the [Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion](../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) is

$$
E_k=\sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2g_Bn_0)},\qquad
\epsilon_k=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_B},
$$

so $E_k\simeq\hbar c_sk$ at long wavelengths, with $c_s=\sqrt{g_Bn_0/m_B}$. Density and phase fluctuations are canonically coupled components of this sound branch, rather than two independent positive-frequency modes. Superfluid helium and dilute atomic condensates supply neutral examples, although the dilute-gas formula is not quantitatively valid for strongly interacting liquid helium.

In a [superconductor](../../../../../superconductor.md), the pair field is charged and its phase couples to the electromagnetic [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md). Only combinations such as $\nabla\theta-q\mathbf A/\hbar$ are physically meaningful. The [Anderson-Higgs mechanism in a superconductor](../../../../../anderson-higgs-mechanism-in-a-superconductor.md) produces electromagnetic screening and removes the neutral sound mode as an independent gapless charged-density oscillation. In a homogeneous three-dimensional continuum, long-range Coulomb [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) give a longitudinal plasma gap, approximately $\omega^2=\omega_p^2+c_s^2k^2$ at small $k$. The static transverse response gives the [Meissner effect](../../../../../meissner-effect.md) and

$$
\lambda_L^{-2}=\mu_0\frac{n_se^2}{m},
$$

when $n_s$ counts superconducting [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) of effective [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m$. Counting pairs instead uses density $n_s/2$, charge $-2e$ and [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $2m$, giving the same stiffness. The condensate amplitude may also have a gapped [Higgs mode](../../../../../higgs-mode.md), but that amplitude oscillation is distinct from the electromagnetic Higgs mechanism. Local gauge transformations remain a redundancy; this is not spontaneous breaking of an observable local symmetry in the same sense as the neutral global phase symmetry.
