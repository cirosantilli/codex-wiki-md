<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A bosonic [coherent state](../../../../../coherent-state.md) is an annihilation-operator eigenstate,

$$
|\alpha\rangle=e^{-|\alpha|^2/2}\sum_{N=0}^{\infty}\frac{\alpha^N}{\sqrt{N!}}|N\rangle,
\qquad a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle.
$$

It has $\langle N\rangle=\operatorname{Var}N=|\alpha|^2$, a selected complex amplitude, and Poisson-distributed particle number. A [coherent-field condensate variational state](../../../../../coherent-field-condensate-variational-state.md) extends this construction to a spatial field with $\langle\widehat\psi(\mathbf r)\rangle=\Psi(\mathbf r)$. Evaluating a normal-ordered interacting Bose Hamiltonian in that state gives a classical-field [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) functional; its variational equation is the [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../gross-pitaevskii-equation.md). This connects a macroscopic phase to [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md), [quantized circulation](../../../../../quantized-circulation.md) and neutral [Josephson effects](../../../../../josephson-effect.md). A fixed-number condensate instead has vanishing one-point field expectation; [off-diagonal long-range order](../../../../../off-diagonal-long-range-order.md) identifies the condensate without requiring exact number fluctuations.

The [BCS ground state](../../../../../bcs-ground-state.md) has the pair-coherent form

$$
|\mathrm{BCS}\rangle=\prod_{\mathbf k}
\left(u_{\mathbf k}+v_{\mathbf k}c_{\mathbf k\uparrow}^\dagger c_{-\mathbf k\downarrow}^\dagger\right)|0\rangle.
$$

It is a product of [Cooper-pair pseudospin coherent states](../../../../../cooper-pair-pseudospin-coherent-state.md): each paired [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) sector mixes its empty and doubly occupied configurations. The anomalous pair expectation is $\langle c_{-\mathbf k\downarrow}c_{\mathbf k\uparrow}\rangle=u_{\mathbf k}^*v_{\mathbf k}$, while an ordinary single-electron expectation vanishes by [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) parity. Pair operators are not unrestricted canonical [boson](../../../../../boson.md) operators, so this state must not be identified with a naive oscillator coherent state of individual [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). Number projection gives a fixed-even-number description with pair correlations. Phase differences between condensates enter the [Josephson effect](../../../../../josephson-effect.md); in a charged condensate that phase difference must be gauge invariant.
