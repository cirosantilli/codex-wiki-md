# Trochoid

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trochoid)

The trajectory of a point at fixed distance $a$ from the centre of a circle of radius $r$ rolling along a line. In coordinates aligned with that line it has a linear centre displacement plus sinusoidal components. The case $a=r$ is a [cycloid](cycloid.md); $a<r$ gives a shortened trajectory and $a>r$ gives loops. An inclined rolling line rotates this geometry relative to horizontal and vertical coordinates.
