# Low-velocity zone

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A [low-velocity zone](low-velocity-zone.md) has a wave-speed decrease with depth over some interval. Surface rays do not turn within its negative-gradient interior; rays penetrating it can return only after encountering sufficient [speed](speed.md) recovery below. This can produce shadow ranges, multiple travel-time branches and poor turning-depth coverage. A wave-specific [low-velocity zone](low-velocity-zone.md) is not identical to a mechanical layer boundary.
