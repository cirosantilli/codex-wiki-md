# Neo-Hookean solid

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Hookean_solid)

For an incompressible neo-Hookean solid, a convenient [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) is $W=\mu(\operatorname{tr}C-3)/2$, where $C$ is the [right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](right-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md). An additive constant in $W$ does not affect the [stress](stress.md). The [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu$ fixes the small-strain stiffness.
