# Fermat quartic surface

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

Over $\mathbb C$, the four partial derivatives show that this projective surface is smooth. Adjunction makes its [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) trivial, and the hypersurface [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) gives $H^1(\mathcal O_X)=0$, so it is a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md). Lines given by $x_0=\zeta x_1$, $x_2=\eta x_3$, with $\zeta^4=\eta^4=-1$, lie on it. Choosing both roots differently gives skew lines, one of which is a section of the elliptic pencil defined by planes through the other.
