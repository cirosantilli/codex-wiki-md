# Right-invariant group metric

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

Right translations are isometries for a right-invariant group metric. Given a compatible [left-invariant group metric](left-invariant-group-metric.md) $d$, the formula $d_R(x,y)=d(x^{-1},y^{-1})$ defines a compatible right-invariant group metric, since inversion is a homeomorphism and $(xa)^{-1}=a^{-1}x^{-1}$. Compatible invariant metrics need not be Lipschitz equivalent; on the additive group $\mathbb R$, both $|x-y|$ and $\sqrt{|x-y|}$ are compatible invariant distances.
