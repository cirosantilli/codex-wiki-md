<h1 id="1-3-5-trioxane">1,3,5-Trioxane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Acetal](acetal.md)

The cyclic trimer of [formaldehyde](formaldehyde.md), $\mathrm{(CH_2O)_3}$. Acid-catalysed acetal cleavage can regenerate [formaldehyde](formaldehyde.md), which is strongly hydrated in water.
