# Kinetic isotope effect

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

A [kinetic isotope effect](kinetic-isotope-effect.md) is a change in a reaction [rate constant](rate-constant.md) caused by isotopic substitution. Solvent isotope effects can constrain proton-transfer pathways, but coupling and kinetic masking prevent interpreting every rate ratio as one isolated bond-breaking event.
