# Energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md)

An [energy balance](energy-balance.md) accounts for the change of stored [energy](energy-split.md) as inputs minus outputs. For mechanical motion, externally supplied [power](power.md) and dissipative losses must be included before imposing a conserved mechanical [energy](energy-split.md).
