# Corner in an integer grid

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

A corner consists of these three points in the [integer](integer.md) grid, all contained in the set being studied. The displacement may be positive or negative, but must be nonzero, so the points are distinct. The [corners theorem](corners-theorem.md) guarantees a corner in every subset of positive fixed [density of a finite subset](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) of a sufficiently large square grid.
