# Ideal sheaf of two closed points

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal sheaf of a closed point](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-point.md)

For distinct [closed points](closed-point.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), evaluation gives the [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to\mathcal I_{\{P,Q\}}\to\mathcal O_X\to k_P\oplus k_Q\to0$. If all global [regular functions](regular-function.md) are constant, the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) embeds $k^2/k(1,1)$ into $H^1(X,\mathcal I_{\{P,Q\}})$. Thus this ideal detects a concrete obstruction to affineness.
