# Irreducible ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

A proper [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ is irreducible if $I=J\cap K$ implies $I=J$ or $I=K$. In a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), every irreducible ideal is a [primary ideal](primary-ideal.md); every proper [ideal](ideal.md) is a finite intersection of irreducible ideals.
