# Temporal coherence from crossed beams

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal optical coherence](temporal-optical-coherence.md)

Split one stationary scalar optical field into equal copies travelling at right angles with common transverse [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md). For wavevectors $k(\widehat{\mathbf x}\pm\widehat{\mathbf y})/\sqrt2$, the relative geometric delay is $\sqrt2y/c$ in [vacuum](vacuum.md), while the delay on the $x$ bisector is zero. The electric-field intensity convention gives the displayed pattern and $I(x)=2I_0$. The mean [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) points along $+x$, with magnitude $I(y)/\sqrt2$ in that convention. A transverse detector can record the $y$ profile if it resolves the period $\lambda/\sqrt2$; a longitudinal $x$ profile is not a transverse camera image.
