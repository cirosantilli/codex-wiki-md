# Symmetric treatment crossover attenuates a risk difference

↑ **Parent:** [Treatment contamination](treatment-contamination.md)

Assume treatment receipt gives event risk $p_1$, nonreceipt gives risk $p_0$, and a fraction $c$ in each equally sized randomized arm crosses to the opposite treatment. The two assignment risks are $(1-c)p_1+cp_0$ and $cp_1+(1-c)p_0$. Subtracting yields the displayed attenuation, with $\Delta_{\rm receipt}=p_0-p_1$. It depends on the stated equal crossover fractions and unchanged receipt-specific risks; it is not a universal formula for every form of [treatment contamination](treatment-contamination.md).
