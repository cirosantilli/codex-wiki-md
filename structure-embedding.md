# Structure embedding

↑ **Parent:** [First-order structure](first-order-structure.md)

A structure embedding is an injective map respecting constants and operations and preserving and reflecting relations. Equivalently it preserves all [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md) and their negations. It identifies the source with a [substructure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of the target. An [elementary embedding](elementary-embedding.md) additionally preserves every [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md).
