# Inverse image functor of a geometric morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

The inverse image is a finite-limit-preserving [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) with [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) $f_*$. It preserves arbitrary [colimits](colimit.md), and hence images as well as [finite limits](finite-limit.md). These properties preserve the interpretation of [geometric formulas](geometric-formula.md). It need not preserve Heyting implication or every universal quantifier.
