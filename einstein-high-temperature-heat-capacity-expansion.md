# Einstein high-temperature heat-capacity expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md)

Expanding the [Einstein model of a solid](einstein-solid.md) internal energy gives $U=3Nk_B[T+T_E^2/(12T)+O(T^{-3})]$, because the constant term in the thermal occupation cancels the [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md). Differentiation yields the displayed [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) and proves absence of a $1/T$ term. The ratio of the $T^{-2}$ and constant coefficients determines $T_E$ independently of sample size.
