# Upsilon meson

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upsilon_meson)

A [bottom quark](bottom-quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) bound state. Its $4S$ resonance provides a source of [B meson](b-meson.md) pairs.
