# Eutectic constituent fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Eutectic microstructure](eutectic-microstructure.md)

The fraction of an alloy that solidifies through the eutectic reaction, equal to the remaining liquid fraction immediately before that reaction in a simple equilibrium [solidification](freezing.md) path. It differs from the total fraction of either solid phase, and later precipitation can change the phase inventory.
