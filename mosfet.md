# MOSFET

↑ **Parent:** [Field-effect transistor](field-effect-transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOSFET)

A metal-oxide-semiconductor [field-effect transistor](field-effect-transistor.md), with an insulating layer separating the gate from the [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). Its gate [electric field](electric-field-split.md) controls the channel's mobile [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md); a [semiconductor inversion layer](semiconductor-inversion-layer.md) can form a [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md).
