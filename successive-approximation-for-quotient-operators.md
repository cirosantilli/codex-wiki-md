# Successive approximation for quotient operators

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient operator](quotient-operator.md)

Let $T:X\to Y$ be a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) with $X$ a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md). Suppose every $\|y\|<1$ has an approximation $Tx$ with $\|x\|<1$ and error less than $\delta\in(0,1)$. Repeatedly approximate the scaled residual to construct $x_n$ with $\|x_n\|<\delta^n$ and residual less than $\delta^{n+1}$. Completeness sums the [series](series-mathematics.md), and continuity gives $T\sum x_n=y$. Since $\|\sum x_n\|<(1-\delta)^{-1}$, scaling proves $B_Y(0,1-\delta)\subseteq T(B_X(0,1))$. Hence $T$ is a [quotient operator](quotient-operator.md), even without assuming completeness of $Y$.
