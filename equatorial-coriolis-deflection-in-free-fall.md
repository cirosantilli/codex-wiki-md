# Equatorial Coriolis deflection in free fall

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational deflection of a falling body](rotational-deflection-of-a-falling-body.md)

A body released from rest relative to [Earth](earth.md) at the equator has unperturbed downward [velocity](velocity.md) $v_U=-gt$. The [Coriolis acceleration in local Earth coordinates](coriolis-acceleration-in-local-earth-coordinates.md) gives eastward acceleration $a_E=-2\Omega_Ev_U=2\Omega_Egt$. Integration gives $x_E=\Omega_Egt^3/3$ and impact time $t_f=\sqrt{2h/g}$, proving the title formula. This is first order in [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md), neglecting air resistance and the height dependence of the effective local [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). For a short fall at latitude $\varphi$, its leading eastward term is multiplied by $\cos\varphi$.
