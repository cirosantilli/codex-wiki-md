# Isotope scaling of diatomic vibration

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced mass](reduced-mass.md)

Near a stable equilibrium [bond length](bond-length.md) $r_e$, a [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) of a [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md)'s [potential energy](potential-energy.md) gives $U(r)\simeq U(r_e)+k(r-r_e)^2/2$, with $k=U''(r_e)>0$. Treat the internal stretch as a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) of [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md), initially omitting [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md). If replacing an [isotope](isotope.md) leaves this local [potential energy](potential-energy.md) curvature unchanged, then

$$
\frac{\nu'}\nu\simeq\sqrt{\frac\mu{\mu'}},\qquad
\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}=\frac1{2\pi c}\sqrt{\frac{k}{\mu}}.
$$

Here $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) and $\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}$ a [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md); use consistent length units for $c$ and $\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}$. The [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) gives adjacent [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue.md) separated by $h\nu$, with $h$ the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). Consequently a vibrational [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) can probe the same [frequency](frequency.md) scale, subject to [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md).

For an explicitly approximate [isotope](isotope.md) example, assign the [atomic masses](atomic-mass.md) of $^{1}\mathrm H$, $^{2}\mathrm H$, $^{35}\mathrm{Cl}$, and $^{37}\mathrm{Cl}$ the mass-number values $1u,2u,35u,37u$, respectively, with $u$ the [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md). These are stipulated approximations to individual [atomic masses](atomic-mass.md), not measured [masses](mass.md) of [molecules](molecule.md) or natural-abundance averages. They give

$$
\mu_{\mathrm H^{35}\mathrm{Cl}}=\frac{35}{36}u,\quad
\mu_{\mathrm D^{35}\mathrm{Cl}}=\frac{70}{37}u,\quad
\frac{\nu_{\mathrm D^{35}\mathrm{Cl}}}{\nu_{\mathrm H^{35}\mathrm{Cl}}}\simeq\sqrt{\frac{37}{72}}\approx0.716860,
\qquad
\frac{\nu_{\mathrm H^{37}\mathrm{Cl}}}{\nu_{\mathrm H^{35}\mathrm{Cl}}}\simeq\sqrt{\frac{665}{666}}\approx0.999249.
$$

The symbol $\mathrm D$ means [deuterium](deuterium.md), $^{2}\mathrm H$. Doubling the light partner's approximate [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) therefore produces a large predicted shift, whereas changing the already heavy [chlorine](chlorine.md) partner by two mass units produces a much smaller one. A quantitative comparison with [infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md) requires actual [isotope](isotope.md)-specific [atomic masses](atomic-mass.md), fitted band parameters, and uncertainties, rather than identifying any arbitrary peak with $\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}$.
