# Subordinate flag for a family of linear isometries

↑ **Parent:** [Universe for spectra](universe-for-spectra.md)

Given a compact parameter space $X$ and a family $f_x:U\to U\prime$ of [linear isometries](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md), a [subordinate flag for a family of linear isometries](subordinate-flag-for-a-family-of-linear-isometries.md) consists of nested cofinal source and target subspaces $V_i,W_i$ with $f_x(V_i)\subset W_i$ for every $x$. After enlarging the target stages, compactness makes this possible for every finite source stage. It permits simultaneous finite-dimensional [Thom space](thom-space.md) constructions.
