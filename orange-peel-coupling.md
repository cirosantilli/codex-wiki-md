# Orange-peel coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)

A magnetostatic coupling caused by correlated roughness at interfaces of separated ferromagnetic films. Its tendency toward parallel alignment can modify a [pseudo spin valve](pseudo-spin-valve.md) switching loop; it is not itself interlayer exchange.
