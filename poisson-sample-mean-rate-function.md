# Poisson sample-mean rate function

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér's theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md)

For sample means of [Poisson random variables](poisson-distribution.md) of mean $\lambda>0$, the [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $\lambda(e^\theta-1)$. Its [Legendre-Fenchel transform](convex-conjugate.md) is the displayed [good rate function](good-rate-function.md), with $0\log0=0$, and is infinite for $x<0$. Near its zero, $I_P(\lambda+h)=h^2/(2\lambda)+O(h^3)$. This local quadratic explains the [Poisson moderate deviation principle](poisson-moderate-deviation-principle.md).
