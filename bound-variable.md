# Bound variable

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A variable occurrence in a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) is bound when it lies in the scope of a [quantifier](quantifier.md) binding that name. The same name can have both bound and free occurrences in one formula. A [first-order substitution](first-order-substitution.md) replaces only free occurrences; a fresh renaming of bound occurrences and their binder preserves the formula up to [alpha equivalence](alpha-equivalence.md).
