# Brownian change of measure by a positive space-time harmonic function

↑ **Parent:** [Doob h-transform](doob-h-transform.md)

If $h>0$ is smooth and $Z_t=h(X_t,t)$ is the unit-mean density martingale of a locally absolutely continuous probability measure relative to Wiener measure, then $h_t+\frac12h_{xx}=0$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $Z=\mathcal E(\int(h_x/h)(X_s,s)dX_s)$ when $Z_0=1$. The true-martingale hypothesis is already supplied by the density process. On each finite horizon the [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) makes $X-\int(h_x/h)(X_s,s)ds$ Brownian under the new measure. Positivity provides local equivalence; global infinite-horizon equivalence and the Novikov condition are not additional requirements.
