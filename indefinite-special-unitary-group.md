# Indefinite special unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md)

The [indefinite special unitary group](indefinite-special-unitary-group.md) preserves a [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) form of signature $(p,q)$ and has [determinant](determinant.md) one: $M^\dagger J M=J$, where $J=\operatorname{diag}(I_p,-I_q)$. With both $p,q$ positive it is noncompact, unlike the group preserving a positive-definite form. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) satisfies $X^\dagger J+JX=0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}X=0$.
