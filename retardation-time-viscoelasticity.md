# Retardation time (viscoelasticity)

↑ **Parent:** [Linear viscoelasticity](linear-viscoelasticity.md)

The characteristic time for a delayed [strain](strain.md) response to approach equilibrium under a step in [stress](stress.md). For a [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md) with elastic modulus $E$ and viscosity $\eta$, the equation $\eta\dot\varepsilon+E\varepsilon=\sigma_0$ gives $\varepsilon=(\sigma_0/E)(1-e^{-t/\tau_r})$, where $\tau_r=\eta/E$. A [relaxation time](relaxation-time.md) instead describes decay of [stress](stress.md) after an imposed strain; the two times need not coincide in a [standard linear solid model](standard-linear-solid-model.md).
