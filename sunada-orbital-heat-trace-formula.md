# Sunada orbital heat-trace formula

↑ **Parent:** [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md)

For a finite [subgroup](subgroup.md) $U$ of a [group](group-split.md) $T$ of [Riemannian isometries](riemannian-isometry.md) of a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) $N$, assume $U$ acts freely and define $I_t(g)=\int_N H_N(t,x,gx)\,dV(x)$. Invariance of the [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md) and change of variables show that $I_t$ is a [class function](class-function.md) on $T$. For $U\le T$, the [heat kernel on a finite isometric quotient](heat-kernel-on-a-finite-isometric-quotient.md) and integration over a [fundamental domain](fundamental-domain.md) give the displayed [heat trace](heat-trace.md) formula. Grouping by [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) proves equality for [Gassmann equivalent](gassmann-equivalence.md) [subgroups](subgroup.md). More generally the same argument allows fixed points of $T$ outside the freely acting [subgroups](subgroup.md); only the quotient under consideration must be smooth.
