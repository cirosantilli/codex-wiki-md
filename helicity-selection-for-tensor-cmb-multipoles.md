# Helicity selection for tensor CMB multipoles

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor cosmological perturbation](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md)

A complex helicity-$+2$ plane wave along the $z$ axis has polarization contraction $\sin^2\theta e^{2i\varphi}$. Its remaining [tensor CMB line-of-sight source](tensor-cmb-line-of-sight-source.md) depends only on $\cos\theta$. Orthogonality of the azimuthal factors of [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md) therefore restricts its temperature coefficients to $m=2$. A real perturbation also includes the conjugate mode.
