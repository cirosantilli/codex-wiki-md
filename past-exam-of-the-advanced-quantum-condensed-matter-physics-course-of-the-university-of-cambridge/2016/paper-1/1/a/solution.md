<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the growth direction as $z$ and the well as $0<z<L$. In the [envelope function approximation](../../../../../envelope-function-approximation.md), write a subband state as $A^{-1/2}e^{i\mathbf k_\parallel\cdot\mathbf r_\parallel}F_n(z)u_{j0}(\mathbf r)$, where $u_{j0}$ is the band-edge cell-periodic Bloch factor. The [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) has

$$
F_n(z)=\sqrt{\frac2L}\sin\frac{n\pi z}{L},\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$

The envelope has the same shape for [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and hole states: changing their [effective masses](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) changes the eigenenergies, but not these infinite-well [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md). With in-plane [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), the leading interband [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) acts on the Bloch factors, while the slow envelopes give

$$
\langle c,n',\mathbf k'|\hat p_x|v,j,n,\mathbf k\rangle\simeq\delta_{\mathbf k'\mathbf k}\langle u_c|\hat p_x|u_{v,j}\rangle\int_0^L F_{n'}^*(z)F_n(z)\,dz.
$$

[orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) therefore yields **the [infinite-well interband selection rule](../../../../../infinite-well-interband-selection-rule.md) $n'=n$**. A nonzero band-level [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) is still required; the overlap rule does not replace [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) selection rules. Finite barriers can make [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and hole envelopes different, and interface asymmetry or band mixing can relax this ideal same-index rule.
