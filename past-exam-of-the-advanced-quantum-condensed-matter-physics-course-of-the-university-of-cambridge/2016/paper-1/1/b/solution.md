<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a fixed allowed band/subband pair, parabolic dispersions give $E_c(\mathbf k)-E_v(\mathbf k)=E_{j,n}^{\rm edge}+\hbar^2k_\parallel^2/(2\mu_j)$. If the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) exceeds the edge, the contour is a circle of radius $k_\parallel$ and the gradient magnitude is $\hbar^2k_\parallel/\mu_j$. Thus

$$
\int_C\frac{dl}{|\nabla_{\mathbf k}(E_c-E_v)|}=\frac{2\pi k_\parallel}{\hbar^2k_\parallel/\mu_j}=\frac{2\pi\mu_j}{\hbar^2}.
$$

Below threshold the contour is absent. With the conventional $d^2k/(2\pi)^2$ normalization, the [two-dimensional joint density of states](../../../../../two-dimensional-joint-density-of-states.md) per area and independent channel is

$$
\boxed{J_{j,n}(\hbar\omega)=\frac{\mu_j}{2\pi\hbar^2}\Theta(\hbar\omega-E_{j,n}^{\rm edge}).}
$$

With a locally energy-independent optical [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md), **each allowed pair therefore adds a constant step** to the continuum [dielectric function](../../../../../dielectric-function.md) in the approximation of the supplied equation. Different hole families need not have equal step heights: [reduced masses](../../../../../reduced-mass.md), [spin](../../../../../spin.md) multiplicities and Bloch-state [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md) differ. Frequency-dependent overall optical prefactors, nonparabolicity, broadening and excitonic Coulomb enhancement can modify the literal step shape outside this approximation.
