Experimental tests of [BCS theory](../../../../../bcs-theory.md) compare several properties of the same superconducting state. In the conventional weak-coupling setting, phonon-mediated attraction pairs opposite momenta and spins, and the [BCS quasiparticle spectrum](../../../../../bcs-quasiparticle-spectrum.md) is $E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+\Delta^2(T)}$. Agreement with a single [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scale and its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence is more discriminating than observing zero resistance alone.

The [superconducting isotope effect](../../../../../superconducting-isotope-effect.md) tests the [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md)'s role. Replacing one [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) by another changes ionic masses while leaving the chemical valence nearly unchanged. [Phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) frequencies scale approximately as $M^{-1/2}$, so a weak-coupling [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) mechanism predicts $T_c\propto M^{-\alpha}$ with $\alpha\simeq1/2$ when other parameters stay fixed. The shift was measured in mercury before the microscopic pairing theory was developed.[1950 primary report, transition-temperature comparison of mercury [isotopes](../../../../../isotope.md)](../../../../../isotope-effect-in-the-superconductivity-of-mercury-by-emanuel-maxwell.md) Deviations from $1/2$ can result from Coulomb, anharmonic and strong-coupling effects; the [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) observation supports lattice-mediated pairing without uniquely proving every assumption of the simplest model.

Thermodynamic measurements test the gapped excitations and condensation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). An isotropic fully gapped [superconductor](../../../../../superconductor.md) has exponentially small low-temperature electronic [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md), rather than the normal linear law. At the transition the [weak-coupling BCS thermodynamic ratios](../../../../../weak-coupling-bcs-thermodynamic-ratios.md) are

$$
\boxed{\frac{2\Delta(0)}{k_BT_c}=2\pi e^{-\gamma_E}\simeq3.528,
\qquad \frac{C_s(T_c^-)-C_n(T_c)}{C_n(T_c)}
=\frac{12}{7\zeta(3)}\simeq1.426.}
$$

Here $C_n$ means the electronic contribution after subtracting the [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) background. The gap closes at $T_c$, and the same framework predicts the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of penetration depth and thermodynamic critical field. These quantities were quantitative comparisons in the original theory.[Abstract and thermodynamic discussion: [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) gap, specific heat and penetration depth](../../../../../theory-of-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen-and-others.md)

[Electron](../../../../../electron.md) tunnelling measures the excitation spectrum more directly. A normal-metal–insulator–[superconductor](../../../../../superconductor.md) junction has, at sufficiently low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and nearly constant tunnelling matrix element, $dI/dV$ proportional to the superconducting [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) at $E=eV$:

$$
\frac{N_s(E)}{N_n(0)}=
\operatorname{Re}\frac{|E|}{\sqrt{E^2-\Delta^2}}.
$$

The suppression below the gap and enhancement at its edges test the [BCS electronic density of states](../../../../../bcs-electronic-density-of-states.md). Giaever's thin oxide-barrier junction measurements established such energy-gap structure.[Physical Review Letters 5, 147–148; tunnel-current and differential-conductance measurements](../../../../../energy-gap-in-superconductors-measured-by-electron-tunneling-by-ivar-giaever.md) For a junction between two [superconductors](../../../../../superconductor.md) the threshold involves the sum of their gaps, so it must not be read as a normal-metal single-gap spectrum without accounting for the two electrodes.

Nuclear spin-lattice relaxation supplies a phase-coherence-sensitive test. In the conventional s-wave case, the [Hebel-Slichter coherence peak](../../../../../hebel-slichter-coherence-peak.md) just below $T_c$ is followed by a falling rate at lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). [Density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) alone is not enough: the [BCS coherence factors](../../../../../bcs-coherence-factors.md) enter the transition matrix elements. The contrast between nuclear relaxation and ultrasonic attenuation was explained using the paired state, unlike a simple two-fluid population model.[Abstract: field-cycling method and comparison of nuclear relaxation with ultrasonic absorption](../../../../../nuclear-spin-relaxation-in-normal-and-superconducting-aluminum-by-l-c-hebel-and-charles-pence-slichter.md)

Finally, [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) quantization in units $h/(2e)$ and [Josephson effects](../../../../../josephson-effect.md) support a coherent charge-$2e$ condensate. Those observations establish pairing and phase coherence more broadly; their existence alone does not specify a [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) mechanism or an isotropic weak-coupling gap. **The converging [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md), spectroscopic, thermodynamic and coherence-factor tests support conventional BCS [superconductivity](../../../../../superconductivity-split.md), with explicit limits to its simplest universal ratios.**
