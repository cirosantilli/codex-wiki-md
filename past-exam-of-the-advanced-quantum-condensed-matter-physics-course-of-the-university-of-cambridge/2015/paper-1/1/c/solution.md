<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed total [density](../../../../../density.md), the unpolarized and fully polarized candidate energies are

$$
\frac{E_u}{V}=A2^{-2/3}n^{5/3}-\frac{\gamma n^2}{4},\qquad
\frac{E_p}{V}=An^{5/3}-\frac{\gamma n^2}{2}.
$$

Polarization increases the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) because one [spin](../../../../../spin.md) species must occupy a larger [Fermi sphere](../../../../../fermi-sphere.md), but gains negative exchange [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The fully polarized candidate wins this endpoint comparison if

$$
A n^{5/3}(1-2^{-2/3})-\frac{\gamma n^2}{4}<0.
$$

Using the defined $r_s$ gives

$$
\boxed{r_s>4\,2^{-1/3}(1-2^{-2/3})=2^{5/3}-2=1.174802\ldots.}
$$

Thus the specified two-state comparison indeed predicts a polarized state below the unpolarized state's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) for $r_s>1.175$.

The [endpoint and continuous polarization thresholds of a contact electron gas](../../../../../endpoint-and-continuous-polarization-thresholds-of-a-contact-electron-gas.md) are different. To check the actual onset within the same uniform Hartree-Fock model, allow $P=(n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow)/n$ to vary continuously. In units $2A(n/2)^{5/3}$,

$$
f(P)=\frac{(1+P)^{5/3}+(1-P)^{5/3}}2-\frac{r_s}{2}(1+P^2),
$$



$$
f(P)-f(0)=\left(\frac59-\frac{r_s}{2}\right)P^2+\frac5{243}P^4+O(P^6).
$$

The unpolarized state loses local stability already at **$r_s=10/9\simeq1.111$**. Full polarization becomes the minimizing endpoint only when $f'(1)\le0$, or $r_s\ge(5/6)2^{2/3}\simeq1.323$. Between those values the optimum is partly polarized. Consequently $1.175$ is the requested endpoint crossing, not the exact onset of spontaneous polarization after all [spin](../../../../../spin.md) populations are allowed.
