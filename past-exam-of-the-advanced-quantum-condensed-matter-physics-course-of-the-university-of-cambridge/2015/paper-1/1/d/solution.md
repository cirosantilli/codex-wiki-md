<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The central limitation is that a [Hartree-Fock electron gas](../../../../../hartree-fock-electron-gas.md) includes exchange but omits the correlation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) that is not representable by one determinant. Opposite-spin [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) can lower their repulsion by avoiding one another in a correlated unpolarized state. Hartree-Fock misses that avoidance while retaining the exchange advantage of aligned spins, so it need not place a magnetic instability at a reliable interaction strength. The relevant [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) differences between competing [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states can be much smaller than their total energies. Correlation calculations of the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) gas explicitly address this competition.[Author manuscript, physical PDF pages 4–8: competing phases, correlated trial functions and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) differences](../../../../../ground-state-of-the-electron-gas-by-a-stochastic-method-by-d-m-ceperley-and-b-j-alder.md)

There are further model restrictions. A constant contact coupling discards the [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) dependence of the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) and the resulting nontrivial screening. Real materials have a [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md), band structure and material-dependent effective interactions. Spatial magnetic textures or other competing states are excluded by a comparison restricted to uniform Fermi spheres. Even before those changes, part c's two endpoints do not find the continuous polarization onset.

The symbol $r_s$ also needs care. Here it is an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) ratio proportional to $\gamma n^{1/3}$. The usual [Dimensionless Wigner-Seitz radius](../../../../../dimensionless-wigner-seitz-radius.md) of a three-dimensional Coulomb [electron](../../../../../electron.md) gas is $r_s^{\rm WS}=[3/(4\pi n)]^{1/3}/a_B$, proportional to $n^{-1/3}$. They are not the same [density](../../../../../density.md) parameter. A quoted experimental value of the latter cannot be compared numerically to $1.175$ without a model-specific mapping. **The contact interaction, missing correlations, restricted variational class and parameter convention prevent the number $1.175$ from being a quantitative experimental prediction.** The paper supplies no particular experimental threshold, so none is invented here.
