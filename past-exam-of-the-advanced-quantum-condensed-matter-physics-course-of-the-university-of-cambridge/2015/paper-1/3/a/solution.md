<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first term assigns [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\epsilon_i$ to occupying the localized orbital at site $i$. The hopping term moves an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) between neighbouring sites, producing kinetic delocalization and band dispersion; [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) conjugate hops make the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). The final term costs $U>0$ only when both [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states occupy the same local orbital. This is a [Hubbard model](../../../../../hubbard-model.md) with an on-site Coulomb penalty, not a classical interaction between magnetic dipoles.

At one [electron](../../../../../electron.md) per site and large $U$, a real hop is energetically costly, but a virtual hop into a doubly occupied intermediate state is possible. Opposite-spin states can exploit these virtual processes and lower their [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), while parallel spins cannot occupy the same one-orbital site. The resulting singlet–triplet splitting produces antiferromagnetic [Hubbard kinetic exchange](../../../../../hubbard-kinetic-exchange.md).

There is a terminology qualification. The paper calls this overlap-mediated process “[direct exchange](../../../../../direct-exchange.md)” because the hopping is directly between neighbouring magnetic orbitals. In the stricter distinction between [direct Coulomb exchange versus kinetic exchange](../../../../../direct-coulomb-exchange-versus-kinetic-exchange.md), the displayed [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) omits an explicit intersite Coulomb exchange integral. The antiferromagnetic $t^2/U$ mechanism derived below is kinetic exchange; a genuine intersite [direct exchange](../../../../../direct-exchange.md) term is separate and can compete with it. This distinction also separates the model from ligand-mediated [superexchange](../../../../../superexchange.md) in part f.
