<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [exciton](../../../../../exciton.md) is an electron-hole bound state and behaves approximately as a boson when the gas is dilute compared with the inverse exciton volume. A condensation experiment needs a long enough lifetime for cooling, high phase-space density and evidence of spontaneous coherence; excessive density causes ionization or a [Mott transition](../../../../../mott-transition.md) instead.

Bulk Cu₂O offers long-lived excitons, but heating, losses and [Auger recombination](../../../../../auger-recombination.md) complicate interpretation. Spectral narrowing or anomalous transport alone is insufficient: early claims required reinterpretation. Coupled quantum wells separate electron and hole layers, increasing lifetime and enabling cold indirect-exciton gases, though disorder, trapping and nonequilibrium flow must be distinguished from condensation.[2012 preprint: experimental history, losses and reinterpreted signatures](../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation-of-excitons-in-cu2o-progress-over-thirty-years-by-david-snoke-and-g-m-kavoulakis.md)

In a semiconductor microcavity, coherent strong coupling mixes an exciton with a light cavity photon to form [polaritons](../../../../../polariton.md) with very small effective mass. Nonresonant pumping supplies particles without imposing the condensate's phase. The 2006 CdTe experiment reported a threshold, strong low-momentum occupation, increased temporal coherence and long-range spatial coherence. These combined signatures support an [exciton-polariton condensate](../../../../../exciton-polariton-condensate.md); a threshold or bright emission alone does not distinguish it from photon lasing. Check that strong coupling persists above threshold. Finite lifetime and pumping make this an open, driven system, so thermalization must be demonstrated rather than inferred merely from the word condensate.[2006: occupation, coherence and polarization measurements](../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation-of-exciton-polaritons-by-j-kasprzak-and-others.md)
