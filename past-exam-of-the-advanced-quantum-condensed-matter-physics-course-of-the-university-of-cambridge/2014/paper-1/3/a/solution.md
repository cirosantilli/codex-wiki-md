<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In weak-probe [optical absorption](../../../../../optical-absorption.md), [Fermi's golden rule](../../../../../fermi-s-golden-rule.md) creates an [electron-hole pair](../../../../../electron-hole-pair.md), with negligible population depletion. Negligible photon momentum makes a [direct band gap](../../../../../direct-band-gap.md) transition nearly vertical in crystal momentum:

$$
\alpha(\omega)\propto\frac1\omega\sum_{cv\mathbf k}|\mathbf e\cdot\mathbf p_{cv}|^2(f_v-f_c)\delta(E_c-E_v-\hbar\omega).
$$

For allowed transitions between three-dimensional parabolic bands, the [joint density of states](../../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) yields **$\alpha\propto\sqrt{\hbar\omega-E_g}/\omega$** above the gap. Selection rules and polarization enter the dipole matrix element. An [indirect band gap](../../../../../indirect-band-gap.md) needs an additional phonon to conserve crystal momentum, with phonon absorption/emission weights and shifted thresholds.

Electron-hole attraction produces [Wannier excitons](../../../../../wannier-mott-exciton.md) below the continuum, approximately $E_n=E_g-R^*/n^2$, with bright $s$-state oscillator strength decreasing as $n^{-3}$. It also enhances the continuum edge. Screening, disorder and phonons broaden these features; heavy/light valence bands can create distinct series. This linear-response picture includes Coulomb binding but excludes a resolved coherent photon–exciton anticrossing: that would require a strong light–matter coupling treatment. “Weak coupling” here is distinguished from neglecting the electron-hole interaction altogether.
