<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a representative unstrained [zinc blende structure](../../../../../zinc-blende-structure.md) [III-V semiconductor](../../../../../iii-v-semiconductor.md) such as [gallium arsenide](../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md), take the valence-band maximum at the [Gamma point](../../../../../gamma-point.md) as zero. The near-centre ordering is an s-like $\Gamma_{6c}$ [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md) doublet above a p-like $\Gamma_{8v}$ valence quartet. [Spin-orbit interaction](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) puts the p-like $\Gamma_{7v}$ doublet below the quartet by $\Delta_{\rm so}$. These are the [zone-centre bands of a zinc-blende III-V semiconductor](../../../../../zone-centre-bands-of-a-zinc-blende-iii-v-semiconductor.md).

The $j=3/2$ quartet produces the [heavy-hole and light-hole bands](../../../../../heavy-hole-and-light-hole-bands.md). Along a chosen symmetry direction, their components have $|m_j|=3/2$ and $1/2$ respectively; their different curvatures define different positive hole [effective masses](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md). They are degenerate at zero wavevector in the unstrained cubic crystal, but separate away from it. The $j=1/2$ doublet is the [split-off valence band](../../../../../split-off-valence-band.md). To leading isotropic order,

$$
\boxed{E_c(k)\simeq E_g+\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_c},\quad
E_{v,\rm hh/lh}(k)\simeq-\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_{\rm hh/lh}},\quad
E_{v,\rm so}(k)\simeq-\Delta_{\rm so}-\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_{\rm so}}.}
$$

Valence-band curvature is negative for electrons; the corresponding hole masses are positive. Real valence bands warp with direction, so these are illustrative effective-mass parabolas rather than an exact spherical band structure.

The [k·p perturbation theory](../../../../../k%C2%B7p-perturbation-theory.md) couples the s-like and p-like states through momentum matrix elements. It controls the conduction-band mass, nonparabolicity and mixing of hole characters. Strain or quantum confinement can split the heavy/light degeneracy even at zero in-plane wavevector. The lack of inversion symmetry in zinc blende also permits spin splitting away from the zone centre; time-reversal symmetry still protects the zone-centre doublets.

The s–p electric-dipole transitions near $\Gamma$ are optically important and produce structures at $E_g$ and $E_g+\Delta_{\rm so}$ with polarization-dependent weights. For [direct band gap](../../../../../direct-band-gap.md) compounds the conduction minimum is also at $\Gamma$, allowing near-edge absorption and emission without a momentum-supplying phonon. **Not every III-V compound is direct-gap**, and wurtzite materials have an additional crystal-field splitting; the cubic scheme should not be imposed on every III-V semiconductor.
