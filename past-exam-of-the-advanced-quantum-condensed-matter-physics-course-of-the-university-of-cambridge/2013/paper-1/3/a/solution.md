<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [organic semiconductor](../../../../../organic-semiconductor.md) has conjugated molecular units with strong intramolecular bonding and comparatively weak intermolecular electronic overlap. Optical excitation commonly promotes a $\pi$ electron from an occupied molecular orbital toward an unoccupied one. Weak dielectric screening and narrow bands favour bound [Frenkel excitons](../../../../../frenkel-exciton.md), sometimes mixed with intermolecular charge-transfer states, rather than immediate production of free carriers. The optical excitation energy is below the unbound electron–hole threshold by the [exciton binding energy](../../../../../exciton-binding-energy.md), with relaxation conventions specified when comparing measured gaps.

The [electric-dipole interaction](../../../../../electric-dipole-interaction.md) selects bright states through their transition dipoles, producing polarization-dependent absorption in oriented samples. From a singlet ground state, strong electric-dipole absorption normally preserves spin and reaches singlet excitons; direct triplet excitation is weak without spin mixing. The [Franck-Condon principle](../../../../../franck-condon-principle.md) treats excitation as vertical on the nuclear-motion timescale. For one displaced harmonic vibration at low temperature, the [displaced-oscillator Franck-Condon progression](../../../../../displaced-oscillator-franck-condon-progression.md) has relative strengths

$$
\boxed{I_\nu\propto e^{-S}\frac{S^\nu}{\nu!},\qquad
\hbar\omega_\nu=E_{00}+\nu\hbar\omega_v.}
$$

Here the [Huang-Rhys factor](../../../../../huang-rhys-factor.md) $S$ measures the displacement and $E_{00}$ is the zero-vibration transition energy. Vibrational relaxation before emission produces a [Stokes shift](../../../../../stokes-shift.md). Molecular packing and excitonic coupling shift or split bright features and change their intensities; disorder and vibrations broaden them. Consequently a universal parabolic-band absorption edge is inappropriate for a localized molecular excitation.[Lectures4–7: molecular levels, Franck–Condon shifts, aggregates and Frenkel/charge-transfer excitons](../../../../../organic-optoelectronics-lecture-notes-by-vladimir-bulovic.md)
