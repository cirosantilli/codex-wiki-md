<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [quasi-one-dimensional quantum transport](../../../../../quasi-one-dimensional-quantum-transport.md), transverse confinement makes discrete subbands while motion along a [quantum wire](../../../../../quantum-wire.md) remains continuous. Only occupied propagating subbands conduct. For elastic scattering between equilibrated reservoirs, the [Landauer formula](../../../../../landauer-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{G=\frac{e^2}{h}\sum_{n,\sigma}\int dE\,[-f'(E)]T_{n\sigma}(E).}
$$

The sum explicitly counts spin. A perfectly transmitting spin-degenerate subband gives $2e^2/h$ at low temperature; a [quantum point contact](../../../../../quantum-point-contact.md) shows steps as a gate opens additional subbands. The one-dimensional [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) cancels the carrier velocity in the current, giving a conductance quantum rather than a material-dependent velocity factor. Finite two-terminal [contact resistance](../../../../../contact-resistance.md) remains even without internal scattering.

Transport is [ballistic transport](../../../../../ballistic-transport.md) for length $L\ll\ell$, where $\ell$ is the [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md). In a multichannel wire with $\ell\ll L$ below its localization length, scattering reduces transmission and gives approximately Ohmic behaviour. If the [phase coherence length](../../../../../phase-coherence-length.md) exceeds the sample length, amplitudes interfere; [weak localization](../../../../../weak-localization.md) and sample-dependent [universal conductance fluctuations](../../../../../universal-conductance-fluctuations.md) can appear. At greater lengths, generic coherent disordered wires undergo [Anderson localization](../../../../../anderson-localization.md), with localization length of order the channel number times $\ell$, up to symmetry-dependent factors. Thermal averaging and dephasing smear the quantum features.

Strong interactions require more than independent-electron transmission. A clean single-channel interacting wire is described at low energy by a [Luttinger liquid](../../../../../luttinger-liquid.md), with collective modes and possible [spin-charge separation](../../../../../spin-charge-separation.md); impurities can generate temperature-dependent power laws. Ideal contacts to noninteracting leads can still fix the two-terminal conductance of a clean wire, so it must not automatically be multiplied by a bulk Luttinger parameter.
