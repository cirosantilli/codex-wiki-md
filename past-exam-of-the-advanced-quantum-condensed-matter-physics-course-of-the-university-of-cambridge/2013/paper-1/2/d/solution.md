<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An in-plane [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) lowers the [Zeeman energy](../../../../../zeeman-energy.md) when moments align with it. It competes with antiferromagnetic [interlayer exchange coupling](../../../../../interlayer-exchange-coupling.md); the layers generally cant or switch toward a parallel configuration and ultimately saturate if the field overcomes coupling and [magnetic anisotropy](../../../../../magnetic-anisotropy.md). **The high-resistance antiparallel state evolves toward the lower-resistance parallel state.** The switching path and field scale depend on anisotropy, pinning and coupling strength, which are not numerically specified.
