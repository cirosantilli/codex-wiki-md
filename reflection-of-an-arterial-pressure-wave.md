# Reflection of an arterial pressure wave

↑ **Parent:** [Arterial pressure wave](arterial-pressure-wave.md)

A [hydraulic resistance](hydraulic-resistance.md) $R$ imposes $q=p/R$. Combining this with forward and backward [arterial pressure waves](arterial-pressure-wave.md), $p=p_++p_-$ and $q=Y(p_+-p_-)$, gives the displayed [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md). A matched load $RY=1$ has no reflected [pressure](pressure.md) wave, while a closed end has [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) one. At a network junction the combined downstream input admittance replaces $1/R$.
