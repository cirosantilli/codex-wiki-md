# The Structure of Some Crystals as Indicated by their Diffraction of X-rays by William Lawrence Bragg

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by William Lawrence Bragg](work-by-william-lawrence-bragg.md)

By [William Lawrence Bragg](william-lawrence-bragg.md), credited as W. L. Bragg. Published in Proceedings of the Royal Society of London, Series A 89, 248–277 (1913). The first page records receipt on 21 June and reading on 26 June 1913. The communication credit to [William Henry Bragg](william-henry-bragg.md) is distinct from authorship.

DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1913.0083](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1913.0083)

Inspected scan: [https://scispace.com/pdf/the-structure-of-some-crystals-as-indicated-by-their-1ugl7pojiz.pdf](https://scispace.com/pdf/the-structure-of-some-crystals-as-indicated-by-their-1ugl7pojiz.pdf)

Cited passages: p. 248, authorship and communication credits; pp. 262–263, Figures 8–9 and Tables VII–IX, sodium chloride sections, orientations, thickness comparison and spot intensities; pp. 264–265, Figure 10, interpenetrating face-centered arrays and the remaining atomic-assignment uncertainty. The historical scattering-weight interpretation is not modern X-ray scattering theory.
