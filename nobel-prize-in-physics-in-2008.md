# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2008

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One half went to [Yoichiro Nambu](yoichiro-nambu.md) for discovering the mechanism of [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) in subatomic [physics](physics-split.md). One quarter each went to [Makoto Kobayashi](makoto-kobayashi.md) and [Toshihide Maskawa](toshihide-maskawa.md) for explaining broken symmetry through a mechanism requiring at least three families of [quarks](quark.md).[Individual official 2008 summary: Nambu one half; Kobayashi and Maskawa one quarter each, with the two distinct symmetry-breaking contributions specified.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2008-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
