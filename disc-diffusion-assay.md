# Disc diffusion assay

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology.md)

A growth-inhibition assay in which a compound diffuses from a disc into a lawn of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). Halo size combines susceptibility with [molecular diffusion](molecular-diffusion.md) and growth conditions and is not directly a molecular [binding affinity](binding-affinity.md).
