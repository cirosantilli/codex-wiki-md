Here $\Delta(t)=\sin^2B(t)$. For $f(b)=\sin^2b$, $f'(b)=\sin2b$ and $f''(b)=2\cos2b$. Since standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) has [quadratic variation](../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) $dt$, [Itô's formula](../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
\boxed{d\Delta(t)=\sin(2B(t))\,dB(t)+\cos(2B(t))\,dt.}
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{V(t)=\sin(2B(t)),\qquad
D(t)=\cos(2B(t))=1-2\Delta(t).}
$$

The squared diffusion coefficient is $V(t)^2=4\Delta(t)(1-\Delta(t))$. Its sign depends on $B(t)$: one cannot replace $\sin(2B(t))$ by the nonnegative square root while retaining the same driving [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md).
