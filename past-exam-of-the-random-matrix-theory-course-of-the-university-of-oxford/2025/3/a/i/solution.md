↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) is

$$
\det(M-zI)=(\cos U-z)^2+\sin^2U
=z^2-2z\cos U+1.
$$

Its roots are $\cos U\pm i\sin U$, so

$$
\boxed{e^{i\Theta^+}=e^{iU},\qquad e^{i\Theta^-}=e^{-iU},
\qquad\Theta^\pm=\pm U\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

For example $(1,i)^T$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) for $e^{iU}$ and $(1,-i)^T$ for $e^{-iU}$. At $U=0$ or $\pi$ the two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) coincide; these events have [probability](../../../../../probability.md) zero under the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md).
