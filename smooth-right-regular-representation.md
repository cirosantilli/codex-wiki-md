# Smooth right regular representation

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth representation of a locally profinite group](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md)

The right action on [compactly supported locally constant functions](compactly-supported-locally-constant-function.md) is a [smooth representation](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md). For a [compact-open subgroup](compact-open-subgroup.md) $K$, its [fixed-vector space](fixed-vector-space.md) has basis the characteristic functions of cosets in $G/K$, since compact support on that discrete quotient is finite support. Consequently this representation is admissible exactly when $G$ is compact.
