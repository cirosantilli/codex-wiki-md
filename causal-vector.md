# Causal vector

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md)

A nonzero [causal vector](causal-vector.md) is timelike or null: $g(v,v)\le0$ with [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$. The zero vector is commonly included when stating the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md).
