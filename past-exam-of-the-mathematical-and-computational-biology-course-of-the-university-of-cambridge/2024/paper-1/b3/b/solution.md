<h1 id="b3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [least-squares normal equations](../../../../../normal-equations-for-linear-least-squares.md) to the transformed [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) and [vector](../../../../../vector.md):

$$
\boxed{(WA)^T(WA)\widehat x_w=(WA)^TWb},
$$

or equivalently

$$
\boxed{A^TW^TWA\widehat x_w=A^TW^TWb}.
$$

Here $W^TW=\operatorname{diag}(w_1^2,\ldots,w_m^2)$. A unique minimizing [vector](../../../../../vector.md) exists when the weighted design [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has full column rank; the equations themselves remain valid without that assumption.
