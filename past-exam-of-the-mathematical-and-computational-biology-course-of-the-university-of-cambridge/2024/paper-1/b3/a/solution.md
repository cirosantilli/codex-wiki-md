<h1 id="b3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the [diagonal matrix](../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) $W=\operatorname{diag}(w_1,\ldots,w_m)$. Multiplying a [vector](../../../../../vector.md) by $W$ scales its $i$th component by $w_i$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\|Wb-WAx\|^2=\sum_{i=1}^mw_i^2\bigl(b_i-(Ax)_i\bigr)^2}.
$$

The [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) objective for ordinary [linear least squares](../../../../../linear-least-squares.md) on $(WA,Wb)$ is exactly the [weighted least squares](../../../../../weighted-least-squares.md) objective, so the sets of minimizing [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) coincide. Zero weights simply discard the corresponding residuals.
