**Yes, the data are centred.** Both coordinate [sample means](../../../../../sample-mean.md) vanish:

$$
\bar x=\frac{7-8+1}{3}=0,\qquad
\bar y=\frac{1+6-7}{3}=0.
$$

Thus [principal component analysis](../../../../../principal-component-analysis.md) can be applied without further [data centering](../../../../../data-centering.md).
