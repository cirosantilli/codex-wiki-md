↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The one-dimensional [principal component analysis](../../../../../principal-component-analysis.md) reconstruction lies along $v_1=(4/5,-3/5)^T$ through the zero [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md). Its discarded [principal component](../../../../../principal-component.md) coordinate is $PC_2=0$, so

$$
\frac35x+\frac45y=0,\qquad y=-\frac34x.
$$

At $x=6$, **$\boxed{\widehat y=-4.5}$.** This intersects the retained [principal component](../../../../../principal-component.md) line with the vertical line at the supplied $x$. It is a reconstruction from that line, rather than an ordinary [linear regression](../../../../../linear-regression-split.md) of $y$ on $x$.
