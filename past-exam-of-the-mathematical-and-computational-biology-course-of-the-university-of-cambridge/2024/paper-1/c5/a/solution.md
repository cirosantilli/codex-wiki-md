↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [phylogenetic epoch](../../../../../phylogenetic-epoch.md) is a time interval between successive branching events during which the number of ancestral branches is constant. Read backward from the four sampled leaves: there are four branches until the first merger, then three until the next, then two until their common root. Write the random durations as $T_4,T_3,T_2$, with [expected values](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $E_k=\mathbb E[T_k]$.

The original solution diagram below places time horizontally and labels each branch by its number of descendant leaves. Type A is the pectinate topology; type B is the balanced topology. The coloured time strips identify the three [phylogenetic epochs](../../../../../phylogenetic-epoch.md); their drawn widths are schematic, not estimated times.
