<h1 id="c5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Under the stated topology [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md), $P(A)=2/3$ and $P(B)=1/3$. Averaging the branch lengths from part (b), the expected length producing the folded $3:1$ class is

$$
B_{3:1}=\tfrac23(4E_4+2E_3+2E_2)+\tfrac13(4E_4+2E_3)
=4E_4+2E_3+\tfrac43E_2.
$$

The factor $2E_2$ in type A includes both the one-descendant and three-descendant root branches. The expected length producing the $2:2$ class is

$$
B_{2:2}=\tfrac23E_3+\tfrac13(E_3+2E_2)
=E_3+\tfrac23E_2.
$$

With a common [mutation rate](../../../../../mutation-rate.md) per unit branch length, it cancels from the ratio of expected counts in this [folded site frequency spectrum](../../../../../folded-site-frequency-spectrum.md):

$$
R=\frac{4E_4+2E_3+\tfrac43E_2}{E_3+\tfrac23E_2}.
$$

For the [Yule process](../../../../../yule-process.md),

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm Yule}=\frac{1+2/3+2/3}{1/3+1/3}=\frac72=3.5}.
$$

For [Kingman's coalescent](../../../../../kingman-s-coalescent.md),

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm coal}=\frac{2/3+2/3+4/3}{1/3+2/3}=\frac83\approx2.67}.
$$

These are ratios of ensemble expected mutation counts, not the [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of the random ratio in one tree. The stipulated topology weights apply to both processes, and their waiting-time rates depend only on the current number of branches.
