Here $s=\log(0.4\times0.7\times0.8\times0.9)=-1.601469743$. The [maximum-likelihood estimate](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) and estimated [standard error](../../../../../standard-error.md) from the [observed Fisher information](../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) are

$$
\widehat\theta=-\frac4s-1=1.497705634,\qquad
\widehat{\operatorname{se}}(\widehat\theta)=J(\widehat\theta)^{-1/2}
=\frac{\widehat\theta+1}{\sqrt4}=1.248852817.
$$

The 95% [Wald confidence interval](../../../../../wald-confidence-interval.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta\pm1.960\,\widehat{\operatorname{se}}(\widehat\theta)
=(-0.95005,\ 3.94546)}.
$$

The whole reported [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md) lies in $\theta>-1$, though its lower endpoint is close to the boundary. With only four observations, the [normal approximation](../../../../../normal-approximation.md) underlying this [Wald confidence interval](../../../../../wald-confidence-interval.md) need not provide accurate 95% coverage.
