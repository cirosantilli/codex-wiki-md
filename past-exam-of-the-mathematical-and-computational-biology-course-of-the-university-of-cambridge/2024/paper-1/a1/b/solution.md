<h1 id="a1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [moment](../../../../../moment.md) of order $q$ with $\theta+q>-1$, integration of the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E[X^q]=(\theta+1)\int_0^1x^{\theta+q}\,dx=\frac{\theta+1}{\theta+q+1}.
$$

Thus the [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\frac{\theta+1}{\theta+2}},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{\theta+1}{\theta+3}-\left(\frac{\theta+1}{\theta+2}\right)^2
=\boxed{\frac{\theta+1}{(\theta+3)(\theta+2)^2}}.
$$

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) on $(0,1)$ is $F(x)=x^{\theta+1}$. Its unique [median](../../../../../median.md) $m$ satisfies $F(m)=1/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{m=2^{-1/(\theta+1)}}.
$$
