<h1 id="a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) must have [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) one. For $\theta>-1$,

$$
1=A\int_0^1x^\theta\,dx=\frac{A}{\theta+1},\qquad \boxed{A=\theta+1}.
$$

The restriction **$\theta>-1$** is essential: for $\theta\leq-1$ the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) diverges and no positive normalizing constant exists.
