↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [independent and identically distributed](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) observations, multiply the [probability density functions](../../../../../probability-density-function.md). On the admissible parameter range $\theta>-1$, the [likelihood function](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) and [log-likelihood](../../../../../log-likelihood.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L(\theta)=(\theta+1)^N\prod_{i=1}^Nx_i^\theta},\qquad
\boxed{\ell(\theta)=N\log(\theta+1)+\theta\sum_{i=1}^N\log x_i}.
$$

These expressions presume that every observation lies strictly between zero and one; an observation outside that support would give zero [likelihood](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) under this model.
