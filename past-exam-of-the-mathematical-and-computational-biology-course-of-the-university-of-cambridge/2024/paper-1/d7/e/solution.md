<h1 id="d7/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The conserved total molecule amount fixes the final common [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md):

$$
c_\infty=\frac{V_cc_c(0)+V_nc_n(0)}{V_c+V_n}.
$$

Solve $c_c-c_n=\Delta c(t)$ together with $V_cc_c+V_nc_n=(V_c+V_n)c_\infty$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{c_c(t)=c_\infty+\frac{V_n}{V_c+V_n}\Delta c(t)},\qquad
\boxed{c_n(t)=c_\infty-\frac{V_c}{V_c+V_n}\Delta c(t)}.
$$

Substituting the decaying [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) difference from part (d) supplies both explicit time dependences. The [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md) approach the volume-weighted initial average, which need not be the arithmetic average if the compartment volumes differ.
