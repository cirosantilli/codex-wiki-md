↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For unbiased one-dimensional [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md), the [mean squared displacement](../../../../../mean-squared-displacement.md) is $\langle x^2(t)\rangle=2Dt$. With $t=n\tau$, compare this to the mutant expression:

$$
2Dn\tau=\frac16n\delta^2,\qquad D=\frac{\delta^2}{12\tau}.
$$

Therefore the mutant [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) is

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{(10\,\mu\mathrm m)^2}{12(50\,\mathrm s)}
=\frac16\,\mu\mathrm m^2\mathrm s^{-1}\approx0.167\,\mu\mathrm m^2/\mathrm s}.
$$
