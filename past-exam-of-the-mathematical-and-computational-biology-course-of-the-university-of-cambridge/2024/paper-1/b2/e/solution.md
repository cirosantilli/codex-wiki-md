<h1 id="b2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At a nonzero [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) of the nonlinear [stage-structured population model](../../../../../stage-structured-population-model.md), the adult balance gives $d\bar z=c\bar y$. Substitute $\bar y=d\bar z/c$ into the juvenile equation and cancel $\bar z>0$:

$$
\frac dc=\frac r{(b+\bar z)^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\bar z=\sqrt{\frac{cr}{d}}-b,\qquad
\bar y=\frac dc\left(\sqrt{\frac{cr}{d}}-b\right)}.
$$

Both coordinates are positive precisely when **$\boxed{cr>db^2}$.**
