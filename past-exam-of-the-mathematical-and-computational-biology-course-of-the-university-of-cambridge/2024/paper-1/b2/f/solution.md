<h1 id="b2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) at the extinction [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is

$$
J_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&r/b^2\\c&1-d\end{pmatrix},
$$

with [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md)

$$
p(q)=q^2-(1-d)q-\frac{cr}{b^2}.
$$

Under $cr>db^2$, $p(1)=d-cr/b^2<0$, whereas $p(q)\to\infty$ as $q\to\infty$. Hence its positive [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
q_+=\frac{1-d+\sqrt{(1-d)^2+4cr/b^2}}2
$$

is greater than one. Its [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) can be taken with both components positive: choose $(r/(b^2q_+),1)^T$. Thus there are biologically admissible positive perturbations that grow. **The extinction [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is unstable** by [linear stability analysis](../../../../../linear-stability.md) for a [discrete dynamical system](../../../../../discrete-dynamical-system-split.md).
