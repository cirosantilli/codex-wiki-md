↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [squared-denominator population map](../../../../../squared-denominator-population-map.md), a [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) satisfies

$$
\bar x=\frac{r\bar x}{(b+\bar x)^2}.
$$

There is always the extinction [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) $\bar x=0$. For a positive [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md), divide by $\bar x$ and use $b+\bar x>0$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\bar x=\sqrt r-b,\qquad r>b^2}.
$$

At equality the candidate merges with zero and is not positive.
