<h1 id="b2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the [squared-denominator population map](../../../../../squared-denominator-population-map.md):

$$
F'(x)=\frac{r(b-x)}{(b+x)^3}.
$$

At the positive [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md), $b+\bar x=\sqrt r$, so its [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is

$$
F'(\bar x)=\frac{2b}{\sqrt r}-1.
$$

Since $0<b/\sqrt r<1$, we have $-1<F'(\bar x)<1$. A small perturbation $\varepsilon_t$ obeys $\varepsilon_{t+1}=F'(\bar x)\varepsilon_t+O(\varepsilon_t^2)$, and hence shrinks locally. Thus **the positive [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is locally asymptotically stable** by [fixed point stability for an iteration](../../../../../fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md).
