Read the ten residue–residue columns from the [BLOSUM62](../../../../../blosum62.md) [substitution matrix](../../../../../substitution-matrix.md). In their order in the supplied [sequence alignment](../../../../../sequence-alignment.md), their contributions are

$$
-2+4+0-2-1+2+5+5+6+7=24.
$$

There are three gap positions, each incurring the stated [linear gap penalty](../../../../../linear-gap-penalty.md) of four, including the leading gap. Therefore **the alignment score is $\boxed{24-3(4)=12}$.**
