The number specifies the sequence-identity threshold used when clustering related sequences before deriving a [BLOSUM](../../../../../blosum.md) [substitution matrix](../../../../../substitution-matrix.md). At 62%, sufficiently similar sequences are grouped and their within-cluster comparisons are excluded. Lowering the threshold merges more sequences, emphasizing comparisons across greater divergence.

Consequently **BLOSUM80 is suited to closer relatives, BLOSUM45 to more divergent sequences, and [BLOSUM62](../../../../../blosum62.md) is intermediate**. The numbers do not specify a required identity of the query and target, nor a number of evolutionary time steps.[pp. 10916–10917, clustering levels and performance across divergence](../../../../../amino-acid-substitution-matrices-from-protein-blocks-by-steven-henikoff-and-jorja-g-henikoff.md)
