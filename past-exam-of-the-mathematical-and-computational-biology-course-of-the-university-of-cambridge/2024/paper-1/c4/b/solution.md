↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A diagonal [substitution matrix](../../../../../substitution-matrix.md) score measures enrichment over a background match, rather than simply rewarding an identical letter. [tryptophan residues](../../../../../tryptophan-residue.md) are comparatively rare, so a W–W match has a small chance background frequency $p_W^2$. Its observed conservation produces a much larger ratio $q_{WW}/p_W^2$ than the corresponding ratio for the more common [leucine](../../../../../leucine.md). The [BLOSUM62](../../../../../blosum62.md) diagonal therefore awards W–W more than L–L. Both the background frequency and the observed pair frequency matter; rarity alone does not determine a diagonal score.[Methods, p. 10916, observed/expected pair ratios; BLOSUM62 score matrix, W–W and L–L entries](../../../../../amino-acid-substitution-matrices-from-protein-blocks-by-steven-henikoff-and-jorja-g-henikoff.md)
