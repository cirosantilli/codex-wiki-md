The retained mean conditional-product log score, **$-74.89$**, is higher than the observed-pattern score, **$-102.14$**, because sampled patterns can improve agreement between neighbouring [CpG sites](../../../../../cpg-site.md) while tolerating some observational errors. The observation-matching pattern maximizes the observation [likelihood function](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) alone, whereas this score also rewards the assumed spatial structure, and its posterior mean is not its maximum.
