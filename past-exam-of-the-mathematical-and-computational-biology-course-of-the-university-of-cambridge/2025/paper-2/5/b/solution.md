<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md), the [survival function](../../../../../survival-function.md) at the [median](../../../../../median.md) equals one half. Thus $e^{-10\delta}=1/2$, and

$$
\boxed{\delta=\frac{\ln2}{10}\approx0.0693147\ \text{day}^{-1}}.
$$

The corresponding mean care time is $1/\delta=10/\ln2\approx14.43$ days; a ten-day [median](../../../../../median.md) should not be substituted as the mean.
