↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Waiting times of a Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-interarrival-times.md) have an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with the arrival rate. Measuring time in days, the [survival function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) is $P(W>w)=e^{-\alpha w}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P(W>1)=e^{-3}\approx0.0497871},
$$

or **about $4.98\%$**.
