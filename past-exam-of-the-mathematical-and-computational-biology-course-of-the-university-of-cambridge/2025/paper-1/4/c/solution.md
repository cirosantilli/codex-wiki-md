The [deviance](../../../../../exponential-family-deviance.md) entries are $-2\ell$ for these fitted models. The completed values are

$$
\boxed{D_0=318.00,\quad D_1=305.96,\quad T=D_0-D_1=12.04,\quad\mathrm{df}=1}.
$$

The [likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test.md) compares a model with one age coefficient against its restriction $\beta_1=0$. Its small [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md), approximately $0.000529$, supports a positive age association under the assumed [Gaussian linear mixed model](../../../../../gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md). Refitting with [maximum likelihood estimation](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimation-split.md) is essential here: [restricted maximum likelihood](../../../../../restricted-maximum-likelihood.md) values from models with different fixed-effect design matrices cannot be compared by this [likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test.md).
