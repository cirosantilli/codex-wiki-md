<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}$ has [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2$. On its [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md), write vectors as $(a,a,c)^{\mathsf T}$. The [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) becomes

$$
\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\4&4\end{pmatrix}\binom ac=\lambda\binom ac,
\qquad (4-\lambda)^2-8=0.
$$

Hence the ordered [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the [covariance matrix](../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) are $4+2\sqrt2$, $2$, and $4-2\sqrt2$. The first two [principal components](../../../../../principal-component.md) explain

$$
\boxed{\frac{(4+2\sqrt2)+2}{10}=\frac{3+\sqrt2}{5}\simeq88.28\%}
$$

of the total [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md). The denominator is the sum of all [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), which equals the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md).
