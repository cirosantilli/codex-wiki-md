After subtracting the same training [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md), E has vector $e=(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}$ and [principal component scores](../../../../../principal-component-score.md) $(0,\sqrt2)$. The difference from D lies entirely along the first [principal component](../../../../../principal-component.md). Its [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) in the retained plane is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{(2+\sqrt2)^2+(\sqrt2-\sqrt2)^2}=2+\sqrt2\simeq3.4142}.
$$

The new point must use the original centring and loadings; recomputing them with E included would change the question.
