↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The vector of column [sample means](../../../../../sample-mean.md) is $\bar x=(1,3,2)$. Subtract it from each observation. The centred data matrix, with cell types in the original order, is

$$
\boxed{X_c=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-3&-2\\-1&1&-2\\-1&1&2\\3&1&2\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Each column now has [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md) zero.
