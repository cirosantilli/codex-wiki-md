$$
\boxed{(u_1^{\mathsf T}d,u_2^{\mathsf T}d)=(2+\sqrt2,\sqrt2)}.
$$

Normalizing the loadings matters: a direction alone does not fix the scale of the [coordinates](../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md).
