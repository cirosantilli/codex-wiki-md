↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The second-largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $2$, with [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}$. A unit [principal component loading](../../../../../principal-component-loading.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{u_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}}.
$$

It is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to the first loading $u_1=(1,1,\sqrt2)^{\mathsf T}/2$. Either sign of a loading is equivalent, provided its [principal component scores](../../../../../principal-component-score.md) use the same sign convention.
