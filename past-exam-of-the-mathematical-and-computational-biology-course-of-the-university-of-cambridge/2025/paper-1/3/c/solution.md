<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The total [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $\operatorname{tr}\Sigma=10$. Retaining two original genes preserves the sum of their marginal [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md); the largest choice includes the third gene and either of the first two, giving $4+3=7$. Thus the largest proportion is **70%**. This refers to retaining measured coordinates themselves, rather than replacing them by [linear combinations](../../../../../linear-combination.md) as in [principal component analysis](../../../../../principal-component-analysis.md).
