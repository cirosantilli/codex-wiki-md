<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $n_k=|C_k|$ and suppose the cluster is nonempty. Expanding the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) through its [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives

$$
\|x-z\|^2=\|(x-\bar x_k)-(z-\bar x_k)\|^2
=\|x-\bar x_k\|^2-2(x-\bar x_k)^{\mathsf T}(z-\bar x_k)
+\|z-\bar x_k\|^2.
$$

Sum over $x\in C_k$. The final term does not depend on $x$, so its sum is $n_k\|z-\bar x_k\|^2$, which proves the requested identity. This expansion is the starting point for the [centroid sum-of-squares identity](../../../../../centroid-sum-of-squares-identity.md).
