↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Combining the first two parts gives the [centroid sum-of-squares identity](../../../../../centroid-sum-of-squares-identity.md)

$$
J_k(z)=J_k(\bar x_k)+n_k\|z-\bar x_k\|^2.
$$

The added term is nonnegative and, since $n_k>0$, vanishes exactly at $z=\bar x_k$. Thus the unique minimizing [centroid](../../../../../centroid.md) in this step of [K-means clustering](../../../../../k-means-clustering.md) is

$$
\boxed{z_k=\bar x_k}.
$$

An empty cluster needs a separate algorithmic convention because its [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md) is undefined and its objective is identically zero.
