<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [score function](../../../../../informant-function.md) and the [observed Fisher information](../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) are

$$
\ell'(\theta)=\frac{2N}{\theta}-S,\qquad
j(\theta)=-\ell''(\theta)=\frac{2N}{\theta^2}>0.
$$

Solving the [score equation](../../../../../score-equation.md) gives the [maximum-likelihood estimate](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md)

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta=\frac{2N}{S}=\frac2{\bar x}}.
$$

The positive [observed Fisher information](../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) means that the [log-likelihood](../../../../../log-likelihood.md) is strictly [concave](../../../../../concave-function.md) on $\theta>0$. Its unique [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) is therefore the global maximum. Equivalently, the [score function](../../../../../informant-function.md) changes from positive to negative at this point. At the estimate, $j(\widehat\theta)=S^2/(2N)$.
