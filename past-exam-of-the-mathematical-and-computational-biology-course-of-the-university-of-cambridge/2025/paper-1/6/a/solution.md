<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For independent [geometric random variables](../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) supported on $1,2,\ldots$, put $S=\sum_i x_i$. Multiplication of their [probability mass functions](../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) gives the [likelihood function](../../../../../likelihood-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L(p)=p^n(1-p)^{S-n}},\qquad0<p<1.
$$

There are $n$ terminal successes and $S-n$ preceding failures.
