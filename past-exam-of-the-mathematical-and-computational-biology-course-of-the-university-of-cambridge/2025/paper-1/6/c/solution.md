↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of a [beta distribution](../../../../../beta-distribution.md), the [posterior mean](../../../../../posterior-mean.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[p\mid x]=\frac{a+n}{a+b+S}}.
$$

Equivalently, it is the weighted average

$$
\frac{a+b}{a+b+S}\frac{a}{a+b}
+\frac{S}{a+b+S}\frac{n}{S}
$$

of the [prior mean](../../../../../prior-mean.md) and the [maximum-likelihood estimate](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) $n/S$.
