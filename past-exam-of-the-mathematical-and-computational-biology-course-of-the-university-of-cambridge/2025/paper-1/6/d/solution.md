<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For fixed prior parameters, divide numerator and denominator by $n$:

$$
\mathbb E[p\mid x]=\frac{1+a/n}{S/n+(a+b)/n}\longrightarrow\frac1{\mathbb E X}=p_0,
$$

under independent sampling from a [geometric distribution](../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) with true success probability $p_0>0$. The [law of large numbers](../../../../../law-of-large-numbers.md) gives $S/n\to1/p_0$. In a large finite sample the [posterior mean](../../../../../posterior-mean.md) is close to $n/S=1/\bar x$, the [maximum-likelihood estimate](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md). The fixed prior's influence vanishes, and the estimate is [consistent](../../../../../consistency-statistics.md).
