This is the [Nicholson-Bailey model](../../../../../nicholson-bailey-model.md) for a [host](../../../../../host-biology.md)-[parasitoid](../../../../../parasitoid.md) interaction with discrete generations. The factor $r$ is the host multiplication factor in the absence of parasitism; $a$ measures effective attack or searching intensity per parasitoid; and $b$ is the number of next-generation parasitoids produced per attacked host, with any survival or unit conversion absorbed into it.

If the number of attacks on a host has a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $aP_t$, then the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of no attack is $e^{-aP_t}$. Consequently $e^{-aP_t}$ is the surviving, unparasitized host fraction, and $1-e^{-aP_t}$ is the attacked fraction. The two update equations count host descendants from surviving hosts and parasitoid descendants from attacked hosts, respectively.
