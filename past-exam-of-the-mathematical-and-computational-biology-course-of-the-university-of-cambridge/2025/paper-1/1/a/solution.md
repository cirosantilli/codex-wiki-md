It tests whether the input [string](../../../../../string.md) is a [palindrome](../../../../../palindrome.md): reversing the characters leaves the [string](../../../../../string.md) unchanged. Comparisons are case-sensitive and include spaces and punctuation. For example, `mystery1("level")` returns **`True`**, whereas `mystery1("lever")` returns **`False`**.
