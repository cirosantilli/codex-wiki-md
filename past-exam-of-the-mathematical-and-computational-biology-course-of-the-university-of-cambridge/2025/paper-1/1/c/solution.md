<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

It filters the first [list](../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md) by membership in the second [list](../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md), retaining the order and all repetitions of qualifying entries in the first [list](../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md). For example, `mystery3([3, 1, 3, 2], [3, 4])` returns **`[3, 3]`**. Thus it is related to [set intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md), but its result is a [list](../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md) and does not discard repeated elements.
