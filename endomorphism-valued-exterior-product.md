# Endomorphism-valued exterior product

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior covariant derivative](exterior-covariant-derivative.md)

Exterior product of [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) combined with [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) composition is associative and generally noncommutative. For an endomorphism-valued one-form $a$, $(a\wedge a)(u,v)=[a(u),a(v)]$. The [endomorphism bundle connection](endomorphism-bundle-connection.md) extends as a graded derivation of this algebra.
