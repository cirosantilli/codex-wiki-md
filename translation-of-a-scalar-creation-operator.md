# Translation of a scalar creation operator

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-ordered free scalar four-momentum](normal-ordered-free-scalar-four-momentum.md)

The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) gives $[P^\mu,a_p^\dagger]=p^\mu a_p^\dagger$ for a free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md). Differentiate the exponential conjugation: its derivative is a scalar multiple of the same operator, yielding the displayed phase. The [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md) receives the opposite phase, so at equal time $e^{-i\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf y}\phi(\mathbf x)e^{i\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf y}=\phi(\mathbf x+\mathbf y)$. The [spatial translation operator](spatial-translation-operator.md) translates a state's position wavefunction in the opposite argument direction.
