# MYC proto-oncogene

↑ **Parent:** [Gene](gene.md)

The [MYC proto-oncogene](myc-proto-oncogene.md) regulates growth-associated gene-expression programmes. Retroviral promoter insertion near it provided an experimental example of transformation through deregulated expression of a cellular gene rather than carriage of a viral oncogene.
