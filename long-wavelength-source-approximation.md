# Long-wavelength source approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded quadrupole field](retarded-quadrupole-field.md)

Dropping the position-dependent source delay in the leading far-zone retarded field requires source size $d$ to be small compared with its variation time $\tau$, in units $c=1$. The [retarded quadrupole field](retarded-quadrupole-field.md) also requires $d/r\ll1$. For a typical nonrelativistic bound source, $\tau\sim d/v$ implies this long-wavelength condition; an arbitrarily rapidly oscillating small displacement is not controlled merely by its instantaneous speed.
