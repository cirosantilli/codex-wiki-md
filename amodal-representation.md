# Amodal representation

↑ **Parent:** [Neural representation](neural-representation.md)

A representation whose relevant information is independent of a particular sensory or output modality. Shared mean activation across modalities is weaker evidence than generalisation of information about the represented content, with confounded stimuli and responses controlled.
