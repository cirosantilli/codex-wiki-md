# Constant speed

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity](velocity.md)

A path has [constant speed](constant-speed.md) when the magnitude of its [velocity](velocity.md) does not vary with [time](time-in-physics.md). Its [acceleration](acceleration.md) may still be nonzero: uniform [circular motion](circular-motion.md) has turning [velocity](velocity.md) and inward [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md). Constant [velocity](velocity.md) is stronger, requiring unchanged direction as well as unchanged [speed](speed.md).
