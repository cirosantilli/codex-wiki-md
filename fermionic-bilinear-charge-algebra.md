# Fermionic bilinear charge algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md)

For constant internal matrices acting on [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md), the equal-time [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) give

$$
[\psi_i^\dagger\psi_j,\psi_k^\dagger\psi_l]=\delta_{jk}\psi_i^\dagger\psi_l-\delta_{il}\psi_k^\dagger\psi_j
$$

with the corresponding spatial delta functions for fields. The quartic terms cancel after fermion reordering. Integrating proves the displayed algebra for well-defined global [Noether charges](noether-charge.md), with [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) and a regulator that preserves the nonanomalous internal symmetry. Orthogonal [chiral projectors](chiral-projector.md) make the left/right mixed commutators zero. This reduces current-charge closure to a finite matrix calculation.
