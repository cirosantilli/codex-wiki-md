# Periodic resolution of a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Integral cohomology of a finite cyclic group](integral-cohomology-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md)

If $C_m=\langle t\rangle$ and $N=1+t+\cdots+t^{m-1}$, a [free resolution](free-resolution.md) of the trivial $\mathbb ZC_m$-module alternates multiplication by $t-1$ and $N$. Applying $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb ZC_m}(-,\mathbb Z)$ for the trivial action alternates the zero map and multiplication by $m$.
