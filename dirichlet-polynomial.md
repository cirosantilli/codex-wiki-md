# Dirichlet polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)

A [Dirichlet polynomial](dirichlet-polynomial.md) is a finite [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md), $A(s)=\sum_{n\le X}a_n n^{-s}$. Vertical integration separates equal product indices from oscillatory unequal ones.
