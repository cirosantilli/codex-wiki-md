# Poisson onset-bin independence

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md)

Mark each member of an independent Poisson infection cohort by its symptom-onset interval, using independent delays. [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md) gives independent onset-bin counts, and sums over independent cohorts remain independent Poisson counts. Fixed cohort totals instead induce multinomial dependence. The marking assumptions are essential.
