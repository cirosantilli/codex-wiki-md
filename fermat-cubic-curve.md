# Fermat cubic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Pencil of plane curves](pencil-of-plane-curves.md)

The Fermat cubic $x^3+y^3+z^3=0$ is a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md): its three first partial derivatives vanish simultaneously only at the forbidden zero vector. The [genus of a smooth plane curve](genus-of-a-smooth-plane-curve.md) formula gives genus one.

This is the degree-three case of a Fermat equation; in general $x^n+y^n+z^n=0$ defines a Fermat curve.
