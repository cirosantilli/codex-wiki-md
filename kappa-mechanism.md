# Kappa mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar pulsation](stellar-pulsation.md)

The opacity mechanism drives [stellar pulsation](stellar-pulsation.md) when compression increases [opacity](opacity.md) in a layer, trapping heat at a phase that feeds mechanical oscillation. Helium [ionization](ionization.md) zones provide this driving in [Cepheid variables](cepheid-variable.md).
