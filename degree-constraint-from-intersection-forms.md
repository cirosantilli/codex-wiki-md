# Degree constraint from intersection forms

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

For a map $f:M\to N$ of connected closed oriented four-manifolds, naturality of the [cup product](cup-product.md) gives

$$
Q_M(f^*u,f^*v)=(\deg f)Q_N(u,v).
$$

When $\deg f\ne0$, nondegeneracy makes $f^*:H^2(N;\mathbb Q)\to H^2(M;\mathbb Q)$ injective. Consequently the target [intersection form](intersection-form.md), multiplied by the [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md), must embed into the source form. For equal second [Betti numbers](betti-number.md), a matrix $P$ for pullback satisfies $P^{\mathsf T}Q_MP=(\deg f)Q_N$; taking determinants often rules out nonzero degrees. In particular, a two-dimensional indefinite source form cannot realize a nonzero multiple of a two-dimensional definite target form.
