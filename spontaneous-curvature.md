# Spontaneous curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

[spontaneous curvature](spontaneous-curvature.md) is the preferred curvature of a membrane or monolayer in a specified elastic description. Headgroup hydration, chain geometry and leaflet composition can change it; the sign depends on the chosen surface-normal convention.
