# Uniform superposition state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state preparation](quantum-state-preparation.md)

In an $N$-dimensional [computational basis](computational-basis.md), the uniform superposition state is

$$
|s_N\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_{x=0}^{N-1}|x\rangle.
$$

Measurement in that basis gives the [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md). For $N=2^n$, [Hadamard gates](hadamard-gate.md) prepare it from $|0^n\rangle$. Its reflection $2|s_N\rangle\langle s_N|-I$ is a [Grover diffusion operator](grover-diffusion-operator.md).
