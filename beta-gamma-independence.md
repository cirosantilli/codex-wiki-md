# Beta-gamma independence

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)

If $X$ and $Y$ are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with [Gamma distributions](gamma-distribution.md) of shapes $a,b>0$ and the same rate $\theta>0$, then $U=X/(X+Y)$ has the [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) with parameters $(a,b)$, and $V=X+Y$ has the [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) of shape $a+b$ and rate $\theta$. Moreover $U$ and $V$ are independent. The inverse [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $(x,y)=(uv,(1-u)v)$ has absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $v$, which makes the [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) factorize on the product domain $0<u<1$, $v>0$. The common rate is essential for this factorization.
