# Joints theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Joint of a line collection](joint-of-a-line-collection.md)

The number of [joints of a line collection](joint-of-a-line-collection.md) formed by $L$ distinct real affine lines in $\mathbb R^n$ is at most $C_n L^{n/(n-1)}$. The [pruning and minimal-degree polynomial argument](pruning-and-minimal-degree-polynomial-argument.md) proves this bound. The exponent is sharp: the coordinate-line grid has $m^n$ [joints of a line collection](joint-of-a-line-collection.md) and $n m^{n-1}$ lines. In three dimensions the bound is $C L^{3/2}$.
