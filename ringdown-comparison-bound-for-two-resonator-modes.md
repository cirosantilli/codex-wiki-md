# Ringdown comparison bound for two resonator modes

↑ **Parent:** [Quality factor from ringdown](quality-factor-from-ringdown.md)

For two weakly damped [normal modes](normal-mode.md) with initial [intensity](intensity-physics.md) ratio $R_0>1$, independent exponential [energy](energy-split.md) decay gives $I_1(t)/I_2(t)=R_0e^{-t(\omega_1/Q_1-\omega_2/Q_2)}$. An observed reversal to a ratio below one implies the displayed upper bound on $Q_1$, provided the denominator is positive. A strict [intensity](intensity-physics.md) comparison does not determine a unique [quality factor](q-factor.md); equality fixes only the crossing threshold.
