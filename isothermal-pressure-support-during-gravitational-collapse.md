# Isothermal pressure support during gravitational collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical fluid dynamics](astrophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

For fixed mass and [temperature](temperature.md), $p\propto L^{-3}$, so the pressure-support scale $pL^3$ stays constant while gravitational binding scales as $L^{-1}$. Isothermal [pressure](pressure.md) therefore becomes relatively less important during homologous contraction.
