# Electron crystallization on liquid helium

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner crystal](wigner-crystal.md)

The 1979 experiment of [C. C. Grimes](c-c-grimes.md) and colleagues studied a two-dimensional sheet of electrons above liquid helium. It reported crystallization at densities $3\times10^8$ to $9\times10^8\,\mathrm{cm}^{-2}$, with melting temperatures $0.35$ to $0.65\,\mathrm K$. The sheet was classical: Coulomb energy competed with thermal motion, unlike the kinetic-energy comparison used for a zero-temperature degenerate [Wigner crystal](wigner-crystal.md). It supplies evidence for electron ordering while keeping these two physical regimes distinct.[Physical Review Letters 42, pp. 795–798, abstract: classical sheet and reported density and melting-temperature intervals](evidence-for-a-liquid-to-crystal-phase-transition-in-a-classical-two-dimensional-sheet-of-electrons-by-c-c-grimes-and-others.md)
