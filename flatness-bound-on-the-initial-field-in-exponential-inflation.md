# Flatness bound on the initial field in exponential inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Inflationary solution of the flatness problem](inflationary-solution-of-the-flatness-problem.md)

For positive slope parameter $A$, the exponential-potential field increases during [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md). The quantity $aH$ grows by $e^{(1-\epsilon_H)N}$ with constant $\epsilon_H=A^2/(16\pi)$ and $N=8\pi(\phi_R-\phi_i)/(Am_{\rm pl})$. Starting from order-one [curvature](curvature.md) and demanding order-one or smaller present [curvature](curvature.md) gives the displayed upper bound on the initial field. A specified tighter final [curvature](curvature.md) tolerance adds one-half its logarithm to the numerator. Thus sufficient [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) sets an upper, not a lower, initial-field bound for this sign of the potential slope. Instantaneous [reheating](reheating.md) and subsequent entropy conservation determine $a_RH_R/a_0H_0$.
