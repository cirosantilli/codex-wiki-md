# Abelian progression

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized arithmetic progression](generalized-arithmetic-progression.md)

An abelian progression is a [generalized arithmetic progression](generalized-arithmetic-progression.md) inside an [abelian group](abelian-group.md). It is the image of an integer box under a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md).
