# Multipartite hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A multipartite [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) has a specified partition of its [vertex of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md) [set](set-split.md) into parts, and every [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) meets each part in at most one [vertex of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md). For an $r$-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) with $r$ parts, an [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) chooses exactly one [vertex of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md) from each part; its indicator is therefore a [function](function-split.md) on the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of those parts. A quadripartite three-uniform [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) instead has four possible types of [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md), according to the omitted part. A choice of one [vertex of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md) from each of its four parts is a [three-uniform tetrahedron](three-uniform-tetrahedron.md) exactly when all four triples are [hypergraph edges](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md). Specifying the parts makes both [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) and labelled counting precise.
