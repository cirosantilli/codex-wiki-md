# Relativistic spherical accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md)

Relativistic spherical accretion is steady radial gas inflow in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), with conserved rest-mass flux and relativistic [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md). A regular solution passes a finite [sonic point](sonic-point.md) outside the [event horizon](event-horizon.md). For an isentropic gas with $\gamma=5/3$ and cold asymptotic [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $c_\infty\ll c$, the critical radius is approximately $3GM/(4cc_\infty)$ and the leading [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) is $\pi G^2M^2\rho_\infty/c_\infty^3$. Relativistic corrections resolve the zero-radius critical point of the Newtonian $\gamma=5/3$ model without eliminating accretion.
