# Microrheology of a Maxwell fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Passive microrheology](passive-microrheology.md)

A [Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md) has a spring and dashpot in series, so its step-stress [creep compliance](creep-compliance.md) is $J(t)=1/G_0+t/\eta$. The [passive microrheology](passive-microrheology.md) relation then gives the displayed three-dimensional [mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md). The plateau scale $k_BT/(\pi aG_0)$ measures elasticity, while the long-time slope $k_BT/(\pi a\eta)$ measures viscosity. An entropic network with mesh size $\xi$ has the estimate $G_0\sim k_BT/\xi^3$, giving plateau scale $\xi^3/(\pi a)$.

The nonzero zero-lag extrapolation belongs to the idealized massless spring-dashpot response. Actual displacement variance starts at zero; fast solvent dynamics or inertia regularize the short-time behavior. Consequently the plateau formula applies after those transients and before the Maxwell relaxation time, if those scales are well separated. A sampling interval comparable with $\tau$ gives little access to a genuine plateau, even when spatial localization is sufficiently precise.
