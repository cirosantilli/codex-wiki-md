# Complex contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

An oriented piecewise differentiable path in the complex plane, used as the domain of a [contour integral](contour-integral.md). Its orientation controls the signs of residues in the [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md).
