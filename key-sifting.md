# Key sifting

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum key distribution](quantum-key-distribution.md)

Selecting the [raw key](raw-key.md) rounds from announced measurement settings and other public acceptance data. Outcomes revealed for parameter estimation must be excluded from the secret raw key. Basis announcements follow receipt of the quantum systems; [premature basis announcement in quantum key distribution](premature-basis-announcement-in-quantum-key-distribution.md) can permit a memory attack.
