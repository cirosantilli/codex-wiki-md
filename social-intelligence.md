# Social intelligence

↑ **Parent:** [Intelligence](intelligence.md)

Proposed abilities involved in understanding and using information about people and social situations. Competing definitions emphasize [social understanding](social-understanding.md), knowledge, memory, effective behavior or self-perceived skill; measures of these are not automatically interchangeable indicators of one factor.
