# Even bound state in every attractive square well

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

For the [finite square well](finite-square-well.md) $V=-V_0$ on $|x|<a$ and $V=0$ outside, with $a,V_0>0$, there is always an even [bound state](bound-state.md), however small $V_0$ is. Define $\rho=a\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$, the dimensionless decay constant $\alpha=a\sqrt{-2mE}/\hbar$, and interior oscillation constant $\beta=a\sqrt{2m(E+V_0)}/\hbar$. [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md) gives $\alpha=\beta\tan\beta$ and $\alpha^2+\beta^2=\rho^2$.

On $0<\beta<\pi/2$, these equations reduce to $\beta/\cos\beta=\rho$. The left side is continuous, strictly increasing from zero to infinity, with derivative $\sec\beta(1+\beta\tan\beta)>0$. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) gives exactly one root on this first branch for every $\rho>0$. It has $0<\beta<\rho$, hence $\alpha>0$ and $-V_0<E<0$. The matched cosine interior and exponential tails therefore give a nonzero even [square-integrable function](square-integrable-function.md) with continuous first derivative, which can be normalized. As the well becomes shallow the binding energy approaches zero but remains negative; see [shallow finite-square-well energy](shallow-finite-square-well-energy.md).
