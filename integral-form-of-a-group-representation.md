# Integral form of a group representation

↑ **Parent:** [P-modular system](p-modular-system.md)

For a [p-modular system](p-modular-system.md) $(K,\mathcal O,k)$, an integral form of a finite-dimensional $KG$-[module](module-mathematics.md) $V$ is a $G$-stable finite free $\mathcal O$-submodule $W\subset V$ with $K\otimes_{\mathcal O}W\cong V$. Starting with a basis lattice $L$, the sum $\sum_{g\in G}gL$ supplies such a form: it is finitely generated and [torsion-free](torsion-free-connection.md), hence free over the [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O$. Reduction gives the $kG$-module $W/\pi W$, where $\pi$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md).
