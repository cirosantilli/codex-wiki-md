# Endoplasmic reticulum-mitochondrial contact site

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane contact site](membrane-contact-site.md)

An [endoplasmic reticulum-mitochondrial contact site](endoplasmic-reticulum-mitochondrial-contact-site.md) brings ER and mitochondrial membranes close while preserving their separate compartments. Specific contacts can contribute to [calcium](calcium.md) handling, lipid exchange and organelle organization.
