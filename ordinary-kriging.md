# Ordinary kriging

↑ **Parent:** [Kriging](kriging.md)

For an unknown constant mean, ordinary kriging minimizes error [variance](variance-split.md) subject to weights summing to one. This unbiasedness constraint distinguishes it from [simple kriging](simple-kriging.md).
