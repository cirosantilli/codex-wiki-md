# Minimal algebraic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Exceptional curve of the first kind](exceptional-curve-of-the-first-kind.md)

A smooth projective surface is minimal when it contains no [exceptional curve of the first kind](exceptional-curve-of-the-first-kind.md).

Minimality here is the smooth-surface condition, rather than a claim about every higher-dimensional minimal model.
