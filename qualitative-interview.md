# Qualitative interview

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A structured or flexible interview used to investigate experiences, meanings and processes. It can identify candidate barriers and explain implementation without by itself estimating their population prevalence or the [causal effect](causal-effect.md) of an intervention.
