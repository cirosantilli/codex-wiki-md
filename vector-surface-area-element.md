# Vector surface area element

↑ **Parent:** [Surface area element](surface-area-element.md)

A vector area element combines the scalar [surface area element](surface-area-element.md) with a chosen [unit normal vector](unit-normal.md). Reversing the order of the two parameter directions reverses its sign. [Flux](flux.md) through an oriented surface is the integral of the [dot product](dot-product.md) of a [vector field](vector-field.md) with this vector element. For a graph $z=f(x,y)$, the upward choice is $(-f_x,-f_y,1)\,dx\,dy$.
