# Nonorientable genus

↑ **Parent:** [Non-orientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md)

For a [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) [nonorientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md), its nonorientable genus $k\geq1$ is the number of [real projective planes](real-projective-plane.md) in its [connected sum of surfaces](connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) representation after capping all [boundary components of a surface](boundary-component-of-a-surface.md) by [closed discs](closed-disc.md). The [classification of compact surfaces](classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) gives $\chi=2-k-b$, with $b$ [boundary components of a surface](boundary-component-of-a-surface.md). The [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) has $k=1$ and the [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md) has $k=2$.
