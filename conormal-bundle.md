# Conormal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conormal_bundle)

For a submanifold $Y\subseteq X$, its conormal bundle consists of covectors annihilating $TY$. A cotangent lift sends $N^*Y$ to $N^*f(Y)$.
