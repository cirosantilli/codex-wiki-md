# Factor map between measure-preserving systems

↑ **Parent:** [Factor of a measure-preserving system](factor-of-a-measure-preserving-system.md)

A measurable measure-preserving map intertwining the transformations of two probability [measure-preserving systems](measure-preserving-system.md). The equalities may be imposed on invariant full-measure domains. It represents the target's observable information inside the source. The [conditional measures of a factor](conditional-measures-of-a-factor.md) describe the information remaining on each fiber.
