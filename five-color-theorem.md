# Five color theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph](planar-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_color_theorem)

Every finite [planar graph](planar-graph.md) admits a proper vertex coloring with at most five colors. [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) supplies a vertex of degree at most five. Inductively color its deletion. The only obstruction is five differently colored neighbours in cyclic order. If opposite neighbours 1 and 3 are in different two-color components, interchange one component. If they are connected, planarity prevents opposite neighbours 2 and 4 from being connected in their two-color subgraph, and that interchange frees a color instead. This is a [Kempe chain](kempe-chain.md) proof.
