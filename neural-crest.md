# Neural crest

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental biology](developmental-biology.md)

A vertebrate embryonic population arising near the neural border, with migratory descendants contributing to cranial connective and skeletal tissues, peripheral neurons and glia, pigment cells and other derivatives. Individual cells and axial populations have restricted subsets of these potentials.
