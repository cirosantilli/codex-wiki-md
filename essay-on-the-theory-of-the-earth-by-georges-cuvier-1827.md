# Essay on the Theory of the Earth by Georges Cuvier (1827)

↑ **Parent:** [History of science bibliography](history-of-science-bibliography.md)

[https://www.gutenberg.org/files/62918/62918-h/62918-h.htm](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/62918/62918-h/62918-h.htm)

Fifth English edition, with separately headed Geological Illustrations by [Robert Jameson](robert-jameson.md). The main essay, printed pp. 239–240, distinguishes the last revolution from earlier changes; [Robert Jameson](robert-jameson.md)'s On the Universal Deluge begins at printed p. 417. Editorial arguments must not silently become [Georges Cuvier](georges-cuvier.md)'s own words.
