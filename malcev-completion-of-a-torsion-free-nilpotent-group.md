# Malcev completion of a torsion-free nilpotent group

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md)

Every finitely generated [torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md) which is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) embeds as a discrete cocompact subgroup of a unique simply connected nilpotent real [Lie group](lie-group.md). Its dimension equals the group's [integral cohomological dimension of a group](integral-cohomological-dimension-of-a-group.md). In dimension three the Lie algebra is either abelian or has a basis with a single nonzero bracket $[Y,T]=X$, giving the [real Heisenberg group](heisenberg-group.md).
