# Fourier transform of a ball indicator over a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform over a local field](fourier-transform-over-a-local-field.md)

For a compact open additive subgroup $H$ of a [local field](local-field.md), translation and [additive character](additive-character.md) orthogonality give the displayed transform of a coset indicator. With the [self-dual Haar measure](self-dual-haar-measure.md), transforming again gives $\mathbf1_{a+H}(-x)$. Every [Schwartz-Bruhat function](schwartz-bruhat-function.md) is a finite linear combination of these indicators.
