# Finite-symmetric-difference colouring of infinite subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Partition relation](partition-relation.md)

Choose a representative $R$ of each class of countably infinite [sets](set-split.md) modulo [finite symmetric difference](finite-symmetric-difference.md), and colour $A$ by the parity of $|A\mathbin\triangle R|$. Removing one element stays in the same class and flips the parity. Every infinite candidate homogeneous set therefore has two countably infinite subsets of opposite colours. With [choice](axiom-of-choice.md), this refutes unrestricted countably infinite-arity positive [partition relations](partition-relation.md) on every infinite cardinal.
