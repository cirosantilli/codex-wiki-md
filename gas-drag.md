# Gas drag

↑ **Parent:** [Drag (physics)](drag-physics.md)

Gas drag is a [drag force](drag-physics.md) on a solid body due to its motion relative to surrounding gas. With [aerodynamic stopping time](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md) $t_s$, the linear approximation gives $\dot{\mathbf v}|_{\rm drag}=-(\mathbf v-\mathbf v_g)/t_s$.
