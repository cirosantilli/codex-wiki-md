# Weak-drag dust density waves

↑ **Parent:** [Dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag](dust-gravitational-dispersion-relation-with-gas-drag.md)

For fixed $\omega^2=\Omega^2-2\pi G\sigma_0|k|+c^2k^2>0$, the two oscillatory roots of the [dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag](dust-gravitational-dispersion-relation-with-gas-drag.md) acquire negative real parts at first order in $\epsilon$. Their frequencies have no first-order shift. The expansion is not uniform near $\omega^2=0$, where the oscillation and damping scales can become comparable.
