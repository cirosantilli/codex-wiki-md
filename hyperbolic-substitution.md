# Hyperbolic substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

A hyperbolic change of integration variable uses identities such as $1+\sinh^2u=\cosh^2u$ to simplify square roots. For an [open matter-dominated Friedmann solution](open-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md), $a=b\sinh^2(\eta/2)$ makes the cosmic-time integral elementary.
