# NaCl conventional cell and coordination

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md)

Ordinary solid [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) has the [rock salt structure](rock-salt-structure.md). Choose a conventional cube of side $a$ with [chlorine](chlorine.md) [anions](anion.md) at the corners and face centers, and [sodium](sodium.md) [cations](cation.md) at the edge centers and cube center. Corner sites are shared by eight cubes, face sites by two and edge sites by four. The [unit cell](unit-cell.md) counts are therefore

$$
N_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}=8\left(\frac18\right)+6\left(\frac12\right)=4,\qquad
N_{\mathrm{Na}^+}=12\left(\frac14\right)+1=4.
$$

There are four [formula units](formula-unit.md) per conventional [unit cell](unit-cell.md), rather than four separate NaCl [molecules](molecule-split.md).[§10.6, “Unit Cells of Ionic Compounds”, NaCl paragraph and Figure 10.60: corner/face chloride sites, edge/center sodium sites and four ions of each kind](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

The left panel includes all boundary sites before sharing is accounted for; the right panel isolates one [sodium](sodium.md) [cation](cation.md) and its six nearest [chlorine](chlorine.md) [anions](anion.md). Grey segments indicate distances, not discrete [molecules](molecule-split.md). Marker sizes do not encode ionic radii.

The [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) is a [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md). One choice of [primitive lattice translation vectors](primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) and [atomic basis](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) is

$$
a_1=\frac a2(0,1,1),\quad a_2=\frac a2(1,0,1),\quad a_3=\frac a2(1,1,0),\qquad
r_{\mathrm{Cl}^-}=0,\quad r_{\mathrm{Na}^+}=\frac a2(1,0,0).
$$

The [determinant](determinant.md) gives a [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) [volume](volume.md) of $a^3/4$; that cell represents one [cation](cation.md) and one [anion](anion.md). Four such volumes make the conventional cube. The union of all [ion](ion.md) positions resembles a [primitive cubic lattice](primitive-cubic-lattice.md) of step $a/2$ only if the chemical labels are discarded: translation by $a(1,0,0)/2$ exchanges unlike [ions](ion.md) and fails to preserve the chemical structure.

Each [ion](ion.md) has six nearest unlike neighbors at distance $a/2$, along the positive and negative directions of the three cube axes. Thus the [coordination number](coordination-number.md) is six. Within either same-species [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md), the nearest same-ion neighbors instead number twelve: their displacements are permutations of $(\pm a/2,\pm a/2,0)$, all of length $a/\sqrt2$. Since $a/\sqrt2>a/2$, those twelve are a more distant shell in the complete [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) structure. The number twelve describes the same-ion [FCC lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) neighborhood; six describes the nearest unlike-ion neighborhood.
