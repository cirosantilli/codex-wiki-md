# Unit flow

↑ **Parent:** [Flow](flow.md)

A unit flow from $a$ to $b$ is an antisymmetric [flow](flow.md) with net outward [strength of a flow](strength-of-a-flow.md) one at $a$, net inward strength one at $b$, and zero [divergence of a flow](divergence-of-a-flow.md) elsewhere. For a unit flow to infinity, only the finite source has nonzero divergence.
