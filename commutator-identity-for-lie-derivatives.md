# Commutator identity for Lie derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

The [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) satisfies $[\mathcal L_X,\mathcal L_Y]=\mathcal L_{[X,Y]}$. On [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) this is the definition of the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md); on [vector fields](vector-field.md) it follows from the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). The [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) and contraction compatibility extend the equality to [tensor fields](tensor-field.md). The cyclic double-commutator identity also follows directly from associativity of operator composition.
