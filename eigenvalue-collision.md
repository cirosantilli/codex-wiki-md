# Eigenvalue collision

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

An eigenvalue collision occurs when distinct branches of [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) coincide in a parameter-dependent family of [matrices](matrix.md) or [linear operators](linear-operator.md). The resulting repeated eigenvalue need not retain a full [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md); its geometric and algebraic multiplicities must be compared to decide [diagonalizability](diagonalizable-matrix.md).
