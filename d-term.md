# D-term

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md)

A D-term is a full [superspace integration](superspace-integration.md) of a real [superfield](superfield.md). In [four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md), a [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) contributes a D-term containing kinetic terms and quadratic [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) terms.
