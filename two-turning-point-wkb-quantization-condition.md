# Two-turning-point WKB quantization condition

↑ **Parent:** [WKB quantization condition](wkb-quantization-condition.md)

For two simple [turning points](classical-turning-point.md) $a(E)<b(E)$ surrounding one classically allowed well,

$$
\frac1\varepsilon\int_{a(E)}^{b(E)}\sqrt{E-V(x)}\,dx
=\left(n+\frac12\right)\pi,
\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.
$$
