# Affine module sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)

For an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) with [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$ and an $A$-module $M$, [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) defines sections on [principal open subsets](principal-open-subscheme.md) by the displayed formula. Restrictions are the natural localization maps. Equivalently, a section is a family of [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) values which locally come from one fraction $m/f^r$. These data form a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md), with [global sections](global-section.md) $M$ and stalk $M_{\mathfrak m_P}$ at $P$. Sheaf [tensor products](tensor-product.md) correspond to tensor products over $A$; [pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md) corresponds to extension of scalars, and affine [direct image](direct-image-sheaf.md) to restriction of scalars.
