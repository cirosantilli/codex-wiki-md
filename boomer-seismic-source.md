# Boomer seismic source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic source](seismic-source.md)

An impulsive marine acoustic source in which an electrically driven plate displaces [water](water.md). The emitted broadband pulse is useful for shallow [seismic reflection](seismic-reflection.md) profiling, with effective penetration and resolution depending on source energy and substrate.
