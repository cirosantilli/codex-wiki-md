# Space of holomorphic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md)

For an [open set](open-set.md) $U\subseteq\mathbb C$, the space $\mathcal O(U)$ consists of all [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) on $U$. Its standard topology is locally uniform convergence, equivalently the [compact-open topology](compact-open-topology.md) defined by the seminorms $f\mapsto\sup_{z\in K}|f(z)|$ for compact $K\subset U$.
