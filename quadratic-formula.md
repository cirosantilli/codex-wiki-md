# Quadratic formula

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_formula)

For a [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md) $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with $a\ne0$, completing the square gives

$$
x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.
$$

Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) both roots exist, with a repeated [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) when the [discriminant](discriminant.md) $b^2-4ac$ is zero. Over the real numbers the discriminant determines whether there are two distinct roots, one repeated root, or no real root.
