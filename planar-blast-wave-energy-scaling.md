# Planar blast-wave energy scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md)

For a planar adiabatic explosion with energy per unit area $E$ in a uniform medium of [mass density](density.md) $\rho_0$, the conserved energy has form $E=I\rho_0Z\dot Z^2$, with a finite positive integral $I$ of the [similarity profiles](similarity-profile.md). Hence $Z=C(E/\rho_0)^{1/3}t^{2/3}$. If $E$ is shared by two fronts, its fraction assigned to either side is absorbed into $C$.
