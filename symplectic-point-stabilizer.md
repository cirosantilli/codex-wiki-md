# Symplectic point stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic parabolic subgroup](symplectic-parabolic-subgroup.md)

For a line $\langle e\rangle$, complete $(e,f)$ to a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) with complementary symplectic space $W$. The [unipotent radical](unipotent-radical.md) has elements $u(b,t)$ sending $e$ to $e$, $w$ to $w-B(w,b)e$ and $f$ to $f+b+te$. Its multiplication is $(b,t)(b',t')=(b+b',t+t'+B(b,b'))$, giving order $q^{2m-1}$. Its commutator is central with parameter $2B(b,b')$. It is Heisenberg-type in odd characteristic and abelian in characteristic two. The full stabilizer also scales $e,f$ inversely and acts symplectically on $W$.
