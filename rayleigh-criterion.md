# Rayleigh criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction-limited resolution](diffraction-limited-resolution.md)

The [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) calls two equal, mutually incoherent [optical point sources](optical-point-source.md) just resolved when one circular-pupil [Airy disk](airy-disk.md)'s centre coincides with the other's first zero. It is a specified discrimination convention, distinct from [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) and from precision in estimating one source's position.

For this circular [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md), the [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) uses angular separation $s_{\rm R}=\theta_1\simeq1.22\lambda/D$, or $s_{\rm R}=r_1\simeq1.22\lambda f/D$ in the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md) to the [focal plane](focal-plane.md). The [point spread functions](point-spread-function-split.md) still overlap.[equations 10.85–10.87: circular-aperture first-zero scale and Rayleigh convention](oscillations-and-waves-by-richard-fitzpatrick/two-dimensional-fourier-optics.md)

For the [Airy disk](airy-disk.md) design example with $D=5.00\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$, the angular convention gives $s_{\rm R}=1.34164\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$, or about $27.67$ [arcseconds](arcsecond.md), and the image-plane separation is $13.4164\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. Two equally bright, mutually incoherent [optical point sources](optical-point-source.md) at separations $0.7$, $1$ and $1.5$ times this scale would have image separations $9.391$, $13.416$ and $20.125\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ respectively. These calculated cases are compared in [incoherent two-source Airy profile](incoherent-two-source-airy-profile.md).

The [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) is useful because it ties an easily recognized two-peak convention to a predictable optical scale. It does not assert that all information vanishes below that separation. Inferring two unknown positions also depends on [photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md), background, unequal brightness and uncertainty in the [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md); locating a single isolated source is a different task. A theoretical analysis shows that alternative optical measurements can estimate the separation of two incoherent [optical point sources](optical-point-source.md) below this scale.[abstract: linear optics and photon counting can estimate sub-Rayleigh separation](quantum-theory-of-superresolution-for-two-incoherent-optical-point-sources-by-mankei-tsang-and-others.md)

The mutual [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md) condition is essential. In [incoherent imaging](incoherent-imaging.md), time-averaged [light intensities](light-intensity.md) add. In [coherent imaging](coherent-imaging.md), [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) add before squaring, leaving a [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) term controlled by relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md). Two holes illuminated by the same coherent [laser](laser-split.md) therefore do not implement the equal-incoherent-source convention merely because their individual patterns resemble [Airy disks](airy-disk.md). The coherent single-aperture laboratory test establishes the response of each aperture; it does not establish the pair's mutual incoherence.
