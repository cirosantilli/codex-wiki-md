# Generalized cross-validation

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-validation](cross-validation.md)

For a fixed [linear smoother](linear-smoother.md), generalized cross-validation replaces the individual leverage corrections of [Leave-one-out cross-validation](leave-one-out-cross-validation.md) by their average. It selects smoothing parameters by minimizing the displayed criterion. It approximates a predictive error criterion; it is not a universal exact estimate of future error and requires care when observation errors are dependent.
