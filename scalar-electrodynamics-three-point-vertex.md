# Scalar electrodynamics three-point vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative interactions in scalar electrodynamics](perturbative-interactions-in-scalar-electrodynamics.md)

Expanding the covariant scalar kinetic term gives $ieA_\mu(\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*-\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi)$. With $D=\partial+ieA$, the vertex for an incoming charged particle of momentum $p$ and an outgoing one of momentum $p'$ is $-ie(p+p')^\mu$. The antiparticle has the opposite charge-flow sign. The quadratic term $e^2A^2\phi^*\phi$ gives the separate [seagull vertex](seagull-vertex.md) $2ie^2\eta^{\mu\nu}$. On equal-mass external shells, contracting the three-point momentum with the photon momentum gives zero, the tree [scalar quantum electrodynamics Ward identity](scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md).
