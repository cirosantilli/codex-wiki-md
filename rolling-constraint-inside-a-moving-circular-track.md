# Rolling constraint inside a moving circular track

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A circular body of radius $r$ rolls inside a circular track of radius $R$. Let $\theta$ be the track's material [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), $\phi$ the absolute angle of the line joining the two centres, and $\psi$ the inner body's absolute spin angle, all with one consistent orientation. Equating contact-point velocities gives the displayed constraint. [Translation](translation-geometry.md) of the track's centre cancels from that equality. A stationary track gives $\dot\psi=-(R-r)\dot\phi/r$.
