# Laser relaxation oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Laser rate equation](laser-rate-equation.md)

Small perturbations of a class-B [laser](laser-split.md) exchange excitation between the upper-state population and cavity [photon number](photon-number.md). [Linearization](linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md) about a four-level lasing state gives $\ddot w+(r/\tau_2)\dot w+(r-1)w/(\tau_c\tau_2)=0$. Its damped [oscillation](oscillation.md) [frequency](frequency.md) is $(2\pi)^{-1}\sqrt{(r-1)/(\tau_c\tau_2)-r^2/(4\tau_2^2)}$. Long population lifetime compared with [photon lifetime](photon-lifetime.md) permits underdamped relaxation.
