# Call option on a put option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)

This compound [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md) gives the right to buy a later-expiring [put option](put-option.md) at time $T_1$ for $K_1$. Its payoff is the positive part of the put value minus this exercise cost. Under the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) the put value decreases strictly with the stock spot, so a nondegenerate positive exercise cost gives a unique upper spot threshold for exercise. With zero exercise cost, the compound claim has exactly the underlying put's price by the [tower property](law-of-total-expectation.md).
