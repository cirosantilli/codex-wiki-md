# Ethylene equivalent

↑ **Parent:** [Diels-Alder reaction](diels-alder-reaction.md)

A reagent combination that installs the two saturated carbons of an ethylene cycloaddition while allowing more favourable reactivity. A removable electron-withdrawing substituent can activate the [dienophile](dienophile.md) and be removed later.
