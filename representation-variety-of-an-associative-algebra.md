# Representation variety of an associative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

For a finitely generated [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md), choose matrices for its generators and impose its polynomial relations. This defines an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md). With prescribed [orthogonal idempotents](orthogonal-idempotent.md) $1=\sum_ie_i$, require their images to be the standard projections on $\bigoplus_i k^{n_i}$. The resulting variety is $\operatorname{Rep}_A(\mathbf n)$, acted on by $\prod_i\operatorname{GL}_{n_i}(k)$. Without a chosen idempotent decomposition, the usual variety is $\operatorname{Rep}_A(r)=\operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-alg}}(A,M_r(k))$. Its orbits are module isomorphism classes, and their stabilizers are automorphism groups.
