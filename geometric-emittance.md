# Geometric emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Beam emittance](beam-emittance.md)

For a centered beam, the displayed rms area in $(z,z')$ coordinates. A matched beam has $\langle z^2\rangle=\epsilon\beta$, where $\beta$ is one of the [Courant-Snyder parameters](courant-snyder-parameters.md). Under ideal acceleration, [adiabatic damping of geometric emittance](adiabatic-damping-of-geometric-emittance.md) decreases this area even when the canonical [phase-space](phase-space.md) area is preserved.
