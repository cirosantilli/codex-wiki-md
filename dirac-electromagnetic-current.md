# Dirac electromagnetic current

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac current](dirac-current.md)

The Dirac electromagnetic current is the electric charge times the [Dirac current](dirac-current.md). Its coupling $-ej^\mu A_\mu$ to the [electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) is the matter interaction of [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md).
