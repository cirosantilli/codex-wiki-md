# Double inclusion proof

↑ **Parent:** [Set inclusion](set-inclusion.md)

To prove equality of two [sets](set-split.md), show each [set inclusion](set-inclusion.md) separately. If both inclusions hold, every element belongs to one [set](set-split.md) exactly when it belongs to the other; the [axiom of extensionality](axiom-of-extensionality.md) gives equality. Each direction may use different hypotheses. For example, when $x\in A$, the condition $x\notin A\setminus B$ implies $x\in B$; without $x\in A$, that inference would be invalid.
