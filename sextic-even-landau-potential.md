# Sextic even Landau potential

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For the [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) $G(\phi)=\phi^6/6+g\phi^4/4+\varepsilon\phi^2/2$, positive $g$ gives a [continuous phase transition](continuous-phase-transition.md) at $\varepsilon=0$. For negative $g$, [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) between the disordered [global minimum](global-minimum.md) $\phi=0$ and the ordered [global minima](global-minimum.md) occurs on $\varepsilon=3g^2/16$, with $\phi^2=-3g/4$. Indeed, on that locus the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is $G(\phi)=\phi^2(\phi^2+3g/4)^2/6\geq0$, so these three zeros are [global minima](global-minimum.md). For $g<0$, the disordered and ordered [spinodal points](spinodal-point.md) are respectively $\varepsilon=0$ and $\varepsilon=g^2/4$; equality of stationary [free energies](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) determines [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md), while loss of a [local minimum](local-minimum.md) determines a [spinodal point](spinodal-point.md).
