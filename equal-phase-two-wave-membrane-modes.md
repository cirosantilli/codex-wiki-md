# Equal-phase two-wave membrane modes

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic membrane](elastic-membrane.md)

Two equal [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) with opposite transverse [wavevector](wavevector.md) components sum to a [cosine](cosine.md) across a strip with edges $y=\pm a/2$. Identical phases satisfy the edge condition only for the odd transverse indices displayed. Equal magnitudes with opposite phase give the [sine](sine.md) family; together the families form all Dirichlet strip modes $n\pi/a$, $n\ge1$. The lowest axial [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) exceeds the unrestricted membrane wave [speed](speed.md), while its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is smaller; neither should be substituted for the other.
