# Weak closure

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

The weak closure $\overline K^w$ of $K$ is its closure in the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md). Thus $x\in\overline K^w$ exactly when every weak neighbourhood of $x$ meets $K$.
