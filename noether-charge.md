# Noether charge

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

A Noether charge is the spatial integral $Q=\int j^0d^3x$ of a [Noether current](noether-current.md). It is time-independent when the current has vanishing flux through the spatial boundary, and in a Hamiltonian formulation it generates the associated continuous symmetry.
