# Relativistic three-body decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

A [particle decay](particle-decay.md) into three daughter particles subject to [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) and the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md). The daughter [energies](energy-split.md) generally vary continuously even in the parent's [rest frame](rest-frame.md). Grouping two daughters into a recoil system of variable [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) turns the remaining kinematics into a [relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md).
