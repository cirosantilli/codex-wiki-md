<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Setting $f=0$ in [b](../b.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V(S,t)=S e^{-D(T-t)}}.
$$

This is a [prepaid forward contract](../../../../prepaid-forward-contract.md): the holder pays now and receives one share at $T$, with no interim dividend payments. Buying $e^{-D(T-t)}$ shares now and reinvesting their [continuous dividend yield](../../../../continuous-dividend-yield.md) grows the shareholding to one at expiry. Thus the displayed initial cost replicates the terminal delivery. When $D=0$, holding one share already does so and $V=S$.
