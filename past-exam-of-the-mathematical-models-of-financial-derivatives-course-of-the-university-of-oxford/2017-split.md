# 2017

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the mathematical models of financial derivatives course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 63–66](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/5/Physics%20Papers%202016.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=63).

Solutions to all three questions and all optional parts. Expectations in Question 1 are conditional on current information. The zero-drift variance is obtained by a limit. The exercise-policy calculation in Question 3(c) is distinguished from the market value of an unrestricted American option.
