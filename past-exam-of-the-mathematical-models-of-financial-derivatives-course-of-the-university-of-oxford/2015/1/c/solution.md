<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [risk-neutral probability](../../../../risk-neutral-probability.md) obeys $R=qu+(1-q)d$. Thus

$$
R=\frac23\frac43+\frac13\frac23=\frac{10}{9},
\qquad R^{-1}=\frac9{10}.
$$

Substitution into the [risk-neutral pricing](../../../../risk-neutral-pricing.md) recursion gives

$$
\boxed{V_t=\frac9{10}\left(\frac23V^u_{t+1}+\frac13V^d_{t+1}\right)
=\frac3{10}\left(2V^u_{t+1}+V^d_{t+1}\right).}
$$
