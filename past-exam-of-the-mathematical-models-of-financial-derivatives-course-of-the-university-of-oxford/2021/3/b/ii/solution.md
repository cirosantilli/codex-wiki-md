<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If the barrier is reached, path [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) gives $S_{\tau_B}=B$ and the early payment is $K-B$. If it is not reached, the terminal payment is exactly the [down-and-out put](../../../../../down-and-out-put.md) payoff. When $r=0$, moving a fixed payment from the hitting time to expiry does not change its value. Hence the [barrier-triggered put](../../../../../barrier-triggered-put.md) price is

$$
\boxed{E_B(S,t)=G(S,t)+(K-B)H(t,S),\qquad r=0.}
$$

Here $G,H$ are evaluated with zero interest rate. If the barrier has already been hit, the exploding put has been exercised and has no remaining value after its payment; the displayed replication concerns an initially alive contract and its cash flows.
