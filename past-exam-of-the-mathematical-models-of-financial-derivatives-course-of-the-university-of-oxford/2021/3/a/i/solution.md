<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a long position in the claim, hold $-V_S$ shares and bank value $SV_S-V$ so that claim plus hedge has zero initial value. The [Black-Scholes equation](../../../../../black-scholes-equation.md) and [Itô's formula](../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) show that the total position is [self-financing](../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) and its Brownian and drift terms cancel. Equivalently, the positive replicating portfolio holds $V_S$ shares and bond value $V-SV_S$.

Under the usual growth, integrability and admissibility conditions, [no-arbitrage](../../../../../no-arbitrage.md) forces the claim price to be $V$: a different price could be traded against the [replicating portfolio](../../../../../replicating-strategy.md). The qualification matters because an arbitrary formal PDE solution without a suitable financial class need not be a valid price.
