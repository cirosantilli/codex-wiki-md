↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The portfolio value is $\Pi_t=\phi_tS_t+\psi_tB_t$. The [self-financing condition](../../../../../self-financing-condition.md) means that changes in holdings are financed by trading within the portfolio, with no external infusion or withdrawal. Thus

$$
\boxed{d\Pi_t=\phi_t\,dS_t+\psi_t\,dB_t
=(\mu\phi_tS_t+r\psi_tB_t)\,dt+\sigma\phi_t\,dW_t.}
$$

The holdings are predictable and satisfy the usual stochastic-integrability and admissibility conditions; without admissibility, doubling strategies would spoil the intended [no-arbitrage](../../../../../no-arbitrage.md) interpretation.
