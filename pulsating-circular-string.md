# Pulsating circular string

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md)

A circular [closed string](closed-string.md) contracts and re-expands with $X^0=Rt$ and $X^1+iX^2=R\cos t\,e^{i\sigma}$. Its [proper length](proper-length.md) is $2\pi R|\cos t|$, while its [energy](energy.md) remains $2\pi TR$. At collapse the [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md) degenerates, but the [Nambu-Goto phase-space action](nambu-goto-phase-space-action.md) supplies a regular continuation.
