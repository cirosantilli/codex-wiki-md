# Emergence and expression accounts of false-belief development

↑ **Parent:** [False-belief understanding](false-belief-understanding.md)

An emergence account assigns a capacity such as [language](language.md) or executive control a role in constructing understanding of [belief](belief.md) over development. An expression account assigns it a role in accessing and displaying already available understanding. The same observed [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is compatible with both. Immediate task-demand manipulations primarily probe expression; lasting transfer from a controlled intervention can constrain emergence, provided strategy and practice explanations are addressed.
