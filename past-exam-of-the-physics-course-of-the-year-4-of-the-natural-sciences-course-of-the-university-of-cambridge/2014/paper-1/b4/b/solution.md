↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [photon](../../../../../photon.md) reaching spacecraft position $x(t)$ at [Earth](../../../../../earth.md) time $t$ was emitted at $t_e=t-x(t)/c$. Differentiating gives $dt_e/dt=1-\beta$. Since $R\,dt_e$ [photons](../../../../../photon.md) arrive in $dt$,

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm hit}=R(1-\beta).}
$$

The emission rate and impact rate refer to different events, even though both are expressed in [Earth](../../../../../earth.md) coordinate time.
