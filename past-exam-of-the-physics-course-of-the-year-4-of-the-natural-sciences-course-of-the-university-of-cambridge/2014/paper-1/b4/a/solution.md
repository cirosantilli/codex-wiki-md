<h1 id="b4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the instantaneous mirror frame the incident [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) is $f_m=\gamma(1-\beta)f$. Ideal reflection reverses propagation without changing that frame's [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). Transforming the backward [photon](../../../../../photon.md) to [Earth](../../../../../earth.md) gives another factor $\gamma(1-\beta)$:

$$
\boxed{f_r=\gamma^2(1-\beta)^2f
=\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}f.}
$$

This uses a classical mirror with negligible recoil per individual [photon](../../../../../photon.md).
