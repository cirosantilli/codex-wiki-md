<h1 id="a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The central value is $S=150\cos45^\circ=106.07$. Treating the stated errors as [independent](../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](../../../../standard-uncertainty.md), convert the angular uncertainty to radians and propagate derivatives:

$$
\sigma_S^2=(\cos2\theta\,\sigma_{S_0})^2+
(2S_0\sin2\theta\,\sigma_\theta)^2,
\qquad
\boxed{S=106.1\pm6.5.}
$$

The two measured values differ by $10.8/\sqrt{6.0^2+5.6^2}=1.32$ combined [standard deviations](../../../../standard-deviation.md). Our prediction differs from them by $0.19$ and $1.07$ combined [standard deviations](../../../../standard-deviation.md) respectively. **All three values are statistically compatible.** If the input errors are strict bounds instead, the first-order bound on the calculated value is about $\pm9.0$; consistency remains possible, but a Gaussian significance test then has no stated justification.
