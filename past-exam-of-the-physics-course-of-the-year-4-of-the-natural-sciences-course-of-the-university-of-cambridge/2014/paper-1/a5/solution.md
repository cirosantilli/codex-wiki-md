<h1 id="a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

Moving one Michelson mirror changes [optical path difference](../../../../optical-path-difference.md) by twice its displacement. Therefore

$$
\boxed{2d=10\lambda,\qquad d=2.50\,\mathrm{\mu m}.}
$$

For [two-wavelength Michelson rephasing](../../../../two-wavelength-michelson-rephasing.md), start from a position where both colours are bright: the next simultaneous bright centre requires $2\Delta d$ to be a common integer multiple of both [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md). The smallest is $6(500\,\mathrm{nm})=5(600\,\mathrm{nm})=3.00\,\mathrm{\mu m}$, giving **an additional mirror movement of $1.50\,\mathrm{\mu m}$**.
