<h1 id="a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

For the [photopion threshold on a photon background](../../../../photopion-threshold-on-a-photon-background.md), write $M=m_pc^2$ and $m=m_\pi c^2$ in [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) units. For a head-on [photon](../../../../photon.md) of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\epsilon$ and a highly relativistic [proton](../../../../proton.md) of total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E$, the invariant squared centre-of-momentum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is approximately $M^2+4E\epsilon$. At threshold the final [proton](../../../../proton.md) and [pion](../../../../pion.md) are at rest relative to one another, so

$$
M^2+4E_{\rm th}\epsilon=(M+m)^2,
\qquad
\boxed{E_{\rm th}=\frac{m(2M+m)}{4\epsilon}.}
$$

Using a typical CMB [photon energy](../../../../photon-energy.md) $\epsilon\simeq2.70k_BT=6.28\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{eV}$ gives

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm th}\simeq1.1\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

Thus photopion losses suppress the arrival of [protons](../../../../proton.md) of order $10^{20}\,\mathrm{eV}$ from sufficiently distant sources. A [blackbody](../../../../blackbody.md) contains a range of [photon energies](../../../../photon-energy.md) and collision angles, so the observed attenuation is gradual and distance-dependent, rather than an absolute maximum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) for every [proton](../../../../proton.md).
