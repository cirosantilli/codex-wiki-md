<h1 id="a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

The solar [flux](../../../../flux.md) at [Earth](../../../../earth.md) is $\sigma T_\odot^4R_\odot^2/L^2$. A spherical [Earth](../../../../earth.md) intercepts sunlight over its cross section but radiates over its whole surface, giving

$$
\pi R_E^2\sigma T_\odot^4\frac{R_\odot^2}{L^2}=4\pi R_E^2\sigma T_E^4,
\qquad
\boxed{T_E=T_\odot\sqrt{\frac{R_\odot}{2L}}=275\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This is **below the observed mean surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md)**. Atmospheric infrared absorption and emission produce a [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) that raises the surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) relative to the effective radiating [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Including reflected sunlight alone would lower the estimate further; [albedo](../../../../albedo.md) does not explain a warmer surface.[Sections The Natural Greenhouse Effect and Effect on Surface Temperature](../../../../climate-and-earth-s-energy-budget-by-nasa-earth-observatory.md)
