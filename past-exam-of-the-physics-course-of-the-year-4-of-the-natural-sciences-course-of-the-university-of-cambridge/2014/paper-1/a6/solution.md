[Zero-point energy](../../../../zero-point-energy.md) is the [ground-state energy](../../../../ground-state-energy.md) above the classical minimum, remaining even at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). For a [harmonic oscillator](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) it is $\hbar\omega/2$; simultaneous vanishing position and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) fluctuations is forbidden by the uncertainty principle.

A concrete test is the Casimir interaction: changing conducting boundaries changes electromagnetic ground-state [modes](../../../../mode-statistics.md) and produces a measurable [force](../../../../force-split.md). Ideal parallel plates have pressure

$$
\boxed{P_C=-\frac{\pi^2\hbar c}{240a^4}.}
$$

A torsion-pendulum experiment measured the attraction of closely spaced conducting surfaces, testing this boundary-dependent interaction.[Physical Review Letters 78, 5 (1997), primary abstract: electromechanical torsion-pendulum measurement; later erratum noted in bibliography](../../../../demonstration-of-the-casimir-force-in-the-0-6-to-6-micrometre-range-by-s-k-lamoreaux.md) Such measurements test **changes in [ground-state energy](../../../../ground-state-energy.md)**, not an arbitrary absolute additive vacuum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); real-material and thermal corrections must be included when comparing precision data.
