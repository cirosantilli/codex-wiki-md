<h1 id="b1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B1](../b1.md)

For an isolated system with equally probable accessible microstates, [Boltzmann entropy](../../../../boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md) is $S=k_B\ln\Omega$. The second law states that an isolated system's total [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) cannot decrease; it remains constant in a reversible process and increases in an irreversible one.

The insulated mixture exchanges no heat with its surroundings. At constant pressure, with only pressure-volume work, its [enthalpy](../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is conserved. Constant and equal [specific heat capacities](../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md) therefore give $T_f=(T_1+T_2)/2$. Calculate each [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) change along a hypothetical reversible [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) path:

$$
\Delta S=Mc_P\ln\frac{T_f}{T_1}+Mc_P\ln\frac{T_f}{T_2}
=\boxed{2Mc_P\ln\left[\frac{T_1+T_2}{2\sqrt{T_1T_2}}\right].}
$$

The surroundings gain no [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) through heat exchange; ideal mechanical work carries no [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md). The [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) gives **$\Delta S\ge0$**, with equality only for $T_1=T_2$. Mixing unequal [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md) is irreversible even though total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is conserved.
