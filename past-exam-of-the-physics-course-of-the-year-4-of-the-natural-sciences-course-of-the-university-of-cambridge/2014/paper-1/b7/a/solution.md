↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The relative slit-centre [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) is $2\pi d\sin\theta/\lambda$. Near the optical axis, $x\simeq L\theta$, so adjacent [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) maxima have spacing

$$
\boxed{\delta x\simeq\frac{\lambda L}{d}=\frac L{1000}.}
$$

Slow diffraction-envelope variation can slightly shift the exact local maxima.
