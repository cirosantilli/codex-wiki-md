<h1 id="b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

[Fresnel diffraction](../../../../fresnel-diffraction.md) retains quadratic aperture-dependent propagation [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) and generally depends on both aperture and screen distance. [Fraunhofer diffraction](../../../../fraunhofer-diffraction.md) neglects those [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) and gives the [Fourier transform](../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) of the aperture in angle space, either sufficiently far away or in a suitable lens's focal plane.

The calculations below use the **Fraunhofer, [paraxial approximation](../../../../paraxial-approximation.md)**. For an overall aperture scale $D\sim1000\lambda$, free propagation requires $L\gg D^2/\lambda\sim10^6\lambda$. The printed $L\gg1000\lambda$ alone only makes the separation small relative to distance; it is not by itself the full far-field condition.
