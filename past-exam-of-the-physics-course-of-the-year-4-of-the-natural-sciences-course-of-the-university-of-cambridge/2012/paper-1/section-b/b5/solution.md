<h1 id="section-b/b5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B5](../b5.md)

In the weak-field [Zeeman effect](../../../../../zeeman-splitting.md), [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) keeps $L,S,J$ good and a level shifts by $\mu_Bg_JBm_J$. At fields large compared with fine-structure coupling but still within the linear electronic Zeeman approximation, $L$ and $S$ decouple: the [Paschen-Back effect](../../../../../paschen-back-effect.md) gives shifts $\mu_BB(m_L+2m_S)$. Electric-dipole transitions obey $\Delta m_L=0,\pm1$, $\Delta m_S=0$ in that limit, recovering an orbital triplet pattern. Intermediate fields mix the coupled states.

For the weak-field [sodium D-line Zeeman pattern](../../../../../sodium-d-line-zeeman-pattern.md), the given [Landé g-factor](../../../../../lande-g-factor.md) formula gives

$$
g({}^2S_{1/2})=2,\qquad g({}^2P_{1/2})=2/3,\qquad g({}^2P_{3/2})=4/3.
$$

Allowed [photon](../../../../../photon.md) shifts are $\delta E=\mu_BB(g_um_u-2m_l)$ with $m_u-m_l=0,\pm1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{D_1:\ \delta E/(\mu_BB)=\pm2/3,\pm4/3,\qquad
D_2:\ \delta E/(\mu_BB)=\pm1/3,\pm1,\pm5/3.}
$$

These are the four- and six-component patterns. Both $\pi$ and $\sigma$ components are represented, so an observation strictly along the field, which removes $\pi$ emission, would not show this complete pattern. [Hyperfine structure](../../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) is neglected.

To determine the [magnetic field from a Zeeman diagram](../../../../../magnetic-field-from-a-zeeman-diagram.md), the unsplit separation provides its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scale:

$$
\Delta E_D=hc(1/\lambda_2-1/\lambda_1)=2.1315\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$

The smallest adjacent split spacing $s_E=2\mu_BB_1/3$ is approximately $0.070$ of that separation in the sketch. Therefore

$$
\boxed{B_1\simeq\frac{3(0.070)\Delta E_D}{2\mu_B}\simeq3.9\,\mathrm T\approx4\,\mathrm T.}
$$

This is a graphical estimate; the drawing does not warrant high precision. Its splitting [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is small compared with $\Delta E_D$, consistent with the weak-field approximation.

For [Doppler resolution of a Zeeman pattern](../../../../../doppler-resolution-of-a-zeeman-pattern.md), thermal [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) give [Gaussian](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) [Doppler broadening](../../../../../doppler-broadening.md) with width

$$
\frac{\Delta\nu_D}{\nu}=\sqrt{\frac{8k_BT\ln2}{m_{\rm Na}c^2}},\qquad m_{\rm Na}=\frac{0.0230}{N_A}\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

At $350\,\mathrm K$, the photon-energy FWHM near $589\,\mathrm{nm}$ is $5.88\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{eV}$. Equating the smallest adjacent Zeeman spacing to this width gives **$B\sim0.15\,\mathrm T$** for loss of individually resolved components. There is no sharp [Gaussian](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) resolution threshold; using whole-pattern width instead gives a smaller field of order $0.03$–$0.04\,\mathrm T$ for complete disappearance into a single envelope. Instrumental and collisional broadening would also affect the estimate.
