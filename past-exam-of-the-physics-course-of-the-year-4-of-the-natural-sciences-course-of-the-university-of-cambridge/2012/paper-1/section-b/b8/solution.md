<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

The far fields obey $E_\theta=cB_\phi$. The radial component of their [Poynting vector](../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is $E_\theta B_\phi/\mu_0$. Averaging $\ddot p^2=p_0^2\omega^4\cos^2\omega(t-r/c)$ and integrating $\int\sin^2\theta\,d\Omega=8\pi/3$ gives

$$
\boxed{P=\frac{\mu_0p_0^2\omega^4}{12\pi c}.}
$$

For a small isotropically polarizable particle, $p_0=\alpha E_0$ and incident [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) is $I=\epsilon_0cE_0^2/2$. Thus the total [Rayleigh scattering](../../../../../rayleigh-scattering.md) cross-section is

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\frac PI=\frac{\alpha^2\omega^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0^2c^4}
=\frac{8\pi^3\alpha^2}{3\epsilon_0^2\lambda^4}.}
$$

Each incident [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) gives the same integrated [power](../../../../../power.md); averaging [unpolarized light](../../../../../unpolarized-light.md) does not add another factor of two. The scalar real [polarizability](../../../../../electric-polarizability.md) approximation neglects absorption and molecular anisotropy.

For [Rayleigh attenuation from refractivity](../../../../../rayleigh-attenuation-from-refractivity.md) in dilute air, [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) is $\mathbf P=N\alpha\mathbf E$. Consequently $\epsilon_r=1+N\alpha/\epsilon_0$ and, with $\mu_r\simeq1$ and $n-1\ll1$,

$$
\boxed{n-1\simeq\frac{N\alpha}{2\epsilon_0}.}
$$

The ideal-gas [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) at the supplied pressure and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is $N=P/(k_BT)=2.47\times10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$, yielding $\alpha=2.15\times10^{-40}\,\mathrm{C\,m^2\,V^{-1}}$. For an explicit representative visible [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$,

$$
\sigma\simeq5.33\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{m^2},\qquad
\boxed{L_{\rm att}=\frac1{N\sigma}\simeq7.6\times10^4\,\mathrm m\approx76\,\mathrm{km}.}
$$

Direct-beam [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) follows $I(x)=I(0)e^{-x/L_{\rm att}}$. Equivalently $L_{\rm att}=3N\lambda^4/[32\pi^3(n-1)^2]$. Its strong [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) dependence gives roughly $21$–$200\,\mathrm{km}$ across $400$–$700\,\mathrm{nm}$ if refractivity is held fixed. This is a uniform-density dry-air scattering estimate, not absorption or transmission through a vertically stratified atmosphere; aerosols and molecular depolarization corrections are outside the given model.
