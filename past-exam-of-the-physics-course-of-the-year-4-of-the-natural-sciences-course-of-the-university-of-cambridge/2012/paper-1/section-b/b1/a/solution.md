<h1 id="section-b/b1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $X_1,X_2$ be longitudinal displacements. [Hooke's law](../../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) gives

$$
m\begin{pmatrix}\ddot X_1\\\ddot X_2\end{pmatrix}
=-k\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}X_1\\X_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The symmetric and antisymmetric [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are respectively $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$, so

$$
\boxed{\omega_{x,+}=\sqrt{k/m},\qquad \omega_{x,-}=\sqrt{3k/m}.}
$$

Ordinary [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md) are these values divided by $2\pi$.
