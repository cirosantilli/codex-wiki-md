<h1 id="section-b/b1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B1](../b1.md)

A [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) is a small motion in which all coordinates oscillate at one common [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) with fixed relative [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). For conservative linearized motion, these are [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of the mass-weighted [stiffness matrix](../../../../../stiffness-matrix.md).

All three [springs](../../../../../spring.md) are initially stretched from $l$ to $2l$, giving equal [tension](../../../../../tension.md) $T_0=kl$ and zero resultant [force](../../../../../force-split.md) on each [mass](../../../../../mass.md). This prestress is essential for the transverse modes found below.

For the final [mixed-axis trajectory of a prestressed spring pair](../../../../../mixed-axis-trajectory-of-a-prestressed-spring-pair.md), the initial displacements excite only the symmetric mode in each direction. Therefore, to first order in $\epsilon/l$,

$$
\boxed{x_1(t)=2l+\epsilon\cos(\sqrt{k/m}\,t),\qquad
y_1(t)=\epsilon\cos(\sqrt{k/(2m)}\,t).}
$$

The second [mass](../../../../../mass.md) has the same displacements about $4l$. Over the first transverse [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) $T_y=2\pi\sqrt{2m/k}$, write $s=t/T_y$:

$$
\frac{x_1-2l}{\epsilon}=\cos(2\pi\sqrt2\,s),\qquad
\frac{y_1}{\epsilon}=\cos(2\pi s),\qquad 0\le s\le1.
$$

The trajectory is not closed after $T_y$, because the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) ratio is $\sqrt2$. It ends at approximately $(-0.858,1)$ in these scaled coordinates rather than $(1,1)$.
