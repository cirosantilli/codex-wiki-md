<h1 id="section-b/b4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B4](../b4.md)

Use [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) units $m=m_pc^2$, $M=m_Ac^2$, $e=E_\gamma$ and $P=pc=\sqrt{E^2-m^2}$. The [soft-photon resonant capture threshold](../../../../../soft-photon-resonant-capture-threshold.md) follows from [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md):

$$
M^2=m^2+2e(E-P\cos\theta),\qquad
E-P\cos\theta=2E_0,\quad E_0=\frac{M^2-m^2}{4e}.
$$

For fixed [proton](../../../../../proton.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) the left side is largest at head-on incidence. Its threshold is $E+P=2E_0$. Combining that with $(E-P)(E+P)=m^2$ gives

$$
\boxed{E_{\min}=E_0+\frac{m^2}{4E_0},\qquad
\frac{E_{\min}}m=\frac{E_0}m+\frac m{4E_0}.}
$$

Equality is allowed for a head-on [photon](../../../../../photon.md). Here $e=k_BT=2.3482\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{eV}$, so

$$
\boxed{E_0=6.786\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad E_{\min}/m\simeq7.23\times10^{11}.}
$$

A very soft [photon](../../../../../photon.md) needs an enormous [proton](../../../../../proton.md) [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) to reach the specified [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md). A true thermal [photon](../../../../../photon.md) distribution and a finite-width [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) would smear this monoenergetic threshold.

For $\theta=0$, $E-P=m^2/(E+P)\le m$, whereas $2E_0\gg m$, so production is impossible. For each nonzero angle, sufficiently large [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is possible, and $P\simeq E$ gives

$$
\boxed{E\simeq\frac{2E_0}{1-\cos\theta}.}
$$

To find the [maximum deflection in soft-photon capture](../../../../../maximum-deflection-in-soft-photon-capture.md), note that the final particle's transverse [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) comes entirely from the [photon](../../../../../photon.md):

$$
\tan\phi=\frac{e\sin\theta}{P+e\cos\theta}
\simeq\frac e{2E_0}\sin\theta(1-\cos\theta).
$$

Writing $x=\cos\theta$, maximization gives $(1-x)(2x+1)=0$ and the interior maximum $x=-1/2$. Therefore, to the same extraordinarily accurate ultrarelativistic order,

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\max}\simeq120^\circ,\qquad
\phi_{\max}\simeq\frac{3\sqrt3\,e}{8E_0}=2.25\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{rad}.}
$$

The neglected relative corrections are of order $m^2/E_0^2$ and $e/E_0$, both around $10^{-24}$. The final particle is effectively collinear with the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) despite a large [photon](../../../../../photon.md) incidence angle.
