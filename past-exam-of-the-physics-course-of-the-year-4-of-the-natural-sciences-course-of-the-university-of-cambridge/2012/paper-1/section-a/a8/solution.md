<h1 id="section-a/a8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A8](../a8.md)

**Prefer molecular [rotational spectroscopy](../../../../../rotational-spectroscopy.md) or [rovibrational spectroscopy](../../../../../rovibrational-spectroscopy.md)**, because nuclear [masses](../../../../../mass.md) enter their [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) directly. For an [isotopologue](../../../../../isotopologue.md) with nuclear [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) $\mu_N$,

$$
E_{\rm rot}\propto\frac1{\mu_NR_e^2},\qquad
\omega_{\rm vib}\simeq\sqrt{\frac K{\mu_N}}.
$$

[Isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) substitution therefore produces appreciable shifts and distinct sets of lines, assuming the same electronic potential and approximately unchanged [bond length](../../../../../bond-length.md) and [force](../../../../../force-split.md) constant. A suitable heteronuclear molecule can provide electric-dipole rotational/vibrational spectra; other molecules may require [Raman spectroscopy](../../../../../raman-spectroscopy.md).

Atomic electronic energies instead depend on the electron-nucleus [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) $m_eM/(m_e+M)$, whose fractional change is suppressed by $m_e/M$. High-resolution atomic [spectroscopy](../../../../../spectroscopy.md) can still detect [isotope shifts](../../../../../isotope-shift.md), including nuclear-size and many-electron [mass](../../../../../mass.md) effects; it is not intrinsically incapable of identifying [isotopes](../../../../../isotope.md). Molecular spectra generally offer the stronger [mass](../../../../../mass.md) sensitivity for this purpose.
