<h1 id="section-a/a10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A10](../a10.md)

For a cylinder of [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $R_c$ in unbounded two-dimensional incompressible [potential flow](../../../../../potential-flow.md), a [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) is $\varphi=u(r+R_c^2/r)\cos\theta$. It gives

$$
v_r=u(1-R_c^2/r^2)\cos\theta,\qquad
v_\theta=-u(1+R_c^2/r^2)\sin\theta.
$$

On the surface $v_r=0$. The points nearest the banks are the sides perpendicular to the stream, $\theta=\pi/2,3\pi/2$, hence **the [speed](../../../../../speed.md) is $2u$ at each**. This is the inviscid outer-flow [speed](../../../../../speed.md); a real no-slip [boundary layer](../../../../../boundary-layer.md) has zero [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) at the material surface. The small-radius/wide-river assumption neglects bank effects.
