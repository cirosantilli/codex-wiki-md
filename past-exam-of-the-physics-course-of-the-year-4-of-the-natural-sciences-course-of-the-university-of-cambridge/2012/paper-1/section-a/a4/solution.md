<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is $\lambda=hc/E=3.5424\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm m$. The [exact half-period zone radii](../../../../../exact-half-period-zone-radii.md) for incident [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) are chosen so that the path to the axial focus increases by $\lambda/2$ per zone:

$$
\sqrt{f^2+r_n^2}-f=\frac{n\lambda}{2},\qquad
\boxed{r_n=\sqrt{nf\lambda+\frac{n^2\lambda^2}{4}}\simeq\sqrt{nf\lambda}.}
$$

Transmit alternate zones and block the others, with $r_0=0$. For $f=1.00\,\mathrm{cm}$, $r_1=0.5952\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. The number of zones is a design choice: **100 zones give outer [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $5.952\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and outermost width about $29.8\,\mathrm{nm}$**. Adjacent open zones then arrive in [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) at the principal focus.

**[Figure 1](#section-a/a4/image-zone-plate-geometry). Zone-plate geometry**. Original illustrative binary amplitude plate with twelve zones for legibility; the numerical design in the solution uses one hundred zones. Boundary radii follow the derived half-period optical-path condition.

The blocking material must be sufficiently opaque at 35 keV; no attenuation coefficient or fabrication thickness is specified. A binary [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) plate also has zero-order transmission and other odd-order foci, so an aperture/order stop may be needed. The principal [focal length](../../../../../focal-length.md) is chromatic, varying as $1/\lambda$ for fixed [radii](../../../../../radius.md).
