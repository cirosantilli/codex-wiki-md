<h1 id="section-a/a9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A9](../a9.md)

The [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) gives $\omega^2=c^2[k_g^2+(\pi/a)^2]$. The [cutoff frequency](../../../../../cutoff-frequency.md) is $f_c=c/(2a)=1.499\,\mathrm{GHz}$. In a lossless empty guide, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) travels at [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md),

$$
\boxed{v_E=\frac{d\omega}{dk_g}=c\sqrt{1-\frac{f_c^2}{f^2}}
=0.8003c\simeq2.40\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The larger [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) $\omega/k_g=c^2/v_E$ is not the [speed](../../../../../speed.md) of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) transport.
