<h1 id="section-a/a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) is proportional to $|a+ix|^2=a^2+x^2$, not to the [complex amplitude](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) itself. For equal small intervals approached from inside the support,

$$
\boxed{P_{0}:P_a=a^2:2a^2=1:2.}
$$
