↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) cancels in the ratio of integrals:

$$
\boxed{P(a/2<x<a)=\frac{\int_{a/2}^a(a^2+x^2)dx}{\int_0^a(a^2+x^2)dx}
=\frac{19a^3/24}{4a^3/3}=\frac{19}{32}.}
$$

A normalized version of the specified [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is $\sqrt3(a+ix)/(2a^{3/2})$ on its support.
