<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Treat the repeated angles as independent measurements of one fixed angle. Their [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md), sample [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) and [standard error](../../../../../standard-error.md) of the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) are

$$
\bar\theta=31.24^\circ,\qquad s_\theta=1.997^\circ,\qquad
\sigma_{\bar\theta}=s_\theta/\sqrt5=0.8931^\circ=0.01559\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$

For $y=A\cos\theta$, uncorrelated [standard uncertainties](../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md) give

$$
\left(\frac{\sigma_y}{|y|}\right)^2
=\left(\frac{\sigma_A}A\right)^2+\tan^2\bar\theta\,\sigma_{\bar\theta}^2.
$$

Interpreting the stated 1% [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) accuracy as a [standard uncertainty](../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md),

$$
\boxed{\sigma_y/|y|=0.01376\simeq1.38\%.}
$$

Use radians in the derivative. This is uncertainty in the result using the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) angle, not the spread of individual angle measurements; the latter convention would use $s_\theta$ and give about $2.34\%$. An accuracy bound or systematic angular calibration error would require a different uncertainty model.
