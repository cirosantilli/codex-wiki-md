<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

For this [molecular escape-speed temperature estimate](../../../../../molecular-escape-speed-temperature-estimate.md), $v_{\rm rms}^2=3k_BT/m$. Equating this to the surface escape-speed square $v_{\rm esc}^2=2GM_\oplus/R_\oplus$ gives

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{2mGM_\oplus}{3k_BR_\oplus}\simeq1.40\times10^5\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This evaluation keeps the paper's $R_\oplus=6.4\times10^6\,\mathrm m$ and $m=28u$, and uses externally documented $GM_\oplus=3.9860\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3s^{-2}}$, $u=1.660539040\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $k_B=1.38064852\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.[Bulk parameters: gravitational parameter GM](../../../../../earth-fact-sheet-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)[Atomic mass constant and Boltzmann constant](../../../../../codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2014-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) The corresponding escape speed is about $11.2\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$.

This is the requested speed-scale comparison. Atmospheric escape can occur from the high-speed tail at lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); molecular dissociation and ionization also prevent treating an actual gas at this [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) as unchanged neutral nitrogen molecules.
