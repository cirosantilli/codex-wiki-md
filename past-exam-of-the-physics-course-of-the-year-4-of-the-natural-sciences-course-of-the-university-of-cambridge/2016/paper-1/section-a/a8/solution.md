<h1 id="section-a/a8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A8](../a8.md)

The [wave numbers](../../../../../wavenumber.md) before and after the drop are $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $q=\sqrt{2m(3E)}/\hbar=\sqrt3\,k$. Continuity of $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at the finite step gives

$$
1+r=t,\qquad k(1-r)=qt,\qquad r=\frac{k-q}{k+q}.
$$

Thus the reflected probability flux fraction is

$$
\boxed{R=|r|^2=\left(\frac{1-\sqrt3}{1+\sqrt3}\right)^2=7-4\sqrt3\simeq0.0718.}
$$

The transmission probability is $(q/k)|t|^2=1-R$, rather than simply $|t|^2$. Reflection is possible at a downward [potential step](../../../../../step-potential.md) even though both sides are classically allowed.
