<h1 id="section-a/a7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A7](../a7.md)

The first ideal linear polarizer transmits half of an unpolarized beam. Each subsequent polarizer is $60^\circ$ from the preceding transmitted [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), so [Malus's law](../../../../../malus-s-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_{\rm out}}{I_{\rm in}}=\frac12\cos^2(60^\circ)\cos^2(60^\circ)=\frac1{32}.}
$$

Intensities, rather than coherent field amplitudes for different input [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) components, are averaged for the unpolarized beam. The output is polarized along the last polarizer's axis.
