<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

For the [ultrarelativistic magnetic bending radius](../../../../../ultrarelativistic-magnetic-bending-radius.md), the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) supplies the transverse [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) change, so $r=p/(eB)$. At this energy the proton is ultrarelativistic, giving $pc\simeq E$ and

$$
\boxed{r\simeq\frac{7\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{eV}}{ec(9\,\mathrm T)}=2.59\times10^3\,\mathrm m\simeq2.6\,\mathrm{km}.}
$$

The electronvolt-to-joule conversion cancels the elementary-charge factor numerically. This uses external CODATA light speed and charge definitions.[Vacuum light speed and electronvolt conversion](../../../../../codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2014-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) If the stated beam energy means total energy, $pc=\sqrt{E^2-m_p^2c^4}$; if it means [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), $pc=\sqrt{T(T+2m_pc^2)}$. Either interpretation gives the quoted radius at the precision of the supplied numbers.
