<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives the radiated [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md)

$$
L=4\pi R_\odot^2\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4\simeq3.69\times10^{26}\,\mathrm W.
$$

For [radiative mass loss at fixed luminosity](../../../../../radiative-mass-loss-at-fixed-luminosity.md), using [mass-energy equivalence](../../../../../mass-energy-equivalence.md), the mass-loss magnitude and one-percent time are

$$
\boxed{|\dot M|=L/c^2\simeq4.10\times10^9\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}},\qquad t_{1\%}=\frac{0.01M_\odot c^2}{L}\simeq4.88\times10^{18}\,\mathrm s\simeq1.55\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

The numerical constants are the external CODATA 2014 $\sigma=5.670367\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-4}}$ and $c=299792458\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; years here mean 365.25 days.[Stefan-Boltzmann constant and vacuum light speed](../../../../../codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2014-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) This estimates radiative mass loss with the supplied radius and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) held constant. It does not predict a real stellar lifetime or include solar-wind mass loss.
