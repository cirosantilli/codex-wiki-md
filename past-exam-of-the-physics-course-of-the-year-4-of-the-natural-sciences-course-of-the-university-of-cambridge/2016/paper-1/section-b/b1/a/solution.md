<h1 id="section-b/b1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the paper's convention $\mathbf J$ is measured in units of $\hbar$, and $g$ is dimensionless. For $m=-J,-J+1,\ldots,J$, the [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and its [Zeeman energy](../../../../../../zeeman-energy.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mu_z=-g\mu_Bm,\qquad E_m=-\mu_zB=g\mu_BBm.}
$$

If $\mathbf J$ instead denotes dimensional angular [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md), its coefficient must contain $1/\hbar$. This resolves the units of the printed moment formula.
