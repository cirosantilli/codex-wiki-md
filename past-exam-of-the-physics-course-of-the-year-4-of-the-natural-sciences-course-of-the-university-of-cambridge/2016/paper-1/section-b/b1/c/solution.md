<h1 id="section-b/b1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $g>0$, $B\to+\infty$ at fixed $T>0$ selects $m=-J$, so all moments align with the field:

$$
\boxed{M\longrightarrow Ng\mu_BJ.}
$$

This is [magnetic saturation](../../../../../../magnetic-saturation.md). The [Curie response of an arbitrary independent spin](../../../../../../curie-response-of-an-arbitrary-independent-spin.md) follows at weak field: $\coth z=z^{-1}+z/3+O(z^3)$ gives

$$
\boxed{M=\frac{N(g\mu_B)^2J(J+1)}{3k_BT}B+O(B^3)\longrightarrow0.}
$$

This is the [Curie law](../../../../../../curie-s-law.md) of independent moments with [paramagnetism](../../../../../../paramagnetism.md). Thermal populations are symmetric at zero field, so the model has no spontaneous [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md). Both limits hold at fixed positive [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md); taking $T\to0$ first is a different limiting procedure.
