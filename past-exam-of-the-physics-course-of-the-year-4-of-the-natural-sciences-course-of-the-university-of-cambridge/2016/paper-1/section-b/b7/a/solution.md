<h1 id="section-b/b7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Invert the given [ladder operators](../../../../../../ladder-operator.md):

$$
\hat x=\sqrt{\frac\hbar{2m\omega}}(\hat a_++\hat a_-),\qquad\hat p=i\sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}2}(\hat a_+-\hat a_-).
$$

Because the [Energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) are orthonormal, $\langle n|\hat a_\pm|n\rangle=0$: each operator changes the state to one orthogonal to $|n\rangle$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle_n=\langle p\rangle_n=0.}
$$

The result also follows from the definite [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) of every oscillator energy eigenfunction, but here it follows directly from the specified operators.
