<h1 id="section-b/b7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [uncertainty product of oscillator number states](../../../../../../uncertainty-product-of-oscillator-number-states.md), the two means vanish, so the [standard deviations](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) are the square roots of the second moments:

$$
\Delta x=\sqrt{\frac\hbar{m\omega}(n+\tfrac12)},\qquad\Delta p=\sqrt{m\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)}.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar(n+\tfrac12).}
$$

Only the ground state $n=0$ saturates the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) in this sequence of [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md); the excited number states have a larger uncertainty product.
