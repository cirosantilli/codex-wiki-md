<h1 id="section-b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed particle number, $dU=T\,dS-P\,dV$. The stated [Maxwell relation](../../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_T=T\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_V-P=\frac{aN^2}{V^2},\qquad U(T,V)=f(T)-\frac{aN^2}{V}+C.
$$

For the intended classical monatomic [Van der Waals gas](../../../../../../van-der-waals-gas.md), [equipartition](../../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) supplies $f(T)=3Nk_BT/2$: the attractive mean-field term is [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) independent and there are three translational quadratic degrees of freedom. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{U_i=\frac32Nk_BT_i-\frac{3aN^2}{V_0}+C.}
$$

The [caloric closure of the van der Waals equation](../../../../../../caloric-closure-of-the-van-der-waals-equation.md) is essential. **Caloric assumption:** the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) equation alone determines the volume dependence, but not the function $f(T)$. The monatomic kinetic-energy model is the additional assumption behind the claimed formula.[Chapter 2: classical monatomic gases and the van der Waals mean-field interaction](../../../../../../statistical-physics-by-david-tong.md)
