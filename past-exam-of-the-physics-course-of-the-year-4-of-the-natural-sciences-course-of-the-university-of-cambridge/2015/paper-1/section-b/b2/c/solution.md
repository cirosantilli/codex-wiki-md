<h1 id="section-b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $b=\pi k_B^2/(3\hbar c)$, so $U=bLT^2$. At fixed length, $dS=C_L\,dT/T$, giving $S=2bLT$ with the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) constant fixed by the unique [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) vacuum. The thermal [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) and generalized [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) follow:

$$
\boxed{S=\frac{2\pi Lk_B^2T}{3\hbar c},\qquad
A=U-TS=-\frac{\pi Lk_B^2T^2}{3\hbar c},\qquad
P=-\left(\frac{\partial A}{\partial L}\right)_T=\frac{\pi k_B^2T^2}{3\hbar c}=\frac UL.}
$$

The [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) constant can also be checked from the exact discrete [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) partition function; the continuum approximation should not be extrapolated blindly below its level-spacing regime. In one dimension $P=U/L$, whereas the isotropic three-dimensional relation is $p=U/(3V)$. Both follow from the number of spatial directions carrying [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) flux.
