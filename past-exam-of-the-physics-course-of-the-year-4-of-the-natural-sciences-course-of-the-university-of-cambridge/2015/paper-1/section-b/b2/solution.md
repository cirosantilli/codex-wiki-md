[Black-body radiation](../../../../../black-body-radiation.md) is the equilibrium electromagnetic radiation associated with a perfect absorber, whose spectrum is fixed by its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and the available modes. [Photons](../../../../../photon.md) can be emitted and absorbed, so their equilibrium [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) is zero. The model here retains two transverse [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) in an effectively one-dimensional cavity; the resulting [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is a one-dimensional mechanical [force](../../../../../force-split.md), not a three-dimensional [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) in pascals.
