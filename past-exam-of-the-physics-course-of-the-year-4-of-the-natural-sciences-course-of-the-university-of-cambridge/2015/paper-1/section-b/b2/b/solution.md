Differentiating at fixed length gives the [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md)

$$
\boxed{C_L=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_L
=\frac{2\pi Lk_B^2T}{3\hbar c}.}
$$

The linear [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence reflects the constant one-dimensional mode density, in contrast to the cubic dependence of a three-dimensional [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) gas.
