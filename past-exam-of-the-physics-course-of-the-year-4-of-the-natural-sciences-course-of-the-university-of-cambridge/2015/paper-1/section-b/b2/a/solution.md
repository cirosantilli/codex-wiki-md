<h1 id="section-b/b2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Standing-wave wave numbers are $k_n=n\pi/L$, with one positive mode index and two [polarizations](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md). Since $\epsilon=\hbar ck$, the continuum [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) is

$$
\boxed{g(\epsilon)=\frac{2L}{\pi\hbar c}.}
$$

The factor two counts [polarizations](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md); counting independent positive and negative traveling momenta in addition would double count standing modes. The thermal [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
U=\int_0^\infty g(\epsilon)\frac{\epsilon\,d\epsilon}{e^{\epsilon/(k_BT)}-1}
=\frac{2L(k_BT)^2}{\pi\hbar c}\frac{\pi^2}{6}.
$$

Thus the [one-dimensional photon-gas thermodynamics](../../../../../../one-dimensional-photon-gas-thermodynamics.md) gives

$$
\boxed{U=\frac{\pi Lk_B^2T^2}{3\hbar c}.}
$$

Replacing the mode sum by an integral requires the stated [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) to be large compared with the level-spacing scale. This is thermal [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) above the vacuum contribution; a separate boundary-dependent vacuum [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is not included.
