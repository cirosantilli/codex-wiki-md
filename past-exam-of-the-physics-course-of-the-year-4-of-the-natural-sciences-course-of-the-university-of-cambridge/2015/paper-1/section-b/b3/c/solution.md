<h1 id="section-b/b3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Path (b) changes [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) before [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). Its [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) integral is unchanged, but the thermal expansion occurs at $P_2$:

$$
\boxed{W_b=\frac12V_1\kappa_T(P_2^2-P_1^2)-V_1\alpha P_2(T_2-T_1).}
$$

Along the straight path (c), $P=P_1+(P_2-P_1)(T-T_1)/(T_2-T_1)$. Thus $\int_{T_1}^{T_2}P\,dT=(P_1+P_2)(T_2-T_1)/2$, while $\int P\,dP$ depends only on the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) endpoints. Consequently

$$
\boxed{W_c=\frac12V_1\kappa_T(P_2^2-P_1^2)
-\frac12V_1\alpha(P_1+P_2)(T_2-T_1)=\frac{W_a+W_b}{2}.}
$$

This [pressure-temperature path-work identity](../../../../../../pressure-temperature-path-work-identity.md) is a consequence of the linearized [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) law and the straight interpolation. It need not hold for strongly varying compressibility or expansion coefficient.
