<h1 id="section-b/b3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The depicted endpoints have $P_2>P_1$ and $T_2>T_1$. For positive thermal expansion coefficient,

$$
W_a-W_b=V_1\alpha(P_2-P_1)(T_2-T_1)>0.
$$

Thus **path (b) requires the least work on the solid, path (a) the most, and path (c) lies midway between them**. Heating at the higher [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) produces more negative work on the system. The ordering would reverse for a negative expansion coefficient; if the product vanishes, the paths have equal work in this approximation.
