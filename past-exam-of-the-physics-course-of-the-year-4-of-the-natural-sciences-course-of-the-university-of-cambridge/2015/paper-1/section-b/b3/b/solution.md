<h1 id="section-b/b3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Within the stated small-volume-change approximation, treat $V,\kappa_T,\alpha$ in the differential constitutive law as constants. Then

$$
\delta W=V_1\kappa_T P\,dP-V_1\alpha P\,dT.
$$

Path (a) first changes [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) at [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) $P_1$, then changes [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) at fixed [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_2$. The two contributions are $-V_1\alpha P_1(T_2-T_1)$ and $V_1\kappa_T(P_2^2-P_1^2)/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{W_a=\frac12V_1\kappa_T(P_2^2-P_1^2)-V_1\alpha P_1(T_2-T_1).}
$$

Work is path dependent even though the endpoints and approximate total [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) change are the same.
