<h1 id="section-b/b3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The quasistatic pressure-volume work done on the body is $\delta W=-P\,dV$. If its [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) remains constant throughout the process, every $dV$ vanishes, so **the work is zero**. Equal initial and final [volumes](../../../../../../volume.md) alone would not suffice: an expansion and compression cycle can have nonzero work. Other work modes are excluded by this simple elastic-volume model.
