↑ **Parent:** [B4](../b4.md)

Three experimental tests probe different consequences of [special relativity](../../../../../special-relativity-split.md). The [Michelson-Morley experiment](../../../../../michelson-morley-experiment.md) compares light round trips along perpendicular interferometer arms while changing the apparatus orientation. Its small observed orientation-dependent fringe shift contradicts the simple stationary-aether prediction and supports orientation-independent round-trip light speed. It does not, alone, exclude every alternative theory.[1887 apparatus and fringe-shift comparison](../../../../../on-the-relative-motion-of-the-earth-and-the-luminiferous-ether-by-albert-abraham-michelson-and-edward-w-morley.md)

In the [muon storage-ring time-dilation experiment](../../../../../muon-storage-ring-time-dilation-experiment.md), fast circulating muons live longer in laboratory time than muons at rest. Measuring decay counts versus elapsed laboratory time and independently determining the beam [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) tests $\tau_{\rm lab}=\gamma\tau_0$. Circular motion includes acceleration, so this also tests the applicability of accumulated [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) along the stored trajectory.[storage-ring lifetime measurements and comparison with the relativistic factor](../../../../../measurements-of-relativistic-time-dilatation-for-positive-and-negative-muons-in-a-circular-orbit-by-j-bailey-and-others.md)

[Bertozzi's electron speed-energy experiment](../../../../../bertozzi-s-electron-speed-energy-experiment.md) measures [electron](../../../../../electron.md) time of flight and deposited [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) independently. An electrostatic accelerator and a linear accelerator increase the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) while the measured speed approaches the light-speed limit. Calorimetry checks that the additional beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is real, rather than inferring it only from an assumed voltage-to-speed relation. The result supports relativistic $K=(\gamma-1)mc^2$ rather than extending $K=mv^2/2$ to arbitrarily large speeds.[1964 abstract: time-of-flight measurements over 0.5–15 MeV and independent calorimetry](../../../../../speed-and-kinetic-energy-of-relativistic-electrons-by-william-bertozzi.md)

For the two ships, set launch time to zero and measure Earth time in years. Their [Lorentz factors](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) are $\gamma_A=5/4$ and $\gamma_B=5/3$. Bob's thirtieth birthday occurs after one year of his [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md), so its Earth coordinates are

$$
t_B=\frac53\,\mathrm{yr},\qquad x_B=-\frac45ct_B=-\frac43c\,\mathrm{yr}.
$$

If Alice sends at Earth time $t_A$, her position is $x_A=3ct_A/5$. The left-going light signal obeys $x=x_A-c(t-t_A)$. Intersection with Bob gives

$$
\frac35t_A-(t_B-t_A)=-\frac45t_B,
\qquad t_A=\frac{t_B}{8}=\frac5{24}\,\mathrm{yr}.
$$

Alice's elapsed [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\tau_A=\frac{t_A}{\gamma_A}=\frac16\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

**She sends two months after her twenty-ninth birthday.** This compares the emission and reception events; the captains' clocks need only be synchronized at launch, not kept numerically synchronized afterward.

A left-going birthday signal intersects Bob's world line one year into his proper time**. Original Earth-frame spacetime calculation with c set to one light-year per year. The emission point is two months into Alice's proper time.

The relative recession speed is

$$
\frac{|u_{B|A}|}{c}=\frac{4/5+3/5}{1+(4/5)(3/5)}=\frac{35}{37}.
$$

The longitudinal [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{f_B=f_0\sqrt{\frac{1-35/37}{1+35/37}}=\frac{f_0}{6}.}
$$

Equivalently, the left-going [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has Earth [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) $f_0/[\gamma_A(1+3/5)]=f_0/2$, and Bob's frame supplies another factor $\gamma_B(1-4/5)=1/3$. Both methods give the same redshift.
