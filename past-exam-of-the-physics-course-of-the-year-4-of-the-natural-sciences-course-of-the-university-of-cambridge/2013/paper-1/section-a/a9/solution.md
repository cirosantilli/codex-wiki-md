<h1 id="section-a/a9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A9](../a9.md)

The transverse-wave impedance of a string is $Z=\sqrt{T\mu}$ for common tension $T$. The density ratio four gives $Z_2/Z_1=2$. Continuity of displacement and transverse [force](../../../../../force-split.md) gives transmitted amplitude ratio $t=2Z_1/(Z_1+Z_2)=2/3$. The [power transmission coefficient](../../../../../power-transmission-coefficient.md) includes the impedance ratio:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T=\frac{Z_2}{Z_1}|t|^2=\frac89.}
$$

The reflected fraction is $1/9$, checking [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation. Squaring the displacement amplitude alone would give the wrong transmitted power.
