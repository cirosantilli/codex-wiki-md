<h1 id="section-a/a6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A6](../a6.md)

The quoted angular diameter gives half-angle $\theta=0.005$. At distance $r=10^{13}\,\mathrm m$, the uniform beam covers area $A\simeq\pi(r\theta)^2=7.85\times10^{21}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Its intensity is $I=P/A\simeq2.93\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. For a vacuum electromagnetic wave, $I=\epsilon_0cE_{\rm rms}^2$, so

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm rms}\simeq1.05\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}.}
$$

No extra factor two belongs in this rms relation. The [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) fixes the wavelength but is unnecessary once the total power and stipulated beam divergence are specified. This is the ideal directed-beam estimate, before propagation or receiver losses.
