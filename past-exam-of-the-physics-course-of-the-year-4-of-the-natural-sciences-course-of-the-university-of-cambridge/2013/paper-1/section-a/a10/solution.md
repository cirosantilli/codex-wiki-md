<h1 id="section-a/a10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A10](../a10.md)

For [photons](../../../../../photon.md) emitted back to back in the measurement frame, their total invariant [mass](../../../../../mass.md) obeys $m^2c^4=(E_1+E_2)^2-(E_1-E_2)^2=4E_1E_2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{E_2=\frac{(125\,\mathrm{GeV})^2}{4(30\,\mathrm{GeV})}\simeq130.2\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

The parent's total laboratory [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is consequently about $160.2\,\mathrm{GeV}$, exceeding its rest [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The measured-frame opposite-directions condition is essential: opposite directions only in the parent's rest frame would hold automatically and would not determine the second laboratory [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by itself.
