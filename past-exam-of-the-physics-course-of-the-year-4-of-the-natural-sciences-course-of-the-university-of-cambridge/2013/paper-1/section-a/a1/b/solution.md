<h1 id="section-a/a1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [electron diffraction](../../../../../../electron-diffraction.md), accelerate [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) through a known voltage and measure the angular scattering pattern from a crystal. Distinct [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) maxima occur where the periodic atomic arrangement gives constructive [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md). Their positions give a wavelength through the crystal spacing and [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) geometry, agreeing with the [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda=h/p$, with $p\simeq\sqrt{2m_e eV}$ in the nonrelativistic regime. Varying the accelerating voltage shifts the maxima in the predicted way. A localized classical projectile model alone would not explain this wavelength-dependent [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) pattern.
