<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

The [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) levels for width $L$ are $E_n=n^2h^2/(8m_eL^2)$. Starting in the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md), the smallest excitation is $1\to2$, and its electric-dipole matrix element is nonzero. It therefore sets the longest absorption wavelength:

$$
\frac{hc}{\lambda_{\max}}=E_2-E_1=\frac{3h^2}{8m_eL^2},\qquad
\boxed{L=\sqrt{\frac{3h\lambda_{\max}}{8m_ec}}\simeq0.302\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The corresponding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) separation is about $12.4\,\mathrm{eV}$. Elastic scattering of light is a different process from the stipulated absorption into an excited well state.
