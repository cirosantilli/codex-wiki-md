<h1 id="section-b/b1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At $r_0=6L/5$, the [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) ratio is $\Omega/\omega_0=3$. There are three radial oscillations during one mean revolution, giving a nearly circular orbit with three outward and three inward extrema. The leading small-amplitude sketch closes after one revolution. Finite-amplitude corrections generally change the [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) ratio and can produce apsidal drift; the linear ratio does not prove an exactly closed finite-amplitude orbit for the full stretched-string potential.
