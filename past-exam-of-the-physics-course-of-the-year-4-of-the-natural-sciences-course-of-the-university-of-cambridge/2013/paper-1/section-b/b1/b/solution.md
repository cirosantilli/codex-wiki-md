<h1 id="section-b/b1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $r_0\gg L$, $\Omega/\omega_0\to2$. The potential approaches the isotropic harmonic-oscillator form $kr^2/2$, and the perturbed path is approximately a small-eccentricity ellipse centered on the fixed point. The radius has two maxima and two minima per revolution. For finite nonzero $L/r_0$, the ellipse is an approximation and slow precession can remain.
