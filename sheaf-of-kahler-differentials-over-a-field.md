<h1 id="sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field">Sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials](sheaf-of-relative-kahler-differentials.md)

For a [variety](algebraic-variety.md) over $k$, the [module of Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md) $\Omega_{A/k}$ on each [affine chart](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) gives a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md). Compatibility with [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) glues these into $\Omega^1_{X/k}$. A [finite presentation of an algebra](finite-presentation-of-an-algebra.md) gives a [finite presentation of a module](finite-presentation-of-a-module.md) for its differentials, so this is a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) on a [variety](algebraic-variety.md).
