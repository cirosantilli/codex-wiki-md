# Multi-slit spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Multi-object spectrograph](multi-object-spectrograph.md)

A multi-slit spectrograph puts separate slits at target positions in the focal plane. Each slit transmits the target plus nearby sky; dispersion places its spatially resolved [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) on the detector.
