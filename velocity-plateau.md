# Velocity plateau

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md)

An interval of particle [velocities](velocity.md) on which an equilibrium [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) is constant. Its zero derivative removes the resonant imaginary part of the linear [dielectric function](dielectric-function.md) for waves with [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) inside the interval. This permits a completely undamped [electron acoustic wave](electron-acoustic-wave.md) in suitable single-population, nonthermal electron distributions. Zero slope alone is necessary but does not guarantee that the real [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) has a root.
