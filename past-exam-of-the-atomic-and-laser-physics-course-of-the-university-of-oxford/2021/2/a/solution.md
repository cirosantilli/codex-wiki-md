<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand the [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) in unperturbed [energy eigenstates](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md),

$$
|\psi(t)\rangle=c_1(t)e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}|1\rangle+c_2(t)e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}|2\rangle.
$$

The $c_j$ are [interaction picture](../../../../interaction-picture.md) [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md), so $|c_j|^2$ are level populations and $|c_1|^2+|c_2|^2=1$ in this closed [two-level system](../../../../two-level-system.md). A classical field $\mathbf E_0\cos\omega t$ supplies the [electric-dipole interaction](../../../../electric-dipole-interaction.md) $-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E_0\cos\omega t$. Choosing the relative phase of the [energy eigenstates](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) makes $\Omega=-\langle1|\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E_0|2\rangle/\hbar$ real. This is the resonant [Rabi angular frequency](../../../../rabi-angular-frequency.md).

Combining the field's two exponentials with the unperturbed [quantum phases](../../../../quantum-phase.md) produces terms oscillating at $\omega-\omega_0=\delta$ and at $\omega+\omega_0$. The [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md) drops the latter, leaving exactly the supplied equations with coupling $\Omega/2$. It requires $|\Omega|,|\delta|\ll\omega_0$ and a field envelope slow enough on the carrier timescale. [Spontaneous emission](../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) and [dephasing](../../../../dephasing-channel.md) must be negligible during the sequence. For a ground-state [hyperfine level](../../../../hyperfine-level.md) transition, the analogous direct drive is a [magnetic dipole transition](../../../../magnetic-dipole-transition.md); the same equations hold with the magnetic [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) replacing the electric one.
