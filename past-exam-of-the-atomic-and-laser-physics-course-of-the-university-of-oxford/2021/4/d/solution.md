<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the initial ground-state [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) at rest in the laboratory. The incident [photon](../../../../photon.md) has [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $h\nu$ and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $h\nu/c$. The excited [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) has [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) $M+\Delta M$ and must carry that [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). [Conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) and [conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) give

$$
Mc^2+h\nu=\sqrt{(M+\Delta M)^2c^4+(h\nu)^2}.
$$

Squaring cancels the photon-energy square:

$$
M^2c^4+2Mc^2h\nu=(M+\Delta M)^2c^4.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\nu=\frac{\Delta Mc^2}{h}\left(1+\frac{\Delta M}{2M}\right)
=\nu_{\rm int}+\frac{h\nu_{\rm int}^2}{2Mc^2},\qquad
h\nu_{\rm int}=\Delta Mc^2.}
$$

The extra positive term is the [photon absorption recoil correction](../../../../photon-absorption-recoil-correction.md) and supplies atomic [recoil energy](../../../../recoil-energy.md). The formula is exact for the initially stationary isolated [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md); it does not assume $\Delta M/M\ll1$. Initial and final [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) cannot both remain at rest during single-[photon](../../../../photon.md) absorption without another body taking [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md).
