<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The closed $1s^22s^2$ shells have $L=S=0$, leaving the equivalent $2p^2$ [electrons](../../../../electron.md). [Addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) and the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) permit the [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md) ${}^3P,{}^1D,{}^1S$. Their [term symbols](../../../../term-symbol.md) use $^{2S+1}L_J$. [Hund's rules](../../../../hund-s-rules.md) put the [triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) below the [singlet states](../../../../singlet-state.md); for a less-than-half-filled $p$ shell the smallest $J$ in ${}^3P$ is lowest. Thus

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{r|c}
(E-E_g)/(hc)\ (\mathrm{cm^{-1}})&1s^22s^22p^2\text{ level}\\\hline
0&{}^3P_0\\
16.4&{}^3P_1\\
43.4&{}^3P_2\\
10192&{}^1D_2\\
21648&{}^1S_0
\end{array}}
$$

All have even [parity](../../../../parity.md). These assignments agree with independent tabulated [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md).[2s²2p² entries: ³P₀,₁,₂ at 0, 16.41671 and 43.41350 cm⁻¹; ¹D₂ at 10192.66 cm⁻¹; ¹S₀ at 21648.02 cm⁻¹](../../../../energy-levels-of-neutral-carbon-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) The rounded source values should not be forced to satisfy an exact [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md); pure first-order [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md) need not account for every measured [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md) correction.
