<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For two decays from a common upper [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md), their photon [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) difference equals the separation of the two lower [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md). Treating both quoted [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) as vacuum values initially gives

$$
\widetilde\nu_1=\frac{10^7}{193.09}=51789.32\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\qquad
\widetilde\nu_2=\frac{10^7}{247.86}=40345.36\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},
$$

whose difference $11443.96\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ is close to $21648-10192=11456\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. The only matching lower pair among the supplied [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md) is ${}^1D_2,{}^1S_0$, with the shorter-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) [photon](../../../../photon.md) ending on ${}^1D_2$. The two independent upper [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) estimates are

$$
\widetilde E_u=10192+51789.32=61981.32\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\qquad
\widetilde E_u=21648+40345.36=61993.36\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\widetilde E_u\simeq6.20\times10^4\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\simeq7.69\,\mathrm{eV},\quad
1s^22s^22p3s\,{}^1P_1^{\rm o}.}
$$

The [electric-dipole selection rules](../../../../electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) require odd [parity](../../../../parity.md), $S=0$, $L=1$ and $J=1$ to connect to both singlet lower [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md). The apparent discrepancy has a concrete convention explanation. The NIST [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) line table lists $193.0906\,\mathrm{nm}$ under **vacuum** [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) and $247.8561\,\mathrm{nm}$ under **air** [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md), reproducing the source's rounded numbers.[Vacuum section: C I 1930.906 Å; air section: C I 2478.561 Å](../../../../strong-lines-of-carbon-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) The second number must therefore be converted to a vacuum [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) before using $E=hc/\lambda$. For the tabulated upper $61981.82\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and lower $21648.02\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md), its vacuum [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) is $247.931\,\mathrm{nm}$, corresponding to an air [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) about $1.00030$. This removes the spurious $12\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ discrepancy. The first calculation alone, which correctly uses a vacuum [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md), already gives the intended upper [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md) to the accuracy of the rounded data. The independent upper value is $61981.82\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.[2s²2p(²P°)3s ¹P°₁ entry at 61981.82 cm⁻¹](../../../../energy-levels-of-neutral-carbon-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
