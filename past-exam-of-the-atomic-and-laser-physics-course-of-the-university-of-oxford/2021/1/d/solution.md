<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Neglect [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md), [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) and the diamagnetic term, and set $b=\mu_BB$, $\varepsilon=\alpha a_0^2$. The [Zeeman energy](../../../../zeeman-energy.md) is $b(L_{\widehat B}+2S_{\widehat B})$. The [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) is free, so every spatial result below is duplicated with an added $\pm b$.

For a [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) parallel to $z$, the old [magnetic quantum number](../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) $m$ remains good and the first-order [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
E_{1s,\pm}&=-\mathrm{Ry}+\tfrac32\varepsilon\pm b,\\
E_{2p_0,\pm}&=-\mathrm{Ry}/4+3\varepsilon\pm b,\\
E_{2s,\pm}&=-\mathrm{Ry}/4+21\varepsilon\pm b,\\
E_{2p_m,\pm}&=-\mathrm{Ry}/4+21\varepsilon+mb\pm b,\quad m=\pm1.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The two $s$ [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) have zero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md), so only their [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) contributes at this order.

For a [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) parallel to $x$, the expectation of $L_x$ in each individual $p_m$ [orbital](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) vanishes. One must still check the degenerate $m=\pm1$ subspace: its projected $L_x$ is zero as well. Thus **there is no first-order orbital splitting in a sufficiently weak transverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md)**. Spin still contributes $\pm b$.

The [transverse orbital Zeeman mixing](../../../../transverse-orbital-zeeman-mixing.md) can be seen without assuming its size. Put $E_0=-\mathrm{Ry}/4+3\varepsilon$, $E_t=-\mathrm{Ry}/4+21\varepsilon$, and $\Delta=E_t-E_0=18\varepsilon$. In the real $p_x,p_y,p_z$ [orbital](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) basis, $p_x$ is unaffected while $p_y,p_z$ have off-diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md) of modulus $b$. Diagonalizing this two-dimensional [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) gives the spatial [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md)

$$
\boxed{E_t,\qquad \frac{E_t+E_0}{2}\pm\sqrt{\left(\frac\Delta2\right)^2+b^2}.}
$$

When $b\ll\Delta$, their shifts relative to the earlier levels are $0,+b^2/\Delta,-b^2/\Delta$, with $\pm b$ added for [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md). The displayed square-root expression also handles $b$ comparable to $\Delta$, provided coupling to other principal shells stays negligible. The $2s$ [orbital](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) retains zero orbital correction.

These quadratic orbital estimates keep the enhanced contribution from mixing the split $2p$ levels. Minimal [magnetic minimal coupling](../../../../magnetic-minimal-coupling.md) also produces $e^2B^2r_\perp^2/(8m_e)$; its [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) is positive and must be added for a full order-$B^2$ calculation. For example it gives $e^2B^2a_0^2/(4m_e)$ for $1s$. It is parametrically smaller than the $2p$ mixing term when $\varepsilon\ll\mathrm{Ry}$.
