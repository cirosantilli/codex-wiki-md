<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Within a fixed $L,S,J$ [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) multiplet, the component of $\mathbf L$ along $\mathbf J$ follows from $2\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf J=\mathbf J^2+\mathbf L^2-\mathbf S^2$. The corresponding result for $\mathbf S$ follows by interchanging $L$ and $S$. For the [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md) $|LSJM_J\rangle$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle L_z\rangle&=M_J\frac{J(J+1)+L(L+1)-S(S+1)}{2J(J+1)},\\
\langle S_z\rangle&=M_J\frac{J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)}{2J(J+1)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are dimensionless [expectation values](../../../../expectation-value.md) in the convention of part (a). Substituting into the first-order [time-independent perturbation theory](../../../../time-independent-perturbation-theory.md) formula $\Delta E=\langle H_Z\rangle$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta E=g_J\mu_BBM_J,\qquad
g_J=1+(g_s-1)\frac{J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)}{2J(J+1)}.}
$$

For $g_s=2$, the [Landé g-factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{g_J=1+\frac{J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)}{2J(J+1)}.}
$$

The [magnetic sublevels](../../../../magnetic-sublevel.md) must first be diagonalized within a degenerate multiplet; choosing $M_J$ along the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) already does this. For $J=0$ the displayed fraction is undefined, but all vector [expectation values](../../../../expectation-value.md) vanish and there is no first-order [Zeeman splitting](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md). A higher-order shift may remain.
