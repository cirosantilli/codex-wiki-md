<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The printed [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) uses dimensionless [angular momentum operators](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md): their physical counterparts are $\hbar\mathbf L$ and $\hbar\mathbf S$. Choose the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) along $z$. Then the [Zeeman energy](../../../../zeeman-energy.md) is $H_Z=\mu_BB(L_z+g_sS_z)$, whose characteristic size is $\mu_BB$. In [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md), treating this as a [time-independent perturbation theory](../../../../time-independent-perturbation-theory.md) requires it to be much smaller than the relevant [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md) separation, rather than merely smaller than the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).

For a concrete [hydrogen atom](../../../../hydrogen-atom.md) estimate take the $2p_{1/2},2p_{3/2}$ pair. The [hydrogen fine-structure correction](../../../../hydrogen-fine-structure-correction.md) is

$$
E_{nj}=-\frac{\mathrm{Ry}}{n^2}-\frac{\mathrm{Ry}\,\alpha_{\rm fs}^2}{n^3}\left(\frac1{j+1/2}-\frac3{4n}\right),
$$

where $\alpha_{\rm fs}$ is the [fine-structure constant](../../../../fine-structure-constant.md), distinguished from the later potential coefficient. Thus

$$
\Delta E_{2p}=\frac{\mathrm{Ry}\,\alpha_{\rm fs}^2}{16}\simeq4.53\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad
\frac{\Delta E_{2p}}{\mu_B}\simeq0.78\,\mathrm T.
$$

Consequently a suitable weak-[magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) condition for this pair is

$$
\boxed{B\ll0.8\,\mathrm T;\quad B\sim10^{-2}\,\mathrm T\text{ is comfortably perturbative.}}
$$

There is no single threshold for every [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md): the relevant [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md) separations decrease with $n$. The $1s$ [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md) has no same-$n$ [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) partner, so it cannot itself define this comparison. This calculation neglects [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md); resolving that smaller scale requires an additional weak-[magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) condition.
