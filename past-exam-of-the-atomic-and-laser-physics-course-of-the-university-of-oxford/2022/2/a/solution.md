<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the dimensionful [angular momentum operators](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) obey $\mathbf F=\mathbf I+\mathbf J$ and take the interaction [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) as $H'=A\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J$. Since

$$
\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J=\tfrac12(\mathbf F^2-\mathbf I^2-\mathbf J^2),
$$

the [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) at fixed $I,J$ are

$$
E_F=\frac{a}{2}\{F(F+1)-I(I+1)-J(J+1)\},\qquad a=A\hbar^2,
$$

where $F=|I-J|,\ldots,I+J$. Subtraction gives the [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md)

$$
\boxed{E_F-E_{F-1}=aF.}
$$

Many atomic conventions use dimensionless [angular momentum operators](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) and call the energy-valued constant itself $A$; then the right-hand side is $AF$. Each [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md) has $2F+1$ [magnetic sublevels](../../../../magnetic-sublevel.md) with the same [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in zero field.

For [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md), the effective interaction is $a_{LS}\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ with dimensionless [angular momentum operators](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md). It originates in the [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md), including the magnetic interaction of [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) with orbital motion and its relativistic correction. Within an isolated [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md) [atomic term](../../../../atomic-term.md), the labels are the [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md), $L,S,J,M_J$, and the interval between adjacent $J$ [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md) is $a_{LS}J$. This is smaller than the electrostatic separation of [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md).

For magnetic-dipole [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md), the [nuclear magnetic dipole moment](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-dipole-moment.md) interacts with the magnetic field generated by the [electrons](../../../../electron.md). Contributions include the [Fermi contact interaction](../../../../fermi-contact-interaction.md) of penetrating $s$ [electrons](../../../../electron.md), orbital magnetic fields, and the dipolar field of [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md). Projected onto one electronic $J$ [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md), their rotationally invariant magnetic-dipole part has the form $a_{IJ}\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J$. The labels are $I,J,F,M_F$, together with the approximate electronic labels such as $L,S$. This splitting is generally smaller than [fine structure](../../../../fine-structure.md), reflecting the much smaller nuclear [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). An electric [nuclear quadrupole moment](../../../../nuclear-quadrupole-moment.md) can add a different interaction and violate the simple [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md).

At the larger electrostatic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) scale, an effective two-[spin](../../../../spin.md) exchange interaction can also be written $a_s\mathbf s_1\cdot\mathbf s_2$ within fixed [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md). Its origin is the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) combined with the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md), rather than a large direct magnetic interaction. It separates [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md) and [triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md); before [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md), useful labels include the [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md), $L,M_L,S,M_S$. General electrostatic [atomic term](../../../../atomic-term.md) separations need not obey a single [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md) as $L$ varies.
