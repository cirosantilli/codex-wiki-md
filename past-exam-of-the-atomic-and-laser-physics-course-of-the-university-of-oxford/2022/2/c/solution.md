<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $a_\nu=a/h$, so the [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md) for [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) intervals in ordinary [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) is $\nu_F-\nu_{F-1}=a_\nu F$. For the first four selected positions, the successive positive differences are

$$
21.19-18.37=2.82,\quad18.37-14.84=3.53,\quad14.84-10.60=4.24\quad\mathrm{GHz}.
$$

Their ratios are approximately $4:5:6$, since

$$
\frac{2.82}{4}=0.7050,\qquad\frac{3.53}{5}=0.7060,\qquad\frac{4.24}{6}=0.7067\quad\mathrm{GHz}.
$$

The decreasing [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) therefore correspond to increasing $F$, with $a_\nu\simeq-0.706\,\mathrm{GHz}$, if increasing printed position means increasing physical [frequency](../../../../frequency.md). Continuing the intervals predicts additional positions near $10.60-7(0.706)\simeq5.66$ and then $5.66-8(0.706)\simeq0.01\,\mathrm{GHz}$. They identify peaks $i$ and $l$ unambiguously. The full assignment for $^{151}\mathrm{Eu}$ is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
\text{peak}&a&b&c&e&i&l\\\hline
F&3&4&5&6&7&8\\
\nu\ (\mathrm{GHz})&21.19&18.37&14.84&10.60&5.63&0.00
\end{array}}
$$

With $J=11/2$, these endpoints require

$$
I+J=8,\qquad |J-I|=3,\qquad\boxed{I_{151}=5/2.}
$$

Equivalently, a complete set of six [hyperfine levels](../../../../hyperfine-level.md) gives $2I+1=6$ when $I<J$. This inference assumes that every upper [hyperfine level](../../../../hyperfine-level.md) contributes an observed position and the negligible lower [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) lets coincident transitions be grouped by their upper [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). A line count without these completeness assumptions would not alone fix [nuclear spin](../../../../nuclear-spin.md).

**The supplied positions obey the [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md) only approximately.** For example, $\nu_e-\nu_i=4.97\,\mathrm{GHz}$, whereas $7(\nu_a-\nu_b)/4=4.935\,\mathrm{GHz}$. Even allowing each printed position a rounding error of $0.005\,\mathrm{GHz}$ does not fully reconcile these two relations. A least-squares dipole-only fit has a largest position residual of about $0.017\,\mathrm{GHz}$. Small additional [hyperfine interactions](../../../../hyperfine-interaction.md), measurement uncertainty beyond the displayed precision, or an error in the tabulated data could account for this; it is inappropriate to claim an exact identity. The grouping and [nuclear spin](../../../../nuclear-spin.md) are nevertheless robust. Reversing the convention for the printed [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) axis reverses the inferred signs of both constants, without changing the following ratio.
