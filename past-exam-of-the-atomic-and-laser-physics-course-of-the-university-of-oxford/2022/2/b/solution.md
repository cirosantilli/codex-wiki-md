<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The $4f^7$ shell carries a large [spin](../../../../spin.md) (its ground [atomic term](../../../../atomic-term.md) under [Hund's rules](../../../../hund-s-rules.md) has $L=0,S=7/2$), and the $6s$ [electron](../../../../electron.md) penetrates the inner shells. Its nonzero [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) at the [atomic nucleus](../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) supplies a potentially large [Fermi contact interaction](../../../../fermi-contact-interaction.md) with the [nuclear magnetic dipole moment](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The $6s$ shell here is singly occupied, so the obligatory paired-$6s^2$ cancellation is absent. Both stable [europium](../../../../europium.md) [isotopes](../../../../isotope.md) have nonzero [nuclear spin](../../../../nuclear-spin.md), as the spectrum below also establishes.

The [term symbol](../../../../term-symbol.md) ${}^8D_{11/2}$ specifies $S=7/2$, $L=2$, $J=11/2$. Its large $J$, combined with appreciable [nuclear spin](../../../../nuclear-spin.md), permits a wide range of total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $F$ and hence a substantial total [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) span. For $I=5/2$, $F=3,\ldots,8$ and the span is $30|a|$. The penetrating $6s$ contribution and the available range of $F$ explain the large [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md); the [term symbol](../../../../term-symbol.md) alone does not determine its constant, which also depends on electronic recoupling, radial [wavefunctions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) and [configuration interaction](../../../../configuration-interaction.md).
