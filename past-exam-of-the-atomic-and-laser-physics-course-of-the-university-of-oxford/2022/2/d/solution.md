<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The remaining six positions give the other [isotope](../../../../isotope.md)'s sequence

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
\text{peak}&d&f&g&h&j&k\\\hline
F&3&4&5&6&7&8\\
\nu\ (\mathrm{GHz})&10.99&9.74&8.17&6.29&4.09&1.58
\end{array}}
$$

The adjacent differences divided by $4,5,6,7,8$ are $0.3125,0.3140,0.3133,0.3143,0.3138\,\mathrm{GHz}$, respectively. Thus $a_{\nu,153}\simeq-0.314\,\mathrm{GHz}$ and the same endpoint argument gives $\boxed{I_{153}=5/2}$.

For the same electronic [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md), the magnetic-dipole [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) constant is proportional to $\mu_I/I$, to leading order in the small [isotope](../../../../isotope.md)-dependent change of the electronic [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md). Since the two [nuclear spins](../../../../nuclear-spin.md) are equal,

$$
\frac{\mu_{151}}{\mu_{153}}\simeq\frac{a_{151}}{a_{153}}.
$$

Using the full spans averages over the approximate [Landé interval rule](../../../../lande-interval-rule.md): $\sum_{F=4}^8F=30$, so

$$
|a_{\nu,151}|\simeq\frac{21.19}{30},\qquad
|a_{\nu,153}|\simeq\frac{10.99-1.58}{30}\quad\mathrm{GHz},
\qquad\boxed{\frac{\mu_{151}}{\mu_{153}}\simeq\frac{21.19}{9.41}\simeq2.25.}
$$

The constants have the same sign, so the [nuclear magnetic dipole moments](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-dipole-moment.md) have the same relative sign. Their absolute sign cannot be obtained from their ratio without the electronic field's sign. Finite nuclear-size corrections to the [hyperfine interaction](../../../../hyperfine-interaction.md), often called the [hyperfine anomaly](../../../../hyperfine-anomaly.md), and electric-quadrupole corrections are neglected at this accuracy.
