<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $5s5d$, [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $L=2$ and $S=0,1$. The even-[parity](../../../../parity.md) [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md) and [atomic fine-structure levels](../../../../atomic-fine-structure-level.md) are

$$
\boxed{{}^1D_2,\qquad{}^3D_1,{}^3D_2,{}^3D_3.}
$$

The [electric-dipole selection rules](../../../../electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) exclude all connections to the even-[parity](../../../../parity.md) $5s^2$ and $5s6s$ [electron configurations](../../../../electron-configuration.md). To the odd-[parity](../../../../parity.md) $5s5p$ [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md), all seven allowed connections in pure [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
{}^1D_2&\leftrightarrow{}^1P_1,\\
{}^3D_1&\leftrightarrow{}^3P_0,{}^3P_1,{}^3P_2,\\
{}^3D_2&\leftrightarrow{}^3P_1,{}^3P_2,\\
{}^3D_3&\leftrightarrow{}^3P_2.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Here a connection is listed independently of whether the $5s5d$ [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md) is above or below its partner.

In a weak [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), use the [Landé g-factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md)

$$
g_J=1+\frac{J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)}{2J(J+1)}.
$$

For the three ${}^3D_J$ [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md), this gives $1/2,7/6,4/3$; for ${}^3P_1,{}^3P_2$ it gives $3/2$. The six-component [spectral line](../../../../spectral-line.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{{}^3D_1\leftrightarrow{}^3P_1.}
$$

A component has [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) displacement $(\mu_BB/h)(m_D/2-3m_P/2)$. The [electric dipole transition](../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md) with $m_D=m_P=0$ has zero [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md), since the relevant $J=1\leftrightarrow1$ rank-one [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) vanish there. The remaining allowed pairs give

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{polarization}&m_D-m_P&h\Delta\nu/(\mu_BB)\\\hline
\pi&0&-1,+1\\
\sigma&+1&+1/2,+3/2\\
\sigma&-1&-3/2,-1/2
\end{array}
$$

Thus all six [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) positions $\pm1/2,\pm1,\pm3/2$ are distinct. Perpendicular to the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), both the $\pi$ and $\sigma$ components are visible, with mutually perpendicular linear [polarizations](../../../../polarization-waves.md). Along the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) the $\pi$ components disappear. This is the weak-field [Zeeman effect](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md), before the [Paschen-Back effect](../../../../paschen-back-effect.md) changes the coupling scheme.
