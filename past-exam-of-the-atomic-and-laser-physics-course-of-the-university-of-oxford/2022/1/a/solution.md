<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md), first add the individual [orbital angular momenta](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) and [spins](../../../../spin.md) separately, then add their totals:

$$
\mathbf L=\sum_i\mathbf l_i,\qquad \mathbf S=\sum_i\mathbf s_i,\qquad \mathbf J=\mathbf L+\mathbf S.
$$

The [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) between [electrons](../../../../electron.md) must separate different [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md) more strongly than the [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) mixes them. Then $L,S,J,M_J$ are useful [quantum numbers](../../../../quantum-number.md), with $J=|L-S|,\ldots,L+S$. This approximation is usually better for lighter [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) and well-separated [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md); strong [configuration interaction](../../../../configuration-interaction.md) or strong [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) can spoil it. The [term symbol](../../../../term-symbol.md) is $^{2S+1}L_J$.

The general [electric-dipole selection rules](../../../../electric-dipole-selection-rule.md), for isolated [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) with definite [parity](../../../../parity.md) and total electronic [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), are a change of [parity](../../../../parity.md), $\Delta J=0,\pm1$ excluding $J=0\leftrightarrow0$, and $\Delta M_J=0,\pm1$, with the accessible [magnetic sublevels](../../../../magnetic-sublevel.md) determined by the light's [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md). In pure [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md), the additional [selection rules](../../../../selection-rule.md) are $\Delta S=0$ and $\Delta L=0,\pm1$, excluding $L=0\leftrightarrow0$. In a one-[electron](../../../../electron.md) description the [electric dipole operator](../../../../electric-dipole-operator.md) also requires $\Delta l=\pm1$ for the active [electron](../../../../electron.md). These latter statements can fail as exact labels when [atomic terms](../../../../atomic-term.md) mix; the general [parity](../../../../parity.md) and total-[angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) restrictions still apply. Unresolved [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) is neglected here.

For [strontium](../../../../strontium.md), the closed core contributes $L=S=0$. The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) makes the two equivalent $5s$ [electrons](../../../../electron.md) a [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md). Different radial [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) permit both a [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md) and a [triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md). Thus the complete lists of [atomic fine-structure levels](../../../../atomic-fine-structure-level.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{configuration}&\text{terms}&\text{levels}\\\hline
5s^2&{}^1S&{}^1S_0\\
5s5p&{}^1P,{}^3P&{}^1P_1,{}^3P_0,{}^3P_1,{}^3P_2\\
5s6s&{}^1S,{}^3S&{}^1S_0,{}^3S_1
\end{array}}
$$

The $5s5p$ [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md) has odd [parity](../../../../parity.md); the other two [electron configurations](../../../../electron-configuration.md) have even [parity](../../../../parity.md).
