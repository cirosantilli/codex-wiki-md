<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The closest components above differ by $\Delta\nu_{\min}=\mu_BB/(2h)\simeq7.0\,\mathrm{GHz\,T^{-1}}B$. At $B=0.1\,\mathrm T$, their separation is about $0.70\,\mathrm{GHz}$. A visible [spectral line](../../../../spectral-line.md) near $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ would consequently require [spectral resolving power](../../../../spectral-resolving-power.md) of order $\nu/\Delta\nu_{\min}\simeq9\times10^5$. An ordinary low-resolution [spectrometer](../../../../spectrometer.md) would merge the components.

A suitable arrangement places an excited low-pressure [strontium](../../../../strontium.md) discharge or a transversely observed [atomic beam](../../../../atomic-beam.md) between wide electromagnet pole pieces. Collect the emission perpendicular to the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), select the desired [spectral line](../../../../spectral-line.md) with a prefilter or [monochromator](../../../../monochromator.md), and send it through a high-finesse scanning [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](../../../../fabry-perot-interferometer.md) to a detector. A rotatable linear [polarizer](../../../../polarizer.md) separates the two $\pi$ components from the four $\sigma$ components. Calibrate both the interferometer's [free spectral range](../../../../free-spectral-range.md) and the local [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md); take zero-field and several field-strength scans, and fit component positions against $B$.

The [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) should be uniform across the viewed volume, with its associated [Zeeman splitting](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) larger than the combined [natural linewidth](../../../../natural-linewidth.md), [Doppler broadening](../../../../doppler-broadening.md), [pressure broadening](../../../../pressure-broadening.md) and instrumental width, but much smaller than the separations of the relevant [atomic fine-structure levels](../../../../atomic-fine-structure-level.md). A collimated transverse [atomic beam](../../../../atomic-beam.md), low collision rate and restricted collection angle reduce unwanted [spectral line broadening](../../../../spectral-line-broadening.md). Avoid appreciable [power broadening](../../../../power-broadening.md), resolve or model [isotope](../../../../isotope.md) and [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md), and check for self-[absorption](../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md). A zero-field scan and the predicted linear scaling help distinguish actual [Zeeman splitting](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) components from neighbouring [spectral lines](../../../../spectral-line.md).
