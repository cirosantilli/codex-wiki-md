<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Rabi oscillations](../../../../rabi-cycle.md) require a coherent drive with a stable phase over several population cycles and an isolated, appreciably coupled pair of [quantum states](../../../../quantum-state.md). For large-amplitude oscillations, the [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) must be comparable to or smaller than the [Rabi angular frequency](../../../../rabi-angular-frequency.md). The population lifetime, [optical coherence time](../../../../optical-coherence-time.md), transit time and drive coherence time should all exceed $2\pi/\Omega_R$ by enough to resolve the cycles. An ensemble also needs sufficiently uniform [Rabi frequencies](../../../../rabi-frequency.md) and [Rabi detunings](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) that phase averaging does not wash the oscillations out. The usual sinusoidal formula additionally assumes the [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md).

The [Einstein coefficients](../../../../einstein-coefficients.md) describe incoherent transition _rates_, rather than the evolving optical [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md). Their simplest use is appropriate for broadband, randomly phased [radiation](../../../../radiation.md), with field correlation time short relative to population evolution, or for rapidly dephased [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) whose optical coherences can be eliminated. The [radiation energy density](../../../../radiation-energy-density.md) per unit [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) must be smooth over the relevant [spectral line](../../../../spectral-line.md). Stimulated upward and downward transitions and [spontaneous emission](../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) then enter [rate equations](../../../../rate-equation.md), without predicting coherent population return. A short observation time can give a small excitation [probability](../../../../probability.md) even for a coherent field; that condition alone does not justify an [Einstein coefficients](../../../../einstein-coefficients.md) rate description. Coherent [laser](../../../../laser-split.md) irradiation can also admit steady [rate equations](../../../../rate-equation.md) after optical coherence has relaxed, with the appropriate [saturation](../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md) response.
