<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $x=I_0/I_s$ and $G=\alpha_0L$. Applying the integrated amplifier law to each section gives

$$
\ln70+69x=G,\qquad
\ln\frac{150}{70}+80x=G.
$$

Eliminating $G$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_0}{I_s}=\frac1{11}\ln\frac{70^2}{150}\simeq0.31694.}
$$

As a consistency check, both amplifiers have $G\simeq26.1175$. The second section receives a larger [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) and adds $80I_0$, compared with $69I_0$ in the first, while its multiplicative [optical gain](../../../../optical-gain.md) is much smaller. This is the expected approach to constant extracted power in [saturation](../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md).
