<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a uniform steady amplifier, with no extra propagation losses, the local [optical gain](../../../../optical-gain.md) law gives

$$
\frac{dI}{dz}=\frac{\alpha_0I}{1+I/I_s},\qquad
\left(\frac1I+\frac1{I_s}\right)dI=\alpha_0\,dz.
$$

Integrate between the two faces of length $L$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\ln\frac{I_{\rm out}}{I_{\rm in}}+\frac{I_{\rm out}-I_{\rm in}}{I_s}=\alpha_0L.}
$$

For $I\ll I_s$, this reduces to exponential amplification. In deep [saturation](../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md), the [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) increment approaches $\alpha_0I_sL$: finite pumping limits the extracted power.
