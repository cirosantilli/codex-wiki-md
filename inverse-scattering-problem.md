# Inverse scattering problem

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_scattering_problem)

An inverse scattering problem reconstructs a medium or obstacle from measured scattered waves. Under the [Born approximation](born-approximation.md), fixed-frequency far-field data sample the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the scattering potential on a restricted frequency surface.
