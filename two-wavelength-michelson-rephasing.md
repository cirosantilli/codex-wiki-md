# Two-wavelength Michelson rephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md)

Moving one mirror by $d$ changes the round-trip optical path by $2d$. Starting at a common bright centre for two commensurate [wavelengths](wavelength.md), the next common bright centre occurs when $2d=m\lambda_1=n\lambda_2$ for the smallest positive integers. This is a common-multiple condition on optical path, not on the mirror displacement itself. Incommensurate exact [wavelengths](wavelength.md) have no finite exact recurrence in the ideal model.
