# First-countable space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-countable_space)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is first-countable when each point has a countable [neighbourhood basis](neighbourhood-basis.md). Every [metric space](metric-space.md) is first-countable: the [open balls](open-ball.md) $B(x,1/n)$ form such a basis. A subset of a first-countable space remains first-countable in its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md), since intersecting a local basis with the subset preserves the defining containment property. Thus a space failing first countability cannot be [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to any [subset](subset.md) of a [metric space](metric-space.md).
