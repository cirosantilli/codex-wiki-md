# Wallis product

↑ **Parent:** [Pi](pi.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallis_product)

The [Wallis product](wallis-product.md) is

$$
\frac\pi2=\prod_{j=1}^{\infty}\frac{(2j)^2}{(2j-1)(2j+1)}.
$$

Its finite product is $W_n=2^{4n}(n!)^4/[(2n+1)((2n)!)^2]$. The [Wallis integrals](wallis-integrals.md) give $2W_n/\pi=I_{2n+1}/I_{2n}\to1$, proving both convergence of the product and its value. The strict integral bounds also give $\pi n/(2n+1)<W_n<\pi/2$.
