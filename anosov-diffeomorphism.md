# Anosov diffeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anosov_diffeomorphism)

An Anosov diffeomorphism is a continuously differentiable [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) of a compact [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) with an invariant splitting of its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) into stable and unstable [vector subbundles](vector-subbundle.md) satisfying the displayed uniform exponential estimates for $n\ge0$, with $C,\lambda>0$. [Hyperbolic toral automorphisms](hyperbolic-toral-automorphism.md) give examples. An [Anosov flow](anosov-flow.md) has a further one-dimensional invariant bundle along the flow direction; its definition is a distinct continuous-time version of uniform hyperbolicity.
