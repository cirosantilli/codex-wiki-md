# Retained-ejecta chemical evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Instantaneous recycling approximation](instantaneous-recycling-approximation.md)

Under the [instantaneous recycling approximation](instantaneous-recycling-approximation.md), forming mass $dM$ locks $ds=(1-\beta)dM$ into [stars](star.md). If a fraction $f$ of returned material remains in the [interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md), the retained original metal mass is $\beta fZ\,dM$, and retained newly made metal mass is $fy\,ds$, where $y$ is the [stellar yield](stellar-yield.md) per locked mass. Subtracting the metal mass $Z\,dM$ initially consumed gives the displayed equation. Pristine [galactic gas inflow](galactic-gas-inflow.md) adds gas but no metals; enriched inflow adds its metal mass separately. The retained fraction applies to both old and newly made metals in this idealization.
