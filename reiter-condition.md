# Reiter condition

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

For a countable discrete [group](group-split.md), the [Reiter condition](reiter-condition.md) requires a sequence of [probability measures](probability-measure.md) in $\ell^1(G)$ asymptotically invariant under every left translation. It is equivalent to the existence of an [invariant mean](invariant-mean.md) and a [Følner sequence](folner-sequence.md). Separation by bounded functions shows that amenability gives approximate invariance simultaneously on each finite set of translations; countability makes the successive finite tests into a sequence.
