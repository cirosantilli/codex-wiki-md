# Resolvent bound for a discrete SIR epidemic

↑ **Parent:** [Adjacency-matrix bound for a discrete SIR epidemic](adjacency-matrix-bound-for-a-discrete-sir-epidemic.md)

When $\beta\rho(A)<1$, summing the [adjacency-matrix bound for a discrete SIR epidemic](adjacency-matrix-bound-for-a-discrete-sir-epidemic.md) gives the displayed [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) bound on the expected total infected count $Z$. For an undirected [graph](graph-split.md), the [adjacency matrix of a graph](adjacency-matrix.md) is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), so its Euclidean [operator norm](operator-norm.md) equals its [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md). The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) then yields $\mathbb EZ\leq\sqrt{nm}/(1-\beta\rho)$ for $m$ initially infected [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
