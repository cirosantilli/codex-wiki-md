# Quotient vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_vector_space)

For a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W\leq V$, the quotient vector space $V/W$ consists of cosets $v+W$, with vector operations induced from $V$.
