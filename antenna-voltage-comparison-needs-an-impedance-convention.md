# Antenna voltage comparison needs an impedance convention

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna gain-effective-area relation](antenna-gain-effective-area-relation.md)

Antennas with effective areas $A_{e,1}$ and $A_{e,2}$ receiving the same polarization-matched [plane wave](plane-wave.md) have available [powers](power.md) in that ratio. If their matched-load resistances are equal, terminal [voltage](voltage.md) magnitudes have ratio $\sqrt{A_{e,1}/A_{e,2}}$. With unequal matched resistances the ratio becomes $\sqrt{A_{e,1}R_1/(A_{e,2}R_2)}$. Open-circuit [voltages](voltage.md) additionally depend on each antenna's source impedance. Geometry alone therefore fixes available-power estimates more directly than unspecified terminal [voltages](voltage.md).
