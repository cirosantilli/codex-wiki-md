# Two-box overturning model

↑ **Parent:** [Atlantic meridional overturning circulation](atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md)

A reduced model with warm and cold mixed reservoirs whose signed exchange [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is proportional to their [density](density.md) difference. For fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) contrast and reference-salinity freshwater forcing, the stationary [salt budget](salt-budget.md) is $|q|\Delta S=ES_0$. A linear [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) then gives $q|q|-k\alpha\Delta T|q|+k\beta S_0E=0$. Positive circulation has two branches below a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md); the stronger positive branch and the reversed branch are stable under the usual [salt budget](salt-budget.md) dynamics.
