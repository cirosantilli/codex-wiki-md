# Boundary inclusion of spectra in a closed unital subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Resolvent components in a closed unital subalgebra](resolvent-components-in-a-closed-unital-subalgebra.md)

For a [closed subalgebra of a Banach algebra](closed-subalgebra-of-a-banach-algebra.md) $B\subseteq A$ with common identity, the [algebra resolvent set](resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md) $\rho_B(x)$ is [clopen](clopen-set.md) in $\rho_A(x)$. A point in $\rho_A(x)$ has a small connected disc on which membership of $\rho_B(x)$ is constant, so it cannot lie in $\partial\sigma_B(x)$. Also $\sigma_A(x)\subseteq\sigma_B(x)$; a point in the interior of the former is in the interior of the latter. Together these observations prove the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) inclusion, without assuming a spectral-boundary theorem.
