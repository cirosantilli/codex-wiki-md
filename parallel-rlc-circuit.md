# Parallel RLC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [RLC circuit](rlc-circuit.md)

An ideal [resistor](resistor.md), [inductor](inductor.md) and [capacitor](capacitor.md) connected across the same [voltage](voltage.md) have admittance $Y=1/R+i(\omega C-1/(\omega L))$ in the $e^{i\omega t}$ convention. At $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$ the reactive branch currents cancel, giving minimum source current $V_0/R$ and maximum impedance $R$. This differs from a lossy series coil placed across a [capacitor](capacitor.md).
