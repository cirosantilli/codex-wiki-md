# Acoustic loading noise

↑ **Parent:** [Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings equation](ffowcs-williams-hawkings-equation.md)

Loading noise is the [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md) radiation of the [force](force.md) exerted by a body on its surrounding fluid. For a compact, slowly moving source, the radiating [acoustic density perturbation](acoustic-density-perturbation.md) is $n\cdot\dot{\mathcal F}(t-R/c_0)/(4\pi c_0^3R)$, where $\mathcal F$ is the total [force](force.md). If this total is constant, finite source-delay corrections can still create higher [acoustic multipole sources](acoustic-multipole-source.md).
