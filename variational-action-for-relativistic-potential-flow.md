# Variational action for relativistic potential flow

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

In signature $(+---)$, the matter action $S=\int\sqrt{-g}[p(\epsilon)+n((\nabla\chi)^2-\epsilon^2)/(2\epsilon)]$ uses independent $n,\epsilon,\chi$. Variations of $n,\epsilon,\chi$ give $(\nabla\chi)^2=\epsilon^2$, $p'(\epsilon)=n$, and [particle number conservation in a relativistic fluid](particle-number-conservation-in-a-relativistic-fluid.md) for $u_a=\nabla_a\chi/\epsilon$. Its [Hilbert stress-energy tensor](hilbert-stress-energy-tensor.md) is $T_{ab}=(\rho+p)u_au_b-pg_{ab}$. The action describes isentropic potential flow, with vanishing [canonical vorticity of a relativistic fluid](canonical-vorticity-of-a-relativistic-fluid.md), rather than every vortical perfect-fluid solution.
