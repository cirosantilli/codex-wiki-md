# XXZ spin chain

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md)

The [XXZ spin chain](xxz-spin-chain.md) has equal transverse [exchange interactions](exchange-interaction.md) and a possibly different longitudinal coupling. For $J>0$ the transverse coupling is antiferromagnetic, while the sign and size of $\Delta$ determine the longitudinal preference. A periodic even chain is bipartite. Its [continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md) is rotation about $z$ for generic $\Delta$, enhanced to full [spin](spin.md) rotation at $\Delta=1$.
