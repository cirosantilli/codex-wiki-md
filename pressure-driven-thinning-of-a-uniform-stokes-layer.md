# Pressure-driven thinning of a uniform Stokes layer

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Let a viscous layer occupy $0<y<h(t)$, with no slip at $y=0$, zero tangential traction at $y=h$, and exterior pressure $p_0-\rho_aE^2x^2/2$. The [Cartesian streamfunction](cartesian-streamfunction.md) ansatz $\psi=xf(y)$ reduces the Stokes equations to $f^{(4)}=0$. The resulting surface velocity is

$$
\dot h=-\frac{\rho_aE^2}{3\mu}h^3,
$$

so the positive thinning rate is $-\dot h=\rho_aE^2h^3/(3\mu)$.
