<h1 id="nobili-s-astatic-galvanometer">Nobili's astatic galvanometer</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Astatic galvanometer](astatic-galvanometer.md)

The historical [Leopoldo Nobili](leopoldo-nobili.md) instrument combines a many-turn coil with an astatic needle pair. In a common geometry one needle responds strongly to the coil while its oppositely magnetized companion reduces the terrestrial restoring torque.[Inventory 1417](astatic-galvanometer-nobili-pattern-by-museo-galileo.md)
