# Placebo analgesia

↑ **Parent:** [Placebo effect](placebo-effect.md)

A reduction in [pain](pain.md) associated with a placebo manipulation. Its influence on responses to others' [pain](pain.md) can test shared modulation, without showing that the two experiences have identical [neural representations](neural-representation.md).
