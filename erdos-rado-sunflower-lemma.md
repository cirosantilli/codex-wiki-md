<h1 id="erdos-rado-sunflower-lemma">Erdős–Rado sunflower lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Delta-system](delta-system.md)

A sunflower is a [delta-system](delta-system.md): its pairwise [intersections](set-intersection.md) all equal one core, and its petals outside the core are pairwise disjoint. For an $s$-[uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md), prove the displayed bound by [induction](mathematical-induction.md) on $s$. If a maximal disjoint subfamily has $r$ members, their core is empty. Otherwise its union has at most $s(r-1)$ points and meets every member. Some point belongs to more than $(s-1)!(r-1)^{s-1}$ members. Remove it and apply the induction hypothesis; putting it back enlarges the common core. The case $s=0$ is immediate.
