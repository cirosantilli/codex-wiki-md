# Isotropic second-rank tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

A second-rank Cartesian [tensor](tensor.md) invariant under every proper [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) is a scalar multiple of the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). Half-turns about the coordinate axes kill its off-diagonal entries, and quarter-turns make its diagonal entries equal. A tensor of the form $v_iw_j$ is an [outer product](outer-product.md) with matrix rank at most one, so it is isotropic in three dimensions only when it vanishes, equivalently when $v=0$ or $w=0$.
