# Based rational-map moduli of sigma-model lumps

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-model lump](sigma-model-lump.md)

Fix the value $w(\infty)=0$ for positive-degree [sigma-model lumps](sigma-model-lump.md). Their holomorphic [rational maps](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) can be written $p/q$, where $q$ is monic of degree $k$, $p$ has degree at most $k-1$, and the polynomials have no common zero. The coefficients of $p$ and the nonleading coefficients of $q$ give $2k$ complex parameters; coprimality is an open condition. The [topological degree](topological-degree.md) is $k$, obtained by counting generic preimages with multiplicity. Allowing a common factor lowers the degree after cancellation and can describe a degeneration rather than another smooth point of this degree-$k$ parameter space.
