# Galois group of a finite field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-field Frobenius automorphism](finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md)

Every [finite field extension](finite-field-extension.md) is a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md), and

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q)
=\langle x\mapsto x^q\rangle\cong C_n.
$$
