# Gauge generator

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge group](gauge-group.md)

A [gauge generator](gauge-generator.md) is the representation [matrix](matrix.md) of a basis element of the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of a continuous [gauge group](gauge-group.md) on a specified set of fields. For Hermitian generators $T^a$, an infinitesimal transformation is $\delta\phi=i\alpha^aT^a\phi$, and $[T^a,T^b]=if^{ab}{}_cT^c$. [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) of a [superpotential](superpotential.md) consequently requires $W_i(T^a)^i{}_j\phi^j=0$ for each generator.
