# Magnetic energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md)

In vacuum or an approximately nonmagnetizable plasma, the field [energy](energy-split.md) per volume is $B^2/(2\mu_0)$. [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) has the same scale, but a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) also exerts directional tension, so its stress is not universally an isotropic pressure. Material magnetization can require a different constitutive treatment.
