# Epidemic

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

An [epidemic](epidemic.md) is a period of growing infection incidence in a population. [Compartmental models](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md) describe transmission, recovery and depletion or renewal of susceptible hosts. An [epidemic invasion threshold](epidemic-invasion-threshold.md) concerns growth after a small introduction; a [final size relation for an epidemic](final-size-relation-for-an-epidemic.md) concerns the total susceptible depletion of a closed outbreak. These are distinct from an endemic [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) with continuing transmission.
