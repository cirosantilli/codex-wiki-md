# Chlorophyll retrieval

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean colour](ocean-colour.md)

Inference of [chlorophyll a](chlorophyll-a.md) concentration from measured optical signals. Simple blue/green ratios rely on assumptions about covarying absorbers and scatterers; coastal mixtures can violate those assumptions.
