# Finite-volume inertial spreading in a triangular channel

↑ **Parent:** [Prismatic triangular-channel gravity-current box model](prismatic-triangular-channel-gravity-current-box-model.md)

With constant buoyancy and no settling or entrainment, the box volume is proportional to $h^2\ell$. Thus $h=h_0\sqrt{x_0/\ell}$ for an initial lock length $x_0$. A [gravity-current front condition](gravity-current-front-condition.md) gives $\dot\ell=F\sqrt{g'h_0}(x_0/\ell)^{1/4}$, so $\ell^{5/4}$ increases linearly with time. This is a late-time integral approximation; it does not reproduce the initial rarefaction or the detailed nose.
