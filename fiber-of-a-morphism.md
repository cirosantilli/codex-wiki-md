# Fiber of a morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

For a [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) $f:X\to Y$ and $y\in Y$, the fiber is the inverse-image variety $X_y=f^{-1}(y)$.

For a point $y$, the underlying [fiber](fiber-of-a-function.md) is the inverse image $f^{-1}(y)$.
