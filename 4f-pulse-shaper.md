# 4f pulse shaper

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral pulse shaping](spectral-pulse-shaping.md)

A [4f pulse shaper](4f-pulse-shaper.md) uses a [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) to disperse an input pulse, a lens to focus its frequencies at different positions in a shared Fourier plane, a spectral mask or [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) there to modify them, and a second lens and grating to recombine them. In the symmetric idealization the distances are $f,f,f,f$, with the lenses separated by $2f$. Shaping the spatially resolved spectrum implements [spectral pulse shaping](spectral-pulse-shaping.md) rather than a direct time-domain mask.
