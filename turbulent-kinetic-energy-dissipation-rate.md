# Turbulent kinetic energy dissipation rate

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md)

The positive rate per unit mass at which molecular viscosity removes [turbulent kinetic energy](turbulent-kinetic-energy.md), with $s'_{ij}$ the fluctuating symmetric velocity-gradient tensor. Its dimensions are velocity squared per time. The integral-scale estimate is $\epsilon\sim q^3/L$, expressing transfer over turnover time $L/q$; numerical constants depend on the convention for the turbulent velocity and length scales.
