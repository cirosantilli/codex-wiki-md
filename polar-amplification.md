# Polar amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_amplification)

Polar amplification is greater temperature change at high latitudes than in the global mean. [Ice-albedo feedback](ice-albedo-feedback.md), the vertical structure of atmospheric warming, the temperature dependence of radiative cooling, ocean heat storage, clouds and energy transport all contribute; the balance differs between the Arctic and Antarctic and between seasons.
