# Rigid-lid approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

The rigid-lid approximation suppresses [free surface](free-surface.md) displacement in a fluid layer while retaining a variable pressure that enforces the mass constraint. At constant depth it gives horizontally nondivergent motion. In a one-layer [quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md), removing free-surface stretching eliminates the $-\psi/R_D^2$ term from [shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](shallow-water-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md). This is also the small-horizontal-scale limit compared with the [Rossby deformation radius](rossby-deformation-radius.md), when that term is negligible rather than exactly absent.
