# Reduction modulo one

↑ **Parent:** [Modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md)

[Reduction modulo one](reduction-modulo-one.md) sends a real number to its unique representative in $[0,1)$ by subtracting its integer part. Applied coordinatewise, it represents points of a torus by a unit cube.
