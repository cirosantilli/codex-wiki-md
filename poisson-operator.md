# Poisson operator

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson structure](poisson-structure.md)

For field [functionals](functional.md), a Poisson operator $J$ is an operator whose displayed pairing of [variational derivatives](variational-derivative.md) defines a [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md). It must be skew under the integral pairing and satisfy the [functional](functional.md) [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). A constant skew [differential operator](differential-operator.md), such as $J=\partial_x$ with periodic or vanishing [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), gives the simplest examples. The [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) evolution is $u_t=J(\delta H/\delta u)$.
