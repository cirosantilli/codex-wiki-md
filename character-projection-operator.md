# Character projection operator

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

For a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ represented unitarily by $U(g)$, the central projector onto the isotypic subspace of an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) $\lambda$ is $P_\lambda=(d_\lambda/|G|)\sum_g\chi_\lambda(g)^*U(g)$, where $d_\lambda$ is its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). [Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) implies that the operator is identity on copies of $\lambda$ and zero on other irreducibles. For a one-dimensional irreducible, applying it to an [atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md) gives either zero or a [symmetry-adapted linear combination](symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) of that [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md), to be normalized afterward. A multidimensional central projector selects an entire isotypic component rather than one uniquely chosen component function.
