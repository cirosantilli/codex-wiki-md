# Instability of the Nicholson-Bailey equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Nicholson-Bailey model](nicholson-bailey-model.md)

At the positive [Nicholson-Bailey model](nicholson-bailey-model.md) [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), $\operatorname{tr}J_*=1+\log r/(r-1)$ lies between one and two, and the displayed [determinant](determinant.md) exceeds one. These follow from $(r-1)/r<\log r<r-1$ for $r>1$. The [discriminant](discriminant.md) is therefore negative, and the conjugate [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have [moduli](modulus.md) greater than one. Nearby annual samples spiral outward rather than approach coexistence.
