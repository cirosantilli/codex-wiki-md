<h1 id="stiefel-whitney-obstruction-to-a-diagonal-projective-immersion">Stiefel–Whitney obstruction to a diagonal projective immersion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes](whitney-product-formula-for-stiefel-whitney-classes.md)

For a map $f:\mathbb{RP}^{2k+1}\to\mathbb{CP}^k$ nontrivial on degree-two mod-two [cohomology](cohomology-split.md), an [immersion](immersion.md) in the [homotopy](homotopy.md) class of $(f,f)$ has a rank-$(2k-1)$ [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) with total [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md) $(1+x)^{2k+2}$. Its forbidden degree-$2k$ [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md) is $(k+1)x^{2k}$, so $k$ must be odd.
