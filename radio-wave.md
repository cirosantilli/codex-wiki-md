# Radio wave

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_wave)

A [radio wave](radio-wave.md) is [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) in the radio portion of the [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md). In vacuum its [frequency](frequency.md) and [wavelength](wavelength.md) obey the [vacuum frequency-wavelength relation](vacuum-frequency-wavelength-relation.md).
