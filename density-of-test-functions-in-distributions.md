# Density of test functions in distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md)

Every [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) is a limit of [test functions](test-function.md) in [weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md). With a [mollifier](mollifier.md) $\rho_\varepsilon$ and an expanding [smooth cutoff function](smooth-cutoff-function.md) $\chi(x/j)$, the functions $\chi(x/j)(u*\rho_{1/j})(x)$ have compact support and converge to $u$. For each fixed test, the cutoff eventually equals one on its support, while the reflected mollifier approximates that test with all derivatives in a common compact set.
