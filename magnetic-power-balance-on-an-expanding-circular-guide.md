# Magnetic power balance on an expanding circular guide

↑ **Parent:** [Motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md)

For laboratory [velocity](velocity.md) $v\hat r+w\hat\theta$ and field $B\hat z$, $F_{B,r}=qBw$ and $F_{B,\theta}=-qBv$. Their [powers](power.md) are opposite. The field-dependent guide-reaction [power](power.md) is $-qBvw$; the total ideal radial reaction for bead [mass](mass.md) $m_b$ and constant expansion [speed](speed.md) additionally contributes the ordinary centripetal term $-m_bvw^2/R$.
