# Weyl-invariance exception for the string among branes

↑ **Parent:** [Auxiliary-metric brane action](auxiliary-metric-brane-action.md)

Under $\gamma_{\mu\nu}\mapsto e^{2\omega}\gamma_{\mu\nu}$, the quadratic kinetic density of a $p$-brane scales as $e^{(p-1)\omega}$ and its constant density scales as $e^{(p+1)\omega}$. At $p=1$ the first factor is one and the constant term in the [auxiliary-metric brane action](auxiliary-metric-brane-action.md) vanishes. The traced metric equation is then an identity, leaving a [Weyl transformation](weyl-transformation.md) freedom. For $p\ne1$, the metric equation instead identifies the auxiliary and [induced worldvolume metrics](induced-worldvolume-metric.md).
