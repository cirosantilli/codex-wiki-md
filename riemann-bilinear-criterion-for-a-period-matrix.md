# Riemann bilinear criterion for a period matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann form on a complex torus](riemann-form-on-a-complex-torus.md)

A [complex torus](complex-torus.md) with [period matrix of a complex torus](period-matrix-of-a-complex-torus.md) $\Omega$ admits a [Hodge metric](hodge-metric.md) precisely when there is a nonsingular integral skew-symmetric matrix $Q$ satisfying the displayed conditions. The entries of $Q$ are the periods of the invariant [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) over lattice two-tori. The first condition imposes type $(1,1)$, and the second imposes positivity; the factor $-i$ corresponds to the convention $\omega(v,Jv)>0$.
