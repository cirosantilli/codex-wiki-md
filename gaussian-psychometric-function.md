# Gaussian psychometric function

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometric function](psychometric-function.md)

Let internal evidence be $R=\mu(x)+\varepsilon$ with zero-mean [Gaussian noise](gaussian-noise.md) of [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) $\sigma$, and respond yes when $R>c$. Taking a distribution tail gives $1-\Phi((c-\mu)/\sigma)$, which equals the displayed expression by symmetry. Its derivative is $\phi((\mu-c)/\sigma)\mu'(x)/\sigma$. Thus sensory gain, variability and criterion all influence the observed relation, and a sigmoid in one stimulus coordinate need not remain symmetric in another.
