# Beltrami equation

↑ **Parent:** [Beltrami coefficient](beltrami-coefficient.md)

The equation $f_{\bar z}=\mu f_z$ prescribing the [Beltrami coefficient](beltrami-coefficient.md) of a [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md). Two homeomorphic solutions on the same source differ by a [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) between their targets.
