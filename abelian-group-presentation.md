# Abelian group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian group](abelian-group.md)

An [abelian group presentation](abelian-group-presentation.md) specifies [generators of a group](generator-of-a-group.md) and additive relations. For generators $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, let $L$ be the [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $\mathbb Z^n$ generated by the relation vectors; the presented [abelian group](abelian-group.md) is $\mathbb Z^n/L$. The orders of the images of the generators can be smaller than the orders imposed by individual relations. A relation $de_i=0$ only requires that the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) divide $d$.
