# Primal-dual optimal point

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)

A primal-dual optimal point consists of a feasible solution of an optimization problem and a feasible solution of its [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md) whose objective values agree. Under differentiability and suitable convexity, such points satisfy the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md).
