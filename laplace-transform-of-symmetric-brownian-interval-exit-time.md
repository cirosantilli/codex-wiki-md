# Laplace transform of symmetric Brownian interval-exit time

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian exit from an interval](brownian-exit-from-an-interval.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero and first exit $\tau_x$ from $(-x,x)$, the [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) is $1/\cosh(x\sqrt{2\lambda})$ for $\lambda>0$. The [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) $e^{-\lambda t}\cosh(\sqrt{2\lambda}B_t)$ is the average of two copies of the [Exponential martingale for Brownian motion](exponential-martingale-for-brownian-motion.md). Its values stopped at $t\wedge\tau_x$ are bounded, so bounded-time application of the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) followed by [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) evaluates the transform without assuming [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) of an unstopped exponential [martingale](martingale-split.md) on the infinite horizon.
