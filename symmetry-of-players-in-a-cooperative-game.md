# Symmetry of players in a cooperative game

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

Players $i,j$ are symmetric when interchanging them does not affect any [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) value. The displayed condition is equivalent to invariance under their transposition. If every pair is symmetric, arbitrary permutations preserve the game, and [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) of equal [cardinality](cardinality.md) have equal values. This is a property of the [transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md), rather than a requirement that a chosen [payoff](payoff.md) allocation treat players equally.
