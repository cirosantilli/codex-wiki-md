# Planar quadrangulation

↑ **Parent:** [Planar map](planar-map.md)

A planar quadrangulation is a [planar map](planar-map.md) in which every face has degree four, with incidences counted with multiplicity. A pointed quadrangulation additionally has a distinguished vertex.

A [squaregraph](squaregraph.md) has separate conditions on bounded faces and interior vertex degrees; its outer face need not be quadrilateral.
