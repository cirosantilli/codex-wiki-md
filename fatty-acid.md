# Fatty acid

↑ **Parent:** [Carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)

A [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md) is a [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md) with a [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) chain. Its chain supplies much of the [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) region when incorporated into a [triglyceride](triglyceride.md) or typical [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md). See [Biology 2e: fatty acids](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-3-lipids).
