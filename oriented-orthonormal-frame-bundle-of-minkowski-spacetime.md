# Oriented orthonormal frame bundle of Minkowski spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal frame bundle](orthonormal-frame-bundle.md)

An oriented pseudo-orthonormal frame in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) consists of its base point $a$ and a Lorentz matrix $L$ acting on a fixed reference frame. The pairs $(a,L)$ form the [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md), and right multiplication by $(0,h)$ sends the frame to $(a,Lh)$ while fixing its base point. Thus the displayed projection is a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) with group $SO(3,1)$. The full frame bundle uses $O(3,1)$; a specified time orientation restricts to the corresponding connected component.
