# Gauge pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md)

[Gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md) is [pressure](pressure-split.md) measured relative to the surrounding [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md); absolute [pressure](pressure-split.md) is instead measured relative to an ideal [vacuum](vacuum.md). At an open surface $p_{\mathrm{surface}}=p_{\mathrm{atmosphere}}$, so the constant-[mass density](density.md) [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) law becomes $p_g=\rho_fgh$. Here $p_g=0$ at the surface denotes equality with [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md). It does not denote zero absolute [pressure](pressure-split.md). [OpenStax, University Physics, §14.2: gauge and absolute pressure](university-physics-volume-1-by-william-moebs-and-others.md)
