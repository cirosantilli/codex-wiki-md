# Valuative criterion for universal closedness

↑ **Parent:** [Universally closed morphism](universally-closed-morphism.md)

Among [quasi-compact morphisms](quasi-compact-morphism.md) of [schemes](scheme.md), [universal closedness](universally-closed-morphism.md) is equivalent to the existence part of the [valuative criterion](valuative-criterion.md): for every [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) $D$ with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$, every compatible pair $\operatorname{Spec}K\to X$, $\operatorname{Spec}D\to S$ extends to $\operatorname{Spec}D\to X$. All [valuation rings](valuation-ring.md) must be tested without further hypotheses. A [universally closed morphism](universally-closed-morphism.md) is automatically a [quasi-compact morphism](quasi-compact-morphism.md).
