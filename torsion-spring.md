# Torsion spring

↑ **Parent:** [Spring](spring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsion_spring)

An ideal linear torsion spring exerts a restoring [torque](torque.md) proportional to the relative [angle](angle.md) between its two ends. Integrating the torque gives its [elastic potential energy](elastic-energy.md). A spring connecting two rotating bodies contributes $\kappa(\theta_2-\theta_1)^2/2$ to the [potential energy](potential-energy.md); a spring connected to a fixed frame is obtained by setting one angle to zero. The coefficient $\kappa$ is the [torsional spring constant](torsion-constant.md).
