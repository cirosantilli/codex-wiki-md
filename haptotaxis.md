# Haptotaxis

↑ **Parent:** [Cell migration](cell-migration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haptotaxis)

[Haptotaxis](haptotaxis.md) is directed [cell migration](cell-migration.md) along spatial variation of an immobilized adhesive or guidance cue. It differs from guidance by a soluble [chemoattractant](chemoattractant.md) field.
