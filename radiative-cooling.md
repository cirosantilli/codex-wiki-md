# Radiative cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiative_cooling)

A body loses [internal energy](internal-energy.md) by emitting [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md) faster than it absorbs radiation or receives other heating. In a gravitating gas this can remove pressure support and allow further contraction.
