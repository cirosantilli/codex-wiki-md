# Martingale increments obstruct finite convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

If a real sequence converges finitely, its successive increments tend to zero. A [martingale](martingale-split.md) whose increments stay bounded away from zero on every sample path cannot have a finite almost-sure limit, regardless of its zero conditional mean increments.
