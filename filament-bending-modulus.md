# Filament bending modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Inextensible filament](inextensible-filament.md)

The filament bending modulus multiplies squared [curvature of a space curve](curvature-of-a-space-curve.md) in the bending energy, $E_b=(B/2)\int\kappa^2\,ds$. It has dimensions force times length squared. In the small-slope [Monge representation](monge-representation.md), the leading elastic force per unit length is $-By_{xxxx}$.
