# Symmetric derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)

The symmetric derivative compares values equally far to either side of a point. If the ordinary [derivative](derivative.md) exists, splitting the symmetric [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) into two ordinary [difference quotients](difference-quotient.md) shows $f^s(x)=f'(x)$. The converse fails: the [absolute value function](absolute-value.md) has $f^s(0)=0$ but distinct left and right [one-sided derivatives](one-sided-derivative.md) at zero.
