# Trivial formal deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Formal associative deformation](formal-associative-deformation.md)

A [formal associative deformation](formal-associative-deformation.md) is trivial if a $k[[t]]$-linear [automorphism](automorphism.md) continuous for the [adic topology](adic-topology.md) $T=\operatorname{id}\pmod t$ fixing the identity transports the deformed multiplication to the original one. This is equivalence by a formal change of coordinates, stronger than merely an abstract [algebra isomorphism](algebra-isomorphism.md) between unspecified middle terms.
