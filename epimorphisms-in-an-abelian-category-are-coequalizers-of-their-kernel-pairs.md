# Epimorphisms in an abelian category are coequalizers of their kernel pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel pair](kernel-pair.md)

Every [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) in an [abelian category](abelian-category.md) is the [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of its [kernel pair](kernel-pair.md). Its kernel-pair square is a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) along an epimorphism and therefore also a pushout. A map equalizing the two projections supplies the same map on both pushout legs and so factors uniquely through the original epimorphism. In particular, all such epimorphisms are [regular epimorphisms](regular-epimorphism.md).
