# Fermi-edge heat-capacity counting

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic heat capacity coefficient](electronic-heat-capacity-coefficient.md)

Only single-particle states within order $k_BT$ of the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) can change occupation at low [temperature](temperature-split.md). Their number is order $g(E_F)k_BT$, and their excitation [energy](energy-split.md) is order $k_BT$, giving $U-U_0\propto T^2$ and electronic [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) proportional to $T$.
