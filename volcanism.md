# Volcanism

↑ **Parent:** [Solid Earth](solid-earth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanism)

Transport and eruption or intrusion of molten rock and associated gases. [Subduction](subduction.md), spreading and mantle upwellings can supply melt; volcanism does not require globally active [plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md). Outgassing couples interior evolution to atmospheric inventories.
