# Airy global modes of a linearly confined Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

On $x>0$ with a homogeneous wall condition, positive [diffusion](diffusion.md) $\gamma$ and growth $\mu(x)=\mu_0-\epsilon\lambda x$, remove constant drift by $A=e^{Ux/(2\gamma)-i\omega t}f$. The scale $\xi=(\epsilon\lambda/\gamma)^{1/3}x$ reduces the [eigenvalue problem](eigenvalue-problem.md) to the [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md). Decay selects $f=\operatorname{Ai}(\xi+z_n)$, where $\operatorname{Ai}(z_n)=0$ enforces the wall. The least negative zero $z_1$ sets the [global instability](global-hydrodynamic-instability.md) threshold $\mu_0>U^2/(4\gamma)+|z_1|(\epsilon^2\gamma\lambda^2)^{1/3}$. A locally absolutely unstable pocket can therefore exist without an unstable [global mode](global-hydrodynamic-mode.md).
