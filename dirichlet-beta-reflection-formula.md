# Dirichlet beta reflection formula

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet beta function](dirichlet-beta-function.md)

The functional equation may be written

$$
\beta(1-s)=\Gamma(s)(\pi/2)^{-s}\sin(\pi s/2)\beta(s).
$$
