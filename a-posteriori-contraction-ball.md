# A posteriori contraction ball

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md)

Let $A$ be differentiable on a neighbourhood of a closed radius-$r$ ball in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), with $\|DA\|\leq\kappa<1$ on that ball. If $\|A(p_0)-p_0\|\leq(1-\kappa)r$, the mean-value estimate gives $A(B)\subseteq B$ and $\|A(p)-A(q)\|\leq\kappa\|p-q\|$. The [contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md) supplies exactly one [fixed point](fixed-point.md) in $B$, and its distance from $p_0$ is at most $\|A(p_0)-p_0\|/(1-\kappa)$.
