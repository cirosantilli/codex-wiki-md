# Two-affine cover of a smooth projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md)

A nonconstant rational function on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) extends to a [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md) to the [projective line](projective-line.md). The inverse images of its two standard affine charts are [affine varieties](affine-algebraic-set.md) covering the curve. The resulting [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md) has only degrees zero and one, so every [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) has zero [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) in degrees at least two.
