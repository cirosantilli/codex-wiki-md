# Homojunction

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homojunction)

A [homojunction](homojunction.md) joins regions of the same [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) material with different [semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md). Its bulk [band gap](band-gap.md) is the same on both sides. This distinguishes the electrostatic [band bending](band-bending.md) of a simple equilibrium [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) from the material-dependent [band offsets](band-offset.md) possible in a [heterojunction](heterojunction.md).
