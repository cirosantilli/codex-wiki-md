# Modular-intersection graph Ramsey lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

Let $p$ be prime and join two $p^2$-subsets of a $p^3$-element set when their intersection size is divisible by $p$. The [modular intersection bound for a set family](modular-intersection-bound-for-a-set-family.md) bounds both its independence and clique numbers by a quantity strictly below $p^{3p}$. Since the graph has $\binom{p^3}{p^2}\geq p^{p^2}$ vertices,

$$
R(p^{3p},p^{3p})\geq p^{p^2},
$$

a lower bound larger than every fixed power of $p^{3p}$ as $p\to\infty$.
