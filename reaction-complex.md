# Reaction complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction network](chemical-reaction-network.md)

A formal combination of [chemical species](chemical-species.md) appearing on one side of a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md). The coefficients $y_i$ are nonnegative integers. A [chemical reaction network](chemical-reaction-network.md) can be viewed as a directed [graph](graph-split.md) whose [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are reaction complexes and whose [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md). The empty complex represents external production or removal.
