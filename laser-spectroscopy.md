# Laser spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic measurement methods](atomic-measurement-methods.md)

[Laser spectroscopy](laser-spectroscopy.md) uses a tunable [laser](laser-split.md) to interrogate [energy levels](energy-level.md) through [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md), [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) or [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md). A narrow optical source is useful only if the sample and readout preserve the information: [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md) can hide closely spaced [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) even when the source itself is narrow.
