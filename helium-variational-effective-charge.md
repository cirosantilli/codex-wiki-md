# Helium variational effective charge

↑ **Parent:** [Variational method](variational-method.md)

A product of two normalized $1s$ orbitals proportional to $e^{-\zeta r}$, combined with a [singlet state](singlet-state.md), gives the fixed-nucleus [helium atom](helium-atom.md) trial [energy](energy-split.md) $E(\zeta)=\zeta^2-4\zeta+5\zeta/8$ in [atomic units](atomic-units.md). Minimization gives $\zeta=27/16$ and $E=-729/256$ Hartree. Nuclear charge remains two in the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md); varying $\zeta$ changes only the trial orbitals. The reduced orbital charge describes screening, while explicit dependence on [electron](electron.md) separation can further lower the trial [energy](energy-split.md) under the [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md).
