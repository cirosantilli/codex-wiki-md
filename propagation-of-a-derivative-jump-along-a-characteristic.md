# Propagation of a derivative jump along a characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Transport equation](transport-equation-split.md)

Suppose $D=a\partial_x+b\partial_y$, $DA=f$, $Dq=0$, and $A,q$ are [smooth functions](smooth-function.md). Then $u=A+g(q)$ solves $Du=f$ wherever $g$ is differentiable. If $g$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md) and piecewise continuously differentiable with a jump in $g'$ at $q=q_0$, the one-sided [gradients](gradient.md) of $u$ differ by $[g']\nabla q$ across that [characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md). Thus a nonzero derivative jump in the data persists along the characteristic unless the relevant component of $\nabla q$ vanishes. A locally [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) $g$ also gives a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) across the curve.
