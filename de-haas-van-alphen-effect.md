# de Haas-van Alphen effect

↑ **Parent:** [Landau level](landau-level.md)

The [de Haas-van Alphen effect](de-haas-van-alphen-effect.md) is an oscillation of a metal's [magnetization](magnetization.md) as changing magnetic field moves [Landau levels](landau-level.md) through the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). The associated [Onsager quantization](onsager-quantization.md) gives inverse-field period $\Delta(1/B)=2\pi e/(\hbar S_F)$ for an extremal Fermi-surface cross-section $S_F$. Oscillatory resistance is instead the [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md).
