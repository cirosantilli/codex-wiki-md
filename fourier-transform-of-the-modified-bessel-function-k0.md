# Fourier transform of the modified Bessel function K0

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md)

The [integral representation of the modified Bessel function of the second kind](integral-representation-of-the-modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md) gives $K_0(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{-x\cosh t}\,dt$ for $x>0$. Taking the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the even continuation and setting $u=\sinh t$ gives $2\int_0^\infty(1+k^2+u^2)^{-1}du=\pi/\sqrt{1+k^2}$. The large-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) terms $\pi/|k|-\pi/(2|k|^3)$ are also obtained from the local [logarithmic singularity](logarithmic-singularity.md) $-\log|x|-(x^2/4)\log|x|$ using the [Fourier transform of the absolute logarithm](fourier-transform-of-the-absolute-logarithm.md).
