# Allowable singular simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Perversity](perversity.md)

An $i$-dimensional [singular simplex](singular-simplex.md) $\sigma:\Delta^i\to X$ is $\bar p$-allowable if $\sigma^{-1}(X_{N-k})$ is contained in the $(i-k+\bar p(k))$-skeleton of $\Delta^i$ for every $k\ge2$. A negative skeleton is empty. Individual faces need not be allowable; this is why the definition of [intersection chains](intersection-chains.md) separately imposes allowability on the total boundary.
