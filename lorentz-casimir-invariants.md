# Lorentz Casimir invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral decomposition of the complex Lorentz algebra](chiral-decomposition-of-the-complex-lorentz-algebra.md)

The quadratic contraction $M_{\mu\nu}M^{\mu\nu}$ is parity even, while $M_{\mu\nu}\widetilde M^{\mu\nu}$ is parity odd. Their linear combinations give the quadratic Casimirs of the two chiral $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ factors.
