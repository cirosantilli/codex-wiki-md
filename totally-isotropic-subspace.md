# Totally isotropic subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic quadratic form](isotropic-quadratic-form.md)

A subspace $E$ is totally isotropic for $B$ when $B(u,v)=0$ for all $u,v\in E$. For a real [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) of inertia $(p,q)$, its dimension is at most $\min(p,q)$.
