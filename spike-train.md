# Spike train

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A sequence of [action potential](action-potential.md) times, represented mathematically as a point process or a sum of [Dirac deltas](dirac-delta-function.md). Its event counts, intervals and correlations summarize different aspects of neural activity.
