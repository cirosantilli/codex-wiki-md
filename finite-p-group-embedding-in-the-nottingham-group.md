# Finite p-group embedding in the Nottingham group

↑ **Parent:** [Nottingham group](nottingham-group.md)

Realize a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) $P$ as the [Galois group](galois-group.md) of a [totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md) $E/\mathbb F_p((t))$, using [Artin-Schreier embedding problems over a characteristic-p field](artin-schreier-embedding-problems-over-a-characteristic-p-field.md). A [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $u$ of $E$ gives $E\cong\mathbb F_p((u))$: every element has a unique convergent Laurent expansion with coefficients in the [residue field](residue-field.md). An automorphism has $u\mapsto a_1u+a_2u^2+\cdots$, and $a_1$ defines a [homomorphism](homomorphism.md) $P\to\mathbb F_p^\times$. Its image is trivial because $|P|$ is a $p$-power and $p\nmid|\mathbb F_p^\times|$. Thus the faithful Galois action lies in the [Nottingham group](nottingham-group.md), embedding $P$.
