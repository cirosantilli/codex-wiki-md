# Tricritical heat-capacity amplitude in a sextic Landau expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field tricritical exponent calculation](mean-field-tricritical-exponent-calculation.md)

For $F=F_0+b(T-T_c)M^2+d'M^6$ with $b,d'>0$, the ordered minimum has $M^4=b(T_c-T)/(3d')$. Its singular [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is $-2b^{3/2}(T_c-T)^{3/2}/\sqrt{27d'}$. Twice differentiating in [temperature](temperature-split.md) gives the displayed ordered-side [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) singularity, with mean-field [critical exponent](critical-exponent.md) $\alpha=1/2$. This path keeps the quartic coefficient tuned to zero; a generic path with a positive quartic term instead crosses over to the ordinary quartic result.
