# Deviatoric strain

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md)

The [deviatoric strain](deviatoric-strain.md) is the trace-free part of the [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md). It removes the isotropic volume change. The decomposition is [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) for the [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md), because a trace-free [matrix](matrix.md) has zero [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md) with $I$. Consequently

$$
|\varepsilon|_F^2=|\operatorname{dev}\varepsilon|_F^2+\frac1d(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)^2.
$$

For $\varepsilon=\tfrac12(\nabla v+(\nabla v)^{\mathsf T})$, its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $\operatorname{div}v$.
