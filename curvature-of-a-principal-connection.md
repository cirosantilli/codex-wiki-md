# Curvature of a principal connection

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature form](curvature-form.md)

This is the principal-bundle version of the [curvature form](curvature-form.md). The curvature of a [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md) is the horizontal equivariant two-form

$$
\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\frac12[\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A].
$$

For horizontal vector fields $X,Y$, it satisfies $\mathcal F(X,Y)=-\mathcal A([X,Y])$.
