# Distance splitting through a small geodesic sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md)

In a connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), choose a small compact [geodesic sphere](geodesic-sphere.md) around $p$ with $0<r<d(p,q)$. Minimize $d(\cdot,q)$ on this sphere. Every path from $p$ to $q$ crosses it, so an almost-minimizing path has length at least $r+\min_{S_r(p)}d(\cdot,q)$. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives the reverse bound, yielding the displayed equality. No global completeness is needed.
