# Homomorphism from a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md)

For any [group](group-split.md) $G$, evaluation at a chosen [generator of a group](generator-of-a-group.md) $x$ of $C_n$ gives a bijection between [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) $C_n\to G$ and elements satisfying $g^n=1$. A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) must send $x^j$ to $g^j$. Conversely, this formula is well-defined because exponents differing by a multiple of $n$ give equal powers, and it respects multiplication. The displayed correspondence is a bijection of sets; it is not claimed to be a group isomorphism for a nonabelian target. In a [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md), the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) is the [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md) of its disjoint cycle lengths, making this correspondence easy to enumerate.
