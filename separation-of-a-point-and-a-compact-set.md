# Separation of a point and a compact set

↑ **Parent:** [Compact subset of a Hausdorff space](compact-subset-of-a-hausdorff-space.md)

In a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), a point $x$ outside a [compact subset](compact-space.md) $K$ can be separated from all of $K$ by [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) [open sets](open-set.md). Choose [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](open-neighbourhood.md) $U_k$ of $k$ and $V_k$ of $x$ for each $k\in K$. Finitely many $U_k$ cover $K$ by [compactness](compact-space.md). Their [union](set-union.md) and the matching finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) of the $V_k$ give the displayed separation. If $K$ is empty, choose $U=\varnothing$, $V=X$. Thus $X\setminus K$ is [open](open-set.md), proving that $K$ is [closed](closed-set.md).
