# Annealed disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)

[Annealed disorder](annealed-disorder.md) allows the variables representing disorder to equilibrate along with the [spins](spin.md). With a specified normalized prior measure over couplings, its [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is the average $\mathbb E_J Z_J$. This differs from preparing samples with fixed couplings and averaging their measured thermodynamics, as in [quenched disorder](quenched-disorder.md).[§4.2, pp. 83–86, fixed disorder versus annealed partition-function averaging](out-of-equilibrium-dynamics-of-complex-systems-by-leticia-fernanda-cugliandolo.md)
