# Curve generation criterion for an abelian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md)

A smooth projective curve $C\subset A$ generates $A$ by its point differences if and only if it meets every prime [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md). If a prime [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md) avoids $C$, its restriction to $C$ has degree zero; [constancy of line bundle degree in a family](constancy-of-line-bundle-degree-in-a-family.md) shows that every translate either contains $C$ or misses it. This forces invariance under $C-C$ and puts the generated subgroup inside the [line bundle translation stabilizer](line-bundle-translation-stabilizer.md). If this subgroup were all of $A$, the divisor would be algebraically trivial, contradicting its positive intersection with an [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md). Conversely a proper closed generated subgroup admits a disjoint divisor by the [pole divisor avoiding a fiber](pole-divisor-avoiding-a-fiber.md) construction, after translating the fiber to contain $C$.
