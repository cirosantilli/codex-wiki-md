# Global warming potential

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse gas](greenhouse-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_warming_potential)

Global warming potential compares the time-integrated radiative forcing of equal-mass emission pulses of a [greenhouse gas](greenhouse-gas.md) and [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), over a stated time horizon $H$. It is not an instantaneous temperature ratio, and its numerical value depends on the horizon and included chemical and climatic responses.
