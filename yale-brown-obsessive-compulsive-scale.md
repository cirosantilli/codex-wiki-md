# Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A clinician-rated measure of obsessive-compulsive symptom severity used to monitor outcomes. Its interpretation depends on assessment and the trial's specified response or remission criterion; a symptom score is not identical to functioning or quality of life.
