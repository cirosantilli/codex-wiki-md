# Vertically integrated wave-drag acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Localized wave-drag response in a stratified channel](localized-wave-drag-response-in-a-stratified-channel.md)

In the [localized wave-drag response in a stratified channel](localized-wave-drag-response-in-a-stratified-channel.md), the full vertical integral of zonal acceleration equals the deposited wave momentum, $-G_0\sin(\pi y/L)$. The fraction inside the directly forced layer is $1-(1-e^{-2s})/(2s)$, where $s=\pi ND/(|f_0|L)$. A small $s$ spreads most acceleration outside the dissipation layer; a large $s$ confines most of it inside.
