# Polarization preparation and dephasing experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A 2018 photonic experiment makes the distinction between populations and [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) controllable. A 400-nm [laser](laser-split.md) pumped a crystal to generate paired [photons](photon.md), filtered near 800 nm. One photon triggered collection; the other entered the apparatus. A [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) and [polarizing beam splitter](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) selected horizontal [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md); another [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) prepared balanced $H,V$ [quantum superpositions](quantum-superposition.md). Separated polarization paths and [diffraction gratings](diffraction-grating.md) let a [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) control their frequency-dependent [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) and spectral weights. Recombined paths passed through [quartz](quartz.md) plates whose adjustable thickness controlled polarization–frequency coupling. Quarter- and [half-wave plates](half-wave-plate.md) plus a [polarizing beam splitter](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) selected tomography bases; jointly registered [photodetector](photodetector.md) events supplied the readout. [Quantum state tomography](quantum-state-tomography.md) inferred polarization [density matrices](density-matrix-split.md) at each thickness. The [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) model preserves diagonal populations while changing off-diagonal [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md). Here the unobserved photon [frequency](frequency.md) acts as the environment for the polarization [qubit](qubit.md). Finite modulator resolution and imperfect spectral shaping limited agreement with the intended dynamics; this experiment does not establish exact $I/2$ preparation or the ideal probabilities above. [Experimental implementation of fully controlled dephasing dynamics and synthetic spectral densities, Nature Communications 9, 3453 (2018), Results: Experimental setup, Fig. 1, equations (1)–(5); Using a photon to simulate qubit coupled to Ising chain, systematic-error discussion after Fig. 3](experimental-implementation-of-fully-controlled-dephasing-dynamics-and-synthetic-spectral-densities-by-zhao-di-liu-and-others.md)
