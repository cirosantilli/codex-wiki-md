# Taq polymerase

↑ **Parent:** [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taq_polymerase)

[Taq polymerase](taq-polymerase.md) is a heat-resistant [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md) used in [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md). It requires a paired strand end and [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) substrates; heat resistance does not confer target recognition.
