# Principle of univariance

↑ **Parent:** [Photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md)

A receptor's absorption-dependent response does not independently label the [wavelength](wavelength.md) of an absorbed [photon](photon.md). Different combinations of spectral composition and [intensity](intensity-physics.md) can yield the same [receptor quantum catch](receptor-quantum-catch.md). Comparing spectrally distinct receptor classes can supply information unavailable from one alone. In the displayed model, $N$ is a [photon](photon.md) distribution and $a$ effective absorptance; an energy spectrum would require the corresponding photon-energy conversion.
