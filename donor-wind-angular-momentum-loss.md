# Donor-wind angular-momentum loss

↑ **Parent:** [Nonconservative binary mass transfer](nonconservative-binary-mass-transfer.md)

An isotropic escaping [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) has the [donor star](donor-star.md)'s mean specific orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). With $\dot M_d<0$, retained fraction $f$, negligible spin and no additional wind torque, $\dot J=(1-f)\dot M_da_d^2\Omega$. The retained matter redistributes [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) internally. Unlike [isotropic re-emission from a binary star](isotropic-re-emission-from-a-binary-star.md), the escaping material originates at the donor rather than the accretor. With no retention this reduces to [Jeans-mode mass loss](jeans-mode-mass-loss.md).
