# Periodic points of the doubling map

↑ **Parent:** [Dyadic transformation](dyadic-transformation.md)

The fixed points of $F^n$ are

$$
x=\frac{j}{2^n-1},
\qquad
j=0,\ldots,2^n-2.
$$

Thus $F^n$ has $2^n-1$ fixed points.
