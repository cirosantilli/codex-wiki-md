# Rational canonical form

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_canonical_form)

The rational canonical form of a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over a [field](field.md) is a direct sum $C(f_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus C(f_r)$ of [companion matrices](companion-matrix.md), where the monic nonconstant [invariant factors of a linear operator](invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md) satisfy $f_1\mid\cdots\mid f_r$. Their degrees sum to the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the space. It exists over any [field](field.md) and classifies [similar matrices](matrix-similarity.md), without requiring their [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) to lie in that field. It comes from the [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md), applied to the [module](module-mathematics.md) in which the indeterminate acts as the given operator.
