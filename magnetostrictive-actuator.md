# Magnetostrictive actuator

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md)

A [magnetostrictive actuator](magnetostrictive-actuator.md) uses [magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md) to produce displacement or [force](force-split.md). Its free motion and its blocked [stress](stress.md) are different boundary conditions, as shown in [free strain and blocked magnetostrictive stress](free-strain-and-blocked-magnetostrictive-stress.md).
