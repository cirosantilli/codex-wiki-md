# Pseudo-isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension](iwasawa-algebra-of-a-zp-extension.md)

A homomorphism between finitely generated one-variable [Iwasawa modules](iwasawa-module.md) is a pseudo-isomorphism if its kernel and cokernel are finite. Finite errors do not change the [Iwasawa-module rank](iwasawa-module-rank.md) or the [characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md) of a torsion module. They can still change its integral structure, so a [pseudo-isomorphism](pseudo-isomorphism.md) is weaker than an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
