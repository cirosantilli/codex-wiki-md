# Earthquake centroid

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

An [earthquake centroid](earthquake-centroid.md) is an effective average location of [seismic moment](seismic-moment.md) release. It differs from the [hypocenter](hypocenter.md), where rupture starts, and can depend on the source representation and observations. An associated [centroid](centroid.md) time similarly averages release rather than marking its first instant.
