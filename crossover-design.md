# Crossover design

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md)

A [crossover design](crossover-design.md) assigns each subject a sequence of [treatments](treatment.md) in successive periods. Subject blocking can reduce between-subject variation; balanced sequences can separate treatment from period effects. A [carryover effect](carryover-effect.md) or irreversible change can invalidate a simple within-subject comparison.
