# Holding force on a contracting nozzle

↑ **Parent:** [Integral momentum equation](integral-momentum-equation.md)

For steady ideal flow through a horizontal contracting nozzle, [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) gives inlet gauge pressure $p_0=\rho Q^2(A_1^{-2}-A_0^{-2})/2$. The [integral momentum equation](integral-momentum-equation.md) gives the fluid's force on the nozzle as $p_0A_0-\rho Q^2(A_1^{-1}-A_0^{-1})$ in the outlet direction. Simplification yields the displayed nonnegative magnitude. The external holding force has equal magnitude and the opposite direction.
