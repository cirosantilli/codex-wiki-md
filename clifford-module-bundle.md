# Clifford module bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A Clifford module bundle is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) over a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with a fibrewise [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md). A compatible [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) satisfies $[\nabla_X,c(\alpha)]=c(\nabla_X\alpha)$ for the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) on covectors. On a graded Clifford module bundle, [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md) exchanges the two grading summands. The associated [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md) is $D=\sum_i c(e^i)\nabla_{e_i}$. Its fibres are modules over the fibres of the [Clifford algebra bundle](clifford-algebra-bundle.md).
