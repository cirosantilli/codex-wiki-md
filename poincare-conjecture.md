<h1 id="poincare-conjecture">Poincaré conjecture</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_conjecture)

A closed [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is homeomorphic to the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md). Now a theorem, this identifies every [homotopy](homotopy.md) [three-sphere](three-sphere.md) with the standard sphere and removes homotopy-sphere ambiguities in three-dimensional connected-sum classification. Smooth structures are also unique in dimension three.
