# XENON100

↑ **Parent:** [Dual-phase xenon detector](dual-phase-xenon-detector.md)

An underground liquid-xenon [dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md) experiment at Gran Sasso. Its two-phase [time projection chamber](time-projection-chamber.md) combined position reconstruction with light-to-charge discrimination. The 2011–2012 search used a $34\ \mathrm{kg}$ inner target for $224.6$ live days and found candidate counts compatible with background.[Abstract](dark-matter-results-from-225-live-days-of-xenon100-data-by-the-xenon100-collaboration.md)
