# Mixed-boundary drift-diffusion eigenmodes on a rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet gauge transform for constant drift](dirichlet-gauge-transform-for-constant-drift.md)

For $u_t=u_{xx}+u_{yy}+\beta u_x$ on $(0,L_x)\times(0,L_y)$, impose zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on the two vertical sides and the top, and a zero [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) at the bottom. The [Dirichlet gauge transform for constant drift](dirichlet-gauge-transform-for-constant-drift.md) $u=e^{-\beta x/2}v$ gives $v_t=\Delta v-\beta^2v/4$. [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) produces the displayed modes, with $m,n\geq1$ and $\lambda_{mn}=(m\pi/L_x)^2+((n-1/2)\pi/L_y)^2+\beta^2/4$. Their spatial parts are orthogonal with weight $e^{\beta x}$; equivalently, expand $e^{\beta x/2}u_0$ in the ordinary sine-cosine basis.
