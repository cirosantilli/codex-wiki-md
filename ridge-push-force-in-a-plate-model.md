# Ridge-push force in a plate model

↑ **Parent:** [Ridge push](ridge-push.md)

The [ridge-push force in a plate model](ridge-push-force-in-a-plate-model.md) is the depth [integral](integral-split.md) of the horizontal [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference between hot and cold compensated columns. To leading order, the old plate's linear excess [density](density.md) gives $F=g\int_0^a s\delta\rho(s)\,ds=\rho_mg\alpha\Delta T a^2/6$. Here $s$ is depth below the old seafloor. Retaining the geometrical contribution from its lowered surface adds $g(\rho_m-\rho_w)h^2/2$ within the linear-density model. [Force](force-split.md) per ridge length has units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$, distinct from [stress](stress.md).
