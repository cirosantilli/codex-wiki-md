# Magnetic reconnection

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive magnetohydrodynamics](resistive-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Magnetic reconnection changes connectivity between [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md). It requires departure from smooth [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md), for example through finite resistivity or other nonideal terms concentrated in a [current sheet](current-sheet.md). Ideal viscous [magnetic relaxation](magnetic-relaxation.md) dissipates mechanical energy while retaining frozen connectivity, so viscosity alone does not provide reconnection.
