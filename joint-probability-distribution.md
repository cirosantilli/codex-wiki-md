# Joint probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_probability_distribution)

The joint probability distribution of [random variables](random-variable-split.md) $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of the [random vector](random-vector.md) $(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$. It records both the marginal distributions and their dependence.
