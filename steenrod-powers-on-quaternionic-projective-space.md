# Steenrod powers on quaternionic projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Steenrod reduced power](steenrod-reduced-power.md)

Choose the degree-four generator with pullback $u\mapsto x^2$ under the [complex inclusion into quaternionic projective space](complex-inclusion-into-quaternionic-projective-space.md). On infinite complex projective space, the [Cartan formula](cartan-formula-algebraic-topology.md) gives $P^i(x^{2k})=\binom{2k}{i}x^{2k+i(p-1)}$. Injectivity of the infinite-space pullback proves the displayed formula for [quaternionic projective space](quaternionic-projective-space.md). Restriction to $\mathbb{HP}^n$ sets powers above $n$ to zero. Coefficients are reduced modulo the odd prime $p$; the exponent is an integer because $p-1$ is even.
