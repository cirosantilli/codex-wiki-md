# Tidal deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal locking](tidal-locking.md)

[Tidal deformation](tidal-deformation.md) is the change in a body's shape caused by differential [gravity](gravity.md) across it, that is, by a [tidal force](tidal-force.md). The familiar two-bulge picture represents an elongated response along the companion direction, rather than a separate force pulling only the nearest surface. If spin and orbital motion differ, the forcing sweeps through the body's material. With [tidal dissipation](tidal-dissipation.md), the response is out of alignment with the instantaneous forcing, allowing a net [torque](torque.md). [Tracy Vogel, NASA: deformation and tidal locking](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/tidal-locking/).
