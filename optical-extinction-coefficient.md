# Optical extinction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Complex refractive index](complex-refractive-index.md)

The imaginary part $\kappa$ of the [complex refractive index](complex-refractive-index.md) makes wave amplitude decay as $e^{-\omega\kappa z/c}$. Since intensity is proportional to amplitude squared, its [absorption coefficient](absorption-coefficient.md) is $\alpha_{\rm abs}=2\omega\kappa/c$.
