# Booleanization of a locale

↑ **Parent:** [Sublocale](sublocale.md)

The double-negation [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) gives a [dense sublocale](dense-sublocale.md) whose [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) is a [complete Boolean algebra](complete-boolean-algebra.md). Its elements are [regular open elements](regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md), with Boolean joins $\neg\neg\bigvee_i a_i$.
