# Aharonov-Bergmann-Lebowitz rule

↑ **Parent:** [Postselection](postselection.md)

For an ideal [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) with the [Lüders rule](luders-rule.md), condition on both an initial [quantum state preparation](quantum-state-preparation.md) and a final successful [postselection](postselection.md). Its outcome probabilities are proportional to $|\langle b|P_j|a\rangle|^2$, where $a$ is the forward-evolved initial state and $b$ the backward-evolved final state. The normalization must be nonzero. The expression is symmetric in these two boundary states, although its derivation uses the ordinary [Born rule](born-rule.md) and conditional state update.
