# Full-line Gaussian moment recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian moment recurrence](gaussian-moment-recurrence.md)

For integer $n\geq2$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $J_n=(n-1)J_{n-2}/2$. The boundary term $-x^{n-1}e^{-x^2}/2$ vanishes at both infinities because [Gaussian decay dominates every power](gaussian-decay-dominates-every-power.md). The full-line initial values are $J_0=\sqrt\pi$ and $J_1=0$, the latter by odd symmetry. Hence

$$
J_{2m}=\frac{(2m-1)!!}{2^m}\sqrt\pi,\qquad J_{2m+1}=0.
$$

Unlike the half-line [Gaussian moment recurrence](gaussian-moment-recurrence.md), full-line odd moments vanish and even moments are twice their half-line values. The [domain of integration](domain-of-integration.md) must be retained when transferring a recurrence between the two settings.
