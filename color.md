# Color

↑ **Parent:** [Color vision](color-vision.md)

The perceived [color](color.md) of a transmitting sample depends on the illuminating spectrum and the wavelength-dependent transmission. Removing a blue band can make a sample appear yellow; absorption maximum alone does not determine a unique visual shade.
