# Generalized character separating complementary prime elements

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer's characterization of characters](brauer-s-characterization-of-characters.md)

Suppose every nonidentity element of a finite group $K$ is either a [pi-element](pi-element.md) or an element for complementary primes. An [elementary subgroup](elementary-group.md) cannot have nontrivial factors of both kinds, since the product of nonidentity commuting coprime-order elements would have mixed prime order. Choose an integer $d$ congruent to $1$ modulo $|K|_\pi$ and to $0$ modulo $|K|_{\pi'}$. Set $f(1)=d$, $f=1$ on nonidentity pi-elements and $f=0$ on the other nonidentity elements. On each elementary subgroup this is an integer combination of the trivial and regular characters. [Brauer's characterization of characters](brauer-s-characterization-of-characters.md) supplies the asserted [generalized character](virtual-character.md).
