# Eutectic temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Eutectic system](eutectic-system.md)

The eutectic temperature is the temperature of simultaneous liquid–two-solid [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) in an [eutectic system](eutectic-system.md). In directional freezing of a solution away from the eutectic composition, a [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) can end at this temperature, where its remaining liquid solidifies.
