# Potassium-40

↑ **Parent:** [Potassium](potassium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potassium-40)

[Potassium-40](potassium-40.md) is a long-lived radioactive [isotope](isotope.md) with 19 [protons](proton.md) and 21 [neutrons](neutron-split.md). It can decay to [calcium-40](calcium-40.md) through [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md), or to [argon-40](argon-40.md) through [electron capture](electron-capture.md) and a rare [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md). The argon-producing branch supports [potassium-argon dating](potassium-argon-dating.md).
