# Differential entropy of a Gaussian law

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

For a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) with [mean](expected-value.md) $m$ and positive [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, expand the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md):

$$
-\ln f(X)=\frac12\ln(2\pi\sigma^2)+\frac{(X-m)^2}{2\sigma^2}.
$$

The second term has [expected value](expected-value.md) $1/2$, hence $h_e(X)=\tfrac12\ln(2\pi e\sigma^2)$. Translation of the [mean](expected-value.md) has no effect, while shrinking the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) lowers [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md). A [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) has approximately $1.41894$ [nats](nat-unit.md). This connects the continuous spread calculation with [entropy power](entropy-power.md), whose normalization matches a [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md)'s [variance](variance-split.md).
