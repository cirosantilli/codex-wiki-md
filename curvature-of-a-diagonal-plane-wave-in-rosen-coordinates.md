# Curvature of a diagonal plane wave in Rosen coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Rosen coordinates for a plane gravitational wave](rosen-coordinates-for-a-plane-gravitational-wave.md)

For $g=-dt^2+A(u)^2dx^2+B(u)^2dy^2+dz^2$, $u=t-z$, with nowhere-zero smooth $A,B$, take $e^0=dt$, $e^1=A\,dx$, $e^2=B\,dy$, $e^3=dz$, $\ell=du$. In signature $(-,+,+,+)$, [Cartan's first structure equation](cartan-s-first-structure-equation.md) and [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) give the lower-index [connection 1-forms](connection-1-form-split.md) $\omega_{01}=\omega_{13}=-(A'/A)e^1$ and $\omega_{02}=\omega_{23}=-(B'/B)e^2$. [Cartan's second structure equation](cartan-s-second-structure-equation.md) gives $\Theta_{01}=\Theta_{13}=-(A''/A)\ell\wedge e^1$ and $\Theta_{02}=\Theta_{23}=-(B''/B)\ell\wedge e^2$. All other independent [curvature 2-forms](curvature-2-form.md) vanish: in particular the $12$ component cancels between the time and longitudinal terms. Contracting $R^a{}_{bad}$ yields $\operatorname{Ric}=-(A''/A+B''/B)du\otimes du$. Thus the [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md) are $A''/A+B''/B=0$, while nonzero $A''/A=-B''/B$ gives opposite transverse tidal curvatures and [plus polarization](plus-polarization.md) relative to these axes.
