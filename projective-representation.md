# Projective representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_representation)

A [projective representation](projective-representation.md) is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) into the projective linear group of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Choosing invertible linear representatives makes multiplication hold up to nonzero scalar factors. A [projective unitary representation](projective-unitary-representation.md) chooses [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) as representatives, whose scalar factors have modulus one.
