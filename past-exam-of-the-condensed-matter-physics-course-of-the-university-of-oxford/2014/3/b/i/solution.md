<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [electron hole](../../../../../electron-hole.md) is a missing [electron](../../../../../electron.md) in an otherwise almost filled [valence band](../../../../../valence-band.md). A completely filled band has no net transport current: its velocities cancel over the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md). Describing a few missing states as mobile excitations is therefore simpler than following almost every occupied [electron](../../../../../electron.md) state.

Near an isotropic valence maximum, write the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) dispersion as $E_v(\mathbf k)=E_v(0)-\hbar^2k^2/(2m_h)$ with $m_h>0$. The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) curvature [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is negative there. Removing that [electron](../../../../../electron.md) creates an excitation with [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $\hbar\mathbf k_h=-\hbar\mathbf k$ and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) measured upward from the filled reference,

$$
E_h(\mathbf k_h)=E_v(0)-E_v(-\mathbf k_h)=\frac{\hbar^2k_h^2}{2m_h}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{m_h^*=-m_{e,\mathrm{valence}}^*>0.}
$$

The sign refers to the positive excitation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of a missing [electron](../../../../../electron.md) near a maximum, not to negative vacuum [mass](../../../../../mass.md). A general maximum gives the corresponding positive hole-curvature tensor.[§16.2, printed pp. 178–179: missing-electron carriers, opposite charge and [band effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md)](../../../../../lecture-notes-for-solid-state-physics-by-steven-h-simon.md)
