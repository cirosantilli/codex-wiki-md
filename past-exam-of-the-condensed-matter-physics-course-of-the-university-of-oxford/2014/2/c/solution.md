<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [electron](../../../../electron.md) losing [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to an acoustic [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) has, in a normal process, $\mathbf k'=\mathbf k_F-\mathbf k_{\rm ph}$, with possible addition of a reciprocal-lattice vector for an [Umklapp process](../../../../umklapp-scattering.md). The [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) transfer is at most of order $\hbar\omega_D=k_B\theta_D$, much smaller than $E_F=k_BT_F$. Hence the magnitudes change very little even though the [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) direction can change substantially. At $T\sim\theta_D\ll T_F$, thermal vacancies near the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md) allow emission into some lower-energy states; exactly at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) emission into an occupied Fermi sea would be blocked by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md).

Using $k'=(1-\delta)k_F$ and the free-electron dispersion, [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) conservation gives

$$
\frac{\hbar^2k_F^2}{2m_e}\left[1-(1-\delta)^2\right]
=\hbar c k_{\rm ph}.
$$

Since $\delta\ll1$, the bracket is $2\delta+O(\delta^2)$. Rearranging yields

$$
\boxed{\frac1{2\delta}\frac{k_{\rm ph}}{k_F}\frac1{k_F}
\simeq\frac\hbar{2m_ec}=\lambda.}
$$

Equivalently $\delta\simeq\hbar c k_{\rm ph}/(2E_F)$. For a Debye-cutoff [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) in the preceding model, $\delta\simeq\theta_D/(2T_F)=1/480=0.00208$, confirming the approximation. Near-elastic scattering constrains the radial distance in wave-vector space, not the [scattering angle](../../../../scattering-angle.md) alone.
