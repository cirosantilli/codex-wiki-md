<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the spin-degenerate [free electron model](../../../../free-electron-model.md), a monovalent metal has conduction-electron [number density](../../../../number-density.md) $n$. The density of wave-vector states is $1/(2\pi)^3$ per unit real-space [volume](../../../../volume.md), with two [spin](../../../../spin.md) states at each [wave vector](../../../../wavevector.md). Filling a sphere at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) gives

$$
n=\frac{2}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi k_F^3}{3}=\frac{k_F^3}{3\pi^2},\qquad
\boxed{T_F=\frac{E_F}{k_B}=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_ek_B}(3\pi^2n)^{2/3}.}
$$

For the [Debye model](../../../../debye-model.md), there are three acoustic polarizations and $3n$ modes per unit [volume](../../../../volume.md) in total. Replacing their dispersions by $\omega=ck$ with the effective [Debye sound speed](../../../../debye-sound-speed.md), mode counting gives

$$
3\frac{1}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi k_D^3}{3}=3n,
\qquad k_D=(6\pi^2n)^{1/3},
\qquad \boxed{\theta_D=\frac{\hbar c}{k_B}(6\pi^2n)^{1/3}.}
$$

The factor two in the [electron](../../../../electron.md) count is [spin](../../../../spin.md), whereas the factor three in the [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) count is polarization; the two counts are not interchangeable. If branch speeds differ, the Debye effective average obeys $c^{-3}=(c_l^{-3}+2c_t^{-3})/3$ in the isotropic approximation.

Dividing gives $T_F/\theta_D=\lambda(3\pi^2n/2)^{1/3}$, where $\lambda=\hbar/(2m_ec)$. A monatomic FCC conventional cell contains four atoms, so $n=4/a^3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_F}{\theta_D}=(6\pi^2)^{1/3}\frac\lambda a.}
$$

Here and below $c$ is the **speed of sound**, not the speed of light. The Debye cutoff counts acoustic modes in a spherical approximation to the [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md); it is not an electron-state cutoff.
