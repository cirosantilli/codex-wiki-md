<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A conventional [face-centered cubic](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell contains four [atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md). With three [conduction electrons](../../../../../conduction-electron.md) per [atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md),

$$
n=\frac{12}{(0.405\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm m)^3}=1.8064\times10^{29}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}},
\quad k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}=1.7488\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$

Using the [bare electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md),

$$
\boxed{E_F=\frac{\hbar^2k_F^2}{2m_e}=11.65\ \mathrm{eV},\qquad
T_F=1.352\times10^5\ \mathrm K.}
$$

The [Drude](../../../../../drude-model.md) transport relation is $\rho=m_e/(ne^2\tau)$, giving

$$
\boxed{\tau=\frac{m_e}{ne^2\rho}=7.02\times10^{-15}\ \mathrm s.}
$$

For a [degenerate electron gas](../../../../../degenerate-electron-gas.md), transport samples [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) near the [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md), so use $v_F=\hbar k_F/m_e=2.025\times10^6$ m/s:

$$
\boxed{\ell_F=v_F\tau=1.42\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm m=14.2\ \mathrm{nm}.}
$$

This is a [transport relaxation time](../../../../../momentum-relaxation-time.md) and associated [transport mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md) inferred from the specified [resistivity](../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md). It need not equal a single-particle quantum lifetime when scattering is strongly angle dependent. The free-electron valence count also neglects the detailed band structure of [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md), as the stipulated model requires. A classical [thermal speed](../../../../../thermal-speed.md) is not the appropriate velocity scale for this strongly degenerate metal.
