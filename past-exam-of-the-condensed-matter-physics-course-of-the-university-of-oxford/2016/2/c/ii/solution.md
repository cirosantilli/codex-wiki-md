<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For one spin-degenerate parabolic two-dimensional band, $k_F=\sqrt{2\pi n}=1.3729\times10^8$ m$^{-1}$. The [free-electron model](../../../../../free-electron-model.md) with $m=m_e$ gives

$$
\boxed{E_F=\frac{\pi\hbar^2n}{m_e}=7.182\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{eV}
=0.7182\ \mathrm{meV},\qquad T_F=8.334\ \mathrm K.}
$$

The given $10^3\ \Omega$ is a [sheet resistivity](../../../../../sheet-resistivity.md), not a bulk [resistivity in ohm-metres](../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md). The two-dimensional [Drude](../../../../../drude-model.md) [sheet conductance](../../../../../electrical-conductance.md) is $ne^2\tau/m_e$, so

$$
\boxed{\tau=\frac{m_e}{ne^2\rho_{\square}}=1.183\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm s.}
$$

Thus $v_F=\hbar k_F/m_e=1.589\times10^4$ m/s and

$$
\boxed{\ell_F=v_F\tau=1.880\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm m=188\ \mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The sample [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is not given. This is specifically the **Fermi-surface** [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md), appropriate in the degenerate regime $T\ll8.3$ K for the bare-mass model. At higher [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) the characteristic [thermal speed](../../../../../thermal-speed.md) should replace $v_F$ for a thermal transport distance. If a real two-dimensional band has mass $m^*$, the stated results scale as $E_F,T_F\propto1/m^*$, $\tau\propto m^*$ and $v_F\propto1/m^*$; the inferred $\ell_F=\hbar k_F/(ne^2\rho_{\square})$ is mass independent under these same [Drude](../../../../../drude-model.md) assumptions. No semiconductor [effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) or valley degeneracy is silently introduced.
