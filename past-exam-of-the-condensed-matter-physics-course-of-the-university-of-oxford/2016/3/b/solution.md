<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A $3d^9$ shell has one hole in the otherwise filled $d$ shell. [Hund's rules](../../../../hund-s-rules.md) give $S=1/2$, $L=2$, and, for a more-than-half-filled shell, $J=L+S=5/2$: the free-ion term is ${}^2D_{5/2}$. Its [Landé factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) is

$$
g_J=1+\frac{J(J+1)+S(S+1)-L(L+1)}{2J(J+1)}=\frac65.
$$

Distinguish the [Curie effective moment](../../../../effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) from a saturated projection:

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm eff}^{\rm free}=g_J\mu_B\sqrt{J(J+1)}=3.55\mu_B,
\qquad\mu_{\rm sat}^{\rm free}=g_JJ\mu_B=3\mu_B.}
$$

The question then explicitly asks us to use [orbital quenching](../../../../orbital-quenching.md) in the [crystal](../../../../crystal.md). [Ligand fields](../../../../crystal-field-theory.md) split and mix orbital states, suppressing their freely orientable [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md). The [spin-only](../../../../spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) approximation has $g\simeq2$, $S=1/2$, so $\mu_{\rm eff}=\sqrt3\mu_B$ but the two [field-axis](../../../../spin-one-half-along-an-axis.md) projections are $\pm\mu_B$. The latter, not $\sqrt3\mu_B$, belong in the tanh argument and [saturation magnetization](../../../../spin-one-half-paramagnetic-magnetization.md).

The sulfate and water environment supplies mainly closed-shell, weakly magnetic surroundings, stabilizes localized Cu2+ sites and separates their moments so that [exchange](../../../../exchange-interaction.md) can be weak on the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) scale considered. [Paramagnetism](../../../../paramagnetism.md) here is an approximation of weakly interacting local spins, not a claim that dilution guarantees no magnetic ordering at any [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Elemental [copper](../../../../copper.md) has a filled $3d$ band and itinerant [conduction electrons](../../../../conduction-electron.md) rather than one localized Cu2+ spin per [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md). [Pauli spin response](../../../../pauli-paramagnetism.md) and orbital/core contributions then replace a local-spin [Curie law](../../../../curie-s-law.md); ordinary pure [copper](../../../../copper.md) has a weak net [diamagnetic](../../../../diamagnetism.md) response.[2015 primary measurement, abstract: negative volume susceptibility of high-purity copper wire](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1090780715000774)

There is one Cu2+ per formula unit, hence $n_s=N_A/V_m=5.5148\times10^{27}$ m$^{-3}$. The [spin-only](../../../../spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) weak-field SI [volume susceptibility](../../../../volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md) is

$$
\chi_0(T)=\frac{\mu_0n_s\mu_B^2}{k_BT}=\frac{0.0431708\ \mathrm K}{T},
\qquad\boxed{\chi_0(300\ \mathrm K)=1.4390\times10^{-4},\quad
\chi_0(2\ \mathrm K)=2.1585\times10^{-2}.}
$$

These neglect [exchange](../../../../exchange-interaction.md), [anisotropic](../../../../anisotropy.md) $g$ factors and background [diamagnetism](../../../../diamagnetism.md). At high field and low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), saturation requires the nonlinear response in [a](../a.md); a field-independent [Curie value](../../../../curie-s-law.md) then describes only the zero-field slope.
