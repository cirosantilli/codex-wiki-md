↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Inelastic neutron scattering](../../../../inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) measures both [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) exchanged with the [crystal](../../../../crystal.md). Comparing incident and outgoing neutron [wavevectors](../../../../wavevector.md) gives $\mathbf Q=\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_f$, while their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) difference gives a created or annihilated [phonon energy](../../../../phonon.md) $\hbar\omega$. [Crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md) conservation identifies the reduced [phonon wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) modulo a [reciprocal-lattice vector](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). Varying scattering geometry maps [dispersion curves](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) and [polarization-dependent](../../../../phonon-polarization.md) [intensities](../../../../intensity-physics.md) across a substantial part of the [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md).

[Inelastic X-ray scattering](../../../../inelastic-x-ray-scattering.md) provides a complementary energy- and momentum-resolved probe, particularly when small samples or high-pressure environments favor photon beams. [Raman](../../../../raman-spectroscopy.md) and [infrared spectroscopy](../../../../infrared-spectroscopy.md) measure optical-frequency resonances subject to different symmetry selection rules. Photon [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) at these frequencies is usually small on the Brillouin-zone scale, so these methods mostly sample near the [zone center](../../../../brillouin-zone.md) rather than an arbitrary [phonon wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md). [Brillouin scattering](../../../../brillouin-scattering.md) and [ultrasonic](../../../../ultrasound.md) measurements probe low-frequency acoustic motion and infer [sound velocities](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) or [elastic constants](../../../../elastic-modulus.md).

[Heat-capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) measurements constrain the thermally weighted [vibrational density of states](../../../../phonon-density-of-states.md) and its low-frequency behavior, but do not by themselves reconstruct a unique wavevector-resolved dispersion. Elastic [diffraction](../../../../x-ray-diffraction.md) and temperature-dependent [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) factors also constrain mean-square motion, while energy-resolved inelastic measurements distinguish individual excitations directly. A monatomic scalar chain has only the [acoustic branch](../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md) derived below; it should not be assigned an [optical branch](../../../../optical-phonon-branch.md) merely because [Raman](../../../../raman-spectroscopy.md) spectroscopy exists for more complicated [crystals](../../../../crystal.md).
