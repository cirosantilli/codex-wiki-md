<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two sites give phases one and $\exp[i\pi(h+k+l)]=(-1)^{h+k+l}$. Thus

$$
F_{\rm CsCl}=f_{\rm Cs^+}+(-1)^{h+k+l}f_{\rm Cl^-},\qquad
\boxed{I_{hkl}=K_{hkl}\left|f_{\rm Cs^+}+(-1)^{h+k+l}f_{\rm Cl^-}\right|^2.}
$$

For real nonresonant [form factors](../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md), even $h+k+l$ gives the square of the sum and odd $h+k+l$ the square of the difference. The factor $K_{hkl}$ represents the remaining experimental corrections, not another basis-interference term. Since the two [ions](../../../../ion.md) differ, a half-body-diagonal translation does not preserve the decorated [crystal](../../../../crystal.md). Its [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is therefore [simple cubic](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md), with a two-ion basis; [CsCl](../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md) is not a monatomic [body-centered cubic](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md).

Replacing **both** sites by identical Fe [atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) changes the amplitude to

$$
F_{\rm Fe}=f_{\rm Fe}[1+(-1)^{h+k+l}],\qquad
\boxed{I_{hkl}^{\rm Fe}=\begin{cases}4K_{hkl}|f_{\rm Fe}|^2,&h+k+l\text{ even},\\0,&h+k+l\text{ odd}.\end{cases}}
$$

Now the body-centering translation does preserve the structure. It is a [body-centered cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) with one Fe [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) per [primitive cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md), or two per conventional cubic cell.

For 111 the parity is odd: [CsCl](../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md) generally has a nonzero amplitude $f_{\rm Cs^+}-f_{\rm Cl^-}$, whereas the identical-Fe 111 reflection is systematically absent. A numerical absolute [CsCl](../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md) [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) requires the angle-dependent [form factors](../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) and experimental normalization; no such values are supplied. Forward [electron](../../../../electron.md) counts, 54 for Cs+ and 18 for Cl-, explain why their amplitudes are substantially unequal, but are not substituted for the actual finite-angle factors without an additional approximation.
