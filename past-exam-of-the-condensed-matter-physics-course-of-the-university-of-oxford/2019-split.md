# 2019

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the condensed matter physics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source paper: [https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/7/Physics%20Papers%202018.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/7/Physics%20Papers%202018.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1)

The cover establishes Part B, Trinity Term 2019, 13 June 2019, A13485W1; the annual bundle's 2018 filename does not determine the examination year. This is distinct from the Part C major option. All four questions are solved. Use SI [magnetic susceptibility](../magnetic-susceptibility.md), $\chi=M/H$, and write $e>0$ for the elementary [electric charge](../electric-charge-split.md). In question 3, $n,p$ and $n_d$ are densities per area, and the band model has one parabolic extremum per carrier species with twofold [spin degeneracy](../spin-degeneracy.md).
