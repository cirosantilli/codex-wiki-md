↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [structure factor](../../../../structure-factor.md) is the coherent [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) of the contents of a [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md) at a specified momentum transfer. Contributions from its atoms add with phases fixed by their positions. At a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) indexed by [Miller indices](../../../../miller-index.md), the [crystal basis structure factor](../../../../crystal-basis-structure-factor.md) is

$$
\boxed{S_{hkl}=\sum_{j=1}^N f_j(\mathbf Q)\exp[2\pi i(hx_j+ky_j+lz_j)]}.
$$

Changing every phase sign conjugates the amplitude for real scattering factors and leaves the intensity unchanged. The positions here are fractions of the chosen cell edges. An [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) should not be confused with a set of primitive translation vectors.

In kinematic elastic scattering the [diffraction intensity](../../../../diffraction-intensity.md) of a [Bragg reflection](../../../../bragg-reflection.md) is proportional to $|S_{hkl}|^2$, after including geometrical, [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md), thermal-displacement and experimental factors. Powder integrated intensities also include multiplicity. The many-cell interference factor selects reciprocal-lattice vectors satisfying the diffraction geometry; the unit-cell factor determines their relative amplitudes and additional extinctions. Ring positions determine spacings through [Bragg's law](../../../../bragg-s-law.md), while intensity patterns and systematic absences constrain lattice centering and atomic arrangements. Intensity loses the overall complex phase, so structure determination is not a direct measurement of every individual atomic phase.

X-rays scatter from [electron density](../../../../electron-density.md), giving an atomic factor that changes with momentum transfer and chemical charge distribution. Neutrons scatter coherently from nuclei with bound coherent scattering lengths, which need not track [atomic number](../../../../atomic-number.md); magnetic neutron scattering is an additional channel in magnetic materials. Their different elemental/isotopic contrasts make the two probes complementary.
