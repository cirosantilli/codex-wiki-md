<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) prevents most filled electronic states from changing occupation at $T\ll T_F$. Only a shell of width of order $k_BT$ around the [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md) participates. Since the [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) near that [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is of order $n/E_F$ per [volume](../../../../volume.md) and [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), the fraction of active [electrons](../../../../electron.md) is of order $k_BT/E_F=T/T_F$. Their typical change of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is of order $k_BT$, so the [thermal energy](../../../../thermal-energy.md) increment per [volume](../../../../volume.md) scales as

$$
\Delta u_e\sim n\frac{T}{T_F}k_BT.
$$

Differentiating with respect to [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) gives the [electronic heat capacity](../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md)

$$
\boxed{C_e=A\,nk_B\frac{T}{T_F}}.
$$

The simple shell argument fixes the linear law, not its coefficient. A [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) using the spin-summed free-electron [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) $g(E_F)=3n/(2E_F)$ gives $C_e=(\pi^2/3)g(E_F)k_B^2T$, hence $A=\pi^2/2$, consistent with the supplied value. The result excludes strong band-structure or interaction corrections and requires a degenerate [electron](../../../../electron.md) population.
