<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) is one quantum of a collective harmonic [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) of a crystal. A mode labelled by [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) and branch has energies $E_m=\hbar\omega(m+1/2)$, with bosonic occupation statistics. The [crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md) $\hbar\mathbf k$ is defined modulo a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md); it is not generally the mechanical momentum of one atom. The [phonon dispersion relation](../../../../phonon-dispersion-relation.md) assigns its [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) and [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) to the [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) and branch.

[Inelastic neutron scattering](../../../../inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) can measure this relation in a single crystal. Determine the incident neutron [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md), scatter at a chosen angle, and analyze the outgoing [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by time of flight or a spectrometer analyzer. [Energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and crystal-momentum conservation give

$$
E_i-E_f=\pm\hbar\omega_s(\mathbf k),\qquad
\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_f=\mathbf G\pm\mathbf k.
$$

The signs distinguish [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) creation and annihilation with consistent momentum convention. Repeating measurements at different momentum transfers maps peaks in the dynamic scattering response into [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) branches. The measured intensity depends on occupation, scattering factors and the projection of the mode [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) along the momentum transfer; a weak or missing peak need not mean that the corresponding mode does not exist.
