<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $y=e^{-r/\xi}$. Differentiating the [Morse potential](../../../../morse-potential.md) gives

$$
V'(r)=\frac1\xi(-2Ay^2+By).
$$

The finite-separation minimum has $y=B/(2A)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{a=\xi\ln\frac{2A}{B}=0.36809\,\mathrm{nm},\qquad V(a)=-\frac{B^2}{4A}=-0.250\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The positive [bond-dissociation energy](../../../../bond-dissociation-energy.md) is $D=B^2/(4A)=0.250\,\mathrm{eV}$, measured relative to the separated-atom value $V(\infty)=0$. This distinguishes the well's negative [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) from the positive [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) required to break the bond.

For the displacement $x=r-a$, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is

$$
V(a+x)=V(a)+\frac12\kappa x^2+O(x^3),\qquad
\kappa=V''(a)=\frac{B^2}{2A\xi^2}=\frac{2D}{\xi^2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\kappa=70.862\,\mathrm{eV\,nm^{-2}}=11.353\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}.}
$$

The restoring [force](../../../../force-split.md) is $-\kappa x$ to leading order, identifying the bond's [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md).
