<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [mass](../../../../mass.md) per [lead](../../../../lead.md) atom is

$$
m=\frac{0.2070\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}}{N_A}=3.4373\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

Let $u_n$ be its longitudinal displacement. [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives the [harmonic chain](../../../../harmonic-chain.md) equation

$$
m\ddot u_n=\kappa(u_{n+1}+u_{n-1}-2u_n).
$$

Insert the [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) $u_n=u\,e^{i(kna-\omega t)}$ to obtain

$$
\omega^2=\frac{4\kappa}{m}\sin^2\frac{ka}{2}.
$$

At long [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md), $\omega\simeq a\sqrt{\kappa/m}\,|k|$. The [sound speed](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) is the corresponding low-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{v_s=a\sqrt{\frac\kappa m}=2.1155\times10^3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The relevant inertia in this chain equation is one atomic [mass](../../../../mass.md). The reduced [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m/2$ belongs to the relative vibration of an isolated pair and should not be substituted for $m$ in the chain's [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md).
