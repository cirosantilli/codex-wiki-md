↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) is a quantum of a [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) of [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) vibration. A harmonic mode of [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ has [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$, and adding one [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) increases its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by $\hbar\omega$. The [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) carries [crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md) $\hbar\mathbf q$, defined modulo a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector.

The first [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md) is the [Wigner-Seitz cell](../../../../wigner-seitz-cell.md) of the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) about the origin: the set of [wavevectors](../../../../wavevector.md) at least as close to the origin as to any other [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) point. Its boundaries are reciprocal-space perpendicular bisectors. It gives one representative of each [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) modulo [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) translations.
