<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The specified two-direction constraint permits magnetic moments $\mu_z=\pm\mu$, with

$$
\mu=g_JS\mu_B=7\mu_B,
$$

where $\mu_B$ is the [Bohr magneton](../../../../bohr-magneton.md). There are four magnetic [gadolinium](../../../../gadolinium.md) ions per conventional [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell; the closed-shell nitrogen ions add no local spin moment. Hence the magnetic-ion [number density](../../../../number-density.md) is

$$
n=\frac4{a^3}=3.20\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

At high [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), neglect interactions and use $B_z\simeq\mu_0H_z$. The one-ion [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) and mean [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) are

$$
Z_1=2\cosh\!\left(\frac{\mu\mu_0H_z}{k_BT}\right),\qquad
\langle\mu_z\rangle=\mu\tanh\!\left(\frac{\mu\mu_0H_z}{k_BT}\right).
$$

In the weak-[magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) limit, $\tanh x\simeq x$, so $M_z=n\langle\mu_z\rangle=\mu_0n\mu^2H_z/(k_BT)$. Thus the dimensionless SI [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) is $\chi=C/T$, with

$$
\boxed{C=\frac{\mu_0n(7\mu_B)^2}{k_B}=12.27\,\mathrm K.}
$$

The coefficient has units of kelvin. The unrestricted-spin expression proportional to $S(S+1)/3$ is inappropriate here: the source explicitly retains only the two orientations with $S_z=\pm S$. In this [two-state Curie law](../../../../two-state-curie-law.md), the squared axial moment is $(g_JS\mu_B)^2$.
