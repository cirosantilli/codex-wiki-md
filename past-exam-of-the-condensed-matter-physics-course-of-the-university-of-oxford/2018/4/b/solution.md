<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An $f$ shell has orbital quantum number $l=3$ and seven orbital states with $m_l=-3,-2,\ldots,3$. The first of [Hund's rules](../../../../hund-s-rules.md) maximizes total [spin](../../../../spin.md) subject to the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). Seven [electrons](../../../../electron.md) occupy the seven orbitals singly with parallel spins, giving

$$
\boxed{S=7\times\frac12=\frac72.}
$$

Their orbital projections sum to zero, so the half-filled shell also has total orbital [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $L=0$, total $J=7/2$, and [Landé g-factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) $g_J=2$. These further values identify the magnetic moment used below.
