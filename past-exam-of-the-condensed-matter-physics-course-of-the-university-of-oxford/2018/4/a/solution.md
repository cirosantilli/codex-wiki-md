A material with [paramagnetism](../../../../paramagnetism.md) develops a [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) parallel to a weak applied [magnetic field intensity](../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md), with positive [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) and no spontaneous [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) in its paramagnetic phase. [Molecular oxygen](../../../../molecular-oxygen.md) is an example; it has unpaired [electron spins](../../../../electron-spin.md).

A material with [diamagnetism](../../../../diamagnetism.md) develops an induced [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) opposing the applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), with negative [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md). A closed-shell atom such as [helium](../../../../helium.md) is an example; its orbital response is diamagnetic.

A [ferromagnet](../../../../ferromagnet.md) has a spontaneously ordered [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) below its [Curie temperature](../../../../curie-temperature.md), even in the limit of vanishing applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). [Iron](../../../../iron.md) below its [Curie temperature](../../../../curie-temperature.md) is an example. Different [magnetic domains](../../../../magnetic-domain.md) can cancel the net moment of an unmagnetized bulk sample, despite nonzero local spontaneous [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md).
