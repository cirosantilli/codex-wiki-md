<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The beta phase has a [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md) with an eight-atom [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md), rather than a [body-centred cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md). A [lattice translation](../../../../lattice-translation.md) must map the entire atomic arrangement onto itself. For example, the putative body-centering vector $(1/2,1/2,1/2)$ maps $(1/4,1/2,0)$ to $(3/4,0,1/2)$ modulo integer coordinates, where the given [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) has no atom. None of the seven nonzero basis positions generates a translation that preserves all eight sites. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{Bravais lattice: primitive cubic; basis: eight atoms.}}
$$

The presence of atoms at both the origin and the body centre alone is insufficient to establish body-centering of the full [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md).
