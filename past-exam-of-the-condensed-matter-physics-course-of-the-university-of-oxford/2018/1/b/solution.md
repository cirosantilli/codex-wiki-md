<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the conventional [body-centred cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md), the [crystal basis structure factor](../../../../crystal-basis-structure-factor.md) of identical atoms is

$$
F_{hkl}=f_{\mathrm W}\bigl[1+e^{i\pi(h+k+l)}\bigr].
$$

It vanishes for odd $h+k+l$ and equals $2f_{\mathrm W}$ for even $h+k+l$. Thus the first [powder diffraction](../../../../powder-diffraction.md) ring is the $\{110\}$ family, with [interplanar spacing](../../../../interplanar-spacing.md) $d_{110}=a/\sqrt2$.

The measured deflection is the angle between incident and scattered rays, $2\theta=40.26^\circ$, so the [Bragg angle](../../../../bragg-angle.md) is $\theta=20.13^\circ$. Using first-order [Bragg diffraction](../../../../bragg-diffraction.md), $2d\sin\theta=\lambda$, gives

$$
\boxed{a=\frac{\lambda}{\sqrt2\sin\theta}=0.31683\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The [mass density](../../../../density.md) follows from two atoms per conventional [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md), with molar mass $M=0.1838\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$ and [Avogadro constant](../../../../avogadro-constant.md) $N_A$:

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{2M}{N_Aa^3}=1.9194\times10^4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}\simeq19.19\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}.}
$$

Using $40.26^\circ$ as the [Bragg angle](../../../../bragg-angle.md) would confuse $\theta$ with $2\theta$ and give an incorrect [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md) and [mass density](../../../../density.md).
