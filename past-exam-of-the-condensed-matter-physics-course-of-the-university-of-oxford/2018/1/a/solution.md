↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is a set of points generated by integer translations along three independent [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md):

$$
\mathbf R=n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3,\qquad n_i\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Every [lattice point](../../../../lattice-point.md) has the same environment under [lattice translations](../../../../lattice-translation.md). A [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md) consists of this [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) with an [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) repeated at every [lattice point](../../../../lattice-point.md); the set of atomic positions need not itself be a [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md).

The two positions in the conventional cubic cell of alpha-[tungsten](../../../../tungsten.md) give a [body-centred cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md), with one [tungsten](../../../../tungsten.md) atom per primitive cell and two per conventional cell. One possible set of [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a_1=\frac a2(-1,1,1),\quad\mathbf a_2=\frac a2(1,-1,1),\quad\mathbf a_3=\frac a2(1,1,-1).}
$$

Their primitive-cell [volume](../../../../volume.md) is $a^3/2$.
