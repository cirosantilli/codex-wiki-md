<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [viscously damped monatomic-chain phonon](../../../../viscously-damped-monatomic-chain-phonon.md), the extra force gives $m\ddot u_j+\Gamma\dot u_j$ on the left of the preceding equation. Keep a real [wave number](../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$ and permit complex frequency in the convention $u_j\propto e^{i(kja-\omega t)}$. The dispersion equation is

$$
\boxed{m\omega^2+i\Gamma\omega-m\omega_0^2(k)=0.}
$$

Writing $\gamma=\Gamma/(2m)$, its two roots are

$$
\boxed{\omega_\pm(k)=-i\gamma\pm\sqrt{\omega_0^2(k)-\gamma^2}.}
$$

The negative imaginary part corresponds to decay with the chosen time convention. Here $\Gamma$ has units of [mass](../../../../mass.md) per time, while $\gamma$ is a decay rate. A driven experiment at specified real frequency would instead solve for a generally complex [wave number](../../../../wavenumber.md); it is a different way of specifying the response, not a contradictory sign choice.
