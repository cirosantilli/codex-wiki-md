<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the supplied effective electrostatic term and enforce [charge neutrality](../../../../charge-neutrality.md) while varying the chain length: $R=Z/(2n_e)$, with $N$ and $Z$ fixed. The total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) per positive ion becomes

$$
\varepsilon(n_e)=-\frac{\alpha Z^2e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0R}+\frac{ZE_F}{3}
=-\frac{\alpha Ze^2}{2\pi\epsilon_0}n_e
+\frac{Z\pi^2\hbar^2}{24m_e}n_e^2.
$$

The positive quadratic term supplies [degeneracy pressure](../../../../degeneracy-pressure.md) against compression. Differentiating gives the [neutral one-dimensional metal equilibrium density](../../../../neutral-one-dimensional-metal-equilibrium-density.md),

$$
\boxed{n_e^*=\frac{6\alpha m_ee^2}{\pi^3\epsilon_0\hbar^2}
=\frac{24\alpha}{\pi^2a_B}\simeq6.37\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},}
$$

where $a_B=4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2/(m_ee^2)$ is the [Bohr radius](../../../../bohr-radius.md). The second derivative $Z\pi^2\hbar^2/(12m_e)>0$ verifies a minimum. Within this specified toy [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), the minimizing [electron](../../../../electron.md) density is independent of $Z$; instead the equilibrium ion spacing is $2R^*=Z/n_e^*$.

At the minimum the electrostatic contribution is minus twice the kinetic contribution, so $\varepsilon_*=-ZE_F^*/3$. These follow from the given electrostatic approximation, not from a full self-consistent calculation of a real one-dimensional metal. In particular, additional Coulomb terms cannot be appended without the corresponding neutral-background subtraction that defines the supplied finite [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
