<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Within the stipulated localized, one-moment-per-atom [spin](../../../../spin.md) model, the number density follows directly from mass density and molar mass:

$$
n=\frac{\rho N_A}{M_{\mathrm{mol}}}
=\frac{8908}{0.058693}N_A
=9.1400\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

The [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) is consistent with four atoms per conventional [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md); no additional factor four is needed once $n$ has been obtained from the density. The [Curie constant](../../../../curie-constant.md) is $C=0.71549\,\mathrm K$. Identifying the model's $\theta=\lambda C$ with the specified [Curie temperature](../../../../curie-temperature.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{627\,\mathrm K}{0.71549\,\mathrm K}=8.7632\times10^2.}
$$

At very low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), a fully ordered single domain has $M_s=n\mu_B=8.4764\times10^5\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$. Its [Weiss molecular field](../../../../weiss-molecular-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{B_{\mathrm{eff}}=\lambda\mu_0M_s
=\frac{k_BT_C}{\mu_B}=9.3343\times10^2\,\mathrm T.}
$$

The cancellation of $n$ in the middle expression provides a useful check. The ordinary magnetostatic scale is only $\mu_0M_s\simeq1.065\,\mathrm T$, smaller by the factor $\lambda$. Thus $B_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is not a plausible ordinary self-generated dipolar [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) of the nickel moments. It represents the [exchange interaction](../../../../exchange-interaction.md) energy in field units, not an additional Maxwell field of that magnitude. This does not assert that such large externally generated fields are forbidden. Nickel is an itinerant [ferromagnet](../../../../ferromagnet.md); the imposed one-$\mu_B$-per-atom model is an estimate rather than its measured microscopic moment.
