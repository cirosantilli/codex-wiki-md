<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At fixed volume and particle number, reversible heating obeys $dS=C_V\,dT/T$. Integrating the [electronic heat capacity](../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md) from zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) gives

$$
S(T)-S(0)=\int_0^T\frac{\gamma T'}{T'}\,dT'=\gamma T.
$$

With no extensive residual ground-state degeneracy, the [third law of thermodynamics](../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) sets the electronic $S(0)=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S_{\mathrm{el}}=\gamma T.}
$$

The [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) normalization follows that of $\gamma$: total, per volume or per mole. Lattice contributions are additional and are not part of this electronic result.
