<h1 id="4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At $300\,\mathrm K$, $k_BT=0.0258520\,\mathrm{eV}$. With the scalar [effective masses](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) and two-spin convention of [a](../../a.md), the [effective density of states](../../../../../effective-density-of-states.md) values are

$$
N_c=3.32684\times10^{24}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}},\qquad
N_v=6.11180\times10^{24}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

The [intrinsic carrier concentration](../../../../../intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) follows from $n=p=n_i$:

$$
\boxed{p=n_i=\sqrt{N_cN_v}\,e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}
=2.1405\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.}
$$

From [b](../../b.md), $\alpha=(3/4)\ln(0.39/0.26)=0.304099$. Taking $E_v=0$ as a reference,

$$
\boxed{\mu_i-E_v=0.562862\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad
E_c-\mu_i=0.547138\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

It lies $7.862\,\mathrm{meV}$ above the middle of the [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md), because the hole [effective density of states](../../../../../effective-density-of-states.md) is larger. Both band edges are more than twenty $k_BT$ away, validating the [nondegenerate semiconductor](../../../../../nondegenerate-semiconductor.md) approximation.
