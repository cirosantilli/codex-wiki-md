<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [angle between two planes](../../../../../angle-between-two-planes.md) defined as the acute angle between their [normal vectors](../../../../../normal-vector.md). The normals $(1,1,1)$ and $(-1,1,1)$ have dot product one and lengths $\sqrt3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\phi=\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac13\right)=70.5288^\circ.}
$$

Choosing an oriented rather than an acute interplanar angle could give the supplementary $109.4712^\circ$; it describes the same unoriented pair of [lattice planes](../../../../../lattice-plane.md).
