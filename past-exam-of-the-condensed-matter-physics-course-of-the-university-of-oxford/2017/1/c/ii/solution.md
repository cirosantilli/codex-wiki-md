<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $(100)$ [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $(1,0,0)$. Its normalized dot product with $(1,1,1)$ is $1/\sqrt3$, so

$$
\boxed{\phi=\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt3}\right)=54.7356^\circ.}
$$

This is the acute angle between the [lattice planes](../../../../../lattice-plane.md), rather than the complementary angle between a plane and the other plane's normal.
