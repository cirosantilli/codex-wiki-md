↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the convention $\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf b_j=2\pi\delta_{ij}$, the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) of a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) is a [body-centered cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md). The reciprocal conventional cube is parallel to the direct conventional cube and has side $4\pi/a$. In direct-cubic coordinates its points are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf G=\frac{2\pi}{a}(h,k,l),\qquad h,k,l\text{ all even or all odd}.}
$$

The even triples supply the corners of that reciprocal cube and the odd triples its body centres. The [atomic basis](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) can change [diffraction intensities](../../../../diffraction-intensity.md) through its [structure factor](../../../../structure-factor.md); it does not change the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) determined by the translation lattice.
