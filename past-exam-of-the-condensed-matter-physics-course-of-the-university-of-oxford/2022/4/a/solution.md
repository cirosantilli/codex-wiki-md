<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a fixed open-shell [electron configuration](../../../../electron-configuration.md) in the [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md) regime, [Hund's rules](../../../../hund-s-rules.md) select the free-[ion](../../../../ion.md) ground term in the following order:

- Maximize the total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $S$, subject to the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md).
- Among terms with that $S$, maximize the total [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$.
- For a less-than-half-filled subshell choose the smallest total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), $J=|L-S|$; for a more-than-half-filled subshell choose the largest, $J=L+S$. A half-filled subshell has $L=0$ and $J=S$.

The first two rules come from the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) between [electrons](../../../../electron.md) within the partially filled subshell, including its [exchange interaction](../../../../exchange-interaction.md). Parallel [spins](../../../../spin.md) require an antisymmetric spatial [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md), reducing close approach and often lowering the repulsive [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Among the allowed high-[spin](../../../../spin.md) terms, the angular dependence of the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) generally favours the largest $L$; a simple magnetic alignment argument alone does not derive this second ordering rule.

The weaker [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) then splits a selected $L,S$ term. In [LS coupling](../../../../ls-coupling.md), its term-level [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is proportional to

$$
\langle\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S\rangle
=\frac{\hbar^2}{2}\left[J(J+1)-L(L+1)-S(S+1)\right].
$$

The effective sign appropriate to less-than-half versus more-than-half filling gives the third rule. These rules assume the electrostatic term separation dominates [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md); strong [crystal field splitting](../../../../crystal-field-splitting.md) or other coupling regimes require further analysis.
