<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the fitted inverse [molar magnetic susceptibility](../../../../molar-magnetic-susceptibility.md) as

$$
\chi_{\rm mol}^{-1}=sT+b=\frac{T-\Theta}{C_{\rm mol}},\qquad
C_{\rm mol}=\frac1s,\qquad \Theta=-\frac bs.
$$

The vertical scale is in $10^6\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$, not unscaled inverse SI [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md). The source line is approximately $8.8\times10^6\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$ at $100\,\mathrm K$ and $19\times10^6\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$ at $300\,\mathrm K$. These readings give

$$
s\simeq5.1\times10^4\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}},\qquad
b\simeq3.7\times10^6\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}.
$$

Therefore the [effective paramagnetic moment](../../../../effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) and [Weiss temperature](../../../../weiss-temperature.md) are

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm eff}=\sqrt{\frac1{(1.57\times10^{-6})s}}
\simeq3.5\text{–}3.6,\qquad
\Theta\simeq-70\,\mathrm K.}
$$

Equivalently, $\mu_{\rm eff}\simeq(3.5\text{–}3.6)\mu_B$. An uncertainty of order $10\,\mathrm K$ in the graphical [Weiss temperature](../../../../weiss-temperature.md) is reasonable; the plotted fit does not justify exact integer parameters. Its positive intercept at $T=0$ requires a negative extrapolated [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md)-axis intercept. Thus the dominant interactions have the [antiferromagnetic](../../../../antiferromagnetism.md) sign in the interpretation of part (b).
