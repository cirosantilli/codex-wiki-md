<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Curie constant](../../../../../curie-constant.md) measures the squared [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) averaged over all orientations of the $J$ multiplet, giving

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm eff}=|\widetilde g|\sqrt{J(J+1)},\qquad
\mu_{\rm eff}=|\widetilde g|\mu_B\sqrt{J(J+1)}.}
$$

For the usual positive [Landé g-factor](../../../../../lande-g-factor.md) of the [ion](../../../../../ion.md) here, the absolute-value sign can be omitted. This [effective paramagnetic moment](../../../../../effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) differs from the maximum projected or [saturation magnetic moment](../../../../../saturation-magnetic-moment.md), $\mu_{\rm sat}=|\widetilde g|J\mu_B$.
