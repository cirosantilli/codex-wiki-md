<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Weiss temperature](../../../../../weiss-temperature.md) $\Theta$ is the extrapolated zero of $1/\chi$ in a [Curie–Weiss law](../../../../../curie-weiss-law.md) fit. It summarizes the leading high-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) effect of interactions between [magnetic moments](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). For the sign convention

$$
H_{\rm ex}=-\sum_{i<j}\mathcal J_{ij}\,\mathbf J_i\cdot\mathbf J_j,
$$

where $\mathbf J_i$ are dimensionless [angular momentum operators](../../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md), [mean-field theory](../../../../../mean-field-theory.md) gives

$$
\Theta=\frac{J(J+1)}{3k_B}\sum_j\mathcal J_{ij}.
$$

Thus positive net [exchange interaction](../../../../../exchange-interaction.md) in this convention favours parallel [magnetic moments](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md), giving $\Theta>0$ and tendencies towards [ferromagnetism](../../../../../ferromagnetism.md); negative net [exchange interaction](../../../../../exchange-interaction.md) favours antiparallel [magnetic moments](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md), giving $\Theta<0$ and [antiferromagnetic](../../../../../antiferromagnetism.md) tendencies. Competing interactions can partially cancel in this sum. The [Weiss temperature](../../../../../weiss-temperature.md) need not equal the actual ordering [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), and its sign alone is not a complete determination of the low-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) ordered structure.
