<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In weak [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and the regime of [paramagnetism](../../../../../paramagnetism.md), localized [magnetic moments](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) with total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $J$ and [Landé g-factor](../../../../../lande-g-factor.md) $\widetilde g$ have SI [volume magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md)

$$
\chi_v=\frac{\mu_0 n_m(\widetilde g\mu_B)^2J(J+1)}{3k_B(T-\Theta)},
$$

within the [Curie–Weiss law](../../../../../curie-weiss-law.md). Here $n_m$ counts magnetic [ions](../../../../../ion.md) per volume, $\mu_0$ is the [vacuum permeability](../../../../../vacuum-permeability.md), $\mu_B$ is the [Bohr magneton](../../../../../bohr-magneton.md), and $\Theta$ is the [Weiss temperature](../../../../../weiss-temperature.md). The factor $J(J+1)/3$ is the zero-field average of the squared angular projection over $m_J=-J,\ldots,J$.

For one magnetic [ion](../../../../../ion.md) per formula unit, a molar volume $V_m$ contains $N_A$ magnetic [ions](../../../../../ion.md), where $N_A$ is the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md). Consequently $n_mV_m=N_A$, and the [molar magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../molar-magnetic-susceptibility.md) is

$$
\chi_{\rm mol}=V_m\chi_v
=\frac{\mu_0N_A\mu_B^2}{3k_B}\,
\frac{\widetilde g^{\,2}J(J+1)}{T-\Theta}.
$$

Defining the dimensionless [effective paramagnetic moment](../../../../../effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) $m_{\rm eff}$ by $\mu_{\rm eff}=m_{\rm eff}\mu_B$, numerical substitution gives

$$
\frac{\mu_0N_A\mu_B^2}{3k_B}=1.5714\times10^{-6}\,
\mathrm{m^3\,K\,mol^{-1}},\qquad
\boxed{\chi_{\rm mol}=(1.57\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3\,K\,mol^{-1}})
\frac{m_{\rm eff}^2}{T-\Theta}.}
$$

The normalization is per mole of formula units, which here also means per mole of magnetic [ions](../../../../../ion.md).
