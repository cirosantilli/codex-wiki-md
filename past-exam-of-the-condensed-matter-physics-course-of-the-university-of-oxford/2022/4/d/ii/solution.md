<h1 id="4/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $J=4$, the measured [effective paramagnetic moment](../../../../../effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) gives the [Landé g-factor](../../../../../lande-g-factor.md)

$$
\widetilde g_{\rm exp}=\frac{m_{\rm eff}}{\sqrt{4(4+1)}}
\simeq\frac{3.5\text{–}3.6}{\sqrt{20}}\simeq0.78\text{–}0.81.
$$

For the free-[ion](../../../../../ion.md) [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) values $S=1$, $L=5$, $J=4$, the stated formula yields

$$
\widetilde g_{\rm free}=\frac32+\frac{2-30}{2\times20}
=\frac32-\frac7{10}=\frac45.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\widetilde g_{\rm exp}\simeq0.8\simeq\widetilde g_{\rm free},\qquad
m_{\rm eff}^{\rm free}=\frac45\sqrt{20}=3.578.}
$$

The source fit is consistent with the unquenched $4f$ orbital [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) of the free [praseodymium](../../../../../praseodymium.md) [ion](../../../../../ion.md), within graphical accuracy. Small deviations need not indicate a change of [electron configuration](../../../../../electron-configuration.md): [crystal field splitting](../../../../../crystal-field-splitting.md), interactions and the fit range can affect a measured [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md).
