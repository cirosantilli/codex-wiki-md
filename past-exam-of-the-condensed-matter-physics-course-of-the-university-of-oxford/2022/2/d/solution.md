<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the same normalization for the [elastic energy](../../../../elastic-energy.md) and electronic [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md): if $\kappa\delta^2$ is an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) per [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md), divide it by $a$ first; if it is already per length, use it directly. Write its coefficient per length as $K>0$ and put $A=\pi^2V_0^2/(4aE_{\rm ZB})>0$. The small-distortion change in total [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) is

$$
\mathcal U_{\rm tot}(\delta)-\mathcal U_{\rm tot}(0)
=\delta^2\left[K-A+2A\ln\frac{C|\delta|}{4}\right]+O(\delta^4).
$$

No minimization calculation is needed. For arbitrarily small nonzero $|\delta|$, the [logarithm](../../../../logarithm.md) is negative with arbitrarily large magnitude. Hence the negative electronic [Peierls energy gain](../../../../logarithmic-energy-gain-from-a-peierls-distortion.md), proportional to $\delta^2\ln(1/|\delta|)$, eventually exceeds any fixed positive quadratic [elastic energy](../../../../elastic-energy.md). There are distorted configurations with lower [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) than the undistorted configuration for every finite $K$ and nonzero coupling. The ideal zero-[temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) infinite-chain model therefore has

$$
\boxed{|\delta_{\rm eq}|>0.}
$$

The source's $\delta_{\rm eq}>0$ is correct when $\delta$ denotes a nonnegative distortion amplitude. If $\delta$ is signed, the [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) is even and the two signs describe equally stable patterns related by a [lattice translation](../../../../lattice-translation.md). For example, $V(x+a/2;\delta)=V(x;-\delta)$, so a negative equilibrium distortion has the same [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) as the positive one. The zero-coupling case $V_0=0$ instead has its minimum at $\delta=0$. The argument assumes a coupled chain, complete lower-[energy band](../../../../energy-band.md) filling and zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md); it does not by itself establish finite-[temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) [long-range order](../../../../long-range-order.md) in an isolated one-dimensional system.
