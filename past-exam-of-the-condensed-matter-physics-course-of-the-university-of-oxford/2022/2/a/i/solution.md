<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The boundary of the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) is $|k|=\pi/a$. The unperturbed [free electron model](../../../../../free-electron-model.md) assigns the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md)

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm ZB}=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2}{2m_ea^2},}
$$

where $m_e$ is the bare [electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md) and $\hbar$ is the [reduced Planck constant](../../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md). This is the free [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) at the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) boundary, not the already split [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) edge. A constant mean [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) may be absorbed into the choice of zero of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
