<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $U(x)=-e\phi(x)$ for the [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) of an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) in an [electrostatic potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md) $\phi$, where $e>0$. With $G=2\pi/a$, the coupling in the [nearly-free electron model](../../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md) is the [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md)

$$
\boxed{V_G=\frac1a\int_0^a U(x)e^{-iGx}\,dx
=-\frac ea\int_0^a\phi(x)e^{-iGx}\,dx.}
$$

If a symbol $V(x)$ already denotes the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), as in part (c), it replaces $U(x)$ without another factor $-e$.

At the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) boundary the two resonant [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) have unperturbed [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_{\rm ZB}$. Their [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) in the two-state basis has diagonal entries $E_{\rm ZB}$ and off-diagonal entries $V_G,V_G^*$; its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $E_{\rm ZB}\pm|V_G|$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Delta=2|V_G|=2\epsilon E_{\rm ZB}.}
$$

This is the local [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) in the two-wave [nearly-free electron model](../../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md).
