<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Without the [Peierls distortion](../../../../../peierls-distortion.md), adjacent [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) are separated by $a/2$. The [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md) contains one [chemical atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md) and contributes one [electron](../../../../../electron.md). A [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) with two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states accommodates two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md), so the first [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) is half filled. Its true first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) extends to $\pm2\pi/a$, while the [Fermi points](../../../../../fermi-point.md) are at $\pm\pi/a$.

Describing the same chain with a doubled [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md) of length $a$ merely folds the half-filled [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) into two branches that touch at those [Fermi points](../../../../../fermi-point.md). The unbroken [lattice translation](../../../../../lattice-translation.md) by $a/2$ prevents an artificial [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) there. A [Peierls distortion](../../../../../peierls-distortion.md) breaks that smaller [lattice translation](../../../../../lattice-translation.md) and supplies the missing [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) at $2k_F=G$; the lower folded [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) can then be completely filled and separated from the upper [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md). Thus the zero-[band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) result at $\delta=0$ agrees with the original one-[electron](../../../../../electron.md) [band filling](../../../../../band-filling.md).
