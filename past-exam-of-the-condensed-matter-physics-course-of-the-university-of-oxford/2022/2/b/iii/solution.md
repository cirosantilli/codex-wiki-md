<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\epsilon\to0^+$, the [Taylor expansions](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) needed in the exact [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) are

$$
\sqrt{4+\epsilon^2}=2+\frac{\epsilon^2}{4}+O(\epsilon^4),\qquad
\ln\frac{2+\sqrt{4+\epsilon^2}}{\epsilon}
=\ln\frac4\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Substitution yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal U(\epsilon)
&=\frac{E_{\rm ZB}}a\left[\frac23-\frac{\epsilon^2}{4}
-\frac{\epsilon^2}{2}\ln\frac4\epsilon+O(\epsilon^4)\right]\\
&=\frac{E_{\rm ZB}}a\left[\frac23-\frac{\epsilon^2}{4}
\left(1-2\ln\frac\epsilon4\right)+O(\epsilon^4)\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1,\qquad\beta=2.}
$$

Here [Big O notation](../../../../../big-o-notation.md) describes the neglected small-coupling terms, and the [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md) is natural. The nonanalytic $\epsilon^2\ln\epsilon$ term comes from the formerly degenerate [quantum states](../../../../../quantum-state.md) near the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) boundary; a uniform nondegenerate [perturbation theory](../../../../../perturbation-theory.md) expansion of the integrand would fail there. This is the same mechanism as the [Peierls energy gain](../../../../../logarithmic-energy-gain-from-a-peierls-distortion.md).
