<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At every $y$ and every $\epsilon>0$,

$$
\sqrt{4y^2+\epsilon^2}>2|y|,
$$

so the lower [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) lies strictly below its zero-coupling counterpart. Its [state counting in a Bloch band](../../../../../state-counting-in-a-bloch-band.md) and complete occupation are unchanged. Subtracting the two [energy densities](../../../../../energy-density.md) directly gives

$$
\mathcal U(\epsilon)-\mathcal U(0)
=-\frac{E_{\rm ZB}}a\int_{-1}^1
\left[\sqrt{4y^2+\epsilon^2}-2|y|\right]dy<0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal U(\epsilon)<\mathcal U(0)\quad(\epsilon>0).}
$$

Equality holds at $\epsilon=0$. This is the downward displacement of the filled lower [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) in the sketch. The upward displacement of the empty upper [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) contributes nothing to the zero-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) electronic [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md).
