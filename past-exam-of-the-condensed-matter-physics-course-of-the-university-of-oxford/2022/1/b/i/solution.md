<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md) has $G_{hkl}=(2\pi/a)\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$. Combining this with [Bragg's law](../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) gives the [Bragg angle](../../../../../bragg-angle.md)

$$
\sin\theta_{hkl}=\frac{\lambda}{2a}\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}.
$$

Using $a=0.375\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $\lambda=0.154\,\mathrm{nm}$ yields

$$
\boxed{2\theta_{110}=33.762^\circ\simeq33.8^\circ,\qquad
2\theta_{111}=41.666^\circ\simeq41.7^\circ.}
$$

These are the full scattering angles; the [Bragg angles](../../../../../bragg-angle.md) themselves are half as large.
