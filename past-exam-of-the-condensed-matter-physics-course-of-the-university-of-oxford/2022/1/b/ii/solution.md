<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At each reflection, the argument of the [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) is

$$
s_{hkl}=\frac{\sin\theta_{hkl}}{\lambda}
=\frac{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}{2a},\qquad
s_{110}^2=3.55556\,\mathrm{nm^{-2}},\quad s_{111}^2=5.33333\,\mathrm{nm^{-2}}.
$$

Substitution into the given [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) approximation gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{\rm Au}(110)&=59.4421,&f_{\rm Cu}(110)&=23.1801,\\
f_{\rm Au}(111)&=51.5618,&f_{\rm Cu}(111)&=20.7241.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the [structure factors](../../../../../structure-factor.md) are $S_{110}=36.2620$ and $S_{111}=113.7340$. The [powder diffraction multiplicity](../../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md) is $m_{110}=12$: choose which [Miller index](../../../../../miller-index.md) is zero in three ways and the signs of the other two in four ways. For $\{111\}$, the eight sign choices give $m_{111}=8$. With the specified instrumental and angular factors omitted, the integrated [powder diffraction](../../../../../powder-diffraction.md) [wave intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) is proportional to $m_{hkl}|S_{hkl}|^2$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_{111}}{I_{110}}=\frac8{12}
\left(\frac{113.7340}{36.2620}\right)^2\simeq6.56.}
$$

It would be incorrect to use the same [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) at the two different [scattering vectors](../../../../../scattering-vector.md), or to omit the [powder diffraction multiplicity](../../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md).
