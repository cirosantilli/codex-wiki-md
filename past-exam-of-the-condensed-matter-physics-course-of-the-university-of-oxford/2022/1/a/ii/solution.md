<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) $\mathbf G_{hkl}=(2\pi/a)(h,k,l)$, sum the [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) with the [atomic basis](../../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) phase factors. Taking the positive phase convention gives the [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md)

$$
S_{hkl}=f_{\rm Au}(G_{hkl})+f_{\rm Cu}(G_{hkl})
\left[e^{i\pi(k+l)}+e^{i\pi(h+l)}+e^{i\pi(h+k)}\right].
$$

Changing the phase convention complex-conjugates the [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) and leaves the [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) [wave intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) unchanged. Since the [Miller indices](../../../../../miller-index.md) are integers,

$$
\alpha_{hkl}=(-1)^{k+l}+(-1)^{h+l}+(-1)^{h+k}.
$$

If all three [Miller indices](../../../../../miller-index.md) have the same [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md), all pairwise sums are even. Otherwise exactly one pairwise sum is even and two are odd. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\alpha_{hkl}=\begin{cases}3,&h,k,l\text{ all even or all odd},\\-1,&\text{mixed parity}.\end{cases}}
$$

The corresponding [structure factors](../../../../../structure-factor.md) are $f_{\rm Au}+3f_{\rm Cu}$ and $f_{\rm Au}-f_{\rm Cu}$. Reflections with mixed [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md) are [superlattice reflections](../../../../../superlattice-reflection.md) that reveal [chemical ordering in alloys](../../../../../chemical-ordering-in-alloys.md).
