<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Specify the normalization of the [alloy sublattice order parameter](../../../../../alloy-sublattice-order-parameter.md) rather than assuming that its endpoint values uniquely define it. Let $p_c$ be the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of [gold](../../../../../gold.md) occupation at a corner site and define

$$
\rho=\frac{p_c-1/4}{1-1/4},\qquad p_c=\frac{1+3\rho}{4}.
$$

The complementary [copper](../../../../../copper.md) [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is $3(1-\rho)/4$, so the corner-site [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle f_c\rangle=\frac14\left[(1+3\rho)f_{\rm Au}+3(1-\rho)f_{\rm Cu}\right].}
$$

The [stoichiometry](../../../../../stoichiometry.md) provides one [gold](../../../../../gold.md) [chemical atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md) per four-site [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md). If all three face sublattices are equivalent, their [gold](../../../../../gold.md) occupation [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $p_f$ obeys $p_c+3p_f=1$. Therefore

$$
p_f=\frac{1-\rho}{4},\qquad 1-p_f=\frac{3+\rho}{4},\qquad
\boxed{\langle f_f\rangle=\frac14\left[(1-\rho)f_{\rm Au}+(3+\rho)f_{\rm Cu}\right].}
$$

At $\rho=1$ the corner and face sites carry pure [gold](../../../../../gold.md) and pure [copper](../../../../../copper.md); at $\rho=0$ they have identical statistical compositions. These formulas describe the branch with $0\leq\rho\leq1$ and equivalent face-site averages.
