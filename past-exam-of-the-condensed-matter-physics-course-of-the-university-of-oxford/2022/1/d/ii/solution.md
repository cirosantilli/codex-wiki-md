<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the same [atomic basis](../../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) phases as in the ordered [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md), replacing each [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) by its site average. For the reflection with equal [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md),

$$
S_{111}=\langle f_c\rangle+3\langle f_f\rangle=f_{\rm Au}+3f_{\rm Cu}.
$$

Conservation of the total composition removes the [alloy sublattice order parameter](../../../../../alloy-sublattice-order-parameter.md) from this amplitude. For the reflection with mixed [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md),

$$
S_{110}=\langle f_c\rangle-\langle f_f\rangle=\rho(f_{\rm Au}-f_{\rm Cu}).
$$

The coherent [powder diffraction](../../../../../powder-diffraction.md) [wave intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) consequently satisfy

$$
\boxed{I_{111}(\rho)=I_{111}(1),\qquad I_{110}(\rho)=\rho^2 I_{110}(1).}
$$

Here the [lattice constant](../../../../../lattice-constant.md), [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md), [powder diffraction multiplicities](../../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md), and omitted experimental factors are held fixed. The ratio of a [superlattice reflection](../../../../../superlattice-reflection.md) to a fundamental reflection can therefore measure the magnitude of the [alloy sublattice order parameter](../../../../../alloy-sublattice-order-parameter.md).
