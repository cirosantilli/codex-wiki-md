<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [stoichiometry](../../../../../stoichiometry.md) fixes the site [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) to $1/4$ [gold](../../../../../gold.md) and $3/4$ [copper](../../../../../copper.md). Every site in the average [crystal structure](../../../../../crystal-structure.md) therefore has the same [atomic scattering factor](../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md)

$$
\bar f=\frac14f_{\rm Au}+\frac34f_{\rm Cu}.
$$

The average [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) is

$$
\bar S_{hkl}=\bar f\left[1+(-1)^{k+l}+(-1)^{h+l}+(-1)^{h+k}\right].
$$

At $(110)$ the bracket vanishes, while at $(111)$ it is four. Hence

$$
\boxed{I_{110}^{\rm Bragg}=0,\qquad
\bar S_{111}=4\bar f=f_{\rm Au}+3f_{\rm Cu}=S_{111}^{\rm ordered}.}
$$

The $(111)$ [powder diffraction multiplicity](../../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md) is unchanged, so its coherent [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) [wave intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) equals the ordered value under the stated equal-[lattice constant](../../../../../lattice-constant.md) approximation.

The zero refers to the coherent [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) peak of the average [crystal structure](../../../../../crystal-structure.md). A random [alloy](../../../../../alloy.md) can still give [occupational diffuse scattering](../../../../../occupational-diffuse-scattering.md) at the same [scattering vector](../../../../../scattering-vector.md): the average of the squared amplitude includes fluctuations as well as the square of the average amplitude. Such background is not a surviving $(110)$ [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) peak.
