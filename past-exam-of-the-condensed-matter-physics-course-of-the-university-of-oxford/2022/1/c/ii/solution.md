<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Replace every coloured site in the drawing by an identical site carrying $\bar f$. The face-centering [lattice translations](../../../../../lattice-translation.md) now preserve the average [crystal structure](../../../../../crystal-structure.md), whose [Bravais lattice](../../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md). One choice of [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) is

$$
\mathbf a_1=\frac a2(0,1,1),\qquad
\mathbf a_2=\frac a2(1,0,1),\qquad
\mathbf a_3=\frac a2(1,1,0).
$$

A coherent [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) peak requires $\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf a_j\in2\pi\mathbb Z$ for all three [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md). Thus $k+l$, $h+l$, and $h+k$ must all be even, equivalent to all three [Miller indices](../../../../../miller-index.md) having the same [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md). This is the [face-centred cubic selection rule](../../../../../face-centred-cubic-selection-rule.md): $(110)$ is forbidden and $(111)$ is allowed. The restoration of these [lattice translations](../../../../../lattice-translation.md) explains the disappearance of the ordered [superlattice reflections](../../../../../superlattice-reflection.md).
