<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are three regimes. At the lowest [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), most [donor impurities](../../../../../donor-impurity.md) retain their [electrons](../../../../../electron.md); thermal ionization gives [semiconductor carrier freeze-out](../../../../../semiconductor-carrier-freeze-out.md). The result from part (b) is

$$
n\simeq\sqrt{N_DN_c}\,e^{-E_i/(2k_BT)}\propto T^{3/4}e^{-E_i/(2k_BT)}.
$$

This produces the approximately straight descending low-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) tail on the [logarithmic scale](../../../../../logarithmic-scale.md) plot against $1/T$. The activation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in the exponential is $E_i/2$, because the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) adjusts with donor ionization.

At intermediate [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the [donor impurities](../../../../../donor-impurity.md) are essentially fully ionized while intrinsic excitation remains small. [Charge neutrality](../../../../../charge-neutrality.md) gives $n\simeq N_D$, producing the extrinsic plateau, roughly from $20$ to $40\,\mathrm K$ in the figure.

At higher [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), excitation from the [valence band](../../../../../valence-band.md) to the [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md) creates [electron-hole pairs](../../../../../electron-hole-pair.md). The [intrinsic carrier concentration](../../../../../intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) grows as $n_i\propto T^{3/2}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$ for constant parabolic [band effective masses](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) and constant [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md). With ionized [donor impurities](../../../../../donor-impurity.md), [charge neutrality](../../../../../charge-neutrality.md) and the [semiconductor mass-action law](../../../../../semiconductor-mass-action-law.md) give

$$
n-p=N_D,\qquad np=n_i^2,\qquad
n=\frac{N_D+\sqrt{N_D^2+4n_i^2}}2.
$$

Thus $n\simeq n_i+N_D/2$ when $n_i\gg N_D$. This explains the renewed rapid rise and the eventual intrinsic branch; that branch is governed by the [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md), rather than the donor binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
