A [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is the set of points

$$
\Lambda=\left\{n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3:n_i\in\mathbb Z\right\},
$$

where the [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) $\mathbf a_i$ are linearly independent. Every lattice point has the same translational environment.

An [atomic basis](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) is the motif of atoms attached to each point of the [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md), specified by positions $\mathbf r_p$ and atomic species. The full crystal consists of atoms at $\mathbf R+\mathbf r_p$, with $\mathbf R\in\Lambda$. The [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) describes translations; the [atomic basis](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) describes what is repeated. A primitive cell contains one lattice point but can contain several atoms.
