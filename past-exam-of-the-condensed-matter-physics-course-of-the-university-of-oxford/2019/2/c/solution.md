<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the ratio of the two measured coefficients so that the sample size cancels:

$$
\frac{A_2}{A_0}=-\frac{T_E^2}{12}=-\frac{7500}{25}\,\mathrm K^2=-300\,\mathrm K^2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{T_E=\sqrt{12\cdot300}\,\mathrm K=60\,\mathrm K.}
$$

The units of the $7500$ coefficient are $\mathrm{J\,K}$, so that its division by $T^2$ has the same [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) units as the leading $25\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ term.
