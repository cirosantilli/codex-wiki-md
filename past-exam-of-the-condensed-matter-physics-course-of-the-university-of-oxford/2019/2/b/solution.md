<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the given small-$x$ expansion with $x=T_E/T$ to the internal energy, including its [zero-point energy](../../../../zero-point-energy.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
U&=3Nk_BT_E\left[\frac12+\frac{T}{T_E}-\frac12+\frac{T_E}{12T}+O(T^{-3})\right]\\
&=3Nk_B\left[T+\frac{T_E^2}{12T}+O(T^{-3})\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

The temperature-independent terms cancel. Taking the derivative gives the [Einstein high-temperature heat-capacity expansion](../../../../einstein-high-temperature-heat-capacity-expansion.md)

$$
C_V=3Nk_B\left[1-\frac{T_E^2}{12T^2}+O(T^{-4})\right].
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{A_0=3Nk_B,\qquad A_1=0,\qquad A_2=-\frac{Nk_BT_E^2}{4}=-\frac{A_0T_E^2}{12}.}
$$

There is no $1/T$ correction to the [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) even though the internal energy has a $1/T$ correction.
