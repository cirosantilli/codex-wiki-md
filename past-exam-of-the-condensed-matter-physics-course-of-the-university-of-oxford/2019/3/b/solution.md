<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Near a parabolic [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) maximum $E_v$, the missing-electron [effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) is $m_h^*>0$. The same two-dimensional state counting gives

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{m_h^*k_BT}{\pi\hbar^2}e^{-\beta(\mu-E_v)}\equiv N_v e^{-\beta(\mu-E_v)}.}
$$

Multiplication eliminates the [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), giving the [semiconductor mass-action law](../../../../semiconductor-mass-action-law.md)

$$
\boxed{np=N_cN_v e^{-E_g/(k_BT)}=m_e^*m_h^*\left(\frac{k_BT}{\pi\hbar^2}\right)^2e^{-E_g/(k_BT)},\qquad E_g=E_c-E_v.}
$$

For an [intrinsic semiconductor](../../../../intrinsic-semiconductor.md), [charge neutrality](../../../../charge-neutrality.md) imposes $n=p=n_i$, so

$$
n_i=\frac{\sqrt{m_e^*m_h^*}\,k_BT}{\pi\hbar^2}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}.
$$

The intrinsic [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) is not exactly midgap when the two [effective masses](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) differ: $\mu_i=(E_c+E_v)/2+(k_BT/2)\log(m_h^*/m_e^*)$. The exponential suppression still justifies the assumed nondegenerate regime here.
