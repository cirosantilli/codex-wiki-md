<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For area $A$, allowed [wavevectors](../../../../wavevector.md) have density $A/(2\pi)^2$. Include two [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) states and fill a disk of radius $k_F$ at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Then

$$
N=2\frac{A}{(2\pi)^2}\pi k_F^2,
\qquad n_s=N/A=\frac{k_F^2}{2\pi}.
$$

The [free electron model](../../../../free-electron-model.md) has [parabolic dispersion](../../../../parabolic-band-dispersion.md) $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m_e)$, with its minimum chosen as zero, so

$$
\boxed{k_F=\sqrt{2\pi n_s},\qquad E_F=\frac{\pi\hbar^2n_s}{m_e}.}
$$

These are the [Fermi wavevector](../../../../fermi-wavevector.md) and [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md) for a spin-degenerate two-dimensional [free electron gas](../../../../free-electron-model.md). An imposed spin polarization or additional valley degeneracy would change the state count.
