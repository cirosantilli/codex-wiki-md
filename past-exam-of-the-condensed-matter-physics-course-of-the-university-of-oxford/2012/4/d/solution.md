<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For one [electron](../../../../electron.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md), the areal [number density](../../../../number-density.md) is $n_s=1/(ab)$. The [Fermi circle](../../../../fermi-circle.md) has radius $k_F=\sqrt{2\pi/(ab)}$. The nearer first-zone boundary is $k_x=\pm\pi/a$, because $a>b$. The circle protrudes precisely when

$$
k_F>\frac\pi a
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{2\pi}{ab}>\frac{\pi^2}{a^2}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\boxed{a>\frac{\pi b}{2}.}
$$

At equality the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md) only touches the boundary. Crossing an extended-zone boundary alone does not make a gap: the [free electron gas](../../../../free-electron-model.md) remains metallic when the ionic [periodic potential](../../../../periodic-potential.md) is neglected.
