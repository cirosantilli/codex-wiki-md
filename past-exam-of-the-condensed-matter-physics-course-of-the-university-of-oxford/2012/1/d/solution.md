<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The high-pressure [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md) follows from its first $(100)$ reflection:

$$
a_P=\frac{0.154\,\mathrm{nm}}{2\sin14.85^\circ}=0.30044\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

Conserve the number of NaCl [formula units](../../../../formula-unit.md). The low-pressure conventional [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md) contains four, whereas the high-pressure cell contains one. The [volume](../../../../volume.md) per [formula unit](../../../../formula-unit.md) changes from $a_0^3/4$ to $a_P^3$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_P}{V_0}=4\left(\frac{a_P}{a_0}\right)^3\simeq0.605.}
$$

The sample contracts by about $39.5\%$. Comparing $a_P^3$ directly with $a_0^3$ would compare different amounts of material.
