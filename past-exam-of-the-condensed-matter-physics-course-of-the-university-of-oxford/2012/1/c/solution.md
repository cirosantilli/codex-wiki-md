<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The high-pressure [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) has the [caesium chloride structure](../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md). Its chemically labelled [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is [simple cubic lattice](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md): exchanging Na and Cl is not a [translation symmetry](../../../../translational-symmetry.md). Its [structure factor](../../../../structure-factor.md) is

$$
F_{hkl}=f_{\rm Na}+(-1)^{h+k+l}f_{\rm Cl}.
$$

Because [ionic bonding](../../../../ionic-bonding-split.md) gives approximately Na$^+$ and Cl$^-$, their bound-electron counts are $10$ and $18$. In the stipulated electron-count approximation, odd-index-sum reflections have $|F|=8$, while even-index-sum reflections have $|F|=28$. In particular, the first ring $\{100\}$ is weak but present; it must not be discarded using a body-centering extinction rule.

The next ring is $\{110\}$, and [Bragg's law](../../../../bragg-s-law.md) gives

$$
\sin\frac{\psi_{110}}2=\sqrt2\sin\frac{29.7^\circ}2,
\qquad\boxed{\psi_{110}=42.50^\circ.}
$$

The [powder diffraction multiplicities](../../../../powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md) are six and twelve. Counting all symmetry-equivalent reflections, while neglecting other angular factors, therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_{110}}{I_{100}}\simeq\frac{12}{6}\left(\frac{28}{8}\right)^2=24.5.}
$$

The corresponding single-directed-reflection ratio would be $12.25$. Neutral-atom counts would give the wrong contrast because the material is ionic.

For measured integrated photon counts, the geometry must also be specified. More generally the ratio is $2|F_{110}/F_{100}|^2$ times the ratio of [Lorentz-polarization factors](../../../../lorentz-polarization-factor.md), thermal-motion factors and detection efficiencies. For example, the common unpolarized symmetric-powder convention has

$$
LP(\theta)\propto\frac{1+\cos^2(2\theta)}{\sin^2\theta\cos\theta},
$$

and gives $24.5\,LP(21.2506^\circ)/LP(14.85^\circ)\simeq11.18$ if all other corrections are suppressed. Thus **24.5 is the multiplicity-and-structure-factor estimate**, rather than a geometry-independent precision count ratio; actual angle-dependent ionic form factors are also not supplied.
