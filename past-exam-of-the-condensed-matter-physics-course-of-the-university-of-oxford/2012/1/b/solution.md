<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the total scattering angle $\psi=2\theta$. For a cubic [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md), [Bragg's law](../../../../bragg-s-law.md) and the [Miller indices](../../../../miller-index.md) give

$$
\sin^2\theta=\frac{\lambda^2}{4a_0^2}(h^2+k^2+l^2).
$$

The four measured values of $\sin^2\theta$, normalized to the first, are $1,1.330,2.655,3.649$. Multiplying by three gives approximately $3,4,8,11$, the initial allowed sequence for a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md):

$$
\{111\},\quad\{200\},\quad\{220\},\quad\{311\}.
$$

The FCC lattice factor is $1+(-1)^{h+k}+(-1)^{h+l}+(-1)^{k+l}$; it vanishes unless all three indices have the same parity. Thus the initial ratios differ from those of a primitive or body-centered cubic lattice. This is the conventional minimal cubic indexing; arbitrarily hiding additional weak reflections could make a short list alone ambiguous.

The low-pressure [rock salt structure](../../../../rock-salt-structure.md) has an FCC [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) with one NaCl [formula unit](../../../../formula-unit.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md), or four per conventional cube. A possible [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) is Na at zero and Cl at $(1/2,0,0)$ in conventional fractional coordinates. The two species have unequal [atomic scattering factors](../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md), so both the odd and even FCC families can occur.

The four independent [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md) estimates are

$$
a_0=\frac{\lambda\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}{2\sin(\psi/2)}
=(0.56312,\ 0.56386,\ 0.56436,\ 0.56446)\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

Their small scatter reflects the rounded angular data. The appropriate reported result is

$$
\boxed{\text{FCC Bravais lattice},\qquad a_0\simeq0.564\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$
