<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) changes the alignment of the manganese [magnetic moments](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The component of their [magnetic structure factor](../../../../magnetic-structure-factor.md) perpendicular to the [scattering vector](../../../../scattering-vector.md) contributes to [magnetic neutron diffraction](../../../../magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md). A field-induced uniform [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) has the same translational periodicity as the nuclear [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md), so its [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) can occur at the existing [reciprocal lattice vectors](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). The [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) [wave intensities](../../../../intensity-physics.md) can therefore change without moving the peaks. Their angular positions still measure the same [interplanar spacings](../../../../interplanar-spacing.md). This mechanism does not require a measurable change of the [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md).
