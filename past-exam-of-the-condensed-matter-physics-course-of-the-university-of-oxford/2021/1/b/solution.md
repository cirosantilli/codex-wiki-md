<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) is nonrelativistic at this [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Its [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) is

$$
\lambda=\frac{h}{\sqrt{2m_nE}}
=\frac{6.62607015\times10^{-34}}{\sqrt{2(1.67493\times10^{-27})(25\times10^{-3})(1.602176634\times10^{-19})}}
=0.180891\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

In [elastic scattering](../../../../elastic-scattering.md), [Bragg's law](../../../../bragg-s-law.md) gives $2d\sin\theta=\lambda$. The measured total deflection is $2\theta$, so it must be halved before taking the sine. The reflection order is absorbed into the [Miller indices](../../../../miller-index.md) of the diffracting [lattice planes](../../../../lattice-plane.md). In the order of the four measured rings, the [interplanar spacings](../../../../interplanar-spacing.md) are

$$
\boxed{d_1=0.42457\,\mathrm{nm},\quad d_2=0.29995\,\mathrm{nm},\quad d_3=0.24462\,\mathrm{nm},\quad d_4=0.21203\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The last digits give the numerical calculation, rather than accuracy beyond the supplied angular precision.
