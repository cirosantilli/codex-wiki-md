↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Determining [antiferromagnetic](../../../../antiferromagnetism.md) [magnetic order](../../../../magnetic-order.md) is a good reason to use [neutron scattering](../../../../neutron-scattering.md). The [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) has a [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md), so its [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) includes the spatial arrangement of electronic [magnetic moments](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). Alternating [magnetic moments](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) can produce [magnetic neutron diffraction](../../../../magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md) peaks even when there is no change of the nuclear [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md). Ordinary nonresonant [X-ray diffraction](../../../../x-ray-diffraction.md) primarily measures the [electron density](../../../../electron-density.md), and is much less sensitive to this [magnetic order](../../../../magnetic-order.md).
