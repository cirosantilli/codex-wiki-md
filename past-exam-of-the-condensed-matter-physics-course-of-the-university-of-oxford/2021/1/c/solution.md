<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [cubic crystal system](../../../../cubic-crystal-system.md), the [Miller indices](../../../../miller-index.md) satisfy

$$
\frac1{d_{hkl}^2}=\frac{h^2+k^2+l^2}{a^2}.
$$

The inverse squared [interplanar spacings](../../../../interplanar-spacing.md) have ratios approximately $1:2.004:3.012:4.010$. With the [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md) close to $0.4\,\mathrm{nm}$, the appropriate squared-index sums are $1,2,3,4$. Thus the four [powder diffraction](../../../../powder-diffraction.md) rings are

$$
\boxed{\{100\},\quad\{110\},\quad\{111\},\quad\{200\}.}
$$

The braces collect all symmetry-related [lattice planes](../../../../lattice-plane.md) with the same [interplanar spacing](../../../../interplanar-spacing.md). In particular, $(100)$ is forbidden for both a [body-centered cubic lattice](../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) and a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md), but occurs for a [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md). These observations identify the [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) as [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md), with a multi-atom [atomic basis](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md), consistent with a [perovskite structure](../../../../perovskite-structure.md).

Each ring independently gives $a_j=d_j\sqrt{j}$:

$$
a_1=0.42457,\qquad a_2=0.42419,\qquad a_3=0.42369,\qquad a_4=0.42406\qquad (\mathrm{nm}).
$$

Their average is $0.42413\,\mathrm{nm}$. Allowing for the rounded measured angles, a suitable final value is

$$
\boxed{a\simeq0.424\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

A larger centred [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md) could relabel reflections, but would conflict with the stated approximate [lattice constant](../../../../lattice-constant.md).
