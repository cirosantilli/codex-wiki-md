<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The number of formula units per volume follows from the mass [density](../../../../density.md):

$$
n_f=\frac{5.9\times10^3\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}}{(69.7+74.9)(1.66053906892\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg})}
=2.4572\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

Each neutral formula unit contains $31+33=64$ [electrons](../../../../electron.md), so $\rho_e=64n_f=1.5726\times10^{30}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Let $R^2$ be the electron-weighted [mean-square orbital radius](../../../../mean-square-orbital-radius.md), averaged over all these [electrons](../../../../electron.md). The supplied [Larmor-Langevin susceptibility](../../../../larmor-langevin-susceptibility.md) then gives the dimensionless SI [volume magnetic susceptibility](../../../../volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md)

$$
\chi_0=-\frac{\mu_0\rho_e e^2}{6m_e}R^2,
\qquad
\boxed{\chi_0=-2.5990\times10^{-5}\left(\frac{R}{a_0}\right)^2,}
$$

where $a_0=0.0529177\,\mathrm{nm}$ is the [Bohr radius](../../../../bohr-radius.md). An explicit rough atomic-scale assumption $R\simeq a_0$ gives $\chi_0\simeq-2.6\times10^{-5}$.

The quoted bulk data do not determine $R$: a nuclear charge does not specify an orbital radius in a screened many-[electron](../../../../electron.md) [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md). Consequently the numerical estimate just made has atomic-model uncertainty; it is not a deduction with guaranteed 30 percent accuracy. For example, the alternative assumed $R=2a_0$ gives four times the magnitude while leaving every supplied bulk number unchanged. Atomic [wavefunctions](../../../../wave-function-split.md), an explicit screened-orbital model, or measured atomic [magnetic susceptibilities](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) of materials exhibiting [diamagnetism](../../../../diamagnetism.md) would be needed to support that accuracy. Keeping $R$ explicit makes the later comparison independent of an unadvertised radius assumption. The bare [electron mass](../../../../electron-mass.md) belongs in this atomic formula; the [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md) [effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) is relevant instead to the large donor envelope in part (c).
