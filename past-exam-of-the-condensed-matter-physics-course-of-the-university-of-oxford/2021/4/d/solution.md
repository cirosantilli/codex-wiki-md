<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [hydrogenic donor state](../../../../hydrogenic-donor-state.md) has an exponentially decaying envelope $\psi(r)\propto e^{-r/a_b}$. Neighbouring donors at typical separation $r_d\sim n^{-1/3}$ therefore have exponentially small orbital overlap. The corresponding [hopping integral](../../../../hopping-integral.md) is of order $E_b e^{-r_d/a_b}$, apart from algebraic factors and factors from the number of neighbours. Hybridizing many such localized states produces an [impurity band](../../../../impurity-band.md) with a [bandwidth](../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) of the same scale:

$$
\boxed{W\sim E_b\exp\left(-\alpha\frac{n^{-1/3}}{a_b}\right).}
$$

The order-one constant $\alpha$ absorbs details of the donor spacing and envelope. The exponential dependence expresses [quantum tunnelling](../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) between weakly overlapping donor orbitals, rather than thermal excitation over the donor binding [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
