<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Undoped [gallium arsenide](../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) is expected to be **diamagnetic** at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Its [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) is filled and its [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md) empty, so there are no mobile [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md) [electrons](../../../../electron.md) to give [Pauli paramagnetism](../../../../pauli-paramagnetism.md). The specified absence of localized [magnetic moments](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) removes a [Curie law](../../../../curie-s-law.md) response and the usual mechanism for [ferromagnetic ordering](../../../../ferromagnetic-ordering.md). The remaining induced orbital response is [diamagnetism](../../../../diamagnetism.md). This classification uses the ordinary weak-field [band insulator](../../../../band-insulator.md) picture; the [band gap](../../../../band-gap.md) suppresses thermally generated carriers as $T\to0$.
