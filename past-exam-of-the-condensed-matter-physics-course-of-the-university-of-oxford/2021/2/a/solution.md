<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Bravais lattice](../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md). Four inequivalent representatives of its [lattice points](../../../../lattice-point.md) in the conventional cubic [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md), in [fractional crystal coordinates](../../../../fractional-crystal-coordinate.md), are

$$
(0,0,0),\qquad\left(0,\frac12,\frac12\right),\qquad
\left(\frac12,0,\frac12\right),\qquad
\left(\frac12,\frac12,0\right).
$$

Boundary copies in the drawing do not constitute additional independent [lattice points](../../../../lattice-point.md). Attach the two-atom [atomic basis](../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Al}:(0,0,0),\qquad
\mathrm{P}:\left(\frac14,\frac14,\frac14\right)}
$$

to each [lattice point](../../../../lattice-point.md). The resulting [aluminium phosphide](../../../../aluminium-phosphide.md) [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md) is the [zinc blende structure](../../../../zinc-blende-structure.md). For example, the four [phosphorus](../../../../phosphorus.md) positions reduced into the conventional [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md) are $(1/4,1/4,1/4)$, $(1/4,3/4,3/4)$, $(3/4,1/4,3/4)$, and $(3/4,3/4,1/4)$, reproducing the heights in the source drawing. There are four [aluminium](../../../../aluminium.md) and four [phosphorus](../../../../phosphorus.md) [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) per conventional [unit cell](../../../../unit-cell.md), but only two [chemical atoms](../../../../atom-physics.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md).
