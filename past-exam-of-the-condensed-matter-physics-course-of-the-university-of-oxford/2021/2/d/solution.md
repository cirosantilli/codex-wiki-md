<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the long-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) linear [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega\simeq v_L|\mathbf k|$ as an estimate. The [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is then

$$
E=\hbar\omega\simeq\hbar v_L\frac{2\pi}{a}
=\frac{h(8433\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})}{0.5431\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm m},
\qquad
\boxed{E\simeq0.0642\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

This extrapolates the measured low-[wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) [speed of sound](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) to the $X$ point, the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md) boundary along $[100]$. It is not an exact [phonon dispersion relation](../../../../phonon-dispersion-relation.md) there: the discrete [crystal structure](../../../../crystal-structure.md) generally bends the branch, and the initial slope alone cannot determine its boundary [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In particular, $(2\pi/a,0,0)$ is not a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) of the [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md); the smallest such axial vector is $(4\pi/a,0,0)$. There is therefore no reason for this [acoustic phonon](../../../../acoustic-phonon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to vanish.
