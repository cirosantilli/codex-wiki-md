<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are two [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) in the [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md), hence six displacement coordinates and six [phonon branches](../../../../../phonon-branch.md) at each [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md). Three rigid [translations](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) cost no restoring [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) in the long-[wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) limit. Consequently

$$
\boxed{3\text{ acoustic phonon branches}.}
$$

For propagation along $[100]$, one [acoustic phonon](../../../../../acoustic-phonon.md) has [longitudinal polarization](../../../../../longitudinal-polarization.md) along $x$ and two have [transverse polarization](../../../../../transverse-polarization.md) along $y,z$. The cubic [crystal symmetry](../../../../../crystal-symmetry.md) makes those two transverse directions equivalent. In terms of the [elastic stiffness tensor](../../../../../elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) and the mass [density](../../../../../density.md),

$$
v_L=\sqrt{\frac{C_{11}}{\rho_m}},\qquad
v_{T,y}=v_{T,z}=\sqrt{\frac{C_{44}}{\rho_m}}.
$$

Thus there are **two distinct acoustic velocities** in general, one longitudinal and a doubly degenerate transverse value. This [degeneracy of normal modes](../../../../../degeneracy-of-normal-modes.md) concerns the two [transverse polarizations](../../../../../transverse-polarization.md); it does not imply equality with the longitudinal [speed of sound](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md).
