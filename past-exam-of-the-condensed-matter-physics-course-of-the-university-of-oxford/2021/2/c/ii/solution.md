↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtracting the three [acoustic phonon branches](../../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md) from the six displacement [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) leaves

$$
\boxed{3\text{ optical phonon branches}.}
$$

At small [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md), these involve relative motion of the two [atomic basis](../../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) sites rather than a rigid [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) of the [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md). Their [angular frequencies](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) remain nonzero at the zone centre. The count uses the [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md); using all eight [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) in the conventional [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md) would spuriously multiply the number of distinct branches in the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md).
