<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the degenerate [electron gas](../../../../electron-gas.md), let $D_g=Am/(\pi\hbar^2)$ include both [spin](../../../../spin.md) states. The [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives

$$
U_g(T)-U_g(0)=\frac{\pi^2}{6}D_g(k_BT)^2,\qquad
C_v=\frac{\pi^2}{3}D_gk_B^2T.
$$

For the gapless symmetric [intrinsic semiconductor](../../../../intrinsic-semiconductor.md), part (d) gave the same coefficient with $D_s=Am^*/(\pi\hbar^2)$. Thus for equal sample areas and equal [spin](../../../../spin.md) and [valley degeneracies](../../../../valley-degeneracy.md),

$$
\boxed{\frac{C_v^{\rm tot}}{C_v}=\frac{m^*}{m};\qquad
\frac{C_v^{\rm tot}}{C_v}=1\text{ if }m^*=m.}
$$

The equality often intended in this comparison requires the same [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md). The source sets $m_e^*=m_h^*$ but does not explicitly equate them to the [electron gas](../../../../electron-gas.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$. Without that additional assumption the ratio is $m^*/m$, or more generally $D_s/D_g$ if sample areas or [degeneracy factors](../../../../degeneracy-factor.md) differ. For example, $m^*=2m$ with equal areas satisfies the stated equal-[effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) condition in the [semiconductor](../../../../semiconductor-split.md) and gives ratio two. The filled [Fermi sea](../../../../fermi-sea.md) does not add a factor $N_e/N_c$: only the thermal shell near its [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md) contributes to its [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md).
