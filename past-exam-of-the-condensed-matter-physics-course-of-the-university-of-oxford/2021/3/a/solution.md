<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [harmonic approximation](../../../../harmonic-approximation.md), each in-plane displacement coordinate contributes one quadratic [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) term and one quadratic restoring [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) term. The [equipartition theorem](../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) assigns $k_BT/2$ to each. There are two such coordinates per [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md), hence

$$
U\simeq2N_ak_BT,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{C_v}{N_a}\longrightarrow2k_B.}
$$

Here the fixed-size [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) is at constant area in this two-dimensional model. On cooling, [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) excitation freezes out and the [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) decreases. For each nonzero [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md), the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) contributes

$$
c_\omega=k_B\frac{x^2e^x}{(e^x-1)^2},\qquad x=\frac{\hbar\omega}{k_BT},
$$

which is below $k_B$ at finite $x$ and approaches zero as $T\to0$. Two linearly dispersing in-plane [acoustic phonon branches](../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md) give the familiar two-dimensional [Debye model](../../../../debye-model.md) scaling $C_v\propto T^2$ at sufficiently low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Out-of-plane modes are excluded by the specified model.
