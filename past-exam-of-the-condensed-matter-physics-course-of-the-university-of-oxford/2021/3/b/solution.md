<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a nondegenerate two-dimensional [electron gas](../../../../electron-gas.md), the two quadratic momentum terms supply $k_BT/2$ each by the [equipartition theorem](../../../../equipartition-theorem.md). There is no lattice restoring [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) for a free [electron](../../../../electron.md). Thus

$$
U=N_ek_BT,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{C_v}{N_e}\longrightarrow k_B.}
$$

On cooling below the [Fermi temperature](../../../../fermi-temperature.md), the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) restricts the thermally active [electrons](../../../../electron.md) to within approximately $k_BT$ of the [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md). The [linear low-temperature heat capacity of a two-dimensional Fermi gas](../../../../linear-low-temperature-heat-capacity-of-a-two-dimensional-fermi-gas.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{C_v}{N_e}\simeq\frac{\pi^2}{3}k_B\frac{k_BT}{E_F}\longrightarrow0.}
$$

The [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) therefore decreases from its classical value as [quantum degeneracy](../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) becomes important. The [particle number](../../../../particle-number.md) of [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and sample area are held fixed when differentiating the [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md).
