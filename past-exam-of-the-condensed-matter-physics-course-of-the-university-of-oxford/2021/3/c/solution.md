<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let the sample area be $A$, set the band touching [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to zero, and write $m^*=m_e^*=m_h^*$. Assume one [electronic valley](../../../../electronic-valley.md) in each [energy band](../../../../energy-band.md) with two [spin](../../../../spin.md) states. The near-edge [parabolic dispersions](../../../../parabolic-band-dispersion.md) are

$$
E_c(k)=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m^*},\qquad E_v(k)=-\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m^*}.
$$

Under [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md), each [spin](../../../../spin.md) occupies reciprocal-space area $(2\pi)^2/A$. Counting the [quantum states](../../../../quantum-state.md) inside a circle gives

$$
\mathcal N_c(E)=2\frac{A}{(2\pi)^2}\pi k^2
=\frac{Am^*}{\pi\hbar^2}E\qquad(E>0).
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is the constant [two-dimensional free-electron density of states](../../../../two-dimensional-free-electron-density-of-states.md). Defining $D=Am^*/(\pi\hbar^2)$, the signed-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) is

$$
\boxed{g_c(E)=D\Theta(E),\qquad g_v(E)=D\Theta(-E).}
$$

The total electronic [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) is therefore $D$ on either side of zero, not $2D$ at every signed [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). If instead one counts positive-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) excitations, the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [hole](../../../../electron-hole.md) species each have [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) $D$, giving $2D$ when summed. A common additional [valley degeneracy](../../../../valley-degeneracy.md) multiplies all these quantities by that factor. The [effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) approximation assumes thermally sampled [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) remain within the near-edge regions of both [energy bands](../../../../energy-band.md).
