# Complex scalar charge operator

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a complex scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

For the global phase transformation $\delta\phi=-i\alpha\phi$, the [Noether current](noether-current.md) is $j^\mu=i(\phi^\dagger\partial^\mu\phi-\partial^\mu\phi^\dagger\,\phi)$. The [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) gives $\partial_\mu j^\mu=0$. With zero vacuum charge, its [normal-ordered](normal-ordering.md) charge is $Q=\int d\Pi_p(a^\dagger a-b^\dagger b)$. Thus $[Q,a^\dagger]=a^\dagger$, $[Q,b^\dagger]=-b^\dagger$ and $[Q,\phi]=-\phi$. Particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) creation carry opposite charges while both increase energy.
