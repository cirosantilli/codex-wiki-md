# Isoperimetric inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoperimetric_inequality)

The [isoperimetric inequality](isoperimetric-inequality.md) bounds enclosed volume in terms of boundary size. In the plane it gives $4\pi A\leq L^2$, with equality for disks; higher-dimensional forms bound the boundary area of a region of prescribed volume.
