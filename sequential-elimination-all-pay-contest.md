# Sequential elimination all-pay contest

↑ **Parent:** [Contest theory](contest-theory.md)

A finite sequence of [all-pay contests](all-pay-auction.md) in which each stage awards one prize and its winner leaves. Losing players remain eligible for later stages. A player can receive at most one prize, while its effort costs are incurred whenever it participates. [Subgame perfect equilibrium](subgame-perfect-equilibrium.md) accounts for both the immediate prize and the value of remaining eligible.
