# Gaussian angular-momentum suppression in a stellar distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

In a spherical [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), use binding energy $E=\psi-v^2/2$ and [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $L$. The displayed stationary [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) has locally Gaussian radial and transverse velocities: $\sigma_r^2=\sigma^2$, $\sigma_\theta^2=\sigma_\phi^2=\sigma^2/(1+r^2/r_a^2)$. Its [mass density](density.md) is $\rho=\rho_c e^{[\psi(r)-\psi(0)]/\sigma^2}/(1+r^2/r_a^2)$. The [velocity-anisotropy parameter](velocity-anisotropy-parameter.md) is $\beta_{\mathrm{an}}=r^2/(r^2+r_a^2)$, so the core is nearly isotropic and the halo nearly radial. These exact Gaussian integrals use the untruncated distribution over all velocities; imposing an escape-energy cutoff changes them.
