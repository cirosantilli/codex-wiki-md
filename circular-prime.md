# Circular prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number-split.md)

A [prime number](prime-number-split.md) for which every cyclic rotation of its [numerical digits](numerical-digit.md) is also a [prime number](prime-number-split.md). In [decimal representation](decimal-representation.md), $197$ is an example because $197,971,719$ are all [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md). A multi-digit [circular prime](circular-prime.md) can contain only $1,3,7,9$: any other digit can be rotated into the units position and force [divisibility](divisibility-split.md) by $2$ or $5$.
