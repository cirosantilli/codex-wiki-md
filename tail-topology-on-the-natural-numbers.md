# Tail topology on the natural numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

The nonempty open sets are the upper tails of the [natural numbers](natural-number.md). Arbitrary nonempty unions select the smallest starting index, and finite intersections select the largest, so these sets form a [topology](topology-split.md). A map into this space is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when the inverse image of every upper tail is open.
