# Hermite oscillator quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Hermite differential equation](hermite-differential-equation.md)

A solution of $b''+(\Lambda-\xi^2)b=0$ decaying at both real infinities has $b=e^{-\xi^2/2}H_n(\xi)$ with a [Hermite polynomial](hermite-polynomial.md). Substitution gives $H''-2\xi H'+(\Lambda-1)H=0$; its power-series recurrence terminates at degree $n$ when $\Lambda=2n+1$. The square-integrable [Hermite functions](hermite-function.md) give the complete oscillator spectrum, including the ground state $n=0$. This quantization also determines [global modes of a quadratically confined Ginzburg-Landau equation](global-modes-of-a-quadratically-confined-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) after removing drift.
