<h1 id="robinson-schensted-correspondence">Robinson–Schensted correspondence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Young tableau](young-tableau.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson–Schensted_correspondence)

Insert the successive letters of a [permutation](permutation.md) by [row insertion](row-insertion.md) to obtain an insertion tableau $P$, recording the insertion times in the new boxes of $Q$. This is a [bijection](bijection.md) between permutations and pairs of [standard Young tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md) of a common shape. Reverse insertion removes the box marked by the latest recording time. The first row of $P$ has length equal to the [longest increasing subsequence](longest-increasing-subsequence.md).
