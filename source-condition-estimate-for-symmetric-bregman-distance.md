# Source-condition estimate for symmetric Bregman distance

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric Bregman distance](symmetric-bregman-distance.md)

Let $K:U\to V$ be a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) from a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) to a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), let $J$ be a proper lower-semicontinuous [convex function](convex-function.md), and let $\alpha>0$ and $\|f^\delta-Ku^\dagger\|\leq\delta$. For an existing minimizer of $\|Ku-f^\delta\|^2/2+\alpha J(u)$ and a [source condition in variational regularization](source-condition-in-variational-regularization.md) $p^\dagger=K^*w\in\partial J(u^\dagger)$, let $e=f^\delta-Ku^\dagger$ and $q=K(u_\alpha-u^\dagger)$. Its optimality [subgradient](subgradient.md) is $p_\alpha=K^*(f^\delta-Ku_\alpha)/\alpha$. Then $\alpha D_J^{\mathrm{sym}}=\langle e-\alpha w,q\rangle-\|q\|^2\leq\|e-\alpha w\|^2/4$. The displayed estimate follows from $\|e-\alpha w\|^2\leq2\delta^2+2\alpha^2\|w\|^2$.
