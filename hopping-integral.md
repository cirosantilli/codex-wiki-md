# Hopping integral

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

A [hopping integral](hopping-integral.md) is an off-diagonal [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) matrix element between localized orbitals on different lattice sites. It has units of energy and controls the dispersion in a [tight-binding model](tight-binding.md). Its sign and phase depend on the orbital phase convention; observable band energies do not. The sign of a real nearest-neighbour $t$ in $E(k)=E_0+2t\cos(ka)$ determines the location of the minimum in that convention.
