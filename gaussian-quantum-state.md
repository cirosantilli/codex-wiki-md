# Gaussian quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A bosonic state with Gaussian phase-space characteristic function, specified by its first and second [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) moments. Vacuum, [coherent states](coherent-state.md), [optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md) and thermal states are examples. Gaussian here describes phase-space statistics, not a Gaussian [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md) envelope.
