# Michelson-Morley experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Tests of special relativity](tests-of-special-relativity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelson–Morley_experiment)

An [interferometer](interferometer.md) compares round-trip [light](light.md) travel times along perpendicular arms as its orientation changes. The absence of the classical stationary-aether fringe shift at the expected scale constrains orientation dependence of the round-trip [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). The result is consistent with [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md), but alone does not select it uniquely from every alternative theory.[1887 report, interferometer arrangement and comparison of predicted and observed orientation-dependent shifts](on-the-relative-motion-of-the-earth-and-the-luminiferous-ether-by-albert-abraham-michelson-and-edward-w-morley.md)
