# Two-wire capacitance at large separation

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance per unit length](capacitance-per-unit-length.md)

Replace two well-separated cylindrical conductors by opposite axial [line charges](line-charge.md). Their surface-to-surface potential difference is $\lambda[\ln(d/a_1)+\ln(d/a_2)]/(2\pi\epsilon)$, giving the displayed [capacitance per unit length](capacitance-per-unit-length.md). The approximation needs $d\gg a_1,a_2$ and a homogeneous surrounding [permittivity](permittivity-split.md).
