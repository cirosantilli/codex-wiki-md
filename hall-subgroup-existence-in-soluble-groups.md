# Hall subgroup existence in soluble groups

↑ **Parent:** [Hall subgroup](hall-subgroup.md)

Every finite [soluble group](solvable-group.md) has a [Hall subgroup](hall-subgroup.md) for each prime set. Induct on the group order using an elementary abelian [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md). In the coprime hard case, lift a minimal normal subgroup of the quotient, choose its Sylow subgroup, and apply the [Frattini argument](frattini-argument.md). A proper normalizer reduces the order; a normal Sylow subgroup allows induction in its quotient. This proves existence without assuming an independent complement theorem.
