# Burke theorem for a multi-server queue

↑ **Parent:** [M-M-s queue](m-m-s-queue.md)

A stable equilibrium [M-M-s queue](m-m-s-queue.md) has a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of departures with the same rate as its input. Its present population is also independent of past departures, a component of [quasireversibility](quasireversibility.md). The [birth-death process](birth-death-process.md) satisfies [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md); reversed upward jumps have a constant arrival rate, so they are generated by an independent [Poisson process](poisson-process.md).
