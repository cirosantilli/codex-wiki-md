# Ideal chain

↑ **Parent:** [Biopolymer mechanics](biopolymer-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_chain)

An [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md), also called a [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md), consists of fixed-length links with independent orientations and no bending energy between successive links. It neglects segment interactions and excluded volume. A [one-dimensional freely jointed chain](one-dimensional-freely-jointed-chain.md) restricts each link to two orientations. At fixed force its [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) factors into one-link angular integrals. The resulting extension is entropic and depends on [temperature](temperature.md) and on whether the orientations are allowed in one, two, or three dimensions.
