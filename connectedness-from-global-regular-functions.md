# Connectedness from global regular functions

↑ **Parent:** [Connected space](connected-space.md)

If a nonempty [scheme](scheme.md) $X$ has no nontrivial [idempotent](idempotent.md) in $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, then its underlying [topological space](topological-space.md) is [connected](connected-space.md). Indeed, a decomposition into two nonempty clopen subsets would produce the global regular function equal to zero on one component and one on the other. In particular, $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ being a [field](field.md) implies connectedness.
