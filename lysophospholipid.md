# Lysophospholipid

↑ **Parent:** [Phospholipid](phospholipid.md)

A [lysophospholipid](lysophospholipid.md) is a phospholipid derivative missing an acyl chain relative to its corresponding diacyl form. The changed headgroup-to-tail geometry can alter curvature preference and bilayer stability.
