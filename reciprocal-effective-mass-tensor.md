# Reciprocal effective mass tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md)

The curvature tensor of a nondegenerate [energy band](energy-band.md), determining the linear relation between force and acceleration in semiclassical band dynamics. From [group velocity](group-velocity.md) $v_i=\hbar^{-1}\partial_{k_i}E$ and $\hbar\dot k_j=F_j$, one obtains $\dot v_i=(M^{-1})_{ij}F_j$. Near a parabolic minimum, $E-E_0=\hbar^2\delta\mathbf k^TM^{-1}\delta\mathbf k/2$. The principal values are reciprocal curvature [effective masses](band-effective-mass.md); a cyclotron experiment measures a direction-dependent combination rather than every tensor entry separately.
