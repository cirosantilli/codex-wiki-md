# Ion-channel deactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

Closure of an activation gate when the activating stimulus is removed. It is distinct from [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md), which reduces channel availability while the activating conditions can remain present.
