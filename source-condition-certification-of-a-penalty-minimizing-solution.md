# Source-condition certification of a penalty-minimizing solution

↑ **Parent:** [Source condition in variational regularization](source-condition-in-variational-regularization.md)

If $Au=f$ and $A^*\mu\in\partial J(u)$, then $u$ is a [J-minimizing solution](j-minimizing-solution.md). For every other exact solution $v$, the [subgradient inequality](subgradient-inequality.md) gives $J(v)\geq J(u)+\langle\mu,Av-Au\rangle=J(u)$. This is a certificate of global optimality supplied by the range of the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md).
