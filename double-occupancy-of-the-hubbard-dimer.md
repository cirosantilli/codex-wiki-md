# Double occupancy of the Hubbard dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Double occupancy](double-occupancy.md)

The ground-state [probability](probability.md) of both [electrons](electron.md) occupying one site is $P_{\rm double}=\tfrac12[1-U/\sqrt{U^2+16t^2}]$. It equals the derivative of the relative ground energy with respect to $U$. It tends to zero for $|t|/U\to0$ and to $1/2$ for $|t|/U\to\infty$.
