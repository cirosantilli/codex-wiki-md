# Retrovirus

↑ **Parent:** [Virus](virus.md)

A [retrovirus](retrovirus.md) replicates through reverse transcription of its RNA into DNA, followed by a DNA stage that can integrate into the host genome. Viral gene expression can then use cellular transcription machinery.
