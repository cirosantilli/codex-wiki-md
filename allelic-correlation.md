# Allelic correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md)

For two segregating biallelic loci, encode the allele on a random population [haplotype](haplotype.md) by indicator variables $X,Y$. Their [covariance](covariance.md) is the [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) coefficient $D$. The squared [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is $r^2=D^2/[p_A(1-p_A)p_B(1-p_B)]$, between zero and one. It measures how well one marker predicts the other and is undefined if either marker is monomorphic.
