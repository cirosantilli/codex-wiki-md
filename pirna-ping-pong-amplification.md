# piRNA ping-pong amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Piwi-interacting RNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md)

Reciprocal guide-directed cleavage produces new [piRNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md) ends from complementary precursor or target [RNAs](ribonucleic-acid.md). A 10-nucleotide overlap of opposite-strand 5′ ends supports this mechanism but does not prove amplification in every [piRNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md) pathway.
