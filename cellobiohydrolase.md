# Cellobiohydrolase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [cellobiohydrolase](cellobiohydrolase.md) releases cellobiose units from glucan chains, often through processive cleavage at an accessible chain end. Chain binding and extraction from an ordered substrate can limit activity.
