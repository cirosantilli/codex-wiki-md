# Sign of a lift of a Legendre parameter transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre parameter orbit](legendre-parameter-orbit.md)

For the [Legendre form of an elliptic curve](legendre-form-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) $y^2=x(x-1)(x-\lambda)$, a change in the $x$ coordinate that permutes its branch values lifts with either of two opposite signs in the $y$ coordinate. These lifts differ by the involution $(x,y)\mapsto(x,-y)$. At $\lambda=e^{\pi i/3}$ the parameter transformation $x\mapsto1-x/\lambda$ has order three. Its lift fixing $y$ has order three, whereas its lift negating $y$ has order six, since its cube is the nontrivial involution. Thus the order must be computed for a specified lift; both imply the same hexagonal shape of the associated [period lattice](period-lattice.md).
