# Cooperative cost game

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

A [transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md) formulated as costs $c(S)$ to be shared among members of each [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) $S$, with $c(\varnothing)=0$. Its cost [core](core-game-theory.md) consists of payment [vectors](vector.md) $p$ satisfying the displayed efficiency and coalition inequalities, where $p(S)=\sum_{i\in S}p_i$. A coalition should not be charged more than its standalone cost. Changing signs to $v=-c$ and $x=-p$ converts these conditions to the usual benefit-game [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md). The [Shapley value](shapley-value.md) can be applied directly to costs, giving payments whose sum is $c(N)$.
