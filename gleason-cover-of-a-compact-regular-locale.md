# Gleason cover of a compact regular locale

↑ **Parent:** [Extremally disconnected locale](extremally-disconnected-locale.md)

For a compact [regular locale](regular-locale.md) $X$, put $B=\mathcal O(X)_{\neg\neg}$ and $\mathcal O(\gamma X)=\operatorname{Idl}(B)$. This [locale](locale.md) is extremally disconnected. Its natural surjection to $X$ has inverse image

$$
a\longmapsto\bigvee_{u\prec a}\mathord\downarrow(\neg\neg u).
$$

The [Joyal extension lemma](joyal-extension-lemma.md) gives the [frame homomorphism](frame-homomorphism.md); its injectivity follows by recovering $a$ as the join of the regular opens in this ideal.
