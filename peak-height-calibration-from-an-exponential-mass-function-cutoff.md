# Peak-height calibration from an exponential mass-function cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md)

Defining characteristic mass by an exponential factor $\exp[-(M/M_{h,\rm ch})^{2\beta}]$ fixes its peak height to $\nu=\sqrt2$, rather than one. Hence $\sigma(M_{h,\rm ch},z)=\delta_{\rm sc}/\sqrt2$ and the variance at other masses follows the same power-law scaling. The precise characteristic-mass convention determines this factor.
