# Inverting operational amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal operational amplifier](ideal-operational-amplifier.md)

Connect a [resistor](resistor.md) $R_{\rm in}$ from the input source to the inverting input, a feedback [resistor](resistor.md) $R_f$ from output to that same input, and ground the non-inverting input. For ideal linear operation the inverting node is a [virtual ground](virtual-ground.md). [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) gives $V_i/R_{\rm in}+V_o/R_f=0$, yielding the displayed voltage gain. The source sees [input impedance](input-impedance.md) $R_{\rm in}$, even though the amplifier input itself has infinite [input impedance](input-impedance.md).
