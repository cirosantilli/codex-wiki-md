# Surface-wave tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic tomography](seismic-tomography.md)

[Surface-wave tomography](surface-wave-tomography.md) combines path-dependent measurements of [seismic surface-wave dispersion](seismic-surface-wave-dispersion.md) to infer lateral and depth variation in elastic properties. Joint [Rayleigh wave](rayleigh-wave.md) and [Love wave](love-wave.md) measurements can help constrain [seismic anisotropy](seismic-anisotropy.md).
