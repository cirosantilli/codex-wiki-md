# Hypercohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercohomology)

[Hypercohomology](hypercohomology.md) applies the derived global-section functor to a complex of sheaves. One may resolve the complex by an [injective](injective-function.md) double complex, take [global sections](global-section.md), and take the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of its total complex. For a complex concentrated in degree zero it is ordinary [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md). The [relative de Rham complex](relative-de-rham-complex.md) uses this construction to compute fibre [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) and its [Hodge filtration](hodge-filtration.md).
