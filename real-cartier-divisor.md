# Real Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

A real Cartier divisor is a finite real linear combination of [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md). A rational Cartier divisor is defined analogously with rational coefficients; after multiplication by a common denominator it is Cartier.
