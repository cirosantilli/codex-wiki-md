# Texture averaging of irradiation growth

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiation growth](irradiation-growth.md)

An ideal orientation average $\overline{\boldsymbol\varepsilon}=\sum_g v_gR_g\boldsymbol\varepsilon^{(g)}R_g^T$. Random orientation averages a traceless single-grain growth tensor to zero, while [crystallographic texture](crystallographic-texture.md) can retain a directional bulk [strain](strain.md). [crystal grain](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) constraints can modify this simple independent-grain estimate.
