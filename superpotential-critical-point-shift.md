# Superpotential critical-point shift

↑ **Parent:** [Superpotential](superpotential.md)

For a canonical [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md), an affine translation $\Phi=\Psi+s$ preserves the kinetic action up to a vanishing full-superspace term. It removes the linear [superpotential](superpotential.md) term exactly when $s$ is a critical point. For $W=\alpha+\kappa\Phi+m\Phi^2/2+g\Phi^3/6$, the shifted quadratic coefficient is $M=m+gs$ with $M^2=m^2-2g\kappa$. A real shift requires a real root; the complex field permits a complex shift as well.
