# Morphism of algebraic curves

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

A [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) whose source and target are [algebraic curves](algebraic-curve.md). A nonconstant morphism between [smooth projective curves](smooth-projective-curve.md) is finite and surjective; its degree is the degree of the induced extension of [function fields](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md).
