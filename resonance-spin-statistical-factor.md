# Resonance spin statistical factor

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

For unpolarized entrance particles, average over their $(2J_a+1)(2J_b+1)$ [spin](spin.md) orientations and sum over the isolated resonance's $2J_R+1$ magnetic substates. The resulting weight multiplies the partial-wave [cross section](cross-section-physics.md). It can exceed one because orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) participates; it is not a standalone probability of forming a state. Polarization or several unresolved resonance [spins](spin.md) requires more detailed channel counting.
