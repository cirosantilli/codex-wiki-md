# Component quotient of the real Lorentz group in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md)

Let $B(x)=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh x&-\sinh x\\-\sinh x&\cosh x\end{pmatrix}$, $T=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and $P=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$. Every real matrix preserving $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ has a unique form $B(x)S$ with $S\in\{I,T,P,TP\}$. The boost subgroup is normal because $TB(x)T=PB(x)P=B(-x)$. Its four [cosets](coset.md) form the [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md), giving the displayed [quotient group](quotient-group.md). In contrast, the four-element subgroup generated by $T,P$ is not normal: $B(x)TB(-x)=B(2x)T$ is not diagonal for $x\ne0$. This distinguishes the normal identity component from a chosen set of component representatives.
