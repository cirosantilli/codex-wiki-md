# Peierls distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Peierls transition](peierls-transition.md)

A [Peierls distortion](peierls-distortion.md) modulates the atomic positions and [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) of a one-dimensional chain with a partly filled [energy band](energy-band.md). In the half-filled example, alternating short and long bonds double the [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md). The distortion is the structural change; the associated [Peierls transition](peierls-transition.md) is the change in electronic conduction.[Entry PT07442: bond alternation caused by distortion of a partially occupied one-dimensional structure](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/peierls-distortion.md)
