# Toric lens

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)

An [optical lens](lens-optics.md) with different refractive powers in two principal meridians. Correcting [regular ocular astigmatism](regular-ocular-astigmatism.md) requires both the power difference and its orientation.
