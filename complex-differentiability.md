# Complex differentiability

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md)

A complex-valued function is complex differentiable at a point if the displayed limit exists for arbitrary nonzero complex increments approaching zero. It is stronger than having directional derivatives along the real and imaginary axes: all approaches must agree. The two axis limits imply the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md). A function complex differentiable at every point of an open set is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) there.
