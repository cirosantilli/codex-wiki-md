# Nonspecific treatment effect

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

An effect attributed to influences shared across interventions, such as [attention](attention.md) or expectations, relative to the mechanism under investigation. Shared does not mean inert, and matching session counts does not establish that every such influence is controlled.
