# Borel probability measure

↑ **Parent:** [Borel measure](borel-measure.md)

A Borel probability measure is a [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) with total mass one. On a compact [metric space](metric-space.md) it is determined by its integrals against continuous functions. On that compact [metric space](metric-space.md), the family of [Borel probability measures](borel-probability-measure.md) is compact for [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md).
