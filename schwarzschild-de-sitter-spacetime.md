# Schwarzschild-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzschild-de_Sitter_spacetime)

The Schwarzschild-de Sitter spacetime is the spherically symmetric vacuum solution with positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md). In static coordinates its metric function is $f(r)=1-2m/r-H^2r^2$, with a black-hole horizon and a cosmological horizon when $mH<1/\sqrt{27}$.
