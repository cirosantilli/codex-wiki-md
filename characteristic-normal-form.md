# Characteristic normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic coordinate](characteristic-coordinate.md)

For a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) in two variables, two independent [characteristic coordinates](characteristic-coordinate.md) remove both pure second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md), leaving a nonzero mixed second [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md). A nonzero [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is essential: two characteristic invariants that become dependent do not give a valid [coordinate transformation](smooth-transition-map.md).
