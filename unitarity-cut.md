# Unitarity cut

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)

A contribution to the [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) relation for the [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) obtained by placing a set of intermediate lines [on shell](on-shell.md) and summing their intermediate states. In a physical-state formulation only physical states enter the sum. When covariant [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) index sums include auxiliary polarizations, matching [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) contributions are needed to reproduce the physical result in a [Non-Abelian gauge theory](yang-mills-theory.md).
