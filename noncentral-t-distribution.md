# Noncentral t-distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Student's t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncentral_t-distribution)

If $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and $U\sim\chi^2_\nu$ are [independent](independent-random-variables.md), then $(Z+\delta)/\sqrt{U/\nu}$ has the noncentral t-distribution with [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) $\nu$ and noncentrality $\delta$. It describes the [test statistic](test-statistic.md) of a [Student's t-test](student-s-t-test.md) under a shifted alternative.
