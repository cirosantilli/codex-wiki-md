# G2 (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G2_(mathematics))

$G_2$ denotes an exceptional [Lie group](lie-group.md) and its associated [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), of rank two and dimension fourteen. Its roots form the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md). The compact real form is the [automorphism group](automorphism-group.md) of the octonion algebra; other real forms and the complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) have the same complex root-system type.
