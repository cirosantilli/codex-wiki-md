# Identity for Dirichlet convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md)

The identity for [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) is $\varepsilon(1)=1$ and $\varepsilon(n)=0$ for $n>1$. It satisfies $f*\varepsilon=f$ for every [arithmetic function](arithmetic-function.md) $f$.
