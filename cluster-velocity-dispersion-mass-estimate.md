# Cluster velocity-dispersion mass estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)

The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) balances a relaxed [galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)'s orbital [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), of order $M\sigma_v^2$, against its [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md), of order $-GM^2/R$. Their ratio gives the displayed mass scale. The numerical coefficient depends on the density profile, radius definition and conversion from line-of-sight [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) to three-dimensional orbital motion. Membership contamination, anisotropy and nonequilibrium dynamics can bias the inference.
