# Aerosol coagulation

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

Growth by collision and attachment of existing particles. Coagulation reduces particle number while conserving particulate material in a closed system without other sources or sinks. Larger particles can have a different scattering-per-mass efficiency and faster [sedimentation](sedimentation.md), so increasing precursor injection need not increase [albedo](albedo.md) proportionally.
