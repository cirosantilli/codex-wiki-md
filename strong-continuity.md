# Strong continuity

↑ **Parent:** [C0-semigroup](c0-semigroup.md)

An operator family $U(t)$ is strongly continuous when $t\mapsto U(t)x$ is norm-continuous for every vector $x$. This is continuity in the [strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md).
