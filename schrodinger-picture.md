<h1 id="schrodinger-picture">Schrödinger picture</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md)

In the Schrödinger picture, states evolve with the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) while observables without explicit time dependence are fixed. For time-independent $H$, the corresponding [Heisenberg picture](heisenberg-picture.md) operator is $O_H(t)=e^{iHt/\hbar}O_Se^{-iHt/\hbar}$.
