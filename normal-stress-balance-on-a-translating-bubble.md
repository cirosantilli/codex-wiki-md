# Normal stress balance on a translating bubble

↑ **Parent:** [Translating surfactant-coated bubble](translating-surfactant-coated-bubble.md)

The dipolar normal [traction](traction.md) is balanced by buoyant [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) and the concentration-dependent normal capillary stress, in addition to the constant gas pressure and equilibrium Laplace pressure. A degree-one shape perturbation translates a sphere rather than deforming it; the balance fixes the terminal speed. A uniform gas pressure alone cannot balance this mode.
