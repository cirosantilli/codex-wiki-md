# Thermal neutron

↑ **Parent:** [Slow neutron](slow-neutron.md)

A thermal [neutron](neutron-split.md) belongs to a population approximately in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with its surroundings. Such a population has a distribution of [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md), not one fixed [energy](energy-split.md). The scale $k_B T$ uses the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) and [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md); it is about $0.0259\,\mathrm{eV}$ at $300\,\mathrm K$. The mean translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) for a classical three-dimensional equilibrium population is $3k_B T/2$. Not every [slow neutron](slow-neutron.md) has reached this equilibrium.
