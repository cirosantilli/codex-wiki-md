# Beta integral

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function](beta-function.md)

For positive [real numbers](real-number.md) $a,b$, the beta integral represents the [beta function](beta-function.md) by $B(a,b)=\int_0^1x^{a-1}(1-x)^{b-1}\,dx$. In particular, for [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) $m,n$, it gives $\int_0^1x^m(1-x)^n\,dx=m!n!/(m+n+1)!$, connecting [integration](integral-split.md) to [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient-split.md).
