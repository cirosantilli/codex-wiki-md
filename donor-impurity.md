# Donor impurity

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

An impurity that can supply an electron to the conduction band. A shallow donor has a localized occupied [impurity level in a crystal](impurity-level-in-a-crystal.md) near and below the conduction-band edge; thermal or other ionization releases its electron. The binding energy depends on the host dielectric response and effective mass.
