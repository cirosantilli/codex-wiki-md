# Fixed-number two-mode condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

All $N$ [bosons](boson.md) occupy the same normalized one-particle superposition with $|C|^2+|S|^2=1$. Its occupation distribution in the original two modes is binomial, not two independent [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md). In particular, $\langle N_1\rangle=N|S|^2$ and $\langle N_0N_1\rangle=N(N-1)|C|^2|S|^2$. These [factorial moments](factorial-moment.md) give finite-particle corrections to interaction [energies](energy-split.md). The state is also the two-mode realization of a spin [coherent state](coherent-state.md) with spin $N/2$.
