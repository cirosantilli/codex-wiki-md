# Boundary loop of a once-punctured torus

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group of a once-punctured torus](fundamental-group-of-a-once-punctured-torus.md)

In the standard [free basis of a group](free-basis-of-a-group.md) $a,b$ for the [fundamental group of a once-punctured torus](fundamental-group-of-a-once-punctured-torus.md), its oriented [boundary loop](boundary-loop.md) represents $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ up to [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) and [conjugation](conjugation.md). The square model proves this: radial [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) takes the inner boundary once around the four sides, whose orientations read $a,b,a^{-1},b^{-1}$. Attaching a [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md) along that boundary kills the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of the [commutator of group elements](group-commutator.md), whenever the hypotheses of the [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) hold.
