# Aromatic nitration

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophilic aromatic substitution](electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.md)

[Aromatic nitration](aromatic-nitration.md) replaces an aromatic [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) with a [nitro group](nitro-group.md). In a common pathway, aromatic attack on a [nitronium ion](nitronium-ion.md) forms a [Wheland intermediate](arenium-ion.md) and [deprotonation](deprotonation.md) restores [aromaticity](aromaticity.md).
