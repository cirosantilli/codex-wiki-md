# Simplicity of a Weyl algebra in characteristic zero

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md)

In a nonzero two-sided ideal choose an element of least [Bernstein filtration](bernstein-filtration.md) degree. Commuting it with any position or differentiation generator lowers degree, so all such commutators vanish. The [ordered monomial basis of a Weyl algebra](ordered-monomial-basis-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) and characteristic zero then force it to be a nonzero scalar. The ideal is the whole ring. The argument fails in positive characteristic, when powers of the generators can be central.
