# Magnetic coercivity

↑ **Parent:** [Hysteresis](hysteresis.md)

[Magnetic coercivity](magnetic-coercivity.md) is the magnitude of a reverse applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) required to bring a previously magnetized specimen's [magnetization](magnetization.md) to zero on a specified [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) branch. It depends on material, preparation and measurement history. It differs from [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md), which is the remaining [magnetization](magnetization.md) at zero applied field.
