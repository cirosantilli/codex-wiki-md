# Spectral filtering of Hamiltonian terms

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian](local-hamiltonian.md)

A real nonnegative normalized filter $w$ defines $g_Z=\int w(t)e^{itH}h_Ze^{-itH}\,dt$. In an energy [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md), it multiplies each matrix element by $\widehat w(E_i-E_j)$. A Fourier cutoff at the [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) removes couplings between a unique [ground state](ground-state.md) and excitations. The filtered terms still sum to $H$, because the total Hamiltonian is unchanged by its own [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md). Each term commutes with the ground projector, but need not be minimized there.
