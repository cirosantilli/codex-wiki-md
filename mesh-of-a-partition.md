# Mesh of a partition

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md)

The mesh of a [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) is its largest subinterval length. A growing number of subintervals does not alone force the mesh to tend to zero: one subinterval could retain a fixed positive length.
