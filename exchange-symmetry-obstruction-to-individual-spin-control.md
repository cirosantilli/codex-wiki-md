# Exchange symmetry obstruction to individual spin control

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency-selective spin control](frequency-selective-spin-control.md)

Let $S$ be the [SWAP gate](swap-gate.md). If the drift [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) and every allowed control [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) commute with $S$, every reachable [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md) also commutes with $S$. Indeed, $SUS$ satisfies the same initial-value [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) as $U$ and starts at $I$, so uniqueness gives $SUS=U$. Yet $S(Z\otimes I)S=I\otimes Z\ne Z\otimes I$. An individual [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) is consequently unavailable within that symmetric control model. Equal nuclear resonances, isotropic [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md), and identical coupling to a common homogeneous [radio frequency](radio-frequency.md) field provide an example. An added interaction that breaks this symmetry can remove the obstruction.
