# Inverse hyperbolic tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic tangent](hyperbolic-tangent.md)

For $|x|<1$, the inverse hyperbolic tangent is

$$
\operatorname{artanh}x=\frac12\log\frac{1+x}{1-x}.
$$
