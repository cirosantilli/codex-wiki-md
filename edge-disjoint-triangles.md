# Edge-disjoint triangles

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md)

A family of [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) is edge-disjoint if no [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) belongs to two members. Any deletion of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) producing a [triangle-free graph](triangle-free-graph.md) must remove at least one distinct [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) for each member. The [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) may share [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
