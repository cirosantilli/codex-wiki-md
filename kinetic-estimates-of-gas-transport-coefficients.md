# Kinetic estimates of gas transport coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

In a dilute [gas](gas.md) whose [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) $\lambda$ is short compared with macroscopic gradient lengths, [particles](particle-physics.md) carry properties from their previous [collisions](collision.md) over distances of order $\lambda$. A first-gradient isotropic estimate gives a transported flux $-n\overline v\lambda\nabla a/3$ for a property $a$ carried per [particle](particle-physics.md). Applying this reasoning to species abundance, directed [momentum](momentum-split.md) $mu_x$ and thermal [energy](energy-split.md) with per-particle [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $c_v$ gives [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D\sim\overline v\lambda/3$, [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\eta\sim nm\overline v\lambda/3$ and [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) $\kappa\sim nc_v\overline v\lambda/3$. These numerical prefactors are estimates, not exact collision-integral results.

For a constant hard-sphere diameter $d$, $\lambda\propto1/(nd^2)$ and $\overline v\propto\sqrt{T/m}$. Consequently $\eta\propto\sqrt{mT}/d^2$, $\kappa\propto c_v\sqrt{T/m}/d^2$ and $D\propto\sqrt{T/m}/(nd^2)$. At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) the last scales as $T^{3/2}/p$. The near pressure-independence of [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) applies only while gas-gas [collisions](collision.md), rather than apparatus walls, determine the [mean free path](mean-free-path.md). Temperature-dependent cross-sections and internal molecular excitations change the simple [power laws](power-law.md).
