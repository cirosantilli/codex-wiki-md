# Sporophyte

↑ **Parent:** [Alternation of generations](alternation-of-generations.md)

The generation developing from a [zygote](zygote.md) and producing [spores](spore.md) through [meiosis](meiosis.md). In the usual land-plant cycle it is [diploid](diploid.md). Its nutritional independence and stature differ markedly between [bryophytes](bryophyte.md) and [vascular plants](vascular-plant.md).
