# Cube flux from a corner charge

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

For a [point charge](point-charge.md) exactly at a cube corner, each of the three opposite faces has outward [electric flux](electric-flux.md) $q/(24\epsilon_0)$. Reflecting the cube in its three incident faces makes a larger cube centred on the charge and proves the result by [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). The three incident faces have zero normal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) away from their singular corner. Their improper integrals vanish; the total is $q/(8\epsilon_0)$. This boundary convention differs from moving a charge to the corner from the interior after computing a closed-surface integral.
