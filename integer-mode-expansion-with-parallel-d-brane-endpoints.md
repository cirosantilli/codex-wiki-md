# Integer-mode expansion with parallel D-brane endpoints

↑ **Parent:** [Open-string mode expansion](open-string-mode-expansion.md)

On $0\le\sigma\le\pi$, a coordinate with [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) at both ends has a constant position, linear time [zero mode](zero-mode.md) and cosine oscillators. A coordinate with [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) at both ends has a linear spatial interpolation between endpoint positions and sine oscillators, with no [momentum](momentum.md) [zero mode](zero-mode.md). Both have [integer](integer.md) frequencies. This differs from an [ND boundary condition](neumann-dirichlet-open-string-boundary-condition.md), which applies different conditions at the two ends of the same coordinate and gives half-integer frequencies.
