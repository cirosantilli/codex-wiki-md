# Central moment

↑ **Parent:** [Moment](moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_moment)

The $n$th central moment is $\mathbb E[(X-\mathbb EX)^n]$. The first central moment is zero and the second is the [variance](variance-split.md).
