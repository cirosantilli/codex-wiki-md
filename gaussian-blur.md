# Gaussian blur

↑ **Parent:** [Image smoothing](image-smoothing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_blur)

[Gaussian blur](gaussian-blur.md) applies [convolution](convolution.md) with $G_\sigma(x)=(2\pi\sigma^2)^{-n/2}e^{-|x|^2/(2\sigma^2)}$ to an [image signal](image-signal.md). It averages over a spatial scale $\sigma$, suppressing rapid variation while also blurring [image edges](image-edge.md). The [heat-kernel convolution](heat-kernel-convolution.md) realizes this filter at time $t=\sigma^2/2$.
