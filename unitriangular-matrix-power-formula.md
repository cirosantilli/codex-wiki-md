# Unitriangular matrix power formula

↑ **Parent:** [Upper unitriangular group](upper-unitriangular-group.md)

For a three-dimensional strictly upper triangular [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) stops after $A^2$. Over a [finite field](finite-field.md) of odd prime characteristic $p$, both coefficients $p$ and $\binom p2$ vanish, so every nonidentity [matrix](matrix.md) in $UT_3(\mathbb F_p)$ has order $p$. In characteristic two, a [matrix](matrix.md) with nonzero $A^2$ instead has order four.
