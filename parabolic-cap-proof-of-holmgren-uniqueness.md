# Parabolic-cap proof of Holmgren uniqueness

↑ **Parent:** [Holmgren uniqueness theorem](holmgren-uniqueness-theorem.md)

Near a non-characteristic plane $x=0$, use the small cap $\omega_\varepsilon=\{x>0,\ x+y^2<\varepsilon\}$. Its curved face is non-characteristic by continuity of the [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) coordinate change flattens that face. Uniform [real analytic](real-analytic-function.md) coefficient bounds and the [uniform Cauchy radius for polynomial forcing](uniform-cauchy-radius-for-polynomial-forcing.md) solve $P^*v=g$ throughout a common cap, with zero [boundary jet](boundary-jet.md) on the curved face. The [complete boundary-jet condition for formal adjoints](complete-boundary-jet-condition-for-formal-adjoints.md) then gives $\int_{\omega_\varepsilon}ug=0$ for every [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $g$. Uniform approximation of $\bar u$ gives $\int|u|^2=0$. Repeating on the other side of the plane proves two-sided local uniqueness.
