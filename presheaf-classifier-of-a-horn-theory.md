# Presheaf classifier of a Horn theory

↑ **Parent:** [Horn theory](horn-theory.md)

Internal Horn models are finite-limit-preserving [functors](functor.md) out of the [Cartesian syntactic category](cartesian-syntactic-category.md). Since that category has [finite limits](finite-limit.md), these are exactly [flat functors](flat-functor.md); the presheaf [Diaconescu equivalence for geometric morphisms](diaconescu-equivalence-for-geometric-morphisms.md) proves the classifying property. Equivalently the classifier is the covariant [functor](functor.md) category on finitely presented set-based models.
