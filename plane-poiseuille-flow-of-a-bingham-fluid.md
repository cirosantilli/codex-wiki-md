# Plane Poiseuille flow of a Bingham fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Bingham plastic](bingham-plastic.md)

For fixed plates $y=\pm h$, no slip, viscosity $\mu_0>0$, yield [stress](stress.md) $\tau_0\geq0$ and $G=-p_{,x}>0$, [force balance](force-balance.md) gives shear [stress](stress.md) $-Gy$. The central [plug flow of a yield-stress fluid](plug-flow-of-a-yield-stress-fluid.md) has half-width $y_0=\tau_0/G$. If $y_0<h$, the [velocity](velocity.md) is

$$
u(y)=\frac{G}{2\mu_0}\left[(h-y_0)^2-(|y|-y_0)_+^2\right].
$$

It is even in $y$; its derivative is $( -Gy+\tau_0\operatorname{sign}y)/\mu_0$ in the yielded layers and zero in the plug. If $Gh\leq\tau_0$, there is no flow. Odd [velocity](velocity.md) is incompatible with this pressure-driven no-slip solution at nonzero flow.
