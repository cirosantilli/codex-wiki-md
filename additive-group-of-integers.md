# Additive group of integers

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md)

The [integers](integer-split.md) form an [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md) under addition, with [identity element](identity-element.md) zero and [inverse element](inverse-element.md) $-n$ for $n$. Its two generators are $1$ and $-1$. Every [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $m\mathbb Z$ for a unique nonnegative integer $m$: if it is nonzero, take its least positive element and use division with remainder to show that every element is a multiple of it. The zero subgroup corresponds to $m=0$.
