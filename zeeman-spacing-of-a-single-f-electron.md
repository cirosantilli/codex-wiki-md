# Zeeman spacing of a single f electron

↑ **Parent:** [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md)

For a free ion with one $f$ [electron](electron.md) in its weak-field ground multiplet, $L=3,S=1/2,J=5/2$ and $g_J=6/7$. The six magnetic substates have adjacent spacing $g_J\mu_BB$ and full spread $5g_J\mu_BB$. A crystal field can change the applicable effective low-energy description.
