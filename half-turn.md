# Half-turn

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation (mathematics)](rotation-mathematics.md)

A [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) through an [angle](angle.md) of $\pi$. In three dimensions, a [half-turn](half-turn.md) about a [straight line](straight-line.md) with [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$ fixes its parallel component and negates its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md), giving the [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) $2\mathbf n\mathbf n^T-I$ by [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md).
