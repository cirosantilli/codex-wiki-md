# NMDA receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic integration](synaptic-integration.md)

An [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) is an [ionotropic receptor](ionotropic-receptor.md) gating an [ion channel](ion-channel.md) with voltage-dependent [magnesium](magnesium.md) block. This voltage dependence permits cooperative amplification when inputs produce sufficient local [depolarization](depolarization.md); [calcium](calcium.md) can also pass through the open channel.[§3.1.2, equation (3.6): voltage-dependent magnesium block and calcium permeability](neuronal-dynamics-from-single-neurons-to-networks-and-models-of-cognition-by-wulfram-gerstner-and-others/3-1-synapses.md)
