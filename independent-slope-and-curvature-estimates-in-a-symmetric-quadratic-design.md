# Independent slope and curvature estimates in a symmetric quadratic design

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

Fit a quadratic mean at three temperatures $x_0,x_0\pm1$, with equal replication $n$ at the off-center temperatures and any positive replication $m$ at the center. The three parameters fit the three group means exactly, giving the displayed estimates. Independent [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) errors make these estimates jointly normal. Their [covariance](covariance.md) is $\tfrac14[\operatorname{Var}(\bar Y_+)-\operatorname{Var}(\bar Y_-)]=0$, so they are independent. Equal off-center replication is essential to this cancellation.
