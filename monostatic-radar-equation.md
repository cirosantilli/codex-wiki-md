# Monostatic radar equation

↑ **Parent:** [Radar](radar.md)

For a far-field free-space link with one reciprocal [antenna](antenna-radio.md) transmitting and receiving at range $D$, the outward intensity is $P_TG/(4\pi D^2)$. The backward scattered intensity at the receiver is this times $\sigma_{\rm back}/(4\pi D^2)$. Multiplying by the matched [antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md) $G\lambda^2/(4\pi)$ gives the displayed received [power](power.md). Attenuation, misalignment and polarization mismatch require additional factors. The threshold for a [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) voltage $V_m$ across a real matched load $R$ is $V_m^2/R$.
