# Formal power series exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

For a [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) $A$ with zero constant term over a characteristic-zero [field](field.md), its exponential is $\exp A=\sum_{k\ge0}A^k/k!$. Every coefficient is a finite sum because $A^k$ starts in degree at least $k$. In finite characteristic, truncated computations require the factorial denominators that occur to be invertible.
