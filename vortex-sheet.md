# Vortex sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Shear layer](shear-layer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex_sheet)

A vortex sheet is a surface across which tangential velocity is discontinuous while normal velocity remains continuous.
