# Scattering-amplitude factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)

Near a one-particle pole, a [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) factors into amplitudes coupling each side to the intermediate state, summed over its physical polarizations. The pole position gives its squared [mass](mass.md), while its [residue](residue.md) encodes its spin and couplings.
