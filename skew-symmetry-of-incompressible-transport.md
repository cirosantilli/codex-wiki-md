# Skew-symmetry of incompressible transport

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)

For a periodic or boundary-tangent [divergence-free vector field](divergence-free-vector-field.md) $u$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\int (u\mathbin\cdot\nabla f)g
=-\int (u\mathbin\cdot\nabla g)f.
$$

Consequently $\int(u\mathbin\cdot\nabla f)f=0$, the cancellation underlying many fluid [energy estimates](energy-estimate.md).
