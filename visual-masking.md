# Visual masking

↑ **Parent:** [Perception](perception.md)

A procedure in which another visual stimulus reduces access to a target stimulus. In backward masking the mask follows the target. Visibility must be evaluated under the relevant timing and task conditions; a short presentation by itself does not establish absence of awareness.
