# Pearlite phase fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Pearlite](pearlite.md)

The proportions of [ferrite](ferrite.md) and [cementite](cementite.md) within the two-phase [pearlite](pearlite.md) microstructure. A [lever rule](lever-rule.md) on a weight-percent-carbon diagram gives mass fractions; converting those to volume fractions requires the phase densities. Proeutectoid phases outside [pearlite](pearlite.md) must be treated separately.
