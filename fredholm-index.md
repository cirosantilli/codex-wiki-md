# Fredholm index

↑ **Parent:** [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md)

For a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md) between [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md), the index is the difference of the finite [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) of its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and [cokernel](cokernel.md). Since the range is closed, its [cokernel](cokernel.md) identifies with $\ker T^*$ by [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). An invertible operator has index zero. Conversely, an index-zero [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md) becomes invertible after adding a [finite-rank operator](finite-rank-operator.md): choose an isomorphism $J:\ker T\to\ker T^*$, extend it by zero on $(\ker T)^\perp$, and observe that $T+J$ maps the two summands bijectively onto $\operatorname{ran}T\oplus\ker T^*$.
