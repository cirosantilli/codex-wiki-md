# Direct electron detection

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

[direct electron detection](direct-electron-detection.md) records incident [electrons](electron.md) in a sensor without first converting them through a scintillator into an optical image. Fast readout permits counting and movies that support motion correction.
