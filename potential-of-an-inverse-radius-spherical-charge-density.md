# Potential of an inverse-radius spherical charge density

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

A charge cloud $\rho=A/r$ for $0<r<a$ encloses $Q(r)=2\pi Ar^2$. With no central point charge and potential zero at infinity, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives $\phi=A(2a-r)/(2\epsilon_0)$ inside and $Aa^2/(2\epsilon_0r)$ outside. The [density](density.md) singularity is integrable, and the potential is finite at the origin.
