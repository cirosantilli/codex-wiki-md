# Comoving entropy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-dominated universe](radiation-dominated-universe.md)

Adiabatic expansion conserves [entropy](entropy.md) in a comoving volume: $s a^3$ is constant. With $s=(2\pi^2/45)g_{*s}T^3$, it gives $(a_i/a_0)^3=(g_{*s,0}/g_{*s,i})(T_0/T_i)^3$. Changes in [entropy](entropy.md) [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) must therefore be retained when transporting an early relic density to today, rather than setting $aT$ constant across all particle thresholds.
