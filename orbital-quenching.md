# Orbital quenching

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal field theory](crystal-field-theory.md)

Suppression of a ground state's orbital [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) by the splitting and mixing of free-ion [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) in a crystal. A nondegenerate orbital state can generally be chosen real in zero field, giving zero expectation of the imaginary [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) operator. [Spin-orbit coupling](spin-orbit-interaction.md) to excited states still produces residual [g-factor](g-factor-physics.md) shifts and anisotropy, so quenching is not absence of every orbital response.
