<h1 id="conductive-state-of-rayleigh-benard-convection">Conductive state of Rayleigh-Bénard convection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

The conductive state is the no-flow equilibrium in which [heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md) alone maintains a linear [temperature](temperature.md) profile across a layer with fixed boundary temperatures. Its [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) follows by integrating $P_b'=-g\rho_b$, with [mass density](density.md) obtained from the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md). Under the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), deviations from this state are the variables used in the linear [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md) problem.
