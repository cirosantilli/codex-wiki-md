# Coriolis force does no work

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md)

The instantaneous [power](power.md) of the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md), evaluated with the [velocity](velocity.md) in the same [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md), vanishes:

$$
\mathbf F_{\rm cor}\cdot\mathbf v_{\rm rot}
=-2m(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf v_{\rm rot})\cdot\mathbf v_{\rm rot}=0.
$$

This follows from the perpendicularity of the [cross product](cross-product.md) and the [dot product](dot-product.md) definition of [power](power.md). The [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) alone does no [work](work.md) and cannot change the rotating-frame [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $m|\mathbf v_{\rm rot}|^2/2$. It can change the direction of the relative [velocity](velocity.md). Actual [forces](force-split.md) and the [centrifugal force](centrifugal-force.md) can still change that [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), as the [free particle in rotating coordinates](free-particle-in-rotating-coordinates.md) illustrates. Conservation of an appropriate total [energy](energy-split.md) has the additional conditions stated under [energy balance in a uniformly rotating frame](energy-balance-in-a-uniformly-rotating-frame.md).
