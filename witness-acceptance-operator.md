# Witness acceptance operator

↑ **Parent:** [QMA](qma.md)

For [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) embedding $J$, verifier unitary $U$ and accepting projector $\Pi$, the [witness acceptance operator](witness-acceptance-operator.md) is the positive contraction $F=J^\dagger U^\dagger\Pi UJ$. The maximum acceptance over normalized [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) is $\lambda_{\max}(F)$ by the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md). Restricting to basis [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) only tests its diagonal entries and need not find that maximum.
