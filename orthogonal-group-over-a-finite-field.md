# Orthogonal group over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix group](matrix-group.md)

The [group](group-split.md) of invertible [linear maps](linear-map.md) preserving a specified [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) over a [finite field](finite-field.md). In characteristic two one must preserve the [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) itself, not merely its polar [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md). For a nonsingular form, any two nonzero [isotropic vectors](isotropic-vector.md) can be incorporated into hyperbolic pairs and standard adapted bases, producing an isometry between them.
