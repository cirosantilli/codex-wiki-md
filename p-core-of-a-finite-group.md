# p-core of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group](finite-group.md)

The p-core is the largest normal [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) of a finite [group](group-split.md). The product of two normal p-subgroups is again a normal p-subgroup, so this subgroup exists and is characteristic.
