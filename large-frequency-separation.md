# Large frequency separation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar acoustic mode](stellar-acoustic-mode.md)

The leading separation between successive high-order low-degree [stellar acoustic mode](stellar-acoustic-mode.md) frequencies is the inverse round-trip acoustic travel time, $\Delta\nu\simeq[2\int_0^Rdr/c_s]^{-1}$. For homologous stars it scales approximately as $(M/R^3)^{1/2}$ and therefore measures mean density.
