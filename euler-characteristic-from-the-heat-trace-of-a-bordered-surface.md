# Euler characteristic from the heat trace of a bordered surface

↑ **Parent:** [Heat trace](heat-trace.md)

For a compact smooth [Riemannian surface](riemannian-surface.md) with smooth boundary and a pure [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) or pure [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md), the constant coefficient in the [heat trace](heat-trace.md) is

$$
\frac1{12\pi}\left(\int_MK\,dA+\int_{\partial M}\kappa\,ds\right)=\frac{\chi(M)}6.
$$

The equality is the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md). Thus such [isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md) have the same [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). A [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) compact connected bordered surface is a disk with $\chi=1$, whereas a nonorientable surface with $c\geq1$ crosscaps and $b\geq1$ boundary components has $\chi=2-c-b\leq0$. These cannot be isospectral in this setting. Corners and mixed [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) contribute additional heat coefficients, so the smooth pure-boundary-condition hypothesis is essential.
