# Stokes phenomenon

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes_phenomenon)

Exponentially small contributions can change their asymptotic representation when a complex parameter crosses a Stokes direction. An all-orders [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) in inverse powers alone does not resolve these terms.
