# Scalar electrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [U(1) gauge symmetry](u-1-gauge-symmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_electrodynamics)

A [charged scalar field](charged-scalar-field.md) coupled to an Abelian [gauge field](gauge-field.md) through $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-iea_\mu$. Under $\phi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\phi$ and $a_\mu\mapsto a_\mu+e^{-1}\partial_\mu\alpha$, its [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) transforms with the same phase. The potential determines whether the perturbative spectrum has a massless [photon](photon.md) or exhibits the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md).
