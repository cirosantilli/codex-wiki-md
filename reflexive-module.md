# Reflexive module

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion-free module](torsion-free-module.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflexive_module)

A finitely generated module $M$ over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$ is reflexive when its [evaluation homomorphism](evaluation-homomorphism.md) $M\to M^{**}$ to the [double dual module](double-dual-module.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
