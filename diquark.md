# Diquark

↑ **Parent:** [Quark model](quark-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diquark)

A correlated pair of [quarks](quark.md). Its color, flavor, spin and orbital states are separate degrees of freedom and must collectively satisfy [Fermi statistics](fermi-dirac-statistics.md). In flavor [SU(3)](su-3-group.md), the antisymmetric pair space is $\Lambda^2\mathbf3\cong\overline{\mathbf3}$; contraction with the invariant [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) realizes the isomorphism. The antisymmetric $ud$ flavor pair is an [isospin](isospin.md) singlet with [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md) $2/3$. Combining two such flavor-antitriplet pairs with a strange [antiquark](antiquark.md) permits the highest weight of the [pentaquark antidecuplet](pentaquark-antidecuplet.md); this flavor construction does not itself establish a bound state.
