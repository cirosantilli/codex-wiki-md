# Post-traumatic stress disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A disorder defined by a qualifying traumatic [exposure](exposure.md) together with the required subsequent symptom, duration and impairment criteria. The [exposure](exposure.md) requirement is a diagnostic condition, not a claim that [exposure](exposure.md) alone is sufficient or that every psychological disorder has the same prerequisite.[DSM-5 Criteria for PTSD, Criterion A and full-syndrome requirement](ptsd-and-dsm-5-by-national-center-for-ptsd.md)
