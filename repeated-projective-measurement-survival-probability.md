# Repeated projective-measurement survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Projective measurement](projective-measurement.md)

For a normalized [quantum state](quantum-state.md) $\psi$ and a time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$, consider ideal [projective measurements](projective-measurement.md) of the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ at intervals $\Delta t=T/N$. Write $U=e^{-iH\Delta t/\hbar}$. A successful check leaves the [post-measurement state](post-measurement-state.md) $\psi$ up to [global phase](global-phase.md). Thus each successive success, conditional on earlier successes, has [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) $|\langle\psi,U\psi\rangle|^2$, and multiplication gives the displayed formula. Equivalently,

$$
(PU)^N\psi=\langle\psi,U\psi\rangle^N\psi,\qquad
\mathbb P_N(T)=\|(PU)^N\psi\|^2.
$$

This is the [probability](probability.md) of the entire sequence of successful checks. It underlies the [quantum Zeno effect](quantum-zeno-effect.md).
