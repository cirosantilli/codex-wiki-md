# Vector bundle isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md)

A [vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md) is an isomorphism if it admits an inverse of the same kind. A smooth fiberwise bijective bundle morphism automatically has a smooth inverse, because inverse [matrices](matrix.md) in [vector bundle trivializations](vector-bundle-trivialization.md) depend smoothly on their entries wherever their determinants are nonzero.
