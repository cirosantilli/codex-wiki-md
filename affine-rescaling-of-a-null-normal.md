# Affine rescaling of a null normal

↑ **Parent:** [Null geodesic congruence](null-geodesic-congruence.md)

If a [null vector](null-vector.md) tangent satisfies $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$, then $U=hK$ is affine when $K(\log h)=-\kappa$. Choose $h=1$ on the initial transverse surface. The [screen-space projector](screen-space-projector.md) kills derivative terms proportional to $K$, so the initial [optical tensor](optical-tensor.md) and its [null expansion](null-expansion.md) agree with those of $K$. In ingoing spherical coordinates, $K=\partial_v+(f/2)\partial_r$ has $\kappa=f'/2$.
