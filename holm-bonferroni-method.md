<h1 id="holm-bonferroni-method">Holm–Bonferroni method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bonferroni correction](bonferroni-correction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holm–Bonferroni_method)

The Holm–Bonferroni method orders p-values and compares the $j$th smallest with $\alpha/(m-j+1)$, stopping at the first failed comparison. It controls the [familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md) under arbitrary dependence.
