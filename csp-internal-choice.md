# CSP internal choice

↑ **Parent:** [CSP choice](csp-choice.md)

The process may internally choose either branch, independently of the environment's offered [CSP events](csp-event.md). Consequently it can have more [CSP stable failures](csp-stable-failure.md) than the corresponding [CSP external choice](csp-external-choice.md), despite having the same [CSP traces](csp-trace.md).
