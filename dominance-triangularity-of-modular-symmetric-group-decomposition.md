# Dominance triangularity of modular symmetric-group decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Decomposition matrix (modular representation theory)](decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md)

Rows of the symmetric-group [decomposition matrix](decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md) are all ordinary shapes, columns are [regular partitions](regular-partition.md), and $d_{\mu\lambda}=[S^\mu:D^\lambda]$. A nonzero [column antisymmetrizer](column-antisymmetrizer-of-a-young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ on $M^\mu$ forces dominance: the first $r$ rows contain at most $\sum_j\min(r,\lambda'_j)$ column entries. For a regular shape its diagonal simple appears once, giving a rectangular triangular matrix and a unitriangular regular-row submatrix.
