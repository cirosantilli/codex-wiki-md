# Spinless fermion t-V chain

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

A [tight binding](tight-binding.md) chain of one species of [fermions](fermion.md) with nearest-neighbour hopping and density interaction. At half filling and $V/|t|\to+\infty$, minimizing the interaction requires alternating occupied and empty sites. An even periodic chain has two such configurations at $t=0$; finite hopping admixes defects through [virtual hopping](virtual-hopping.md). A finite ring can select [superpositions](superposition-principle.md) rather than one ordered product state. Two uncoupled copies have four limiting ordered choices when each is half filled, without a general claim of exact finite-hopping fourfold [state degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md).
