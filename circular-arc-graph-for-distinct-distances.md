# Circular-arc graph for distinct distances

↑ **Parent:** [Székely distinct-distance bound](szekely-distinct-distance-bound.md)

Around each of $n$ points draw its at most $t$ distinct-distance [circles](circle.md). On every [circle](circle.md) containing at least three points, join cyclically consecutive points by arcs. The resulting loopless [multigraph](multigraph.md) has at least $n(n-1)-2nt$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and a drawing with $O(n^2t^2)$ crossings. A pair joined by several arcs has every corresponding centre on its [perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md), which is the key to bounding multiplicity.
