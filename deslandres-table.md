# Deslandres table

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic transition](electronic-transition.md)

A two-index arrangement of vibronic transition [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md). Differences along rows isolate lower-state spacings; differences down columns isolate upper-state spacings. Deviations from exact separability can expose measurement uncertainty, rotational structure or incorrect assignments.
