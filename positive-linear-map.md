# Positive linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)

A positive linear map sends every [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) to a positive semidefinite matrix. Positivity need not persist after tensoring with the identity map on an ancillary system; requiring that stronger property gives a [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md).
