# Bosonic sigma-model Weyl anomaly coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md)

To first order in $\alpha'$, the conventional Euclidean [dilaton](dilaton.md) coupling gives $\overline\beta^G_{\mu\nu}=\alpha'[R_{\mu\nu}-H_{\mu\rho\sigma}H_\nu{}^{\rho\sigma}/4+2\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu\Phi]$ and $\overline\beta^B_{\mu\nu}=\alpha'[-\nabla^\rho H_{\rho\mu\nu}/2+(\nabla^\rho\Phi)H_{\rho\mu\nu}]$. The scalar coefficient is $(d-26)/6+\alpha'[(\nabla\Phi)^2-\nabla^2\Phi/2-H^2/24]$. Here $H$ is the [Kalb-Ramond field strength](kalb-ramond-field-strength.md). Vanishing of these coefficients, including higher-order corrections, is the background-field condition for quantum [Weyl transformations](weyl-transformation.md) to remain a gauge symmetry. The scalar coefficient includes the [central charge](central-charge.md) deficit and is not implied by the metric equation alone.
