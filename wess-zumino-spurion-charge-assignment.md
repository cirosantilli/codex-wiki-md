<h1 id="wess-zumino-spurion-charge-assignment">Wess–Zumino spurion charge assignment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphy argument for superpotential non-renormalization](holomorphy-argument-for-superpotential-non-renormalization.md)

For quadratic and cubic interactions, assigning the couplings chiral [spurion](spurion.md) transformations makes each monomial formally covariant. The ordinary $U(1)$ leaves $\theta$ neutral, while the [R-symmetry](r-symmetry.md) gives it charge one and the [superpotential](superpotential.md) charge two. The neutral ratio $g\Phi/m$ and the covariant factor $m\Phi^2$ give the symmetry-allowed holomorphic form $m\Phi^2f(g\Phi/m)$. Perturbative regularity and tree matching are additionally needed for [superpotential non-renormalization](non-renormalization-theorem.md); symmetry alone leaves an arbitrary function. Fixed numerical couplings need not enjoy these formal symmetries as actual field symmetries.
