# Kolmogorov first similarity hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md)

In the locally isotropic [equilibrium range](equilibrium-range.md), the small-scale [velocity increment](velocity-increment.md) statistics depend on the separation, mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) and [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md), independently of large-scale forcing details. [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) leaves the [Kolmogorov microscales](kolmogorov-microscales.md) as normalization scales and gives the displayed second-order [longitudinal structure function](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md). Universality of $F$ is part of the hypothesis, not something proved by [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md).
