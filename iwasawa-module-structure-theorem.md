# Iwasawa module structure theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md)

A finitely generated [module](module-mathematics.md) over $\Lambda=\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$ is [pseudo-isomorphic](pseudo-isomorphism.md) to

$$
\Lambda^r\oplus\bigoplus_i\Lambda/(p^{a_i})\oplus\bigoplus_j\Lambda/(f_j^{b_j}),
$$

where the $f_j$ are irreducible [distinguished polynomials](distinguished-polynomial.md). Height-one localizations are discrete valuation rings, which give the elementary divisors; the remaining errors are supported only at the maximal ideal and are finite. This is a finite-error classification, not necessarily an actual direct-sum decomposition.
