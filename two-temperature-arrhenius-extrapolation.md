# Two-temperature Arrhenius extrapolation

↑ **Parent:** [Arrhenius equation](arrhenius-equation.md)

For a constant-parameter [Arrhenius equation](arrhenius-equation.md) $R(T)=Ae^{-\beta/T}$ at positive absolute [temperatures](temperature-split.md), subtracting logarithms eliminates the [Arrhenius prefactor](pre-exponential-factor.md). The ratio of the two resulting equations gives the displayed prediction. It applies to a [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) only when composition and other factors relating rate to the [rate constant](rate-constant.md) are held fixed. Equal increments of [temperature](temperature-split.md) do not give equal multiplicative rate changes, since the linear variable is $1/T$.
