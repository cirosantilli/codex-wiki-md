# Scalar equation of Brans-Dicke theory

↑ **Parent:** [Brans-Dicke theory](brans-dicke-theory.md)

Assume four spacetime dimensions, $\Phi\ne0$, constant $\omega$, no [scalar](scalar.md) potential and matter independent of $\Phi$. In the stated action convention, [scalar](scalar.md) [variation](variation.md) gives $\Phi R+\omega\Phi^{-1}(\nabla\Phi)^2-2\omega\Box\Phi=0$. The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) equation gives $\Phi R+\omega\Phi^{-1}(\nabla\Phi)^2+3\Box\Phi=8\pi G T$. Their subtraction proves the displayed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) with $T=g^{ab}T_{ab}$. The [degenerate coupling of Brans-Dicke theory](degenerate-coupling-of-brans-dicke-theory.md) needs separate treatment before dividing by its coefficient.
