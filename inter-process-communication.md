# Inter-process communication

↑ **Parent:** [Operating system](operating-system-split.md)

Communication between [operating-system processes](process-computing.md), including controlled message delivery and deliberately shared [computer memory](computer-memory.md). A [microkernel](microkernel.md) supplies [IPC](inter-process-communication.md) across [protection domains](protection-domain.md), allowing [user-space services](user-space-service.md) to cooperate without making all their private [computer memory](computer-memory.md) accessible to each other.
