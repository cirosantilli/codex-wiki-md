<h1 id="debye-huckel-approximation">Debye–Hückel approximation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)

The Debye–Hückel approximation linearizes mobile-ion Boltzmann densities about a neutral bulk electrolyte. It is valid for ionic potential energies small compared with $k_BT$. The inverse [Debye–Hückel screening length](debye-huckel-screening-length.md) satisfies $\kappa_D^2=\sum_i n_i z_i^2e^2/(\varepsilon k_BT)$. The quadratic screened charging free energy includes both [electric field](electric-field.md) energy and the linearized ionic-response term $\varepsilon\kappa_D^2\phi^2/2$.
