# N6-methyladenosine

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

An [adenosine](adenosine.md) modification bearing a methyl group on the exocyclic N6 nitrogen. Its presence can alter recognition by an [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md) directly or through [RNA secondary structure](rna-secondary-structure.md).
