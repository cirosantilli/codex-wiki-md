# Loop-space homology

↑ **Parent:** [Loop space](loop-space.md)

Loop-space homology is the [homology](homology-split.md) of the based [loop space](loop-space.md). It also carries the Pontryagin product induced by concatenation of loops. For $n>2$, a [Morse theory](morse-theory.md) cell structure on $\Omega S^n$ has one cell in each dimension $j(n-1)$, so its integer homology is $\mathbb Z$ in those dimensions and zero elsewhere.
