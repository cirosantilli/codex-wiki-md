# Hydrodynamic attraction of a plane wall to a point source

↑ **Parent:** [Point source](point-source.md)

An incompressible [point source](point-source.md) of volume [flux](flux.md) $Q$ at height $a$ above an impermeable plane has [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) $\phi=-Q(1/r_++1/r_-)/(4\pi)$, with an equal image source below the plane. On the wall, $u_s=Qs/[2\pi(s^2+a^2)^{3/2}]$. The [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) gives $p-p_\infty=-\rho Q^2s^2/[8\pi^2(s^2+a^2)^3]$. Integrating the [pressure](pressure.md) deficit over the plane gives the displayed attraction force relative to ambient [pressure](pressure.md). If source strength instead denotes the coefficient $Q/(4\pi)$ of radial inverse-square velocity, the force is $\pi\rho m^2/a^2$.
