# 't Hooft symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual differential form](self-dual-differential-form.md)

For the orientation $dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3\wedge dx^4$, define $\eta^a_{ij}=\epsilon_{aij}$, $\eta^a_{i4}=\delta_{ai}$, and extend antisymmetrically. The three forms $\tfrac12\eta^a_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu\wedge dx^\nu$ are self-dual. Reversing the sign of the components involving index four gives the anti-self-dual symbols $\bar\eta$. They identify the two three-dimensional eigenspaces of the [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md) on [differential two-forms](2-form.md) and give compact formulas for the [BPST instanton](bpst-instanton.md).
