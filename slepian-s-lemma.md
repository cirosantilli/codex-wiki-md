<h1 id="slepian-s-lemma">Slepian's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slepian's_lemma)

For centered [multivariate normal distributions](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) represented by vectors $U,V$ with equal corresponding [variances](variance-split.md) and $\mathbb E[U_iU_j]\geq\mathbb E[V_iV_j]$ for all distinct indices, $\mathbb P(U_i\leq u_i\text{ for all }i)\geq\mathbb P(V_i\leq u_i\text{ for all }i)$ for every threshold vector. In particular the [supremum](supremum.md) of $U$ is stochastically smaller than the [supremum](supremum.md) of $V$. Equal corresponding [variances](variance-split.md) are essential to this formulation. The related [Sudakov-Fernique inequality](sudakov-fernique-inequality.md) compares [expected values](expected-value.md) of maxima using [variances](variance-split.md) of increments and does not require equal pointwise [variances](variance-split.md).
