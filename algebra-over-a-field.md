# Algebra over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_field)

An algebra over a [field](field.md) $k$ is a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $A$ over $k$ equipped with a bilinear multiplication $A\times A\to A$.
