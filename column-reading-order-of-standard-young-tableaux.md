# Column-reading order of standard Young tableaux

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md)

Read each column from top to bottom, with columns ordered from left to right, and compare the resulting words lexicographically. With [Young symmetrizer](young-symmetrizer.md) convention $h_t=c_tr_t$, this order gives [triangular vanishing of Young-symmetrizer products](triangular-vanishing-of-young-symmetrizer-products.md). Changing the symmetrizer product convention requires a compatible order, rather than retaining a fixed vanishing direction automatically.
