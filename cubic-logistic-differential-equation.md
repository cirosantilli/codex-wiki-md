# Cubic logistic differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Separable differential equation](separable-differential-equation.md)

The substitution $z=y^{-2}$ turns the nonzero solutions into $\dot z=1-z$. Thus $y=\pm(1+Ke^{-t})^{-1/2}$ where the denominator is positive, together with $y=0$. The nonzero [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $\pm1$ are attracting and $0$ is unstable. The sign of nonzero initial data is preserved.
