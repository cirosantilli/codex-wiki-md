# Momentum eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenstate](eigenstate.md)

A momentum eigenstate is an eigenstate of the [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md). In one dimension it is a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) $e^{ikx}$ with momentum eigenvalue $\hbar k$.
