# Good-solvent concentration-blob scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration blob](concentration-blob.md)

In a three-dimensional semidilute [good solvent](good-solvent.md), take $g$ statistical segments per blob with size $\xi\sim ag^{3/5}$ and concentration matching $\phi\sim ga^3/\xi^3$. Eliminating $g$ gives the displayed relations. A developed blob regime requires $1\ll g\ll N$ and a [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) well below close packing. Within a blob the chain retains isolated swollen statistics; on larger scales interpenetrating chains screen excluded-volume effects. The exponents use $\nu=3/5$ and the site-volume assumption. For general $\nu$, $\xi\sim a\phi^{-\nu/(3\nu-1)}$.
