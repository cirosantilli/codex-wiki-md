# Normal surface weight

↑ **Parent:** [Normal surface coordinates](normal-surface-coordinates.md)

The weight of a [normal surface](normal-surface.md) counts its intersections with the edges of the [triangulation](triangulation.md). It is a positive linear function of its coordinates: each corner of a normal [disk](disk-mathematics.md) contributes the reciprocal of the number of tetrahedral sectors around its edge. Thus a nontrivial decomposition into compatible coordinate vectors gives both summands strictly smaller weight.
