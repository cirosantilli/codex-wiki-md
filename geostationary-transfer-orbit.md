# Geostationary transfer orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)

An elliptical transfer trajectory whose apogee is near the radius of a [geostationary orbit](geostationary-orbit.md). Apogee maneuvers can circularize the trajectory and reduce inclination.
