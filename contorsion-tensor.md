# Contorsion tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion tensor](torsion-tensor.md)

For a metric-compatible [affine connection](affine-connection.md), its difference from the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is the contorsion [tensor](tensor.md). It is determined by the [torsion tensor](torsion-tensor.md). In derivative-last notation let $T_{ab}{}^c=\Gamma^c{}_{ab}-\Gamma^c{}_{ba}$, the negative of geometric torsion, and lower the last slot with the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). Then $C_{ab c}=\tfrac12(T_{ab c}+T_{bc a}-T_{ca b})$. This is the actual correction added to the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md); a convention writing $\Gamma=S-K$ has $K=-C$.
