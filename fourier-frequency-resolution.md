# Fourier frequency resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md)

For $N$ equally spaced samples at [sampling rate](sampling-rate.md) $f_s$, the [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) has [frequency](frequency.md) bins spaced by $f_s/N=1/T_{\mathrm{obs}}$. A rectangular observation window broadens a sinusoid into a [sinc function](sinc-function.md) profile whose nearest zeros are $1/T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ from its central [frequency](frequency.md). This sets a conventional scale for distinguishing nearby spectral components. It is not a universal lower bound on the uncertainty of [frequency estimation](frequency-estimation.md) for an isolated tone: a specified signal model and noise level can allow precision finer than the bin spacing. Padding with zeros interpolates the spectral display without increasing the observation duration.
