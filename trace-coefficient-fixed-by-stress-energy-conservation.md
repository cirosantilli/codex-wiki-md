# Trace coefficient fixed by stress-energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md)

In four dimensions, the [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) gives $(1/2-\alpha)\partial_\mu R=-\kappa\nabla^\nu T_{\mu\nu}$ for the displayed equation. Its trace is $(1-4\alpha)R=-\kappa T$. For $\alpha\ne1/4$,

$$
\nabla^\nu T_{\mu\nu}=\frac{1/2-\alpha}{1-4\alpha}\partial_\mu T.
$$

Compatibility with covariant [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) for unrestricted conventional matter therefore requires $\alpha=1/2$, yielding the [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md). Other coefficients impose constant trace on conserved sources. The exceptional $\alpha=1/4$ equation instead requires $T=0$; conservation additionally makes $R$ constant. A trace-free equation sourced by $T_{\mu\nu}-g_{\mu\nu}T/4$ is a different system and does not impose vanishing matter trace.
