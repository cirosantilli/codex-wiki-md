# Gaussian antenna beam solid angle

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna beam pattern](antenna-beam-pattern.md)

For a narrow angular [power](power.md) pattern $\exp[-(u^2+w^2)/(2\alpha^2)]$ about an equatorial beam centre, the [solid angle](solid-angle.md) measure is locally $du\,dw$. Extending the small lobe to the plane gives its beam [solid angle](solid-angle.md) $\Omega_A\simeq2\pi\alpha^2$. Both sides of the azimuthal lobe must be included. A lossless [antenna](antenna-radio.md) then has peak [antenna gain](antenna-gain.md) $G_0\simeq2/\alpha^2$ and peak [antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md) $A_0\simeq\lambda^2/(2\pi\alpha^2)$.
