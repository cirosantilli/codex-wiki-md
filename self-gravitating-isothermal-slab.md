# Self-gravitating isothermal slab

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

A plane-symmetric, self-gravitating [stellar population](stellar-population.md) with constant vertical [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) satisfies the [vertical Jeans equation](vertical-jeans-equation.md) and the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md). With $\Phi'(0)=0$, their solution is

$$
\rho=\rho_0\operatorname{sech}^2(z/z_0),\quad \Phi-\Phi(0)=2\sigma_z^2\log\cosh(z/z_0),\quad z_0^2=\frac{\sigma_z^2}{2\pi G\rho_0}.
$$

Its [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is $\Sigma=2\rho_0z_0$, so $z_0=\sigma_z^2/(\pi G\Sigma)$. Its outer density tail is exponential with [scale height](scale-height.md) $z_0/2$; its central profile is not exponential.
