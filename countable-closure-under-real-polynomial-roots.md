# Countable closure under real polynomial roots

↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)

Every [countable set](countable-set.md) $B$ of [real numbers](real-number.md) is contained in a [countable set](countable-set.md) closed under taking all real [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) with coefficients in that set. Let $\phi(A)$ consist of these [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md), excluding the zero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). There are countably many finite coefficient lists over a [countable set](countable-set.md), and each nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has finitely many [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md); hence $\phi(A)$ is countable. Define $B_0=B$ and $B_{n+1}=B_n\cup\phi(B_n)$. The [countable union of countable sets](countable-union-of-countable-sets.md) theorem shows that $X=\bigcup_nB_n$ is countable. Every finite coefficient list in $X$ belongs to one stage $B_N$, so its real [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) belong to $B_{N+1}\subseteq X$. Retaining $B_n$ at the next stage matters: $\phi(A)$ need not contain $A$.
