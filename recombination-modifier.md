# Recombination modifier

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)

An inherited variant that changes the rate or pattern of [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md). Its spread requires a reproductive advantage to its own lineage, directly or through associations with other variants, rather than a benefit only to a distant future species.
