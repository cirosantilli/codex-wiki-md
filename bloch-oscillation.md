# Bloch oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem-split.md)

In a single isolated [energy band](energy-band.md), a [wave packet](wave-packet.md) subject to a constant force satisfies $\hbar\dot k=F$. Its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is periodic in $k$ with reciprocal-lattice period $2\pi/a$, so its position oscillates with period $2\pi\hbar/(|F|a)$ when its mean velocity over a band is zero. For $E(k)=E_0-2J\cos ka$, integrating the sinusoidal velocity gives the oscillation explicitly. This ideal description neglects scattering and interband tunnelling.
