# Laboratory spin in rectilinear Fermi-Walker transport

↑ **Parent:** [Spin four-vector](spin-four-vector.md)

For the orthogonal parametrization $s^\mu=\gamma(\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf v/c,\mathbf s)$, torque-free acceleration along a fixed direction keeps the longitudinal laboratory component constant and the transverse component proportional to $1/\gamma$. The invariant proper-spin length remains constant; these changing three-components are not a torque-induced loss of spin.
