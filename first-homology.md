# First homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

The first homology group $H_1(X)$ is the group of one-dimensional cycles modulo boundaries. For a path-connected space it is the [abelianization](abelianization.md) of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md).
