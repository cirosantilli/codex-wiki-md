# Isopycnic centrifugation

↑ **Parent:** [Density-gradient centrifugation](density-gradient-centrifugation.md)

In [isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md), a species concentrates near the position where the liquid's [density](density.md) matches its [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md). In a [density gradient](density-gradient.md) increasing outward, material displaced inward encounters less-dense liquid and tends to move outward; material displaced outward encounters denser liquid and tends to move inward. [Diffusion](diffusion.md) broadens the resulting band. If the [density gradient](density-gradient.md) never reaches the species' [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md), a matching equilibrium position need not exist within the vessel.[An Introduction to Ultracentrifugation, Isopycnic Centrifugation: matching buoyant density and diffusion in equilibrium gradients](https://www.beckman.com/resources/technologies/centrifugation/ultracentrifugation/)

The 1958 paper [The replication of DNA in Escherichia coli by Matthew Meselson and Franklin W. Stahl](the-replication-of-dna-in-escherichia-coli-by-matthew-meselson-and-franklin-w-stahl.md) (pp. 672–676, Figures 1–4) makes the distinction concrete. Its separation apparatus was a Spinco Model E ultracentrifuge with an [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) lysate in a [cesium chloride density gradient](cesium-chloride-density-gradient.md). Ultraviolet [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md) photographs of the rotating sample were scanned with a recording microdensitometer: peak position reported [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md), while signal above baseline reported [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [concentration](concentration.md).[p. 676, Experimental: Spinco Model E and recording microdensitometer; p. 675, Figure 4: peak position and baseline](the-replication-of-dna-in-escherichia-coli-by-matthew-meselson-and-franklin-w-stahl.md)

Cultures received labeled [nitrogen](nitrogen.md); chilled [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) were collected as [centrifugation pellets](centrifugation-pellet.md) and disrupted before molecular banding.[pp. 673–674, Experimental: labeled growth medium, chilled collection and detergent disruption](the-replication-of-dna-in-escherichia-coli-by-matthew-meselson-and-franklin-w-stahl.md)

Mixed lysates labeled fully with the [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [isotope](isotope.md) $^{15}\mathrm{N}$ and unlabeled lysates supplied reference bands; a mixture including intermediate material checked its position relative to those references. Figure 1 followed band formation over time, helping distinguish a migrating distribution from an approximately stationary one. These controls address resolution, calibration and approach to equilibrium. They do not make every ultraviolet-absorbing component identifiable as [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), or turn a band into a direct image of individual strands. The [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) article develops the copying inference; here the key result is a separation by [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md), not a claim that heavier-labeled [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is physically larger.[pp. 672–673, Figures 1–2; p. 675, Figure 4 density references and microdensitometer baseline; p. 676, Experimental: Spinco Model E and ultraviolet photographs](the-replication-of-dna-in-escherichia-coli-by-matthew-meselson-and-franklin-w-stahl.md)
