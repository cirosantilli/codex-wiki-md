# Chained modular Bell inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Bell theorem](bell-theorem.md)

For outcomes in $\{0,\ldots,d-1\}$, let $[x]_d$ be their nonnegative modular representative. A [deterministic local hidden-variable model](deterministic-local-hidden-variable-model.md) satisfies

$$
I_{N,d}=\sum_{j=1}^N\mathbb E\big([A_j-B_j]_d\big)+\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\mathbb E\big([B_j-A_{j+1}]_d\big)+\mathbb E\big([B_N-A_1-1]_d\big)\geq d-1.
$$

The unreduced chain telescopes to $-1$, so its reduced nonnegative sum is at least $d-1$ for each assignment. For binary outcomes and two settings, it is a form of the [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md): $I_{2,2}=2-(E_{11}+E_{21}+E_{22}-E_{12})/2$.
