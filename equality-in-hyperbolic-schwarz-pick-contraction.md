# Equality in hyperbolic Schwarz-Pick contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) between hyperbolic [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md) lifts to a disk map. If the lift is not an automorphism, strict [Schwarz-Pick lemma](schwarz-pick-theorem.md) derivative inequality makes every nonconstant geodesic shorter. Equality of distance for one distinct pair therefore forces the map to be a [covering map](covering-space.md) and a local [isometry](isometry.md). Covering maps attain equality for sufficiently close pairs in a single lift of a small convex metric ball; they need not preserve all global distances. A punctured disk covers a thrice-punctured sphere by including a cyclic parabolic subgroup into $\Gamma(2)$, so equality is possible in that example.
