# Branch vertex of a graph subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Graph subdivision](graph-subdivision.md)

A [branch vertex of a graph subdivision](branch-vertex-of-a-graph-subdivision.md) is a retained original [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) in a specified [graph subdivision](graph-subdivision.md). Its [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) in that [graph subdivision](graph-subdivision.md) equals its original [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). It need not have [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) at least three: $B,C,D$ in the example are also [branch vertices of a graph subdivision](branch-vertex-of-a-graph-subdivision.md). When the target itself has [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) two, adjacency alone need not identify which [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) were original; the specified target mapping supplies that distinction.
