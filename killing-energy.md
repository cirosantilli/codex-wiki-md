# Killing energy

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector](geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md)

For a geodesic tangent $u$ and a stationary [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $K$, the [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) is $E=-g(K,u)$. It is conserved along the [geodesic](geodesic.md). When $K$ is future timelike and $u$ is future causal, $E>0$ for a nonzero tangent; it need not be positive where $K$ becomes spacelike.
