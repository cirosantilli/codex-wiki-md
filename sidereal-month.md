# Sidereal month

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar phase](lunar-phase.md)

A [sidereal month](sidereal-month.md) is the [Moon](moon.md)'s [orbital period](orbital-period.md) relative to distant stars, about $27.32166$ days. It is shorter than the [synodic month](synodic-month.md) because the sunward direction changes as [Earth](earth.md) travels around the [Sun](sun.md). The lunar spin period associated with [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) matches the [sidereal month](sidereal-month.md) in the mean. [Fred Espenak, NASA: the sidereal month](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEhelp/moonorbit.html).
