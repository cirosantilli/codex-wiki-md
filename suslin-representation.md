# Suslin representation

↑ **Parent:** [Descriptive set theory](descriptive-set-theory-split.md)

Let $T\subseteq(X\times\omega)^{<\omega}$ be a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md). Its projection is

$$
p[T]=\{x\in\omega^\omega:\exists y\in X^\omega\ ((y,x)\in[T])\}.
$$

The tree $T$ is a Suslin representation of $p[T]$.
