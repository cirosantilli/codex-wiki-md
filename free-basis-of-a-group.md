# Free basis of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

A subset $X$ is a free basis of a [group](group-split.md) $G$ when the natural map from the [free group](free-group.md) on $X$ to $G$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Equivalently, $X$ generates $G$ and no nonempty freely reduced word in $X\cup X^{-1}$ equals the identity. A generating set is not automatically a free basis.
