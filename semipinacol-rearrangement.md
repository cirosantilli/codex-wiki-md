# Semipinacol rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Formation of a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) accompanies migration of an adjacent C–H or C–C bond and departure of a [leaving group](leaving-group.md). An [alkoxide](alkoxide.md)-assisted [hydride](hydride.md) migration can convert a suitably aligned [halohydrin](halohydrin.md) into a [ketone](ketone.md).
