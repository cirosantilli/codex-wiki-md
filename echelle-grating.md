# Echelle grating

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echelle_grating)

An echelle grating works in high [diffraction orders](diffraction-order.md). A separate [cross-disperser](cross-disperser.md) separates their overlapping [optical spectra](optical-spectrum.md) on a two-dimensional detector.
