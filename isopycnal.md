# Isopycnal

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isopycnal)

An isopycnal is a surface of constant [mass density](density.md). In a [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), it is equivalently a surface of constant buoyancy.
