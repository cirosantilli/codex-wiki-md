# Linear regression model

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

A [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) specifying a response as a linear combination of known regressor columns and an error. Common assumptions are $\mathbb E[\varepsilon]=0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon)=\sigma^2I$, although a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) is an additional assumption, not part of the displayed equation alone. Full [column rank](column-rank.md) of the [design matrix](design-matrix.md) makes the coefficients identifiable. Statistical association does not by itself give a [causal effect](causal-effect.md).
