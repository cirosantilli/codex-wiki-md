# Generator of a strongly continuous semigroup is closed and densely defined

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md)

The [Yosida averaging of a semigroup](yosida-averaging-of-a-semigroup.md) gives $x_h=h^{-1}\int_0^hS(t)x\,dt\in D(A)$ with $x_h\to x$, proving that the [generator domain](generator-domain.md) is dense. For $x\in D(A)$, the [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md) identity $S(t)x-x=\int_0^tS(s)Ax\,ds$ holds. If $x_n\to x$ and $Ax_n\to y$, pass to the limit in this identity and divide by $t$. The [strongly continuous semigroup](c0-semigroup.md) property makes the resulting limit $y$, so $x\in D(A)$ and $Ax=y$. This proves that the [infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md) is a [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md).
