# Reduced product

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

Given nonempty [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) $\mathcal M_i$ and a proper [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) $F$, a reduced product identifies product functions $f,g$ when $\{i:f(i)=g(i)\}\in F$. Operations are interpreted coordinatewise, and relations hold when their coordinate truth sets belong to $F$. For an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) this is an [ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md). [Primitive positive formulas](primitive-positive-formula.md) transfer through an arbitrary proper filter, whereas [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) and [logical negation](negation.md) can fail to transfer.
