# Exact dilution obstruction from Schmidt rank

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement dilution](entanglement-dilution.md)

For $0<p<1$, every bipartition of $\Psi_p^{\otimes n}$ has [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md) $2^n$, while $m$ copies of a [GHZ state](greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md) have [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md) $2^m$. [Schmidt-rank contraction under product operators](schmidt-rank-contraction-under-product-operators.md) therefore forbids any exact successful [LOCC](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) branch when $m<n$. Approximate [entanglement dilution](entanglement-dilution.md) avoids this obstruction by retaining only a high-weight [quantum typical subspace](quantum-typical-subspace.md), whose dimension has logarithm $nh_2(p)+o(n)$. Exact finite-block conversion and asymptotically faithful conversion are different tasks.
