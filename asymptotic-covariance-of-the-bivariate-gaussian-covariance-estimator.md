# Asymptotic covariance of the bivariate Gaussian covariance estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum-likelihood covariance estimator for centered Gaussian data](maximum-likelihood-covariance-estimator-for-centered-gaussian-data.md)

Writing $a=\Sigma_{11}$, $c=\Sigma_{12}$, and $b=\Sigma_{22}$, the vector $(\widehat\Sigma_{11},\widehat\Sigma_{12},\widehat\Sigma_{22})$ has asymptotic covariance

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2a^2&2ac&2c^2\\
2ac&ab+c^2&2bc\\
2c^2&2bc&2b^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
