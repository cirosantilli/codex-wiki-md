# Rickettsia parkeri

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)

[Rickettsia parkeri](rickettsia-parkeri.md) is a spotted-fever-group intracellular bacterium used to study Sca2-dependent [actin](actin.md) assembly. Its motility machinery should not be generalized to every rickettsial species.
