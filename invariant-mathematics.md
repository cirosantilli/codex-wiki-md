# Invariant (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariant_(mathematics))

An [invariant](invariant-mathematics.md) is a property or quantity unchanged by a specified class of transformations. The transformations are part of the definition: an invariant of a [group action](group-action.md) and an [invariant set](invariant-set-of-a-measure-preserving-transformation.md) of a measure-preserving transformation concern different transformation classes.
