# Stationary-direction bound for singular support

↑ **Parent:** [Conic support of an oscillatory amplitude](conic-support-of-an-oscillatory-amplitude.md)

For a homogeneous [phase function](phase-function.md) $\Phi$ and finite-order [symbol class](symbol-class.md) amplitude $a$, the [singular support](singular-support.md) of $I_\Phi(a)$ lies in the spatial projection of the closed conic amplitude support intersected with $\nabla_\theta\Phi=0$. Away from that set, the frequency gradient is uniformly nonzero on compact spatial sets and supported directions. Repeated frequency [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) lowers the amplitude order until every desired spatial derivative is absolutely integrable. If the ordinary amplitude support is already conic, it gives the same bound.
