# Wave reflection by odd extension

↑ **Parent:** [D'Alembert's formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md)

For the half-line [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$ with a zero [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) at $x=0$, extend both the initial displacement and initial [velocity](velocity.md) as [odd functions](odd-function.md) to the full line. [D'Alembert's formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md) then gives an [odd](odd-function.md) solution in $x$, so $u(0,t)=0$. Restriction to $x\geq0$ solves the desired [initial-boundary value problem](initial-boundary-value-problem.md). With zero initial [velocity](velocity.md), the displayed formula shows reflection at the fixed endpoint with reversal of the displacement sign. Continuous piecewise smooth displacement data give a [weak solution](weak-solution.md); a [classical solution](classical-solution.md) requires sufficient additional regularity. In particular, a kink in the initial displacement can prevent the pointwise time [derivative](derivative.md) at that kink from taking the assigned initial [velocity](velocity.md), even though the initial [velocity](velocity.md) is attained in the $L^2$ sense.
