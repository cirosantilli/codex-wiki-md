# No-scale supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

In a no-scale sector, the [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) satisfies $K_iK^{i\bar j}K_{\bar j}=3$. If the [superpotential](superpotential.md) is independent of that sector, its covariant derivatives contribute $3e^K|W|^2$, cancelling the universal $-3e^K|W|^2$ in the [supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md). Spectator sectors can still contribute to the potential. This cancellation permits zero-energy minima with broken [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and exact flat scalar directions.
