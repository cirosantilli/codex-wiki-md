# Transonic spherical accretion rate in a power-law potential

↑ **Parent:** [Transonic spherical flow in a power-law potential](transonic-spherical-flow-in-a-power-law-potential.md)

With reservoir density $\rho_\infty$ and [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_\infty$, put $q=2/\beta-1/2$ and $h=1-q(\gamma-1)>0$. The selected [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) is $4\pi\rho_\infty(A\beta/2)^{2/\beta}c_\infty^{1-4/\beta}h^{q-1/(\gamma-1)}$. It follows by evaluating the conserved spherical flux at the [sonic point](sonic-point.md).
