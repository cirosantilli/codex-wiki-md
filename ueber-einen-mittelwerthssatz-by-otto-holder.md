By [Otto Hölder](otto-holder.md), credited as O. Hölder. Published in Nachrichten von der Königl. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften und der Georg-Augusts-Universität zu Göttingen, pp. 38–47, 1889, Göttingen: Dieterichsche Verlags-Buchhandlung. German-language article. Wikisource hosts the inspected transcription and links the scanned original. The named presenter is not a coauthor.

Transcription and scan provenance: [https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Ueber_einen_Mittelwerthssatz](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Ueber_einen_Mittelwerthssatz)

Inspected passages: opening §§1–2, printed pp. 38–39. The 1889 date is the original article publication year.
