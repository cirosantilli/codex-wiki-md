# Frobenius inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product-split.md)

For complex [matrices](matrix.md), the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\langle A,B\rangle_F=\operatorname{tr}(A^*B)$ is conjugate-linear in its first argument. On real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) it becomes $\operatorname{tr}(AB)$. In particular $\langle A,xx^T\rangle_F=x^TAx$, identifying [quadratic forms](quadratic-form-split.md) with linear tests on [matrices](matrix.md).
