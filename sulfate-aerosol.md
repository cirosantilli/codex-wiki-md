# Sulfate aerosol

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

Atmospheric particles containing [sulfate](sulfate.md), commonly in sulfuric-acid/[water](water.md) or neutralized-salt mixtures. Particle size, [water](water.md) uptake, acidity and accompanying material determine scattering, absorption, [cloud](cloud.md) activation and heterogeneous chemistry; [sulfate](sulfate.md) [mass](mass.md) alone does not specify [radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md).
