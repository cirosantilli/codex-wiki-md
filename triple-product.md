# Triple product

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_product)

For three vectors in oriented Euclidean three-space, the scalar [triple product](triple-product.md) is $a\cdot(b\times c)$ and the vector triple product is $a\times(b\times c)$. The former is their signed volume; the latter is the vector operation appearing in the [vector triple product identity](vector-triple-product.md).
