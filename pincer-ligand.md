# Pincer ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A ligand with three donor sites arranged to bind one [metal](metal.md) in a meridional pattern. The framework may impose a preferred geometry and may itself undergo redox or proton-transfer changes.
