# Affine expression

↑ **Parent:** [Affine function](affine-function.md)

An expression of the form $a_0+\sum_{j=1}^m a_jx_j$, with fixed coefficients $a_j$. It contains a constant term and terms of degree one, without products of variables. It represents an [affine function](affine-function.md) once the scalar domain is specified.
