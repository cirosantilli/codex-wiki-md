# Fourier coefficient of a measure on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a finite measure](fourier-transform-of-a-finite-measure.md)

For a finite [complex measure](complex-measure.md) on the additive [circle group](circle-group.md) $\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)$, its Fourier coefficients are integrals against the [group characters](continuous-unitary-character.md) $e^{-inx}$, $n\in\mathbb Z$. They obey $|\widehat\mu(n)|\leq\|\mu\|$, where the norm is the [total variation norm of a measure](total-variation-norm-of-a-measure.md). A [probability measure](probability-measure.md) has zeroth coefficient one. The normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) $m(dx)=dx/(2\pi)$ has all nonzero-frequency coefficients zero. A bounded family of finite [Radon measures](radon-measure.md) converges in the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) exactly when its coefficients converge to those of the proposed limit: extend linear combinations to all continuous tests using the density of [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md) and the common variation bound.
