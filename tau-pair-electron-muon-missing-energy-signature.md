# Tau-pair electron-muon missing-energy signature

↑ **Parent:** [SPEAR Mark I tau discovery experiment](spear-mark-i-tau-discovery-experiment.md)

In the modern [weak charged current](charged-current.md) interpretation, one allowed assignment is

$$
e^+e^-\to\tau^-\tau^+,\qquad
\tau^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\tau,\qquad
\tau^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu\bar\nu_\tau.
$$

The [electron antineutrino](electron-antineutrino.md), [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md), [muon neutrino](muon-neutrino.md) and [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) of the [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md) carry away [energy](energy-split.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md). There are four undetected [particles](particle-physics.md) in this decay assignment. Interchanging which parent decays electronically gives the opposite [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) assignment. Each parent conserves [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and, in this approximation, total [lepton number](lepton-number.md); [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) applies separately to production and to each [particle decay](particle-decay.md).

If $B_e$ and $B_\mu$ denote the corresponding [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md), independent parent decays give an inclusive mixed-flavour fraction $2B_eB_\mu$. The factor two counts the two assignments to opposite parent charges. For chosen $B_e=B_\mu=1/5$, the mixed fraction is $2/25$, before acceptance and [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md). Correlations between the parents' [spins](spin.md) can affect angular selection even when the fully inclusive [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md) factorize.

The [neutrinos](neutrino.md) in that equation are a physical interpretation to test. Neither a deficit in visible [energy](energy-split.md) nor two unusual tracks alone establishes their identities or uniquely counts them. Even a compatible [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) and an angular distribution require [particle identification](particle-identification.md), background checks and agreement across further [particle decay](particle-decay.md) channels. The direct detection milestone for the [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md) was in 2000, separate from the charged [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) discovery.
