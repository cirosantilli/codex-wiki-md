# Chiral Z-boson partial width

↑ **Parent:** [Z boson](z-boson.md)

At leading order, for final [fermions](fermion.md) much lighter than the [Z boson](z-boson.md), the partial [decay width](decay-width.md) is proportional to $N_c(|g_L|^2+|g_R|^2)$. Here $N_c$ is the final-state [color multiplicity in a decay width](color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md), and $g_L=T_{3L}-Q\sin^2\theta_W$, $g_R=T_{3R}-Q\sin^2\theta_W$ are the dimensionless left- and right-[chirality](chirality-physics.md) couplings. The common normalization cancels in ratios.

For an [electron](electron.md), $g_L=-1/2+\sin^2\theta_W$, $g_R=\sin^2\theta_W$. For one active [neutrino](neutrino.md) flavour, $g_L=1/2$, $g_R=0$. Therefore

$$
\frac{\Gamma(Z\to\nu\bar\nu)}{\Gamma(Z\to e^-e^+)}=\frac{1/4}{(-1/2+\sin^2\theta_W)^2+\sin^4\theta_W}.
$$

There is no extra flavour factor for a single specified [neutrino](neutrino.md) species. Summing the three active species produces three times that invisible partial width, within the same massless approximation.
