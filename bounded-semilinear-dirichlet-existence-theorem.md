# Bounded semilinear Dirichlet existence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder fixed-point theorem](schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md)

For a smooth bounded domain and a bounded continuous scalar function $f$, this problem has a [weak solution](weak-solution.md). Let $S$ be the zero-Dirichlet inverse of $-\Delta$, defined by the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md). The [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) gives $\|Sh\|_2\leq C_P^2\|h\|_2$. The [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) makes $S:L^2\to L^2$ compact. The [Nemytskii operator](nemytskii-operator.md) $N_f$ is continuous and has bounded image, so $T=S\circ N_f$ is a [nonlinear compact operator](nonlinear-compact-operator.md) mapping a sufficiently large closed $L^2$ ball into itself. The [Schauder fixed-point theorem](schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md) gives a [fixed point](fixed-point.md), necessarily in $H_0^1$, and its defining identity is the desired [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md). This proves existence; uniqueness requires additional assumptions.
