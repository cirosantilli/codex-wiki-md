# Pfaff transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md)

With compatible branches and initially near $z=0$,

$$
F\left(a,c-b;c;\frac{z}{z-1}\right)
=(1-z)^aF(a,b;c;z).
$$

It follows by applying a Möbius transformation and a dependent-variable rescaling to the same [Papperitz symbol](papperitz-symbol.md), then matching the normalized analytic solution at zero.
