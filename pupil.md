# Pupil

↑ **Parent:** [Iris](iris.md)

The opening in the [iris](iris.md) through which [light](light.md) enters the eye. Its aperture affects admitted pencils of rays; the [cornea](cornea.md) and [crystalline lens](crystalline-lens.md) provide focusing power.
