# Accommodation (vertebrate eye)

↑ **Parent:** [Eye](eye.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accommodation_(vertebrate_eye))

Adjustment of the [eye](eye.md)'s optical power to maintain a focused image on the [retina](retina.md) as object distance changes. For near vision the [ciliary muscle](ciliary-muscle.md) contracts, tension in the [zonular fibres](zonular-fibre.md) decreases and the elastic [crystalline lens](crystalline-lens.md) becomes more rounded.
