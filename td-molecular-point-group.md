# Td molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The 24-operation molecular [point group](point-group.md) of an ideal regular [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md), including improper operations. Its [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) are $A_1,A_2,E,T_1,T_2$, with dimensions $1,1,2,3,3$. The polar-vector representation is $T_2$, the axial-vector representation is $T_1$, and symmetric quadratic functions transform as $A_1+E+T_2$.
