# Polymer coil in slit confinement

↑ **Parent:** [de Gennes confinement scaling](de-gennes-confinement-scaling.md)

In a slit, a confinement blob is three-dimensional but the chain of blobs forms a two-dimensional [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md). With $g\sim(D/b)^{5/3}$ and in-plane exponent $3/4$, $R_\parallel\sim D(N/g)^{3/4}$. There is no single preferred axial extension as in a tube. A strictly planar chain confined further to a narrow strip is a different geometry and gives $X\sim L(b/D)^{1/3}$ under the two-dimensional blob estimate.
