# Nuclear reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reaction)

A [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) transforms one or more [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md) through interactions with particles or other nuclei. Total energy, momentum, electric charge and nucleon number constrain ordinary low-energy reaction bookkeeping. [Nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md), [alpha capture](alpha-capture.md), [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) and [photodisintegration](nuclear-photodisintegration.md) are important examples in [stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md). Weak processes can also change the proton-to-neutron ratio.
