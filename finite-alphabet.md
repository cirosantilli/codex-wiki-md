# Finite alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Alphabet](alphabet.md)

A [finite alphabet](finite-alphabet.md) is an [alphabet](alphabet.md) whose symbols form a [finite set](finite-set.md). The binary [alphabet](alphabet.md) $\{0,1\}$ and a finite vocabulary of possible messages are examples.
