# Symplectic form

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

A symplectic form is a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) of degree two whose value on every tangent space is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form. In canonical position-momentum coordinates it is $\sum_i dq_i\wedge dp_i$.
