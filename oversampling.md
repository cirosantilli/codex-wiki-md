# Oversampling

↑ **Parent:** [Analog-to-digital converter](analog-to-digital-converter.md)

[oversampling](oversampling.md) takes samples faster than the minimum rate required by the desired [signal](signal.md) [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md). With suitable uncorrelated conversion [noise](noise-signal-processing.md), digital filtering removes [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) outside that [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) before decimation. Independent-error averaging improves [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) as $N^{-1/2}$; fourfold averaging ideally gains one bit of amplitude resolution. Repetition of an unchanged biased code does not produce the same benefit.
