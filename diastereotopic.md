# Diastereotopic

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

Two sites are [diastereotopic](diastereotopic.md) if replacing one or the other with the same test group gives [diastereomers](diastereomer.md). In an achiral solvent they can have different [chemical shifts](chemical-shift.md), unlike a simple pair of symmetry-related sites. A rigid chiral metal chelate can make the two hydrogens of a linker methylene diastereotopic.
