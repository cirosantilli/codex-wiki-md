# Ray in an exponential-speed medium

↑ **Parent:** [Fermat principle](fermat-principle.md)

For positive $\lambda$ and light speed $c(y)=c_0e^{\lambda y}$, the [Fermat principle](fermat-principle.md) uses the time integrand $c_0^{-1}e^{-\lambda y}\sqrt{1+y'^2}$. The [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) sets $e^{-\lambda y}/\sqrt{1+y'^2}$ constant. Solving this first integral gives the displayed symmetric ray between $(\pm a,0)$ when $0<\lambda a<\pi/2$. Its travel time is $2\sin(\lambda a)/(c_0\lambda)$. The ray's central height diverges as $\lambda a\uparrow\pi/2$, while its travel time tends to $2/(c_0\lambda)$.
