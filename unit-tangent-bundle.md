# Unit tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_tangent_bundle)

The unit tangent bundle of a Riemannian manifold $M$ is the [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) of its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). Its points are pairs $(x,v)$ with $x\in M$, $v\in T_xM$, and $\lVert v\rVert=1$.
