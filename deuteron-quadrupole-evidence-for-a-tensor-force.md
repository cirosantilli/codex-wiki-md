# Deuteron quadrupole evidence for a tensor force

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear tensor force](nuclear-tensor-force.md)

An isotropic pure $L=0$ spatial state has zero [electric quadrupole moment](electric-quadrupole-moment.md). The nonzero [deuteron](deuteron.md) quadrupole moment consequently requires anisotropic structure. With ground-state $J^P=1^+$, a [nuclear tensor force](nuclear-tensor-force.md) can mix $\,{}^3S_1$ and $\,{}^3D_1$ waves while preserving total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [parity](parity.md). A purely central spin-independent potential cannot produce this mixing. The inference of an orientation-dependent force was already explicit in 1939.[Abstract and opening discussion, Nature 144, 476–477](the-electric-quadrupole-moment-of-the-deuteron-and-the-field-theory-of-nuclear-forces-by-christian-moller-and-leon-rosenfeld.md)
