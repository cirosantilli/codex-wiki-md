# Position four-vector

↑ **Parent:** [Four-vector](four-vector.md)

The spacetime coordinates of an event combine into a [four-vector](four-vector.md) $X^a=(ct,x,y,z)$ under Lorentz transformations between inertial frames sharing an origin. For a massive particle, differentiation with respect to [proper time](proper-time.md) gives its [four-velocity](four-velocity.md). Under a spacetime translation, positions acquire an additive offset, while displacement four-vectors transform linearly under the Lorentz part.
