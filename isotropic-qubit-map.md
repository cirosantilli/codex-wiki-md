# Isotropic qubit map

↑ **Parent:** [Superoperator](superoperator.md)

This one-parameter family maps every [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) $\mathbf s$ to $r\mathbf s$. It is unital, trace preserving and Hermiticity preserving for every real $r$. It is a [positive map](positive-linear-map.md) exactly when $|r|\leq1$, because the output eigenvalues of a state are $(1\pm r|\mathbf s|)/2$. Its [Choi matrix](choi-matrix.md) has eigenvalues $(1+3r)/2$ and $(1-r)/2$ with multiplicities one and three in the trace-two convention, so [Choi's theorem on completely positive maps](choi-s-theorem-on-completely-positive-maps.md) gives complete positivity exactly for $-1/3\leq r\leq1$. In that range the [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md) is a mixture of conjugations by $I,X,Y,Z$ with probabilities $(1+3r)/4,(1-r)/4,(1-r)/4,(1-r)/4$. The physically allowed maps are qubit [depolarizing channels](quantum-depolarizing-channel.md), including a limited range of negative Bloch scaling.
