# Lambertian reflectance

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambertian_reflectance)

Lambertian reflectance is the ideal diffuse-reflection model with direction-independent outgoing radiance, the frequency-integrated [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md). A surface with hemispherical reflectance $\rho$ receiving irradiance $F$ has outgoing radiance $\rho F/\pi$. The observed radiant intensity still acquires the projected-area cosine factor described by [Lambert's cosine law](lambert-s-cosine-law.md).
