# Dirichlet convection-diffusion contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Energy method](energy-method.md)

For zero endpoint values and real constant drift, integration by parts gives $\tfrac12 d\|u\|_{L^2}^2/dt=-\|u_x\|_{L^2}^2$. The drift contributes only a zero boundary term in the real part. Thus the solution is contractive, uniformly in the drift. Multiplication by $e^{\kappa x}$ converts the equation to a [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with constant killing rate $\kappa^2$, providing existence and uniqueness as well as the direct [energy estimate](energy-estimate.md).
