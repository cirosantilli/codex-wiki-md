# Phase-known genotype

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype](genotype.md)

At several [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md), a phase-known genotype specifies which [alleles](allele.md) occur together on each chromosome, hence its two [haplotypes](haplotype.md). An unphased multilocus [genotype](genotype.md) may be compatible with several haplotype pairs. For two heterozygous loci, $AB/ab$ and $Ab/aB$ have identical unphased genotypes but different transmission probabilities when [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md) is present.
