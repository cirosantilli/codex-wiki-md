# Cardinal collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

A [forcing](forcing-split.md) collapses a ground-model [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$ if it forces a [bijection](bijection.md) from a smaller [ordinal](ordinal.md) onto the ground [ordinal](ordinal.md) $\kappa$. The [ordinal](ordinal.md) itself is preserved; its being an initial [ordinal](ordinal.md) is lost.
