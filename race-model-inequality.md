# Race model inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Redundant-signals race model](redundant-signals-race-model.md)

Under context-invariant channel distributions, $P(\min(T_A,T_V)\leq t)=P(T_A\leq t\ \mathrm{or}\ T_V\leq t)\leq F_A(t)+F_V(t)$ by the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md); [probability](probability.md) is also at most one. No [independence](independent-random-variables.md) assumption is needed. A reliable violation rejects this model with those marginals, not every possible separate-channel or decision account.
