# Metallicity distribution in a leaky box

↑ **Parent:** [Leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution](leaky-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

For initially pristine gas, zero bulk mass return, a constant [stellar yield](stellar-yield.md) $y$ and a well-mixed [galactic outflow](galactic-outflow.md) of [mass-loading factor](mass-loading-factor.md) $\nu$, gas conservation gives $dM_g=-(1+\nu)dM_s$ and metal accounting gives $M_g\,dZ=y\,dM_s$. Consequently $M_g=M_{g,0}e^{-Z/y_e}$ with $y_e=y/(1+\nu)$. Since $dM_s/dZ=M_g/y$, complete depletion gives a normalized mass-weighted [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) $p(Z)=e^{-Z/y_e}/y_e$ and mean [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md) $y_e$. A time-independent [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) makes the distribution of surviving tracers proportional to this distribution; a sample selected by light need not have the same weights.
