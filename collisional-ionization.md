# Collisional ionization

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

In [collisional ionization](collisional-ionization.md), an energetic collision frees a bound [Electron](electron.md). The process consumes kinetic energy and changes the [Electron](electron.md) and ion populations controlling plasma cooling. In a thermal gas its rate competes with recombination and depends strongly on the [ionization](ionization.md) threshold relative to temperature.
