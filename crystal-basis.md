# Crystal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_basis)

A crystal basis is the combinatorial limit of a basis of a quantum-group representation. Its colored directed graph records the actions of the [Kashiwara lowering operators](kashiwara-operator.md).
