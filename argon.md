# Argon

↑ **Parent:** [Noble gas](noble-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argon)

The [chemical element](chemical-element.md) with [atomic number](atomic-number.md) eighteen. [Argon](argon.md) is a colourless [noble gas](noble-gas.md). It can provide a protective atmosphere around hot, reactive materials, for example in [welding](welding.md), where excluding [oxygen](oxygen.md) limits [oxidation](oxidation.md). Commercial production separates it by [distillation](distillation.md) of liquid air.[Periodic Table by Royal Society of Chemistry; Fact box, Uses and Natural abundance: atomic number, group, protective atmosphere and liquid-air distillation](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/argon-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)

Its discovery shows why a small disagreement can matter more than a reassuringly repeatable result. Two preparations can each give consistent measurements while producing different [mixtures](mixture-split.md). The [Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment](rayleigh-ramsay-argon-density-experiment.md) investigated just such a discrepancy. [Gas density](gas-density.md) links the weighing to a mean [molar mass](molar-mass.md); a separate [separation process](separation-process.md) tests which component accounts for it.
