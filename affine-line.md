# Affine line

↑ **Parent:** [Affine scheme](affine-scheme.md)

The affine line over a field $k$ is $\mathbb A_k^1=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$.

The one-dimensional scheme has an [affine space](affine-space.md) of rational points over its base field; its scheme structure also records non-rational points and local rings.
