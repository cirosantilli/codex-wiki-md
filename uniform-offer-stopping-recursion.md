# Uniform-offer stopping recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal stopping](optimal-stopping.md)

With independent [uniform distributions](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[0,1]$ as sequential offers and a finite number of rounds, the continuation value with $j-1$ offers remaining is $v_{j-1}$. Accept the current offer when it reaches that threshold. Integrating the maximum of the offer and continuation value gives the recursion, by the [Snell envelope](snell-envelope.md) principle.
