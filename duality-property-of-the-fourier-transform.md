# Duality property of the Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

For the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $\mathcal Ff(\omega)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt$, [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) at $-\omega$ gives $f(-\omega)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{\mathbb R}(\mathcal Ff)(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt$. Thus transforming twice reflects the argument and multiplies by $2\pi$. The identity holds directly on the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md), and extends to the [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) and to [tempered distributions](tempered-distribution.md). It lets an already known transform pair be used in reverse without another integration.
