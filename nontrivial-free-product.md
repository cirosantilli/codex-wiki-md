# Nontrivial free product

↑ **Parent:** [Free product](free-product.md)

A [free product](free-product.md) $A*B$ is nontrivial as a splitting when both factors differ from the [trivial group](trivial-group.md). Alternating words $(ab)^r$ with $a,b\ne1$ in different factors show that every such splitting gives an [infinite group](infinite-group.md). The distinct normal forms $ab$ and $ba$ also show that the group is not an [abelian group](abelian-group.md).
