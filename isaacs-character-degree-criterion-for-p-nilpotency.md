# Isaacs' character-degree criterion for p-nilpotency

↑ **Parent:** [P-nilpotent group](p-nilpotent-group.md)

The integer $h=|G:G'|_p$ divides $s=\sum_{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{p'}(G)}\chi(1)^2$. The group is [p-nilpotent](p-nilpotent-group.md) exactly when $p\nmid s/h$. To see this, let $N=O^p(G)$. Invariant p-prime-degree characters of $N$ extend, and each has exactly $h$ extensions of p-prime degree. Conjugation by the p-group $G/N$ then gives $s/h\equiv|N|\pmod p$. Thus the quotient is prime to $p$ precisely when $N$ is a [normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md).
