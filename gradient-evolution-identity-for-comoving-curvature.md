# Gradient evolution identity for comoving curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md)

For adiabatic scalar perturbations without anisotropic stress, define $A=4\pi Ga^2(\bar\rho+\bar P)=\mathcal H^2-\mathcal H'$ and $c_a^2=\bar P'/\bar\rho'$. In [conformal Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md), $\mathcal R=-\phi-\mathcal H(\phi'+\mathcal H\phi)/A$. Background conservation gives $A'/A=-\mathcal H(1+3c_a^2)$. Differentiating the expression for $\mathcal R$ and using the scalar-potential equation gives the displayed identity. For regular adiabatic modes with nonzero background enthalpy, evolution is suppressed on scales $k\ll\mathcal H$.
