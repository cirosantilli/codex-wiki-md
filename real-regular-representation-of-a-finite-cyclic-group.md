# Real regular representation of a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Real representation](real-representation.md)

The real regular representation of $C_n$ is the direct sum of the [irreducible real representations of a finite cyclic group](irreducible-real-representations-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md), each once. A real Fourier basis consists of the constant vector, the alternating vector when $n$ is even, and cosine-sine pairs for frequencies $1\leq k<n/2$.
