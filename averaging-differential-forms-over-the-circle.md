# Averaging differential forms over the circle

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

Let $S^1$ act on $M\times S^1$ by rotation of the second factor. Averaging a [differential form](differential-form-split.md) over this action is a cochain map. Every rotation is [homotopic](homotopy.md) to the identity through smooth rotations, and integrating [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) along them gives a [cochain homotopy](cochain-homotopy.md) between the averaging map and the identity. Consequently every [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class has a rotation-invariant representative, and an invariant exact form has an invariant primitive obtained by averaging any primitive.
