# Acetonitrile

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Acetonitrile](acetonitrile.md), $\mathrm{CH_3CN}$, can coordinate through [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) as a neutral two-electron donor. Bound [acetonitrile](acetonitrile.md) can be displaced by a stronger donor or an intramolecular chelating group.
