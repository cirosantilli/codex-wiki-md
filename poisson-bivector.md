# Poisson bivector

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

A smooth antisymmetric contravariant two-tensor defines a biderivation $\{F,G\}=\omega(dF,dG)$. It is a Poisson bivector when this bracket satisfies the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). Its rank may vary; where it is nondegenerate it inverts to a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). The [coordinate Jacobi condition for a Poisson bivector](coordinate-jacobi-condition-for-a-poisson-bivector.md) makes the integrability constraint explicit.
