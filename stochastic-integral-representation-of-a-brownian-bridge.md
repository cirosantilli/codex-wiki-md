# Stochastic integral representation of a Brownian bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md)

For $t<1$ this [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) solves $dX_t=dB_t-X_t(1-t)^{-1}dt$. The [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md) also gives $X_t=B_t-(1-t)\int_0^tB_s(1-s)^{-2}ds$. The process is centered [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md), with [covariance](covariance.md) $\min(s,t)-st$, and has a continuous extension $X_1=0$. [Time reversal of a Brownian bridge](time-reversal-of-a-brownian-bridge.md) proves endpoint continuity without assuming it at the start.
