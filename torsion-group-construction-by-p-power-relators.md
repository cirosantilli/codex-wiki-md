# Torsion group construction by p-power relators

↑ **Parent:** [P-deficiency](p-deficiency.md)

Enumerate all nonidentity words $w_i$ in the rank-two [free group](free-group.md) and impose relations $w_i^{p^{i+2}}=1$. The resulting two-generated [group](group-split.md) has

$$
\operatorname{def}_p\geq2-\sum_{i\geq1}p^{-(i+2)}=2-\frac1{p^2(p-1)}>1.
$$

It is infinite by [p-deficiency at least one implies infinitude](p-deficiency-at-least-one-implies-infinitude.md). Each element has order a power of $p$, so it is a [torsion group](torsion-group.md). The infinitely many relators are essential to this particular construction; finite generation does not imply a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md).
