# Laurent monomial

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md)

A [monomial](monomial.md) allowing negative integer exponents in invertible variables. Finite sums of Laurent monomials form a [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md). In a graded coordinate ring each has total degree $\sum_i a_i$.
