# Acetic acid fermentation

↑ **Parent:** [Fermentation](fermentation.md)

The historical name for microbial oxidation of [ethanol](ethanol.md) into [acetic acid](acetic-acid.md) with [oxygen](oxygen.md). Its aerobic metabolism differs from the narrow biochemical definition of [fermentation](fermentation.md).
