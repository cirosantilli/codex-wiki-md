# Square

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangle](rectangle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square)

A [rectangle](rectangle.md) with all four side lengths equal. It is a regular four-sided [polygon](polygon.md). Rotations and translations preserve this definition; its sides need not be parallel to the coordinate axes.
