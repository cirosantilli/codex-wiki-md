# Regular theory

↑ **Parent:** [Regular logic](regular-logic.md)

A regular theory has axioms that are sequents between [regular formulas](regular-formula.md). It has a [regular syntactic category](regular-syntactic-category.md) and a classifying topos of sheaves for the [regular coverage](regular-coverage.md). Its regular formulas enjoy the [disjunction property of regular theories](disjunction-property-of-regular-theories.md) when a finite disjunction is allowed as an outer coherent conclusion.
