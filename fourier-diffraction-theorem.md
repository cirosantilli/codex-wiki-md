# Fourier diffraction theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse scattering problem](inverse-scattering-problem.md)

The [Born approximation for scalar wave scattering](born-approximation-for-scalar-wave-scattering.md) relates an incident [plane wave](plane-wave.md) and the transverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of its scattered field to samples of the three-dimensional Fourier transform of a [scattering potential](scattering-potential.md) on a shifted [Ewald sphere](ewald-sphere.md). One incident direction at one frequency samples a surface, rather than an entire three-dimensional frequency region. Additional illuminations, frequencies or structural constraints are needed for a full reconstruction.
