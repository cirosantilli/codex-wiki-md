# Regression spline

↑ **Parent:** [Spline approximation](spline-approximation.md)

A [regression spline](regression-spline.md) fits a [regression function](regression-function.md) in a finite-dimensional [spline approximation](spline-approximation.md) space with selected knots. Evaluating its basis at predictor values gives a [design matrix](design-matrix.md), reducing fitting to regression on basis coefficients.
