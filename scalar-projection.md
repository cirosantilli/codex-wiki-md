# Scalar projection

↑ **Parent:** [Dot product](dot-product.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_projection)

The [scalar projection](scalar-projection.md) of a real [Euclidean vector](euclidean-vector.md) $\mathbf a$ onto a nonzero [Euclidean vector](euclidean-vector.md) $\mathbf c$ is its signed component along the [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf c/\|\mathbf c\|$:

$$
\operatorname{comp}_{\mathbf c}\mathbf a=\mathbf a\cdot\frac{\mathbf c}{\|\mathbf c\|}.
$$

For $\mathbf a=(3,4)$, the [scalar projection](scalar-projection.md) onto $(2,0)$ is $3$, while that onto $(-2,0)$ is $-3$. Reversing the measuring direction reverses the sign; the [scalar projection](scalar-projection.md) is not necessarily a nonnegative length. For nonzero $\mathbf a$, it also equals $\|\mathbf a\|\cos\theta$, using the [angle](angle.md) with $\mathbf c$ and its [cosine](cosine.md). For the [zero vector](zero-vector.md) it is zero, even though that [angle](angle.md) is undefined.
