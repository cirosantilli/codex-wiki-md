# Complete statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Statistic](statistic.md)

A statistic is complete for a parameter family if the displayed implication holds for every measurable $g$ integrable under all parameter values. This property does not itself assert sufficiency; a [complete sufficient statistic](complete-sufficient-statistic.md) has both properties. A nonconstant unbiased estimator of a known constant provides a direct obstruction to completeness.
