# Dorsal raphe nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A brainstem nucleus containing many [serotonin](serotonin.md)-releasing [neurons](neuron-split.md). In stress-controllability experiments its activity depends on network regulation, illustrating why global transmitter amount alone is an inadequate account of behaviour.
