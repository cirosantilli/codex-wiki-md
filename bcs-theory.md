# BCS theory

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCS_theory)

BCS theory describes a superconducting instability caused by attractive interactions between opposite-momentum fermions near a [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md).

[John Bardeen](john-bardeen.md), [Leon Neil Cooper](leon-neil-cooper.md) and [John Robert Schrieffer](john-robert-schrieffer.md) developed [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) in 1957, using attraction mediated by [phonons](phonon.md).[pp.1175–1177, introduction and microscopic pairing mechanism](theory-of-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen-and-others.md)

Weak-coupling [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md) overlap. The [Cooper instability](cooper-instability.md) reorganizes an unpaired [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md); it is not [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md) of compact, pre-existing [molecules](molecule-split.md). The original paper explicitly distinguishes these pictures.[p.1177, footnote 18 and coherence-distance discussion](theory-of-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen-and-others.md)

Two complementary clues made the microscopic problem concrete. The [superconducting isotope effect](superconducting-isotope-effect.md) connected the transition to [phonons](phonon.md).[p.55, isotope effect as a clue to the microscopic mechanism](electron-phonon-interactions-and-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen.md)

A 1957 transmission experiment used thin [lead](lead.md) and [tin](tin.md) films as samples in far-[infrared](infrared.md) and millimeter-[microwave](microwave.md) measurements. The measured transmission was converted to complex [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md), normalized to the normal-state value. Comparing film thickness, resistance, annealing and the two metals tested the response's reproducibility. Weak low-[photon](photon.md)-[energy](energy-split.md) absorption followed by an onset supported a [superconducting energy gap](superconducting-energy-gap.md); its extraction required a thin-film electrodynamic model.[Abstract: sample metals, transmission method, normal-state normalization and comparisons; inferred excitation gap](conductivity-of-superconducting-films-for-photon-energies-between-0-3-and-40ktc-by-r-e-glover-iii-and-m-tinkham.md)

The later [Giaever superconducting tunnelling experiment](giaever-gap-experiment.md) provided another way to resolve the [superconducting energy gap](superconducting-energy-gap.md) through [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md).[p.61, section 4, tunnelling conductance and the superconducting density of states](electron-phonon-interactions-and-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen.md) The derivations below explain what the model predicts, including the distinction between a single [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)'s minimum [energy](energy-split.md) and the threshold for making two of them.
