# Klein quadric

↑ **Parent:** [Plücker embedding](plucker-embedding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klein_quadric)

The [Plücker embedding](plucker-embedding.md) of $\operatorname{Gr}(2,\mathbb C^4)$ is a smooth projective quadric in $\mathbb{CP}^5$. An antisymmetric $4\times4$ matrix is nonzero and decomposable precisely when its Pfaffian vanishes, giving the displayed equation. The wedge pairing of two points on this quadric vanishes exactly when the corresponding two-planes intersect. Thus the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md) carries the complex conformal null-separation relation of [complexified compactified Minkowski space](complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md).
