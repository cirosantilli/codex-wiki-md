# Inverse Lorentz action on gamma matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

With $\{\gamma_\mu,\gamma_\nu\}=2\eta_{\mu\nu}$, $\Omega=\omega^{\mu\nu}\gamma_{\mu\nu}/4$, and $\Lambda=e^\omega$, the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) gives $[\Omega,\gamma^\rho]=-\omega^\rho{}_{\mu}\gamma^\mu$. Exponentiation proves the inverse Lorentz action displayed above, equivalently $S^{-1}\gamma^\rho S=\Lambda^\rho{}_{\mu}\gamma^\mu$. This second order is what combines with $\psi\mapsto S\psi$ and the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) action $\bar\psi\mapsto\bar\psi S^{-1}$ to make the gamma bilinears transform as ordinary contravariant [tensors](tensor.md).
