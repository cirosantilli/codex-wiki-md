# Monotonicity of a measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure-split.md)

For [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) $A\subseteq B$, the [disjoint union](disjoint-union.md) $B=A\sqcup(B\setminus A)$ and [finite additivity](finite-additivity-of-a-set-function.md) give $\mu(B)=\mu(A)+\mu(B\setminus A)\geq\mu(A)$. This uses nonnegativity and is valid with infinite values; it does not subtract infinities. [Monotonicity of a measure](monotonicity-of-a-measure.md) implies that increasing [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) have nondecreasing [measures](measure-split.md), but the equality of their [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) with the [measure](measure-split.md) of their [union](set-union.md) uses the stronger axiom of [countable additivity](countable-additivity.md).
