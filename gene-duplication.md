# Gene duplication

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

Creation of an additional inherited copy of a [gene](gene.md). Copies can be lost, retain shared functions, undergo [subfunctionalization](subfunctionalization.md), or undergo [neofunctionalization](neofunctionalization.md); duplication alone does not guarantee a useful novelty.
