# Lorentz-invariant specific intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic beaming](relativistic-beaming.md)

The [photon](photon.md) phase-space occupation is a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md). For [radiation](radiation.md) propagating through vacuum, this makes [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) divided by cubed [frequency](frequency.md) invariant under a change of inertial observer. With [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md) $D$, $I_\nu(\nu)=D^3I'_{\nu'}(\nu/D)$. This describes corresponding ray directions; it does not assert that an angularly integrated [radiation](radiation.md) field is isotropic in both frames.
