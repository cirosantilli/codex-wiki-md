# Integration by parts for Riemannian divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior derivative of contracted volume form](exterior-derivative-of-contracted-volume-form.md)

On a compact oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) without boundary, the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) applied to $f\iota_X\omega_g$ gives the displayed identity. The product expansion is $d(f\iota_X\omega_g)=[X(f)+f\operatorname{div}X]\omega_g$. With boundary, the omitted boundary integral is $\int_{\partial M}f\iota_X\omega_g$.
