# Underdispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md)

Underdispersion means less variation than prescribed by a reference statistical model, corresponding to a working [dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md) below one. It is not, by itself, evidence that the reference mean function is improved.
