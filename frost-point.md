# Frost point

↑ **Parent:** [Frost](frost.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) at which the given water-vapour partial pressure equals saturation vapour pressure over ice. It is defined relative to ice, whereas the usual [dew point](dew-point.md) uses equilibrium with liquid water.
