# Increasing union of deductively closed sets

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)

If

$$
T_0\subsetneq T_1\subsetneq T_2\subsetneq\cdots
$$

are deductively closed, then each $T_i$ is consistent and their union is consistent and deductively closed. The union is not finitary. Each conclusion follows from the [finite character of formal proofs](finite-character-of-formal-proofs.md).
