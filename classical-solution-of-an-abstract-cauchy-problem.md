# Classical solution of an abstract Cauchy problem

↑ **Parent:** [Abstract Cauchy problem](abstract-cauchy-problem.md)

For a [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md) $L$ on a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $H$, a positive-time classical solution belongs to $C([0,T],H)\cap C^1((0,T],H)$, takes values in $D(L)$ for positive time, and has $Lu\in C((0,T],H)$. It satisfies the [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) and the initial condition in $H$. Classical regularity also at time zero requires the corresponding regularity of the initial datum, in particular $u_0\in D(L)$. For an [analytic semigroup](analytic-semigroup.md) and [Hölder continuous](holder-condition.md) forcing, the [variation-of-constants formula](variation-of-constants-formula.md) gives such a solution: subtracting the forcing at the current time turns the singular estimate $\|LS(r)\|\leq C/r$ into the integrable bound $Cr^{\theta-1}$.
