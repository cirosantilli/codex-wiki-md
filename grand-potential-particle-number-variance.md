# Grand-potential particle-number variance

↑ **Parent:** [Grand potential](grand-potential.md)

Differentiating the grand [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [volume](volume.md) gives $\langle N\rangle=-\partial_\mu\Phi$ and $\operatorname{Var}N=-k_BT\partial_\mu^2\Phi=k_BT\partial_\mu\langle N\rangle$. It follows because derivatives of the logarithm of the state sum generate cumulants of [particle number](particle-number.md). Nonnegative [variance](variance-split.md) makes the [grand potential](grand-potential.md) concave in [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) in a stable grand-canonical description.
