# Ideal class group of Q of square root of minus thirty-three

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's bound](minkowski-s-bound.md)

The field has ring of integers $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-33}]$, discriminant $-132$, and Minkowski bound below eight. The nonprincipal ramified ideals above two and three generate four classes, and the split primes above seven introduce no new class. Hence

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-33}))
\cong C_2\times C_2.
$$
