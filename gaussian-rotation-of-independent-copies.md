# Gaussian rotation of independent copies

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md)

If $X,Y$ are [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with a centered [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) and [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\Sigma$, then $(U_\theta,V_\theta)$ has the same [probability law](probability-distribution.md) as $(X,Y)$. Its two marginal [covariance matrices](covariance-matrix.md) are $\Sigma$ and its cross-[covariance](covariance.md) is zero. The pair has a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), so the two components are [independent](independent-random-variables.md). This remains valid when $\Sigma$ is singular.
