# Fructose-6-phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Fructose-6-phosphate](fructose-6-phosphate.md) is a phosphorylated sugar involved in glycolysis and metabolic regulation. In liver glucose sensing it favours the inhibitory glucokinase–regulatory-protein interaction.
