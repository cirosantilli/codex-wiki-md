<h1 id="poincare-map">Poincaré map</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_map)

A local transverse section to a flow is mapped back to itself by the next intersection of an orbit. Fixed points represent [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md), and the derivative of this return map determines their transverse stability.
