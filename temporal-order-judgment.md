# Temporal order judgment

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

A judgment of which of two events occurred first or second. Changing the response rule, modality reliability or [attention](attention.md) can alter measured order without implying a corresponding change in physical stimulus timing.
