# Radial wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function-split.md)

[Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) in a [central potential](central-potential.md) writes a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) as $R_{nl}(r)Y_l^m(\theta,\phi)$. With normalized [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md) the radial factor satisfies $\int_0^\infty r^2|R_{nl}|^2dr=1$. The reduced radial function is $u=rR$, with [normalization](normalization.md) $\int|u|^2dr=1$; the two functions have different behavior at the origin. For a regular Coulomb [eigenstate](eigenstate.md), $R_{nl}\propto r^l$ near the origin.
