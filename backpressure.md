# Backpressure

↑ **Parent:** [Flow control](flow-control.md)

A dependency by which an unready receiver delays upstream acceptance of further work. In [synchronous message passing](synchronous-message-passing.md) it can propagate through a chain of blocked sends; a finite buffer postpones this propagation until its capacity is used.
