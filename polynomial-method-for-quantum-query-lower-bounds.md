# Polynomial method for quantum query lower bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum query complexity](quantum-query-complexity.md)

After $T$ input-bit queries, every [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $T$, because the bit-query matrix entries are affine in the input bits. An acceptance [probability](probability.md) is a sum of squared absolute amplitudes and has [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $2T$. [Multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube](multilinear-reduction-on-the-boolean-cube.md) does not increase degree. Exact acceptance therefore implies $Q_E(f)\ge\lceil\deg(f)/2\rceil$. For bounded error the acceptance polynomial only approximates the [Boolean function](boolean-function.md); its exact representing degree is not the relevant bound.
