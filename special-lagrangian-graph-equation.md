# Special Lagrangian graph equation

↑ **Parent:** [Special Lagrangian submanifold](special-lagrangian-submanifold.md)

For a graph $z=x+i\nabla f$ in $\mathbb C^3$, symmetry of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) makes the graph Lagrangian. Pulling back $\Omega=dz^1\wedge dz^2\wedge dz^3$ gives $\det(I+iD^2f)$. Its imaginary part is $\operatorname{tr}D^2f-\det D^2f$, while its real part is $1-\sigma_2(D^2f)$. Vanishing of the imaginary part and positivity of the real part give the displayed equation and oriented branch. The equation is nonlinear even though its linearization near a flat graph is the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md).
