# Complex covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance](covariance.md)

For square-integrable complex [random variables](random-variable-split.md), the Hermitian covariance is $\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathbb C}(Z,W)=\mathbb E[(Z-\mathbb EZ)\overline{(W-\mathbb EW)}]$. Its diagonal is nonnegative and equals $\mathbb E|Z-\mathbb EZ|^2$. The conjugation distinguishes it from the [pseudo-covariance](pseudo-covariance.md).
