# Quadratic uniformity of a set

↑ **Parent:** [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $A$ of a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md) $G$ with [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) $\eta=|A|/|G|$ is quadratically $\alpha$-uniform, in the [norm](norm.md) convention, if its [balanced subset indicator](balanced-indicator-function-of-a-finite-subset.md) $g=1_A-\eta$ has [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md) at most $\alpha$. This controls correlations on additive three-dimensional cubes after the [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) contribution is removed. If a convention instead bounds the eighth power of the [norm](norm.md) by $\alpha$, its parameter corresponds to the [norm](norm.md) parameter $\alpha^{1/8}$. The convention must therefore be stated when giving quantitative thresholds.
