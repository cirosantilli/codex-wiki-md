# Deficient number

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deficient_number)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) whose [proper-divisor sum](proper-divisor-sum.md) is smaller than itself; equivalently, its [sum-of-divisors function](sum-of-divisors-function.md) satisfies $\sigma(n)<2n$. For example, $s(8)=7<8$; also $s(1)=0<1$.
