# Reverse Fatou lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md)

If $Y_n$ are [random variables](random-variable-split.md) bounded above by an [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) $Z$, with all relevant expectations well defined, [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) applied to the nonnegative variables $Z-Y_n$ gives $\limsup_n\mathbb E Y_n\leq\mathbb E\limsup_nY_n$. In particular, bounded [indicator random variables](indicator-random-variable.md) that tend almost surely to zero have probabilities tending to zero.
