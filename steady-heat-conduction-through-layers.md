# Steady heat conduction through layers

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md)

In one-dimensional steady [heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md) without internal heat sources, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives constant [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) $q=-kT'$. Through a layer of thickness $L_j$ and positive [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) $k_j$, the temperature drop is $qL_j/k_j$. If adjoining layers are in perfect thermal contact, temperature and [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) are continuous at each interface, so summing the layer drops proves the displayed formula. Temperature is piecewise linear and its slope may jump when [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) changes.
