<h1 id="superspace-differential-realization-of-n-1-supercharges">Superspace differential realization of N=1 supercharges</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

With left [Grassmann derivatives](grassmann-derivative.md), define $\mathcal P_\mu=-i\partial_\mu$, $\mathcal Q_\alpha=-i\partial_{\theta^\alpha}-\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\gamma}\bar\theta^{\dot\gamma}\partial_\mu$ and $\bar{\mathcal Q}_{\dot\beta}=i\partial_{\bar\theta^{\dot\beta}}+\theta^\gamma\sigma^\mu_{\gamma\dot\beta}\partial_\mu$. The anticommutators of the derivative parts and of the coordinate parts vanish. Each mixed derivative/coordinate pair contributes $-i\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu$ by the graded product rule, giving the displayed mixed [anticommutator](anticommutator.md). Equal-chirality anticommutators vanish, and all these operators commute with translations. Under [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) their spinor indices transform in conjugate [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) representations, completing the minimal [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md). A convention for left derivatives is essential to the signs.
