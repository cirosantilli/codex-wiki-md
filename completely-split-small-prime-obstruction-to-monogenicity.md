# Completely split small-prime obstruction to monogenicity

↑ **Parent:** [Monogenic field](monogenic-field.md)

Let $K$ have degree $n$, and suppose a rational prime $p$ factors into $n$ distinct prime ideals of norm $p$. If $n>p$, then $K$ is not monogenic. Otherwise the [Dedekind factorization theorem](dedekind-factorization-theorem.md) would force the degree-$n$ minimal polynomial of a power-basis generator to have $n$ distinct linear factors over $\mathbb F_p$, although only the $p$ polynomials $X-a$ are available.
