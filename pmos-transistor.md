# PMOS transistor

↑ **Parent:** [MOSFET](mosfet.md)

In the ideal digital switch model, an enhancement-mode [PMOS transistor](pmos-transistor.md) controlled by a low gate signal conducts, and a high gate signal disconnects its channel. The input's conducting literal is therefore negated relative to an [NMOS transistor](nmos-transistor.md). Complementary [PMOS transistors](pmos-transistor.md) form the high-supply network of static [CMOS](cmos.md) gates.
