# Larmor formula

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larmor_formula)

The nonrelativistic radiated power of an accelerated point charge is $P=q^2\mu_0a^2/(6\pi c)$ in [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units. Integrating its dipole radiation pattern over solid angle gives this result; relativistic motion requires the Lienard generalization.
