# Temperature sensitivity of a catalytic ozone balance

↑ **Parent:** [Chapman cycle](chapman-cycle.md)

Suppose a reduced steady balance is $P=AX^2+BX$, where $X=[\mathrm O_3]$, and hold $P$ and all specified background [concentrations](concentration.md) fixed. With $u=1/T$, $d\ln A/du=-a$ and $d\ln B/du=-b$, implicit differentiation gives $d\ln X/du=(aAX^2+bBX)/(2AX^2+BX)$. The quadratic loss term includes temperature-dependent O/O3 partitioning as well as the direct reaction coefficient. Holding the loss ratio fixed over a finite change is an additional approximation; solving the new quadratic provides a check.
