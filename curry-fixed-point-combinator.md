# Curry fixed-point combinator

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point combinator](fixed-point-combinator.md)

With $A_f=\lambda x.f(xx)$, the [combinator](combinator.md) $Y=\lambda f.A_fA_f$ satisfies $Yf\equiv_\beta f(Yf)$. Every finite reduct of $Y$ is, up to [alpha equivalence](alpha-equivalence.md), $\lambda f.f^n(A_fA_f)$: the remaining copy of $A_fA_f$ is its only [beta-redex](beta-redex.md).
