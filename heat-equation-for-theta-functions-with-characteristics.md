# Heat equation for theta functions with characteristics

↑ **Parent:** [Theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md)

Normal convergence allows termwise differentiation of the defining [theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md). A tau derivative multiplies a summand by $\pi i(n+\alpha)^2$, while its second spatial derivative multiplies it by $-4\pi^2(n+\alpha)^2$. This proves the displayed [heat equation](heat-equation.md), linking its lattice-sum construction with diffusion and analytic continuation.
