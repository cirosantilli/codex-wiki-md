# Exponential closed-box gas consumption

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

With gross [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md) $\psi=kM_g$, constant returned mass fraction $F$, and the [instantaneous recycling approximation](instantaneous-recycling-approximation.md), the net locked mass rate is $(1-F)\psi$. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot M_g=-(1-F)kM_g$. For constant [stellar yield](stellar-yield.md) $Y$ per unit locked mass and initially pristine gas, metal conservation gives $M_g\dot Z=Y(1-F)\psi$, so [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) grows as $Z=Y(1-F)kt$. Gross star formation and permanent locking have different normalizations.
