Here $\psi=\Gamma'/\Gamma$ is the [digamma function](digamma-function.md), $B=\log2+\frac12\log\pi-1-\gamma/2$, and $\gamma$ is the [Euler--Mascheroni constant](euler-s-constant.md). The sum runs over [Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) with multiplicities. Each displayed paired summand is $O_s(|\rho|^{-2})$, so it converges locally uniformly away from zeros. Differentiate the genus-one [Hadamard factorization](hadamard-factorization-theorem.md) of the [Riemann xi function](riemann-xi-function.md) and use the [Gamma function recurrence](gamma-function-recurrence.md). Unlike an unpaired reciprocal-zero sum, this expression does not require an unstated summation convention.
