# Earthquake pressure and tension axes

↑ **Parent:** [Focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md)

The negative and positive principal directions of a double-couple moment tensor. They bisect the nodal-plane quadrants, but are not automatically the principal axes of regional [stress](stress.md). A [stress](stress.md) inversion needs additional faulting assumptions and multiple mechanisms.
