# Reactor thermal hydraulics

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear engineering](nuclear-engineering.md)

The coupled analysis of heat generation, [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) and coolant [fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md) in a [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md). A change in fuel geometry can affect [neutron moderation](neutron-moderation.md), hydraulic resistance and thermal margins together, so holding one quantity fixed does not generally hold the others fixed.
