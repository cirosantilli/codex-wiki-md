# Mixing angle

↑ **Parent:** [Flavour mixing](flavour-mixing.md)

An angle parametrizing a rotation between interaction and propagation bases. Examples include the [Cabibbo angle](cabibbo-angle.md) and the three angles in the [lepton mixing matrix](lepton-mixing-matrix.md). The value must be associated with a specified matrix parametrization and basis ordering.
