# Complex tautological line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md)

Over complex [projective space](projective-space-split.md), its fibre at a line $\ell\subseteq\mathbb C^{n+1}$ is $\ell$ itself. It is a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) contained in the trivial bundle. Its dual is the [hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md).
