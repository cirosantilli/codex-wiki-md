# Solar cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md)

The solar cycle is the recurrent variation of magnetic activity, with a characteristic sunspot-number period of about eleven years. The large-scale magnetic polarity reverses from one activity cycle to the next, giving an approximately twenty-two-year magnetic cycle. Activity cycles vary in length and amplitude. A satisfactory [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md) model must account for polarity reversal, equatorward activity-belt migration in the [solar butterfly diagram](solar-butterfly-diagram.md), the phase relation between polar and active-region fields, and long-term modulation including [solar grand minima](solar-grand-minimum.md).
