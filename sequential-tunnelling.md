# Sequential tunnelling

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)

Transport by successive real charge-state changes across weak tunnel barriers. Charge [electrochemical potentials](electrochemical-potential.md) must enter the source-drain bias window; outside it sequential transport is Coulomb blocked. Higher-order virtual [cotunnelling](cotunnelling.md) is a distinct process.
