# Heat parametrix

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md)

An approximate [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md) with the correct delta initial limit and a residual extending with enough regularity to time zero. A cutoff Gaussian times transport coefficients supplies a local construction. Increasing the expansion order, or summing the full asymptotic series smoothly, makes the residual regular enough for a [Volterra parametrix correction](volterra-parametrix-correction.md).
