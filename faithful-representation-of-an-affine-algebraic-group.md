# Faithful representation of an affine algebraic group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)

Every affine algebraic group has a faithful finite-dimensional rational [group representation](group-representation.md). Choose algebra generators of $k[G]$ and place them in a finite-dimensional subspace stable under right translations. An element acting trivially on that subspace has the same coordinate values as the identity and therefore is the identity.
