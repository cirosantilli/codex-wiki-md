# Group coboundary

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

A group coboundary is a cochain in the image of the preceding coboundary map. Quotienting [group cocycles](group-cocycle.md) by group coboundaries gives [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md).
