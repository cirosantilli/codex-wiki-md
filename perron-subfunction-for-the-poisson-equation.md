# Perron subfunction for the Poisson equation

↑ **Parent:** [Perron method](perron-method.md)

A continuous function $v$ is a Perron subfunction for $\Delta u=f$ when it lies below every [classical solution](classical-solution.md) of that [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) on a relatively compact [open ball](open-ball.md) whenever it lies below that solution on the ball's boundary. For $C^2$ functions, this is equivalent to $\Delta v\geq f$. A superfunction reverses the comparison and has $\Delta v\leq f$ when $C^2$. Adding a boundary upper bound gives the family whose supremum defines the [Perron method for the Dirichlet problem](perron-method.md).
