# Link of a simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

For a [simplex](simplex.md) $\sigma$ in a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) $K$, its link consists of the [simplexes](simplex.md) disjoint from $\sigma$ whose union with $\sigma$ is a [simplex](simplex.md) of $K$:

$$
\operatorname{lk}_K(\sigma)=\{\rho\in K:\rho\cap\sigma=\varnothing,\ \rho\cup\sigma\in K\}.
$$

This is a [simplicial subcomplex](simplicial-subcomplex.md), because taking a nonempty face preserves both conditions. At a vertex of a [closed combinatorial surface](closed-combinatorial-surface.md) its link is a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md); at a boundary vertex in a triangulated [surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md) it is a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md). Geometrically, the incident triangles form a cyclic fan or a fan with two ends. Deleting one incident triangular face from a cyclic fan changes the vertex link into a path.
