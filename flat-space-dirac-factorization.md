# Flat-space Dirac factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

The [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) and commuting [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) give $(\not\partial)^2=\Box$. Thus every free [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) solution also solves the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) componentwise. The converse is false without the first-order [spinor](spinor.md) constraint: the second-order equation alone allows twice as much independent initial data.
