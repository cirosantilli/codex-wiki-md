# Random cut lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Cut (graph theory)](cut-graph-theory.md)

Every finite graph with $m$ edges has a [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md) of size at least $m/2$, and hence a spanning [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) with at least $m/2$ edges. Put each vertex independently on either side with equal probability. Every edge crosses with probability $1/2$, so [linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md) gives expected cut size $m/2$.
