# Radial vector field in a normal neighbourhood

↑ **Parent:** [Radial distance in a normal neighbourhood](radial-distance-in-a-normal-neighbourhood.md)

The unit radial [vector field](vector-field.md) is $E=\nabla r$ on the punctured [normal neighbourhood](normal-neighbourhood.md), where $r$ is the [radial distance in a normal neighbourhood](radial-distance-in-a-normal-neighbourhood.md). Along $\gamma_u(t)=\exp_p(tu)$ with $|u|=1$, it satisfies $E(\gamma_u(t))=\dot\gamma_u(t)$. The scaled radial field $W=rE=\nabla(r^2/2)$ is smooth at the centre and vanishes there. These conventions have different associated radial functions.
