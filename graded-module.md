# Graded module

↑ **Parent:** [Graded ring](graded-ring.md)

A graded module over a [graded ring](graded-ring.md) $R=\bigoplus_iR_i$ is an $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) with a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) decomposition $M=\bigoplus_dM_d$ satisfying $R_iM_d\subseteq M_{i+d}$. A graded homomorphism preserves these degrees. Over a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) with positive variable degrees, a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) of this kind is bounded below and has finite-dimensional graded components over the coefficient [field](field.md).
