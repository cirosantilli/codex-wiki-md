# Kinetic-energy decomposition about the centre of mass

↑ **Parent:** [Center-of-mass reduction](center-of-mass-reduction.md)

For fixed particle [masses](mass.md) and positive total [mass](mass.md), write $\mathbf x_i=\mathbf R+\mathbf y_i$ with $\mathbf R$ the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md). The weighted relative [velocities](velocity.md) sum to zero, so expanding the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) eliminates the mixed term and yields the displayed decomposition. For a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md), the relative contribution is the [rotational kinetic energy](rotational-kinetic-energy.md) $I\omega^2/2$ about the chosen centre-of-mass axis.
