# Normal modes of two harmonically coupled identical particles

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For equal masses in identical quadratic traps with an interaction depending quadratically on $x_1-x_2$, the displayed [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) coordinates of the [normal modes](normal-mode.md) separate the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) into a collective and a relative oscillator. [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) preserves both the kinetic Laplacian and the absolute coordinate Jacobian. Particle exchange fixes $y_+$ and negates $y_-$. Hence [bosonic exchange symmetry](bosonic-exchange-symmetry.md) selects even relative oscillator levels, and [fermionic exchange symmetry](fermi-dirac-statistics.md) selects odd ones. For a trap [frequency](frequency.md) $\omega$ and interaction $-m\omega^2\sin^2\alpha\,(x_1-x_2)^2/4$, the two frequencies are $\omega$ and $\omega\cos\alpha$; the relative oscillator length diverges when the latter approaches zero.
