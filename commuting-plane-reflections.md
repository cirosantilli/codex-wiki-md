# Commuting plane reflections

↑ **Parent:** [Householder transformation](householder-transformation.md)

For real [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) $a,b$, the [Householder reflections](householder-transformation.md) $H_a=I-2aa^T$ and $H_b=I-2bb^T$ commute exactly when their normals are parallel or [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). Expanding their products proves the formula. If $a\cdot b\ne0$, a zero commutator requires $ba^T=ab^T$; applying this identity to $a$ gives $b=(a\cdot b)a$, hence parallelism. Orthogonal normals make the prefactor zero.

Equality on one selected [vector](vector.md) is weaker than equality of the operators. For nonparallel, nonorthogonal normals in three dimensions, the commutator's [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is the line perpendicular to both normals, spanned by $a\times b$. A vector on that line is fixed by both reflections even though the operators do not commute.
