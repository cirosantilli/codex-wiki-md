# Coadjoint orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Coadjoint representation](coadjoint-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coadjoint_orbit)

A [coadjoint orbit](coadjoint-orbit.md) is the orbit of $\ell\in\mathfrak g^*$ under $\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\ell$, defined by $\langle\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\ell,\xi\rangle=\langle\ell,\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\xi\rangle$. For a connected Lie group these orbits are the [symplectic leaves](symplectic-leaf.md) of its Lie-Poisson manifold. Their tangent vectors are infinitesimal coadjoint actions, and their natural two-form is the [Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau symplectic form](kirillov-kostant-souriau-symplectic-form.md).
