# Derivation (differential algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivation_(differential_algebra))

An algebraic derivation of an [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) is a [linear map](linear-map.md) satisfying the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) for its multiplication: $D(xy)=D(x)y+xD(y)$. This definition makes sense even when the multiplication is not associative. A [derivation of an algebra](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) uses an associative product; a [derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) uses the [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) as its product.
