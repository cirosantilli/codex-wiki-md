# Nova-like variable

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md)

A [cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md) whose persistent luminous disk resembles a [dwarf nova](dwarf-nova.md) in outburst. A sufficiently high transfer rate can keep its [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) on the hot stable branch of the [hydrogen-ionization disk instability](hydrogen-ionization-disk-instability.md), avoiding ordinary dwarf-nova cycles.
