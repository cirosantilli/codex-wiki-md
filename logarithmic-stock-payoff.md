# Logarithmic stock payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md)

Under the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) with interest rate $\rho$, volatility $\sigma$ and remaining time $\Delta$, [risk-neutral pricing](risk-neutral-pricing.md) gives $V(S)=S[\log S+(\rho+\sigma^2/2)\Delta]$. This follows by differentiating the lognormal exponential moment. Its [option delta](option-delta.md) is $\log S+1+(\rho+\sigma^2/2)\Delta$ and its second stock derivative is $1/S$.
