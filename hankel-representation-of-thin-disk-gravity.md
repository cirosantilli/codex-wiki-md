# Hankel representation of thin-disk gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

For an axisymmetric reflection-symmetric [thin disk](thin-disk.md), the [Laplace equation in cylindrical coordinates](laplace-equation-in-cylindrical-coordinates.md) separates into regular radial [Bessel functions](bessel-function.md) and vertical decaying exponentials. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) imposes $\Phi_z^+-\Phi_z^-=4\pi G\Sigma$. [Hankel transform](hankel-transform.md) inversion gives $S(k)=-2\pi G\int_0^\infty\Sigma(R)J_0(kR)R\,dR$. Its radial derivative gives the [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md). Infinite scale-free models may require subtracting an additive divergent constant.
