# p-radical subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [P-subgroup](p-subgroup.md)

A [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) is p-radical if it equals the [p-core](p-core.md) of its [normalizer](normalizer.md). Every [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) is p-radical by [defect groups are centralizer-conjugate Sylow intersections](defect-groups-are-centralizer-conjugate-sylow-intersections.md). Being an intersection of arbitrary Sylow subgroups alone is weaker than the [centralizer](centralizer.md)-conjugate statement used in that argument.
