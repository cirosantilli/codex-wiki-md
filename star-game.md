# Star game

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal combinatorial game](infinitesimal-combinatorial-game.md)

The [impartial game](impartial-game.md) $*=\{0\mid0\}$, so either player can move to zero. It is equivalent to a [Nim heap](nim-heap.md) of size one. It is incomparable with zero, and $*+*=0$ under the [disjunctive sum of games](disjunctive-sum-of-games.md).
