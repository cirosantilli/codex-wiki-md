# Homogeneous ideal Bose gas condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

For a uniform three-dimensional ideal gas of spinless [bosons](boson.md) with energy $p^2/(2m)$ and ground energy set to zero, the excited number density is $n_{\rm ex}=\lambda_T^{-3}\operatorname{Li}_{3/2}(z)$, with [quantum gas fugacity](fugacity-statistical-mechanics.md) $z=e^{\mu/(k_BT)}\leq1$. Its finite maximum $\zeta(3/2)/\lambda_T^3$ gives the displayed [critical temperature](critical-temperature.md). Below it, particle conservation gives [condensate fraction](condensate-fraction.md) $N_0/N=1-(T/T_c)^{3/2}$. The excited-state [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is $U=(3/2)Vk_BT\lambda_T^{-3}\operatorname{Li}_{5/2}(z)$, with $z\to1$ below the transition. The exponent differs from that of a harmonic trap. Condensation is not by itself a demonstration of [superfluidity](superfluidity.md); the ideal quadratic dispersion has vanishing Landau critical speed.
