# Localization of relative almost periodicity to base sets

↑ **Parent:** [Relatively almost periodic observable](relatively-almost-periodic-observable.md)

Multiplying a [relatively almost periodic observable](relatively-almost-periodic-observable.md) by a base-set indicator preserves [relative almost periodicity](relatively-almost-periodic-observable.md): on each fiber an orbit value is either unchanged or zero, so add zero to the finite list of centers. [Conditional norm covariance under a factor map](conditional-norm-covariance-under-a-factor-map.md) also proves closure under convergence in the [uniform conditional L2 norm](uniform-conditional-l2-norm.md).
