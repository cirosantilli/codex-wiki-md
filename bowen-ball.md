# Bowen ball

↑ **Parent:** [Bowen metric](bowen-metric.md)

A radius-$\epsilon$ ball for a [Bowen metric](bowen-metric.md) consists of the points whose first $n$ iterates stay within $\epsilon$ of the corresponding iterates of the centre. Balls at a fixed small radius distinguish forward [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) segments at that observational resolution.
