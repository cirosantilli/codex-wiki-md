# CM period

↑ **Parent:** [Complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md)

A complex CM period compares an algebraic invariant differential on a [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) with its analytic lattice coordinate. At an ordinary prime, a $p$-adic CM period compares the same differential with the invariant differential of the multiplicative formal group: an isomorphism $\eta$ is normalized by $\log_{\widehat E}\eta(T)=\Omega_p\log(1+T)$. Matched periods make normalized critical [Hecke L-function](hecke-l-function.md) values algebraic and give precise [p-adic L-function](p-adic-l-function.md) interpolation.
