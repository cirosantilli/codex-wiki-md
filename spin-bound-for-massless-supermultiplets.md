# Spin bound for massless supermultiplets

↑ **Parent:** [Helicity spectrum of a massless supermultiplet](helicity-spectrum-of-a-massless-supermultiplet.md)

In four-dimensional [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), a finite-[helicity](helicity.md) [massless supermultiplet](massless-supermultiplet.md) has [helicity](helicity.md) interval of width $\mathcal N/2$. If every state obeys $|h|\le s$, that interval fits inside one of width $2s$, giving $\mathcal N\le4s$. The bounds $4$ for $s=1$ and $8$ for $s=2$ are attained by the [four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory](four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory.md) [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) and the [four-dimensional N=8 supergravity](four-dimensional-n-8-supergravity.md) [supergravity multiplet](supergravity-multiplet.md). Spin bounds alone do not prove that an arbitrary action is renormalizable.
