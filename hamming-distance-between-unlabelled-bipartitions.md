# Hamming distance between unlabelled bipartitions

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md)

This [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md) on partitions identifies a group with its complement because exchanging the two group names does not change the partition. Complementation is an [isometry](isometry.md) of the ordinary [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md), so minimizing over this two-element group action gives a quotient metric and preserves the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Negating a zero-one indicator is not complementation; with $\pm1$ membership vectors, it is.
