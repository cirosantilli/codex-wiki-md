# Cohomology class of a cooriented submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Thom class](thom-class.md)

An oriented normal bundle of a compact embedded [submanifold](submanifold.md) has a [Thom class](thom-class.md). A [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) and excision identify it with a relative class in $H^d(X,X\setminus Y)$. Its image in absolute [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is $\varepsilon_Y$. It restricts to zero off Y and, in an oriented closed ambient [manifold](topological-manifold.md), represents the [Poincare dual](poincare-dual.md) of Y with the normal-first induced orientation.
