# Dirichlet oscillator determinant ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian path integral](gaussian-path-integral.md)

On $[0,T]$ with vanishing endpoint fluctuations, the sine-mode [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $(\pi n/T)^2-\omega^2$. Divide by the free [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and use the [sine infinite product](sine-infinite-product.md). The ratio determines the [harmonic oscillator transition kernel](harmonic-oscillator-transition-kernel.md) once the free [configuration-space path integral](configuration-space-path-integral.md) is normalized. Zeros correspond to caustics where the fluctuation operator has a zero mode.
