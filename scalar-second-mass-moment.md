# Scalar second mass moment

↑ **Parent:** [Second mass moment tensor](second-mass-moment-tensor.md)

The scalar second mass moment is the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [second mass moment tensor](second-mass-moment-tensor.md). The [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about a specified axis instead integrates squared distance from that axis. For an isotropic distribution each axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $2I/3$; the sum over three orthogonal axes is $2I$.
