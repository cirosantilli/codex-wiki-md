# Accelerator mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerator_mass_spectrometry)

[Accelerator mass spectrometry](accelerator-mass-spectrometry.md) uses ion acceleration and particle identification to count rare isotopes relative to abundant ones. For [radiocarbon dating](radiocarbon-dating.md), it measures [carbon-14](carbon-14.md) directly rather than waiting to count its [beta decays](beta-decay.md).
