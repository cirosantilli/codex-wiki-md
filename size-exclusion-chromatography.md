# Size-exclusion chromatography

↑ **Parent:** [Liquid chromatography](liquid-chromatography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Size-exclusion_chromatography)

[Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) separates dissolved [molecules](molecule-split.md) by their [hydrodynamic size](hydrodynamic-size.md) in the chosen [solvent](solvent.md). A larger species that cannot enter the packing's pores travels with the liquid between particles and elutes earlier. Smaller species explore more internal liquid and elute later. The column is not a sieve that permanently traps the large species: exclusion makes them leave first. “[Gel filtration](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)” is common for aqueous separations; “[gel permeation chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)” is common for organic-solvent analysis of [polymers](polymer-split.md). [SEC molar mass calibration](sec-molar-mass-calibration.md) translates size-dependent elution into a standard-relative estimate of [molar mass](molar-mass.md), rather than a universal absolute measurement.

This makes [SEC](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) useful for separating [proteins](protein-split.md) from small dissolved species, comparing [protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md), and characterizing dissolved [polymer](polymer-split.md) chains. The [residual-group monitoring of polyurethane formation](residual-group-monitoring-of-polyurethane-formation.md) experiment already illustrates [polymer](polymer-split.md) calibration: its reported [molar masses](molar-mass.md) are relative to [polystyrene](polystyrene.md) standards. [Size-exclusion characterization of chain polymers](size-exclusion-characterization-of-chain-polymers.md) explains why an elution trace alone cannot count chains or establish their growth mechanism.
