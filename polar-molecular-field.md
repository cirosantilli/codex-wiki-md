# Polar molecular field

↑ **Parent:** [Polar order parameter](polar-order-parameter.md)

The polar molecular field is the [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) of the [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) with respect to the [polar order parameter](polar-order-parameter.md). In the positive-derivative convention, dissipative dynamics is $D_t\mathbf p=-\Gamma\mathbf h$ with positive [kinetic coefficient](kinetic-coefficient.md). For a quartic local term and an isotropic gradient penalty,

$$
h_i=(a+b|\mathbf p|^2)p_i-\kappa\nabla^2p_i.
$$

Some authors instead define the molecular field with a minus sign, which changes the associated stress and evolution conventions.
