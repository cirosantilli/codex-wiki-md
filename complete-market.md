# Complete market

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental theorem of asset pricing](fundamental-theorem-of-asset-pricing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_market)

A complete market is a financial market in which every contingent claim in the chosen class can be replicated by an admissible [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md). In an arbitrage-free finite market, completeness is equivalent to uniqueness of the [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md).
