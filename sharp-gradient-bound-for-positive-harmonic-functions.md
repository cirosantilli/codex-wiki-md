# Sharp gradient bound for positive harmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Harnack inequality on the unit disk](harnack-inequality-on-the-unit-disk.md)

For a positive [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), choose a global [harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md) and apply the [Carathéodory derivative bound for the right half-plane](caratheodory-derivative-bound-for-the-right-half-plane.md) to $u+iv$. This gives $|\nabla u(0)|\le2u(0)$ by the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md). A [disk automorphism](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) sending zero to $z$ has derivative modulus $1-|z|^2$, so the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the displayed bound. Composing a boundary-pole [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) with a [disk automorphism](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) attains equality, proving the coefficient is optimal at every point.
