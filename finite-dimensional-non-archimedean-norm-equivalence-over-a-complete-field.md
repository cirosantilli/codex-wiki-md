# Finite-dimensional non-Archimedean norm equivalence over a complete field

↑ **Parent:** [Unique extension of an absolute value to a finite extension](unique-extension-of-an-absolute-value-to-a-finite-extension.md)

Let $K$ be complete for a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md) and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with a norm satisfying $\|av\|=|a|\|v\|$ and the [ultrametric inequality](ultrametric-inequality.md). All such norms are equivalent to the maximum of coordinates in any [basis](basis.md). The upper bound is immediate. For the lower bound, induct on dimension. The span $W$ of all but the last [basis](basis.md) vector is complete by induction, hence closed for the given norm. Thus $\delta=\inf_{w\in W}\|e_n-w\|>0$. For $v=w+ae_n$, $\|v\|\geq |a|\delta$; also $\|w\|\leq\max(\|v\|,|a|\|e_n\|)$. The induction bound on coordinates of $w$ now bounds all coordinates by a constant times $\|v\|$. This proof needs no [local compactness](locally-compact-space.md).
