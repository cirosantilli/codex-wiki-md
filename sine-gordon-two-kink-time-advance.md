# Sine-Gordon two-kink time advance

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon two-kink solution](sine-gordon-two-kink-solution.md)

For two equal-charge [Sine-Gordon kinks](sine-gordon-kink.md) with speeds $\pm v$, $0<v<1$, the positive spatial transition satisfies $X_+(T)=v|T|-\log v/(m\gamma)+o(1)$, where $\gamma=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}$. Labeling a [soliton](soliton.md) by its preserved [rapidity](rapidity.md), the right-moving incoming and outgoing intercepts differ by $-2\log v/(m\gamma)$. The [soliton time delay](soliton-time-delay.md) at a fixed distant location is minus this shift divided by $v$, giving the displayed negative value. Reversing the entire field gives two [antikinks](antikink.md) with the same shifts. The sign means an advance relative to extrapolation of the incoming line; labels tied to left and right positions instead exchange [velocities](velocity.md) during reflection.
