# Density at the maximum speed of an isothermal breeze

↑ **Parent:** [Subsonic isothermal breeze](subsonic-isothermal-breeze.md)

A steady spherical [subsonic isothermal breeze](subsonic-isothermal-breeze.md) with [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $a$ obeys $(u-a^2/u)u'=2a^2/r-GM/r^2$. Since $0<u<a$, its finite-radius speed maximum lies at $r_M=GM/(2a^2)$. Spherical [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) then gives the displayed density, where $\mathcal M_M=u(r_M)/a\in(0,1)$ is the peak [Mach number](mach-number.md). This free parameter is selected by a boundary condition, not by $T$, $M$ and $\dot M$ alone. Therefore $\rho_M>\dot M a^3/(\pi G^2M^2)$, while a peak [Mach number](mach-number.md) of order unity gives that expression as a characteristic scale.

A direct demonstration of the free parameter comes from the first integral $u^2/2-a^2\ln(u/a)=2a^2\ln(r/r_M)+GM/r+C$. Its additive constant remains free for a breeze; imposing the regular [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) would be a different condition.
