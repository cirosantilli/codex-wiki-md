# Compact Nakayama lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Nakayama lemma](nakayama-lemma.md)

For a compact continuous [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension](iwasawa-algebra-of-a-zp-extension.md), lifts of a basis of $M/(p,T)M$ generate $M$. Their generated image is compact. The quotient $Q$ satisfies $Q=(p,T)Q$; in every finite continuous quotient, $(p,T)$ acts nilpotently, since the group action is pro-$p$. Thus all finite quotients of $Q$ vanish, and compactness gives $Q=0$.
