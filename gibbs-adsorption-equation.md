# Gibbs adsorption equation

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption isotherm](adsorption-isotherm.md)

For the simple one-solute interface convention, [surface excess](surface-excess.md) per molecule is related to the derivative of [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) $\gamma$ with respect to the logarithm of solute activity $a$ by the displayed equation. For ideal dilute behavior one may use concentration ratios in place of activity ratios. In molar units, $\Gamma_{\rm mol}=-(RT)^{-1}d\gamma/d\ln a$. A local molecular area estimate is $\Sigma=-k_BT/(d\gamma/d\ln a)$. Ionic surfactants can require counterion and activity factors; the simple single-component expression must not be transferred to them without specifying those constraints.
