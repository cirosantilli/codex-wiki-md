# Horn theory

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian theory](cartesian-theory.md)

In the positive finite-limit convention, a [Horn theory](horn-theory.md) uses atomic finite-conjunction sequents; uniquely witnessed existential extensions remain Cartesian. Its models are the finite-limit-preserving [functors](functor.md) out of its [Cartesian syntactic category](cartesian-syntactic-category.md). This convention does not insert arbitrary existential witnesses or falsity as Cartesian constructors. Its classifier is the [presheaf classifier of a Horn theory](presheaf-classifier-of-a-horn-theory.md).
