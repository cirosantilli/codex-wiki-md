# Approximate group

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximate_group)

A $K$-approximate group is a finite [symmetric subset of a group](symmetric-subset-of-a-group.md) $A$ for which some set $X$ with $|X|\leq K$ satisfies $A^2\subseteq XA$. In additive notation the covering condition is $A+A\subseteq X+A$.
