# Fourier transform of a finite measure

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

The angular-frequency [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of a [finite measure](finite-measure.md), or a complex measure of finite total variation, is defined by the displayed integral. It is bounded by total variation and continuous by [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md). An integrable density recovers the ordinary [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of a function. A measure supported on a curved surface instead leads to a [Fourier extension operator](fourier-extension-operator.md).
