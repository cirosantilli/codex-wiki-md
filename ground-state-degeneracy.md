# Ground-state degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Ground state](ground-state.md)

The [ground-state degeneracy](ground-state-degeneracy.md) counts independent states minimizing the energy. For a classical continuous-symmetry system it can instead be described by a manifold of minimizing configurations, such as the sphere of orientations of a [Néel state](neel-state.md) in an isotropic [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md).
