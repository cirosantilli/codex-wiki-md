# Multi-anvil press

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)

A high-pressure apparatus in which several anvils compress a relatively large specimen. Compared with a [diamond anvil cell](diamond-anvil-cell.md), it typically offers greater sample volume for synthesis and bulk measurements, with less optical access and a lower ultimate pressure range.
