# The Mass of the Black Hole in Cygnus X-1 by Jerome A. Orosz and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Jerome A. Orosz](work-by-jerome-a-orosz.md)

The original study combines optical light curves, companion-star radial velocities, rotational broadening and a distance constraint to infer the compact object's mass. The result $14.8\pm1.0$ solar [masses](mass.md) is the estimate from this 2011 analysis, rather than a claim about the latest mass determination.

[https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3689](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3689)

[https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/742/2/84](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/742/2/84)
