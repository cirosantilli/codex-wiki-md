# Bounded diffusion coefficient gives square martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

For a zero-starting solution with $|\sigma|\le C$, the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) gives $\mathbb EX_t^2\le C^2t$, so $X$ is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md) on every finite horizon. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) represents the displayed process as $2\int_0^tX_s\sigma(X_s)dB_s$, whose expected squared integrand integral is at most $2C^4t^2$. Thus it too is a true square-integrable [martingale](martingale-split.md). No [continuity](continuous-function.md) or Lipschitz condition on the coefficient is needed once a solution is given.
