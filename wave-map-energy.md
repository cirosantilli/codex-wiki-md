# Wave map energy

↑ **Parent:** [Wave map](wave-map.md)

The conserved [energy](energy.md) of a [wave map](wave-map.md) from Minkowski spacetime to a Riemannian target is $E=\frac12\int(|\phi_t|^2+|\nabla\phi|^2)\,dx$, with norms measured in the target metric. Contraction of the [wave map](wave-map.md) equation with the velocity and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives conservation.
