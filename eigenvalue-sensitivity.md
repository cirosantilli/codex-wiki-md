# Eigenvalue sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Simple eigenvalue](simple-eigenvalue.md)

For unit [left eigenvector](left-eigenvector.md) $u$ and [right eigenvector](right-eigenvector.md) $v$ of a real matrix at a [simple eigenvalue](simple-eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$, its normwise sensitivity is

$$
s(\lambda)=\frac1{|u^Tv|}.
$$

It measures the first-order amplification of a matrix perturbation into an eigenvalue perturbation.
