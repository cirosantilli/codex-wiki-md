# Filament tension

↑ **Parent:** [Inextensible filament](inextensible-filament.md)

Filament tension is the axial force enforcing local [inextensibility](inextensible-filament.md). Its force density is $\partial_s(T\mathbf r_s)$. A pre-existing constant tension contributes $Ty_{xx}$ to small-slope transverse dynamics; in an unloaded, initially straight filament, tension induced by transverse motion begins at second order in amplitude.
