# Prime factor

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number-split.md)

A [prime factor](prime-factor.md) of a nonzero [integer](integer-split.md) $n$ is a [prime number](prime-number-split.md) $p$ satisfying $p\mid n$. For $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$, the distinct [prime factors](prime-factor.md) are $2,3,5$, while counting repeated factors gives six prime entries. A [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) retains their multiplicities: knowing only the distinct [prime factors](prime-factor.md) does not distinguish $12=2^2\cdot3$ from $18=2\cdot3^2$. A negative [integer](integer-split.md) has the same [prime factors](prime-factor.md) as its [absolute value](absolute-value.md).
