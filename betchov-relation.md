# Betchov relation

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md)

For differentiable homogeneous [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), $\langle\operatorname{tr}A^3\rangle=0$. One divergence representation is $\operatorname{tr}A^3=\partial_i[u_j(\partial_j u_k)(\partial_k u_i)-u_i\operatorname{tr}A^2/2]$. Splitting the [velocity gradient tensor](velocity-gradient-tensor.md) into strain and rotation gives $\langle\omega_i\omega_jS_{ij}\rangle=-4\langle abc\rangle$. It connects mean [vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) to the cubic strain statistic, without requiring isotropy.
