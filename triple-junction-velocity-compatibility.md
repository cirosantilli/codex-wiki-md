# Triple-junction velocity compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Triple junction](triple-junction.md)

For a boundary with in-plane normal $n$, an admissible junction [velocity](velocity.md) $U$ obeys $(U-v_{\rm boundary})\cdot n=0$. A ridge uses the mean of adjacent plate [velocities](velocity.md) in the symmetric model, a trench uses the [overriding plate](overriding-plate.md) [velocity](velocity.md), and a transform uses either adjoining plate [velocity](velocity.md). Compatibility requires a common intersection of the three velocity-space lines.
