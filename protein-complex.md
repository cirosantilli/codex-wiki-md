# Protein complex

↑ **Parent:** [Protein quaternary structure](protein-quaternary-structure.md)

A [protein complex](protein-complex.md) is an assembly of associated [proteins](protein-split.md). Its composition specifies component identities and their [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md); variable accessory occupancy and transient partners can make the assembly a population of related forms rather than one fixed object.
