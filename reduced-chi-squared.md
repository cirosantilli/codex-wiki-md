# Reduced chi-squared

↑ **Parent:** [Goodness of fit](goodness-of-fit.md)

The [reduced chi-squared](reduced-chi-squared.md) divides a weighted squared-residual statistic by its [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md). For an identifiable Gaussian linear model with $N$ independent observations, known correct errors and $p$ fitted coefficients, $\nu=N-p$ and its expectation is one. An observed value close to one is compatible with the model but does not prove it. Unknown [systematic errors](systematic-error.md), dependent residuals or fitted [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) scales change this interpretation.
