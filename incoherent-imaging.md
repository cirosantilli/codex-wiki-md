# Incoherent imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md)

In [incoherent imaging](incoherent-imaging.md), contributions from distinct object points add as [light intensities](light-intensity.md) because their mutual [spatial optical coherence](spatial-optical-coherence.md) is negligible. A single [convolution](convolution.md) additionally requires [translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) of the [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md).
