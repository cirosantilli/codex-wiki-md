# Quadratically weighted inverse-radius Hessian integral

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor integral](isotropic-tensor-integral.md)

Away from the origin the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of $r^{-1}$ is $(3x_kx_l-r^2\delta_{kl})/r^5$. Multiplication by $x_ix_j$ makes the singularity locally integrable. Rotational invariance and the symmetries in each index pair reduce the integral to $A\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+B(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk})$. The Laplacian trace gives $3A+2B=0$, while contracting the two pairs as $k=i,l=j$ gives $3A+12B=\int_{r<a}2/r\,dV=4\pi a^2$. Solving these two equations yields the displayed expression.
