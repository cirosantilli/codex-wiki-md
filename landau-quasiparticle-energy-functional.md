# Landau quasiparticle energy functional

↑ **Parent:** [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md)

Changing one [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) occupation changes the energies of others. To second order around equilibrium,

$$
\delta(E-\mu N)=\sum_{p\sigma}\xi_p\delta n_{p\sigma}+\frac{1}{2\mathcal V}\sum_{p\sigma,p'\sigma'}f_{\sigma\sigma'}(p,p')\delta n_{p\sigma}\delta n_{p'\sigma'}.
$$

Here $\mathcal V$ is [volume](volume.md), occupations are per [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [spin](spin.md) state, and $f$ has units of [energy](energy-split.md) times [volume](volume.md).[p.70, equation (1); integrate the energy shift to obtain the quadratic functional](on-the-theory-of-the-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

For a toy two-state change at fixed [volume](volume.md), take $\delta n_a=\delta n_b=\eta$, $f_{aa}=f_{bb}=0$, $f_{ab}=f_{ba}=g\mathcal V$, and unperturbed energies $\xi_a=\xi_b=e$. The expression gives $2e\eta+g\eta^2$. The factor $1/2$ counts the interaction of the same pair once; it does not halve each excitation's energy. This finite-state illustration only explains the bookkeeping of the [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md).
