# Quadratic scan statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Scan statistic](scan-statistic.md)

A quadratic scan statistic maximizes a directional empirical second moment over candidate unit vectors. For a [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md) sample with a [rank-one covariance spike](rank-one-covariance-spike.md) in one of those directions, the matching statistic has its scale multiplied by the spike's [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). A [chi-squared concentration inequality](chi-squared-concentration-inequality.md) controls each direction.
