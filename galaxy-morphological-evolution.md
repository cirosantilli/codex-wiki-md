# Galaxy morphological evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)

Galaxy morphological evolution changes the relative prevalence and internal structure of discs, spheroids, bars, and irregular systems over time. Comparing epochs requires consistent wavelength, spatial resolution, and selection. [Galaxy mergers](galaxy-merger.md), gas accretion and internal instabilities can all contribute.
