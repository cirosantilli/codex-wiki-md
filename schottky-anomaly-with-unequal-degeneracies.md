# Schottky anomaly with unequal degeneracies

↑ **Parent:** [Schottky anomaly](schottky-anomaly.md)

For independent units with lower and upper [energy levels](energy-level.md) separated by $\Delta>0$, let their degeneracies be $g_0$ and $g_1$. Their shifted [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $q=g_0+g_1e^{-x}$, with $x=\Delta/(k_{\rm B}T)$. Differentiation gives

$$
\frac{C_V}{Nk_{\rm B}}=x^2\frac{g_0g_1e^{-x}}{(g_0+g_1e^{-x})^2}.
$$

The [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) vanishes at both low and high [temperature](temperature-split.md). Degeneracy changes both the maximum and its [temperature](temperature-split.md); the equal-degeneracy result is not universal. Independent lower-level degeneracy also leaves [residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) $Nk_{\rm B}\ln g_0$ in the ideal model.
