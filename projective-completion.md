# Projective completion

↑ **Parent:** [Projective variety](projective-variety.md)

A projective completion of an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) $X$ is a [projective variety](projective-variety.md) containing $X$ as a dense open subvariety. Homogenizing affine defining equations and taking their projective zero set gives the projective closure, although it may add singularities at infinity.
