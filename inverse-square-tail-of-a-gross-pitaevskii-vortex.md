<h1 id="inverse-square-tail-of-a-gross-pitaevskii-vortex">Inverse-square tail of a Gross–Pitaevskii vortex</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

In healing-length units a straight [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) obeys $f''+f'/r-\mathcal N^2f/r^2+(1-f^2)f=0$ and $f\to1$. Inserting $f=1-a/r^2+\cdots$ makes its order-$r^{-2}$ balance $-\mathcal N^2+2a=0$, proving the tail. The physical [healing length](healing-length.md) convention must be stated; the deficit of density $1-f^2$ has twice the leading coefficient of the amplitude deficit $1-f$.
