# Wavelet completion of a multiresolution filter

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature mirror filter](quadrature-mirror-filter.md)

For a [scaling refinement equation](scaling-refinement-equation.md) $\phi=\sum_na_n\phi(2\cdot-n)$, set $m(t)=\frac12\sum_na_ne^{-int}$. The [quadrature mirror filter](quadrature-mirror-filter.md) identity makes the two rows $(m(t),m(t+\pi))$ and $(q(t),q(t+\pi))$ a unitary matrix, where $q(t)=-e^{-it}\overline{m(t+\pi)}$. The complementary coefficients $b_n=(-1)^n\overline{a_{1-n}}$ yield $\psi=\sum_nb_n\phi(2\cdot-n)$. In a [multiresolution analysis](multiresolution-analysis.md), coefficient-space completeness shows its translates form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $V_1\ominus V_0$, hence its dilations and translates form an [orthonormal wavelet](orthonormal-wavelet.md) basis of the whole space.
