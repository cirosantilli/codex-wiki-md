# Test-particle approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Test particle](test-particle.md)

Treating a moving body as a [test particle](test-particle.md) neglects its effect on the externally prescribed field. For a small meteorite moving near a planet, $m/M\ll1$ permits neglect of the planet's recoil and replacement of the relative two-body gravitational parameter $G(M+m)$ by $GM$. This is separate from neglecting [drag](drag-physics.md) or assuming a [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md).
