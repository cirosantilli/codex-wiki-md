# Inextensible filament

↑ **Parent:** [Biopolymer mechanics](biopolymer-mechanics.md)

An inextensible filament keeps the length of every material segment fixed. With material [arc length](arc-length.md) $s$, its centerline obeys $|\mathbf r_s|=1$ and has [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) $\mathbf t=\mathbf r_s$. A [filament tension](filament-tension.md) acts as a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) for this constraint.
