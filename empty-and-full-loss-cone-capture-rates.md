# Empty and full loss-cone capture rates

↑ **Parent:** [Loss cone](loss-cone.md)

Here $\mathcal R_{\rm lc}=J_{\rm lc}^2/J_c^2$ is the orbital [loss cone](loss-cone.md) fraction, $P$ an [orbital period](orbital-period.md), and $t_{\rm rel}$ a [stellar relaxation time](stellar-relaxation-time.md). When angular diffusion per orbit is small relative to the cone, a steady two-dimensional diffusion solution is logarithmic and gives the empty-cone flux. Rapid refilling maintains an approximately isotropic full cone and gives the second rate. Numerical total rates need a stellar density and orbital-energy distribution, not merely a star count.
