# Countable torsion-free embedding with two conjugacy classes

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md)

Every nontrivial countable [torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md) embeds in a countable torsion-free group with precisely two [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md), the identity and all nonidentity elements. At each stage, enumerate all ordered pairs of nonidentity elements of the current group and successively adjoin [HNN extensions](hnn-extension.md) identifying their infinite cyclic subgroups. [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md) preserves embeddings and [torsion in an HNN extension](torsion-in-an-hnn-extension.md) preserves torsion-freeness. Repeat on the resulting countable union, so pairs involving newly introduced elements are eventually included. A final increasing union is countable and torsion-free, and any two nonidentity elements become conjugate at a later stage. No effective enumeration of nonidentity elements is claimed.
