# Spherically symmetric adiabatic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A smooth radial [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md) with a fixed [adiabatic exponent](heat-capacity-ratio.md) conserves $r^2\rho u$ and $p/\rho^\gamma$. In a prescribed [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), its [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md) is $u^2/2+c^2/(\gamma-1)+\Phi$, with $c$ the [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md). Eliminating the density derivative yields $(u-c^2/u)u'=2c^2/r-\Phi'$.
