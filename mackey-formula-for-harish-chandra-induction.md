# Mackey formula for Harish-Chandra induction

↑ **Parent:** [Harish-Chandra induction](harish-chandra-induction.md)

For rational [parabolic subgroups](parabolic-subgroup.md) $P=LU$ and $Q=MV$, define $\mathcal S(M,L)=\{x:M\cap{}^xL\text{ contains a maximal algebraic torus of }G\}$. Then $\,{}^*R_M^G R_L^G$ is the sum over $M^F\backslash\mathcal S(M,L)^F/L^F$ of $R_{M\cap{}^xL}^{M}\,{}^*R_{M\cap{}^xL}^{{}^xL}\operatorname{ad}x$, with the intersection parabolics understood. It follows from ordinary finite-group [Mackey restriction formula](mackey-restriction-formula.md), taking $V^F$-invariants, and the compatible-Levi intersection lemma for parabolics.
