# Induction of the planar square representation to the symmetric group on four letters

↑ **Parent:** [Induced representation](induced-representation.md)

Embed the order-eight [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) as $H=\langle(1234),(12)(34)\rangle$ in the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_4$, and let $W$ be its two-dimensional [complex representation](complex-representation.md) obtained from its [square](square.md) action. Let $C$ be the three-dimensional [complex representation](complex-representation.md) from the [rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md). In the class order $1,(12),(12)(34),(123),(1234)$, the [induced character](induced-character.md) is $(6,0,-2,0,0)$: only the identity and the square's half-turn have nonzero [character](character-of-a-representation.md) values. This equals the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of $C\otimes(\mathbf1\oplus\operatorname{sgn})$, since $\chi_C=(3,-1,-1,0,1)$. [Characters distinguish finite-dimensional complex semisimple representations](characters-distinguish-finite-dimensional-complex-semisimple-representations.md), giving the displayed [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Its [trivial character](trivial-character.md) multiplicity is $(6-6)/24=0$.
