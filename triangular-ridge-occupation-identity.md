# Triangular ridge occupation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential ridge-draft model](exponential-ridge-draft-model.md)

For nonoverlapping triangular [sea-ice pressure ridges](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md) with a common along-track side slope $\tan\delta$, a ridge whose peak exceeds $h$ contributes $2\cot\delta\,dh$ of track length in a draft interval $dh$. Summing over peaks proves the identity. Above the cutoff of an [exponential ridge-draft model](exponential-ridge-draft-model.md), $g(h)=Ae^{-bh}$ with $A=2B/(b\tan\delta)$. This draft occupation is not the normalized peak [probability density function](probability-density-function.md); level ice and shallower parts supply the remaining probability.
