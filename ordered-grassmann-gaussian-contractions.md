# Ordered Grassmann Gaussian contractions

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md)

Use the [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) orientation $\int\prod_i d\bar\theta_i d\theta_i\,\theta_1\bar\theta_1\cdots\theta_n\bar\theta_n=1$. For an invertible [matrix](matrix.md) $B$, the normalized weight $e^{-\bar\theta B\theta}$ has $\langle\theta_i\bar\theta_j\rangle=(B^{-1})_{ij}$. Differentiate its [determinant](determinant.md) with respect to $B_{ji}$ to obtain this formula; two derivatives give the displayed four-point formula. The second pairing carries a [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md). These are ordered contractions, so exchanging two odd factors changes their sign.
