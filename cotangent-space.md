# Cotangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent space](tangent-space.md)

The cotangent space at $p$ is the [dual vector space](linear-functional.md) $T_p^*M=(T_pM)^*$. Its elements are [covectors](covector.md) on the [tangent space](tangent-space.md). In a [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md) $x$, the basis $dx^i|_p$ is dual to $\partial_i|_p$. Under a coordinate change to $y$, the coefficients of $\alpha=a_i dx^i=b_jdy^j$ obey $b_j=\sum_i a_i\partial x^i/\partial y^j$. The disjoint union of these spaces is the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md).
