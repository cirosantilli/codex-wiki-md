# Lottery

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility](expected-utility.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) over possible outcomes. A finite lottery assigning probabilities $p_j$ to outcomes $\omega_j$ has [expected utility](expected-utility.md) $\sum_jp_j u(\omega_j)$. Mixing [lotteries](lottery.md) mixes their outcome probabilities; the [independence axiom for lottery preferences](independence-axiom-for-lottery-preferences.md) describes how this operation preserves preference comparisons.
