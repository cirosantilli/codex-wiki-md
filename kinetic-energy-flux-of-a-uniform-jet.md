# Kinetic energy flux of a uniform jet

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum flux](momentum-flux.md)

A steady uniform incompressible jet has [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) $\rho Au$, [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $\rho Au^2$ and kinetic-energy flux $\rho Au^3/2$. These use the area and velocity at the same cross section. A nonuniform profile instead requires integrating powers of local speed; using mean speed in a cubic flux without a profile correction is generally wrong.
