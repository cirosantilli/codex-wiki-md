# Irrotational vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Curl](curl.md)

A continuously differentiable [vector field](vector-field.md) on an open subset of three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) is irrotational when its [curl](curl.md) is zero. Under the Euclidean identification $\alpha=X_i\,dx^i$, this says that the [differential one-form](one-form.md) $\alpha$ is a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md). The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) gives a [potential of a conservative vector field](potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) locally. A global potential additionally requires zero [line integrals](line-integral.md) around every closed curve; [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) domains ensures this. On the punctured plane extended in the third direction, $X=(-y,x,0)/(x^2+y^2)$ has zero curl but integral $2\pi$ around a circle, so it is locally conservative and has no single-valued global potential.
