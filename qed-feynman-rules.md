# QED Feynman rules

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

The QED Feynman rules combine the [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md), [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md), and vertex $-ie\gamma^\mu$ with external Dirac spinors and photon polarization vectors. In a covariant gauge they generate perturbative electron-photon scattering amplitudes.
