# Quadratic assignment problem

↑ **Parent:** [Integer programming](integer-programming.md)

A [permutation](permutation.md) assigns facilities to locations, and the objective combines their pairwise interactions with pairwise location costs. Ordered-pair and unordered-pair conventions differ by a factor of two for symmetric data. The decision version is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md): letting $A$ record a directed cycle makes the objective exactly the length of a [travelling salesman problem](travelling-salesman-problem.md) tour under the ordering given by the [permutation](permutation.md). The optimization version is [NP-hard](np-hardness.md).
