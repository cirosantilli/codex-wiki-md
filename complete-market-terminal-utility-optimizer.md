# Complete-market terminal utility optimizer

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

For an increasing strictly concave [utility function](utility-function-split.md) satisfying the [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md), put $I=(U')^{-1}$ and let $\zeta$ be the positive terminal [state-price density](state-price-density.md). In a finite complete market, select $y>0$ so that $\mathbb E[\zeta I(y\zeta)]$ equals initial wealth. The displayed payoff is optimal under that budget: the supporting-line inequality for utility has marginal slope $y\zeta$, and its expected linear error vanishes on the budget constraint. [Market completeness](complete-market.md) supplies its replicating strategy.
