# Circular orbit perpendicular to a uniform rod

↑ **Parent:** [Axial and transverse gravity of a uniform rod](axial-and-transverse-gravity-of-a-uniform-rod.md)

For a light test [mass](mass.md) in the rod's perpendicular-bisector plane, axial [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) makes the attraction radial. Equating $mv^2/r$ to the transverse [force](force-split.md) gives the title [speed](speed.md). Far outside the rod it is Keplerian, proportional to $r^{-1/2}$. Well inside the half-length but outside its physical thickness, the rod approximates an infinite line and the circular [speed](speed.md) is nearly constant, $\sqrt{GM/a}$. Extrapolation to an orbit inside the material or a zero-radius orbit is outside this thin-rod model.
