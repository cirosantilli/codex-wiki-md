# Rotational invariance of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero in $\mathbb R^n$, every fixed [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md) preserves the process law. Its transformed increments remain [independent](independent-random-variables.md) centred [Gaussian random vectors](gaussian-random-vector.md) with [covariance](covariance.md) $(t-s)I$, and its paths remain continuous. Consequently its exit law from any centred sphere is normalized surface measure. Translations give the same assertion for spheres centred at its starting point.
