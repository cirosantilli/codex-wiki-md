# A norm-closed dual subspace need not be weak-star closed

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)

The [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) of $\ell^1$ is $\ell^\infty$ under the complex-linear coordinate pairing. Its subspace $c_0$ is [norm](norm.md) closed: uniform approximation by a sequence with a small tail leaves a small tail. Nevertheless its preannihilator in $\ell^1$ is zero, because the coordinate [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) belong to $c_0$ and test every coordinate. Its double continuous annihilator is therefore all of $\ell^\infty$. It is weak-star dense directly as well: the finite truncations of any bounded sequence lie in $c_0$ and converge against every $\ell^1$ test by absolute summability. This demonstrates why [norm](norm.md) [closure](closure-topology.md) cannot replace [closure](closure-topology.md) in the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) in the dual-side annihilator theorem.
