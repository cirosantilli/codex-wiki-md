# Voltage source

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_source)

An ideal voltage source prescribes an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md); its [electric current](electric-current.md) is determined by the connected [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md). A [dependent electrical source](dependent-electrical-source.md) can prescribe a voltage controlled by another circuit variable instead.
