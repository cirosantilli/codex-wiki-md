# Kuzmin disk

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

A Kuzmin disk is an infinitesimally thin axisymmetric [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md) whose [relative potential](relative-potential.md) is $\psi=Gm/\sqrt{R^2+(|z|+b)^2}$, with $m,b>0$. Away from the disk this is a point-mass vacuum field. The jump of its vertical derivative is $-2Gmb/(R^2+b^2)^{3/2}$, so the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md), written $\nabla^2\psi=-4\pi G\rho$, gives the displayed [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). Integrating $2\pi R\Sigma$ gives total [mass](mass.md) $m$.
