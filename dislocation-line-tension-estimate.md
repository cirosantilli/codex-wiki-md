# Dislocation line-tension estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

A curved [dislocation](dislocation.md) can be approximated as a line with tension $T_\ell$ of order $Gb^2$. The coefficient depends on elastic constants, character and logarithmic length scales. The simple estimate $T_\ell\simeq Gb^2/2$ gives a pinning bypass [stress](stress.md) $Gb/\ell$ for a semicircular segment of free span $\ell$.
