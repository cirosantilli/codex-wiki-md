# Finite-dimensional Lindbladians have stationary states

↑ **Parent:** [Lindbladian](lindbladian.md)

For any initial [density operator](density-matrix.md), average its time-independent [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md) trajectory as $\bar\rho_T=T^{-1}\int_0^T\rho(t)\,dt$. These averages stay in the compact convex set of [density operators](density-matrix.md). Along a convergent subsequence, $\mathcal L(\bar\rho_T)=(\rho(T)-\rho(0))/T\to0$, so the limit is stationary. Thus a finite-dimensional physical [Affine Bloch equation](affine-bloch-equation.md) has at least one physical equilibrium, even though an arbitrary affine real equation can have none.
