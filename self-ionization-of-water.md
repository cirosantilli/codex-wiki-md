# Self-ionization of water

↑ **Parent:** [Water](water.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-ionization_of_water)

Two [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) can exchange a [proton](proton.md), producing [hydronium](hydronium.md) and [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) [ions](ion.md): $2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O\rightleftharpoons\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^++\mathrm{OH}^-$. Even pure [water](water.md) therefore supplies [ions](ion.md); extreme [dilution](dilution.md) eventually invalidates the [strong acid](strong-acid.md) assumption that it supplies all the [hydronium](hydronium.md).
