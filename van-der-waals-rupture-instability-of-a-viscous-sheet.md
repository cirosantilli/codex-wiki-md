# Van der Waals rupture instability of a viscous sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Disjoining pressure](disjoining-pressure.md)

For a viscous sheet of half-thickness $h_0$, attractive [disjoining pressure](disjoining-pressure.md) $V/h^3$, and [surface tension](surface-tension.md) $\gamma$, a symmetric Fourier mode of dimensionless wavenumber $K=kh_0$ has a destabilizing molecular contribution and a stabilizing capillary contribution. The ratio

$$
\Gamma=\frac{\gamma h_0^2}{3V}
$$

sets the cutoff $K=\Gamma^{-1/2}$.
