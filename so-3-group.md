<h1 id="so-3-group">SO(3) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

The SO(3) group consists of real three-by-three [orthogonal matrices](orthogonal-matrix.md) with [determinant](determinant.md) one. It acts by [rotation in three dimensions](rotation-in-three-dimensions.md). Its [group manifold](group-manifold.md) is $\mathbb{RP}^3$, since the [Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3)](adjoint-double-cover-from-su-2-to-so-3.md) identifies antipodal points of the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md).
