# Counter-rotating weak-transition amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md)

The first-order response to a cosine drive contains both resonant and counter-rotating terms. The [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md) removes the rapidly oscillating contribution only under near-resonant, weak-drive and appropriate time-scale assumptions. It does not remove the separate requirement of weak excitation when the initial amplitude is held fixed.
