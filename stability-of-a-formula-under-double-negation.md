# Stability of a formula under double negation

↑ **Parent:** [Intuitionistic first-order logic](intuitionistic-first-order-logic.md)

A [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) $A$ is stable when [intuitionistic first-order logic](intuitionistic-first-order-logic.md) proves $\neg\neg A\to A$. Double negations and [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md) are stable. [Logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) of stable [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) is stable; a [logical implication](logical-implication.md) is stable when its consequent is stable; and [universal quantification](universal-quantification.md) preserves stability. For [logical implication](logical-implication.md), from $\neg\neg(A\to B)$ and $A$, assuming $\neg B$ contradicts the first hypothesis, giving $\neg\neg B$ and then $B$. For universality, obtain $\neg\neg A(t)$ from $\neg\neg\forall x\,A(x)$ for arbitrary $t$, then use stability and generalize.
