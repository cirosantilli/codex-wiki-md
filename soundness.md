# Soundness

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundness)

A proof system is [sound](soundness.md) with respect to its semantics when every derivable conclusion is true in every interpretation satisfying its premises. Both [soundness theorem for propositional logic](soundness-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md) and [soundness theorem for first-order logic](soundness-theorem-for-first-order-logic.md) establish this property for particular languages and semantics.
