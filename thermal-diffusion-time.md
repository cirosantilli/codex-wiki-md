# Thermal diffusion time

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md)

The thermal diffusion time across a distance $L$ is $L^2/\kappa$, where $\kappa$ is the [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md). It is the time required for a temperature disturbance to spread over that distance by [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md).
