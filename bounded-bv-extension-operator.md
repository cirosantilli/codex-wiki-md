# Bounded BV extension operator

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

For a bounded [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), functions can be extended with the displayed bound and common [compact support](compact-support.md). Reflect across local Lipschitz boundary graphs, combine the reflected functions with a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md), and multiply by a fixed [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md). Lipschitz coordinate changes control [derivative](derivative.md) [measures](measure.md); the product rule bounds [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) terms by the $L^1$ [norm](norm.md). Zero extension is also possible with an estimate for [BV traces on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md), but its boundary jump must be included in the variation.
