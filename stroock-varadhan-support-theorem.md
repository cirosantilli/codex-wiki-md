# Stroock-Varadhan support theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

For smooth bounded vector fields, the support of a Stratonovich diffusion in uniform path topology is the closure of solutions to the controlled [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) with the same drift and controls in the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md). The [support of enhanced Brownian motion](support-of-enhanced-brownian-motion.md) and the [universal limit theorem](universal-limit-theorem.md) prove this through continuity of the enhanced solution map. For an equation written in Itô form, the controlled drift is the Itô drift minus $\tfrac12\sum_iDV_iV_i$.
