# Simon coset state

↑ **Parent:** [Simon's algorithm](simon-s-algorithm.md)

In [Simon's algorithm](simon-s-algorithm.md), conditioning on a function value leaves the first [quantum register](quantum-register.md) in an equal coherent sum of its two [preimages](preimage.md). This is a [pure state](pure-state.md), even though discarding the second [quantum register](quantum-register.md) without retaining its outcome generally leaves a [mixed state](mixed-state.md). The label $x$ depends on the measured value; the period $s$ does not.
