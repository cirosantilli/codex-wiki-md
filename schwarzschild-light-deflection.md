# Schwarzschild light deflection

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

A null Schwarzschild geodesic with $b\gg r_s$ is deflected through $2r_s/b=4GM/(bc^2)$ to first order.

This is the leading spherical-mass deflection underlying a [gravitational lens](gravitational-lens.md).
