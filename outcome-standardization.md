# Outcome standardization

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

Outcome standardization averages fitted conditional outcome risks over a specified target population's pretreatment covariate distribution. The same target distribution is used for each treatment strategy. Under [conditional exchangeability](conditional-exchangeability.md), the [positivity assumption](positivity-assumption.md) and consistency, this average identifies the target mean of the corresponding [potential outcomes](potential-outcome.md). For a target consisting of treated subjects, average over their covariate distribution. A conditional [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md) cannot generally be applied directly to an aggregate mean risk: the nonlinear odds-to-probability conversion must be performed before averaging. [Outcome standardization](outcome-standardization.md) supplies that individual-risk calculation.
