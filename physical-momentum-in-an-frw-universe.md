# Physical momentum in an FRW universe

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving momentum](comoving-momentum.md)

A freely falling particle's physical [momentum](momentum.md) is measured in the local [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) of a [comoving observer](comoving-observer.md). With proper [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) $\mathbf v=a\,d\mathbf x/dt$ and [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}$, it is $m\gamma\mathbf v$. It differs by one factor of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) from the conserved [comoving momentum](comoving-momentum.md). For a [nonrelativistic particle](nonrelativistic-particle.md), its [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) therefore decays as $a^{-1}$.
