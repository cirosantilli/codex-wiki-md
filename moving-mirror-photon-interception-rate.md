# Moving-mirror photon interception rate

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-mirror Doppler shift](moving-mirror-doppler-shift.md)

For a [photon](photon.md) emitted from the origin and arriving at receding mirror position $x(t)$, its emission time is $t_e=t-x(t)/c$. Thus $dt_e/dt=1-\beta$ and a steady source rate $R$ gives impact rate $R(1-\beta)$ in source-frame coordinate time. This is distinct from the emission rate and from a rate per mirror [proper time](proper-time.md).
