# T3 space

↑ **Parent:** [Regular topological space](regular-topological-space.md)

A [T3 space](t3-space.md) is a [regular topological space](regular-topological-space.md) which is also a [T1 space](t1-space.md). Every [metric space](metric-space.md) has this property: singletons are closed, and a sufficiently small ball about a [point](point-geometry.md) outside a [closed set](closed-set.md) has [closure](closure-topology.md) avoiding that set. A second-[countable](countable-set.md) [T3 space](t3-space.md) is a [metrizable space](metrizable-space.md) by [Urysohn's metrization theorem](urysohn-s-metrization-theorem.md).
