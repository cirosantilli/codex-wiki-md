# Contact distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Contact structure](contact-structure.md)

A [contact form](contact-form.md) $\alpha$ defines the hyperplane distribution $\xi=\ker\alpha$. Multiplying $\alpha$ by a nowhere-zero function leaves this distribution unchanged. The [contact distribution symplectic form](contact-distribution-symplectic-form.md) distinguishes it from an integrable hyperplane distribution.
