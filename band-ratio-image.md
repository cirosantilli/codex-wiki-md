# Band ratio image

↑ **Parent:** [Multispectral imaging](multispectral-imaging.md)

An image formed by dividing corresponding calibrated pixels in two spectral bands. If their signals have a common multiplicative factor, $L_1=k\rho_1$ and $L_2=k\rho_2$, their ratio is $\rho_1/\rho_2$ and does not depend on $k$. This explains suppression of some common illumination effects and enhancement of spectral contrasts. Additive atmospheric radiance and small denominators prevent exact cancellation and can amplify errors. [Normalized difference vegetation index](normalized-difference-vegetation-index.md) is a related normalized spectral contrast.
