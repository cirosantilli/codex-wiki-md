# Infinite-Prandtl-number convection

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

In thermal-time units, taking the [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) large removes momentum inertia at leading order. The [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) momentum equation then determines [velocity](velocity.md) instantaneously from the [temperature](temperature.md) perturbation, while the nonlinear [heat equation](heat-equation.md) retains its time derivative. Small [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) does not generally give this model. An exact contrasting shear in a free-slip layer is $u=U_0e^{-\operatorname{Pr}\pi^2t}\cos\pi z$, $w=\theta=0$: it decays slowly as the [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) tends to zero but is excluded by the instantaneous Stokes equation.
