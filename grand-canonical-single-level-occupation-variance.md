# Grand-canonical single-level occupation variance

↑ **Parent:** [Grand canonical partition function](grand-canonical-partition-function.md)

For one [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [fermion](fermion.md) level, occupations 0 and 1 form a [Bernoulli distribution](bernoulli-distribution.md) and give $\operatorname{Var}n=\bar n(1-\bar n)$. For one ideal [boson](boson.md) level in contact with a reservoir, its [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) gives $\operatorname{Var}n=\bar n(1+\bar n)$. Both approach [variance](variance-split.md) equal to mean at small occupation. Pauli exclusion suppresses fluctuations of nearly full [fermion](fermion.md) states, whereas large [boson](boson.md) occupation gives [variance](variance-split.md) of order mean squared. Fixed-total-number constraints change condensate fluctuation statements.
