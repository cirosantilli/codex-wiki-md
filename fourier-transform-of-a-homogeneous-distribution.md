# Fourier transform of a homogeneous distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous distribution](homogeneous-distribution.md)

The [scaling property of the Fourier transform](scaling-property-of-the-fourier-transform.md) extends by duality to

$$
\widehat{\delta_tu}=t^{-n}\delta_{1/t}\widehat u.
$$

Consequently a [homogeneous distribution](homogeneous-distribution.md) of degree $\sigma$ transforms into one of degree $-n-\sigma$.
