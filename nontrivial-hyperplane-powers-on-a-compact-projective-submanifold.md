# Nontrivial hyperplane powers on a compact projective submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md)

Let $X$ be a nonempty positive-dimensional compact [complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md) of [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md). On a positive-dimensional connected component choose a hyperplane meeting, but not containing, that component. Its linear equation gives a [holomorphic section](holomorphic-section.md) of $\mathcal O_X(1)$ with a zero and with a nonzero value. For $m>0$, its $m$th power cannot be a section of a trivial [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md), since such sections are constant on the compact connected component. Negative powers are also nontrivial by duality. Every positive-dimensional compact projective component meets every hyperplane: otherwise its affine coordinate functions would all be constant.
