# Transformer (electrical engineering)

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer)

An [electrical transformer](transformer-electrical-engineering.md) transfers [energy](energy-split.md) between windings through [electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md). Its operation depends on changing [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), rather than a static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) alone.
