# Focal point of a hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike geodesic vector field](timelike-geodesic-vector-field.md)

A focal point of a hypersurface along an orthogonal geodesic is a point where a nonzero normal [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) arising from variations of the initial point vanishes. Beyond the first focal point, that geodesic no longer locally maximizes proper time from a spacelike initial hypersurface.
