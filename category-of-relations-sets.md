# Category of relations (sets)

↑ **Parent:** [Category of relations](category-of-relations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_of_relations)

The category of relations on [sets](set-split.md) has sets as objects and [binary relations](binary-relation.md) as morphisms. Relational composition declares $aRc$ when some intermediate $b$ satisfies both component relations; the identity is the diagonal relation.
