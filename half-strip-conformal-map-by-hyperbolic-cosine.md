# Half-strip conformal map by hyperbolic cosine

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

For $b>0$, the [conformal map](conformal-map.md) $\cosh(\pi z/b)$ takes $\{\operatorname{Re}z>0,\ 0<\operatorname{Im}z<b\}$ bijectively onto the upper half-plane. The exponential maps this half-strip onto the upper half-plane exterior to the unit circle; then $\cosh(\pi z/b)=(w+w^{-1})/2$ is a scaled [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md). All critical points of the entire map are $z=inb$, mapping alternately to $1$ and $-1$.
