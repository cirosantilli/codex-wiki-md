# Navier-Stokes equation with spectrally truncated advection

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)

Replacing the advecting velocity by a fixed finite Stokes projection $P_Nu$ makes the three-dimensional nonlinearity controlled by $\lambda_N^{1/4}|u|\|v\|\|w\|$. This yields global existence and uniqueness at the weak energy level.
