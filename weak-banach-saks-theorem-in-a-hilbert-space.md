<h1 id="weak-banach-saks-theorem-in-a-hilbert-space">Weak Banach–Saks theorem in a Hilbert space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence in a Hilbert space](weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md)

If $x_n\rightharpoonup x$ in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), some subsequence $(x_{n_k})$ has [Cesaro means](cesaro-mean.md) converging in norm to $x$. For $x=0$, choose the subsequence so successive vectors have summably small inner products; expansion of the squared norm of its averages then proves convergence.

Thus [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md) have the weak form of the [Banach-Saks property](banach-saks-property.md).
