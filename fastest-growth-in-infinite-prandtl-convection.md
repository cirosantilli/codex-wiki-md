# Fastest growth in infinite-Prandtl convection

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite-Prandtl-number convection](infinite-prandtl-number-convection.md)

For a stress-free, fixed-temperature layer, the infinite-[Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) growth rate of the fundamental vertical [normal mode](normal-mode.md) is $\lambda_\infty=Rk^2/(k^2+\pi^2)^2-(k^2+\pi^2)$. For large [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md), its maximum has $k^2=\pi^2-8\pi^6/R+O(R^{-2})$ and $\lambda_{\rm max}=R/(4\pi^2)-2\pi^2+O(R^{-1})$. The infinite-[Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) limit must be taken first, or inertia must remain small on the resulting growth-time scale. This optimum differs from the [free-slip convection neutral curve](free-slip-convection-neutral-curve.md) minimum, which selects onset rather than fastest growth far above it.
