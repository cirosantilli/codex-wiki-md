# Multiphase flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A flow containing two or more distinguishable material phases, such as two immiscible liquids or a gas-liquid mixture. Each phase can have its own [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md), [density](density.md) and [velocity](velocity.md). Cross-sectional or ensemble averaging produces coupled phase balance equations whose mass, momentum and energy exchange terms must be specified consistently. A common [pressure](pressure-split.md) alone does not automatically make an averaged model well posed.
