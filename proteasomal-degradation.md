# Proteasomal degradation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[Proteasomal degradation](proteasomal-degradation.md) destroys selected proteins through the proteasome. Regulated substrate targeting, often involving ubiquitin, allows a cell to terminate activities by removing the protein rather than merely reversing a modification.
