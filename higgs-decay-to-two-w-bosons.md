# Higgs decay to two W bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

The tree-level two-body [decay width](decay-width.md) from $2m_W^2HW^+_\mu W^{-\mu}/v$ is

$$
\Gamma=\frac{m_H^3}{16\pi v^2}\sqrt{1-4x}\,(1-4x+12x^2),\qquad x=m_W^2/m_H^2,
$$

above the on-shell threshold. The charged pair is distinguishable, so no identical-particle factor divides this result. Below threshold, physical multi-particle decays through virtual [W bosons](w-boson.md) require a different [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md).
