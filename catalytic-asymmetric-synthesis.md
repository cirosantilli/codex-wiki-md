# Catalytic asymmetric synthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

A [chiral](chirality-chemistry.md) catalyst preferentially forms one stereoisomer from an achiral or stereochemically labile substrate. Product [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md) depends on competing transition-state [free energies](thermodynamic-free-energy.md), rather than a catalyst simply transferring its own R or S label.
