# Inelastic X-ray scattering

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray scattering techniques](x-ray-scattering-techniques.md)

[Inelastic X-ray scattering](inelastic-x-ray-scattering.md) measures [energy](energy-split.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) transferred from [X-rays](x-ray.md) to matter. With sufficiently high [energy resolution](energy-resolution.md), it can map a [phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md) by measuring inelastic peaks at different momentum transfers. [Crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) is determined modulo a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector.
