# Self-avoiding walk

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice path](lattice-path.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-avoiding_walk)

An $n$-step [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) on a [graph](graph-split.md) is a sequence $v_0,\ldots,v_n$ of distinct [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between successive [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). It is a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) with $n$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and $n+1$ [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). On a transitive [graph](graph-split.md), the exponential growth rate of rooted [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) counts is the [connective constant](connective-constant.md).
