# Lindemann melting criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Melting](melting.md)

[Frederick Lindemann](frederick-lindemann-1st-viscount-cherwell.md)'s heuristic associates [melting](melting.md) with vibrational displacement reaching a specified fraction of the nearest-neighbor distance $d$. The displacement convention must be stated: one-coordinate variance and full three-dimensional variance differ by a factor of three. With the high-temperature [Einstein mean-square displacement](einstein-mean-square-displacement.md), the displayed convention gives $T_m=c_L^2d^2mk_BT_E^2/\hbar^2$. Neither $c_L$ nor the independent-oscillator approximation is universal.
