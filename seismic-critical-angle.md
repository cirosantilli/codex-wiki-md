# Seismic critical angle

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic head wave](seismic-head-wave.md)

For a P ray incident from a layer of [speed](speed.md) $\alpha_1$ onto a faster layer $\alpha_2$, the transmitted ray is tangential when $\sin i_c=\alpha_1/\alpha_2$. Angles are measured from the interface normal.
