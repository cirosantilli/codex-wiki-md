# Energy recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)

Returning [energy](energy-split.md) from a used flow or beam to an upstream supply or a useful reservoir. An [electron](electron.md) beam decelerated before collection returns much of its [acceleration](acceleration.md) [energy](energy-split.md); the net input then covers unrecovered [energy](energy-split.md) and auxiliary losses.
