# Group-velocity minimum of a flexural-gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Flexural-gravity wave](flexural-gravity-wave.md)

The [group velocity](group-velocity.md) of a [flexural-gravity wave](flexural-gravity-wave.md) diverges on the long-wave gravity branch and on the short-wave bending branch, and therefore has a positive minimum between them when the [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) is positive. This minimum bounds the propagation speed of narrow [wave packets](wave-packet.md) within the ideal model. It does not by itself determine a wind-generation threshold: steady forcing requires matching [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), while dissipation and coupling determine growth.
