# Curvature formula for a holomorphic subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form of a holomorphic subbundle](second-fundamental-form-of-a-holomorphic-subbundle.md)

In an adapted smooth unitary frame with input-index connection convention, the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) matrix has blocks $(\theta_F,B;-\overline B^t,\theta_Q)$, where the [second fundamental form of a holomorphic subbundle](second-fundamental-form-of-a-holomorphic-subbundle.md) $B$ has type $(1,0)$. The [Cartan curvature equation with input indices](cartan-curvature-equation-with-input-indices.md) gives the displayed formula. If the ambient curvature is zero, $\Theta_F(X,\overline X)=-B(X)B(X)^\dagger$ is negative semidefinite. Dualizing the [determinant line bundle](determinant-line-bundle.md) therefore gives semipositive curvature.
