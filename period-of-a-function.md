# Period of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Period_of_a_function)

A nonzero number $T$ is a period of a [function](function-split.md) $f$ when $f(x+T)=f(x)$ throughout its domain.
