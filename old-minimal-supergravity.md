# Old-minimal supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal four-dimensional supergravity](minimal-four-dimensional-supergravity.md)

Old-minimal four-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md) adds a complex scalar $M$ and a real vector $b_\mu$ to the [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) and [gravitino](gravitino.md). These six real bosonic [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) components have no propagating kinetic terms. They balance the [off-shell component count of minimal supergravity](off-shell-component-count-of-minimal-supergravity.md) and permit the local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) algebra to close without imposing the propagating [Euler-Lagrange field equations](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md).
