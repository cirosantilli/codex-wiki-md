# KRAS G12C protein

↑ **Parent:** [Ras](ras.md)

A KRAS [protein](protein-split.md) bearing cysteine in place of glycine at residue 12. The introduced cysteine permits selective covalent chemistry at a suitable pocket; this strategy does not automatically apply to other [Ras](ras.md) [mutations](mutation.md).
