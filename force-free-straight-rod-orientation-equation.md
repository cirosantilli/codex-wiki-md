# Force-free straight-rod orientation equation

↑ **Parent:** [Straight-rod resistance in a linear flow](straight-rod-resistance-in-a-linear-flow.md)

The orientation of a [force-free](force-free.md), [torque-free](torque-free.md) infinitely slender straight rod follows background rotation plus the transverse component of strain. The last term preserves $|\mathbf p|=1$. Finite aspect ratio changes the strain coefficient and permits periodic tumbling in a [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md).
