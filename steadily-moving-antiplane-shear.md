# Steadily moving antiplane shear

↑ **Parent:** [Antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md)

For subsonic motion $|V|<c$, an [antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md) [displacement](displacement.md) $w(x_1-Vt,x_2)$ satisfies $\beta^2w_{xx}+w_{x_2x_2}=0$. The displayed rescaling makes it a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) of $(x,y)$, so locally $w=\operatorname{Re}H(x+iy)$ for a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $H$. Set $F=\beta H$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) then give $\mu F'=\beta\sigma_{13}-i\sigma_{23}$. Keeping this potential normalization explicit avoids losing a factor of $\beta$ in the dynamic [stress](stress.md) field. At sonic or supersonic speeds the elliptic harmonic reduction does not apply.
