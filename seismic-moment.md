# Seismic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

For uniform slip on a shear fault, scalar [seismic moment](seismic-moment.md) is $M_0=\mu A d$, with [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu$, fault [area](surface-area.md) $A$ and slip magnitude $d$. More generally it integrates rigidity-weighted slip. It measures source strength rather than radiated [energy](energy-split.md) alone.
