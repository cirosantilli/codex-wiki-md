# Principal divisor on an algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

For a nonzero [rational function](rational-function.md) $f$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) $C$, its principal divisor is

$$
(f)=\operatorname{div}(f)=\sum_{p\in C}\operatorname{ord}_p(f)p.
$$

It records the zeros of $f$ with positive multiplicity and its poles with negative multiplicity. Every principal divisor has [degree](degree-of-a-divisor.md) zero.
