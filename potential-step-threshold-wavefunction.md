# Potential-step threshold wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Step potential](step-potential.md)

For a positive [step potential](step-potential.md) and left incidence, take $E\to V_0^+$ at fixed incident [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) $A$. The transmitted [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) tends to zero while the incident wavenumber tends to $k=\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$. [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md) gives reflected amplitude $A$ and transmitted amplitude $2A$, producing the spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) in the title. The left [probability density](probability-density.md) is $4|A|^2\cos^2kx$ and the right density $4|A|^2$. The transmitted [probability current](probability-current.md) still tends to zero because its velocity tends to zero. Over adjacent intervals of length $2\pi/k$, the right integrated density is twice the left. These ratios use finite local integrals of a non-normalizable stationary scattering state.
