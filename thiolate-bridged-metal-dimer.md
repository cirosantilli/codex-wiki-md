# Thiolate-bridged metal dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md)

A pair of [metal](metal.md) centres connected through one or more [thiolate](thiolate.md) [sulfur](sulfur.md) donors. A $\mathrm{M_2(\mu-SR)_2}$ diamond can coexist with a direct [metal](metal.md)–[metal](metal.md) bond; each bridging [thiolate](thiolate.md) supplies a donor interaction at both [metals](metal.md).
