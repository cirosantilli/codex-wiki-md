# Knapsack problem

↑ **Parent:** [Integer programming](integer-programming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knapsack_problem)

The knapsack problem selects items of prescribed weights and profits under a total-weight capacity. The [0-1 knapsack problem](0-1-knapsack-problem.md) permits each item at most once; bounded and unbounded multiplicity variants use different integer restrictions. Nonnegative weights and capacity are assumed here.
