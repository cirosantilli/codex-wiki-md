# Homology comparison proof of Freudenthal suspension

↑ **Parent:** [Freudenthal suspension theorem](freudenthal-suspension-theorem.md)

The [integral homology of the loop space of a sphere](integral-homology-of-the-loop-space-of-a-sphere.md) implies that the unit $S^{r-1}\to\Omega S^r$ has vanishing [relative homology](relative-homology.md) through degree $2r-3$. Both spaces are simply connected for $r\geq3$. The absolute [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) first identifies their degree-two [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) with [homology](homology-split.md); the relative [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) then rules out a first nonzero [relative homotopy group](relative-homotopy-group.md) in degrees three through $2r-3$. The relative [homotopy](homotopy.md) sequence makes the unit an isomorphism on $\pi_i$ through degree $2r-4$. Loop-suspension adjunction identifies this map with [suspension](suspension-topology.md), yielding $\pi_i(S^{r-1})\cong\pi_{i+1}(S^r)$ in that range.
