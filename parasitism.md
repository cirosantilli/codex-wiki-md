# Parasitism

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology](ecology.md)

[Parasitism](parasitism.md) is an interaction in which a [parasite](parasite.md) obtains resources from a [host](host-biology.md), reducing some component of the [host](host-biology.md)'s performance. Its demographic effects depend on exposure, within-host processes and transmission opportunities.
