# Current of a translated Gaussian coherent state

↑ **Parent:** [Probability current](probability-current.md)

Here $\alpha>0$ and $q,p,\gamma$ are real functions of time. Differentiation in position gives $\psi_x=[-\alpha(x-q)+ip/\hbar]\psi$. The standard [probability current](probability-current.md) is therefore

$$
j=\frac\hbar m\operatorname{Im}(\overline\psi\psi_x)=\frac pm\sqrt{\frac\alpha\pi}\,e^{-\alpha(x-q)^2}.
$$

For a [coherent state](coherent-state.md) moving in a quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), the centre follows the classical equation $\dot q=p/m$, so the current is the normalized [Gaussian probability density](normal-distribution.md) transported at its centre velocity. The factor $\sqrt{\alpha/\pi}$ is essential: omitting it changes the current by a dimensional constant and violates the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) for the normalized density. A spatially constant phase does not affect the current.
