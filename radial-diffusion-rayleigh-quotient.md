# Radial diffusion Rayleigh quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md)

For axisymmetric [heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md) in a unit disk, separated modes satisfy $-(r\kappa R')'=\nu rR$, with weight $r$, outer [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $R(1)=0$, and zero radial flux at the origin. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md). Its [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) under fixed $\int rR^2$ recovers the mode equation; the natural endpoint term is $r\kappa R'\delta R$. The [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md) says that every admissible nonzero trial profile gives an upper bound on the bottom of the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md), and on the lowest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) when this bottom is attained by a [fundamental diffusion mode](fundamental-diffusion-mode.md). Finite weighted energy, rather than pointwise existence of a second derivative at the origin, is the relevant trial admissibility requirement.
