# Cycle notation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A [cycle](permutation-cycle.md) $(a_1\,a_2\,\ldots\,a_k)$ sends $a_1$ to $a_2$, continuing cyclically back from $a_k$ to $a_1$, and fixes elements outside its support. Products conventionally act from right to left. Every [permutation](permutation.md) is a product of disjoint [cycles](permutation-cycle.md), unique up to their ordering and cyclic rotation of each displayed list.
