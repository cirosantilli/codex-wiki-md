# Orthogonal-invariant scattering channels

↑ **Parent:** [O(N)-invariant S-matrix](o-n-invariant-s-matrix.md)

The two-vector [tensor product](tensor-product.md) splits into a trace singlet, symmetric traceless tensors and antisymmetric tensors. For an [O(N)-invariant S-matrix](o-n-invariant-s-matrix.md), the corresponding [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $s_0=NS_1+S_2+S_3$, $s_+=S_2+S_3$ and $s_-=S_2-S_3$. For $N\ge2$, analytic [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) requires $s_a(\theta)s_a(-\theta)=1$. With [Hermitian analyticity of a two-particle S-matrix](hermitian-analyticity-of-a-two-particle-s-matrix.md), each channel has unit modulus on the real [rapidity](rapidity.md) axis.
