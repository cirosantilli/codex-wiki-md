# Negative supercoiling

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[Negative supercoiling](negative-supercoiling.md) describes torsionally constrained DNA with linking number below its relaxed reference value. Under appropriate conditions this stress can favour local strand opening or a transition to a different helical conformation, including [Z-DNA](z-dna.md).
