# Gram-positive bacteria

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram-positive_bacteria)

Typical [Gram-positive bacteria](gram-positive-bacteria.md) have a relatively thick [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md) layer outside the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md), without the [bacterial outer membrane](bacterial-outer-membrane.md) of typical [Gram-negative bacteria](gram-negative-bacteria.md). This is a representative [cell envelope](cell-envelope.md) pattern, not a complete description of every member of [bacteria](bacteria.md).
