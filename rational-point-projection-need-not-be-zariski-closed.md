# Rational-point projection need not be Zariski closed

↑ **Parent:** [Closedness of projection from projective space](closedness-of-projection-from-projective-space.md)

Over the real numbers, the closed equation $y_0^2=t y_1^2$ in $\mathbb A^1\times\mathbb P^1$ projects on real points to $\{t\ge0\}$, which is not [Zariski closed](zariski-closed-set.md). Projective properness concerns scheme or geometric images, not the image of rational points over an arbitrary field. Similarly, $y_0^2+y_1^2$ has no real projective zero, but its [homogeneous ideal](homogeneous-ideal.md) contains no power of the [irrelevant ideal of projective space](irrelevant-ideal-of-projective-space.md), since it has a complex projective zero.
