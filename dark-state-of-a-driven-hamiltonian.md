# Dark state of a driven Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md)

Such a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) remains an [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) of $H_0+f(t)H_1$ for every [control input](control-input.md). Its projector commutes with all available generators, so its population is conserved. This obstructs full-space [pure-state controllability](pure-state-controllability.md) even when the orthogonal bright subspace can be controlled. An instantaneous dark state of a slowly varying [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) instead supports [adiabatic quantum control](adiabatic-quantum-control.md) if a [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) and slowness condition are maintained.
