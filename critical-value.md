# Critical value

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point of a smooth map](critical-point-of-a-smooth-map.md)

A [critical value](critical-value.md) is the image of at least one [critical point of a smooth map](critical-point-of-a-smooth-map.md). A [regular value](regular-value.md) has no critical point in its fiber. Values outside the image are regular vacuously.
