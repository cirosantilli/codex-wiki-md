# Synthetic zero-coupon bond

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md)

For a non-dividend-paying [stock](stock.md), buy $1/F_t^T$ shares and short a [forward contract](forward-contract.md) for the same number of shares at its fair delivery price $F_t^T>0$. The combined payoff is one at $T$, and the initial cost is $S_t/F_t^T$. This replicates a unit [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) without initially assuming an independently traded bank account. A single maturity quote determines that discount factor; it does not determine the entire future short-rate process.
