# Photopigment

↑ **Parent:** [Photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md)

A light-absorbing pigment involved in a receptor response. [Spectral sensitivity](spectral-sensitivity.md) depends on pigment [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) together with factors such as optical filtering and pigment density; a pigment name does not specify a perceived [color](color.md).
