# Emulsion stability versus molecular miscibility

↑ **Parent:** [Emulsion](emulsion.md)

A surfactant-stabilized dispersion can remain macroscopically uniform while its two liquids remain distinct phases. Lower [interfacial tension](interfacial-tension.md) reduces the [work](work.md) of making droplets, and repulsive interfacial films can slow [droplet coalescence](droplet-coalescence.md). Kinetic [emulsion](emulsion.md) stability does not by itself establish [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) molecular miscibility or a thermodynamically stable [microemulsion](microemulsion.md).
