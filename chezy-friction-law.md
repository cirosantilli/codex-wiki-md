<h1 id="chezy-friction-law">Chézy friction law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

The [Chézy law](chezy-friction-law.md) relates mean open-channel [speed](speed.md) to [hydraulic radius](hydraulic-radius.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) slope through a [friction](friction.md) coefficient. Equivalently one can use bed shear $\tau_b=\rho f u|u|$; for positive uniform flow, balance $\rho gAI=\tau_bP_b$ gives $u=\sqrt{gI/f}\sqrt{A/P_b}$. The absolute value makes drag oppose motion if reverse flows are admitted.
