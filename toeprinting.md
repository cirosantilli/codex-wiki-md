# Toeprinting

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[Toeprinting](toeprinting.md) maps a reverse-transcription stop caused by an assembled ribosome or initiation complex on RNA. It can report occupancy near a translation start site, but controls are needed for stops caused by RNA structure or other bound proteins.
