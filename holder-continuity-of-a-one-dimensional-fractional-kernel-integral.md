<h1 id="holder-continuity-of-a-one-dimensional-fractional-kernel-integral">Hölder continuity of a one-dimensional fractional kernel integral</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder condition](holder-condition.md)

For $f\in L^2(0,1)$ and $0<\alpha<1/2$, the integral $g(x)=\int_0^1f(y)|x-y|^{-\alpha}\,dy$ is well defined for every real $x$. Scaling the squared kernel difference over the real line and using the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) give $|g(x+h)-g(x)|\leq C_\alpha\|f\|_2|h|^{1/2-\alpha}$.
