# Graph power

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

The $j$th power of a [graph](graph-split.md) joins distinct [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) whenever their distance in the original [graph](graph-split.md) is at most $j$. For example, the [square of a cycle](square-of-a-cycle.md) adds [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) connecting [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) two steps apart. Graph powers turn constraints on short paths into adjacency constraints.
