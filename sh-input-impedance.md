# SH input impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Shear-horizontal wave](shear-horizontal-wave.md)

The [SH input impedance](sh-input-impedance.md) is the negative ratio of vertical [traction](traction.md) to particle [velocity](velocity.md) for a [shear-horizontal wave](shear-horizontal-wave.md) field, with positive depth and tension-positive [traction](traction.md). A superposition of upward and downward waves generally has a depth-dependent complex ratio. A downward progressive wave in a uniform medium has $Z=\mu p_\beta$, while the upward wave has $Z=-\mu p_\beta$. The input ratio includes reflection from deeper structure and can have poles at [velocity](velocity.md) nodes.
