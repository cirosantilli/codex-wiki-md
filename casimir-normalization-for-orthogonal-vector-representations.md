# Casimir normalization for orthogonal vector representations

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md)

For the defining [representation](group-representation.md) of the [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md), Hermitian [Lie algebra generators](lie-algebra-generator.md) $t_{ij}=i(E_{ij}-E_{ji})/\sqrt2$ have [trace](matrix-trace.md) normalization $\operatorname{tr}(t_{ij}t_{kl})=\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}$ for ordered pairs. Summing their squares gives $\sum_{i<j}t_{ij}^2=(N-1)I/2$. Consequently the [Casimir eigenvalue](casimir-eigenvalue.md) is $(N-1)/2$ and the [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md) is one. Doubling the trace normalization doubles both numbers; a [gauge coupling](gauge-coupling.md) must be rescaled inversely with the [Lie algebra generators](lie-algebra-generator.md). With long [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) of squared length two, the first convention is $C_2(\lambda)=(\lambda,\lambda+2\rho)/2$.
