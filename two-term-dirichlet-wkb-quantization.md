# Two-term Dirichlet WKB quantization

↑ **Parent:** [WKB quantization condition](wkb-quantization-condition.md)

For smooth positive $K$ on an interval with regular Dirichlet [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), there is no turning-point phase shift. Combining conjugate [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) branches to vanish at the first endpoint makes the total phase at the second an integer multiple of $\pi$. The first correction is the integrated [WKB phase correction for a slowly varying oscillator](wkb-phase-correction-for-a-slowly-varying-oscillator.md). For $K=x+1$ on $[0,1]$ the resulting [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $4\pi^2n^2/9-3/16+O(n^{-2})$.
