# Material volume

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid element](fluid-element.md)

A [material volume](material-volume.md) is a region containing the same fluid particles at every time: its boundary moves with the fluid [velocity field](velocity-field.md). Its [mass](mass.md) is fixed by [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md), while its [volume](volume.md) can change in compressible motion. Applying the [Reynolds transport theorem](reynolds-transport-theorem.md) to its [volume](volume.md) gives $d|V(t)|/dt=\int_{V(t)}\nabla\cdot\mathbf u\,dV$. A [material volume element](material-volume-element.md) is the infinitesimal version.
