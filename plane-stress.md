# Plane stress

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

A [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) state with all traction components involving one normal direction zero. In an isotropic solid, its two remaining normal stresses obey $\tau_x=E(e_x+\nu e_y)/(1-\nu^2)$ and $\tau_y=E(e_y+\nu e_x)/(1-\nu^2)$. The out-of-plane [strain](strain.md) need not vanish: $e_z=-\nu(\tau_x+\tau_y)/E$. Constraining the out-of-plane deformation instead would be a different boundary condition.
