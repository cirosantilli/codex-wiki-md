# Vizing fan lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Vizing's theorem](vizing-s-theorem.md)

In a partial proper edge colouring with one uncoloured edge incident with a vertex $x$, a Vizing fan is a sequence of neighbours of $x$ whose incident edge colours are missing at earlier fan vertices. With one more colour than the maximum degree, fan rotations and a two-colour [Kempe chain](kempe-chain.md) free one common colour at the endpoints of the uncoloured edge.
