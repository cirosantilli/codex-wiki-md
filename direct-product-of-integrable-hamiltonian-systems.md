# Direct product of integrable Hamiltonian systems

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

The direct product of integrable Hamiltonian systems is integrable. Pull each family's commuting [integrals of motion](integral-of-motion.md) back to the product [phase space](phase-space.md). Integrals from different factors have zero [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) because they use disjoint canonical coordinates, and their [differentials](total-differential.md) are independent wherever the differentials within each factor are independent.
