# Ramification of the splitting field of X3 plus pX plus p

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

For an odd prime $p$, this [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) generates a totally ramified cubic root field. Its [discriminant](discriminant.md) is $-p^2(4p+27)$. For $p>3$ the unit part is a square exactly when $p\equiv1\pmod3$. The [splitting field](splitting-field.md) then has cyclic [Galois group](galois-group.md) of order three; otherwise it has [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_3$, with an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of degree two and [inertia group](inertia-group.md) $C_3$. Both cases are [tamely ramified](tamely-ramified-extension.md), so all positive [ramification groups](ramification-group.md) are trivial.

At $p=3$, adjoining $\beta=\sqrt{-39}$ gives a ramified quadratic subfield, and the [splitting field](splitting-field.md) has total [ramification index](ramification-index.md) six. A root $\alpha$ has normalized [valuation](valuation.md) two, while $\beta$ has [valuation](valuation.md) three, so $\pi=\beta/\alpha$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). The derivative $3\alpha^2+3$ has [valuation](valuation.md) six, and all distinct root differences have [valuation](valuation.md) three. A three-cycle fixes $\beta$, giving $v_L(\sigma\pi-\pi)=2$. A transposition fixing $\alpha$ negates $\beta$, giving [valuation](valuation.md) one; conjugation handles the other transpositions. Hence $G_0=S_3$, $G_1=C_3$, and $G_i=1$ for $i\ge2$. The [different exponent](different-exponent.md) is seven.
