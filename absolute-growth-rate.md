# Absolute growth rate

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md)

The absolute growth rate is the exponential [growth rate](growth-rate.md) of a localized impulse at a fixed position in the chosen observation frame. Under the convention $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$, it is the [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) of the physically selected [absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md). Positive values give [absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md); negative values can still coexist with [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md).
