# Pressure coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

An atmospheric vertical coordinate using [pressure](pressure-split.md) instead of geometric height. Under [hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) decreases upward and [geopotential](geopotential.md) obeys $\partial_p\Phi=-1/\rho$. With the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md), $\partial_{\ln p}\Phi=-RT$. Horizontal [gradients](gradient.md) taken at fixed $p$ lie on an [isobaric surface](isobaric-surface.md). A [log-pressure coordinate](log-pressure-coordinate.md) is a useful related coordinate with the opposite upward sign.
