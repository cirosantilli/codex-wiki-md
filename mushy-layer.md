# Mushy layer

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

A mushy layer contains solid crystals and liquid in local phase equilibrium, rather than a single sharp [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md). Its [solid fraction](solid-fraction.md) changes as heat and solute are transported. A concentration-dependent [liquidus](liquidus.md) couples temperature to liquid composition; a connected crystal framework makes the residual liquid behave as a [porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md).
