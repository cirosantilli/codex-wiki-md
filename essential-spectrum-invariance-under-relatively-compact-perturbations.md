# Essential-spectrum invariance under relatively compact perturbations

↑ **Parent:** [Essential spectrum of a closed operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md)

Let $A$ be a [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md) and let $V:D(A)\to H$ be [compact](compact-space.md) when the domain carries its [graph norm](graph-norm.md). If $A+V$ is closed on the same domain, [compact perturbation invariance of Fredholm operators](compact-perturbation-invariance-of-fredholm-operators.md) applied to the maps $D(A)\to H$ proves invariance of the Fredholm [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md). For self-adjoint operators this is also the [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md) obtained by deleting isolated finite-multiplicity [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). A bounded potential tending to zero at spatial infinity is relatively compact with respect to a second-order constant-coefficient operator: local [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) gives compactness on bounded intervals and the small tails give compactness globally.
