# Steady quasi-geostrophic response to localized heating

↑ **Parent:** [Diabatically forced quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](diabatically-forced-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

In the weak-forcing steady response about rest, [diabatically forced quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](diabatically-forced-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) gives $\beta\psi_x=(f_0/N^2)R_z$. For $\beta\ne0$, an eastern zero-flow condition fixes the horizontal circulation through $\psi=-(f_0/\beta N^2)\int_x^\infty R_z\,dX$, up to a horizontally uniform pressure gauge. The thermodynamic equation gives $w=R/N^2$. A western zonal wake can remain even though the heating and vertical velocity vanish there. This is a formal steady interior solution: specified initial boundary buoyancy can prevent it from being the attained steady state. For example, under a rigid lid where the heating vanishes, initially uniform boundary buoyancy remains uniform at linear order.
