# Thurston norm

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston_norm)

For a compact oriented [three-manifold](3-manifold.md), the integral relative class $u$ has norm the minimum of $\chi_-(F)=\sum_j\max(0,-\chi(F_j))$ over properly embedded oriented [topological surfaces](topological-surface.md) representing $u$. Homogeneity and continuity extend it to real [relative homology](relative-homology.md). It is a [seminorm](seminorm.md), since spheres, disks, [annuli](annulus-mathematics.md) and [tori](torus.md) have zero cost.
