# Stirred-condensate vortex experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

Rotating a trap anisotropy can nucleate vortices in an atomic condensate. Expansion followed by [absorption imaging](absorption-imaging.md) resolves depleted cores, while varying rotation and waiting after removing the stirrer tests their preparation and persistence. The interpretation is quantized rotational response, rather than only the appearance of a condensed density profile.
