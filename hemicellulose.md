# Hemicellulose

↑ **Parent:** [Polysaccharide](polysaccharide.md)

[hemicellulose](hemicellulose.md) denotes a group of plant-wall polysaccharides distinct from [cellulose](cellulose.md). Their association with [cellulose](cellulose.md) can limit access, and complementary degrading enzymes can improve deconstruction.
