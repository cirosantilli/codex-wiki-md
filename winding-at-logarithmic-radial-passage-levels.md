# Winding at logarithmic radial passage levels

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic radius of planar Brownian motion](logarithmic-radius-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started at $1$, continuous winding angles sampled at the displayed radii have independent stationary increments. At $T_r$, apply the [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md), rotate by the current angle, and rescale space by $e^r$ and time by $e^{-2r}$. The future radial-level winding increment has the original law and is independent of the past. The logarithmic radius and winding angle are independent Brownian coordinates on the common clock $\int|B_s|^{-2}ds$. At radial passage they are therefore $U_{\eta_r}=-r$ and $V_{\eta_r}$, where $\eta_r$ is a [Brownian first-passage time](brownian-first-passage-time.md). Its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) gives the displayed characteristic function. The level process is a symmetric [Cauchy process](cauchy-process.md); replacing the passage clock by its right-continuous version supplies a [càdlàg](cadlag.md) modification without changing any fixed-level law.
