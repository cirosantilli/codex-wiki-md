# Apparent-horizon area comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose inequality](penrose-inequality.md)

The heuristic derivation of the [Penrose inequality](penrose-inequality.md) from [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md) needs an initial area comparison, in addition to enclosure of the [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) by the [event horizon](event-horizon.md). Set inclusion alone is not an area inequality. An [outer area-minimizing surface](outer-area-minimizing-surface.md) or appropriate enclosing-area hypotheses supply the needed comparison in formulations where it is valid; arbitrary slice-dependent apparent-horizon area need not do so.
