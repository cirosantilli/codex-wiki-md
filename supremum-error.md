# Supremum error

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md)

For [functions](function-split.md) taking values in [real numbers](real-number.md) on a nonempty [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) $X$, the [supremum error](supremum-error.md) is the [supremum](supremum.md) of the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of the approximation error over all inputs. It may be infinite and need not be attained. When finite, it is the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of $f_n-f$; [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) is exactly $E_n\to0$. Bounding this one quantity gives an accuracy guarantee for every input, including inputs that change with $n$.
