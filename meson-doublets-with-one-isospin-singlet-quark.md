# Meson doublets with one isospin-singlet quark

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

An isospin-zero [quark](quark.md) $h$ paired with $\overline u$ or $\overline d$ gives a [meson](meson.md) [isospin](isospin.md) doublet of charges $q_h-2/3,q_h+1/3$. Spin addition at zero [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) gives both [pseudoscalar mesons](pseudoscalar-meson.md) and [vector mesons](vector-meson.md). Pairing the new [antiquark](antiquark.md) with $u,d$ gives the charge-conjugate doublets. For strange or charm flavour these are the kaon or D-meson families and their vector partners.
