# Hypergraph chromatic index

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph colouring](hypergraph-colouring.md)

The chromatic index of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) is the minimum number of colours needed to colour its edges so that intersecting edges have different colours. Each colour class is a [matching in a hypergraph](matching-in-a-hypergraph.md). It is at least the maximum [hypergraph vertex degree](hypergraph-vertex-degree.md), and at most $r(\Delta-1)+1$ for an $r$-uniform [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) of maximum degree $\Delta$, by greedily colouring edges: an edge meets at most $r(\Delta-1)$ others.
