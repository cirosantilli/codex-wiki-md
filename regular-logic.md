# Regular logic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

Regular logic has atomic formulas and equality, truth, finite conjunction and unrestricted existential quantification. Its categorical semantics uses [regular categories](regular-category.md): conjunction is intersection and existential quantification is image. Disjunction and falsity are not added as formula constructors in the regular fragment.
