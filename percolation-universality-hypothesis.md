# Percolation universality hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Universality class](universality-class.md)

Ordinary short-range independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) and [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) on regular lattices of the same spatial dimension are expected to share their [percolation critical exponents](percolation-critical-exponents.md) and large-scale limiting behavior. Their [percolation critical probabilities](percolation-critical-probability.md) can differ. Long-range or correlated models can lie in a different [universality class](universality-class.md). A theorem for one lattice alone does not establish this transfer.
