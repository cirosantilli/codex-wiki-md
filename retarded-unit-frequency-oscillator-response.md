# Retarded unit-frequency oscillator response

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md)

The [retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md) for $d^2/dt^2+1$ is $G_R(t)=\Theta(t)\sin t$, with [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $[1-(\omega+i0^+)^2]^{-1}$ in the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention. Both poles lie below the real axis. Convolution with a force gives the solution with no incoming homogeneous oscillation. A source that is nonzero at negative times can have a nonzero response before a chosen zero of time without violating [causality](causality.md).
