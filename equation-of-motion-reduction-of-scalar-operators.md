# Equation-of-motion reduction of scalar operators

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension-six operators of a real scalar field](dimension-six-operators-of-a-real-scalar-field.md)

At first order in the suppression of an [effective operator](effective-operator.md), a factor of the leading [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) defines a [redundant operator](redundant-operator.md) removable by a local [field redefinition](field-redefinition.md). For $\mathcal L_0=(\partial\phi)^2/2-m^2\phi^2/2-\lambda\phi^4/24$, one obtains $\phi^3\Box\phi\doteq-m^2\phi^4-\lambda\phi^6/6$ and $(\Box\phi)^2\doteq m^4\phi^2+m^2\lambda\phi^4/3+\lambda^2\phi^6/36$. Lower-dimensional parameter shifts must accompany this reduction when $m\ne0$; it is not an identity for untransformed [off shell](off-shell.md) [correlation functions](correlation-function.md).
