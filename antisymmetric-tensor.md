# Antisymmetric tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisymmetric_tensor)

An [antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md) changes sign under exchange of two indices in a specified subset of its indices. A [totally antisymmetric tensor](totally-antisymmetric-tensor.md) has this property for every pair of indices.
