# Generalized angular momentum in a radial monopole field

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)

For a radial conservative force plus the magnetic-type force $\dot{\mathbf r}\times(\mu\mathbf r/r^3)$, the torque equals $\mu\,d\widehat{\mathbf r}/dt$. Subtracting $\mu\widehat{\mathbf r}$ from ordinary [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) therefore produces a constant vector. Its dot product with $\widehat{\mathbf r}$ is $-\mu$, so motion lies on a cone about that vector. At $\mu=0$, ordinary planar [central force](central-force.md) motion is recovered. This is the classical radial [magnetic monopole](magnetic-monopole.md) angular-momentum correction.
