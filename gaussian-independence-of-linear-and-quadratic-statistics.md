# Gaussian independence of linear and quadratic statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Uncorrelated jointly normal variables are independent](uncorrelated-jointly-normal-variables-are-independent.md)

When $L$ has full column rank, its [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $L^TL$ is [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) and $BLL^TL(L^TL)^{-1}=BL=0$. The stacked normal vector $(BY,L^TY)$ therefore has zero cross [covariance](covariance.md), so its blocks are independent. Since $Y^TAY=\|L^TY\|^2$, the linear and quadratic statistics are independent. This is the projection argument behind independence of [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) estimators in a [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md).
