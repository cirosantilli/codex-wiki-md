# Unique forbidden beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Forbidden beta decay](forbidden-beta-decay.md)

An $l$th-forbidden [beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md) with the maximal leading angular-momentum transfer $l+1$. Only the spin-dependent $S=1$ coupling can attain that transfer at this orbital order. Thus a $0^+\to2^-$ transition is first-forbidden unique, whereas a same-parity $\Delta J=2$ transition is second-forbidden nonunique. Unique refers to the leading weak multipole structure, not a claim that every correction to the decay vanishes.
