# Von Foerster equation

↑ **Parent:** [Age-structured population model](age-structured-population-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Foerster_equation)

The [Von Foerster equation](von-foerster-equation.md) transports a population density through age while mortality removes individuals. A birth boundary condition at age zero closes the model; for a prescribed fertility rate it has the form $n(0,t)=\int b(a,t)n(a,t)\,da$.
