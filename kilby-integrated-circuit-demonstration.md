# Kilby integrated circuit demonstration

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md)

In his 2000 lecture, [Jack St. Clair Kilby](jack-st-clair-kilby.md) describes an August 1958 comparison circuit made from separate [silicon](silicon.md) elements. [Resistors](resistor.md) were cut and etched; [capacitors](capacitor.md) came from diffused, metallized [semiconductor wafers](semiconductor-wafer.md). It tested whether [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) elements could perform all the required functions, but was still a [discrete circuit](discrete-circuit.md). For the September prototype he used already diffused [germanium](germanium.md) material with contacts, cut into bars, and hand-applied wax masks to define a [transistor](transistor.md) and a distributed [resistor](resistor.md)-[capacitor](capacitor.md) region. Applying supply [power](power.md) produced an oscillation.[Kilby's 2000 Nobel lecture, ChemPhysChem reprint (2001), pp. 486–487, separate-element comparison and oscillator preparation](turning-potential-into-realities-the-invention-of-the-integrated-circuit-nobel-lecture-by-jack-st-clair-kilby.md)

An inspected eyewitness interview describes the [germanium](germanium.md) on a microscope slide with protruding wires; changing its connections changed the [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) display from a straight line to a [sinusoid](sinusoid.md). This supplied a qualitative connected/disconnected comparison, not a calibrated record of [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), loading or repeatability.[Oral History of Jay W. Lathrop, recorded 7 May 2009, p. 11, demonstration and oscilloscope comparison](oral-history-of-jay-w-lathrop.md)

The inference was feasibility: integrated [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) elements could cooperate as an operating [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md). The hand-wired [germanium](germanium.md) prototype did not yet supply the deposited [integrated circuit interconnects](integrated-circuit-interconnect.md) of [Robert Noyce](robert-noyce.md)'s [silicon](silicon.md) design. Nor does a working prototype establish manufacturing yield or long-term reliability.
