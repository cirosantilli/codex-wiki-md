# Compact-metric representation by Cantor-space pullback

↑ **Parent:** [Cantor-space representation of positive functionals](cantor-space-representation-of-positive-functionals.md)

Given a continuous surjection $h:\Omega\to K$ from [Cantor space](cantor-space.md) to a nonempty [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md), the pullback $f\mapsto f\circ h$ is a unital [isometric embedding](isometric-embedding.md) of $C(K)$ into $C(\Omega)$. Transport a normalized [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) to its image, take a [positive extension from a unital subspace of C(K)](positive-extension-from-a-unital-subspace-of-c-k.md), and represent that extension on Cantor space. The [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) $h_*P$ then represents the original functional.
