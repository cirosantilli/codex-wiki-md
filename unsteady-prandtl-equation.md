# Unsteady Prandtl equation

↑ **Parent:** [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md)

The unsteady form of the [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md) evolves the tangential boundary-layer [velocity](velocity.md) under advection, outer pressure forcing and normal viscous diffusion. Incompressibility supplies the normal [velocity](velocity.md). A stationary wall imposes the [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md), and tangential [velocity](velocity.md) matches the prescribed outer slip [velocity](velocity.md) far from the wall. The normal far-field disturbance need not vanish.
