# Cn Dynkin diagrams

↑ **Parent:** [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md)

For $n\ge2$, the finite [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) has a chain of [short roots](short-root.md) $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_{n-1}$ with single bonds, followed by a double bond to the [long root](long-root.md) $\alpha_n$, pointing toward $\alpha_{n-1}$. The [Extended Dynkin diagram](extended-dynkin-diagram.md) adds the long root $\alpha_0=-2\varepsilon_1$, doubly bonded to $\alpha_1$ with arrow toward $\alpha_1$. All other bonds remain. For $n=1$, the finite diagram is a single vertex; the affine diagram has two equal-length vertices joined by a double bond without an arrow, with [Cartan matrix](cartan-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-2&2\end{pmatrix}$.
