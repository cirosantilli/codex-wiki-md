# Trauma reminder

↑ **Parent:** [Reexperiencing](reexperiencing.md)

A cue associated with a traumatic experience that can prompt [emotion](emotion.md) or recollection. Its relation may be perceptual, situational or semantic, and need not be consciously recognised at the moment of response.
