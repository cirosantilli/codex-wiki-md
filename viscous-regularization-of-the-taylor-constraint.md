# Viscous regularization of the Taylor constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor constraint](taylor-constraint.md)

With small viscosity, the azimuthal cylindrical balance permits [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) torque to be cancelled by viscous stress. For ordinary no-slip rotating boundaries an [Ekman layer](ekman-layer.md) gives stress of order $\rho\Omega LU\sqrt E$, larger than the order-$E$ bulk viscous correction at fixed velocity. Thus bounded small-$E$ flows approach a [magnetostrophic Taylor state](magnetostrophic-taylor-state.md) but need not satisfy its constraint exactly at finite viscosity. An order-one uncompensated magnetic torque would require a divergent flow as $E\to0$, invalidating the assumed small-inertia balance. Stress-free boundaries remove the leading no-slip friction and require their own torque/regularity conditions.
