# Representation over the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A [representation over the rational numbers](representation-over-the-rational-numbers.md) is a [group representation](group-representation.md) on a finite-dimensional rational [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Choosing a basis gives rational matrices. It differs from a [rational representation of an algebraic group](rational-representation.md), where rational refers to regular matrix-coefficient functions rather than the ground [field](field.md). Scalar extension yields [representations](group-representation.md) over every characteristic-zero [field](field.md).
