# Volterra iteration for a singular power-series problem

↑ **Parent:** [Volterra integral equation](volterra-integral-equation.md)

The iteration $y_0=x$, $y_n=x-\int_0^x(x-s)y_{n-1}(s)/s\,ds$ successively appends terms of the displayed series. Induction verifies the iterates, and factorial denominator bounds give convergence with derivatives on compact sets. Its limit is the [power series](power-series.md) solution of $xy''+y=0$, $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=1$.
