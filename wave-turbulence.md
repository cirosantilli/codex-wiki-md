# Wave turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_turbulence)

Wave turbulence describes the statistical transfer of energy between interacting [waves](wave.md). The [wave period](wave-period.md), nonlinear interaction time, phase correlations and resonances determine the transfer rate. A [weak wave cascade](weak-wave-cascade.md) treats the change in one interaction as small; [critical balance](critical-balance.md) instead makes the nonlinear interaction rate comparable to the linear wave frequency.
