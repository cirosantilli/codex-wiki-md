# Artinian decomposition into local factors

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md)

Every nonzero commutative [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md) is a finite product of [Artinian local rings](artinian-local-ring.md). The primary components of zero have distinct maximal radicals and are pairwise [comaximal ideals](comaximal-ideals.md), so the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives the product. Each local factor has nilpotent maximal ideal and finite [composition length](composition-length.md). These factors and the components of zero are unique.
