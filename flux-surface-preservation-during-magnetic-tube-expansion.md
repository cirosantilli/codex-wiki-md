# Flux-surface preservation during magnetic-tube expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md)

Suppose the [Cartesian magnetic flux function](cartesian-magnetic-flux-function.md) is advected, the initial axial velocity is zero, $B_y=f(\psi)$ initially, and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=g(\psi,t)$. Then $B_y=f(\psi)\exp[-\int_0^t g(\psi,s)\,ds]$ remains a function of the flux label and time. The axial [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) vanishes because its transverse derivative along a [magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md) vanishes. Smooth ideal evolution therefore preserves zero axial motion.
