# Molecular dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

[molecular dynamics](molecular-dynamics.md) simulates time evolution of a molecular system using a chosen interaction model. Simulated pathways and state populations require adequate sampling and validation against measured observables; a plausible trajectory alone does not establish a biological mechanism.
