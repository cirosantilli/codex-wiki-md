# Valuative criterion for separatedness

↑ **Parent:** [Separated morphism](separated-morphism.md)

For a finite-type morphism of [Noetherian schemes](noetherian-scheme.md), separatedness is equivalent to uniqueness in every lifting problem $\operatorname{Spec}K\to X$ over $\operatorname{Spec}R\to Y$, where $R$ is a [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$.

This is the uniqueness part of the [valuative criterion](valuative-criterion.md).
