# Oxidative addition

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

In a standard [oxidative addition](oxidative-addition.md), a bond X–Y is cleaved and both fragments become [metal](metal.md) ligands, increasing formal [metal](metal.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) by two. A B–Br bond can give a [metal](metal.md)–boryl bond and a [bromide ligand](bromide-ligand.md) without cleaving a [borazine](borazine.md) ring.
