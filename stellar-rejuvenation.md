# Stellar rejuvenation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

Stellar rejuvenation occurs when mixing brings fresh hydrogen into the core of a mass-gaining or merged [main sequence](main-sequence.md) star, making its remaining nuclear lifetime resemble that of a younger star. The amount of rejuvenation depends on the accretor's internal mixing and prior composition.
