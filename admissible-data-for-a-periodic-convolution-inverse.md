# Admissible data for a periodic convolution inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic convolution operator](periodic-convolution-operator.md)

When all multipliers are nonzero, the [Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator](moore-penrose-inverse-of-an-operator.md) acts by dividing each [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) $y_n$ by $c_n$. Its data domain is precisely the square-summability condition displayed. The dense range need not be closed, and writing the formal inverse series does not establish the [Picard criterion](picard-criterion.md).
