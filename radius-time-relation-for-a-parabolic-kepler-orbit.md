# Radius-time relation for a parabolic Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Barker equation](barker-equation.md)

On a nonradial zero-[energy](energy.md) [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), conserved [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h$ gives periapsis $r_p=h^2/(2GM)$ and $\dot r^2=2GM(r-r_p)/r^2$. Integrating with $s=\sqrt{r-r_p}$ yields $t-t_p=\pm\sqrt{2/(GM)}(r+2r_p)\sqrt{r-r_p}/3$. Squaring gives the displayed version of the [Barker equation](barker-equation.md), valid on both incoming and outgoing branches. The time origin $t_p$ is passage through [periapsis](periapsis.md), not an arbitrary point on the orbit.
