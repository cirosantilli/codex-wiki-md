# Latent image

↑ **Parent:** [Photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_image)

A [latent image](latent-image.md) is the pattern of exposure-induced changes in a [photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md) before [photographic processing](photographic-processing.md) makes it visible. A missing visible mark before development therefore says nothing about whether a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) passed through the coating.
