# Photolytic branching in the chlorine dimer cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Chlorine monoxide dimer cycle](chlorine-monoxide-dimer-cycle.md)

For dimer [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) frequency $J$ and competing [thermal dissociation](thermal-dissociation.md) frequency $k_d[M]$, the successful branch fraction is $f=J/(J+k_d[M])$. With steady dimer abundance and rapid subsequent recycling, [ozone](ozone.md) destruction is $2f k_a[M][\mathrm{ClO}]^2$. The factor two counts [ozone](ozone.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) consumed per productive dimer event.
