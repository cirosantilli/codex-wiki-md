# Continuous random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

A [random variable](random-variable-split.md) whose [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is continuous, equivalently whose [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) assigns zero [probability](probability.md) to every singleton. In elementary applications, one usually additionally assumes a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md); that stronger property means its law is an [absolutely continuous probability distribution](absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md). A continuous [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) alone need not admit a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md).
