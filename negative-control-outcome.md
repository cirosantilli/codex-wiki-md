# Negative control outcome

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

A negative control outcome cannot plausibly be caused by treatment, so an estimated effect can reveal bias.

Unlike treatment comparison alone, this [scientific control](scientific-control.md) checks for an association that should vanish under the intended causal model.
