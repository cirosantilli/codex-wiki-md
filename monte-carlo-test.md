# Monte Carlo test

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md)

Generate independent replicated datasets under a fully specified [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) and calculate the same [predictive discrepancy statistic](predictive-discrepancy-statistic.md) for them and the observation. Their null exchangeability gives a finite-simulation rank test. The displayed add-one tail probability is valid with conservative treatment of ties, and avoids reporting zero solely because no replicate exceeds the observation.
