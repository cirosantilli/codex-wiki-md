# Divisorial ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md)

For a [normal variety](normal-variety.md) with affine [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$ and a [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md) $D$, the fractional ideal of functions whose principal [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md) is at least $D$ is

$$
I_D=\{q\in\operatorname{Frac}(A):\operatorname{ord}_P(q)\ge\operatorname{coeff}_P D\text{ for all prime divisors }P\}\cup\{0\}.
$$

For an effective prime divisor this is its height-one [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). If the divisor is Cartier locally, this ideal is locally principal. A nonprincipal height-one [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) in a normal [local ring](local-ring.md) can therefore detect a non-Cartier divisor.
