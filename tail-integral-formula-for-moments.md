# Tail integral formula for moments

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value.md)

For $q>0$ and a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ with finite absolute $q$th moment, [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E|X|^q=q\int_0^\infty t^{q-1}\mathbb P(|X|\geq t)\,dt.
$$
