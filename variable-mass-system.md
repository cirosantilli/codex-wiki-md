# Variable-mass system

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable-mass_system)

A body exchanging [mass](mass.md) with material of inertial [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf u$ satisfies the displayed [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) law for one stream, with signed $\dot m$ positive for entry and negative for exit. The [force](force.md) $\mathbf F$ excludes the incoming or outgoing momentum flux. A short time interval gives $d(m\mathbf v)/dt=\mathbf F+\mathbf u\dot m$, proving the formula. For stationary incoming matter, $d(m\mathbf v)/dt=\mathbf F$; for matter leaving at the body's own [velocity](velocity.md), $m\dot{\mathbf v}=\mathbf F$. The [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) and accreting-drop motion are applications.
