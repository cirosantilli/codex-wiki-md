<h1 id="pushforward-of-a-poincare-inequality-by-a-lipschitz-function">Pushforward of a Poincaré inequality by a Lipschitz function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality in probability theory](poincare-inequality-in-probability-theory.md)

If $\mu$ has Poincaré constant $C$ and $\phi$ is $L$-Lipschitz, then the [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) $\phi_*\mu$ has Poincaré constant at most $CL^2$. Apply the original inequality to $g\circ\phi$ and use the [chain rule](chain-rule.md).
