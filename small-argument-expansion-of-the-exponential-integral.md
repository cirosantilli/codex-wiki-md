# Small-argument expansion of the exponential integral

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential integral](exponential-integral.md)

As $x\downarrow0$, the [exponential integral](exponential-integral.md) has

$$
E_1(x)=-\gamma-\log x+x-\frac{x^2}4+O(x^3),
$$

where $\gamma$ is the [Euler--Mascheroni constant](euler-s-constant.md). Integrating the [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) of $E_1'(x)=-e^{-x}/x$ gives every nonconstant coefficient; the constant follows from the limiting definition of $\gamma$. A logarithm of a stretched variable can create a [switchback term](switchback-term.md) in a [matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md).
