# Black-hole reflection spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole spin measurement](black-hole-spin-measurement.md)

An inference from [radiation](radiation.md) reflected by an irradiated [accretion disc](accretion-disk.md), including iron-line and continuum features. [Doppler effects](doppler-effect.md), [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) and ray propagation determine its profile. Associating the innermost reflecting [radius](radius.md) with the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) can constrain spin, conditional on illumination, ionization, abundance, inclination and disk structure.
