# Glycosyloxocarbenium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Oxocarbenium ion](oxocarbenium-ion.md)

An [oxocarbenium ion](oxocarbenium-ion.md) at the [anomeric carbon](anomeric-carbon.md) of a cyclic glycosyl fragment. Positive charge is delocalized between that [carbon](carbon.md) and the ring [oxygen](oxygen.md). A counterion can remain nearby and influence which face a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) approaches.
