# Volume-coupling relation for M-theory on K3

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory K3 duality](m-theory-k3-duality.md)

A wrapped [M5-brane](m5-brane.md) fixes the heterotic [string tension](string-tension.md) proportional to $V/\ell_p^6$. Reduction of the gravitational coupling gives $G_7\propto\ell_p^9/V$. Therefore $G_7/\ell_{s,H}^5\propto(V/\ell_p^4)^{3/2}$, yielding the displayed dimensionless coupling relation. This derivation separates the K3 volume modulus from its shape [Narain moduli space](narain-moduli-space.md).
