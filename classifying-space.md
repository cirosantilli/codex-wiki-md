# Classifying space

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classifying_space)

For a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) $G$, a universal [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) $EG\to BG$ has contractible total space and classifies numerable principal $G$-bundles by pullback. Over a paracompact base, isomorphism classes correspond to [homotopy classes](homotopy-class.md) of maps to $BG$. For a discrete group, its [classifying space of a discrete group](classifying-space-of-a-discrete-group.md) is a $K(G,1)$; for the circle, $BS^1\simeq\mathbb{CP}^\infty$.
