# Frictional electricity

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

Electrical charging associated historically with rubbing materials. It supplies ordinary [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), not a separate electrical substance. Compared with a chemical source, a friction machine can have a very different voltage, charge delivery and internal impedance.
