# Minimum potential energy principle in elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic energy](elastic-energy.md)

Let a convex [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) have derivative equal to the actual [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). Under mixed displacement and [traction](traction.md) data, any equilibrated [stress](stress.md) $\sigma_0$ with the prescribed [body force](body-force.md) and boundary [tractions](traction.md) represents the external work. For any compatible trial [strain](strain.md) $\varepsilon'$, the convex supporting-plane inequality gives $W(\varepsilon')-W(\varepsilon)\ge\sigma:(\varepsilon'-\varepsilon)$. The integral of $(\sigma-\sigma_0):(\varepsilon'-\varepsilon)$ vanishes by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). Thus the actual [strain](strain.md) minimizes $\int(W-\sigma_0:\varepsilon)$.
