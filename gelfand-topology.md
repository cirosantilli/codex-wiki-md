# Gelfand topology

↑ **Parent:** [Character space of an algebra](character-space-of-an-algebra.md)

The Gelfand topology on $\Phi_A$ is the weak-star topology inherited from $A^*$, equivalently the coarsest topology making every map $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(a)$ continuous.

This is the topology on the character space used in the [Gelfand representation](gelfand-representation.md).
