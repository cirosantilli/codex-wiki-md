# Lie derivative of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

For a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) $f$ and a [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$, the Lie derivative is $\mathcal L_Xf=X(f)=df(X)$. It is the derivative of $f$ along the [local flow](local-flow.md) of $X$.
