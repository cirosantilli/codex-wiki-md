# Gaussian interaction energy of a trapped condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md)

For a normalized condensate orbital $\phi=\pi^{-3/4}a_0^{-3/2}e^{-r^2/(2a_0^2)}$ and $\psi=\sqrt N\phi$, the [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) functional is $E_I=(U_0/2)\int|\psi|^4d^3r$. The [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) gives $\int|\phi|^4d^3r=[(2\pi)^{3/2}a_0^3]^{-1}$. With $U_0=4\pi\hbar^2a/m$, this becomes $E_I/(N\hbar\omega)=Na/(\sqrt{2\pi}a_0)$. An exact number-state pair count replaces $N^2$ by $N(N-1)$.
