# Quadrant photodiode

↑ **Parent:** [Photodiode](photodiode.md)

A [photodiode](photodiode.md) divided into four independently read segments. Differences of opposite segment signals can encode a light distribution's displacement after [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md).
