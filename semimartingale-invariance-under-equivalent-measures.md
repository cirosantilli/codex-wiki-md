# Semimartingale invariance under equivalent measures

↑ **Parent:** [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md)

On a fixed finite horizon, equivalent probability measures have the same [semimartingales](semimartingale.md). For a strictly positive continuous [Radon-Nikodym density martingale](radon-nikodym-density-martingale.md) $Z$ and a continuous local martingale $L$ under the original measure, $L-\int Z^{-1}d[L,Z]$ is a local martingale under the new one, by the [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md). Thus the finite-variation part of a continuous [semimartingale decomposition](semimartingale-decomposition.md) changes by the opposite covariation correction.
