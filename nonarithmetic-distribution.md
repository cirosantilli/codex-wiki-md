# Nonarithmetic distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

A probability distribution is nonarithmetic when it is not concentrated on $d\mathbb Z$ for any $d>0$. Every law with a probability density is nonarithmetic. This hypothesis removes lattice oscillations from the [key renewal theorem](key-renewal-theorem.md).
