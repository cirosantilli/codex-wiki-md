# Quadratic Casimir operator

↑ **Parent:** [Casimir element](casimir-element.md)

For a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) with generators referred to an orthonormal invariant form, the sum $\sum_at_at_a$ commutes with every generator. Indeed, the [commutator](commutator.md) is proportional to a contraction of antisymmetric [structure constants of a Lie algebra](structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md) with the symmetric product $t_at_b+t_bt_a$. The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) makes it scalar in an irreducible complex representation; that scalar is its quadratic [Casimir eigenvalue](casimir-eigenvalue.md). With [trace](matrix-trace.md) normalization $\operatorname{tr}(t_at_b)=C(R)\delta_{ab}$, taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) gives $C(R)\dim\mathfrak g=C_2(R)\dim R$.
