# Affine line with doubled origin

↑ **Parent:** [Affine line](affine-line.md)

Glue two copies of the [affine line](affine-line.md) over a base along the [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(t)$ by the identity. This [scheme](scheme.md) has two copies of the zero section and a canonical [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) to the original [affine line](affine-line.md). Over $\mathbb Z$, its [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) assigns to a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$ the pairs $(r,e)$ with $e$ an [idempotent](idempotent.md) in $R/(r)$. Indeed, $r$ gives the map to the [affine line](affine-line.md), while $e$ partitions $V(r)$ into the two [clopen sets](clopen-set.md) assigned to the zero sections. Complementing the opposite part gives two [open subsets](open-set.md) covering $\operatorname{Spec}R$, whose intersection is $D(r)$; the coordinate $r$ defines maps on them which glue. This also proves that no lifting of $e$ to an [idempotent](idempotent.md) of $R$ is necessary.
