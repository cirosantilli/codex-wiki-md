# Friction-induced instability of a collapsible-tube flow

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md)

Let $R(\alpha)\propto\alpha^{-n}$, with $n>0$, and use disturbances $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ about uniform [velocity](velocity.md) $U$. The intrinsic [frequency](frequency.md) is $\nu=\omega-kU$. Linearized [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and the decrease in friction as area grows give the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). For nonzero $k$ and positive flow, one branch grows exactly when $nU>c_0$. At $nU=c_0$, one branch is neutral, $\nu=kc_0$, while the other remains damped, $\nu=-kc_0-iR_0$. The maintained external-[pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) supplies the energy; positive friction alone does not imply linear stability of a driven flow.
