# Fourier decay of repeated interval convolutions

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

For the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) convention $\widehat f(\xi)=\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i\xi x}\,dx$, integration gives $\widehat{1_{[0,1]}}(\xi)=(1-e^{-2\pi i\xi})/(2\pi i\xi)$, with value one at zero. The [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md) makes the transform of the $m$-fold [convolution](convolution.md) its $m$th power. This is a simple way to construct compactly supported functions with prescribed polynomial Fourier decay.
