# Three-ball

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A three-ball is a [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) [homeomorphic](local-homeomorphism.md) to the closed unit [ball](ball-mathematics.md) in $\mathbb R^3$. Its [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) is a [two-sphere](two-sphere.md). Attaching a three-ball to a spherical boundary component is called capping that boundary; marked endpoints may be joined by a straight arc inside the cap.
