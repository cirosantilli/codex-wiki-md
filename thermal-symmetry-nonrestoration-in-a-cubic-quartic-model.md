# Thermal symmetry nonrestoration in a cubic-quartic model

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature-dependent cubic-quartic Landau free energy](temperature-dependent-cubic-quartic-landau-free-energy.md)

When $\gamma\geq1$ in the stated model, the broken, nonzero minimum remains globally preferred at every finite [temperature](temperature-split.md). A metastable zero state can still exist for $T>T_0$, but its lifetime and a [nucleation](nucleation.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) are kinetic questions. Its disappearance at the [spinodal point](spinodal-point.md) is not an equilibrium coexistence [temperature](temperature-split.md).
