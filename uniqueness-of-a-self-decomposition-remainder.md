# Uniqueness of a self-decomposition remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md)

Suppose $M_X$ is finite on a neighborhood of zero, $0<c<1$, and $X$ is independent of each of $R,R'$, with $cX+R$ and $cX+R'$ both having the [law of a random variable](probability-distribution-split.md) $X$. Independence and the [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) first give $M_X(t)=M_X(ct)M_R(t)$ as an extended nonnegative [integral](integral-split.md). Since $0<M_X(ct)<\infty$ and $M_X(t)<\infty$, both remainder [moment-generating functions](moment-generating-function.md) are finite and equal to the displayed ratio on the same neighborhood. The [uniqueness theorem for moment-generating functions](uniqueness-theorem-for-moment-generating-functions.md) gives equality of their [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md). No independence between $R$ and $R'$ is required.
