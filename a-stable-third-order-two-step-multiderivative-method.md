# A-stable third-order two-step multiderivative method

↑ **Parent:** [Multiderivative multistep method](multiderivative-multistep-method.md)

The endpoint expansion of this [multiderivative multistep method](multiderivative-multistep-method.md) has unscaled defect $h^4y^{(4)}/3+O(h^5)$ and [zero-stability](zero-stability.md) roots $1,1/7$, giving third-order convergence with suitably accurate starting values. On the [Dahlquist test equation](dahlquist-test-equation.md) its amplification roots obey $(7-6z+2z^2)\zeta^2-8\zeta+1=0$. The [complex quadratic Schur criterion](complex-quadratic-schur-criterion.md) puts both roots in the open unit disk for $\operatorname{Re}z<0$, and only the simple root one reaches the boundary at $z=0$. Thus it is [A-stable](a-stability.md); its higher derivative places it outside the hypotheses of the [Second Dahlquist barrier](second-dahlquist-barrier.md).
