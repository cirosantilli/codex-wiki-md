<h1 id="novikov-s-condition">Novikov's condition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Doléans-Dade exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novikov's_condition)

If a continuous local martingale $M$ satisfies

$$
\mathbb E\exp\!\left(\frac12[M]_T\right)<\infty,
$$

then its [stochastic exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md) is a true martingale through time $T$.
