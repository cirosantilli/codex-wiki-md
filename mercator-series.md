# Mercator series

↑ **Parent:** [Natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercator_series)

Integrating the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) for $1/(1+x)$ from zero to $x$ gives the displayed [power series](power-series.md) for $|x|<1$. It has [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) one. At $x=1$ the [alternating series test](alternating-series-test.md) gives [conditional convergence](conditional-convergence.md) to $\ln2$; at $x=-1$ the negative [harmonic series](harmonic-series.md) diverges. Replacing $x$ by $-w$ gives $\ln(1-w)=-\sum_{n\ge1}w^n/n$. An expression formed by substituting a nonlinear function for $w$ need not have the same convergence range after it is re-expanded as a [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) in the new variable.
