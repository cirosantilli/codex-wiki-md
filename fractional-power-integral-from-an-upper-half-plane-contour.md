# Fractional-power integral from an upper-half-plane contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

Take $a>0$ and define $z^s$ using $-\pi/2<\arg z<3\pi/2$, so the [branch cut](branch-cut.md) lies on the negative imaginary axis. An indented upper semicircle encloses the simple pole $ia$. Its two straight segments contribute $(1+e^{i\pi s})$ times the desired real integral, while the [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) gives $\pi a^{s-1}e^{i\pi s/2}$. The large arc vanishes as its radius to the power $s-1$, and the small arc as its radius to the power $s+1$, giving the stated convergence range and result. This contour avoids placing a branch cut on either straight integration segment.
