# Products of convex functions need not be convex

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

The [affine functions](affine-function.md) $x$ and $-x$ are both [convex functions](convex-function.md) on $\mathbb R$, but their product is $-x^2$. Its value at zero exceeds the average of its values at $-1$ and $1$, contradicting [convexity](convex-function.md). Thus closure under nonnegative linear combinations does not imply closure under products.
