# Real structure of the SU4 exterior square

↑ **Parent:** [Two-index antisymmetric representation](two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md)

On $\bigwedge^2\mathbb C^4$, the invariant Hermitian form and volume [tensor](tensor.md) define the displayed [antilinear map](antilinear-map.md) with square one, commuting with [SU(4)](su-4-group.md). Its fixed space satisfies $T^{12}=\overline{T^{34}}$, $T^{13}=-\overline{T^{24}}$, and $T^{14}=\overline{T^{23}}$. Three complex choices give six real coordinates, and this fixed space has the original exterior square as its complexification. It is therefore a [real representation](real-representation.md) of dimension six, rather than merely the twelve-dimensional realification of an arbitrary six-dimensional complex representation.
