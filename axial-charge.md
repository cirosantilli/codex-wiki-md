# Axial charge

↑ **Parent:** [Axial current](axial-current.md)

An [axial charge](axial-charge.md) is the spatial integral of the time component of an [axial current](axial-current.md). For a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) and an internal generator $t$, it is $\int\psi^\dagger\gamma_5t\psi\,d^3x$. Its canonical action rotates left- and right-handed fields oppositely. It is conserved only when the current divergence and flux at infinity vanish; [Dirac masses](dirac-mass-term.md) and a [chiral anomaly](chiral-anomaly.md) can obstruct conservation. The nonsinglet [quark](quark.md) generators have no color anomaly and obey the [nonsinglet axial charge algebra of quark densities](nonsinglet-axial-charge-algebra-of-quark-densities.md).
