# Psychosis

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopathology](psychopathology.md)

A clinical description involving marked disturbances in perception, belief, thought or related functioning, assessed in context. [Psychosis](psychosis.md) is not synonymous with [schizophrenia](schizophrenia.md), dangerousness or loss of all capacities.
