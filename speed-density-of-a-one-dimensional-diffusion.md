# Speed density of a one-dimensional diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Markov diffusion](markov-diffusion.md)

For a diffusion with generator $(\sigma^2/2)\partial_{xx}+b\partial_x$ and increasing [scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion](scale-function-stochastic-processes.md) $s$, its speed density is $m=2/(\sigma^2s')$. Scale records hitting probabilities; speed records expected occupation and exit times. Multiplying scale by a positive constant divides speed by the same constant, leaving their Green-kernel product unchanged.
