# Subdirect product of pairwise nonisomorphic simple modules

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple module](semisimple-module.md)

Suppose the $S_i$ are pairwise nonisomorphic [simple modules](irreducible-module.md). Any [submodule](submodule.md) $W$ of their finite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) that projects onto every summand is the entire sum. Induct on $k$. Projection onto the first $k-1$ factors is onto by induction. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is a [submodule](submodule.md) of $S_k$, hence zero or all of $S_k$. If zero, $W$ is isomorphic to the first $k-1$ factors but also has quotient $S_k$, contradicting their distinct [composition factors](composition-factor.md) and the [Jordan–Hölder theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md). Therefore the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $S_k$, proving the assertion. Consequently, if $S_i=Ax_i$, the map $a\mapsto(ax_i)_i$ is onto and gives $A/(\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_A(x_i))\cong\bigoplus_iS_i$ as left modules. The annihilators of these vectors need only be left ideals.
