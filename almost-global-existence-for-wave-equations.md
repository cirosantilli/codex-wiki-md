# Almost global existence for wave equations

↑ **Parent:** [Semilinear wave equation](semilinear-wave-equation.md)

Almost global existence means a lifespan which grows exponentially in the inverse size of small initial data, typically $T_\varepsilon\geq\exp(c/\varepsilon)-1$ for derivative-quadratic [semilinear wave equations](semilinear-wave-equation.md) in three dimensions. [Commuted wave energy](commuted-wave-energy.md) and the [Klainerman-Sobolev inequality](klainerman-sobolev-inequality.md) lead to a logarithmic accumulation $\varepsilon\log(1+T)$ in a [bootstrap argument](bootstrap-argument.md). This implies existence up to every fixed inverse power $\varepsilon^{-N}$ when the data are small enough depending on $N$.
