# Unidirectional flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid flow](fluid-flow.md)

A unidirectional flow has velocity parallel to a single fixed direction, for example $\boldsymbol u=u(y)\boldsymbol e_x$. If the speed is independent of the coordinate along that direction, the convective acceleration $(\boldsymbol u\cdot\nabla)\boldsymbol u$ vanishes. The steady [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) then reduces to a linear balance of the pressure gradient and viscosity, as in [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) and [Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md).
