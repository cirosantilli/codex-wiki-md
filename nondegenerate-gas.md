# Nondegenerate gas

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas.md)

A gas is nondegenerate when its occupation numbers are low enough that [quantum statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md) reduce to the [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). A common criterion is $n\lambda_{\rm th}^3\ll1$, where $\lambda_{\rm th}$ is the thermal de Broglie wavelength.

For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md), this is the classical statistical regime; nondegeneracy alone does not assert the absence of interactions.
