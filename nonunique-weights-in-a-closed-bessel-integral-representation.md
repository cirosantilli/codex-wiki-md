# Nonunique weights in a closed Bessel integral representation

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md)

Choose the square-root branch asymptotic to $\zeta$ outside the cut $[-i,i]$. The Laurent series of its reciprocal gives the displayed [integral](integral-split.md) by termwise residue calculation. Adding any [entire function](entire-function.md) to the weight leaves the [integral](integral-split.md) unchanged, since its product with $e^{z\zeta}$ is entire. Thus a nonzero solution represented by a [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) [integral](integral-split.md) does not by itself determine the weight uniquely; an inference from the [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) to an equation for the weight needs an injective representation or an explicit construction assumption.
