# Two-domain-wall degeneracy of a Potts ring

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic Potts chain](periodic-potts-chain.md)

For $Q,L\ge2$, a [periodic Potts chain](periodic-potts-chain.md) has $Q$ uniform [ground states](ground-state.md) with [energy](energy-split.md) $-JL$. A single [domain wall](domain-wall.md) cannot close around the ring. Its [first excited states](first-excited-state.md) have two [domain walls](domain-wall.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) $-JL+2J$. Choose the wall bonds in $\binom L2$ ways and assign distinct states to the two resulting arcs in $Q(Q-1)$ ways. This proves the displayed [degeneracy factor](degeneracy-factor.md) without identifying globally relabelled configurations.
