# Schottky group

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_group)

A [Schottky group](schottky-group.md) is a free, discrete group of [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) constructed by pairing disjoint Jordan curves on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), taking the exterior of one bounded disk to the interior of its partner. For round circles it is classical. Strictly separated defining disks give a [Cantor set](cantor-set.md) as the [limit set of a Kleinian group](limit-set-of-a-kleinian-group.md). The tangent-disk extension permits parabolic elements and is often called a noded or T-Schottky group; its [Kleinian limit set](limit-set-of-a-kleinian-group.md) need not be a [Cantor set](cantor-set.md).
