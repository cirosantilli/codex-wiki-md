# Frazil export enthalpy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Frazil ice](frazil-ice.md)

Let $q$ be the depth-integrated volume flux, $E$ the [entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) rate, $R$ the upward export of pure ice, and $e=c_p(T-T_\infty)-L\phi$. Then $q'=E-R$, $[\overline Cq]'=C_\infty E$ and $[eq]'=-f-R[c_p(T-T_\infty)-L]$. Export carries negative relative [enthalpy](enthalpy.md), so it is not a heat-loss term with the same sign as atmospheric cooling. Total production is the increase in advected ice plus its exported amount. When both retained ice fraction and total processed fraction are small, equilibrium [salinity](salinity.md) and [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) give production $f/(L+c_pmC_\infty)$ even when retained and exported fractions differ.
