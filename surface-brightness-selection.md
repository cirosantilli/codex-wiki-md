# Surface-brightness selection

↑ **Parent:** [Selection bias](selection-bias.md)

Detection and usable measurement of an extended source depend on its [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) and angular extent as well as its total [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md). A diffuse [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) can be missed even if its integrated flux exceeds a nominal point-source limit. Artificial-source injection over a range of sizes and profiles tests this form of survey incompleteness.
