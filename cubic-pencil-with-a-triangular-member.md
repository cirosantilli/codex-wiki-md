# Cubic pencil with a triangular member

↑ **Parent:** [Pencil of plane curves](pencil-of-plane-curves.md)

The pencil generated by the [Fermat cubic curve](fermat-cubic-curve.md) and $xyz=0$ contains both a smooth [genus one](genus-one-curve.md) curve and the union of the three coordinate lines. Its total space in $\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^1$ is irreducible because the two generating cubics have no common factor.
