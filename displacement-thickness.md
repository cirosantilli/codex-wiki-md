# Displacement thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary layer thickness](boundary-layer-thickness.md)

For constant-[mass density](density.md) [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), the [displacement thickness](displacement-thickness.md) measures the lost [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit span divided by the outer [velocity](velocity.md):

$$
\delta^*=\int_0^\infty\left(1-\frac{u}{U}\right)\,dy,\qquad U\delta^*=\int_0^\infty(U-u)\,dy.
$$

An outer [inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md) encountering the same [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) deficit behaves, at leading order, as though the wall were displaced outward by $\delta^*$. In a [Blasius boundary layer](blasius-boundary-layer.md), numerical [integration](integral.md) gives $\delta^*\simeq1.720788\sqrt{\nu x/U}$. This equivalent displacement is smaller than the [boundary layer thickness](boundary-layer-thickness.md) and is not the position of a physical surface.[Boundary-layer Thickness, Skin friction, and Energy dissipation](modules-on-high-reynolds-number-flows/boundary-layer-thickness-skin-friction-and-energy-dissipation.md)
