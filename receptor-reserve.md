# Receptor reserve

↑ **Parent:** [G protein-coupled receptor](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md)

The capacity of a signalling system to produce a maximal response without occupancy or activation of all receptors. It makes observed maximal response an unreliable direct measure of intrinsic [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) efficacy.
