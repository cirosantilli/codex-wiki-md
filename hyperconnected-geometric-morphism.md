# Hyperconnected geometric morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

A [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) $h$ is hyperconnected when its [inverse image functor of a geometric morphism](inverse-image-functor-of-a-geometric-morphism.md) is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md) and its essential image is closed under [subobjects](subobject.md). It then identifies all subobjects of $h^*X$ with subobjects of $X$, and its image is closed under quotients as well, by descending the kernel-pair relation.
