# Gromov width of the monotone product of projective lines

↑ **Parent:** [Gromov width](gromov-width.md)

When each projective line has area $\pi$, the product $\mathbb{CP}^1\times\mathbb{CP}^1$ has Gromov width $\pi$. The lower bound comes from the product of two area-$\pi$ discs; the upper bound comes from a J-holomorphic sphere of area $\pi$ through the center of any proposed ball.
