# Discrete subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A subgroup $G$ of a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) is discrete when the identity has a neighbourhood containing no other element of $G$. Translation then isolates every group element. In a Hausdorff [topological group](topological-group-split.md), such a subgroup is closed: any accumulation at a point would make differences of distinct nearby group elements approach the identity. Consequently its intersection with a [compact set](compact-space.md) is finite. For isometries of [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md), this converts compactness of the set of isometries moving one point a bounded distance into a [properly discontinuous group action](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md).
