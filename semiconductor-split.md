# Semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor)

A semiconductor has an electronic [band gap](band-gap.md) and a conductivity strongly affected by temperature, impurities and carrier injection. Optical absorption can create mobile electrons and holes, enabling a [photodiode](photodiode.md).

The useful feature of a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) is that its [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) population can be controlled. [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md) changes the supply of mobile [electrons](electron.md) or [electron holes](electron-hole.md); [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md) changes their population away from [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). A [transistor](transistor.md) turns that control into an [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md): a small input [signal](signal.md) controls a larger flow of supplied [energy](energy-split.md).

For a uniform [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) in a weak [electric field](electric-field-split.md), a simple drift model writes its [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) as

$$
\sigma=q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p).
$$

Here $q>0$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), $n,p$ are the [number densities](number-density.md) of mobile [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md), and $\mu_n,\mu_p$ their positive [charge carrier mobilities](charge-carrier-mobility.md). The two contributions add because opposite [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) drift in opposite directions and therefore carry [electric current](electric-current.md) in the same direction. This model assumes fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and mobilities and omits [diffusion](diffusion.md) from density gradients.

An original dimensionless example isolates the effect of [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md). Suppose initially $p\ll n=n_0$ and $\mu_n=2\mu_p$. Adding equal excess populations $\Delta n=\Delta p=0.1n_0$ gives $\Delta\sigma/\sigma_0=0.15$, even though the added [electron holes](electron-hole.md) remain a [minority carrier](minority-carrier.md) population. A [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) can respond strongly to a small injected population without changing its underlying [band gap](band-gap.md).
