# Bubble rise near a distant stress-free free surface

↑ **Parent:** [Clean-bubble Stokes drag](clean-bubble-stokes-drag.md)

For a clean inviscid bubble a distance $d\gg a$ below a gravity-restored approximately flat [free surface](free-surface.md), the surface displacement scales as $F/(\rho gd^2)=O(a^3/d^2)$. Zero normal [velocity](velocity.md) and zero tangential traction are imposed to leading order by an opposite normal image [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) at distance $2d$ from the bubble. Its incident [velocity](velocity.md) is $-F/(8\pi\mu d)$, which gives the displayed correction at fixed [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). The image can be represented by a same-radius inviscid drop of density $2\rho$ in liquid of density $\rho$. Its deformation-producing strain is $O(F/(\mu d^2))$, so the leading fractional capillary deformation is $O(\mathrm{Bo}(a/d)^2)$; small [Bond number](bond-number.md) is the conventional stronger sufficient criterion.
