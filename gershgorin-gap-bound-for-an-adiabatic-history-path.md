# Gershgorin gap bound for an adiabatic history path

↑ **Parent:** [History-subspace propagation Hamiltonian](history-subspace-propagation-hamiltonian.md)

For $M(s)=(1-s)D+sE$ in the unary history subspace, the first [Gershgorin disc](gershgorin-disc.md) lies in $[0,s]$ and every other disc has real part at least $1-s$. For $s<1/2$, the first disc is isolated and contains the unique [ground state](ground-state.md) energy. Thus the [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) is at least $1-2s$, in particular at least $1/3$ for $s\leq1/3$.
