# Toric blowup along an orbit closure

↑ **Parent:** [Star subdivision](star-subdivision.md)

On a smooth [toric variety](toric-variety.md), the [blowup](blowing-up-algebraic-geometry.md) along the [orbit closure in a toric variety](orbit-closure-in-a-toric-variety.md) of a cone generated by part of a [lattice basis](basis-of-a-toric-lattice.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ is the [star subdivision](star-subdivision.md) at their sum. Locally the center has ideal $(x_1,\ldots,x_k)$; its blowup charts adjoin $x_j/x_i$, producing precisely these subdivided cones. This extends the [toric blowup at a torus-fixed point](toric-blowup-at-a-torus-fixed-point.md) to invariant centers of any codimension.
