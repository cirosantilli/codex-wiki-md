# Dense-below generic meeting lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Dense below a forcing condition](dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md)

If $p\in G$ and a ground-model [set](set-split.md) $D$ is [dense below a forcing condition](dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md) $p$, then the [generic filter](generic-filter.md) $G$ meets $D$. Add all [incompatible forcing conditions](incompatible-forcing-conditions.md) with $p$ to $D$. The enlarged [set](set-split.md) is globally dense: a condition compatible with $p$ first has a common strengthening, then a further strengthening in $D$. Genericity meets the enlargement, and directedness excludes its incompatible part.
