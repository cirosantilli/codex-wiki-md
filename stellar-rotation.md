# Stellar rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_rotation)

The spin of a star about its axis, which can be [solid-body rotation](solid-body-rotation.md) or vary through the interior. [Angular momentum transport](angular-momentum-transport.md) and rotational mixing can change the [stellar composition profile](stellar-composition-profile.md) and available fuel. An observed surface rotation rate does not determine the entire interior profile.
