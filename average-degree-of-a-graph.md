# Average degree of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Degree (graph theory)](degree-graph-theory.md)

The average [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md) is the sum of its vertex degrees divided by its order. [Double counting](double-counting-proof-technique.md) edge endpoints gives $\overline d(G)=2e(G)/|V(G)|$. It lies between the [minimum degree of a graph](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) and the [maximum degree of a graph](maximum-degree-of-a-graph.md).
