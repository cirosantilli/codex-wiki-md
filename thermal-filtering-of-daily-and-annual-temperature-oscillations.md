# Thermal filtering of daily and annual temperature oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Annual ground-temperature wave](annual-ground-temperature-wave.md)

The [thermal penetration depth](thermal-penetration-depth.md) of a harmonic [ground temperature](ground-temperature.md) disturbance scales as the inverse square root of its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). For yearly and daily forcing, a 365-day year gives $\delta_Y/\delta_D=\sqrt{365}\simeq19.1$. At ten daily penetration depths the daily component is attenuated by $e^{-10}$, whereas the annual one retains about $59.2\%$ of its surface amplitude. At ten annual penetration depths, even the annual component is attenuated by $e^{-10}$. These are consequences of the linear [thermal diffusion wave](thermal-diffusion-wave.md) model, with homogeneous constant [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md).
