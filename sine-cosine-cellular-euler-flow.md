# Sine-cosine cellular Euler flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stream function](stream-function.md)

With $u=\psi_y$ and $v=-\psi_x$, this steady incompressible solution of the [Euler equations](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) has velocity $(-\sin x\sin y,-\cos x\cos y)$ and [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) $2\psi$. Its pressure is $C+\rho(\cos2x-\cos2y)/4$. The Bernoulli function is $C_0-\psi^2$, so it is constant along streamlines but generally varies between them.
