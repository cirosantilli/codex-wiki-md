# Riesz dual basis in an inner product space

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

For a basis $(u_i)$ of a finite-dimensional [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) with conjugate-linear first argument, define $G_{ij}=(u_i,u_j)$ and $\widehat u_k=\sum_l(G^{-1})_{lk}u_l$. Then $(u_i,\widehat u_k)=\delta_{ik}$ and $(\widehat u_j,\widehat u_k)=(G^{-1})_{jk}$. This is the inner-product representation of the [dual basis](dual-basis.md); requiring the dual vectors to lie in the given span is essential.
