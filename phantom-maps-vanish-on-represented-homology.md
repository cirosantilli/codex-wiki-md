# Phantom maps vanish on represented homology

↑ **Parent:** [Phantom map of spectra](phantom-map-of-spectra.md)

For a [phantom map of spectra](phantom-map-of-spectra.md) $p:D\to E$ and a finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $K$, [Spanier-Whitehead duality](spanier-whitehead-duality.md) identifies $D_k(K)$ with maps from the finite spectrum $\Sigma^k(\Sigma^\infty K)^\vee$. Hence $p_*=0$ there. An arbitrary [CW complex](cw-complex.md) is the filtered union of its finite subcomplexes, and [represented homology theory](represented-homology-theory.md) commutes with that filtered union because spheres are compact. Thus $p$ induces zero on the entire [represented homology theory](represented-homology-theory.md).
