# Spin representation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_representation)

A spinor representation is a representation of a [Spin group](spin-group.md), or infinitesimally of a [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md), constructed from a [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md). In even dimensions it can split into two chiral spinor representations.
