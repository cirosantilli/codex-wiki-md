# Specific reading-comprehension difficulty

↑ **Parent:** [Reading disorder](reading-disorder.md)

Poor understanding of text despite relatively adequate word-reading accuracy. Assessment should examine [oral language](oral-language.md), fluency and higher-level processes rather than infer a single cause from the profile.[Psychological Science 21, 1106–1116; text/oral/combined interventions, untreated comparison and eleven-month follow-up](ameliorating-children-s-reading-comprehension-difficulties-a-randomized-controlled-trial-by-paula-j-clarke-and-others.md)
