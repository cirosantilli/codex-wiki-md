# Parabolic-channel dry-bed dam break

↑ **Parent:** [Prismatic-channel shallow water equations](prismatic-channel-shallow-water-equations.md)

For water initially at rest at depth $H$ on one side of a dam and a dry bed on the other, a channel with $b(z)=C\sqrt z$ has $c_0=\sqrt{2gH/3}$. The centered [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) has $u+3c=3c_0$ and $x/t=u-c$. Thus $c=(3c_0-x/t)/4$ and $u=3(c_0+x/t)/4$ within $-c_0t<x<3c_0t$. The reservoir remains undisturbed to the left and the bed dry to the right. This is an outer [shallow-water approximation](shallow-water-approximation.md) before finite-reservoir boundary signals return.
