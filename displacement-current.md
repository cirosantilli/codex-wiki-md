# Displacement current

↑ **Parent:** [Ampère's circuital law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displacement_current)

In vacuum, the displacement-current density is $\epsilon_0\partial E/\partial t$. Its contribution to magnetic circulation makes the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) compatible with changing electric charge.
