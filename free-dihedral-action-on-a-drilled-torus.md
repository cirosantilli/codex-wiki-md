# Free dihedral action on a drilled torus

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

Let $D_n$ act on [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) by rotations through $2\pi/n$ about a vertical axis and half-turns about $n$ equally spaced horizontal axes. A round [solid torus](solid-torus.md) about the vertical axis, drilled through radially along the $2n$ places where the horizontal axes cross its tube, has a smooth [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $2n+1$ after equivariant rounding of its rims. All nonidentity rotation axes lie in the complement of this [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md), so the induced [group action](group-action-split.md) is a [free action of a group](free-action-of-a-group.md). Each bore replaces two [closed discs](closed-disc.md) by an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md), lowering [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) by two and increasing [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) by one. The [boundary orientation](boundary-orientation.md) is preserved; its induced [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces-split.md) has an unramified [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) two by the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md).
