# Mixed log-likelihood derivative obstruction to parameter separation

↑ **Parent:** [Nuisance parameter](nuisance-parameter.md)

On a common positive support, a factorization $f=\phi_0(x)\phi_C(x;\psi)\phi_S(x;\lambda)$ implies $\partial_\psi\partial_\lambda\log f=0$. Thus any nonzero mixed derivative prevents this exact separation. For $n$ independent [gamma distributed](gamma-distribution.md) observations with shape $a$ and rate $b$, this mixed derivative is $n/b$, so exact separation of shape and rate into such factors is impossible.
