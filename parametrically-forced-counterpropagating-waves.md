# Parametrically forced counterpropagating waves

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

Two counterpropagating modes transform as $(A,B)\mapsto(e^{ik\delta}A,e^{-ik\delta}B)$ under [spatial translation](spatial-translation.md). Forcing at twice their natural frequency breaks continuous temporal phase symmetry but preserves simultaneous sign reversal. Hence $\overline B$ transforms like $A$ and $\overline A$ like $B$, allowing linear parametric coupling. An equal-amplitude phase-locked [standing wave](standing-wave.md) has $|A|^2=|B|^2=z>0$ with $|\beta+\gamma|^2z^2-2\mu\operatorname{Re}(\beta+\gamma)z+\mu^2-\nu^2=0$. A nonnegative [discriminant](discriminant.md) is only an algebraic compatibility condition: a physical nonzero wave also requires a positive root.
