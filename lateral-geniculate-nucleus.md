# Lateral geniculate nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Thalamus](thalamus.md)

A thalamic visual structure receiving retinal input and participating in pathways to visual cortex. Chromatic nulling experiments constrain how particular cells combine cone signals, while recurrent and descending interactions complicate a pure feed-forward relay description.
