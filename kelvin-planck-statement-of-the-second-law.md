# Kelvin-Planck statement of the second law

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md)

No cyclic device can have as its sole effect the extraction of [heat](heat.md) from one [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) and its complete conversion into net [work](work.md). A putative device absorbing $Q_h>0$ and returning to its starting state with $W=Q_h$ and no other transfer would conserve [energy](energy.md), yet violate the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md). Rejecting [heat](heat.md) to colder surroundings is an essential part of the ordinary two-[thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) [heat engine](heat-engine.md) budget.[§5.1, statement 1 and Figure 5.1: complete conversion as the sole result](thermodynamics-and-propulsion/5-1-concept-and-statements-of-the-second-law-why-do-we-need-a-second-law.md)

The cyclic and sole-effect qualifications matter. A single [isothermal expansion](isothermal-expansion.md) of an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) can absorb [heat](heat.md) and deliver equal [work](work.md) while leaving its [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) changed; restoring the initial state must also be counted. Likewise, a device discharging stored [internal energy](internal-energy.md) or consuming fuel has another effect. Converting supplied electrical [work](work.md) completely to [heat](heat.md) through [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) reverses the proposed conversion and is allowed. The statement also allows cyclic devices that exchange [heat](heat.md) with one [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) while consuming net [work](work.md); it forbids positive net [work](work.md) output with that single [heat](heat.md) source as the only external change.

Conversely, a hypothetical complete-conversion device could take $100\,\mathrm J$ from the hot [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) and supply $100\,\mathrm J$ of [work](work.md) to an ideal [refrigerator](refrigerator.md) that extracts $100\,\mathrm J$ from the cold [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) and delivers $200\,\mathrm J$ to the hot one. At $600\,\mathrm K$ and $300\,\mathrm K$, that [refrigerator](refrigerator.md) is itself admissible: its reservoir [entropy](entropy.md) changes cancel. The composite would consume no external [work](work.md) and move a net $100\,\mathrm J$ from cold to hot, violating the [Clausius statement of the second law](clausius-statement-of-the-second-law.md). Together with the construction there, this proves the equivalence of the two impossibility statements.[§I.D, printed p. 8: engine driving a refrigerator](8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles/statistical-mechanics-i-lecture-2.md)

For the two fixed positive-[temperature](temperature.md) [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) hypotheses, [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md) gives the sharper restriction $\eta\le1-T_c/T_h<1$. A [reversible thermodynamic process](reversible-thermodynamic-process.md) reaches that bound in the ideal [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md); an ordinary device's smaller [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md) reflects its [entropy production](entropy-production.md) and measurement boundary. An inefficient observed engine is compatible with the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md), rather than evidence that the bound has been experimentally determined.
