# Box-Muller transform

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method.md)

Independent [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) draws $U_1,U_2$ on $(0,1)$ produce independent standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) draws $X_1=\sqrt{-2\log U_1}\cos(2\pi U_2)$ and $X_2=\sqrt{-2\log U_1}\sin(2\pi U_2)$. The proof transforms an independent uniform angle and radial density $re^{-r^2/2}$ through the polar-coordinate Jacobian.
