# Inner Lindblad resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Lindblad resonance](lindblad-resonance.md)

The $k_R=-1$ [orbital resonance](orbital-resonance.md) of an $m$-fold perturbation of a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md). For near-circular [stellar orbits](stellar-orbit.md), $\Omega_R$ is the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md). The harmonic changes [radial action](radial-action.md) and azimuthal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in the ratio $\Delta J_R/\Delta J_\varphi=-1/m$, so an orbit losing [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) gains random radial motion.
