# Inverse-variance weighted mean

↑ **Parent:** [Linear unbiased estimator](linear-unbiased-estimator.md)

For independent [unbiased estimators](unbiased-estimator.md) $X_i$ of a common mean with known positive [variances](variance-split.md) $v_i$, the [linear unbiased estimator](linear-unbiased-estimator.md) with smallest variance has weights $w_i=v_i^{-1}/\sum_jv_j^{-1}$ and variance $(\sum_jv_j^{-1})^{-1}$. Unbiasedness requires $\sum_iw_i=1$. Minimizing $\sum_iv_iw_i^2$ by a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) gives the weights, and the strictly positive diagonal [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) proves a unique global minimum. This conclusion requires no normality assumption.
