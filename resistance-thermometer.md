# Resistance thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Thermometer](thermometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistance_thermometer)

A [thermometer](thermometer.md) that infers [temperature](temperature-split.md) from a calibrated [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). Measuring current can heat its sensing element; extrapolation toward zero current controls that bias in the [saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement](saturation-pressure-equilibrium-measurement.md).
