# Principle of minimal sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md)

This prescription selects an arbitrary scale or scheme parameter by making a truncated perturbative observable stationary under its variation. The complete physical observable is independent of the [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md), but truncation leaves residual dependence. Stationarity is a prescription, not a guarantee of uniquely optimal accuracy or of a stationary point's existence.
