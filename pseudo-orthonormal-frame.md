# Pseudo-orthonormal frame

↑ **Parent:** [Frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

For a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) carrying a smooth nondegenerate symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) of constant [metric signature](metric-signature.md), a pseudo-orthonormal frame is a smooth local [frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) whose Gram matrix is the constant diagonal signature matrix $\eta$. It identifies the metric bundle locally with a fixed model $\mathbb R^{p,q}$. Two such frames differ by a smooth map into the corresponding [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md). Positive-definite signature is included as the ordinary orthonormal case. A global pseudo-orthonormal frame is a [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md) of the [orthonormal frame bundle](orthonormal-frame-bundle.md) and trivializes that [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md).
