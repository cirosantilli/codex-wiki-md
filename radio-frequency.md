# Radio frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency](frequency.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_frequency)

A radio frequency is a [frequency](frequency.md) in the range used for [radio waves](radio-wave.md). It describes the rapid [oscillation](oscillation.md) of a [radio wave](radio-wave.md), which can differ from the rate at which information is transmitted in [radio communication](radio-communication.md).
