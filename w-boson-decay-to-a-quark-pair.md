# W boson decay to a quark pair

↑ **Parent:** [W boson](w-boson.md)

Ignoring accessible quark masses and strong corrections, the [W boson](w-boson.md) flavor width is $\Gamma(W^+\to q\bar q')=N_cG_FM_W^3|V_{qq'}|^2/(6\pi\sqrt2)$. The [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) fixes the flavor factor, while the [color multiplicity in a decay width](color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md) supplies $N_c=3$. Only the up and charm rows are accessible for an on-shell [W boson](w-boson.md), giving a total hadronic width $G_FM_W^3/(\pi\sqrt2)$ by row unitarity.
