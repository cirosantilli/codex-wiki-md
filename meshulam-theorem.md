# Meshulam theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For each fixed odd prime $p$ and density $\alpha>0$, every sufficiently large subset of $\mathbb F_p^n$ of density at least $\alpha$ contains a nontrivial three-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md).
