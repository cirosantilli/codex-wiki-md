# Logarithmic-potential two-integral distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Two-integral galactic distribution function](two-integral-galactic-distribution-function.md)

The singular [axisymmetric logarithmic gravitational potential](axisymmetric-logarithmic-gravitational-potential.md) admits this stationary [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) with

$$
A=\frac{4\sqrt2(1-q^2)}{\pi^{5/2}Gq^2V^3},\qquad B=\frac{2q^2-1}{4\pi^{5/2}Gq^2V}.
$$

The zero of [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) is the printed logarithmic convention. Products of [Gaussian integrals](gaussian-integral.md) normalize its two spatial density terms. For $1/2\le q^2\le1$ the two terms are separately nonnegative; mildly prolate models require a bound on their sum.
