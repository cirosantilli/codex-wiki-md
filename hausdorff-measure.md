# Hausdorff measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausdorff_measure)

Hausdorff measure measures a [set](set-split.md) by the small-scale cost of covers, using the $s$th powers of their diameters and a standard normalization. In Euclidean dimension one, $\mathcal H^1$ agrees with arc length on [smooth](smooth-function.md) curves; in particular a line segment has its usual length.
