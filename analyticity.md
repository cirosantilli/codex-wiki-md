# Analyticity

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)

Analyticity at a point means that a [function](function-split.md) agrees in a neighbourhood of that point with a convergent [power series](power-series.md). This is stronger than merely having [derivatives](derivative.md) of every order: the [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) of a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) can fail to recover its values. For a real variable this property defines a [real analytic function](real-analytic-function.md). For a complex variable it is equivalent to being a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) on a neighbourhood of the point.
