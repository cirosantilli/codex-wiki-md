# Poisson counting in a coherent state

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

The normalized [coherent state](coherent-state.md) has number amplitude $e^{-|\alpha|^2/2}\alpha^n/\sqrt{n!}$, hence the displayed Poisson law with mean and [variance](variance-split.md) $|\alpha|^2$. A product of coherent modes has factorized number [probabilities](probability.md).
