# Transmitted and untransmitted alleles under multiplicative penetrance

↑ **Parent:** [Family pseudo-control genotype](family-pseudo-control-genotype.md)

Under random mating and [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](hardy-weinberg-principle.md), label each parental allele by whether it is transmitted. Let $(A,B)$ and $(C,D)$ be the transmitted/untransmitted pairs of two parents. Before ascertainment all four draws are independent with population frequencies $\pi$. If offspring disease weights the transmitted pair by $\psi_A\psi_C$, then

$$
\mathbb P(A=a,B=b,C=c,D=d\mid\text{offspring disease})=\pi_a^*\pi_c^*\pi_b\pi_d.
$$

The transmitted pair is an affected-case genotype and the untransmitted pair a population [family pseudo-control genotype](family-pseudo-control-genotype.md) under this homogeneous model. For unordered genotypes, compatible labelled transmissions must be summed. Conditioning on parental genotypes instead removes nuisance population frequencies and gives the usual [transmission disequilibrium test](transmission-disequilibrium-test.md).
