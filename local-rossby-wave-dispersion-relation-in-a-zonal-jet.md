# Local Rossby-wave dispersion relation in a zonal jet

↑ **Parent:** [Rossby-wave equation for a sheared zonal current](rossby-wave-equation-for-a-sheared-zonal-current.md)

When a zonal current varies slowly compared with the wavelength, freeze its velocity and background [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) gradient locally in the [Rossby-wave equation for a sheared zonal current](rossby-wave-equation-for-a-sheared-zonal-current.md). The resulting local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) includes curvature $U^{\prime\prime}$ and, for a free surface, the basic surface-slope contribution $U/R_D^2$. In the nondivergent limit it becomes $\omega=kU-k(\beta-U^{\prime\prime})/(k^2+l^2)$.
