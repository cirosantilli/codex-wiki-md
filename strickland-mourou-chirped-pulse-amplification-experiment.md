# Strickland-Mourou chirped pulse amplification experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Chirped pulse amplification](chirped-pulse-amplification.md)

The 1985 experiment launched $150\,\mathrm{ps}$ pulses from a [mode-locked laser](mode-locked-laser.md) into $1.4\,\mathrm{km}$ of [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md). Dispersion and [self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md) produced a broader spectrum and approximately $300\,\mathrm{ps}$ pulses. A Pockels cell selected a pulse for repeated amplification in neodymium glass, giving approximately $2\,\mathrm{mJ}$ before the double-pass [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) compressor. Its transmission was 50 percent. The amplified output was reported as $1\,\mathrm{mJ}$ with a $2\,\mathrm{ps}$ width limited by the streak camera response. The compressor was adjusted on fiber-output pulses using [intensity autocorrelation](intensity-autocorrelation.md); that measurement gave $1.5\,\mathrm{ps}$. [Steve Williamson](steve-williamson.md) was acknowledged for help developing the concept, and [Philippe Bado](philippe-bado.md) and [Marcel Bouvier](marcel-bouvier.md) for technical support.[pp.219–221, Figs.1–4 and acknowledgments: preparation, compressor adjustment, amplified readout and response limit](compression-of-amplified-chirped-optical-pulses-by-donna-strickland-and-gerard-mourou.md)

The crucial comparison is compression before and after amplification. It asks whether enough usable [spectral phase](spectral-phase.md) structure survives the amplifier, rather than merely whether its output is bright. A response-limited trace cannot establish the true width below the detector's resolution or prove that every higher-order phase coefficient vanished. An [intensity autocorrelation](intensity-autocorrelation.md) is also not a direct unique measurement of the complex [electric field](electric-field-split.md). These are limits on the inference, not extra controls attributed to the experiment.

[Donna Strickland](donna-strickland.md)'s 2018 lecture describes setting the compressor with the high-repetition oscillator pulses and making the amplified-pulse measurement with [Steve Williamson](steve-williamson.md)'s streak camera. It explicitly notes imperfect chirp matching.[Generating High-Intensity Ultrashort Optical Pulses, p.168: separate autocorrelation and streak-camera measurements](generating-high-intensity-ultrashort-optical-pulses-by-donna-strickland.md) The first apparatus therefore illustrates the strategy without establishing a universal lossless or exactly phase-matched amplifier.

[Donna Strickland](donna-strickland.md) leads an ultrafast [laser](laser-split.md) group at Waterloo investigating intense [laser](laser-split.md) interactions and [nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md).[Biography and research interests: professional appointment and experimental research](donna-strickland-by-university-of-waterloo.md) [Gérard Mourou](gerard-mourou.md)'s later work includes coherent combination of [laser](laser-split.md) beams in the XCAN project at École polytechnique.[Professor emeritus and XCAN prototype: coherent combination of 61 beams](the-first-laser-pixels-by-ecole-polytechnique.md) Their complementary research settings connect the original short-pulse experiment with the continuing problems of producing useful [energy](energy-split.md), [peak power](peak-power.md) and average [power](power.md) together.
