# Kauffman bracket skein module

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md)

For an oriented 3-manifold, take the linear span of [isotopy](isotopy.md) classes of [framed links](framed-link.md), including the empty [link](link.md), and impose the local bracket smoothing relation and the trivial-circle relation with factor $-A^2-A^{-2}$. Fixing framed boundary endpoints gives its relative version. Gluing manifolds or stacking cylinders induces maps between skein [modules](module-mathematics.md); framed [isotopy](isotopy.md), rather than unframed Reidemeister-I invariance, is built into this construction.
