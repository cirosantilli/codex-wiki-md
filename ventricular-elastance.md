# Ventricular elastance

↑ **Parent:** [Left ventricle](left-ventricle.md)

Time-dependent elastance models the [pressure](pressure-split.md) generated by a [cardiac ventricle](cardiac-ventricle.md) at its current stressed volume. The reference $V_0$ is its zero-[pressure](pressure-split.md) volume. The inverse of elastance is compliance at fixed time. Rising elastance increases [pressure](pressure-split.md) without volume change during [isovolumetric contraction](isovolumetric-contraction.md); an approximately constant systolic value $E_S$ gives compliance $C_s=1/E_S$ during a simplified ejection stage.
