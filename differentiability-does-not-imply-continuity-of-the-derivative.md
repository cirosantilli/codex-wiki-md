# Differentiability does not imply continuity of the derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiable function](differentiable-function.md)

Extend the displayed function by $f(0)=0$. Its [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) at zero is $h\sin(1/h)$, which tends to zero. Away from zero its [derivative](derivative.md) is $2x\sin(1/x)-\cos(1/x)$. At $x_k=1/(2\pi k)$ this derivative equals $-1$, whereas at $y_k=1/((2k+1)\pi)$ it equals $+1$. Thus [differentiability implies continuity](differentiability-implies-continuity.md) of the function but does not imply [continuity](continuous-function.md) of its derivative.
