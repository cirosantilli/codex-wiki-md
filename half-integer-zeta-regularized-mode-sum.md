# Half-integer zeta-regularized mode sum

↑ **Parent:** [Zeta function regularization](zeta-function-regularization.md)

For $r=n+1/2$, $n\geq0$, the convergent frequency zeta function, expressed through the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md), is $\sum_r r^{-s}=(2^s-1)\zeta_R(s)$ when $\operatorname{Re}s>1$. Its [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) at $s=-1$ gives $(1/2-1)(-1/12)=1/24$. A common exponential frequency cutoff gives $\sum_r r e^{-\varepsilon r}=\varepsilon^{-2}+1/24+O(\varepsilon^2)$, so the same finite part follows by subtracting the leading divergence. In contrast, integer frequencies have regulated sum $-1/12$.
