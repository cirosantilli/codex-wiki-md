# Q-ball

↑ **Parent:** [Non-topological soliton](non-topological-soliton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-ball)

For a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) with global $U(1)$ invariance and Lagrangian $\mathcal L=\tfrac12|\partial_t\phi|^2-\tfrac12|\nabla\phi|^2-U(|\phi|)$, a [Q-ball](q-ball.md) is a localized phase-rotating configuration with conserved [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) $Q=\int\operatorname{Im}(\bar\phi\phi_t)$. At fixed charge its real profile can minimize

$$
E_Q[f]=\int\left(\frac12|\nabla f|^2+U(f)\right)+
\frac{Q^2}{2\int f^2}.
$$

Such solutions require a suitable potential and charge range; the complex nature of a field alone does not ensure existence. A common condition is $\inf_{f>0}2U(f)/f^2<U''(0)$.
