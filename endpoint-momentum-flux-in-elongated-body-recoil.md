# Endpoint momentum flux in elongated-body recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Recoil correction in elongated-body theory](recoil-correction-in-elongated-body-theory.md)

With $r=x-\bar x$, lateral relative [velocity](velocity.md) $w=(\partial_t+U\partial_x)h$, $Q=\int mw\,dx$ and $P=\int rmw\,dx$, the [Lighthill elongated-body theory](lighthill-elongated-body-theory.md) [force](force.md) on the fluid is $F_z=(\partial_t+U\partial_x)(mw)$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $\int F_zdx=\dot Q+U[mw]_0^L$ and $\int rF_zdx=\dot P+U[rmw]_0^L-UQ$. The endpoint terms are [momentum](momentum.md) fluxes and must not be discarded when a tail has nonzero [added mass](added-mass.md). If both endpoint added masses vanish, total lateral body-plus-fluid [momentum](momentum.md) is conserved; a finite-mass body cannot then finish with zero relative crossflow and a new nonzero transverse [velocity](velocity.md).
