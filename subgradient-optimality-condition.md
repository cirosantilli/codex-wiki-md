# Subgradient optimality condition

↑ **Parent:** [Subgradient](subgradient.md)

A proper convex function $f$ is minimized at $x$ exactly when $0\in\partial f(x)$. For a differentiable convex term $g$ and another convex term $h$, the [subdifferential sum rule](subdifferential-sum-rule.md) gives the condition $0\in\nabla g(x)+\partial h(x)$.
