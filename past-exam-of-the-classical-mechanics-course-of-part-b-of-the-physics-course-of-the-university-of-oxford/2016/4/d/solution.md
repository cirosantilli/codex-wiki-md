<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An autonomous [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) system with $n$ degrees of freedom is [Liouville integrable](../../../../integrable-hamiltonian-system.md) if it has $n$ conserved functions $I_1,\ldots,I_n$, including the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md), whose differentials are independent on a regular open set and which are in involution:

$$
\boxed{dI_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dI_n\ne0,\qquad\{I_i,I_j\}=0.}
$$

The equations can then be reduced locally to quadratures. The statement about action-angle coordinates on invariant tori additionally requires compact connected regular common level sets; integrable unbounded motion need not be periodic or lie on a torus.
