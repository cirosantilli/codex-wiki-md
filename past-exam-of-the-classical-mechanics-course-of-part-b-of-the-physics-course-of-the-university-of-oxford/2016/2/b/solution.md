<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $m_1=m_2$, the explicitly time-dependent coefficient becomes one. This restores [time-translation symmetry](../../../../time-translation-symmetry.md), and the total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) becomes conserved. No further analogous restoration occurs merely from setting $m_3$ equal to them: the three arbitrary interaction functions still distinguish their arguments and particle labels.

If the question counts the extra **ordinary unweighted [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md)** of the $x$ coordinate labels, the conventional generator count is as follows. For $m_1=m_2\ne m_3$, [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) of $(x_1,x_2)$ preserve both the kinetic and oscillator terms, so one ordinarily displayed [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) charge joins the restored [energy](../../../../energy-split.md): two displayed generators beyond the elementary list. For three equal masses, the full ordinary [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) group of $(x_1,x_2,x_3)$ has three generators; together with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) this gives four, or two more than in the preceding equal-pair case.

There is a necessary qualification to interpreting those numbers as genuinely new independent conservation laws. The weighted charges in [solution](../a/solution.md) deform continuously to those same ordinary [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) charges at equality. For example $\mathcal J_{12}$ is conserved for every positive $m_1,m_2$ and becomes $x_1p_{x_2}-x_2p_{x_1}$ when the masses agree. Thus

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\text{Restricted ordinary-coordinate generator count: }2\text{ and }4.\\
\text{New generic energy conservation: }1\text{ at }m_1=m_2;\quad
0\text{ further at }m_3=m_1.
\end{gathered}}
$$

The restriction must be stated if using the first count. Equal [mass](../../../../mass.md) does not create three previously absent independent oscillator integrals. If $\lambda=0$, [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) conservation was already present; special choices of the interaction functions can add further symmetries and are excluded by the generic assumption.
