# Ground state of a finite square well

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

For $V(x)=0$ on $0<x<a$ and $V(x)=V_w>0$ outside, the lowest [bound state](bound-state.md) is even about $a/2$, has no nodes, and has $0<E<V_w$. Write $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_w-E)}/\hbar$. The interior [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is proportional to $\cos[k(x-a/2)]$; the exterior tails decay exponentially. [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md) gives the displayed condition. Its lowest root has $0<ka<\pi$, so $E<\pi^2\hbar^2/(2ma^2)$, the [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) energy. As $V_w$ increases, the penetration length $1/\kappa$ tends to zero and that limiting energy is approached from below.
