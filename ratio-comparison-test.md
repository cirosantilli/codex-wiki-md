# Ratio comparison test

↑ **Parent:** [Comparison test for series](comparison-test-for-series.md)

For positive [sequences](sequence.md) $(a_n),(b_n)$, suppose $a_{n+1}/a_n\leq b_{n+1}/b_n$ eventually. Then $a_n/b_n$ is eventually nonincreasing, so $a_n\leq Cb_n$ beyond a fixed index. The [comparison test for series](comparison-test-for-series.md) proves convergence of $\sum a_n$ whenever $\sum b_n$ converges. Reversing the ratio inequality gives an eventual lower bound $a_n\geq cb_n$ and hence divergence when the positive comparison [series](series-mathematics.md) diverges. The initial finite set of terms does not affect either conclusion.
