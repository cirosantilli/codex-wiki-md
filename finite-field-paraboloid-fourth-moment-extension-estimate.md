# Finite-field paraboloid fourth-moment extension estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete paraboloid](discrete-paraboloid.md)

For the three-dimensional [discrete paraboloid](discrete-paraboloid.md) over an odd prime field, the surface transform away from zero has magnitude at most $p^{-1}$, by completing squares and evaluating [Quadratic Gauss sums](quadratic-gauss-sum.md). The adjoint-composition convolution kernel is the transform of the surface measure. Subtract its unit mass at the origin. Convolution by the remainder has $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ norm at most $p^{-1}$ and $\ell^2\to\ell^2$ norm at most $p$. The [Riesz-Thorin interpolation theorem](riesz-thorin-theorem.md) gives $\ell^{4/3}\to\ell^4$ norm at most one; adding the identity kernel gives at most two. The adjoint-composition identity and duality therefore give the displayed extension constant for normalized surface measure and ambient counting measure. This argument works for either quadratic character of minus one.
