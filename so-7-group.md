<h1 id="so-7-group">SO(7) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

The compact [group](group-split.md) of real seven-by-seven [orthogonal matrices](orthogonal-matrix.md) with [determinant](determinant.md) one. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has [B3 root system](b3-root-system.md). Its simply connected double cover is the [Spin group](spin-group.md) $\operatorname{Spin}(7)$; the eight-dimensional [spin representation](spin-representation.md) belongs to that cover and does not descend to [SO(7)](so-7-group.md).
