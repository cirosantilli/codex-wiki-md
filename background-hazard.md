# Background hazard

↑ **Parent:** [Relative survival](relative-survival.md)

In [relative survival](relative-survival.md), the [background hazard](background-hazard.md) is an individual's expected mortality hazard in a suitable reference population. Its dependence on age, sex and calendar time can be obtained from population life tables. In a heterogeneous cohort, estimating a common [excess hazard](excess-hazard.md) requires averaging these known [background hazards](background-hazard.md) over the current [risk set](risk-set.md), rather than over the original cohort.
