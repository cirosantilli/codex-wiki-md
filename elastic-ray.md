# Elastic ray

↑ **Parent:** [Ray tracing](ray-tracing.md)

In a smooth isotropic medium, an [elastic ray](elastic-ray.md) is the path followed by a local [wavefront](wavefront.md) normal, with the appropriate P or S [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c$. Arclength $s$ gives the displayed trajectory and travel-time equations. Its neighboring ray paths determine geometrical spreading, while the [eikonal equation](eikonal-equation.md) determines the normals. A [caustic](wave-caustic.md) occurs where the neighboring paths focus and the elementary ray [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) description ceases to be uniform.
