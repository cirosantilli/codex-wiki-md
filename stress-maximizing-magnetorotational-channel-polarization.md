# Stress-maximizing magnetorotational channel polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-diffusivity magnetorotational channel](equal-diffusivity-magnetorotational-channel.md)

For complex velocity amplitudes $v_x=v_y=V$ and magnetic amplitudes $b_x=-b_y=B$ in Alfvén-speed units, phase averaging gives the displayed stress. For fixed horizontal velocity energy, equal radial and azimuthal components maximize their positive [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md). Opposite equal magnetic components maximize the outward tension contribution of the [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md), since $-B_xB_y$ is then positive. These conclusions follow from $2|ab|\leq |a|^2+|b|^2$. The vertical channel transports angular momentum outward and extracts energy from decreasing angular velocity.
