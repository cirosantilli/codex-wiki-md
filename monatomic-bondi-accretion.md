# Monatomic Bondi accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md)

For isentropic spherical accretion with this [adiabatic index](heat-capacity-ratio.md), define $\lambda=\dot M c_\infty^3/(4\pi G^2M^2\rho_\infty)$ and $x=rc_\infty^2/(GM)$. The steady-flow equation is $\sqrt\lambda[\mathcal M^{3/2}/2+3\mathcal M^{-1/2}/2]=1+3x/2$. The bracket has minimum two at Mach one. Reaching arbitrarily small positive radii therefore requires $\lambda\le1/4$. For smaller rates the outer-rest branch remains subsonic; at the maximal rate it approaches Mach one only as $r\to0$, not at a finite [sonic point](sonic-point.md). Supersonic outer branches fail the specified resting finite-density condition, and larger rates terminate at a positive minimum radius. An additional inner boundary prescription is needed to select a unique rate among the globally defined subsonic solutions.
