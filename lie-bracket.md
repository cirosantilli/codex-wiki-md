# Lie bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_bracket)

The Lie bracket is the product in a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). For matrix Lie algebras it is the [commutator](commutator.md) $[X,Y]=XY-YX$.
