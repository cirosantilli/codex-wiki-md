# Transit dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit depth](exoplanet-transit-depth.md)

Constant extra light makes an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md) appear shallower. If the host [star](star.md) supplies out-of-transit [observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md) $F_\star$ and an unresolved source supplies $F_c$, let $D=F_c/F_\star$. Then $\delta_{\rm obs}=\delta_{\rm host}/(1+D)$. For $D=1$, a true [exoplanet transit depth](exoplanet-transit-depth.md) of $0.010$ appears as $0.005$; ignoring [transit dilution](transit-dilution.md) gives a [radius](radius.md) ratio $\sqrt{0.005}\simeq0.07071$ instead of $0.10$, under the same uniform-disc assumptions.
