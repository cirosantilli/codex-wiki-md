# Cardinality of a vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

For a nonzero [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with an infinite [field](field.md) or infinite [basis](basis.md), [cardinality](cardinality.md) is the displayed maximum, since vectors are finite [linear combinations](linear-combination.md) of [basis vectors](basis-vector.md). Over a [finite field](finite-field.md), infinite underlying size forces infinite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). Over a countably infinite [field](field.md), every positive finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is already countably infinite. The [theory](first-order-theory.md) of infinite underlying spaces is therefore categorical in every infinite cardinal for a [finite field](finite-field.md), but only in uncountable cardinals for a countably infinite [field](field.md).
