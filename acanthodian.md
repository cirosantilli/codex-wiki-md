# Acanthodian

↑ **Parent:** [Gnathostome](gnathostome.md)

A member of a traditionally recognized assemblage of Palaeozoic spiny jawed fishes. Many analyses resolve these taxa as a succession on the [Chondrichthyes](chondrichthyes.md) stem rather than a separate complete [clade](clade.md).
