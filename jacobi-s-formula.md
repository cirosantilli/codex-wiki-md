<h1 id="jacobi-s-formula">Jacobi's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative of the determinant](derivative-of-the-determinant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi's_formula)

For an arbitrary square [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, the [derivative of the determinant](derivative-of-the-determinant.md) in direction $H$ is the displayed expression. Differentiating the [determinant](determinant.md)'s [multilinear](multilinear-map.md) expansion gives the sum of entries of $H$ weighted by their cofactors, equal to the displayed [trace](matrix-trace.md). If $A$ is invertible, $\operatorname{adj}(A)=(\det A)A^{-1}$ gives $(\det A)\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}H)$, and division by $\det A$ gives the [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) for the logarithmic [derivative](derivative.md). The adjugate expression remains valid at singular [matrices](matrix.md).
