# Stellar angular diameter from a visibility envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform-disk stellar visibility](uniform-disk-stellar-visibility.md)

For a uniform circular stellar disc, the first zero of $J_1$ at $x=3.831706\ldots$ gives $Y_0=1.21967\lambda/\alpha$ when $\alpha$ is full [angular diameter](angular-diameter.md). In contrast a one-dimensional top-hat angular profile of full width $\alpha$ has a sinc envelope with $Y_0=\lambda/\alpha$. Diameter estimates therefore require a brightness-profile convention. A binary additionally multiplies the envelope by its separation-dependent [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) factor.
