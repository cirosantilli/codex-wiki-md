# Fermi velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md)

The [group velocity](group-velocity.md) of an excitation evaluated on the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). For an isotropic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\epsilon(p)$, $v_F=(d\epsilon/dp)_{p_F}$, where $p_F$ is the [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md).
