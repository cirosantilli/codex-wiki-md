# Distinguished polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass preparation theorem](weierstrass-preparation-theorem.md)

A distinguished polynomial over $\mathbb Z_p$ is monic and has every non-leading coefficient divisible by $p$. The [Weierstrass preparation theorem](weierstrass-preparation-theorem.md) expresses any nonzero element of $\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$ as a power of $p$ times a unit times a distinguished polynomial.
