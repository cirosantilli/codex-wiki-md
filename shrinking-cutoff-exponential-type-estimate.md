# Shrinking-cutoff exponential-type estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a compactly supported distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-compactly-supported-distribution.md)

Let a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) have support in a [compact convex set](compact-convex-set.md) $K$. Fixed neighborhood cutoffs initially give an exponential bound larger than the [support function](support-function.md) $H_K$. Cutoffs of width $\varepsilon$ cost powers of $\varepsilon^{-1}$ in derivative estimates but enlarge the exponential by only $e^{C\varepsilon|\operatorname{Im}z|}$. Choosing $\varepsilon=(1+|z|)^{-1}$ absorbs the cutoff cost into a polynomial and retains the exact exponential $e^{H_K(\operatorname{Im}z)}$.
