# H-alpha

↑ **Parent:** [Balmer series](balmer-series.md)

The [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) Balmer emission line from the transition between principal quantum numbers three and two. Recombination-line emission from gas photoionized by short-lived massive stars makes it a star-formation tracer, subject to dust attenuation, escape of ionizing [photons](photon.md) and contamination by other excitation sources.
