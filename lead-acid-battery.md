# Lead-acid battery

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead–acid_battery)

A rechargeable aqueous sulfuric-acid cell using lead and lead dioxide [electrodes](electrode.md). Discharge converts both active materials to lead [sulfate](sulfate.md); charging reverses that conversion subject to corrosion, [gas](gas.md) evolution and morphology limitations.
