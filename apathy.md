# Apathy

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive neurology](cognitive-neurology.md)

Reduced goal-directed activity or [motivation](motivation.md) relative to a person’s previous functioning. Initiation, valuation, effort and emotional experience require separate assessment; inactivity is not automatically [anhedonia](anhedonia.md) or motor incapacity.
