# Vieta formulas for a cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Vieta's formulas](vieta-s-formulas.md)

Take the stipulated [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $-2,1,5$. Their first three [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) are

$$
e_1=-2+1+5=4,\qquad
e_2=(-2)(1)+(-2)(5)+(1)(5)=-7,\qquad
e_3=(-2)(1)(5)=-10.
$$

Their [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{(x+2)(x-1)(x-5)=x^3-4x^2-7x+10.}
$$

The constant [coefficient](coefficient.md) is $10$, while the [product](product-of-numbers.md) of the three [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) is $-10$: for a [cubic polynomial](cubic-polynomial.md), these have opposite signs. Expanding the factors directly verifies the same [polynomial identity](polynomial-identity.md).

**[Figure 3](#image-the-roots-2-1-5-determine-the-signed-coefficients-of-a-cubic). The roots $-2,1,5$ determine the signed coefficients of a cubic**. An original exact calculation: the graph shows the stipulated [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), and the ledger groups choices of one, two or three root positions into [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md). The alternating factors $(-1)^k$ convert these values into [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) [coefficients](coefficient.md).

For the nonmonic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $-3x^3+12x^2+21x-30$, the same ratios give [sum](sum.md) $-12/(-3)=4$, pairwise-product sum $21/(-3)=-7$, and [product](product-of-numbers.md) $-(-30)/(-3)=-10$. It is $-3$ times the [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) above, so its [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) are the same. Checking only the [sum](sum.md) and [product](product-of-numbers.md) would leave the middle [elementary symmetric polynomial](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) unchecked; all three are needed to recover a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) three.
