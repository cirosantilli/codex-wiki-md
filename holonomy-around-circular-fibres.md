# Holonomy around circular fibres

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomy](holonomy.md)

For the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $dr^2+f(r)^2d\phi^2$, where $f>0$ and $\phi$ has period $2\pi$, [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) around $r=r_0$ acts on the [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) components of a [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) by a rotation through $-2\pi f'(r_0)$. Indeed, those components obey $u'=f'(r_0)v$ and $v'=-f'(r_0)u$ as functions of $\phi$. Thus all [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md) return to themselves exactly when $f'(r_0)\in\mathbb Z$. The integer criterion concerns [holonomy](holonomy.md) and is stronger than local flatness.
