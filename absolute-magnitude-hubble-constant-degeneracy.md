<h1 id="absolute-magnitude-hubble-constant-degeneracy">Absolute magnitude–Hubble constant degeneracy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type Ia supernova cosmology](type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md)

In [Type Ia supernova cosmology](type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md), the predicted [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md) depends on the common [absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md) $M_0$ and the [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md) only through $\beta=M_0-5\log_{10}(H_0/H_{\rm ref})$, after choosing a fixed reference $H_{\rm ref}>0$. A flat [improper prior](improper-prior.md) on $M_0$ makes the likelihood integrated over $M_0$ independent of $H_0$, by translation of the integration variable. If the other prior factors are independent and the posterior is proper, the marginal posterior of $H_0$ equals its prior. An external calibration of $M_0$ can break this lack of [identifiability](identifiability.md).
