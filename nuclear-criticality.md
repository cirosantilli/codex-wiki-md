# Nuclear criticality

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A self-sustaining neutron balance in which production offsets absorption and leakage. In a steady [one-group neutron diffusion equation](one-group-neutron-diffusion-equation.md), criticality is a compatibility condition between material parameters and the allowed spatial mode.
