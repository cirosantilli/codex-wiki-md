# Cell fate

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md)

[Cell fate](cell-fate.md) is the developmental outcome of a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) or its descendants in a specified setting. What it normally becomes and what it can become after an experimental intervention are different questions. The [intestinal nucleus transfer experiment](intestinal-nucleus-transfer-experiment.md) tests potential after [cellular reprogramming](cellular-reprogramming.md), rather than directly tracing the normal [cell fate](cell-fate.md) of the donor.
