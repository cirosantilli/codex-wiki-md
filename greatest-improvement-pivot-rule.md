# Greatest improvement pivot rule

↑ **Parent:** [Simplex method](simplex-method.md)

For a feasible [simplex dictionary](simplex-dictionary.md), let $r_j>0$ be a nonbasic variable's improving [reduced cost](reduced-cost.md) and let $\theta_j$ be its largest feasible increase from the [simplex ratio test](simplex-ratio-test.md). The [greatest improvement pivot rule](greatest-improvement-pivot-rule.md) chooses an entering variable maximizing $r_j\theta_j$. It measures the improvement over the entire edge, whereas the [Dantzig pivot rule](dantzig-pivot-rule.md) maximizes only $r_j$.
