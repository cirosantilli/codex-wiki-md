# Principal congruence modular curve genus

↑ **Parent:** [Genus formula for a modular curve](genus-formula-for-a-modular-curve.md)

For $N\geq3$, the [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md) has projective index $\mu=\frac12N^3\prod_{p\mid N}(1-p^{-2})$, no effective elliptic stabilizers, and all [cusp widths](width-of-a-cusp.md) equal to $N$. Hence the number of cusps is $\mu/N$, and the [genus formula for a modular curve](genus-formula-for-a-modular-curve.md) gives the displayed formula. The exceptional levels one and two both have genus zero; the full genus-zero list is $N=1,2,3,4,5$. Level six has genus one and every larger level has positive genus.
