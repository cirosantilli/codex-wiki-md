# Polygenic trait

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

A [phenotypic trait](phenotypic-trait.md) influenced by variation at multiple [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md). Absence of a single shared large-effect region does not exclude repeated evolution through many small inherited effects.
