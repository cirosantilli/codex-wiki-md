# Functional mean test

↑ **Parent:** [Functional data analysis](functional-data-analysis.md)

A functional mean test tests whether the mean of a [Hilbert-space-valued random variable](hilbert-space-valued-random-variable.md) is zero. The squared-norm statistic $n\lVert\overline X\rVert^2$ has a weighted chi-squared limit under the null, while an FPCA test standardizes and truncates the coordinates.
