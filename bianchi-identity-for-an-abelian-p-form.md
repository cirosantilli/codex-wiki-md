# Bianchi identity for an Abelian p-form

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian p-form gauge field](abelian-p-form-gauge-field.md)

For an [Abelian p-form gauge field](abelian-p-form-gauge-field.md) with local field strength $F=dA$, the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) identity $d^2=0$ gives $dF=0$. In a first-order duality formulation, this identity can be enforced with a Lagrange multiplier, whose elimination restores the local gauge potential.
