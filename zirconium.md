# Zirconium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)

[Zirconium](zirconium.md) is a group-4 [metal](metal.md); Zr(IV) is formally $d^0$. Its cationic alkyl complexes can catalyze [polymerization](polymerization.md) through [alkene](alkene.md) [migratory insertion](migratory-insertion.md).
