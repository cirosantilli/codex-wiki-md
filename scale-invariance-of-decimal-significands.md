# Scale invariance of decimal significands

↑ **Parent:** [Benford law](benford-law.md)

A decimal significand lies in $[1,10)$, obtained by removing the integer power of ten from a positive number. Multiplication by a constant rotates the fractional logarithm modulo one. Invariance under every such rotation makes the fractional logarithm uniform and hence implies [Benford law](benford-law.md). This is an invariant significand distribution, not a proper scale-invariant distribution for the entire positive number.
