# Integrin

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrin)

An [integrin](integrin.md) is a transmembrane adhesion receptor linking extracellular ligands to intracellular signalling and [cytoskeleton](cytoskeleton.md) interactions. Integrin-dependent contacts transmit force in many forms of [cell migration](cell-migration.md); their necessity depends on the physical environment.
