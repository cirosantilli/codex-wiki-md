# Percentage point

↑ **Parent:** [Percentage](percentage.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percentage_point)

A [percentage point](percentage-point.md) is one hundredth on a [probability](probability.md) or proportion scale. A change from $20\%$ to $25\%$ is $5$ [percentage points](percentage-point.md), while the relative increase is $(25-20)/20=25\%$. These answer different questions and have different denominators.
