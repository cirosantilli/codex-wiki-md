# Gyroradius

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

The [gyroradius](gyroradius.md) is the [radius](radius.md) of the transverse [circular motion](circular-motion.md) of a [charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md). In nonrelativistic [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md), with zero [electric field](electric-field.md) and no other [forces](force.md), it is $mv_\perp/(|q|B)$, where $v_\perp$ is the [speed](speed.md) perpendicular to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). The parallel component of [velocity](velocity.md) changes the advance of the [helix](helix.md), rather than its transverse [radius](radius.md).
