# Rigid translation from a momentum term in the Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Particle in a ring](particle-in-a-ring.md)

For a [free particle](free-particle.md) on the line or a circle, $H_0=P^2/(2m)$ commutes with the [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md) $P$. The commuting [operator exponentials](operator-exponential.md) in the title therefore factorize. Since $P=-i\hbar\partial_x$, the factor $e^{isPt/\hbar}=e^{st\partial_x}$ translates a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) to $f(x+st)$, as is verified on each [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md). Thus the [probability density](probability-density.md) translates with velocity $-s$ relative to the $H_0$ evolution. A real constant $C$ changes only the [global phase](global-phase.md). In particular a superposition in one degenerate [energy eigenspace](energy-eigenspace.md) translates rigidly under the added term $-sP$.
