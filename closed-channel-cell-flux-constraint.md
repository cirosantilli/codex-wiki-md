# Closed-channel cell-flux constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Cell conservation in a swimming suspension](cell-conservation-in-a-swimming-suspension.md)

A genuinely closed steady channel with impermeable sidewalls and ends must have zero integrated axial cell flux through every section, not only zero fluid flux. In the bulk phototactic ansatz $n'=\beta n$, $\omega_y=-w'$, and $\langle p_z\rangle=-K_1w'/(2D_r)$, the axial flux is $[1+V_s\beta K_1/(2D_r)]\int nw\,dx$. Meanwhile the momentum equation, zero fluid flux and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give $g'\int nw\,dx=-\mu\int(w')^2dx$. Thus a nontrivial bulk profile is generally incompatible with exact closed-end cell conservation unless the prefactor vanishes. Additional axial concentration variation is needed; neglecting end regions does not itself remove this global constraint.
