# Kaolinite

↑ **Parent:** [Clay mineral](clay-mineral.md)

A [clay mineral](clay-mineral.md) with composition $\mathrm{Al_2Si_2O_5(OH)_4}$. Relative to [gibbsite](gibbsite.md), its greater silica content allows higher dissolved silica [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) to favor its formation, as quantified by the appropriate [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md).
