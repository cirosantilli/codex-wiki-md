# Volatile organic compound

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

An organic compound readily present in the gas phase under the conditions of interest. In polluted-air chemistry, its oxidation can generate [peroxy radicals](peroxy-radical.md) which convert [nitrogen monoxide](nitrogen-monoxide.md) to [nitrogen dioxide](nitrogen-dioxide.md) without consuming [ozone](ozone.md). Volatility alone does not determine reactivity or [ozone](ozone.md) yield.
