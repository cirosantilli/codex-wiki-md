# Lithospheric rejuvenation

↑ **Parent:** [Lithosphere](lithosphere.md)

Thermal or chemical modification that changes an older [lithosphere](lithosphere.md)'s properties, potentially reducing its strength or effective thickness. [Magma](magma.md) transport, [mantle plumes](mantle-plume.md) and other [mantle flow](mantle-flow.md) can contribute. A younger thermal signature does not automatically imply complete replacement of the old [crust](planetary-crust.md) or [lithospheric mantle](lithospheric-mantle.md).
