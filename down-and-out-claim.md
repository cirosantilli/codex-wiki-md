# Down-and-out claim

↑ **Parent:** [Barrier option](barrier-option.md)

A [down-and-out claim](down-and-out-claim.md) expires worthless if the [stock](stock.md) reaches a lower barrier before maturity. A matching [down-and-in claim](down-and-in-claim.md) plus a [down-and-out claim](down-and-out-claim.md) pays the ordinary terminal claim on every path; this is in-out parity.
