# Cubic map period-two branches

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system.md)

The displayed map has a symmetric [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) $\{\pm\sqrt{\mu+1}\}$ for $\mu>-1$, with multiplier $(-2\mu-3)^2>1$. For $\mu>2$, put $s_\pm=(\mu\pm\sqrt{\mu^2-4})/2$. The two additional period-two orbits are $\{\sqrt{s_+},\sqrt{s_-}\}$ and their negatives. Their multiplier is $9-2\mu^2$, so they are stable for $2<\mu<\sqrt5$ and undergo a [period-doubling bifurcation](period-doubling-bifurcation.md) at $\sqrt5$. At $\mu=2$ these points coincide with fixed points, not genuine two-cycles.
