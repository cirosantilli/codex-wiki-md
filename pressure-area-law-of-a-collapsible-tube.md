# Pressure-area law of a collapsible tube

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md)

The [pressure-area law of a collapsible tube](pressure-area-law-of-a-collapsible-tube.md) describes transverse mechanical equilibrium of a deformable tube wall. On a stable branch $\widetilde P'(\alpha)>0$. Linearizing mass and momentum conservation about a stationary uniform tube gives the displayed [wave speed](wave-speed.md). External [pressure](pressure.md) is prescribed separately and is not part of the elastic wall stiffness.
