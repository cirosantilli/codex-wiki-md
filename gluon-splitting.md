# Gluon splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Gluon](gluon.md)

A [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) branching in which a [gluon](gluon.md) produces a [quark](quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) pair or two [gluons](gluon.md). Quark-pair production supplies a perturbative source of [sea quarks](sea-quark.md). Its rates depend on scale and on the allowed daughter [quark masses](quark-mass.md).
