# First uncountable ordinal of an inner model

↑ **Parent:** [Projectively well-ordered inner model](projectively-well-ordered-inner-model.md)

For an [inner model](inner-model.md) $M$, the ordinal $\omega_1^M$ is the least ordinal that $M$ regards as uncountable. Equivalently, it is the supremum of the order types of the [well-order codes](well-order-code.md) belonging to $M$. It can be countable in the ambient universe.
