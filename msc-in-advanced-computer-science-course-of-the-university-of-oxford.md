# MSc in advanced computer science course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-advanced-computer-science).

At least six options. The [current teaching list](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/teaching/MSCinCS/) distinguishes written examinations from mini-projects and sets out the specialist streams. Shared examined modules include [Automata, logic and games course of the University of Oxford](automata-logic-and-games-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), [Categories, proofs and processes course of the University of Oxford](categories-proofs-and-processes-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) and [Quantum processes and computation course of the University of Oxford](quantum-processes-and-computation-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md). Independent supervised project during Trinity term and the summer.
