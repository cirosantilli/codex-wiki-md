# First isomorphism theorem for modules

↑ **Parent:** [Module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md)

For a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $f:M\to N$, the map $m+\ker f\mapsto f(m)$ is a [module isomorphism](module-isomorphism.md) $M/\ker f\to\operatorname{Im}f$. The formula is well-defined because two representatives differ by an element of the [kernel of a module homomorphism](kernel-of-a-module-homomorphism.md) exactly when their images agree; the same observation proves injectivity, and surjectivity onto the image follows by its definition.
