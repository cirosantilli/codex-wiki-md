# Two-stage rocket with proportional fuel and body masses

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

For zero initial [velocity](velocity.md), body [masses](mass.md) $kM,(1-k)M$ and fuel [masses](mass.md) $km_0,(1-k)m_0$, the [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) gives the displayed final second-stage [speed](speed.md) after burning the first-stage fuel, discarding its body without impulse, and burning the second-stage fuel. It assumes $M>0$ and $0\le k<1$. The speed exceeds the single-stage value when $0<k<1$ and fuel is positive. Its limit as $k\to1^-$ concerns a surviving second stage of vanishing [mass](mass.md); at $k=1$ no such stage exists.
