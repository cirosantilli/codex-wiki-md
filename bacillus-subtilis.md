# Bacillus subtilis

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacillus_subtilis)

A bacterial species that is a natural host of [Bacillus virus phi29](bacillus-virus-phi29.md). The host identity matters when distinguishing that [bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) from unrelated [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) [bacteriophages](bacteriophage.md).
