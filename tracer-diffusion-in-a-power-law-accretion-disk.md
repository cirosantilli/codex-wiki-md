# Tracer diffusion in a power-law accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

In a steady [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) with $\Sigma=\Sigma_0R^{-2}$, $\nu=\nu_0R^2$, and tracer diffusivity $k=\zeta\nu$, the trace [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) concentration obeys the displayed constant-coefficient [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) in logarithmic radius. Contaminant mass is proportional to $C\,dY$, because $2\pi R\sigma\,dR=2\pi\Sigma_0 C\,dY$. A radial impulse $C_0\delta(R-R_0)$ therefore has logarithmic amplitude $C_0/R_0$ and evolves by the [Gaussian heat kernel](gaussian-heat-kernel.md) centered at $\ln R_0-vt$. The full-line solution idealizes radii far from the disk boundaries.
