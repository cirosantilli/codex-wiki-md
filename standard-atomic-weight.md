# Standard atomic weight

↑ **Parent:** [Relative atomic mass](relative-atomic-mass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_atomic_weight)

A recommended [atomic weight](relative-atomic-mass.md) applicable to normal materials, rather than the result for every possible enriched sample. The tabulated [neon](neon.md) value $20.1797(6)$ and the arithmetic result above need not agree in every last displayed digit. Some [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) have intervals of [standard atomic weight](standard-atomic-weight.md) because their [isotopic abundances](isotopic-abundance.md) vary across normal materials.[Standard Atomic Weights 2024](standard-atomic-weights-2024.md)
