# Homology orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Turaev torsion](turaev-torsion.md)

A homology orientation chooses an orientation of the real vector space $\bigoplus_iH_i(M;\mathbb R)$. It fixes the sign in refined [Turaev torsion](turaev-torsion.md).
