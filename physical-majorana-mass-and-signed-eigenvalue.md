# Physical Majorana mass and signed eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Majorana mass term](majorana-mass-term.md)

A real symmetric [one-family Dirac-Majorana mass matrix](one-family-dirac-majorana-mass-matrix.md) can have a negative algebraic [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). A rephasing of the corresponding Weyl field puts its physical [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) at the absolute value. Relative Majorana phases can still affect coherent lepton-number-violating amplitudes.
