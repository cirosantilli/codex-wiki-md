# Baer criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Injective module](injective-module.md)

This criterion characterizes an [injective module](injective-module.md) using extension from ideals. A left $R$-module is injective exactly when every homomorphism from a left ideal of $R$ extends to a homomorphism from $R$.
