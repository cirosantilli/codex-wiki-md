<h1 id="thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate">Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md)

Neglecting density-gradient [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in the stationary [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) gives $n=(\mu-V)_+/g$ for repulsive interactions. For a radial harmonic trap $V=m\omega_r^2r^2/2$, this is $n=n_0(1-r^2/R_r^2)_+$ with $R_r=\sqrt{2gn_0/(m\omega_r^2)}$. Hard boundaries can truncate this natural support. The approximation fails in vortex cores and boundary healing regions and requires a cloud much larger than its [healing length](healing-length.md).
