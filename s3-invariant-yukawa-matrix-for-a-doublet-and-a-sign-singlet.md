# S3-invariant Yukawa matrix for a doublet and a sign singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa matrix](yukawa-matrix.md)

Let the left and right [fermions](fermion.md) transform in $D\oplus\varepsilon$, where $D$ is the two-dimensional [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) of the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_3$ and $\varepsilon$ is the [sign representation](sign-representation.md). Let the [scalar field](scalar-field.md) be a [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). In matching orthonormal [bases](basis.md), invariance of $\bar\psi_L\lambda\psi_R$ says that $\lambda$ commutes with $D\oplus\varepsilon$. The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) forces each diagonal irreducible block to be scalar and both mixed blocks to vanish, giving the displayed form. Thus after a [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $h$, the physical [fermion mass matrix](fermion-mass-matrix.md) has [singular values](singular-value.md) $|ha|,|ha|,|hb|$: unbroken $S_3$ enforces a degenerate doublet. In a common nonorthonormal [basis](basis.md) with invariant [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $K$, the doublet coefficient is $aK$; canonical normalization recovers $aI_2$.
