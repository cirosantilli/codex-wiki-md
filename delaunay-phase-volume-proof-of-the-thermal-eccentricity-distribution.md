# Delaunay phase-volume proof of the thermal eccentricity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal eccentricity distribution](thermal-eccentricity-distribution.md)

For bound [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md), the [Delaunay variables](delaunay-variables.md) obey $0<G<L$, $-G<H<G$, and three periodic angles. A [canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md) preserves [phase space](phase-space.md) volume. At fixed $L$, integrating $H$ supplies the measure $2G\,dG$. Since $G=L\sqrt{1-e^2}$, the fraction with [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) less than $e$ is $L^{-2}\int_{L\sqrt{1-e^2}}^L2G\,dG=e^2$. Thus the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $2e$, independently of any normalizable energy-only [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md). A [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) is unnecessary. Even the time-dependent density $F(E(L))[1+\eta\cos(l-n(L)t)]$, $|\eta|<1$, has this same marginal because integrating the [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md) removes its oscillatory part; an eccentricity marginal alone does not imply equilibrium.
