# Linearity of the Shapley value

↑ **Parent:** [Shapley value](shapley-value.md)

The [Shapley value](shapley-value.md) is a linear map on real characteristic [functions](function-split.md) with zero value at the empty [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md). In each player [permutation](permutation.md), the [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) to $av+bw$ is $a$ times the marginal to $v$ plus $b$ times the marginal to $w$. Taking their [expectations](expected-value-split.md) proves the formula for arbitrary real $a,b$. This directly proves real homogeneity as well as [cooperative-game additivity](additivity-of-a-cooperative-game-value.md).
