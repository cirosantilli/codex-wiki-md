# Cellular differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental integration](developmental-integration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_differentiation)

[Cell differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md) is the acquisition of a specialized [cell state](cell-state.md), involving changes in structure, function and [gene expression](gene-expression.md). A [neuron](neuron.md) and a [red blood cell](red-blood-cell.md) illustrate different outcomes: signaling and [oxygen](oxygen.md) transport. Different functions usually do not require a different inherited [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence for each function. The shared-[genome](genome.md) principle concerns nucleated precursors and most nucleated descendants: a mature mammalian [red blood cell](red-blood-cell.md) has lost its [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) and [mitochondria](mitochondria.md), so it is not an example of two mature [biological cells](biological-cell.md) both retaining the same nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). [Biology 2e, §40.2, Red Blood Cells: specialization and absence of a nucleus and mitochondria at mammalian maturity](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

The puzzle is how a shared set of [genes](gene.md) supports different jobs. [Gene expression](gene-expression.md) changes which [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) and [proteins](protein.md) a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) produces, and in what amounts. A [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) can regulate several [genes](gene.md); different combinations of regulators can interpret the same signal differently. Stable regulatory patterns can persist through the [cell cycle](cell-cycle.md), providing memory of developmental history. Specialization therefore usually changes use of inherited information, rather than replacing the [genome](genome.md) wholesale. [Alberts et al., The Molecular Genetic Mechanisms That Create Specialized Cell Types, subsections The Expression of a Set of Genes Can Be Coordinated by a Single Protein and Stable Patterns of Gene Expression Can Be Transmitted to Daughter Cells, and Summary](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

Original schematic with three invented [genes](gene.md) H, A and B. Both nucleated [biological cells](biological-cell.md) retain all three [genes](gene.md); colored outputs indicate different illustrative expression patterns. These are not measured [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) or [protein](protein.md) amounts, named real [genes](gene.md), or a claim that real [gene expression](gene-expression.md) is simply on or off. The separate mammalian [red blood cell](red-blood-cell.md) note marks an important limit to the shared nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) comparison.

A small counting example separates sequence from activity. Suppose the same three toy [genes](gene.md) are present in every nucleated [biological cell](biological-cell.md), and independently assign each [gene](gene.md) an on/off state. There are $2^3=8$ possible expression profiles with no [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) replacement. The figure shows only two: H and A on versus H and B on. Real [gene expression](gene-expression.md) is graded and coordinated, so this count illustrates possible descriptions, not a prediction of eight actual cell types. The interesting consequence is that a stable [cell state](cell-state.md) can depend on how a shared record is used.

[Stem cells](stem-cell.md) combine self-renewal with production of differentiated descendants; [progenitor cells](progenitor-cell.md) generally have more restricted developmental options and renewal capacity. Neither term means that a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) must give rise to every cell type. [Terminal differentiation](terminal-differentiation.md) denotes advanced specialization within a lineage, often accompanied by exit from the [cell cycle](cell-cycle.md). [Cell fate](cell-fate.md) concerns the developmental outcome of a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) or its descendants, whereas [cell state](cell-state.md) describes its current properties. A temporary change in [gene expression](gene-expression.md) after [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md) is not, by itself, proof of a new [cell fate](cell-fate.md). [Anatomy and Physiology 2e, §3.6, Stem Cells and Differentiation: renewal, restricted differentiation potential and regulation of gene expression](anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others.md)
