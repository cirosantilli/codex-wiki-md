# Zero-free Schwarz lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md)

For a zero-free [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$, put $c=f(0)$ and $\alpha=-\log|c|$. A [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md) makes $-\log f$ have positive real part. The [Carathéodory derivative bound for the right half-plane](caratheodory-derivative-bound-for-the-right-half-plane.md) yields the displayed sharp bound. Equality functions are $f(z)=c\exp[-2\alpha\omega z/(1-\omega z)]$, $|\omega|=1$. The [Harnack inequality on the unit disk](harnack-inequality-on-the-unit-disk.md) also yields the sharp pointwise bounds $|c|^{(1+r)/(1-r)}\le|f(w)|\le|c|^{(1-r)/(1+r)}$ for $r=|w|$. Aligning $\omega w=\pm r$ attains the two endpoints.
