# Dissociation error of a doubly occupied bonding orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)

A normalized product of two identical bonding orbitals built from separated orthogonal centers has finite weight in configurations with both [electrons](electron.md) on one center. Repulsion penalizes that ionic weight even when hopping disappears. Mixing configurations can instead approach one [electron](electron.md) per center while preserving the required [spin](spin.md) and exchange symmetries.
