# Michael Faraday

↑ **Parent:** [List of chemists](list-of-chemists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Faraday)

A [chemist](chemist.md) whose route into experimental research began with a bookbinding apprenticeship (1805–1812). He became a laboratory assistant at the Royal Institution in 1813, its laboratory director in 1825, and Fullerian Professor of [chemistry](chemistry-split.md) from 1833 to 1867.[Biography: Ri positions and bookbinding apprenticeship; Fullerian chemistry professorship, 1833–1867](michael-faraday-1791-1867.md)
