# Choice blindness

↑ **Parent:** [Introspection illusion](introspection-illusion.md)

Failure to notice a mismatch between a choice and the outcome presented as that choice, sometimes followed by an explanation of the substituted outcome. Face-choice experiments provide an operational example; not every failure to detect a substitution has the same cause.[2005, abstract](failure-to-detect-mismatches-between-intention-and-outcome-in-a-simple-decision-task-by-petter-johansson-and-others.md)
