# Integral membrane protein

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_membrane_protein)

An [integral membrane protein](integral-membrane-protein.md) is firmly incorporated into a membrane, commonly through contact with the [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) interior of the [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). Extraction behavior is an operational distinction: it resists treatments that remove many [peripheral membrane proteins](peripheral-membrane-protein.md). [Transmembrane proteins](transmembrane-protein.md) are one class; integral does not mean spanning.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Membrane Proteins Can Be Associated with the Lipid Bilayer in Various Ways, Figure 10-17 and extraction-based distinction](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/membrane-proteins.md)
