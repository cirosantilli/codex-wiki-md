# de Rham theorem

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

The complex of smooth [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) resolves the constant complex sheaf by the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) and consists of fine sheaves. Its global cohomology therefore agrees with [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) of that constant sheaf. A good-cover [Čech-de Rham double complex](cech-de-rham-double-complex.md) gives an explicit comparison, used in [Čech-de Rham curvature descent](cech-de-rham-curvature-descent.md).
