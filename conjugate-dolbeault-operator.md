# Conjugate Dolbeault operator

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md)

The conjugate [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) is the type-$(1,0)$ component of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md): $\partial:\mathcal A^{p,q}\to\mathcal A^{p+1,q}$. [Complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) interchanges $\partial$ and $\bar\partial$. On a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), $d=\partial+\bar\partial$, $\partial^2=\bar\partial^2=0$, and $\partial\bar\partial+\bar\partial\partial=0$.
