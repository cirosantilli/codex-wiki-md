# Introspection

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive psychology](cognitive-psychology.md)

Examination or reporting of one's own mental states. A report of an experience, an accurate prediction of performance and an explanation of the process causing a response are different claims. Experiments can test them separately.[Distinction between reports and access to higher-order processes](telling-more-than-we-can-know-verbal-reports-on-mental-processes-by-richard-e-nisbett-and-timothy-d-wilson.md)
