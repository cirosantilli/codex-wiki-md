<h1 id="hilbert-s-syzygy-theorem">Hilbert's syzygy theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Free resolution](free-resolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_syzygy_theorem)

Every [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) with a grading over the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $S=k[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ has a finite graded [free resolution](free-resolution.md) of length at most $n$. The [Koszul resolution](koszul-resolution.md) of $k$ has length $n$, so $\operatorname{Tor}_i^S(M,k)=0$ for $i>n$. In a [minimal graded free resolution](minimal-graded-free-resolution.md), this [Tor functor](tor-functor.md) is $F_i/(X_1,\ldots,X_n)F_i$; the [graded Nakayama lemma](graded-nakayama-lemma.md) forces $F_i=0$ for $i>n$.
