# Differential scanning calorimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md)

[differential scanning calorimetry](differential-scanning-calorimetry.md) measures differences in heat flow between sample and reference during a controlled temperature scan. Transition peaks can reveal cooperative lipid-chain melting, with scan rate, composition and reversibility affecting interpretation.
