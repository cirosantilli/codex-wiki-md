# Kernel in a category

↑ **Parent:** [Pointed category](pointed-category.md)

The kernel of $f:A\to B$ is the universal morphism $k:K\to A$ satisfying $fk=0$. It is the [equalizer](equaliser.md) of $f$ and the zero morphism.
