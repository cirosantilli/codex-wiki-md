# Dry galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)

A dry galaxy merger joins galaxies with little cold gas, so dissipation and merger-triggered star formation are weak. Conservation of energy followed by [re-virialization](virial-theorem.md) can make repeated dry minor mergers increase a galaxy's radius faster than its mass.
