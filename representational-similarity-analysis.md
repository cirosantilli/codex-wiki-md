# Representational similarity analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate pattern analysis](multivariate-pattern-analysis.md)

Comparison of the pairwise relationships among measured or model response patterns. The same stimuli are presented to the systems being compared, and their representational dissimilarity matrices are related. A match can constrain representation without requiring one-to-one unit correspondence or establishing the underlying learning rule.
