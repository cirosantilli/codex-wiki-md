# Dielectric

↑ **Parent:** [Permittivity](permittivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dielectric)

A [dielectric](dielectric.md) is a material in which an [electric field](electric-field.md) produces [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) while transport of [electric charge](electric-charge.md) is sufficiently small for the intended use. An insulating solid in a [capacitor](capacitor.md) is an example; real [dielectrics](dielectric.md) can still have [dielectric loss](dielectric-loss.md) and nonzero [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md).
