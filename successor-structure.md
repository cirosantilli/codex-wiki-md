# Successor structure

↑ **Parent:** [First-order structure](first-order-structure.md)

The standard successor structure has the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) as domain, the [successor function](successor-function.md) $s(n)=n+1$, and the named element zero. Its complete [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) is the [theory of successor](theory-of-successor.md). Without addition, multiplication, or order, elements outside the zero-generated component are possible in other models of the same [complete theory](complete-theory.md).
