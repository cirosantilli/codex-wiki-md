# Proper mean lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Mean lifetime (physics)](mean-lifetime-physics.md)

The [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) of an unstable [particle](particle-physics.md) measured in its [rest frame](rest-frame.md), using elapsed [proper time](proper-time.md). In the [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) model, survival to [proper time](proper-time.md) $\tau$ has [probability](probability.md) $e^{-\tau/\tau_0}$. For constant [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma$, [time dilation](time-dilation.md) gives laboratory [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) $\gamma\tau_0$. A [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) is a statistical mean, rather than a fixed decay time.
