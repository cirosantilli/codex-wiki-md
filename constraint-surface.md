# Constraint surface

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

The common zero locus of constraint functions $C_a$ in [phase space](phase-space.md) is the constraint surface. A physical trajectory lies on this surface, but off-surface identities are often needed when analyzing a [constraint algebra](constraint-algebra.md).
