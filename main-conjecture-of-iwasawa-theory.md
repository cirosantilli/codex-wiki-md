# Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa theory](iwasawa-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_conjecture_of_Iwasawa_theory)

The classical Iwasawa main conjecture, now a theorem over $\mathbb Q$, identifies characteristic ideals of appropriate cyclotomic [Iwasawa modules](iwasawa-module.md) with ideals generated by [p-adic L-function](p-adic-l-function.md) power series. For a nontrivial even character in the p-ramified convention, $\operatorname{char}_\Lambda X_\chi=(g_\chi)$ with $g_\chi((1+p)^{1-s}-1)=L_p(\chi,s)$. The [Kummer reflection in Iwasawa theory](kummer-reflection-in-iwasawa-theory.md) accounts for the reflected odd class-group convention.
