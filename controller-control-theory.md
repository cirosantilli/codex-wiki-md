# Controller (control theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Control theory](control-theory-split.md)

A [controller](controller-control-theory.md) is the rule or mechanism that chooses a [control signal](control-input.md). An [open-loop control](open-loop-control.md) can depend on a precomputed schedule; a [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) also uses a [measured output](measured-output.md) and can have its own memory, as in [integral action](integral-action.md).
