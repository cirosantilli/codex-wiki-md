# Real closure

↑ **Parent:** [Real closed field](real-closed-field.md)

A real closure of an ordered [field](field.md) $K$ is a real closed algebraic extension whose order extends the chosen order of $K$. Existence is the [real closure theorem](real-closure-theorem.md); uniqueness holds up to ordered field isomorphism over $K$. A different ordering of a formally real field can produce a different ordered real closure.
