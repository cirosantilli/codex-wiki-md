# Jump conditions for stratified inviscid shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md)

At a material interface without [surface tension](surface-tension.md), continuity of displacement and of the pressure evaluated on the displaced interface gives

$$
\left[\frac{\widehat w}{U-c}\right]=0,\qquad
\left[(U-c)\widehat w'-U'\widehat w-\frac{g\rho}{\rho_0}\frac{\widehat w}{U-c}\right]=0.
$$

The first follows from the [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) $ik(U-c)\widehat\eta=\widehat w$. The second uses $\widehat p=\rho_0[(U-c)\widehat w'-U'\widehat w]/(ik)$ and hydrostatic displacement $[\widehat p-g\rho\widehat\eta]=0$. These conditions apply to density, velocity and vorticity jumps within the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md).
