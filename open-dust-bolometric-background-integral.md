# Open dust bolometric background integral

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological bolometric background from conserved sources](cosmological-bolometric-background-from-conserved-sources.md)

For an [open matter-dominated Friedmann solution](open-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md), put $u=H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_0}\,\tau$, $a=\Omega_0(\cosh u-1)/[2(1-\Omega_0)]$ and $\cosh u_0=2/\Omega_0-1$. The [cosmological bolometric background from conserved sources](cosmological-bolometric-background-from-conserved-sources.md) has $J(u)=\sinh(2u)/4-2\sinh u+3u/2$, since $J'=(\cosh u-1)^2$ and $dt=a\,du/[H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_0}]$. At fixed $H_0$, the limit $\Omega_0\to0$ is $cLn_0/(2H_0)$, whereas the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) gives $2cLn_0/(5H_0)$. Keeping the cosmic age fixed instead reverses this ordering: the two values are respectively $cLn_0t_0/2$ and $3cLn_0t_0/5$.
