# Taphonomic bias

↑ **Parent:** [Taphonomy](taphonomy.md)

Systematic distortion of biological information through differential preservation and recovery. [teeth](tooth.md) and delicate soft tissues need not have the same probability of entering the [fossil record](fossil-record.md); even a locally buried assemblage can therefore give a biased account of its living community.
