# Global harmonic conjugate criterion by vanishing periods

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md)

For a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $u$, the function $g=u_x-iu_y$ is holomorphic. It is the [derivative](derivative.md) of a single-valued [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) exactly when its integrals around all closed curves vanish. A primitive then has [real part](real-part.md) $u$ after adjusting a real constant. [Simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) domains satisfy this condition; on an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $u=\log|z|$ fails because the [derivative](derivative.md) has period $2\pi i$.
