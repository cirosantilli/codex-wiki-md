# Scalar representation

↑ **Parent:** [Trivial representation](trivial-representation.md)

A scalar representation is the one-dimensional [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md): every group element acts as the identity. Scalar polarization labels are unchanged by the relevant rotation group.
