# Critically damped RLC response

↑ **Parent:** [Series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md)

When $R^2=4L/C$, the homogeneous [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) has one repeated [characteristic root](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md), $-R/(2L)$. Its current is $e^{-Rt/(2L)}(A+Bt)$, with constants determined by the initial data.
