# Stainless steel

↑ **Parent:** [Steel](steel.md)

A chromium-bearing [steel](steel.md) whose protective surface film can confer [corrosion](corrosion.md) resistance. Composition, [microstructure](microstructure.md) and environment determine whether that protection persists.
