# Identity component

↑ **Parent:** [Connected component](connected-component.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_component)

The identity component is the [connected component](connected-component.md) containing the [identity element](identity-element.md). It is a closed [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md): products and inverses of connected sets through the identity remain in that component, and conjugation preserves it. It need not be open; in the additive group $\mathbb Q$ with its usual topology it is the nonopen singleton $\{0\}$.
