# Biological replicate

↑ **Parent:** [Replication in experimental design](replication-in-experimental-design.md)

A [biological replicate](biological-replicate.md) repeats a procedure using a distinct biological preparation, such as another [plant](plant.md) or a separately established [cell culture](cell-culture.md). The relevant level must be specified: several [cell cultures](cell-culture.md) derived from one donor can still form [nested measurements](nested-measurements.md) for a donor-level question. Calling a sample a [biological replicate](biological-replicate.md) does not by itself establish an independent [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md).
