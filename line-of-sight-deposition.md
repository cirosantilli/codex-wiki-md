# Line-of-sight deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Thin film](thin-film.md)

Deposition in which material travels approximately ballistically from a source to the substrate. Shadowing produces poor coverage of hidden surfaces; [gas](gas.md) [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) must substantially exceed the relevant source-substrate path.
