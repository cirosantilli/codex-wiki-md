# Translation continuity in Lp on a locally compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For left [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), $T_xf(t)=f(x^{-1}t)$ is an isometry on $L^p(G)$, $1\leq p<\infty$. For compactly supported continuous functions, uniform continuity on a slightly enlarged compact support proves norm continuity. Approximation by those functions and the triangle inequality extend it to all $L^p$. The continuous image of a compact set of translation parameters is consequently compact in $L^p$.
