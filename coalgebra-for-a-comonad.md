# Coalgebra for a comonad

↑ **Parent:** [Comonad](comonad.md)

For a [comonad](comonad.md), a coalgebra is a map $a:A\to GA$ satisfying $\epsilon_Aa=1_A$ and $\delta_Aa=Ga\,a$. A morphism $f:(A,a)\to(B,b)$ obeys $bf=Gf\,a$. This is distinct from the tensor-based notion of a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) in algebra.
