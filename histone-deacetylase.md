# Histone deacetylase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that removes acetyl groups from [histones](histone.md). Its local recruitment can oppose [histone acetylation](histone-acetylation.md) and favor repressive [chromatin](chromatin.md).
