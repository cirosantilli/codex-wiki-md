# Two-stock binomial market without a supplied bond

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial options pricing model](binomial-options-pricing-model.md)

Suppose two positive stock prices have joint one-period multipliers $(u_1,u_2)$ or $(d_1,d_2)$, with $u_1>u_2$. If $d_1<d_2$, the [Arrow state prices](arrow-debreu-state-price.md) are $(d_2-d_1)/(u_1d_2-u_2d_1)$ and $(u_1-u_2)/(u_1d_2-u_2d_1)$, both strictly positive. The two stocks therefore span both state payoffs, including a [synthetic zero-coupon bond](synthetic-zero-coupon-bond.md), and backward induction excludes [arbitrage](arbitrage.md) in the corresponding finite multiperiod market. If $d_1\geq d_2$, equal initial currency exposures long the first stock and short the second give an arbitrage when both states have positive probability.
