# Farad

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farad)

The [farad](farad.md), symbol $\mathrm F$, is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [capacitance](capacitance.md):

$$
1\,\mathrm F=1\,\frac{\mathrm C}{\mathrm V}.
$$

A [capacitor](capacitor.md) of [capacitance](capacitance.md) $1\,\mathrm F$ carries separated [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) magnitude $1\,\mathrm C$ at terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $1\,\mathrm V$. A picofarad is $10^{-12}\,\mathrm F$, so the worked [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md)'s [capacitance](capacitance.md) $88.54\,\mathrm{pF}$ is $8.854\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm F$. [OpenStax University Physics 2, §8.1: the farad](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/8-1-capacitors-and-capacitance).
