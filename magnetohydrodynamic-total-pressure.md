# Magnetohydrodynamic total pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md)

The [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md) is $\Pi=p+B^2/(2\mu_0)$, the sum of gas [pressure](pressure.md) and [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md). The remaining [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) is [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md), so this scalar does not include the entire magnetic stress.
