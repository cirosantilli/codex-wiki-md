# Iterated integral of a path

↑ **Parent:** [Signature of a bounded variation path](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md)

For a continuous path of [bounded variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md), integrate successively over the ordered simplex using [Riemann-Stieltjes integrals](riemann-stieltjes-integral.md). These integrals form the levels of its [path signature](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md). Appending the final integration variable gives the differential recursion $dS^{(k)}_t=S^{(k-1)}_t\otimes dx_t$.
