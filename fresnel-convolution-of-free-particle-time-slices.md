# Fresnel convolution of free-particle time slices

↑ **Parent:** [Free-particle propagator](free-particle-propagator.md)

Completing the square in the [integral](integral-split.md) over an intermediate [position](position.md) shows that two [free-particle propagators](free-particle-propagator.md) compose to the [propagator](propagator.md) for the sum of their positive time intervals. For equal slices, the quadratic identity is

$$
\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{k}+(x_1-x)^2
=\frac{k+1}{k}\left(x-\frac{x_0+kx_1}{k+1}\right)^2
+\frac{(x_1-x_0)^2}{k+1}.
$$

The [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md) contributes a factor proportional to $\sqrt{k/(k+1)}$; multiplying successive factors gives $1/\sqrt N$. A consistent convergence regulator fixes the phases of these square roots.
