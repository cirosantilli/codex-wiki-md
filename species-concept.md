# Species concept

↑ **Parent:** [Species](species.md)

A [species concept](species-concept.md) states what should make [organisms](organism-split.md) members of the same [species](species.md); an operational criterion states what observations will test that claim. [Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md), distinguishable [morphology](morphology-biology.md) and ancestry inferred through [phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md) answer different questions. In a lineage-based interpretation, they are evidence for separation of evolving [biological populations](population-biology.md), and need not appear together.
