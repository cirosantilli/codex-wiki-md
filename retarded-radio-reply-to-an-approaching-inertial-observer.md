# Retarded radio reply to an approaching inertial observer

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md)

Let a receiver be initially at distance $D$ from a stationary responder and approach at speed $\beta c$. A light signal takes $D/c$ to reach the responder, which waits time $\delta$ and replies. Intersecting the reply's [null worldline](null-worldline.md) with the receiver's [worldline](world-line.md) gives

$$
t_D=\frac{2D/c+\delta}{1+\beta},\qquad\tau_D=\frac{t_D}{\gamma}.
$$

The receiver's [proper time](proper-time.md) is $\tau_D$. These expressions describe reception before arrival when the reply is sent before the receiver reaches the responder; otherwise the trajectory after arrival must be specified.
