# Character value of a representation

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

At an element $g$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md), a complex character value is the sum of the [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) which are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of its representing [matrix](matrix.md). It is therefore an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md), and every [algebraic conjugate](conjugate-element-field-theory.md) has modulus at most the degree.
