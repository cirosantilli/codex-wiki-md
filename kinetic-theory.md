# Kinetic theory

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

Describe many-particle systems through distributions on [phase space](phase-space.md). Transport moves particles along trajectories, while collision or diffusion terms modify their velocity distributions. The [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md), [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md) and [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md) are central models.
