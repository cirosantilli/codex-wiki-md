# Simply connected closed three-manifolds are homology spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Homology sphere](homology-sphere.md)

A simply connected closed three-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md) is orientable, since its orientation monodromy factors through its trivial [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md). Its first integral homology is the [abelianization](abelianization.md) of that fundamental group and is zero. The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) gives $H^1(M;\mathbb Z)=0$, and [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) gives $H_2=0$, $H_3=\mathbb Z$. Connectedness gives $H_0=\mathbb Z$, and duality gives zero homology above dimension three. Thus it is an integral [homology sphere](homology-sphere.md), without needing any homeomorphism classification.
