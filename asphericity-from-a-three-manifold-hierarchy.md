# Asphericity from a three-manifold hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Three-manifold hierarchy](three-manifold-hierarchy.md)

In an [orientable](orientable-surface.md) [irreducible three-manifold](irreducible-three-manifold.md), an incompressible cut is [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)-injective by the [Loop theorem](loop-theorem.md). Lift a [three-manifold hierarchy](three-manifold-hierarchy.md) to the [universal cover](universal-cover.md). The terminal ball pieces are [contractible](contractible-space.md) and their gluing [surfaces](topological-surface.md) have [contractible](contractible-space.md) universal covers. The incidence graph is a tree, by the [van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) normal form for successive cuts. Finite subtrees are [contractible](contractible-space.md) by successively attaching a terminal piece along a [contractible](contractible-space.md) intersection. Every [sphere](sphere.md) map has [compact](compact-space.md) image contained in a finite subtree, so all higher [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) of the [universal cover](universal-cover.md) vanish.
