# Quantum coherence in a specified basis

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix.md)

For a chosen [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), the off-diagonal entries of a [density operator](density-matrix.md) encode quantum coherence between distinct basis states. A diagonal density operator is an incoherent mixture in that basis. This property is basis-dependent: diagonalizing the operator does not imply that it lacked coherence in the measurement or energy basis of interest. Relative phases affect these entries, while a global wavefunction phase cancels from the density operator. Dephasing damps the entries and can spoil [stimulated Raman adiabatic passage](stimulated-raman-adiabatic-passage.md) by removing the ground-state interference that maintains the dark state.
