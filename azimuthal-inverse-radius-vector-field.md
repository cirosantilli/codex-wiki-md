# Azimuthal inverse-radius vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

The [vector field](vector-field.md) $\mathbf G=(-y,x,0)/(x^2+y^2)$ has zero [curl](curl.md) away from its excluded axis, but has circulation $2\pi$ around a positively oriented circle enclosing that axis. [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) gives zero total circulation for a smooth annular surface avoiding the axis, whose inner and outer boundary circles have opposite orientations. It cannot be applied directly to a disk meeting the axis, because the field is undefined there.
