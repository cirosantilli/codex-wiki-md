# Configurations of three ultraparallel lines

↑ **Parent:** [Common perpendicular of ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](common-perpendicular-of-ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md)

Three pairwise [ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md) may have disjoint common perpendiculars or coincident ones. In the upper-half-plane model, three semicircles of radius $1/2$ centred at $-5/2,0,5/2$ have mutually disjoint common perpendiculars. Three concentric semicircles instead have the same vertical common perpendicular. Thus pairwise ultraparallelism alone does not determine the incidence of their perpendiculars.
