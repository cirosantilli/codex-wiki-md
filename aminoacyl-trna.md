# Aminoacyl-tRNA

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer RNA](transfer-rna.md)

A [transfer RNA](transfer-rna.md) carrying an amino acid through an activated ester linkage. [Aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase](aminoacyl-trna-synthetase.md) supplies the charging reaction; the linkage helps drive peptide-bond formation.
