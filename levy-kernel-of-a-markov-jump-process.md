<h1 id="levy-kernel-of-a-markov-jump-process">Lévy kernel of a Markov jump process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md)

A kernel specifying the jump rate from state $x$ for each displacement $z$. With the displacement convention the [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) is $L\varphi(x)=\int(\varphi(x+z)-\varphi(x))K(x,dz)$. The destination convention instead records a rate measure on the new state, translating each displacement by $x$. For finite total rate, that total is the exponential holding rate and the normalized measure is the next-jump distribution.
