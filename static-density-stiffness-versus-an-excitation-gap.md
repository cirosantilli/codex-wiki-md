# Static density stiffness versus an excitation gap

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic density-phase action](quadratic-density-phase-action.md)

A positive density curvature $g$ makes uniform static density fluctuations costly. It does not imply an additional independent gapped excitation: the first-order term makes density and phase conjugate. For a simple nonrelativistic [weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md), their coupled quadratic dynamics yields one positive [Bogoliubov spectrum](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md), which is gapless at zero [momentum](momentum-split.md). The word massive for the density field describes its static curvature in this setting.
