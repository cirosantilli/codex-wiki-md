# Weyl law

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_law)

For the positive scalar [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on a closed $d$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the number of [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) at most $\lambda$, counted with multiplicity, has the displayed leading asymptotic. The exponent determines the dimension and the coefficient determines the total volume of the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md). There is an analogous result with fixed [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) or [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) on a compact smooth domain.
