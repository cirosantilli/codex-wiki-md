# Fuchs-van de Graaf inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

For all finite-dimensional [density operators](density-matrix-split.md), including [pure states](pure-state.md) and [mixed states](mixed-state.md), the [unsquared quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) and [trace distance](trace-distance.md) obey

$$
1-F(\rho,\sigma)\leq D(\rho,\sigma)\leq\sqrt{1-F(\rho,\sigma)^2}.
$$

In the [squared quantum fidelity](squared-quantum-fidelity.md) convention this is $1-\sqrt{F_{\mathrm{sq}}}\leq D\leq\sqrt{1-F_{\mathrm{sq}}}$. The bounds translate a high [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) into a small operationally meaningful [trace distance](trace-distance.md). If $F\geq1-\varepsilon$ with $0\leq\varepsilon\leq1$, then $D\leq\sqrt{2\varepsilon-\varepsilon^2}$; if $D\leq\delta$, then $F\geq1-\delta$. The upper bound is attained by the [pure-state trace distance and fidelity identity](pure-state-trace-distance-and-fidelity-identity.md). For the [fidelity of commuting mixed states](fidelity-of-commuting-mixed-states.md) example, the bounds read $0.0652\lesssim0.3\lesssim0.3551$. [Fuchs and van de Graaf, Cryptographic Distinguishability Measures for Quantum Mechanical States, Theorem 1, equation 46; original preprint 1997, journal publication 1999](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9712042)
