# Homotopy classification of cosmological defects

↑ **Parent:** [Topological defect](topological-defect.md)

Disconnected vacuum components support walls, noncontractible vacuum loops support line defects, and nontrivial vacuum two-spheres support monopoles in three spatial dimensions. Allowed [topology](topology-split.md) does not alone predict the number formed or their subsequent survival.
