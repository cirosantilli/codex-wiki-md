# Derivative orthogonality when the Sturm-Liouville potential vanishes

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions](orthogonality-of-sturm-liouville-eigenfunctions.md)

For the [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md) $-(py_n')'=\lambda_nwy_n$, assume that the boundary term $[py_n'y_m]_a^b$ vanishes. One [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed identity. Distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) make the right side zero by [orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions](orthogonality-of-sturm-liouville-eigenfunctions.md), so the derivatives are orthogonal with weight $p$, while the original functions are orthogonal with weight $w$. This conclusion uses the absence of the $qy$ term: for a general potential the extra integral $-\int qy_ny_m$ need not vanish. For the [Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind](chebyshev-polynomial-of-the-second-kind.md) of degree $n-1$, the two weights are $w=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $p=(1-x^2)^{3/2}$.
