# Elastic strain

↑ **Parent:** [Strain](strain.md)

Elastic strain is the reversible part of [strain](strain.md) that determines stress through the elastic [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md). In a small-strain model with plastic and thermal effects, subtract the [plastic strain](plastic-strain.md) and [thermal strain](thermal-strain.md) from total strain to obtain it. A stress-free heated body can therefore have nonzero total strain but zero elastic strain; after plastic loading, unloading can likewise remove elastic strain while leaving permanent deformation.
