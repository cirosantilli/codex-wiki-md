# NOx-limited ozone production

↑ **Parent:** [Photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md)

A regime in which additional available [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) tends to increase net [photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md) when other factors are fixed. Reducing [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) generally suppresses that production, but local [ozone](ozone.md) [concentration](concentration.md) also depends on transport, deposition and NO titration.
