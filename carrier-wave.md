# Carrier wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave packet](wave-packet.md)

A [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md) is the rapid reference oscillation in a modulated [wave](wave-split.md). In the narrow-packet representation $\psi=A(x,t)e^{i(k_0x-\omega_ct)}$, choose $k_0$ near the centre of the [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md) and $\omega_c=\omega(k_0)$ on its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Its constant-[wave phase](phase-waves.md) reference moves at [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $\omega_c/k_0$. This reference need not coincide with a [local maximum](local-maximum.md) of the complete real wavefield when the [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md) varies. Moving the reference $k_0$ shifts some slow [wave phase](phase-waves.md) into $A$ without changing $\psi$.
