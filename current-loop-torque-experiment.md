# Current-loop torque experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole torque](magnetic-dipole-torque.md)

The PHYWE guide [Magnetic moment in the magnetic field](magnetic-moment-in-the-magnetic-field.md) makes the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) measurable. A circular test [current loop](current-loop.md) sits between a pair of [Helmholtz coils](helmholtz-coil.md), with separate supplies and meters for the test [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$ and field-producing [electric current](electric-current.md) $I_H$. The test [current loop](current-loop.md) is mounted on a [torsion dynamometer](torsion-dynamometer.md). The guide varies $I_H$, the number of turns, the test [electric current](electric-current.md), diameter, and the [angle](angle.md) between the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Its angular settings are spaced by $15\degree$. The leads hang loosely and are twisted together to suppress extra [torque](torque.md); the instrument zero is checked frequently because moving leads can shift it. Its reported results include approximately linear dependence on each [electric current](electric-current.md) and on winding count, and approximately quadratic dependence on diameter.[pp. 4–9: theory, equipment, setup, angular settings, Table 1 and Figures 2–6](magnetic-moment-in-the-magnetic-field.md)

The readout is a balance of [torques](torque.md). According to the manufacturer's [Torsion dynamometer 0.01 N operating instructions](torsion-dynamometer-0-01-n-operating-instructions.md), a metallic band supplies the restoring [torque](torque.md). First set the indication to zero with the applied [force](force-split.md) absent and center the lever using the zero knob. Apply the load, then turn the indicating knob until the lever returns to its original zero position. The scale then reads a force equivalent, not a [torque](torque.md) directly. The quoted scale division is $0.1\,\mathrm{mN}$. For a [force](force-split.md) applied perpendicular to the calibration [lever arm](lever-arm.md) at distance $\ell$, the conversion is $\tau=F\ell$; a [current loop](current-loop.md) applies a [couple](couple-mechanics.md), so its [torque](torque.md) must be compared with the calibrated restoring [torque](torque.md).[§§1–4: metallic-band compensation, zero procedure, force indication and scale specification](torsion-dynamometer-0-01-n-operating-instructions.md)

For a quantitative repeat, independently check that conversion with known [forces](force-split.md) at a measured perpendicular [lever arm](lever-arm.md), and check the imposed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at the test [current loop](current-loop.md). These are proposed calibration checks, not additional measurements reported here. The ideal [Helmholtz coil](helmholtz-coil.md) model predicts

$$
B_0=\left(\frac45\right)^{3/2}\frac{\mu_0N_HI_H}{R_H},
\qquad
|\tau|=NI\pi R^2 B_0|\sin\theta|,
$$

where $N_H$ and $R_H$ are the winding count and radius of each field-producing [Helmholtz coil](helmholtz-coil.md), distinct from $N$ and $R$ of the test [current loop](current-loop.md). Derive $B_0$ by adding the [on-axis magnetic field of a circular current loop](on-axis-magnetic-field-of-a-circular-current-loop.md) at distances $R_H/2$ from both coils. Independently calibrating $B_0$ and the restoring [torque](torque.md) lets a fitted [torque](torque.md) amplitude determine $m$ as amplitude divided by $B_0$, to compare with $NI\pi R^2$.

Useful additional controls are to reverse only $I$, reverse only $I_H$, and reverse both: the signed [torque](torque.md) should reverse in either single reversal and be unchanged in the double reversal. Record baselines with each [electric current](electric-current.md) separately zero, and subtract a nearby zero reading to reduce [torsion balance zero drift](torsion-balance-zero-drift.md). Repeat angular settings in both directions to reveal lead-dependent offsets. These are suggested controls. Limits include finite [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) uniformity, uncertainty in effective winding area and angular zero, meter calibration, [Joule heating](joule-heating.md), and residual lead [torque](torque.md). An apparently sinusoidal trace alone does not establish the absolute [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). No raw laboratory data or new measured values are claimed here.
