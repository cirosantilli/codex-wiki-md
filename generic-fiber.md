# Generic fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md)

For a [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) $X\to S$ with $S$ an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md) and [generic point](generic-point.md) $\eta$, the generic fiber is its [scheme-theoretic fiber](scheme-theoretic-fiber.md) over $\eta$. On [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) $S=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $X=\operatorname{Spec}B$, it is $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A\operatorname{Frac}A)$. It can be empty, and it need not be a [reduced scheme](reduced-scheme.md) or an [irreducible scheme](irreducible-scheme.md) without further assumptions.
