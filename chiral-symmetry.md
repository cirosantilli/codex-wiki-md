# Chiral symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md)

[Chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md) allows independent flavor rotations of left-handed and right-handed fields. For two massless quark flavors, the non-Abelian chiral transformations form $SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R$. The additional vector $U(1)$ symmetry of [baryon number](baryon-number.md) acts trivially on the [pion](pion.md) field $U$. The low-energy [pion](pion.md) field $U\in SU(2)$ transforms as $U\mapsto LUR^\dagger$. Its derivative [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) terms are invariant under constant $L,R$. A chosen vacuum $U=1$ preserves the diagonal subgroup $L=R$, which acts by conjugation and is the [isospin](isospin.md) symmetry. An ordinary common nonzero [pion](pion.md) mass explicitly breaks the independent left/right symmetry to that vector subgroup; vacuum selection in the massless theory is instead [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). The singlet axial anomaly is a separate issue and is not part of this two-flavor non-anomalous group.
