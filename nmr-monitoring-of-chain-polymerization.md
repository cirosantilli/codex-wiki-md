# NMR monitoring of chain polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)

[NMR monitoring of chain polymerization](nmr-monitoring-of-chain-polymerization.md) uses nuclear magnetic resonance signals to follow [monomer](monomer.md) and other distinguishable [chemical species](chemical-species.md). In a proton experiment, suitable signal [integrals](integral-split.md) can track changes in their relative amounts. Disappearance of a [monomer](monomer.md) signal is a consumption readout; it is not a direct count of [polymerization-active sites](polymerization-active-site.md). Overlapping signals, baseline errors and competing [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md) can obstruct the inference. A pre-reaction reference and independent product characterization make the consumption interpretation stronger.
