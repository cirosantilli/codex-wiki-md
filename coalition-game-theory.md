# Coalition (game theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

A [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) is a subset of the players in a [transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md), considered as a group that can act together. The game assigns it value $v(S)$. The [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md) constrains the total payoff assigned to every [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md), while a [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) measures the value gained by adding one player.
