# Markov semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Markov operator](markov-operator.md)

The transition operators of a time-homogeneous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) satisfy $P_{t+s}=P_tP_s$ by the [Markov property](markov-property.md). They preserve constants and positivity. When the generator and function domain permit differentiation, $\partial_tP_tg=L(P_tg)$ is the [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md). [Transition semigroup of a continuous-time Markov chain](transition-semigroup-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is the discrete-state special case.
