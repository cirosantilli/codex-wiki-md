# Aligned eccentric ring

↑ **Parent:** [Eccentric debris ring](eccentric-debris-ring.md)

An aligned eccentric ring consists of nested [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) with nearly common [longitude of periapsis](longitude-of-periapsis.md). Its width and [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) depend on both the distribution of [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) values and the radial gradient of [forced eccentricity](forced-eccentricity.md).
