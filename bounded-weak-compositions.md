# Bounded weak compositions

↑ **Parent:** [Stars and bars (combinatorics)](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md)

To count nonnegative tuples with $\sum_{i=0}^r n_i=N$ and $n_i<a_i$, mark violations $n_i\geq a_i$. An intersection of violations indexed by $J$ is in [bijection](bijection.md) with unrestricted tuples of sum $N-\sum_{i\in J}a_i$, by subtracting the bounds from those coordinates. [Stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) counts this intersection; the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) then gives the displayed formula. A negative remaining sum contributes zero.
