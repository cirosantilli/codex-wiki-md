# Cross-spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-spectrum)

A cross-spectrum relates the [Fourier transforms](fourier-transform-split.md) of two [random fields](random-field.md) or two [stochastic processes](stochastic-process-split.md). The [cross-spectrum of two stationary time series](cross-spectrum-of-two-stationary-time-series.md) gives the discrete-time case.

For statistically homogeneous random fields, the three-dimensional [cross-power spectrum](cross-spectrum.md) is defined by

$$
\langle U(\mathbf k)V^*(\mathbf k')\rangle=(2\pi)^3\delta_{\rm D}^{(3)}(\mathbf k-\mathbf k')P_{UV}(\mathbf k)
$$

under the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $U(\mathbf x)=\int d^3k\,U(\mathbf k)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}/(2\pi)^3$. For isotropic fields it depends only on $k=|\mathbf k|$. A deterministic relation $V(\mathbf k)=A(k)U(\mathbf k)$ implies $P_{UV}=A^*P_{UU}$. An angular [cross-power spectrum](cross-spectrum.md) is defined analogously from [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) coefficients.
