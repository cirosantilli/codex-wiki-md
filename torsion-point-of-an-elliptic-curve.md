# Torsion point of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

The $n$-torsion subgroup $E[n]$ is the kernel of multiplication by $n$. When the characteristic does not divide $n$, it is isomorphic over an algebraic closure to $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^2$.
