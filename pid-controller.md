# PID controller

↑ **Parent:** [Controller (control theory)](controller-control-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PID_controller)

An ideal [PID controller](pid-controller.md) combines [proportional control](proportional-control.md), [integral action](integral-action.md), and a term proportional to the [derivative](derivative.md) of the [tracking error](tracking-error.md). With $k_d=0$, it gives [proportional-integral control](proportional-integral-control.md). The [integral](integral.md) stores past error, while the [derivative](derivative.md) responds to its current rate of change. Practical implementations must account for [measurement noise](measurement-noise.md), which an ideal differentiator can amplify. [Åström and Murray, PID Control](https://www.fbswiki.org/wiki/index.php/PID_Control).
