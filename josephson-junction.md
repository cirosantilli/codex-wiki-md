# Josephson junction

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

A [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) couples two regions with [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) through a weak link. A tunnel barrier is one implementation.

The remarkable possibility is a steady [electric current](electric-current.md) across an insulating barrier at zero [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). This is the [DC Josephson effect](dc-josephson-effect.md), carried by coherent transfer of [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md). It differs from [single-electron superconducting tunneling](single-electron-superconducting-tunneling.md), which probes the [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) excitation spectrum and produces a dissipative [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md). Both contributions can occur in the same superconducting tunnel device. [Brian D. Josephson](brian-d-josephson.md) predicted the pair contribution in 1962; his paper treats transfer that leaves the [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) distribution unchanged. [Josephson (1962), pp. 251–252, opening paragraph and equation (3)](possible-new-effects-in-superconductive-tunnelling-by-brian-d-josephson.md)

The [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md) turns a difference of [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) into a measurable [electric current](electric-current.md), and an applied [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) into a rate of phase advance. The [Giaever gap experiment](giaever-gap-experiment.md) instead turns an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) into an excitation [energy](energy-split.md) scale. These complementary measurements expose two aspects of [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md): its coherent [order parameter](order-parameter.md) and its gapped [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) spectrum. The microscopic pairing calculation belongs to [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) and the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md). A [tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md) uses a different device, a semiconductor [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md).

Adding [capacitance](capacitance.md) and an external bias makes the [Gauge-invariant Josephson phase](gauge-invariant-josephson-phase.md) dynamical. [Current-biased Josephson phase dynamics](current-biased-josephson-phase-dynamics.md) explains how its trapped [energy levels](energy-level.md) and escape can be measured electrically.
