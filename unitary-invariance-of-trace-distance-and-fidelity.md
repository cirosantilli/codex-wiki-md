# Unitary invariance of trace distance and fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

For any unitary $U$, the unique [positive square root of an operator](positive-square-root-of-an-operator.md) obeys $\sqrt{UAU^\dagger}=U\sqrt A U^\dagger$. Conjugating the [positive operators](positive-operator.md) appearing in [trace distance](trace-distance.md) and [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) therefore conjugates their square roots. Cyclicity of the [trace](matrix-trace.md) removes the unitary factors, proving invariance of both quantities under simultaneous unitary transformations of the states.
