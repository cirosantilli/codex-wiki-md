# Hazard multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional hazards model](proportional-hazards-model.md)

In a [proportional hazards model](proportional-hazards-model.md) $h_i(t)=h_0(t)\phi_i$, the [hazard multiplier](hazard-multiplier.md) $\phi_i>0$ is the relative hazard against the reference multiplier one. In a [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md) it is $e^{x_i^T\beta}$. Ratios of two time-constant multipliers give constant [hazard ratios](hazard-ratio.md); time-dependent multipliers require a corresponding extension.
