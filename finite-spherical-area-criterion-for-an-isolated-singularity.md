# Finite spherical area criterion for an isolated singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical derivative of a meromorphic function](spherical-derivative-of-a-meromorphic-function.md)

A [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on a punctured disk has finite pulled-back spherical area precisely when it extends to the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) at the puncture. A removable singularity or [pole](pole.md) gives a smooth spherical extension and finite area. At an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md), the meromorphic version of the [Great Picard theorem](great-picard-theorem.md) gives infinitely many preimages of all but at most two spherical targets. The [area formula](area-formula-geometric-measure-theory.md) then makes the area infinite. The logarithmically weighted annular area divided by $\log(1/r)$ tends to the total unweighted area divided by $4\pi$, by averaging a nondecreasing area function.
