# Geometric bounce sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md)

For repeated vertical collisions with a fixed plate and constant [coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md) $0\le\gamma<1$, each collision multiplies the upward speed by $\gamma$, so successive peak heights form the displayed [geometric sequence](geometric-progression.md). The initial fall plus both legs of every rebound have total distance $h_0(1+\gamma^2)/(1-\gamma^2)$. The idealized impact times accumulate in finite time when $\gamma<1$; real contact mechanics replaces the limiting sequence by rest.
