# Spatially varying tension in filament bending

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

Adding $\frac12\int\sigma(x)(h')^2dx$ to the quadratic bending energy of an [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) contributes $-(\sigma h')'$ to its [variational derivative](variational-derivative.md). The endpoint term is $Ah''\eta'+(\sigma h'-Ah''')\eta$. If the real [filament tension](filament-tension.md) vanishes at both ends, the usual [self-adjoint endpoint conditions for filament bending](self-adjoint-endpoint-conditions-for-filament-bending.md) remain valid. A complete [eigenfunction expansion](eigenfunction-expansion.md) diagonalizes the energy even when the [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) have no explicit formula. Thermal [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) arguments require positive eigenvalues after fixing any [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md); strong compression can violate this requirement.
