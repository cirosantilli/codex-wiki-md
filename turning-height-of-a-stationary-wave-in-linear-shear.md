# Turning height of a stationary wave in linear shear

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical trapping of an atmospheric gravity wave](vertical-trapping-of-an-atmospheric-gravity-wave.md)

For constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$ and $U=U_0+Sz$ with $S>0$, a stationary component of horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ turns at $z_t=(N/k-U_0)/S$ if it propagates at the source. Its vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) vanishes there; [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) fails and an [Airy function](airy-function.md) transition connects reflection to an upper [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md).
