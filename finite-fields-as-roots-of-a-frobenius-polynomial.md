# Finite fields as roots of a Frobenius polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Finite field](finite-field.md)

In a [splitting field](splitting-field.md) of $x^{p^n}-x$ over a [prime field](prime-field.md), the [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) form a [field](field.md). Frobenius identities close the [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) under [addition](addition.md) and [multiplication](multiplication.md), and nonzero [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) have inverses which are again [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md). The [derivative](derivative.md) is minus one, so the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has exactly $p^n$ distinct [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md). This constructs a [field](field.md) of every prime-power order.
