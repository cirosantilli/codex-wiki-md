# Decay threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

A parent of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$ can decay to on-shell daughters of [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) $m_i$ only if $M\geq\sum_i m_i$. In the parent [rest frame](rest-frame.md), each daughter [energy](energy-split.md) is at least its [rest energy](rest-energy.md), so [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) proves necessity. The excess [energy](energy-split.md) controls the available [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md). A [two-body decay threshold](two-body-decay-threshold.md) is the two-daughter special case; spin and other [selection rules](selection-rule.md) can still forbid an energetically allowed channel.
