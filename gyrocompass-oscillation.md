# Gyrocompass oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Gyrocompass](gyrocompass.md)

For a rapidly spinning [gyrocompass](gyrocompass.md) with axle constrained horizontally, take azimuth $\theta$ positive from north towards west and positive spin [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J_s=I_1\omega$. In the terrestrial [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md), the vertical component of the [torque](torque.md) equation, with zero external vertical [torque](torque.md), is $I_2\ddot\theta+J_s\Omega_E\cos\lambda\sin\theta\simeq0$. Here $I_2$ is the vertical [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and terms of order $\Omega_E^2$ have been neglected. The [small-angle approximation](small-angle-approximation.md) about $\theta=0$ gives [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with the displayed squared [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). Linearizing about $\theta=\pi$ reverses its sign and gives an unstable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). At the poles this restoring effect vanishes. For a thin rotationally symmetric flywheel, the [perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) gives $I_1=2I_2$.
