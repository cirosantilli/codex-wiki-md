# Locally finite family of subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A family $(A_i)$ of subsets of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is locally finite if every point has a neighbourhood meeting only finitely many of its members. On a compact space such a family has only finitely many nonempty members. For singleton subsets of a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), this is the condition permitting prescribed poles of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) without accumulation inside the surface.
