# Sparse photoactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Photoactivated localization microscopy](photoactivated-localization-microscopy.md)

A small subset of photoactivatable labels is fluorescent at any one time so that their overlapping [point spread functions](point-spread-function-split.md) can usually be avoided. Repetition supplies many spatial localizations without requiring all labels to emit simultaneously.
