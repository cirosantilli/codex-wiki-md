# Resonance-overlap encounter estimate

↑ **Parent:** [First-order mean-motion resonance](first-order-mean-motion-resonance.md)

A local weak [gravitational assist](gravitational-assist.md) estimate for a nonradiating circular grain gives $\delta\epsilon\simeq32\mu^2\epsilon^{-5}$, where $\mu=M_p/M_\star$. [Conjunction-longitude sensitivity to an encounter](conjunction-longitude-sensitivity-to-an-encounter.md) then gives $|\delta\Lambda|\simeq128\pi\mu^2/(3\epsilon^7)$. Requiring the change to exceed one full turn and using $\epsilon\simeq2/(3j)$ gives $j_c\simeq(2/729)^{1/7}\mu^{-2/7}$. This motivates loss of coherent resonant conjunctions, rather than proving that every initial phase escapes. The coefficient depends on the encounter convention and the approximations; near-planet [Hill equations](hill-equations.md) require a consistent rotating-frame shear instead of mixing local inertial and rotating velocities.
