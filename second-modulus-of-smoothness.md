# Second modulus of smoothness

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

For a continuous periodic function set

$$
\omega_2(f,h)=\sup_{|u|\leq h}\|f(\cdot+u)-2f+f(\cdot-u)\|_\infty.
$$

It measures smoothness through second differences without requiring a derivative. For $h>0$, factoring the translation difference gives $\omega_2(f,t)\leq(1+t/h)^2\omega_2(f,h)$. The [second-difference integral formula](second-difference-integral-formula.md) gives $\omega_2(f,h)\leq h^2\|f''\|_\infty$ for twice continuously differentiable functions.
