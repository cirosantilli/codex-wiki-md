# Multiply twinned nanoparticle

↑ **Parent:** [Nanoparticle](nanoparticle.md)

A [nanoparticle](nanoparticle.md) formed from several crystallographic domains joined by twin boundaries. Low-energy external facets can compensate the internal boundary and elastic-strain cost; fivefold motifs cannot extend as an unstrained periodic bulk crystal.
