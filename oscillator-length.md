# Oscillator length

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

The oscillator length fixes the spatial scale of a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) of mass $m$ and angular frequency $\omega$. Its normalized ground [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is $\varphi_0(x)=\pi^{-1/4}\ell^{-1/2}\exp[-x^2/(2\ell^2)]$, so $|\varphi_0(0)|^2=1/(\sqrt\pi\ell)$ and $\langle x^2\rangle=\ell^2/2$. The definition of $\ell$ is distinct from the root-mean-square displacement $\ell/\sqrt2$.
