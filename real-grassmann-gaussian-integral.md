# Real Grassmann Gaussian integral

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md)

For an [antisymmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) $A$, a [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) extracts the top coefficient of the exponential. Expansion into pairings gives the [Pfaffian](pfaffian.md), with a constant sign depending on the order of the differentials. This is a polynomial in the entries of $A$, not the positive square root of its [determinant](determinant.md): in dimension two it is proportional to $A_{12}$ and changes sign with that entry. The identity $\operatorname{Pf}(A)^2=\det A$ determines its square but does not discard its orientation.
