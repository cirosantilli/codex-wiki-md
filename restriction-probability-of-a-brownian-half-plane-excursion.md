# Restriction probability of a Brownian half-plane excursion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian excursion in the upper half-plane](brownian-excursion-in-the-upper-half-plane.md)

For a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) avoiding zero, let $\psi_A$ map its complement to the half-plane, fix zero, and have derivative one at infinity. The [Brownian excursion in the upper half-plane](brownian-excursion-in-the-upper-half-plane.md) avoids $A$ with probability $\psi_A'(0)$. From an interior starting point $z$, the corresponding transformed process avoids $A$ with probability $\operatorname{Im}g_A(z)/\operatorname{Im}z$; taking $z=i\varepsilon$ and $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ gives the boundary derivative.
