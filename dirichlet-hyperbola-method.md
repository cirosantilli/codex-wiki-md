# Dirichlet hyperbola method

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_hyperbola_method)

The Dirichlet hyperbola method evaluates a summatory [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) by splitting the lattice points $ab\leq x$ at $a,b\leq\sqrt x$. For the [divisor function](divisor-function.md),

$$
\sum_{n\leq x}\tau(n)
=2\sum_{a\leq\sqrt x}\left\lfloor\frac xa\right\rfloor
-\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor^2.
$$
