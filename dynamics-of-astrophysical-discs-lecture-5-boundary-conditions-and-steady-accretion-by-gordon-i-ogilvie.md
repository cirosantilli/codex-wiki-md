# Dynamics of Astrophysical Discs, Lecture 5: Boundary Conditions and Steady Accretion by Gordon I. Ogilvie

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Gordon I. Ogilvie](work-by-gordon-i-ogilvie.md)

Cambridge Mathematical Tripos Part III, Lent Term 2020, authored lecture 5. Section 5.2, printed pp.3–4, derives the zero-inner-torque steady disc and discusses [energy](energy-split.md) transported by internal [torque](torque.md). The infinite-outer-radius [luminosity](luminosity.md) formula must not be applied unchanged to an arbitrary finite annulus.
