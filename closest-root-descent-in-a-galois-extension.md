# Closest-root descent in a Galois extension

↑ **Parent:** [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md)

Let $L/K$ be a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of a field complete for a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md). Suppose $\alpha$ is uniquely closest to $\beta$ among the roots of a polynomial over $K$ lying in $L$. Every element of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K(\beta))$ preserves [absolute values on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md) and permutes these roots. It sends $\alpha$ to an equally close root, hence fixes $\alpha$. The [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md) gives $K(\alpha)\subseteq K(\beta)$. The converse inclusion need not hold: for roots $0,\sqrt p,-\sqrt p$, the element $p\sqrt p$ is uniquely closest to zero but does not belong to $\mathbb Q_p$.
