# Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplace–Runge–Lenz_vector)

For the signed [inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md) $\boldsymbol F=-\kappa\boldsymbol r/r^3$ on a particle of [mass](mass.md) $m$, with Hamiltonian $H=p^2/(2m)-\kappa/r$, the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) $\boldsymbol A=\boldsymbol p\times\boldsymbol L-m\kappa\widehat{\boldsymbol r}$ is conserved. Attraction has $\kappa>0$ and repulsion has $\kappa<0$. It obeys $\boldsymbol A\cdot\boldsymbol L=0$ and $A^2=m^2\kappa^2+2mHL^2$. For unit [mass](mass.md) in a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), $\kappa=GM$.
