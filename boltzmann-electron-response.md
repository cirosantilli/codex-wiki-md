# Boltzmann electron response

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic plasma wave](electrostatic-plasma-wave.md)

Rapid parallel thermal [electron](electron.md) streaming can approximately balance electrostatic and isothermal [pressure](pressure-split.md) forces, giving $n_e\propto e^{e\phi/T_e}$ and its linear form in the title. This assumes a suitable connection along the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), $|\omega|\ll k_\parallel v_{\mathrm{th},e}$, and an approximately electrostatic branch. A spatially constant potential on a closed line requires its own particle-number constraint.
