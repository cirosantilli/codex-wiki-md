# Passivity in linear viscoelasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

A passive [viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md) law cannot deliver net [work](work.md) in a periodic steady deformation without an external source of energy. With [strain](strain.md) $\epsilon=\epsilon_0\cos\omega t$ and [stress](stress.md) $\sigma=\epsilon_0(G'\cos\omega t-G''\sin\omega t)$, the cycle-averaged supplied power is $\langle\sigma\dot\epsilon\rangle=\omega\epsilon_0^2G''/2$. Thus a necessary passivity condition, using the convention $e^{i\omega t}$, is a nonnegative [loss modulus](loss-modulus.md) at every positive frequency. Equivalently the [work](work.md) per cycle is $\pi\epsilon_0^2G''$. A stable differential equation for stress does not by itself ensure this energetic condition.
