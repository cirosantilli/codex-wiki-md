# Competitive exclusion principle

↑ **Parent:** [Food web](food-web.md)

Two consumers with different break-even requirements cannot both have bounded persistent abundance on a single well-mixed limiting resource when their per-capita growth is linear in that resource. For $\dot Y/Y=cX-d$ and $\dot Z/Z=eX-f$, differentiation of $\log Y/c-\log Z/e$ gives the nonzero constant $-d/c+f/e$ when their break-even levels differ. This excludes simultaneous bounded persistence without assuming convergence to an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). Additional resources, environmental variation or different interactions can invalidate the simple one-resource argument.
