# Massless neutrino-electron contact cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md)

For a left-handed incoming neutrino, unpolarized electrons, and electron vector/axial couplings $c_V,c_A$, the [four-fermion interaction](four-fermion-interaction.md) gives $\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=4G_F^2[(c_V+c_A)^2s^2+(c_V-c_A)^2u^2]$. Integrating massless [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) yields $\sigma=G_F^2s(c_V^2+c_Vc_A+c_A^2)/(3\pi)$. The electron spin average is included, while no sterile neutrino helicity is averaged in.
