# Row-insertion bumping-path monotonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Row insertion](row-insertion.md)

During [row insertion](row-insertion.md) into a [near Young tableau](near-young-tableau.md), the successive carried entries strictly increase while the columns in which they are bumped weakly decrease. Every old cell keeps its entry or receives a smaller one. These facts follow from increasing rows and strictly increasing columns.
