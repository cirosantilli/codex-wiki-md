# First-to-default swap

↑ **Parent:** [Credit default swap](credit-default-swap.md)

A basket credit contract whose protection payment is triggered by the first specified [default](credit-default.md) among its reference entities. The loss and settlement terms refer to the defaulting name according to the contract. Premium generally stops at that first [default](credit-default.md) or maturity. Pricing requires the joint default-time distribution; separate marginal [default](credit-default.md) probabilities alone do not determine the first-default law.
