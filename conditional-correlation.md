# Conditional correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Pearson correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md)

The [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) computed under a [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md), using its [conditional covariance](conditional-covariance.md) and positive [conditional variances](conditional-variance.md). It can depend on the observed conditioning value. For a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), conditioning on other coordinates gives the corresponding [partial correlation](partial-correlation.md).
