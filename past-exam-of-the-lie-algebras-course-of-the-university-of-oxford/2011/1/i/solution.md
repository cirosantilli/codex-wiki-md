<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [lower central series of a Lie algebra](../../../../lower-central-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) as $\mathfrak g^1=\mathfrak g$ and $\mathfrak g^{j+1}=[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g^j]$. Since the [nilpotent Lie algebra](../../../../nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) is nonzero, there is a last nonzero term $\mathfrak g^c$, with $\mathfrak g^{c+1}=0$. Thus $[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g^c]=0$, so

$$
0\ne\mathfrak g^c\subseteq Z(\mathfrak g).
$$

Consequently **the centre is nonzero**. This argument works over every [field](../../../../field.md) and includes an abelian algebra, for which $c=1$.
