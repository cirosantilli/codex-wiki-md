<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every $2\times2$ [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) has the unique decomposition

$$
A=\frac{\operatorname{tr}A}{2}I+\left(A-\frac{\operatorname{tr}A}{2}I\right).
$$

Both summands define invariant subspaces for the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Scalar matrices commute with everything, so $\mathbb CI$ is $L_0$. The traceless matrices form $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ with [basis](../../../../basis.md) $e,h,f$ of weights $2,0,-2$. Their brackets satisfy $[e,f]=h$, $[h,e]=2e$, $[h,f]=-2f$; the raising and lowering operations connect all three weight lines. This is the [simple module](../../../../irreducible-module.md) $L_2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{gl}_2(\mathbb C)\cong L_0\oplus L_2.}
$$

As a check, its zero-[weight space](../../../../weight-space.md) has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) two and its one-[weight space](../../../../weight-space.md) is zero, agreeing with part (ii).
