↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md) over $\mathbb C$, every finite-dimensional [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) is a [direct sum](../../../../direct-sum.md) of simple modules $L_m$, indexed by integers $m\ge0$. The [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) $L_m$ has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $m+1$ and a [basis](../../../../basis.md) $v_0,\ldots,v_m$ with

$$
\begin{aligned}
hv_j&=(m-2j)v_j,\\
ev_j&=jv_{j-1},\\
fv_j&=(m-j)v_{j+1},
\end{aligned}
$$

with out-of-range vectors interpreted as zero. In particular $v_0$ is a [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) vector of weight $m$. The weights are $m,m-2,\ldots,-m$, each with multiplicity one. The multiplicities of the $L_m$ in a finite [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) determine that [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) up to isomorphism.
