<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the [simple module](../../../../irreducible-module.md) $L_m$, weight $0$ occurs once if $m$ is even and never if $m$ is odd. Weight $1$ occurs once if $m$ is odd and never if $m$ is even. Thus each simple summand contributes exactly one to $\dim V_0+\dim V_1$.

[Complete reducibility of semisimple Lie algebra representations](../../../../weyl-s-theorem-on-complete-reducibility.md) writes $V=\bigoplus_mL_m^{\oplus a_m}$. Taking the two [weight spaces](../../../../weight-space.md) commutes with this finite [direct sum](../../../../direct-sum.md), so

$$
\boxed{\dim V_0+\dim V_1=\sum_ma_m.}
$$

The right side counts irreducible summands **with multiplicity**, independently of a chosen decomposition.
