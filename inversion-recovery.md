# Inversion recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic resonance imaging](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md)

An ideal resonant inversion pulse reverses the longitudinal magnetization. Subsequent [spin-lattice relaxation](spin-lattice-relaxation.md) gives the displayed recovery law. Observation at a chosen inversion time provides contrast or nulling for a specified relaxation time; an RF inversion alone does not amplify the initial polarization magnitude.
