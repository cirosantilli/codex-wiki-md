# Mollification of a divergence-forcing equation

↑ **Parent:** [Mollifier](mollifier.md)

The distributional equation $\Delta u=\operatorname{div}(bu)+cu+f$ becomes the smooth interior equation $\Delta u_\sigma=\operatorname{div}(bu)_\sigma+(cu)_\sigma+f_\sigma$. The products are convolved as wholes. Only coefficient bounds on compact interior sets are needed, and the [divergence-forcing interior H1 estimate](divergence-forcing-interior-h1-estimate.md) provides a uniform first derivative bound.
