# Nonorthogonal nearest-neighbour tight-binding band

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

For one localized orbital per site in a chain, take on-site matrix elements $H_{jj}=E_0$, nearest-neighbour matrix elements $H_{j,j\pm1}=h$, overlaps $S_{jj}=1$ and $S_{j,j\pm1}=s$, with other terms neglected and $h,s$ real. A [Bloch state](bloch-state.md) reduces the [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) $Hc=ESc$ to

$$
E_0+2h\cos(ka)=E[1+2s\cos(ka)],\qquad
E(k)=\frac{E_0+2h\cos(ka)}{1+2s\cos(ka)}.
$$

The overlap metric must be positive for the retained model; $|s|<1/2$ is sufficient for all $k$. Dropping it yields the cosine band of the [orthogonal tight-binding approximation](orthogonal-tight-binding-approximation.md).
