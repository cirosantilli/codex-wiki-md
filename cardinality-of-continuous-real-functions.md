# Cardinality of continuous real functions

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

The [set](set-split.md) of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ has [cardinality](cardinality.md) $2^{\aleph_0}$. [Constant functions](constant-function.md) provide the lower bound. Restriction to the [dense set](dense-set.md) $\mathbb Q$ is [injective](injective-function.md), since [continuity](continuous-function.md) determines a [function](function-split.md) from its values there. [Real sequences](sequence.md) have [cardinality](cardinality.md) $2^{\aleph_0}$, by encoding a [sequence](sequence.md) using its countably many rational lower cuts. Restriction therefore supplies the [upper bound](upper-and-lower-bounds.md) and [Cantor-Schröder-Bernstein](cantor-schroder-bernstein-theorem.md) gives [equality](logical-equality.md).
