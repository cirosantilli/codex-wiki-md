# Fourth moment

↑ **Parent:** [Moment](moment.md)

The [fourth moment](fourth-moment.md) is $\mathbb E[X^4]$. Its finiteness is equivalent to square integrability of $X^2$. For centered variables, dividing it by the squared [variance](variance-split.md) gives [kurtosis](kurtosis.md).
