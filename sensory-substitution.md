# Sensory substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Perception](perception.md)

Access to information normally supplied through one [sensory modality](sensory-modality.md) by recoding it for another. Successful use can restore a function without restoring the missing receptor system or establishing ordinary experience in that modality. Device engineering, [learning](learning.md) and subjective reports address different aspects.
