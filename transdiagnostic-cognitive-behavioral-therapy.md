# Transdiagnostic cognitive behavioral therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

An approach addressing processes shared across diagnostic categories. It can still use an individual [clinical formulation](clinical-formulation.md) and disorder-relevant behavioural experiments; transdiagnostic delivery need not mean identical exercises for every patient.
