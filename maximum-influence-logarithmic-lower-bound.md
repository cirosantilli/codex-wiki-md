# Maximum-influence logarithmic lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Kahn-Kalai-Linial theorem](kahn-kalai-linial-theorem.md)

For a zero-one [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) on the unbiased [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) with flip-probability [influences](influence-of-a-variable.md) $\beta_i\leq\beta$, the displayed bound holds for sufficiently small $\beta>0$. If it fails, write $b=\log(1/\beta)/3$. The [total influence](total-influence.md) identity puts at least half the nonconstant [Fourier weight](fourier-weight.md) on levels $1\leq|S|\leq b$. The [hypercontractive weighted influence bound](hypercontractive-weighted-influence-bound.md) at $\delta=e^{-1}$ then gives $\sum_i\beta_i^{2/(1+\delta)}\geq2b\delta^b\operatorname{Var}(f)$. Its upper bound $\beta^{(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)}\sum_i\beta_i$ contradicts $(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)>1/3$.
