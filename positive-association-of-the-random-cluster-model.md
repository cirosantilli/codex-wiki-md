# Positive association of the random-cluster model

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md)

For $0\leq p\leq1$ and $q\geq1$, the [FKG inequality](fkg-inequality.md) implies that increasing functions of a random-cluster configuration have nonnegative covariance. The conclusion can fail for $0<q<1$.
