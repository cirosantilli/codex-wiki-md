# Approximate gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

A [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) function is approximately differentiable at almost every point with respect to [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). Its [approximate gradient](approximate-gradient.md) is the density of the absolutely continuous part of its [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md). This does not imply membership in a [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md).
