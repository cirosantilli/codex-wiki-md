# Spectral line strength

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line profile](spectral-line-profile.md)

The integrated [absorption coefficient](absorption-coefficient.md) of an isolated [spectral line](spectral-line.md) is its line strength. For a mass coefficient write $\kappa_\nu=S\phi_\nu$, where $\int\phi_\nu d\nu=1$. The units of $S$ include the chosen spectral-coordinate unit; a strength integrated over [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is not numerically interchangeable with one integrated over [frequency](frequency.md). Changing normalized width at fixed strength redistributes opacity without changing its spectral integral.
