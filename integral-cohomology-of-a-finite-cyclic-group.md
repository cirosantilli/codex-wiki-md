# Integral cohomology of a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For the trivial action of the [finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md) $C_m$ on $\mathbb Z$,

$$
H^0(C_m,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z,
\qquad H^{2k+1}(C_m,\mathbb Z)=0,
\qquad H^{2k}(C_m,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z
$$

for $k\geq1$. The [periodic resolution of a finite cyclic group](periodic-resolution-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md) proves this directly.
