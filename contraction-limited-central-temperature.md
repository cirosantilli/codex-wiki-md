# Contraction-limited central temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen-burning minimum mass](hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md)

A fixed [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) profile with $P=\rho\mathcal RT/\mu_i+K_{\rm nr}\rho^{5/3}$ gives

$$
T_c=\frac{\mu_i}{\mathcal R}\left(AGM/R-BK_{\rm nr}M^{2/3}/R^2\right),
$$

where $A,B>0$. As a function of $1/R$ this is a concave quadratic; its maximum is $A^2G^2\mu_iM^{4/3}/(4BK_{\rm nr}\mathcal R)$, at $R=2BK_{\rm nr}M^{-1/3}/(AG)$. At the maximum, the central [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) and thermal pressure are equal. If this temperature is insufficient for sustained [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md), the object becomes a [brown dwarf](brown-dwarf.md). Only the positive-temperature segment represents a physical sequence.
