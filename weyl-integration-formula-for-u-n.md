<h1 id="weyl-integration-formula-for-u-n">Weyl integration formula for U(n)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl integration formula](weyl-integration-formula.md)

For the [unitary group](unitary-group.md), the diagonal [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md) has coordinates $z_i=e^{i\theta_i}$ and [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) $S_n$. The [Weyl integration formula](weyl-integration-formula.md) [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) independent uniform angles by $n!^{-1}\prod_{i<j}|z_i-z_j|^2$. The [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) $\det(z_i^{n-j})$ has squared [torus](torus.md) integral $n!$, by [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md). Multiplying an [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$ by this [determinant](determinant.md) gives an alternating [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md). Its leading alternant has coefficient one; [character orthogonality for compact groups](character-orthogonality-for-compact-groups.md) forces every other alternant coefficient to vanish. This yields the quotient $\det(z_i^{\lambda_j+n-j})/\det(z_i^{n-j})$.
