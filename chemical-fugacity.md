# Chemical fugacity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md)

An effective gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) defined by $\mu=\mu^\circ+RT\ln(f/p^\circ)$ in a specified standard-state convention. It approaches the gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) at low density and differs from the dimensionless [fugacity](fugacity-statistical-mechanics.md).
