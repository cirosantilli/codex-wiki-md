# Unitary conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary operator](unitary-operator.md)

Unitary conjugation maps an operator $A$ to $UAU^\dagger$ for a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $U$. It preserves [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), [operator norms](operator-norm.md), and algebraic relations such as products and adjoints.
