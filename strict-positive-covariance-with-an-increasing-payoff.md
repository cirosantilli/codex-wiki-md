# Strict positive covariance with an increasing payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance](covariance.md)

If $Z$ is nondegenerate, $g$ is strictly increasing and the relevant products are [integrable](integrability.md), an independent copy $Z'$ gives $2\operatorname{Cov}(g(Z),Z)=\mathbb E[(g(Z)-g(Z'))(Z-Z')]$. The integrand is positive exactly when $Z\ne Z'$, so the [covariance](covariance.md) is strictly positive. The same argument applies within each conditional law and yields a positive terminal [stock](stock.md) hedge for an attainable increasing payoff.
