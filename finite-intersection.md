# Finite intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Set intersection](set-intersection.md)

A [finite intersection](finite-intersection.md) is an [intersection](set-intersection.md) of finitely many [sets](set-split.md). The indexing [set](set-split.md) is finite, regardless of the size of the intersected [sets](set-split.md). For an empty indexed family of [subsets](subset.md) of a specified ambient [set](set-split.md) $X$, the convention is that the [intersection](set-intersection.md) equals $X$.
