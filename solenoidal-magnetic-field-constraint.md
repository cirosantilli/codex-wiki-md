# Solenoidal magnetic-field constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field.md)

The [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) has zero divergence. The [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) preserves this constraint from initial data, since the divergence of its curl is zero.
