# RSA cryptosystem

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_cryptosystem)

For $N=pq$, choose $e$ coprime to $\phi(N)$ and a private exponent $d$ with $ed\equiv1\pmod{\phi(N)}$. Encryption and decryption are

$$
c\equiv m^e\pmod N,
\qquad
m\equiv c^d\pmod N.
$$
