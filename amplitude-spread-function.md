# Amplitude spread function

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent imaging](coherent-imaging.md)

The [amplitude spread function](amplitude-spread-function.md) is the complex field response to an [optical point source](optical-point-source.md). Its squared magnitude, with a specified [point spread function normalization](point-spread-function-normalization.md), gives the intensity [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md).
