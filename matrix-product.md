# Matrix product

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md)

For [matrices](matrix.md) $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ and $B\in\mathbb F^{n\times p}$, their matrix product is the [matrix](matrix.md) $AB\in\mathbb F^{m\times p}$ with entries $(AB)_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^nA_{ik}B_{kj}$. The operation producing it is [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md), representing composition of the corresponding [linear maps](linear-map.md).
