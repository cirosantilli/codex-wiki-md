# Auditory verbal agnosia

↑ **Parent:** [Speech perception](speech-perception.md)

A disproportionate difficulty recognizing spoken language despite better access through other routes. [Hearing](hearing.md), phonological discrimination, reading and nonverbal semantic performance must be compared before attributing failure to a [speech](speech.md)-input mechanism.
