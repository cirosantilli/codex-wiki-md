# Thrust acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md)

The instantaneous [acceleration](acceleration.md) contribution from [rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md) $T$ acting on remaining rocket [mass](mass.md) $M$ is $a_T=T/M$. Gravity and other external [forces](force-split.md) contribute separately. A constant [rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md) therefore produces an increasing [thrust acceleration](thrust-acceleration.md) as [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md) is consumed.
