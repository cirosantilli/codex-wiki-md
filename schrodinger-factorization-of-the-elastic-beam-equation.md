# Schrodinger factorization of the elastic beam equation

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md)

Writing $q=u+iv$ in the [free Schrodinger equation](free-schrodinger-equation.md) gives $u_t=-v_{xx}$ and $v_t=u_{xx}$, hence $u_{tt}+u_{xxxx}=0$. To encode initial velocity $u_1$, choose the decaying primitive $v_0(x)=-\int_x^\infty(s-x)u_1(s)ds$. A prescribed endpoint curvature becomes the time derivative of the imaginary boundary trace. This turns the normalized [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md) into a complex second-order boundary problem.
