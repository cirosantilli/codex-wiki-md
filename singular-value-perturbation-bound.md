# Singular value perturbation bound

↑ **Parent:** [Singular value](singular-value.md)

For equally sized [matrices](matrix.md), corresponding ordered [singular values](singular-value.md) differ by at most the [spectral norm](matrix-2-norm.md) of the perturbation. Indeed, let $B_{j-1}$ be a best approximation of [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) at most $j-1$. The [Eckart-Young-Mirsky theorem](eckart-young-mirsky-theorem.md) and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) give $\sigma_j(A)\leq\|A-B_{j-1}\|_2\leq\|A-B\|_2+\sigma_j(B)$. Interchanging $A,B$ proves the bound.
