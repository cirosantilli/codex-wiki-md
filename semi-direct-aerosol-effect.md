# Semi-direct aerosol effect

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol-cloud interaction](aerosol-cloud-interaction.md)

[Cloud](cloud.md) changes caused by [aerosol](aerosol.md) absorption of radiation and the resulting heating. Heating can evaporate [clouds](cloud.md) and reduce shortwave reflection, but the effect can reverse when heating changes stability and [cloud](cloud.md) maintenance differently. The forcing sign depends on the relative positions of [cloud](cloud.md) and absorbing [aerosol](aerosol.md) and on dynamical adjustment.
