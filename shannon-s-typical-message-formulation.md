<h1 id="shannon-s-typical-message-formulation">Shannon's typical-message formulation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic equipartition property](asymptotic-equipartition-property.md)

In [A Mathematical Theory of Communication by Claude Shannon](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md) (1948, section 7), [Claude Shannon](claude-shannon.md) explains that the common symbol counts in a long independent message make its negative logarithmic probability approximately the message length times the source entropy. Theorem 3 separates an exceptional collection of small total probability from messages with this normalized information cost; Theorem 4 relates the number needed to accumulate a fixed probability to the entropy. The finite-state source setting matters to the historical statement.[section 7, Theorems 3–4, reprint pp. 13–14; Appendix 3](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md)
