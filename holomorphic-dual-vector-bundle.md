# Holomorphic dual vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

Dualize the fibres of a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md). If its transitions are $g_{ij}$, the dual transitions are $g_{ij}^{-T}$, which are holomorphic and preserve the evaluation pairing. These define the natural holomorphic structure on the smooth [dual bundle](dual-bundle.md).
