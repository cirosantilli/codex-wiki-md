# Pinch map

↑ **Parent:** [Wedge sum](wedge-sum.md)

A pinch map collapses a separating subspace to a point so that the quotient splits into a [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md). For a [connected sum of oriented manifolds](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md), collapsing the separating boundary sphere gives a map $M\mathbin\#N\to M\vee N$. The two wedge projections have [degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) one with compatible orientations. Consequently their pullbacks identify the two top [orientation classes](fundamental-class.md), while positive-degree classes from different summands have zero [cup product](cup-product.md).
