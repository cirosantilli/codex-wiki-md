# Rectangular potential barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangular_potential_barrier)

A [rectangular potential barrier](rectangular-potential-barrier.md) has a constant elevated potential over a finite interval and a lower constant potential outside it. Matching wavefunctions and derivatives at the interval endpoints gives its reflection and transmission amplitudes. It is a special [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md).
