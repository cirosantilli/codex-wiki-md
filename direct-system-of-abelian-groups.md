# Direct system of abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered colimit of modules](filtered-colimit-of-modules.md)

A direct system indexed by a [directed set](directed-set.md) $A$ consists of [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) $G_a$ and homomorphisms $\phi_{ab}:G_a\to G_b$ for $a\leq b$, with $\phi_{aa}=1$ and $\phi_{bc}\phi_{ab}=\phi_{ac}$.
