# Sphere in a uniform straining Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Let the ambient velocity be $\mathbf E\mathbf x$, with $\mathbf E$ symmetric and trace free, and centre a rigid sphere of radius $a$ at the origin. It is [force-free](force-free.md) and [torque-free](torque-free.md), with zero translation and rotation. Its disturbance is

$$
\mathbf u'=-\frac{a^5}{r^5}\mathbf E\mathbf x
-\frac{5a^3}{2r^5}\left(1-\frac{a^2}{r^2}\right)
(\mathbf E:\mathbf x\mathbf x)\mathbf x,
\qquad p'=-\frac{5\mu a^3}{r^5}(\mathbf E:\mathbf x\mathbf x).
$$

The total velocity vanishes at $r=a$, and the leading disturbance is a [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md). Harmonic dipole and quadrupole potentials in the [Papkovich–Neuber representation](papkovich-neuber-representation.md) give the complete field.
