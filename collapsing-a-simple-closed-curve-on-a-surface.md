# Collapsing a simple closed curve on a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsing a pair theorem](collapsing-a-pair-theorem.md)

A simple closed curve in a [closed orientable surface](closed-orientable-surface.md) has an annular [collar neighbourhood](collar-neighbourhood.md), so its collapse is governed by the [collapsing a pair theorem](collapsing-a-pair-theorem.md). In the [long exact sequence in relative homology](long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md), the decisive map is $H_1(A;\mathbb Z)\to H_1(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)$, sending the [circle](circle.md) generator to its [homology class](homology-class.md). That class is zero for a separating curve and a [primitive homology class](primitive-homology-class.md) for a nonseparating curve. The two possibilities lead to [homology after collapsing a separating surface curve](homology-after-collapsing-a-separating-surface-curve.md) and [homology after collapsing a nonseparating surface curve](homology-after-collapsing-a-nonseparating-surface-curve.md).
