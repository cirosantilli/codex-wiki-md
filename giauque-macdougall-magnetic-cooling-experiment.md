# Giauque-MacDougall magnetic cooling experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic demagnetization](adiabatic-demagnetization.md)

[William Francis Giauque](william-francis-giauque.md)'s 1949 lecture, Some Consequences of Low Temperature Research in Chemical Thermodynamics, credits [Peter Debye](peter-debye.md) with an independent proposal. It describes [gadolinium sulfate octahydrate](gadolinium-sulfate-octahydrate.md) in a jacket immersed in liquid [helium](helium.md), with an external [solenoid](solenoid.md). Exchange gas conducts away [heat](heat-split.md) during [magnetization](magnetization.md); evacuation isolates the sample before field reduction. A liquid-[nitrogen](nitrogen.md) shield reduces radiative [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). Its photograph identifies the 1933 apparatus operated with [Duncan Peck MacDougall](duncan-peck-macdougall.md). An [inductance](inductance.md) bridge reads the sample's [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md); measuring coils remain outside the evacuated jacket in liquid [helium](helium.md). Increasing [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) indicates cooling, but converting it to [temperature](temperature-split.md) using [Curie's law](curie-s-law.md) becomes approximate as that law fails.[Giauque, Nobel lecture, pp. 237–245, Figs. 16–20 and 23–25](some-consequences-of-low-temperature-research-in-chemical-thermodynamics-by-william-francis-giauque.md)

The measurement logic deserves a separate check. A changed [inductance](inductance.md) alone is not an absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) calibration: the sample geometry, applied measuring [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), and constitutive response enter the inference. A modern control would compare the susceptibility-based [thermometer](thermometer.md) with an independently calibrated [thermometer](thermometer.md) over their common range, check recovery after repeated sweeps, and measure subsequent warming to bound incoming [heat](heat-split.md). These are proposed controls derived from the readout principle, rather than additional procedures attributed to the 1933 experiment. The historical working salt has a richer magnetic level structure than the illustrative [two-level paramagnetic cooling model](two-level-paramagnetic-cooling-model.md).
