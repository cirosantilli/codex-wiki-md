# Future-complete horizon focusing proof of the area theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md)

For future-complete generators of an [event horizon](event-horizon.md), the [null convergence condition](null-convergence-condition.md) and hypersurface orthogonality reduce the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) to $\theta'\leq-\theta^2/2$. If $\theta_0<0$, integration gives focusing within affine distance $2/|\theta_0|$. The [null focusing theorem](null-focusing-theorem.md) identifies this with a [conjugate point to a spacelike surface](conjugate-point-to-a-spacelike-surface.md). A null generator cannot remain on an [achronal boundary](achronal-boundary.md) beyond such a focal point, contradicting future completeness on the horizon. Thus $\theta\geq0$, and $dA/d\lambda=\theta A\geq0$ gives the [second law of black-hole mechanics](second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md). New horizon generators may join toward the future at crease sets, adding area, but generators have no future endpoints on a regular event horizon under these assumptions.
