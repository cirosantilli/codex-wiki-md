<h1 id="glen-s-flow-law">Glen's flow law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)

A constitutive law for [ice](ice.md) creep relates [deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md) $\tau_{ij}$ and [strain rate](strain-rate.md) $\dot\epsilon_{ij}$ through an effective stress $\tau_e$ and a temperature-dependent rate factor $\mathcal A$. The commonly used exponent is near three. Homogeneity of degree $n$ explains the combination $u/N^n$ in [pressure](pressure-split.md)-dependent [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md) laws when geometry and rate-factor scales are fixed.
