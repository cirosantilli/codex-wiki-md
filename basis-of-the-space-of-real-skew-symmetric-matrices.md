# Basis of the space of real skew-symmetric matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md)

For real $n\times n$ [skew-symmetric matrices](skew-symmetric-matrix.md), each upper-triangular entry can be chosen freely, the corresponding lower-triangular entry is its negative, and diagonal entries vanish. Thus the displayed [matrix unit](matrix-unit.md) differences span the space and are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md): inspecting entry $(i,j)$ with $i<j$ recovers its coefficient. There are $n(n-1)/2$ such pairs, giving the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md).
