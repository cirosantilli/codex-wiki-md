# Monosaccharide

↑ **Parent:** [Sugar](sugar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monosaccharide)

A [monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md) is a single-unit [carbohydrate](carbohydrate.md) that cannot be split into smaller [sugars](sugar.md) by [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md). [Ribose](ribose.md), [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md), and [glucose](glucose.md) are examples. “Single-unit” does not mean a single [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) or a permanently open chain. See [McMurry: simple carbohydrates](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/25-1-classification-of-carbohydrates).
