# SLE4 angle martingale

↑ **Parent:** [SLE angle process](sle-angle-process.md)

For a fixed [interior](interior-topology.md) point and parameter four, the [SLE angle process](sle-angle-process.md) has zero drift and satisfies $\theta_t=\arg z+2\int_0^tY_s|Z_s|^{-2}\,dB_s$. Its values in $(0,\pi)$ make this continuous [local martingale](local-martingale.md) a true bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md). The identity $\log(\Upsilon_t/\Upsilon_0)=-[\theta]_t$, together with the [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md), prevents its [Loewner conformal radius](conformal-radius-under-a-chordal-loewner-flow.md) from vanishing at a finite lifetime. Since the parameter-four trace is simple and does not meet the real [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) at positive times, finite swallowing would require a visit to the point and vanishing radius. Thus its [Loewner swallowing time](interior-point-swallowing-time-for-a-loewner-chain.md) is infinite almost surely. This gives a global continuous bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md), not just one defined before swallowing.
