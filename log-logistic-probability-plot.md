# Log-logistic probability plot

↑ **Parent:** [Log-logistic distribution](log-logistic-distribution.md)

For $F(x)=1-[1+(x/\alpha)^{\beta-1}]^{-1}$, plotting the logits of $p_i=i/(n+1)$ against sorted log observations gives an approximately straight line under the model. The [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes $F(X_{(i)})$ a uniform order statistic with expectation $i/(n+1)$, motivating these plotting positions. With those axes the slope is $\beta-1$ and the intercept is $-(\beta-1)\log\alpha$.
