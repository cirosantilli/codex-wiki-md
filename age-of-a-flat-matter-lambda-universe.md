# Age of a flat matter-Lambda universe

↑ **Parent:** [Age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md)

For positive [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) density, positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), negligible radiation and zero curvature, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $dt=\sqrt a\,da/[H_0\sqrt{\Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda a^3}]$. Substituting $u=\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda/\Omega_m}\,a^{3/2}$ gives the displayed [age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md) measured from $a=0$. Its zero-vacuum limit is $2a^{3/2}/(3H_0\sqrt{\Omega_m})$; the pure positive-vacuum flat patch has no finite big-bang age.
