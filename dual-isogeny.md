# Dual isogeny

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_isogeny)

The dual of an isogeny $\phi:E\to E'$ of degree $n$ is the unique isogeny $\widehat\phi:E'\to E$ satisfying

$$
\widehat\phi\phi=[n],\qquad \phi\widehat\phi=[n].
$$
