# Lexicographically ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Linearly ordered group](linearly-ordered-group.md)

For ordered [Abelian groups](abelian-group.md), compare tuples at their first differing coordinate. This gives a two-sided invariant [total order](total-order.md) on their direct product. The additive group $\mathbb Z^2$ with its first coordinate dominant is not an [Archimedean ordered group](archimedean-ordered-group.md), since $n(0,1)<(1,0)$ for every positive integer $n$.
