# Routh-Hurwitz stability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

The [Routh-Hurwitz criterion](routh-hurwitz-stability-criterion.md) tests whether all roots of a real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) have negative real part, by sign conditions on [determinants](determinant.md) formed from its coefficients. Applied to a [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md), it gives an algebraic test for strict linear asymptotic stability.
