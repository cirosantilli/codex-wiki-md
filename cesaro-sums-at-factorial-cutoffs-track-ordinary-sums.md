<h1 id="cesaro-sums-at-factorial-cutoffs-track-ordinary-sums">Cesàro sums at factorial cutoffs track ordinary sums</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro mean](cesaro-mean.md)

For a formal cosine [series](series-mathematics.md) $\sum_ja_j\cos(j!t)$ with uniformly bounded coefficients, the Fourier [Cesàro mean](cesaro-mean.md) at $N_J$ differs from its ordinary partial sum by $-(N_J+1)^{-1}\sum_{j\leq J}j!a_j\cos(j!t)$. Since $\sum_{j\leq J}j!\leq2J!$, the difference is $O(1/J)$ uniformly in $t$. Thus any divergent behavior of these ordinary cutoff sums persists along the same Cesàro [subsequence](subsequence.md). Averaging by the sparse coefficient index is a different summation operation from averaging the actual Fourier frequency cutoffs.
