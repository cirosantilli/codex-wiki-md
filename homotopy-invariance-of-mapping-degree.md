# Homotopy invariance of mapping degree

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

For maps between oriented connected [closed manifolds](closed-manifold.md) of the same dimension, a [homotopy](homotopy.md) induces equal maps on top-dimensional [homology](homology-split.md), hence equal coefficients on the [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md). For smooth maps and a smooth homotopy, the [degree by integration of a pullback volume form](degree-by-integration-of-a-pullback-volume-form.md) gives another proof: the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) on the homotopy cylinder makes the difference of endpoint pullback integrals zero because the target top form is closed. This is an obstruction to extending a degree-one sphere identity over a ball.
