# Turaev surface

↑ **Parent:** [Link diagram](link-diagram.md)

Replace each crossing of a diagram by a saddle joining its A smoothing above the projection [sphere](sphere.md) to its B smoothing below; cap the upper and lower state [circles](circle.md). For a connected classical [link diagram](link-diagram.md) this constructs a connected closed orientable [surface](topological-surface.md). Its Euler characteristic is $s_A+s_B-c(D)$, so $s_A+s_B\leq c(D)+2$. For an alternating diagram the [surface](topological-surface.md) has genus zero.
