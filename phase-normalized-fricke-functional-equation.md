# Phase-normalized Fricke functional equation

↑ **Parent:** [Mellin transform of a cusp-form L-function](mellin-transform-of-a-cusp-form-l-function.md)

With the [determinant-normalized slash operator](determinant-normalized-slash-operator.md), the map $Jf=i^kf|_kW_N$ satisfies $J^2=1$. The scaled imaginary-axis functions satisfy $F(y)=y^{-k}G(1/y)$, and their [Mellin transforms](mellin-transform.md) give the displayed entire functional equation. The phase must be specified; omitting it changes the sign in odd or twice-odd weights.
