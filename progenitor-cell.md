# Progenitor cell

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progenitor_cell)

A [progenitor cell](progenitor-cell.md) is a precursor with relatively restricted differentiation potential and, generally, more limited self-renewal than a [stem cell](stem-cell.md). Usage depends on the lineage and assay; an expression marker alone does not establish renewal capacity.
