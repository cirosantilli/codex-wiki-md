# Hamiltonian energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian conservation law](hamiltonian-conservation-law.md)

Along [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md), spatial derivatives of the [Hamiltonian function](hamiltonian-function.md) cancel in its total time derivative. Thus $dH/dt=\partial_tH$. An autonomous [Hamiltonian function](hamiltonian-function.md) is conserved; explicit time dependence generally prevents conservation even though [phase space](phase-space.md) is still preserved.
