<h1 id="martin-s-axiom">Martin's axiom</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin's_axiom)

For a [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, $\mathrm{MA}_\kappa$ says that for every [forcing](forcing-split.md) with the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) and every family of at most $\kappa$ dense [subsets](subset.md), some [filter in an ordered set](filter-mathematics.md) meets them all. Full Martin axiom asserts this for all $\kappa<2^{\aleph_0}$. The strict upper bound is part of the definition.
