# Glutamine

↑ **Parent:** [Proteinogenic amino acid](proteinogenic-amino-acid.md)

[Glutamine](glutamine.md) has a neutral side-chain amide. Its N–H groups can donate [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) and its carbonyl [oxygen](oxygen.md) can accept them; its amide [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) is not an ordinary amine base.
