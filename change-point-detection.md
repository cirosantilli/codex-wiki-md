# Change-point detection

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)

Change-point detection estimates locations where a sequence's distribution or mean changes. A piecewise-constant mean has a sparse vector of adjacent differences; [one-dimensional total variation denoising](one-dimensional-total-variation-denoising.md) exploits this sparsity without fixing the number of changes in advance.
