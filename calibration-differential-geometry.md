# Calibration (differential geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Calibrated geometry](calibrated-geometry.md)

A calibration is a closed real [differential form](differential-form-split.md) whose [comass](comass.md) is at most one. Restricting it to any oriented unit tangent plane therefore gives at most that plane's volume. A [calibrated submanifold](calibrated-submanifold.md) attains equality everywhere, and [calibration implies volume minimization](calibration-implies-volume-minimization.md) among homologous competitors.
