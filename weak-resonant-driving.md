# Weak resonant driving

↑ **Parent:** [Shaken harmonic trap](shaken-harmonic-trap.md)

A sinusoidal trap displacement with amplitude $\varepsilon$ and duration $T$ produces a resonant first-order excitation amplitude proportional to $\varepsilon\omega T/a_0$, where $a_0$ is the [oscillator length](oscillator-length.md). Keeping its square much smaller than one is the long-time condition for weak excitation. The small-displacement condition $\varepsilon/a_0\ll1$ alone does not ensure uniform validity as $T$ grows. Exact linear driving remains solvable as a [coherent state](coherent-state.md) after this first-order approximation fails.
