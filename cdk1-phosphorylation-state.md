# CDK1 phosphorylation state

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclin-dependent kinase 1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md)

A [CDK1 phosphorylation state](cdk1-phosphorylation-state.md) identifies modified [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md). Different sites have opposite effects: in studied [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), Thr14/Tyr15 are inhibitory, while Thr161 [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) is activating. T denotes a [threonine residue](threonine-residue.md), Y a [tyrosine residue](tyrosine-residue.md), and numbers are [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) positions. Do not assume this numbering for every [species](species.md).[Solomon, Lee and Kirschner (1992), abstract: mapped Xenopus Thr14/Tyr15, loss of activation with Thr161→Ala, and cyclin binding by the triple mutant](role-of-phosphorylation-in-p34cdc2-activation-identification-of-an-activating-kinase-by-m-j-solomon-and-others.md)

The 1992 study supplies a useful separation of mechanisms: replacing Thr14, Tyr15 and Thr161 still permitted [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) binding, but replacing Thr161 prevented activation of [histone H1](histone-h1.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). Binding therefore did not suffice for catalytic activity in that preparation. Activating [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) also occurred with catalytically inactive [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), supporting a separate [CDK-activating kinase](cdk-activating-kinase.md) rather than a requirement for intramolecular [autophosphorylation](autophosphorylation.md).[Solomon, Lee and Kirschner (1992), abstract: cyclin-binding triple mutant and activating phosphorylation of the kinase-inactive K33R mutant](role-of-phosphorylation-in-p34cdc2-activation-identification-of-an-activating-kinase-by-m-j-solomon-and-others.md)

Cyclin B binding and site-specific CDK1 phosphorylation states answer different questions**. Original qualitative schematic for the studied [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md). All four right-hand boxes contain bound [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md); only the green box has activating Thr161 [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) with both inhibitory sites clear. “Inhibitory P present” groups Thr14 and/or Tyr15 modification. Arrows show changes of state, not an obligatory sequence, measured rates, or a complete [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md) model. [Myt1](myt1.md) can add either inhibitory modification; [Cdc25](cdc25.md) removes inhibitory [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups.

In this deliberately coarse classification, bound [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md), activating [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md), and cleared inhibitory sites must coincide for the activity-permissive state. Removing inhibitory [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups does not supply missing [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) or activating [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). Conversely, adding activating [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) does not erase inhibitory modification. Actual preparations contain mixtures of states and additional regulators; this classification gives no numerical [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md).
