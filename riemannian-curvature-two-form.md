# Riemannian curvature two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature form](curvature-form.md)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), the [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) is the endomorphism-valued two-form

$$
R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z.
$$

It has [tensoriality](tensoriality.md) in all three arguments. Its [first Bianchi identity](first-bianchi-identity.md) follows from torsion-freeness: the cyclic sum is $\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}(\nabla_X[Y,Z]-\nabla_{[Y,Z]}X)=\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}[X,[Y,Z]]=0$ by the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md).
