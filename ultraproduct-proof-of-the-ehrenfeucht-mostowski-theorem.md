# Ultraproduct proof of the Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem](ehrenfeucht-mostowski-theorem.md)

Expand an infinite [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) by [Skolem functions](skolem-function.md), choose distinct elements $b_n$, and fix a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) $\mathcal U$ on $\mathbb N$. For each finite subset $F$ of the required index order, form the [ultrapower](ultrapower.md) over $\mathbb N^F$ using the ordered [Fubini product of ultrafilters](fubini-product-of-ultrafilters.md). Pullback along coordinate projections gives coherent elementary maps by the [Łoś theorem](los-theorem.md). In their [directed limit of elementary embeddings](directed-limit-of-elementary-embeddings.md), the classes of the coordinate [functions](function-split.md) $\mathbf n\mapsto b_{n_i}$ are distinct and form an [order-indiscernible sequence](order-indiscernible-sequence.md); every [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) on increasing coordinates has the same nested [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) test. Their [Skolem hull](skolem-hull.md) gives an [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski model](ehrenfeucht-mostowski-model.md). An index-[order automorphism](order-automorphism.md) sends a term in the generators to the same term in the transported generators; indiscernibility makes this well defined. Uniqueness is in the specified [Skolem expansion](skolem-expansion.md), not necessarily in its reduct. If generators must respect a definable infinite order, first produce an increasing [sequence](sequence.md) by an [ultrapower](ultrapower.md) of finite increasing chains.
