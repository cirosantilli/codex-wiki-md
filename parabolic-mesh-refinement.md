# Parabolic mesh refinement

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

A parabolic refinement keeps the time step proportional to the square of the spatial step: $k=rd^2$ for a fixed positive [diffusion Courant number](diffusion-courant-number.md) $r$. A consistency bound $O(k+d^2)$ then becomes $O(d^2)$, though its separate time order remains one.
