# Subset construction with epsilon transitions

↑ **Parent:** [Epsilon-NFA](epsilon-nfa.md)

The subset construction converts an epsilon-NFA into a [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md). Its states are subsets of NFA states, its initial state is the [epsilon closure](epsilon-closure.md) of the NFA initial state, and every symbol transition is followed by another epsilon closure.
