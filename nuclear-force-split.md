# Nuclear force

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_force)

The residual [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) between [nucleons](nucleon.md). Its long-distance part involves [pion](pion.md) exchange and depends on [spin](spin.md) and [isospin](isospin.md). It is distinct from directly exchanging gluons between color-singlet nucleons.

Why do [protons](proton.md) and [neutrons](neutron-split.md) bind into an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md), even though [protons](proton.md) repel electrically? An attractive [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md) can lower the total [energy](energy-split.md) below that of separated [nucleons](nucleon.md), producing [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md). The [deuteron](deuteron.md), containing one [proton](proton.md) and one [neutron](neutron-split.md), is the simplest such [bound state](bound-state.md).

In [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md), [nucleons](nucleon.md) are composite [colour singlets](colour-singlet.md) built from [quarks](quark.md) and [gluons](gluon.md). Their residual [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md) has a longest-range strong contribution from [one-pion exchange](one-pion-exchange.md), with additional interactions at shorter distances.[Modern Theory of Nuclear Forces, inspected preprint sections I and I.B](modern-theory-of-nuclear-forces-by-evgeny-epelbaum-and-others.md)
