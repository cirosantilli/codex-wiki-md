# Hurdle decomposition of a positive random sum

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

For positive claim sizes, a [random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md) is zero exactly when its count is zero. Its law is a [mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md) of a zero atom of mass $p=\mathbb P(N=0)$ and, with weight $1-p$, a random sum whose count has the [zero-truncated claim-count distribution](zero-truncated-claim-count-distribution.md). An independent [Bernoulli random variable](bernoulli-distribution.md) multiplying that positive component gives the same law. This is distinct from arbitrarily inserting additional zeros into an otherwise unchanged count law.
