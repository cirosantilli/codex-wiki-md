# Zero-rate distortion threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Rate-distortion function](rate-distortion-function.md)

For the finite [alphabets](alphabet.md) considered here, the [zero-rate distortion threshold](zero-rate-distortion-threshold.md) is the smallest [expected distortion](expected-distortion.md) obtainable by always outputting one fixed reconstruction. Such a reconstruction is [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of the source, so its [mutual information](mutual-information.md) is zero. A randomized output that is [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of the source averages the costs of fixed reconstructions and cannot improve their minimum. Thus $R(D)=0$ exactly when $D\geq D_{\mathrm{zero}}$. For a fair [bit](bit.md) under [Hamming distortion](hamming-distortion.md), $D_{\mathrm{zero}}=1/2$; for a [Bernoulli random variable](bernoulli-distribution.md) of parameter $p$, it is $\min\{p,1-p\}$. The threshold depends on the source [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md), rather than only the [alphabet](alphabet.md).
