# Uniform-twist central axial field bound

↑ **Parent:** [Flux-tube axial field virial identity](flux-tube-axial-field-virial-identity.md)

For a [flux tube](flux-tube.md) with $B_\phi=\gamma(s/s_0)B_z$, regularity at the axis and [cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md) give $B_z(0)^2=B_p^2-2\mu_0p(0)+\gamma^2\langle B_z^2\rangle$. The [flux-tube axial field virial identity](flux-tube-axial-field-virial-identity.md) and $p\geq0$ give the bound. A twisted [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md) can support a central axial field above the untwisted [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) limit even though its cross-sectional mean obeys that limit.
