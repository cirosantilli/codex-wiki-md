# Extremal graph for disjoint cliques

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal number](extremal-number.md)

For fixed positive integers $r,s$ and sufficiently large $n$, the unique extremal [graph](graph-split.md) forbidding $s$ vertex-disjoint copies of the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_{r+1}$ is the [join of graphs](join-graph-theory.md) $K_{s-1}+T_r(n-s+1)$, up to [isomorphic graphs](graph-isomorphism.md). Each forbidden [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) would require a distinct [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of $K_{s-1}$.
