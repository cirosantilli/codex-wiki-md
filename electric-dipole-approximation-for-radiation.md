# Electric-dipole approximation for radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md)

For a source of characteristic size $a$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$, the electric-dipole approximation requires

$$
\frac{\omega a}{c}\ll1,
$$

so the source is much smaller than its radiation wavelength. Observing the pure $1/R$ radiation field additionally requires the [radiation zone](radiation-zone.md), $R\gg c/\omega$ and $R\gg a$.
