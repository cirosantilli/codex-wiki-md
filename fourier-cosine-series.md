# Fourier cosine series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

A Fourier cosine series has the form

$$
\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\cos(nx).
$$

It represents the [even function](even-function.md) obtained by reflecting its data across the origin.
