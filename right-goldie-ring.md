# Right Goldie ring

↑ **Parent:** [Goldie ring](goldie-ring.md)

A right Goldie ring satisfies the right-sided conditions in the definition of a [Goldie ring](goldie-ring.md). Every [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md) does: its [right annihilators](right-annihilator.md) are [right ideals](right-ideal.md), and its right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) has finite [uniform dimension](uniform-dimension.md).
