<h1 id="fenchel-nielsen-twist">Fenchel–Nielsen twist</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md)

After cutting a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) along a simple closed [geodesic](geodesic.md), reglue its two boundary copies after an arclength displacement $\tau$. The boundary length remains fixed, while the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) varies. In marked [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md), the twist is real-valued; changing it by one boundary length corresponds to a [Dehn twist](dehn-twist.md) in the marking. This construction also applies to a hyperbolic pair of pants with a fixed cone angle and two geodesic boundaries.
