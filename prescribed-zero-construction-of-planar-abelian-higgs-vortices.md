# Prescribed-zero construction of planar Abelian Higgs vortices

↑ **Parent:** [Taubes equation](taubes-equation.md)

Choose positions $z_a$ and positive integer multiplicities $m_a$. Solve the [Taubes equation](taubes-equation.md) with $h\to0$ at infinity and $h=2m_a\log|z-z_a|+O(1)$ near each position. The planar existence theorem supplies such a solution for every finite multiset of positions; see [the original existence theorem](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01197552). Set $\chi=\sum_am_a\arg(z-z_a)$, $\Phi=e^{h/2+i\chi}$ and $A=\nabla\chi-J\nabla h/2$, with $J(v_1,v_2)=(-v_2,v_1)$. The singular terms cancel in $A$, while $\Phi$ has precisely the selected zeros. These fields satisfy the [Bogomolny vortex equations](bogomolny-vortex-equation.md), with $B=(1-|\Phi|^2)/2$. Their [vortex number](vortex-number.md) is $\sum_am_a$. Complex conjugation of $\Phi$ together with $A\mapsto-A$ gives antivortices.
