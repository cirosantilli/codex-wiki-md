# Jacobi fields vanishing at their initial point

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md)

For $\gamma(t)=\exp_p(tV)$, every [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) with $J(0)=0$ is uniquely of the form

$$
J(t)=(d\exp_p)_{tV}(tB),\qquad B=D_tJ(0)\in T_pM.
$$

Differentiate the [geodesic variation](geodesic-variation.md) $\exp_p(t(V+sB))$ in $s$ to obtain the formula. Uniqueness of the Jacobi equation with prescribed initial value and derivative shows that all such fields are obtained. A nontrivial field vanishing at $t=L>0$ therefore corresponds to a nonzero vector in the kernel of $(d\exp_p)_{LV}$, giving the [conjugate point](conjugate-point.md) criterion.
