# Free variable

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

An occurrence of a variable is free when it is outside the scope of a quantifier binding that variable.

The distinction belongs to [free variables and bound variables](free-variables-and-bound-variables.md); an occurrence bound by an enclosing quantifier is not free.
