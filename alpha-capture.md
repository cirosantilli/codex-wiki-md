# Alpha capture

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md)

Capture of an [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) increases the mass number by four and atomic number by two. Radiative alpha capture releases a photon, while reactions such as $(\alpha,n)$ can instead emit a neutron. [Carbon-12 alpha capture](carbon-12-alpha-capture.md) competes with helium production of carbon during [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md).
