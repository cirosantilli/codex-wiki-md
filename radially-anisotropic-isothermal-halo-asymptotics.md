# Radially anisotropic isothermal halo asymptotics

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian angular-momentum suppression in a stellar distribution](gaussian-angular-momentum-suppression-in-a-stellar-distribution.md)

For a self-consistent [Gaussian angular-momentum suppression in a stellar distribution](gaussian-angular-momentum-suppression-in-a-stellar-distribution.md), put $y=[\psi(r)-\psi(0)]/\sigma^2$ and $s=\log(r/r_a)$. At large radius the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) reduces to $y_{ss}+y_s\sim-\lambda e^y$, where $\lambda=4\pi G\rho_c r_a^2/\sigma^2$. The asymptotic balance $y_s\sim-1/s$ gives $e^y\sim1/(\lambda s)$, the displayed density, and [circular speed](circular-speed.md) $v_c^2\sim\sigma^2/\log(r/r_*)$. A sufficiently small regular core can have an intermediate isotropic [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) envelope with $\rho\simeq\sigma^2/(2\pi Gr^2)$ and $v_c^2\simeq2\sigma^2$. At the actual regular centre, however, $\rho\to\rho_c$ and $v_c\propto r$; the intermediate power law must not be mistaken for a central limit.
