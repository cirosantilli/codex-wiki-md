# eIF2

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

A eukaryotic initiation factor carrying initiating [methionyl-tRNA](methionyl-trna.md) in a [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md)-bound ternary complex. Its alpha-subunit [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) can inhibit nucleotide exchange by [eIF2B](eif2b.md) and lower initiation capacity.
