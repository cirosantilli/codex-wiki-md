# Quasistatic vertical-field magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

When the magnetic field supplies instantaneous damping rather than an evolving magnetic perturbation, the roll amplitudes obey a two-variable linear system with [trace](matrix-trace.md) $-[s+\sigma(s+Q\pi^2/s)]<0$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) vanishes at the displayed stationary threshold. Therefore its initial instability cannot be a [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md). This reduced model must not be confused with a three-variable magnetoconvection model carrying finite magnetic diffusivity and an additional oscillatory mode.
