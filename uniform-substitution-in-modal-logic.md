# Uniform substitution in modal logic

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)

[Uniform substitution in modal logic](uniform-substitution-in-modal-logic.md) replaces each occurrence of a [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) letter by the same [modal formula](modal-formula.md). Normal modal [formal proof](formal-proof.md) systems with axiom schemes are closed under this operation: substituted axioms remain instances, modus ponens is preserved, and necessitation commutes with substitution. Fresh auxiliary letters permit substituting a derived fixed point into fixed-point uniqueness theorems without changing other parameters.
