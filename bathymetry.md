# Bathymetry

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean](ocean.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathymetry)

The depth and shape of an underwater surface. Its variation affects [seismic travel times](seismic-travel-time.md) and the [water](water.md)-column contribution to a marine [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md).
