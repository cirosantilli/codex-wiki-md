# Tidal dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal heating](tidal-heating.md)

[Tidal dissipation](tidal-dissipation.md) converts [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) associated with a time-varying [tidal deformation](tidal-deformation.md) into [heat](heat.md). It can reduce [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), drive [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md), and contribute deep planetary heating. A [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) with exact [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md), perpendicular spin axis and no additional forcing has a static [tidal deformation](tidal-deformation.md) in the body's frame and need not sustain substantial [tidal heating](tidal-heating.md).
