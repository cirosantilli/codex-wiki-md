# Tidal dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal heating](tidal-heating.md)

Tidal dissipation converts mechanical [energy](energy.md) associated with a time-varying tidal deformation into [heat](heat.md). It can circularize an eccentric orbit, synchronize spin, and contribute deep planetary heating. A perfectly synchronized circular orbit with no additional forcing need not sustain substantial [tidal heating](tidal-heating.md).
