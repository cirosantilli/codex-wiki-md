# Climate feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)

A climate feedback is a response to [climate change](climate-change.md) that alters the initial warming or cooling. Positive feedback amplifies the change; negative feedback opposes it. Positive feedback alone does not prove an irreversible transition.
