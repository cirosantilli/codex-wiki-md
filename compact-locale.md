# Compact locale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

A [locale](locale.md) is compact when every directed family of opens whose join is $1$ contains $1$. Equivalently every open cover has a finite subcover. The truth-valued test $a\mapsto[a=1]$ is a [preframe](preframe.md) homomorphism exactly when the locale is compact.
