# Spacecraft trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Escape velocity](escape-velocity.md)

A [spacecraft trajectory](spacecraft-trajectory.md) is the path traced by a spacecraft's [position](position.md) over [time](time-in-physics.md). An unpowered exterior arc can approximate a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md); other bodies' [gravity](gravity.md), [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md), and propulsion can change it.
