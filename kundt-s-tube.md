<h1 id="kundt-s-tube">Kundt's tube</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kundt's_tube)

A [Kundt's tube](kundt-s-tube.md) turns an invisible [standing wave](standing-wave.md) into a measurable spatial pattern. A driven air column supports a [longitudinal wave](longitudinal-wave.md); measuring its [frequency](frequency.md) and the spacing of repeated [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md) gives the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md). The same drive can compare the [wavelengths](wavelength.md) in a gas and a vibrating solid. [August Kundt](august-kundt.md)'s 1866 paper introduced acoustic dust figures; its footnote explicitly counts successive [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md) as half a [wavelength](wavelength.md).[1866 publisher record; original p. 502 footnote on half-wavelength spacing](ueber-eine-neue-art-akustischer-staubfiguren-und-uber-die-anwendung-derselben-zur-bestimmung-der-schallgeschwindigkeit-in-festen-korpern-und-gasen-by-august-kundt.md)

The attractive feature is the separation of measurements: a clock or [frequency counter](frequency-counter.md) measures repetition in time, while a ruler measures repetition in space. Their product supplies a propagation [speed](speed.md). The [pressure](pressure-split.md) pattern and the fluid-motion pattern are different, however; identifying which one a detector responds to prevents a quarter-[wavelength](wavelength.md) mistake.
