# Root stability of a cubic multistep polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md)

The polynomial $\rho(w)=(w-1)(w^2+w+a)$ satisfies the [root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md) exactly for $0\leq a\leq1$. For $a<0$ a root is below $-1$; for $a>1$ the quadratic root product forces a root outside the unit disc. The endpoints have simple unit-modulus roots, and the repeated root at $a=1/4$ is strictly inside. A consistent method with this polynomial is convergent throughout the closed interval.
