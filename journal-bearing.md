# Journal bearing

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

A journal bearing supports a rotating axle inside a surrounding cylindrical sleeve with a thin lubricating film. An eccentric gap generates [lubrication pressure](lubrication-pressure.md) as rotation transports fluid into and out of narrower regions. A [full-film journal bearing](full-film-journal-bearing.md) model imposes periodic [pressure](pressure.md) around the entire circumference; a model with [cavitation](cavitation.md) needs additional free-boundary conditions. The two models can predict different loads and dynamics.
