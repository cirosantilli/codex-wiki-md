# Hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypergraph)

A hypergraph consists of a [vertex set](vertex-set.md) $V$ and a family $E$ of subsets of $V$, called hyperedges. Unlike a [graph](graph-split.md), a hyperedge may contain more than two vertices. A [uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) restricts all hyperedges to one fixed size.
