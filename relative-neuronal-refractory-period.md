# Relative neuronal refractory period

↑ **Parent:** [Neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md)

During a [relative neuronal refractory period](relative-neuronal-refractory-period.md), another [action potential](action-potential.md) is possible, but incomplete recovery changes the excitation needed to obtain it. Remaining [sodium](sodium.md) [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) and elevated [potassium](potassium.md) [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) both matter. A [hyperpolarization](hyperpolarization.md) after an [action potential](action-potential.md) can increase the voltage excursion needed for firing, while extra [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) can drain an injected pulse faster. Thus a single fixed [neuronal firing threshold](neuronal-firing-threshold.md) measured from the instantaneous voltage misses relevant state variables.[§2.2.3, Refractoriness, Figure 2.9: a stronger second pulse can evoke an earlier second spike; increased conductance shortens the voltage response](neuronal-dynamics-from-single-neurons-to-networks-and-models-of-cognition-by-wulfram-gerstner-and-others/2-2-hodgkin-huxley-model.md)

Recovery can depend on earlier spikes as well as the last one; the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md) and [neural adaptation](neural-adaptation.md) provide richer histories than an imposed reset. A fixed hold in a [leaky integrate-and-fire model](leaky-integrate-and-fire-model.md) is a useful simplification with a specified domain, rather than a complete explanation of a [relative neuronal refractory period](relative-neuronal-refractory-period.md).[§1.4.3, Adaptation, Refractoriness, and Burst Firing: dependence on preceding activity](neuronal-dynamics-from-single-neurons-to-networks-and-models-of-cognition-by-wulfram-gerstner-and-others/1-4-limitations-of-the-leaky-integrate-and-fire-model.md)
