# General-position hemisphere count

↑ **Parent:** [Open hemisphere](open-hemisphere.md)

Choose $N$ points of $S^d$ such that every $d+1$ of their position vectors are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Any equatorial hyperplane then contains at most $d$ points. If $N-d\geq2r-1$, at least one of each opposite pair of [open hemispheres](open-hemisphere.md) contains $r$ points. Such configurations exist: take distinct real parameters $t_i$ and normalize $(1,t_i,\ldots,t_i^d)$; the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) proves the required independence. This weaker one-of-two guarantee already suffices for the topological lower bound in [Lovász theorem on Kneser graphs](lovasz-theorem-on-kneser-graphs.md).
