# Cyclopentadiene

↑ **Parent:** [Diene](diene.md)

A five-carbon cyclic conjugated diene. Cycloaddition across its two [double bonds](double-bond.md) produces a norbornene framework while retaining the original five-membered [carbon](carbon.md) ring.
