# Topological group module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

A topological [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$ with a continuous action of a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) $G$ by group [automorphisms](automorphism.md). Continuity means joint continuity of $G\times A\to A$. For a discrete coefficient group this is equivalent to every element of $A$ having an open stabilizer.
