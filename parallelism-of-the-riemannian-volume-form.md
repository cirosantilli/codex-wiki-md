# Parallelism of the Riemannian volume form

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

The [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) of an oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) preserves its [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md). In a positively oriented orthonormal frame its connection matrix is skew-symmetric. Differentiating the wedge of the dual coframe gives minus the trace of that matrix times the [volume form](volume-form.md), which is zero. Equivalently [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) preserves the metric and orientation, so it preserves the normalized [volume form](volume-form.md).
