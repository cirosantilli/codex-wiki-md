# Langevin dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

[Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md) describes a dynamical variable driven by deterministic forces and random noise. [Underdamped Langevin dynamics](underdamped-langevin-dynamics.md) retains inertia, whereas [overdamped Langevin dynamics](overdamped-langevin-dynamics.md) neglects it. At [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md), the [fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle](fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-langevin-particle.md) relates [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) strength to drag and [temperature](temperature.md).
