# Convective hydrodynamic instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

A [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md) amplifies an advected localized disturbance while its amplitude decays at any fixed position. The distinction from [absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md) depends on the observation frame.
