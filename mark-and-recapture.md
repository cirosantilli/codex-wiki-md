# Mark and recapture

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_and_recapture)

Mark and recapture estimates [cardinality](cardinality.md) or survival from repeated observations after marking and releasing individuals. In the simplest closed-population experiment, overlap between two samples estimates the fraction marked. More elaborate [capture-recapture models](capture-recapture-model.md) allow changing survival and detection probabilities.
