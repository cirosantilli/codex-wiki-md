# Iterated consistency progression

↑ **Parent:** [Consistency-strength preorder](consistency-strength-preorder.md)

Starting from a recursively axiomatized theory $T_0$, define

$$
T_{n+1}=T_n+\operatorname{Con}(T_n).
$$

Assuming consistency, [Gödel second incompleteness theorem](godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md) makes every step strict in the [consistency-strength preorder](consistency-strength-preorder.md), while the effective union $T_\omega=\bigcup_{n<\omega}T_n$ lies strictly above every finite stage.
