# Translation invariance of Lebesgue measure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md)

Translating a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) by a real number preserves measurability and its [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). [Translation invariance of Lebesgue outer measure](translation-invariance-of-lebesgue-outer-measure.md) and [Carathéodory's criterion](caratheodory-s-criterion.md) give the measurability assertion and equality. Applying the result to [indicator functions](indicator-function.md), then [simple functions](simple-function.md), and finally increasing simple approximations gives $\int_{\mathbb R}u(x-t)\,dx=\int_{\mathbb R}u(x)\,dx$ for every nonnegative [Lebesgue measurable function](lebesgue-measurable-function.md). For a [Lebesgue integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md), apply the identity to its [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) and [negative part](negative-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md).
