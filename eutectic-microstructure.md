# Eutectic microstructure

↑ **Parent:** [Eutectic system](eutectic-system.md)

A two-solid [microstructure](microstructure.md) formed by the coupled transformation of liquid at a [eutectic point](eutectic-point.md). Lamellae and rods are common morphologies. A hypoeutectic alloy can contain both primary crystals and a [eutectic microstructure](eutectic-microstructure.md); those [microconstituents](microconstituent.md) are not the same accounting categories as its total phase fractions.
