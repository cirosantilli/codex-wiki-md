# Occupation time of a continuous-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

The occupation time records the duration spent in a state of a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md). Conditional on $Z(0)=r$, its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\int_0^t p_{rs}(u)\,du$, where $p_{rs}$ is the [transition probability](transition-probability.md).
