# Inertial-wave vorticity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial wave](inertial-wave.md)

Linearize the [Euler equation in a uniformly rotating frame](euler-equation-in-a-uniformly-rotating-frame.md) about rest and impose [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$. Taking the [curl](curl.md) eliminates the reduced [pressure](pressure-split.md). For constant $\boldsymbol\Omega$, $\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u)=-(\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$, giving the displayed [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) equation. Taking another curl and time derivative yields $\partial_t^2\nabla^2\mathbf u+4(\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\nabla)^2\mathbf u=0$. These equations describe the restoring influence of background rotation on small disturbances without buoyancy or viscosity.
