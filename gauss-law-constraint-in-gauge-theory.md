# Gauss law constraint in gauge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

The time component of a gauge field's equation of motion constrains the spatial fields and their momenta. In a [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) it has the covariant form $D_iE_i=\rho$; its Abelian form is [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md). Time-dependent [collective coordinates](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md) of a [gauge-theory soliton](gauge-theory-soliton.md) require a temporal gauge field, or an equivalent horizontal projection, that satisfies this constraint before the kinetic metric is read from the action.
