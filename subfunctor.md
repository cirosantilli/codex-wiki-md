# Subfunctor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor-split.md)

A subfunctor $H$ of a set-valued [functor](functor-split.md) $F$ assigns a subset $H(T)\subseteq F(T)$ to each object, preserved by every action of $F$ on a morphism. The inclusions form a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md). Equivalently, it is a [subobject](subobject.md) of $F$ in the [functor category](functor-category.md).
