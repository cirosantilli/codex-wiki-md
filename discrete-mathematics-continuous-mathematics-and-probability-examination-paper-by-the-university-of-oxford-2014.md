# Discrete Mathematics, Continuous Mathematics and Probability examination paper by the University of Oxford (2014)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The ten-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Computer Science Prelims paper dated 26 June 2014. The public [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `4223.pdf`. Question 1's displayed [function](function-split.md) requires a domain restriction. Question 3's fractional-size [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) needs a base case and an integer-rounding convention. Question 6's parameter range includes negative values, so the critical-point classification requires a split at $\alpha=-1$ as well as $\alpha=1$; its final derivative request also has a typographical superscript on $f$. These points were checked against printed pp. 2, 4 and 7.
