# Dual pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space-split.md)

The [dual pairing](dual-pairing.md) evaluates a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on a [vector](vector.md):

$$
\langle\varphi,v\rangle=\varphi(v),\qquad (\varphi,v)\in V^*\times V.
$$

It is [bilinear](bilinear-map.md): it is linear in each argument separately. Even over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), this algebraic evaluation has no [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). An [inner product](inner-product-split.md) instead has two [vectors](vector.md) as inputs and, over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), is [sesquilinear](sesquilinear-form.md). For $\varphi(x,y)=2x-y$ and $v=(3,1)$, the [dual pairing](dual-pairing.md) equals five. Its value is independent of the [basis](basis.md) used to describe its two inputs.
