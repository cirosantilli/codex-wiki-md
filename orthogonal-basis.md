# Orthogonal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal vectors](orthogonal-vectors.md)

An [orthogonal basis](orthogonal-basis.md) of an inner-product [vector space](vector-space-split.md) consists of nonzero mutually [orthogonal vectors](orthogonal-vectors.md) that span the space. The [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of $v$ onto a finite-dimensional span is $\sum_i\langle v,u_i\rangle u_i/\langle u_i,u_i\rangle$. Dividing each vector by its norm produces an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). For centered random variables, the inner product is [covariance](covariance.md); an orthogonal basis therefore consists of uncorrelated variables, without requiring unit variance.
