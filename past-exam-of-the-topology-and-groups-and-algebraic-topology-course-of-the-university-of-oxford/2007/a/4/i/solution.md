<h1 id="a/4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the [torus](../../../../../torus.md) with basepoint $(1,1)$ take $p(z,w)=(z^2,w)$ and $p'(z,w)=(z,w^2)$. Both are [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) two-sheeted coverings. Their induced subgroups of $\pi_1(S^1\times S^1)=\mathbb Z^2$ are $2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Z\times2\mathbb Z$, which are different. Equivalence over the fixed base would force equal based subgroups. Thus **the two degree-two covers are inequivalent**.
