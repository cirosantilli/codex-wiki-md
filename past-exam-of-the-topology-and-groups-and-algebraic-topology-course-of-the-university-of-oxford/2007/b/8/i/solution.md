↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Yes**, for a nonnegative integer $g$: the closed [orientable surface](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) of [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ has precisely these groups. A standard polygon cell structure has one vertex, $2g$ one-cells and one two-cell attached by a product of commutators. Its integral cellular boundary maps are zero, because each generator occurs once in each [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) in the attaching word. Thus $H_0=H_2=\mathbb Z$ and $H_1=\mathbb Z^{2g}$. At [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) zero use the sphere.
