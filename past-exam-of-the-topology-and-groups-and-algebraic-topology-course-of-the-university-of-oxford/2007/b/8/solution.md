<h1 id="b/8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

With the usual second-countability convention, an $n$-[manifold](../../../../topological-manifold-split.md) is a [Hausdorff](../../../../hausdorff-space.md) second-countable space in which every point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$. A [local orientation of a manifold](../../../../local-orientation-of-a-manifold.md) at $x$ is a choice of generator of

$$
H_n(M,M\setminus\{x\};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z.
$$

Excision and a coordinate ball identify this relative [group](../../../../group-split.md) with $H_n(\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})$. An [orientation of a topological manifold](../../../../orientation-of-a-topological-manifold.md) is a coherent choice of such generators: near each point the choices are induced by one relative class on a neighbourhood, equivalently by an atlas with [orientation](../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving transition maps.

For a [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) closed oriented [manifold](../../../../topological-manifold-split.md), its [fundamental class](../../../../fundamental-class.md) $[M]\in H_n(M;\mathbb Z)$ is the unique class mapping to every chosen local generator. Here closed means [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) without boundary. [Poincare duality](../../../../poincare-duality.md) states that cap product with this class is an isomorphism

$$
\boxed{-\frown[M]:H^k(M;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }H_{n-k}(M;\mathbb Z)}.
$$

Over rational coefficients it gives $b_k=b_{n-k}$. Therefore

$$
\chi(M)=\sum_k(-1)^kb_k=(-1)^n\sum_k(-1)^kb_k.
$$

When $n$ is odd this says $\chi=-\chi$, so **$\chi(M)=0$**. Torsion does not contribute to the Betti numbers or this [Euler characteristic](../../../../euler-characteristic.md).
