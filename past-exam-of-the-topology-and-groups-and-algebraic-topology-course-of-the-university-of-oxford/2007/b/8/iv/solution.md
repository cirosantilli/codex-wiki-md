**No.** The dimension would again be three. The [universal coefficient theorem](../../../../../universal-coefficient-theorem.md) gives $H^1=0$, since both $\operatorname{Hom}(H_1,\mathbb Z)$ and $\operatorname{Ext}(H_0,\mathbb Z)$ vanish. [Poincare duality](../../../../../poincare-duality.md) then forces $H_2=0$, contradicting the proposed [torsion group](../../../../../torsion-group.md). In general the second integral [homology](../../../../../homology-split.md) of a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) closed oriented three-[manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md) is free abelian, being dual to its first [homology](../../../../../homology-split.md).
