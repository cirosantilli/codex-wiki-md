↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**Yes:** real projective three-space $\mathbb{RP}^3$ has these groups. Its usual cell structure has one cell in each dimension zero through three, with boundary maps $\partial_1=0$, $\partial_2=2$ and $\partial_3=0$. Hence its integral [homology](../../../../../homology-split.md) is $\mathbb Z$ in degrees zero and three, $\mathbb Z/2$ in degree one, and zero in degree two. It is a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) closed oriented [manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md): the [antipodal map](../../../../../antipodal-map.md) on $S^3$ preserves [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), so the sphere's [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) descends to the quotient.
