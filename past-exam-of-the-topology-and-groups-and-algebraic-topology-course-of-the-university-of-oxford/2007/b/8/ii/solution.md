↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No.** Its top nonzero [group](../../../../../group-split.md) would force dimension three. [Poincare duality](../../../../../poincare-duality.md) requires $H_2\cong H^1$, and the [universal coefficient theorem](../../../../../universal-coefficient-theorem.md) gives $H^1\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_1,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z$, since $H_0$ is free. This contradicts the proposed vanishing of $H_2$. Equivalently its listed Betti numbers give [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $-1$, impossible for a closed oriented odd-dimensional [manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md).
