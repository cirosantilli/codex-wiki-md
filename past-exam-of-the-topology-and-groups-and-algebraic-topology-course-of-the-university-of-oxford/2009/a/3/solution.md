<h1 id="a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A [lift](../../../../lift-force-split.md) of $f:Y\to X$ is a continuous map $\widetilde f:Y\to\widetilde X$ satisfying $p\widetilde f=f$. The [covering homotopy theorem](../../../../homotopy-lifting-theorem-for-a-covering-map.md) says that a [homotopy](../../../../homotopy-split.md) $H:Y\times I\to X$ has a unique [lift](../../../../lift-force-split.md) once a continuous [lift](../../../../lift-force-split.md) of its initial map $H(-,0)$ is prescribed. No additional local connectivity assumption on $Y$ is needed for this [homotopy](../../../../homotopy-split.md)-lifting statement.

To prove injectivity on fundamental groups, let a loop $\widetilde\gamma$ based at $\widetilde b$ project to a based null-homotopic loop. [Lift](../../../../lift-force-split.md) its null-[homotopy](../../../../homotopy-split.md), starting with $\widetilde\gamma$. The two endpoint tracks [lift](../../../../lift-force-split.md) constant paths at $b$ starting at $\widetilde b$, so remain at $\widetilde b$ by uniqueness of path lifting. The final path lies in the discrete fibre over $b$ and starts at $\widetilde b$, so it is constant. Thus the lifted [homotopy](../../../../homotopy-split.md) contracts $\widetilde\gamma$ relative its basepoint, proving

$$
\boxed{p_*:\pi_1(\widetilde X,\widetilde b)\hookrightarrow\pi_1(X,b)}.
$$

In the following constructions, take $x$ around the [circle](../../../../circle.md) factor and $y$ around the projective-plane factor. Above each sheet point, the [circle](../../../../circle.md) has one incoming and one outgoing lifted $x$ edge. A fixed sheet for $y$ carries an identity cover of $\mathbb{RP}^2$; a transposed pair of sheets is joined as the antipodal fibre of a [sphere](../../../../sphere.md) covering $\mathbb{RP}^2$. These pieces give genuine covering neighbourhoods at the wedge point as well as away from it.
