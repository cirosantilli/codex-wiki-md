<h1 id="a/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) double cover $S^2\to\mathbb{RP}^2$ and denote the two points above the wedge point by $v_0,v_1$. Attach one $x$ [circle](../../../../../circle.md) at each point, mapping identically onto the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) summand. At both points there is one incoming and one outgoing $x$ branch, so this is a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) two-sheeted cover of the wedge.

The [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) is simply [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), while each attached [circle](../../../../../circle.md) supplies one generator. Join the two attachment points by an arc in the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) and collapse that contractible arc; the [fundamental group](../../../../../fundamental-group.md) is the [free product](../../../../../free-product.md) of the two [circle](../../../../../circle.md) groups, hence free of rank two. A [lift](../../../../../lift-force-split.md) of $y$ joins $v_0$ to $v_1$. The two loop generators therefore project to

$$
\boxed{x,\qquad yxy^{-1}=yxy}.
$$

Injectivity of the [covering map](../../../../../covering-space.md) identifies their subgroup with the **[free group](../../../../../free-group.md) on these two generators**.
