<h1 id="b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify $S^1$ with the unit complex numbers and put $\alpha_d(z)=z^d$ for any integer $d$, including the constant map for $d=0$. A [lift](../../../../../lift-force-split.md) of its angular coordinate to the real line is $t\mapsto dt$, so it sends the oriented [circle](../../../../../circle.md)'s [fundamental class](../../../../../fundamental-class.md) to $d$ times itself. Thus $\deg(\alpha_d)=d$.

The natural [suspension isomorphism](../../../../../suspension-isomorphism.md) in [reduced homology](../../../../../reduced-homology.md) identifies $\widetilde H_{j+1}(\Sigma X)$ with $\widetilde H_j(X)$ and intertwines a map with its suspension. This [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) follows from [Mayer-Vietoris sequence](../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) for the two contractible suspension cones, and its naturality gives the intertwining. Since $\Sigma S^j\cong S^{j+1}$, suspending a [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) self-map preserves degree. Repeating this construction gives

$$
\boxed{\text{a self-map of }S^n\text{ of every degree }d\in\mathbb Z,\quad n\geq1}.
$$

The positive-dimension convention is necessary for the arbitrary-degree assertion; self-maps of the two-point space do not realize arbitrary integers on [reduced homology](../../../../../reduced-homology.md).
