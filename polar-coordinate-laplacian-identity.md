# Polar-coordinate Laplacian identity

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md)

In $\mathbb R^{d+1}$, $g=dr^2+r^2g_{S^d}$ and the volume density is $r^d$. With the nonnegative [Laplacian](laplacian.md) convention, $\widetilde\Delta=-\partial_r^2-(d/r)\partial_r+r^{-2}\Delta_{S^d}$. The identity separates radial homogeneity from the angular [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) and derives the [spectrum of the Laplacian on a sphere](spectrum-of-the-laplacian-on-a-sphere.md).
