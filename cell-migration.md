# Cell migration

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)

[Cell migration](cell-migration.md) is movement of a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) relative to its surroundings. In crawling cells, contact forces with a substrate can propel the cell, while [chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md) can bias its direction in response to a chemical [gradient](gradient.md).
