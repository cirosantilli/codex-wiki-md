# Finite representation type of a quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

A [quiver](quiver.md) has finite representation type over a [field](field.md) when it has only finitely many [isomorphism classes](isomorphism.md) of indecomposable finite-dimensional [representations](representation-of-a-quiver.md).
