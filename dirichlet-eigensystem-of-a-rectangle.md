# Dirichlet eigensystem of a rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Laplacian](dirichlet-laplacian.md)

On $0<x<L_x$, $0<y<L_y$ with positive side lengths and zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) for $-\Delta u=\lambda u$ gives

$$
u_{mn}(x,y)=\sin(m\pi x/L_x)\sin(n\pi y/L_y),\qquad m,n\geq1,
$$

with the displayed [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). The one-dimensional endpoint conditions exclude constant and hyperbolic factors and force the positive integer sine frequencies. The product [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) basis is complete in the square-integrable functions on the rectangle. Consequently an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) with prescribed $\lambda$ is a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of exactly those modes with $\lambda_{mn}=\lambda$; if none match, only the zero solution exists. Distinct pairs with the same sum give an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) with [geometric multiplicity](geometric-multiplicity.md) greater than one. On a square of side $\pi$, for instance, $\lambda=25$ has precisely the pairs $(3,4)$ and $(4,3)$, so its [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) two.
