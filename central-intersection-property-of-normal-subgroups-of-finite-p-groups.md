# Central intersection property of normal subgroups of finite p-groups

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)

A nontrivial [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $N$ of a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) meets the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md) nontrivially. The [conjugation action](conjugation-action.md) of $G$ on $N$ has singleton orbits exactly at $N\cap Z(G)$. The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) makes every other orbit size divisible by $p$, so $|N\cap Z(G)|\equiv|N|\equiv0\pmod p$. Since the intersection contains the identity, its size is at least $p$.
