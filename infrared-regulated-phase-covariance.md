# Infrared-regulated phase covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-difference variance](phase-difference-variance.md)

Fix the uniform [order-parameter phase](order-parameter-phase.md) and retain finite system size $L$ before evaluating a massless [Gaussian functional integral](gaussian-functional-integral.md). In $d\le2$, its coincident and separated [order-parameter phase](order-parameter-phase.md) [covariances](covariance.md) each acquire a divergent additive constant as $L\to\infty$. Their difference $2[G_L(0)-G_L(r)]$ instead has a finite fixed-separation limit for $d>0$. This is why physical [XY model](xy-model.md) [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) are obtained from [Gaussian phase averaging](gaussian-phase-averaging.md) of angle differences. An [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) also remains necessary for coincident-point quantities; infrared cancellation does not remove it. The logarithmic two-dimensional [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) follows from the $d\to2$ power-law expression only after subtracting its constant pole.
