# Decay of coefficients of an almost-everywhere convergent trigonometric series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

If symmetric partial sums $\sum_{r=-n}^n a_re^{irt}$ converge almost everywhere on the circle, then $a_n,a_{-n}\to0$. Their successive differences $T_n=a_ne^{int}+a_{-n}e^{-int}$ tend to zero almost everywhere. If $M_n=|a_n|+|a_{-n}|$ failed to tend to zero, choose a subsequence with $M_n\ge\varepsilon$. The normalized $T_n/M_n$ are bounded by one and tend to zero almost everywhere, so [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) makes their squared integrals tend to zero. [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md) instead makes those integrals $(|a_n|^2+|a_{-n}|^2)/M_n^2\ge1/2$, a contradiction.
