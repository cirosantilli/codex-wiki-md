# Scalar shell integral in six dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md)

For $m>0$ and $0<\Lambda<\Lambda_0$, define

$$
I_r=\int_{\Lambda<|q|<\Lambda_0}\frac{d^6q}{(2\pi)^6}(q^2+m^2)^{-r}=\frac1{64\pi^3}\int_\Lambda^{\Lambda_0}\frac{q^5\,dq}{(q^2+m^2)^r}.
$$

The radial formula uses the area $\pi^3$ of the unit five-sphere. Its [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) is $6-2r$. As $\Lambda_0\to\infty$, $I_1$ diverges quartically, $I_2$ quadratically, $I_3$ logarithmically, and $I_r$ is ultraviolet finite for $r\ge4$ at fixed $\Lambda,m$. This follows directly by comparison with $q^{5-2r}$.
