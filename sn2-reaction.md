# SN2 reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SN2_reaction)

An [SN2 reaction](sn2-reaction.md) couples backside nucleophilic bond formation to leaving-group departure in one elementary substitution step. At a suitable stereogenic reaction centre, the geometry produces inversion.
