# Hodge star on top holomorphic forms

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Hodge star operator](complex-hodge-star-operator.md)

For the complex-linear [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) and complex orientation on a [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$, every $(n,0)$-form has the displayed eigenvalue. In an adapted orthonormal real coframe, each factor $dx_j+i\,dy_j$ has two-dimensional star eigenvalue $-i$. Regrouping the $n$ factors introduces $(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}$, giving $(-1)^{n(n+1)/2}i^n$. This is a pointwise metric identity; the metric need not be [Kähler](kahler-manifold.md).
