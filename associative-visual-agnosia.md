# Associative visual agnosia

↑ **Parent:** [Visual agnosia](visual-agnosia.md)

A visual-recognition disorder attributed to difficulty accessing meaning from a relatively adequate visual representation. Copying can provide evidence about [perception](perception.md), but line-by-line copying alone does not demonstrate normal integration.[1971, copying and visual recognition](associative-visual-agnosia-by-alan-b-rubens-and-others.md)
