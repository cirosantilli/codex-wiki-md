# Lower p-series

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)

The displayed descending [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) combines powers and [group commutators](group-commutator.md). In a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md), take the [closure](closure-topology.md) of the generated product at each step. For a [powerful p-group](powerful-p-group.md) these layers are $P_i(G)=G^{p^{i-1}}$, with iterated closed power [subgroups](subgroup.md) in the infinite case. The successive quotients are [elementary abelian p-groups](elementary-abelian-group.md).
