# Twin-prime constant

↑ **Parent:** [Twin prime conjecture](twin-prime-conjecture.md)

This positive convergent product corrects for the dependence of primality of $n$ and $n+2$ at each odd prime. The associated singular series is $2C_2$: the factor at two is two, and at an odd prime the local correction is $(1-2/p)/(1-1/p)^2$. It appears in the [Hardy–Littlewood twin-prime asymptotic conjecture](hardy-littlewood-twin-prime-asymptotic-conjecture.md) and in upper bounds from the [Selberg sieve](selberg-sieve.md).
