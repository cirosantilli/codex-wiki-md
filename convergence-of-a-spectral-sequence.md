# Convergence of a spectral sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral sequence](spectral-sequence.md)

The stable page identifies the [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md) of a filtration of the abutment. For a finite filtration this is an exhaustive, separated finite extension description. It does not itself determine a canonical splitting. Unbounded filtrations require further convergence hypotheses.
