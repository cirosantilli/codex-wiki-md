# Truncated polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial quotient ring](polynomial-quotient-ring.md)

For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$ and $n\geq1$, this [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) imposes $Y^n=0$. Each class has a unique representative $a_0+a_1Y+\cdots+a_{n-1}Y^{n-1}$ by [monic polynomial division over a ring](monic-polynomial-division-over-a-ring.md). Multiplication is ordinary [polynomial multiplication](polynomial-multiplication.md) followed by discarding all powers of $Y$ of exponent at least $n$.
