# Durfee square

↑ **Parent:** [Young diagram](young-diagram.md)

The largest square of cells in the upper-left corner of a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md). For an [integer partition](integer-partition.md) $\lambda$, its side length is $d=\max\{i:\lambda_i\ge i\}$.
