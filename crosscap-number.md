# Crosscap number

↑ **Parent:** [Classification theorem for surfaces](classification-theorem-for-surfaces.md)

For a closed connected [nonorientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md), the crosscap number is the number of [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) summands in its [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) classification. It is at least one, and its [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is $2-h$. The projective plane has one crosscap and the [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md) has two.
