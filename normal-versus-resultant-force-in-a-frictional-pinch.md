# Normal versus resultant force in a frictional pinch

↑ **Parent:** [Friction angle](friction-angle.md)

For symmetric upper faces inclined by alpha from the vertical, inward normals push partly downward. Limiting upward friction gives vertical support $2N(\mu\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)$. Balancing weight yields the displayed minimum normal grip. The corresponding minimum total contact magnitude per face is $N_{\min}\sqrt{1+\mu^2}$. It is achieved at the limiting [friction angle](friction-angle.md). The formula assumes symmetric contacts with cancelling horizontal force and torque and positive denominator.
