# Tide

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal force](tidal-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide)

A [tide](tide.md) is a periodic change in ocean level driven mainly by the differential gravitational [forces](force-split.md) of the [Moon](moon.md) and [Sun](sun.md), together with the [Earth](earth.md)'s rotation. Their [tidal forces](tidal-force.md) act across the extended planet; a uniform acceleration shared by the whole Earth does not by itself generate a tide. Ocean-basin [resonance](resonance.md) and coastal geometry modify the local response.
