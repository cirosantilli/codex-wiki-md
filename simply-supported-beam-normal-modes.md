# Simply supported beam normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md)

A uniform [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) with endpoint conditions $y=y_{xx}=0$ has [normal modes](normal-mode.md) $\sin(n\pi x/L)$ for positive integers $n$. Substitute $y=Xe^{-i\omega t}$ into $EIy_{xxxx}+\rho_\ell y_{tt}=0$ and impose the four endpoint conditions to obtain the displayed spectrum. Here $\rho_\ell$ is [mass](mass.md) per unit [length](length.md), $I$ is a [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md), and $EI$ is [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md). [frequencies](frequency.md) proportional to $n^2$ distinguish [flexural waves](flexural-wave.md) from the nondispersive ideal stretched string.
