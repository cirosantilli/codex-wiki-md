# Pullback of a Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

For a smooth map $f:M\to N$, the pullback of a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) is $(f^*g)_p(u,v)=g_{f(p)}(df_pu,df_pv)$. It is positive definite precisely when $df_p$ is injective at every point, that is, when $f$ is an [immersion](immersion.md). In coordinates, $(f^*g)_{ij}=g_{ab}(f(y))\partial_i f^a\partial_j f^b$.
