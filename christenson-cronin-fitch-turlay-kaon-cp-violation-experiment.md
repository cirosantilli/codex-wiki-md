# Christenson-Cronin-Fitch-Turlay kaon CP violation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

[James H. Christenson](james-h-christenson.md), [James Watson Cronin](james-watson-cronin.md), [Val Logsdon Fitch](val-logsdon-fitch.md) and [René Turlay](rene-turlay.md) reported the result in 1964. At Brookhaven's AGS, 30 GeV [protons](proton.md) struck beryllium; [collimators](collimator.md) selected a neutral [particle beam](particle-beam.md) at 30 degrees. A downstream helium decay volume reduced interactions.[p.138, preparation and helium volume](evidence-for-the-2-pi-decay-of-the-k%E2%82%82%E2%81%B0-meson-by-james-h-christenson-and-others.md)

The flight distance suppressed the short-lived [kaon](kaon.md) component. Each arm of a two-arm [magnetic spectrometer](magnetic-spectrometer.md) had [spark chambers](spark-chamber.md) before and after its magnet. [Cherenkov detectors](cherenkov-detector.md) and [scintillation counters](scintillation-counter.md) triggered photographic recording in coincidence.[Fitch, The Discovery of Charge-Conjugation Parity Asymmetry, pp.598–600, apparatus and reconstruction](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-3.pdf)

**[Figure 3](#image-from-a-neutral-kaon-beam-to-a-two-track-invariant-mass). From a neutral kaon beam to a two-track invariant mass**. Original functional schematic, not a scale drawing. The apparatus functions follow the 1964 paper p.138 and Fitch's lecture pp.598–600. The lower example is dimensionless synthetic [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md), not a historical event.

The helium sample yielded $45\pm9$ signal events; $(2.0\pm0.4)\times10^{-3}$ was the ratio to all charged [decay channels](decay-channel.md).[pp.139–140, forward peak and charged-mode ratio](evidence-for-the-2-pi-decay-of-the-k%E2%82%82%E2%81%B0-meson-by-james-h-christenson-and-others.md) That denominator must be retained when comparing this result with a total [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md).

The evidence was kinematic. Later regenerated-beam [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md) established that the short- and long-lived states reached the same two-[pion](pion.md) final state, excluding alternatives such as distinct pion-like particles.[Cronin, CP Symmetry Violation — The Search for Its Origin, pp.570–573, original inference and subsequent interference](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-3.pdf) The 1964 observation established evidence for [CP violation](cp-violation.md); it did not determine every parameter of the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). Inferring [time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md) violation from [CP violation](cp-violation.md) invokes [CPT symmetry](cpt-symmetry.md), rather than a direct comparison of time-reversed transitions in that experiment.
