# Oriented bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Directed percolation](directed-percolation.md)

Independently open the bonds of a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) with a common [probability](probability.md) $p$, and ask whether an infinite open directed path starts at a specified root. On the oriented [square lattice](square-lattice.md), edges run from $(x,n)$ to $(x\pm1,n+1)$ with $x+n$ even. The critical probability is the infimum of parameters with positive survival probability. The [incoming-edge coupling of site and bond percolation](incoming-edge-coupling-of-site-and-bond-percolation.md) compares this threshold with that of [oriented site percolation](oriented-site-percolation.md).
