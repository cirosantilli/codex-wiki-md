# Molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecule)

A molecule consists of two or more [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) joined by [chemical bonds](chemical-bond.md), with zero net [electric charge](electric-charge.md). This follows the terminology of the [IUPAC Gold Book, molecule](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/M04002). The [continuum approximation](continuum-approximation.md) uses bulk fields in place of individual molecular positions and motions.
