# Chronic disease

↑ **Parent:** [Disease](disease.md)

A chronic disease persists over an extended period. A [continuous-time multi-state model](continuous-time-multi-state-model.md) can represent its progression through severity states, but long duration alone does not imply irreversible progression or justify ignoring state misclassification.
