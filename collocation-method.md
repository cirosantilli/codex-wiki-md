# Collocation method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collocation_method)

A [collocation method](collocation-method.md) chooses an approximate solution from a finite-dimensional trial space and forces the residual of a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) to vanish at selected nodes. [Collocation Runge-Kutta methods](collocation-runge-kutta-method.md) apply this principle to polynomial approximations on each time step.
