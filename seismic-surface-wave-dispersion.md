# Seismic surface-wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

[Seismic surface-wave dispersion](seismic-surface-wave-dispersion.md) is the [frequency](frequency.md) dependence of [Rayleigh wave](rayleigh-wave.md) or [Love wave](love-wave.md) propagation caused by depth-dependent elastic properties, mode structure and possibly intrinsic anelasticity. [Phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is $\omega/k$; [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $d\omega/dk$. Different [periods](period-of-a-function.md) and branches have different depth sensitivities, enabling [surface-wave tomography](surface-wave-tomography.md).
