# Friis transmission equation

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna (radio)](antenna-radio.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friis_transmission_equation)

The [Friis transmission equation](friis-transmission-equation.md) describes a far-field free-space antenna link with aligned directions, matched polarizations and negligible additional loss. It follows by multiplying the transmitter's axial [energy flux](energy-flux.md) $P_tG_t/(4\pi d^2)$ by the receiver's [antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md) $\lambda^2G_r/(4\pi)$. A receiving impedance mismatch multiplies the available received power by $1-|\Gamma_L|^2$. Wavelength scaling must state which gains, apertures and impedances are held fixed.
