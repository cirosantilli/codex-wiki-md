# Fungus

↑ **Parent:** [Eukaryote](eukaryote.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungus)

A [fungus](fungus.md) is a [eukaryote](eukaryote.md) that obtains nutrients by absorption rather than [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md). [Fungi](fungus.md) include unicellular [yeasts](yeast.md) and multicellular forms. Their [cell walls](cell-wall.md) differ from bacterial [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md) walls; having a [cell wall](cell-wall.md) does not make a [fungus](fungus.md) a [plant](plant.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 24.1: fungal nutrition and organization](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/24-1-characteristics-of-fungi).
