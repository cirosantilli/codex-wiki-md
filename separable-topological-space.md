# Separable topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is separable when it has a countable [dense subset](dense-set.md). The topology is part of the assertion: a dual may be separable in its [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) and not in its [norm topology](norm-topology.md). A compact [metric space](metric-space.md) is separable by taking the union of finite nets at scales tending to zero.
