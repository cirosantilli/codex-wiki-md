# Compact subsets of Tychonoff spaces are C-embedded

↑ **Parent:** [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md)

A [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on a [compact subset](compact-space.md) of a [Tychonoff space](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md) extends to the whole space. Embed the space into a cube of closed unit intervals. The [Tychonoff theorem](tychonoff-s-theorem.md) makes the cube [compact](compact-space.md) [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) and thus normal. The [compact subset](compact-space.md) is closed in that cube, so the [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md) extends its bounded function to the cube. Restrict this extension to the embedded original space. Normality of the original space is unnecessary.
