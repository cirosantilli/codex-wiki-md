# Closed-subgroup theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed-subgroup_theorem)

Every closed [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a finite-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md) is an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) and a [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md). Closedness alone forces the compatible smooth structure; smoothness need not be assumed in advance. The proof constructs the [Lie algebra of a closed subgroup](lie-algebra-of-a-closed-subgroup.md) from [one-parameter subgroups](one-parameter-subgroup.md), then uses [local product coordinates for a closed subgroup](local-product-coordinates-for-a-closed-subgroup.md).
