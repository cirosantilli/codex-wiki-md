# Crystallization

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

Formation of a [crystal](crystal.md) from a disordered phase. In a cooling [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md), [crystallization](crystallization.md) of the ionic interior releases latent heat and can redistribute chemical species, affecting the relation between [luminosity](luminosity.md) and [white-dwarf cooling age](white-dwarf-cooling-age.md).
