# Future null infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal completion](conformal-completion.md)

Future null infinity is the boundary reached by future-directed outgoing null geodesics in an [asymptotically flat spacetime](asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md). In four dimensions it has topology $\mathbb R\times S^2$ under the standard asymptotic-flatness assumptions.

It is the future component of [null infinity](null-infinity.md); the past component is $\mathcal I^-$.
