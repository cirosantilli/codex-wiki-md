# Log-density domination in an exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential family](exponential-family-split.md)

On a bounded parameter set, $\log f_\theta(x)=\theta x-K(\theta)+\log f_0(x)$ has an [integrable envelope of a function class](integrable-envelope-of-a-function-class.md) whenever $K$ is bounded and $|X|+|\log f_0(X)|$ is integrable under the sampling law. A [compact](compact-space.md) subset of the interior natural-parameter domain bounds the [cumulant function of an exponential family](cumulant-function-of-an-exponential-family.md) and provides moments under model sampling; log-base-density [integrability](integrability.md) is an additional condition.
