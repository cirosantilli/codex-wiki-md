<h1 id="mumford-s-compactness-theorem">Mumford's compactness theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Moduli space of Riemann surfaces](moduli-space-of-riemann-surfaces.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumford's_compactness_theorem)

The subset of the unmarked [moduli space of Riemann surfaces](moduli-space-of-riemann-surfaces.md) with [hyperbolic systole](hyperbolic-systole.md) at least $\varepsilon>0$ is [compact](compact-space.md) for fixed [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g\geq2$. Use a [Bers pants decomposition theorem](bers-pants-decomposition-theorem.md) bound, finitely many topological decomposition types and [Dehn twists](dehn-twist.md) to place representatives in finitely many [compact](compact-space.md) boxes in [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md).
