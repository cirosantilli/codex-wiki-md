# Radiative temperature uncertainty after background subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Background subtraction](background-subtraction.md)

If emitted [power](power.md) obeys $P=AT^4$ with known constant $A$, then small relative [temperature](temperature-split.md) uncertainty is one quarter of relative net-power uncertainty. For measured quantities $X,Y$ with [covariance](covariance.md) $C_{XY}$, $\operatorname{Var}(X-Y)=\sigma_X^2+\sigma_Y^2-2C_{XY}$. A weak net signal obtained by subtracting a large background can therefore have poor relative precision even when both original [powers](power.md) are measured accurately. Correlated [standard uncertainties](standard-uncertainty.md) and worst-case error bounds require different propagation rules.
