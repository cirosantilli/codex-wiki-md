# Centripetal acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Circular motion](circular-motion.md)

For uniform [circular motion](circular-motion.md) of radius $R$ and [angular speed](angular-speed.md) $\omega$, the [acceleration](acceleration.md) is directed toward the centre and has magnitude $\omega^2R=v^2/R$. Writing $\mathbf r$ for the [position](position.md) relative to the centre gives $\ddot{\mathbf r}=-\omega^2\mathbf r$. The required resultant [force](force.md) is $m\ddot{\mathbf r}$; it may be supplied by a [normal force](normal-force.md), gravity, or another physical interaction.
