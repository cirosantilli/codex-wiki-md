<h1 id="lopsided-lovasz-local-lemma">Lopsided Lovász local lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md)

Let $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ be bad events with a lopsidependency graph. If numbers $x_i\in[0,1)$ satisfy

$$
\mathbb P(A_i)\leq x_i\prod_{j\sim i}(1-x_j)
$$

for every $i$, then $\mathbb P(\bigcap_iA_i^c)>0$. Unlike the ordinary [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md), a lopsidependency graph may omit pairs whose interaction can only make their simultaneous avoidance easier.
