# Focal-distance identity for a hyperbola

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbola](hyperbola.md)

For $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$, let $c=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ and place the [foci of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) at $(\pm c,0)$. On the right branch write $x=a\cosh t$, $y=b\sinh t$. Direct expansion gives

$$
d_-^2=(x+c)^2+y^2=(c\cosh t+a)^2,\qquad
d_+^2=(x-c)^2+y^2=(c\cosh t-a)^2.
$$

Since $c>a>0$, both square roots are positive and $d_--d_+=2a$. On the reflected left branch the signed difference reverses, while its absolute value stays $2a$. This proves the constant-difference characterization of a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) and keeps track of which branch a [hyperbolic function](hyperbolic-function.md) parametrization covers.
