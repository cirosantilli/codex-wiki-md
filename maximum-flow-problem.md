# Maximum flow problem

↑ **Parent:** [Flow network](flow-network.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_flow_problem)

The maximum flow problem maximises the [strength of a flow](strength-of-a-flow.md) from a source to a sink subject to [flow conservation](flow-conservation.md) and [flow network edge capacities](flow-network-edge-capacity.md). The [max-flow min-cut theorem](max-flow-min-cut-theorem.md) identifies the optimum with the least capacity of a [cut of a flow network](cut-of-a-flow-network.md). A feasible [flow](flow.md) and a [cut of a flow network](cut-of-a-flow-network.md) of equal value certify optimality without requiring a particular choice of [augmenting paths](augmenting-path.md).
