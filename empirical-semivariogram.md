# Empirical semivariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Semivariogram](semivariogram.md)

For a distance bin $B$, the [empirical semivariogram](empirical-semivariogram.md) is $\widehat\gamma(B)=(2|B|)^{-1}\sum_{(i,j)\in B}(z_i-z_j)^2$. A nonconstant drift adds half its squared pairwise differences to the expected raw [semivariogram](semivariogram.md). Residual [semivariograms](semivariogram.md) estimate spatial dependence after adjusting for the drift.
