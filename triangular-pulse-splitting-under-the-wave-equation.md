# Triangular pulse splitting under the wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [D'Alembert's formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md)

For the [triangular function](triangular-function.md) $f(x)=(1-|x|)_+$ released with zero initial velocity in the whole-line [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), the [D'Alembert formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md) gives the displayed solution. Here $r_+=\max(r,0)$. Two equal half-amplitude triangles translate in opposite directions, overlapping at early times and separating after $|c|t>1$. This defines a [weak solution](weak-solution.md), because the initial slope has jumps; away from the translated corner lines it is a classical solution. Its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) is $2(1-\cos k)\cos(ckt)/k^2$, with removable value one at $k=0$.
