# Orbital derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md)

The orbital derivative of a differentiable function $V$ along the autonomous system $\dot x=f(x)$ is

$$
\dot V(x)=\nabla V(x)\mathbin{\cdot}f(x).
$$

It is the ordinary [derivative](derivative.md) of $V(x(t))$ along each trajectory.
