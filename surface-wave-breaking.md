# Surface-wave breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

A [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) breaks when its smooth free-surface motion becomes unstable and overturns or forms a strongly dissipative front. In the nondispersive [shallow-water approximation](shallow-water-approximation.md), intersecting compressive [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) signal failure of a smooth [simple wave](simple-wave.md); a [hydraulic bore](hydraulic-bore.md) replaces it at the integral-model level. Dispersion can instead produce an oscillatory bore, while physical breaking involves turbulence and energy loss beyond the inviscid equations.
