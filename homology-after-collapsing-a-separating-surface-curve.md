# Homology after collapsing a separating surface curve

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsing a simple closed curve on a surface](collapsing-a-simple-closed-curve-on-a-surface.md)

Collapse a separating simple closed curve on a [closed orientable surface](closed-orientable-surface.md) of genus $g$. If its two sides have genera $g_1,g_2$, the quotient is homeomorphic to $\Sigma_{g_1}\vee\Sigma_{g_2}$. Its [integral homology](integral-homology.md) is $\mathbb Z$ in degree zero, $\mathbb Z^{2g}$ in degree one, $\mathbb Z^2$ in degree two, and zero above degree two. In [relative homology](relative-homology.md), the [circle](circle.md) maps to zero in $H_1(\Sigma_g)$, and the second relative group fits into a split sequence $0\to\mathbb Z\to H_2(\Sigma_g,A)\to\mathbb Z\to0$. The two collapsed sides retain independent [fundamental classes](fundamental-class.md).
