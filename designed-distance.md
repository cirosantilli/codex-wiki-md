# Designed distance

↑ **Parent:** [BCH code](bch-code.md)

The [designed distance](designed-distance.md) of a [BCH code](bch-code.md) is the value $\delta$ specified by requiring $\delta-1$ consecutive [defining zeros of a cyclic code](zero-of-a-cyclic-code.md). The [BCH bound](bch-bound.md) guarantees [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) at least $\delta$, but the actual [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) can be larger. To prove equality, one can exhibit a nonzero [codeword](codeword.md) of [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) exactly $\delta$.
