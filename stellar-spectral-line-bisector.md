# Stellar spectral-line bisector

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar noise](stellar-noise.md)

The midpoint of the two wings of a stellar absorption line at each depth. A changing line bisector can reveal profile distortions caused by active regions or blends rather than a rigid Doppler shift of the whole spectrum.
