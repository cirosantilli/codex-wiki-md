# Galilean spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md)

A geometric background for [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md) consisting of a four-dimensional [affine space](affine-space.md), an absolute time covector $t_a$, a rank-three positive spatial inverse [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) $h^{ab}$ with $t_ah^{ab}=0$, and a flat torsion-free [affine connection](affine-connection.md) preserving both. A trajectory parametrized by absolute time has tangent $u$ with $t_a u^a=1$. Its absolute [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $\nabla_u u$; an unforced particle obeys $\nabla_u u=0$. The structure distinguishes [inertial motion](inertial-motion.md) without selecting an absolute state of uniform rest. Additional force laws, including a Newtonian gravitational force when wanted, specify a dynamical theory on this background.
