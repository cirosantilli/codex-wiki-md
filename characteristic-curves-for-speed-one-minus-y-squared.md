# Characteristic curves for speed one minus y squared

↑ **Parent:** [Travel-time coordinate for a one-dimensional variable-speed wave equation](travel-time-coordinate-for-a-one-dimensional-variable-speed-wave-equation.md)

For $c(y)=1-y^2$ in the strip $|y|<1$, the travel-time coordinate is $s(y)=\operatorname{artanh}y$. The [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) are

$$
\operatorname{artanh}y\pm x=\text{constant},
$$

equivalently $y=\tanh(C\mp x)$. Outside the strip the same separated [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) gives [hyperbolic cotangent](hyperbolic-cotangent.md) branches, and the equilibrium curves $y=\pm1$ are also characteristic.
