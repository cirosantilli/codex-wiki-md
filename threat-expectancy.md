# Threat expectancy

↑ **Parent:** [Threat appraisal](threat-appraisal.md)

An expectation that a specified adverse outcome will occur under particular conditions. An informative [behavioral experiment](behavioral-experiment.md) states the prediction and what observed outcome would count against it.
