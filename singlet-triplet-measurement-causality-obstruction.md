# Singlet-triplet measurement causality obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic causality constraint on an ideal nonlocal measurement](relativistic-causality-constraint-on-an-ideal-nonlocal-measurement.md)

The binary [Lüders rule](luders-rule.md) measurement of singlet versus triplet preserves the entire triplet subspace, but its nonselective channel permits signalling. Starting with $|00\rangle$, Bob retains $|0\rangle$. If Alice instead applies a local [Pauli X gate](pauli-x-gate.md), the input is $|10\rangle$ and the channel outputs the equal mixture of $|\Psi^+\rangle$ and $|\Psi^-\rangle$; Bob has [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) $I/2$. Thus no spacelike local implementation can realize this channel. A [Bell-state nondemolition measurement](bell-state-nondemolition-measurement.md) has a finer triplet readout and a different nonselective channel, so this obstruction does not exclude it.
