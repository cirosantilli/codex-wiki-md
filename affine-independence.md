# Affine independence

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

Points $p_0,\ldots,p_k$ in an [affine space](affine-space.md) are [affinely independent](affine-independence.md) if the only affine dependence displayed above has all coefficients zero, using any choice of origin to read the [vector](vector.md) equation. Since the coefficients sum to zero, replacing the origin leaves that equation unchanged. Substituting $\lambda_0=-\sum_{i=1}^k\lambda_i$ shows that this condition is equivalent to [linear independence](linear-independence.md) of $p_1-p_0,\ldots,p_k-p_0$. It also says that an affine combination of the points has unique coefficients. In $\mathbb R^n$, at most $n+1$ points can be [affinely independent](affine-independence.md); $n+1$ such points span a full-dimensional [simplex](simplex.md).
