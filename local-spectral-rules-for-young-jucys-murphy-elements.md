<h1 id="local-spectral-rules-for-young-jucys-murphy-elements">Local spectral rules for Young–Jucys–Murphy elements</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md)

For the [joint spectrum](joint-spectrum.md) of the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](jucys-murphy-element.md), adjacent coordinates $a_i,a_{i+1}$ are distinct. If $a_{i+1}-a_i=\pm1$, the adjacent [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) $s_i$ acts on that line by $\pm1$. Otherwise interchanging the coordinates gives a spectral vector in the same [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). These rules follow from $s_iX_is_i+s_i=X_{i+1}$ and its two-dimensional eigenspace calculation. Together with the [braid relation in a Coxeter group](braid-relation-in-a-coxeter-group.md), they exclude the consecutive patterns $(a,a\pm1,a)$.
