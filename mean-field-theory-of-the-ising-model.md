# Mean-field theory of the Ising model

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

The [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) neglects products of [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) deviations, replacing each [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md)'s neighbours by the common [spin magnetization](spin-magnetization.md). With [coordination number of a lattice](coordination-number-of-a-lattice.md) $q$, the effective field is $h+qJm$. The [self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md) equates the assumed mean [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) with its independent-spin expectation in that effective field. It predicts $k_BT_c=qJ$ and the usual quartic [mean-field critical exponents](mean-field-critical-exponent.md), while neglecting spatially correlated fluctuations.
