# Periodic N-point rectangle-rule error

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic trapezoidal Fourier aliasing](periodic-trapezoidal-fourier-aliasing.md)

For a two-periodic function and $N$ equally spaced samples,

$$
\frac2N\sum_{k=-N/2+1}^{N/2}h(2k/N)
=2\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}\widehat h_{jN}.
$$

Consequently

$$
e_N(h)=-2\sum_{j\ne0}\widehat h_{jN}.
$$

Exponential [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) decay therefore gives exponential convergence in $N$.
