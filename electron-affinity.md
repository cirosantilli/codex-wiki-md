# Electron affinity

↑ **Parent:** [Mulliken electronegativity](mulliken-electronegativity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_affinity)

[Electron affinity](electron-affinity.md) measures how strongly an isolated neutral species binds an additional [Electron](electron.md). In the spectroscopic convention used here, it is the positive [energy](energy.md) required to detach that [Electron](electron.md) from a bound [anion](anion.md). With the free [Electron](electron.md) at rest assigned zero [energy](energy.md),

$$
A_{\rm ea}=E(X)+E(e^-)-E(X^-),\qquad
\Delta E_{\rm attach}=-A_{\rm ea}.
$$

The [quantum states](quantum-state.md) of $X$ and $X^-$ must be specified, usually their [ground states](ground-state.md). Positive $A_{\rm ea}$ means that attachment lowers the [energy](energy.md); a signed attachment [energy](energy.md) is then negative. This convention agrees with the IUPAC definition and with the atomic tables used for [Mulliken electronegativity](mulliken-electronegativity.md).[electron affinity](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/electron-affinity.md)

For [chlorine](chlorine.md), a published [photodetachment threshold](photodetachment-threshold.md) is $A_{\rm ea}/(hc)=29138.59(22)\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, approximately $3.612725\,\mathrm{eV}$ per [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). Here $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), $c$ the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md), and the inverse-length unit expresses an [energy](energy.md) through a spectroscopic [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). The parentheses give the reported uncertainty in the last digits, not a range of possible sign conventions.[§2.3, printed pp. 6–7: reported threshold 29138.59(22) inverse centimetres](isotope-shift-in-the-electron-affinity-of-chlorine-by-u-berzinsh-and-others.md)

**[Figure 14](#image-electron-affinity-attachment-detachment-levels). Electron affinity of chlorine: positive detachment energy and negative attachment energy connect the same isolated-species levels; a finite-temperature electron attachment enthalpy needs additional corrections**.

An [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md) is neither an [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) nor a [lattice energy](lattice-energy.md). The first describes a specified isolated-species [energy](energy.md) difference, [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) compares attraction for [electron density](electron-density.md) in a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md), and [lattice energy](lattice-energy.md) refers to an entire [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) and separated gaseous [ions](ion.md). A stable [anion](anion.md) can therefore help explain [ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md) without describing the full [energy](energy.md) balance for making a [salt](salt-chemistry.md).
