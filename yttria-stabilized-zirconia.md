# Yttria-stabilized zirconia

↑ **Parent:** [Solid electrolyte](solid-electrolyte.md)

Zirconia stabilized by addition of yttria. At suitable elevated [temperatures](temperature-split.md), [oxygen](oxygen.md) vacancies allow oxide-ion transport, making the material useful in an [oxygen concentration cell](oxygen-concentration-cell.md). Its oxide-ion transference number must remain sufficiently close to one for a simple ionic [Nernst equation](nernst-equation.md) interpretation.
