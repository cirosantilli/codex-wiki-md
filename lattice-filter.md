# Lattice filter

↑ **Parent:** [Distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md)

A lattice filter is a nonempty upward-closed subset closed under finite meets. A proper filter omits the bottom element.

A [lattice filter](lattice-filter.md) is a [filter in an ordered set](filter-mathematics.md) when that order is a lattice; directedness and upward closure then imply closure under finite meets.
