# Dimension-six operators of a real scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Operator basis in effective field theory](operator-basis-in-effective-field-theory.md)

In four dimensions, a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) and a [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) each have [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) one. Seven scalar monomials of dimension six can be listed before [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md): $\phi^6$, $\phi^2(\partial\phi)^2$, $\phi^3\Box\phi$, $\phi\Box^2\phi$, $\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\Box\phi$, $(\Box\phi)^2$, and $(\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\phi)^2$. They reduce to the three displayed representatives modulo [total derivatives](total-derivative.md). Leading [equations of motion](equation-of-motion.md) reduce them further to a single new $\phi^6$ interaction, together with shifts of the existing [mass](mass.md) and quartic [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md).
