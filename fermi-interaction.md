# Fermi interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Four-fermion interaction](four-fermion-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_interaction)

The Fermi interaction is the low-energy four-fermion approximation to the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). Integrating out a [W boson](w-boson.md) gives its coefficient $G_F/\sqrt2=g^2/(8M_W^2)$ at tree level.
