# Hydride transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

A [hydride transfer](hydride-transfer.md) moves hydrogen with its bonding [electron](electron.md) pair from donor to acceptor. Reduced nicotinamide cofactors transfer hydride from their ring C4; this is not transfer of a hydrogen radical.
