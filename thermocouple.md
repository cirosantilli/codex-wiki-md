# Thermocouple

↑ **Parent:** [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermocouple)

A [thermocouple](thermocouple.md) uses two different [electrical conductors](electrical-conductor.md) and the [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md) to infer a measuring-junction [temperature](temperature.md) from a voltage and a known reference [temperature](temperature.md). The reference material and the reference [temperature](temperature.md) are distinct choices: B specifies the comparison [Seebeck coefficient](seebeck-coefficient.md), while $T_c$ specifies the lower limit of the [integral](integral.md). The two-material experiment in [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md) gives the geometry, sign convention, and calibration limitations.
