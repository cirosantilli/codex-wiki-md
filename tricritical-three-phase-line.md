# Tricritical three-phase line

↑ **Parent:** [Tricritical wing](tricritical-wing.md)

For $V=rm^2/2+um^4/4+vm^6/6-hm$ with $v>0$, the displayed line has three [global minima](global-minimum.md), $m=0$ and $m=\pm\sqrt{-3u/(4v)}$. The factorization $V=(v/6)m^2(m^2+3u/(4v))^2$ proves their coexistence. Two nonzero-field [tricritical wings](tricritical-wing.md) and the zero-field ordered coexistence sheet meet along this line. Its order-parameter discontinuity tends to zero as it terminates at the [tricritical point](tricritical-point.md).
