# Self-focused attention

↑ **Parent:** [Attention](attention.md)

[Attention](attention.md) directed toward one's own sensations, performance or imagined appearance. In [social anxiety disorder](social-anxiety-disorder.md), internal sensations may be mistaken for reliable evidence about how an observer sees the person, while [attention](attention.md) to the actual interaction is reduced.
