# Time reversal of a complex scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [T-symmetry](t-symmetry.md)

For a charged [spin](spin.md)-zero [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md), time reversal preserves charge and can include an intrinsic phase. In its free-field [mode expansion of a free field](mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md), [antiunitarity](antiunitary-operator.md) conjugates the plane-wave coefficients. Relabel spatial momentum to its negative and compare the annihilation and creation terms: $\hat Ta(\boldsymbol p)\hat T^{-1}=\eta_Ta(-\boldsymbol p)$ and $\hat Tb^\dagger(\boldsymbol p)\hat T^{-1}=\eta_Tb^\dagger(-\boldsymbol p)$. Particle annihilation remains annihilation; unlike [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md), this operation does not exchange particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) operators.
