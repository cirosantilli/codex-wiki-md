# Free step-N nilpotent Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

Inside the [truncated tensor algebra](truncated-tensor-algebra.md), let $\mathfrak g^N(V)$ be the span of iterated [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md) of elements of $V$ of lengths at most $N$. Its exponential is the connected simply connected free nilpotent [Lie group](lie-group.md) of step $N$, with tensor multiplication. The bracket is the commutator in the [tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md). Its grading defines dilations that multiply degree $k$ by $\lambda^k$.
