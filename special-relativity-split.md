# Special relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_relativity)

[Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) describes [physics](physics-split.md) in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), where [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) assign different space and time coordinates to the same [spacetime events](event-relativity.md). Its two postulates are [Einstein's principle of relativity](einstein-s-principle-of-relativity.md) and the same vacuum [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$ in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), independent of the source's motion. Together they replace the [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md) with the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md), coupling space and time rather than providing a universal clock. Einstein's 1905 paper developed this account through measuring rods, clocks, and light signals. [Einstein (1905), On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies, introduction and sections 1–3; English translation](https://fourmilab.ch/etexts/einstein/specrel/www/).

A [spacetime event](event-relativity.md) is an occurrence at a definite place and time: a pulse reaching a detector, for example. An [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) supplies a [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) and clocks at rest throughout it. [Einstein synchronisation](einstein-synchronisation.md) sets a distant clock's reflection reading to the midpoint between a light signal's departure and return readings at the sending clock. The resulting coordinate time records the local synchronized clock at each [spacetime event](event-relativity.md); it differs from the time when a distant observer receives its image. This distinction makes [time dilation](time-dilation.md), [length contraction](length-contraction.md), and the [relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md) precise measurements. [Einstein (1905), section 1: definition of simultaneity](https://fourmilab.ch/etexts/einstein/specrel/www/).

The beauty of [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) is that these different measurements fit one geometry: all [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) agree on the [Minkowski interval](minkowski-interval.md). It lets us translate an experiment between a laboratory and a moving apparatus while preserving the light-signal relations between [spacetime events](event-relativity.md).
