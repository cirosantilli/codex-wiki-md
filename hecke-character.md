# Hecke character

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hecke_character)

A Hecke character of a [number field](number-field.md) is a continuous character of its [idèle class group](idele-class-group.md). Its finite conductor records the local unit subgroups on which it is trivial. As an ideal character it is defined on fractional ideals prime to that conductor, with a prescribed rule on principal ideals coming from its archimedean character. A [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) yields an algebraic Hecke character with one CM embedding in its [infinity type of a Hecke character](infinity-type-of-a-hecke-character.md).
