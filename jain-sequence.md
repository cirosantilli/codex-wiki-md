# Jain sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Composite fermion](composite-fermion.md)

A family of [fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md) fillings obtained by attaching $2p$ flux quanta and filling $q$ effective [Landau levels](landau-level.md) of [composite fermions](composite-fermion.md). Positive integers $p,q$ and the relative effective-field direction specify the branch.
