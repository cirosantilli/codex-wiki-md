# Bound-state ladder termination by norm positivity

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb angular-momentum intertwining](coulomb-angular-momentum-intertwining.md)

For a normalized bound eigenstate of a factorized Coulomb radial [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), the displayed squared norm must be nonnegative. At fixed negative $E$, increasing $l$ indefinitely would make it negative. The intertwining chain must therefore terminate with zero norm, giving $E=-\hbar^2/[2ma^2(L+1)^2]$. The positivity contradiction does not apply to the positive-energy continuum; domain preservation and normalizability are necessary assumptions.
