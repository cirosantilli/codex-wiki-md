<h1 id="poynting-robertson-drag-invariant">Poynting–Robertson drag invariant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md)

The orbit-averaged [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md) equations preserve $C=a(1-e^2)e^{-4/5}$ for $0<e<1$. This relates shrinking [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) to decreasing [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md); a circular orbit is treated by its separate limiting solution.
