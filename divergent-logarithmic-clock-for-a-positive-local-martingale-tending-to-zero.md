# Divergent logarithmic clock for a positive local martingale tending to zero

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic logarithm](stochastic-logarithm.md)

Write a positive continuous local martingale as $M=\exp(X-\langle X\rangle/2)$ using its [stochastic logarithm](stochastic-logarithm.md). If $M_t\to0$, its logarithmic bracket must diverge. Otherwise the [finite-bracket convergence lemma](finite-bracket-convergence-lemma.md) makes $X$ converge finitely and the exponential has a positive limit.
