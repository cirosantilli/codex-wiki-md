# Gaussian evaluation of a free scalar generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian functional integral](gaussian-functional-integral.md)

Completing the square in the free scalar [path integral](path-integral.md) gives a quadratic exponential in the source. If $\Delta_F$ includes the factor $i$ in the momentum-space [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md), the normalized Minkowski functional is $\exp[-\int J\Delta_FJ/2]$. Two source derivatives with their $1/i^2$ factor recover $\Delta_F$, while higher derivatives give [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md).
