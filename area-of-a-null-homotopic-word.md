# Area of a null-homotopic word

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

For a word $w\in F(S)$ representing the identity in $\langle S\mid R\rangle$, its area is the least $N$ for which

$$
w=\prod_{i=1}^N u_i r_i^{\varepsilon_i}u_i^{-1}
$$

in $F(S)$, where $r_i\in R$ and $\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,1\}$.
