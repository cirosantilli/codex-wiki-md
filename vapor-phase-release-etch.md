# Vapor-phase release etch

↑ **Parent:** [Sacrificial layer](sacrificial-layer.md)

Selective removal of a [sacrificial layer](sacrificial-layer.md) using gaseous reactants rather than a bulk [liquid](liquid.md) bath. A compatible vapor-phase oxide etch can avoid the [liquid](liquid.md) drying step responsible for one form of [stiction](stiction.md). Selectivity, byproducts, humidity and access to the undercut region still matter.
