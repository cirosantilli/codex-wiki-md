# Interspike interval

↑ **Parent:** [Spike train](spike-train.md)

An [interspike interval](interspike-interval.md) is the elapsed time between consecutive [action potentials](action-potential.md) in a [spike train](spike-train.md). Its [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) and dependence on previous activity can reveal a [neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md) even when the average firing rate is unchanged.
