# Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor theorem](taylor-theorem.md)

If $f$ is $n$ times differentiable between $a$ and $x$, then for some $\xi$ strictly between them,

$$
f(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{f^{(j)}(a)}{j!}(x-a)^j
+\frac{f^{(n)}(\xi)}{n!}(x-a)^n.
$$
