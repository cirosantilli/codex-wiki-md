# Running of the scalar spectral index

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar spectral index](scalar-spectral-index.md)

The running describes the scale dependence of the [scalar spectral index](scalar-spectral-index.md). Differentiating $n_s-1\simeq-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V$ with the [potential slow-roll flow equations](potential-slow-roll-flow-equations.md) gives the displayed second-order slow-roll result. It includes the [third potential slow-roll parameter](third-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) and is generally smaller than the leading spectral tilt.
