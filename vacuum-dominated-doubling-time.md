# Vacuum-dominated doubling time

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter scale factor in flat slicing](de-sitter-scale-factor-in-flat-slicing.md)

In a spatially flat universe dominated by a positive constant [energy density](energy-density.md) $\epsilon$, the [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md) give $H^2=8\pi G\epsilon/(3c^2)$. On the expanding branch $a(t)=a_i e^{H(t-t_i)}$, so the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) doubles after $(\log2)/H$. This concerns a linear length scale; the enclosed physical volume grows by a factor of eight over the same interval.
