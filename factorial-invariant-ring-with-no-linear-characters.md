# Factorial invariant ring with no linear characters

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

If a [finite group](finite-group.md) has no nontrivial homomorphism to $\mathbb C^\times$, its [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md) is a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md). Factor an invariant in the ambient [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) and collect its irreducible factors into orbit products. Each orbit product transforms by a [linear character](linear-character.md), hence is invariant. It is prime in the invariant ring, and the invariant factorization is a product of these primes.
