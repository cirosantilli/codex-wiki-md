# Constant-concentration inlet solution

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md)

The half-line solution of $C_t+UC_x=DC_{xx}$ with initially zero concentration, unit inlet concentration for positive time, and decay at infinity at fixed time. Both [complementary error functions](complementary-error-function.md) are needed to satisfy the inlet exactly. A [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) gives $\widetilde C=p^{-1}e^{(U-\sqrt{U^2+4Dp})x/(2D)}$. A finite layer requires an additional downstream boundary condition.
