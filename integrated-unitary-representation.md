# Integrated unitary representation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary representation](unitary-representation.md)

For a strongly continuous [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md), integration against $f\in L^1(G)$ defines an operator satisfying $\|\pi(f)\|\le\|f\|_1$. [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) gives $\pi(f*g)=\pi(f)\pi(g)$, and the group-algebra involution gives $\pi(f^*)=\pi(f)^*$. On an Abelian group, the operator-norm closure is a commutative [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md). Its [algebra characters](character-of-an-algebra.md) produce continuous unitary [group characters](continuous-unitary-character.md), allowing the [Commutative Gelfand--Naimark theorem](commutative-gelfand-naimark-theorem.md) and [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) to turn a positive vector functional into a finite spectral measure.
