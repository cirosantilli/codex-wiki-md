# Black-Scholes equation with claim dividends

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes equation](black-scholes-equation.md)

If the claim holder receives a cash-flow rate $k(S_t,t)$ before maturity, the [self-financing](self-financing-portfolio.md) hedge matches the claim's cum-dividend gain $dp+kdt$. Its stock holding is the [option delta](option-delta.md) $p_x$ and its bank-account value is $p-xp_x$. Comparing [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) with portfolio gains gives the displayed equation, with terminal value equal to the terminal payoff. The positive source term $k$ represents dividends paid by the claim; dividends on the underlying stock would instead alter the stock's pricing drift. [Risk-neutral pricing](risk-neutral-pricing.md) includes both the discounted terminal payoff and the integral of discounted interim payments.
