<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A [psychological treatment](../../../psychotherapy.md) has a specific effect only relative to a clearly defined alternative explanation. Benefit beyond waiting is different from benefit beyond credible therapeutic contact, and both differ from evidence that a named technique works through its proposed mechanism. No single research design establishes all three. A persuasive programme combines controlled clinical comparisons with component and process experiments, while keeping patient-relevant outcomes central.

An uncontrolled before-and-after study cannot isolate a treatment effect. People may enter care when [symptoms](../../../symptom.md) are unusually severe, creating regression toward their longer-term level; difficulties can improve naturally; expectations, concurrent care and repeated assessment can also change outcomes. A waiting-list [randomised](../../../randomization.md) trial controls some time-related explanations but adds unequal expectations and contact. It can establish benefit from being offered the treatment package compared with waiting under those conditions, without identifying which ingredient produced the difference.

For specificity beyond nonspecific therapy effects, use a [randomised](../../../randomization.md) comparison with a credible active intervention. Match time, [attention](../../../attention.md), therapist competence and assessment opportunities as far as the question permits. Measure credibility and expected benefit rather than assuming equivalence from similar session counts. Keep relevant usual care available in both arms. [Allocation concealment](../../../allocation-concealment.md) prevents recruitment decisions from anticipating the next assignment, while [blinded](../../../blinding.md) independent outcome assessment reduces a different source of bias after allocation. Therapists and patients usually cannot be fully [blinded](../../../blinding.md) to psychological procedures.

[Daniel Freeman](../../../daniel-freeman.md) and colleagues' Feeling Safe trial illustrates this stronger package comparison. Patients with persistent [persecutory delusions](../../../persecutory-delusion.md) received the programme or befriending delivered by the same therapists, alongside usual care. At six months, the primary continuous conviction measure favoured Feeling Safe. This supports an effect beyond the particular befriending comparator, but does not establish that each module is necessary or that the whole benefit travelled through one nominated [mediator](../../../causal-mediator.md).[Lancet Psychiatry 8, 696–707; 130 patients, same-therapist befriending, six-month continuous conviction and categorical recovery distinction](../../../comparison-of-a-theoretically-driven-cognitive-therapy-the-feeling-safe-programme-with-befriending-for-the-treatment-of-persistent-persecutory-delusions-a-parallel-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)

The comparator should be interpreted fairly. A weak or implausible control can exaggerate apparent specificity, while an active comparator may itself change the proposed mechanism. If befriending improves social confidence, it is not a biologically inert placebo. A null result may mean two useful treatments have similar effects, both are ineffective, or the study is too imprecise. Failure of a superiority test does not demonstrate equivalence; an equivalence or [noninferiority](../../../noninferiority-trial.md) question requires prespecified clinically justified margins and appropriate precision.

A [dismantling trial](../../../dismantling-trial.md) varies a specified component while keeping the surrounding treatment adequate and similar. For example, compare the same [exposure](../../../exposure-therapy.md) programme with and without a [safety behavior](../../../safety-behavior.md) intervention. This estimates a conditional contribution: absence of an additional benefit does not imply that the component is useless in every context, because another part may produce similar [learning](../../../learning.md). A [factorial design](../../../factorial-design.md) can independently vary an action and its explanatory rationale, estimating their effects and interaction when the study has adequate [power](../../../statistical-power.md).

The early panic [safety behavior](../../../safety-behavior.md) experiment provides both an example and a caution. Greater change after brief [exposure](../../../exposure-therapy.md) with precautions reduced supports the combined manipulation. Yet behavioral instructions and the explanation of the exercise were not fully separated. The appropriate next test would vary those elements independently and check actual behavior and expectancy change. Simply renaming the conditions 'cognitive' and 'behavioral' would not remove the [confounding](../../../confounding.md).[Behaviour Research and Therapy 37, 559–574; brief exposure manipulation and repeated behavioural test](../../../an-experimental-investigation-of-the-role-of-safety-seeking-behaviours-in-the-maintenance-of-panic-disorder-with-agoraphobia-by-paul-salkovskis-and-others.md)

A mechanistic experiment can provide more direct leverage than a full treatment comparison. [Colin MacLeod](../../../colin-macleod.md) and colleagues trained [attentional bias](../../../attentional-bias.md) using a modified [dot-probe task](../../../dot-probe-task.md) and tested later responses to stress. This asked whether experimentally changing [information processing](../../../information-processing.md) altered emotional vulnerability. It remains necessary to establish that the manipulation engaged the intended process rather than only a task-specific strategy, and that any effect transfers to the clinical phenomenon of interest. Laboratory emotional vulnerability is not itself [diagnostic remission](../../../diagnostic-remission.md).[Journal of Abnormal Psychology 111, 107–123; modified dot-probe training and responses to a standardised stress task](../../../selective-attention-and-emotional-vulnerability-assessing-the-causal-basis-of-their-association-through-the-experimental-manipulation-of-attentional-bias-by-colin-macleod-and-others.md)

Likewise, [Daniel Freeman](../../../daniel-freeman.md) and colleagues used virtual social environments to compare [exposure](../../../exposure-therapy.md) against threat testing with [safety behavior](../../../safety-behavior.md) reduction. Both groups encountered graded situations, making the content of [learning](../../../learning.md) more directly investigable than in a therapy-versus-waiting comparison. The combined manipulation and brief follow-up constrain the mechanism claim. A useful [manipulation check](../../../manipulation-check.md), an independent behavioral outcome and later transfer assessment strengthen the design more than a treatment label does.[British Journal of Psychiatry 209, 62–67; 30 participants, graded social environments, safety reduction versus exposure and real-world assessment](../../../virtual-reality-in-the-treatment-of-persecutory-delusions-randomised-controlled-experimental-study-testing-how-to-reduce-delusional-conviction-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)

[Mediation](../../../statistical-mediation.md) analysis is informative but insufficient on its own. [Randomising](../../../randomization.md) treatment identifies the total effect of assignment under the trial's assumptions; it does not automatically [randomise](../../../randomization.md) the [mediator](../../../causal-mediator.md). [Symptom](../../../symptom.md) improvement could change reported [beliefs](../../../belief.md), or an unmeasured process could influence both the [belief](../../../belief.md) score and the outcome. Measure the proposed [mediator](../../../causal-mediator.md) before the downstream outcome, assess competing pathways and perform sensitivity analyses. Replication using a different way to alter the same process can provide converging evidence; stronger identification may require experimental manipulation of that process itself.

[Clinical trials](../../../clinical-trial.md) also need suitable sampling, prespecified outcomes, adequate [power](../../../statistical-power.md), [intention-to-treat analysis](../../../intention-to-treat-analysis.md) and transparent [missing data](../../../missing-data.md) handling. Outcomes should include functioning and possible deterioration, with follow-up long enough to distinguish transient reassurance from durable benefit. [Treatment fidelity](../../../treatment-fidelity.md) and competence must be assessed in both arms. [Therapist allegiance](../../../therapist-allegiance.md), clustering of patients within therapists and differential [dropout](../../../study-attrition.md) can affect interpretation. [Statistical significance](../../../statistical-significance.md) alone does not establish a meaningful improvement, especially when many outcomes or subgroup comparisons are explored.

Pragmatic implementation trials answer a complementary question: does the intervention add benefit in ordinary care, at acceptable cost and burden? [Jaime Delgadillo](../../../jaime-delgadillo.md) and colleagues' therapist-cluster trial tested an automated feedback system linked to supervisory review within routine services. Its comparison isolates the added feedback arrangement more closely than an observational association between high-performing services and monitoring. It still tests a specified package of information and response, rather than an abstract effect of measurement divorced from clinical action.[Lancet Psychiatry 5, 564–572; 77 participating therapists, 2233 patients, cluster allocation and not-on-track outcomes](../../../feedback-informed-treatment-versus-usual-psychological-treatment-for-depression-and-anxiety-a-multisite-open-label-cluster-randomised-controlled-trial-by-jaime-delgadillo-and-others.md)

The strongest conclusion consequently states its level of specificity. A well-conducted [active control](../../../active-control.md) trial can establish an incremental treatment-package effect. A dismantling or factorial trial can identify a component's contribution within a defined context. An experimental process manipulation, supported by temporally ordered measurements and converging clinical evidence, can constrain the mechanism. Keeping these claims separate prevents a successful therapy from being treated as proof of its entire theory, while preserving the practical importance of improvements patients actually experience.
