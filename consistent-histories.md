# Consistent histories

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consistent_histories)

An approach assigning probabilities to compatible families of time-ordered quantum alternatives. A [decoherence functional](decoherence-functional.md) measures [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) between histories; when its relevant cross-terms vanish, coarse-grained probabilities add as in [probability theory](probability-theory-split.md). Different incompatible families must not be combined as if they were one classical history space. The formalism does not require a fundamental external measurement apparatus.
