# Sublattice pseudospin in graphene

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac valleys in graphene](dirac-valleys-in-graphene.md)

At $\mathbf K_+$ and positive [energy](energy-split.md), a normalized [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) for $\mathbf q=|\mathbf q|(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)$ is $(1,e^{i\phi})/\sqrt2$. Its [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md) expectation values give the displayed direction. This [pseudospin](pseudospin.md) records the relative amplitudes and phase on the two sublattices. Real [spin](spin.md) supplies another label; confusing these two labels would double-count states and misinterpret the two-band [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md).
