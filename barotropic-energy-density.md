# Barotropic energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

For a [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) with [pressure](pressure.md) $P=P(\rho)$, choose an energy density $U$ satisfying $\rho U'(\rho)-U=P$. The [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) then gives $\partial_tU+\nabla\cdot(U\mathbf u)=-P\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$. For an [isothermal equation of state](globally-isothermal-equation-of-state.md) $P=c_s^2\rho$, one choice is $U=c_s^2\rho\ln(\rho/\rho_{\rm ref})$. A fixed reference density changes $U$ only by a conserved mass term. The same construction holds with [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and vertically integrated [pressure](pressure.md). This closure energy need not equal the microscopic internal energy of a gas maintained at fixed [temperature](temperature.md).
