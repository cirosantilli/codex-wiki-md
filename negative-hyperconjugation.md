# Negative hyperconjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

[Negative hyperconjugation](negative-hyperconjugation.md) is donation from an occupied lone-pair or related orbital into an antibonding sigma orbital. Nitrogen-to-P–X sigma-star donation contributes to [phosphazene](phosphazene.md) bonding. Short P–N bonds therefore do not require a model dominated by phosphorus $3d$ pi orbitals.
