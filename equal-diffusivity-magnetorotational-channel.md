# Equal-diffusivity magnetorotational channel

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md)

For $0<A<2\Omega$ and equal [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) and [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md), a vertical wavevector admits $\mathbf v=(V,V,0)$ and $\mathbf b=(i\omega_AV/A,-i\omega_AV/A,0)$ in Alfvén-speed units. Substitution into the [magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md) equations gives the displayed frequency and growth rate. In a [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md), growth of this polarization requires $B_z^2/(\mu_0\rho)>5\nu\Omega/4$. This is a bound for this selected channel and its wavelength, not a universal threshold for every MRI mode in an unbounded domain.
