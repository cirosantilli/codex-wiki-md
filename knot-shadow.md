# Knot shadow

↑ **Parent:** [Knot diagram](knot-diagram.md)

A [knot shadow](knot-shadow.md) is the [curve](curve.md) in the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md) underlying a [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md) after its overcrossing and undercrossing information has been erased. At a crossing the two local branches still continue across one another; the drawing does not specify which lies higher in space.
