# Diffusion current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

A [diffusion current](diffusion-current.md) results from a spatial [carrier density](carrier-density.md) gradient. For semiconductor electrons and holes in one dimension, conventional-current densities are $J_n=eD_n\,dn/dx$ and $J_p=-eD_p\,dp/dx$. Their different signs reflect negative and positive carrier charge. At equilibrium each species' [diffusion current](diffusion-current.md) is balanced by its [drift current](drift-current.md), giving zero net current while the individual transport contributions need not vanish.
