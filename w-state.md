# W state

↑ **Parent:** [Dicke state](dicke-state.md)

The $n$-[qubit](qubit.md) [W state](w-state.md) is the [Dicke state](dicke-state.md) with one excitation:

$$
|W_n\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{j=1}^n|0\cdots010\cdots0\rangle=|D_1^n\rangle.
$$

For three [qubits](qubit.md) it is $(|001\rangle+|010\rangle+|100\rangle)/\sqrt3$. Its [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) on any one [qubit](qubit.md) is $\operatorname{diag}((n-1)/n,1/n)$, from the [Dicke state](dicke-state.md) recursion. For $n>1$ both diagonal entries are nonzero, so that [qubit](qubit.md) is entangled with the rest: a pure [product state](product-state.md) would have a pure marginal. A [symmetric-arm reduction of an exchange Hamiltonian](symmetric-arm-reduction-of-an-exchange-hamiltonian.md) supplies a dynamical preparation from one initial excitation.
