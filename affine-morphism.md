# Affine morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_morphism)

A morphism $f:X\to Y$ is affine when the inverse image of every affine open subscheme of $Y$ is affine. For a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ on $X$, one has $R^qf_*\mathcal F=0$ for $q>0$.
