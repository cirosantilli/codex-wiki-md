# Body-frame and space-frame frequencies of free symmetric-top precession

↑ **Parent:** [Torque-free precession of a symmetric top](torque-free-precession-of-a-symmetric-top.md)

For principal inertias $I_\perp,I_\perp,I_\parallel$, the transverse angular-velocity components rotate in body axes at signed rate $(I_\parallel-I_\perp)\omega_3/I_\perp$. The symmetry axis precesses about the inertially fixed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) at rate $L/I_\perp$. These are different frame-dependent descriptions of the same motion.
