# W1p trace theorem on a half-space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev trace theorem](sobolev-trace-theorem.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$, boundary restriction on smooth functions extends uniquely to a bounded linear map $T:W^{1,p}(\mathbb R_+^n)\to L^p(\mathbb R^{n-1})$. The estimate follows by applying the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) on normal line segments and then integrating over the boundary variables.
