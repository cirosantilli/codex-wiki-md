# Deuterium equilibrium abundance

↑ **Parent:** [Deuterium bottleneck](deuterium-bottleneck.md)

Use [natural units](natural-units.md) and a positive [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md) $\varepsilon_D=m_n+m_p-m_D$. [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) of $n+p\leftrightarrow D+\gamma$ requires $\mu_D=\mu_n+\mu_p$. The [nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number density](nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density.md) gives

$$
\frac{n_D}{n_nn_p}=\frac{g_D}{g_ng_p}\left(\frac{m_D}{m_nm_p}\right)^{3/2}\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^{3/2}e^{\varepsilon_D/T}.
$$

Here $g_D=3$ and $g_n=g_p=2$ count the spin states. Taking $m_n\simeq m_p\simeq m_N$ and $m_D\simeq2m_N$, then writing $X_i=n_i/n_B$, $n_B=\eta n_\gamma$ and using the [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) yields the displayed abundance. The coefficient is approximately $8.14$. Omitting the spin-state ratio $3/4$ gives the rougher prefactor $16\zeta(3)/\sqrt\pi$. A convention $B=m_D-m_n-m_p<0$ instead writes the exponential as $e^{-B/T}$. The small [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) suppresses stable [deuterium](deuterium.md) until the [temperature](temperature.md) is well below the binding scale, producing the [deuterium bottleneck](deuterium-bottleneck.md).
