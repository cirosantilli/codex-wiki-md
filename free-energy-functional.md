# Free-energy functional

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md)

A [free-energy functional](free-energy-functional.md) assigns a [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) to an entire spatial [order parameter](order-parameter.md) configuration rather than only to one uniform value. In [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md), a [local derivative expansion](local-derivative-expansion.md) gives $\mathcal A[m]=\int d^Dx\{\kappa(\nabla m)^2/2+V(m)\}$, with appropriate boundary terms and regular backgrounds. The [Landau approximation](landau-approximation.md) minimizes this functional; a fluctuating [statistical field theory](statistical-field-theory.md) instead integrates its [Boltzmann weight](boltzmann-factor.md) over configurations. These procedures need not give identical [critical exponents](critical-exponent.md).
