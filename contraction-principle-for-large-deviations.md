# Contraction principle for large deviations

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

If $X_N$ satisfies a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) in $E$ with [good rate function](good-rate-function.md) $I$ and $f:E\to F$ is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md), then $f(X_N)$ has the same [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md) and [good rate function](good-rate-function.md)

$$
J(y)=\inf\{I(x):f(x)=y\}.
$$

The preimages of open and closed sets give the two [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) inequalities. The identity $\{J\leq M\}=f(\{I\leq M\})$ follows from compactness and [lower semicontinuity](lower-semicontinuity.md) by taking minimizing sequences in the fibres; hence $J$ is good.
