# Hering-Breuer inflation reflex

↑ **Parent:** [Pulmonary ventilation](pulmonary-ventilation.md)

A lung-inflation [reflex](reflex.md) carried through [vagus nerve](vagus-nerve.md) sensory pathways that inhibits inspiration. It was described in 1868 by [Ewald Hering](ewald-hering.md) and [Josef Breuer](josef-breuer.md), after the 1867 examination.
