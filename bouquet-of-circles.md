# Bouquet of circles

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

A [bouquet of circles](bouquet-of-circles.md) identifies one point of each of a family of [circles](circle.md) to a common point, using the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md). As a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) it has one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and one [graph loop](loop-graph-theory.md) for each [circle](circle.md). The empty family means a single point. Its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is the [free group](free-group.md) on the family, as described by the [fundamental group of a bouquet of circles](fundamental-group-of-a-bouquet-of-circles.md).
