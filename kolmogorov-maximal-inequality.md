# Kolmogorov maximal inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Doob maximal inequality for a nonnegative submartingale](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) zero-mean [square-integrable](square-integrable-function.md) increments, their partial sums form a [martingale](martingale-split.md), and their squares form a nonnegative [submartingale](submartingale.md). Apply the [Doob maximal inequality](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md) to the squared process at threshold $x^2$. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) and zero [means](expected-value.md) make its terminal [second moment](second-moment.md) the sum of the increment [variances](variance-split.md). Identical distributions are not required.
