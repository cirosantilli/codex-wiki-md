↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac's_theorem)

Every graph on $n\ge3$ vertices with minimum degree at least $n/2$ has a Hamilton cycle.
