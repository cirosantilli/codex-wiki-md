# Volume derivative form of the heat-capacity difference

↑ **Parent:** [Difference between constant-pressure and constant-volume heat capacities](difference-between-constant-pressure-and-constant-volume-heat-capacities.md)

For a fixed-composition simple compressible [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) with smooth [equations of state](equation-of-state.md), write the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) as $U(T,V)$ and the [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) as $H=U+pV$. Differentiating $H$ at constant [pressure](pressure-split.md) and applying the [multivariable chain rule](multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives

$$
C_p=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_p+p\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p,\qquad\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_p=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_V+\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_T\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p.
$$

Subtracting the [heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) proves the displayed identity. For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), $(\partial U/\partial V)_T=0$ and $(\partial V/\partial T)_p=Nk_B/p$, so $C_p-C_V=Nk_B$. For a nonideal material the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) change with [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) contributes as well as expansion [work](work.md).
