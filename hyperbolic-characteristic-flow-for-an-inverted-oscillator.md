# Hyperbolic characteristic flow for an inverted oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md)

The [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $(v^2-x^2)/2$ gives $\dot x=v$, $\dot v=x$. Its [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) is multiplication by $A_t$, with $A_tA_s=A_{t+s}$ and determinant one. The backward map is $A_{-t}$. This expanding-contracting linear flow preserves phase-space [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) and every finite-$p$ [integral](integral.md) of a transported density, despite stretching its level sets.
