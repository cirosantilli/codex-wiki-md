# Polonyi supersymmetry branches

↑ **Parent:** [Polonyi model](polonyi-model.md)

For the [Polonyi model](polonyi-model.md) with canonical [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) and $W=m^2(z+\beta)$, $\beta>0$, a constant [supersymmetric vacuum](supersymmetric-vacuum.md) with $m\ne0$ requires real $z=(-\beta\pm\sqrt{\beta^2-4})/2$ and exists exactly for $\beta\geq2$. Its [supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md) is negative because $W\ne0$. For $0<\beta<2$, the [supergravity auxiliary field](supergravity-auxiliary-field.md) cannot vanish. If $m=0$, the model is flat with unbroken [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). This parameter-dependent statement is sharper than asserting that every linear [superpotential](superpotential.md) breaks [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) in [supergravity](supergravity.md).
