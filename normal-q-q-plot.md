# Normal Q-Q plot

↑ **Parent:** [Q-Q plot](q-q-plot.md)

A [Q-Q plot](q-q-plot.md) comparing ordered observations with quantiles of a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Approximate linearity supports a normal location-scale shape, while systematic curvature reveals deviations such as heavy tails or asymmetry.
