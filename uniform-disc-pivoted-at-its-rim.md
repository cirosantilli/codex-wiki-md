# Uniform disc pivoted at its rim

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

A uniform circular disc of radius $a$ and [mass](mass.md) $m$, pivoted on a perpendicular axis through its rim, has [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I_A=3ma^2/2$ by the [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md). Its hanging [small oscillations](small-oscillation.md) have period $2\pi\sqrt{3a/(2g)}$. Measured from the upright position, the falling equation is $\ddot\theta=2g\sin\theta/(3a)$. Exact upright rest remains an unstable equilibrium; the energy level of that state describes a limiting [separatrix](separatrix.md) after perturbation, with $\dot\theta^2=4g(1-\cos\theta)/(3a)$. The center acceleration and pivot reaction follow by resolving radial and tangential components.
