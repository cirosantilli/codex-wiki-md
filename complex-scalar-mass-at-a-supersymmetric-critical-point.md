# Complex scalar mass at a supersymmetric critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric mass degeneracy](supersymmetric-mass-degeneracy.md)

For one canonical chiral field at $W'(v)=0$, the quadratic [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) is $|W''(v)|^2|\delta\varphi|^2$. Writing $\delta\varphi=(a+ib)/\sqrt2$ gives two canonically normalized real scalars with squared mass $|W''(v)|^2$. The [chiral-superfield fermion mass matrix](chiral-superfield-fermion-mass-matrix.md) has the same physical mass. A complex bilinear coefficient or its algebraic square is not itself a signed physical squared mass.
