# Coaxial symmetric tensors

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

Two real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) are coaxial when they share an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). Equivalently they commute. Degenerate [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) can have several choices of shared axes.
