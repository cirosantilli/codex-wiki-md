# Graded-index optical fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

A [graded-index optical fiber](graded-index-optical-fiber.md) has an [optical fiber core](optical-fiber-core.md) whose [refractive index](refractive-index.md) varies smoothly with distance from the [optical axis](optical-axis.md). [Refraction](refraction.md) bends [light rays](light-ray.md) through the varying medium; the straight zigzag of a [step-index optical fiber](step-index-optical-fiber.md) no longer describes their paths. The local launch cone can depend on entry position.[Fiber Holders and Force Sensors for Multi-Axis Flexure Stages, Insights into Optical Fiber, p. 4, Is the max acceptance angle constant across the core of a multimode fiber?, Figs. 3–5; revised January 2, 2019](fiber-holders-and-force-sensors-for-multi-axis-flexure-stages-by-thorlabs.md)
