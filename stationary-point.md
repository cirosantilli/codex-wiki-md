# Stationary point

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary_point)

A point is stationary for a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) or [functional](functional.md) when its first [derivative](derivative.md) or [first variation](first-variation.md) vanishes in every admissible direction.
