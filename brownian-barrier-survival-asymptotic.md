# Brownian barrier survival asymptotic

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

For standard one-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and a fixed $h>0$, the [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives the survival [probability](probability.md) $2\Phi(h/\sqrt t)-1$. The [standard normal distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md) has derivative $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$ at zero, giving the displayed asymptotic. Thus the persistence exponent is one half, with leading constant $h\sqrt{2/\pi}$.
