# Entropy bound for a binary mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Concavity of Von Neumann entropy](concavity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md)

The entropy of a binary mixture also satisfies

$$
S(p\rho_1+(1-p)\rho_2)\leq pS(\rho_1)+(1-p)S(\rho_2)+H(p),
$$

where $H$ is [binary entropy](binary-entropy.md). This follows by applying the [operator monotonicity of logarithm](operator-monotonicity-of-logarithm.md) to $p\rho_1\leq p\rho_1+(1-p)\rho_2$ and its counterpart for $\rho_2$.
