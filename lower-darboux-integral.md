# Lower Darboux integral

↑ **Parent:** [Upper and lower Darboux integrals](upper-and-lower-darboux-integrals.md)

The lower Darboux integral of a bounded real function on a closed interval is the [supremum](supremum.md) of its [lower Darboux sums](lower-darboux-sum.md) over all [partitions of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md). Refinement increases a [lower Darboux sum](lower-darboux-sum.md). A common refinement of two arbitrary [partitions of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) proves that every [lower Darboux sum](lower-darboux-sum.md) is at most every [upper Darboux sum](upper-darboux-sum.md), hence the lower Darboux integral never exceeds the [upper Darboux integral](upper-darboux-integral.md).
