# Linear stability of a planar equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium point of a dynamical system](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md)

For a hyperbolic equilibrium of a smooth planar system, the eigenvalues of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) determine local stability. If its determinant and trace are positive, both eigenvalues have positive real part and the equilibrium is a repeller.
