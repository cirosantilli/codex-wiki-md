# Generalized Pell equation

↑ **Parent:** [Pell equation](pell-equation.md)

A generalized Pell equation is $x^2-dy^2=N$ for a fixed nonsquare positive integer $d$ and nonzero integer $N$. Multiplication by units of a real [quadratic number field](quadratic-field.md) preserves the norm up to sign, reducing solutions to finitely many unit orbits when an appropriate bounded reduction interval is used.
