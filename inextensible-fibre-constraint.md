# Inextensible fibre constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

A unit reference fibre direction $m_0$ retains its length under this constraint on the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md). Its reaction contributes an undetermined tension along the current fibre direction, independently of the pressure reaction enforcing [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md).
