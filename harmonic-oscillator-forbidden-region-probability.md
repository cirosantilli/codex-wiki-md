# Harmonic-oscillator forbidden-region probability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For the normalized ground state of a reduced [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), $\phi_0(q)=\pi^{-1/4}e^{-q^2/2}$ and $E_0=1/2$. Its [classical turning points](classical-turning-point.md) are $q=\pm1$. The [Born rule](born-rule.md) assigns [probability](probability.md) $P=2\pi^{-1/2}\int_1^\infty e^{-q^2}\,dq=\operatorname{erfc}(1)\simeq0.157299$ to the [classically forbidden region](classically-forbidden-region.md). This is a stationary [probability](probability.md) of detection outside the classical interval. It is not a transmitted flux or an escape rate: the real stationary [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) has zero [probability current](probability-current.md).
