# Genetic variance of penetrance

↑ **Parent:** [Penetrance](penetrance.md)

For a binary disease trait $Y$ with [genotype](genotype.md) $G$, the genetic variance of [penetrance](penetrance.md) is the between-genotype term in the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md). The averaged within-genotype term is $\mathbb E[q_G(1-q_G)]$. Under [multiplicative diallelic penetrance](multiplicative-diallelic-penetrance.md), the genetic term is $p^2(\beta^2-\alpha^4)$. This decomposition attributes variance to the modeled locus, not automatically to all hereditary causes.
