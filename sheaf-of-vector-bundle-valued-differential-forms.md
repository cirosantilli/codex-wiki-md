# Sheaf of vector-bundle-valued differential forms

↑ **Parent:** [Vector-bundle-valued differential form](vector-bundle-valued-differential-form.md)

For a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $E$, this [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) assigns to $U$ the smooth $E$-valued [differential forms of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) on $U$. Its [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) acts coefficientwise in a [holomorphic local frame](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) and squares to zero.
