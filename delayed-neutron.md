# Delayed neutron

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron](neutron-split.md)

A [neutron](neutron-split.md) emitted following a radioactive transformation of a precursor, rather than promptly in the original [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md). The waiting time reflects the precursor lifetime. It is distinct from the later detection of a prompt neutron after scattering or moderation.
