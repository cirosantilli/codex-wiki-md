# Glued ring categories counterexample to regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Regular category](regular-category.md)

Take two copies of the [category of rings](category-of-rings.md), identify their terminal zero rings, and adjoin a [strict initial object](strict-initial-object.md) $\bot$. Same-copy finite [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) are ordinary ring limits; products of nonzero objects in different copies are $\bot$. Ring surjections and $1_\bot$ are precisely the [strong epimorphisms](strong-epimorphism.md). They are stable under pullback along [monomorphisms](monomorphism.md), so [image factorizations](image-factorization.md) exist and [Frobenius reciprocity for subobjects](frobenius-reciprocity-for-subobjects.md) holds. But pulling $\mathbb Z$ in one copy $\to0$ back along $\mathbb Z[x]$ in the other copy $\to0$ gives $\bot\to\mathbb Z[x]$. This is not epic: evaluations $x\mapsto0,1$ agree after precomposing with it. Thus the category is not regular, since strong epimorphisms would then be regular and pullback-stable.
