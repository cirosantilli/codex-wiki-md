# Efficient score

↑ **Parent:** [Nuisance tangent space](nuisance-tangent-space.md)

The efficient score is the residual after [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of the parametric [score function](informant-function.md) onto the [nuisance tangent space](nuisance-tangent-space.md). It is centered and orthogonal to every nuisance direction. Its squared [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) is the [efficient information](efficient-information.md).
