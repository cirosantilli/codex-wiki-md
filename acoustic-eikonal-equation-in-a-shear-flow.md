# Acoustic eikonal equation in a shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)

For a slowly varying parallel [shear flow](shear-flow.md) $U(y)\mathbf e_x$, the leading [WKB method](wkb-method.md) neglects mean-velocity gradients while retaining local advection. The [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and [linearized Euler equations](linearized-euler-equations.md) then give the displayed acoustic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). On the positive intrinsic-frequency branch, $\omega=Uk_x+c_0|\mathbf k|$ and the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $U\mathbf e_x+c_0\mathbf k/|\mathbf k|$. A [wavefront](wavefront.md) normal is parallel to $\mathbf k$; the acoustic energy-ray direction generally differs because of the advection term.
