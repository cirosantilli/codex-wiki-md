# Prone and Postural Respiration in Drowning, and Other Forms of Apnoea (review, 1858)

↑ **Parent:** [Anatomy and physiology bibliography](anatomy-and-physiology-bibliography-split.md)

[https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5186098/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5186098/)

Contemporary anonymous review in The British and Foreign Medico-Chirurgical Review, volume 21, pp. 285–297, of the 1857 work by [Marshall Hall](marshall-hall.md). The review documents historical terminology and experimental concerns; its postural method is not current resuscitation guidance.
