# Coefficient of friction

↑ **Parent:** [Friction](friction.md)

The dimensionless ratio between an appropriate tangential [friction](friction.md) [force](force-split.md) and normal load in a specified contact model. Static limiting and sliding coefficients differ, and nanoscale [adhesion](adhesion.md) can invalidate a simple load-independent ratio.
