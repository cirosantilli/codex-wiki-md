# Iterated contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md)

If a self-map $T$ of a nonempty [complete metric space](complete-metric-space.md) has an iterate $T^n$ that is a [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md), the [contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md) gives a unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md) $a$ of $T^n$. The maps commute, so $T^n(Ta)=T(T^na)=Ta$, and uniqueness forces $Ta=a$. Conversely every [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of $T$ is fixed by $T^n$. Thus $a$ is the unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of $T$.
