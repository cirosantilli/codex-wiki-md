# Additive freedom of a velocity potential

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity potential](velocity-potential.md)

Adding a spatially constant function of time to a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) leaves its [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$ unchanged. When integrating the [Euler equations](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) in space, this freedom can absorb the time-only integration term in the potential form of momentum balance. A spatially constant zero-frequency potential represents no acoustic velocity and must not be counted as a propagating [normal mode](normal-mode.md) merely because a frequency determinant vanishes at zero. Pressure conventions and time dependence must still be fixed consistently when the potential is used to recover physical fields.
