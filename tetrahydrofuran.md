# Tetrahydrofuran

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Tetrahydrofuran](tetrahydrofuran.md) is a cyclic [ether](ether.md) solvent. Its [oxygen](oxygen.md) can coordinate [lithium](lithium.md) ions and alter the aggregation of [organolithium reagents](organolithium-reagent.md).
