# Finite-state row shortening of a combing diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Finite presentation from a synchronous combing](finite-presentation-from-a-synchronous-combing.md)

For a null loop of length $n$, a horizontal row of its [combing](combing-of-a-group.md) diagram is encoded by an ordered $n$-tuple of rung words of length at most $K$. If there are $D$ such words, there are at most $D^n$ row types. Between two identical labelled rows one can delete the entire intervening annular band and glue the equal boundary [paths](continuous-path.md). Repeating this leaves at most $D^n$ layers of $n$ bounded-perimeter cells. Thus a synchronous [combable group](combable-group.md) has a recursive exponential isoperimetric bound. This does not require the [combing](combing-of-a-group.md) language to be regular or computable.
