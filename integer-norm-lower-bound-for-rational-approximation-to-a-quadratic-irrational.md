# Integer norm lower bound for rational approximation to a quadratic irrational

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic irrational number](quadratic-irrational-number.md)

For a positive nonsquare integer $D$ and positive integers $a,q$, the integer $Dq^2-a^2$ is nonzero. Therefore $|\sqrt D-a/q|=|Dq^2-a^2|/(q^2(\sqrt D+a/q))$. If $a/q\leq\sqrt D+1$, the denominator factor is at most $2\sqrt D+1$; otherwise the approximation error already exceeds one. This proves that a quadratic irrational of this form is a [badly approximable number](badly-approximable-number.md). For $D=2$, splitting instead at $a/q=2$ gives the strict bound $|\sqrt2-a/q|>1/(4q^2)$.
