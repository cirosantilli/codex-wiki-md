# Fiber-counting lemma for a quotient map

↑ **Parent:** [Large lifted product from a coset progression](large-lifted-product-from-a-coset-progression.md)

Let $\pi:G\to G/N$ be a [quotient group](quotient-group.md) map, let $A$ be finite and symmetric, and suppose $P\subseteq\pi(A^m)$ has $|P|\geq\delta|\pi(A)|$. Then

$$
|\pi^{-1}(P)\cap A^{m+2}|\geq\delta|A|.
$$

Choose one lift in $A^m$ of each member of $P$ and multiply those lifts by $A^2\cap N$. Distinct fibers are disjoint, while $|\pi(A)|\,|A^2\cap N|\geq|A|$.
