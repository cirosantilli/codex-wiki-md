# Facet

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polytope](convex-polytope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_(geometry))

A facet of a full-dimensional [convex polytope](convex-polytope.md) in $\mathbb R^n$ is a face of dimension $n-1$. Each irredundant supporting [closed half-space](closed-half-space.md) defines one facet.
