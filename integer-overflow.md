# Integer overflow

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_overflow)

An [integer arithmetic](integer-arithmetic.md) result outside the range of its chosen representation. A [programming language](programming-language-split.md) may specify wrapping, trapping or another behavior; a proof over mathematical [integers](integer-split.md) must account for that difference.
