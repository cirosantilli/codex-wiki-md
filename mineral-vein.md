# Mineral vein

↑ **Parent:** [Structural geology](structural-geology.md)

A fracture filled by [minerals](mineral.md) precipitated from a fluid. [Quartz](quartz.md) and [calcite](calcite.md) are common examples. Growth textures and cross-cutting relations can constrain opening and fluid-flow history, but mineral filling alone does not establish whether the fracture also experienced [fault slip](fault-slip.md).
