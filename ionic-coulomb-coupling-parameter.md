# Ionic Coulomb coupling parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar equation-of-state regime diagram](stellar-equation-of-state-regime-diagram.md)

For one ionic species of [number density](number-density.md) $n_i$, the ion-sphere radius is $a_i=(3/(4\pi n_i))^{1/3}$. The ionic Coulomb coupling parameter compares the interaction energy from [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) at that separation with thermal energy $k_BT$. At fixed composition, $\Gamma\propto\rho^{1/3}/T$. Values near one mark significant corrections to the ideal ion gas; strong coupling can lead to an ordered solid. Screening, mixtures and quantum motion modify detailed transitions, so this estimate is not a universal crystallization boundary. It is separate from the electron degeneracy criterion based on the [electron Fermi temperature](electron-fermi-temperature.md).
