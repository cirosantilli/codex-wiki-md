<h1 id="mobius-conjugacy">Möbius conjugacy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic conjugacy](holomorphic-conjugacy.md)

Two rational sphere maps are Möbius conjugate if $S=h\circ R\circ h^{-1}$ for a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $h$. This is precisely global [holomorphic conjugacy](holomorphic-conjugacy.md) between Riemann-sphere self-maps. It preserves degree, [local degrees of a holomorphic map](local-degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md), exact periods and return [periodic-orbit multipliers](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md), and transports the [Fatou set](fatou-set.md) and [Julia set](julia-set.md) by $h$. Since a Möbius map and its inverse are Lipschitz on the [compact](compact-space.md) sphere, this last assertion follows directly from [equicontinuity](equicontinuity.md) of the iterate families. An affine [holomorphic conjugacy](holomorphic-conjugacy.md) is the special case where $h$ fixes infinity.
