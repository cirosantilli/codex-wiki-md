# Semiclassical action integral

↑ **Parent:** [Two-turning-point WKB quantization condition](two-turning-point-wkb-quantization-condition.md)

The semiclassical action integral

$$
f(E)=\int_{a(E)}^{b(E)}\sqrt{E-V(x)}\,dx
$$

is strictly increasing for a confining one-dimensional potential. Consequently the [two-turning-point WKB quantization condition](two-turning-point-wkb-quantization-condition.md) has at most one energy at each quantum number.
