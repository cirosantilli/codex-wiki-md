# Finite square barrier transmission at half barrier height

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)

For a square [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md) of width $2a$ and height $U_0=2E$, put

$$
k=\frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar}.
$$

The oscillatory wavenumber outside and decay constant inside are both $k$. Matching the [wavefunction](wave-function.md) and its [derivative](derivative.md) at both faces gives transmission amplitude

$$
t=\frac{e^{-2ika}}{\cosh(2ka)}
$$

and transmission probability $\operatorname{sech}^2(2ka)$.
