# Chirality matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)

With metric $(+---)$, the chirality matrix has $(\gamma^5)^2=1$ and anticommutes with each [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md). Its eigenspaces define [chirality](chirality-physics.md); $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$ and $P_R=(1+\gamma^5)/2$ are the corresponding projectors. These identities follow directly by moving a [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) through the ordered product and using the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md).
