# Inferring incident power from a transmitted plasma field

↑ **Parent:** [Cold collisionless plasma dielectric response](cold-collisionless-plasma-dielectric-response.md)

For a downward transverse wave in an isotropic [plasma](plasma-physics.md), $E_z/E_x=\tan\theta_t$ fixes the transmitted direction. Tangential [phase matching](phase-matching.md) gives $\sin\theta_i=n_p\sin\theta_t$. Continuity of tangential electric and [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) then separates incident and reflected amplitudes. A measured transmitted electric amplitude alone cannot be identified with the incident one; mean [power](power.md) for peak phasors includes a factor of one half, and interface-normal flux includes a cosine.
