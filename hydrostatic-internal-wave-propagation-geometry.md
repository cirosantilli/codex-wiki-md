# Hydrostatic internal-wave propagation geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

For a positive-frequency non-rotating [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) with $\omega=N|k|/|m|$, differentiation gives [group velocity](group-velocity.md) $(\omega/k,-\omega/m)$. Its [wavevector](wavevector.md) $(k,m)$ is orthogonal to this vector, so [energy](energy-split.md) travels along constant-phase lines and perpendicular to normal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). The horizontal crest-intersection speed $\omega/k$ is distinct from the horizontal component $\omega k/(k^2+m^2)$ of that normal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). The [hydrostatic approximation](hydrostatic-approximation.md) requires $|k/m|\ll1$.
