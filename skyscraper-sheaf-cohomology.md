# Skyscraper sheaf cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Skyscraper sheaf](skyscraper-sheaf.md)

For any point inclusion $i:\{x\}\hookrightarrow X$, the [skyscraper sheaf](skyscraper-sheaf.md) $i_*A$ has restriction maps that are identities or maps to zero. It is [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md), so its higher [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) vanishes and its [global sections](global-section.md) are $A$. This requires no assumption that $x$ is closed.
