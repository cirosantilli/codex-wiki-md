# Wall-induced motion of a planar potential dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Planar potential dipole](planar-potential-dipole.md)

A transported [planar potential dipole](planar-potential-dipole.md) follows the surrounding regular [velocity](velocity.md), so its position satisfies the displayed equation after its own singular term is removed. The [conjugation](conjugation.md) comes from the convention $w'=v_x-iv_y$. Reflected image parameters are held fixed when taking the spatial [derivative](derivative.md), then evaluated at the instantaneous [dipole](planar-potential-dipole.md) position.

For a unit circular wall, the [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) gives image potential $\bar m z/(1-\bar a z)$ and therefore $\dot{\bar a}=\bar m/(1-|a|^2)^2$. For a horizontal straight wall the image is $\bar m/(z-\bar a)$, giving $\dot{\bar a}=-\bar m/(a-\bar a)^2$. For the line $y=-x$, reflected [conjugation](conjugation.md) gives image $-i\bar m/(z+i\bar a)$ and hence $\dot{\bar a}=i\bar m/(a+i\bar a)^2$. In each case the image lies outside the fluid region and makes the wall a [streamline](streamline.md); differentiation of the regular image proves the motion formula. Several compatible straight-wall reflections can be summed before the same operation is applied.
