# Critical dimension of a monomial scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Mass dimension](mass-dimension.md)

With a canonical [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md), a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) has [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) $(D-2)/2$. A [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md) multiplying $\phi^n$ therefore has [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) $D-n(D-2)/2$. For $n>2$, it is dimensionless precisely at $D_c=2n/(n-2)$, giving six for a cubic interaction and four for a [quartic interaction](quartic-interaction.md). This is a [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) statement about perturbation theory, not a claim that the corresponding quantum [beta function](beta-function.md) vanishes.
