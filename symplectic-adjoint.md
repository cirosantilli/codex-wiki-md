# Symplectic adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md)

For a finite-dimensional [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md), the symplectic adjoint of a [linear map](linear-map.md) $T$ is the unique [linear map](linear-map.md) $T^*$ satisfying the displayed identity. Existence and uniqueness follow because the [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) identifies the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with its [dual space](dual-space-split.md). If $\omega$ has [matrix](matrix.md) $J$ in a [basis](basis.md), then $T^*=J^{-1}T^{\mathsf T}J$. Thus $(ST)^*=T^*S^*$ and $(T^*)^*=T$, and membership in the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) is equivalent to $T^*=-T$. This operation is linear over the ground [field](field.md); it does not involve complex conjugation. It preserves [diagonalizability](diagonalizable-matrix.md) and the property of being a [nilpotent linear map](nilpotent-linear-map.md), since it is conjugate to [matrix transpose](transpose.md).
