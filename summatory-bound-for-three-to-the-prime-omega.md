# Summatory bound for three to the prime omega

↑ **Parent:** [Three to the distinct-prime-factor count](three-to-the-distinct-prime-factor-count.md)

The [Euler product](euler-product.md) and [Mertens second theorem](mertens-second-theorem.md) give $\sum_{n\leq D}3^{\omega(n)}/n\ll\log^3D$. The [log-weighted convolution bound for three to the prime omega](log-weighted-convolution-bound-for-three-to-the-prime-omega.md), with the [Chebyshev estimate](chebyshev-estimate.md) $\psi(y)\ll y$, then gives $\sum_{\sqrt D\leq n\leq D}3^{\omega(n)}\ll D\log^2D$. A direct bound for the full range follows from $3^{\omega(n)}\leq\tau_3(n)$, where $\tau_3(n)=\#\{(a,b,c):abc=n\}$:

$$
\sum_{n\leq D}3^{\omega(n)}\leq\sum_{abc\leq D}1\leq D\sum_{a\leq D}\frac1a\sum_{b\leq D}\frac1b\ll D\log^2D.
$$
