# Double-couple fault source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

Tangential fault slip $\mathbf b$ in an [isotropic](isotropy.md) elastic material has the displayed moment density. It is symmetric and trace-free, with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\mu|b|,-\mu|b|,0$. The two off-diagonal force dipoles form equal couples with opposite [torques](torque.md), producing zero resultant force and [torque](torque.md). For a uniform patch of area $A$, the scalar seismic moment is $M_0=\mu A|b|$. [Traction](traction.md) continuity removes the single-force layer in the [elastodynamic surface-jump representation](elastodynamic-surface-jump-representation.md).
