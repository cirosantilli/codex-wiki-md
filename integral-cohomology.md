# Integral cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

Integral cohomology is [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) with integer coefficients, $H^*(X;\mathbb Z)$. Its [cup product](cup-product.md) makes it a graded [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md), retaining torsion and integral information that can disappear after changing coefficients to a [field](field.md).
