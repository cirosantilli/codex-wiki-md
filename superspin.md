# Superspin

↑ **Parent:** [Massive supermultiplet](massive-supermultiplet.md)

For a massive four-dimensional N=1 [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md), [superspin](superspin.md) $j$ is the [spin](spin.md) of its [Clifford vacuum](clifford-vacuum.md), the state annihilated by the two lowering [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). The creation operators produce spins $j,j,j+1/2,j-1/2$, omitting the last when $j=0$. The corresponding [superspin](superspin.md) Casimir has an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) proportional to $j(j+1)$; this representation label is distinct from the [spin](spin.md) of any one constituent.
