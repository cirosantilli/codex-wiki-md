# Short-time Gaussian spin-bath decoherence

↑ **Parent:** [Random-coupling spin-bath decoherence](random-coupling-spin-bath-decoherence.md)

For initially equatorial spins and uniform couplings on $[0,g_*]$, expand $\log\cos(2g_kt)=-2g_k^2t^2+O(g_k^4t^4)$. The [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) then gives $\sum g_k^2\simeq Ng_*^2/3$. On the scale $t=O((g_*\sqrt N)^{-1})$, the total fourth-order remainder tends to zero, giving the Gaussian coherence envelope. This approximation concerns early times and does not remove [finite spin-bath coherence recurrence](finite-spin-bath-coherence-recurrence.md).
