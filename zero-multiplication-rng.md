# Zero-multiplication rng

↑ **Parent:** [Rng (algebra)](rng-algebra.md)

Any [abelian group](abelian-group.md) becomes a [nonunital ring](rng-algebra.md) by declaring every product zero. [Associativity](associative-property.md) and the [distributive property](distributive-property.md) hold because both sides are zero. When the group has more than one element, no multiplicative [identity element](identity-element.md) exists: an identity would have to satisfy $ex=x$, whereas every such product is zero. This differs from the [zero ring](zero-ring.md), which has just one element.
