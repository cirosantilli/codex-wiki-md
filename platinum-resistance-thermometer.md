# Platinum resistance thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Resistance thermometer](resistance-thermometer.md)

A [resistance thermometer](resistance-thermometer.md) using [platinum](platinum.md) as its sensing material. It provides the [temperature](temperature-split.md) readout in the [saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement](saturation-pressure-equilibrium-measurement.md).

Transduction turns [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) into a [temperature](temperature-split.md) indication. A uniform wire has $R=\rho_{\rm el}\ell/A$, with [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) $\rho_{\rm el}$, [length](length.md) $\ell$, and cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) $A$. Stronger atomic vibrations at higher [temperature](temperature-split.md) scatter [electrons](electron.md) more, increasing [platinum](platinum.md)'s [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md); impurities and [crystallographic defects](crystallographic-defect.md) also contribute.[Guide on Secondary Thermometry: Industrial Platinum Resistance Thermometers, §2.1, printed pp. 6–7: resistivity, electron scattering, and impurities](guide-on-secondary-thermometry-industrial-platinum-resistance-thermometers-by-jonathan-pearce-and-others.md)

A small known [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$ and measured [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ give $R=V/I$ through [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md); a [resistance bridge](resistance-bridge.md) can instead compare the element with a reference [resistor](resistor.md). Inverting the calibrated response then gives the sensing element's [temperature](temperature-split.md). This makes electrical readout convenient, but the [platinum resistance thermometer lead resistance error](platinum-resistance-thermometer-lead-resistance-error.md), [resistance thermometer self-heating](resistance-thermometer-self-heating.md), and [resistance thermometer immersion test](resistance-thermometer-immersion-test.md) explain why a stable display alone is insufficient. The [gas thermometer](gas-thermometer.md) supplies a contrasting route through an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md); the [saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement](saturation-pressure-equilibrium-measurement.md) shows why a trustworthy independent [temperature](temperature-split.md) readout matters.
