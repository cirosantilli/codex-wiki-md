<h1 id="komar-integral-of-a-five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-metric">Komar integral of a five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom metric</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom black hole](five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-black-hole.md)

For $f=1-s/r^2+q/r^4$ and the spacetime orientation $-dt\wedge dr\wedge d\Omega_3$, the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) gives $\int_{S^3}\star dk^\flat=2\pi^2r^3f^{\prime}\to4\pi^2s$. A prefactor $1/(16\pi)$ produces $\pi s/4$, whereas the dimension-adjusted ADM mass normalization produces $3\pi s/8$. The normalization and orientation must be specified rather than imported from four dimensions.
