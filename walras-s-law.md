<h1 id="walras-s-law">Walras's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [General equilibrium theory](general-equilibrium-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walras's_law)

At any price vector, the total value of aggregate excess demand is zero when consumers exhaust their budgets. After normalization to a price [simplex](simplex.md), choosing a price that maximizes the value of a candidate excess-demand vector is a useful [Kakutani fixed-point theorem](kakutani-fixed-point-theorem.md) construction.
