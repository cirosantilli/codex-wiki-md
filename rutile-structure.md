# Rutile structure

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

A tetragonal $AB_2$ [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) with sixfold cation and threefold anion [coordination numbers](coordination-number.md). A common fractional-coordinate description has A at $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/2,1/2,1/2)$, and B at $\pm(u,u,0)$ and $\pm(1/2+u,1/2-u,1/2)$, interpreted modulo integer lattice translations. The cation coordination is a distorted octahedron and the anion coordination is a planar triangle. [Titanium dioxide](titanium-dioxide.md) in its rutile polymorph is an example.
