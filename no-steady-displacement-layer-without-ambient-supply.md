# No steady displacement layer without ambient supply

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement ventilation](displacement-ventilation.md)

Consider entraining plumes supplied with total source [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $S$, a separate lower-layer ambient inflow $L$, and ceiling exhaust $F=S+L$. Write $P(h)=S+E(h)$, where $E(h)$ is total [fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) from the lower layer. [Volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) gives $A\dot h=L-E(h)$. A steady positive lower layer therefore requires $L=E(h)>0$. If $L=0$ and $E(h)>0$ for every $h>0$, no positive steady [displacement-ventilation interface height](displacement-ventilation-interface-height.md) exists. In a shifted [pure plume](pure-plume.md) approximation $P_i(h)=\lambda B_i^{1/3}(h+z_i)^{5/3}$ matched to source flux $S_i$ at $h=0$, this follows immediately from $P_i(h)>S_i$ for $h>0$. This is a statement about the ideal two-layer model, not a prohibition of all possible stratification in a real ventilated room.
