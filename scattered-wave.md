# Scattered wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave scattering](wave-scattering.md)

The [scattered wave](scattered-wave.md) is the part of the total solution produced by the scatterer relative to the prescribed [incident wave](incident-wave.md). It obeys an outgoing radiation condition. For a rough reflecting surface, this convention includes both specular flat-surface reflection and the roughness-dependent correction. Its [coherent field](coherent-wave-field.md) is its ensemble mean, which need not equal the full mean intensity.
