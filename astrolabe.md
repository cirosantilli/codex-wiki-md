# Astrolabe

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomy](astronomy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrolabe)

A planispheric [astrolabe](astrolabe.md) uses a [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) of the [celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md) as a physical calculating device. A rotating star map is compared with a latitude-specific plate; a sighting rule measures an object's angular [altitude](altitude.md). Aligning that measurement with the engraved geometry permits time and celestial-configuration calculations.[Epact: components and time-telling procedure](astrolabe-by-silke-ackermann.md)
