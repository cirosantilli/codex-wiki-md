# First variation of geodesic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy of a curve](energy-of-a-curve.md)

For $E(\gamma)=\tfrac12\int_0^1|\dot\gamma|^2dt$ and [variation vector field](variation-vector-field.md) $V$, the first variation is $[\langle V,\dot\gamma\rangle]_0^1-\int_0^1\langle V,D_t\dot\gamma\rangle dt$. For fixed endpoints the boundary term vanishes, so the [critical points](critical-point.md) of the energy are exactly affinely parametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md).
