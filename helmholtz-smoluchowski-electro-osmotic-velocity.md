# Helmholtz-Smoluchowski electro-osmotic velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Electro-osmosis](electro-osmosis.md)

In a half-space with no [pressure](pressure-split.md) gradient, Stokes balance and Poisson electrostatics give $\eta v\prime\prime=\varepsilon E\psi\prime\prime$. No slip at the wall and zero bulk shear imply $v=\varepsilon E(\psi-\zeta)/\eta$. The bulk [velocity](velocity.md) is $U=-\varepsilon\zeta E/\eta$; it is not the fluid speed at the no-slip wall.
