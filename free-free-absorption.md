# Free-free absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md)

[Free-free absorption](free-free-absorption.md) is the inverse of [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md): a free [electron](electron.md) absorbs a [photon](photon.md) during an encounter with an ion, which permits [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md). In [local thermodynamic equilibrium](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) its absorption coefficient and the [thermal free-free emissivity](thermal-free-free-emissivity.md) obey [Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation](kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermal-radiation.md), $j_\nu=\alpha_\nu B_\nu(T)$. For a nonrelativistic [plasma](plasma-physics.md) at $h\nu\ll k_BT$, $\alpha_\nu\propto n_e\sum_i Z_i^2n_i T^{-3/2}\nu^{-2}g_{\rm ff}$, including the stimulated-emission correction in the net coefficient.
