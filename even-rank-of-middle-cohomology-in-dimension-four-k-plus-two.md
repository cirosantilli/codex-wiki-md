# Even rank of middle cohomology in dimension four k plus two

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality pairing](poincare-duality-pairing.md)

For a closed oriented manifold, the rational middle-dimensional cup-product pairing is nondegenerate by [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md). Its degree is odd, so graded commutativity makes it skew-symmetric. A nondegenerate skew-symmetric form has even dimension. In particular a closed oriented six-manifold cannot have a rank-one third cohomology group.
