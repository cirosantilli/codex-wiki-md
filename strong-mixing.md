# Strong mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodicity](ergodicity.md)

A probability [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md) is strongly mixing when $\mu(T^{-n}A\cap B)\to\mu(A)\mu(B)$ for every pair of measurable sets $A,B$. Unlike weak mixing, this is an ordinary limit along all sufficiently large times.
