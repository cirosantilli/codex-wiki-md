# Exchange current density

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemical polarization](electrochemical-polarization.md)

The equal magnitudes of the opposing partial electrode-reaction [current densities](current-density.md) at equilibrium. Their net current is zero. The area convention, activities and [temperature](temperature-split.md) must be specified; an [exchange current density](exchange-current-density.md) alone does not specify a nonlinear current-potential curve.
