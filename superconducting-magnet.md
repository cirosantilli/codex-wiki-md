# Superconducting magnet

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconducting_magnet)

An electromagnet whose winding uses a superconductor, allowing large currents without the normal winding's steady resistive loss in its superconducting state. Refrigeration, strain, field-dependent critical current and [superconducting quench](superconducting-quench.md) protection remain practical constraints. Magnetic resonance imaging, nuclear magnetic resonance and particle accelerators are applications.
