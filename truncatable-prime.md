# Truncatable prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number-split.md)

A [prime number](prime-number-split.md) that remains [prime](prime-number-split.md) after successively deleting digits from a specified end of its [positional notation](positional-notation.md). Left truncation, right truncation, and requiring both are distinct conditions. For example, $3797$ is truncatable from both ends: $3797,797,97,7$ and $3797,379,37,3$ consist entirely of [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md).
