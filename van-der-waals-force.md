# Van der Waals force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_der_Waals_force)

The Van der Waals force is a distance-dependent nonbonded interaction between atoms or molecules, including dispersion and dipolar contributions. In the attractive nonretarded pair model, an interaction energy $U(r)=-\beta/r^6$ gives force magnitude $6\beta/r^7$. Macroscopic integration introduces a [Hamaker constant](hamaker-constant.md); it does not replace the sign change and molecular cutoff needed at very short distances.
