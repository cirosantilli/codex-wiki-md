# Monolayer

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monolayer)

A [monolayer](monolayer.md) is a single layer of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) or [molecules](molecule-split.md). In the [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md), a complete [monolayer](monolayer.md) occupies every [adsorption site](adsorption-site.md); partial [surface coverage](surface-coverage.md) leaves vacancies within that first layer. One layer need not mean one [molecule](molecule-split.md) per surface [chemical atom](atom-physics.md).
