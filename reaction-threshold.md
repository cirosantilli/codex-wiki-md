# Reaction threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic kinematics](relativistic-kinematics.md)

The kinematic minimum energy needed to produce an allowed final state. In the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), the threshold for freely separated massive final particles is the sum of their [rest energies](rest-energy.md), with no relative kinetic energy. Conservation of total [energy](energy-split.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) then determines the corresponding laboratory threshold. Charges and other selection rules must also permit the reaction; the mass bound alone does not guarantee an observable transition.
