# Homoclinic integrals for a quadratic-force oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Homoclinic balance for a quadratic-force oscillator](homoclinic-balance-for-a-quadratic-force-oscillator.md)

The conservative equation $u''=u^2-a^2$ has a saddle loop with $v_+(u)=\sqrt{2/3}(a-u)\sqrt{u+2a}$ for $-2a\le u\le a$. Its [homoclinic orbit](homoclinic-orbit.md) integrals are $I_0=2\int_{-2a}^av_+(u)du$ and $I_1=2\int_{-2a}^au v_+(u)du$. Substitution $s=u+2a$ evaluates them as displayed. For a weak perturbation $\varepsilon(\beta+u)u'$, integrating the energy derivative gives $\beta I_0+I_1=0$, hence $\beta=5a/7$. The nonzero derivative $I_0$ makes this a simple first-order splitting zero.
