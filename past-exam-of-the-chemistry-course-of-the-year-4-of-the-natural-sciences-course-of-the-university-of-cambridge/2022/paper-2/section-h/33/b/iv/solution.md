<h1 id="section-h/33/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The revised question page explicitly gives the cluster as **$\mathrm{\{Ga_{22}[N(SiMe_3)_2]_{10}\}^{2-}}$**. Its charge supplies two electrons, so

$$
\boxed{N_e=22(3)-10+2=58,}
$$

a **jellium shell closure**, through 1G. The reported structure has a Ga atom inside an eleven-atom Ga shell, with ten further ligand-bearing Ga units outside that core.[2002, central Ga@Ga11 core and ten external GaR units](../../../../../../../a-metalloid-cluster-compound-with-a-variation-of-the-ga22-framework-by-andreas-schnepf-and-others.md)

The canonical exam copy omits the 2− charge. Taken literally as a neutral formula it gives 56 electrons, which is **not** a listed shell closure; assigning 58 without identifying this difference would be unjustified. The revised public source resolves the discrepancy rather than requiring an invented charge.[physical page 81, part b(iv)](../../../../../../../revised-question-33-of-the-2022-cambridge-part-iii-chemistry-paper-2.md)
