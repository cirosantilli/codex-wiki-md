<h1 id="section-h/33/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the neutral-fragment skeletal count: each AlMe supplies two electrons, each CEt supplies three and the bridging H supplies one. Hence

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm skeletal}=8(2)+5(3)+1=32=16\text{ pairs}.}
$$

There are **13 nonhydrogen skeletal vertices**, eight Al plus five C, and $16=n+3$ pairs. In the usual PSEP classification this is therefore **arachno**, corresponding to a 15-vertex closo parent with two vertices absent. The μ$_4$-H cap contributes an electron but is not counted as a carbon-like skeletal vertex. The aluminium cube is a convenient subframework; its eight corners alone are not the complete Wade skeleton. Calculations on capped cubic carbaalanes likewise identify 16 skeletal pairs for the five-carbon-cap/one-hydride-cap case.[2007, five conical carbon caps and one hydride cap](../../../../../../../electron-counting-in-carbaalane-clusters-with-cubic-aluminum-core-by-benoit-creton-and-others.md)
