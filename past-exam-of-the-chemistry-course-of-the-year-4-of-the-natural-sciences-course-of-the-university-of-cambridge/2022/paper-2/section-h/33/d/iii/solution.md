<h1 id="section-h/33/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The requested equality is $32$ electrons if each unsaturated $C=CHPh$ unit is counted as a four-electron donor to the chosen extended cluster framework.** It needs that additional convention; a count confined to the localized $\mathrm{Al}_7\mathrm C_5$ skeleton gives $28$ instead.

For $B$, an $\mathrm{AlR}$ fragment contributes two skeletal electrons, a singly substituted cap $\mathrm{CR}$ contributes three, and the bridging hydrogen contributes one:

$$
N_B=8(2)+5(3)+1=32.
$$

For the second formula, let $x$ be the contribution assigned to each unsaturated unit. The comparison is

$$
N=7(2)+3(3)+2x+1=24+2x.
$$

Thus $N=N_B$ requires $x=4$. A bookkeeping partition that includes the two alkene $\pi$ pairs in the extended cluster electron pool gives

$$
N_{\rm extended}=7(2)+3(3)+2(2+2)+1=32.
$$

In contrast, assigning the $C=C$ [double bond](../../../../../../../double-bond.md) entirely to the exoskeletal substituent leaves each corresponding cage carbon with two electrons for the cage:

$$
N_{\rm localized}=7(2)+3(3)+2(2)+1=28.
$$

The difference is exactly the two $\pi$ pairs, four electrons, rather than an arithmetic discrepancy. These are different electron partitions: **do not compare $28$ from one partition with $32$ from the other as though both were the same skeletal count**, and do not infer a structural classification merely by inserting the extended count into [Wade's rules](../../../../../../../polyhedral-skeletal-electron-pair-theory.md) for twelve localized skeletal vertices.

Both the original and revised question pages print the same neutral molecular formula; the revised copy does not supply a charge, a drawing, or an explicit instruction about these $\pi$ electrons.[Part (d)(iii), physical page 81 of the later public bundle; the formula is unchanged from the original question](../../../../../../../revised-question-33-of-the-2022-cambridge-part-iii-chemistry-paper-2.md) The primary study identifies the open cluster with the two genuine $C=C$ groups and reports their [hydroalumination](../../../../../../../hydroalumination.md) to give the capped aluminium cube. Its accessible abstract does not establish the orbital partition needed to select the four-electron convention.[Abstract: open cluster 2 and its conversion to compound 4; Organometallics 19 (2000), 4536–4543](../../../../../../../new-carbaalanes-containing-clusters-of-aluminum-and-carbon-atoms-by-werner-uhl-and-others.md)

**Conditional conclusion:** the advertised $32$-electron equality follows under the explicit extended-framework convention above. Without evidence that those $\pi$ pairs belong in the cluster electron pool, retain the $28$-electron localized count and this qualification. The source checks do not justify asserting a typo or inventing a charge to force agreement.
