<h1 id="section-e/26/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $B_{\rm hex}<B_{\square}$, the hexagonal lattice has larger accessible cell area at any common allowed total area $A>B_{\square}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{F_{\rm hex}<F_{\square},\qquad S_{\rm hex}-S_{\square}=Nk_B\ln(a_c^{\rm hex}/a_c^{\square})>0.}
$$

The kinetic entropies cancel at the same temperature. **The triangular lattice is favoured and has higher [entropy](../../../../../../../entropy-split.md) within this cell model**, even though both structures look ordered. More efficient packing leaves more local displacement freedom at a fixed number density. This comparison does not by itself establish whether either crystal is stable against the fluid.
