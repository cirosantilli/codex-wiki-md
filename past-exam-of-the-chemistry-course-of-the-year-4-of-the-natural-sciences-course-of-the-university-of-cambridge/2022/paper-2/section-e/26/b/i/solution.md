<h1 id="section-e/26/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Freeze neighbouring particles and approximate the mobile particle's accessible area by $a_c$. Its one-particle translational contribution to the [canonical partition function](../../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is $a_c/\Lambda^2$ in two dimensions. Neglecting correlations between different cages,

$$
\boxed{Z_{\rm cell}\simeq(a_c/\Lambda^2)^N,\qquad F=-Nk_BT\ln(a_c/\Lambda^2).}
$$

There is no additional uncancelled $N!$: assigning particles to cages removes permutations, while restoring all $N!$ assignments cancels the indistinguishability factor. The result is a free-volume approximation; collective cage motion is excluded by construction.
