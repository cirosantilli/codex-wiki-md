<h1 id="section-e/26/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At high density, disorder can frustrate efficient local packing and leave particles with very little room to move. An ordered arrangement can give every particle a larger cage of allowed displacements. The extensive gain in vibrational/configurational freedom within these cages can outweigh the loss of distinct spatial arrangements of cages. **Visual order is therefore not a reliable measure of [entropy](../../../../../../../entropy-split.md).** At low density the fluid's freedom to rearrange dominates, whereas sufficiently dense packing can favour a crystal through accessible free volume.
