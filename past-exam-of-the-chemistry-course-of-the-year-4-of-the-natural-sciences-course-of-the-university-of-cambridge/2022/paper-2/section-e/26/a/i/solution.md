<h1 id="section-e/26/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [hard disc](../../../../../../../hard-disc.md) configuration, the potential energy is zero if no particles overlap and infinite otherwise. All allowed configurations therefore have the same configurational Boltzmann factor. At fixed $N,A,T$, their relative free energies differ through the volume of accessible configuration space:

$$
F=U-TS,\qquad U_{\rm configurational}=0,\qquad\boxed{\text{equilibrium maximizes accessible configurational entropy}.}
$$

The kinetic contribution is common to the phases at the same temperature. “Entropy-driven crystallization” does not mean that particles develop an energetic attraction; excluded volume changes the number of possible many-particle arrangements.
