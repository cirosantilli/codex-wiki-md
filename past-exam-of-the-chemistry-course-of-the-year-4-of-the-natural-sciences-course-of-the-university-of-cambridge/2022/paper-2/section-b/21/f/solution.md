↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For Co, the full field-polarized ionic value is

$$
\boxed{M_{\rm sat}=N_AgS\mu_B\simeq4.25\ \mu_B\text{ per Co}\simeq2.37\times10^4\ \mathrm{G\,cm^3\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

This is distinct from the much smaller weak-ferromagnetic domain moment, approximately 450 G cm$^3$ mol$^{-1}$ at low field. A low-temperature isothermal loop should have a **small hysteretic central component superposed on the antiferromagnetic field response**. Reversal changes the selected weak-ferromagnetic domain and produces remanence and coercivity. At larger fields the [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) continues to increase as the opposed sublattice moments rotate; a spin-flop or spin-reorientation feature may occur depending on field direction and anisotropy. Only sufficiently strong fields approach the full ionic saturation value, so the small low-field loop must not be labelled as saturation at 4.25 $\mu_B$.
