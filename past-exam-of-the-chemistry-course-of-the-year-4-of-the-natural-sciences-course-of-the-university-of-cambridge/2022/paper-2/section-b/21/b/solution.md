↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At high temperature, approximately independent paramagnetic ions give the appropriate [Curie constant](../../../../../../curie-constant.md). The negative [Weiss temperatures](../../../../../../weiss-temperature.md) and the reduction of $\chi T$ on cooling initially indicate **predominantly antiferromagnetic correlations**. The sharp rise near the ordering temperature nevertheless reveals a strongly enhanced low-field magnetic response.

A net moment could arise from a [canted antiferromagnet](../../../../../../canted-antiferromagnet.md), a [ferrimagnet](../../../../../../ferrimagnetism.md), or a ferromagnet with competing antiferromagnetic interactions. Differences between [field-cooled magnetization](../../../../../../field-cooled-magnetization.md) and [zero-field-cooled magnetization](../../../../../../zero-field-cooled-magnetization.md) suggest history-dependent domain selection or slow relaxation, but are not alone proof of a particular microscopic [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) arrangement. An ordinary compensated antiferromagnet would not account for a large ordering-related ferromagnetic-like low-field contribution without an additional mechanism.
