↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**The product is [1,3,4-Oxadiazole](../../../../../../1-3-4-oxadiazole.md).** Protonation or phosphorylation activates one formyl carbonyl of the diacylhydrazine. The oxygen of the other carbonyl attacks intramolecularly, closing a five-membered ring containing the atom sequence O–C–N–N–C. Proton transfers and loss of water produce the conjugated ring; deprotonation restores the neutral aromatic heterocycle.

The overall transformation is $\mathrm{C_2H_4N_2O_2\to C_2H_2N_2O+H_2O}$. Polyphosphoric acid provides carbonyl activation and an acidic medium. P$_4$O$_{10}$ acts as a strong dehydrating agent, consuming water and maintaining the condensation-driving environment. Neither nitrogen is expelled, and the oxygen retained in the heterocycle originates from a carbonyl rather than an inserted reagent oxygen.
