<h1 id="section-f/29/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**A consistent proposed sequence is desilylation, five-membered hemiketal formation, retro hetero-Diels–Alder opening, intramolecular aldol addition and hemiacetal closure.** The essential carbon rearrangement is loss of the original ketone–side-chain bond and formation of a bond between its two neighbouring carbons; it is not a simple [Michael addition](../../../../../../michael-addition.md).

For an explicit atom map, label the starting six-membered ring $A-B=C-D-E-O_r-A$, with the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) at $D$, methyl substituents at $A$ and $B$, the [hydroxy group](../../../../../../hydroxy-group.md) at $A$, and the side chain $E-X-T-\mathrm{Et}$. Here $X$ is the methylene and $T$ is the carbon bearing the protected [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md). Let $O_D$ be the original [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) oxygen and $O_T$ the protected [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) oxygen. These labels identify atoms, not new substituents.

First, [Fluoride](../../../../../../fluoride.md) removes the silyl protecting group from $O_T$. The resulting [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) attacks the protonated [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) at $D$, forming the five-membered [hemiketal](../../../../../../hemiketal.md) $D-E-X-T-O_T-D$. The original oxygen $O_D$ is now a [hydroxy group](../../../../../../hydroxy-group.md); the original pyran ring remains intact at this stage.

Next, The original pyran ring can undergo a [retro hetero-Diels-Alder reaction](../../../../../../retro-hetero-diels-alder-reaction.md). The three electron-pair movements are $B=C\to C=D$, $D-E\to E=O_r$ and $O_r-A\to A=B$: break $D-E$ and $O_r-A$, and form the three indicated [double bonds](../../../../../../double-bond.md). The result is the tethered aldehyde/dienol ester

$$
\mathrm{Me}C_A(OH)=C_B(\mathrm{Me})-CH_C=C_D(OH)-O_T-CH_T(\mathrm{Et})-CH_{2,X}-CH_E=O_r.
$$

The temporary $D-O_T$ bond keeps both reacting ends within the same [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md). At $D$, both oxygen substituents remain attached, and the new $D-O_T$ tether prevents separation into independent fragments.

Then The $C=D$ [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) attacks the tethered aldehyde at $E$. Formation of $C-E$, restoration of $D=O_D$, and proton transfer give the six-membered [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) ring $C-D-O_T-T-X-E-C$, with $E-O_rH$. This is an [intramolecular aldol reaction](../../../../../../intramolecular-aldol-reaction.md), and it supplies the crucial new carbon–carbon bond without losing any carbon atom.

Finally, [Keto-enol tautomerism](../../../../../../keto-enol-tautomerism.md) at $A=B$ gives the methyl [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) at $A$ and introduces the hydrogen at $B$. The $E-O_rH$ [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) then attacks that [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md), closing the five-membered [hemiacetal](../../../../../../hemiacetal.md) ring $A-B-C-E-O_r-A$. The resulting fused rings have exactly the product's connectivity: $D$ is the [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) carbonyl, $O_T$ is its ring oxygen, and $O_r$ is the five-membered-ring oxygen.

The [stereocentre](../../../../../../stereocentre.md) at $T$ survives throughout this sequence. The configuration initially at $E$ is erased at the aldehyde stage, and those at $A$, $B$, $C$ and $E$ arise during the later cyclization and proton-transfer steps. A folded [intramolecular aldol reaction](../../../../../../intramolecular-aldol-reaction.md) followed by reversible [hemiacetal](../../../../../../hemiacetal.md) formation is consistent with the drawn cis ring junction, but connectivity and orbital bookkeeping alone do not prove that it is the unique selected [diastereomer](../../../../../../diastereomer.md). In particular, a claim of simple retention at $E$ would contradict this atom map.

A related pyranone-to-fused-[lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) rearrangement under aqueous [hydrogen fluoride](../../../../../../hydrogen-fluoride.md) is experimentally reported; that account also explicitly qualifies the relative [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) as assigned on mechanistic grounds. The sequence above is a proposed explanation of the examination structure, rather than a mechanism claimed to have been experimentally established in that paper.[Pages 5220–5221, Schemes II–III and the discussion of pyranones 10 and 13 giving lactone 12](../../../../../../total-synthesis-of-%C2%B1-tirandamycin-a-by-philip-deshong-and-others.md)
