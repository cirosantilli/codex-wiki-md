<h1 id="section-f/29/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first two operations invert the allylic hydroxyl group. Triphenylphosphine adds to the azodicarboxylate, generating the reagent pair that converts the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) into an alkoxyphosphonium leaving group. Benzoate displaces it with inversion at carbon, giving the inverted benzoate [ester](../../../../../../ester.md). Methoxide then cleaves that [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) by [nucleophilic acyl substitution](../../../../../../nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md), **retaining the newly inverted carbon–oxygen [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md)** because cleavage occurs at the acyl group, not by substitution at the stereogenic carbon.

The inverted allylic [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) exchanges with the orthoacetate under acid catalysis. Ethanol loss produces an allyl ketene acetal, schematically $\mathrm{CH_2=C(OEt)-O-(allyl)}$. A six-membered suprafacial [Johnson-Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../../johnson-claisen-rearrangement.md) breaks the allylic C–O bond, shifts the ring [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) by one position and forms the new bond from the original [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) terminus to the ketene-acetal CH$_2$. The result is the $\mathrm{-CH_2CO_2Et}$ substituent at the carbon that was the remote end of the original double bond. A chair-like [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md) placing the larger substituents away from axial crowding explains the stereochemical transfer; the inverted [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) configuration selects the corresponding face.

Finally, TiCl$_4$ activates the anomeric methoxy group and methoxide departure creates an [oxocarbenium ion](../../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md). Hydride transfer from triethylsilane replaces that methoxy substituent by hydrogen. The ring oxygen and pendant OTBS group remain intact. **The sequence is inversion, stereospecific allylic rearrangement, then reductive removal of the anomeric methoxy group**, rather than direct reduction or hydrolysis of the [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) introduced by the Claisen step.
