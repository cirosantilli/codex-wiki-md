<h1 id="section-a/19/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Hydroxide attacks a PLA [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) carbonyl, moving the $\pi$ electrons onto oxygen. Collapse of the tetrahedral intermediate expels an alkoxide; proton transfer produces an [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) and a carboxylate. The carboxylate stabilizes the hydrolysis products and makes this [saponification](../../../../../../saponification.md) effectively irreversible under the basic conditions. Repetition cleaves the PLA block, including its terminal [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) linkage to the junction [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md), while the carbon–carbon [polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) backbone remains intact.

**The principal surviving [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) is a [polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) chain bearing the junction $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH(Ph)-}$ end and its nitroxide-derived distal end.** In [gel permeation chromatography](../../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md), the intact [block copolymer](../../../../../../block-copolymer.md) normally elutes earlier than this smaller surviving [polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) coil. Lactate and short hydrolysis fragments elute later still, provided the column and detector resolve such small solutes. The [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) peak therefore shifts to larger elution volume; its height need not remain constant under a mass-sensitive detector. Elution reflects [hydrodynamic volume](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-volume.md), so calibration against [polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) standards does not supply an exact absolute mass for the original mixed block.
