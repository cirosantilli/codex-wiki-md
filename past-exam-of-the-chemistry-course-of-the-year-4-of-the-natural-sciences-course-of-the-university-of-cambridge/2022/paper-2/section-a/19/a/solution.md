<h1 id="section-a/19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $R=\mathrm{CH_2CH(Ph)-O-TEMPO}$, so B is $\mathrm{HO-R}$. Its hydroxyl group initiates [ring-opening polymerization](../../../../../../ring-opening-polymerization.md) of lactide, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{C=H-[OCH(Me)CO]_{2n}-O-CH_2CH(Ph)-O-TEMPO}.}
$$

Each lactide contributes two lactyl repeat units. Dry [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md), exclusion of water and an alcohol-compatible catalyst such as tin(II) 2-ethylhexanoate under heating provide a practical route. [Alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md)/metal-alkoxide exchange followed by lactide coordination and acyl–oxygen cleavage extends the [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) chain while retaining a terminal hydroxyl group.

Heating C under oxygen-free conditions homolytically cleaves its benzylic C–O bond to the nitroxide, releasing a benzylic radical and TEMPO. The radical adds styrene repeatedly; reversible trapping by TEMPO controls the active radical concentration. D is therefore a **poly(lactide)–[polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) [block copolymer](../../../../../../block-copolymer.md)** with junction $\mathrm{PLA-OCH_2CH(Ph)-[CH_2CH(Ph)]_m-O-TEMPO}$ and the hydroxyl end of PLA retained. This is [nitroxide-mediated polymerization](../../../../../../nitroxide-mediated-polymerization.md); a temperature around 120–130 °C is a representative condition for a TEMPO/styrene system, rather than an exact universal optimum.
