**A is poly(norbornene); B is the corresponding sequential [block copolymer](../../../../../../block-copolymer.md) with a methyl-ester-substituted second block.** The process is [ring-opening metathesis polymerization](../../../../../../ring-opening-metathesis-polymerization.md). Each strained bicyclic [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) becomes a cyclopentane unit whose two backbone attachment positions are joined to neighbouring units through $\mathrm{-CH=CH-}$ links. The second block retains its $\mathrm{CO_2Me}$ substituent on the cyclopentane framework. Thus a useful connectivity representation is $\mathrm{end-[CH=CH-C_5H_8]_{n}-[CH=CH-C_5H_7(CO_2Me)]_{m}-end}$, with the cyclopentane attachment positions at 1 and 3. This representation specifies connectivity rather than a single backbone [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) geometry.

The ruthenium [alkylidene](../../../../../../alkylidene.md) reacts with a [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) through a [metallacyclobutane](../../../../../../metallacyclobutane.md), then fragments with the other pair of bonds broken. A new ruthenium [alkylidene](../../../../../../alkylidene.md) remains at the growing end, allowing propagation and then addition of the second [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md). Relief of the bicyclic [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md)'s [ring strain](../../../../../../ring-strain.md) supplies the principal enthalpic driving force; the loss of [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) translational [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) opposes [polymerization](../../../../../../polymerization.md). Sequential addition yields blocks only if the first population of active ends survives until the second addition.<a id="section-a/18/a/image-original-polymer-connectivity-fragments-and-the-reductive-ozonolysis-product"></a>
