<h1 id="section-a/18/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**Reductive [ozonolysis](../../../../../../ozonolysis.md) cuts the unsaturated backbone into cyclopentane-1,3-dicarbaldehyde units**, apart from end fragments. Both [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) carbons carry a hydrogen, so the carbonyl products are [aldehydes](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) rather than [ketones](../../../../../../ketone.md). Ring substituent [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) is inherited from the [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) repeat; backbone E/Z geometry does not survive cleavage.

Ozone adds across a backbone [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) by [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md), giving a primary ozonide. Its fragmentation produces a [carbonyl group](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and a [carbonyl oxide](../../../../../../carbonyl-oxide.md), which recombine to a secondary ozonide. [Dimethyl sulfide](../../../../../../dimethyl-sulfide.md) reduces the peroxide-containing intermediate, forming dimethyl sulfoxide and the two carbonyl termini. Repetition at every backbone double bond liberates the dialdehyde. An oxidizing work-up would instead risk oxidation to carboxylic acids, so the specified reductive work-up is essential.
