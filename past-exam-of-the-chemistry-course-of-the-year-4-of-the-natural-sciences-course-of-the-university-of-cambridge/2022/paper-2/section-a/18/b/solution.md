<h1 id="section-a/18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With one growing chain per ruthenium initiator and negligible [chain transfer](../../../../../../chain-transfer-in-chain-polymerization.md), consumption of [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) gives

$$
\boxed{DP_n=\frac{[M]_0}{[I]_0}x=50(0.98)=49.}
$$

The conversion factor applies to incorporated [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) units, not the number of active chains. Slow initiation or inactive initiator would invalidate this ideal [living polymerization](../../../../../../living-polymerization.md) estimate.
