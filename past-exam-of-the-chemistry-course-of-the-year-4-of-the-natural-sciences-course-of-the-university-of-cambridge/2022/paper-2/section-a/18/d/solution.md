<h1 id="section-a/18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**C has a saturated backbone and pendant $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ groups in its second block.** [Hydrogenation](../../../../../../hydrogenation.md) converts every backbone $\mathrm{CH=CH}$ to $\mathrm{CH_2CH_2}$; [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) then reduces each $\mathrm{CO_2Me}$ to $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$. The resulting connectivity is $\mathrm{[CH_2CH_2-C_5H_8]_{50}-[CH_2CH_2-C_5H_7(CH_2OH)]_{75}}$.

[Hydrogenation](../../../../../../hydrogenation.md) removes local double-bond rigidity and commonly lowers the segmental barrier to motion, favouring a lower [glass transition temperature](../../../../../../glass-transition-temperature.md). [Ester](../../../../../../ester.md) reduction changes polarity and side-group volume, while the new hydroxyl groups introduce [hydrogen bonding](../../../../../../hydrogen-bonding.md), which can raise the hydroxyl-rich block's [glass transition temperature](../../../../../../glass-transition-temperature.md) and strengthen segregation. These competing effects preclude a numerical transition shift or an unconditional direction for both transitions from structure alone.

The repeat-unit formulae after both operations are $\mathrm{C_7H_{12}}$ and $\mathrm{C_8H_{14}O}$. Taking standard atomic masses,

$$
\boxed{M_{n,\mathrm{repeat}}=50(96.173)+75(126.199)=1.4274\times10^4\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

This is **approximately 14.27 kg mol$^{-1}$ plus the net end-group mass**. The termination chemistry is unspecified, so a more precise whole-chain mass is not determined; the methyl group lost during [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) reduction must not remain in the second repeat's mass.
