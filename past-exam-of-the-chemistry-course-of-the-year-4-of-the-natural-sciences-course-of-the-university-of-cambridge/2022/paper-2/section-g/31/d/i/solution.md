<h1 id="section-g/31/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With an attractive [Lennard-Jones potential](../../../../../../../lennard-jones-potential.md) and $k_BT\ll\epsilon$, a short run can remain **trapped in an initial conformation or a metastable compact state**. A distance trace may show a long apparently stationary segment, an initial collapse, or occasional jumps between local structures; its histogram depends on initialization and is not necessarily an equilibrium distribution. An extended start need not collapse on the available run length, and a compact start need not already be the global minimum.

Accepting only downhill moves would make this trapping worse and would not sample the specified finite-temperature canonical ensemble. The sketch should therefore label this trace as unequilibrated and avoid assigning its mean a thermodynamic interpretation.
