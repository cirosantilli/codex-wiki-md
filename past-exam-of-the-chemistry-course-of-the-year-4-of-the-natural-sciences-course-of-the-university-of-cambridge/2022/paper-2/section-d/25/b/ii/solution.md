↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Here $r\gg\lambda_D$, so the thin-double-layer result gives

$$
\boxed{\mu_e=\frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(78.5)(0.045)}{10^{-3}}=3.13\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{m^2\,V^{-1}\,s^{-1}},}
$$

and therefore **$v=3.13\times10^{-5}$ m s$^{-1}$**, along the field for the positive [zeta potential](../../../../../../../zeta-potential.md).

In the opposite limit $r\ll\lambda_D$, the [Hückel electrophoretic mobility](../../../../../../../huckel-electrophoretic-mobility.md) is $\mu_e=2\epsilon\zeta/(3\eta)$, giving **$2.08\times10^{-8}$ m$^2$ V$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$**, two-thirds of the thin-layer value at the same $\zeta$. Both asymptotic expressions are independent of radius at fixed [zeta potential](../../../../../../../zeta-potential.md); the crossover depends on $r/\lambda_D$. These classical approximations neglect double-layer relaxation and strong-potential corrections.
