<h1 id="section-d/25/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the limiting area, the spherical core radius is $R=\ell=1.672$ nm. Its [aggregation number](../../../../../../../aggregation-number.md) is

$$
N_{\rm agg}=\frac{4\pi R^2}{a}=\frac{4\pi R^3}{3v}\simeq55.9.
$$

Above the [critical micelle concentration](../../../../../../../critical-micelle-concentration.md), free [monomers](../../../../../../../monomer.md) remain approximately at 8 mM, so 42 mM of surfactant units are in aggregates. Thus

$$
\boxed{c_{\rm micelle}\simeq\frac{50-8}{55.9}=0.751\ \mathrm{mM}.}
$$

The concentration is of whole aggregates, not the concentration of surfactant molecules inside them. The estimate neglects [micelle](../../../../../../../micelle.md) polydispersity and concentration-dependent growth.
