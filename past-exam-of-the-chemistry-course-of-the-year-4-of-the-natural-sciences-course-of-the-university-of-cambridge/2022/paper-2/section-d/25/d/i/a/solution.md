<h1 id="section-d/25/d/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A stable charge-stabilized dispersion has an interaction barrier large compared with $k_BT$.** In [DLVO theory](../../../../../../../../dlvo-theory.md), the total pair potential combines attractive [Van der Waals forces](../../../../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) with repulsion from overlapping [electric double layers](../../../../../../../../electric-double-layer.md). Similar surface charges raise the free energy of overlap; a sufficiently high positive barrier prevents thermally diffusing particles from reaching the deep primary minimum near contact.

Other stabilization mechanisms include [steric stabilization](../../../../../../../../steric-stabilization.md) by solvated surface chains and combined electrosteric protection by charged chains. Such contributions are additional to the simplest DLVO pair potential. Increasing salt shortens the [Debye length](../../../../../../../../debye-length.md), so double-layer repulsion becomes shorter-ranged and the protective barrier can decrease.
