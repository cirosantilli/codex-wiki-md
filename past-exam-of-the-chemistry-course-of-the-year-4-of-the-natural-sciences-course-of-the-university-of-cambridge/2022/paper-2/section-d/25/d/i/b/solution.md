<h1 id="section-d/25/d/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Primary-minimum aggregation occurs when particles can cross the repulsive barrier and reach near contact.** Its deep attractive well usually makes escape slow on experimental timescales, giving effectively irreversible coagulation. Higher electrolyte concentration screens the repulsion and can eliminate the barrier. The [critical coagulation concentration](../../../../../../../../critical-coagulation-concentration.md) is the electrolyte concentration at which rapid, approximately diffusion-limited coagulation sets in under the specified conditions; in an idealized DLVO description it is associated with loss of the protective barrier. Ion valence and surface boundary conditions strongly influence this threshold.
