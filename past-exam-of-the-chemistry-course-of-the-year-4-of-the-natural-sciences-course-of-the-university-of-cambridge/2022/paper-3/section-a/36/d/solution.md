<h1 id="section-a/36/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\mathrm{Ar}=2,6$-dimethylphenyl and $\mathrm{Ar'}=2$-methoxyphenyl. After conversion of the Pd(OAc)$_2$ precatalyst into an active phosphine–Pd(0) species, [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) of $\mathrm{Ar'Cl}$ gives $\mathrm{L_nPd^{II}(Ar')Cl}$. Carbonate-assisted replacement of chloride by the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md)'s [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) produces $\mathrm{L_nPd^{II}(Ar')[OC(Me)_2Ar]}$.

In the key [beta-carbon elimination](../../../../../../beta-carbon-elimination.md), the [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) forms a C=O bond while the bond between its carbinol [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and $\mathrm{Ar}$ cleaves, transferring $\mathrm{Ar}$ to [palladium](../../../../../../palladium.md). This gives **[acetone](../../../../../../acetone.md) and a diarylpalladium(II) complex**, $\mathrm{L_nPd^{II}(Ar')(Ar)}$. [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) creates the new biaryl bond and regenerates Pd(0). Thus the [tertiary alcohol](../../../../../../tertiary-alcohol.md) functions as an aryl donor by [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond cleavage, rather than being incorporated as a carbinol substituent. The overall organic products are **the cross-coupled biaryl and [acetone](../../../../../../acetone.md)**. The carbinol [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) has no [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), so ordinary [beta-hydride elimination](../../../../../../beta-hydride-elimination.md) from this [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) cannot explain the observed aryl transfer. The bulky electron-rich phosphine helps access the aryl-chloride [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) and the sterically congested biaryl-forming elimination.
