<h1 id="section-a/36/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Schwartz reagent](../../../../../../schwartz-reagent.md) adds syn across the terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md), placing [zirconium](../../../../../../zirconium.md) at its terminal [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) at the substituted [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The organozirconium intermediate is **$\mathrm{PhCH(OMe)CH(Me)CH_2ZrCp_2Cl}$**. The two faces of the achiral starting [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) give enantiomeric organometallic intermediates.

Boron trifluoride coordinates the [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), making the benzylic C–OMe bond susceptible to cleavage. The C–Zr bond then transfers its [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) terminus intramolecularly to the benzylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) as the activated methoxy group departs, closing a cyclopropane. This can be represented as a concerted, strongly assisted displacement rather than requiring a freely equilibrating long-lived benzylic [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md). [hydrozirconation](../../../../../../hydrozirconation.md) fixes the relative disposition of the new C–H and C–Zr bonds, and the preferred closure conformation places the bulky phenyl and methyl groups on opposite faces. The result is **racemic trans-1-methyl-2-phenylcyclopropane**, with much less steric crowding than the cis alternative. The ring contains the original two [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) and the original benzylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); no carbene [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is added.
