<h1 id="section-a/36/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first equivalent of [titanocene methylidene](../../../../../../titanocene-methylidene.md) methylenates the [ester](../../../../../../ester.md): the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md) form an [oxatitanacyclobutane](../../../../../../oxatitanacyclobutane.md), whose fragmentation gives **$\mathrm{PhC(OMe)=CH_2}$** and a [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) product. The second equivalent adds across this electron-rich double bond. The initial adduct is a [titanacyclobutane](../../../../../../titanacyclobutane.md) with cyclic connectivity $\mathrm{Cp_2Ti-CH_2-C(Ph)(OMe)-CH_2-}$, closing from the last methylene to [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md). The [ester](../../../../../../ester.md)'s phenyl-substituted [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) lies opposite Ti; the two new methylenes originate from the two methylidene equivalents.

Beta-methoxy elimination opens that metallacycle to **B, $\mathrm{Cp_2Ti(OMe)[CH_2C(Ph)=CH_2]}$**, the [allyltitanium reagent](../../../../../../allyltitanium-reagent.md). The C–OMe bond breaks, OMe transfers to Ti, one Ti–C bond becomes the new C=C bond, and the other Ti–C bond remains as the allylic [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md). The cyclic adduct is therefore a precursor to the opened reagent B; treating B as merely the one-equivalent [enol ether](../../../../../../enol-ether.md) loses the second reagent [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md).

The [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) coordinates to [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md). Allylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) transfer to its [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), accompanied by movement of the C=O pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), gives the [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md) [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) $\mathrm{PhCH_2CH_2CH(O{-}Ti)CH_2C(Ph)=CH_2}$. Acidic aqueous workup cleaves O–Ti and protonates [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), giving **$\mathrm{PhCH_2CH_2CH(OH)CH_2C(Ph)=CH_2}$**. A six-membered allyl-transfer arrangement is a convenient mechanistic model. No enantioselectivity is imposed by these achiral reagents.
