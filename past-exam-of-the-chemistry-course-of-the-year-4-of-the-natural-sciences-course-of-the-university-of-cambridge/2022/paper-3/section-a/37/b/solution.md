<h1 id="section-a/37/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[acetone](../../../../../../acetone.md) first forms a [proline](../../../../../../proline.md) [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) by reversible iminium formation and alpha-deprotonation. The [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) attacks the aromatic [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) in a hydrogen-bonded arrangement involving the catalyst's carboxylic acid. [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) of the resulting iminium releases the beta-hydroxyketone and regenerates [proline](../../../../../../proline.md). The usual L-proline arrangement gives the **$R$ [secondary alcohol](../../../../../../secondary-alcohol.md)**.

The sterically crowded aryl–[amide](../../../../../../amide.md) axis is selected at the same bond-forming step: rapid rotation in the [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) permits the catalyst to react preferentially with the conformer that puts the smaller carbonyl-oxygen side of the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) towards the approaching aldol transition structure and the bulky diisopropylamino side away. A chair-like [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md)–[aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) model minimizes the interaction of the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) with the catalyst-side axial [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md); reversing the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) orientation creates the less favourable diastereomeric [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md). Replacing planar CHO by tetrahedral CH(OH)CH$_2$COMe then raises the rotation barrier and traps the axis. This is a **[dynamic kinetic resolution](../../../../../../dynamic-kinetic-resolution.md)**, not resolution of two configurationally stable starting atropisomers.

To specify the predicted major axis without relying on a flat bond-line formula, look from the aromatic ipso [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) towards the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Put the higher-priority aryl path, the trimethylsilyl-bearing ortho side, at nine o'clock. The preferred model puts the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) at approximately six o'clock and the diisopropylamino group at twelve o'clock. This is **$S_a$** using the near-to-far high-priority convention; [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) outranks [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) at the far end. Coupled with the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) assignment, the model predicts **$(R,S_a)$**. The drawing defines the configuration even if an alternative viewing direction is used. The dynamic-resolution mechanism is established by the primary aldol study; the product-axis assignment here is a stereochemical-model prediction for the printed substrate.[Organic Letters 6, pp. 2051–2053: simultaneous alcohol and amide-axis control](../../../../../../dynamic-kinetic-resolution-of-atropisomeric-amides-by-vincent-chan-and-others.md)
