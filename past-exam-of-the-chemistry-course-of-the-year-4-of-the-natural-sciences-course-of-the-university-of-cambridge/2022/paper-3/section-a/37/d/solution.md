<h1 id="section-a/37/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

One valid asymmetric route uses **N-Boc-(R)-proline as a [chiral](../../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md) starting material**. Convert its acid to the [Weinreb amide](../../../../../../weinreb-amide.md) with N,O-dimethylhydroxylamine and a coupling reagent such as EDC, then add phenylmagnesium bromide at low temperature. [chelation](../../../../../../chelation.md) of the magnesium tetrahedral intermediate prevents a second addition; aqueous workup releases the phenyl [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md). Reduce it with [sodium borohydride](../../../../../../sodium-borohydride.md), separate the resulting [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) [diastereomers](../../../../../../diastereomer.md), and retain the one with **$R$ configuration at both pyrrolidine C2 and the benzylic carbinol**. This is an asymmetric synthesis through a pre-existing enantiopure centre, even though the final [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) need not be perfectly diastereoselective. If the other protected [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) is isolated, Mitsunobu esterification with a carboxylic acid followed by [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) inverts just the carbinol centre. Keeping [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) Boc-protected during that inversion avoids neighbouring-amine participation. Mild Boc removal followed by reductive benzylation with benzaldehyde and [sodium triacetoxyborohydride](../../../../../../sodium-triacetoxyborohydride.md) gives **D, the $(R,R)$ amino [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md)**. Use conditions that avoid epimerizing the amino [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).

For ring expansion, activate D's [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) with methanesulfonyl chloride and a [tertiary amine](../../../../../../tertiary-amine.md) base. Its own tertiary [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) attacks the benzylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) from the back, displacing mesylate with inversion and making a fused [aziridinium ion](../../../../../../aziridinium-ion.md). Acetate, supplied as a soluble acetate salt, attacks the **original pyrrolidine C2** from the opposite face and breaks the old C2–N bond. The new N–benzylic-C bond is retained, incorporating that side-chain [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) into the six-membered ring. Inversion at the two successively attacked [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) gives **the cis acetate/phenyl piperidine E** in the displayed orientation. Attack at the benzylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) instead would simply open the three-membered [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) back to a five-membered-ring derivative; specifying the attacked [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is the crucial regiochemical step.
