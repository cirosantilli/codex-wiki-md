<h1 id="section-a/37/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An unsaturated Rh(I)–[BINAP](../../../../../../binap.md) complex undergoes [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) of the aldehydic C–H bond, giving a Rh(III) **acyl [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md)**. Coordination of the tethered [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) brings it into the [chiral](../../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md) coordination sphere. [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) migration to the internal, tert-butyl-substituted [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) places Rh on the terminal methylene. The resulting acyl–alkyl complex has the connectivity required for a five-membered ring. [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) between its acyl and alkyl [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) closes the C–C bond, releases the cyclopentanone and regenerates Rh(I).

The [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) becomes the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); its [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) ultimately reaches the alkene-derived internal [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The original tert-butyl substituent remains attached to the internal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), which becomes the beta [stereocentre](../../../../../../stereocentre.md) of the cyclopentanone. [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) coordination and insertion favour the [chiral](../../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md) pocket arrangement with the bulky tert-butyl group in the less crowded region of the [BINAP](../../../../../../binap.md) complex. For the stated (S)-BINAP system the observed product is **the $R$ beta-tert-butylcyclopentanone**, equivalently the product with tert-butyl on the solid-wedge face in the supplied ring orientation. Its priorities are tert-butyl, the shorter carbonyl-side ring path, the other ring path, and [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). The ligand's S label alone is not a general rule that every product [stereocentre](../../../../../../stereocentre.md) must be S; the substrate orientation in the catalyst must also be specified. No decarbonylation is needed in the productive [hydroacylation](../../../../../../hydroacylation.md) cycle.
