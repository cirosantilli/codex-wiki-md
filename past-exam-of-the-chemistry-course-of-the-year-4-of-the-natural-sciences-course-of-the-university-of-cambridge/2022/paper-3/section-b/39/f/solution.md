<h1 id="section-b/39/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**The 375 meV pair should shift most fractionally, towards zero bias**, because C–H stretching has strong [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) displacement. A reduced-mass estimate gives

$$
\frac{\omega_{CD}}{\omega_{CH}}\simeq\sqrt{\frac{12/13}{24/14}}=\sqrt{\frac7{13}}=0.734,
$$

so the pair would move approximately from $\pm375$ to **$\pm275$ mV**. A frozen-carbon estimate gives $1/\sqrt2$, or about 265 mV; the actual [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) includes coupled motion. Whole-molecule modes change much less: the [molecular mass](../../../../../../molecular-mass.md) rises only from approximately 41 to 44, giving a translational estimate $\sqrt{41/44}\simeq0.965$.
