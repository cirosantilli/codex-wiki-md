<h1 id="section-b/39/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[inverse photoemission spectroscopy](../../../../../../inverse-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) is appropriate:** the fitted minimum lies above the [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md), so the state is unoccupied in equilibrium. Injection of [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) followed by [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) emission probes unoccupied states. Ordinary ultraviolet photoemission removes [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) from occupied states and cannot map this empty minimum without an additional population mechanism.
