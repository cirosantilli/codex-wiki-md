<h1 id="section-b/39/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An Au(111) Shockley state requires the near-surface band structure and projected bulk gap of a sufficiently thick [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) region. In an ultrathin film, its [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) reaches the [platinum](../../../../../../platinum.md) interface, where hybridization and leakage into [platinum](../../../../../../platinum.md) states can shift, broaden or destroy the bound [surface state](../../../../../../surface-state.md). As the [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) thickness exceeds the relevant decay length, the top surface becomes electronically decoupled from the substrate and the gold-like state emerges. **A monolayer's atomic identity is insufficient to guarantee a bulk-gold surface electronic structure.**
