↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The same [wave vectors](../../../../../../wavevector.md) now differ in energy by only 0.070 eV, so the curvature is $A'=0.14A=3.64756\ \mathrm{eV}\,\mathring{\mathrm A}^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m^{*\prime}=1.0445m_e},\qquad
E_0'=5.00-A'(\pi/20)^2=\boxed{4.910\ \mathrm{eV}}\quad\text{above }E_F.
$$

The nearly free-electron in-plane mass and high unoccupied energy suggest an **image-potential-derived state**, with an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) confined outside the film by its image attraction and surface barrier but relatively free parallel to it. Relative to the supplied clean-platinum vacuum level, its minimum is 0.84 eV below vacuum, a plausible scale for an image state. [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) can change the [work function](../../../../../../work-function.md), so 0.84 eV is conditional on using the supplied clean-surface value; an [adsorbate](../../../../../../adsorbate.md) work-function measurement is needed for a quantitative assignment. The dispersion alone supports the interpretation without proving it uniquely.
