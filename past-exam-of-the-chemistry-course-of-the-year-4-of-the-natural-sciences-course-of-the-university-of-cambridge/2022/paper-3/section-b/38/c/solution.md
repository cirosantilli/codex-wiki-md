<h1 id="section-b/38/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Fixed entrance retardation and fixed pass energy select the same pre-retardation [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), 111.4 eV. The required [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) energy is therefore

$$
h\nu_C=E_{B,C}+\phi_a+K_a=284.8+4.4+111.4=\boxed{400.6\ \mathrm{eV}}.
$$

It is lower than the original excitation by exactly the difference between the two core [binding energies](../../../../../../binding-energy.md), 114.4 eV.
