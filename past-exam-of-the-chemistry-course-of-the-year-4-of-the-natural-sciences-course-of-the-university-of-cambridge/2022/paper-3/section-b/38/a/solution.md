<h1 id="section-b/38/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [binding energies](../../../../../../binding-energy.md) are referred to the common [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md). Immediately outside the [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) surface, [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
K_s=h\nu-E_B-\phi_s=515.0-399.2-4.0=\boxed{111.8\ \mathrm{eV}}.
$$

This is the emitted [photoelectron](../../../../../../photoelectron.md) energy at the sample. On reaching the electrically connected, unbiased analyser, its energy is instead $K_a=h\nu-E_B-\phi_a=\boxed{111.4\ \mathrm{eV}}$. The 0.4 eV difference is the contact-potential correction, not an additional binding-energy shift. Distinguishing these two reference positions is essential for the retarding-voltage calculation.
