<h1 id="section-g/48/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A suitable sequence is **selective alpha-bromination, then [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) O-methylation**. Use one equivalent of Br$_2$ under controlled acidic [enolization](../../../../../../enolization.md) conditions to introduce bromine at the enolizable alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) that becomes the brominated vinyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The other alpha site is a constrained bridgehead and cannot form the required ordinary [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) double bond. Then O-alkylate the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) with [trimethyloxonium tetrafluoroborate](../../../../../../trimethyloxonium-tetrafluoroborate.md) and remove the brominated alpha proton with a nonnucleophilic base such as triethylamine. Collapse of the O-methylated [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) oxonium gives **the alpha-bromo methyl [enol ether](../../../../../../enol-ether.md) E**.

The methyl group belongs on [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), not on the alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Simply writing methyl iodide with an unspecified [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) would not guarantee this outcome because competitive C-alkylation is possible. Use conditions designed for [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) O-alkylation followed by [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md), and limit bromine to avoid overbromination or damage to the [protecting group](../../../../../../protecting-group.md).
