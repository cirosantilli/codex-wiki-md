<h1 id="section-g/48/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Manganese(III) removes one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) from the [enol](../../../../../../enol.md)/[enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) of H, and loss of its hydroxyl proton gives a **carbon-centred [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) at the tether-bearing alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of the resulting 1,3-dicarbonyl system**. The enolic OH becomes the second [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). This [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) attacks the terminal CH$_2$ of the tethered isopropenyl group, forming a new C–C bond and a six-membered ring. The residual [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) is on the substituted [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing methyl, which is more stable than the primary [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) from the alternative regiochemical closure.

Dioxygen traps that [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) as a peroxyl intermediate. [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md)/proton transfer and O–O cleavage in the ensuing oxygenation/workup sequence give the **new [tertiary alcohol](../../../../../../tertiary-alcohol.md) on the methyl-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)**. The drawn net transformation specifies that oxygenated product, not a persistent [hydroperoxide](../../../../../../hydroperoxide.md); individual [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)/proton transfers require the reaction medium and workup and cannot be fixed uniquely from the abbreviated reagent line alone. The rigid cage and folded tether select the closure face and therefore the relative configuration of the new ring junction.

Finally the new hydroxyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks silicon of [TBSCl](../../../../../../tert-butyldimethylsilyl-chloride.md), with imidazole assisting [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) and chloride departure, giving the **tertiary [silyl ether](../../../../../../silyl-ether.md) I**. The hindered pre-existing hydroxyls remain as shown. Track the two key atom changes: the terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) becomes a methylene in the new ring, and the former substituted [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) acquires [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and retains its methyl group. Calling the first step a two-electron aldol reaction would miss both the single-electron oxidant and the role of dioxygen.
