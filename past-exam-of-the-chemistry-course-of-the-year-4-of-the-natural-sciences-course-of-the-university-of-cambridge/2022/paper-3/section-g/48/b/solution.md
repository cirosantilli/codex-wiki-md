<h1 id="section-g/48/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Number the epoxide-bearing ring [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) carrying CH$_2$OH as C1, then proceed through the other [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C2 around the ring to C6. Heating with water opens the [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) at the less substituted C2 by backside attack: the incoming [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) becomes C2–OH on the front face, while the original [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) remains as C1–OH on the back face. Proton transfers complete formation of the triol. Thus the two original [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [oxygens](../../../../../../oxygen.md)' roles are distinct: one is retained from B and the new hydroxyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) comes from water.

Iodine next activates the remaining C4=C5 bond as an iodonium [ion](../../../../../../ion.md). The pendant [primary alcohol](../../../../../../primary-alcohol.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks intramolecularly at C5 to make the five-membered cyclic [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) with path **O–CH$_2$–C1–C6–C5**, opening the iodonium anti and leaving iodine at C4 on the opposite face. Bicarbonate removes the oxonium proton and neutralizes acid; it is not the source of the [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The folded tether selects the face of closure compatible with the bridged structure, accounting for the [ether](../../../../../../ether.md)/iodine [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md).

The remaining two hydroxyl groups react with acid-activated 2,2-dimethoxypropane. Successive [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attack, methanol departure and intramolecular closure form the **isopropylidene [ketal](../../../../../../ketal.md)** of C, protecting that diol without changing its [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) configurations. The terminal primary hydroxyl has already become the cyclic [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) and is not one of the two acetonide [oxygens](../../../../../../oxygen.md). This atom tracking distinguishes the [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md), [iodoetherification](../../../../../../iodoetherification.md) and protection stages.
