<h1 id="section-g/48/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Silver(I) promotes iodide departure, with precipitation of AgI. The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) of [dimethyl sulfoxide](../../../../../../dimethyl-sulfoxide.md) substitutes at the iodine-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) to form an alkoxydimethylsulfonium intermediate. Triethylamine removes the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) on that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); collapse forms C=O and breaks O–S, releasing dimethyl sulfide. The result is **[ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) D by a [Kornblum oxidation](../../../../../../kornblum-oxidation.md)**. In arrow terms, the C–H [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) form the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) as the O–S bond breaks. The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing iodine becomes the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); the existing bridged [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) and acetonide are spectators. The iodine centre loses its tetrahedral [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) because it becomes trigonal planar.
