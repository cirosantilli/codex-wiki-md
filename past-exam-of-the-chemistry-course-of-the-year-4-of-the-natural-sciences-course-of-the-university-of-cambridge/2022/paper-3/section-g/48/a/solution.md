<h1 id="section-g/48/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The free allylic [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) directs epoxidation of the adjacent [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) rather than the remote double bond. Applying the D-(−)-tartrate mnemonic gives **the [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) on the back face in the source orientation**, with both new C–O bonds dashed if the original six-membered ring is kept in the plane. The CH$_2$OH substituent at the lower [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) consequently projects towards the front in an explicit tetrahedral product drawing. The remote double bond is retained. The opposite tartrate would reverse both newly created [epoxide](../../../../../../epoxide.md) [stereocentres](../../../../../../stereocentre.md); it would not alter the location of the epoxidized [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md).
