<h1 id="section-g/49/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[methyl fluorosulfonate](../../../../../../methyl-fluorosulfonate.md) alkylates the tertiary [isoxazolidine](../../../../../../isoxazolidine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md), giving a **quaternary N-methyl ammonium salt**; the methyl remains attached to [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) in F. [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) from [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) then cleaves the N–O bond, releasing the corresponding [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) as an aluminium-bound [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) while restoring neutral [tertiary amine](../../../../../../tertiary-amine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md). Aqueous workup protonates the [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) to give the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md).

Thus the C–O bond made in the [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) survives as the alcohol-bearing C–O bond, and the formaldehyde-derived methylene survives as the extra [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) between [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) framework. The N–O bond is the one removed. **N-methylation followed by reductive N–O cleavage gives the bridged amino [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) F** without requiring cleavage of its newly formed C–C bond or hydrogenation of a nonexistent residual [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md).
