<h1 id="section-g/49/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A route from the supplied cyclohexenone starts with **conjugate methyl addition**. Form an [organocuprate](../../../../../../organocuprate.md) from methylmagnesium bromide and Cu(I), or use catalytic Cu(I) with controlled methylmagnesium addition, to obtain 3-methylcyclohexanone after [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md). [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) directs 1,4 rather than direct 1,2 addition, so the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) remains intact.

Generate its less hindered [kinetic enolate](../../../../../../kinetic-enolate.md) at the alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) **opposite the methyl-bearing side**, using LDA at low temperature, and trap with phenylselenyl chloride. [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) of the resulting alpha-selenyl [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) with [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) peroxide produces a selenoxide, whose syn elimination introduces the enone double bond on that side. Renumbering the double bond from the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) gives **5-methylcyclohex-2-en-1-one A**. The selenium-containing phenyl group is a removable reagent fragment, not a [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) building block incorporated into A; the only new product [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is the allowed methyl group. Regioselective kinetic [enolization](../../../../../../enolization.md) is important: enolizing the other alpha site would regenerate an enone with the methyl in the wrong position. This route produces racemic A unless a separate asymmetric step is introduced, which is sufficient for the stereochemically unspecified starting [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).
