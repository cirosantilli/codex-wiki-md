<h1 id="section-g/49/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The oxime is reduced at its C=N bond rather than cleaving N–O: [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) addition to the [imine](../../../../../../imine.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) followed by [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) gives an **N-substituted hydroxylamine**. In a chair-like model of the cis-fused ring system, the fused framework and methyl group make the two faces inequivalent. The favoured [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) trajectory accesses the face that places the newly installed C–H bond behind the original drawing and the retained **C–NHOH bond in front**, giving D. It avoids the more congested trajectory into the folded bicyclic framework; a flat hexagon comparison alone misses this facial distinction.

The existing methyl and junction centres are retained. The selectivity is therefore **diastereoselective substrate control**, not enantioselectivity from [sodium cyanoborohydride](../../../../../../sodium-cyanoborohydride.md), which is achiral. For a racemic starting bicycle the same relative stereochemical preference gives a racemic pair of D [enantiomers](../../../../../../enantiomer.md).
