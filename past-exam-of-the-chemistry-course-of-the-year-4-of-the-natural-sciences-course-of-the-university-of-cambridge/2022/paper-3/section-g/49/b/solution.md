<h1 id="section-g/49/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Tin tetrachloride coordinates the enone [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), lowering the dienophile pi-star energy. Butadiene reacts in an endo-oriented Diels–Alder [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md): its terminal [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond to the enone's two [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md), its central bond becomes the new cyclohexene double bond, and the enone C=C pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) become a new [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md). All three bond reorganizations occur in a [suprafacial](../../../../../../suprafaciality.md) six-electron cycle.

Approach from the face **opposite the methyl substituent** avoids steric crowding and gives the indicated relationship between methyl and the non-enolizable junction [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). The immediate stereospecific [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) gives a cis-fused junction relationship. The junction [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) next to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) can subsequently enolize; reprotonation on either face epimerizes this centre without breaking either newly formed C–C bond. This accounts for the mixture at the wavy-bond position while the other junction and methyl relationship remains fixed. The modest 1.5:1 ratio should not be mistaken for failure of [suprafaciality](../../../../../../suprafaciality.md) in the [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) itself. Later oxime formation removes the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md)'s ordinary [enolization](../../../../../../enolization.md) pathway and furnishes the depicted isolated junction [diastereomer](../../../../../../diastereomer.md).
