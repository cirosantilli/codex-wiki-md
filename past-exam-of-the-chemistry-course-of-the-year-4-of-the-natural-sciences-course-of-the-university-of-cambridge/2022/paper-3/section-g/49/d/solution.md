<h1 id="section-g/49/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The secondary hydroxylamine condenses with formaldehyde. [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) attacks the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), giving a carbinolamine; proton transfer and loss of water generate the tethered [nitrone](../../../../../../nitrone.md) **$\mathrm{R-N^+(O^-)=CH_2}$**, where $R$ is the entire bicyclic substituent attached to [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) in D. Molecular sieves remove water, favouring this condensation.

The [nitrone](../../../../../../nitrone.md) undergoes an intramolecular [1,3-dipolar cycloaddition](../../../../../../1-3-dipolar-cycloaddition.md) with the retained cyclohexene double bond. To define E precisely, label the former oxime [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) $C_A$, the adjacent upper ring junction $C_B$, the other junction $C_C$, and the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) $C_D$ and $C_E$ such that the chain from $C_A$ to the more remote [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) terminus is $C_A-C_B-\mathrm{CH_2}-C_D=C_E-\mathrm{CH_2}-C_C$. Let $C_F$ be the new formaldehyde-derived [nitrone](../../../../../../nitrone.md) methylene. The [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) forms **$C_F-C_D$ and O–$C_E$**, retains N–O and N–$C_A$, and changes N=$C_F$ and $C_D=C_E$ into single bonds. The new five-membered heterocycle is **N–$C_F$–$C_D$–$C_E$–O**. These connections, together with the original bicycle, specify **the bridged [isoxazolidine](../../../../../../isoxazolidine.md) E**.

The inwardly tethered [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) is [suprafacial](../../../../../../suprafaciality.md) at the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) and preserves the existing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [stereocentres](../../../../../../stereocentre.md); the C–C and C–O additions occur on the same accessible [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) face. The pre-existing N-substituent orientation restricts which fold can bring both reacting ends together without crossing the bridge. This is a genuine two-new-bond [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md), not a Mannich reaction followed by unrelated oxygenation.
