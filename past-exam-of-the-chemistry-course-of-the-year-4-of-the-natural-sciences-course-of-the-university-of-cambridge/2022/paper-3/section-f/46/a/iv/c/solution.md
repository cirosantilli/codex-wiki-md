<h1 id="section-f/46/a/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $E\gg V_0$, expand the reciprocal to obtain

$$
\boxed{P(E)=1-\frac{V_0^2}{4E^2}\sin^2(\alpha a)+O[(V_0/E)^3]}.
$$

Retain the exact $\alpha$ in the bounded oscillatory factor unless a further phase expansion is controlled. Reflection is small, with an oscillatory interference correction of order $(V_0/E)^2$. The [wavefunction](../../../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is nearly a travelling wave throughout; its wave number inside is slightly smaller than outside, so its [wavelength](../../../../../../../../wavelength.md) is slightly larger there. The reflected wave adds weak standing-wave modulation on the incident side.
