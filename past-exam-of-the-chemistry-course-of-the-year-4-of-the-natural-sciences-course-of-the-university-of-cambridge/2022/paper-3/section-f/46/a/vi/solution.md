<h1 id="section-f/46/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

With $\beta=1/(k_BT)$ and a consistently normalized reactant [partition function](../../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) $Q_r$, the one-dimensional channel formula is

$$
k(T)Q_r=\frac1{2\pi\hbar}\int_0^\infty e^{-\beta E}P(E)\,dE.
$$

Classically the integral starts at $V_0$, giving

$$
\boxed{k_{\rm cl}(T)=\frac{e^{-\beta V_0}}{2\pi\hbar\beta Q_r}}.
$$

An infinite free reactant half-line has no finite canonical population, so define a reactant normalization length $L$, for which $Q_r=L\sqrt{m/(2\pi\beta\hbar^2)}$. Then **$k_{\rm cl}=L^{-1}\sqrt{k_BT/(2\pi m)}\,e^{-V_0/(k_BT)}$**. Equivalently $kL$ is a one-dimensional thermal flux per unit reactant density, with dimensions of velocity. A [rate constant](../../../../../../../rate-constant.md) without either this normalization or a specified reactant well is not dimensionally complete.
