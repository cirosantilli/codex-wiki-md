↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**An individual bipyramid is [chiral](../../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md).** Matching helicities at the two [iron](../../../../../../iron.md) centres give the homochiral $\Delta\Delta$ and $\Lambda\Lambda$ [enantiomers](../../../../../../enantiomer.md), with the three spanning connections winding in opposite senses in the two structures. The idealized molecular framework has a threefold axis but no mirror plane, inversion centre or improper rotation that converts it into itself. Achiral synthesis can give a [racemic mixture](../../../../../../racemic-mixture.md) of these [chiral](../../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md) cages; racemic bulk composition does not make each cage achiral. The corresponding experimentally characterized methyl-substituted Fe–Pd bipyramid contains both homochiral [enantiomers](../../../../../../enantiomer.md) in its crystal.[Figure 1 and discussion of the bipyramidal crystal structure](../../../../../../dynamic-complex-to-complex-transformations-of-heterobimetallic-systems-influence-the-cage-structure-or-spin-state-of-iron-ii-ions-by-matthias-hardy-and-others.md)
