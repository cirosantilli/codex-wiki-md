<h1 id="section-e/45/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each primary [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) arm of 2 condenses with one [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) 1, eliminating three waters overall and forming a tripodal tris(pyridylimine) ligand $L$. The three [imine](../../../../../../imine.md) [nitrogens](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and the three adjacent pyridine [nitrogens](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) chelate one Fe(II), giving **$[\mathrm{Fe}L]^{2+}$**, with three outward-facing terminal pyridines still free. The central [tertiary amine](../../../../../../tertiary-amine.md) is the tethering apex, not a seventh donor in the six-coordinate model.

The Fe coordination is approximately octahedral and is propeller-chiral: three bidentate chelates can have **$\Delta$ or $\Lambda$ configuration**. Achiral starting components and conditions give a racemic population unless a separate resolving influence is introduced. [imine](../../../../../../imine.md) condensation and metal coordination cooperate in [subcomponent self-assembly](../../../../../../subcomponent-self-assembly.md); the metal template selects the convergent ligand arrangement.
