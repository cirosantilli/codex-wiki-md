<h1 id="section-e/44/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of the basic [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) host weakens its [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) binding. [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) transfers to 2, displacing [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md), which can then bind the crown-like site of 3. The predicted switched distribution is **protonated 1, $[\mathrm{Zn}(2)]^{2+}$ and $[\mathrm{Li}(3)]^+$**, giving **512 nm emission**. The strain penalty for [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) binding 2 is accepted when the better competing host is protonated.

Without [fluorescence](../../../../../../fluorescence-split.md), compare $^1$H [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) spectra of the mixture with independently prepared free and metal-bound hosts, looking for changes near the [amine](../../../../../../amine.md), pyridyl and crown-ether donors. $^7$Li [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) can follow [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) environments; chemical-shift changes and exchange-dependent line shapes should be interpreted using titrations rather than treating one peak as definitive proof of one complex. [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md)(II) is diamagnetic, so ordinary [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) observation is possible. UV–visible [absorption](../../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) can independently track changes in 2 and 3. Acid-only controls for each component test whether direct [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) shifts the reporter independently of [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md); metal-only and acid titrations test the proposed redistribution. The stipulated one-equivalent response is an idealized acid–base sorting cycle, not a pH measurement in aqueous buffer.
