<h1 id="section-e/44/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The predominant self-sorted distribution is **$[\mathrm{Zn}(1)]^{2+}$, $[\mathrm{Li}(2)]^+$ and free 3**, so the reporter remains mainly in its **554 nm** state. [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) benefits from the six [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) donors of 1 without forcing 2 into its strained zinc-binding conformation. [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) occupies 2 because its [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) affinity exceeds that of 3. Each metal is consumed in the preferred complex, leaving the reporter unoccupied.

This is a dominant-species prediction. A tenfold affinity difference alone would not guarantee quantitative [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) sorting if the absolute affinities were weak or if free metal and all competing complexes had appreciable populations. Concentration and [equilibrium constants](../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) are not supplied, so an exact species fraction is not justified.
