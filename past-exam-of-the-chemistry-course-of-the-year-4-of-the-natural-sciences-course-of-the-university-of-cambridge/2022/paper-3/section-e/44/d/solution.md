<h1 id="section-e/44/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[DBU](../../../../../../dbu.md) removes the proton from 1, restoring its zinc-binding ability. [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) returns from 2 to 1; the regenerated free 2 captures [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) from 3, restoring the preferred initial sorting. Thus **the reporter returns predominantly to 554 nm**, while [DBU](../../../../../../dbu.md) becomes its [conjugate acid](../../../../../../conjugate-acid.md) and trifluoroacetate remains as counterion. This assumes the added base is strong enough under the solvent conditions and does not itself compete effectively for either metal. Reversing a [fluorescence](../../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) shift without checking speciation would not establish all these transfers on its own.
