↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [gyromagnetic ratio](../../../../../../gyromagnetic-ratio.md) is the signed proportionality coefficient $\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma\mathbf I$ between a [nuclear magnetic moment](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) and its [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../spin.md). With the angular-frequency convention, $\omega_0=|\gamma|B_0$, and $\gamma$ is expressed in rad s$^{-1}$ T$^{-1}$.
