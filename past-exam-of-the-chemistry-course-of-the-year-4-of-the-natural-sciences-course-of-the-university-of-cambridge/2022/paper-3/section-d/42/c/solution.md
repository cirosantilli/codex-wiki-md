↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The energy separation is $\Delta E=\hbar|\gamma|B_0$. At 10 T this gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta E_H=2.816\times10^{-25}\ \mathrm J=1.757\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{eV}},\qquad
\boxed{\Delta E_N=2.847\times10^{-26}\ \mathrm J=1.777\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{eV}}.
$$

At 300 K, $k_BT=4.142\times10^{-21}\ \mathrm J=0.02585\ \mathrm{eV}$, so the splitting-to-thermal-energy ratios are $6.80\times10^{-5}$ and $6.87\times10^{-6}$. The two [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) levels therefore have nearly equal populations. Their equilibrium population [polarizations](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md) are approximately half these ratios, **$3.40\times10^{-5}$ and $3.44\times10^{-6}$**, explaining weak thermal [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) sensitivity despite well-defined resonant transition energies.
