↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) probes the chosen [isotope](../../../../../../isotope.md)'s local environments and motion in the bulk without electrical contacts. Line narrowing, relaxation and exchange measurements can expose hopping across a broad range of timescales, separate sites with resolved shifts, and provide microscopic [activation energies](../../../../../../activation-energy.md). Pulsed-field-gradient measurements can sometimes measure longer-range displacement directly; local relaxation measurements usually infer it through a model.

Limitations include low sensitivity for dilute or low-abundance nuclei, overlap of inequivalent sites, quadrupolar broadening for [spins](../../../../../../spin.md) above one-half, and contributions from paramagnetic impurities or other relaxation mechanisms. Heating can change defect populations or phase composition. Most importantly, **local hopping need not produce long-range [ionic conductivity](../../../../../../ionic-conductivity.md)**: back-and-forth motion, correlated jumps and trapped [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) can relax [spins](../../../../../../spin.md) without carrying charge over macroscopic distances. Connecting $T_1$ to a [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) requires an explicit jump model and comparison with a bulk transport measurement.
