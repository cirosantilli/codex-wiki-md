<h1 id="section-d/42/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For an exponentially correlated local field, a single-frequency illustrative relaxation rate is $T_1^{-1}=C\tau_c/(1+\omega_0^2\tau_c^2)$. Its derivative with respect to $\tau_c$ changes sign at $\omega_0\tau_c=1$. Slow motion gives $T_1\propto\tau_c$, and fast motion gives $T_1\propto1/\tau_c$; an [Arrhenius plot](../../../../../../arrhenius-plot.md) consequently has a **minimum of $T_1$** between opposite-slope limits.

For identical spin-one-half nuclei with homonuclear dipolar relaxation, include both transition [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md):

$$
\frac1{T_1}=C\left[\frac{\tau_c}{1+\omega_0^2\tau_c^2}+\frac{4\tau_c}{1+4\omega_0^2\tau_c^2}\right].
$$

Writing $x=\omega_0\tau_c$, the maximum of the rate satisfies

$$
\frac{1-x^2}{(1+x^2)^2}+4\frac{1-4x^2}{(1+4x^2)^2}=0,
\qquad x=x_*\simeq0.616.
$$

Let $\ell$ be jump length, $\tau_j$ the total waiting time, $\tau_c=c\tau_j$, and $f_D$ the [diffusion jump correlation factor](../../../../../../diffusion-jump-correlation-factor.md). In three dimensions $D=f_D\ell^2/(6\tau_j)=f_Dc\ell^2/(6\tau_c)$. Combining this with $D=D_0\exp[-E_D/(k_BT)]$ gives

$$
\boxed{D_0\exp\!\left[-\frac{E_D}{k_BT^*}\right]=\frac{f_Dc\ell^2\omega_0}{6x_*}},\qquad \omega_0=|\gamma|B_0.
$$

The numerical constant is one for the single-frequency illustration and 0.616 for the homonuclear dipolar model. Assumptions are a single activated process, exponential field correlations, a temperature-independent prefactor and coupling amplitude, and an established relation between local correlation and translational motion. Defect creation can enter the measured [activation energy](../../../../../../activation-energy.md); it cannot automatically be identified with a bare migration barrier. The fluctuation-spectrum approach originates in the relaxation theory of [Nicolaas Bloembergen](../../../../../../nicolaas-bloembergen.md), [Edward Mills Purcell](../../../../../../edward-mills-purcell.md) and [Robert Pound](../../../../../../robert-vivian-pound.md).[Physical Review 73, pp. 679–712: dipolar relaxation and motional correlation](../../../../../../relaxation-effects-in-nuclear-magnetic-resonance-absorption-by-nicolaas-bloembergen-and-others.md)
