<h1 id="section-d/42/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a positive [gyromagnetic ratio](../../../../../../gyromagnetic-ratio.md) the [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is parallel to the [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../spin.md); for a negative ratio it is antiparallel. The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) $\hat H_Z=-\gamma B_0\hat I_z$ gives $E_m=-\gamma\hbar B_0m$. Thus **$m=+1/2$ is lower for the proton, whereas $m=-1/2$ is lower for nitrogen-15**. Their [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) precession senses are opposite for a common positive field convention; measurable [absorption](../../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md) and energy separations remain positive.
