↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Yes, for all three ideal lattices under the stated assumptions.** Cubic symmetry and equal rates make $\boldsymbol\sigma=\sigma I$ with $\sigma>0$, so $\mathbf j=\sigma\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}$. Positive [ions](../../../../../../../ion.md) drift along the field. The current coefficient contains $Q^2$, so negative charge carriers would also produce a conventional current along the field, although their particle drift would reverse. Anisotropic barriers or a field strong enough to invalidate [linear response](../../../../../../../linear-response-theory.md) would require a new calculation.
