↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding the [scalar product](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) gives $j_\alpha=[nQ^2/(2k_BT)]\sum_j f\,r_{ij,\alpha}\sum_\beta r_{ij,\beta}\mathcal E_\beta$. Comparing coefficients of the independently variable field components yields

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\alpha\beta}=\frac{nQ^2}{2k_BT}\sum_{j=nn(i)}f(\omega_0,\Delta E_{ij},T)\,r_{ij,\alpha}r_{ij,\beta}}.
$$

The [tensor](../../../../../../../tensor.md) is symmetric and [positive semidefinite](../../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) because its contraction with any vector twice is a nonnegative sum of squares.
