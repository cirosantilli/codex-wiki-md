↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

At fixed [number density](../../../../../../../number-density.md), attempt [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md), nearest-neighbour distance and barrier, the conductivities have ratio **$\sigma_{\rm sc}:\sigma_{\rm bcc}:\sigma_{\rm fcc}=1:4/3:2$**. Thus the face-centred cubic array is largest. Numerically,

$$
F=10^{13}\exp\!\left[-\frac{0.35}{(8.61733\times10^{-5})(300)}\right]\simeq1.32\times10^7\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},
$$



$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm fcc}=\frac{2nQ^2Fa^2}{k_BT}\simeq1.47\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}}.
$$

The small value is consistent with the low supplied carrier [number density](../../../../../../../number-density.md) and strongly activated hopping. This comparison holds $n$ fixed rather than comparing fully occupied [crystal structures](../../../../../../../crystal-structure.md), whose site densities at the same neighbour distance differ.
