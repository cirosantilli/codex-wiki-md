<h1 id="section-d/43/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The conventional lattice-defect description is **vacancy-mediated [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md)**: an [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) moves into a neighbouring vacancy and leaves a vacancy behind. [sodium chloride](../../../../../../sodium-chloride.md) is another familiar example. In a real alpha-alumina specimen the dominant species and transport path depend on [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) chemical potential, dopants and grain boundaries, so this label should not be read as a universal microscopic assignment for every aluminium and [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) experiment. A vacancy mechanism is different from an interstitial [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) moving between otherwise empty interstitial sites.
