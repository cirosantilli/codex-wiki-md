<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Treat this as standalone [organophosphorus chemistry](../../../../../../organophosphorus-chemistry.md). The phosphorus(III) lone pair attacks the terminal nitrogen of the [organic azide](../../../../../../organic-azide.md). Cyclization of the initial phosphazide and loss of nitrogen give an [iminophosphorane](../../../../../../iminophosphorane.md):

$$
\mathrm{ArN_3+PPh_2(C_6H_4CONH_2)
\longrightarrow ArN=PPh_2(C_6H_4CONH_2)+N_2}.
$$

The ortho [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) provides a neighboring acidic NH [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) and a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) oxygen donor. A [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) relay to the imino nitrogen and intramolecular O$\to$P attack generate a five-membered, O-bound pentacoordinate phosphorus intermediate. Water can then attack phosphorus, with [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) to nitrogen, followed by P–N cleavage and opening of the neighboring-group contact.

The overall hydrolysis products are

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{ArNH_2+Ph_2P(O)(C_6H_4CONH_2)+N_2}.}
$$

The [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) group assists the hydrolysis and is regenerated; the product is **an aniline and the ortho-carboxamide [phosphine oxide](../../../../../../phosphine-oxide.md)**, rather than an amide-linked ligation product. The favorable five-membered intramolecular interaction and [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) delivery help hydrolyze a normally rather persistent aryl [iminophosphorane](../../../../../../iminophosphorane.md). This explanation concerns only the printed chemical transformation.
