<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $L^-=\mathrm{Ph_2P(O)CH=C(tBu)NH^-}$, with its O and N donors available for [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) coordination. A plausible symmetric tetramer has a **Li$_4$O$_4$ cubane-type core**, with alternating Li and phosphoryl O vertices and an additional enamide N donor attached to each Li. The O,N-chelating [Ligand in coordination chemistry](../../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) can be arranged equivalently around the aggregate, consistent with one $^7$Li environment. This is a connectivity model, not a uniquely reconstructed diffraction structure.

The initially formed nitrile-addition product has an acidic $\mathrm{CH_2}$ group next to the [phosphine oxide](../../../../../../phosphine-oxide.md). Transfer of one of these [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) to the nitrile-derived N gives a delocalized [lithiated enamide](../../../../../../lithiated-enamide.md), reinforced by O,N coordination and [organolithium aggregation](../../../../../../organolithium-aggregation.md). The organic framework becomes $\mathrm{P(O)-CH=C(tBu)-NHLi}$.

Assign **$\delta_H=4.82$ to the vinylic CH and $\delta_H=3.25$ to NH**. Each integrates to one [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), as required after hydrogen migration. Their singlet appearance does not mean that the original carbon has become a freely rotating, equivalent-proton methylene group.
