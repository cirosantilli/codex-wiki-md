↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The carbon–phosphorus coupling in the $^{13}$C spectrum is consistent with deprotonation of the methyl group rather than loss of that group:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{A=Ph_2P(O)CH_2Li}.}
$$

Addition to the nitrile initially gives $\mathrm{Ph_2P(O)CH_2C(tBu)=NLi}$. An intramolecular [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) then converts the imidoyl anion into a conjugated [lithiated enamide](../../../../../../lithiated-enamide.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{B=[Li\{N(H)C(tBu)=CHP(O)Ph_2\}]_4}.}
$$

The phosphorus–carbon bond is retained. **The rearrangement is a hydrogen migration, not a migration of the phosphoryl group to nitrogen.** Addition of half an equivalent of [TMEDA](../../../../../../tmeda.md) per Li gives the hemisolvated dimer

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{C=[Li_2\{N(H)C(tBu)=CHP(O)Ph_2\}_2(TMEDA)]}.}
$$

The tetrameric and dimeric nuclearities are established for this reaction system; the spectra in the question are consistent with them, but spectra alone would not uniquely prove every detail of the aggregates.[Abstract and structures of the unsolvated enamide and its TMEDA hemisolvate, pages 1401–1402](../../../../../../lithiated-organophosphorus-enamines-a-new-synthetic-approach-and-the-first-crystal-structures-by-william-clegg-and-others.md)
