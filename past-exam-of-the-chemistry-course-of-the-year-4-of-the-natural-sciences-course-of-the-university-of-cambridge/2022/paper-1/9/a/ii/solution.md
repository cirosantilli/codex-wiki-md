<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The nonnucleophilic magnesium [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) metalates the triazole carbon **between the two ring nitrogen atoms**, conventionally C5 of 1-propyl-1,2,4-triazole. That [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) is more readily removed because the resulting heteroaryl anion is stabilized by the neighboring nitrogen atoms; the reagent therefore acts as a base rather than adding a tetramethylpiperidinyl group.

The heteroaryl organomagnesium intermediate attacks the allylic bromide, forming a new C–C bond. The product is **5-(cyclohex-2-en-1-yl)-1-propyl-1,2,4-triazole**. Ordinary allylic substitution and an $S_\mathrm N2'$ description give the same unlabelled cyclohexenyl connectivity for this unsubstituted allylic ring, so the structural assignment does not distinguish those trajectories.

The [molecular formula](../../../../../../molecular-formula.md) checks the assignment: N-propyltriazole is $\mathrm{C_5H_9N_3}$; replacing a ring H by $\mathrm{C_6H_9}$ gives **$\mathrm{C_{11}H_{17}N_3}$**, as printed.
