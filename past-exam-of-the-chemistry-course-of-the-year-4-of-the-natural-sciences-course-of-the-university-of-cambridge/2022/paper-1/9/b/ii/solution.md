<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The analogous N-oxide rearrangement uses the carbon next to nitrogen that most readily supports addition and rearomatization. In [isoquinoline](../../../../../../isoquinoline.md) this is C1. Thus replace the quinolin-2-yl group of D by isoquinolin-1-yl:

$$
\boxed{\text{2-phenyl-3-(isoquinolin-1-yl)chromen-4-one}.}
$$

The ynone-to-chromone atom mapping is unchanged. The change is **attachment at isoquinoline C1**, rather than copying quinoline numbering and assigning an isoquinolin-2-yl carbon substituent at the nitrogen position.
