↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**An approximately alternating styrene–maleic anhydride copolymer** is produced. Its backbone alternates a $\mathrm{-CH_2-CH(Ph)-}$ segment with the two former alkene carbons of a maleic anhydride unit; those latter carbons remain connected to the intact five-membered [cyclic anhydride](../../../../../../cyclic-anhydride.md) ring.

[Homolysis](../../../../../../homolysis.md) of [benzoyl peroxide](../../../../../../benzoyl-peroxide.md) supplies initiating [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md). Cross-propagation between electron-rich [styrene](../../../../../../styrene.md) and electron-poor [maleic anhydride](../../../../../../maleic-anhydride.md) is favored over successive additions of the same [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md). Both carbon–carbon [double bonds](../../../../../../double-bond.md) become backbone [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md), without loss of a small [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) from each [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md). Initiator-derived benzoyloxy or phenyl fragments can occur at chain ends, with carbon dioxide formed when benzoyloxy [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) decarboxylate.
