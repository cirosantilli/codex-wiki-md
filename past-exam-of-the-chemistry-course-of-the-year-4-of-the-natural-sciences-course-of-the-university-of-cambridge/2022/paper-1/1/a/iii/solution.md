↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Predominantly head-to-tail poly(3-hexylthiophene)** is formed, with thiophene rings connected through their 2 and 5 positions. The hexyl groups occupy equivalent 3 positions along the [conjugated polymer](../../../../../../conjugated-polymer.md).

Selective iodine–magnesium exchange first generates an organomagnesium [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) while retaining the carbon–bromine bond. Nickel-catalyzed [Kumada coupling](../../../../../../kumada-coupling.md) then joins the organomagnesium end of one ring to the brominated end of another. Transfer of the nickel center to the new chain end permits [catalyst-transfer polymerization](../../../../../../catalyst-transfer-polymerization.md) and favors head-to-tail [regioregularity](../../../../../../regioregularity.md). Organic side products of the activation include isopropyl iodide; magnesium halides accompany the coupling. The repeat unit itself contains neither iodine nor bromine.
