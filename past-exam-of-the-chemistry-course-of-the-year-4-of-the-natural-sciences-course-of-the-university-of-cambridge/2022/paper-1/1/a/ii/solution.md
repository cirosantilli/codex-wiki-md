↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Syndiotactic [polypropylene](../../../../../../polypropylene.md)** is the expected principal product:

$$
\boxed{[\mathrm{-CH_2-CH(CH_3)-}]_n,\qquad\text{alternating configurations at successive substituted carbons}.}
$$

The bridged cyclopentadienyl–fluorenyl [metallocene](../../../../../../metallocene.md) has approximately $C_s$ site [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). Activation by [methylaluminoxane](../../../../../../methylaluminoxane.md) gives a cationic zirconium–alkyl [polymerization-active site](../../../../../../polymerization-active-site.md). Repeated [coordination chemistry](../../../../../../coordination-chemistry.md) and [migratory insertion](../../../../../../migratory-insertion.md) of [propene](../../../../../../propene.md) extend the chain. The two environments encountered during successive insertions favor alternating [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md), giving syndiotactic [tacticity](../../../../../../tacticity.md). There is no stoichiometric small [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) eliminated in the propagation step; initiator fragments and chain-transfer products belong to the end groups.
