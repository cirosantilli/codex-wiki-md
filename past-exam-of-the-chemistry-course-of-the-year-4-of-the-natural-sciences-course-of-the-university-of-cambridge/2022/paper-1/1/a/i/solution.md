**An aromatic–aliphatic [polyamide](../../../../../../polyamide.md) and methanol are formed.** Let $\mathrm{Ar}=p\text{-}\mathrm{C_6H_4-C_6H_4}\text{-}p$. The [polymer repeat unit](../../../../../../polymer-repeat-unit.md) is

$$
\boxed{[\mathrm{-NH(CH_2)_3NH-C(O)-Ar-C(O)-}]_n.}
$$

Each [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) linkage comes from [nucleophilic acyl substitution](../../../../../../nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md): an [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) attacks an [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), the [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md) expels [methoxide](../../../../../../methoxide.md), and [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) produces [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md). There are approximately $2n$ [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) of [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md) for $n$ complete repeat units in a long chain; the precise finite-chain count depends on its end groups. Removal of [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md) drives this [step-growth polymerization](../../../../../../step-growth-polymerization.md).
