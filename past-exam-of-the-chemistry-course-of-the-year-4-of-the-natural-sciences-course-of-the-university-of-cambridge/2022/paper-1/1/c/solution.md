↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the ideal equimolar [step-growth polymerization](../../../../../step-growth-polymerization.md), $p=0.5$ gives

$$
\boxed{\overline X_n=2.}
$$

This counts individual starting [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md), so it is only roughly one diamine–diester pair per average chain, rather than a long [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md). In a successful [radical polymerization](../../../../../radical-polymerization.md), many [monomers](../../../../../monomer.md) normally add between initiation and [radical termination](../../../../../radical-termination.md). **The styrene–maleic anhydride copolymer in (iv) therefore has the larger expected [degree of polymerization](../../../../../degree-of-polymerization.md).**

Conversion alone does not specify the radical-chain length: the conclusion assumes ordinary polymer-forming conditions, rather than arbitrarily high initiator concentration or extremely strong [chain transfer in chain polymerization](../../../../../chain-transfer-in-chain-polymerization.md).
