<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Gel permeation chromatography](../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) separates by solution [hydrodynamic volume](../../../../../hydrodynamic-volume.md), rather than [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) alone. At equal [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md), a more extended chain accesses fewer pores and elutes earlier. **The relatively rigid, conjugated poly(3-hexylthiophene) is the natural first-eluting candidate**, compared with the more flexible chains, provided all samples are molecularly dissolved.

This is a structural prediction, not an unconditional ordering: [solvent quality](../../../../../solvent-quality.md), [polymer aggregation](../../../../../polymer-aggregation.md) and column interactions affect retention, and the hydrogen-bonding [polyamide](../../../../../polyamide.md) may not dissolve adequately in tetrahydrofuran. An insoluble sample cannot be assigned an ordinary solution [gel permeation chromatography](../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) elution time.

A differential [refractive index detector](../../../../../refractive-index-detector.md) can detect all four [polymers](../../../../../polymer-split.md) when their refractive-index contrasts are nonzero. [Light scattering](../../../../../scattering-of-light.md) with an appropriate concentration detector can additionally provide [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md). A single ultraviolet detector is unsuitable as the universal detector because [polypropylene](../../../../../polypropylene.md) lacks the strong chromophores present in the aromatic and conjugated products.
