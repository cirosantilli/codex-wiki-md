↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a fixed-$N,V,T$ [canonical ensemble](../../../../../canonical-ensemble-split.md), sample the normalized [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) in [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md):

$$
\boxed{\pi(\mathbf r,\mathbf p)=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta H(\mathbf r,\mathbf p)},\qquad\beta=(k_{\rm B}T)^{-1}.}
$$

After integrating out the momenta for an ordinary separable [Hamiltonian mechanics](../../../../../hamiltonian-mechanics-split.md), configurational sampling uses $\pi(\mathbf r)\propto e^{-\beta U(\mathbf r)}$. The correct equilibrium distribution is ensemble-dependent; the Boltzmann form is the canonical answer, rather than a universal prescription for every possible ensemble.
