<h1 id="7/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Most high-dimensional [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) has negligible equilibrium [probability](../../../../../probability.md). Uniform sampling spends nearly all its work in these irrelevant regions and seldom visits the small subset that dominates the [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) or the numerator of an equilibrium average. The resulting estimator can have enormous [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md).

[Importance sampling](../../../../../importance-sampling.md) places samples where the product of statistical weight and observable matters. Sampling the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md), for example, converts a weighted phase-space integral into an equilibrium [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md). If a [Markov chain Monte Carlo](../../../../../markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) algorithm is used, adequate exploration of separate free-energy basins and allowance for [autocorrelation](../../../../../autocorrelation.md) remain necessary.
