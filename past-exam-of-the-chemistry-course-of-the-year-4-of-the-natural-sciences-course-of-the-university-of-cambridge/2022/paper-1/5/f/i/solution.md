<h1 id="5/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A path with $n$ visits makes $n$ departures from $B$ and $n$ departures from $I$. Its conditional mean duration is therefore $n(\tau_B+\tau_I)$. Averaging over the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md),

$$
\boxed{T_{AB}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n(\tau_B+\tau_I)q^{n-1}p.}
$$

Only the mean waiting times are needed here: [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) adds their contributions without requiring each residence time to equal its mean.
