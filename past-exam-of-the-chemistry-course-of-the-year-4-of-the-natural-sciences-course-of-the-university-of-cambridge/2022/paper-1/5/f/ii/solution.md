↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) identity gives

$$
\boxed{T_{AB}=\frac{p(\tau_B+\tau_I)}{(1-q)^2}
=\frac{\tau_B+\tau_I}{p}.}
$$

A direct renewal check gives the identical result: $T_B=\tau_B+T_I$ and $T_I=\tau_I+qT_B$, so $pT_B=\tau_B+\tau_I$. This also checks the units: [mean first-passage time](../../../../../../mean-first-passage-time.md) has units of time, unlike the dimensionless step count.
