<h1 id="5/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each visit to $I$ corresponds to two steps, one out of $B$ and one out of $I$. Hence a successful path with $n$ visits contains $2n$ steps. Taking its [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) over the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{S_{AB}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2n\,q^{n-1}p.}
$$

The last pair is $B\to I\to A$; it contributes two steps just like each previous $B\to I\to B$ excursion.
