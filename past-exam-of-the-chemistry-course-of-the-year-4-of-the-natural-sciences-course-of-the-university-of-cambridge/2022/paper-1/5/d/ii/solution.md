<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $p>0$, equivalently $0\leq q<1$, the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}q^{n-1}p=\frac p{1-q}=1.}
$$

Thus [absorption probability](../../../../../../splitting-probability.md) is one. The qualification matters: if $p=0$, the process alternates between $B$ and $I$ forever and never reaches $A$.
