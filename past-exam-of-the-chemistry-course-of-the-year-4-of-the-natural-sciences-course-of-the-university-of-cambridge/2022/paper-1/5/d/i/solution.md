<h1 id="5/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $q=P_{BI}$ and $p=P_{AI}=1-q$. A path with $n$ visits to $I$ makes $n-1$ unsuccessful excursions back to $B$, followed by one successful step to $A$. Every $B\to I$ step has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) one, so

$$
\boxed{\Pr(N=n)=q^{n-1}p,\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The visit count has a [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md), with success meaning the final $I\to A$ step.
