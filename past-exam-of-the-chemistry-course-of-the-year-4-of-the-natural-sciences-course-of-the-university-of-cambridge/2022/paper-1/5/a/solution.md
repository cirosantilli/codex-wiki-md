<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Asakura-Oosawa model](../../../../../asakura-oosawa-model.md), nonadsorbing [polymers](../../../../../polymer-split.md) are treated as ideal, mutually penetrable particles, but their centers cannot enter a layer around a hard [colloid](../../../../../colloid.md). The [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) [osmotic pressure](../../../../../osmotic-pressure.md) is $\Pi=\rho k_{\rm B}T$ in the ideal dilute limit, with the appropriate number density in the space dimension considered.

For fixed [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) number, reducing the inaccessible volume increases the available volume and lowers the [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md). Equivalently, at fixed [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), the [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) contribution to the effective colloid potential is

$$
\boxed{U_{\rm dep}=\Pi\,\Delta V_{\rm excluded}=-\Pi V_{\rm overlap}.}
$$

When the excluded layers overlap, their union occupies less space than two separate layers. More [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) configurations become available, so the resulting [depletion force](../../../../../depletion-force.md) attracts the colloids. It is an [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md)-driven effective interaction, even though each microscopic colloid–[polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) interaction is purely repulsive.
