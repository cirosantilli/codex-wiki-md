<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each face excludes [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) centers out to distance $a/2$. At face contact, the combined overlap strip has width $a$ and length $L$. Ignoring end corrections of order $a^2$, the two-dimensional [Asakura-Oosawa model](../../../../../../asakura-oosawa-model.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{U_{\rm min}\simeq-\rho k_{\rm B}T\,a
\left[\ell-\sqrt{(\ell+d)^2-\ell^2}\right]
\simeq-\rho k_{\rm B}T\,a\ell.}
$$

The final expression retains only the leading term in $d/\ell$. Its dimensions are correct because $\rho$ is a number per area. The zero of this effective [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) is the well-separated, nonoverlapping-exclusion-layer configuration.
