<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At higher coverage, neighboring outward dipoles generate a local field opposing each other's polarization. This [dipole depolarization](../../../../../dipole-depolarization.md) reduces the incremental dipole per added [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md), so the [work function](../../../../../work-function.md) becomes less sensitive to additional [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md).

Crowding can also tilt the [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md), alter their coordination or make them occupy less strongly donating sites. Any of these changes can decrease the normal dipole increment. **A reduced slope is consistent with coverage-dependent depolarization and geometry changes; it is not evidence that coverage has stopped increasing.**
