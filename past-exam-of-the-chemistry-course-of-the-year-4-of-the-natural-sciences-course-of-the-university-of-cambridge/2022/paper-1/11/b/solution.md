<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\Gamma$ be coverage in $\mathrm{mol\,m^{-2}}$. Then $n_s=N_A\Gamma$ and the Helmholtz surface-dipole relation is

$$
\Delta\Phi_{\rm eV}=-\frac{N_A\Gamma p_\perp}{\epsilon_0},
$$

where a voltage in volts has the same numerical value as the electron-energy change in [electronvolts](../../../../../electronvolt.md). Converting the slope from square centimeters to square meters gives $-5\times10^5\ \mathrm{V\,m^2\,mol^{-1}}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_\perp=\frac{\epsilon_0(5\times10^5)}{N_A}
=7.35\times10^{-30}\ \mathrm{C\,m}=2.20\ \mathrm D.}
$$

**Each added [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) produces an effective outward surface dipole of about $2.2$ D**, lowering the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [work function](../../../../../work-function.md). This is the incremental adsorbate-plus-surface dipole, not necessarily the isolated molecular [electric dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md).
