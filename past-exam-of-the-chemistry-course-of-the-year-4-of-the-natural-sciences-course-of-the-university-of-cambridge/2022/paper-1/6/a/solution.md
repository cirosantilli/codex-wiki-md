<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This is an **[Ireland–Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../ireland-claisen-rearrangement.md)**. Number the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) carbon $C_0$, its enolizable carbon $C_\alpha$, and the three allylic ring carbons $C_1-C_2=C_3$, starting at the carbon bearing the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) oxygen.

[lithium hexamethyldisilazide](../../../../../lithium-hexamethyldisilazide.md) removes an $\alpha$ [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and [trimethylsilyl chloride](../../../../../trimethylsilyl-chloride.md) traps the oxygen of the resulting [ester enolate](../../../../../ester-enolate.md), giving a [silyl ketene acetal](../../../../../silyl-ketene-acetal.md). Heating permits a six-electron, \[3,3\]-[sigmatropic rearrangement](../../../../../sigmatropic-rearrangement.md) through the array

$$
C_\alpha=C_0-O-C_1-C_2=C_3.
$$

The electron-pair movements are $\pi(C_\alpha=C_0)\to C_0=O$, $\sigma(O-C_1)\to\pi(C_1=C_2)$, and $\pi(C_2=C_3)\to\sigma(C_3-C_\alpha)$. Acidic work-up cleaves the silyl [ester](../../../../../ester.md) to the [carboxylic acid](../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md). The benzyl ether remains a protecting group; it is not the migrating bond.

The [suprafacial reaction](../../../../../suprafacial-reaction.md) transfers the starting allylic [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) to $C_3$, where the new side chain is delivered from the original oxygen face. In the product's printed orientation, **the new C$_3$–C$_\alpha$ bond is behind the ring and the C$_3$ methyl group is toward the viewer**. At that ring star, the [Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority rules](../../../../../cahn-ingold-prelog-priority-rules.md) orders the groups as side chain, alkenyl ring direction, saturated ring direction, methyl, giving **$R$**.

The second star is $C_\alpha$. Its priorities are carboxyl, substituted ring carbon, $\mathrm{CH_2CH_2OBn}$, H. A chair-like transition state normally places the bulky side chain pseudoequatorial. However, **its absolute configuration also depends on the E/Z geometry of the trapped [silyl ketene acetal](../../../../../silyl-ketene-acetal.md)**: opposite geometries give opposite configurations at this star under the same suprafacial ring transfer. The printed conditions do not state solvent or the ketene-acetal geometry. Thus the secure assignment is the ring-star $R$ configuration and conditional $\alpha$-star [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md), rather than an unqualified absolute assignment to both stars. Drawing the assumed ketene-acetal geometry and its chair is necessary before selecting one $\alpha$ epimer.
