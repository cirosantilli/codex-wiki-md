<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Disconnect the two new bonds of the [isoxazolidine](../../../../../isoxazolidine.md) as a **[nitrone](../../../../../nitrone.md) \[3+2\] [cycloaddition](../../../../../cycloaddition.md)**. Suitable achiral partners are $\mathrm{PhCH=N^+(Ph)O^-}$ and the trans enone $\mathrm{(2\!\! -\!pyridyl\ N\!\! -\!oxide)CO-CH=CHPh}$.

A practical [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md) family is **Cu(II) with an enantiopure bis(oxazoline) [Ligand in coordination chemistry](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)**, for example a Cu(OTf)$_2$–BOX complex. The enone can bind through its [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) oxygen and pyridine N-oxide oxygen, giving an O,O-chelated assembly. Chelation both activates the conjugated double bond and fixes its orientation in the chiral pocket. Highly enantioselective [cycloadditions](../../../../../cycloaddition.md) of this substrate class with Cu(II)–BOX complexes are experimentally established.[Abstract, Organic Letters 13, pages 436–439](../../../../../highly-enantioselective-nitrone-cycloadditions-with-2-alkenoyl-pyridine-n-oxides-catalyzed-by-cu-ii-box-complexes-by-santiago-barroso-and-others.md)

The [nitrone](../../../../../nitrone.md) carbon bonds to the enone $\alpha$ carbon and the [nitrone](../../../../../nitrone.md) oxygen bonds to the $\beta$ carbon. Thus the carbonyl-bearing carbon lies between the two phenyl-bearing ring carbons, as in the target. The trans enone gives the required trans relationship of its original acyl and phenyl substituents. Selection between endo/exo dipole approaches gives the remaining relative [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md); the sterically preferred chelated transition-state arrangement places the two ring phenyl substituents on the same face and the acyl group on the opposite face, matching the drawing.

One [enantiomer](../../../../../enantiomer.md) of the BOX [Ligand in coordination chemistry](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) exposes one enone face; its mirror image reverses the product's absolute [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md). The exact [Ligand in coordination chemistry](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) substituents, metal counterion and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) require optimization. The proposal identifies an experimentally supported [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md) platform and its stereochemical control elements, rather than claiming that an arbitrary BOX complex must give a specified numerical enantiomeric excess.
