<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A coherent mechanism is an **allene cyclization–[norbornene](../../../../../norbornene.md) insertion–cyclopropane closure cascade**. Label the propargyl chain $\mathrm{N-CH_2-C\equiv CH}$ as $D-E-F$. Base-assisted propargyl-to-allenyl isomerization gives $\mathrm{N-CH=C=CH_2}$. The hydrogen relocation is important: the terminal carbon must become a methylene before the final C–H substitution.

Reduction of the palladium precursor gives a phosphine-ligated Pd(0) [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md). [Oxidative addition](../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) into the aryl C–I bond supplies an aryl–Pd(II) intermediate. Intramolecular [carbopalladation](../../../../../carbopalladation.md) of the allene forms the aryl–$E$ bond and the six-membered lactam ring, retaining $D=E$ and placing Pd on the terminal carbon $F$. This allylic organopalladium connectivity may be represented by its interconverting sigma and pi forms.

[Norbornene](../../../../../norbornene.md) approaches from its less hindered exo face and inserts into the $F$–Pd bond. Carbon $F$ bonds to one former [norbornene](../../../../../norbornene.md) alkene carbon, while Pd bonds to the adjacent one. The resulting intermediate is geometrically unable to undergo an ordinary favorable [beta-hydride elimination](../../../../../beta-hydride-elimination.md): the relevant syn arrangement is blocked, or would produce a bridgehead alkene.

Base-assisted activation of an $F$–H bond gives a cyclopropane-forming dialkylpalladium connectivity, represented as a four-membered Pd–C–C–$F$ ring. [Reductive elimination](../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) closes the bond between $F$ and the Pd-bearing [norbornene](../../../../../norbornene.md) carbon, releases Pd(0), and gives the cyclopropane. The lactam alkene $D=E$ remains intact. Carbonate removes the acid equivalent generated in the overall arylation.

This mapping accounts for **the six-membered heterocycle, the new three-membered ring and exo addition to [norbornene](../../../../../norbornene.md)**. The [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md) and substrates are achiral, so the drawn relative [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) represents a racemic product. This is a proposed organopalladium pathway; the question's product alone does not uniquely establish the lifetime or order of every palladium intermediate.
