<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [Flory–Huggins solution theory](../../../../../flory-huggins-solution-theory.md), $\phi$ is the [volume fraction](../../../../../volume-fraction.md), $N$ is the number of equal-sized lattice segments per chain, $T$ is [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), $k_{\rm B}$ is the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md), and $\chi$ is the dimensionless [Flory interaction parameter](../../../../../flory-interaction-parameter.md). The stated $f$ is the mixing [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) per lattice site. The first two terms describe mixing [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md); the last describes the contact-energy contribution.

At fixed $T$ and composition-independent $\chi$,

$$
\frac{f'}{k_{\rm B}T}=\frac{\ln\phi+1}{N}-\ln(1-\phi)-1+\chi(1-2\phi),
\qquad
\frac{f''}{k_{\rm B}T}=\frac1{N\phi}+\frac1{1-\phi}-2\chi.
$$

Hence the [spinodal](../../../../../spinodal.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi_s(\phi)=\frac12\left[\frac1{N\phi}+\frac1{1-\phi}\right],\quad0<\phi<1.}
$$

The homogeneous state is locally unstable when $\chi>\chi_s(\phi)$. The minimum gives the [critical point of a Flory–Huggins polymer solution](../../../../../critical-point-of-a-flory-huggins-polymer-solution.md):

$$
\boxed{\phi_c=\frac1{1+\sqrt N},\qquad\chi_c=\frac12(1+N^{-1/2})^2.}
$$

If $\chi$ depends on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the equation $\chi(T)=\chi_s(\phi)$ converts this result into a [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md)–composition boundary.
