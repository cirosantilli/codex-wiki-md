<h1 id="4/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For two equal spheres of radius $R$, the effective radius in the [Derjaguin approximation](../../../../../../derjaguin-approximation.md) is $R_{\rm eff}=R/2$. The nonretarded attractive [van der Waals potential](../../../../../../van-der-waals-potential.md) is

$$
U(h)=-\frac{A R_{\rm eff}}{6h}=-\frac{AR}{12h}.
$$

Differentiating with respect to the surface gap gives

$$
\boxed{F(h)=-\frac{AR}{12h^2}
=-\frac{(4.0\times10^{-19})(10^{-9})}{12(2\times10^{-9})^2}
=-8.3\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm N.}
$$

Thus the requested approximation gives **an attraction of about $8.3$ pN**. Here $h/R=2$, whereas the [Derjaguin approximation](../../../../../../derjaguin-approximation.md) requires $h\ll R$. This is the formal estimate requested by the hint, not a quantitatively reliable near-contact approximation for the printed dimensions.
