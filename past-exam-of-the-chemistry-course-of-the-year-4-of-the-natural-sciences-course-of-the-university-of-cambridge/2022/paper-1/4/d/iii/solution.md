<h1 id="4/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Stern layer](../../../../../../stern-layer.md) is the compact near-surface layer of specifically adsorbed or closely associated ions. The [diffuse layer](../../../../../../diffuse-layer.md) is the more extended, thermally distributed excess of counterions that screens the surface charge.

For water near $25^\circ\mathrm C$, the [Debye length](../../../../../../debye-length.md) can be written

$$
\lambda_D\simeq\frac{0.304\ \mathrm{nm}}{\sqrt{I/(\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}})}},\qquad
I=\frac12\sum_i c_i z_i^2.
$$

For $0.01$ M KCl, $I=0.01$ M and **$\lambda_D\simeq3.0$ nm**. For a fully dissociated 2:2 [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) at the same formula-unit concentration, $I=4c=0.04$ M, giving **$\lambda_D\simeq1.5$ nm**, half as large. These are linear-screening estimates, not sharp boundaries of the [diffuse layer](../../../../../../diffuse-layer.md).
