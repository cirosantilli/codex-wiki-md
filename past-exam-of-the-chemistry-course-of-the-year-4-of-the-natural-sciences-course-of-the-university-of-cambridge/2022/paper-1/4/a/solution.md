<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a homogeneous [polymer solution](../../../../../polymer-solution.md), examine the curvature of the mixing [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) with respect to composition. A composition fluctuation into equal volumes at $\phi\pm\delta\phi$ changes the average free energy by

$$
\frac{f(\phi+\delta\phi)+f(\phi-\delta\phi)}2-f(\phi)
=\frac12 f''(\phi)(\delta\phi)^2+O((\delta\phi)^4).
$$

Thus $f''>0$ locally resists fluctuations and $f''<0$ amplifies them, leading to [spinodal decomposition](../../../../../spinodal-decomposition.md). **The [spinodal](../../../../../spinodal.md) curve is the locus $f''=0$**, separating locally stable or metastable homogeneous compositions from locally unstable ones. The coexistence [binodal](../../../../../binodal.md) instead follows a common tangent to the free energy. Between the [binodal](../../../../../binodal.md) and [spinodal](../../../../../spinodal.md), separation needs [nucleation](../../../../../nucleation.md).
