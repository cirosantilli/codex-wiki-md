↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [critical micelle concentration](../../../../../../critical-micelle-concentration.md) is the approximate total [surfactant](../../../../../../surfactant.md) concentration above which additional [surfactant](../../../../../../surfactant.md) predominantly enters [micelles](../../../../../../micelle.md). Below it, most [surfactant](../../../../../../surfactant.md) is present as separate [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md); above it, the free-monomer concentration changes much more slowly while the [micelle](../../../../../../micelle.md) population increases.

The crossover occurs when aggregation balances the unfavorable exposure of hydrophobic tails to water against head-group repulsion and the loss of molecular translational [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md). **It is an aggregation threshold, not the concentration at which every [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) becomes micellar.**
