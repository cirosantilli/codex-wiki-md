↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A longer hydrophobic tail makes transfer from water into a [micelle](../../../../../../micelle.md) more favorable, lowering the [critical micelle concentration](../../../../../../critical-micelle-concentration.md) within this homologous series. Ranking the four quoted concentrations therefore gives

$$
\boxed{B:\mathrm C_4,\qquad D:\mathrm C_8,\qquad A:\mathrm C_{10},\qquad C:\mathrm C_{14}.}
$$

This assignment uses the chain lengths and concentration ranking printed in the question; it does not identify the compounds by substituting an external table of measured concentrations.
