↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Product 6 is a remotely oxidized [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) of the undecylammonium chain**, retaining the ammonium group and its counterion. Number from the ammonium-bearing carbon as C1. Crown-mediated recognition positions the remote C8–C9 region near the Mn–oxo center. A representative major-site product is the C8 [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{[H_3N^+-(CH_2)_7-C(O)-(CH_2)_2-CH_3]BF_4^-}.}
$$

Its adjacent possible regioisomer is the C9 [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) $\mathrm{[H_3N^+-(CH_2)_8-C(O)-CH_2-CH_3]BF_4^-}$. The precise single-site assignment is a limitation discussed below.

The [crown ether](../../../../../crown-ether.md) binds the primary ammonium end through several N–H···O [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md). This [host–guest complex](../../../../../host-guest-complex.md) holds a folded portion of the chain close to the manganese oxidant, so the effective local concentration of a remote secondary C–H bond can overcome the intrinsic advantage of a free tertiary C–H bond. Such recognition-driven reversal of substrate preference is experimentally established.[Abstract and competitive substrate oxidation experiments](../../../../../enzyme-like-substrate-selectivity-in-c-h-oxidation-enabled-by-recognition-by-giorgio-olivo-and-others.md)

The key intermediates are the crown-bound ammonium–Mn=O assembly, a remote carbon radical after H abstraction, and the rebound secondary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). A second oxidation removes the remaining hydrogen on the carbinol carbon to give the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md). The overall oxygen-equivalent count is

$$
\mathrm{RCH_2R'+H_2O_2\to RCH(OH)R'+H_2O},
\qquad
\mathrm{RCH(OH)R'+H_2O_2\to RC(O)R'+2H_2O}.
$$

Thus **one [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) requires two equivalents of peroxide**, explaining a maximum near 50% [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) yield with one equivalent and near-complete conversion with two.

The primary remote-oxidation study reports preference for **C8 and C9**, not a universally unique carbon. The schematic [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md) drawing and the oxygen budget alone cannot distinguish those positions quantitatively. The “single product” wording is therefore treated as an idealized remote-site assignment; a uniquely verified C8:C9 ratio is not invented.[Abstract, remote C8–C9 selectivity](../../../../../supramolecular-recognition-allows-remote-site-selective-c-h-oxidation-of-methylenic-sites-in-linear-amines-by-giorgio-olivo-and-others.md)
