<h1 id="14/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Ba$^{2+}$ competes strongly for the [crown ether](../../../../../crown-ether.md) receptors. With the recognition sites occupied, the undecylammonium chain can no longer be effectively preorganized near manganese. **The reaction should move back towards oxidation of methylcyclohexane to 1-methylcyclohexan-1-ol**, with suppression of the selectively formed remote amino [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md).

The single available peroxide equivalent is then sufficient for tertiary C–H hydroxylation, whereas a secondary-chain [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) would require two oxidations. Competitive binding constants and metal-induced structural changes would determine the exact conversion and residual selectivity; neither a numerical yield nor complete inhibition of all oxidation follows merely from adding two equivalents of barium salt.
