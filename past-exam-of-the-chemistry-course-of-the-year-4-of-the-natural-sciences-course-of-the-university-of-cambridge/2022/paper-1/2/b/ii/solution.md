<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Formal ionic counting gives La(III), N(−III), and therefore Re(VI), with a $5d^1$ configuration. An isolated localized $d^1$ center would carry a [saturation magnetic moment](../../../../../../saturation-magnetic-moment.md) of order $1\mu_{\rm B}$, much larger than the value quoted. It is not a $d^0$ ion made diamagnetic simply by an octahedral [ligand field](../../../../../../ligand-field.md).

The spatially extended Re $5d$ orbitals overlap strongly with N $2p$ orbitals. Re–N $\sigma$ interactions raise the $e_g$-derived antibonding states, while $\pi$ interactions mix the $t_{2g}$ states with the nitrogen network. Repetition through corner-sharing [octahedra](../../../../../../octahedron.md) turns these combinations into broad [energy bands](../../../../../../energy-band.md). The nominal $d$ [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) occupies a delocalized, partially filled band rather than an isolated magnetic orbital.

Only [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) close to the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) change their occupations appreciably in an applied [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Consequently a [Pauli paramagnetism](../../../../../../pauli-paramagnetism.md) has a small, nonzero field-induced [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md):

$$
M\simeq\mu_{\rm B}^2 g(E_F)B
$$

when $g$ is the density of states including both [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) directions, expressed per formula unit and per unit energy. This band model explains **a very small moment without requiring the absence of [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) in the Re-derived band**. The reported material has metallic transport and a Pauli-like response.[Magnetic and transport measurements, Figure 4](../../../../../../preparation-of-bulk-phase-nitride-perovskite-laren3-and-topotactic-reduction-to-lanio2-type-laren2-by-simon-d-kloss-and-others.md)

The description “saturated” in the question should not be interpreted as complete alignment of one localized [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) per Re: a nearly linear metallic response over a laboratory field range need not be a true fully spin-polarized saturation moment.
