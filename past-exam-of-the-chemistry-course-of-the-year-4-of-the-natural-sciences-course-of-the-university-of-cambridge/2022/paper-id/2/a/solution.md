<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take a fixed amount of dry air, with no phase change, moving quasistatically so that its [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) matches the surrounding atmosphere. From the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) and $H=U+pV$,

$$
dH=dU+p\,dV+V\,dp=dq+V\,dp.
$$

For adiabatic motion $dq=0$. Treating the parcel as an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) of fixed composition makes its [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) depend only on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), so for $n$ moles $dH=nC_p\,dT$ even though the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) changes. Hence $nC_p\,dT=V\,dp$. The environment is in [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $dp/dz=-\rho g$, and the parcel is taken to have the ambient density at the displacement point. Using $\rho=nM/V$ gives

$$
nC_p\frac{dT}{dz}=V\frac{dp}{dz}=-nMg,
\qquad\boxed{\frac{dT}{dz}=-\frac{Mg}{C_p}=-\frac{g}{c_p}.}
$$

Here $C_p$ is molar and $c_p=C_p/M$ is mass-specific [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md). Positive $z$ points upward; the commonly quoted positive [dry adiabatic lapse rate](../../../../../dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md) is $\Gamma_d=-dT/dz$. Approximate constancy of $g$ and [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) gives a constant gradient. Condensation, external heating, [entrainment](../../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) or composition changes invalidate this dry-parcel relation.
