↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Lower winter temperatures favour formation and persistence of [polar stratospheric clouds](../../../../../polar-stratospheric-cloud.md), providing particle surfaces on which reservoir [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) can be activated, for example

$$
\mathrm{HCl+ClONO_2\longrightarrow Cl_2+HNO_3},\qquad
\mathrm{HCl+HOCl\longrightarrow Cl_2+H_2O}.
$$

These are heterogeneous surface processes; cooling changes particle abundance, adsorption, phase and reaction uptake, so a simple gas-phase Arrhenius argument is insufficient. Removal of [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md) in sedimenting particles can also delay conversion of active [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) back into [chlorine nitrate](../../../../../chlorine-nitrate.md). The relevant condensation thresholds depend on [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and [water](../../../../../water.md)/nitric-acid abundances, rather than a single universal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).

Once sunlight is present, $\mathrm{Cl_2+h\nu\to2Cl}$ provides active [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md). In the [chlorine monoxide dimer cycle](../../../../../chlorine-monoxide-dimer-cycle.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(\mathrm{Cl+O_3}&\mathrm{\longrightarrow ClO+O_2}),\\
\mathrm{ClO+ClO+M}&\mathrm{\rightleftharpoons ClOOCl+M},\\
\mathrm{ClOOCl}+h\nu&\mathrm{\longrightarrow Cl+ClOO},\\
\mathrm{ClOO+M}&\mathrm{\longrightarrow Cl+O_2+M}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The net reaction is $\mathrm{2O_3\to3O_2}$, with [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) regenerated. Low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) favours formation of the [ClOOCl dimer](../../../../../cloocl-dimer.md) and suppresses its [thermal dissociation](../../../../../thermal-dissociation.md), making [photodissociation](../../../../../photodissociation.md) rather than thermal return of two ClO radicals more competitive. Laboratory flow experiments formed the dimer at 220–240 K and distinguished its infrared and [ultraviolet](../../../../../ultraviolet.md) signatures, supporting the chemical identity needed for this mechanism.[Journal of Physical Chemistry 91 (1987), pp. 433–436; NASA record 19870061046, flow-system generation and spectral identification](../../../../../production-of-chlorine-oxide-cl2o2-from-the-self-reaction-of-the-chlorine-oxide-clo-radical-by-l-t-molina-and-m-j-molina.md)

**Colder polar conditions can prepare and sustain greater springtime [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) loss, but darkness alone does not drive the photolytic cycle.** In polar night reactive precursors can accumulate; sunlight is required for rapid dimer-cycle [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) destruction. This differs from the middle-stratospheric gas-phase cooling response derived above.[Polar [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) depletion discussion: low temperatures, heterogeneous activation and sunlight-dependent chemistry](../../../../../scientific-assessment-of-ozone-depletion-2022-executive-summary.md)
