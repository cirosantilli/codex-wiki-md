<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a vertical-beam, effective-wavelength [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../../beer-lambert-law.md) approximation, the [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) above altitude $z$ is $\tau(z)=\sigma_{\mathrm O_2}N_{\mathrm O_2}(z)+\sigma_{\mathrm O_3}N_{\mathrm O_3}(z)$. Using centimetres consistently gives

$$
\tau_{20}=1.50+12.60=14.10,\qquad
\tau_{40}=0.084+0.340=0.424.
$$

The transmitted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) flux is proportional to $e^{-\tau}$. If the parent-gas [absorption cross section](../../../../../absorption-cross-section.md) and [photodissociation](../../../../../photodissociation.md) yield are otherwise the same, they cancel in the ratio of [photolysis rate coefficients](../../../../../photolysis-rate-coefficient.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{J(40\ \mathrm{km})}{J(20\ \mathrm{km})}=e^{\tau_{20}-\tau_{40}}
=e^{13.676}=8.70\times10^5.}
$$

The corresponding fractions of the unattenuated beam are about $7.52\times10^{-7}$ and $0.654$. Strong shielding by the overlying [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and especially [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) therefore makes destruction vastly faster higher in the [stratosphere](../../../../../stratosphere.md), even though the molecular absorption properties have not changed.

This numerical ratio uses the intended simplified radiation model. A real [photolysis rate coefficient](../../../../../photolysis-rate-coefficient.md) integrates wavelength-dependent [actinic flux](../../../../../actinic-flux.md), [absorption cross section](../../../../../absorption-cross-section.md) and [quantum yield](../../../../../quantum-yield.md). A nonvertical direct beam would use slant [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) $\tau/\cos\theta$; diffuse scattering and the true solar spectrum would also matter. The supplied effective cross sections do not specify a unique broadband radiative-transfer calculation.
