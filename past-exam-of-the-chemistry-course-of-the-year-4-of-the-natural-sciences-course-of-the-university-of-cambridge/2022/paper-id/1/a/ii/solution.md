↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A small [global warming potential](../../../../../../global-warming-potential.md) requires both a **small [greenhouse gas radiative efficiency](../../../../../../greenhouse-gas-radiative-efficiency.md) per unit emitted [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and a short [atmospheric lifetime](../../../../../../atmospheric-lifetime.md)**. Weak absorption in the terrestrial [atmospheric window](../../../../../../atmospheric-window.md) is desirable, particularly where absorption by existing gases is weak; strong carbon–[fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) vibrational bands can make even an ozone-safe substitute an efficient [greenhouse gas](../../../../../../greenhouse-gas.md). Rapid removal by lower-atmospheric chemistry limits the integrated burden, provided it does not generate abundant, long-lived radiatively active products. The atmospheric residence time and the absorption spectrum must therefore be assessed separately.
