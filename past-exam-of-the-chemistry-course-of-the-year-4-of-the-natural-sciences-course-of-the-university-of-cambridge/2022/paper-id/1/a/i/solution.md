<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Prefer a replacement that releases little reactive [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) or [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) into the [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md): ideally omit those elements, or give the parent gas a short [troposphere](../../../../../../troposphere.md) removal time compared with transport to the [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md). A carbon–hydrogen bond can permit rapid attack by the [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md); other readily oxidized functionality can also shorten the atmospheric residence time. The degradation products must themselves avoid transporting long-lived reactive halogen to altitude. Merely retaining fewer [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) atoms is less effective than preventing their delivery altogether.

Both [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) and [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) participate in catalytic [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) destruction. [Bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) is much more effective per delivered atom, by a factor of roughly 60 in the usual lower-stratospheric comparison; the precise effectiveness depends on chemical partitioning and location. [Fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) is largely trapped in very stable [hydrogen fluoride](../../../../../../hydrogen-fluoride.md) and does not sustain an analogous ozone-destroying cycle. Thus **short-lived gases without [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) or [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) have the smallest direct [ozone-depletion potential](../../../../../../ozone-depletion-potential.md)**. Fluorination alone does not ensure a small climate impact.[Tropospheric [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) and [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) assessments: per-atom effectiveness and the absence of direct fluorine-driven [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) depletion](../../../../../../scientific-assessment-of-ozone-depletion-2022-executive-summary.md)
