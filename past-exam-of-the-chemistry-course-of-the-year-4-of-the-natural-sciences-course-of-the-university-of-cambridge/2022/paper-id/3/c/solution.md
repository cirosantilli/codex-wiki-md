<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a liquid [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) at fixed [water](../../../../../water.md) content and geometrical thickness, more [cloud condensation nuclei](../../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md) can produce more, smaller droplets. The increased total extinction area raises [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) and reflected sunlight: the [Twomey effect](../../../../../twomey-effect.md) gives **negative shortwave [radiative forcing](../../../../../radiative-forcing.md)**. It is an indirect [aerosol](../../../../../aerosol.md) effect mediated by [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) microphysics, rather than direct [aerosol](../../../../../aerosol.md) scattering.[Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences 34 (1977), pp. 1149–1152, fixed-water-content droplet-number and reflectance effect](../../../../../the-influence-of-pollution-on-the-shortwave-albedo-of-clouds-by-s-twomey.md)

Smaller droplets can also delay [collision-coalescence](../../../../../collision-coalescence.md) and drizzle, potentially increasing liquid-water retention, [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) lifetime or coverage; that adjustment would usually strengthen cooling for low reflective [clouds](../../../../../cloud.md). It is not universal: evaporation and mixing with dry air can reduce [water](../../../../../water.md) content or [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) cover, and dynamical changes can oppose the simple lifetime argument. Absorbing particles can heat an atmospheric layer, sometimes evaporating [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) and producing a positive semi-direct forcing, but their vertical position can instead stabilize a [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) layer and reverse the response. [Aerosols](../../../../../aerosol.md) can additionally alter ice nucleation and mixed-phase precipitation, with no single forcing sign independent of [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) type. The net response combines shortwave reflection and longwave thermal effects, and depends on [cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) height, [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) and adjustments. Thus **the fixed-water Twomey sign is robust within its assumptions; adjustment and ice-cloud signs require context**.[IPCC AR6 Working Group I, Chapter 7, [aerosol](../../../../../aerosol.md)–[cloud](../../../../../cloud.md) forcing and cloud-adjustment assessment](../../../../../the-earth-s-energy-budget-climate-feedbacks-and-climate-sensitivity-by-piers-forster-and-others.md)
