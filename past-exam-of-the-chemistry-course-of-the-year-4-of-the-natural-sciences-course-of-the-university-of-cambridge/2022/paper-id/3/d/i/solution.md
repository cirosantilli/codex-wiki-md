<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Hold liquid-water content and [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) thickness fixed, as required for a partial sensitivity to droplet number. Then $\tau=K N_d^{1/3}$, so $d\tau/dN_d=\tau/(3N_d)$. Differentiating the [albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) relation gives $dA/d\tau=7/(\tau+7)^2$, hence

$$
\frac{dA}{dN_d}=\frac{7\tau}{3N_d(\tau+7)^2}.
$$

Since $A=\tau/(\tau+7)$ and $1-A=7/(\tau+7)$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{dA}{dN_d}=\frac{A(1-A)}{3N_d}.}
$$

The factor $A(1-A)$ peaks at $A=1/2$, so the [albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) response to a fixed fractional droplet-number change is greatest at intermediate [albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) and weaker near either saturation limit.
