<h1 id="3/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Activation as a [cloud condensation nucleus](../../../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md) depends primarily on dry particle size, soluble composition and hygroscopicity, together with wetting and surface-tension effects. In [Köhler theory](../../../../../../kohler-theory.md), droplet curvature raises the equilibrium [vapor pressure](../../../../../../vapor-pressure.md), while dissolved solute lowers [water](../../../../../../water.md) activity. Each particle therefore has a [critical supersaturation](../../../../../../critical-supersaturation.md); larger or more hygroscopic particles generally activate at a lower value. A size distribution cannot be replaced by an undifferentiated total [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) count.[Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics 7 (2007), pp. 1961–1971, dry diameter, hygroscopicity parameter and [critical supersaturation](../../../../../../critical-supersaturation.md)](../../../../../../a-single-parameter-representation-of-hygroscopic-growth-and-cloud-condensation-nucleus-activity-by-m-d-petters-and-s-m-kreidenweis.md)

Actual droplet number also depends on the supersaturation attained in the [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) updraught. Adding particles increases competition for condensing [water vapor](../../../../../../water-vapor.md) and can lower the peak supersaturation, leaving some particles unactivated. Updraught speed, [entrainment](../../../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) and the [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) size distribution therefore make the number of activated droplets a nonlinear function of the available nuclei. **Suitable nuclei need sufficiently low activation thresholds, and additional [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) does not guarantee one additional droplet per particle.**
