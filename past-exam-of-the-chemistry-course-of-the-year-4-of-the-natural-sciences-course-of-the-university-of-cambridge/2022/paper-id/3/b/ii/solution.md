<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Excited $\mathrm O({}^1D)$ has two competing fates. Collisional deactivation returns ground-state [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), which reforms [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) rapidly; reaction with [water](../../../../../../water.md) instead gives net [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) loss and two OH radicals. The [chemical branching fraction](../../../../../../chemical-branching-fraction.md) for the latter is

$$
f_w=\frac{k_3[\mathrm{H_2O}]}{k_2[\mathrm M]+k_3[\mathrm{H_2O}]}
=\frac{3.30\times10^7}{6.38\times10^8+3.30\times10^7}
=0.0491803.
$$

Thus only about 4.92% of these [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) photolyses directly lead to the specified net chemical loss; treating every [photodissociation](../../../../../../photodissociation.md) as permanent removal would overestimate the sink. The corresponding first-order rate is $J_1f_w=9.8361\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Converting the given hydroperoxyl [mixing ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md) to a [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) gives $[\mathrm{HO_2}]=20\times10^{-12}[\mathrm M]=4.4\times10^8\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, so $k_4[\mathrm{HO_2}]=9.6800\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

For a well-mixed [marine atmospheric boundary layer](../../../../../../marine-atmospheric-boundary-layer.md) of depth $H=2\times10^5\ \mathrm{cm}$, the downward surface flux is $v_{\rm dep}[\mathrm O_3]$. Dividing by the layer [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) per unit area gives a first-order [dry deposition](../../../../../../dry-deposition.md) rate $v_{\rm dep}/H=7.5\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The air density cancels on expressing the entire balance in [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) [mixing ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md) $\chi$:

$$
\frac{d\chi}{dt}=P_\chi-\left(J_1f_w+k_4[\mathrm{HO_2}]+\frac{v_{\rm dep}}H\right)\chi.
$$

At [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state-chemistry.md), with $P_\chi=0.25/3600\ \mathrm{ppb\,s^{-1}}$,

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\mathrm O_3}=\frac{0.25/3600}{2.70161\times10^{-6}}
=25.7\ \mathrm{ppb}.}
$$

The effective removal time is about 4.28 days. This calculation assumes a uniform layer of the diagnosed depth, fixed chemical conditions and a local time-independent balance.
