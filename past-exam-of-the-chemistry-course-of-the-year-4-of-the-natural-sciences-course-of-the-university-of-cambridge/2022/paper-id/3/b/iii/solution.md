↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The observed value is about 4 ppb higher than the local chemical–[dry deposition](../../../../../../dry-deposition.md) balance predicts. [Entrainment](../../../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) from the ozone-richer [free troposphere](../../../../../../free-troposphere.md) adds a term $(w_e/H)(\chi_{\rm FT}-\chi_{\rm BL})$; horizontal [Advection](../../../../../../advection.md) can likewise import [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) or longer-lived precursors from other regions. Time dependence, including the day–night cycle of [photodissociation](../../../../../../photodissociation.md) and changes in mixing depth, is also omitted. The approximately four-day model adjustment time means that air arriving at the measurement location need not have attained the assumed local [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state-chemistry.md). These transport and history terms can raise [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) without changing the given local chemical constants.
