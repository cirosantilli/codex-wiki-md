↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The profile shows a low-ozone mixed layer up to approximately **2 km**, with roughly **30 ppb [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md)**, and an overlying free-tropospheric value of roughly **50 ppb**. The rise occurs through a transition layer extending to about 2.8 km, so these are approximate regime readings, not exact values for every altitude. The horizontal axis is already a [mixing ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md); it does not provide a [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) without additional air-density information.
