↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For the chemical sink, $\tau_{\rm CH_4}\sim1/(k[\mathrm{OH}])$. Warming increases the [methane](../../../../../../methane.md)–[hydroxyl radical](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) [rate constant](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) and, with increased [water vapor](../../../../../../water-vapor.md), often increases [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) production, tending to shorten the chemical lifetime. Changes in [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) [photodissociation](../../../../../../photodissociation.md), [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) recycling, [lightning](../../../../../../lightning.md) and competing [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md)/[VOC](../../../../../../volatile-organic-compound.md)/[methane](../../../../../../methane.md) consumption can reinforce or oppose that trend. Increasing [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) itself consumes [hydroxyl radicals](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) and lengthens its [methane perturbation lifetime](../../../../../../methane-perturbation-lifetime.md). The physical/chemical warming tendency should therefore be separated from scenarios with changed emissions; **shorter chemical lifetime at fixed precursor conditions is a tendency, not an unconditional prediction for the future atmosphere**.[IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 6 (2021), §§6.3.1 and 6.4.5: methane chemical versus [methane perturbation lifetime](../../../../../../methane-perturbation-lifetime.md) and climate–chemistry feedbacks](../../../../../../short-lived-climate-forcers-by-sophie-szopa-and-others.md)
