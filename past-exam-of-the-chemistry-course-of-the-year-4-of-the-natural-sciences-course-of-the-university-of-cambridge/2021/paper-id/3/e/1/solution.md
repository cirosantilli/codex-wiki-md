<h1 id="3/e/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Increasing [greenhouse gases](../../../../../../greenhouse-gas.md) generally strengthen the wave-driven [Brewer-Dobson circulation](../../../../../../brewer-dobson-circulation.md): tropical stratospheric upwelling, poleward transport and extratropical descent intensify in model projections. This can shorten transit times, reduce tropical lower-stratospheric [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) by importing ozone-poor air, and increase [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) supply to extratropical regions or the [troposphere](../../../../../../troposphere.md). Changes in wave propagation and momentum deposition drive the residual circulation; it is not simply a direct thermal overturning inferred from local [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone. Regional branches and variability need not all accelerate equally.[Ozone, climate, and circulation: greenhouse-gas-driven acceleration of global stratospheric transport](../../../../../../scientific-assessment-of-ozone-depletion-2022-executive-summary.md)
