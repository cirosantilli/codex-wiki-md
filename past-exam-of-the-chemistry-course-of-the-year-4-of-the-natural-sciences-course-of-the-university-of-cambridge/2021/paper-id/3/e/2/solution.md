↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

At fixed precursor emissions, more tropospheric [water vapor](../../../../../../water-vapor.md) in a warmer climate promotes the ozone-photolysis/O(${}^1D$)-water loss route, especially in clean tropical lower air. Warmer conditions also accelerate some precursor chemistry; [lightning](../../../../../../lightning.md) [nitrogen oxides](../../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md), stagnation and stronger stratosphere-to-troposphere [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) transport can increase [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) elsewhere. Thus near-surface regional [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) and the integrated tropospheric burden can respond differently. **There is no universal decrease of the global burden from the [humidity](../../../../../../humidity.md) effect alone**; competing chemistry and transport must be evaluated consistently.
