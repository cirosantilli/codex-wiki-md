<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [albedo-to-forcing conversion](../../../../../../albedo-to-forcing-conversion.md), Earth intercepts sunlight over a disk but distributes the global mean over its full sphere, giving incident mean flux $S_0/4$. The absorbed shortwave flux is $F_{\rm abs}=(1-A)S_0/4$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta F_{\rm abs}=-\frac{1370}{4}(4\times10^{-8})
=-1.37\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}.}
$$

The cooling magnitude is $1.37\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ for the assumed one-year effect of one [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) unit of injection. [Albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) does not change the sunlight incident at the top of the atmosphere; it changes the absorbed/net incoming flux. The minus sign therefore accompanies the [radiative cooling](../../../../../../radiative-cooling.md).
