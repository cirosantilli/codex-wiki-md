<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Injected [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) is oxidized, principally through [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) chemistry, to [sulfuric acid](../../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), which nucleates or condenses into aqueous [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) particles. A suitably sized, weakly absorbing particle population scatters some incoming sunlight back to space, increasing planetary [albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) and decreasing absorbed solar energy. The [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) are precursors, not the persistent reflective population.

The [albedo](../../../../../../albedo.md) lifetime reflects [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) formation, growth/[aerosol coagulation](../../../../../../aerosol-coagulation.md), [sedimentation](../../../../../../sedimentation.md), stratospheric transport and eventual return to efficiently scavenged tropospheric air. The [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md) has little [atmospheric precipitation](../../../../../../atmospheric-precipitation.md), so the effect can outlast analogous tropospheric [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md). The assumed year is an effective persistence scale, not a universal lifetime of [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) or an exact clock for every particle.
