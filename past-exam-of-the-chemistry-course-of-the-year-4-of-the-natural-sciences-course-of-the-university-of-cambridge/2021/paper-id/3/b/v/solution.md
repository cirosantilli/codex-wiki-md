The logarithmic forcing relation implies decreasing incremental forcing per additional ppm, explaining the two annual-increment results. It does not imply that a higher total [carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) burden requires less compensation relative to a fixed original climate. The chosen reference and whether an end-of-year, mean-year or steady maintained [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) burden is intended must remain explicit.

The numerical [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) estimates use an unusually simple, stipulated constant albedo-per-mass calibration. Real particle size and lifetime change with loading, [aerosol coagulation](../../../../../../aerosol-coagulation.md), injection altitude/latitude and circulation. [Sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) absorbs radiation as well as scattering it and changes stratospheric chemistry. A global-mean shortwave/longwave [radiative forcing](../../../../../../radiative-forcing.md) balance also need not reproduce regional [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), rainfall or transient ocean heat uptake; it does not undo CO2-driven acidification. Chemistry–climate simulations explicitly resolve some of these competing aerosol-size, [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) effects.[Environmental Research Letters 4 (2009), 045108, Abstract: coupled aerosol-size and chemistry–climate simulations](../../../../../../the-impact-of-geoengineering-aerosols-on-stratospheric-temperature-and-ozone-by-p-heckendorn-and-others.md)
