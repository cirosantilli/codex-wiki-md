<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For independent first-order sinks acting on the same well-mixed burden, loss frequencies add:

$$
k_{\rm tot}=\frac14+\frac14+\frac18=\frac58\ \mathrm{day^{-1}},
\qquad\boxed{\tau_{\rm SO_2}=1.60\ \mathrm{days}.}
$$

The fraction converted to [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) before removal by the competing direct-deposition sink is

$$
\boxed{f_{\rm oxidation}=\frac{1/4+1/8}{5/8}=0.60.}
$$

The individual event fractions are $0.40$ for direct [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) deposition, $0.40$ for [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) and $0.20$ for [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md). These are branching fractions for the [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) fate; [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) formed by [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) can subsequently be deposited as well. The argument assumes the supplied channel lifetimes are compatible effective first-order rates, not residence times of isolated air masses that never exchange.
