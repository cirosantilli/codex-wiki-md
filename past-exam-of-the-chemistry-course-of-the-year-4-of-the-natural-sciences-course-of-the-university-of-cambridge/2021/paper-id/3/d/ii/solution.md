First, weakly absorbing [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) scatters shortwave [electromagnetic radiation](../../../../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) and tends to cool the surface/planetary energy budget, with magnitude and sign influenced by [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) background and particle optics. Second, [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) can supply [cloud condensation nuclei](../../../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md): at fixed [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) [water](../../../../../../water.md), more activated droplets give smaller droplets and greater [cloud albedo](../../../../../../cloud-albedo.md) through the [Twomey effect](../../../../../../twomey-effect.md); [atmospheric precipitation](../../../../../../atmospheric-precipitation.md) and water-content adjustments can modify that idealized cooling. Third, [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) particles change atmospheric composition through uptake, aqueous chemistry and heterogeneous surface reactions, with their acidity and available [reactants](../../../../../../reactant.md) controlling what is converted. Particle surfaces alone do not guarantee polar-style [stratospheric chlorine activation](../../../../../../stratospheric-chlorine-activation.md) in an ordinary tropospheric [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md).[1977, fixed-water-content relation between droplet number and shortwave cloud albedo](../../../../../../the-influence-of-pollution-on-the-shortwave-albedo-of-clouds-by-s-twomey.md)
