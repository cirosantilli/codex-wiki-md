[Carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) is relatively well mixed, its abundance is measured accurately, and its spectroscopy and large-scale radiative effect are well constrained. [Aerosols](../../../../../../aerosol.md) are short lived and spatially heterogeneous; uncertain emissions, removal, size, composition, [water](../../../../../../water.md) uptake and height change both scattering and absorption. Their [aerosol-cloud interaction](../../../../../../aerosol-cloud-interaction.md) effects and subsequent [cloud](../../../../../../cloud.md) adjustments add uncertainty, and the unobserved preindustrial [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) baseline complicates anthropogenic attribution. Thus total [aerosol](../../../../../../aerosol.md) forcing combines poorly constrained physical pathways and a less certain counterfactual, rather than simply an uncertain molecular absorption coefficient.[IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 7 (2021), §§7.3.2–7.3.3: greenhouse-gas and aerosol effective radiative forcing](../../../../../../the-earth%E2%80%99s-energy-budget-climate-feedbacks-and-climate-sensitivity-by-piers-forster-and-others.md)
