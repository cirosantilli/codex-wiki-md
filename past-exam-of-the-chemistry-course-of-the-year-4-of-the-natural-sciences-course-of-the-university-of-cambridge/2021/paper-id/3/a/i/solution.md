<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Absorption depends on an allowed transition with a nonzero [transition dipole moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-transition-moment.md) and appreciable line strength at [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) carrying terrestrial [thermal radiation](../../../../../../thermal-radiation.md). [Molecular vibration](../../../../../../molecular-vibration.md) and [molecular rotation](../../../../../../molecular-rotation.md), level populations and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md)/temperature-dependent line shapes determine the spectrum. A band in an [atmospheric window](../../../../../../atmospheric-window.md) can be more effective incrementally than one already strongly absorbed by [water](../../../../../../water.md) or [carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md). Symmetry can make a vibration [infrared](../../../../../../infrared.md) inactive even when the [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) has many [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md). [Atmospheric lifetime](../../../../../../atmospheric-lifetime.md) affects the persistence of a released gas, **not the intrinsic absorption strength of one [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md)**.
