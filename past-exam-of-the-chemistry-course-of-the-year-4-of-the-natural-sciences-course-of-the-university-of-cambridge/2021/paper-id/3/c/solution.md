<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the transmitted-flux form of the [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../../beer-lambert-law.md). The [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) falls from $0.25$ to $0.225$, so

$$
\frac{F'}{F}=\frac{e^{-0.225}}{e^{-0.25}}=e^{0.025},
\qquad\boxed{100\left(\frac{F'}F-1\right)=2.532\%\ \mathrm{increase}.}
$$

A ten-percent loss of [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) therefore does not imply a ten-percent increase of transmitted UV. The response depends on the initial [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md), [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) and path geometry. This calculation uses the stated effective [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) and holds the incident flux and other absorbers/scatterers fixed.
