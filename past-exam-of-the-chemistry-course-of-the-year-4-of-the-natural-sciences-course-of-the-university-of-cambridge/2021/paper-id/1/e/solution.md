<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md), the key is **chemical sequestration**, not an inability of free F to react. [Fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) atoms readily form very stable [hydrogen fluoride](../../../../../hydrogen-fluoride.md) through reaction with hydrogen-containing gases. Breaking [hydrogen fluoride](../../../../../hydrogen-fluoride.md) or regenerating a sustained free-fluorine [radical](../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) population under ordinary stratospheric conditions is difficult, so an efficient analogous catalytic family is not maintained.

For [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md), the traditional approximation is **limited delivery to the [stratosphere](../../../../../stratosphere.md)**. Many [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) source gases are short lived in the [troposphere](../../../../../troposphere.md), and their reactive products are efficiently taken up or removed before slow large-scale ascent. This historically motivated omitting [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) from the major stratospheric [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md)/[bromine](../../../../../bromine.md) budget. It does not establish that [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) atoms are intrinsically ineffective catalysts, or that stratospheric [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) is exactly zero.

Indeed, the blanket omission needs qualification even for this exam's date. Aircraft observations published in 2020 detected both [IO](../../../../../iodine-monoxide.md) and particulate [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) in stratospheric air and inferred continuing multiphase recycling and [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) loss. Gas/particle partitioning and measurement sensitivity must be considered together; a small gas-phase [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) alone can miss an active reservoir. Thus the traditional supply argument is the intended contrast with [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md)'s stable-HF sink, but **zero stratospheric [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) chemistry is not supported by those observations**.[PNAS 117 (2020), pp. 1860–1866, Abstract and Significance: aircraft IO/particulate-iodine measurements and nonzero stratospheric injection](../../../../../quantitative-detection-of-iodine-in-the-stratosphere-by-theodore-k-koenig-and-others.md)
