<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Outside the cold polar activation regime, cooling generally slows temperature-activated gas-phase [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) destruction and can increase [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) if other conditions are held fixed. This is a weaker, less universal conclusion in the lower [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md) because transport and imported [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) are important. Enhanced sulfate-surface activation or unusually cold cloud-forming air can instead activate [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) and reverse the purely gas-phase tendency. **Distinguish a local chemical cooling effect from the dynamical response of the midlatitude [ozone-column photolysis shielding](../../../../../../ozone-column-photolysis-shielding.md).**
