↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the upper [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md), cooling slows temperature-dependent odd-oxygen destruction, including $\mathrm O+\mathrm O_3\to2\mathrm O_2$ and important catalytic reactions. With photochemical production otherwise fixed, the local steady [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) abundance tends to rise. This chemical response is one reason increasing [carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) can enhance upper-stratospheric [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) while cooling that region. It is compatible with enhanced chemical depletion in cold activated polar air; the controlling regimes differ.[Future ozone changes and Ozone, climate, and circulation: upper-stratospheric cooling, polar cloud formation and circulation are distinct drivers](../../../../../../scientific-assessment-of-ozone-depletion-2022-executive-summary.md)
