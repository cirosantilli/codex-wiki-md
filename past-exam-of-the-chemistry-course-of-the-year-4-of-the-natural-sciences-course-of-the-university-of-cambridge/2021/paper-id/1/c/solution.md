<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under the proposed steady balance,

$$
k_9[\mathrm{Cl}][\mathrm{CH_4}]=k_{10}[\mathrm{OH}][\mathrm{HCl}],
\qquad
[\mathrm{OH}]=\frac{k_9[\mathrm{Cl}]x_{\mathrm{CH_4}}}{k_{10}x_{\mathrm{HCl}}}.
$$

The air [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) cancels from the ratio. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\mathrm{OH}]=\frac{(10^{-14})(1.2\times10^5)(0.6\times10^{-6})}
{(4.5\times10^{-13})(1.1\times10^{-9})}
=1.45\times10^6\ \mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}}.}
$$

The [reservoir relaxation test for a chemical steady state](../../../../../reservoir-relaxation-test-for-a-chemical-steady-state.md) gives a [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md) relaxation time of $1/(k_{10}[\mathrm{OH}])=1.53\times10^6\ \mathrm s\simeq17.7$ days. [Hydrogen chloride](../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md) is a reservoir rather than a rapidly equilibrating [radical](../../../../../radical-chemistry.md). A steady state is plausible only if [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) abundance, [methane](../../../../../methane.md), [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) and transport vary slowly compared with several weeks. **It is doubtful during rapid vortex breakdown and reservoir reformation**, precisely when production and mixing are changing. The numerical [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) value is conditional on the steady-state hypothesis, not an independent measurement of [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md).
