<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The two initial reservoirs each contain one [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) atom, so complete activation supplies a total [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [mixing ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md) of $2.2\times10^{-9}$. Under the specified approximation that active [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) in sunlight is predominantly [chlorine monoxide](../../../../../../chlorine-monoxide.md),

$$
[\mathrm{ClO}]=(2.2\times10^{-9})(1.2\times10^{18})
=2.64\times10^9\ \mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}}.
$$

The daytime loss is consequently

$$
L_d=1.4(9\times10^{-32})(1.2\times10^{18})(2.64\times10^9)^2
=\boxed{1.054\times10^6\ \mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

For twelve sunlit hours per day, the daily [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) decrement is

$$
\Delta x_{\mathrm O_3}=\frac{L_d(43200)}{1.2\times10^{18}}
=\boxed{3.794\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{day^{-1}}
=37.94\ \mathrm{ppb\,day^{-1}}=0.03794\ \mathrm{ppm\,day^{-1}}.}
$$

The full-day mean [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) loss is $5.269\times10^5\ \mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}\,s^{-1}}$. Maintaining that rate would consume the initial [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) in about $79.1$ days. This is a fixed-active-chlorine estimate: reservoir reformation, transport and [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) partitioning change during a real depletion event, and the approximation fails when insufficient [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) remains to recycle [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) rapidly.
