↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) favours association into the dimer and, especially, suppresses its thermally activated dissociation back to [chlorine monoxide](../../../../../../chlorine-monoxide.md). More of the dimer can therefore follow [photodissociation](../../../../../../photodissociation.md) instead of the chemically unproductive thermal return. The supplied branching fraction is a local condition, not a temperature-independent universal yield.

Cold polar air also permits [polar stratospheric clouds](../../../../../../polar-stratospheric-cloud.md) and efficient heterogeneous conversion of [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) reservoirs into photolabile forms. The [stratospheric polar vortex](../../../../../../stratospheric-polar-vortex.md) isolates and cools that air. Darkness permits activation and storage, but **rapid catalytic [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) loss requires the return of sunlight**. These chemical and meteorological conditions explain the springtime maximum; low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone does not supply the [photodissociation](../../../../../../photodissociation.md) photons.
