[Methane](../../../../../../methane.md) is an infrared-active [greenhouse gas](../../../../../../greenhouse-gas.md) with absorption in the terrestrial thermal spectrum. Its direct forcing is supplemented by chemically induced changes in [tropospheric ozone](../../../../../../tropospheric-ozone.md) and stratospheric [water vapor](../../../../../../water-vapor.md). Its finite [atmospheric lifetime](../../../../../../atmospheric-lifetime.md) makes sustained emissions and emission pulses different experiments: stopping emissions permits the [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) burden to decline much faster than the long tail of a carbon-dioxide perturbation. Quantitative comparisons require a specified emission [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), time horizon and treatment of indirect effects, as formalized by [global warming potential](../../../../../../global-warming-potential.md).
