[Methane](../../../../../../methane.md) consumes [hydroxyl radicals](../../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) during its initial [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) and therefore influences atmospheric oxidative capacity and the removal of other trace gases. Subsequent [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) generates [peroxy radicals](../../../../../../peroxy-radical.md). In the presence of sufficient [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md), these convert [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md) to [nitrogen dioxide](../../../../../../nitrogen-dioxide.md) without consuming [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md), allowing net [photochemical ozone production](../../../../../../photochemical-ozone-production.md). At low [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md), peroxide formation and ozone-destroying [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) reactions compete. [Methane](../../../../../../methane.md) [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) ultimately produces [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) and [carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md); the sign and rate of the associated [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) response depend on the [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md)/[nitrogen oxides](../../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) regime, not [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) alone.
