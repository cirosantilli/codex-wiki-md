↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Methane](../../../../../../methane.md) reaching the [stratosphere](../../../../../../stratosphere.md) is oxidized and is an important source of stratospheric [water vapor](../../../../../../water-vapor.md). That [water](../../../../../../water.md) can feed [HOx radical family](../../../../../../hox-radical-family.md) chemistry and alter [radiative cooling](../../../../../../radiative-cooling.md) and polar-cloud conditions. [Methane](../../../../../../methane.md) also converts [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) atoms into [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md), opposing some chlorine-driven [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) loss. These effects compete: increasing [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) does not have one universal [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) sign at every altitude and latitude.
