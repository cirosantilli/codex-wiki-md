<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Lower [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) and [VOC](../../../../../volatile-organic-compound.md) emissions generally reduce the supply of [peroxy radicals](../../../../../peroxy-radical.md) from their [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) and therefore reduce [photochemical ozone production](../../../../../photochemical-ozone-production.md) where [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md) is available. Lower [nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) abundance suppresses the [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md)-to-[nitrogen dioxide](../../../../../nitrogen-dioxide.md) pathway in [NOx-limited ozone production](../../../../../nox-limited-ozone-production.md) regions. These are reasons for a regional/background [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) decrease when fossil-fuel precursor emissions decline.

The response can have the opposite local sign in a [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md)-rich urban core: reducing [nitrogen monoxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md) weakens [ozone titration](../../../../../ozone-titration.md), and reducing [nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) in a [VOC-limited ozone production](../../../../../voc-limited-ozone-production.md) regime can initially lessen [radical termination](../../../../../radical-termination.md) and raise [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md). Less [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) can also leave more [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) available to remove [methane](../../../../../methane.md), partly opposing the [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) demand from leaked hydrogen. Changes in precursor transport, chemistry and sunlight matter. **Reduced emissions usually reduce net regional [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) production, but do not guarantee a lower [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) at every monitoring site.**
