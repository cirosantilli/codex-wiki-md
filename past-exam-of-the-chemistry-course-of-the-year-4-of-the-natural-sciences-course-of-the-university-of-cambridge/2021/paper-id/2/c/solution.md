↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The lost [ozone-column photolysis shielding](../../../../../ozone-column-photolysis-shielding.md) is $\Delta N=20(2.69\times10^{16})=5.38\times10^{17}\ \mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-2}}$, so its optical-depth decrement is $\sigma\Delta N=0.0538$. Under the stipulated effective-cross-section Beer-Lambert model, the transmitted [photodissociation](../../../../../photodissociation.md) flux increases by $\exp(0.0538)$.

The short-lived [oxygen singlet D atom](../../../../../oxygen-singlet-d-atom.md) satisfies $[\mathrm O({}^1D)]\simeq J_{\mathrm O_3}[\mathrm O_3]/(k_q[M])$ when quenching dominates. At fixed local [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md), [water](../../../../../water.md) and air density, [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) production is proportional to $J_{\mathrm O_3}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{P'_{\rm OH}-P_{\rm OH}}{P_{\rm OH}}
=e^{0.0538}-1=0.0553,\quad\text{a }5.53\%\text{ increase}.}
$$

A linear optical-depth approximation would give $5.38\%$. A realistic calculation integrates the wavelength-dependent [absorption cross section](../../../../../absorption-cross-section.md), [quantum yield](../../../../../quantum-yield.md) and [actinic flux](../../../../../actinic-flux.md); [clouds](../../../../../cloud.md) and multiple scattering modify the simple transmitted-beam model.

[Ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) change can also alter the radiative [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) structure, [water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md) and circulation, thereby changing the [water](../../../../../water.md) reaction channel and the supply of [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md)/precursors to the [troposphere](../../../../../troposphere.md). If the held-fixed [tropospheric ozone](../../../../../tropospheric-ozone.md) assumption is relaxed, altered [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) itself changes the photochemical source of [oxygen singlet D atom](../../../../../oxygen-singlet-d-atom.md). These are additional feedbacks, distinct from the isolated UV-transmission calculation above.
