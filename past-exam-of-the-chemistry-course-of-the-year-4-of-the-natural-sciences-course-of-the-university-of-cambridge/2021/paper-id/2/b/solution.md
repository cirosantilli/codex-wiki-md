<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [humidity](../../../../../humidity.md) assumption is necessary: warming does not by itself specify how much [water](../../../../../water.md) is available. At fixed [relative humidity](../../../../../relative-humidity.md), the [Clausius-Clapeyron equation](../../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) gives the first-order [vapor pressure](../../../../../vapor-pressure.md) response

$$
\Delta\ln e_s\simeq\frac{45050}{8.314(285)^2}(4)=0.2668.
$$

If [water](../../../../../water.md) abundance is approximated as proportional to $e_s$, the usual first-order estimate is **a $26.7\%$ increase in [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) production**. Integrating the constant-latent-heat expression instead of linearizing gives $e_s(289)/e_s(285)=\exp[(45050/8.314)(1/285-1/289)]=1.3010$.

There is a small but explicit [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) correction when the square brackets are literal [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md) per volume. Since $[\mathrm H_2\mathrm O]=\phi e_s/(k_BT)$ and $P_{\rm OH}=2k[\mathrm O({}^1D)][\mathrm H_2\mathrm O]$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta P_{\rm OH}}{P_{\rm OH}}
\simeq\left(\frac{\Delta H_{\rm vap}}{RT^2}-\frac1T\right)\Delta T
=0.2528\quad\text{at fixed relative humidity}.}
$$

The corresponding finite-change [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) result is $(285/289)(1.3010)-1=0.2830$. The $25.3\%$ linear [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) estimate and the $26.7\%$ [vapor pressure](../../../../../vapor-pressure.md) approximation express different stated approximations; neither follows without a [humidity](../../../../../humidity.md) condition.

Extra [water](../../../../../water.md) increases the fraction of [oxygen singlet D atoms](../../../../../oxygen-singlet-d-atom.md) reacting to form [hydroxyl radicals](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) rather than being collisionally quenched and recycled into [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md). In clean, low-[nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) air this tends to increase [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) loss. In [nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md)-rich air, increased [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) can accelerate precursor [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) and [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) production, while [radical termination](../../../../../radical-termination.md) also changes. **Greater [hydroxyl radical](../../../../../hydroxyl-radical.md) production therefore does not imply a universally positive or negative [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) response.**
