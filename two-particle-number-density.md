# Two-particle number density

↑ **Parent:** [Two-particle distribution function](two-particle-distribution-function.md)

The spatial marginal $n_2=\int f_2\,d^3v\,d^3v_\star$ of a number-normalized [two-particle distribution function](two-particle-distribution-function.md). It counts ordered distinct pairs, so its integral over both positions is $N(N-1)$. Exchange symmetry gives $n_2(\mathbf x,\mathbf x_\star)=n_2(\mathbf x_\star,\mathbf x)$. In a homogeneous isotropic fluid it is $n^2g(|\mathbf x_\star-\mathbf x|)$, where $g$ is the [radial distribution function](radial-distribution-function.md).
