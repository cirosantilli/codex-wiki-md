# Finitary abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

A cocomplete [abelian category](abelian-category.md) is finitary in the directed-union sense if every [cocone under a diagram](cocone-under-a-diagram.md) indexed by a nonempty directed [poset](partially-ordered-set.md), with all its legs [monomorphisms](monomorphism.md), induces a monomorphism from the colimit to the cocone vertex. The [category of abelian groups](category-of-abelian-groups.md) has this property: two colimit representatives mapped to the same vertex become equal at a common upper index because that cocone leg is injective. This use of finitary concerns directed unions, rather than the preservation-of-filtered-colimits meaning used for [finitary monads](finitary-monad.md).
