# Quadratic stationary endpoint with quadratically vanishing amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

The stationary endpoint contributes $ce^{3i\pi/4}\sqrt\pi/(4x^{3/2})$ by [method of steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md) along $t=e^{i\pi/4}r$. Its ordinary $x^{-1/2}$ term vanishes because $a(0)=0$. A nonstationary upper endpoint with $a(b)\neq0$ contributes $a(b)e^{ixb^2}/(2ibx)$ and therefore dominates the stationary contribution. The [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md) must retain both this boundary term and the first nonzero local-amplitude term.
