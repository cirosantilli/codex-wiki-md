# Manhattan distance

↑ **Parent:** [Metric](metric.md)

The [metric](metric.md) induced by the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) gives [taxicab geometry](taxicab-geometry.md). Each difference in [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) contributes its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) independently, so an axis-aligned route with no backtracking realizes the endpoint distance.
