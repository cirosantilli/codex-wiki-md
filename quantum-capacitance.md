# Quantum capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

The electronic contribution to differential [capacitance](capacitance.md) arising from a change of [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) as particles are added. For noninteracting [electrons](electron.md) at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), it is $e^2$ times the [density of states](density-of-states.md) of the whole sample at the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). The [quantum capacitance of a graphene cone](quantum-capacitance-of-a-graphene-cone.md) gives a worked example.
