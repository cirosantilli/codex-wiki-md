# Extremal derivative of a disc map into a symmetric annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md)

Let $L>0$ and consider [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) $f$ from the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) into $e^{-L}<|w|<e^L$, normalized by $f(0)=1$. Their largest possible [derivative](derivative.md) modulus is $4L/\pi$. Lift $f$ to its [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md) $h$ with $h(0)=0$, so $|\operatorname{Re}h|<L$. The function $\tan(\pi h/(4L))$ maps the disc into itself and fixes zero. The [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md) gives $|h'(0)|\le4L/\pi$. Equality holds precisely for

$$
f_\theta(z)=\exp\left(\frac{4L}{\pi}\arctan(e^{i\theta}z)\right),\qquad\theta\in\mathbb R.
$$

Here $\arctan$ is its [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) branch vanishing at zero; $(4L/\pi)\arctan z$ is a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) from the disc onto the vertical strip. Thus the maximal [derivative](derivative.md) modulus is attained, with a circle of distinct extremizers rather than a unique map.
