# Ragone plot

↑ **Parent:** [Specific energy](specific-energy.md)

A logarithmic comparison of [specific power](specific-power.md) against [specific energy](specific-energy.md) for storage or conversion devices. With consistent [mass](mass.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) units, constant-duration lines satisfy $p_s=e_s/t$. The plotted capability depends on operating conditions and [mass](mass.md) convention.
