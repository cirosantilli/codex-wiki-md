# Newton map in optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's method in optimization](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable real [function](function-split.md), the Newton map sends an input $p$ to the next iterate of [Newton's method in optimization](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md). It is defined where the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) $H(p)$ is invertible. Iterating this map seeks a [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) of the [gradient](gradient.md); additional information is needed to determine whether a resulting [stationary point](stationary-point.md) is a [local minimum](local-minimum.md).
