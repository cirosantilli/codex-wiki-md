# Supersymmetric action

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

A supersymmetric action is invariant under [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) up to boundary terms. For ordinary four-dimensional [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md), a full [superspace integration](superspace-integration.md) of a real [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) and a chiral [superspace integration](superspace-integration.md) of a [superpotential](superpotential.md) provide the two-derivative ungauged action.
