# Defective renewal equation

↑ **Parent:** [Renewal equation](renewal-equation.md)

A defective renewal equation is $z(u)=g(u)+\int_{[0,u]}z(u-x)Q(dx)$ where the nonnegative kernel measure has total mass less than one. Its [renewal representation](renewal-representation.md) is a convergent sum of convolutions. [Exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md) can turn the kernel into a probability measure, enabling the [key renewal theorem](key-renewal-theorem.md).
