# Outer expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_expansion)

An [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) describes the solution away from a thin or distant region resolved by an [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md). It is matched to that [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md) in their common [overlap region](overlap-region.md). Its range of validity must be specified: an approximation at each fixed positive $x$ need not have [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) down to an endpoint.

For the [singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md) problem $\epsilon y''+y'=0$, $y(0)=0$, $y(1)=1$, try the [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion-split.md) $y\sim y_0+\epsilon y_1+\epsilon^2y_2+\cdots$ at fixed $x>0$. Substitution gives $y_0'=0$ and $y_n'=-y_{n-1}''$ for $n\geq1$. Expanding the right [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) gives $y_0(1)=1$ and $y_n(1)=0$ for $n\geq1$. Therefore $y_0=1$ and every higher coefficient vanishes. The missing [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) is exponentially small at each fixed positive $x$, invisible to all these powers of $\epsilon$.

The exact solution supplies a quantitative limit. For every fixed $a\in(0,1)$,

$$
\sup_{a\leq x\leq1}|y_\epsilon(x)-1|
=\frac{e^{-a/\epsilon}-e^{-1/\epsilon}}{1-e^{-1/\epsilon}}
\leq\frac{e^{-a/\epsilon}}{1-e^{-1/\epsilon}}\longrightarrow0.
$$

This is [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on $[a,1]$. On $[0,1]$ the same [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of the error is exactly one. The [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md) supplies the endpoint information that this [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) cannot encode.
