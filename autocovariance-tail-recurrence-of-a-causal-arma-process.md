# Autocovariance tail recurrence of a causal ARMA process

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive moving-average model](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md)

For a causal [autoregressive moving-average model](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) driven by [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [white noise](white-noise.md), multiply its recursion by $X_{t-k}$ and take [covariance](covariance.md). When $k>q$, all driving [white noise](white-noise.md) values in its moving-average term occur strictly after $t-k$, so their [covariances](covariance.md) with $X_{t-k}$ vanish. The resulting homogeneous tail recurrence has the same characteristic roots as the [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md) recursion. Distinct roots of modulus below one give exponential [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) decay.
