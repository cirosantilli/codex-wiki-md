# Columns partition for finite-sums systems

↑ **Parent:** [Finite sums theorem](finite-sums-theorem.md)

Introduce a variable $z_I$ for every nonempty $I\subseteq[k]$ and the homogeneous equations $z_I-\sum_{i\in I}z_{\{i\}}=0$ for $|I|\ge2$. In their [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, partition the columns by the displayed blocks. For each $j$, the vector with coordinate $1$ exactly when $j\in I$ lies in the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $A$. Its nonzero coordinates occur in $B_j$ and earlier blocks only; every coordinate in $B_j$ is one. Hence the first block's column sum is zero and every subsequent block sum is in the [linear span](linear-span.md) of earlier columns. The [columns condition](columns-property.md) holds, so [Rado's theorem](rado-s-theorem.md) forces a positive [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) solution, and all nonempty finite sums of its singleton coordinates have the same colour.
