# Unit-integral normalization of a B-spline

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

For strictly increasing [spline knots](spline-knot.md), $M_i(t)=k[t_i,\ldots,t_{i+k}](\cdot-t)_+^{k-1}$ is a nonnegative integral-normalized [B-spline](b-spline.md). Integrating the [truncated power function](truncated-power-function.md) over its [support](support.md) replaces its [spline knot](spline-knot.md) value by $(u-t_i)^k/k$. The order-$k$ [divided difference](divided-difference.md) of this degree-$k$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is its [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) $1/k$. Multiplying by the prefactor $k$ in $M_i$ gives integral one. The corresponding partition-normalized [B-spline](b-spline.md) is $N_i=(t_{i+k}-t_i)M_i/k$.
