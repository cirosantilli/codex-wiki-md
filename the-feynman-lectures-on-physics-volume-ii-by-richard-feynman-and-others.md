Additional cited passages: II.2–7, Second derivatives of vector fields, equations (2.52)–(2.54), the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) and the rectangular-component statement after (2.54); II.2–8, Pitfalls, equation (2.60) and final coordinate-system caution. II.12–4, equations (12.19)–(12.22), neutron flux proportional to a scalar diffusion coefficient times the negative density [gradient](gradient.md), and local conservation. Equation (12.22) retains the coefficient inside the [divergence](divergence.md); reducing it to a constant times the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) requires an additional constancy assumption.
