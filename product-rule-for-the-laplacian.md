# Product rule for the Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian](laplacian.md)

For twice differentiable scalar fields,

$$
\nabla^2(fg)
=f\nabla^2g+2\nabla f\mathbin\cdot\nabla g
+g\nabla^2f.
$$
