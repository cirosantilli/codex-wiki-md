# Convergent regularization of an inverse problem

↑ **Parent:** [Regularization of an inverse problem](regularization-of-an-inverse-problem.md)

A regularization $R_\alpha$ is convergent when its parameter can be chosen from the noise level and data so that the regularized reconstruction converges to the exact [Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator](moore-penrose-inverse-of-an-operator.md) solution as the noise level tends to zero.
