# Energy eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue.md)

An energy eigenstate is a nonzero [quantum state](quantum-state.md) represented by an eigenvector of the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). Its time evolution changes only its overall phase.
