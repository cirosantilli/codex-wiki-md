# Variable-coefficient conservative diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)

With positive diffusion coefficient $a(x)$, this [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) expresses conservation with flux $-a(x)u_x$. For a differentiable coefficient, its spatial operator is $a u_{xx}+a'u_x$, rather than either $a u_x$ or $a u_{xx}$ alone. A [symmetric half-grid diffusion consistency](symmetric-half-grid-diffusion-consistency.md) discretization uses the difference of the two adjacent interface fluxes, preserving that divergence structure. Suitable [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) make the spatial operator dissipative in the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md).
