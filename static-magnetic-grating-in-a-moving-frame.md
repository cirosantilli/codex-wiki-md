# Static magnetic grating in a moving frame

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic field invariants](electromagnetic-field-invariants.md)

Boosting a static spatially periodic [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) produces temporal oscillations together with an [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Its spatial-grating four-wavevector remains spacelike, rather than the null four-wavevector of a vacuum [plane wave](plane-wave.md). For a boost along the grating axis, $|E'|/|B'|=v<c$. Ultrarelativistic motion can make the field approximately wave-like along a prescribed trajectory without making it an exact source-free radiation field.
