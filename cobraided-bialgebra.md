# Cobraided bialgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Bialgebra](bialgebra.md)

A [bialgebra](bialgebra.md) equipped with a [coquasitriangular structure](coquasitriangular-structure.md), namely a convolution-invertible scalar form $r:H\otimes H\to k$ satisfying $r(ab,c)=\sum r(a,c_{(1)})r(b,c_{(2)})$, $r(a,bc)=\sum r(a_{(1)},c)r(a_{(2)},b)$ and $\sum r(a_{(1)},b_{(1)})a_{(2)}b_{(2)}=\sum b_{(1)}a_{(1)}r(a_{(2)},b_{(2)})$, with unit normalization. The induced right-[comodule](comodule.md) [braiding](braiding.md) is $v\otimes w\mapsto\sum w_{(0)}\otimes v_{(0)}r(v_{(1)},w_{(1)})$.
