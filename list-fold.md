# List fold

↑ **Parent:** [Functional programming](functional-programming.md)

The [structural recursion](structural-recursion.md) that replaces an empty [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) by a chosen value and a cons constructor by a combining [function](function-split.md) applied to the head and folded tail. A direct [list fold](list-fold.md) is strict in the list spine at its outermost constructor, even when the combining function does not demand the folded tail. Its algebraic recognition rule is the [fold factorisation criterion](fold-factorisation-criterion.md).
