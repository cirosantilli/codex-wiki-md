<h1 id="karman-howarth-equation">Kármán-Howarth equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md)

The [Kármán-Howarth equation](karman-howarth-equation.md) evolves two-point correlations in homogeneous [isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md). In trace form it is $\partial_tC=r^{-2}\partial_r[r^{-1}\partial_r(r^4u^3K)]+2\nu r^{-2}\partial_r(r^2C\prime)$. Integrating with radial volume weight reduces conservation of the [Saffman integral](saffman-integral.md) to vanishing nonlinear and viscous boundary fluxes.
