# Cauchy-Pompeiu formula

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md)

For a continuously differentiable function on a bounded planar domain, the [Cauchy-Pompeiu formula](cauchy-pompeiu-formula.md) supplements the [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) with an area integral of its [Wirtinger derivative](wirtinger-derivatives.md). Apply the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) to $f(\zeta)d\zeta/(\zeta-z)$ on a punctured domain; the small-circle integral supplies $2\pi i f(z)$. If the boundary term vanishes at infinity, it gives the [Cauchy-Green operator](cauchy-green-operator.md) representation $f(z)=\pi^{-1}\int f_{\bar\zeta}(\zeta)/(z-\zeta)dA$.
