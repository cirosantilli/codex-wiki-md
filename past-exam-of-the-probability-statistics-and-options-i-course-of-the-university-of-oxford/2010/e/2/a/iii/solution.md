↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the natural projection $p:X\to X/R$, the [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) declares $U\subseteq X/R$ open exactly when $p^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$. It is the finest topology on the quotient making $p$ continuous.
