A map $f:X\to Y$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) if the inverse image of every open subset of $Y$ is open in $X$:

$$
V\in\mathcal T_Y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f^{-1}(V)\in\mathcal T_X.
$$
