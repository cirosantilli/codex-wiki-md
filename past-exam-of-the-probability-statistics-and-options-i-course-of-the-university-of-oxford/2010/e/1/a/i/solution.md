A [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) $\mathcal T$ on $X$ contains $\varnothing$ and $X$, is closed under arbitrary unions, and is closed under finite intersections. Its members are the [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md). A subset $F\subseteq X$ is [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) precisely when $X\setminus F$ is open.
