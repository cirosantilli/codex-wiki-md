↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

We prove (ii)$\Rightarrow$(iii). If $B$ is open in $\overline B$, its complement $\overline B\setminus B$ is closed in the subspace $\overline B$. A relatively closed subset of a closed subset of $X$ is closed in $X$: it is the intersection of that closed subset with another closed subset of $X$. Since a [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) is closed, **$\overline B\setminus B$ is closed in $X$**.
