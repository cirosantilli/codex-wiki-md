<h1 id="e/1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

We prove (iii)$\Rightarrow$(i). If $C=\overline B\setminus B$ is closed in $X$, set $U=X\setminus C$ and $F=\overline B$. Then $U$ is open, $F$ is closed, and

$$
\boxed{U\cap F=(X\setminus(\overline B\setminus B))\cap\overline B=B.}
$$

Together the three implications prove the equivalence. These are the standard characterizations of a [locally closed subset](../../../../../../locally-closed-subset.md).
