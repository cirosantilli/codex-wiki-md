<h1 id="a/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In [field characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, the intermediate binomial coefficients in the $p$th power vanish. Hence

$$
(\alpha+1)^p-(\alpha+1)-a=\alpha^p+1-\alpha-1-a=0.
$$

Repeated translation gives all $p$ roots $\alpha+j$, $j\in\mathbb F_p$. They are distinct because the prime [field](../../../../../field.md) embeds in $F$. They exhaust the roots by [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md); also the formal derivative of $X^p-X-a$ is $-1$, so this is a [separable polynomial](../../../../../separable-polynomial.md). If one [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) belongs to $F$, they all do, and

$$
\boxed{X^p-X-a=\prod_{j\in\mathbb F_p}(X-\alpha-j).}
$$

This is the [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md)-translation property of an [Artin–Schreier polynomial](../../../../../artin-schreier-polynomial.md).
