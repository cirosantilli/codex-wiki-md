↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $z_i=e^{-t_i}-c$. Assume the measurement errors are independent and centered, so $Y_i=\alpha+\beta z_i+\epsilon_i$ with independent $\epsilon_i\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$. Treat the observation times as fixed. The [design matrix](../../../../../../design-matrix.md) has rows $(1,z_i)$ and parameter vector $(\alpha,\beta)^{\mathsf T}$. Require at least two distinct times so its columns are independent, equivalently $\sum_i z_i^2>0$. This gives the required Gaussian [linear regression model](../../../../../../linear-regression-model.md).
