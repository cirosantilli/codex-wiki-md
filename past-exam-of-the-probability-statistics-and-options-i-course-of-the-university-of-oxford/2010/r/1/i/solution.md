<h1 id="r/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Count [facial boundary walks](../../../../../facial-boundary-walk.md) with each edge-side counted once. Their total length is $2m$. Every face walk has length at least four: simplicity excludes loops and parallel-edge two-cycles, absence of triangles excludes length three, and a length-two face walk consisting solely of a bridge traversed twice could be the whole boundary only for the excluded connected graph on two [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). Bridges may occur twice in a longer boundary walk and are still correctly counted. Thus $4f\leq2m$.

The [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](../../../../../euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) gives $2=n-m+f\leq n-m+m/2=n-m/2$. Rearranging yields

$$
\boxed{m\leq2n-4.}
$$
