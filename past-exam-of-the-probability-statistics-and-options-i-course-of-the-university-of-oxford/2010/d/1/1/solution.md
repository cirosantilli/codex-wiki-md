<h1 id="d/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use the countable identity

$$
f^{-1}((b,\infty))=\bigcup_{n\geq1}f^{-1}([b+1/n,\infty)).
$$

If $f(x)>b$, some $n$ has $1/n\leq f(x)-b$, proving the nontrivial inclusion. Each preimage on the right is measurable by [statistical hypothesis](../../../../../statistical-hypothesis.md), so closure of a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) under countable unions proves **the strict-superlevel preimage is measurable**.
