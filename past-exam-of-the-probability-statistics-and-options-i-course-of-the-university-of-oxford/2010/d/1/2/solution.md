<h1 id="d/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For each real $a$,

$$
\{x:-g(x)\geq a\}=\{x:g(x)\leq-a\}
=E\setminus\{x:g(x)>-a\}.
$$

The final strict-superlevel set is measurable by part 1 and the given criterion for [measurable functions](../../../../../measurable-function.md). A complement relative to the measurable domain is measurable, so **$-g$ is measurable**.
