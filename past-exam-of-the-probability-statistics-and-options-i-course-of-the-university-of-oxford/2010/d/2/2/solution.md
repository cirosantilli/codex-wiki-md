↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For $x\geq1$, the bound $|\sin u|\leq|u|$ gives $|g(x)|\leq x^{-3/2}$, whose tail integral is finite. Thus **$g\mathbf1_{[1,\infty)}$ is integrable**.

Near one, $h(x)=[(x-1)(x+1)]^{-1/2}$ has an integrable inverse-square-[root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) singularity. At infinity, however, $h(x)\geq1/x$ for $x>1$, and the harmonic tail diverges. Thus **$h\mathbf1_{[1,\infty)}$ is not integrable**. Assigning $h(1)=0$ changes neither conclusion, since one point has [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) zero.
