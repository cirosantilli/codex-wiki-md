<h1 id="k/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Seek nonzero [time-harmonic waves](../../../../time-harmonic-wave.md) of the form $\phi=\operatorname{Re}[F(y)e^{i(kx-\omega t)}]$ and $\eta=\operatorname{Re}[A e^{i(kx-\omega t)}]$, taking $k>0$. [Laplace's equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md) reduces to $F''-k^2F=0$. The impermeable-bed condition $F'(-h)=0$ gives $F(y)=C\cosh(k(y+h))$.

The linearized kinematic and dynamic [linearized free-surface boundary conditions](../../../../linearized-free-surface-boundary-conditions.md) at zero become

$$
Ck\sinh(kh)=-i\omega A,\qquad
-i\omega C\cosh(kh)+gA=0.
$$

Eliminating $A$ and the nonzero amplitude $C$ gives

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=gk\tanh(kh).}
$$

Conversely, if this [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) holds, either amplitude can be chosen nonzero and the other set by the second boundary equation, producing a wave satisfying every condition. The expression also holds for negative $k$ because $k\tanh(kh)$ is even.
