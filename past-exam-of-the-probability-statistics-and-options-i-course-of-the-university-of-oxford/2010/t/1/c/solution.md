<h1 id="t/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Player I can choose each row with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1/m$. Against column $j$ the expected payoff is $m^{-1}\sum_i a_{ij}$. Nonnegativity and a strictly positive entry in each column make every one of these finitely many numbers strictly positive. Their minimum

$$
\delta=\min_j\frac1m\sum_i a_{ij}>0
$$

is therefore a guaranteed payoff against every mixture of columns. By the [minimax theorem](../../../../../minimax-theorem.md), **$v\geq\delta>0$**.
