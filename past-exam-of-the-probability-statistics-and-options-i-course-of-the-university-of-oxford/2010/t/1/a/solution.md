A [mixed strategy](../../../../../mixed-strategy.md) for Player II is a [probability](../../../../../probability.md) vector $q=(q_1,\ldots,q_n)$, with $q_j\geq0$ and $\sum_jq_j=1$. The player chooses pure strategy $j$ with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $q_j$, independently of the opponent's randomization.
