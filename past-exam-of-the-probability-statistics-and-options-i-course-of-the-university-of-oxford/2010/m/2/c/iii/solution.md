<h1 id="m/2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Within the class $\{1,2\}$, the two-step [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) one and $1/9$, with stationary distribution $(1/2,1/2)$. Diagonalizing this symmetric [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), or solving the recursion $p_{n+1}=4/9+p_n/9$ from $p_0=0$, gives

$$
\mathbb P(X_{2n}=2\mid X_0=1)=\frac12\left[1-\left(\frac19\right)^n\right].
$$

Consequently **the limit is $1/2$**. Restriction to even times is essential because the original chain has period two.
