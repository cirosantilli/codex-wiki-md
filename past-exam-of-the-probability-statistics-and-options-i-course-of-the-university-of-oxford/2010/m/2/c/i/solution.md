<h1 id="m/2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The chain alternates between the classes $\{1,2\}$ and $\{3,4\}$. It is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) and has period two, since no odd return is possible and each state has a positive two-step return [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). Matrix multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{P^2=\begin{pmatrix}5/9&4/9&0&0\\4/9&5/9&0&0\\0&0&5/9&4/9\\0&0&4/9&5/9\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The even-time sampled chain stays in its initial class and moves between its two states with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $4/9$ at each sampled step.
