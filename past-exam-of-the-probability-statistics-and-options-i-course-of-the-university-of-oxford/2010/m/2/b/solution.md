<h1 id="m/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Condition on the full sampled history through time $n$. The [Markov property](../../../../../markov-property.md) of $X$ implies that its future after $2n$ depends on that history only through $X_{2n}=Y_n$. Summing over the intermediate state gives

$$
\mathbb P(Y_{n+1}=j\mid Y_0,\ldots,Y_n=i)
=\sum_kP_{ik}P_{kj}=(P^2)_{ij}.
$$

Thus **$Y$ is a Markov chain with transition [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $P^2$**. This is a deterministic-time sampled [Markov chain](../../../../../markov-chain-split.md).
