↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take **$Y=aX_1+b$**. From the definition of a [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md), $\mathbb Ee^{tY}=e^{bt}\mathbb Ee^{atX_1}=e^{bt}M_1(at)$, for $t$ where the expectation is finite.
