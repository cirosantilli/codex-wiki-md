<h1 id="m/1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take **$Y=UX_1$**, where $U$ is uniform on $(0,1)$ and independent of $X_1$. Conditioning on $U$ gives

$$
\mathbb Ee^{tUX_1}=\int_0^1\mathbb E(e^{tuX_1})du=\int_0^1M_1(ut)du.
$$

For $t$ near zero this is finite because the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of $X_1$ is finite on a neighborhood of zero; [Tonelli's theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) justifies the conditioning integral for the nonnegative exponential.
