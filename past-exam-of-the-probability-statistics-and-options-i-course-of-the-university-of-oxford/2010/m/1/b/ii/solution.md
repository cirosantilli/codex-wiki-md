<h1 id="m/1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take **$Y=X_1+X_2$**. Independence makes the joint exponential expectation factor:

$$
\mathbb Ee^{t(X_1+X_2)}=\mathbb E(e^{tX_1}e^{tX_2})=M_1(t)M_2(t).
$$

This is the product rule for the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of an independent sum.
