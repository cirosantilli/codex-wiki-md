<h1 id="m/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Recognize the [mixture distribution](../../../../../mixture-distribution.md)

$$
M_Y(t)=\tfrac12\frac1{1-t}+\tfrac12\frac2{2-t}.
$$

It is a half-and-half mixture of [exponential distributions](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rates one and two. Uniqueness of a [moment-generating function](../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) finite near zero identifies the law. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(Y\leq1)=\tfrac12(1-e^{-1})+\tfrac12(1-e^{-2})
=1-\tfrac12(e^{-1}+e^{-2}).}
$$
