The definition of [convergence in probability](../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\text{for every }\varepsilon>0,\quad
\mathbb P(|Z_n-Z|>\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0\quad(n\to\infty).}
$$

The variables are defined on a common [probability](../../../../../probability.md) space so that these difference events make sense.
