<h1 id="m/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Pointwise, $X\geq a\mathbf1_{\{X\geq a\}}$, since $X$ is nonnegative. Taking expectations and dividing by $a>0$ proves the [Markov inequality](../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\geq a)\leq\mathbb EX/a.}
$$

If the expectation is infinite, the inequality remains true in the extended-real sense.
