<h1 id="r/1/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Partition the vertices by the parity of the number of one-bits. Every edge reverses that parity, giving a two-colouring; at least two colours are needed because an edge exists. Therefore **the vertex [chromatic number](../../../../../../chromatic-number.md) is two**.
