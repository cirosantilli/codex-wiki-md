<h1 id="r/1/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three faces incident to any cube vertex are pairwise adjacent along edges, forcing three colours. Three suffice by giving each pair of opposite faces a common colour, using a different colour for each of the three pairs. Hence **the face [chromatic number](../../../../../../chromatic-number.md) is three**.
