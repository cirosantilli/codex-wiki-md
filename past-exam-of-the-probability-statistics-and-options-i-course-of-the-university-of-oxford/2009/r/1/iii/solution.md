↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A vertex is a bit string of length $k$, and adjacent vertices differ in one coordinate. Therefore the parity of the number of one-bits changes along every edge. The even-parity and odd-parity strings are a [bipartition](../../../../../bipartition-of-a-graph.md), proving **$Q_k$ is bipartite** for every $k\geq1$, in particular for the requested range.
