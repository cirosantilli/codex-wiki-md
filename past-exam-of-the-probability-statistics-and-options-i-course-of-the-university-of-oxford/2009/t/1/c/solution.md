<h1 id="t/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose the unique optimum $x^*$ were $(1-t)u+tv$ for distinct feasible points $u,v$ and $0<t<1$. Feasibility is preserved by convex combinations. Optimality gives $c^Tu\leq c^Tx^*$ and $c^Tv\leq c^Tx^*$, while linearity gives their strictly weighted average equal to $c^Tx^*$. Both inequalities must therefore be equalities. Then $u$ and $v$ are also optimal, contradicting uniqueness. Hence **the unique optimum is an [extreme point](../../../../../extreme-point.md) of the feasible set**.
