<h1 id="e/2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The family contains the whole space and the empty set. A finite intersection of nontrivial members is $(\max_j a_j,\infty)$. A nonempty union of rays is $(\inf_j a_j,\infty)$ when the infimum is finite, and is the whole line when the infimum is $-\infty$. Empty unions and unions involving the whole space are already included. Hence **$\tau$ is a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md)**. This is the [upper-ray topology on the real line](../../../../../../upper-ray-topology-on-the-real-line.md).
