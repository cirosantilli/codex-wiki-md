<h1 id="e/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\{U_i\}$ be an open cover of $f(X)$. By [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) the inverse images $f^{-1}(U_i)$ are open and cover $X$. Compactness provides finitely many of them covering $X$, and the corresponding $U_i$ then cover $f(X)$. Thus **a continuous image of a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) space is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md)**.
