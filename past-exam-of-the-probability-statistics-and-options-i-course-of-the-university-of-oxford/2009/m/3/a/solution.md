The iid [central limit theorem](../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) states that if $Y_1,Y_2,\ldots$ are independent and identically distributed with finite [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\mu$ and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $0<\sigma^2<\infty$, then

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^nY_i-n\mu}{\sigma\sqrt n}\xrightarrow{\mathrm d}N(0,1).}
$$

Equivalently, its cumulative distribution functions converge at every real argument to the standard normal cumulative distribution function.
