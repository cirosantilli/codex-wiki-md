<h1 id="m/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $w\geq0$, $\mathbb P(W\leq w)=\mathbb P(R\leq\sqrt w)=1-e^{-w/2}$. Differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{f_W(w)=\tfrac12e^{-w/2}\mathbf1_{\{w\geq0\}}.}
$$

This is an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $1/2$, with [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) two and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) four.
