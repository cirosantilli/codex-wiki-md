<h1 id="m/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The independent variables $W_i$ have [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) two and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) four by part (b). Their standardization is

$$
\frac{\sum_iW_i-2n}{2\sqrt n}
=\frac1{2\sqrt n}\sum_iW_i-\sqrt n.
$$

The [central limit theorem](../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) therefore proves the claimed convergence to the standard normal law, whose continuous cumulative distribution function is $(2\pi)^{-1/2}\int_{-\infty}^x e^{-u^2/2}du$ at every real $x$.
