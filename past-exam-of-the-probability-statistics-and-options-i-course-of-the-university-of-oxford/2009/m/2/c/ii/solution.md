<h1 id="m/2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The total eventual mass of the closed class $\{4,5\}$ is $\lambda_4+\lambda_5+(1-p)\lambda_1$. The other closed class cannot contribute to state five. Since $|1-a-b|<1$, the two-state chain within the last class converges to its [stationary distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md), with state-five mass $a/(a+b)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P(X_n=5)
=\frac a{a+b}\bigl[\lambda_4+\lambda_5+(1-p)\lambda_1\bigr].}
$$
