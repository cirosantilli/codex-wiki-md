<h1 id="h/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The derivative of the pair's total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $q_1\times F-q_2\times F=(q_1-q_2)\times F=0$, since $F$ is parallel to the separation vector. Therefore **the combined [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) of particles one and two is conserved**, despite the possible exchange between them individually.
