<h1 id="h/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The third particle is free, so

$$
\frac d{dt}(m_3q_3\times\dot q_3)=q_3\times m_3\ddot q_3=0.
$$

Thus **particle three's [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved**.
