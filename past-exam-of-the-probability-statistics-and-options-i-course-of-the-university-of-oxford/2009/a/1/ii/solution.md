<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Replacing $x$ by $-x$ in the irreducible [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) from part (i) gives $x^6-x^3+1$. This substitution is an invertible automorphism of $\mathbb Q[x]$, so it preserves irreducibility. The roots are the primitive eighteenth roots, and the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{18})$. They are all powers of one such [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), so its irreducible degree-six [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta_{18}):\mathbb Q]=6.}
$$

In fact this is the same [field](../../../../../field.md) as in part (i): $\zeta_9=\zeta_{18}^2$ and $\zeta_{18}=-\zeta_9^5$.
