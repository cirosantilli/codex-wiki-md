<h1 id="o/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an interior true parameter and large $n$, standard [asymptotic normality of a maximum likelihood estimator](../../../../../asymptotic-normality-of-a-maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\widehat p\ \dot\sim\ N\left(p,\frac{p^2(1-p)}{nr}\right).}
$$

Estimate its standard error by $s_e=\widehat p\sqrt{(1-\widehat p)/(nr)}$. A two-sided approximate [Wald confidence interval](../../../../../wald-confidence-interval.md) is $(L,U)=(\widehat p-z_{1-\alpha/2}s_e,\widehat p+z_{1-\alpha/2}s_e)$, intersected with the parameter range $(0,1)$. The normal quantile is chosen so a standard normal lies between its positive and negative values with probability $1-\alpha$.
