<h1 id="c/1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Modulo $p$, the term $p^{q-1}$ is zero and [Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) gives $q^{p-1}\equiv1$, since $q\ne p$. Modulo $q$, the roles reverse. Hence the sum minus one is divisible by both distinct primes. Their coprimality gives

$$
\boxed{p^{q-1}+q^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod{pq}.}
$$
