↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The full-[rank](../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) [constraint qualification](../../../../../constraint-qualification.md) gives a multiplier vector $\lambda\in\mathbb R^2$ such that

$$
\boxed{\nabla g(x_0)=Df(x_0)^T\lambda.}
$$

Equivalently the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) of the objective lies in the span of the two constraint gradients. The point also satisfies $f(x_0)=0$.
