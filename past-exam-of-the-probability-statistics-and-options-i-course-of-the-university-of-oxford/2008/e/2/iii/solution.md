↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The unit circle is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md), since it is the continuous image of the interval $[0,2\pi]$ under $\theta\mapsto(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. It is therefore connected, and part (ii) makes $f(C)$ connected. A connected subset of $\mathbb R$ is an interval: omission of a point lying between two of its points would separate it by the two open rays about that point.

Moreover, the circle is compact, so the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) is compact. Hence here the image is **a closed bounded interval, possibly a singleton**.
