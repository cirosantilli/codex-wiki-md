A [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ is one for which $f^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$ whenever $U$ is open in $Y$. This defines continuity using the two [topologies](../../../../../../topology-split.md), without a metric or a coordinate description.
