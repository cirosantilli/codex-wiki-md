↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [metric spaces](../../../../../../metric-space.md), continuity at $x_0$ means that for every $\epsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{dist}_X(x,x_0)<\delta
\ \Longrightarrow\
\operatorname{dist}_Y(f(x),f(x_0))<\epsilon.}
$$

The choice of $\delta$ may depend on both $\epsilon$ and $x_0$.
