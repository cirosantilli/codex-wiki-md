<h1 id="e/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $f$ is continuous, the two sets are inverse images of the open rays $(-\infty,\lambda)$ and $(\lambda,\infty)$, so they are open. Conversely, suppose both families of inverse images are open. For any interval $(a,b)$,

$$
f^{-1}((a,b))=\{f>a\}\cap\{f<b\},
$$

which is open. By part (c)(i) and the [basis of a topology](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) criterion from part (b), all inverse images of open sets are open. Hence **$f$ is continuous**.
