<h1 id="d/2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function is measurable and $0\leq\cos^2(1/x)\leq1$. Since $(0,1)$ has finite [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md), comparison gives $\int_0^1g\leq1$. Therefore **$g$ is integrable on $(0,1)$**, despite oscillating infinitely often near zero.
