<h1 id="d/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For positive integers $n,m$, let $E_{n,m}=\{x:f(x)\geq1/n\}\cap[-m,m]$. This measurable set has finite measure, and $f\geq n^{-1}1_{E_{n,m}}$. Monotonicity of the [Lebesgue integral](../../../../../lebesgue-integral.md) gives

$$
0=\int_{\mathbb R}f\geq\frac1n\,\operatorname{meas}(E_{n,m}),
$$

so every $E_{n,m}$ is a [Lebesgue null set](../../../../../lebesgue-null-set.md). The set where $f>0$ is the countable union of these sets over $n,m$. Part (a) makes it null, hence

$$
\boxed{f=0\quad\text{almost everywhere}.}
$$

The bounded truncation ensures that only integrable indicator functions are used with the stated comparison rule.
