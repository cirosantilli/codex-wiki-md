<h1 id="d/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Lebesgue null set](../../../../../lebesgue-null-set.md) $E\subset\mathbb R$ can, for every $\epsilon>0$, be covered by countably many open intervals whose total length is less than $\epsilon$. Equivalently, its Lebesgue outer measure is zero.

If $E_j$ is null, choose for it such a cover of total length below $\epsilon 2^{-j}$, $j\geq1$. The union of these countably many countable covers covers $\bigcup_jE_j$ and has total length below $\epsilon$. Since $\epsilon$ is arbitrary, **a countable union of null sets is null**.
