<h1 id="k/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For steady [inviscid flow](../../../../../inviscid-flow.md) of constant density without a body-force potential, [Bernoulli's theorem](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac p\rho+\frac12|\mathbf u|^2
=\text{constant along each streamline}.}
$$

The constant can differ between streamlines, because this flow has nonzero [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md). Dotting the steady Euler equation with $\mathbf u$ gives $\mathbf u\cdot\nabla(p/\rho+|\mathbf u|^2/2)=0$, which proves precisely the along-streamline form.
