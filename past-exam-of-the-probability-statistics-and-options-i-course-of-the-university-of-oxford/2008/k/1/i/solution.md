<h1 id="k/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the two-dimensional [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. Here

$$
u=-\sin x\sin y,\qquad v=-\cos x\cos y.
$$

The [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is $u_x+v_y=-\cos x\sin y+\cos x\sin y=0$, so **the flow is incompressible**.
