<h1 id="c/1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every integer has one of the six residues $0,1,2,3,4,5$ modulo $6$. A [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) other than $2$ or $3$ is divisible by neither $2$ nor $3$, eliminating residues $0,2,3,4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{p\equiv1\text{ or }-1\pmod6.}
$$
