A [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) to $M$ at $p$ is a velocity $\gamma'(0)$ of a smooth curve $\gamma:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to M$ with $\gamma(0)=p$. The [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md) is the set of all such velocities:

$$
\boxed{T_pM=\{\gamma'(0):\gamma(0)=p,\ \gamma\text{ a smooth curve in }M\}.}
$$

It is a $k$-dimensional linear subspace of $\mathbb R^n$; for a local parametrization $\phi$, it is the image of the injective [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) $D\phi$ at the corresponding parameter point.
