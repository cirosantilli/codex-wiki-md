<h1 id="o/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $t_{\nu,\beta}$ denote a quantile of the [Student t-distribution](../../../../../student-s-t-distribution.md) with $\nu=n-1$ degrees of freedom. Symmetry gives $\mathbb P(|Y|\leq t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2})=1-\alpha$. Solving for $\mu$ gives the exact central [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md)

$$
\boxed{\left[\overline X-t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}\frac S{\sqrt n},\ 
\overline X+t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}\frac S{\sqrt n}\right].}
$$
