↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $F_{n-1}$ be the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of the [Student t-distribution](../../../../../student-s-t-distribution.md). The two-sided [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md) is $2[1-F_{n-1}(|t_0|)]$. If it is less than $\alpha$, strict monotonicity gives $|t_0|>t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}$, hence

$$
|\overline x|>t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}\frac s{\sqrt n}.
$$

The magnitude of the interval's centre is larger than its half-width, so **zero lies outside the confidence interval**. This is the [duality between confidence intervals and hypothesis tests](../../../../../duality-between-confidence-intervals-and-hypothesis-tests.md).
