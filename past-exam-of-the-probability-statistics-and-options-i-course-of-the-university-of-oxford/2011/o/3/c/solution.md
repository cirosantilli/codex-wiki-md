<h1 id="o/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under $H_0:\mu=0$, the statistic $T=\sqrt n\,\overline X/S$ has [Student t-distribution](../../../../../student-s-t-distribution.md) with $n-1$ degrees of freedom. Reject in either tail, splitting the null error probability equally. Thus the [critical region](../../../../../rejection-region.md) is

$$
\boxed{C=\left\{\mathbf x:\left|\frac{\sqrt n\,\overline x}{s}\right|\geq k\right\},\qquad k=t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}.}
$$

The distribution is continuous, so the boundary convention does not change the size $\alpha$.
