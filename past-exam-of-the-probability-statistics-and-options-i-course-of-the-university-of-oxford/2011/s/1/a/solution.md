↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The affine transformation

$$
\boxed{T(u)=2u-1}
$$

maps the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$ to the uniform distribution on $(-1,1)$. Indeed for $-1<v<1$, $\mathbb P(2U-1\leq v)=(v+1)/2$, whose derivative is the constant density $1/2$.
