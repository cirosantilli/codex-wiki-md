<h1 id="j/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The one-dimensional [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi''(x)+V(x)\psi(x)=E\psi(x).
$$

For the [infinite square well](../../../../infinite-square-well.md) on $[0,a]$, $V=0$ inside and the [wave function](../../../../wave-function-split.md) is zero outside. The boundary conditions are $\psi(0)=\psi(a)=0$. For positive integer $n$, $\psi_n=A\sin(n\pi x/a)$ satisfies them, and $\psi_n''=-(n\pi/a)^2\psi_n$. Substitution gives the [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md)

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}.}
$$

Normalization requires $1=\int_0^a|\psi_n|^2dx=A^2a/2$. The requested real positive constant is therefore

$$
\boxed{A=\sqrt{\frac2a}.}
$$
