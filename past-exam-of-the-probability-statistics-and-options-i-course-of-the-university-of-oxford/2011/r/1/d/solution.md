<h1 id="r/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the convention that $W_n$ is the [wheel graph](../../../../../wheel-graph.md) with rim $C_n$ and one additional hub; the assumption $n\geq3$ gives the rim at least three vertices. The rim needs two colours for even $n$ and three for odd $n$. Since the hub is adjacent to every rim vertex, its colour must be a new one. These lower bounds are attained by properly colouring the rim and adding that extra colour. Thus

$$
\boxed{\chi(W_n)=\begin{cases}3,&n\text{ even},\\4,&n\text{ odd}.\end{cases}}
$$

If a convention indexes a wheel by its total number of vertices instead, replace the rim size $n$ by $n-1$ in this formula.
