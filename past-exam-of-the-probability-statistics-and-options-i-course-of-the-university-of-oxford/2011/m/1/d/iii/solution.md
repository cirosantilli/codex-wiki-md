<h1 id="m/1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The full-day count has [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with mean and variance $2\cdot450=900$. Its [normal approximation](../../../../../../normal-approximation.md) is $N(900,900)$, with standard deviation $30$. With the usual [continuity correction](../../../../../../continuity-correction.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N_{450}\geq930)\approx1-\Phi\left(\frac{929.5-900}{30}\right)\approx0.163.}
$$

Without the half-unit correction the central-limit approximation gives $1-\Phi(1)\approx0.159$.
