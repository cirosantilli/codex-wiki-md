<h1 id="m/1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The one-minute increment has [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with parameter two, independently of where that interval occurs. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N_{11:01}-N_{11:00}=0)=e^{-2}\approx0.1353.}
$$
