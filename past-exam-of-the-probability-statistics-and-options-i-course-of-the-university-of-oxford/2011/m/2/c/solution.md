<h1 id="m/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The finite irreducible aperiodic [Markov chain](../../../../../markov-chain-split.md) converges to its [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md), regardless of its initial law. Therefore $\mathbb E[X_j]\to\pi_+-\pi_-$. Taking the arithmetic means of this convergent sequence gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\mathbb E[S_n]=\frac{1-p-q}{1-p+q}.}
$$

This uses [linearity of expectation](../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and convergence of Cesàro means; it does not require the variables $X_j$ to be independent.
