<h1 id="m/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) requires

$$
1=k\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^2x\,dx=k\frac\pi2,
$$

using $\cos^2x=(1+\cos2x)/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{k=\frac2\pi.}
$$
