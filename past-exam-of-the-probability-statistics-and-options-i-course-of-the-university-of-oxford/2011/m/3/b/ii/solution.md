↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The density is an even function on a symmetric interval, so the integrand $x f(x)$ is odd. Its [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\frac2\pi\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}x\cos^2x\,dx=0.}
$$
