↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A linear combination of independent [normal random variables](../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md) is normal. Its mean is $2\cdot0+0=0$, and independence removes the cross-covariance term in its variance:

$$
\boxed{X=2Z_1+Z_2\sim N(0,2^2+1^2)=N(0,5).}
$$
