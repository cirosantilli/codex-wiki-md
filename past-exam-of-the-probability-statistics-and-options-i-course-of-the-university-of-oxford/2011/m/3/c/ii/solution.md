<h1 id="m/3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The identity $Y=aX+W$ forces $b=1-2a$ and $c=1-a$. Independence will follow from zero [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md), because $X,W$ are jointly [Gaussian random variables](../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md). That covariance is

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,W)=2b+c=3-5a.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{a=\frac35,\qquad b=-\frac15,\qquad c=\frac25.}
$$

These values give $W=(-Z_1+2Z_2)/5$ with variance $1/5$ and zero covariance with $X$. Joint normality then proves the requested independence.
