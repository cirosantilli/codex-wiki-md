<h1 id="m/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The marginal [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is $f_Y(y)=\int_{\mathbb R}f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx$. For $y$ with $f_Y(y)>0$, the [conditional density](../../../../../conditional-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_{X\mid Y=y}(x)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}.}
$$

If the conditional first absolute moment is finite, the [conditional expectation](../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X\mid Y=y]=\int_{\mathbb R}x\,\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}\,dx.}
$$

Conditioning here uses a density, since the individual event $Y=y$ normally has probability zero; a conditional law is specified only up to marginal-null values of $y$.
