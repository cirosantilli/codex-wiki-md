For a [group action](../../../../../group-action-split.md), the [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) and [stabilizer](../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) are

$$
Gx=\{gx:g\in G\},\qquad G_x=\{g\in G:gx=x\}.
$$

The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) for a [finite group](../../../../../finite-group.md) states

$$
\boxed{|G|=|Gx|\,|G_x|.}
$$
