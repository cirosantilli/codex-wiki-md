<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose a [rational function](../../../../../rational-function.md) $P(t)/Q(t)\in\mathbb F_p(t)$, with nonzero [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P,Q$, had $p$th power $t$. Then $P(t)^p=tQ(t)^p$, so comparison of [degrees](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) would give $p\deg P=1+p\deg Q$, impossible modulo $p$. Thus $X^p-t$ has no [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) in $K$, and part (b) proves it is [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md).

In a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) containing $\alpha$ with $\alpha^p=t$,

$$
\boxed{X^p-t=(X-\alpha)^p.}
$$

It has one distinct [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) with multiplicity $p$, so it is inseparable. This is the [purely inseparable polynomial over a rational function field](../../../../../purely-inseparable-polynomial-over-a-rational-function-field.md).
