A real [measurable function](../../../../../measurable-function.md) is one whose inverse image of every open subset of $\mathbb R$ is [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\{x:f(x)>a\}\text{ is Lebesgue measurable for every real }a.}
$$

These upper rays generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md), making this equivalent to requiring measurable inverse images of all [Borel sets](../../../../../borel-set.md).
