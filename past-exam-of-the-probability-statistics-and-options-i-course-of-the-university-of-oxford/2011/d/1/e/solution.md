<h1 id="d/1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

If $a\geq0$, the set $\{f>a\}$ is contained in $E$, since $f=0$ outside $E$. It is therefore a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) and is [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md). If $a<0$, its complement $\{f\leq a\}$ is contained in $E$ and is measurable for the same reason. Complements of measurable sets are measurable. Thus every upper level set is measurable, proving **$f$ is a measurable function**.
