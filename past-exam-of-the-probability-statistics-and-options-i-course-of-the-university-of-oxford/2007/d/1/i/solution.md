The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) states that if measurable functions $f_n$ are nonnegative and $f_n\uparrow f$ pointwise, then $f$ is measurable and

$$
\boxed{\int f\,d\mu=\lim_{n\to\infty}\int f_n\,d\mu,}
$$

allowing either side to be infinite. The same conclusion holds under almost-everywhere [monotone convergence](../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) after changing values on a null set. No assumption that the integrals stay bounded is required.
