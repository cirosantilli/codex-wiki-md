The [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) is $\mathcal T_A=\{A\cap U:U\in\mathcal T\}$. For every open $U\subseteq X$,

$$
i^{-1}(U)=A\cap U\in\mathcal T_A.
$$

The inverse-image definition of [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) therefore gives **the inclusion is continuous** from $(A,\mathcal T_A)$ to $(X,\mathcal T)$.
