<h1 id="e/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every open set of the subspace is $A\cap U$ for some $U\in\mathcal T$. Since $f$ takes values in $A$,

$$
f^{-1}(A\cap U)=(i\circ f)^{-1}(U).
$$

The right side is open in $Z$ by the assumed [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the composition. Hence [preimages](../../../../../preimage.md) of every $\mathcal T_A$-open set are open, proving **$f:Z\to(A,\mathcal T_A)$ is continuous**.
