↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** A connected graph has an [Eulerian cycle](../../../../../eulerian-cycle.md) exactly when every vertex has even degree. In $K_n$ every degree is $n-1$, so it has an [Eulerian cycle](../../../../../eulerian-cycle.md) precisely when $n$ is odd. The [complete graph](../../../../../complete-graph.md) $K_4$ is a counterexample to the statement for all $n\geq3$.
