↑ **Parent:** [Ix](../ix.md)

**False.** In a [Prüfer code](../../../../../prufer-sequence.md), the degree of a label is one plus its number of occurrences. Here labels $1,\ldots,n-2$ occur once and labels $n-1,n$ do not occur. Thus the former have degree two and the latter degree one. The decoded [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) is the path

$$
(n-1),1,2,\ldots,n-2,n
$$

with the evident single-edge interpretation when $n=2$. Independently, no vertex of a simple [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) on $n$ vertices can have degree $n$ at all. The degree claim in the statement is therefore impossible.
