**False.** The cube has eight vertices and twelve edges. [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](../../../../../euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) for its connected [plane graph](../../../../../plane-graph.md) gives $8-12+F=2$, so

$$
\boxed{F=6.}
$$

The nested-square embedding has four faces between the squares, one inside the smaller square and the unbounded exterior face.
