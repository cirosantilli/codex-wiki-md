↑ **Parent:** [X](../x.md)

**True.** Each [connected component](../../../../../connected-component.md) of a [forest](../../../../../forest.md) is a [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md). If the component sizes are $n_1,\ldots,n_k$, their edge counts are $n_1-1,\ldots,n_k-1$, including isolated vertices as size-one components. Summing gives $m=n-k$, hence

$$
\boxed{k=n-m.}
$$
