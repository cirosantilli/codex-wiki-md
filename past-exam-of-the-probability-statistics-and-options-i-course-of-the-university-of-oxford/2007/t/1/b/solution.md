<h1 id="t/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With primal constraints $Ax=b$, the dual variable is unrestricted in sign:

$$
\boxed{\text{minimize }b^Ty
\quad\text{subject to }A^Ty\geq c,\quad y\in\mathbb R^m.}
$$

Membership of $x$ in the [nonnegative orthant](../../../../../nonnegative-orthant.md) still yields $c^Tx\leq y^TAx$, but the next step is now the equality $y^TAx=y^Tb$, so no sign condition on $y$ is needed. Equivalently an equality constraint can be replaced by two opposite inequalities whose nonnegative multipliers combine into one free multiplier.
