# Norm subgroup of a local field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md)

For a finite extension of local fields $L/K$, its norm subgroup is the image of the [field norm](field-norm.md) $N_{L/K}:L^\times\to K^\times$. For an abelian extension, it is the kernel of the induced local Artin map to $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$.
