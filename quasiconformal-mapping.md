# Quasiconformal mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiconformal_mapping)

An [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) with locally $L^2$ [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md) and essentially bounded infinitesimal distortion. In [holomorphic coordinates](holomorphic-coordinate.md), its [Beltrami coefficient](beltrami-coefficient.md) satisfies $\|\mu\|_\infty<1$, and the [maximal dilatation](maximal-dilatation.md) is $(1+\|\mu\|_\infty)/(1-\|\mu\|_\infty)$.
