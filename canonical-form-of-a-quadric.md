# Canonical form of a quadric

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

The equation of a [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md) after [orthogonal diagonalization](orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md) of its [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) and appropriate [translation](translation-geometry.md). The [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md) removes mixed quadratic terms, and completing squares removes linear terms in directions with nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Linear terms in zero-[eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) directions can remain and generate [elliptic paraboloids](elliptic-paraboloid.md) or [hyperbolic paraboloids](hyperbolic-paraboloid.md). The signs, zero coefficients and constant distinguish ordinary surfaces, [degenerate conics](degenerate-conic.md) in plane sections, and empty real loci.
