# Terrain correction

↑ **Parent:** [Bouguer gravity anomaly](bouguer-gravity-anomaly.md)

A correction for the difference between real surrounding relief and the assumed simple topographic slab. Its computation needs terrain geometry and an assumed [density](density.md), and can matter in rough [surface topography](surface-topography.md).
