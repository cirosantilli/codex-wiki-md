# Laplace-Lagrange inclination matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Complex inclination](complex-inclination.md)

For circular planetary orbits with [angular momenta](angular-momentum.md) $L_j=M_j\sqrt{GM_\star a_j}$, the inclination matrix has positive off-diagonal entries, zero row sums, and $L_jB_{jk}=L_kB_{kj}$. [Positive diagonal symmetrization of a matrix](positive-diagonal-symmetrization-of-a-matrix.md) with $W=\operatorname{diag}(L_j)$ makes $W^{1/2}BW^{-1/2}$ a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). Moreover,

$$
\mathbf z^\dagger WB\mathbf z=-\sum_{j<k}L_jB_{jk}|z_j-z_k|^2\le0.
$$

All [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are real and nonpositive. The [zero eigenvalue](zero-eigenvalue.md) is a common tilt of every orbital plane; nonzero modes describe relative nodal precession.
