# Invariant complement from an equivariant projection

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md)

If a submodule $U$ of a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) $V$ admits an intertwining map $P:V\to U$ with $P|_U=I_U$, then $P^2=P$ and its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is an invariant complement. For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the [Hom representation](hom-representation.md) on maps $V\to U$ whose restriction is a scalar identity reduces finding $P$ to [splitting of a trivial quotient for a semisimple Lie algebra](casimir-splitting-of-a-trivial-quotient.md).
