# Helical orbit of a Euclidean Killing vector

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean Killing vector field](euclidean-killing-vector-field.md)

An infinitesimal rotation in one coordinate plane plus a constant translation has [integral curves of a vector field](integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) that rotate about a shifted centre in that plane and move uniformly in the remaining directions. Nonzero transverse drift gives helices; zero drift gives circles, with zero radius allowing straight or stationary curves.
