# Curie heat-capacity density from an Arrott plot

↑ **Parent:** [Heat-capacity jump in quartic Landau theory](heat-capacity-jump-in-quartic-landau-theory.md)

For [density](density.md) $F=\alpha(T-T_c)M^2/2+bM^4/4-\mu_0HM$, the zero-field ordered minimum is $-\alpha^2(T-T_c)^2/(4b)$. Its constant-field heat-capacity [density](density.md) jump is $\Delta c_H=T_c\alpha^2/(2b)$. In an [Arrott plot](arrott-plot.md), let physical slope be $S=\mu_0/b$ and horizontal intercept be $X_0(T)=a(T)/\mu_0$. Then

$$
\Delta c_H=\frac{T_c\mu_0S}{2}\left(\frac{dX_0}{dT}\right)^2.
$$

This makes numerical sensitivity explicit: uncertainty in the intercept slope is squared. A volumetric [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) determines a volumetric heat-capacity jump; total or molar values need further sample information.
