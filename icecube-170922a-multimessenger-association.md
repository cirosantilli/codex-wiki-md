# IceCube-170922A multimessenger association

↑ **Parent:** [IceCube Neutrino Observatory](icecube-neutrino-observatory.md)

The 2018 collaboration paper associated the 22 September 2017 [neutrino](neutrino.md) alert with the flaring [blazar](blazar.md) [TXS 0506+056](txs-0506-plus-056.md) through directional and multiwavelength observations. Its approximately $3\sigma$ evidence included trial corrections and assumed relationships between [neutrino](neutrino.md) and [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) emission. A single association did not establish a production mechanism or explain the entire diffuse [neutrino flux](neutrino-flux.md).[The neutrino alert; Neutrino-blazar coincidence analysis; Discussion, PDF pp. 2–3, 11–13, 62–66](multi-messenger-observations-of-a-flaring-blazar-coincident-with-high-energy-neutrino-icecube-170922a-by-icecube-collaboration-and-others.md)
