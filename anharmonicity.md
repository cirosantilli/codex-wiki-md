# Anharmonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillation](oscillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anharmonicity)

[Anharmonicity](anharmonicity.md) is departure from the motion of a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md), often caused by nonquadratic terms in a [potential energy](potential-energy.md). It can make the [oscillation](oscillation.md) [frequency](frequency.md) depend on [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and generate higher harmonics. A [quartic oscillator](quartic-oscillator.md) has one such anharmonic [potential energy](potential-energy.md).
