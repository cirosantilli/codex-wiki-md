# Koebe function

↑ **Parent:** [Univalent function](univalent-function.md)

The Koebe function $k(z)=z/(1-z)^2$ is a [univalent function](univalent-function.md) from the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) onto $\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,-1/4]$. To see the image, $(1+z)/(1-z)$ maps the disc onto the right half-plane and $k(z)=(((1+z)/(1-z))^2-1)/4$. Its normalization $k(0)=0$, $k'(0)=1$ proves sharpness of the [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md).
