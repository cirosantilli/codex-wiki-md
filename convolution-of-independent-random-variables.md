# Convolution of independent random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md)

If independent real random variables $X$ and $Y$ have densities $f_X$ and $f_Y$, their sum has the [convolution](convolution.md) density

$$
f_{X+Y}(z)=(f_X*f_Y)(z)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(x)f_Y(z-x)\,dx.
$$
