# Heisenberg Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

The three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra has a basis $a,b,c$ with $[a,b]=c$ and $c$ central. It is a two-step [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md).
