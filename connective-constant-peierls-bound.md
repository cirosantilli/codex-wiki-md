# Connective-constant Peierls bound

↑ **Parent:** [Peierls argument](peierls-argument.md)

Independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md) satisfies $p_c\leq1-\mu^{-1}$, where $\mu$ is its [connective constant](connective-constant.md). If $(1-p)\mu<1$, closed dual [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md) have a summable large-length tail: their counts are bounded by a polynomial factor times the count of [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md). Conditioning a sufficiently large finite box to be open excludes short enclosing [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md) without changing the law of the remaining [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). With positive conditional [probability](probability.md) no enclosing closed dual [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) remains, so the origin belongs to an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md).
