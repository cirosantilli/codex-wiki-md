# Invariant potential of one SU2 scalar doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar potential](scalar-potential.md)

The [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) $SU(2)$ acts transitively on every sphere in its defining $\mathbb C^2$: for $|u|^2+|v|^2=1$, the [matrix](matrix.md) with columns $(u,v)^T$ and $(-\bar v,\bar u)^T$ is in $SU(2)$. Thus any invariant real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in a single commuting doublet and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) depends only on $\phi^\dagger\phi$. Through total degree four it is $V_0+m^2\phi^\dagger\phi+\lambda(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2$. A holomorphic [polynomial invariant](polynomial-invariant.md) of positive degree does not exist for one doublet: $\operatorname{Sym}^d\mathbf2$ is a nontrivial [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) for every $d>0$.
