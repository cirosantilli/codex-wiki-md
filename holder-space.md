<h1 id="holder-space">Hölder space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder condition](holder-condition.md)

A [Hölder space](holder-space.md) consists of functions satisfying a [Hölder condition](holder-condition.md). A bounded function belongs to $C^{0,\alpha}(\mathbb R^n)$ when

$$
\sup_{x\ne y}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{|x-y|^\alpha}<\infty.
$$
