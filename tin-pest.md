# Tin pest

↑ **Parent:** [Tin](tin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin_pest)

Degradation associated with the slow transformation of [white tin](white-tin.md) to lower-density [grey tin](grey-tin.md). [Nucleation](nucleation.md), impurities and thermal history strongly affect its kinetics.
