# Kamiokande

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino astronomy](neutrino-astronomy.md)

[Kamiokande](kamiokande.md), associated with [Masatoshi Koshiba](masatoshi-koshiba.md), was an underground [water](water.md) [Cherenkov detector](cherenkov-detector.md) viewed by [photomultipliers](photomultiplier.md). Improved [water](water.md) purity and reduced background enabled [solar neutrino](solar-neutrino.md) observations. A concentration of reconstructed recoil directions toward the [Sun](sun.md) distinguished a source signal above an approximately direction-independent background.[pp. 1011–1015; Figs. 1, 7–9](nobel-lecture-birth-of-neutrino-astrophysics-by-masatoshi-koshiba.md)

For [solar neutrinos](solar-neutrino.md), [electron](electron.md) [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) supplies a directional recoil, with different [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md) for active [neutrino flavors](neutrino-flavor.md). Neither the [Homestake experiment](homestake-experiment.md) deficit nor the [SN 1987A neutrino burst](sn-1987a-neutrino-burst.md) alone established [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md); later evidence compared complementary channels and propagation effects.[pp. 1013, 1016–1018: scattering and combined oscillation evidence](nobel-lecture-birth-of-neutrino-astrophysics-by-masatoshi-koshiba.md)
