# Norm subgroup of a 2-power cyclotomic extension of the 2-adic numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Norm subgroup of a local field extension](norm-subgroup-of-a-local-field-extension.md)

For every positive integer $n$,

$$
N_{\mathbb Q_2(\zeta_{2^n})/\mathbb Q_2}
\bigl(\mathbb Q_2(\zeta_{2^n})^\times\bigr)
=2^{\mathbb Z}(1+2^n\mathbb Z_2).
$$

Under [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md), the cyclotomic character identifies the quotient with $(\mathbb Z/2^n\mathbb Z)^\times$: a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) acts trivially on the totally ramified extension, while a [2-adic unit](2-adic-unit.md) acts through its residue class modulo $2^n$.
