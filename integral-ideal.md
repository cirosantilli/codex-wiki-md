# Integral ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md)

An integral ideal is a [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md) of an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $A$ that is contained in $A$. For a [number field](number-field.md), take $A$ to be its [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) $\mathcal O_K$.
