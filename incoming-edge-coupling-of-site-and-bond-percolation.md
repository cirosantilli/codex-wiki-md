# Incoming-edge coupling of site and bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Directed percolation](directed-percolation.md)

Suppose every vertex of a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) has at most $\Delta$ incoming bonds. In [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) with parameter $q$, open a site whenever at least one incoming bond is open. Incoming bond sets have different heads and hence are disjoint, so these site indicators are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md). Supplement their [probabilities](probability.md) to $r=1-(1-q)^\Delta$. Every vertex reached by a nontrivial open [directed path](directed-path.md) is now open. Conditioning the root to be open gives $\theta_s(r)\ge r\theta_b(q)$, since a simple [directed path](directed-path.md) from the root never uses a bond entering the root. Thus $p_H^s\le1-(1-p_H^b)^\Delta$.
