# Antianyon

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion rule](anyon-fusion-rule.md)

The dual charge to an [anyon](anyon.md) $a$, characterized by $N^1_{a\bar a}=1$. Reversing the orientation of an [anyon](anyon.md) strand replaces its label by its [antianyon](antianyon.md). Its [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md) satisfies $d_{\bar a}=d_a$.
