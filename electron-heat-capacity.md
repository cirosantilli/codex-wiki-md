# Electron heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

The contribution of [electrons](electron.md) to [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). For a uniform noninteracting three-dimensional [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) at fixed [particle number](particle-number.md) and [volume](volume.md), its low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) value is $C_V\simeq(\pi^2/2)Nk_B T/T_F$; only states near the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) can change occupation readily.
