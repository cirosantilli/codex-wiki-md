# Center of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_of_a_group)

The centre of a [group](group-split.md) is $Z(G)=\{z\in G:zg=gz\text{ for every }g\in G\}$. It is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md). A nontrivial finite [p-group](p-group.md) has nontrivial centre: in the [class equation](class-equation.md), each noncentral conjugacy class has size divisible by $p$, so $|Z(G)|\equiv|G|\equiv0\pmod p$.
