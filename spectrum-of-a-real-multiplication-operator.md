# Spectrum of a real multiplication operator

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication operator](multiplication-operator.md)

For a real [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $m$ on a [sigma-finite measure](sigma-finite-measure.md) space, multiplication on $D(M_m)=\{f\in L^2:mf\in L^2\}$ is an [unbounded self-adjoint operator](unbounded-self-adjoint-operator.md). Testing its adjoint relation on $\{|m|\leq n\}$ identifies the same maximal domain. Outside the [essential range](essential-range.md), multiplication by $(m-z)^{-1}$ is a bounded resolvent. Inside it, normalized indicators of finite-positive-measure sets where $m$ is close to $z$ are [approximate eigenvectors](approximate-eigenvector.md), so the inverse cannot be bounded.
