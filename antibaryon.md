# Antibaryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

An [antibaryon](antibaryon.md) is the [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) of a [baryon](baryon.md). In the valence [quark model](quark-model.md) it consists of three [antiquarks](antiquark.md) in a colour singlet. Charge conjugation reverses its additive charges and third component of [isospin](isospin.md), preserving total [isospin](isospin.md) and spin.
