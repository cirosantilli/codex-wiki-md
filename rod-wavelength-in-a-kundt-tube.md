# Rod wavelength in a Kundt tube

↑ **Parent:** [Kundt's tube](kundt-s-tube.md)

A freely moving rod end has zero axial [stress](stress.md), not zero axial [displacement](displacement.md). For a uniform slender rod of length $\ell$, its low-frequency extensional modes have shapes $\xi_r(x)\propto\cos(n\pi x/\ell)$ and [wavelengths](wavelength.md) $\lambda_r=2\ell/n$. A centre clamp is compatible with the $n=1$ displacement node and $\lambda_r=2\ell$; clamps at $\ell/4$ and $3\ell/4$ are compatible with $n=2$ and $\lambda_r=\ell$. The support positions therefore matter before a gas [wavelength](wavelength.md) can determine a rod [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md).

For a chosen model with $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$, $\rho_r=8000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $\ell=1.60\,\mathrm m$, the slender-rod extensional relation $c_r=\sqrt{E/\rho_r}$ gives $5000\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The two-quarter-support mode then has $f=3125\,\mathrm{Hz}$. If the gas has stipulated $c_g=343\,\mathrm{m/s}$, its half-[wavelength](wavelength.md) is $d_g=54.88\,\mathrm{mm}$, and

$$
\frac{c_r}{c_g}=\frac{\lambda_r}{\lambda_g}=\frac{1.60\,\mathrm m}{2(0.05488\,\mathrm m)}\simeq14.5773.
$$

The centre-supported fundamental would instead have $f=1562.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and twice that gas spacing. The same rod material has not changed its wave speed. In this slender-rod model, $E$ is [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md); a bulk compressional wave involves a different elastic-modulus combination, so the two wave speeds are not interchangeable.
