# Wave attenuation in sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

Wave attenuation is a reduction of a chosen wave component as it travels through [sea ice](sea-ice.md). An amplitude coefficient $\alpha$ corresponds to an energy coefficient $2\alpha$. [Wave scattering at a planar interface](wave-scattering-at-a-planar-interface.md) can remove energy from the forward wave without dissipating total energy; [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md), collisions and internal ice losses irreversibly convert mechanical [energy](energy.md) to [heat](heat.md).
