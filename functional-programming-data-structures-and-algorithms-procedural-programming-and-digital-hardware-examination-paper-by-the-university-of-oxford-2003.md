# Functional programming, data structures and algorithms, procedural programming, and digital hardware examination paper by the University of Oxford (2003)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The eight-page Preliminary Examination in Computer Science, Paper 1, Long Vacation 2003. Original source: [Released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `4213-1.pdf`. Printed pp. 2–3 cover [functional programming](functional-programming.md), p. 4 covers [algorithms](algorithm-split.md), pp. 5–6 cover [imperative programming](imperative-programming.md), and pp. 7–8 cover [digital electronics](digital-electronics.md). The timing diagram on printed p. 8 permits a new [clock cycle](clock-cycle.md) pulse immediately after a cycle that rearms the circuit.
