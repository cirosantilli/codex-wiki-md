# Van Hove singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Hove_singularity)

A nonanalyticity of the [density of states](density-of-states.md) associated with a stationary point of a band [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Its form depends on dimension and on whether the stationary point is an extremum or a saddle.
