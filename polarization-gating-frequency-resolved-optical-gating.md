# Polarization-gating frequency-resolved optical gating

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency-resolved optical gating](frequency-resolved-optical-gating.md)

An optical Kerr gate rotates a probe's polarization in proportion to a delayed replica's [intensity](intensity-physics.md) in the weak-gate regime. Resolving the gated spectrum gives a pulse-characterization record with a third-order gate law, distinct from a second-harmonic gate.
