# Highest-weight classification of rational GL representations

↑ **Parent:** [Rational representation of the general linear group](rational-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md)

The irreducible rational $GL_m$ [modules](module-mathematics.md) are $D_\lambda(V)=(\det V)^{\lambda_m}\otimes D_{\lambda-\lambda_m(1,\ldots,1)}(V)$. The second factor is a [Schur module](schur-module.md). Clearing [determinant](determinant.md) denominators reduces completeness to the [Schur algebra](schur-algebra.md) classification of [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) [modules](module-mathematics.md). Distinct dominant integer tuples have distinct highest torus weights. Their [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) are symmetric [Laurent polynomials](laurent-polynomial.md); only [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) [modules](module-mathematics.md) have [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) defined at every singular endomorphism.
