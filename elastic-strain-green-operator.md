# Elastic strain Green operator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

For a homogeneous comparison [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) $C^0$, the [elastic strain Green operator](elastic-strain-green-operator.md) maps an [elastic polarization](elastic-polarization.md) to minus the induced [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md). In a bounded domain the induced [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) has zero boundary data; in an infinite medium it is the decaying perturbation. The defining [static elastic equilibrium](static-elastic-equilibrium.md) equation is $\nabla\cdot(C^0\operatorname{sym}\nabla u+\tau)=0$. The bounded-domain operator is self-adjoint, annihilates constant [elastic polarizations](elastic-polarization.md), has zero mean output, and satisfies $\Gamma C^0\Gamma=\Gamma$.
