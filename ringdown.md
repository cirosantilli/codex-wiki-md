# Ringdown

↑ **Parent:** [Damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md)

Ringdown is the freely decaying motion of a [resonator](resonator.md) after an applied drive is removed. For an underdamped [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md), the amplitude decays as $e^{-\gamma t}$ while the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\nu=\sqrt{\Omega^2-\gamma^2}$. In the weak-damping limit, the cycle-averaged stored [energy](energy-split.md) decays as $e^{-2\gamma t}$, giving the [quality factor from ringdown](quality-factor-from-ringdown.md) $Q\simeq\Omega/(2\gamma)$.
