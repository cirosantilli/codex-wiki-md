# Circumbinary orbital-period correction

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole potential of a circular binary](quadrupole-potential-of-a-circular-binary.md)

For a distant coplanar [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) about a [binary star](binary-star.md), the averaged [quadrupole potential of a circular binary](quadrupole-potential-of-a-circular-binary.md) gives

$$
P=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{x^3}{GM}}\left(1+\frac{3\mu a^2}{4Mx^2}\right)^{-1/2}.
$$

At fixed outer radius $x$, the quadrupole shortens the period relative to the monopole result. If the inner binary evolves slowly, the outer [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) stays constant in the axisymmetric averaged potential, but the outer energy can change. The outer radius must then be allowed to adjust when computing a period derivative.
