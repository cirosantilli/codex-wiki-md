# Fault-propagation fold

↑ **Parent:** [Folding](folding.md)

A fold formed as displacement is transferred ahead of a propagating [fault](geological-fault.md) tip into surrounding [rock](rock-geology.md). It is one mechanism for surface deformation above a [blind reverse fault](blind-reverse-fault.md).
