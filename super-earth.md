# Super-Earth

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super-Earth)

A super-Earth is an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) more massive than [Earth](earth.md) but below the ice-giant mass range. The label does not guarantee a rocky [exoplanet interior](exoplanet-interior.md), a specific [radius](radius.md), or habitability. Radius-based usage can overlap the sub-Neptune population.
