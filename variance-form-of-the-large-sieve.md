# Variance form of the large sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Large sieve](large-sieve.md)

For coefficients $b_n$ supported on an interval of $H$ consecutive [integers](integer.md), set $B=\sum b_n$ and $B_p(a)=\sum_{n\equiv a\pmod p}b_n$. Then

$$
\sum_{p\leq Q}p\sum_{a\bmod p}|B_p(a)-B/p|^2\leq(Q^2+2\pi H)\sum|b_n|^2.
$$

The [orthogonality of roots of unity](orthogonality-of-roots-of-unity.md) gives $p\sum_a|B_p(a)-B/p|^2=\sum_{r=1}^{p-1}|\sum_nb_ne(rn/p)|^2$. The distinct fractions $r/p$ have [circular spacing](circular-spacing.md) at least $Q^{-2}$; now apply the [exponential-sum large sieve](exponential-sum-large-sieve.md).
