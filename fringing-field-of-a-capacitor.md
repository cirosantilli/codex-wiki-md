# Fringing field of a capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md)

The [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) is the nonuniform [electric field](electric-field.md) near the edges of its [electrical conductors](electrical-conductor.md), extending outside the gap. For a [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md), neglecting it is useful when the gap is much smaller than both plate widths; the approximation $C\simeq\epsilon A/d$ is less reliable when these lengths become comparable.

Finite plates break the translational [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) of the infinite-plate model. Each [conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md) is an [equipotential](equipotential.md), so the [electric field](electric-field.md) must meet a smooth conducting surface normally. Near an edge, the [equipotentials](equipotential.md) curve into the surrounding space and the [electric field](electric-field.md) changes both direction and magnitude. The [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) adjusts to enforce the conducting [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md); it is not uniform all the way to a sharp edge. The [electrostatic boundary conditions at a conductor](electrostatic-boundary-conditions-at-a-conductor.md) explain why zero [electric field](electric-field.md) inside the metal does not imply zero [electric field](electric-field.md) just outside it.

The practical issue is where the nearly uniform region ends. A probe near the centre can support $E\simeq V/d$ while a probe close to an edge detects a lateral component and a different magnitude. A total [capacitance](capacitance.md) reading also includes [electric flux](electric-flux.md) outside the simple footprint. The [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) can therefore matter in [permittivity](permittivity.md) measurements even when a central [electric field](electric-field.md) measurement looks nearly ideal. Keysight's three-[electrode](electrode.md) fixture diverts the fringe contribution away from the measured response.[Solution: fringe capacitance and the three-electrode 16451B fixture](materials-measurement-dielectric-materials.md)
