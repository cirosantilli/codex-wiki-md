# Chemical polarity

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_polarity)

[Chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md) concerns unequal distribution of [electron density](electron-density.md) in a [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) or [molecule](molecule-split.md). A [polar molecule](polar-molecule.md) has a permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md); the effects of individual bonds must be combined with their spatial arrangement. In a symmetric linear [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md), opposing bond-dipole contributions cancel. In [water](water.md), its [bent molecular geometry](bent-molecular-geometry.md) prevents that cancellation, giving a permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md).[Chemistry 2e, 7.6: geometry and molecular polarity](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
