# Cuspidal representation of a finite reductive group

↑ **Parent:** [Harish-Chandra restriction](harish-chandra-restriction.md)

An irreducible complex $G^F$-module is cuspidal if its [Harish-Chandra restriction](harish-chandra-restriction.md) is zero for every proper rational [parabolic subgroup](parabolic-subgroup.md). By adjunction, it occurs in no [Harish-Chandra induction](harish-chandra-induction.md) from a proper rational [Levi subgroup](levi-subgroup.md). This is a finite-group notion, distinct from a cuspidal automorphic representation.
