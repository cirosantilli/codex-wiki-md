# Circle reduction of eleven-dimensional supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Toroidal reduction of eleven-dimensional supergravity](toroidal-reduction-of-eleven-dimensional-supergravity.md)

For circle-independent fields, the metric gives the type-IIA string-frame metric, [dilaton](dilaton.md) and RR one-form. Decomposing $A_3=C_3+B_2\wedge dy$ gives the RR three-form and [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md). With $H_3=dB_2$, $F_2=dC_1$, the horizontal four-form is $\widetilde F_4=dC_3-C_1\wedge H_3$. The Einstein term reduces, modulo a total derivative, to $\sqrt{-g}[e^{-2\Phi}(R+4(\nabla\Phi)^2)-\tfrac14F_{2,\mu\nu}F_2^{\mu\nu}]$. The eleven-dimensional form norm gives $\sqrt{-g}[e^{-2\Phi}|H_3|^2+|\widetilde F_4|^2]$, where $|F_k|^2=F^2/k!$. Ordinary reduction generates massless [type IIA supergravity](type-iia-supergravity.md), not [Romans mass](romans-mass.md).
