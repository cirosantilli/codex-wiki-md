# Coplanar transit limit

↑ **Parent:** [Transit impact parameter](transit-impact-parameter.md)

For two exactly coplanar [circular orbits](circular-orbit.md) around the same [star](star.md), [transit impact parameters](transit-impact-parameter.md) scale as [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md), hence as $P^{2/3}$. Any overlap by the outer [planet](planet.md) requires $b_2<1+k_2$. In the point-planet limit the period ratio is bounded by $b_1^{-3/2}$. Tangency has zero duration, so this is a geometric supremum; practical detection also requires sufficient [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) and survey coverage.
