# Ising spin variable

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

An Ising [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) variable is a classical two-state degree of freedom. Its thermal mean can lie anywhere in $[-1,1]$ even though each microscopic value is $-1$ or $1$. It should not be confused with an arbitrary quantum-spin operator. Products of neighbouring [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) supply the pair interactions of the [Ising model](ising-model.md), and their average defines [spin magnetization](spin-magnetization.md).
