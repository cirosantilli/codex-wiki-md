# Corotating energy invariant of an axisymmetric magnetic wind

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind](axisymmetric-magnetohydrodynamic-wind.md)

For a pressure-free flowing [poloidal flux surface](axisymmetric-magnetic-flux-surface.md), subtracting its angular [velocity](velocity.md) times the azimuthal momentum equation from the mechanical energy equation shows that $\epsilon_J$ is constant. It equals poloidal kinetic energy plus $(u_\phi-R\omega)^2/2+\Phi-R^2\omega^2/2$. This is the total [magnetohydrodynamic Bernoulli invariant](magnetohydrodynamic-bernoulli-invariant.md) minus $\omega$ times the total [magnetohydrodynamic angular-momentum invariant](magnetohydrodynamic-angular-momentum-invariant.md), not the total energy alone.
