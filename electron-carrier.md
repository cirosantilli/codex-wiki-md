# Electron carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md)

An [electron carrier](electron-carrier.md) cycles between [oxidation](oxidation.md) and [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md). [Ubiquinone](ubiquinone.md) carries two [electrons](electron.md), while [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md) carries one at a time.[§7.4, Q and Complex II; Complex III: mobile carriers and electron capacities](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

For example, transferring the two [electrons](electron.md) supplied by one [NADH](nadh.md) requires two one-[electron](electron.md) deliveries by [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md). It does not require two permanently consumed [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md): an available [electron carrier](electron-carrier.md) can unload, return and reload. [Electron carriers](electron-carrier.md) recycle material while the net [redox](redox.md) conversion supplies [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
