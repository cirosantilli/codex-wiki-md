# Bipyramid

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipyramid)

A [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) formed by joining two pyramids along a common polygonal base on opposite sides of its [plane](plane.md). The common base is internal; the external boundary consists of the two sets of triangular side faces. A triangular [bipyramid](bipyramid.md) has five [vertices of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md), nine edges and six triangular faces.
