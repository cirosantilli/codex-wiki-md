# Zero-dimensional scalar field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field](scalar-field.md)

A [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with no spacetime coordinates reduces its [path integral](path-integral.md) to an ordinary integral. It preserves [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) and [Feynman-diagram symmetry factors](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md), making it a useful model of [perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md).
