# Second-harmonic feedback in a long-wave convection amplitude equation

↑ **Parent:** [Long-wave convection equation with broken Boussinesq symmetry](long-wave-convection-equation-with-broken-boussinesq-symmetry.md)

In a [weakly nonlinear expansion](weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md) of the [long-wave convection equation with broken Boussinesq symmetry](long-wave-convection-equation-with-broken-boussinesq-symmetry.md) at $\mu=2$, a critical [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $\Theta_1=Ae^{ix}+\mathrm{c.c.}$ generates $\Theta_2=(2is/9)A^2e^{2ix}+\mathrm{c.c.}$. The quadratic interaction of these first and second harmonics feeds back $8s^2|A|^2A/9$ into the critical [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md), while the cubic gradient nonlinearity contributes $-3|A|^2A$. The [Fredholm solvability condition](fredholm-solvability-condition.md) is consequently $\mu_2A-g|A|^2A=0$. The sign of $g$ distinguishes supercritical and subcritical branches; $g=0$ requires a higher-order [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md).
