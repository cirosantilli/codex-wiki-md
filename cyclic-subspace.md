# Cyclic subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic vector](cyclic-vector.md)

A [cyclic subspace](cyclic-subspace.md) generated by $x$ under a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$ is the [span](linear-span.md) of its successive iterates $x,Tx,T^2x,\ldots$. In finite dimension it is already generated by the first $n$ iterates. The first linear dependence expresses the next iterate in previous ones and proves invariance without using the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem.md).
