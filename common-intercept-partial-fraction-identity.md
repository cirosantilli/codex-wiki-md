# Common-intercept partial fraction identity

↑ **Parent:** [Partial fraction decomposition](partial-fraction-decomposition.md)

For $A\ne0$ and $m\ne n$, combining the right-hand fractions gives numerator $A(m-n)$ and proves the identity. Integration on an interval avoiding the poles gives $\ln|(A+mx)/(A+nx)|/[A(m-n)]$ plus a constant. The overall $1/A$ cannot be dropped unless the variable has already been normalized to $A=1$.
