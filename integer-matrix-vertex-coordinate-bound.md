# Integer-matrix vertex coordinate bound

↑ **Parent:** [Active constraint](active-constraint.md)

At an [extreme point](extreme-point.md) of $\{x:Ax\ge b\}$, the active rows span the ambient dimension: otherwise a nonzero direction orthogonal to all active rows permits small feasible moves of both signs. Select $n$ independent active rows to form an integer matrix $B$. Its nonzero determinant has absolute value at least one. [Cramer's rule](cramer-s-rule.md) and the determinant expansion bound each coordinate by $n!U^n$, where $U$ bounds the entries of $A$ and $b$. The vector $b$ may be real; only the denominator matrix needs to be integer.
