# Oscillator ladder-operator sign convention

↑ **Parent:** [Creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md)

For real coefficients in $a_\pm=c_\pm X+id_\pm P$, imposing $a_-^\dagger=a_+$, $[a_-,a_+]=I$ and $H=\hbar\omega(a_+a_-+\lambda I)$ gives

$$
c_+=c_-=\varepsilon\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}},\qquad
d_-=-d_+=\frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}},\qquad
\lambda=\frac12,\qquad\varepsilon=\pm1.
$$

Indeed the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) condition makes the two $c$ coefficients equal and the two $d$ coefficients opposite. The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) gives $2\hbar c_-d_-=1$, and matching the coefficients of $X^2$ and $P^2$ fixes their magnitudes. Both signs change together. The conventional [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) choose $\varepsilon=1$; the other sign is an equivalent phase convention for the [number states](number-state.md).
