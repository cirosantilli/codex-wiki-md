# Common nonseparating disks in a Heegaard splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md)

Two nonseparating compressing disks on opposite sides with the same boundary form an embedded sphere. Cutting each [handlebody](handlebody.md) along its disk leaves it connected, so the sphere is nonseparating in the glued [three-manifold](3-manifold.md). Capping the two resulting sphere boundaries gives a splitting of one lower genus of the remaining summand; the removed summand is $S^1\times S^2$.
