# Delta derivatives from polynomial oscillatory amplitudes

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier representation of the Dirac delta function](fourier-representation-of-the-dirac-delta-function.md)

With inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) normalization $(2\pi)^{-1}$, $\int e^{is\theta}\theta^j\,d\theta=2\pi i^{-j}\delta^{(j)}(s)$ as an [oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md). The factor $i^{-j}$ and the sign convention $\langle\delta',g\rangle=-g'(0)$ are essential. For instance, amplitude $-ix_2\theta/(2\pi)$ with phase $x_1\theta$ gives $-x_2\delta'(x_1)$.
