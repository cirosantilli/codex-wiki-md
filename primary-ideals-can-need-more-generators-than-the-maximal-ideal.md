# Primary ideals can need more generators than the maximal ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Primary ideal](primary-ideal.md)

In the [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) $A=k[[u,v]]$ with [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m=(u,v)$, the [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) is two but $\mathfrak m^r$ needs $r+1$ generators for every $r\geq1$. The degree-$r$ monomials $u^r,u^{r-1}v,\ldots,v^r$ generate $\mathfrak m^r$, and their images form a [basis](basis.md) of $\mathfrak m^r/\mathfrak m^{r+1}$, so no smaller generating list exists. These [ideals](ideal.md) are $\mathfrak m$-primary: if $ab\in\mathfrak m^r$ and $a\notin\mathfrak m^r$, then $b$ cannot be a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md); hence $b\in\mathfrak m$ and $b^r\in\mathfrak m^r$. Consequently [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) bounds the minimum over all maximal-primary [ideals](ideal.md), because the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) itself is eligible, but does not bound the number of generators of each such [ideal](ideal.md).
