# Roche-lobe radius response exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer](dynamical-stability-of-binary-mass-transfer.md)

For [conservative binary mass transfer](conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md) and $R_L\propto a(M_d/M)^{1/3}$,

$$
\zeta_L=2q-\frac53,\qquad q=\frac{M_d}{M_a}.
$$

The [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $d\log a/d\log M_d=-2(1-q)$. Since a mass loss has $d\log M_d<0$, the donor moves inside its lobe when $\zeta_{\rm ad}>\zeta_L$. An ideal fully convective donor with $\zeta_{\rm ad}=-1/3$ is therefore stable for $q<2/3$ and unstable for $q>2/3$ under these assumptions.
