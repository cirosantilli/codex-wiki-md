# Negation introduction rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

If a [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) subproof derives [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md) from a temporary assumption $P$, discharge that assumption to conclude $\neg P$. Other undischarged assumptions remain available. This gives a constructive proof of a [negation](negation.md) and does not require [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md).
