# Strong duality

↑ **Parent:** [Weak duality](weak-duality.md)

Strong duality means that the primal and dual optimal values are equal. It holds for every feasible bounded [linear program](linear-programming.md) and for broad classes of [convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md) problems under a constraint qualification.
