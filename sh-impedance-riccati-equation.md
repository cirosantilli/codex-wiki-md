# SH impedance Riccati equation

↑ **Parent:** [SH input impedance](sh-input-impedance.md)

With $e^{ikx-i\omega t}$ phasors, positive depth $z$, [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu=\rho\beta^2$, vertical [traction](traction.md) $T$ and particle [velocity](velocity.md) $V$, the shear-wave equations give $V'=-i\omega T/\mu$ and $T'=-i\omega\mu p_\beta^2V$, where $p_\beta^2=\beta^{-2}-k^2/\omega^2$. Define $Z=-T/V$ wherever $V\ne0$. Differentiation gives the displayed [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md) and $V'=i\omega ZV/\mu$. A pole of $Z$ can be an ordinary zero of $V$, not a singular physical wavefield.
