# Logarithmically divergent derivative at a regular singular endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Regular singular point](regular-singular-point.md)

For $xy''+a(x)y=0$ with analytic coefficient and a continuous solution having $a(0)y(0)\ne0$, the equation gives $y''\sim-a(0)y(0)/x$. Integrating towards the endpoint gives the displayed logarithmic divergence of the [derivative](derivative.md). A bounded solution value therefore does not imply a finite [derivative](derivative.md) at a [regular singular point](regular-singular-point.md).
