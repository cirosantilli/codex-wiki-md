# Supersymmetric vacuum manifold of a bilinear hybrid superpotential

↑ **Parent:** [F-term hybrid inflation](f-term-hybrid-inflation.md)

For nonzero $g$ and $\zeta$, these field values give $W=0$ and all [Kähler covariant derivatives of a superpotential](kahler-covariant-derivative-of-a-superpotential.md) zero, hence zero [supergravity auxiliary fields](supergravity-auxiliary-field.md) and zero vacuum energy. With only the supplied chiral fields, the product condition gives a complex vacuum manifold rather than one isolated point. A specified gauged opposite-charge symmetry can add a [D-flatness](d-flatness.md) condition and a gauge quotient, but no such gauge sector should be invented from the field labels alone.
