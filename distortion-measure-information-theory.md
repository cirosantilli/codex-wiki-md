# Distortion measure (information theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Rate-distortion function](rate-distortion-function.md)

A [distortion measure](distortion-measure-information-theory.md) assigns a nonnegative cost to replacing a source symbol $x$ by a reconstruction $\hat x$. It need not be a [metric](metric.md): the two [alphabets](alphabet.md) may differ, and neither symmetry nor the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) is required. Choosing the cost is part of the model. For numerical symbols, $d(x,\hat x)=(x-\hat x)^2$ gives [mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md) after averaging; for exact symbol agreement, use [Hamming distortion](hamming-distortion.md).
