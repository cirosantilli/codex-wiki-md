# Zero-residue constraint for idempotent ultrafilters

↑ **Parent:** [Idempotent ultrafilter](idempotent-ultrafilter.md)

An [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) on the positive integers selects exactly one [residue class](residue-class.md) modulo a fixed positive integer $q$. Under [addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](addition-on-the-stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md), selected residues add. An [idempotent ultrafilter](idempotent-ultrafilter.md) must therefore select a residue $r$ with $2r=r$ in the finite cyclic group, hence $r=0$. In particular, the multiples of every fixed positive integer belong to every additive [idempotent ultrafilter](idempotent-ultrafilter.md).
