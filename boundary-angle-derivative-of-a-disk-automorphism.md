# Boundary-angle derivative of a disk automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism of the unit disk](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md)

For $|w|<1$, the [disk automorphism](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) $f_w(z)=(z-w)/(1-\overline wz)$ maps the boundary circle to itself. If $g(\theta)$ is a continuous argument of $w-e^{i\theta}$, direct conjugation gives $\phi_w(\theta)=-\theta+2g(\theta)$ modulo $2\pi$. Differentiating the argument gives

$$
g'(\theta)=\frac{1-\operatorname{Re}(we^{-i\theta})}{|w-e^{i\theta}|^2},
$$

and substitution yields the displayed positive derivative. Its integral over one circuit is $2\pi$, so the boundary map is orientation preserving of degree one. Transforming a uniform exit angle at the centre by its inverse gives the [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) density $(2\pi)^{-1}\phi_w'(\theta)$ for a [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started at $w$. This is the [Poisson kernel for the unit disk](poisson-kernel-for-the-unit-disk.md) with its probability normalization.
