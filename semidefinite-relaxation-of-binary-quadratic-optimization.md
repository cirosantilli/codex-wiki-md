# Semidefinite relaxation of binary quadratic optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md)

For real symmetric $A$, replace sign-vector lifts $xx^T$ by all [matrices](matrix.md) in the [elliptope](elliptope.md):

$$
\max_{X\succeq0,\ X_{ii}=1}\operatorname{tr}(AX).
$$

Every sign [vector](vector.md) gives a feasible rank-one [matrix](matrix.md) with the same objective, so the relaxed maximum is an upper bound. The omitted rank-one condition is substantive: an arbitrary feasible [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) need not come from signs.
