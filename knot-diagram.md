# Knot diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_diagram)

A knot diagram is a generic projection of a [knot](knot.md) to a plane together with overcrossing and undercrossing information at every double point.
