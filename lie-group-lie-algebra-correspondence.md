<h1 id="lie-group-lie-algebra-correspondence">Lie group–Lie algebra correspondence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

Differentiation sends [Lie groups](lie-group.md) to [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) and smooth [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) to bracket-preserving [linear maps](linear-map.md). On connected [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [Lie groups](lie-group.md), this is an equivalence with finite-dimensional real [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md): the [Lie third theorem](lie-third-theorem.md) gives existence, and [integration of a Lie algebra homomorphism](integration-of-a-lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) gives unique maps. General connected [groups](group-split.md) require discrete central quotient data, and disconnected [groups](group-split.md) additionally require component and conjugation data.
