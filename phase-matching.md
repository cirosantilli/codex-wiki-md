# Phase matching

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

A relation between [wave phases](phase-waves.md) that lets contributions from successive interaction regions add coherently. In [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md), the condition along the [particle](particle-physics.md) trajectory is $\omega=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v$, relating [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), [wave vector](wavevector.md) and particle [velocity](velocity.md).
