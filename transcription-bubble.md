# Transcription bubble

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription (biology)](transcription-biology.md)

The locally opened region of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in which [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) exposes a template for [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis. Its stability depends on base pairing, stacking and interactions with the enzyme; dye separation in a [FRET](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) reporter depends on the labelling geometry and need not change with the same sign for every reporter.
