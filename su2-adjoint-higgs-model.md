# SU2 adjoint Higgs model

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

An $SU(2)$ [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) with a real adjoint scalar and a vacuum $\phi=ve_3$ breaks to $U(1)$. The [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) gives two charged vectors mass $gv$, leaves one massless neutral vector, and gives the radial scalar mass $\sqrt{2\lambda}\,v$ for the quartic potential. Unlike the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md), this model has no additional hypercharge field and no massive neutral [Z boson](z-boson.md).
