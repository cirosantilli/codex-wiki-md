# Topological quantum matter

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_quantum_matter)

Topological quantum matter has robust low-energy properties controlled by global topology rather than local order parameters.

The central question is which [quantum states](quantum-state.md) can be joined by a smooth change of a local [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) while keeping a nonzero bulk [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md). A [Chern insulator](chern-insulator.md) makes this question concrete: two [band insulators](band-insulator.md) can have different integer [Chern numbers](chern-number.md), forcing a gap closure between them. The [Haldane phase](haldane-phase.md) supplies a one-dimensional example in which a protecting symmetry matters. These are distinct from [intrinsic topological order](intrinsic-topological-order.md), with its [anyons](anyon.md) and topology-dependent ground-state degeneracy. The [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md) concerns a separate mechanism involving [vortices](phase-vortex.md); topology organizes more than one kind of physical phenomenon.
