<h1 id="hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds">Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md)

For a compact Kähler manifold,

$$
H^k_{dR}(X;\mathbb C)=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}(X),
$$

and every class has a unique harmonic representative of each type.

This is the compact Kähler specialization of [Hodge theory](hodge-theory.md).
