# p-biased product measure

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md)

Under the $p$-biased product measure $\mu_p$ on $\{-1,1\}^n$, the coordinates are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with $\mathbb P(x_i=-1)=p$ and $\mathbb P(x_i=1)=1-p$. Writing $\mu=1-2p$, $\sigma=2\sqrt{p(1-p)}$, and $\phi_i=(x_i-\mu)/\sigma$, the products $\phi_S=\prod_{i\in S}\phi_i$ form the $p$-biased [Fourier basis](fourier-walsh-transform.md).
