# Saddle growth rate along a ray

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

On a ray $x/t=V$, a localized [wave packet](wave-packet.md) is governed by a physically selected [saddle point](saddle-point.md) satisfying $d\omega/dk=V$ in its analytic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). The exponent's real growth is $\operatorname{Im}(\omega_\star-Vk_\star)$. The fixed-frame case $V=0$ gives the [absolute growth rate](absolute-growth-rate.md). This ray formulation makes the frame dependence of [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md) explicit.
