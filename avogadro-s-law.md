<h1 id="avogadro-s-law">Avogadro's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md)

Equal [volumes](volume.md) of [ideal gases](ideal-gas-split.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) contain equal [amounts of substance](amount-of-substance.md). Named after [Amedeo Avogadro](amedeo-avogadro.md). From $pV=nRT$, $V/n=RT/p$ is common to them.
