# Poisson count change-point model with gamma priors

↑ **Parent:** [Change-point detection](change-point-detection.md)

For independent [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) with rate $\lambda$ up to an unknown index $m$ and rate $\phi$ after it, independent shape-rate [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) priors give the displayed [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) when $m$ is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ and $S_m=\sum_{i\leq m}x_i$. The model differs from a continuous-time [Poisson change-point posterior](poisson-change-point-posterior.md). The endpoint $m=N$ represents no observed change: the second segment is empty and its rate keeps its prior law.
