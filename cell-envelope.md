# Cell envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_envelope)

The [cell envelope](cell-envelope.md) comprises a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)'s surrounding boundary layers, including its [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) and a [cell wall](cell-wall.md) or additional outer structures when present. The term is especially useful when discussing [bacteria](bacteria.md), where the layers must be distinguished.
