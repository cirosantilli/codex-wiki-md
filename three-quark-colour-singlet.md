# Three-quark colour singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

The invariant colour state of three fundamental [quarks](quark.md) is the normalized completely antisymmetric tensor $\epsilon_{abc}/\sqrt6$. With this colour factor, the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) requires the remaining spatial-spin-flavour wavefunction to be symmetric. Colour and approximate [flavour symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) are distinct SU(3) actions.
