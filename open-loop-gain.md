# Open-loop gain

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-loop_gain)

The [open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md) of an [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) is the ratio of its output voltage to its differential input voltage before an external [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) connection is applied. A common small-signal model is $A(s)=A_0/(1+s/\omega_a)$, with a large direct-current gain $A_0$ and a dominant pole at $-\omega_a$. Above $\omega_a$ its magnitude falls approximately inversely with [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md).
