# Critical level of a shear-flow wave

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

For a wave with zonal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c$ in a basic shear $U(z)$, a critical level is a height $z_c$ at which $U(z_c)=c$. The Doppler-shifted material frequency then vanishes. This means equality of the laboratory velocities; the $U$ and $-c$ terms cancel in the wave frame. It does not mean $U=-c$. A [semi-infinite Eady edge wave](semi-infinite-eady-edge-wave.md) with zero interior [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) anomaly can remain regular through its steering height.
