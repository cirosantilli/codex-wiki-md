# Viscous-sheet drawing

↑ **Parent:** [Slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md)

Viscous-sheet drawing stretches a thin ribbon between rollers. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md), axial force balance, lateral [surface tension](surface-tension.md), and edge kinematics jointly determine its half-thickness, half-width, and axial speed.
