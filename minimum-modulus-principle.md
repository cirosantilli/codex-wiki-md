# Minimum modulus principle

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md)

A nonconstant [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) cannot have a positive interior local minimum of its modulus. Wherever it is nonzero, its reciprocal is holomorphic; a positive modulus minimum would give a modulus maximum of the reciprocal, contradicting the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md). Thus if a holomorphic function on a neighborhood of a closed disk has modulus at the center strictly smaller than its minimum boundary modulus, it has a zero inside the disk. Otherwise the [Cauchy estimate](cauchy-estimate.md) applied to its reciprocal would give the opposite inequality.
