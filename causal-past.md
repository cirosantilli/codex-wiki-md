# Causal past

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

The [causal past](causal-past.md) is the time-reversed [causal future](causal-future.md). Relative to a specified component $\mathcal I^+$ of [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md), the [black hole](black-hole-split.md) region is the complement of its [causal past](causal-past.md): $\mathcal B=\mathcal M\setminus J^-(\mathcal I^+)$.
