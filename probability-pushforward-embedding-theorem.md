# Probability pushforward embedding theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md)

A homeomorphic embedding of a [metric](metric.md) space induces a homeomorphic embedding of its [probability measures](probability-measure.md) for their weak topologies. The inverse continuity follows from the open-set [Portmanteau theorem](portmanteau-theorem.md), since every relatively [open set](open-set.md) is the trace of an ambient [open set](open-set.md). This does not give surjectivity onto $P(Z)$ when points of $Z$ are outside the embedded image.
