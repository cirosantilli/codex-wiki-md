# Cellulose microfibril

↑ **Parent:** [Cellulose](cellulose.md)

A [cellulose microfibril](cellulose-microfibril.md) is an organized assembly of [cellulose](cellulose.md) chains. Surface accessibility and chain packing help determine which glycosidic bonds enzymes can attack.
