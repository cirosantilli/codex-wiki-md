# Collisional de-excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional excitation](collisional-excitation.md)

A collision transfers an atom or ion from an excited level to a lower level while increasing the collider's [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). It competes with [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) and quenches line radiation when its rate is comparable to or larger than the radiative rate. The collision itself does not emit the line photon.
