# Continuous local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Local martingale](local-martingale.md)

A continuous local martingale is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md) whose sample paths are continuous almost surely. Its [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) determines much of its pathwise behaviour.
