# Radiation quadrupole

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-field moment](radiation-field-moment.md)

The angular multipole of order two in a local [radiation](radiation.md) intensity distribution. A monopole is isotropic and a dipole has one directional axis; a quadrupole has a second-order angular pattern. [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) of an incident [radiation quadrupole](radiation-quadrupole.md) can generate [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md), which is central to [Cosmic microwave background polarization](cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md).
