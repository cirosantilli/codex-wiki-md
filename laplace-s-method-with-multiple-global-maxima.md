<h1 id="laplace-s-method-with-multiple-global-maxima">Laplace's method with multiple global maxima</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md)

If a [Laplace integral](laplace-s-method.md) has finitely many separated nondegenerate global maxima with the same phase value, its leading term is the sum of their local Gaussian contributions. An interior maximum contributes a full Gaussian integral, while a [stationary endpoint maximum](laplace-s-method-at-a-nondegenerate-stationary-endpoint.md) contributes half of one.
