# Stratospheric polar vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Stratosphere](stratosphere.md)

A wintertime region of strong circumpolar flow that can isolate polar stratospheric air and sustain cold conditions. Isolation, [cloud](cloud.md) formation, sunlight return and subsequent breakup jointly influence seasonal [ozone](ozone.md) chemistry. Cooling alone does not determine the full [ozone](ozone.md) evolution.
