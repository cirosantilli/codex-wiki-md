# Ozmidov length

↑ **Parent:** [Stable density stratification](stable-density-stratification.md)

Equating an inertial eddy turnover time to the inverse [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) gives the scale separating buoyancy-affected motions from a smaller approximately isotropic cascade. It is distinct from the viscous [Kolmogorov microscales](kolmogorov-microscales.md). In very stable local surface-layer scaling it is proportional to the [Obukhov length](monin-obukhov-length.md), up to closure constants.
