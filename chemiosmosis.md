# Chemiosmosis

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemiosmosis)

[Chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md) couples transmembrane [ion](ion.md) movement down an [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) to a chemical transformation. For mitochondrial [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) synthesis, [protons](proton.md) return from the [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md) to the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md).[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps, Summary: inward proton flow driving ATP synthesis](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

An original thermodynamic example separates the electrical and chemical contributions. At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$, use ideal dilute [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md) $a$ and the [electric potential](electric-potential.md) $\psi$ on each side. The molar [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) difference for inward transfer of a singly charged [proton](proton.md) is

$$
\Delta\widetilde\mu_{\rm in}
=RT\ln\!\left(\frac{a_{\rm matrix}}{a_{\rm outside}}\right)
+F(\psi_{\rm matrix}-\psi_{\rm outside}).
$$

Here $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md), $F$ the [Faraday constant](faraday-constant.md), and $\ln$ the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md). The sign convention is destination minus origin: a negative difference makes inward transfer favourable. Both lower [proton](proton.md) activity and a more negative [electric potential](electric-potential.md) inside favour inward movement.

Stipulate $T=300\,\mathrm K$, $a_{\rm matrix}/a_{\rm outside}=0.1$, $\psi_{\rm matrix}-\psi_{\rm outside}=-0.12\,\mathrm V$, $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ and $F=96485\,\mathrm{C\,mol^{-1}}$. These illustrative inputs give approximately $-5.743$ and $-11.578\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ for the chemical and electrical terms, hence $-17.321\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ per transferred [proton](proton.md). For a hypothetical device coupling three inward [protons](proton.md) per [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) to an [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md)-formation cost of $45\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$,

$$
\Delta G_{\rm coupled}=45+3\Delta\widetilde\mu_{\rm in}
\approx-6.964\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

The sign follows by adding changes in the [state function](state-function.md) [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md). It establishes thermodynamic feasibility for this chosen model, not its [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md). Three is a stipulated coupling count, not a universal [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md); transport costs and leaks can also consume the available [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
