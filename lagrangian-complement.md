# Lagrangian complement

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md)

A [Lagrangian complement](lagrangian-complement.md) to $L$ in a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) is a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md) $K$ with $E=L\oplus K$. Such complements exist smoothly for Lagrangian subbundles. Identify any initial complement with $L^*$ using the symplectic cross-pairing. If its internal pairing is $b(\eta,\zeta)$, correct its lift by a vector $a\eta\in L$ with $\zeta(a\eta)=-b(\eta,\zeta)/2$. The corrected complement has zero internal pairing.
