# Kernel of a directed graph

↑ **Parent:** [Directed graph](directed-graph.md)

A digraph kernel is an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) $K$ such that each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) outside $K$ has a [directed edge](directed-edge.md) to a vertex in $K$. A directed cycle of even length has a kernel, obtained by taking alternate vertices; a directed triangle has none. The [kernel-perfect directed graph](kernel-perfect-directed-graph.md) property requires existence of such a set in every [induced subgraph](induced-subgraph.md).
