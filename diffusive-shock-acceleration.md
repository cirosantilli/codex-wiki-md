# Diffusive shock acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Fermi acceleration](first-order-fermi-acceleration.md)

[Diffusive shock acceleration](diffusive-shock-acceleration.md) repeatedly returns particles to a [shock wave](shock-wave.md) by scattering them in the upstream and downstream [plasma](plasma-physics.md). For fluid [speeds](speed.md) $u_1>u_2$, particle [speed](speed.md) $v\gg u_1$, and [shock compression ratio](shock-compression-ratio.md) $r=u_1/u_2$, the cycle gain is $\langle\Delta p\rangle/p=4(u_1-u_2)/(3v)$ and the cycle escape probability is $4u_2/v$. Their ratio gives $f(p)\propto p^{-3r/(r-1)}$ for the isotropic phase-space [one-particle distribution function](one-particle-distribution-function.md). For $r=4$ this is $p^{-4}$, corresponding to $dN/dE\propto E^{-2}$ in the ultrarelativistic regime.
