# Conduction-electron-mediated exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md)

A localized [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) can polarize a metal's [conduction electrons](conduction-electron.md), whose response changes the energy of a second moment. This indirect [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) can be long ranged and oscillate in sign with separation because of the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). In the three-dimensional isotropic free-electron asymptotic limit its range dependence is proportional to $\cos(2k_Fr)/r^3$, with coupling-dependent prefactor and sign convention. The original calculation for nuclear moments used their contact [hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md) with conduction electrons; it was motivated by nuclear magnetic resonance line widths in metallic silver.[1954 abstract and introduction: second-order hyperfine coupling, indirect spin interaction and broad natural-silver resonance lines](indirect-exchange-coupling-of-nuclear-magnetic-moments-by-conduction-electrons-by-m-a-ruderman-and-charles-kittel.md)
