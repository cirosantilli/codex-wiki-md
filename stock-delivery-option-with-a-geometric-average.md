# Stock-delivery option with a geometric average

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete geometric average under the stock-numeraire measure](discrete-geometric-average-under-the-stock-numeraire-measure.md)

A delivery $S_T\max(r,c/A)$ has value $S_0[r\Phi(d)+ce^{-m+v/2}\Phi(-d+\sqrt v)]$, where $m,v$ are the mean and [variance](variance-split.md) of $\log A$ under the [stock-numeraire measure in the Black-Scholes model](stock-numeraire-measure-in-the-black-scholes-model.md), and $d=(m-\log(c/r))/\sqrt v$. Split the normal integral at $\log(c/r)$ and complete the square in $e^{-y}$ times its [normal probability density](normal-density.md).
