# Secondary flow

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity field](velocity-field.md)

A secondary flow is a usually smaller velocity component superposed on a primary flow selected by symmetry or a simplified force balance. Examples include meridional circulation around a rotating sphere, or transverse circulation in a curved channel. Its direction depends on the forcing mechanism: elastic [normal stresses](normal-stress.md) and inertial effects can produce opposite circulations.
