# 2010

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the approximation theory and finite element methods course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to both sections and all six questions of the Approximation Theory and Finite Element Methods paper sat on 10 June 2010. Source: [released Mathematics papers](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), `Part C 2007 11.pdf`. Question 1 gives the sharp bound for the printed functional; question 4 distinguishes the printed normal condition from the conormal condition needed for the requested symmetric variational construction. Statements are not reproduced.
