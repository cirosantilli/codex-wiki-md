<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Taking $w=z$ in the dual problem, and using $e^x\geq1$, gives

$$
\|z\|_{H^1}^2\leq a(z,z)=\int_0^1z\psi
\leq\|z\|_2\|\psi\|_2\leq\|z\|_{H^1}\|\psi\|_2.
$$

Hence $\|z\|_{H^1}\leq\|\psi\|_2$. Testing with smooth compactly supported functions identifies $-z''+e^xz=\psi$ in [distributions](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Therefore $z''\in L^2$ and $z\in H^2(0,1)$, with

$$
\boxed{\|z''\|_2\leq e\|z\|_2+\|\psi\|_2\leq(e+1)\|\psi\|_2.}
$$

[integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) with arbitrary endpoint test values also gives the natural [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) $z'(0)=z'(1)=0$.
