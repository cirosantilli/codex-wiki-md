<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume $A$ is nonempty, as is necessary for an approximating point to exist. The [reverse triangle inequality](../../../../../reverse-triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
|d(u,q)-d(u,r)|\leq d(q,r).
$$

Thus the distance from the fixed point $u$ is a continuous, indeed [Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md), real-valued function on $A$. A [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) on a nonempty [compact set](../../../../../compact-space.md) attains its [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), so some $p\in A$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{d(u,p)=\min_{q\in A}d(u,q)=\inf_{q\in A}d(u,q).}
$$

If empty compact sets are allowed, the printed statement needs this nonemptiness qualification.
