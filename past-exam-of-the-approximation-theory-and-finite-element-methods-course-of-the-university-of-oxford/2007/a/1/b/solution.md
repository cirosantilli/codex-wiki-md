<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) contains zero, whose approximation error is $\|u\|$. For any $q$ with $\|q\|>2\|u\|$, the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives $\|u-q\|\geq\|q\|-\|u\|>\|u\|$, so such points cannot improve on zero.

Restrict to $K=\{q\in A:\|q\|\leq2\|u\|+1\}$. This is nonempty, closed and bounded in a finite-dimensional [normed space](../../../../../normed-vector-space-split.md), hence compact. The [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $q\mapsto\|u-q\|$ attains a [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) on $K$ by part (a). Outside $K$ its value exceeds the error at zero, so that minimizer is best on all of $A$:

$$
\boxed{\|u-p\|\leq\|u-q\|\quad(q\in A).}
$$

Completeness of the ambient space is unnecessary; [compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) is used only in the finite-dimensional [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md).
