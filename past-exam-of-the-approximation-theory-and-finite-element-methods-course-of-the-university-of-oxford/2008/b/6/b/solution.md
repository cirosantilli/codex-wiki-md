<h1 id="b/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For smooth $u$, [Green's first identity](../../../../../green-s-first-identity.md) rewrites the weak equation as

$$
\int_\Omega(-\Delta u+u-f)v+\int_\Gamma\partial_nu\,v=0.
$$

Compactly supported test [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) yield $-\Delta u+u=f$. Since the test space is the full $H^1(\Omega)$ and has unrestricted boundary trace, the remaining identity gives the homogeneous Neumann condition.

For a merely [weak solution](../../../../../weak-solution.md) on a polygon, the normal [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is understood as its weak flux trace. Here $\nabla u\in L^2$ and $\operatorname{div}\nabla u=u-f\in L^2$, so this trace is defined through Green's formula. It vanishes by the same identity. Thus **the natural [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) is $\partial_nu=0$**, without assuming global $H^2$ regularity on an arbitrary polygon.
