# Metric squared-loss two-point bound

↑ **Parent:** [Le Cam two-point lemma](le-cam-two-point-lemma.md)

For any [estimator](estimator.md) $T$ in a [metric](metric.md) parameter space, $d(T,f)^2+d(T,g)^2\geq d(f,g)^2/2$ by the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Average the two [risk functions](risk-function.md) and replace their [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md) by their minimum. This proves $\max(R_f,R_g)\geq d(f,g)^2\int\min(p_f,p_g)/4$. The overlap equals one minus [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md). The same proof applies to squared error for a real-valued [statistical functional](statistical-functional.md) of the parameter, without requiring that functional to be injective.
