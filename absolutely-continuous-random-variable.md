# Absolutely continuous random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous random variable](continuous-random-variable.md)

A [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f$, so its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is $F(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f(t)\,dt$. Equivalently, its law is an [absolutely continuous probability distribution](absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md). In particular, the [probability](probability.md) of every singleton is zero.
