# Germ of a sheaf section

↑ **Parent:** [Stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md)

The germ $s_x$ of a section $s\in\mathcal F(U)$ is its image in the [stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F_x$. It is zero exactly when $s$ restricts to zero on some neighbourhood of $x$.
