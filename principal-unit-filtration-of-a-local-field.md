# Principal-unit filtration of a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field](multiplicative-group-of-a-non-archimedean-local-field.md)

The groups $U_m$ form a compact-open neighbourhood basis at one inside the units of a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md). A [smooth character](smooth-character.md) trivial on $U_{i+1}$ but nontrivial on $U_i$ cannot have a nonzero fixed vector under $U_m$ for $m\le i$. This elementary conductor comparison controls admissibility and smooth duals of direct sums.
