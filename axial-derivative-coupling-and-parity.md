# Axial derivative coupling and parity

↑ **Parent:** [Axial current](axial-current.md)

For an ordinary [scalar field](scalar-field.md), its derivative is a polar covector, while the [axial current](axial-current.md) is a pseudovector. Their contraction is invariant under the [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md) but changes sign under [parity](parity.md). If the field is a [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md), the extra transformation sign makes this coupling parity even. In four spacetime dimensions its [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) is five, so Lorentz invariance alone does not imply power-counting [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md).
