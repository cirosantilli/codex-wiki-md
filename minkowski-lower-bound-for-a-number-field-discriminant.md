# Minkowski lower bound for a number-field discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's bound](minkowski-s-bound.md)

Since every nonzero integral ideal has norm at least one, the [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md) implies

$$
|d_K|\geq
\left(\frac\pi4\right)^{2r_2}
\left(\frac{n^n}{n!}\right)^2
$$

for every number field of degree $n$ and signature $(r_1,r_2)$.
