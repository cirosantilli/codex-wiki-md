# Thom space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

For a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) over a compact base, its Thom space is the quotient of its disk bundle by its [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md), with the collapsed sphere bundle as basepoint. Equivalently it is the one-point compactification of the total space. An oriented bundle's [Thom class](thom-class.md) and Thom isomorphism identify the reduced cohomology of this space with shifted cohomology of the base.
