# Type-II superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

A [type-II superconductor](type-ii-superconductor.md) can admit [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) through [quantized vortices](quantum-vortex.md) while retaining [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) between their cores. Its [superconducting mixed state](superconducting-mixed-state.md) lies between the [lower critical magnetic field](lower-critical-magnetic-field.md) and [upper critical magnetic field](upper-critical-magnetic-field.md). These are equilibrium bulk distinctions; sample shape, [flux pinning](flux-pinning.md) and preparation affect a measured magnetic response.

The motivating thin-film experiments changed an apparently minor preparation detail. [Nikolay Zavaritskii](nikolay-zavaritskii.md) evaporated metal onto glass; [Alexander Shalnikov](alexander-shalnikov.md) proposed cooling the substrate to helium [temperatures](temperature-split.md) to suppress agglomeration. The resulting critical-field behavior no longer matched the small-parameter approximation. [Alexei Alexeyevich Abrikosov](alexei-alexeyevich-abrikosov.md) found a different regime within the same theory, rather than discarding its equations.[p.975, film preparation and the second group](nobel-lecture-type-ii-superconductors-and-the-vortex-lattice-by-alexei-alexeyevich-abrikosov.md)
