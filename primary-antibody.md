# Primary antibody

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

In an indirect [Western blot](western-blot.md), the [primary antibody](primary-antibody.md) binds an [epitope](epitope.md) on the proposed target [protein](protein.md). Its binding must be tested in the actual sample and assay conditions through [Western blot antibody specificity controls](western-blot-antibody-specificity-control.md).
