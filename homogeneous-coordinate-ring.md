# Homogeneous coordinate ring

↑ **Parent:** [Projective variety](projective-variety.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_coordinate_ring)

For $X=V_+(I)\subseteq\mathbb P^n$, the homogeneous coordinate ring is the [graded ring](graded-ring.md) $k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]/I$.
