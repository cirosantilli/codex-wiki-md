↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the sensing and perceiving light, colour and materials course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all six essay options in the [sensing and perceiving light, colour and materials course of the University of Oxford](../sensing-and-perceiving-light-colour-and-materials-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination of 26 May 2022. Both printed parts of question 5 have separate solutions; its combined answer and each other essay are below 1,800 words. The scientific evidence used predates the examination. Source release: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member a16861w1.pdf.
