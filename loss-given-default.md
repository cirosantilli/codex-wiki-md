# Loss given default

↑ **Parent:** [Credit default](credit-default.md)

Loss given default is the loss conditional on a default, measured as an amount or as a fraction of exposure under the chosen convention. In an independent marked default model the loss amounts are independent marks attached to the events of a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md). Their raw second moment, not just their variance, controls the aggregate [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) variance.
