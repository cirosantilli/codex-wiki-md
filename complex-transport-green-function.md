# Complex transport Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)

For $L_k=k\partial_z+k^{-1}\partial_{\bar z}$, $k\ne0$, $|k|\ne1$, this kernel satisfies $L_kG_k=\delta_0$. A real-linear change of complex coordinate reduces its derivation to the [Cauchy-Green operator](cauchy-green-operator.md); the determinant changes sign at the unit circle. For $|k|=1$ the operator becomes a real directional [transport equation](transport-equation.md), whose characteristic singularity requires limiting prescriptions. The kernel has $G_k\sim k/(\pi z)$ at zero and $G_k\sim1/(\pi k\bar z)$ at infinity.
