# Psychological resilience

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

Successful adaptation in the context of significant [adversity](adversity.md), specified by outcome domain, developmental context and time. It is not simply the absence of disorder in an unexposed person, a universally favorable [personality](personality.md) profile or proof of invulnerability. Capacities and resources proposed to explain adaptation should be measured separately from the outcome they are meant to explain.[2000, definition and multidimensionality](the-construct-of-resilience-by-suniya-s-luthar-and-others.md)
