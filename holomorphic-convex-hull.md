# Holomorphic convex hull

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md)

For [compact](compact-space.md) $K$ in a plane domain $\Omega$, its [holomorphic convex hull](holomorphic-convex-hull.md) is

$$
\widehat K_{\Omega}=\{w\in\Omega:|f(w)|\le\sup_K|f|\text{ for every }f\in\mathcal O(\Omega)\}.
$$

It is $K$ together with the components of $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus K$ lying entirely in $\Omega$. Inclusion of those components follows from the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md). For exclusion, suppose $w$ lies in a complementary component meeting $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\Omega$. Choose an allowed [pole](pole.md) there. The open-and-closed resolvent argument in the proof of [Runge theorem](runge-s-theorem.md) approximates $(z-w)^{-1}$ uniformly on $K$ by functions $h$ [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) on $\Omega$. Then $1-(z-w)h(z)$ has value one at $w$ and arbitrarily small modulus on $K$, separating $w$ from the hull. For $\Omega=\mathbb C$, this is the [polynomial hull in one complex variable](polynomial-hull-in-one-complex-variable.md).
