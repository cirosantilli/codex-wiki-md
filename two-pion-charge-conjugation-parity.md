# Two-pion charge-conjugation parity

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

For a neutral [pion](pion.md) pair in a state of definite relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$, [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) interchanges $\pi^+$ and $\pi^-$. Relabelling the particle slots reverses the relative momentum and contributes $(-1)^L$. The individual charged-pion phase conventions cancel in the pair. Thus a neutral vector [rho meson](rho-meson.md) decaying strongly to a pion pair has $L=1$ and $C=-1$. A charged [rho meson](rho-meson.md) is taken to the state of opposite [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), so it is not itself a charge-conjugation eigenstate.
