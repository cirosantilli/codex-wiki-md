# Scalar Gauss equation

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

For the convention $K_{\mu\nu}=-\perp^\rho{}_\mu\nabla_\rho n_\nu$, contraction of the [Gauss equation](gauss-equation.md) gives

$$
{}^{(4)}R+2R_{\mu\nu}n^\mu n^\nu
={}^{(3)}R+K^2-K_{\mu\nu}K^{\mu\nu}.
$$
