# Syllable in a free product

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form theorem for a free product](normal-form-theorem-for-a-free-product.md)

A syllable is a nonidentity element of one factor in a [free product](free-product.md) word. A reduced normal form has successive syllables in different factors; its syllable length is the number of these elements. This length records factor elements, rather than letters in a chosen generating set.
