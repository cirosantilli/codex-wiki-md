# Inhibitory-learning exposure strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

A strategy intended to strengthen [learning](learning.md) that competes with a threat association and improve its later retrieval. Expectancy testing and varied contexts are examples. A coherent [learning](learning.md) rationale does not itself demonstrate the optimal clinical implementation for every developmental group.[Behaviour Research and Therapy 58, 10–23; theoretical framework and illustrative implementation strategies](maximizing-exposure-therapy-an-inhibitory-learning-approach-by-michelle-g-craske-and-others.md)
