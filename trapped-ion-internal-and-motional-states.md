# Trapped-ion internal and motional states

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped-ion quantum control](trapped-ion-quantum-control.md)

An internal [qubit](qubit.md) uses two distinguishable electronic or [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) [quantum states](quantum-state.md), denoted $|g\rangle,|e\rangle$. The [ion](ion.md)'s centre-of-mass motion has a different [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). In one approximately harmonic direction, a [number state](number-state.md) $|n\rangle$ has [energy](energy-split.md) $E_n=\hbar\nu(n+1/2)$, where $\nu$ denotes a motional [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), not ordinary [frequency](frequency.md). The [tensor product of quantum systems](tensor-product-of-quantum-systems-split.md) gives $|g,n\rangle$. Cooling towards $n=0$ prepares motion; it does not automatically prepare the internal [qubit](qubit.md). Nor does the [ground state](ground-state.md) mean a stationary point particle: [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) and position spread remain.

For several [ions](ion.md), [Coulomb forces](coulomb-force.md) couple their displacements into collective [normal modes](normal-mode.md). Each selected [normal mode](normal-mode.md) has its own [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [number states](number-state.md), while each [ion](ion.md) retains internal [qubit](qubit.md) states. A pulse addressing one [ion](ion.md) can change a shared mode. This supplies a route to correlations between distant internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md); it also makes residual motional excitation and unwanted excitation of other [normal modes](normal-mode.md) relevant. A single-ion pulse calculation alone establishes no experimental multi-ion [quantum gate](quantum-logic-gate.md) performance.
