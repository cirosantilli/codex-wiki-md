# Fermi contours of an isotropic Rashba band

↑ **Parent:** [Rashba effect](rashba-effect.md)

For the [Rashba effect](rashba-effect.md) with $k_R>0$, put $D=\sqrt{k_R^2+2mE_F/\hbar^2}$. If $-E_R<E_F<0$, both [Fermi contours](fermi-contour.md) belong to the lower branch and bound the singly occupied [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $k_R-D<k<k_R+D$; the central disk and exterior are empty. At $E_F=0$, the lower occupied disk has outer radius $2k_R$, while the origin is a degenerate point at the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). For $E_F>0$, the lower [Fermi contour](fermi-contour.md) has radius $k_-=D+k_R$ and the upper [Fermi contour](fermi-contour.md) radius $k_+=D-k_R$. The inner disk has two occupied spin-split [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) at each [momentum](momentum-split.md), the intervening [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) has one, and the exterior is empty. Consequently $k_--k_+=2k_R$, whereas below the crossing the two lower-branch radii add to $2k_R$. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), changing occupation values only on these [Fermi contours](fermi-contour.md) affects a [null set](null-set.md) and leaves the integrated [carrier density](carrier-density.md) unchanged.
