# Local ring of a smooth algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md)

At every point of a [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md), the [local ring](local-ring.md) is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md). Its valuation measures the [order of vanishing](order-of-vanishing.md) of a [rational function](rational-function.md) at that point.
