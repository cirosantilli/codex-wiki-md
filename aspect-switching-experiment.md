# Aspect switching experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bell test](bell-test.md)

In 1982, [Alain Aspect](alain-aspect.md), [Jean Dalibard](jean-dalibard.md) and [Gérard Roger](gerard-roger.md) used a [calcium](calcium.md) cascade and an acoustic [standing wave](standing-wave.md) in [water](water.md) to route each [photon](photon.md) toward one of two fixed [polarizers](polarizer.md). Switching took about $10\,\mathrm{ns}$, against $40\,\mathrm{ns}$ for light to cross between stations. [Photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md) [coincidence circuit](coincidence-circuit.md) rates, with delayed-background subtraction and [polarizer](polarizer.md)-removal normalization, violated their [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md). Supplementary [polarizers](polarizer.md) checked each channel against [Malus's law](malus-s-law.md). The timing attacked the [locality loophole](locality-loophole.md), but the authors emphasized that switching was quasiperiodic, not random; incomplete switching and inefficient collection also limited the test. Its normalized statistic differs from the [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) convention used below.[pp. 1805-1807, Figs. 2-4; final discussion](experimental-test-of-bell-s-inequalities-using-time-varying-analyzers-by-alain-aspect-and-others.md)
