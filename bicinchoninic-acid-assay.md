# Bicinchoninic acid assay

↑ **Parent:** [Analytical chemistry](analytical-chemistry.md)

An assay that estimates [protein](protein-split.md) [concentration](concentration.md) from a colour-producing [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md), measured by [absorbance](absorbance.md) and compared with known [protein](protein-split.md) reference samples. [Bovine serum albumin](bovine-serum-albumin.md) is commonly used as the reference [protein](protein-split.md). The readout estimates [protein](protein-split.md) content rather than identifying each [protein](protein-split.md) in a [mixture](mixture-split.md).
