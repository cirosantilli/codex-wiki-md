# Momentum-shell decomposition of a scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md)

Split [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) modes into a low-momentum ball and its disjoint shell. The quadratic scalar action has no cross term between these supports. Integrating the shell at fixed low field defines the lower-cutoff [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md).
