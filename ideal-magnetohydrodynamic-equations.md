# Ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The smooth ideal-MHD system couples [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md), [momentum](momentum.md), adiabatic [pressure](pressure.md) transport and the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md), subject to the [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md). Without gravity it is a [quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md) system. Across shocks, conservative total energy replaces smooth adiabatic [pressure](pressure.md) transport.
