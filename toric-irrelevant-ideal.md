# Toric irrelevant ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Cox construction](cox-construction.md)

In the [Cox ring](cox-ring.md) $S=k[z_\rho]$, the irrelevant ideal is generated by $\prod_{\rho\notin\sigma(1)}z_\rho$ as $\sigma$ runs over maximal cones. The [Cox construction](cox-construction.md) takes the quotient of $\operatorname{Spec}S\setminus V(B_\Sigma)$ by the diagonalizable group whose character group is the divisor class group.
