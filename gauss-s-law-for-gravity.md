<h1 id="gauss-s-law-for-gravity">Gauss's law for gravity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

The outward [flux](flux.md) of the [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) through a closed surface is $-4\pi G$ times the enclosed [mass](mass.md). A sphere around a [point mass](point-mass.md) establishes the coefficient; [superposition](superposition-principle.md) extends the integral law to general [mass distributions](mass-distribution.md). The minus sign expresses attractive gravity. A symmetric [Gaussian surface](gaussian-surface.md) can turn the flux relation into a local field calculation, but the integral law alone does not imply that the field is uniform on an arbitrary surface.
