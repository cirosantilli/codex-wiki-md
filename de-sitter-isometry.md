# de Sitter isometry

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

Four-dimensional [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) has ten independent isometries. In the expanding flat slicing they act on the spatial boundary as three translations, three rotations, one dilation, and three special conformal transformations.
