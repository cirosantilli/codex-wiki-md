# Electron cryotomography

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[Electron cryotomography](electron-cryotomography.md) reconstructs three-dimensional electron-scattering density from a tilt series of a vitrified specimen. It can reveal native cellular assemblies such as receptor arrays, subject to dose, sampling and resolution limits.
