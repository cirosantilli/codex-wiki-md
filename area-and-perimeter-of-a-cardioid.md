# Area and perimeter of a cardioid

↑ **Parent:** [Cardioid](cardioid.md)

For $a>0$, the [cardioid](cardioid.md) $r=a(1+\cos\theta)$ traces its boundary once over $0\leq\theta\leq2\pi$. The [polar area element](polar-area-element.md) gives

$$
\mathcal A=\frac{a^2}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}(1+\cos\theta)^2d\theta=\frac{3\pi a^2}{2}.
$$

The [polar arc length](polar-arc-length.md) integrand is $2a|\cos(\theta/2)|$. Splitting at the cusp parameter $\theta=\pi$ gives perimeter $8a$. The powers of $a$ distinguish the scaling of [area](surface-area.md) and [length](length.md).
