# Thermonuclear runaway

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

Temperature-sensitive [nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md) can heat material faster than cooling and expansion can regulate it, accelerating the burning. In a [classical nova](classical-nova.md), [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) accumulated on a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) ignites; initial [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) helps suppress the usual expansion response. The eruption can eject the envelope without destroying the underlying [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md).
