# Right Riemann sum

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md)

A right [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) on a partition $a=x_0<\cdots<x_N=b$ samples each subinterval at its right endpoint. For a continuous [function](function-split.md) its limit as the mesh tends to zero is the [integral](integral-split.md), by the [Riemann sum convergence theorem](riemann-sum-convergence-theorem.md). On a uniform partition of width $h$, comparison of endpoint weights in the [composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md) gives $R_N-T_N=h(f(b)-f(a))/2$. Thus $N^{-5}\sum_{j=1}^N j^4$ is a right [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) for $\int_0^1x^4\,dx$, and its limit is $1/5$.
