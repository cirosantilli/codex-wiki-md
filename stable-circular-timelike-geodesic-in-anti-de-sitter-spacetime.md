# Stable circular timelike geodesic in anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Static coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](static-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

In [static coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](static-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md), write $f=1+r^2/L^2$, conserved [energy](energy-split.md) per unit rest [mass](mass.md) $E=f\dot t$, and conserved specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $h=r^2\dot\phi$. Timelike normalization gives $\dot r^2=E^2-V_{\rm eff}$ with $V_{\rm eff}=f(1+h^2/r^2)$. A circular [geodesic](geodesic.md) at $r_0>0$ obeys $V_{\rm eff}'=0$, so $h^2=r_0^4/L^2$ and $E=f(r_0)$. Thus $\dot t=1$ and $\dot\phi=\pm1/L$. Both its static-coordinate period and its own [proper time](proper-time.md) period are $2\pi L$, independent of radius. Stability follows from $V_{\rm eff}''(r_0)=8/L^2>0$. A static clock at the orbital radius measures the different period $2\pi L\sqrt{f(r_0)}$, which does depend on radius.
