# Opposite tapping task

↑ **Parent:** [Inhibitory control](inhibitory-control.md)

An experimenter taps once or twice and the [child](child.md) must produce the opposite count. The task taxes [inhibition](inhibitory-control.md) of [imitation](imitation.md) together with maintenance of the response rule; its score is not a process-pure measure.[Measures: peg-tapping inhibitory-control task](relating-effortful-control-executive-function-and-false-belief-understanding-to-emerging-math-and-literacy-ability-in-kindergarten-by-clancy-blair-and-rachel-peters-razza.md)
