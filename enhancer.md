# Enhancer

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

A regulatory [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) region that can increase [gene expression](gene-expression.md) in a suitable cellular context through interactions with regulatory machinery. The specificity of an [enhancer](enhancer.md) can permit [cis-regulatory evolution](cis-regulatory-evolution.md) of one developmental deployment.
