# Pentamethylcyclopentadienyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclopentadienyl ligand](cyclopentadienyl-ligand.md)

The [pentamethylcyclopentadienyl ligand](pentamethylcyclopentadienyl-ligand.md) is the anion $\mathrm{C_5Me_5^-}$, normally a six-[electron](electron.md) $\eta^5$ donor in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md). Its methyl substituents change [steric hindrance](steric-hindrance.md) and [electron](electron.md) donation relative to an unsubstituted [cyclopentadienyl ligand](cyclopentadienyl-ligand.md).
