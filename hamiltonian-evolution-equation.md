# Hamiltonian evolution equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian field equation](hamiltonian-field-equation.md)

An evolution equation has Hamiltonian form when a Hamiltonian operator applied to the [variational derivative](variational-derivative.md) of a functional gives its time [derivative](derivative.md). The induced functional bracket must satisfy the Poisson properties, including Jacobi.
