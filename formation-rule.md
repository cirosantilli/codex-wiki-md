# Formation rule

↑ **Parent:** [Formal grammar](formal-grammar.md)

A [formation rule](formation-rule.md) specifies how well-formed expressions can be constructed from simpler expressions in a [formal language](formal-language.md). The [formal grammar](formal-grammar.md) of [propositional formulas](propositional-formula.md) has rules for [propositional variables](propositional-variable.md), [negation](negation.md) and binary [logical implication](logical-implication.md).
