# Free modules are flat

↑ **Parent:** [Flat module](flat-module.md)

For a [free module](free-module.md) $F=\bigoplus_{i\in J}Ae_i$, the natural map $U\otimes_AF\to\bigoplus_{i\in J}U$ sends $u\otimes\sum_i a_ie_i$ to $(a_iu)_i$ and is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), with inverse sending the tuple $(u_i)$ to $\sum_i u_i\otimes e_i$. For any [injection](injective-function.md) $U\hookrightarrow V$, its [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) with $F$ is therefore the coordinatewise [injection](injective-function.md) $\bigoplus_JU\hookrightarrow\bigoplus_JV$. Thus every [free module](free-module.md) is flat, including those of infinite rank.
