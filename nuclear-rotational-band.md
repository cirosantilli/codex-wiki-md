# Nuclear rotational band

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

A sequence of nuclear [energy levels](energy-level.md) interpreted as different total [angular momenta](angular-momentum.md) built on approximately the same intrinsic structure. For an axially symmetric, positive-[parity](parity.md), $K=0$ [ground state](ground-state.md) band of an [even-even nucleus](even-even-nucleus.md), the allowed sequence is $I=0,2,4,\ldots$. The simple constant-inertia rotor has

$$
H_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac{\widehat{\mathbf I}^{\,2}}{2\mathcal J},\qquad
\widehat{\mathbf I}^{\,2}|IM\rangle=\hbar^2I(I+1)|IM\rangle,\qquad
E_I-E_0=\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mathcal J}I(I+1).
$$

Here $\mathcal J$ is an effective [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md), and $\hbar$ the reduced [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). The even-$I$ restriction belongs to this particular intrinsic [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and band, rather than to all nuclear excitations. The [angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md) eigenvalue supplies $I(I+1)$, rather than the classical replacement $I^2$.

Set $\epsilon=E_2-E_0$. Since $2(2+1)=6$, the model predicts

$$
\frac{E_4-E_0}{\epsilon}=\frac{20}{6}=\frac{10}{3},\qquad
\frac{E_6-E_0}{\epsilon}=\frac{42}{6}=7.
$$

For a chosen model scale $\epsilon=90\,\mathrm{keV}$, the excitation [energies](energy-split.md) are $0,90,300,630\,\mathrm{keV}$. The corresponding successive [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) [energies](energy-split.md) are approximately $90,210,330\,\mathrm{keV}$, neglecting [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md). Level [energies](energy-split.md) and emitted line [energies](energy-split.md) are different lists. These numbers illustrate the formula and are not a measured [isotope](isotope.md) spectrum.

Original chosen limiting models, both normalized to the same first $2^+$ excitation. The [nuclear rotational band](nuclear-rotational-band.md) has $E(4)/E(2)=10/3$; the harmonic [nuclear quadrupole vibration](nuclear-quadrupole-vibration.md) has a two-quantum $0^+,2^+,4^+$ multiplet at twice the one-quantum [energy](energy-split.md). Arrows show selected [electric quadrupole transitions](electric-quadrupole-transition.md); their widths do not represent measured strengths. Higher vibrational multiplets are omitted.

The fitted $\mathcal J=3\hbar^2/\epsilon$ is a response parameter, not automatically the [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) of a classical rigid charge distribution. [Nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) changes this response; [Ben Roy Mottelson](ben-roy-mottelson.md) connected observed inertias with correlations beyond independent-particle motion.[continuation after p. 237, pairing correlations and Fig. 1 potential-energy surfaces](elementary-modes-of-excitation-in-the-nucleus-by-ben-roy-mottelson.md)

At larger [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), changing intrinsic structure or [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) can invalidate a single constant $\mathcal J$. A useful diagnostic is to compare $(E_I-E_0)/[I(I+1)]$ across several levels: constancy tests the simple formula, while a drift identifies the need for additional physics. A successful ratio alone neither specifies every microscopic motion nor measures a literal solid-body rotation.
