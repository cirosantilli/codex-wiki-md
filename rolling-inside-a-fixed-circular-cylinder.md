# Rolling inside a fixed circular cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A round body of radius $R_0$ in a fixed cylinder of radius $R_1$ has [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) path radius $a=R_1-R_0$. With orbital angle $\theta$ and spin [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\Omega$ both positive about the same axis, [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) gives $a\dot\theta+R_0\Omega=0$. For [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I=\gamma mR_0^2$, its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $\frac12ma^2(1+\gamma)\dot\theta^2$. Release from rest at angle $\alpha$ gives, by [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md), a descent time $\sqrt{1+\gamma}$ times that of frictionless sliding along the same [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) path. The spin sign reverses if its positive direction is chosen opposite to the orbital angle.
