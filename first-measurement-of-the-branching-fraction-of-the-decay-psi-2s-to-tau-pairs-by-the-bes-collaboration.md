<h1 id="first-measurement-of-the-branching-fraction-of-the-decay-psi-2s-to-tau-pairs-by-the-bes-collaboration">First Measurement of the Branching Fraction of the Decay psi(2S) to tau pairs by the BES Collaboration</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0010072](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0010072)

The experiment identifies [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) pairs with mixed [electron](electron.md)-[muon](muon.md) decays and missing [energy](energy-split.md), and subtracts a nonresonant continuum contribution. This provides a concrete implementation of a leptonic [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) selection near the charmonium threshold.
