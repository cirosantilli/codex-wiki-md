# Stokes resolvent kernel with advection

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial root splitting for the dispersive Stokes resolvent](spatial-root-splitting-for-the-dispersive-stokes-resolvent.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, let $r_-$ and $r_1,r_2$ be the [characteristic roots](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of $r^3+r+p=0$, split by the signs of their [real parts](real-part.md). Put $d_j=3r_j^2+1$. The real-line [Green function](green-s-function.md) of $p+\partial_x+\partial_x^3$ is

$$
R_p(s)=\begin{cases}e^{r_-s}/d_-,&s\ge0,\\-\sum_{j=1}^2e^{r_js}/d_j,&s<0.\end{cases}
$$

The [reciprocal-polynomial root identities](reciprocal-polynomial-root-identities.md) imply continuity of $R_p,R_p'$ and jump $R_p''(0+)-R_p''(0-)=1$. The [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) therefore gives $(p+\partial_s+\partial_s^3)R_p=\delta_0$. The transport term changes the roots: the pure [Airy resolvent kernel](airy-resolvent-kernel.md) with $r^3+p=0$ cannot be substituted unchanged.
