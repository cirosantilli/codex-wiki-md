# Cusp bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Codimension-two bifurcation](codimension-two-bifurcation.md)

A cusp is a two-parameter degeneracy at which two [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) curves meet and the reduced steady equation has a triple root. In the displayed scalar [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md), simultaneous vanishing of the polynomial and its derivative gives $\beta_2=3x^2$, $\beta_1=-2x^3$. Thus the folds satisfy $4\beta_2^3=27\beta_1^2$, with $\beta_2\geq0$. Between the folds there are three real [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), and outside them one. A transverse stable direction may be present in a higher-dimensional realization.
