# Spin-zero two-body helicity selection

↑ **Parent:** [Helicity suppression](helicity-suppression.md)

A spin-zero parent at rest decaying to two particles requires their helicities to be equal. Choosing particle 1 along $+z$ and particle 2 along $-z$ gives total projection $J_z=h_1-h_2=0$. In negative-[pion](pion.md) decay, the massless [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) has positive [helicity](helicity.md), so the negative charged [lepton](lepton.md) must also have positive helicity. This is the suppressed component for a left-chiral [weak charged current](charged-current.md).
