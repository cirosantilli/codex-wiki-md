# Acetylide

↑ **Parent:** [Alkyne](alkyne.md)

An anion formed by removing the terminal proton of an [alkyne](alkyne.md), $\mathrm{RC{\equiv}C^-}$. It can act as a [carbon](carbon.md) [nucleophile](nucleophile.md), for example in addition to a [ketone](ketone.md).
