# Brans-Dicke theory

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-tensor theory](scalar-tensor-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brans–Dicke_theory)

The [Brans-Dicke theory](brans-dicke-theory.md) adds a dynamical [scalar field](scalar-field.md) $\Phi$ to a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) theory of gravity, with constant dimensionless coupling $\omega$. In a $(+---)$ signature and the reversed-curvature convention used here, the displayed Lagrangian is supplemented by matter and integrated against the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) volume density with prefactor $1/(16\pi G)$. Its [scalar](scalar.md) multiplier of the [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) lets the [curvature](curvature.md) coupling vary in spacetime. Both the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and [scalar](scalar.md) are varied; the [scalar equation of Brans-Dicke theory](scalar-equation-of-brans-dicke-theory.md) follows by eliminating [curvature](curvature.md) between the [scalar](scalar.md) equation and the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) equation. The original construction is described in [Brans and Dicke's 1961 article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.124.925).
