# Erlang fixed point approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md)

The Erlang fixed point approximation treats resource blocking as independent and applies the [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md) to traffic screened by other resources. Its self-consistency equations have a unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md) under [fixed routing](fixed-routing.md), but can have several under [alternative routing](alternative-routing.md).
