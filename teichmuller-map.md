<h1 id="teichmuller-map">Teichmüller map</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller theory](teichmuller-theory.md)

A [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md) whose [Beltrami coefficient](beltrami-coefficient.md) is $k|q|/q$ for an integrable nonzero [holomorphic quadratic differential](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md) and constant $0<k<1$. In a [flat coordinate](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md) for $q$, it stretches the horizontal and vertical directions with ratio $K=(1+k)/(1-k)$. Positive multiples of $q$ define the same coefficient.
