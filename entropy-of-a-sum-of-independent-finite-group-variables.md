# Entropy of a sum of independent finite-group variables

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md)

If [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) $X,Y$ take values in a [finite additive group](finite-additive-group.md), then

$$
H(X+Y)\geq\max\{H(X),H(Y)\}.
$$

Indeed, $H(X+Y)\geq H(X+Y\mid Y)=H(X)$, and symmetrically for $Y$. Independence is essential: taking $Y=-X$ makes the sum constant.
