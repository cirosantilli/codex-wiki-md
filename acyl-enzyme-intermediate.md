# Acyl-enzyme intermediate

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A covalent [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) intermediate bearing an acyl group during catalysis. Its formation and breakdown can occur at different rates, producing [burst kinetics](burst-kinetics.md) if the first released [reaction product](product-chemistry.md) reports a fast step.
