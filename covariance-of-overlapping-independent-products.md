# Covariance of overlapping independent products

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance](covariance.md)

For mutually independent square-integrable [random variables](random-variable-split.md) $A,B,C$, factor the joint moment as $\mathbb E[AB^2C]=\mathbb EA\,\mathbb E[B^2]\,\mathbb EC$, then subtract the product of the two means. This proves the formula. Sharing one factor can therefore produce nonzero [covariance](covariance.md) even when all original factors are independent. Products of disjoint groups of independent factors are themselves [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md).
