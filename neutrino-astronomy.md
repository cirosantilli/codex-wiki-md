# Neutrino astronomy

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrino_astronomy)

[Neutrino astronomy](neutrino-astronomy.md) infers properties of astronomical sources from detected [neutrino](neutrino.md) reactions. [Solar neutrino detection](solar-neutrino-detection.md) probes ongoing [stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md); the [SN 1987A neutrino burst](sn-1987a-neutrino-burst.md) probes a brief episode of [core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md) evolution. A detector observes reaction products, so source inference depends on [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md), [neutrino flavor](neutrino-flavor.md), thresholds and [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md).
