# Solenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field.md)

A solenoid is a helical winding of conducting wire. For an ideal very long winding with $N$ turns per unit length and negligible pitch, [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives a uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $B=\mu_0NI$ inside and zero field outside. Finite-length end fields and the pitch correction are excluded by this approximation. The [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) of a length $l$ and cross-sectional area $A$ is $L=\mu_0N^2Al$.
