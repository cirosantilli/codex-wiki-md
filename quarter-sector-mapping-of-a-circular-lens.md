# Quarter-sector mapping of a circular lens

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

For the lens $\operatorname{Im}z>0$ and $|z+i|<\sqrt2$, the [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $w=(1+z)/(1-z)$ maps its real boundary segment to the positive real axis and its circular boundary arc to the ray with argument $\pi/4$. Its interior maps bijectively to $0<\arg w<\pi/4$. The fourth power therefore maps this lens conformally onto the upper half-plane. Composing with $(F-i)/(F+i)$ maps it onto the unit disc. The endpoints $\pm1$ are boundary points, so the pole and zero introduce no interior singularity or branching.
