# Oxo ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A coordinated oxide, formally $\mathrm O^{2-}$. A terminal $\mathrm{M{=}O}$ unit is conventionally counted as an $X_2$ ligand giving four [electrons](electron.md) in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md). Additional oxygen-to-metal [pi donation](pi-donation.md) is an orbital-bonding issue and must not be silently mixed with another counting convention.
