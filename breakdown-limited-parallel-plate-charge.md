# Breakdown-limited parallel-plate charge

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric breakdown](dielectric-breakdown.md)

For an ideal uniform [dielectric](dielectric.md) between large parallel plates, $Q=CV=(\epsilon A/d)(Ed)=\epsilon AE$. A prescribed breakdown field $E_{\rm bd}$ therefore limits charge independently of plate separation. [Voltage](voltage.md) capacity still grows with separation. This ideal result neglects field enhancement near edges and any dependence of the breakdown field on gap geometry or environmental conditions.
