# Island of a meromorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Ahlfors covering surface theory](ahlfors-covering-surface-theory.md)

An island over a [Jordan domain](jordan-domain.md) $D$ is a relatively compact component $U$ of its inverse image on which the [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) is a [proper map](proper-map.md) onto $D$. A component reaching the boundary of the source is instead a peninsula. The island has an integer mapping degree; degree one means that the map is a [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md). A simply connected island can have larger degree and need not be a simple island.
