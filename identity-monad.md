# Identity monad

↑ **Parent:** [Computational monad](computational-monad.md)

The [computational monad](computational-monad.md) whose result is the [identity function](identity-function.md) and whose sequencing is ordinary application. It adds no effects and provides a starting point for a stack of [monad transformers](monad-transformer.md).
