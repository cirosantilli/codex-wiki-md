# Frontier molecular orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

A [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) near the boundary between occupied and unoccupied levels. The [highest occupied molecular orbital](highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) and [LUMO](lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md) are central examples. Their energies, occupancies, spatial form and symmetry help predict which new [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) can form when fragments approach.
