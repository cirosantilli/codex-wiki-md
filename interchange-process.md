# Interchange process

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

Place one distinct label at each vertex of a finite connected weighted [graph](graph-split.md). At rate $c_{uv}=c_{vu}\geq0$, interchange the labels at $u$ and $v$. This [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) has a [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md) as its [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md). The position of one label is the [random walk](random-walk.md) with the same rates.
