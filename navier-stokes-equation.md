# Navier-Stokes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier–Stokes_equations)

For an incompressible Newtonian fluid of constant density and viscosity,

$$
\rho\left(\partial_tu+u\mathbin{\cdot}\nabla u\right)
=-\nabla p+\mu\nabla^2u+\rho f,
\qquad \nabla\mathbin{\cdot}u=0.
$$
