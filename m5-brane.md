# M5-brane

↑ **Parent:** [M-brane](m-brane.md)

The [M5-brane](m5-brane.md) has five spatial dimensions and carries the magnetic charge dual to the [M2-brane](m2-brane.md)'s electric three-form charge. Wrapping it on a compact cycle lowers its effective spatial dimension and multiplies its [brane tension](brane-tension.md) by the cycle volume.
