# Axisymmetric incompressible epicyclic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

For $k_y=0$ and $k_z\ne0$, [pressure](pressure.md) elimination gives $\dot v_x=2\Omega\chi v_y-\nu k^2v_x$ and $\dot v_y=-2(\Omega-A)v_x-\nu k^2v_y$, where $\chi=k_z^2/k^2$. The inviscid frequency is the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) times $\sqrt\chi$. With $w=v_x/\sqrt\chi$, $(\Omega-A)|w|^2+\Omega|v_y|^2$ is conserved when [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) vanishes. In a Keplerian flow it bounds kinetic-energy amplification by four, whereas with zero rotation the same system has the algebraic [lift-up effect](lift-up-effect.md).
