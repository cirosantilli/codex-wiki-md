# Generalized eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md)

A generalized eigenfunction solves the [eigenvalue equation](eigenvalue-equation.md) for a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) in an enlarged space, often a space of [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), although it may fail to belong to the underlying [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) $e^{ikx}$ on the whole [real line](real-line.md) is a generalized [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md), with [momentum](momentum.md) $\hbar k$, and is not [square-integrable](square-integrable-function.md). This usage differs from a [generalized eigenvector](generalized-eigenvector.md) in a [Jordan chain](jordan-chain.md), where a higher power of $L-\lambda$ vanishes rather than $L-\lambda$ itself.
