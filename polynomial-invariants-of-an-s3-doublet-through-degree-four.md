# Polynomial invariants of an S3 doublet through degree four

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant](polynomial-invariant.md)

Let the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_3$ act by $(x,y)\mapsto(\alpha x,\alpha^{-1}y)$ and $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$, where $\alpha^3=1$ and $\alpha\ne1$. Rotation invariance requires the exponents of each [monomial](monomial.md) to differ by a multiple of three. After symmetrizing, factor out a power of $xy$; the remainder is $x^{3k}+y^{3k}$. These power sums are [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in $x^3+y^3$ and $x^3y^3=(xy)^3$, by the recurrence $s_k=(x^3+y^3)s_{k-1}-(xy)^3s_{k-2}$. Hence the entire [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md) is generated by $xy$ and $x^3+y^3$, which are algebraically independent. Their degrees are two and three, giving one invariant in each of degrees two, three and four, and none in degree one.
