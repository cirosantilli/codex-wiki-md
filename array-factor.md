# Array factor

↑ **Parent:** [Phased array](phased-array.md)

The [array factor](array-factor.md) describes the [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) between the elements of a [phased array](phased-array.md), separately from the radiation pattern of each element. For $N$ identical equally spaced elements,

$$
F(\theta)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}e^{ij\chi}=e^{i(N-1)\chi/2}\frac{\sin(N\chi/2)}{\sin(\chi/2)},\qquad \chi=\psi-kd\sin\theta.
$$

At $\chi=2\pi m$ the limiting value has magnitude $N$. The total [intensity](intensity-physics.md) is proportional to $|F|^2$ times the single-element [antenna beam pattern](antenna-beam-pattern.md).
