# Rigid-lid pressure in two-layer flow

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid-lid approximation](rigid-lid-approximation.md)

The [rigid-lid approximation](rigid-lid-approximation.md) removes external surface displacement from volume conservation while retaining its pressure as a constraint multiplier. It does not mean zero horizontal pressure gradient. In two-layer geostrophic flow, $f_0\psi_1=\Pi$ and $f_0\psi_2=\Pi-g\prime\chi$, so the upper-layer depth anomaly is $\chi=f_0(\psi_1-\psi_2)/g\prime$. This pressure difference supplies internal potential-vorticity coupling.
