# Instanton fluctuation prefactor

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton](instanton.md)

For a one-dimensional [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) [instanton](instanton.md), the prefactor combines nonzero Gaussian fluctuation [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with the translation zero-mode Jacobian. Here $L_I=-\partial_t^2+V''(q_I)/m$, $L_0=-\partial_t^2+\omega^2$, and the prime excludes the zero mode. Common endpoint regularization and reference normalization define the [determinant](determinant.md) ratio. The resulting $K$ has frequency units, while $Ke^{-S_I/\hbar}$ is the dilute crossing rate. This [determinant](determinant.md)/collective-coordinate structure is also discussed in [Dunne, Sulejmanpasic and Ünsal's treatment](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.10128); unequal neighbouring well frequencies require care and need not share the symmetric-double-well result.
