# Detailed balance for a continuous-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md)

For a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with transition rates $q_{ij}$, detailed balance is

$$
\pi_iq_{ij}=\pi_jq_{ji}
$$

for every pair of distinct states. A probability distribution satisfying these equations is an [invariant distribution of a continuous-time Markov chain](invariant-distribution-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md), because summing over incoming rates gives $\pi Q=0$. Under the standard nonexplosion assumptions, the equilibrium chain is reversible.
