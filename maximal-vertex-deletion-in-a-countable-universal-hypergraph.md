# Maximal-vertex deletion in a countable universal hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Universal countable uniform hypergraph](universal-countable-uniform-hypergraph.md)

Label the vertices of a [universal countable uniform hypergraph](universal-countable-uniform-hypergraph.md) by the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) and remove the largest vertex of each [hyperedge](hyperedge.md). The resulting $(r-1)$-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) is complete. For any prescribed $(r-1)$-set $F$, apply the [hypergraph extension property](hypergraph-extension-property.md) to a finite initial segment containing $F$, prescribing that $F$ extend to an edge. The resulting vertex is larger than every member of $F$, so this deletion produces $F$. This supplies a useful example of a projection that destroys the nonedge information of a random structure.
