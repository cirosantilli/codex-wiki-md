# Rotational latency

↑ **Parent:** [Hard disk drive](hard-disk-drive.md)

The wait for the requested sector to reach the disk head after positioning on its track. For a uniformly distributed arrival phase and rotation period $T_{\rm rot}$, its [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is half a rotation. At $r$ revolutions per minute, this is $30/r$ seconds.
