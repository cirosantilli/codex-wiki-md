# Interaction eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

A [neutrino](neutrino.md) state selected by the [charged lepton](charged-lepton.md) accompanying a [weak charged current](charged-current.md) production or detection process. It is generally a coherent [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) of [mass eigenstates](mass-eigenstate.md), rather than a state of definite propagating [mass](mass.md).
