# Corallicolid

↑ **Parent:** [Parasite](parasite.md)

A [corallicolid](corallicolid.md) is a member of a coral-associated apicomplexan lineage. Retention of chlorophyll-biosynthesis genes without photosystem genes illustrates why a partial pigment pathway is not evidence for complete [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md); its physiological role requires further testing.
