# Myhill-Nerode equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Myhill-Nerode theorem](myhill-nerode-theorem.md)

For a language $A\subseteq\Sigma^*$, define

$$
v\sim_Aw
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall u\in\Sigma^*,\
vu\in A\Longleftrightarrow wu\in A.
$$

This is a right congruence, and its equivalence classes are the states of the minimal [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) for $A$.
