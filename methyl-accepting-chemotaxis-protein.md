# Methyl-accepting chemotaxis protein

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor (biochemistry)](receptor-biochemistry.md)

A [methyl-accepting chemotaxis protein](methyl-accepting-chemotaxis-protein.md) is a bacterial chemoreceptor whose signalling and adaptation are coupled to receptor modification. Receptor arrays interact with CheW and CheA to regulate downstream phosphorylation.
