# Cubic population iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point stability for an iteration](fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md)

The [fixed points](fixed-point.md) of this cubic [iteration of a map](iterated-function.md) satisfy $u=0$ or $u^2=1-1/\lambda$. The nonzero real pair exists for $\lambda<0$ or $\lambda>1$. Its derivative multipliers are $\lambda$ at zero and $3-2\lambda$ at either nonzero point. The [fixed point stability for an iteration](fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md) criterion therefore proves local asymptotic stability of zero for $-1<\lambda<1$ and of the nonzero pair for $1<\lambda<2$. A multiplier of modulus one requires a nonlinear argument rather than this strict derivative test.
