# Uniformizer

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md)

A uniformizer of a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) $R$ is a generator $\pi$ of its unique nonzero [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md). Every nonzero $x$ in the [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) has a unique expression $x=u\pi^n$ with $u$ a unit and $n\in\mathbb Z$.
