# Tor for a principal non-zero-divisor quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Tor functor](tor-functor.md)

For a [non-zero-divisor](non-zero-divisor.md) $a\in A$, resolve $A/(a)$ by $0\to A\xrightarrow{a}A\to A/(a)\to0$. Tensoring with $M$ gives the kernel and cokernel in the displayed formula; all higher [Tor functors](tor-functor.md) vanish. Resolving either argument gives the same homology by the two filtrations of the double complex of the two free resolutions. For $M=A/(a)$, both degrees zero and one equal $A/(a)$.
