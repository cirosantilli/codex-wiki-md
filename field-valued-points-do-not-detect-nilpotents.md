# Field-valued points do not detect nilpotents

↑ **Parent:** [Functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md)

Let $A$ be a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) and $I$ an [ideal](ideal.md) all of whose elements are [nilpotent elements](nilpotent.md). Every [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) from $A$ to a [field](field.md) kills $I$, because a [field](field.md) has no nonzero [nilpotent elements](nilpotent.md). Thus the [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$ induces a [bijection](bijection.md) on points valued in every [field](field.md), even when it is not an [isomorphism of schemes](isomorphism-of-schemes.md). The [dual numbers](dual-number.md) provide the smallest example. Testing the [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) on all [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) does detect this information, by [affine test schemes determine scheme morphisms](affine-test-schemes-determine-scheme-morphisms.md).
