# Leptonic three-body mass correction

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic decay](leptonic-decay.md)

At tree level in a local [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md) with negligible neutrino masses, the charged daughter mass modifies the integrated three-body [V-A interaction](v-a-interaction.md) rate by $f(m_a^2/m_l^2)$. The function tends to one at zero daughter mass and to zero at threshold. It includes both the [phase space](phase-space.md) restriction and the momentum dependence of the [matrix element](matrix-element.md).
