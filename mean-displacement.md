# Mean displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement](displacement.md)

The mean displacement is the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of a particle's [displacement](displacement.md) over a time interval. It describes systematic transport, while the [mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md) also measures fluctuations. For one component with finite second moment,

$$
\langle(\Delta x)^2\rangle=\operatorname{Var}(\Delta x)+\langle\Delta x\rangle^2.
$$

For constant drift speed $u$ and ordinary [diffusion](diffusion.md) of coefficient $D$, the mean displacement is $ut$ and its centered [variance](variance-split.md) is $2Dt$, so the uncentered [mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md) is $u^2t^2+2Dt$. With fixed initial velocity $v_0$ and free inertial [Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md), the mean displacement is $v_0\tau_v(1-e^{-t/\tau_v})$, where $\tau_v$ is the [velocity relaxation time](velocity-relaxation-time.md). Its fluctuation variance is given by the [integrated Ornstein-Uhlenbeck displacement](integrated-ornstein-uhlenbeck-displacement.md).
