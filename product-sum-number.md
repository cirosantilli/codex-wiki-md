# Product-sum number

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $N$ that is both the [sum](sum.md) and the [product](product-of-numbers.md) of one [multiset](multiset.md) of $k$ [positive integers](positive-integer.md). For factors at least $2$ with product $P$, sum $S$, and count $t$, appending $P-S$ ones gives $k=t+P-S$ without changing the [product](product-of-numbers.md). For $k\ge2$, the example $2,k$ with $k-2$ ones proves that the smallest product-sum value is at most $2k$.
