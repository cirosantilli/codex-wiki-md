# Projective embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Projective variety](projective-variety.md)

A projective embedding realizes a variety as a closed subvariety of [projective space](projective-space-split.md). A [very ample line bundle](very-ample-line-bundle.md) gives such an embedding through its global sections.
