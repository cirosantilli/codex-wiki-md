# Permittivity

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permittivity)

Permittivity describes a medium's response to an [electric field](electric-field.md). In a linear isotropic medium, the electric displacement is $\mathbf D=\epsilon\mathbf E$; [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) is its vacuum value $\epsilon_0$.
