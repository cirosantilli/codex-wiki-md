# Achirality

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (chemistry)](chirality-chemistry.md)

[Achirality](achirality.md) means that a [molecule](molecule-split.md) is superposable on its mirror image. A [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) plane that leaves the complete labelled spatial structure unchanged establishes [achirality](achirality.md). Absence of such a plane alone is not a complete test: other orientation-reversing spatial symmetries can also make a rigid structure [achiral](achirality.md).
