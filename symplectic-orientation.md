# Symplectic orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic volume](symplectic-volume.md)

A [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) on a $2n$-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) determines the [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) in which the nowhere-zero [volume form](volume-form.md) $\omega^n/n!$ is positive. Consequently every [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) is orientable. In dimension two this forbids a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) on the [Möbius strip](mobius-band.md).
