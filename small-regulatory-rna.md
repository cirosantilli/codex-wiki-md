# Small regulatory RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

A [small regulatory RNA](small-regulatory-rna.md) can alter target-RNA translation or stability through base pairing or protein interactions. In bacteria, effects on initiation and RNA degradation should be separated experimentally with transcript and protein measurements.
