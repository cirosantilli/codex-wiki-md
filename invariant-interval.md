# Invariant interval

↑ **Parent:** [Forward-invariant set](forward-invariant-set.md)

An [invariant interval](invariant-interval.md) for a scalar map is an interval whose points remain in that interval under every forward iterate. Here invariance means forward invariance and does not assert surjectivity onto the interval.
