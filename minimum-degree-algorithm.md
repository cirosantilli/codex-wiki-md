# Minimum degree algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse Gaussian elimination](sparse-gaussian-elimination.md)

At each step choose a remaining vertex of minimum degree in the current [elimination graph](elimination-graph.md). This heuristic reduces the size of the immediate [Schur complement](schur-complement.md) update and often reduces [fill-in](fill-in.md), but does not guarantee a globally optimal ordering. Its degrees must reflect earlier fill, rather than just the original [graph](graph-split.md).
