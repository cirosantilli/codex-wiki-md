# Transition function of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Local trivialization](local-trivialization.md)

Two local frames of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) differ on their overlap by an invertible matrix-valued function. These functions satisfy the [Čech cocycle condition](cech-cocycle-condition.md). For a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) they are [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md), and for a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) they are scalar holomorphic units.
