# Geostrophic adjustment of a Gaussian height ridge

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic adjustment](geostrophic-adjustment.md)

An initially resting, along-ridge-uniform [shallow water](shallow-water-approximation.md) layer with height disturbance $\zeta_0(x)$ conserves $q-f\zeta/h=-f\zeta_0/h$. Its balanced remainder satisfies the displayed [modified Helmholtz equation](modified-helmholtz-equation.md), with $R_D=\sqrt{gh}/f$. On the infinite line,

$$
\zeta_s(x)=\frac1{2R_D}\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-|x-x'|/R_D}\zeta_0(x')\,dx',\qquad u_s=0,\quad v_s=\frac gf\zeta_s'.
$$

For each [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) component, $\widehat\zeta(t)=\widehat\zeta_s+(\widehat\zeta_0-\widehat\zeta_s)\cos(\sqrt{f^2+ghk^2}\,t)$. The oscillatory part is [inertia-gravity waves](inertia-gravity-wave.md). For a localized [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) on the infinite line those waves spread away, leaving the steady component locally; a reflecting finite basin generally retains oscillations.
