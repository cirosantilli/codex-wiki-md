# Dike swarm

↑ **Parent:** [Dike](dike.md)

A spatially related group of [dikes](dike.md), commonly formed during an episode of extension and [magmatism](magmatism.md). Matching ages, compositions and restored geometries of now-separated [dike swarms](dike-swarm.md) can test a [continental reconstruction](continental-reconstruction.md); similar orientations without age control are much weaker evidence.
