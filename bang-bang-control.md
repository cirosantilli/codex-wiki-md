# Bang-bang control

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal control](optimal-control.md)

A [control input](control-input.md) taking only extreme admissible values except possibly on a switching or singular set. If the [Hamiltonian of an optimal-control problem](hamiltonian-of-an-optimal-control-problem.md) is affine in a scalar control $u\in[0,1]$, a nonzero coefficient selects $u=0$ or $u=1$; a zero coefficient does not determine the control.
