# Radial quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)

[Radial quantization](radial-quantization.md) treats circles around the origin as equal-time slices and logarithmic radius as Euclidean time. A local insertion at the origin prepares a state on a surrounding circle. Contours of the stress tensor act on that insertion, turning its singular [operator product expansion](operator-product-expansion.md) into the [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) action on the corresponding state.
