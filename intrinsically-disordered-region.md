# Intrinsically disordered region

↑ **Parent:** [Protein structure](protein-structure.md)

A segment of a [protein](protein-split.md) that does not maintain one stable folded structure under the specified conditions. An otherwise folded protein can contain such a region, so a region should not be equated with an entire [intrinsically disordered protein](intrinsically-disordered-protein.md).
