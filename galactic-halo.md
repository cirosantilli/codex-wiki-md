# Galactic halo

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

An approximately extended component surrounding a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md), distinguished according to whether one means the [stellar halo](stellar-halo.md), gaseous halo or [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md). These components have different constituents and scales.
