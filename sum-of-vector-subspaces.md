# Sum of vector subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)

The sum of vector subspaces $U,W\leq V$ is

$$
U+W=\{u+w:u\in U,\ w\in W\}.
$$

It is the smallest [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) containing both $U$ and $W$.
