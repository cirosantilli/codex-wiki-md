# Rate function of an exponential sample mean

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér's theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md)

For [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\lambda>0$, the sample mean has [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md) $n$ and the displayed [good rate function](good-rate-function.md), infinite for $x\le0$. The [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $\log[\lambda/(\lambda-\theta)]$ for $\theta<\lambda$. Its [Legendre-Fenchel transform](convex-conjugate.md) has maximizing parameter $\theta=\lambda-1/x$. For $x\le0$, sending $\theta\to-\infty$ gives infinite cost. The cost has its unique zero at $1/\lambda$ and diverges at both ends of its positive domain.
