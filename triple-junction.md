# Triple junction

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)

A point where three plate boundaries meet. Kinematic stability means that one instantaneous junction [velocity](velocity.md) is compatible with all three moving boundaries, so a connected junction can persist. It is not a guarantee of dynamical stability for arbitrary future plate motion.
