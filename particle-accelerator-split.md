# Particle accelerator

↑ **Parent:** [Experimental apparatus](experimental-apparatus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_accelerator)

A particle accelerator increases the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of [charged particles](charged-particle.md) through the [work](work.md) of an [electric field](electric-field-split.md). A single [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ gives an [energy](energy-split.md) gain of magnitude $|q|V$ to a particle of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q$ crossing in the accelerating direction. Reusing a smaller [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) many times can give a much larger final [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) without requiring the whole apparatus to withstand the equivalent single voltage. A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can return the particles to the accelerating region: [magnetic forces do no work](magnetic-forces-do-no-work.md), but they make repeated [energy](energy-split.md) gains possible. The [cyclotron](cyclotron.md) turns this separation between bending and acceleration into a particularly elegant machine.
