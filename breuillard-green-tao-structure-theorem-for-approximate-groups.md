# Breuillard-Green-Tao structure theorem for approximate groups

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate group](approximate-group.md)

For every finite $K$-[approximate group](approximate-group.md) $A$ in a group $G$, there are subgroups $H\trianglelefteq C<G$ such that

$$
H\subseteq A^4,
$$

$C/H$ is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) of class $O_K(1)$, and $A$ is contained in the union of $O_K(1)$ left [cosets](coset.md) of $C$.
