# Compact Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A real Lie algebra is compact when it is the Lie algebra of some [compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md). Equivalently, it has a positive-definite [invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md). It is a direct sum of an abelian center and a compact [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). For its semisimple part, the [compactness criterion from the Killing form](compactness-criterion-from-the-killing-form.md) is negative-definiteness of the [Killing form](killing-form.md). This definition concerns the algebra; a global group with that algebra can still have a noncompact central covering direction.
