# Universal evaluation phantom map

↑ **Parent:** [Phantom map of spectra](phantom-map-of-spectra.md)

Choose one representative of each [finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md) and form $X=\bigvee_{(F,u:F\to D)}F$. Let $e:X\to D$ evaluate each summand and let $p:D\to\operatorname{cofib}(e)$. Every map from a [finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md) factors through its corresponding summand, so $p$ is a [phantom map of spectra](phantom-map-of-spectra.md). If $p=0$, exactness supplies a section of $e$, making $D$ a [retract](retract.md) of $X$. Consequently a spectrum that is not a retract of any wedge of finite spectra has a nonzero such phantom.
