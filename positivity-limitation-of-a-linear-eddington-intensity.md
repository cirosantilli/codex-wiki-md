# Positivity limitation of a linear Eddington intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Eddington closure approximation](eddington-closure-approximation.md)

For the affine angular ansatz $I=A+C\cos\theta$, the [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) is $F=4\pi C/3$. Setting the inward hemispheric flux to zero gives $A(0)=2C/3$, but then the formal intensity at $\cos\theta=-1$ is negative when $F>0$. Exact no-incoming radiation and nonnegative intensity cannot both be represented by a nonzero affine polynomial on all directions. The [Eddington surface boundary condition](eddington-surface-boundary-condition.md) therefore specifies approximate moments, rather than an exact emergent angular radiation field.
