# Uniform unloaded space-elevator cable

↑ **Parent:** [Space elevator](space-elevator.md)

A radial co-rotating cable of constant [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\lambda$ extends from planetary radius $R$ to $b=\eta'R$, with zero applied endpoint [tension](tension.md). If the [geostationary orbit](geostationary-orbit.md) has radius $\eta R$, integration of the effective [force](force-split.md) gives $\eta'(\eta'+1)=2\eta^3$ for a nonzero cable. At position $r$ its [tension](tension.md) is $\lambda[GM(1/R-1/r)-\omega^2(r^2-R^2)/2]$. Differentiation shows the maximum at the [geostationary orbit](geostationary-orbit.md). An anchor load or counterweight alters the integrated condition.
