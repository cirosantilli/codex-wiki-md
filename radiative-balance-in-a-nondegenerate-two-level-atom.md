# Radiative balance in a nondegenerate two-level atom

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md)

Let $A_{21}$ be the [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) coefficient and let $B\rho$ be the induced transition rate per atom under a resonant radiation spectral energy density $\rho$. For nondegenerate levels the [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md) obey $B_{12}=B_{21}=B$. In a closed [two-level system](two-level-system.md) with no collisional or other population transfer, steady state requires

$$
N_1B\rho=N_2B\rho+N_2A_{21}.
$$

For nonzero upper population and drive, division gives the ratio of total spontaneous and stimulated emission rates in the title. In particular $N_1>N_2$ when $A_{21}>0$; continuous driving of this closed model approaches equal populations but cannot create a [population inversion](population-inversion.md). No thermal population law is assumed.
