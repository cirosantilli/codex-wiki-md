# Thermal diffusivity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_diffusivity)

Thermal diffusivity is $\kappa=k/(\rho c_p)$, the ratio of [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) to volumetric [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). It sets the diffusion length $\sqrt{\kappa t}$ over time $t$.
