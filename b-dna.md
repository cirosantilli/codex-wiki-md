# B-DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[B-DNA](b-dna.md) is the familiar hydrated right-handed duplex family, with predominantly C2′-endo sugars and accessible [major groove](major-groove.md) base edges. Its local geometry varies with sequence and bound molecules; approximately 10.5 base pairs per turn is an average rather than an exact universal repeat.
