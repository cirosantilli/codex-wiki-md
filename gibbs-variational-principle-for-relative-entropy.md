# Gibbs variational principle for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For a [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $Q$ of full support on a finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) and a real function $g$,

$$
\log\mathbb E_Q e^g
=\sup_P\{\mathbb E_Pg-D_e(P\Vert Q)\},
$$

where $D_e$ uses [natural logarithms](natural-logarithm.md). The unique maximizing [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) is the exponential tilt $P^*(x)=Q(x)e^{g(x)}/\mathbb E_Qe^g$.
