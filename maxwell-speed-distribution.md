# Maxwell speed distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md)

Integrating the [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md) over directions gives

$$
g(v)=4\pi v^2
\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}
e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)},
\qquad v\geq0.
$$
