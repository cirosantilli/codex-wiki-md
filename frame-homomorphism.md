# Frame homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

A map between [frames](complete-heyting-algebra.md) preserving every [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and every finite [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), including the empty join $0$ and empty meet $1$. Such a map is automatically order-preserving. Inverse image under a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) is an example.
