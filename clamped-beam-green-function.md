# Clamped-beam Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md)

For a unit-length [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) with unit bending stiffness, the [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) of $D_x^4$ with $G=G_x=0$ at both endpoints is the displayed kernel. It is piecewise cubic and continuous through its second [derivative](derivative.md), while $G_{xxx}(\xi+,\xi)-G_{xxx}(\xi-,\xi)=1$. A constructive form is $G=ax^2+bx^3+(x-\xi)_+^3/6$, with $a=\xi(1-\xi)^2/2$ and $b=-(1-\xi)^2(1+2\xi)/6$. The right endpoint conditions determine these coefficients. Thus $y(x)=\int_0^1G(x,\xi)f(\xi)d\xi$ solves the clamped static beam problem.
