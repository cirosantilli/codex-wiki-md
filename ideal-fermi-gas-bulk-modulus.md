# Ideal Fermi gas bulk modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md)

For a nonrelativistic three-dimensional ideal electron gas at zero temperature, the pressure is $p=2nE_F/5$ and the electronic [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) is $B=2nE_F/3$. This is the electronic model contribution, not automatically the total bulk modulus of a real crystal.
