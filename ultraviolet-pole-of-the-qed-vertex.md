# Ultraviolet pole of the QED vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Electron electromagnetic vertex form factors](electron-electromagnetic-vertex-form-factors.md)

Write the [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) vertex as $-ie(\gamma^\mu+\Lambda^\mu)$. In [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md) and $d=4-2\epsilon$, the one-loop [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md) has the local form $\Lambda^\mu_{\rm UV}=e^2\gamma^\mu/(16\pi^2\epsilon)$. Its coefficient follows from the large-[momentum](momentum-split.md) [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) contraction $\gamma^\alpha\not k\gamma^\mu\not k\gamma_\alpha\mapsto(d-2)^2k^2\gamma^\mu/d$. It is independent of external kinematics, whereas the full finite vertex requires additional structures [off shell](off-shell.md). Its cancellation verifies the [Ward identity for QED renormalization constants](ward-identity-for-qed-renormalization-constants.md).
