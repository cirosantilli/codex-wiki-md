# Color-focused arithmetic progression

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waerden theorem](van-der-waerden-theorem.md)

Several monochromatic [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) are color-focused when they have different colors and extend by one further term to the same point. Such focused families give an elementary induction proof of the length-three case of the [Van der Waerden theorem](van-der-waerden-theorem.md).
