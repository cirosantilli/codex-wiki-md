# General linear Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group](lie-algebra-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md)

The [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) form a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) under the [commutator](commutator.md) $[A,B]=AB-BA$. It is the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md); the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) follows by expanding associative products.
