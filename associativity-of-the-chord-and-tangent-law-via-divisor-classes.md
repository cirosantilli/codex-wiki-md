# Associativity of the chord-and-tangent law via divisor classes

↑ **Parent:** [Chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md)

For a smooth [genus one curve](genus-one-curve.md) with origin $O$, the map $P\mapsto[(P)-(O)]$ identifies geometric points with the [degree-zero Picard group of a curve](degree-zero-picard-group-of-a-curve.md). [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives a unique effective representative for each divisor class of degree one: its Riemann-Roch space has dimension one. A line cuts a plane cubic in a divisor linearly equivalent to $3(O)$ when $O$ is the Weierstrass flex. The two successive line intersections in the [chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md) therefore give addition of the corresponding divisor classes. Associativity follows by injectivity of the identification and associativity of divisor-class addition, including tangent intersection multiplicities.
