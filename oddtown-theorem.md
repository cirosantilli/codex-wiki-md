# Oddtown theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

If all members of a [set family](set-family.md) on $[n]$ have odd size and all distinct pairwise [intersections](set-intersection.md) have even size, its [characteristic vectors](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) over the [finite field](finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_2$ are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Their pairwise [dot products](dot-product.md) are zero, and each vector has [dot product](dot-product.md) one with itself. Taking the [dot product](dot-product.md) of a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) with each vector makes every coefficient vanish, so there are at most $n$ vectors. The [set family](set-family.md) of all singleton [sets](set-split.md) attains this bound.
