# Cosmic ionizing background

↑ **Parent:** [Intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md)

The cosmic ionizing background is the diffuse radiation field capable of [photoionization](photoionization.md) of intergalactic gas. Its [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) depends on source [luminosity functions](luminosity-function-astronomy.md), [ionizing photon escape fractions](ionizing-photon-escape-fraction.md), cosmological redshifting, and [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) through absorbers. [Quasars](quasar.md) and [star formation](star-formation.md) supply major contributions with different spectral shapes and evolution.
