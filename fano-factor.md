# Fano factor

↑ **Parent:** [Variance](variance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fano_factor)

For a nonnegative count with positive mean, the Fano factor compares its [variance](variance-split.md) to its mean. A [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) has factor one. Correlated production bursts or weak restoring feedback can give factors larger than one even at large molecular copy number.
