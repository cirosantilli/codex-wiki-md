# Spectrum of a nontrivial bounded projection

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (functional analysis)](spectrum-functional-analysis.md)

Let $P$ be a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) on a complex [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) with $P^2=P$, $P\ne0$ and $P\ne I$. Its [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) contain nonzero [vectors](vector.md), giving [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) one and zero. For $\lambda\notin\{0,1\}$,

$$
(\lambda I-P)^{-1}=\lambda^{-1}(I-P)+(\lambda-1)^{-1}P.
$$

Direct multiplication proves the formula, so the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is exactly $\{0,1\}$ and its [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) is one. Since $P^j=P$ and $\|P\|\geq1$, the [spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md) is explicit: $\inf_{j\geq1}\|P^j\|^{1/j}=\lim_{j\to\infty}\|P\|^{1/j}=1$.
