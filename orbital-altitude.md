# Orbital altitude

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)

Height above a stated planetary reference surface. For a spherical body of radius $R_p$ and a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) of central radius $r$, it is $r-R_p$. Confusing altitude with central radius changes both the [orbital period](orbital-period.md) calculation and surface viewing geometry.
