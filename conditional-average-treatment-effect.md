# Conditional average treatment effect

↑ **Parent:** [Average treatment effect](average-treatment-effect.md)

The conditional average treatment effect at covariates $X=x$ is

$$
\tau(x)=\mathbb E[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid X=x].
$$
