# Common quotient obstruction to commutation of fixed points and orbits

↑ **Parent:** [Commutation of limits and colimits](commutation-of-limits-and-colimits.md)

If [groups](group-split.md) $G,H$ have a common nontrivial quotient $K$, act on $K$ by left multiplication through $G$ and right inverse multiplication through $H$. The actions commute. There are no [fixed points of a group action](fixed-point-of-a-group-action.md) for $G$, but the $H$ orbit set is a singleton. The comparison $A^G/H\to(A/H)^G$ is therefore the map from the empty set to a singleton, which is not an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
