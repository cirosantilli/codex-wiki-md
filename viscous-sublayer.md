# Viscous sublayer

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent boundary layer](turbulent-boundary-layer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscous_sublayer)

The near-wall region where molecular momentum diffusion dominates turbulent transport. Using the [shear velocity](shear-velocity.md) $u_*=\sqrt{\tau_d/\rho}$ gives the viscous wall length $\nu/u_*$. The precise extent depends on the chosen crossover criterion and wall condition. In this region constant stress and no slip give $U\simeq u_*^2z/\nu$; the logarithmic [law of the wall](law-of-the-wall.md) applies outside it.
