# Superconducting coherence length

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md)

The gradient and quadratic terms of the stated model balance at the amplitude-variation scale $\xi$. It is a superconducting length scale, distinct from optical [coherence length](optical-coherence-length.md). The weak-field [London penetration depth](london-penetration-depth.md) in the same normalization obeys

$$
\xi^2=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_*|\alpha|},\qquad
\lambda^2=\frac{m_*}{\mu_0q^2|\psi_0|^2}.
$$

A core suppresses the pairing amplitude over a scale set by $\xi$; screening [electric currents](electric-current.md) and the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) involve $\lambda$. Neither scale is a universal particle size.
