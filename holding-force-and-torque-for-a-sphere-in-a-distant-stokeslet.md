# Holding force and torque for a sphere in a distant Stokeslet

↑ **Parent:** [Translating sphere in Stokes flow](translating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md)

A fixed [sphere](sphere.md) in a slowly varying incident [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) requires an external [force](force.md) $\mathbf F=-6\pi\mu a\mathbf u_\infty$ and external couple $\mathbf G=-4\pi\mu a^3\boldsymbol\omega_\infty$ to leading order. These follow from [Faxén translation law](faxen-s-first-law.md) and [Faxén rotation law](faxen-s-rotational-law.md); the incident [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) supplies $\mathbf u_\infty$ and $\boldsymbol\omega_\infty$. Finite-radius [Laplacian](laplacian.md) terms enter the translational [force](force.md) at the next nonzero order. The signs here refer to applied holding loads, not hydrodynamic loads.
