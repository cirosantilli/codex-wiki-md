# Geometric correction for heliocentric velocity dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Heliocentric projection of a spherical velocity dispersion](heliocentric-projection-of-a-spherical-velocity-dispersion.md)

For tracers sampled uniformly on a Galactocentric shell of radius $r$, with the Sun at radius $R_\odot$, the correction in the [heliocentric projection of a spherical velocity dispersion](heliocentric-projection-of-a-spherical-velocity-dispersion.md) is

$$
A=\frac{r^2+R_\odot^2}{4r^2}
-\frac{(r^2-R_\odot^2)^2}{8r^3R_\odot}
\ln\left|\frac{r+R_\odot}{r-R_\odot}\right|.
$$

The limiting value at $r=R_\odot$ is $1/2$; it tends to $2/3$ as $r/R_\odot\to0$ and to $2R_\odot^2/(3r^2)$ at large radius. Uniform heliocentric [solid angle](solid-angle.md) at fixed $r>R_\odot$ instead gives $A=2R_\odot^2/(3r^2)$ exactly. Specify the selection measure when applying the correction.
