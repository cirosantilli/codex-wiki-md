# Localic geometric morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

A [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) $p:\mathcal D\to\mathcal F$ is localic when $\mathcal D$ is, over $\mathcal F$, the topos of sheaves on an internal [locale](locale.md) of $\mathcal F$. Equivalently, subobjects of the objects $p^*X$, with $X\in\mathcal F$, form a separating family in $\mathcal D$.
