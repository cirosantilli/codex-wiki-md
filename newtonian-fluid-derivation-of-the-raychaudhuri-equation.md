# Newtonian fluid derivation of the Raychaudhuri equation

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md)

For a homogeneous pressureless fluid in comoving coordinates, the [Euler equation](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) gives

$$
\nabla\bar\Phi=-a\ddot a\,\mathbf x,
$$

while the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) gives

$$
\nabla^2\bar\Phi=\frac{4\pi G}{c^2}\bar\rho a^2.
$$

Taking the [divergence](divergence.md) of the first relation and comparing them yields

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3c^2}\bar\rho.
$$

This local fluid derivation describes a homogeneous infinite universe without choosing a physical centre of expansion.
