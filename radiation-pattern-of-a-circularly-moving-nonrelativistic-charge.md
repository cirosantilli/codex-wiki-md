# Radiation pattern of a circularly moving nonrelativistic charge

↑ **Parent:** [Angular distribution of electric dipole radiation](angular-distribution-of-electric-dipole-radiation.md)

For an orbit of [radius](radius.md) $b$ in the $xy$ plane, $\mathbf a=-b\omega^2(\cos\omega t,\sin\omega t,0)$. In [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), at [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ from the orbit normal, $\langle(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf a)^2\rangle=b^2\omega^4\sin^2\theta/2$. The [acceleration covariance in nonrelativistic radiation](acceleration-covariance-in-nonrelativistic-radiation.md) therefore gives the displayed pattern. Its axial [power](power.md) per [solid angle](solid-angle.md) is twice its equatorial value; no observation direction has zero time-averaged [power](power.md). Integrating gives $\langle P\rangle=q^2b^2\omega^4/(6\pi\epsilon_0c^3)$. The condition $b\omega\ll c$ is essential: relativistic [synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md) has a different beamed pattern.
