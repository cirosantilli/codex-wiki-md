# Uniform potential flow around a fixed sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For a fixed impermeable [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $a$ and incident [uniform flow](uniform-flow.md) $U\mathbf e_z$, the [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) is the displayed expression and the [Stokes streamfunction](stokes-streamfunction.md) is $\Psi=(U/2)(r^2-a^3/r)\sin^2\theta$. Differentiation gives $u_r=U(1-a^3/r^3)\cos\theta$ and $u_\theta=-U(1+a^3/(2r^3))\sin\theta$. Thus the [no-penetration boundary condition](no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) holds, the poles are [stagnation points](stagnation-point.md), and the equatorial surface speed is $3U/2$. The disturbance is a [potential dipole](potential-dipole.md) decaying as $r^{-3}$ in [velocity](velocity.md). The [sphere theorem for a Stokes streamfunction](sphere-theorem-for-a-stokes-streamfunction.md) constructs this solution from $\Psi_0=Ur^2\sin^2\theta/2$.
