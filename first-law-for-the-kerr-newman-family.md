# First law for the Kerr-Newman family

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr-Newman metric](kerr-newman-metric.md)

Differentiate $M=(r_+^2+a^2+Q^2)/(2r_+)$, $J=Ma$ and $A=4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)$. Eliminating $dr_+$ and $da$ gives the displayed relation, with $\Omega_H=a/(r_+^2+a^2)$ and $\Phi_H=Qr_+/(r_+^2+a^2)$. Matching [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) to this [First law of black-hole mechanics](first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) fixes the [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md) as $A/4$.
