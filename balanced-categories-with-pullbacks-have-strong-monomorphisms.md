# Balanced categories with pullbacks have strong monomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md)

In a [balanced category](balanced-category.md) with [pullbacks in a category](pullback-category-theory.md), pull a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) back across the bottom map of a lifting square whose left map is an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md). The resulting monic projection is epic because its composite with the lifted top map is epic. Balancedness makes that projection invertible, producing the unique lift. Conversely, if all [monomorphisms](monomorphism.md) are [strong monomorphisms](strong-monomorphism.md), applying the lifting property of a bimorphism to its own square gives its inverse, so the [category](category-split.md) is balanced. Pullback stability of epimorphisms is not needed.
