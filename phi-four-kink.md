# Phi-four kink

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field kink](scalar-field-kink.md)

For the static energy

$$
E[\phi]=\frac12\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\left[\phi'^2+(c^2-\phi^2)^2\right]dx,
$$

the kink joining the vacua $-c$ and $c$ is

$$
\phi(x)=c\tanh(c(x-x_0)).
$$

It saturates the [Bogomolny bound](bogomolny-bound.md) $E\geq4c^3/3$ by satisfying the first-order equation $\phi'=c^2-\phi^2$.
