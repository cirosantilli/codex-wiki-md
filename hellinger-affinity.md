# Hellinger affinity

↑ **Parent:** [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md)

The [Hellinger affinity](hellinger-affinity.md) of dominated [probability measures](probability-measure.md) is the integral of the geometric mean of their [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md). It lies in $[0,1]$ by [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). The unnormalized squared [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md) is $2-2$ times this affinity; this identity makes the relation with [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) particularly simple.
