# Variable-width shallow-water characteristics

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For a rectangular channel with stationary width $b(x)$ and constant [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'$, [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) give $h_t+uh_x+hu_x=-hu b'/b$ and $u_t+uu_x+g'h_x=0$. With $c=\sqrt{g'h}$ the [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) have speeds $u\pm c$. The combinations $u\pm2c$ obey the displayed compatibility equations; they are [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md) only when the width source term vanishes.
