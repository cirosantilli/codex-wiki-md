# Pentaprism

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentaprism)

A [pentaprism](pentaprism.md) redirects a beam through a right [angle](angle.md) using two reflecting faces whose planes meet at $45^\circ$. In the meridional section their combined action is a $90^\circ$ rotation, preserving the relevant image-top direction as the beam turns. It does not by itself perform the additional lateral reversal of a roof [pentaprism](pentaprism.md); the latter has an extra reflecting face.
