# Electron-phonon interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

The [electron-phonon interaction](electron-phonon-interaction.md) couples electronic states to the atomic displacements represented by [phonons](phonon.md). A lattice vibration changes the potential seen by an [electron](electron.md); its quantized displacement permits phonon absorption and emission. This interaction affects [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), electronic [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md), lattice dynamics and pairing in conventional superconductors.
