# Upward unit normal to an upper circular cone

↑ **Parent:** [Normal vector](normal-vector.md)

On $x_3^2=x_1^2+x_2^2$ with $x_3>0$, the [gradient](gradient.md) of the defining function gives a normal proportional to $(-x_1,-x_2,x_3)$. Its [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) is $\sqrt2\,x_3$, proving the displayed normalization. This upward normal lies on the boundary of the [Lorentz cone](second-order-cone.md), so [self-duality of a second-order cone](self-duality-of-a-second-order-cone.md) makes its pairing with every point of the closed upper cone nonnegative.
