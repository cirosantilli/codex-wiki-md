# Normal velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity](velocity.md)

The [normal velocity](normal-velocity.md) is the scalar component of a [velocity](velocity.md) in a specified unit-normal direction. Its sign depends on the orientation of the normal. At a material interface, no penetration equates the fluid and interface [normal velocities](normal-velocity.md); it does not require their tangential components to agree. [Surface acoustic impedance](surface-acoustic-impedance.md) uses a fixed inward normal-velocity convention to determine the sign of energy absorbed by a boundary.
