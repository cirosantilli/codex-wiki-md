# Column density

↑ **Parent:** [Number density](number-density.md)

Column density is the [number density](number-density.md) integrated along a [line of sight](line-of-sight.md). Multiplication by an absorption cross-section gives an [optical depth](optical-depth.md). A small column can result from a small local density, a small path length, or a small fraction of atoms in the absorbing state, so density inference requires these quantities to be constrained.
