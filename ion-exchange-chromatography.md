# Ion-exchange chromatography

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatography](chromatography-split.md)

[Ion-exchange chromatography](ion-exchange-chromatography.md) separates molecules by interactions with a charged stationary phase. Buffer pH controls charge state, and ionic strength can modulate retention and elution; the relevant charge depends on the whole molecular surface.
