# Gaussian unitary ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Random matrix](random-matrix.md)

In the unscaled convention with diagonal [variance](variance-split.md) one, a Gaussian unitary ensemble [matrix](matrix.md) $H$ is a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) with independent real diagonal entries $N(0,1)$ and upper off-diagonal entries $(A_{ij}+iB_{ij})/\sqrt2$, where all the $A_{ij},B_{ij}$ and diagonal entries are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) and have the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md). Its density on the real coordinates of [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) is proportional to $\exp[-\operatorname{tr}(H^2)/2]$. Its exchangeably labelled real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have symmetric [joint probability density function](joint-probability-density.md) proportional to $\prod_{i<j}(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)^2e^{-\sum_i\lambda_i^2/2}$. The squared [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) suppresses coincident [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md); [orthogonal polynomial projection kernels](orthogonal-polynomial-projection-kernel.md) express the [correlation functions of a point process](correlation-function-of-a-point-process.md) as [determinants](determinant.md).
