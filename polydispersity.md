# Polydispersity

↑ **Parent:** [Particle size distribution](particle-size-distribution.md)

A spread of particle or molecular dimensions rather than a single size. A quantitative measure must state the weighting and statistic; particle-size variation and [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain-length variation are related examples but use different common indices.
