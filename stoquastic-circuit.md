# Stoquastic circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)

A [stoquastic circuit](stoquastic-circuit.md) verifier uses reversible classical [quantum gates](quantum-logic-gate.md), represented by [permutation matrices](permutation-matrix.md), with zero and plus-state [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) and a final [Hadamard basis](hadamard-basis.md) output measurement. A basis input remains an entrywise nonnegative state; a general [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) need not be a basis input. The resulting acceptance structure is used in [StoqMA](stoqma.md).
