# Metal-insulator transition

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

A change between a material behaving as an [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) and as an [electrical insulator](electrical-insulator.md). The distinction concerns the limiting electronic response as [temperature](temperature-split.md) tends to zero; finite thermally activated [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) alone does not establish a metallic state. A [Peierls transition](peierls-transition.md) opens a [band gap](band-gap.md) through a change in the [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md).
