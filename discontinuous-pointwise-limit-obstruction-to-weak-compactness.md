# Discontinuous pointwise limit obstruction to weak compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly compact set](weakly-compact-set.md)

A bounded sequence in the [space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md) that has a discontinuous pointwise limit cannot lie in a weakly compact set. Compactness would give a weakly convergent subnet, and continuous point-evaluation functionals would identify its continuous limit with the prescribed pointwise limit. No assumption of sequential compactness is needed.
