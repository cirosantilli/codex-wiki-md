# MSc in cognitive behavioural therapy (research) course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

2 years part time. Official programme title: MSc in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (Research); course code TM\_GT9P9C5. This is the separately advertised MSc extension to the Oxford CBT diploma, with an assessed project, and is not an MSc by Research. Expected start 10 October 2027; open for 2027-28 entry. The prerequisite diploma is not listed as a separate programme.

Official course page: [https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-cognitive-behavioural-therapy-research](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-cognitive-behavioural-therapy-research)
