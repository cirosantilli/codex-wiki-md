# Row-column collision lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Young symmetrizer](young-symmetrizer.md)

If the rows of a [Young tableau](young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ have no repeated intersection with the columns of one of shape $\mu$, then $\sum_{i\leq k}\lambda_i\leq\sum_{i\leq k}\mu_i$ for every $k$. If also $\lambda\geq\mu$ in [dictionary order on integer partitions](dictionary-order-on-integer-partitions.md), the shapes are equal. Saturation of the prefix bounds places one entry of each eligible row in each column, giving $u=rct$ with $r\in R(t)$ and $c\in C(t)$. A collision instead gives a [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) that makes row symmetrization followed by the relevant column antisymmetrization vanish.
