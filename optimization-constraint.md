# Optimization constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constrained optimization](constrained-optimization.md)

An optimization constraint restricts the feasible points in [constrained optimization](constrained-optimization.md), for example by an equality $g(x)=0$ or an inequality $h(x)\leq0$. Extrema are considered on the resulting feasible set rather than on the whole domain of the objective.
