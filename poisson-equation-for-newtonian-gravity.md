# Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

For [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) satisfies the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$. The gravitational acceleration is $-\nabla\Phi$. An isolated system also requires the exterior vacuum solution and decay at infinity; solving the interior equation alone does not fix a globally physical field.
