# Cavity equilibrium destabilized by a second void

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform gravitational field in an off-center spherical cavity](uniform-gravitational-field-in-an-off-center-spherical-cavity.md)

A uniform [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) inside one spherical void can be cancelled at a point by the repulsive field representing another removed sphere. The latter field has a [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) proportional to $I-3\hat r\hat r^T$: its axial eigenvalue is negative and both transverse eigenvalues positive. The equilibrium is restoring along the axis but unstable transversely. A zero force is thus not enough for stable rest.
