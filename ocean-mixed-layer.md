# Ocean mixed layer

↑ **Parent:** [Density stratification](density-stratification.md)

The ocean mixed layer is a near-surface layer with relatively weak vertical variations of [temperature](temperature.md) and [mass density](density.md), maintained by mixing. Beneath it, [stable density stratification](stable-density-stratification.md) can isolate colder water from recent surface heating. The mixed-layer depth is not necessarily the full local water depth.
