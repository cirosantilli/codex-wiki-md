# Improper mixed state

↑ **Parent:** [Local quantum state under objective collapse](local-quantum-state-under-objective-collapse.md)

An improper mixed state is the [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) of a subsystem entangled with unobserved degrees of freedom. A [quantum state readout device](quantum-state-readout-device.md) returns that reduced state until a relevant collapse lies in its past light cone.
