# Almost simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almost_simple_group)

A group between a nonabelian simple group and its automorphism group. Finite two-transitive groups are either of this type or [affine two-transitive groups](affine-two-transitive-group.md), a structural theorem rather than an order-divisibility test.
