<h1 id="bezout-coefficient">Bézout coefficient</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bézout's identity](bezout-s-identity.md)

Bézout coefficients for [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b$ are [integers](integer-split.md) $x,y$ satisfying [Bézout's identity](bezout-s-identity.md) $ax+by=\gcd(a,b)$. The [extended Euclidean algorithm](extended-euclidean-algorithm.md) computes one pair. They need not be unique: both $(x,y)=(7,-2)$ and $(-10,3)$ work for $(a,b)=(5,17)$, since $35-34=1$ and $-50+51=1$.
