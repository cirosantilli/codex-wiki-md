# Dirichlet boundary data

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md)

Dirichlet boundary data prescribe the boundary values of a solution as a function $\varphi$ on the boundary of its domain. Continuity on a polygon requires agreement of the values at corners, but continuity alone does not ensure solvability if the equation loses [uniform ellipticity](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) at a boundary side.
