# Modular layer vanishing lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md)

Let $p$ be a [prime number](prime-number.md), $0\leq a<p$, $1\leq s<p$, and $n\geq a+s$. A [multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md) over $\mathbb F_p$ of degree less than $s$ that vanishes on every Boolean vertex whose weight is not congruent to $a$ modulo $p$ vanishes everywhere. Alternating sums over intervals of $s$ free coordinates eliminate the possible nonzero weight levels one at a time. This proves independence of the auxiliary functions used for the uniform [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md) bound.
