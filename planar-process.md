# Planar process

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planar_process)

The [planar process](planar-process.md) fabricates [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) devices from a common surface while retaining a protective oxide over the exposed ends of their [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md). [Jean Hoerni](jean-hoerni.md)'s patent describes selective openings in [silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md) for [semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md) and contacts, with oxide left over the [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md). Protection and access are designed together; the surface is not simply stripped after fabrication. This was a distinct enabling contribution to the [monolithic integrated circuit](monolithic-integrated-circuit.md).[Description, opening problem statement and Figs. 6–10, masking, retained oxide and contact openings](method-of-manufacturing-semiconductor-devices-by-jean-hoerni.md)
