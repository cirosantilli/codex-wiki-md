# Anyon monodromy

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon R-matrix](anyon-r-matrix.md)

The full double [braiding](braiding.md) of two [anyons](anyon.md). The [anyon balancing identity](anyon-balancing-identity.md) gives $M^{ab}_c=\theta_c/(\theta_a\theta_b)$ in a permitted [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md) channel. Unlike a single [anyon R-matrix](anyon-r-matrix.md) entry, this scalar is invariant under an [anyon vertex gauge transformation](anyon-vertex-gauge-transformation.md).
