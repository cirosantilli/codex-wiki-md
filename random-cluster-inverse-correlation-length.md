# Random-cluster inverse correlation length

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite-volume wired random-cluster measure](infinite-volume-wired-random-cluster-measure.md)

[Positive association of random variables](positive-association-of-random-variables.md) and [translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) give $C_{m+n}\ge C_mC_n$ for axis connection [probabilities](probability.md). The [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) applied to $-\log C_n$ proves the displayed [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) and the bound $C_n\le e^{-n\alpha}$. The lower single-edge conditional bound gives $C_n\ge[p/(p+q(1-p))]^n$, so the decay rate is finite and nonnegative. It may be zero.
