# Raman spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raman_spectroscopy)

[Raman spectroscopy](raman-spectroscopy.md) measures shifted [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) to infer internal [energy](energy-split.md) spacings and the molecular response that makes them visible. In a modern illustrative arrangement, narrowband [laser](laser-split.md) illumination replaces the historical arc, an [optical filter](optical-filter.md) rejects the strong excitation line, and a [spectrograph](spectrograph.md) resolves the weak shifted [spectral lines](spectral-line.md). The illumination, rejection and spectral dispersion are separate jobs. Increasing rejection can conceal small [Raman shifts](raman-shift.md) close to the excitation line.

A proposed measurement should record the excitation [wavelength](wavelength.md), a dark signal, a blank container and the liquid, then compare calibrated line positions and integrated signals under the same collection conditions. Measure [optical filter](optical-filter.md) transmission and detector response before using the [Thermal Raman line ratio](thermal-raman-line-ratio.md) for [temperature](temperature-split.md). Repeat at lower incident [power](power.md) to look for heating or altered sample response. These are suggested checks, not an assertion that the 1928 apparatus implemented modern calibration procedures.

Its appeal is the conversion of internal molecular [energy](energy-split.md) differences into visible [spectral lines](spectral-line.md): changing the excitation can move the whole pattern while preserving characteristic [Raman shifts](raman-shift.md). Combined with [vibrational infrared activity](vibrational-infrared-activity.md), [Raman vibrational activity](raman-vibrational-activity.md) supplies complementary information about [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md). A line pattern can constrain a material's identity or environment, but overlapping bands, [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) backgrounds and the finite [spectral resolution element](spectral-resolution-element.md) prevent an unqualified identification from one peak alone.
