# Magnetization density

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)

For a lattice [spin](spin.md) system, [magnetization density](magnetization-density.md) often means longitudinal [spin](spin.md) per site, $\langle S^z_{\rm tot}\rangle/N$. A magnetic moment per physical volume instead includes the gyromagnetic conversion and lattice-site [number density](number-density.md). The convention should be specified when comparing a spin-chain result with SI [magnetization](magnetization.md).
