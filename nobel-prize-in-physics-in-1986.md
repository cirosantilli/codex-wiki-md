# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1986

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

[Ernst Ruska](ernst-ruska.md) received one half for fundamental work in [electron optics](electron-optics.md) and designing the first [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md). [Gerd Binnig](gerd-binnig.md) and [Heinrich Rohrer](heinrich-rohrer.md) received one quarter each for designing the [scanning tunneling microscope](scanning-tunneling-microscope.md).[Individual official 1986 summary: Ruska one half for electron optics and the first electron microscope; Binnig and Rohrer one quarter each for scanning tunneling microscopy.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1986-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
