# Normality of a quadratic cone

↑ **Parent:** [Normal variety](normal-variety.md)

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two, a nondegenerate quadratic form of rank at least three is irreducible, its cone has only the vertex singular, and its hypersurface ring is Cohen-Macaulay. Thus $(R_1)$ and $(S_2)$ in the [Serre criterion for normality](serre-s-criterion-for-normality.md) hold. In characteristic two, the reduced zero variety of $\sum_i t_i^2$ is instead the hyperplane $\sum_i t_i=0$, which is normal. The unreduced double-hyperplane scheme is not normal.
