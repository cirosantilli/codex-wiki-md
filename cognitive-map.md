# Cognitive map

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A representation of relations among locations or task states that can support inference beyond memorized stimulus–response pairs. The represented space need not be physically spatial. A cognitive-map hypothesis specifies which states and relations are encoded and what behaviour can use them.
