# The Collected Mathematical Papers of James Joseph Sylvester, Volume I

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by James Joseph Sylvester](work-by-james-joseph-sylvester.md)

Collected papers by [James Joseph Sylvester](james-joseph-sylvester.md), edited by [Henry Frederick Baker](henry-frederick-baker.md), credited as H. F. Baker in the prefatory note dated April 1904. Cambridge University Press published Volume I in 1904. The collection date is distinct from the original dates of its individual papers. Internet Archive hosts the inspected digitized volume and OCR text.
