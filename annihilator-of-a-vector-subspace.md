# Annihilator of a vector subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space.md)

For $U\leq V$,

$$
U^\circ=\{f\in V^*:f|_U=0\}.
$$

In finite dimensions, $\dim U^\circ=\dim V-\dim U$.
