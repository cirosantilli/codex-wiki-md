# Annihilator of a vector subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space-split.md)

For a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $U\leq V$,

$$
U^\circ=\{f\in V^*:f|_U=0\}.
$$

In finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md), $\dim U^\circ=\dim V-\dim U$.

An [annihilator of a vector subspace](annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) collects the [linear functionals](linear-functional.md) that ignore every input from that [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md). For $U=\operatorname{span}\{(1,2)\}\subseteq\mathbb R^2$, a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\varphi(x,y)=ax+by$ vanishes on $U$ exactly when $a+2b=0$. Hence $U^\circ$ is the one-dimensional [span](linear-span.md) of $(x,y)\mapsto2x-y$. The zero [level set](level-set.md) in the [linear functional](linear-functional.md) diagram is precisely $U$.
