# Theta temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

The [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md) is the temperature at which the leading effective two-segment interaction of a dilute long [polymer](polymer-split.md) vanishes, so its large-scale size is approximately that of an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md). In the simplest [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) this is the condition $\chi=1/2$. It is the infinite-chain limit of the upper critical temperature in the usual energetic model, not the critical temperature of every finite chain.
