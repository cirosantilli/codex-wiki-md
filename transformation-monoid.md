# Transformation monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)

A [transformation monoid](transformation-monoid.md) is a collection of [self-maps](self-map.md) of a fixed [set](set-split.md) $X$ that contains the [identity function](identity-function.md) and is closed under [function composition](function-composition.md). The [associative property](associative-property.md) comes from [function composition](function-composition.md). A [monoid action](monoid-action.md) describes transformations of a [set](set-split.md) that obey a [monoid](monoid.md)'s multiplication and identity laws.
