# Padded convolution of words

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)

The [padded convolution of words](padded-convolution-of-words.md) reads two finite [words over an alphabet](string.md) in parallel, extending the shorter with a fresh padding symbol $\#$ until the longer ends. Its length is $\max(|u|,|v|)$. Padding may only occur as a suffix, and a $(\#,\#)$ column is excluded. This encodes synchronous two-tape relations as [formal languages](formal-language.md) over a finite paired [alphabet](alphabet.md).
