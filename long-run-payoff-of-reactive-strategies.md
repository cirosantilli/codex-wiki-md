# Long-run payoff of reactive strategies

↑ **Parent:** [Reactive strategy (game theory)](reactive-strategy-game-theory.md)

With initially [independent](independent-random-variables.md) actions, same-round [independence](independent-random-variables.md) is preserved by reactive updates: each next action uses the other [independent](independent-random-variables.md) previous action and fresh [independent](independent-random-variables.md) randomization. Marginal cooperation follows $x'=q_1+(p_1-q_1)y$, $y'=q_2+(p_2-q_2)x$. If the product of response slopes has [modulus](modulus.md) below one, these recurrences converge and the joint distribution is their product. Averaging $R,S,T,P$ over that distribution gives the long-time [payoffs](payoff.md). At deterministic response slopes of [modulus](modulus.md) one, initial-memory-specific cycles must instead be averaged directly.
