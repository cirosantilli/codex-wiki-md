# Module automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md)

A module automorphism is an invertible [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md). Equivalently, it is a bijective [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) from a [module](module-mathematics.md) to itself; its inverse is automatically a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md). They form a [group](group-split.md) under composition.
