# Gravitational fragmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Star formation](star-formation.md)

[Gravitational fragmentation](gravitational-fragmentation.md) divides a self-gravitating gas distribution into condensations that can undergo separate collapse. Pressure support, cooling and rotation influence the scales and outcome; it is not equivalent to an instantaneous equal-mass fission of a rigidly rotating sphere. [Isothermal fragmentation](isothermal-fragmentation.md) is a useful limiting case in which thermal adjustment keeps the gas temperature approximately fixed.
