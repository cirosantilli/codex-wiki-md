# Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

Unit-radius [two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md) is the [hyperboloid](hyperboloid.md) $(X^0)^2-(X^1)^2-(X^2)^2=-1$ in three-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). Its closed coordinates are $X^0=\sinh t$, $X^1=\cosh t\cos\chi$, $X^2=\cosh t\sin\chi$, with $t\in\mathbb R$ and $\chi$ periodic modulo $2\pi$. Unwrapping $\chi$ gives its universal covering spacetime instead.
