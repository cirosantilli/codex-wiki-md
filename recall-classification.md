# Recall (classification)

↑ **Parent:** [Precision and recall](precision-and-recall.md)

The fraction of genuinely positive cases recovered by a classifier. The denominator counts all reference-positive cases, $TP+FN$, rather than positive predictions. In a diagnostic setting this is [diagnostic sensitivity](diagnostic-sensitivity.md).
