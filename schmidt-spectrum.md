# Schmidt spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)

The nonzero eigenvalues, including multiplicities, of either reduced density operator of a bipartite pure state. A [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md) $|\psi\rangle=\sum_k\sqrt{p_k}|a_kb_k\rangle$ gives $\rho_A=\sum_kp_k|a_k\rangle\langle a_k|$ and the corresponding expression for $\rho_B$ by [partial trace](partial-trace.md). Thus unequal marginal spectra rule out a pure joint state. The amplitudes $\sqrt{p_k}$ are often called Schmidt coefficients; the spectrum here uses the probabilities $p_k$.
