# Focus (dynamical systems)

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A planar equilibrium whose linearization has a complex-conjugate pair of nonreal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with nonzero real part. Negative real part gives a [stable focus](stable-spiral.md); positive real part gives an unstable focus.
