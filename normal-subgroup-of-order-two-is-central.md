# Normal subgroup of order two is central

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

If $N=\{1,z\}$ is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of a [group](group-split.md) $G$, conjugation by any element of $G$ preserves $N$ and fixes $1$. It must therefore fix $z$ as well. Thus $N$ lies in the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md). This fact can turn a small [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) into a commuting factor without assuming the whole group is abelian.
