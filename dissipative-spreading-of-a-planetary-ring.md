# Dissipative spreading of a planetary ring

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md)

In a [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), an [epicyclic guiding center](epicyclic-guiding-center.md) $x_0$ carries rotating-frame energy $-3\Omega^2x_0^2/8$, plus nonnegative radial and vertical oscillation energies. [Inelastic collisions](inelastic-collision.md) dissipate the total [Jacobi energy in a shearing sheet](jacobi-energy-in-a-shearing-sheet.md), while [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) preserves $\sum x_0$. Thus $\sum x_0^2$ must grow: the ring spreads about its fixed mean radius. The effect is an [angular momentum transport](angular-momentum-transport.md) process, with some particles moving inward and others outward.
