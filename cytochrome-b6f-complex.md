# Cytochrome b6f complex

↑ **Parent:** [Electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md)

The [cytochrome b6f complex](cytochrome-b6f-complex.md) transfers electrons between the two photosystems in oxygenic photosynthesis and contributes to proton-gradient formation. Its regulated throughput links lumen conditions to intersystem electron flow.
