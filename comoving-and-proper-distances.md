# Comoving and proper distances

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comoving_and_proper_distances)

[Comoving and proper distances](comoving-and-proper-distances.md) are two cosmological distance conventions. For simultaneous radial separations in an FLRW model, proper distance equals the comoving radial distance multiplied by the scale factor at that cosmic time. A [comoving coordinate](comoving-coordinate.md) remains fixed on an ideal comoving observer's worldline.
