# Magnetic flux separation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

In nonlinear [magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md), magnetic flux separation is coexistence of weak-field regions with vigorous broad [convection](convection.md) and strong-field regions with inhibited or small-scale [convection](convection.md). [Flux expulsion](flux-expulsion.md) provides a mechanism for segregation. Conserved mean vertical [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) implies $fB_c+(1-f)B_q=B_0$ for an idealized two-region area fraction $f$. Weak field in convecting regions therefore requires enhanced field elsewhere. The existence and stability of the segregated state depend on parameters and boundaries.
