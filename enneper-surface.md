# Enneper surface

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal surface](minimal-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneper_surface)

The [isothermal coordinates](isothermal-coordinates.md) $(u,v)$ give the [immersion](immersion.md) $(u-u^3/3+uv^2,v-v^3/3+u^2v,u^2-v^2)$. Its coordinate functions are harmonic and its metric is $(1+u^2+v^2)^2(du^2+dv^2)$, so it is a complete immersed [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) with self-intersections.
