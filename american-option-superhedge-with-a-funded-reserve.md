# American-option superhedge with a funded reserve

↑ **Parent:** [American option](american-option.md)

If a nonnegative value $V$ dominates the payoff and $a=rV-\mathcal L_rV\geq0$, delta holdings $V\prime(S)$ combined with a reserve satisfying $dD=(rD+a(S))dt$ give a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) of wealth $V(S)+D$. The reserve retains the surplus instead of consuming it, so the wealth dominates the payoff at every exercise time.
