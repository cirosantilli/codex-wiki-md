# Geomagnetic reversal

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomagnetic_reversal)

A geomagnetic reversal changes the large-scale polarity of Earth's intrinsic [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Crustal [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) records past reversals. The reversal times are not a regular clock; their occurrence reflects time-dependent [dynamo action](dynamo-action.md) rather than rigid rotation of a permanent magnet.
