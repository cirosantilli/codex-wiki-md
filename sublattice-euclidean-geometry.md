# Sublattice (Euclidean geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md)

A sublattice is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md). It is a [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) in its real [linear span](linear-span.md), which may have smaller [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) than the original ambient [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). The equal-parity integer points in $\mathbb Z^d$ form a full-rank [sublattice](sublattice-euclidean-geometry.md) of index $2^{d-1}$: reduction of all coordinate differences modulo two gives the quotient.
