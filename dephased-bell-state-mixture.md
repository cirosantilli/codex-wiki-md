# Dephased Bell-state mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Bell state](bell-state-split.md)

For real $-1\leq\alpha\leq1$, this two-[qubit](qubit.md) [density operator](density-matrix.md) has equal $00$ and $11$ populations and off-diagonal coherences $\alpha/2$. It is a [pure state](pure-state.md) exactly when $\alpha=\pm1$, and a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) exactly when $\alpha=0$. Its [partial transpose](partial-transpose.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $1/2,1/2,\alpha/2,-\alpha/2$, directly witnessing entanglement for every nonzero admissible coherence.
