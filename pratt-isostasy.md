# Pratt isostasy

↑ **Parent:** [Isostasy](isostasy.md)

A local compensation model in which columns of different [density](density.md) reach a common compensation depth. Higher [surface topography](surface-topography.md) is associated with a less dense column; [density](density.md) variation replaces the variable-root mechanism of an Airy model.
