# Two-form gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

An antisymmetric tensor potential $B$ has gauge transformation $B\mapsto B+d\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is a one-form. Its field strength is the three-form $H=dB$, invariant because $d^2=0$. The [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md) is the string-theory example.
