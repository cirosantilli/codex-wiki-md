# Jacobi equation in geodesic polar coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's lemma (Riemannian geometry)](gauss-s-lemma-riemannian-geometry.md)

For $h(r,\theta)=\sqrt{G(r,\theta)}$, the angular [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) equation on a surface becomes

$$
h_{rr}+K(r,\theta)h=0,
\qquad h(0,\theta)=0,
\qquad h_r(0,\theta)=1.
$$

The [Riemannian area element](area-element-of-a-surface.md) is $h(r,\theta)\,dr\,d\theta$.
