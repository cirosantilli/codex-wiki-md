# Positive ramification quotients are elementary abelian

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

For $\sigma\in G_i$, the residue of $(\sigma\pi_L-\pi_L)/\pi_L^{i+1}$ gives a homomorphism to the additive residue field with kernel $G_{i+1}$. On composing two automorphisms, their leading displacement coefficients add because each fixes residues and changes the uniformizer only in degree at least two. These successive quotients are [elementary abelian groups](elementary-abelian-group.md) in residue characteristic $p$. The filtration terminates, so the [wild inertia group](wild-inertia-group.md) is a finite $p$-group.
