# Complement of a finite union of finite-index subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md)

In an [infinite group](infinite-group.md) $G$, the complement of a finite union $H_1\cup\cdots\cup H_k$ of [finite-index subgroups](finite-index-subgroup.md) is either empty or infinite. To prove this, their intersection $L$ has finite [subgroup index](index-of-a-subgroup.md) by the [finite intersection of finite-index subgroups](finite-intersection-of-finite-index-subgroups.md) bound, so it is infinite: otherwise finitely many finite [left cosets](left-coset.md) would cover $G$. If $g$ lies outside the union, the whole infinite [left coset](left-coset.md) $gL$ does too. Indeed, $g\ell\in H_i$ and $\ell\in L\subseteq H_i$ would imply $g\in H_i$.
