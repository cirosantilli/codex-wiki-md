# Angular momentum expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md)

For a normalized periodic angular [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md), $\langle L_z\rangle=-i\hbar\int_0^{2\pi}\psi^*\partial_\phi\psi\,d\phi$. A real periodic [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) with matching boundary values has zero mean, even though nonzero angular momenta can have positive measurement probabilities.
