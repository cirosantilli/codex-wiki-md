# Eckhaus instability

↑ **Parent:** [Real Ginzburg–Landau equation](real-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

For $r,\xi,g>0$, longitudinal phase modulation of a detuned [convection roll](convection-roll.md) yields diffusion coefficient $D_\phi=\xi(r-3\xi Q^2)/(r-\xi Q^2)$. The expression follows by eliminating the fast amplitude correction from the coupled linear amplitude-phase equations at small modulation [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). Thus [convection rolls](convection-roll.md) become unstable for $Q^2>r/(3\xi)$ although they exist for $Q^2<r/\xi$. At the boundary the leading phase diffusion vanishes and higher spatial orders matter.
