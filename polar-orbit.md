# Polar orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_orbit)

An [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) passing near both geographic poles of its central body. Planetary rotation brings different longitudes beneath successive passes, useful for global [remote sensing](remote-sensing.md); one satellite still samples each location intermittently.
