# Catalan power bound from abc

↑ **Parent:** [abc conjecture](abc-conjecture.md)

Put $M=x^p$ for positive bases $x,y\ge2$. The [coprime](coprime-integers.md) triple $(y^q,1,x^p)$ has radical at most $xy<M^{2/3}$. The [abc conjecture](abc-conjecture.md) with $\epsilon=1/6$ gives $M\le C_{1/6}M^{7/9}$, proving the bound. Both bases and both exponents are then bounded, since $2^p\le M$ and $2^q\le M-1$. Unlike a homogeneous Fermat equation, no common-scaling ambiguity occurs.
