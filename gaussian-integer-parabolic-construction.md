# Gaussian-integer parabolic construction

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)

For $n\in\mathbb Z[i]$ and a nonzero integer $r$, the displayed determinant-one matrix has trace two and represents a nonidentity [parabolic Möbius transformation](parabolic-element-of-psl2-r.md) fixing $n/r$. Its fixed-point equation is $(rz-n)^2=0$. These parabolic fixed points are dense in the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), proving that the Gaussian-integer determinant-one [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md) has full limit set.
