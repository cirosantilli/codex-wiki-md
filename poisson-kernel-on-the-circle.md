# Poisson kernel on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson integral on the unit disk](poisson-integral-on-the-unit-disk.md)

For $0<r<1$, the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives $P_r(t)=1+2\sum_{n\ge1}r^n\cos nt$. Hence this positive even kernel has normalized integral one, and normalized [convolution](convolution.md) with it multiplies the $n$th [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) by $r^{|n|}$. Its mass outside any fixed neighbourhood of zero tends to zero as $r\uparrow1$. If an integrable function has finite one-sided limits $A,B$ at zero, evenness gives half the mass to each side and the convolution tends to $(A+B)/2$.
