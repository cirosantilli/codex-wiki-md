# Linearized Euler equations

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md)

The linearized Euler equations retain only first-order perturbations about a specified inviscid fluid state. Together with the linearized [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and a pressure-density constitutive relation, they describe small-amplitude [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md). About a uniform resting state, $\rho_0\partial_t\mathbf u'=-\nabla p'$, $\partial_t\rho'+\rho_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf u'=0$, and an isentropic pressure perturbation has $p'=c_0^2\rho'$. Gradients and advection of a moving background must be retained when its variation is not negligible.
