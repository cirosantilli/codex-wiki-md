# Linear integral equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integral equation](integral-equation.md)

A [linear integral equation](linear-integral-equation.md) depends linearly on its unknown function. For example, $(I+K)f=g$ with $(Kf)(x)=\int K(x,y)f(y)dy$ has a trivial homogeneous null space whenever its inhomogeneous problem has a unique solution: otherwise a nonzero homogeneous solution could be added to that solution. This is the uniqueness step used in dressing constructions of a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md).
