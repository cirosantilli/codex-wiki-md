# Norm convergence of elastic net regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic net regularization in Hilbert sequence space](elastic-net-regularization-in-hilbert-sequence-space.md)

Assume an exact solution of finite penalty exists and select the unique $J$-minimizing exact solution $u^\dagger$. Minimality gives $\|Ku_\alpha^\delta-f^\delta\|^2/2+\alpha J(u_\alpha^\delta)\leq\delta^2/2+\alpha J(u^\dagger)$. If $\alpha\to0$ and $\delta^2/\alpha\to0$, boundedness, [weak sequential compactness of bounded sequences in a reflexive Banach space](weak-sequential-compactness-of-bounded-sequences-in-a-reflexive-banach-space.md) and lower semicontinuity identify every weak limit with $u^\dagger$. The separate lower semicontinuity of $\|u\|_1$ and $\|u\|_2^2$, together with the upper bound on $J$, forces the Hilbert norms to converge. The [Radon-Riesz theorem](radon-riesz-theorem.md) then gives [norm convergence](norm-convergence.md).
