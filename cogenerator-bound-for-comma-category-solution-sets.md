# Cogenerator bound for comma-category solution sets

↑ **Parent:** [Limit form of the special adjoint functor theorem](limit-form-of-the-special-adjoint-functor-theorem.md)

Under the hypotheses of the [limit form of the special adjoint functor theorem](limit-form-of-the-special-adjoint-functor-theorem.md), intersect all [subobjects](subobject.md) of $C$ supporting $x:B\to GC$. Limit preservation gives a smallest supporting $(C_0,x_0)$. If $G(u)x_0=G(v)x_0$ for maps $u,v:C_0\to Q_i$, their [equalizer](equaliser.md) supports $x_0$, so minimality makes it invertible and $u=v$. Thus the indicated map of [hom-sets](hom-set.md) is injective. The [evaluation embedding into cogenerator products](evaluation-embedding-into-cogenerator-products.md) embeds $C_0$ in a product of cogenerators indexed by the realized subsets of the fixed sets $\mathcal D(B,GQ_i)$. There are only a set of such products, a set of their [subobjects](subobject.md), and a set of maps from $B$ into their images under $G$. These data give a [weakly initial set](weakly-initial-set.md) in $(B\downarrow G)$. Using realized subsets avoids assuming maps into every cogenerator exist.
