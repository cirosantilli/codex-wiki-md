# Liquid scintillator

↑ **Parent:** [Scintillation (physics)](scintillation-physics.md)

A [liquid](liquid.md) that produces [particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md) when deposited [energy](energy-split.md) excites its components. [Photomultipliers](photomultiplier.md) can convert the emitted [light](light.md) into detector pulses.
