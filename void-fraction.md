# Void fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

The [void fraction](void-fraction.md) is the fraction of a mixture's local volume occupied by gas. For a horizontally uniform bubbly liquid it is the transported concentration in a one-dimensional gas-volume [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md). It is distinct from gas mass fraction because gas and liquid have different [mass densities](density.md).
