# Standing wave

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical physics](mathematical-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_wave)

A standing wave is the superposition of equal-frequency waves travelling in opposite directions. Its spatial nodes remain fixed while its amplitude oscillates in time.
