# Mean-preserving error tangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Nuisance tangent space](nuisance-tangent-space.md)

For independent-error regression with a zero-mean error, bounded density paths $f_t=f(1+t\gamma)$ must preserve both normalization and the first [moment](moment.md). Their [score functions](informant-function.md) therefore satisfy two constraints. When $0<E_f\varepsilon^2<\infty$, truncation followed by two small bounded moment corrections shows these scores are dense in the displayed [closed subspace of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md). By [independence](independent-random-variables.md), they are orthogonal to every $\varepsilon\psi(X)$ with $\psi\in L^2(v)$. The same constraints apply to other paths only under regularity permitting differentiation of the first [moment](moment.md).
