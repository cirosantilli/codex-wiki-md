# Galactic disk flaring

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

[Galactic disk flaring](galactic-disk-flaring.md) is an outward increase of vertical thickness. A constant vertical [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) in an approximately thin-sheet stellar [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) gives $h\propto1/\Sigma_*$. An [exponential galactic disk](exponential-galactic-disk.md) then predicts $h\propto e^{R/R_d}$ for a gas tracer. Inside a thick stellar layer the potential is instead approximately harmonic, and the tracer width is $\sigma_z/\nu_z$ with [vertical epicyclic frequency](vertical-epicyclic-frequency.md) $\nu_z$.
