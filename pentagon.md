# Pentagon

↑ **Parent:** [Polygon](polygon.md)

A [polygon](polygon.md) with five sides and five vertices. A regular pentagon has equal sides and angles; its [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md) is the order-ten [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md), consisting of five rotations and five reflections. Each reflection axis passes through a vertex and the midpoint of the opposite edge.
