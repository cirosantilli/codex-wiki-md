# Antenna gain-effective-area relation

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md)

For a reciprocal [antenna](antenna-radio.md) with matched polarization and load, the available [antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md) is proportional to the directional [antenna gain](antenna-gain.md). For a lossless [antenna](antenna-radio.md), a [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) argument fixes the constant: one polarization of [Rayleigh-Jeans law](rayleigh-jeans-law.md) radiance gives incident [power](power.md) $(k_BT/\lambda^2)\int A_{\rm eff}\,d\Omega\,d\nu$, and matched [thermal electrical noise](thermal-electrical-noise.md) gives $k_BT\,d\nu$. Thus $\int A_{\rm eff}\,d\Omega=\lambda^2$. Since $\int G\,d\Omega=4\pi$, the displayed relation follows. Reciprocal losses multiply both area and gain by the same efficiency.
