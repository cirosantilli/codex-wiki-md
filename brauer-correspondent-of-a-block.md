# Brauer correspondent of a block

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer correspondence](brauer-correspondence.md)

The Brauer correspondent of a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) with [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$ in $G$ is the corresponding block of $N_G(D)$ furnished by [Brauer first main theorem](brauer-first-main-theorem.md). The more general induced block $b^G$ is defined by [block induction](block-induction.md) when the subgroup contains the centralizer of a defect group of $b$.
