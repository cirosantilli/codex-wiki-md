# Conjunction-pattern precession near a mean-motion resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant conjunction geometry](resonant-conjunction-geometry.md)

With constant [mean motions](mean-motion.md) and $P_c/P_b=(1+\delta)(p+q)/p$, mean [conjunctions](conjunction-astronomy.md) advance by $\Delta\Lambda=2\pi/(P_c/P_b-1)$ each [synodic period](synodic-period.md). Tracking one branch every $q$ [conjunctions](conjunction-astronomy.md) and subtracting $2\pi p$ gives

$$
\dot\Lambda=\frac{q\Delta\Lambda-2\pi p}{qP_{\rm syn}}
=-\frac{2\pi p\delta}{qP_b(1+\delta)}.
$$

The usual formula $-2\pi p\delta/(qP_b)$ keeps only first order in $\delta$. A labelled branch takes $qP_b(1+\delta)/(p|\delta|)$ to turn once, whereas the full set of $q$ interchangeable directions repeats after $P_b(1+\delta)/(p|\delta|)$. Finite [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) introduces true-conjunction corrections.
