# Conjugate index vector

↑ **Parent:** [Index state](index-state.md)

Fix paired orthonormal bases and let $|\phi\rangle_A=\sum_jc_j|j\rangle_A$. Its conjugate index vector in the other [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) uses the conjugated coefficients. For the unnormalized [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md) $|\widetilde\Psi\rangle=\sum_j|j\rangle_A|j\rangle_B$, the partial inner product is $(I\otimes\langle\phi_B^*|)|\widetilde\Psi\rangle=|\phi\rangle_A$. Both the conjugation and the lack of normalization of $\widetilde\Psi$ matter. Applying a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on the first subsystem gives the corresponding [relative state](relative-state-of-a-bipartite-vector.md) of the operated vector.
