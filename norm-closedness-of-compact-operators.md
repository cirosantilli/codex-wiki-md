# Norm-closedness of compact operators

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

An [operator norm](operator-norm.md) limit of compact operators between [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) is compact. Approximate the image of the unit ball by the image under one compact operator and transfer a finite epsilon-net across the small operator-norm error.
