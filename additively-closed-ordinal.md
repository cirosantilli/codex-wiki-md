# Additively closed ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Additively indecomposable ordinal](additively-indecomposable-ordinal.md)

A nonzero ordinal $\delta$ is additively closed when $\beta+\gamma<\delta$ for all $\beta,\gamma<\delta$. These are exactly the ordinals $\omega^\alpha$: sufficiency follows from [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md), while necessity follows by splitting any leading coefficient or nonzero tail below $\delta$ into two smaller summands.
