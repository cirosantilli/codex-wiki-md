# Spin-degeneracy shift of a charge step

↑ **Parent:** [Single-orbital interacting defect](single-orbital-interacting-defect.md)

Near the first addition threshold of a repulsive single-orbital defect, the empty and singly occupied grand weights are 1 and $2e^{\beta(\mu-\epsilon)}$. Equal weights occur at $\mu=\epsilon-k_BT\ln2$. Near the second threshold, the single/double aggregate weights are in ratio $2:e^{\beta(\mu-\epsilon-U)}$, giving half-step position $\epsilon+U+k_BT\ln2$. These are entropic finite-temperature shifts, which vanish in [energy](energy-split.md) units as $T\to0$. They must still be retained when evaluating statistics exactly at an unshifted degeneracy point.
