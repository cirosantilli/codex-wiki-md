# Tracking detector

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)

A [particle detector](particle-detector.md) that samples successive positions along charged-particle trajectories. Together with a known [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), a fitted trajectory measures [momentum](momentum-split.md), charge sign and an interaction or [decay vertex](decay-vertex.md).
