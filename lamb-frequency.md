# Lamb frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md)

The Lamb frequency is the horizontal acoustic angular frequency of a spherical-harmonic component of degree $\ell$. With [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_s$, $S_\ell^2=\ell(\ell+1)c_s^2/r^2$. It helps determine inner acoustic turning points and vanishes for radial modes.
