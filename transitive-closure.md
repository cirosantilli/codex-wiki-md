# Transitive closure

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md)

The transitive closure $\operatorname{TC}(x)$ is the least transitive set containing every member of $x$. It is obtained by taking the union of all finite iterates of the union operation starting from $x$. This set-theoretic construction uses the membership [binary relation](binary-relation.md); [transitive closure of a relation](transitive-closure-relation.md) instead closes an arbitrary [binary relation](binary-relation.md) under composition.
