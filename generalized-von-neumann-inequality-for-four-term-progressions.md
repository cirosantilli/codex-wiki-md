# Generalized von Neumann inequality for four-term progressions

↑ **Parent:** [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md)

For functions bounded by one on a cyclic group of prime order greater than three, three applications of [Cauchy-Schwarz](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bound the four-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) average by the smallest [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md) of its factors. Zero extension yields the corresponding interval bound with an absolute factor. This makes [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md) a measure of uniformity relevant to four-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) counts.
