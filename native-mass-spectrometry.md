# Native mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

[native mass spectrometry](native-mass-spectrometry.md) transfers intact biomolecular assemblies into the gas phase under conditions intended to preserve noncovalent association sufficiently for mass analysis. Charge-state assignment and possible loss of subunits or bound [lipids](lipid.md) must be considered when inferring [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md).
