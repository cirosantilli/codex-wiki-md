# Affine velocity field

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity field](velocity-field.md)

A [velocity field](velocity-field.md) with spatially constant [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) $K$. Differences of fluid [velocities](velocity.md) satisfy $\mathbf u(\mathbf x+\mathbf R)-\mathbf u(\mathbf x)=K\mathbf R$, even when the uniform translation $\mathbf b$ is nonzero. [Incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) requires $\operatorname{tr}K=0$.
