# Propositional geometric theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric theory](geometric-theory.md)

A geometric theory with only nullary relation symbols has formulas built from primitive propositions by finite conjunctions and set-indexed disjunctions. Its sequents present a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md); its [locale](locale.md) carries a canonical model in its [sheaf topos](grothendieck-topos.md), where a proposition is interpreted as the corresponding open subterminal sheaf.
