# Saturation intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Saturation parameter](saturation-parameter.md)

The [saturation intensity](saturation-intensity.md) is the characteristic [light intensity](light-intensity.md) used to normalize the on-resonance [saturation parameter](saturation-parameter.md) of a specified optical transition. Its value depends on the transition and the stated illumination convention.
