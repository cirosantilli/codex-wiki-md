# Joint probability density

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

A joint probability density $f_{X,Y}$ satisfies

$$
\mathbb P((X,Y)\in A)=\iint_A f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx\,dy.
$$

Its coordinate integrals are the [marginal densities](marginal-distribution.md), and integrating it over a rectangle gives the corresponding [joint distribution function](joint-distribution-function.md).
