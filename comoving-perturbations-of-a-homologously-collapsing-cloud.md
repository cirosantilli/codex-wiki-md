# Comoving perturbations of a homologously collapsing cloud

↑ **Parent:** [Pressureless homologous spherical collapse](pressureless-homologous-spherical-collapse.md)

A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) advected with a [homologous spherical flow](homologous-spherical-flow.md) has phase $\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r/R(t)$. Its [material derivative](material-derivative.md) in the background vanishes because $\dot{\mathbf k}=-(\dot R/R)\mathbf k$. Thus contraction shortens its physical wavelength while preserving its phase on each fluid element. Linearizing the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and pressureless [momentum](momentum.md) equation gives $\dot\delta=-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf u_1$ and $\dot{\mathbf u}_1+(\dot R/R)\mathbf u_1=-i\mathbf k\Phi_1$. Combining these with the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) yields $\ddot\delta+2(\dot R/R)\dot\delta-4\pi G\rho\delta=0$ for nonzero compressive [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). It is a local bulk calculation, without the moving cloud-boundary conditions.
