# Prime-filter locale of a commutative ring

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional geometric theory](propositional-geometric-theory.md)

For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$, this [locale](locale.md) is presented by basic opens $U_r$ recording membership in a multiplicatively closed prime filter. Products give intersections. A ring map induces a [locale](locale.md) map in the opposite direction. The basic opens satisfy $U_r\vee U_s=1$ exactly when $(r,s)=R$, making unit-ideal calculations a substitute for choosing prime ideals.
