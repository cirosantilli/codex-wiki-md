# Node (dynamical systems)

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A planar equilibrium with real nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the same sign. Negative eigenvalues give a [stable node](stable-node.md); positive eigenvalues give an [unstable node](unstable-node.md). Repeated real eigenvalues may give a degenerate or improper node, depending on the eigenspace.
