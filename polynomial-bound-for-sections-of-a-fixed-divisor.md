# Polynomial bound for sections of a fixed divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert polynomial](hilbert-polynomial.md)

For a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ of support dimension $d$ on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md) and a fixed [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$, $h^0(X,\mathcal F(mD))=O(m^d)$. Choose a sufficiently high ample divisor $A$ whose section avoids the [associated points](associated-point-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) of $\mathcal F$ and for which $D+A$ is ample. Multiplication by the $m$th power of that section injects $\mathcal F(mD)$ into $\mathcal F(m(D+A))$. The ample [Hilbert polynomial](hilbert-polynomial.md), together with [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md), bounds the latter section space by $O(m^d)$.
