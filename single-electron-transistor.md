# Single-electron transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-electron_transistor)

A small charge island coupled to source and drain through [tunnel junctions](tunnel-junction.md) and capacitively to a gate. Resolved island charging makes sequential tunnelling depend on discrete charge states.
