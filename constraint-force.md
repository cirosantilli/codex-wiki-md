# Constraint force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A constraint force enforces a restriction on the allowed [position](position.md) or [velocity](velocity.md) of a mechanical system. For a bead sliding on a smooth wire, it has zero [dot product](dot-product.md) with the wire's [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md). A moving wire can nevertheless do [work](work.md) because the bead's absolute [velocity](velocity.md) need not be along that instantaneous [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md).
