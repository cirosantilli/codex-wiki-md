# Celestial meridian

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md)

An observer’s [celestial meridian](celestial-meridian.md) is the [great circle](great-circle.md) containing the [zenith](zenith.md) and the [celestial poles](celestial-pole.md). Its upper half passes through the local north and south directions.
