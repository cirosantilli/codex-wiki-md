# Oxymercuration

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

[electrophilic](electrophilicity.md) activation of an [alkene](alkene.md) by [mercury](mercury-element.md)(II), followed by nucleophilic opening of a bridged [mercurinium ion](mercurinium-ion.md). [water](water.md) usually attacks the more substituted [carbon](carbon.md); subsequent [demercuration](demercuration.md) replaces C–Hg with C–H.
