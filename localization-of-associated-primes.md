# Localization of associated primes

↑ **Parent:** [Associated prime of a module](associated-prime-of-a-module.md)

For a commutative [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), an [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) $Q=\operatorname{Ann}_R(m)$ contained in $P$ localizes to the [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) of the nonzero $m/1$. Conversely, contract an associated prime of $M_P$ represented by $m/1$ to an [ideal](ideal.md) $Q$. Choose finitely many generators of $Q$ and one denominator outside $P$ clearing all their annihilation equations. For this denominator $s$, the element $sm$ is nonzero and has [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) exactly $Q$. This proves the formula; finite generation of the contracted [ideal](ideal.md) is the key use of [Noetherianity](noetherian-ring.md).
