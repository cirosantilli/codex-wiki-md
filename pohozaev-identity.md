# Pohozaev identity

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear analysis](nonlinear-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohozaev_identity)

A Pohozaev identity is an integral identity for a nonlinear partial differential equation, obtained by multiplying the equation by a scaling vector field such as $x\mathbin{\cdot}\nabla u$ and applying [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). It relates kinetic, potential, and nonlinear energies.
