# SlowMo

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy for psychosis](cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-psychosis.md)

A blended digital and face-to-face reasoning intervention for [paranoia](paranoia.md). Its 2021 trial did not establish a significant benefit on the prespecified total [paranoia](paranoia.md) score at 24 weeks, despite favourable secondary results. This illustrates why endpoint, timing and process engagement matter when evaluating innovation.[JAMA Psychiatry 78, 714–725; 361 in analysis, prespecified 24-week GPTS total endpoint and secondary outcomes](effects-of-slowmo-a-blended-digital-therapy-targeting-reasoning-on-paranoia-among-people-with-psychosis-a-randomized-clinical-trial-by-philippa-garety-and-others.md)
