# MSW effect

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino matter potential](neutrino-matter-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikheyev–Smirnov–Wolfenstein_effect)

Matter changes the effective [mixing angle](mixing-angle.md) and level splitting of propagating [neutrinos](neutrino.md). The electron-flavour [charged current](charged-current.md) potential is $\sqrt2G_FN_e$ relative to the other active flavours. In the two-flavour [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), equality of its two diagonal entries gives the displayed resonance condition. Adiabatic evolution through a varying density can therefore cause a large flavour change even when the local vacuum [mixing angle](mixing-angle.md) is smaller. The equality alone does not guarantee adiabatic propagation.
