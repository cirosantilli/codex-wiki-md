# Time reversal of an electromagnetic gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [T-symmetry](t-symmetry.md)

The vector [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) has an even temporal component and odd spatial components under [time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md). Invariance of its real minimal coupling therefore gives the displayed transformation of the [gauge field](gauge-field.md), up to a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). Differentiation at $x_T=(-x^0,\boldsymbol x)$ yields $F_{\mu\nu}(x)\mapsto-t_\mu t_\nu F_{\mu\nu}(x_T)$, with $t=(1,-1,-1,-1)$. Thus the [electric field](electric-field.md) is even and the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is odd.
