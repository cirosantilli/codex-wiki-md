# Absorption cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Photon absorption](photon-absorption.md)

The effective area per target for absorbing incident radiation at a specified [wavelength](wavelength.md). For independent absorbers with number column $N$, the absorption [optical depth](optical-depth.md) is $\tau=\sigma_{\rm abs}N$. Absorption does not guarantee a particular chemical product; a channel's [quantum yield](quantum-yield.md) is also needed in its [photolysis rate coefficient](photolysis-rate-coefficient.md).
