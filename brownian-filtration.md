# Brownian filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$ is a [Brownian filtration](brownian-filtration.md) for $B$ when $B$ is an [adapted process](adapted-process.md) and each future increment $B_t-B_s$ is independent of $\mathcal F_s$. The [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md) of a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) has this property. Arbitrary enlargement by future information need not preserve it.
