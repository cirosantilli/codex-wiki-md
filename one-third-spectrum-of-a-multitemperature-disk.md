# One-third spectrum of a multitemperature disk

↑ **Parent:** [Multitemperature blackbody disk](multitemperature-blackbody-disk.md)

For a [blackbody](blackbody.md) disk with $T\propto r^{-3/4}$, the substitution $x=h\nu/(k_BT)$ in $F_\nu\propto\nu^3\int r(e^x-1)^{-1}dr$ produces $F_\nu\propto\nu^{1/3}\int x^{5/3}(e^x-1)^{-1}dx$. When the inner and outer temperatures make the limits approximately zero and infinity, that convergent integral is independent of [frequency](frequency.md). The law applies between the outer-edge turnover and the hot inner-disk cutoff.
