# Quantum reflection at a downward step

↑ **Parent:** [Step potential](step-potential.md)

For $V_0<0$, $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ tends to zero as $E\to0^+$ while $q=\sqrt{2m(E+|V_0|)}/\hbar$ stays finite. Matching gives [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) $r=(k-q)/(k+q)$ and $t=2k/(k+q)$, so [quantum reflection probability](quantum-reflection-probability.md) $R=r^2\to1$ and [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) $T=(q/k)|t|^2\sim4k/q\to0$. The final region is classically allowed; reflection results from the abrupt spatial change in the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md)'s [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). These are limits of positive-energy incoming states, not a flux ratio evaluated at exactly zero incident current.
