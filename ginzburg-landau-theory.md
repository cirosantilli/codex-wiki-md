# Ginzburg-Landau theory

↑ **Parent:** [Landau theory](landau-theory.md)

A long-wavelength order-parameter description extending a local [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) by gradient terms. For a one-component complex field, a common isotropic form is $\mathcal F=\int[\alpha|\Psi|^2+\beta|\Psi|^4/2+K|\nabla\Psi|^2]d^3r$. A charged field instead uses a covariant gradient. Static minimization determines equilibrium profiles; a dynamical law requires additional input. [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md) and the [Ginzburg-Landau functional of a dilute Bose gas](ginzburg-landau-functional-of-a-dilute-bose-gas.md) realize this structure with different microscopic normalizations.
