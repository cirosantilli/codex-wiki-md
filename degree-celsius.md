# Degree Celsius

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature.md)

The degree Celsius has the same size as the [kelvin](kelvin.md), with a shifted temperature origin: $t/{}^\circ\mathrm C=T/\mathrm K-273.15$. Thus a change of one degree Celsius is a change of one [kelvin](kelvin.md), but temperature ratios in [Carnot's theorem](carnot-s-theorem.md) must use absolute [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md).[The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
