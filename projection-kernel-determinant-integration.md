# Projection kernel determinant integration

↑ **Parent:** [Integral kernel](integral-kernel.md)

Suppose an [integral kernel](integral-kernel.md) reproduces under convolution and has integrable diagonal with integral $r$, and the displayed single-variable integrals exist. In the [determinant](determinant.md) $D_n=\det[K(x_i,x_j)]$, [permutations](permutation.md) fixing $n$ contribute $rD_{n-1}$ after integration. For every [permutation](permutation.md) on $n-1$ letters there are $n-1$ ways to insert $n$ into a cycle; convolution contracts the two adjacent factors, and insertion reverses the [permutation](permutation.md) sign. These terms contribute $-(n-1)D_{n-1}$. This proves the formula without a symmetry assumption on the kernel.
