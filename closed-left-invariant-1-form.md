# Closed left-invariant 1-form

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant differential form](bi-invariant-differential-form.md)

On a connected [Lie group](lie-group.md), a [left-invariant](left-invariant-differential-form.md) [differential 1-form](one-form.md) is [closed](closed-differential-form.md) exactly when it is [bi-invariant](bi-invariant-differential-form.md). Connectedness is essential: on $O(2)$ every left-invariant 1-form is closed because its Lie algebra is abelian, while reflection conjugation negates every nonzero one.
