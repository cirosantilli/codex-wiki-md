# Crossmodal correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory perception](multisensory-perception.md)

A systematic association between attributes in different [sensory modalities](sensory-modality.md), such as [pitch](pitch.md) and visual size. It may influence matching, response compatibility or perceptual binding. Such an association does not by itself establish an additional synesthetic experience or multisensory fusion.
