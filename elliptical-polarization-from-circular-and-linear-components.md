# Elliptical polarization from circular and linear components

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md)

Two coherent waves with common propagation direction and [frequency](frequency.md) can combine into a [polarization ellipse](polarization-ellipse.md). Take circular components $E_x=A\cos\psi$, $E_y=A\sin\psi$ and add $B\cos\psi$ along $x$. The resulting semiaxes are $|A+B|$ and $|A|$. Choosing positive $A,B$ gives a nondegenerate ellipse with unequal axes. Uncorrelated relative phases do not describe the same fixed pure polarization.
