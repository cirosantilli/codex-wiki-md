# Pure state on a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [State on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

A pure state is an extreme point of the [state space](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md). The [Krein-Milman theorem](krein-milman-theorem.md) guarantees pure states, and every positive element attains its norm at some pure state.
