# Commuting maps preserve eigenspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Commuting operators](commuting-operators.md)

If $CD=DC$ and $Dx=\lambda x$, then $D(Cx)=C(Dx)=\lambda Cx$. Therefore each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $D$ is invariant under $C$. The vector $Cx$ may be zero; invariance does not require it to be a nonzero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md). This observation is the starting point for [simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md).
