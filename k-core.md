# K-core

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

The unique maximal [induced subgraph](induced-subgraph.md) with minimum [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) at least $k$, possibly empty. It is found by repeatedly deleting vertices of degree below $k$. The [k-core peeling invariant](k-core-peeling-invariant.md) proves that every subgraph satisfying that degree bound survives inside the result; in particular the final vertex set does not depend on deletion order.
