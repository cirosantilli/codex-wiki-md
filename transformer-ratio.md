# Transformer ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma wakefield acceleration](plasma-wakefield-acceleration.md)

The ratio of the witness accelerating field to the driver decelerating field, with peak or loaded-field conventions stated. Under ideal common-length depletion, it also relates witness [energy](energy-split.md) gain to driver [energy](energy-split.md) loss.
