# Spinodal

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinodal)

The [spinodal](spinodal.md) is the boundary where a metastable phase loses local stability. In a mean-field free-energy description the relevant curvature vanishes there, separating local minima from unstable configurations.
