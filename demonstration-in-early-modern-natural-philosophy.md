# Demonstration in early modern natural philosophy

↑ **Parent:** [Natural philosophy](natural-philosophy.md)

A proposed [proof](mathematical-proof.md) of a physical claim, as distinguished in a particular controversy from a calculation that succeeds under a supposition. Historical standards of [demonstration](demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) and the authority to judge one must be reconstructed from the participants' arguments.
