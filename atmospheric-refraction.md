# Atmospheric refraction

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_refraction)

Atmospheric refraction bends a ray through the vertical [refractive index](refractive-index.md) gradient, shifting its apparent direction. Because of [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md), the shift varies with [wavelength](wavelength.md).
