# Actinic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)

The photon flux incident from all propagation directions, resolved by wavelength when needed for [photodissociation](photodissociation.md). It is distinct from the net radiative flux through a horizontal surface; scattered and diffuse photons can contribute even when their net vertical flux cancels.
