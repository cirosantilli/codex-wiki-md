<h1 id="howard-s-semicircle-theorem">Howard's semicircle theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

If an inviscid shear-flow mode has complex wave speed $c$ and $U_-\leq U\leq U_+$, then

$$
\left(\operatorname{Re}c-\frac{U_++U_-}{2}\right)^2
+(\operatorname{Im}c)^2
\leq\left(\frac{U_+-U_-}{2}\right)^2.
$$
