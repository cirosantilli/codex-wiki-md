# Correlation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

For a random vector with [covariance](covariance.md) $\Sigma$ and positive coordinate [variances](variance-split.md), let $D=\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma)$. Its [correlation matrix](correlation-matrix.md) is the [covariance](covariance.md) of $D^{-1/2}(X-\mathbb EX)$, so it is positive semidefinite with unit diagonal. Each entry is the [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) of the corresponding pair of variables. In particular $\operatorname{tr}(C)=p$, explaining why an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) in correlation-based [principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md) represents that [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) divided by $p$ of the total [variance](variance-split.md). The empirical version is the [sample correlation matrix](sample-correlation-matrix.md).
