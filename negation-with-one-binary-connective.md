# Negation with one binary connective

↑ **Parent:** [Functional completeness](functional-completeness.md)

Among sixteen binary Boolean truth functions, eight become functionally complete together with [negation](negation.md). The eight affine functions cannot express [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) because composition preserves affinity. Each of the other eight [truth tables](truth-table.md) has exactly one or three true rows. Negating the output if needed and selected inputs transforms its unique true row to the [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) table. Thus [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) and [negation](negation.md) become definable and [disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md) expresses every [Boolean function](boolean-function.md).
