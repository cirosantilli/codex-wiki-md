# Particle in a rectangular box

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

Dirichlet separation in a rectangular box gives products of sine waves and energy proportional to $n_x^2/a^2+n_y^2/b^2+n_z^2/c^2$.

The [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) supplies the one-dimensional factors of this rectangular-box construction.
