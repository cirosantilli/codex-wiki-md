# Connectedness criterion for the orientation double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation double cover](orientation-double-cover.md)

For a connected [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), every component of the [orientation double cover](orientation-double-cover.md) maps onto the base by the [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md). There can therefore be at most two components. A global [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) splits the cover into two copies of the base. Conversely, if there are two components, each meets every fibre once, so its inverse projection gives a smooth global choice of [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
