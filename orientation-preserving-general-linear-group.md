# Orientation-preserving general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group](general-linear-group.md)

The real invertible matrices with positive [determinant](determinant.md) form a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). The identity has determinant one, [multiplicativity of the determinant](multiplicativity-of-the-determinant.md) preserves positivity under products, and $\det(A^{-1})=1/\det A>0$ preserves it under inversion. More precisely this is the [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $A\mapsto\operatorname{sgn}(\det A)$ to $\{1,-1\}$, so it is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of index two for $n\geq1$. Its elements preserve an [orientation of a vector space](orientation-of-a-vector-space.md).
