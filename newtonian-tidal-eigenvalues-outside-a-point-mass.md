# Newtonian tidal eigenvalues outside a point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

For $\Phi=-GM/r$, the acceleration [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) is

$$
T_{ij}=-\partial_i\partial_j\Phi=
\frac{GM}{r^3}(3n_i n_j-\delta_{ij}).
$$

Its radial eigenvalue is $2GM/r^3$ and its two transverse eigenvalues are $-GM/r^3$. This stretches a small freely falling cloud radially and compresses it transversely. The zero trace expresses the vacuum [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) outside the source; within matter the trace is $-4\pi G\rho$.
