# Parametric curve interrogation

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric curve](parametric-curve.md)

A [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) interface supplies its parameter domain, evaluation, and [derivatives](derivative.md) where they exist. Robust geometric algorithms also need restricted subcurves and certified range or [derivative](derivative.md) bounds on parameter intervals. Tangent, speed and [curvature](curvature.md) are derived from point and [derivative](derivative.md) enquiries when the relevant [derivatives](derivative.md) are regular. Finite point samples alone cannot certify the absence of every plane intersection of an arbitrary smooth curve.
