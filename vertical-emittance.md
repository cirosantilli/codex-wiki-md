# Vertical emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Beam emittance](beam-emittance.md)

The phase-space area measure in the vertical transverse plane. If coupling is described by $k=\varepsilon_y/\varepsilon_x$, changing the coupling and changing the [natural emittance](natural-emittance.md) both alter its equilibrium value.
