# Inner mitochondrial membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

The [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) separates [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) from [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md). Its [selective permeability](selective-permeability.md) permits distinct chemical conditions, while embedded [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) and [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) support [oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md). [Cristae](crista.md) increase its [surface area](surface-area.md).[Chapter 14, The Mitochondrion Contains an Outer Membrane, an Inner Membrane, and Two Internal Compartments; Figure 14-8](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

A selective boundary and a productive [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) have different jobs. Even active respiratory [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) cannot sustain a useful [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) if [protons](proton.md) return freely across the boundary. Conversely, an intact boundary without an [electron donor](electron-donor.md) does not supply the [energy](energy-split.md) for continued pumping. The [isolated mitochondria respiration experiment](isolated-mitochondria-respiration-experiment.md) separates some of these dependencies by additions to the same preparation.
