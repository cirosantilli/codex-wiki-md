# Pauli spin blockade

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-to-charge conversion](spin-to-charge-conversion.md)

Suppression of charge transfer by spin symmetry and the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). For a [double quantum dot](double-quantum-dot.md), a $(1,1)$ [singlet state](singlet-state.md) can enter the lowest $(0,2)$ singlet, whereas a $(1,1)$ [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) is blocked if the required $(0,2)$ triplet lies above the accessible energy window. Spin mixing or relaxation can lift blockade.
