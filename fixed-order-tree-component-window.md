# Fixed-order tree-component window

↑ **Parent:** [Tree-component expectation in the Erdős-Rényi model](tree-component-expectation-in-the-erdos-renyi-model.md)

For fixed $k\geq2$ and $\omega\to\infty$, the expected number of [tree components](tree-component.md) of order $k$ is asymptotic to $k^{k-2}n^kp^{k-1}e^{-knp}/k!$ throughout the displayed window. It tends to infinity: split $np$ at $1$ and $(\log n)/(2k)$ to obtain lower bounds of orders $\omega^{k-1}$, $\sqrt n$, and $e^\omega$. Distinct overlapping vertex sets cannot both be components; disjoint sets have joint-to-product probability ratio $(1-p)^{-k^2}=1+o(1)$. Thus the relative variance vanishes, proving a component of order $k$ exists [with high probability](with-high-probability.md).
