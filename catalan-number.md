# Catalan number

↑ **Parent:** [Dyck path](dyck-path.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_number)

The Catalan number

$$
C_n=\frac1{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}
$$

counts [Dyck paths](dyck-path.md) of semilength $n$. Its ordinary generating function $C(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}C_nx^n$ satisfies $C(x)=1+xC(x)^2$.
