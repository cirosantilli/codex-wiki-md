# Deformable-mirror Fourier-mode counting

↑ **Parent:** [Deformable mirror](deformable-mirror.md)

For actuator pitch $D/N$, full-cycle [spatial frequency](spatial-frequency.md) components are limited to $N/2$ by sampling. Integer lattice counting in a circular [frequency](frequency.md) disk gives approximately $\pi N^2/4$ signed points; conjugate pairing halves the number of pairs, and two real [wave phase](phase-waves.md) quadratures per pair restore approximately $\pi N^2/4$ real coefficients. Counting only positive-quadrant [frequency](frequency.md) representatives gives $\pi N^2/16$, but it omits other orientations or quadratures unless a restricted convention is specified. Area approximations have boundary corrections and do not give exact integer ranks. A square sampling cutoff and a circular illuminated actuator footprint are distinct geometric restrictions.
