# Cooling-limited disk fragmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk](gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk-split.md)

A [gas](gas.md) disk near $Q_T\sim1$ need not form bound fragments: gravitational compression and shocks can heat it into a self-regulated spiral state. Fast enough cooling, often parameterized by $\beta=\Omega t_{\rm cool}$, permits collapse to continue. A numerical critical beta depends on thermodynamics, dimensionality and the cooling model; it is not universal.[Local razor-thin cooling experiment and its model-specific fragmentation threshold](nonlinear-outcome-of-gravitational-instability-in-cooling-gaseous-disks-by-charles-f-gammie.md)
