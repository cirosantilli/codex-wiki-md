# Reflection-symmetric one-to-one Hopf resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

Two complex Hopf [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) of even and odd spatial parity transform under [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) as $(A,B)\mapsto(A,-B)$. In a nonresonant double [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md), the cubic terms are $A|A|^2,A|B|^2$ and their $B$ counterparts. At equal temporal frequencies, common [phase](phase-waves.md) covariance also permits $B^2\overline A$ and $A^2\overline B$. Distinct frequencies alone do not guarantee nonresonance: a ratio of two allows the quadratic terms $B^2$ and $A\overline B$. The frequency assumptions must therefore be included when deriving the [normal form of a dynamical system](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md).
