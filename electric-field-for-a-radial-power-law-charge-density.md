# Electric field for a radial power-law charge density

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

Let the spherically symmetric [volume](volume.md) [charge density](charge-density.md) be $\rho(r)=Ar^p$, with $p>-3$ so the origin contains finite charge. Integration gives $Q(r)=4\pi Ar^{p+3}/(p+3)$. [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) yields the radial [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\mathbf E=Ar^{p+1}\widehat{\mathbf r}/[\epsilon_0(p+3)]$ for $r>0$. The field tends to zero at the centre if $p>-1$; finite enclosed charge alone does not guarantee a regular central field.
