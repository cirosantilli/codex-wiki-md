<h1 id="huckel-method">Hückel method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hückel_method)

A model of conjugated $\pi$ [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md) with an orthonormal atomic basis, one-centre energies $\alpha_i$, and resonance matrix elements $\beta_{ij}$ between selected neighbouring sites. Solving $H\mathbf c=E\mathbf c$ gives orbital [energies](energy-split.md) and coefficients. Its simplicity makes symmetry and electron filling explicit, but quantitative charge predictions can depend strongly on the chosen parameters and omitted orbital relaxation.
