# Field-line angular velocity of an axisymmetric wind

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind](axisymmetric-magnetohydrodynamic-wind.md)

In steady axisymmetric [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), induction makes $\omega$ constant on regular connected [poloidal flux surfaces](axisymmetric-magnetic-flux-surface.md). It describes field-line rotation. The material angular [velocity](velocity.md) is $u_\phi/R=\omega+kB_\phi/(\rho R)$ and differs when the toroidal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) contributes. If the [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md) vanishes, the flux-surface integration requires separate treatment.
