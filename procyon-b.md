# Procyon B

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

A [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) companion in the nearby Procyon binary system. A measured [bolometric luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$ and [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) $T$ give its [stellar radius](stellar-radius.md) through $R=\sqrt{L/(4\pi\sigma T^4)}$.[Abstract identifies Procyon B as a DQZ white dwarf](hubble-space-telescope-astrometry-of-the-procyon-system.md)
