# Gravity of a concentric hollow sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md)

For constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ on $a<r<b$, the [shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives zero [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) for $r\le a$, inward magnitude $4\pi G\rho(r^3-a^3)/(3r^2)$ for $a<r<b$, and $4\pi G\rho(b^3-a^3)/(3r^2)$ outside. These formulas agree continuously at both boundaries. The empty cavity has zero [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) but generally nonzero constant [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md).
