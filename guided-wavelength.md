# Guided wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md)

The [guided wavelength](guided-wavelength.md) is the distance for a $2\pi$ change of axial [wave phase](phase-waves.md) in a propagating [normal mode](normal-mode.md). For the [vacuum](vacuum.md)-filled [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md),

$$
\lambda_g=\frac{\lambda_0}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}},\qquad
\frac1{\lambda_g^2}=\frac1{\lambda_0^2}-\frac1{(2a)^2}
$$

for $\mathrm{TE}_{10}$, with $\lambda_0=c/f$ and $f>f_c$. It exceeds the [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md) at the same [frequency](frequency.md). This longer axial spacing is consistent with $v_{\rm ph}>c$: multiplying $\lambda_g$ by $f$ gives the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), not the [group velocity](group-velocity.md).

Two oppositely traveling fields at the same [frequency](frequency.md) form a [standing wave](standing-wave.md). For a perfect electric short at $z=0$, the total [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is proportional to $\sin(\beta z)\sin(\pi x/a)$, so successive axial electric nodes are separated by

$$
d=\frac\pi\beta=\frac{\lambda_g}{2}.
$$

Thus neighboring minima in a [slotted line](slotted-line.md) scan give half a [guided wavelength](guided-wavelength.md). In the original $a=30\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=1.25f_c$ example, $d=40\,\mathrm{mm}$ implies $\lambda_g=80\,\mathrm{mm}$, not $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Combined with the chosen $f$, the inferred broad dimension is $a=\lambda_0/[2\sqrt{1-(\lambda_0/\lambda_g)^2}]=30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Below cutoff, $\beta$ is imaginary and $2\pi/\beta$ is not a physical periodic spacing.
