<h1 id="su-3-highest-weight-coordinates">SU(3) highest-weight coordinates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight coordinates](highest-weight-coordinates.md)

With $H_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)/2$ and $H_2=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)/(2\sqrt3)$, the simple roots are $(1,0)$ and $(-1/2,\sqrt3/2)$. Their coroots give [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $a=2p$ and $b=\sqrt3q-p$ for Cartesian [highest-weight coordinates](highest-weight-coordinates.md) $(p,q)$. Finite-dimensional irreducible representations require $a,b\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$. The vertical coordinate is $\sqrt3/2$ times [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md).
