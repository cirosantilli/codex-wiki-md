# Dielectric strength

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric breakdown](dielectric-breakdown.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dielectric_strength)

[Dielectric strength](dielectric-strength.md) characterizes the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) at [dielectric breakdown](dielectric-breakdown.md) under specified test conditions. In an ideal uniform gap, it sets a voltage scale $V_{\rm bd}\simeq E_{\rm bd}d$.[§8.5: dielectric strength definition and voltage limit for a specified plate separation](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/8-5-molecular-model-of-a-dielectric.md) The [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) model consequently gives the stored [energy density](energy-density.md) scale

$$
u_{\rm bd}\simeq\frac12\epsilon E_{\rm bd}^2.
$$

This is a model scale, not an operating rating: [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) make local [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) differ from $V/d$, while leakage and [dielectric loss](dielectric-loss.md) spend some supplied [energy](energy-split.md) as heat. Comparing usable [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) therefore requires insulation and breakdown performance as well as [permittivity](permittivity-split.md).
