# Seismic tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

[Seismic tomography](seismic-tomography.md) infers a spatial model of elastic-wave propagation from many source-receiver observations. Uneven illumination, finite [wavelength](wavelength.md), parameter tradeoffs and [regularization](regularization.md) limit its resolution. A seismic-velocity image is not directly an image of [temperature](temperature-split.md) or flow [velocity](velocity.md).
