# Power-sum border-strip multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md)

Multiplication of an [alternant](monomial-alternant.md) by $p_r$ is the sum of alternants obtained by raising one row exponent by $r$. Equal exponents give zero. Otherwise sort the shifted [beta number of a partition](beta-number-of-a-partition.md) back into decreasing order. The resulting partition adds a size-$r$ [rim hook](rim-hook.md); the number of crossed exponents is its height and supplies the [determinant](determinant.md) sign. This proves the formula through the [bialternant formula](bialternant-formula.md). Iteration counts signed [border-strip tableaux](border-strip-tableau.md).
