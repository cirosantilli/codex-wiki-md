# Complexification preserves semisimplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Complexification of a Lie algebra](complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$, the [solvable radical](radical-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of its [complexification of a Lie algebra](complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is invariant under complex conjugation. Its real fixed subspace is a solvable ideal of $\mathfrak g$, and complexifying that fixed subspace recovers the whole radical. Conversely the complexification of any real solvable ideal is solvable. These inclusions prove the displayed identity; in particular $\mathfrak g$ is semisimple if and only if $\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}$ is semisimple.
