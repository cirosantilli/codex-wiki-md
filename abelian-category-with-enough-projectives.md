# Abelian category with enough projectives

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

An [abelian category](abelian-category.md) has enough projectives when every object admits an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) from a [projective object in a category](projective-object.md). Repeating this on the [kernel in a category](kernel-in-a-category.md) constructs a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of every object. Finite direct sums of projective objects remain projective and give the degreewise split horseshoe construction.
