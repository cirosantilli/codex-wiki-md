# Boolean multilinearization

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md)

Replace each positive power $x_i^d$ by $x_i$ in a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). This gives a [multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md) of no larger [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) with the same values on $\{0,1\}^n$. In the [polynomial method in combinatorics](polynomial-method-in-combinatorics.md), these functions lie in a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with a [basis](basis.md) consisting of the square-free [monomials](monomial.md) of the required [polynomial degrees](degree-of-a-polynomial.md).
