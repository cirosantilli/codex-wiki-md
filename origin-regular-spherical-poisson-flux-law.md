# Origin-regular spherical Poisson flux law

↑ **Parent:** [Spherically symmetric solution of Poisson equation](spherically-symmetric-solution-of-poisson-equation.md)

For a spherically symmetric [gradient](gradient.md) field regular at the origin and satisfying [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md), the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) on a ball determines its radial magnitude from the enclosed source integral. It depends only on the source at smaller radii. Regularity excludes the source-free but origin-singular addition $Cx/|x|^3$; without that condition, solving the local equation on a punctured domain leaves this extra term undetermined.
