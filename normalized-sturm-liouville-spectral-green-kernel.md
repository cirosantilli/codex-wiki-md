# Normalized Sturm-Liouville spectral Green kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral Green function of a regular Sturm-Liouville problem](spectral-green-function-of-a-regular-sturm-liouville-problem.md)

Let $L=w^{-1}[-(d/dx)(p\,d/dx)+q]$ be the regular self-adjoint operator of a [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md), with [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) orthonormal for the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) $\int w\overline uv$. For $\mu$ outside its [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md), the displayed kernel satisfies $(L_x-\mu)G_\mu=\delta(x-\xi)$ with an ordinary, unweighted [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md). Indeed weighted completeness gives $w(\xi)\sum_ny_n(x)y_n(\xi)^*=\delta(x-\xi)$. Thus $u(x)=\int G_\mu(x,\xi)f(\xi)d\xi$ solves $(L-\mu)u=f$. A kernel integrated against $w(\xi)d\xi$ instead omits the prefactor. For real coefficients, the weighted reciprocity relation is $G_\mu(x,\xi)/w(\xi)=G_\mu(\xi,x)/w(x)$ when the spectral parameter is real and outside the spectrum. A zero eigenvalue prevents inversion at $\mu=0$ without a forcing compatibility condition or a projected inverse.
