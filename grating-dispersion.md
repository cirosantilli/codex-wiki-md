# Grating dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Grating equation](grating-equation.md)

At fixed incidence angle, differentiating the [grating equation](grating-equation.md) gives $d\beta/d\lambda=m/(d\cos\beta)$. A camera with [focal length](focal-length.md) $f$ therefore has local focal-plane dispersion $q=fm/(d\cos\beta)$ near its optical axis. With coordinate $X=f\tan(\beta-\beta_0)$, the full derivative is $q=f\sec^2(\beta-\beta_0)m/(d\cos\beta)$.
