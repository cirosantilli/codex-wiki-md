# Lattice obstruction to normal approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov distance](kolmogorov-distance.md)

If a [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is constant between consecutive lattice points spaced by $h$ near zero, then its [Kolmogorov distance](kolmogorov-distance.md) from the [standard normal distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md) is at least half the increase of that function across one such interval. This gives a lower bound of order $h$.
