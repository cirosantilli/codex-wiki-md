# Induced matrix norm

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix norm](matrix-norm.md)

A [vector norm](norm.md) on a finite-dimensional real or complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md) induces the displayed [matrix norm](matrix-norm.md) on its [linear operators](linear-operator.md). It is the corresponding [operator norm](operator-norm.md), and the [supremum](supremum.md) can equivalently be taken over the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md). Applying the length bound twice proves [submultiplicativity](submultiplicativity.md): $\|BCx\|\leq\|B\|\|C\|\|x\|$, hence $\|BC\|\leq\|B\|\|C\|$.
