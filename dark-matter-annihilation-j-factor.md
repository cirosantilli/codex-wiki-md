# Dark-matter annihilation J-factor

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter annihilation radiation](dark-matter-annihilation-radiation.md)

The line-of-sight, solid-angle integral of squared dark-matter density used for [annihilation](annihilation.md) fluxes with a position-independent rate coefficient. It separates an astrophysical density model from particle yields and [annihilation](annihilation.md) rates. A decay signal instead scales with a single power of density. Substructure or spatially varying velocity-dependent rates can prevent a simple smooth-density factorization.
