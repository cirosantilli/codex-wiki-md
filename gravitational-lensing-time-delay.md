# Gravitational-lensing time delay

↑ **Parent:** [Strong gravitational lensing](strong-gravitational-lensing.md)

For a thin lens, relative arrival times follow the difference of $\tfrac12|\theta-\beta|^2-\psi(\theta)$ between images, multiplied by $(1+z_l)D_lD_s/(cD_{ls})$. Both geometry and [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) contribute; an additive delay common to all images is unobservable.
