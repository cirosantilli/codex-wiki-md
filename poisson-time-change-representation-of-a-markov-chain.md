# Poisson time-change representation of a Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

A jump process with event types $k$, increments $\nu_k$, and rates $a_k(X)$ can be represented as $X(t)=X(0)+\sum_k\nu_k P_k(\int_0^t a_k(X(s-))\,ds)$, with independent unit-rate [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md). This represents the [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md) through time-changed counts.
