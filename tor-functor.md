# Tor functor

↑ **Parent:** [Homological algebra](homological-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tor_functor)

The Tor functor is the [homology](homology-split.md) of a [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) with a [free resolution](free-resolution.md): $\operatorname{Tor}_i^R(M,N)=H_i(F_\bullet\otimes_RN)$ for a [free resolution](free-resolution.md) $F_\bullet$ of $M$. It measures the failure of tensoring to preserve [exact sequences](exact-sequence.md). For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), one may resolve either argument; the [double complex](double-complex.md) of the two resolutions proves that both computations agree.
