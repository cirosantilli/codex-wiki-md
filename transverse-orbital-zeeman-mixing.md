# Transverse orbital Zeeman mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md)

For an axially split $p$ multiplet with $p_z$ [energy](energy-split.md) $E_0$ and equal $p_x,p_y$ [energies](energy-split.md) $E_t$, a transverse $x$-directed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) leaves $p_x$ unchanged and couples $p_y,p_z$ through the [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) operator $L_x$. The [matrix element](matrix-element.md) has modulus $\mu_BB$, giving the two [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) in the title. For $\mu_BB\ll|E_t-E_0|$, the orbital shifts are quadratic despite a linear [Zeeman energy](zeeman-energy.md) operator. A full quadratic-field calculation also includes the diamagnetic term; [electron spin](electron-spin.md) gives its own linear contribution.
