# Kneser graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kneser_graph)

The Kneser graph has as [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) the $k$-element subsets of $[n]$, with an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between two exactly when they are disjoint. A [graph colouring](graph-coloring.md) of this [graph](graph-split.md) partitions the $k$-sets into [intersecting families](intersecting-family.md).
