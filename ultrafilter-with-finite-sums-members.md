# Ultrafilter with finite-sums members

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)

An ultrafilter with finite-sums members is an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) on the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) all of whose members are [IP sets](ip-set.md). Such an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) exists by extending the [IP-star filter](ip-star-filter.md) using the [ultrafilter lemma](ultrafilter-lemma.md). If a member were not IP, its complement would be an [IP-star set](ip-star-set.md) and hence another member, contradicting properness. Conversely, every such ultrafilter contains the entire IP-star filter, since it cannot contain the non-IP complement of an IP-star set. This characterization does not assert that the ultrafilter is idempotent.
