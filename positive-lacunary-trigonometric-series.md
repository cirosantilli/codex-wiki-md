# Positive lacunary trigonometric series

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric Chebyshev alternation theorem](trigonometric-chebyshev-alternation-theorem.md)

For an odd [integer](integer.md) $q\ge3$ and positive summable [coefficients](coefficient.md) $a_k$, the [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md) makes $f(x)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_k\cos(q^kx)$ [continuous](continuous-function.md). Its partial sum over $q^k\le n$ is the unique [best uniform approximation](best-uniform-approximation.md) by degree-at-most-$n$ [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md), for $n\ge1$. If $Q$ is the first omitted frequency, at $x_j=j\pi/Q$ every omitted cosine equals $(-1)^j$ because all frequency ratios are odd. Thus the tail attains alternating extrema equal to its coefficient sum at $2Q\ge2n+2$ points. The [trigonometric Chebyshev alternation theorem](trigonometric-chebyshev-alternation-theorem.md) proves the formula. For $a_k=r^{-k}$, $r>1$, the largest decay exponent is $\log r/\log q$.
