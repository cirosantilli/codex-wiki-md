# Logarithmic overlap creates a switchback term

↑ **Parent:** [Switchback term](switchback-term.md)

If a stretched variable is $x=\epsilon r$ and an [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) contains $\epsilon^2\log x$, its expression in the fixed-$r$ region is $\epsilon^2\log r+\epsilon^2\log\epsilon$. The parameter logarithm must therefore be present in the [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md), often multiplying a homogeneous solution selected by a boundary condition. An ansatz containing only integer powers with parameter-independent coefficients misses this [switchback term](switchback-term.md). The same reasoning applies to other powers of $\epsilon$ and other stretched-coordinate scalings.
