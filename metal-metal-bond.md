# Metal-metal bond

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

A [metal-metal bond](metal-metal-bond.md) joins two [metal](metal.md) centers directly through overlap of their [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md). A single bond contributes one [electron](electron.md) to the local count at each [metal](metal.md) in conventional [electron counting](electron-counting.md). [Bridging ligands](bridging-ligand.md) can accompany direct bonding, but a short bridged separation alone does not establish its [bond order](bond-order.md).
