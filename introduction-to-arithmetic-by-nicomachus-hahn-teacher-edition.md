# Introduction to Arithmetic by Nicomachus (Hahn teacher edition)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Martin Luther D'Ooge](work-by-martin-luther-d-ooge.md), [Work by Nathanael Hahn](work-by-nathanael-hahn.md), [Work by Nicomachus](work-by-nicomachus.md)

By [Nicomachus](nicomachus.md); translated by [Martin Luther D'Ooge](martin-luther-d-ooge.md) in 1926, and revised for classroom use by [Nathanael Hahn](nathanael-hahn.md) in 2025. The editor's note distinguishes the translated text from added headings, introductions, footnotes, images and exercises. Our citations refer to the translated mathematical passages.

Online text: [https://stoicheiamath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Nicomachus-Introduction-to-Arithmetic-Teacher-Edition.pdf](https://stoicheiamath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Nicomachus-Introduction-to-Arithmetic-Teacher-Edition.pdf)

Cited passages: Book II, Chapter 8, printed pp. 63–64 (PDF pages 68–69), describes triangular rows; Chapter 12, §§1–2, printed p. 70 (PDF page 75), combines consecutive triangular counts into squares; Chapter 20, §5, printed p. 75 (PDF page 80), groups consecutive odd integers into cubes. Publication and contributor credits appear on PDF page 2; notes distinguishing editorial additions appear on PDF page 3.
