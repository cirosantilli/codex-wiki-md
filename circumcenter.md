# Circumcenter

↑ **Parent:** [Perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumcenter)

The circumcenter of a nondegenerate triangle is the common intersection of its three [perpendicular bisectors](perpendicular-bisector.md). It is equidistant from the three vertices and is the center of the triangle's circumcircle.
