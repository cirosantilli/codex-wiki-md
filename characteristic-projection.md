# Characteristic projection

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md)

For the first-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) $a(x,y,z)z_x+b(x,y,z)z_y=c(x,y,z)$, a lifted [characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md) satisfies $(x',y',z')=(a,b,c)$. Its characteristic projection is the image $(x(s),y(s))$ in the plane of independent variables. The projection need not be a regular curve: if $a=b=0$, it can be stationary while $z$ varies. In that situation the lifted curve cannot lie in the graph of a single-valued classical solution unless the required compatibility holds. [Noncharacteristic Cauchy data](noncharacteristic-cauchy-data.md) require the tangent to the initial curve and the projected characteristic vector to be linearly independent.
