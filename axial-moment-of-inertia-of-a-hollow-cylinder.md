# Axial moment of inertia of a hollow cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

For a cylinder of uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, length $L$, inner radius $R_i$ and outer radius $R_o$, an annular element has $dm=2\pi\rho Lr\,dr$. Thus the axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $I=\int r^2dm=\pi\rho L(R_o^4-R_i^4)/2$. Dividing by $M=\pi\rho L(R_o^2-R_i^2)$ yields the displayed result. At fixed [mass](mass.md), the solid-cylinder limit is $MR_o^2/2$ and the thin-shell limit is $MR^2$.
