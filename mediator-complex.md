# Mediator complex

↑ **Parent:** [RNA polymerase II](rna-polymerase-ii.md)

The [Mediator complex](mediator-complex.md) is a multiprotein coactivator involved in coupling regulatory proteins to RNA-polymerase-II transcription. A requirement for mediator activity can be tested by removal and restoration in a reconstituted transcription system.
