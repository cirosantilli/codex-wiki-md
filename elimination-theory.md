# Elimination theory

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elimination_theory)

Elimination theory studies how to remove [variables](variable-mathematics.md) from systems of [polynomial equations](polynomial-equation.md). A [resultant](resultant.md) eliminates one [variable](variable-mathematics.md) from two equations; back-substitution and checks in the original equations recover and verify full solutions.
