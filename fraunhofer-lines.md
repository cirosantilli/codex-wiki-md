# Fraunhofer lines

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption spectrum](absorption-spectrum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraunhofer_lines)

Dark absorption features in the solar [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md), mapped and labelled by [Joseph von Fraunhofer](joseph-von-fraunhofer.md). They give reproducible wavelength landmarks for [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) and optical measurements. Solar and terrestrial absorbers contribute different features to a ground-observed solar spectrum.
