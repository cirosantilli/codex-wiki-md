# Elastic wave in an isotropic solid

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

For density $\rho$ and [Lamé parameters](lame-parameter.md) $\lambda,\mu$, the displacement satisfies

$$
\rho\mathbf u_{tt}
=(\lambda+\mu)\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf u)+\mu\nabla^2\mathbf u.
$$

Its longitudinal and transverse wave speeds are

$$
c_P=\sqrt{\frac{\lambda+2\mu}{\rho}},
\qquad
c_S=\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\rho}}.
$$
