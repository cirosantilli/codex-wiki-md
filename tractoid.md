# Tractoid

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)

The [tractoid](tractoid.md) is the [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md) of a unit [tractrix](tractrix.md). For $X(\theta,v)=(h(\theta),\sin\theta\cos v,\sin\theta\sin v)$ with $0<\theta<\pi/2$, its coordinate [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) are orthogonal and have squared lengths $\cot^2\theta,\sin^2\theta$. The coordinates $x=v$, $y=\csc\theta$ transform this [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) into $(dx^2+dy^2)/y^2$, giving a [local isometry](local-isometry.md) to the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md). Thus its [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) is minus one. The angular coordinate is periodic globally, while its unwrapped coordinate is a real variable; this distinguishes the local metric identification from a global embedding of the whole [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md).
