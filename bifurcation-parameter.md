# Bifurcation parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

A bifurcation parameter labels a family of [dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md) in which a qualitative change of [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) or other invariant sets is studied. Near a simple critical [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) crossing, a nonzero derivative with respect to the physical control parameter permits using that eigenvalue itself as a local parameter. At a [codimension-two bifurcation](codimension-two-bifurcation.md), two independent parameters are generally needed.
