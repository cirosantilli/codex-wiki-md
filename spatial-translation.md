# Spatial translation

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter isometry](de-sitter-isometry.md)

A spatial translation in de Sitter flat slicing sends $\mathbf x\mapsto\mathbf x+\mathbf a$ at fixed conformal time. Translation invariance of a momentum-space correlator produces its momentum-conserving [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md).
