# Normal-ordered product

↑ **Parent:** [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md)

A [normal-ordered product](normal-ordered-product.md) of free-field oscillator operators places all [creation operators](creation-operator.md) to the left of all [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md), including the [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md) required to reorder fermions. Its vacuum expectation vanishes if any nontrivial oscillator factors remain. The [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) expresses a free-field [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) through these products and all [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md).
