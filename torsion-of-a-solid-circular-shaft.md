# Torsion of a solid circular shaft

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion spring](torsion-spring.md)

A circular shaft twisted uniformly through small end angle $\phi$ over length $L$ has local engineering [shear strain](shear-strain.md) $r\phi/L$ at radius $r$. Its [shear stress](shear-stress.md) is $Gr\phi/L$, so the integrated [torque](torque.md) is $C=\int_A r(Gr\phi/L)\,dA=GJ_p\phi/L$, using the [polar second moment of area](polar-second-moment-of-area.md). The restoring torque has the opposite sign to the imposed twist. The stored elastic [energy](energy-split.md) is $C\phi/2=GJ_p\phi^2/(2L)$. The circular cross-section is important: general noncircular sections can warp and have a different torsion constant.
