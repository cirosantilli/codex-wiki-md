# Green operator potential for ddbar exactness

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic orthogonality criterion for ddbar exactness](harmonic-orthogonality-criterion-for-ddbar-exactness.md)

For a $d$-closed pure-type form $\alpha$ orthogonal to [harmonic forms](harmonic-differential-form.md) on a [compact](compact-space.md) [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), let $G_{\bar\partial}$ be the inverse of the [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) on the harmonic complement, zero on the harmonic space. The [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md) makes it commute with $\partial$ and $\bar\partial$. Thus $\alpha=\bar\partial\bar\partial^*G_{\bar\partial}\alpha$ and $G_{\bar\partial}\alpha=\partial\partial^*G_{\bar\partial}^2\alpha$. The mixed anticommutator $\bar\partial^*\partial+\partial\bar\partial^*=0$ gives $\alpha=\partial\bar\partial\beta$. This is an explicit potential for the [ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md), with one lower degree of each type; using the inverse of the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) instead multiplies the displayed expression by four.
