# Cantharidin

↑ **Parent:** [Vesicant](vesicant.md)

A toxic natural [vesicant](vesicant.md) obtained from [blister beetles](blister-beetle.md). Its topical action can cause [acantholysis](acantholysis.md) and [blister](blister.md) formation.[NDA 212905, §5.3, p. 32](fda-multidisciplinary-review-of-ycanth.md)
