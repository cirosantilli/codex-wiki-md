# Total ramification of a holomorphic map

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md)

Count [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) upstairs in $X$, weighted by $e_p-1$, rather than counting distinct [branch values of a holomorphic map](branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) downstairs in $Y$. Grouping by [fibres](fiber-of-a-function.md) gives

$$
R_f=\sum_{y\in Y}\left(d-\#f^{-1}(y)\right).
$$

The cardinality counts distinct points; the [degree of a holomorphic map](degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) counts them with their [analytic ramification indices](ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md). Thus each summand is the number of missing distinct preimages in that [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md).[Lecture 5, Definition 5.16, printed p. 19: total branching index as the sum of fibre deficits](riemann-surfaces-by-constantin-teleman-lent-term-2003.md)

The phrase [branch point](branch-point.md) varies across conventions: to keep the two surfaces distinct, use [ramification point](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) for $p\in X$ and [branch value of a holomorphic map](branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) for $f(p)\in Y$. The existing name [branch point of a holomorphic map](branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) here denotes the latter. Several [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) can share one [branch value of a holomorphic map](branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md).
