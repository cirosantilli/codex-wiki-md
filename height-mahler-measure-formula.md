# Height-Mahler measure formula

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

If the primitive minimal polynomial of an algebraic number $\alpha$ has degree $d$, then

$$
H(\alpha)^d=M(f_\alpha).
$$
