# Association mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic association](genetic-association.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_mapping)

Association mapping localizes genetic influences on a [phenotype](phenotype.md) by testing population [genetic association](genetic-association.md) with markers. [Linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) allows a typed marker to tag a nearby causal variant; historical [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) can narrow the associated interval. [Population stratification](population-stratification.md) can produce association without a local causal relation.
