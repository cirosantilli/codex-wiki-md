# Conduction electron

↑ **Parent:** [Conduction band](conduction-band.md)

An [electron](electron.md) occupying an extended state in a [conduction band](conduction-band.md). Its response to an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) depends on its band [group velocity](group-velocity.md), occupation and scattering. Merely counting such electrons does not by itself determine [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md).
