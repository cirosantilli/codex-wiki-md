# Connective constant

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connective_constant)

If $b_n$ counts the $n$-step [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md) from a fixed vertex of a transitive lattice, the connective constant is the exponential growth rate

$$
\kappa=\lim_{n\to\infty}b_n^{1/n}.
$$

Submultiplicativity of $b_n$ and the [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) applied to $\log b_n$ prove existence of this limit.
