<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Over a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) $D$, an $m$-by-$n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is in [Smith normal form](../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) if all entries off the main diagonal vanish and its nonzero diagonal entries $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ occur first and satisfy

$$
\boxed{d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_r}.
$$

All remaining diagonal entries are zero. The zero [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is allowed, with $r=0$. For an arbitrary [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $M$, the [Smith normal form](../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) theorem gives [invertible matrices](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) $P\in\operatorname{GL}_m(D)$ and $Q\in\operatorname{GL}_n(D)$ with

$$
PMQ=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_r,0,\ldots,0),
$$

where the right side is interpreted as a rectangular diagonal [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) when necessary. The $d_j$ are determined up to multiplication by [units](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). Positivity can be required over $\mathbb Z$, but is not a condition over the [Gaussian integers](../../../../../gaussian-integer.md).
