<h1 id="3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Each entry of the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $Q$ from part (b)(iii) has unique real and imaginary [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) coefficients. Write

$$
Q=U+iV,\qquad U,V\in M_2(\mathbb Z).
$$

It already belongs to the [general linear group](../../../../../general-linear-group.md) $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z[i])$. Expanding the relation from part (b)(iii) gives

$$
0=(iI-A)(U+iV)e_2=(-AU-V)e_2+i(U-AV)e_2.
$$

The real and imaginary parts must both vanish, so

$$
\boxed{AUe_2=-Ve_2,\qquad AVe_2=Ue_2,\qquad U+iV\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z[i])}.
$$

These are the required identities, with $e_2=(0,1)^{\mathsf T}$.
