<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $(v,Av)$, the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) given by $A$ sends $v$ to the second [basis](../../../../../basis.md) vector and sends $Av$ to $A^2v=-v$. Equivalently,

$$
AE=(Av,-v)=E\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Multiplying on the left by the [matrix inverse](../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) $E^{-1}$ gives

$$
\boxed{E^{-1}AE=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

This [change of basis](../../../../../change-of-basis.md) produces the [companion matrix](../../../../../companion-matrix.md) found in part (a)(ii).
