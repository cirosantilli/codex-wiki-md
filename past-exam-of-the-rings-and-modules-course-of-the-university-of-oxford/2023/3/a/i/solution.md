<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $A^2+I=0$, its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) $m_A$ divides $X^2+1$. The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $X^2+1$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over the [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md): a reducible quadratic would have a rational root, whereas $r^2+1>0$ for every $r\in\mathbb Q$.

Because $n>0$, the constant [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) one evaluates to the nonzero [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md) $I$, so $m_A\ne1$. Both $m_A$ and $X^2+1$ are [monic polynomials](../../../../../monic-polynomial.md); hence the only remaining divisor is

$$
\boxed{m_A(X)=X^2+1}.
$$
