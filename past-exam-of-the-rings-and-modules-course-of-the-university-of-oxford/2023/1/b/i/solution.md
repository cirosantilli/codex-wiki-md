<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since the [irreducible element](../../../../../irreducible-element.md) $x$ is not a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), the [principal ideal](../../../../../principal-ideal.md) $(x)$ is proper. Suppose $(x)\subseteq J\subseteq R$. As $R$ is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md), write $J=(d)$. Then $x=dr$ for some $r\in R$. The definition of an [irreducible element](../../../../../irreducible-element.md) says that $d$ or $r$ is a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md).

If $d$ is a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), then $J=R$. If $r$ is a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), then $d$ and $x$ are [associate elements](../../../../../associate-element.md), so $J=(x)$. Thus $(x)$ is a [maximal ideal](../../../../../maximal-ideal.md). By the permitted criterion that a [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) by a [maximal ideal](../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) is a [field](../../../../../field.md),

$$
\boxed{R/(x)\text{ is a field}}.
$$
