↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A subset $E\subseteq M$ is a [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md) if every $m\in M$ has a unique expression

$$
\boxed{m=\sum_{e\in E}r_ee,\qquad r_e\in R,\qquad r_e=0\text{ for all but finitely many }e}.
$$

Uniqueness concerns the entire family of coefficients, including the zero coefficients. Equivalently, $E$ generates $M$ and is [linearly independent in a module](../../../../../linear-independence-in-a-module.md): every finite relation $\sum_{e\in H}r_ee=0$, with $H\subseteq E$, has all $r_e=0$.

A [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is a [free module](../../../../../free-module.md) if it has a [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md). An infinite [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md) still uses only finite sums in each expression; arbitrary infinite sums are not part of the [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) operations.
