<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Put $N=\langle E'\rangle$ and $P=\langle E''\rangle$, the [submodules](../../../../../submodule.md) generated by the two parts of the [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md). Every finite expression in the [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md) splits into its $E'$ terms and its $E''$ terms. Thus $M=N+P$.

If $m\in N\cap P$, there are finite expressions

$$
m=\sum_{e\in H'}a_ee=\sum_{e\in H''}b_ee,
\qquad H'\subseteq E',\quad H''\subseteq E''.
$$

Subtracting gives a finite relation among distinct members of $E$, because $E'\cap E''=\varnothing$. Their [linear independence in a module](../../../../../linear-independence-in-a-module.md) forces every coefficient to vanish, so $m=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{M=\langle E'\rangle\oplus\langle E''\rangle}.
$$

This proves the [basis partition gives a module direct sum](../../../../../basis-partition-gives-a-module-direct-sum.md) lemma, also when one part is empty.
