↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes.** An $\mathbb F$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md) is a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md). Start with a finite generating list. If it is [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md), one vector has a nonzero coefficient in a [linear relation](../../../../../linear-relation.md); dividing by that coefficient expresses the vector using the others, so it can be removed without changing the [span](../../../../../linear-span.md). Repeating terminates with a [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) generating list, which is a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) and therefore a [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md).

Thus every [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md) is a [free module](../../../../../free-module.md). For the zero [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md), the empty list is the required [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md).
