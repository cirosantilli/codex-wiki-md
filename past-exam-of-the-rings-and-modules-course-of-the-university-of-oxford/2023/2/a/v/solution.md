<h1 id="2/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Choose a countable generating family $(n_k)$ for $N$. For each $k$, the expression of $n_k$ in the [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md) $E$ has a finite set $H_k$ of nonzero coefficients, its [coordinate support in a free module](../../../../../coordinate-support-in-a-free-module.md). Let

$$
E'=\bigcup_kH_k.
$$

By the [countable union of countable sets](../../../../../countable-union-of-countable-sets.md) property, $E'$ is a [countable set](../../../../../countable-set.md). Every $n_k$ belongs to $\langle E'\rangle$, so all finite $R$-linear combinations of the $n_k$ do too. Therefore

$$
\boxed{N\subseteq\langle E'\rangle\quad\text{for a countable }E'\subseteq E}.
$$

Here and below, [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) includes finite. The same construction applies to a finite or empty generating family. This is the [countable basis support lemma](../../../../../countable-basis-support-lemma.md).
