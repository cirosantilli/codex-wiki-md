<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under the temporary assumption that $E$ is a [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md) for $B$, part (a)(v) applied to the countably generated [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) $F$ gives a [countable set](../../../../../countable-set.md) $E'\subseteq E$ such that $F\subseteq C=\langle E'\rangle$. By part (a)(iv),

$$
B=C\oplus\langle E\setminus E'\rangle.
$$

Use part (a)(iii) to project onto the second [direct summand](../../../../../direct-summand.md). This gives a [module homomorphism](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) $\pi:B\to B$ with

$$
\boxed{F\subseteq\ker\pi=C}.
$$

It remains to check the required size of this [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). Enumerate $E'$ as $b_1,b_2,\ldots$, with the finite case treated the same way. Every element of $C$ is a finite sum of these elements with [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) coefficients. For each $m$, the set $\langle b_1,\ldots,b_m\rangle$ is the image of the [countable set](../../../../../countable-set.md) $\mathbb Z^m$, so it is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md). Taking their [countable union](../../../../../countable-union.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\ker\pi\text{ is countable}}.
$$

This is the [countability of countably generated abelian groups](../../../../../countability-of-countably-generated-abelian-groups.md) argument; it uses the countability of the scalar [ring](../../../../../ring.md) $\mathbb Z$.
