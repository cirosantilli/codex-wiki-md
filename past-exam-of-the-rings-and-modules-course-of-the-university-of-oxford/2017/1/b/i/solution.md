↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take **$R=\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$**, a [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) with four residue classes. The class $\overline2$ is nonzero but $\overline2\,\overline2=\overline0$. Thus $R$ has a [zero divisor](../../../../../zero-divisor.md) and is not an [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md).
