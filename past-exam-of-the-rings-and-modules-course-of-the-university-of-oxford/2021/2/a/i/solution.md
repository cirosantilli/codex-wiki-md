<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a finite [generating set of a module](../../../../../generating-set-of-a-module.md) $n_1,\ldots,n_t$ for the [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) $N$. For any $m\in M$, the [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) of the [module homomorphism](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) $\phi$ gives $n\in N$ with $\phi(n)=m$. Write $n=\sum_{j=1}^t r_jn_j$. Since $\phi$ is a [module homomorphism](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md),

$$
m=\phi\left(\sum_{j=1}^t r_jn_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^t r_j\phi(n_j).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{M=R\phi(n_1)+\cdots+R\phi(n_t)}
$$

is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md). The argument includes $N=0$, in which case [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) forces $M=0$ and the empty [generating set of a module](../../../../../generating-set-of-a-module.md) suffices.
