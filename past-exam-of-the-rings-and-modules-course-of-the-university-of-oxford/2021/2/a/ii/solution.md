<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $n_1,\ldots,n_t$ generate the [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) $N$, and let $m_1+N,\ldots,m_s+N$ generate the [quotient module](../../../../../quotient-module.md) $M/N$. Choose each $m_i\in M$ as a representative of its class. For any $m\in M$, there are $r_1,\ldots,r_s\in R$ such that

$$
m+N=\sum_{i=1}^s r_i(m_i+N).
$$

Therefore $m-\sum_i r_im_i\in N$. Expressing that difference using the [generating set of a module](../../../../../generating-set-of-a-module.md) $n_1,\ldots,n_t$ gives

$$
m=\sum_{i=1}^s r_im_i+\sum_{j=1}^t u_jn_j.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{M=Rm_1+\cdots+Rm_s+Rn_1+\cdots+Rn_t}
$$

is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md). This proves that [finite generation is preserved by module extensions](../../../../../finite-generation-is-preserved-by-module-extensions.md).
