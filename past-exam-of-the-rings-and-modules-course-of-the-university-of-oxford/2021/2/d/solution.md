<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Assume $H=\ker\psi$ is a [finitely generated module](../../../../finitely-generated-module.md). The restriction $q:H\to L$ is a [surjective](../../../../surjective-function.md) [module homomorphism](../../../../module-homomorphism.md), so part (a)(i) makes $L$ a [finitely generated module](../../../../finitely-generated-module.md).

The [free module](../../../../free-module.md) $R^m$ has its finite standard [basis of a module](../../../../basis-of-a-module.md). Its [quotient module](../../../../quotient-module.md) $R^m/\operatorname{Im}q$ is therefore a [finitely generated module](../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) by part (a)(i). Part (c) transfers this property to the isomorphic [quotient module](../../../../quotient-module.md) $\ker\phi/L$. Finally apply part (a)(ii) to the [submodule](../../../../submodule.md) $L\subseteq\ker\phi$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{\ker\psi\text{ finitely generated}\ \Longrightarrow\ \ker\phi\text{ finitely generated}}.
$$

More concretely, take the images under $q$ of finitely many generators of $H$, and adjoin lifts in $\ker\phi$ of finitely many generators of $\ker\phi/L$. This finite list generates $\ker\phi$. The argument shows why [finite presentation of a module](../../../../finite-presentation-of-a-module.md) is independent of the chosen finite [generating set of a module](../../../../generating-set-of-a-module.md).
