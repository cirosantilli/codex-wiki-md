# Mean-square contraction of synchronously coupled diffusions

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous coupling](synchronous-coupling.md)

If $2(x-y)(b(x)-b(y))+(\sigma(x)-\sigma(y))^2\leq-k(x-y)^2$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) for the squared difference and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) give $\mathbb E|X_t-Y_t|^2\leq e^{-kt}\mathbb E|X_0-Y_0|^2$. Localization of $e^{kt}|X_t-Y_t|^2$ as a nonnegative local supermartingale gives the same estimate under suitable existence hypotheses.
