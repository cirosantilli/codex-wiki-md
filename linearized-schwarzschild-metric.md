# Linearized Schwarzschild metric

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

For a static spherical point source, the weak-field solution in isotropic radius $\rho$ has $g_{tt}=1-2GM/\rho$ and spatial metric $-(1+2GM/\rho)(d\rho^2+\rho^2d\Omega^2)$. The [isotropic-to-areal weak-field radius change](isotropic-to-areal-weak-field-radius-change.md) gives the displayed metric with $r_S=2GM$. Its validity requires $r_S/r\ll1$. Treating it as an exact metric near $r\sim r_S$ can turn omitted terms into large corrections; it does not establish horizons or strong-field photon dynamics.
