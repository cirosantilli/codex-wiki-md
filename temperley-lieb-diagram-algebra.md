# Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md)

The Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra is generated by planar cap-cup [tangles](tangle.md), with composition by stacking and each closed circle replaced by $\delta$. Its local relations $e_i^2=\delta e_i$ and $e_ie_{i+1}e_i=e_i$ make the second and third [Reidemeister moves](reidemeister-move.md) direct algebraic identities for $R_i=A I+A^{-1}e_i$ when $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$.
