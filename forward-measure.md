# Forward measure

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_measure)

A forward measure is the martingale measure associated with taking a zero-coupon bond as [numéraire](numeraire.md). Prices divided by that bond are martingales under the corresponding measure.
