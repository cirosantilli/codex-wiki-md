# Bijective monomial cusp parametrization with nonregular inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of affine varieties](morphism-of-affine-varieties.md)

The [morphism](morphism.md) $\mathbb A^1\to V(x^4-y^3)$ given by $t\mapsto(t^3,t^4)$ is [bijective](bijection.md) on [points](point-geometry.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), but is not an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). At a nonzero curve [point](point-geometry.md), $t=y/x$ is the unique inverse parameter, while the origin has unique preimage zero. The induced map on [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md) has [image](image-of-a-function.md) $k[t^3,t^4]\subset k[t]$, which lacks the [monomial](monomial.md) $t$: all its [monomial](monomial.md) exponents have the form $3a+4b$, with $a,b\geq0$. Nor can $t$ be regular at the [cusp](cusp-algebraic-geometry.md), since an equality $t=P(t^3,t^4)/Q(t^3,t^4)$ with $Q(0)\neq0$ would produce a nonzero degree-one term in $tQ$ and none in $P$. Thus [pointwise](pointwise-convergence.md) bijectivity does not guarantee an algebraic inverse, even though the inverse is regular away from one [singular point](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md).
