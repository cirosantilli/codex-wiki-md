<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At $T\ll T_F$, Pauli exclusion restricts thermally changed occupations to an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) interval of order $k_BT$ about the [Fermi energy](../../../../../fermi-energy.md). The affected fraction is of order $T/T_F$; their typical excitation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is of order $k_BT$, so the thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) correction is proportional to $T^2$ and the [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) is linear in $T$.

More precisely, with $g(E_F)$ the total [density](../../../../../density.md) of one-particle states per unit [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), including [spin](../../../../../spin.md),

$$
\boxed{C_V=\frac{\pi^2}{3}g(E_F)k_B^2T+O(T^3).}
$$

For a three-dimensional [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) with quadratic dispersion, $g(E_F)=3N/(2E_F)$, hence

$$
\boxed{C_V=\frac{\pi^2}{2}Nk_B\frac{T}{T_F},\qquad T_F=E_F/k_B.}
$$

The fixed-particle-number [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives $U=3NE_F/5+(\pi^2/4)N(k_BT)^2/E_F+\cdots$. The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) must be included when obtaining this expression; holding it artificially fixed changes the intermediate [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) correction.
