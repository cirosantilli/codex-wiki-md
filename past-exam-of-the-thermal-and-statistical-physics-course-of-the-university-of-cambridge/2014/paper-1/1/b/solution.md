<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using $C_V=T(\partial S/\partial T)_V$ and the stated [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md), interchange derivatives within a smooth single [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md):

$$
\left(\frac{\partial C_V}{\partial V}\right)_T
=T\frac{\partial}{\partial T}\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_T
=T\left(\frac{\partial^2p}{\partial T^2}\right)_V=0.
$$

Therefore **$C_V$ depends only on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md)** for the given temperature-linear [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md). It need not be constant in [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
