<h1 id="section-b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [volume](../../../../../volume.md) and the numbers of all other species, the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) is $\mu_\alpha=(\partial F/\partial N_\alpha)_{T,V,N_{\alpha'\ne\alpha}}$. For the combined noninteracting mixture, add the free [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) from (a) and (b). Since the [thermal de Broglie wavelengths](../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) are independent of [particle](../../../../../particle-physics.md) number at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm CO}=k_BT\ln(n_{\rm CO}\lambda_{\rm CO}^3),\qquad
\mu_{\rm O_2}=k_BT\ln(n_{\rm O_2}\lambda_{\rm O_2}^3),}
$$



$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm CO_2}=k_BT\ln(n_c\lambda_c^3)-\frac\epsilon2.}
$$

Each logarithm has a dimensionless argument, and each [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) has units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md). The binding offset must be kept consistently when comparing species.
