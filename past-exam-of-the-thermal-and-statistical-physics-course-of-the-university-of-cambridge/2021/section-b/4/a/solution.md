<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\beta=1/(k_BT)$ and define the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) of species $\alpha$ by

$$
\lambda_\alpha=\frac{h}{\sqrt{2\pi m_\alpha k_BT}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi\hbar^2\beta}{m_\alpha}}.
$$

Ignoring the internal [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) as stipulated, each [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md)'s translational [canonical partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is $V/\lambda_\alpha^3$. The two species do not interact, so their [partition functions](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) multiply; the treatment of [indistinguishable particles](../../../../../indistinguishable-particles.md) requires a separate factorial for each species, not a factorial of the total [particle](../../../../../particle-physics.md) number. Thus

$$
\boxed{Z_{\mathrm{CO-O_2}}=\frac{(V/\lambda_{\rm CO}^3)^{N_{\rm CO}}}{N_{\rm CO}!}\frac{(V/\lambda_{\rm O_2}^3)^{N_{\rm O_2}}}{N_{\rm O_2}!}.}
$$

Using $F=-k_BT\ln Z$ and [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../stirling-formula.md) gives the extensive [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md)

$$
\boxed{F_{\mathrm{CO-O_2}}=k_BT\sum_{\alpha=\mathrm{CO,O_2}}N_\alpha\left[\ln\left(\frac{N_\alpha\lambda_\alpha^3}{V}\right)-1\right].}
$$

These are [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) expressions, valid for $n_\alpha\lambda_\alpha^3\ll1$, with $n_\alpha=N_\alpha/V$. The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) zero has been chosen so that separated CO and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md) have zero internal reference [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
