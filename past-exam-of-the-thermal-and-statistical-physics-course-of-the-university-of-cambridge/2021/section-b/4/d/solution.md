<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Introduce an [extent of reaction](../../../../../extent-of-reaction.md) $\xi$ increasing in the dissociation direction. The allowed particle-number changes conserve both carbon and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) atoms:

$$
dN_c=-2\,d\xi,\qquad dN_{\rm CO}=2\,d\xi,\qquad dN_{\rm O_2}=d\xi.
$$

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [volume](../../../../../volume.md), the resulting [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) change is

$$
dF=\left(-2\mu_c+2\mu_{\rm CO}+\mu_{\rm O_2}\right)d\xi.
$$

At an interior [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md), both forward and backward changes are allowed, so the coefficient must vanish:

$$
\boxed{2\mu_{\rm CO_2}=2\mu_{\rm CO}+\mu_{\rm O_2}.}
$$

Equality of the three individual [chemical potentials](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) would be wrong: a reaction exchanges species in its stoichiometric proportions rather than [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) for [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md). Stability is also explicit here, since

$$
\frac{d^2F}{d\xi^2}=k_BT\left(\frac4{N_c}+\frac4{N_{\rm CO}}+\frac1{N_{\rm O_2}}\right)>0
$$

when all three species are present. Thus the equilibrium composition minimizes the free [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) along the conserved-composition constraint.
