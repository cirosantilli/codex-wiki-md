<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The given binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\epsilon$ belongs to the formation of two carbon-dioxide [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md). Relative to the reference used in (a), the internal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) offset of one carbon-dioxide [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) is therefore $-\epsilon/2$, not $-\epsilon$. Its mass is $m_c=m_{\rm CO}+m_{\rm O_2}/2$ and its [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) is $\lambda_c=h/\sqrt{2\pi m_ck_BT}$, where the subscript $c$ denotes $\mathrm{CO_2}$.

The single-molecule [canonical partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is $(V/\lambda_c^3)e^{\beta\epsilon/2}$. For $N_c$ indistinguishable noninteracting [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md),

$$
\boxed{Z_{\rm CO_2}=\frac{(V/\lambda_c^3)^{N_c}}{N_c!}\exp\left(\frac{\beta\epsilon N_c}{2}\right).}
$$

The corresponding [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md), using [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../stirling-formula.md), is

$$
\boxed{F_{\rm CO_2}=N_ck_BT\left[\ln\left(\frac{N_c\lambda_c^3}{V}\right)-1\right]-\frac{\epsilon N_c}{2}.}
$$

The negative sign in the last term expresses the stabilizing binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and ensures that low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) favors association.
