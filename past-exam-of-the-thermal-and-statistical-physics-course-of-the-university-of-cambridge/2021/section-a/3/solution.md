<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A nonzero [superconducting energy gap](../../../../superconducting-energy-gap.md) suppresses the population of thermally excited [quasiparticles](../../../../quasiparticle.md). When $k_BT\ll\Delta$, their [Boltzmann factor](../../../../boltzmann-factor.md) produces an approximately activated electronic [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md),

$$
C_e(T)\simeq A(T)\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta}{k_BT}\right),
$$

where the prefactor $A(T)$ varies algebraically rather than exponentially. For the rough estimate requested, neglect its change between the two quoted [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md). Taking a ratio eliminates its unknown magnitude:

$$
\frac{C_e(3\,\mathrm K)}{C_e(2\,\mathrm K)}\simeq14
=\exp\left[\frac{\Delta}{k_B}\left(\frac1{2\,\mathrm K}-\frac1{3\,\mathrm K}\right)\right].
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta}{k_B}\simeq6\ln14\,\mathrm K=15.8\,\mathrm K\approx16\,\mathrm K.}
$$

The corresponding excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $\Delta\simeq2.19\times10^{-22}\,\mathrm J\simeq1.36\,\mathrm{meV}$. The units $\mathrm{mJ\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ of the two heat-capacity readings cancel in their ratio.

This is a [heat-capacity activation estimate](../../../../heat-capacity-activation-estimate.md), not an exact determination of a gap from two data points. More generally, if $C_e=AT^p e^{-\Delta/(k_BT)}$, the same measurements give

$$
\frac{\Delta}{k_B}=6\left[\ln14-p\ln\frac32\right]\mathrm K.
$$

For example, the low-temperature [BCS theory](../../../../bcs-theory.md) form for a fully gapped isotropic [superconductor](../../../../superconductor.md) has $p=-3/2$ and would give about $19.5\,\mathrm K$. The stated approximate data and constant-gap assumption support the simpler $16\,\mathrm K$ estimate; they do not specify a detailed [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) or heat-capacity prefactor. Here $\Delta$ means the minimum [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), rather than automatically twice that value.
