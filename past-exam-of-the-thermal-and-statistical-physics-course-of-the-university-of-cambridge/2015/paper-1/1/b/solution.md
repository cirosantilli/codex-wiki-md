<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the Euler relation for a single-component [simple compressible system](../../../../../simple-compressible-system.md):

$$
dU=T\,dS+S\,dT-p\,dV-V\,dp+\mu\,dN+N\,d\mu.
$$

The thermodynamic fundamental relation for the same system is $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dN$. Subtraction gives

$$
S\,dT-V\,dp+N\,d\mu=0.
$$

Dividing by $N$, with $s=S/N$ and $v=V/N$, proves the [Gibbs-Duhem equation](../../../../../gibbs-duhem-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{d\mu=-s\,dT+v\,dp}.
$$

The underlying Euler relation assumes extensive bulk thermodynamics and no additional work coordinates. The result says that the intensive quantities are constrained; the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) cannot be varied independently of [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) within one homogeneous phase.
