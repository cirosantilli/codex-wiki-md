<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $E$ denote the fluctuating microscopic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and $U=\langle E\rangle$ its mean, at fixed $N,V$. In a [canonical ensemble](../../../../../canonical-ensemble-split.md) with $\beta=1/(k_BT)$,

$$
U=-\partial_\beta\ln Z,\qquad
\partial_\beta^2\ln Z=\langle E^2\rangle-\langle E\rangle^2.
$$

Since $dT/d\beta=-k_BT^2$, this gives

$$
\boxed{\langle(\Delta U)^2\rangle=-\partial_\beta U
=k_BT^2\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_{V,N}
=k_BT^2C_V}.
$$

Here $\Delta U$ denotes an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) fluctuation about the mean, as in the question. The relation is not restricted to ideal gases when the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has no explicit [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence. Alternatively, the stated equilibrium free-energy curvature gives a Gaussian [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) weight proportional to $\exp[-(\Delta U)^2/(2k_BT^2C_V)]$, with the same [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md).

For the [relative energy fluctuation of a monatomic ideal gas](../../../../../relative-energy-fluctuation-of-a-monatomic-ideal-gas.md), $U=3Nk_BT/2$ and $C_V=3Nk_B/2$, hence

$$
\frac{\sqrt{\langle(\Delta U)^2\rangle}}{U}
=\boxed{\sqrt{\frac{2}{3N}}}.
$$

Taking room conditions as approximately $T=300$ K and $p=10^5$ Pa, with $V=10^{-6}$ m$^3$, gives

$$
N=\frac{pV}{k_BT}\simeq2.41\times10^{19},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{\sigma_U}{U}\simeq1.66\times10^{-10}}.
$$

The mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is about $0.15$ J and its [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) about $2.49\times10^{-11}$ J. The tiny relative fluctuation explains why macroscopic equilibrium [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) behaves as a sharply defined thermodynamic quantity. The estimate uses a [thermal reservoir](../../../../../heat-bath.md), not a particle reservoir; allowing $N$ itself to fluctuate changes the relevant fluctuation problem.
