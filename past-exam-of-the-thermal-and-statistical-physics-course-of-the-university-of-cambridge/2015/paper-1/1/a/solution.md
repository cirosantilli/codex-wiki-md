<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [canonical ensemble](../../../../../canonical-ensemble-split.md), $F=-k_BT\ln Z$ and $p=-(\partial F/\partial V)_{T,N}=k_BT(\partial_V\ln Z)_{T,N}$. The kinetic and [factorial](../../../../../factorial.md) factors do not depend on $V$, so differentiating only the volume-dependent terms gives

$$
p=k_BT\left[\frac{N}{V-Nb}-\frac{aN^2}{k_BTV^2}\right].
$$

Thus the [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(p+\frac{aN^2}{V^2}\right)(V-Nb)=Nk_BT},
\qquad V>Nb.
$$

The excluded-volume correction raises [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) relative to the same gas without that correction; the attractive mean-field term lowers it. No [Stirling formula](../../../../../stirling-formula.md) is required for this [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md).
