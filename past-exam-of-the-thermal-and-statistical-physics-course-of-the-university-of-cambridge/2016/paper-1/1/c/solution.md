<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a complete single-particle state, the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle n(\epsilon)\rangle=\frac1{\exp[(\epsilon-\mu_{\rm ch})/(k_BT)]+1}.}
$$

Here $\mu_{\rm ch}$ denotes the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), distinct from the magnetic-moment symbol in (b). At zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) it approaches the [Fermi energy](../../../../../fermi-energy.md) $E_F$ and the occupation has a sharp edge. At $T\ll T_F=E_F/k_B$, thermal changes occur within an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) interval of order $k_BT$ about that edge.

The [Fermi-edge heat-capacity counting](../../../../../fermi-edge-heat-capacity-counting.md) argument uses the fact that only order $g(E_F)k_BT$ [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) can be rearranged because states well below the edge are blocked by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). Each active excitation carries [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of order $k_BT$, so $U(T)-U(0)\sim g(E_F)(k_BT)^2$ and **$C_{V,e}\propto T$**. For a smooth [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md), the [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{C_{V,e}=\frac{\pi^2}{3}g(E_F)k_B^2T.}
$$

Here $g$ is the total single-particle [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md), including [spin](../../../../../spin.md). In a three-dimensional free-electron gas, $g(E_F)=3N/(2E_F)$, giving $C_{V,e}=(\pi^2/2)Nk_B(T/T_F)$. This is the low-temperature normal-state electronic contribution, not the lattice [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) or a description across a superconducting transition.[Sections 3.6.2–3.6.4: Fermi edge, low-temperature heat capacity and Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../../quantum-gases-by-david-tong.md)
