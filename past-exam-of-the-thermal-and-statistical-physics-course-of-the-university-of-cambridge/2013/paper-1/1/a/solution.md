<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the classical equilibrium circuit, the [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) stores $CV^2/2$. [Equipartition](../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) assigns this quadratic term mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $k_BT/2$, so

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm rms}=\sqrt{\frac{k_BT}{C}}\simeq6.4\,\mathrm{\mu V}\quad(T\simeq300\,\mathrm K).}
$$

The mean [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) is zero and its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is $k_BT/C$. Resistance determines the equilibration time and noise spectrum, not this full-band equilibrium [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md). A finite-bandwidth or appreciably loading readout can measure less than the ideal result.
