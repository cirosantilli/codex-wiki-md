<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $H=0$ and $a<0$, the [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is a symmetric double well. The central stationary point is a maximum; the two stable minima are

$$
\boxed{M_0=\pm\sqrt{-\frac ab},\qquad F_{\min}=-\frac{a^2}{4b}.}
$$

These are the magnetisations of the two symmetry-broken [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md), selected by an infinitesimal field or a [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md). An exactly symmetric finite-system ensemble can have $\langle M\rangle=0$ by averaging both wells; that does not make the central maximum a stable homogeneous [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md).
