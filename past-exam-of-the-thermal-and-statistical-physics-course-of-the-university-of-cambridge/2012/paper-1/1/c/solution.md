<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For either [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md), the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) differential at fixed composition is

$$
dS=\frac{C_P}{T}\,dT-\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P dP
=\frac{C_P}{T}\,dT-V\alpha_T\,dP.
$$

The pressure derivative uses the [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) from $dG=-S\,dT+V\,dP$. Differentiating the equality of [entropies](../../../../../entropy-split.md) along coexistence gives the general relation

$$
\frac{\Delta C_P}{T}\,dT=\Delta(V\alpha_T)\,dP.
$$

For a differentiable coexistence curve with pressure varying, equality of [Gibbs free energies](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) also implies $-\Delta S\,dT+\Delta V\,dP=0$. Since $\Delta S=0$, the two [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) then share $V$. Thus, with $\Delta C_P\ne0$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{dT}{dP}=\frac{TV\Delta\alpha_T}{\Delta C_P}.}
$$

This is the [Ehrenfest entropy-continuity relation](../../../../../ehrenfest-entropy-continuity-relation.md). [Entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) equality alone would give $T\Delta(V\alpha_T)/\Delta C_P$ without the shared-volume condition; a constant-pressure boundary or vanishing discontinuities needs separate limiting analysis.
