<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda_T=h/\sqrt{2\pi mk_BT}$ and spacing $a=n^{-1/3}$. [Degeneracy onset from thermal wavelength](../../../../../degeneracy-onset-from-thermal-wavelength.md) occurs at

$$
\boxed{T_\lambda=\frac{h^2n^{2/3}}{2\pi mk_B}.}
$$

The numerical factor depends on the convention for a typical [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), but the scale $\hbar^2n^{2/3}/(mk_B)$ does not. For [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) the [Fermi temperature](../../../../../fermi-temperature.md) $T_F=\hbar^2(3\pi^2n)^{2/3}/(2mk_B)$ gives a comparable scale for spin degeneracy two.

Representative estimates, with the assumed [densities](../../../../../density.md) stated explicitly, are:

| System | Assumed [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) | Assumed particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) | $T_\lambda$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Conduction electrons](../../../../../conduction-electron.md) | $10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ | $m_e$ | $1.2\times10^5\,\mathrm K$ |
| Dilute heavy atomic cloud | $10^{19}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ | $87u$ | $1.6\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm K$ |
| [Neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) at nuclear-scale [density](../../../../../density.md) | $10^{44}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ | $m_n$ | $6.5\times10^{11}\,\mathrm K$ |

Here $u$ is the atomic [mass](../../../../../mass.md) constant. Thus the characteristic orders are **$10^5\,\mathrm K$ for [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), sub-microkelvin for a dilute heavy atomic gas, and $10^{12}\,\mathrm K$ for [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md)**. There is no density-independent quantum-degeneracy [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) for atomic gases: for example, taking $n=2.5\times10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ and [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $4u$ instead gives about $0.065\,\mathrm K$. These are illustrative input estimates, not values supplied by the paper. Denser neutron-star cores require relativistic and interaction corrections; the estimate identifies the degeneracy scale rather than a precise stellar [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
