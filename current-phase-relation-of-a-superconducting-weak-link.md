# Current-phase relation of a superconducting weak link

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting weak link](superconducting-weak-link.md)

The [current-phase relation of a superconducting weak link](current-phase-relation-of-a-superconducting-weak-link.md) gives its pair [electric current](electric-current.md) $I_s$ as a function of the [Gauge-invariant Josephson phase](gauge-invariant-josephson-phase.md) $\phi$, at specified [temperature](temperature-split.md) and other conditions. For the ideal junction used below, $I_s=I_c\sin\phi$ with $I_c>0$. More general periodic relations can contain higher [Fourier harmonics](fourier-harmonic.md); their maximum magnitude defines the corresponding [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md).
