# Hamiltonian perturbation preserving one regular level

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

For independent integrals $G_1=H,G_2,\ldots,G_r$, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $K=H+\frac12\sum_jG_j^2$ agrees with $H$ to first order on the common zero level. On a nonempty level $G=c\ne0$, the vector-field difference is $\sum_jc_jX_{G_j}$ and is nowhere zero by independence of the [differentials](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md). Nonemptiness is necessary if one expresses the conclusion as inequality between restricted [vector fields](vector-field.md).
