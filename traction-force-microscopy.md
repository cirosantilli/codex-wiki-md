# Traction force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[traction force microscopy](traction-force-microscopy.md) estimates forces that cells exert on a deformable substrate from measured substrate displacement and a mechanical model. Calibration, boundary conditions and material properties affect the inferred forces.
