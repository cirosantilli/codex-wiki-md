# Universal conductance fluctuations

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum transport](quantum-transport.md)

A coherent disordered mesoscopic conductor has reproducible, sample-dependent conductance variations when magnetic field, carrier energy or disorder configuration changes. Their typical scale is of order $e^2/h$ in a fully coherent diffusive sample, with numerical factors determined by symmetry, dimensionality and geometry. Thermal averaging and finite [phase coherence length](phase-coherence-length.md) reduce the measured amplitude.
