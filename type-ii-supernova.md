# Type II supernova

↑ **Parent:** [Core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md)

A [type II supernova](type-ii-supernova.md) has conspicuous [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) features in its observed spectrum and is associated with collapse of a massive stellar core retaining a hydrogen-rich envelope. Its spectral label should not be confused with a unique light-curve shape or a unique remnant type.
