# Coordinate basis

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent space](tangent-space.md)

A [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md) with coordinates $x^\mu$ induces a smooth [local frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md), with [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md) $\partial_\mu=\partial/\partial x^\mu$ forming a [basis](basis.md) of each [tangent space](tangent-space.md). Their [Lie brackets of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) vanish because mixed [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) of smooth functions commute. Under a change of coordinates they transform by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md): $\partial/\partial y^\nu=(\partial x^\mu/\partial y^\nu)\partial/\partial x^\mu$, with coefficients given by the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md).
