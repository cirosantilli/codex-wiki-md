# Leading checkerboard Ising decimation recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Checkerboard decimation of the square-lattice Ising model](checkerboard-decimation-of-the-square-lattice-ising-model.md)

For dimensionless nearest-neighbour coupling $K$ and diagonal coupling $L$, the second cumulant in [checkerboard decimation of the square-lattice Ising model](checkerboard-decimation-of-the-square-lattice-ising-model.md) generates $K^2$ for each pair of retained neighbours of an eliminated centre. A retained diagonal pair shares two centres, and an axial pair at distance $2a$ shares one. The displayed two-coupling recursion neglects $K^4$, $K^2L$, and $L^2$, treating $L$ as order $K^2$. Its finite nontrivial [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) is $(K,L)=(1/3,1/9)$. Higher orders generate additional operators, so the closure is a property of the truncation.
