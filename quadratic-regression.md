# Quadratic regression

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial regression](polynomial-regression.md)

A [polynomial regression](polynomial-regression.md) of degree two is linear in its unknown [regression coefficients](regression-coefficient.md) despite being curved in the predictor. Its [design matrix](design-matrix.md) has columns $1,x,x^2$; three distinct predictor values give full column [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md).
