# Minkowski inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Lp norm](lp-norm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_inequality)

For $1\leq p\leq\infty$, the [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) satisfies the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). For $1<p<\infty$, apply the [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) to $\int(|f|+|g|)|f+g|^{p-1}$, then divide by $\|f+g\|_p^{p-1}$ when it is nonzero. The cases $p=1$ and $p=\infty$ follow directly from the pointwise triangle inequality. In particular, a series whose term norms have a finite sum converges in the complete $L^p$ space.
