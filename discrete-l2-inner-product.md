# Discrete L2 inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product.md)

The discrete L2 inner product is the cell-volume-weighted [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-form.md) on a uniform grid. Its associated [norm](norm.md) is the [discrete L2 norm](discrete-l2-norm.md). It provides the finite-dimensional analogue of spatial [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) and continuous [energy method](energy-method.md) identities.
