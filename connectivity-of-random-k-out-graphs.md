# Connectivity of random k-out graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Random k-out graph](random-k-out-graph.md)

For $k=2$, a disconnected [graph](graph-split.md) has a component of size $3\le s\le n/2$. The [probability](probability.md) that a fixed $s$-set has no crossing [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) is at most $(s/n)^{2s}(1-s/n)^{2(n-s)}$. The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) and $\binom ns\le(n/s)^s[n/(n-s)]^{n-s}$ bound the failure [probability](probability.md) by $\sum_{s=3}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}(s/n)^s=o(1)$. Split this sum at $\sqrt n$. The [random k-out graph](random-k-out-graph.md) coupling extends the result to every fixed $k\ge2$.
