# Critical adiabatic index for dipolar accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube](transonic-accretion-in-a-dipolar-flux-tube.md)

For [transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube](transonic-accretion-in-a-dipolar-flux-tube.md), the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) at the [sonic point](sonic-point.md) contains $c_s^2[1/(\gamma-1)-5/2]$. A positive reservoir energy requires $\gamma<7/5$. Compression in the $r^3$ [dipolar flux-tube area](dipolar-flux-tube-area.md) raises the [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) more rapidly than in the $r^2$ geometry of spherical [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md), whose corresponding upper index is $5/3$.
