# Local projector cone in a frustration-free chain

↑ **Parent:** [Detectability lemma](detectability-lemma.md)

To evaluate a layered product on $A_x|\psi_0\rangle$, scan the bond [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md) from right to left. A projection disjoint from the current support commutes through the accumulated operator and disappears against the common [ground state](ground-state.md). Retained projections expand the support by at most one site per layer. Their product $L$ gives a local operator $LA_x$ with both $K^rA_x|\psi_0\rangle=LA_x|\psi_0\rangle$ and $\langle\psi_0|LA_x=\langle\psi_0|A_x$.
