# Noble gas

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_gas)

A [chemical element](chemical-element.md) in group 18 of the [periodic table](periodic-table.md). [Argon](argon.md) is a familiar example: its [chemical inertness](chemical-inertness.md) under many ordinary conditions makes it useful when a process needs a gas that does not readily react with the material being processed. [Chemical inertness](chemical-inertness.md) is a statement about conditions, rather than an assertion that no compound can exist.
