# Debye asymptotic for oscillatory Bessel functions

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md)

For fixed $0<\alpha<\pi/2$, the [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md) has an interior nondegenerate [stationary point](stationary-point.md) in its cosine integral. The [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md) gives the displayed oscillatory leading term and an additive $O(n^{-3/2})$ remainder. The approximation loses uniformity near $\alpha=0$, where the [stationary point](stationary-point.md) reaches the endpoint and the curvature vanishes. For negative integer-order arguments, parity supplies a factor $(-1)^n$.
