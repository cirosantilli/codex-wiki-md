# Positronium

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

[Positronium](positronium.md) is the electromagnetic bound state of an [electron](electron.md) and a [Positron](positron.md). Their equal masses give [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) $m_e/2$, half the hydrogenic Rydberg binding scale and approximately twice the electron-proton separation of hydrogen. Its [spin](spin.md) singlet and triplet differ in hyperfine [energy](energy-split.md); [annihilation](annihilation.md) also affects their physical lifetimes and splitting.
