# Homogeneous solution

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

A homogeneous solution solves the associated linear equation with zero forcing. The difference of any two solutions to the same forced linear equation is homogeneous.

A homogeneous solution satisfies the associated [homogeneous linear differential equation](homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md), obtained by setting the forcing term to zero. Such solutions form the kernel of the differential operator.
