# Centred simple linear regression

↑ **Parent:** [Simple linear regression](simple-linear-regression.md)

In [simple linear regression](simple-linear-regression.md) written as $Y_i=\alpha+\beta(x_i-\bar x)+\varepsilon_i$, the constant and centred predictor columns are orthogonal. Differentiating the sum of squared residuals gives the displayed [ordinary least squares estimators](ordinary-least-squares-estimators.md), provided $S_{xx}=\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2>0$. For independent mean-zero errors of common variance $\sigma^2$, both estimators are unbiased, their variances are $\sigma^2/n$ and $\sigma^2/S_{xx}$, and their covariance is zero. The uncentred intercept is $\widehat\alpha-\widehat\beta\bar x$.
