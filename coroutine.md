# Coroutine

↑ **Parent:** [Continuation (computer science)](continuation-computer-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coroutine)

A computation that can suspend and later resume. A [first-class continuation](first-class-continuation.md) can represent its suspended control state while mutable [computer memory](computer-memory-split.md) remains shared.
