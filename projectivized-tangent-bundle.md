# Projectivized tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Projective bundle](projective-bundle.md)

The projectivized [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) of a positive-dimensional smooth [manifold](topological-manifold-split.md) is the bundle of unoriented one-dimensional subspaces of its [tangent spaces](tangent-space.md). A local tangent trivialization gives $P(TM)|_U\cong U\times\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$. If a base-chart transition is $F$, the line coordinates transform by $[v]\mapsto[DF_xv]$. In any projective chart, this is a ratio of smooth linear expressions with nonzero denominator. It therefore defines a smooth manifold of dimension $2n-1$.

For the two-sphere, a point consists of $x\in S^2$ and a line $L\subset x^\perp$. Choosing a unit vector $v\in L$ gives an oriented [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $(x,v,x\times v)$. A unique [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) takes one chosen frame to another, so $SO_3$ acts transitively. Its [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) consists of the identity and the half-turn about $x$, which reverses $v$ while preserving its unoriented line. Thus $P(TS^2)\cong SO_3/C_2$.
