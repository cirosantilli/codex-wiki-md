# Volume magnetostriction

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostrictive strain](magnetostrictive-strain.md)

[Volume magnetostriction](volume-magnetostriction.md) is the relative [volume](volume.md) change associated with a magnetic-state change. To first order it is the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [magnetostrictive strain](magnetostrictive-strain.md). Zero [trace](matrix-trace.md) is an additional restriction, not a consequence of [isotropy](isotropy.md) alone.
