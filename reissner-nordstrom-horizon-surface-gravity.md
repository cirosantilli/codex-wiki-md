# Reissner-Nordstrom horizon surface gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For $M>0$ and $M\geq|Q|$, put $r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2}$. The stationary [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) normalized at infinity generates the outer [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md). In an ingoing null chart its covector is $dr$ on that horizon; the [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) then gives $\nabla_k k=\tfrac12 f'(r_+)k$, where $f=(r-r_+)(r-r_-)/r^2$. Thus the positive nonextremal [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) is $(r_+-r_-)/(2r_+^2)$. It tends to zero when $M=|Q|$, where the horizon becomes a [degenerate Killing horizon](degenerate-killing-horizon.md). The past horizon branch has the opposite signed nonaffinity for the same time-translation field.
