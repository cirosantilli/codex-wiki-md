<h1 id="b8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The filled [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) $\pi$ [molecular orbital](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) donates into a vacant [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) acceptor orbital directed toward the center of the C=C bond. In the reverse direction, a filled [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) $d$ orbital can overlap with the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) $\pi^*$ [antibonding molecular orbital](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md). These are the donation and [pi backbonding](../../../../../../pi-backbonding.md) components of the [Dewar-Chatt-Duncanson model](../../../../../../dewar-chatt-duncanson-model.md), illustrated in the lower part of the bonding schematic. The [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) is an $\eta^2$ [alkene ligand](../../../../../../alkene-ligand.md), attached through both carbons. Increasing backbonding weakens and lengthens C=C and can push the bonding toward a metallacyclopropane description.

**Zr(IV) is $d^0$, so [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md)-to-[metal](../../../../../../metal.md) donation predominates.** It has suitable empty orbitals but no occupied [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) $d$ set to provide substantial conventional backbonding. An [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)-deficient cationic zirconocene can consequently bind and activate an [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) largely through its Lewis acidity; it should not be drawn as a strongly backbonded late-[metal](../../../../../../metal.md) [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) merely because its formal coordination mode is $\eta^2$.
