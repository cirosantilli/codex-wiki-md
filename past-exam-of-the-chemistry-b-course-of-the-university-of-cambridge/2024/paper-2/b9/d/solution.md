<h1 id="b9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the [chromium](../../../../../chromium.md) complex, Cr(0) is $d^6$ and receives substantial donation from four [carbonyl ligands](../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md). Its occupied orbitals can give [pi backbonding](../../../../../pi-backbonding.md) into the boron-rich acceptor orbitals of the B$_2$N$_2$ ring, while the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md)-rich occupied ring orbitals donate to the [metal](../../../../../metal.md). This favors binding across the ring face, approximately $\eta^4$, with all four ring atoms participating. A nearly planar ring maintains the p-orbital alignment needed for that interaction and accounts for comparable Cr–B and Cr–N distances. A four-[electron](../../../../../electron.md) ring donor also gives the useful count $6_d+8_{\mathrm{CO}}+4_{\mathrm{ring}}=18$.

Ti(IV) in TiCl$_4$ is $d^0$ and strongly [electron](../../../../../electron.md) deficient. It is a good acceptor for the two [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md), but has no filled $d$ set for comparable backbonding into the boron acceptors. **[chromium](../../../../../chromium.md) therefore favors a face-bound pi complex, while [titanium](../../../../../titanium.md) favors N,N sigma coordination.** Donation makes the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) environments more nearly tetrahedral and permits the [2,4-Diazadiboretidine](../../../../../2-4-diazadiboretidine.md) ring to pucker. The borons are not directly bound in the same way, explaining $d(\mathrm{Ti}\cdots\mathrm B)>d(\mathrm{Ti{-}N})$. The distinction follows from [electron](../../../../../electron.md) availability, rather than simply calling every short [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–ring contact a separate ordinary [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md).
