**$B$ is a 1:1 [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md)-bound adduct, with $\mathrm{N\rightarrow AlBr_3}$ at one ring [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md).** Its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is diverted from N-to-B pi donation into the new coordinate bond. That [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) becomes approximately tetrahedral and the ring can pucker. The two B–N bonds adjacent to it lose pi character and lengthen; the neighboring unbound [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) atoms can donate more strongly into those borons, shortening the next pair of B–N bonds. The remaining pair forms a third class. The second structural panel marks these three two-bond sets; it represents connectivity and bond classes, not a measured geometry.

In the slow-exchange structure, two boron atoms flank the bound [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and are equivalent to one another, while the third boron is unique. This gives **two $^{11}$B environments in a $2:1$ ratio**. At room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), rapid exchange of the [Lewis acid](../../../../../../lewis-acid.md) among the three equivalent [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) sites averages every boron over the same environments, giving one [boron-11 nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../../boron-11-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) signal. Cooling slows exchange relative to the resonance-frequency difference and resolves the two environments.

This is [Chemical exchange in NMR](../../../../../../chemical-exchange-in-nmr.md), not restoration of equal instantaneous B–N bonds. A spectrum alone cannot distinguish intramolecular migration from reversible dissociation and reassociation; concentration dependence and crossover experiments would test that distinction. Related N-bound [borazine](../../../../../../borazine.md) adducts have been characterized by diffraction and variable-[temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md), supporting the structural logic while not supplying the exact [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of this particular hexamethyl compound.[2008, pp. 137–142, N-bound adduct structures and variable-temperature NMR](../../../../../../aluminium-and-gallium-trihalide-adducts-of-2-4-6-triorganylborazines-and-the-synthesis-of-triorganylborazinium-tetrabromoaluminates-by-b-gemund-and-others.md)
