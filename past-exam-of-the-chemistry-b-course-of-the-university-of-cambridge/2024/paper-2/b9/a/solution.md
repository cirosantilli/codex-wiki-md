In [borazine](../../../../../borazine.md), each B and N is approximately trigonal planar. The alternating ring contains a sigma framework with perpendicular p orbitals; the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md) can donate into the boron acceptor orbitals. Six pi [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) can therefore be distributed around the ring, giving partial B–N multiple-bond character rather than six ordinary isolated single bonds. Equal ring distances are compatible with this symmetric delocalization.

The [electronegativity](../../../../../electronegativity-split.md) difference makes each B–N bond strongly polar, $\mathrm{B^{\delta+}{-}N^{\delta-}}$. The occupied pi orbitals are weighted toward N, and the empty acceptors toward B. In [benzene](../../../../../benzene.md), equivalent [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) centers give much more even pi distribution and strong resistance to addition that destroys the aromatic system. **[borazine](../../../../../borazine.md) resembles [benzene](../../../../../benzene.md) geometrically but has substantially more polarized, more reactive bonding.** It presents electrophilic boron sites to [nucleophiles](../../../../../nucleophile.md) and [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) donors to [Lewis acids](../../../../../lewis-acid.md). Polar reagents can add across B–N, and [water](../../../../../water.md) can cleave the ring; [benzene](../../../../../benzene.md) ordinarily favors substitution under suitable conditions rather than facile polar addition or [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md). The label “inorganic [benzene](../../../../../benzene.md)” should therefore not be interpreted as identical aromatic stabilization or reactivity. [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md)-bound Lewis-acid adducts and hydrogen-halide additions demonstrate this contrast experimentally.[2008, p. 136 introduction and pp. 137–142, addition chemistry and nitrogen-bound adducts](../../../../../aluminium-and-gallium-trihalide-adducts-of-2-4-6-triorganylborazines-and-the-synthesis-of-triorganylborazinium-tetrabromoaluminates-by-b-gemund-and-others.md)
