<h1 id="b6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A suitable structure is a triangular Ti$_3$ [metal cluster](../../../../../../metal-cluster.md) with one [1,2-Bis(dimethylphosphino)ethane](../../../../../../1-2-bis-dimethylphosphino-ethane.md) chelate and one terminal [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) at each [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md), and one [bridging ligand](../../../../../../bridging-ligand.md) $\mu$-Cl spanning each edge. Include a direct Ti–Ti single [metal-metal bond](../../../../../../metal-metal-bond.md) along each edge. The last panel distinguishes these direct bonds from the [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) bridges.

The six chlorides have total formal charge $-6$, so equivalent centers are **Ti(II), $d^2$**. At each [titanium](../../../../../../titanium.md) the two [phosphorus](../../../../../../phosphorus.md) donors supply four [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), the terminal [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) supplies two, and the two incident [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) bridges each donate a pair in ionic [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md). Two incident Ti–Ti single bonds supply another two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{VE(Ti)}=2_d+4_{\mathrm{P,P}}+2_{\mathrm{Cl}}+2(2)_{\mu\mathrm{-Cl}}+2_{\mathrm{Ti-Ti}}=14.}
$$

Each $d^2$ center can contribute one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) to each of its two edge bonds, making three bonds from six [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) overall. **This is a 14-[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) early-[metal cluster](../../../../../../metal-cluster.md), not an 18-[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) complex.** Forcing the [18-electron rule](../../../../../../18-electron-rule.md) here would invent excessive bond orders. [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) bridges donate to more than one center, so summing local counts is not the same as counting distinct [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) of the whole cluster.
