**The dimer has two unpaired [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and a triplet ground state, $S=1$.** Its approximately threefold-symmetric Fe$_2$(CO)$_3$ core supplies a pair of degenerate frontier [molecular orbitals](../../../../../molecular-orbital.md). The last two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) occupy separate members with parallel [spins](../../../../../spin.md), favored by exchange, rather than pairing in one. A useful qualitative picture is a [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) plus two perpendicular, singly occupied pi half-bonds: together these support a formal Fe–Fe double bond while retaining [paramagnetism](../../../../../paramagnetism.md). The [spin-only magnetic moment](../../../../../spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) for $S=1$ is $\sqrt8\,\mu_B\simeq2.83\,\mu_B$; real moments can include orbital and [spin](../../../../../spin.md)–orbit effects.

The formal 18-[electron](../../../../../electron.md) count does not require every [molecular orbital](../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) to be doubly occupied. It is therefore compatible with an open-shell dimer. The primary structural/magnetic study establishes this unusual [triplet state](../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md); an isolated-atom high-[spin](../../../../../spin.md) $d^7$ diagram would not describe the coupled [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–ligand system.[1985, pp. 4561–4562, triplet electronic structure of the dinuclear 18-electron species](../../../../../dinuclear-18-electron-species-having-a-triplet-ground-state-isolation-characterization-and-crystal-structure-of-photogenerated-eta-5-c5me5-2fe2-mu-co-3-by-josephine-paw-blaha-and-others.md)
