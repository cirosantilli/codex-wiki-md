<h1 id="b7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the ionic assignment, both tetrahedral anions would require Mn(VII), $d^0$. Such a high [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) is strongly oxidizing and is best supported by strong bonding to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Short Mn–O bonds combine good [orbital overlap](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md) with [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md)'s resistance to further oxidation. This stabilizes [permanganate](../../../../../../permanganate.md) relative to a hypothetical all-[sulfide](../../../../../../sulfide.md) analogue.

[sulfide](../../../../../../sulfide.md) is larger, more polarizable and much easier to oxidize than [oxide](../../../../../../oxide.md). An attempted Mn(VII) [sulfide](../../../../../../sulfide.md) would therefore combine an exceptionally oxidizing [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) center with reducing ligands: internal [redox](../../../../../../redox.md) can reduce [manganese](../../../../../../manganese.md) while oxidizing [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md), for example toward S–S-bonded or elemental [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) products. The larger [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) donors also provide less favorable short multiple bonding to the small, highly charged [manganese](../../../../../../manganese.md) center. **The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) compound supports Mn(VII); the [sulfide](../../../../../../sulfide.md) combination is [redox](../../../../../../redox.md)-incompatible.** This predicts instability without requiring a specific unprovided decomposition product.
