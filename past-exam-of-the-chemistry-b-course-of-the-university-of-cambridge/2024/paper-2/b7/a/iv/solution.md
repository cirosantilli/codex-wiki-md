↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**The all-carbonyl [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md), HCo(CO)$_4$, is more acidic.** Loss of $\mathrm H^+$ gives $[\mathrm{Co(CO)_4}]^-$, whose negative charge is stabilized by [pi backbonding](../../../../../../pi-backbonding.md) into four [carbonyl ligands](../../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md). Replacing one CO by [triphenyl phosphite](../../../../../../triphenyl-phosphite.md) reduces the net [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)-withdrawing character of the ligand set relative to four carbonyls. The substituted [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) is more [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) rich and its conjugate-base anion is less stabilized against [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md).

$$
\mathrm{HCo(CO)_3L}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm H^++[\mathrm{Co(CO)_3L}]^-,\qquad L=\mathrm{CO}\ \text{or}\ \mathrm{P(OPh)_3}.
$$

Both [conjugate bases](../../../../../../conjugate-base.md) have a conventional 18-[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count, so the difference is not a missing-[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) argument. It is the relative stabilization of acid and base by the [ligands in coordination chemistry](../../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md). This concerns heterolytic [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md), not the homolytic Co–H [bond-dissociation energy](../../../../../../bond-dissociation-energy.md).
