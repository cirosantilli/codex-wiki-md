<h1 id="b10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The zinc ion is coordinated by protein donors and a [water](../../../../../water.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) placed near the reacting C6 center. **Zinc acts as a [Lewis acid](../../../../../lewis-acid.md) that activates and positions [water](../../../../../water.md).** Its positive charge withdraws [electron density](../../../../../electron-density.md) from [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), lowers the effective p$K_a$ of coordinated [water](../../../../../water.md) and facilitates formation of the attacking [hydroxide](../../../../../hydroxide.md), potentially with assistance from a nearby base. The same [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) interaction can stabilize developing negative charge as the addition intermediate forms. Zinc stays Zn(II); this is [metal ion catalysis](../../../../../metal-ion-catalysis.md), not a zinc [redox](../../../../../redox.md) cycle.

The structure identifies a plausible reaction geometry, but a static X-ray model does not by itself establish the [water](../../../../../water.md)'s [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) state, proton-transfer order or the rate-limiting step. Structural work with bound transition-state analogues independently supports a zinc-containing catalytic center.[1991, pp. 1278–1284, catalytic zinc and the bound transition-state analogue](../../../../../atomic-structure-of-adenosine-deaminase-complexed-with-a-transition-state-analog-understanding-catalysis-and-immunodeficiency-mutations-by-david-k-wilson-and-others.md) An appropriate experimental test would compare native [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md), a [metal](../../../../../metal.md)-depleted preparation and stoichiometrically zinc-reconstituted [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md), checking retained fold and active [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) concentration. Loss and recovery of activity with those controls distinguishes a catalytic [metal](../../../../../metal.md) requirement from nonspecific denaturation.
