<h1 id="b10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The reactive fragment is the C6 amidine-like portion of the [purine](../../../../../purine.md) ring; the N9–[ribose](../../../../../ribose.md) attachment is retained. Write it locally as $\mathrm{N1{=}C6(NH_2){-}}$, with the rest of the fused ring understood. One productive [acid catalysis](../../../../../acid-catalysis.md) route protonates N1, making C6 more electrophilic. [water](../../../../../water.md) then adds at C6 while the C6=N1 pi [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) move onto N1. Subsequent [proton transfers](../../../../../proton-transfer.md) generate a [carbinolamine](../../../../../carbinolamine.md) and then a protonated amino leaving group:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{N1{=}C6(NH_2)}&\xrightarrow{\ +H^+\ }\mathrm{N1H^+{=}C6(NH_2)}\\
&\xrightarrow{\ +H_2O\ }\mathrm{N1H{-}C6(OH_2^+)(NH_2)}\\
&\xrightarrow{\ -H^+\ }\mathrm{N1H{-}C6(OH)(NH_2)}\\
&\xrightarrow{\ +H^+\ }\mathrm{N1H{-}C6(OH)(NH_3^+)}\\
&\xrightarrow{\ -NH_3\ }\mathrm{N1H{-}C6({=}OH^+)}\\
&\xrightarrow{\ -H^+\ }\mathrm{N1H{-}C6({=}O)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are local bonding fragments: C6 retains its bond to the adjoining ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) at every step. In the addition step, an [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) forms C6–O and the C6=N1 [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) opens. During collapse, an [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) reforms C6=O and the C6–N6 bond breaks toward the departing [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md). Solvent mediates each [proton transfer](../../../../../proton-transfer.md); no direct unsupported transfer across a long distance is required.

**[water](../../../../../water.md) supplies the new C6 [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), and [ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) is expelled after [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of the leaving group.** Final [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives the [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) form of [inosine](../../../../../inosine.md). In acidic bulk solution the released [ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) is largely trapped as [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md). The catalytic [proton transfers](../../../../../proton-transfer.md) regenerate acid around the substitution cycle; [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) trapping separately affects the net proton balance. This is [hydrolytic deamination](../../../../../hydrolytic-deamination.md), not cleavage of the [glycosidic bond](../../../../../glycosidic-bond.md).
