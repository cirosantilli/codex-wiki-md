↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**His238 contributes strongly to turnover, with a plausible acid/base or electrostatic role near the attacking [water](../../../../../../water.md).** Replacing its [imidazole](../../../../../../imidazole.md) by the methyl side chain of [alanine](../../../../../../alanine.md) removes both proton-transfer capacity and a positioned polar group, so the large fall in [turnover number](../../../../../../turnover-number.md) fits impaired chemical catalysis. A candidate role is acceptance of a proton from zinc-bound [water](../../../../../../water.md), or stabilization and subsequent proton shuttling around the attacking [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md).

Little change in the [Michaelis constant](../../../../../../michaelis-constant.md) suggests that the major kinetic defect is not a gross loss of [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) recognition, but $K_M$ is not generally a binding [dissociation constant](../../../../../../dissociation-constant.md): $K_M=(k_{-1}+k_{\mathrm{cat}})/k_1$ for the elementary scheme. Nor does reduced $k_{\mathrm{cat}}$ uniquely prove that His238 is the initial general base. Purified-protein controls should check fold, zinc occupancy and active concentration. Importantly, primary mouse-[enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md) experiments found that His238 mutants could still hydrate a [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) analogue, directly illustrating this mechanistic limitation.[1996, abstract and pp. 15019–15028, purine-riboside hydration by mutant enzyme](../../../../../../site-directed-mutagenesis-of-histidine-238-in-mouse-adenosine-deaminase-substitution-of-histidine-238-does-not-impede-hydroxylate-formation-by-v-sideraki-and-others.md)
