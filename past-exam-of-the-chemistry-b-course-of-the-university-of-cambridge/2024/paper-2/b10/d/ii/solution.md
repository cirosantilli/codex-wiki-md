<h1 id="b10/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**An ionizable Asp296 carboxyl group is not essential to the measured catalytic pathway at pH 7.** [Asparagine](../../../../../../asparagine.md) retains a similar side-chain size and an amide donor/acceptor pair, while losing the carboxyl group's ordinary acid/base chemistry and negative charge. Preserved kinetics are therefore consistent with a binding or positioning [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) that survives the conservative substitution, or with a dispensable/redundant contact.

This does not prove that Asp296 makes no interaction in the native [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md), nor that every substitution at that position would be harmless. The displayed Asp296 contact is also distinct from Asp295, which coordinates zinc; their neighboring residue numbers must not be conflated. Other pH values could expose a contribution hidden by the assay condition.
