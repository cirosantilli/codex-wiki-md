<h1 id="b10/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) installed at C6 is derived from solvent [water](../../../../../../water.md).** An attacking zinc-bound [water](../../../../../../water.md) or [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) therefore provides the straightforward explanation of the [oxygen-18](../../../../../../oxygen-18.md) label. The result supports hydrolytic addition rather than direct [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) transfer from a pre-existing protein carboxyl group as the sole [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) source.

It does not by itself establish the identity of the base, the timing of [proton transfers](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md), or exclusion of a transient covalent [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md) intermediate: [water](../../../../../../water.md) could introduce the labeled [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) during breakdown of such an intermediate. Product [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) exchange must also be excluded. Incubate preformed unlabeled [inosine](../../../../../../inosine.md) in labeled [water](../../../../../../water.md) with and without [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md), and use early-time quenched reactions to compare incorporation with product formation. These controls test whether labeling reports the formation step rather than later exchange.
