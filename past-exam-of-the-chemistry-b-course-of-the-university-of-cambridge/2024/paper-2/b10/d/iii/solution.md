↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Glu217 is important for catalysis, most naturally through [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) at the [purine](../../../../../../purine.md) ring.** Changing a [glutamic acid residue](../../../../../../glutamic-acid-residue.md) to [glutamine](../../../../../../glutamine.md) preserves much of the side-chain size and hydrogen-bonding geometry, while removing an ordinary [carboxylic acid](../../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md)/base pair. Its placement near N1 makes acid donation there a plausible way to activate C6 for [water](../../../../../../water.md) addition and stabilize the resulting nonaromatic intermediate. The large turnover defect with little change in the [Michaelis constant](../../../../../../michaelis-constant.md) supports that catalytic interpretation, subject to the same $K_M$ and protein-integrity qualifications as above.

The protonated carboxyl drawn at pH 7 need not behave like free glutamate in [water](../../../../../../water.md): local electrostatics and burial can shift its p$K_a$, and [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) may change during the cycle. The mutation alone cannot choose between an initially protonated Glu217 acid and a Glu217 residue that first accepts and then relays a proton. Primary mutational work supports catalytic roles for active-site [histidine](../../../../../../histidine.md) and glutamate groups while explicitly comparing purified [enzymes](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and zinc content.[1993, pp. 5464–5470, kinetic and zinc-content comparisons](../../../../../../mutational-analysis-of-active-site-residues-of-human-adenosine-deaminase-by-d-bhaumik-and-others.md)
