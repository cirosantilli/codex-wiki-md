↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At ordinary physiological pH, the [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md) side chain is predominantly [guanidinium](../../../../../../guanidinium.md). Its N–H groups are donors, and its positive charge can reinforce favorable electrostatic interactions. **[guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) exposes adjacent O6 and N7 acceptors in the [major groove](../../../../../../major-groove.md)**, allowing two [guanidinium](../../../../../../guanidinium.md) N–H donors to make a paired interaction without breaking Watson–Crick pairing. The diagram places this [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md) contact on the exposed [guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) edge, not between the paired bases.

[adenine](../../../../../../adenine.md) also exposes N7, but at the corresponding neighboring position it has the exocyclic N6 amino donor rather than a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) acceptor. It therefore cannot make the same donor-to-two-acceptors match with [guanidinium](../../../../../../guanidinium.md). A single N7 contact would be less discriminating. The classic structural recognition analysis explicitly proposed paired [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md)–[guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) contacts in the [major groove](../../../../../../major-groove.md).[1976, pp. 804–808, major-groove arginine–guanine recognition model](../../../../../../sequence-specific-recognition-of-double-helical-nucleic-acids-by-proteins-by-nadrian-c-seeman-and-others.md) [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) geometry, hydration and phosphate contacts also affect sequence recognition; this local motif explains a preference rather than guaranteeing that every [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md)-containing [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) recognizes every G–C site.
