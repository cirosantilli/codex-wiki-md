<h1 id="b5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the standard Watson–Crick edges, with the glycosidic attachments at purine N9 and pyrimidine N1. The A–T pair has **two [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md)**: [adenine](../../../../../../adenine.md) N6–H donates to [thymine](../../../../../../thymine.md) O4, and [thymine](../../../../../../thymine.md) N3–H donates to [adenine](../../../../../../adenine.md) N1. The C–G pair has **three**: [cytosine](../../../../../../cytosine.md) N4–H donates to [guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) O6, [guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) N1–H donates to [cytosine](../../../../../../cytosine.md) N3, and [guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) N2–H donates to [cytosine](../../../../../../cytosine.md) O2. The middle and right panels above show these atoms and the sugar-attachment directions.

The major-groove side is the edge toward purine N7 and toward [thymine](../../../../../../thymine.md) O4/its methyl group or [cytosine](../../../../../../cytosine.md) N4. The minor-groove side is toward purine N3 and [adenine](../../../../../../adenine.md) C2–H or [guanine](../../../../../../guanine.md) N2, and toward pyrimidine O2 and the sugar attachments. Groove labels refer to the exposed outer edges of the paired bases; the [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) joining the bases are not themselves empty grooves. The resulting patterns differ even when the bases remain paired inside duplex [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).
