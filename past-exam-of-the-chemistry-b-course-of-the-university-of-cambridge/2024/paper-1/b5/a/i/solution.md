↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**A: [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is the best acceptor; there is no conventional strong O–H or N–H donor.** The N-methylpyrrole [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) contributes its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) to aromatic bonding and is not an ordinary [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) acceptor. A formyl C–H or ring C–H can participate in weak contacts, but is not comparable to the donors in B and C.

**B: [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is the best acceptor and the phenolic OH is the best donor.** Its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) can accept more weakly, but [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) provides the primary basic site in the depicted hydroxypyridine form.

**C: lactam [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is the best acceptor and the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) N–H is the donor.** [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is not a useful acceptor because its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is delocalized toward the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).

**D: the secondary [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is the best acceptor, and its N–H is the only conventional donor, but a weak one here.** Its [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is also an acceptor, although weaker than the [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) site in this comparison. These assignments distinguish functional-group identity and lone-pair availability, rather than counting heteroatoms.
