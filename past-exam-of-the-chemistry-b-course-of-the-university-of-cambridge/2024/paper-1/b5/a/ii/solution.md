4-Fluorophenol probes acceptor strength because it donates its OH [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). The qualitative order is **C $>$ D $>$ B $>$ A**: the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)-rich lactam [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is the strongest acceptor here, the secondary [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) is next, [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is intermediate, and [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is weakest. Resonance donation from [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) increases the lactam [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md)'s basicity relative to an ordinary [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) or [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The N-methylpyrrole site in A does not add an independent [amine](../../../../../../amine.md)-like acceptor.

N-Methylpyrrolidin-2-one is a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) acceptor without N–H, so it instead probes the solute's donor ability. **B $>$ C $\gg$ A and D**: B's phenolic OH is a strong donor, the lactam N–H of C is appreciable, A lacks a conventional donor, and D's neutral secondary-[amine](../../../../../../amine.md) N–H is weak. The [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) can be a good acceptor and a poor donor at the same time; the two properties are not opposites on one scale.

These are association equilibria in cyclohexane, where solvent competition for [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) is weak. The quoted constants should be compared within each probe series, not treated as solvent-independent measures. At 298 K, as an illustrative common temperature, a tenfold increase in $K_a$ corresponds to a binding free-energy advantage $RT\ln10\simeq5.7\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. This does not imply the experiment's temperature was specified, nor that every difference is the energy of one isolated [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md).
