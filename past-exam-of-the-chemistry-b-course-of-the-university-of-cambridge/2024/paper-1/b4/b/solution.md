<h1 id="b4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Acid protonates one methoxy group of [2,2-Dimethoxypropane](../../../../../2-2-dimethoxypropane.md). [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) departure gives the oxocarbenium species $\mathrm{Me_2C(OMe)^+}$, which is captured by one diol [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). After [proton](../../../../../proton.md) transfer, [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) and loss of the remaining methoxy group generate a second oxocarbenium center. Intramolecular attack by the other diol [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) closes the five-membered ring; [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) regenerates acid. The net [transacetalization](../../../../../transacetalization.md) exchanges two methoxy groups for the two diol oxygens, releasing two [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) molecules.

**The two starred [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) centers retain their original configurations, and the two [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) side chains on the [acetonide](../../../../../acetonide.md) are trans.** No C–O bond at either original [stereocenter](../../../../../stereocenter.md) is broken; reaction takes place at the reagent-derived CMe$_2$ acetal [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The apparent change in wedge/dash presentation comes from redrawing the backbone into the five-membered ring, not inversion at a starred center. In the planar pentagon used in the diagram, draw the alkynyl-bearing side chain behind and the $\mathrm{CH_2OPMB}$ side chain in front. The unstarred methyl-bearing center also retains its [configuration](../../../../../mechanical-configuration.md). The acetal [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) itself is not stereogenic because its two methyl substituents are identical.
