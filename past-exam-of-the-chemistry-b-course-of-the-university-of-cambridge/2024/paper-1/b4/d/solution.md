<h1 id="b4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The free propargylic OH first reacts with an aluminium hydride, releasing [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and forming an O–Al bond. Coordination of the nearby [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) to this aluminium center organizes a five-membered O–C–C–C–Al unit. Hydride addition from the opposite side of the activated [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) gives a cyclic [alkenyl aluminate](../../../../../alkenyl-aluminate.md): aluminium bonds to the distal [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), while hydride is delivered to the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) nearer $\mathrm{CH_2O}$.

**The key step is directed trans [hydroalumination](../../../../../hydroalumination.md).** The new C–H and C–Al bonds are anti. Aqueous workup protonolyzes C–Al with retention of its [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) geometry and hydrolyzes the O–Al attachment. Replacing that C–Al bond by C–H therefore produces the **E allylic alcohol**, with $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ and the rest of the chain on opposite [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) sides. A generic syn [hydroalumination](../../../../../hydroalumination.md) drawing followed by simple [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) would predict the wrong geometry.

The free OH provides the directing tether; the silyl-protected [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is not equivalently available. Primary [electrophile](../../../../../electrophile.md)-trapping studies of [Red-Al](../../../../../red-al.md)-treated [propargylic alcohols](../../../../../propargylic-alcohol.md) support the alkenyl-alanate description.[2006, pp. 3761–3764, trans-hydroalumination and trapping of alkenyl alanates](../../../../../trans-hydroalumination-alkylation-one-pot-synthesis-of-trisubstituted-allylic-alcohols-by-neil-f-langille-and-timothy-f-jamison.md)
