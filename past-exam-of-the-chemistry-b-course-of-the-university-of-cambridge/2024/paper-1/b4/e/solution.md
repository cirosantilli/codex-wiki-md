<h1 id="b4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The alcohol [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) of [methanesulfonic anhydride](../../../../../methanesulfonic-anhydride.md). Cleavage of the anhydride S–O bond releases methanesulfonate, and the [amine](../../../../../amine.md) base removes the O–H [proton](../../../../../proton.md), giving the corresponding [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md). This activation preserves the substrate C–O bond while replacing the poor hydroxide leaving group by $\mathrm{OSO_2Me}$.

The base then removes a [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) from the adjacent methylene on the side toward the vinyl [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md). In a favorable antiperiplanar arrangement, C–H [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) form the new C=C bond while the C–OMs bond breaks. **An [E2 elimination](../../../../../e2-elimination.md) creates the [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) between the former alcohol [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and that methylene.** It links the two neighboring unsaturated regions into the extended conjugated triene segment, while the pre-existing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton and vinyl [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) are retained.

$$
\mathrm{{-}C(Me){=}CH{-}CH(OH){-}CH_2{-}CH{=}C(Cl)R}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{{-}C(Me){=}CH{-}CH{=}CH{-}CH{=}C(Cl)R}.
$$

The left-hand polyenoate portion is unchanged. The base is [diisopropylethylamine](../../../../../diisopropylethylamine.md); it accepts acid during [mesylation](../../../../../mesylation.md) and promotes elimination. The product's E/Z mixture concerns the newly formed [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md), and no single geometry needs to be assigned.
