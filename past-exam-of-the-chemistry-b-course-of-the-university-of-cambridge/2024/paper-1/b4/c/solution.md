<h1 id="b4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The reacting [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) pi bond and two Os=O units undergo a concerted cycloaddition to give a five-membered [cyclic osmate ester](../../../../../cyclic-osmate-ester.md). Both new [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) bonds form on the same face. Hydrolysis and [proton](../../../../../proton.md) transfer cleave the Os–O attachments, releasing the vicinal diol and a reduced osmium-containing species.

**This is syn [dihydroxylation](../../../../../dihydroxylation.md) of the terminal [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md).** The terminal [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) becomes $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$, and the adjacent [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) becomes $\mathrm{CH(OH)}$; the remote vinyl [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) remains as drawn. The protected region denoted by $R$ is a spectator for this local mechanism. No explanation of [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) chemoselectivity is required here. Since no terminal reoxidant is specified, do not invent a catalytic regeneration step for Os(VIII); osmium reduction and product release suffice for the stated transformation.
