↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same base first makes $\mathrm{PhC^-(OPh)CN}$. This time addition occurs at a [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) para to the [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md), giving a [sigma-H adduct](../../../../../../sigma-h-adduct.md). The attacked [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) temporarily bears both its original H and the new [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) substituent. Negative charge is again delocalized through the ring toward the nitro oxygens.

A further base removes the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) on that aromatic addition site. Its C–H [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) form an exocyclic bond toward the incoming [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), while that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) expels [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md). This is a base-induced elimination across the new ring–side-chain bond. Redistribution of the [nitronate](../../../../../../nitronate.md)/quinoid pi system restores an [aromatic ring](../../../../../../aromatic-ring.md) and gives the side-chain carbanion $\mathrm{p{-}NO_2C_6H_4{-}C^-(Ph)CN}$. [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) during acidic workup yields **$\mathrm{p{-}NO_2C_6H_4{-}CH(Ph)CN}$**.

This is [vicarious nucleophilic substitution](../../../../../../vicarious-nucleophilic-substitution.md): the ring loses [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) while the [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md) sacrifices its phenoxy leaving group. **[hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) is not expelled as hydride**, and the product's side-chain [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) can be supplied on workup rather than moving directly from the ring in a compulsory hydride shift. Base and isotope experiments in the primary mechanistic work support reversible addition followed by base-induced elimination, while not uniquely choosing every E2/E1cb limit.[1983, pp. 3860–3861, isotope effects, base dependence and hydrogen/halogen competition](../../../../../../reaction-of-organic-anions-part-108-on-the-mechanism-of-the-vicarious-nucleophilic-substitution-of-hydrogen-in-nitroarenes-by-mieczyslaw-makosza-and-tomasz-glinka.md) The displayed para product is consistent with access to the less crowded activated position.
