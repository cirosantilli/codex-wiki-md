<h1 id="b2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Base again generates $\mathrm{PhS^-}$ catalytically. In $\mathrm{Ph_2P(O){-}CH{=}C{=}CH_2}$, attack at the central [allene](../../../../../../allene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) moves the adjacent C=C pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) onto the [phosphorus](../../../../../../phosphorus.md)-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). This makes the stabilized alpha-phosphoryl carbanion

$$
\mathrm{Ph_2P(O){-}C^-H{-}C(SPh){=}CH_2}.
$$

[protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) at that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) gives **$\mathrm{Ph_2P(O){-}CH_2{-}C(SPh){=}CH_2}$**, the second product drawn above. The remaining double bond is terminal, so no E/Z pair is possible. The new [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) bond is at the central [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of the original [allene](../../../../../../allene.md), not at the terminal methylene.

This regiochemistry places the developing negative charge beside the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)-withdrawing phosphine-oxide group, instead of at a less stabilized vinylic position. It is an ionic addition/[proton](../../../../../../proton.md)-transfer cycle, rather than radical [thiol](../../../../../../thiol.md)–ene chemistry. [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) donation by another [thiophenol](../../../../../../thiophenol.md) molecule regenerates [thiolate](../../../../../../thiolate.md). One [thiophenol](../../../../../../thiophenol.md) is incorporated without loss of atoms, giving **$\mathrm{C_{21}H_{19}OPS}$**.
