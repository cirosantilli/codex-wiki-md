<h1 id="b1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**[HMPA](../../../../../../hexamethylphosphoramide.md) coordinates Li$^+$ through its phosphoryl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and makes the anionic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) sites more available for acylation.** Strong cation solvation changes contact [ion pairing](../../../../../../ion-pair.md), chelation and aggregate structure. It can therefore accelerate trapping of both the [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) and [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md) by the [chloroformate](../../../../../../chloroformate.md), helping realize the observed O-acylation pathway.

It is too strong to assert that [HMPA](../../../../../../hexamethylphosphoramide.md) necessarily produces completely free, monomeric anions. In a primary study of a [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) in [THF](../../../../../../tetrahydrofuran.md), low-temperature multinuclear [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) and reaction kinetics identified an [HMPA](../../../../../../hexamethylphosphoramide.md)-coordinated dimer as the reactive form, even with excess [HMPA](../../../../../../hexamethylphosphoramide.md).[2004, pp. 259–268, HMPA titration, aggregate assignment and alkylation kinetics](../../../../../../effects-of-hmpa-on-the-structure-and-reactivity-of-the-lithium-enolate-of-cyclopentanone-in-thf-the-dimer-is-responsible-for-alkylation-and-proton-exchange-reactions-by-masaaki-suzuki-and-others.md) The useful conclusion here is altered [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) coordination and enhanced [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) accessibility; the exact aggregate of this bifunctional substrate is not supplied.
