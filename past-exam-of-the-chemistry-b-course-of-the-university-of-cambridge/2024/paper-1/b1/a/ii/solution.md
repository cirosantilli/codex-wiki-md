<h1 id="b1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Lithium diisopropylamide](../../../../../../lithium-diisopropylamide.md) removes an alpha [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the ethyl side of the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md). The C–H [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) form the C=C part of a [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md), while the C=O pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) move to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The phenyl side has no competing alpha C–H site. Diisopropylamine is the conjugate-acid byproduct.

$$
\mathrm{PhCOCH_2Me}\xrightarrow{\mathrm{LDA}}\mathrm{PhC(O^-Li^+){=}CHMe}.
$$

The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) end of this [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) attacks the primary [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing iodine in $\mathrm{ICH_2CH_2CH_2OMe}$. The C–I bond breaks to [iodide](../../../../../../iodide.md) in an [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md), and [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) reformation gives

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{PhCOCH(Me)CH_2CH_2CH_2OMe}.}
$$

Thus **a three-[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) methoxypropyl chain is attached at the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)**. The remote methyl [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) survives. The hindered [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) base is chosen to generate the [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) rather than to add to the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). No absolute stereochemical preference is specified for the new [stereocenter](../../../../../../stereocenter.md) under these [achiral](../../../../../../achirality.md) conditions.
