<h1 id="b3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**P is H2**, the acetal methine between two oxygens; its only resolved coupling is to the attached methyl, giving the quartet. **S is that C2 methyl**, whose doublet has the same 6.9 Hz coupling. **T is tert-butyl**, integrating to nine equivalent [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) with no adjacent [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)-bound methine [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) across its quaternary attachment.

**U is equatorial H5**, coupled to the four [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) at C4 and C6. The two equivalent equatorial methylene [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) are **Q**, each a doublet of doublets with a 12.3 Hz [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) to its axial partner and a 1.3 Hz coupling to H5. The two equivalent axial methylene [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) are **R**, each with the same 12.3 Hz [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) and a 3.7 Hz [vicinal coupling](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md) to H5. Equivalence between C4 and C6 makes two pairs rather than four separately shifted methylene [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md).

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
J_{P,S}&=6.9\ \mathrm{Hz},\\
|J_{Q_4,R_4}|=|J_{Q_6,R_6}|&=12.3\ \mathrm{Hz},\\
J_{U,R_4}=J_{U,R_6}&=3.7\ \mathrm{Hz},\\
J_{U,Q_4}=J_{U,Q_6}&=1.3\ \mathrm{Hz}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Coupling U to two equivalent R [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and two equivalent Q [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) produces its triplet of triplets. The different small [vicinal couplings](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md) are consistent with oblique H–C–C–H torsions for equatorial H5; the 12.3 Hz value is geminal and must not be mistaken for a large vicinal trans-diaxial coupling.
