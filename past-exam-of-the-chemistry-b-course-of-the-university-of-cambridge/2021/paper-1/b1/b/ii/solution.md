<h1 id="b1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The new ring is formed by intramolecular Friedel-Crafts addition to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).** [titanium tetrachloride](../../../../../../titanium-tetrachloride.md) coordinates the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) of the cyclic [beta-ketoester](../../../../../../beta-ketoester.md), increasing electrophilicity at that [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The nearby electron-rich aryl ring attacks this activated [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) at the available position ortho to its ethyl tether.

The electron-flow sequence is: an aromatic $\pi$ bond forms the new aryl–carbonyl-carbon bond, the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) $\pi$ bond moves onto [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and loss of the aromatic [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) restores [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md). The two-carbon tether and the shared quaternary [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) make the newly closed carbocycle six-membered. Hydrolytic work-up converts the titanium-bound [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) into the tertiary [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md).

The [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) survives. This is an addition to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) with retention of its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) as OH, rather than an acyl substitution that removes that [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The pendant terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) is not involved. These bond changes account for the fused [aromatic ring](../../../../../../aromatic-ring.md) system and the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) in C without needing to assign the new [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md).
