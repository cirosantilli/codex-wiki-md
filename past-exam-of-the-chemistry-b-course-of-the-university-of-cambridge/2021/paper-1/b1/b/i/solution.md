↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathrm{Ar}$ denote the 2,5-dimethoxyphenyl group. **A is the doubly C-alkylated [beta-ketoester](../../../../../../beta-ketoester.md)**

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{EtO-C(=O)-C(CH_2CH_2Ar)(CH_2CH_2CH{=}CH_2)-C(=O)-CH_2-O-tBu}}.
$$

The central [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) has no [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) after the second step. The [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md), [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) and tert-butyl [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) remain intact.

[carbonate](../../../../../../carbonate.md) reversibly removes a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the [methylene group](../../../../../../methylene-group.md) between the two [carbonyl groups](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). The resulting resonance-stabilized [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) attacks the primary iodoethyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of the aryl-containing reagent by an $S_\mathrm N2$ displacement, releasing [iodide](../../../../../../iodide.md). The monoalkylated intermediate still has one [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) at the same activated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). A second [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) followed by $S_\mathrm N2$ attack on the primary [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) introduces the but-3-en-1-yl group.

The beta-dicarbonyl position is much more acidic than the [methylene group](../../../../../../methylene-group.md) next to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) and [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) at the other end, explaining the alkylation site. Counting the retained [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton gives $\mathrm{C_{24}H_{36}O_6}$, including the two [carbonyl groups](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and one terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md). The second panel of the original product drawing specifies this connectivity.
