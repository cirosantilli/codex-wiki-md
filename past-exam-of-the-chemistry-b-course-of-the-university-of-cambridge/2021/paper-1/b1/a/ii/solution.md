<h1 id="b1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) converts its OH group into a [water](../../../../../../water.md) [leaving group](../../../../../../leaving-group.md). Departure of [water](../../../../../../water.md) initially gives the secondary [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md) $\mathrm{CH_3-CH^+-CH(CH_3)_2}$. A **[1,2-hydride shift](../../../../../../1-2-hydride-shift.md)** from the neighboring [methine group](../../../../../../methine-group.md) transfers its C–H bonding pair to the electron-deficient [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md):

$$
\mathrm{CH_3-CH^+-CH(CH_3)_2\longrightarrow CH_3CH_2-C^+(CH_3)_2}.
$$

The resulting tertiary [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md) is the tert-amyl [electrophile](../../../../../../electrophile.md). An aromatic $\pi$ bond of the dimethoxybenzene attacks that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), producing a [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../../arenium-ion.md). Removal of the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the attacked ring [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) restores [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md) and regenerates acid. Repeating this [Friedel-Crafts alkylation](../../../../../../friedel-crafts-alkylation.md) at the symmetry-related second site gives the disubstituted product.

The [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) migration explains the new ethyl group and the absence of a benzylic [methine group](../../../../../../methine-group.md) [proton](../../../../../../proton.md). Simply attaching the original [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) skeleton at its OH-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) would not explain the twelve-proton [NMR singlet](../../../../../../nmr-singlet.md).
