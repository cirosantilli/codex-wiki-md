**Two [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) of the [carboxylic acid](../../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md) form a cyclic carboxylic-acid [dimer](../../../../../../dimer.md).** Let Ar denote the meta-methylphenyl group. One [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) donates its OH [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) to the other's [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and the second donates back:  
The two contacts close an eight-membered hydrogen-bonded ring, shown explicitly below.

Both [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) therefore act once as hydrogen-bond donor and once as hydrogen-bond acceptor, giving two O–H···O=C contacts. This is the conventional self-complementary [dimer](../../../../../../dimer.md) motif; the schematic is not a prediction of every weaker contact in an unprovided [crystal structure](../../../../../../crystal-structure.md).
