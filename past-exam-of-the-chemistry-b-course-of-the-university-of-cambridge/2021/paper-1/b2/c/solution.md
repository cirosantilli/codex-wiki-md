<h1 id="b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Label the [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) bonded to [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) C1 and its [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) partner C2; C6 is the other [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) next to C1. **B is the C-alkylated [iminium ion](../../../../../iminium.md) [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md)**. Its new substituent at C2 is $\mathrm{-CH_2-C(CO_2Et){=}CH_2}$, while C1 is double-bonded to the [pyrrolidine](../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), which bears positive charge. [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md) is the [counterion](../../../../../counterion.md), accounting for Br in the [empirical formula](../../../../../empirical-formula.md) without requiring a retained C–Br bond.

The C2 end of the [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md) attacks the bromomethyl [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) by $S_\mathrm N2$ displacement. Simultaneously, donation of the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) creates the C1=N bond. This is ordinary [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md) C-alkylation, not N-alkylation.

[proton transfer](../../../../../proton-transfer.md) removes an alpha [proton](../../../../../proton.md) at C6, converting the [iminium ion](../../../../../iminium.md) into the [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md) regioisomer C1=C6. That [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md) attacks the terminal $\mathrm{CH_2}$ of the tethered acrylate in an intramolecular [Michael addition](../../../../../michael-addition.md). [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) donation restores the [iminium ion](../../../../../iminium.md), and [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) completes the sequence.

The new bridge is $\mathrm{C2-CH_2-CH(CO_2Et)-CH_2-C6}$. Together with the original ring and its C1 bridge this produces the **bicyclo$[3.3.1]$nonane [iminium ion](../../../../../iminium.md) skeleton** in C. It retains the [pyrrolidine](../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) group and the [ester](../../../../../ester.md); hydrolysis to a [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) is not part of the displayed reaction. The second panel of the original annulation figure shows the [chemical atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md) connectivity and distinguishes ionic [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md) in B from a covalent [bromine](../../../../../bromine.md) substituent.
