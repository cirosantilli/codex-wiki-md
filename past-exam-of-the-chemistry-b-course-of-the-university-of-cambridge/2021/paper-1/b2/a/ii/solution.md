<h1 id="b2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an **amine-first pathway**, use [triethylamine](../../../../../../triethylamine.md) as a [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md) in a reversible [conjugate addition](../../../../../../conjugate-addition.md). Its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) attacks C3 of the [enone](../../../../../../enone.md); [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) shifts through C2 and the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) give a zwitterionic [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) with a C3–$\mathrm{NEt_3^+}$ bond.

The C2 nucleophilic site of that [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) attacks $\mathrm{Br_2}$, installing C2–Br and releasing [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md). Removal of C2–H by base then reforms C2=C3 while the C3–N bond breaks back to neutral [triethylamine](../../../../../../triethylamine.md). This addition–bromination–elimination sequence gives the same **C2-brominated [enone](../../../../../../enone.md)** and regenerates the nucleophilic amine, with another base equivalent accepting the eliminated [proton](../../../../../../proton.md).

These are alternative formal sequences requested by the question, not a kinetic determination that one must dominate under unspecified conditions. In particular, the amine-first sequence does not require [triethylamine](../../../../../../triethylamine.md) to generate a high concentration of a vinylic [carbanion](../../../../../../carbanion.md) by direct [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md).
