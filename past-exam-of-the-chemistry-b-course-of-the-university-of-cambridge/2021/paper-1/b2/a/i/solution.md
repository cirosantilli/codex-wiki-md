<h1 id="b2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Number the [enone](../../../../../../enone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C1, the adjacent [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C2 and the ethoxy-bearing [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C3. In the **bromine-first pathway**, the enol-ether-like $\mathrm{C2{=}C3-OEt}$ unit attacks $\mathrm{Br_2}$ at C2, breaking the Br–Br bond. The intermediate has a new C2–Br bond and positive charge at C3, stabilized as an oxonium [chemical resonance](../../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) contributor by donation from the ethoxy [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md).

[triethylamine](../../../../../../triethylamine.md) removes the remaining C2 [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). Its C–H bonding pair reforms C2=C3 and neutralizes the oxonium system. The result is **replacement of the vinylic C2–H by C2–Br**, with the original [enone](../../../../../../enone.md) and ethoxy group retained. If [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) first captures the cationic intermediate, the equivalent vicinal dibromide can eliminate HBr to reach the same product.

The ethoxy group makes this [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) sufficiently electron-rich for the electrophilic-addition description. It is important to place [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) at C2 and restore the [double bond](../../../../../../double-bond.md), rather than stop at a saturated addition product.
