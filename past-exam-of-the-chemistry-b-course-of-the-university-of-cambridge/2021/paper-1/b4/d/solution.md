<h1 id="b4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The hydroxyl group first binds [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md), normally by [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) to an [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md) [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md) with release of [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). Coordination also brings [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md) close to the [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md). **[directed trans-hydroalumination](../../../../../directed-trans-hydroalumination.md)** then puts [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) on the [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) proximal to CH2O and [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md) on the distal [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md).

One useful mechanistic description has external [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) attacking the coordinated [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) opposite the aluminium-containing face. The resulting [vinylaluminate](../../../../../vinylaluminate.md) is stabilized by a five-membered O–Al–C–C–CH2 chelate. This is not the ordinary undirected syn addition of a neutral dialkylaluminium [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md). Propargylic-alcohol-directed trans-hydroalumination with [Red-Al](../../../../../red-al.md) gives [vinylaluminate](../../../../../vinylaluminate.md) intermediates that can be intercepted by [electrophiles](../../../../../electrophile.md).[Abstract, stereoselective vinylalanate formation from propargylic alcohols](../../../../../trans-hydroalumination-alkylation-one-pot-synthesis-of-trisubstituted-allylic-alcohols-by-neil-f-langille-and-timothy-f-jamison.md)

[iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) replaces the distal C–Al bond with C–I without scrambling the [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) geometry; aqueous work-up releases the [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) and [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) are consequently **trans** across the [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md), while [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) and the CH2OH group are on the same side.

By [Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority rules](../../../../../cahn-ingold-prelog-priority-rules.md) priority the product is therefore **$Z$**: CH2OH outranks H at one [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and I outranks CH2R' at the other. Describing the two [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) chains as trans would not make this an $E$ [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md), because the [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) changes which substituent has higher priority.
