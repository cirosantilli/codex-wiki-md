<h1 id="b4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) of methanesulfonyl [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md), [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) leaves, and [triethylamine](../../../../../triethylamine.md) removes the O–H [proton](../../../../../proton.md). This gives an **O-mesylate with retention of the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) configuration**: the original [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) bond has not been displaced.

An accessible chair places the [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md) axial. [triethylamine](../../../../../triethylamine.md) then removes the anti axial [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) on the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) between the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) and [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md). Its C–H bond forms the new C=C bond while the C–O bond expels [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md). The result is the **conjugated cyclohexenone**.

The neighboring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) on the [acetonide](../../../../../acetonide.md) side has its relevant axial position occupied by its [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) substituent, rather than an anti axial [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). The elimination toward the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) is therefore favored both by the trans-diaxial geometry and by [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) of the resulting [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) with C=O. The [acetonide](../../../../../acetonide.md) remains intact. No inversion of the original [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) is required during [mesylation](../../../../../mesylation.md), and it becomes planar during elimination.
