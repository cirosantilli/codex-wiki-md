<h1 id="b4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**B is the major product: the newly formed OH is the dashed bond in the printed zigzag structure.** Retain the existing solid-wedge OH and the configuration at the carbamate-bearing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md).

[zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) coordinates the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and the adjacent hydroxyl [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), forming a five-membered O–Zn–O chelate. This constrains rotation around the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)–alpha-carbon bond. In the favored chelated [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md) the bulky $\mathrm{CH(NHCO_2R')CH_2OR}$ group shields one face of the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md), while the alpha [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) leaves the other more accessible. [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) attacks from that less crowded face, the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) $\pi$ bond opens onto [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), and aqueous work-up gives the second [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md).

Mapping this **[Cram chelation model](../../../../../cram-chelation-model.md)** back onto the printed zigzag gives B, not A. Merely placing a new OH “on the same side” of an unrotated page drawing is not a valid [chelation](../../../../../chelation.md) argument: the reactive chelate and that drawing use different [conformations](../../../../../conformation.md). Zinc-borohydride reductions of alpha-hydroxy [ketones](../../../../../ketone.md) are experimentally known to favor the corresponding anti-1,2-diol family under [chelation](../../../../../chelation.md) control.[Abstract, chelation-controlled zinc-borohydride reductions](../../../../../stereoselective-reduction-of-2-hydroxy-ketones-towards-syn-and-anti-1-2-diols-by-s-m-husain-and-others.md)

The reagent combination can generate [zinc borohydride](../../../../../zinc-borohydride.md) and related zinc-associated reducing species. The stereochemical justification is coordination-controlled facial selectivity; it does not require identifying a unique solution aggregate from the reagent list. The quoted yield is not itself a measurement of a 95:5 diastereomeric ratio.
