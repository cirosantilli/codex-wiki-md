<h1 id="b4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The first step is a **vinylogous [aldol condensation](../../../../../aldol-condensation.md) at C5 of the unsaturated [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md)**. Number [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) N1, the [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) C2, and then the methoxy-substituted [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) C3=C4 and the [methylene group](../../../../../methylene-group.md) C5.

[hydroxide](../../../../../hydroxide.md) reversibly removes a C5 [proton](../../../../../proton.md). The resulting [vinylogous enolate](../../../../../vinylogous-enolate.md) is delocalized through C5–C4–C3–C2–O: it is not necessary to create an unstabilized isolated [carbanion](../../../../../carbanion.md) beside [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md). Its C5 nucleophilic contributor attacks acetone. [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) gives a C5-linked tertiary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md), $\mathrm{C5-C(OH)(Me)_2}$. Further removal of the remaining C5 [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and elimination through an aldol-dehydration sequence forms **C5=C(Me)2**, leaving the original methoxy [enol ether](../../../../../enol-ether.md) in place.

In the second step, **aqueous acid hydrolyses that [enol ether](../../../../../enol-ether.md) to a [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) at C4**. [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) at C3 gives an oxygen-stabilized cation at C4. [water](../../../../../water.md) attacks C4, [proton transfers](../../../../../proton-transfer.md) form a hemiacetal-like intermediate, and loss of [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) followed by [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) produces C4=O and the C3 [methylene group](../../../../../methylene-group.md).

The [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and N–H group remain, as does the exocyclic isopropylidene group. This accounts for replacement of the C3=C4–OMe unit by C3H2–C4=O without inserting a new [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) into the ring skeleton.
