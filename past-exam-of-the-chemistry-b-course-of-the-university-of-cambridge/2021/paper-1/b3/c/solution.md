<h1 id="b3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Number the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) N1, the phenyl-bearing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C2, and then continue through C3–C6 around the piperidine. Call the solid-wedge [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) face up; the [phenyl group](../../../../../phenyl-group.md) is down in both starting structures. The bulky [phenyl group](../../../../../phenyl-group.md) favors a pseudoequatorial orientation.

[epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) opening follows the **Fürst-Plattner preference for a chair-like, trans-diaxial opening trajectory**. Attack at the two [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md) is not equivalent: the favored route evolves toward an ordinary chair, whereas the alternative demands a less favorable twist/boat-like distortion. Such conformational control of epoxy-2-arylpiperidine opening is supported by synthetic studies.[Scheme 1, chair-like trans-diaxial epoxide opening; applied here with the free-NH conformer](../../../../../1-3-allylic-strain-as-a-strategic-diversification-element-for-constructing-libraries-of-substituted-2-arylpiperidines-by-thomas-c-coombs-and-others.md)

For the first [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md), **azide attacks C5 from down**, breaking the C5–O bond. The residual C4–O bond is retained, so work-up gives **C5–N3 down and C4–OH up**, with the original C2–Ph down. For the second, down-face [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md), **azide attacks C4 from up**, leaving **C4–N3 up and C5–OH down**. These assignments are shown in the middle row of the original stereochemical map.

In each mechanism an azide terminal [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) attacks the [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), the C–O bonding pair goes to [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), and [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) gives the [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). Each attacked center inverts, and the azide and OH are trans. The phenyl-bearing center is unchanged. Reversing the [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) face changes which [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) has the favored axial attack trajectory; a steric rule based only on distance from phenyl misses that conformational effect.
