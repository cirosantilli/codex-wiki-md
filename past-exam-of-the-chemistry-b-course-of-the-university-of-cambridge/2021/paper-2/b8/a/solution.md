↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $z$ be normal to the Cp ring through its centre. The five [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $p_z$ orbitals form [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) with angular patterns $m=0$, $|m|=1$, and $|m|=2$. The all-in-phase $a_1$ combination has sigma symmetry about this axis and can interact with $d_{z^2}$. The $e_1$ pair matches $(d_{xz},d_{yz})$ by pi symmetry, while the $e_2$ pair matches $(d_{xy},d_{x^2-y^2})$ by delta symmetry. Rotating within either degenerate pair changes the drawing but not the physical interaction.

For the usual eta-five geometry, **the [metal](../../../../../metal.md) $d$ interactions are ranked qualitatively pi \> delta \> sigma**. The $d_{xz},d_{yz}$ lobes overlap effectively with the occupied ring combinations. The delta interaction is weaker and involves the higher, initially unoccupied Cp combinations; the $d_{z^2}$ interaction with the ring is particularly weak because its axial and equatorial parts contribute little net overlap in this geometry. Thus the statement that a [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) is always stronger than a [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) cannot be transferred indiscriminately to this delocalized ring–[metal](../../../../../metal.md) overlap problem. [metal](../../../../../metal.md) $s$ and $p_z$ orbitals also have the symmetry of the $a_1$ [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) combination and contribute to overall bonding, so weak $d_{z^2}$ overlap does not mean the entire sigma-symmetry channel is absent.

In ionic [electron counting](../../../../../electron-counting.md), Cp is $\mathrm{C_5H_5^-}$ with six pi [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in its three occupied ring combinations, hence **a six-electron donor**. The same [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) is assigned five [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in neutral counting.
