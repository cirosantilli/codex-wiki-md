<h1 id="b9/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each trisilanol cage loses its three hydroxyl protons and supplies a **facial tridentate [silanolate ligand](../../../../../../silanolate-ligand.md) of charge $3-$**. Two such [coordination ligands](../../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) replace all six chlorides of [tungsten](../../../../../../tungsten.md)(VI), giving

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{W}\{\mathrm{Cy_7Si_7O_9(O)_3}\}_2}
$$

with approximately octahedral $\mathrm{WO_6}$ coordination. [tungsten](../../../../../../tungsten.md) remains $+6$, is $d^0$, and is bound to six [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) donors, three from each cage. The six liberated protons combine with the six chlorides to give six HCl; no W=O bond or [tungsten](../../../../../../tungsten.md) reduction is needed. The [molecular formula](../../../../../../molecular-formula.md) is $\mathrm{C_{84}H_{154}Si_{14}O_{24}W}$. This bis(cage) [tungsten](../../../../../../tungsten.md) structure was directly prepared and characterized in the primary literature.[Inorganic Chemistry 37, 6583–6586: synthesis from tungsten hexachloride and the cyclohexyl-substituted trisilanol](../../../../../../preparation-and-characterization-of-a-bis-silsesquioxane-tungsten-complex-by-peter-smet-and-others.md)
