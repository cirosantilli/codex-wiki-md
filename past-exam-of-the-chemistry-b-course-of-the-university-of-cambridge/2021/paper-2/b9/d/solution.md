<h1 id="b9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Ring cleavage of [1,3,5-Trioxane](../../../../../1-3-5-trioxane.md) regenerates **[formaldehyde](../../../../../formaldehyde.md)**, whose strong [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) stabilizes the monomer; in water much of it is further stabilized as its hydrate. [acetal hydrolysis](../../../../../acetal-hydrolysis.md), particularly under acid catalysis, provides a route between the cyclic trimer and these monomeric forms.

The corresponding formal depolymerization of a [siloxane](../../../../../siloxane.md) would create **$\mathrm{R_2Si=O}$ [silanone](../../../../../silanone.md) units**. Such ordinary small-substituent [silanones](../../../../../silanone.md) have much less favourable pi bonding and are very reactive toward forming new Si–O–Si linkages. The connected [siloxane](../../../../../siloxane.md) is consequently preferred over a persistent aqueous solution of [silanone](../../../../../silanone.md) monomers. This explains the intended contrast, but aqueous stability is conditional: acid or base can catalyse [siloxane](../../../../../siloxane.md) cleavage to [silanols](../../../../../silanol.md), and those [silanols](../../../../../silanol.md) can condense again. Neither neutral-water inertness of every [siloxane](../../../../../siloxane.md) nor instantaneous neutral-water dissociation of every trioxane should be inferred.
