↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the two-atom inorganic backbone unit, $Z_{\rm Si}+Z_{\rm O}=14+8=22$ and $Z_{\rm P}+Z_{\rm N}=15+7=22$. Their valence-electron sums also agree: $4+6=5+5=10$. Since each unit bears the same two organic groups, **$\mathrm{R_2SiO}$ and $\mathrm{R_2PN}$ are [isoelectronic species](../../../../../isoelectronic-species.md)**. This electron-count relationship does not require identical bond localization, bond angles or chemical reactivity.
