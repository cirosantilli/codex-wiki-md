<h1 id="b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed $+2$ [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md), increasing [effective nuclear charge](../../../../../effective-nuclear-charge.md) across the first transition series generally contracts the [ionic radius](../../../../../ionic-radius.md). The first expectation is therefore **shorter average [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–[nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–[sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) distances toward copper**. This is a trend in comparable coordination environments, rather than a requirement that every individual contact shorten monotonically. The occupation of [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–[coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) [antibonding orbitals](../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md), donor identity and lattice distortion all affect the observed distances.

The high-spin $d^5$ and $d^6$ ions both have two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in the strongly sigma-antibonding $e_g$ set, so adding the sixth [electron](../../../../../electron.md) to $t_{2g}$ does not itself introduce another sigma-antibonding [electron](../../../../../electron.md). Copper(II), however, has $e_g^3$ occupation and is subject to the [Jahn-Teller effect](../../../../../jahn-teller-effect.md). A [tetragonal Jahn-Teller distortion](../../../../../tetragonal-jahn-teller-distortion.md) commonly gives four shorter and two longer contacts, so an elongated copper contact can oppose the overall size trend. Here the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) donors are already chemically inequivalent; the supplied schematic alone does not determine which trans pair becomes longest. One should compare actual M–N and M–S sets separately, rather than infer a unique axis or a numerical distance from the [magnetic moments](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The experimentally determined structures illustrate why the donor geometry and individual contacts matter.[Crystallographic characterization and comparison of the layered structures](../../../../../strengthening-the-magnetic-interactions-in-pseudobinary-first-row-transition-metal-thiocyanates-m-ncs-2-by-euan-n-bassey-and-others.md)
