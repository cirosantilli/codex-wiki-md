<h1 id="b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each [thiocyanate](../../../../../thiocyanate.md) is a **three-metal bridge**, $\mu_3$-$\kappa N:\kappa S:\kappa S$: its [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) donates to one [metal](../../../../../metal.md) and its [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) donates to two other [metals](../../../../../metal.md). Thus the [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) is ambidentate, but the structure uses both ends and shares the [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) donor. Each [metal](../../../../../metal.md) receives two [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and four [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) donors, giving an approximately octahedral $\mathrm{MN_2S_4}$ [coordination sphere](../../../../../coordination-sphere.md). Six donor contacts per [metal](../../../../../metal.md) are consistent with two [coordination ligands](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) per [metal](../../../../../metal.md) because each [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) contributes three contacts across the lattice. Sharing these contacts produces a [coordination polymer](../../../../../coordination-polymer.md) within each layer; the interlayer [Van der Waals force](../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) does not add coordination bonds. The index $\mu_3$ counts [metal](../../../../../metal.md) centres, whereas a [hapticity](../../../../../hapticity.md) label would count adjacent coordinated [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) atoms and would not describe this connectivity correctly.[Structural discussion of the layered first-row metal thiocyanates](../../../../../strengthening-the-magnetic-interactions-in-pseudobinary-first-row-transition-metal-thiocyanates-m-ncs-2-by-euan-n-bassey-and-others.md)
