A distinct possibility is **terminal [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) coordination**, $\mathrm{M\leftarrow S-C\equiv N}$. Unlike the [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) donor in the layer, this [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) need bind only one [metal](../../../../../metal.md). The limiting $\mathrm{^-S-C\equiv N}$ [resonance structure](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) suggests **$r_{\rm CN}\approx1.15\,\mathring{\mathrm A}$ and $r_{\rm CS}\approx1.8\,\mathring{\mathrm A}$**. These are single-/triple-bond-based estimates, not exact predictions for an unspecified [metal](../../../../../metal.md) complex. The M–S–C linkage should be bent, consistent with [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) lone-pair donation.
