<h1 id="b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For **A, linear [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) coordination with predominantly nitrile-like bonding** is consistent with all three measurements:

$$
\mathrm{M\leftarrow N\equiv C-S^-}.
$$

The short C–N and long C–S distances correspond approximately to a [triple bond](../../../../../triple-bond.md) and a [single bond](../../../../../single-bond.md). An approximately $sp$ [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) donor has its donor [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) directed along the N–C axis, explaining the linear M–N–C angle.

For **B, bent [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) coordination with increased $\mathrm{^-N=C=S}$ [chemical resonance](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) character** accounts for the longer C–N and shorter C–S bonds. In this limiting description both internal bonds have substantial double-bond character and [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) is approximately $sp^2$. One [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) can bind a [metal](../../../../../metal.md) with an M–N–C angle near $120^\circ$; alternatively, donation of both [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md) can give a trigonal, nitrogen-bridging unit:

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\mathrm{M}\\[-2pt]
\diagdown\\[-3pt]
\mathrm{N=C=S}\\[-3pt]
\diagup\\[-2pt]
\mathrm{M'}
\end{array}
$$

The nitrogen-bridging drawing is a compatible proposed bonding mode, but the listed internal distances and one angle alone do not prove the number of attached [metals](../../../../../metal.md). In particular, B cannot be identified simply as terminal [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) coordination: the specified angle explicitly contains an M–N bond. [resonance structures](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) explain relative [bond orders](../../../../../bond-order.md); they do not imply an isolated [coordination ligand](../../../../../ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) with permanently localized integer bonds.
