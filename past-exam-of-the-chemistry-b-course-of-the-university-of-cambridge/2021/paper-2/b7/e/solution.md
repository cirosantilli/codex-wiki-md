<h1 id="b7/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Photoinduced CO loss gives a 16-electron fragment. In the ester-substituted complex the pendant [ester](../../../../../ester.md) carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) can reach [chromium](../../../../../chromium.md) and capture that vacant site. A suitable structure is **$\mathrm{Cr}(\eta^6\!\text{-}\mathrm{C_6H_5CO_2Me})(\mathrm{CO})_2(\kappa O\!\text{-ester})$**, retaining the coordinated [arene](../../../../../arene.md) and adding intramolecular coordination of its [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). The added [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) lone-pair donation restores an 18-electron count. The gaseous by-product is **[carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md)**.

[heptane](../../../../../heptane.md) is a poor donor, but [intramolecular ligand trapping](../../../../../intramolecular-ligand-trapping.md) avoids needing a donor solvent. Benzene has no pendant donor. Although the [methoxy group](../../../../../methoxy-group.md) has [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md), its directly attached [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) does not have the same reach and favourable geometry as the projecting [ester](../../../../../ester.md) carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md); possession of a [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) alone is insufficient. For these two substrates, [acetonitrile](../../../../../acetonitrile.md) can trap the unsaturated fragment through [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) to give $\mathrm{Cr}(\eta^6\!\text{-arene})(\mathrm{CO})_2(\mathrm{NCMe})$. Without a suitable trap, CO recombination can reverse the initial photochemical dissociation.
