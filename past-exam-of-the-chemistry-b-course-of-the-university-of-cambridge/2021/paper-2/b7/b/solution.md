<h1 id="b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [arene](../../../../../arene.md) and three [carbonyl ligands](../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md) are neutral, so **[chromium](../../../../../chromium.md) is in [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) zero**, with a $d^6$ [metal](../../../../../metal.md) count. The eta-six [arene](../../../../../arene.md) contributes six [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and each CO contributes two:

$$
\boxed{6+6+3(2)=18\text{ valence electrons}.}
$$

The [methoxy group](../../../../../methoxy-group.md) changes the electronic properties of the [arene](../../../../../arene.md) without changing this formal [electron](../../../../../electron.md) count. The complex is a [piano-stool complex](../../../../../piano-stool-complex.md), with the coordinated [arene](../../../../../arene.md) as the seat and the three [carbonyl ligands](../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md) as the legs.
