<h1 id="b2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [cyclohexanone](../../../../../../cyclohexanone.md) [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) attacks the beta [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of the cyclopentenone. Its [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) forms an [iminium](../../../../../../iminium.md) C=N bond as the [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) forms the new C–C bond; simultaneously the [enone](../../../../../../enone.md) pi system shifts onto its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) converts the resulting cyclopentanone [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) back to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).

A final [iminium](../../../../../../iminium.md) [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) regenerates an [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md), but the position of its double bond matters. Label the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md)-bearing [cyclohexane](../../../../../../cyclohexane.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) $h_1$, its initially nucleophilic neighbour $h_2$, and the other neighbour $h_6$. After addition, $h_2$ bears the cyclopentanone [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md). **[deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) at $h_6$ gives $h_1{=}h_6$, the less substituted, less hindered [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) drawn in A.** Merely restoring the original $h_1{=}h_2$ bond would give a different regioisomer. Interconversion through the [iminium ion](../../../../../../iminium.md) permits this [enamine regioisomerization](../../../../../../enamine-regioisomerization.md) under the reaction conditions. The methyl and [mesylate](../../../../../../mesylate.md) [substituents](../../../../../../substituent.md) are spectators during this first [Michael addition](../../../../../../michael-addition.md).
