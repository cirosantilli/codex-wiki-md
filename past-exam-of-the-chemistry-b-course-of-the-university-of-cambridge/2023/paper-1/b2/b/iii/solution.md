<h1 id="b2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Warm C with dilute aqueous [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) to hydrolyse the [iminium ion](../../../../../../iminium.md) to the second [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).** [water](../../../../../../water.md) attacks the [iminium](../../../../../../iminium.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), [proton transfers](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) give a [carbinolamine](../../../../../../carbinolamine.md), and C–N cleavage releases [pyrrolidine](../../../../../../pyrrolidine.md), present predominantly as its [ammonium](../../../../../../ammonium.md) salt in the acidic medium. The two [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bonds constructed in the preceding Michael additions remain intact. This replaces the [iminium](../../../../../../iminium.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md) by [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and gives the diketone D; neither [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) of the skeleton nor [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) of the original [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) is required.
