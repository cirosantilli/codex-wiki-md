↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The phenolic O–H is most acidic; the benzylic CH2 adjacent to the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) is second.** Phenolic [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives a [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md) stabilized by [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md). Removing an alpha-[amide](../../../../../../amide.md) C–H gives an [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md)/carbanion stabilized by the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and the adjoining aryl group, but requires substantially stronger basicity for a comparable equilibrium concentration. The morpholine [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is an acylated tertiary [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and has no N–H [proton](../../../../../../proton.md). The other morpholine and aromatic C–H positions do not have the same direct [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) stabilization.
