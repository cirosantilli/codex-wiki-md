<h1 id="b2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) removes an alpha-[amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from G, giving a [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md). Its intramolecular attack on the nitroaryl ipso [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), which currently bears the [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), forms a five-membered cyclic [Meisenheimer complex](../../../../../../meisenheimer-complex.md). Count its atoms along the existing tether: alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), the original phenyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing the side chain, its adjacent phenoxy [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and attacked nitroaryl ipso [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The ortho [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md) again stabilizes the temporarily dearomatized [anion](../../../../../../anion.md).

Rearomatization now breaks the nitroaryl–O bond, leaving [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md) on the original ring. [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) gives **$\mathrm{(2{-}HOC_6H_4){-}CH(2{-}NO_2C_6H_4){-}C(=O)N_{morph}}$**. This is a [Truce-Smiles rearrangement](../../../../../../truce-smiles-rearrangement.md): the nitroaryl group migrates from [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) to [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), while the original phenoxy [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) stays on its original ring and becomes OH. No nitro-group migration or [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) cleavage is needed. G and H have identical formulas; only the bond connections and a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) position change. [carbonate](../../../../../../carbonate.md) need not produce a high equilibrium [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) concentration: a small population can be consumed by favourable intramolecular cyclization and rearomatization. The specified transformation alone does not establish its detailed [proton](../../../../../../proton.md)-transfer sequence.
