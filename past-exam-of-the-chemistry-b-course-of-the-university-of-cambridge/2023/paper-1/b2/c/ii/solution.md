**G is the diaryl [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) $\mathrm{(2{-}NO_2C_6H_4){-}O{-}(2{-}CH_2C(=O)N_{morph}C_6H_4)}$.** Here $N_{morph}$ denotes the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) of the intact morpholine ring; the phenoxy [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and phenylacetamide chain are ortho on their original ring. [potassium](../../../../../../potassium.md) [carbonate](../../../../../../carbonate.md) first generates [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md) from F. Its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks the [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md)-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of 1-fluoro-2-[nitrobenzene](../../../../../../nitrobenzene.md), forming a [Meisenheimer complex](../../../../../../meisenheimer-complex.md). Delocalization of its negative charge into the ortho [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md) stabilizes this addition intermediate. Fluoride elimination restores [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md) and completes [nucleophilic aromatic substitution](../../../../../../nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md).

This first step forms an O–aryl bond, rather than immediately arylating the less acidic alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The neutral [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md) has formula $\mathrm{C_{18}H_{18}N_2O_5}$, obtained from the two [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md)s by net loss of HF. The connectivity is shown beside B in the independently drawn diagram above. The [amide](../../../../../../amide.md)-[substrate](../../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md) O-to-C migration strategy has direct primary precedent.[2014, pp. 1925–1934, amide substrates and five-membered rearrangement pathways](../../../../../../developing-the-scope-of-o%E2%86%92c-aryl-migrations-exploring-amide-substrates-as-potential-precursors-for-asymmetric-reactions-by-dana-ameen-and-timothy-j-snape.md)
