<h1 id="b2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first [reagent](../../../../../reagent.md) supplies a vinyl [nucleophile](../../../../../nucleophile.md), $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH{-}}$, rather than an [allyl group](../../../../../allyl-group.md). **Two successive [conjugate additions](../../../../../conjugate-addition.md) install vinyl at C2 and a 2-formylethyl chain at C1.** Number the original nitro-bearing [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C1. Vinylmagnesium [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md) attacks the beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C2 of the [nitroalkene](../../../../../nitroalkene.md), shifting the original C1=C2 [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) into a [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) [nitronate](../../../../../nitronate.md). The new C2–vinyl bond remains intact during the second stage; its terminal double bond is not the [electrophilic](../../../../../electrophilicity.md) site in that stage.

The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) end of the [nitronate](../../../../../nitronate.md) at C1 then attacks the terminal beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) of [acrolein](../../../../../acrolein.md). Move the [acrolein](../../../../../acrolein.md) C=C [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) towards its [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and the C=O [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) onto [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). This forms the C1–CH2CH2CHO connectivity through an [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md); [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) by [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) and [tautomerization](../../../../../tautomerization.md) restore the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md). The [nitronate](../../../../../nitronate.md) returns to a neutral [nitro group](../../../../../nitro-group.md) as it makes the second C–C bond. Thus the chain is attached through the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) formerly beta to the [acrolein](../../../../../acrolein.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), rather than by a direct addition to its [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md).

After the first addition, C2 bears the [vinyl group](../../../../../vinyl-group.md) while the [nitronate](../../../../../nitronate.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C1 remains approximately planar. Approach of the second [electrophile](../../../../../electrophile.md) to the less crowded face, opposite the adjacent vinyl [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md), gives the new formylethyl chain trans to vinyl. Consequently **nitro and vinyl are cis**, as depicted: at C1 the chain and [nitro group](../../../../../nitro-group.md) occupy opposite faces. This is a [substrate](../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md)-controlled [diastereoselective](../../../../../diastereoselectivity.md) [tandem Michael addition](../../../../../tandem-michael-addition.md). The [reagents](../../../../../reagent.md) and starting material contain no [chiral](../../../../../chirality-chemistry.md) source, so the depicted [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) represents a racemic pair rather than an enantioselective synthesis. Steric facial selection explains the relative configuration without requiring a uniquely established [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) aggregate or transition-state [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md).
