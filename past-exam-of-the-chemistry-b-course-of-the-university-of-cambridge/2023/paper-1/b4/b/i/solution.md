↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [lactol](../../../../../../lactol.md) is a cyclic [hemiacetal](../../../../../../hemiacetal.md) and can open to **$\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2{-}CH(OBn){-}CHO}$**, retaining its pre-existing alkoxy-bearing stereocentre. Its two [anomers](../../../../../../anomer.md) lose their anomeric configuration on opening to the planar [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md), so both can feed the same addition pathway. A first equivalent of decylmagnesium [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) can be consumed by the free OH, producing decane and a [magnesium](../../../../../../magnesium.md) alkoxide; the excess [reagent](../../../../../../reagent.md) supplies the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md) for addition.

[magnesium](../../../../../../magnesium.md) can coordinate both the [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and the adjacent benzyloxy [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). This produces a five-membered chelate, with ring sequence Mg–O([carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md))–C([aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md))–C(alpha)–O(benzyloxy), that restricts rotation about the [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md)–alpha-[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond. Decyl attack on the less crowded face, away from the alpha-[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)'s hydroxyethyl chain in the chelated arrangement, forms the new C–C bond and a [magnesium](../../../../../../magnesium.md) alkoxide at the former [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Acidic workup protonates both alkoxides to give G. The OBn bond is retained; it is not displaced by the [Grignard reagent](../../../../../../grignard-reagent.md).

**The [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md) configuration is the one with OBn and the newly formed OH both projecting forward in the specified zigzag chain**, while the opposite configuration at the new [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is disfavoured. This is the [Cram chelation model](../../../../../../cram-chelation-model.md) for an alpha-alkoxy [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md), rather than [stereochemical](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) memory of the starting [lactol](../../../../../../lactol.md) OH. Coordination is a mechanistic explanation consistent with MgBr2 and the [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md); the scheme does not quantify individual organomagnesium aggregates.[p. 3, section 2, Chelation and Addition–Cram Chelate Rule](../../../../../../felkin-anh-and-cram-chelate-by-university-of-windsor.md) The relevant primary synthetic sequence is documented independently.[2022, synthetic sequence leading through the protected oxygen heterocycle](../../../../../../highly-stereoselective-asymmetric-total-synthesis-of-minus-jimenezin-via-sequential-intramolecular-amide-enolate-alkylation-by-thien-nhan-lu-and-others.md)
