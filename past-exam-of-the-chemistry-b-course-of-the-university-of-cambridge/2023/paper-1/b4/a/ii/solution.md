<h1 id="b4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Both permitted alternatives follow the same condensation–activation–migration sequence. [sodium](../../../../../../sodium.md) [acetate](../../../../../../acetate.md) liberates a useful concentration of [hydroxylamine](../../../../../../hydroxylamine.md) from its hydrochloride while providing a buffered medium. [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) of NH2OH attacks the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md); [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) gives a [carbinolamine](../../../../../../carbinolamine.md). [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of its [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)-derived OH and loss of [water](../../../../../../water.md), with [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) lone-pair donation, then gives the [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) after [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md). The two C=N geometries can be separated.

For the **B-to-D route**, [oxime O-tosylation](../../../../../../oxime-o-tosylation.md) begins when [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) of [tosyl chloride](../../../../../../tosyl-chloride.md) and [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) leaves; [triethylamine](../../../../../../triethylamine.md) accepts the released [proton](../../../../../../proton.md). The resulting O-tosyl [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) has an excellent leaving group. The $K{-}R_a$ bond, anti to N–OTs, migrates to [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) while N–O cleaves, giving a ring-expanded [nitrilium ion](../../../../../../nitrilium-ion.md). Its local connectivity is $R_s{-}K\equiv N^+{-}R_a$, with the existing scaffold still joining the two paths. [water](../../../../../../water.md) attacks the nitrilium [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) $K$. [proton transfers](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) and imidic-[acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)/[amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [tautomerization](../../../../../../tautomerization.md) produce D, with [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) inserted on the aromatic side of the old [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).

For the **C-to-E route**, the same O-tosylation activates the other [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) geometry. Now the anti bond is $K{-}R_s$; its migration gives $R_a{-}K\equiv N^+{-}R_s$, and aqueous trapping produces E, with [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) inserted on the spiro-junction side. **Each [Beckmann rearrangement](../../../../../../beckmann-rearrangement.md) expands the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md)'s six-membered ring to a seven-membered [lactam](../../../../../../lactam.md).** The original [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) remains the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is the inserted atom. In this mechanism the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is lost during [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) condensation, the [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) leaves with the [sulfonate](../../../../../../sulfonate.md) group, and [water](../../../../../../water.md) supplies the new [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md).
