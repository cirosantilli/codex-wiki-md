<h1 id="b4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The C–C bond anti to the activated N–O bond migrates.** This is [antiperiplanar migration in the Beckmann rearrangement](../../../../../../antiperiplanar-migration-in-the-beckmann-rearrangement.md). Donation from that [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) into the developing [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) deficiency at [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) requires the appropriate [antiperiplanar geometry](../../../../../../antiperiplanar-geometry.md). Migration and leaving-group departure are coupled, giving a [nitrilium ion](../../../../../../nitrilium-ion.md) rather than an equilibrating free [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md). B places the aromatic-side bond in the migrating orientation and therefore gives D; C places the spiro-side bond in that orientation and gives E. The two [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md)s consequently record the [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) geometries. A rule based only on which group seems intrinsically more [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) rich would miss this geometric constraint; retention of configuration in a migrating group is also consistent with concerted bond migration.
