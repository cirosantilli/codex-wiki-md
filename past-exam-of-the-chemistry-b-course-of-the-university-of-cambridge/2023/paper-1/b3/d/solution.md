↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With [triethylamine](../../../../../triethylamine.md) present, [proton transfer](../../../../../proton-transfer.md) from [thiophenol](../../../../../thiophenol.md) produces a more nucleophilic [thiolate](../../../../../thiolate.md) population. [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) attacks the anomeric [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C1 from the face opposite its [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) C–O bond. Displacement by [backside attack](../../../../../backside-attack.md) opens the [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md), breaks C1–O, and leaves the former [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) attached to C2. [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of that alkoxide by [thiophenol](../../../../../thiophenol.md) or triethylammonium gives **G: SPh at C1 on the front face and OH at C2 on the back face**, a trans relationship. This is inversion at C1 with retention of the C2 C–O bond; there is no need to invert every stereocentre. Rapid [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) also suppresses competing silyl transfer by the alkoxide.

Without added [base](../../../../../base-chemistry.md), neutral [thiophenol](../../../../../thiophenol.md) is less nucleophilic. [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)-assisted cleavage at the anomeric [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) bond can instead be accompanied by donation from the ring [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), giving an [oxocarbenium ion](../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md) with charge shared with the ring oxygen. [thiophenol](../../../../../thiophenol.md) capture from the face that puts SPh back on the original [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) face, followed by [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md), gives **H with SPh and C2–OH both on the back face**, the net-retention pathway. An ion-pair or highly asynchronous opening can provide this outcome; the drawing does not establish a long-lived free [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md).

The distinction is configuration, not a mandatory fixed chair. For the closely matching trisilylated [substrate](../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md), the primary study obtained both [anomers](../../../../../anomer.md) with [thiophenol](../../../../../thiophenol.md) alone, and established an unusual inverted chair for the alpha [thioglycoside](../../../../../thioglycoside.md) by [NMR](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) and [crystallography](../../../../../crystallography.md). Thus H should be explained as an accessible [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md), not asserted to be the sole [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) or necessarily to have axial SPh in its preferred [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md).[1997, Schemes 1–2 and Figure 1; neutral reaction mixture and configuration/conformation assignment](../../../../../reaction-of-thiophenol-with-glucal-epoxides-x-ray-structure-of-3-4-6-tri-o-tert-butyldimethylsilyl-1-s-phenyl-1-thio-alpha-d-glucopyranoside-by-caroline-walford-and-others.md)
