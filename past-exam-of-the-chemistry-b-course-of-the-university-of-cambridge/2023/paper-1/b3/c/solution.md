<h1 id="b3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**E, with the axial anomeric [acetate](../../../../../acetate.md), hydrolyses faster.** The tert-butyl [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md) strongly favours the chair in which it is equatorial, so the two [substrate](../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md)s cannot freely interchange axial and equatorial [acetate](../../../../../acetate.md) simply by an inexpensive [ring inversion](../../../../../ring-inversion.md). [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) activates the anomeric acetoxy leaving group. As its C–O bond cleaves to release [acetic acid](../../../../../acetic-acid.md), a [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) on the endocyclic [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) donates into the developing anomeric orbital, producing an [oxocarbenium ion](../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md). [water](../../../../../water.md) attacks that [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), and [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives the cyclic [hemiacetal](../../../../../hemiacetal.md). Attack from either face accounts for the unspecified anomeric configuration of OH in the [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md).

The axial C–OAc bond in E can adopt [antiperiplanar geometry](../../../../../antiperiplanar-geometry.md) relative to an endocyclic [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md). This favourable $n_O\rightarrow\sigma^*_{C-O}$ interaction assists departure in the ionization [transition state](../../../../../transition-state.md). The corresponding bond in F is poorly aligned in its preferred chair; achieving comparable overlap requires an unfavourable chair change, placing tert-butyl axial, or substantial ring distortion. The distinction is [stereoelectronic control of acetal hydrolysis](../../../../../stereoelectronic-control-of-acetal-hydrolysis.md), rather than merely counting steric crowding in the [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md)s.

An axial [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) can itself receive ground-state stabilization from the anomeric effect, so “axial means unstable” is not an adequate rate argument. What matters is the relative [Gibbs energy of activation](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-activation.md) for the departing bond and the geometry of lone-pair assistance. Conformationally locked model [acetals](../../../../../acetal.md) experimentally demonstrate faster [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)-catalysed cleavage of the axially oriented [isomer](../../../../../isomer.md), although their numerical rate ratios should not be transferred to these different [substrate](../../../../../substrate-chemistry.md)s.[1983, abstract and pp. 1627–1632, acid-catalysed axial/equatorial comparison](../../../../../stereoelectronic-control-of-the-hydrolysis-of-a-conformationally-locked-acetal-by-anthony-j-kirby-and-robert-j-martin.md)
