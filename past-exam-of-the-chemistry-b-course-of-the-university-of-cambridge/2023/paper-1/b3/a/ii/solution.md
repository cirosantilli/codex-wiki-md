<h1 id="b3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The bridged, sterically hindered [biphenyl](../../../../../../biphenyl.md) framework is twisted and does not rapidly interconvert the two faces of each [methylene group](../../../../../../methylene-group.md) on the [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) timescale. Label one [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) on each CH2 $H_A$ and the other $H_B$. Replacing $H_A$ versus $H_B$ by a test [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md) gives diastereomeric environments, so these [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) are [diastereotopic](../../../../../../diastereotopic.md) and need not have equal [chemical shifts](../../../../../../chemical-shift.md) even in an [achiral](../../../../../../achirality.md) solvent. Molecular symmetry relates the two $H_A$ sites to each other and the two $H_B$ sites to each other.

Each methylene [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) undergoes [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) to its partner, giving the two doublets, each accounting for two [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) overall. Their separation is $0.21$ ppm; at operating frequency $\nu_0$ in MHz it corresponds to $0.21\nu_0$ Hz. This separation is not the [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) constant, which must be measured from the line spacing within a doublet. If the shift separation is not large compared with that coupling, an AB pattern with second-order distortion is expected rather than ideal first-order doublets. A racemic pair of twisted [conformations](../../../../../../conformation.md) still has these signals: [enantiomers](../../../../../../enantiomer.md) have the same spectrum in an [achiral](../../../../../../achirality.md) medium, while the two [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) environments within each [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) remain different.
