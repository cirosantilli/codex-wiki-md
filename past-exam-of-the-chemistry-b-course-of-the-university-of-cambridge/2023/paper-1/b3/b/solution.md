<h1 id="b3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In both cases the [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) undergoes concerted [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) transfer from [metachloroperbenzoic acid](../../../../../metachloroperbenzoic-acid.md). The [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) forms one C–O bond to the terminal [peroxide](../../../../../peroxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md); [electron](../../../../../electron.md) redistribution forms the second C–O bond, breaks O–O, and transfers the peracid [proton](../../../../../proton.md) to its [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) as the [carboxylic acid](../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md) [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) forms. This cyclic [epoxidation](../../../../../epoxidation.md) pathway delivers both C–O bonds from one face, without a freely rotating [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) intermediate.

For 1-methylcyclohexene, the two [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) faces are [enantiotopic](../../../../../enantiotopicity.md). Attack from opposite faces gives the two mirror-related [epoxides](../../../../../epoxide.md), and an [achiral](../../../../../achirality.md) peracid in an [achiral](../../../../../achirality.md) environment cannot distinguish their [Gibbs energies of activation](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-activation.md). **The [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) is racemic, 1:1.**

For the cis-fused bicyclic [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md), the junction configuration and its puckered ring [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md) make approach from the two faces sterically different. The folded saturated ring shields the concave face; [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) transfer preferentially approaches the more exposed convex face, directed away from that ring. The alternative attack places the incoming peracid closer to the folded skeleton. **The 95:5 ratio represents facial [diastereoselectivity](../../../../../diastereoselectivity.md)**, while concerted syn [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) delivery is retained for either [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md). It should not be explained by free rotation or by one [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) subsequently changing configuration. For scale only, if this ratio were measured under irreversible kinetic conditions at 298 K, it would correspond to $RT\ln(95/5)\simeq7.30\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ difference between the competing [Gibbs energies of activation](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-activation.md); the exam does not specify that [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
