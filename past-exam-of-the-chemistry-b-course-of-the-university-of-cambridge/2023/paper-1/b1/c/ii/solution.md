<h1 id="b1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The [bromines](../../../../../../bromine.md) ortho and para to OH are removed, leaving the meta [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) at C3.** A suitable polar mechanism begins with [electrophilic](../../../../../../electrophilicity.md) ipso [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of a brominated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) at C4 or C6 gives a [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../../arenium-ion.md) stabilized by donation of the phenolic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md); an important contributor has an O-protonated cyclohexadienone-like framework. Corresponding [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) at C3 lacks this direct stabilization by [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md) from OH.

[iodide](../../../../../../iodide.md) can capture/reduce the departing [electrophilic](../../../../../../electrophilicity.md) [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) as the C–Br bond [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) restore the aromatic pi system. Thus the incoming [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) remains on [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) while [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) is removed, unlike ordinary [electrophilic substitution](../../../../../../electrophilic-substitution.md) in which the incoming [electrophile](../../../../../../electrophile.md) remains and a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) leaves. [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) trapping can be represented through IBr formation followed by reaction with further [iodide](../../../../../../iodide.md) to give [iodine](../../../../../../iodine.md) and [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md). For each replacement the balanced net [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) is

$$
\mathrm{ArBr+2HI\longrightarrow ArH+HBr+I_2}.
$$

Applying it at C4 and C6 gives **3-bromo-2,5-dimethylphenol**, with formula $\mathrm{C_8H_9BrO}$. This explains why the position least activated by OH is the one at which [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) survives. It is a proposed [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)-assisted [protodebromination](../../../../../../protodebromination.md) mechanism consistent with the selectivity; the preparative sequence alone does not prove every [proton](../../../../../../proton.md)-transfer or [halogen](../../../../../../halogen.md)-transfer intermediate. It should not be drawn as [iodide](../../../../../../iodide.md) simply displacing [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) from an unactivated aryl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md).
