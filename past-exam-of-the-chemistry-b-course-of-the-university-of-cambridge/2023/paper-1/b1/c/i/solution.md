<h1 id="b1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Number the phenolic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C1, with methyl groups at C2 and C5. **All three remaining aromatic [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) are replaced: $H$ is 3,4,6-tribromo-2,5-dimethylphenol.** The starting [phenol](../../../../../../phenol.md) is $\mathrm{C_8H_{10}O}$; replacing three [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) gives $\mathrm{C_8H_7Br_3O}$, agreeing with the intermediate's composition. Bromination of methyl side chains would not follow the stated [Lewis acid](../../../../../../lewis-acid.md)-promoted aromatic pathway.

[aluminium tribromide](../../../../../../aluminium-tribromide.md) polarizes Br–Br. The [aromatic ring](../../../../../../aromatic-ring.md) attacks the [electrophilic](../../../../../../electrophilicity.md) [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) end, forming a [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../../arenium-ion.md); removal of the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) on the attacked [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) restores [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md). Repetition fills C4 and C6, the available para and ortho positions activated by OH, and C3, which is activated by the methyl [substituents](../../../../../../substituent.md) when the other positions are occupied. The formula fixes the extent of substitution without requiring an experimentally unreported order for the individual brominations. The preparative perbromination/selective-debromination sequence is documented in the primary synthetic work.[2019, Scheme 6, upper route from 2,5-xylenol](../../../../../../total-synthesis-of-nor-illudalane-sesquiterpenes-based-on-a-c-sp3-h-activation-strategy-by-romain-melot-and-others.md)
