<h1 id="b1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Number the ring with [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) at C1 and the methylsulfonyl group at C4. **The new [nitro group](../../../../../nitro-group.md) is at C2, equivalently C6 by the starting ring's symmetry:** the [product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) is 1-chloro-2-nitro-4-(methylsulfonyl)[benzene](../../../../../benzene.md). Both [substituents](../../../../../substituent.md) favour this site. Although [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) deactivates the ring inductively, its [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) donation stabilizes ortho/para [Wheland intermediates](../../../../../arenium-ion.md). The [sulfone](../../../../../sulfone.md) is strongly [electron](../../../../../electron.md) withdrawing and meta directing; attack ortho or para to it produces especially unfavourable cationic contributors to [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md). C2 is ortho to [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) and meta to [sulfone](../../../../../sulfone.md), whereas C3 has the opposite, less favourable relationship.

[sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) protonates [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md), followed by loss of [water](../../../../../water.md) to produce the [nitronium ion](../../../../../nitronium-ion.md). A ring [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) attacks [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), giving the [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../arenium-ion.md) with the new C–N bond. [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) at the attacked [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) restores [aromaticity](../../../../../aromaticity.md). This is [electrophilic aromatic substitution](../../../../../electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.md): the aryl [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) and the [sulfone](../../../../../sulfone.md) both remain attached. The formula check is $\mathrm{C_7H_7ClO_2S+NO_2-H=C_7H_6ClNO_4S}$. It confirms replacement of one ring [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) rather than [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md), but directing effects, rather than the formula alone, establish the position.
