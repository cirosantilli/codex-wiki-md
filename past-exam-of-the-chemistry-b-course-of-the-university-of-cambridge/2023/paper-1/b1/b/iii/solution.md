<h1 id="b1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The first [base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) equivalent removes O–H; the second removes the alpha C–H.** The first equivalent of [lithium diisopropylamide](../../../../../../lithium-diisopropylamide.md) converts the [carboxylic acid](../../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md) to its [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) [carboxylate](../../../../../../carboxylate.md). A further equivalent removes the remaining [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) on the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) between the two [allyl groups](../../../../../../allyl-group.md), giving a [carboxylic acid dianion](../../../../../../carboxylic-acid-dianion.md):

$$
\mathrm{HO_2C{-}CH(L)_2\longrightarrow {}^-O_2C{-}CH(L)_2\longrightarrow {}^-O_2C{-}C^-(L)_2}.
$$

The second negative charge has [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md)-like delocalization into the carboxyl group; [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) coordination and aggregation are not represented by this simple charge-bookkeeping structure. The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md) attacks [methyl iodide](../../../../../../methyl-iodide.md) with C–I bond cleavage, forming the new C–Me bond. [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) workup reprotonates the [carboxylate](../../../../../../carboxylate.md), giving **$F=\mathrm{HO_2C{-}C(Me)(L)_2}$**. The two equivalents are therefore chemically necessary for the stated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [alkylation](../../../../../../alkylation.md) rather than simply an arbitrary excess. A single equivalent is consumed predominantly by the much more acidic carboxylic [proton](../../../../../../proton.md). The [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md) has formula $\mathrm{C_9H_{14}O_2}$ and still has two identical [allyl groups](../../../../../../allyl-group.md), so this methylation does not create a [stereocenter](../../../../../../stereocenter.md).
