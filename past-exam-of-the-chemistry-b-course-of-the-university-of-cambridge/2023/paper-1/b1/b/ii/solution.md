<h1 id="b1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) attacks an [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) to form a [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md). Collapse expels ethoxide, and [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)–[base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) transfer gives the [carboxylate](../../../../../../carboxylate.md) and [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md). Repeating this [nucleophilic acyl substitution](../../../../../../nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md) at the other [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) produces the [malonate](../../../../../../malonate.md) dianion; [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) workup gives $\mathrm{HO_2C{-}C(L)_2{-}CO_2H}$, with $L=\mathrm{CH_2CH{=}CH_2}$ as defined above.

On heating, one [carboxylic acid](../../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md) group is lost through a six-membered cyclic [proton](../../../../../../proton.md)-transfer/bond-cleavage arrangement. A [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) of the retained group accepts the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the departing carboxyl OH as its C–C bond breaks and [carbon dioxide](../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) forms; [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) redistribution gives the [enol](../../../../../../enol.md)-like form of the retained [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md). [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md)/[tautomerization](../../../../../../tautomerization.md) then produces

$$
\boxed{E=\mathrm{HO_2C{-}CH(L)_2}}.
$$

The net [decarboxylation](../../../../../../decarboxylation.md) is $\mathrm{C_9H_{12}O_4\to C_8H_{12}O_2+CO_2}$. The central [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) gains a [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), both allyl [substituents](../../../../../../substituent.md) survive, and only one carboxyl group is removed. It would be incorrect to draw [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) directly giving the final [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) without accounting for this [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) loss. The favourable cyclic pathway is characteristic of [malonic acid decarboxylation](../../../../../../malonic-acid-decarboxylation.md).
