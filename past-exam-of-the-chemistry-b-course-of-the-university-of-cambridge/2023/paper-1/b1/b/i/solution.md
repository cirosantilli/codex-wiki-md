<h1 id="b1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The [malonate](../../../../../../malonate.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) receives two [allyl groups](../../../../../../allyl-group.md).** With $L=\mathrm{CH_2CH{=}CH_2}$, attached through its first [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), the [product](../../../../../../product-chemistry.md) is

$$
\boxed{D=\mathrm{EtO_2C{-}C(L)_2{-}CO_2Et}}.
$$

[sodium hydride](../../../../../../sodium-hydride.md) removes a methylene [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from [diethyl malonate](../../../../../../diethyl-malonate.md); [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) formation accompanies production of an [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) stabilized by [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md). Its [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the bromomethylene [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of [allyl bromide](../../../../../../allyl-bromide.md) in an [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md), expelling [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md). The monoalkylated [malonate](../../../../../../malonate.md) still has one [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) between the [carbonyls](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). Excess [base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) removes that [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), and a second equivalent of [allyl bromide](../../../../../../allyl-bromide.md) produces a second [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond. The final [malonate](../../../../../../malonate.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) has no remaining [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and cannot undergo a third analogous [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) there.

The two [alkenes](../../../../../../alkene.md) are preserved because the substitution occurs at the primary [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md). Counting the introduced groups gives **$\mathrm{C_{13}H_{20}O_4}$** for the diallylated diethyl [ester](../../../../../../ester.md). Its two identical [allyl groups](../../../../../../allyl-group.md) also mean that the central [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is not a [stereocenter](../../../../../../stereocenter.md). This stage is the [alkylation](../../../../../../alkylation.md) portion of the [malonic ester synthesis](../../../../../../malonic-ester-synthesis.md).
