<h1 id="b5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Transfer is favourable when new [water](../../../../../../water.md)–solute interactions compensate the cavity and reorganization costs of accommodating the solute.** The [Gibbs energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) also includes the loss of solute–[alkane](../../../../../../alkane.md) interactions; it is not simply a count of [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md). [isobutane](../../../../../../isobutane.md) cannot compensate those costs with strong directional interactions, explaining its positive [transfer Gibbs energy](../../../../../../transfer-gibbs-energy.md). [acetone](../../../../../../acetone.md) and both [amines](../../../../../../amine.md) can accept [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) from [water](../../../../../../water.md) and have favourable electrostatic [solvation](../../../../../../solvation.md), making their transfer moderately favourable. [isopropylamine](../../../../../../isopropylamine.md) can additionally donate to [water](../../../../../../water.md), consistent with a somewhat greater preference for [water](../../../../../../water.md) than [trimethylamine](../../../../../../trimethylamine.md).

[acetamide](../../../../../../acetamide.md) combines a strong [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) acceptor with N–H donors and has the most favourable hydration in the group, accounting for its much more negative [transfer Gibbs energy](../../../../../../transfer-gibbs-energy.md). Its pure solid lattice is not an extra sublimation penalty to insert here: the stated process starts from a dilute [alkane](../../../../../../alkane.md) solution and ends in dilute [water](../../../../../../water.md), rather than dissolving the pure crystal. The nonmonotonic relationship between [hydrogen-bond acceptor parameter](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond-acceptor-parameter.md) and [transfer Gibbs energy](../../../../../../transfer-gibbs-energy.md) is expected because donor strength, nonpolar surface, [solvent cavity formation](../../../../../../solvent-cavity-formation.md), and all other [solvation](../../../../../../solvation.md) contributions also enter.

For matched [standard states](../../../../../../standard-state.md), $\Delta G^\circ_{\mathrm{tr}}=-RT\ln K_{\mathrm{tr}}$, with $K_{\mathrm{tr}}$ the [water](../../../../../../water.md)-to-[alkane](../../../../../../alkane.md) activity ratio. As an illustrative 298 K calculation, the [hydrocarbon](../../../../../../hydrocarbon.md) value implies $K_{\mathrm{tr}}\simeq7.0\times10^{-4}$, whereas the [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) value implies approximately $3.6\times10^4$. These are standard-state partition preferences, not absolute aqueous solubilities. [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) could introduce an additional pH-dependent contribution to an experimentally measured [distribution coefficient](../../../../../../distribution-coefficient.md), but no such speciation information is supplied and it is not needed for the molecular comparison.
