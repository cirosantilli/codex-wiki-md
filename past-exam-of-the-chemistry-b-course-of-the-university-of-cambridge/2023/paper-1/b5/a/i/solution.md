↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The acceptor-strength trend is **[amide](../../../../../../amide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) strongest, [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) weaker, [hydrocarbon](../../../../../../hydrocarbon.md) essentially nonaccepting**. [trimethylamine](../../../../../../trimethylamine.md) and [isopropylamine](../../../../../../isopropylamine.md) each provide a relatively available [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md); adding N–H bonds changes donor ability much more conspicuously than it changes the acceptor ranking in this comparison. Alkyl substitution and [solvation](../../../../../../solvation.md) modify the precise values, so the matching [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) parameters should be read as the stated empirical strengths rather than a universal equality of all [amines](../../../../../../amine.md).

In [acetamide](../../../../../../acetamide.md), [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) lone-pair donation contributes an $\mathrm{O^-{-}C{=}N^+}$ form contributing to [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md), increasing [electron density](../../../../../../electron-density.md) at [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and making it a strong [hydrogen bond acceptor](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond-acceptor.md). **The [amide](../../../../../../amide.md) acceptor is [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), not [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md)**: the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is delocalized into the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). [acetone](../../../../../../acetone.md) also accepts at [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), but lacks this [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md)-to-[carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) donation and has the smaller [hydrogen-bond acceptor parameter](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond-acceptor-parameter.md). [isobutane](../../../../../../isobutane.md) has neither a heteroatom [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) nor a comparable acceptor site; its zero value is consistent with the absence of an ordinary directional [hydrogen bond acceptor](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond-acceptor.md).
