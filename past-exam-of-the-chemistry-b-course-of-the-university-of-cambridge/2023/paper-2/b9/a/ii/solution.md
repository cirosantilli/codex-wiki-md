<h1 id="b9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Successive Si–O links have the same local bonding role**, unlike the unequal links selected by an alternating [phosphazene](../../../../../../phosphazene.md) [conformation](../../../../../../conformation.md). The [polysiloxane](../../../../../../polysiloxane.md) backbone is described primarily by polar Si–O [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md), with local [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) lone-pair interactions rather than a localized single/double-bond sequence. There is therefore no alternating Si–O bond-order pattern that requires two characteristic distances. Equal chemical environments give equal bond lengths in the ideal repeating structure; local strain or packing can perturb individual distances.
