[polysulfur](../../../../../../polysulfur.md) has a chain of S–S [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) with filled lone-pair orbitals. Its occupied states do not supply a partly filled delocalized band, so the chain is an [electrical insulator](../../../../../../electrical-insulator.md).

In [polythiazyl](../../../../../../polythiazyl.md), an approximately planar S–N chain permits overlap of orbitals perpendicular to the backbone. In an elementary [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) picture, [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) supplies one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) two to this manifold: **three pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) per SN unit**, rather than a completely filled, isolated two-centre bonding pair. The extended states are therefore not all fully occupied. More realistic descriptions include the crystallographic repeat, interchain interactions and overlapping bands crossing the [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md); the odd [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count of a chosen chemical repeat is suggestive, not by itself a proof of metallicity.

The essential distinction is **occupied, localized bonding/lone-pair states in [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) chains versus available extended states at the [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md) in crystalline [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) nitride**. The measured high conductivity along the [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) fibres and its metallic temperature dependence provide the experimental basis, alongside the structural evidence for the chain. Conductivity depends on order and defects as well as chemical composition.[J. Chem. Soc., Chem. Commun., pp. 476–477; X-ray structure and chain-direction metallic conductivity](../../../../../../synthesis-and-structure-of-the-polymeric-metal-sn-x-and-its-precursor-s2n2-by-alan-g-macdiarmid-and-others.md)
