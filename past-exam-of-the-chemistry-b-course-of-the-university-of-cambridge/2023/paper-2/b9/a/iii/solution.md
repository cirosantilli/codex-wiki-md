↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Neither backbone has a partially filled, extended electronic band available for easy charge transport.** In [polysiloxanes](../../../../../../polysiloxane.md) the bonding and nonbonding states are filled and separated from empty states by a large [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md). [phosphazene](../../../../../../phosphazene.md) local lone-pair interactions do not create the continuously delocalized, partially occupied pi band suggested by a naive P=N drawing. Their flexibility or formal multiple-bond notation does not imply electronic conductivity. This comparison concerns the undoped materials, not conductivity supplied by added mobile ions.
