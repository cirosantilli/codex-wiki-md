<h1 id="b9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**The chemical requirement for permanence is retention of the backbone and network in a wet environment.** A suitably prepared [polysiloxane](../../../../../polysiloxane.md) has strong Si–O bonding, relatively slow backbone cleavage under mild aqueous conditions, and flexible chains that can be retained in a cross-linked elastic network. In the comparison intended here, [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) of susceptible [phosphazene](../../../../../phosphazene.md) substituents and P–N linkages leads to [chain scission](../../../../../chain-scission.md), reduced [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) and loss of mechanical integrity, which conflicts with permanence.

This is a comparison of chemical durability, not a universal statement that every [polyphosphazene](../../../../../polyphosphazene.md) degrades rapidly or that every [polysiloxane](../../../../../polysiloxane.md) is indefinitely inert. [phosphazene](../../../../../phosphazene.md) side groups can strongly change access of [water](../../../../../water.md) and [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) rates; siloxanes can also be cleaved under sufficiently acidic or basic conditions. Those structural and kinetic qualifications matter even before any biological suitability is considered.
