<h1 id="b9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

First hydrolyse both Si–Cl groups to give a [silanol](../../../../../../silanol.md) of [monomer functionality](../../../../../../monomer-functionality.md) two, then condense Si–OH groups to form the Si–O–Si backbone:

$$
\mathrm{R_2SiCl_2+2H_2O\longrightarrow R_2Si(OH)_2+2HCl},
$$



$$
\mathrm{{\equiv}Si{-}OH+HO{-}Si{\equiv}\longrightarrow{\equiv}Si{-}O{-}Si{\equiv}+H_2O}.
$$

**[monomer functionality](../../../../../../monomer-functionality.md) two favours chains, with cyclic oligomers also possible.** Removal of condensation products or equilibration under appropriate conditions favours high [molar mass](../../../../../../molar-mass.md). The [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) and condensation can overlap in a one-pot process; the equations distinguish their chemical functions rather than requiring isolation of every silanediol.
