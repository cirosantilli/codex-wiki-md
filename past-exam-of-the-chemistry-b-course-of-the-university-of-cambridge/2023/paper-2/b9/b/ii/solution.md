↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**R$_3$SiCl acts as a monofunctional [chain termination](../../../../../../chain-termination.md) reagent.** [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) gives R$_3$SiOH, which forms a terminal R$_3$Si–O unit but cannot continue growth through a second [silanol](../../../../../../silanol.md) group. Increasing its proportion therefore increases the number of capped ends and lowers the attainable average chain length. The resulting material generally has lower viscosity and less chain entanglement, rather than acquiring a cross-linked network merely from end-capping.
