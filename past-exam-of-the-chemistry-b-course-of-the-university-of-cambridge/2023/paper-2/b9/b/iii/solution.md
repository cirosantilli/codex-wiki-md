**RSiCl$_3$ introduces branching and, at sufficient conversion and loading, [cross-links](../../../../../../cross-link.md).** [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) gives RSi(OH)$_3$, with three possible siloxane connections per [silicon](../../../../../../silicon.md). Incorporation links chains into a network, suppressing flow and usually increasing stiffness and dimensional retention. A sufficiently connected permanent network behaves as a [thermoset](../../../../../../thermoset.md) or cross-linked [elastomer](../../../../../../elastomer.md) rather than an ordinary flowing linear [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md). A small concentration need not immediately produce a macroscopic gel; conversion and network connectivity matter.
