↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[platinum](../../../../../../platinum.md) is in group 10, so subtracting its [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) gives **$d^8$ for Pt(II) and $d^6$ for Pt(IV)**. Adding two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) from each coordinated donor gives the local valence [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md) results

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{cisplatin}:&\quad8+2(2)_{\rm NH_3}+2(2)_{\rm Cl}=16,\\
\mathrm{satraplatin}:&\quad6+2(2)_{\rm amine}+2(2)_{\rm Cl}+2(2)_{\rm OAc}=18.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Each [acetate](../../../../../../acetate.md) in the depicted structure is monodentate, binding through one [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The 16-electron count of the square-planar complex is normal for a low-spin late-metal $d^8$ centre; it is not a missing ligand inferred from the [18-electron rule](../../../../../../18-electron-rule.md).
