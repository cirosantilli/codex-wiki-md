<h1 id="b6/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Both complexes have all their $d$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) paired, so $S=0$. Their [spin-only magnetic moment](../../../../../../spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm eff}=2\sqrt{S(S+1)}\,\mu_{\rm B}=0\ \mathrm{B.M.}\quad\text{for both complexes}.}
$$

They exhibit [diamagnetism](../../../../../../diamagnetism.md). The formula describes the spin contribution; it does not say that a closed-shell sample has no diamagnetic response to an applied field.
