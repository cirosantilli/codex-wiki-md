↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**[ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) and [cyclohexylamine](../../../../../../cyclohexylamine.md) are [sigma-donor ligands](../../../../../../sigma-donor-ligand.md); [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) and [acetate](../../../../../../acetate.md) are sigma- and [pi-donor ligands](../../../../../../pi-donor-ligand.md).** The [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) of each [amine](../../../../../../amine.md) points toward the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) and forms the donor interaction. These saturated [amines](../../../../../../amine.md) have no low-lying conjugated acceptor system that would make them conventional [pi-acceptor ligands](../../../../../../pi-acceptor-ligand.md).

[chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) uses a [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) for [sigma donation](../../../../../../sigma-donation.md) and has additional occupied $p$ orbitals perpendicular to the bond that can donate with pi symmetry. An oxygen-bound [acetate](../../../../../../acetate.md) similarly has occupied oxygen-centred orbitals available for sigma and [pi donation](../../../../../../pi-donation.md), although its carboxylate [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md) changes their energies and distribution. Neither should be classified as a pi acceptor merely because its bonding can be drawn with multiple bonds or [resonance in chemistry](../../../../../../resonance-in-chemistry.md) contributors. **None of these four is a standard sigma-donor/[pi-acceptor ligand](../../../../../../pi-acceptor-ligand.md).**
