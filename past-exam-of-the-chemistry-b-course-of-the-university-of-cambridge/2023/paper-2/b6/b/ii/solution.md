**[cyclohexylamine](../../../../../../cyclohexylamine.md) is expected highest in the [spectrochemical series](../../../../../../spectrochemical-series.md) for comparable [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) environments.** Its alkyl substituent increases the electron-donating ability of [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) relative to [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md), strengthening the sigma interaction and raising the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–ligand sigma-antibonding levels. The anionic pi donors tend to raise occupied [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) $d_\pi$ levels and give smaller splittings. Steric effects and the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) can modify the exact ordering, so the comparison is a [ligand field](../../../../../../ligand-field.md) trend rather than a universal basicity-to-splitting identity.
