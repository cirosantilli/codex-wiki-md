↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The favoured insertion leaves [nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md) attached to the methyl-substituted [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); the aryl group bonds to the SiMe$_3$-substituted [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md).** This is the connectivity leading to the illustrated ring-closed product: the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) carrying methyl subsequently attacks the ester carbonyl.

The bulky trimethylsilyl substituent is kept away from the crowded phosphine-ligated [nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md) centre in the insertion arrangement. The alternative orientation would place Ni next to the more demanding SiMe$_3$ end. [regioselectivity](../../../../../../regioselectivity.md) can also reflect electronic polarization of a silyl-substituted [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) and stabilization of the resulting alkenyl complex. The ortho ester helps capture the insertion product by [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) coordination and subsequent ring closure. **Steric discrimination at insertion, reinforced by the accessible intramolecular cyclization, accounts for the observed direction**; the product drawing alone is not a quantitative separation of steric and electronic contributions.
