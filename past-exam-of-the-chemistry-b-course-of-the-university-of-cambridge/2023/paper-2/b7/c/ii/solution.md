<h1 id="b7/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**B is [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md); D is [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) insertion into Ni–aryl followed by carbonyl-oxygen coordination; E is carbonyl insertion into Ni–alkenyl.** The distinction is visible from the bonds made and from consistent [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md).

For B the depicted Ni(0) starting complex has two phosphine and two MeCN donors, giving $10+4+4=18$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Addition across the aryl C–Br bond produces two X-type ligands and Ni(II). The drawn product has lost the two [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) donors, giving $8+4+2+2=16$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Intrinsic [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) to the unsolvated 14-electron $\mathrm{Ni^0(dppe)}$ fragment raises its count to 16; the overall drawn $18\to16$ change also includes loss of four solvent-donor [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). **A falling displayed count does not turn this [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) into a [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md).**

Before D, the aryl–[alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) intermediate is Ni(I), $d^9$, with $9+4_{\rm P,P}+2_{\rm aryl}+2_{\rm alkyne}=17$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Syn migration couples aryl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) to one [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and leaves a Ni–alkenyl bond. Insertion alone removes the separate two-electron [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) donor and would give a 15-electron alkenyl fragment. The ester carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) then binds, as drawn, restoring the count to $9+4+2_{\rm alkenyl}+2_{\rm O}=17$. Ni remains $+1$.

In E the Ni-bound alkenyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the coordinated ester carbonyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), forming the new ring-closing C–C bond. The C=O pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) move to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and an Ni–O [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) bond replaces Ni–C. This is carbonyl [migratory insertion](../../../../../../migratory-insertion.md), equivalently intramolecular addition to a coordinated carbonyl. A neutral O donor plus an alkenyl X ligand become one [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) X ligand, so the drawn count falls **$17\to15$**, while Ni remains **$+1$**. A solvating molecule could fill the resulting vacancy, but it is not shown and should not be silently added to the stated count.
