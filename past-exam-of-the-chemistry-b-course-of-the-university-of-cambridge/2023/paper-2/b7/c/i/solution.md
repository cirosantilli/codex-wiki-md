↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**[zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) reduction is required in A, C and F.** Reading the drawn ligands with consistent ionic bookkeeping gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{step}&\text{nickel change}&\text{electrons accepted per Ni}\\\hline
\mathrm A&\mathrm{Ni^{II}\to Ni^0}&2\\
\mathrm C&\mathrm{Ni^{II}\to Ni^I}&1\\
\mathrm F&\mathrm{Ni^I\to Ni^0}&1
\end{array}}
$$

In A the two [chlorides](../../../../../../chloride.md) of the precursor are removed and neutral [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) donors occupy the reduced centre. In C the [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) is removed while the [aryl ligand](../../../../../../aryl-ligand.md) remains and the neutral [alkyne](../../../../../../alkyne.md) coordinates; the drawn neutral intermediate has Ni(I). In F an anionic alkoxide-derived product is released and the neutral solvated Ni(0) complex is regenerated. [zinc](../../../../../../zinc.md) supplies [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) by oxidation to Zn(II); halide and [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) ions can be taken up in zinc-containing products.

A is [precatalyst](../../../../../../precatalyst.md) activation. The two one-electron reductions C and F together require two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) per catalytic turnover, corresponding to one Zn atom if its oxidation ends at $+2$. The diagram does not establish whether each formal reduction is a single elementary electron-transfer event or a sequence of surface reactions and ligand transfers.
