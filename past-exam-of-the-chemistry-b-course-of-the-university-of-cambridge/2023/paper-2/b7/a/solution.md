<h1 id="b7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the usual **octahedral Ni(II) hydration-isomer series**, distinguish coordinated [water](../../../../../water.md) from [water](../../../../../water.md) of crystallization:

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
&[\mathrm{Ni(H_2O)_6}]\mathrm{Cl_2},\\
&[\mathrm{NiCl(H_2O)_5}]\mathrm{Cl}\cdot\mathrm{H_2O},\\
&\mathrm{cis}\! -[\mathrm{NiCl_2(H_2O)_4}]\cdot2\mathrm{H_2O},\\
&\mathrm{trans}\! -[\mathrm{NiCl_2(H_2O)_4}]\cdot2\mathrm{H_2O}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The first two [coordination spheres](../../../../../coordination-sphere.md) have no cis/trans choice because they contain zero or one [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md). The last hydration class has two [geometrical isomers](../../../../../geometrical-isomer.md): the [chlorides](../../../../../chloride.md) occupy adjacent or opposite octahedral positions. Thus there are **three hydration classes and four structures when cis/trans isomerism is included**. Every formula retains two [chlorides](../../../../../chloride.md) and six [waters](../../../../../water.md) overall; the [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) remains six. The schematic in B6(c) displays the distinct octahedral connectivities. This enumeration states the usual six-coordinate interpretation, rather than treating unspecified changes in [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) as further members of the same elementary series.
