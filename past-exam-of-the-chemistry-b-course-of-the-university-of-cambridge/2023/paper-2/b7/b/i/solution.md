<h1 id="b7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The neutral product is **cis-$[\mathrm{NiCl_2(dppe)}]$**, and the displaced material is **six [waters](../../../../../../water.md) per [nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md)**:

$$
\mathrm{NiCl_2\cdot6H_2O+dppe\longrightarrow[NiCl_2(dppe)]+6H_2O}.
$$

The two [phosphorus](../../../../../../phosphorus.md) donors of [dppe](../../../../../../1-2-bis-diphenylphosphino-ethane.md) chelate the same [metal](../../../../../../metal.md), forcing adjacent donor positions. The [diamagnetism](../../../../../../diamagnetism.md) identifies a square-planar, paired $d^8$ Ni(II) centre rather than a tetrahedral high-spin $d^8$ centre. Its valence count is $8+4_{\rm P,P}+4_{\rm Cl,Cl}=16$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). The reaction is [ligand substitution](../../../../../../ligand-substitution.md), not reduction of Ni(II).
