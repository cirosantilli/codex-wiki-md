↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) interacts with the **extra in-plane bonding system of [benzyne](../../../../../../benzyne.md)**, not by replacing the ordinary out-of-plane aromatic pi system. Take the ring and [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) to lie in the $xz$ plane, with the metal-to-C–C-midpoint direction along $z$ and C1–C2 along $x$. The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) orbitals directed toward the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) form an occupied symmetric combination and an empty antisymmetric combination.

The occupied in-plane $\pi$ combination donates into an empty [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) orbital of matching symmetry. An occupied [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) $d_{xz}$-type [orbital overlaps](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md) with the in-plane $\pi^*$ combination, providing back-donation. These are the same symmetry principles as [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) binding, rotated into the plane of the ring. The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) $p_y$ orbitals perpendicular to the ring continue to participate in its aromatic system. **The strained formal triple bond therefore does not supply two independent, ordinary out-of-plane [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) interactions.**

Strong population of the in-plane acceptor can be described as reduction of the aryne to a dianionic, metallacyclopropene-like ligand. That description emphasizes two M–C [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) and loss of the extra C–C [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md). It is particularly useful for early-metal aryne complexes. The lower panels of the orbital schematic distinguish the interacting in-plane orbitals from the perpendicular aromatic set.
