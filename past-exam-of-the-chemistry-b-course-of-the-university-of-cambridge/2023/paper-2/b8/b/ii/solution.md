<h1 id="b8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A useful strongly bound limit is **Zr(IV), $d^0$, and Ta(V), $d^0$, with an aryne dianion in each complex**. Charge balance uses two Cp$^-$ ligands for [zirconium](../../../../../../zirconium.md), and Cp\*$^-$ plus two [methyl ligands](../../../../../../methyl-ligand.md) for [tantalum](../../../../../../tantalum.md):

$$
x_{\rm Zr}-2-2=0,\qquad x_{\rm Ta}-1-2-2=0.
$$

Equivalently, an unreduced neutral-aryne description starts from Zr(II) and Ta(III), both $d^2$, and transfers that pair strongly into the aryne acceptor. These are alternative bookkeeping limits for the same covalent interaction; saying that a formal $d^0$ assignment has no available [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) must not erase the strong [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bonds in the reduced-ligand description.

Both ligands have therefore lost much of the extra in-plane C–C bonding of free [benzyne](../../../../../../benzyne.md) and approach the same reduced, approximately double-bond-length C1–C2 framework. **Their identical $1.364\,\text{Å}$ C–C distances are consistent with similar aryne reduction in both complexes.** The distance is intermediate in a delocalized ring, so it should not be converted into an exact integer bond order.

The M–C distances depend additionally on [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) size, effective nuclear attraction and [orbital overlap](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md). The high formal charge of Ta(V), the size effects associated with the [lanthanide contraction](../../../../../../lanthanide-contraction.md), and effective $5d$ covalent bonding favour shorter Ta–C contacts than Zr–C contacts. This is consistent with $2.075$ versus $2.250\,\text{Å}$. Different coordination envelopes and ancillary ligands also contribute, so this pair is not a controlled measurement of a radius or a [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond energy. **Similar internal ligand bond order can coexist with different [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–ligand distances.**
