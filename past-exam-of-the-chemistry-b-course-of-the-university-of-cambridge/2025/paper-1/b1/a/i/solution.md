<h1 id="b1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Number the fused [heterocycle](../../../../../../heterocyclic-compound.md) as a 2,3-dihydrobenzofuran: [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is position 1 and the acetamido [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md) occupies aromatic position 6. **Position 5 is the preferred site of [electrophilic substitution](../../../../../../electrophilic-substitution.md).** It is para to the ring [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and ortho to the acetamido group, so both groups stabilize its [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../../arenium-ion.md) by [resonance donation](../../../../../../resonance-donation.md). Position 7 also receives donation, but lies between the two [substituents](../../../../../../substituent.md) and is more crowded. Position 4 lacks the same combination of directing effects.

The aryl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) donates a [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) into the aromatic system. The acetamido [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) can also donate into the ring, although its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is shared with its own [carbonyl group](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), so it is less activating than a free amino group. Comparing resonance-stabilized intermediates and steric accessibility gives the position-5 prediction; simply counting heteroatoms does not.
