<h1 id="b1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Hydride removes the remaining alpha [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) at the substituted benzylic [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), releasing [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and forming the conjugated [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md). Its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of allyl chloroformate. Collapse of the [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md) expels chloride and gives the **allyl [enol carbonate](../../../../../../enol-carbonate.md)**, with the double bond between the original [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and the cyanomethyl-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md).

This is [O-acylation of an enolate](../../../../../../o-acylation-of-an-enolate.md): the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is trapped as $\mathrm{OCO_2CH_2CH{=}CH_2}$, retaining the enol double bond. The competing carbon-centred resonance representation explains possible C-acylation in other circumstances, but it would give a different connectivity from E. The indicated product selects the oxygen-trapping pathway and the conjugated enolate regioisomer.
