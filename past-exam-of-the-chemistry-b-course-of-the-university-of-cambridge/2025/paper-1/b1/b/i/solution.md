<h1 id="b1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first stage is a [Stork enamine reaction](../../../../../../stork-enamine-reaction.md). [Pyrrolidine](../../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) attacks the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), and [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfers give a [carbinolamine](../../../../../../carbinolamine.md). Loss of [water](../../../../../../water.md) gives an [iminium ion](../../../../../../iminium.md); removal of the benzylic alpha [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) produces the [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) conjugated with the [aromatic ring](../../../../../../aromatic-ring.md). Removal of [water](../../../../../../water.md) and prolonged heating favour this condensation. That conjugated [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) is preferred over the alternative with its double bond on the other side of the original [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).

The nucleophilic [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the methylene [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of bromoacetonitrile by an [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md), displacing bromide and making the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond. It is the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) beside the aromatic fusion that acquires $\mathrm{CH_2CN}$, as in D. Finally, [water](../../../../../../water.md) adds to the alkylated iminium [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfers and cleavage of the C–N bond regenerate the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) and release [pyrrolidine](../../../../../../pyrrolidine.md). This explains [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) alkylation, its position, and restoration of the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) rather than treating the amine as a permanent [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md).
