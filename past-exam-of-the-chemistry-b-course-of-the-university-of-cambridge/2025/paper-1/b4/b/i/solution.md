<h1 id="b4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Protonation of a [spiroacetal](../../../../../../spiroacetal.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) makes the adjacent C–O bond easier to cleave. Reversible opening gives an [oxocarbenium ion](../../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md), in which the spiro [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is locally planar. The tethered alcohol can attack either face; [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) closes the [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) and regenerates acid. Repetition interconverts the spiro epimers while retaining the other [stereogenic centres](../../../../../../stereocenter.md). Methanol can participate in reversible [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfer or trapping, but there is no need to invert the remote [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) centres.
