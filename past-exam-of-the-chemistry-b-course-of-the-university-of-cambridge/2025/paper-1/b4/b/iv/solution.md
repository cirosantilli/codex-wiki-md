The [anomeric effect](../../../../../../anomeric-effect.md) is the preference, in suitable cyclic [acetals](../../../../../../acetal.md), for an electronegative [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md) at an anomeric [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) to occupy an axial position even when simple steric reasoning favours equatorial. An important orbital contribution is donation from an endocyclic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) into the [antibonding orbital](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md) of the adjacent exocyclic C–O bond, $n_{\rm O}\to\sigma^*_{\rm C-O}$. The chair's axial arrangement supplies favourable antiperiplanar overlap. Electrostatic and [solvation](../../../../../../solvation.md) contributions can also affect the observed conformational preference.
