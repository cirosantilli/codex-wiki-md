<h1 id="b4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The diagnostic feature is the large [vicinal coupling](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md) characteristic of an axial–axial relationship in a chair. The td patterns for $H_a$ and $H_c$ contain two large couplings to neighbouring axial [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), so both are axial. The ddd patterns for $H_b$ and $H_d$ also include a large ring [vicinal coupling](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md), with the smaller values reflecting the other ring [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and the [side chain](../../../../../../side-chain.md). The assignments are

$$
\boxed{H_a,\ H_b,\ H_c,\ H_d\ \text{all axial}.}
$$

The [chemical shifts](../../../../../../chemical-shift.md) alone do not determine this assignment. In particular, three unequal splittings of a methine are not a geminal-coupling pattern.
