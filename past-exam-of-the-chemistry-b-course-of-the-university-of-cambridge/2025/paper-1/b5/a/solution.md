↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For hydrogen-bond acceptance, the three strongest groups are **the terminal [carboxylate](../../../../../carboxylate.md)**, **the Cys–CO–N(Pro) tertiary-amide [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)**, and **the Pro–CO–NH(Tyr) secondary-amide [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)**, in that qualitative order. Negative charge makes [carboxylate](../../../../../carboxylate.md) a particularly strong acceptor. Both [amide](../../../../../amide.md) [oxygens](../../../../../oxygen.md) accept strongly; greater [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) alkyl donation generally makes a tertiary [amide](../../../../../amide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) more basic than the comparable secondary-amide [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). This last ordering is a functional-group estimate, not a precise calculation of a folded [tripeptide](../../../../../tripeptide.md)'s site parameters.

For donation, the order is **terminal ammonium $>$ [tyrosine](../../../../../tyrosine.md) phenolic OH $>$ the peptide NH between Pro and Tyr**. The [thiol](../../../../../thiol.md) is a weaker donor. Protonated ammonium has no available [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) to accept; the [proline](../../../../../proline.md) [amide](../../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) has no N–H bond to donate, and [amide](../../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md) are delocalized into [carbonyl groups](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). The [carboxylate](../../../../../carboxylate.md) has no O–H donor in the drawn protonation state.
