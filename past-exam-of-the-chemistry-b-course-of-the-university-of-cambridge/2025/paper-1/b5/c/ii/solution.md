<h1 id="b5/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The second strand is the complete [reverse complement](../../../../../../reverse-complement.md) of the first. They can therefore form a **fully matched antiparallel 15-base-pair heteroduplex**, replacing the less fully paired hairpin/self-duplex states under conditions supporting hybridization:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
5'&\mathrm{TCGACTCAACAGTCG}&3'\\
3'&\mathrm{AGCTGAGTTGTCAGC}&5'.
\end{array}
$$

The lower line is written antiparallel; read it from its own 5′ end to recover the added oligomer.
