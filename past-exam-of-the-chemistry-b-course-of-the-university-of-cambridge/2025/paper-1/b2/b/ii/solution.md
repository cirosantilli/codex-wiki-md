<h1 id="b2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The qualitative [nucleophilicity](../../../../../../nucleophilicity.md) order is **$p$-OMe $>$ $p$-Me $>$ H $>$ $p$-Cl $\gg$ $p$-NO$_2$**, matching the product-yield order at the stated stopping time. Electron donation raises the availability of the [aniline](../../../../../../aniline.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) for attack. A para [methoxy group](../../../../../../methoxy-group.md) donates by resonance, while methyl is a weaker donor. Chlorine withdraws inductively and gives an [aniline](../../../../../../aniline.md) less nucleophilic than the unsubstituted compound. A para [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md) strongly withdraws by resonance: electron flow from [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) through the ring into the [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md) stabilizes a charge-separated form and greatly reduces [nucleophilicity](../../../../../../nucleophilicity.md).

The key arrows for the nitroaniline comparison move the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) into its own [aromatic ring](../../../../../../aromatic-ring.md), shift the ring $\pi$ bonds toward the para [nitro group](../../../../../../nitro-group.md), and shift a nitro N=O bond toward [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The methoxy donor produces the opposite electronic bias. These are finite-time yields, so they support a kinetic explanation qualitatively; they are not by themselves measured [rate constants](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) or [equilibrium constants](../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md).
