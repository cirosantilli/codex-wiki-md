<h1 id="b2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [aniline](../../../../../../aniline.md) [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) attacks the ipso [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md). The aromatic $\pi$ electrons shift to give a [Meisenheimer complex](../../../../../../meisenheimer-complex.md), with negative charge delocalized to the positions ortho and para to the attacked [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). At either nitro [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md), that charge can be delocalized further onto nitro [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Subsequent [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfer and fluoride elimination restore [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md) and give the diarylamine.

Both the ortho and para [nitro groups](../../../../../../nitro-group.md) stabilize the addition intermediate in 1-fluoro-2,4-dinitrobenzene. The para-mononitro compound lacks the additional ortho stabilization, so addition is less favourable and generally slower under the stated conditions. This comparison concerns stabilization of the addition pathway; the presence of [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) does not make the reaction an ordinary aliphatic [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md).
