<h1 id="b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Base deprotonates the [sulfonamide](../../../../../sulfonamide.md). Its [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) attacks one [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) from the back, breaking the C–O bond at that [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and producing an adjacent [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md). The [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) attachments on the [cyclopentane](../../../../../cyclopentane.md) are therefore **trans**. The [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md) then attacks the aromatic [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) bearing the [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) ortho to the sulfonyl group. Intramolecular [nucleophilic aromatic substitution](../../../../../nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md) expels fluoride and closes a seven-membered [heterocycle](../../../../../heterocyclic-compound.md) with the path $\mathrm{Ar{-}S{-}N{-}C_1{-}C_2{-}O{-}Ar}$. The para [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) remains.

This tandem epoxide-opening/ring-closing pathway is required by the formula of F: simple [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) addition would retain both [fluorines](../../../../../fluorine.md), whereas F contains only one. In the second stage, [pyrrolidine](../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) replaces the remaining para [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) by another [nucleophilic aromatic substitution](../../../../../nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md), producing G. The mass balance is F plus [pyrrolidine](../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) minus HF, as required by the given formula of G.

The meso [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) has [enantiotopic](../../../../../enantiotopicity.md) ends. With achiral reagents, opening at either end gives an enantiomeric product, so **F is a racemic pair with the same trans relative configuration; G retains that configuration**.
