<h1 id="b2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A undergoes a [Robinson annulation](../../../../../../robinson-annulation.md). For an unambiguous connectivity description, call the original ring [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C1 and the side-chain-bearing alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) C6. Label the appended chain $\mathrm{C6{-}a{-}b{-}c(=O){-}d}$, where d is initially methyl. Base makes the enolate at d, which attacks C1 intramolecularly to close the six-membered ring $\mathrm{C1{-}C6{-}a{-}b{-}c{-}d}$. The [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) $\pi$ electrons move to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and protonation gives the beta-hydroxy [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).

An [E1cb elimination](../../../../../../e1cb-elimination.md) then removes the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) on d and the hydroxy group on C1, giving the conjugated enone **with C1=d and a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) at c**. The original ring methyl remains on the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) next to C1 on the other side. The two rings share the C1–C6 bond. The molecular formula is consistent with loss of one [water](../../../../../../water.md) molecule from the diketone.
