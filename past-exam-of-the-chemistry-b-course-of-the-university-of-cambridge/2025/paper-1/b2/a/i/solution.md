<h1 id="b2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Pyrrolidine](../../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) and 2-methylcyclohexanone first form an [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md), through [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) addition, [carbinolamine](../../../../../../carbinolamine.md) formation, dehydration to an [iminium ion](../../../../../../iminium.md), and alpha [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md). The less hindered [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is on the side opposite the methyl-bearing alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). It attacks the terminal beta [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of [methyl vinyl ketone](../../../../../../methyl-vinyl-ketone.md), while the conjugated $\pi$ system shifts to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Protonation gives an alkylated iminium species; aqueous acid then hydrolyses it back to the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md).

The result is a [Michael addition](../../../../../../michael-addition.md) product: the previously unsubstituted alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bears $-\mathrm{CH_2CH_2COCH_3}$. In drawing the arrows, start the new C–C bond at the [enamine](../../../../../../enamine.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), not at [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md). The iminium bond is restored during addition and removed by [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md). The sequence is an enamine-mediated conjugate addition, rather than direct addition to the [methyl vinyl ketone](../../../../../../methyl-vinyl-ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).
