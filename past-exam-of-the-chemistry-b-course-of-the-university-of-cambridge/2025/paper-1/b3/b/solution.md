<h1 id="b3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Reduction makes [stereogenic centres](../../../../../stereocenter.md) at C2 and C3 of the indan-1-one framework. The four products are the **cis pair $(2R,3S)$ and $(2S,3R)$**, and the **trans pair $(2R,3R)$ and $(2S,3S)$**. Each pair consists of [enantiomers](../../../../../enantiomer.md); any cis member is diastereomeric to either trans member. There is no meso product because the carbonyl-containing and aromatic sides of the ring do not make C2 and C3 symmetry-equivalent.

For checking the [Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority rules](../../../../../cahn-ingold-prelog-priority-rules.md), at C2 the order is carbonyl-side [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $>$ phenyl $>$ C3 $>$ H. At C3 it is fused aromatic [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $>$ phenyl $>$ C2 $>$ H. The fused aromatic path outranks the separate phenyl path because it reaches the carbonyl-containing part of the framework.
