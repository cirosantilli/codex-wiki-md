<h1 id="b3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The products are the two mirror-image **2,3-epoxy-2,3-diphenylindan-1-ones**, shown in the bottom row of the stereoisomer diagram. The original [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) remains, and an additional [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) bridges the former double bond, giving the stated formula.

In the [Weitz-Scheffer epoxidation](../../../../../weitz-scheffer-reaction.md), hydroperoxide anion adds conjugatively to the enone. The resulting enolate closes onto the attached [peroxide](../../../../../peroxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) with O–O bond cleavage and loss of hydroxide. Attack on the two faces of the achiral planar substrate gives the two [enantiomers](../../../../../enantiomer.md) in equal amounts. This is a racemic [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) pair, rather than a one-to-one mixture of different diastereomeric [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) connectivities.
