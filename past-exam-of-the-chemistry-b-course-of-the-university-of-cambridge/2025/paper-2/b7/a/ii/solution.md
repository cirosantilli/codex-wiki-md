↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The usual qualitative order of increasing [ligand field](../../../../../../ligand-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Cl^-}<\mathrm{P(^{t}Bu)_2Ph}<\mathrm{PPh_3}<\mathrm{CO}.}
$$

A [chloride ligand](../../../../../../chloride-ligand.md) is a [sigma-donor ligand](../../../../../../sigma-donor-ligand.md) and [pi-donor ligand](../../../../../../pi-donor-ligand.md); its occupied pi orbitals raise the metal $t_{2g}$-derived levels and reduce the splitting. A [carbonyl ligand](../../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md) is a particularly effective [pi-acceptor ligand](../../../../../../pi-acceptor-ligand.md), lowering those levels and producing a large splitting. Both [phosphine ligands](../../../../../../phosphine-ligand.md) are sigma donors with acceptor orbitals of mainly P–C [antibonding molecular orbital](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md) character. Replacing [phenyl groups](../../../../../../phenyl-group.md) by electron-releasing [tert-butyl groups](../../../../../../tert-butyl-group.md) increases sigma basicity but generally weakens pi acceptance, so the alkyl-rich phosphine is placed below [triphenylphosphine](../../../../../../triphenylphosphine.md) in this qualitative pi-bonding comparison. It would be wrong to equate a stronger sigma donor automatically with a stronger-field ligand, or to invoke empty phosphorus $3d$ orbitals as the essential acceptors.[1992, pp. 4391–4398, sigma basicity, pi acidity and phosphorus orbital participation](../../../../../../metal-phosphine-bonding-revisited-sigma-basicity-pi-acidity-and-the-role-of-phosphorus-d-orbitals-in-zerovalent-metal-phosphine-complexes-by-gianfranco-pacchioni-and-paul-s-bagus.md)

This is a qualitative [spectrochemical series](../../../../../../spectrochemical-series.md) prediction, not a universal numerical ranking for all metals and geometries. The opposing sigma and pi trends mean that precise comparisons require matched complexes and measured splittings; a [Tolman electronic parameter](../../../../../../tolman-electronic-parameter.md) measures net effects on CO backbonding and is not itself a ligand-field splitting.
