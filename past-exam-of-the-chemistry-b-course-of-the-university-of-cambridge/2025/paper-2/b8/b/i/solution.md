<h1 id="b8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $L^-$ for the cyclopentadienyl ligand bearing its $\mathrm{CH_2}$–2-pyridyl arm; $\eta^5\!:\!\kappa N$ means that its ring and pyridyl [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) both bind the same metal. **$D=[\mathrm{Mo}(\eta^5\!:\!\kappa N\text{-}L)(\mathrm{CO})_2(\mathrm{NCMe})]^+$.** The base removes the pyridinium N–H [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) in $C$, restoring a [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md). Intramolecular [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) coordination then replaces one [acetonitrile](../../../../../../acetonitrile.md) ligand and closes a [chelate ring](../../../../../../chelate-ring.md). Both CO ligands are retained; the [molybdenum](../../../../../../molybdenum.md) remains Mo(II), $d^4$.

This is an [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) followed by [chelation](../../../../../../chelation.md), with metal [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count $4+6+2_N+4_{\mathrm{CO}}+2_{\mathrm{NCMe}}=18$. The [chelate effect](../../../../../../chelate-effect.md), favorable formation of the Mo–N bond, and release of a solvent molecule explain the driving force. Merely invoking a 16-to-18-electron change would be misleading because $C$ is already 18-electron. In the preceding acid treatment, protonation of the $\eta^3$ [allyl ligand](../../../../../../allyl-ligand.md) allows release of [propene](../../../../../../propene.md); the other [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) resides on the pendant [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md), accounting for $C$'s overall $2+$ charge. One [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) is removed here; the cyclopentadienyl tether is not deprotonated or detached.
