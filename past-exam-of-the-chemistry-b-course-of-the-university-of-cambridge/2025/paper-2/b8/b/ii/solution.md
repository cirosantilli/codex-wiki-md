<h1 id="b8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**$E=\mathrm{Mo}(\eta^5\!:\!\kappa N\text{-}L)(\mathrm{CO})_2\mathrm{Cl}$**, with chloride replacing the remaining [acetonitrile](../../../../../../acetonitrile.md). The pendant [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md) remains attached. The substitution removes the positive [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) on $D$ but does not change the Mo(II) [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md). In ionic [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md), both neutral [acetonitrile](../../../../../../acetonitrile.md) and anionic [chloride ligand](../../../../../../chloride-ligand.md) donate two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), giving $4+6+2_N+4_{\mathrm{CO}}+2_{\mathrm{Cl}}=18$ in $E$.

[acetonitrile](../../../../../../acetonitrile.md) is the remaining weak, exchangeable monodentate donor. Its displacement forms a favorable Mo–Cl bond at a cationic center, retains the intramolecular Mo–N bond, and restores an 18-electron product after substitution. The following formulas specify the coordination changes directly:

$$
\underbrace{[\mathrm{Mo}(\eta^5\!:\!\kappa N\text{-}L)(\mathrm{CO})_2(\mathrm{NCMe})]^+}_{D}
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cl^-}}
\underbrace{\mathrm{Mo}(\eta^5\!:\!\kappa N\text{-}L)(\mathrm{CO})_2\mathrm{Cl}}_{E}+\mathrm{MeCN}.
$$

[Tetramethylammonium](../../../../../../tetramethylammonium.md) is a spectator counterion; it is not a [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) donor to Mo.
