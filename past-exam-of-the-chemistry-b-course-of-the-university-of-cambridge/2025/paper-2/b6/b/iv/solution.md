↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**$B$ removes more blue-green light and appears orange-red; $C$ removes shorter-wavelength blue light and appears yellow.** The observed [color](../../../../../../color.md) is the combined transmitted part of the visible spectrum, approximately complementary to the absorbed band, rather than the color corresponding to the absorption maximum itself. The longer-wavelength band of the O-bound [nitrito ligand](../../../../../../nitrito-ligand.md) complex therefore shifts the transmitted balance toward orange/red compared with the N-bound [nitro ligand](../../../../../../nitro-ligand.md) complex. Band width, other absorption bands, [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) and optical path length affect the shade and darkness; a single maximum does not quantitatively determine the perceived [color](../../../../../../color.md).
