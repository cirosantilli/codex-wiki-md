**The N-bound isomer $C$ is the thermodynamically favored form; heating accelerates access to it.** Formation of $B$ retains the original metal–[oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attachment and is a plausible [kinetic control](../../../../../../kinetic-control.md) outcome. Rearranging that attachment requires overcoming an [activation free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-activation.md), especially at a substitutionally inert low-spin [cobalt](../../../../../../cobalt.md)(III) center. Raising [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) speeds the [linkage isomerization](../../../../../../linkage-isomerization.md), allowing the more stable N-bound [nitro ligand](../../../../../../nitro-ligand.md) to accumulate. Its stronger [ligand field](../../../../../../ligand-field.md) and different covalent bonding help rationalize the stability, but a larger field splitting alone is not a complete calculation of the isomerization [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md). The classical crystallographic comparison establishes the different attachments and thermodynamic direction.[1979, pp. 1869–1874, structural comparison and solid-state isomerization](../../../../../../nitrito-nitro-linkage-isomerization-in-the-solid-state-2-a-comparative-study-of-the-structures-of-nitrito-and-nitropentaamminecobalt-iii-dichloride-by-ingmar-grenthe-and-ellika-nordin.md)
