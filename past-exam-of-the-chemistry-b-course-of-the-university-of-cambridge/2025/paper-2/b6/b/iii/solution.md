<h1 id="b6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The relevant [spectrochemical series](../../../../../../spectrochemical-series.md) order is O-bound nitrito $<$ [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) $<$ N-bound nitro. A [pi-donor ligand](../../../../../../pi-donor-ligand.md) tends to raise the metal orbitals derived from $t_{2g}$, reducing the [octahedral splitting](../../../../../../octahedral-splitting.md); a [pi-acceptor ligand](../../../../../../pi-acceptor-ligand.md) stabilizes those orbitals, increasing the splitting. Since [photon energy](../../../../../../photon-energy.md) is $E=hc/\lambda$, the stronger-field complex absorbs at the shorter [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda_C=458\ \mathrm{nm},\qquad
\lambda_{[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+}}=474\ \mathrm{nm},\qquad
\lambda_B=486\ \mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The corresponding [spectroscopic wavenumbers](../../../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) are approximately $21834$, $21097$ and $20576\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. These are the observed band energies, not three exact independent determinations of $\Delta_o$: low-spin $d^6$ transition energies also contain [electron repulsion](../../../../../../electron-repulsion.md) contributions, and replacing one ligand lowers the ideal octahedral [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). The relative assignment is nevertheless robust.
