↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**$A=[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(OH)}]^{2+}$, $B=[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(ONO)}]^{2+}$, $C=[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(NO_2)}]^{2+}$, and the by-product is $\mathrm{HNO_2}$.** The first transformation is [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) of coordinated [water](../../../../../../water.md), not loss of an [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) ligand. The [hydroxo ligand](../../../../../../hydroxo-ligand.md) in $A$ is then nitrosated at [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), retaining its original Co–O attachment in the initially formed O-bound [nitrito ligand](../../../../../../nitrito-ligand.md). Atom and [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) balance give

$$
[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(OH)}]^{2+}+\mathrm{N_2O_3}
\longrightarrow[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(ONO)}]^{2+}+\mathrm{HNO_2}.
$$

The [relative molecular mass](../../../../../../relative-molecular-mass.md) of [nitrous acid](../../../../../../nitrous-acid.md) is approximately $1+14+2(16)=47$. Heating converts the O-bound [nitrito ligand](../../../../../../nitrito-ligand.md) to the N-bound [nitro ligand](../../../../../../nitro-ligand.md); these are [linkage isomers](../../../../../../linkage-isomer.md), with Co remaining in [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) $+3$ throughout.
