<h1 id="b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [nitrite](../../../../../nitrite.md) ion is an [ambidentate ligand](../../../../../ambidentate-ligand.md): its [Lewis structures](../../../../../lewis-structure.md) leave donor [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md) on both [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). In the N-bound mode, $\mathrm{M{-}N(O)_2}$, the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) makes a [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) to the metal, while a suitable unoccupied [nitrite](../../../../../nitrite.md) [molecular orbital](../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) can accept metal $d$ [electron](../../../../../electron.md) density by [pi backbonding](../../../../../pi-backbonding.md). Thus **N-bound nitro is a sigma donor and pi acceptor**. The [nitrite](../../../../../nitrite.md) group remains bent at [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md); N coordination does not make it a linear nitrosyl.

In the O-bound mode, $\mathrm{M{-}O{-}N{=}O}$, an [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) makes the metal–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md). A second occupied [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) orbital can donate sideways into an available metal orbital: **O-bound nitrito is a sigma and pi donor**, rather than the strong pi acceptor of the N-bound description. [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md)'s greater [electronegativity](../../../../../electronegativity-split.md) lowers its donor orbital energies, but the occupied [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) orbitals still explain its pi-donor classification. Actual mixing depends on metal [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) and geometry.

A further possibility is [bidentate coordination](../../../../../bidentate-coordination.md), $\mathrm{M}(\kappa^2\!\text{-}O,O\text{-}\mathrm{NO_2})$, with both [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) atoms attached to one metal. Each attachment has oxygen-to-metal sigma donation; additional pi donation depends on orbital alignment. [nitrite](../../../../../nitrite.md) can also be a [bridging ligand](../../../../../bridging-ligand.md) between metals. Neither alternative should be confused with [linkage isomerism](../../../../../linkage-isomerism.md), which specifically compares attachment through different atoms at the same binding site.
