The six P–N bonds are not well described as three permanently localized single bonds alternating with three ordinary double bonds. The strongly polar P–N framework has contributions from [ionic bonding](../../../../../ionic-bonding-split.md) and [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) donation into neighboring phosphorus-centered acceptor combinations, including P–Cl $\sigma^*$ orbitals. This [negative hyperconjugation](../../../../../negative-hyperconjugation.md) supplies additional P–N bonding and equivalent environments around the ring. Approximate planarity is compatible with these interactions, but equal lengths and planarity alone do not establish benzene-like [aromaticity](../../../../../aromaticity.md). Large phosphorus $3d$ participation is not required.[1997, abstract and bonding analysis, ionic sigma bonding and nitrogen-to-P–X sigma-star donation](../../../../../molecular-structures-and-conformations-of-polyphosphazenes-a-study-based-on-density-functional-calculations-of-oligomers-by-huai-sun.md)

**Gallium binds to a ring [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), and the two P–N bonds flanking that [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) lengthen.** Formation of the N$\to$GaCl$_3$ [coordinate covalent bond](../../../../../coordinate-covalent-bond.md) changes the available [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [electron](../../../../../electron.md) density and local geometry, weakening its contribution to the adjacent P–N interactions. The other four P–N bonds retain greater multiple-bond character and are correspondingly shorter. Loss of the threefold equivalence and altered local geometry permit the ring to pucker. This is a [Lewis acid-base adduct](../../../../../lewis-acid-base-adduct.md), not replacement of a P–Cl bond by GaCl$_3$. Single-crystal structures and variable-temperature multinuclear [nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) establish this class of nitrogen-bound group-13 adducts.[2011, pp. 8937–8945, adduct structures and variable-temperature NMR](../../../../../group-13-lewis-acid-adducts-of-pcl2n-3-by-zin-min-tun-and-others.md)
