**Y91F implicates the Tyr hydroxyl, while E57Q implicates the Glu carboxylic-acid chemistry.** Replacing [tyrosine](../../../../../../tyrosine.md) by [phenylalanine](../../../../../../phenylalanine.md) retains an aromatic side chain but removes its hydroxyl, supporting a role for Tyr91 as a [hydrogen bond donor](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond-donor.md) that polarizes the steroid C3 carbonyl and stabilizes an enolate-like intermediate or [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md). Its hydroxyl could participate in a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) relay, but inactivity alone does not establish that it supplies the final [proton](../../../../../../proton.md).

Replacing a [glutamate residue](../../../../../../glutamate-residue.md) by [glutamine](../../../../../../glutamine.md) approximately preserves side-chain size while replacing a titratable carboxylic acid by an amide. Loss of activity, combined with the acidic activity range, supports protonated Glu57 as a carbonyl-activating acid and possible [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) donor. The two residues can cooperate to stabilize [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) charge during reduction. The inhibitor-binding structure supports local contacts; it is not a direct picture of a [testosterone](../../../../../../testosterone.md) transition state, and an N–H contact of the aza inhibitor need not occur in [testosterone](../../../../../../testosterone.md).

To distinguish these chemical roles from indirect effects, compare mutant and wild-type abundance, membrane insertion and folding, [cofactor](../../../../../../cofactor-biochemistry.md) binding, and steroid binding. Measure initial rates with matched enzyme amounts and include no-enzyme and inhibitor-treated wild-type controls. If binding survives but turnover fails, the evidence for a catalytic defect is stronger. The published study combines structure, mutagenesis and modeled [testosterone](../../../../../../testosterone.md) placement, so the proposed residue roles should be distinguished from direct observation of [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) trajectories.[2020, Fig. 3a,c–e, structural contacts, docking and mutant activity comparison](../../../../../../structure-of-human-steroid-5-alpha-reductase-2-with-the-anti-androgen-drug-finasteride-by-qingpin-xiao-and-others.md)
