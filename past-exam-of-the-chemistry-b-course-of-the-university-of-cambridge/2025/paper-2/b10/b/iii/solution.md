<h1 id="b10/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Hydride is delivered to steroid C5 and a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) to C4; the C3 ketone is retained.** Use the conjugated fragment $\mathrm{O{=}C3{-}C4{=}C5}$. Carbonyl [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) from Tyr91–OH and protonated Glu57 lower the barrier to [conjugate reduction](../../../../../../conjugate-reduction.md). Draw a curved arrow from the nicotinamide C4–H bond of [NADPH](../../../../../../nadph.md) to steroid C5, shift the C4=C5 pi pair toward C3–C4, and shift C3=O pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) onto [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The nicotinamide ring rearomatizes to [NADP+](../../../../../../nadp-plus.md); the steroid becomes a bound [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md), or its protonated [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) equivalent. [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) shifts across the nicotinamide ring must accompany C–H bond cleavage rather than leaving an unexplained neutral [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) radical.

Next, reform C3=O and protonate steroid C4 from an appropriately positioned acid, directly or through an [enol](../../../../../../enol.md)/proton-relay route. This regenerates the catalytic acid after solvent reprotonation. The enzyme fixes the hydride-accepting face so that the new C5–H has **the 5α configuration**, giving [dihydrotestosterone](../../../../../../dihydrotestosterone.md); delivery to the opposite face would give a different ring-junction configuration. This is not simple 1,2 reduction of the C3 carbonyl to an alcohol. The net local bookkeeping is one hydride plus one [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) across C4=C5, with two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) supplied by [NADPH](../../../../../../nadph.md). The original reactive-fragment diagram shows the enolate-forming arrows and the separate protonation step.
