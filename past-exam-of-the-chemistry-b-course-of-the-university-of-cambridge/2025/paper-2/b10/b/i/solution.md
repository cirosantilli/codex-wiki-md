↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**[catalysis](../../../../../../catalysis.md) requires a protonated group, consistent with a general-acid contribution.** Loss of its [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) as [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) rises would remove carbonyl activation or the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) needed to finish the conjugate reduction. A [glutamate residue](../../../../../../glutamate-residue.md) with a shifted active-site $pK_a$ is a plausible candidate, but a [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) cutoff alone neither identifies the residue nor measures a precise $pK_a$. Irreversible unfolding or changes in substrate binding must also be excluded by reversible [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) jumps, protein-integrity checks and initial-rate measurements.

For the mechanistic notation below, the reduced [cofactor](../../../../../../cofactor-biochemistry.md) is written [NADPH](../../../../../../nadph.md), the established cofactor of human steroid [5α-reductase](../../../../../../5-alpha-reductase.md). The original PDF explicitly labels it [NADH](../../../../../../nadh.md); its abbreviated N-substituent does not display the phosphate distinction. [NADH](../../../../../../nadh.md) and [NADPH](../../../../../../nadph.md) have the same reactive reduced nicotinamide ring, so the local hydride-transfer arrows are unchanged, although their biochemical specificity is different.[2020, Fig. 3 and catalytic-mechanism discussion: NADPH-dependent reduction and protonated E57](../../../../../../structure-of-human-steroid-5-alpha-reductase-2-with-the-anti-androgen-drug-finasteride-by-qingpin-xiao-and-others.md)
