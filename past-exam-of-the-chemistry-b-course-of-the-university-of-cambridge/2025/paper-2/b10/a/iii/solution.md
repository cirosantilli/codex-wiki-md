↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 7, a [glutamate residue](../../../../../../glutamate-residue.md) is normally carboxylate. **Its most natural role is general-base activation of the attacking [water](../../../../../../water.md).** Draw the carboxylate [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) toward a [water](../../../../../../water.md) H, the [water](../../../../../../water.md) O–H pair toward its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and that [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md)'s [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) toward C3 while the conjugated pi system moves toward the carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md):

$$
\mathrm{Glu{-}CO_2^-\cdots H{-}OH\cdots C3}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{Glu{-}CO_2H}+\text{bound beta-hydroxy enolate}.
$$

This can be concerted, avoiding a freely diffusing hydroxide intermediate. The protonated glutamate subsequently returns its [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) to the [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) system, directly or through solvent, regenerating carboxylate. A favorable arrangement is needed; ordinary carboxylate does not quantitatively deprotonate bulk [water](../../../../../../water.md) in a prior equilibrium.

The side-chain amide of [glutamine](../../../../../../glutamine.md) is neutral. **Its N–H can donate a [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) to the carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and stabilize developing [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) charge**, while its amide [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) can accept a [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) to orient attacking [water](../../../../../../water.md). Draw $\mathrm{Gln{-}CONH_2\cdots O{=}C1{-}C2{=}C3}$, with the dotted interaction from amide N–H to substrate [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). This assists polarization and [transition state stabilization](../../../../../../transition-state-stabilization.md); the amide [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) is not an ordinary amine base because its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is delocalized into its own carbonyl.

The guanidinium group of [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md) is positively charged. **It can stabilize developing negative charge by hydrogen bonding and electrostatic attraction**, drawn $\mathrm{Arg{-}guanidinium^+\cdots O^{\delta-}{-}C1}$. Its delocalized cation is not the preferred neutral general base at [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 7. Productive [catalysis](../../../../../../catalysis.md) requires stronger stabilization of the reacting arrangement than of the unreactive ground-state complex.

[Phenylalanine](../../../../../../phenylalanine.md) and [leucine](../../../../../../leucine.md) have hydrocarbon side chains and provide no conventional acid/base or hydrogen-bond donor/acceptor group here. They can position the [enone](../../../../../../enone.md), exclude unproductive solvent and select the attacked face through [hydrophobic effect](../../../../../../hydrophobic-effect.md) and [steric hindrance](../../../../../../steric-hindrance.md). Those binding effects can assist [catalysis](../../../../../../catalysis.md) but should not be drawn as an imaginary side-chain [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) relay. The original schematic depicts the Glu [water](../../../../../../water.md) relay and the Gln/Arg oxyanion-stabilizing contacts.
