<h1 id="b10/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The C–O-forming transition state has a partial bond from [water](../../../../../../water.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) to C3, positive charge developing on that [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), and negative charge delocalizing toward the carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md).** A compact drawing is

$$
\left[\mathrm{R{-}C1(\overset{\delta-}{O})\cdots C2H\cdots C3H(R')\cdots\overset{\delta+}{OH_2}}\right]^\ddagger.
$$

Here the two C–C dotted connections denote redistribution of pi bonding, not cleavage of either [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) sigma bond. In a bond-order drawing retain the full sigma skeleton: C1–O is changing from double toward single, C1–C2 from single toward double, C2–C3 from double toward single, and C3–O from absent toward single. The incoming [water](../../../../../../water.md) approaches a face of the approximately planar conjugated unit and C3 begins to become tetrahedral. The total [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) remains zero. If [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfer to another [water](../../../../../../water.md) occurs concertedly, include a partially broken incoming-water O–H bond and a partially formed relay O–H bond; that is a solvent-assisted variant, not a different regioselectivity. The original mechanism schematic identifies these bond changes without depicting a falsely isolated free hydroxide reagent.
