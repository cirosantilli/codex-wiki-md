<h1 id="b10/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The beta-carbon bond-forming [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md) retains the same basic connectivity across this [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) range, but the predominant proton-transfer route and enzyme [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) change. Near the acidic end, **protonated glutamate can act as a general acid**, donating a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) or a strong [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) to carbonyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and making C3 more [electrophilic](../../../../../../electrophilicity.md); it is less available as the carboxylate general base that activates [water](../../../../../../water.md). Above its local $pK_a$, **carboxylate-mediated [water](../../../../../../water.md) activation is favored**. Acid [catalysis](../../../../../../catalysis.md) therefore shifts positive-charge stabilization toward a protonated carbonyl, whereas base-assisted attack reduces positive-charge buildup on incoming [water](../../../../../../water.md). Neither a unique transition-state geometry nor a numerical [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) optimum follows from [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) alone.

Using a free-solution glutamate side-chain $pK_a\simeq4.2$ only as an illustration, the deprotonated fraction is $[1+10^{pK_a-\mathrm{pH}}]^{-1}$: about 0.059 at [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 3 and 0.998 at [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 7. An enzyme environment can shift that $pK_a$ substantially. The [glutamine](../../../../../../glutamine.md) amide and the hydrocarbon side chains remain in their ordinary forms from [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 3 to 10; [arginine](../../../../../../arginine.md) guanidinium, with a typical free side-chain $pK_a$ around 12.5, remains predominantly positive even at [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 10. Thus the major side-chain switch among those listed is glutamate. At the alkaline end, bulk hydroxide can also contribute to nonenzymatic [conjugate addition](../../../../../../conjugate-addition.md). A measured pH-rate profile must control substrate stability and enzyme folding before assigning every loss of activity to an active-site titration.
