<h1 id="b5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [water](../../../../../../water.md), separated [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) require separate hydration environments around nonpolar surface. Bringing them together buries part of that surface and reorganizes the surrounding [water](../../../../../../water.md). The resulting [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) contribution favours association relative to [cyclohexane](../../../../../../cyclohexane.md), where [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) was already well solvated by dispersion. **This [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md)-mediated contribution is the [hydrophobic effect](../../../../../../hydrophobic-effect.md)**, not formation of a new [covalent bond](../../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md) or a claim that [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) has become a conventional [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md)-bond acceptor like [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md).

The supplied constants imply a thirtyfold enhancement. With matched [standard states](../../../../../../standard-state.md),

$$
\Delta\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln(0.9/0.03)=-RT\ln30\simeq\boxed{-8.43\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}}
$$

at an illustrative 298 K. The [water](../../../../../../water.md) value is still slightly below $1\ \mathrm{M^{-1}}$, so enhancement relative to [cyclohexane](../../../../../../cyclohexane.md) does not mean a negative standard association free energy for a 1 M [standard state](../../../../../../standard-state.md), nor does it imply that dilute [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) is all dimer. No universal “[entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) only” explanation is needed: [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) both contribute and depend on [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md).
