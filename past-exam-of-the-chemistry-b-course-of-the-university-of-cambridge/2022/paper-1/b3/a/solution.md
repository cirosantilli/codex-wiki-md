<h1 id="b3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Number the OH-bearing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C1 and the adjacent brominated [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C2. In the trans [bromohydrin](../../../../../bromohydrin.md), [base](../../../../../base-chemistry.md) generates an [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md). A [chair conformation](../../../../../chair-conformation.md) can place O at C1 and Br at C2 trans-diaxial, aligning the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../nucleophile.md) for [backside attack](../../../../../backside-attack.md) at C2. Intramolecular SN2 closure expels [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md) and inverts C2: **the retained C1–O bond and the newly formed C2–O bond are both on the original OH face**, giving the [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) shown. [ring inversion](../../../../../ring-inversion.md) can provide the reactive chair even if it is not the dominant ground-state conformer.

The cis [bromohydrin](../../../../../bromohydrin.md) cannot place OH and Br trans-diaxial: one is axial when the other is equatorial. In the chair with Br axial, the [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) on C1 can instead be antiperiplanar to C2–Br. An [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md)-assisted semipinacol pathway moves the C1–H [bonding pair](../../../../../bond-pair.md) to C2 while the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) forms C1=O and [bromide](../../../../../bromide.md) departs. **[hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) migration gives the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) without changing the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton.** The [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) is transferred to C2 rather than expelled as a free [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md). The original phenyl-bearing stereocentre is not involved in either bond-reorganization sequence. Configuration controls which properly aligned displacement or migration can occur; a flat ring drawing alone conceals that distinction.
