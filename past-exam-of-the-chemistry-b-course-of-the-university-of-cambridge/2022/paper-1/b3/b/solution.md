<h1 id="b3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of the secondary OH permits [water](../../../../../water.md) departure. The neighbouring phenylthio [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) can assist this departure by forming a bond to the adjacent [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), generating a [thiiranium ion](../../../../../thiiranium-ion.md) spanning that [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and the original [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md)-bearing [cyclohexane](../../../../../cyclohexane.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Label the latter Q and the former L. **The temporary Q–S–L bridge transfers the phenylthio attachment from Q to L when its Q–S bond subsequently breaks.** [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) attack opposite the departing OH accounts for the new C–S [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) without requiring a freely rotating [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md).

For the protected terminal [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md), no free tethered OH is available to trap the bridge. Removal of a beta [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) from a [cyclohexane](../../../../../cyclohexane.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) adjacent to Q forms the ring [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) while Q–S breaks. [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) remains bonded to L, giving the allylic phenyl [thioether](../../../../../thioether.md). [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) is regenerated by the [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md), so the [catalyst](../../../../../catalyst.md) need not be consumed.

For the free terminal [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md), its [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks Q intramolecularly, opening Q–S instead. Count the new [ether](../../../../../ether.md) ring: **O–CH2–CH(Me)–L–Q**, a five-membered ring sharing Q with the [cyclohexane](../../../../../cyclohexane.md). [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives the spirocyclic [ether](../../../../../ether.md). This is capture of the same neighbouring-group-stabilized intermediate rather than migration of the [methyl group](../../../../../methyl-group.md). The pre-existing methyl-bearing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) retains its configuration; its wedge/dash appearance changes when the product is redrawn in a different ring projection. The two substrates differ in whether a suitably placed [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../nucleophile.md) is available for intramolecular trapping.
