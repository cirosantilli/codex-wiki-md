<h1 id="b3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Distinguish the original [thiirane](../../../../../thiirane.md) [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) $S_o$ from the [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) $S_n$ supplied by [thiocyanate](../../../../../thiocyanate.md). [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) attack by $\mathrm{{}^-S_n{-}C\equiv N}$ at one [thiirane](../../../../../thiirane.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) opens its C–$S_o$ bond with inversion. This produces a [thiolate](../../../../../thiolate.md) at the neighbouring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and a C–$S_n$–CN group at the attacked [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The OH-bearing remote [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) is unaffected.

The original [thiolate](../../../../../thiolate.md) $S_o^-$ now attacks the nitrile [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) intramolecularly. Shift one C≡N pi pair onto [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md). The resulting cyclic imidodithiocarbonate has the five-membered sequence **ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–$S_o$–C(=N$^-$)–$S_n$–adjacent ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)**; its two [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) atoms are also part of the original six-membered carbocycle, exactly giving the hinted bicyclic intermediate.

Collapse can reform C≡N while breaking the nitrile-[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–$S_n$ bond. This transfers the CN group to $S_o$ and generates $S_n^-$ as the [thiolate](../../../../../thiolate.md) on the other ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). That [thiolate](../../../../../thiolate.md) attacks the remaining C–$S_o$ bond from the back, expelling $\mathrm{{}^-S_o{-}C\equiv N}$ and reclosing the [thiirane](../../../../../thiirane.md). **Each [thiirane](../../../../../thiirane.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) has undergone one inversion, so the new [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) bridge lies on the opposite face; the OH configuration is retained.** The [thiocyanate](../../../../../thiocyanate.md) [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) has entered the ring and the original ring [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) has left as [thiocyanate](../../../../../thiocyanate.md). This atom-labelled exchange mechanism explains how the same elemental formula can conceal [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) replacement; [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) labelling would be needed to test that exchange experimentally. A single [epimerization](../../../../../epimerization.md) at the OH [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) would give the wrong [stereochemical](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) outcome.
