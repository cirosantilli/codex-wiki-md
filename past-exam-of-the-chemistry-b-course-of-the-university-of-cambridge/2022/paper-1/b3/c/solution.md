<h1 id="b3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Direct [iodide](../../../../../iodide.md) attack at the terminal CH2–OMs would replace the [leaving group](../../../../../leaving-group.md) without disturbing the adjacent stereocentre, so it cannot by itself explain racemization. **[sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) participation produces an intermediate with two equivalent methylene paths.** Label the original S-adjacent methylene A, the stereogenic [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) C, and its CH2–OMs [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md) B. The [thioether](../../../../../thioether.md) [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) attacks B intramolecularly and expels [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md), forming a four-membered thietanium circuit S–A–C–B–S. The rest of the original six-membered ring remains as the other path between S and C.

In this bridged [sulfonium ion](../../../../../sulfonium-ion.md), A and B are both CH2 groups joining the same S and C centres. Exchanging them no longer changes the labelled molecular connectivity: the distinction that made C stereogenic has been removed. [iodide](../../../../../iodide.md) can open the strained intermediate by [backside attack](../../../../../backside-attack.md) at either A or B. Opening one S–CH2 bond gives one configuration of the six-membered product, while opening its equivalent partner exchanges which methylene is in the ring and which is the iodomethyl [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md), giving the opposite configuration.

**The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) iodinated in each opening is a methylene; racemization appears at the adjacent [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) through exchange of equivalent paths.** It is not inversion at a CH2 stereocentre, nor does the [mesylate](../../../../../mesylate.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) have to form a primary free [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md). An [achiral](../../../../../achirality.md) intermediate in an [achiral](../../../../../achirality.md) medium gives equal mirror-related outcomes. [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) participation alone in a simple terminal displacement would not provide this specific equivalent-path explanation.
