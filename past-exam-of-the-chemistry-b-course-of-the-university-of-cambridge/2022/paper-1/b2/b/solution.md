<h1 id="b2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Number the cyclopentenone [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $C_1$, its [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md) $C_2$ and $C_3$ with OR at $C_3$, and its saturated [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md) $C_4$ and $C_5$. The [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) has alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $X$, bearing Me and CO2Et, and beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $Y$, bearing the protected carbon substituent. **[lithium diisopropylamide](../../../../../lithium-diisopropylamide.md) generates the ordinary [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) at the saturated alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $C_5$.** The [acetonide](../../../../../acetonide.md) is retained.

The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) end of this [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) attacks [ester](../../../../../ester.md) beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) Y. Its pi system shifts through X onto the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), forming the first new bond $C_5{-}Y$ and an [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) at X. That [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) now attacks the original cyclopentenone beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) $C_3$ intramolecularly, giving the second new bond $X{-}C_3$. The original [enone](../../../../../enone.md) [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) move onto its [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), and [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of the final [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) completes the product. The OR [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md) is retained at the new bridgehead; it need not leave in either [Michael addition](../../../../../michael-addition.md).

The atom map provides a useful structural check. Between bridgeheads $C_3$ and $C_5$, the three paths are $C_3{-}C_2{-}C_1{-}C_5$, $C_3{-}C_4{-}C_5$, and $C_3{-}X{-}Y{-}C_5$. Their numbers of internal atoms are **2, 1 and 2**, establishing the [norbornane](../../../../../norbornane.md) skeleton. Both original [carbonyls](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) survive and both initial [alkenes](../../../../../alkene.md) are consumed. The two successive [conjugate additions](../../../../../conjugate-addition.md) give this atom map without requiring a concerted [cycloaddition](../../../../../cycloaddition.md).
