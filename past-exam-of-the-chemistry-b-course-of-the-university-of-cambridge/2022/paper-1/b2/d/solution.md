<h1 id="b2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\mathrm{Ar}=\mathrm{4{-}NO_2C_6H_4}$ and number the carbon substituent from [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md): $\mathrm{ArO{-}CH_2(1){-}CH_2(2){-}CH_2(3){-}CN}$. [sodium hydride](../../../../../sodium-hydride.md) removes a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) from C3, alpha to the nitrile, forming [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and a nitrile-stabilized [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) [anion](../../../../../anion.md). That [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the nitroaryl ipso [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) bearing O. The tether closes a five-membered cyclic Meisenheimer intermediate with sequence **C3–C2–C1–O–aryl ipso [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)**. The para [nitro group](../../../../../nitro-group.md) stabilizes the negative charge generated by temporary loss of [aromaticity](../../../../../aromaticity.md).

Rearomatization breaks the aryl–O bond, releasing the tether [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) as an [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md) while retaining the new aryl–C3 bond. [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) gives **$\mathrm{Ar{-}CH(CN){-}CH_2{-}CH_2OH}$**. This is a Truce–[Smiles rearrangement](../../../../../smiles-rearrangement.md): the aryl group migrates from [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The nitrile remains attached to its original side-chain [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md); the original [ether](../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) becomes the terminal [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). The substrate and product have identical atomic composition. Ordinary substitution at a saturated chain [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) would not produce this aryl–[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) bond or restore the terminal OH.
