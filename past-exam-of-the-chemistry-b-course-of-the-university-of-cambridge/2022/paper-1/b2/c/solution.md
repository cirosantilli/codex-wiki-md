<h1 id="b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The new bridge is made by an intramolecular [Michael addition](../../../../../michael-addition.md).** On the [cyclohexanone](../../../../../cyclohexanone.md) side, the methyl-bearing fused alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) is quaternary and cannot be deprotonated there. [triethylamine](../../../../../triethylamine.md) can instead generate a small equilibrium population of the [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) at the other, methylene alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Its [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the beta [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) of the [enone](../../../../../enone.md) in the seven-membered ring. The [enone](../../../../../enone.md) pi [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) move towards its [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and then onto [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), creating the new bridge and a second [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md).

[protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of that [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) at the former methyl-substituted [enone](../../../../../enone.md) alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) restores the left-hand [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md). The initial [enone](../../../../../enone.md) C=C has therefore become a saturated C–C bond while one new transannular C–C bond forms. Both [ketones](../../../../../ketone.md) are retained, and the initial two-ring framework becomes a three-ring framework without changing the total [index of hydrogen deficiency](../../../../../degree-of-unsaturation.md). The methyl originally at the fused quaternary centre does not migrate; the other methyl ends up on a protonated, formerly unsaturated [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). A strong-[base](../../../../../base-chemistry.md) concentration large enough for quantitative [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) generation is unnecessary for a favourable intramolecular trapping reaction, and neither an aldol [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md) nor a methyl shift is needed.
