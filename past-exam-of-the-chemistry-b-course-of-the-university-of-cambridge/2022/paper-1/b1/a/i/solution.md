↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a monocyclic, approximately planar, continuously conjugated pi system, [Hückel's rule](../../../../../../huckel-s-rule.md) predicts aromatic closed-shell filling for $4n+2$ pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), with $n=0,1,2,\ldots$. Every atom on the circuit must have an appropriately oriented orbital; counting [double bonds](../../../../../../double-bond.md) without checking [pi conjugation](../../../../../../pi-conjugation.md) is insufficient.

**The two outer [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) rings are clearly aromatic; the central [pyrone](../../../../../../pyrone.md) ring has a weaker, polarized six-pi aromatic contribution.** Each outer ring has the familiar six-[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) circuit. For the central ring, a useful charge-separated contributor moves the exocyclic [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) pi pair onto [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and donates one endocyclic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) into the ring. Its four fused [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) p centres contribute four [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and the ring-[oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) supplies two: a six-pi, pyrylium-like circuit, with positive charge on the ring [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and negative charge on the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). This does not make three independent [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) rings of equal aromatic stabilization; localized [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) bonding and the fused [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) circuits remain important contributors.

The three [oxygens](../../../../../../oxygen.md) play different roles. **The endocyclic [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) donates a p-type [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) to [pi conjugation](../../../../../../pi-conjugation.md). The [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) accepts pi density in the charge-separated contributor. The phenolic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) donates into the outer ring as a [substituent](../../../../../../substituent.md).** Its in-plane [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is not an extra pair to add to every ring's [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count. In particular, neither the external C=O pair nor both [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) on a ring [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) should be counted as independent extra ring [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Structural studies of [xanthone](../../../../../../xanthone.md) support a largely planar conjugated framework, while the division into strongly aromatic outer rings and a more polarized central ring is an electronic-structure interpretation.[1990, pp. 1725–1727, molecular geometry](../../../../../../structure-of-xanthone-by-shigeki-onuma-and-others.md)
