↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**B is 1,3-dibromo-2-hydroxyxanthone:** both [bromines](../../../../../../bromine.md) are on the hydroxy-substituted outer ring, at its two available positions ortho to OH. Define C2 as the OH-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); C1 lies between it and the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)-side ring junction, and C3 lies on its other side. These labels specify the substitution independently of the orientation of the printed drawing.

Phenolic [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) donation strongly activates its ortho and para positions towards [electrophilic substitution](../../../../../../electrophilic-substitution.md). The para position is a fused junction and has no replaceable [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), leaving C1 and C3 as the two directly activated sites. C1 also benefits from the conjugative influence of the ring [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Although other positions receive some activation from that [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), they lack the stronger local phenolic activation of the selected pair. Excess [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) can therefore replace both ortho [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). Replacing two [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) in $\mathrm{C_{13}H_8O_3}$ gives **$\mathrm{C_{13}H_6Br_2O_3}$**, consistent with the stated composition. Addition across an outer-ring [double bond](../../../../../../double-bond.md) would sacrifice [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md) and would not account for the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) loss.
