<h1 id="b1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The activated aromatic pi system polarizes [bromine](../../../../../../bromine.md) and attacks its [electrophilic](../../../../../../electrophilicity.md) end while Br–Br cleaves. This makes a C–Br bond at C1 or C3 and gives a [Wheland intermediate](../../../../../../arenium-ion.md). Donation from phenolic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) stabilizes contributors with positive charge at its attached ring [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). [acetate](../../../../../../acetate.md) or [bromide](../../../../../../bromide.md) then removes the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the attacked [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); the C–H bond [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) restore the aromatic pi system. Repetition at the other activated ortho site gives the dibrominated product.

**Each step replaces C–H by C–Br and restores [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md).** No [radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) initiator or benzylic abstraction is needed, and no [Lewis acid](../../../../../../lewis-acid.md) [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md) should be invented for this already activated phenolic substrate. The overall substitution balance is $\mathrm{ArH_2+2Br_2\rightarrow ArBr_2+2HBr}$.
