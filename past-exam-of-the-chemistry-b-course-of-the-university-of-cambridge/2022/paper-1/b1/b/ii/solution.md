<h1 id="b1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[amide ion](../../../../../../amide-ion.md) removes a methyl-side alpha [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) of [pinacolone](../../../../../../pinacolone.md). The C–H [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) form the [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) C=C bond as the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) pi pair moves onto [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md); [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) is formed. The tert-butyl-side alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) has no [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), fixing the ordinary [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) regiochemistry.

The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) end of D attacks the [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Move the C=O pi pair onto [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), then collapse the [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md) with [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) departure. This [nucleophilic acyl substitution](../../../../../../nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md) gives $\mathrm{tCO{-}CH_2{-}COt}$ and constructs the bond between the original [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) methylene and the incoming [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). **The observed product requires [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) acylation, rather than an O-acyl [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) [ester](../../../../../../ester.md).** A further equivalent of D removes the much more acidic central [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) of the new [1,3-diketone](../../../../../../1-3-diketone.md), generating its extensively delocalized [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) and returning that second D equivalent to [pinacolone](../../../../../../pinacolone.md). Aqueous [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) workup protonates the product [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md); keto–[enol](../../../../../../enol.md) equilibration gives the conjugated, internally stabilized by a [hydrogen bond](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) observed by [NMR](../../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md). The [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md) contributes its entire pivaloyl group; neither [tert-butyl group](../../../../../../tert-butyl-group.md) is lost.
