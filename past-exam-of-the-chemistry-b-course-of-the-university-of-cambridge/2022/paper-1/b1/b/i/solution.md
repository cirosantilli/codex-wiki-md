↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $t=\mathrm{C(CH_3)_3}$. **D is the [sodium](../../../../../../sodium.md) [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) $\mathrm{t{-}C(O^-){=}CH_2\ Na^+}$.** Its two resonances in the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md)-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) region belong to the two [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) of the terminal [methylene group](../../../../../../methylene-group.md). One is cis to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and the other trans to [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md), so they are not related by a [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) operation even though both belong to the same [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Replacing one or the other gives different alkene geometries, so they are [diastereotopic](../../../../../../diastereotopic.md). Their weak or unresolved [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) is consistent with the reported singlet descriptions. Without an additional spatial assignment, it is not justified to decide uniquely which numerical shift belongs to which of those two orientations. The nine-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) singlet is the [tert-butyl group](../../../../../../tert-butyl-group.md). The formula $\mathrm{C_6H_{11}ONa}$ follows from removing one alpha [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) from [pinacolone](../../../../../../pinacolone.md).

**E is the [enol](../../../../../../enol.md)-dominated [1,3-diketone](../../../../../../1-3-diketone.md) $\mathrm{tCO{-}CH_2{-}COt}$, observed as $\mathrm{tC(OH){=}CH{-}COt}$.** The very downfield broad signal belongs to the [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) OH in a six-membered intramolecular O–H···O chelate. The one-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) signal in the vinyl region is its central [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) CH; a predominantly diketo structure would instead require a two-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) central CH2 signal and no [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) OH. The eighteen-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) singlet accounts for both [tert-butyl groups](../../../../../../tert-butyl-group.md). Rapid exchange between the two degenerate [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) orientations can average their different local environments; accidental unresolved equality cannot be excluded by the supplied spectrum alone. Thus one should not infer a permanently symmetric single [enol](../../../../../../enol.md) [Lewis structure](../../../../../../lewis-structure.md) from that singlet. The total integration is $1+1+18=20$, agreeing with **$\mathrm{C_{11}H_{20}O_2}$**.
