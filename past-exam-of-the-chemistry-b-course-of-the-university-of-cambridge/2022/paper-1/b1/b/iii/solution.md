<h1 id="b1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**One equivalent supplies [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md); the other acts as a [base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) for the product.** The newly formed [1,3-diketone](../../../../../../1-3-diketone.md) is appreciably more acidic than the [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) from which D was prepared. [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) from its central CH2 to a second [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) equivalent is therefore favourable. Product-[enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) stabilization also helps drive the acyl-substitution sequence towards completion.

The second [enolate](../../../../../../enolate.md) is consequently consumed as [base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md), yielding [pinacolone](../../../../../../pinacolone.md), rather than supplying a second [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) fragment to E. [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) workup reverses the product [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md). Ignoring this [acid](../../../../../../acid-chemistry.md)–[base](../../../../../../base-chemistry.md) step would predict that a single equivalent of D is sufficient to consume every equivalent of [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md), despite the product immediately quenching part of the available [reagent](../../../../../../reagent.md).
