<h1 id="b4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A is the predicted major [diastereomer](../../../../../diastereomer.md).** At the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) adjacent to the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md), take phenyl as the large group, vinyl as medium, and [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) as small. There is no adjacent heteroatom donor that would force a five-membered chelate. The appropriate first model is therefore the non-chelating [Felkin-Anh model](../../../../../felkin-anh-model.md).

View along the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)-[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–stereocentre bond. Rotate to place the large alpha [phenyl group](../../../../../phenyl-group.md) approximately perpendicular to the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) plane. Of the two resulting approaches opposite that large group, the favourable [Bürgi-Dunitz trajectory](../../../../../burgi-dunitz-trajectory.md) passes nearer [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) rather than vinyl. Propyl transfer to the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of its [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) give the configuration labelled A: in the printed projection the new OH projects forward and the propyl bond backward, while the original vinyl bond remains backward. Rotation used to construct a [Newman projection](../../../../../newman-projection.md) must not be mistaken for inversion of the original stereocentre.

This is a relative-[selectivity](../../../../../selectivity.md) prediction, not a numerical product ratio. Simple attack on the face that happens to look less crowded in the initial flat drawing is unreliable because the relevant C–C single bond can rotate. B is the competing product from the less favourable approach beside vinyl. The model and its preference for the trajectory beside the small group are described in the institutional treatment.[pp. 1–2, section 1, nonpolar alpha substituents and the small-group trajectory](../../../../../felkin-anh-and-cram-chelate-by-university-of-windsor.md)
