<h1 id="b4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) activates the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) of the anomeric [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) bridge. Cleavage of its bond to the [anomeric carbon](../../../../../anomeric-carbon.md) releases that tether [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) as the terminal primary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md), while the other ring [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) stabilizes the developing anomeric [electrophile](../../../../../electrophile.md) by [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) donation. The resulting [oxocarbenium](../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md) description has a nearly planar reactive anomeric centre; it need not be drawn as a long-lived free [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md).

[methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) attacks that centre, giving a protonated methyl [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md), and [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) regenerates [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md). **The former bridge becomes CH2OH; the anomeric [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md) becomes OMe.** The other C–O bonds and existing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) configurations are retained. The ring can adopt a different chair once the bridge constraint is removed, so an apparent movement of [substituents](../../../../../substituent.md) in the projection does not imply inversion at all the other [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md).

The depicted anomeric OMe is axial in the product chair. This geometry allows an endocyclic [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) to align antiperiplanar to the exocyclic C–OMe bond, providing $n_O\rightarrow\sigma^*_{C-O}$ stabilization associated with the [anomeric effect](../../../../../anomeric-effect.md). Acidic conditions also permit cleavage and recapture at the anomeric centre, so selection is not simply backside displacement at an unchanged tetrahedral starting [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The [anomeric effect](../../../../../anomeric-effect.md) rationalizes the specified axial product; the scheme alone gives neither its numerical equilibrium population nor evidence that every other [anomer](../../../../../anomer.md) is absent.
