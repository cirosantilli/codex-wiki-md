<h1 id="b4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[triethylamine](../../../../../triethylamine.md) removes an alpha [proton](../../../../../proton.md) from [nitromethane](../../../../../nitromethane.md) to generate a [nitronate](../../../../../nitronate.md) population. Its [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) attacks the cyclic [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) of E, forming a new C–CH2NO2 bond while the C=O pi pair moves onto [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) by triethylammonium or another [proton](../../../../../proton.md) donor gives the tertiary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) F and regenerates [base](../../../../../base-chemistry.md). **This is a [Henry reaction](../../../../../henry-reaction.md), also called a [nitroaldol reaction](../../../../../henry-reaction.md):** the former [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) now bears both OH and CH2NO2.

The [nitro group](../../../../../nitro-group.md) remains attached to its original [nitromethane](../../../../../nitromethane.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md); attack through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) would give a different connectivity. The methyl [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) and the original [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) [substituents](../../../../../substituent.md) need not be cleaved or displaced. This addition produces the isolated connectivity without requiring [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md) to a [nitroalkene](../../../../../nitroalkene.md). The stated complex mixture is compatible with competing additions or [stereoisomers](../../../../../stereoisomer.md), but the scheme supplies no basis for claiming F is the unique or kinetically dominant product. Only its formation is being explained.
