<h1 id="b4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) attacks [electrophilic](../../../../../electrophilicity.md) [mercury](../../../../../mercury-element.md)(II), with [acetate](../../../../../acetate.md) departure as appropriate, to give a bridged [mercurinium ion](../../../../../mercurinium-ion.md) between the ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and the exocyclic methylene. That bridge is polarized: the more substituted ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) can better stabilize developing positive charge than the terminal CH2 [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). **[water](../../../../../water.md) attacks the ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)**, opening the [mercury](../../../../../mercury-element.md) bridge there, and [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives the tertiary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) with an exocyclic CH2–HgOAc [substituent](../../../../../substituent.md).

Subsequent [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md)-borohydride [demercuration](../../../../../demercuration.md) replaces the terminal C–Hg bond by C–H. Thus the original exocyclic CH2 becomes **Me**, and the new OH remains on the substituted ring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The result is Markovnikov hydration of the exocyclic [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md). There is no need for a freely equilibrating tertiary [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md), and no rearrangement of the [carbohydrate](../../../../../carbohydrate.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton or cleavage of the methyl [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) is required. The question does not require the [mercury](../../../../../mercury-element.md)-stage facial selection or a detailed [demercuration](../../../../../demercuration.md) mechanism.
