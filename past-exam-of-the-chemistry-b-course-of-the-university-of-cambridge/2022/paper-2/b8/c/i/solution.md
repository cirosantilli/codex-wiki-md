<h1 id="b8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Step 1 is [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) of Si–H to Co(I), giving Co(III)–H and Co(III)–SiR$_3$.** It cleaves the Si–H bond and forms two [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–ligand bonds, increasing the formal [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) by two. The phosphine dissociation shown before this numbered step makes room for the reaction; [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) binding and phosphine exchange are ligand-substitution events rather than [oxidative additions](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md).

**Step 2 is [migratory insertion](../../../../../../migratory-insertion.md) of the coordinated [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) into Co–H**, equivalently intramolecular [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) migration to the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md). One [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) gains H and the other gains the Co–C bond. Co remains formally Co(III); a two-electron [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) ligand plus a [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) is replaced by one alkyl ligand, reducing the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count by two before phosphine reassociation.

**Step 3 is C–Si [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md)** between the alkyl and [silyl ligands](../../../../../../silyl-ligand.md). It releases the hydrosilylated alkane and regenerates Co(I). The [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count for the drawn sequence is consistent: the one-PMe$_3$ [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) adduct is 16-electron; [oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) gives 18; insertion gives 16; PMe$_3$ association gives 18; [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) returns the 16-electron [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md). Because insertion occurs into Co–H, this is the conventional [Chalk-Harrod mechanism](../../../../../../chalk-harrod-mechanism.md).
