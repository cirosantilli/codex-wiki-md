<h1 id="b8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[oxidative addition](../../../../../../oxidative-addition.md) of an intact Si–H bond initially places **H and SiR$_3$ cis** in the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) coordination sphere; this is the local arrangement generated by addition of a [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md). [hydride](../../../../../../hydride.md) and [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) must also attain adjacent sites for intramolecular insertion, and the eventual alkyl and silyl groups must be cis for direct [reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md). Subsequent rearrangements should not be confused with the [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) of the bond-addition event itself.

For an alkyl-substituted terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md), insertion puts **Co on the terminal [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and H on the substituted [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)**, producing $\mathrm{Co{-}CH_2CH_2R}$. It avoids the more crowded secondary [metal](../../../../../../metal.md)–alkyl arrangement. Replacement of that Co–C bond by C–Si therefore gives **the linear product $\mathrm{RCH_2CH_2SiR_3}$**, an [Anti-Markovnikov addition](../../../../../../anti-markovnikov-addition.md) outcome. Hydrometallation is syn at the coordinated [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md), although this simple terminal substrate generally does not retain two substituents whose relative [stereochemistry](../../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) could diagnose the complete sequence.
