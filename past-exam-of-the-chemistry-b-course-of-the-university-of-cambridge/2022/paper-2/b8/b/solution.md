<h1 id="b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use ionic [electron counting](../../../../../electron-counting.md), counting each ordinary donor atom once and keeping ligand charge distinct from [denticity](../../../../../denticity.md). **A is 16-electron, B is 14-electron and C is 16-electron**, so none is an 18-electron complex under this convention.

A contains a modified, monoanionic PNP pincer: the exocyclic double bond and altered pyridine-ring bond pattern in the drawing matter. Its two phosphines and its central N donor provide six [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) altogether, while neutral N$_2$ provides two. Neutrality then gives Co(I), $d^8$, and $8+6+2=16$. Treating the modified pincer as a neutral intact [pyridine](../../../../../pyridine.md) ligand would incorrectly produce Co(0) and a 17-electron count. A four-coordinate approximately [square planar molecular geometry](../../../../../square-planar-molecular-geometry.md) in a $d^8$ complex can leave the strongly sigma-antibonding orbital empty and be stable at 16 [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). The underlying modified-pincer [chemistry](../../../../../chemistry-split.md) has been examined using structure, spectroscopy and electronic-structure calculations.[JACS 136, 9211–9224; modified PNP framework and dinitrogen derivative](../../../../../four-coordinate-cobalt-pincer-complexes-electronic-structure-studies-and-ligand-modification-by-homolytic-and-heterolytic-pathways-by-scott-p-semproni-and-others.md)

B has a neutral tertiary [amine](../../../../../amine.md) donor at the centre of its tripod and three anionic amido arms. Their combined charge is $-3$, so Re is Re(III), $d^4$. The four N donors give eight [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and N$_2$ gives two: $4+8+2=14$. Such an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) count is not inherently unexpected in an amido-supported complex: strong amido [pi donation](../../../../../pi-donation.md) and the imposed five-coordinate geometry alter the orbital energies, and the empty orbitals are not nine equivalent slots that must all be filled. Steric protection and [chelation](../../../../../chelation.md) also help stabilize this coordinatively restricted structure. The 14-electron figure uses the usual two-electron ionic count for each amido donor; pi bonding should not be appended to it inconsistently.

C has an anionic acyl [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) donor and a neutral phosphine in the [chelate](../../../../../chelate.md), together with two neutral PMe$_3$ donors. Thus Co(I), $d^8$, receives $2+2+4=8$ ligand [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), giving $8+8=16$. This is again a reasonable four-coordinate $d^8$ structure. The acyl C=O is part of a carbon-bound [acyl ligand](../../../../../acyl-ligand.md), not an additional free CO donor.
