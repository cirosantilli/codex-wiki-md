The [phosphorus](../../../../../../phosphorus.md) centre is approximately tetrahedral, with three P–C [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) and one short terminal P–O bond. Both $\mathrm{Me_3P{=}O}$ and $\mathrm{Me_3P^+{-}O^-}$ are useful Lewis contributors; **the bond is strongly polar and has additional multiple-bond character**. [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) lone-pair donation into suitable P–C antibonding combinations provides a description of the additional stabilization without requiring a substantial localized phosphorus-3d/oxygen-2p [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md). A combined diffraction and orbital study illustrates this sigma-bond/negative-hyperconjugation description.[§3.2 and Fig. 2a–c, orbital analysis of free Me3PO](../../../../../../computational-and-experimental-approaches-to-the-molecular-structure-of-the-hcl-adduct-of-me3po-by-andreas-orthaber-and-others.md)

Compared with the phosphate triester, this compound's methyl substituents are less [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) withdrawing. The comparison of the two terminal P–O stretching force constants and the appropriate experimental control is given with the triester below.
