<h1 id="b9/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A reasonable product is **sodium facial [trimetaphosphate](../../../../../trimetaphosphate.md)–[molybdenum](../../../../../molybdenum.md) tricarbonyl**, with the [anion](../../../../../anion.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{[Mo(CO)_3(\kappa^3O,O',O''\!\text{-}P_3O_9)]^{3-}},\qquad \text{gas}=3\mathrm{CO}.}
$$

One terminal O from each [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md) binds Mo; the three O donors occupy one octahedral face, while the three carbonyls occupy the opposite face. The ring bridges remain P–O–P bridges and are not replaced by Mo–P bonds. Mo remains Mo(0), $d^6$, and $6+3(2)+3(2)=18$ [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). Overall,

$$
\mathrm{Na_3P_3O_9+Mo(CO)_6\longrightarrow Na_3[Mo(CO)_3(P_3O_9)]+3CO}.
$$

[acetonitrile](../../../../../acetonitrile.md) can transiently replace CO during heating, but it is not required in the final six-coordinate proposal. The same [anion](../../../../../anion.md) has been structurally characterized with a different counterion: that primary study independently supports the facial O$_3$/CO$_3$ connectivity, without establishing identical solubility or yield for the sodium salt.[pp. 195–196, Scheme 2 and Fig. 1; §4.3 for Mo(CO)6 in refluxing acetonitrile](../../../../../synthesis-and-characterization-of-the-trimetaphosphate-molybdenum-tricarbonyl-anion-p3o9-mo-co-3-3-as-its-tris-bis-triphenylphosphine-iminium-salt-by-christopher-r-clough-and-others.md)
