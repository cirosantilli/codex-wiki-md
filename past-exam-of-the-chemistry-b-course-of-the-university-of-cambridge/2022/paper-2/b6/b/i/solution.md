<h1 id="b6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Neutral [pyridine](../../../../../../pyridine.md) contributes no [formal charge](../../../../../../formal-charge.md). If $n$ of the two [dioxolene ligands](../../../../../../dioxolene-ligand.md) are dianions, the combined ligand charge is $-2-n$, so the [cobalt](../../../../../../cobalt.md) [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) is $2+n$. The distinct charge assignments are therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{metal}&\text{two dioxolene ligands}\\ \hline
\mathrm{Co^{II}}&L^-+L^-\\
\mathrm{Co^{III}}&L^-+L^{2-}\\
\mathrm{Co^{IV}}&L^{2-}+L^{2-}
\end{array}}
$$

Interchanging the two ligand labels in the middle row does not make another charge combination. Co(IV) belongs in this unrestricted charge-balance list even though the subsequent magnetic analysis excludes it.
