<h1 id="b6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A Co(III) centre has $d^6$, and charge balance requires one closed-shell [catecholate ligand](../../../../../../catecholate-ligand.md) and one $S=1/2$ [semiquinonate ligand](../../../../../../semiquinonate-ligand.md). For low-spin Co(III), $t_{2g}^6(e_g^*)^0$, the [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) has $S=0$, giving an [independent-spin magnetic moment](../../../../../../independent-spin-magnetic-moment.md) of

$$
\mu_{\rm eff}/\mu_B=2\sqrt{0+\tfrac12(\tfrac12+1)}=\sqrt3=1.732.
$$

Thus **$A^1$ contains low-spin Co(III), $S_{\rm Co}=0$**, with its observed moment residing on the [free radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) ligand. High-spin Co(III), $t_{2g}^4(e_g^*)^2$, would have $S=2$ and give $\sqrt{27}=5.20$ under the stated independent-spin approximation, so it is inconsistent with this measurement.

For Co(II), $d^7$, charge balance instead requires two $S=1/2$ [semiquinonate ligands](../../../../../../semiquinonate-ligand.md). High-spin Co(II) has $t_{2g}^5(e_g^*)^2$ and $S=3/2$, so

$$
\mu_{\rm eff}/\mu_B=2\sqrt{\tfrac32\tfrac52+2\left(\tfrac12\tfrac32\right)}=\sqrt{21}=4.583.
$$

Therefore **$A^2$ contains high-spin Co(II), $S_{\rm Co}=3/2$**. Low-spin Co(II), $t_{2g}^6(e_g^*)^1$, would give $\sqrt9=3.00$ when the two ligand [spins](../../../../../../spin.md) are included. These four checks distinguish both [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) and [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) [spin](../../../../../../spin.md).

The larger [octahedral splitting](../../../../../../octahedral-splitting.md) at Co(III) exceeds the effective [pairing energy](../../../../../../pairing-energy.md) and favours a [low-spin complex](../../../../../../low-spin-complex.md); the smaller [octahedral splitting](../../../../../../octahedral-splitting.md) at Co(II) favours a [high-spin complex](../../../../../../high-spin-complex.md) with occupation of both $e_g^*$ orbitals before further pairing. Interconversion also transfers an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) between [cobalt](../../../../../../cobalt.md) and a [dioxolene ligand](../../../../../../dioxolene-ligand.md), so this is [valence tautomerism](../../../../../../valence-tautomerism.md), rather than a metal-only [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) change. Squared moments add here because the supplied model treats the centres as independent; adding the three [spins](../../../../../../spin.md) first would be a different, exchange-coupled model.
