<h1 id="b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The anionic [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) donor replaces iodide to give

$$
\boxed{A=\mathrm{CpFe(CO)_2(\kappa N\!\text{-}C_4H_4N)},\qquad \text{byproduct}=\mathrm{KI}.}
$$

Here Cp denotes $\eta^5$ cyclopentadienyl and $\kappa N$ specifies attachment through the ring [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) of the pyrrolyl [anion](../../../../../anion.md), $\mathrm{C_4H_4N^-}$. Fe(II), $d^6$, receives six [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) from Cp, four from the two CO ligands and two from an N-bound [pyrrolyl ligand](../../../../../pyrrolyl-ligand.md): $6+6+4+2=18$.

Two coupled CO stretching modes, approximately symmetric and antisymmetric combinations, account for the two strong carbonyl-region absorptions. The bent two-CO arrangement of this [piano-stool complex](../../../../../piano-stool-complex.md) permits both combinations to be infrared active. They are not evidence for two different chemical products.

Heating expels **two CO molecules** and changes the binding of the [pyrrolyl ligand](../../../../../pyrrolyl-ligand.md) from $\eta^1$ to $\eta^5$, giving the sandwich **[azaferrocene](../../../../../azaferrocene.md)**,

$$
\boxed{B=\mathrm{Fe(\eta^5-C_5H_5)(\eta^5-C_4H_4N)}.}
$$

The four [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) lost with CO are replaced by the four additional [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) donated on changing the [hapticity](../../../../../hapticity.md) of the [pyrrolyl ligand](../../../../../pyrrolyl-ligand.md). Thus $B$ is also Fe(II), $d^6$, and 18-electron: $6+6+6=18$. Each complex has one time-averaged Cp proton environment, two pairs of protons from the [pyrrolyl ligand](../../../../../pyrrolyl-ligand.md), one adjacent to N and one farther from N. Hence **three proton environments, with integrals $5:2:2$**, fit both structures. For [proton nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../proton-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md), three signals means three [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) environments; the individual resonances can still show coupling.
