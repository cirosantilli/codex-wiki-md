<h1 id="b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In ionic counting, two $\eta^5$ cyclopentadienyl [anions](../../../../../anion.md) donate $12$ [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). Therefore **[ferrocene](../../../../../ferrocene.md), [cobaltocene](../../../../../cobaltocene.md) and [nickelocene](../../../../../nickelocene.md) contain 18, 19 and 20 [valence electrons](../../../../../valence-electron.md)**, respectively: Fe(II) is $d^6$, Co(II) $d^7$ and Ni(II) $d^8$. [ferrocene](../../../../../ferrocene.md) fills the bonding and approximately nonbonding levels while leaving the next [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–ring antibonding pair empty. [cobaltocene](../../../../../cobaltocene.md) and [nickelocene](../../../../../nickelocene.md) add one and two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) to that antibonding set.

Increasing antibonding occupation lowers the net [metal](../../../../../metal.md)–ring [bond order](../../../../../bond-order.md). It therefore explains the simultaneous **increase in M–C distance and decrease in dissociation enthalpy from Fe to Co to Ni**. The trend does not follow the shrinking size of the isolated [metal](../../../../../metal.md) [ions](../../../../../ion.md); the orbital occupation is decisive here. The specified enthalpy separates the entire sandwich into a gaseous [metal](../../../../../metal.md) dication and two gaseous ring [anions](../../../../../anion.md). It measures that complete ionic dissociation process, not ten independent localized M–C bond energies, so dividing it by ten is not a justified bond-energy determination.
