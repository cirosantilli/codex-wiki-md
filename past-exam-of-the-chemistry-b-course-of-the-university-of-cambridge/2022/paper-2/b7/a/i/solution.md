<h1 id="b7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Four [chloride ligands](../../../../../../chloride-ligand.md) give Ti(IV), $d^0$. Ionic [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md) gives

$$
\boxed{N_e=0+4(2)=8.}
$$

**[tetrahedral molecular geometry](../../../../../../tetrahedral-molecular-geometry.md)** separates the four donors without placing them at $90^\circ$. A $d^0$ centre has no occupied [d orbitals](../../../../../../d-orbital.md) providing a square-planar stabilization; the 18-electron rule is not a requirement for this early-metal compound.
