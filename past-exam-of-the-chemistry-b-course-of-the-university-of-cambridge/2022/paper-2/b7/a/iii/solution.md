<h1 id="b7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Neutral CO ligands require formal Cr($-\mathrm{II}$), hence $d^8$. With five two-electron [carbonyl ligands](../../../../../../carbonyl-ligand.md),

$$
\boxed{N_e=8+5(2)=18.}
$$

A **[trigonal-bipyramidal molecular geometry](../../../../../../trigonal-bipyramidal-molecular-geometry.md)** is expected, analogous in [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count to $\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}$. Strong [pi-acceptor ligands](../../../../../../pi-acceptor-ligand.md) stabilize a low [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) through backbonding, and this five-coordinate structure accommodates the closed-shell 18-electron configuration. The negative [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) is a formal assignment; it does not mean that a free [chromium](../../../../../../chromium.md) dianion is trapped inside the molecule.
