<h1 id="b7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Six neutral [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) donors leave Cu(II), $d^9$, so

$$
\boxed{N_e=9+6(2)=21.}
$$

The donor skeleton has **[octahedral molecular geometry](../../../../../../octahedral-molecular-geometry.md) with a pronounced tetragonal Jahn–Teller distortion**, normally four shorter equatorial and two longer axial Cu–N distances. The $t_{2g}^6e_g^3$ occupation is orbitally degenerate in the ideal octahedral limit. An [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count above 18 is not a contradiction: [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) is primarily a sigma donor and the occupied metal-derived antibonding levels make this a weak-field coordination complex rather than a low-valent carbonyl closed shell.
