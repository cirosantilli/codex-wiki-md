# Old subspace of cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp form](cusp-form.md)

The old subspace is spanned by $h(tz)$ for [cusp forms](cusp-form.md) $h$ of level $M<N$, where $M\mid N$ and $t\mid N/M$. It is stable under the [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md), but need not have a basis of eigenvectors for every bad-prime operator. Its [Petersson inner product](petersson-inner-product.md) [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) is the [new subspace of cusp forms](new-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md).
