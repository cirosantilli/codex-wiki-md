# Unpolarized resonance spin factor

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

For unpolarized incident particles, the resonant cross section sums over the resonance's magnetic substates and averages over the incident spin states. This gives the displayed statistical weight under the conventional [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md) normalization. A spin-zero resonance formed from two spin-half particles has $g=1/4$, whereas a spin-one resonance has $g=3/4$. This counting is distinct from final-state [color charge](color-charge.md) multiplicities.
