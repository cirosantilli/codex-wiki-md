# DELLA protein

↑ **Parent:** [Plant hormone](plant-hormone.md)

A family of growth-regulatory proteins whose degradation is promoted by gibberellin signaling. Direct interactions with [phytochrome-interacting factors](phytochrome-interacting-factor.md) connect hormone status to light-regulated growth.
