# Principal series of a finite reductive group

↑ **Parent:** [Deligne-Lusztig induction](deligne-lusztig-induction.md)

For a rational [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) $B=TU$, the principal series is $\operatorname{Ind}_{B^F}^{G^F}\widetilde\theta$, with $\widetilde\theta$ inflated from a [linear character](linear-character.md) of $T^F$. The [Mackey restriction formula](mackey-restriction-formula.md) and rational [Bruhat decomposition](bruhat-decomposition.md) give its self-inner-product as the size of the Weyl stabilizer of $\theta$. It is irreducible precisely when that stabilizer is trivial.
