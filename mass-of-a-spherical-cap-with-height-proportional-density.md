# Mass of a spherical cap with height-proportional density

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical cap](spherical-cap.md)

For a solid [spherical cap](spherical-cap.md) $r^2+z^2\leq R^2$, $z\geq c$, where $0\leq c\leq R$ and the [mass density](density.md) is $\rho=kz$ with $k\geq0$, a [volume integral](volume-integral.md) in [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) gives

$$
M=\pi k\int_c^Rz(R^2-z^2)\,dz=\frac{\pi k}{4}(R^2-c^2)^2.
$$

Here the height is measured from the centre of the [sphere](sphere.md), rather than from the bottom of the cap. Specifying that origin matters for a nonuniform [mass density](density.md).
