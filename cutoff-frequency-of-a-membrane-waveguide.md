# Cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane waveguide](membrane-waveguide.md)

The [cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide](cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md) is the lowest [frequency](frequency.md) at which a specified transverse [normal mode](normal-mode.md) can propagate longitudinally. For fixed edges separated by $b$, $\omega_{c,n}=n\pi v/b$ and $f_{c,n}=\omega_{c,n}/(2\pi)$. Below cutoff the longitudinal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is imaginary and a bounded excitation is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md). Above it, $v_g=v\sqrt{1-(\omega_{c,n}/\omega)^2}$ and $v_pv_g=v^2$.
