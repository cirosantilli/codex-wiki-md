<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Bounded completeness of an arithmetic theory](../../../../../bounded-completeness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) means that every bounded [arithmetic](../../../../../arithmetic-split.md) [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) true in the standard [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md) is provable in $S$. If $S$ is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) and proves a false [bounded arithmetic sentence](../../../../../bounded-arithmetic-sentence.md) $B$, [bounded completeness of an arithmetic theory](../../../../../bounded-completeness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) also proves its true bounded [negation](../../../../../negation.md), a [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md). Conversely, if every bounded theorem is true, $S$ cannot be inconsistent, because inconsistency would prove the false [bounded arithmetic sentence](../../../../../bounded-arithmetic-sentence.md) $0=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Con}(S)\iff\Sigma_0\text{-soundness}.}
$$

If $S$ is [one-consistent](../../../../../one-consistency.md) and proves $\exists xB(x)$ with bounded matrix, that [existential sentence](../../../../../existential-sentence.md) cannot be false: otherwise every $\neg B(\bar n)$ would be a true [bounded arithmetic sentence](../../../../../bounded-arithmetic-sentence.md) and hence provable, contrary to [one-consistency](../../../../../one-consistency.md). Thus $S$ has [Sigma-1 soundness](../../../../../sigma-1-soundness.md).

Conversely, [Sigma-1 soundness](../../../../../sigma-1-soundness.md) excludes inconsistency. If $S\vdash\exists xB(x)$, it supplies a standard witness $n$ with $B(n)$ true. [Bounded completeness of an arithmetic theory](../../../../../bounded-completeness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) proves $B(\bar n)$, so [first-order consistency](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) forbids a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of $\neg B(\bar n)$. Hence there cannot simultaneously be refutations of all standard instances. Therefore

$$
\boxed{1\text{-consistency}\iff\Sigma_1\text{-soundness}.}
$$

Finite blocks of existential [logical variables](../../../../../logical-variable.md) are treated as finite [tuples](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) of witnesses, with the same argument.
