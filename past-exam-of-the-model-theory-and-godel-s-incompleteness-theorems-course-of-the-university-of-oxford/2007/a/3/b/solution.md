<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Abbreviate $P(\ulcorner A\urcorner)$ by $\Box A$. Since $S\vdash\neg X$, it proves $X\to G$. The theoremhood and distribution conditions give

$$
S\vdash\Box X\to\Box G.
$$

The fixed-point equation gives $S\vdash G\to(\Box G\to X)$, because $G$ implies $\neg\Box G$ and a false antecedent implies any conclusion. Applying theoremhood and distribution yields

$$
S\vdash\Box G\to\Box(\Box G\to X).
$$

[Positive introspection](../../../../../positive-introspection.md) gives $\Box G\to\Box\Box G$; distribution then gives $\Box G\to\Box X$. Thus $S\vdash\Box G\leftrightarrow\Box X$. Combining with $G\leftrightarrow\neg\Box G$ proves

$$
\boxed{S\vdash G\leftrightarrow\neg\Box X.}
$$

In particular, choosing a fixed logical [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) for $X$ identifies this [Gödel sentence](../../../../../godel-sentence.md) with the [formal consistency statement](../../../../../formal-consistency-statement.md) of $S$.
