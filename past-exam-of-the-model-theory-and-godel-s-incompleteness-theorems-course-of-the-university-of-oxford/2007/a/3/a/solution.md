<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\Box A$ for $P(\ulcorner A\urcorner)$. A standard [arithmetical provability predicate](../../../../../arithmetical-provability-predicate.md) obeys the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](../../../../../hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md):

$$
S\vdash A\Longrightarrow S\vdash\Box A,\qquad
S\vdash\Box(A\to B)\to(\Box A\to\Box B),\qquad
S\vdash\Box A\to\Box\Box A.
$$

The first condition is a rule about theorems; the latter two are statements proved within the [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md). When the terminology also includes external adequacy, require that $P(\bar n)$ be true in the standard [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md) exactly when the [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) with code $n$ has an $S$-proof.

For [Peano arithmetic](../../../../../peano-arithmetic.md), code finite strings, [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) and [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) sequences by [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md). [Formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) checking is effective and has an arithmetized bounded checking relation with its computation data. Existentially assert the existence of a correct [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) code and any required checking data. The resulting [predicate symbol](../../../../../predicate-symbol.md) is Sigma-1, and its standard truth means exactly that an actual finite PA [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) exists. Effective operations on [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) codes combine an implication [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) with an antecedent [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md), and turn a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) into a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) that its code is a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md); PA verifies those constructions. They give distribution and [positive introspection](../../../../../positive-introspection.md), while an actual theorem's finite [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) gives its internal provability assertion. This is the requested rough construction, with the three internal [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) properties distinguished from external agreement.
