<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

First establish the transfer of provability requested in the hint. The propositional theorem $X\to(\neg G\to X)$ and the [derivability conditions](../../../../../hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md) give

$$
S\vdash\Box X\to\Box(\neg G\to X).
$$

The supplied arithmetized deduction equivalence identifies the right-hand provability assertion, within $S^*$, with $\Box^*X$. Hence

$$
S^*\vdash\Box X\to\Box^*X.
$$

Choose a fixed [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) $\bot$ for $X$. Part (b) gives $S\vdash G\leftrightarrow\neg\Box\bot$, so adjoining $\neg G$ gives $S^*\vdash\Box\bot$. The transfer implication now gives $S^*\vdash\Box^*\bot$.

Apply (b) inside $S^*$ to its own [Gödel sentence](../../../../../godel-sentence.md): it proves $G^*\leftrightarrow\neg\Box^*\bot$. The last provability assertion therefore entails

$$
\boxed{S^*\vdash\neg G^*.}
$$

Thus the extension proves the [negation](../../../../../negation.md) of its own [Gödel sentence](../../../../../godel-sentence.md) as well. When $S$ is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md), (c) says this phenomenon occurs in a [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) extension; the extension asserts an internal [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) without there being an actual standard [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md).
