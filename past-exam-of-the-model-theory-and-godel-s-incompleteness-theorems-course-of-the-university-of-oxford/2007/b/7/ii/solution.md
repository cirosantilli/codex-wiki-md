<h1 id="b/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\vec a\in A^n$, with $A$ finite, and put $T=\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal A)$. There are only finitely many competing [tuples](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) $\vec b\in A^n$. For each whose [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) differs from that of $\vec a$, choose a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\phi_{\vec b}$ true at $\vec a$ and false at $\vec b$. Such a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) exists by the inequality of their [complete types](../../../../../complete-type.md), negating a distinguishing [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) if necessary.

Let $\theta$ be the [conjunction](../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) of this finite list, or [logical truth](../../../../../logical-truth.md) if the list is empty. It is true at $\vec a$, and every [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) satisfying it has the same [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) as $\vec a$. For each $\psi\in\operatorname{tp}(\vec a)$, the [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md)

$$
\forall\vec x\,(\theta(\vec x)\to\psi(\vec x))
$$

is therefore true in $\mathcal A$ and belongs to $T$. The [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\theta$ is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) with $T$ because it is realized at $\vec a$. It is consequently a [principal formula of a type](../../../../../principal-formula-of-a-type.md) of the [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md), proving **every [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) realized in a finite [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) is principal**. Finiteness of the [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md), rather than finiteness of the [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md), is what makes the [conjunction](../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) finite.
