<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An $n$-type over a [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $T$ is a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) in the displayed $n$ [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md) [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) with $T$. A [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) chooses exactly one of $\phi$ and $\neg\phi$ for each such [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md). Equivalently it is a [maximal consistent](../../../../../maximal-consistent-set-of-first-order-sentences.md) such [set](../../../../../set-split.md). For parameter-free [partial types](../../../../../partial-type.md) the [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) use the original [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md); [partial types](../../../../../partial-type.md) over parameters use additional names for those parameters.

A [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) $p(\vec x)$ is principal if some [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\theta(\vec x)$ [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) with $T$ implies every member of $p$ modulo $T$:

$$
T\models\forall\vec x\,(\theta(\vec x)\to\phi(\vec x))\qquad(\phi\in p).
$$

Such a $\theta$ is a [principal formula of a type](../../../../../principal-formula-of-a-type.md) of the [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md). For a [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) it isolates that [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md), so [principal complete types](../../../../../principal-complete-type.md) are exactly [isolated types](../../../../../isolated-type.md).

For a [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) $\vec a$ in a [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) $\mathcal A$, its parameter-free [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tp}^{\mathcal A}(\vec a)=
\{\phi(\vec x):\mathcal A\models\phi(\vec a)\}.}
$$

It is a [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) over $\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal A)$ because the [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) decides the truth of every [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) at that [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md).
