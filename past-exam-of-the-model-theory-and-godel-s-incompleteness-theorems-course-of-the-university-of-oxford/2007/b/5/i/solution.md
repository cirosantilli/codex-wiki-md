<h1 id="b/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [canonical first-order structure](../../../../../canonical-first-order-structure.md) is generated by its names: every element is the value of a [closed term](../../../../../closed-term.md). In the function-free setting permitted here, this means every element is the interpretation of a [constant symbol](../../../../../constant-symbol.md). Equivalently, the natural map from [closed terms](../../../../../closed-term.md) modulo [logical equality](../../../../../logical-equality.md) in the [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) onto its carrier is surjective. When canonical is defined specifically using constant names, the same definition and all the arguments below apply in this function-free setting.

A [full set of first-order sentences](../../../../../full-set-of-first-order-sentences.md) has the witness property in its own [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md): whenever it proves an [existential sentence](../../../../../existential-sentence.md) $\exists x\phi(x)$, some [closed term](../../../../../closed-term.md) $t$ satisfies that the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) proves $\phi(t)$. In a deductively closed [set](../../../../../set-split.md), one can equivalently state this using membership of the [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md). In the function-free setting the witness [first-order term](../../../../../first-order-term.md) is a [constant symbol](../../../../../constant-symbol.md). This is the [Henkin witness property](../../../../../henkin-witness-property.md); fullness does not by itself replace the separate requirements of [first-order consistency](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) and completeness.
