<h1 id="b/8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Here $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A$ means a [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md), so the smaller carrier contains all named constants, is closed under every [function symbol](../../../../../function-symbol.md), and has each relation restricted from the larger [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md). [First-order term](../../../../../first-order-term.md) evaluation on [tuples](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) from $B$ agrees in the two [first-order structures](../../../../../first-order-structure.md). Consequently [atomic formulas](../../../../../atomic-formula.md) agree, and [induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) on Boolean connectives makes every [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) agree as well.

Write an arbitrary [universal sentence](../../../../../universal-sentence.md) of $T$ as $\forall\vec x\,\theta(\vec x)$ with quantifier-free matrix. Every [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) from $B$ is also a [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) from $A$, so $\theta$ holds at that [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) in $\mathcal A$. Quantifier-free agreement transfers it to $\mathcal B$. Since the [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) was arbitrary, $\mathcal B$ satisfies the [universal sentence](../../../../../universal-sentence.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models T,\ \mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A
\Longrightarrow\mathcal B\models T.}
$$

An arbitrary [subset](../../../../../subset.md) without the [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) operations and relation restrictions would not be the hypothesis used by this [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md).
