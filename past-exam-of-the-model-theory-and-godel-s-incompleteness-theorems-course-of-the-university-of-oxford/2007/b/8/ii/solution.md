<h1 id="b/8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of closed-term values is nonempty because the [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md) has a constant. It contains the value of every constant. If $f$ is an $r$-ary [function symbol](../../../../../function-symbol.md) and $t_1,\ldots,t_r$ are [closed terms](../../../../../closed-term.md), then

$$
f^{\mathcal A}(t_1^{\mathcal A},\ldots,t_r^{\mathcal A})
=(f(t_1,\ldots,t_r))^{\mathcal A},
$$

which is another closed-term value. Hence the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) is closed under every operation. Restrict the operations and relations of $\mathcal A$ to it; this defines a [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $\mathcal B$ whose carrier is exactly the specified [set](../../../../../set-split.md).

Part (i) now applies to the universal [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md), giving

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A,\qquad\mathcal B\models T.}
$$

It is the [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) generated by the [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md)'s constants, often called the [ground-term substructure](../../../../../ground-term-substructure.md). The source's constant-symbol assumption ensures its nonemptiness, even when no other elements can be generated.
