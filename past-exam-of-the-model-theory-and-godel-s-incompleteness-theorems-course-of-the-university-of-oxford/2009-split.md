# 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part C paper covers Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems in section A and [Model theory](../model-theory-split.md) in section B, cross-listed for Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Mathematics and Philosophy. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive contains the complete editable five-page transcription. Bounded official-source searches found no advertised answers for this exact paper. A2(c) is answered with a counterexample to its literal semantic hypothesis and a proof of the intended canonical case. The vector-space comparison keeps infinitude of the underlying [set](../set-split.md) distinct from infinite [dimension](../dimension-vector-space.md).
