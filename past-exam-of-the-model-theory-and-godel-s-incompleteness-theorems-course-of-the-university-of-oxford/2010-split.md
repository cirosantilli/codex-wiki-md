# 2010

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source release: [Oxford examination archive release thread](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), Part C bundle pages 300–304. This is one combined paper covering both courses: section A concerns [Gödel second incompleteness theorem](../godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md) and [provability logic](../provability-logic.md), and section B concerns [model theory](../model-theory-split.md). The cover cross-lists Mathematics and Mathematics and Philosophy, both Part C, Thursday 10 June 2010, 2.30pm–5.30pm.

Answer availability checked on 28 October 2026: bounded official Oxford searches by exact year, paper label and both subjects found no advertised exact-paper answers. The [official Part C syllabus for the 2010 examination](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/system/files/attachments/PartC%202009-10.pdf) confirms the paired course identity. No later-year answer-release policy was assumed applicable.
