↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** [Universal sentences](../../../../../../universal-sentence.md) are preserved under [substructures](../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md). Every infinite $K$-vector space has its zero subspace as an $L$-substructure, since zero is named and every [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) preserves it. This one-element structure is not infinite and is not a [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$. Thus the [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) class is not closed under [substructures](../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) and has no universal axiomatization, even relative to the vector-space [axioms](../../../../../../axiom.md).
