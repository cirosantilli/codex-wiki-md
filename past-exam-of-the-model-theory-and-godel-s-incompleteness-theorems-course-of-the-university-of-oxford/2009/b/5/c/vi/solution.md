<h1 id="b/5/c/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Every [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) has a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) and is classified up to [linear isomorphism](../../../../../../linear-isomorphism.md) by its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Over a [finite field](../../../../../../finite-field.md), a vector is a finite [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) [Elements](../../../../../../euclid-s-elements.md). If the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) has infinite [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\lambda$, the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) of finite such combinations has [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\lambda$: there are at least $\lambda$ [basis vectors](../../../../../../basis-vector.md) and at most a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of finite powers of $\lambda$, each of size $\lambda$. Thus the underlying [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) of an infinite space equals its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).

Any two [models](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\kappa\ge\aleph_0$ therefore have [bases](../../../../../../basis.md) of [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\kappa$. A [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) between their [bases](../../../../../../basis.md) extends uniquely to an $L$-isomorphism. A space with that [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) size exists, giving the required existence clause as well. Hence **$T$ is categorical in every infinite cardinal**.
