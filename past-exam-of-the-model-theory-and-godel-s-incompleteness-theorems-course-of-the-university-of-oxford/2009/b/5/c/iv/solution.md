<h1 id="b/5/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**No.** If a finite [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) $\phi$ axiomatized the infinite spaces, it would follow from the vector-space [axioms](../../../../../../axiom.md) together with all the $D_n$. Compactness would make it follow from finitely many of those [axioms](../../../../../../axiom.md) and [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) requirements. Let $N$ be at least every [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) mentioned there. The finite-dimensional space $K^N$ satisfies all vector-space [axioms](../../../../../../axiom.md) and those finitely many $D_n$, hence would satisfy $\phi$. But it is a [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md) and so cannot be a [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of the purported axiomatization. This [contradiction](../../../../../../contradiction.md) proves failure of [finite axiomatizability](../../../../../../finite-axiomatizability.md), including [finite axiomatizability](../../../../../../finite-axiomatizability.md) relative to [vector spaces](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md).
