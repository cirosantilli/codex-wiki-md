<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $F_n$ be the formulas whose [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md) are among $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. Define $\phi\,E_n(T)\,\psi$ when $T\models\forall\bar x(\phi\leftrightarrow\psi)$. The [n-variable Lindenbaum algebra](../../../../../n-variable-lindenbaum-algebra.md) quotient is a [Boolean algebra](../../../../../boolean-algebra.md), with meet, join and complement induced by [conjunction](../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md), disjunction and [negation](../../../../../negation.md); its zero and one are [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) and truth classes. Its order is $[\phi]\le[\psi]$ exactly when $T\models\forall\bar x(\phi\to\psi)$.

The [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](../../../../../ryll-nardzewski-theorem.md) states that for a [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) with infinite [models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) language, the following are equivalent: the [theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) is countably categorical; for every finite $n$ there are only finitely many complete $n$-types over the empty [set](../../../../../set-split.md); and every [Boolean algebra](../../../../../boolean-algebra.md) $F_n/E_n(T)$ is finite. Equivalently each such type is isolated. The countable-language and completeness hypotheses are essential parts of this formulation.
