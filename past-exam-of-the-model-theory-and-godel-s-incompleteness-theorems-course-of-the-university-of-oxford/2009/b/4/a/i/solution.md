<h1 id="b/4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An $L$-embedding $\pi:A\to B$ is an [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) map preserving the interpretations of all constants and functions and preserving and reflecting every relation. Explicitly $\pi(c^A)=c^B$, $\pi(f^A(\bar a))=f^B(\pi\bar a)$, and $A\models R(\bar a)$ if and only if $B\models R(\pi\bar a)$. Injectivity handles [logical equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md). Equivalently it is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../structure-isomorphism.md) onto an $L$-substructure of $B$, or a map preserving the truth of all [quantifier-free formulas](../../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md). Preserving relations in only one direction would instead give a weaker homomorphism notion, not an [embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md).
