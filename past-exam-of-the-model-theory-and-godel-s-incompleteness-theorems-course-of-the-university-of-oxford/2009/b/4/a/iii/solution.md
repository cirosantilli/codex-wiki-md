↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Tarski-Vaught test](../../../../../../tarski-vaught-test.md) says that a [substructure](../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $A\subseteq B$ is elementary exactly when for every formula $\phi(x,\bar y)$ and tuple $\bar a\in A$, if $B\models\exists x\phi(x,\bar a)$, some $b\in A$ satisfies $B\models\phi(b,\bar a)$. Thus **every existential assertion with parameters from $A$ that has a witness in $B$ already has a witness in $A$**. The matrix may contain [quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md); restricting it to [quantifier-free formulas](../../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) would not state the full test.
