<h1 id="b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Existential sentences](../../../../../existential-sentence.md) are preserved under extensions: witnesses remain present and quantifier-free truth is unchanged by an [embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md). Therefore any existential axiomatization implies the specified extension-closure property.

For [existential axiomatization from extension closure](../../../../../existential-axiomatization-from-extension-closure.md), let $E$ be all existential consequences of $T$, and suppose $A\models E$. We claim that $T\cup\operatorname{Th}_\forall(A)$ is satisfiable. Otherwise compactness supplies finitely many [universal sentences](../../../../../universal-sentence.md) $\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_m$ true in $A$ such that $T\models\neg(\theta_1\land\cdots\land\theta_m)$. The finite [conjunction](../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) is universal after renaming [bound variables](../../../../../bound-variable.md), so its [negation](../../../../../negation.md) is existential. It belongs to $E$ but is false in $A$, a [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md).

The preceding universal-theory [embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) result now yields $B\models T$ and $A^*\equiv A$ with $B\subseteq A^*$. By the assumed extension closure, $A^*\models T$; [elementary equivalence](../../../../../elementary-equivalence.md) implies $A\models T$. Thus $E$ axiomatizes exactly the [models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$, proving **existential axiomatizability is equivalent to closure under extensions**. This includes the [inconsistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) case, whose empty [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) class is axiomatized by an existential [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md).
