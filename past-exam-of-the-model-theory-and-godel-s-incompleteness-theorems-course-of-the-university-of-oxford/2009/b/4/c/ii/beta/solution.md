<h1 id="b/4/c/ii/beta/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Β](../beta.md)

If $B\models T$ and $B\subseteq A^*\equiv A$, every [universal sentence](../../../../../../../universal-sentence.md) of $A$ holds in $A^*$ and is preserved by passage to the [substructure](../../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $B$. Thus $B$ witnesses satisfiability of the joint [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md).

For [universal theory and substructures of elementarily equivalent models](../../../../../../../universal-theory-and-substructures-of-elementarily-equivalent-models.md), conversely let $B$ satisfy $T$ together with the universal [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) of $A$. Every [existential sentence](../../../../../../../existential-sentence.md) true in $B$ must be true in $A$: if an existential $\sigma$ were false in $A$, its universal [negation](../../../../../../../negation.md) would belong to $\operatorname{Th}_\forall(A)$ and hold in $B$, a [contradiction](../../../../../../../contradiction.md). Hence $\operatorname{Th}_\exists(B)\subseteq\operatorname{Th}(A)$. In particular $\operatorname{Th}(A)\cup\operatorname{Th}_\exists(B)$ is satisfiable, with [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) $A$. Apply the preceding [embedding](../../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) result to the [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $\operatorname{Th}(A)$ and structure $B$. It supplies a structure $A^*\models\operatorname{Th}(A)$ containing $B$. Therefore **$A^*\equiv A$ and $B\subseteq A^*$**, as required. [Elementary equivalence](../../../../../../../elementary-equivalence.md) here does not assert that $A$ itself contains a copy of $B$.
