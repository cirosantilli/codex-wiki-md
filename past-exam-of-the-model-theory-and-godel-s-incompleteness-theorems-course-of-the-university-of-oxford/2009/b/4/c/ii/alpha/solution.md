<h1 id="b/4/c/ii/alpha/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

If a [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) $B$ of $T$ contains $A$ as a [substructure](../../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md), every [existential sentence](../../../../../../../existential-sentence.md) true in $A$ remains true in $B$: use the same witness tuple and preservation of quantifier-free truth. Hence $B$ satisfies the required joint [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md).

For the [existential-theory embedding criterion](../../../../../../../existential-theory-embedding-criterion.md), conversely suppose that joint [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) has a [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md). Expand the language by a constant $c_a$ for each $a\in A$, and add the atomic and negated [atomic diagram](../../../../../../../diagram-mathematical-logic.md) $D(A)$ to $T$. Any finite collection of its literals is a quantifier-free [conjunction](../../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) $\theta(\bar c)$ true of the corresponding tuple in $A$. Replacing those named constants by variables and existentially closing gives a sentence in $\operatorname{Th}_\exists(A)$. A [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of the assumed joint [theory](../../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) can supply its witnesses, and hence an expansion satisfying those finitely many diagram requirements and any finite [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $T$.

Compactness therefore gives a [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T+D(A)$. Its named [Elements](../../../../../../../euclid-s-elements.md) define an [embedding](../../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) of $A$ into its $L$-reduct: inequalities ensure injectivity, and all function and relation literals ensure preservation and [reflection](../../../../../../../full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md). Replace the ambient [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) by an isomorphic copy identifying this image with $A$ itself. Thus **a [model](../../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$ containing $A$ exists**. The diagram step explains how parameter-free existential information nevertheless yields an [embedding](../../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) of the whole structure.
