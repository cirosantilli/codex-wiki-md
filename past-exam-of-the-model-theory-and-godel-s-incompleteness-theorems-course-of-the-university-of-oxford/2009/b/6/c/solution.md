<h1 id="b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An $\aleph_0$-saturated [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) realizes every [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) in finitely many variables over every finite parameter [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of itself. It suffices equivalently to require one-variable types and then realize finite tuples successively. The definition puts no countability restriction on the size of the [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md).

For any [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) $M$, collect all [complete types](../../../../../complete-type.md) over its finite parameter [sets](../../../../../set-split.md). Add to its [elementary diagram](../../../../../elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md), for each such type, a new tuple of constants satisfying all that type's formulas. Any finite selection of these requirements is realizable in $M$: each finite type fragment has its existential closure true there, since it is [consistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) with the [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) of the corresponding named finite tuple. Distinct new tuples have no additional mutual restrictions. Compactness gives an [elementary extension](../../../../../elementary-extension.md) realizing all these types simultaneously.

Start from any [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) $M_0\models T$ and apply this construction repeatedly to obtain $M_0\preccurlyeq M_1\preccurlyeq\cdots$, with each successor realizing all types over finite [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of its predecessor. The [elementary-chain theorem](../../../../../elementary-chain-theorem.md) makes $M_\omega=\bigcup_nM_n$ a [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$ with every stage elementary in it. A finite parameter [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $A\subseteq M_\omega$ lies in some $M_n$. Every type over $A$ [consistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) with $M_\omega$ is [consistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) with $M_n$, since the expanded complete [theories](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) at those parameters agree by elementarity. It is realized in $M_{n+1}$, hence in the [union](../../../../../set-union.md). Therefore **an $\aleph_0$-saturated [model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) always exists**, possibly with uncountable [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md).
