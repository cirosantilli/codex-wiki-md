<h1 id="b/6/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

There are $2^{\aleph_0}$ distinct irrational cuts of the named rational order. Each defines a [consistent](../../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) complete one-type as above, already over the empty additional parameter [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), since the rational constants are part of the language. Different cuts cannot be realized by the same element: a named rational between two cuts gives contradictory required inequalities.

An $\aleph_0$-saturated [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) must realize every one of these types, so it has at least $2^{\aleph_0}$ [Elements](../../../../../../euclid-s-elements.md). Consequently **there is no [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) $\aleph_0$-saturated [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$**. This does not contradict existence of a [saturated model](../../../../../../saturated-model.md) from the preceding construction, whose size was unrestricted, or existence of the [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) [prime model](../../../../../../prime-model.md), whose requirement is different. No continuum-hypothesis assumption is involved.
