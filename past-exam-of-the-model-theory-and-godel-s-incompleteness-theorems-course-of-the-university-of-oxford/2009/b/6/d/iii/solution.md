↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes: $Q^+$ is prime.** In any [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of its [complete diagram](../../../../../../elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md), map each rational to the interpretation of its named constant. The diagram ensures this is an [embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md), and [quantifier elimination for dense linear orders without endpoints](../../../../../../quantifier-elimination-for-dense-linear-orders-without-endpoints.md) makes it elementary. Alternatively every finite tuple of $Q^+$ has its [complete type](../../../../../../complete-type.md) isolated by the [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) equating its coordinates to their named rational constants. Thus it is a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) [atomic model](../../../../../../atomic-model.md), and the earlier prime-model equivalence applies. A [prime model](../../../../../../prime-model.md) may omit many types; primeness is not saturation.
