↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [unrestricted consistency self-strengthening is inconsistent](../../../../../unrestricted-consistency-self-strengthening-is-inconsistent.md), again take $A=S+\neg\operatorname{Con}_S$. In $S^{++}$ the [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md) $\operatorname{Con}_S$ and the preceding implication prove $\operatorname{Con}_A$. The additional rule then proves $\operatorname{Con}_{A+\operatorname{Con}_A}$.

However [consistency](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) of an extension implies [consistency](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) of its subtheory by the [formalized inclusion of proofs](../../../../../monotonicity-of-inconsistency.md): $S\vdash\operatorname{Con}_A\to\operatorname{Con}_S$. Thus the ordinary [theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $A+\operatorname{Con}_A$ contains $\neg\operatorname{Con}_S$ and derives $\operatorname{Con}_S$, so it has a finite [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) proof. Checking that concrete proof in arithmetic gives $S\vdash\neg\operatorname{Con}_{A+\operatorname{Con}_A}$. This theorem is also present in $S^{++}$, contradicting the result of the new rule. Therefore **$S^{++}$ is [inconsistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md)**.

The error in the added rule is its unrestricted internal use on arbitrary arithmetically presented systems, including systems containing false [consistency](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) assertions. A true external [consistency](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) progression for a specified sound system is not this unrestricted [inference rule](../../../../../inference-rule.md).
