<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [fixed point of boxed negated provability](../../../../../fixed-point-of-boxed-negated-provability.md) gives, in fact, that [GL](../../../../../godel-lob-logic.md) proves $F\leftrightarrow\Box\bot$. Here is a direct derivation. Since $F$ is provably equivalent to a boxed formula, [positive introspection](../../../../../positive-introspection.md) and [necessitation](../../../../../necessitation-rule.md) of that equivalence give $F\to\Box F$. Another introspection step gives $F\to\Box\Box F$. The stipulated [fixed point](../../../../../logical-fixed-point.md) also gives $F\to\Box\neg\Box F$. Combining these two boxed contradictories yields $F\to\Box\bot$. Conversely $\Box\bot\to\Box\neg\Box F$ follows by normal boxed explosion, and the [fixed point](../../../../../logical-fixed-point.md) then yields $\Box\bot\to F$.

[Arithmetical soundness of GL](../../../../../arithmetical-soundness-of-gl.md), proved by translating [distribution](../../../../../normal-modal-distribution-axiom.md) into the second derivability condition, [necessitation](../../../../../necessitation-rule.md) into the first, and Löb's [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md) into its internal arithmetic theorem, now gives

$$
\mathrm{PA}\vdash F^*\leftrightarrow\operatorname{Pr}_{\mathrm{PA}}(\ulcorner\bot\urcorner).
$$

Any remaining propositional parameters may be assigned arbitrary arithmetic sentences; this equivalence is uniform in those assignments. If PA is [one-consistent](../../../../../one-consistency.md), it is [consistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) and $\Sigma_1$ sound. Its standard [provability predicate](../../../../../arithmetical-provability-predicate.md) for a [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) is then a false $\Sigma_1$ sentence and cannot be a theorem. Hence **$F^*$ is not provable in PA**. Moreover its [negation](../../../../../negation.md) is equivalent to PA's [consistency](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) statement and is unprovable by the [second incompleteness theorem](../../../../../godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md). Thus this [fixed point](../../../../../logical-fixed-point.md) is, under the stated hypothesis, undecidable rather than an unboxed [reflection](../../../../../full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md) principle.
