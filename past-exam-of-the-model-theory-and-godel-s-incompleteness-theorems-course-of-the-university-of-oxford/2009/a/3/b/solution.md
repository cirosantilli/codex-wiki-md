<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose $S+A$ proves every specified [reflection](../../../../../full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md) instance. Use only the instance with $X=\neg A$. The [deduction theorem](../../../../../deduction-theorem-for-first-order-logic.md) gives

$$
S\vdash A\to(\Box\neg A\to\neg A).
$$

[Classical propositional reasoning](../../../../../classical-propositional-logic.md) turns this into $S\vdash\Box\neg A\to\neg A$: if $\neg A$ already holds the implication is true, while otherwise $A$ holds and the displayed theorem supplies it. [Löb's theorem](../../../../../lob-s-theorem.md) now yields $S\vdash\neg A$, making $S+A$ [inconsistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md). Therefore **no sentence [consistent](../../../../../consistent-first-order-theory.md) with $S$ can supply all the local [reflection](../../../../../full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md) instances in a one-sentence extension**. The proof uses just one well-chosen instance, and never applies [necessitation](../../../../../necessitation-rule.md) to a derivation with the extra assumption still open.
