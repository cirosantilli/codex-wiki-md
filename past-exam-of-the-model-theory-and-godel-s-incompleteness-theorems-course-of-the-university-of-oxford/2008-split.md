# 2008

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part C paper is cross-listed for Mathematics and Mathematics and Philosophy. Section A concerns [Gödel second incompleteness theorem](../godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md) and [provability logic](../provability-logic.md); section B concerns [model theory](../model-theory-split.md). [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive contains a complete editable six-page transcription. Bounded official-source searches found no advertised solutions to this exact paper. All 37 source leaves are answered below. The solutions state the proof-numbering convention in A2(d), the naming convention for diagram inclusions in B6(ii), and the literal printed stability definition in B8(ii)(e).
