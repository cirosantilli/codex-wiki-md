<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Prime elements](../../../../../prime-element.md) are first-order definable in the [ring](../../../../../ring.md) [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md). For instance let $\operatorname{Unit}(u)$ mean $\exists v\ (uv=1)$ and use

$$
\operatorname{Prime}(x)\equiv x\ne0\wedge\neg\operatorname{Unit}(x)\wedge
\forall u\forall v\ (uv=x\to\operatorname{Unit}(u)\vee\operatorname{Unit}(v)).
$$

In the [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) [ring](../../../../../ring.md) this defines [prime elements](../../../../../prime-element.md) up to the harmless choice of sign. Expand the [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md) by names for every [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) and a new constant $c$. Take the [elementary diagram](../../../../../elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md) of $\mathcal Z$ together with

$$
\operatorname{Prime}(c),\qquad c\ne\bar a\quad(a\in\mathbb Z).
$$

Every finite [subset](../../../../../subset.md) is [satisfiable](../../../../../satisfiable-first-order-theory.md) in $\mathcal Z$: choose a positive prime [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) outside the finitely many excluded values, using the [Euclid's theorem](../../../../../euclid-s-theorem.md). [Compactness theorem](../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) gives a [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of the entire [set](../../../../../set-split.md). The complete-diagram argument embeds $\mathcal Z$ elementarily into its reduct; identify that copy with the original [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) [ring](../../../../../ring.md).

The resulting [elementary extension](../../../../../elementary-extension.md) contains $c$, a [prime element](../../../../../prime-element.md) unequal to every named standard [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal Z\preccurlyeq\mathcal Z^*,\qquad
\mathcal Z^*\models\operatorname{Prime}(c),\qquad c\notin\mathbb Z.}
$$

The construction rules out all standard [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md), not merely the finite list appearing in any one approximation.
