<h1 id="b/7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) is definable without parameters in the [ring](../../../../../ring.md) [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md). For $a\ge0$, use $x=\bar a$; for $a=-m<0$, use $x+\bar m=0$. For a finite [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) $\vec a$, the [conjunction](../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) $\delta_{\vec a}(\vec x)$ of these coordinate [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) uniquely defines that [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) in $\mathcal Z$.

If $\phi(\vec x)$ belongs to its [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md), the [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md)

$$
\forall\vec x\ (\delta_{\vec a}(\vec x)\to\phi(\vec x))
$$

is true in $\mathcal Z$, since there is only the specified [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) to check. It therefore belongs to $T=\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal Z)$. The [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\delta_{\vec a}$ is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) with $T$, since it is realized in $\mathcal Z$, and implies every member of the [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) modulo $T$. Hence **every [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) realized in $\mathcal Z$ is a [principal complete type](../../../../../principal-complete-type.md)**, isolated by its tuple-defining [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md).
