↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $L_A$ expand $L$ by a constant $c_a$ for each $a\in A$, and let $\mathcal A_A$ be the canonical expansion interpreting $c_a$ as $a$. Its [diagram of a structure](../../../../../diagram-mathematical-logic.md) is the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of all atomic and negated atomic $L_A$-sentences true in $\mathcal A_A$. Its [elementary diagram](../../../../../elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md) is the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of all $L_A$-sentences true there:

$$
\operatorname{Diag}(\mathcal A)=\{\theta:\theta\text{ atomic or negated atomic},\ \mathcal A_A\models\theta\},
$$



$$
\operatorname{CDiag}(\mathcal A)=\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal A_A).
$$

A [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) inclusion $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal B$ means that $A\subseteq B$, constants have the same interpretations, the operations of $\mathcal B$ restrict to those of $\mathcal A$, and each relation restricts to the corresponding relation on $A$.

An [elementary substructure](../../../../../elementary-substructure.md) additionally preserves the truth of every [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) with parameters from $A$:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\preccurlyeq\mathcal B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal A\models\phi(\vec a)\iff\mathcal B\models\phi(\vec a)
\text{ for every }\phi,\vec a\in A.}
$$

Here the right-hand condition includes the [substructure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) requirements by applying it to atomic [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) and their [negations](../../../../../negation.md).
