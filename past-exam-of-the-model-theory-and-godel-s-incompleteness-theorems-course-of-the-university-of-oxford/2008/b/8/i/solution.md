<h1 id="b/8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](../../../../../ryll-nardzewski-theorem.md) says that for a [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) $T$ in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md) with infinite [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md), the following are equivalent: $T$ is $\aleph_0$-categorical; for every positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$, the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $S_n(T)$ of complete parameter-free $n$-types is finite; every such finite-arity [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) is isolated by a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md). The countable-language hypothesis and the requirement for every finite arity are important.

The [omitting types theorem](../../../../../omitting-types-theorem.md) says that, for a [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md), a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) family of [nonprincipal partial types](../../../../../nonprincipal-partial-type.md) in finite [tuples](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) of [logical variables](../../../../../logical-variable.md) can all be omitted in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md). Here nonprincipal means that no [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) with the [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) implies every [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) of the [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md). Omission means that no [tuple](../../../../../finite-tuple.md) in the constructed [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) satisfies all [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) of that [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md). The [complete one-type](../../../../../complete-one-type.md) version is the special case of one [partial type](../../../../../partial-type.md) in one [logical variable](../../../../../logical-variable.md).
