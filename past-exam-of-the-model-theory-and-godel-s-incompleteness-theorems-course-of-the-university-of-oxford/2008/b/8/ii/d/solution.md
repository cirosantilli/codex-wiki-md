<h1 id="b/8/ii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The same [complete theory](../../../../../../complete-theory.md) $T_\infty$ of an infinite pure [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) provides this property directly. For $n$ [logical variables](../../../../../../logical-variable.md), each [complete type](../../../../../../complete-type.md) specifies exactly which [logical variables](../../../../../../logical-variable.md) are equal. [First-order consistency](../../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) requires an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) on the finite index [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), and infinitude ensures every such partition is realizable. Hence

$$
\boxed{|S_n(T_\infty)|=B_n<\infty,}
$$

where $B_n$ is the $n$th [Bell number](../../../../../../bell-number.md), counting partitions of an $n$-element [set](../../../../../../set-split.md). For example the first values are $B_1=1$, $B_2=2$ and $B_3=5$. The isolating [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) lists [logical equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md) within blocks and inequality between blocks. This explicitly verifies the hypothesis which Ryll-Nardzewski identifies with $\aleph_0$-categoricity.
