<h1 id="b/8/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the [first-order theory](../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) of an [infinite set](../../../../../../infinite-set.md) in the [first-order language](../../../../../../first-order-language.md) of [logical equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md) alone, with an axiom asserting at least $n$ distinct elements for each positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$. It is complete and eliminates [quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md): a new [logical variable](../../../../../../logical-variable.md) either equals a specified old [logical variable](../../../../../../logical-variable.md) or is required to avoid only finitely many existing values, which an infinite domain permits.

For every infinite cardinal $\kappa$, any two [first-order models](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of size $\kappa$ are [isomorphic](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) because every [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) between their underlying [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) preserves [logical equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md), the whole [first-order language](../../../../../../first-order-language.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{T_{\infty}\text{ is categorical in every infinite cardinality}.}
$$

Its complete $n$-types record only the partition of coordinate indices according to [logical equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md). There are finitely many such partitions, and each is isolated by the corresponding [logical equalities](../../../../../../logical-equality.md) and inequalities. Ryll-Nardzewski therefore recovers [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) categoricity; there are no nonprincipal complete finite-arity [partial types](../../../../../../partial-type.md) to omit in this example.
