↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An $L$-structure has a nonempty underlying [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $A$. It assigns to each [constant symbol](../../../../../constant-symbol.md) $c_k$ an element $c_k^{\mathcal A}\in A$, to each $n$-ary [function symbol](../../../../../function-symbol.md) $f_j$ a [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $A^n\to A$, and to each $n$-ary [predicate symbol](../../../../../predicate-symbol.md) $P_i$ a relation contained in $A^n$. [Logical equality](../../../../../logical-equality.md), when included as [logical equality](../../../../../logical-equality.md), is interpreted as actual identity.

The arities belong to the specification of the [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md). An assignment of [logical variables](../../../../../logical-variable.md) to elements of $A$ then evaluates [first-order terms](../../../../../first-order-term.md) recursively and determines satisfaction of [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) through the [logical connectives](../../../../../logical-connective.md) and [quantifiers](../../../../../quantifier.md). A [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) has no [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md), so its truth in the [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) is independent of the assignment.
