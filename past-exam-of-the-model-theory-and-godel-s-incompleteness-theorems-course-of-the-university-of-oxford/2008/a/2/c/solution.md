<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If PA is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) and already proves $\neg X$, it cannot also prove $X$. By the correctness of the ordinary arithmetized [arithmetical proof relation](../../../../../arithmetical-proof-relation.md), $\mathbb N\models\neg\operatorname{Pr}_{PA}(\ulcorner X\urcorner)$.

If PA is inconsistent, it proves $X$ by explosion, so an actual [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) code witnesses $\operatorname{Pr}_{PA}(\ulcorner X\urcorner)$ in the standard [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{PA\text{ consistent}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathbb N\models\neg\operatorname{Pr}_{PA}(\ulcorner X\urcorner).}
$$

The hypothesis that $X$ is refutable is essential to this [first-order consistency](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) characterization for an arbitrary fixed [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md).
