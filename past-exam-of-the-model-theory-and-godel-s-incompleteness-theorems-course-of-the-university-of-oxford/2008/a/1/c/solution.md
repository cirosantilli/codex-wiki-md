<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By the standard interpretation of the representing [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md),

$$
\mathbb N\models G_S\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S\nvdash G_S.
$$

If $S$ is [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md), part (b) supplies the right-hand side, so $G_S$ is true. If $S$ is inconsistent, explosion gives $S\vdash G_S$, so the representing existential statement is true and $G_S$ is false. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb N\models G_S\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S\text{ is consistent}.}
$$

This is an external truth assertion about the standard [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md). Formalizing that assertion inside a [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) requires the additional facts explicitly supplied in part (d).
