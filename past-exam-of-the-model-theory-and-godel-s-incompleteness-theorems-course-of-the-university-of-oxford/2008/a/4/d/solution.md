<h1 id="a/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Under the arithmetical interpretation $\square A=P_S(\ulcorner A\urcorner)$, the Löb axiom says that a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of the local reflection implication for $A$ already entails a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of $A$:

$$
P_S(\ulcorner P_S(\ulcorner A\urcorner)\to A\urcorner)\to P_S(\ulcorner A\urcorner).
$$

It is a formalized constraint on self-verification of a [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) system. Externally, [Löb's theorem](../../../../../lob-s-theorem.md) says that if the system itself proves its own local reflection implication for $A$, it proves $A$. Distribution reflects closure of [formal proofs](../../../../../formal-proof.md) under implication, necessitation reflects that a theorem has an arithmetically recognizable [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md), and [positive introspection](../../../../../positive-introspection.md) reflects [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) verification inside [arithmetic](../../../../../arithmetic-split.md).

Taking $A$ to be a refutable [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md), especially a [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md), recovers the obstruction behind the second incompleteness theorem: a [first-order consistent](../../../../../first-order-consistency.md) sufficiently strong system cannot prove the corresponding claim that no [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) of that [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) exists. This does not identify provability with truth, nor grant the unrestricted factivity principle $\square A\to A$ as a theorem of the system. The Löb axiom is the [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) additional scheme of Gödel–Löb [provability logic](../../../../../provability-logic.md).
