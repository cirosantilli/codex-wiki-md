A [term-generated model](../../../../../../term-generated-model.md) is a structure in which every element is the value of a [closed term](../../../../../../closed-term.md) of the original language. Equivalently its [substructure](../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) generated by the empty set, including interpretations of named constants, is the entire structure. This is the relevant meaning of “term model” for a model of an arbitrary [complete theory](../../../../../../complete-theory.md), rather than a claim about one particular syntactic construction.

Each [closed term](../../../../../../closed-term.md) $t$ gives the parameter-free defining formula $x=t$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{term-generated}\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{pointwise definable}.}
$$

The next parts show that pointwise definability implies atomicity, and [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) atomicity is equivalent to primeness. Therefore property (i) implies all three other properties.
