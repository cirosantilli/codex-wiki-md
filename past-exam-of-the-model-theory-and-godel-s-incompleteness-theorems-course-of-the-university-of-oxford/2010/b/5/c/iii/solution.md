↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** The parameter-free [partial type](../../../../../../partial-type.md)

$$
\{x>n\cdot1:n\in\mathbb N\}
$$

is finitely satisfiable in the ordered real field: choose a number larger than the finitely many bounds. By [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) it extends to a [complete type](../../../../../../complete-type.md) over the empty set. But no real number realizes it, by the [Archimedean property](../../../../../../archimedean-property.md). Therefore the ordered real field is not an [omega-saturated model](../../../../../../omega-saturated-model.md).

The named unit and addition make every integer bound expressible, even without extra parameters. This is why the obstruction is present in the ordered-field language and absent in the two reducts above.
