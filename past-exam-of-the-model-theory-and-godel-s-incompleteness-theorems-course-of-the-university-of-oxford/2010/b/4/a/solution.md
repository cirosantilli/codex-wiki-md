↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) is $\kappa$-categorical when it has a model with underlying [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) $\kappa$ and any two of its models of that [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) are [isomorphic structures](../../../../../structure-isomorphism.md). Thus categoricity means uniqueness up to isomorphism at the specified [cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md), not a unique underlying set or a unique isomorphism.

For the infinite [cardinals](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) relevant here, the existence clause is automatic from the stated infinite model and the [Löwenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md). One may equivalently require exactly one isomorphism class of models of size $\kappa$.
