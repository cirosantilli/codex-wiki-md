<h1 id="b/4/f/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The distinguished chain generated by zero is [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), and every other component in the [classification of models of the successor theory](../../../../../../classification-of-models-of-the-successor-theory.md) is a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) copy of the integer successor chain. A model of [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\aleph_1$ therefore has exactly $\aleph_1$ additional components: a finite or [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) collection would be [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), while the union of $\kappa$ [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) components has size $\max(\kappa,\aleph_0)$.

Given two such models, match their zero chains in the forced way and choose a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) between their additional components. On each component choose an origin and match corresponding integer iterates; this commutes with successor. The union of these maps is an [isomorphism of structures](../../../../../../structure-isomorphism.md). Existence is given by a disjoint union with $\aleph_1$ integer chains. Hence

$$
\boxed{T_S\text{ is }\aleph_1\text{-categorical}.}
$$

The same argument works at every [uncountable](../../../../../../uncountable-set.md) [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md). [Countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) models can still differ by the number of extra components, as part (ii) shows.
