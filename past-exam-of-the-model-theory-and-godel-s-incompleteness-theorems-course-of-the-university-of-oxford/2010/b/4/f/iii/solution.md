<h1 id="b/4/f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each [natural number](../../../../../../natural-number.md) $n$, the formula $x=s^n(0)$ defines the singleton $\{n\}$ in the standard [successor structure](../../../../../../successor-structure.md). These are pairwise distinct parameter-free definable subsets. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\{\{n\}:n\in\mathbb N\}\text{ is an infinite family of definable subsets}.}
$$

In particular the criterion in part (e) applies already at arity one.
