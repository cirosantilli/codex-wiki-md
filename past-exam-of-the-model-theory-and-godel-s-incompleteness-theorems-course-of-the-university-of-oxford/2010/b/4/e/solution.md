<h1 id="b/4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [omitting types theorem](../../../../../omitting-types-theorem.md) says that for a consistent theory in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) language and countably many nonprincipal types of finite arities, there is a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) model omitting all those types. In particular a nonisolated [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) of $T$ can be omitted in a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) model.

Interpret “definable” here as **without parameters**. If parameters were allowed freely, every infinite structure would have infinitely many definable singleton sets, and the claimed implication would be false even for a [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md).

Infinitely many distinct parameter-free definable subsets of $A^n$ imply infinitely many complete $n$-types: if there were only $r$ such types, each formula would select a subset of those $r$ types, giving at most $2^r$ distinct definable subsets. There must also be a nonisolated [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md). To see this, suppose each [complete type](../../../../../complete-type.md) $p$ had an isolator $\theta_p$. The family of their basic open sets covers the [type space](../../../../../type-space.md). Its [compactness theorem](../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) gives a finite subcover, which would account for only finitely many [complete types](../../../../../complete-type.md), a contradiction. Equivalently apply logical [compactness theorem](../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) to $T\cup\{\neg\theta_p(c):p\text{ complete}\}$.

Choose a nonisolated type $p$. The [omitting types theorem](../../../../../omitting-types-theorem.md) gives a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) model omitting it, while part (d) gives a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) model realizing it. An [isomorphism of structures](../../../../../structure-isomorphism.md) preserves realization of parameter-free types, so these models are not isomorphic. Thus $\boxed{T\text{ is not }\aleph_0\text{-categorical}}$.
