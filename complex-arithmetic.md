# Complex arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

Arithmetic operations on [complex numbers](complex-number.md). Writing two inputs as $a+bi$ and $c+di$ turns multiplication into $(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i$ and addition into $(a+c)+(b+d)i$. This is distinct from the specialized theory of [complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md) of [elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md).
