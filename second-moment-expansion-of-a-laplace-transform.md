# Second-moment expansion of a Laplace transform

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md)

For nonnegative $X$ with finite [second moment](second-moment.md), [differentiation](differentiation.md) under the [expectation](expected-value.md) gives $\phi'(\lambda)=-\mathbb E[Xe^{-\lambda X}]$ for $\lambda>0$. The exponential Taylor remainder divided by $\lambda^2$ tends pointwise to zero and is bounded in absolute value by $X^2/2$. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) therefore proves the displayed expansion without requiring a third moment.
