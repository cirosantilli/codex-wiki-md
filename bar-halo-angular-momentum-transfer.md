# Bar-halo angular-momentum transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar bar](stellar-bar.md)

Exchange of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) between a [stellar bar](stellar-bar.md) and a [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) through orbital [resonances](resonance.md). If the unperturbed halo [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) depends only on [energy](energy-split.md) and decreases with it, the [resonant torque from a rotating perturbation](resonant-torque-from-a-rotating-perturbation.md) is nonnegative for positive pattern [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). Strict transfer requires nonzero resonant overlap. The reaction [torque](torque.md) slows a rigid bar of constant positive [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md); evolving bar structure and halo redistribution require updating the response.
