# Ozone-depletion potential

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

A relative measure of stratospheric [ozone](ozone.md) destruction associated with emissions of a substance, conventionally referenced to CFC-11. It depends on atmospheric survival, transport, halogen delivery and catalytic effectiveness, not only the number of [chlorine](chlorine.md) atoms in the formula. Fluorine-only replacements can have negligible direct [ozone-depletion potential](ozone-depletion-potential.md) while remaining strong [greenhouse gases](greenhouse-gas.md).
