# Herbrand quotient of a finite module

↑ **Parent:** [Herbrand quotient](herbrand-quotient.md)

For a finite [module](module-mathematics.md) acted on by a cyclic [group](group-split.md), write $D=\sigma-1$ and $N=1+\sigma+\cdots+\sigma^{n-1}$. The two [Tate cohomology](tate-cohomology-of-a-finite-group.md) [group](group-split.md) sizes are $|\ker D|/|NM|$ and $|\ker N|/|DM|$. The equalities $|NM|=|M|/|\ker N|$ and $|DM|=|M|/|\ker D|$ show that these sizes are equal. The exact-sequence multiplication rule for the [Herbrand quotient](herbrand-quotient.md) consequently makes it unchanged by finite-index modifications of lattices.
