# Superheated two-phase Stefan similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann solution of the Stefan problem](neumann-solution-of-the-stefan-problem.md)

For equal phase thermal properties, a roof undercooling $\Delta T$ and liquid superheat $\Theta$, the planar [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md) has interface $a=2\lambda\sqrt{\kappa t}$. The solid [error function](error-function.md) profile and liquid complementary-error-function profile give $S\lambda=e^{-\lambda^2}[1/\operatorname{erf}\lambda-(\Theta/\Delta T)/\operatorname{erfc}\lambda]/\sqrt\pi$. Here $S=L/(c_p\Delta T)$ is the [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md). Liquid heat supplied to the interface reduces crust growth.
