# Nottingham group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_group)

Over $k=\mathbb F_p$, the [Nottingham group](nottingham-group.md) is $\mathcal N(k)=\{f(t)=t+\sum_{i\ge2}a_it^i:a_i\in k\}$ with composition and the $t$-adic [topology](topology-split.md). Equivalently it is the [group](group-split.md) of continuous $k$-automorphisms of the [Laurent series field](laurent-series-field.md) $k((t))$ that act trivially on $(t)/(t^2)$. With ordinary series composition, the [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) sends $f$ to the automorphism $h(t)\mapsto h(f^{-1}(t))$; this inverse avoids reversing the [group](group-split.md) product. It is a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md), recovered from its finite truncations, and contains elements of arbitrarily large finite $p$-power order.
