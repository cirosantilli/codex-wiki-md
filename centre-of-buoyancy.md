# Centre of buoyancy

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

The [centre of buoyancy](centre-of-buoyancy.md) is the point through which the resultant hydrostatic [buoyant force](buoyancy.md) acts. In a uniform [fluid](fluid.md) under uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md), it is the [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) [centroid](centroid.md) of the displaced [fluid](fluid.md). For a floating body its position generally changes when the body tilts, because the displaced [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) changes shape.
