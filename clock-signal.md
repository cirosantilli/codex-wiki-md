# Clock signal

↑ **Parent:** [Digital electronics](digital-electronics.md)

A recurring digital signal whose designated [clock edge](clock-edge.md) triggers a state update. A rising [clock edge](clock-edge.md) is the transition from the low level to the high level; a falling [clock edge](clock-edge.md) is the reverse.
