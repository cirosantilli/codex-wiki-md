# Renorming a bounded multiplicative semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra norm](algebra-norm.md)

Let $S$ be a bounded multiplicatively closed subset of a unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md). The displayed auxiliary [norm](norm.md) $p$ is equivalent to the original norm, and each left multiplication $L_s$ is a contraction for $s\in S$. Taking the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) of the faithful left regular representation produces an equivalent [algebra norm](algebra-norm.md) with $\|1\|_S=1$ and $\|s\|_S\leq1$. If $M=\max(\|1\|,\sup_S\|s\|)$, then $\|a\|/M\leq\|a\|_S\leq M\|a\|$. The auxiliary norm is itself submultiplicative, since $p(ab)\leq p(a)\|b\|\leq p(a)p(b)$, but $p(1)=M$ can exceed one. The operator norm enforces the required unital normalization.
