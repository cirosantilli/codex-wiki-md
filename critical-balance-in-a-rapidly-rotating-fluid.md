# Critical balance in a rapidly rotating fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Critical balance](critical-balance.md)

Balance the [inertial wave](inertial-wave.md) rate against horizontal nonlinear advection. The anisotropy $\epsilon=|k_\parallel|/k_\perp$ is then of the same order as the [Rossby number](rossby-number.md) $k_\perp U/(2\Omega)$. Horizontal [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) gives kinematic [pressure](pressure-split.md) amplitude $\delta p\sim2\Omega U/k_\perp\sim U^2/\epsilon$. The first [ageostrophic flow](ageostrophic-flow.md) is $O(\epsilon U)$ and supplies the small horizontal divergence balancing $-\partial_z u_\parallel$. Rapid inertial oscillations are excluded by this slow-time ordering.
