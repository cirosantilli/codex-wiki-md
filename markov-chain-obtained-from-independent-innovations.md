# Markov chain obtained from independent innovations

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

Let $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ be [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), independent of an initial state $Z_0$, and let $h$ be a [measurable function](measurable-function.md). The displayed recursion defines a time-homogeneous [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md): conditioning on the state history leaves $\xi_{n+1}$ with its original [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md), so its next-state law depends only on $Z_n$. Its [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) is $K(z,A)=\mathbb P(h(z,\xi_1)\in A)$. Running sums use $h(z,x)=z+x$; running maxima use $h(z,x)=\max(z,x)$. Independence from the complete past, rather than merely from the current state, supplies the [Markov property](markov-property.md).
