# Entropy generation by mixing equal thermal masses

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy generation in finite-temperature heat transfer](entropy-generation-in-finite-temperature-heat-transfer.md)

For equal [masses](mass.md) $M$ of the same liquid with constant specific heat $c_P$, adiabatic constant-pressure mixing gives final [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_f=(T_1+T_2)/2$ by [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) conservation. The [entropy](entropy-split.md) change is $Mc_P\ln(T_f/T_1)+Mc_P\ln(T_f/T_2)=2Mc_P\ln[(T_1+T_2)/(2\sqrt{T_1T_2})]$. The [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) proves nonnegativity, with equality only for equal initial [temperatures](temperature-split.md). There is no separate compositional mixing [entropy](entropy-split.md) for identical fluids.
