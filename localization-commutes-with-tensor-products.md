# Localization commutes with tensor products

↑ **Parent:** [Localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md)

For a multiplicatively closed set $T$ in a commutative ring $A$, the map sends $(m\otimes n)/t$ to $(m/t)\otimes(n/1)$. An inverse sends $(m/s)\otimes(n/t)$ to $(m\otimes n)/(st)$. The fraction relations and the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) balancing relation make these maps well-defined and mutually inverse.
