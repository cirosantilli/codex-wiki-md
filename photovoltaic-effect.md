# Photovoltaic effect

↑ **Parent:** [Photon absorption](photon-absorption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_effect)

The [photovoltaic effect](photovoltaic-effect.md) is the production of an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) or an [electric current](electric-current.md) by [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) in a material or device. In a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), absorbed [photons](photon.md) generate [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md); asymmetric transport and selective collection can maintain distinct [quasi-Fermi levels](quasi-fermi-level.md) and deliver electrical [power](power.md) to a load. An equilibrium [built-in potential](built-in-potential.md) alone supplies no continuous electrical [power](power.md).
