# Zone folding in an equal-mass diatomic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

Putting $M=m$ in the [diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs](diatomic-chain-dispersion-relation-with-equal-springs.md) gives $\omega_-=2\sqrt{K/m}|\sin(kd/2)|$ and $\omega_+=2\sqrt{K/m}|\cos(kd/2)|$ in the reduced zone $|k|\leq\pi/(2d)$. The branches touch at its edge. If the sites are actually identical, the true [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) has length $d$; the apparent [optical phonon branch](optical-phonon-branch.md) is the outer part of the [monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md) folded by an unnecessarily doubled cell. A gap does not appear merely from redrawing the zone.
