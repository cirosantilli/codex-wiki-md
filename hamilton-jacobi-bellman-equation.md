# Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation

↑ **Parent:** [Bellman equation](bellman-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman_equation)

The Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman equation is the continuous-time [Bellman equation](bellman-equation.md). For state dynamics $\dot x=f(x,u,t)$, running cost $L(x,u,t)$ and terminal cost $\Phi(x)$, a smooth [value function](value-function.md) satisfies

$$
V_t+\inf_u\{L+\nabla V\mathbin{\cdot}f\}=0,
\qquad V(x,h)=\Phi(x).
$$
