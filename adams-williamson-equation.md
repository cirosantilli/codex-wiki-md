# Adams-Williamson equation

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

In a chemically homogeneous adiabatic spherical region, $d\rho/dr=-\rho g/\Phi$, coupled to $dm/dr=4\pi r^2\rho$ and $g=Gm/r^2$. Seismic [velocities](velocity.md) supply $\Phi$; [density](density.md) normalization and jumps require further data and assumptions.
