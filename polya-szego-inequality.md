<h1 id="polya-szego-inequality">Pólya-Szegő inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric decreasing rearrangement](symmetric-decreasing-rearrangement.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya-Szegő_inequality)

For $u\in W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^d)$, the [symmetric decreasing rearrangement](symmetric-decreasing-rearrangement.md) satisfies

$$
\|\nabla u^*\|_{L^p}\leq\|\nabla u\|_{L^p}.
$$
