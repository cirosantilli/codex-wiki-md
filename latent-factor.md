# Latent factor

↑ **Parent:** [Factor analysis](factor-analysis.md)

A [latent factor](latent-factor.md) is an unobserved coordinate introduced to explain shared variation among measurements. In an [orthogonal factor model](orthogonal-factor-model.md), [latent factors](latent-factor.md) have [variance](variance-split.md) one and are mutually uncorrelated, with coefficients specified by the [factor loadings](factor-loading.md). They differ from a [regression factor](regression-factor.md), which is a categorical predictor. A fitted [covariance](covariance.md) alone generally leaves the [latent factor](latent-factor.md) orientation undetermined; [factor rotation](factor-rotation.md) gives equivalent representations.
