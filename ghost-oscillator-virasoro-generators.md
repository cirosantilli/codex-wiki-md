# Ghost oscillator Virasoro generators

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet ghost field](worldsheet-ghost-field.md)

Contracting an antighost with the cubic [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) term of the [BRST operator](brst-operator.md) produces the bilinear generators. They give $[L_m,b_n]=(m-n)b_{m+n}$ and $[L_m,c_n]=-(2m+n)c_{m+n}$. Their zero-mode additive constant depends on the vacuum and [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) convention; it must agree with the corresponding $c_0$ term in the charge.
