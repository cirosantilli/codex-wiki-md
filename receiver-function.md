# Receiver function

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A processed record that isolates relative arrival times and amplitudes of converted and reverberated [seismic waves](seismic-wave.md) by reducing the common source and propagation response. [P-to-S conversion](p-to-s-conversion.md) and [S-to-P conversion](s-to-p-conversion.md) at contrasts in [seismic velocity](seismic-velocity.md) can constrain interface depths and sharpness. Interpretation requires a [velocity model](velocity-model.md) and checks for crustal multiples, dipping interfaces and [seismic anisotropy](seismic-anisotropy.md).
