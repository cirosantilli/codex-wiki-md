# Stern-Brocot tree

↑ **Parent:** [Rational number](rational-number.md)

A [binary tree](binary-tree.md) enumerating every positive [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md) exactly once. Begin with boundary fractions $0/1$ and $1/0$; between neighboring fractions $a/b<c/d$, insert their mediant $(a+c)/(b+d)$ and recurse into the two new intervals. The [determinant](determinant.md) relation $bc-ad=1$ is preserved, and the new [denominator](denominator.md) is $b+d$.
