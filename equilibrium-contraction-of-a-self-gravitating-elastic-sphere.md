# Equilibrium contraction of a self-gravitating elastic sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Mean stress and strain boundary identities](mean-stress-and-strain-boundary-identities.md)

For a uniform traction-free sphere, first-order self-gravity is $b=-gx/a$. Static averaging gives $\overline\sigma=-\rho gaI/5$. Homogeneous [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) then gives $\overline e=-\rho gaI/(15\kappa)$. The boundary strain identity identifies $\overline e=u(a)I/a$, proving the displayed positive radius decrease. The local strain is not uniform, and the cancellation of the [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) concerns only the mean contraction.
