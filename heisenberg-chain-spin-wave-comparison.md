# Heisenberg-chain spin-wave comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

With unit lattice spacing and $\hbar=1$, a nearest-neighbour [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md) has quadratic low-momentum [ferromagnetic magnons](ferromagnetic-magnon.md) and linear antiferromagnetic modes in the [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md). The ferromagnetic polarized vacuum is an exact [ground state](ground-state.md), whereas the assumed antiferromagnetic ordered vacuum has [quantum depletion of Néel order](quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md). In one dimension its logarithmic [infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md) invalidates finite ordered [magnetization](magnetization.md) and prevents the linear approximation from deciding the exact gap.
