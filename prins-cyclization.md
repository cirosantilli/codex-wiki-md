# Prins cyclization

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

Acid-promoted union of a carbonyl-derived [oxocarbenium ion](oxocarbenium-ion.md) with a tethered [alkene](alkene.md) to form a ring. Cation trapping and the preferred cyclization conformation determine the final substitution and [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md).
