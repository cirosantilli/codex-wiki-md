# Two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov-Smirnov test](kolmogorov-smirnov-test.md)

The [two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test](two-sample-kolmogorov-smirnov-test.md) compares two [empirical distribution functions](empirical-distribution-function.md) from [independent](independent-random-variables.md) samples, each internally [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), under the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) that their underlying [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) coincide. Both curves are estimated from data, so calibration depends on both [sample sizes](sample-size.md); the usual asymptotic scaling is $\sqrt{nm/(n+m)}D_{n,m}$. Tied data can be handled by an appropriate [permutation test](permutation-test.md) under the required [exchangeability](exchangeable-random-variables.md), rather than by assuming the untied continuous-null law.[Kolmogorov-Smirnov Tests (ks.test, R stats documentation)](kolmogorov-smirnov-tests-ks-test-r-stats-documentation.md)
