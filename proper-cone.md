# Proper cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cone](convex-cone.md)

In [conic optimization](conic-optimization.md), a proper cone is a closed [convex cone](convex-cone.md) that is a [pointed cone](pointed-cone.md) and has nonempty interior in its ambient finite-dimensional space. These conditions permit strict cone feasibility and nondegenerate barrier geometry.
