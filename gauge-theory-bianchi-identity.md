# Gauge-theory Bianchi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md)

The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) for [gauge covariant derivatives](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) gives $D_{[\mu}F_{\nu\rho]}=0$. In four dimensions this is $D_\mu\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}=0$, with $\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}=\frac12\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}F_{\rho\sigma}$. It holds independently of the [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md).
