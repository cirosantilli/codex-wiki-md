# Hermitian metric on a smooth complex vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md)

This is a smoothly varying positive-definite [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) on each fiber, without requiring a holomorphic base or bundle. A [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) glues local positive metrics into a global one. Applying smooth Gram-Schmidt to local frames then makes the transitions unitary, reducing the [structure group of a vector bundle](structure-group-of-a-vector-bundle.md) to $U(k)$. For a line bundle its unit vectors form a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) for the [circle group](circle-group.md).
