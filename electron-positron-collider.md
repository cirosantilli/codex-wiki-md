# Electron-positron collider

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)

A [particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md) that brings counterpropagating [electron](electron.md) and [Positron](positron.md) beams into collision. The known initial [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and tunable [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md) make resonance scans and missing-energy measurements possible.
