# Electrical calorimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md)

Use measured electrical energy input to calibrate or infer a thermal response. A heater's terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md) determine the supplied energy; receiver [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) and losses must also be accounted for.
