# Real-slice diagnosis of chiral-scalar vacua

↑ **Parent:** [F-term scalar potential](f-term-scalar-potential.md)

A [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) has a [complex scalar](complex-scalar-field.md). Minimizing its potential only on the real axis can miss [supersymmetric vacua](supersymmetric-vacuum.md). For example $W'(\varphi)=1+\varphi^2$ has positive potential $(1+x^2)^2$ for real $x$, yet its complex vacua $\varphi=\pm i$ have zero energy. [Supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md) must therefore be tested in the full complex field space, not inferred from a positive minimum on an arbitrarily chosen real slice.
