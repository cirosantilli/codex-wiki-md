# Drift estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Drift coefficient](drift-coefficient.md)

From a continuously observed [Itô diffusion](ito-diffusion.md), a local drift estimator divides $\int_0^T\mathbf1_{[x-h,x+h]}(X_t)\,dX_t$ by the corresponding [diffusion occupation time](diffusion-occupation-time.md). Its error separates into a local [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) bias and an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) divided by occupation time. The [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) gives a pointwise noise scale $(Th)^{-1/2}$ when the invariant density is locally positive.
