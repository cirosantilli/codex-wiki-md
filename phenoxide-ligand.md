# Phenoxide ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [phenoxide ligand](phenoxide-ligand.md) binds through the negatively charged [oxygen](oxygen.md) of deprotonated [phenol](phenol.md) or a substituted phenol. It is distinguished from a neutral phenol donor and from an ordinary neutral ether.
