# Conserved octant dynamics of a cyclic amplitude system

↑ **Parent:** [Signed cyclic three-mode amplitude equations](signed-cyclic-three-mode-amplitude-equations.md)

When $c=e$ in the [signed cyclic three-mode amplitude equations](signed-cyclic-three-mode-amplitude-equations.md), $\dot X=2X(\mu-aX)$ and $\dot V=3V(\mu-aX)$ for $V=xyz$. Thus $V/X^{3/2}$ is conserved wherever $X>0$, precluding asymptotically stable isolated equilibria. On $X=\mu/a$, each octant contains closed contours of $V$ around the equal-magnitude equilibrium, bounded by heteroclinic arcs between coordinate axes if $c\ne0$. For $c=0$ the sphere consists entirely of equilibria.
