# Nested-model F-test

↑ **Parent:** [F-test](f-test.md)

For nested Gaussian linear models differing by $r$ parameters, the statistic

$$
\frac{(RSS_0-RSS_1)/r}{RSS_1/(n-p_1)}
$$

has an $F_{r,n-p_1}$ distribution under the reduced model.
