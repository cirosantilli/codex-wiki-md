# Slotted line

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)

A [slotted line](slotted-line.md) samples an [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) along a guide with a movable probe introduced through a longitudinal slot. Positions of [standing wave](standing-wave.md) minima determine the [guided wavelength](guided-wavelength.md); a detector response need not be a calibrated field amplitude.
