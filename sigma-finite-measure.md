# Sigma-finite measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma-finite_measure)

A [measure](measure.md) is sigma-finite when its space is a countable [union](set-union.md) of measurable sets of finite measure.
