# Euler characteristic of a one-variable Iwasawa module

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa module structure theorem](iwasawa-module-structure-theorem.md)

Assume the invariant and coinvariant groups are finite and $f_M$ generates the [characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md) over $\mathbb Z_p[[S]]$, $S=\gamma-1$. The structure theorem and multiplicativity of finite Euler characteristics prove the formula; finite module errors have Euler characteristic one. If $f_M\mid f_N$ and the two Euler characteristics agree, the quotient has p-adic-unit constant term and is therefore a unit power series. Thus divisibility plus this finite arithmetic comparison upgrades to equality of characteristic ideals.
