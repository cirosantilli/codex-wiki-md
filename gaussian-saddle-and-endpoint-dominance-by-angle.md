# Gaussian saddle and endpoint dominance by angle

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic complex endpoint descent path](quadratic-complex-endpoint-descent-path.md)

For a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) from $-1/\sqrt2$ to $e^{i\theta}$, a [saddle point](saddle-point.md) contribution dominates for $0\leq\theta\leq\pi/4$. The moving [endpoint contribution in steepest descent](endpoint-contribution-in-steepest-descent.md) dominates for $\pi/4<\theta<5\pi/6$. At $5\pi/6$ the two endpoints have equal exponential rates and both are needed; for larger angles the fixed endpoint dominates. Saddle connectivity switches at $\pi/2$, but that change is subdominant in the leading endpoint regime.
