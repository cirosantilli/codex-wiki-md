# Pound-Rebka experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound–Rebka_experiment)

The experiment compares gamma-ray emission and resonant absorption at different heights in Earth's field, using the [Mössbauer effect](mossbauer-effect-split.md) to resolve the small [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md). Its weak-field result is the frequency shift $\Delta\nu/\nu=-gH/c^2$ for an upward ray over height $H$. Any metric theory with the corresponding static lapse predicts this leading effect; the test alone does not distinguish [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) from the weak-field prediction of [Nordstrom theory of gravitation](nordstrom-s-theory-of-gravitation.md).
