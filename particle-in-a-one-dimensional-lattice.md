# Particle in a one-dimensional lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_in_a_one-dimensional_lattice)

A quantum particle in a one-dimensional lattice experiences a periodic potential. Bloch states organize its spectrum into bands and gaps; the [Kronig-Penney model](kronig-penney-model.md) realizes this behavior with an idealized array of barriers or narrow delta barriers.
