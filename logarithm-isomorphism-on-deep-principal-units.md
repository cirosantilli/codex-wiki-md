# Logarithm isomorphism on deep principal units

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic logarithm](p-adic-logarithm.md)

For a mixed-characteristic [local field](local-field.md) with [residue characteristic](residue-characteristic.md) $\ell$, the [p-adic logarithm](p-adic-logarithm.md) and [p-adic exponential function](p-adic-exponential-function.md) are mutually inverse continuous group homomorphisms on these domains. Bounds on the [valuations](valuation.md) of the denominators show that higher terms have greater [valuation](valuation.md) than the linear term. Dividing the logarithm by $\pi^r$ identifies the multiplicative [higher principal-unit group](higher-principal-unit-group.md) with the additive integer ring; in particular it is torsion-free.
