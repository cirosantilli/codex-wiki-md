# Phonon heat capacity of a dilute Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

One linear Bogoliubov branch $E_k=\hbar c_sk$ in three dimensions gives thermal [energy](energy-split.md) $\pi^2V(k_BT)^4/(30\hbar^3c_s^3)$ and the displayed cubic [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). There is one branch, rather than the three polarizations in a simple crystal's Debye model. The regime requires $\hbar c_s/L\ll k_BT\ll\mu$ for a box: the lower inequality permits a continuum integral, and the upper one selects [phonons](phonon.md). Fixed finite boxes ultimately have discrete-level behaviour.
