# Branched surface action from two generators

↑ **Parent:** [Branched covering](branched-covering.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md) generated by $a,b$, take the regular covering of a thrice-punctured [sphere](sphere.md) with [monodromy](monodromy.md) $a,b,(ab)^{-1}$ and fill each lifted puncture by a disk. Locally the projection is $z\mapsto z^m$, where $m$ is the order of its monodromy. The result is a closed oriented [topological surface](topological-surface.md) with an orientation-preserving group action. The nontrivial [stabiliser subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) are the conjugates of $\langle a\rangle$, $\langle b\rangle$ and $\langle ab\rangle$ at the three types of ramification points. Counting lifted cells gives the displayed [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). A subgroup acts freely exactly when it meets every conjugate of these three cyclic groups trivially. Its quotient is then a [regular covering](regular-covering.md) of degree equal to the subgroup order, and quotient [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is divided by that order.
