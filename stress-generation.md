# Stress generation

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological stress](psychological-stress.md)

A process in which symptoms or related characteristics increase the probability of subsequent stressful experiences. It differs from sensitization, which concerns a changed response to [psychological stress](psychological-stress.md), although the two can participate in a feedback loop.
