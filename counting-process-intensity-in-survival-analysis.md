# Counting-process intensity in survival analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Counting process](counting-process.md)

An observed event [counting process](counting-process.md) has conditional increment $\mathbb E(dN_i(t)\mid\mathcal H_{t-})=Y_i(t)h_i(t)\,dt$, where $Y_i$ is the [at-risk process](at-risk-process.md). The [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) records the past observed events, censoring, and [covariates](covariate.md); the integral of $Y_i h_i$ is the [compensator of a counting process](compensator-of-a-counting-process.md).
