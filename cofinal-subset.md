# Cofinal subset

↑ **Parent:** [Cofinality](cofinality.md)

A subset $C$ of an ordered set $P$ is cofinal if every element of $P$ is at most some element of $C$. For an [ordinal](ordinal-split.md), a cofinal subset has the whole ordinal as its [supremum](supremum.md).
