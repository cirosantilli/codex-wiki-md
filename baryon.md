# Baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Hadron](hadron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryon)

A baryon is a hadron with half-integer spin and baryon number one; ordinary baryons consist of three valence quarks.
