# Singlet state

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singlet_state)

Two equal spins $j$ have the unique total-spin-zero state

$$
|0,0\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt{2j+1}}
\sum_{m=-j}^j(-1)^{j-m}|j,m\rangle|j,-m\rangle.
$$

It is invariant under joint rotations.

A [singlet state](singlet-state.md) is the one-dimensional rotation representation; this construction supplies the angular-momentum-zero case for two equal spins.
