# RLC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RLC_circuit)

An RLC circuit combines [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), [inductance](inductance.md) and [capacitance](capacitance.md). Stored [energy](energy.md) can oscillate between magnetic and electric forms, while [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) dissipates it.
