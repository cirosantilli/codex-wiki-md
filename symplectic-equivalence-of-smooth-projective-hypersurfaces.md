# Symplectic equivalence of smooth projective hypersurfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Moser's trick](moser-s-trick.md)

Any two smooth degree-$d$ hypersurfaces in $\mathbb{CP}^n$, equipped with the restricted [Fubini-Study form](fubini-study-form.md), are symplectomorphic. Join them through the connected complement of the discriminant in the parameter space, use the resulting smooth family to identify the fibers, and apply [Moser's trick](moser-s-trick.md) to the cohomologous restricted forms.
