# Mixed-colour closed walk criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Strongly connected component](strongly-connected-component.md)

A [directed graph](directed-graph.md) with black and white [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) has a [closed walk](closed-walk.md) containing both colours exactly when some [strongly connected component](strongly-connected-component.md) has internal [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) of both colours. Join the two chosen [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) with [graph paths](path-in-a-graph.md) inside the [strongly connected component](strongly-connected-component.md). A corresponding claim for a [simple cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) is false: differently coloured [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md) can meet at a single [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md).
