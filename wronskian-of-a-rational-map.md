# Wronskian of a rational map

↑ **Parent:** [Rational map (complex analysis)](rational-map-complex-analysis.md)

For a reduced [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) $R=p/q$, its derivative is $W/q^2$ with $W=p'q-pq'$. Away from target-coordinate poles, the zeros of this [Wronskian](wronskian.md) identify its [ramification points of a holomorphic map](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md); at a pole use the reciprocal coordinate $q/p$. A pole of order $m$ contributes ramification order $m-1$, and infinity must also be checked in local coordinates. For degree $N$ the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives total ramification $2N-2$. The affine polynomial $W$ can have fewer finite zeros because some ramification lies at infinity. In the [rational map approximation for Skyrmions](rational-map-approximation-for-skyrmions.md), these directions have zero angular baryon density.
