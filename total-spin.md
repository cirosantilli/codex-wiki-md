# Total spin

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The [total spin](total-spin.md) is the [vector](vector.md) sum of subsystem [spin operators](spin-operator.md) and generates their simultaneous [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). For a rotationally invariant [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md), both its squared magnitude and any chosen component commute with the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). An anisotropic exchange or field can preserve only a component.
