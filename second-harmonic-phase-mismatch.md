# Second-harmonic phase mismatch

↑ **Parent:** [Phase matching in second-harmonic generation](phase-matching-in-second-harmonic-generation.md)

The difference between the harmonic's [wave number](wavenumber.md) and that of the quadratic driving [electric polarization](polarization-density.md). It has units of inverse [length](length.md). For collinear modes, $\Delta k=2\omega(n_{2\omega}-n_\omega)/c$, so matching [frequencies](frequency.md) alone does not ensure matching spatial [wave phases](phase-waves.md).
