# Coleman power series for a CM formal group

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

For a height-one [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) formal group and compatible $\mathfrak p^n$-division parameters $t_n$, an integral Coleman series interpolates a norm-compatible sequence of local units $u_n$. Its norm relation is multiplication of its translates by the $\mathfrak p$-torsion, with coefficient Frobenius where required by the unramified base. [Coates–Wiles homomorphisms](coates-wiles-homomorphism.md) and the [Frobenius-corrected logarithm](frobenius-corrected-logarithm.md) turn this local series into critical values and measures.
