# Miscibility criterion for a two-component condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Two-component Bose-Einstein condensate](two-component-bose-einstein-condensate.md)

In a homogeneous overlapping [two-component Bose-Einstein condensate](two-component-bose-einstein-condensate.md), the long-wavelength energy change at fixed species numbers is one half the integral of the density perturbation [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) with matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}U_{11}&U_{12}\\U_{12}&U_{22}\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Strict [positive definiteness](positive-definiteness.md) gives the displayed criterion. Strong positive cross-repulsion favors separation; sufficiently strong cross-attraction instead produces an unstable joint density mode. Equality is marginal at this order, not strict stability.
