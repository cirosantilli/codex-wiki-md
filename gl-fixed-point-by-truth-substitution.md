# GL fixed point by truth substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneous boxed fixed-point elimination in GL](simultaneous-boxed-fixed-point-elimination-in-gl.md)

Let $U=\Box Y(\top)$ and $V=\Box Y(U)$. Boxed replacement gives $\Box U\to(U\leftrightarrow V)$, and [positive introspection](positive-introspection.md) gives $U\to V$. For the reverse implication rewrite it as $V\to(\Box U\to U)$, necessitate, use $V\to\Box V$, and apply the Löb [axiom](axiom.md) to obtain $V\to\Box U\to U$. This explicit one-variable construction needs no [reflection](full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md) [axiom](axiom.md).
