# Ghost-scalar determinant cancellation in a complex quadratic gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition](complex-quadratic-abelian-gauge-condition.md)

A massless complex bosonic [charged scalar field](charged-scalar-field.md) with $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+iA_\mu$ contributes $[\det(-D^2)]^{-1}$ to a Gaussian background-field integral. A [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) pair contributes $\det\mathcal O_A$. On the formal [complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition](complex-quadratic-abelian-gauge-condition.md), $\mathcal O_A=-D^2$, so the two determinants cancel and their effective-action contributions sum to zero. Use matching regulators, boundary conditions and removed zero modes. This is a formal cancellation of closed loops; the [reality obstruction for a complex Euclidean gauge condition](reality-obstruction-for-a-complex-euclidean-gauge-condition.md) precludes deducing that physical [scalar quantum electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md) has no quantum effects.
