# Bregman iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Bregman divergence](bregman-divergence.md)

Starting with $u^0=0$ and $p^0=0\in\partial J(0)$, update $p^{k+1}=p^k+K^*(f-Ku^{k+1})/\alpha$. The optimality condition makes $p^{k+1}\in\partial J(u^{k+1})$. The iteration replaces a fixed penalty by its [Bregman distance](bregman-divergence.md) from the preceding iterate and accumulates residual information in the dual variable. The first update uses $k=0$.
