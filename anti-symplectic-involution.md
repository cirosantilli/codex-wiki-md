# Anti-symplectic involution

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-symplectic map](anti-symplectic-map.md)

An anti-symplectic involution is an [anti-symplectic map](anti-symplectic-map.md) whose square is the identity. Its nonempty [fixed-point set](fixed-point-set.md) is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md); the linear version has complementary $+1$ and $-1$ [Lagrangian subspaces](lagrangian-subspace.md).
