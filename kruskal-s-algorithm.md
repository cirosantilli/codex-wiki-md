<h1 id="kruskal-s-algorithm">Kruskal's algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruskal's_algorithm)

Process [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) in increasing weight, accepting an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) exactly when its endpoints lie in different current [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md). A rejected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) can never cross a later component's boundary, since components only merge. Thus each accepted [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) is lightest across the [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md) given by one current component. The [minimum spanning tree cut property](minimum-spanning-tree-cut-property.md) proves that the final [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) is minimum.
