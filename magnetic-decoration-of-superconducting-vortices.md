# Magnetic decoration of superconducting vortices

↑ **Parent:** [Abrikosov vortex lattice](abrikosov-vortex-lattice.md)

[U. Essmann](u-essmann.md) and [H. Träuble](h-trauble.md) reported triangular vortex arrays, including defects, using [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) on lead-indium and [niobium](niobium.md) samples in the remanent state.[Publisher abstract, materials, electron microscopy and remanent-state lattices](the-direct-observation-of-individual-flux-lines-in-type-ii-superconductors-by-u-essmann-and-h-trauble.md) Such a prepared state need not display the equilibrium array at a uniform applied field. Images of regular positions and a proof of unrestricted vortex motion would answer different questions.
