# Rank-one quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Rank of a quadratic form](rank-of-a-quadratic-form.md)

A rank-one quadratic form is the square of a [linear form](linear-functional.md) up to a scalar. More generally, a bilinear expression $(u^Tx)(v^Ty)$ is rank one because its coefficient matrix is the [outer product](outer-product.md) $uv^T$.
