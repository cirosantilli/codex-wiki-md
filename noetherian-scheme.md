# Noetherian scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_scheme)

A Noetherian scheme has a finite cover by affine schemes $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ with each $A_i$ a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). Equivalently, it is quasi-compact and locally Noetherian.
