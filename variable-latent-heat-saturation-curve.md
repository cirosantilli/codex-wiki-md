# Variable-latent-heat saturation curve

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md)

For a dilute [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) [vapor](vapor.md) whose specific volume greatly exceeds that of its condensate, the [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) integrates to the displayed formula. If $L(T)=L_*(T_c-T)/(T_c-T_*)$, the result is $\ln(p/p_*)=L_*[T_c(1/T_*-1/T)-\ln(T/T_*)]/[R(T_c-T_*)]$. Near the [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md), both the [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) approximation and the neglect of condensed-phase volume fail; zero [latent heat](latent-heat.md) alone does not determine the limiting coexistence slope.
