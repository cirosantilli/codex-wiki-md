# Termination of the Coulomb radial series

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

For a negative Coulomb energy, remove the [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) and regular radial power to obtain a confluent-hypergeometric [series](series-mathematics.md). Its recurrence terminates only when $n=q+\ell+1$, with $q$ a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md). Regularity at the origin and normalizability at infinity then give the hydrogenic [energy levels](energy-level.md). Without those physical [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) the radial [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) has other solutions.
