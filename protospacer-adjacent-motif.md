# Protospacer adjacent motif

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md)

A short target-adjacent [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) motif recognized by a CRISPR-associated nuclease. The motif requirement depends on the nuclease; the commonly used [Cas9](cas9.md) from [Streptococcus pyogenes](streptococcus-pyogenes.md) recognizes NGG.
