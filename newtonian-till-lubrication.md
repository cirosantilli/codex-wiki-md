# Newtonian till lubrication

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial till](subglacial-till.md)

A thin layer of [subglacial till](subglacial-till.md) idealized as a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) of [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\lambda\mu$ and thickness $l$ undergoes [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) between stationary bedrock and [ice](ice.md) moving at speed $u$. Its resisting basal [shear stress](shear-stress.md) has magnitude $\tau_b=\lambda\mu u/l$ and acts opposite to the sliding motion. Combining this drag with the hydrostatic driving force gives the bulk approximation used in the [friction-dominated grounding-line evolution law](friction-dominated-grounding-line-evolution-law.md).
