# Irregularity of a product of curves

↑ **Parent:** [Irregularity of an algebraic surface](irregularity-of-an-algebraic-surface.md)

For smooth projective curves $C$ and $D$, the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives

$$
q(C\times D)=g(C)+g(D).
$$
