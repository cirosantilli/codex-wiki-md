# Tadpole counterterm

↑ **Parent:** [Counterterm Lagrangian](counterterm-lagrangian.md)

A linear scalar [counterterm](counterterm.md) fixes the one-point function or the expansion point of the scalar potential. An unbroken [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) forbids this term when the scalar is a [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md). A vanishing one-loop [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) alone would not prove the all-orders restriction without the symmetry argument.
