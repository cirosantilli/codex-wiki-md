# Realification determinant identity

↑ **Parent:** [Realification of a complex matrix](realification-of-a-complex-matrix.md)

The [realification of a complex matrix](realification-of-a-complex-matrix.md) has real determinant $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2$. Indeed the invertible complex coordinate change $(x,y)\mapsto(x+iy,x-iy)$ conjugates its complexified block matrix to $\operatorname{diag}(A,\overline A)$. Taking determinants gives the identity, including singular matrices.
