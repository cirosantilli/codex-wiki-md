# Electron paramagnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)

[electron paramagnetic resonance](electron-paramagnetic-resonance.md) measures resonant transitions involving unpaired [electron](electron.md) magnetic moments. A spin label attached to a [protein](protein-split.md) or [lipid](lipid.md) can report local motion or distances under appropriately designed pulse experiments.
