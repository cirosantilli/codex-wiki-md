# Detrainment-limited gravity-current runout

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-current box model](gravity-current-box-model.md)

If a finite-volume [gravity current](gravity-current.md) loses or dilutes the scalar that supplies its [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md), its front can approach a finite [runout length of a gravity current](runout-length-of-a-gravity-current.md). A box model couples the scalar balance to a [gravity-current front condition](gravity-current-front-condition.md); eliminating time then gives the front position directly as a function of the remaining scalar concentration.
