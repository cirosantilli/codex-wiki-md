# Exposure therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

Planned contact with appropriate feared cues or situations to support new [learning](learning.md) and reduce maladaptive avoidance. Its rationale requires informed, collaborative tasks and [attention](attention.md) to the behaviors that could block [learning](learning.md); exposure does not mean deliberately creating genuine danger.
