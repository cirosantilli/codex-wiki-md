# Complement bundle on a complex Stiefel manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Stiefel manifold](complex-stiefel-manifold.md)

The orthogonal complements of the universal frames define a [complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md) of rank $n-k$ inside the trivial $\mathbb C^n$ bundle. The orthogonal projector $I-\sum_i v_iv_i^*$ is smooth of constant rank, giving local bundle frames. Its underlying real bundle has canonical orientation and rank $2(n-k)$. Its [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) is exactly $V_{k+1}(\mathbb C^n)$, by appending the unit vector in the complement to the frame.
