# Monad transformer

↑ **Parent:** [Computational monad](computational-monad.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monad_transformer)

A construction taking a [computational monad](computational-monad.md) $M$ to another [computational monad](computational-monad.md) $T M$, normally with a lift from $M A$ to $T M A$. The order of [monad transformers](monad-transformer.md) can affect which information survives an exception or a control transfer.
