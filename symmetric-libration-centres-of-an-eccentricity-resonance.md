# Symmetric libration centres of an eccentricity resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant-argument libration](resonant-argument-libration.md)

In the weak, non-crossing, leading-eccentricity planar model, exterior orders one to three have positive resonant [disturbing function](disturbing-function.md) coefficients $B_q$. Interior eccentricity-type coefficients of these orders have sign $(-1)^q$. A local resonant [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=-AJ^2/2-Be^q\cos\phi$, with $A>0$, gives $\ddot\phi=ABe^q\sin\phi$. [Linear stability analysis](linear-stability.md) therefore selects $\phi=\pi$ for $B>0$ and $\phi=0$ for $B<0$. Exterior symmetric centres are $\pi$; interior centres are $0,\pi,0$ for orders one, two and three. These are conditional leading-harmonic predictions, not universal centres at arbitrary [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md); [asymmetric resonant-argument libration](asymmetric-resonant-argument-libration.md) changes the picture.
