# Short Weierstrass form

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Over a field of characteristic other than two or three, every elliptic curve has a short Weierstrass equation $y^2=x^3+Ax+B$ with $4A^3+27B^2\ne0$.
