# Angle of incidence

↑ **Parent:** [Refraction](refraction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_of_incidence_(optics))

The [angle of incidence](angle-of-incidence.md) is the [angle](angle.md) between the incident [light ray](light-ray.md) and the line of the surface [normal vector](normal-vector.md) at the point of incidence. Use the acute [angle](angle.md) on the incident side, with $0\leq\theta_i<90^\circ$ for a nongrazing incident [light ray](light-ray.md). An [angle](angle.md) measured from the surface itself is its complement $90^\circ-\theta_i$. The same convention applies to [wavevectors](wavevector.md) in the transparent media with [isotropy](isotropy.md) considered here.
