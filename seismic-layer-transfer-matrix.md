# Seismic layer transfer matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md)

At normal incidence, a uniform lossless [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) layer of thickness $h_j$, speed $c_j$ and [seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md) $q_j=\rho_jc_j$ has [wave phase](phase-waves.md) thickness $\delta_j=\omega h_j/c_j$. With phasors proportional to $e^{-i\omega t}$, particle [velocity](velocity.md) $V$ and compressive [traction](traction.md) $P=-T$, the displayed [matrix](matrix.md) maps $(V,P)$ from the top to the bottom. It has [determinant](determinant.md) one. Multiplying these [matrices](matrix.md) in physical order accounts for all coherent multiple reflections. Continuity of $V,P$ connects adjacent layers without another propagation factor.
