# Confidence interval for a reciprocal rate

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence interval](confidence-interval.md)

If a positive rate $q$ has a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) $[L,U]$, monotonicity transforms it into $[1/U,1/L]$ for the reciprocal mean $1/q$. This preserves the coverage event exactly and reverses the endpoints. It applies directly to mean exponential [holding times](holding-time.md).
