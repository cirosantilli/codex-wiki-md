# Carryless multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial multiplication](polynomial-multiplication.md)

Carryless multiplication treats binary digits as coefficients of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) over the [field with two elements](field-with-two-elements.md). Products are formed by [polynomial multiplication](polynomial-multiplication.md), with [exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) combining equal-degree terms instead of carrying to the next binary position.
