# Derivative of matrix inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md)

On the open set of invertible square matrices, the inversion map is [differentiable](differentiable-map.md) and

$$
D(A\mapsto A^{-1})_A[H]=-A^{-1}HA^{-1}.
$$
