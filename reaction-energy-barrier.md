# Reaction energy barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Energy profile (chemistry)](energy-profile-chemistry.md)

A [reaction energy barrier](reaction-energy-barrier.md) is the height of a specified peak relative to the starting state in an [energy profile](energy-profile-chemistry.md). State which [energy](energy-split.md) is used and which direction is being considered. A schematic barrier is not automatically the empirical [activation energy](activation-energy.md) obtained from the temperature dependence of a [rate constant](rate-constant.md).
