# Constant baryon conversion mapping of halo and stellar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar-to-halo mass ratio](stellar-to-halo-mass-ratio.md)

If every counted halo hosts one counted galaxy and the baryon fraction and conversion efficiency are mass-independent without scatter, [galactic stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md) is a constant multiple of halo mass. The galaxy mass function is obtained by changing variable with $dM/dM_*=1/\epsilon_*$. Satellites, efficiency variation and scatter prevent this mapping from being generally exact.
