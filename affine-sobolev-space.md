# Affine Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

For prescribed boundary data $\varphi$, the affine Sobolev space

$$
\varphi+W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)
$$

consists of the $W^{1,p}$ functions whose [trace](sobolev-trace-theorem.md) equals the trace of $\varphi$. It is the natural admissible set for a variational [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md).
