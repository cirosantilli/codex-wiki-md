# Mutual inductance of a straight wire and square coil

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md)

An N-turn square coil of side a lies in the plane of a long straight wire, with nearest edge at perpendicular distance s and parallel to the wire. Integrating $B=\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$ across the coil gives flux-linkage magnitude $\Lambda=\mu_0NaI\ln[(s+a)/s]/(2\pi)$. Its coefficient of I is the [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md) magnitude; its sign depends on the selected loop normal and current directions. [Flux](flux.md) through one turn and N-turn [flux](flux.md) linkage must be distinguished.
