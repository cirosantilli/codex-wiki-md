# Double dissociation

↑ **Parent:** [Neuropsychology](neuropsychology.md)

Two interventions or patient groups show opposite relative impairments on two tasks. This constrains a simple account based only on general task difficulty, but does not prove that the underlying [neural circuits](neural-circuit.md) are independent: task sensitivity, lesion extent and alternative strategies still matter.
