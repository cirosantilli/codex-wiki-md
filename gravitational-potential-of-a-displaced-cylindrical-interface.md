# Gravitational potential of a displaced cylindrical interface

↑ **Parent:** [Critically rotating self-gravitating cylinder](critically-rotating-self-gravitating-cylinder.md)

For a uniform-[mass density](density.md) cylinder with surface displacement $\eta e^{im\phi}$, the interior and exterior potential perturbations solve [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md) and are proportional to $R^m$ and $R^{-m}$. Continuity of potential and the Poisson derivative jump $\Phi'_{R,\mathrm{out}}-\Phi'_{R,\mathrm{in}}=4\pi G\rho_0\eta$ give the displayed surface amplitude. The surface [mass density](density.md) term must be retained even though bulk incompressibility makes the interior [mass density](density.md) perturbation zero.
