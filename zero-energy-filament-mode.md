# Zero-energy filament mode

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal covariance of an elastic filament](thermal-covariance-of-an-elastic-filament.md)

A zero-energy mode lies in the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the quadratic [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) operator. For pure bending, zero curvature makes the displacement affine. Free-free endpoints therefore leave translation and tilt modes, while slope-constrained, force-free endpoints leave only translation. Both clamped-clamped and hinged-hinged endpoints remove these modes. An unrestricted modal amplitude has constant [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) weight, so no normalizable [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) or finite displacement [variance](variance-split.md) exists. Fixing the rigid degrees of freedom allows the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) on the positive complement.
