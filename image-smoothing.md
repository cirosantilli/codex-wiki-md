# Image smoothing

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion image processing](diffusion-image-processing.md)

[Image smoothing](image-smoothing.md) suppresses rapid fluctuations attributed to noise. Linear [heat equation](heat-equation.md) [image smoothing](image-smoothing.md) attenuates Fourier frequencies by $e^{-t|\xi|^2}$ but also blurs [image edges](image-edge.md). Nonlinear filtering uses [image signal](image-signal.md) geometry to distinguish fluctuations from meaningful transitions.
