# Resonant semi-major axis

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior mean-motion resonance](exterior-mean-motion-resonance.md)

The nominal [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) of an exterior $(p+q):p$ [mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md) follows from $(p+q)n_r=pn_p$ and [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md). Apsidal precession displaces the actual libration center because stationarity of the [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) requires $(p+q)n-pn_p-q\dot\varpi=0$.
