# k-Sperner family

↑ **Parent:** [Antichain](antichain.md)

A $k$-Sperner family contains no inclusion chain of length $k+1$. Every [maximal chain in a Boolean lattice](maximal-chain-in-a-boolean-lattice.md) therefore meets it in at most $k$ members, so its [Lubell mass](lubell-mass.md) is at most $k$.
