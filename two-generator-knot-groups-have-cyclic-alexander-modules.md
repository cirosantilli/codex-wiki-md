# Two-generator knot groups have cyclic Alexander modules

↑ **Parent:** [Knot group](knot-group.md)

The abelianization of a [knot group](knot-group.md) is infinite cyclic. Nielsen changes of a two-generator free basis reduce its exponent vector to $(1,0)$. In the cyclic-cover [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md), the first boundary is then $(t-1,0)$, whose kernel is a free rank-one Laurent [module](module-mathematics.md). Quotienting by the lifted relator boundaries gives a [cyclic module](cyclic-module.md), regardless of the number of relators. A noncyclic [Alexander module](alexander-module-of-a-knot.md) therefore obstructs every two-generator knot-group presentation.
