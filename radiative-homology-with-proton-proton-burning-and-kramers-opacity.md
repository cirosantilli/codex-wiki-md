# Radiative homology with proton-proton burning and Kramers opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md)

With [proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) energy generation $\epsilon\propto\rho T^4$ and [Kramers' opacity law](kramers-opacity-law.md) $\kappa\propto\rho T^{-7/2}$, [homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md) give

$$
R\propto M^{1/13},\qquad L\propto M^{71/13},\qquad
T_{\rm eff}\propto L^{69/284}.
$$

The [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) relation has $d\log L/d\log T_{\rm eff}=284/69$, with [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) increasing toward the left in the usual plotting convention.
