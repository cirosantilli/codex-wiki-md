# Continuity of probability

↑ **Parent:** [Probability measure](probability-measure.md)

For increasing or decreasing sequences of [events](event.md), the [probability](probability.md) of their union or intersection, respectively, is the limit of their probabilities. Increasing events follow from [countable additivity](countable-additivity.md) applied to successive disjoint differences; decreasing events follow by taking complements. This is [continuity from below of a measure](continuity-from-below-of-a-measure.md) and [continuity from above of a measure](continuity-from-above-of-a-measure.md) for a finite [probability measure](probability-measure.md).
