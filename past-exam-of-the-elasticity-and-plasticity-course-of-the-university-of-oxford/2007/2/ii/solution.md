<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [tension](../../../../tension.md) term $Tw_{xx}$ is the transverse restoring force from a nearly straight, tensioned rod. The bending term $-EIw_{xxxx}$ is the force from Euler-Bernoulli flexure, where $E$ is [Young's modulus](../../../../young-s-modulus.md) and $I$ the [second moment of area](../../../../second-moment-of-area.md). The right side is transverse [inertia](../../../../inertia.md). In the displayed one-dimensional equation $\rho$ denotes [linear mass density](../../../../linear-mass-density.md); if it denotes volumetric [density](../../../../density.md) instead, the inertial coefficient must include cross-sectional area.

For a [Fourier mode](../../../../fourier-mode.md), $w_{xx}=-k^2w$, $w_{xxxx}=k^4w$ and $w_{tt}=-\omega^2w$, giving

$$
\boxed{\rho\omega^2=Tk^2+EIk^4.}
$$

For $k>0$ the [phase speed](../../../../phase-speed.md) is $c_p=\omega/k=\sqrt{T/\rho+EIk^2/\rho}$, strictly increasing with $k$ when $EI>0$ and $T\geq0$. Thus shorter [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) propagate faster in [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md). The [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) $c_g=(T+2EIk^2)/\sqrt{\rho(T+EIk^2)}$ is also increasing, so the statement is consistent with wave-packet transport.

At a simple support the [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) and [bending moment](../../../../bending-moment.md) vanish: $w=0$ and $EIw_{xx}=0$ at $x=0,L$. The [eigenmodes](../../../../normal-mode.md) are $\sin(n\pi x/L)$ for $n=1,2,\ldots$. Setting $T=0$ in the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\omega_n=\sqrt{\frac{EI}\rho}\frac{n^2\pi^2}{L^2}.}
$$

The integer is positive for a nonzero mode; $n=0$ gives the identically zero sine function, and negative integers repeat the same modes. [Bending stiffness](../../../../bending-stiffness.md) makes the frequencies quadratic in mode number, unlike a purely tensioned string.
