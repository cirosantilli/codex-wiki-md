<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For dry [friction](../../../../friction.md), the tangential contact force opposes sliding and has limiting magnitude $\mu_fN_c$, where $N_c\geq0$ is the compressive [normal force](../../../../normal-force.md) and $\mu_f=\tan\phi$. Sticking requires $|F|\leq\mu_fN_c$; impending sliding reaches equality.

The cohesionless [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](../../../../mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md) applies the same local condition on internal planes: yield begins when some plane has $|\tau_{\rm shear}|=N_c\tan\phi$. In the tension-positive Cauchy convention used here, $N_c=-N$ for a compressive plane, so

$$
\boxed{|F|\leq-N\tan\phi\quad(N\leq0),\qquad |F|=-N\tan\phi\text{ at yield}.}
$$

A cohesive material would add a positive intercept, but the later formulas in this paper are explicitly the zero-[cohesion](../../../../cohesion.md) case. The model does not supply a tensile strength law.
