<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Airy stress function](../../../../../airy-stress-function.md) determines the in-[plane stress](../../../../../plane-stress.md) through its second derivatives. If two functions give the same [stress](../../../../../stress.md), their difference $B$ has $B_{xx}=B_{xy}=B_{yy}=0$. On a connected domain this implies that its gradient is constant. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A\mapsto A+ax+by+c}
$$

is exactly the local affine gauge freedom. It also preserves biharmonicity. An imposed decay condition for $A$ can fix this freedom; adding an arbitrary biharmonic function would usually change the physical [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and is not a [gauge transformation](../../../../../gauge-transformation.md).
