<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Plane strain](../../../../plane-strain.md) sets all [strains](../../../../strain.md) involving $z$ to zero, including $e_{zz}=0$, while $e_{xx}=u_x$, $e_{yy}=v_y$ and $e_{xy}=(u_y+v_x)/2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\tau=\begin{pmatrix}
(\lambda+2\mu)u_x+\lambda v_y&\mu(u_y+v_x)&0\\
\mu(u_y+v_x)&\lambda u_x+(\lambda+2\mu)v_y&0\\
0&0&\lambda(u_x+v_y)
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

In particular, $\tau_{zz}$ generally does not vanish: the suppressed out-of-[plane strain](../../../../plane-strain.md) requires a reaction [stress](../../../../stress.md). Setting that entry to zero would instead be a plane-[stress](../../../../stress.md) assumption.
