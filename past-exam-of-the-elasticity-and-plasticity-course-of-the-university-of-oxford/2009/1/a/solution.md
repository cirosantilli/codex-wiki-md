↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [infinitesimal strain tensor](../../../../infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) is the symmetric part of the [displacement gradient tensor](../../../../displacement-gradient-tensor.md):

$$
\boxed{e_{ij}=\frac12\left(\frac{\partial u_i}{\partial x_j}+\frac{\partial u_j}{\partial x_i}\right).}
$$

Its diagonal entries are infinitesimal normal extensions; twice an off-diagonal entry is the corresponding engineering shear [strain](../../../../strain.md). An infinitesimal rigid rotation has an antisymmetric [displacement gradient tensor](../../../../displacement-gradient-tensor.md) and therefore contributes no linearized [strain](../../../../strain.md).
