<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Apply [energy uniqueness for a linear elastic traction problem](../../../../energy-uniqueness-for-a-linear-elastic-traction-problem.md). Subtract two solutions with the same loading, writing their [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) difference as $\mathbf v$. Linearity makes its [body force](../../../../body-force.md) and [boundary traction](../../../../traction.md) zero. The preceding energy identity therefore yields $\int_B2W(e(\mathbf v))\,dV=0$. [Positive definiteness](../../../../positive-definiteness.md) and continuity imply $e(\mathbf v)=0$ everywhere. By the permitted zero-strain characterization,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v=\mathbf a+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x.}
$$

Thus the solution is unique modulo infinitesimal rigid [translations](../../../../translation-geometry.md) and [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), on each connected body. Conversely adding such a [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) changes neither [strain](../../../../strain.md) nor [stress](../../../../stress.md), so these are exactly the unconstrained modes. This is conditional [uniqueness](../../../../uniqueness.md); existence of a pure-[traction](../../../../traction.md) solution additionally requires balanced resultant force and [torque](../../../../torque.md).
