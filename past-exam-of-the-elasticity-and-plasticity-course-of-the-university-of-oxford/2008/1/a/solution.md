<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [infinitesimal strain](../../../../infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) is $e_{ij}=(u_{i,j}+u_{j,i})/2$. A constant [translation](../../../../translation-geometry.md) has zero [gradient](../../../../gradient.md). For the remaining term, $u_i=\epsilon_{ikl}\omega_kx_l$ has $u_{i,j}=\epsilon_{ikj}\omega_k$, a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{e_{ij}=0.}
$$

Physically $\mathbf a$ is a [translation](../../../../translation-geometry.md) and $\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x$ is an infinitesimal [rigid rotation](../../../../rigid-transformation.md), with axis along $\boldsymbol\omega$ and [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) angle $|\boldsymbol\omega|$. It is the linearized [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) [displacement](../../../../displacement.md), not the exact [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) of a general finite [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). This distinction matters because the [infinitesimal strain](../../../../infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) omits quadratic [displacement](../../../../displacement.md)-[gradient](../../../../gradient.md) terms.
