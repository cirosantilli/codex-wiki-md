<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose zero energy in the unstrained state. The required [strain energy density](../../../../strain-energy-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{W(e)=\frac\lambda2(\operatorname{tr}e)^2+\mu e:e.}
$$

Its [variation](../../../../variation.md) is $\delta W=(\lambda\operatorname{tr}(e)I+2\mu e):\delta e$, giving the prescribed [stress](../../../../stress.md) as the [tensor](../../../../tensor.md) [gradient](../../../../gradient.md). This variational notation handles the symmetric off-diagonal components without a spurious factor of two.

Decompose $e=e^{\rm dev}+(\operatorname{tr}e)I/3$ into [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) deviatoric and spherical parts. Then

$$
\boxed{W=\mu\,e^{\rm dev}:e^{\rm dev}+\frac{3\lambda+2\mu}{6}(\operatorname{tr}e)^2.}
$$

The two squares vanish together only for $e=0$. Thus $\mu>0$ and $3\lambda+2\mu>0$ are sufficient for [positive definiteness](../../../../positive-definiteness.md). They are necessary by testing a nonzero [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md)-free [strain](../../../../strain.md) and a pure [dilation](../../../../uniform-dilation.md) separately. Equivalently both [shear modulus](../../../../shear-modulus.md) $\mu$ and [bulk modulus](../../../../bulk-modulus.md) $K=\lambda+2\mu/3$ must be positive.
