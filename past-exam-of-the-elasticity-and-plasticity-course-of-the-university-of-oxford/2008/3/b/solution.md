<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\boxed{\xi=s/L,\quad\lambda=PL^2/(\pi^2EI)}$. The endpoint interval becomes $[0,1]$, and multiplication of the dimensional equation by $L^2/EI$ gives

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\xi\xi}+\pi^2\lambda\sin\theta=0,\qquad\theta(0)=\theta(1)=0.}
$$

The energy in these variables is $U=(EI/L)\int_0^1[\theta_\xi^2/2-\pi^2\lambda(1-\cos\theta)]\,d\xi$. Thus $\lambda$ measures load relative to the first linearized [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) for the angular endpoint conditions actually imposed.
