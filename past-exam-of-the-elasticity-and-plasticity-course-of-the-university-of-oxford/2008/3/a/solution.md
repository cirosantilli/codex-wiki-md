<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an admissible [variation](../../../../variation.md) $\eta$ with $\eta(0)=\eta(L)=0$,

$$
\delta U=\int_0^L(EI\theta'\eta'-P\sin\theta\,\eta)\,ds=-\int_0^L(EI\theta''+P\sin\theta)\eta\,ds.
$$

Stationarity for every [variation](../../../../variation.md) therefore gives the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{EI\theta''+P\sin\theta=0.}
$$

The first energy term is [bending energy](../../../../bending-energy.md): the [curvature](../../../../curvature.md) of an inextensible planar beam is $\theta_s$. The shortening relative to the straight reference is $\int(1-\cos\theta)\,ds$. A prescribed compressive [dead load](../../../../dead-loading.md) does positive work on that shortening, so its potential-energy contribution is minus $P$ times that integral. A [minimizer](../../../../global-minimizer.md) must satisfy the stationary equation, but a stationary solution need not be a [minimizer](../../../../global-minimizer.md).
