<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $z$ positive upwards, so the source's sign convention makes $p$ a downward applied load. The small-[slope](../../../../slope.md) [tension](../../../../tension.md) contribution on a short interval is $Tw''$. Let $r\geq0$ be the upward contact reaction. Force balance gives $Tw''-p+r=0$. [Nonpenetration](../../../../nonpenetration-constraint.md) is $w\geq f$, and reaction is absent where $w>f$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{w-f\geq0,\qquad Tw''-p=-r\leq0,\qquad(w-f)(Tw''-p)=0.}
$$

The contact boundary must have continuous $w$ to avoid a broken string, and continuous [slope](../../../../slope.md) in this smooth-contact model with no concentrated point force. Integrating force balance over a vanishing interval around a contact endpoint gives $T[w']=0$ when the load and reaction are locally bounded. This is the one-dimensional [obstacle problem](../../../../obstacle-problem.md), with its reaction acting as a nonnegative [constraint](../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) force.
