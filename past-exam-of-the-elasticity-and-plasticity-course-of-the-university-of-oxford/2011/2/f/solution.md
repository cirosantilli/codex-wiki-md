<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The material occupies $Y\geq0$, so its outward [unit normal](../../../../unit-normal.md) at the surface is $n=-e_Y$. A free surface has zero [traction](../../../../traction.md), $\tau n=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\tau_{XY}(X,0,t)=0,\qquad\tau_{YY}(X,0,t)=0,\qquad\tau_{ZY}(X,0,t)=0.}
$$

The third condition is automatic for the two-dimensional [displacement field](../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md) with zero third component and no dependence on the third coordinate. The first two impose zero shear and normal [stress](../../../../stress.md) at the free surface.
