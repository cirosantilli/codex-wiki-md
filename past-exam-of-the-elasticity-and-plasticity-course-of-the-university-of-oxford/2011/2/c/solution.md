<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Divide the [Navier-Cauchy equation](../../../../navier-cauchy-equation.md) by [mass density](../../../../density.md). Since $\mu/\rho=c_T^2$ and $(\lambda+\mu)/\rho=c_L^2-c_T^2$, it becomes

$$
\boxed{u_{tt}=c_T^2\Delta u+(c_L^2-c_T^2)\nabla\operatorname{div}u.}
$$

Equivalently, using the [vector calculus](../../../../vector-calculus.md) identity $\Delta u=\nabla\operatorname{div}u-\nabla\times(\nabla\times u)$,

$$
\boxed{u_{tt}=c_L^2\nabla\operatorname{div}u-c_T^2\nabla\times(\nabla\times u).}
$$

The [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) and [curl](../../../../curl.md) consequently propagate at $c_L$ and $c_T$, respectively.
