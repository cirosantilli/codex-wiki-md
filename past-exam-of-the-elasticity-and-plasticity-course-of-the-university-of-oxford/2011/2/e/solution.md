<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the $b_1$ root the [elastic wave polarization](../../../../elastic-wave-polarization.md) is parallel to the complex [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) vector $(ik,-b_1)$, giving

$$
\boxed{B_1=\frac{i b_1}{k}A_1.}
$$

For the $b_2$ root the field has zero [divergence](../../../../divergence.md), $ikA_2-b_2B_2=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{B_2=\frac{ik}{b_2}A_2.}
$$

These relations also follow by inserting each decay root into either row of the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md). The complete decaying [displacement field](../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md) has complex components

$$
U(Y)=A_1e^{-b_1Y}+A_2e^{-b_2Y},\qquad
V(Y)=\frac{ib_1}{k}A_1e^{-b_1Y}+\frac{ik}{b_2}A_2e^{-b_2Y},
$$

multiplied by $e^{ik(X-ct)}$, followed by the [real part](../../../../real-part.md). The first constituent is irrotational and the second is divergence-free; both are needed to cancel the two surface [tractions](../../../../traction.md).
