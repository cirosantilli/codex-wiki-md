<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Write the in-plane [displacement field](../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md) as $(u,v)$. The [infinitesimal strain tensor](../../../../infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) gives $2e_{XY}=u_Y+v_X$, $e_{YY}=v_Y$, and $\operatorname{tr}e=u_X+v_Y$. The two nontrivial zero-[traction](../../../../traction.md) conditions become

$$
\boxed{u_Y+v_X=0,\qquad\lambda u_X+(\lambda+2\mu)v_Y=0\quad(Y=0).}
$$

Equivalently for the [complex amplitudes](../../../../complex-amplitude.md) they are $U'(0)+ikV(0)=0$ and $ik\lambda U(0)+(\lambda+2\mu)V'(0)=0$. Because the [constitutive equation](../../../../constitutive-equation.md) is real and linear, vanishing of these complex [amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) enforces the conditions at every position and time.
