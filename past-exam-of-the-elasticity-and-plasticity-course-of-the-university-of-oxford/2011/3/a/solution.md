<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $s$ be [arc length](../../../../arc-length.md), so [inextensibility](../../../../inextensible-filament.md) makes $r'=d_3$ a [unit tangent vector](../../../../unit-tangent-vector.md). The [curvature](../../../../curvature.md) is $\kappa=\theta'$, hence $m'=EI\theta''e_z$ for uniform [bending stiffness](../../../../bending-stiffness.md). [Balance of linear momentum](../../../../balance-of-linear-momentum.md) in static equilibrium gives a constant resultant $n=Fe_x+Ge_y$.

Taking the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) directly,

$$
d_3\times n=(\cos\theta\,e_x+\sin\theta\,e_y)\times(Fe_x+Ge_y)
=(G\cos\theta-F\sin\theta)e_z.
$$

The scalar component of [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) therefore becomes

$$
\boxed{EI\theta''+G\cos\theta-F\sin\theta=0.}
$$

Together with $x'=\cos\theta$ and $y'=\sin\theta$, this [Euler elastica](../../../../euler-elastica.md) equation determines the centerline once the appropriate [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) are supplied.
