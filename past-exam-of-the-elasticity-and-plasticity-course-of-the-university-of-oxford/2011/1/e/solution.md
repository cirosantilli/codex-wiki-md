<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the axisymmetric [deformation map](../../../../deformation-map.md), take $\theta=\Theta$, $z=Z$, and $r=r(R)$. A reference cylindrical shell of unit axial length has [volume](../../../../volume.md) $2\pi R\,dR$, and the corresponding current shell has [volume](../../../../volume.md) $2\pi r\,dr$. [Elastic incompressibility](../../../../elastic-incompressibility.md) therefore gives $r\,dr=R\,dR$.

Integrating from the inner surface, where $r(A)=a$, gives $r^2-a^2=R^2-A^2$. The positive radius is consequently

$$
\boxed{r(R)=\sqrt{R^2+a^2-A^2},\qquad A\leq R\leq B.}
$$

The outer surface is not an additional independent unknown: $\boxed{b=\sqrt{B^2+a^2-A^2}}$, and hence $h=b-a$.
