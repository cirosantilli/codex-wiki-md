<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $B=A+H$ for the reference outer radius and $b=a+h$ for the current outer radius. The inner material normal is $-e_r$. A fluid [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) $P$ applies [traction](../../../../traction.md) $-Pn=Pe_r$ there, so the radial [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md) component must be negative:

$$
\boxed{t_r(a)=-P,\qquad t_r(b)=0.}
$$

The second condition uses zero external gauge [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md). Both faces also have zero shear [traction](../../../../traction.md); the axisymmetric diagonal [stress](../../../../stress.md) field satisfies those conditions automatically. The axial constraint is [plane strain](../../../../plane-strain.md), so supports may supply an axial reaction; it does not imply zero axial [stress](../../../../stress.md).
