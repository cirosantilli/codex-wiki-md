<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [current configuration](../../../../current-configuration.md), [balance of linear momentum](../../../../balance-of-linear-momentum.md) is the [Cauchy momentum equation](../../../../cauchy-momentum-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{div}_x\tau+\rho b=\rho\left(\partial_t v+(v\cdot\nabla_x)v\right).}
$$

Here $\tau$ is the [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md), $\rho$ is current [mass density](../../../../density.md), $b$ is [body force](../../../../body-force.md) per unit [mass](../../../../mass.md), and $v(x,t)$ is the [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) of the material particle currently at $x$. The expression in parentheses is the [material acceleration](../../../../material-acceleration.md). The [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) is taken in current spatial coordinates, with $(\operatorname{div}\tau)_i=\partial_j\tau_{ij}$.

Equivalently the right side is $\rho\partial_t^2x(X,t)$ evaluated at the particle, because this is the same [material acceleration](../../../../material-acceleration.md) in [Lagrangian coordinates](../../../../lagrangian-coordinate.md). For the subsequent static inflation with negligible [body force](../../../../body-force.md), the equation reduces to $\operatorname{div}\tau=0$.
