<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [deformation gradient](../../../../deformation-gradient.md) is the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) of the [deformation map](../../../../deformation-map.md) with respect to the [reference configuration](../../../../reference-configuration.md) coordinates, at fixed time:

$$
\boxed{F_{iA}(X,t)=\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial X_A}(X,t),\qquad dx=F\,dX.}
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $J=\det F$ is the local ratio of current to reference [volume](../../../../volume.md): $dv=J\,dV$ for an orientation-preserving [deformation in continuum mechanics](../../../../deformation-mechanics.md). Thus [elastic incompressibility](../../../../elastic-incompressibility.md) is the pointwise constraint $\boxed{J=1}$, rather than a requirement that each individual [principal stretch](../../../../principal-stretch.md) be one.
