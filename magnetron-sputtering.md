# Magnetron sputtering

↑ **Parent:** [Sputtering](sputtering.md)

A sputter-deposition method using a magnetic field near a target to confine [electrons](electron.md) and sustain ionization efficiently. Multiple targets permit co-deposition. A local flux proportional to target [power](power.md) is a useful calibration model over a restricted range, rather than a universal linear law for composition or thickness.
