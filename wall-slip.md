# Wall slip

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

Relative tangential motion between material and its solid boundary. In [rheometry](rheometry.md) it can produce an apparent shear rate greater than the actual bulk rate and distort inferred [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) or modulus.
