# Primordial black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole-split.md)

A [black hole](black-hole-split.md) formed in the early universe rather than as the endpoint of a star. It is not subject to the ordinary stellar-remnant [baryon](baryon.md) budget in the same way as a [black hole](black-hole-split.md) formed from a baryonic progenitor. Formation scenarios and observational constraints depend strongly on its [mass](mass.md).
