# Cubic polytropic interior

↑ **Parent:** [Polytrope of index one](polytrope-of-index-one.md)

The interior [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) for a [polytrope of index one](polytrope-of-index-one.md) also admits a positive solution vanishing on the faces of a cube:

$$
\rho(x,y,z)=\rho_c\sin\frac{\pi x}{L}\sin\frac{\pi y}{L}\sin\frac{\pi z}{L},
\qquad L^2=\frac{3\pi K}{2G}.
$$

Its central value is $\rho_c$ and its average is $\bar\rho=8\rho_c/\pi^3$. Together with $\Phi=C-2K\rho$, it solves the interior [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md). A local interior solution need not match the external field of its own mass.
