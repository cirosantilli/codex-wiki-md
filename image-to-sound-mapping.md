# Image-to-sound mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Sensory substitution system](sensory-substitution-system.md)

A rule converting image properties into acoustic features. One column-scanning example assigns vertical row to $f_i$, horizontal column to scan time, and brightness to [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $A(I_{ij})$, yielding $s_j(t)=\sum_i A(I_{ij})\sin(2\pi f_it+\phi_i)$. Distinct orthogonal tones can preserve column coefficients in ideal conditions, while finite windows, masking and limited [hearing](hearing.md) resolution constrain [perception](perception.md).
