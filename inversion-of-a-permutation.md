# Inversion of a permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

For a [permutation](permutation.md) $\pi$ of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, an inversion is a pair $i<j$ with $\pi(i)>\pi(j)$. Its number equals the [Coxeter length](coxeter-length.md) for adjacent [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md): each adjacent swap changes the count by one, and swapping adjacent descents sorts the permutation in exactly that many steps.
