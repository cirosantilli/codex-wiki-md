# Residual finiteness of a free group

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

Every finitely generated [free group](free-group.md) is a [residually finite group](residually-finite-group.md). For $F_2$, the [ping-pong lemma](ping-pong-lemma.md) embeds it into $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$, and [reduction modulo a prime in an integral matrix group](reduction-modulo-a-prime-in-an-integral-matrix-group.md) separates each nonidentity element in a finite quotient.
