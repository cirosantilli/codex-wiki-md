# Contact-interaction Hartree-Fock spin energy

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock electron gas](hartree-fock-electron-gas.md)

In a uniform three-dimensional [Hartree-Fock electron gas](hartree-fock-electron-gas.md) with repulsive contact coupling $\gamma$, a spin-independent neutralizing background can cancel the [Hartree energy](hartree-energy.md). The remaining same-spin exchange term is negative and quadratic in each [spin](spin.md) [density](density.md). Integrating the filled single-spin [Fermi sphere](fermi-sphere.md) gives $A=3\hbar^2(6\pi^2)^{2/3}/(10m)$. At fixed total [density](density.md) the exchange term favors [spin polarization](spin-polarization.md), whereas the kinetic term opposes it.
