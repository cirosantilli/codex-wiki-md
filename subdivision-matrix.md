# Subdivision matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision mask](subdivision-mask.md)

A local [subdivision matrix](subdivision-matrix.md) maps the [control points](control-point.md) in a refinement-invariant neighborhood to the refined neighborhood. Row sums one express constant reproduction. Its constant [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) one; remaining [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) govern decay of shape modes. Near an [extraordinary subdivision vertex](extraordinary-subdivision-vertex.md), tangent modes and a [characteristic map of a subdivision surface](characteristic-map-of-a-subdivision-surface.md) are needed to test geometric smoothness, beyond simple convergence.
