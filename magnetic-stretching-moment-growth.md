# Magnetic stretching moment growth

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian white-noise magnetic stretching](gaussian-white-noise-magnetic-stretching.md)

Twice integrating the [radial magnetic-field Fokker-Planck equation](radial-magnetic-field-fokker-planck-equation.md) by parts gives the displayed law when moments and endpoint terms are controlled. In particular $\langle B^2\rangle$ grows at rate $10D=2\gamma$, with $\gamma=5\kappa_2/4$. The corresponding [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) density is $\langle B^2\rangle/(8\pi)$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md).
