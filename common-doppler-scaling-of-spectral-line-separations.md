# Common Doppler scaling of spectral-line separations

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md)

If two [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) come from the same uniformly moving source along the same line of sight, each [wavelength](wavelength.md) is multiplied by the same longitudinal [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md) $D$. Subtracting $\lambda_{2,\rm obs}=D\lambda_{2,\rm emit}$ and $\lambda_{1,\rm obs}=D\lambda_{1,\rm emit}$ proves the displayed identity. Recession has $D>1$ and approach has $D<1$. Defining positive radial velocity for recession gives $\beta=(D^2-1)/(D^2+1)$. A measured increase in wavelength separation therefore cannot be reconciled with strictly approaching longitudinal motion merely by changing the speed's magnitude.
