<h1 id="nielsen-schreier-theorem">Nielsen–Schreier theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen–Schreier_theorem)

Every [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a [free group](free-group.md) is itself a [free group](free-group.md). The theorem follows by representing the subgroup by a connected [covering graph](covering-graph.md) of a rose and choosing a maximal subtree.
