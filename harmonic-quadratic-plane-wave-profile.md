# Harmonic quadratic plane-wave profile

↑ **Parent:** [Plane-fronted gravitational wave](plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md)

A homogeneous quadratic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in the transverse coordinates $x,y$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) exactly when the sum of its $x^2$ and $y^2$ coefficients is zero. Thus it has the displayed form. For the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $du\,dv+dx^2+dy^2$, a [metric perturbation](linearized-gravity-split.md) $h=\psi\,du^2$ has zero [trace](matrix-trace.md), zero [divergence](divergence.md) and $\Box h=0$, since $\partial_v\psi=0$ and $\partial_x^2\psi+\partial_y^2\psi=0$. The two coefficient [functions](function-split.md) describe the two [gravitational wave polarizations](gravitational-wave-polarization.md).
