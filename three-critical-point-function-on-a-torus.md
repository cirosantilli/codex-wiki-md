# Three-critical-point function on a torus

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)

Take $u,v$ modulo $\pi$; shifting either coordinate by $\pi$ leaves $h$ unchanged. Its partial derivatives are $h_u=\sin v\sin(2u-v)$ and $h_v=\sin u\sin(u-2v)$. The only [critical points](critical-point.md) are $(0,0)$, $(\pi/3,2\pi/3)$ and $(2\pi/3,\pi/3)$. The latter are a nondegenerate minimum and maximum, with values $\mp3\sqrt3/8$. At the origin the leading cubic is $uv(u-v)$, linearly equivalent to a [monkey saddle](monkey-saddle.md); its [critical point](critical-point.md) is isolated but degenerate. Thus three smooth [critical points](critical-point.md) coexist with the Morse lower bound of four for the [torus](torus.md).
