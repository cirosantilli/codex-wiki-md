# Magnetized-fluid tensor virial theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

For an isolated self-gravitating fluid obeying [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), let $I_{ij}=\int\rho x_ix_jdV$ and $K_{ij}=\frac12\int\rho u_iu_jdV$. Use the force-stress convention $\rho D_tu_i=\partial_jT_{ij}$, with [pressure](pressure.md), magnetic and negative [Newtonian gravitational stress tensor](newtonian-gravitational-stress-tensor.md) contributions. Two time derivatives of $I$ give $4K_{ij}$ plus $\int(x_i\partial_kT_{jk}+x_j\partial_kT_{ik})dV$. Integration by parts and symmetry yield the theorem when surface terms vanish. This fluid version complements the collisionless formulation of the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md).
