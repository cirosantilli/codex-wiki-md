# Historical source for Chernoff information

↑ **Parent:** [Chernoff information](chernoff-information.md)

Stanford's archive records a technical report by [Herman Chernoff](herman-chernoff.md), dated November 1951, on asymptotic efficiency of hypothesis tests based on sums of observations.[Stanford Statistics technical-report record: SOL ONR 6, author H. Chernoff, report date November 1951](a-measure-of-asymptotic-efficiency-for-tests-of-an-hypothesis-based-on-the-sum-of-observations-technical-report-by-herman-chernoff.md) This is historical source metadata, rather than a claim to have inspected the report's full proof. The modern theorem cited above connects that efficiency question to the [log-likelihood ratio](log-likelihood-ratio.md) accumulated across repeated observations.
