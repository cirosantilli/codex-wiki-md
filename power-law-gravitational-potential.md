# Power-law gravitational potential

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md)

A radial [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) $\Phi=-Ar^{-\beta}$ produces inward acceleration $A\beta r^{-\beta-1}$. The point-mass case has $\beta=1$ and $A=GM$. Treating the [power-law gravitational potential](power-law-gravitational-potential.md) as prescribed does not require it to be sourced by the flowing gas.
