# Locally free resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md)

A locally free resolution of a [sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules-split.md) $F$ is an [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) $\cdots\to G_2\to G_1\to G_0\to F\to0$ with each $G_i$ a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md). If every [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) is the quotient of a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) of finite rank on a [noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md), iteratively taking a [kernel sheaf](kernel-sheaf.md) constructs such a resolution with [coherent sheaves](coherent-sheaf.md) at every stage. The [kernel sheaves](kernel-sheaf.md) need not be [locally free sheaves](locally-free-sheaf.md), so a finite resolution requires additional hypotheses. A uniform bound on [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) can nevertheless make a finite initial segment sufficient for [dimension shifting in sheaf cohomology](dimension-shifting-in-sheaf-cohomology.md).
