# Hill-Robertson interference

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

Reduced efficacy of [natural selection](natural-selection.md) caused by associations between selected [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) in finite populations. [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) can relieve some of this interference by changing the associations.
