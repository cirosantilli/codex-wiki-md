# Axisymmetric magnetic flux surface

↑ **Parent:** [Poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md)

A regular level surface of the axisymmetric [poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md) contains both the poloidal and toroidal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) directions. Conservation along field lines can be expressed locally as a function of the flux label on connected regular surfaces. Degenerate points with no [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md) and disconnected branches with the same label require separate treatment.
