# Morse theory

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_theory)

Morse theory relates the [critical points](critical-point.md) of a [Morse function](morse-function.md) to changes in the [homotopy type](homotopy-type.md) of its sublevel sets. Crossing a nondegenerate critical value attaches a cell or handle of dimension equal to the [Morse index](morse-index.md). The theory also applies to [geodesic energy](energy-of-a-curve.md) through broken-geodesic approximations.
