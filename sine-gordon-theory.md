# Sine-Gordon theory

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

A relativistic [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) theory with a periodic cosine potential. In physical coordinates $y^\mu$, one normalization is $\mathcal L=\tfrac12\partial_\mu\varphi\partial^\mu\varphi-m^2(1-\cos\beta\varphi)/\beta^2$. With $\phi=\beta\varphi$ and $x^\mu=my^\mu$, its [action](action.md) is $S=\beta^{-2}\int d^2x[\tfrac12(\partial\phi)^2+\cos\phi-1]$. Its [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is the [Sine-Gordon equation](sine-gordon-equation.md). Distinct [scalar-field vacua](scalar-field-vacuum.md) differ by $2\pi$ in $\phi$, permitting a [Sine-Gordon kink](sine-gordon-kink.md).
