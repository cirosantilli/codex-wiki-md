# Albedo

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albedo)

[Albedo](albedo.md) is the fraction of incident [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md) [power](power.md) reflected, summed over the outgoing directions included in the measurement. For incident [power](power.md) $P_{\rm in}>0$ and reflected [power](power.md) $P_{\rm ref}$,

$$
\alpha=\frac{P_{\rm ref}}{P_{\rm in}},\qquad 0\leq\alpha\leq1.
$$

Thus $\alpha=0.30$ means 30 percent reflected. For an opaque surface the remaining 70 percent is absorbed; for a transmitting layer, transmission must also be accounted for. Specify the incident [wavelengths](wavelength.md) and illumination geometry: a visible-band [surface albedo](surface-albedo.md) and a sunlight-weighted broadband [surface albedo](surface-albedo.md) need not agree. NASA's definition explicitly allows a specified spectral band. [NASA, My NASA Data: Albedo Values](https://mynasadata.larc.nasa.gov/basic-page/albedo-values)

For a whole [planet](planet.md), broadband [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) sums reflected [power](power.md) over all outgoing directions and all incident [wavelengths](wavelength.md), weighted by the illuminating spectrum. It is the fraction needed for a whole-planet absorbed-sunlight budget. A local [surface albedo](surface-albedo.md) describes one surface under specified illumination; it cannot simply replace the [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) of [Earth](earth.md), whose clouds and [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md) also reflect and absorb sunlight. Nor is [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) the same as [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md), which compares brightness in a particular viewing geometry. NASA's account of [Earth](earth.md)'s budget separates reflection by clouds, particles and the surface from absorption within the [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md) and at the surface. [NASA Earth Observatory, Climate and Earth's Energy Budget, 2009](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/climate-and-earths-energy-budget/)

The usefulness of [albedo](albedo.md) is that it turns optical behavior into an [energy](energy.md) budget. Bright [snow](snow.md) and [sea ice](sea-ice.md) usually absorb less sunlight than exposed water under comparable illumination, but their actual [surface albedos](surface-albedo.md) depend on the spectrum and surface conditions. The [ice-ocean albedo feedback](ice-ocean-albedo-feedback.md) makes that optical property part of the dynamics, while the following [zero-dimensional planetary energy balance](zero-dimensional-planetary-energy-balance.md) isolates the geometry and competing [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md).
