# Halo baryon-to-star conversion efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md)

The integrated [halo star-formation efficiency](halo-baryon-to-star-conversion-efficiency.md) is the fraction of a [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)'s reference baryonic mass converted into stars: $\epsilon_*=M_*/(f_bM_h)$, with [cosmic baryon fraction](cosmic-baryon-fraction.md) $f_b$. It is distinct from [star-formation efficiency per free-fall time](star-formation-efficiency-per-free-fall-time.md). Cooling, gas supply and retention, [stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md) and [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md) make it strongly dependent on [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) mass. With stellar [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) $\Upsilon_*$, a simple central-galaxy mapping is $L=\epsilon_*f_bM_h/\Upsilon_*$. Changing this efficiency changes both the mapping and the logarithmic Jacobian between the [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) mass function and the [luminosity function](luminosity-function-astronomy.md).
