# Chern insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum matter](topological-quantum-matter-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_insulator)

A [Chern insulator](chern-insulator.md) is a two-dimensional [band insulator](band-insulator.md) whose occupied [energy bands](energy-band.md) have a nonzero total [Chern number](chern-number.md). It has a bulk [band gap](band-gap.md), yet an interface with a topologically trivial [band insulator](band-insulator.md) carries a net excess of [quantum Hall edge states](quantum-hall-edge-state.md) propagating in one direction. [Time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md) must be broken. The [Haldane model](haldane-model.md) shows that a nonzero average [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) is unnecessary.

Why distinguish two solids that both have a [band gap](band-gap.md)? The [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) tell us whether an excitation costs [energy](energy-split.md); the [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) also describe how the state changes around the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). Their [Berry curvature](berry-curvature.md) can integrate to a nonzero integer. A small deformation changes the [Berry curvature](berry-curvature.md) continuously, but cannot continuously change an integer. A [Chern insulator](chern-insulator.md) and a trivial [band insulator](band-insulator.md) therefore cannot be connected through a path of isolated occupied [energy bands](energy-band.md) with a common bulk [band gap](band-gap.md). The distinction survives sufficiently weak local perturbations. It does not make every microscopic quantity or every boundary state immune to every disturbance.

For a single filled [energy band](energy-band.md), set the [Berry connection](berry-connection.md) to $\mathcal A_j=i\langle u_-|\partial_{k_j}u_-\rangle$, and orient the momentum plane by $(k_x,k_y)$. Then

$$
\Omega_-=\partial_{k_x}\mathcal A_y-\partial_{k_y}\mathcal A_x,
\qquad C=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}\Omega_-\,d^2k.
$$

This is the [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) evaluated on the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md), a [torus](torus.md). Smooth [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) may require multiple patches; their transition phases have integer [winding numbers](winding-number.md). For charge $-e$ and conventional [electric current](electric-current.md) $j_i=\sigma_{ij}E_j$, these conventions give $\sigma_{yx}=Ce^2/h$ and $\sigma_{xy}=-Ce^2/h$. The measurable magnitude agrees with [Chern number and integer Hall conductivity](chern-number-and-integer-hall-conductivity.md). Reversing the momentum orientation or the sign of the [Berry connection](berry-connection.md) reverses the displayed $C$.
