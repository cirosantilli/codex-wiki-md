# Gaussian forced-choice sensitivity relation

↑ **Parent:** [Sensitivity index](sensitivity-index.md)

Compare independent Gaussian samples $R_s,R_n$ with means $\mu_s,\mu_n$ and common [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$. Choosing the larger is correct when $R_s-R_n>0$. The difference has mean $\mu_s-\mu_n$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2\sigma^2$, so standardization proves the displayed relation. Correlated samples, unequal variances or a different response rule need a different calculation.
