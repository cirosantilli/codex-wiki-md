# Exponential concentration of terminal stochastic attrition survivors

↑ **Parent:** [Squared-population near-invariant for stochastic attrition](squared-population-near-invariant-for-stochastic-attrition.md)

Start the [symmetric stochastic attrition process](symmetric-stochastic-attrition-process.md) at $(N,\lfloor\alpha N\rfloor)$, with $0<\alpha<1$, and let $X^N$ be the final first-population size divided by $N$. The bound holds for all sufficiently large $N$, depending on $\alpha,\delta>0$. At absorption, one coordinate is zero. If the first survives, the terminal squared difference is $N^2(X^N)^2$; if it is extinguished, that difference is nonpositive. In either case the indicated deviation forces an error of at least $\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}\,\delta N^2$ from the limiting squared invariant. The initial rounding error and total conditional drift are only $O(N)$. Apply [Azuma-Hoeffding inequality](azuma-s-inequality.md) to the centered [squared-population near-invariant for stochastic attrition](squared-population-near-invariant-for-stochastic-attrition.md), using its $2N$ increments bounded by $4N$, to obtain the displayed exponent.
