# Commuting elements

↑ **Parent:** [Commutativity](commutativity.md)

Two elements commute when their two products in opposite orders agree. A particular pair of [matrices](matrix.md) may commute even when [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) is not a [commutative](commutativity.md) operation on the whole [set](set-split.md) of [matrices](matrix.md).
