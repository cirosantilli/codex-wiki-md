# J gate in measurement-based quantum computation

↑ **Parent:** [One-bit teleportation](one-bit-teleportation.md)

For the printed equatorial-basis convention,

$$
J(\alpha)=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&e^{i\alpha}\\1&-e^{i\alpha}\end{pmatrix}=H\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\alpha}).
$$

It is the [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) after a [phase gate](phase-gate.md). The identities $J(\alpha)Z=XJ(\alpha)$ and $J(\alpha)X=e^{i\alpha}ZJ(-\alpha)$ follow by multiplying their matrices. For $\alpha=0,\pi/2$ these are [Clifford gates](clifford-gate.md). In particular $J(-\pi/2)=XJ(\pi/2)$, permitting a sign change of the measurement angle to be absorbed into a [Pauli frame](pauli-frame.md).
