# Electromagnetic Bianchi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md)

Because the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) is the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) $F=dA$ of a potential, $dF=d^2A=0$. For a torsion-free connection this is

$$
\nabla_aF_{bc}+\nabla_bF_{ca}+\nabla_cF_{ab}=0,
$$

which is the covariant form of the two homogeneous [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md).
