# Matching the amplitude of a massive critical correlation tail

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher scaling relation](fisher-scaling-relation.md)

A [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) with critical [anomalous dimension](anomalous-dimension.md) $\eta$ scales as $G(r)=\xi^{-(D-2+\eta)}\mathcal G(r/\xi)$. If its large-distance shape is $s^{-(D-1)/2}e^{-s}$, substitution of $s=r/\xi$ produces the displayed amplitude. The extra $\xi^{-\eta}$ relative to the [massive Gaussian field correlation tail](massive-gaussian-field-correlation-tail.md) is necessary for matching at $r$ of order $\xi$. Integrating over space gives [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) proportional to $\xi^{2-\eta}$; omitting that factor would instead make the long-distance contribution scale as $\xi^2$.
