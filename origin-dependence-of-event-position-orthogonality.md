# Origin dependence of event-position orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

The [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md) of two event-position [four-vectors](four-vector.md) is invariant under a common [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) fixing the origin, but changes under a common [translation](translation-geometry.md). It is therefore not a criterion for the causal separation of the events. For example $(cT,0)$ and $(0,R)$ are orthogonal while their separation has squared interval $c^2T^2-R^2$, which can have any sign. Causal classification uses their difference, not their individual positions.
