# Coordinate functional representation of an operator into bounded indexed functions

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded scalar functions on an index set](bounded-scalar-functions-on-an-index-set.md)

A [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:X\to\ell_\infty(\Gamma)$ is exactly a uniformly bounded family of [bounded linear functionals](continuous-linear-functional.md) $f_\gamma\in X^*$ through $(Tx)(\gamma)=f_\gamma(x)$. Evaluation shows $\|f_\gamma\|\le\|T\|$; conversely the family bound gives $\|Tx\|\le(\sup\|f_\gamma\|)\|x\|$, proving equality. Taking all functionals in the dual unit ball gives an [isometric embedding](isometric-embedding.md) by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md).
