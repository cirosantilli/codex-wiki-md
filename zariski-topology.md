# Zariski topology

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zariski_topology)

The Zariski topology on an affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ has closed sets $V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}$ for ideals $I\subseteq A$. Its basic open sets are the [principal open subschemes](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(f)$.
