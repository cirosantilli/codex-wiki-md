# Bernoulli invariant laws of finite symmetric exclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric simple exclusion process](symmetric-simple-exclusion-process.md)

On a finite [graph](graph-split.md) with symmetric [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md)-swap rates, the [stirring representation of symmetric exclusion](stirring-representation-of-symmetric-exclusion.md) transports the occupations by a random [permutation](permutation.md). An independent identically distributed [Bernoulli distribution](bernoulli-distribution.md) occupation field is invariant under every deterministic [permutation](permutation.md), and hence under this random [permutation](permutation.md) when it is independent of the initial field. Therefore the displayed [product measure](product-measure.md) is invariant, including densities zero and one. Equivalently, an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) swap preserves the number of particles and has the same reverse rate, giving [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md).
