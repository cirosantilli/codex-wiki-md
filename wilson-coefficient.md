# Wilson coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

A Wilson coefficient weights a local operator in an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md). For an operator of [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) $d$ in four spacetime dimensions, a dimensionless coefficient is conventionally accompanied by $\Lambda^{4-d}$. The coefficient encodes short-distance dynamics and can be a matrix in flavour space.
