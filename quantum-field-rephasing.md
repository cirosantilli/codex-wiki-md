# Quantum field rephasing

↑ **Parent:** [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md)

Multiplying individual fields by constant phases changes the apparent phases of interaction coefficients. A physical [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md) survives the available changes of convention, as in the three-generation [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md).
