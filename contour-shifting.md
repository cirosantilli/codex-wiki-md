# Contour shifting

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

Contour shifting deforms a complex integration path through a region where the integrand is [meromorphic](meromorphic-function.md). The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) says that the change equals $2\pi i$ times the sum of residues of the poles crossed.
