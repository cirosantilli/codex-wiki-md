# Well-inside relation

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

This relation says that the closure of the open $u$ is contained in $v$. A [regular locale](regular-locale.md) has $a=\bigvee_{u\prec a}u$ for every $a$. If $u\prec a$, then $\neg\neg u\leq a$ and $\neg\neg u\prec a$.
