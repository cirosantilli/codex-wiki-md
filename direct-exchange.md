# Direct exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md)

[Direct exchange](direct-exchange.md) comes from Coulomb matrix elements between overlapping localized electronic orbitals together with the antisymmetry required by the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). It produces a spin-dependent energy without being a classical dipole interaction. Its competition with kinetic processes and orbital occupation helps determine whether neighbouring moments prefer parallel or antiparallel alignment.
