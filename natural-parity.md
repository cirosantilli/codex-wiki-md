# Natural parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity](parity.md)

For a state of integer total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J$, natural [parity](parity.md) means $\pi=(-1)^J$: $0^+,1^-,2^+,3^-,\ldots$. The other sign is called unnatural parity. The terminology is useful when a spin-zero positive-parity parent emits a spin-zero positive-parity particle: [angular momentum conservation](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) fixes relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L=J$, and [parity conservation](parity-conservation.md) then forces natural parity for the daughter.
