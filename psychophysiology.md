# Psychophysiology

↑ **Parent:** [Psychology](psychology-split.md)

The study of relationships between psychological processes and bodily measurements. In [emotion](emotion.md) research it combines [self-report](self-report.md), behavior and physiological recordings; agreement among these measures is an empirical question.
