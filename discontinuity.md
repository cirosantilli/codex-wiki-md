# Discontinuity

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

A point of the domain at which a [function](function-split.md) fails to be [continuous](continuous-function.md). Every [discontinuity](discontinuity.md) of a [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is a positive jump, and there are at most countably many: for each positive [integer](integer-split.md) $r$, at most $r$ jumps can have size at least $1/r$.
