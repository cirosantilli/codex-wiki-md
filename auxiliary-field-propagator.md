# Auxiliary-field propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md)

For a real [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) with quadratic [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) $-a\sigma^2/2$, $a\ne0$, the [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md) is the inverse quadratic kernel multiplied by $i$: $D_\sigma(p)=-i/a$. It is independent of [momentum](momentum-split.md); in position space it is the contact [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) $-i\delta^{(d)}(x-y)/a$. Two [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md) $ig$ joined by this [auxiliary-field propagator](auxiliary-field-propagator.md) give $ig^2/a$. Completing the square in $-a\sigma^2/2+g\sigma B$ produces $g^2B^2/(2a)$, proving the equivalence to a local interaction by a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md).
