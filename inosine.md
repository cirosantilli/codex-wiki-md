# Inosine

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleoside](nucleoside.md)

[Inosine](inosine.md) is the ribonucleoside of [hypoxanthine](hypoxanthine.md). [Hydrolytic deamination](hydrolytic-deamination.md) of [adenosine](adenosine.md) replaces its exocyclic amino group with a carbonyl [oxygen](oxygen.md) while retaining its sugar attachment.
