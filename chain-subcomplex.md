# Chain subcomplex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

A chain subcomplex $D_\bullet\subseteq C_\bullet$ consists of subgroups $D_i\leq C_i$ preserved by the [boundary operator](boundary-operator.md): $\partial(D_i)\subseteq D_{i-1}$.
