# Soap

↑ **Parent:** [Surfactant](surfactant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap)

[Soap](soap.md) consists of [sodium](sodium.md) or [potassium](potassium.md) salts of long-chain [fatty acids](fatty-acid.md). Its [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) [carboxylate](carboxylate.md) head and [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) tail make it an [amphiphile](amphiphile.md); [saponification](saponification.md) of [triglycerides](triglyceride.md) produces these salts.
