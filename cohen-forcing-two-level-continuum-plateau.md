# Cohen forcing two-level continuum plateau

↑ **Parent:** [Cohen forcing](cohen-forcing.md)

Over a model of [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), add $\aleph_3$ Cohen reals with finite binary [partial functions](partial-function.md). The [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md) proves the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md). The generic reals give the continuum lower bound $\aleph_3$, while nice [forcing names](forcing-name.md) for [subsets](subset.md) of $\omega_1$ number at most $(\aleph_3)^{\aleph_1}=\aleph_3$ in the ground model. [Cardinal](cardinal-number.md) preservation then gives $2^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_1}=\aleph_3$.
