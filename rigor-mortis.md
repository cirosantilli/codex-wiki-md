# Rigor mortis

↑ **Parent:** [Postmortem change](postmortem-change.md)

Postmortem muscular stiffening associated with loss of sufficient [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) to maintain normal detachment of actin–myosin cross-bridges. It is distinct from freezing and heat coagulation.
