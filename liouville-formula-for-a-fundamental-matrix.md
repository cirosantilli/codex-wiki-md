# Liouville formula for a fundamental matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental matrix of a linear differential equation](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md)

If $\dot\Phi=A(t)\Phi$ and $\Phi(0)=I$, differentiation of the [determinant](determinant.md) gives $\det\Phi(t)=\exp(\int_0^t\operatorname{tr}A(s)\,ds)$. For a planar [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) this gives the nontrivial [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md), since the tangent multiplier is one.
