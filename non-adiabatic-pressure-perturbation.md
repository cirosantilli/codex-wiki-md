# Non-adiabatic pressure perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md)

The non-adiabatic pressure perturbation is

$$
\delta P_{\rm nad}=\delta P-\frac{\dot{\bar P}}{\dot{\bar\rho}}\delta\rho.
$$

On [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md) it sources the evolution of the [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) through a convention-dependent signed relation proportional to $H\delta P_{\rm nad}/(\bar\rho+\bar P)$.
