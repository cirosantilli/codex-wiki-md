# Translation-dilation group of the plane

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

This [Lie group](lie-group.md) acts on the plane by $v\mapsto x+\rho v$ with $\rho>0$. Its multiplication is $(\rho,x)(\rho',x')=(\rho\rho',x+\rho x')$, and its inverse is $(\rho^{-1},-\rho^{-1}x)$. It is a [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) in which positive dilation acts on the translation subgroup. A dilation generator $D$ and translation generators $T_1,T_2$ have [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md) $[D,T_a]=T_a$ and $[T_1,T_2]=0$.
