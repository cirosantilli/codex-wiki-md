# Exact self-dual rectangle crossing probability

↑ **Parent:** [Planar duality for rectangle crossings](planar-duality-for-rectangle-crossings.md)

For the lattice rectangle with [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) $\{0,\ldots,n\}\times\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, let $H_n$ be an open left-to-right crossing. Its complementary event is a closed dual top-to-bottom crossing. The dual crossing rectangle has width $n-1$ and height $n$, so rotation and translation identify it with the original rectangle. Boundary [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) along the starting and ending sides do not affect the crossing event. At $p=1/2$ the primal and dual [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) states have identical laws, hence

$$
\mathbb P_{1/2}(H_n)=1-\mathbb P_{1/2}(H_n)=\frac12.
$$

The one-unit adjustment in the side lengths makes the symmetry exact rather than an informal assertion about a finite [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) square.
