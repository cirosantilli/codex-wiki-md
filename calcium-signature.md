# Calcium signature

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md)

The temporal and spatial pattern of free intracellular [calcium ion](calcium-ion.md) concentration during a response, including amplitude, duration, frequency and compartment. Calling a pattern a signature does not establish that it uniquely specifies a stimulus.
