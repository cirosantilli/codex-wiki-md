# Lattice in toric geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Toric geometry](toric-geometry-split.md)

A toric lattice is a finite-rank [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md) $N$, embedded as a discrete full-rank subgroup of $N_{\mathbb R}=N\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R$. Its dual is $M=\operatorname{Hom}(N,\mathbb Z)$. This lattice of integral vectors is distinct from an order-theoretic [lattice](lattice.md). It supplies the integral structure for [toric cones](cone-in-toric-geometry.md) and their dual monomials.
