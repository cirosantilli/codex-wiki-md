# Euclidean reality structure on twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor space](twistor-space.md)

Choose an antilinear quaternionic structure $J$ on $\mathbb C^4$. It induces a fixed-point-free involution on [projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md). A real Euclidean spacetime point is a $J$-invariant complex two-plane, equivalently a quaternionic line in $\mathbb H^2$. These form $\mathbb{HP}^1\cong S^4$, the [conformal compactification of Euclidean space](conformal-compactification-of-euclidean-space.md). Every projective twistor lies on the unique invariant line $\mathbb P\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}(Z,JZ)$, giving a fibration $\mathbb{CP}^3\to S^4$ with fiber $\mathbb{CP}^1$.
