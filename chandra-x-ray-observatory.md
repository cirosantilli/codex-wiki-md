# Chandra X-ray Observatory

↑ **Parent:** [Telescope](telescope.md)

An [X-ray](x-ray.md) observatory used for high-resolution imaging and spectroscopy of astronomical sources. Its observations of the [Perseus cluster](perseus-cluster.md) reveal structure in the emitting intracluster [gas](gas.md) around radio bubbles.[2003, abstract](a-deep-chandra-observation-of-the-perseus-cluster-shocks-and-ripples-by-a-c-fabian-and-others.md)
