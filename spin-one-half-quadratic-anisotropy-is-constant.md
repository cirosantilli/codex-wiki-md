# Spin-one-half quadratic anisotropy is constant

↑ **Parent:** [Spin operator](spin-operator.md)

For [spin](spin.md) one-half, the Pauli representation makes every diagonal quadratic component a multiple of the identity. Thus $AS_z^2+B(S_x^2-S_y^2)=A\hbar^2I/4$ cannot split the doublet or cause [spin](spin.md) reversal. This conclusion is special to this quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) and does not exclude linear Zeeman dynamics.
