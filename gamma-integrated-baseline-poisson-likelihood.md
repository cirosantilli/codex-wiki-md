# Gamma-integrated baseline Poisson likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma–Poisson hierarchical model](gamma-poisson-hierarchical-model.md)

For independent [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) with means $u e^{\eta_k}$ and a shape–rate [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) prior on $u$, integrating $u$ adds $a$ to the total-count exponent and $b$ to the sum of relative rates. This distinguishes gamma mixing from conditioning on the total count, which gives a [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md).
