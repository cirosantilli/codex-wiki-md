# Electrical conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance and conductance](electrical-resistance-and-conductance.md)

For an ohmic element with finite nonzero [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R$, its [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) is $G=1/R$: an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $\Delta V$ drives [electric current](electric-current.md) $I=G\Delta V$. Greater positive [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) means a greater [electric current](electric-current.md) for the same positive [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) is instead a bulk material coefficient relating [current density](current-density.md) to [electric field](electric-field-split.md).
