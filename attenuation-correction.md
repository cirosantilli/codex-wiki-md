# Attenuation correction

↑ **Parent:** [Medical imaging](medical-imaging.md)

Compensation for photons lost along an emission-imaging path. In PET/CT or SPECT/CT, a registered [computed tomography](computed-tomography.md) attenuation map supplies information for this correction.
