# Radial buoyancy frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md)

The radial buoyancy frequency describes restoration of a radially displaced parcel in a stratified [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md). With a dimensionless [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) $S$, its square is proportional to $-\rho^{-1}(dP/dr)(dS/dr)$. Negative $N_r^2$ means an adverse entropy stratification, but [epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md) can still stabilize the parcel. For smooth [thin disk](thin-disk.md) profiles, $|N_r^2|$ is usually of order $(H/r)^2\Omega^2$.
