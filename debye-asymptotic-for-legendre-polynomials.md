# Debye asymptotic for Legendre polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md)

For fixed $0<\theta<\pi$,

$$
P_n(\cos\theta)
\sim
\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi n\sin\theta}}
\cos\left(\left(n+\frac12\right)\theta-\frac\pi4\right).
$$
