# Strain-driven steady mush

↑ **Parent:** [Mushy layer](mushy-layer.md)

A uniform-permeability [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) on an impermeable eutectic floor, under the quadratic roof pressure $p_0-E^2x^2/2$, has [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) $\mathbf q=\alpha(x,-z)$, where $\alpha=\Pi E^2/\mu$. With pulling velocity $-V\mathbf e_z$, pure primary crystals, [solid fraction](solid-fraction.md) $\phi$, liquid [concentration](concentration.md) $C$ and constant phase properties, its one-dimensional balances are

$$
\kappa T''+(V+\alpha z)T'-\frac{VL}{c_p}\phi'=0,\qquad [V(1-\phi)C]'=-\alpha zC',\qquad T=-mC.
$$

The [solidification](freezing.md) product at the eutectic floor retains bulk composition $C_s=(1-\phi(0))C_E$. Integration gives $C_s=C_0-(\alpha/V)\int_0^h(C-C_0)\,dz$: horizontal strain removes solute before the material freezes. A negative predicted $C_s$ is inadmissible, and diagnoses failure of the assumed steady branch rather than a negative-composition product.
