# Unbounded continued-fraction coefficients exclude quadratic irrationality

↑ **Parent:** [Liouville approximation theorem](liouville-approximation-theorem.md)

For an irrational $x$ with [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md) $p_n/q_n$, one has $|x-p_n/q_n|\leq1/(q_nq_{n+1})\leq1/(a_{n+1}q_n^2)$. The [Liouville approximation theorem](liouville-approximation-theorem.md) for a [quadratic irrational](quadratic-irrational-number.md) gives a positive lower bound $C/q_n^2$ instead. Thus unbounded partial quotients preclude quadratic irrationality, and in particular imply $x^2$ is irrational.
