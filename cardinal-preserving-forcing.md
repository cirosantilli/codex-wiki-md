# Cardinal-preserving forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

A forcing order preserves cardinals over a ground model if every ground-model [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) remains a cardinal in every [generic extension](generic-extension.md). This assertion and any sufficient [chain condition for forcing](chain-condition-for-forcing.md) are evaluated inside the ground model. External countability of a [countable transitive model](countable-transitive-model.md) does not make all of its forcing orders internally ccc.
