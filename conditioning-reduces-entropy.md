# Conditioning reduces entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md)

For discrete random variables, $H(U\mid V)\leq H(U)$, with equality exactly when $U$ and $V$ are [independent](independent-random-variables.md).
