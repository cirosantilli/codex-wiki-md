# Yield-limited leak-before-break thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Leak-before-break criterion](leak-before-break-criterion.md)

A conservative material-screening upper bound obtained by requiring wall penetration before fast fracture even at a stated yield-limited [stress](stress.md). Substituting a thickness-dependent pressure [stress](stress.md) instead yields a different fixed-pressure inequality; the two comparisons must not be conflated.
