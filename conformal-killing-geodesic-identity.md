# Conformal Killing geodesic identity

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md)

Let $X$ be a [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md) with [conformal Killing factor](conformal-killing-factor.md) $\lambda$, and let $U$ be the [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md) of a [geodesic](geodesic.md) with [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) $s$. The [product rule](product-rule.md), [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) and [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) give

$$
\frac{d}{ds}g(X,U)=g(\nabla_UX,U)+g(X,\nabla_UU)
=U^aU^b\nabla_{(a}X_{b)}=\lambda g(U,U).
$$

Every [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) therefore has the [conserved quantity](conserved-quantity.md) $g(X,U)$. On a [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) with [proper time](proper-time.md) as parameter, the derivative is $-\lambda$ in [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$. With a nonaffine parameter satisfying $\nabla_UU=fU$, an extra term $fg(X,U)$ appears; the conserved expression for a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) is tied to its [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) normalization.
