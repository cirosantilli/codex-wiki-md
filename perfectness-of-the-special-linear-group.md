# Perfectness of the special linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect group](perfect-group.md)

For dimension at least three, $[I+aE_{ik},I+E_{kj}]=I+aE_{ij}$ for distinct $i,j,k$, with $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$. In dimension two over a field with more than three elements, choose $s$ with $s^2\ne1$ and commute an elementary matrix with $\operatorname{diag}(s,s^{-1})$. In both cases all generating [transvections](transvection.md) lie in the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md), so the [special linear group over a finite field](special-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md) is perfect outside $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_2)$ and $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_3)$.
