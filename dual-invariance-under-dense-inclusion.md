# Dual invariance under dense inclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space-split.md)

If a normed subspace $Z$ is [dense](dense-set.md) in a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) $X$ and carries the inherited [norm](norm.md), restriction gives an onto linear [isometry](isometry.md) from the [dual space](dual-space-split.md) of $X$ to that of $Z$. A bounded functional on $Z$ extends uniquely by limits along sequences from $Z$; its [norm](norm.md) is unchanged because its inequality passes to the limit. Thus completion does not change the [continuous](continuous-function.md) dual. For example, the subspace of sequences with $\sum j|a_j|<\infty$, equipped with the unweighted $\ell^1$ [norm](norm.md), has dual $\ell^\infty$, since it contains the [dense subspace](dense-subspace.md) of finitely supported sequences. The inherited [norm](norm.md) matters; the weighted [norm](norm.md) would give a different dual [norm](norm.md).
