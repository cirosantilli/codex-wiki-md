# Order-dense subset

↑ **Parent:** [Total order](total-order.md)

A subset of a [total order](total-order.md) meeting every nonempty open interval between two distinct points. A countable [order-dense subset](order-dense-subset.md) witnesses separability in the [order topology](order-topology.md) of a dense order.
