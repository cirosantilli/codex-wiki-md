# Weight projection matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Branching rule](branching-rule.md)

A coordinate matrix expressing restriction of [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) to the [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) of a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). With [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) as column [vectors](vector.md), its rows encode the subalgebra's coroots in terms of the original coroots. It need not be an idempotent [projection operator](projection-linear-algebra.md): an equal-rank embedding can give an invertible matrix. Applying it to the full [weight system](weight-system.md), including [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md), permits reconstruction of the restricted [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md).
