# Electron-acoustic recoil length

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon scattering](electron-phonon-scattering.md)

For free-electron recoil and a linear acoustic branch, $E_e(q)=\hbar^2q^2/(2m_e)$ and $E_{\rm ph}(q)=\hbar c_sq$. Their ratio is $q\lambda$, so equality occurs at $q=1/\lambda$. This is a momentum/localization crossover scale, formally half a sound-speed analogue of the [Reduced Compton wavelength](reduced-compton-wavelength.md). It does not replace the speed of light in electron relativity or determine a [mean free path](mean-free-path.md). A Fermi-surface electron emitting a phonon has fractional radial loss $\delta\simeq q/(2\lambda k_F^2)$ when the transferred energy is small.
