# Small oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

Near a stable equilibrium, retaining quadratic Lagrangian terms gives a linear system $\mathsf M\ddot z+\mathsf Kz=0$.

The resulting linearized motion decomposes into [normal modes](normal-mode.md) near the stable equilibrium.
