# Quasirandom group

↑ **Parent:** [Non-abelian additive combinatorics](non-abelian-additive-combinatorics.md)

A [finite group](finite-group.md) is $m$-quasirandom if every nontrivial [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) has degree at least $m$. The [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) is excluded. Large $m$ suppresses correlations of products of arbitrary subsets, through [product mixing in a quasirandom group](product-mixing-in-a-quasirandom-group.md).
