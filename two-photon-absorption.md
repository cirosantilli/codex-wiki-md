# Two-photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Two-photon transition](two-photon-transition.md)

[absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) of two [photons](photon.md) whose summed [energy](energy-split.md) matches an atomic transition. In the weak-field regime its rate scales with the product of the two intensities. Two [electric-dipole interactions](electric-dipole-interaction.md) preserve the initial [parity](parity.md).
