# Circular orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circular motion](circular-motion.md)

A circular orbit maintains constant distance from its centre of attraction. Its [velocity](velocity.md) changes direction even when its [speed](speed.md) stays constant. The inward [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md) has magnitude $v^2/r$, so the radial [force](force-split.md) must satisfy $F_r=-mv^2/r$. For a [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) with [standard gravitational parameter](standard-gravitational-parameter.md) $\mu$, this gives $v=\sqrt{\mu/r}$.
