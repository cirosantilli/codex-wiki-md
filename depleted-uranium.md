# Depleted uranium

↑ **Parent:** [Uranium](uranium.md)

[uranium](uranium.md) with less [uranium-235](uranium-235.md) than natural [uranium](uranium.md). Addition to an immobilised [plutonium](plutonium.md) inventory can dilute [uranium-235](uranium-235.md) subsequently formed by [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md); chemical coexistence is not irreversible protection against deliberate separation.
