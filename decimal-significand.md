# Decimal significand

↑ **Parent:** [Scale invariance of decimal significands](scale-invariance-of-decimal-significands.md)

For $x>0$, the [decimal significand](decimal-significand.md) is the unique $S_{10}(x)\in[1,10)$ such that $x=10^kS_{10}(x)$ for an [integer](integer.md) $k$. Explicitly, $k=\lfloor\log_{10}x\rfloor$ and $S_{10}(x)=10^{\{\log_{10}x\}}$, using the [floor function](floor-function.md) and the [fractional part](fractional-part.md). For $0.0042$, $4.2$ and $4200$, the [decimal significand](decimal-significand.md) is $4.2$ and the first nonzero digit is $\lfloor S_{10}(x)\rfloor=4$. In the classical terminology of [logarithm](logarithm.md) tables, the logarithmic mantissa is $\{\log_{10}x\}$, which differs from the [decimal significand](decimal-significand.md) $10^{\{\log_{10}x\}}$.
