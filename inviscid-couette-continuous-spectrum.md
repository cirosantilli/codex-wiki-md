# Inviscid Couette continuous spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

For inviscid plane [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) $U=z$ between impermeable walls at $z=\pm1$, the nonzero real streamwise [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $\alpha$ gives no nontrivial smooth discrete vertical-velocity modes. Instead, the vorticity variable $q=(D^2-\alpha^2)w$ obeys $(\partial_t+i\alpha z)q=0$. Localized [vorticity sheets](vortex-sheet.md) give [generalized eigenfunctions](generalized-eigenfunction.md) with real frequencies $\omega_\xi=\alpha\xi$. Their closure forms a neutral [continuous spectrum](continuous-spectrum.md).
