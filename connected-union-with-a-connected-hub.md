# Connected union with a connected hub

↑ **Parent:** [Connected subset](connected-subset.md)

Suppose [connected subsets](connected-subset.md) $U_j$ all meet one [connected](connected-space.md) member $U_1$. A separation of their union would place $U_1$ entirely on one side. Each $U_j$ is also entirely on one side and intersects $U_1$, so every $U_j$ lies on the same side, contradicting nonempty separation. Thus the union is [connected](connected-space.md). If the subsets are [path-connected](path-connected-space.md), join two points by a [path](continuous-path.md) to $U_1$, a [path](continuous-path.md) inside $U_1$, and a [path](continuous-path.md) back out. This needs pairwise contact with the hub, not one point shared by every subset.
