# Nonlinear optical coherence length

↑ **Parent:** [Phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md)

The distance over which a constant [phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md) accumulates [phase difference](phase-difference.md) $\pi$. In unseeded, undepleted [sum-frequency generation](sum-frequency-generation.md), the generated [intensity](intensity-physics.md) first reaches a maximum at this length and returns to zero at $2\ell_c$. This length concerns relative phases of interacting waves, rather than the temporal [optical coherence length](optical-coherence-length.md) of one source.
