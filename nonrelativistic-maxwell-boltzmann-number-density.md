<h1 id="nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density">Nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number density</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics](maxwell-boltzmann-statistics.md)

Integrating [Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics](maxwell-boltzmann-statistics.md) over momentum for [nonrelativistic particles](nonrelativistic-particle.md) of mass $m$, spin-state count $g_s$, and energy $mc^2+p^2/(2m)$ gives

$$
n=g_s\left(\frac{2\pi mk_BT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}
e^{(\mu-mc^2)/(k_BT)}.
$$
