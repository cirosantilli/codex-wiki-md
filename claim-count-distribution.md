# Claim count distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate claims model](aggregate-claims-model.md)

The [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of the nonnegative integer number of claims over a specified accounting period. Its [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) determines the count probabilities. A [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) gives equal count [expected value](expected-value.md) and [variance](variance-split.md); a [Poisson mixture](poisson-mixture.md) allows heterogeneity in the underlying risk intensity.
