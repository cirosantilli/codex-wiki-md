# Moving-surface retarded Jacobian

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

For a source surface parameterized by $y(p,q,\tau)$, the [retarded time](retarded-time.md) equation is $t=\tau+|x-y(p,q,\tau)|/c_0$. Integrating a [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) enforcing that equation produces the reciprocal [derivative](derivative.md) $|1-M_r|^{-1}$ at each simple root. For subsonic motion there is a unique retarded root. Multiple roots require summation; a zero [derivative](derivative.md) is outside the simple-root rule. This factor is the moving-source counterpart of a delta change-of-variable Jacobian.
