# Pair-energy double counting in Hartree orbital sums

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree method](hartree-method.md)

For Hartree direct integrals $J_{ij}$ and orbital [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\epsilon_i$, the product-state [energy](energy-split.md) is $E=\sum_i\epsilon_i-\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}$. Each pair enters two orbital mean fields but only once in the total interaction [energy](energy-split.md). Orbital energies are therefore not independent additive particle energies.
