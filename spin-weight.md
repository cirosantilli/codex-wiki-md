# Spin weight

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

A spin-weight-$s$ field on an oriented sphere acquires a phase under rotation of its local tangent-frame basis. [Spin-weighted spherical harmonics](spin-weighted-spherical-harmonic.md) form the angular basis for such fields. The polarization combinations $Q\pm iU$ carry opposite spin weights of magnitude two; ordinary scalar functions have spin weight zero.
