# Surface with boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Topological surface](topological-surface.md)

A surface with boundary is a two-dimensional [manifold with boundary](manifold-with-boundary.md): it is locally homeomorphic to the plane or to the closed upper half-plane. Points represented on the edge of a half-plane chart form its one-dimensional boundary.
