# Mean-vector prediction risk

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic risk](quadratic-risk.md)

The mean-vector prediction risk measures squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) error in estimating the deterministic response mean. For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) future noise with [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\sigma^2I_n$, prediction of a new noisy response adds $n\sigma^2$ to this risk. A Gaussian [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) $P$ of rank $k$ has risk $\|(I-P)\mu\|^2+k\sigma^2$.
