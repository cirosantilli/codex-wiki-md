# Near-infrared

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared](infrared.md)

The short-wavelength portion of [infrared](infrared.md) radiation adjacent to visible red. Healthy leaf structure strongly scatters much of this radiation, producing a high [reflectance](reflectance.md) used in vegetation mapping.
