# Long-wavelength inference of diatomic-chain parameters

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs](diatomic-chain-dispersion-relation-with-equal-springs.md)

For alternating [masses](mass.md) $m_A,m_B$, nearest-neighbour spacing $a$ and equal [spring constants](spring-constant.md) $\alpha$, the zone-centre optical [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and acoustic [group velocity](group-velocity.md) obey

$$
\omega_{\rm op}^2(0)=2\alpha\left(\frac1{m_A}+\frac1{m_B}\right),\qquad
v_s^2=\frac{2\alpha a^2}{m_A+m_B}.
$$

Therefore $\alpha=m_Am_B\omega_{\rm op}^2(0)/[2(m_A+m_B)]$ and $a=v_s\sqrt{(m_A+m_B)/(2\alpha)}$. These infer effective model parameters from a measured intercept and initial slope of a [phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md). Fitting a real polar crystal to a nearest-neighbour scalar model does not guarantee that every measured point or its structural spacing is reproduced exactly.
