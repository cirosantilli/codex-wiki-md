# Bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

A bifurcation is a qualitative change in the phase portrait of a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md) as a parameter varies, for example creation of [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) or a change in their stability.
