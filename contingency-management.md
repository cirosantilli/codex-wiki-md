# Contingency management

↑ **Parent:** [Operant conditioning](operant-conditioning.md)

Arranging consequences contingent on clearly specified behaviour to change its future occurrence. The target, measurement, timing and alternative responses determine what is reinforced; [reinforcement](reinforcement.md) is not identical to [punishment](punishment.md).
