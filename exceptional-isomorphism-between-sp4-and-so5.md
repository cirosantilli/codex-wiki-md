# Exceptional isomorphism between sp4 and so5

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

Over $\mathbb C$, the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) on a four-dimensional [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) acts faithfully on its five-dimensional [primitive exterior square](primitive-exterior-square.md), preserving its [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md). Faithfulness follows because an operator killing all wedges $e_i\wedge e_j$ has all off-diagonal coefficients zero and satisfies $A_{ii}+A_{jj}=0$ for every pair, hence is zero. The source and target [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) both have [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) ten, so this faithful map is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) to the [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{so}_5$.
