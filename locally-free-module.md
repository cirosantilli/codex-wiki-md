# Locally free module

↑ **Parent:** [Module (mathematics)](module-mathematics.md)

A [locally free module](locally-free-module.md) is one whose associated sheaf is locally a [free module](free-module.md). In the finite constant-rank case, it becomes free of rank $r$ on a [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) cover of $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Equivalently it is a [finite projective module](finite-projective-module.md) of constant rank $r$. A surjection onto it splits, so the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is also finite projective. Its associated sheaf is a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) and defines an algebraic [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md).
