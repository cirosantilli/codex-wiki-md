# Thermistor

↑ **Parent:** [Resistance thermometer](resistance-thermometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermistor)

A [thermistor](thermistor.md) is a [temperature](temperature-split.md)-sensitive [resistor](resistor.md) used as a [resistance thermometer](resistance-thermometer.md). Its calibrated [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) supplies a [temperature](temperature-split.md) indication; probe [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) and unwanted [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) can affect its response and immersion error.
