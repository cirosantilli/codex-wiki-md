# Even unimodular lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Unimodular lattice](unimodular-lattice.md)

An [integral quadratic lattice](integral-quadratic-lattice.md) that is both an [even lattice](even-lattice.md) and a [unimodular lattice](unimodular-lattice.md). The [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md) has signature $(3,19)$, while the positive-definite [E8 lattice](e8-lattice.md) has rank eight.
