# p-residual of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

The p-residual is the smallest [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) with p-group quotient, equivalently the subgroup generated by all elements of order prime to $p$. It is [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) and satisfies $O^p(O^p(G))=O^p(G)$: an extension of two finite p-groups is a p-group. A finite group has a [normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md) exactly when its p-residual has order prime to $p$.
