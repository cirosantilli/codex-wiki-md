# Competitive Lotka-Volterra model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

For $A,B,\rho>0$, these [Lotka-Volterra equations](lotka-volterra-equations.md) describe two populations limited by their own density and depressed by the other population. The nonnegative quadrant is invariant. Positive [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are intersections of the non-axis [nullclines](nullcline.md) $u+Av=a$ and $Bu+v=b$; their existence and stability distinguish stable coexistence, competitive exclusion, and [bistability](bistability.md). Positive solutions have no periodic orbits: multiplying the vector field by the [Dulac function](dulac-function.md) $1/(uv)$ makes its divergence $-1/v-\rho/u<0$, so the [Bendixson-Dulac theorem](bendixson-dulac-theorem.md) applies. Boundedness, boundary equilibria, and their stable manifolds must still be checked before concluding convergence to one particular equilibrium.
