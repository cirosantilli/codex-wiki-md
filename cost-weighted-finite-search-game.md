# Cost-weighted finite search game

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md)

In a hide-and-search [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md) with positive inspection costs $c_i$ and $c=\sum_i c_i$, hiding with probabilities $c_i/c$ gives the same expected search cost for every complete search order. For three locations, beginning at $i$ with probability $c_i/c$ and randomizing the remaining two orders also equalizes every hiding location. The common value is the displayed quantity.
