# Shared computer bus

↑ **Parent:** [Computer bus](computer-bus.md)

A [computer bus](computer-bus.md) whose communication resources serve multiple attached components. A common physical path reduces wiring, but competing [bus transactions](bus-transaction.md) must share its capacity. [Address bus](address-bus.md), [data bus](data-bus.md) and [control bus](control-bus.md) name logical roles; they need not be three permanently separate bundles of wires. Some designs reuse lines for addresses and data in different phases.[John Kubiatowicz, CS152 lecture 21, slides 20–24 and 33: shared paths, bus organization and transaction roles](cs152-computer-architecture-and-engineering-fall-1999/lecture-21-buses-and-i-and-o.md)
