# Riesz-Fischer theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riesz-Fischer_theorem)

For $1\leq p\leq\infty$, the Lebesgue space $L^p$ is complete in its usual norm.
