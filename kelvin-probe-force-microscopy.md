# Kelvin probe force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

An electrostatic null method that adjusts probe–sample bias to cancel an alternating [force](force-split.md) or force-gradient component. The compensation [voltage](voltage.md) estimates a spatially weighted [contact potential difference](contact-potential-difference.md), with the sign set by the electrode-bias convention. Calibration and surface-state effects matter for work-function interpretation.
