# Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md)

For $z,w$ in the upper half-plane,

$$
\cosh d(z,w)=1+\frac{|z-w|^2}{2\operatorname{Im}z\operatorname{Im}w}.
$$
