# Radiometer

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiometer)

A [radiometer](radiometer.md) measures [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) through its received [power](power.md). A radio [radiometer](radiometer.md) can express that [power](power.md) as a [noise temperature](noise-temperature.md), allowing an unknown source to be compared with calibrated reference [temperatures](temperature-split.md).
