# Hubble constant

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

The [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md) is the present value $H_0=H(t_0)$ of the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). The word "constant" fixes the epoch; it does not mean $H(t)$ stays unchanged throughout [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md). It sets the present instantaneous proportionality in [Hubble's law](hubble-s-law.md) and the approximate nearby observed [redshift](redshift.md)-distance relation.

For illustration, stipulate $H_0=70\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$; this is an example input, not a claim about a measured consensus value. A million [parsecs](parsec.md) is $3.085677581\ldots\times10^{19}\,\mathrm{km}$, giving

$$
H_0\simeq2.26855\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad
H_0^{-1}\simeq4.40811\times10^{17}\,\mathrm s\simeq13.97\,\mathrm{Gyr}.
$$

Here a year means $365.25$ days of $86400\,\mathrm s$, and $\mathrm{Gyr}$ denotes a billion such years. The [parsec](parsec.md) conversion follows [IAU 2015 Resolution B2, note 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.06262). This [Hubble time](hubble-time.md) is a present expansion timescale; the [age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md) also depends on the earlier evolution of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) and need not equal $H_0^{-1}$.
