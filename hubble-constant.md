# Hubble constant

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

The Hubble constant is the present value $H_0$ of the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). It sets the local proportionality between recession velocity and distance in the [Hubble law](hubble-s-law.md).
