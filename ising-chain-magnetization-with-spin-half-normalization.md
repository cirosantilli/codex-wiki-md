# Ising-chain magnetization with spin-half normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model](transfer-matrix-for-the-one-dimensional-ising-model.md)

For $H=-2J\sum S_i^zS_{i+1}^z-g\mu_BB\sum S_i^z$, with dimensionless $S_i^z=\pm1/2$, define $K=\beta J/2$ and $b=\beta g\mu_BB/2$. The [transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model](transfer-matrix-for-the-one-dimensional-ising-model.md) has largest eigenvalue $e^K[\cosh b+\sqrt{\sinh^2b+e^{-4K}}]$. Differentiating its logarithm gives the displayed moment per site. In particular the zero-field susceptibility per site is $(g\mu_B)^2e^{\beta J}/(4k_BT)$. Factors of two differ from a model whose spin variables themselves take values $\pm1$.
