# Interfacial gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

An interfacial gravity wave is a displacement of an interface between two fluids of different [mass density](density.md), restored by [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) when the denser fluid is below. For two deep layers in the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), density difference $\delta\rho$ and horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ give intrinsic squared [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $g\delta\rho/(2\rho_0k)$. Background flow shifts the laboratory [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) by its local speed.
