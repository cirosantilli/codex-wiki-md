# Electric quadrupole

↑ **Parent:** [Electric multipole expansion](electric-multipole-expansion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_quadrupole)

An electric quadrupole is a charge distribution whose total charge and [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) vanish while its second charge moment does not. Its potential decays as $r^{-3}$.
