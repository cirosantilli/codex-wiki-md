# Parity-constrained variational bound

↑ **Parent:** [Variational method](variational-method.md)

An even one-dimensional confining potential preserves even and odd [parity](parity.md). Restricting a normalized trial state to the odd sector makes its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) an upper bound on the lowest odd [energy](energy-split.md), which is the first excited [energy](energy-split.md) for the usual nonsingular one-dimensional bound-state problem. This avoids contamination by the even [ground state](ground-state.md). For an adjustable-width odd Gaussian, optimize its own width rather than borrowing the ground-state optimum.
