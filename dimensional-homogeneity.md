# Dimensional homogeneity

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)

[Dimensional homogeneity](dimensional-homogeneity.md) requires all terms added in a physical [equation](equation.md), and both sides of that [equation](equation.md), to have the same [physical dimension](physical-dimension.md). It permits a meaningful comparison independently of the chosen [units of measurement](unit-of-measurement.md).
