# Ultraviolet pole

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md)

An [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md) appears as a [pole](pole.md) of a dimensionally continued [Feynman integral](feynman-integral.md). In $d=4-2\epsilon$, a logarithmically divergent one-loop integral typically has a $1/\epsilon$ term. [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) can also produce [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md) with the same formal regulator, so the origin must be identified before interpreting a pole as an ultraviolet [counterterm](counterterm.md).
