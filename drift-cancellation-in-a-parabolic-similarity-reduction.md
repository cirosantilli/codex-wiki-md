# Drift cancellation in a parabolic similarity reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Similarity solution](similarity-solution.md)

The drift $-x u_x/(2t)$ exactly cancels the coordinate-motion term of the [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) $u=t^{-1/2}f(\eta)$, $\eta=x/\sqrt t$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
u_t=t^{-3/2}(-f/2-\eta f'/2),\qquad u_{xx}=t^{-3/2}f'',\qquad -\frac{x}{2t}u_x=-t^{-3/2}\eta f'/2.
$$

Consequently the nonautonomous [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) reduces to the autonomous [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) $f''=f^3-f/2$. The balance fixes the same length scale $x\sim\sqrt t$ as [diffusion](diffusion.md), but removes the first-derivative term that usually appears in the profile equation. A first-integral argument can then determine a whole profile family without solving a nonautonomous second-order equation.
