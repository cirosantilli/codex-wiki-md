# Delay-period activity

↑ **Parent:** [Working memory](working-memory.md)

Neural signals observed between encoding and a later memory-guided response. They may reflect retention, priority, anticipated action, time or control. Content-specific predictions and suitable task controls are needed before interpreting them as storage.
