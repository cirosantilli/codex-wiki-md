# DFS edge classification

↑ **Parent:** [Depth-first search](depth-first-search.md)

Classify [directed edges](directed-edge.md) relative to a [depth-first search](depth-first-search.md) forest. Tree [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) discover a new [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md); forward [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) reach a proper descendant without being its discovery [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md); back [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) reach an active ancestor; cross [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) connect completed branches or trees. Discovery and finish intervals are nested for ancestry. The classification depends on the chosen search order, not only on the [graph](graph-split.md).
