# Dynamic link

↑ **Parent:** [Stack frame](stack-frame.md)

A [pointer](pointer-computer-programming.md) from an activation's [stack frame](stack-frame.md) to its caller's [stack frame](stack-frame.md), used to restore the caller when the activation returns. The [dynamic link](dynamic-link.md) is independent of a [static link](static-link.md) to the lexically enclosing environment.
