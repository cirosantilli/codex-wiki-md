# Tidal impulse heating of a spherical galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated tidal tensor of a straight-line flyby](integrated-tidal-tensor-of-a-straight-line-flyby.md)

If the initial stellar velocities are uncorrelated with the [impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md) kicks, mean specific heating is $|\Delta\mathbf v|^2/2$. Spherical position averaging gives total heating $4G^2m_p^2m_g\langle r^2\rangle/(3v^2p^4)$, where the mass-weighted mean square radius must be finite.
