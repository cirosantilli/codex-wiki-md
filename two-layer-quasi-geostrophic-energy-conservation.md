# Two-layer quasi-geostrophic energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-layer quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](two-layer-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

For inviscid unforced two-layer QG dynamics, $H_1F_1=H_2F_2=f_0^2/g\prime$ makes the coupling symmetric in the layer-depth inner product. Multiplication by $H_i\psi_i$ gives the positive energy $E=\tfrac12[\sum_iH_i|\nabla\psi_i|^2+(f_0^2/g\prime)(\psi_1-\psi_2)^2]$. It is conserved after integration when the lateral energy flux vanishes. The interface term is [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md); the gradient terms are [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
