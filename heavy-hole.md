# Heavy hole

↑ **Parent:** [Electron hole](electron-hole.md)

A hole in the upper valence manifold associated near a chosen propagation axis with the $j=3/2$, $m_j=\pm3/2$ branch. Its band curvature is relatively small, hence its [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) relatively large. The label is direction dependent in a general anisotropic band structure.
