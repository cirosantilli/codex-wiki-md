# Primed pluripotency

↑ **Parent:** [Pluripotency](pluripotency.md)

A pluripotent state associated with later [epiblast](epiblast.md) and readiness to respond to lineage-specifying signals. It is experimentally and molecularly distinct from [naive pluripotency](naive-pluripotency.md).
