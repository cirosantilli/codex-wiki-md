# Mobility correction from a fixed distant sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md)

For equal [spheres](sphere.md) of radius $a$ separated by $R\gg a$, one held fixed and the other forced with $\mathbf F=6\pi\mu a\mathbf V$, put $\zeta=6\pi\mu a$, $\mathbf n=\mathbf X/R$ and $\mathsf A=3a(I+\mathbf n\mathbf n)/(4R)$. The [method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md) gives $\mathbf U=\mathbf V-\mathsf A^2\mathbf V+O(Va^4/R^4)$, since the fixed [sphere](sphere.md) creates the reflected [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) with [force](force.md) $-6\pi\mu a\mathsf A\mathbf V$. The leading [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md) correction therefore reduces longitudinal motion more strongly than transverse motion.
