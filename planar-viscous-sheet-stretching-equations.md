# Planar viscous-sheet stretching equations

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous sheet](viscous-sheet.md)

A symmetric thin [viscous sheet](viscous-sheet.md) with shear-free faces has leading longitudinal [velocity](velocity.md) independent of thickness coordinate $z$, and [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives $w=-zu_x$. [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) and the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) give $\sigma_{zz}=-p_a+\gamma h_{xx}/2$ and $\sigma_{xx}=-p_a+\gamma h_{xx}/2+4\mu u_x$. The effective longitudinal tension per unit transverse width is $\mathcal T=4\mu h u_x+\gamma hh_{xx}/2-\gamma h_x^2/4+2\gamma$. The last two terms include the horizontal projections of the two [surface tension](surface-tension.md) pulls. [Force balance](force-balance.md) gives $\mathcal T_x=0$, yielding the displayed longitudinal equation; the face kinematic conditions give the displayed [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) equation.
