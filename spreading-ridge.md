# Spreading ridge

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)

An accreting divergent plate boundary. In the symmetric normal-spreading model, its normal [velocity](velocity.md) equals the mean normal [velocity](velocity.md) of the adjoining plates; motion parallel to the ridge does not change that normal compatibility condition.
