# Real numerical divisor classes

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical equivalence of divisors](numerical-equivalence-of-divisors.md)

The finite-dimensional [real vector space](real-vector-space.md) $N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}$ consists of real combinations of [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md) modulo [numerical equivalence of divisors](numerical-equivalence-of-divisors.md). Its elements are numerical divisor classes. The space of curve classes paired with it is $N_1(X)_{\mathbb R}$.
