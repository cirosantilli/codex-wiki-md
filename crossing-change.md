# Crossing change

↑ **Parent:** [Knot diagram](knot-diagram.md)

A crossing change exchanges the overpassing and underpassing strands at one crossing of a [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md). It generally changes the [knot](knot.md); unlike a [Reidemeister move](reidemeister-move.md), it is not an [isotopy](isotopy.md). Two oriented band moves realize a crossing change as a genus-one [knot cobordism](knot-cobordism.md).
