# Cardinality of the family of strict total orders

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal arithmetic](cardinal-arithmetic.md)

For an infinite well-orderable [cardinal number](cardinal-number-split.md) $\kappa$, each strict total order is a subset of $\kappa^2$, giving at most $2^\kappa$ possibilities by the [square of an infinite cardinal](square-of-an-infinite-cardinal.md). For a lower bound, order $\kappa$ two-element blocks by the ordinal index and independently reverse each block according to a subset of $\kappa$. Transport along $\kappa\times2\cong\kappa$. Distinct subsets give distinct orders. The usual [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) development supplies these product identities; for well-orderable cardinals they are already provable in [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md).
