# Case-elimination Hilbert calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert proof system](hilbert-proof-system.md)

One presentation of [classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md) uses the schemes $A\to(B\to A)$, $(A\to(B\to C))\to((A\to(\neg B\to C))\to(A\to C))$, and $(A\to B)\to(A\to(\neg B\to C))$, together with [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) and the case rule from $A\to B$ and $\neg A\to B$ to $B$. Its [deduction theorem for propositional logic](deduction-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md) follows by induction on [logical derivations](logical-derivation.md): [conditional modus ponens](conditional-modus-ponens.md) handles a [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) line, and the second scheme handles a case-rule line.
