# Consumer (food chain)

↑ **Parent:** [Ecosystem](ecosystem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_(food_chain))

A [consumer](consumer-food-chain.md) obtains organic material by feeding on other [organisms](organism-split.md) or their products. A [consumer](consumer-food-chain.md) feeding on [primary producers](primary-producer.md) is a primary [consumer](consumer-food-chain.md); one feeding on primary [consumers](consumer-food-chain.md) is a secondary [consumer](consumer-food-chain.md). These names describe positions in a [food chain](food-chain.md), rather than permanent labels for a [species](species.md).
