# Stoquastic Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian](local-hamiltonian.md)

A [stoquastic Hamiltonian](stoquastic-hamiltonian.md) is a real Hermitian [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) with nonpositive off-diagonal entries in a specified basis, usually the [computational basis](computational-basis.md). The property depends on that basis. [Permutation matrices](permutation-matrix.md) in a [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)'s propagation terms and $|-\rangle\langle-|$ input penalties give a useful family of such operators.
