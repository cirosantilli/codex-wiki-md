# Cardinality constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md)

A [cardinality constraint](cardinality-constraint.md) restricts how many members of a specified collection are selected or satisfy a property. For [Boolean variables](boolean-variable.md), exactly $k$ selected entries gives the displayed [sum](sum.md); upper and lower bounds give at-most and at-least variants. [Constraint propagation](constraint-propagation.md) can maintain the number already selected and the number still available. A requirement involving many [variables](variable-mathematics.md) is a global constraint, even when it has a compact arithmetic description.
