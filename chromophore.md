# Chromophore

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromophore)

The part of a [molecule](molecule-split.md) responsible for absorption in a specified spectral region. Its electronic [excited states](excited-state.md) and local environment influence the optical response.
