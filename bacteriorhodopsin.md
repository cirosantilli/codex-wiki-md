# Bacteriorhodopsin

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacteriorhodopsin)

A light-driven [proton](proton.md) pump with a seven-helix membrane-spanning structure and a [retinal](retinal.md) chromophore. Its mechanical unfolding involves membrane anchoring as well as [protein](protein-split.md) and [lipid](lipid.md) interactions.
