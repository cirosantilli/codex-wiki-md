<h1 id="carre-du-champ-operator">Carré du champ operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carré_du_champ_operator)

For a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) generator and suitable real functions, this bilinear expression records the local quadratic energy. [Positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) of $\Gamma(f,f)$ follows by differentiating $P_t(f^2)\geq(P_tf)^2$. For $L=D^2-xD$ it is $f'g'$, giving the [Gaussian Dirichlet energy](gaussian-dirichlet-energy.md) after integration against the invariant measure.
