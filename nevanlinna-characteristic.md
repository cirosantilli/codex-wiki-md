# Nevanlinna characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna theory](nevanlinna-theory.md)

The characteristic of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) is the sum of its [Nevanlinna proximity function](nevanlinna-proximity-function.md) and [Nevanlinna integrated counting function](nevanlinna-integrated-counting-function.md). It treats large values and actual poles together. Replacing $f$ by $f-a$ changes the characteristic by at most $\log(1+|a|)$.
