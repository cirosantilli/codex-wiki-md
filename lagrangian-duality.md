# Lagrangian duality

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

For a [minimization problem](minimization-problem.md), the infimum of the [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) over its primal variables gives a lower bound for each allowable multiplier. Maximizing these lower bounds is the [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md). [Weak duality](weak-duality.md) always holds; [strong duality](strong-duality.md) requires further hypotheses and holds for feasible bounded [linear programs](linear-programming.md).
