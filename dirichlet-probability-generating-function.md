# Dirichlet probability generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md)

For a positive integer-valued [random variable](random-variable-split.md) of finite support, this transform is a finite [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md). Its values encode ordinary moments, $q_X(-r)=\mathbb E[X^r]$, and its derivatives at zero encode logarithmic moments: $(-1)^rq_X^{(r)}(0)=\mathbb E[(\log X)^r]$. Positive support is essential to these definitions; the transform is distinct from the ordinary [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) $\mathbb E[s^X]$.
