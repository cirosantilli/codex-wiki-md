# Solymosi sum-product theorem over the complex numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Sum-product phenomenon](sum-product-phenomenon.md)

For finite $U,V,W\subseteq\mathbb C$ with $U\ne\{0\}$ and $W\ne\{0\}$,

$$
|U+V|\,|UW|
\geq\frac1{56}|U|^{3/2}|V|^{1/2}|W|^{1/2}.
$$

In particular, addition and multiplication cannot both expand a nontrivial finite set of [complex numbers](complex-number.md) only slightly.
