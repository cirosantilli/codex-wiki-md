# Supersymmetric Ward identity

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

If an odd [graded derivation](graded-derivation.md) $Q$ preserves the action and integration measure, then $\langle QX\rangle=0$ whenever boundary contributions and anomalies vanish. This applies to finite-dimensional [zero-dimensional supersymmetric field theories](zero-dimensional-supersymmetric-field-theory.md) as well as suitably regulated field integrals.
