# Kleinman symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear-susceptibility tensor symmetry](nonlinear-susceptibility-tensor-symmetry.md)

Far from relevant resonances, when [frequency](frequency.md) dispersion of the nonlinear response can be neglected, a susceptibility can acquire full permutation symmetry among spatial indices. For second order this reduces a general symmetric-input [tensor](tensor.md)'s 18 components to 10 before material point-group reductions. Mere transparency does not establish this approximation. The name refers to [D. A. Kleinman](d-a-kleinman.md).[abstract, electronic high-frequency response and additional symmetry constraints](nonlinear-dielectric-polarization-in-optical-media-by-d-a-kleinman.md)
