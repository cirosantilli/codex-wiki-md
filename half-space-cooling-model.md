# Half-space cooling model

↑ **Parent:** [Lithosphere](lithosphere.md)

The [half-space cooling model](half-space-cooling-model.md) solves the one-dimensional [heat equation](heat-equation.md) for initially hot material cooled at a fixed-temperature surface. For constant [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) $\kappa$, a fixed interior-temperature isotherm deepens proportional to $\sqrt\tau$. Applied to an oceanic [lithosphere](lithosphere.md), it explains the leading age dependence of cooling; finite plate thickness and basal heat supply limit its extrapolation to old ages.
