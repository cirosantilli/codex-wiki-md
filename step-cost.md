# Step cost

↑ **Parent:** [Path cost](path-cost.md)

A [step cost](step-cost.md) is the cost of one action or transition in a [search problem](search-problem.md). The accumulated [path cost](path-cost.md) is the [sum](sum.md) of the [step costs](step-cost.md) along its action history. A uniform positive lower bound on [step costs](step-cost.md) makes every bounded-cost action history have bounded [search depth](search-depth.md); positivity of each individual term alone does not establish such a bound.
