<h1 id="min-25-sieve">Min_25 sieve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md)

A family of [algorithms](algorithm-split.md) for summatory [multiplicative functions](multiplicative-function.md) that first computes suitable sums over [primes](prime-number-split.md) at quotient values and then incorporates [prime powers](prime-power.md). The required prime-sum formulas and complexity depend on the particular [multiplicative function](multiplicative-function.md).
