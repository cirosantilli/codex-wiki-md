# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2005

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One quarter each went to [John L. Hall](john-l-hall.md) and [Theodor W. Hänsch](theodor-w-hansch.md) for developing precision [laser spectroscopy](laser-spectroscopy.md), including the [optical frequency comb](optical-frequency-comb.md) technique.[Individual official 2005 summary: Glauber one half for quantum optical coherence; Hall and Hänsch one quarter each for laser-based precision spectroscopy including frequency combs.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2005-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
