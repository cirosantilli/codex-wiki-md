# PCR annealing

↑ **Parent:** [PCR thermal cycle](pcr-thermal-cycle.md)

[PCR annealing](pcr-annealing.md) is association of [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) with exposed complementary [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) regions by [base pairing](base-pairing.md). It supplies the paired starting ends required for [PCR extension](pcr-extension.md). Cooling can permit this association without itself joining new [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) onto the [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md). The two templates can also pair with one another again, so exposure after [PCR denaturation](pcr-denaturation.md) is an opportunity for binding, not a guarantee of productive copying.[Description, first and final paragraphs: binding to complementary templates precedes primer-dependent copying](principles-of-pcr-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)

In the example below, $5'\text{-}\mathrm{AGTC}\text{-}3'$ pairs with the aligned template segment $3'\text{-}\mathrm{TCAG}\text{-}5'$. The opposite end labels show [antiparallel nucleic acid strands](antiparallel-nucleic-acid-strands.md). Pairing the right letters in the wrong directional display does not supply a correctly depicted starting end. These four-[nucleotide](nucleotide.md) strands are a teaching example, not a laboratory [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) recommendation.

The binding [temperature](temperature-split.md) balances enough productive pairing against unwanted binding. Its appropriate value depends on the [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) and reaction conditions; increasing it can improve [PCR specificity](pcr-specificity.md) but can also prevent useful binding. A predicted binding setting therefore needs experimental assessment rather than being inferred from the label PCR.[Primer annealing optimization, paragraphs beginning In this step, Note that the calculated, and If nonspecific PCR products](pcr-cycling-parameters-six-key-considerations-for-success-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
