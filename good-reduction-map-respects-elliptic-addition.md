# Good-reduction map respects elliptic addition

↑ **Parent:** [Good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

An integral [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) with unit [elliptic-curve discriminant](elliptic-curve-discriminant.md) defines a smooth proper elliptic curve over the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md). Properness identifies points over the [local field](local-field.md) with integral sections. The group law, constructed by adding degree-zero divisor classes, is a morphism over the valuation ring, and base change to the [residue field](residue-field.md) preserves it. Hence reducing primitive projective coordinates is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md), including exceptional affine addition cases.
