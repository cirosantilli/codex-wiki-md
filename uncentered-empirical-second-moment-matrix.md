# Uncentered empirical second-moment matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Sample mean and covariance](sample-mean-and-covariance.md)

The uncentered empirical second-moment matrix averages the outer products of the observations. Its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\operatorname{Cov}(X)+\mathbb E[X]\mathbb E[X]^\top$. It is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) when the population [expected value](expected-value.md) is known to be zero. It differs from a [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md) centered at the sample mean.
