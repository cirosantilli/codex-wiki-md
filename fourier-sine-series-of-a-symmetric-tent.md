# Fourier sine series of a symmetric tent

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md)

On $0\leq x\leq L$, with $L>0$, the tent $f(x)=\min(x,L-x)$ has [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) coefficients $b_n=4L\sin(n\pi/2)/(\pi^2n^2)$. Split the coefficient integral at $L/2$ and integrate its two linear pieces by parts; their cosine endpoint terms cancel and their sine terms add. Reflection about the midpoint makes every even-index coefficient zero, and the remaining odd-index coefficients alternate in sign. The $n^{-2}$ bound gives [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) and [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md); the series equals the continuous tent, including its zero endpoints.
