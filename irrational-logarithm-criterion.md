# Irrational logarithm criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

For [integers](integer.md) $a,b>1$, rationality of $\log_a b$ is equivalent to equality of some positive [integer](integer.md) powers $a^p=b^q$. Exponentiation proves one direction and taking [logarithms](logarithm.md) proves the other. Thus multiplicatively independent [integers](integer.md) give an [irrational number](irrational-number.md) as their logarithm ratio. For distinct prime bases and arguments, [unique factorization](unique-factorization-in-an-integral-domain.md) supplies that independence.
