# Amplitude transmittance

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics-split.md)

[Amplitude transmittance](amplitude-transmittance.md) is the local ratio of transmitted to incident [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) in a thin optical-element model. It includes [wave phase](phase-waves.md) as well as attenuation. For identical incident and transmitted media at normal incidence, the corresponding intensity [transmittance](transmittance.md) is $|t|^2$; $t$ itself is not the transmitted [power](power.md) fraction. A pure [phase screen](phase-screen.md) has $|t|=1$.
