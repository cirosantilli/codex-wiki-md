# Bipolar disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A grouping of disorders involving manic or hypomanic episodes and, commonly, depressive episodes. Shared [genetic liability](genetic-liability.md) with another diagnosis does not make the diagnoses interchangeable or establish an identical clinical course.
