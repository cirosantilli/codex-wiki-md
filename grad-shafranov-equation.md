# Grad-Shafranov equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grad–Shafranov_equation)

The Grad-Shafranov equation governs the poloidal magnetic flux of an axisymmetric magnetohydrostatic equilibrium. Pressure and toroidal-field contributions depend on the flux function. The [Grad-Shafranov equation for a force-free magnetic field](grad-shafranov-equation-for-a-force-free-magnetic-field.md) is its zero-pressure-gradient reduction.
