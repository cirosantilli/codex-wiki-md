# Statistical weight of an atomic level

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics.md)

The statistical weight of an atomic level counts its degenerate quantum states. With no external field resolving magnetic substates, a specified total angular momentum $J$ gives $2J+1$ states. An unresolved term can have a larger summed weight. These weights enter the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md), [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md) and detailed-balance relations; term and resolved-level weights must not be interchanged.
