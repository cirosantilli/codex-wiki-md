# Complex Hahn-Banach theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md)

A bounded complex-linear [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on a complex [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of a [normed space](normed-vector-space-split.md) extends to the whole space with the same [operator norm](operator-norm.md). To deduce this from the real [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md), extend the real part $u$ under the bound $\|\ell\|\|x\|$ on the underlying real [vector space](vector-space-split.md), then put $L(x)=u(x)-iu(ix)$. This is complex-linear and agrees with $\ell$ on its original domain. Given $x$, choose a complex number $a$ of modulus one with $aL(x)=|L(x)|$. Then $|L(x)|=\operatorname{Re}L(ax)=u(ax)\leq\|\ell\|\|x\|$. Restriction gives the reverse [operator norm](operator-norm.md) inequality.
