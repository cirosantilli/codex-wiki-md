# XY exchange interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Single-excitation subspace](single-excitation-subspace.md)

The isotropic XY exchange interaction between two [qubits](qubit.md) is $E=(X\otimes X+Y\otimes Y)/2$. Its action is

$$
E|00\rangle=E|11\rangle=0,\qquad E|01\rangle=|10\rangle,\qquad E|10\rangle=|01\rangle.
$$

Indeed, $X|0\rangle=|1\rangle$, $X|1\rangle=|0\rangle$, $Y|0\rangle=i|1\rangle$, and $Y|1\rangle=-i|0\rangle$. The two terms cancel on equal bits and add on unequal bits. Thus a network [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=\sum_{a,b}K_{ab}E_{ab}$ preserves excitation number; in the [single-excitation subspace](single-excitation-subspace.md), it is the weighted adjacency [matrix](matrix.md), moving an excitation from $a$ to $b$ with hopping coefficient $K_{ab}$.
