# Universal covering Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

The [universal cover](universal-cover.md) of a connected [Lie group](lie-group.md) carries a unique lifted [Lie group](lie-group.md) structure with a chosen identity, making its [covering map](covering-space.md) a [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md). The kernel is a discrete central [subgroup](subgroup.md): conjugation of any kernel element is a continuous map from a connected [group](group-split.md) into a discrete set, hence constant. Every connected [Lie group](lie-group.md) is therefore a quotient of its [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) cover by a discrete central [subgroup](subgroup.md).
