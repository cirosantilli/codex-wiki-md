# Stable density stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Density stratification](density-stratification.md)

A fluid has stable density stratification when a small vertical displacement produces a restoring [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) force. In a gravitational field this normally means that [mass density](density.md) increases downward.
