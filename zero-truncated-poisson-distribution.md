# Zero-truncated Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

A [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) conditioned on a positive count has [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $\mathbb P(Y=y)=\theta^y/[y!(e^\theta-1)]$ for $y\ge1$ and $\theta>0$.
