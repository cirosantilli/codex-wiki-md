# Nascent chain

↑ **Parent:** [Polypeptide](polypeptide.md)

A [nascent chain](nascent-chain.md) is a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) still being synthesized by a [ribosome](ribosome.md). Its exposed targeting information can direct entry into the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) before [biological translation](translation-biology.md) finishes.
