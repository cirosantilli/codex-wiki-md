# Smooth-cutoff scalar propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md)

A [smooth-cutoff scalar propagator](smooth-cutoff-scalar-propagator.md) is the inverse of a positive regulated quadratic kernel. It agrees with the unregulated [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md) at low [momentum](momentum.md) and decreases rapidly at high [momentum](momentum.md). Smooth suppression is not identical to vanishing support. Its cutoff derivative is the line weight in an exact [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md).
