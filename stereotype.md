# Stereotype

↑ **Parent:** [Social cognition](social-cognition.md)

A belief or expectation about characteristics of members of a social group. Its application to a person can ignore relevant individuating information; a [stereotype](stereotype.md) is not identical to an evaluative attitude or discriminatory action.
