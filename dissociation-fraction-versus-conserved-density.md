# Dissociation fraction versus conserved density

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction formula-unit conservation](reaction-formula-unit-conservation.md)

For $A\rightleftharpoons2B$, $\alpha^2/(1-\alpha)=K_N/(4M)$. The left side increases monotonically on $0<\alpha<1$, so increasing the conserved formula-unit [density](density.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) reduces dissociation in the ideal model.
