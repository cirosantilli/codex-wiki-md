# Two-dimensional harmonic-trap Bose condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

For an ideal [Bose gas](bose-gas.md) in a two-dimensional isotropic harmonic trap, the leading [density of states](density-of-states.md) measured above its [ground state](ground-state.md) is $D(E)=E/(\hbar\omega)^2$. At saturation of the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md), $N_{\mathrm{ex}}=(k_BT/(\hbar\omega))^2\int_0^\infty u/(e^u-1)\,du=(\pi^2/6)(k_BT/(\hbar\omega))^2$. The finite excited-state capacity gives the displayed [critical temperature](critical-temperature.md) and [condensate fraction](condensate-fraction.md) $N_0/N=1-(T/T_c)^2$ below it. The appropriate trapped-gas [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) fixes $N\omega^2$ as $N\to\infty$ and $\omega\to0$; a finite trap exhibits [finite-size rounding](finite-size-rounding.md). The [number density](number-density.md) is inhomogeneous, and the physical [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) approaches the oscillator [ground state](ground-state.md) [energy](energy-split.md), not an unshifted zero by convention.
