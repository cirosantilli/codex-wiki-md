# Wavelength linewidth of a damped-oscillator power resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Absorbed-power bandwidth of a viscously damped oscillator](absorbed-power-bandwidth-of-a-viscously-damped-oscillator.md)

Converting the two half-power [frequencies](frequency.md) using $\lambda=2\pi c/\omega$ gives $\Delta\lambda=2\pi c(1/\omega_--1/\omega_+)$. Since $\omega_-\omega_+=\omega_0^2$, this reduces exactly to the displayed expression. The [wavelength](wavelength.md) endpoints are not symmetric around $2\pi c/\omega_0$; a symmetric narrow-line sketch is an approximation. This conversion concerns the same measured response versus a reparametrized axis, not a transformation between [spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) conventions.
