# Fermi contour

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

For a two-dimensional [energy band](energy-band.md), a Fermi contour is the set of [wavevectors](wavevector.md) at the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). It is the two-dimensional counterpart of a [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). At a regular value it consists locally of curves; at a band extremum or a crossing it can acquire isolated points or intersecting branches. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), each band's occupied region satisfies $E_n(\mathbf k)<E_F$, so the contours must be labelled by band when determining occupations. The [Fermi contours of an isotropic Rashba band](fermi-contours-of-an-isotropic-rashba-band.md) illustrate why two contours need not belong to two different bands.
