# Quadratic functional

↑ **Parent:** [Functional](functional.md)

A quadratic functional on a real vector space combines a [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) $B(v,v)$, a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell(v)$ and a constant. If $B$ is a symmetric [bounded bilinear form](bounded-bilinear-form.md), its [first variation](first-variation.md) is $DI(v)[w]=2B(v,w)-2\ell(w)$. For a strictly positive $B$, an existing stationary point is the unique minimizer by the [quadratic variational principle for a symmetric positive operator](quadratic-variational-principle-for-a-symmetric-positive-operator.md).
