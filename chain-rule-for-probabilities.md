# Chain rule for probabilities

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional probability](conditional-probability.md)

When all conditioning events have positive [probability](probability.md), repeatedly apply the definition of [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) to factor a joint event into successive conditional factors. For a sequence of discrete responses, this yields the product of conditional masses along the actual outcome path. It requires no unconditional independence assumption.
