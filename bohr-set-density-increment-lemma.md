# Bohr-set density increment lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Bourgain bound for three-term-progression-free sets](bourgain-bound-for-three-term-progression-free-sets.md)

Let $A$ have relative density $\alpha$ in a rank-$d$ [Regular Bohr set](regular-bohr-set.md) $B$, and suppose $A$ has no nonconstant three-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md). Then either

$$
|A|\leq(d/\alpha)^{O(d)}|B|^{1/2},
$$

or some translate of $A$ has relative density at least $(1+c\alpha)\alpha$ in a regular [Bohr set](bohr-set.md) $B'\subseteq B$ of rank at most $d+1$ and width at least $\rho(B)(\alpha/d)^{O(1)}$.
