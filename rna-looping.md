# RNA looping

↑ **Parent:** [RNA secondary structure](rna-secondary-structure.md)

Bringing separated [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) segments into proximity while an intervening segment forms a loop. Multivalent [RNA-binding proteins](rna-binding-protein.md) can stabilize this geometry, with consequences for accessibility and splice-site use.
