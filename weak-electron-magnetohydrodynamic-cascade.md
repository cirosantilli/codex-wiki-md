# Weak electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade](electron-magnetohydrodynamic-cascade.md)

For $b_ll_\parallel/l_\perp\ll1$, the [weak wave cascade](weak-wave-cascade.md) estimate gives $\tau_{\mathrm{cas}}\sim l_\perp^3/(v_Ad_i b_l^2l_\parallel)$. Constant [energy flux](energy-flux.md) $\epsilon\sim v_A^2b_l^2/\tau_{\mathrm{cas}}$ yields the displayed amplitude. An additionally isotropic local cascade has $b_l\propto l^{1/2}$ and shell-integrated [magnetic energy spectrum](magnetic-energy-spectrum.md) $E_B(k)\propto k^{-2}$. These statements are phenomenological consequences of the chosen locality and decorrelation assumptions.
