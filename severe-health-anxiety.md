# Severe health anxiety

↑ **Parent:** [Health anxiety](health-anxiety.md)

Persistent and disabling concern about illness, often involving threatening interpretations, checking, [reassurance seeking](reassurance-seeking.md) or avoidance. Its [psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md) is distinct from deciding whether a physical illness exists; a [clinical formulation](clinical-formulation.md) must allow both to be relevant.
