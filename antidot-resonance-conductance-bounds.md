# Antidot resonance conductance bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant antidot backscattering](resonant-antidot-backscattering.md)

A spin-resolved resonant path adds at most $e^2/h$ to the [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) of directly transmitted spectator channels. Conversely, resonant transfer into a returning edge removes at most $e^2/h$ from an otherwise open channel. For an isolated level, $T(E)=\Gamma_L\Gamma_R/[(E-\epsilon_d)^2+(\Gamma_L+\Gamma_R)^2/4]$ reaches one only for equal couplings. Integer offsets follow the channel connectivity and do not follow from a local [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) alone. [temperature](temperature-split.md), asymmetry, coherent background paths and inelastic processes can change the apparent resonance height and shape.
