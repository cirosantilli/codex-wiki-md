# Equal-angle relativistic elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

For two identical particles of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$, one initially stationary and the other with [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $T$, symmetric final paths at $\pm\theta$ force equal final [energies](energy-split.md) $Mc^2+T/2$. The [relativistic energy-momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md) and longitudinal [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) give the title formula. The angle between the paths is $2\theta$, tending to $90^\circ$ at low [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and to zero at high [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
