# Blowing up (algebraic geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Birational geometry](birational-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blowing_up)

The [projective morphism](projective-morphism.md) $\operatorname{Proj}_X\bigoplus_{j\ge0}\mathcal I^j\to X$ associated to the powers of the ideal sheaf of a closed centre. Off that centre it is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Blowing up a smooth point of an $n$-dimensional smooth variety replaces the point by a [projective space](projective-space-split.md) $\mathbb P^{n-1}$, the [algebraic exceptional divisor](algebraic-exceptional-divisor.md).
