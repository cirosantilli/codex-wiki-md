# Correlation volume

↑ **Parent:** [Correlation length](correlation-length.md)

A volume over which order-parameter fluctuations are correlated. For one isotropic [correlation length](correlation-length.md) $\xi$, its scale is $\xi^D$; with anisotropic lengths it is the product of the appropriate directional lengths. A [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md) compares fluctuations averaged over this volume with the squared mean [order parameter](order-parameter.md).
