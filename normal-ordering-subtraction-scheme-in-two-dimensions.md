# Normal-ordering subtraction scheme in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md)

For a massive [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) in two spacetime dimensions with a polynomial potential and no derivative interactions, every connected subgraph has [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) $\omega=2(1-V)$, where $V$ counts [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md). The only divergent loop subgraphs with external legs have one vertex and consist of self-contractions. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) removes them. With $T_\Lambda=\int_{|k|<\Lambda}d^2k/[(2\pi)^2(k^2+m^2)]$, the ordinary-field potential representing the normal-ordered interaction is $e^{-T_\Lambda\partial_\phi^2/2}V(\phi)$. The exponential terminates for a polynomial. After these subtractions all remaining loop subgraphs have negative [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md), so no additional divergent field-dependent [counterterms](counterterm.md) occur. Constant [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) subtractions and finite [on-shell renormalization scheme](on-shell-renormalization-scheme.md) conditions are separate.
