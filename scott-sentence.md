# Scott sentence

↑ **Parent:** [Countable infinitary logic](countable-infinitary-logic.md)

A Scott sentence for a countable structure is a sentence of [countable infinitary logic](countable-infinitary-logic.md) satisfied, among countable structures in the same [first-order language](first-order-language.md), exactly by its [isomorphic](isomorphism.md) copies. It may also have uncountable models. [Scott formulas](scott-formula.md) and stabilized [back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md) equivalences give its construction.
