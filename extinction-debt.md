# Extinction debt

↑ **Parent:** [Extinction](extinction.md)

A delayed future loss of [species](species.md) caused by past environmental change. Habitat loss or fragmentation can leave surviving populations that are not viable in the long term, so present [species richness](species-richness.md) exceeds what the altered system can sustain. The delay provides a potential opportunity for restoration or reconnection before losses are realized.[Abstract: delayed losses following habitat fragmentation](habitat-destruction-and-the-extinction-debt-by-david-tilman-and-others.md)
