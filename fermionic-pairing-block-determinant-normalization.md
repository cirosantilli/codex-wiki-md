# Fermionic pairing-block determinant normalization

↑ **Parent:** [BCS pairing-field effective action](bcs-pairing-field-effective-action.md)

A two-component [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md) $(\psi_{k\uparrow},\bar\psi_{-k\downarrow})$ for every k accounts for both spin species once after relabelling. Its [Grassmann integral](berezin-integral.md) contributes one [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md). A fully doubled spin-particle-hole basis instead has redundancy and requires one half in the [trace](matrix-trace.md) logarithm or a corresponding Pfaffian convention. A reliable check is the zero-gap limit: the [grand potential](grand-potential.md) must become $-2\beta^{-1}\sum_k\ln(1+e^{-\beta\xi_k})$, with exactly two spin states and no invented vacuum offset.
