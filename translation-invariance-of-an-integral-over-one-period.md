# Translation invariance of an integral over one period

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

For an [integrable](integrability.md) [periodic function](periodic-function.md) of period $P>0$, its [integral](integral-split.md) over an interval of length $P$ is independent of the starting point. Write $a=mP+r$ with $m$ an [integer](integer-split.md) and $0\leq r<P$. By a [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) and the defining identity of a [periodic function](periodic-function.md),

$$
\int_a^{a+P}f=\int_r^{r+P}f=\int_r^Pf+\int_P^{P+r}f=\int_r^Pf+\int_0^rf=\int_0^Pf.
$$

No [differentiability](differentiability.md) is required. This also allows the [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) of a [periodic function](periodic-function.md) to be computed on any interval of one full [period](period-of-a-function.md).
