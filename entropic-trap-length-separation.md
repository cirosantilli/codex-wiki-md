# Entropic-trap length separation

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

Repeated transitions between a broad reservoir and a narrow channel can accumulate length-dependent capture and relaxation delays. Uniform inside-channel drift alone may be [length](length.md) independent and therefore insufficient for separation. A time-dependent drive can retain the length-sensitive delays, while stronger [advection](advection.md) raises throughput but can erase selectivity. A quantitative ordering requires the actual barrier, chain dynamics and driving protocol.
