# Orowan bypass

↑ **Parent:** [Precipitation hardening](precipitation-hardening.md)

A [dislocation](dislocation.md) unable to shear an obstacle can bow around it and leave a loop. Balancing the Peach-Koehler force against the tension of a curved [dislocation](dislocation.md) gives the critical scale $\Delta\tau\simeq2T_\ell/(b\ell)$, where $\ell$ is the free obstacle span on the slip plane. Centre pitch and free span are not interchangeable when particle size is appreciable.
