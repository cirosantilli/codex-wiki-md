# Universal sentence

↑ **Parent:** [Universal first-order theory](universal-first-order-theory.md)

A [universal sentence](universal-sentence.md) has the form $\forall\mathbf x\,\psi(\mathbf x)$ with [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md) $\psi$, including an empty quantifier block. Its truth passes from a [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) to every [substructure of a first-order structure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md): tuples from the smaller domain are also tuples of the larger domain, and quantifier-free truth agrees. Therefore a [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) of [universal sentences](universal-sentence.md) has a [first-order model](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) class closed under [substructures of a first-order structure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md).
