# Expected utility

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

The [expected value](expected-value.md) of the [utility function](utility-function-split.md) applied to a random outcome, using the decision-maker's probabilities. For a finite lottery it is $\sum_xp(x)u(x)$. Under [risk neutrality](risk-neutrality.md) and [quasilinear utility](quasilinear-utility.md), auction utility is valuation minus payment upon winning and zero upon losing, so its expectation is the winning probability times the payoff conditional on winning.
