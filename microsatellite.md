# Microsatellite

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)

A short tandem repeat in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Repeat-length changes can arise through [DNA polymerase slippage](dna-polymerase-slippage.md), making both biological instability and amplification artifacts relevant to measurement.
