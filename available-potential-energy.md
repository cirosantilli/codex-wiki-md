# Available potential energy

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)

The portion of a fluid system's [potential energy](potential-energy.md) that can be released by admissible rearrangement relative to a stable reference state. In a uniformly stratified incompressible fluid, the quadratic density is $A=\rho_0N^2\xi_z^2/2=\rho_0b^2/(2N^2)$, where $\xi_z$ is vertical [fluid displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) and $b$ the buoyancy perturbation. It is positive for stable stratification and measures the restoring energy of [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md).
