# Source-volume blocking of displacement ventilation

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement ventilation](displacement-ventilation.md)

A source volume $Q$ makes roof outflow exceed floor inflow by $Q$. At zero floor inflow, an entraining plume cannot support a steady cold lower layer; the warm layer reaches the floor. With [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'=B/Q$, the entire hydrostatic head drives roof discharge $Q=A_e\sqrt{g'H}$. This gives the stated threshold. Equal splitting of [pressure](pressure.md) head is inappropriate at blocking. The same result in physical-area notation is $Q_c=(2C_d^2A_{\rm geom}^2BH)^{1/3}$.
