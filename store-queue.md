# Store queue

↑ **Parent:** [Store buffer](store-buffer.md)

An age-ordered structure tracking [store instructions](store-instruction.md), their [memory addresses](memory-address.md) and data, and their eligibility to become visible. An implementation may distinguish a speculative [store queue](store-queue.md) from the committed [store buffer](store-buffer.md).
