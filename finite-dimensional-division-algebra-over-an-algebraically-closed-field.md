# Finite-dimensional division algebra over an algebraically closed field

↑ **Parent:** [Division algebra](division-algebra.md)

Let $D$ be a finite-dimensional [division algebra](division-algebra.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$. Left multiplication by $a\in D$ has an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda\in k$ and a nonzero eigenvector $v$. Thus $(a-\lambda)v=0$; multiplying by $v^{-1}$ gives $a=\lambda$. Every element is scalar, so $D=k$.
