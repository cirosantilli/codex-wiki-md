# D-wave superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [D-wave superconductivity](d-wave-superconductivity.md)

A [superconductor](superconductor.md) whose pairing [order parameter](order-parameter.md) has the angular or crystal-symmetry structure of [d-wave superconductivity](d-wave-superconductivity.md). The sign of the gap depends on momentum direction and nodes can permit low-energy [fermionic Bogoliubov quasiparticles](fermionic-bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md).
