# Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For Lorentzian boundary metric $\eta$ and $z>0$, this [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) patch has unit curvature radius. Its [orthonormal coframe](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) has connection $\omega^a{}_3=-\theta^a$ and curvature $\Omega^a{}_b=-\theta^a\wedge\theta_b$, giving [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) $-1$. The full local [isometry](isometry.md) algebra is $\mathfrak{so}(d-1,2)$ in spacetime dimension $d$. A patch need not support every local Killing flow for all parameter values.
