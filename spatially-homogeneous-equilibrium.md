# Spatially homogeneous equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

A spatially homogeneous equilibrium of a [reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md) is a constant state $u_*$ satisfying $f(u_*)=0$. Since its spatial derivatives vanish, its stability to homogeneous perturbations is determined by the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) $Df(u_*)$; diffusion can additionally destabilize nonconstant modes.
