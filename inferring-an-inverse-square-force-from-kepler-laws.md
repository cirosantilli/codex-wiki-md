# Inferring an inverse-square force from Kepler laws

↑ **Parent:** [Binet equation](binet-equation.md)

Constant [areal velocity](areal-velocity.md) gives constant specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $l=r^2\dot\theta$, hence zero transverse acceleration and a [central force](central-force.md). For a focus-based [ellipse](ellipse.md) $u=1/r=(1+\varepsilon\cos\theta)/A$, one has $u_{\theta\theta}+u=1/A$. The [Binet equation](binet-equation.md) then gives the displayed radial acceleration, proving an attractive [inverse-square law](inverse-square-law.md). An orbit's shape alone does not specify its acceleration without a time law.
