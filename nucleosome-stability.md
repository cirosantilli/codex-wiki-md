# Nucleosome stability

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleosome](nucleosome.md)

Resistance of a histone–DNA particle to loss or rearrangement of its components under specified conditions. Competitor-induced [histone transfer](histone-transfer.md) can reveal destabilization even while a [DNA-binding protein](dna-binding-protein.md) remains attached.
