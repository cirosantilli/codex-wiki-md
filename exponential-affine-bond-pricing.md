# Exponential-affine bond pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md)

If a discrete-time [martingale deflator](martingale-deflator.md) obeys $Y_t/Y_{t-1}=e^{X_t}$ for an [affine process](affine-process.md) $X$, unit [zero-coupon bonds](zero-coupon-bond.md) have exponential-affine prices. For one-step coefficients $A,B$, the recursion is $\alpha(0)=\beta(0)=0$, $\alpha(n+1)=A(1+\alpha(n))$, $\beta(n+1)=\beta(n)+B(1+\alpha(n))$.
