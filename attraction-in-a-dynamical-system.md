# Attraction in a dynamical system

↑ **Parent:** [Attractor](attractor.md)

[Attraction in a dynamical system](attraction-in-a-dynamical-system.md) means convergence of nearby forward trajectories toward a specified invariant state or set. Attraction and [Lyapunov stability](lyapunov-stability.md) are separate properties; together they give the usual local [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md) of an equilibrium.
