# Second harmonic and mean displacement of a weakly cubic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [First-order canonical removal of a cubic oscillator perturbation](first-order-canonical-removal-of-a-cubic-oscillator-perturbation.md)

With $\vartheta=\omega t+\delta$, a weak cubic potential produces a nonzero mean position and a second harmonic at first order. There is no first-order [frequency](frequency.md) shift. Higher-order [frequency](frequency.md) changes can accumulate [phase](phase-waves.md) over sufficiently long times, so the first-order expression is not a uniform all-time approximation.
