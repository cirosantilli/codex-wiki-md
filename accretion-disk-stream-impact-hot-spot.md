# Accretion-disk stream-impact hot spot

↑ **Parent:** [Nonmagnetic cataclysmic variable](nonmagnetic-cataclysmic-variable.md)

The bright region where the stream from [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) strikes an [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md). Dissipation of relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) produces a localized source of light whose visibility changes around the [orbital period](orbital-period.md).
