# Kinetic interchange advection cancellation

↑ **Parent:** [Interchange instability](interchange-instability.md)

In the steep-gradient, low-[plasma beta](plasma-beta.md) limit of [kinetic magnetohydrodynamics](kinetic-magnetohydrodynamics.md), magnetic induction gives $\delta b_x=ik_\parallel\xi_x$ and $u_x=-i\omega\xi_x$. Therefore the forcing of the [drift-kinetic equation](drift-kinetic-equation.md) is $(u_x+v_\parallel\delta b_x)f_0'=(-i\omega+ik_\parallel v_\parallel)\xi_xf_0'$. It cancels the streaming operator on $\delta f=-\xi_xf_0'$, leaving no leading [kinetic phase mixing](free-transport-phase-mixing.md) on this displacement branch. Freezing the parallel derivative to the equilibrium direction would lose this cancellation.
