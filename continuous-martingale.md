# Continuous martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md)

A [continuous martingale](continuous-martingale.md) is a [continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md) whose paths are continuous outside one null event. Continuity does not remove the integrability or conditional-expectation requirements in the [martingale](martingale-split.md) definition.
