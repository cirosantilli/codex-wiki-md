# Dirichlet energy dissipation with a dynamic heat boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic boundary condition for the heat equation](dynamic-boundary-condition-for-the-heat-equation.md)

For a smooth [heat equation](heat-equation.md) solution $w_t=\Delta w$ with $w_t+\alpha\partial_nw=0$ on the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) and nonnegative $\alpha$, [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md) gives the displayed dissipation identity for $E=\int_V|\nabla w|^2$. The outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) is used. Writing the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) loss as $\alpha(\partial_nw)^2$ avoids division by $\alpha$ and remains valid where $\alpha=0$. A stationary solution is harmonic with $\alpha\partial_nw=0$; it need not be a particular fixed-data [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) solution.
