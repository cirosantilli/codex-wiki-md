# Gamete

↑ **Parent:** [Sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md)

A gamete is a reproductive [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) that can fuse with another [gamete](gamete.md) during [fertilization](fertilization.md). In the ordinary [diploid](diploid.md) inheritance model, a [haploid](haploid.md) [gamete](gamete.md) carries one copy at each [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md).
