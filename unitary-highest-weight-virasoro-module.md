# Unitary highest-weight Virasoro module

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

A [unitary highest-weight Virasoro module](unitary-highest-weight-virasoro-module.md) is generated by a normalized vector satisfying the displayed relations, with a positive-definite [inner product](inner-product.md) and $L_n^\dagger=L_{-n}$. Its [Virasoro descendants](virasoro-descendant.md) have $L_0$ eigenvalues $h+N$ for nonnegative integral levels $N$. The commutator gives $\|L_{-1}|h\rangle\|^2=2h$, so unitarity requires $h\ge0$. Such a module is a quotient of a [Virasoro Verma module](virasoro-verma-module.md); its [Unitary Virasoro discrete series](unitary-virasoro-discrete-series.md) describes the cases with [central charge](central-charge.md) below one.
