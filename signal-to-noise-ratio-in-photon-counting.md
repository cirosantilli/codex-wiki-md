# Signal-to-noise ratio in photon counting

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

For source counts $S$ and independent background counts $B$ with [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md), the ideal counting [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) is $\sqrt{S+B}$, so the signal-to-noise ratio is $S/\sqrt{S+B}$. Detector noise and uncertainty in the estimated background add further variance. A larger sky aperture raises $B$, while better throughput raises $S$; both affect the faintness limit of a [spectrograph](spectrograph.md).
