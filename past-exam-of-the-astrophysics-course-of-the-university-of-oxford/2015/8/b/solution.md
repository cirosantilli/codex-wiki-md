<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A transverse physical size is angle times [angular diameter distance](../../../../angular-diameter-distance.md), not [luminosity distance](../../../../luminosity-distance.md) or [comoving distance](../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md). Converting the apparent diameter into radians gives

$$
\boxed{d=5\left(\frac{\pi}{180\times3600}\right)D_A=43.8\ \mathrm{kpc}}.
$$

This is the galaxy's projected proper diameter at the emission epoch. Its corresponding comoving diameter is three times larger.
