<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using $M=1.4M_\odot$, $R=10^4$ m and the stated [opacity](../../../../opacity.md) gives

$$
L_{\rm Edd}=2.06\times10^{31}\ \mathrm W,\qquad
\dot M_{\rm Edd}=1.11\times10^{15}\ \mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}=1.76\times10^{-8}M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr^{-1}}.
$$

If the observed X-rays represent isotropic, radiatively efficient surface [accretion](../../../../accretion.md) power, then

$$
\boxed{\frac{\dot M}{\dot M_{\rm Edd}}=\frac{L_X}{L_{\rm Edd}}=48.6,\qquad
\dot M\simeq5.38\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}}.
$$

A bolometric correction, beaming, radiation advected into the flow or [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) carried by a wind would change the mapping from the measured band [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md) to the supplied [mass](../../../../mass.md) rate. This estimate keeps the assumptions needed for comparison with the [torque](../../../../torque.md) calculation.
