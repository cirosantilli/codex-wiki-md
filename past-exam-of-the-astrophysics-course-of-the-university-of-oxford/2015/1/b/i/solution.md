<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The binary of [neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md) has $M=2.8M_\odot$ and $\mu=0.7M_\odot$. Inserting these into the circular [orbital decay timescale](../../../../../orbital-decay-timescale.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A_{\max}=2.38\times10^9\ \mathrm m,\qquad P_{\max}=10.5\ \mathrm h}.
$$

Using the integrated ten-billion-year merger criterion instead would give $15.2$ h.
