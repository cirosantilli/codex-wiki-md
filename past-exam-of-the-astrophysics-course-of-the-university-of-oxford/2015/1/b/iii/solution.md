<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For two [black holes](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) of equal [mass](../../../../../mass.md), $M=20M_\odot$ and $\mu=5M_\odot$. The prescribed instantaneous [orbital decay timescale](../../../../../orbital-decay-timescale.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A_{\max}=1.04\times10^{10}\ \mathrm m,\qquad P_{\max}=35.9\ \mathrm h}.
$$

The integrated ten-billion-year criterion gives $51.9$ h. These large initial periods compared with a neutron-star pair reflect the stronger radiation reaction of the more massive system.
