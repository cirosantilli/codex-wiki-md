<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the mixed pair, $M=11.4M_\odot$ and $\mu=(14/11.4)M_\odot=1.228M_\odot$. The prescribed instantaneous [orbital decay timescale](../../../../../orbital-decay-timescale.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A_{\max}=5.53\times10^9\ \mathrm m,\qquad P_{\max}=18.4\ \mathrm h}.
$$

The integrated ten-billion-year merger criterion instead gives $26.6$ h. Both are pre-disruption circular-orbit estimates; the final contact/capture process differs from that for two [neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md).
