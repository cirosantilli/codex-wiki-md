↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The changing [mass quadrupole moment](../../../../../mass-quadrupole-moment.md) produces an inspiral [gravitational wave](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) signal whose [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and amplitude increase toward merger. The [chirp mass](../../../../../chirp-mass.md) controls the leading [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) evolution, while higher-order phasing and the merger carry information about [binary mass ratio](../../../../../binary-mass-ratio.md) and spins. [Neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md) also introduce tidal effects. These signals motivate a search with an interferometer such as [LIGO](../../../../../ligo.md); the supplied ranges quantify the paper's proposed detection prospects, without assuming any particular later observing-run performance.
