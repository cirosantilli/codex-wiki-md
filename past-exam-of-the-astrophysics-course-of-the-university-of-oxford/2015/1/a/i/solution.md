A [neutron star merger](../../../../../neutron-star-merger.md), or a [neutron star](../../../../../neutron-star.md) tidally disrupted by a [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) outside its capture region, can leave a hot [accretion disk](../../../../../accretion-disk.md) around the remnant. Rapid [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) can power a relativistic jet and a short [gamma-ray burst](../../../../../gamma-ray-burst.md). Beaming means that an observer must lie sufficiently near the jet axis, and not every merger produces an observable burst. A clean [binary black hole](../../../../../binary-black-hole.md) merger has no substantial baryonic fuel and is not ordinarily expected to power this mechanism. Disruption in a neutron-star–black-hole pair depends on [binary mass ratio](../../../../../binary-mass-ratio.md), [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) and the [neutron star](../../../../../neutron-star.md)'s [stellar radius](../../../../../stellar-radius.md).
