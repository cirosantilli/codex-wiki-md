↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Neutron-rich material ejected dynamically or in remnant-disk outflows can undergo the [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md): successive neutron captures, followed by beta decays, make heavy nuclei. The yield depends on the amount ejected, its entropy, expansion and [electron](../../../../../electron.md) fraction; not all outflow is equally neutron rich. Radioactive products can power a [kilonova](../../../../../kilonova.md). A neutron-star–black-hole event contributes if matter escapes capture. An isolated [binary black hole](../../../../../binary-black-hole.md) merger has no analogous reservoir of neutron-rich baryonic material, so it contributes essentially no such [nucleosynthesis](../../../../../nucleosynthesis.md).
