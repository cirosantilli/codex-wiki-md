<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Infer the component [masses](../../../../mass.md) and spins from the [gravitational wave](../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) phasing, using the [chirp mass](../../../../chirp-mass.md) together with [binary mass ratio](../../../../binary-mass-ratio.md) information. The paper's representative pairs have total [masses](../../../../mass.md) $2.8$, $11.4$ and $20M_\odot$, and the more massive mergers terminate their inspirals at lower frequencies. A black-hole ringdown and its quasi-normal modes identify the final compact remnant, although both a neutron-star–black-hole merger and a sufficiently massive neutron-star pair can also leave a [black hole](../../../../black-hole-split.md).

Tidal deformability and any disruption cutoff provide a more direct signature of a [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md) than total [mass](../../../../mass.md) alone. A disrupted [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md) may supply a [gamma-ray burst](../../../../gamma-ray-burst.md), [kilonova](../../../../kilonova.md) and afterglow; these help distinguish matter-containing mergers from a clean black-hole pair. Their absence does not establish a black-hole pair, because jets are beamed and ejecta may be faint or swallowed. Measured [gravitational wave](../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md) are redshifted detector-frame [masses](../../../../mass.md); association with a host can supply the [redshift](../../../../redshift.md) needed for source-frame [masses](../../../../mass.md). Classification should combine the waveform and any electromagnetic evidence, allowing for parameter degeneracies.
