<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A hydrogen [recombination line](../../../../recombination-line.md) is a discrete [spectral line](../../../../spectral-line.md) produced when [radiative recombination](../../../../radiative-recombination.md) captures an [electron](../../../../electron.md) into an excited bound level and a subsequent [radiative cascade](../../../../radiative-cascade.md) emits [photons](../../../../photon.md). The free-to-bound capture itself produces a continuum; the later bound-to-bound transitions produce the [recombination lines](../../../../recombination-line.md). For example, [H-alpha](../../../../h-alpha.md) is the $n=3\to2$ transition in the [Balmer series](../../../../balmer-series.md).

For the neutral hydrogen absorbing an ionizing stellar continuum, the principal bound-free transition is [photoionization](../../../../photoionization.md) from the $1s$ [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) into the continuum. Most neutral hydrogen is in its [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md), and the [photoionization](../../../../photoionization.md) [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) is largest near its $13.6\ \mathrm{eV}$ threshold, falling approximately as $\nu^{-3}$ above it. Dominance of the _population-weighted opacity_ is intended here; this does not mean that every excited-state microscopic [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) is smaller at every [frequency](../../../../frequency.md).

In an ionization-bounded, optically thick nebula, direct [radiative recombination](../../../../radiative-recombination.md) into $1s$ emits an ionizing [photon](../../../../photon.md) that promptly photoionizes another hydrogen atom. One ion disappears and another is created: there is no net removal of an ion from the global budget. [Case B recombination](../../../../case-b-recombination.md) therefore uses $\alpha_B=\sum_{n\geq2}\alpha_n$, with local recycling of ground-state recombination [photons](../../../../photon.md). This is the on-the-spot approximation, rather than an unconditional property of [radiative recombination](../../../../radiative-recombination.md): if those [photons](../../../../photon.md) escape, direct capture does produce net neutralization and a Case A treatment is appropriate.
