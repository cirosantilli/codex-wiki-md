<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $Q=\dot N_\gamma$ for the [hydrogen-ionizing photon production rate](../../../../hydrogen-ionizing-photon-production-rate.md), counting only [photons](../../../../photon.md) above the hydrogen [ionization energy](../../../../ionization-energy.md), not all stellar [photons](../../../../photon.md). In steady [photoionization equilibrium](../../../../photoionization-equilibrium.md), the external supply balances the volume integral of net [Case B recombination](../../../../case-b-recombination.md):

$$
Q=\int_{r_{\rm in}}^{r_S}\alpha_rn_en_p\,4\pi r^2dr.
$$

For uniform, almost completely ionized pure hydrogen, $n_e=n_p=n_H$. Hence

$$
Q=\frac{4\pi}{3}\alpha_rn_H^2(r_S^3-r_{\rm in}^3),\qquad
\boxed{r_S=\left(r_{\rm in}^3+\frac{3Q}{4\pi\alpha_rn_H^2}\right)^{1/3}
\simeq\left(\frac{3Q}{4\pi\alpha_rn_H^2}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

The final approximation requires $r_{\rm in}\ll r_S$. The [Strömgren sphere](../../../../stromgren-sphere.md) also assumes spherical symmetry, a sharp [ionization front](../../../../ionization-front.md), a steady source older than the [recombination time](../../../../recombination-time.md), uniform [number density](../../../../number-density.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) so that $\alpha_r$ is constant, negligible dust absorption of ionizing [photons](../../../../photon.md), negligible leakage, and no important helium correction or [collisional ionization](../../../../collisional-ionization.md). Clumping would replace $n_H^2$ by a volume average of $n_en_p$.
