In an old [globular cluster](../../../../globular-cluster.md)'s [colour-magnitude diagram](../../../../colour-magnitude-diagram.md), the expected principal sequences are the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md) below the [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md), the [subgiant](../../../../subgiant.md) branch leading to the [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md), the [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) above the [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md) and extending across colour, and an [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) above the [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md), generally near and somewhat blueward of the [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md). A sufficiently deep diagram can also show a faint blue [white dwarf](../../../../white-dwarf.md) cooling sequence; [blue stragglers](../../../../blue-straggler.md) may lie above and blueward of the old [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md).

The two panels use the [colour index](../../../../color-index.md) $B-V$, increasing toward cooler, redder [stars](../../../../star.md). On a conventional apparent-[astronomical magnitude](../../../../astronomical-magnitude.md) vertical axis, brighter [stars](../../../../star.md) are higher. These coordinate conventions locate the branches just described.

**The supplied PDF does not permit a complete identification of the plotted branches.** Its upper image data are corrupt and render as a grey rectangle, covering the [main-sequence turnoffs](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md) and evolved populations; only lower fragments and $B-V$ axes survive. Thus the list above identifies the relevant [stellar evolution](../../../../stellar-evolution.md) phases and how to recognize them on an intact plot, rather than claiming that every listed sequence is visibly present in this damaged source. In particular, neither a plotted [white dwarf](../../../../white-dwarf.md) sequence nor individual [blue stragglers](../../../../blue-straggler.md) can be confirmed from it.
