<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For ordinary single [stars](../../../../star.md) in these old [globular clusters](../../../../globular-cluster.md), **no current [core-collapse supernovae](../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) are expected, and no new [black holes](../../../../black-hole-split.md) are expected from the normal plotted population**. Their [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md) are typically around $0.8M_\odot$, far below the roughly $8M_\odot$ initial-[mass](../../../../mass.md) scale for advanced burning and core collapse. More massive original [stars](../../../../star.md) exhausted their much shorter [main-sequence lifetimes](../../../../main-sequence-lifetime.md) long ago. The precise transition [mass](../../../../mass.md) depends on composition and mass loss, but this large separation makes the conclusion robust.

The low-[mass](../../../../mass.md) [stars](../../../../star.md) now leaving the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md) evolve through the [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md), helium ignition, [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) and [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md). They lose their envelopes and leave mainly carbon-oxygen [white dwarfs](../../../../white-dwarf.md), typically around $0.5$–$0.6M_\odot$ for current old-cluster progenitors, supported by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md). A visible [planetary nebula](../../../../planetary-nebula.md) is possible if the exposed core heats before the ejecta disperse; it is not guaranteed for every low-[mass](../../../../mass.md) remnant. Lower-[mass](../../../../mass.md) [main-sequence stars](../../../../main-sequence-star.md) repeat this evolution only much later, and the very lowest-[mass](../../../../mass.md) isolated [stars](../../../../star.md) can ultimately leave helium-rich degenerate remnants without igniting helium.

The clusters may already contain [neutron stars](../../../../neutron-star.md) and [black holes](../../../../black-hole-split.md) made by their original massive [stars](../../../../star.md); these remnants need not appear on an optical [colour-magnitude diagram](../../../../colour-magnitude-diagram.md). Binary interactions can instead produce stripped helium [white dwarfs](../../../../white-dwarf.md), mergers or a possible [Type Ia supernova](../../../../type-ia-supernova.md); such exceptions are distinct from normal single-[star](../../../../star.md) core collapse. No such special progenitor can be established from the damaged plots. In particular, a luminous [red giant](../../../../red-giant.md) is not by itself evidence for a massive [core-collapse supernova](../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) progenitor.
