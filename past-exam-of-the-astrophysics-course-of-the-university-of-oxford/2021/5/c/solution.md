The main intrinsic parameters for old [globular clusters](../../../../globular-cluster.md) are age, [metallicity](../../../../metallicity.md), helium abundance and the distribution of envelope [masses](../../../../mass.md) after [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md) mass loss. Distance and foreground [interstellar extinction](../../../../interstellar-extinction.md) alter the appearance and must first be removed before assigning intrinsic differences.

Age controls the [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md): a more massive [star](../../../../star.md) has a shorter [main-sequence lifetime](../../../../main-sequence-lifetime.md), approximately fuel divided by [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md). An older coeval population therefore has a smaller turnoff [mass](../../../../mass.md) and a fainter, generally redder [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md). The separation in [astronomical magnitude](../../../../astronomical-magnitude.md) between the [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md) and [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) is useful because a common [distance modulus](../../../../distance-modulus.md) cancels.

At comparable age, larger [metallicity](../../../../metallicity.md) increases stellar-envelope [opacity](../../../../opacity.md) and atmospheric line blanketing. This generally makes the [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md) and [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md) redder; metal-poor populations tend to have hotter, bluer [red giant branches](../../../../red-giant-branch.md). [Metallicity](../../../../metallicity.md) also affects [hydrogen burning](../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) through the [CNO cycle](../../../../cno-cycle.md), so a quantitative age inference needs composition-dependent stellar models.

The [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) is especially sensitive to envelope [mass](../../../../mass.md): at similar helium-core [mass](../../../../mass.md), a thinner hydrogen envelope gives a higher [effective temperature](../../../../effective-temperature.md) and hence a bluer location. [Red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md) mass loss, age and helium abundance all influence that envelope and produce the familiar “second parameter” complexity. Enhanced helium also changes mean molecular weight, [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md) and evolutionary lifetime, and can shift [main sequences](../../../../main-sequence.md) and [horizontal branches](../../../../horizontal-branch.md).

[Interstellar extinction](../../../../interstellar-extinction.md) dims the diagram, while wavelength-dependent [interstellar extinction](../../../../interstellar-extinction.md) reddens $B-V$; distance primarily supplies a vertical [distance modulus](../../../../distance-modulus.md) shift. A systematic bend or different [horizontal branch](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) colour distribution cannot be explained by distance alone. Because the source hides the evolved branches, it is not possible to assign “older”, “more metal rich”, or “bluer horizontal branch” to a particular panel reliably. The preceding mechanisms give the warranted physical interpretation without inventing the lost observational differences.
