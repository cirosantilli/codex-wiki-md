<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md), central [hydrogen burning](../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) converts four protons into helium, positrons, [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) and released [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). The net reaction is $4p\to{}^4\mathrm{He}+2e^++2\nu_e$, with positron annihilation contributing to the total roughly $26.7\ \mathrm{MeV}$ release before subtracting escaping [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In the low-[mass](../../../../mass.md) [main-sequence stars](../../../../main-sequence-star.md) of an old [globular cluster](../../../../globular-cluster.md), the [proton–proton chain](../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) generally dominates. The [CNO cycle](../../../../cno-cycle.md) makes the same net conversion and becomes important in hotter burning regions.

After central hydrogen exhaustion, a [subgiant](../../../../subgiant.md) contracts its helium core and establishes a [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md). On the [red giant branch](../../../../red-giant-branch.md), that shell supplies most of the [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md), commonly through the temperature-sensitive [CNO cycle](../../../../cno-cycle.md), while the inert helium core grows and becomes supported mainly by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md). The envelope expands and its surface cools. The helium core is not yet powered by helium [nuclear fusion](../../../../nuclear-fusion.md).

Helium ignition in a sufficiently low-[mass](../../../../mass.md) degenerate core produces the [helium flash](../../../../helium-flash.md), removing the strong [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) dominance. A [horizontal-branch star](../../../../horizontal-branch.md) subsequently burns helium in its core through the [Triple-alpha process](../../../../triple-alpha-process.md), $3\,{}^4\mathrm{He}\to{}^{12}\mathrm C+\gamma$, and through ${}^{12}\mathrm C(\alpha,\gamma){}^{16}\mathrm O$. A surrounding [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) can continue simultaneously.

After central helium exhaustion, the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) has an inert carbon-oxygen core with a [helium-burning shell](../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) and an outer [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md). Alternation between quiescent burning and helium-shell thermal pulses drives mixing and substantial mass loss. Subsequent [post-asymptotic giant branch evolution](../../../../post-asymptotic-giant-branch-evolution.md) exposes a hot core that may ionize its expelled envelope as a [planetary nebula](../../../../planetary-nebula.md). Finally a [white dwarf](../../../../white-dwarf.md) cools mainly by loss of stored thermal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), rather than sustained central [nuclear fusion](../../../../nuclear-fusion.md).
