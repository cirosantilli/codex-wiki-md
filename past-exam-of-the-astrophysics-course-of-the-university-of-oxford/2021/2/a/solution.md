<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the initially expanding branch $\dot r_i=H_ir_i$; the printed squared [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) alone also permits an initially contracting branch, which would not reach a future maximum radius. Multiply the [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) by $\dot r$ and integrate. The conserved outer-shell [specific orbital energy](../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is

$$
\epsilon=\frac12\dot r^2-\frac{GM}{r}.
$$

The initial [mass](../../../../mass.md) follows from the background [critical density](../../../../critical-density.md):

$$
M=\frac{4\pi}{3}r_i^3\rho_{m,i}(1+\delta_i)
=\frac{H_i^2r_i^3}{2G}(1+\delta_i).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\epsilon=-\frac12H_i^2r_i^2\delta_i<0.}
$$

If $E$ means the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) of a test shell of [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$, $E=m\epsilon$. If it means the total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) of the entire uniform, homologously moving perturbation, direct integration instead gives $K=3M\dot r^2/10$, $U=-3GM^2/(5r)$ and

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm sphere}=\frac35M\epsilon=-\frac3{10}MH_i^2r_i^2\delta_i.}
$$

The common convention of writing the first integral as $E$ is therefore an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) _per unit shell mass_, not the total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) of the sphere.

At [spherical-collapse turnaround](../../../../turnaround-of-spherical-collapse.md), $\dot r=0$, so $\epsilon=-GM/r_m$. Hence

$$
\boxed{r_m=\frac{GM}{|\epsilon|}=r_i\frac{1+\delta_i}{\delta_i}.}
$$

Any positive initial [density contrast](../../../../density-contrast.md) binds this pressureless perturbation in the stated [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md).
