<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Interpret $\sigma_{\mathrm{DM}}$ as a constant one-dimensional, isotropic [velocity dispersion](../../../../velocity-dispersion.md). The effective [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is $p=\rho\sigma_{\mathrm{DM}}^2$, corresponding to the stated gas-temperature analogy. For $\rho=A/r^2$, the spherical enclosed mass is

$$
M_{\mathrm{sph}}(r)=4\pi\int_0^r s^2\rho(s)\,ds=4\pi Ar.
$$

The two sides of the [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation are then

$$
\frac1\rho\frac{dp}{dr}=-\frac{2\sigma_{\mathrm{DM}}^2}{r},\qquad
-\frac{GM_{\mathrm{sph}}(r)}{r^2}=-\frac{4\pi GA}{r}.
$$

Equating them fixes the [singular isothermal sphere](../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere.md):

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{DM}}^2}{2\pi G},\qquad
\rho(r)=\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{DM}}^2}{2\pi Gr^2}.}
$$

For collisionless [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md) this is an isotropic Jeans-equation construction, not a claim that the particles thermalize by gas collisions. The central singularity and infinite total mass show that the untruncated profile is an idealization.
