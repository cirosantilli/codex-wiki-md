<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a massive star undergoing [core-collapse supernova](../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) formation, an iron-rich core cannot gain further support from exothermic fusion. [Electron captures](../../../../electron-capture.md) and nuclear [photodisintegration](../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) promote collapse. The inner core stiffens near nuclear [density](../../../../density.md) and bounces, launching a [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) into the infalling outer core. Dissociation and [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) losses drain its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), so the prompt [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) generally stalls while matter continues to accrete. [Neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) absorption behind the [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md), aided by multidimensional flow, can revive it and eject the envelope; sufficiently rapid rotation and strong magnetic fields offer additional possibilities. The remnant may be a [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md) or [black hole](../../../../black-hole-split.md). Major uncertainties include progenitor structure, nuclear [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md), [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) transport and interactions, turbulent flow, rotation and magnetic fields.

The [standing accretion shock instability](../../../../standing-accretion-shock-instability.md) is a nonradial instability of this stalled [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) and accretion flow. Feedback between advected disturbances and acoustic response can amplify large-scale sloshing or spiral motions, producing an asymmetric [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) even without an initially unstable convective gradient.[Abstract and nonradial simulations: growth of turbulence from a distorted standing shock](../../../../stability-of-standing-accretion-shocks-with-an-eye-toward-core-collapse-supernovae-by-john-m-blondin-and-others.md)

An asymmetric explosion can impart an opposite recoil to the remnant by [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md). In neutrino-driven calculations, long-lasting gravitational attraction by asymmetric, slower inner ejecta can dominate the acceleration: the gravitational tug-boat mechanism. [SASI](../../../../standing-accretion-shock-instability.md) and convection can seed the asymmetries, but the kick is not necessarily an instantaneous hydrodynamic push or uniquely attributable to [SASI](../../../../standing-accretion-shock-instability.md).[Abstract and mechanism discussion: anisotropic ejecta, gravitational acceleration and subdominant hydrodynamic forces](../../../../neutron-star-kicks-by-the-gravitational-tug-boat-mechanism-in-asymmetric-supernova-explosions-by-hans-thomas-janka.md)
