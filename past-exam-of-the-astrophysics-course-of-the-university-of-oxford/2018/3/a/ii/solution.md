The short [gamma-ray burst](../../../../../gamma-ray-burst.md) GRB 170817A followed the merger signal by about $1.7\,\mathrm s$, providing direct evidence that at least some short [gamma-ray bursts](../../../../../gamma-ray-burst.md) arise from [neutron star mergers](../../../../../neutron-star-merger.md). The association connects their high-energy emission and relativistic outflows to compact-binary coalescence. It does not establish that every short burst has this progenitor, nor that the observed gamma-ray luminosity is independent of viewing angle.[Abstract and multiwavelength counterpart discussion: GRB 170817A delay and association](../../../../../multi-messenger-observations-of-a-binary-neutron-star-merger-by-ligo-scientific-collaboration-and-others.md)
