[GW170817](../../../../../gw170817.md) supplied the first direct [gravitational wave](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) observation of a binary [neutron star](../../../../../neutron-star.md) inspiral. The chirping waveform tests compact-binary [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md), constrains masses and tidal effects, and links a class of known compact binaries to observable sources of [gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md). The electromagnetic counterpart identifies the host and distance, enabling complementary source and cosmological inference. This is distinct from the earlier binary-black-hole detections; it established a system involving matter that could also be observed electromagnetically.[Abstract and source-inference discussion: first binary-neutron-star inspiral, mass range and electromagnetic association](../../../../../gw170817-observation-of-gravitational-waves-from-a-binary-neutron-star-inspiral-by-ligo-scientific-collaboration-and-virgo-collaboration.md)
