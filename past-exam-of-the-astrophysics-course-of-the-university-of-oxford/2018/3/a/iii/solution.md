↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The evolving optical and infrared [kilonova](../../../../../kilonova.md) was consistent with radioactive heating of neutron-rich merger ejecta. Rapid neutron capture, the [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md), can synthesize nuclei heavier than iron; decay of the newly formed unstable nuclei powers the transient. Its redward evolution and large inferred opacity supported production of heavy-element-rich ejecta. Thus mergers are a demonstrated important site for heavy-element production, while the fractions of individual elements and their total cosmic contribution remain model-dependent. The transient was not a direct identification of every very heavy element.[Kilonova interpretation and conclusions: optical/infrared evolution and radioactive r-process heating](../../../../../multi-messenger-observations-of-a-binary-neutron-star-merger-by-ligo-scientific-collaboration-and-others.md)
