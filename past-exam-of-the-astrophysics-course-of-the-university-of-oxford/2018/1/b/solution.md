<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use a steady, plane, nonradiative [normal shock wave](../../../../normal-shock-wave.md) in a [perfect gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), and measure the normal speeds $v_{\mathrm u},v_{\mathrm d}>0$ in the [shock frame](../../../../shock-frame.md). The labels denote upstream and downstream. Conservation of mass, normal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gives

$$
\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}=\rho_{\mathrm d}v_{\mathrm d},\qquad
p_{\mathrm u}+\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}^2=p_{\mathrm d}+\rho_{\mathrm d}v_{\mathrm d}^2,
$$

and

$$
\frac12v_{\mathrm u}^2+\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}\frac{p_{\mathrm u}}{\rho_{\mathrm u}}
=\frac12v_{\mathrm d}^2+\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}\frac{p_{\mathrm d}}{\rho_{\mathrm d}}.
$$

The enthalpy term includes the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) work. For very large upstream [Mach number](../../../../mach-number.md), neglect $p_{\mathrm u}$ compared with $\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}^2$. If $r=\rho_{\mathrm d}/\rho_{\mathrm u}$ is the [shock compression ratio](../../../../shock-compression-ratio.md), the first two equations imply

$$
v_{\mathrm d}=\frac{v_{\mathrm u}}r,\qquad
p_{\mathrm d}=\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}^2\left(1-\frac1r\right).
$$

Substitution into the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) equation gives

$$
1=\frac1{r^2}+\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma-1}\frac{r-1}{r^2},\qquad
(r-1)\left(r-\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}\right)=0.
$$

Discard the no-shock solution $r=1$. For a nonrelativistic monatomic gas, $\gamma=5/3$, so the [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) become

$$
\boxed{\frac{\rho_{\mathrm d}}{\rho_{\mathrm u}}=\frac{v_{\mathrm u}}{v_{\mathrm d}}=4,\qquad
p_{\mathrm d}=\frac34\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}^2.}
$$

The factor four requires this [adiabatic index](../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) and negligible radiative, magnetic and escaping-particle contributions to the flux budget. For example, the same strong, nonradiative calculation with $\gamma=4/3$ gives compression seven, not four.
