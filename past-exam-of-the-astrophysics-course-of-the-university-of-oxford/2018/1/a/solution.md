A [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md) is a thin, irreversible compression layer across which fluid [density](../../../../density.md), [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) change rapidly, while conserved fluxes satisfy the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md). In the [shock frame](../../../../shock-frame.md), a compressive hydrodynamic [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) converts ordered upstream [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) into downstream thermal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and increases [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md). The upstream normal flow is supersonic; the downstream normal flow is subsonic for an ordinary gas-dynamic compression [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md).

In an accreting [binary star](../../../../binary-star.md), a supersonic [stellar wind](../../../../stellar-wind.md) can be captured by a compact companion and shocked. An [accretion stream](../../../../accretion-stream.md) striking an [accretion disk](../../../../accretion-disk.md), an [accretion-disk boundary layer](../../../../accretion-disk-boundary-layer.md), and a magnetically guided [accretion column](../../../../accretion-column.md) provide other sites where infall [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is dissipated and high-energy radiation is generated. [Shocks](../../../../shock-wave.md) heat and decelerate material; a disk also needs transport of [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) before its material can reach the compact star.

In [diffusive shock acceleration](../../../../diffusive-shock-acceleration.md), charged particles scatter from magnetic irregularities on both sides of a [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) and repeatedly cross the converging flows. The average [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gain is first order in the [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) jump divided by the particle speed. Escape competes with acceleration, producing a nonthermal distribution rather than a single downstream [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). [Supernova remnants](../../../../supernova-remnant.md) are important candidate sources of Galactic [cosmic rays](../../../../cosmic-ray.md); [shocks](../../../../shock-wave.md) associated with [active galactic nuclei](../../../../active-galactic-nucleus.md), their jets and lobes, and [gamma-ray bursts](../../../../gamma-ray-burst.md) are candidate extragalactic accelerators. Ultrarelativistic energies require sufficient size, [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and lifetime, and losses must not outpace acceleration. In particular a relativistic particle's [Larmor radius](../../../../gyroradius.md) must fit within the accelerator; the existence of a strong [shock](../../../../shock-wave.md) alone does not guarantee unlimited particle [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
