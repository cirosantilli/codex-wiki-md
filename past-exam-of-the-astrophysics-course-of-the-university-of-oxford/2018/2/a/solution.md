<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand the [density contrast](../../../../density-contrast.md) in spatial [Fourier modes](../../../../fourier-mode.md) using fixed [comoving coordinates](../../../../comoving-coordinate.md):

$$
\delta(\mathbf x,t)=\int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\,\delta_{\mathbf k}(t)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}.
$$

The spatial [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md) multiplies a mode by $-k^2$, while its comoving [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) $k=|\mathbf k|$ is time-independent. Writing $H=\dot a/a$, the [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) equation becomes

$$
\ddot\delta_{\mathbf k}+2H\dot\delta_{\mathbf k}
+\left(\frac{c_s^2k^2}{a^2}-4\pi G\bar\rho\right)\delta_{\mathbf k}=0.
$$

The proper [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) is $k^{(p)}=k/a$, since the proper [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) is $2\pi a/k$. The proper [Jeans wavenumber](../../../../jeans-wavenumber.md) balances the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and gravity terms:

$$
\boxed{k_J^{(p)}=\frac{\sqrt{4\pi G\bar\rho}}{c_s},\qquad
\ddot\delta_{\mathbf k}+2H\dot\delta_{\mathbf k}
+c_s^2\left[(k^{(p)})^2-(k_J^{(p)})^2\right]\delta_{\mathbf k}=0.}
$$
