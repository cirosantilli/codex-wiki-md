<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $k^{(p)}\ll k_J^{(p)}$, neglect [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) support. The growing and decaying [Jeans instability](../../../../jeans-instability.md) modes obey

$$
\ddot\delta_{\mathbf k}+2H\dot\delta_{\mathbf k}-4\pi G\bar\rho\,\delta_{\mathbf k}=0.
$$

In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $a\propto t^{2/3}$ gives $H=2/(3t)$, and the matter-density relation $8\pi G\bar\rho=3H^2$ gives $4\pi G\bar\rho=2/(3t^2)$. Thus

$$
\ddot\delta_{\mathbf k}+\frac4{3t}\dot\delta_{\mathbf k}-\frac2{3t^2}\delta_{\mathbf k}=0.
$$

Try a power law $\delta_{\mathbf k}\propto t^p$. Its [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
p(p-1)+\frac43p-\frac23=0,
\qquad (p-2/3)(p+1)=0.
$$

Therefore the [Einstein-de Sitter density-growth modes](../../../../einstein-de-sitter-density-growth-modes.md) are

$$
\boxed{\delta_{\mathbf k}(t)=C_+t^{2/3}+C_-t^{-1}
=D_+a+D_-a^{-3/2}.}
$$

This is a linear result while $|\delta|\ll1$; a growing mode eventually leaves that regime.
