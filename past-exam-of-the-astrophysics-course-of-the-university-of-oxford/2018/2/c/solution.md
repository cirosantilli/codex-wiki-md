<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the formal static-background limit, $H=0$ and $\bar\rho$ is constant. The gravity-dominated [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) equation reduces to

$$
\ddot\delta_{\mathbf k}-\omega_J^2\delta_{\mathbf k}=0,\qquad
\omega_J=\sqrt{4\pi G\bar\rho}.
$$

The [Static-universe Jeans modes](../../../../static-universe-jeans-modes.md) are

$$
\boxed{\delta_{\mathbf k}=A_+e^{t/t_J}+A_-e^{-t/t_J},\qquad
 t_J=(4\pi G\bar\rho)^{-1/2}.}
$$

The unstable perturbation grows exponentially rather than as $t^{2/3}$. In the expanding case, the $2H\dot\delta$ term resists peculiar motion and the falling background [density](../../../../density.md) also lengthens the gravitational growth timescale. A globally homogeneous static self-gravitating fluid is not obtained merely by turning off expansion; this calculation is the conventional local Jeans-background idealization, with the uniform background force removed or otherwise supported.
