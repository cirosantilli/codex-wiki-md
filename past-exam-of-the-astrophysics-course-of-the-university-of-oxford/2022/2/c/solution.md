<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a family of spherical profiles with a fixed dimensionless shape, write the [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) as $\Omega=-\alpha GM^2/R$, where $\alpha>0$ depends on that shape. The [virial theorem](../../../../virial-theorem.md) in [b](../b.md) and the sphere volume $V=4\pi R^3/3$ give

$$
\boxed{\overline P=\frac{\alpha}{4\pi}\frac{GM^2}{R^4}\propto\frac{GM^2}{R^4}.}
$$

For a uniform [density](../../../../density.md), $\alpha=3/5$; formally integrating $-\int_0^R GM(r)\,dM(r)/r$ for $M(r)=Mr/R$ gives $\alpha=1$. Neither the [virial theorem](../../../../virial-theorem.md) nor the initial profile guarantees a specific final shape, so the proportionality requires the stated homologous-profile assumption. Surface [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) must be handled consistently with [b](../b.md) before using either coefficient as an equilibrium model.
