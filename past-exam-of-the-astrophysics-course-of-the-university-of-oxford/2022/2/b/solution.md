<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume an isolated, bound, nonrotating Newtonian gas cloud, negligible surface [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), negligible magnetic and radiation stresses, no persistent bulk flow, and a stationary or time-averaged virialized state. The [stellar virial theorem](../../../../stellar-virial-theorem.md) then reduces to

$$
3\int P(\mathbf x)\,dV+\Omega=0,
$$

where $\Omega<0$ is the total [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md). Define the volume-averaged [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) $\overline P=V^{-1}\int P\,dV$ and average gravitational [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) $u_g=\Omega/V$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\overline P=-u_g/3.}
$$

Equivalently, for a nonrelativistic [monatomic gas](../../../../monatomic-gas.md), the thermal [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) $U=(3/2)\int P\,dV$ obeys $2U+\Omega=0$. This second derivation must not count the thermal motions again as a separate bulk [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). If the external [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is $P_s$, the correct result is $\overline P-P_s=-u_g/3$; non-negligible bulk motion adds $2T_{\rm bulk}$ to the [virial theorem](../../../../virial-theorem.md). The initial sharp-edged $r^{-2}$ profile need not survive virialization; maintaining that profile hydrostatically to its edge would generally require a surface term.
