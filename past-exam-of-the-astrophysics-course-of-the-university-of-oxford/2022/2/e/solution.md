<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a gas-pressure-supported [stellar core](../../../../stellar-core.md) of fixed [mass](../../../../mass.md) and composition, neglect boundary terms for the illustrative whole-core estimate. The [virial theorem](../../../../virial-theorem.md) gives $E=U+\Omega=-U$, and the [monatomic gas](../../../../monatomic-gas.md) gives $U=3M_ck_B\overline T/(2\mu m_u)$. Thus the effective [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) along the equilibrium sequence is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dE}{d\overline T}=-\frac{3M_ck_B}{2\mu m_u}<0.}
$$

This [negative heat capacity of a virialized gravitational system](../../../../negative-heat-capacity-of-a-virialized-gravitational-system.md) means that net [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) loss makes a self-gravitating core contract and heat. It is different from the positive local material [specific heat capacity](../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md). On the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md), [hydrogen burning](../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) supplies most of the radiated [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), so rapid contraction is arrested. Hydrogen depletion increases the [mean molecular weight](../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md); slow structural readjustment usually raises the central [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) rather than making it fall.

After core hydrogen exhaustion, a massive [stellar core](../../../../stellar-core.md) contracts and heats more rapidly until a later nuclear fuel ignites. There is no general reversal to cooling simply upon leaving the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md). If [radiation pressure](../../../../radiation-pressure.md) becomes dominant, the [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) becomes $U=U_{\rm gas}+U_{\rm rad}$ with $3\int P\,dV=2U_{\rm gas}+U_{\rm rad}$. The [virial theorem](../../../../virial-theorem.md) therefore gives $E=-U_{\rm gas}$; the radiation-dominated limit is marginally bound and its effective [adiabatic index](../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) approaches $4/3$.

Quasistatic contraction can still maintain [virial equilibrium](../../../../virial-equilibrium.md). It fails dynamically only when sufficient support is lost, for example if pair creation reduces the effective [adiabatic index](../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) below $4/3$, or when an iron core undergoes [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) and photodisintegration. A fixed-composition gas-dominated contracting core with $T\propto1/R$ is already a counterexample to any assertion that leaving the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md) alone reverses the heating or destroys [virial equilibrium](../../../../virial-equilibrium.md).
