<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An ultrarelativistic [electron](../../../../electron.md) has [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) much larger than its rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md): $(\gamma-1)m_ec^2\gg m_ec^2$, equivalently $\gamma\gg1$. Its [speed](../../../../speed.md) obeys $v/c=\sqrt{1-\gamma^{-2}}\simeq1-1/(2\gamma^2)$ and its total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is approximately $pc$. At $\gamma=10^6$, $E=\gamma m_ec^2\simeq511\ \mathrm{GeV}$.
