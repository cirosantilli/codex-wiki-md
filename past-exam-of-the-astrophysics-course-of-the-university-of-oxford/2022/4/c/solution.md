<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), so it does no [work](../../../../work.md) and $\gamma$ remains constant over an orbit when radiative losses are neglected. For the transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), $p_\perp=\gamma m_ev_\perp$ and $|\dot{\mathbf p}_\perp|=\omega_gp_\perp=eBv_\perp$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nu_g=\frac{\omega_g}{2\pi}=\frac{eB}{2\pi\gamma m_e}.}
$$

Since $10\ \mu\mathrm G=10^{-9}\ \mathrm T$,

$$
\boxed{\nu_g=2.80\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{Hz},\qquad
\omega_g=1.76\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad T_g=3.57\times10^4\ \mathrm s\simeq9.9\ \mathrm h.}
$$

This is the [gyromotion](../../../../gyromotion.md) [frequency](../../../../frequency.md), not the much higher characteristic [synchrotron radiation](../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) [frequency](../../../../frequency.md).
