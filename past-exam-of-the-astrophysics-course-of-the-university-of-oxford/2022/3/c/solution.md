<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The ordinary [Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../sachs-wolfe-effect.md) combines intrinsic last-scattering [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) with the gravitational redshift of [photons](../../../../photon.md) escaping the last-scattering [gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). For adiabatic large-scale perturbations in matter domination, with the potential convention in the question, the combination is $\Theta=\phi/(3c^2)$, up to the observer monopole. It is distinct from the [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md) caused by a time-varying potential along the later [line of sight](../../../../line-of-sight.md).

Use the [Fourier transform](../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention in which $P_\delta(k)=Ak$ is the [matter power spectrum](../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) and $\langle\delta_{\mathbf k}\delta_{\mathbf k'}^*\rangle=(2\pi)^3\delta_D(\mathbf k-\mathbf k')P_\delta(k)$. The [Poisson equation](../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) becomes $-k^2\phi_{\mathbf k}=4\pi G\bar\rho_0\delta_{\mathbf k}$, giving

$$
P_\phi(k)=\frac{(4\pi G\bar\rho_0)^2A}{k^3},\qquad
\Delta_\phi^2=\frac{(4\pi G\bar\rho_0)^2A}{2\pi^2c^4}.
$$

With the matter-dominated critical-density approximation $\bar\rho_0=3H_0^2/(8\pi G)$,

$$
\boxed{\Delta_\phi^2=\frac{9AH_0^4}{8\pi^2c^4},}
$$

independent of $k$. Thus the [Harrison-Zeldovich spectrum](../../../../harrison-peebles-zeldovich-spectrum.md) gives scale-invariant potential fluctuations and the requested $H_0^4$ dependence. Keeping a general present matter fraction would insert $\Omega_m^2$; the answer here follows the stipulated critical matter-dominated normalization. In this [Fourier transform](../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention, $A$ has dimensions of length to the fourth power.
