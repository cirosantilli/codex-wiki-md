<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat the long-wavelength nearly uniform spherical perturbation as a separate locally homogeneous expanding region, neglecting pressure gradients and tidal corrections. Its local [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
\boxed{H_s^2+\frac{K}{a_s^2}=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_s.}
$$

The curvature term represents the [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) in the local patch; for the convention $a_s$ dimensionless, $K=kc^2$ has units of inverse time squared. Positive $K$ corresponds to a closed, more tightly bound patch. Here and below the printed $\rho$ is interpreted as [mass density](../../../../density.md) or [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) divided by $c^2$; literal SI [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) requires $G\rho/c^2$ on the right.

The region generally has its own [scale factor](../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) and [Hubble parameter](../../../../hubble-parameter.md). The two printed equations with an identical $H(t)$ can be compared as a leading-order _equal-expansion_ construction: on a slicing where $H_s=H$, use $a_s=a$ in the already first-order curvature term to obtain $K/a^2=(8\pi G/3)\delta\rho$. This [equal-expansion curvature overdensity](../../../../equal-expansion-curvature-overdensity.md) construction does not make them both exact evolution equations with a permanently identical expansion history at common proper time. For example, a growing dust [density contrast](../../../../density-contrast.md) obeys the [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md) $H_s-H=-\dot\delta/3$, which is nonzero when $\delta\propto a$. This explicit example prevents an inconsistent identification of the two local histories.
