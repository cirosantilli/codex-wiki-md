<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a $1\ \mathrm{Mpc}$ mode, evolve the regular superhorizon [density contrast](../../../../density-contrast.md) as $\delta\propto a^2$ until [cosmological horizon entry](../../../../cosmological-horizon-entry.md). In the [radiation domination](../../../../radiation-domination.md) approximation its entry scale is $a_{\rm ent}\simeq(H_0R/c)\sqrt{\Omega_\gamma}=2.2\times10^{-6}$, well before [matter-radiation equality](../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md). At that point use the [Mészáros equation](../../../../meszaros-equation.md) basis of [d](../d.md), fixing both constants by continuity of $\delta$ and $\dot\delta$, equivalently $\delta$ and $d\delta/d\ln a$.

Explicitly, for entry data $\delta_e$ and $\delta'_e=2\delta_e/y_e$, solve

$$
\begin{pmatrix}D_1(y_e)&D_2(y_e)\\D_1'(y_e)&D_2'(y_e)\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A_1\\A_2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}\delta_e\\2\delta_e/y_e\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The nonzero [Wronskian](../../../../wronskian.md) guarantees a unique match. This produces slow logarithmic growth before equality, then $\delta\propto a$ with a decaying transient after equality. For example, at $y_e\ll1$, $A_2\simeq-2\delta_e$ and $A_1\simeq\delta_e+2\delta_e[\ln(4/y_e)-3]$. An abrupt match estimates the growth history; a precise amplitude requires a common gauge and the coupled relativistic perturbation equations through entry. Linear evolution also ceases to apply once $|\delta|$ approaches unity, and late [dark energy](../../../../dark-energy.md) further suppresses growth.
