<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Adopt the stipulated neutral fraction $f_{\rm HI}=10^{-6}$, independently of the equilibrium estimate in [e](../e.md). For pure [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md), $n_e=(1-f_{\rm HI})n_{\rm HI}/f_{\rm HI}\simeq8.3\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$. The [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) measures integrated electron [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md):

$$
y=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}\int n_ek_BT_e\,ds
\simeq\frac{\sigma_Tk_BT_e}{m_ec^2}\frac{N_{\rm HI}}{f_{\rm HI}}.
$$

Using the same absorbing [line of sight](../../../../line-of-sight.md) as in [d](../d.md),

$$
\boxed{y=1.44\times10^{-11},\qquad y/(10^{-9})=0.0144.}
$$

Thus one such filament is below the stated measurement sensitivity. If the [number density](../../../../number-density.md) inferred transversely is instead used to view the _same cylinder_ end-on, its path is twenty times longer and $y=2.9\times10^{-10}$, still below the stated sensitivity. Changing the assumed path of the original absorption measurement while keeping its [column density](../../../../column-density.md) fixed does not change its predicted $y$. The quoted neutral-fraction floor is a premise of this estimate, not a universal bound derivable from [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md).
