<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the unresolved [Lyman-alpha line](../../../../lyman-alpha-line.md), the lower $1s$ and combined upper $2p$ [state degeneracies](../../../../state-degeneracy.md) are $g_1=2$ and $g_2=6$, so the [Einstein coefficients](../../../../einstein-coefficients.md) give

$$
B_{12}=3\frac{A_{21}c^3}{8\pi h\nu_{12}^3}=2.03\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{m^3\,J^{-1}\,s^{-2}}.
$$

The measured [equivalent width](../../../../equivalent-width.md) $W=7.0\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm m$ then implies

$$
N_{\rm HI}=\frac{8\pi W\nu_{12}^4}{3A_{21}c^3}
=1.29\times10^{17}\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}=1.29\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{cm^{-2}}.
$$

Assume a central transverse [line of sight](../../../../line-of-sight.md) through a uniform filament, so $\ell=d=0.5\ \mathrm{Mpc}=1.54\times10^{22}\ \mathrm m$. Nearly all neutral [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) is in its ground state at this [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), hence

$$
\boxed{n_{\rm HI}=N_{\rm HI}/d=8.3\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}=8.3\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}.}
$$

The cylinder's length $L$ determines its total volume but does not enter a transverse [column density](../../../../column-density.md). Geometry must be specified: an axial [line of sight](../../../../line-of-sight.md) has $\ell=L$ and gives $n_{\rm HI}=4.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$ for the same measured [equivalent width](../../../../equivalent-width.md). Intermediate chords give intermediate path-dependent estimates. The numbers above treat $W$ as a rest-frame [equivalent width](../../../../equivalent-width.md); an observed value at [redshift](../../../../redshift.md) $z$ must first be divided by $1+z$.
