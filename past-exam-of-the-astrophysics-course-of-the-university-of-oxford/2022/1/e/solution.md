<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

True [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) requires a thermal ionizing [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) field as well as chemical balance; equality of [photoionization](../../../../photoionization.md) and recombination rates alone would require a specified external [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) field. Under the stronger equilibrium assumption, apply the [Saha equation](../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md) to pure [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md). The electron spin factor cancels the ground-state electronic degeneracy, giving

$$
\frac{n_en_p}{n_{\rm HI}}=S(T),\qquad
S(T)=\left(\frac{2\pi m_ek_BT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}e^{-\chi/(k_BT)}.
$$

At $T=10^4\ \mathrm K$, $k_BT=0.862\ \mathrm{eV}$ and $S=3.38\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Charge neutrality gives $n_e=n_p$. Importantly, the known quantity from [d](../d.md) is the _neutral_ [number density](../../../../number-density.md), not the total [number density](../../../../number-density.md); use [Saha neutral fraction from a measured neutral density](../../../../saha-neutral-fraction-from-a-measured-neutral-density.md) to obtain $n_p=\sqrt{Sn_{\rm HI}}=5.31\times10^7\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{1-x_e=\frac{n_{\rm HI}}{n_p+n_{\rm HI}}=\frac{1}{1+\sqrt{S/n_{\rm HI}}}\simeq1.57\times10^{-13}.}
$$

This extraordinarily small neutral fraction is the result of applying [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) to dilute intergalactic gas; it is not a realistic inference from [photoionization equilibrium](../../../../photoionization-equilibrium.md) with a nonthermal cosmic background. For the axial geometry of [d](../d.md), the equilibrium neutral fraction is smaller by $\sqrt{20}$.
