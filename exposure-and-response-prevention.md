# Exposure and response prevention

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

Exposure to appropriate [obsession](obsession.md)-related triggers while refraining from [compulsions](compulsion.md) and functionally equivalent reassurance or neutralization. The target is [learning](learning.md) to respond differently to the cue and uncertainty, rather than first eliminating every intrusive thought.
