# Extremal weight space

↑ **Parent:** [Weight space](weight-space.md)

For a finite-dimensional irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) with highest weight $\lambda$, every weight in the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) orbit $W\lambda$ is extremal and has [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) one.
