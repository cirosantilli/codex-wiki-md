# Thermalization

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

Approach of a physical system to thermal equilibrium, usually through interactions with other degrees of freedom. Equality of a reduced [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) with a [quantum Gibbs state](quantum-gibbs-state.md) at one instant does not alone demonstrate a thermalization mechanism or equilibration over time.
