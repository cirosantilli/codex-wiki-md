# Total-variation process

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-variation process](finite-variation-process.md)

For a path of [finite variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md), its total-variation process is

$$
V_t(A)=\sup_\pi\sum_{[u,v]\in\pi}|A_v-A_u|,
$$

where the [supremum](supremum.md) is over every [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) of $[0,t]$. It is an increasing process, and the signed measure $dA$ satisfies $|dA|=dV(A)$.
