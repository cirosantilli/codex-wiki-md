# Graeco-Latin square

↑ **Parent:** [Mutually orthogonal Latin squares](mutually-orthogonal-latin-squares.md)

A Graeco-Latin square is the superposition of two [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) [Latin squares](latin-square.md) of the same order. Each symbol of either square occurs once per row and column, and every cross-square pair occurs once. It permits four mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) additive factor spaces: rows, columns, and the two symbol factors.
