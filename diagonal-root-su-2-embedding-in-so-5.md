<h1 id="diagonal-root-su-2-embedding-in-so-5">Diagonal-root SU(2) embedding in SO(5)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Left SU(2) subgroup of SO(4)](left-su-2-subgroup-of-so-4.md)

The long [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) $\pm(1,1)$ and $\pm(1,-1)$ give the commuting $SU(2)$ factors of the standard $SO(4)$ subgroup. Choosing $(1,1)$ for the left factor pairs a [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) $(x,y)$ with its [coroot](coroot.md) as $x+y$. The five-dimensional vector then gives two doublets and a singlet, whereas a short-axis root would give a triplet and two singlets.
