# A4 root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The [root system](root-system.md) $\{e_i-e_j:1\le i\ne j\le5\}$ belongs to the complex [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_5(\mathbb C)$. Its four consecutive-difference [simple roots](simple-root.md) give a four-node [An Dynkin diagram](an-dynkin-diagram.md). The compact real form is the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of $SU(5)$.
