# Operator-norm distance between unitary gates

↑ **Parent:** [Universal quantum gate set](universal-quantum-gate-set.md)

The largest [pure-state norm distance](pure-state-norm-distance.md) between outputs of two [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) on the same normalized input. By definition of the [operator norm](operator-norm.md), it is $\sup_{\|\psi\|_2=1}\|(U-V)|\psi\rangle\|_2$. If it is at most $\varepsilon$, every input is approximated to that accuracy. A version disregarding the operation's [global phase](global-phase.md) minimizes $\|U-e^{i\gamma}V\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ over one common $\gamma$. Error in a finite [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) accumulates at most additively by the [quantum circuit gate-error telescoping bound](quantum-circuit-gate-error-telescoping-bound.md).
