# Cluster-beam deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Nanoparticle](nanoparticle.md)

Deposition of clusters produced in a gas-phase aggregation source. Source conditions control the size distribution, and optional [mass](mass.md) selection narrows it. Landing [energy](energy-split.md) and substrate interactions can change the structure delivered to a surface.
