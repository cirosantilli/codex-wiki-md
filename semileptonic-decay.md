# Semileptonic decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semileptonic_decay)

A [semileptonic decay](semileptonic-decay.md) contains both final-state [hadrons](hadron.md) and [leptons](lepton.md), typically through a [weak charged current](charged-current.md). This distinguishes it from a purely [leptonic decay](leptonic-decay.md).
