# Intergroup contact

↑ **Parent:** [Social psychology](social-psychology.md)

Interaction between people associated with different social groups. Contact provides an opportunity for learning, relationship formation and [recategorization](recategorization.md); its association with lower [prejudice](prejudice.md) must be distinguished from selection into contact.
