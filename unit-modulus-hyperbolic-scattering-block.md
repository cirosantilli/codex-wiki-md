# Unit-modulus hyperbolic scattering block

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)

For real $u$, this scalar [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) block satisfies $S_u(\theta)S_u(-\theta)=1$ and [Hermitian analyticity of a two-particle S-matrix](hermitian-analyticity-of-a-two-particle-s-matrix.md). It therefore has modulus one for real [rapidity](rapidity.md) differences. For $0<u<\pi$ its [pole](pole.md) at $\theta=iu$ has [residue](residue.md) $2i\sin u$ and admits a direct-channel [bound-state pole](bound-state-pole-of-the-scattering-amplitude.md) interpretation. Its crossed block is $\cosh[(\theta-iu)/2]/\cosh[(\theta+iu)/2]$. The endpoints $u=0$ and $u=\pi$ are removable degeneracies giving constant amplitudes, so a particle cannot be inferred there from the uncancelled denominator alone. This block specifies an amplitude; a complete theory must also obey the remaining [crossing symmetry](crossing-symmetry.md) and [bootstrap fusion](bound-state-fusion-of-factorized-s-matrices.md) constraints.
