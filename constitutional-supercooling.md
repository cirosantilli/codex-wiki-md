# Constitutional supercooling

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

Constitutional [supercooling](supercooling.md) occurs when liquid ahead of a solidification [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md) lies below its local [liquidus](liquidus.md). In a saline liquid with $T_L=-mC$ and $m>0$, [salt rejection](salt-rejection.md) enriches the interface liquid and depresses its [liquidus](liquidus.md); as the [concentration](concentration.md) decreases away from the interface, the [liquidus](liquidus.md) rises. If it rises faster than the actual [temperature](temperature.md), a supercooled interval appears. This can make a planar solidification [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md) unstable.
