# Coefficient normalization in an ideal of a tensor product

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of central simple algebras](tensor-product-of-central-simple-algebras.md)

Let $A,B$ be unital [associative algebras](associative-algebra-split.md) over a [field](field.md), each a [simple ring](simple-ring.md), and $J\ne0$ a [two-sided ideal](two-sided-ideal.md) of their [tensor product](tensor-product.md). Choose $0\ne x=\sum_{i=1}^n a_i\otimes b_i\in J$ of minimal [two-factor tensor rank](two-factor-tensor-rank.md). Both factor families are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Since $Aa_1A=A$, choose $\sum_j u_ja_1v_j=1$ and replace $x$ by $\sum_j(u_j\otimes1)x(v_j\otimes1)$. The result has first coefficient $1$, is nonzero by the [linear independence](linear-independence.md) of the $b_i$, and retains minimal [two-factor tensor rank](two-factor-tensor-rank.md); its first factor family is therefore still [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Now apply the same construction in $B$ to make the first second-factor coefficient $1$. The result is again nonzero of minimal [two-factor tensor rank](two-factor-tensor-rank.md), and both its coefficient families are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md).

If $Z(A)=k$, commutation with $a\otimes1$ gives a [tensor](tensor.md) in $J$ expressible with at most $n-1$ [pure tensors](pure-tensor.md), hence zero by minimality. Independence forces every $c_i$ to be central, hence a [scalar multiple](scalar-multiple.md) of $1$. Independence of $1,c_2,\ldots,c_n$ then forces $n=1$. Thus $1\otimes1\in J$, proving simplicity of the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) without a splitting-field argument.
