# Product-to-sum formula

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

Product-to-sum formulas turn products of [trigonometric functions](trigonometric-function.md) into sums at combined arguments. The identity $2\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)$ is especially useful for verifying [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of sine [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) modes on an interval.
