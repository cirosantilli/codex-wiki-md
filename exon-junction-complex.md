# Exon junction complex

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)

A multiprotein assembly associated with spliced [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) that can communicate processing history to later [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) regulation. A downstream complex can contribute to termination surveillance; it is not required for every form of [nonsense-mediated decay](nonsense-mediated-decay.md).
