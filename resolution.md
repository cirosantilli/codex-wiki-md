# Resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Proof theory](proof-theory.md)

An [inference rule](inference-rule.md) deriving a [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) from two [clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) containing complementary literals. In [propositional logic](propositional-logic.md) the displayed rule eliminates the atom $A$. For [first-order clauses](first-order-clause.md), complementary atoms can first be made identical by a suitable [first-order substitution](first-order-substitution.md).
