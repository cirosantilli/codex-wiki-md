# Internal conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear de-excitation](nuclear-de-excitation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_conversion)

[Internal conversion](internal-conversion.md) transfers nuclear [excitation energy](excitation-energy.md) directly to a bound atomic [electron](electron.md), which is emitted, rather than to a real [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md). Photon counts alone then underestimate the number of nuclear transitions unless the conversion contribution is included.
