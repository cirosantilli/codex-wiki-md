# Quadratic-inflaton Hubble oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Effective equation of state of an oscillating scalar field](effective-equation-of-state-of-an-oscillating-scalar-field.md)

For a homogeneous canonical [inflaton](inflaton.md) oscillating near $V=m^2\phi^2/2$, use reduced-Planck units and require $m\gg H$. The slowly varying ansatz $\phi=A(t)\cos mt$ gives $H_m^2\simeq m^2A^2/6$. The [Nonincreasing Hubble rate for a canonical inflaton](nonincreasing-hubble-rate-for-a-canonical-inflaton.md) becomes

$$
\dot H\simeq-\frac32H_m^2(1-\cos2mt).
$$

Cycle averaging gives $\dot H_m=-3H_m^2/2$, so $H_m=2/[3(t-t_*)]$. Integrating the rapidly varying term while holding its coefficient approximately constant gives the displayed correction. It is smaller than the mean by $O(H_m/m)$. Although the correction oscillates, the total [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) remains nonincreasing. Equal average kinetic and [potential energies](potential-energy.md) give pressureless-matter-like expansion, with amplitude $A\propto a^{-3/2}$.
