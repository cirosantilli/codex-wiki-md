# Protein sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [Protein sequence](protein-sequence.md)

For fixed length $n$ and the standard twenty-amino-acid alphabet, there are $20^n$ sequences: each position has twenty choices. This counts possible sequences, not stable [protein folds](protein-fold.md); the sequence-to-structure relationship is many-to-one and some sequences do not form a stable folded protein.
