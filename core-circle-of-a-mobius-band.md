<h1 id="core-circle-of-a-mobius-band">Core circle of a Möbius band</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius band](mobius-band.md)

The core circle is the image of the centre line in the rectangle defining a [Möbius band](mobius-band.md). Its endpoints are identified, so it is a [circle](circle.md). The formula

$$
H_t([u,v])=[u,(1-t)v],\qquad 0\le t\le1,
$$

defines a [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto that [circle](circle.md): it respects $(0,v)\sim(1,-v)$, starts at the [identity map](identity-function.md), and fixes the core throughout. Consequently the [Möbius band](mobius-band.md) has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) isomorphic to $\mathbb Z$, just like a [circle](circle.md); this alone cannot distinguish it from an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md).
