# Photon mean free path in an ionized universe

↑ **Parent:** [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md)

For a dilute ionized plasma with nonrelativistic [electrons](electron.md) and photon energies well below $m_ec^2$, independent [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) events give photon survival probability $\exp(-n_e\sigma_Ts)$ over proper distance $s$. Its mean gives the displayed [mean free path](mean-free-path.md). The scattering rate is $\Gamma_\gamma=n_e\sigma_Tc$. Photons are tightly coupled when this rate greatly exceeds the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md); [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md) becomes possible as the free-electron fraction falls. Frequent elastic scattering does not by itself imply chemical equilibration of the photon number.
