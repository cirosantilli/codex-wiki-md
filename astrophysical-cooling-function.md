# Astrophysical cooling function

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md)

An [astrophysical cooling function](astrophysical-cooling-function.md) packages radiative losses of a plasma into a temperature-dependent coefficient. In the hydrogen-density-squared convention, the volume energy loss is $n_H^2\Lambda(T)$, so the coefficient has units $\mathrm{erg\,cm^3\,s^{-1}}$. Its dependence on [ionization](ionization.md) state, chemical abundances and radiation field must be specified; a low-density collisional-equilibrium curve is not universal.
