# Profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Profinite_group)

A profinite group is an [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of finite discrete groups. Equivalently, it is a compact Hausdorff totally disconnected [topological group](topological-group-split.md).
