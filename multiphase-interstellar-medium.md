# Multiphase interstellar medium

↑ **Parent:** [Interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md)

Interstellar gas occupies distinct thermal and [ionization](ionization.md) regimes because heating, cooling and compression depend on [density](density.md), radiation and composition. Cold and warm neutral gas can coexist over a finite [pressure](pressure-split.md) range, while ionized gas, [molecular clouds](molecular-cloud.md) and hot shock-heated regions add further components. [turbulence](turbulence-split.md), magnetic stress and finite cooling times mean that these are dynamically exchanging phases, not necessarily a common global equilibrium.
