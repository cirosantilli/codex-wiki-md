# Elastic pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_pendulum)

A point mass attached to a massless straight [spring](spring.md) can change its radial distance $r$ from a fixed support while swinging in a vertical plane. Its lower [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) length is $r_0=l+mg/k$. Linearizing the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) there gives independent radial and angular [normal modes](normal-mode.md) with squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $k/m$ and $g/r_0$. The formal upper stationary state has $r=l-mg/k$ and exists in positive-length coordinates only when $l>mg/k$; its angular mode is unstable.
