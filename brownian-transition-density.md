# Brownian transition density

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian transition semigroup](brownian-transition-semigroup.md)

The transition density of standard Brownian motion in $\mathbb R^d$ is the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

$$
p_t(x,y)=\frac{1}{(2\pi t)^{d/2}}
\exp\left(-\frac{|y-x|^2}{2t}\right),
\qquad t>0.
$$

Thus $\mathbb P_x(X_t\in A)=\int_Ap_t(x,y)\,dy$.
