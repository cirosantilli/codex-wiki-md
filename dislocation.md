# Dislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dislocation)

A line defect in a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). Its [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) measures the closure failure of a circuit around the line. Motion can produce permanent [plasticity](plasticity-physics.md) without simultaneously breaking every bond across a whole plane.
