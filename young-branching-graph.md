# Young branching graph

↑ **Parent:** [Young's lattice](young-s-lattice.md)

The Young branching graph is the graded cover graph of the [Young lattice](young-s-lattice.md). Its level $n$ consists of partitions of $n$, and its edges add one box. It is also the branching graph for complex [Specht modules](specht-module.md) under the [restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md).
