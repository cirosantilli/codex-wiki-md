# Parsec

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsec)

A parsec is the distance corresponding to one arcsecond of annual parallax for a baseline of one [astronomical unit](astronomical-unit.md). It is approximately $3.086\times10^{16}$ metres. A megaparsec is one million parsecs.
