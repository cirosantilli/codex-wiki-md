# Thomson cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md)

A free nonrelativistic charge driven by a weak [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) has peak [acceleration](acceleration.md) $|q|E_0/m$. Its time-averaged [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md) [power](power.md) is $q^4E_0^2/(12\pi\epsilon_0m^2c^3)$. Divide by incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md) $\epsilon_0cE_0^2/2$ to obtain the displayed [cross section](cross-section-physics.md), independent of [frequency](frequency.md). For an [electron](electron.md), it is $8\pi r_e^2/3$. The approximation assumes negligible [Compton recoil kinematics](compton-recoil-kinematics.md) and small driven [velocity](velocity.md) compared with the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md).
