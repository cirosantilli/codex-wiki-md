# Minkowski inverse-Hessian relation for an effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

With $W=-i\log Z$, $\varphi=\delta W/\delta J$ and $\Gamma=W-\int J\varphi$, variation gives $\delta\Gamma/\delta\varphi=-J$. Differentiating through the source-to-field map gives

$$
\int dz\,\Gamma^{(2)}(x,z)W^{(2)}(z,y)=-\delta(x-y).
$$

The inverse is taken on the regulated nonsingular fluctuation space. Since $W^{(2)}=iC_2$, the full connected [propagator](propagator.md) is determined by the inverse effective-action quadratic kernel. Factors of $i$ and the minus sign depend on the explicitly chosen Minkowski [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) convention.
