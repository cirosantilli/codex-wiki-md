# Top-gate density tuning

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md)

A gate separated by [dielectric](dielectric.md) thickness $d$ has geometric [capacitance](capacitance.md) per area $C/A=\epsilon/d$ in the parallel-plate approximation. An electron-density change is $\Delta n_s=(\epsilon/ed)\Delta V_g$ with the stated polarity/reference convention. Fringing, trapped charge, depletion and finite [quantum capacitance](quantum-capacitance.md) can modify the relation. An absolute gate voltage additionally requires the zero-density offset or another calibration.
