# Injective hull of an Ore domain

↑ **Parent:** [Injective hull](injective-hull.md)

For a domain satisfying the left [Ore condition](ore-condition.md), its [division ring](division-ring.md) of fractions $Q$ is an injective essential extension of the left regular module. For a homomorphism $f:I\to Q$ on a nonzero left ideal, choose $a\in I\setminus\{0\}$. A common left multiple $sx=ta$ gives $sf(x)=tf(a)$, hence $f(x)=xa^{-1}f(a)$; right multiplication by $a^{-1}f(a)$ extends it. The [Baer criterion](baer-criterion.md) proves injectivity. Clearing a left denominator proves essentiality. The same calculation gives $\operatorname{End}_R({}_RQ)\cong Q^{\rm op}$, with the opposite ring required because right multiplications compose in reversed order.
