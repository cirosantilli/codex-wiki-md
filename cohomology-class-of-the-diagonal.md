# Cohomology class of the diagonal

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md)

Let $M$ be a closed oriented $n$-manifold, let $e_{p,i}$ be a homogeneous basis of $H^p(M;\mathbb Q)$, and choose its Poincare-dual basis $e_{p,i}^*\in H^{n-p}(M;\mathbb Q)$ by $\langle e_{p,i}e_{p,j}^*,[M]\rangle=\delta_{ij}$. With the product-orientation convention, the [Poincare dual](poincare-dual.md) of the diagonal is

$$
\varepsilon_\Delta=\sum_{p,i}(-1)^{np}e_{p,i}^*\times e_{p,i}.
$$
