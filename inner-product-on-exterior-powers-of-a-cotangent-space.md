# Inner product on exterior powers of a cotangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent space](cotangent-space.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) induces a dual [inner product](inner-product.md) on each [cotangent space](cotangent-space.md). On decomposable covectors define $\langle\xi_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\xi_p,\zeta_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\zeta_p\rangle=\det(\langle\xi_i,\zeta_j\rangle)$ and extend bilinearly. Increasing wedges of an orthonormal coframe form an orthonormal basis. This positive-definite inner product is the one used to define the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md).
