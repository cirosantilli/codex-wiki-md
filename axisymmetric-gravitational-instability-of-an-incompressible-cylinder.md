# Axisymmetric gravitational instability of an incompressible cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational instability](gravitational-instability.md)

An inviscid uniform-density self-gravitating cylinder in vacuum, with no rotation or [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md), has the displayed axisymmetric [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md), where $\kappa=|k_z|R$. To derive it, let $W=\psi+\pi/\rho=\epsilon\Pi_0I_0(kr)e^{i(kz-\omega t)}$. The linear [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) gives $\Pi_0=\omega^2/[kI'_0(\kappa)]$. The [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) vanishes on the moving [free surface](free-surface.md), giving $\pi(R)=2\pi G\rho^2R\eta$. Substitute the [axisymmetric gravitational potential of a displaced cylindrical interface](axisymmetric-gravitational-potential-of-a-displaced-cylindrical-interface.md) and use $I'_0=I_1$ to obtain the relation. Its nonzero neutral root is $\kappa_c\simeq1.0667$: wavelengths longer than $2\pi R/\kappa_c\simeq5.89R$ have $\omega^2<0$ and grow. The fastest-growing mode has $\kappa\simeq0.58$, so its [wavelength](wavelength.md) is about $11R$. Negative $\omega^2$ must be interpreted through an imaginary [frequency](frequency.md), not through a real oscillatory ansatz.
