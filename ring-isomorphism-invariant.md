# Ring isomorphism invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)

A property of [rings](ring.md) is a [ring isomorphism invariant](ring-isomorphism-invariant.md) when any two rings related by a [ring isomorphism](ring-isomorphism.md) either both have the property or both lack it. Being an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) is such a property: a [ring isomorphism](ring-isomorphism.md) preserves nonzero elements, products, and zero.
