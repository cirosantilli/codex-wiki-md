# Brane tension

↑ **Parent:** [Brane](brane.md)

The brane tension is the energy per unit spatial [brane](brane.md) volume and multiplies its geometric action. In units $\hbar=c=1$, a $p$-brane tension has mass dimension $p+1$. The $p=1$ case is the [string tension](string-tension.md).
