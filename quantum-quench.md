# Quantum quench

↑ **Parent:** [Sudden approximation](sudden-approximation.md)

A [quantum quench](quantum-quench.md) changes a [quantum system](quantum-system.md)'s control parameters rapidly and studies the subsequent [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md). In the ideal sudden limit, write an initial [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) $|m_i\rangle$ using a complete final [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md):

$$
|m_i\rangle=\sum_n c_n|n_f\rangle,\qquad c_n=\langle n_f|m_i\rangle,\qquad
|\psi(t)\rangle=\sum_n c_ne^{-iE_n^ft/\hbar}|n_f\rangle.
$$

The [Born rule](born-rule.md) [probabilities](probability.md) for a nondegenerate final [energy measurement](energy-measurement.md) are $|c_n|^2$, with $\sum_n|c_n|^2=1$. For a degenerate [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue.md), sum the [probabilities](probability.md) over its orthonormal [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md). Subsequent relative [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) can alter other measurement [probabilities](probability.md) even though these final [energy](energy.md) populations stay fixed. A [quench](quench-statistical-physics.md) need not begin in an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md); the same expansion applies to any initial [pure state](pure-state.md).
