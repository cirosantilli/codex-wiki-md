# Uniform dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform module](uniform-module.md)

The uniform dimension of a [module](module-mathematics.md) is the supremum of the numbers of nonzero [submodules](submodule.md) that can occur in an internal [direct sum](direct-sum.md). Finite uniform dimension is equivalent to having no infinite internal [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of nonzero [submodules](submodule.md). A finite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [uniform modules](uniform-module.md) contained as an [essential submodule](essential-submodule.md) gives that number as the dimension. The [finite uniform decomposition of a Noetherian module](finite-uniform-decomposition-of-a-noetherian-module.md) therefore gives finite uniform dimension.
