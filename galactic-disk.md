# Galactic disk

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

A [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md) is a flattened stellar or gaseous component of a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md), commonly supported mainly by ordered rotation. Its radial mass profile determines its [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md); thickness, truncation and external forces also matter. A [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md) need not be an [exponential galactic disk](exponential-galactic-disk.md), and its neutral-gas absorption radius need not equal a stellar scale length.
