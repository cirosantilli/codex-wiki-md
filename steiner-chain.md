# Steiner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

A cyclic chain of distinct [circles](circle.md) between two disjoint [circle](circle.md) boundaries, each tangent to both boundaries and its two neighbours. In concentric coordinates with inner radius $\rho$ and outer radius $R$, the chain [circles](circle.md) have radius $r=(R-\rho)/2$ and centre distance $d=(R+\rho)/2$. A simple disjoint chain with $n\geq3$ closes when $r/d=\sin(\pi/n)$.
