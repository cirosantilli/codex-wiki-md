# Determinant-normalized slash operator

↑ **Parent:** [Slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md)

For a rational [matrix](matrix.md) $\gamma$ of positive [determinant](determinant.md), use the positive real power of $\det\gamma$ in the displayed [slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md). It satisfies $(f|_k\gamma)|_k\eta=f|_k(\gamma\eta)$ by the [automorphy factor](automorphy-factor.md) identity and multiplicativity of the [determinant](determinant.md). It agrees with the usual operator on the [modular group](modular-group.md). The determinant factor is essential for the stated normalization of the [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md).
