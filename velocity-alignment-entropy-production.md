# Velocity-alignment entropy production

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field alignment equation](mean-field-alignment-equation.md)

For the [mean-field alignment equation](mean-field-alignment-equation.md) in $d$ position and $d$ [velocity](velocity.md) coordinates, put $\rho=\int f\,d^dv$. Its force has [velocity-space divergence](velocity-space-divergence.md) $-d\int K\rho_\star\,d^dx_\star$. Consequently [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the nonnegative derivative in the title, assuming suitable positivity, decay and integrability. The fine-grained [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md) decreases because these dynamics contract [phase space](phase-space.md); the interaction is dissipative.
