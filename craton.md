# Craton

↑ **Parent:** [Continental crust](continental-crust.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craton)

A long-lived stable part of a continent, commonly with a thick, depleted [lithospheric mantle](lithospheric-mantle.md) keel; that keel is distinct from the [continental crust](continental-crust.md).
