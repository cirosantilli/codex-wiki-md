# Prime-multiplication coefficient splitting for formal groups

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant differential of a formal group law](invariant-differential-of-a-formal-group-law.md)

For a [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) over any [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$, the [invariant differential of a formal group law](invariant-differential-of-a-formal-group-law.md) satisfies $[p]^*\omega=p\omega$. Its coefficient is a [unit in a ring](unit-in-a-ring.md), so $[p]'(T)\in pR[[T]]$. If $[p](T)=\sum a_mT^m$, this means $ma_m\in pR$. When $p\nmid m$, the [integer](integer-split.md) [Bézout's identity](bezout-s-identity.md) implies $a_m\in pR$, even if $p$ is a [zero divisor](zero-divisor.md). Put those coefficients into $pf(T)$ and the exponents divisible by $p$ into $g(T^p)$.
