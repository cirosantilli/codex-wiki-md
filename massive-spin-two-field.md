# Massive spin-two field

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic quantum field](relativistic-quantum-field-split.md)

A free massive spin-two field is a [symmetric tensor](symmetric-tensor.md) obeying a massive [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md), vanishing divergence, and vanishing [trace](matrix-trace.md). Its rest-frame polarizations form the [symmetric traceless square](symmetric-trace-free-square-of-the-defining-orthogonal-representation.md) of the $SO(D-1)$ vector, with $(D-2)(D+1)/2$ degrees of freedom.
