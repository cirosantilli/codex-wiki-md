# Quantum integer

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum enveloping algebra of sl2](quantum-enveloping-algebra-of-sl2.md)

The symmetric quantum integer is the displayed [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md), with $[0]_q=0$. It obeys $[n+1]_q=q^{-1}[n]_q+q^n$ and $[r]_q[s+1]_q-[s]_q[r+1]_q=[r-s]_q$. At a primitive odd $\ell$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md), $[n]_q=0$ exactly when $\ell$ divides $n$.
