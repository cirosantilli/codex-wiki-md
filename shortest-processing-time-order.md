# Shortest-processing-time order

↑ **Parent:** [Scheduling](scheduling.md)

On one server with all jobs initially available, nonnegative fixed durations and no [sequence](sequence.md)-dependent setup, a [nondecreasing](nondecreasing-function.md)-duration order minimizes the sum of completion times. Swapping adjacent durations $a>b$ reduces their combined contribution by $a-b$ and changes no later completion time, because their total duration is unchanged. Repeated adjacent exchanges remove every inversion. The sum of pre-service waiting times has the same optimum, differing by the fixed total duration.
