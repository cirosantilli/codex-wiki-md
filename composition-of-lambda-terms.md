# Composition of lambda terms

↑ **Parent:** [Untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md)

Choose $x$ fresh for the [free variables](free-variable.md) of $p,q$. This operation on [lambda terms](lambda-term.md) is associative under [beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md), and the [I combinator](i-combinator.md) is a two-sided identity under [beta-eta equivalence](beta-eta-equivalence.md). The latter use of [eta conversion](eta-conversion.md) is necessary in general.
