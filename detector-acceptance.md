# Detector acceptance

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

The fraction of produced events falling within a detector's geometrical and kinematic selection. Reconstruction and trigger efficiencies are additional factors; conventions must state whether they are folded into the quoted acceptance. A changed decay spectrum can alter acceptance even at the same parent [mass](mass.md).
