# Rodrigues rotation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigues_rotation_formula)

For a real [angle](angle.md) $\alpha$ and a unit [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf n$, the [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) about $\mathbf n$ sends

$$
\mathbf x\longmapsto\cos\alpha\,\mathbf x+\sin\alpha\,(\mathbf n\times\mathbf x)+(1-\cos\alpha)\mathbf n(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf x).
$$

The [vector triple product identity](vector-triple-product.md) gives the equivalent expression $\mathbf x+\sin\alpha\,\mathbf n\times\mathbf x+(1-\cos\alpha)\mathbf n\times(\mathbf n\times\mathbf x)$. To see why it is a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), decompose $\mathbf x$ into its parallel and perpendicular components relative to $\mathbf n$. The parallel component is fixed, while the perpendicular component becomes $\cos\alpha\,\mathbf x_\perp+\sin\alpha\,\mathbf n\times\mathbf x_\perp$, the usual plane [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). It is also the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) of the [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $H\mathbf x=\alpha\mathbf n\times\mathbf x$: the identity $H^3=-\alpha^2H$ reduces its [exponential series](exponential-series.md) to these three terms.
