# Spin singlet state

↑ **Parent:** [Bell state](bell-state-split.md)

The two-spin singlet is

$$
|\Psi^-\rangle=\frac{|01\rangle-|10\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Spin measurements along axes $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ have correlation $E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$, and equal axes give perfect anticorrelation.

This is the [spin-one-half singlet state](spin-one-half-singlet-state.md), an example of an [angular momentum singlet state](singlet-state.md). The general singlet condition is zero total angular momentum, not the choice of a particular two-qubit Bell basis.
