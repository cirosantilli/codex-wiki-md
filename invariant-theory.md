# Invariant theory

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariant_theory)

[Invariant theory](invariant-theory.md) studies functions and tensors unchanged by a [group action](group-action.md). For a linear representation, the [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md) records invariant [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) functions on the representation space. Tensor invariants and commuting algebra actions connect it with [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md).
