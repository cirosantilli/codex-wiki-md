# Stable equilibrium in Lagrangian mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

At an equilibrium, a positive-definite [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of the potential gives stable [small oscillations](small-oscillation.md). If a continuous symmetry supplies zero modes, positive definiteness on the directions transverse to the symmetry gives stability of the relative configuration.
