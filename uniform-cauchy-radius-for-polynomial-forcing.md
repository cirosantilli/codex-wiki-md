# Uniform Cauchy radius for polynomial forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md)

For a linear [real analytic](real-analytic-function.md) non-characteristic [Cauchy problem for a partial differential equation](cauchy-problem.md) with zero data, the local [real analytic](real-analytic-function.md) radius may be chosen uniformly over [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) forcing terms. Fix an analyticity exponent $R^{-1}$. Each [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), including a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) obtained by a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) coordinate change, satisfies the corresponding factorial [derivative](derivative.md) bound with some finite prefactor, uniformly over a compact parameter range. Divide the forcing by that prefactor to put all such data in one fixed majorant class. The [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md) supplies a common radius, and linearity rescales the solution back without shrinking it. This argument does not bound the amplitudes of the resulting solutions uniformly.
