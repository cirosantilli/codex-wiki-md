# Reconstruction of a principal connection from local gauge potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Local principal connection form](local-principal-connection-form.md)

A [local principal connection form](local-principal-connection-form.md) $A$ and a fiber coordinate $\gamma$ reconstruct the connection on a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) in a local section. Changing that section by $s'=su$ changes $\gamma$ to $u^{-1}\gamma$ and $A$ to $u^{-1}Au+u^{-1}du$. Substitution cancels the two terms involving $du$, so the reconstructed forms agree on overlaps. Here $A$ is Lie-algebra-valued and matrix conjugation denotes the adjoint action. Agreement across trivializations differs from the right-action equivariance $R_h^*\omega=\operatorname{Ad}_{h^{-1}}\omega$.
