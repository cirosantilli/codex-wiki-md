<h1 id="stackel-potential">Stäckel potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

A [Stäckel potential](stackel-potential.md) allows additive separation of the [Hamilton-Jacobi equation](hamilton-jacobi-equation.md) in an orthogonal coordinate system. In axisymmetric [prolate spheroidal coordinates](prolate-spheroidal-coordinates.md), the relative-potential form is $\psi=[G(\lambda)-G(\mu)]/(\lambda-\mu)$. It gives [energy](energy.md), axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and an independent third separation [integral](integral.md). Here $G$ is a one-variable generating function, not the gravitational constant. Generic bounded [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) are regular invariant-torus motions; resonance and coordinate degeneracy require separate treatment.
