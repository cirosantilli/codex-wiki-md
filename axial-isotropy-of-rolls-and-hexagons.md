# Axial isotropy of rolls and hexagons

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational hexagon amplitude equations](rotational-hexagon-amplitude-equations.md)

Write the translation [group action](group-action.md) as $(A,B,C)\mapsto(e^{i\theta_1}A,e^{i\theta_2}B,e^{-i(\theta_1+\theta_2)}C)$. Modulo lattice translations, the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $(h,h,h)$, with nonzero real $h$, is the sixfold rotation group. Its [fixed-point subspace of a group action](fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) is the real line $(a,a,a)$. The [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $(r,0,0)$, with nonzero real $r$, consists of the circle $\theta_1=0$ together with a half-turn; its [fixed-point subspace of a group action](fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) is the real line $(a,0,0)$. The [equivariant branching lemma](equivariant-branching-lemma.md) therefore supplies generic branches of both axial types.
