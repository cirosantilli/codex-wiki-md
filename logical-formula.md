# Logical formula

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)

A [logical formula](logical-formula.md) is a finite expression constructed by the [formation rules](formal-grammar.md) of a [formal language](formal-language.md). [Propositional formulas](propositional-formula.md) combine [propositional variables](propositional-variable.md) by [logical connectives](logical-connective.md). First-order [formulas](logical-formula.md) additionally contain [terms](first-order-term.md), [atomic relations](atomic-formula.md) and [quantifiers](quantifier.md). A [logical formula](logical-formula.md) is a syntactic object; its truth is determined only after specifying a suitable interpretation and values for its [free variables](free-variable.md).
