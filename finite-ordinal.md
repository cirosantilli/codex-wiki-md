# Finite ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal](ordinal.md)

A finite [ordinal](ordinal.md) is the order type of a finite [well-order](well-order.md); in the von Neumann representation it is one of $0,1,2,\ldots$. They are exactly the [ordinals](ordinal.md) $x$ such that $x$ and each member of $x$ are either zero or [successor ordinals](successor-ordinal.md). If an [ordinal](ordinal.md) is infinite, it either is the nonzero limit $\omega$ or contains $\omega$ as a member, so it fails this test. Thus the [finite ordinals](finite-ordinal.md) give the usual representation of the [natural numbers](natural-number.md).
