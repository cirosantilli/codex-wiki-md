# Renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renormalization)

Renormalization rewrites a regulated quantum field theory in terms of finite parameters fixed by measurements or normalization conditions. Dependence on the regulator is absorbed into [counterterms](counterterm.md), while dependence on the chosen [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md) is governed by a [beta function](beta-function-physics.md).
