# Gaussian scale mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture model](mixture-model.md)

A Gaussian scale mixture is a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X=RZ$, where $Z$ has a [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) and is independent of a positive random scale $R$. Its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is the average of the conditional normal densities. Random scales can produce larger [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) than a single normal law.
