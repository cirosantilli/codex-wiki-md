# Confinement deflection length

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

A stiff chain's transverse wandering over contour [length](length.md) $s$ scales as $s^3/\ell_p$. Equating this to the squared confining radius gives the displayed deflection [length](length.md) when $R\ll\ell_p$. A free-energy cost of order $k_BT$ per deflection yields confinement [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per inserted contour [length](length.md) of order $k_BT/\lambda$. Numerical prefactors depend on geometry and boundary constraints.
