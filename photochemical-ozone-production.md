# Photochemical ozone production

↑ **Parent:** [Tropospheric ozone](tropospheric-ozone.md)

Net daylight [ozone](ozone.md) production occurs when [peroxy radicals](peroxy-radical.md) oxidize NO to NO2 without using [ozone](ozone.md), followed by NO2 [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) and oxygen-atom addition to O2. The competing reaction NO + O3 to NO2 + O2 only recycles odd [oxygen](oxygen.md). Radical initiation, termination, precursor loading and transport determine the net rate.
