# Cb-embedded subset

↑ **Parent:** [C-embedded subset](c-embedded-subset.md)

A [subset](subset.md) is [Cb-embedded](cb-embedded-subset.md) if every bounded [continuous](continuous-function.md) [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) on it extends to a bounded [continuous](continuous-function.md) [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) on the ambient space. A bounded extension can be clipped to preserve the original bound. This is weaker than [C-embeddedness](c-embedded-subset.md): the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) in their [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md) have the bounded extension property, but their unbounded identity [function](function-split.md) cannot extend to a [compact space](compact-space.md).
