# CT number

↑ **Parent:** [Computed tomography](computed-tomography.md)

A dimensionless relative attenuation value calibrated so that water is zero and air is approximately $-1000$. It depends on the [computed tomography](computed-tomography.md) spectrum and material composition. It is not directly a [linear attenuation coefficient](linear-attenuation-coefficient.md) at the $511\,\mathrm{keV}$ energy used in [positron emission tomography](positron-emission-tomography.md).
