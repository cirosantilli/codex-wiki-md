# Coverage of antipodal points by a randomly oriented sector

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md)

For a sector of width $\phi$ and an independent uniformly distributed central bearing, a specified bearing is covered with probability $\phi/(2\pi)$. Two antipodal bearings cannot both be covered when $\phi<\pi$. For $\phi\ge\pi$, the allowable centre arcs overlap in two components of length $\phi-\pi$, proving the displayed [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md). Averaging over the width's [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) gives unconditional coverage probabilities.
