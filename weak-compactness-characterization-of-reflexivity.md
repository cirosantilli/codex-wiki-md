# Weak compactness characterization of reflexivity

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md)

A [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is [reflexive](reflexive-banach-space.md) if and only if its closed unit ball is compact in the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md). One direction applies the [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) to the unit ball in the bidual; the converse follows because [Goldstine theorem](goldstine-theorem.md) makes the canonical image of the unit ball weak-star dense, while weak compactness makes that image weak-star closed.
