# Oppenheimer-Snyder model

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppenheimer–Snyder_model)

The [Oppenheimer-Snyder model](oppenheimer-snyder-model.md) describes a homogeneous ball of [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) collapsing from rest, with a closed [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) inside and a [Schwarzschild metric](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) outside. In geometric units its interior is $ds^2=-d\tau^2+a^2(d\chi^2+\sin^2\chi\,d\Omega^2)$, with $a=a_{\max}(1+\cos\eta)/2$ and $\tau=a_{\max}(\eta+\sin\eta)/2$. The boundary is a fixed comoving angle. [Density](density.md) conservation gives $\rho a^3$ constant, and the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) at the initial turning point gives $8\pi\rho_0a_{\max}^2/3=1$.
