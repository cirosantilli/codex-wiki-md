# Digital comparator

↑ **Parent:** [Combinational logic](combinational-logic.md)

A [combinational logic](combinational-logic.md) device comparing two fixed-width [binary representations](binary-number.md). For unsigned inputs $a,b$, a two-output encoding is $L=1$ exactly when $a<b$ and $G=1$ exactly when $a>b$. Equality gives $(L,G)=(0,0)$; $(1,1)$ is unreachable. A one-[bit](bit.md) implementation is $L=\neg a\land b$, $G=a\land\neg b$.
