<h1 id="tsirelson-s-bound">Tsirelson's bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsirelson's_bound)

A bound on the correlations attainable by [measurement in quantum measurements](quantum-measurement-split.md) for a specified Bell expression. The [CHSH Tsirelson bound](chsh-tsirelson-bound.md) is $2\sqrt2$; the number depends on which Bell expression is being optimized.
