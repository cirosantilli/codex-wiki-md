# Collision-induced absorption and emission

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption coefficient](absorption-coefficient.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collision-induced_absorption_and_emission)

A collision or transient molecular pair can possess a dipole even when the isolated molecules do not. The resulting [opacity](opacity.md) can be important in a dense [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) atmosphere. Pair absorption per unit length scales approximately as the product of the two partner number densities, so its [mass absorption coefficient](mass-absorption-coefficient.md) is generally density-dependent. It supplies continuum absorption between ordinary spectral lines.
