# HeLa culture viability experiment

↑ **Parent:** [HeLa](hela.md)

A published teaching experiment used ATCC CCL-2 [HeLa](hela.md) to make the distinction between a [cell line](cell-line.md) and the condition of individual [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) visible. Cultures were expanded in treated flasks and supplied as adherent dishes. A wash with [phosphate-buffered saline](phosphate-buffered-saline.md) preceded [trypsin](trypsin.md) treatment to detach them; added [fetal bovine serum](fetal-bovine-serum.md) stopped the digestion. The dispersed preparation was mixed with an equal [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of [trypan blue](trypan-blue.md) and loaded into a counting chamber under an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md).[Procedure, Materials; HeLa cell washing and dissociation; HeLa cell counting](introducing-mammalian-cell-culture-and-cell-viability-techniques-in-the-undergraduate-biology-laboratory-by-kristen-bowey-dellinger-and-others.md)

For the [ethanol](ethanol.md) comparison, treatment groups and an untreated control were brought to the same final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md); the control received [phosphate-buffered saline](phosphate-buffered-saline.md). Dye-excluding counts were compared across [replicates](replicate.md). The paper reports representative student measurements in Figure 3A and documents the untreated control in its discussion. Its alternative [temperature](temperature-split.md) comparison used three [replicates](replicate.md) per condition and a $30\,\mathrm{min}$ exposure.[HeLa cell viability—biology majors, volume-matched control; Figure 3A; Evidence of student learning—biology majors; HeLa cell viability—general biology education](introducing-mammalian-cell-culture-and-cell-viability-techniques-in-the-undergraduate-biology-laboratory-by-kristen-bowey-dellinger-and-others.md)

The paper's disposable chamber had a different count-to-[number density](number-density.md) calibration from the [hemocytometer](hemocytometer.md) above. Dye exclusion does not establish future division; recovery and mixing also affect counts. The constructed [hemocytometer](hemocytometer.md) example is independent of those observations.[Sample data—general biology education, Glasstic Slide 10 calibration; Faculty instructions, chamber equations can differ](introducing-mammalian-cell-culture-and-cell-viability-techniques-in-the-undergraduate-biology-laboratory-by-kristen-bowey-dellinger-and-others.md)
