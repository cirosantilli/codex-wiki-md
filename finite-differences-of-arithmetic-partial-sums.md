# Finite differences of arithmetic partial sums

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic series](arithmetic-series.md)

Adding one [sequence term](sequence-term.md) changes a [partial sum](partial-sum.md) by exactly that entry, so $S_{n+1}-S_n=a_{n+1}$. Taking another [finite difference](finite-difference-split.md) gives

$$
S_{n+2}-2S_{n+1}+S_n=a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}=d.
$$

Thus arithmetic [sequence terms](sequence-term.md) produce [partial sums](partial-sum.md) with constant second [finite differences](finite-difference-split.md). Conversely, if these second [finite differences](finite-difference-split.md) are a fixed $d$ and $a_{n+1}=S_{n+1}-S_n$, then those entries form an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md). For nonzero $d$, the displayed sum formula is a [quadratic function](quadratic-function.md); for $d=0$, it reduces to the linear expression $S_n=na_1$.
