# Concentration of measure

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

For a [metric space](metric-space.md) with a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) $\mu$, the concentration function is $\alpha(\varepsilon)=\sup_{\mu(A)\geq1/2}(1-\mu(A_\varepsilon))$, where $A_\varepsilon$ is the metric neighbourhood of radius $\varepsilon$. A sequence exhibits [concentration of measure](concentration-of-measure.md) if these functions tend to zero for every fixed positive radius. The metric scale is part of this definition.
