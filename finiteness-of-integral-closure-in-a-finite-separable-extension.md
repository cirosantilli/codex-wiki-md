# Finiteness of integral closure in a finite separable extension

↑ **Parent:** [Integral closure](integral-closure.md)

For a normal [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) $A$ that is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), the [integral closure](integral-closure.md) in a finite [separable field extension](separable-extension.md) of its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) is finite as an $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md). An [integral field basis by denominator clearing](integral-field-basis-by-denominator-clearing.md) gives a finite lattice. Integral traces place the closure in its finite [trace-dual lattice](trace-dual-lattice.md); [Noetherianity](noetherian-ring.md) makes that submodule finitely generated.
