<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) under the integral, and integrate the second spatial derivative by parts twice. For sufficiently decaying temperature and first derivative, the boundary terms vanish:

$$
\partial_t\widetilde\theta(k,t)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-ikx}\partial_x^2\theta\,dx=(ik)^2\widetilde\theta(k,t).
$$

Hence the transformed [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) is the [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\widetilde\theta=-k^2\widetilde\theta.}
$$
