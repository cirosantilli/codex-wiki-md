<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Solving the transformed [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) with its [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $\widetilde\theta(k,t)=e^{-k^2t}\widetilde\theta(k,0)$. The [Fourier inversion theorem](../../../../../fourier-inversion-theorem.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\theta(x,t)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}e^{ikx-k^2t}\widetilde\theta(k,0)\,dk.}
$$

For $t>0$ the Gaussian multiplier suppresses large spatial frequencies, which explains the smoothing produced by [heat conduction](../../../../../thermal-conduction.md).
