<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplying by $x^2$ makes the first two terms a total [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md):

$$
x(x-1)y''+(2x-1)y'=\left[x(x-1)y'\right]'.
$$

The resulting [Sturm-Liouville form](../../../../../sturm-liouville-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{-(p y')'+l(l+1)y=\lambda w y,\qquad p=x(x-1),\quad w=x^2.}
$$

Equivalently, an integrating multiplier $\mu$ must satisfy $(\mu(1-1/x))'=\mu(2/x-1/x^2)$, which reduces to $\mu'/\mu=2/x$ and gives $\mu=x^2$ up to a constant. The coefficient $p$ is positive for $1<x<R$ and vanishes linearly at the left endpoint; the weight $w$ is positive throughout. The boundedness condition removes the logarithmic local branch, giving the [no-log boundary condition at a linearly vanishing Sturm-Liouville endpoint](../../../../../no-log-boundary-condition-at-a-linearly-vanishing-sturm-liouville-endpoint.md).
