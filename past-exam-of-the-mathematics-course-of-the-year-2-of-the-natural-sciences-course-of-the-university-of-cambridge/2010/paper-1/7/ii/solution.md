<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $\nabla v=(-u_y,u_x)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=-u_xu_y+u_yu_x=0.}
$$

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is normal to a regular [level set](../../../../../level-set.md), so these curves intersect orthogonally wherever their [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) are nonzero. At a [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md), where both [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) vanish, a regular curve tangent need not exist; the algebraic identity remains valid.
