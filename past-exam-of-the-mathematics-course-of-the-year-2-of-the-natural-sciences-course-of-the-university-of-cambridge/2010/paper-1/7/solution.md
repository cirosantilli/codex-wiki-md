<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Evaluate the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) along real and imaginary increments. For a [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md), both give the same value:

$$
f'(z)=u_x+iv_x=\frac{u_y+iv_y}{i}=v_y-iu_y.
$$

Equating real and imaginary parts yields the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.}
$$
