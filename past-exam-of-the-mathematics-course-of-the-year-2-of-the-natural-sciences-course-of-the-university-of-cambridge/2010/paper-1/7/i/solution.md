<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) and use equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md), which holds because [holomorphic functions](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) are smooth:

$$
\boxed{\Delta u=(v_y)_x-(v_x)_y=0,\qquad\Delta v=-(u_y)_x+(u_x)_y=0.}
$$

Thus both parts are [harmonic functions](../../../../../harmonic-function.md).
