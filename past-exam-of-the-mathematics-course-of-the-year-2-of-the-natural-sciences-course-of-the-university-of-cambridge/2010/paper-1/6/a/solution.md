<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Complete the square in the [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md):

$$
Q_1=(x_1+2x_2)^2+x_2^2+6x_3^2.
$$

The real invertible [linear transformation](../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
\boxed{x=Ly,\qquad L=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1/\sqrt6\end{pmatrix}}
$$

sets $x_1+2x_2=y_1$, $x_2=y_2$, $\sqrt6x_3=y_3$, giving $Q_1=y_1^2+y_2^2+y_3^2$. The same transformation gives

$$
\boxed{Q_2=y_1^2+y_2^2-y_3^2.}
$$

Its signature is $(2,1)$. By [Sylvester's law of inertia](../../../../../sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md) no real invertible change of variables can turn this second form into a sum of three positive squares.
