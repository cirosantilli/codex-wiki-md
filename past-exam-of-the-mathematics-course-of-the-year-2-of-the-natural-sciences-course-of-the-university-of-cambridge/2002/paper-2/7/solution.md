A [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) is a fixed displacement pattern whose components share a common harmonic time dependence in the equations for [small oscillations](../../../../small-oscillation.md). Its [normal frequency](../../../../eigenfrequency.md) is the associated oscillation frequency; below, $\omega$ denotes the angular frequency, so the frequency in cycles per unit time is $\omega/(2\pi)$. A [normal coordinate](../../../../normal-coordinate.md) is the amplitude of one such pattern, chosen so that the linear equations decouple. For [mass matrix](../../../../mass-matrix.md) $M$ and [stiffness matrix](../../../../stiffness-matrix.md) $K$, the patterns solve $Kv=\omega^2Mv$. Zero eigenvalues describe free rigid motions rather than periodic restoring oscillations.
