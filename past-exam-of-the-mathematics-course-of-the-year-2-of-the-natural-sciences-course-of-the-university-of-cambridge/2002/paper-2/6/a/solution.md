<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [Cartesian tensors](../../../../../cartesian-tensor.md), let $R$ be the [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) taking old vector components to new ones, $v'_i=R_{ij}v_j$. A rank-$n$ [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) transforms by one copy of $R$ on each index:

$$
\boxed{T'_{i_1\cdots i_n}=R_{i_1j_1}\cdots R_{i_nj_n}T_{j_1\cdots j_n}.}
$$

Repeated indices are summed according to [Einstein notation](../../../../../einstein-notation.md). In general coordinates a [contravariant tensor](../../../../../contravariant-tensor.md) index instead carries the corresponding [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) $\partial x'^i/\partial x^j$, and a [covariant tensor](../../../../../covariant-tensor.md) index carries its inverse; in [orthonormal bases](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of Cartesian axes their component laws coincide with the displayed form.

An [isotropic tensor](../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) has identical components after every proper [orthogonal transformation](../../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md), so $T'=T$ for every $R$ with $R^TR=I$ and $\det R=1$. Requiring reflection invariance as well is a stronger convention for odd tensor ranks. The [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) is invariant under all [orthogonal transformations](../../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md), while the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is invariant under proper rotations and acquires a determinant sign under reflections.
