<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

As $C$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../unitary-matrix.md), $C^{-1}=C^\dagger$. Let $M=C^\dagger AC$. Its [conjugate transpose](../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) is $M^\dagger=C^\dagger A^\dagger C=M$, so $M$ and every positive integer power $M^n$ are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and have real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).

More explicitly, if $Av_j=a_jv_j$ with $a_j$ real, then $C^{-1}v_j$ form an [eigenbasis](../../../../../eigenbasis.md) and

$$
M^n(C^{-1}v_j)=C^{-1}A^nv_j=a_j^n(C^{-1}v_j).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{spec}\bigl((C^{-1}AC)^n\bigr)=\{a_j^n:j=1,\ldots,n_A\}\subset\mathbb R,}
$$

where $n_A$ denotes the matrix size, to distinguish it from the power $n$.
