<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Multiply the differential equation by $-p(x)$ and compare the coefficients of $y''$ and $y'$ with $-(py')'=-py''-p'y'$. The required [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) satisfies $p'=-bp$. Thus one may take

$$
\boxed{p(x)=\exp\left(-\int_{x_*}^x b(s)\,ds\right),\qquad q(x)=-p(x)c(x),\qquad w(x)=p(x)d(x).}
$$

Then $-(py')'+qy=\lambda wy$, the desired [Sturm-Liouville form](../../../../../sturm-liouville-form.md), with the same homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). A common positive constant multiplying $p,q,w$ is immaterial. For the usual regular [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) one also assumes real sufficiently regular coefficients and $d>0$, ensuring $p,w>0$.
