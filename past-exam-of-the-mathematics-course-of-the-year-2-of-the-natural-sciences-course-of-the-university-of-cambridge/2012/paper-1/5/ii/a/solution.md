<h1 id="5/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a positive integer $k$, cancellation of $U^\dagger U=I$ gives $A^k=UD^kU^\dagger$. The [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is invariant under [matrix similarity](../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md), since $\operatorname{tr}(XY)=\operatorname{tr}(YX)$, and the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is multiplicative. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A^k)=\operatorname{tr}(D^k)=\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_j^k,\qquad \det(A^k)=\det(D^k)=\prod_{j=1}^n\lambda_j^k.}
$$

This includes zero eigenvalues without requiring $A$ to be invertible.
