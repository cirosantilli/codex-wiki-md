↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The convergent [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) defining the [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) gives $e^A=Ue^DU^\dagger$, with $e^D=\operatorname{diag}(e^{\lambda_1},\ldots,e^{\lambda_n})$. Hence the [matrix exponential determinant identity](../../../../../../matrix-exponential-determinant-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{\det(e^A)=\prod_{j=1}^ne^{\lambda_j}=e^{\sum_j\lambda_j}=e^{\operatorname{tr}A}.}
$$

The diagonalization proof uses the given Hermitian hypothesis; the identity itself holds for every square real or complex matrix.
