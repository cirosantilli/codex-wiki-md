<h1 id="5/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Applying $A^2=A$ to an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) gives $\lambda_j^2=\lambda_j$, so each [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is either zero or one. If all are one, the [spectral theorem](../../../../../../spectral-theorem.md) gives $A=I$, hence $\det A=1$ and $\operatorname{tr}A=n$. Otherwise, if $m$ eigenvalues are one, with $0\leq m<n$, then

$$
\boxed{\det A=0,\qquad\operatorname{tr}A=m<n.}
$$

Thus the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of this [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) equals its [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md), including $m=0$ for the zero matrix.
