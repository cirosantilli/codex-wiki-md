↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For any [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $K$, the scalar $x^\dagger Kx$ is real because its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) equals $x^\dagger K^\dagger x=x^\dagger Kx$. Applying this to $H,S$ in part (a) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}\lambda=x^\dagger Hx,\qquad\operatorname{Im}\lambda=x^\dagger Sx.}
$$

These are the real and imaginary [quadratic forms](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) associated with the [numerical range](../../../../../../numerical-range.md) of $A$.
