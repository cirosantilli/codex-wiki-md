<h1 id="6/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Multiply the [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) $Ax=\lambda x$ on the left by $x^\dagger$. The [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) normalization is $x^\dagger x=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\lambda=x^\dagger Ax=x^\dagger Hx+i\,x^\dagger Sx.}
$$

No [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) assumption on $A$ is needed: this is an identity for each normalized eigenvector.
