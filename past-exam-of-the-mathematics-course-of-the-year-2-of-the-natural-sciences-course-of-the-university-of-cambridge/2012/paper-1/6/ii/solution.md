↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) $\langle u,v\rangle=u^\dagger v$ on $\mathbb C^n$. The distinct real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) permit a full orthonormal eigenvector expansion; the two child solutions establish that expansion and apply it to the proposed [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md).
