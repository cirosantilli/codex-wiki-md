<h1 id="6/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $Bx_i=\lambda_ix_i$, Hermitian symmetry gives

$$
\lambda_jx_i^\dagger x_j=x_i^\dagger Bx_j=(Bx_i)^\dagger x_j=\lambda_ix_i^\dagger x_j.
$$

Distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) therefore imply $x_i^\dagger x_j=0$ for $i\ne j$. Together with the unit normalizations,

$$
\boxed{x_i^\dagger x_j=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

The $n$ mutually orthogonal nonzero vectors are linearly independent and span the $n$-dimensional space, so they form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Here $\delta_{ij}$ is the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md).
