<h1 id="6/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand $v=\sum_jc_jx_j$, where $c_j=x_j^\dagger v$. By [orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md),

$$
\boxed{\beta=\sum_j|c_j|^2(\lambda_j-a)(\lambda_j-b).}
$$

Since the [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) avoids $[a,b]$, each $\lambda_j$ is either below $a$ or above $b$, making every product strictly positive. This proves [positivity of a quadratic polynomial across a spectral gap](../../../../../../positivity-of-a-quadratic-polynomial-across-a-spectral-gap.md):

$$
\boxed{\beta\geq\delta\|v\|^2,\qquad\delta=\min_j(\lambda_j-a)(\lambda_j-b)>0.}
$$

In particular **$\beta>0$ for every nonzero $v$**. For $v=0$, $\beta=0$; the printed strict claim for an arbitrary vector must exclude this case.
