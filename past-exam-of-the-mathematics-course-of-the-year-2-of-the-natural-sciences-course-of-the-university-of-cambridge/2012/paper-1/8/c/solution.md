<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\ell=0$ the terminating even solution is constant; for $\ell=1$ the terminating odd solution is proportional to $x$. For $\ell=2$, the recurrence gives $a_2=-3a_0$; for $\ell=3$ it gives $a_3=-5a_1/3$. Imposing the [Legendre polynomial](../../../../../legendre-polynomial.md) normalization at $1$ yields

$$
\boxed{P_0(x)=1,\quad P_1(x)=x,\quad P_2(x)=\frac{3x^2-1}{2},\quad P_3(x)=\frac{5x^3-3x}{2}.}
$$
