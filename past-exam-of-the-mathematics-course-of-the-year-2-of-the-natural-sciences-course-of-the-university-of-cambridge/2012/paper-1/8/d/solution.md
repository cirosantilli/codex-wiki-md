<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $W=P_\ell Q_\ell'-P_\ell'Q_\ell$. Differentiation cancels the cross terms, and the [Legendre differential equation](../../../../../legendre-differential-equation.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
W'&=P_\ell Q_\ell''-P_\ell''Q_\ell\\
&=\frac{2x}{1-x^2}(P_\ell Q_\ell'-P_\ell'Q_\ell)=\frac{2x}{1-x^2}W.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $[(1-x^2)W]'=0$, so

$$
\boxed{W(x)=\frac{A_\ell}{1-x^2},}
$$

with $A_\ell$ independent of $x$ on each interval avoiding the singular endpoints. This is the [Abel identity](../../../../../abel-s-identity.md). Linear independence forces $A_\ell\ne0$, and its value depends on the chosen normalization of $Q_\ell$.
