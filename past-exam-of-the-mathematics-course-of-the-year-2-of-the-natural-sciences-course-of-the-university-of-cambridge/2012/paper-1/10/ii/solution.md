Here $p=w=1$ and $q=x^2$ on the whole real line. The [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) energy form is $Q[\psi]=\int_{\mathbb R}(|\psi'|^2+x^2|\psi|^2)dx$. Admissible trials have a square-integrable [weak derivative](../../../../../weak-derivative.md) and finite potential energy; they need not have a classical second derivative everywhere.
