<h1 id="10/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For any nonzero constant $C$, differentiating $\psi_0=Ce^{-x^2/2}$ gives $\psi_0''=(x^2-1)\psi_0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{(-d^2/dx^2+x^2)\psi_0=\psi_0,\qquad\lambda_0=1.}
$$

It is indeed the lowest [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), since the factorization $H=(-d/dx+x)(d/dx+x)+1$ makes $\langle\psi,H\psi\rangle\geq\|\psi\|^2$, with equality for this Gaussian. The variational bound exceeds the exact value by $\sqrt{10/7}-1\simeq0.195229$, or about **$19.52\%$**.

The PDF's prefactor $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$ does not normalize this [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md): its squared norm is $1/(2\sqrt\pi)$. The normalized [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) has prefactor $\pi^{-1/4}$. This normalization error does not affect the eigenvalue, because the equation is homogeneous in the wavefunction.
