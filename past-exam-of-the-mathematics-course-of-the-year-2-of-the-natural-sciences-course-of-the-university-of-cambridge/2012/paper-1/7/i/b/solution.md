The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of a [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) can be evaluated using an increment along the real axis:

$$
f'(z)=u_x+iv_x=u_x-iu_y.
$$

Identifying the components of the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) $\mathbf V=\nabla u$ gives

$$
\boxed{f'(z)=V_x-iV_y.}
$$
