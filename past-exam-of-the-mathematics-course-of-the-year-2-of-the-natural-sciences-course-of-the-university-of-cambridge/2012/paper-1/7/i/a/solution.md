<h1 id="7/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $\mathbf V=\nabla u$, its [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) is the [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) of the velocity potential. Using the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) and equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf V=u_{xx}+u_{yy}=(v_y)_x-(v_x)_y=0.}
$$

Thus this is an [incompressible flow](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md), and $u$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md).
