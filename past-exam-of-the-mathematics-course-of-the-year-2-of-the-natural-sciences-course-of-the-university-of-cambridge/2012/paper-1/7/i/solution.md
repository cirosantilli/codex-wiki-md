↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) combines the [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) $u$ with the [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) $v$. For its [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) representation, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) are $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$.
