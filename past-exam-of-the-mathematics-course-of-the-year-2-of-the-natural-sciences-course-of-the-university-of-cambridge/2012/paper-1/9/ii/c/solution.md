<h1 id="9/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $c>1$, the [catenary](../../../../../../catenary.md) has a positive minimum height $x_0=(c-1)/\beta$ above the road. Its derivatives are

$$
x'(y)=\sinh\left[\frac\beta c(y-y_0)\right],\qquad x''(y)=\frac\beta c\cosh\left[\frac\beta c(y-y_0)\right]>0.
$$

Thus the [light ray](../../../../../../light-ray.md) is symmetric about $y=y_0$, descends towards a horizontal tangent at its minimum, and then rises. It never intersects the road. This smooth upward bending in the [refractive index](../../../../../../refractive-index.md) gradient produces an inferior [mirage](../../../../../../mirage.md), without reflection at the road.
