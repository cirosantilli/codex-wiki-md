<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [Fermat principle](../../../../../fermat-principle.md), a [light ray](../../../../../light-ray.md) makes optical travel time stationary. Multiplication by the constant vacuum [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md) does not change the stationary paths, so the functional can be taken to be the [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) integrated over [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md). The constant denoted $c$ below is an integration constant, not that vacuum speed.
