<h1 id="9/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $f$ is independent of $y$, then $f_y=0$ and the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) reduces to $d f_{y'}/dx=0$. Therefore the [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_{y'}=C.}
$$

This is the conserved generalized momentum for a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md).
