<h1 id="9/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The boundary term $[f_{y'}\eta]_a^b$ vanishes, so stationarity and the [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) give the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{f_y-\frac{d}{dx}f_{y'}=0.}
$$
