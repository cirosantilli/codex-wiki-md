<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the closed surface with its outward orientation. For sufficiently smooth fields defined throughout the solid, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) and the identity that the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) of a [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is zero give

$$
I=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot(\nabla\Omega+\nabla\times\mathbf U)\,dV=\boxed{\iiint_V\Delta\Omega\,dV.}
$$

Thus the [curl](../../../../../curl.md) term carries no net outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md), and $I$ is independent of $\mathbf U$. The hole in the [solid torus](../../../../../solid-torus.md) does not invalidate the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md); singularities of $\mathbf U$ _inside the [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) region_ would invalidate the smoothness argument and must be excluded.
