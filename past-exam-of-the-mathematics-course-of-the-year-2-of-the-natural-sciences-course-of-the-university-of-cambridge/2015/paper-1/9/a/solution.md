<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For fixed endpoint values and smooth admissible variations, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dx}\frac{\partial f}{\partial z'}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}=0.}
$$

The first variation follows by integrating $\int(f_z\eta+f_{z'}\eta')dx$ by parts and using $\eta=0$ at the endpoints.

For the claimed first [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md), differentiate directly:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}(f-z'f_{z'})=f_x+z'\left(f_z-\frac{d}{dx}f_{z'}\right).
$$

Along an extremal the bracket vanishes. Thus when $f_x=0$, the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../beltrami-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{f-z'f_{z'}=\text{constant}.}
$$

For a further regular constraint $G[z]=G_0$, introduce a constant [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) $\lambda$ and make $F-\lambda G$ stationary. Apply the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) to the combined integrand, then impose the endpoints and $G=G_0$ to determine the [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) constants and multiplier.
