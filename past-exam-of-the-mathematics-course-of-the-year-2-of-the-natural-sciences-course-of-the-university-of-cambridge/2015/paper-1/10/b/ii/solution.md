<h1 id="10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The radial [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) is

$$
k_{\rm trial}^2=\frac{\int_0^ar^2(\psi'_{\rm trial})^2dr}{\int_0^ar^2\psi_{\rm trial}^2dr}.
$$

For the given trial profile, the numerator is $4a^{-4}\int_0^ar^4dr=4a/5$. The denominator is

$$
\int_0^ar^2(1-r^2/a^2)^2dr=a^3\left(\frac13-\frac25+\frac17\right)=\frac{8a^3}{105}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{k_0^2\leq k_{\rm trial}^2=\frac{21}{2a^2}=\frac{10.5}{a^2}.}
$$

The profile is regular at the origin and vanishes at the wall, so it belongs to the admissible trial space. This is the variational estimate for the [radial Dirichlet modes of a spherical cavity](../../../../../../radial-dirichlet-modes-of-a-spherical-cavity.md).
