↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [conjugacy class](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) of $g$ is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}_G(g)=\{hgh^{-1}:h\in G\}.}
$$

If $N$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) and $g\in N$, then normality gives $hgh^{-1}\in N$ for every $h$. Thus the whole conjugacy class of each member of $N$ is contained in $N$, and

$$
\boxed{N=\bigcup_{g\in N}\operatorname{Cl}_G(g).}
$$

Conjugacy classes partition the group, so this is a union of complete disjoint classes, never selected fractions of classes.
