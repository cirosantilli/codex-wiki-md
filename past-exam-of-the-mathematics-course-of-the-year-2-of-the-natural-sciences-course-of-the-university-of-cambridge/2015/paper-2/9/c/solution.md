↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md) records the lengths of the disjoint cycles in a [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md), including fixed points as length-one cycles. The possibilities for three objects are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{cycle structure}&\text{elements}&\text{number}\\\hline
1+1+1&e&1\\
2+1&(12),(13),(23)&3\\
3&(123),(132)&2
\end{array}.}
$$

These exhaust the six elements of the [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_3$, denoted $\Sigma_3$ in the source. They are also the [conjugacy classes of the symmetric group on three letters](../../../../../conjugacy-classes-of-the-symmetric-group-on-three-letters.md).
