↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Preserving Cartesian lengths and [Euclidean inner products](../../../../../dot-product.md) requires an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md):

$$
\boxed{M^TM=MM^T=I,\qquad M_{ik}M_{jk}=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

Its determinant is $\pm1$. Relative to an originally right-handed frame, $\det M=+1$ gives a right-handed frame and $\det M=-1$ a left-handed frame. More generally, this sign determines whether the two frames have the same or opposite orientation.

The [tensor component transformation law](../../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{T'_{ij}=M_{ik}M_{j\ell}T_{k\ell},\qquad T'=MTM^T.}
$$

For a [tensor field](../../../../../tensor-field.md), the components are compared at the same physical point, whose coordinates satisfy $x=M^Tx'$.
