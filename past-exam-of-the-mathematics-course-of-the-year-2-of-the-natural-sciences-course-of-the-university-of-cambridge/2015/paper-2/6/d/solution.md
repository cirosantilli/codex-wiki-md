<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [scalar, symmetric-traceless and axial tensor decomposition](../../../../../scalar-symmetric-traceless-and-axial-tensor-decomposition.md) fixes the scalar part by the trace:

$$
\boxed{P=\frac13\operatorname{tr}F=\frac43r^2.}
$$

Subtracting this isotropic part from the [symmetric tensor](../../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) in part (c) gives

$$
\boxed{\widehat S=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}-x_1^2-4x_2^2&3x_1x_2&0\\3x_1x_2&-4x_1^2-x_2^2&0\\0&0&5r^2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its trace is zero. The [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) part is

$$
\boxed{\widehat A=\frac12(F-F^T)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-r^2&x_1-x_2\\r^2&0&-x_1-x_2\\-x_1+x_2&x_1+x_2&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Adding $PI+\widehat S+\widehat A$ reconstructs every entry of $F$.

Subtracting a scalar multiple of the identity preserves every [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md): if $Sv=\lambda v$, then $(S-PI)v=(\lambda-P)v$. Thus the [principal axes](../../../../../principal-axis.md) are **the same as in part (c)**, with shifted principal values

$$
\boxed{-r^2/3\text{ along }e_r,\qquad-4r^2/3\text{ along }e_\theta,\qquad5r^2/3\text{ along }e_3.}
$$

The two tensors are [coaxial symmetric tensors](../../../../../coaxial-symmetric-tensors.md). At $r=0$ both vanish, so neither specifies unique axes.
