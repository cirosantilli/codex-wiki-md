<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $f$ be [meromorphic](../../../../../../meromorphic-function.md) on and inside a positively oriented simple closed [contour](../../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) $C$, with finitely many isolated poles $z_k$ inside and none on $C$. The [residue theorem](../../../../../../residue-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oint_C f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_{z_k\text{ inside }C}\operatorname{Res}_{z=z_k}f(z).}
$$

The positive orientation is counterclockwise. A clockwise contour reverses the sign, and the [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) is the coefficient of $(z-z_k)^{-1}$ in the local [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md).
