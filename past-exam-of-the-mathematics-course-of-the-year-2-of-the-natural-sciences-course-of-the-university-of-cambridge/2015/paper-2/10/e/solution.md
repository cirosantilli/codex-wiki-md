<h1 id="10/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is the trace. For the planar representation, the rotation trace and reflection trace give

$$
\boxed{\chi_2(R^k)=2\cos\frac{k\pi}{3},\qquad\chi_2(m_j)=0.}
$$

In the source’s order $(I,R,R^2,R^3,R^4,R^5,m_1,\ldots,m_6)$, these values are $(2,1,-1,-2,-1,1,0,0,0,0,0,0)$.

In the [regular representation](../../../../../regular-representation.md), the trace counts basis vectors fixed by left multiplication. The equation $gh=h$ forces $g=I$, so a nonidentity element fixes none, while the identity fixes all 12. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\rm reg}(I)=12,\qquad\chi_{\rm reg}(g)=0\quad(g\ne I).}
$$

In particular $\chi_{\rm reg}(m_4)=0$, consistently with the zero diagonal in part (d). The two characters have different identity values because the representation dimensions are two and twelve.
