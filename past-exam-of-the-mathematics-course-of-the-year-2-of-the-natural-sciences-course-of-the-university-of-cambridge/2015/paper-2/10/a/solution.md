<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The table describes the [symmetry group](../../../../../symmetry-group.md) of a regular [hexagon](../../../../../hexagon.md). Choose $R$ as a counterclockwise rotation through $\pi/3$ and $m_1$ as reflection in one symmetry axis. With $s=m_1$, the relations are

$$
R^6=I,\qquad s^2=I,\qquad sRs=R^{-1}.
$$

The rotation entries already are $I,R,\ldots,R^5$, and the table gives $Rm_j=m_{j+1}$ with indices modulo six. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m_j=R^{j-1}s\quad(j=1,\ldots,6),\qquad G=\{R^k,R^ks:0\leq k<6\}.}
$$

The source writes $D_6$ with the subscript counting polygon vertices; this [dihedral group](../../../../../dihedral-group.md) has order 12.
