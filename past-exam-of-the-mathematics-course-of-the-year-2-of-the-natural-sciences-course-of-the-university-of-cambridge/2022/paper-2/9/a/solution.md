<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $g\in G$, the left [coset](../../../../../coset.md) of $H$ represented by $g$ is

$$
\boxed{gH=\{gh:h\in H\}.}
$$

It is a subset of $G$ obtained by multiplying every element of $H$ on the left by the same [group](../../../../../group-split.md) element. The identity representative gives $eH=H$.
