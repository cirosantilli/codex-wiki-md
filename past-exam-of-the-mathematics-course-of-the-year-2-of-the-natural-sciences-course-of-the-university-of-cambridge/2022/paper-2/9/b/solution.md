<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For each $g$, the map

$$
L_g:H\longrightarrow gH,\qquad h\longmapsto gh
$$

is surjective by definition of a [coset](../../../../../coset.md) and injective by left cancellation. Its inverse is multiplication by $g^{-1}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|gH|=|H|\quad\text{for every }g\in G.}
$$

The same argument with right multiplication proves the analogous result for right [cosets](../../../../../coset.md). This equality of sizes is the counting step in [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md).
