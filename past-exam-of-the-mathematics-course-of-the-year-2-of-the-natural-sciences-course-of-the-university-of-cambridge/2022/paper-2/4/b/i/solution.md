<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The factor $z^{-1/4}$ has [algebraic branch points](../../../../../../algebraic-branch-point.md) at zero and infinity. Near zero the leading behaviour is $z^{-1/4}$; with $w=1/z$, the behaviour near infinity is $g(1/w)\sim w^{9/4}$. These are four-sheeted branch points.

The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) denominator vanishes at $z=\pm i$, with nonzero [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) $2z$, and the fractional factor is finite and nonzero near either point on any chosen local branch. Hence

$$
\boxed{z=\pm i\text{ are simple poles};\qquad z=0,\infty\text{ are branch points}.}
$$

A single-valued [holomorphic](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) branch is obtained by choosing a [branch cut](../../../../../../branch-cut.md) joining zero to infinity. Points along the chosen cut mark its discontinuity, rather than additional isolated poles.
