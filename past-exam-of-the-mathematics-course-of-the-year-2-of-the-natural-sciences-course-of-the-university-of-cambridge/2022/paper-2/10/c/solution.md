<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three order-four [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) are

$$
\boxed{G_1=\{W,X,\widehat W,\widehat X\}=\langle X\rangle,\quad
G_2=\{W,Y,\widehat W,\widehat Y\}=\langle Y\rangle,\quad
G_3=\{W,Z,\widehat W,\widehat Z\}=\langle Z\rangle.}
$$

In each case the generator has powers $A,A^2=\widehat W,A^3=\widehat A,A^4=W$, so all three are cyclic and mutually isomorphic to $C_4$, a [cyclic group of order four](../../../../../cyclic-group-of-order-four.md). For $G_1$ the table is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccc}
&W&X&\widehat W&\widehat X\\\hline
W&W&X&\widehat W&\widehat X\\
X&X&\widehat W&\widehat X&W\\
\widehat W&\widehat W&\widehat X&W&X\\
\widehat X&\widehat X&W&X&\widehat W
\end{array}}
$$

The maps $X^j\mapsto Y^j$ and $X^j\mapsto Z^j$ are explicit [group isomorphisms](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md). These are all order-four [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md): any such [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) contains an element other than $W,\widehat W$, and that element has order four and already generates the [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md). They share the order-two [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $\{W,\widehat W\}$ and are each normal, being of index two.
