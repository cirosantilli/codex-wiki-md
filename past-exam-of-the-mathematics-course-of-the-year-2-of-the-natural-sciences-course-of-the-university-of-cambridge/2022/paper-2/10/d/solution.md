<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $G_1=\langle X\rangle$, define the one-dimensional complex [group representation](../../../../../group-representation.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho(X^j)=i^j,\qquad j=0,1,2,3.}
$$

Explicitly $W\mapsto1$, $X\mapsto i$, $\widehat W\mapsto-1$ and $\widehat X\mapsto-i$. Since $i^4=1$, the map is well-defined on exponents modulo four and preserves products. Its four images are distinct, so it is also a [faithful representation](../../../../../faithful-representation.md). The same construction applies to either of the other cyclic [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md).
