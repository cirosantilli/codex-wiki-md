<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The explicit [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) are

$$
W=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
X=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix},\quad
Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
Z=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
\boxed{WW=W,\quad XX=YY=ZZ=\widehat W,\quad XY=Z,\quad YZ=X,\quad ZX=Y.}
$$

The reverse products have the opposite signs: $YX=\widehat Z$, $ZY=\widehat X$, $XZ=\widehat Y$. Since $W$ is the [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md) and $X^2=Y^2=Z^2=-W$,

$$
\boxed{W^{-1}=W,\qquad X^{-1}=\widehat X,\qquad Y^{-1}=\widehat Y,\qquad Z^{-1}=\widehat Z.}
$$

These are the usual quaternion multiplication relations in the [complex matrix representation of quaternions](../../../../../complex-matrix-representation-of-quaternions.md).
