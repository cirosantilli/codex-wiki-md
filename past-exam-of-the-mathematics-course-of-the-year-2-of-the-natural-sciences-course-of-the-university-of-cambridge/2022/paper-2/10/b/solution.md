<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The products in (a), together with $(-A)B=A(-B)=-(AB)$ and $(-A)(-B)=AB$, give closure of the eight-element set. [Associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) is inherited from [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md), $W$ is the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md), and the inverses from (a), with $( -A)^{-1}=-A^{-1}$, remain in the set. Thus the [group axioms](../../../../../group-axioms.md) hold. This is the [quaternion group](../../../../../quaternion-group.md) $Q_8$.

In the element order specified in the question, the [multiplication table](../../../../../multiplication-table.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
&W&X&Y&Z&\widehat W&\widehat X&\widehat Y&\widehat Z\\\hline
W&W&X&Y&Z&\widehat W&\widehat X&\widehat Y&\widehat Z\\
X&X&\widehat W&Z&\widehat Y&\widehat X&W&\widehat Z&Y\\
Y&Y&\widehat Z&\widehat W&X&\widehat Y&Z&W&\widehat X\\
Z&Z&Y&\widehat X&\widehat W&\widehat Z&\widehat Y&X&W\\
\widehat W&\widehat W&\widehat X&\widehat Y&\widehat Z&W&X&Y&Z\\
\widehat X&\widehat X&W&\widehat Z&Y&X&\widehat W&Z&\widehat Y\\
\widehat Y&\widehat Y&Z&W&\widehat X&Y&\widehat Z&\widehat W&X\\
\widehat Z&\widehat Z&\widehat Y&X&W&Z&Y&\widehat X&\widehat W
\end{array}}
$$

Interpreting “character of the [group](../../../../../group-split.md)” as the [character of a representation](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) specified by these [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md), take the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of each [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md):

$$
\boxed{\chi(W)=2,\qquad\chi(\widehat W)=-2,\qquad
\chi(X)=\chi(Y)=\chi(Z)=\chi(\widehat X)=\chi(\widehat Y)=\chi(\widehat Z)=0.}
$$

A character belongs to a [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md); the underlying abstract [group](../../../../../group-split.md) can have other characters.
