<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For two bars, after subtracting the gravitational [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md),

$$
K=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad M=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&m_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

A [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) $\boldsymbol\eta=\mathbf v e^{i\omega t}$ requires the [generalized eigenvalue problem](../../../../../../generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) $(K-\omega^2M)\mathbf v=0$. With $z=\omega^2$,

$$
0=(2-z)(2-m_2z)-1=m_2z^2-2(m_2+1)z+3.
$$

Thus, for $m_2>0$,

$$
\boxed{\omega_{1,2}=\sqrt{\frac{m_2+1\mp\sqrt{m_2^2-m_2+1}}{m_2}},\qquad\omega_1<\omega_2.}
$$

Both squared [angular frequencies](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) are positive. A corresponding [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) is $(1,2-\omega_j^2)^T$, from the first row of the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) equation.
