<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $z=\omega_1^2$, so $\omega_2^2=4z$. The sum and product of roots of the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) in (i) give

$$
5z=\frac{2(m_2+1)}{m_2},\qquad 4z^2=\frac3{m_2}.
$$

Eliminating $z$ yields

$$
16(m_2+1)^2=75m_2,\qquad16m_2^2-43m_2+16=0.
$$

Therefore the [octave tuning in a two-mass complete spring graph](../../../../../../octave-tuning-in-a-two-mass-complete-spring-graph.md) has two positive solutions:

$$
\boxed{m_2=\frac{43\pm5\sqrt{33}}{32}\simeq0.446162086\text{ or }2.241337914.}
$$

The two values are reciprocals. Both meet the requested [frequency ratio](../../../../../../frequency-ratio.md); substituting either into the expressions in (i) gives $\omega_2/\omega_1=2$.
