<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For three unit [masses](../../../../../mass.md), $M=I$ and

$$
K=k\begin{pmatrix}3&-1&-1\\-1&3&-1\\-1&-1&3\end{pmatrix}
=k(4I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T).
$$

The uniform vector has $\mathbf1\mathbf1^T\mathbf1=3\mathbf1$, whereas every vector with coordinate sum zero is annihilated by $\mathbf1\mathbf1^T$. One orthonormal choice of [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf v_1&=\frac1{\sqrt3}(1,1,1)^T,&\omega_1&=\sqrt{k},\\
\mathbf v_2&=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,-1,0)^T,&\omega_2&=2\sqrt{k},\\
\mathbf v_3&=\frac1{\sqrt6}(1,1,-2)^T,&\omega_3&=2\sqrt{k}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

If $k=1$ is retained from (b), the [angular frequencies](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) are $1,2,2$. The equal upper [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) follow from [degenerate normal modes from permutation symmetry](../../../../../degenerate-normal-modes-from-permutation-symmetry.md): the stiffness is scalar on the two-dimensional sum-zero subspace. In the uniform mode the inter-bar strings do not change length, but all three beam connections stretch, giving the nonzero lower [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). The motion is an arbitrary sine-cosine [superposition](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) of these modes about the gravitational [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
