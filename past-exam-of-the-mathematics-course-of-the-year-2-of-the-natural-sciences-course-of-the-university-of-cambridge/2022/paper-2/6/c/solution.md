<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The required contractions are

$$
s=A^TA=5,\qquad TA=\begin{pmatrix}10\\90\\-70\end{pmatrix},\qquad
q=A^TTA=250,\qquad\tau=100.
$$

Substituting into the [axis decomposition of a symmetric matrix](../../../../../axis-decomposition-of-a-symmetric-matrix.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=25,\qquad\beta=5,\qquad B=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-2\\-4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=\begin{pmatrix}-15&16&32\\16&3&6\\32&6&12\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Indeed $A^TB=-4+4=0$, $CA=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}C=-15+3+12=0$. Adding $25I+5AA^T+BA^T+AB^T+C$ reconstructs the given [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md).
