<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Map each symmetry to the [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) it induces on the three vertices. This is a [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), because composing geometric motions composes their vertex permutations. In the labels chosen in (b), the map is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
g&e&r&r^2&s&rs&r^2s\\\hline
\Phi(g)&e&(123)&(132)&(23)&(12)&(13)
\end{array}}
$$

All six images are distinct and exhaust the [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_3$. Equivalently, the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is trivial because a triangle symmetry fixing all three vertices is the identity. Thus the map is bijective and

$$
\boxed{D_3\cong S_3.}
$$
