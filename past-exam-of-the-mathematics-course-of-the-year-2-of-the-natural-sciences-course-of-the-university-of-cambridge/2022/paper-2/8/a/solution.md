The [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) of a finite [group](../../../../../group-split.md) is its number of elements, $\boxed{|G|=\#G}$. A [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ is normal when [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) by any [group](../../../../../group-split.md) element leaves it unchanged:

$$
\boxed{gHg^{-1}=H\quad\text{for every }g\in G.}
$$

Equivalently, its left and right [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) agree: $gH=Hg$ for every $g$.
