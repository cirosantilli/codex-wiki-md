↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is the oriented [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) of the three [coordinate basis](../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) vectors:

$$
J=\det\frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,\phi)}=(\mathbf h_u\times\mathbf h_v)\cdot\mathbf h_\phi.
$$

Here $\mathbf h_u\times\mathbf h_v=(u^2+v^2)(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi,0)$, so

$$
\boxed{J=uv(u^2+v^2),\qquad dV=uv(u^2+v^2)\,du\,dv\,d\phi.}
$$

The sign is nonnegative on the stated range, and the absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) also equals $h_uh_vh_\phi$ as expected for [orthogonal coordinates](../../../../../orthogonal-coordinates.md).
