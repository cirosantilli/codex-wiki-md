<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the position [vector](../../../../../vector.md) with respect to the three coordinates to obtain the [coordinate basis](../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) vectors:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf h_u=\begin{pmatrix}v\cos\phi\\v\sin\phi\\u\end{pmatrix},\qquad\mathbf h_v=\begin{pmatrix}u\cos\phi\\u\sin\phi\\-v\end{pmatrix},\qquad\mathbf h_\phi=\begin{pmatrix}-uv\sin\phi\\uv\cos\phi\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $d\mathbf x=\mathbf h_u du+\mathbf h_v dv+\mathbf h_\phi d\phi$. The [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](../../../../../scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) are the corresponding [norms](../../../../../norm.md):

$$
\boxed{h_u=h_v=\sqrt{u^2+v^2},\qquad h_\phi=uv.}
$$

Direct [dot products](../../../../../dot-product.md) give $\mathbf h_u\cdot\mathbf h_v=uv-uv=0$, $\mathbf h_u\cdot\mathbf h_\phi=0$, and $\mathbf h_v\cdot\mathbf h_\phi=0$. Thus the [parabolic coordinates](../../../../../parabolic-coordinates.md) are orthogonal wherever the coordinate vectors are nondegenerate. Their degeneracy on $uv=0$ is the usual axis degeneracy of the azimuthal coordinate.
