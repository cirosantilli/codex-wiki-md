<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $t=1-x$ and $Y(t)=y(x)$. The [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) becomes

$$
tY''+Y'+(c^2t+\lambda)Y=0.
$$

Substitute the [Frobenius solution](../../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) $Y=t^\sigma\sum_{n\geq0}a_nt^n$. The lowest power has coefficient $\sigma^2a_0$, so

$$
\boxed{\sigma=0\quad\text{is the repeated indicial root}.}
$$

Equating successive coefficients gives, with $a_{-1}=0$,

$$
\boxed{n^2a_n+\lambda a_{n-1}+c^2a_{n-2}=0\quad(n\geq1),\qquad a_n=-\frac{\lambda a_{n-1}+c^2a_{n-2}}{n^2}.}
$$

Thus $a_0\ne0$ is the only free coefficient: $a_1=-\lambda a_0$, $a_2=(\lambda^2-c^2)a_0/4$, and so on. Since every denominator is nonzero, there is exactly one independent pure [Frobenius solution](../../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) of this form. The repeated root supplies no second free coefficient; the other solution is logarithmic, as parts (iii) and (iv) demonstrate.
