<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous equation is a [Cauchy-Euler equation](../../../../../cauchy-euler-equation.md). With the [power-law ansatz](../../../../../power-law-ansatz.md) $y=x^r$ on $x>0$, its [indicial equation](../../../../../indicial-equation.md) is

$$
r(r-1)+\alpha r+\frac{(\alpha-1)^2}{4}=\left(r+\frac{\alpha-1}{2}\right)^2=0.
$$

Thus $r=(1-\alpha)/2$ is a repeated [indicial root](../../../../../indicial-root.md). The two independent solutions are $x^r$ and $x^r\log x$, giving

$$
\boxed{y_h(x)=x^{(1-\alpha)/2}(C_1+C_2\log x),\qquad x>0.}
$$

Their [Wronskian](../../../../../wronskian.md), with $W=y_1y_2'-y_1'y_2$, is $x^{2r-1}=x^{-\alpha}\ne0$, verifying [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md).
