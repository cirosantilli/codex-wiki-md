↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $w_j=\epsilon_{j\ell m}\partial_\ell u_m$ and contract the [Levi-Civita symbols](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md):

$$
(\mathbf w\times\mathbf u)_i=\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{j\ell m}(\partial_\ell u_m)u_k=u_\ell\partial_\ell u_i-u_m\partial_i u_m=u_\ell\partial_\ell u_i-\partial_i\frac{|\mathbf u|^2}{2}.
$$

This proves the [convective acceleration identity](../../../../../convective-acceleration-identity.md), with the sign set by the displayed order $\mathbf w\times\mathbf u$.
