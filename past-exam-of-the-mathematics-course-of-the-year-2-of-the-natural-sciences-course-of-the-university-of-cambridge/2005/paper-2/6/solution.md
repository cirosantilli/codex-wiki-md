<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Under an orthogonal Cartesian change $x'_i=R_{ij}x_j$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\partial'_i=(\partial x_j/\partial x'_i)\partial_j=R_{ij}\partial_j$. Thus the derivative operator transforms as a rank-one [Cartesian tensor](../../../../cartesian-tensor.md). Under a general coordinate change it has the covector transformation law when applied to a scalar; derivatives of vector components then need the usual connection terms. The calculations below use Cartesian coordinates.
