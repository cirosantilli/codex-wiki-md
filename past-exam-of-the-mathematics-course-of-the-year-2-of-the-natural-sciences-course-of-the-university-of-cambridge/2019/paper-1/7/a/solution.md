<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $f=u+iv$ analytic in $z=x+iy$, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) are

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.}
$$

They follow by computing the same complex [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) along the real and imaginary coordinate directions.
