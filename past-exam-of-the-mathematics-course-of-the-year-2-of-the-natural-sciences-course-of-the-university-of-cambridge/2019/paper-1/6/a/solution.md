<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By the [spectral theorem](../../../../../spectral-theorem.md), a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) has real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and an orthonormal eigenbasis. Choose column vectors $u_j$ with $Hu_j=\lambda_ju_j$ and $u_i^\dagger u_j=\delta_{ij}$, and form $U=(u_1\ \cdots\ u_n)$. Then $U^\dagger U=I$, so $U$ is unitary, and $HU=U\Lambda$ gives

$$
\boxed{U^\dagger HU=\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n).}
$$

The entries of $\Lambda$ are the real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), repeated according to their multiplicities. Within a degenerate [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md), any [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) may be used. Equivalently $H=U\Lambda U^\dagger$.
