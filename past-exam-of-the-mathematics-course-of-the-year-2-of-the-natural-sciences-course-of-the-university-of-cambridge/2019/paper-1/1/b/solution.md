<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an oriented piecewise smooth surface $S$ with boundary curve $C=\partial S$, and a continuously differentiable vector field $\mathbf F$ near $S$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r.}
$$

Here $\mathbf n$ is the chosen [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md), $dS$ is positive surface area, and $d\mathbf r$ is the directed tangent [line element](../../../../../line-element.md). The positive traversal of $C$ is fixed by the right-hand rule relative to $\mathbf n$. Thus the [flux](../../../../../flux.md) of the [curl](../../../../../curl.md) through the surface equals the circulation along its oriented boundary.
