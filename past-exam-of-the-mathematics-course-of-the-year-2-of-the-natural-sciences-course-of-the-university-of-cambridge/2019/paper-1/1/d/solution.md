<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $u>0$, eliminating $v$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^2}{a^2\cosh^2u}+\frac{y^2}{a^2\sinh^2u}=1.}
$$

Thus constant $u$ surfaces are confocal [elliptic cylinders](../../../../../elliptic-cylinder.md) parallel to the $z$ axis. The limiting $u=0$ surface is the vertical strip $y=0$, $|x|\leq a$.

For generic $v$, eliminating $u$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^2}{a^2\cos^2v}-\frac{y^2}{a^2\sin^2v}=1.}
$$

These are [hyperbolic cylinders](../../../../../hyperbolic-cylinder.md) with the same focal lines. A specified angular value selects the half-branch with $\operatorname{sgn}x=\operatorname{sgn}(\cos v)$ and $\operatorname{sgn}y=\operatorname{sgn}(\sin v)$, because $u\geq0$. At $v=0,\pi$ the surfaces are the vertical half-planes $y=0$, $x\geq a$ or $x\leq-a$; at $v=\pi/2,3\pi/2$ they are $x=0$, $y\geq0$ or $y\leq0$.

Finally, constant $z$ gives horizontal planes. The three surface families express the [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of [elliptic cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../elliptic-cylindrical-coordinates.md).
