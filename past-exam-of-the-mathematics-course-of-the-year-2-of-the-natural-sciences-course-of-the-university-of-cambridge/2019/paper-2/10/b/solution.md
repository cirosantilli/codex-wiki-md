<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md), two [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md) are in the same [conjugacy class](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) exactly when they have the same [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md). For $S_4$ the five possibilities and their sizes are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{cycle type}&\text{representative}&\text{class size}\\ \hline
1^4&e&1\\
2\,1^2&(12)&6\\
2^2&(12)(34)&3\\
3\,1&(123)&8\\
4&(1234)&6
\end{array}}.
$$

There are $\binom42=6$ [transpositions](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md), three pairings into two unordered pairs, $\binom43(3-1)!=8$ three-cycles, and $(4-1)!=6$ four-cycles. Together with the identity these counts sum to $24=|S_4|$.
