<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two [one-dimensional representations](../../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md) are the [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md), $\rho_+(g)=1$, and the [sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md), $\rho_-(g)=\operatorname{sgn}(g)$. The first respects multiplication immediately. For the second, the [sign of a permutation](../../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md) is the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of its [permutation matrix](../../../../../permutation-matrix.md), and multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) proves $\rho_-(gh)=\rho_-(g)\rho_-(h)$. They are inequivalent since a [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) has images $1$ and $-1$, and conjugation by a nonzero scalar cannot change a one-dimensional matrix.

Their [group homomorphism kernels](../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) are

$$
\boxed{\ker\rho_+=S_4,\qquad \ker\rho_-=A_4}.
$$

Both are [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md), indeed [normal subgroups](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), because they are kernels. In the second case $A_4$ is the [alternating group](../../../../../alternating-group.md) of even [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md), with twelve elements.

The [characters](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) are their scalar values, since the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of a one-dimensional matrix is its only entry. In the class order from part (b),

$$
\chi_+=(1,1,1,1,1),\qquad \chi_-=(1,-1,1,1,-1).
$$

The [character inner product](../../../../../character-inner-product.md) is the group average of $\chi_+\overline{\chi_-}$, so

$$
\boxed{\langle\chi_+,\chi_-\rangle
=\frac1{24}(1-6+3+8-6)=0}.
$$

Each has squared norm $(1+6+3+8+6)/24=1$, in agreement with [character orthogonality](../../../../../character-orthogonality.md) for distinct [irreducible representations](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md).
