↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A weighted [Sturm-Liouville operator](../../../../../sturm-liouville-operator.md) has the form

$$
\boxed{Ly=\frac1{w(x)}\left[-\frac{d}{dx}\left(p(x)\frac{dy}{dx}\right)+q(x)y\right]},
$$

where $p,q,w$ are real, $p>0$ and $w>0$ in the interval's interior, and the coefficients have the regularity required by the derivatives. The corresponding [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) is $-(py')'+qy=\lambda wy$. Its natural [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md) is $\langle f,g\rangle_w=\int w f\bar g\,dx$. Suitable endpoint conditions must also annihilate $[p(f\bar g'-f'\bar g)]$ for a [self-adjoint differential operator](../../../../../self-adjoint-differential-operator.md); the displayed differential expression alone does not specify those conditions.
