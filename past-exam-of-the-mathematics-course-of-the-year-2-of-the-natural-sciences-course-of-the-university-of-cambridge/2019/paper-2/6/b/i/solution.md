<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Introduce the second moment [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) $M_{ij}=\int_Vx_ix_j\,d^3x$. The [spherical shell](../../../../../../spherical-shell.md) is preserved by every [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), so this is an [isotropic tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md), $M_{ij}=A\delta_{ij}$. Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) determines the constant:

$$
3A=M_{ii}=\int_Vr^2\,d^3x
=4\pi\int_{R_1}^{R_2}r^4\,dr
=\frac{4\pi}{5}(R_2^5-R_1^5).
$$

Contracting with the components of the two unit [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_V(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf n_1)(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf n_2)\,d^3x
=\frac{4\pi}{15}(R_2^5-R_1^5)(\mathbf n_1\cdot\mathbf n_2)}.
$$

This is the second-order case of [radial tensor moments in three dimensions](../../../../../../radial-tensor-moments-in-three-dimensions.md).
