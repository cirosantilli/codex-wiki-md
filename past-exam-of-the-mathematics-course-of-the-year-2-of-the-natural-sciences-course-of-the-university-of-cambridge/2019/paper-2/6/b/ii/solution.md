<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under inversion $\mathbf x\mapsto-\mathbf x$, the [spherical shell](../../../../../../spherical-shell.md) and its volume element are unchanged, while the product of three [scalar products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) changes sign. Its integral is therefore its own negative:

$$
\boxed{\int_V(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf n_1)(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf n_2)
(\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf n_3)\,d^3x=0}.
$$

Equivalently, its rank-three moment [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) is completely symmetric and cannot have a nonzero multiple of the antisymmetric [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md).
