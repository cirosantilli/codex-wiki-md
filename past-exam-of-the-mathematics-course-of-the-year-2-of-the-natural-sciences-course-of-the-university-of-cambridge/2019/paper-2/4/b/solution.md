<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) of the [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md) $g$ gives

$$
\frac{g(z)}{z-z_0}=\frac{g(z_0)}{z-z_0}+g'(z_0)+O(z-z_0).
$$

The coefficient of $(z-z_0)^{-1}$, and therefore the [residue](../../../../../residue.md), is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_{z=z_0}\frac{g(z)}{z-z_0}=g(z_0)}.
$$

If $g(z_0)=0$, the apparent [pole](../../../../../pole.md) is a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md) and the [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is zero.
