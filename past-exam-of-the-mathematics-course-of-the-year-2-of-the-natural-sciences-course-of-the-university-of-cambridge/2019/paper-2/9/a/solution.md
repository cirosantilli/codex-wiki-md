↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $f:G\to G'$ satisfies $f(gh)=f(g)f(h)$ for all $g,h\in G$. It consequently sends the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) to the identity and inverses to inverses. Its [group homomorphism kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ker f=\{g\in G:f(g)=e_{G'}\}}.
$$

A [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) $H$ of $G$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) invariant under [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md):

$$
\boxed{gHg^{-1}=H\quad\text{for every }g\in G}.
$$

Equivalently, $gH=Hg$ for every $g$, so left and right [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) coincide.
