↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**No.** The [quaternion group](../../../../../quaternion-group.md)

$$
Q_8=\{1,-1,i,-i,j,-j,k,-k\},\qquad
i^2=j^2=k^2=ijk=-1,
$$

is another [non-abelian group](../../../../../non-abelian-group.md) of order eight: for example $ij=k$ while $ji=-k$. Its multiplication comes from the associative [quaternion](../../../../../quaternion.md) algebra, so it does define a [group](../../../../../group-split.md).

It is not [isomorphic](../../../../../isomorphism.md) to the [dihedral group](../../../../../dihedral-group.md) of the square. In $Q_8$, only $-1$ has order two; the six elements $\pm i,\pm j,\pm k$ have order four. The square's [dihedral group](../../../../../dihedral-group.md) has five elements of order two: four reflections and the half-turn. [Group isomorphisms](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) preserve element orders, so these groups are distinct. The hypothesis in part (c) excludes $Q_8$, since its only element of order two is already in each cyclic subgroup of order four.
