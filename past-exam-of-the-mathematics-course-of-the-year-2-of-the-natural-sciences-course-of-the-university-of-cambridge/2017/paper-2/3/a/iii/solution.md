<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The domain is the three-dimensional upper [half-space](../../../../../../half-space.md). Reflect the source point to $\mathbf r'^*=(x',y',-z')$. The [method of images](../../../../../../method-of-images.md) places an opposite source at this reflected point, giving the [Dirichlet image kernel for the three-dimensional modified Helmholtz equation](../../../../../../dirichlet-image-kernel-for-the-three-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{G_D(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=-\frac1{4\pi}\left[\frac{e^{-\kappa|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}-\frac{e^{-\kappa|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'^*|}}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'^*|}\right],\qquad\kappa=|k|.}
$$

On $z=0$, the two [Euclidean distances](../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) are equal, so the terms cancel exactly. The image lies outside the domain, so its contribution satisfies the homogeneous [modified Helmholtz equation](../../../../../../modified-helmholtz-equation.md) everywhere inside; only the original source contributes a [Dirac delta](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md). Both terms decay at infinity, including for $k=0$, and the homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) follows.
