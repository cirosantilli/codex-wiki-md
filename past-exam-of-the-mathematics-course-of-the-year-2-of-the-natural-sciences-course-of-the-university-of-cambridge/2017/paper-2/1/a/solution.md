The relevant [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md) is $\langle u,v\rangle_w=\int_0^1w(x)u(x)v(x)\,dx$. The normalized [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md) of the [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) have the [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) property

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1w(x)y_n(x)y_m(x)\,dx=\delta_{nm}.}
$$

Here $\delta_{nm}$ is the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md): the integral is one for $n=m$ and zero otherwise. The weight is $w$, rather than $p$ or the unweighted measure.
