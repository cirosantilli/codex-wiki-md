<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A member of $H$ is $A(t,b,t)$. The multiplication law from part (a) becomes

$$
A(t,b,t)A(s,d,s)=A(t+s,\ b+d+ts,\ t+s).
$$

It stays in $H$ and is unchanged when the two factors are exchanged, since $ts=st$. The identity lies in $H$, and $A(t,b,t)^{-1}=A(-t,t^2-b,-t)$ also lies in $H$. Thus $H$ is an [Abelian subgroup](../../../../../abelian-subgroup.md), namely the [Abelian plane subgroup of a finite Heisenberg group](../../../../../abelian-plane-subgroup-of-a-finite-heisenberg-group.md) in this coordinate convention.

There are five choices each for $t,b$, so

$$
\boxed{|H|=25,\qquad [G:H]=\frac{125}{25}=5.}
$$

This verifies [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md): the subgroup order divides the group order, and the number of distinct left [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) is five.

Explicitly, the map $\varphi(A(a,b,c))=a-c$ is a surjective [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) to the additive group of $\mathbb F_5$, by the product formula. Its [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is $H$. Thus $H$ is even a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md); the five left [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) are the level sets $a-c=d$ for $d=0,1,2,3,4$, with representatives $A(d,0,0)$. Each contains 25 elements, providing a direct count as well.
