↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$, the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of the proposed [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is

$$
H^\dagger=-i(B^\dagger A^\dagger-A^\dagger B^\dagger)
=-i(BA-AB)=i(AB-BA)=H.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{i[A,B]\ \text{is Hermitian}.}
$$

The [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) of two [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is skew-Hermitian, and multiplication by $i$ changes it into a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). No simplicity assumption on the spectra is needed for this part.
