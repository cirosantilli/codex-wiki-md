<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf G$ on a bounded volume $V$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary $\partial V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_{\partial V}\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf G\,dV,}
$$

where $\mathbf n$ is the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md). Thus total outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md) through a closed surface equals the integral of the local [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) inside it. A nonclosed surface first needs a suitable closure, with the added surface's [flux](../../../../../flux.md) accounted for separately.
