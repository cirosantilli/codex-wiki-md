<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$. Therefore

$$
|\nabla u|^2=u_x^2+u_y^2=v_y^2+v_x^2=|\nabla v|^2.
$$

Taking the nonnegative square root yields

$$
\boxed{|\nabla u|=|\nabla v|.}
$$

The two [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) are also orthogonal, since $\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=v_yv_x-v_xv_y=0$, although equality of their lengths alone is the requested result.
