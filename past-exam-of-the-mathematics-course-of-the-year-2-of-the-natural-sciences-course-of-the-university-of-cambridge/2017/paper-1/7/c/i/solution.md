<h1 id="7/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For coefficients $a_n=1/\ln n$, the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) gives $R^{-1}=\limsup|a_n|^{1/n}$. Here

$$
|a_n|^{1/n}=\exp\left[-\frac{\ln\ln n}{n}\right]\longrightarrow1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{R=1.}
$$

Omitting the first two powers has no effect on the [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md).
