<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The monotonic horizontal coordinate allows the path to be written as a graph. Its [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) element is $ds=\sqrt{1+y'^2}\,dx$, so the variational density is $f(y,y')=g(y)\sqrt{1+y'^2}$. It has no explicit horizontal-coordinate dependence. The [Beltrami identity](../../../../../../beltrami-identity.md) becomes

$$
f-y'f_{y'}=g\sqrt{1+y'^2}-\frac{g y'^2}{\sqrt{1+y'^2}}
=\frac{g}{\sqrt{1+y'^2}}.
$$

Consequently every smooth minimizing path satisfies

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}\left(\frac{g(y)}{\sqrt{1+y'^2}}\right)=0.}
$$

This is a first integral of the weighted arc-length problem; fixed endpoint values remove the endpoint variation terms.
