↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $N\leq G$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) when conjugation by every group element preserves it:

$$
\boxed{gNg^{-1}=N\quad\text{for all }g\in G.}
$$

Equivalently, $gN=Ng$ for every $g$; normality permits the cosets to inherit a quotient-group multiplication.

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) is a map preserving multiplication:

$$
\boxed{\phi:G_1\to G_2,\qquad\phi(gh)=\phi(g)\phi(h).}
$$

It consequently sends identity to identity and inverses to inverses, since $\phi(e)=\phi(e)^2$ and $\phi(g)\phi(g^{-1})=\phi(e)$.
