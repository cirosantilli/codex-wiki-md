<h1 id="8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $|G|=p$ be prime and choose $g\ne e$. Its order divides $p$ by part (iii), and cannot be one. Hence its order is $p$, so its generated subgroup has the same number of elements as the whole group:

$$
\boxed{G=\langle g\rangle\cong C_p.}
$$

This proves that [a group of prime order is cyclic](../../../../../a-group-of-prime-order-is-cyclic.md). In fact every nonidentity element is a generator. No commutativity assumption was needed; it follows from cyclicity.
