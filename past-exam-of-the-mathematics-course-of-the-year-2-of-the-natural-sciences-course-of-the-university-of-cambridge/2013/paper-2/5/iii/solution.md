<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the identities from part (ii) twice. The second time derivative becomes $\partial_t^2\widetilde u$ and the second spatial derivative becomes $(ik)^2\widetilde u=-k^2\widetilde u$. The [Fourier transform method for the wave equation](../../../../../fourier-transform-method-for-the-wave-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\widetilde u_{tt}+c^2k^2\widetilde u=0.}
$$

For each fixed [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$, this is a [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) equation with angular frequency $|ck|$. At $ck=0$ its solutions are affine in time rather than two independent oscillatory exponentials.
