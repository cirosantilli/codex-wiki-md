<h1 id="6/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [tensor contraction](../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) of a symmetric tensor with an antisymmetric one vanishes. Rename the two summed indices and use their opposite exchange symmetries:

$$
R_{ij}A_{ij}=R_{ji}A_{ji}=R_{ij}(-A_{ij})=-R_{ij}A_{ij}.
$$

Hence $R_{ij}A_{ij}=0$. Using the decomposition from part (iii) gives

$$
\boxed{R_{ij}B_{ij}=R_{ij}(S_{ij}+A_{ij})=R_{ij}S_{ij}.}
$$

This is orthogonality of the real symmetric and antisymmetric matrix subspaces under the [Frobenius inner product](../../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md). It is a summed scalar identity, not an assertion about the unsummed products of individual entries.
