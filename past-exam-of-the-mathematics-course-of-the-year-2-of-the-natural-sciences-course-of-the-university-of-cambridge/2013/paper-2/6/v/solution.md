<h1 id="6/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $B$ be the rank-two tensor and $v$ the vector. The components of their [tensor product](../../../../../tensor-product.md) are $T_{ijk}=B_{ij}v_k$. Applying the transformation rules gives

$$
T'_{ijk}=B'_{ij}v'_k=Q_{ia}Q_{jb}Q_{kc}B_{ab}v_c=Q_{ia}Q_{jb}Q_{kc}T_{abc}.
$$

The product therefore obeys the three-index [tensor component transformation law](../../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md), proving that **$B_{ij}v_k$ is a rank-three tensor**. Tensor-product order adds because no pair of indices is summed out.
