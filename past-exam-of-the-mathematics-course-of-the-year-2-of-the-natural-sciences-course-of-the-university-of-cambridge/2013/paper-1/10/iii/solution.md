<h1 id="10/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle R_m,R_n\rangle_r=\int_0^ar\overline{R_m(r)}R_n(r)\,dr.}
$$

Multiply the equation for $R_n$ by $\overline R_m$, subtract the complex conjugate equation for $R_m$ times $R_n$, and integrate. Since the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real,

$$
(\lambda_n-\lambda_m)\langle R_m,R_n\rangle_r=\left[r\left(\overline{R_m'}R_n-\overline R_mR_n'\right)\right]_0^a=0.
$$

The boundary term vanishes as before. Thus **eigenfunctions with distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal**. The full disk [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) differs only by the angular factor $2\pi$.
