<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Summing the [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{1-x^3}.}
$$

There is a particularly short verification using the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md):

$$
(1-x^3)y''-6x^2y'-6xy=\bigl[(1-x^3)y\bigr]''=1''=0.
$$

Also $y(0)=1$ and $y'(0)=0$. The [radius of convergence](../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) of the series is one, set by the three complex cube roots of unity; the closed expression continues the solution beyond that disk wherever its denominator is nonzero. The real initial-value solution extends on the interval $(-\infty,1)$.
