<h1 id="8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Two integrations give the general [reduction of order](../../../../../reduction-of-order.md) formula

$$
\boxed{v(x)=C_1+C_2\int^x\frac{dt}{(1-t^2)P_n(t)^2},\qquad y(x)=P_n(x)\left[C_1+C_2\int^x\frac{dt}{(1-t^2)P_n(t)^2}\right].}
$$

The integral is local on intervals avoiding zeros of $P_n$. A pole of the integral at an interior polynomial zero need not be a singularity of $y$: the product extends by [reduction of order across a zero of the known solution](../../../../../reduction-of-order-across-a-zero-of-the-known-solution.md). After a standard normalization the independent second solution is the [Legendre function of the second kind](../../../../../legendre-function-of-the-second-kind.md) $Q_n$.
