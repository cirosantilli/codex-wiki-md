<h1 id="8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Substitute $y=vP_n$ into the [Legendre differential equation](../../../../../legendre-differential-equation.md) and expand using the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md). The terms proportional to $v$ cancel because $P_n$ is itself a solution. What remains is

$$
\boxed{(1-x^2)P_nv''+\left[2(1-x^2)P_n'-2xP_n\right]v'=0.}
$$

Multiplying by $P_n$ identifies an exact [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dx}\left[(1-x^2)P_n^2v'\right]=0.}
$$

The latter form is convenient for [reduction of order](../../../../../reduction-of-order.md) on intervals where $P_n\ne0$ and $x\ne\pm1$.
