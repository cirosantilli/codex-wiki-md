<h1 id="8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $y=\sum_{j\geq0}a_jx^j$ and equate coefficients in the [power series](../../../../../power-series.md). The constant coefficient gives $a_2=0$. For $m\geq1$, the coefficient of $x^m$ gives

$$
(m+2)(m+1)a_{m+2}-[(m-1)(m-2)+6(m-1)+6]a_{m-1}=0.
$$

The square bracket is $(m+1)(m+2)$, so the [recurrence relation](../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) is $a_{j+3}=a_j$ for $j\geq0$. The [initial conditions](../../../../../initial-condition.md) give $a_0=1$, $a_1=0$, $a_2=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=1+x^3+x^6+x^9+\cdots,\qquad |x|<1.}
$$
