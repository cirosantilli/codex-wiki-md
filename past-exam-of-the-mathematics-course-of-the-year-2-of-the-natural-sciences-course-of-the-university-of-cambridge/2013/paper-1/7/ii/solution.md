<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) and use equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md):

$$
\boxed{\Delta u=u_{xx}+u_{yy}=(v_y)_x-(v_x)_y=0.}
$$

Thus the real part is a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md). The same calculation gives $\Delta v=0$. Every [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) has the required smoothness.
