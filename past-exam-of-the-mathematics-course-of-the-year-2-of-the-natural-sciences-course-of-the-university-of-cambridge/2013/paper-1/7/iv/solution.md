<h1 id="7/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

On the specified [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md), the semiaxes are $\sqrt2$ and $1$, so its elliptic coordinate is $u_0=\operatorname{arcosh}\sqrt2=\log(\sqrt2+1)$. Thus in the exterior,

$$
\boxed{\phi=\operatorname{Re}\log\left(z+\sqrt{z^2-1}\right)-\log(\sqrt2+1)}
$$

is a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) with a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on that ellipse. Choose the square root with $\sqrt{z^2-1}\sim z$ at infinity. Although a global complex logarithm requires a cut, its real part $\log|z+\sqrt{z^2-1}|$ is well defined throughout the exterior and gives the same $\phi$.

Expanding at large $|z|$ yields $z+\sqrt{z^2-1}=2z[1-1/(4z^2)+O(|z|^{-4})]$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi=\log\frac{2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}{\sqrt2+1}+O\bigl((x^2+y^2)^{-1}\bigr).}
$$

In particular the potential grows logarithmically in the distance, in every direction at infinity. It is the exterior solution; no [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) continuation through the focal segment in the ellipse's interior is claimed.
