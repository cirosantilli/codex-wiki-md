<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md), the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) are $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=u_xv_x+u_yv_y=0,\qquad |\nabla u|^2=u_x^2+u_y^2=v_x^2+v_y^2=|\nabla v|^2.}
$$

A [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is normal to its [level set](../../../../../level-set.md), so these identities give orthogonal level curves wherever the gradients are nonzero. At a critical point where $f'=0$, both gradients vanish and a regular tangent to a [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) need not exist; the geometric orthogonality statement is restricted to regular intersections.
