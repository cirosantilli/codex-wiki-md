<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Taking the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of $MM^{-1}=I$ and $M^{-1}M=I$ reverses the product order and gives

$$
(M^{-1})^\dagger M^\dagger=I,\qquad M^\dagger(M^{-1})^\dagger=I.
$$

Thus $(M^{-1})^\dagger$ is the two-sided [matrix inverse](../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) of $M^\dagger$, proving

$$
\boxed{(M^\dagger)^{-1}=(M^{-1})^\dagger.}
$$
