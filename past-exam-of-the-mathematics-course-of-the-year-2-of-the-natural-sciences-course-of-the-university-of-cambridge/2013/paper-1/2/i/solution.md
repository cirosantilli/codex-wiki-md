↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md), take $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$ in the [damped wave equation](../../../../../damped-wave-equation.md). Dividing by $c^2XT$, with $c>0$, gives $X''/X=(T''+\alpha T')/(c^2T)=-k^2$. The separated [ordinary differential equations](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{X''+k^2X=0,\qquad T''+\alpha T'+c^2k^2T=0.}
$$

The negative separation constant anticipates the positive spatial [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) selected by the fixed endpoints.
