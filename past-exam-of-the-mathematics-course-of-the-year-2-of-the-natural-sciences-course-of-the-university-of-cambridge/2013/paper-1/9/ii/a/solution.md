<h1 id="9/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

When $F_y=0$, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) reduces immediately to $dF_{y'}/dx=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{F_{y'}=C.}
$$

This is conservation of the generalized momentum associated with a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md).
