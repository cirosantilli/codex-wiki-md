<h1 id="9/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Along a differentiable curve, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dx}(F-y'F_{y'})=F_x+y'F_y-y'\frac{dF_{y'}}{dx}=F_x
$$

by the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). Thus when $F_x=0$ the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../../beltrami-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{F-y'F_{y'}=C.}
$$
