↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Here $F$ has no explicit $x$ dependence, so use the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../beltrami-identity.md). With $p=y'$, one has $F_{y'}=yp/\sqrt{1+p^2}$ and therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{y}{\sqrt{1+(y')^2}}-\lambda y=C.}
$$

This [first integral](../../../../../first-integral.md) reduces the second-order [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) to a first-order relation.
