<h1 id="6/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Collect the paired [orthonormal bases](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) from part (iv), followed by an orthonormal basis of $\ker M^2=\ker M$. The matrix $Q$ of these columns is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), and

$$
\boxed{Q^TMQ=\bigoplus_{j=1}^r\begin{pmatrix}0&-\mu_j\\\mu_j&0\end{pmatrix}\ \oplus\ 0_{n-2r},\qquad \mu_j>0.}
$$

Indeed, each pair obeys $Me_j=\mu_jf_j$, $Mf_j=-\mu_je_j$, and all kernel vectors are annihilated. This proves the real block form of a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) directly, including repeated block parameters. In particular its [rank](../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) is $2r$, always even.
