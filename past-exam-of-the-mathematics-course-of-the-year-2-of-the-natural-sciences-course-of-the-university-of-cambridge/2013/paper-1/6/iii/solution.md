<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose $e_1$ real, as permitted by the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md). Matrix powers commute, so

$$
M^2(Me_1)=M(M^2e_1)=-\mu_1^2Me_1.
$$

The proposed partner $e_2=Me_1$ is nonzero because

$$
\|e_2\|^2=-e_1^TM^2e_1=\mu_1^2\|e_1\|^2>0.
$$

Also the real scalar $e_1^TMe_1$ equals its own transpose $e_1^TM^Te_1=-e_1^TMe_1$, so it vanishes. Therefore **$e_1$ and $e_2$ are orthogonal eigenvectors with the same eigenvalue**. This is the basic pairing in the [paired eigenspaces of the square of a real skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../paired-eigenspaces-of-the-square-of-a-real-skew-symmetric-matrix.md).
