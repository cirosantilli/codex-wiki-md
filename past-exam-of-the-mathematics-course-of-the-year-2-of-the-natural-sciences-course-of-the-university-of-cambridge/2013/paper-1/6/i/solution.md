<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md), $M^T=-M$. Invariance of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) under [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\det M=\det M^T=\det(-M)=(-1)^n\det M.}
$$

For odd $n$, $2\det M=0$, so **$\det M=0$** over the real or complex numbers.
