<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [finite group](../../../../../finite-group.md) over the complex numbers, the sum of the squared dimensions of its inequivalent [irreducible representations](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) equals the [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md). There is always a one-dimensional [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md), and part (b) supplies an [irreducible representation](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of dimension three. Thus the remaining squared dimensions sum to

$$
12-1^2-3^2=2.
$$

They must be two further dimensions equal to one. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{the irreducible dimensions are }1,1,1,3.}
$$

The number of inequivalent [irreducible representations](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) equals the number of [conjugacy classes](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md), so $A_4$ has four classes. There are only three [cycle types](../../../../../cycle-type.md) in part (a); consequently [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md) cannot classify conjugacy within $A_4$.

More explicitly, the identity is a singleton class, and [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) by three-cycles permutes the three double [transpositions](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) transitively. For a three-cycle, the [centralizer](../../../../../centralizer.md) in $A_4$ consists of its three powers, so its [conjugacy class](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) has $12/3=4$ elements. The eight three-cycles therefore split into two classes of four:

$$
\boxed{\text{class sizes }1,3,4,4;\quad\text{the three-cycle type splits into two classes}.}
$$

For example, $(123)$ and $(132)$ have the same [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md) but are not conjugate in $A_4$. Any [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) conjugating the first to its inverse must fix its unique fixed vertex and reverse the orientation of the remaining three labels, so it is odd. They are conjugate in $S_4$, illustrating exactly why restricting the ambient [group](../../../../../group-split.md) changes the answer.
