<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A rigid [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) of a regular [tetrahedron](../../../../../tetrahedron.md) permutes its four vertices, giving an injective [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from its [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) [group](../../../../../group-split.md) to $S_4$: fixing every vertex [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) the [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) to be the identity. Its seven nontrivial [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) axes are four lines through a vertex and the opposite face centre, and three lines through midpoints of opposite edges.

Each vertex-face axis admits [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) by $\pm2\pi/3$, giving eight three-cycles on the vertices. Each opposite-edge axis admits a half-turn, giving three double [transpositions](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md). Along with the identity there are twelve [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). All their induced [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md) are even, so the injective image is a twelve-element [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of $A_4$, hence the whole [group](../../../../../group-split.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{the tetrahedron rotation group is isomorphic to }A_4.}
$$

Its action on $\mathbb R^3$ supplies a three-dimensional [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md). To prove irreducibility even after complexification, choose vertices

$$
(1,1,1),\quad(1,-1,-1),\quad(-1,1,-1),\quad(-1,-1,1).
$$

The [group](../../../../../group-split.md) contains the half-turns $D_x=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)$ and their cyclic variants, as well as the coordinate cycle $(x,y,z)\mapsto(y,z,x)$. If a nonzero [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) over the complex numbers contains a [vector](../../../../../vector.md) with nonzero $x$ component, applying $(I+D_x)/2$ shows it contains the $x$ axis. If another component is nonzero, use the corresponding projection instead. The coordinate cycle then supplies the other two axes. Every nonzero [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) is therefore the full space, proving

$$
\boxed{A_4\text{ has an irreducible representation of dimension }3.}
$$

This also gives an elementary proof without presupposing the [character table](../../../../../character-table.md).
