<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [sign homomorphism](../../../../../sign-homomorphism.md) $\operatorname{sgn}:S_n\to\{+1,-1\}$ records [parity of a permutation](../../../../../parity-of-a-permutation.md). The [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) in this case is the [alternating group](../../../../../alternating-group.md) $A_n$, consisting of even [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md). For $n\geq2$ it has index two in the [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) and order $n!/2$.

The [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md) is the multiset of lengths of the disjoint cycles, including fixed points if desired. For $A_4$ the complete count is

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{cycle type}&\text{kind}&\text{number}\\\hline
1^4&\text{identity}&1\\
(3,1)&\text{three-cycle}&\binom43\,2=8\\
(2,2)&\text{two disjoint transpositions}&3
\end{array}
$$

A single [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) and a four-cycle are odd and are excluded. Thus

$$
\boxed{|A_4|=12,\quad\text{with cycle types }1^4,\ (3,1),\ (2,2).}
$$
