<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For real $f$, [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) commutes with its [absolutely convergent](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) Fourier [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md):

$$
[\widetilde f(-\omega)]^*
=\left[\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(t)e^{i\omega t}\,dt\right]^*
=\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{[\widetilde f(-\omega)]^*=\widetilde f(\omega).}
$$

This [Hermitian symmetry](../../../../../../hermitian-symmetry.md) pairs the positive- and negative-frequency components of a real signal; its real part is even and its imaginary part is odd.
