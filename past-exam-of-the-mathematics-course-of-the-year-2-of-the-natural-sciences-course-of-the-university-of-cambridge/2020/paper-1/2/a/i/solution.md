<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) term by term inside its [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md), and reindex the second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) so that every coefficient multiplies $x^m$. The [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) becomes

$$
\sum_{m=0}^\infty\left[(m+2)(m+1)a_{m+2}+
\{n(n+1)-m(m+1)\}a_m\right]x^m=0.
$$

The uniqueness of power-series coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{a_{m+2}=\frac{m(m+1)-n(n+1)}{(m+1)(m+2)}a_m
=\frac{(m-n)(m+n+1)}{(m+1)(m+2)}a_m,\quad m\geq0.}
$$

The even coefficients depend only on $a_0$ and the odd coefficients only on $a_1$. These are the two local solution branches of the [Legendre differential equation](../../../../../../legendre-differential-equation.md) around its [ordinary point](../../../../../../ordinary-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) $x=0$.
