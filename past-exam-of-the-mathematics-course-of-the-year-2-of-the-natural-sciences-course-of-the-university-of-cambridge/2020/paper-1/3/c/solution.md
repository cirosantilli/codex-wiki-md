<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $A_1$, the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $\cos^2\alpha_1-(i\sin\alpha_1)^2=1$. For $A_2$ it is $\cos^2\alpha_2+\sin^2\alpha_2=1$, and for $A_3$ it is $e^{i\alpha_3}e^{-i\alpha_3}=1$. Multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\det A_1=\det A_2=\det A_3=1,\qquad\det(A_1A_2A_3)=1.}
$$

Together with [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md), this places the [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) and their product in [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md).
