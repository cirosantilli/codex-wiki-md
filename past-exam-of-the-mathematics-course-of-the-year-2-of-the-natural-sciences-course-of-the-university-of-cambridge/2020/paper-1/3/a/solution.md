<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
\tau_1^2=\tau_2^2=\tau_3^2=I_2.
$$

For example, the diagonal entries of $\tau_2^2$ are $(-i)i=i(-i)=1$, and its off-diagonal entries vanish. Associativity now reduces every positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) power to its [integer parity](../../../../../parity-mathematics.md):

$$
\boxed{\tau_j^{2m}=I_2,\qquad\tau_j^{2m+1}=\tau_j.}
$$

These are the standard [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md), each a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and an [involution](../../../../../involution.md).
