<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Writing the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) explicitly gives

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}ix_3&ix_1+x_2\\ix_1-x_2&-ix_3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is

$$
\det M=x_3^2-(ix_1+x_2)(ix_1-x_2)
=\boxed{x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2.}
$$

Since $\det A_1^\dagger=(\det A_1)^*=1$,

$$
\boxed{\det(A_1^\dagger MA_1)=\det M=|\mathbf x|^2.}
$$

The [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) therefore preserves the squared Euclidean length encoded in $M$.
