<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The radial [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) in the [spherical coordinate system](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) is

$$
\mathbf e_r=\frac{\mathbf r}{r}=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta).
$$

The coordinate tangent in the polar direction has length $r$, so its corresponding [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) is

$$
\mathbf e_\theta=\frac1r\frac{\partial\mathbf r}{\partial\theta}
=(\cos\theta\cos\phi,\cos\theta\sin\phi,-\sin\theta).
$$

Differentiating $\mathbf e_r$ at fixed $\phi$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial\mathbf e_r}{\partial\theta}=\mathbf e_\theta,\qquad\alpha=1.}
$$

The factor is not $r$: the corresponding [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](../../../../../scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) occur in the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the position [vector](../../../../../vector.md), whereas the question differentiates its unit direction. The formula is understood away from the [coordinate singularities](../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) where a complete spherical [basis](../../../../../basis.md) is not uniquely specified.
