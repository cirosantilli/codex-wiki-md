<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the position [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) as $\mathbf r(\rho,\phi,z)=(\rho\cos\phi,\rho\sin\phi,z)$. Its coordinate tangent [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are

$$
\mathbf r_\rho=(\cos\phi,\sin\phi,0),\qquad
\mathbf r_\phi=(-\rho\sin\phi,\rho\cos\phi,0),\qquad
\mathbf r_z=(0,0,1).
$$

Their pairwise [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) vanish. Their lengths, the [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](../../../../../../scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md), are

$$
\boxed{h_\rho=1,\qquad h_\phi=\rho,\qquad h_z=1.}
$$

Thus the [cylindrical coordinate system](../../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) is orthogonal wherever it is a nonsingular coordinate chart. At $\rho=0$, the angular tangent [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) vanishes and the azimuth is undefined; this is the usual [coordinate singularity](../../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) on the axis, not a failure of the [Euclidean geometry](../../../../../../euclidean-geometry-split.md).
