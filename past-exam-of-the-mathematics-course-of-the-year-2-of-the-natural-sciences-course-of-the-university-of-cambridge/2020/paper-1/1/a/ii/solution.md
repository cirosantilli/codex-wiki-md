<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Dividing the coordinate tangents by their [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](../../../../../../scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) gives an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md)

$$
\mathbf e_\rho=(\cos\phi,\sin\phi,0),\quad
\mathbf e_\phi=(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi,0),\quad
\mathbf e_z=(0,0,1).
$$

Evaluate the Cartesian [cross products](../../../../../../cross-product.md) componentwise:

$$
\mathbf e_\rho\times\mathbf e_\phi=(0,0,\cos^2\phi+\sin^2\phi)=\mathbf e_z,
$$



$$
\mathbf e_\phi\times\mathbf e_z=(\cos\phi,\sin\phi,0)=\mathbf e_\rho,
\qquad
\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf e_\rho=(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi,0)=\mathbf e_\phi.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf e_\rho\times\mathbf e_\phi=\mathbf e_z,\quad
\mathbf e_\phi\times\mathbf e_z=\mathbf e_\rho,\quad
\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf e_\rho=\mathbf e_\phi.}
$$

The ordered cylindrical [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) is right-handed: its [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) $\mathbf e_\rho\cdot(\mathbf e_\phi\times\mathbf e_z)$ equals $+1$.
