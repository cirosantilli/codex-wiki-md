<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $f$ is analytic on and inside a positively oriented simple closed contour except for finitely many isolated [poles](../../../../../pole.md) $z_j$ strictly inside it, then the [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oint_Cf(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_{z_j\text{ inside }C}\operatorname{Res}_{z=z_j}f.}
$$

There must be no singularity on the contour. More generally each [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is multiplied by the contour's [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md) about its point.
