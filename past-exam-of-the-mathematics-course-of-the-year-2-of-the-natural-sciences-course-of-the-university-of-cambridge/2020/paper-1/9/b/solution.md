<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a breathing [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) take $\mathbf u_i=q(t)\mathbf r_i$. Every [spring](../../../../../spring.md) has equilibrium length $\sqrt2$ in the given length units, so its linear extension is $\sqrt2q$. At the vertex $\mathbf r_i$, the four adjacent equilibrium vertices sum to zero. Therefore the [spring](../../../../../spring.md) [force](../../../../../force-split.md) there is

$$
\mathbf F_i=-kq\sum_{j\sim i}(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j)=-4kq\mathbf r_i.
$$

It remains radial and proportional to the original [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) at every vertex, proving that this symmetric [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md). [Newton's second law](../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
m\ddot q=-4kq,\qquad
\boxed{\omega_b=2\sqrt{\frac km},\qquad f_b=\frac1\pi\sqrt{\frac km}.}
$$

The first is [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) and the second is cycles per unit time. For $k$ in $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$ and $m$ in $\mathrm{kg}$, these have units $\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{Hz}$ respectively. An [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) check gives $T=3m\dot q^2$ and $V=12kq^2$, leading to the same ratio $\omega_b^2=24k/(6m)$.
