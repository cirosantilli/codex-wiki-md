<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the same right-handed active [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) convention as $R_z$, the $y$ direction is fixed, $\mathbf e_z$ rotates towards $\mathbf e_x$, and $\mathbf e_x$ towards $-\mathbf e_z$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R_y(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&0&\sin\theta\\0&1&0\\-\sin\theta&0&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its columns give the images of the three Cartesian unit vectors, which also makes the signs of this [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) explicit.
