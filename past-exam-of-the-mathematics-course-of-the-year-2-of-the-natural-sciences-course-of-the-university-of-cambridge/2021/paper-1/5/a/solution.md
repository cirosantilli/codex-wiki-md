<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The columns of $R_z$ are an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), since $\cos^2\phi+\sin^2\phi=1$ and the first two columns have zero [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md). Thus $R_z^TR_z=I$: it is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md). Consequently

$$
|\mathbf v'|^2=(R_z\mathbf v)^T(R_z\mathbf v)=\mathbf v^TR_z^TR_z\mathbf v=\mathbf v^T\mathbf v,
$$

and hence $\boxed{|\mathbf v'|=|\mathbf v|}$. This is the preservation of the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) by a [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md).
