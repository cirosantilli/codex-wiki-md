<h1 id="5/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A product of [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) is orthogonal. In fact,

$$
R_1^TR_1=R_y^TR_z^TR_zR_y=R_y^TR_y=I.
$$

Thus $\boxed{R_1\text{ is orthogonal for every }\theta,\phi.}$ Its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is also one, since each factor has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one, so it remains a proper [rotation matrix](../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md).
