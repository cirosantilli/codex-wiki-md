<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Both factors and their product are real, so the [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) equals the [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md). Part (i) therefore gives

$$
R_1^\dagger R_1=R_1^TR_1=I.
$$

Hence $\boxed{R_1\text{ is unitary for every }\theta,\phi.}$ A real [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) is always a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md).
