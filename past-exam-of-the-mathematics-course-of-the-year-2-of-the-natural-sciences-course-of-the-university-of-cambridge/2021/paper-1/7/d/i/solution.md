<h1 id="7/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $z_0\ne0$, factor out $z_0$ and use the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md):

$$
\frac1{z_0+z}=\frac1{z_0}\frac1{1+z/z_0}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n}{z_0^{n+1}}z^n,\qquad |z|<|z_0|.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{a_n=\frac{(-1)^n}{z_0^{n+1}},\qquad R=|z_0|.}
$$

The [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) is the distance to the nearest singularity, the [simple pole](../../../../../../simple-pole.md) at $z=-z_0$. The case $z_0=0$ must be treated separately: the function is $1/z$, has no [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) about zero and is already its own [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) on $0<|z|<\infty$.
