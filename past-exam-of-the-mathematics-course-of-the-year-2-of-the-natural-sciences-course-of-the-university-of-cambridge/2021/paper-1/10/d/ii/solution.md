<h1 id="10/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The added sine vanishes at zero. Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) at one is $B\cos B$, so nontrivial admissible choices are

$$
\boxed{B=\left(m+\frac12\right)\pi,\qquad m=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Negative values give the same trial directions after changing the sign of $C$; $B=0$ adds nothing. For each choice, minimize the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) of $xe^{-x}+C\sin(Bx)$ over $C$, or optimize over several coefficients simultaneously. The original trial is included, so the best bound is no larger than $5/4$. Enlarging the trial subspace successively is the [Rayleigh-Ritz method](../../../../../../rayleigh-ritz-method.md); the boundary-compatible half-integer sines provide additional independent shapes.
