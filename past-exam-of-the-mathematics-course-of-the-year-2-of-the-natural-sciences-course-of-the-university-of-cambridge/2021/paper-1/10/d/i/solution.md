<h1 id="10/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the added trial term $q_n=x^ne^{-Ax}$, the left [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) is automatic for a positive integer $n$. At the right endpoint,

$$
q_n'(1)=e^{-A}(n-A).
$$

To preserve $y'(1)=0$ for an arbitrary variational coefficient $C$, choose $\boxed{A=n}$. Then minimize the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) of $xe^{-x}+Cx^ne^{-nx}$ over $C$.

Since $C=0$ remains available, this optimization cannot worsen the original upper bound. For $n=1$, the added function is a multiple of the first trial, so the quotient does not change: an independent choice $n\geq2$ is needed for extra freedom. More generally, several such functions form the [exponential-polynomial trial space for a mixed Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../../exponential-polynomial-trial-space-for-a-mixed-sturm-liouville-problem.md), and the [Rayleigh-Ritz method](../../../../../../rayleigh-ritz-method.md) reduces minimization to a small [generalized eigenvalue problem](../../../../../../generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md).
