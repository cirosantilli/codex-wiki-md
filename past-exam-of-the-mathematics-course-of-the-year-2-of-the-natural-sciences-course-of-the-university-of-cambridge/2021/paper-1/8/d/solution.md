<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the larger [indicial root](../../../../../indicial-root.md) $\sigma=3/2$, the recurrence in part (b) becomes

$$
2k[(2k+2)a_k+2ka_{k-1}]=0,\qquad k\geq1.
$$

No factor being cancelled is zero in this range, so

$$
\boxed{a_k=-\frac{k}{k+1}a_{k-1}=\frac{(-1)^k}{k+1}a_0.}
$$

Thus the resulting [Frobenius solution](../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=a_0x^{3/2}\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{(-x)^k}{k+1}=a_0x^{3/2}\left(1-\frac x2+\frac{x^2}3-\cdots\right).}
$$

There is exactly **one free parameter**, $a_0$, setting the overall scale. The analytic series factor has [radius of convergence](../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) one. For a real-valued expression take $x>0$; a complex version requires a local square-root branch.
