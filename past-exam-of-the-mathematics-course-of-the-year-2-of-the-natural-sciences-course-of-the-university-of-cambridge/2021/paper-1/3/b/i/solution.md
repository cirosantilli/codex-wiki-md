<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose the piecewise smooth [Green function](../../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) has jump $[G]_\xi=G(\xi^+;\xi)-G(\xi^-;\xi)$. Its first distributional [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) then contains $[G]_\xi\delta(x-\xi)$, and its second contains $[G]_\xi\delta'(x-\xi)$. In the normalized equation from part (a), the first-derivative term can supply a delta but no delta [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md). The right side has only a delta. Thus its delta-derivative coefficient must vanish:

$$
\boxed{[G]_\xi=0.}
$$

Equivalently, the two homogeneous branches must join continuously. This is the continuity part of the [jump condition](../../../../../../jump-condition.md) for a second-order [Green function](../../../../../../green-s-function-split.md).
