<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $V\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a bounded region with piecewise smooth closed boundary $\partial V$, let $\mathbf n$ be its outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md), and let $\mathbf A$ be a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) on a neighbourhood of its closure. The [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf A\,dV=\int_{\partial V}\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.}
$$

Here $dV$ is the positive volume measure, $dS$ is the surface area measure, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=\partial_xA_x+\partial_yA_y+\partial_zA_z$, and the right side is the total outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md). All boundary components must be included, with their normals pointing away from $V$.
