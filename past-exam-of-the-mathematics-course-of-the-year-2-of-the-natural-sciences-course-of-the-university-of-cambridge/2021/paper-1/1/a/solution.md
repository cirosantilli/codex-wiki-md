<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), write $\partial_i=\partial/\partial x_i$ and use the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). The [contraction of two Levi-Civita symbols](../../../../../contraction-of-two-levi-civita-symbols.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)]_i
&=\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{klm}\partial_j\partial_l A_m\\
&=(\delta_{il}\delta_{jm}-\delta_{im}\delta_{jl})\partial_j\partial_l A_m\\
&=\partial_i\partial_jA_j-\partial_j\partial_jA_i.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) selects the indicated indices, and continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) allow their order to be interchanged. Identifying the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md), [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) and componentwise [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md) proves the [curl of the curl identity](../../../../../curl-of-the-curl-identity.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A)-\nabla^2\mathbf A.}
$$
