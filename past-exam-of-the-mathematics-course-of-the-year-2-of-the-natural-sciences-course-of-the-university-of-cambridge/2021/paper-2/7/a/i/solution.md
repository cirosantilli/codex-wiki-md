<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The four spring extensions are $x_1$, $x_2-x_1$, $x_3-x_2$ and $-x_3$. [Hooke's law](../../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) therefore gives [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) $k[x_1^2+(x_2-x_1)^2+(x_3-x_2)^2+x_3^2]/2$, while the [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $m\sum_i\dot x_i^2/2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{L=\frac m2\sum_{i=1}^3\dot x_i^2-\frac k2[x_1^2+(x_2-x_1)^2+(x_3-x_2)^2+x_3^2],}
$$

with the matrices in the requested [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md)

$$
\boxed{T=mI_3,\qquad V=k\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The equilibrium offsets disappear because the expansion is in displacements from static equilibrium.
