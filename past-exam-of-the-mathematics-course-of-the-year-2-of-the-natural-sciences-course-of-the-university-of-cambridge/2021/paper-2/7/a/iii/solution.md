<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using the three real [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) and positive frequencies in part (ii), the general motion is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x(t)=\sum_{j=1}^3\mathbf u_j[A_j\cos(\omega_jt)+B_j\sin(\omega_jt)].}
$$

There are **six independent real constants**, $A_1,A_2,A_3,B_1,B_2,B_3$, corresponding to the three initial displacements and three initial velocities. With the displayed orthonormal [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), $A_j=\mathbf u_j^T\mathbf x(0)$ and $B_j=\mathbf u_j^T\dot{\mathbf x}(0)/\omega_j$. This spans all solutions because the normal-mode [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) form a basis.
