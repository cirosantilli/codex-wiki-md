<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $n=p\geq3$ be prime. Any [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) contained in $\langle R\rangle\cong C_p$ is either $\{I\}$ or the whole rotation [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md). Both are normal: the trivial [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) always is, and the rotation [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) has index two, or equivalently is preserved by $mRm^{-1}=R^{-1}$.

If a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) contains any reflection, it contains its entire [conjugacy class](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md). Part (b) shows that this is every reflection for odd $p$. Products of reflections include a generator of the rotations, since $(Rm)m=R$. Such a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) must therefore be the whole dihedral group.

Thus the complete list of proper [normal subgroups](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), including the trivial [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), is

$$
\boxed{\{I\}\quad\text{and}\quad\langle R\rangle=\{I,R,\ldots,R^{p-1}\}.}
$$

This also agrees with [classification of subgroups of a dihedral group](../../../../../classification-of-subgroups-of-a-dihedral-group.md): the reflection [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) of order two are proper but are not normal when $p$ is odd.
