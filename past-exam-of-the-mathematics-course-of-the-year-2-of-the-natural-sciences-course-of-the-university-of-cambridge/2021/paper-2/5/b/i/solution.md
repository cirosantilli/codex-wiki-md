<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Transform only the time variable. Two [integrations by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give $\mathcal F_t[y_{tt}]=(i\omega)^2\widetilde y=-\omega^2\widetilde y$, when the ordinary transform boundary terms vanish; the same derivative rule holds for [tempered distributions](../../../../../../tempered-distribution.md). Spatial derivatives and the fixed spatial delta commute with the transform. Thus

$$
-\omega^2\widetilde y=\widetilde y_{xx}+\widetilde F(\omega)\delta(x-x_0),
$$

or

$$
\boxed{\widetilde y_{xx}=-\omega^2\widetilde y-\widetilde F(\omega)\delta(x-x_0).}
$$

The spatial [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) transform to $\widetilde y(\omega,0)=\widetilde y(\omega,L)=0$.
