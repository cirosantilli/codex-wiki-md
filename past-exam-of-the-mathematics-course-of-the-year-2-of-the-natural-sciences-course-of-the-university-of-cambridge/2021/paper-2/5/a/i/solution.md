<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With the stated [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention, the exponential transform is a shifted [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md):

$$
\boxed{\widetilde f_1(\omega)=2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0).}
$$

Writing the sine as $(e^{i\omega_0t}-e^{-i\omega_0t})/(2i)$ then gives

$$
\boxed{\widetilde f_2(\omega)=\frac\pi i[\delta(\omega-\omega_0)-\delta(\omega+\omega_0)].}
$$

These are [tempered distributions](../../../../../../tempered-distribution.md), since the time-domain functions are not integrable on the full real line. The second formula also correctly vanishes when $\omega_0=0$.
