<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $B(x)$ for the displayed matrix. The hyperbolic addition identities give

$$
B(x)B(y)=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh(x+y)&-\sinh(x+y)\\-\sinh(x+y)&\cosh(x+y)\end{pmatrix}=B(x+y).
$$

Thus [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) is closed in $G$. It is associative because ordinary [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) is associative. The identity is $B(0)=I$, and $B(x)B(-x)=I$ gives the inverse $B(x)^{-1}=B(-x)$. Hence $\boxed{G\text{ is a group}}$.

Moreover, $x\mapsto B(x)$ is an injective homomorphism from the additive real numbers, since $\sinh x$ determines $x$. Therefore $\boxed{G\cong(\mathbb R,+)}$; in particular it is abelian. These matrices are the connected boost [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) relevant to the [component quotient of the real Lorentz group in two dimensions](../../../../../component-quotient-of-the-real-lorentz-group-in-two-dimensions.md).
