<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) generated by $T,P$ is $H=\{I,T,P,-I\}$, a [Klein four-group](../../../../../klein-four-group.md). It is not normal in $G'$. Indeed,

$$
B(x)TB(-x)=B(2x)T=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh2x&\sinh2x\\-\sinh2x&-\cosh2x\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For $x\ne0$ the off-diagonal entries are nonzero, whereas every member of $H$ is diagonal. Thus this conjugate of $T$ is outside $H$, giving

$$
\boxed{\langle T,P\rangle\not\trianglelefteq G'.}
$$

The [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) in part (c) is the continuous boost [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md); choosing one representative from each of its [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) does not make those representatives a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md).
