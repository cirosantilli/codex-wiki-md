<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the specified passive change of [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), each Cartesian [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) index acquires one copy of $L$. Thus a [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) of order $n$ transforms as

$$
\boxed{S'_{a_1\cdots a_n}=L_{a_1b_1}\cdots L_{a_nb_n}S_{b_1\cdots b_n}.}
$$

Repeated old-basis indices are summed from one to three. [Orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the new basis gives $LL^T=I$. Taking [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md) yields $(\det L)^2=1$, hence $\boxed{\det L=+1\text{ or }-1}$. The positive case preserves orientation and the negative case reverses it.
