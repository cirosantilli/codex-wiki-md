<h1 id="9/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The initial and final slopes from part (i) are $(1+\alpha)^{-1/3}$ and $(1+\alpha)^{-1/2}$. The prescribed ratio gives

$$
(1+\alpha)^{-1/2}=\frac12(1+\alpha)^{-1/3}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(1+\alpha)^{1/6}=2.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\alpha=63}.
$$
