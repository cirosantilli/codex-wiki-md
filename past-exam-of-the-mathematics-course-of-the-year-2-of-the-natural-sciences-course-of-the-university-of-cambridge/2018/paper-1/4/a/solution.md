<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using [Fourier inversion](../../../../../fourier-inversion-theorem.md) with the stated convention,

$$
f(-\omega)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\widetilde f(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt.
$$

Hence applying the transform twice gives the [duality property of the Fourier transform](../../../../../duality-property-of-the-fourier-transform.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F[\widetilde f](\omega)=2\pi f(-\omega)}.
$$

The formula holds directly for rapidly decreasing smooth functions, and extends through the usual [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) or [tempered distribution](../../../../../tempered-distribution.md) formulations when absolute convergence is unavailable.
