<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Set $\alpha=1$ in part (d), obtaining $\mathcal F[e^{-|t|}](\omega)=2/(1+\omega^2)$. Apply the [duality property of the Fourier transform](../../../../../duality-property-of-the-fourier-transform.md) from part (a):

$$
2\mathcal F\left[\frac1{1+t^2}\right](\omega)
=2\pi e^{-|\omega|}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\widetilde s(\omega)=\pi e^{-|\omega|}}.
$$
