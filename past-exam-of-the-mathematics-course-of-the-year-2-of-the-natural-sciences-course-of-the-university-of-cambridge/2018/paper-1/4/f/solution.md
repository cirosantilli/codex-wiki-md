<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For $T>0$ the difference of [Heaviside step functions](../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) is an interval indicator, and $v=1/2$ on $(-T,T)$ and zero elsewhere. Its [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) is

$$
\widetilde v(\omega,T)=\frac12\int_{-T}^{T}e^{-i\omega t}\,dt
=\boxed{\frac{\sin(\omega T)}{\omega}},\qquad
\boxed{\widetilde v(0,T)=T}.
$$

The apparent singularity at $\omega=0$ is removable. The formula remains valid for real negative $T$, because the original step-function difference and the oriented integral both reverse sign; for $T=0$ both vanish. This is the [Fourier transform of an interval indicator](../../../../../fourier-transform-of-an-interval-indicator.md) and produces a scaled [sinc function](../../../../../sinc-function.md).
