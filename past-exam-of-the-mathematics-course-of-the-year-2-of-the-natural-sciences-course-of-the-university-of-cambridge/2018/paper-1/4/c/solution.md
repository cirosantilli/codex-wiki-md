<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Heaviside step function](../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) restricts the integral defining the [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) to $t\geq0$, so

$$
\widetilde g(\omega)=\int_0^\infty e^{-(\alpha+i\omega)t}\,dt
=\boxed{\frac1{\alpha+i\omega}},\qquad \operatorname{Re}\alpha>0.
$$

The convergence condition is essential. For real $\alpha$ it means $\alpha>0$, which is the decay assumption used in the subsequent parts. At $\alpha=0$ a [distributional Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-tempered-distribution.md) is needed; for negative real $\alpha$ the defining improper integral diverges.
