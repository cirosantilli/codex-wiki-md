<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For either exponent, the recurrence from part (c) implies

$$
\left|\frac{a_{n+1}x^{n+1}}{a_nx^n}\right|
=\frac{|x|}{|[2(n+1+\sigma)-1](n+\sigma)|}
\longrightarrow0
$$

for every finite complex $x$. The [ratio test](../../../../../ratio-test.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{R=\infty\quad\text{for both power-series factors}}.
$$

The factor multiplying $x^\sigma$ is an [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md) in each case. For $\sigma=1/2$, this does not make the full [Frobenius solution](../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) single-valued at zero: its square-root prefactor still requires a branch. The infinite radius refers to $\sum a_nx^n$, not to a Taylor expansion of the branched full solution.
