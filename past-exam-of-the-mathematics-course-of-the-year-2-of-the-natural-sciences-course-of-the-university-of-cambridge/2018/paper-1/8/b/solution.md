<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [Frobenius method](../../../../../frobenius-method.md). For $y=x^\sigma\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$, the coefficient of the lowest power $x^\sigma$ comes only from $2x^2y''-xy'+y$; the remaining term $xy$ starts one power higher. As $a_0\ne0$, this lowest coefficient gives the [indicial equation](../../../../../indicial-equation.md)

$$
2\sigma(\sigma-1)-\sigma+1=2\sigma^2-3\sigma+1=(2\sigma-1)(\sigma-1)=0.
$$

Therefore the two possible [characteristic exponents at a regular singular point](../../../../../characteristic-exponent-at-a-regular-singular-point.md) are

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\frac12\quad\text{or}\quad\sigma=1}.
$$

Their difference is not an integer, so the two corresponding [Frobenius solutions](../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) do not require a logarithmic correction.
