<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitute $T=e^{-\nu t}R(r)$ into the axisymmetric [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) and cancel the exponential:

$$
-\nu R=\frac1r(r\kappa R')'.
$$

Thus the radial [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and its [Sturm-Liouville form](../../../../../sturm-liouville-form.md) are

$$
\boxed{(r\kappa R')'+\nu rR=0,\qquad -(pR')'=\nu wR,\quad p(r)=r\kappa(r),\quad w(r)=r}.
$$

The weight in this [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) is therefore $\boxed{w=r}$. The outer [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) is $R(1)=0$, and the physical solution is regular at the centre. The factor $r$ in the [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md) is also the radial part of the polar area element.
