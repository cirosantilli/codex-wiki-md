<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [linearly damped string](../../../../../linearly-damped-string.md), substitute $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$ and divide by $XT$:

$$
\frac{X''}{X}=\frac{T''+2\lambda T'}{T}=-\alpha^2.
$$

Thus [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{X''+\alpha^2X=0,\qquad T''+2\lambda T'+\alpha^2T=0}.
$$

The fixed-end [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) require $X(0)=X(L)=0$. Nonzero solutions are $X_n(x)=\sin(n\pi x/L)$, with $\alpha_n=n\pi/L$, $n\geq1$. Neither $\alpha=0$ nor a negative spatial [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) produces a nonzero function obeying both endpoint conditions.
