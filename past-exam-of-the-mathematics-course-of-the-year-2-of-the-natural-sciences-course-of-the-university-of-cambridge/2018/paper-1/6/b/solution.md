<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every row sums to six, so $\mathbf e=(1,1,1)^T$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) six. Since $M$ is a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), its other two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), say $\lambda_2,\lambda_3$, are real. The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\lambda_2+\lambda_3=\operatorname{tr}M-6=0.
$$

Also, for a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), $\operatorname{tr}(M^2)$ is the sum of the squares of all its entries. Here

$$
\operatorname{tr}(M^2)=1^2+3^2+2^2+2(2^2+3^2+1^2)=42.
$$

Thus $\lambda_2^2+\lambda_3^2=42-36=6$. Together with their zero sum, this implies

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{spec}M=\{6,\sqrt3,-\sqrt3\}}.
$$
