<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [divergence of a cross product](../../../../../../divergence-of-a-cross-product.md) identity and the vacuum [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) imply

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf E)
&=\mathbf E\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)-\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf E)\\
&=\frac1{c^2}\mathbf E\cdot\partial_t\mathbf E+\mathbf B\cdot\partial_t\mathbf B\\
&=\partial_t\left(\frac{|\mathbf E|^2}{2c^2}+\frac{|\mathbf B|^2}{2}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

The [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) therefore gives the required balance with

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac12\left(\frac{|\mathbf E|^2}{c^2}+|\mathbf B|^2\right)},\qquad
\boxed{\frac\partial{\partial t}\int_V\lambda\,dV=\oint_A(\mathbf B\times\mathbf E)\cdot d\mathbf A}.
$$

An additive time-independent constant in $\lambda$ is immaterial. This is the vacuum [Poynting theorem](../../../../../../poynting-theorem.md) in the normalization of the given equations: equivalently $\partial_t\lambda+\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E\times\mathbf B)=0$. The order of the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) accounts for the plus sign in the stated integral identity.
