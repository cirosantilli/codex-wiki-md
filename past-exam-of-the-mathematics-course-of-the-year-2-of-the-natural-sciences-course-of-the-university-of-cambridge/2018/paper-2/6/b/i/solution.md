<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [symmetric gradient and curl decomposition](../../../../../../symmetric-gradient-and-curl-decomposition.md) separately to the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md):

$$
\boxed{S^E_{ij}=\frac12(\partial_iE_j+\partial_jE_i),\qquad
S^B_{ij}=\frac12(\partial_iB_j+\partial_jB_i)}.
$$

The corresponding axial parts are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u^E=\frac12\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\frac12\partial_t\mathbf B,
\qquad
\mathbf u^B=\frac12\nabla\times\mathbf B=\frac1{2c^2}\partial_t\mathbf E}.
$$

The second equalities use the vacuum [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md). Their [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) constraints also show that both symmetric [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) [tensors](../../../../../../tensor.md) are traceless.
