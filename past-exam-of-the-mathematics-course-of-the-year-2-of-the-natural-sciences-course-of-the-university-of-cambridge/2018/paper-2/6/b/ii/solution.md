<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate $\mathbf u^E=\frac12\nabla\times\mathbf E$ with respect to time. The vacuum [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) give $\partial_t\mathbf E=c^2\nabla\times\mathbf B$, so

$$
\partial_t\mathbf u^E=\frac{c^2}{2}\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf B).
$$

The [curl of the curl identity](../../../../../../curl-of-the-curl-identity.md) is $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf B)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf B)-\nabla^2\mathbf B$. Since $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial\mathbf u^E}{\partial t}=-\frac{c^2}{2}\nabla^2\mathbf B}.
$$
