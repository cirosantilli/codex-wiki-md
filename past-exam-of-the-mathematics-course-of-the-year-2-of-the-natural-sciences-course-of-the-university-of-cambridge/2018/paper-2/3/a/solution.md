<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the convention $\Delta G=+\delta^{(3)}$. The [Dirichlet Green function](../../../../../dirichlet-green-function.md) for this [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) satisfies, for each source point $\mathbf r'\in V$,

$$
\boxed{\Delta_{\mathbf r}G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\quad\text{in }V,
\qquad G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=0\quad\text{on }S}.
$$

It is a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) away from its source, with a unit delta singularity there. Its [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) is homogeneous even when the original potential has nonzero boundary values; those values enter through the boundary integral. This sign convention is the one giving the plus signs in the formula requested in part (b).
