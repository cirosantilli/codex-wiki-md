<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\Psi=U(r)V(\phi)$ in the [Laplace equation](../../../../../laplace-equation.md) in [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md). After multiplication by $r^2/(UV)$, [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\frac{r^2U''+rU'}U=-\frac{V''}V=n^2.
$$

The angular function is $2\pi$-periodic and single-valued, so its nonconstant modes are $\cos(n\phi),\sin(n\phi)$ with integer $n\geq1$. Their radial [Euler differential equation](../../../../../cauchy-euler-equation.md) has solutions $r^n,r^{-n}$; finiteness at the origin excludes $r^{-n}$.

The zero angular mode has constant angular part and radial solutions $1,\log r$. The logarithmic term is again excluded at the origin. [Superposition](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\Psi(r,\phi)=A_0+\sum_{n=1}^\infty r^n[A_n\cos(n\phi)+B_n\sin(n\phi)]}.
$$

This is the regular harmonic expansion inside the disk.
