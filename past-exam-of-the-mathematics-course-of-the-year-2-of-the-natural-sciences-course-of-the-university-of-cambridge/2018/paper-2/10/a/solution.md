↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $W\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) for the [group representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) $D$ if

$$
\boxed{D(g)W\subseteq W\quad\text{for every }g\in G}.
$$

Because $g^{-1}$ is also in the [group](../../../../../group-split.md) and $D(g)$ is invertible, the inclusion is equivalently $D(g)W=W$ for every $g$.

For $n>0$, the representation is an [irreducible representation](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) when its only invariant [vector subspaces](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) are

$$
\boxed{W=\{0\}\quad\text{and}\quad W=\mathbb C^n}.
$$

Thus no nonzero proper subspace is preserved by all representing [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md).
