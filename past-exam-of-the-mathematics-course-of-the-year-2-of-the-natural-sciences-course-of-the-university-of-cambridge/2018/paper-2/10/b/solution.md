<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every $D_2(g)$ is invertible because both inversion and the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) preserve invertibility. For $g,h\in G$, the [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) property of $D_1$ and reversal of multiplication under the adjoint give

$$
\begin{aligned}
D_2(gh)&=[D_1((gh)^{-1})]^\dagger\\
&=[D_1(h^{-1})D_1(g^{-1})]^\dagger\\
&=[D_1(g^{-1})]^\dagger[D_1(h^{-1})]^\dagger
=D_2(g)D_2(h).
\end{aligned}
$$

Also $D_2(e)=I$. Hence **$D_2$ is a [group representation](../../../../../group-representation.md)**. It is the [inverse-adjoint representation](../../../../../inverse-adjoint-representation.md) of $D_1$; the inverse and adjoint each reverse product order, so together they preserve it.
