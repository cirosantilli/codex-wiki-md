<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The hypothesis implies $g^2=e$ for every $g\in G$, including the identity, so $g^{-1}=g$. For arbitrary $a,b\in G$, their product also equals its own inverse. The inverse-of-a-product rule therefore gives

$$
ab=(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}=ba.
$$

Thus **$G$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../abelian-group.md)**. Finiteness is not needed for this implication; it holds for every [group](../../../../../group-split.md) in which each element squares to the identity.
