<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $n=\operatorname{ord}(a)$, which is finite. A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) preserves powers and the identity, so

$$
f(a)^n=f(a^n)=f(e)=e'.
$$

If $r=\operatorname{ord}(f(a))$, divide $n$ by $r$: $n=qr+s$ with $0\leq s<r$. Then $e'=f(a)^n=f(a)^s$, and minimality of $r$ forces $s=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(f(a))\mid\operatorname{ord}(a),\qquad
\operatorname{ord}(f(a))\leq\operatorname{ord}(a)}.
$$

If $f$ is a [group isomorphism](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md), apply the same argument to its inverse: $\operatorname{ord}(a)\leq\operatorname{ord}(f(a))$. The two inequalities yield

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(f(a))=\operatorname{ord}(a)}.
$$
