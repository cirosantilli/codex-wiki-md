<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two products are conjugate:

$$
a^{-1}(ab)a=ba.
$$

[Conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) by $a^{-1}$ is a [group automorphism](../../../../../group-automorphism.md), so part (a) proves

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(ab)=\operatorname{ord}(ba)}.
$$

Directly, cancellation gives $(ba)^r=a^{-1}(ab)^ra$ for every positive integer $r$. Thus a power of one product is the identity exactly when the same power of the other is the identity. This proves that [conjugation preserves element order](../../../../../conjugation-preserves-element-order.md), even without the finiteness assumption.
