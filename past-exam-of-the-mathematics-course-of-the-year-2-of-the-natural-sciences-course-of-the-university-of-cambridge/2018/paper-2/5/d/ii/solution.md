<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\kappa>\Omega$, put $\eta=\sqrt{\kappa^2-\Omega^2}$, so $0<\eta<\kappa$ and the [poles](../../../../../../pole.md) are $i(\kappa\pm\eta)$. Closing in the upper half-plane for $t>0$ again includes both [simple poles](../../../../../../simple-pole.md). The [residue theorem](../../../../../../residue-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{G(t)=e^{-\kappa t}\frac{\sinh(\eta t)}\eta
=\frac{e^{-(\kappa-\eta)t}-e^{-(\kappa+\eta)t}}{2\eta}}.
$$

This [overdamped](../../../../../../overdamping.md) response has no oscillation. Its slow tail is $e^{-(\kappa-\eta)t}/(2\eta)$, and it tends to zero because $\eta<\kappa$.
