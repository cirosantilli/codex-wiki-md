<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The only denominator zero is $z_0=-1$, which lies strictly inside the given circle. The numerator is an [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md). Apply the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) form of the [Cauchy integral formula](../../../../../cauchy-integral-formula.md) with $n=6$:

$$
\oint_C\frac{\sin z}{(z+1)^7}\,dz
=\frac{2\pi i}{6!}\left.\frac{d^6}{dz^6}\sin z\right|_{z=-1}.
$$

The sixth [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $-\sin z$, so its value at $-1$ is $\sin1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\frac{\sin z}{(z+1)^7}\,dz=\frac{\pi i\sin1}{360}}.
$$
