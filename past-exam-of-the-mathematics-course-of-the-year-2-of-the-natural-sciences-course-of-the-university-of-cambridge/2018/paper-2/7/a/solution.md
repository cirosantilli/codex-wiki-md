<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose [generalized coordinates](../../../../../generalized-coordinate.md) $q_i$ measured from a static [equilibrium point](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), and assume a conservative system with no velocity-dependent forces. Keeping terms of degree two in displacements and velocities gives the [small oscillation](../../../../../small-oscillation.md) [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac12\dot{\mathbf q}^{\,T}A\dot{\mathbf q}-\frac12\mathbf q^TB\mathbf q}.
$$

Here $A$ is the positive-definite symmetric [mass](../../../../../mass.md) [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) evaluated at equilibrium, and $B$ is the symmetric [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) of the [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) there. The linear potential term is zero by equilibrium, and an additive constant has been dropped. Coordinate-dependent corrections to the kinetic [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) contribute only at higher order.

The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are $A\ddot{\mathbf q}+B\mathbf q=0$. For a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) $\mathbf q=\mathbf v e^{i\omega t}$, they give the [generalized eigenvalue problem](../../../../../generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) $(B-\omega^2A)\mathbf v=0$. A nonzero [mode shape](../../../../../mode-shape.md) exists exactly when

$$
\boxed{\det(B-\omega^2A)=0}.
$$

This is a degree-$n$ [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $\omega^2$. At a stable equilibrium its roots are nonnegative; zero roots correspond to [zero modes](../../../../../zero-mode.md).
