<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose positive $\phi$ so the bob is displaced to the right relative to the cart. Its [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) are

$$
X=x+L\sin\phi,\qquad Y=-L\cos\phi.
$$

Differentiation gives $\dot X^2+\dot Y^2=\dot x^2+2L\cos\phi\,\dot x\dot\phi+L^2\dot\phi^2$. Thus the exact [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) are

$$
T=\frac12(m+M)\dot x^2+ML\cos\phi\,\dot x\dot\phi+\frac12ML^2\dot\phi^2,
\qquad V=\frac12kx^2+MgL(1-\cos\phi).
$$

Retain quadratic terms, using $\cos\phi=1+O(\phi^2)$ in the kinetic cross term and $1-\cos\phi=\phi^2/2+O(\phi^4)$ in the potential. The [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac12(m+M)\dot x^2+ML\dot x\dot\phi+\frac12ML^2\dot\phi^2
-\frac12kx^2-\frac12MgL\phi^2}.
$$

Its [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) give

$$
\boxed{(m+M)\ddot x+ML\ddot\phi+kx=0,\qquad
ML\ddot x+ML^2\ddot\phi+MgL\phi=0}.
$$

The kinetic cross term is essential: acceleration of the support couples the pendulum and cart. These equations define the [normal modes of a spring-mounted pendulum cart](../../../../../../normal-modes-of-a-spring-mounted-pendulum-cart.md).
