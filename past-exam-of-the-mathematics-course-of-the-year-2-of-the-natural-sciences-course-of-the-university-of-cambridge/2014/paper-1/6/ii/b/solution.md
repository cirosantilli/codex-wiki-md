<h1 id="6/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\lambda>0$ and $\lambda_3=-\gamma<0$. Then

$$
\frac{x_1'^2+x_2'^2}{k/\lambda}-\frac{x_3'^2}{k/\gamma}=1.
$$

This is a **[one-sheet hyperboloid](../../../../../../one-sheet-hyperboloid.md) of revolution** around the $x_3'$ axis. Its circular waist has radius $\sqrt{k/\lambda}$ at $x_3'=0$, and its radius grows with $|x_3'|$. In the original coordinates the axis is the [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) direction belonging to the negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
