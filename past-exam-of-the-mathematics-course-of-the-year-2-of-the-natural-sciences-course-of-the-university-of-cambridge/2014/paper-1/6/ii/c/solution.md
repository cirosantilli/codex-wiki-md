<h1 id="6/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The coordinate $x_1'$ does not occur. The perpendicular cross-section is

$$
\frac{x_2'^2}{k/\lambda_2}+\frac{x_3'^2}{k/\lambda_3}=1.
$$

Thus the surface is an **[elliptic cylinder](../../../../../../elliptic-cylinder.md)** extending in both directions along the zero-eigenvalue axis. Its cross-sectional semiaxes are $\sqrt{k/\lambda_2}$ and $\sqrt{k/\lambda_3}$. It becomes a circular cylinder when the two positive [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) coincide.
