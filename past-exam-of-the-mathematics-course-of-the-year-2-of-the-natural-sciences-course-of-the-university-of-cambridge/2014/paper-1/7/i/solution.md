<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For real-direction increments, [complex differentiability](../../../../../complex-differentiability.md) gives $f'=u_x+iv_x$. For imaginary-direction increments it gives $f'=(u_y+iv_y)/i=v_y-iu_y$. Equating these expressions gives the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.}
$$

A [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) is smooth, so mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) commute. Differentiating these equations gives

$$
u_{xx}+u_{yy}=v_{yx}-v_{xy}=0,\qquad v_{xx}+v_{yy}=-u_{yx}+u_{xy}=0.
$$

Thus both real and imaginary parts are [harmonic functions](../../../../../harmonic-function.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2u=\nabla^2v=0.}
$$
