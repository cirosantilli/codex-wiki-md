<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Squaring the ratio of moduli gives

$$
(x-a)^2+y^2=\lambda^2[(x+a)^2+y^2].
$$

For $\lambda\neq1$, complete the square to obtain the [Apollonius circle](../../../../../circles-of-apollonius.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(x-\frac{a(1+\lambda^2)}{1-\lambda^2}\right)^2+y^2=\left(\frac{2a\lambda}{|1-\lambda^2|}\right)^2.}
$$

Its centre is $(a(1+\lambda^2)/(1-\lambda^2),0)$ and its radius is $2a\lambda/|1-\lambda^2|$. The centres are positive for $\lambda<1$ and negative for $\lambda>1$.

**At the allowed exceptional value $\lambda=1$, the locus is the line $x=0$**, the [perpendicular bisector](../../../../../perpendicular-bisector.md) of the two fixed points. It is a generalized circle, but not an ordinary finite-radius circle. The point $z=-a$ is excluded from the original ratio.
