<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate under the [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md):

$$
\frac{d^n\widetilde f}{dk^n}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(-ix)^nf(x)e^{-ikx}\,dx=(-i)^n\widetilde g(k).
$$

Hence the [Fourier transform of a polynomial multiple](../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-polynomial-multiple.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde g(k)=i^n\frac{d^n\widetilde f(k)}{dk^n}.}
$$

Absolute integrability of the required moments justifies differentiation under the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). The identity also extends to [tempered distributions](../../../../../tempered-distribution.md).
