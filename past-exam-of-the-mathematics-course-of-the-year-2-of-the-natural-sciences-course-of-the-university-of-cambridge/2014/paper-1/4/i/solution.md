↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With the stated negative sign in the forward exponential, the [inverse Fourier transform](../../../../../inverse-fourier-transform.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\widetilde f(k)e^{ikx}\,dk.}
$$

For example, this holds pointwise for [Schwartz functions](../../../../../schwartz-function.md); more general square-integrable functions use the corresponding mean-square interpretation.
