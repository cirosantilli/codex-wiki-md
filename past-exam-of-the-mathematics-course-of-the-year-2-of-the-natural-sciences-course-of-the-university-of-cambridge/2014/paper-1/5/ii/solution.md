<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The absolutely convergent [matrix exponential](../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) series gives

$$
U^\dagger=(e^{iH})^\dagger=e^{(iH)^\dagger}=e^{-iH}
$$

for Hermitian $H$. The exponents $iH$ and $-iH$ commute, so their exponentials multiply to the exponential of their sum:

$$
\boxed{U^\dagger U=e^{-iH}e^{iH}=I,\qquad UU^\dagger=I.}
$$

Hence $U$ is unitary. Alternatively, an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) of $H$ turns the exponential [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) into $e^{i\lambda_j}$, all on the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md).
