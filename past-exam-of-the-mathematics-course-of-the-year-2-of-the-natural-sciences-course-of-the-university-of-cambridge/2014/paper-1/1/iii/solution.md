<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the full field in the PDF, including the exponential term missing from the transcription. Its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\nabla\cdot F=[z\sin(yz)+3x^2]-z\sin(yz)+3y^2=3(x^2+y^2).
$$

The exponential in the third component has zero $z$ [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md). In [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md), the solid has $0\leq r\leq2$ and $0\leq z\leq4-r^2$. Therefore the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives the outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_S F\cdot\hat n\,dS&=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^2\int_0^{4-r^2}3r^2\,r\,dz\,dr\,d\phi\\
&=6\pi\int_0^2(4r^3-r^5)\,dr=\boxed{32\pi}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) includes the entire closed boundary, both cap and base.
