<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a sufficiently smooth [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $A$ on a volume with piecewise smooth closed boundary and outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md), the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\cdot A\,dV=\int_S A\cdot\hat n\,dS.}
$$

Expanding the [divergence of a cross product](../../../../../divergence-of-a-cross-product.md) by the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) gives $\nabla\cdot(F\times G)=G\cdot(\nabla\times F)-F\cdot(\nabla\times G)$. Apply the theorem to $A=F\times G$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\int_V[G\cdot(\nabla\times F)-F\cdot(\nabla\times G)]\,dV=\int_S(F\times G)\cdot\hat n\,dS.}
$$
