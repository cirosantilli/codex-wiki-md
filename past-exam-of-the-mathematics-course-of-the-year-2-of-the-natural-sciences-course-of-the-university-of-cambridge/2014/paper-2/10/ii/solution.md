<h1 id="10/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $a^4=I$, multiplication adds exponents modulo four: $a^ja^k=a^{j+k\bmod4}$. Hence the [Cayley table](../../../../../cayley-table.md) of the [cyclic group of order four](../../../../../cyclic-group-of-order-four.md), with the left factor indexing rows, is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\cdot&I&a&a^2&a^3\\\hline
I&I&a&a^2&a^3\\
a&a&a^2&a^3&I\\
a^2&a^2&a^3&I&a\\
a^3&a^3&I&a&a^2
\end{array}.}
$$

The table is symmetric because the group is Abelian. Its row for each element permutes all four members, as cancellation requires.
