<h1 id="9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ of a finite [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $G$, [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{|G|=[G:H]|H|.}
$$

Here $[G:H]$ is the number of distinct left [cosets](../../../../../coset.md). Every coset has $|H|$ members, and the cosets partition $G$, proving the identity. In particular **the order of a subgroup divides the order of the group**. Divisibility is generally necessary, not sufficient, for a subgroup of a given order to exist.
