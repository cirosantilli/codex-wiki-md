<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The relation $Rm=mR^{-1}$ implies $mR^j=R^{-j}m$. Move each reflection generator to the right in any word, cancel pairs using $m^2=I$, and reduce exponents modulo $N$. Every element then has a normal form $R^j$ or $R^jm$, $0\leq j<N$.

For the actual [symmetry group](../../../../../symmetry-group.md) of a regular polygon these are $N$ distinct rotations and $N$ distinct reflections. The determinant distinguishes rotations from reflections in the planar action. Hence the source's [dihedral group](../../../../../dihedral-group.md) $D_N$ has order $2N$, with its subscript counting polygon vertices. The calculations below use that convention.
