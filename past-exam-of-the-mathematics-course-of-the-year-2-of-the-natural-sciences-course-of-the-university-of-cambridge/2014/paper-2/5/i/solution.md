<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) of $u_{xx}$ is $-k^2\widetilde u$: two [integrations by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) have no boundary terms under the stated decay assumption. Time differentiation commutes with the spatial transform. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\widetilde u_{tt}+2\gamma\widetilde u_t+(\gamma^2+c^2k^2)\widetilde u=0.}
$$

For every fixed spatial [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$, this is a constant-coefficient [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) in time.
