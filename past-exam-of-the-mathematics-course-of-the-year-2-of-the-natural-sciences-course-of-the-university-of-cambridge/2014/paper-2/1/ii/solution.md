<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The useful coordinate for this scale-dependent differential operator is $t=\ln x$. With $Y(t)=y(e^t)$, the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
xy'=Y_t,\qquad x^2y''=Y_{tt}-Y_t,\qquad Ly=-Y_{tt}-Y.
$$

The interval becomes $0\leq t\leq1$, and the endpoint values become $Y(0)=Y(1)=0$. This [logarithmic change of variable in a Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../logarithmic-change-of-variable-in-a-sturm-liouville-problem.md) reduces the calculation to a constant-coefficient Dirichlet problem. Also $dx/x=dt$, so the transformation preserves the appropriate [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md).
