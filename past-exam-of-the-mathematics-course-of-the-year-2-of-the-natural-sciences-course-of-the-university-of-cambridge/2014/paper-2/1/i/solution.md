<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md), the factor $w$ cancels the $w^{-1}$ in the [Sturm-Liouville operator](../../../../../sturm-liouville-operator.md). Assuming the coefficients are real, two applications of [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle f,Lg\rangle_w-\langle Lf,g\rangle_w
&=\int_a^b[-f^*(pg')'+(pf'^*)'g]dx\\
&=\left[p(f'^*g-f^*g')\right]_a^b.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the boundary form in the [Lagrange identity for a Sturm-Liouville operator](../../../../../lagrange-identity-for-a-sturm-liouville-operator.md). The potential terms cancel; if $q$ were complex, an additional integral of $(q-q^*)f^*g$ would remain, so suitable real coefficients are part of formal self-adjointness. The endpoint restrictions in part (a) eliminate the displayed form for every pair of functions in the domain.
