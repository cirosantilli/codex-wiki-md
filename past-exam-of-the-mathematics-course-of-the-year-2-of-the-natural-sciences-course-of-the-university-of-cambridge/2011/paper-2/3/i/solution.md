<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the grounded wall, use an image charge $-q$ at $(2b,0)$. Define the [Euclidean distances](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ and $r_* =\sqrt{(x-2b)^2+y^2}$. The [method of images](../../../../../method-of-images.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V_D(x,y)=\frac q{2\pi}\log\frac r{r_*},\qquad x<b.}
$$

The real source has the required [Dirac delta function](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) coefficient, and the image is outside the domain. On $x=b$, $r=r_*$, so $V_D=0$ exactly. This verifies the [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) rather than only its derivative.
