↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the insulating wall, the image instead has the same strength $q$. With the distances defined above,

$$
\boxed{V_N(x,y)=\frac q{2\pi}(\log r+\log r_*)+C,\qquad x<b.}
$$

It has the required source in the physical domain. Its normal [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\partial_xV_N=\frac q{2\pi}\left[\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}+\frac{x-2b}{(x-2b)^2+y^2}\right].
$$

At $x=b$ the terms are $b/(b^2+y^2)$ and $-b/(b^2+y^2)$, so they cancel. The [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) leaves the additive constant $C$ free.
