<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) sending the standard [basis](../../../../../basis.md) vectors to the given columns is

$$
\boxed{U=\frac1{\sqrt6}\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt3&-i\sqrt3&0\\1&i&-2\\1+i&-1+i&1+i\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

With the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md), the squared column [norms](../../../../../norm.md) are $3/6+1/6+2/6=1$, $3/6+1/6+2/6=1$, and $4/6+2/6=1$. The off-diagonal products are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf u_1^\dagger\mathbf u_2&=\frac{-3i+i+(1-i)(-1+i)}6=0,\\
\mathbf u_1^\dagger\mathbf u_3&=\frac{-2+(1-i)(1+i)}6=0,\\
\mathbf u_2^\dagger\mathbf u_3&=\frac{2i+(-1-i)(1+i)}6=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the columns form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), so $\boxed{U^\dagger U=I}$ and $U$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../unitary-matrix.md). Here the operation maps the old [basis](../../../../../basis.md) vectors to the new vectors; the inverse coordinate [change of basis](../../../../../change-of-basis.md) uses $U^\dagger$.
