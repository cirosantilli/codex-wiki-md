<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) reverses the order of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md). Since $A$ and $B$ are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md),

$$
(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger=BA.
$$

Consequently $\boxed{AB\text{ is Hermitian}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ AB=BA}$: the condition is exactly that the two [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) [commute](../../../../../../commuting-elements.md).
