<h1 id="5/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For the [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) $C$, $C^{-1}=C^\dagger$. Therefore $D=C^{-1}AC$ is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md):

$$
D^\dagger=(C^\dagger AC)^\dagger=C^\dagger AC=D.
$$

Every positive integer power remains a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), since $(D^p)^\dagger=(D^\dagger)^p=D^p$. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real by the [finite-dimensional spectral theorem](../../../../../../finite-dimensional-spectral-theorem.md). Equivalently, [matrix similarity](../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) gives

$$
D^p=C^{-1}A^pC,
$$

whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\boxed{\lambda_j^p\in\mathbb R}$, where the $\lambda_j$ are the real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$.
