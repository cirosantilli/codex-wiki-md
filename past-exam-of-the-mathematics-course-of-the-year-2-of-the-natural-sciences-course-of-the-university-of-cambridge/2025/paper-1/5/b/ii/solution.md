↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $\boxed{c=1}$. Indeed the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) is

$$
(AB+BA)^\dagger=BA+AB=AB+BA,
$$

so the [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). The original PDF, p. 6, supplies the factor $BA$ that is corrupted in the local OCR.
