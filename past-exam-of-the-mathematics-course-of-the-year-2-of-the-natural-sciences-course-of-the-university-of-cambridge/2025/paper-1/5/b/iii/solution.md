<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) and the [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) assumptions,

$$
\boxed{(ABA)^\dagger=A^\dagger B^\dagger A^\dagger=ABA.}
$$

Thus $ABA$ is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), whether or not $A$ and $B$ [commute](../../../../../../commuting-elements.md).
