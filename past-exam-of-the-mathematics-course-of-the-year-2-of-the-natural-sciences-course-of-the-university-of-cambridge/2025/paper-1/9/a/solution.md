<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [generalized momentum](../../../../../generalized-momentum.md) of this [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
p=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot x}=\frac{m\dot x}{\sqrt{1-\dot x^2}},\qquad\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial x}=-V'(x).
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{m\dot x}{\sqrt{1-\dot x^2}}\right)+V'(x)=0,\qquad \frac{m\ddot x}{(1-\dot x^2)^{3/2}}=-V'(x).}
$$

Because $\mathcal L$ has no explicit time dependence, the energy form of the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../beltrami-identity.md) gives a conserved quantity $E=\dot x\,\partial\mathcal L/\partial\dot x-\mathcal L$. Here

$$
E=\frac{m\dot x^2}{\sqrt{1-\dot x^2}}+m\sqrt{1-\dot x^2}+V(x)=\boxed{\frac{m}{\sqrt{1-\dot x^2}}+V(x)}.
$$

For a direct check of [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md), differentiate:

$$
\frac{dE}{dt}=\dot x\left[\frac{m\ddot x}{(1-\dot x^2)^{3/2}}+V'(x)\right]=0.
$$

This is the rest-plus-kinetic energy of a [relativistic particle](../../../../../relativistic-particle.md), in units with light speed one, plus its [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md).
