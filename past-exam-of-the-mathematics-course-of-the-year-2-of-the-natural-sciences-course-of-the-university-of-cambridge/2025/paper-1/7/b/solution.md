<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) determine the [harmonic conjugate](../../../../../harmonic-conjugate.md). First

$$
u_x=e^x[(x+1)\cos y-y\sin y],
$$

so $v_y=u_x$ integrates with respect to $y$ to

$$
v=e^x(x\sin y+y\cos y)+h(x).
$$

Now

$$
u_y=e^x[-(x+1)\sin y-y\cos y],
$$

and the second [Cauchy-Riemann equation](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md), $v_x=-u_y$, forces $h'(x)=0$. Hence

$$
f=e^x\{x\cos y-y\sin y+i(x\sin y+y\cos y)\}+iC=ze^z+iC.
$$

The normalization at zero gives $C=0$, so the required [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(z)=ze^z.}
$$
