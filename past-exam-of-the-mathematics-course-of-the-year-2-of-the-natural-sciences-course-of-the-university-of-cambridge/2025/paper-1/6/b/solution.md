<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) implies

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(MN-NM)=\operatorname{Tr}(MN)-\operatorname{Tr}(NM)=0.
$$

Taking the [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the asserted equality therefore gives $nc=0$. For a positive integer $n$ over the complex numbers this requires $\boxed{c=0}$. It is also sufficient: choose $M=N=0$, or any two [commuting](../../../../../commuting-elements.md) [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md). Thus a finite-dimensional [operator commutator](../../../../../operator-commutator.md) cannot be a nonzero scalar multiple of the [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md).
