<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [anticommutator](../../../../../anticommutator.md) condition and invertibility give the [matrix similarities](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md)

$$
B^{-1}AB=-A,\qquad A^{-1}BA=-B.
$$

Since [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is invariant under [matrix similarity](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md),

$$
\operatorname{Tr}A=\operatorname{Tr}(-A)=-\operatorname{Tr}A,\qquad\operatorname{Tr}B=-\operatorname{Tr}B.
$$

Hence $\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}A=\operatorname{Tr}B=0}$. Taking [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md) in the first [matrix similarity](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) also gives

$$
\det A=\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A.
$$

Since $A$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), $\det A\ne0$, and $\boxed{n\text{ is even}}$. In terms of [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), the same [matrix similarity](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) pairs each nonzero [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ with $-\lambda$, with the same [algebraic multiplicity](../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md), so an odd number of them is impossible.
