<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$, the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) gives

$$
\|Cv\|^2=v^\dagger C^\dagger Cv=0.
$$

The [norm](../../../../../norm.md) vanishes only on the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md), so $Cv=0$ for every $v$ and $\boxed{C=0}$. In contrast,

$$
\boxed{N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne0,\qquad N^2=0}
$$

is a nonzero [nilpotent matrix](../../../../../nilpotent-matrix.md). Multiplying a [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) by its [conjugate transpose](../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) detects column [norms](../../../../../norm.md); multiplying it by itself has no corresponding positivity property.
