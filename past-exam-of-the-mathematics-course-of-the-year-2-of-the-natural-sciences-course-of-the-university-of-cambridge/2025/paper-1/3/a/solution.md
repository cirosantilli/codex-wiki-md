<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous [linear differential equation](../../../../../linear-differential-equation.md) can be written in [divergence form](../../../../../divergence-form.md):

$$
(x^{n+1}u')'=0.
$$

One integration gives $u'=C x^{-n-1}$. Therefore the two cases are

$$
\boxed{u_h(x)=A+B x^{-n}\quad(n\ne0),\qquad u_h(x)=A+B\log x\quad(n=0).}
$$

In each case the displayed functions have [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md) throughout the interval. Imposing both homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) forces $A=B=0$: for $n\ne0$, $2^n\ne1$, and for $n=0$, $\log2\ne0$. This also establishes uniqueness for the inhomogeneous [boundary value problem](../../../../../boundary-value-problem-split.md).
