<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [convolution](../../../../../convolution.md) $(g*g)(x)$ is the length of the overlap of $(-1,1)$ with $(x-1,x+1)$. This overlap has length $2-|x|$ when $|x|<2$ and zero otherwise, so $u=g*g$. The [convolution theorem](../../../../../convolution-theorem.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{\widetilde u(k)=\widetilde g(k)^2=\frac{4\sin^2 k}{k^2},\qquad \widetilde u(0)=4.}
$$

The value at zero is also the area of the [triangular function](../../../../../triangular-function.md), providing a normalization check on its [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md).
