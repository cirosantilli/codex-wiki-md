<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Direct evaluation of the [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) gives, for $k\ne0$,

$$
\widetilde g(k)=\int_{-1}^1e^{-ikx}\,dx=\frac{e^{ik}-e^{-ik}}{ik}=\boxed{\frac{2\sin k}{k}}.
$$

At $k=0$ the defining [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is the interval length, so $\boxed{\widetilde g(0)=2}$. This also equals the continuous limit of the displayed formula.
