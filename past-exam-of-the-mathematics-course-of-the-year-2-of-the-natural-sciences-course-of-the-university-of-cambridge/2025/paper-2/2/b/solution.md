<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $u=\cos\theta$. The boundary data simplify to $u+2u^2=\frac23P_0(u)+P_1(u)+\frac43P_2(u)$. The [axisymmetric harmonic extension from a sphere](../../../../../axisymmetric-harmonic-extension-from-a-sphere.md) keeps only decaying radial powers outside and regular powers inside. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Psi_{\rm out}=\frac{2a}{3r}+\frac{a^2}{r^2}\cos\theta+\frac{4a^3}{3r^3}P_2(\cos\theta),\quad r\ge a,}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\Psi_{\rm in}=\frac23+\frac ra\cos\theta+\frac{4r^2}{3a^2}P_2(\cos\theta),\quad r\le a.}
$$

Each [Legendre polynomial](../../../../../legendre-polynomial.md) coefficient agrees at $r=a$. The exterior solution tends to zero, and the interior solution is finite at $r=0$. The [maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) proves [uniqueness](../../../../../uniqueness.md) in these respective classes.
