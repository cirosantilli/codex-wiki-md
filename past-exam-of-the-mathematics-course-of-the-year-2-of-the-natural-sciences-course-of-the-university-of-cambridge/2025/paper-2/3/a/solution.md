<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is [irrotational](../../../../../irrotational-vector-field.md), introduce an [electrostatic potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md) with $\mathbf E=-\nabla\varphi$. [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) becomes the [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{(\partial_x^2+\partial_y^2)\varphi=-\rho/\epsilon_0,\qquad\mathbf E=-\nabla\varphi.}
$$

The curl equation is automatic because the [curl of a gradient](../../../../../curl-of-a-gradient.md) vanishes. Independence of $z$ makes $E_z$ constant, and its required decay forces it to be zero, so a $z$-independent potential suffices.
