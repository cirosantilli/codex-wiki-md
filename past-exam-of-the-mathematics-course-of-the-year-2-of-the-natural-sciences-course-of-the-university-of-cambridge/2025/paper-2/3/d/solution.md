<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Differentiate the [Green-function representation](../../../../../green-function-representation.md), valid away from the charged sheet:

$$
E_y=\frac1{2\pi\epsilon_0}\int_0^L\frac{y\,ds}{(x-s)^2+y^2}
=\frac{\arctan(x/y)-\arctan((x-L)/y)}{2\pi\epsilon_0}.
$$

For $0<x<L$, the two [inverse tangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) limits at $y\downarrow0$ are $\pi/2$ and $-\pi/2$; approaching from below reverses both signs. Thus

$$
\boxed{E_y(x,0^+)=\frac1{2\epsilon_0},\quad E_y(x,0^-)=-\frac1{2\epsilon_0},\quad |\Delta E_y|=\frac1{\epsilon_0}.}
$$

This agrees with the normal [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) jump obtained by applying [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) to a thin pillbox enclosing unit [surface charge density](../../../../../surface-charge-density.md).
