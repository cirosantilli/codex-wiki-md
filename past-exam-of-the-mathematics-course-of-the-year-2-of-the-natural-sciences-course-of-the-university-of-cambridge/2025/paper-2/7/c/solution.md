<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The disk still has only **three degrees of freedom and hence three normal modes**: two centre coordinates and one in-plane rotation. Five springs alter the restoring [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) but add no disk coordinates. The fivefold [rotational symmetry](../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) makes the translational stiffness proportional to the two-dimensional [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md), and makes translation-rotation coupling vanish. Therefore **the two translation modes are always degenerate**; the rotational mode is separate, with possible accidental equality of its frequency for particular parameters. The general formulas in [small oscillations of a disk attached to prestretched radial springs](../../../../../small-oscillations-of-a-disk-attached-to-prestretched-radial-springs.md) apply with $N=5$.
