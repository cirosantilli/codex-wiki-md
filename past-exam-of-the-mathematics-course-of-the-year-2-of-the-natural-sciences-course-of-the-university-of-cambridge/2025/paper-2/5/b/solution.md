<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $q=i\omega$. A constant-coefficient [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) has [transfer function](../../../../../transfer-function.md) $1/P(q)$, where $P(q)=\sum_{n=1}^c a_nq^n$. The specified simple [poles](../../../../../pole.md) correspond to $q=0,-2,1$. A minimal-order choice is therefore

$$
P(q)=q(q+2)(q-1)=q^3+q^2-2q.
$$

Thus one possible system is

$$
\boxed{x'''+x''-2x'=F,\qquad c=3,\quad(a_1,a_2,a_3)=(-2,1,1),}
$$

up to a common nonzero multiplicative constant. The root $q=1$ permits an exponentially growing homogeneous solution; the pole data therefore describe an unstable system. This does not prevent the requested algebraic reconstruction of its [transfer function](../../../../../transfer-function.md).
