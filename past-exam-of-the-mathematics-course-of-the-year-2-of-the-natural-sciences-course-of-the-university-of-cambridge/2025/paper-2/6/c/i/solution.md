↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Relabel the two [dummy indices](../../../../../../dummy-index.md) and use the respective symmetries:

$$
X_{ij}A_{ij}=X_{ji}A_{ji}=-X_{ij}A_{ij}=0.
$$

Thus the [symmetric tensor](../../../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) and [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) subspaces are [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) in the [Frobenius inner product](../../../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md).
