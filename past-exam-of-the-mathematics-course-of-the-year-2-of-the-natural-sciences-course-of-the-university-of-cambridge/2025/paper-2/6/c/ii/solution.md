<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The contraction is $\operatorname{tr}(A^3)$. Since $A^T=-A$, [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) and invariance of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) give

$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^3)=\operatorname{tr}((A^3)^T)=\operatorname{tr}((-A)^3)=-\operatorname{tr}(A^3)=0.
$$

This is the odd-power case of [vanishing odd complete contractions of an antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../vanishing-odd-complete-contractions-of-an-antisymmetric-matrix.md).
