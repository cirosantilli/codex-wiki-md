<h1 id="6/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A complete pairwise [tensor contraction](../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) of copies of $A$ is represented by closed index cycles. Each cycle is a [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $\operatorname{tr}(A^r)$, possibly with a sign from reversing an index pair. If a contraction is internal to one copy, it contains the already vanishing $\operatorname{tr}A$. Otherwise an odd total number of copies forces at least one cycle of odd length, since a sum of even lengths is even. That trace vanishes by the transposition argument in part (ii). Therefore **every such complete contraction is zero**. This proves [vanishing odd complete contractions of an antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../vanishing-odd-complete-contractions-of-an-antisymmetric-matrix.md) without assuming that all contractions form a single trace.
