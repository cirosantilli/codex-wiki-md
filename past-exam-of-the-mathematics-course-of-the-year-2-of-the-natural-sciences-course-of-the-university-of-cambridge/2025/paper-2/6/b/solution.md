<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) first: the [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) has zero diagonal and $X$ is traceless, so $T_{ii}=3t$. Taking symmetric and antisymmetric parts then gives the [scalar, symmetric-traceless and axial tensor decomposition](../../../../../scalar-symmetric-traceless-and-axial-tensor-decomposition.md):

$$
\boxed{t=\frac13T_{kk},\quad A_{ij}=\frac12(T_{ij}-T_{ji}),\quad X_{ij}=\frac12(T_{ij}+T_{ji})-\frac13T_{kk}\delta_{ij}.}
$$

The [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) satisfies $\delta_{ii}=3$, which verifies $X_{ii}=0$. Substitution verifies the original decomposition and also proves its [uniqueness](../../../../../uniqueness.md).
