↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) $R\in SO(3)$, the Cartesian [tensor component transformation law](../../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{T'_{ijkl}=R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}T_{abcd}.}
$$

An [isotropic tensor](../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) is unchanged under every such rotation, so its components satisfy $T_{ijkl}=R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}T_{abcd}$ for every $R\in SO(3)$. The repeated indices are summed by the [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../einstein-notation.md).
