<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [group axioms from two-sided local inverse identities](../../../../../group-axioms-from-two-sided-local-inverse-identities.md). Choose any $a$ in the nonempty [semigroup](../../../../../semigroup.md) and put $e_R=a_L^{-1}a$, $e_L=aa_R^{-1}$. The given identities say $be_R=b$ and $e_Lb=b$ for every $b$. Therefore $e_Le_R=e_L$ by the right-identity property, and $e_Le_R=e_R$ by the left-identity property. Thus $e_L=e_R=e$ is a two-sided [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md).

For an arbitrary $a$, set $b=a_L^{-1}$ and $c=a_R^{-1}$. Inserting $e$ into the original identities gives $ba=e$ and $ac=e$. By [associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md),

$$
b=be=b(ac)=(ba)c=ec=c.
$$

Thus every element has a two-sided [inverse element](../../../../../inverse-element.md). Closure and [associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) were assumed, so **all the group axioms hold**. No cancellation or identity was used before it had been established.
