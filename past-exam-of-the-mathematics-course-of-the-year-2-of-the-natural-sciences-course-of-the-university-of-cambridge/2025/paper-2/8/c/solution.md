<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the [dihedral group](../../../../../dihedral-group.md) as $D_6=\langle r,s\mid r^6=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$, where $r$ rotates through $\pi/3$. There are six rotations and six reflections, so **the order is 12**. Conjugation by $s$ reverses a rotation, and conjugation by $r$ sends $r^js$ to $r^{j+2}s$. Consequently the [conjugacy classes](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) and their sizes are

$$
\boxed{\{1\}\ (1),\quad\{r^3\}\ (1),\quad\{r,r^5\}\ (2),\quad\{r^2,r^4\}\ (2),\quad\{s,r^2s,r^4s\}\ (3),\quad\{rs,r^3s,r^5s\}\ (3).}
$$

The last two classes distinguish the reflection axes through opposite vertices and through opposite edge midpoints. Their sizes sum to 12.
