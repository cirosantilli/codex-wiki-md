<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_3$ is generated by transpositions, each of order two. A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) to $\mathbb Z_3$ must map every transposition to zero, because the only target element annihilated by two is zero. Hence **the map to $\mathbb Z_3$ is necessarily trivial**.

For $S_3\to\mathbb Z_2$, the 3-cycles map to zero because their order divides three. All transpositions are [conjugate group elements](../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md), and a map to an [abelian group](../../../../../abelian-group.md) takes conjugate elements to the same value. That common value is either zero or one. Both choices work: they give **the trivial map and the sign homomorphism**, with an odd permutation sent to $1$ and an even permutation sent to $0$.
