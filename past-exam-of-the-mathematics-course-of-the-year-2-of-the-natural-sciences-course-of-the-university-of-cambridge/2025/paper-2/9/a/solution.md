↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $f:G\to H$ preserves the operation: $f(ab)=f(a)f(b)$ for all $a,b\in G$. A [group isomorphism](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) is a bijective [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md); its inverse also preserves the operation. The [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is $\ker f=\{g\in G:f(g)=e_H\}$, a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of $G$.
