<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from the additive [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z_6$ is determined by the image $t$ of its generator $1$, with the consistency condition $6t=0$ in the target. Every element of either target satisfies this condition. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Z_6\to\mathbb Z_2:\quad f_t([n]_6)=[tn]_2,\quad t=0,1,}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Z_6\to\mathbb Z_3:\quad f_t([n]_6)=[tn]_3,\quad t=0,1,2.}
$$

These formulas are well-defined because changing $n$ by six changes $tn$ by a target multiple of its modulus. They list respectively two and three maps.
