<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $v=xu$. Direct [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $u''+2u'/x=v''/x$, so $-v''=\lambda v$. Finiteness of $u$ at zero requires $v(0)=0$, and the other endpoint gives $v(\pi/2)=0$. The [Dirichlet eigenvalues](../../../../../dirichlet-eigenvalue.md) are positive: [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\lambda\int|v|^2=\int|v'|^2$, and a nonzero $v$ cannot have zero right-hand side. Thus $v=C\sin(\sqrt\lambda x)$, and the second endpoint gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda_n=4n^2,\qquad u_n(x)=\frac{\sin(2nx)}x,\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Define $u_n(0)=2n$ by its [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md). This is the [radial Dirichlet spectrum in three dimensions](../../../../../radial-dirichlet-spectrum-in-three-dimensions.md) with radius $\pi/2$. Its weighted squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is $\int_0^{\pi/2}x^2u_n^2dx=\pi/4$.
