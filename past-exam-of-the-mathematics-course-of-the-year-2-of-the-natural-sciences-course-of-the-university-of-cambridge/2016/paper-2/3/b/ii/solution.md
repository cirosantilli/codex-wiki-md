<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Translation and rotation invariance allow a radial [Green function](../../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) $G(R)$, with $R=|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|$. Outside the source, $G''+2G'/R=0$, so $G=A/R+B$. Decay at infinity gives $B=0$.

Integrate the defining equation over a small ball centred on the source. The [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) fixes its flux:

$$
\int_{R<\varepsilon}\nabla^2G\,dV=4\pi\varepsilon^2G'(\varepsilon)=-4\pi A=-1.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=\frac1{4\pi|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}.}
$$

This is the [Green function of the Laplacian](../../../../../../green-function-of-the-laplacian.md) with the stated negative-delta sign. A homogeneous $1/R$ addition would change the delta normalization, so the singular coefficient is fixed, not an arbitrary integration constant.
