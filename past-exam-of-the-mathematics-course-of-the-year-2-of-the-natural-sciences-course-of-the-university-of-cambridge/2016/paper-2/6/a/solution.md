<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A second-order Cartesian [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) assigns components in each orthonormal coordinate system according to

$$
\boxed{T'_{ij}=R_{ik}R_{j\ell}T_{k\ell},\qquad T'=RTR^T,\qquad R^TR=I.}
$$

Here $R$ is the matrix taking old [vector](../../../../../vector.md) components to new ones, and repeated indices are summed. This is the [tensor component transformation law](../../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md); an arbitrary array of nine numbers is not a tensor unless its components in different frames obey this rule. No symmetry $T_{ij}=T_{ji}$ is implied by being a second-order tensor.
