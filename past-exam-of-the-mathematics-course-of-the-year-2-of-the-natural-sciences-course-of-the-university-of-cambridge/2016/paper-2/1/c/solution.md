<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The left boundary condition is $y(0)=0$, while the right one is $y'(\ell)=-ky(\ell)$. Hence $[yy']_0^\ell=-ky(\ell)^2$. The [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) becomes

$$
\lambda\int_0^\ell y^2\,dx=\int_0^\ell\big[(y')^2+qy^2\big]dx+ky(\ell)^2.
$$

Every term on the right is nonnegative. If the whole expression were zero, in particular $\int_0^\ell(y')^2dx=0$, so $y$ would be constant. Its zero value at the left endpoint would force $y\equiv0$, contrary to its being an [eigenfunction](../../../../../eigenfunction.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda>0.}
$$

This is the positivity mechanism for [Dirichlet-Robin eigenvalues on a finite interval](../../../../../dirichlet-robin-eigenvalues-on-a-finite-interval.md). The sign of the boundary contribution is positive for the convention $y'+ky=0$ with $k>0$.
