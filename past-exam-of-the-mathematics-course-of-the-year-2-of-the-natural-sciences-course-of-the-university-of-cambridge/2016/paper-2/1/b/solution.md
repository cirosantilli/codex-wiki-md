<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply the [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) of the [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) by $y$ and integrate. [Integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\lambda\int_a^b\omega y^2\,dx=\int_a^byLy\,dx=-[pyy']_a^b+\int_a^b\big[p(y')^2+qy^2\big]dx.
$$

Since $y$ is a nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../eigenfunction.md) and $\omega>0$, the denominator is strictly positive. Thus the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{\displaystyle\int_a^b[p(y')^2+qy^2]dx-[pyy']_a^b}{\displaystyle\int_a^b\omega y^2\,dx}.}
$$

The endpoint term must be retained for a general [Robin boundary condition](../../../../../robin-boundary-condition.md); it vanishes for homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) but need not vanish in the present general problem.
