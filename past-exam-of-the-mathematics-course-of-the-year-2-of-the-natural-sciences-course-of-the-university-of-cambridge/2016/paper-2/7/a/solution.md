<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [zero-natural-length spring](../../../../../zero-natural-length-spring.md), extension is the full distance between its endpoints, so the squared spring lengths are quadratic functions of the Cartesian coordinates. The [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
L={}&\frac{m_1}{2}(\dot X_1^2+\dot Y_1^2)+\frac{m_2}{2}(\dot X_2^2+\dot Y_2^2)\\
&-\frac{k_1}{2}(X_1^2+Y_1^2)-\frac{k_2}{2}\big[(X_2-X_1)^2+(Y_2-Y_1)^2\big]\\
&-g(m_1Y_1+m_2Y_2).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) has positive derivative in the upward coordinate, giving downward forces. Applying the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) to all four coordinates gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
m_1\ddot X_1+(k_1+k_2)X_1-k_2X_2&=0,\\
m_2\ddot X_2+k_2X_2-k_2X_1&=0,\\
m_1\ddot Y_1+(k_1+k_2)Y_1-k_2Y_2&=-m_1g,\\
m_2\ddot Y_2+k_2Y_2-k_2Y_1&=-m_2g.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The two spring forces are equal and opposite on the two masses for the connecting spring, as required by [Newton's third law](../../../../../newton-s-third-law.md).
