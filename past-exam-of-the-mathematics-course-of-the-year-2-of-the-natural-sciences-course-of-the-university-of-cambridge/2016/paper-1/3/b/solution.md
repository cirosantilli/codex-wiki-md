<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The left-hand value at the junction is

$$
y(\mu-)=1-e^{-\mu}-e^{-\mu}\sinh\mu=\frac12-e^{-\mu}+\frac12e^{-2\mu}.
$$

The right-hand value is

$$
y(\mu+)=(\cosh\mu-1)e^{-\mu}=\frac12+\frac12e^{-2\mu}-e^{-\mu}.
$$

Thus the piecewise [Green function](../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) solution is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), with

$$
\boxed{y(\mu)=\frac12(1+e^{-2\mu}-2e^{-\mu})=\frac12(1-e^{-\mu})^2.}
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is continuous as well: both one-sided values equal $-\tfrac12(1-e^{-\mu})^2$. Only the second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jumps, consistently with the step in the forcing and the absence of a [Dirac delta](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) source.
