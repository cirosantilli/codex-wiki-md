<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Writing $\zeta=\xi+i\eta$,

$$
u-iv=\frac1{\zeta+2}=\frac{\xi+2-i\eta}{(\xi+2)^2+\eta^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u=\frac{(\xi+2,\eta)}{(\xi+2)^2+\eta^2},\qquad |\mathbf u|=\frac1{\sqrt{(\xi+2)^2+\eta^2}}.}
$$

The [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) points radially **away from $(-2,0)$**, with magnitude inversely proportional to distance from that point. It diverges on approach to that boundary point and tends to zero far away. This is the radial [potential flow](../../../../../../potential-flow.md) described by the logarithmic [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md).
