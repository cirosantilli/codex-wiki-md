<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the specified ratios, the two factors in the modal amplitude become $\sin(n\pi/5)$ and $\sin(n\pi/14)$. Hence a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) is unexcited precisely when one of these factors is zero:

$$
\boxed{n\in5\mathbb N\ \cup\ 14\mathbb N.}
$$

That includes all multiples of five or fourteen, with multiples of seventy counted once. The mode $n=0$ is not a fixed-end string mode.
