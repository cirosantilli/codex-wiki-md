<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a bounded volume $V$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary $S=\partial V$, and a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ on a neighbourhood of $\overline V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\iint_S\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV.}
$$

Here $\hat{\mathbf n}$ is the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md). The left side is the outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md); the right side integrates the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) over the enclosed volume.
