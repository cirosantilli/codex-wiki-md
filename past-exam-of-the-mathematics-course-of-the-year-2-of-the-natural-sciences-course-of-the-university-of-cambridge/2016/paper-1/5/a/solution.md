<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The inverse is assumed to exist. First,

$$
(I+M)(I-M)=I-M^2=(I-M)(I+M).
$$

By [matrix commutation with an inverse](../../../../../matrix-commutation-with-an-inverse.md), multiplying on both sides by $(I-M)^{-1}$ gives

$$
\boxed{(I+M)(I-M)^{-1}=(I-M)^{-1}(I+M).}
$$

An equivalent calculation writes $I+M=2I-(I-M)$, so both products equal $2(I-M)^{-1}-I$. No convergent infinite [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) series is needed.
