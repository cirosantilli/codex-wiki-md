<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) in the [operator domain](../../../../../../operator-domain.md), $H\psi_n=E_n\psi_n$. Substitution into the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) gives

$$
\boxed{F[\psi_n]=\frac{E_n\langle\psi_n,\psi_n\rangle}{\langle\psi_n,\psi_n\rangle}=E_n.}
$$

The cancellation only needs a finite nonzero [norm](../../../../../../norm.md); orthonormalization makes both denominator and [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) equal to one.
