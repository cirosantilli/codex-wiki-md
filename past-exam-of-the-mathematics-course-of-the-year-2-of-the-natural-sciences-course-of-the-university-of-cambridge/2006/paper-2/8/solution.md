<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

A [group](../../../../group-split.md) has a closed [binary operation](../../../../binary-operation.md), associativity, an identity, and an inverse for each element. For a finite group, [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) states $|G|=[G:H]|H|$ for each subgroup $H$, so $|H|$ divides $|G|$.

The [order of a group element](../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) $g$ is the least positive $n$ with $g^n=e$, or infinity if none exists. In a finite group the powers form the [cyclic subgroup](../../../../cyclic-subgroup.md) $\langle g\rangle$ of that order; Lagrange's theorem proves $\operatorname{ord}(g)\mid|G|$. If $|G|=p$ is prime and $g\ne e$, its order must be $p$. Thus $g$ generates the whole group, proving $G\cong C_p$.

If every nonidentity element has order two, every element is its own inverse. Then $ab=(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}=ba$. This proves that a [group of exponent two is abelian](../../../../group-of-exponent-two-is-abelian.md).
