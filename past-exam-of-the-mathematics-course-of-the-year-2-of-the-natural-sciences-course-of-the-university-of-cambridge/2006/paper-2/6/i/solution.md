<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [Cartesian tensor](../../../../../cartesian-tensor.md) under a proper orthogonal change $R$, the rank-$n$ law is $T'_{i_1\cdots i_n}=R_{i_1j_1}\cdots R_{i_nj_n}T_{j_1\cdots j_n}$, with repeated indices summed. An [isotropic tensor](../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) has $T'=T$ for every such rotation.

For rank two, $T=RTR^T$. A half-turn about a coordinate axis, obtained by applying a quarter-turn twice, reverses each off-diagonal component with exactly one index on that axis. Invariance forces every off-diagonal entry to vanish. A quarter-turn about the third axis exchanges $T_{11}$ and $T_{22}$; quarter-turns about the other axes equate them with $T_{33}$. Thus the [isotropic second-rank tensor](../../../../../isotropic-second-rank-tensor.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_{ij}=\lambda\delta_{ij}.}
$$

Conversely $R(\lambda I)R^T=\lambda I$, proving sufficiency.
