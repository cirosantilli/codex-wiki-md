<h1 id="3/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $v_x=2x$, $v_y=2-2y$. Integrating and matching both derivatives gives the [harmonic conjugate](../../../../../../harmonic-conjugate.md)

$$
\boxed{v=x^2+2y-y^2+C,\qquad f(z)=2z+iz^2+iC,\quad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The real part of this polynomial is exactly the required $2x(1-y)$.
