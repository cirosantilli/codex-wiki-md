<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) is complex differentiable throughout an open region; equivalently it is locally represented by a convergent complex power series. For $f=u+iv$, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) are $u_x=v_y$, $u_y=-v_x$. With continuous first partial derivatives, these equations imply holomorphicity and $f'=u_x+iv_x$.

For $e^z=e^x\cos y+i e^x\sin y$, the equations hold and $u_x+iv_x=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)=e^z$. For $\sin z=\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y$, they also hold, and $u_x+iv_x=\cos x\cosh y-i\sin x\sinh y=\cos z$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dz}e^z=e^z,\qquad\frac{d}{dz}\sin z=\cos z.}
$$
