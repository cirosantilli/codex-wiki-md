<h1 id="3/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

There are **[logarithmic branch points](../../../../../../logarithmic-branch-point.md) at $z=-3i$ and infinity**. At zero, every local branch has $\log(3i)=\ln3+i(\pi/2+2\pi k)\ne0$, so division by $z^2$ gives **a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) of order two at zero**. Decay at infinity does not remove its branching: with $w=1/z$, the function is $w^2[\log(1+3iw)-\log w]$, which changes branch when $w$ encircles zero.
