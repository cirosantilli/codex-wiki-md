↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The numerator is nonzero at $1$ and $-2/3$, so the finite singularities are **a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) of order three at $1$ and a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) of order two at $-2/3$**. The numerator degree exceeds the denominator degree by three, with leading behaviour $z^3/9$. Hence **infinity is a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) of order three**.
