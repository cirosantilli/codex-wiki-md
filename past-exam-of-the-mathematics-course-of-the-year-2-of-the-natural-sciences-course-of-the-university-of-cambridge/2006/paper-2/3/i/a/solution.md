<h1 id="3/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The denominator factors as $(z+1-i)(z+1+i)$ and neither factor cancels with the numerator. Thus there are **[simple poles](../../../../../../simple-pole.md) at $z=-1\pm i$**. At infinity the quotient tends to one and has a regular expansion in $1/z$, so it has a [removable singularity at infinity](../../../../../../removable-singularity-at-infinity.md), with extended value one.
