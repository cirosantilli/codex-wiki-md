<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

For the normalized second-order equation $y''+P(x)y'+Q(x)y=0$, an [ordinary point](../../../../ordinary-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) has analytic $P,Q$. A [regular singular point](../../../../regular-singular-point.md) is a [singular point](../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) where $(x-x_0)P$ and $(x-x_0)^2Q$ extend analytically through $x_0$. The two finite singular points here are $0$ and $1/2$. At zero, the respective limiting coefficients are $1/2,0$; at $1/2$, they are $-1,0$. Thus **both are regular singular points**, with indicial roots $0,1/2$ and $0,2$, respectively.
