<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Decompose $\mathbb R^n=\operatorname{span}\{a\}\oplus a^\perp$. The [rank-one matrix update](../../../../../rank-one-matrix-update.md) $aa^T$ acts as multiplication by $\|a\|^2$ along $a$ and as zero on its [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\parallel(A^\pm)=1\pm\|a\|^2,\quad v_\parallel\in\operatorname{span}\{a\}\setminus\{0\};\qquad\lambda_\perp=1,\quad v_\perp\in a^\perp\setminus\{0\}.}
$$

The perpendicular eigenvalue has multiplicity $n-1$ and is absent when $n=1$. Consequently $A^+$ is always an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), while

$$
\boxed{A^-\text{ is invertible if and only if }\|a\|\ne1.}
$$
