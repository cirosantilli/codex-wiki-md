<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $A$ and $B$ are real [symmetric matrices](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), $U^\dagger=A-iB$. Expanding the [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md) condition gives

$$
I=U^\dagger U=(A-iB)(A+iB)=A^2+B^2+i(AB-BA).
$$

Equality of the entrywise real and imaginary parts yields

$$
\boxed{A^2+B^2=I,\qquad AB=BA.}
$$

These are the identities for the [real and imaginary parts of a symmetric unitary matrix](../../../../../real-and-imaginary-parts-of-a-symmetric-unitary-matrix.md).
