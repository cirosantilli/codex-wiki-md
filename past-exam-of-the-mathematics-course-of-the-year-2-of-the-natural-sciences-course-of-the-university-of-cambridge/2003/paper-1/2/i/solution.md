↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) measured relative to the fixed-temperature end, the [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) and its data are

$$
\boxed{u_t=\nu u_{xx}\quad(0<x<L,t>0),\qquad u(0,t)=0,\qquad k u_x(L,t)=H,\qquad u(x,0)=0.}
$$

Here $\nu>0$ is thermal [diffusivity](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) and $k>0$ is [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md). The sign of the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) condition describes incoming [heat flux](../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) at $L$: the outward conductive [heat flux](../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) is $-k u_x=-H$.
