<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under an orthogonal Cartesian change of axes $Q$, the [vector](../../../../../vector.md) components transform as $v_i'=Q_{ia}v_a$, while $v_k'v_k'=v_av_a$ and $\delta_{ij}=Q_{ia}Q_{ja}$. Substitution gives

$$
P_{ij}'=\delta_{ij}-\frac{Q_{ia}v_aQ_{jb}v_b}{v_cv_c}=Q_{ia}Q_{jb}\left(\delta_{ab}-\frac{v_av_b}{v_cv_c}\right)=Q_{ia}Q_{jb}P_{ab}.
$$

This is the rank-two [Cartesian tensor](../../../../../cartesian-tensor.md) transformation law. The nonzero assumption ensures that the denominator is positive. In [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) form $P=I-vv^T/(v^Tv)$ is the [orthogonal projection matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) onto the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) of $v$.
