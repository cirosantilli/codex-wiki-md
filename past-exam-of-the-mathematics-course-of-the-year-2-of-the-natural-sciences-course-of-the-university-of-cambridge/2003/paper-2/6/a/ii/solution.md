↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) condition is $v_ju_j=0$, so

$$
\boxed{P_{ij}u_j=u_i-v_i\frac{v_ju_j}{v_kv_k}=u_i.}
$$

Every [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) is fixed by the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md).
