<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Contract with $v_j$ and use its squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md):

$$
\boxed{P_{ij}v_j=v_i-v_i\frac{v_jv_j}{v_kv_k}=0.}
$$

Thus $v$ lies in the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of this [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md).
