<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the proposed [vector](../../../../../vector.md), the first horizontal component of $Kq$ is $-k(\alpha+\beta)/2$. Since the corresponding component of $q$ is zero, a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) requires $\beta=-\alpha$. The vertical components then give $Kq=2kq$. With an arbitrary nonzero overall amplitude, choose $\alpha=1$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{q_y=(0,1,0,-1,0,-1,0,1)^T,\qquad\omega=\sqrt{\frac{2k}m}.}
$$

The analogous pattern invariant under [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) across the vertical axis is

$$
\boxed{q_x=(1,0,-1,0,-1,0,1,0)^T,\qquad\omega=\sqrt{\frac{2k}m}.}
$$

Direct multiplication confirms $Kq_x=2kq_x$. The two patterns give independent [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with the same [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), as expected from the square's [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) symmetry.
