<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first two [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) displace every particle identically in the horizontal or vertical direction. Every relative [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is zero, so every [spring](../../../../../spring.md) has zero extension to first order. The third [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is $(y_i,-x_i)$ evaluated at the equilibrium points, which is an infinitesimal clockwise [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). For any edge the relative [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is perpendicular to that edge, again giving zero extension.

These are the [rigid-motion zero modes of a spring framework](../../../../../rigid-motion-zero-modes-of-a-spring-framework.md): **two [translations](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) and one infinitesimal [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md)**. Each has $Kq=0$, so $\boxed{\omega=0}$. In the linearized dynamics its amplitude has the form $A+Bt$, expressing free rigid motion rather than a restoring oscillation. Exact finite rigid [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) also preserve every [spring](../../../../../spring.md) length; the mode [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is their first-order tangent.
