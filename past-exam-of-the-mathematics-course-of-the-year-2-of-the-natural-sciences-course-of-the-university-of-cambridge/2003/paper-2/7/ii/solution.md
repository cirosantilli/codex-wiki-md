<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [breathing mode](../../../../../breathing-mode.md), each [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is proportional to its equilibrium position. A representative [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is

$$
q_b=(-1,1,1,1,-1,-1,1,-1)^T.
$$

Multiplying by the [stiffness matrix](../../../../../stiffness-matrix.md) gives $Kq_b=4kq_b$. Thus

$$
\boxed{q\propto q_b,\qquad\omega^2=\frac{4k}m,\qquad\omega=2\sqrt{\frac km}.}
$$

Positive and negative amplitudes are uniform expansion and contraction about the center.
