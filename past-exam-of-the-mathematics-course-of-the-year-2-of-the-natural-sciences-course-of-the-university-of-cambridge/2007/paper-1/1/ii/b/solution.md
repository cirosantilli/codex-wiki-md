<h1 id="1/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The radial component of the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) in [spherical coordinates](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) is

$$
(\nabla\times\mathbf f)_r=\frac1{r\sin\theta}\left[\partial_\theta(\sin\theta f_\phi)-\partial_\phi f_\theta\right].
$$

It contains no $f_r$, so $R$ cannot contribute. Substituting the tangential components gives

$$
\partial_\theta(\sin\theta f_\phi)-\partial_\phi f_\theta=\frac1r\left[G_{\phi\theta}-G_{\theta\phi}+\partial_\theta(\sin\theta F_\theta)+\frac{F_{\phi\phi}}{\sin\theta}\right].
$$

The mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of smooth $G$ agree, so its terms cancel. Multiplication by $r$, to take the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the position [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf r=r\mathbf e_r$, does not change this independence from $R,G$.
