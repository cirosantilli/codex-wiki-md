<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the right-handed orthonormal basis $(\mathbf e_r,\mathbf e_\theta,\mathbf e_\phi)$. The [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\mathbf e_r$ rotates tangential components by a right angle; taking its [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) again in the opposite order projects a [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) onto the tangent plane. The [radial curl of angular gradient fields](../../../../../radial-curl-of-angular-gradient-fields.md) then depends only on the rotated-gradient part. All coordinate calculations are made away from the polar coordinate singularities and extend wherever the original fields are smooth.
