<h1 id="1/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $\hat{\mathbf r}=\mathbf e_r$, the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) formula gives

$$
\mathbf e_r\times\nabla F=-\frac{F_\phi}{r\sin\theta}\mathbf e_\theta+\frac{F_\theta}{r}\mathbf e_\phi.
$$

The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives $(\mathbf e_r\times\nabla G)\times\mathbf e_r=\nabla G-\mathbf e_rG_r=(G_\theta/r)\mathbf e_\theta+[G_\phi/(r\sin\theta)]\mathbf e_\phi$. Adding the radial part yields

$$
\mathbf f=R\mathbf e_r+\frac1r\left(G_\theta-\frac{F_\phi}{\sin\theta}\right)\mathbf e_\theta+\frac1r\left(\frac{G_\phi}{\sin\theta}+F_\theta\right)\mathbf e_\phi.
$$
