↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The remaining terms give

$$
\boxed{(\nabla\times\mathbf f)\cdot\mathbf r=\frac1r\Delta_{\theta,\phi}F,\qquad\Delta_{\theta,\phi}=\frac1{\sin\theta}\partial_\theta(\sin\theta\,\partial_\theta)+\frac1{\sin^2\theta}\partial_\phi^2.}
$$

This is the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](../../../../../../laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on the unit [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md). Taking the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the unit radial [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) instead would give $r^{-2}\Delta_{\theta,\phi}F$; the extra factor $r$ here comes from the position [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the question.
