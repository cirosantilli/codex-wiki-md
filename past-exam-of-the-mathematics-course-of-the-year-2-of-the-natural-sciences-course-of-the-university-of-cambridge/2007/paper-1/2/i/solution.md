<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives $X''=-k^2X$ and $T'=-\kappa k^2T$. The periodic matching of both the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) and its first [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) selects $k=n\pi/L$, with the constant mode at $n=0$. Thus the [periodic diffusion on a circle](../../../../../periodic-diffusion-on-a-circle.md) solution is

$$
u(x,t)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[a_n\cos\frac{n\pi x}{L}+b_n\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right]e^{-\kappa(n\pi/L)^2t},
$$

where the [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) of the initial [function](../../../../../function-split.md) are

$$
a_n=\frac1L\int_{-L}^Lu_0(x)\cos\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx,\qquad b_n=\frac1L\int_{-L}^Lu_0(x)\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx.
$$

The formula for $a_n$ also gives $a_0$ at $n=0$. The constant mode preserves the initial spatial [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md); every nonconstant [Fourier mode](../../../../../fourier-mode.md) decays. For initial data in [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), the series approaches those data in [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) as $t\downarrow0$ and is smooth at positive times.
