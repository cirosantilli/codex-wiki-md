<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The denominator factors as $(z+2-i)(z+2+i)$. Neither root cancels against the numerator, and both are simple, so the finite singularities are

$$
\boxed{z=-2+i,\quad z=-2-i\quad\text{(simple poles).}}
$$

This is a [rational function](../../../../../rational-function.md), so it has no [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md). At infinity, putting $w=1/z$ gives $w/(1+4w+5w^2)$, analytic at zero; infinity is therefore a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md) with value zero.
