<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $r_0=|\mathbf x_0|$ and $\mathbf x_0^*=a^2\mathbf x_0/r_0^2$ for $0<r_0<a$. The [Dirichlet Green function inside a sphere](../../../../../dirichlet-green-function-inside-a-sphere.md) is

$$
\boxed{G_B(\mathbf x;\mathbf x_0)=-\frac1{4\pi}\left(\frac1{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|}-\frac{a/r_0}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0^*|}\right).}
$$

On $|\mathbf x|=a$, the distance identity $|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0^*|=(a/r_0)|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|$ cancels the two terms. The inverse point lies outside the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md), so it introduces no interior source. This weighted inversion is the [Kelvin transform](../../../../../kelvin-transform.md). For a central source, take the limit to get $G_B(\mathbf x;0)=-(4\pi)^{-1}(1/|\mathbf x|-1/a)$.
