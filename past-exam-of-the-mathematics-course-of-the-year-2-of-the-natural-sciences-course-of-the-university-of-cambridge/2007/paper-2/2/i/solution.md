<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathbf x_0^-=(x_0,y_0,-z_0)$ be the reflection of the source across the plane. The [method of images](../../../../../method-of-images.md) gives the [Dirichlet Green function](../../../../../dirichlet-green-function.md)

$$
\boxed{G_H(\mathbf x;\mathbf x_0)=-\frac1{4\pi}\left(\frac1{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|}-\frac1{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0^-|}\right).}
$$

The two source distances agree on $z=0$, so the boundary value vanishes. The reflected source lies outside the upper half-space, leaving only the required [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) inside. Both terms vanish at infinity.
