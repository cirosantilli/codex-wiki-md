<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For $s=|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|>0$, the radial [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md) gives $\nabla^2(1/s)=s^{-2}\partial_s(s^2\partial_s(1/s))=0$. The outward [flux](../../../../flux.md) of $\nabla[-1/(4\pi s)]$ through a small [sphere](../../../../sphere.md) centred at the source is $[1/(4\pi s^2)]4\pi s^2=1$. Applying [Green's second identity](../../../../green-second-identity.md) to a smooth [test function](../../../../test-function.md) outside that small sphere and shrinking its radius therefore gives the distributional identity

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\left(-\frac1{4\pi|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|}\right)=\delta(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0).}
$$

The kernel tends to zero at infinity. Thus it is the [fundamental solution](../../../../fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) with the sign convention $\Delta G=+\delta$. The sign will reverse when converting it into the [electrostatic potential](../../../../electric-potential.md) of a positive [point charge](../../../../point-charge.md) through [Poisson's equation](../../../../poisson-s-equation.md).
