<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Write $R=|x-x_0|$. A radial function [harmonic](../../../../harmonic-function.md) away from the source satisfies $(R^2G')'=0$, hence $G=A+B/R$. Decay at infinity forces $A=0$. Integrating $\Delta G=\delta(x-x_0)$ over a small sphere, the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) requires flux one:

$$
4\pi\epsilon^2G'(\epsilon)=-4\pi B=1.
$$

Therefore the [fundamental solution](../../../../fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) in this sign convention is

$$
\boxed{G_0(x,x_0)=-\frac1{4\pi|x-x_0|}.}
$$

Adding point sources outside the domain changes only a [harmonic function](../../../../harmonic-function.md) inside it. The [method of images](../../../../method-of-images.md) selects their positions and strengths to cancel boundary values. The sign reverses for the alternative convention $-\Delta G=\delta$.
