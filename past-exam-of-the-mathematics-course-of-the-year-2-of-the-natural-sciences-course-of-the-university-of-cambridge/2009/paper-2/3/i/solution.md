<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the source be $x_0=(a,b,c)$ with all three coordinates positive. Reflect it independently in the three coordinate planes: the eight points are $x_\epsilon=(\epsilon_1a,\epsilon_2b,\epsilon_3c)$, with $\epsilon_j\in\{1,-1\}$. Assign strength $s_\epsilon=\epsilon_1\epsilon_2\epsilon_3$. Then

$$
\boxed{G(x,x_0)=-\frac1{4\pi}\sum_{\epsilon\in\{\pm1\}^3}\frac{\epsilon_1\epsilon_2\epsilon_3}{|x-x_\epsilon|},\qquad N=7.}
$$

The all-positive point is the original source. On any boundary plane, two terms differing only in that plane's sign have equal distances and opposite strengths, so cancel. All seven additional points lie outside the positive octant, leaving exactly the required interior source. This is the octant [Dirichlet Green function](../../../../../dirichlet-green-function.md).
