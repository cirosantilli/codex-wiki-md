↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The even extension is the [triangular function](../../../../../../triangular-function.md) $f(x)=(1-|x|)$ for $|x|<1$ and zero outside. Its [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) is real and even:

$$
\widetilde f(k)=2\int_0^1(1-x)\cos(kx)\,dx,
\qquad\boxed{\widetilde f(k)=\frac{2(1-\cos k)}{k^2}=\left(\frac{\sin(k/2)}{k/2}\right)^2.}
$$

At zero the removable limiting value is one, also obtained directly by integrating the triangle. This is the [Fourier transform of a triangular function](../../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-triangular-function.md).
