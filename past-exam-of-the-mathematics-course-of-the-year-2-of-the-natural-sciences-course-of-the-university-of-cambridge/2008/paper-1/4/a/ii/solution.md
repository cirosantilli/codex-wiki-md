<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the odd extension, the cosine integral vanishes and the sine integral doubles. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\widetilde f(k)=-2i\int_0^1(1-x)\sin(kx)\,dx,
\qquad\boxed{\widetilde f(k)=-\frac{2i(k-\sin k)}{k^2}.}
$$

The value at $k=0$ is zero by the [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md) symmetry, or by the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) of the sine. The transform is purely imaginary and odd.
