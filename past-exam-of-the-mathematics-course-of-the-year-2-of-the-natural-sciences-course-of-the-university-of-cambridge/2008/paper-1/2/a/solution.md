<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take a nonzero separated solution $\varphi=X(x)Y(y)$. Dividing by $XY$ separates the [Laplacian eigenfunction](../../../../../laplacian-eigenfunction.md) equation into $X''=-\mu X$ and $Y''=-(\lambda-\mu)Y$. The zero-endpoint [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) admit no nonzero linear or hyperbolic factors, so both separation constants must be positive squares. Hence

$$
\boxed{\varphi_{mn}(x,y)=C\sin\frac{m\pi x}{\ell}\sin\frac{n\pi y}{\ell},\qquad
\lambda_{mn}=\frac{\pi^2(m^2+n^2)}{\ell^2},\quad m,n\geq1.}
$$

All separated nonzero solutions have this form. At a repeated [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), linear combinations of the corresponding modes are also solutions, though they need not remain separable.
