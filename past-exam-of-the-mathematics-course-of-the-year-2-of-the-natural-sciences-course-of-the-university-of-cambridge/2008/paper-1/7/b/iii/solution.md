<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exponential [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) gives $e^{-1/z}=\sum_{m=0}^\infty(-1)^m/(m!z^m)$ for $z\ne0$. Hence all singular terms are

$$
\boxed{\sum_{m=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^m}{m!z^m}=-\frac1z+\frac1{2z^2}-\frac1{6z^3}+\cdots.}
$$

Infinitely many negative powers occur, so zero is an [essential singularity](../../../../../../essential-singularity.md).
