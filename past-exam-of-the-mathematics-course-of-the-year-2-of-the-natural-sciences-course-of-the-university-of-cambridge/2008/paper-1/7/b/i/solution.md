<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $w=z-i\pi$. Since $\sinh(w+i\pi)=-\sinh w$ and $\sinh w=w(1+w^2/6+O(w^4))$, the local [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) is

$$
\frac1{\sinh^3z}=-w^{-3}(1+w^2/6+O(w^4))^{-3}=-w^{-3}+\frac12w^{-1}+O(w).
$$

Thus all singular terms are

$$
\boxed{-\frac1{(z-i\pi)^3}+\frac1{2(z-i\pi)}.}
$$

There is a third-order [pole](../../../../../../pole.md), with no second-order term.
