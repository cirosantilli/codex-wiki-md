<h1 id="7/b/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

On a punctured neighborhood of the center, an [essential singularity](../../../../../../essential-singularity.md) occurs exactly when **infinitely many negative-power Laurent coefficients are nonzero**. If all negative-power coefficients vanish, the singularity is removable; if finitely many are nonzero, the singularity is a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md). The punctured-neighborhood assumption matters: an annulus separated from its center cannot by itself classify the function there.
