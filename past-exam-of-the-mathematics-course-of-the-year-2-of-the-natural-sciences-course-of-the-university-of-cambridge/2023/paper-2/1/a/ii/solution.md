<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

First, setting $f=g=y_n$ in the [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) identity shows that $\lambda_n\langle y_n,y_n\rangle_w$ is real, so every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is real. For two [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md),

$$
\lambda_n\langle y_m,y_n\rangle_w
=\langle y_m,\mathcal Ly_n\rangle_w
=\langle\mathcal Ly_m,y_n\rangle_w
=\lambda_m\langle y_m,y_n\rangle_w.
$$

Distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) therefore imply

$$
\boxed{\langle y_m,y_n\rangle_w=\int_a^b y_m^*y_nw\,dx=0.}
$$

This is [orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions](../../../../../../orthogonality-of-sturm-liouville-eigenfunctions.md).
