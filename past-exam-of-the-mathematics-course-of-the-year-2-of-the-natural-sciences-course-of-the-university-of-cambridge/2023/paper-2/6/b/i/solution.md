↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The integration domain is invariant under every [rotation matrix](../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md), and its volume element is unchanged. The substitution $\mathbf y=R\mathbf x$ therefore proves

$$
R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}I_{abcd}=\int_B y_iy_jy_ky_l\,dV_{\mathbf y}=I_{ijkl}.
$$

Thus $I$ is an [isotropic tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md). It is also completely symmetric in its four indices, because the coordinate factors commute. In the general isotropic form, exchanging indices $j,k$ equates $\alpha$ and $\beta$, and exchanging $j,l$ equates $\alpha$ and $\gamma$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{I_{ijkl}=A(\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}),\qquad\alpha=\beta=\gamma=A.}
$$
