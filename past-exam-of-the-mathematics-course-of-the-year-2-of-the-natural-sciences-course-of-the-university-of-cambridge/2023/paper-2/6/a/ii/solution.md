↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use three components of the proposed [isotropic tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) form. Since $T_{1122}=0$, one gets $\alpha=0$. Since $T_{1212}=\epsilon_{123}^2=1$, one gets $\beta=1$. Finally $T_{1221}=\epsilon_{123}\epsilon_{213}=-1$ gives $\gamma=-1$. Hence [contraction of two Levi-Civita symbols](../../../../../../contraction-of-two-levi-civita-symbols.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_{ijm}\epsilon_{klm}=\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}-\delta_{il}\delta_{jk},\qquad(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(0,1,-1).}
$$
