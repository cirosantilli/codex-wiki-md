<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $R$, the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) satisfies

$$
R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{mc}\epsilon_{abc}=(\det R)\epsilon_{ijm}.
$$

Use this twice, and use $R_{mc}R_{mf}=\delta_{cf}$ for the contracted index. It follows that

$$
R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}\epsilon_{abe}\epsilon_{cde}
=(\det R)^2\epsilon_{ijm}\epsilon_{klm}=T_{ijkl}.
$$

Therefore $\boxed{T'_{ijkl}=R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}T_{abcd}=T_{ijkl}}$: it is a fourth-rank [isotropic tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md). Although a single alternating symbol is a [pseudotensor](../../../../../../pseudotensor.md) under reflections, the two determinant factors multiply to one, so this product is an ordinary [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) even for improper orthogonal transformations.
