<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Factor the polynomial denominator as $(z-i)^2(z+i)^2$. The numerator has values $i$ and $-i$ at the two roots, so neither cancels. Therefore

$$
\boxed{z=i\text{ and }z=-i\text{ are double poles}.}
$$

The factor $z^{-1/2}$ gives a [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md) at zero, with local behaviour $f(z)\sim z^{-1/2}$. Infinity is also a [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md), since in the coordinate $w=1/z$ the leading behaviour is $f(1/w)\sim w^{5/2}$. A single-valued branch requires a [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) joining these points. Away from that cut and the two [poles](../../../../../pole.md), the selected branch is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md). For the integral below, use $0<\arg z<2\pi$, putting the cut along the positive real axis.
