<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A function is an [analytic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) at $z_0$ if it is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) throughout some open neighbourhood of $z_0$, equivalently if it has a convergent [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) there. In real components $g=u+iv$, continuous first [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) in a neighbourhood together with the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x}
$$

throughout that neighbourhood are sufficient, and for an analytic function they hold there. Satisfying these equations only at the single point is not enough to establish analyticity in a neighbourhood.
