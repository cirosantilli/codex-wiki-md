<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $s=|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|$. Away from the source, the radial [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) in three dimensions gives

$$
\Delta_{\mathbf r}G_0=\frac1{s^2}\frac d{ds}\left(s^2\frac d{ds}\left[-\frac1{4\pi s}\right]\right)
=\frac1{s^2}\frac d{ds}\frac1{4\pi}=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\Delta G_0=0\text{ for }\mathbf r\ne\mathbf r'}$. At the source it is a [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), not a smooth function; its outward gradient flux through a small sphere is $4\pi s^2/(4\pi s^2)=1$, giving $\Delta G_0=\delta$.
