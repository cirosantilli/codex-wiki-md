↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [character of a representation](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of its representing matrix. For the rotation powers,

$$
\chi(U^j)=1+2\cos(j\pi/2).
$$

Each $U^jV$ has a planar reflection block of trace zero and third diagonal entry $-1$, giving character $-1$. In the chosen element order,

$$
\boxed{(\chi(g_0),\ldots,\chi(g_7))=(3,1,-1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1).}
$$

Equivalently its values on the [conjugacy classes](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) $\{I\}$, $\{U^2\}$, $\{U,U^3\}$, $\{V,U^2V\}$, $\{UV,U^3V\}$ are $3,-1,1,-1,-1$.
