<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $H\leq G$ and $g\in G$, the left and right [cosets](../../../../../coset.md) are $gH=\{gh:h\in H\}$ and $Hg=\{hg:h\in H\}$. A [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) satisfies $gHg^{-1}=H$ for every $g$. Multiplying this set equality on the right by $g$ gives

$$
\boxed{gH=Hg\quad\text{for every }g\in G.}
$$

Conversely this equality implies $gHg^{-1}=H$, so it is an equivalent definition of normality.
