<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Subtraction fails [associativity](../../../../../../associative-property.md): $(0*0)*1=-1$ but $0*(0*1)=1$. Thus it violates the [group axioms](../../../../../../group-axioms.md) and is **not a group**. It has a right identity $0$, but no element can be a two-sided [identity element](../../../../../../identity-element.md).
