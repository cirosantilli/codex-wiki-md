<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The operation is not defined for every pair in the proposed set: choosing $a=1,b=-1$ makes its denominator zero. Thus it is not even a [binary operation](../../../../../../binary-operation.md) on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$, and is **not a group**. In particular, removing zero from the domain does not prevent the sum in the denominator from vanishing.
