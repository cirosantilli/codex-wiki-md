<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The substitution $F(a)=e^a$, with $F(-\infty)=0$, is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) onto $[0,\infty)$ and obeys

$$
F(a*b)=F(a)+F(b).
$$

Thus the [log-sum-exp function](../../../../../../log-sum-exp-function.md) transfers nonnegative addition to the given set. It is closed, associative and commutative, with [identity element](../../../../../../identity-element.md) $-\infty$. But a finite $a$ has $e^a>0$, and adding a nonnegative number cannot give zero, so no finite $a$ has an [inverse element](../../../../../../inverse-element.md). Therefore it is a [commutative monoid](../../../../../../commutative-monoid.md) but **not a group**.
