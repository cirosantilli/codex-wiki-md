<h1 id="8/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The sum of two [odd integers](../../../../../../odd-integer.md) plus one is odd, so the operation is closed. Both associations of a triple give $a+b+c+2$, proving [associativity](../../../../../../associative-property.md). Its [identity element](../../../../../../identity-element.md) is $-1$, and the [inverse element](../../../../../../inverse-element.md) of $a$ is $-a-2$, also odd, since $a+(-a-2)+1=-1$. Thus it is an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md).

More explicitly, [shifted addition on odd integers](../../../../../../shifted-addition-on-odd-integers.md) is ordinary integer addition in different coordinates:

$$
\boxed{F(a)=\frac{a+1}{2},\qquad F(a*b)=F(a)+F(b).}
$$

This [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) is a [group isomorphism](../../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) to $(\mathbb Z,+)$.
