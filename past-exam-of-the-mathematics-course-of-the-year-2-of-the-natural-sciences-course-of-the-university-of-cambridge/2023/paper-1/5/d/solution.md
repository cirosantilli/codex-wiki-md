<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The required actions have already been identified:

$$
\boxed{Mu_1=\mu_1v_1,\qquad Mv_1=-\mu_1u_1.}
$$

In three dimensions choose a unit [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $w$ perpendicular to both. Its one-dimensional [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) to the pair is invariant under $M$. On a one-dimensional real space, a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) must act as zero: $Mw=\eta w$ would imply $\eta=w^TMw=0$. Thus $Mw=0$.

The columns of $W=(u_1\ v_1\ w)$ form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), so $W$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md). The displayed actions give

$$
W^TMW=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\mu_1&0\\\mu_1&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}=B,
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{M=WBW^T.}
$$

If $M=0$, take $\mu_1=0$ and any [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $W$. Otherwise its nonzero two-dimensional block supplies the pair used above.
