↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $t<0$, all forcing points satisfy $\tau>t$, so the [Green's function](../../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) formula becomes

$$
x(t)=-\frac14e^{2t}\int_0^1\tau e^{-2\tau}\,d\tau.
$$

An [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\int\tau e^{-2\tau}d\tau=-(\tau/2+1/4)e^{-2\tau}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{x(t)=-\frac{1-3e^{-2}}{16}e^{2t},\qquad t<0.}
$$

The exponential has the required decay as $t\to-\infty$.
