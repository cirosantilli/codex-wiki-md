<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $t>1$, every forcing point has $\tau<t$. Hence the [Green's function](../../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) gives

$$
x(t)=-\frac14e^{-2t}\int_0^1\tau e^{2\tau}\,d\tau.
$$

Since $\int\tau e^{2\tau}d\tau=(\tau/2-1/4)e^{2\tau}$, the remaining [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) is $(e^2+1)/4$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{x(t)=-\frac{e^2+1}{16}e^{-2t},\qquad t>1.}
$$

This decays at positive infinity.
