<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The three [coordinate basis](../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) vectors are the partial [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) of the position [vector](../../../../../vector.md) with respect to the three coordinates. Direct differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf h_\xi=\frac{\partial\mathbf x}{\partial\xi}=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh\xi\sin\theta\cos\phi\\\cosh\xi\sin\theta\sin\phi\\g'(\xi)\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},\qquad\mathbf h_\theta=\frac{\partial\mathbf x}{\partial\theta}=\begin{pmatrix}\sinh\xi\cos\theta\cos\phi\\\sinh\xi\cos\theta\sin\phi\\-g(\xi)\sin\theta\end{pmatrix},}
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathbf h_\phi=\frac{\partial\mathbf x}{\partial\phi}=\begin{pmatrix}-\sinh\xi\sin\theta\sin\phi\\\sinh\xi\sin\theta\cos\phi\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The multivariable [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) then gives $d\mathbf x=\mathbf h_\xi d\xi+\mathbf h_\theta d\theta+\mathbf h_\phi d\phi$. These are coordinate [tangent vectors](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md), not necessarily unit [vectors](../../../../../vector.md).
