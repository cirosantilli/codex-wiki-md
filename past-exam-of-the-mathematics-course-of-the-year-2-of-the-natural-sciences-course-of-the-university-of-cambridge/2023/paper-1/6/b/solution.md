<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) has real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md)

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}5&-4&0\\-4&5&0\\0&0&9\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its unit [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) can be chosen as $e_u=(1,1,0)/\sqrt2$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$, and $e_v=(1,-1,0)/\sqrt2$, $e_w=(0,0,1)$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $9$. Set

$$
u=\frac{x_1+x_2}{\sqrt2},\qquad v=\frac{x_1-x_2}{\sqrt2},\qquad w=x_3.
$$

The [orthogonal diagonalization](../../../../../orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md) turns the surface equation into

$$
u^2+9v^2+9w^2=9,\qquad\boxed{\frac{u^2}{9}+v^2+w^2=1.}
$$

It is an [ellipsoid](../../../../../ellipsoid.md), specifically a [prolate spheroid](../../../../../prolate-spheroid.md) centered at the origin. Its long principal axis is the line in direction $\boxed{(1,1,0)/\sqrt2}$, with semiaxis length $\boxed{3}$. Each direction in the plane perpendicular to that line has semiaxis length $\boxed{1}$; the displayed $e_v,e_w$ give one orthonormal choice of the two short principal axes. Their nonuniqueness reflects the repeated [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $9$ and rotational symmetry about the long axis.
