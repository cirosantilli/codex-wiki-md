<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By the two [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) rules in part (b),

$$
\mathcal F[f'']=-k^2\widetilde f,\qquad\mathcal F[x^2f]=i^2\widetilde f''=-\widetilde f''.
$$

Consequently transforming the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) gives

$$
-k^2\widetilde f+\widetilde f''=\mu\widetilde f,
$$

or

$$
\boxed{\widetilde f''(k)-k^2\widetilde f(k)=\mu\widetilde f(k).}
$$

The inverse [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) gives the reverse implication under the same regularity hypotheses. This expresses the Fourier symmetry of the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) operator.
