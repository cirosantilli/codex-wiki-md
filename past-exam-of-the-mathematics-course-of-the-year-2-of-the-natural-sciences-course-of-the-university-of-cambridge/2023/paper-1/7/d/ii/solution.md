<h1 id="7/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) to the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) coefficients $a_n=[n/(n+\alpha)]^{n^2}$ for $n\geq1$:

$$
|a_n|^{1/n}=\left(1+\frac\alpha n\right)^{-n}\longrightarrow e^{-\alpha}.
$$

For example, taking logarithms gives $-n\log(1+\alpha/n)\to-\alpha$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R=e^\alpha.}
$$

Any convention for the isolated $n=0$ coefficient has no effect on the [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md).
