<h1 id="7/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The denominator vanishes at $z=\pm i\sqrt2$, giving two [simple poles](../../../../../../simple-pole.md). Their distances from the center are both $|1\mp i\sqrt2|=\sqrt3$. There are no nearer singularities, so the [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) has [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md)

$$
\boxed{R=\sqrt3.}
$$
