<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose $g\ne e$ in a group of prime [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) $p$. Its [order of a group element](../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) divides $p$ by (c) and is not $1$, hence equals $p$. Consequently its [cyclic subgroup](../../../../../cyclic-subgroup.md) already contains all elements of $G$:

$$
\boxed{G=\langle g\rangle\cong C_p.}
$$

Thus every nonidentity element is a [group generator](../../../../../generator-of-a-group.md).
