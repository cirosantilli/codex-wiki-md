<h1 id="8/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

By (d), every group of [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) three is a [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md). Writing its elements as $e,a,a^2$, every product is determined by $a^3=e$; the [multiplication table](../../../../../multiplication-table.md) is

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
\cdot&e&a&a^2\\\hline
e&e&a&a^2\\
a&a&a^2&e\\
a^2&a^2&e&a
\end{array}
$$

The map $a^j\mapsto j\pmod3$ is a bijective [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) to addition modulo three. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G\cong\mathbb Z_3,}
$$

so there is exactly one such [group](../../../../../group-split.md) **up to isomorphism**.
