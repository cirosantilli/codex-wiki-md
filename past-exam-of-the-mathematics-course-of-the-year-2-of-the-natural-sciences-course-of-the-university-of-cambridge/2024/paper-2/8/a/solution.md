↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) is its [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md), $\boxed{|G|=\#\{g:g\in G\}}$. The [order of a group element](../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) $g$ is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(g)=\min\{n\geq1:g^n=e\},}
$$

where $e$ is the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md). In a [finite group](../../../../../finite-group.md) such an $n$ always exists, since two nonnegative powers of $g$ must coincide.
