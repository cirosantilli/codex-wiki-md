<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $d=\operatorname{ord}(g)$. The powers

$$
\langle g\rangle=\{e,g,\ldots,g^{d-1}\}
$$

are distinct: equality of two with exponents between $0$ and $d-1$ would give a smaller positive power equal to $e$. Reducing exponents modulo $d$ proves closure and supplies inverses, so this is a [cyclic subgroup](../../../../../cyclic-subgroup.md) of [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) $d$. [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(g)=|\langle g\rangle|\mid |G|.}
$$
