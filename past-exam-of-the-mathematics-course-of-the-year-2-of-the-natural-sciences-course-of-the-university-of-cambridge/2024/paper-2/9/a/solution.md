<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Both [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) contain the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md), so $H\cap K$ is nonempty. If $x,y\in H\cap K$, then $xy\in H$ and $xy\in K$, so $xy\in H\cap K$. Similarly $x^{-1}$ belongs to both subgroups and hence to their intersection. [Associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) is inherited from $G$. Thus

$$
\boxed{H\cap K\leq G.}
$$

This verifies the [group axioms](../../../../../group-axioms.md); finiteness is unnecessary.
