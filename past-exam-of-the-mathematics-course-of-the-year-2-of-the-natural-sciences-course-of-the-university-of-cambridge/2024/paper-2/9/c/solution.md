<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [center of a group](../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) contains $e$. If $z,w$ lie in the center, then for every $g\in G$,

$$
g(zw)g^{-1}=(gzg^{-1})(gwg^{-1})=zw,
\qquad
gz^{-1}g^{-1}=(gzg^{-1})^{-1}=z^{-1}.
$$

It is therefore closed under the [group operation](../../../../../group-operation.md) and [inverse elements](../../../../../inverse-element.md), and inherits [associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md), proving it is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md). Each of its elements commutes with every element of $G$, in particular with every other central element. Hence

$$
\boxed{Z(G)\leq G\text{ and }zw=wz\text{ for all }z,w\in Z(G).}
$$

Thus the center is an [abelian subgroup](../../../../../abelian-subgroup.md).
