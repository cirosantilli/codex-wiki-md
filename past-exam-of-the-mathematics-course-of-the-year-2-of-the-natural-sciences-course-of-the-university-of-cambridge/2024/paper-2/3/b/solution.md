↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Dirichlet Green function](../../../../../dirichlet-green-function.md) is the solution, with the source point held fixed, of

$$
\boxed{\Delta_{\mathbf r}G(\mathbf r;\mathbf r')=\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\text{ in }S,\qquad G(\mathbf r;\mathbf r')=0\text{ on }C.}
$$

Its singularity is the logarithmic one found in (a); subtracting that [fundamental solution](../../../../../fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) leaves a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md). An unbounded region also requires a condition at infinity, here boundedness.
