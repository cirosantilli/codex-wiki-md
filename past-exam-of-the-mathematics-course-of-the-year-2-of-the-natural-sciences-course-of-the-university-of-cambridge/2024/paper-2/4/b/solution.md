<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [simple pole](../../../../../simple-pole.md) at $z_0$ means that $f$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) in a punctured neighbourhood and $(z-z_0)f(z)$ extends holomorphically to a nonzero value at $z_0$. Equivalently its [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) begins with $a_{-1}/(z-z_0)$ and has no higher negative powers. The [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}(f,z_0)=\lim_{z\to z_0}(z-z_0)f(z).}
$$

In particular, if $f=g/h$ with $g(z_0)\ne0$, $h(z_0)=0$ and $h'(z_0)\ne0$, this becomes $\operatorname{Res}(f,z_0)=g(z_0)/h'(z_0)$. The associated [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) is

$$
\boxed{\oint_C f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_{z_j\text{ inside }C}\operatorname{Res}(f,z_j)}
$$

for a positively oriented simple [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) with no poles on it.
