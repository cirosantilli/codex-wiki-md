<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a Cartesian coordinate change $x'_i=R_{ij}x_j$ with $R$ an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), a rank-$n$ [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{T'_{i_1\cdots i_n}=R_{i_1j_1}\cdots R_{i_nj_n}T_{j_1\cdots j_n},}
$$

with repeated indices summed according to [Einstein notation](../../../../../einstein-notation.md). A [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) is **isotropic** if $T'_{i_1\cdots i_n}=T_{i_1\cdots i_n}$ for every proper [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) $R$. Under proper rotations the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is an [isotropic third-rank tensor](../../../../../isotropic-third-rank-tensor.md); if reflections are included, it is a [pseudotensor](../../../../../pseudotensor.md). The convention using proper rotations is the one relevant to the rank-three classification here.
