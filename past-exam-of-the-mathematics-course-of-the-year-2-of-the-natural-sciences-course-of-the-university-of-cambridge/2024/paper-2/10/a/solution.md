<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) preserves multiplication:

$$
\boxed{\Phi(g_1g_2)=\Phi(g_1)\Phi(g_2)\quad\text{for every }g_1,g_2\in G.}
$$

It automatically sends the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) to the identity and sends [inverse elements](../../../../../inverse-element.md) to inverses. A [group isomorphism](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) is a **bijective group homomorphism**. For finite groups this requires equal [group orders](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md), and, once those orders agree, either injectivity or surjectivity of the [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) is sufficient. Equal orders alone do not suffice.
