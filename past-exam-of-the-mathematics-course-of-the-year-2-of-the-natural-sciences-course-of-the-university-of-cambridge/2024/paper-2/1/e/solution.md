<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For an admissible nonzero real trial function, [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) and the zero-[flux](../../../../../flux.md) [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) reduce the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) to

$$
\frac{\int_{-L}^Ly\mathcal Ly\,dx}{\int_{-L}^Lwy^2\,dx}
=\frac{\int_{-L}^Lp(y')^2\,dx}{\int_{-L}^Lwy^2\,dx}\geq0.
$$

A nonzero constant trial function attains zero. Positivity of $p$ also shows that equality requires $y'=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda_0=0,\qquad f_0(x)=C,\qquad c_{\rm eq}(x)=C e^{-\beta V(x)}.}
$$

If the conserved total amount is $M=\int_{-L}^Lc\,dx$, the normalized [stationary solution](../../../../../stationary-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{c_{\rm eq}(x)=\frac{M e^{-\beta V(x)}}{\int_{-L}^Le^{-\beta V(s)}\,ds}.}
$$

Its [diffusive flux](../../../../../diffusive-flux.md) vanishes everywhere. The [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) fixes the spatial profile, while the [diffusivity](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) affects the relaxation toward it. Boundedness of $V$ makes the normalization finite on this finite interval.
