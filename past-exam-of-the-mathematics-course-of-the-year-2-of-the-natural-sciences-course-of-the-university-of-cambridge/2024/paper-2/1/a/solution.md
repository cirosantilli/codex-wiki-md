<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitution into the [diffusive flux](../../../../../diffusive-flux.md) gives

$$
J(x,t)=-e^{-\lambda t}D(x)\bigl(u'(x)+\beta V'(x)u(x)\bigr).
$$

The [continuity equation](../../../../../continuity-equation.md) for this [drift-diffusion equation with a potential](../../../../../drift-diffusion-equation-with-a-potential.md) therefore reduces, after cancelling the nonzero exponential, to

$$
\boxed{\bigl[D(u'+\beta V'u)\bigr]'=-\lambda u.}
$$

The reflecting [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) become $D(u'+\beta V'u)=0$ at both endpoints. Thus each solution of this [eigenvalue problem](../../../../../eigenvalue-problem.md) supplies a separated solution of the original [diffusion equation](../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md).
