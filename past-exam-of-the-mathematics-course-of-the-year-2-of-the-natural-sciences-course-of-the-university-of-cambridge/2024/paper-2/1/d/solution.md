<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Apply the identity from (c) to the two real [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md). The equations of their [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) give

$$
0=\int_{-L}^L\left(f_n\mathcal Lf_m-f_m\mathcal Lf_n\right)dx
=(\lambda_m-\lambda_n)\int_{-L}^Lwf_nf_m\,dx.
$$

Since the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) differ,

$$
\boxed{\int_{-L}^Lw(x)f_n(x)f_m(x)\,dx=0.}
$$

This is [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) in the [weighted inner product](../../../../../weighted-inner-product.md).
