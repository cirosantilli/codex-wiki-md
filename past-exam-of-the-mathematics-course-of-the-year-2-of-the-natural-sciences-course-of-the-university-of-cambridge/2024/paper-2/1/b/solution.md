<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) makes the drift term cancel:

$$
u'+\beta V'u=e^{-\beta V}y'.
$$

Consequently the transformed [eigenvalue problem](../../../../../eigenvalue-problem.md) is

$$
-\bigl(De^{-\beta V}y'\bigr)'=\lambda e^{-\beta V}y,
\qquad
\boxed{p=De^{-\beta V},\quad q=0,\quad w=e^{-\beta V}.}
$$

Both $p$ and the weight $w$ are positive, as required for this [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md).
