<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an exponential trial $y=e^{rx}$, the [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
r^2+2ar+a^2+b^2=(r+a)^2+b^2=0.
$$

Its distinct complex [roots](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) are $r=-a\pm ib$. Thus the general real solution of the homogeneous [second-order linear differential equation](../../../../../second-order-linear-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_h(x)=e^{-ax}[C_1\cos(bx)+C_2\sin(bx)].}
$$

The two displayed solutions have nonzero [Wronskian](../../../../../wronskian.md) $be^{-2ax}$ and hence [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md). The same formula permits complex solutions when $C_1,C_2$ are complex.
