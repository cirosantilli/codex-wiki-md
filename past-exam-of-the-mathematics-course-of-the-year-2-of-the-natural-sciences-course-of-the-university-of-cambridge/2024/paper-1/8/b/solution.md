<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $t=x-m$ and use the [Frobenius solution](../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) $y=t^\nu(a_0+a_1t+\cdots)$ with $a_0\ne0$. From part (a), the leading coefficients are $P\sim1/t$ and $Q\sim m^2\omega^2/t^2$. The coefficient of the lowest power $t^{\nu-2}$ is therefore

$$
[\nu(\nu-1)+\nu+m^2\omega^2]a_0.
$$

The [indicial equation](../../../../../indicial-equation.md) reduces to $\nu^2+m^2\omega^2=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\nu_+=im\omega,\qquad\nu_-=-im\omega.}
$$

Since $m\omega>0$, the two [indicial roots](../../../../../indicial-root.md) differ by a nonzero imaginary number, not an integer. Thus the two local [Frobenius solutions](../../../../../frobenius-solution.md) exist independently on a chosen branch of $\log(x-m)$.
