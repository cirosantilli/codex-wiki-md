Here $A^2=-I$. Splitting the [matrix exponential](../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) into even and odd powers therefore gives

$$
U=I\cos1+A\sin1=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}\cos1&\sin1\\-\sin1&\cos1\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

For the second [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md), $(I+A)(I-A)=I-A^2=2I$, so $(I+A)^{-1}=(I-A)/2$. Hence

$$
V=\frac12(I-A)^2=\frac12(I-2A+A^2)=-A=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Both are real [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), and hence also [unitary matrices](../../../../../unitary-matrix.md).
