<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$. Taking the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) with $v$ gives $v^\dagger Av=\lambda\|v\|^2$. For an [anti-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md),

$$
\overline{v^\dagger Av}=v^\dagger A^\dagger v=-v^\dagger Av.
$$

Consequently $\overline\lambda\|v\|^2=-\lambda\|v\|^2$. As $\|v\|^2>0$, the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) satisfies $\boxed{\overline\lambda=-\lambda}$, or $\boxed{\lambda=i\theta\text{ with }\theta\in\mathbb R}$. Zero is included as a purely imaginary [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
