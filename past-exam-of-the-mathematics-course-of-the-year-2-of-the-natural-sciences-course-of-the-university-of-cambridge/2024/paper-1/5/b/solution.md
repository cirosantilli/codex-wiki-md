<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $I+A$ were a [singular matrix](../../../../../singular-matrix.md), a nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ would satisfy $(I+A)v=0$, so $Av=-v$. This would make $-1$ an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the [anti-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md), contrary to part (a). Thus $\boxed{I+A\text{ is invertible}}$. The same argument shows $I-A$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), which will also be used below.
