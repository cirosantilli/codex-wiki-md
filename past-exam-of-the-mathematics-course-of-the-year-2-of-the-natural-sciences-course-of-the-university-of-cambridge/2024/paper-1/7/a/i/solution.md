<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $z=x+iy$ and square the [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) equation. It becomes

$$
x^2+(y+b)^2=P^2[x^2+(y-b)^2].
$$

For $P\ne1$, completing the square gives the [Apollonius circle](../../../../../../circles-of-apollonius.md)

$$
x^2+\left(y-\frac{b(P^2+1)}{P^2-1}\right)^2=\frac{4b^2P^2}{(P^2-1)^2}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{center }\left(0,\frac{b(P^2+1)}{P^2-1}\right),\qquad\text{radius }\frac{2bP}{|P^2-1|}.}
$$

The printed statement needs the exception $\boxed{P=1\ \Longrightarrow\ y=0}$: equal distances from $ib$ and $-ib$ give their perpendicular bisector, a straight line rather than a finite-radius [circle](../../../../../../circle.md). For $P>1$ the center lies in the upper half-plane; this is the case needed in part (ii). The original PDF confirms the numerator $z+ib$, correcting the duplicated, corrupted OCR formula.
