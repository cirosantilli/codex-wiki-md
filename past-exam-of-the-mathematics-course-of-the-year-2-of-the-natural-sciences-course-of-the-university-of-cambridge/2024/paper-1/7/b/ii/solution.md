<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For real $z>0$, apply [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) to the [gamma integral](../../../../../../gamma-integral.md) with $u=t^z$ and $dv=e^{-t}dt$:

$$
\Gamma(z+1)=\int_0^{\infty}t^ze^{-t}\,dt=[-t^ze^{-t}]_0^{\infty}+z\int_0^{\infty}t^{z-1}e^{-t}\,dt.
$$

The boundary term vanishes: $t^z\to0$ at zero, and exponential decay dominates the power at infinity. This proves the [Gamma function recurrence](../../../../../../gamma-function-recurrence.md)

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(z+1)=z\Gamma(z).}
$$
