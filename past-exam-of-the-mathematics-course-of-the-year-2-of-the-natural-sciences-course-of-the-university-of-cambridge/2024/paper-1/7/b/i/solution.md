<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Substituting $z=1$ into the defining [gamma integral](../../../../../../gamma-integral.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(1)=\int_0^{\infty}e^{-t}\,dt=[-e^{-t}]_0^{\infty}=1.}
$$

Both endpoints are convergent, so no continuation of the [Gamma function](../../../../../../gamma-function.md) is needed here.
