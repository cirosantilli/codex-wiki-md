<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [divergence of a cross product](../../../../../divergence-of-a-cross-product.md) and $\nabla\times\mathbf a=0$ give $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf A)=-\mathbf a\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)$. On the boundary the [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) gives $(\mathbf a\times\mathbf A)\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=-\mathbf a\cdot(\hat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf A)$. Thus the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives

$$
-\mathbf a\cdot\int_V\nabla\times\mathbf A\,dV=-\mathbf a\cdot\int_S\hat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf A\,dS.
$$

Because $\mathbf a$ is arbitrary, the [curl](../../../../../curl.md) identity follows:

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\times\mathbf A\,dV=\int_S\hat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf A\,dS.}
$$
