<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf u$ on a bounded region $V\subset\mathbb R^3$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary $S=\partial V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf u\,dV=\int_S\mathbf u\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS=\int_S\mathbf u\cdot d\mathbf S.}
$$

Here $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$ is its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md), $dV$ is the volume element, $dS$ is the surface area element, $\hat{\mathbf n}$ is the outward [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md), and $d\mathbf S=\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS$. Thus a [volume integral](../../../../../volume-integral.md) of the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) equals the outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md) through the boundary.
