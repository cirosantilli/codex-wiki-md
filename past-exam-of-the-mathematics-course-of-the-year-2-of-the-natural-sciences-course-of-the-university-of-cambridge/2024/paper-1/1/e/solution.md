<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) to the [tensor field](../../../../../tensor-field.md), without assuming any symmetry of $\Sigma$:

$$
\partial_j(r_i\partial_k\Sigma_{kj})=\delta_{ij}\partial_k\Sigma_{kj}+r_i\partial_j\partial_k\Sigma_{kj}=\partial_k\Sigma_{ki}+r_i\partial_j\partial_k\Sigma_{kj}.
$$

Integrating, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) converts each of the two [divergences](../../../../../divergence.md) into a boundary term:

$$
\boxed{\int_Vr_i\partial_j\partial_k\Sigma_{kj}\,dV=\int_Sr_i\partial_k\Sigma_{kj}\,dS_j-\int_S\Sigma_{ki}\,dS_k.}
$$

The repeated indices are summed according to [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../einstein-notation.md), while $i$ labels the component of the resulting [vector](../../../../../vector.md) identity.
