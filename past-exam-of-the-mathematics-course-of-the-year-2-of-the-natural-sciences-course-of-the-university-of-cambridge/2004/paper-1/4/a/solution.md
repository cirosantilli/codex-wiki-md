<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) obeys $H^\dagger=H$, where the dagger is conjugate transpose. If $Hv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, then $v^\dagger Hv=\lambda v^\dagger v$. This scalar is real because its complex conjugate equals $v^\dagger H^\dagger v=v^\dagger Hv$. Since $v^\dagger v>0$, the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ is real.

For the [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md), conjugate symmetry of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives

$$
(G^\dagger)_{ij}=\overline{G_{ji}}=\overline{\langle u_j,u_i\rangle}=\langle u_i,u_j\rangle=G_{ij}.
$$

Thus $G$ is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). The assumption that the vectors form a basis additionally makes $G$ positive definite, though only Hermiticity is needed here.
