A [regular singular point](../../../../../regular-singular-point.md) is a nonordinary point $x_0$ for which $(x-x_0)p(x)$ and $(x-x_0)^2q(x)$ extend analytically across $x_0$. These pole restrictions permit the [Frobenius method](../../../../../frobenius-method.md).
