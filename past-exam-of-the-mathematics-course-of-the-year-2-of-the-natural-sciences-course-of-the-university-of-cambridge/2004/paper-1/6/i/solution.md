↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [ordinary point](../../../../../ordinary-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) of the normalized second-order equation is a point where both coefficient functions $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ are analytic in a neighborhood. Ordinary continuity is enough for a local existence theorem, but analyticity is the condition used for a convergent local [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) solution.
