<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the integrand has no explicit $x$ dependence, differentiate $f-y'f_{y'}$ along a stationary path:

$$
\frac d{dx}(f-y'f_{y'})=f_x+y'\left(f_y-\frac d{dx}f_{y'}\right)=0.
$$

The last equality uses $f_x=0$ and the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). This proves the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../beltrami-identity.md), the [first integral](../../../../../first-integral.md) $\boxed{f-y'f_{y'}=C}$.
