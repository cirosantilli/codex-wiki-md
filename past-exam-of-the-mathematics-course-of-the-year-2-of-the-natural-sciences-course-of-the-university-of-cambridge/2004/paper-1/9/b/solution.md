↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $f$ has no explicit $y$ dependence, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) becomes $d(f_{y'})/dx=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{f_{y'}=C.}
$$

The variable $y$ is then a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md). This is the case used for the azimuth in the ramp calculation.
