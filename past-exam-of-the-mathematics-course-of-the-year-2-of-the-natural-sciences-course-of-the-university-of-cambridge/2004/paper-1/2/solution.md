<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

With constant positive [thermal diffusivity](../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) $\nu$, no internal heat source and one spatial coordinate, the [heat equation](../../../../heat-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\theta=\nu\partial_x^2\theta,\qquad0<x<L.}
$$

[Separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md) makes each spatial mode decay by $e^{-\nu k^2t}$. The endpoint conditions determine whether the initial triangular profile is expanded in a [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) or a [Fourier cosine series](../../../../fourier-cosine-series.md).
