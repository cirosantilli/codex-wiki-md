<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the argument interval $-3\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$, which makes the logarithm real on the positive real axis and puts its cut on the positive imaginary axis. Parametrize $z=Re^{i\theta}$. The constant term $\ln R$ contributes $\ln R\oint dz=0$, leaving

$$
I=-R\int_{-3\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\theta e^{i\theta}d\theta=\boxed{-2\pi R.}
$$

Equivalently the cut-crossing point is $iR$, so the [circle integral of a logarithm with a radial branch cut](../../../../../../circle-integral-of-a-logarithm-with-a-radial-branch-cut.md) is $2\pi i(iR)=-2\pi R$. The different cut changes where the discontinuity lies on the circle; it is not merely an additive constant change of branch along the entire contour.
