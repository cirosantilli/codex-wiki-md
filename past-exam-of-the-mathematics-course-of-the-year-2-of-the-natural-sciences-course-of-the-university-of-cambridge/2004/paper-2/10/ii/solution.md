<h1 id="10/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $h=aga^{-1}$, the representation law gives $D(h)=D(a)D(g)D(a)^{-1}$. Trace invariance under this [similarity transformation](../../../../../similarity-transformation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\chi_D(h)=\chi_D(g).}
$$

Thus the [character of a representation](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is constant on every [conjugacy class](../../../../../conjugacy-class.md).
