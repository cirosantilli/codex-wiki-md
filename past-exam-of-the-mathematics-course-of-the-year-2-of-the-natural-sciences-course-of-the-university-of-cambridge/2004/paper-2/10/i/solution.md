<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is invariant under a [similarity transformation](../../../../../similarity-transformation.md), by cyclicity of the trace:

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\widetilde D}(g)=\operatorname{tr}(SD(g)S^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(D(g)S^{-1}S)=\chi_D(g).}
$$

Hence [equivalent representations](../../../../../equivalence-of-group-representations.md) have the same [character of a representation](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md).
