↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

A [group representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is a homomorphism $D:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ into invertible linear maps. It is a [faithful representation](../../../../faithful-representation.md) when it is injective, equivalently when its kernel contains only the identity. A faithful real two-dimensional representation of the [cyclic group of order four](../../../../cyclic-group-of-order-four.md) is

$$
\boxed{D(1)=I,\quad D(i)=J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad D(-1)=-I,\quad D(-i)=-J.}
$$

These are rotations of the plane through $0,\pi/2,\pi,3\pi/2$. Since $J^2=-I$ and $J^4=I$, their multiplication agrees with the group, and all four matrices are distinct.

For any invertible $S$, set $\widetilde D(g)=SD(g)S^{-1}$. Then

$$
\widetilde D(g)\widetilde D(h)=SD(g)S^{-1}SD(h)S^{-1}=SD(gh)S^{-1}=\widetilde D(gh),
$$

so this also is a [group representation](../../../../group-representation.md). The [character of a representation](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $\chi_D(g)=\operatorname{tr}D(g)$. For the displayed quarter-turn representation, its values on $1,i,-1,-i$ are $2,0,-2,0$.
