# Airy function asymptotics in the Digital Library of Mathematical Functions

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)

[Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, section 9.7](https://dlmf.nist.gov/9.7) gives the [Airy function](airy-function.md) [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion-split.md). Equations 9.7.5 and 9.7.7 give the positive-argument exponential forms; equations 9.7.9 and 9.7.11 give the negative-argument oscillatory forms. Their leading terms determine the factor two and phase shift in the [Airy turning-point connection formula](airy-turning-point-connection-formula.md).
