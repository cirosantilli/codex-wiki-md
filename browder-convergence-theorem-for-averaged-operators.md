# Browder convergence theorem for averaged operators

↑ **Parent:** [Averaged operator](averaged-operator.md)

In finite-dimensional Euclidean space, iterating an [averaged operator](averaged-operator.md) with a nonempty fixed-point set converges in norm to a fixed point from every start. The [averaged-operator inequality](averaged-operator-inequality.md) proves boundedness and a vanishing residual; compactness produces a fixed cluster point and [Fejér monotonicity](fejer-monotonicity.md) turns subsequential convergence into convergence of the full sequence. A fixed point must exist.
