# Pitch of a helix

↑ **Parent:** [Helix](helix.md)

The pitch is the axial displacement per complete turn. For a circular [helix](helix.md) with $z=a\phi+b$, the signed pitch for increasing angle is $2\pi a$, and its magnitude is $2\pi|a|$. Pitch is independent of the cylinder radius.
