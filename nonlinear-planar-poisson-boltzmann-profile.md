# Nonlinear planar Poisson-Boltzmann profile

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

For a symmetric monovalent point-ion electrolyte next to a plane, define $u=e\psi/(k_BT)$ and $\kappa_D^2=2e^2n_\infty/(\varepsilon k_BT)$, with $n_\infty$ the [density](density.md) of each sign. The [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) becomes $u''=\kappa_D^2\sinh u$. Multiplying by $u'$ and matching zero field and potential in the bulk gives $(u')^2=2\kappa_D^2(\cosh u-1)$. The decaying branch integrates to the displayed profile, reducing to $u_0e^{-\kappa_Dz}$ at weak potential. This solution assumes a salt reservoir and is distinct from a counterion-only [diffuse layer](diffuse-layer.md).
