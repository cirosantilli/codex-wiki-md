# Local spin constraint in the Schwinger boson representation

↑ **Parent:** [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md)

The [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md) satisfies $S_n^2=(N_n/2)(N_n/2+1)$, where $N_n$ is the local total [boson](boson.md) [number operator](number-operator.md). Thus a prescribed local [spin](spin.md) $s$ requires the displayed operator constraint at every site. A condition on $\langle N_n\rangle$ alone is insufficient: for $\langle N_n\rangle=2s$, $\langle S_n^2\rangle=s(s+1)+\operatorname{Var}(N_n)/4$. Projecting onto the required [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) at each site removes the unwanted local [irreducible spin representations](irreducible-spin-representation.md).
