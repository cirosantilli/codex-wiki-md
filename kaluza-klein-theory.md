# Kaluza-Klein theory

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluza-Klein_theory)

Kaluza-Klein theory obtains lower-dimensional fields from a higher-dimensional metric and other fields on compact dimensions. In the displayed fixed-radius circle ansatz, the off-diagonal metric supplies a vector. The coordinate change $y\mapsto y+\lambda(x)$ is compensated by $A\mapsto A-d\lambda$, producing an Abelian [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md). Allowing the radius to vary adds a scalar modulus. [Kaluza-Klein modes](kaluza-klein-mode.md) also include nonzero internal momenta, giving a tower of massive fields.
