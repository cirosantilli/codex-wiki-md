# Mixed Green function for an Euler operator with exponents one and two

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md)

[variation of parameters](variation-of-parameters.md) with the fundamental solutions $x,x^2$ gives

$$
G(x,t)=\frac1{3t^3}
\begin{cases}(x^2-x)(t-4),&x\leq t,\\
(t-1)(x^2-4x),&t\leq x.\end{cases}
$$

The kernel is continuous across $x=t$ and its first derivative jumps by $t^{-2}$, which gives [unit in a ring](unit-in-a-ring.md) source after multiplication by the leading coefficient. It satisfies both endpoint conditions. The homogeneous solution $Ax+Bx^2$ satisfies them only when $A=B=0$, proving uniqueness.
