# Self-map

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

A [self-map](self-map.md) is a [function](function-split.md) whose [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) and [codomain](codomain.md) are the same [set](set-split.md). It need not be a [bijection](bijection.md): a [constant function](constant-function.md) on a two-element [set](set-split.md) is a [self-map](self-map.md) that merges its [inputs](input-computer-science.md). [Self-maps](self-map.md) can always be composed with one another, and all [self-maps](self-map.md) of a specified [set](set-split.md) form its [full transformation monoid](full-transformation-monoid.md).
