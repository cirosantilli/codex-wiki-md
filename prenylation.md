# Prenylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[prenylation](prenylation.md) covalently attaches an isoprenoid-derived lipid group to a target [protein](protein-split.md). In many signaling proteins, this helps membrane association but needs additional features to specify precise localization.
