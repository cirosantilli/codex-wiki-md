# Index of a subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Subgroup](subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_of_a_subgroup)

The index $[G:H]$ is the number of left cosets of $H$ in $G$.
