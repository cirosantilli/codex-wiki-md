# Destructive interference intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

For the same mutually coherent, equally polarized [monochromatic waves](monochromatic-wave.md), the [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) minimum occurs at [phase difference](phase-difference.md) $\delta=(2m+1)\pi$, with [integer](integer.md) $m$. The [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) subtract, giving $I_{\min}=I_1+I_2-2\sqrt{I_1I_2}$. Complete cancellation requires equal individual [wave intensities](intensity-physics.md); $1$ and $0.25$ leave $0.25$ in their common unit. A dark band is a local minimum, and the average across an [interference fringe spacing](interference-fringe-spacing.md) still obeys [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).
