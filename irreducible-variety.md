# Irreducible variety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

An [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) whose underlying [topological space](topological-space.md) with its [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md) is an [irreducible topological space](irreducible-topological-space.md): it is nonempty and cannot be a union of two proper closed subsets. For a reduced affine variety, this is equivalent to its [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) being an [integral domain](integral-domain.md).
