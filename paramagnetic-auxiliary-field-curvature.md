# Paramagnetic auxiliary-field curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md)

For a uniform exchange field with cost $NM^2/(2U)$ and spin-band energies $\epsilon_k-\sigma M$, the [grand potential](grand-potential.md) is even in M. Its curvature at zero field is $N[U^{-1}-\chi_0]$, with $\chi_0=\int\nu(-n_F')d\epsilon$ and spin-summed [density of states](density-of-states.md) per site. Negative curvature proves local paramagnetic instability. It does not by itself exclude a first-order transition before that instability, nor determine the stable finite-field amplitude.
