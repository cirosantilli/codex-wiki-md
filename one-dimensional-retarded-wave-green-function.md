# One-dimensional retarded wave Green function

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional wave equation](one-dimensional-wave-equation.md)

For $(\partial_t^2-c^2\partial_x^2)G_R=\delta(t)\delta(x)$, zero response before the impulse gives the displayed [retarded fundamental solution](retarded-fundamental-solution.md). Its initial displacement is zero and its initial velocity is $\delta(x)$. The response fills the interior of the forward [causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation](causal-cone-for-the-constant-speed-wave-equation.md), rather than living only on its boundary. Changing the overall sign of the differential operator changes the Green function's sign. Equivalently, the spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) is $\Theta(t)\sin(c|k|t)/(c|k|)$, whose inverse is a rectangular profile. Values assigned exactly on the wave fronts do not change the associated [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
