# Lasso dropout knot

↑ **Parent:** [Lasso regularization path](lasso-regularization-path.md)

On a least-angle segment $\beta(\gamma)=\beta+\gamma d$, a nonzero active [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) reaches zero at the displayed first positive root. Continue only as far as the first dropout, inactive-entry event or zero-correlation endpoint. At a dropout remove the vanishing [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) from the active direction and recompute. This preserves agreement between nonzero [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) signs and residual-score signs, which the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions for the Lasso](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions-for-the-lasso.md) require. A removed [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) may re-enter later; the active set need not grow monotonically.
