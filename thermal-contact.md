# Thermal contact

↑ **Parent:** [Zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md)

Two [thermodynamic systems](thermodynamic-system.md) are in thermal contact when their boundary permits [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). They need not already be in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md): contact between unequal [temperatures](temperature-split.md) is precisely how equilibration can begin. Contact specifies an allowed exchange; equilibrium specifies its balance.
