# Minimum-variance combination of unbiased sample means

↑ **Parent:** [Antithetic variates](antithetic-variates.md)

For two [unbiased estimators](unbiased-estimator.md) $A,B$ of the same parameter with [variances](variance-split.md) $V_X,V_Y$ and [covariance](covariance.md) $C$, the fixed-weight estimator $wA+(1-w)B$ is unbiased and has [variance](variance-split.md) $w^2V_X+(1-w)^2V_Y+2w(1-w)C$. Completing the square gives $w_*=(V_Y-C)/(V_X+V_Y-2C)$ and minimum [variance](variance-split.md) $(V_XV_Y-C^2)/(V_X+V_Y-2C)$ when the denominator is positive. These are optimal among fixed linear unbiased combinations. Substituting weights estimated from the same observations need not preserve unbiasedness.
