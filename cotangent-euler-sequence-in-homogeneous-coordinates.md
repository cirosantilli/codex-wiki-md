# Cotangent Euler sequence in homogeneous coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Euler sequence](euler-sequence.md)

For a degree-zero rational function regular on an open subset of [projective space](projective-space-split.md), each derivative $\partial f/\partial X_i$ is a regular homogeneous rational function of degree $-1$. These derivatives define a [derivation](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) with values in $\mathcal O(-1)^{\oplus(n+1)}$, hence a map from the [Kähler differential sheaf](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md). The last map is $(g_i)\mapsto\sum_iX_ig_i$. On $X_i\ne0$, put $y_j=X_j/X_i$ and use frame $X_i^{-1}$. The derivative of $y_j$ has coordinate vector with $1$ at $j$, $-y_j$ at $i$ and zero elsewhere. Those vectors form a free basis of the kernel of $(b_i)\mapsto b_i+\sum_{j\ne i}y_jb_j$, proving exactness directly in every characteristic.
