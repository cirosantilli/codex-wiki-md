# Counterterm closure of a massive Yukawa theory

↑ **Parent:** [Counterterm closure](counterterm-closure.md)

Without a symmetry excluding odd scalar powers, a real-scalar [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md) with a massive [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) requires a general scalar potential through degree four. For a constant background, the fermion mass becomes $M+y\phi$, and its divergent local [determinant](determinant.md) contribution contains $(M+y\phi)^4$. Linear and cubic terms are therefore generated when $M\ne0$. A zero renormalized tadpole is a [renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md), not a prohibition of its [counterterm](counterterm.md). An exact discrete chiral symmetry in a massless fermion theory can instead enforce an even scalar potential.
