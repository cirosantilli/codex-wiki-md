# Split-off valence band

↑ **Parent:** [Valence band](valence-band.md)

[Spin-orbit coupling](spin-orbit-interaction.md) separates the $j=1/2$ valence states from the upper $j=3/2$ manifold at the zone centre in common cubic [semiconductors](semiconductor-split.md). If its maximum lies $\Delta_{\rm so}>0$ below the upper [valence band](valence-band.md) edge, its thermal [electron hole](electron-hole.md) population is suppressed by $e^{-\Delta_{\rm so}/(k_BT)}$ apart from [effective density of states](effective-density-of-states.md) factors. Neglect requires $k_BT\ll\Delta_{\rm so}$; it is not valid at every [temperature](temperature-split.md) or in every material.
