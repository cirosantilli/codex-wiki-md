# Rankine vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Phase vortex](phase-vortex.md)

A [Rankine vortex](rankine-vortex.md) models a finite rotating core joined continuously to an irrotational exterior. In cylindrical [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md), its radial and vertical velocities vanish and its azimuthal [velocity](velocity.md) has the displayed form, where $a$ is the core radius and $\Omega$ is a signed angular velocity. The axial [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) is $r^{-1}\partial_r(ru_\theta)$, equal to $2\Omega$ inside and zero outside. The velocity is continuous at $r=a$, although its first radial derivative has different one-sided values. The circulation outside the core is $2\pi\Omega a^2$.
