# Harmonic-trap chemical-potential pinning

↑ **Parent:** [Bose condensation in a three-dimensional harmonic trap](bose-condensation-in-a-three-dimensional-harmonic-trap.md)

With the ground [energy](energy-split.md) set to zero, the ideal-gas [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) approaches zero from below at condensation and remains there to leading thermodynamic order. Above onset it is determined by the trap [density of states](density-of-states.md). The shape differs from that for a uniform gas.
