# Liquid-crystalline chromosome organization

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatin](chromatin.md)

[Liquid-crystalline chromosome organization](liquid-crystalline-chromosome-organization.md) describes ordered dense DNA packing with orientational organization distinct from a conventional nucleosome array. Microscopic order does not by itself identify the proteins or interactions required to maintain it.
