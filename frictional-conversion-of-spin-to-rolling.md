# Frictional conversion of spin to rolling

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a circular rigid body of radius a, [mass](mass.md) M and axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) I on a fixed horizontal surface, choose positive spin so that rolling means $v=a\omega$. During purely horizontal frictional relaxation, an [impulse](impulse.md) J changes $Mv$ by J and $I\omega$ by $-aJ$. Hence $I\omega+Mav$ is constant. At final [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md), $v_f=(Ia\omega_0+Ma^2v_0)/(I+Ma^2)$. Subtracting initial from final translational-plus-rotational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives the title formula. The coefficient of friction controls the relaxation time, but not this loss once the no-slip state is reached. Vertical landing losses and rolling resistance are excluded.
