# Coordination complex

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

A [coordination complex](coordination-complex.md) contains a central atom, often a metal [ion](ion.md), bound to surrounding [ligands in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md). The metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) and the ligand donor types are separate pieces of its description.
