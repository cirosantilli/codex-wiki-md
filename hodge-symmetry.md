# Hodge symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge number](hodge-number.md)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), complex conjugation commutes with the real [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) and exchanges harmonic forms of bidegrees $(p,q)$ and $(q,p)$. The identification of [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md) with harmonic forms therefore gives the symmetry of [Hodge numbers](hodge-number.md).
