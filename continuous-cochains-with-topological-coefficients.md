# Continuous cochains with topological coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For a [topological group module](topological-group-module.md), use continuous maps with pointwise addition and the inhomogeneous group-cohomology differential. The kernel modulo the image defines continuous [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md) as an abelian group. With general topological coefficients the quotient topology need not be Hausdorff; the cohomology here is the algebraic quotient of continuous cocycles by continuous coboundaries. With trivial discrete coefficients in a [prime field](prime-field.md), its first cohomology is the space of continuous [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) to that field's additive group.
