# Skew-symmetrized transport form

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-symmetry of incompressible transport](skew-symmetry-of-incompressible-transport.md)

For a velocity field that need not be divergence free, the corrected transport

$$
\widetilde B(u,v)=(u\mathbin\cdot\nabla)v+\frac12(\nabla\mathbin\cdot u)v
$$

satisfies $\langle\widetilde B(u,v),w\rangle=-\langle\widetilde B(u,w),v\rangle$ and therefore preserves the quadratic energy cancellation.
