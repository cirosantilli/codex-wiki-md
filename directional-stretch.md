# Directional stretch

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

For a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $u$ in a real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), stretching by a factor $\alpha$ along $u$ and leaving its perpendicular subspace fixed gives

$$
T=I+(\alpha-1)uu^T.
$$

Decompose $x=(u^Tx)u+(x-(u^Tx)u)$. The first term is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the line; the second is perpendicular to it. The displayed map multiplies the first by $\alpha$ and fixes the second. In the plane, if $u=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)^T$, then $T=R(\theta)\operatorname{diag}(\alpha,1)R(-\theta)$.
