# Cosine-exponential Brownian local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md)

For independent standard [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) $B,W$, [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $dY=e^W\cos B\,dW-e^W\sin B\,dB$: the two second-order drifts cancel. Independence gives zero cross [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md), so the displayed bracket follows. The process itself is not Brownian because its bracket is random. Its inverse-bracket time change is Brownian motion starting at one, in the time-changed [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md); subtracting one gives standard Brownian motion. The [Divergence of a driftless Brownian exponential clock](divergence-of-a-driftless-brownian-exponential-clock.md) guarantees that the inverse is finite at every new time.
