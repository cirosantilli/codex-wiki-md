# Magnus expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Time-ordered exponential](time-ordered-exponential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_expansion)

For $\dot U=A(t)U$ and $U(0)=I$, the Magnus expansion writes the logarithm of the propagator as iterated integrals of [commutators](commutator.md). Its first terms are $\Omega_1=\int_0^TA(t)dt$ and $\Omega_2=\tfrac12\int_0^Tdt\int_0^tds\,[A(t),A(s)]$. For a [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), $A=-iH/\hbar$, so a noncommuting time-dependent control has corrections beyond the exponential of its integrated [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). This formula is a local convergent expansion under suitable smallness conditions, or a formal expansion; it must not be assumed globally convergent for arbitrary pulse durations.
