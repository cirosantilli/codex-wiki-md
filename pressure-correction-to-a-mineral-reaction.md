# Pressure correction to a mineral reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs energy of reaction](gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md)

At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), $dG=V\,dP$ gives the pressure correction for the participating solids. Constant reaction volume reduces the integral to $\Delta V_{\rm solids}(P-P_0)$. A gas product treated by [chemical fugacity](chemical-fugacity.md) instead contributes $\nu RT\ln(f/P_0)$ relative to its reference state. Adding a separate gas-volume correction to that term would double count the same pressure dependence.
