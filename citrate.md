# Citrate

↑ **Parent:** [Citric acid](citric-acid.md)

Citrate denotes an [ion](ion.md) derived from [citric acid](citric-acid.md). The commonly specified form has [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) equal to that of three [electrons](electron.md) and [chemical formula](chemical-formula.md) $\mathrm C_6\mathrm H_5\mathrm O_7^{3-}$; it results from removing three [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ions](ion.md) from [citric acid](citric-acid.md). Forms with additional [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ions](ion.md) attached through [protonation](protonation.md) also occur. [ChEBI, citrate(3−)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:16947)
