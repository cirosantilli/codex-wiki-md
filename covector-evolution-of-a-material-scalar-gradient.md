# Covector evolution of a material scalar gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Passive scalar](passive-scalar.md)

Differentiate the [advection equation](transport-equation.md) componentwise: $D_t(\partial_i\theta)=-(\partial_i u_j)\partial_j\theta$. Along a [Lagrangian trajectory](lagrangian-trajectory.md), the scalar [gradient](gradient.md) therefore evolves by the negative transpose of the [velocity gradient tensor](velocity-gradient-tensor.md). If $F$ is the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md), this says $\boldsymbol k(t)=F(t)^{-T}\boldsymbol k(0)$, as expected for a [covector](covector.md). Fluid material vectors and scalar gradients transform oppositely under stretching.
