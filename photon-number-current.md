# Photon number current

↑ **Parent:** [Photon number density](photon-number-density.md)

In an [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md), $J^{\hat0}=\int f\,d^3p$ is the [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) and $J^{\hat i}=\int f e^i\,d^3p$ is its number flux. Integrating the [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md) gives $\nabla_\mu J^\mu=\int C[f]\,d^3p/E$ when momentum-boundary terms vanish. Collisionless propagation and photon-number-conserving scattering give zero [divergence](divergence.md). [Photon emission](photon-emission.md), [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md), [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md), [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) and [double Compton scattering](double-compton-scattering.md) generally do not. This is a number current, not an electric charge current: [photons](photon.md) are neutral.
