# Universal consequences of a theory

↑ **Parent:** [Universal first-order theory](universal-first-order-theory.md)

The universal consequences $T_\forall$ of a [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) $T$ are all universal [first-order sentences](first-order-sentence.md) entailed by $T$. Their model class is exactly the class of [substructures](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of models of $T$, up to [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md). This characterization follows from the [diagram embedding criterion for universal theories](diagram-embedding-criterion-for-universal-theories.md).
