# Dual basis of cumulative coordinate sums

↑ **Parent:** [Dual basis](dual-basis.md)

For the [basis](basis.md) $v_j=e_1+\cdots+e_j$ obtained from a [basis](basis.md) $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, the [dual basis](dual-basis.md) is obtained by successive differences of the original [dual basis](dual-basis.md). Indeed, if $x=\sum_jc_jv_j$, its original [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) are $x_i=\sum_{j\geq i}c_j$. Hence $c_i=x_i-x_{i+1}$ for $i<n$, and $c_n=x_n$. These are precisely the new coordinate [linear functionals](linear-functional.md). This gives an elementary example of the inverse relation between [change of basis](change-of-basis.md) and [covector transformation under a change of basis](covector-transformation-under-a-change-of-basis.md).
