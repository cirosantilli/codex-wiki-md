# Random-orientation distribution of an apparent oblate axis ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Projected axis ratio of an oblate spheroid](projected-axis-ratio-of-an-oblate-spheroid.md)

For an unoriented symmetry axis chosen uniformly on the sphere, $\cos i$ is uniform on $[0,1]$. At fixed intrinsic ratio $q$, the [change-of-variables formula for a probability density](change-of-variables-formula-for-a-probability-density.md) gives

$$
p(Q\mid q)=\frac{Q}{\sqrt{1-q^2}\sqrt{Q^2-q^2}},
\qquad q\leq Q\leq1.
$$
