# Mean-zero score identity under quadratic-mean differentiability

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiability in quadratic mean](differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md)

For a [differentiable-in-quadratic-mean path](differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md), normalization gives $\langle(\sqrt{p_t}-\sqrt p)/t,\sqrt{p_t}+\sqrt p\rangle=0$. The two factors converge in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) to $g\sqrt p/2$ and $2\sqrt p$. Continuity of the [inner product](inner-product.md) therefore gives $Pg=0$. This proves [score function](informant-function.md) centering without differentiating the density [integral](integral.md).
