# GTPase-activating protein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A [GTPase-activating protein](gtpase-activating-protein.md) accelerates [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) hydrolysis by a target GTPase. It changes the kinetics of a [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) switch rather than loading [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) in place of [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md).
