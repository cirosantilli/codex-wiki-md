# Upper central series

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md)

Repeatedly take the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md) after quotienting by the preceding term. A [group](group-split.md) is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) exactly when a finite term is the whole group. This yields the [normalizer condition for nilpotent groups](normalizer-condition-for-nilpotent-groups.md).
