# Global characteristic flow for a Hamiltonian with bounded Hessian

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md)

If $H$ is $C^2$ and its spatial [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is uniformly bounded on every finite time interval, its [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) is globally [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) in [phase space](phase-space.md) with at most linear growth. The [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](picard-lindelof-theorem.md) and [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) give a unique [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md) for all finite forward and backward times. Bounds on $\nabla_zH(t,0)$ follow from time continuity on compact intervals.
