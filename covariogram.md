# Covariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Geostatistics](geostatistics.md)

The [covariogram](covariogram.md) is the displacement-dependent [covariance](covariance.md) $C(h)=\operatorname{Cov}[Z(s+h),Z(s)]$ of a second-order stationary field. It is an even [positive-definite kernel](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md). A [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) determines differences $C(0)-C(h)$, but not an arbitrary constant [covariance](covariance.md) component.
