# Double-well potential

↑ **Parent:** [Potential well](potential-well.md)

A [potential energy](potential-energy.md) landscape with two local minima separated by a barrier. In a symmetric one-dimensional case, its two lowest [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) are an [even function](even-function.md) and an [odd function](odd-function.md), with their [energies](energy-split.md) split by [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md). A [two-mode approximation for a Bose gas](two-mode-approximation-for-a-bose-gas.md) retains these two [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md) when higher [energy levels](energy-level.md) are sufficiently remote.
