# Cross-pollination

↑ **Parent:** [Pollination](pollination.md)

Transfer of [pollen](pollen.md) between different individuals of the same [species](species.md); compare [self-pollination](self-pollination.md).[Biology 2e, §32.2 opening paragraphs: self-pollination and cross-pollination](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/32-2-pollination-and-fertilization.md)

A [flower](flower.md) can contain both reproductive groups and still receive [pollen](pollen.md) from another individual. Conversely, transfer between two [flowers](flower.md) on one [plant](plant.md) does not by itself imply [cross-pollination](cross-pollination.md). Following the donor and recipient individuals makes [pollination](pollination.md) useful for studying [genetics](genetics-split.md): organ position alone cannot identify whose [alleles](allele.md) meet at [fertilization](fertilization.md).
