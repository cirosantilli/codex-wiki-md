# Parallel differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

A differential form is parallel when its [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) vanishes. Parallel transport then preserves it, and every parallel form is harmonic.

The form is preserved by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md); this is the alternating-tensor case of vanishing [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md).
