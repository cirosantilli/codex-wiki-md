# Capacitance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

For a collection of fixed [electrical conductors](electrical-conductor.md) in linear electrostatics, the [capacitance matrix](capacitance-matrix.md) relates conductor charges to potentials relative to a specified reference: $Q_i=\sum_jC_{ij}V_j$. It includes mutual electrostatic effects omitted when each conductor is treated as an isolated capacitor. Its [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) is $U=\tfrac12\mathbf V^{\mathsf T}\mathbf C\mathbf V=\tfrac12\sum_iQ_iV_i$. Geometry and separation are needed to determine its entries.
