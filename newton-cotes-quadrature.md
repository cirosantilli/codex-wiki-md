# Newton-Cotes quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Interpolatory quadrature](interpolatory-quadrature.md)

A [Newton-Cotes quadrature](newton-cotes-quadrature.md) is an [interpolatory quadrature rule](interpolatory-quadrature.md) whose [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md) are equally spaced. Integrating the [Lagrange interpolation polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md) gives [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) $W_j=\int_a^b\ell_j(x)\,dx$. A [Newton-Cotes closed quadrature](newton-cotes-closed-quadrature.md) includes both endpoints; an open rule uses interior [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md). [Simpson's rule](simpson-s-rule.md) is the closed rule with three [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md). Its [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) are $(b-a)(1,4,1)/6$.
