# Accreting X-ray pulsar

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md)

An accreting X-ray pulsar contains a magnetized [neutron star](neutron-star.md) whose [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) channels the accreting gas toward its magnetic poles. Rotation carries the bright accretion regions through the observer's view and produces pulses. The input [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) can change the spin period; this differs from the rotational energy source of an isolated [pulsar](pulsar.md).
