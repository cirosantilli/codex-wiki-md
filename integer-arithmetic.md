# Integer arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)

Integer arithmetic computes with [integers](integer-split.md). [Addition](addition.md), [subtraction](subtraction.md) and [multiplication](multiplication.md) preserve [integer](integer-split.md) values; [division](division-mathematics.md) can instead return a [quotient](quotient.md) and [remainder](remainder.md) through [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md). For example, $102=20\cdot5+2$ gives five complete groups and a remainder of two. The [ceiling count for fixed-size batches](ceiling-count-for-fixed-size-batches.md) requires six groups to hold all 102 items.

Mathematical [integers](integer-split.md) have no fixed size bound. A computer representation may have one, so an exact arithmetic formula still requires sufficient space for its inputs and intermediate values. The [floor function](floor-function.md) and [ceiling function](ceiling-function.md) define mathematical bounds independently of a chosen representation.
