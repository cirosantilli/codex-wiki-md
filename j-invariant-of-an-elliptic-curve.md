# j-invariant of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

The j-invariant is unchanged by admissible changes of [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). With $b_2=a_1^2+4a_2$ and $b_4=a_1a_3+2a_4$, set $c_4=b_2^2-24b_4$ and $j=c_4^3/\Delta$, where $\Delta$ is the [elliptic-curve discriminant](elliptic-curve-discriminant.md). For [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over a [local field](local-field.md), an integral equation with unit discriminant shows that $j$ lies in the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md). Thus a negative [valuation](valuation.md) of $j$ proves bad reduction, even if the displayed equation has not been proved minimal.
