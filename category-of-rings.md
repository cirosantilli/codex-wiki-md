# Category of rings

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

The category of unital [rings](ring.md) has identity-preserving [ring homomorphisms](ring-homomorphism.md) as morphisms. The zero ring, allowing $0=1$, is terminal, and $\mathbb Z$ is initial. Nonzero rings admit no maps from the zero ring. Monomorphisms are injective: maps from $\mathbb Z[x]$ probe individual elements. It is a [regular category](regular-category.md), with surjective homomorphisms as [regular epimorphisms](regular-epimorphism.md); these are distinct from arbitrary categorical [epimorphisms](epimorphism.md).
