# Molar enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md)

[Molar enthalpy](molar-enthalpy.md) is [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) per [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n$, measured in $\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}$. With $u_{\rm m}=U/n$ and [molar volume](molar-volume.md) $v_{\rm m}=V/n$, $h_{\rm m}=u_{\rm m}+pv_{\rm m}$. Its fixed-[pressure](pressure-split.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) derivative is the corresponding [molar heat capacity](molar-heat-capacity.md) $C_{p,\rm m}$, at fixed composition.
