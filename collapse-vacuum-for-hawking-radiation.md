# Collapse vacuum for Hawking radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md)

In a collapse spacetime, choose the quantum field's incoming state to have no incoming particles at [past null infinity](past-null-infinity.md) and to be regular in freely falling coordinates near the forming [event horizon](event-horizon.md). The late outgoing modes then have the [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md), while there is no incoming thermal bath. This collapse-state boundary condition differs from an equilibrium state on an eternal [black hole](black-hole.md). The [Hawking exponential ray map](hawking-exponential-ray-map.md) and the [thermal ratio of Hawking Bogoliubov coefficients](thermal-ratio-of-hawking-bogoliubov-coefficients.md) determine the leading late-time particle spectrum; [greybody factors](greybody-factor.md) describe scattering outside the [event horizon](event-horizon.md).
