# Initial coefficients of the elliptic formal group at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For a [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), use the [local parameter](local-parameter-on-a-smooth-algebraic-curve.md) $t=-x/y$ and put $w=-1/y$. The [formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation](formal-kernel-of-a-minimal-weierstrass-equation.md) construction gives $w=t^3+a_1t^4+(a_1^2+a_2)t^5+(a_1^3+2a_1a_2+a_3)t^6+O(t^7)$. Substitution into the [elliptic curve group law](elliptic-curve-group-law-from-riemann-roch.md) yields the displayed initial coefficients and the [formal inverse](formal-inverse.md) $-t/(1-a_1t-a_3w(t))$. In [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), the [invariant differential on an elliptic curve](invariant-differential-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) becomes $(1+a_1t+(a_1^2+a_2)t^2+O(t^3))\,dt$, giving the [formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md) $t+a_1t^2/2+(a_1^2+a_2)t^3/3+O(t^4)$. The addition coefficients are integral polynomial expressions and hence also apply in positive [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md).
