# Spring tide

↑ **Parent:** [Tide](tide.md)

A [spring tide](spring-tide.md) has an enhanced high-to-low tidal range because the [Moon](moon.md) and [Sun](sun.md) are approximately aligned as seen from the [Earth](earth.md), so their principal tidal contributions reinforce. Such alignments occur twice in a lunar phase cycle.
