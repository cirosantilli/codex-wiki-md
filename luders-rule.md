<h1 id="luders-rule">Lüders rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Projective measurement](projective-measurement.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lüders_rule)

Conditional on outcome $i$ of a [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md), the Lüders rule updates the state to

$$
\rho_i=\frac{P_i\rho P_i}{\operatorname{Tr}(P_i\rho)}.
$$
