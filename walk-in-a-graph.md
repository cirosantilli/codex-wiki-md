# Walk in a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A sequence of vertices and edges of a [graph](graph-split.md), with each edge joining its two consecutive vertices. Vertices and edges may repeat; a [path in a graph](path-in-a-graph.md) imposes distinctness conditions. A [closed walk](closed-walk.md) ends where it starts.
