# Fourfold product bound from small tripling

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative Ruzsa triangle inequality](noncommutative-ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md)

If a finite subset $A$ of a group satisfies $|A^3|\leq K|A|$, then repeated use of the [Noncommutative Ruzsa triangle inequality](noncommutative-ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
|A^4|\leq K^3|A|\leq K^4|A|.
$$
