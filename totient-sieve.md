# Totient sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md)

A totient sieve computes the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md) for many integers at once. Initialize $\phi(n)=n$ and, for each [prime](prime-number-split.md) $p$, replace $\phi(n)$ by $\phi(n)(1-1/p)$ for every multiple $n$ of $p$.
