# User-space service

↑ **Parent:** [Operating system](operating-system-split.md)

An [operating system](operating-system-split.md) service executing at an application's [processor privilege level](processor-privilege-level.md), outside the [operating-system kernel](kernel-operating-system.md). A [microkernel](microkernel.md) can host a [file system](file-system-split.md) or [device driver](device-driver.md) this way. Its granted [security capabilities](capability-computer-security.md) and [memory protection](memory-protection.md) determine its authority; the label [user-space service](user-space-service.md) does not imply that it has little authority.
