# Incubation period

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubation_period)

The [incubation period](incubation-period.md) is the interval from infection to symptom onset in a person who develops symptoms. Its [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) describes onset times, not the full timing of infectiousness. Exposure may only be known to fall in an interval, requiring a corresponding observation model when estimating this distribution.
