# Hermitian conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

Hermitian conjugation takes the complex conjugate and reverses the order of a product of operators: $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$. An operator satisfying $A^\dagger=A$ is [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md).
