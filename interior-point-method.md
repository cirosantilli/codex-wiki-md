# Interior-point method

↑ **Parent:** [Conic optimization](conic-optimization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior-point_method)

An interior-point method solves constrained [mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md) problems by maintaining variables in the interiors of their feasible cones or inequality domains. Barrier derivatives are defined there, and damped [Newton methods](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) keep subsequent iterates in their domains. A [central path](central-path.md) gives a family of barrier-regularized optima whose duality gap approaches zero; a [conic phase-I problem](conic-phase-i-problem.md) can supply a strict feasible starting point.
