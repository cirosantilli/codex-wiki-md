# Stationary encoding in an aleph-one-like dense order

↑ **Parent:** [Aleph-one-like linear order](aleph-one-like-linear-order.md)

For a subset $S$ of the nonzero countable limit ordinals, let $B_\alpha=1+\mathbb Q$ when $\alpha\in S$ and $B_\alpha=\mathbb Q$ otherwise. The [order sum](order-sum.md) is an [aleph-one-like linear order](aleph-one-like-linear-order.md) whose nonempty proper initial segments are $\mathbb Q$ or $\mathbb Q+1$. At the limit cut before block $\delta$, the complementary final segment has a least point exactly when $\delta\in S$. [Club agreement of countable filtrations](club-agreement-of-countable-filtrations.md) makes this condition invariant modulo a nonstationary difference. [Disjoint stationary subsets of omega-one](disjoint-stationary-subsets-of-omega-one.md) consequently supply $2^{\aleph_1}$ nonisomorphic orders.
