# Skein relation

↑ **Parent:** [Link diagram](link-diagram.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skein_relation)

A skein relation relates the invariants of [link diagrams](link-diagram.md) agreeing outside a disk and differing by a positive crossing, a negative crossing, and a smoothing within it. Its signs and normalization determine the particular invariant convention.
