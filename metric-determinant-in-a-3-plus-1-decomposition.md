<h1 id="metric-determinant-in-a-3-plus-1-decomposition">Metric determinant in a 3+1 decomposition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md)

For a positive [lapse function](lapse-function.md) $\alpha$, [shift vector](shift-vector.md) $\beta^i$ and spatial [induced metric](induced-metric.md) $\gamma_{ij}$, the block metric has $g_{00}=-\alpha^2+\gamma_{ij}\beta^i\beta^j$ and $g_{0i}=\gamma_{ij}\beta^j$. Its block determinant is $g=-\alpha^2\det\gamma$. Therefore the [metric volume tensor](metric-volume-tensor.md) contracted with the future [unit normal](unit-normal.md) restricts to the spatial volume tensor: $n^\mu\epsilon_{\mu ijk}=\sqrt{\det\gamma}\,[ijk]$.
