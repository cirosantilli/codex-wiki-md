# Tie probability for independent binomial counts

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

For [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) $X,Y$ with the same [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)$, partition the tie event into the mutually exclusive events $X=Y=j$. Independence makes each probability the square of its binomial probability, proving the formula. At $p=1/2$, [Vandermonde's identity](vandermonde-s-identity.md) gives $\Pr(X=Y)=4^{-n}\binom{2n}{n}$. Symmetry under exchange of the two variables then gives $\Pr(X>Y)=\Pr(Y>X)=(1-\Pr(X=Y))/2$.
