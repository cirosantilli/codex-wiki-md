## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2026

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Francis Halzen](physicist.md#francis-halzen) received the full award for decisive contributions to [IceCube](particle-detection.md#icecube-neutrino-observatory) and the discovery of high-energy [neutrinos](standard-model.md#neutrino) from astrophysical sources.[6 October 2026 announcement, award decision and motivation, lines 60–65](https://www.kva.se/en/news/the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2026/)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2025

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[John Clarke](physicist.md#john-clarke), [Michel H. Devoret](physicist.md#michel-h-devoret) and [John M. Martinis](physicist.md#john-m-martinis) shared the award equally for discovering macroscopic [quantum tunnelling](quantum-mechanics.md#quantum-tunnelling) and quantized energy levels in an [electric circuit](electrical-circuit.md).[Individual laureate entries, one-third shares and joint motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2025/summary/)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Pierre Agostini](physicist.md#pierre-agostini), [Ferenc Krausz](physicist.md#ferenc-krausz) and [Anne L’Huillier](physicist.md#anne-l’huillier) shared the award equally for experimental methods generating [attosecond](physics.md#attosecond) light pulses to study [electron](physics.md#electron) dynamics in matter.[Individual laureate entries, one-third shares and joint motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2023/summary/)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Syukuro Manabe](geophysics.md#syukuro-manabe) and [Klaus Hasselmann](physicist.md#klaus-hasselmann) each received one quarter for physical [climate models](geophysics.md#climate-model) quantifying variability and predicting [global warming](geophysics.md#global-warming). [Giorgio Parisi](physicist.md#giorgio-parisi) received one half for discovering how disorder and fluctuations interact in physical systems from atomic to planetary scales.[Individual laureate entries, quarter/quarter/half shares and two distinct award contributions](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2021/summary/)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

One half went to [Roger Penrose](mathematics.md#roger-penrose) for showing that [black hole](black-hole.md) formation is a robust prediction of [general relativity](general-relativity.md). One quarter each went to [Reinhard Genzel](astronomy.md#reinhard-genzel) and [Andrea Ghez](astronomy.md#andrea-m-ghez) for discovering a supermassive compact object at the centre of the [Milky Way](galaxy.md#milky-way).[Individual official summary: Penrose one half for robust relativistic black-hole formation; Genzel and Ghez one quarter each for the Galactic-centre compact object.](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2020-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2019

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

One half went to [James Peebles](physicist.md#p-james-peebles) for theoretical discoveries in physical [cosmology](cosmology.md). One quarter each went to [Michel Mayor](astronomy.md#michel-mayor) and [Didier Queloz](astronomy.md#didier-queloz) for discovering an [exoplanet](exoplanet.md) orbiting a solar-type [star](stellar-astrophysics.md#star).[Individual official summary: Peebles one half for physical cosmology; Mayor and Queloz one quarter each for an exoplanet around a solar-type star.](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2019-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2018

One quarter each went to [Gérard Mourou](physicist.md#gerard-mourou) and [Donna Strickland](physicist.md#donna-strickland) for a method of generating intense, ultrashort optical pulses.[Individual official summary: Ashkin one half for optical tweezers and biological applications; Mourou and Strickland one quarter each for intense ultrashort optical pulses.](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2018-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2013

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 2008

Awarded to [Charles Glover Barkla](physicist.md#charles-glover-barkla) for characteristic [X-rays](electromagnetic-radiation.md#x-ray) of the [chemical elements](chemical-substance.md#chemical-element); he received it in 1918. The [Barkla characteristic X-ray experiment](electromagnetic-radiation.md#barkla-characteristic-x-ray-experiment) describes the experimental separation of components.[Official award record: Barkla, characteristic radiation and deferred receipt in 1918](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1917-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1915

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

Awarded jointly to [William Henry Bragg](physicist.md#william-henry-bragg) and [William Lawrence Bragg](physicist.md#william-lawrence-bragg) for methods of determining [crystal structures](physical-chemistry.md#crystal-structure) using [X-rays](electromagnetic-radiation.md#x-ray). [Bragg's law](quantum-scattering.md#bragg-s-law) connects measured angles to lattice-plane spacings.[Official award record: laureates and crystal-structure subject](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1915-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1914

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

Awarded to [Max von Laue](physicist.md#max-von-laue) for the discovery of [X-ray diffraction](quantum-scattering.md#x-ray-diffraction) by crystals.[Official award record: laureate and diffraction subject](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1914-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1913

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

Awarded to [Heike Kamerlingh Onnes](physicist.md#heike-kamerlingh-onnes) for investigations of matter at low [temperatures](temperature.md), including [helium liquefaction](temperature.md#helium-liquefaction). The award's scope includes the experimental means of reaching those conditions.[Official award record: low-temperature properties and liquid-helium production](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1913-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1912

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

Awarded to [Gustaf Dalén](engineering.md#gustaf-dalen) for automatic regulators used with gas accumulators for lighthouse and buoy lighting. The [sun valve](engineering.md#sun-valve) and [Dalén light](engineering.md#dalen-light) connect the award to mechanical engineering.[Official award record: automatic regulators and marine lighting](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1912-by-nobel-prize-outreach)

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1911

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Wilhelm Wien](physicist.md#wilhelm-wien) received the award for discoveries concerning the laws of [thermal radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md#thermal-radiation).[Physics 1911: laureate and award motivation](physics-bibliography.md#all-nobel-prizes-in-physics-by-nobel-prize-outreach) [Wien's displacement law](astrophysics.md#wien-s-displacement-law) connects a [blackbody spectrum](statistical-physics.md#blackbody-spectrum)'s peak [wavelength](wave-equation.md#wavelength) to its [temperature](temperature.md).

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1910

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Johannes Diderik van der Waals](physicist.md#johannes-diderik-van-der-waals) received the award for an [equation of state](pressure.md#equation-of-state) describing gases and liquids.[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1910-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Van der Waals equation](pressure.md#van-der-waals-equation) connects that description to a [Van der Waals critical point](pressure.md#van-der-waals-critical-point) and [Van der Waals liquid-gas coexistence](pressure.md#van-der-waals-liquid-gas-coexistence).

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1909

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Guglielmo Marconi](physicist.md#guglielmo-marconi) and [Karl Ferdinand Braun](physicist.md#karl-ferdinand-braun) shared the award equally for developing [wireless telegraphy](electrical-engineering.md#wireless-telegraphy).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1909-by-nobel-prize-outreach) [Braun's receiver coupling experiment](electrical-engineering.md#braun-s-receiver-coupling-experiment) connects that history to [resonance](dynamical-systems.md#resonance) and the selectivity of a receiver.

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1908

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Gabriel Lippmann](physicist.md#gabriel-lippmann) received the award for a photographic colour-recording method based on [interference](physical-optics.md#interference-wave-propagation).[Physics 1908: laureate and award motivation](physics-bibliography.md#all-nobel-prizes-in-physics-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Lippmann colour-recording experiment](optics.md#lippmann-colour-recording-experiment) stores a [standing wave](mathematical-physics.md#standing-wave) pattern in a photographic layer.

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1907

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Albert Abraham Michelson](physicist.md#albert-abraham-michelson) received the award for precise optical instruments and their use in [spectroscopy](physics.md#spectroscopy) and [metrology](physics.md#metrology).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1907-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Michelson interferometer](physical-optics.md#michelson-interferometer) converts relative [wave phase](wave.md#phase-waves) into a readout for precise [length](physics.md#length) measurements.

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1906

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Joseph John Thomson](physicist.md#joseph-john-thomson) received the award for theoretical and experimental research on [electric currents](electric-charge.md#electric-current) through [gases](thermodynamics.md#gas).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1906-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Thomson charge-to-mass experiment](relativistic-electromagnetism.md#thomson-charge-to-mass-experiment) connects [cathode rays](physics.md#cathode-ray) to the measurable [charge-to-mass ratio](relativistic-electromagnetism.md#charge-to-mass-ratio) of an [electron](physics.md#electron).

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1905

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Philipp Lenard](physicist.md#philipp-lenard) received the award for research on [cathode rays](physics.md#cathode-ray).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1905-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Lenard cathode-ray window experiment](relativistic-electromagnetism.md#lenard-cathode-ray-window-experiment) separates the source region from the region where the emerging [electrons](physics.md#electron) are studied.

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1904

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

[Lord Rayleigh](physicist.md#john-william-strutt-3rd-baron-rayleigh) received the award for gas-[mass density](fluid-mechanics.md#density) investigations and the discovery of [argon](chemical-substance.md#argon).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1904-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment](chemical-substance.md#rayleigh-ramsay-argon-density-experiment) shows how a small reproducible density discrepancy can reveal a new [chemical element](chemical-substance.md#chemical-element).

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics.md)

One half went to [Henri Becquerel](physicist.md#henri-becquerel) for discovering spontaneous [radioactivity](physics.md#radioactive-decay). [Pierre Curie](physicist.md#pierre-curie) and [Marie Curie](physicist.md#marie-curie) each received one quarter for investigating that radiation.[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1903-by-nobel-prize-outreach) [Becquerel's uranium-radiation experiment](physics.md#becquerel-s-uranium-radiation-experiment) and [Curie ionization-current measurement](physics.md#curie-ionization-current-measurement) expose two complementary experimental routes into [radioactivity](physics.md#radioactive-decay).

## Nobel Prize in Physics in 1902

[Hendrik Antoon Lorentz](physicist.md#hendrik-antoon-lorentz) and [Pieter Zeeman](physicist.md#pieter-zeeman) shared the award equally for research on magnetic influences on [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md).[Official summary: laureates, prize shares and motivation](physics-bibliography.md#the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1902-by-nobel-prize-outreach) The [Zeeman discovery experiment of 1896](atomic-physics.md#zeeman-discovery-experiment-of-1896) connects this award to changes in [spectral lines](stellar-astrophysics.md#spectral-line).
