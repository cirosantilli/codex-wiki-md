# Finite-part inverse-absolute-value distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard finite-part integral](hadamard-finite-part-integral.md)

Subtract a [test function](test-function.md)'s value at zero before integrating $1/|k|$ near zero, and retain the ordinary tail integral. This defines an even [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md). Changing the subtraction cutoff adds a multiple of the [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md). Multiplication by $k$ gives $\operatorname{sgn}k$.
