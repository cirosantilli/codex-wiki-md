# Local equality of phase and group velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Phase velocity and group velocity](phase-velocity-and-group-velocity.md)

For a differentiable [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) and nonzero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, [group velocity](group-velocity.md) equals [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) precisely where $dv_p/dk=0$. Equality at an isolated stationary point of $v_p$ does not establish a nondispersive branch. For a [gravity-capillary wave](gravity-capillary-wave.md), the equality occurs at $k=\sqrt{\rho g/T}$ and the phase-speed minimum, even though the relation is dispersive elsewhere.
