# Symmetry of second derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed partial derivative](mixed-partial-derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_of_second_derivatives)

When the second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) are continuous in a neighborhood, the order of the [mixed partial derivatives](mixed-partial-derivative.md) can be interchanged. Expressing a rectangular finite difference as either iterated integral by the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) and shrinking the rectangle proves the equality. Mere existence of both derivatives does not imply this symmetry.
