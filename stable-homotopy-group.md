# Stable homotopy group

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (topology)](spectrum-topology.md)

The $k$th [stable homotopy group](stable-homotopy-group.md) of $E$ is $\pi_kE=[\Sigma^k\mathbb S,E]_{\mathrm{st}}$, equivalently $\operatorname{colim}_j\pi_{k+j}E_j$ in a sequential model.
