# Separation proof in the constructible universe

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

To separate a [subset](subset.md) of a constructible [set](set-split.md) by a formula interpreted in the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md), apply the [reflection theorem for definable hierarchies](reflection-theorem-for-definable-hierarchies.md) to the formula and its subformulas. At a sufficiently large reflecting level containing the parameters, the desired [subset](subset.md) is definable and belongs to the next [constructible hierarchy](constructible-hierarchy.md) level. The reflection proof uses least witness stages and ambient [Axiom schema of replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), so does not presuppose [Axiom schema of separation](axiom-schema-of-specification.md) within the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md).
