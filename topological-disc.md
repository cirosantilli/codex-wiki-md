# Topological disc

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

An $n$-dimensional topological disc is a space homeomorphic to a Euclidean ball in $\mathbb R^n$, with its open or closed convention specified. A [closed disc](closed-disc.md) is homeomorphic to $D^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:|x|\leq1\}$. The planar [disk](disk-mathematics.md) is the Euclidean two-dimensional instance, rather than the whole abstract topological class.
