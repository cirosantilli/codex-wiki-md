# Noise temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometer](radiometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noise_temperature)

A [noise temperature](noise-temperature.md) expresses received or internally added noise [power](power.md) on an equivalent [temperature](temperature-split.md) scale. For one matched receiving channel in the classical thermal limit, an ideal rectangular [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) $\Delta\nu$ gives $P=k_{\rm B}T_N\Delta\nu$, with [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) $k_{\rm B}$. An equivalent [noise temperature](noise-temperature.md) need not equal a component's physical [temperature](temperature-split.md).
