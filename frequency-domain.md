# Frequency domain

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

A time-dependent response can be represented by its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) in [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). With $\widehat f(\omega)=\int f(t)e^{i\omega t}dt$, a [time derivative](time-derivative.md) transforms to $-i\omega\widehat f$ when boundary terms vanish. A time-translation-invariant response convolution becomes multiplication by a [frequency](frequency.md)-dependent response coefficient. This is why a dispersive [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) is naturally specified in the frequency domain. The inverse-transform modes use $e^{-i\omega t}$.
