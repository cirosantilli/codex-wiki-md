# Complete negative filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration of a ring](filtration-of-a-ring.md)

A negative [filtration of a ring](filtration-of-a-ring.md) has $F_iR=R$ for nonnegative indices. It is complete and separated when the displayed map to the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Products make $F_{-q}R$ two-sided [ideals](ideal.md). Series whose terms eventually belong to every $F_{-q}R$ converge. In particular, for $x\in F_{-1}R$ and $r\in R$, the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) $\sum_{j\geq0}(xr)^j$ inverts $1-xr$, so the [unit criterion for the Jacobson radical](unit-criterion-for-the-jacobson-radical.md) gives $F_{-1}R\subseteq J(R)$.
