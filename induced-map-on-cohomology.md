# Induced map on cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

A continuous map $f:X\to Y$ induces a pullback $f^*:H^q(Y;R)\to H^q(X;R)$. It preserves sums and [cup products](cup-product.md), and $(g\circ f)^*=f^*\circ g^*$.
