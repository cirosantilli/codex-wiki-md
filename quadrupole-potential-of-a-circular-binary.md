# Quadrupole potential of a circular binary

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole approximation](quadrupole-approximation.md)

A [binary star](binary-star.md) of total mass $M$, [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) $\mu$ and separation vector $\mathbf a$ has far-field [potential function](potential-function.md)

$$
\Phi=-\frac{GM}{x}-\frac{G\mu a^2}{2x^3}(3\cos^2\theta-1)+O(GMa^3/x^4),
$$

where $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf x$ and $\mathbf a$. The dipole term vanishes in [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) coordinates. Averaging a circular coplanar binary over its fast phase gives $\overline\Phi=-GM/x-G\mu a^2/(4x^3)$.
