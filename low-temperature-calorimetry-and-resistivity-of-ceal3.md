# Low-temperature calorimetry and resistivity of CeAl3

↑ **Parent:** [Heavy fermion material](heavy-fermion-material.md)

The 1975 measurements of [CeAl3](ceal3.md) below $0.2\,\mathrm K$ found an [electronic heat capacity coefficient](electronic-heat-capacity-coefficient.md) of $1.62\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-2}}$ and a quadratic electrical-resistivity coefficient of $35\,\mathrm{\mu\Omega\,cm\,K^{-2}}$. These are complementary thermal and transport signatures of an unusually dense low-energy electronic spectrum.[Abstract: the two measured coefficients and low-temperature range](4f-virtual-bound-state-formation-in-ceal3-at-low-temperatures-by-klaus-andres-and-others.md) A temperature sweep and a $C/T$ versus $T^2$ fit separate the electronic intercept from a Debye lattice term; the transport comparison tests a [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md) temperature law.
