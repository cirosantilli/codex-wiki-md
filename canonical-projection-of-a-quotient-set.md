# Canonical projection of a quotient set

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient set](quotient-set.md)

The canonical projection of a quotient set is the [function](function-split.md) $q(x)=[x]$. It is [surjective](surjective-function.md): every [set element](element-of-a-set.md) of $X/{\sim}$ is $[x]$ for some $x\in X$. Two [set elements](element-of-a-set.md) have the same image exactly when they are related by the [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md). Its [fibers](fiber-of-a-function.md) are precisely the [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md):

$$
q^{-1}(\{[x]\})=[x].
$$

Here the left side is the [preimage](preimage.md) of a [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) [subset](subset.md) of the [quotient set](quotient-set.md); its members are the original [set elements](element-of-a-set.md) in the class $[x]$. In the diagram, the three arrows arriving at $[1]$ start at $1$, $4$ and $7$.
