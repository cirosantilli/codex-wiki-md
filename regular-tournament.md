# Regular tournament

↑ **Parent:** [Tournament (graph theory)](tournament-graph-theory.md)

A regular [tournament](tournament-graph-theory.md) has equal [outdegree](outdegree.md) and indegree at every vertex. On $2k+1$ vertices, label by residues modulo $2k+1$ and direct $i\to j$ when $j-i\in\{1,\ldots,k\}$. This gives an explicit regular tournament. It contains no outward-oriented star with more than $k$ leaves.
