# Piston-driven shallow-water bore

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic bore](hydraulic-bore.md)

A wall moving at constant [speed](speed.md) $U>0$ into stationary water of depth $h_0$ produces a [hydraulic bore](hydraulic-bore.md) of speed $V>U$ in the uniform-state [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) model. Behind the bore the fluid [velocity](velocity.md) equals $U$ and its depth $H$ obeys $H/h_0=V/(V-U)$. Put $F=U/\sqrt{gh_0}$ and $v=V/U$. The moving [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) give $F^2v=[(H/h_0)^2-1]/2$, hence the displayed equation for $v>1$. Its left side is a [strictly increasing function](strictly-increasing-function.md) from $F^2$ to infinity, while its right side is a [strictly decreasing function](strictly-decreasing-function.md) from infinity to one. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) therefore gives exactly one admissible $V$ for every $F>0$.
