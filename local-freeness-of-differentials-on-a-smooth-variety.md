# Local freeness of differentials on a smooth variety

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md)

An invertible maximal-rank minor in the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) eliminates generators and gives a local surjection from $\mathcal O_X^n$ to the [Kähler differential sheaf](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md). For an integral smooth [variety](algebraic-variety.md) the map is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) at the [generic point](generic-point.md). Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is a submodule of a [free module](free-module.md) over an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), so it is a [torsion-free module](torsion-free-module.md); vanishing generically forces it to be zero. This gives local free frames for differentials.
