# Isothermal dipolar accretion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube](transonic-accretion-in-a-dipolar-flux-tube.md)

A narrow [dipolar flux-tube area](dipolar-flux-tube-area.md) $A(z)\propto z^3$ gives $A\rho v=\dot M$ for inward speed $v>0$. The [isothermal magnetic Bernoulli integral](isothermal-magnetic-bernoulli-integral.md) in $\Phi=-GM/z$ then gives $(v-c_s^2/v)v\prime=3c_s^2/z-GM/z^2$. A smooth [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) crosses at $z_s=GM/(3c_s^2)$ with $v_s=c_s$, $\rho_s=e^{5/2}\rho_\infty$ and $\dot M=\rho_sc_sA(z_s)$. The crossing must lie in the domain; subsonic branches need not attain it.
