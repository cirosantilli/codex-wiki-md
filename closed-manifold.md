# Closed manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_manifold)

A closed manifold is a compact [manifold with boundary](manifold-with-boundary.md) whose boundary is empty. Here “closed” includes compactness.
