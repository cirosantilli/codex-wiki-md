# Polynomial coefficient sum norm

↑ **Parent:** [Norm](norm.md)

For a real or complex [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p(t)=\sum_{k\geq0}a_kt^k$, this finite sum defines a [norm](norm.md): definiteness follows from uniqueness of [polynomial coefficients](coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md), [absolute homogeneity of a norm](absolute-homogeneity-of-a-norm.md) follows termwise, and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) follows by summing $|a_k+b_k|\leq|a_k|+|b_k|$. On $[0,1]$, its [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) satisfies $\|p\|_\infty\leq\|p\|_{\mathrm{coef},1}$, since $|t^k|\leq1$. The reverse comparison has no constant independent of the [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md): for $p_n(t)=(1-t)^n$, the two [norms](norm.md) are $1$ and $2^n$ respectively, by the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md).
