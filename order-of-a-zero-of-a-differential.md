# Order of a zero of a differential

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md)

If a local [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) $r$-differential is $z^m u(z)\,dz^r$, where $u(0)\ne0$, its [zero order](order-of-a-zero-of-a-differential.md) is $m$. The order is unchanged by a [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) coordinate change with nonzero [derivative](derivative.md). For a [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md) the [cone angle](cone-angle.md) is $2\pi(m+1)$, and for a [holomorphic quadratic differential](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md) it is $(m+2)\pi$.
