# Surface conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Surface current density](surface-current-density.md)

For a thin layer with essentially uniform tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md), integrating [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) through its thickness gives the [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) [tensor](tensor.md) $\boldsymbol\sigma_s$. The two-dimensional form of [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) is $\mathbf K=\boldsymbol\sigma_s\mathbf E_\parallel$, with [surface current density](surface-current-density.md) $\mathbf K$. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is $\mathrm S=\mathrm{A/V}$: [amperes](ampere.md) per [metre](metre.md) divided by [volts](volt.md) per [metre](metre.md). When response is the same in every in-plane direction, the [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) reduces to a scalar $\Sigma$; for uniform bulk [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) $\sigma$ and thickness $t$, $\Sigma=\sigma t$.
