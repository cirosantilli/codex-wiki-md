# Surface conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Surface current density](surface-current-density.md)

Surface conductivity is the conductivity of a thin layer integrated through its thickness. The sheet form of [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) is $\mathbf K=\Sigma\mathbf E_\parallel$.
