# Robust optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)

Robust optimization chooses a decision while accounting for every parameter in a prescribed uncertainty set. A worst-case maximization takes the form $\sup_x\inf_{r\in\mathcal U}F(x,r)$ with the decision chosen before the uncertain parameter. With affine dependence on the uncertainty, the [support function](support-function.md) and [linear programming duality](linear-programming-duality.md) often replace the inner optimization by explicit constraints.
