# Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

On a proper scheme over a field, the [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) is $\chi(X,\mathcal F)=\sum_i(-1)^i\dim H^i(X,\mathcal F)$. The sum is finite by [Grothendieck vanishing](grothendieck-vanishing.md), and [long exact sequences in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) make it additive in [short exact sequences of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md).
