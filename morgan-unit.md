# Morgan (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic map](genetic-map.md)

A morgan is a unit of [genetic map](genetic-map.md) distance: an [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of one exchange along the transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md) in the interval. One morgan equals 100 [centimorgans](centimorgan.md); it does not mean a [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) of one.
