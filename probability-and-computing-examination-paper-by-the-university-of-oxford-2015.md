# Probability and Computing examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The three-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals examination dated 1 June 2015, Trinity Term 2015. The released [examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains the original as `2015-probc.pdf`. Questions 1–2 occupy printed p. 2; Question 2(f)–(g) and Question 3 occupy printed p. 3. The original p. 2 defines $x_j$ as an [expected value](expected-value-split.md); its assertions in Question 2(b) and (c) are false for two bins, rather than transcription gaps.
