# Variance-stabilizing transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Delta method](delta-method.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variance-stabilizing_transformation)

If a response has mean $\mu$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $V(\mu)$, the [delta method](delta-method.md) gives $\operatorname{Var}(g(Y))\approx g'(\mu)^2V(\mu)$. Choosing $g'(\mu)$ proportional to $V(\mu)^{-1/2}$ makes this first-order [variance](variance-split.md) independent of $\mu$. For $V(\mu)=k\mu^2$ on positive means, the resulting transformation is the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md). This is a local approximation and requires the transformation to be defined on the observations; it does not make arbitrary normal responses positive or preserve an exact [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
