# Ring of integers of Q of pth root p

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$ and $\alpha=\sqrt[p]{p}$, the polynomial $X^p-p$ is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md) at $p$, and

$$
\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(\alpha)}=\mathbb Z[\alpha].
$$

The [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) reduces the proof to the prime $p$. At the unique prime ideal above $p$, one has $v(\alpha)=1$ and $v(p)=p$, which rules out an integral element $g(\alpha)/p$ with $g\notin p\mathbb Z[X]$.
