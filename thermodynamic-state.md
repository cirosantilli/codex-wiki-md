# Thermodynamic state

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)

An equilibrium [thermodynamic state](thermodynamic-state.md) specifies sufficient independent macroscopic properties, together with material identity and constraints, to determine the other equilibrium properties. For a uniform fixed-composition [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), its [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) determine its [pressure](pressure-split.md) through the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md). These properties specify a state, without specifying a [thermodynamic process](thermodynamic-process.md) that prepared it.[Thermodynamics and Propulsion, §1.2.3: state specification](thermodynamics-and-propulsion.md)
