# Weyl algebra has no nonzero finite-dimensional modules

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md)

For the complex [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md) $A_n$ with $n\geq1$, a unital finite-dimensional [module](module-mathematics.md) would give matrices satisfying $[\partial_1,x_1]=I$. Their [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) gives $0=\dim_\mathbb C M$, so the [module](module-mathematics.md) is zero. Characteristic zero and $n\geq1$ are essential.
