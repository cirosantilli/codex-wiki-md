# Killing dual of a coroot

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean subspace of a Cartan subalgebra](euclidean-subspace-of-a-cartan-subalgebra.md)

Let $\mathcal B(H)=B(H,\cdot)$ be the [Killing form](killing-form.md) identification of a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) with its dual. The [root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md) gives $B(H,K)=\sum_{\beta\in\Phi}\beta(H)\beta(K)$, and therefore the displayed root expansion. On the real [coroot](coroot.md) span its coefficients are real, proving $\mathcal B(\mathfrak h_{\mathbb R})\subseteq\mathbb R P$. For $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ and $H_{12}=E_{11}-E_{22}$, the formula gives $\mathcal B(H_{12})=6(L_1-L_2)$. The normalization is $B(X,Y)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}X\operatorname{ad}Y)$, not a rescaled trace form.
