<h1 id="wien-s-displacement-law">Wien's displacement law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Planck function](planck-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wien's_displacement_law)

For a [blackbody](blackbody.md), the wavelength at which $B_\lambda(T)$ is largest obeys

$$
\lambda_{\max}T=b,
$$

where $b$ is [Wien displacement constant](wien-displacement-constant.md).
