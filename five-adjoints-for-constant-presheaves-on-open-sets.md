# Five adjoints for constant presheaves on open sets

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoints to constant presheaves on open sets](adjoints-to-constant-presheaves-on-open-sets.md)

For a nonempty [topological space](topological-space.md) $S$, the [constant diagram functor](constant-diagram-functor.md) on its open-set [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md) lies in this five-term [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md) chain. The leftmost [functor](functor.md) sends $A$ to the [categorical presheaf](presheaf-category-theory.md) equal to $A$ at the empty open set and empty elsewhere. The rightmost sends $A$ to the presheaf equal to $A$ at $S$ and a singleton elsewhere. A [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) from the former is determined by its empty-open component; a transformation into the latter is determined by its whole-space component. The middle [adjunctions](adjoint-functors.md) follow from restriction maps to the empty open set and from the whole space. Neither endpoint extends: the leftmost functor fails to preserve the [terminal object](terminal-object.md), and the rightmost fails to preserve the [initial object](initial-object.md).
