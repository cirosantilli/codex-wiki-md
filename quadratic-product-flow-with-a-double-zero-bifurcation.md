# Quadratic-product flow with a double-zero bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation](bogdanov-takens-bifurcation.md)

For $\lambda>0$, the [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are the origin and $(s,s^2)$ with $s=(1\pm\sqrt{1+4\mu})/2$. The nonzero branches have Jacobian trace $s-\lambda$ and determinant $\lambda s(2s-1)$. They have a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $\mu=-1/4$, $s=1/2$; the origin exchanges stability with the lower branch at the [transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md) $\mu=0$. A [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) occurs on the upper branch at $\mu=\lambda(\lambda-1)$ for $\lambda>1/2$. The point $(\mu,\lambda)=(-1/4,1/2)$ has a double zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and a nontrivial Jordan block. Its generic unfolding includes a saddle-loop curve and the intervening stable [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) region.
