# Gamma 1 congruence subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Congruence subgroup](congruence-subgroup.md)

The subgroup

$$
\Gamma_1(N)=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z):
a\equiv d\equiv1\pmod N,\ c\equiv0\pmod N
\right\}
$$

is a congruence subgroup of the [modular group](modular-group.md).
