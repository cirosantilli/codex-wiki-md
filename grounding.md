# Grounding

↑ **Parent:** [Conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md)

Connecting a [conductor](electrical-conductor.md) to a large reference reservoir fixes its [electric potential](electric-potential.md), conventionally at zero, while allowing [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) to enter or leave. Grounding does not fix the conductor's net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). In [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md), its surface is then a prescribed-potential boundary for [Poisson's equation](poisson-s-equation.md).
