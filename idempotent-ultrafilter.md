# Idempotent ultrafilter

↑ **Parent:** [Addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](addition-on-the-stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

An additive idempotent [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) satisfies $\mathcal U+\mathcal U=\mathcal U$. On positive integers it is necessarily nonprincipal, since a principal ultrafilter at $n$ adds to itself to give the principal ultrafilter at $2n$. The [idempotent-ultrafilter star-set lemma](idempotent-ultrafilter-star-set-lemma.md) converts this algebraic property into a recursive construction of finite sums. If zero is included, the trivial principal idempotent at zero must be excluded for that increasing-sequence application.
