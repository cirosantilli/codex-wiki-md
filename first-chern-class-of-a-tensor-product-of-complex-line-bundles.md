# First Chern class of a tensor product of complex line bundles

↑ **Parent:** [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md)

The [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) turns the [tensor product of vector bundles](tensor-product-of-vector-bundles.md) of [complex line bundles](complex-line-bundle.md) into addition in integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). This follows by computing the universal [complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md) on the product of two [Complex projective spaces](complex-projective-space.md) and restricting to each factor. The proof retains [torsion elements](torsion-element.md), which differential forms alone cannot detect.
