# Connected nondegenerate transition chain generates special unitary control

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

Let $H_0$ be diagonal and $H_1=\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}d_j(E_{j,j+1}+E_{j+1,j})$, with every $d_j\ne0$ and the adjacent absolute energy gaps distinct and nonzero. The squared drift adjoint action has distinct eigenvalues on these coupling components, so [Lagrange interpolation](lagrange-polynomial.md) isolates every adjacent symmetric coupling. One more [commutator](commutator.md) gives its antisymmetric partner. Their mutual brackets produce adjacent traceless diagonals, while brackets along the connected chain give all longer-range matrix units. Thus the [special unitary Lie algebra](special-unitary-lie-algebra.md) is generated. Any nonzero-trace generator also supplies the central direction, giving all of $\mathfrak u(N)$. This is a sufficient condition in the finite-dimensional freely timed real-control model; connectivity alone does not remove frequency degeneracies.
