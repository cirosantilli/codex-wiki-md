# Real-axis singular limit of the Riemann zeta function at one

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For $p>0$, [integral comparison](integral-comparison-for-a-nonincreasing-nonnegative-function.md) for $x^{-1-p}$ bounds its convergent positive series by $1/p\leq\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-1-p}\leq1+1/p$. Multiplication by $p$ gives $1\leq p\zeta(1+p)\leq1+p$, so the [squeeze theorem](squeeze-theorem.md) proves the displayed real-axis limit. This establishes the leading $1/p$ divergence by real integration alone. The convergence of the series at each fixed positive $p$ follows from bounded increasing partial sums via the [monotone convergence theorem for sequences](bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md).
