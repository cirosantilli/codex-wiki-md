# Dot-probe task

↑ **Parent:** [Attentional bias](attentional-bias.md)

A task presenting competing stimuli followed by a probe, with responses used to investigate [attention](attention.md). Altering the relation between emotional stimuli and probe location can train a contingency; transfer to emotional vulnerability and clinical functioning must be tested.[Journal of Abnormal Psychology 111, 107–123; modified dot-probe training and responses to a standardised stress task](selective-attention-and-emotional-vulnerability-assessing-the-causal-basis-of-their-association-through-the-experimental-manipulation-of-attentional-bias-by-colin-macleod-and-others.md)
