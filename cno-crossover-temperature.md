# CNO crossover temperature

↑ **Parent:** [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md)

If [proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) heating scales as $X_H^2\rho T^{\nu_{pp}}$ and [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) heating as $X_HZ_{\mathrm{CNO}}\rho T^{\nu_{\mathrm{CNO}}}$, their ratio at fixed hydrogen abundance scales as $Z_{\mathrm{CNO}}T^{\nu_{\mathrm{CNO}}-\nu_{pp}}$. Equating the rates proves the displayed crossover-temperature relation. It applies to a local power-law approximation, with unchanged coefficient normalizations apart from the catalyst abundance.
