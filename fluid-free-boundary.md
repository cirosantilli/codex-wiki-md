# Fluid free boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A [fluid free boundary](fluid-free-boundary.md) is an initially unknown material interface separating a fluid from its exterior. For a [polytropic elliptical patch in a shearing sheet](polytropic-elliptical-patch-in-a-shearing-sheet.md) bounded by vacuum, it is the level set $p=\rho=Q=0$, and the velocity is tangent to it in a steady solution.
