# Axial normal modes of an octahedral spring framework

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Place equal [masses](mass.md) at the six vertices $\pm\mathbf e_i$ of a regular [octahedron](octahedron.md) and identical unstressed axial [springs](spring.md) on its twelve edges. For the invariant axial [displacement](displacement.md) family $\mathbf u_{\pm i}=\pm q_i\mathbf e_i$, the quadratic potential and kinetic energies are

$$
V=k[(q_1+q_2)^2+(q_1+q_3)^2+(q_2+q_3)^2],\qquad
T=m\sum_i\dot q_i^2.
$$

Hence $m\ddot{\mathbf q}=-k(I+\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T})\mathbf q$. The breathing [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf1=(1,1,1)$ has squared [frequency](frequency.md) $4k/m$, while every traceless axial [vector](vector.md) with $q_1+q_2+q_3=0$ has squared [frequency](frequency.md) $k/m$. [Orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto these subspaces solves any [initial condition](initial-condition.md) in this family without an eighteen-dimensional [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md).
