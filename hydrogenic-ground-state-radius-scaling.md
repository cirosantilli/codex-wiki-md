# Hydrogenic ground-state radius scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Radial normalization of the hydrogen ground state](radial-normalization-of-the-hydrogen-ground-state.md)

For the one-electron [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) $-KZ/r$ with fixed [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md), the normalized spherically symmetric [ground state](ground-state.md) is $\psi_Z=(\alpha_Z^3/\pi)^{1/2}e^{-\alpha_Zr}$, where $\alpha_Z=Z/a_*$ and $a_*=\hbar^2/(mK)$. Its [radial probability density](radial-probability-density.md) is $p_Z(r)=4\alpha_Z^3r^2e^{-2\alpha_Zr}$. The [factorial](factorial.md) integral gives

$$
\langle r\rangle_Z=4\alpha_Z^3\frac{3!}{(2\alpha_Z)^4}=\frac3{2\alpha_Z}=\frac{3a_*}{2Z}.
$$

More generally $\langle r^q\rangle_Z=\Gamma(q+3)/[2(2\alpha_Z)^q]$ for real $q>-3$, by the [gamma function](gamma-function.md) integral. The density transformation $p_Z(r)=Zp_1(Zr)$ expresses the full scaling, not just its mean. It applies to a [hydrogen-like ion](hydrogen-like-ion.md) with one [electron](electron.md); the exact neutral [helium atom](helium-atom.md) radius additionally depends on the two-electron [Coulomb interaction](coulomb-interaction.md).
