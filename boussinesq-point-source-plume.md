# Boussinesq point-source plume

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric pure plume](axisymmetric-pure-plume.md)

A steady [top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md) supplied with kinematic [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) $f_0>0$ but zero source [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) and [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) has

$$
b=az,\qquad w=Kz^{-1/3},\qquad g'=\frac43K^2z^{-5/3},\qquad
a=\frac{6\alpha}{5},\quad K^3=\frac{25f_0}{48\alpha^2}.
$$

The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) and momentum balance yield these constants. Its [Froude number](froude-number.md) satisfies $w^2/(g'b)=5/(8\alpha)$. The [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md) requires $g'/g\ll1$ and therefore fails at the ideal point source itself.
