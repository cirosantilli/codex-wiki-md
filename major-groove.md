# Major groove

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

The [major groove](major-groove.md) of an ordinary [B-DNA](b-dna.md) duplex exposes base-pair edges whose donor, acceptor and hydrophobic patterns distinguish oriented base pairs. Groove accessibility changes with helical conformation, and protein recognition can combine these patterns with backbone and shape contacts.
