# Bochner-Kodaira-Nakano identity

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md)

For the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) of a Hermitian [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) on a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the two [Dolbeault Laplacians](dolbeault-laplacian.md) differ by $[iF\wedge,\Lambda]$, where $F$ is its [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) and $\Lambda$ is the [adjoint Lefschetz operator](adjoint-lefschetz-operator.md). The curvature term controls vanishing estimates.
