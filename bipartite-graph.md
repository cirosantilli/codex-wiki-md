# Bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipartite_graph)

A graph is bipartite when its vertices split into two classes and every edge joins the two different classes.
