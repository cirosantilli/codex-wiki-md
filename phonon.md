# Phonon

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonon)

A phonon is a bosonic quantum of a crystal's normal modes. Because phonon number is not conserved in thermal equilibrium, its [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is zero.
