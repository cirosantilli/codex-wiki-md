# Floquet multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floquet_multiplier)

A [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) is an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of a one-period linear evolution map. For a homogeneous [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) with continuous coefficients of period $T>0$, this map is the [monodromy matrix of a periodic linear system](monodromy-matrix-of-a-periodic-linear-system.md) $N$. If $Nv=mv$ for a nonzero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $v$, the associated [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) with $Y(0)=v$ satisfies $Y(t+T)=mY(t)$. Its exponential rate is $T^{-1}\log|m|$, the corresponding [Floquet growth rate](floquet-growth-rate.md). A [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) equal to one gives a $T$-[periodic solution](periodic-solution.md) in that [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) direction; a nontrivial [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) at a unit-modulus [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) can still produce polynomial growth.

For a nonlinear [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md), the [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md) are [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the one-period map of its [variational equation](variational-equation.md). A nonconstant [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) of a smooth [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md) has a [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) equal to one in its tangent direction: differentiating the [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) with respect to its starting phase gives a [periodic solution](periodic-solution.md) of the [variational equation](variational-equation.md). The derivative of a transverse [Poincaré map](poincare-map.md) removes this phase direction and retains the transverse [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md). Transverse [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md) strictly inside the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) give local attraction, while one outside gives instability; a transverse [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) requires further analysis. For a planar [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md), the transverse [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) is given by the [divergence test for a planar periodic orbit](divergence-test-for-a-planar-periodic-orbit.md).
