# HAT selection

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)

[HAT selection](hat-selection.md) uses hypoxanthine, aminopterin and thymidine to distinguish cells able to maintain nucleotide supply through salvage metabolism under de novo synthesis blockade. In a conventional hybridoma scheme it selects suitable fusion products, not antigen specificity.
