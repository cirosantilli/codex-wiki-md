<h1 id="norm-conservation-of-a-semidiscrete-schrodinger-equation">Norm conservation of a semidiscrete Schrödinger equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Method of lines](method-of-lines.md)

If a spatial [method of lines](method-of-lines.md) has a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $H$, its generator $-iH$ is a [skew-Hermitian matrix](skew-hermitian-matrix.md). The [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) $e^{-itH}$ is a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md), so it preserves the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) and any constant-volume [discrete L2 norm](discrete-l2-norm.md). Equivalently, differentiating the squared [norm](norm.md) gives $2\operatorname{Re}(-iU^*HU)=0$. A real sampled potential preserves the Hermitian property of a symmetric discrete Laplacian. Subsequent time discretization must be assessed separately.
