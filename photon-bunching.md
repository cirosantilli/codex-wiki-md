# Photon bunching

↑ **Parent:** [Photon statistics](photon-statistics.md)

[Photon bunching](photon-bunching.md) is enhanced near-coincident detection relative to an appropriate independent-count reference. It need not require nonclassical [light](light.md). In a classical intensity model with nonnegative instantaneous [light intensity](light-intensity.md) $I$, ideal detection with a conditional [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) gives

$$
g^{(2)}(0)=\frac{\langle I^2\rangle}{\langle I\rangle^2}
=1+\frac{\operatorname{Var}I}{\langle I\rangle^2}\geq1.
$$

A fluctuating intensity therefore bunches counts. For a stationary classical intensity with finite second moment, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) further gives $\langle I(t)I(t+\tau)\rangle\leq\langle I^2\rangle$. A zero-delay correlation smaller than one at a later delay cannot be explained by this model.
