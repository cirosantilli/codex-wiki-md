# Schrieffer-Wolff transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md)

A perturbative [unitary conjugation](unitary-conjugation.md) eliminating couplings between chosen separated energy sectors. For $H=H_0+V$ and an [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) $S$ satisfying $[S,H_0]=-V$ at leading order, the transformed [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) contains $\tfrac12[S,V]$ at second order. Exactly resonant coupled states must be retained in a common block. The method was used for the Anderson-to-Kondo relation by [John Robert Schrieffer](john-robert-schrieffer.md) and [P. A. Wolff](p-a-wolff.md).
