# Longitudinal wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_wave)

A [longitudinal wave](longitudinal-wave.md) has an oscillating [displacement](displacement.md) parallel to the direction of propagation. An [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) in a fluid supplies a familiar example.
