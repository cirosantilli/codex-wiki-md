# Screening effect

↑ **Parent:** [Atom (Physics)](atom-physics.md)

[Electrons](electron.md) partially shield an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). At large distances a neutral [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) therefore does not generate the unscreened [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) of its bare [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md).
