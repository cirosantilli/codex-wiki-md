# Classification of incompressible surfaces in Seifert fibered spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md)

In an orientable irreducible [Seifert fibered space](seifert-fibered-space.md), an essential two-sided [incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md) that is also a [boundary-incompressible surface](boundary-incompressible-surface.md) can be isotoped to a [horizontal surface in a Seifert fibered space](horizontal-surface-in-a-seifert-fibered-space.md) or a [vertical surface in a Seifert fibered space](vertical-surface-in-a-seifert-fibered-space.md).
