# Standard affine charts of the quotient Grassmannian

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmannian of locally free quotients](grassmannian-of-locally-free-quotients.md)

Choose $r$ of the $n$ [basis](basis.md) vectors and require their images in the quotient to be a [basis](basis.md). Normalize those columns to the identity; the remaining $r(n-r)$ entries are arbitrary affine coordinates. Between column sets $I,J$, the overlap is the [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) where $\det A_J$ is a unit, and the new normalized [matrix](matrix.md) is $A_J^{-1}A$. These [matrix](matrix.md) transformations satisfy the cocycle identity. Locally every surjective [matrix](matrix.md) has an invertible maximal minor, so the charts cover all rank-$r$ locally free quotients. The normalized [matrices](matrix.md) glue the universal quotient and give the natural identification with its [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md).
