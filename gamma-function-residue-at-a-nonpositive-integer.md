# Gamma function residue at a nonpositive integer

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

The [Gamma function recurrence](gamma-function-recurrence.md) and $\Gamma(z)\sim1/z$ at zero imply a [simple pole](simple-pole.md) at every $z=-n$, $n\geq0$, with [residue](residue.md) $(-1)^n/n!$.
