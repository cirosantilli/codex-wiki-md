# Gravitational field of an infinite plane sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

An infinite plane sheet of surface [mass density](density.md) $\Sigma$ has constant gravitational-field magnitude $2\pi G\Sigma$ on either side, directed toward the sheet. A Gaussian pillbox has equal inward [flux](flux.md) on its two caps and enclosed [mass](mass.md) $\Sigma A$, so $-2gA=-4\pi G\Sigma A$. A uniform infinite slab has the same exterior formula with $\Sigma=\rho h$. Translating an infinite sheet does not change its exterior field, whereas adding a slab changes its surface [mass density](density.md).
