# Dopant freeze-out

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

At sufficiently low [temperature](temperature-split.md), donor or acceptor ionization becomes energetically unfavourable. Carriers remain bound to dopants rather than contributing to conduction, reducing the mobile [carrier density](carrier-density.md). This [dopant freeze-out](dopant-freeze-out.md) is distinct from the high-temperature intrinsic regime, where thermal excitation across the [band gap](band-gap.md) overwhelms the net dopant contribution.
