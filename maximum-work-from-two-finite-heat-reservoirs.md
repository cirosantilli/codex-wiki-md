# Maximum work from two finite heat reservoirs

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

With constant [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) $C_1,C_2$, a [reversible heat engine](reversible-heat-engine.md) taking two bodies to a common [temperature](temperature-split.md) has $T_f=T_1^{C_1/(C_1+C_2)}T_2^{C_2/(C_1+C_2)}$. The maximum [work](work.md) is $C_1T_1+C_2T_2-(C_1+C_2)T_f$. [heat](heat-split.md) isolation does not prohibit transferring [work](work.md) to a store included in the total isolated system.
