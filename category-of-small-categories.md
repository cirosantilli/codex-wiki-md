# Category of small categories

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

Objects are [small categories](small-category.md) and [morphisms](morphism.md) are [functors](functor.md). Its products pair objects and arrows componentwise. Its [exponential objects](exponential-object.md) are [functor categories](functor-category.md), as shown by the [currying adjunction for small categories](currying-adjunction-for-small-categories.md).
