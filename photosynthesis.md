# Photosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photosynthesis)

Conversion of light energy into chemical energy used to build biological material. Oxygenic photosynthesis uses [water](water.md) as an electron donor and releases [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md); other forms do not necessarily produce oxygen. Its atmospheric oxygen source is a major component of the modern terrestrial [atmospheric biosignature gas](atmospheric-biosignature-gas.md) interpretation.
