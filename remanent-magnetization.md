# Remanent magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)

Remanent magnetization is [magnetization](magnetization.md) retained after the applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) has been removed. Magnetic minerals cooling in an ambient field can preserve information about that field in a planetary crust. Such a record may outlive the [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md) which originally produced it. Crustal remanence therefore differs from a present field maintained by conducting-fluid motion.
