# Fixed-point subspace of a group action

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md)

For a linear [group representation](group-representation.md), the common fixed vectors of a subgroup form an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) for any [equivariant map](equivariant-map.md) restricted to it. Its dimension is central to symmetry-based branching theorems. It need not be invariant under the entire group; conjugate subgroups have conjugate fixed-point subspaces.
