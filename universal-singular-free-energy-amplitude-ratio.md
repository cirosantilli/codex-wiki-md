# Universal singular free-energy amplitude ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Universality class](universality-class.md)

Under the [scaling hypothesis for critical phenomena](scaling-hypothesis-for-critical-phenomena.md), $F_s=A_f|a_tt|^{D/y_t}\Phi_\pm(0)$ at zero field. The common free-energy and thermal metric factors cancel from $A_+/A_-$, leaving a property of the [universality class](universality-class.md). This concerns the singular part at equal $|t|$, not the ratio of full [free energies](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) including arbitrary analytic identity terms. Matching constants in $\Phi_\pm$ belong to the same scaling solution and cannot be assigned independently.
