# High-frequency eigenvalue error of the discrete Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet discrete Laplacian](dirichlet-discrete-laplacian.md)

For $-\Delta+I$ on the unit square with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the continuous [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda_{m,n}=1+\pi^2(m^2+n^2)$, whereas the mesh-$1/N$ [five-point Laplacian](five-point-laplacian.md) gives $\Lambda_{m,n}=1+4N^2[\sin^2(m\pi/(2N))+\sin^2(n\pi/(2N))]$. The inequality $\sin z\leq z$ yields $\Lambda_{m,n}\leq\lambda_{m,n}$. For fixed indices, [Taylor's theorem](taylor-s-theorem.md) gives an error of order $N^{-2}$; for indices $m=n=N-1$, division by $N^2$ gives the positive [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) $2\pi^2-8$. Thus an error estimate for fixed smooth [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) need not be uniform over all grid-resolvable [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md).
