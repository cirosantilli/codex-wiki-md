# Increasing-gradient functional for poorly conducting convection

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov functional](lyapunov-functional.md)

For the [conserved-mean convection amplitude equations](conserved-mean-convection-amplitude-equations.md) before [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) reduction, the functional $V=\langle\mu|\nabla T|^2/2-(\Delta T)^2/2-|\nabla T|^4/4\rangle$ generates a [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) with increasing $V$. Periodic [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) identifies its variational gradient with the evolution equation. Completing the square gives $V=\mu^2/4-\langle(|\nabla T|^2-\mu)^2/4+(\Delta T)^2/2\rangle\leq\mu^2/4$. Hence $\int_0^\infty\langle T_t^2\rangle\,dt<\infty$ along a globally smooth solution. Under compactness of the orbit, its limiting states are stationary; a nonstationary periodic orbit is impossible.
