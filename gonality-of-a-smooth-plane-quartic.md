# Gonality of a smooth plane quartic

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth plane quartic](smooth-plane-quartic.md)

Projection from a point of a [smooth plane quartic](smooth-plane-quartic.md) gives a degree-three map to the [projective line](projective-line.md). A degree-two map would make the curve [hyperelliptic](hyperelliptic-curve.md), but its [canonical map](canonical-map-of-a-smooth-plane-curve.md) is its plane embedding, whereas a hyperelliptic canonical map is not an embedding. Its [gonality](gonality.md) is therefore three.
