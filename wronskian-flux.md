# Wronskian flux

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For $E''+k^2(x)E=0$ with real $k^2$, differentiation gives $\mathcal J'=\operatorname{Im}(E^*E'')=0$. This conserved [Wronskian](wronskian.md) measures directed [wave](wave-split.md) flux. In a one-dimensional [ordinary plasma wave](ordinary-plasma-wave.md), the mean [Poynting flux](poynting-flux.md) is $c^2\mathcal J/(8\pi\omega)$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md). It proves $R+T=1$ for a lossless two-channel scattering problem.
