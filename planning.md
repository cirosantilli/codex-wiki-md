# Planning

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence-split.md)

Computing or comparing sequences of actions using representations of their expected consequences and relevant constraints. A plan can be revised after a goal, transition or inferred state changes. [Planning](planning.md) is distinct from merely evaluating one fixed control policy or repeating a cached response.
