# Laguerre differential operator on polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md)

On polynomials with weighted inner product

$$
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_0^\infty f(x)g(x)e^{-x}\,dx,
$$

the operator

$$
Lf=xf''+(1-x)f'
$$

is self-adjoint because $e^{-x}Lf=(xe^{-x}f')'$. Its eigenvalues on polynomials of degree at most $n$ are $0,-1,\ldots,-n$; its eigenvectors are scalar multiples of the [Laguerre polynomials](laguerre-polynomial.md).
