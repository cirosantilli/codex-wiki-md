# Codazzi equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss–Codazzi equations](gauss-codazzi-equations.md)

For a Euclidean embedded submanifold, the covariant derivative of the second fundamental form satisfies

$$
(\nabla_XA)(Y,Z)=(\nabla_YA)(X,Z).
$$
