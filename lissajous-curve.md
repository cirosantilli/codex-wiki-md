# Lissajous curve

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric curve](parametric-curve.md)

A Lissajous curve is a [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) with coordinates $x(t)=A\sin(at+\delta)$ and $y(t)=B\sin(bt)$. The frequency ratio and phase shift control its crossings and whether its trajectory closes.
