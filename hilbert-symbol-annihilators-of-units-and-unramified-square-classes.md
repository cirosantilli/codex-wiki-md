# Hilbert-symbol annihilators of units and unramified square classes

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Hilbert symbol](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md)

In the [square-class group of a p-adic field](square-class-group-of-a-p-adic-field.md), the unit [subgroup](subgroup.md) consists of classes of even [valuation](valuation.md). The unramified [subgroup](subgroup.md) consists of the trivial class and the unique nonsquare class defining an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of degree two. For odd $p$, the two [subgroups](subgroup.md) coincide with $\{1,u\}$ for a nonsquare unit $u$. For $p=2$, they are $\{1,-1,5,-5\}$ and $\{1,5\}$. Norms from an unramified quadratic [local field](local-field.md) extension have even [valuation](valuation.md), and every unit is a norm. The norm characterization of the [quadratic Hilbert symbol](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md) therefore gives both displayed annihilator identities. Over $\mathbb Q_2$, the unramified class is $5$ because $(1+\sqrt5)/2$ satisfies $T^2-T-1$, whose reduction modulo two is irreducible and separable.
