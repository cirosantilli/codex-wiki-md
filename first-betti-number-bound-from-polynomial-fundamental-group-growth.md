# First Betti-number bound from polynomial fundamental-group growth

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial growth of properly discontinuous isometry groups](polynomial-growth-of-properly-discontinuous-isometry-groups.md)

For a compact connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md), its finitely generated [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) acts freely and [properly discontinuously](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md) on the complete [universal cover](universal-cover.md). The preceding [polynomial growth of properly discontinuous isometry groups](polynomial-growth-of-properly-discontinuous-isometry-groups.md) bound applies. The degree-one [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) identifies first integral [homology](homology-split.md) with the [abelianization](abelianization.md) of the fundamental group. If its free rank is $b_1(M)$, this group maps onto $\mathbb Z^{b_1(M)}$. Choosing lifts of a basis gives at least a constant times $m^{b_1(M)}$ distinct elements in a word ball of radius a constant times $m$. The upper bound of order $m^{\dim M}$ forces the displayed inequality.
