# Single-vertex scalar two-point graph

↑ **Parent:** [Tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md)

For interaction $\lambda_{2k}\phi^{2k}/(2k)!$, one vertex with two labelled external legs has $k-1$ independently paired [tadpole diagrams](tadpole-diagram.md). Counting contractions gives symmetry factor $S=2^{k-1}(k-1)!$. In a normalized [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md), its amputated contribution is $-\lambda_{2k}T_D(m)^{k-1}/S$, where $T_D$ is the [tadpole integral](tadpole-integral.md). It is linear in the coupling but has $k-1$ loops and a highest four-dimensional [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md) of order $k-1$ for nonzero [mass](mass.md).
