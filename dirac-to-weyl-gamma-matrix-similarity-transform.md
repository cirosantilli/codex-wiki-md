# Dirac-to-Weyl gamma-matrix similarity transform

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md)

For the [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md), the [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md)

$$
U=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}I_2&-I_2\\I_2&I_2\end{pmatrix}
$$

sends $\widetilde\gamma^0$ to $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&I_2\\I_2&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ while commuting with every spatial [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md). This is the standard [Weyl representation of the gamma matrices](weyl-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md) with spatial blocks $(0,\sigma^i;-\sigma^i,0)$. The [similarity transformation](similarity-transformation.md) preserves the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) relations. Changing block-order or chirality conventions can change the displayed transformation, so its direction must be specified.
