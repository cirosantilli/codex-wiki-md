# Hyperbolic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic geometry](hyperbolic-geometry.md)

A surface with a complete [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) of constant [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) $-1$. Its local model is the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md); the orientable case is a quotient by a free [properly discontinuous group action](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md) of orientation-preserving isometries.
