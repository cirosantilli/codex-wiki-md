# Electric current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_current)

An [electric current](electric-current.md) measures how quickly signed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) crosses a chosen fixed [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md) $S$. Choose its [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$; positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) crossing along $\mathbf n$ counts positively, while crossing against it counts negatively. If $Q_{\mathrm{cross}}(t)$ is the accumulated signed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) transported through $S$, then

$$
I_S(t)=\frac{dQ_{\mathrm{cross}}}{dt}=\int_S\mathbf J(\mathbf r,t)\cdot\mathbf n\,dA,\qquad Q_{\mathrm{cross}}(t_2)-Q_{\mathrm{cross}}(t_1)=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}I_S(t)\,dt.
$$

Here $\mathbf J$ is the [current density](current-density.md); the [surface integral](surface-integral.md) collects its local normal contributions. Reversing the [unit normal](unit-normal.md) reverses $I_S$. The [electric current](electric-current.md) is a signed [scalar](scalar.md) associated with the whole chosen [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md), whereas [current density](current-density.md) is a [vector](vector.md) at each point. The [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is the [ampere](ampere.md), one [coulomb](coulomb.md) per second. [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, University Physics II, §9.1: transported charge and current](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-1-electrical-current); [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.2: current density and its surface integral](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-2-model-of-conduction-in-metals).

For a constant [electric current](electric-current.md) of $2\,\mathrm A$ over a [time](time-in-physics.md) interval of $3\,\mathrm s$, the transported [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is $6\,\mathrm C$. This need not be [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) accumulating in the wire: in steady transport, [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) enters and leaves. For a closed [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md) with outward [unit normal](unit-normal.md), [electric charge conservation](charge-conservation.md) instead gives $I_{\mathrm{out}}=-dQ_{\mathrm{inside}}/dt$. Keeping transported and enclosed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) distinct connects [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) bookkeeping to the local [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md).
