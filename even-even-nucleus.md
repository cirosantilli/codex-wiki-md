# Even-even nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

An [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) with even [proton](proton.md) number $Z$ and even [neutron](neutron-split.md) number $N$. The simple [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) picture couples the constituent pairs to zero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The collective examples below concern a $0^+$ [ground state](ground-state.md), where the superscript denotes positive [parity](parity.md), and its associated low-lying excitations; even $A=Z+N$ alone would also allow odd $Z$ and odd $N$.
