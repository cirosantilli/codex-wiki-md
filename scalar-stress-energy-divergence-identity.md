# Scalar stress-energy divergence identity

↑ **Parent:** [Minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md)

For a [minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md) with a [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) and the [symmetric Hessian of a scalar field](symmetric-hessian-of-a-scalar-field.md) give the displayed off-shell identity. It is a special case of the [diffeomorphism Noether identity for a scalar field](diffeomorphism-noether-identity-for-a-scalar-field.md), and gives [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) when the scalar equation is satisfied. No gravitational field equation is needed.
