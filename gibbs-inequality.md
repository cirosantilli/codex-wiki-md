# Gibbs inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbs_inequality)

For [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) $p$ and $q$ on the same finite set,

$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\Vert q)
=\sum_i p_i\log\frac{p_i}{q_i}\geq0,
$$

with equality exactly when $p=q$ on the support of $p$.
