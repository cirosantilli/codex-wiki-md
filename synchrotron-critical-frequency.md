# Synchrotron critical frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md)

For an ultrarelativistic circular orbit of curvature radius $\rho$, the conventional critical angular [frequency](frequency.md) is $\omega_c=3\gamma^3c/(2\rho)$. It sets the broad spectral scale rather than a strict maximum [frequency](frequency.md).[Critical energy and critical mode number](synchrotron-radiation-in-the-geant4-physics-reference-manual-by-geant4-collaboration.md)
