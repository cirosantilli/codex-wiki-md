# Tangent through the origin to an exponential curve

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent line](tangent-line.md)

For a nonzero real [constant](constant-mathematics.md) $a$, the [tangent line](tangent-line.md) to $y=e^{ax}$ at $x_*$ passes through the origin precisely when $e^{ax_*}-x_*ae^{ax_*}=0$. Thus $x_*=1/a$, $y_*=e$, and its [slope](slope.md) is $k=ae$. This gives the unique such [tangent line](tangent-line.md). If $a=0$, the graph is the horizontal line $y=1$ and no [tangent line](tangent-line.md) through the origin exists. The equality between the [derivative](derivative.md) and the slope from the origin, $f'(x_*)=f(x_*)/x_*$, is useful for other [differentiable functions](differentiable-function.md) too.
