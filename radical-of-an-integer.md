# Radical of an integer

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic function](arithmetic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_of_an_integer)

The radical of a positive [integer](integer.md) is the product of its distinct [prime factors](prime-factor.md), with $\operatorname{rad}(1)=1$. It is the squarefree integer recording which primes occur, independently of their exponents. Equality of radicals is equivalent to mutual divisibility by sufficiently high positive powers.
