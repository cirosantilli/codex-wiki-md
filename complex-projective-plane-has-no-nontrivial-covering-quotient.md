# Complex projective plane has no nontrivial covering quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point property of even-dimensional complex projective space](fixed-point-property-of-even-dimensional-complex-projective-space.md)

The [Complex projective plane](complex-projective-plane.md) is simply connected. If it were the total space of a covering of degree at least two, it would be the [universal cover](universal-cover.md) and have a nonidentity [deck transformation](deck-transformation.md). Such a transformation acts by $u\mapsto\pm u$ on $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2)=\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3)$, giving [Lefschetz number](lefschetz-number.md) 1 or 3. It therefore has a fixed point, impossible for a nonidentity deck transformation.
