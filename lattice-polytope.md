# Lattice polytope

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polytope](convex-polytope.md)

A lattice polytope is the convex hull of finitely many points of a [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md). It need not contain the origin. Integral translation changes its associated homogeneous semigroup ring by a grading-preserving isomorphism.
