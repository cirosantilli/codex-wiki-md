# Vanishing component theorem for a Lorentz four-vector

↑ **Parent:** [Four-vector](four-vector.md)

If a fixed component of a [Lorentz four-vector](four-vector.md) vanishes in every [Lorentz frame](lorentz-frame.md), the vector is zero. For its time component, arbitrary infinitesimal boosts force every spatial component to vanish; for a spatial component, first use rotations and then a boost. Applied to the incoming-minus-outgoing total [four-momentum](four-momentum.md), this shows that energy balance in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) implies full [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md). The statement fails for general [tensors](tensor.md): a nonzero antisymmetric tensor has zero diagonal components in all frames.
