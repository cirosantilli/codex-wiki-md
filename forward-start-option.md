# Forward start option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_start_option)

A [financial option](option-finance.md) whose strike or other exercise terms are fixed at a specified future date, with expiry later still. A [forward-start call option](forward-start-call-option.md) pays the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) of terminal [stock](stock.md) price minus that future strike. Specifying the rule for fixing the strike is part of defining the [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md).
