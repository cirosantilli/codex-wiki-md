# Null focusing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md)

For a hypersurface-orthogonal null congruence under the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) in $D$ spacetime dimensions, an initially negative expansion $\theta_0$ diverges to $-\infty$ within affine distance at most $(D-2)/|\theta_0|$.
