# Wave pseudomomentum

↑ **Parent:** [Wave action (fluid dynamics)](wave-action-fluid-dynamics.md)

The [wavevector](wavevector.md) times [wave action](wave-action-fluid-dynamics.md) gives the canonical momentum associated with translations of a wave's [phase](phase-waves.md). It describes momentum exchange with a slowly varying mean [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md), rather than simply the spatial average of oscillating fluid momentum. For horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, its horizontal component is $kE/\widehat\omega$. A convention using [displacement pseudomomentum of an internal gravity wave](displacement-pseudomomentum-of-an-internal-gravity-wave.md) instead has the opposite sign.
