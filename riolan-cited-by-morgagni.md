# Riolan cited by Morgagni

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicians](list-of-physicians.md)

The physician named only as Riolanus in the Oxford extract from [The Seats and Causes of Diseases Investigated by Anatomy by Giovanni Battista Morgagni](the-seats-and-causes-of-diseases-investigated-by-anatomy-by-giovanni-battista-morgagni.md), cited for duodenal compression. The extract alone does not distinguish the elder from the younger Riolan. A portrait and exact Wikipedia article for the younger were verified as candidates, without assigning them to this uncertain reference.
