# Product surface without low-genus curves

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)

If $C$ is a smooth projective curve of genus at least three, then the smooth projective surface $C\times C$ contains no curve of geometric genus below three. On the normalization of any curve in the product, at least one coordinate projection to $C$ is nonconstant, and the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) cannot decrease genus.
