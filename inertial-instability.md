# Inertial instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inertial_instability)

Inertial instability occurs when displaced fluid parcels amplify their displacement by conserving absolute momentum in a rotating flow. For a uniformly stratified basic state on an [f-plane](f-plane.md), a necessary condition is that the [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md) have the opposite sign to the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md).
