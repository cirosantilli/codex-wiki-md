# Semicoloring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

A $k$-semicoloring of a finite [graph](graph-split.md) assigns one of $k$ colors to every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and is a proper [graph colouring](graph-coloring.md) on an [induced subgraph](induced-subgraph.md) containing at least half the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). This is weaker than a proper coloring of the whole [graph](graph-split.md), and is useful as an intermediate step in a [randomized algorithm](randomized-algorithm.md).
