# Simulated annealing

↑ **Parent:** [Heuristic optimization](heuristic-optimization.md)

For a maximization objective, [simulated annealing](simulated-annealing.md) accepts every improving proposed neighbor and accepts a worsening move of loss $\Delta$ with [probability](probability.md) $\exp(-\Delta/T)$ at temperature $T>0$. Decreasing the temperature reduces exploratory moves. Retain the best feasible solution seen. Finite practical cooling schedules carry no general global-optimality guarantee.
