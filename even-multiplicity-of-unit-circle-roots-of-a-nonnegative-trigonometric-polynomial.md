# Even multiplicity of unit-circle roots of a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md)

If $P=z^dp$ and $p$ is nonnegative on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), a [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $\zeta=e^{i\theta_0}$ there has even [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md). Indeed $p(e^{i\theta})$ is a [real analytic](real-analytic-function.md) nonnegative function, whose first nonzero term in its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) at a zero must have even order. The map $\theta\mapsto e^{i\theta}-\zeta$ has a simple zero, and multiplication by $e^{-id\theta}$ is nonvanishing, so the order equals the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) multiplicity.
