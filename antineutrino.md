# Antineutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antineutrino)

An antineutrino is the antiparticle of a neutrino and carries the opposite additive lepton number.
