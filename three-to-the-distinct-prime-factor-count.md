# Three to the distinct-prime-factor count

↑ **Parent:** [Prime omega function](prime-omega-function.md)

The [multiplicative arithmetic function](multiplicative-function.md) $f(n)=3^{\omega(n)}$ counts assignments of three labels to each distinct [prime factor](prime-factor.md). It also counts pairs of [squarefree divisors](squarefree-divisor.md) whose [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md) is the [radical of an integer](radical-of-an-integer.md) $n$.
