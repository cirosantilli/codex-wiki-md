# Prandtl ratio of scales

↑ **Parent:** [Stratified quasi-geostrophic inversion](stratified-quasi-geostrophic-inversion.md)

The Prandtl ratio compares horizontal and vertical scales in rotating [density stratification](density-stratification.md). In the [three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) operator, horizontal curvature scales as $\psi/L^2$ and vertical stretching as $(f_0^2/N^2)\psi/H^2$. Comparable terms therefore require $H/L\sim |f_0|/N$. For constant $N$, the stretched coordinate $Z=(N/|f_0|)z$ makes the operator isotropic. This aspect ratio concerns rotational and stratification effects, not the viscous-to-thermal diffusivity [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md).
