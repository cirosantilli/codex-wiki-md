# Chromatic polynomial after attaching a leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

Attaching a new [leaf](leaf-of-a-graph.md) to a graph multiplies its [chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md) by $t-1$, because after colouring the old graph the leaf may receive any colour except its neighbour's.
