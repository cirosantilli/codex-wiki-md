# Chromatic polynomial after attaching a leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

Attaching a new [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) that is a [leaf of a graph](leaf-of-a-graph.md) multiplies the [chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md) by $t-1$: after colouring the old graph, the new [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) may receive any colour except its neighbour's. Starting with one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and repeatedly adding a [leaf of a graph](leaf-of-a-graph.md) constructs any finite [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md). Thus a [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) with $n\geq 1$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) has

$$
P_T(t)=t(t-1)^{n-1}.
$$

In contrast, every pair of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in a [complete graph](complete-graph.md) must receive different colours, so the [multiplication principle](rule-of-product.md) gives the [falling factorial](falling-factorial.md)

$$
P_{K_n}(t)=t(t-1)\cdots(t-n+1).
$$

For positive [integers](integer-split.md) $t<n$ this product vanishes, expressing the impossibility of assigning distinct colours to all $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
