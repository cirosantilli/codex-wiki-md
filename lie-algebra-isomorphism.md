# Lie algebra isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A Lie algebra isomorphism is a bijective [linear map](linear-map.md) $\varphi:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak h$ satisfying $\varphi([x,y])=[\varphi(x),\varphi(y)]$. Its inverse automatically preserves the [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md).
