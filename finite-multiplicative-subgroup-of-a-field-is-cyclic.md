# Finite multiplicative subgroup of a field is cyclic

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field-is-cyclic.md)

Every finite [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of the multiplicative group of a [field](field.md) is [cyclic](cyclic-group.md). If $m$ is the exponent of $H$, commutativity lets one combine elements whose orders realize the prime-power factors of $m$, producing an element of order $m$. Every element of $H$ is a root of $X^m-1$, so the [Lagrange root bound over a field](lagrange-root-bound-over-a-field.md) gives $|H|\leq m$. Since $m\leq|H|$, equality holds and that element generates $H$.
