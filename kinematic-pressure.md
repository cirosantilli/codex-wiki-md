# Kinematic pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure.md)

Dividing a [pressure](pressure.md) disturbance by the constant reference [mass density](density.md) $\rho_0$ gives a quantity whose [gradient](gradient.md) enters the [Boussinesq equations](boussinesq-equations.md) directly as an [acceleration](acceleration.md). Its units are squared [velocity](velocity.md). Distinguish this convention from physical [pressure](pressure.md) when calculating [energy flux](energy-flux.md) or comparing [wave polarization](polarization-waves.md) formulae.
