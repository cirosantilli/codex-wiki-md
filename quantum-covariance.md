# Quantum covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Robertson-Schrödinger uncertainty relation](robertson-schrodinger-uncertainty-relation.md)

For [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md) and an admissible [pure state](pure-state.md), the [quantum covariance](quantum-covariance.md) is the real, symmetrized second moment of two centered [observables](observable.md). The displayed expression uses their [anticommutator](anticommutator.md); equivalently $C_{AB}=\operatorname{Re}\langle A'\psi|B'\psi\rangle$, which requires only the individual finite second moments. It reduces to ordinary [covariance](covariance.md) for commuting [observables](observable.md). For noncommuting [observables](observable.md), its definition does not assert that both have simultaneous sharp values drawn from a joint classical [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md).
