# Superfactorial

↑ **Parent:** [Factorial](factorial.md)

The superfactorial of a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) $n$ is the product $\prod_{k=1}^n k!$. This convention differs from other similarly named iterated factorial operations, so the product definition should be stated.
