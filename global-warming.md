# Global warming

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)

Global warming is an increase in Earth's global mean surface temperature. It is one aspect of [climate change](climate-change.md), which also includes changes in precipitation, circulation, extremes and the cryosphere. Warming driven by additional [greenhouse gases](greenhouse-gas.md) can be amplified or moderated by [climate feedbacks](climate-feedback.md).
