# Special unitary Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary Lie algebra](unitary-lie-algebra.md)

The real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of the [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) consists of traceless [skew-Hermitian matrices](skew-hermitian-matrix.md). Differentiating unitarity and determinant one gives the two constraints; exponentiating any such matrix proves their sufficiency. The [commutator](commutator.md) is again skew-Hermitian and traceless. Its real dimension is $n^2-1$, and its complexification is $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb C)$.
