# Orthogonality of supported and harmonic Dirichlet functions

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet energy space](dirichlet-energy-space.md)

Let $U\subseteq D$ be open. Regard $H_{\mathrm{supp}}=H_0^1(U)$ as functions on $D$ by [zero extension of H01](zero-extension-of-h01.md), and let $H_{\mathrm{harm}}$ consist of the [weakly harmonic Sobolev functions](weakly-harmonic-sobolev-function.md) on $U$ belonging to $H_0^1(D)$. For $h\in H_{\mathrm{harm}}$, the [Dirichlet inner product](dirichlet-inner-product.md) $(h,\phi)_\nabla$ vanishes for each [test function](test-function.md) supported in $U$. Approximate any $u\in H_{\mathrm{supp}}$ by those [test functions](test-function.md) in the [gradient](gradient.md) [norm](norm.md) and use the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). This gives $\boxed{(h,u)_\nabla=0}$. The proof also works with the inhomogeneous [zero-boundary Sobolev space](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) convention, and with a homogeneous completion realized as weak functions.
