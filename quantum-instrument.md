# Quantum instrument

↑ **Parent:** [No information without disturbance](no-information-without-disturbance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_instrument)

A quantum instrument associates each classical outcome with a completely positive trace-nonincreasing map; summing the outcome maps gives a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md). It specifies both outcome probabilities and conditional output states.
