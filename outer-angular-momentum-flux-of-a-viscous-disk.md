# Outer angular-momentum flux of a viscous disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md)

Integrating the [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) diffusion equation gives $dJ/dt=6\pi\sqrt{GM_\star}[r^{3/2}\partial_r(\bar\nu\Sigma)]_{\rm inner}^{\rm outer}$ when the angular-momentum integral and boundary limits are legitimate. An inner [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) alone does not eliminate the outer [angular-momentum flux](angular-momentum-flux.md). For divergent total $J$, use finite-radius cutoffs before making a conservation claim.
