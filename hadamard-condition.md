# Hadamard condition

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory in curved spacetime](quantum-field-theory-in-curved-spacetime.md)

The [Hadamard condition](hadamard-condition.md) requires the two-point function of a physical free-field state to have the universal local short-distance singularity prescribed by the spacetime geometry and field equation. It permits state-dependent smooth contributions and enables local renormalization of the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md). It does not select a unique [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md).
