# All-time boundedness of a wave equation with a reaction term

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For $u_{tt}=\Delta u+\alpha u$ with a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), let $\lambda_1>0$ be the first [Dirichlet Laplacian eigenvalue](dirichlet-laplacian-eigenvalue.md). On a bounded one-dimensional interval, finite-energy displacement remains bounded for all time and all initial data exactly when $\alpha<\lambda_1$. The conserved energy $\tfrac12(\|u_t\|_2^2+\|\nabla u\|_2^2-\alpha\|u\|_2^2)$ controls the gradient and, by the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) and one-dimensional integration, the maximum [norm](norm.md). At equality a mode can grow linearly; above it a mode can grow exponentially. Bounded oscillations need not have a long-time limit.
