# Positive introspection

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)

Positive introspection says that a boxed assertion can itself be boxed. For an [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md) it is the third of the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md): provability of $A$ implies provability of the assertion that $A$ is provable. It follows from formalized [Sigma-1 completeness](sigma-1-completeness.md) when provability assertions are Sigma-1. It is not the reflection principle $\Box A\to A$.
