# Bipartite stability from few odd cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Edit-distance stability for clique-free graphs](edit-distance-stability-for-clique-free-graphs.md)

Fix an odd cycle length. If the [graph](graph-split.md) has [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) density at least $1/2-\epsilon$ and sufficiently few copies of that cycle, [odd-cycle copies from positive triangle density](odd-cycle-copies-from-positive-triangle-density.md) forces few [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). The [triangle removal lemma](triangle-removal-lemma.md) deletes at most $\epsilon\binom n2$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), after which the clique-free edit bound applies with two parts. Absorbing its linear rounding error into the density margin gives the displayed bound for large order.
