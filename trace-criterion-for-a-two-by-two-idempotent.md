# Trace criterion for a two-by-two idempotent

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

For $P=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$, the off-diagonal equations in $P^2=P$ are $b(a+d-1)=c(a+d-1)=0$. Thus either $b=c=0$ or the [trace](matrix-trace.md) is one. The condition alone is not sufficient: $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) one but is not an [idempotent matrix](idempotent-matrix.md). For a two-by-two [matrix](matrix.md) of [trace](matrix-trace.md) one, idempotence is equivalent to zero [determinant](determinant.md), by the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem-split.md).
