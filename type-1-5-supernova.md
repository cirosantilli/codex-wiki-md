<h1 id="type-1-5-supernova">Type 1.5 supernova</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supernova](supernova.md)

A hypothetical explosion in which a degenerate [carbon-oxygen core](carbon-oxygen-core.md) within a single evolved star grows toward the [Chandrasekhar mass](chandrasekhar-limit.md) and ignites explosive [carbon burning](carbon-burning.md) while retaining an envelope. This differs from accretion onto an already exposed [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) in standard [Type Ia supernova](type-ia-supernova.md) scenarios. [Evolutionary calculations by Doherty et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5431) explain the strong dependence of this proposed channel on envelope [mass loss](mass-loss-astrophysics.md); their adopted winds remove the carbon-oxygen envelope before this outcome. It is a proposed possibility under different assumptions, rather than an established endpoint for normal $5M_\odot$ stars.
