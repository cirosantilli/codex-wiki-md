# Marine air-gun array

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic source](seismic-source.md)

An array of compressed-air sources fired in water to generate repeatable broadband pressure pulses for a [seismic reflection survey](seismic-reflection-survey.md). Source tuning reduces coherent bubble oscillations but does not eliminate [seismic source ghosts](seismic-source-ghost.md). Its energy and low-frequency content can provide greater penetration than small high-frequency [boomer seismic sources](boomer-seismic-source.md) or [sparker seismic sources](sparker-seismic-source.md).
