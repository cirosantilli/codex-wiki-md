# Monge optimal transport problem

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

Given a cost $c$ and [probability measures](probability-measure.md) $\mu,\nu$, the Monge problem minimizes $\int c(x,T(x))\,d\mu(x)$ over measurable [transport maps](transport-map.md) with [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) $T_\#\mu=\nu$. The feasible set may be empty because a map cannot split an [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md).
