# Stefan-Boltzmann constant

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md)

The [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](stefan-boltzmann-constant.md) converts the fourth power of absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) into [blackbody](blackbody.md) [radiant exitance](radiant-exitance.md):

$$
\sigma=\frac{2\pi^5k_B^4}{15h^3c^2}
\approx5.670374419\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}.
$$

Here $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md), $h$ the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and $c$ the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). In the current [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md), these three constants have exact defining values, so $\sigma$ is exact as an expression in them and $\pi$. The displayed finite decimal is an approximation, not an exact decimal or a measured uncertainty. The 2022 recommended-constants table lists an ellipsis and an exact uncertainty for $\sigma$.[2022 table, Stefan-Boltzmann constant row; Boltzmann constant, Planck constant and speed of light in vacuum rows](fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)

A dimensional check uses $[k_B]=\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, $[h]=\mathrm{J\,s}$ and $[c]=\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Consequently $[k_B^4/(h^3c^2)]=\mathrm{J\,s^{-1}\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}=\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}$, exactly the units needed in the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md).

For an equilibrium [photon gas](photon-gas.md), the [energy density](energy-density.md) is $u=a_{\rm rad}T^4$ with [radiation constant](radiation-constant.md) $a_{\rm rad}=4\sigma/c$. The [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is $p_{\rm rad}=u/3=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$. These describe the radiation filling an equilibrium cavity; $u$ has units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$, whereas the outward [radiant exitance](radiant-exitance.md) through a small opening is $cu/4$. The factor $1/4$ comes from averaging propagation directions and their [projected area](projected-area.md) over a radiation field with [isotropy](isotropy.md), not from reducing the opening's [emissivity](emissivity.md).
