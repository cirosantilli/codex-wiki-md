# Stefan-Boltzmann constant

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md)

The [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](stefan-boltzmann-constant.md) $\sigma$ is the proportionality constant in the blackbody flux $F=\sigma T^4$. The blackbody [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is $p_{\rm rad}=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$.
