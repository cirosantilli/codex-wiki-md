# Gravitational collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_collapse)

[Gravitational collapse](gravitational-collapse.md) is contraction driven by a system's own gravitational attraction or an enclosing potential. Pressure, angular momentum and other stresses can oppose it. A free-fall timescale measures the dynamical contraction rate when pressure support is removed; gas cooling determines whether thermal support can be removed fast enough.
