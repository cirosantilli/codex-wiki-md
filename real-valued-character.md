# Real-valued character

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

A [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is real-valued when all its values are [real numbers](real-number.md), equivalently $\chi=\overline\chi$. By the [character inversion identity](character-inversion-identity.md), this is equivalent to $\chi(g)=\chi(g^{-1})$ for every $g$. This condition alone does not assert that the underlying [complex representation](complex-representation.md) can be realized by real [matrices](matrix.md).
