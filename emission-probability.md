# Emission probability

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden Markov model](hidden-markov-model.md)

An [emission probability](emission-probability.md) in a [Hidden Markov model](hidden-markov-model.md) is the probability of an observation conditional on its hidden state. For a finite observation alphabet, each state's emission probabilities sum to one. A [match state](match-state.md) can therefore emit several different characters, each with its own probability.
