# Closed orbits with an inverse-cube perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit equation for combined inverse-square and inverse-cube attraction](orbit-equation-for-combined-inverse-square-and-inverse-cube-attraction.md)

In the [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $-\mu(1/r+a/r^2)$, a regular noncircular bound [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) requires $L^2>2\mu a$ and has $u=\mu(L^2-2\mu a)^{-1}[1+e\cos\nu(\psi-\psi_0)]$ with $0<e<1$. Each radial period advances the angle by $2\pi/\nu$. The full position and [velocity](velocity.md) return after finitely many radial periods precisely when $\nu$ is rational. Irrational $\nu$ gives a dense rosette on the two-frequency [invariant torus](invariant-torus.md). Rationality permits a fifth [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) label fixing the apsidal phase on that resonant [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) family, but does not give global superintegrability for all nearby angular momenta. When $a=0$, every regular bound [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) is closed and the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) supplies the globally defined additional integral.
