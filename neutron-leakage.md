# Neutron leakage

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

Escape of [neutrons](neutron-split.md) from a specified region before they participate in further reactions there. A leakage fraction depends on the region's geometry, surrounding materials and energy-dependent transport and absorption, rather than its dimensions alone.
