# Propeller effect

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically channelled accretion](magnetically-channelled-accretion.md)

A rapidly spinning magnetosphere adds [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) to inflowing matter outside the [corotation radius](corotation-radius.md), inhibiting accretion and potentially ejecting gas. The centrifugal onset condition does not by itself ensure that every parcel escapes to infinity.
