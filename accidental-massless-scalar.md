# Accidental massless scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar mass matrix](scalar-mass-matrix.md)

An [accidental massless scalar](accidental-massless-scalar.md) has zero quadratic mass without being required by the stated broken continuous symmetry. A singlet with potential $\kappa z^4$ around $z=0$ is an example: its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) vanishes although no continuous symmetry of that single real field protects a mass term. Such a zero mass can require tuning once quantum corrections are included.
