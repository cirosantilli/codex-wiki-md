# Color discrimination

↑ **Parent:** [Color vision](color-vision.md)

The ability to distinguish stimuli using their chromatic differences under stated conditions. Controlled tests suppress [luminance](luminance.md) and other unintended cues and estimate a [psychometric function](psychometric-function.md). [Color matching](color-matching.md), [color](color.md) naming and discrimination are different observations.
