# Fair division

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

Allocation of resources among agents according to a specified fairness requirement. For indivisible items, each bundle is a [set](set-split.md) of whole items, and a complete allocation assigns every item exactly once. An [envy-free allocation](envy-free-allocation.md) is one fairness criterion. Existence depends on the permitted allocations: indivisibility can prevent fairness that would be possible after splitting resources or permitting unallocated items.
