# Fan in toric geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Toric geometry](toric-geometry-split.md)

A [fan in toric geometry](fan-in-toric-geometry.md) is a finite collection of rational strongly convex [cones in toric geometry](cone-in-toric-geometry.md) in a real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with a [lattice](lattice.md), closed under taking [faces of a polyhedral cone](face-of-a-polyhedral-cone.md), such that the intersection of two cones is a face of each. A fan determines a [toric variety](toric-variety.md).

A fan $\Sigma$ in $N_{\mathbb R}$ is a finite collection of strictly convex rational polyhedral cones closed under taking faces, such that the intersection of two cones is a face of each.
