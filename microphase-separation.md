# Microphase separation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase separation](phase-separation.md)

Formation of finite-scale composition domains when complete macroscopic separation is frustrated, for example by the covalent connection of incompatible blocks in a [diblock copolymer](diblock-copolymer.md). Interfacial cost and chain entropy jointly select the domain scale.
