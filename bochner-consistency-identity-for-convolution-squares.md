# Bochner consistency identity for convolution squares

↑ **Parent:** [Bochner's theorem](bochner-s-theorem.md)

For finite sums of compactly supported convolution squares, $\widehat p,\widehat q\geq0$. Insert the [Bochner theorem](bochner-s-theorem.md) representation of $q$ into $p*q$ and apply the [Fubini theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md): its representing measure is $\widehat p\,\mu_q$. Reversing $p,q$ and using uniqueness gives the displayed identity. It makes the local quotients $\mu_p/\widehat p$ consistent wherever $\widehat p>0$.
