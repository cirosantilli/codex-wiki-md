# Causal structure

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Causal_structure)

The [causal structure](causal-structure.md) of a spacetime classifies separations and tangent directions as timelike, null or spacelike through its light cones. It determines which events can communicate by causal curves. A [null curve](null-curve.md) is one member of this broader geometric structure.
