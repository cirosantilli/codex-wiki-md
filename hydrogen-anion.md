# Hydrogen anion

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen](hydrogen.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_anion)

A negative hydrogen ion is a [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) nucleus with two bound electrons. Its weakly bound extra electron permits absorption at visible and infrared wavelengths in cool stellar atmospheres.
