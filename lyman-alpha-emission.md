# Lyman-alpha emission

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha line](lyman-alpha-line.md)

Radiative decay from the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) $2p$ level to $1s$ produces a Lyman-alpha photon. In an optically thin collisionally excited gas, escape of this photon removes the excitation energy deposited by an [Electron](electron.md) collision. The excitation threshold is responsible for the rapid temperature sensitivity of this cooling channel.
