# Determinant of helical resistance in resistive-force theory

↑ **Parent:** [Axial resistance matrix of a slender helix](axial-resistance-matrix-of-a-slender-helix.md)

Local [resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md) for a [helix](helix.md) gives $AD-B^2=\ell^2a^2\xi_\parallel\xi_\perp$. Positive local drag therefore guarantees a positive-definite axial resistance matrix and positive viscous power loss. The identity also keeps the denominator in counterrotating two-helix balances positive.
