# Common tendinous ring

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit (anatomy)](orbit-anatomy.md)

A fibrous attachment at the apex of the [orbit (anatomy)](orbit-anatomy.md) from which the four rectus [extraocular muscles](extraocular-muscle.md) arise. Its relations distinguish structures passing through the [optic canal](optic-canal.md) and different parts of the [superior orbital fissure](superior-orbital-fissure.md).
