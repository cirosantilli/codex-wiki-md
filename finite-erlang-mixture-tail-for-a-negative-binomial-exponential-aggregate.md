# Finite Erlang-mixture tail for a negative-binomial exponential aggregate

↑ **Parent:** [Negative-binomial sum of exponential claims](negative-binomial-sum-of-exponential-claims.md)

For independent rate-$\lambda$ [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) claims and a zero-based [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md) count of shape $k$ and parameter $p$, the [random-sum transform identity](random-sum-transform-identity.md) gives $M_S(t)=[p(\lambda-t)/(p\lambda-t)]^k$. This equals the transform of a [compound binomial distribution](compound-binomial-distribution.md) with count $\operatorname{Bin}(k,1-p)$ and rate-$p\lambda$ [claim sizes](claim-size.md). Conditional on its positive count $n$, the total has [Erlang distribution](erlang-distribution.md), whose [survival function](survival-function.md) is the inner exponential-polynomial sum. Conditioning yields the displayed finite [mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md) formula for $x>0$. It replaces an infinite mixture and includes the zero-total atom $p^k$ through the omitted $n=0$ term.
