# Thomas precession

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_precession)

[Thomas precession](thomas-precession.md) is the kinematic rotation arising when the successive instantaneous rest frames of an accelerated particle are connected by noncollinear [Lorentz boosts](lorentz-boost.md). Its contribution is needed in deriving the leading atomic [spin-orbit coupling](spin-orbit-interaction.md): for an electron in central potential energy $U(r)$, the corrected term is $H_{\rm so}=[2m_e^2c^2r]^{-1}(dU/dr)\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$. A rest-field argument alone gives twice this coefficient.[§3.1.3, printed pp. 71–75, especially the correction from the accelerated electron frame](topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
