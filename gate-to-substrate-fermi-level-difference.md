# Gate-to-substrate Fermi-level difference

↑ **Parent:** [MOSFET](mosfet.md)

An ideal insulating gate blocks particle exchange between the gate and substrate of a [MOSFET](mosfet.md). Their [electrochemical potentials](electrochemical-potential.md), represented by their band-diagram [Fermi levels](fermi-level.md), therefore need not coincide. An applied gate voltage fixes their difference; with $e>0$ and voltage defined by an electron reservoir, $\widetilde\mu_g-\widetilde\mu_s=-eV_g$. Contact work-function differences also affect [band bending](band-bending.md), so equal Fermi levels do not imply flat bands. Each conducting region can separately have a spatially constant equilibrium electrochemical potential.
