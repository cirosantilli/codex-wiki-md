# Open disk

↑ **Parent:** [Disk (mathematics)](disk-mathematics.md)

The [open disk](open-disk.md) is the interior of a planar [disk](disk-mathematics.md). Any such [open disk](open-disk.md) is [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to $\mathbb R^2$, and in particular is [contractible](contractible-space.md) and [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md).
