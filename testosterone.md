# Testosterone

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Testosterone](testosterone.md) is a steroid with a conjugated C3-ketone/C4=C5 fragment. [5α-reductase](5-alpha-reductase.md) adds hydride at C5 and a [proton](proton.md) at C4 to make [dihydrotestosterone](dihydrotestosterone.md).
