# Absolute homogeneity of a norm

↑ **Parent:** [Norm](norm.md)

Absolute homogeneity is the [norm](norm.md) axiom $\|av\|=|a|\|v\|$ for every [scalar](scalar.md) $a$ and [vector](vector.md) $v$. It distinguishes a [norm](norm.md) from a merely homogeneous expression of a different degree; for example the fourth root in the [box norm](box-norm.md) converts a degree-four expression into a degree-one [norm](norm.md).
