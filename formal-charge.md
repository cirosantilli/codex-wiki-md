# Formal charge

↑ **Parent:** [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_charge)

The [formal charge](formal-charge.md) of a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) in a [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) assigns it all of its nonbonding [electrons](electron.md) and half of the [electrons](electron.md) in its connections:

$$
\mathrm{FC}=v-n-\frac b2.
$$

Here $v$ is the neutral [chemical atom](atom-physics.md)'s [valence electron](valence-electron.md) count, $n$ is the number of nonbonding [electrons](electron.md) assigned to it, and $b$ is the number of bonding [electrons](electron.md) incident on it. Count [electrons](electron.md), not [electron pairs](electron-pair.md): two [lone pairs](lone-pair.md) mean $n=4$, and two double connections mean $b=8$. The number $\mathrm{FC}$ gives [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in units of the positive [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). Equal splitting is a bookkeeping convention, regardless of the actual [electron density](electron-density.md). See the [IUPAC Gold Book, formal charge](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/08169).

![](lewis-carbon-dioxide-formal-charge.png)

**[Figure 8](#image-sixteen-valence-electrons-in-two-carbon-dioxide-lewis-structures-mathrm-o-c-o-has-zero-formal-charges-while-mathrm-o-c-equiv-o-plus-has-separated-formal-charges). Sixteen valence electrons in two carbon dioxide Lewis structures: $\mathrm{O=C=O}$ has zero formal charges, while $\mathrm{O^-{-}C\equiv O^+}$ has separated formal charges**. The first [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) has two [lone pairs](lone-pair.md) on each [oxygen](oxygen.md). The second has three [lone pairs](lone-pair.md) on the singly connected [oxygen](oxygen.md), one on the triply connected [oxygen](oxygen.md), and none on [carbon](carbon.md). Both use four [bond pairs](bond-pair.md) and four [lone pairs](lone-pair.md); both satisfy the [octet rule](octet-rule.md). Their contributions to [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md) are unequal, and the drawn [formal charges](formal-charge.md) are not [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md).

For $\mathrm{O=C=O}$, each [oxygen](oxygen.md) has $v=6$, $n=4$, $b=4$, giving [formal charge](formal-charge.md) $6-4-4/2=0$. [Carbon](carbon.md) has $v=4$, $n=0$, $b=8$, giving [formal charge](formal-charge.md) $4-0-8/2=0$. For $\mathrm{O^-{-}C\equiv O^+}$, the left [oxygen](oxygen.md) has [formal charge](formal-charge.md) $6-6-2/2=-1$, [carbon](carbon.md) still has [formal charge](formal-charge.md) zero, and the right [oxygen](oxygen.md) has [formal charge](formal-charge.md) $6-2-6/2=+1$. The net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is zero in either drawing.

The [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) with no separated [formal charges](formal-charge.md) is the dominant contributor in this elementary comparison; the charge-separated drawing is a less important contributor, rather than an equally dominant alternative. This is a qualitative selection rule, not a calculation of [energy](energy-split.md) or numerical [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md) weights. See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, 7.4: comparing carbon dioxide structures](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-4-formal-charges-and-resonance). A zero [formal charge](formal-charge.md) does not imply zero [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md): the latter depends on partitioning the actual [electron density](electron-density.md), which a [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) does not supply.

As a short consistency derivation, summing $n+b/2$ over all [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) counts every drawn [electron](electron.md) once: a nonbonding [electron](electron.md) is assigned once, and a bonding [electron](electron.md) contributes half at each of its two endpoints. Thus

$$
\sum_i\mathrm{FC}_i=\sum_i v_i-N_{\mathrm{valence}},
$$

where $N_{\mathrm{valence}}$ is the total drawn [valence electron](valence-electron.md) count. The right side is the net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in units of [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). This catches a wrong budget even when individual [octet rule](octet-rule.md) checks look plausible.
