# Gaussian function

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential function](exponential-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_function)

A Gaussian function has the form $A\exp[-a(x-b)^2]$ with $a>0$. Its integral and moments reduce by translation and scaling to the [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md).
