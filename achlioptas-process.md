# Achlioptas process

↑ **Parent:** [Random graph](random-graph.md)

At each step offer a fixed number of independent uniform candidate [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) on a fixed vertex set, and let a rule select one. The standard two-choice model offers two candidates. The rule may depend on the entire previously exposed history. Repeated already present [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) make no change in the independent-candidate version; another usual version offers only absent [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). The [forced merging of large components](forced-merging-of-large-components.md) and [persistence of unsampled graph components](persistence-of-unsampled-graph-components.md) apply to both over a linear number of steps. Fixed choice is essential in the [continuity of fixed-choice percolation](continuity-of-fixed-choice-percolation.md) argument.
