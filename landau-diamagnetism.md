# Landau diamagnetism

↑ **Parent:** [Diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_diamagnetism)

[Landau diamagnetism](landau-diamagnetism.md) is the orbital response of mobile [electrons](electron.md) to an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), arising from quantization of their transverse motion. For a noninteracting three-dimensional isotropic [electron](electron.md) [gas](gas.md) with quadratic dispersion, [electron spin](electron-spin.md) with $g=2$ and [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) weak enough for the smooth-response limit, its susceptibility is $\chi_L=-\chi_P/3$, where $\chi_P$ is the [Pauli paramagnetism](pauli-paramagnetism.md) susceptibility. Band effective masses, interactions, dimensionality and resolved magnetic quantum oscillations can change this simple relation.
