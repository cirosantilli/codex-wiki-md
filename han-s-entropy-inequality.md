<h1 id="han-s-entropy-inequality">Han's entropy inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Han's inequality for relative entropy](han-s-inequality-for-relative-entropy.md)

For a random vector $X_{1:n}$,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^nH(X^{(i)})\geq(n-1)H(X_{1:n}).
$$

This is equivalent to [Han's inequality for relative entropy](han-s-inequality-for-relative-entropy.md) after expansion against a product reference measure.
