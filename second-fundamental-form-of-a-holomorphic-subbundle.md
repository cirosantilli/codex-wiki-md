# Second fundamental form of a holomorphic subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Projected Chern connection](projected-chern-connection.md)

For a holomorphic subbundle $S\subset E$ and quotient $Q=E/S$, the second fundamental form is $A(s)=(I-\pi_S)D_Es$. The [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) gives $A(fs)=fA(s)$, and holomorphicity of the subbundle makes its $(0,1)$ part vanish. Thus $A\in\mathcal A^{1,0}(\operatorname{Hom}(S,Q))$: the form factor is a [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md), not a tangent bundle.
