# Upper-half-plane indentation rule

↑ **Parent:** [Residue theorem](residue-theorem.md)

Suppose a contour follows the [real axis](real-line.md), closes in the upper half-plane, and avoids each simple real pole $a_j$ by a small semicircle above it. Each indentation is clockwise and tends to

$$
-i\pi\operatorname{Res}(f,a_j).
$$

If the large arc vanishes and there are no enclosed poles away from the real axis, the [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) is therefore

$$
\operatorname{PV}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx
=i\pi\sum_j\operatorname{Res}(f,a_j).
$$
