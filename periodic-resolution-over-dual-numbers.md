# Periodic resolution over dual numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Dual number](dual-number.md)

For $A=k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$ and $k=A/(\varepsilon)$, the kernel and image of multiplication by $\varepsilon$ both equal $(\varepsilon)$. Thus the displayed sequence is a [free resolution](free-resolution.md). Applying $\operatorname{Hom}_A(-,k)$ makes every differential zero, so $\operatorname{Ext}_A^i(k,k)\cong k$ for every $i\ge0$. The module $k$ therefore has infinite [projective dimension](projective-dimension.md).
