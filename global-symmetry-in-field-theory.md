# Global symmetry in field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory-split.md)

A global symmetry has transformation parameters constant across spacetime and preserves the action. It can relate physically distinct states. Continuous global symmetries give [Noether currents](noether-current.md) and conserved charges when boundary fluxes vanish; discrete symmetries do not receive currents from the same infinitesimal theorem. A [gauge redundancy](gauge-redundancy.md) instead uses local parameters to relate descriptions of the same physical state, except that nontrivial boundary transformations may carry physical charges. The [global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field](global-phase-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md) is an important internal example.
