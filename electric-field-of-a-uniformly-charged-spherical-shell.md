# Electric field of a uniformly charged spherical shell

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

Place the same positive [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $Q$ uniformly on the [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$, with no external field. Every concentric interior [Gaussian surface](gaussian-surface.md) encloses zero charge; [spherical symmetry](spherical-symmetry.md) then makes the interior [electric field](electric-field.md) zero. Outside, it is again $Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)$. At $R$, the inner and outer limits differ by $Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0R^2)=\sigma/\varepsilon_0$, with [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma=Q/(4\pi R^2)$. The ideal surface charge makes the field discontinuous, so the plot displays one-sided limits rather than assigning a single field value on the charged sheet. An isolated charged conducting sphere in [electrostatic equilibrium](electrostatic-equilibrium.md) realizes this exterior/interior pattern.
