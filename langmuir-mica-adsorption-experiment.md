# Langmuir mica adsorption experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)

In 1918, [Irving Langmuir](irving-langmuir.md) measured [adsorption](adsorption.md) using freshly split [mica](mica.md) strips, handled at their edges, separated by [iron](iron.md) spacers and held with [brass](brass.md) fasteners. A [glass](glass.md) bulb contained the bundle; a similar blank contained matching [iron](iron.md) and [brass](brass.md) but no [mica](mica.md). Evacuated [glass](glass.md) connections, [Mercury](mercury-planet.md) seals, [liquid air](liquid-air.md) traps, and [McLeod gauges](mcleod-gauge.md) provided controlled [gas](gas.md) handling. Calibrated compartment [volumes](volume.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) readings before and after expansion gave free-gas amounts; their deficit indicated uptake. Lowering [pressure](pressure-split.md) and measuring released [gas](gas.md) tested [desorption](desorption.md).[Langmuir 1918, printed pp. 1376–1377: Experiments with Mica, preparation, blank, apparatus and Methods I–II](the-adsorption-of-gases-on-plane-surfaces-of-glass-mica-and-platinum-by-irving-langmuir.md)

[Hydrogen](hydrogen.md) blank runs calibrated cooling-related [pressure](pressure-split.md) differences. [Irving Langmuir](irving-langmuir.md) pre-cooled the bulbs to reduce thermal delay; he suggested slow [diffusion](diffusion.md) between strips could control the remaining approach to equilibrium. Critically, blank uptake was not subtracted: he estimated the [mica](mica.md)-bulb results were 10–30% too high. [Liquid air](liquid-air.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) drift was unrecorded. These limits constrain inference of intrinsic binding from the uptake curve. The paper's [pressure](pressure-split.md) unit called “bar” was $1\,\mathrm{dyn\,cm^{-2}}=0.1\,\mathrm{Pa}$, unlike the modern bar used above.[Langmuir 1918, printed p. 1365: pressure-unit definition; pp. 1377–1378: cooling calibration; p. 1381: equilibration; p. 1385: blank bias and bath drift](the-adsorption-of-gases-on-plane-surfaces-of-glass-mica-and-platinum-by-irving-langmuir.md)
