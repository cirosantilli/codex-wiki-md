# One-sided fluid-loaded membrane radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Point-force radiation from a fluid-loaded sheet](point-force-radiation-from-a-fluid-loaded-sheet.md)

For one acoustic half-space and vacuum on the other side, a harmonic point [force](force.md) gives displacement amplitude $F\gamma/D$ and fluid [acoustic velocity potential](acoustic-velocity-potential.md) amplitude $i\omega F e^{-\gamma y}/D$. For time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, the [outgoing acoustic square-root branch](outgoing-acoustic-square-root-branch.md) has $\gamma>0$ for $|k|>k_0$ and $\gamma=-i\sqrt{k_0^2-k^2}$ for $|k|<k_0$. The causal limit $\omega\mapsto\omega+i0$ places the positive guided-mode pole above the inversion contour and the negative pole below. The [saddle point](saddle-point.md) generates cylindrical radiation, whereas guided residues decay normally to the membrane.
