# Matrix-unit basis of the unitary Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary Lie algebra](unitary-lie-algebra.md)

A real basis of the [unitary Lie algebra](unitary-lie-algebra.md) is $iE_{ii}$, $E_{ij}-E_{ji}$ and $i(E_{ij}+E_{ji})$ for $i<j$, where $E_{ij}$ are [matrix units](matrix-unit.md). These separately span the imaginary diagonal and real and imaginary upper off-diagonal entries.
