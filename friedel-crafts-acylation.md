# Friedel-Crafts acylation

↑ **Parent:** [Friedel-Crafts reaction](friedel-crafts-reaction.md)

[Friedel-Crafts acylation](friedel-crafts-acylation.md) attaches an acyl group to an aromatic carbon through electrophilic substitution. Lewis-acid activation of an acyl chloride is often represented by an acylium ion or strongly polarized contact pair.
