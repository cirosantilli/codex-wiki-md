<h1 id="kepler-s-first-law">Kepler's first law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler's laws of planetary motion](kepler-s-laws-of-planetary-motion.md)

The bound relative [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) is an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with the attracting centre at one [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md). In [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md), its separation is the displayed function of [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md) $f$, the angle measured from [periapsis](periapsis.md). For $a=1$ and $e=0.6$, it becomes $r=0.64/(1+0.6\cos f)$, giving $0.4$ at $f=0$ and $1.6$ at $f=\pi$. Setting [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) to zero gives a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md). This specifies the path; [Kepler's second law](kepler-s-second-law.md) specifies how it is traversed.
