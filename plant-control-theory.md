# Plant (control theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Control theory](control-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_(control_theory))

The [controlled plant](plant-control-theory.md) is the part of a [control theory](control-theory-split.md) model that receives the [control input](control-input.md) and produces the [plant output](output-control-theory.md), including the mechanism that applies the command. The [controller](controller-control-theory.md) is specified separately. In the illustrative [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) $\dot x=u+w$, the [controlled plant](plant-control-theory.md) accumulates the [control input](control-input.md) and [control disturbance](control-disturbance.md); it does not itself choose $u$.
