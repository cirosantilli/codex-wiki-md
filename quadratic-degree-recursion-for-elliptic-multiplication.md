# Quadratic degree recursion for elliptic multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of an isogeny](degree-of-an-isogeny.md)

The degree parallelogram identity for endomorphisms of an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) gives $d_{n+1}+d_{n-1}=2d_n+2$ for $d_n=\deg[n]$, with $d_0=0$ and $d_1=1$. Induction gives $d_n=n^2$ in every characteristic. A zero pullback on differentials therefore does not mean that multiplication is the zero morphism.
