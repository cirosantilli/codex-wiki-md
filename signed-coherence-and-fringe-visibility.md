# Signed coherence and fringe visibility

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md)

Combining equal-intensity beams with complex degree of first-order coherence $g$ gives a fringe modulation proportional to $\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\varphi}g)$. Maximizing and minimizing over observation [phase](phase-waves.md) gives visibility $V=|g|$. A symmetric source can make $g$ real, but it may still be negative. Its sign changes fringe [phase](phase-waves.md) by $\pi$; it does not represent negative physical contrast.
