# Hoeffding lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Hoeffding inequality](hoeffding-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoeffding_lemma)

If $a\leq X\leq b$ almost surely, then

$$
\log\mathbb E e^{\lambda(X-\mathbb EX)}
\leq\frac{\lambda^2(b-a)^2}{8}.
$$
