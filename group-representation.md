# Group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_representation)

A group representation is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from a [group](group-split.md) to the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). It realizes abstract group elements as invertible linear transformations.
