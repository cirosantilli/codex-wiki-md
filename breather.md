# Breather

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)

A [breather](breather.md) is a spatially localized nonlinear-wave solution periodic in time. It does not separate into independently traveling lumps while it breathes. A [Sine-Gordon breather](sine-gordon-breather.md) arises from a complex-conjugate soliton pair. In the quantum sine-Gordon model, the associated neutral kink-antikink bound particles are also called [breathers](breather.md).
