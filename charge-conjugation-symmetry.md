# Charge conjugation symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

[Charge conjugation symmetry](charge-conjugation-symmetry.md) is invariance of the dynamics under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md). It pairs a process with its particle-antiparticle conjugate at equal rates. [C violation](violation-of-charge-conjugation.md) is the failure of this invariance.
