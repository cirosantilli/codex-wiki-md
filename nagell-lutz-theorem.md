<h1 id="nagell-lutz-theorem">Nagell–Lutz theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagell–Lutz_theorem)

The Nagell–Lutz theorem states that a nonzero rational torsion point on an integral short Weierstrass curve has integral coordinates; if its $y$-coordinate is nonzero, then its square divides the discriminant.
