# Intrinsic galaxy alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md)

A correlation of galaxy shapes caused by their formation environment or tidal fields rather than by the gravitational lensing of their light. It can contaminate a weak-shear correlation used to infer structure growth and [dark energy](dark-energy.md). Redshift separation, population dependence and physically motivated modelling help distinguish it from a lensing contribution.
