# Detector conversion gain

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

[Detector conversion gain](detector-conversion-gain.md) is the number of collected [Electrons](electron.md) per [analogue-to-digital unit](analogue-to-digital-unit.md). With independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) photoelectron counts, the shot-noise [variance](variance-split.md) in count units is the mean count divided by the gain. Detected-photon and [Electron](electron.md) conventions agree only when each detected [photon](photon.md) contributes one [Electron](electron.md).
