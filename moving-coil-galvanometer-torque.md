# Moving-coil galvanometer torque

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md)

For a rectangular winding with $N$ turns, active side [length](length.md) $\ell$, side separation $w$, and per-turn [area](surface-area.md) $A=w\ell$, the [magnetic force on a current-carrying wire](magnetic-force-on-a-current-carrying-wire.md) gives equal and opposite side [forces](force-split.md) of magnitude $N|I|\ell B$. Their [force couple](couple-mechanics.md) has [lever arm](lever-arm.md) $w$. Choosing the positive rotation to match positive [electric current](electric-current.md) in the illustration gives the signed [torque](torque.md) and spring balance

$$
\tau_{\mathrm m}=NIAB,\qquad \tau_{\mathrm s}=-k\theta,\qquad NIAB-k\theta=0,\qquad \theta=\frac{NAB}{k}I.
$$

Here $B$ is the working-gap [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md), $k>0$ is the [torsional spring constant](torsion-constant.md) in $\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$, and $\theta$ is the deflection [angle](angle.md) in [radians](radian.md) from the no-current zero. The balance holds after motion has settled, within the radial-field and [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) ranges. Do not identify $\theta$ with the [angle](angle.md) between a [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and a fixed uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md): that different geometry has [torque](torque.md) magnitude $NIAB|\sin\alpha|$. [§4.9.1, equations (4.20)–(4.24), and §4.10, equation (4.26): fixed-field versus radial-field torque](physics-part-i-class-xii/moving-charges-and-magnetism.md)

For an original illustrative design, choose $N=120$, $A=3.00\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $B=0.250\,\mathrm T$, and $k=3.00\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$. Then $NAB=9.00\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\,m/A}$. At $I=+40.0\,\mathrm{\mu A}$, the magnetic [torque](torque.md) is $+3.60\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and $\theta=+0.120\,\mathrm{rad}\simeq+6.88^\circ$. Reversing the [electric current](electric-current.md) reverses both the [torque](torque.md) and the deflection, giving $-6.88^\circ$ without changing the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
