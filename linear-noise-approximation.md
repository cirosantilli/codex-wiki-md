# Linear noise approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion approximation of a birth-death process](diffusion-approximation-of-a-birth-death-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_noise_approximation)

Near a stable deterministic equilibrium, replace the drift by its linearization and the diffusion coefficient by its equilibrium value. The resulting Ornstein-Uhlenbeck approximation has a stationary [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
