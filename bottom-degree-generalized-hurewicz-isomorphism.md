# Bottom-degree generalized Hurewicz isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Connective spectrum](connective-spectrum.md)

If $E$ is [connective spectrum](connective-spectrum.md) in the sense of a [connective spectrum](connective-spectrum.md), $\pi_0E\cong\mathbb Z$, and $X$ is $(n-1)$-connected with $n\ge2$, then $\widetilde E_n(X)\cong\pi_n(X)$. In the [homological Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence](homological-atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence.md), the only nonzero term in total degree $n$ is $\widetilde H_n(X;\pi_0E)$. Incoming differentials have negative coefficient degree, and outgoing differentials have spatial degree below $n$. The [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) identifies this term with $\pi_nX$.
