# Tetrahedral qubit POVM

↑ **Parent:** [Informationally complete POVM](informationally-complete-povm.md)

Choose the four unit [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) at the vertices of a [regular tetrahedron](regular-tetrahedron.md), so $\sum_i\mathbf n_i=0$ and $\sum_i\mathbf n_i\mathbf n_i^T=4I/3$. The displayed four positive rank-one effects sum to $I$. On input $(I+\mathbf r\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$, their probabilities are $p_i=(1+\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf n_i)/4$, giving the reconstruction $\mathbf r=3\sum_i p_i\mathbf n_i$. This realizes the smallest fixed qubit [informationally complete POVM](informationally-complete-povm.md).
