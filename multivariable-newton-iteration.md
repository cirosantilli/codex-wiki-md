# Multivariable Newton iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Newton iteration in a Banach space](newton-iteration-in-a-banach-space.md)

The finite-dimensional [Newton root-finding iteration](newton-root-finding-iteration.md) for a vector equation $G(x)=0$. At a point with invertible [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md), solve the displayed [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md). For $G=\nabla F$, its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md), giving [Newton's method in optimization](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md). The update solves the affine first-order approximation $G(x_k)+DG(x_k)s_k=0$; it can converge to any nondegenerate [critical point](critical-point.md), including a [saddle point](saddle-point.md).
