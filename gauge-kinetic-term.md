# Gauge kinetic term

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

The field-strength quadratic term supplies gauge-field dynamics. In the canonical Abelian normalization it is $-F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}/4$; a non-Abelian theory includes the invariant sum or trace over internal indices. A vector mass inferred from a scalar kinetic term is physical only after specifying this normalization.
