# Hexamethylphosphoramide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Hexamethylphosphoramide](hexamethylphosphoramide.md) has a strongly donating phosphoryl [oxygen](oxygen.md) that coordinates [lithium](lithium.md). It changes [ion pairing](ion-pair.md) and aggregate structures, often increasing the reactivity of anionic [oxygen](oxygen.md) donors. It does not universally produce free monomeric anions.
