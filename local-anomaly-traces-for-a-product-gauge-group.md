# Local anomaly traces for a product gauge group

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md)

For left-handed [Weyl fermions](weyl-spinor.md) in representations $(R_i,Q_i)$ of a simple semisimple factor times $U(1)$, the displayed [traces](matrix-trace.md) cancel the local cubic non-Abelian, mixed non-Abelian–Abelian, and cubic Abelian [gauge anomalies](gauge-anomaly.md). Include spectator multiplicities in every [trace](matrix-trace.md). A triangle with one non-Abelian insertion and two Abelian insertions vanishes by tracelessness. Coupling to gravity also requires $\sum_i\dim(R_i)Q_i=0$, and [global anomalies](global-anomaly.md) must be checked separately. For a [conjugate representation](conjugate-representation.md) the cubic coefficient changes sign but the [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md) $T(R)$ does not.
