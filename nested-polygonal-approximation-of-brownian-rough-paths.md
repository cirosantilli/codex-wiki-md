# Nested polygonal approximation of Brownian rough paths

↑ **Parent:** [Enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md)

For deterministic nested partitions with mesh tending to zero, lift the polygonal interpolation of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by its ordinary iterated integrals. These [path signatures](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md) converge almost surely to [enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md) in every Hölder rough-path metric of exponent below $1/2$, hence in $p$-variation for $p>2$. Conditioning on the partition values gives a nested [martingale](martingale-split.md) for the first level and the off-diagonal second levels. Moment bounds and interpolation upgrade pointwise convergence to convergence in the [rough path metric](rough-path-metric.md).
