# Word ladder

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

A [search problem](search-problem.md) whose states are words of fixed length, with a transition changing exactly one letter and requiring the resulting word to belong to a specified dictionary. Unit [path costs](path-cost.md) make a shortest [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) a minimum-mutation solution. [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md) to the target is a [consistent heuristic](consistent-heuristic.md): one transition changes this distance by at most one. Dictionary restrictions may force a longer solution or make the target unreachable.
