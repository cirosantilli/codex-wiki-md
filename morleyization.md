# Morleyization

↑ **Parent:** [Quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md)

[Morleyization](morleyization.md) adds a [predicate symbol](predicate-symbol.md) for each [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) and an axiom equating that [predicate symbol](predicate-symbol.md) with its defining [logical formula](logical-formula.md). Every original [first-order model](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) has a unique expansion. Translating an expanded [logical formula](logical-formula.md) back to the original [first-order language](first-order-language.md) and then using its named [predicate symbol](predicate-symbol.md) gives [quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md). Completeness of the original [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) implies completeness of the expanded [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md).
