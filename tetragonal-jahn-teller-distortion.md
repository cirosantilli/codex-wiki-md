# Tetragonal Jahn-Teller distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Jahn-Teller effect](jahn-teller-effect.md)

A symmetry-lowering change separating two axial [metal](metal.md)–ligand distances from four equatorial ones. Occupancy of the split orbitals determines the electronic energy gain, while elastic cost and pre-existing ligand asymmetry affect the observed structure.
