# Spin-one reversal under quadratic anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-one Cartesian basis](spin-one-cartesian-basis.md)

For a spin-one [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=AS_z^2+B(S_x^2-S_y^2)$, Cartesian basis energies are $\hbar^2(A-B)$, $\hbar^2(A+B)$ and zero along the $x,y,z$ basis labels. The $S_x=\hbar$ state combines the latter two basis vectors equally. Their relative phase produces the displayed spin-reversal probability. Complete reversal occurs at odd multiples of $\pi/(|A+B|\hbar)$ if $A+B\ne0$; when it vanishes those two components remain degenerate. Individual matrix signs depend on basis conventions, while the [commutators](commutator.md) and physical probability must remain consistent.
