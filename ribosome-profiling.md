# Ribosome profiling

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Sequencing of [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) fragments protected by [ribosomes](ribosome.md) to measure their positions on transcripts. Occupancy measures a combination of entry, passage and dwell time; more footprints can reflect slower elongation rather than more completed [protein](protein-split.md).
