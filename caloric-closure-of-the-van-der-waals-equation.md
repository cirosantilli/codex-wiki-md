# Caloric closure of the van der Waals equation

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

The thermodynamic identity $(\partial U/\partial V)_T=T(\partial P/\partial T)_V-P$ fixes the volume-dependent term $-aN^2/V$ but leaves an arbitrary function of [temperature](temperature-split.md). Classical monatomic mean-field modelling adds $3Nk_BT/2$, giving $U=3Nk_BT/2-aN^2/V+C$.
