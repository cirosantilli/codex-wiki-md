# Ore extension

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ore_extension)

An Ore extension uses a unital endomorphism $\sigma$ of $A$ and a $\sigma$-derivation $\delta$, satisfying $\delta(ab)=\sigma(a)\delta(b)+\delta(a)b$. Its elements have unique expressions $\sum_i a_it^i$ and multiplication is determined by the displayed relation. Applying the relation to $t(ab)$ shows why the endomorphism and [derivation](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) conditions are exactly the compatibility conditions for [associativity](associative-property.md). A differential Ore extension has $\sigma=\operatorname{id}$. Adjoining ordinary partial derivatives successively to a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) gives the ordered-monomial construction of a [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md).
