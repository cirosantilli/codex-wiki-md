# Augustin-Louis Cauchy by Hans Freudenthal

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Hans Freudenthal](work-by-hans-freudenthal.md)

By [Hans Freudenthal](hans-freudenthal.md). Scholarly biographical article, reproduced in the University of St Andrews archive. Cited for its mathematical-physics discussion and its reproduction of [Augustin-Louis Cauchy](augustin-louis-cauchy.md)'s 1822 explanation of internal pressures and tensions. The 2008 copyright on the digital compilation is not the date of that 1822 communication.

Online source: [https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Cauchy.pdf](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Cauchy.pdf)
