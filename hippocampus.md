# Hippocampus

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A brain structure involved in [episodic memory](episodic-memory.md) and spatial processing. In computational accounts, [dentate gyrus](dentate-gyrus.md) input transformation, recurrent [hippocampal CA3](hippocampal-ca3.md) processing and [hippocampal CA1](hippocampal-ca1.md) output processing supply complementary operations; these assignments are hypotheses constrained by recordings and interventions.
