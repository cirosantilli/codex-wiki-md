# Parallel transport around a spherical triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel transport](parallel-transport.md)

For the positively oriented boundary of a [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md) on the unit sphere, [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) is rotation by its angle excess modulo $2\pi$. The tangent is parallel along each geodesic side and turns by $\pi-\alpha$ at a corner of angle $\alpha$. The three corner turns sum to $3\pi-\alpha-\beta-\gamma$, and combining them with parallel transport returns the initial tangent. Hence the transport angle is the negative of that sum modulo $2\pi$, giving the formula. The [spherical excess formula](spherical-excess-formula.md) identifies the angle with enclosed area; reversing traversal changes its sign.
