<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-fourier-phase-space-action">Neveu–Schwarz Fourier phase-space action</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md)

The open NS action has integer bosonic modes and half-integer fermionic matter modes, together with even Virasoro and odd supercurrent multipliers. Opposite [fermion](fermion.md) signs at the two endpoints make the doubled [fermion](fermion.md) antiperiodic and remove a matter zero mode. The $b_r$ matter variables are distinct from the $bc$ reparameterization [FP ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md). Canonical quantization yields $[\alpha_k^m,\alpha_l^n]=k\eta^{mn}\delta_{k+l,0}$ and $\{b_r^m,b_s^n\}=\eta^{mn}\delta_{r+s,0}$.
