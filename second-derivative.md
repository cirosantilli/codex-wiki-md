# Second derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_derivative)

The [derivative](derivative.md) of the first [derivative](derivative.md). For a twice [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md), its sign governs local [convexity](convex-function.md) and its magnitude controls the second-order [Taylor remainder](taylor-remainder.md).
