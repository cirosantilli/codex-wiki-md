# Branch-cycle conjugacy class

↑ **Parent:** [Tame inertia quotient](tame-inertia-quotient.md)

For a [Finite Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of $K(T)$ with algebraically closed characteristic-zero constants, the local decomposition and inertia groups coincide and are cyclic. A fixed compatible choice of primitive [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) selects a generator through its [uniformizer character of inertia](uniformizer-character-of-inertia.md). Changing the prime above a base point conjugates this generator, and changing the [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) does not change its character. Without the roots-of-unity convention, only the inertia-subgroup [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is intrinsic. Over $\mathbb C$, positive local orientation gives the standard convention.
