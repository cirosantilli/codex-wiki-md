# Exact factorization of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)

Two [subgroups](subgroup.md) give an exact factorization of a [group](group-split.md) when every element has a unique expression $h_1h_2$ with $h_i\in H_i$. Existence and trivial intersection are equivalent to this uniqueness: if $h_1h_2=k_1k_2$, then $k_1^{-1}h_1=k_2h_2^{-1}\in H_1\cap H_2$, so both sides are the identity. Conversely, an element $a$ in the intersection gives the two factorizations $a\cdot e=e\cdot a$, forcing $a=e$. For finite [groups](group-split.md) the multiplication map $H_1\times H_2\to G$ is therefore a [bijection](bijection.md) and $|G|=|H_1||H_2|$. It need not be a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md): an exact factorization is not automatically a [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md). If $H_1$ is normal, it gives an internal [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md).
