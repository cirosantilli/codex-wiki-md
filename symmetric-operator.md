# Symmetric operator

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

A [densely defined operator](densely-defined-operator.md) $T$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is symmetric when

$$
\langle Tv,w\rangle=\langle v,Tw\rangle\qquad(v,w\in D(T)).
$$

Equivalently, $T$ agrees with its [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) on $D(T)$, while $D(T^*)$ may be larger. A [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) additionally requires equality of these [operator domains](operator-domain.md).
