# Relevant direction of a fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Relevant operator](relevant-operator.md)

A perturbation of a [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) is relevant if it grows as the coarse-graining length increases. For a continuous flow $d\delta g/d\ell=M\delta g$, an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the linearized [matrix](matrix.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\sigma$ grows as $e^{\sigma\ell}$; a positive real part is relevant, a negative real part is irrelevant and a zero real part requires nonlinear analysis. For a discrete transformation with length factor $b>1$, its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) multiplier $\lambda$ instead gives $\delta g_n=\lambda^n\delta g_0$: $|\lambda|>1$ is relevant, $|\lambda|<1$ is irrelevant and $|\lambda|=1$ requires nonlinear analysis. For a positive multiplier the scaling exponent is $y=\log\lambda/\log b$. Thus a positive discrete [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) smaller than one is an [irrelevant operator](irrelevant-operator.md) direction, while the sign criterion belongs to the continuous generator.
