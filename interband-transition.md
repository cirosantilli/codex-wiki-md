# Interband transition

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)

An [interband transition](interband-transition.md) moves an [electron](electron.md) between distinct [energy bands](energy-band.md). A direct optical transition approximately conserves [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) because optical [photon](photon.md) momentum is small compared with a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. A phonon-assisted transition can supply the momentum difference needed near an [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md). Transition energies alone do not fix their optical intensities, which also depend on [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) and occupations.
