# Overdamped trap relaxation after an inertial boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Initial-value solution of a damped harmonic trap](initial-value-solution-of-a-damped-harmonic-trap.md)

For $\epsilon=km/\gamma^2\ll1$, [momentum](momentum-split.md) relaxes in $\tau_m=m/\gamma$ while the trap relaxes in $\tau_k=\gamma/k$. After the fast transient, the slow amplitude is $x_0+\tau_m v_0+O(\epsilon)$. Neglecting the initial-[velocity](velocity.md) contribution additionally requires $|\tau_m v_0|\ll|x_0|$; small [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) alone does not prove this condition.
