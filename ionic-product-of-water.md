# Ionic product of water

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

The product of hydrogen-ion and hydroxide-ion activities at a specified temperature. In dilute aqueous solution at 25 degrees Celsius, its usual [concentration](concentration.md) approximation is ten to the minus fourteen.
