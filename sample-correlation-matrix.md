# Sample correlation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Sample correlation coefficient](sample-correlation-coefficient.md)

A [sample correlation matrix](sample-correlation-matrix.md) rescales a [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md) $S$ to unit diagonal, for variables with positive sample variance. Its off-diagonal entries are [sample correlation coefficients](sample-correlation-coefficient.md). Strong correlations between predictors can indicate [multicollinearity](multicollinearity.md), but pairwise correlations alone do not exclude higher-order linear dependence.
