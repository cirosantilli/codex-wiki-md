# Spontaneous and elicited social cognition

↑ **Parent:** [Social cognition](social-cognition.md)

The distinction between what a person does without explicit instruction and what they can do when a relevant strategy is prompted. Differences can reflect [attention](attention.md), [motivation](motivation.md), familiarity or control rather than absolute absence of capacity.
