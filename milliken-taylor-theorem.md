<h1 id="milliken-taylor-theorem">Milliken–Taylor theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hindman theorem](hindman-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milliken–Taylor_theorem)

The Milliken–Taylor theorem gives monochromatic systems of separated block sums with a fixed compressed coefficient vector, such as $(1,2,3)$. Systems associated with two nonproportional compressed coefficient vectors need not have the same color: in particular, there is a finite coloring for which a [finite-sums set](finite-sums-set.md) and the $(1,2,3)$ Milliken–Taylor system cannot be monochromatic together.
