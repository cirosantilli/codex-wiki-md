# Nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

A nontrivial zero of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) lies in the [critical strip](critical-strip.md) $0<\Re s<1$. The [functional equation of the Riemann zeta function](functional-equation-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) reflects such zeros across the [critical line](critical-line.md) $\Re s=1/2$.
