# Magnetic Jeans mass

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

Replacing the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) in a [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md) estimate by the [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) $B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$ gives the displayed magnetic-support [mass](mass.md) scale. A sphere whose diameter is the corresponding wavelength gives $C=\pi^{5/2}/6$; the [crossing-time cloud support mass](crossing-time-cloud-support-mass.md) instead gives $C=(4\pi/3)(3\pi/32)^{3/2}$. The coefficient is model dependent, and a real [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) supplies anisotropic stresses rather than isotropic gas [pressure](pressure-split.md).

During isotropic [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md), $B\propto\rho^{2/3}$, so $M_B$ and the number of such masses in a fixed material cloud remain constant. This is another expression of a conserved [mass-to-flux ratio](mass-to-flux-ratio.md). It contrasts with the decreasing thermal [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md) of [isothermal fragmentation](isothermal-fragmentation.md), and does not rule out motion or fragmentation along field lines.
