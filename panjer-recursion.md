# Panjer recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Panjer claim-count class](panjer-claim-count-class.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjer_recursion)

For positive integer severities with [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $f_j$ and independent count in the [Panjer claim-count class](panjer-claim-count-class.md), the aggregate [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) starts at $g_0=p_0$ and satisfies the displayed recurrence. If $P,F,G$ are the count, severity and aggregate [probability generating functions](probability-generating-function.md), respectively, then $G=P\circ F$ and $(1-aF)G'=(a+b)F'G$. Equating the coefficient of $z^{r-1}$ gives $r g_r=\sum_{j=1}^r[ a(r-j)+(a+b)j]f_jg_{r-j}$. The positivity assumption excludes zero severities; otherwise the initial value and recursion denominator change.
