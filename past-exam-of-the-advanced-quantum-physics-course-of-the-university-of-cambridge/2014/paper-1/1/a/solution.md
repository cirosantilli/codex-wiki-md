<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The normalized [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) has $|A|^2=\sqrt{m\omega/(\pi\hbar)}$ and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_0=\hbar\omega/2$. Its [classical turning points](../../../../../classical-turning-point.md) are $x=\pm\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$. With $y=x\sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}$, the [ground-state oscillator probability beyond the turning points](../../../../../ground-state-oscillator-probability-beyond-the-turning-points.md) is

$$
\boxed{P=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\int_1^\infty e^{-y^2}\,dy=1-\operatorname{erf}(1)\simeq0.16.}
$$

The stated rounded [error function](../../../../../error-function.md) gives $0.16$; the more precise Gaussian integral is $0.15730$.
