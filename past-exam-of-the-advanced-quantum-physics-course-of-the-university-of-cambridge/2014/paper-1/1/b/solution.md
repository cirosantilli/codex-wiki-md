<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An impulse integrated across its full support produces the [impulsive quantum kick](../../../../../impulsive-quantum-kick.md) $K=e^{-i\hat U/\hbar}$. Thus the leading transition amplitude is $-iU_{21}/\hbar$ and

$$
\boxed{P_{1\to2}=\frac{|U_{21}|^2}{\hbar^2}+O(|U_{21}/\hbar|^4).}
$$

Here $U_{21}$ has units of action. The [off-diagonal two-state impulse](../../../../../off-diagonal-two-state-impulse.md) is exactly $P=\sin^2(|U_{21}|/\hbar)$ for a Hermitian two-state impulse with zero diagonal. No [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) denominator occurs: the impulse is instantaneous.
