<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For two equivalent $p$ [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), antisymmetry permits

$$
\boxed{{}^3P_{0,1,2},\qquad{}^1D_2,\qquad{}^1S_0.}
$$

Their $9+5+1=15$ states exhaust $\binom62$. [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) first maximize [spin](../../../../../spin.md), then orbital [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md); the less-than-half-filled $p$ shell puts the smallest $J$ lowest. Hence the ground level is **${}^3P_0$**. This is the [allowed LS terms of a p-squared configuration](../../../../../allowed-ls-terms-of-a-p-squared-configuration.md).[Ground configuration2s²2p² and its³P,¹D,¹S levels](../../../../../energy-levels-of-neutral-carbon-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
