<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At zero field, the [spin-one-half triplet state](../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) has [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\Delta/4$ and degeneracy three; the [spin-one-half singlet state](../../../../../spin-one-half-singlet-state.md) has [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $-3\Delta/4$ and degeneracy one:

$$
\boxed{E_t=\Delta/4\ (3),\qquad E_s=-3\Delta/4\ (1).}
$$

For $\Delta>0$ the singlet is lower; for $\Delta<0$ the triplet is lower. If $\Delta=0$, all four states coincide.
