<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $|w|\gg|\Delta|$, the mixed states approach opposite-spin product states and

$$
\boxed{E_\pm\simeq-\frac\Delta4\pm\frac{|w|}{2},\qquad E_{\uparrow\uparrow,\downarrow\downarrow}=\frac\Delta4\mp\frac u2.}
$$

The omitted mixed-branch correction is of order $\Delta^2/|w|$. The four levels are **generically nondegenerate**, apart from special moment choices or crossings.

For equal-and-opposite signed moments, $g_2=-g_1$, $u=0$ and the parallel-spin level remains exactly twofold degenerate at $\Delta/4$; the other two approach $-\Delta/4\pm|g_1\mu_0B|$. Equal moment magnitudes are an extra assumption, not implied by opposite charges alone. If $g_1=g_2$, the differential Zeeman term vanishes and the singlet/triplet-zero block stays unmixed by the field. This is the [strong-field limit of a two-spin exchange spectrum](../../../../../strong-field-limit-of-a-two-spin-exchange-spectrum.md).
