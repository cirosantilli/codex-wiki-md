<h1 id="3/solution-energy-sketch">Solution (energy sketch)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The square-root branches avoid one another when $\Delta\ne0$; crossings with a parallel-spin branch are allowed because total [spin](../../../../spin.md) projections differ. At zero field the positive-$\Delta$ sketch has one lower singlet and a triplet above it. The source supplies no numerical moment ratio, so the two panels show explicit illustrative signed choices, including the exactly compensated case.
