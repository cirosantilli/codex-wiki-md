<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [central field approximation](../../../../../central-field-approximation.md) replaces the anisotropic many-electron environment by an average spherical potential, giving one-electron orbitals labelled by $n,l,m$. Screening changes the effective radial attraction and removes the pure Coulomb degeneracy between different $l$.

The [Hartree method](../../../../../hartree-method.md) improves the potential self-consistently. For normalized occupied orbitals,

$$
\left[-\frac{\hbar^2\nabla^2}{2m_e}-\frac{Ze^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}+\sum_{j\ne i}\frac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\int\frac{|\phi_j(\mathbf r')|^2}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d^3r'\right]\phi_i=\epsilon_i\phi_i.
$$

Guess orbitals, construct their mean fields, solve the [Hartree equations](../../../../../hartree-equations.md), and iterate to convergence. The $j\ne i$ sum excludes self-interaction in this product-state model. A spherical average can enforce the central-field specialization. A [Hartree product state](../../../../../hartree-product-state.md) omits fermionic exchange and dynamical correlation; [Hartree-Fock method](../../../../../hartree-fock-method.md) adds antisymmetry and exchange through a [Slater determinant](../../../../../slater-determinant.md). Converged orbital [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) must not simply be added as the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), because that double counts pair interactions.[Authored Atoms chapter,§7.3.1: Hartree self-consistency and product-state limitations](../../../../../topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
