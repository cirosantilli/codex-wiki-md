↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [postulates of quantum mechanics](../../../../../postulates-of-quantum-mechanics.md) assign a pure state to a normalized ray in a complex [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), and an observable to a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md). A spectral outcome projector $P_a$ has [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $p_a=\langle\psi|P_a|\psi\rangle$. An ideal [projective measurement](../../../../../projective-measurement.md) updates the state to $P_a|\psi\rangle/\sqrt{p_a}$ when $p_a>0$; degeneracy requires the full outcome projector.

Between measurements, an isolated system obeys $i\hbar\partial_t|\psi\rangle=H|\psi\rangle$, giving unitary evolution. Composite systems use [tensor products](../../../../../tensor-product.md); identical particles additionally obey the appropriate exchange symmetry. Mixed preparations use a [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md), with $p_a=\operatorname{tr}(\rho P_a)$. These postulates require a specified [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and measurement model; they do not assign those automatically.[Quantum states, observables, measurement and Schrödinger evolution](../../../../../quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
