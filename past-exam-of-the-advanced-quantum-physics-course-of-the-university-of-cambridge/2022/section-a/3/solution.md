<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Write the trap [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) as $\omega$ and the single-particle [oscillator length](../../../../oscillator-length.md) as $\ell=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$. The [center of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) coordinate $X=(x_1+x_2)/2$ and relative coordinate $r=x_1-x_2$ give independent [quantum harmonic oscillators](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), both of frequency $\omega$, with masses $2m$ and [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) $m/2$. Label their excitation numbers by $N$ and $n$. The unperturbed [energy eigenvalue](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is $\hbar\omega(N+n+1)$.

Particle exchange leaves $X$ unchanged and sends $r$ to $-r$. The [exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions](../../../../exchange-symmetry-of-two-identical-spin-one-half-fermions.md) therefore requires even $n$ to accompany the [spin-one-half singlet state](../../../../spin-one-half-singlet-state.md), and odd $n$ to accompany a [spin-one-half triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md). For the [contact shifts of two spin-one-half fermions in a harmonic trap](../../../../contact-shifts-of-two-spin-one-half-fermions-in-a-harmonic-trap.md), the [delta potential](../../../../delta-potential.md) depends only on $r$. For odd $n$, the relative [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanishes at $r=0$, so its contact shift is zero. Within the first-excited manifold, both perturbations are diagonal in the labels $(N,n,S,M)$: this is a [perturbation diagonal within a degenerate eigenspace](../../../../perturbation-diagonal-within-a-degenerate-eigenspace.md). For $n=0$, the relative [oscillator length](../../../../oscillator-length.md) is $\sqrt2\ell$, giving the [first-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../first-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) shift

$$
U=\lambda|\varphi_{\mathrm{rel},0}(0)|^2
=\frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}\ell}.
$$

The [Zeeman splitting](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) is $2\mu_BB_zM$ for total [spin](../../../../spin.md) projection $\hbar M$. Thus the five lowest states, counted with multiplicity, have

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
(N,n)&S&M&E\text{ to first order}\\ \hline
(0,0)&0&0&\hbar\omega+U\\
(0,1)&1&-1,0,1&2\hbar\omega+2\mu_BB_zM\\
(1,0)&0&0&2\hbar\omega+U
\end{array}}
$$

under the assumed conditions $|U|,2\mu_B|B_z|\ll\hbar\omega$. The three [triplet states](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) have slopes $-2\mu_B,0,2\mu_B$, while both [singlet states](../../../../singlet-state.md) are field independent at this order. The three [triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) branches coincide at zero [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Their crossing with the excited [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md) is allowed because the [total-spin sectors](../../../../total-spin-sector.md) do not mix. The odd relative [wavefunctions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) are in fact unaffected by this [delta potential](../../../../delta-potential.md) at any coupling; the displayed [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md) shifts retain only first order.
