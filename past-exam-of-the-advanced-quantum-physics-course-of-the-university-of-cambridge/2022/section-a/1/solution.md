<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

By [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md), combining the first two [spin one-half](../../../../spin-one-half.md) particles gives $0\oplus1$. Adding the third gives the [three spin-one-half angular-momentum decomposition](../../../../three-spin-one-half-angular-momentum-decomposition.md)

$$
\left(\frac12\right)^{\otimes3}
=\left(0\oplus1\right)\otimes\frac12
=\frac12\oplus\frac12\oplus\frac32.
$$

Thus there is one $S=3/2$ [total-spin sector](../../../../total-spin-sector.md) and two independent $S=1/2$ sectors. Since the particles are distinguishable, no [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md) restriction removes either copy. In a simultaneous [angular momentum eigenstate](../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md), $\hat S^2$ and $\hat S^{(z)}$ have [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar^2S(S+1)$ and $\hbar M$, so

$$
\boxed{E_{S,M}=\epsilon_t S(S+1)+\epsilon_z M.}
$$

The [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md) and their generic multiplicities are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
S&M&E_{S,M}&\text{multiplicity}\\ \hline
3/2&-3/2,-1/2,1/2,3/2&15\epsilon_t/4+\epsilon_zM&1\text{ for each }M\\
1/2&-1/2,1/2&3\epsilon_t/4+\epsilon_zM&2\text{ for each }M
\end{array}
$$

The count $4+2\times2=8$ agrees with the [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the three-particle [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md). These multiplicities must be added whenever numerical [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) coincide. In particular, for $\epsilon_z=0$ and $\epsilon_t\ne0$, there are two fourfold [degenerate energy levels](../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md), $15\epsilon_t/4$ and $3\epsilon_t/4$. For $\epsilon_t=0$ and $\epsilon_z\ne0$, the multiplicities at $M=-3/2,-1/2,1/2,3/2$ are $1,3,3,1$. If both coefficients vanish, the single [energy level](../../../../energy-level.md) has multiplicity eight. Other coincidences between the two [total-spin sectors](../../../../total-spin-sector.md) occur when $3\epsilon_t+(M-m)\epsilon_z=0$, with $M$ from the quartet and $m=\pm1/2$ from a doublet.
