<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

A [density matrix](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) has unit [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md). The [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) are traceless, so $\operatorname{Tr}\hat\rho=C$ and hence $C=1$. Using $\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_i\sigma_j)=2\delta_{ij}$ in the [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) gives

$$
\langle\sigma_j\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\sigma_j)=r_j.
$$

Consequently the required [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md) and [density matrix](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{C=1,\qquad \mathbf r=\left(\frac12,0,0\right),\qquad
\rho=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/4\\1/4&1/2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The two [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3/4$ and $1/4$, both positive, so this is a valid [density operator](../../../../density-matrix-split.md). Its [purity of a density operator](../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2=\left(\frac34\right)^2+\left(\frac14\right)^2=\frac58<1.
$$

Therefore it is a [mixed state](../../../../mixed-state.md). Equivalently, its [spectral decomposition](../../../../spectral-decomposition.md) is $\rho=(3/4)|+x\rangle\langle+x|+(1/4)|-x\rangle\langle-x|$, an ensemble of the two distinct [spin one-half along an axis](../../../../spin-one-half-along-an-axis.md) states, rather than a single [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md).
