<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For this [spin-boson model](../../../../../spin-boson-model.md), take $H_0=H_1+H_2$. Since $[H_0,\sigma_z]=0$, the [interaction picture](../../../../../interaction-picture.md) observable is

$$
\sigma_z^I(t)=e^{iH_0t/\hbar}\sigma_ze^{-iH_0t/\hbar}=\sigma_z.
$$

The normalized vectors $|\phi_\uparrow\rangle$ and $|\phi_\downarrow\rangle$ belong to different [eigenspaces](../../../../../eigenspace.md) of the [Hermitian operator](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $\sigma_z$, and are therefore orthogonal. This remains true even though each vector includes the oscillator degrees of freedom. Hence all cross terms in the [expectation value](../../../../../expectation-value.md) vanish, while the diagonal terms have [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $+1$ and $-1$:

$$
\boxed{\langle\psi_\sigma^I(t)|\sigma_z^I(t)|\psi_\sigma^I(t)\rangle
=|c_{\uparrow\sigma}(t)|^2-|c_{\downarrow\sigma}(t)|^2.}
$$

Normalization also gives $|c_{\uparrow\sigma}|^2+|c_{\downarrow\sigma}|^2=1$, so these are the [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) of the two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) outcomes.
