<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the normalized [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md) basis, the only nonzero potential [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md) are

$$
V_{mn}=-\frac\lambda2\bigl(\delta_{m,n+1}+\delta_{m,n-1}\bigr).
$$

Thus a useful small parameter is $|\lambda|/E_L\ll1$, which suppresses admixture of well-separated [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md). [First-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../first-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) further requires $|\lambda|/2\ll|E_n-E_{n\pm1}|$ for the coupled states. This latter condition fails near a half-integer $\alpha$ even for very small $\lambda$; there one must use [degenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) in the nearly degenerate pair. Smallness is measured against the gaps to the other states, not the vanishing gap inside this pair.

Near $\alpha=1/2$, keep $n=0,1$. The resulting [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) matrix and its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
H_{\mathrm{pair}}=\begin{pmatrix}E_L\alpha^2&-\lambda/2\\-\lambda/2&E_L(1-\alpha)^2\end{pmatrix},
$$



$$
\widetilde\epsilon_{0,1}\simeq E_L\left[(\alpha-1/2)^2+\frac14\right]
\mp\sqrt{E_L^2(\alpha-1/2)^2+\frac{\lambda^2}{4}}.
$$

In particular,

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\epsilon_{0,1}(\pi\hbar/L)
=\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{2mL^2}\mp\frac{|\lambda|}{2}
+O(\lambda^2/E_L).}
$$

For positive $\lambda$, the lower [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) at the crossing is the symmetric combination of $n=0,1$, and the upper state is the antisymmetric combination. A negative $\lambda$ exchanges these combinations but leaves the ordered [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) unchanged.

This [cosine-potential gap in a quantum ring](../../../../../cosine-potential-gap-in-a-quantum-ring.md) appears in the middle panel above: each lowest-branch crossing opens into an [avoided crossing](../../../../../avoided-crossing.md) of gap $|\lambda|$ to first order. Away from these crossings, the diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md) vanish, so the leading shifts are second order. At integer $\alpha$, the two first-excited [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) differ by two units of $n$ and therefore have no first-order coupling; their smaller splitting begins at second order. The numerical plot includes that splitting.
