<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Define a transformed [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) by

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\psi(x)=e^{-iax/\hbar}\psi(x).}
$$

Indeed, writing $\psi=e^{iax/\hbar}\widetilde\psi$ gives

$$
(p-a)\psi=e^{iax/\hbar}p\widetilde\psi,
\qquad H_a\psi=e^{iax/\hbar}\frac{p^2}{2m}\widetilde\psi.
$$

This is the electromagnetic [gauge transformation](../../../../../gauge-transformation.md) generated by $\chi(x)=-A_xx$: $A_x'=A_x+\partial_x\chi=0$ and $\psi'=e^{iq\chi/\hbar}\psi$. The transformed [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is consequently $p^2/(2m)$, but the original [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) becomes the [twisted boundary condition](../../../../../twisted-boundary-condition.md)

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\psi(x+L)=e^{-iaL/\hbar}\widetilde\psi(x).}
$$

For the domain of the [self-adjoint](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) kinetic [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), the derivative has the same twist, $\widetilde\psi'(x+L)=e^{-iaL/\hbar}\widetilde\psi'(x)$. Thus the [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) has moved into the [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md). The transformation is a single-valued gauge on periodic representatives only when $aL/\hbar$ is an integer multiple of $2\pi$; otherwise it is understood on the cut interval with the displayed identification of its endpoints.
