<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) be $q=-e$, where $e>0$ is the [elementary charge](../../../../../elementary-charge.md). [Minimal coupling](../../../../../minimal-coupling.md) to a tangential [magnetic vector potential](../../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md) gives

$$
H=\frac{(p-qA_x)^2}{2m}.
$$

A constant tangential [magnetic vector potential](../../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md) is an available gauge on the ring, so $a=qA_x$. By [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md), its circulation is the oriented [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md)

$$
\Phi_C=\oint_{+x}\mathbf A\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=LA_x,
\qquad \boxed{aL=q\Phi_C=-e\Phi_C.}
$$

The orientation matters here. In the original diagram, increasing $x$ goes anticlockwise, whose associated positive normal points out of the page, whereas the indicated positive [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) $\Phi$ points into the page. Hence $\Phi_C=-\Phi$ for that diagram, and its convention gives $\boxed{aL=e\Phi}$. If instead $\Phi$ is defined with the normal associated to $+x$, the relation is $aL=-e\Phi$.

There is no requirement that the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) be nonzero on the ring: it can be confined to a [solenoid](../../../../../solenoid.md) inside it. The nonzero circulation of the [magnetic vector potential](../../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md) still affects the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) through the [Aharonov-Bohm effect](../../../../../aharonov-bohm-effect.md).
