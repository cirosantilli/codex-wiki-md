<h1 id="section-b/4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $\Lambda=|\lambda|\gg E_L$. Changing the sign of $\lambda$ translates the potential by $L/2$ without changing its [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md), so take a minimum at $x=0$ and expand there. The [parabolic approximation](../../../../../parabolic-approximation.md) gives

$$
V(x)\simeq-\Lambda+\frac\Lambda2\left(\frac{2\pi x}{L}\right)^2
=-\Lambda+\frac12m\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}^2x^2,
\qquad \omega_{\mathrm{loc}}=\frac{2\pi}{L}\sqrt{\frac\Lambda m}.
$$

The low-lying [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are localized well inside one period. A local [gauge transformation](../../../../../gauge-transformation.md) removes $a$ from their [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) equation, giving

$$
\widetilde\epsilon_n(a)\simeq-\Lambda+\hbar\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\left(n+\frac12\right).
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\epsilon_1(0)-\widetilde\epsilon_0(0)
\simeq\frac{2\pi\hbar}{L}\sqrt{\frac{|\lambda|}{m}}
=\sqrt{2|\lambda|E_L}.}
$$

The sketch consists of two nearly horizontal branches, separated by this gap, as in the right panel above. The [parabolic approximation](../../../../../parabolic-approximation.md) makes them completely independent of [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md); in the actual ring they retain exponentially small periodic dependence through [quantum tunnelling](../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) around the ring. Corrections from the nonquadratic part of the potential shift the mean energies and gap by relative order $\sqrt{E_L/\Lambda}$, whereas the tunnelling dependence is smaller than every power of that parameter.
