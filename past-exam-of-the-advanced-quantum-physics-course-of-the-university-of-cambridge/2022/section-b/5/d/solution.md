<h1 id="section-b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Normalize the trial [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) as $(|\phi_0\rangle+c|\phi_1\rangle)/\sqrt{1+c^2}$. With the phases used above, the off-diagonal position [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) is $x_{01}=-32a/(9\pi^2)$. Define

$$
\Delta=\epsilon_1^{(0)}-\epsilon_0^{(0)}=3K,
\qquad W=eEx_{01}=-\frac{32eEa}{9\pi^2}.
$$

The diagonal perturbation [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md) vanish by parity, so the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) is

$$
\mathcal E(c)=\frac{\epsilon_0^{(0)}+2cW+c^2\epsilon_1^{(0)}}{1+c^2}.
$$

The stationarity equation is $W+\Delta c-Wc^2=0$. The minimum continuously connected to the unperturbed ground [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) has

$$
\boxed{c_{\min}=-\frac W\Delta+O(E^3)
=\frac{256meEa^3}{27\pi^4\hbar^2}+O(E^3).}
$$

Equivalently, diagonalize the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) matrix in this two-dimensional trial space. Its lower [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is

$$
\mathcal E_{\min}=\frac{\epsilon_0^{(0)}+\epsilon_1^{(0)}}2
-\sqrt{\left(\frac\Delta2\right)^2+W^2}
=\epsilon_0^{(0)}-\frac{W^2}\Delta+O(E^4).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E_{\min}=\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{8ma^2}
-\frac{8192me^2E^2a^4}{243\pi^6\hbar^2}+O(E^4).}
$$

This retains exactly the $k=1$ term of the full ground-state [second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../second-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) sum. Every omitted term is negative, so the full quadratic correction is more negative than this restricted one. This is consistent with the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](../../../../../rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md): minimizing over a two-state subspace gives an upper bound on the exact ground energy. The bound applies to the unexpanded two-state minimum; a truncated series is to be compared order by order.
