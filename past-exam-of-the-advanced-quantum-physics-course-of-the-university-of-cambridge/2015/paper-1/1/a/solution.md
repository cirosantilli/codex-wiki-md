<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) allows each one-particle magnetic substate only once, since the [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) share the other [quantum numbers](../../../../../quantum-number.md). To maximize the total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) projection, occupy $m_j=5/2,3/2,1/2,-1/2$, giving

$$
M_{\max}=\frac52+\frac32+\frac12-\frac12=4.
$$

The corresponding [Slater determinant](../../../../../slater-determinant.md) is a [highest-weight vector](../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md): every attempted raising operation either tries to exceed $j$ or duplicates an occupied substate and vanishes. Therefore it belongs to a multiplet with **$\boxed{J_{\max}=4}$**, not the distinguishable-particle value $4j=10$. Equivalently, four particles in a six-state shell have two holes whose maximum [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) projection is $5/2+3/2=4$. The general [maximum total angular momentum of equivalent fermions](../../../../../maximum-total-angular-momentum-of-equivalent-fermions.md) is $J_{\max}=Nj-N(N-1)/2$ for $N\leq2j+1$.
