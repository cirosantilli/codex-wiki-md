<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $+x$ [eigenstate](../../../../../eigenstate.md) is $|+x\rangle=(|\uparrow\rangle+|\downarrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$, since $\sigma_x(1,1)^T=(1,1)^T$. Use a resonant quarter-turn pulse, $\tau_{\pi/2}=\pi/(2\Omega)$, and choose its [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) so that

$$
\varphi+\omega_0\tau_{\pi/2}=\frac\pi2\pmod{2\pi}.
$$

The laboratory-frame relative amplitude then is $i e^{-i\pi/2}=1$, giving

$$
\boxed{|\psi(\tau_{\pi/2})\rangle\sim|+x\rangle,\qquad
\langle s_x\rangle=\hbar/2.}
$$

In rotating axes the equivalent target is obtained with $\varphi=\pi/2$; the extra laboratory [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) above compensates [Larmor precession](../../../../../larmor-precession.md) during the pulse. After switching the RF field off, the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) continues to precess in the static field, so this statement specifies the preparation instant rather than a state fixed along laboratory $x$ forever.
