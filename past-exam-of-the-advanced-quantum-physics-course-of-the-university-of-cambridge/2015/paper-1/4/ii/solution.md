<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $+y$ [eigenstate](../../../../../eigenstate.md) is $|+y\rangle=(|\uparrow\rangle+i|\downarrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with $\sigma_y(1,i)^T=(1,i)^T$. Use the same duration $\tau_{\pi/2}=\pi/(2\Omega)$ but [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md)

$$
\varphi+\omega_0\tau_{\pi/2}=0\pmod{2\pi}.
$$

The relative amplitude is now $i$, giving

$$
\boxed{|\psi(\tau_{\pi/2})\rangle\sim|+y\rangle,\qquad
\langle s_y\rangle=\hbar/2.}
$$

The two transverse preparations differ by a quarter-cycle of RF [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md), not by changing the [pulse area](../../../../../quantum-pulse-area.md). Reversing the desired transverse direction adds $\pi$ to the chosen [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md).
