<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Centre the [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) at zero. Its [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is $\psi(x)=\sqrt{2/L}\cos(\pi x/L)$ inside the well and zero outside. The [momentum representation in quantum mechanics](../../../../../momentum-representation-in-quantum-mechanics.md) gives the [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) amplitude

$$
\phi(k)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\psi(x)e^{-ikx}dx
=2\sqrt{\pi L}\,\frac{\cos(kL/2)}{\pi^2-(kL)^2}.
$$

Consequently the unnormalised [wavevector distribution of an infinite-well ground state](../../../../../wavevector-distribution-of-an-infinite-well-ground-state.md) is

$$
\boxed{p(k)\propto\frac{\cos^2(kL/2)}{[\pi^2-(kL)^2]^2}.}
$$

The apparent singularities at $k=\pm\pi/L$ are removable. The normalized [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is $4\pi L$ times the boxed expression and has limit $L/(4\pi)$ there. Translating the well changes only the [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md)'s phase. The [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) is continuously distributed; a box [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) is not a free [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md).
