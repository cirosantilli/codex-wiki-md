<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\mathbf J=\mathbf S_1+\mathbf S_2$. [Angular momentum addition](../../../../../angular-momentum-addition.md) gives $j=0,1,2,3$, and the [spin-dot-product eigenvalue](../../../../../spin-dot-product-eigenvalue.md) is

$$
\frac{\hbar^2}{2}\left[j(j+1)-2\frac32\frac52\right].
$$

Thus the [spin-three-halves pair spectrum](../../../../../spin-three-halves-pair-spectrum.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\hbar^2}{4}(-15,-11,-3,9),\quad\text{with multiplicities }1,3,5,7.}
$$

For identical [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md), [exchange parity of two coupled identical spins](../../../../../exchange-parity-of-two-coupled-identical-spins.md) makes $j=0,2$ spin-antisymmetric and $j=1,3$ spin-symmetric. Corresponding spatial exchange symmetries make the full state antisymmetric; without a specified spatial state, none of these [total-spin sectors](../../../../../total-spin-sector.md) is excluded.
