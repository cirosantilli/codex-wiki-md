<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the orbital and [spin](../../../../../spin.md) operators act on different factors of the [tensor product](../../../../../tensor-product.md). Write $\mathbf S=\mathbf S_1+\mathbf S_2$. The identity

$$
\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=\frac12\left(S^2-S_1^2-S_2^2\right)
=\frac12\left(S^2-\frac32\hbar^2\right)
$$

shows that the [exchange interaction](../../../../../exchange-interaction.md) commutes with $S^2$ and $S_z$. The sum of the one-particle kinetic energies also commutes with them and with particle exchange. Thus one may use orbital [quantum numbers](../../../../../quantum-number.md) together with the [total-spin sector](../../../../../total-spin-sector.md) $S=0,1$ and projection $M=-S,\ldots,S$, with physical $S_z=\hbar M$.

For identical [spin one-half](../../../../../spin-one-half.md) particles the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) further restricts the states. The [spin-one-half singlet state](../../../../../spin-one-half-singlet-state.md) is antisymmetric in [spin](../../../../../spin.md) and must multiply a symmetric spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md); a [spin-one-half triplet state](../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) must multiply an antisymmetric spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md). Thus $n_1,n_2$ label unordered orbital occupations in symmetry-adapted states, not arbitrary ordered product states. This gives the labels $|n_1,n_2,S,M\rangle$, with the paper's last label expressed in units of $\hbar$.
