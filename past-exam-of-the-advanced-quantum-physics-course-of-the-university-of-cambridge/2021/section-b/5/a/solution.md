<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The components of the [momentum operator](../../../../../momentum-operator.md) do not generally commute with a position-dependent [magnetic vector potential](../../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md). Expanding [minimal coupling](../../../../../minimal-coupling.md) with the ordering intact gives

$$
H=\frac{p^2}{2m}+V+
\frac e{2m}\bigl(\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf A+\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf p\bigr)
+\frac{e^2}{2m}\mathbf A^2.
$$

Acting on a [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) shows that $\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf A-\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf p=-i\hbar\nabla\cdot\mathbf A$. In the [Coulomb gauge](../../../../../coulomb-gauge.md) the divergence vanishes, so the two cross terms agree. Discarding the quadratic term in the weak radiation amplitude gives

$$
\boxed{H=H_0+H_1(t)+O(A^2),\qquad
H_0=\frac{p^2}{2m}+V,\qquad
H_1(t)=\frac em\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf A(\mathbf r,t).}
$$

The equality of the two operator orderings follows from the gauge condition, not from treating $\mathbf p$ and $\mathbf A$ as commuting numbers component by component.
