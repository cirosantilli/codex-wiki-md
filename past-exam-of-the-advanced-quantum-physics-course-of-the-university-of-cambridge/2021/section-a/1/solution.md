<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The initial [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md) is a [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md). Since $a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle$ and the state is normalized, its mean [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md) is $N=\langle a^\dagger a\rangle=|\alpha|^2$. Each [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [energy eigenstate](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) acquires the phase $e^{-i\omega(n+1/2)t}$, so

$$
|\alpha(t)\rangle=e^{-i\omega t/2}|\alpha e^{-i\omega t}\rangle,
\qquad \langle a\rangle_t=\alpha e^{-i\omega t}.
$$

The prefactor is a [global phase](../../../../global-phase.md) and does not change an [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md). Inverting the definitions of the [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) gives $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$. If $\alpha=\sqrt N e^{i\varphi}$, then

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle_t=\sqrt{\frac{2\hbar N}{m\omega}}\cos(\omega t-\varphi).}
$$

For a classical [harmonic oscillator](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with amplitude $A$, the conserved [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $E=m\omega^2A^2/2$. Setting $E=\hbar\omega N$ gives exactly the same amplitude, $A=\sqrt{2\hbar N/(m\omega)}$. Thus these [coherent-state oscillator expectation values](../../../../coherent-state-oscillator-expectation-values.md) follow the classical orbit.

The full quantum mean [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is nevertheless $\langle H\rangle=\hbar\omega(N+1/2)$: the extra [zero-point energy](../../../../zero-point-energy.md) belongs to the nonzero position and momentum uncertainties required by the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md), rather than to the motion of the packet centre. In particular, $N=0$ gives zero mean displacement but a finite ground-state width. A [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) follows a classical mean trajectory while retaining finite position and momentum uncertainties.
