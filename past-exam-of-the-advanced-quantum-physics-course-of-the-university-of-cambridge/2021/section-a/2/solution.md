<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Use the uncoupled [angular momentum eigenstates](../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) $|m_L,\uparrow\rangle$ and $|m_L,\downarrow\rangle$, with $m_L=-1,0,1$. For the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) actually written in the paper, these are the [energy eigenstates](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), with

$$
E=\mu_BB_z(m_L+2m_S),\qquad m_S=\pm\frac12.
$$

The initial $J=3/2$ states are not all [energy eigenstates](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) of this operator. To find their energy populations, first perform the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md). The extremal states are $|3/2,3/2\rangle=|1,\uparrow\rangle$ and $|3/2,-3/2\rangle=|-1,\downarrow\rangle$. Applying the total [angular momentum lowering operator](../../../../angular-momentum-lowering-operator.md) to the first gives

$$
\sqrt3\,|3/2,1/2\rangle=\sqrt2\,|0,\uparrow\rangle+|1,\downarrow\rangle.
$$

Similarly,

$$
|3/2,-1/2\rangle=\sqrt{\frac23}|0,\downarrow\rangle+\sqrt{\frac13}|-1,\uparrow\rangle.
$$

The initial preparation is an equal incoherent mixture of the four coupled states. Each extremal state contributes population $1/4$ to its one [energy eigenvalue](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md), while each nonextremal state contributes $2/3$ and $1/3$ of its own $1/4$ population to the two displayed components. The resulting [Zeeman splitting](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
E/(\mu_BB_z)&-2&-1&0&1&2\\ \hline
\text{fraction of atoms}&1/4&1/6&1/6&1/6&1/4\\
\text{energy multiplicity}&1&1&2&1&1
\end{array}}
$$

for nonzero $B_z$. The relative populations are $\boxed{3:2:2:2:3}$ in this order; the two zero-energy states each have population $1/12$. These [Zeeman populations from an unpolarized angular-momentum multiplet](../../../../zeeman-populations-from-an-unpolarized-angular-momentum-multiplet.md) remain constant under subsequent evolution by the stated static [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), although off-diagonal [quantum coherence](../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) can evolve.

There is a distinct weak-field model in which a retained [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) keeps $J=3/2$ isolated and one projects the magnetic perturbation into that quartet. The [Landé g-factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) is then $4/3$, giving four shifts $\mu_BB_z(\pm2,\pm2/3)$ with equal populations. That calculation requires the additional projection or dominant [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md); it is not diagonalization of the full magnetic [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) specified here, which also mixes the $J=1/2$ sector.
