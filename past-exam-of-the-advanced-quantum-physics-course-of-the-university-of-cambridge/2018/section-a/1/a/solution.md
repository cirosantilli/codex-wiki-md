<h1 id="section-a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $|m_1,m_2\rangle$ for a product of the two individual spin-one [spin eigenstates](../../../../../spin-eigenstate.md), with [magnetic quantum numbers](../../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) $m_i=-1,0,1$. Total $S_z=0$ requires $m_1+m_2=0$, leaving precisely

$$
\boxed{|1,-1\rangle,\qquad |0,0\rangle,\qquad |-1,1\rangle.}
$$

Here these are product labels, not labels of the total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). Write their coefficients in this order as $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$. With physical [spin ladder operators](../../../../../spin-ladder-operator.md) $S_\pm=S_{1\pm}+S_{2\pm}$, each nonzero individual step has [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $\sqrt2\hbar$, so

$$
S_+|\psi\rangle=\sqrt2\hbar[(\alpha+\beta)|1,0\rangle+(\beta+\gamma)|0,1\rangle],
$$



$$
S_-|\psi\rangle=\sqrt2\hbar[(\alpha+\beta)|0,-1\rangle+(\beta+\gamma)|-1,0\rangle].
$$

Both must vanish for a [singlet state](../../../../../singlet-state.md). The independent [product states](../../../../../product-state.md) therefore require $\alpha=\gamma=-\beta$. [Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) gives the [spin-one singlet state](../../../../../spin-one-singlet-state.md)

$$
\boxed{|S=0,M=0\rangle=\frac{|1,-1\rangle-|0,0\rangle+|-1,1\rangle}{\sqrt3},}
$$

up to an overall [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md). Conversely, $S_z|\psi\rangle=S_+|\psi\rangle=0$ implies $S^2|\psi\rangle=0$ through $S^2=S_-S_++S_z^2+\hbar S_z$, so these conditions really select total [spin](../../../../../spin.md) zero. Distinguishability imposes no additional exchange restriction. The ladder hint uses dimensionless [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) units; restoring its common factor $\hbar$ does not change the coefficient ratios.
