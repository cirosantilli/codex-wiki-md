<h1 id="section-a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a bound [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) with a [nondegenerate energy eigenvalue](../../../../../nondegenerate-energy-eigenvalue.md) of an even one-dimensional potential, the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) can be chosen with definite [parity](../../../../../parity.md). A uniform [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) $\mathcal E$ gives the [electric-dipole interaction](../../../../../electric-dipole-interaction.md) $H'=-q\mathcal E x$, which is odd under reflection. Hence

$$
\Delta E_n^{(1)}=-q\mathcal E\langle n|x|n\rangle=0,
$$

since $x|\psi_n(x)|^2$ is odd. [Second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../second-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) then gives the leading allowed [Stark effect](../../../../../stark-effect.md) shift:

$$
\boxed{\Delta E_n=q^2\mathcal E^2\sum_{r\ne n}\frac{|\langle r|x|n\rangle|^2}{E_n^{(0)}-E_r^{(0)}}+O(\mathcal E^4).}
$$

Only opposite-[parity](../../../../../parity.md) intermediate states have nonzero [position](../../../../../position.md) [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md). More generally, reflection maps $H(\mathcal E)$ to $H(-\mathcal E)$, so an analytic nondegenerate [energy level](../../../../../energy-level.md) is an even function of the field. This explains the absence of all odd orders and the usual [quadratic Stark effect](../../../../../quadratic-stark-effect.md).

A level separation changes by the difference of the two quadratic coefficients. Symmetry alone does not guarantee a nonzero differential shift: in a [displaced quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../displaced-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) every level moves by the same $-q^2\mathcal E^2/(2m\omega^2)$, leaving every spacing unchanged. Degenerate states would instead require [degenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) and can split linearly; the [nondegeneracy of one-dimensional bound states](../../../../../nondegeneracy-of-one-dimensional-bound-states.md) applies to ordinary isolated well levels.
