<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In a large periodic quantization volume $\mathcal V$, one [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) state occupies $(2\pi)^3/\mathcal V$ in [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) space. There are two transverse [photon polarizations](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md), and each oscillator contributes $\hbar ck/2$. A lower [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) cutoff $\lambda_{\min}$ corresponds to $k_{\max}=2\pi/\lambda_{\min}$, so the [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) is

$$
u_{\rm vac}
=2\int_{|\mathbf k|\leq k_{\max}}\frac{\mathrm d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{\hbar ck}{2}
=\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi^2}\int_0^{k_{\max}}k^3\,\mathrm dk
=\frac{\hbar c\,k_{\max}^4}{8\pi^2}
=\frac{2\pi^2\hbar c}{\lambda_{\min}^4}.
$$

For $\lambda_{\min}=10^{-10}\,\mathrm m$,

$$
\boxed{u_{\rm vac}\simeq6.24\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}.}
$$

The factor two is [polarization](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md), not an extra independent count of positive and negative frequencies. The continuum [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) integral already counts independent travelling-wave modes in the usual field quantization convention. The quartic cutoff dependence also shows why an assumed shortest [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) is crucial.
