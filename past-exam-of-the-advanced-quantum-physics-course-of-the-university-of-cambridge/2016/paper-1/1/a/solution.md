<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The open shell contains one $f$ [electron](../../../../../electron.md), so $L=3$ and $S=1/2$. For a less-than-half-filled shell, [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) select the smaller total angular [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), $J=L-S=5/2$, giving the free-ion ground level $ {}^2F_{5/2}$. The [Landé g-factor](../../../../../lande-g-factor.md) is

$$
g_J=\frac32+\frac{(1/2)(3/2)-3(4)}{2(5/2)(7/2)}
=\boxed{\frac67}.
$$

In a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) weak compared with the spin-orbit splitting, the electronic Zeeman shifts are $\delta E_{m_J}=g_J\mu_BBm_J$, for $m_J=-5/2,-3/2,\ldots,5/2$. Thus **six equally spaced magnetic substates** appear. At $B=1\,\mathrm T$, the adjacent-level spacing is

$$
\boxed{\Delta E_{\rm adjacent}=\frac67\mu_B B
=7.95\times10^{-24}\,\mathrm J
=4.96\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The complete lowest-to-highest multiplet spread is five spacings,

$$
\boxed{\Delta E_{\rm full}=5\Delta E_{\rm adjacent}
=3.97\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm J
=2.48\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

Giving both quantities removes the ambiguity in the word splitting. This is the free-ion, weak-field result; no crystal-field doublet or [hyperfine structure](../../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) is specified.
