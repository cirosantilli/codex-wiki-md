<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the isolated [nuclear spin](../../../../nuclear-spin.md) [Zeeman effect](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) implied by the supplied [nuclear magnetic moment](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-moment.md), with [hyperfine structure](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) neglected. The [proton](../../../../proton.md) [gyromagnetic ratio](../../../../gyromagnetic-ratio.md) is

$$
\gamma_p=\frac{g_p\mu_N}{\hbar}=2.68\times10^8\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}\,T^{-1}}.
$$

The two [spin](../../../../spin.md) levels are separated by $g_p\mu_NB_0$. Resonant [nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) therefore requires the [Larmor frequency](../../../../larmor-frequency.md)

$$
\boxed{\omega=\gamma_p B_0=2.68\times10^8\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}},\qquad \frac{\omega}{2\pi}=42.7\ \mathrm{MHz}.
$$

The duration is a [Rabi pi pulse](../../../../rabi-pi-pulse.md): in a frame rotating with the resonant transverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the [spin](../../../../spin.md) must rotate through $\pi$. If $B_1$ denotes the magnitude of a circularly rotating field with the correct sense of [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), the [Rabi angular frequency](../../../../rabi-angular-frequency.md) is $\Omega_R=\gamma_pB_1$. The shortest pulse is then

$$
\boxed{\tau=\frac{\pi}{\gamma_pB_1}=1.17\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{s}=117\ \mu\mathrm{s}}.
$$

There is an amplitude convention to specify. If the applied transverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is instead the linearly oscillating $B_1\cos(\omega t)\hat{\mathbf x}$, its resonant circular component has magnitude $B_1/2$. The [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md) gives $\Omega_R=\gamma_pB_1/2$ and hence

$$
\boxed{\tau_{\rm linear}=\frac{2\pi}{\gamma_pB_1}=234\ \mu\mathrm{s}}.
$$

The wording does not specify this polarization convention, so the two pulse durations should be distinguished. Odd multiples of either shortest [Rabi pi pulse](../../../../rabi-pi-pulse.md) also invert the [spin](../../../../spin.md) within the corresponding ideal model.
