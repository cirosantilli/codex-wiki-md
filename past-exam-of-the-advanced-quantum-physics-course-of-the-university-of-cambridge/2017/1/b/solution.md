<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The operators associated with the two angular momenta commute with one another, and each squared [angular momentum operator](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) commutes with every component of that same [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md). Two useful identities are

$$
J_z=J_{1z}+J_{2z},\qquad
\mathbf J_1\cdot\mathbf J_2=\frac12(J^2-J_1^2-J_2^2).
$$

Consequently the two maximal commuting subsets of the displayed collection are

$$
\boxed{\{J_1^2,J_2^2,J_{1z},J_{2z},J_z\}},\qquad
\boxed{\{J_1^2,J_2^2,J^2,J_z,\mathbf J_1\cdot\mathbf J_2\}}.
$$

The first set gives the uncoupled [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md) $|j_1m_1\rangle\otimes|j_2m_2\rangle$. Its $J_z$ [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is already fixed by $m=m_1+m_2$. The second gives the coupled [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md) $|j_1j_2;jm\rangle$ for the [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md). Its scalar-product [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is already fixed by $j,j_1,j_2$. Thus an independent [complete set of commuting observables](../../../../complete-set-of-commuting-observables.md) uses four operators in either case; literal maximality within the question's list includes the dependent fifth operator.

For generic nonzero angular momenta, no further listed operator can be added. In particular,

$$
[J_{1z},\mathbf J_1\cdot\mathbf J_2]
=i\hbar(J_{1y}J_{2x}-J_{1x}J_{2y})
$$

is generally nonzero, and $[J_{1z},J^2]=2[J_{1z},\mathbf J_1\cdot\mathbf J_2]$. The same obstruction applies to $J_{2z}$. Their sum $J_z$ commutes because the two [commutators](../../../../commutator.md) cancel.
