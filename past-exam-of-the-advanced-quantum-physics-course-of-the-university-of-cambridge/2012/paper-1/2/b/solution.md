<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a point, stationary proton, the leading hydrogen [fine structure](../../../../../fine-structure.md) comes from three order-$1/c^2$ contributions to the nonrelativistic electron [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md):

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm fs}=-\frac{p^4}{8m_e^3c^2}+\frac1{2m_e^2c^2r}\frac{dV}{dr}\,\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S+\frac{\hbar^2}{8m_e^2c^2}\nabla^2V,\qquad V=-\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0r}.}
$$

These are the [relativistic kinetic-energy correction](../../../../../relativistic-kinetic-energy-correction.md), [spin-orbit interaction](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) and [Darwin term](../../../../../darwin-term.md). The spin-orbit coefficient includes the [Thomas precession](../../../../../thomas-precession.md) factor of one half; $\langle\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S\rangle=\hbar^2[j(j+1)-l(l+1)-3/4]/2$. The Coulomb Darwin contribution is a positive contact term, relevant for S states. These corrections distinguish total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $j=l\pm1/2$, while leaving magnetic substates degenerate without an external field.

Writing $\alpha_{\rm fs}=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar c)$, the combined [hydrogen fine-structure correction](../../../../../hydrogen-fine-structure-correction.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta E_{nj}=-\frac{m_ec^2\alpha_{\rm fs}^4}{2n^3}\left[\frac1{j+1/2}-\frac3{4n}\right].}
$$

Thus the splitting is of order $\alpha_{\rm fs}^2$ times the principal binding energy. In the $n=2$ shell, $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ remain degenerate in this approximation, while $2P_{3/2}$ is higher by $m_ec^2\alpha_{\rm fs}^4/32$. The [Lamb shift](../../../../../lamb-shift.md) lifts the remaining S–P degeneracy; [hyperfine structure](../../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) is a separate nuclear-spin effect. Neither is part of the displayed correction. Nuclear recoil and finite nuclear size have also been neglected.
