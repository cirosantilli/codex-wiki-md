<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [coherent state](../../../../../coherent-state.md) satisfies $\langle a\rangle=\alpha$, $\langle a^\dagger\rangle=\alpha^*$ and $\langle a^\dagger a\rangle=|\alpha|^2$. The [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) adds the vacuum term $\langle aa^\dagger\rangle=|\alpha|^2+1$. Therefore

$$
\langle\hat E\rangle=E_0(\alpha+\alpha^*),\qquad
\langle\hat E^2\rangle=E_0^2[\alpha^2+(\alpha^*)^2+2|\alpha|^2+1],
$$

and the [coherent-state electric-field variance](../../../../../coherent-state-electric-field-variance.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\Delta E)^2=\langle\hat E^2\rangle-\langle\hat E\rangle^2=E_0^2,\qquad \Delta E=E_0.}
$$

Here $E_0$ is taken positive as the field-amplitude scale. The [coherent state](../../../../../coherent-state.md) displaces the mean field without changing this vacuum [optical quadrature](../../../../../optical-quadrature.md) noise.
