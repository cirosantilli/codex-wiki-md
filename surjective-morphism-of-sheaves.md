# Surjective morphism of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md)

A morphism of [sheaves of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md) is surjective when the induced map on every [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) is onto. Equivalently every target section can locally be lifted. It need not be onto on [global sections](global-section.md); the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) describes that obstruction.
