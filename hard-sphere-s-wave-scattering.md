# Hard-sphere S-wave scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Hard-sphere limit](hard-sphere-limit.md)

For a hard sphere of [radius](radius.md) $R$, the exterior radial [S wave](s-wave.md) is proportional to $\sin[k(r-R)]$. Matching it to $\sin(kr+\delta_0)$ gives [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md) $\delta_0=-kR$ modulo $\pi$ and [scattering length](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) $R$. The [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is $f_0=-e^{-ikR}\sin(kR)/k$, giving the displayed [cross section](cross-section-physics.md). The zero-energy limit is $4\pi R^2$, its zeros occur at positive integer multiples $kR=n\pi$, and its high-energy envelope decreases as $k^{-2}$. This is one [partial wave](partial-wave.md), not the total high-energy hard-sphere result obtained by summing all [partial waves](partial-wave.md).
