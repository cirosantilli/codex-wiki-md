<h1 id="lovasz-theorem-on-kneser-graphs">Lovász theorem on Kneser graphs</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kneser graph](kneser-graph.md)

For integers $n\geq2k\geq2$, the [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) of the [Kneser graph](kneser-graph.md) is $n-2k+2$. The upper bound colours by the least element among the first $n-2k+1$ elements, with one final colour for all other sets. The [Gale hemisphere lemma](gale-hemisphere-lemma.md) and the [Lusternik-Schnirelmann-Borsuk theorem](lusternik-schnirelmann-theorem.md) give the matching lower bound.
