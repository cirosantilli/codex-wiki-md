# Small-delay expansion of an epidemic transfer rate

↑ **Parent:** [Delay differential equation](delay-differential-equation.md)

For a smooth delayed-transfer epidemic model, the incidence $rI(t-\tau)S(t-\tau)$ equals $rIS-r\tau(\dot I S+I\dot S)+O(\tau^2)$. Substituting the zero-delay equations only inside the correction gives $rIS+\tau rIS\{a+r(I-S)\}+O(\tau^2)$. The correction has opposite signs in the receiving and departing [compartments](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md), preserving their transfer balance.
