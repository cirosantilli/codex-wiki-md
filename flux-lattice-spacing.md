# Flux-lattice spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Abrikosov vortex lattice](abrikosov-vortex-lattice.md)

Assume a triangular array of straight, singly quantized [Abrikosov vortices](abrikosov-vortex.md) with nearest-neighbor distance $a$. Here $\overline B>0$ is the magnitude of the **spatially averaged internal** [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), and $\Phi_0=h/(2e)$ is the [superconducting flux quantum](superconducting-flux-quantum.md); $e>0$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). Two lattice vectors of [length](length.md) $a$ meeting at $60^\circ$ span [area](surface-area.md) $A_c=\sqrt3a^2/2$. One flux quantum per cell gives

$$
\overline B A_c=\Phi_0,\qquad
n_v=\frac{\overline B}{\Phi_0},\qquad
a=\sqrt{\frac{2\Phi_0}{\sqrt3\,\overline B}}.
$$

Thus multiplying $\overline B$ by five decreases $a$ by $\sqrt5$; it does not multiply the flux of each vortex by five. Using the exact SI [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), $\Phi_0\simeq2.06783\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.[2022 CODATA table, exact Planck constant and elementary charge](fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)

For chosen internal fields $0.20\,\mathrm T$ and $1.0\,\mathrm T$, the spacings are approximately **$109\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $48.9\,\mathrm{nm}$**. These are geometric model calculations, not measurements from a particular sample. The local [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) varies within a cell; replacing $\overline B$ by the field at a core would not give this counting relation.
