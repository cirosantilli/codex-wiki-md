# Conditional norm covariance under a factor map

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional L2 norm](conditional-l2-norm.md)

For invertible probability systems linked by a [factor map](factor-map-between-measure-preserving-systems.md), invariance and uniqueness of disintegration identify the pushforward of each conditional measure with the conditional measure on the transformed fiber. The displayed norm identity follows for all integer times on a common full-measure set. It transports uniform conditional error bounds along whole orbits.
