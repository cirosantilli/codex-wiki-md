# Power object of a subobject

↑ **Parent:** [Power object](power-object.md)

For a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) $Y\hookrightarrow X$ in a [Cartesian category](cartesian-category.md), intersect the universal membership relation on $X\times PX$ with $Y\times PX$. The resulting [subobject](subobject.md) is classified by $r:PX\to PX$, and $r^2=r$. The [equalizer](equaliser.md) of $r$ and the identity represents precisely families of subobjects of $X$ contained in $Y$, hence is a [power object](power-object.md) for $Y$. This construction only requires the power object of $X$ and finite limits.
