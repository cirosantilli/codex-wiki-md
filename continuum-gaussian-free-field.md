# Continuum Gaussian free field

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian free field](gaussian-free-field.md)

The continuum Gaussian free field on a planar domain $D$ is a centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) indexed by finite-energy [measures](measure.md) or [test functions](test-function.md), with [covariance](covariance.md) given by the [Green function of the Laplacian](green-function-of-the-laplacian.md) on $D$.
