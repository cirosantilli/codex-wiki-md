# Resonant sine modes of a quadratic variational integral

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic functional](quadratic-functional.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $y''+ky=0$. For positive $k$ a nonzero stationary [function](function-split.md) exists exactly when $\sqrt k$ is a positive integer $n$, and all such [functions](function-split.md) are $B\sin(nx)$. These values are the [Dirichlet eigenvalues](dirichlet-eigenvalue.md) of $-d^2/dx^2$ on the interval. At $k=0$ the solutions are affine and the endpoint conditions leave only zero; negative $k$ likewise leaves only zero. Stationarity alone does not assert minimization of the [quadratic functional](quadratic-functional.md).
