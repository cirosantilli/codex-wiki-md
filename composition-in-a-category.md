# Composition in a category

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

Given [morphisms](morphism.md) $f:A\to B$ and $g:B\to C$, their composite is $gf:A\to C$. Composition is associative and has the [identity morphisms](identity-morphism.md) as units.
