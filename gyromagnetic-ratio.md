# Gyromagnetic ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyromagnetic_ratio)

The proportionality coefficient between a system's [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) when $\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma\mathbf J$ applies. In the angular convention, $\gamma$ is expressed in radians per second per [tesla](tesla-unit.md); $\gamma/(2\pi)$ is the corresponding coefficient in cycles per second per [tesla](tesla-unit.md). Its sign determines the relative orientations of the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
