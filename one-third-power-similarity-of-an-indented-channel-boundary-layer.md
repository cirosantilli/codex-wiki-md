# One-third-power similarity of an indented channel boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric viscous interaction in an indented Poiseuille channel](symmetric-viscous-interaction-in-an-indented-poiseuille-channel.md)

For indentation $F=bx^{1/3}$, the common layer displacement is $H=bx^{1/3}/2$. The [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) [stream function](stream-function.md) $\psi=x^{2/3}G(zx^{-1/3})$ gives [velocity](velocity.md) $U=x^{1/3}G'$ and [pressure](pressure.md) $P=\bar p x^{2/3}$. Substitution into the [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md) yields the displayed equation, with $G(0)=G'(0)=0$ and $G'\sim6\eta+3b$. This is a conditional similarity construction: the constant $\bar p$ must permit a solution of the [boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem.md).
