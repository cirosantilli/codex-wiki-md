# Lorentz-invariant dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md)

[Dynamical equations](dynamical-equation.md) whose solutions transform into solutions under a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md), holding fixed the theory's physical background data. A scalar matter equation constructed from the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) has this property; introducing a preferred timelike [vector field](vector-field.md) into the matter equation can destroy it.
