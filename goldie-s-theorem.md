<h1 id="goldie-s-theorem">Goldie's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Right Goldie ring](right-goldie-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldie's_theorem)

A [semiprime ring](semiprime-ring.md) has a [classical right ring of quotients](classical-right-ring-of-quotients.md) that is a [semisimple ring](semisimple-ring.md) and [Artinian](artinian-ring.md) exactly when it is a [right Goldie ring](right-goldie-ring.md). In particular, the theorem applies to a [semiprime ring](semiprime-ring.md) that is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md). It extends the fraction-field construction from commutative domains to rings whose quotient can be a finite product of matrix rings over division rings.
