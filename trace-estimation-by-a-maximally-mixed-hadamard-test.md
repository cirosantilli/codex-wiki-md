# Trace estimation by a maximally mixed Hadamard test

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed-state Hadamard-test quadratures](mixed-state-hadamard-test-quadratures.md)

Prepare the auxiliary register in the [maximally mixed state](maximally-mixed-state.md) $I/N$ and estimate both [mixed-state Hadamard-test quadratures](mixed-state-hadamard-test-quadratures.md). Multiplying the normalized expectation by $N$ estimates the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of $U$. A uniformly randomized preparation of any orthonormal basis produces $I/N$; the eigenvectors of $U$ need not be known.
