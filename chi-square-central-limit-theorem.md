# Chi-square central limit theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Residual sum of squares in a normal sample](residual-sum-of-squares-in-a-normal-sample.md)

If $Q_m\sim\chi_m^2$, then $(Q_m-m)/\sqrt{2m}$ converges in distribution to $N(0,1)$.
