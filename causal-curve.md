# Causal curve

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

A [causal curve](causal-curve.md) has an everywhere nonspacelike tangent of consistent time orientation; its smooth segments are timelike or null. More generally, curves with local [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) and future causal tangent almost everywhere are allowed. The [causal future](causal-future.md) and [causal past](causal-past.md) are defined by reachability along these curves.
