# Trace orthogonality of symmetric and antisymmetric matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md)

Let $S$ be a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) and $A$ a real [antisymmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) of the same size. Invariance of the [trace](matrix-trace.md) under [transpose](transpose.md) and the cyclic [trace](matrix-trace.md) identity give

$$
\operatorname{tr}(SA)=\operatorname{tr}((SA)^T)=\operatorname{tr}(A^TS^T)=-\operatorname{tr}(AS)=-\operatorname{tr}(SA).
$$

Hence $\operatorname{tr}(SA)=0$. Equivalently these two [matrices](matrix.md) are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) for the [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md), since $\langle S,A\rangle_F=\operatorname{tr}(S^TA)=\operatorname{tr}(SA)$. Together with the unique [symmetric and antisymmetric parts of a matrix](symmetric-and-antisymmetric-parts-of-a-matrix.md), this is an [orthogonal decomposition](orthogonal-decomposition-by-a-closed-subspace.md) of the real square [matrices](matrix.md).
