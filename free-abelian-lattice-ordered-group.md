# Free Abelian lattice-ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian lattice-ordered group](abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md)

The free object on generators $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ has the [universal property](universal-property.md) that an arbitrary assignment of these generators to an [Abelian lattice-ordered group](abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md) extends uniquely to a [lattice-ordered group homomorphism](lattice-ordered-group-homomorphism.md). It can be constructed from terms using addition, negation, zero, [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), modulo identities valid in every Abelian lattice-ordered group. Its underlying group need not be finitely generated even when there are only two lattice generators.
