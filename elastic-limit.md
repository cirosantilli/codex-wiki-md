# Elastic limit

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

The boundary of the loading range within which a material recovers its original configuration on unloading. [Elasticity](elasticity-physics-split.md) need not be linear; beyond the elastic range, permanent [plastic deformation](plastic-deformation.md) or damage may remain.
