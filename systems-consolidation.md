# Systems consolidation

↑ **Parent:** [Memory consolidation](memory-consolidation.md)

Time-dependent reorganization of the [neural circuits](neural-circuit.md) supporting retention and retrieval, often involving interactions between the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md) and [cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md). Whether a particular detailed episode becomes independent of the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md) is a further empirical question.
