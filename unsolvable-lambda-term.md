# Unsolvable lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Head normal form](head-normal-form.md)

An unsolvable [lambda term](lambda-term.md) has no [head normal form](head-normal-form.md), equivalently it is not a [solvable lambda term](solvable-lambda-term.md). [Capture-avoiding substitution](capture-avoiding-substitution.md) and [lambda application](lambda-application.md) preserve unsolvability: until a head variable appears, head contractions proceed within the original term and cannot inspect the supplied terms.
