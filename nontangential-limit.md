# Nontangential limit

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

A boundary limit taken while approaching within a cone that stays away from tangency. At zero in the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), every fixed cone $|\operatorname{Re}z|\leq A\operatorname{Im}z$ is allowed. A [nontangential limit](nontangential-limit.md) need not be a limit along all paths approaching the boundary.
