# Infinite disconnected subspace of a cofinite product

↑ **Parent:** [Connected subsets of a cofinite space](connected-subsets-of-a-cofinite-space.md)

For an infinite [set](set-split.md) $X$ with its [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md), every infinite [subset](subset.md) is [connected](connected-space.md), but $X\times X$ has an infinite disconnected [topological subspace](topological-subspace.md). Choose $a\ne b$ and use $D=\{a,b\}\times X$. The [open sets](open-set.md) $(X\setminus\{b\})\times X$ and $(X\setminus\{a\})\times X$ have disjoint nonempty traces $\{a\}\times X$ and $\{b\}\times X$ on $D$, covering it. Thus $D$ is a product of a discrete two-point subspace with an infinite cofinite factor. The ambient open rectangles may intersect; only their traces must be disjoint for a [separation](axiom-schema-of-specification.md) of $D$.
