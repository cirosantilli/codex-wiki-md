# Stabilizer octahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch ball](bloch-ball.md)

The [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the six [Pauli eigenstates](pauli-eigenstate.md) in the [Bloch ball](bloch-ball.md). A mixture has a [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) obeying the displayed inequality. Conversely, assign weights $|s_x|,|s_y|,|s_z|$ to the corresponding signed vertices and distribute the leftover weight over any opposite pair; this realizes every vector satisfying the inequality. Single-qubit [Clifford gates](clifford-gate.md) permute its vertices, while a [T gate](t-gate.md) can take a vertex to a pure state outside it.
