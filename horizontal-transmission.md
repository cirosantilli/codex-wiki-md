# Horizontal transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Epidemic threshold](epidemic-threshold.md)

Infection transferred between individuals in the same generation, often modeled by a [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md) term proportional to susceptible and infected populations. This contrasts with [vertical transmission](vertical-transmission.md) from parent to offspring.
