# Photonic band gap

↑ **Parent:** [Photonic band structure](photonic-band-structure.md)

A [frequency](frequency.md) interval without bulk propagating electromagnetic modes in the specified directions and polarizations. A complete gap excludes all directions and polarizations; a one-dimensional stop band need not do so.
