# Pentalene

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md)

A fused pair of five-membered [carbon](carbon.md) rings with formula $\mathrm{C_8H_6}$. Its neutral conjugated framework has eight pi [electrons](electron.md). Coordination and reduction alter its [electron](electron.md) distribution and allow several metal-binding [hapticities](hapticity.md).
