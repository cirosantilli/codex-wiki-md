# Ample cone

↑ **Parent:** [Nef cone](nef-cone.md)

The ample cone consists of [real numerical divisor classes](real-numerical-divisor-classes.md) represented by [ample real divisors](ample-real-divisor.md). It is an open [convex cone](convex-cone.md), and [Kleiman's criterion](kleiman-s-criterion.md) identifies it with the interior of the [nef cone](nef-cone.md).
