# Isotropy of nondegenerate ternary quadratic forms over finite fields

↑ **Parent:** [Ternary quadratic form](ternary-quadratic-form.md)

Over a [finite field](finite-field.md) of odd cardinality $q$, a diagonal nondegenerate [ternary quadratic form](ternary-quadratic-form.md) $A x^2+B y^2+C z^2$ has a zero with $z=1$: the sets $\{A x^2\}$ and $\{-C-B y^2\}$ both have $(q+1)/2$ elements and must intersect. This gives a nonzero nonsingular zero. Over a local integer ring with these coefficients units, the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts it to an isotropic vector in the fraction field.
