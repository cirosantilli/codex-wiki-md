# Oxygen-neon-magnesium core

↑ **Parent:** [Super-AGB star](super-agb-star.md)

An oxygen-neon-magnesium core is the degenerate product of carbon burning, composed mainly of oxygen and neon with a smaller magnesium abundance. It can become an oxygen-neon white dwarf or collapse after electron captures if it approaches the [Chandrasekhar mass](chandrasekhar-limit.md).
