# Fermi liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_liquid)

A Fermi liquid is an interacting fermion phase whose low-energy excitations are long-lived quasiparticles near a [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). Its effective interactions are classified by scaling only the frequency and momentum normal to that surface.
