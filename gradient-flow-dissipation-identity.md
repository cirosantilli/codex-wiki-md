# Gradient-flow dissipation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient flow](gradient-flow.md)

For the downward [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) $\dot x=-\nabla_gf$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $d f(x(t))/dt=-\|\nabla_gf(x(t))\|_g^2$. Integrating converts a bound on the decrease of $f$ into a bound on elapsed time wherever its [Riemannian gradient](riemannian-gradient.md) is bounded away from zero.
