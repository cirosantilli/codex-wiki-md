# Closed-set criterion for continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

A [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ between [topological spaces](topological-space.md) is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when the [inverse image](preimage.md) of every [closed set](closed-set.md) in $Y$ is [closed](closed-set.md) in $X$. The defining [open set](open-set.md) criterion is equivalent, because $f^{-1}(Y\setminus A)=X\setminus f^{-1}(A)$. This criterion concerns [inverse images](preimage.md); a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) need not be a [closed map](closed-map.md).
