# Thermal Langevin force normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md)

For $m\dot v_i=-\zeta v_i+\xi_i$, classical equilibrium [white noise](white-noise.md) satisfies $\langle\xi_i(t)\xi_j(t')\rangle=2\zeta k_BT\delta_{ij}\delta(t-t')$. The normalization produces the equipartition velocity variance $k_BT/m$.
