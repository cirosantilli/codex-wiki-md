# Ruby R-line shift

↑ **Parent:** [Ruby pressure scale](ruby-pressure-scale.md)

The [ruby R-line shift](ruby-r-line-shift.md) compares the same emission feature under two conditions. It is distinct from the [Stokes shift](stokes-shift.md), which compares absorption and emission features. In [ruby](ruby.md), [chromium](chromium.md) [ions](ion.md) in the [corundum](corundum.md) [crystal](crystal.md) provide the [electronic transitions](electronic-transition.md); compression changes their local environment and transition [energies](energy-split.md).[Introduction and hydrostatic-pressure discussion: chromium fluorescence in alumina and stress-sensitive R lines](stress-measurements-in-alumina-by-optical-fluorescence-revisited-by-robert-f-cook-and-chris-a-michaels.md)

For consistently defined [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelengths](wavelength.md), the reciprocal relation gives

$$
\Delta(1/\lambda)=\frac{1}{\lambda_0+\Delta\lambda}-\frac1{\lambda_0}
=-\frac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda_0(\lambda_0+\Delta\lambda)}.
$$

Thus a positive [wavelength](wavelength.md) shift means a negative reciprocal shift, and $E=hc/\lambda$ gives lower [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md); $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and $c$ the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). For [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md), use [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md) and keep that convention consistent. A calibration specified in air [wavelength](wavelength.md) must retain its convention or be converted consistently. The reciprocal relation is nonlinear: a line exactly linear in $\Delta\lambda$ is not exactly linear in $\Delta(1/\lambda)$.

For a purely mathematical check with chosen [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelengths](wavelength.md) $\lambda_0=700.0\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $\Delta\lambda=2.0\,\mathrm{nm}$, the reciprocal shift is $-40.7000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. The small-shift approximation $-\Delta\lambda/\lambda_0^2$ gives $-40.8163\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, about $0.286\%$ too large in magnitude. These chosen values illustrate the conversion; they are not measured [ruby](ruby.md) lines or an additional [pressure calibration](pressure-calibration.md).
