<h1 id="riemann-roch-theorem-for-algebraic-surfaces">Riemann–Roch theorem for algebraic surfaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic surface](algebraic-surface.md)

For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) on a smooth projective surface, the displayed formula computes its [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md). Combined with [Serre duality](serre-duality.md), it turns [intersection numbers](intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) into lower bounds for section spaces.
