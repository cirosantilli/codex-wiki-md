# Local field potential

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

An extracellular voltage reflecting weighted contributions from nearby and sometimes more distant neuronal currents. Its spatial sensitivity depends on anatomy, electrode placement and signal properties; it is not simply the firing rate of the nearest cell.
