# Hfq

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[Hfq](hfq.md) is a bacterial RNA-binding protein that can facilitate interactions between regulatory RNAs and target transcripts. Its contribution depends on the particular RNA pair and does not make every Hfq-dependent expression change a direct binding effect.
