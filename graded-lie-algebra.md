# Graded Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A [graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md) has a [graded Lie bracket](graded-lie-bracket.md) preserving total degree, with $[u,v]=-(-1)^{|u||v|}[v,u]$ and $[u,[v,w]]=[[u,v],w]+(-1)^{|u||v|}[v,[u,w]]$ for homogeneous vectors in the indicated degrees. A [Gerstenhaber algebra](gerstenhaber-algebra.md) uses this sign rule after shifting degrees by one.
