# Mie scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mie_scattering)

The electromagnetic scattering solution for a sphere describes the transition between [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md) and [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md). Grain-size-dependent efficiencies can produce structure in the [radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md).
