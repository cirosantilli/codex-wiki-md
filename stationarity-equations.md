# Stationarity equations

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md)

For a discrete-time [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) with [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) $P$, these equations, together with $\pi_j\geq0$ and $\sum_j\pi_j=1$, say that $\pi$ is a [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md). Incoming [probability](probability.md) mass equals the current mass at each state. They are global balance equations and do not require [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md).
