# Image vortex at a plane wall

↑ **Parent:** [Line vortex](line-vortex.md)

The [method of images](method-of-images.md) enforces impermeability at a plane wall by reflecting a [line vortex](line-vortex.md) across it and reversing its [circulation](circulation-physics.md). The two logarithmic [stream functions](stream-function.md) cancel on the wall, which is consequently a [streamline](streamline.md). For a vortex of [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$ at $(0,b)$ above $y=0$, the image of [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $-\Gamma$ at $(0,-b)$ induces velocity $(\Gamma/(4\pi b),0)$ at the real vortex. The signed drift parallel to the wall is therefore $\Gamma/(4\pi b)$.
