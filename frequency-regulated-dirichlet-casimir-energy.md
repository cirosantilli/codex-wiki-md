# Frequency-regulated Dirichlet Casimir energy

↑ **Parent:** [Casimir force](casimir-force.md)

For a massless real scalar in one spatial dimension, applying $e^{-\omega/(\pi\Lambda)}$ to both the Dirichlet mode sum and the unbounded-line integral gives the same leading bulk energy $\pi L\Lambda^2/2$. Their finite difference is $-\pi/(24L)$ in units $\hbar=c=1$, producing the attractive force $-\pi/(24L^2)$. This scalar boundary force is distinct from the retarded atom-surface Casimir-Polder interaction.
