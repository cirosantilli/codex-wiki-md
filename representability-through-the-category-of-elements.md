# Representability through the category of elements

↑ **Parent:** [Universal arrow from an object to a functor](universal-arrow-from-an-object-to-a-functor.md)

For $F:\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$, the comma category $(1\downarrow F)$ is the category of elements: an object is $(A,a)$ with $a\in F(A)$. Its initial objects are exactly the [representations](representation-of-a-functor.md) of $F$.
