# Thermosolutal convection

↑ **Parent:** [Double-diffusive convection](double-diffusive-convection.md)

[Thermosolutal convection](thermosolutal-convection.md) is [double-diffusive convection](double-diffusive-convection.md) driven by temperature and concentration gradients. Their buoyancy contributions can oppose one another, while unequal [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) and [solutal diffusivity](solutal-diffusivity.md) allow steady, oscillatory and finite-amplitude states. For a layer heated from below with a stabilizing concentration gradient, thermal buoyancy is destabilizing and solutal buoyancy restoring.
