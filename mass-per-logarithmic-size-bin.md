# Mass per logarithmic size bin

↑ **Parent:** [Power-law size distribution](power-law-size-distribution.md)

For equal-density spherical bodies with differential number distribution $n(D)\propto D^{-\alpha}$, a fixed logarithmic bin has width $dD=D\,d\log D$. Multiplying the body mass proportional to $D^3$ by $n(D)dD$ gives $m_{\log}\propto D^{4-\alpha}$. Distinguishing differential-number and logarithmic-mass slopes is essential in [collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md) plots.
