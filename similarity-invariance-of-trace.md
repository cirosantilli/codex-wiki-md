# Similarity invariance of trace

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

For an invertible [matrix](matrix.md) $S$, the [cyclic property of the trace](cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(SAS^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(AS^{-1}S)=\operatorname{tr}A$. Thus the diagonal sum defining the [trace](matrix-trace.md) does not depend on the choice of [basis](basis.md) for an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md). Cyclic permutation of factors is valid inside a [trace](matrix-trace.md); arbitrary permutation of factors need not be.
