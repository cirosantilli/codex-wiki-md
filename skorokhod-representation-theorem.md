# Skorokhod representation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md)

For [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) of random elements in a separable complete [metric](metric.md) space, one can construct copies of the sequence and its limit on one probability space which converge almost surely. The copies preserve the individual laws; they need not preserve the original dependence between sequence elements. Suitable more general versions permit a separably supported limit in a [metric](metric.md) space.
