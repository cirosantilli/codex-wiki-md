# Cartesian formula for the angular Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

Writing $D_r=\mathbf x\cdot\nabla=r\partial_r$, the spherical-coordinate [Laplacian](laplacian.md) gives $r^2\Delta=D_r^2+D_r+\Delta_{S^2}$. Thus the positive angular [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) has the displayed Cartesian expression and [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $l(l+1)$ on [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). Omitting $D_r$ is incorrect when the square means composition: on $x_1$, the truncated expression gives $x_1$, whereas the degree-one angular [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is two.
