# Quantum-confined Stark effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum well](quantum-well.md)

An electric field normal to a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) [quantum well](quantum-well.md) shifts the [electron](electron.md) and [hole](electron-hole.md) confinement energies and displaces their envelopes in opposite directions. The [exciton](exciton.md) optical transition usually redshifts and its oscillator strength falls as their overlap decreases. In a symmetric unbiased well the permanent dipole $p_0$ vanishes, so the leading weak-field shift is quadratic. This optical shift includes confinement and binding changes; it is not solely a change in [exciton](exciton.md) binding [energy](energy-split.md).
