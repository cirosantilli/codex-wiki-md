# Anti addition

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

An [anti addition](anti-addition.md) places the two newly attached groups on opposite faces of an unsaturated unit. Backside opening of a cyclic [bromonium ion](bromonium-ion.md) is a standard example.
