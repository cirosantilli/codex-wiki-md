# Three-body threshold phase space

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md)

For three massive daughters with a smooth nonzero [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) at the [decay threshold](decay-threshold.md), the integrated [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) vanishes as $Q^2$, where $Q$ is the parent's [mass](mass.md) minus the sum of daughter [masses](mass.md). The spatial [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) leaves six independent [momentum](momentum-split.md) coordinates. Rescaling each by $Q^{1/2}$ contributes $Q^3$, while the [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) contributes $Q^{-1}$. The factors $1/(2E)$ are smooth nonzero constants at the [decay threshold](decay-threshold.md). Thus the [decay width](decay-width.md) also scales as $Q^2$ when the [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) has a nonzero constant term. A vanishing [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md), a massless daughter, or a threshold singularity can change this law. In three spatial dimensions the same argument gives $Q^{(3N-5)/2}$ for $N$ massive daughters.
