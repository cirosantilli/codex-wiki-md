# Counterbalanced gyroscope precession

↑ **Parent:** [Slow steady gyroscope precession](slow-steady-gyroscope-precession.md)

Let a horizontal [gyroscope](gyroscope.md) have signed spin [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L_x$ along the axle. The disk's [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is at $+l\widehat{\mathbf x}$ and a counterweight is at $-l_c\widehat{\mathbf x}$. Gravity supplies [torque](torque.md) $g(ml-m_cl_c)\widehat{\mathbf y}$. Since $\boldsymbol\tau=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf L$, steady horizontal [precession](precession.md) gives the displayed signed vertical [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). Neglect the axle's [mass](mass.md) and the counterweight's axial spin [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). The zero-torque choice $m_cl_c=ml$ allows the axle to remain fixed. Moving the counterweight through this position reverses the gravitational [torque](torque.md) and the associated slow [precession](precession.md).
