# Futures pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Futures contract](futures-contract.md)

With continuous cash settlement and a money-market [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md), discounted futures gains have zero drift; under the necessary true-[martingale](martingale-split.md) [integrability](integrability.md), the futures quote is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) ending at the terminal spot price. The [forward contract](forward-contract.md) discount-weighted [expectation](expected-value.md) is a different valuation expression.
