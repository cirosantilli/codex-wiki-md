# Moment equations of a linear birth-death process

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death master equation](birth-death-master-equation.md)

For per-capita birth and death rates $B$ and $D$, the rates are $b_n=Bn$ and $d_n=Dn$. If $\mu=\mathbb EN_t$ and $\sigma^2=\operatorname{var}N_t$, then

$$
\mu'=(B-D)\mu,
\qquad
(\sigma^2)'=2(B-D)\sigma^2+(B+D)\mu.
$$

The [expected value](expected-value.md) sees only the net growth rate $B-D$, whereas the [variance](variance-split.md) also sees the total event rate $B+D$.
