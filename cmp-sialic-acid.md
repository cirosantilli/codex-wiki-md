# CMP-sialic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide sugar](nucleotide-sugar.md)

[CMP-sialic acid](cmp-sialic-acid.md) is an activated donor containing a sialic acid residue linked to CMP. Transfer requires an appropriate [glycosyltransferase](glycosyltransferase.md) and acceptor; access to the correct compartment can be limiting.
