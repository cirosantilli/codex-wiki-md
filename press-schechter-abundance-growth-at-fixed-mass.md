# Press-Schechter abundance growth at fixed mass

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md)

For a fixed narrow mass bin with time-independent smoothing slope, the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) has time dependence $n\propto\nu e^{-\nu^2/2}$, with [halo peak height](halo-peak-height.md) $\nu\propto D^{-1}$. Differentiating gives the displayed rate. It is positive only for $\nu>1$; the bin can lose objects to higher masses later. Cumulative counts require integrating the differential abundance, and a moving mass boundary adds a selection term.
