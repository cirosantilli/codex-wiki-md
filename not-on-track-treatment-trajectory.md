# Not-on-track treatment trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Outcome feedback](outcome-feedback.md)

A trajectory classified by a specified rule as unlikely to reach an expected outcome. An alert should prompt clinical review; its validity, false alarms and feasible responses require evaluation.
