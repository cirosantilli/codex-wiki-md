# Service-capacity allocation in an open migration process

↑ **Parent:** [Open migration process](open-migration-process.md)

For positive traffic rates in single-server colonies, a total service budget $F>\sum_j\alpha_j$ minimizes the stationary mean population when the spare rates $\phi_j-\alpha_j$ are proportional to $\sqrt{\alpha_j}$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) proves the optimum directly.
