# Noether gauging procedure

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge theory](gauge-theory.md)

Localizing a parameter of a global [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) produces its [Noether current](noether-current.md) times a derivative of that parameter. Introduce a [gauge field](gauge-field.md) with an inhomogeneous transformation and couple it to the [Noether current](noether-current.md) to cancel this variation. Recompute the remaining variation and add the required interaction and transformation terms. Closure of the local [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) algebra constrains the completion. For [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), its [supercurrent](supercurrent.md) couples to the [gravitino](gravitino.md), and the translation in the [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) algebra requires a [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md).
