# Pure-investment arbitrage

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption arbitrage](investment-consumption-arbitrage.md)

A pure-investment [arbitrage](arbitrage.md) has zero initial cost, no intervening [consumption](consumption.md), and a nonnegative terminal payoff that is strictly positive with positive [probability](probability.md).
