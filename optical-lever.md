# Optical lever

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md)

An angular readout using a reflected [light ray](light-ray.md): rotating a [mirror](mirror.md) by $\theta$ rotates the reflected [light ray](light-ray.md) by $2\theta$, giving spot [displacement](displacement.md) $x\simeq2D\theta$ at distance $D$ for small [angles](angle.md).
