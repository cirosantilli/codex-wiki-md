# Constant-head supply for a gravity-driven thin film

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-volume gravity-driven thin-film current](finite-volume-gravity-driven-thin-film-current.md)

The scaling $x\sim Ch^2t$ and $V\sim hx$ gives $V\sim Ch^3t$. Maintaining an asymptotically constant front height therefore requires constant volume flux of order $Ch^3$.
