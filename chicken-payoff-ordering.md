# Chicken payoff ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Hawk-Dove game](hawk-dove-game.md)

The [Chicken payoff ordering](chicken-payoff-ordering.md) describes a symmetric two-player [bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md) in which aggression earns temptation $T$ against yielding, mutual yielding earns $R$, yielding to aggression earns $S$, and mutual aggression earns $P$, with the displayed strict ordering. Aggression is a [best response](best-response.md) to yielding, and yielding is a [best response](best-response.md) to aggression. Thus the two asymmetric action pairs are [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) [Nash equilibria](nash-equilibrium.md). The [Hawk-Dove game](hawk-dove-game.md) has this ordering in its positive-resource regime where the damage cost exceeds the resource value. In the [prisoner's dilemma](prisoner-s-dilemma.md), the ordering of $S$ and $P$ is reversed, making defection a [strictly dominant strategy](strictly-dominant-strategy.md) instead.
