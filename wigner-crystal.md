# Wigner crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Electron gas](electron-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner_crystal)

A Wigner crystal is an ordered arrangement of electrons stabilized by their mutual repulsion when localization costs sufficiently little kinetic or thermal energy. In a degenerate low-density gas, the [Coulomb-to-kinetic energy ratio of an electron gas](coulomb-to-kinetic-energy-ratio-of-an-electron-gas.md) increases with mean spacing. Classical electrons can also order at large Coulomb-to-thermal coupling, as in [electron crystallization on liquid helium](electron-crystallization-on-liquid-helium.md). A neutralizing background or confining environment is needed to discuss a stable electron assembly. Dimension, screening and disorder affect the transition.
