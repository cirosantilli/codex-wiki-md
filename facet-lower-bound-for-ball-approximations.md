# Facet lower bound for ball approximations

↑ **Parent:** [Facet](facet.md)

If $r\geq\sqrt2$ and a [convex polytope](convex-polytope.md) satisfies $r^{-1}B^n\subseteq P\subseteq B^n$, then it has at least $(1-r^{-2})^{-n/2}\geq e^{n/(2r^2)}$ [facets](facet.md). Its outward facet normals define [spherical caps](spherical-cap.md) covering the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md); the [spherical cap area upper bound](spherical-cap-area-upper-bound.md) supplies the estimate.
