# Matching in effective field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

[Matching in effective field theory](matching-in-effective-field-theory.md) fixes [Wilson coefficients](wilson-coefficient.md) by requiring a low-energy theory and its [ultraviolet completion](ultraviolet-completion.md) to give the same low-energy amplitudes through a specified order. Both calculations must treat shared infrared physics consistently. Contributions from retained light particles are reproduced inside the [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md); their common nonanalytic parts are not replaced by arbitrary local heavy coefficients.
