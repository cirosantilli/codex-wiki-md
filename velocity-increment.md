# Velocity increment

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal velocity structure function](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md)

A velocity increment is the difference of a [velocity field](velocity-field.md) between two positions. Its longitudinal component is the projection onto their separation direction. Moments of increments define [longitudinal structure functions](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md); the [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md) estimates a typical inertial-range magnitude as $(\epsilon r)^{1/3}$.
