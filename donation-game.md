# Donation game

↑ **Parent:** [Prisoner's dilemma](prisoner-s-dilemma.md)

A social game in which [cooperation](cooperation.md) costs its actor $c$ and benefits its partner by $b$, with $b>c>0$. Mutual [cooperation](cooperation.md) yields $b-c$ to each, unilateral defection yields $b$ to the defector and $-c$ to the cooperator, and mutual defection yields zero.
