# Lipid vesicle

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A lipid vesicle is a closed [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) enclosing fluid. Its shape and [thermal membrane undulations](thermal-membrane-undulation.md) depend on [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md), [membrane bending modulus](membrane-bending-modulus.md), and constraints such as fixed enclosed volume.
