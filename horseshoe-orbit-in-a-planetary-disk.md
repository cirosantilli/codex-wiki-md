# Horseshoe orbit in a planetary disk

↑ **Parent:** [Corotation torque](corotation-torque.md)

A disk-[gas](gas.md) trajectory that turns from one side of a [planet](planet.md)'s orbital [radius](radius.md) to the other in the corotating frame. Its angular-momentum exchange contributes to the [corotation torque](corotation-torque.md); repeated libration without restoring gradients can saturate that contribution.
