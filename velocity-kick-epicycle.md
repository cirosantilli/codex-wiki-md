# Velocity-kick epicycle

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

A small planar [velocity](velocity.md) kick on a stellar [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) produces a local [epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md) in the source's [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md). With outward $x$, forward $y$, and initial displacements zero, [Hill equations](hill-equations.md) give $x/(\gamma a)=2\cos\theta(1-\cos nt)+\sin\theta\sin nt$ and $y/(\gamma a)=\cos\theta(4\sin nt-3nt)+2\sin\theta(\cos nt-1)$. Tangential kicks combine an epicycle with [Keplerian shear](keplerian-shear.md); a radial kick gives a closed leading-order [epicyclic ellipse](epicyclic-ellipse.md).
