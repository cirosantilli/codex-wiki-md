# Riccati cross-ratio superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md)

Three distinct particular solutions $g,h,j$ of a [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md) determine every solution by one constant [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md). Indeed, for $f'=af^2+bf+c$, differences satisfy $(f-g)'=(a(f+g)+b)(f-g)$. The logarithmic derivatives cancel in the displayed cross-ratio, so it is constant. Solving for $f$ gives a [rational function](rational-function.md) of the three solutions and $K$, interpreted on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) at poles.
