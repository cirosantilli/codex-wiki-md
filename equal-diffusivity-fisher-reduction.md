# Equal-diffusivity Fisher reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher's equation](fisher-s-equation.md)

In $u_t=u(1-u-\beta v)+u_{xx}$, $v_t=-\delta uv+v_{xx}$, the line $v=1-u$ is invariant when $\beta=1-\delta$. On it $u$ satisfies [Fisher's equation](fisher-s-equation.md) with growth coefficient $\delta$. The reduction also requires compatible initial and boundary data: parameter tuning alone does not reduce arbitrary two-field solutions. Positive [concentrations](concentration.md) and positive $\beta$ in this normalization require $0<\delta<1$.
