# Log-cosh energy on a Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

The inequalities $0\le\log\cosh t\le t^2/2$ make this energy finite on $H^1$. Since the second [derivative](derivative.md) of its [scalar](scalar.md) potential is $\operatorname{sech}^2t\in(0,1]$, its [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md) is $DI(v)[h]=\int(\nabla v\cdot\nabla h+\tanh(v)h)$, with [Taylor remainder](taylor-remainder.md) between zero and half the squared $H^1$ [norm](norm.md) of $h$. The [derivative](derivative.md) operator is strictly monotone because the [hyperbolic tangent](hyperbolic-tangent.md) is strictly increasing.
