# Destructive interference

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

[Destructive interference](destructive-interference.md) occurs when coherent contributions partially or completely cancel their [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md). For two equal contributions, a [phase difference](phase-difference.md) of $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$ gives zero resultant [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md). The missing local [light intensity](light-intensity.md) is consistent with [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) because the full spatial [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) pattern redistributes [power](power.md).
