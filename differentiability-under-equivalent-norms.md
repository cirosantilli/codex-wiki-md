# Differentiability under equivalent norms

↑ **Parent:** [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md)

Suppose $f(a+h)=f(a)+Lh+r(h)$ and $|r(h)|/\|h\|_1\to0$. If $c\|h\|_1\le\|h\|_2\le C\|h\|_1$, then $|r(h)|/\|h\|_2\le c^{-1}|r(h)|/\|h\|_1\to0$. The boundedness of $L$ is also preserved, and the same argument in reverse proves equivalence of differentiability. The [derivative](derivative.md) is the same [linear map](linear-map.md), not a [norm](norm.md)-dependent replacement.
