# Oceanic thermal subsidence

↑ **Parent:** [Half-space cooling model](half-space-cooling-model.md)

Cooling oceanic [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) contracts and increases its column [mass](mass.md), causing isostatic deepening. Integrating a half-space cooling profile gives an excess [mass](mass.md) proportional to $\sqrt t$. Converting this to seafloor depth includes the [density contrast](density-contrast.md) between [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) and seawater. Finite-plate cooling and basal [heat](heat-split.md) supply limit indefinite square-root subsidence at old ages.
