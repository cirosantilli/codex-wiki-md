# Schur algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial representation of the general linear group](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schur_algebra)

The Schur algebra is the [commutant](centralizer.md) of place permutations on a [tensor power](tensor-power.md). It is the linear span of the diagonal [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) action, by [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md). [Modules](module-mathematics.md) over $S(m,n)$ correspond to homogeneous degree-$n$ [polynomial representations](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md). Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) it is a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md): decompose the [tensor power](tensor-power.md) as a [module](module-mathematics.md) for the semisimple [group algebra](group-algebra.md) $\mathbb CS_n$, and take its endomorphism algebra.
