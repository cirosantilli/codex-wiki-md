# Golgi lumen

↑ **Parent:** [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md)

The [Golgi lumen](golgi-lumen.md) is the enclosed interior of a [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md), distinct from the surrounding [cytosol](cytosol.md).

The membrane-sidedness rule in [secretory protein routing](secretory-protein-routing.md) still applies within the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md). For a local worked example, stipulate a [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md) with a [carbohydrate](carbohydrate.md) chain exposed to [Golgi lumen](golgi-lumen.md) and a tail exposed to [cytosol](cytosol.md). An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) confined to [Golgi lumen](golgi-lumen.md) can contact that chain, but cannot directly contact the cytosolic tail without a boundary-crossing step. A change in which [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md) contains the [protein](protein-split.md) need not turn the [protein](protein-split.md) over. Thus chemical accessibility and cargo location are different questions: moving cargo onward preserves its membrane sides under the budding and fusion operations described in [secretory protein routing](secretory-protein-routing.md).
