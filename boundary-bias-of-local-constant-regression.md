# Boundary bias of local constant regression

↑ **Parent:** [Nadaraya–Watson estimator](nadaraya-watson-estimator.md)

A local constant average reproduces constants but need not reproduce a linear [regression function](regression-function.md) near an endpoint. For $x_i=i/n$, $K=\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}$, $m(x)=x$, and $nh\to\infty$, its deterministic estimate at zero is $(\lfloor nh\rfloor+1)/(2n)\sim h/2$. A local linear fit removes this first-order boundary [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md).
