# Full well capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

The charge that an integrating detector well can hold within its stated operating specification before saturation or overflow compromises the response. It differs from the digitizer limit and from the largest signal that is accurately linear. In a [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md), excess charge can cause blooming into adjacent pixels.
