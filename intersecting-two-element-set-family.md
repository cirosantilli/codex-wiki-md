# Intersecting two-element set family

↑ **Parent:** [Intersecting family](intersecting-family.md)

A pairwise [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md) of two-element sets is contained in a [star graph](star-graph-theory.md) or in the three [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) of a [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). If two members are $\{a,b\},\{a,c\}$ and a third avoids $a$, the third must be $\{b,c\}$. Every member meeting all three belongs to that triangle.
