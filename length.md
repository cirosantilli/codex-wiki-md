# Length

↑ **Parent:** [Physical quantity](physical-quantity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length)

Length measures spatial extent or separation. It is a base [physical dimension](physical-dimension.md) in [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md), distinguished from the choice of units used to express a particular value.
