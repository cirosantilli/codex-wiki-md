# Tuned spark wireless apparatus

↑ **Parent:** [Wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md)

[Guglielmo Marconi](guglielmo-marconi.md)'s 1909 lecture describes [antennas](antenna-radio.md) with earth connections and separate coupled receiving circuits. His 1900 system coupled a closed [capacitor](capacitor.md)–[inductor](inductor.md)–[spark gap](spark-gap.md) loop to a transmitting [antenna](antenna-radio.md). Coil spacing adjusted coupling; [inductance](inductance.md) and [capacitance](capacitance.md) adjusted tuning. All four circuits were tuned together. By 1909 his company's stations mainly used magnetic receivers, rather than [coherers](coherer.md).[1909 lecture, Figs. 2–3, 6 and 8 and associated paragraphs; receiver paragraph at Fig. 25](wireless-telegraphic-communication-by-guglielmo-marconi.md)

Those are a participant's retrospective descriptions, not a complete account of priority. An earth connection is part of this [antenna](antenna-radio.md) arrangement; it is not a universal requirement for [radio communication](radio-communication.md). Nor does tuning an isolated [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md) ensure that its [frequency](frequency.md) remains unchanged when strongly coupled to another circuit. The [coupled resonant circuit model](coupled-resonant-circuit-model.md) quantifies that distinction.
