# From renormalizability to electroweak predictions

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization of spontaneously broken gauge theories](renormalization-of-spontaneously-broken-gauge-theories.md)

The 1999 recognition of [Gerard 't Hooft](gerardus-t-hooft.md) and [Martinus J. G. Veltman](martinus-justinus-godefridus-veltman.md) concerned the theoretical quantum structure of the [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md).[Nobel Prize paragraph](gerard-t-hooft-homepage-by-gerardus-t-hooft.md) [Renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) makes repeated [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) predictive from a fixed set of inputs. It does not imply that the [perturbation series](perturbation-series.md) converges, nor that [renormalization](renormalization.md) alone chooses the correct [particle](particle-physics.md) content or excludes all physics beyond the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md).

The [Gargamelle](gargamelle.md) collaboration prepared an experimental test by exposing its heavy-liquid [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) to [neutrino](neutrino.md) and [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) beams at CERN. The readout selected events producing [hadrons](hadron.md) without an identified [muon](muon.md) or [electron](electron.md), compared with events containing a charged [lepton](lepton.md). Its 1974 report assessed alternative origins from [neutrons](neutron-split.md) and long-lived neutral [kaons](kaon.md), finding them insufficient to explain the sample. That supported [neutral currents](neutral-current.md); it did not isolate a [radiative correction](radiative-correction.md) or demonstrate [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md).[Abstract, Nuclear Physics B 73 (1974), pp. 1–22](observation-of-neutrino-like-interactions-without-muon-or-electron-in-the-gargamelle-neutrino-experiment-1974-by-f-j-hasert.md)

The distinction matters when using the [tree-level electroweak gauge-boson masses](tree-level-electroweak-gauge-boson-masses.md):

$$
m_W=m_Z\cos\theta_W.
$$

Beyond tree level, [self-energies](self-energy.md), [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md) and [counterterms](counterterm.md) alter how chosen input [observables](observable.md) predict another [observable](observable.md). The [Weinberg angle](weinberg-angle.md) then needs a specified [renormalization scheme](renormalization-scheme.md); inserting its value from one convention into a formula from another can create an apparent discrepancy. A meaningful higher-order test must state both its independent experimental inputs and the calculated [loop order](loop-order.md).
