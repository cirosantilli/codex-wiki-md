# Schur complement presentation of a theta singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann singularity theorem](riemann-singularity-theorem.md)

For a square evaluation matrix representing a degree-$g-1$ [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$, remove an invertible minor of size $N-h^0(L)$. [Holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) row and column operations leave a square matrix $M$ of size $r=h^0(L)$ with zero constant term. Its linear part in a tangent direction $\xi\in H^1(\mathcal O_X)$ is $\xi\smile-:H^0(L)\to H^1(L)$. The [invertible cup-product direction for a line bundle on a curve](invertible-cup-product-direction-for-a-line-bundle-on-a-curve.md) makes the leading degree-$r$ [determinant](determinant.md) nonzero, establishing order exactly $r$ rather than just a lower bound.
