# Conditional scale density in a curved Gaussian family

↑ **Parent:** [Curved exponential family](curved-exponential-family.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) $N(\mu,\mu^2)$ observations with $\mu>0$, the [sufficient statistics](sufficient-statistic.md) are their sum and squared norm. Condition on $a=\sqrt n\,\|y\|/\sum_i y_i>0$ and write $v=\widehat\mu=\|y\|/(q\sqrt n)$, where $q=(\sqrt{1+4a^2}+1)/(2a)$. [Polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) supply radial measure $\|y\|^{n-1}d\|y\|$, while the conditional angular factor does not depend on $\mu$. This proves the displayed shape. Its normalization depends on $a,n$ but not $\mu$ after the substitution $v=\mu z$, and agrees with the normalized [P-star approximation](p-star-approximation.md).
