# Regular hyperbolic polygon with prescribed area

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic polygon area](hyperbolic-polygon-area.md)

In curvature $-1$, a regular $n$-gon of circumradius $R>0$ has interior angle $\alpha(R)=2\arctan(\cot(\pi/n)/\cosh R)$, by the right-triangle angle form of the [hyperbolic law of cosines](hyperbolic-law-of-cosines.md). Thus its [hyperbolic polygon area](hyperbolic-polygon-area.md) is $(n-2)\pi-n\alpha(R)$, continuously and strictly increasing from $0$ to $(n-2)\pi$. Every area in that open interval is therefore attained at exactly one circumradius.
