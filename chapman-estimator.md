# Chapman estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-population capture-recapture model](closed-population-capture-recapture-model.md)

The [Chapman estimator](chapman-estimator.md) is a correction intended to reduce the small-sample [bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md) of the [Lincoln-Petersen estimator](lincoln-petersen-estimator.md). Under the fixed-size [hypergeometric](hypergeometric-distribution.md) sampling assumptions it is exactly an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) when $M+C\geq N$; it can have nonzero [bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md) otherwise. This qualification and the formula are stated in [Barker, §1.1.1, equation (1.3), citing Chapman's work through Seber](https://www.maths.otago.ac.nz/~rbarker/Thesis/thesis.pdf). For the toy observations,

$$
\widehat N_{\mathrm{Ch}}=\frac{(20+1)(25+1)}{5+1}-1
=\frac{546}{6}-1=90.
$$

It is an alternative [estimator](estimator.md), not a [maximum-likelihood estimate](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) for this [capture-recapture model](capture-recapture-model.md). The [Chapman estimator](chapman-estimator.md) remains finite when $R=0$, but a finite output does not supply the missing overlap information or repair unequal catchability, lost marks, or population change.
