# Model of ZFC without weakly inaccessible cardinals

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly inaccessible cardinal](weakly-inaccessible-cardinal.md)

Consistency of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) implies consistency of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) with the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) and no [weakly inaccessible cardinals](weakly-inaccessible-cardinal.md). Pass to the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md). If it has an inaccessible, cut at its least one; that rank segment still models [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) and the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), but has no inaccessibles. In this model the [singular cardinal enumeration](singular-cardinal-enumeration.md) is continuous at every nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) index. This is a relative-consistency construction, not a deduction of a [transitive model](transitive-model.md) from bare consistency.
