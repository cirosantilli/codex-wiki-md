# Factorial proof of Mertens first theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md)

The [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) of $N!$ gives $\log N!=\sum_{p^j\le N}\lfloor N/p^j\rfloor\log p$. The [Chebyshev estimate](chebyshev-estimate.md) bounds the error on replacing each floor by its argument by $O(N)$. Dividing by $N$ and using the [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md) gives $\sum_{p^j\le N}(\log p)/p^j=\log N+O(1)$. The contribution of $j\ge2$ is bounded by the convergent series $\sum_{n\ge2}(\log n)/(n(n-1))$. This proves the [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md) without the [Prime number theorem](prime-number-theorem.md).
