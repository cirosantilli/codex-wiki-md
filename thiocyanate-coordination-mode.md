# Thiocyanate coordination mode

↑ **Parent:** [Thiocyanate](thiocyanate.md)

[thiocyanate](thiocyanate.md) can coordinate through [nitrogen](nitrogen.md), [sulfur](sulfur.md), or both ends, and can bridge several [metal](metal.md) centres. A linkage label specifies donor atoms, whereas the bridging index $\mu_n$ specifies the number of connected [metals](metal.md). A short C–N bond and long C–S bond favour an $\mathrm{N\equiv C-S^-}$ description; greater C–S and smaller C–N [bond orders](bond-order.md) give more $\mathrm{^-N=C=S}$ character. Internal distances alone need not determine how many [metals](metal.md) are attached.
