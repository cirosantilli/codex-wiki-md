# Bifurcate Killing horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md)

A bifurcate Killing horizon consists of two intersecting null [Killing horizons](killing-horizon.md) of the same [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md), which vanishes on their spacelike intersection, the [bifurcation surface](bifurcation-surface.md). In regular Kruskal coordinates the horizons are $U=0$ and $V=0$, and their intersection is $U=V=0$. The displayed field is tangent and normal to each horizon branch and zero on the intersection.
