# Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

Examination paper of the [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md), Preliminary Examination in Computer Science, Trinity Term 2015, dated 19 June 2015. The original has seven pages; the last contains the prescribed [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) rules. Released archive: [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2015-cs4.pdf`. The paper's printed section order is [linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md), [digital electronics](digital-electronics.md), and introduction to [formal proof](formal-proof.md), despite the different ordering in its cover title.
