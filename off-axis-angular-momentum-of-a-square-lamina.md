# Off-axis angular momentum of a square lamina

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia of a uniform square plate](moment-of-inertia-of-a-uniform-square-plate.md)

For a uniform square of side $a$, the principal [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(ma^2/12,ma^2/12,ma^2/6)$. A rotation axis tilted $45^\circ$ to the surface has equal in-plane and normal angular-velocity components. Multiplying each by its own principal moment gives the stated magnitude. In general $I_{\rm axis}\omega$ is only the axial projection of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
