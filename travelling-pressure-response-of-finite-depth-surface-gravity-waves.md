# Travelling-pressure response of finite-depth surface gravity waves

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

For an [inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md) of constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ and depth $h$, impose excess [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_1\cos(k(x-ct))$ with $k>0$ on a [free surface](free-surface.md). Neglect [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md). A travelling solution of the [linearized free-surface boundary conditions](linearized-free-surface-boundary-conditions.md) is

$$
\eta=A\cos(k(x-ct)),\qquad
\phi=\frac{Ac}{\sinh(kh)}\cosh(k(y+h))\sin(k(x-ct)).
$$

The [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) satisfies the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) and the bed [no-penetration boundary condition](no-penetration-boundary-condition.md). Its surface [time derivative](time-derivative.md) is $-Ac^2k\coth(kh)\cos(k(x-ct))$, so the dynamic condition gives the displayed signed response. The nonnegative [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is $|A|$. It is finite except at

$$
c_*^2=\frac{g\tanh(kh)}k,
$$

the squared [phase speed](phase-speed.md) of a free [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) at that [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). Equality produces [resonance](resonance.md). This describes a travelling forced component; free [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md) must be added to meet general [initial conditions](initial-condition.md). At exact [resonance](resonance.md), a solution of the [initial value problem](initial-value-problem.md) generally grows with time rather than having a bounded periodic [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md).
