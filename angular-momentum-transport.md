# Angular momentum transport

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)

Angular momentum transport moves angular momentum between locations through matter, stresses, waves, or fields. An [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) must transport angular momentum outward for most of its mass to move inward.
