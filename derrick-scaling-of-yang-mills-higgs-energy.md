# Derrick scaling of Yang-Mills-Higgs energy

↑ **Parent:** [Derrick scaling](derrick-scaling.md)

In three space dimensions, rescale $A_i(x)$ to $\lambda A_i(\lambda x)$ and $\Phi(x)$ to $\Phi(\lambda x)$. The [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) then scales with two derivatives, and the [adjoint covariant derivative](adjoint-covariant-derivative.md) of the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) with one. Magnetic, Higgs-gradient and potential energies give the displayed formula. Stationarity requires $V_B=V_D+3V_U$, which permits nonzero energies. In the [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md) limit $V_U=0$, the [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md) give $V_B=V_D$.
