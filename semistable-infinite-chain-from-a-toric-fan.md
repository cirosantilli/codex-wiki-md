# Semistable infinite chain from a toric fan

↑ **Parent:** [Toric scheme over a base ring](toric-scheme-over-a-base-ring.md)

Take rays $(i,1)$ and cones generated by consecutive rays in $\mathbb Z^2$. Their dual generators $(1,-i)$ and $(-1,i+1)$ give $x_i=tq^{-i}$, $y_i=t^{-1}q^{i+1}$. Adjacent charts glue by $x_{i+1}=y_i^{-1}$, $y_{i+1}=x_i y_i^2$. The fibre $q=0$ is a chain of [projective lines](projective-line.md): each component has two affine-line charts joined by reciprocal coordinates, and consecutive components meet at one node. Inverting $q$ identifies all charts with the [multiplicative group scheme](multiplicative-group-scheme.md) over $\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$. The local node algebra is free over $\mathbb Z[q]$, with [basis](basis.md) $1,x_i^a,y_i^b$ for $a,b\geq1$, so the morphism is flat.
