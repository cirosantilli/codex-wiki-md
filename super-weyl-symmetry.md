# Super-Weyl symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md)

A local fermionic gauge symmetry shifting the gamma-trace part of the [worldsheet gravitino](worldsheet-gravitino.md), schematically $\delta\chi_\mu=\gamma_\mu\rho$. Together with local [worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) it permits the gravitino to be set to zero locally in [superconformal gauge](superconformal-gauge.md). Global spin structures and moduli still remain.
