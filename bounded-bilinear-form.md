# Bounded bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

A bilinear form $B:X\times X\to\mathbb R$ on a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is bounded when some $C<\infty$ satisfies $|B(u,v)|\leq C\lVert u\rVert\lVert v\rVert$ for all $u,v\in X$.
