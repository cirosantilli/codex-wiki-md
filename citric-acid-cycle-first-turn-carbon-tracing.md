# Citric acid cycle first-turn carbon tracing

↑ **Parent:** [Citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md)

Net [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) does not identify individual [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). In the ordinary forward [citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md), neither entering [acetyl group](acetyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md) is released in that same first turn: both released [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) came from the starting [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md). The entering [carbon](carbon.md) remains in regenerated intermediates.[The Citric Acid Cycle, slide numbered 4, Origin of C-Atoms in CO2: entering acetyl carbons retained and released carbons from starting oxaloacetate](the-citric-acid-cycle-boston-university-lecture-slides.md)

Consider a chosen ideal first-turn experiment, with negligible natural [carbon-13](carbon-13.md) abundance and no exchange, side pathways, repeated turns or [isotope](isotope.md) effects. Supply $10\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of initially unlabelled [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md) and $10\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of [acetyl-CoA](acetyl-coa.md), of which $6\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ has both [acetyl group](acetyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md) positions labelled with [carbon-13](carbon-13.md). The other $4\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ has neither position labelled. The label refers only to the [acetyl group](acetyl-group.md), not to [coenzyme A](coenzyme-a.md).

The input contains $2\times6=12\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of labelled [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). The $10\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of [citrate](citrate.md) initially formed contains $60\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), so its labelled-[carbon](carbon.md) fraction is $12/60=20\%$. After exactly one forward traversal per starting [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md), the $20\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of released [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) has zero label, while the regenerated $10\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md) contains all $12\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ of labelled [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) among $40\,\mathrm{\mu mol}$ total: $30\%$.

First-turn carbon-13 accounting distinguishes net stoichiometry from chemical atom identity**. Original ideal calculation, not historical or measured [isotope](isotope.md) data. Stacked bars show the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), not amounts of whole [molecules](molecule-split.md). The first two bars are alternative stages; the last two are simultaneous outputs whose totals sum to the preceding [citrate](citrate.md) bar. [Carbon-13](carbon-13.md) is restricted initially to the incoming [acetyl groups](acetyl-group.md); natural abundance and side pathways are excluded.

The conservation check is $60=20+40$ for total [carbon](carbon.md) and $12=0+12$ for label. In this model, $60\%$ of regenerated [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) carry label, distinct from the $30\%$ labelled-[carbon](carbon.md) fraction. The regenerated [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md) is the same [chemical species](chemical-species.md) and the same [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) as the initial acceptor pool, but its [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) identities have changed. Saying that the cycle oxidizes an [acetyl group](acetyl-group.md) to two [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) is a net ledger, not a claim that those exact two incoming [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) immediately leave. Later-turn label release requires positional tracking and mixing assumptions; the first-turn balance alone does not determine it.
