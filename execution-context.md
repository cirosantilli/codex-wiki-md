# Execution context

↑ **Parent:** [Context switch](context-switch.md)

State needed to resume a suspended execution stream, including saved [processor registers](processor-register.md) and its position in a [call stack](call-stack.md). An [operating-system kernel](kernel-operating-system.md) can keep parts in a per-task record and parts on a kernel [call stack](call-stack.md). An [execution context](execution-context.md) identifies and preserves that stream's state; it is not a duplicate of all the [computer memory](computer-memory.md) reachable through its [address space](address-space.md).
