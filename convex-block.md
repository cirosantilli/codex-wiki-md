# Convex block

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

A convex block of a sequence in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is a sequence of [convex combinations](convex-combination.md) supported on successively disjoint finite intervals, with $p_n<q_n<p_{n+1}$, $a_i\ge0$, and $\sum_{i=p_n}^{q_n}a_i=1$. Zero coefficients can fill gaps and ensure strict endpoint inequalities. By the [Mazur theorem](mazur-theorem.md), every [weakly null sequence](weakly-null-sequence.md) has convex blocks tending to zero in norm.
