# Gain bandwidth limit on a laser pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Time-bandwidth product](time-bandwidth-product.md)

A [transform-limited pulse](transform-limited-pulse.md) needs coherent phases across its contributing [frequency](frequency.md) range. A laser's usable [gain bandwidth](gain-bandwidth.md) therefore sets a duration scale through the [time-bandwidth product](time-bandwidth-product.md), as displayed. The wavelength interval must first be converted to frequency; the simple conversion $c\Delta\lambda/\lambda^2$ is only a narrow-band approximation. Numerical constants depend on pulse shape and width convention: a Gaussian intensity envelope has $\Delta t_{\rm FWHM}\Delta\nu_{\rm FWHM}=2\ln2/\pi\simeq0.441$. A hard gain interval is not automatically a spectral FWHM. [Mode locking](mode-locking.md) can arrange the longitudinal modes' phases, while [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md) must be controlled to approach the transform limit. The cavity round-trip time sets pulse spacing, rather than this bandwidth-limited width.
