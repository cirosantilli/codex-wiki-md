# Ductile deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Structural geology](structural-geology.md)

Distributed permanent change of shape without requiring a discrete through-going brittle rupture. Its mechanisms and rate depend on [temperature](temperature-split.md), [stress](stress.md), composition and timescale; deep ductile structures need not be active earthquake [faults](geological-fault.md).
