[https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0208194](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0208194)

The author abstract describes a negatively charged scanning tip depleting the two-dimensional electron gas, producing conductance-response images and mode-dependent angular patterns. It distinguishes branches and inelastic scattering effects.
