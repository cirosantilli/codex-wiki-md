# Category of pointed sets

↑ **Parent:** [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md)

The category of [pointed sets](pointed-set.md) and basepoint-preserving [functions](function-split.md) is a [pointed category](pointed-category.md). It is equivalent to the [category of partial functions](category-of-partial-functions.md) by adjoining or deleting the basepoint. It is not isomorphic to the category of all actual sets and partial functions: there are many distinct singleton [zero objects](zero-object.md), whereas the empty set is the sole zero object in that category.
