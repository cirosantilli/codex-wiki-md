# Dust stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md)

A pressureless fluid with proper number density $n_0$, rest mass $m$ and [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $u$ has tensor $mn_0u^\mu u^\nu$. In its local rest frame there is energy density but no spatial stress. Particle-number conservation and the Lorentz force make its divergence the electromagnetic force density.
