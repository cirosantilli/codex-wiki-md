# Planar cluster-count identity

↑ **Parent:** [Planar dual graph](planar-dual-graph.md)

For a fixed finite plane graph, the complementary dual configuration opens a dual edge exactly when its primal edge is closed. Its components correspond to faces of the spanning open primal graph. The componentwise [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) gives the displayed identity. The outer face and isolated primal vertices are included; the identity also handles disconnected graphs, bridges and loops.
