# Algebraic cotangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md)

At a [closed point](closed-point.md) of a [variety](algebraic-variety.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), the cotangent space is $\mathfrak m_P/\mathfrak m_P^2$. The [universal property of Kähler differentials](universal-property-of-kahler-differentials.md) identifies its [dual vector space](linear-functional.md) with $k$-derivations of the [local ring](local-ring.md) into the residue field, hence with the [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md). The [Kähler differential sheaf](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md) has the same fibre because a derivation kills constants and products of two elements of the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md).
