# Normal space to a quiver orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Base change action on quiver representations](base-change-action-on-quiver-representations.md)

The infinitesimal [base change action on quiver representations](base-change-action-on-quiver-representations.md) is $\xi_x(u)_\rho=u_{t(\rho)}x_\rho-x_\rho u_{s(\rho)}$. Its image is the [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md) to the orbit, because the stabilizer is a smooth open subset of the [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md). The [extension complex of quiver representations](extension-complex-of-quiver-representations.md) identifies the quotient of the ambient tangent space by this image with $\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(X,X)$. Consequently [rigid quiver representations have open orbits](rigid-quiver-representations-have-open-orbits.md).
