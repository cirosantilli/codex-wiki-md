# Gaussian fourth moment

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

For a centered [normal random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md) $Z\sim N(0,v)$, $\mathbb EZ^4=3v^2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Z^2)=2v^2$. This converts squared [normal](normal-distribution.md) increments into exact [variance](variance-split.md) calculations, including the degenerate case $v=0$.
