# Counterion condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterion_condensation)

Localization of a finite fraction of oppositely charged [ions](ion.md) near a charged object even as the confining volume grows without bound. In the salt-free infinite-cylinder mean-field model, the [Manning parameter](manning-parameter.md) must exceed one for a nonzero limiting local density. The condensed charge does not include [ions](ion.md) that escape to infinity; finite-cell charge neutrality and the local infinite-volume limit must not be interchanged.
