# M-M-s queue

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

Poisson arrivals of rate $\lambda$ and $s$ independent exponential servers of rate $\mu$ give a [birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md) with departure rate $\mu\min(n,s)$. The process is positive recurrent for $\lambda<s\mu$, null recurrent at equality, and transient above capacity.
