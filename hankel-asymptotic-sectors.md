# Hankel asymptotic sectors

↑ **Parent:** [Hankel function](hankel-function.md)

For fixed order, the standard [Hankel function](hankel-function.md) expansions in $e^{\pm iz}z^{-1/2}$ are valid in different sectors on a chosen argument sheet. Analytic continuation can mix exponentials; the sectors must be specified rather than treating a formal inverse-power series as global.
