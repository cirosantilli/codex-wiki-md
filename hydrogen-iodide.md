# Hydrogen iodide

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen halide](hydrogen-halide.md)

The [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) containing one [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) atom and one [iodine](iodine.md) atom. Its measured [spectroscopic wavenumbers](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) include anharmonic corrections beyond the two-term [Morse oscillator](morse-oscillator.md) model.[Ground-state diatomic constants and higher anharmonic coefficients](hydrogen-iodide-constants-of-diatomic-molecules-by-klaus-p-huber-and-others.md)
