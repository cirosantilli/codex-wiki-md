# Boltzmann excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md)

In [local thermodynamic equilibrium](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), the ratio of two internal-level populations includes their [energy-level degeneracies](energy-level-degeneracy.md) $g_i,g_j$ and their excitation-energy difference. Temperature-sensitive [spectral line](spectral-line.md) ratios can constrain the excitation temperature when line formation and radiative transfer are modelled consistently. Ionization fractions additionally require the [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md).
