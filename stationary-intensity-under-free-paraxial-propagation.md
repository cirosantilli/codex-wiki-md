# Stationary intensity under free paraxial propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md)

The free [parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md) multiplies each transverse [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) by $e^{-i\nu^2x/(2k)}$, of unit modulus. A statistically stationary input therefore keeps its uncentered spectral measure and mean intensity. A pure phase screen of unit incident amplitude has total mean intensity one even when its coherent intensity is smaller. This statement does not require a Gaussian joint phase distribution; second-order stationarity and free paraxial propagation suffice.
