# Eligibility criteria for a clinical trial

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

Eligibility criteria specify which individuals may enter a [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md), defining its enrolled population and excluding individuals for whom the intervention or measurements are unsuitable. Screening applies those criteria before [randomization](randomization.md). The treatment comparison's [external validity](external-validity.md) depends on how this population relates to the population of interest.
