# Conductive contribution to complex permittivity

↑ **Parent:** [Complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md)

With phasor convention $e^{-i\omega t}$, the [Ampere-Maxwell law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) in a scalar conductor reads $\nabla\times\mathbf H=(\sigma-i\omega\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r)\mathbf E$. Factoring $-i\omega\varepsilon_0$ gives the displayed effective relative [complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md). Its positive imaginary conductive term expresses loss in this convention; the sign reverses for $e^{+i\omega t}$. This combines conduction and displacement currents for the same field and must not be confused with the material's loss-free relative permittivity alone.
