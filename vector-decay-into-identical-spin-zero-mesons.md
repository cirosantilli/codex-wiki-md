# Vector decay into identical spin-zero mesons

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-statistics theorem](spin-statistics-theorem.md)

A spin-one parent cannot decay into two identical spin-zero particles in the same internal state while preserving rotational [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and Bose exchange [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). Their final total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is orbital $L=1$, which is antisymmetric under interchange. Bose [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) requires even $L$ instead. An energetically allowed decay with matching [parity](parity.md) can therefore remain forbidden independently of the interaction's coupling strength.
