# Translation-invariant linear forms on p-adic continuous functions vanish

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete antidifferentiation on the p-adic integers](discrete-antidifferentiation-on-the-p-adic-integers.md)

Let $T:\mathcal C(\mathbb Z_p,\mathbb Q_p)\to\mathbb Q_p$ be linear and invariant under translation by one. For any $f$, choose its continuous [discrete antiderivative](discrete-antiderivative.md) $Jf$. Then $T(f)=T(\Delta Jf)=T(Jf(\cdot+1))-T(Jf)=0$. No [continuity](continuous-function.md) or boundedness of $T$ is assumed. In particular this applies to a form invariant under every translation by a [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md).
