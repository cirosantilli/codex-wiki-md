<h1 id="radial-solberg-hoiland-instability-criterion">Radial Solberg–Høiland instability criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Radial buoyancy frequency](radial-buoyancy-frequency.md)

For ideal axisymmetric radial displacements in a [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), the hydrodynamic squared oscillation frequency is $N_r^2+\kappa^2$, with [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) $\kappa=\Omega$. Exponential instability occurs when $N_r^2+\kappa^2<0$. This is the radial special case of rotating-fluid convective stability, rather than the complete set of three-dimensional Solberg–Høiland criteria.
