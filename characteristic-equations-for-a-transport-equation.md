# Characteristic equations for a transport equation

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

For a [transport equation](transport-equation.md) $\partial_tf+b(t,z)\cdot\nabla_zf=h$, solve $\dot z=b(t,z)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) then gives $d[f(t,z(t))]/dt=h(t,z(t))$. Initial data and the time [integral](integral.md) of this source determine the solution along each [characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md).
