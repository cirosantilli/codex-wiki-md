# Transmembrane protein

↑ **Parent:** [Integral membrane protein](integral-membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmembrane_protein)

A [transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md) has at least one [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) crossing the complete [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). One crossing is single-pass; several crossings in one chain are multipass. [Hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) regions face the membrane interior, while [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) regions can face [water](water.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Cell Membranes, Membrane Proteins: spanning regions, single and multiple crossings](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/cell-membranes.md)
