# Stalk inclusion into rational sections of a locally free sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Rational section of a sheaf of modules](rational-section-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md)

On an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md), every nonempty [open subset](open-set.md) is dense, so a [germ](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) determines a [rational section](rational-section-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md). For a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md), this map is injective: a local section which vanishes on a dense open has all its regular coefficient functions zero, hence is zero. Local freeness is important; a section of a torsion sheaf can vanish generically without having zero [germ](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md).
