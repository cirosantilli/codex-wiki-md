# Dual number

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative ring](commutative-ring.md)

The dual-number ring over a [field](field.md) $k$ consists of elements $a+b\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon^2=0$. The element $\varepsilon$ is a nonzero [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md); its ideal is the ring's only [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). Hence its [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) has the same one-point topology as $\operatorname{Spec}k$ while its [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) differs.
