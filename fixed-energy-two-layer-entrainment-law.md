# Fixed-energy two-layer entrainment law

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-energy turbulent mixed layer](fixed-energy-turbulent-mixed-layer.md)

For [potential energy of two-layer entrainment](potential-energy-of-two-layer-entrainment.md) with constant $B=g'h$, the [fixed-energy turbulent mixed layer](fixed-energy-turbulent-mixed-layer.md) assumption gives $u_U^2h=C_K^2\Omega^2R^2h_0$. If $u_I=C_Iu_U$ and the [interfacial power model for entrainment](interfacial-power-model-for-entrainment.md) applies with fixed coefficients, integration gives the displayed depth law with $A=5C_Wc_DC_I^3C_K^3\Omega^2R^3/(Bh_0)$. The relation applies only before the layer reaches the tank bottom. Its [interfacial Richardson number](interfacial-richardson-number.md) $Bd_I/(h u_I^2)$ stays constant for fixed interface thickness, whereas it decreases like $h^{-1}$ in the constant-speed entrainment model.
