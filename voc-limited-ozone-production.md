# VOC-limited ozone production

↑ **Parent:** [Photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md)

A regime in which precursor/[radical](radical-chemistry.md) supply and NOx-dependent [radical termination](radical-termination.md) make net [ozone](ozone.md) production more responsive to additional reactive organic compounds than to additional [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md). Reducing [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) can initially raise [ozone](ozone.md) in some such conditions. Diagnose the chemical regime before extrapolating an emission reduction to every location.
