# Write-allocate cache

↑ **Parent:** [Cache (computing)](cache-computing.md)

A [memory cache](cache-computing.md) policy that allocates the relevant [cache line](cache-line.md) on a write [cache miss](cache-miss.md). Fetching that [cache line](cache-line.md) can delay a [store instruction](store-instruction.md) unless other mechanisms hide the delay.
