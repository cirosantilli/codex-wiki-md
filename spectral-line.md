# Spectral line

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_line)

A narrow spectral feature records enhanced emission or absorption near a transition frequency. In a [stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md), its strength depends on atomic populations, [opacity](opacity.md), velocity fields and the [source function](radiative-transfer-source-function.md). Lines therefore diagnose composition as well as the physical atmosphere in which they form.
