# Electrostatic potential energy of point charges

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md)

For distinct [point charges](point-charge.md) $q_i$ at positions $r_i$, the electrostatic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is

$$
U=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\sum_{i<j}\frac{q_iq_j}{|r_i-r_j|}.
$$

When some charges are fixed, the terms depending on a movable charge form its potential energy in their [electric potential](electric-potential.md).
