<h1 id="goodman-and-kruskal-s-lambda">Goodman and Kruskal's lambda</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Contingency table](contingency-table.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodman_and_Kruskal's_lambda)

Predict categorical response $Y$ by a most frequent category. Without knowing $X$, the number of mistakes in a [contingency table](contingency-table.md) is $E_0=n-\max_j n_{+j}$. Allowing a separate most frequent response for each predictor row gives $E_1=n-\sum_i\max_j n_{ij}$. [Goodman and Kruskal's lambda](goodman-and-kruskal-s-lambda.md) is the fractional reduction in those mistakes when $E_0>0$. Its direction matters, and it can vanish despite [statistical association](statistical-association.md): if every row has the same most frequent response category, knowledge of $X$ changes no modal predictions even when the row distributions differ.
