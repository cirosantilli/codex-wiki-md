# Norm units in a tamely totally ramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Norm subgroup of a local field extension](norm-subgroup-of-a-local-field-extension.md)

For a tamely totally ramified extension of degree $q-1$ of a local field with residue field $\mathbb F_q$, the unit norms are exactly the principal units. Reduction sends a unit norm to its $(q-1)$st power, and the [norm-unit index in an abelian local extension](norm-unit-index-in-an-abelian-local-extension.md) shows that this containment has the correct index.
