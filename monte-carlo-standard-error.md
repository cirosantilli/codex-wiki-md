# Monte Carlo standard error

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo error](monte-carlo-error.md)

The [Monte Carlo standard error](monte-carlo-standard-error.md) estimates the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) of a simulation-based estimate across repeated simulation runs. For an average of $N$ [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with finite [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma_h^2$, it is $\sigma_h/\sqrt N$. Under a suitable [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) for [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md), correlated draws instead give the approximation $\sigma_h/\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}$, where $N_{\rm eff}$ is the [effective sample size of a Markov chain](effective-sample-size-of-a-markov-chain.md) for the particular estimated function $h$. This measures numerical uncertainty in an estimate, not uncertainty in the unknown parameter itself.
