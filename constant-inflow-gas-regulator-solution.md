# Constant-inflow gas regulator solution

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic gas regulator](galactic-gas-regulator.md)

With constant inflow $\Phi$, no [mass](mass.md) return or outflow, and $\Psi=\epsilon M_g$, [mass](mass.md) conservation gives $M_g=\Phi/\epsilon+(M_0-\Phi/\epsilon)e^{-\epsilon t}$. Positive $\epsilon$ makes the [gas](gas.md) equilibrium stable, with perturbations decaying on timescale $\epsilon^{-1}$. The [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md) tends to the inflow, not necessarily to zero.
