# Robust infeasibility under uniform constraint relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Farkas' lemma](farkas-lemma.md)

Suppose a [linear program](linear-programming.md) has a [Farkas' lemma](farkas-lemma.md) certificate $\lambda\geq0$, $A^T\lambda\geq0$, $b^T\lambda=-1$. A purported [feasible point](feasible-point.md) of $Ax\leq b+\varepsilon\mathbf1$, $x\geq0$ would imply $0\leq\lambda^TAx\leq-1+\varepsilon\sum_i\lambda_i$. Hence every relaxation below the displayed positive threshold remains infeasible. For rational inputs, bounds on the encoding length of a certificate supply the precision gap used by the [ellipsoid method](ellipsoid-method.md).
