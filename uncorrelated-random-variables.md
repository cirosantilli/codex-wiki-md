# Uncorrelated random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance](covariance.md)

Two square-integrable random variables $X,Y$ are uncorrelated when $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$. [Independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with finite second moments are uncorrelated, but the converse generally fails.
