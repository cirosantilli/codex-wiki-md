# Conformal compactification of Euclidean space

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal compactification](conformal-compactification.md)

Inverse stereographic projection identifies Euclidean four-space with a sphere minus one point. Multiplying its flat metric by $4/(1+|x|^2)^2$ gives the round metric, which extends smoothly over the added point. In the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md) description, the compact space is the projectivized real null cone of signature $(1,5)$. It differs topologically from [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md).
