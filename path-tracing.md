# Path tracing

↑ **Parent:** [Ray tracing (computer graphics)](ray-tracing-computer-graphics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_tracing)

A [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method-split.md) for estimating light transport through random paths of successive surface interactions. A sampled continuation contributes its scattering response and cosine divided by its sampling [probability density function](probability-density-function.md). [Next-event estimation](next-event-estimation.md) makes direct source connections explicit, including connections to ideal point lights.
