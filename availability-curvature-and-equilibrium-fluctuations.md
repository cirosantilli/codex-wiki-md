# Availability curvature and equilibrium fluctuations

↑ **Parent:** [Exergy](exergy.md)

After integrating out other variables, let $A(x)$ include the constrained [entropy](entropy-split.md) and the [measure](measure-split.md) appropriate to the chosen coordinate $x$. Its equilibrium [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is proportional to $e^{-A(x)/(k_BT_R)}$. Near a stable interior minimum, $A(x)=A(x_0)+A''(x_0)(x-x_0)^2/2+\cdots$ with $A''(x_0)>0$. The resulting [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) has [variance](variance-split.md) $k_BT_R/A''(x_0)$. This is the leading [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md); a coordinate-dependent [measure](measure-split.md) contributes to the effective $A$, and higher-order terms matter near a vanishing curvature.
