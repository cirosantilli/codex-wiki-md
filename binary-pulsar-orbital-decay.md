# Binary-pulsar orbital decay

↑ **Parent:** [Binary pulsar](binary-pulsar.md)

Decreasing [orbital period](orbital-period.md), distinct from spin slowdown. [Joseph Hooton Taylor Jr.](joseph-hooton-taylor-jr.md) inferred [masses](mass.md) using [binary-pulsar periastron advance](binary-pulsar-periastron-advance.md) and [binary-pulsar Einstein delay](binary-pulsar-einstein-delay.md), then tested the independent period derivative against the [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md). Correcting differential Galactic [acceleration](acceleration.md) gave indirect [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md) evidence. This was not a terrestrial strain measurement.[1993 lecture, V–VI, pp. 84–87, equations 5–9, Figures 10–11](binary-pulsars-and-relativistic-gravity-by-joseph-hooton-taylor-jr.md)

In particular, the raw measured derivative schematically contains

$$
\left(\frac{\dot P_b}{P_b}\right)_{\rm obs}=\left(\frac{\dot P_b}{P_b}\right)_{\rm intrinsic}+\frac{a_\parallel^{\rm relative}}{c}+\hbox{other kinematic contributions}.
$$

Here $a_\parallel^{\rm relative}$ is positive when the source's recession accelerates. Its contribution follows by differentiating the leading [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md). A decreasing observed [orbital period](orbital-period.md) alone therefore does not identify a unique energy-loss mechanism.

For fixed [masses](mass.md), [binary orbital energy](two-body-orbital-energy.md) is $-Gm_1m_2/(2a)$ and [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) gives $P_b\propto a^{3/2}$. Positive outgoing [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md) predicted by the [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md) makes the orbital [energy](energy-split.md) more negative, reducing $a$ and $P_b$. This sign argument explains shrinking orbits, but a quantitative test also needs the predicted magnitude and [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) dependence.
