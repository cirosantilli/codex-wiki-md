# Deuteron

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

The bound [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) containing one [proton](proton.md) and one [neutron](neutron-split.md). Its ground-state [spin](spin.md) is one and its isospin is zero. The appropriately quantized [toroidal two-Skyrmion](toroidal-two-skyrmion.md) models these quantum numbers.
