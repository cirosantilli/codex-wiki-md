# Harmonic phase mode of a quantum rotor chain

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled quantum rotor chain](coupled-quantum-rotor-chain.md)

Expanding the cosine gives a wave equation $I\phi_{tt}=J\phi_{xx}$ and a gapless phase mode with physical frequency $\sqrt{J/I}|q|$. The exact harmonic lattice frequency is $2\sqrt{J/I}|\sin(q/2)|$. It resembles [superfluid](superfluid.md) sound. In one dimension, Gaussian phase fluctuations and possible compact [phase slips](phase-slip.md) require care before inferring true long-range order from this branch.
