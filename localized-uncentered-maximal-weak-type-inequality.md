# Localized uncentered maximal weak-type inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Uncentered maximal weak-type inequality](uncentered-maximal-weak-type-inequality.md)

For a positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on $\mathbb R^d$, let $E_\alpha=\{M_u\nu>\alpha\}$, using open balls in the [uncentered maximal function of a finite measure](uncentered-maximal-function-of-a-finite-measure.md). Every ball witnessing the inequality lies entirely in $E_\alpha$. Cover a compact subset by finitely many witnessing balls and select disjoint balls greedily by decreasing radius. Their threefold dilations cover the compact set. Their total measure is at most $\nu(E_\alpha)$, giving the bound by [inner regularity of Lebesgue measure](inner-regularity-of-lebesgue-measure.md). For a signed or complex measure, apply this to its [total variation measure](variation-measure.md).
