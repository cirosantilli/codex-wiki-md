# Exterior multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md)

Exterior multiplication by a fixed [differential form](differential-form-split.md) is the linear operator taking the [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md) on the left. For a one-form $\omega$, it raises degree by one. Anticommutativity gives $e(\omega)e(\eta)+e(\eta)e(\omega)=0$ for one-forms. On the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) of an inner product space, pairing this operation with contraction produces [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md) $c(v)=e(v^\flat)-\iota_v$.
