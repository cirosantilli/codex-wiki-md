# Diffusion tensor for image filtering

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion image processing](diffusion-image-processing.md)

A positive tensor $D=a_ne_ne_n^T+a_te_te_t^T$ selects different diffusion strengths normal and tangent to an estimated [image edge](image-edge.md). Taking $0<a_n\ll a_t$ smooths along the [image edge](image-edge.md) while reducing mixing across it. A [structure tensor](structure-tensor.md) provides robust orientation estimates. Positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) ensure forward local parabolicity.
