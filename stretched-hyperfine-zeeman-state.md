# Stretched hyperfine Zeeman state

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Rabi formula](breit-rabi-formula.md)

For electronic $J=1/2$, an extremal total projection has only one product state rather than a two-dimensional [spin](spin.md) block. With dimensionless angular-momentum operators and nuclear Zeeman coefficient $g_I$ in Bohr-magneton units, its [energy](energy-split.md) is $AI/2\pm\mu_BB(g_J/2+g_I I)$. Extending both signs of a two-state [Breit-Rabi formula](breit-rabi-formula.md) square root to each stretched projection would invent an extra state.
