# Kurtosis

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

For a real [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ with [mean](expected-value.md) $\mu=\mathbb EX$, finite positive [variance](variance-split.md) $0<\sigma^2=\operatorname{Var}(X)<\infty$, and finite [fourth moment](fourth-moment.md), [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) is the fourth [central moment](central-moment.md) divided by the square of the [variance](variance-split.md):

$$
\kappa(X)=\frac{\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^4]}{\sigma^4}=\mathbb E[Z^4],\qquad Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}.
$$

The [standard score](standard-score.md) $Z$ measures distance from the [mean](expected-value.md) in [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md). Its fourth power gives a value ten [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md) away a weight $10^4=10000$ inside the [expected value](expected-value.md). Thus a small [probability mass](probability-mass.md) far from the [mean](expected-value.md) can strongly affect [kurtosis](kurtosis.md). It is useful for describing the contribution of extreme [standard scores](standard-score.md), but does not by itself describe the height or shape of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) near its peak.[§2, Terminology, definition of κ; §4, counterexamples with different peaks; §5, central and tail contributions](kurtosis-as-peakedness-1905-2014-r-i-p-by-peter-h-westfall.md)

The [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) has [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) $3$. A random scale can increase [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) even when the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) is normal. A constant [random variable](random-variable-split.md) has zero [variance](variance-split.md), so this ratio is undefined. If the [variance](variance-split.md) is finite and positive but the fourth [central moment](central-moment.md) is infinite, the ratio can be written $+\infty$ in an extended sense; it is not a finite [kurtosis](kurtosis.md). If the [variance](variance-split.md) itself is infinite or the [mean](expected-value.md) is undefined, the displayed normalization is unavailable.
