# Kurtosis

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

For finite [fourth moment](fourth-moment.md) and positive [variance](variance-split.md), [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) is $\mathbb E[(X-\mathbb EX)^4]/\operatorname{Var}(X)^2$. The [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) has [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) $3$. A random scale can increase [kurtosis](kurtosis.md) even when the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) is normal.
