# Finite coefficient field in positive-characteristic local fields

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-characteristic complete discretely valued field](equal-characteristic-complete-discretely-valued-field.md)

For a characteristic-p [local field](local-field.md) with [residue field](residue-field.md) $\mathbb F_q$, the simple roots of $X^q-X$ lift uniquely by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). Their lifts form a copy of $\mathbb F_q$, since qth powers preserve addition and multiplication. Choose a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$. Repeatedly subtracting the lifted residue and dividing by $\pi$ produces a unique convergent expansion $\sum_{i\ge0}a_i\pi^i$ for each integral element. Allowing finitely many negative powers gives a valued-field isomorphism $K\cong\mathbb F_q((T))$, $T\mapsto\pi$.
