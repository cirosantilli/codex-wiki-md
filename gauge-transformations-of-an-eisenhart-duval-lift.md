# Gauge transformations of an Eisenhart-Duval lift

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenhart-Duval lift](eisenhart-duval-lift.md)

A time-independent [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) $\mathbf A\mapsto\mathbf A+\nabla\chi$ in an [Eisenhart-Duval lift](eisenhart-duval-lift.md) is the coordinate change $u\mapsto u+2\chi$. It preserves the metric one-form $du-2\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf x-Udt$ and adds only $-2d\chi/dt$ to the reduced Lagrangian.
