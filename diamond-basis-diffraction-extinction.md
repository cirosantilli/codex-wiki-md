# Diamond-basis diffraction extinction

↑ **Parent:** [Systematic absence](systematic-absence.md)

The identical two-atom motif adds a factor $1+\exp[i\pi(h+k+l)/2]$ to the FCC lattice [structure factor](structure-factor.md). Among even-index FCC diffraction reflections, only those with $h+k+l$ divisible by4 survive. The allowed FCC reflection200 therefore vanishes for equal motif [scattering amplitudes](scattering-amplitude.md).
