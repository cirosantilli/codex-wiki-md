# Stratospheric denitrification

↑ **Parent:** [Polar stratospheric cloud](polar-stratospheric-cloud.md)

Removal of reactive nitrogen from an air [mass](mass.md) through formation and [sedimentation](sedimentation.md) of nitric-acid-containing particles. This can delay reformation of [chlorine nitrate](chlorine-nitrate.md) and prolong active [chlorine](chlorine.md) chemistry. It is a physical/chemical atmospheric removal process, distinct from biological denitrification.
