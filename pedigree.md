# Pedigree

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)

A pedigree records parent–offspring relationships. A [founder in a pedigree](founder-in-a-pedigree.md) has no recorded parents; all other individuals have a genotype conditional on their parental genotypes. Inbreeding can produce loops in the undirected diagram while the directed ancestry remains acyclic.
