# Bisimulation game

↑ **Parent:** [Bisimulation](bisimulation.md)

The Spoiler takes one successor step from the current vertex of either [rooted directed graph](rooted-directed-graph.md); the Duplicator must take a matching successor step in the other. The Duplicator wins by never failing at a finite stage, including when the Spoiler cannot move. A winning strategy gives a [bisimulation](bisimulation.md) by collecting endpoint pairs of all finite plays consistent with it.
