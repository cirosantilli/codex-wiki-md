# Wald estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Instrumental variable](instrumental-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wald_estimator)

For a binary [instrumental variable](instrumental-variable.md) $Z$, exposure $X$, and outcome $Y$, the Wald estimator is the ratio of the instrument's effect on the outcome to its effect on the exposure:

$$
\widehat\beta_{\rm Wald}
=\frac{\overline Y_{Z=1}-\overline Y_{Z=0}}
{\overline X_{Z=1}-\overline X_{Z=0}}.
$$

Under instrumental-variable assumptions it estimates a causal effect.
