# Two-point scaling at an ultraviolet fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraviolet fixed point](ultraviolet-fixed-point.md)

If $g(t)\to g_*$, $\gamma(g(t))\to\gamma_*$ and the reference correlator has a finite nonzero fixed-point limit, the [characteristic solution of the multiplicative Callan-Symanzik equation](characteristic-solution-of-the-multiplicative-callan-symanzik-equation.md) gives $\log f(t)=2\gamma_*t+o(t)$. A simple attractive fixed point and smooth [anomalous dimension](anomalous-dimension.md) give a finite prefactor multiplying $e^{2\gamma_*t}$. A nonsimple fixed point can retain slower corrections.
