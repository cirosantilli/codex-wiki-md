# Static Green function across a circle compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded circle-compactified massless propagator](retarded-circle-compactified-massless-propagator.md)

At zero frequency, sum the [Kaluza-Klein tower](kaluza-klein-tower.md) of three-spatial-dimensional Yukawa kernels: $[4\pi Rr]^{-1}(1+2\sum_{n\geq1}e^{-2\pi nr/R})$. The geometric series gives the displayed function. It behaves as $1/(4\pi^2r^2)$ for $r\ll R$, the four-spatial-dimensional Coulomb kernel, and as $1/(4\pi Rr)$ for $r\gg R$, the three-spatial-dimensional kernel with a zero-mode normalization factor.
