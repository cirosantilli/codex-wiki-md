# Spherical far-field range condition

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse scattering problem](inverse-scattering-problem.md)

For an outgoing field outside a sphere of radius $R$, write $u_s=k\sum_{n,m}i^{n+1}a_n^m h_n^{(1)}(kr)Y_n^m$. Its [far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md) is $\sum a_n^mY_n^m$. If its trace on that sphere lies in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), Parseval's identity gives $k^2\sum|h_n^{(1)}(kR)|^2|a_n^m|^2<\infty$. The [fixed-argument growth of spherical Hankel functions](fixed-argument-growth-of-spherical-hankel-functions.md) yields the displayed equivalent range condition, with the low-order weights defined finitely. Square summability of the far-field coefficients alone does not ensure continuation to the chosen sphere.
