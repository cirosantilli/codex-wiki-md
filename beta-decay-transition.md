# Beta decay transition

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_decay_transition)

A nuclear [beta decay](beta-decay.md) connects initial and final [quantum states](quantum-state.md) through vector and axial-vector parts of the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). At leading order in the lepton wavelength expansion, [Fermi transitions](fermi-transition.md) carry lepton-pair [spin](spin.md) $S=0$, while [Gamow-Teller transitions](gamow-teller-transition.md) carry $S=1$. If the lepton pair carries [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $l$, its total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is $J=l$ for $S=0$, or $J=|l-1|,\ldots,l+1$ for $S=1$. The nuclear states must obey $|J_i-J_f|\leq J\leq J_i+J_f$, and their [parities](parity.md) satisfy $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^l$ in this leading orbital classification. The least permitted $l$ labels the degree of forbiddenness. Real forbidden transitions involve several weak multipole operators; the elementary spin classification does not assert that only one operator or nuclear matrix element exists.
