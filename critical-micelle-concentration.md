# Critical micelle concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Micelle](micelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_micelle_concentration)

The critical micelle concentration is the approximate [surfactant](surfactant.md) [concentration](concentration.md) above which additional dissolved [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) predominantly enters [micelles](micelle.md) rather than remaining as isolated molecules. It is a concentration crossover governed by equality of the molecular [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) in the two environments, not a claim that absolutely no aggregates exist below a sharp threshold. [Temperature](temperature-split.md), ionic conditions and molecular structure affect its value.
