# Order of an entire function

↑ **Parent:** [Entire function](entire-function.md)

For a nonconstant [entire function](entire-function.md), put $M_f(R)=\max_{|s|\leq R}|f(s)|$. Its order measures the exponent in its exponential growth. Bounds $\log M_f(R)=O(R\log R)$ imply order at most one; a matching lower bound along the positive real axis proves order exactly one. This distinction is stronger than mere entire analyticity and controls the genus in [Hadamard factorization](hadamard-factorization-theorem.md).
