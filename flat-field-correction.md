# Flat-field correction

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat-field_correction)

[Flat-field correction](flat-field-correction.md) compensates stable multiplicative variations in [photodetector](photodetector.md) response using calibrated illumination. Identically illuminated exposure ratios cancel the stable response in their mean, but their [photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md) remains signal dependent.
