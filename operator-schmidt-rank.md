# Operator Schmidt rank

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)

The operator Schmidt rank of a bipartite [linear operator](linear-operator.md) is its shortest expansion as a sum of product [linear operators](linear-operator.md). Expanding in local orthonormal operator bases gives a coefficient [matrix](matrix.md); its [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) is the [operator Schmidt rank](operator-schmidt-rank.md), by a [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md). It is unchanged by invertible local operator-basis changes. A rank-one projector onto a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md) of [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md) $d$ has [operator Schmidt rank](operator-schmidt-rank.md) $d^2$: its expansion contains all $d^2$ independent products of local matrix units.
