# Gene-environment correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Behavioral genetics](behavioral-genetics.md)

Covariation between inherited characteristics and environments encountered or elicited. It differs from [genotype-environment interaction](genotype-environment-interaction.md), in which the effect of inherited variation depends on the environment.
