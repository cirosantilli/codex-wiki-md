# Schwartz-Bruhat space

↑ **Parent:** [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md)

For a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md) $F$, the Schwartz-Bruhat space $\mathcal S(F)$ consists of the locally constant, compactly supported complex-valued functions on $F$.
