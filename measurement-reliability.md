# Measurement reliability

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)

Consistency or precision of measurements under stated sources of variation. Reliability depends on the population, conditions and intended score; it is not a permanent property of an instrument alone. [Inter-rater reliability](inter-rater-reliability.md) and [test-retest reliability](test-retest-reliability.md) address different sources of variation. Reliability does not establish [construct validity](construct-validity.md).
