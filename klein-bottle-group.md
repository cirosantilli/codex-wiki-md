# Klein bottle group

↑ **Parent:** [Bass-Serre theory](bass-serre-theory.md)

The fundamental group of the Klein bottle has presentation

$$
\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\rangle.
$$

Its subgroup $\langle a^2,b\rangle\cong\mathbb Z^2$ has index two.
