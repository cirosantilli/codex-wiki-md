# Conformal invariance of SLE

↑ **Parent:** [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

Chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) is defined on any simply connected domain with two marked boundary points by applying a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) from the upper half-plane. Its law is independent of the chosen map, up to a deterministic change of parameterization.
