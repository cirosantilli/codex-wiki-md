# Coleman-Noll procedure

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Duhem inequality](clausius-duhem-inequality.md)

Requiring the [Clausius-Duhem inequality](clausius-duhem-inequality.md) for independent reversible mechanical and thermal variations constrains a [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md). For $U(F,\eta,\xi)$ it gives $P_{Ii}=\rho_0U_{,F_{iI}}$ and $\theta=U_{,\eta}$. Combining the [Lagrangian internal energy balance](lagrangian-internal-energy-balance.md) with the [Lagrangian entropy inequality](lagrangian-entropy-inequality.md) leaves $f_r\dot\xi_r-q_I\theta_{,I}/\theta\ge0$. The independence assumptions are part of the constitutive argument.
