# Apsidal precession

↑ **Parent:** [Secular perturbation](secular-perturbation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apsidal_precession)

Apsidal precession is the gradual rotation of an orbit's line of apsides, equivalently a change in its [longitude of periapsis](longitude-of-periapsis.md).
