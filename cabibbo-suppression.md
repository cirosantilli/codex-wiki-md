# Cabibbo suppression

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo angle](cabibbo-angle.md)

A small [weak charged current](charged-current.md) amplitude factor associated with $\sin\theta_C$. A doubly suppressed amplitude contains two such factors, giving a rate suppression of order $\tan^4\theta_C$ relative to a favoured process when other matrix elements are comparable.
