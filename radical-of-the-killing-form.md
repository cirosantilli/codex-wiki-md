# Radical of the Killing form

↑ **Parent:** [Killing form](killing-form.md)

The [radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) of the [Killing form](killing-form.md) is the [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\{x:B(x,y)=0\text{ for all }y\}$. It is zero for a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). Every element of the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md) lies in this radical.
