# Troponin

↑ **Parent:** [Actomyosin](actomyosin.md)

A calcium-responsive regulatory protein complex on thin muscle filaments. Changes in its occupancy and conformation couple calcium signals to movement of [tropomyosin](tropomyosin.md) and accessibility of myosin-binding sites on actin.
