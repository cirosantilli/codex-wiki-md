# Geodesic Hessian formula

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

For a [geodesic](geodesic.md) $\gamma$ with initial velocity $v$, $(f\circ\gamma)^{\prime\prime}(0)=\operatorname{Hess}f(v,v)$, because the covariant acceleration vanishes. Summing over an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of the tangent space gives $\Delta f(p)=-\sum_i(f\circ\gamma_i)^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the nonnegative [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md).
