# Ultrafilter selection from a partition-rich family

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter lemma](ultrafilter-lemma.md)

Let $S$ be a nonempty [set](set-split.md) and $\mathscr P\subseteq\mathcal P(S)$ be upward closed. An [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) all of whose members lie in $\mathscr P$ exists exactly when every finite [set partition](set-partition.md) of $S$ has a cell in $\mathscr P$. For sufficiency, the complements of sets outside $\mathscr P$ have the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md): a finite cover by such sets could be disjointified into a partition with no cell in $\mathscr P$. The [ultrafilter lemma](ultrafilter-lemma.md) extends the generated proper [filter](filter-set-theory.md). Necessity follows because an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) selects exactly one cell of every finite partition. This criterion requires partition richness of the whole ground set; it does not assert that the complement family is closed under unions.
