<h1 id="positivity-criterion-for-a-radial-kahler-potential">Positivity criterion for a radial Kähler potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation-invariant Kähler potential on the complex plane](rotation-invariant-kahler-potential-on-the-complex-plane.md)

For a potential already smooth on all of $\mathbb C$, the associated form is a [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) exactly when the displayed two positivity conditions hold. The first gives a positive metric coefficient off the origin; the second is positivity of $f_{z\bar z}(0)$. The associated real metric is $2f_{z\bar z}(dx^2+dy^2)$. Positivity away from the origin alone permits a degenerate metric at the origin.
