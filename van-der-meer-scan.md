# Van der Meer scan

↑ **Parent:** [Collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md)

An absolute [collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md) calibration that scans the transverse separation of two colliding beams while measuring an interaction rate. Known bunch populations and the overlap integral determine the visible monitor [cross section](cross-section-physics.md). Correlations between horizontal and vertical profiles require a joint overlap treatment.
