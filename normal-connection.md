# Normal connection

↑ **Parent:** [Metric connection](metric-connection.md)

The ambient [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) induces this [metric connection](metric-connection.md) on the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) of an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md). Its tangential counterpart appears in the [Weingarten formula](weingarten-formula.md). Projecting the derivative preserves the [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) Leibniz rule and the fiber [inner product](inner-product.md).
