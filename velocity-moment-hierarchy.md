# Velocity-moment hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity moment](velocity-moment.md)

Taking [velocity moments](velocity-moment.md) of a kinetic transport equation couples the equation for a moment of order $r$ to a spatial derivative of a moment of order $r+1$. A finite fluid description therefore requires closure. For example, the [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) equation involves the [third central velocity moment](third-central-velocity-moment.md).
