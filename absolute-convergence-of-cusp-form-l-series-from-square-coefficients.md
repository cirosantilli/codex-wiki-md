# Absolute convergence of cusp-form L-series from square coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Positive unfolding of a cusp-form square](positive-unfolding-of-a-cusp-form-square.md)

By [positive unfolding of a cusp-form square](positive-unfolding-of-a-cusp-form-square.md), $\sum|a_n|^2n^{-\beta}<\infty$ for $\beta>k$. Choose $k<\beta<2\sigma-1$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bounds the absolute sum for the [L-function of a cusp form](l-function-of-a-cusp-form.md) by

$$
\left(\sum_{n\geq1}|a_n|^2n^{-\beta}\right)^{1/2}\left(\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-(2\sigma-\beta)}\right)^{1/2}<\infty.
$$
