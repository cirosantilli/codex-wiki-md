# Underdamped RLC response

↑ **Parent:** [Series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md)

When $R^2<4L/C$, the homogeneous [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) response oscillates with angular frequency $\sqrt{1/(LC)-R^2/(4L^2)}$ and envelope $e^{-Rt/(2L)}$. Its [characteristic roots](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) form a complex-conjugate pair with negative [real part](real-part.md) when $R>0$.
