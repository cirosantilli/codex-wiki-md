# Intersection of two planes

↑ **Parent:** [Plane](plane.md)

Two [planes](plane.md) $n_1\cdot r=d_1$, $n_2\cdot r=d_2$ with independent [normal vectors](normal-vector.md) meet in a [straight line](straight-line.md) with direction $n_1\times n_2$. Any difference of two points on both [planes](plane.md) is perpendicular to both [normal vectors](normal-vector.md), which proves the direction statement. A particular point follows by solving the two [linear equations](linear-equation.md); adding a real multiple of the direction gives the [parametric equation of a straight line](parametric-equation-of-a-straight-line.md). For parallel [normal vectors](normal-vector.md), the [planes](plane.md) are either coincident or disjoint instead.
