# Photogate

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific instrument](scientific-instrument.md)

A [photogate](photogate.md) detects interruption of a light beam. Timing that interruption and independently measuring the flag length gives its average [speed](speed.md) during passage; the direction must be assigned separately.
