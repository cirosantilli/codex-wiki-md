# Normal modes of inductively coupled LC circuits

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Two identical circuits with self-[inductance](inductance.md) $L$, capacitance $C$ and [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md) $\mathcal M$ have same-sign and opposite-sign charge modes with $\omega_\pm^2=1/[C(L\pm\mathcal M)]$. The [inductance](inductance.md) matrix acts as the [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md); positivity requires $|\mathcal M|<L$.
