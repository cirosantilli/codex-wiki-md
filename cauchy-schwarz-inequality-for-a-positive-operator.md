# Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for a positive operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

For a bounded [positive operator](positive-operator.md) $T$, the form $B(x,y)=\langle Tx,y\rangle$ is positive semidefinite and Hermitian. Expanding $B(x+ty,x+ty)\geq0$ for complex $t$ and minimizing its quadratic polynomial gives the displayed inequality. If $B(y,y)=0$, varying $t$ along the negative direction of $B(x,y)$ forces $B(x,y)=0$, covering the degenerate case. In particular $\langle Tx,x\rangle=0$ implies $Tx=0$, by testing against arbitrary $y$.
