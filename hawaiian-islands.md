# Hawaiian Islands

↑ **Parent:** [Geology](geology.md)

A volcanic island chain in the Pacific, used to study [ocean-island basalt](ocean-island-basalt.md), [volcanic loading](volcanic-loading.md) and [lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md). The narrow volcanic load and the broad [Hawaiian swell](hawaiian-swell.md) probe different support mechanisms.
