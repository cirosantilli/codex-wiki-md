# Financial market

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

A model specifying traded [financial assets](financial-asset.md), their price and payment processes, available information, and allowed [admissible trading strategies](admissible-trading-strategy.md). A [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) generates wealth using gains on these assets. Absence of [arbitrage](arbitrage.md) and [market completeness](complete-market.md) respectively concern whether trading can create a riskless profit from zero capital and whether every specified [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md) can be replicated.
