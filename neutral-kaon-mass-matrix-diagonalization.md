# Neutral-kaon mass matrix diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md)

With [CPT symmetry](cpt-symmetry.md), a Hermitian [kaon](kaon.md) [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) is $M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}r_0&z\\z^*&r_0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $r_0\pm|z|$. For correlated roots $a^2=z$, $b^2=z^*$ and $ab=|z|$, the normalized lower and upper [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are $(a,-b)/\sqrt{2|z|}$ and $(a,b)/\sqrt{2|z|}$. They are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). This is a [mass](mass.md)-only result; including decay requires the generally non-Hermitian effective matrix $M-i\Gamma/2$.
