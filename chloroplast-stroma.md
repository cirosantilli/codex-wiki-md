# Chloroplast stroma

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast](chloroplast.md)

The [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) is the fluid compartment inside the [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) envelope but outside its [thylakoids](thylakoid.md). The [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) operates here; the interior of a [thylakoid](thylakoid.md) is a separate compartment.
