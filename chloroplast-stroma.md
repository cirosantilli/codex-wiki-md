# Chloroplast stroma

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast](chloroplast.md)

The [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) is the fluid compartment inside the [inner chloroplast envelope membrane](inner-chloroplast-envelope-membrane.md) and outside [thylakoids](thylakoid.md), containing [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) [enzymes](enzyme-split.md).[Biology 2e, 8.1, Figures 8.6–8.7: stroma and Calvin cycle location](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis.md)

A [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) location is therefore more informative than an unspecified “inside the [chloroplast](chloroplast.md)” location. In the boundary model above, entering [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) has not yet entered [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md). [Protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) must distinguish these destinations; a fraction containing [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md) does not establish which internal compartment contains a detected [protein](protein-split.md).
