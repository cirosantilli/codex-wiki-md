# Minute and second of arc

↑ **Parent:** [Angle](angle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minute_and_second_of_arc)

A minute of arc is one sixtieth of a degree; a second of arc is one sixtieth of a minute of arc. These units describe small astronomical [angles](angle.md), including [astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md).
