# Primitive tetragonal lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Tetragonal crystal system](tetragonal-crystal-system.md)

A [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) with mutually perpendicular primitive vectors of lengths $a,a,c$, with $c\ne a$. A chemically ordered [L10 crystal structure](l10-crystal-structure.md) has this translation lattice even when drawn using a larger four-site cell that resembles a distorted FCC conventional cell.
