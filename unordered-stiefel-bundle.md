# Unordered Stiefel bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel manifold](stiefel-manifold.md)

For a rank-$n$ Euclidean [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), take its ordered orthonormal $k$-frame bundle and quotient by the symmetric group permuting the $k$ vectors. The whole fiber is generally positive-dimensional. A prescribed global framing selects a finite subcover consisting of the unordered $k$-element subsets of its distinguished frame vectors.
