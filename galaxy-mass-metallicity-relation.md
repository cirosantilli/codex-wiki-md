<h1 id="galaxy-mass-metallicity-relation">Galaxy mass--metallicity relation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic metallicity](galactic-metallicity.md)

The galaxy mass--metallicity relation is the empirical tendency for galaxies with greater [galaxy stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md) to have higher [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) and [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md). Mass-dependent metal loss, gas inflow, star-formation efficiency, and recycling all contribute.
