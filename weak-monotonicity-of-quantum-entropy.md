# Weak monotonicity of quantum entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Strong subadditivity of quantum entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md)

Every tripartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) satisfies $S(AC)+S(BC)\geq S(A)+S(B)$. Purify to $ABCD$ and apply [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md) to $ACD$, conditioned on $A$. The [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md) gives $S(AD)=S(BC)$ and $S(ACD)=S(B)$, proving the inequality. The original tripartite state need not be pure.
