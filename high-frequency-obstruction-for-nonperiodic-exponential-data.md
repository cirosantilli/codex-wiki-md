# High-frequency obstruction for nonperiodic exponential data

↑ **Parent:** [Admissible data for a periodic convolution inverse](admissible-data-for-a-periodic-convolution-inverse.md)

For a continuously differentiable periodic kernel, integration by parts and the [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md) give $c_n=o(1/|n|)$. Nonperiodic data $e^{\alpha x}$ with $\alpha\notin i\mathbb Z$ have [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) asymptotic to a nonzero multiple of $1/n$. Their inverse coefficients $y_n/c_n$ therefore fail square summability, and there is no Hilbert-space inverse or least-squares minimizer. Periodic exponential data $\alpha=im$ instead give the single-mode solution $e^{imx}/c_m$.
