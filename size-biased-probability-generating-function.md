# Size-biased probability generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Size-biased distribution](size-biased-distribution.md)

For a positive [integer](integer-split.md)-valued [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with finite mean $\mu$, the [size-biased distribution](size-biased-distribution.md) has probabilities $k\mathbb P(Y=k)/\mu$. They sum to one by the definition of [expected value](expected-value-split.md). Multiplying the differentiated [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) by $s/\mu$ gives the displayed identity for $0\leq s<1$, with the endpoint interpreted by its limit.
