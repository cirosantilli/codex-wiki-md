# Isophotal photometry

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)

[isophotal photometry](isophotal-photometry.md) measures an extended source's [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) inside a contour of fixed [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md). It misses light outside that contour. The fraction lost depends on the intrinsic brightness profile, convolution with the [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md), [sky background](sky-brightness.md) estimation and the chosen threshold. A per-pixel threshold is not a fundamental limit on fitting or averaging many faint pixels together.
