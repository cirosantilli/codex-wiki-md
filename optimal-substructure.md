# Optimal substructure

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_substructure)

[Optimal substructure](optimal-substructure.md) means that the relevant parts of an optimal solution solve their own subproblems optimally. For a [0-1 knapsack problem](0-1-knapsack-problem.md), removing a chosen item leaves an optimal selection among the other allowed items at the reduced capacity. For sequential [mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md), this becomes the [dynamic programming principle](dynamic-programming-principle.md): after fixing the next decision and resulting [dynamic programming state](dynamic-programming-state.md), use an optimal continuation. [Optimal substructure](optimal-substructure.md) justifies the [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md); overlap between subproblems explains why storing a [value function](value-function.md) can save work.
