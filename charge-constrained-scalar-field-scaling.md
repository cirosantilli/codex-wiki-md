# Charge-constrained scalar-field scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Q-ball](q-ball.md)

For $f_\lambda(x)=f(\lambda x)$ in $d$ space dimensions, write $T=\tfrac12\int|\nabla f|^2$, $W=\int U(f)$ and $I=\int f^2$. Eliminating the rotation frequency by $\omega=Q/I$ gives the displayed fixed-charge scaling. The positive-power charge term can balance the gradient and potential terms even for $d\ge3$. This evades the static [Derrick theorem](derrick-s-theorem.md) because the field is time-dependent and the relevant variations preserve its [Noether charge](noether-charge.md).
