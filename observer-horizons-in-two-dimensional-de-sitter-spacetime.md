# Observer horizons in two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal cylinder of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](conformal-cylinder-of-two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For the complete observer $\chi=0$ in [two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md), let $d(\chi,0)\in[0,\pi]$ be shortest angular distance. An event can signal to the observer exactly when $d(\chi,0)<\pi/2-T$, and can receive its signals exactly when $d(\chi,0)<T+\pi/2$. Equality describes its future and past [observer event horizons](observer-event-horizon.md). Their intersection is the [static patch of de Sitter spacetime](static-patch-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md) containing the observer. These are observer-dependent cosmological horizons; constant-$T$ circles are [Cauchy hypersurfaces](cauchy-surface.md), so there are no [Cauchy horizons](cauchy-horizon.md) in the full spacetime.
