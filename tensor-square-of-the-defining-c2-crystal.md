# Tensor square of the defining C2 crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal of the defining symplectic representation](crystal-of-the-defining-symplectic-representation.md)

Under the [crystal tensor-product rule](crystal-tensor-product-rule.md) that uses the first factor for lowering when $\varphi_i(a)>\varepsilon_i(b)$, the four-letter [C2 root system](c2-root-system.md) crystal has highest tensor vertices $1\otimes1$, $1\otimes2$ and $1\otimes\bar1$. Their [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) are $2\omega_1$, $\omega_2$ and zero. Lowering generates connected components of sizes ten, five and one, respectively; their union exhausts all sixteen vertices. The corresponding representation decomposition is the symmetric square plus the primitive exterior square and the invariant symplectic line.
