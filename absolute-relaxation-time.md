# Absolute relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Relaxation time](relaxation-time.md)

For a finite [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) with at least two states that is an [aperiodic Markov chain](aperiodic-markov-chain.md) and a [reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md), put $\gamma_*=1-\max_{j\geq2}|\lambda_j|$ and $t_{\mathrm{rel},*}=1/\gamma_*$. This is the reciprocal of the [absolute L2 spectral gap of a reversible Markov chain](absolute-l2-spectral-gap-of-a-reversible-markov-chain.md). It agrees with the reciprocal of the ordinary [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) $1-\lambda_2$ for a [lazy Markov chain](lazy-markov-chain.md), but need not agree when there are negative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
