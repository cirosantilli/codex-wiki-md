<h1 id="su-3-quadratic-casimir-eigenvalue">SU(3) quadratic Casimir eigenvalue</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md)

For generators normalized by $\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf3}(t_at_b)=\delta_{ab}/2$, the [quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md) on the irreducible [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) representation of [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(a,b)$ has the displayed scalar value. In [SU(3) highest-weight coordinates](su-3-highest-weight-coordinates.md) $(p,q)$ it is $p^2+q^2+p+\sqrt3q$.

To derive this, write the operator as $H_1^2+H_2^2+\sum_{i=1}^3(E_+^iE_-^i+E_-^iE_+^i)$. On a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md), the raising operators vanish, and the three root commutators sum to $H_1+\sqrt3H_2$. Centrality and the [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) extend this value to the representation. The singlet, fundamental, adjoint and decuplet values are $0,4/3,3,6$, respectively. Tensor labels and multiplet dimensions are summarized in [the Particle Data Group's SU(n) multiplet guide](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-young-diagrams.pdf).
