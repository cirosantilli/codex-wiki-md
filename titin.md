# Titin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titin)

A large structural [protein](protein-split.md) of muscle whose modular structure and mechanical unfolding are studied by [single-molecule force spectroscopy](single-molecule-force-spectroscopy.md). A worm-like-chain fit alone does not establish that a specimen is titin.
