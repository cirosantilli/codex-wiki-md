# Affineness from a unit-ideal principal affine cover

↑ **Parent:** [Affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md)

A classical [variety](algebraic-variety.md) is affine if finitely many global [regular functions](regular-function.md) generate the [unit ideal](unit-ideal.md) and their nonvanishing opens are affine. [Localization of global sections on a principal open](localization-of-global-sections-on-a-principal-open.md) identifies their [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md) with $A_{f_i}$, where $A=\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$. Choose a finite-type subalgebra of $A$ containing the functions, their unit-ideal coefficients and numerators of generators of every $A_{f_i}$. Its affine variety has the same principal affine charts, whose [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md) glue globally. This avoids assuming finite generation of $A$ before affineness is known.
