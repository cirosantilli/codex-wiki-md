# Smooth upstream tidal-wave admissibility

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For frictional wide-channel [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md), a smooth increasing [wave](wave-split.md) connecting $(h,u)=(1,1)$ to [water depth](water-depth.md) $H>1$ and moving upstream has $c=(H^{3/2}+1)/(H-1)$ and $h'=[h^3-(1+c-ch)^2]/[h^3-(1+c)^2F^2]$. Positivity of the denominator at the upstream endpoint requires $F<1/(1+c)$. For the numerator put $r=\sqrt h$, $R=\sqrt H$: its nontrivial factor is $(r+1)(r-R)(r-R/(R-1))$. The endpoint at $H$ is the first positive downstream zero exactly when $R\leq2$, giving the amplitude restriction. At $H=4$ that zero is double; otherwise the profile cannot cross an intervening equilibrium.
