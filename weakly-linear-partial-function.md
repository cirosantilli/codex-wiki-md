# Weakly linear partial function

↑ **Parent:** [Partial function](partial-function.md)

A [weakly linear partial function](weakly-linear-partial-function.md) on the [real numbers](real-number.md) has a domain closed under rational [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md) and [addition](addition.md), and it preserves [addition](addition.md) on that domain. A nonempty domain is a [vector space over the rational numbers](vector-space-over-the-rational-numbers.md), and additivity implies [rational homogeneity of an additive function](rational-homogeneity-of-an-additive-function.md) by [integer](integer-split.md) [multiplication](multiplication.md) and division. The [empty function](empty-function.md) also satisfies the defining conditions vacuously. This terminology does not assert homogeneity under arbitrary real scalars.
