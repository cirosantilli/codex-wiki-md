# Tolman wormhole

↑ **Parent:** [Conformally flat metric](conformally-flat-metric.md)

For $\lambda>0$, this four-dimensional Euclidean metric has two asymptotically flat ends. Inversion $\rho=\lambda/r$ puts the metric near the $r=0$ end into the same form with $\rho\to\infty$. On $r>0$, its [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) vanishes because $\Delta(r^{-2})=0$, but its [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) is $R_{ij}=4\lambda(\delta_{ij}-4x_ix_j/r^2)/(r^2+\lambda)^2$. It therefore does not satisfy the [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md) unless $\lambda=0$. For negative $\lambda$ the conformal factor vanishes at a finite radius, so that metric is not a smooth wormhole across that sphere.
