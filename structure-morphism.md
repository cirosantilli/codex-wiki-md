# Structure morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The structure morphism of a [scheme](scheme.md) $X$ is its unique morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$. More generally, an $S$-scheme comes equipped with a specified morphism to the base scheme $S$.
