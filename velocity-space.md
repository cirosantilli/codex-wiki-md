# Velocity space

↑ **Parent:** [Phase space](phase-space.md)

Velocity space is the space of velocity vectors at a fixed spatial position. Integrating a [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) over velocity space produces [mass density](density.md); its normalized moments determine mean velocity and [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md).
