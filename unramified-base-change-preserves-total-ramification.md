# Unramified base change preserves total ramification

↑ **Parent:** [Totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md)

If $L/K$ is an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) and $M/K$ is a [totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md) of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md) inside a fixed [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md), then $LM/L$ is totally ramified of degree $[M:K]$. A [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) of $M$ has an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) over $K$. Since a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) of $K$ remains a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) in $L$, that polynomial remains Eisenstein over $L$ and generates $LM$.
