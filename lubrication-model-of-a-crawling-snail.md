# Lubrication model of a crawling snail

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

For a periodic thin mucus gap $h(x)$ translating as a backward wave relative to a force-free foot, put $I_j=\int_0^Lh^{-j}dx$. In the wave frame, the plane and foot have horizontal [velocities](velocity.md) $V-U$ and $V$. A [Couette-Poiseuille flow](couette-poiseuille-flow.md) with periodic [pressure](pressure.md) has flux $q=(V-U/2)I_2/I_3$. Zero net plane [force](force.md) gives the displayed crawling speed. The leading [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) per wavelength is $\Phi=2\mu UVI_1$.
