# Truth-matrix countermodel for a Hilbert axiom set

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix semantics for a propositional proof system](matrix-semantics-for-a-propositional-proof-system.md)

Take ordinary material [logical implication](logical-implication.md) on $\{0,1\}$, designate $1$, and interpret [negation](negation.md) by the constant-zero operation $N$. Then $A\to(B\to A)$ and $(NB\to A)\to(NA\to B)$ are always designated, and [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) and [logical implication](logical-implication.md) discharge preserve consequence. But $(NB\to NA)\to(A\to B)$ is false for $A=1,B=0$. Thus the first two schemes and the deduction rule do not derive the last scheme. This detects the loss of a genuine classical-negation principle even though all the displayed schemes are tautologies under the usual classical [negation](negation.md).
