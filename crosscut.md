# Crosscut

↑ **Parent:** [Simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md)

A crosscut of a planar domain is a simple open arc inside the domain whose two endpoints are distinct boundary points, interpreted as [prime ends](prime-end.md) when necessary. In a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) it divides the domain into two components. In the half-plane, a bounded crosscut joining two real points separates the intervening real interval from infinity; a [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) completing that crosscut swallows this interval.
