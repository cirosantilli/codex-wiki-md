# Generator of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic group](cyclic-group.md)

A [generator of a group](generator-of-a-group.md) is a single element $g$ for which $G=\langle g\rangle$: every [group element](group-element.md) is an [integer](integer.md) power of $g$. A [group](group-split.md) admitting such an element is a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md). In additive notation, every element must be an [integer](integer.md) multiple of $g$.

Distinguish a single [generator of a group](generator-of-a-group.md) from a [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md). The [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) needs two elements to generate it, although it has only four elements; no single element visits all four. A [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) need not have a unique [generator of a group](generator-of-a-group.md): $\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z$ has $1$ and $5$, while the additive [integers](integer.md) have $1$ and $-1$.
