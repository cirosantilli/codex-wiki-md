# Quadratic form of a standard normal vector

↑ **Parent:** [Score test under a simple null](score-test-under-a-simple-null.md)

If $Z\sim N_p(0,I_p)$, then $Z^TZ=\sum_{j=1}^pZ_j^2\sim\chi_p^2$.
