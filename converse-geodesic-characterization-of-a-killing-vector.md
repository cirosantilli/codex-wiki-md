# Converse geodesic characterization of a Killing vector

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector](geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md)

Along an affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md), differentiating the contraction of a [vector](vector.md) field $K$ with its tangent $V$ gives $d(K_aV^a)/ds=V^aV^b\nabla_{(a}K_{b)}$. If this is zero along every [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md), arbitrary timelike initial tangents at each point make the symmetric [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) vanish on the whole [timelike cone](timelike-cone.md). That cone is open, so its [quadratic polynomial](quadratic-polynomial.md) must vanish identically. Thus $K$ is a [Killing vector](killing-vector-field-split.md). The converse follows directly from the same identity. One selected orbit cannot establish the converse; the quantifier over all initial timelike directions is essential.
