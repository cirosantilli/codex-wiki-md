# Prime-to-characteristic geometric torsion

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

If $\ell$ is a prime different from the characteristic, multiplication by $\ell$ on an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) has degree $\ell^2$ and nonzero differential. It is a [separable isogeny](separable-isogeny.md), so its geometric kernel has $\ell^2$ points, all killed by $\ell$. This gives the displayed group over an algebraic closure; the rational subgroup over the base field may be smaller.
