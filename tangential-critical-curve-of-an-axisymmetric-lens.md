# Tangential critical curve of an axisymmetric lens

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational-lensing critical curve](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md)

For circular reduced deflection $\alpha(r)=r\bar\kappa(r)$, a displacement perpendicular to the radial direction is multiplied by $1-\bar\kappa$. Vanishing of this [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) gives a tangential [gravitational-lensing critical curve](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md). Its source position is $\beta=r-\alpha(r)=0$, so its caustic is the origin in the circular model. Near it, images are stretched tangentially into arcs or an [Einstein ring](einstein-ring.md).
