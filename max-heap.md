# Max-heap

↑ **Parent:** [Heap data structure](heap-data-structure.md)

A [heap data structure](heap-data-structure.md) in which every parent key is at least as large as its children's keys. The root therefore stores a largest key, allowing a [priority queue](priority-queue.md) to retrieve a current maximum efficiently.
