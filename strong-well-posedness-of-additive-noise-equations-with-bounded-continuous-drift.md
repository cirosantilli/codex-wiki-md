# Strong well-posedness of additive-noise equations with bounded continuous drift

↑ **Parent:** [Scale transform for an additive-noise diffusion](scale-transform-for-an-additive-noise-diffusion.md)

The [zero extension of a scale diffusion coefficient at finite endpoints](zero-extension-of-a-scale-diffusion-coefficient-at-finite-endpoints.md) makes the transformed equation globally Lipschitz. Inverting its solution gives the original additive-noise equation. The pathwise bound $|X_t|\leq|x|+\sup_{s\leq T}|W_s|+\|b\|_\infty T$ prevents the inverse scale from reaching infinity at any finite time. This proves global strong existence and [pathwise uniqueness](pathwise-uniqueness.md) even when the original drift is not Lipschitz.
