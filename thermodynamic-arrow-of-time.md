# Thermodynamic arrow of time

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

The temporal orientation associated with the typical increase of macroscopic [entropy](entropy-split.md) away from suitable low-entropy conditions. It is a statistical claim about specified initial or boundary conditions and a macroscopic description, rather than a universal monotonicity theorem for every microscopic trajectory. [Time reversal in classical mechanics](time-reversal-in-classical-mechanics.md) pairs suitable increasing trajectories with decreasing ones, while [fine-grained entropy](fine-grained-gibbs-entropy.md) is conserved under regular volume-preserving Hamiltonian flow.
