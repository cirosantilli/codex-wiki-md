# Strict comparison of one-sided and absolute Brownian maxima

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For $t>0$, let $M_t=\sup_{s\leq t}B_s$ and $A_t=\sup_{s\leq t}|B_s|$. The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives $M_t\overset d=|B_t|$, hence $\|M_t\|_2=\sqrt t$. Also $A_t\geq M_t$, with strict inequality on the positive-probability event $\{B_t<-\lambda,M_t<\lambda\}$: reflection gives its probability as $\mathbb P(B_t>\lambda)-\mathbb P(B_t>3\lambda)>0$. The [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md) bounds $\|A_t\|_2\leq2\sqrt t$, so $\sqrt t<\|A_t\|_2\leq2\sqrt t$. At $t=0$ all quantities vanish and the lower inequality is equality.
