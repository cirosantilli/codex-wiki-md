# Three-dimensional reconstruction from serial sections

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscopy](optical-microscopy.md)

Aligned two-dimensional sections constrain the three-dimensional structure of a specimen. For parallel sections of known spacing $\Delta z$, the [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) $\sum_iA_i\Delta z$ approximates volume. Distortion, missing sections and alignment errors constrain the reconstruction; the volume estimate alone does not determine the identity or developmental fate of each visible structure.
