# Spurious wakeup

↑ **Parent:** [Monitor (synchronization)](monitor-synchronization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spurious_wakeup)

A wait returning without the application predicate necessarily becoming true. Rechecking that predicate in a loop also handles the separate possibility that another caller consumed the resource before the awakened caller could acquire the [monitor](monitor-synchronization.md).
