# Nuclear-recoil quenching factor

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md)

The relative light or charge yield of a [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) compared with an [electron recoil](electron-recoil.md) at the same deposited [energy](energy-split.md) in a specified material and field configuration. It connects an electron-equivalent [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) scale with a recoil-energy scale. It can depend on [energy](energy-split.md) and measurement conditions; assuming a constant or extrapolating below [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) changes light-WIMP interpretations. It does not mean [energy](energy-split.md) conservation fails: [energy](energy-split.md) can enter channels not measured by that readout.
