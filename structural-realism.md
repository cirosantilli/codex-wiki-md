# Structural realism

↑ **Parent:** [Structuralism (philosophy of science)](structuralism-philosophy-of-science.md)

A form of [scientific realism](scientific-realism.md) emphasizing warranted knowledge of physical relations or structure despite changes in proposed underlying entities. An account must specify which interpreted relations survive and in which domains, rather than merely point to recurring notation.[discussion of the Fresnel–Maxwell transition](structural-realism-the-best-of-both-worlds-by-john-worrall.md)
