# Shear velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Shear stress](shear-stress.md)

The [shear velocity](shear-velocity.md) is the [velocity](velocity.md) scale $u_*=\sqrt{|\tau|/\rho}$ associated with a [shear stress](shear-stress.md) $\tau$ in a fluid of [mass density](density.md) $\rho$. It is a stress scale, not automatically the actual local fluid [velocity](velocity.md) at a grain.
