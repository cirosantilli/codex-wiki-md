# Frameshift mutation

↑ **Parent:** [Reading frame](reading-frame.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frameshift_mutation)

A [frameshift mutation](frameshift-mutation.md) changes downstream grouping into [codons](codon.md) through insertion or deletion of a number of [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) not divisible by three. In the constructed [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) example, insert C immediately after AUG while retaining the same initiation position: $\mathrm{AUG\;CGC\;UUU\;CUG}\;(\mathrm{A})$. The first [codon](codon.md) is preserved, but the original downstream groups GCU, UUC and UGA disappear. The displayed fragment now has no complete standard [stop codon](stop-codon.md); what happens later depends on its continuation.

The short proof is positional: every downstream [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) moves by $k$ positions after an insertion of length $k$. Boundaries recur every three positions, so they retain the original phase exactly when $k$ is divisible by three. An insertion of three [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) can still change the [peptide](peptide.md), but it does not shift the downstream [reading frame](reading-frame.md). This distinction helps interpret [genetic variants](genetic-variant.md) without predicting a particular [phenotype](phenotype.md) from grouping alone.[§15.1, The Genetic Code, Figure 15.5: nucleotide deletion and shifted grouping](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
