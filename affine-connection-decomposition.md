# Affine connection decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

Relative to a nondegenerate [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), any [affine connection](affine-connection.md) is uniquely the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) plus a [contorsion tensor](contorsion-tensor.md) contribution and a [disformation tensor](disformation-tensor.md) contribution. In derivative-last notation, with $T_{ab}{}^c=\Gamma^c{}_{ab}-\Gamma^c{}_{ba}$ and $N_{ab c}=\nabla_cg_{ab}$, the lowered difference is $A_{ab c}=\tfrac12(T_{ab c}+T_{bc a}-T_{ca b}+N_{ab c}-N_{bc a}-N_{ca b})$. This follows by solving $T_{ab c}=A_{ab c}-A_{ba c}$ and $N_{ab c}=-A_{ac b}-A_{bc a}$. The [difference of affine connections is a tensor](difference-of-affine-connections-is-a-tensor.md).
