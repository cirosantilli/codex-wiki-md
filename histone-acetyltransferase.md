# Histone acetyltransferase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [histone acetyltransferase](histone-acetyltransferase.md) catalyses acetyl-group transfer to histone residues. The resulting changes in charge and reader-protein recruitment can influence chromatin accessibility; the effect depends on the modified site and regulatory context.
