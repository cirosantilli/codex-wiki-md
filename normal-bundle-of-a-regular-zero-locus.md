# Normal bundle of a regular zero locus

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic normal bundle](holomorphic-normal-bundle.md)

The derivative of a section defining a [regular zero locus](regular-zero-locus.md) patches intrinsically along that locus because changes of trivialization introduce derivative terms multiplied by the vanishing section. Its tangent kernel and surjectivity give $N_V\cong E|_V$. Combining this with [adjunction for a smooth submanifold](adjunction-for-a-smooth-submanifold.md) gives $K_V=(K_M\otimes\det E)|_V$.
