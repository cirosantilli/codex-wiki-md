# Reverberation

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverberation)

[Reverberation](reverberation.md) is the persistence of [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md) after a source stops, produced by repeated reflections in an enclosure. A diffuse-room approximation gives an approximately exponential decay of mean acoustic [energy](energy-split.md); an individual [normal mode](normal-mode.md) may instead be described as a damped [resonator](resonator.md).
