# Multiplication excess noise

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-multiplying CCD](electron-multiplying-ccd.md)

Random multiplication increases the noise relative to deterministic gain. If the output from one input electron has mean $g$ and variance $g^2$, a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of input electrons with mean $\mu$ produces output variance $2g^2\mu$, by the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md). The high-gain analog-mode excess-noise factor is $\sqrt2$.
