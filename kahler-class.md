<h1 id="kahler-class">Kähler class</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler cone](kahler-cone.md)

The [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class of a [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) is a Kähler class. Such classes form the [Kähler cone](kahler-cone.md). They have positive integral on every complex curve, and their top self-intersection is positive.
