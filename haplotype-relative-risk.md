# Haplotype-relative-risk

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic association](genetic-association.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplotype-relative-risk)

The haplotype-relative-risk design compares transmitted [alleles](allele.md) to the corresponding untransmitted parental copies, used as internal controls for an affected offspring. If allele-1/allele-2 transmitted counts are $w,x$ and untransmitted counts are $y,z$, the allele-1 [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md) estimate is $wz/(xy)$. An unmatched [Pearson chi-squared test of independence](pearson-chi-squared-test-of-independence.md) treats this as a two-by-two table. The [transmission disequilibrium test](transmission-disequilibrium-test.md) instead keeps parent-specific pairing and uses only discordant transmissions. The sampling assumptions of the unmatched calculation and any missing-parent reconstruction must be justified separately.
