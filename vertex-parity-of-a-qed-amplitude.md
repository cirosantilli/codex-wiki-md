# Vertex parity of a QED amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [QED Feynman rules](qed-feynman-rules.md)

Each elementary [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) vertex has one photon leg, so counting these legs gives $V=2I_\gamma+E_\gamma$. An amplitude with two external photons has even $V$. In particular [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md) has no cubic-order elementary-vertex contribution. With four external legs and $V=4$, a connected diagram has four internal lines and one loop.
