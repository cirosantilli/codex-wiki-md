# Hookean dumbbell conformation equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md)

Multiply the [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md) configuration equation by $R_iR_j$ and integrate twice by parts, with sufficient decay at large separation. The [linear velocity field](linear-velocity-field.md) contributes $KC+CK^T$, the spring drift contributes $-4HC/\zeta$, and [diffusion](diffusion.md) contributes $4k_BTI/\zeta$. Thus the dimensionless [conformation tensor](conformation-tensor.md) $A=HC/(k_BT)$ obeys $\dot A-KA-AK^T=-(A-I)/\lambda$, with [polymer relaxation time](polymer-relaxation-time.md) $\lambda=\zeta/(4H)$. Its left side is an [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md) for this gradient convention.
