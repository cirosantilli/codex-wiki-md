# Dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)

[Dilution](dilution.md) lowers a [solute](solute.md)'s [concentration](concentration.md) by adding [solvent](solvent.md). When the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of that [solute](solute.md) is conserved and $c$ denotes its amount per [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), $c_1V_1=c_2V_2$. This conservation statement does not by itself preserve the [pH](ph.md): a [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) may change as the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) is diluted.
