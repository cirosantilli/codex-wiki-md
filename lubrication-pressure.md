# Lubrication pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

Lubrication pressure is the pressure variation required to drive viscous flow through a thin gap. Its characteristic scale is $P\sim\mu UL/H^2$ for velocity scale $U$, gap thickness $H$, and streamwise length $L$.
