# Wind power

↑ **Parent:** [Renewable energy](renewable-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_power)

Conversion of atmospheric kinetic energy into useful power, commonly by turbines driving generators. Annual energy equals nameplate capacity times [capacity factor](capacity-factor.md) times elapsed time, while the contribution to reliable peak supply is a separate system property. Weather correlation, transmission, flexibility and curtailment affect large-scale integration.
