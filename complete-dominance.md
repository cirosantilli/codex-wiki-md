# Complete dominance

↑ **Parent:** [Dominance (genetics)](dominance-genetics.md)

For a specified characteristic, complete dominance means that the [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) has the same [phenotype](phenotype.md) as one of the two [homozygotes](homozygote.md). It does not mean that the dominant [allele](allele.md) is more common, more advantageous, or transmitted more often. [Penetrance](penetrance.md) specifies the [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) of an expressed [phenotype](phenotype.md) given a [genotype](genotype.md) and is a separate modeling assumption.
