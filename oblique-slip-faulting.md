# Oblique-slip faulting

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

[Fault slip](fault-slip.md) with both dip-slip and strike-slip components. The [focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md) can have an off-centre nodal intersection, with the central polarity indicating its dip-slip sense under a specified shading convention. A reverse component with horizontal shear is compatible with [transpression](transpression.md).
