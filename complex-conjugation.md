# Complex conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md)

Complex conjugation sends a [complex number](complex-number.md) $z=a+ib$ to its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) $\overline z=a-ib$. It is an involution and a [field automorphism](field-automorphism.md) of $\mathbb C$, with fixed field $\mathbb R$. Applied componentwise to a complex vector, it produces the conjugated vector appearing in a [Choi state](choi-state.md) for a rank-one [Kraus operator](kraus-operator.md).
