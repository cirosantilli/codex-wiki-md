# Solvation shell

↑ **Parent:** [Solvation](solvation.md)

A [solvation shell](solvation-shell.md) is the region of [solvent](solvent.md) constituents surrounding a [solute](solute.md), with local arrangements influenced by their interactions. The first [solvation shell](solvation-shell.md) contains the closest neighbors under a stated distance or interaction criterion. A [coordination number](coordination-number.md) counts those neighbors; it requires such a criterion and can fluctuate. Neither a drawn ring nor a few illustrated [molecules](molecule.md) determines a measured [coordination number](coordination-number.md).

In a [liquid](liquid.md), a [solvation shell](solvation-shell.md) is an evolving neighborhood: [solvent](solvent.md) [molecules](molecule.md) reorient and exchange with the surroundings. Different [ions](ion.md) can produce different local dynamics. [Spectroscopic measurement of hydration dynamics](spectroscopic-measurement-of-hydration-dynamics.md) provides an experimental way to distinguish some of those environments without pretending that a shell is a fixed cage.
