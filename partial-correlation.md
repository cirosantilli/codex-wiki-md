# Partial correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Pearson correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md)

The partial [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is the [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) of residuals after linearly projecting each of two variables on the conditioning variables and an intercept. With nonsingular [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), subtracting the two least-squares predictors gives residual [covariance](covariance.md) $\sigma_{12}-\sigma_{13}\sigma_{23}/\sigma_{33}$ and residual [variances](variance-split.md) $\sigma_{ii}-\sigma_{i3}^2/\sigma_{33}$. Dividing gives the displayed formula. For a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), it is also the [conditional correlation](conditional-correlation.md), independent of the conditioning value; this equality need not hold for general distributions.
