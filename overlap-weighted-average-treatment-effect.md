# Overlap-weighted average treatment effect

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional average treatment effect](conditional-average-treatment-effect.md)

The overlap-weighted average treatment effect averages the [conditional average treatment effect](conditional-average-treatment-effect.md) with weight proportional to $e(X)\{1-e(X)\}$, where $e(X)$ is the [propensity score](propensity-score.md). It emphasizes covariate strata in which both treatments are plausible.
