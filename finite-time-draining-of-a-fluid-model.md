# Finite-time draining of a fluid model

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid model](fluid-model.md)

A nonnegative [fluid model](fluid-model.md) drains in finite time if all its coordinates approach zero at a finite terminal time. A ratio-based [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) can become undefined at that endpoint; an absorbing extension is an additional modeling convention.
