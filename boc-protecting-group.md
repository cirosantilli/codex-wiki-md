# Boc protecting group

↑ **Parent:** [Protecting group](protecting-group.md)

A tert-butoxycarbonyl group used to protect an [amine](amine.md) as a carbamate. Acid-promoted cleavage releases [carbon](carbon.md) dioxide and restores the [amine](amine.md).
