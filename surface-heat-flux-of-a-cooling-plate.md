# Surface heat flux of a cooling plate

↑ **Parent:** [Oceanic plate cooling model](oceanic-plate-cooling-model.md)

For a hot initial plate cooled from above, [Fourier's law](fourier-s-law.md) gives the displayed upward [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md). At old ages only the steady value $k\Delta T/a$ remains. At young ages the sum approaches a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md), giving $q_s\sim k\Delta T/\sqrt{\pi\kappa t}$. This divergence is an idealized sudden surface cooling, not an infinite observed ridge [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md).
