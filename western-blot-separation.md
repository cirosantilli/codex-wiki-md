# Western blot separation

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot](western-blot.md)

In the common [SDS-PAGE](sds-page.md) version, [protein denaturation](denaturation-biochemistry.md) and binding of an anionic [surfactant](surfactant.md) reduce differences due to folded shape and intrinsic [electric charge](electric-charge.md). An [electric field](electric-field.md) drives the resulting [protein](protein.md) complexes through the gel; smaller chains generally migrate farther. A lane of reference [proteins](protein.md) supplies positions for estimating apparent [mass](mass.md). This estimate depends on the separation conditions: it is not a direct weighing of a [molecule](molecule.md), and different [proteins](protein.md) can migrate together.
