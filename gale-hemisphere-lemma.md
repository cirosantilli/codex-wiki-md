# Gale hemisphere lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Open hemisphere](open-hemisphere.md)

For integers $n=2k+d$ with $k\geq1$ and $d\geq0$, there are $n$ labelled points of $S^d$ such that every [open hemisphere](open-hemisphere.md) contains at least $k$ of them. The normalized vectors $(-1)^i(1,t_i,\ldots,t_i^d)$ for increasing $t_i$ give a signed [moment curve](moment-curve.md) construction. A nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $d$ cannot have enough sign changes for fewer than $k$ positive alternating sample values. Zero sample values can be perturbed negatively by a [Lagrange interpolation polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md). Labels allow repeated positions when $d=0$.
