# Alpha-linolenic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)

[alpha-linolenic acid](alpha-linolenic-acid.md) is an 18-carbon omega-3 fatty acid with three double bonds. Its presence in conventional plant oils is distinct from substantial synthesis of EPA or DHA.
