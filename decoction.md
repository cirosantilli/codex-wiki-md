# Decoction

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmaceutical preparation](pharmaceutical-preparation.md)

A preparation made by boiling medicinal material in a liquid, followed by the specified separation and volume adjustment. Suitability depends on the [solubility](solubility.md) and heat stability of the desired constituents.
