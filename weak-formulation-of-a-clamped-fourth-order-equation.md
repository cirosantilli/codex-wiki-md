# Weak formulation of a clamped fourth-order equation

↑ **Parent:** [Clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md)

For continuous real coefficients with $p>0$ and $q,r\geq0$ on a bounded interval, use the [clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md) and the displayed [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md). A forcing in $L^2$ defines $\ell(v)=\int fv$. The [weak solution](weak-solution.md) satisfies $a(u,v)=\ell(v)$ for every clamped test vector. Compactly supported [test functions](test-function.md) give $(pu'')''-(qu')'+ru=f$ as a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md); density extends that equation to the energy space. Continuous coefficients alone do not justify differentiating them classically. The [clamped interval derivative norm bound](clamped-interval-derivative-norm-bound.md) gives coercivity, so [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) supplies a unique solution and the quadratic energy has that unique minimum.
