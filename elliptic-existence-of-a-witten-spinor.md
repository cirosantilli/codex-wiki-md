# Elliptic existence of a Witten spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md)

On complete smooth asymptotically flat [spin](spin.md) initial data without inner boundary, satisfying the Einstein constraints and the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md), the [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md) admits a spinor approaching each prescribed asymptotic constant, under the standard weighted decay assumptions. The integrated [Witten-Nester identity](witten-nester-identity.md) gives a coercive estimate and excludes a decaying homogeneous kernel. Weighted elliptic solvability then supplies the desired correction to a spinor extended from infinity. Positivity of the boundary integral is a conditional identity until this analytic existence step is supplied; see [the rigorous analytic proof](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/parker/witten.pdf).
