# Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

[Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence](magnetohydrodynamic-turbulence.md) is irregular nonlinear motion of an electrically conducting fluid coupled to a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). [Alfvén waves](alfven-wave.md) and [magnetosonic waves](magnetosonic-wave.md) give propagation times in addition to the nonlinear mixing time. A strong guide field distinguishes directions parallel and perpendicular to it, so the resulting cascades need not be isotropic.
