# Atomic membership truth lemma for forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Syntactic forcing relation](syntactic-forcing-relation.md)

For [forcing names](forcing-name.md) $\sigma,\tau$ and a [generic filter](generic-filter.md) $G$, $\sigma^G\in\tau^G$ exactly when some $p\in G$ syntactically forces $\sigma\in\tau$. Assuming the equality truth lemma, interpreted membership supplies an active pair $(\rho,r)\in\tau$ and a condition in $G$ forcing $\sigma=\rho$; a common strengthening forces membership. Conversely the defining equality witnesses occur [dense below a forcing condition](dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md) forcing membership. The [dense-below generic meeting lemma](dense-below-generic-meeting-lemma.md) supplies one in $G$, and the equality truth lemma recovers interpreted membership.
