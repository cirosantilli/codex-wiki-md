# Direct summands of flat modules are flat

↑ **Parent:** [Flat module](flat-module.md)

If $F=P\oplus Q$ is a [flat module](flat-module.md), then $P$ and $Q$ are [flat](flat-module.md). For an [injection](injective-function.md) $u:U\hookrightarrow V$, distributivity of the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) identifies $u\otimes F$ with $(u\otimes P)\oplus(u\otimes Q)$. This map is [injective](injective-function.md) by flatness of $F$. Each component is therefore [injective](injective-function.md), proving flatness of both summands.
