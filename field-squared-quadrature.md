# Field-squared quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

Hermitian combinations of pair creation and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). With $K_3=(N+1/2)/2$, their [commutators](commutator.md) are $[K_1,K_2]=-4iK_3$, $[K_2,K_3]=iK_1$ and $[K_3,K_1]=iK_2$. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md) has both [variances](variance-split.md) $\langle N\rangle+1/2$, saturating the corresponding uncertainty product.
