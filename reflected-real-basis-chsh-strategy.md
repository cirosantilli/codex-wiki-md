# Reflected real-basis CHSH strategy

↑ **Parent:** [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md)

For the [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) $|\phi_+\rangle$, let both zero-input measurements use the [computational basis](computational-basis.md), and let the one-input bases be rotated by $\theta$ for Alice and $-\theta$ for Bob in the real [qubit](qubit.md) plane. In the [CHSH game](chsh-game.md), the four conditional winning [probabilities](probability.md) are $1,\cos^2\theta,\cos^2\theta,\sin^2(2\theta)$. They follow by contraction of the [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) with vectors from the real [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md) used for the [basic measurements](basic-measurement.md). Their average is the displayed expression. On $0\leq\theta\leq\pi/2$, this beats $3/4$ exactly when $0<\theta<\pi/4$ and reaches $13/16$ at $\theta=\pi/6$, since $t-t^2=1/4-(t-1/2)^2$.
