# Complex wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Wavenumber](wavenumber.md)

A [complex number](complex-number.md) used as a [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) to encode both spatial oscillation and exponential variation of a [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md). With the convention $e^{i\omega t-iKx}$, a disturbance decaying for increasing $x$ has $\operatorname{Im}K<0$. The sign depends on the convention; [thermal diffusion waves](thermal-diffusion-wave.md) provide a concrete example.
