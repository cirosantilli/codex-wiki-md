<h1 id="telegrapher-s-equations">Telegrapher's equations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegrapher's_equations)

A distributed [transmission line](transmission-line.md) with per-unit-length series resistance $R'$, series [inductance](inductance.md) $L'$, shunt conductance $G'$ and shunt [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C'$ obeys the displayed coupled first-order equations by applying [Kirchhoff's voltage law](kirchhoff-cycle-law.md) and [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) to a short element. With $R'=G'=0$, both voltage and current satisfy a [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) with speed $1/\sqrt{L'C'}$ and [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) $\sqrt{L'/C'}$. The quantities $R',L',G',C'$ are distributed parameters, not a single lumped resistor or [capacitor](capacitor.md).
