# Independence system

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial structure](combinatorial-structure.md)

An [independence system](independence-system.md) consists of a [finite set](finite-set.md) $E$ and a nonempty family $\mathcal I$ of [subsets](subset.md) such that $I\in\mathcal I$ and $J\subseteq I$ imply $J\in\mathcal I$. This is the hereditary condition: deleting chosen elements keeps a selection admissible. In particular, the [empty set](empty-set.md) belongs to $\mathcal I$. An [independence system](independence-system.md) becomes a [matroid](matroid.md) when it also satisfies the [matroid independence axioms](matroid-independence-axioms.md) for exchange. The [hereditary nonmatroid greedy obstruction](hereditary-nonmatroid-greedy-obstruction.md) shows why deletion alone does not justify a [greedy algorithm](greedy-algorithm.md).
