# Saha-Thomson decoupling estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Thomson decoupling rate criterion](thomson-decoupling-rate-criterion.md)

Combining the [hydrogen-only Saha equation](hydrogen-only-saha-equation.md) with the [Thomson decoupling rate criterion](thomson-decoupling-rate-criterion.md) gives an elementary estimate of [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md). With present [baryon](baryon.md) density about $0.25\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$, [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) fraction about $0.75$, present [photon](photon.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) $2.725\ \mathrm K$ and a matter-era normalization $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_m}\simeq38\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, the coupled estimate gives a [temperature](temperature-split.md) near $3100\ \mathrm K$. [Hydrogen](hydrogen.md) chemical equilibrium fails during the actual kinetic recombination history, and the local scattering-rate criterion differs from the maximum of the visibility function; the estimate is not a precision prediction.
