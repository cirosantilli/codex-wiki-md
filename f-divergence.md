# f-divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/f-divergence)

For a [convex function](convex-function.md) $f$ satisfying $f(1)=0$, the f-divergence between [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) $P$ and $Q$ is

$$
D_f(P\Vert Q)=\int qf(p/q)\,d\lambda,
$$

where $p=dP/d\lambda$, $q=dQ/d\lambda$, and the integrand uses the lower-semicontinuous perspective convention at $q=0$.
