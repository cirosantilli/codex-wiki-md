# Weyl sigma matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)

For mostly-minus [metric signature](metric-signature.md), the Weyl sigma matrices are $\sigma^\mu=(I,\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)$ and $\bar\sigma^\mu=(I,-\sigma_1,-\sigma_2,-\sigma_3)$, where $\sigma_i$ are the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). The [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) proves

$$
\sigma^\mu\bar\sigma^\nu+\sigma^\nu\bar\sigma^\mu=2\eta^{\mu\nu}I.
$$

A real [Lorentz four-vector](four-vector.md) is equivalently a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $X=x^0I+x^i\sigma_i$ with $\det X=(x^0)^2-|\mathbf x|^2$. The transformation $X\mapsto MXM^\dagger$ for $M\in\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)$ preserves this [determinant](determinant.md) and gives the [Lorentz spinor double cover](lorentz-spinor-double-cover.md). The indices $\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}$ therefore convert a vector index into one undotted and one dotted [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) index. They are the invariant tensors in the mixed [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) anticommutator $\{Q_\alpha,\bar Q_{\dot\beta}\}=2\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}P_\mu$.
