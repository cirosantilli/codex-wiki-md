# Reflection of a string wave from a freely moving endpoint mass

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md)

For a semi-infinite ideal string on $x\le0$ with [tension](tension.md) $T$ and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$, let a free transverse endpoint [mass](mass.md) be $m$. Use a [time-harmonic wave](time-harmonic-wave.md) $y=\operatorname{Re}\{A(e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx})e^{-i\omega t}\}$, where $k=\omega\sqrt{\mu/T}>0$. The endpoint [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) from [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) is $my_{tt}=-Ty_x$. It gives the title displacement [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md), with [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md) $Z=\sqrt{T\mu}$. Since numerator and denominator have equal moduli, all mean incident [power](power.md) is reflected. The endpoint [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) varies within a cycle, but its mean rate of change is zero. A free [mass](mass.md) changes the reflected [phase](phase-waves.md), and does not by itself provide [dissipation](dissipation.md). The limits $m\to0$ and $m\to\infty$ give $R=+1$ and $R=-1$ respectively at fixed positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md).
