# Spinor sign under a full spatial rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

For a [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md) a spatial rotation acts in each chiral block by a [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) $e^{\pm i\theta\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma/2}$, depending on angle orientation. Since $(\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=I_2$ for a unit axis, the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives this exponential as $\cos(\theta/2)I_2\pm i\sin(\theta/2)\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$. At $\theta=2\pi$ it is $-I_2$, and at $4\pi$ it is $I_2$. A [vector](vector.md) returns already at $2\pi$. This describes the lift of the rotation loop to the [Spin group](spin-group.md), rather than a single-valued representation of the ordinary rotation group on spinors.
