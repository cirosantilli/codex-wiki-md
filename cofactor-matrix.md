# Cofactor matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md)

The [cofactor matrix](cofactor-matrix.md) consists of the signed minors of a [matrix](matrix.md). It is the transpose of the [adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md). The inverse expression applies to invertible matrices; the definition by minors also applies to singular matrices. Cofactors transform oriented hypersurface area vectors in a [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md).
