<h1 id="eilenberg-maclane-space">Eilenberg–MacLane space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy group](homotopy-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eilenberg–MacLane_space)

A connected Eilenberg–MacLane space $K(G,n)$, $n\geq1$, has $\pi_n\cong G$ and all other positive-degree [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) trivial. The group $G$ must be abelian when $n\geq2$, but may be nonabelian when $n=1$. For an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), the [Dold–Kan correspondence](dold-kan-correspondence.md) constructs a simplicial model from the [chain complex](chain-complex.md) concentrated in degree $n$. A [classifying space of a discrete group](classifying-space-of-a-discrete-group.md) gives the general $K(G,1)$ case.
