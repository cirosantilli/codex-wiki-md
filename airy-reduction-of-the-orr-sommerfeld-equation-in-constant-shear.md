# Airy reduction of the Orr-Sommerfeld equation in constant shear

↑ **Parent:** [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](orr-sommerfeld-equation.md)

For nondimensional constant shear $U=y$ and streamwise wavenumber one, put $W=(D^2-1)\widehat v$. The [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](orr-sommerfeld-equation.md) gives $W''=[1+iRe(y-c)]W$. The displayed substitution transforms it into the [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md), because $(e^{i\pi/6})^3=i$. The scale $Re^{-1/3}$ balances shear advection against viscous diffusion.
