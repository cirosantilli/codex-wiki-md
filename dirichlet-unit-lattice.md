# Dirichlet unit lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic embedding of number field units](logarithmic-embedding-of-number-field-units.md)

The logarithms of a fundamental system of $r+s-1$ units form a basis of the hyperplane

$$
H=\{x\in\mathbb R^{r+s}:x_1+\cdots+x_{r+s}=0\},
$$

and their integer span is a full lattice in $H$.
