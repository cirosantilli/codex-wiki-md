# Inertia tensor of a displaced uniform ball

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md)

For a uniform ball of radius $R>0$ and positive [mass](mass.md) $M$, let $G$ be its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md). Spherical symmetry gives the second-moment matrix $\int uu^Tdm=(MR^2/5)I$, so the central [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md) is $(2MR^2/5)I$. The [tensor parallel-axis theorem](tensor-parallel-axis-theorem.md) gives the displayed shifted tensor. For $X\ne0$ its [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) along $X$ has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $2MR^2/5$, while the two-dimensional [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) has repeated eigenvalue $M(2R^2/5+|X|^2)$. Thus it is an [isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md) exactly at $P=G$. Displacement along a chosen axis does not change that axis's moment; the transverse moments increase by $M|X|^2$.
