# Symplectic foliation

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

The [symplectic foliation](symplectic-foliation.md) of a [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md) consists of maximal connected integral manifolds of its Hamiltonian distribution. Each leaf has a nondegenerate closed two-form inverse to the restricted Poisson structure, with compatible sign conventions. Ranks can differ between leaves, making this a singular foliation. Hamiltonian flows remain within a leaf, and [Casimir functions of a Poisson manifold](casimir-function-of-a-poisson-manifold.md) are constant along every leaf; a Casimir level set need not be a single leaf.
