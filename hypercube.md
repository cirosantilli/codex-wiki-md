# Hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercube)

A hypercube is the Cartesian product of $n$ equal-length closed intervals in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). The centered cube $[-1,1]^n$ has $2^n$ vertices, each with coordinates in $\{-1,1\}$. Antipodal vertices determine $2^{n-1}$ body diagonals.
