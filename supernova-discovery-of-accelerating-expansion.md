# Supernova discovery of accelerating expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Accelerating expansion of the universe](accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md)

The 1998 [High-Z Supernova Search Team](high-z-supernova-search-team.md) paper by [Adam Riess](adam-riess.md), [Brian Schmidt](brian-schmidt.md) and collaborators presented [supernova](supernova.md) evidence for acceleration.[Byline and abstract, PDF pp.1–2](observational-evidence-from-supernovae-for-an-accelerating-universe-and-a-cosmological-constant-by-adam-riess-and-others.md)

The 1999 [Supernova Cosmology Project](supernova-cosmology-project.md) paper by [Saul Perlmutter](saul-perlmutter.md) and collaborators independently analysed 42 distant events. [Alexei V. Filippenko](alexei-v-filippenko.md) coauthored both papers.[Byline and abstract, PDF p.1](measurements-of-omega-and-lambda-from-42-high-redshift-supernovae-by-saul-perlmutter-and-others.md)

The [High-Z Supernova Search Team](high-z-supernova-search-team.md) search used the CTIO Blanco 4-m telescope and a $2048\times2048$ [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md), comparing fields across dark runs. Difference images located rising candidates; Keck, MMT and ESO [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) classified them and measured host [redshifts](redshift.md). Follow-up [photometry](photometry-optics.md) used filters approximating emitted B and V bands. Calibrated field stars transferred the [magnitude zero point](magnitude-zero-point.md); late host images removed [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) light, [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md) fits measured brightness, and inserted artificial stars tested recovery.[Sections 2.1–2.2, PDF pp.5–8](observational-evidence-from-supernovae-for-an-accelerating-universe-and-a-cosmological-constant-by-adam-riess-and-others.md)

The [Supernova Cosmology Project](supernova-cosmology-project.md) likewise discovered rising events with CTIO 4-m [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md) imaging, then followed their [light curves](light-curve.md) with several telescopes. Keck LRIS and ESO [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) supplied classification and [redshift](redshift.md), preferably from narrow host lines. Cross-filter [K corrections](k-correction.md) connected received R/I observations to emitted B/V light.[Section 2, PDF pp.2–3: apparatus and corrections](measurements-of-omega-and-lambda-from-42-high-redshift-supernovae-by-saul-perlmutter-and-others.md)

Why does fainter mean accelerating? First it means a larger inferred [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) only at fixed standardized [luminosity](luminosity.md) and corrected attenuation. Next it favours acceleration only after a comparison family is specified: an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), representative propagation of light, common nearby and distant standardization, and controlled [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md) and selection. For the usual [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) models containing nonnegative [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) and a [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the shape of the [Hubble diagram](hubble-diagram.md) constrains $\Omega_{m,0},\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$ and thus $q_0=\Omega_{m,0}/2-\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$. A positive residual relative to one decelerating model rejects that prediction; it does not alone prove $q_0<0$ across arbitrary expansion histories or arbitrary opacity laws.

The essential advance is that a nearby sample fixes an intercept while a range of distant [redshifts](redshift.md) measures the shape. The two teams supplied independent observations and analysis, although common nearby calibrators and [Type Ia supernova](type-ia-supernova.md) physics can produce shared uncertainties. The [dark-energy acceleration criterion](dark-energy-acceleration-criterion.md) explains the interpretation within [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md); the [worked supernova distance comparison](worked-supernova-distance-comparison.md) shows the size and sign of the effect without fitting any survey data.
