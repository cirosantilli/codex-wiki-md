# Product Lagrangian immersion

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian immersion](lagrangian-immersion.md)

If $\gamma_i:L_i\to(M_i,\omega_i)$ are one-dimensional immersed curves in symplectic surfaces, then

$$
\gamma_1\times\gamma_2:L_1\times L_2\longrightarrow(M_1\times M_2,\omega_1\oplus\omega_2)
$$

is a [Lagrangian immersion](lagrangian-immersion.md). A double point of one factor times the other curve becomes a clean self-intersection circle.
