# Transport cost function

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md)

The transport cost [function](function-split.md) assigns a cost to a unit of mass moved from $x$ to $y$. A [transport plan](transport-plan.md) has total cost $\int c\,d\pi$. Costs separating as $a(x)+b(y)$ have the same well-defined total for every coupling of fixed marginals.
