# Dyon

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic monopole](magnetic-monopole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyon)

A dyon carries both electric and magnetic charge. In an appropriate [extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md) theory, a [BPS state](bps-state.md) with these charges has mass fixed by their complex combination in the [central charge in supersymmetry](central-charge-in-supersymmetry.md). Not every electrically and magnetically charged state must saturate that bound.
