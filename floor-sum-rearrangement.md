# Floor-sum rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Floor sum](floor-sum.md)

For an [arithmetic function](arithmetic-function-split.md) $a$, changing the order of the finite [summation](summation.md) gives $\sum_{d=1}^n a(d)\lfloor n/d\rfloor=\sum_{t=1}^n\sum_{d\le\lfloor n/t\rfloor}a(d)$. Both sides count pairs of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $(d,t)$ with $dt\le n$, assigning weight $a(d)$ to each pair. This expresses a weighted [floor sum](floor-sum.md) in terms of a [summatory function](summatory-function.md).
