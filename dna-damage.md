# DNA damage

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

A chemical or structural alteration of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) that can disrupt its normal function or copying. Damage, activation of a damage response and successful repair are separate measurements.
