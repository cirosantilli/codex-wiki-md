# Exchange repulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Intermolecular force](intermolecular-force.md)

Short-range repulsion associated with the antisymmetry requirement for overlapping electronic states, related to the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). It competes with attractive electrostatic and [London dispersion forces](london-dispersion-force.md) at small intermolecular separations.
