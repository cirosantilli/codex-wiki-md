# Parietal lobe

↑ **Parent:** [Cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md)

A cerebral lobe containing sensory and association regions involved in spatial [attention](attention.md), body-related processing and transformations used to guide action. Symptoms depend on subregion, [brain lesion](brain-lesion.md) extent and connections.
