# Prescribed zeros in a plane domain via Runge approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md)

Every [closed discrete subset](closed-discrete-subset.md) of a plane [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) is the zero set of a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md), with assigned finite positive multiplicities if desired. Exhaust the domain by relatively filled compact sets. For a prescribed point outside an earlier compact, choose a rational factor with that zero and its possible pole outside the domain in the same complementary component. A slit joining zero and pole allows a [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md) near the earlier compact. Approximate that logarithm by [Runge theorem](runge-s-theorem.md) and multiply the rational factor by the negative exponential of the approximant. The resulting factors tend to one with summable errors on each compact. Their [infinite product](infinite-product.md) has the prescribed zeros and no additional zeros.
