# Nernst-Einstein relation

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic conductivity](ionic-conductivity.md)

For dilute, independently migrating carriers of number density $n$, charge $q$ and [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, balancing drift with [diffusion](diffusion.md) gives mobility $D/(k_BT)$ per applied force. Multiplying drift velocity by charge density yields $\sigma=nq^2D/(k_BT)$. State which species defines $n$ and $D$: a vacancy diffusivity must be combined with vacancy density. Correlated motion requires a correction rather than automatic use of a tracer diffusivity.
