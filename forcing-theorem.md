# Forcing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Generic extension](generic-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forcing_theorem)

The forcing theorem identifies truth in a [generic extension](generic-extension.md) with the forcing relation: a formula is true in $M[G]$ exactly when some condition in $G$ forces it.
