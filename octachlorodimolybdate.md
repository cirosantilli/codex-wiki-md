# Octachlorodimolybdate

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination complex](coordination-complex.md)

The idealized eclipsed ion $[\mathrm{Mo_2Cl_8}]^{4-}$ has two formally Mo(II), d$^4$ centres and a [quadruple bond](quadruple-bond.md). Each metal has four chloride ligands arranged approximately in a square.
