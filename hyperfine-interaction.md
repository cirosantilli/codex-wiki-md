# Hyperfine interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md)

An interaction between a nucleus's magnetic or electric multipole moments and its electronic surroundings. Magnetic contact and dipolar terms can couple [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) to [electron spin](electron-spin.md) or orbital magnetic moments. The resulting small energy shifts contribute to [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md); the contact term can also mediate [conduction-electron-mediated exchange](conduction-electron-mediated-exchange.md) between nuclear moments in a metal.
