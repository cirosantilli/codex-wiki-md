<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

[Convolution](../../../../convolution.md) preserves [probability measures](../../../../probability-measure.md): its defining pushforward is nonnegative and its total mass is the product of the two unit masses. For every fixed integer $n$ the previous coefficient identity gives

$$
\widehat{\mu_k*\nu_k}(n)=\widehat\mu_k(n)\widehat\nu_k(n)\longrightarrow\widehat\mu(n)\widehat\nu(n)=\widehat{\mu*\nu}(n).
$$

The [probability](../../../../probability.md) version of the Fourier coefficient criterion therefore proves

$$
\boxed{\mu_k*\nu_k\overset{*}{\rightharpoonup}\mu*\nu.}
$$

The fixed compact underlying group and the uniform [probability](../../../../probability.md) masses supply precisely the bounds needed in the dense-test argument.
