↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [complex measure](../../../../complex-measure.md), its [total variation measure](../../../../variation-measure.md) is

$$
|\mu|(E)=\sup\left\{\sum_j|\mu(E_j)|:E=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^mE_j,\ E_j\text{ Borel}\right\}.
$$

Its norm is **$\|\mu\|=|\mu|(\mathbb T)$**. For [Radon measures](../../../../radon-measure.md) on the compact circle this is also $\sup_{\|f\|_\infty\leq1,f\in C(\mathbb T)}|\int f\,d\mu|$. The stated convergence is weak-star convergence against continuous tests:

$$
\boxed{\mu_k\overset{*}{\rightharpoonup}\mu\iff\int f\,d\mu_k\to\int f\,d\mu\quad\text{for every }f\in C(\mathbb T).}
$$

It is the [weak-star topology](../../../../weak-star-topology.md) from the identification of these [measures](../../../../measure-split.md) with the dual of $C(\mathbb T)$, rather than convergence of their values on every [Borel set](../../../../borel-set.md).
