↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [restriction of a measure to a measurable subset](../../../../../restriction-of-a-measure-to-a-measurable-subset.md) is $(\mu|_M)(A)=\mu(A\cap M)$ for every measurable $A$. [Absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md), written $\mu\ll\kappa$, means $\kappa(A)=0\implies\mu(A)=0$. The [measures](../../../../../measure-split.md) are [mutually singular](../../../../../mutually-singular-measures.md) when a measurable $S$ satisfies $\kappa(S)=0$ and $\mu(X\setminus S)=0$. Thus singularity means concentration on a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) of the other [measure](../../../../../measure-split.md); it does not assert that every [measurable set](../../../../../measurable-set.md) is null for one of them.
