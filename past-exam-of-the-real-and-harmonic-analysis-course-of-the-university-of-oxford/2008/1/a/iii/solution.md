<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the finite nonnegative [measures](../../../../../measure-split.md) there exist a unique nonnegative integrable density $h\in L^1(\kappa)$, up to $\kappa$-almost-everywhere equality, and a unique nonnegative [measure](../../../../../measure-split.md) $\mu^s\perp\kappa$, such that

$$
\boxed{\mu(A)=\int_Ah\,d\kappa+\mu^s(A)\quad(A\in\mathfrak M).}
$$

This combines the [Lebesgue decomposition theorem](../../../../../lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md) with the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md). In particular $\mu^a=h\kappa$ and $h=d\mu^a/d\kappa$; if $\mu\ll\kappa$ the singular term is zero.
