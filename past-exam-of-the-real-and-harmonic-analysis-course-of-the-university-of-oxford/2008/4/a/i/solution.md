↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The centered [Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](../../../../../hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md) is

$$
Mf(x)=\sup_{r>0}\frac1{2r}\int_{x-r}^{x+r}|f(y)|dy.
$$

The [Hardy-Littlewood maximal inequality](../../../../../hardy-littlewood-maximal-inequality.md) gives, for example,

$$
\boxed{\lambda(\{Mf>t\})\leq\frac3t\|f\|_1\quad(t>0).}
$$

This valid constant follows from the disjoint-interval covering argument: a compact subset of the superlevel set has a finite cover by intervals of average above $t$; select disjoint intervals whose triples cover it, bound their total length by $\|f\|_1/t$, and use [inner regularity](../../../../../inner-regular-measure.md). An optimal constant is not required.
