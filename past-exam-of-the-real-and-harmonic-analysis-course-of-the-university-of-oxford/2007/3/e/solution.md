<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For each $n\geq0$, choose an open arc $I_n$ about $e^{in}$ of arclength $\pi2^{-n-2}$, and put $G^1=\bigcup_{n\geq0}I_n$. It contains every point used by every empirical [measure](../../../../measure-split.md), whereas

$$
|G^1|\leq\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\pi2^{-n-2}=\frac\pi2<\frac{|\mathbb T|}2.
$$

Thus **$\mu_N^1(G^1)=1$ for all $N$**. Let $K=\mathbb T\setminus G^1$. This is closed and $\mu_N^1(K)=0$ for all $N$. But $1/\pi$ is irrational, so the weak-star limit is normalized arclength and

$$
\boxed{\mu^1(K)=1-\frac{|G^1|}{2\pi}\geq\frac34>0.}
$$

Therefore the proposed implication is **false**. Weak-star convergence tests [continuous functions](../../../../continuous-function.md) and does not assert convergence of the [indicator function](../../../../indicator-function.md) of an arbitrary closed set.
