<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](../../../../riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) identifies the continuous dual of $C(\mathbb T)$ with the finite complex [Radon measures](../../../../radon-measure.md) on $\mathbb T$: every bounded complex-[linear functional](../../../../linear-functional.md) $L$ has a unique representation

$$
\boxed{L(f)=\int_{\mathbb T}f\,d\mu,\qquad \|L\|=|\mu|(\mathbb T).}
$$

The right side is the total variation norm of the [measure](../../../../measure-split.md). On this compact [metric space](../../../../metric-space.md) [Radon measures](../../../../radon-measure.md) are regular Borel [measures](../../../../measure-split.md). A functional is positive precisely when its representing [measure](../../../../measure-split.md) is positive; then $\|L\|=L(1)=\mu(\mathbb T)$.
