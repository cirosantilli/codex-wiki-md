<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $m$ be normalized arclength on the [unit circle](../../../../complex-unit-circle.md), so in angular coordinates $dm=dt/(2\pi)$. It is a finite [Radon measure](../../../../radon-measure.md). The given identity says that $\mu$ and $m$ represent the same [continuous linear functional](../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) on $C(\mathbb T)$. Uniqueness in the [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](../../../../riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mu(E)=m(E)=\frac{|E|}{2\pi}\quad\text{for every Borel }E\subseteq\mathbb T.}
$$

The printed phrase “[Borel sets](../../../../borel-set.md) in $E$” is interpreted as Borel subsets $E$ of the circle.
