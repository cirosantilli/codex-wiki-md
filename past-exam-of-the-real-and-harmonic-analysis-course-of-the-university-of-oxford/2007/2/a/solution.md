An [outer measure](../../../../outer-measure.md) on $X$ is a function $\mu:\mathcal P(X)\to[0,\infty]$ satisfying $\mu(\varnothing)=0$, [monotonicity](../../../../monotonic-function.md), and [countable subadditivity](../../../../countable-subadditivity-of-a-measure.md):

$$
\mu\left(\bigcup_jE_j\right)\leq\sum_j\mu(E_j).
$$

Its [measurable sets](../../../../measurable-set.md) are those satisfying [Caratheodory measurability criterion](../../../../caratheodory-s-criterion.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak M_\mu^*=\{A\subseteq X:\mu(E)=\mu(E\cap A)+\mu(E\setminus A)\text{ for every }E\subseteq X\}.}
$$

The test runs over all subsets $E$, including nonmeasurable ones.
