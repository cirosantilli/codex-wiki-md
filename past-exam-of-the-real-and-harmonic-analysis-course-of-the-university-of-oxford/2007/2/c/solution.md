↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [metric outer measure](../../../../metric-outer-measure.md) on a [metric space](../../../../metric-space.md) $(X,d)$ is an [outer measure](../../../../outer-measure.md) satisfying

$$
\boxed{d(A,B)>0\Longrightarrow\mu(A\cup B)=\mu(A)+\mu(B).}
$$

Here $d(A,B)=\inf\{d(a,b):a\in A,b\in B\}$. Every [Borel set](../../../../borel-set.md) is measurable for a [metric outer measure](../../../../metric-outer-measure.md). Consequently its measurable sigma-algebra contains the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md); it may be strictly larger.
