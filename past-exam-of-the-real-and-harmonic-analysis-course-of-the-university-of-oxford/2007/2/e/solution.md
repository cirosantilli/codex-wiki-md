<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A [regular outer measure](../../../../regular-outer-measure.md) has, for every $E\subseteq X$, a measurable hull $H\supseteq E$ with $\mu(H)=\mu(E)$.

Suppose $\mathcal C$ is an algebra containing $\mathfrak M_\mu^*$ and $\mu$ is finitely additive on $\mathcal C$. Fix $A\in\mathcal C$ and an arbitrary $E$ of finite [outer measure](../../../../outer-measure.md). Take a measurable hull $H$ of $E$. Both $H\cap A$ and $H\setminus A$ belong to $\mathcal C$, so

$$
\mu(E)=\mu(H)=\mu(H\cap A)+\mu(H\setminus A)\geq\mu(E\cap A)+\mu(E\setminus A).
$$

Outer subadditivity gives equality. If $\mu(E)=\infty$, outer subadditivity already forces the sum on the right to be infinite, again giving equality. Therefore $A$ satisfies the [Caratheodory criterion](../../../../caratheodory-s-criterion.md). We have proved $\mathcal C\subseteq\mathfrak M_\mu^*$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathcal C=\mathfrak M_\mu^*.}
$$

This proves the [maximal additivity algebra of a regular outer measure](../../../../maximal-additivity-algebra-of-a-regular-outer-measure.md) without any sigma-finiteness assumption.
