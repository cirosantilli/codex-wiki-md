<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $S\subseteq M_-$, then $\mu^+(S)=0$, so $\mu(S)=-|\mu|(S)\leq0$. Suppose instead that $S\cap M_-=\varnothing$ but $\mu(S)<0$. The preceding construction gives $R\subseteq S$ with $\mu^+(R)=0$ and $|\mu|(R)=-\mu(R)>0$. Then $M_-\cup R$ is $\mu^+$-null with variation strictly larger than that of $M_-$, a contradiction. Hence **$\mu(S)\geq0$ on the complement of $M_-$**. This is a [Hahn decomposition](../../../../../hahn-decomposition.md).

On $M_+=X\setminus M_-$ every partition has nonnegative [signed measures](../../../../../signed-measure.md), so $|\mu|(E)=\mu(E)=\mu^+(E)$ for $E\subseteq M_+$. On $M_-$, $\mu^+$ vanishes. Splitting an arbitrary [measurable set](../../../../../measurable-set.md) between these two sets gives

$$
\boxed{\mu^+(E)=\int_E\mathbf1_{M_+}\,d|\mu|,\qquad \frac{d\mu^+}{d|\mu|}=\mathbf1_{M_+}\quad |\mu|\text{-a.e.}}
$$

In particular $\mu^+\ll|\mu|$, and the exhibited [Radon-Nikodym derivative](../../../../../radon-nikodym-derivative.md) verifies the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md) for this pair directly.
