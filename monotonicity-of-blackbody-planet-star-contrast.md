# Monotonicity of blackbody planet-star contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal eclipse depth](thermal-eclipse-depth.md)

For [blackbodies](blackbody.md) with $T_p<T_s$, the contrast $r_\lambda=(R_p/R_s)^2(e^{x_s}-1)/(e^{x_p}-1)$ increases with wavelength, where $x_i=hc/(\lambda k_BT_i)$. Indeed $d\log r/d\log\lambda=G(x_p)-G(x_s)>0$, with $G(x)=x/(1-e^{-x})$ increasing for $x>0$ because $1-(1+x)e^{-x}>0$. The contrast approaches $(R_p/R_s)^2T_p/T_s$ in the [Rayleigh-Jeans law](rayleigh-jeans-law.md) limit. This behavior differs from the peaked absolute [blackbody](blackbody.md) flux.
