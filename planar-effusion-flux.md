# Planar effusion flux

↑ **Parent:** [Effusion](effusion.md)

For an isotropic two-dimensional [velocity](velocity.md) distribution, a straight opening of width $w$ in a region of number per [area](surface-area.md) $n_2$ loses particles at the displayed rate. The angular integral is $\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos\theta\,d\theta/(2\pi)=1/\pi$. At fixed equilibrium [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) the [speed distribution](speed-distribution.md) scales as $m^{-1/2}$, so the rate has the same scaling. A fixed second moment alone does not determine the first moment without a distribution assumption.
