↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itô_diffusion)

An Itô diffusion solves a stochastic differential equation $dX_t=b(X_t)dt+\sigma(X_t)dB_t$ driven by [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
