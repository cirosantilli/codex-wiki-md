# Uniform finiteness of short geodesic classes

↑ **Parent:** [Length spectrum](length-spectrum.md)

In a compact smooth family of marked closed [hyperbolic surfaces](hyperbolic-surface.md), only finitely many [free homotopy](free-homotopy.md) classes of [closed geodesics](closed-geodesic.md) have length below a fixed bound somewhere in the family. A uniform [bilipschitz equivalence](bilipschitz-equivalence.md) with a fixed metric reduces this to local finiteness of its [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md).
