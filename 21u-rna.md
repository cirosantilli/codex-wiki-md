# 21U RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Piwi-interacting RNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md)

A class of approximately 21-nucleotide [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) piRNAs associated with [PRG-1](prg-1.md). They are not generated by the standard fly ping-pong system.
