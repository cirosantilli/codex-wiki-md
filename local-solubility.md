# Local solubility

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md)

A [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md) is soluble at a place of a [number field](number-field.md) if it has a solution over the corresponding [completion of a valued field](completion-of-a-valued-field.md). It is locally soluble everywhere if this holds at all finite and infinite places. Over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md), these are the real field and all [p-adic fields](p-adic-field.md). Local solubility is necessary for a [rational point](rational-point.md), but the [local-to-global gap in isogeny descent](local-to-global-gap-in-isogeny-descent.md) shows it need not suffice.
