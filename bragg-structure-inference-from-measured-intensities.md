# Bragg structure inference from measured intensities

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray crystallographic structure inference](x-ray-crystallographic-structure-inference.md)

[William Lawrence Bragg](william-lawrence-bragg.md)'s 1913 structure paper connects a slit-selected beam, an oriented crystal and an [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) to strength-versus-angle curves. Repeated [sine](sine.md) ratios and matching [absorption coefficients](absorption-coefficient.md) identify common radiation. Narrower slits resolve blended lines. Absolute [wavelengths](wavelength.md) remain undetermined there; intercrystal comparisons first give relative scales. The [Bragg crystal reflection experiment of 1913](bragg-crystal-reflection-experiment-of-1913.md) covers the alignment controls.[pp. 268–272, especially Fig. 16: angular ionization scans, order/absorption comparison, slit resolution and wavelength-scale limitation](the-structure-of-some-crystals-as-indicated-by-their-diffraction-of-x-rays-by-william-lawrence-bragg.md)

[William Henry Bragg](william-henry-bragg.md)'s 1915 Bakerian lecture, X-rays and Crystal Structure, separates the geometric and intensity arguments. Its Figure 9 compares reflection positions and strengths from three [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) orientations; odd and even [diffraction orders](diffraction-order.md) differ in strength along one orientation. The interpretation uses alternating scattering layers. Later, the [calcite](calcite.md) example identifies an internal [oxygen](oxygen.md) coordinate that [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) alone cannot fix. Improved [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) measurements revise a previously rough estimate. Thus a lattice assignment can precede determination of positions inside the [unit cell](unit-cell.md); intensity interpretation also has experimental and theoretical limits. This is the father's 1915 lecture, distinct from the joint 1913 reflection paper.[W. H. Bragg (1915), p. 253 authorship; pp. 260–261, Fig. 9 and alternating-layer argument; pp. 265–266, calcite coordinate and revised estimate](bakerian-lecture-x-rays-and-crystal-structure-by-william-henry-bragg.md)
