# Thick lens

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)

A [thick lens](thick-lens.md) retains the separation of its refracting surfaces when predicting [image formation](image-formation.md). In the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md) with air on both sides, object and image distances measured from the respective [principal planes](principal-plane.md) satisfy the familiar imaging equation. Measuring instead from one mechanical centre generally introduces an error.[University of Tennessee physics teaching resource, Refracting systems (details), final paragraph: object and image distances must refer to the principal planes](refracting-systems-details-university-of-tennessee-physics-teaching-resource.md)
