# Concentration cell

↑ **Parent:** [Galvanic cell](galvanic-cell.md)

A [galvanic cell](galvanic-cell.md) whose reversible [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) arises from different reactant activities at otherwise corresponding interfaces. The [Nernst equation](nernst-equation.md) relates the measured [voltage](voltage.md) to the activity ratio, provided the transported charge and reaction stoichiometry are specified.
