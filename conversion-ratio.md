# Conversion ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

The ratio of the rate of formation of fissile nuclei to the rate at which the specified fissile inventory is consumed, under stated reaction and accounting conventions. A [conversion ratio](conversion-ratio.md) below one indicates a replacement shortfall in that inventory. It is distinct from neutron multiplication: a reactor's instantaneous neutron balance and its long-term fuel-inventory balance are different questions.
