# Yield criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Plasticity (physics)](plasticity-physics.md)

A [yield criterion](yield-criterion.md) specifies the admissible elastic [stress](stress.md) region, possibly depending on internal hardening variables $q$. Plastic loading occurs on its boundary. The [Tresca yield criterion](tresca-yield-criterion.md) uses maximum principal-[stress](stress.md) difference, while the [Von Mises yield criterion](von-mises-yield-criterion.md) uses a quadratic deviatoric-[stress](stress.md) invariant. Thus a numerical [stress](stress.md) difference must be compared to a [yield stress](yield-stress.md) using the convention of the selected criterion.
