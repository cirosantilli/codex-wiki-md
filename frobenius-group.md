# Frobenius group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group](finite-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_group)

A Frobenius group has a nontrivial proper subgroup $H$ satisfying $H\cap H^g=\{1\}$ for every $g\notin H$, with $H^g=g^{-1}Hg$. Such $H$ is a [Frobenius complement](frobenius-complement.md). The [Frobenius kernel theorem](frobenius-kernel-theorem.md) turns the identity together with elements outside all conjugates of $H$ into a normal subgroup $N$, giving $G=N\rtimes H$.
