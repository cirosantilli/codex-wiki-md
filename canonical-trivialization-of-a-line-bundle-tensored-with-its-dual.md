# Canonical trivialization of a line bundle tensored with its dual

↑ **Parent:** [Dual bundle](dual-bundle.md)

For a real or complex [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$, the evaluation isomorphism $L^*\otimes L\cong\operatorname{End}(L)$ identifies the identity endomorphisms with a nowhere-zero global section. Consequently $L^*\otimes L$ is trivial.
