# Derivative of a continuous bilinear map

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear map](bilinear-map.md)

A continuous bilinear map obeys $\|f(h,k)\|\le C\|h\|\|k\|$. Expanding $f(a+h,b+k)$ leaves the displayed linear term and remainder $f(h,k)$. For product [norm](norm.md) $\rho=(\|h\|^2+\|k\|^2)^{1/2}$, the remainder has [norm](norm.md) at most $C\rho^2/2$, hence is $o(\rho)$. This proves the [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md) formula.
