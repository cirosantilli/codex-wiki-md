# Potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_vorticity)

Potential vorticity combines [vorticity](vorticity.md) with [vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) and [density stratification](density-stratification.md). It also applies to nonrotating stratified flow through [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md). In the inviscid [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md),

$$
q=\frac{f+\zeta}{h},
$$

where $\zeta$ is [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md) and $h$ is layer thickness.
