# Selection assay

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

A [selection assay](selection-assay.md) preferentially retains, amplifies or permits survival of candidates satisfying a criterion. It differs from directly measuring every candidate and can fail if the selectable phenotype becomes detached from the intended biochemical function.
