<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[Spacetime](../../../spacetime.md) geometry gives an acceptable structural explanation of [length contraction](../../../length-contraction.md), provided the relation between material [rods](../../../measuring-rod.md), measurement and the [metric tensor](../../../metric-tensor.md) is included.** It is not a force that squeezes a rod, and the mere existence of a quadratic form cannot guarantee how arbitrary matter behaves. The explanatory issue is whether a common geometrical constraint is sufficient for the phenomenon at issue, or whether a [constructive theory](../../../constructive-theory.md) of the rod is also required.

For an equilibrium [rod](../../../measuring-rod.md) with [proper length](../../../proper-length.md) $L_0$, use its [rest frame](../../../rest-frame.md) $S'$, where its endpoint histories are $x'_A=0$ and $x'_B=L_0$. In a laboratory frame use

$$
x'=\gamma(x-vt),\qquad \gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}.
$$

A laboratory measurement compares endpoints at the same laboratory [time](../../../time-in-physics.md). Subtract the two endpoint equations at $\Delta t=0$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{L=\Delta x=L_0/\gamma.}
$$

The two frames measure different pairs of [spacetime events](../../../event-relativity.md). There is no contradiction in each assigning the other's moving [rods](../../../measuring-rod.md) smaller [lengths](../../../length.md); their [simultaneity](../../../simultaneity.md) relations differ. This is neither an optical distortion nor an invariant compression diagnosed by an attached strain gauge. A photograph also involves unequal emission [times](../../../time-in-physics.md) and propagation of [light](../../../light.md), so it is not this simultaneous endpoint measurement.

The geometrical explanation has genuine unifying content. Once all suitable equilibrium [rods](../../../measuring-rod.md) measure the spatial distances induced by the same [Minkowski metric](../../../minkowski-metric.md), the result is independent of chemical composition. It relates [length contraction](../../../length-contraction.md) to [time dilation](../../../time-dilation.md) and [relativity of simultaneity](../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md), rather than postulating separate mechanisms for every device. A structural explanation need not be a temporal causal story: the result compares descriptions of an existing [world tube](../../../world-tube.md), not successive states of a rod being accelerated.

The main objection asks what warrants the italicized measurement premise. Matter on a mathematical [Minkowski spacetime](../../../minkowski-spacetime.md) could obey equations involving an additional preferred [vector field](../../../vector-field.md), as in [3](../3.md). Such matter need not form devices with the usual transformation behaviour. [Harvey R. Brown](../../../harvey-r-brown.md) and [Oliver Pooley](../../../oliver-pooley.md) argue that a constructive understanding must attend to the [Lorentz-invariant dynamics](../../../lorentz-invariant-dynamics.md) of matter.[§§3–5, critique of assigning constructive explanatory work to bare geometry](../../../minkowski-space-time-a-glorious-non-entity-by-harvey-r-brown-and-oliver-pooley.md) This objection has force against treating a bare [metric tensor](../../../metric-tensor.md) as an independently acting agent. It does not invalidate the endpoint derivation once the physical metric interpretation is established.

Acceleration introduces a different explanatory task. A rod pushed at one end can undergo stresses, heating and oscillations; its final [proper length](../../../proper-length.md) can change. A dynamical calculation is needed to determine whether it settles into an equivalent equilibrium state. The comparison $L=L_0/\gamma$ assumes a specified equilibrium [proper length](../../../proper-length.md), and does not assert that every acceleration history preserves it. Geometry constrains the measurement of that state; material [dynamics](../../../dynamics-mechanics.md) explain its formation and stability. Those explanatory roles can coexist without making either empty.
