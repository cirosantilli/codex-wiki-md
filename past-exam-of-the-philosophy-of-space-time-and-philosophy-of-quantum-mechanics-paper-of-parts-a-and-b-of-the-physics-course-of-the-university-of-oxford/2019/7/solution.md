<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**[Quantum decoherence](../../../quantum-decoherence.md) explains the practical stability of records and suppression of observable [interference](../../../interference-wave-propagation.md); it does not alone select one outcome.** The [measurement problem](../../../measurement-problem.md) arises when universal [unitary time evolution](../../../unitary-time-evolution.md), a complete [wave function](../../../wave-function-split.md) description and a unique definite outcome are all demanded. A theory must explain which commitment is changed or supplemented, not merely replace a pure composite description by a reduced one.

Model an ideal [quantum measurement](../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) and its environment by

$$
(a|0\rangle+b|1\rangle)|A_*\rangle|E_*\rangle
\longmapsto
|\Psi\rangle=a|0,A_0,E_0\rangle+b|1,A_1,E_1\rangle,
\qquad |a|^2+|b|^2=1.
$$

Taking the [partial trace](../../../partial-trace.md) over the environment gives the system–apparatus [reduced density matrix](../../../reduced-density-matrix.md)

$$
\rho_{SA}=|a|^2|0,A_0\rangle\langle0,A_0|
+|b|^2|1,A_1\rangle\langle1,A_1|
+ab^*\langle E_1|E_0\rangle|0,A_0\rangle\langle1,A_1|+\text{adjoint}.
$$

Environmental records make $\langle E_1|E_0\rangle$ very small for macroscopically distinct alternatives. If independent fragments have overlaps $d_k$, their combined overlap is $\prod_kd_k$, explaining the rapid suppression. The apparatus interaction also favours robust records, giving [environment-induced pointer-basis selection](../../../environment-induced-pointer-basis-selection.md) rather than an arbitrary choice of basis.[1981 paper, apparatus–environment coupling and stable pointer records](../../../pointer-basis-of-quantum-apparatus-into-what-mixture-does-the-wave-packet-collapse-by-w-h-zurek.md)

What follows is approximate agreement, for local apparatus [observables](../../../observable.md), with a classical probabilistic mixture. What does not follow is that either term has ceased to exist. Even with orthogonal environmental records, the global [quantum state](../../../quantum-state.md) is a [pure state](../../../pure-state.md) with [quantum entanglement](../../../entangled-state.md). For orthogonal branches $|B_0\rangle,|B_1\rangle$, the global [observable](../../../observable.md) $|B_0\rangle\langle B_1|+|B_1\rangle\langle B_0|$ has expectation $2\operatorname{Re}(a^*b)$ in the superposition and zero in the corresponding incoherent mixture. Its practical inaccessibility is not its mathematical disappearance. The reduced state is an [improper mixed state](../../../improper-mixed-state.md), not proof that an unknown single branch was selected.

Different interpretations use this result differently. In [Bohmian mechanics](../../../de-broglie-bohm-theory.md), disjoint environmental configurations make one occupied branch an effective description of the actual apparatus. In the [many-worlds interpretation](../../../many-worlds-interpretation.md), robust branches supply approximately autonomous relative records, without a unique global outcome. In the [GRW model](../../../ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory.md), a separate stochastic change of the [wave function](../../../wave-function-split.md) selects a dominant component; environmental [decoherence](../../../quantum-decoherence.md) helps explain ordinary records but is not the collapse law. These are different completions of the argument.

Nor does diagonality derive the [Born rule](../../../born-rule.md). Interpreting the diagonal entries as experienced outcome [probabilities](../../../probability.md) requires a probability rule or an interpretive argument. Extremely small overlaps also leave approximations and possible [quantum recoherence](../../../quantum-recoherence.md); a finite controlled environment can in principle be manipulated to reveal the global coherence. The appropriate verdict is that [decoherence](../../../quantum-decoherence.md) resolves much of the preferred-record-basis and effective-classicality problem while leaving the unique-outcome and probability problems dependent on further theory. It is an essential physical mechanism in many accounts, not a self-contained solution by relabelling a [partial trace](../../../partial-trace.md) as collapse.
