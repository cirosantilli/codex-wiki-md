<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen paradox](../../../../einstein-podolsky-rosen-paradox.md) begins with correlations enabling certain predictions about one system from alternative measurements on a separated partner. Its [EPR criterion of reality](../../../../epr-criterion-of-reality.md) promotes certainty without disturbance to a sufficient condition for an element of reality. Together with a locality premise, the alternative position and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) predictions motivate simultaneously real quantities not represented by one standard [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md). The intended conclusion is incompleteness, not a mathematical inconsistency of the predictions.[1935, pp. 777–780, reality criterion and alternative distant predictions](../../../../can-quantum-mechanical-description-of-physical-reality-be-considered-complete-by-albert-einstein-and-others.md)

[Niels Bohr](../../../../niels-bohr.md) challenges the inference from absence of mechanical disturbance to a context-independent assignment of reality. He accepts that there is no mechanical disturbance of the distant system at the relevant stage. His response invokes the experimental conditions defining the possible predictions: arrangements establishing a spatial reference and arrangements enabling a [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) balance are complementary. On this view, changing the local measurement changes the whole phenomenon to which a definite property attribution belongs. It does not remotely kick the other particle. His diaphragm discussion illustrates why specifying the apparatus and its reference frame is part of the physical claim.[1935, pp. 699–700, diaphragm arrangements and the paragraph distinguishing mechanical disturbance from conditions for prediction](../../../../can-quantum-mechanical-description-of-physical-reality-be-considered-complete-by-niels-bohr.md)

A [spin singlet state](../../../../spin-singlet-state.md) gives a clean reconstruction. Its state is

$$
|\Psi^-\rangle=\frac{|0_z1_z\rangle-|1_z0_z\rangle}{\sqrt2}
=-\frac{|0_x1_x\rangle-|1_x0_x\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Here $|0_x\rangle=|+\rangle$ and $|1_x\rangle=|-\rangle$; the minus sign is an irrelevant overall [quantum phase](../../../../quantum-phase.md). Measuring the first [spin](../../../../spin.md) along either axis predicts the other's same-axis outcome with certainty. But the two alternative local [quantum measurements](../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) define different conditional ensembles. Averaging over the unread outcome leaves the distant [reduced density matrix](../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) $I/2$ in both cases. The contrast between conditional prediction and the unchanged distant marginal is [quantum steering](../../../../quantum-steering.md) together with [quantum no-signalling](../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md).

This explains how consistent quantum predictions can coexist with the absence of an operational remote disturbance. It also identifies what a Bohrian rejection of the EPR inference amounts to: certainty in alternative contexts does not license combining the corresponding property attributions into one context-independent account. Merely invoking the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) would not suffice, since EPR specifically argued that locality permits the attribution before any incompatible distant measurements are jointly attempted.

**The reply is satisfactory as an articulation of [quantum complementarity](../../../../complementarity-physics.md), but not a demonstration that the EPR locality premise and completeness are jointly sustainable.** The critic can distinguish a change in our permissible predictions from a change in the distant physical condition. If the latter really is unchanged, why must the alternative predictions not reveal pre-existing properties? Bohr's contextual standard rejects that demand rather than deriving its impossibility from neutral premises. Later [Bell inequalities](../../../../bell-inequality.md) sharpen the issue: [quantum no-signalling](../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md) does not imply [Bell local causality](../../../../bell-local-causality.md). A satisfactory contemporary account should acknowledge this difference, rather than crediting Bohr with a proof of Bell-local completeness or interpreting his reply as faster-than-light mechanical influence.
