<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The familiar result follows if the [force laws](../../../../force-law.md) have [Galilean invariance](../../../../galilean-invariance.md); the three abstract [Newton's laws of motion](../../../../newton-s-laws-of-motion.md) do not guarantee that invariance.** [Isaac Newton](../../../../isaac-newton.md)'s Corollary V correctly identifies a central feature of ordinary [Newtonian mechanics](../../../../newtonian-mechanics-split.md), but its general physical scope requires assumptions about interactions as well as about the relation between [force](../../../../force-split.md) and [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md).[Axioms, Corollary V and the accompanying equal-acceleration argument](../../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)

Take a constant [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf U$ and a [Galilean transformation](../../../../galilean-transformation.md)

$$
\mathbf x_i'=\mathbf x_i-\mathbf U t,\qquad t'=t.
$$

Then $\ddot{\mathbf x}'_i=\ddot{\mathbf x}_i$, $\mathbf x_i'-\mathbf x_j'=\mathbf x_i-\mathbf x_j$, and $\dot{\mathbf x}'_i-\dot{\mathbf x}'_j=\dot{\mathbf x}_i-\dot{\mathbf x}_j$. Any interaction depending only on these relative quantities, with unchanged parameters, supplies the same [forces](../../../../force-split.md) for the transformed initial data. [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) consequently gives the same relative [accelerations](../../../../acceleration.md); uniqueness of the resulting [initial value problem](../../../../initial-value-problem.md) supplies the same relative histories. An inverse-square central interaction is a standard example. [Newton's third law](../../../../newton-s-third-law.md) helps establish the common [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) result, but the boost argument principally needs invariant interactions and the second law.

Here is a counterexample to deduction from the three laws alone. Give two equal [masses](../../../../mass.md) a preferred [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) and write $\mathbf r=\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_2$, $\mathbf V=(\dot{\mathbf x}_1+\dot{\mathbf x}_2)/2$. For fixed $k>0$ and speed scale $u_*>0$, specify

$$
\mathbf F_{12}=-k\left(1+\frac{|\mathbf V|^2}{u_*^2}\right)\mathbf r,
\qquad \mathbf F_{21}=-\mathbf F_{12}.
$$

The [forces](../../../../force-split.md) are equal, opposite and central, the [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md) is inertial, and each body obeys $m\ddot{\mathbf x}_i=\mathbf F_i$. An elementary body with no [force](../../../../force-split.md) is inertial. Nevertheless,

$$
\ddot{\mathbf r}=-\frac{2k}{m}\left(1+\frac{|\mathbf V|^2}{u_*^2}\right)\mathbf r.
$$

Boosting an isolated pair from $\mathbf V=0$ to $\mathbf V=\mathbf U$ changes its internal oscillation frequency. All three motion laws hold, but the proposed universal boost equivalence fails. The extra preferred structure in the [force law](../../../../force-law.md) is what makes the failure possible.

A passive [coordinate transformation](../../../../smooth-transition-map.md) of this same model remains possible: transform the preferred frame's description too. That mathematical freedom does not establish an active [dynamical symmetry](../../../../dynamical-symmetry.md) which changes the physical pair's [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) relative to the preferred structure while holding the laws' background data fixed. Confusing those operations would hide the counterexample.

Finally, a change to rotating coordinates introduces [centrifugal forces](../../../../centrifugal-force.md) and [Coriolis forces](../../../../coriolis-force.md); their dependence on [position](../../../../position.md) or [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) can change internal experiments. Constant translational [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) has no analogous effect when the interactions have [Galilean invariance](../../../../galilean-invariance.md). Corollary V expresses that physical symmetry, with the interaction assumption made explicit, rather than a consequence of action–reaction alone.
