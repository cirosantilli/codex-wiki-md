# Topological ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md)

A [topological ordering](topological-ordering.md) is a [topological order](topological-order.md): a list containing every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) once and placing the source of every [directed edge](directed-edge.md) before its destination. In the parent-and-child language of a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md), every parent precedes every child, and hence every ancestor precedes every descendant along a [directed path](directed-path.md). This is an ordering of the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), whereas [topological sorting](topological-sorting.md) is the task of finding it. See [topological order](topological-order.md) for the precise position condition and [Kahn's algorithm](kahn-s-algorithm.md) for a construction.
