# Optical chopper

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_chopper)

An [optical chopper](optical-chopper.md) periodically interrupts a beam of [light](light.md), for example with a rotating slotted wheel. Its timing can provide a reference for a [lock-in amplifier](lock-in-amplifier.md) measuring the modulated [photodiode](photodiode.md) [signal](signal.md).
