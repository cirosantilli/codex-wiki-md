# F-Brownian bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md)

For a [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$ and a standard [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md) $\mathbb G$, the F-Brownian bridge is the centred [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) $\mathbb G_F=\mathbb G\circ F$. Its [covariance](covariance.md) is $F(\min(s,t))-F(s)F(t)$, by substituting $F(s),F(t)$ into the bridge covariance. It can jump when $F$ jumps. Its law is tight in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), since composition with $F$ is a continuous map from the separable space of continuous bridge paths.
