# Complex matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A [matrix](matrix.md) whose entries are [complex numbers](complex-number.md). For an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of $\mathbb C^n$, [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) and [unitary matrices](unitary-matrix.md) are distinguished by their behavior under the [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md).
