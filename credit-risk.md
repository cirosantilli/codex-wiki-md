# Credit risk

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

Credit risk is the risk of loss through a counterparty's inability or failure to meet a financial obligation, or deterioration in its credit quality. A loss model distinguishes [credit default](credit-default.md) frequency from [loss given default](loss-given-default.md); shared exposures or systematic factors may produce dependence beyond an independent Poisson model.
