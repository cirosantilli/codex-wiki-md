# Maximum laboratory deflection in relativistic elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

A projectile of [mass](mass.md) $m_1$ elastically scattering from a stationary target of [mass](mass.md) $m_2<m_1$ has the displayed maximum laboratory angle. In $c=1$ units, let its incident [energy](energy-split.md) be $E>m_1$ and $s=m_1^2+m_2^2+2m_2E$. Boosting the outgoing [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) from the [center-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) gives $\tan\theta=\sin\varphi/[\Gamma(\cos\varphi+B)]$, with $\Gamma=(E+m_2)/\sqrt s$ and $B=(m_1^2+m_2E)/[m_2(E+m_2)]>1$. The maximum at $\cos\varphi=-1/B$ has $\tan\theta_{\max}=1/[\Gamma\sqrt{B^2-1}]=m_2/\sqrt{m_1^2-m_2^2}$. Equality is allowed; a strict upper bound would exclude a realizable outgoing direction. The angular cap is independent of incident [energy](energy-split.md).
