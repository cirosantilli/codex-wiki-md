<h1 id="kruskal-s-tree-theorem">Kruskal's tree theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruskal's_tree_theorem)

Finite [rooted trees](rooted-tree.md) are [well-quasi-ordered](well-quasi-ordering.md) by [rooted-tree homeomorphic embedding](homeomorphic-embedding-of-a-rooted-tree.md). Consequently every infinite [sequence](sequence.md) contains an earlier tree embedding into a later one. Size-controlled finite forms follow by applying [König infinity lemma](konig-s-lemma.md) to the finitely branching tree of bad prefixes.
