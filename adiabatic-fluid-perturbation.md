# Adiabatic fluid perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian perturbation of a fluid variable](lagrangian-perturbation-of-a-fluid-variable.md)

An adiabatic fluid perturbation preserves the [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) of each displaced parcel. For a [perfect gas](ideal-gas.md), [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\Delta\rho=-\rho\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi$ and the [adiabatic equation of state](adiabatic-equation-of-state.md) gives $\Delta p=\gamma p\Delta\rho/\rho$. The corresponding [Eulerian perturbation of a fluid variable](eulerian-perturbation-of-a-fluid-variable.md) is $\delta p=-\gamma p\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi-\boldsymbol\xi\cdot\nabla p$. An isothermal equilibrium may still have adiabatic perturbations, because the thermodynamic constraint on displaced parcels differs from the equilibrium temperature profile.
