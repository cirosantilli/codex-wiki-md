# Colony (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Organism](organism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_(biology))

A [biological colony](colony-biology.md) is an associated group of [organisms](organism.md) or [biological cells](biological-cell.md). The name alone specifies neither [cell differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md) nor a particular mode of [biological development](biological-development.md). A colony can arise from related descendants or from previously separate members; see [clonal development](clonal-development.md) and the Queller and Strassmann discussion cited under [unicellular organism](unicellular-organism.md).
