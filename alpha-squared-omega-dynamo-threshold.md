# Alpha-squared Omega dynamo threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha-Omega dynamo](alpha-omega-dynamo.md)

For constant coefficients and a transverse wavenumber $\ell\ne0$, a planar [mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md) retaining both alpha feedback and shear has dispersion $(\sigma+\eta p)^2=\alpha^2p+i\alpha\Omega k$, $p=k^2+\ell^2$. Marginal stability gives $\alpha_m^2=4\eta^4p^4/(4\eta^2p^3+\Omega^2k^2)$. Differentiate with respect to $k^2$: an interior minimum solves $4\eta^2p^3+\Omega^2(3k^2-\ell^2)=0$. Its derivative with respect to $k^2$ is positive, so a unique positive minimizing $k^2$ exists only above the displayed shear threshold. Below it the minimum is at $k=0$. The sign of the marginal oscillation frequency is fixed by $2\eta p\omega=\alpha\Omega k$.
