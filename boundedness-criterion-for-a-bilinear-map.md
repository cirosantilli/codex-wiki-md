# Boundedness criterion for a bilinear map

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear map](bilinear-map.md)

A [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) between [normed vector spaces](normed-vector-space-split.md) is jointly continuous exactly when it satisfies the displayed product bound. [Continuity](continuous-function.md) at zero gives a bound on a product of balls; rescale each nonzero input separately to obtain the inequality. Conversely, expand the increment as $F(x+h,y+k)-F(x,y)=F(h,y)+F(x,k)+F(h,k)$ and apply the product bound to every term. Thus [continuity](continuous-function.md) at zero already implies joint [continuity](continuous-function.md) everywhere for a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md).
