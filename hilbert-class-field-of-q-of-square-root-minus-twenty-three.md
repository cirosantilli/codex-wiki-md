# Hilbert class field of Q of square root minus twenty-three

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

The three reduced primitive [binary quadratic forms](binary-quadratic-form.md) of discriminant $-23$ are $(1,1,6)$ and $(2,\pm1,3)$, so the [class number](class-number.md) is three. The displayed cubic has [discriminant](discriminant.md) $-23$ and is irreducible. Its [splitting field](splitting-field.md) has [Galois group](galois-group.md) $S_3$ and is cyclic cubic over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-23})$. Modulo $23$ it factors as $(X-10)^2(X-3)$. Lift the simple factor; the remaining quadratic has discriminant valuation one, so its local [inertia group](inertia-group.md) has order two. Its intersection with $A_3$ is trivial. Thus the relative cyclic cubic extension is everywhere unramified and equals the [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md).
