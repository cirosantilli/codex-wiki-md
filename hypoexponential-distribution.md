# Hypoexponential distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypoexponential_distribution)

A hypoexponential distribution is the distribution of a sum of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) variables with [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md). For two positive unequal rates $\lambda,\mu$, [convolution of independent random variables](convolution-of-independent-random-variables.md) gives the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

$$
f(s)=\frac{\lambda\mu}{\mu-\lambda}(e^{-\lambda s}-e^{-\mu s}),\qquad s\geq0.
$$

It is zero for negative $s$. Its equal-rate limit is the shape-two [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with rate $\lambda$.
