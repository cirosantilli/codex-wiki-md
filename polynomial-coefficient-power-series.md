# Polynomial-coefficient power series

↑ **Parent:** [Radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md)

For a nonzero polynomial $p$, the [power series](power-series.md) $\sum_{n\geq0}p(n)z^n$ has [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) $1$. If $p$ has degree $d$ and leading coefficient $c\ne0$, then $p(n)\sim cn^d$ and $|p(n+1)/p(n)|\to1$, so the [ratio test](ratio-test.md) applies inside the unit disk. At every point of its boundary, the terms fail to tend to zero. The zero polynomial is the exceptional case with [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) $\infty$.
