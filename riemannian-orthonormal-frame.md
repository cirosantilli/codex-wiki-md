# Riemannian orthonormal frame

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

A smooth local basis of [vector fields](vector-field.md) whose [inner products](inner-product.md) under a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) are the identity matrix. Tracing the [Riemannian Hessian](riemannian-hessian.md) in such a frame gives the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md), with the chosen sign.
