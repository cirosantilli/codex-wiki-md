# Localization precision

↑ **Parent:** [Single-molecule fluorescence microscopy](single-molecule-fluorescence-microscopy.md)

Uncertainty in an estimated emitter position. It can be much smaller than the width of its [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md), but it does not by itself guarantee that densely packed emitters or an entire labelled structure are resolved.
