# Baroclinic energy ratio and deformation scale

↑ **Parent:** [Two-layer quasi-geostrophic energy conservation](two-layer-quasi-geostrophic-energy-conservation.md)

Depth-weighted decomposition gives reduced depth $H_r=H_1H_2/(H_1+H_2)$ and internal radius $R_d^2=g\prime H_r/f_0^2$. On scale $l$, the ratio of interface [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md) to baroclinic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is of order $(l/R_d)^2$. Comparable depths recover $l^2f_0^2/(g\prime H_i)$; for strongly unequal depths the thinner layer sets the scale.
