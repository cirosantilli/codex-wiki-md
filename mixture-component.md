# Mixture component

↑ **Parent:** [Finite mixture model](finite-mixture-model.md)

A [mixture component](mixture-component.md) is the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of $X$ given a component label $Z=j$. Its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f_j$ integrates to one; its weighted contribution $\pi_jf_j$ to the [mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md) integrates to the [mixture weight](mixture-weight.md) $\pi_j$. Thus a [mixture component](mixture-component.md) is a distribution, rather than a region assigned to it by a clustering rule.
