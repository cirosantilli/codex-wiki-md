# Correlated random-intercept and random-slope model

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian linear mixed model](gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md)

A subject-specific [random intercept](random-intercept.md) and [random slope](random-slope.md) may have a joint [bivariate normal distribution](bivariate-normal-distribution.md) with arbitrary positive-definite [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $D$. With independent [normal](normal-distribution.md) measurement errors of [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, the marginal [covariance](covariance.md) between a person's readings at $s,t$ is $D_{00}+(s+t)D_{01}+stD_{11}$, plus $\sigma^2$ on the diagonal. Different subjects remain independent. Allowing $D_{01}$ distinguishes this model from an [independent random-intercept and random-slope model](independent-random-intercept-and-random-slope-model.md).
