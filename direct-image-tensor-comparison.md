# Direct-image tensor comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md)

Tensoring sections on $f^{-1}V$ gives a balanced pairing of the two [direct image sheaves](direct-image-sheaf.md) on $V$. The [universal property of the tensor product of modules](universal-property-of-the-tensor-product-of-modules.md) and [sheafification](sheafification.md) produce the displayed morphism. Composing with the [pullback-direct-image adjunction unit](pullback-direct-image-adjunction-unit.md) gives the [projection formula for sheaves](projection-formula.md). The comparison itself need not be an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
