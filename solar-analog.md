# Solar analog

↑ **Parent:** [Star](star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_analog)

A [star](star.md) with broadly Sun-like properties; the range depends on the stated classification criterion. In elementary planetary estimates, a [solar mass](solar-mass.md), [solar radius](solar-radius.md), and solar [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) are common approximate inputs, rather than consequences of the name alone.
