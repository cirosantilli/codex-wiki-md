# Meldable heap

↑ **Parent:** [Heap data structure](heap-data-structure.md)

A [heap data structure](heap-data-structure.md) supporting a meld operation that combines two heaps into one while preserving the [heap order](heap-order.md). The cost of melding distinguishes implementations such as a [leftist heap](leftist-heap.md) from ordinary [binary heaps](binary-heap.md).
