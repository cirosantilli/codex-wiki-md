# Lie superalgebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie superalgebra](lie-superalgebra.md)

A Lie superalgebra representation acts on a parity-graded vector space by linear operators preserving the graded brackets. Odd generators reverse parity, and their brackets are represented by [anticommutators](anticommutator.md).
