# Shifted Poisson data fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson data fidelity](poisson-data-fidelity.md)

For $u\ge0$ and a [positivity-preserving operator](positivity-preserving-linear-operator-on-l1.md), this is the negative [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) for independent [Poisson observations](poisson-observation.md) with intensity $1+Tu$, up to constants depending only on the data and the unit background. For bounded $g\ge0$, its scalar [derivatives](derivative.md) are $1-g/(1+s)$ and $g/(1+s)^2$ on $s=Tu\ge0$. It is [convex](convex-function.md), and [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) in the predicted intensity when $g>0$ almost everywhere. On a unit-area domain, the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives $D_g(u)\ge\|Tu\|_1-\|g\|_\infty\log(1+\|Tu\|_1)$. The shift makes $\log(1+Tu)$ integrable whenever $Tu\in L^1$; no integrability of $\log u$ is needed.
