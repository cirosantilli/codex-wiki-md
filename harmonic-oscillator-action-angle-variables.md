# Harmonic oscillator action-angle variables

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

For a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=p^2/(2m)+m\omega^2q^2/2$, where $m,\omega>0$, define

$$
I=\frac12\left(m\omega q^2+\frac{p^2}{m\omega}\right),\qquad
q=\sqrt{\frac{2I}{m\omega}}\sin\theta,\qquad
p=\sqrt{2Im\omega}\cos\theta.
$$

These [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md) are defined for $I>0$, with a local [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ modulo $2\pi$. A proof of canonicity is $\theta_q=p/(2I)$ and $\theta_p=-q/(2I)$, which give $\{\theta,I\}=1$ using the [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md). Substitution gives $H=\omega I$. [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md) then prove $I(t)=I(0)$ and $\theta(t)=\theta(0)+\omega t$. The [action integral](action-integral.md) around the corresponding [phase space](phase-space.md) [ellipse](ellipse.md) is $\oint p\,dq=2\pi I$, so $I$ is the usual normalized [action variable](action-variable.md). The [angle](angle.md) is undefined at the zero-energy origin.
