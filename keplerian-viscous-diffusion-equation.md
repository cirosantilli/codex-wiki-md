# Keplerian viscous diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Vertically averaged viscous disk equations](vertically-averaged-viscous-disk-equations.md)

Use $l=\sqrt{GM_*r}$ and $\Omega'=-(3/2)\Omega/r$ in the [vertically averaged viscous disk equations](vertically-averaged-viscous-disk-equations.md). The radial drift is $v_r=-3(\Sigma\sqrt r)^{-1}\partial_r(\sqrt r\bar\nu\Sigma)$. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives this nonlinear [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md). It assumes the central rotation law remains fixed and excludes wind/surface [torque](torque.md) terms.
