# Sulfonium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A positively charged three-coordinate [sulfur](sulfur.md) centre. Cyclic sulfonium intermediates can transfer a neighbouring [thioether](thioether.md) attachment or render formerly distinct [carbon](carbon.md) paths equivalent.
