# Two-body orbital energy

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

In the centre-of-mass frame of two Newtonian point masses, total orbital energy is reduced mass times [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md): $E=\mu[|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2/2-GM/r]$. The factor $\mu$ multiplies both kinetic and potential contributions, while $GM$ is the relative gravitational parameter.
