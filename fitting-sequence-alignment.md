# Fitting sequence alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence alignment](sequence-alignment.md)

A [fitting sequence alignment](fitting-sequence-alignment.md) aligns all of a short query to a substring of a longer target, leaving target flanks unpenalized. It differs from unrestricted [local sequence alignment](local-sequence-alignment.md), which may also omit part of the query.
