# Euler product of a Hecke eigenform

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke eigenform](hecke-eigenform.md)

For a normalized weight-$k$ cuspidal [Hecke eigenform](hecke-eigenform.md), the local recurrence gives $\sum_{r\geq0}a_{p^r}X^r=(1-a_pX+p^{k-1}X^2)^{-1}$. Multiplicativity therefore gives $L(f,s)=\prod_p(1-a_pp^{-s}+p^{k-1-2s})^{-1}$ in a right half-plane. Each degree-two local factor packages all the prime-power [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md).
