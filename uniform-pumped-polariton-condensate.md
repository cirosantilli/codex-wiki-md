# Uniform pumped polariton condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton-polariton condensate](exciton-polariton-condensate.md)

For the cubic [complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) with $\alpha,\beta>0$, $\Psi=\sqrt{\alpha/\beta}\,e^{-i\mu t}$ is spatially uniform, with $\mu=g\alpha/\beta-s$. In the [reservoir reduction of a polariton condensate](reservoir-reduction-of-a-polariton-condensate.md), its pump threshold is $P_{\rm th}=\gamma_C\gamma_R/R_R$ and its [number density](number-density.md) is $(\gamma_R/R_R)(1-P_{\rm th}/P)$. The full two-field homogeneous model instead gives $(P-P_{\rm th})/\gamma_C$. These agree near threshold only to the expansion's accuracy. Existence of the homogeneous solution does not imply stability for arbitrary interaction coefficients.
