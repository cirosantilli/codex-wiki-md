# Sleep spindle

↑ **Parent:** [Non-rapid eye movement sleep](non-rapid-eye-movement-sleep.md)

A transient thalamocortical oscillatory event, often around 10–16 Hz in humans, with frequency boundaries depending on the measurement convention. Its timing relative to other events can matter beyond its total count.
