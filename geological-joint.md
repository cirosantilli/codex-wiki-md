# Geological joint

↑ **Parent:** [Structural geology](structural-geology.md)

An opening fracture in [rock](rock-geology.md) with little or no shear displacement across its faces. A [geological joint](geological-joint.md) is approximately normal to the least compressive [principal stress](principal-stress.md) when it forms in a homogeneous medium. It can remain open or become a [mineral vein](mineral-vein.md). Unlike a [fault](geological-fault.md), a [geological joint](geological-joint.md) need not contain crushed [fault rock](fault-rock.md).
