# Plate tectonics

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plate_tectonics)

A mobile-lid mode of planetary evolution in which coherent pieces of the [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) move, form at spreading regions and can recycle through [subduction](subduction.md). [Slab pull](slab-pull.md), [ridge push](ridge-push.md) and mantle flow compete with deformation resistance. It affects heat transport, [volcanism](volcanism.md), [earthquakes](earthquake.md) and the [carbonate-silicate cycle](carbonate-silicate-cycle.md). It is not guaranteed solely by an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md)'s mass.
