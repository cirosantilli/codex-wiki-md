# Electron objective lens

↑ **Parent:** [Electron optics](electron-optics.md)

The principal specimen-adjacent focusing lens of an [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md). Its role depends on the mode: forming a small incident probe in scanned imaging differs from forming an enlarged transmitted image in conventional imaging.[Official glossary entry, objective-lens function](objective-lens-by-jeol.md)
