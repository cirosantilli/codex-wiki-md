# Nuclear charge radius

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md)

The [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) radius of a [nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md), referred to its center. Define its charge-weighted second moment by

$$
\langle r^2\rangle_{\mathrm{ch}}=\frac1{Ze}\int r^2\rho_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathbf r)\,d^3r.
$$

For a spherical [charge density](charge-density.md) with finite fourth moment, insert the [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) $\sin u/u=1-u^2/6+O(u^4)$ into the [nuclear charge form factor](nuclear-charge-form-factor.md):

$$
F(q)=1-\frac{q^2}{6}\langle r^2\rangle_{\mathrm{ch}}+O(q^4),
\qquad
\langle r^2\rangle_{\mathrm{ch}}=-6\left.\frac{dF}{d(q^2)}\right|_{q=0}.
$$

Thus a radius is a low-[momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md) slope, not necessarily the position of a sharp edge. In this real static model,

$$
|F(q)|^2=1-\frac{q^2}{3}\langle r^2\rangle_{\mathrm{ch}}+O(q^4).
$$

The factor of two between the slopes of $F$ and $|F|^2$ matters when interpreting a [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md). A [proton charge radius](proton-charge-radius.md) is the corresponding size concept for the [proton](proton.md); the spatial arrangement of many [nucleons](nucleon.md) introduces an additional scale in a heavier [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md).
