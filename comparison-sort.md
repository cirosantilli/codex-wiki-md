# Comparison sort

↑ **Parent:** [Sorting algorithm](sorting-algorithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_sort)

A [comparison sort](comparison-sort.md) learns the ordering of [sort keys](sort-key.md) only by comparing pairs. The keys are otherwise opaque: their numeric values, digits or encodings cannot choose an [array](array-data-structure.md) index or a branch. It may move complete [sorting records](sorting-record.md) and retain previous answers. [Merge sort](merge-sort.md) and the adjacent-swap [insertion sort](insertion-sort.md) are [comparison sorts](comparison-sort.md); [counting sort](counting-sort.md) and [radix sort](radix-sort.md) exploit additional information.
