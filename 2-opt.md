# 2-opt

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling salesman problem](travelling-salesman-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-opt)

A 2-opt tour move removes two boundary arcs and reverses the intervening block to reconnect one tour. Such moves define a local-search neighbourhood; block reversals of length two include adjacent swaps and hence connect permutation schedules. For directed costs the [asymmetric 2-opt reversal cost](asymmetric-2-opt-reversal-cost.md) must include every reversed internal arc, not just the boundary changes.
