# Risk seeking

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

An increasing convex [utility function](utility-function-split.md) prefers a nondegenerate lottery to its sure mean by the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md). Its [relative risk aversion coefficient](relative-risk-aversion-coefficient.md) is negative when its second derivative is strictly positive. This differs from [risk neutrality](risk-neutrality.md), which corresponds to affine utility.
