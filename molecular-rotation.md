# Molecular rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule.md)

[Molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) changes a [molecule](molecule.md)'s orientation. Its [rotational kinetic energy](rotational-kinetic-energy.md) depends on the arrangement and [masses](mass.md) of its [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). A [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) freezes internal distances to isolate this motion; [rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md) tests the resulting [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue.md) through transitions between [quantum states](quantum-state.md). The [molecular beam electric resonance](molecular-beam-electric-resonance.md) experiment provides a complementary probe of the coupling to an [electric field](electric-field.md).
