# Discrete integrating factor

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-coefficient affine recurrence](variable-coefficient-affine-recurrence.md)

For $x_{n+1}=a_nx_n+b_n$, put $U_0=1$ and $U_n=\prod_{j=1}^na_j$, with $a_j\ne0$. Dividing by this accumulated homogeneous growth gives $z_n=x_n/U_{n-1}$ and $z_{n+1}-z_n=b_n/U_n$. A [telescoping series](telescoping-series.md) then proves

$$
x_{n+1}=U_n\left(x_1+\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{b_j}{U_j}\right).
$$

This is the discrete counterpart of the [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) for a [first-order linear differential equation](first-order-linear-differential-equation.md).
