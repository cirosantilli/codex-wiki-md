# Unit-direction kinetic-energy identity

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)

For a twice differentiable position away from the origin, write $\mathbf r=r\mathbf u$ with radial [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf u$. Its unit-length constraint gives $\mathbf u\cdot\dot{\mathbf u}=0$ and $\mathbf u\cdot\ddot{\mathbf u}=-|\dot{\mathbf u}|^2$, separating radial and directional contributions to speed and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). The identity is kinematic and does not require a particular force law.
