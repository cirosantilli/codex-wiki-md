# Microphone

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic time-of-flight speed measurement](acoustic-time-of-flight-speed-measurement.md)

A microphone is an [acoustic transducer](acoustic-transducer.md) that converts an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) into an electrical [signal](signal.md). In an [acoustic time-of-flight speed measurement](acoustic-time-of-flight-speed-measurement.md), its response affects which recorded feature is identified as arrival.
