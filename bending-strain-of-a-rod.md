# Bending strain of a rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic rod](elastic-rod.md)

For planar [director kinematics of a rod](director-kinematics-of-a-rod.md) measured in reference [arc length](arc-length.md) $S$, define the signed bending strain by $d\mathbf d_3/dS=u\mathbf d_1$. If $s$ is current [arc length](arc-length.md) and $\alpha=ds/dS>0$ is the [stretch ratio](stretch-ratio.md), the signed spatial [curvature](curvature.md) is $u/\alpha$: the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $d\mathbf d_3/ds=(u/\alpha)\mathbf d_1$. Thus bending strain and spatial [curvature](curvature.md) coincide for an [inextensible rod](inextensible-rod.md), but differ in general. An intrinsic value $\hat u$ is the preferred reference-length bending strain in an energy density $B(u-\hat u)^2/2$ per unit reference length.
