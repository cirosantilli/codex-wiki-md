# Conjugate points and indices of round-sphere geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Morse index theorem](morse-index-theorem.md)

On the unit round [sphere](sphere.md) $S^n$, a geodesic of speed $L$ on $[0,1]$ has normal [Jacobi equation](jacobi-equation.md) $y^{\prime\prime}+L^2y=0$. Its conjugate parameters are $j\pi/L$, each with multiplicity $n-1$. If $L/\pi$ is not an integer, its index is $(n-1)\lfloor L/\pi\rfloor$. For $L=m\pi>0$, its index is $(m-1)(n-1)$ and its nullity is $n-1$.
