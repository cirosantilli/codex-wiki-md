# Hybrid dysgenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Transposable element](transposable-element.md)

A cross-dependent syndrome of [germline](germline.md) defects caused in classic [Drosophila](drosophila.md) systems by failure to control inherited [transposable elements](transposable-element.md). Reciprocal crosses can differ because of maternal [piRNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md) inheritance.
