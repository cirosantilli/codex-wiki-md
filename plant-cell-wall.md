# Plant cell wall

↑ **Parent:** [Cell wall](cell-wall.md)

A [plant cell wall](plant-cell-wall.md) provides reinforcement outside the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), with [cellulose](cellulose.md) as a major structural component. It is a composite rather than a solid sheet of pure [cellulose](cellulose.md). Wall construction varies with the kind and developmental state of the [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).
