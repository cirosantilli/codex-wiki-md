# Principle of marginality

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction term](interaction-term.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_marginality)

A hierarchical [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) that contains an [interaction term](interaction-term.md) retains its associated lower-order effects. For two [categorical predictors](regression-factor.md) this means keeping both main effects when keeping their interaction. A nonsignificant averaged main effect may coexist with large opposite effects in different groups. Dropping its lower-order term while retaining the interaction can make the model depend on arbitrary factor coding.
