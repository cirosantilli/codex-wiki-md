# Polytropic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A polytropic flow uses a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md). When $K$ and the [adiabatic exponent](heat-capacity-ratio.md) are constant, a smooth streamline has [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) $h=\gamma K\rho^{\gamma-1}/(\gamma-1)$ and [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_s^2=\gamma K\rho^{\gamma-1}$. Spatially varying entropy can make $K$ depend on streamline; the constant-$K$ spherical wind is a special case.
