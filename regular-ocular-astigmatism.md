# Regular ocular astigmatism

↑ **Parent:** [Astigmatism (eye)](astigmatism-eye.md)

The two principal meridians have different powers and are perpendicular. A paraxial pencil forms two perpendicular line foci at distinct depths. A [cylindrical lens](cylindrical-lens.md) or [toric lens](toric-lens.md) can supply the required meridional correction.[Regular astigmatism](physiology-of-astigmatism-by-the-american-academy-of-ophthalmology.md)
