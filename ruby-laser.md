# Ruby laser

↑ **Parent:** [Three-level laser](three-level-laser.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_laser)

A [ruby laser](ruby-laser.md) uses [chromium](chromium.md) [ions](ion.md) in [ruby](ruby.md) as its amplifying medium. Maiman's report identifies a metastable state fed by optical excitation and red emission near $694.3\,\mathrm{nm}$.[Stimulated Optical Radiation in Ruby, opening and level-scheme paragraphs](stimulated-optical-radiation-in-ruby-by-theodore-harold-maiman.md) The pump stores excitation in the medium; [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) transfers it into the optical field. The simplified [three-level laser rate equation](three-level-laser-rate-equation.md) explains why the stored population and the output pulse evolve together.
