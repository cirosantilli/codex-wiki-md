# Diffusion amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Itô diffusion](ito-diffusion.md)

The coefficient $\sigma$ multiplying the [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) increment in a scalar [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md). The local variance rate is $\sigma^2$, and the second-order coefficient in the backward [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md) is $\sigma^2/2$. This distinguishes the amplitude from the physical [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) convention $D=\sigma^2/2$.
