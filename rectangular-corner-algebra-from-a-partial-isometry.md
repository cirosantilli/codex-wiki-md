# Rectangular corner algebra from a partial isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Partial isometry](partial-isometry.md)

For a nonzero [partial isometry](partial-isometry.md) $u$ in a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md), put $p=uu^*$ and $q=u^*u$. The closed linear space $pAq$ becomes a unital [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) with the displayed operations and inherited [norm](norm.md), with unit $u$. The map $a\mapsto u^*a$ is an isometric _-isomorphism onto the ordinary corner $qAq$, with inverse $b\mapsto ub$. The identities $uu^*a=a$ and $u^*ub=b$ verify the inverse maps, while contractivity of both multiplications gives [isometry](isometry.md). Products and involutions transfer directly, giving all algebra and C_-[norm](norm.md) axioms. This construction turns a rectangular operator corner into a square algebra by using the [partial isometry](partial-isometry.md) as its unit.
