# Interferometric visibility

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interferometric_visibility)

Interferometric visibility measures the contrast of a scanned interference fringe. For normalized paths with amplitudes $\alpha,\beta$ and identical internal states, the output [probability](probability.md) is $1/2+\operatorname{Re}(\alpha^*\beta e^{i\theta})$, so $\mathcal V=2|\alpha\beta|$. A [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) fringe alone determines transverse [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) but not the sign of the population difference.
