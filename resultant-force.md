# Resultant force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

The [vector sum](vector-sum.md) of applied [forces](force-split.md). To replace a force system on a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) by a single [force](force-split.md), its line of action must also reproduce the original [torque](torque.md); a nonzero [force couple](couple-mechanics.md) cannot be replaced by one [force](force-split.md).
