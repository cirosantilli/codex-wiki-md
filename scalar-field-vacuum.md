# Scalar-field vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md)

A scalar-field vacuum is a constant field at a minimum of its [potential energy](potential-energy.md), with the minimum shifted to zero when discussing finite-energy [classical field-theory solitons](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md). Distinct isolated vacua allow a [scalar-field kink](scalar-field-kink.md) to approach different values at the two ends of space.
