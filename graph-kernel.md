# Graph kernel

↑ **Parent:** [2-core of a graph](2-core-of-a-graph.md)

For a connected graph whose [2-core](2-core-of-a-graph.md) contains a vertex of degree at least three, suppress each maximal path with degree-two internal vertices to a single edge, allowing its two endpoints to coincide. The resulting [multigraph](multigraph.md), retaining loops and parallel edges, is its kernel. Suppression preserves [graph excess](graph-excess.md) and leaves [minimum degree](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) at least three. A purely cyclic core is a separate unicyclic case; it has no branching vertex to anchor this construction.
