# Length-preserving directions and principal directions of simple shear

↑ **Parent:** [Simple shear](simple-shear.md)

For $\gamma>0$, direct multiplication gives $|FN|^2-|N|^2=\gamma N_2(2N_1+\gamma N_2)$. Thus length-preserving line directions form the union of two planes in direction space: $N_2=0$ and $2N_1+\gamma N_2=0$. Their intersection is the third coordinate axis. This concerns segment length rather than pointwise fixed lines; a segment can move and turn while keeping its length.

The two nontrivial [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) are $v_\pm=(\sqrt{4+\gamma^2}\pm\gamma)/2$, with $v_+v_-=1$. The maximizing reference direction is proportional to $(1,v_+,0)$. Consequently it tends to $(1,1,0)$ as $\gamma$ tends to zero from above. At zero itself all stretches equal one, so every direction maximizes length; the limit selects a direction from a degenerate endpoint. This follows from the largest eigenvector of the [right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](right-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md).
