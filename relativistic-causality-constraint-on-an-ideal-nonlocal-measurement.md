# Relativistic causality constraint on an ideal nonlocal measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum no-signalling](quantum-no-signalling.md)

The nonselective channel of a hypothetical spacelike nonlocal [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) must not allow a distant input change to alter local output statistics. For a rank-one measurement in the rotated even and odd two-qubit bases with angle $\theta$, Alice's output satisfies

$$
\langle Z_A\rangle_{\rm out}=\cos^2(2\theta)\langle Z_A\rangle_{\rm in}+\sin2\theta\cos2\theta\langle X_AX_B\rangle_{\rm in}.
$$

Bob can change the last correlation using only his local [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) while Alice keeps the input $|+\rangle$. The resulting signal excludes $0<\theta<\pi/4$. Degenerate observables that do not distinguish this basis are not covered by the rank-one argument. Locally recording a jointly encoded result, which is decoded later by [local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md), is compatible with [quantum no-signalling](quantum-no-signalling.md).
