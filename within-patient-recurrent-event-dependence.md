# Within-patient recurrent-event dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)

Repeated episodes from one person share susceptibility, treatment and history, so row-wise [independence](independent-random-variables.md) is generally inappropriate. Patient-clustered [sandwich covariance matrices](sandwich-covariance-matrix.md) estimate uncertainty using whole-patient score contributions under a suitable working model. [Shared frailty models](shared-frailty-model.md) instead model persistent latent heterogeneity. These methods require independent sampling units at the patient level and do not necessarily estimate the same marginal versus conditional effect.
