# Debye-Scherrer camera

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md)

A powder-diffraction camera with photographic film wrapped around a cylindrical specimen axis. [Diffraction](diffraction.md) cones intersect the film in paired curved lines; angular position along the cylindrical film differs from the $R=L\tan2\theta$ relation for a forward flat plate. Back reflections can also be recorded.
