# Maximum entropy of a quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)

On a $d$-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is maximized uniquely by $I/d$. Indeed, [nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy](nonnegativity-of-quantum-relative-entropy.md) gives

$$
0\leq D(\rho\|I/d)=\log_2d-S(\rho),
$$

with equality exactly when $\rho=I/d$.
