# Cyclic logarithmic coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Finite coloring](finite-coloring.md)

For $b>1$ and an [integer](integer.md) $q\geq2$, assign $x\geq1$ the color $\lfloor\log_b x\rfloor\bmod q$. The [floor function](floor-function.md) partitions the domain into half-open geometric bins. If $1<\log_b(y/x)<q-1$, their bin indices differ by an [integer](integer.md) in $\{1,\ldots,q-1\}$, so their colors differ. For example, $b=3/2$ and $q=3$ separates every ratio in $[1.9,2]$. Composing this [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) with another [logarithm](logarithm.md) can obstruct [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) multiplicative configurations whose [logarithms](logarithm.md) have such ratios.
