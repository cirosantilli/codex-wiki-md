# Point-contact transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-contact_transistor)

A [point-contact transistor](point-contact-transistor.md) has two closely spaced rectifying contacts, the emitter and collector, and a third low-[electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) base contact to the [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). The [Bardeen-Brattain point-contact transistor experiment](bardeen-brattain-point-contact-transistor-experiment.md) established amplification using this arrangement. It does not require two fabricated bulk [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md).
