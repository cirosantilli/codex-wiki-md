# Binary tree child slot

↑ **Parent:** [Binary tree](binary-tree.md)

One of the two distinguished positions, left or right, at a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [binary tree](binary-tree.md). Each [binary tree child slot](binary-tree-child-slot.md) is occupied by a child and its [subtree](subtree.md), or is empty. A reference representation can use `null` for an empty [binary tree child slot](binary-tree-child-slot.md). Two slots are capacity for at most two [children in a rooted tree](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md), rather than a requirement for two actual [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
