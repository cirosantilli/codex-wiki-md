# Dirichlet density

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_density)

A set $S$ of prime ideals has Dirichlet density

$$
\delta(S)=\lim_{s\to1^+}
\frac{\sum_{\mathfrak p\in S}N\mathfrak p^{-s}}
{\log(1/(s-1))},
$$

when the limit exists.
