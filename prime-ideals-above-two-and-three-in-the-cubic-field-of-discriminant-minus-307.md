# Prime ideals above two and three in the cubic field of discriminant minus 307

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic field of discriminant minus 307](cubic-field-of-discriminant-minus-307.md)

For $g(X)=X^3-5X^2+11X-12$, the reductions are

$$
\overline g=X(X^2+X-1)\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_3
$$

and

$$
\overline g=X(X^2+X+1)\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2.
$$

Both quadratic factors are irreducible, so

$$
(3)=(3,\beta)(3,\beta^2+\beta-1)
$$

and

$$
(2)=(2,\beta)(2,\beta^2+\beta+1).
$$
