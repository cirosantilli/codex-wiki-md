# Incremental net monetary benefit

↑ **Parent:** [Cost-effectiveness analysis](cost-effectiveness-analysis.md)

At a specified willingness-to-pay threshold $\lambda$, the [incremental net monetary benefit](incremental-net-monetary-benefit.md) is the value of the effect increment minus the cost increment. It avoids dividing by a potentially near-zero or negative effect estimate. For estimates with [variances](variance-split.md) $v_E,v_C$ and [covariance](covariance.md) $v_{CE}$, its estimated [variance](variance-split.md) is $\lambda^2v_E+v_C-2\lambda v_{CE}$. A [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md) therefore gives a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) directly on the net-benefit scale. The economic sign criterion does not require a positive effect increment.
