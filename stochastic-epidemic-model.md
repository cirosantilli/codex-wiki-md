# Stochastic epidemic model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)

A [stochastic epidemic model](stochastic-epidemic-model.md) represents infection and recovery as random events. A [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) can track integer counts, while a [stochastic SIR model](stochastic-sir-model.md) illustrates random fade-out and finite-population outbreak variability that a deterministic [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) does not show.
