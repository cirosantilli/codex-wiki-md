# Zeroth law of thermodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Laws of thermodynamics](laws-of-thermodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeroth_law_of_thermodynamics)

If two systems are each in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) with a third, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other. Equilibrium classes can therefore be labelled by a common [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md). The corresponding [Zeroth law of black-hole mechanics](zeroth-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) states constancy of [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) over an equilibrium horizon; [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md) identifies that quantity with physical temperature up to its universal conversion factor.
