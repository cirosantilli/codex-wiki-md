# Dynamic structure factor

↑ **Parent:** [Correlation function](correlation-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_structure_factor)

The dynamic structure factor is the space-time [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of an equilibrium [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md). It resolves fluctuations by [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and [wavevector](wavevector.md) and is related to the dissipative part of a [retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md) by the [Fluctuation-dissipation theorem](fluctuation-dissipation-theorem.md).
