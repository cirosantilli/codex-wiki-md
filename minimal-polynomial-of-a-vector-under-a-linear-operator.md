# Minimal polynomial of a vector under a linear operator

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md)

For a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$ on an $n$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over a [field](field.md), the polynomials annihilating a fixed vector $v$ form an ideal of $K[X]$. It is nonzero because $v,Tv,\ldots,T^nv$ are linearly dependent. Since $K[X]$ is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md), its unique monic generator $m_v$ exists and has degree at most $n$. It divides $f$ exactly when $f(T)v=0$. For $v=0$ it equals one. Its degree is the dimension of the cyclic subspace generated by $v$: the vectors up to $T^{d-1}v$ are independent by minimality and span an invariant space by the annihilating recurrence.
