# Arithmetized deduction theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Deduction theorem for first-order logic](deduction-theorem-for-first-order-logic.md)

For standard effective proof presentations, the syntactic [deduction theorem](deduction-theorem-for-first-order-logic.md) and its reverse give effective transformations between proofs in a one-sentence extension and proofs of an implication in the base [theory](first-order-theory.md). Arithmetic can verify these transformations, yielding the displayed internal equivalence under the usual representation and proof-predicate hypotheses. It distinguishes internal proof transformations from an unlicensed [reflection](full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md) step taking provability to truth.
