# Geometric sequences with accumulating ratios span c0

↑ **Parent:** [Space of sequences converging to zero](space-of-sequences-converging-to-zero.md)

The [closed linear span](closed-linear-span.md) of these vectors is all of $c_0$. Otherwise the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) gives a nonzero continuous functional annihilating them, represented by $a\in\ell_1$. At the first index $m$ where $a_m\ne0$, divide $\sum_ja_jt_n^j=0$ by $t_n^m$. The remaining tail has magnitude at most $t_n\sum_{j>m}|a_j|\to0$, forcing $a_m=0$, a contradiction. This is an elementary coefficient-isolation argument for [density by annihilators](density-by-annihilators.md).
