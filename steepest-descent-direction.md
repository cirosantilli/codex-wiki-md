# Steepest descent direction

↑ **Parent:** [Descent direction](descent-direction.md)

For a nonzero [gradient](gradient.md), this unit direction minimizes the [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) among Euclidean unit directions. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\nabla f(x)^Tp\geq-\|\nabla f(x)\|_2$, with equality for the displayed direction. The [steepest descent method](gradient-descent-split.md) usually uses the equivalent unnormalized direction $-\nabla f(x)$ and chooses a separate step length. A different choice of norm can change the steepest direction.
