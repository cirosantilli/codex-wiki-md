# Equal-argument beta integral

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function](beta-function.md)

In $B(n,n)=\int_0^1[x(1-x)]^{n-1}dx$, put $v=2x-1$. Then $x(1-x)=(1-v^2)/4$, and [evenness](even-function.md) gives

$$
B(n,n)=2^{2-2n}\int_0^1(1-v^2)^{n-1}dv.
$$

The further substitution $u=v^2$ turns this into $2^{1-2n}B(1/2,n)$. The symmetry of the [Euler beta function](beta-function.md) yields the title identity. This proof works for every positive real $n$, without requiring $n$ to be an integer or using the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md).
