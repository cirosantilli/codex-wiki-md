# Zero location bound from Schwarz-Pick

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md)

If a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $f$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) satisfies $|f|\leq M$ and $f(a)=0$, the [Schwarz-Pick lemma](schwarz-pick-theorem.md) applied to $f/M$ gives $|f(0)|/M\leq|a|$. Equality for a nonzero central value forces a scaled [automorphism of the unit disk](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md). If $f$ extends past the unit circle and $|f(0)|$ is smaller than its minimum boundary modulus, the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) applied to $1/f$ proves that a zero occurs inside the circle.
