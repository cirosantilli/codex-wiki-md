↑ **Parent:** [Untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md)

Beta reduction performs [capture-avoiding substitution](capture-avoiding-substitution.md):

$$
(\lambda x.M)N\longrightarrow_\beta M[x:=N].
$$
