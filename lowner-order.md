<h1 id="lowner-order">Löwner order</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Löwner_order)

For [Hermitian operators](hermitian-operator.md) $A$ and $B$, one writes $A\leq B$ when $B-A$ is positive semidefinite. This partial order is also called the semidefinite order.
