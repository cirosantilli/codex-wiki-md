# Boundary loop

↑ **Parent:** [Based loop](based-loop.md)

A [boundary loop](boundary-loop.md) travels around a boundary [circle](circle.md) of a [topological surface](topological-surface-split.md). To regard it as an element of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) at a [base point](base-point.md) away from the boundary, join that [base point](base-point.md) to the boundary by a [path](continuous-path.md), go around the boundary, and follow the reverse [path](continuous-path.md). Changing that joining [path](continuous-path.md) conjugates the element; reversing the boundary orientation inverts it.
