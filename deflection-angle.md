# Deflection angle

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

For a [trajectory](trajectory.md) with incoming and outgoing straight [asymptotes](asymptote.md), the deflection angle is the [angle](angle.md) between its limiting incoming and outgoing [velocity](velocity.md) directions. For an attractive [hyperbolic orbit](hyperbolic-orbit.md) of [eccentricity](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) $e$ and [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $d$, $\sin(\delta/2)=1/e$ and $\tan(\delta/2)=GM/(dv^2)$, where $v$ is the incoming [speed](speed.md) at infinity. The incoming [velocity](velocity.md) points opposite to the incoming radial direction; ignoring that reversal gives the wrong [angle](angle.md).
