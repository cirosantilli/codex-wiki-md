# Mulliken electronegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md)

[Mulliken electronegativity](mulliken-electronegativity.md) uses both the cost of removing an [electron](electron.md) and the [energy](energy-split.md) released by accepting an [electron](electron.md). For specified isolated-atom states, the common ground-state convention is

$$
\chi_{\rm M}=\frac{I+A_{\rm ea}}{2},\qquad
I=E(X^+)-E(X),\qquad A_{\rm ea}=E(X)-E(X^-).
$$

The free [electron](electron.md)'s [energy](energy-split.md) is the zero reference. Here $I$ is the [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) and $A_{\rm ea}$ is the [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md), positive for exothermic attachment. If attachment is instead tabulated as the signed [energy](energy-split.md) change $\Delta E_{\rm attach}=E(X^-)-E(X)$, the same expression is $(I-\Delta E_{\rm attach})/2$. Stating the sign convention prevents adding the wrong quantities. The 1934 original treated suitable valence states, which are not automatically the ground states used in this example.[A New Electroaffinity Scale; Together with Data on Valence States and on Valence Ionization Potentials and Electron Affinities by Robert S. Mulliken](a-new-electroaffinity-scale-together-with-data-on-valence-states-and-on-valence-ionization-potentials-and-electron-affinities-by-robert-s-mulliken.md)

[Mulliken electronegativity](mulliken-electronegativity.md) has [energy](energy-split.md) units before any additional rescaling. With $I$ and $A_{\rm ea}$ in [electronvolts](electronvolt.md) per [chemical atom](atom-physics.md), $\chi_{\rm M}$ is in [electronvolts](electronvolt.md); with both in $\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$, it is a molar [energy](energy-split.md). For [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), the RSC ground-state inputs are $I=1312.05$ and $A_{\rm ea}=72.769\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$, giving $\chi_{\rm M}=692.4095\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\approx7.176\,\mathrm{eV}$ per [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). For [chlorine](chlorine.md), $I=1251.186$ and $A_{\rm ea}=348.575\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ give $799.8805\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\approx8.290\,\mathrm{eV}$. These are calculated ground-state [Mulliken electronegativities](mulliken-electronegativity.md), not the RSC's tabulated [Pauling electronegativities](pauling-electronegativity.md).[Hydrogen — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/hydrogen-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)[Chlorine — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/chlorine-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)

Both schemes place [chlorine](chlorine.md) above [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) here, but their numerical differences cannot be subtracted from one another or equated. Dividing a molar [Mulliken electronegativity](mulliken-electronegativity.md) by an [energy](energy-split.md) conversion factor changes units; it does not transform it into a [Pauling electronegativity](pauling-electronegativity.md). A further empirical mapping would require its own calibration and stated domain.
