# Limit cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_cycle)

A limit cycle is an isolated [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) of a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md). It is attracting if nearby [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) approach it in forward [time](time-in-physics.md). A continuous family of closed [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) around a [center equilibrium](center-equilibrium.md) does not consist of limit cycles, because its members are not isolated.
