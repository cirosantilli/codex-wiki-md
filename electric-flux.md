# Electric flux

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_flux)

The electric flux through an oriented surface is the surface integral of the normal component of its [electric field](electric-field.md). For a closed surface with outward normal, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) makes the [electric flux](electric-flux.md) equal to enclosed charge divided by $\varepsilon_0$ in SI units. It depends on surface orientation and may be negative.
