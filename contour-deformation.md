# Contour deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy's integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md)

A contour deformation changes the path of a [contour integral](contour-integral.md) through a region where its integrand is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). The [Cauchy integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) preserves the integral when the endpoints are fixed and the connecting boundary terms vanish. Crossing a [pole](pole.md) instead contributes the appropriately oriented [residue](residue.md); a [branch cut](branch-cut.md) limits which deformations are permitted on one analytic sheet.
