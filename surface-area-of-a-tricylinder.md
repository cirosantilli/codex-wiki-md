# Surface area of a tricylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Tricylinder](tricylinder.md)

For three radius-$a$ [circular cylinders](circular-cylinder.md) with mutually perpendicular axes through one point, the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of their common intersection has three congruent [cylindrical surface](cylindrical-surface.md) pieces. On $x^2+y^2=a^2$, the height restriction is $|z|\leq a\min(|\sin\theta|,|\cos\theta|)$. Eight congruent sectors give [surface area](surface-area.md) $8a^2(2-\sqrt2)$ per piece. Pairwise intersections are [curves](curve.md) of zero [surface area](surface-area.md), so the three pieces add.
