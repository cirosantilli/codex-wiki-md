# Semiconductor detector

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor_detector)

Ionizing radiation creates [electron](electron.md)-hole pairs in a depleted [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). An applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) collects their charge, allowing precise energy spectroscopy when charge loss and electronic noise are controlled.
