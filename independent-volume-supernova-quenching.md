# Independent-volume supernova quenching

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md)

Before local expulsion, a linear star-formation law $\psi=\nu\rho_g$ without return gives $\rho_g=\rho_0e^{-\nu t}$. If $q=\gamma E_{\rm SN}$ is the energy supplied per unit stellar mass, accumulated energy is $q\rho_0(1-e^{-\nu t})$. A fixed [binding energy](binding-energy.md) per unit remaining gas mass $B(r)$ gives the threshold $q(e^{\nu t}-1)=B(r)$. This assumes a specified retained gravitational profile and neglects exchange between volumes.
