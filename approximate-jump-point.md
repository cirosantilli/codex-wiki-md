# Approximate jump point

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate mean limit of a function](approximate-mean-limit-of-a-function.md)

A scalar function in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) has an approximate jump at a point when its mean on each of two half-balls approaches a different finite value. The half-balls are determined by a unit normal $\nu_u$. Reversing the normal exchanges the two [BV traces on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md). Their collection is the [jump set of a BV function](jump-set-of-a-bounded-variation-function.md).
