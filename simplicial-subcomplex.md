# Simplicial subcomplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

A simplicial subcomplex $L\subseteq K$ is a subfamily that is itself a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md). In particular, every face of a simplex in $L$ also lies in $L$.
