# Mean-field Ising critical bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field theory of the Ising model](mean-field-theory-of-the-ising-model.md)

For a ferromagnetic [Ising model](ising-model.md) with [coordination number of a lattice](coordination-number-of-a-lattice.md) $z$, its [mean-field self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md) is $M=\tanh(zJM/(k_BT))$. Put $A=T_c/T$. For $A\leq1$, the only solution is zero. For $A>1$, [strict concavity](strict-concavity.md) of $\tanh(AM)-M$ on $M>0$ gives one positive solution and its spin-reversed partner. Expanding $\tanh(AM)=AM-(AM)^3/3+O(M^5)$ gives $M^2=3(A-1)/A^3+O((A-1)^2)$, proving a continuous onset with [order-parameter critical exponent](order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) $\beta=1/2$. The [Bragg-Williams free energy of the Ising model](bragg-williams-free-energy-of-the-ising-model.md) has a positive quartic coefficient at $T_c$, so these are the stable ordered minima rather than a first-order jump.
