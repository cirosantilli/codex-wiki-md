# Brenier theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Knott–Smith optimality criterion](knott-smith-optimality-criterion.md)

For [probability measures](probability-measure.md) on $\mathbb R^d$ with finite second [moments](moment.md) and a source satisfying [absolute continuity of measures](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) with respect to [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), the quadratic [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md) has a unique optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md). It is induced by the [gradient](gradient.md) of a [convex function](convex-function.md), which also uniquely solves the [Monge optimal transport problem](monge-optimal-transport-problem.md) up to a source-null set.
