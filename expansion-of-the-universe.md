# Expansion of the universe

↑ **Parent:** [Scale factor (cosmology)](scale-factor-cosmology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_of_the_universe)

Cosmic expansion is the increase of physical distances between comoving observers as the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) grows. Its instantaneous fractional rate is the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md).
