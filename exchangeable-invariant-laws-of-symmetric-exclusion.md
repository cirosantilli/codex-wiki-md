# Exchangeable invariant laws of symmetric exclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Product self-duality of symmetric exclusion](product-self-duality-of-symmetric-exclusion.md)

If the probability of occupation at every site in a finite set depends only on its cardinality, the law is exchangeable. The [product self-duality of symmetric exclusion](product-self-duality-of-symmetric-exclusion.md) and conservation of the finite dual particle count imply that all these occupation-product moments are unchanged by evolution. [Inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) reconstructs every finite cylinder probability from them, proving invariance. This direct argument does not require a representation theorem for exchangeable laws.
