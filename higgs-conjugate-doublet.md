# Higgs conjugate doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs field](higgs-field.md)

Set $\varepsilon=i\tau_2$, whose [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md) identity is $\varepsilon\tau_i^*=-\tau_i\varepsilon$. Exponentiation gives $\varepsilon U^*=U\varepsilon$ for $U\in SU(2)$. Hence complex conjugating a [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) and multiplying by $\varepsilon$ restores the [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) of $SU(2)$, while reversing [hypercharge](hypercharge.md). A Higgs field of hypercharge $1/2$ yields a conjugate doublet of hypercharge $-1/2$. Its product $\widetilde\phi^\dagger L$ with a lepton doublet $L$ of hypercharge $-1/2$ transforms in the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) of the whole electroweak [gauge group](gauge-group.md). At vacuum $(0,v)^T/\sqrt2$, this composite is $v\nu_L/\sqrt2$.
