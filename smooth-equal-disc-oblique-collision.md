# Smooth equal-disc oblique collision

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

Let one equal-mass disc arrive with [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v$ while the other is stationary, and let $\mathbf n$ be the unit line of centres at contact. A smooth central elastic [impulse](impulse.md) exchanges their normal [velocity](velocity.md) components and leaves tangential components unchanged: $\mathbf v_A'=\mathbf v-(\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n$ and $\mathbf v_B'=(\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n$. The final translational [velocities](velocity.md) are orthogonal when both are nonzero, and no rotation about a disc centre is generated by the normal [impulse](impulse.md). This result concerns nonrelativistic smooth discs; roughness introduces tangential contact dynamics.
