# Maximal p-hook character formula

↑ **Parent:** [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md)

Let $\kappa$ be the [core of a partition](core-of-a-partition.md) $\lambda$ at prime modulus $p$, with quotient component sizes $w_i$ and total weight $w$. Complete $p$-hook removal sequences number $N_p(\lambda)=\binom{w}{w_0,\ldots,w_{p-1}}\prod_if^{\lambda^{(i)}}$. Their signs agree because abacus bead-crossing parities telescope to the [permutation](permutation.md) taking the initial labeled beads to the packed core order. Repeated [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) gives the displayed formula for any [permutation](permutation.md) $\pi$ on the remaining letters.
