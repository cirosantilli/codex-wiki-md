# Clearing denominators relative to an independent module subset

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md)

Let a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) have a finite generating set $S$ and a maximal independent subset $T$. For each $s\notin T$, dependence of $T\cup\{s\}$ gives a nonzero coefficient $a_s$ with $a_ss\in\langle T\rangle$. The product of those coefficients is nonzero and carries the whole [module](module-mathematics.md) into $\langle T\rangle$.
