# Energy decay of a viscously damped harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For a [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) $\ddot y+\gamma\dot y+\omega_0^2y=0$, differentiation of the [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) $E=m(\dot y^2+\omega_0^2y^2)/2$ gives $\dot E=-m\gamma\dot y^2$. This identity is exact. In the weak-damping regime $\gamma\ll\omega_0$, the leading [period average](period-average.md) has equal [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md), so $\overline{\dot y^2}\simeq\overline E/m$ and $\dot{\overline E}\simeq-\gamma\overline E$. Thus the averaged [energy](energy-split.md) has [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md), while the instantaneous [energy](energy-split.md) has a small oscillatory correction. Its derivative vanishes at every turning point, ruling out an exact pointwise nonconstant exponential. The [quality factor](q-factor.md) is $Q\simeq\omega_0/\gamma$ in this approximation.
