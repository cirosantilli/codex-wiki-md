# Entropy power inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy power](entropy-power.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_power_inequality)

For independent $n$-dimensional [random vectors](random-vector.md) with densities and finite [differential entropies](differential-entropy.md), the [entropy power inequality](entropy-power-inequality.md) states

$$
2^{2h(X+Y)/n}\geq2^{2h(X)/n}+2^{2h(Y)/n}.
$$

The sum's [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) is required to be defined; a value $+\infty$ makes the inequality immediate. Equality for finite nondegenerate densities occurs for [Gaussian distributions](normal-distribution.md) with proportional [covariance matrices](covariance-matrix.md).
