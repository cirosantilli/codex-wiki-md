# Saponification of triglycerides

↑ **Parent:** [Saponification](saponification.md)

A [triglyceride](triglyceride.md) contains three [ester](ester.md) linkages joining [glycerol](glycerol.md) to [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)-derived [acyl groups](acyl-group.md). Complete [saponification](saponification.md) therefore consumes three [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) equivalents per [triglyceride](triglyceride.md), yielding one [glycerol](glycerol.md) and three [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md) [carboxylates](carboxylate.md).[Organic Chemistry by John McMurry; §27.2, opening chemical definition and fat hydrolysis equation](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/27-2-soap.md)

Writing the three [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) groups separately allows their compositions to differ:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{C_3H_5[OC(=O)R_1][OC(=O)R_2][OC(=O)R_3]}+3\mathrm{OH^-}\\
&\qquad\longrightarrow\mathrm{C_3H_5(OH)_3}
+\mathrm{R_1COO^-}+\mathrm{R_2COO^-}+\mathrm{R_3COO^-}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the preceding [ester](ester.md) [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) applied three times: each linkage supplies one [carboxylate](carboxylate.md), and each formerly esterified [oxygen](oxygen.md) on the [glycerol](glycerol.md) scaffold gains one [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). The two sides retain the same three $\mathrm R_i$ groups and have net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-3$.

[Soap](soap.md) consists of [sodium](sodium.md) or [potassium](potassium.md) salts of long-chain [fatty acids](fatty-acid.md). Its [carboxylate](carboxylate.md) head is [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md), while its long [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) tail is [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md). This makes it an [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) and a [surfactant](surfactant.md): the tails can associate with grease while the heads remain exposed to [water](water.md), helping disperse grease for rinsing. An [acetate ion](acetate-ion.md) is also a [carboxylate](carboxylate.md), but lacks the long [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) tail that gives [soap](soap.md) this cleaning role.[Organic Chemistry by John McMurry; §27.2, chemical definition and paragraphs on the ionic head, hydrocarbon tail and removal of grease](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/27-2-soap.md)
