# Holomorphic splitting of a twistor patching matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose-Ward correspondence](penrose-ward-correspondence.md)

A bundle trivial on a [twistor line](twistor-line.md) has a global holomorphic [bundle frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) there. Comparing it with the given two patch [bundle frames](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) produces invertible [matrices](matrix.md) $H,\widetilde H$, holomorphic on their respective affine patches, with $F=\widetilde H H^{-1}$. The obstruction to varying such a [bundle frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) locally with space-time is $H^1(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O)^{n^2}=0$, so the splitting factors can be chosen holomorphically in a neighbourhood. If two splittings differ by $H\mapsto Hg$ and $\widetilde H\mapsto\widetilde Hg$, then $g$ is holomorphic on the compact [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) and hence independent of its projective coordinate. It is the space-time gauge freedom.
