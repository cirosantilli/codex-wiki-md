# Homology of a torus with two parallel circles collapsed

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md)

Collapse two distinct parallel circles of a [torus](torus.md) to two separate points. Cutting between the circles gives two annuli, each of which becomes a two-sphere with the two collapsed points as its poles. The resulting [CW complex](cw-complex.md) has two vertices, two edges connecting them, and two two-cells with zero cellular boundary. Its homology is as displayed, with all higher groups zero; it is homotopy equivalent to $S^1\vee S^2\vee S^2$.
