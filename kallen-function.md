<h1 id="kallen-function">Källén function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

The Källén function controls two-body relativistic kinematics. In the rest frame of total invariant mass $\sqrt s$, the magnitude of each final momentum is $\sqrt{\kappa(s,m_1^2,m_2^2)}/(2\sqrt s)$. It also determines the [invariant flux factor](invariant-flux-factor.md) in a two-particle collision.
