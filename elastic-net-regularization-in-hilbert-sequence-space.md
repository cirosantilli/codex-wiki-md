# Elastic net regularization in Hilbert sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic net regularization](elastic-net-regularization.md)

Extend $J$ by infinity outside $\ell^1$. For a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $K:\ell^2\to\ell^2$, the objective $\|Ku-f\|_2^2/2+\alpha J(u)$ is coercive, weakly lower semicontinuous and [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) for $\alpha>0$, so it has a unique minimizer. The quadratic penalty ensures existence and uniqueness even if $K$ has a [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). Its limiting exact solution minimizes $J$ in the data fiber, which need not be the [minimum-norm least-squares solution](minimum-norm-least-squares-solution.md).
