# Left and right translation on a Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

For a [Lie group](lie-group.md) $G$, left and right translation are the [diffeomorphisms](diffeomorphism.md) $L_g(h)=gh$ and $R_g(h)=hg$. They commute: $L_gR_h=R_hL_g$.
