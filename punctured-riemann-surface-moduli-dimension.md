# Punctured Riemann surface moduli dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Moduli space of Riemann surfaces](moduli-space-of-riemann-surfaces.md)

For stable compact [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md) with $n$ distinct labelled marked points, where $2g-2+n>0$, the displayed complex dimension counts independent conformal shapes and positions after conformal automorphisms are removed. On the sphere it is $n-3$, since three points fix a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md). On a torus it is $1+n-1=n$: one shape modulus and $n$ positions, less one translation. For [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g\ge2$, the unmarked shape dimension is $3g-3$ and continuous conformal automorphisms vanish. The shape dimension also follows from the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) applied to [holomorphic quadratic differentials](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md).
