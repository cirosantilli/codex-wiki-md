# Transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically channelled accretion](magnetically-channelled-accretion.md)

The [dipolar flux-tube area](dipolar-flux-tube-area.md) has $A(r)=A_\star(r/R_\star)^3$. Applying [transonic accretion in a power-law tube](transonic-accretion-in-a-power-law-tube.md) with $n=3$ and a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) gives

$$
c_s^2=\frac{2c_0^2}{7-5\gamma},
\qquad
r_s=\frac{GM(7-5\gamma)}{6c_0^2},
\qquad
\rho_s=\rho_0\left(\frac2{7-5\gamma}\right)^{1/(\gamma-1)}.
$$

Here $c_0$ and $\rho_0$ are the reservoir [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) and [mass density](density.md), and $c_s$ is the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) at the [sonic point](sonic-point.md). The [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) is

$$
\dot M=A_\star\left(\frac{r_s}{R_\star}\right)^3\rho_s c_s.
$$

For two equal polar caps of surface angular radius $\theta_\star\ll1$, $A_\star\simeq2\pi R_\star^2\theta_\star^2$. One loaded cap gives half the total rate. The finite-radius [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) exists for $1<\gamma<7/5$, must cross outside the star ($r_s>R_\star$), and requires $\theta_\star^2r_s/R_\star\ll1$ for the sonic region to remain a narrow [flux tube](flux-tube.md).
