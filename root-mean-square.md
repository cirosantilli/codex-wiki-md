# Root mean square

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_mean_square)

The root mean square of a real function or data set is the [square root](square-root.md) of its mean square. A sinusoid of amplitude $a$ has root mean square $|a|/\sqrt2$. For orthogonal or uncorrelated zero-mean components, the squared root mean square of their sum is the sum of their mean squares; this follows by expanding the square and observing that all cross terms average to zero.
