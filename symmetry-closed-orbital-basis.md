# Symmetry-closed orbital basis

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

An orbital set whose span is invariant under every [molecular symmetry operation](molecular-symmetry-operation.md). Completeness here means closure of the chosen representation space; it does not mean that a finite orbital set spans the entire electronic Hilbert space.
