# Electron-phonon Umklapp threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon scattering](electron-phonon-scattering.md)

For a spherical [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) of radius $k_F$ lying inside a zone with shortest [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector magnitude $G>2k_F$, a translated neighbouring Fermi sphere is separated from it by $q_U=G-2k_F$. An electron-phonon [Umklapp scattering](umklapp-scattering.md) process satisfying $\mathbf k+\mathbf q=\mathbf k'+\mathbf G$ therefore needs a phonon wavevector of at least $q_U$. For an approximately linear acoustic dispersion its activation scale is

$$
T_U\sim\frac{\hbar v_s(G-2k_F)}{k_B}.
$$

At $T\ll T_U$ these phonons are sparsely occupied; once they become thermally available, momentum-relaxing scattering can grow rapidly. Normal small-angle scattering and residual disorder need not vanish below this scale, so it is a crossover estimate rather than a sharp universal conductivity threshold.
