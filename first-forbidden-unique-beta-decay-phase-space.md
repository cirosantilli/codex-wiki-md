# First-forbidden unique beta-decay phase space

↑ **Parent:** [Unique forbidden beta decay](unique-forbidden-beta-decay.md)

A first-forbidden unique [beta decay](beta-decay.md) has $|\Delta J|=2$ with a [parity](parity.md) change. Its leading lepton shape factor contains two additional powers of momentum relative to an [allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md). In the massless, no-Coulomb limit, $\int_0^Q E^2(Q-E)^2[E^2+(Q-E)^2]dE=2Q^7/105$. Thus its microscopic reduced nuclear matrix element multiplies a seventh-power phase-space factor, rather than the fifth power in the [Sargent rule](sargent-rule.md). A fifth-power comparison across different forbiddenness classes instead extracts convention-dependent effective strengths.
