# Absolute wave-packet instability

↑ **Parent:** [Spatiotemporal wave-packet stability](spatiotemporal-wave-packet-stability.md)

A localized perturbation has positive asymptotic exponential growth at a fixed observation point in the chosen reference frame. The relevant [spatial pinch point](spatial-pinch-point.md) of the [Briggs-Bers criterion](briggs-bers-criterion.md) has positive imaginary [frequency](frequency.md). Merely finding a growing temporal mode or a formal complex saddle is not sufficient; the saddle must contribute to the localized impulse response.
