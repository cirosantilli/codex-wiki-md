# Calkin algebra

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calkin_algebra)

For an infinite-dimensional complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the Calkin algebra is the unital quotient of the [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) by the closed two-sided ideal of [compact operators](compact-operator-split.md). Its spectrum records invertibility up to compact errors. An operator is [Fredholm](fredholm-operator.md) exactly when its image in this quotient is invertible.
