# Tensor-square decomposition of the defining sp4 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

For the defining four-dimensional representation $V$ of the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sp}_4$, with the short [simple root](simple-root.md) numbered first, its [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is $\omega_1$. Its [tensor square](tensor-square.md) decomposes as

$$
V\otimes V\cong V(2\omega_1)\oplus V(\omega_2)\oplus V(0),
$$

with dimensions $10,5,1$. The first summand is the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md); the other two form the [exterior square](exterior-square.md), split by [symplectic contraction of an exterior square](symplectic-contraction-of-an-exterior-square.md).
