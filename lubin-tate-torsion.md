<h1 id="lubin-tate-torsion">Lubin–Tate torsion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md)

The $\pi^n$-torsion of a [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md) is

$$
\mu_{f,n}=\{x\in\overline{\mathfrak m}: [\pi^n]_f(x)=0\}.
$$

It is a free rank-one module over $\mathcal O_K/\pi^n\mathcal O_K$.
