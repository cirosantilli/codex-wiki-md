# Optical quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

A phase-selected Hermitian field observable. With [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md) $a$ and $[a,a^\dagger]=1$, define $X_1=(a+a^\dagger)/2$ and $X_2=(a-a^\dagger)/(2i)$. Then $[X_1,X_2]=i/2$ and the [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md) has variance $1/4$ in either quadrature. This dimensionless convention gives $X_\vartheta=X_1\cos\vartheta+X_2\sin\vartheta$. [Balanced homodyne detection](balanced-homodyne-detection.md) selects $\vartheta$ through the reference [wave phase](phase-waves.md).
