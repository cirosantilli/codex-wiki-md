# Exponential growth

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential function](exponential-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_growth)

A positive quantity undergoes [exponential growth](exponential-growth.md) at constant rate $r>0$ when $y'=ry$. For $y_0>0$, the [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) is $y(t)=y_0e^{rt}$. Every interval of duration $\Delta t$ multiplies the value by the same factor $e^{r\Delta t}$, independently of its starting value.
