# Chern classes of a tautological-line complement

↑ **Parent:** [Projective bundle](projective-bundle.md)

For the line-projectivization of a rank-$n$ complex bundle, $p^*E=S\oplus S^\perp$. The [Whitney sum formula for Chern classes](whitney-sum-formula-for-chern-classes.md) gives $c_t(S^\perp)=p^*c_t(E)/(1+xt)$ as a formal power series. Comparing coefficients proves the formula. Since $S^\perp$ has rank $n-1$, its degree-$n$ [Chern class](chern-class.md) vanishes and yields the monic projective-bundle relation.
