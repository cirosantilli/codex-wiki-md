# Regular triangle counting lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Regular clique counting lemma](regular-clique-counting-lemma.md)

If three disjoint [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) classes have size $L$, and their three pairs are $\varepsilon$-[regular pairs of vertex sets](regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md) of [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) at least $d$, where $0<\varepsilon\leq d/2$ and $\varepsilon<1/2$, they contain at least the displayed number of transversal [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). All but $2\varepsilon L$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in the first class have at least $(d-\varepsilon)L$ neighbours in both other classes. Regularity between those two neighbour sets supplies the third [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). This strengthens a mere [triangle embedding lemma for regular pairs](triangle-embedding-lemma-for-regular-pairs.md) to a cubic lower count.
