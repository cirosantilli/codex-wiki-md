# Symmetric algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_algebra)

The symmetric algebra is the quotient of $T(V)$ by the ideal generated by $v\otimes w-w\otimes v$. It is graded by [symmetric powers](symmetric-power.md):

$$
S(V)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}S^nV.
$$
