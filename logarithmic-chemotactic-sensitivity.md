# Logarithmic chemotactic sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Keller--Segel model](keller-segel-model.md)

For positive chemical concentration $c$, taking $\chi(c)=\alpha/c$ makes the [chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md) drift velocity $\chi(c)c_x=\alpha\partial_x\log c$. Cells then respond to a relative chemical gradient. This singular response can remain finite where the concentration tends to zero if its logarithmic derivative has a finite limit.
