# Two-sample exact period recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Exact period recovery from a Fourier sample](exact-period-recovery-from-a-fourier-sample.md)

Suppose a function has least period $r\mid N$ and distinct values on distinct period cosets. Two independent [Fourier samples](fourier-sample.md) have the form $c_j=s_jN/r$ with uniform $s_j\in\mathbb Z_r$. The displayed candidate is $r/\gcd(r,s_1,s_2)$, so exact recovery occurs when no prime divisor of $r$ divides both residues. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives probability $\prod_{\ell\mid r}(1-\ell^{-2})\ge1/\zeta(2)=6/\pi^2$, using the [Euler product](euler-product.md). Unlike a single-sample coprimality probability, this bound is uniformly greater than one half. A candidate-period test makes successful recovery heralded when such a test is available.
