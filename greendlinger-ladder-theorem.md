# Greendlinger ladder theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Greendlinger lemma](greendlinger-lemma.md)

A reduced $C'(1/6)$ [disc diagram](disc-diagram.md) is a single vertex, a single closed cell, a [disc diagram ladder](disc-diagram-ladder.md), or contains at least three exposed [disc diagram spurs](disc-diagram-spur.md) and/or [disc diagram shells](disc-diagram-shell.md) with at most three interior pieces. This is the strong diagram form of the [Greendlinger lemma](greendlinger-lemma.md). It follows from the combinatorial curvature inequality after suppressing valence-two vertices: if there are not three positively curved exposed features, all remaining blocks must occur in a chain. In a nontrivial multi-block ladder its two ends are exposed features. Marking two boundary corners and excluding shells/spurs in the side interiors therefore forces the ladder alternative. This is the width-one case of [McCammond and Wise, Theorem 9.4](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~jon.mccammond/papers/fanladder.pdf); reduced $C'(1/6)$ diagrams satisfy its arc-reduced $C(6)$–$T(3)$ hypotheses.
