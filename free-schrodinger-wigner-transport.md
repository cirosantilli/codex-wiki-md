# Free Schrodinger Wigner transport

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner distribution](wigner-distribution.md)

For $i\Psi_t=-\Delta\Psi/2$, differentiate the two-point product $\Psi(x+y/2)\overline\Psi(x-y/2)$. The difference of its two Laplacians is $2\nabla_x\cdot\nabla_y$, so Fourier transformation in $y$ gives the displayed free-transport equation for the [Wigner transform](wigner-distribution.md). Its solution is $w(x,\xi,t)=w(x-t\xi,\xi,0)$.
