# NS5-brane

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)

An [NS5-brane](ns5-brane.md) has five spatial dimensions and is magnetically charged under the [Neveu–Schwarz two-form](neveu-schwarz-two-form.md). Its string-frame [brane tension](brane-tension.md) scales as $g_s^{-2}$.
