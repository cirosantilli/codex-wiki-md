# Stretching energy

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic energy](elastic-energy.md)

The part of [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) associated with a change of material length. A simple quadratic [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) assigns energy density $C(\alpha-1)^2/2$ per reference [arc length](arc-length.md), where $C>0$ is stretching stiffness and $\alpha$ is the [stretch ratio](stretch-ratio.md). Such a small-[strain](strain.md) model need not remain realistic as $\alpha\to0$.
