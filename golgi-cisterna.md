# Golgi cisterna

↑ **Parent:** [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md)

A [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md) is a flattened membrane-enclosed sac. Stacking several sacs does not make their interiors one open space: membrane separates [Golgi lumen](golgi-lumen.md) from intervening [cytosol](cytosol.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Organization of the Golgi, Figure 9.22: membrane-enclosed cisternae](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-golgi-apparatus.md)
