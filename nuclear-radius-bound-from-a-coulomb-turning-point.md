# Nuclear radius bound from a Coulomb turning point

↑ **Parent:** [Rutherford scattering](rutherford-scattering.md)

For a head-on repulsive [Rutherford scattering](rutherford-scattering.md) encounter, relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) becomes [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) energy at closest approach. With projectile charge $ze$ and target charge $Ze$, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives the displayed separation. If the Coulomb description remains valid down to that separation, it bounds the target's interaction radius from above, treating the projectile as pointlike. For a stationary target of mass $M$ and incident projectile mass $m$, use centre-of-mass energy $E_{\rm cm}=ME_{\rm lab}/(M+m)$; neglecting [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) amounts to $M\gg m$.
