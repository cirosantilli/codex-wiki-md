# Phase-mixed radial probability of a Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-mixed orbit](phase-mixed-orbit.md)

For a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with [pericentre distance](pericentre-distance.md) $q$, [apocentre distance](apocentre-distance.md) $Q$, and [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a=(q+Q)/2$, a uniform [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md) gives

$$
p_r(r)=\frac{r}{\pi a\sqrt{(r-q)(Q-r)}},\qquad q<r<Q.
$$

The two radial passages per [orbital period](orbital-period.md) are included, and $\int_q^Qp_r(r)\,dr=1$.
