# AceK

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[AceK](acek.md) is a bifunctional isocitrate-dehydrogenase kinase/phosphatase. Metabolic effectors regulate its competing activities; in [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) it controls ICD but not the separate IDH [enzyme](enzyme-split.md).
