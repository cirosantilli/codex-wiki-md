# Probability vector

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_vector)

A [probability vector](probability-vector.md) lists the [probabilities](probability.md) of the outcomes in a finite [sample space](sample-space.md). Its entries are nonnegative and sum to one, so a column [probability vector](probability-vector.md) is a [point](point-geometry.md) of a [probability simplex](probability-simplex.md). Row and column conventions describe the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md), but they determine whether a [stochastic matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) acts on the right or its [transpose](transpose.md) acts on the left.
