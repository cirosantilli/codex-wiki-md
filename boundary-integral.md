# Boundary integral

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral-split.md)

An [integral](integral-split.md) over the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a region, such as a [line integral](line-integral.md) around a planar region or a [surface integral](surface-integral.md) over the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a three-dimensional region. [Green's identities](green-s-identities.md) convert certain [volume integrals](volume-integral.md) into [boundary integrals](boundary-integral.md).
