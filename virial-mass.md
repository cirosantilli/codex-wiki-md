# Virial mass

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

A dynamical [mass](mass.md) estimate inferred from an equilibrium size and [velocity](velocity.md) scale. For a pressure-supported spheroid, $M\sim C R_e\sigma_{\rm los}^2/G$ depends on the structural coefficient, orbital anisotropy and observational aperture.
