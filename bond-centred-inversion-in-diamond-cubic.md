# Bond-centred inversion in diamond cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Diamond cubic structure](diamond-cubic-structure.md)

The point $(1/8,1/8,1/8)$ is an [inversion centre](inversion-centre.md) when an atom is at the origin. Inversion sends $\mathbf r$ to $(1/4,1/4,1/4)-\mathbf r$ and exchanges the identical FCC sublattices. The individual atomic sites are not [inversion centres](inversion-centre.md).
