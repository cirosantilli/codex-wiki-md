# Singly excited helium state

↑ **Parent:** [Helium atom](helium-atom.md)

A bound-state configuration with one [electron](electron.md) predominantly in $1s$ and the other in a different orbital $n\ell$. The two assignments of identical [electrons](electron.md) to these orbitals must be combined with the appropriate [exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions](exchange-symmetry-of-two-identical-spin-one-half-fermions.md). Symmetric spatial combinations accompany the [singlet state](singlet-state.md); antisymmetric combinations accompany the [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md).
