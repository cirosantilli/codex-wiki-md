# Ramified prime classes for discriminant minus four d

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

Let $d>1$ be a positive [square-free integer](square-free-integer.md) congruent to one modulo four, with distinct prime factors $p_1,\ldots,p_k$. The [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-d}]$. Each ramified [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P_i=(p_i,\sqrt{-d})$ satisfies $P_i^2=(p_i)$, so its [ideal class](ideal-class.md) has order dividing two. Their product is the principal ideal $(\sqrt{-d})$.

There are no further subset relations. For a nonempty proper subset $S$, put $m=\prod_{i\in S}p_i$. If $\prod_{i\in S}P_i=(u+v\sqrt{-d})$, the [ideal norm](ideal-norm.md) gives $m=u^2+dv^2$. But $1<m<d$ forces $v=0$ and makes the square-free integer $m$ a square, a contradiction. Thus the homomorphism $\mathbb F_2^k\to\operatorname{Cl}(K)$ defined by the ramified classes has exactly the all-ones relation, and its image is $C_2^{k-1}$. This identifies a subgroup, without asserting that these classes exhaust the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md).
