# Ideal class group of Q of square root minus thirty-one

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

The [ideal-form correspondence for imaginary quadratic fields](ideal-form-correspondence-for-imaginary-quadratic-fields.md) and the reduction bound $a\leq\sqrt{31/3}$ give exactly the forms $[1,1,8]$, $[2,1,4]$ and $[2,-1,4]$. Thus the [class number](class-number.md) is three and the ideal class group is cyclic of order three.
