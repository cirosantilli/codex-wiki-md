# Projective measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)

A projective measurement is specified by mutually orthogonal projections $P_i$ with $\sum_iP_i=I$. On a state $\rho$, outcome $i$ has probability $\operatorname{Tr}(P_i\rho)$.

This is the projection-valued special case of a [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md); general POVM effects need not be projections.
