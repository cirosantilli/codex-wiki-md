# Canonical Coleman decomposition of power-series units

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman norm-fixed lift](coleman-norm-fixed-lift.md)

Let $W$ be the [subgroup](subgroup.md) fixed by the [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md). The [Coleman norm-fixed lift](coleman-norm-fixed-lift.md) gives the unique factorization $f=w(\overline f)v$, with $v\in1+pR$. Multiplication consequently gives the displayed [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md), while reduction identifies $W$ with $\mathbb F_p[[T]]^\times$. This is a decomposition of [unit groups](unit-group.md), not an identification of a power-series [ring](ring.md) with a product of groups.
