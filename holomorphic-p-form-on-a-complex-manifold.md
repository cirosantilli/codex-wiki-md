# Holomorphic p-form on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of holomorphic differential forms](sheaf-of-holomorphic-differential-forms.md)

A [differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) of type $(p,0)$ is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) when its local coefficients in [holomorphic coordinates](holomorphic-coordinate.md) are [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md), equivalently $\bar\partial\alpha=0$. It need not be $d$-closed on every [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md). Top-degree [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) forms are closed by bidegree. On a [compact](compact-space.md) [complex surface](complex-surface.md), [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) forms of all degrees are closed, even without a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md).
