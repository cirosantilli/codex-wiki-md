# Competing dehydrochlorination during copolymer saponification

↑ **Parent:** [Vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate copolymer](vinyl-chloride-vinyl-acetate-copolymer.md)

Alkaline conditions that hydrolyse acetate side groups can also promote elimination from chlorinated backbone units. Dechlorination, unsaturation, changed solubility and possible subsequent cross-linking complicate isolation of an ideal [vinyl chloride-vinyl alcohol copolymer](vinyl-chloride-vinyl-alcohol-copolymer.md). The competition is condition-dependent: analytical work used staged treatment and precipitation to restrain dehydrochlorination, so difficulty under generic conditions is not a proof that every hydroxyl-containing chloride copolymer is impossible.[Abstract and pp. 619–623: staged treatment, polymer precipitation and suppressed dehydrochlorination](determination-of-vinyl-acetate-in-vinyl-chloride-acetate-copolymers-by-takanori-shimazaki-and-others.md)
