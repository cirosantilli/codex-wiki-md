# Transmission line

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_line)

A [transmission line](transmission-line.md) guides travelling voltage and current waves. Its distributed electrical properties determine its [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md). A mismatch at a terminal produces reflected waves; available [power](power.md), delivered [power](power.md) and returned [power](power.md) are distinct quantities.
