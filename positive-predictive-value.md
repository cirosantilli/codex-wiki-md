# Positive predictive value

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical classification](statistical-classification.md)

For a binary [classifier](classifier.md), the [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) of the target condition given a positive classification. If its [base rate](base-rate.md) is $p$, its true-positive [probability](probability.md) is $s$, and its false-positive [probability](probability.md) is $f$, [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) gives $P(E\mid +)=sp/(sp+f(1-p))$. The denominator is positive whenever a positive classification can occur.
