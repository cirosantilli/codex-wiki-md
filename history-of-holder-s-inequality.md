<h1 id="history-of-holder-s-inequality">History of Hölder's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder's inequality](holder-s-inequality.md)

[Otto Hölder](otto-holder.md)'s 1889 paper begins with a weighted-mean inequality for a [function](function-split.md) with increasing [derivative](derivative.md). In current terminology, its opening statement is a weighted form of [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md): applying a [convex function](convex-function.md) after taking a [weighted mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) gives a lower bound for the weighted mean of its values. This connects product estimates to the geometry of [convex functions](convex-function.md), rather than treating them as isolated formulas.[Otto Hölder, Ueber einen Mittelwerthssatz (1889), §§1–2, printed pp. 38–39: weighted means and increasing derivative](ueber-einen-mittelwerthssatz-by-otto-holder.md)
