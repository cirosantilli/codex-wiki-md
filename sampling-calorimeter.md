# Sampling calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic calorimeter](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md)

A [sampling calorimeter](sampling-calorimeter.md) alternates passive shower-forming material with active signal layers. Only part of the deposited [energy](energy-split.md) is measured directly. Fluctuations of that fraction contribute a [stochastic](statistical-fluctuation.md) energy-resolution term. Fine segmentation and robust [absorbers](sampling-calorimeter.md) can be economical, while fine sampling and a larger active fraction generally improve resolution.
