# Damped pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Simple pendulum](simple-pendulum.md)

In units where the small-amplitude natural frequency is one, linear velocity drag gives $\ddot\theta+c\dot\theta+\sin\theta=0$ with $c>0$. The even multiples of $\pi$ are attracting equilibria; the odd multiples are [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md). Small displacements obey a [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md); the [energy dissipation of a damped pendulum](energy-dissipation-of-a-damped-pendulum.md) governs the global [phase portrait](phase-portrait.md).
