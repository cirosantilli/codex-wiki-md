# Negative-norm photon state

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant photon Fock space](covariant-photon-fock-space.md)

For a temporal [photon](photon.md) [wave packet](wave-packet.md) $|f,0\rangle=\int d^3p\,f(\mathbf p)a_{\mathbf p}^{0\dagger}|0\rangle/(2\pi)^3$, the oscillator [commutator](commutator.md) gives $\langle f,0|f,0\rangle=-\int d^3p\,|f(\mathbf p)|^2/(2\pi)^3<0$ when $f\ne0$. This is the negative direction of the [indefinite Hermitian form](indefinite-hermitian-form.md), not the divergent normalization of an unsmeared [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md). It is excluded by the physical condition in [Gupta-Bleuler quantization](gupta-bleuler-formalism.md).
