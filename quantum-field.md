# Quantum field

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)

A [quantum field](quantum-field.md) is an operator-valued [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) on spacetime: smearing $\Phi(x)$ against a smooth test function gives an operator, whereas pointwise products can require renormalization. For example, a free [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) contains particle [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) [creation operators](creation-operator.md). Its [microcausality](microcausality.md) condition makes field commutators vanish at spacelike separation. For [fermions](fermion.md), the corresponding locality condition uses anticommutators. This combines quantum operators with the spacetime locality of a [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md).
