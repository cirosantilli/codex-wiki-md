# Hecke parameter of a BN-pair

↑ **Parent:** [Iwahori-Hecke algebra of a BN-pair](iwahori-hecke-algebra-of-a-bn-pair.md)

For a representative $\dot x_i\in N$, the Hecke parameter is

$$
q_i=[B:B\cap\dot x_iB\dot x_i^{-1}].
$$

The [Iwahori-Hecke algebra of a BN-pair](iwahori-hecke-algebra-of-a-bn-pair.md) is obtained from the generic algebra by $a_i\mapsto q_i\cdot1_k$.
