# Supergravity multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

A supergravity multiplet is a [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md) containing a [graviton](graviton.md) and its supersymmetric partners. In [four-dimensional N=8 supergravity](four-dimensional-n-8-supergravity.md), the irreducible [massless supermultiplet](massless-supermultiplet.md) contains $128$ [boson](boson.md) and $128$ [fermion](fermion.md) states.
