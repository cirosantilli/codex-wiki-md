# Projected area

↑ **Parent:** [Radiance](radiance.md)

The [projected area](projected-area.md) of a planar patch viewed from a direction is the [area](surface-area.md) of its perpendicular shadow onto a plane normal to that direction. If $\mathbf n$ and $\mathbf s$ are its [unit normal](unit-normal.md) and viewing [unit vector](unit-vector.md), then $A_\perp=A|\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s|$ by the [dot product](dot-product.md). On the outward side, this is $A\cos\theta$. For a [rectangle](rectangle.md) tilted around one edge, the width perpendicular to that edge contracts by $\cos\theta$ and the edge length stays fixed, proving the formula. At grazing view $A_\perp=0$; interpret the [radiance](radiance.md) definition by a limit rather than dividing by zero.
