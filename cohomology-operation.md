# Cohomology operation

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_operation)

A cohomology operation is a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) between cohomology functors. Thus for every continuous map $f$, its pullback commutes with the operation: $\theta(f^*a)=f^*\theta(a)$. This makes operations additional invariants of [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md), beyond the additive groups and their [cup products](cup-product.md).
