# Nonmonotone monetary utility

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility](expected-utility.md)

A [utility function](utility-function-split.md) on monetary amounts need not be increasing merely because preferences obey the [expected utility hypothesis](expected-utility-hypothesis.md). The outcome labels do not themselves supply a preference order. For example, values $u(1)=2$, $u(2)=0$, $u(4)=3$ define consistent [expected utility](expected-utility.md) comparisons even though the amount $2$ is least preferred. Preferring more money is an additional assumption. It is distinct from [risk aversion](risk-aversion.md), which concerns the effect of spreading uncertainty for a specified increasing [utility function](utility-function-split.md).
