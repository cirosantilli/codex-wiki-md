# Vacuum pairing weights for two even scalar vertices

↑ **Parent:** [Wick contraction](wick-contraction.md)

For two identical even-degree $n$ scalar [Feynman vertices](interaction-vertex.md), choose $r$ half-edges on each and pair them across the vertices. The remaining $n-r$ half-edges pair locally. This gives the displayed number of [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md), with even $r$ and $(-1)!!=1$. The coefficient in the unnormalized [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $N_{n,r}/[2!(n!)^2]$. The $r=0$ contribution is disconnected and is removed from the logarithm, but remains in the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) itself. This rule produces the quartic and sextic oscillator vacuum graph coefficients without drawing-dependent symmetry-factor guesses.
