# Gimel hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Gimel function](gimel-function.md)

For every singular infinite [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, the [Gimel function](gimel-function.md) has the smallest value permitted by [König theorem for cardinal numbers](konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) and the exponent: $\kappa^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}=\max\{\kappa^+,2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}\}$. Where $2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}<\kappa$, this is the [singular cardinals hypothesis](singular-cardinals-hypothesis.md). The hypothesis imposes no separate successor-power condition on regular [cardinals](cardinal-number.md).
