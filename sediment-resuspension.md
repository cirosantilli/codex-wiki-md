# Sediment resuspension

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)

Sediment resuspension transfers deposited bed particles into a [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry.md). It can supply particle [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) to a [particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md) if erosion or upward mixing exceeds settling losses. Ambient fluid without particles dilutes concentration without supplying particle mass.
