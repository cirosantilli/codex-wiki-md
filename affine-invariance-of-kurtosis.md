# Affine invariance of kurtosis

↑ **Parent:** [Kurtosis](kurtosis.md)

Changing units or shifting the origin preserves [kurtosis](kurtosis.md). For real constants $a\ne0$ and $b$, the [mean](expected-value.md) of $Y=aX+b$ is $a\mu+b$, its [variance](variance-split.md) is $a^2\sigma^2$, and its fourth [central moment](central-moment.md) is $a^4\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^4]$. The factors $a^4$ cancel in the ratio, even for negative $a$. Hence [excess kurtosis](excess-kurtosis.md) is also unchanged. For example, $100X+7$ and $X$ have the same [kurtosis](kurtosis.md), although their [means](expected-value.md) and [variances](variance-split.md) differ. The case $a=0$ produces zero [variance](variance-split.md) and is excluded.
