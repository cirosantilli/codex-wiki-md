<h1 id="thompson-s-coprime-character-extension-theorem">Thompson's coprime character extension theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Character extension](character-extension.md)

Let $N\triangleleft G$ and let $\theta\in\operatorname{Irr}(N)$ be invariant under $G$. If $\theta(1)$ is coprime to $[G:N]$, then $\theta$ extends to $G$ if and only if its [determinant character](determinant-character.md) extends to a linear character of $G$. In particular, if the determinant order is also coprime to the index, there is a unique extension whose determinant order is coprime to the index. When $G/N$ is a p-group and $N=O^p(N)$, every invariant irreducible character of degree prime to $p$ satisfies this latter determinant condition.
