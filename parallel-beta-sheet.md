# Parallel beta sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Beta sheet](beta-sheet.md)

A [parallel beta sheet](parallel-beta-sheet.md) has neighboring [beta strands](beta-strand.md) running in the same direction along their [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) sequences, from the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) toward the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md).
