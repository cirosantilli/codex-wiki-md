# Geodesic sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md)

A geodesic sphere is a level set of [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) from a point. For sufficiently small positive $r$, it is the [exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) image of the radius-$r$ tangent sphere, a smooth compact hypersurface. The [Gauss lemma](gauss-s-lemma-riemannian-geometry.md) makes its tangent space orthogonal to the radial direction. Larger distance spheres need not be smooth at the [cut locus](cut-locus.md).
