# Closed equivalence relation with an open quotient map

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

If an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) is closed in a space's square and the [quotient map](quotient-map.md) is open, the [quotient](quotient.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md). An inequivalent pair has an open rectangle avoiding the relation. The two factor [images](image-of-a-function.md) are open [quotient](quotient.md) neighborhoods and are disjoint, since an [intersection](set-intersection.md) would give equivalent representatives in that rectangle. Conversely, a [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) [quotient](quotient.md) always has a closed [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) by pulling back its closed diagonal.
