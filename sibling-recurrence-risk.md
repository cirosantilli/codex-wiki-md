# Sibling recurrence risk

↑ **Parent:** [Recessive inheritance](recessive-inheritance.md)

Sibling recurrence risk is the [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) that another child has the disease, given an affected sibling. For unaffected parents, complete [penetrance](penetrance.md) and a single recessive disease allele, the affected child identifies both parents as [genetic carriers](genetic-carrier.md). Independent [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) then gives recurrence probability $1/4$, whether or not the parents are related. Incomplete penetrance, phenocopies, different causal loci or affected parents change this calculation.
