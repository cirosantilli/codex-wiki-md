# Kepler radial energy equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

For unit [mass](mass.md) in $\Phi=-GM/r$, [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) splits the [velocity](velocity.md) norm into $\dot r^2+L^2/r^2$. [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) then gives $\tfrac12\dot r^2+L^2/(2r^2)=E+GM/r$. It separates radial dynamics from the reconstruction of the orbital angle.
