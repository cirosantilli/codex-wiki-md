# Love number

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal force](tidal-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_number)

A Love number measures a body's tidal response to an applied potential. The degree-two potential response $k_2$ is sensitive to radial density structure and central concentration, and can complement mass and radius constraints on an [exoplanet interior](exoplanet-interior.md). Inferring it from orbital precession requires separating other contributions and assumptions about rotation and the tidal response.
