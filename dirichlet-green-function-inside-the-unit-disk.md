# Dirichlet Green function inside the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

With the convention $\Delta_zG_D=\delta(z-\zeta)$, the displayed [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) vanishes on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). Indeed $|z-\zeta|=|1-z\bar\zeta|$ when $|z|=1$. For $\zeta\ne0$, the second logarithm corresponds to an opposite image source at $\zeta/|\zeta|^2$, outside the [unit disc](unit-disc.md); it is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) throughout the interior. At $\zeta=0$ the formula becomes $(2\pi)^{-1}\log|z|$. Its outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) in the boundary variable is $(2\pi)^{-1}(1-|\zeta|^2)/|z-\zeta|^2$, the [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md) divided by $2\pi$. [Green's second identity](green-second-identity.md) therefore gives the [Poisson integral on the unit disk](poisson-integral-on-the-unit-disk.md).
