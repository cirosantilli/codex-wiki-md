<h1 id="burnside-s-theorem">Burnside's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group](finite-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnside's_theorem)

Every [finite group](finite-group.md) whose order is divisible by at most two distinct [primes](prime-number.md) is a [solvable group](solvable-group.md). A nonabelian [simple group](simple-group.md) must therefore have at least three distinct prime divisors.
