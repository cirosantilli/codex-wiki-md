# Laplacian spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

The [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of a specified self-adjoint [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md). On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) of positive dimension, it is a discrete sequence of nonnegative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) tending to infinity, counted with the dimensions of their [Laplacian eigenspaces](laplacian-eigenspace.md). On a noncompact manifold or a manifold with boundary, the domain and boundary conditions are part of the operator definition and continuous spectrum can occur.
