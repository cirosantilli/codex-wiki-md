# Mass normalization in mollified singular integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate identity](approximate-identity.md)

For a bounded [Fourier multiplier operator](fourier-multiplier-operator.md) $T$ on $L^2$ and a [bump function](bump-function.md) $\phi$, the displayed convolution identity follows by multiplying Fourier transforms. The convergence holds in $L^2$ and [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) by the [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) theorem, after accounting for the integral of the kernel. The limit is $T(f)$ when $\int\phi=1$. Smoothness and compact support alone do not imply this normalization.
