# Rheumatism

↑ **Parent:** [Rheumatology](rheumatology.md)

Historical umbrella for painful musculoskeletal disorders, including conditions now separated as [acute rheumatic fever](acute-rheumatic-fever.md), [rheumatoid arthritis](rheumatoid-arthritis.md) and [osteoarthritis](osteoarthritis.md). It is not a single modern pathological diagnosis.
