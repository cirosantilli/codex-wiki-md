# Computer Networks examination paper of the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) finals paper, Saturday 13 June 2015, 2:30 pm–4:00 pm. Its three questions concern [line coding](line-code.md), [cyclic redundancy checks](cyclic-redundancy-check.md), [network address translation](network-address-translation.md), [IP multicast](ip-multicast.md) and [TCP](transmission-control-protocol.md). The public [archive supplied by the University of Oxford](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-networks.pdf`. Questions occupy printed pages 2–3.
