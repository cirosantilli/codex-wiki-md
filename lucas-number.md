# Lucas number

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence and series](sequence-and-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_number)

The [Lucas numbers](lucas-number.md) obey the same second-order recurrence as the [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) but start with $2,1$, so their sequence is $2,1,3,4,7,11,\ldots$. For $n\ge1$, $L_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n+1}$. Substitution in the [Fibonacci addition formula](fibonacci-addition-formula.md) gives $F_{2n}=F_nL_n$. Consecutive [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) are coprime by the [Euclidean algorithm](euclidean-algorithm.md); since $L_n=F_n+2F_{n-1}$, [Bézout's identity](bezout-identity.md) gives $\gcd(F_n,L_n)=\gcd(F_n,2)\le2$.
