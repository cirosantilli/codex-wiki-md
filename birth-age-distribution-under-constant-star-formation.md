# Birth-age distribution under constant star formation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar population](stellar-population.md)

If a population forms at a constant number rate for duration $T$ and every birth object is retained in the census, its present ages are uniform on $[0,T]$. A fixed [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) with finite mean birth mass also makes constant mass-based [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md) equivalent to constant number rate.
