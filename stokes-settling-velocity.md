# Stokes settling velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Settling velocity](settling-velocity.md)

The Stokes settling velocity is the terminal relative velocity obtained by balancing gravity, buoyancy, and the [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md) for a dilute isolated sphere.
