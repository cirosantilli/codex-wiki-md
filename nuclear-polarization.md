# Nuclear polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

The mean orientation of nuclear [spin angular momentum](spin.md) in a chosen direction. For nuclei with nonzero [spin](spin.md) quantum number $I$, the vector-polarization component is $P=\langle J_z\rangle/(I\hbar)$ and lies between $-1$ and $1$. Equal populations of all projections give $P=0$; complete alignment gives $P=1$.
