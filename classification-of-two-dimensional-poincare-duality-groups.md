# Classification of two-dimensional Poincare duality groups

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md)

Every integral dimension-two [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md) is the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a closed aspherical [topological surface](topological-surface.md). Orientable examples are the torus and closed surfaces of genus at least two. Nonorientable aspherical closed surfaces supply the orientation-twisted examples.
