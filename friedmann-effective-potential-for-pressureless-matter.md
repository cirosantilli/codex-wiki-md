# Friedmann effective potential for pressureless matter

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For pressureless matter normalized by $\rho=3\mu/(8\pi a^3)$ in units $G=c=1$, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
\dot a^2+V(a)=-k,
\qquad
V(a)=-\frac{\mu}{a}-\frac{\Lambda a^2}{3}.
$$

Allowed scale factors satisfy $V(a)\leq-k$, and equality marks a [turning point](turning-point.md).
