# Scheme-theoretic fibre

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber product of schemes](fiber-product-of-schemes.md)

The fibre of a [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) over $y\in Y$ is its base change to the [residue field](residue-field.md) $\kappa(y)$. For $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$, with $y$ corresponding to $\mathfrak p$, its fibre ring is $B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Its [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) records nilpotents and multiplicities that the set-theoretic inverse image cannot see.
