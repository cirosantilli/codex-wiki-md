# Schwartz-Zippel lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial method in combinatorics](polynomial-method-in-combinatorics.md)

For a nonzero [multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md) of [total degree](total-degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $d$ over a [field](field.md), and a finite subset $S$ of that field, at most $d|S|^{n-1}$ points of $S^n$ are zeros. For one variable this is the [root bound for a polynomial](lagrange-root-bound-over-a-field.md). If $d\ge|S|$, the bound is already trivial. Otherwise, in the inductive proof write the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) as degree $k$ in its last variable, with a nonzero leading coefficient of degree at most $d-k$ in the other variables. The leading coefficient vanishes on at most $(d-k)|S|^{n-2}$ fibers; outside those fibers there are at most $k$ [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) per fiber. Counting the bad fibers with the trivial $|S|$ bound gives the asserted inequality.
