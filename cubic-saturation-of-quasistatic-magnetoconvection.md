# Cubic saturation of quasistatic magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Quasistatic vertical-field magnetoconvection](quasistatic-vertical-field-magnetoconvection.md)

The primary roll and [temperature](temperature.md) modes force only a horizontally uniform [temperature](temperature.md) mode proportional to $\sin(2\pi z)$ at quadratic order. Its amplitude is $c=-\alpha ab/(8\pi)$. Its feedback on the primary [temperature](temperature.md) mode saturates [magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md), giving the displayed positive squared amplitude above threshold. Thus a simple roll onset is a supercritical [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) for every $Q\ge0$. The three-mode [Fourier truncation](fourier-truncation.md) and full perturbation calculation agree through the cubic solvability condition because all forced quadratic modes are retained; this is not an assertion that the truncation gives an exact finite-amplitude PDE solution.
