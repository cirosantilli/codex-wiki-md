# Steinhaus first-moment lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Steinhaus random variable](steinhaus-random-variable.md)

For independent [Steinhaus random variables](steinhaus-random-variable.md), condition on their acute angles and use the [sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality](sharp-rademacher-second-moment-inequality.md) on the $2d$ sign coefficients $a_i\cos\theta_i,ia_i\sin\theta_i$. Their squared moduli sum to $\sum_i|a_i|^2$ independently of the angles, so the conditional first moment is at least the same fixed square-root bound. Averaging proves the assertion.
