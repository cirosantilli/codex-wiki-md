# Dihedral fixed field of a two-variable rational function field

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed field](fixed-field.md)

On $K=\mathbb Q(x,y)$, let $\sigma(x,y)=(y,-x)$ and $\tau(x,y)=(x,-y)$. They generate a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order eight, and

$$
K^{\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle}
=\mathbb Q(x^2+y^2,x^2y^2).
$$

The inclusion from right to left is immediate. In the other direction, $x^2,y^2$ solve a quadratic over the displayed field and adjoining their square roots has degree at most four; the resulting total degree is at most eight, while the [automorphism-count bound for a finite field extension](automorphism-count-bound-for-a-finite-field-extension.md) gives the reverse bound.
