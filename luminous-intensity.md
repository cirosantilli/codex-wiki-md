# Luminous intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminous_intensity)

[Luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md) is [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) per outgoing [solid angle](solid-angle.md), measured in [candelas](candela.md). It is the visually weighted counterpart of [radiant intensity](radiant-intensity.md). The same total [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) concentrated into a narrower cone can give a greater [luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md) within that cone.[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)
