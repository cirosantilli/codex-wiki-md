# Reciprocal primes in a fixed arithmetic progression

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-arithmetic-progressions.md)

For fixed coprime integers $a,q$, the [Prime number theorem](prime-number-theorem.md) in the [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) gives $\pi(x;q,a)\sim x/(\varphi(q)\log x)$. [Partial summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) expresses the reciprocal-prime sum as $\pi(x;q,a)/x+\int_2^x\pi(t;q,a)t^{-2}\,dt$. The integral of the relative error is $o(\log\log x)$: fix a threshold beyond which that error is at most $\varepsilon$, bound its remaining integral by $\varepsilon\log\log x$, and then let $\varepsilon$ decrease to zero. The modulus is fixed in this statement.
