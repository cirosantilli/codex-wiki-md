# Turbulent mixing of a secularly unstable dust layer

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-drag secular gravitational growth rate](weak-drag-secular-gravitational-growth-rate.md)

[Turbulence](turbulence-split.md) can raise dust random speeds, increase layer thickness and mix density enhancements by [eddy diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md), delaying [secular gravitational instability](secular-gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md). On sufficiently long wavelengths, weak-drag growth is proportional to $|k|$, whereas simple diffusive damping is proportional to $k^2$. Thus diffusion alone need not remove all long-wave growth in an infinite idealization, although finite size and lifetime can make it ineffective. Coherent turbulent concentration can also increase local [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), so the net effect requires a stochastic transport model.
