# Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md)

Choose a topological generator $\gamma$ of the [Galois group](galois-group.md) of a [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md) and set $T=\gamma-1$. The [completed group algebra](completed-group-algebra.md) becomes the [formal power series ring](formal-power-series.md) $\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$, a two-dimensional complete regular local [integral domain](integral-domain.md) with maximal ideal $(p,T)$. Its finite-layer quotient is $\Lambda/((1+T)^{p^n}-1)\cong\mathbb Z_p[\Gamma/\Gamma^{p^n}]$.
