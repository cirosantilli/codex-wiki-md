# Chiral Riemann curvature spinor decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral spinor curvature operator](chiral-spinor-curvature-operator.md)

Metric compatibility makes the curvature action on a two-component spinor symplectic, so its lowered curvature spinor is symmetric in each pair. The pair-exchange symmetry of the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) makes $X_{ABCD}=X_{CDAB}$. This six-component tensor decomposes into the five-component totally symmetric [Weyl curvature spinor](weyl-curvature-spinor.md) $\Psi_{ABCD}=X_{(ABCD)}$ and one scalar term. In the displayed normalization, with alternating forms satisfying $\epsilon^{AB}\epsilon_{AB}=2$, its scalar is $\Lambda=X_{ABCD}\epsilon^{AC}\epsilon^{BD}/3$. This coefficient follows by contracting the displayed scalar term; different normalizations of that term give different scalar conventions.
