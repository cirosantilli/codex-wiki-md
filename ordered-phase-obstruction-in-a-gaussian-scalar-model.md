# Ordered-phase obstruction in a Gaussian scalar model

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian field theory](gaussian-field-theory.md)

The uniform mode of a pure [Gaussian field theory](gaussian-field-theory.md) with negative squared mass has an unbounded quadratic energy. Its [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) diverges even with a finite-volume ultraviolet regulator, so it supplies no stable ordered branch. At positive squared mass the zero-source average is zero. Thus the scaling dimension of the field can yield a formal [order-parameter critical exponent](order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) without establishing a negative-reduced-temperature [magnetization](magnetization.md) law. A stabilizing quartic interaction is relevant below four dimensions and ordinarily changes the critical theory to the [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md); above four its coefficient is a [dangerously irrelevant coupling](dangerously-irrelevant-coupling.md) for the ordered phase.
