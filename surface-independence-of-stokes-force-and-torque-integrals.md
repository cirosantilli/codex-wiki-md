# Surface independence of Stokes force and torque integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

For [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) without body forces, $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma=0$. Symmetry of the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) also gives $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf x\times\boldsymbol\sigma)=0$, interpreted componentwise as the angular-momentum flux. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) makes the resultant [force](force.md) and [torque](torque.md) the same on any two homologous enclosing surfaces lying in a nonsingular fluid region. In a [boundary perturbation of a nearly spherical particle](boundary-perturbation-of-a-nearly-spherical-particle.md), evaluating each perturbation field on a fixed enclosing surface avoids spurious geometric force terms: the geometric terms from changing the surface normal and area cancel those from evaluating the base stress at the displaced surface.
