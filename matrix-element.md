# Matrix element

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A [matrix element](matrix-element.md) $A_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$ and column $j$ of a [matrix](matrix.md) $A$.

For a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$ on an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) with an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $(e_i)$, its [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) are $T_{ij}=\langle e_i,T e_j\rangle$, using the convention that the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) is linear in its second argument. For a finite-dimensional space, the expansion $T e_j=\sum_iT_{ij}e_i$ explains why these numbers represent the [linear operator](linear-operator.md) in that [basis](basis.md). In [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), the expression $\langle\phi|T|\psi\rangle$ is also called a [matrix element](matrix-element.md) between the two states; it need not be diagonal or an [expectation value](expectation-value.md).
