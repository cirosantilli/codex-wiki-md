# Spectral decomposition of a quantum-plane point

↑ **Parent:** [Points of a noncommutative algebra](points-of-a-noncommutative-algebra.md)

For finite matrices satisfying $YX=qXY$, with $q$ not a [root of unity](root-of-unity.md), $Y$ sends the [generalized eigenspace](generalized-eigenspace.md) of $X$ at $\lambda$ into the one at $q^{-1}\lambda$. Finiteness of the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) makes $Y$ nilpotent on the sum of the nonzero $X$ [generalized eigenspaces](generalized-eigenspace.md). Exchanging $X,Y$ gives the corresponding statement for $X$. Splitting the zero [generalized eigenspace](generalized-eigenspace.md) of $X$ by that of $Y$ gives the displayed invariant direct sum; both operators are nilpotent on $U$.
