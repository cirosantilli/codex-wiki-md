# Compact interval

↑ **Parent:** [Closed real interval](closed-real-interval.md)

A [compact interval](compact-interval.md) in the [real numbers](real-number.md) is either the empty [set](set-split.md) or a closed bounded [interval](interval-mathematics.md) $[a,b]$ with $a\leq b$. Its [compactness](compact-space.md) follows from the [Heine-Borel theorem](heine-borel-theorem.md).
