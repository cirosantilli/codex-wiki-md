# Two-sublattice Ising mean-field approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic Ising model](antiferromagnetic-ising-model.md)

On a regular [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) with coordination number $q$, replace each bond product by $\sigma_B\sigma_W\simeq M_W\sigma_B+M_B\sigma_W-M_BM_W$. For $N$ sites there are $Nq/2$ bonds, so the constant in the effective [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $-NqJM_BM_W/2$. The [mean-field self-consistency equations](self-consistency-equation.md) are $M_B=\tanh\beta(h+g-qJM_W)$ and $M_W=\tanh\beta(h-g-qJM_B)$. At zero fields the antisymmetric branch has a continuous [mean-field Ising critical bifurcation](mean-field-ising-critical-bifurcation.md) at $k_BT_c=qJ$.
