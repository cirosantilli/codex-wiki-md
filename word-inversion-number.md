# Word inversion number

↑ **Parent:** [Inversion of a word](inversion-of-a-word.md)

The number of [inversions of a word](inversion-of-a-word.md): pairs of positions $i<j$ with $w_i>w_j$. Unlike an [inversion of a permutation](inversion-of-a-permutation.md), this definition permits repeated letters in the word.
