# Action variable

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

With angle period $2\pi$, the action is the enclosed phase-space area divided by $2\pi$:

$$
I=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint p\,dq.
$$

This is the action component of [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), rather than the entire coordinate construction.
