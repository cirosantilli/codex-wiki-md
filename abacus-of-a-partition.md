# Abacus of a partition

↑ **Parent:** [Beta set of a partition](beta-set-of-a-partition.md)

An $e$-runner abacus arranges the nonnegative integers by their residues modulo $e$ and places beads at the positions in a beta set. Moving a bead up one place on its runner removes an $e$-hook.

An abacus represents the [beta set of a partition](beta-set-of-a-partition.md) by beads at integer positions. Positions with the same residue modulo the chosen runner count lie on one runner; moving a bead to an empty position above it represents the corresponding rim-hook removal.
