# Kac entropy production

↑ **Parent:** [Relative entropy in Kac's model](relative-entropy-in-kac-s-model.md)

For the [Kac master equation](kac-master-equation.md) and finite differentiable entropy, mass and energy conservation remove the non-logarithmic entropy terms. Symmetrizing each pair rotation then gives $D_N(F)=[2\pi(N-1)]^{-1}\sum_{i<j}\int\int(F\circ R_{ij,\theta}-F)\log((F\circ R_{ij,\theta})/F)\,dv\,d\theta$. The integrand is nonnegative by monotonicity of the logarithm, with the usual extended convention at zeros. Zero dissipation corresponds to radiality; it need not imply zero relative entropy.
