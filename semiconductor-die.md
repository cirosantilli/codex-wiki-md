# Semiconductor die

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor wafer](semiconductor-wafer.md)

A piece of [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) carrying a fabricated device or [integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md), ordinarily separated from a larger [semiconductor wafer](semiconductor-wafer.md). One package can contain multiple [semiconductor dies](semiconductor-die.md); the package boundary and the [semiconductor die](semiconductor-die.md) boundary are different concepts.
