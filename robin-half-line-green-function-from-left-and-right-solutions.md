# Robin half-line Green function from left and right solutions

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)

For a monic second-order operator, choose a homogeneous solution $u$ satisfying the left [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md) and a solution $v$ satisfying the right decay condition. With $W=uv'-u'v\ne0$, the displayed kernel is continuous and its first derivative has jump one. It therefore satisfies $LG=\delta$ and the two boundary conditions. The operator's first derivative term generally makes $W$ nonconstant and the resulting [Green function](green-s-function.md) nonsymmetric in the unweighted variables.
