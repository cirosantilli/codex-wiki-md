# Variational image processing

↑ **Parent:** [Image processing](image-processing.md)

A variational model balances agreement with observed data against a regularity or geometric penalty. Examples include [total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md), the [relaxed graph-area functional](relaxed-graph-area-functional.md), and the [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md). Convex penalties support unique reconstruction, while segmentation energies can have multiple competing partitions.
