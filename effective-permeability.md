# Effective permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md)

An equivalent large-scale [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) relating imposed pressure drop to total [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) through a heterogeneous sample. Parallel flow paths add conductances, whereas successive resistive segments add pressure drops. It depends on direction and boundary conditions; it need not be the arithmetic mean of the local permeabilities.
