# Effective permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md)

[Effective permeability](effective-permeability.md) is a bulk [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) that reproduces the total [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) through a heterogeneous sample under specified boundary conditions. For a horizontal sample with length $L$, [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\mu$, and positive inlet-to-outlet [fluid pressure](fluid-pressure.md) drop $\Delta p$, define $k_{\rm eff}$ by $Q/A=k_{\rm eff}\Delta p/(\mu L)$. The description is useful when individual layers or passages are smaller than the scale of the calculation. It depends on direction, geometry and boundary conditions; it is not generally the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) of local [intrinsic permeabilities](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md).

[Layered porous permeability](layered-porous-permeability.md) illustrates the mechanism: when every path must cross a resistant layer, successive [hydraulic resistances](hydraulic-resistance.md) add; when more permeable paths span the whole sample alongside less permeable ones, their discharges add. This is the same organizing idea as series and parallel [resistors](resistor.md). For fixed [fluid](fluid.md) properties the corresponding [hydraulic conductivities](hydraulic-conductivity.md) average in the same way.
