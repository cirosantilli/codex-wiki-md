# Kernel density estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kernel_density_estimation)

Given [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, the kernel density estimate is

$$
\widehat f_{n,h,K}(x)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nK_h(x-X_i).
$$
