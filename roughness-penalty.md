# Roughness penalty

↑ **Parent:** [Regularization](regularization.md)

A [roughness penalty](roughness-penalty.md) penalizes variation of derivatives, such as $\int(m^{\prime\prime})^2$. The second-derivative penalty vanishes precisely on [affine functions](affine-function.md) and is represented in a chosen basis by a [roughness penalty matrix](roughness-penalty-matrix.md).
