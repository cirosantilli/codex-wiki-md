# Cone (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone)

A geometric cone joins a fixed [point](point-geometry.md) to points of a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) by straight [lines](straight-line.md). A circular cone uses a [circle](circle.md) as that curve; its solid version includes the enclosed circular cross-sections. For a right circular cone of height $h$ and base radius $R$, the cross-section radius varies linearly along the axis, and its [volume](volume.md) is $\pi R^2h/3$ by [integration](integral-split.md). An [elliptic cone](elliptic-cone.md) allows elliptical cross-sections. The construction differs from the abstract [topological cone](cone-topology.md), which does not specify lengths or angles.
