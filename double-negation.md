# Double negation

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)

In [propositional logic](propositional-logic.md) with the usual two truth values, negating a proposition twice preserves its truth value. Thus the [complement of a set](complement-of-a-set.md) taken twice, in one fixed [universal set](universal-set.md), is the original [set](set-split.md).
