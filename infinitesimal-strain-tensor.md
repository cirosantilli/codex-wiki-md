# Infinitesimal strain tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Strain](strain.md)

The [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) is the symmetric part of a [displacement gradient tensor](displacement-gradient-tensor.md). It is the linear approximation to a finite [strain](strain.md) measure for small displacement gradients. An infinitesimal rigid rotation contributes no [strain](strain.md), so the [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) depends on the symmetric part rather than the entire gradient.
