# Conditional-expectation martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

For an integrable random variable $Z$ and an increasing [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_n)$, the process $M_n=\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_n]$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md). The [tower property of conditional expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) gives the martingale identity.
