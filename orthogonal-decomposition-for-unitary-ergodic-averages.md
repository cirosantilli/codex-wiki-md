# Orthogonal decomposition for unitary ergodic averages

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann mean ergodic theorem](von-neumann-mean-ergodic-theorem.md)

For a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $U$, the orthogonal complement of $\operatorname{Ran}(I-U)$ is $\ker(I-U^*)=\ker(I-U)$. On the fixed space, [Cesaro averages](cesaro-mean.md) act as the identity. On $(I-U)g$ they telescope to $(g-U^Ng)/N$. Their uniform norm bound extends convergence to the closure of the range, proving the [Von Neumann mean ergodic theorem](von-neumann-mean-ergodic-theorem.md).
