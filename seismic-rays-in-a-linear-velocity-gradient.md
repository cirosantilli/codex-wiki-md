# Seismic rays in a linear velocity gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic ray theory](seismic-ray-theory.md)

For $v(z)=v_s+\beta z$ with $\beta>0$, a surface-to-surface diving ray is circular. Its turning speed is $v_t=1/p$, and its range and time are $X=2\sqrt{v_t^2-v_s^2}/\beta$ and $T=2\operatorname{arcosh}(v_t/v_s)/\beta$. Differentiation gives $dT/dX=p$, the [seismic ray parameter](seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md). In the limit $\beta\to0$ at fixed range, $T\to X/v_s$.
