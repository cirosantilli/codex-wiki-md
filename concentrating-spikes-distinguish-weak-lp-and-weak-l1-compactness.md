# Concentrating spikes distinguish weak Lp and weak L1 compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence](weak-convergence.md)

The displayed spikes have $L^p$ [norm](norm.md) one. Pairing with $g\in L^{p'}$ and applying [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) bounds the pairing by $(\int_0^{1/j}|g|^{p'})^{1/p'}$, which tends to zero. At exponent one, however, $j\mathbf1_{(0,1/j)}$ cannot converge weakly in $L^1$: every interval away from zero forces any limit to vanish there, while the constant [test function](test-function.md) preserves integral one. Their failure of [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) records exactly the obstruction in the [Dunford-Pettis theorem](dunford-pettis-theorem.md).
