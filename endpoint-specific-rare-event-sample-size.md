# Endpoint-specific rare-event sample size

↑ **Parent:** [Sample size for comparing two proportions](sample-size-for-comparing-two-proportions.md)

A [sample size](sample-size.md) calculation for a binary endpoint requires its own baseline [probability](probability.md), not merely the rate of a related outcome. A fatal-event probability equals the probability of the underlying event times its conditional fatality probability; knowing only that product does not identify the incidence of all events. Thus a mortality rate can size a mortality trial but cannot determine the size for all fatal and nonfatal events without further assumptions. The significance level, allocation, target [statistical power](statistical-power.md) and effect definition must also be specified.
