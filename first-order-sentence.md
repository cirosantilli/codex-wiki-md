# First-order sentence

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A first-order sentence is a formula with no free variables, so its truth in a structure is independent of a variable assignment.

A [sentence](sentence-mathematical-logic.md) has no free variables; a [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) is its first-order instance.
