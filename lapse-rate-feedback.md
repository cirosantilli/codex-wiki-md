# Lapse-rate feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)

Lapse-rate feedback is the radiative effect of changes in the vertical temperature profile beyond a vertically uniform temperature change. In the tropics, enhanced warming aloft often increases radiative cooling and is stabilizing. In a stably stratified polar atmosphere, warming concentrated near the surface can yield less compensating cooling aloft and help produce [polar amplification](polar-amplification.md). The sign and magnitude depend on the profile and radiation physics.
