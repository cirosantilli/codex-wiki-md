# Square wave

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_wave)

A [square wave](square-wave.md) alternates between two constant values for equal halves of a [period](period-of-a-function.md). For the $2\pi$-periodic function equal to $1$ on $(0,\pi)$ and $-1$ on $(\pi,2\pi)$, the [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) are $a_0=a_n=0$ and $b_n=2[1-(-1)^n]/(\pi n)$. Its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) contains only odd [sine](sine.md) modes. At a [jump discontinuity](jump-discontinuity.md) the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) converges to the midpoint $0$, irrespective of the assigned point value.
