# Centralizer lower bound for a root vector

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra](centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

In a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) whose [rank of a semisimple Lie algebra](rank-of-a-semisimple-lie-algebra.md) is $\ell$, a [root vector](root-vector.md) $x$ satisfies $\dim Z_{\mathfrak g}(x)\geq3\ell-2$. To see this, select $\ell-1$ roots whose images form a basis of the real root span modulo $\mathbb R\alpha$, where $x\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$. Take the highest endpoints of the [root strings](root-string.md) through both signs of each selected root. Their $2(\ell-1)$ distinct [root spaces](root-space.md) commute with $x$. Together with $\ker\alpha\subset\mathfrak t$ and $\mathbb Cx$, these give $2(\ell-1)+(\ell-1)+1$ independent vectors in the [Lie algebra centralizer](centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
