# Dipolar flux-tube area

↑ **Parent:** [Dipole magnetic-field line](dipole-magnetic-field-line.md)

Near the polar axis of a [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md), $B\propto r^{-3}$. Conservation of [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) in a [flux tube](flux-tube.md) consequently gives $A(r)\propto r^3$. A [dipole magnetic-field line](dipole-magnetic-field-line.md) intersecting a sphere of radius $R_\star$ at small polar angle $\theta_\star$ obeys

$$
\theta(r)^2\simeq\theta_\star^2\frac r{R_\star},
\qquad
A(r)\simeq\pi r^2\theta(r)^2
=\pi\theta_\star^2\frac{r^3}{R_\star}.
$$

This area is for one polar tube. The approximation requires $\theta_\star^2r/R_\star\ll1$; the full [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) does not remain a narrow polar tube at arbitrarily large radius.
