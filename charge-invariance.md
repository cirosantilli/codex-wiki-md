# Charge invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge_invariance)

[Charge invariance](charge-invariance.md) means that the total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) of a specified collection of particles is unchanged by a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md). It differs from [conservation of electric charge](charge-conservation.md), which compares the same isolated system at different times. For a convective charge distribution with rest density $\rho_0$, the [four-current](four-current.md) is $J^\mu=\rho_0U^\mu$, where $U^\mu$ is its [four-velocity](four-velocity.md); its measured [charge density](charge-density.md) increases under a boost while the simultaneous spatial volume contracts, leaving total charge unchanged.
