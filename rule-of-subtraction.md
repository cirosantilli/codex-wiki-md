# Rule of subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

For [finite sets](finite-set.md), removing the elements of $B$ from $A$ leaves $|A|-|A\cap B|$ elements. Indeed $A$ is the [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) union of $A\setminus B$ and $A\cap B$, so the [rule of sum](addition-principle.md) proves the formula. If $B\subseteq A$, it reduces to $|A\setminus B|=|A|-|B|$.
