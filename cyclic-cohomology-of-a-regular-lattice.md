# Cyclic cohomology of a regular lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Tate cohomology of a cyclic group](tate-cohomology-of-a-cyclic-group.md)

Let $G$ be cyclic and let $R[G]$ carry the regular [permutation action](group-action.md). Invariants are constant coefficient vectors, which are the images of the norm operator. A vector with coefficient sum zero is the difference of a vector and its cyclic translate, by successively solving the coordinate differences. Thus the norm [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) equals $(\sigma-1)R[G]$. Both cyclic [Tate cohomology](tate-cohomology-of-a-finite-group.md) [groups](group-split.md) vanish. This applies to $R=\mathbb Z$ and $R=\mathbb Z_p$, and to finite [direct sums](direct-sum.md) of regular lattices.
