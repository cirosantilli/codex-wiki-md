# Signed divisor-two obstruction for an isogeny covering

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic covering in a two-isogeny descent](quartic-covering-in-a-two-isogeny-descent.md)

The [two-isogeny descent quartics](quartic-covering-in-a-two-isogeny-descent.md) associated with square classes $2$ and $-2$ on $Y^2=X^3+24X^2+64X$ have no rational points. A rational solution can be scaled to integral $W,U,V$ with [coprime integers](coprime-integers.md) $U,V$. If $U$ is odd, reduction modulo sixteen gives $2$ or $10$ for the positive class, and $14$ or $6$ for the negative class; none is a [quadratic residue](quadratic-residue.md). If $U=2u$ is even, then $V$ is odd and the right side is $32(\pm u^4+3u^2V^2\pm V^4)$, with the same sign on the two signed terms. The bracket is odd, so its [2-adic valuation](2-adic-valuation.md) is exactly five, impossible for a nonzero square. These two parity cases exhaust primitive solutions.
