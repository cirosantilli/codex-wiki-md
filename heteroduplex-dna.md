# Heteroduplex DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[heteroduplex DNA](heteroduplex-dna.md) is a duplex whose strands originate from different parental molecules. Related but nonidentical sequences can introduce mismatches; heteroduplex formation does not require that every position be mismatched.
