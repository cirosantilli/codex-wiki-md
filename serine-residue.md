# Serine residue

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

A [serine](serine.md) unit within a [polypeptide](polypeptide.md). Its side-chain hydroxyl can participate in [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) or, in a suitable [active site](active-site.md), act as a catalytic [nucleophile](nucleophile.md).
