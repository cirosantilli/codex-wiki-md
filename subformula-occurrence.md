# Subformula occurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Subformula](subformula.md)

A [subformula occurrence](subformula-occurrence.md) specifies a particular position in the [syntax tree of a formula](syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md). Two occurrences can contain the same [logical formula](logical-formula.md) while being different nodes. Replacement of one occurrence does not necessarily replace the others.
