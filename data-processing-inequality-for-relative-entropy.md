# Data processing inequality for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Data processing inequality for f-divergences](data-processing-inequality-for-f-divergences.md)

This is the [data processing inequality for f-divergences](data-processing-inequality-for-f-divergences.md) specialized to [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md). Passing two [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) through the same [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) cannot increase their [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md). For a kernel $K$,

$$
D(PK\Vert QK)\leq D(P\Vert Q).
$$
