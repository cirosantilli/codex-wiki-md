# Orthogonality of cusp forms and holomorphic Eisenstein series

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md)

For the full modular group, a cusp form is orthogonal under the [Petersson inner product](petersson-inner-product.md) to every holomorphic Eisenstein series of the same weight. Unfolding reduces the integral to the constant Fourier coefficient of the cusp form, which is zero.
