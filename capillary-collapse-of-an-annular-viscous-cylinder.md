# Capillary collapse of an annular viscous cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Annular viscous extension](annular-viscous-extension.md)

For equal internal and external [pressures](pressure.md), zero [axial strain rate](axial-strain-rate.md) and positive [surface tension](surface-tension.md), [annular viscous extension](annular-viscous-extension.md) gives constant $A=R_e^2-R_i^2$ and thickness $D(t)=R_e(0)-R_i(0)+\gamma t/(2\mu)$. Since $R_i=(A/D-D)/2$, the central hole closes at

$$
t_* =\frac{2\mu}{\gamma}\left[\sqrt{A(0)}-R_e(0)+R_i(0)\right].
$$

The formula holds until closure and requires a genuine initial annulus; the subsequent topology has no inner cylindrical interface.
