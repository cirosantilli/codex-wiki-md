# Essential range

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable function](measurable-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essential_range)

The essential range of a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $m$ is the set of scalars $z$ for which $\mu(\{x:|m(x)-z|<\varepsilon\})>0$ for every $\varepsilon>0$. It depends on the measure as well as the function. On a nonzero $L^2$ space over a [sigma-finite measure](sigma-finite-measure.md), it describes the [spectrum of a real multiplication operator](spectrum-of-a-real-multiplication-operator.md) when $m$ is real.
