# Static chain

↑ **Parent:** [Call stack](call-stack.md)

The sequence of [stack frames](stack-frame.md) reached by following [static links](static-link.md). A variable declared $d$ lexical levels outside the current procedure is accessed by following $d$ [static links](static-link.md) and applying its known frame offset.
