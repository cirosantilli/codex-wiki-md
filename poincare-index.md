<h1 id="poincare-index">Poincaré index</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

The Poincare index of a planar vector field around a simple closed curve containing no zero on the curve is the winding number of the vector-field direction along that curve.

The index is therefore a [winding number](winding-number.md) for the normalized vector-field direction.
