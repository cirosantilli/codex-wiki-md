# Laser oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

Sustained stimulated optical emission in a resonator mode, supplied by the pump and reinforced through optical feedback. Its onset is governed by the [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md); an amplifying single pass alone does not establish oscillation.
