<h1 id="coupled-hydrogen-helium-saha-equations">Coupled hydrogen--helium Saha equations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Free-electron fraction during hydrogen--helium recombination](free-electron-fraction-during-hydrogen-helium-recombination.md)

Writing $\mathcal F=n_e/n_B$ couples the two equilibrium equations:

$$
\mathcal F\frac{X_{H^+}}{1-X_{H^+}}=K(T)e^{-I_H/(k_BT)},\qquad
\mathcal F\frac{X_{He^+}}{1-X_{He^+}}=2K(T)e^{-I_{He}/(k_BT)}.
$$
