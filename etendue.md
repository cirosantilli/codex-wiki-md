# Etendue

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md)

[Etendue](etendue.md) measures the area and angular extent of a [light](light.md) bundle: $G=\iint n^2\cos u\,dA\,d\Omega$, approximately $n^2A\Omega$ for a narrow bundle. Ideal lossless specular [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md) conserves it, which constrains how [optical instruments](optical-instrument.md) reformat a field.
