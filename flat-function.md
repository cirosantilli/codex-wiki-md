# Flat function

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth function](smooth-function.md)

A [smooth function](smooth-function.md) is flat at $a$ when every [derivative](derivative.md), including order zero, vanishes at $a$. The function $e^{-1/x^2}$ off zero, extended by zero at zero, is flat there but not identically zero near zero. Thus its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) does not determine it. A [real analytic function](real-analytic-function.md) that is flat at a point vanishes on a neighborhood of that point.
