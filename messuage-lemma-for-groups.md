# Messuage lemma for groups

↑ **Parent:** [Adian–Rabin theorem](adian-rabin-theorem.md)

In the effective group form used for recognition problems, a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md) of $K$ and a word $w$ yield, by an algorithm, a finite presentation of a group $K_w$. If $w=1$ in $K$, then $K_w$ is trivial; if $w\ne1$ in $K$, then the specified generators embed $K$ in $K_w$. One can additionally arrange that the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of the image of $w$ is all of $K_w$. The finite presentation can be written without first deciding which case holds. This is the collapse-versus-embedding construction behind the [Adian–Rabin theorem](adian-rabin-theorem.md).
