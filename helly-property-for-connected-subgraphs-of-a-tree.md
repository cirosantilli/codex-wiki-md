# Helly property for connected subgraphs of a tree

↑ **Parent:** [Connected subgraph of a tree](connected-subgraph-of-a-tree.md)

A finite family of nonempty [connected subgraphs of a tree](connected-subgraph-of-a-tree.md) with pairwise nonempty intersections has a common [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). For a finite ambient [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md), prove this by deleting a [leaf of a tree](leaf-of-a-tree.md) $v$. If one member is $\{v\}$, pairwise intersection proves the result immediately. Otherwise remove $v$ from every member containing it. Each such member remains nonempty and connected and contains the unique neighbour of $v$. Hence every pair still intersects, even if its original intersection consisted only of $v$, and [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) applies. This is the [Helly property](helly-property.md) specialized to [trees](tree-graph-theory-split.md).
