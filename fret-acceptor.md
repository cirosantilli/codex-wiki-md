# FRET acceptor

↑ **Parent:** [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md)

A [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md) receives the [excited state](excited-state.md) [energy](energy.md). Its absorption must support [FRET spectral overlap](fret-spectral-overlap.md) with the [FRET donor](fret-donor.md). [FRET sensitized emission](fret-sensitized-emission.md) requires a fluorescent [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md); measuring a shortened [FRET donor lifetime](fret-donor-lifetime.md) does not.
