# Lithium dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md)

The neutral molecule Li-two. In the elementary valence [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) description, its two [valence electrons](valence-electron.md) occupy the bonding sigma-two-s orbital, giving [bond order](bond-order.md) one and a closed-shell ground state.
