# Homeomorphism invariance of the fixed-point property

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point property](fixed-point-property.md)

If $h:A\to B$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) and $A$ has the fixed-point property, then a continuous $f:B\to B$ induces $h^{-1}fh:A\to A$. A fixed point of the conjugate map is carried by $h$ to a fixed point of $f$.
