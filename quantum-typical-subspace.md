# Quantum typical subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Schumacher compression](schumacher-compression.md)

For a [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\pi=\sum_iq_i|\phi_i\rangle\langle\phi_i|$, the quantum typical subspace is spanned by product eigenvectors of $\pi^{\otimes n}$ whose eigenvalues $q_{i_1}\cdots q_{i_n}$ satisfy

$$
\left|-\frac1n\log_2(q_{i_1}\cdots q_{i_n})-S(\pi)\right|\leq\varepsilon.
$$

It applies the classical [weakly typical sequence](weakly-typical-sequence.md) definition to the spectrum. If $P_\varepsilon^{(n)}$ is its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md), then

$$
\dim\mathcal T_\varepsilon^{(n)}\leq2^{n(S(\pi)+\varepsilon)},\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}(\pi^{\otimes n}P_\varepsilon^{(n)})\longrightarrow1.
$$

The first statement follows from the [typical-set cardinality bounds](typical-set-cardinality-bounds.md), and the second from the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md). This subspace holds nearly all the source probability while using exponentially fewer dimensions than the whole space when $S(\pi)<\log_2d$.
