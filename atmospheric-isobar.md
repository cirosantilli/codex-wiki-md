# Atmospheric isobar

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md)

An [atmospheric isobar](atmospheric-isobar.md) is a line of equal [pressure](pressure-split.md) on a specified atmospheric map. Its spacing indicates the horizontal [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) at that map's reference level; a closer spacing means a larger gradient for the same contour interval.
