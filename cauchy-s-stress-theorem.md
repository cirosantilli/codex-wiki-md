<h1 id="cauchy-s-stress-theorem">Cauchy's stress theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md)

[Cauchy's stress theorem](cauchy-s-stress-theorem.md) states that local contact [traction](traction.md) depends linearly on the oriented unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md): $t_i(\mathbf n)=\sigma_{ij}n_j$, with $\sigma_{ij}=\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf t(\mathbf e_j)$. It follows by balancing [forces](force-split.md) on a shrinking tetrahedron with three coordinate faces and one oblique face. Contact [forces](force-split.md) scale as the face [area](surface-area.md), while bounded [body force](body-force.md) and inertial contributions scale as its [volume](volume.md); dividing by the oblique face [area](surface-area.md) and shrinking eliminates the latter. The coordinate-face projected [areas](surface-area.md) are the oblique face [area](surface-area.md) times $n_j$, giving the displayed linear map. Symmetry of the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) requires the additional balance of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in a continuum without independent couples.
