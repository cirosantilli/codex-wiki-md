# Partition regular matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

A matrix $A$ over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) is partition regular when every [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) of the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) admits a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) vector $x$ satisfying $Ax=0$, with every coordinate a positive integer. Repeated coordinates are permitted.
