# Interior sphere condition

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf lemma](hopf-lemma.md)

A domain satisfies the interior sphere condition at a boundary point if an [open ball](open-ball.md) contained in the domain is tangent to its boundary there. It gives the local barrier geometry for the [Hopf boundary point lemma](hopf-lemma.md). The condition at a single point suffices for the corresponding local derivative conclusion.
