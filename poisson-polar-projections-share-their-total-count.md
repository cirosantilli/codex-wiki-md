# Poisson polar projections share their total count

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson mapping theorem](poisson-mapping-theorem.md)

For unit volume intensity in the unit ball of $\mathbb R^d$, polar coordinates give intensity $r^{d-1}dr\,\sigma(d\omega)$. The separate radius and direction images are Poisson processes, but each counts the same random total number of original points. Their count [covariance](covariance.md) is the displayed intensity of the intersection of their two preimages. Conditional on the total count, radii and directions are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) samples, which does not imply unconditional [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of the two point processes.
