# Hilbert-Schmidt kernel bound

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md)

For $(Tg)(v)=\int k(v,w)g(w)\,dw$, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\|Tg\|_2\leq\|k\|_{L^2(v,w)}\|g\|_2$. The kernel norm is the [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md), which bounds the [operator norm](operator-norm.md). Uniform kernel bounds apply also to spatially parametrized operators after integrating over position.
