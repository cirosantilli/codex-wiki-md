# Reflection symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry (physics)](symmetry-physics.md)

Invariance under a spatial [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md). A one-dimensional [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) invariant under $x\mapsto a-x$ admits [energy eigenfunctions](energy-eigenfunction.md) of definite even or odd midpoint [parity](parity.md). Even and odd functions are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) on a reflection-symmetric interval.
