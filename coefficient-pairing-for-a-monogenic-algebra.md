# Coefficient pairing for a monogenic algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Trace pairing](trace-pairing.md)

For a monic degree-$n$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $g$ over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$, let $A=R[X]/(g)$ and let $\lambda$ take the coefficient of $X^{n-1}$ in the reduced representative. The pairing $(a,b)\mapsto\lambda(ab)$ is unimodular on $1,X,\ldots,X^{n-1}$: reversing one basis order gives a triangular matrix with diagonal ones. When $A$ embeds in a separable field extension, [Lagrange interpolation](lagrange-polynomial.md) identifies $\lambda(a)$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(a/g'(x))$. This yields the derivative formula for the [inverse different](inverse-different.md).
