# Static vacuum conformal spatial metric

↑ **Parent:** [Static spacetime](static-spacetime.md)

Rescaling the spatial metric by the squared static [lapse function](lapse-function.md) gives a useful three-dimensional quotient metric $\gamma$. In [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md), its [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) obeys $R_{ij}(\gamma)=2\partial_iU\,\partial_jU$, while $U$ is [harmonic](harmonic-function.md). The [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) recovers the harmonic equation without dividing at critical points.
