# Kinetic-energy estimate from wavefunction nodes

↑ **Parent:** [Node of a wavefunction](node-of-a-wavefunction.md)

A [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) with extent $a$ and many nearly equally spaced internal [nodes of a wavefunction](node-of-a-wavefunction.md) has local [wavevector](wavevector.md) $k\sim\pi n/a$. Thus $\langle K\rangle=\hbar^2\int|\psi'|^2/(2m\int|\psi|^2)$ scales as $\hbar^2n^2/(ma^2)$. Exact coefficients require the envelope and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). An order-one node-count offset is needed for low states: a nodeless localized state still has positive [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) by the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
