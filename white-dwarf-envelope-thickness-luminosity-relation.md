# White-dwarf envelope thickness-luminosity relation

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical thickness of a white-dwarf radiative envelope](geometrical-thickness-of-a-white-dwarf-radiative-envelope.md)

For fixed mass, degenerate radius and composition, the [matching of a white-dwarf envelope to a degenerate core](matching-of-a-white-dwarf-envelope-to-a-degenerate-core.md) gives $L\propto T_c^{7/2}$, while the [geometrical thickness of a white-dwarf radiative envelope](geometrical-thickness-of-a-white-dwarf-radiative-envelope.md) gives $s\propto T_c$ to leading thin-layer order. Cooling therefore thins this idealized envelope. At finite thickness the ratio additionally contains $(1+q_2)/(1+q_1)$.
