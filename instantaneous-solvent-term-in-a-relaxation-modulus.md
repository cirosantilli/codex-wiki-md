# Instantaneous solvent term in a relaxation modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Relaxation modulus](relaxation-modulus.md)

For a [linear viscoelastic fluid](linear-viscoelastic-fluid.md), write the solvent stress explicitly as $2\mu_0E(t)$, or use the displayed kernel with a causal endpoint delta satisfying $\int_0^\infty\delta_+(s)f(s)\,ds=f(0)$. If a symmetric delta is integrated with half its weight at the endpoint, its coefficient must instead be $2\mu_0$. Mixing those conventions loses a factor of two. The [Oldroyd-B model](oldroyd-b-model.md) has the displayed exponentially decaying polymer part after linearization.
