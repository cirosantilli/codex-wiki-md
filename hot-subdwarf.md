# Hot subdwarf

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

A compact hot evolved [star](star.md) with little remaining envelope. A [hot subdwarf](hot-subdwarf.md) B is commonly interpreted as a core-helium-burning object with a very thin [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) envelope; spectral subtype and evolutionary state must be specified.
