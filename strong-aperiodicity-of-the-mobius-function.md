<h1 id="strong-aperiodicity-of-the-mobius-function">Strong aperiodicity of the Möbius function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md)

The Möbius function stays far in [pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md) from every [Archimedean character](archimedean-character.md). One useful uniform form is

$$
\inf_{|t|\leq2(\log x)^{1/10}}
\mathbb D(\mu,n^{it};x)^2\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

This follows by estimating the prime sum $\sum_{p\leq x}(1+\cos(t\log p))/p$.
