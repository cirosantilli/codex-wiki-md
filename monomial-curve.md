# Monomial curve

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md)

Over a [field](field.md) $k$, an affine curve parametrized by positive integer powers of one parameter. Its coordinate [ring](ring.md) is the subalgebra generated by those powers, and its defining [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) is the kernel of $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\to k[t]$ sending $x_i$ to $t^{a_i}$. Equal exponent sums yield binomial relations. Studying those relations connects an additive semigroup of exponents to the geometry of the curve.
