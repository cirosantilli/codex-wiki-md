# Field isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Field homomorphism](field-homomorphism.md)

A field isomorphism is a bijective [field homomorphism](field-homomorphism.md); its inverse is also a field homomorphism. For fields extending a specified field $K$, an isomorphism may be required to fix $K$ pointwise, a stronger requirement than an abstract field isomorphism.
