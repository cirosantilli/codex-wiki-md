# Carrier density from spin-degenerate quantum oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Filling factor (quantum Hall effect)](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md)

One resolved spin branch of a two-dimensional [Landau level](landau-level.md) has degeneracy $eB/h$ per area. Thus the total [carrier density](carrier-density.md) is $n_s=\nu eB/h$, where the [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) $\nu$ counts all occupied spin and other internal branches, including partial filling. If consecutive unsplit orbital peaks change filling by an unresolved degeneracy $g_d$,

$$
n_s=\frac{g_d e}{h\Delta(1/B)}.
$$

For only spin degeneracy, $g_d=2$. Once spin splitting is resolved, adjacent individual branches differ by one rather than two; counting split subpeaks as unsplit orbital peaks gives a factor-two error.
