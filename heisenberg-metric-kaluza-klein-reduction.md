# Heisenberg metric Kaluza-Klein reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein decomposition along a Killing field](kaluza-klein-decomposition-along-a-killing-field.md)

For $h=\alpha dx^2+\beta dy^2+\chi(dz-y\,dx)^2$, the [Kaluza-Klein decomposition along a Killing field](kaluza-klein-decomposition-along-a-killing-field.md) $\partial_x$ has $V=\alpha+\chi y^2$, $A=-\chi y\,dz/V$ and $\gamma=\beta dy^2+\alpha\chi\,dz^2/V$. For a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), positive constants make the fibre norm and quotient metric positive everywhere.
