# Even part of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The even part of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $P$ is $(P(z)+P(-z))/2$. It retains exactly the terms whose exponents are even. Applied to the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md), it extracts sums of even-indexed [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md).
