# Time-correlated single-photon counting

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence lifetime](fluorescence-lifetime.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-correlated_single-photon_counting)

[TCSPC](time-correlated-single-photon-counting.md) accumulates a [histogram](histogram.md) of detected [photon](photon.md) delays relative to repeated excitation pulses. Each cycle supplies a timing reference; many cycles provide the decay distribution. The [fluorescence instrument response](fluorescence-instrument-response.md) broadens that distribution. In conventional first-event acquisition, losing later [photons](photon.md) in a cycle preferentially preserves early arrivals: this pile-up can falsely shorten the inferred [fluorescence lifetime](fluorescence-lifetime.md). Keep detection probability per cycle low and check stability when the illumination is attenuated.[How Does TCSPC Work?, delay histogram, instrument response and first-photon pile-up paragraphs](time-correlated-single-photon-counting-tcspc-by-picoquant.md)
