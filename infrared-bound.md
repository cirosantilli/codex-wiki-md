# Infrared bound

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian domination](gaussian-domination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_bound)

The infrared bound controls the nonzero Fourier modes of a spin correlation by the inverse lattice dispersion. Its momentum integral is finite in dimension at least three and can force [spontaneous magnetization](spontaneous-magnetization.md) at low temperature.
