# Prize allocation rule

↑ **Parent:** [Contest theory](contest-theory.md)

A prize allocation rule assigns winning probabilities or divisible prize shares to a profile of efforts. In an [all-pay auction](all-pay-auction.md), the greatest effort wins; a [proportional allocation contest](proportional-allocation-contest.md) instead assigns shares continuously according to relative effort. The rule is essential data of a contest model.
