# Fibonacci recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

The [linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md) defining the [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md), with $F_0=0$ and $F_1=1$. Its state $(F_{n+1},F_n)^T$ advances by the [matrix](matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, so [matrix powers](matrix-power.md) describe repeated updates.
