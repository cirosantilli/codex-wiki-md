# Minimum vertex cover

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex cover](vertex-cover.md)

A [minimum vertex cover](minimum-vertex-cover.md) is a [vertex cover](vertex-cover.md) of smallest [cardinality](cardinality.md) among all [vertex covers](vertex-cover.md) of the same finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md). The optimum size is the [vertex cover number](vertex-cover-number.md). By the [complement of a set](complement-of-a-set.md) correspondence, a [minimum vertex cover](minimum-vertex-cover.md) leaves a largest possible [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) unselected.

Minimum size is stronger than merely being unable to remove a selected [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). In the [star graph](star-graph-theory.md) $K_{1,3}$, the three leaves form a [vertex cover](vertex-cover.md) from which no [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) can be removed: each leaf covers its own [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). Nevertheless, the centre alone is a [minimum vertex cover](minimum-vertex-cover.md) of size one. Such an inclusion-minimal [vertex cover](vertex-cover.md) can therefore be much larger than a [minimum vertex cover](minimum-vertex-cover.md).
