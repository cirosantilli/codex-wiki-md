# Tagged encoding of a finite tuple

↑ **Parent:** [Finite tuple](finite-tuple.md)

Using distinct fixed tags $t_1,\ldots,t_n$, encode a [tuple](finite-tuple.md) $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ by the [set](set-split.md) $\{\langle t_i,a_i\rangle:1\le i\le n\}$. [Injectivity](injective-function.md) of the [ordered-pair construction](ordered-pair.md) makes the unique member with first coordinate $t_i$ recover $a_i$. Thus a [set](set-split.md) can retain the order of a [finite tuple](finite-tuple.md) when its entries are tagged, even if some coordinates coincide.
