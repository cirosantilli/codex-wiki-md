# Quasistatic temperature of a conductively cooled film

↑ **Parent:** [Thermocapillary thin-film equation](thermocapillary-thin-film-equation.md)

For a film with fixed lower temperature $T_0$ and upper condition $-\kappa T_z=\alpha(T_s-T_0+\Delta T)$, negligible horizontal thermal [diffusion](diffusion.md) and thermal [advection](advection.md) reduce the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) to $T_{zz}=0$. Thus $T=T_0-\alpha\Delta T z/(\kappa+\alpha h)$. The local approximation requires $(h/L)^2\ll1$ and $Uh^2/(\kappa L)\ll1$, together with slow time variation relative to $h^2/\kappa$. For $\alpha h/\kappa\ll1$, $T_s\simeq T_0-\Delta T\alpha h/\kappa$. The coefficient $\kappa$ is [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md), and the boundary coefficient $\alpha$ is normalized accordingly.
