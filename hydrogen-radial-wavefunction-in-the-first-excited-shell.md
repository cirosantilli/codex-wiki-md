# Hydrogen radial wavefunction in the first excited shell

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

Writing $\rho=r/a_0$, the normalized [radial wavefunctions](radial-wavefunction.md) are $R_{20}=(2-\rho)e^{-\rho/2}/(2\sqrt2a_0^{3/2})$ and $R_{21}=\rho e^{-\rho/2}/(\sqrt{24}a_0^{3/2})$. Radial [normalization](normalization.md) uses $\int r^2|R|^2dr=1$. The first function has one positive [radial node](radial-node.md) at $2a_0$; the second has none. Their behavior at the origin differs from that of the reduced radial function $rR$ and from the [radial probability density](radial-probability-density.md) $r^2|R|^2$.
