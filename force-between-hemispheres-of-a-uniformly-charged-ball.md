# Force between hemispheres of a uniformly charged ball

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

For uniform [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho_e=3Q/(4\pi R^3)$, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives the interior [electric field](electric-field.md) $E=\rho_e r/(3\varepsilon_0)$. Integrate its vertical force density $\rho_e^2z/(3\varepsilon_0)$ over the upper hemisphere: $\int z\,dV=\pi R^4/4$. Internal pair forces in that hemisphere cancel, so its net force is exactly the force from the lower hemisphere. The result is upward and has magnitude $3Q^2/(64\pi\varepsilon_0R^2)$.
