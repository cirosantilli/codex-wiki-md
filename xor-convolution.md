# XOR convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md)

For arrays indexed by $b$-bit [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md), their XOR convolution is $(a*_\oplus c)_r=\sum_i a_i c_{i\oplus r}$, where $\oplus$ is [bitwise exclusive or](exclusive-or.md). If entries count choices of [Grundy numbers](grundy-number.md), its repeated application counts sums of [impartial games](impartial-game.md) with a specified combined [Grundy number](grundy-number.md). The [Hadamard transform](hadamard-transform.md) turns this operation into pointwise multiplication.
