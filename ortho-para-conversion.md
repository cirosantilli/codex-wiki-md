# Ortho-para conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md)

Interconversion of [orthohydrogen](orthohydrogen.md) and [parahydrogen](parahydrogen.md), which changes the coupled nuclear-spin and rotational populations. An active conversion mechanism allows the mole fractions to follow equilibrium as [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes; slow conversion instead gives a [frozen ortho-para mixture](frozen-ortho-para-mixture.md). Whether conversion remains active during a thermal measurement must therefore be distinguished from how the initial sample was prepared.
