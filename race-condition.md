# Race condition

↑ **Parent:** [Thread (computing)](thread-computing.md)

A dependence of behavior on the relative timing or ordering of concurrent activities. In the abstract example under [software thread](thread-computing.md), two increments can leave $x=1$ or $x=2$ depending on their interleaving. A C data race is a language-defined conflict with undefined behavior, rather than a name for every [race condition](race-condition.md).
