# Decay tunnel

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino beam](neutrino-beam.md)

A [decay tunnel](decay-tunnel.md) provides a low-density flight region where selected unstable particles decay before remaining charged particles are removed or absorbed. For a pion-produced [neutrino beam](neutrino-beam.md), its length balances pion decay probability against backgrounds from longer-lived daughters.
