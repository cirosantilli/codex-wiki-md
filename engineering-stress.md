# Engineering stress

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

[Engineering stress](engineering-stress.md) is axial [force](force-split.md) divided by the initial cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md). The initial area remains the denominator throughout a [tensile modulus experiment](tensile-modulus-experiment.md), even if the specimen later changes shape.
