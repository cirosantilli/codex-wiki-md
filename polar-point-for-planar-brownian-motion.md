# Polar point for planar Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Recurrence of planar Brownian motion](recurrence-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

A prescribed point is almost surely never visited by [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started elsewhere. The [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $\log|z-w|$ gives an annular hitting probability that tends to zero as the inner radius decreases to zero. This does not contradict [recurrence of planar Brownian motion](recurrence-of-planar-brownian-motion.md), which concerns neighbourhoods.
