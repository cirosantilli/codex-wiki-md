# Forward-backward bias of phase slips

↑ **Parent:** [Thermally activated phase slip](thermally-activated-phase-slip.md)

In a mobility-one noisy [Adler phase equation](adler-phase-equation.md), matching [Kramers escape rate](kramers-escape-rate.md) prefactors and a barrier difference $2\pi\omega$ give $p_+/p_-=e^{2\pi\omega/T_{\rm eff}}$. Positive mismatch favors forward winding. The ratio is a low-noise barrier-crossing result, not a claim that the particle remains in one well at arbitrarily long times.
