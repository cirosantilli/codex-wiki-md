# Genetic recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_recombination)

Genetic recombination rearranges inherited genetic material into new combinations. In the [meiosis](meiosis.md) example considered here, [chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md) exchanges segments between nonsister [chromatids](chromatid.md) of [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md). A transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md) can consequently contain [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from both parental homologues. This produces new [haplotypes](haplotype.md) without requiring a new [mutation](mutation.md) at either scored [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md). See [Meiosis](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/meiosis.md).

For [phase-known genotype](phase-known-genotype.md) $AB/ab$, a single [chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md) between the selected [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) can yield $Ab$ and $aB$ on the participating [chromatids](chromatid.md); the other two [chromatids](chromatid.md) can remain $AB$ and $ab$. Thus an exchange in the four-[chromatid](chromatid.md) bundle does not mean every resulting [gamete](gamete.md) is recombinant. Only a [chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md) encountered along the transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md) within the selected interval affects its two-locus score.

Follow parental origin along the transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md) from the first [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) to the second. Each exchange switches that origin. An odd number of such [chromosomal crossovers](chromosomal-crossover.md) changes the endpoint combination; an even number restores parental phase. Two exchanges can therefore leave the scored [haplotype](haplotype.md) as $AB$ even though an internal segment came from the other parental [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md). Recombinant status is relative to the chosen [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md), not a complete record of exchanges along the [chromosome](chromosome.md).

This [integer parity](parity-mathematics.md) distinction connects the physical process of [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) to the observed [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md). It also explains why a long [genetic map](genetic-map.md) interval can accumulate many exchanges while its endpoint [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) approaches $1/2$ in the [Haldane mapping function](haldane-mapping-function.md) model.
