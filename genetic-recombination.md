# Genetic recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_recombination)

Genetic recombination produces a chromosome whose inherited segments come from different parental homologues. In a two-locus meiosis, the [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) is the probability that the transmitted combination is recombinant relative to the parental phase.
