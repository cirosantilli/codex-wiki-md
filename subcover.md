# Subcover

↑ **Parent:** [Open cover](open-cover.md)

A [subcover](subcover.md) of an [open cover](open-cover.md) $\{U_i:i\in I\}$ of a [subset](subset.md) $K$ is a selection of its members whose [union](set-union.md) still contains $K$. The selected [open sets](open-set.md) retain their original sizes; selecting a [subcover](subcover.md) does not mean replacing them by smaller [open sets](open-set.md).
