# Equal-environments assumption

↑ **Parent:** [Twin study](twin-study.md)

The assumption that environmental similarity relevant to the measured outcome does not systematically account for the additional resemblance of monozygotic compared with [dizygotic twins](dizygotic-twin.md) in the genetic model. It does not require identical treatment of all twins in every respect.
