# Cubic velocity anti-damping

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity-only forcing in averaged oscillator equations](velocity-only-forcing-in-averaged-oscillator-equations.md)

For $y''+y=\varepsilon(y')^3$ with positive $\varepsilon$, the oscillator [energy](energy.md) increases at rate $\varepsilon(y')^4$. The [amplitude-phase equations](amplitude-phase-equations-for-a-weakly-perturbed-oscillator.md) give $R(T)=R_0(1-3R_0^2T/4)^{-1/2}$. The [weakly nonlinear expansion](weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md) fails when the growing amplitude makes $\varepsilon R^2$ large, so its formal finite-time divergence does not itself control the exact late-time trajectory.
