# CHSH self-testing

↑ **Parent:** [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md)

Maximal quantum [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) violation certifies an embedded maximally entangled qubit pair and its ideal measurement action, up to local isometries and unused ancillary systems, under the separated-device quantum assumptions. It does not identify every microscopic component of an arbitrary device. Near-maximal observed correlations require robust error bounds rather than exact identification.[Robust Self Testing of the Singlet, arXiv:1203.2976, abstract: local bases isolate an approximate EPR pair, potentially encoded within a larger environment or ancilla](robust-self-testing-of-the-singlet-by-matthew-mckague-and-others.md)
