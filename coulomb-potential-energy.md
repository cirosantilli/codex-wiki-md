# Coulomb potential energy

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md)

Two point charges have the displayed [potential energy](potential-energy.md) when it is set to zero at infinite separation. Integrating the radial force in [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md), or differentiating $V$ via $F_r=-dV/dr$, gives this expression. Opposite charges give an attractive negative potential, as in the [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md). Dividing by the test charge instead gives the source charge's scalar [electric potential](electric-potential.md).
