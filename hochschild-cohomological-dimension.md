# Hochschild cohomological dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md)

The Hochschild cohomological dimension of an [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) over a [field](field.md) is $\operatorname{Dim}(A)=\operatorname{pd}_{A\otimes A^{\mathrm{op}}}A$. Equivalently, $HH^n(A,M)$ vanishes above that dimension for every [bimodule](bimodule.md) $M$. A bound by one implies $HH^2(A,A)=0$ and hence [formal rigidity from vanishing second Hochschild cohomology](formal-rigidity-from-vanishing-second-hochschild-cohomology.md).
