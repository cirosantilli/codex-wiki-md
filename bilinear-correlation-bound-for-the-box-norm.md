# Bilinear correlation bound for the box norm

↑ **Parent:** [Box norm](box-norm.md)

With uniform [expectations](expected-value.md) and real or complex [functions](function-split.md),

$$
|\mathbb E_{x,y}f(x,y)u(x)v(y)|\leq\|f\|_{\square}\|u\|_2\|v\|_2.
$$

Apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) first in $y$. Expand the remaining square, and apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) in $(x,x')$ to $u(x)u(x')$ and $\mathbb E_y f(x,y)f(x',y)$. Their squared averages are $\|u\|_2^4$ and $\|f\|_{\square}^4$, respectively.
