# Complete-intersection candidate family

↑ **Parent:** [T-intersecting family](t-intersecting-family.md)

Two members of this [uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md) have at least $2(t+i)-(t+2i)=t$ common points in its distinguished prefix, so it is a [t-intersecting family](t-intersecting-family.md). Nonempty indices satisfy $0\leq i\leq r-t$. Consecutive-family size comparison reduces to the threshold $n_{i+1}=(r-t+1)(2+(t-1)/(i+1))$: gain sets contain both newly introduced coordinates and $t+i-1$ earlier points; loss sets contain neither and $t+i$ earlier points. Counting these sets with [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) proves increase below, equality at and decrease above this threshold.
