# Chern number and integer Hall conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Integer quantum Hall effect](integer-quantum-hall-effect.md)

For filled, separated, noninteracting magnetic Bloch bands, the [Berry curvature](berry-curvature.md) integral over the magnetic [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) is an integer [Chern number](chern-number.md) $C$, giving $|\sigma_{xy}|=|C|e^2/h$. Patches of [Berry connection](berry-connection.md) meet through phases with integer windings; continuous gap-preserving changes cannot change $C$. [D. J. Thouless](david-james-thouless.md), [M. Kohmoto](m-kohmoto.md), [M. P. Nightingale](m-p-nightingale.md) and [M. den Nijs](m-den-nijs.md) developed this band argument; it does not establish the interacting fractional case.[pp. 405–407, Kubo formula and phase winding](quantized-hall-conductance-in-a-two-dimensional-periodic-potential-by-d-j-thouless-and-others.md)
