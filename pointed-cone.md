# Pointed cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cone](convex-cone.md)

A [convex cone](convex-cone.md) containing no nonzero line through the origin, equivalently $K\cap(-K)=\{0\}$. The [copositive cone](copositive-cone.md) is pointed: a [matrix](matrix.md) in both it and its negative has zero [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) on the [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md); testing $e_i$ and $e_i+e_j$ kills all entries.
