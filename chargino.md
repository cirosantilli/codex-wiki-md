# Chargino

↑ **Parent:** [Sparticle](sparticle.md)

A charged fermionic [mass](mass.md) eigenstate formed by mixing charged electroweak [gauginos](gaugino.md) and [higgsinos](higgsino.md) in a supersymmetric model. Its production and decay can yield [leptons](lepton.md) and [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md) when a stable neutral lightest state is present.
