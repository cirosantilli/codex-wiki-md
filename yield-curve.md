# Yield curve

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yield_curve)

The maturity-indexed curve of [interest rates](interest-rate.md) inferred from prices of [zero-coupon bonds](zero-coupon-bond.md). Continuously compounded yields $y(t,T)=-\log P(t,T)/(T-t)$ average the [instantaneous forward rate](instantaneous-forward-rate.md) over $[t,T]$. Modelling a curve requires both dependence across maturities and consistent evolution through observation time; specifying unrelated one-dimensional rate distributions is insufficient.
