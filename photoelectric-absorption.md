# Photoelectric absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)

A [photon](photon.md) transfers its energy to a bound [electron](electron.md), ejecting it if energetically allowed. Subsequent atomic relaxation can emit characteristic radiation or additional electrons. Its material and energy dependence differs from [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md), which matters when converting [computed tomography](computed-tomography.md) attenuation to the higher photon energy of [positron emission tomography](positron-emission-tomography.md).
