# Iterated contraction gives a summable orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric series](geometric-series.md)

If a map on a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) satisfies $\|T(x)\|\le q\|x\|$ for every $x$, with $0\le q<1$, induction gives $\|T^n(x)\|\le q^n\|x\|$. The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) then proves [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) of its orbit series whenever the ambient [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is complete. The scalar map $T(x)=\sin(qx)$ satisfies the bound because $|\sin u|\le|u|$.
