# Conservation of mechanical energy

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md)

For constant masses, a time-independent [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $V$ and forces $\boldsymbol F_i=-\nabla_iV+\boldsymbol R_i$ with zero total extra power $\sum_i\boldsymbol R_i\cdot\boldsymbol v_i=0$, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) gives $dT/dt=-dV/dt$. Thus [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) is conserved. Smooth stationary constraint reactions and a purely magnetic [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) supply zero power. This is more restrictive than [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) in general, which may include thermal or other internal forms.
