# Conditional entropy of a countable measurable partition

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional information function](conditional-information-function.md)

The conditional entropy is the integral of the [conditional information function](conditional-information-function.md). Its chain rule expresses the entropy of a [join of measurable partitions](join-of-measurable-partitions.md) as unconditional entropy plus conditional entropy. [Conditioning reduces entropy](conditioning-reduces-entropy.md), also for countable partitions of finite entropy.
