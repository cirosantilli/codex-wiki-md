# Adjustment coefficient with independent claim expenses

↑ **Parent:** [Adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md)

If each claim includes an independent expense $A$, replace its payment law by the [convolution of independent random variables](convolution-of-independent-random-variables.md) $X+A$. Its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $M_X(r)M_A(r)$ and its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\mathbb EX+\mathbb EA$. Keeping the [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) $\theta$ fixed therefore changes the premium rate as well. The new coefficient solves $M_X(R)M_A(R)-1=(1+\theta)(\mathbb EX+\mathbb EA)R$, within the common finite-transform domain.
