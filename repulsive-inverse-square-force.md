# Repulsive inverse-square force

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md)

The repulsive inverse-square force $\mathbf F=k\mathbf r/r^3$ has [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $V(r)=k/r$ and scatters positive-energy trajectories along hyperbolic conics.
