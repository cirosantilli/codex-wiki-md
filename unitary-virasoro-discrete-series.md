# Unitary Virasoro discrete series

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

An irreducible unitary [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) module of the [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) with $c<1$ has integer $m\ge2$, $1\le r\le m-1$, $1\le s\le m$, and the displayed weights. Labels $(r,s)$ and $(m-r,m+1-s)$ represent the same module. It is obtained from the [Verma module](verma-module.md) by quotienting its zero-norm submodules; the two generating singular-vector levels are $rs$ and $(m-r)(m+1-s)$. For $m=2$ this is the trivial $c=h=0$ module. For $m=3$, the three weights are $0$, $1/2$, $1/16$.
