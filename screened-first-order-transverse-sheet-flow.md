# Screened first-order transverse sheet flow

↑ **Parent:** [Brinkman swimming sheet](brinkman-swimming-sheet.md)

With $s=\sqrt{1+A^2}$, a dimensionless decaying first-order transverse sheet has $f=(s e^{-y}-e^{-sy})/(s-1)$, $f(0)=1$, $f'(0)=0$, and [pressure](pressure.md) $-s(s+1)e^{-y}\cos(x-t)$. The coincident-root limit is $(1+y)e^{-y}$, the ordinary [transverse mode of a Taylor swimming sheet](transverse-mode-of-a-taylor-swimming-sheet.md).
