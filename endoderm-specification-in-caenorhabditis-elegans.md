# Endoderm specification in Caenorhabditis elegans

↑ **Parent:** [Endoderm](endoderm.md)

The [E blastomere](e-blastomere.md) produces the intestinal lineage. Maternal [SKN-1](skn-1.md) and the P2-to-EMS inductive interaction converge on an [endoderm](endoderm.md) [gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md) with parallel inputs and partly redundant [GATA transcription factors](gata-transcription-factor.md). Loss of one late marker does not establish loss of the lineage.[2015, pp. 66–79, MED-binding-site perturbations and quantitative gut phenotypes](med-gata-factors-promote-robust-development-of-the-c-elegans-endoderm-by-morris-f-maduro-and-others.md)
