# Radial magnetic induction scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md)

For [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) velocity and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), with constant [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta$, the [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md) implies

$$
\partial_tP+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla P=\mathbf B\cdot\nabla Q+\eta\Delta P,\qquad Q=\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf x.
$$

The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $D_tP=\mathbf x\cdot D_t\mathbf B+\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf B$ and $\Delta P=\mathbf x\cdot\Delta\mathbf B+2\nabla\cdot\mathbf B$. Consequently the radial velocity, through $Q$, is the source term for the radial [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md).
