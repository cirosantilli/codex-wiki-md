# Closed sublocale

↑ **Parent:** [Sublocale](sublocale.md)

The [closed nucleus](closed-nucleus.md) $c(a)$ determines the complement of the open $a$. A [sublocale](sublocale.md) with nucleus $j$ is dense precisely when $j(0)=0$, since its closure is the [closed sublocale](closed-sublocale.md) defined by $j(0)$.
