# Handlebody

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handlebody)

A genus-$g$ handlebody is a three-ball with $g$ [one-handles](one-handle.md) attached. Its boundary is a closed orientable [topological surface](topological-surface.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$.
