# Canonical product

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass factorization theorem](weierstrass-factorization-theorem.md)

A canonical product builds a prescribed discrete zero set from [Weierstrass elementary factors](weierstrass-elementary-factor.md). Choose the orders $p_n$ so that the logarithms of tail factors converge uniformly on each compact subset. [Infinite product convergence from logarithmic tails](infinite-product-convergence-from-logarithmic-tails.md) then gives an [entire function](entire-function.md) with exactly those zeros, counted with the prescribed multiplicities. When $\sum_n|a_n|^{-1}<\infty$, factors of order zero suffice.
