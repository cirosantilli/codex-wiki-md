# Degree does not classify general manifold maps

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

Although [topological degree](topological-degree.md) classifies maps $S^n\to S^n$ up to [homotopy](homotopy.md), it is not a complete invariant for other manifolds. On the [torus](torus.md), the identity and the integer shear $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ both have degree one, but induce distinct maps on $H_1(T^2;\mathbb Z)$ and cannot be homotopic.
