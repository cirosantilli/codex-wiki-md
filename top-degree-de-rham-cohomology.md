# Top-degree de Rham cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

On a [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) oriented $n$-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) without boundary, integration is a [linear isomorphism](linear-isomorphism.md) $H^n_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\to\mathbb R$. Thus two top-degree [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) have the same [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class exactly when their integrals agree.
