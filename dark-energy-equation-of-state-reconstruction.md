# Dark-energy equation-of-state reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical dark energy](dynamical-dark-energy.md)

[energy](energy-split.md) conservation relates a separately conserved dark-energy density to its equation-of-state history. Distance measurements integrate the associated expansion history, making rapid variation difficult to recover uniquely. Growth and potential-evolution probes add information but depend on gravity and clustering assumptions. Consistency with $w=-1$ over a measured interval cannot prove that a field is exactly constant everywhere.
