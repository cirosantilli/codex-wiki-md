# Definable continuous hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

A definable continuous hierarchy is a definable class function from the [ordinals](ordinal.md) to [sets](set-split.md), with increasing levels $H_\alpha$ and the displayed continuity condition at nonzero [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md). Its union is a definable [class in set theory](class-set-theory.md). One often additionally requires [transitive sets](transitive-set.md) as levels. Finite collections of [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) reflect along this hierarchy by the [reflection theorem for definable hierarchies](reflection-theorem-for-definable-hierarchies.md).
