# Generalized Pauli matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md)

A traceless [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) in a [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md) orthonormal basis of the real vector space of traceless Hermitian $N$-by-$N$ matrices. One choice uses symmetric and imaginary antisymmetric off-diagonal matrix units, normalized by $1/\sqrt2$, and traceless diagonal generators. Together with $I/\sqrt N$ they form an orthonormal basis of all Hermitian matrices. The coordinates $s_k=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\sigma_k)$ give the [Generalized Bloch representation](generalized-bloch-representation.md).
