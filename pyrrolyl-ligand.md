# Pyrrolyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

The [anion](anion.md) formed by N–H deprotonation of [pyrrole](pyrrole.md). It can bind through [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) as a two-electron $\eta^1$ donor, or through its [aromatic ring](aromatic-ring.md) as a six-electron $\eta^5$ donor. Specifying [hapticity](hapticity.md) is essential for [electron counting](electron-counting.md).
