# Escapement

↑ **Parent:** [Clock](clock.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escapement)

A mechanism coupling a clock's oscillator to its driven gear train: it releases the train in controlled steps and supplies impulses that compensate the oscillator's energy losses. The [balance wheel](balance-wheel.md) or pendulum regulates the release, while the energy ultimately comes from the clock's power source.
