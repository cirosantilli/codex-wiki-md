# London penetration depth

↑ **Parent:** [London equations](london-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_penetration_depth)

For uniform vortex-free [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md), the [London equation](london-equations.md) and stationary Maxwell equations give $\nabla^2\mathbf B=\mathbf B/\lambda_L^2$. A field tangential to a planar superconducting half-space decays as $\mathbf B(z)=\mathbf B(0)e^{-z/\lambda_L}$. A nonzero normal boundary field is incompatible with this translation-invariant decaying Meissner solution, because divergence-free magnetic flux would require that component to remain constant.
