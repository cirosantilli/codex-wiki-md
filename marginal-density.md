# Marginal density

↑ **Parent:** [Marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md)

A marginal density is a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) for one component of a [joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md). Integrating the [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) over the other coordinates gives it; for two components, $f_X(x)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy$.
