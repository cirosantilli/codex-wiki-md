# Dirichlet resonance in an equilateral triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Laplacian](dirichlet-laplacian.md)

If $\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3$ are the affine barycentric coordinates of an [equilateral triangle](equilateral-triangle.md) of side $\ell$, then $h=\prod_j\sin(\pi\beta_j)=\frac14\sum_j\sin(2\pi\beta_j)$ vanishes on the boundary and satisfies $\Delta h=-16\pi^2h/(3\ell^2)$. Thus the problem $\Delta q-4\lambda q=0$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) has distinct solutions zero and $h$ at the displayed negative parameter. Their normal traces differ, demonstrating failure of a uniquely determined [Dirichlet-to-Neumann map](dirichlet-to-neumann-map.md) at resonance.
