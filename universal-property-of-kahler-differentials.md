<h1 id="universal-property-of-kahler-differentials">Universal property of Kähler differentials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler differential](kahler-differential.md)

Composing an $A$-linear map with $d:A\to\Omega_{A/k}$ gives a $k$-[derivation](derivation-of-an-algebra.md). Every derivation factors uniquely this way because its linearity and product rule are exactly the defining relations of the [module of Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md).
