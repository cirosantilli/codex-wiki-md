# Chirp mass

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian binary chirp](newtonian-binary-chirp.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chirp_mass)

The [chirp mass](chirp-mass.md) is the combination of two component [masses](mass.md) controlling the leading [Newtonian binary chirp](newtonian-binary-chirp.md). Inverting its frequency equation gives

$$
\mathcal M=\frac{c^3}{G}\left[\frac{5}{96}\pi^{-8/3}f^{-11/3}\dot f\right]^{3/5}.
$$

Use $f$ in hertz and $\dot f$ in hertz per second to obtain kilograms. For equal component [masses](mass.md) $m$, $\mathcal M=m/2^{1/5}$. A stipulated $20+20$ [solar mass](solar-mass.md) system therefore has $\mathcal M\approx17.411$ [solar masses](solar-mass.md). In a cosmological observation the time and frequency measured at the detector instead yield $(1+z)\mathcal M_{\rm source}$, where $z$ is [redshift](redshift.md); phase evolution alone does not independently determine the source [mass](mass.md) and [redshift](redshift.md).
