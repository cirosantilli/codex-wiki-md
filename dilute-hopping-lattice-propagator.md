# Dilute-hopping lattice propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

For equivalent minima with nearest-neighbour hopping magnitude $\Delta$, paths with $r$ right steps and $s$ left steps have $r-s=n-m$. Summing $(\Delta\tau/\hbar)^{r+s}/(r!s!)$ and applying the [Fourier representation of a Kronecker delta](fourier-representation-of-a-kronecker-delta.md) gives the displayed [modified Bessel function](modified-bessel-function.md) kernel in a normalized localized-site basis. Its Fourier exponent gives the [tight-binding model](tight-binding.md) energy $E(\theta)=E_{\rm well}-2\Delta\cos\theta$. Position-endpoint kernels can have an additional common local-wavefunction prefactor.
