# Disk bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The disk bundle of a metric [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) consists of the vectors of norm at most one. Its boundary is the [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) $S(E)$, and radial contraction makes $D(E)$ homotopy equivalent to its zero section.
