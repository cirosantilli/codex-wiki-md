# Rotational temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational_temperature)

For a [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md), [rotational temperature](rotational-temperature.md) expresses the rotational [energy](energy-split.md) constant in kelvins: $E_J=k_B\theta_rJ(J+1)$, where $I$ is the perpendicular [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). With all integer $J\geq0$ allowed and degeneracies $2J+1$, its rotational [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $Z=\sum_J(2J+1)e^{-\theta_rJ(J+1)/T}$. It approaches $1+3e^{-2\theta_r/T}$ at low [temperature](temperature-split.md) and $T/\theta_r$ at high [temperature](temperature-split.md). The latter limit gives mean rotational [energy](energy-split.md) $k_BT$ and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $k_B$ per rotor.
