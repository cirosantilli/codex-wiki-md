# Technetium-99m

↑ **Parent:** [Radiopharmaceutical](radiopharmaceutical.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technetium-99m)

A metastable nuclear isomer used in diagnostic tracers, with approximately six-hour [half-life](half-life.md) and $140\,\mathrm{keV}$ imageable [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md). Its chemical carrier determines biological uptake.
