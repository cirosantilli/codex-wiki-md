# Coherency strain

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent crystal interface](coherent-crystal-interface.md)

Elastic deformation required to match differing natural lattice spacings across a [coherent crystal interface](coherent-crystal-interface.md). Its energy can affect precipitate shape and loss of coherency.
