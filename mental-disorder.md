# Mental disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Psychiatry](psychiatry-split.md)

A clinically significant disturbance in psychological functioning associated with relevant distress or impairment. Diagnostic categories specify patterns for assessment and research; sharing a diagnosis does not establish identical causes or an identical treatment need.
