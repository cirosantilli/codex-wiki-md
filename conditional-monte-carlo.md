# Conditional Monte Carlo

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method.md)

Replacing a simulated quantity $H$ by $E[H\mid\mathcal G]$ preserves its expectation and reduces variance by the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md). This is [Rao-Blackwellization](rao-blackwellization.md). For a random sum, conditioning on the count and all but one summand replaces an event indicator by a [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) evaluation. The resulting estimator needs that conditional expectation to be computable.
