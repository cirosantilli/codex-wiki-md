# Bipartite planar graph edge bound

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph edge bound](planar-graph-edge-bound.md)

If a finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) is a [connected graph](connected-graph.md), a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md), and a [planar graph](planar-graph.md), with $n\geq3$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), then $m\leq2n-4$. Every [facial boundary walk](facial-boundary-walk.md) has even length and length at least three, hence at least four. Thus $4f\leq2m$; substitute the [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md).[Mathematics for Computer Science, §12.5, Lemma 12.5.2 and Theorem 12.5.3, printed pp. 486–487](mathematics-for-computer-science-by-eric-lehman-and-others.md)

The square [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) attains equality: $4=2\cdot4-4$. The three-[vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) also attains equality: $2=2\cdot3-4$. Its repeated [bridges in a graph](bridge-graph-theory.md) illustrate why counting [facial boundary walk](facial-boundary-walk.md) length correctly matters. Neither bound applies unchanged to a [multigraph](multigraph.md): two [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) may support arbitrarily many parallel [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) in a [plane graph](plane-graph.md).
