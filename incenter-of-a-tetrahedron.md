# Incenter of a tetrahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md)

The center of the sphere tangent to all four faces is the positive face-area-weighted combination of the opposite vertices. If $V$ is the [volume](volume.md) and $S$ the total face area, its distance from each face is $3V/S$. Indeed its weight for vertex $A$ is $S_A/S$ and the corresponding altitude is $3V/S_A$, so their product is the common distance. Positive weights place it inside the [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md).
