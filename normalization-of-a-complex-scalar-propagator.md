# Normalization of a complex scalar propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a complex scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

For positive kinetic coefficient $\zeta$ in $\mathcal L_0=\zeta(\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-m^2\phi^*\phi)$, the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is $\pi_\phi=\zeta\dot\phi^*$. Positive-energy oscillator modes with unit oscillator [commutators](commutator.md) have amplitude $1/\sqrt{2\zeta E_{\mathbf p}}$. The mixed time-ordered [propagator](propagator.md) is $C=\zeta^{-1}D_F$, with $D_F$ the ordinary [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md). This also follows by inverting the quadratic form in the [path integral](path-integral.md). Rescaling $\phi$ changes interaction coefficients as well as external correlation-function factors.
