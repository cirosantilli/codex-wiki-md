# Coherer

↑ **Parent:** [Wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coherer)

A filings coherer changes from high to low [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) following electrical excitation. A local [battery](electric-battery.md) supplies the readout [electric current](electric-current.md); a tap resets the detector. It records events rather than the incident [radio wave](radio-wave.md) waveform.

Marconi described nickel and silver filings between silver plugs, with a cell and sensitive [telegraph relay](telegraph-relay.md) controlling a tapper and recorder.[1909 lecture, printed p. 197, Fig. 1 and following paragraph: coherer construction and local readout](wireless-telegraphic-communication-by-guglielmo-marconi.md)
