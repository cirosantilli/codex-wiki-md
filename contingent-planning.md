# Contingent planning

↑ **Parent:** [Automated planning](automated-planning.md)

[Automated planning](automated-planning.md) whose result may branch on information revealed during execution. In [partially observable search](partially-observable-search.md), an [AND-OR search](and-or-search.md) chooses an action at each OR node and requires a continuation for each possible observation at its AND branches. A contingent plan can therefore be a conditional tree or a policy on [belief states](belief-state.md), rather than one fixed action sequence.
