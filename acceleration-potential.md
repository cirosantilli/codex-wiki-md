# Acceleration potential

↑ **Parent:** [Material acceleration](material-acceleration.md)

A [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) whose negative spatial [gradient](gradient.md) is the [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md). For a smooth global potential, the spatial [curl](curl.md) of the [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md) vanishes. On a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) the converse holds under the usual smoothness assumptions. Its physical dimension is squared [length](length.md) divided by squared [time](time-in-physics.md).
