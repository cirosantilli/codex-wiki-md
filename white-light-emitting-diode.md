# White light-emitting diode

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md)

An [additive color](additive-color.md) display mixes separate red, green and blue emitters. A [phosphor-converted white light-emitting diode](phosphor-converted-white-light-emitting-diode.md) converts some blue [photons](photon.md) to longer-wavelength [light](light.md) in a [phosphor](phosphor.md), mixing it with unconverted blue to produce perceived white. [Hiroshi Amano](hiroshi-amano.md) distinguishes display and [phosphor](phosphor.md)-lighting applications.[§I, p. 1133: full-color displays and phosphor-based white light](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/pdf/10.1103/RevModPhys.87.1133)
