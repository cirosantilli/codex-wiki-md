<h1 id="quadratic-variation-obstruction-to-holder-regularity">Quadratic variation obstruction to Hölder regularity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

A function with finite [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md) of exponent $\alpha>1/2$ has squared-increment sum on a uniform $N$-interval partition bounded by $C^2N^{1-2\alpha}$, hence tending to zero. This conflicts with a nonzero quadratic-variation limit. In particular [dyadic quadratic variation of Brownian motion](dyadic-quadratic-variation-of-brownian-motion.md) prevents a Brownian coordinate law supported on such a [Hölder space](holder-space.md). This argument alone gives no conclusion at the critical exponent $1/2$.
