# Labelled unicyclic graph count

↑ **Parent:** [Unicyclic component](unicyclic-component.md)

A labelled [unicyclic component](unicyclic-component.md) has a unique cycle of length $k\geq3$, with a rooted forest attached to its vertices. Choose its vertices in $\binom nk$ ways and its unoriented cycle in $(k-1)!/2$ ways. The [labelled forest count with prescribed roots](labelled-forest-count-with-prescribed-roots.md) gives $kn^{n-k-1}$ attachments. Summing over $k$ and putting $j=n-k$ yields the displayed identity.
