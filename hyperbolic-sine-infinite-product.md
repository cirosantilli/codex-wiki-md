# Hyperbolic-sine infinite product

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite product](infinite-product.md)

This [infinite product](infinite-product.md) converges uniformly on compact subsets of the complex plane. Pair the factors at $iz$ and $-iz$ in the [Weierstrass product for the reciprocal gamma function](weierstrass-product-for-the-reciprocal-gamma-function.md), and use the [Gamma reflection formula](gamma-reflection-formula.md) together with $\Gamma(1+w)=w\Gamma(w)$. This gives $[\Gamma(1+iz)\Gamma(1-iz)]^{-1}=\sinh(\pi z)/(\pi z)$ and the displayed product. The removable value at $z=0$ is one. Matching zeros alone would not exclude multiplication by a nonvanishing [entire function](entire-function.md); the normalized product fixes that ambiguity.
