# Pyruvate kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Pyruvate kinase](pyruvate-kinase.md) catalyzes [phosphate](phosphate.md) transfer from [phosphoenolpyruvate](phosphoenolpyruvate.md) to [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md), producing [pyruvate](pyruvate.md) and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) in [glycolysis](glycolysis.md).
