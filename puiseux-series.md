# Puiseux series

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent series](laurent-series.md)

A Puiseux series permits fractional exponents with a fixed common denominator: $\sum_{n\geq n_0}a_nz^{n/q}$. These are natural local expansions of branches of algebraic functions. A [fractional-power root expansion](fractional-power-root-expansion.md) uses such powers to resolve roots that coalesce as a parameter tends to zero.
