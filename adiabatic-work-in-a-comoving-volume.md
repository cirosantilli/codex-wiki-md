# Adiabatic work in a comoving volume

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md)

For a homogeneous cosmic fluid, the physical volume bounded by fixed [comoving coordinates](comoving-coordinate.md) obeys $V\propto a^3$. The [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) therefore gives $d(\rho c^2V)=-P\,dV$. This is the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) with conserved [entropy](entropy-split.md) and no additional chemical work. The [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) of that volume need not be constant: a component with negative [pressure](pressure-split.md) gains [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) as the universe expands. Local [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) does not imply a global conserved [energy](energy-split.md) for an arbitrary expanding spacetime.
