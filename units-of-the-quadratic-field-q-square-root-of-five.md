# Units of the quadratic field Q square root of five

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$,

$$
\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z\left[\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right].
$$

Writing $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, the full unit group is

$$
\mathcal O_K^\times=\{\mathord\pm\phi^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
$$
