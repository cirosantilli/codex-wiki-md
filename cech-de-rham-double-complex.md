<h1 id="cech-de-rham-double-complex">Čech-de Rham double complex</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md)

On a good cover, combine Čech degree $p$ and differential-form degree $q$ in the double complex of forms on intersections. The Čech differential and [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) commute, so the total differential $\delta+(-1)^p d$ squares to zero. A partition of unity and the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) identify its cohomology with both constant-sheaf and [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md). The sign convention makes [Čech-de Rham curvature descent](cech-de-rham-curvature-descent.md) explicit.
