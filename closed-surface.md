# Closed surface

↑ **Parent:** [Closed manifold](closed-manifold.md)

A [compact](compact-space.md) two-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md) without boundary. A [connected](connected-space.md) [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md) is classified by its [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md); for [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ its [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is $2-2g$.
