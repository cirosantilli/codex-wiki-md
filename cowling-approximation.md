# Cowling approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear adiabatic stellar oscillation equations](linear-adiabatic-stellar-oscillation-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowling_approximation)

The Cowling approximation neglects the perturbation of the gravitational potential while retaining the equilibrium gravitational force in a [stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md). It simplifies short-wavelength mode propagation, but can be inaccurate for low-order modes whose perturbed self-gravity matters.
