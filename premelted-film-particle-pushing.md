# Premelted-film particle pushing

↑ **Parent:** [Particle pushing by a freezing front](particle-pushing-by-a-freezing-front.md)

For a sphere partially below a planar melting isotherm, write $c=\cos\theta_0$ and depth $a(\cos\theta-c)$. Local [premelting](premelting.md) gives film thickness $d^3=K/(\cos\theta-c)$. Conservation in the thin spherical film gives flux $Va\sin\theta/2$ and [pressure](pressure.md) $p=3\mu Va^2(\cos\theta-c)^2/K$. Integrating vertical repulsive and hydraulic stresses over the wetted cap yields the displayed speed. The unknown thermodynamic constant $K$ cancels, but a narrow rim where the film ceases to be thin is excluded from the leading approximation.
