# Wiener-Hopf equation

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener-Hopf method](wiener-hopf-method.md)

A scalar Wiener-Hopf equation couples two [Half-range Fourier transforms](half-range-fourier-transform.md) analytic in complementary half-planes. After dividing by a kernel factor, additive splitting separates the known forcing. Source poles, boundary terms and contour prescriptions are part of the problem data.
