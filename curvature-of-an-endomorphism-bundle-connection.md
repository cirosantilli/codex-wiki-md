# Curvature of an endomorphism bundle connection

↑ **Parent:** [Endomorphism bundle connection](endomorphism-bundle-connection.md)

For the [endomorphism bundle connection](endomorphism-bundle-connection.md), $(\widetilde\nabla_X\phi)(s)=\nabla_X(\phi s)-\phi(\nabla_Xs)$. Expanding two derivatives and subtracting $\widetilde\nabla_{[X,Y]}$ gives

$$
F_{\operatorname{End}(A)}(X,Y)\phi
=F_A(X,Y)\circ\phi-\phi\circ F_A(X,Y).
$$

Thus its [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) is the commutator action of the original curvature.
