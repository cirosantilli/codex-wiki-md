# Gaussian interior layer of a conservative drift equation

↑ **Parent:** [Matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md)

The equation $\epsilon y''+xy'+y=0$ integrates to $\epsilon y'+xy=C$. Equal endpoint data $y(-1)=y(1)=A$ force $C=0$ and give the unique Gaussian solution above. Its central width is $\sqrt\epsilon$, with amplitude $A e^{1/(2\epsilon)}$. The exponentially large central mode is missed by an assumed bounded algebraic [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) $C/x$. Changes of order one relative to either endpoint value occur over distance $O(\epsilon)$ from that endpoint.
