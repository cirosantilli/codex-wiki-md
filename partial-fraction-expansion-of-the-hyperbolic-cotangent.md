# Partial-fraction expansion of the hyperbolic cotangent

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic cotangent](hyperbolic-cotangent.md)

The [hyperbolic-sine infinite product](hyperbolic-sine-infinite-product.md) gives, by taking its logarithmic derivative,

$$
\coth z=\frac1z+2z\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{z^2+\pi^2n^2}.
$$

The identity holds away from the poles, with locally convergent sums. For real $z>0$, it makes $(z\coth z-1)/z^2$ positive and strictly decreasing. It is useful for [Hartmann flow](hartmann-flow.md) flux monotonicity and small-argument expansions of [hyperbolic functions](hyperbolic-function.md).
