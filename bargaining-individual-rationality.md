# Bargaining individual rationality

↑ **Parent:** [Nash bargaining problem](nash-bargaining-problem.md)

A payoff is individually rational for a [Nash bargaining problem](nash-bargaining-problem.md) when neither player receives less than its [disagreement point](disagreement-point.md) payoff. Essentiality permits strict improvement for both. Maximizing a positive [Nash product](nash-product.md) therefore selects a strictly individually rational payoff.
