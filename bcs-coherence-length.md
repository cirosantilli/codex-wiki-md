# BCS coherence length

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The clean isotropic weak-coupling [BCS coherence length](bcs-coherence-length.md) is conventionally $\xi_0=\hbar v_F/(\pi\Delta(0))$, with [Fermi velocity](fermi-velocity.md) $v_F$. It characterizes the spatial extent of pairing correlations.[pp.1195–1196, equations (5.43) and (5.50)](theory-of-superconductivity-by-john-bardeen-and-others.md)

The scale follows from the same spectral calculation. Linearizing the normal [energy](energy-split.md) near the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) gives $\xi_k\simeq\hbar v_F(k-k_F)$. The region $|\xi_k|\lesssim\Delta$ therefore has radial width $\delta k\sim\Delta/(\hbar v_F)$. [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md) associates this width with a distance $\ell\sim1/\delta k\sim\hbar v_F/\Delta$. This argument establishes the scale, not the conventional factor $1/\pi$. A small [superconducting energy gap](superconducting-energy-gap.md) consequently permits pairing correlations over many interparticle spacings.
