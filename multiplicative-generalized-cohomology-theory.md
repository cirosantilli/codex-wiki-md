# Multiplicative generalized cohomology theory

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md)

A [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md) with natural unital cup products compatible with relative groups, suspension, and the cohomology axioms. Its coefficient groups form a graded ring. Thom orientations make the cohomology of a bundle's [Thom space](thom-space.md) a shifted module over the cohomology of the base.
