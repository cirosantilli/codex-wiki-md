# Brewster solar elevation

↑ **Parent:** [Brewster's angle](brewster-s-angle.md)

For sunlight reflected from a horizontal flat [dielectric](dielectric.md) interface, incidence angle is measured from the vertical while solar elevation is measured from the horizontal. The Brewster condition $\tan\theta_B=n_2/n_1$ therefore gives $h_B=\pi/2-\theta_B=\tan^{-1}(n_1/n_2)$. Reflected light has its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) perpendicular to the vertical incidence plane, so its surviving [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) is horizontal. This ideal result assumes real [refractive indices](refractive-index.md), negligible absorption, nonmagnetic media and a smooth interface.
