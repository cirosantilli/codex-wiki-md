<h1 id="britton-s-lemma">Britton's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britton's_lemma)

In an HNN extension, every word containing a stable letter and having no pinch $t^{-1}ct$ or $tdt^{-1}$ is nonidentity. In particular, the natural map from the base group to its HNN extension is injective.
