# Tensile conversion of an obstacle shear stress

↑ **Parent:** [Orowan bypass](orowan-bypass.md)

An obstacle calculation produces a resolved shear-stress increment. A single crystal needs its [Schmid factor](schmid-factor.md), while a polycrystal needs a deformation-geometry or [Taylor factor](taylor-factor.md). The relation $\Delta\sigma=M\Delta\tau$ therefore requires an explicit orientation assumption rather than a universal tensile prefactor.
