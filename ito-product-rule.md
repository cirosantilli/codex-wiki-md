<h1 id="ito-product-rule">Itô product rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Itô's lemma](ito-s-lemma.md)

For continuous semimartingales,

$$
d(X_tY_t)=X_t\,dY_t+Y_t\,dX_t+d[X,Y]_t.
$$

It is the stochastic counterpart of the ordinary [product rule](product-rule.md); the [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md) supplies the additional second-order term.
