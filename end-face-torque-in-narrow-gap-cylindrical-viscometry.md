# End-face torque in narrow-gap cylindrical viscometry

↑ **Parent:** [Couette flow](couette-flow.md)

A rotating inner cylinder of radius $R$ and height $H$ has a uniform gap $h$ from a fixed housing at its side and both ends. For $h\ll R,H$, [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md) and laminar [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) give side [torque](torque.md) $2\pi\eta\omega R^3H/h$. Each end contributes $\int_0^R(\eta\omega s/h)(2\pi s\,ds)s=\pi\eta\omega R^4/(2h)$. Summing both end faces proves the displayed expression. Shaft area, corner regions, gas slip and viscous heating are neglected.
