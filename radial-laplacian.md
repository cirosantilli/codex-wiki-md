# Radial Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radial_Laplacian)

For radial $u(r)$ in $n$ dimensions, $\Delta u=u\prime\prime+(n-1)u\prime/r$.
