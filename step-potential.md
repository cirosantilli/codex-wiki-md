# Step potential

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_potential)

For constant [mass](mass.md) $m$, a finite jump from [potential energy](potential-energy.md) zero at $x<0$ to $V_0>0$ at $x>0$ requires continuity of the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) and its first [derivative](derivative.md). With incidence only from the left and $E>V_0$, put $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $q=\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}/\hbar$. Matching $A+B=C$ and $k(A-B)=qC$ gives reflected [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) $B/A=(k-q)/(k+q)$ and [transmission amplitude](transmission-amplitude.md) $C/A=2k/(k+q)$. The [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) is $(q/k)|C/A|^2$, conserving [probability current](probability-current.md).

For $0<E<V_0$, replace $q$ by $i\kappa$, where $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$ is the [tunnelling decay constant](tunnelling-decay-constant.md). The right-hand [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is $Ce^{-\kappa x}$, with zero [probability current](probability-current.md). Its [probability density](probability-density.md) decays on length $1/(2\kappa)$, while its amplitude decays on length $1/\kappa$. The semi-infinite raised region gives total reflection, unlike a finite [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md) allowing [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) into a second allowed region.
