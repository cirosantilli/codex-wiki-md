# Cross-ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-ratio)

For four distinct points of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), their cross-ratio is

$$
[z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4]
=\frac{(z_4-z_2)(z_1-z_3)}
{(z_4-z_3)(z_1-z_2)},
$$

with the evident limiting conventions at infinity. Every Möbius transformation preserves it.
