# Excited-state variational constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md)

Unrestricted minimization of a [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) seeks the ground state. To bound the $k$th excited [energy](energy-split.md) from above, restrict normalized trial [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) to the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of the exact lower-energy eigenspaces. In an exact [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), all remaining expansion coefficients multiply energies at least $E_k$, so their weighted average is at least $E_k$. A known [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) sector can supply some of these exclusions automatically. [Orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) only to inaccurate approximate lower states does not automatically retain the same rigorous excited-state upper bound.
