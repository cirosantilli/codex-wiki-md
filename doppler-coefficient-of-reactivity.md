# Doppler coefficient of reactivity

↑ **Parent:** [Reactivity (nuclear engineering)](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md)

The fuel-temperature derivative of [reactor reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) associated with thermal broadening of nuclear resonances, with the other state variables specified. In ordinary uranium-fueled water reactors it is negative: heating the fuel increases effective resonance capture. Its magnitude depends on spectrum, fuel state and resonance self-shielding, so its sign does not determine how its magnitude changes under a lattice redesign.[Doppler coefficient entry](reactor-glossary-by-the-united-states-nuclear-regulatory-commission.md)
