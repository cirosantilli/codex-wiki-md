# Neurotransmitter clearance

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md)

[Neurotransmitter clearance](neurotransmitter-clearance.md) removes released [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) from the region where it can activate [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md), through [diffusion](diffusion.md), cellular uptake or [enzyme](enzyme.md)-mediated breakdown. Its routes and rates help delimit a [chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md)'s response in space and time; see the mechanisms and source under [chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md).
