# Measurement channel

↑ **Parent:** [Measure-and-prepare channel](measure-and-prepare-channel.md)

A measurement channel writes a [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md)'s outcome into an orthogonal classical register:

$$
\Phi(X)=\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}(E_aX)|a\rangle\langle a|.
$$

For any input [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $|j\rangle$, the [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) $K_{a,j}=|a\rangle\langle j|\sqrt{E_a}$ realize this map and satisfy $\sum_{a,j}K_{a,j}^\dagger K_{a,j}=I$. It is therefore a trace-preserving [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md).
