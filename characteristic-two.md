# Characteristic two

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md)

In a [field](field.md) of characteristic two, $1+1=0$. The usual splitting of a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) into its [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) and [exterior square](exterior-square.md) by $(I\pm\tau)/2$ is unavailable.
