<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

By June 2022, important principles of [reading](../../../reading.md) acquisition were established, including the need to learn written word forms and to understand the [language](../../../language.md) they express. The major unanswered questions concerned how these skills become efficient and integrated, why [children](../../../child.md) respond differently to experience and instruction, and how experimental gains become sustained comprehension and participation. Useful future research should target those uncertainties with explicit competing predictions rather than reopen every instructional question as if no evidence existed.

One question concerns what is learned from a successful encounter with a new written word. Does the [child](../../../child.md) acquire an item-specific [spelling](../../../spelling.md), a reusable mapping, a contextual guess, or some combination? [David L. Share](../../../david-l-share.md)'s delayed [learning](../../../learning.md) experiments motivate the distinction, while [artificial orthography](../../../artificial-orthography.md) work shows that strong item [learning](../../../learning.md) and generalisation need not move together. Future studies should therefore distinguish recognition of practised items from transfer to novel combinations and later retention, rather than use one immediate naming test as a complete index of [learning](../../../learning.md).[Journal of Experimental Child Psychology 72, 95–129; second-grade pseudoword reading, delayed spelling-choice/naming/spelling and recoding controls](../../../phonological-recoding-and-orthographic-learning-a-direct-test-of-the-self-teaching-hypothesis-by-david-l-share.md) [Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition 37, 60–76; sixteen/32 adults, novel script, frequency/consistency and pretraining](../../../the-influence-of-consistency-frequency-and-semantics-on-learning-to-read-an-artificial-orthography-paradigm-by-joanne-s-h-taylor-and-others.md)

A specific experiment could [randomise](../../../randomization.md) unfamiliar but pronounceable items to matched exposure schedules varying the consistency of a [spelling](../../../spelling.md)–sound relation. Hold the number of encounters, visual complexity and oral [vocabulary](../../../vocabulary.md) knowledge constant. Assess trained [spellings](../../../spelling.md) against homophonic alternatives after a delay, and test untrained recombinations that require the relevant mapping. [Eye tracking](../../../eye-tracking.md) during text [reading](../../../reading.md) could establish where the new form attracts [attention](../../../attention.md), while comprehension questions verify meaningful engagement. The experiment should specify whether its prediction concerns initial [learning](../../../learning.md), retention or generalisation.

A second question is how the distribution of teaching examples interacts with the learner's existing knowledge. [Keith S. Apfelbaum](../../../keith-s-apfelbaum.md) and colleagues found that varied irrelevant consonants helped first graders learn vowel mappings. It remains necessary to identify when variation helps, whether its effects depend on prior [decoding](../../../word-decoding.md) and how long the advantage lasts. A multisite classroom experiment could independently vary relevant consistency and irrelevant variation while matching total teaching time and opportunities to respond.[Developmental Psychology 49, 1348–1365; 224 first graders, vowel mappings with varied/similar consonants and transfer tasks](../../../statistical-learning-in-reading-variability-in-irrelevant-letters-helps-children-learn-phonics-skills-by-keith-s-apfelbaum-and-others.md)

Pretraining measures should be collected before [randomisation](../../../randomization.md), and interaction tests [powered](../../../statistical-power.md) prospectively. A significant benefit in one subgroup and a nonsignificant effect in another does not demonstrate different treatment effects. Instructional [fidelity](../../../treatment-fidelity.md) and actual exposure need observation because a teacher may adapt a nominally identical activity in different ways. Broader [spelling](../../../spelling.md), unfamiliar-word [reading](../../../reading.md) and later passage comprehension would distinguish a useful instructional principle from a narrow training-task effect.

A third question is how best to coordinate word-level and language-level support. [Margaret J. Snowling](../../../margaret-snowling.md) and colleagues' longitudinal profiles show that [dyslexia](../../../dyslexia.md), [DLD](../../../developmental-language-disorder.md) and their co-occurrence can lead to comprehension difficulty through different constraints. The unresolved question is not whether either dimension matters, but which combination, timing and intensity of support helps a specified [child](../../../child.md). Diagnostic categories are too coarse to substitute for a measured profile.[Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry 61, 672–680; dyslexia-only, DLD-only and comorbid groups at eight, comprehension at eight/nine](../../../dyslexia-and-developmental-language-disorder-comorbid-disorders-with-distinct-effects-on-reading-comprehension-by-margaret-j-snowling-and-others.md)

A factorial teaching trial could retain adequate core [reading instruction](../../../reading-instruction.md) for everyone and independently [randomise](../../../randomization.md) additional [decoding](../../../word-decoding.md) and [oral language](../../../oral-language.md) components, with contact and resource use measured. Include [children](../../../child.md) across the skill distribution rather than only extreme, mutually exclusive categories. Prespecify [primary outcomes](../../../primary-outcome.md) for word [reading](../../../reading.md), [language](../../../language.md) and comprehension, with delayed follow-up. A subsequent adaptive stage could [randomise](../../../randomization.md) a well-defined change for [children](../../../child.md) not responding, instead of attributing improvement after an uncontrolled adjustment to personalisation itself.

The trial would also address a fourth question: what constitutes meaningful transfer? [Janice M. Keenan](../../../janice-m-keenan.md) and colleagues showed that comprehension assessments differ in their reliance on component skills. An intervention can look more successful simply because its outcome task resembles training. Future evaluations need independent passages, several assessment formats and checks that the construct retains a comparable meaning across time and groups. Function in the classroom and the [child](../../../child.md)'s experience should accompany test scores, without treating either as a substitute for accurate measurement.[Scientific Studies of Reading 12, 281–300; five measures, differential decoding/listening dependence and developmental changes](../../../reading-comprehension-tests-vary-in-the-skills-they-assess-differential-dependence-on-decoding-and-oral-comprehension-by-janice-m-keenan-and-others.md)

A fifth uncertainty concerns how findings generalise across writing systems, [language](../../../language.md) backgrounds and educational environments. Artificial-script work offers experimental control, but [Joanne S. H. Taylor](../../../joanne-s-h-taylor.md) and colleagues' instruction study used adults [learning](../../../learning.md) small novel [vocabularies](../../../vocabulary.md). It cannot establish the full trajectory of a multilingual [child](../../../child.md) encountering an unfamiliar [language](../../../language.md) of instruction. Coordinated cross-language studies should define the mappings and [language](../../../language.md) experiences that differ, rather than merely compare national averages.[Journal of Experimental Psychology: General 146, 826–858; 24 adults, two novel scripts, eight-day training, generalisation and fMRI](../../../comparing-and-validating-methods-of-reading-instruction-using-behavioural-and-neural-findings-in-an-artificial-orthography-by-joanne-s-h-taylor-and-others.md)

Recruitment should include varied socioeconomic circumstances and [children](../../../child.md) whose [language](../../../language.md) development differs from the majority sample. Assessment needs to separate opportunity to learn from persistent difficulty and to examine the validity of the measures in each context. Shared protocols and culturally appropriate materials can coexist. The aim is to test which mechanisms generalise and which depend on script, [vocabulary](../../../vocabulary.md) or teaching, not presume that every observed group difference reveals an intrinsic deficit.

Computational models can sharpen all these questions. A model should learn from a specified developmental input, make predictions for held-out items and trajectories, and face comparison with alternatives that use different representations or [learning](../../../learning.md) rules. Matching average skilled-reader accuracy is weaker than predicting the effect of an experimental change in input. Parameters should not be allowed to absorb every individual difference without independent constraints; failure should inform revision rather than invite unrestricted refitting.

Finally, the timescale of research must match the educational claim. Short experiments identify candidate processes, longitudinal cohorts reveal ordering and trajectories, and controlled implementation studies assess delivery and lasting benefit. [Preregistration](../../../preregistration.md), transparent exclusions, appropriate handling of school clustering and missing follow-up, and independent replication make the chain more credible. The central unanswered problem is how to connect the [child](../../../child.md)'s particular [learning](../../../learning.md) history and profile with an instructionally manipulable process that produces durable, generalisable gains.
