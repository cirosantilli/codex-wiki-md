# Moduli-space approximation for solitons

↑ **Parent:** [Collective coordinate of a soliton](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md)

Substitute a static soliton family with slowly time-dependent [collective coordinates](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md) into the action. The kinetic energy induces a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $g$ on the family, after imposing any [Gauss law constraint in gauge theory](gauss-law-constraint-in-gauge-theory.md) constraint and projecting out gauge directions. If its static energy is constant, the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) are the [geodesic](geodesic.md) equations of $g$. An approximately flat family instead carries a potential $V(q)$. This approximation neglects excitations of the other field modes and is justified only when their effects are small on the time and energy scales studied.
