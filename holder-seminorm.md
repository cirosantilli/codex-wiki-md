<h1 id="holder-seminorm">Hölder seminorm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder space](holder-space.md)

The Hölder seminorm of exponent $0<\alpha\leq1$ is

$$
[f]_{C^{0,\alpha}}=\sup_{x\ne y}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{|x-y|^\alpha}.
$$

It measures [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) without including the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of the function itself.
