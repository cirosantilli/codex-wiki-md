↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

These are the one-dimensional [shallow water equations](../../../../shallow-water-equations.md) with bed slope and [friction](../../../../friction.md). The coordinate $s$ measures distance downstream, $t$ is time, $A(s,t)$ is the wetted cross-sectional area, $u(s,t)$ is section-averaged downstream [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), and $h(s,t)$ is [water](../../../../water.md) [water depth](../../../../water-depth.md) above the bed. Thus $Au$ is [water](../../../../water.md) volume flux and the first equation expresses [mass conservation](../../../../mass-conservation.md).

In the [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) equation, $u_t+uu_s$ is the material acceleration, $g$ is gravitational acceleration, and $S$ is the positive downward bed slope, so $gS$ drives the flow. The wetted perimeter is $l$ and $f$ is a dimensionless [friction](../../../../friction.md) coefficient in the stated convention. The frictional deceleration is $flu^2/A$ for downstream flow, while $-gh_s$ is the [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) contribution from surface-height variation relative to the sloping bed. The formula $u^2$ assumes the chosen positive-flow direction; a bidirectional [friction](../../../../friction.md) model uses $u|u|$.
