<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $B$ be the constant canal width, large compared with [water depth](../../../../water-depth.md). Then $A\simeq Bh$ and $l\simeq B$, so [friction](../../../../friction.md) is $fu^2/h$. Choose a reference uniform [water depth](../../../../water-depth.md) $H$, normal-flow speed $U$ satisfying $fU^2/H=gS$, and scales

$$
\ell=H/S,\qquad t_0=\ell/U,\qquad h=H\widehat h,\quad u=U\widehat u,\quad s=\ell\widehat s,\quad t=t_0\widehat t.
$$

The [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md) becomes $\widehat h_{\widehat t}+(\widehat h\widehat u)_{\widehat s}=0$. Dividing [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) by $gS$ and dropping hats gives

$$
\boxed{h_t+(hu)_s=0,\qquad F^2(u_t+uu_s)=1-\frac{u^2}{h}-h_s,\qquad F=\frac{U}{\sqrt{gH}}=\sqrt{\frac Sf}.}
$$

The [Froude number](../../../../froude-number.md) compares [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) to long gravity-[wave speed](../../../../wave-speed.md). At the reference state, $F<1$ is [subcritical flow](../../../../subcritical-flow.md), $F=1$ critical flow, and $F>1$ [supercritical flow](../../../../supercritical-flow.md). In dimensional variables the [wave](../../../../wave-split.md) characteristics travel at $u\pm\sqrt{gh}$: one can propagate upstream in subcritical flow, while both move downstream in supercritical flow. Away from the reference state the local [Froude number](../../../../froude-number.md) is $F|u|/\sqrt h$.
