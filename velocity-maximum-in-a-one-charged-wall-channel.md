# Velocity maximum in a one-charged-wall channel

↑ **Parent:** [One-charged-wall electro-osmotic channel](one-charged-wall-electro-osmotic-channel.md)

Differentiating the screened-channel [velocity](velocity.md) gives $x_{\max}=d-\kappa^{-1}\operatorname{arsinh}[(\cosh\kappa d-1)/(\kappa d)]$. For $\kappa d\gg1$, $x_{\max}\simeq\log(\kappa d)/\kappa$. The outer flow declines almost linearly to the passive no-slip wall rather than forming a symmetric plug.
