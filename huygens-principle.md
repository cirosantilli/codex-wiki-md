# Huygens' principle

↑ **Parent:** [Huygens-Fresnel principle](huygens-fresnel-principle.md)

Each point on a [wavefront](wavefront.md) acts as a source of secondary wavelets, whose envelope gives the next wavefront. The geometric construction gives [refraction from secondary wavelets](refraction-from-secondary-wavelets.md). The [Huygens-Fresnel principle](huygens-fresnel-principle.md) additionally keeps the amplitudes and phases needed to calculate [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) and [diffraction](diffraction.md).
