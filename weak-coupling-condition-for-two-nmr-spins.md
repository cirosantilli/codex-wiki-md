# Weak coupling condition for two NMR spins

↑ **Parent:** [NMR scalar coupling](nmr-scalar-coupling.md)

For the two-[spin](spin.md) [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=(\omega_1ZI+\omega_2IZ+J(XX+YY+ZZ))/2$, juxtaposition of two [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) denotes their [tensor product](tensor-product.md). Put $\Delta=\omega_1-\omega_2$. The exchange matrix element between $|01\rangle$ and $|10\rangle$ is $J$, whereas their diagonal separation is $\Delta$. [Nondegenerate perturbation theory](nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) therefore gives admixtures $\pm J/\Delta$ and second-order frequency shifts $\pm J^2/\Delta$. Neglecting the exchange terms is justified to leading order when $|J/\Delta|\ll1$. A large common [Zeeman energy](zeeman-energy.md) is insufficient. To approximate accumulated phases by this leading-order [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), one also needs $J^2t/|\Delta|\ll1$ over the observation time, or must include the second-order shifts.

For fixed nonidentical [chemical shifts](chemical-shift.md), increasing the static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) increases $|\Delta|$ in angular-frequency units while the [NMR scalar coupling](nmr-scalar-coupling.md) changes little. If $\Delta=0$ remains exact, the approximation fails at every nonzero $J$.
