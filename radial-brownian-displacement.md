# Radial Brownian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For isotropic three-dimensional [diffusion](diffusion.md) with coefficient $D$, the vector mean displacement is zero, the radial RMS is $\sqrt{6Dt}$ and the mean radial magnitude is $4\sqrt{Dt/\pi}$. The radial distribution is the [Maxwell speed distribution](maxwell-speed-distribution.md) arising from three independent Gaussian coordinate increments. State the statistic before calling a quantity an average distance; it is not the cumulative path length.
