# Simple compressible system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)

A [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) for which the reversible mechanical [work](work.md) is entirely [pressure](pressure.md)-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) work. At fixed composition its [internal energy](internal-energy.md) is specified by [entropy](entropy.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md); additional work modes require additional state variables.
