# Boroxine

↑ **Parent:** [Organoboron compound](organoboron-compound.md)

A six-membered alternating [boron](boron.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) ring, commonly $\mathrm{(RBO)_3}$. Three-coordinate [boron](boron.md) has a vacant p orbital that accepts [oxygen](oxygen.md) lone-pair donation. This stabilizing delocalization does not imply the same [aromaticity](aromaticity.md) or equal bond polarity as benzene.
