# X-ray emission spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic X-ray](characteristic-x-ray.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_emission_spectroscopy)

[X-ray emission spectroscopy](x-ray-emission-spectroscopy.md) studies the [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md) of emitted [X-rays](x-ray.md). The [spectral line](spectral-line.md) positions identify electronic [energy](energy-split.md) differences; their strengths also depend on excitation, [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) inside the sample, geometry and detector response. An [X-ray tube](x-ray-tube.md) may excite a sample with [electrons](electron.md), while [X-ray fluorescence](x-ray-fluorescence.md) uses incident [photons](photon.md). The distinction matters when choosing what the experiment measures: identifying a [chemical element](chemical-element.md) from a line position is different from determining its abundance from a line strength.
