# Direct revelation mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

A direct revelation mechanism asks agents to report their types directly, then computes allocation and payments from those reports. Being direct does not itself imply [strategyproofness](strategyproofness.md); the allocation and payment rules must supply the incentive guarantee.
