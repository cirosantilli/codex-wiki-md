# Stellar spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md)

The distribution of radiation from a [star](star.md) with [wavelength](wavelength.md) or frequency, including its continuum and [absorption lines](absorption-line.md). Continuum colors and temperature-dependent line strengths provide complementary estimates of [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md). Spectra of an unresolved [binary star](binary-star.md) may need to be separated by eclipse observations or their different velocity shifts before the component [effective temperatures](effective-temperature.md) can be inferred.
