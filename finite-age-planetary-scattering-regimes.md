# Finite-age planetary scattering regimes

↑ **Parent:** [Comet energy diffusion](comet-energy-diffusion.md)

At fixed stellar [mass](mass.md) $M_\star$, planetary [mass density](density.md) $\rho_p$, age $t_\star$ and initial ratio $\kappa=a_0/a_p$, the order-unity [Safronov number](safronov-number.md) comparison and [comet energy diffusion](comet-energy-diffusion.md) give boundaries

$$
M_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{\frac{3}{32\pi}}M_\star^{3/2}\rho_p^{-1/2}a_p^{-3/2},
\qquad
M_{\rm age}=M_\star\sqrt{\frac{P_p}{100t_\star}}\kappa^{-1/4}.
$$

Their logarithmic slopes in planetary [mass](mass.md) versus radius are $-3/2$ and $3/4$. Above both, strong kicks and a short estimated diffusion time favour ejection; below the escape boundary, repeated weak encounters favour collision statistically. Below the age boundary, the stated diffusion mechanism has not completed on its characteristic clock. Collision lifetimes require additional cross-section and encounter-rate information, so this map alone cannot establish retention or [accretion](accretion.md) within the age.
