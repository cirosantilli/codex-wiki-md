# Practical range

↑ **Parent:** [Range of a semivariogram](range-of-a-semivariogram.md)

A practical range specifies a distance at which a chosen fraction of the structured sill is reached. For the [Gaussian semivariogram](gaussian-semivariogram.md) $\sigma^2(1-e^{-(h/a)^2})$, the 95-percent practical range is $a\sqrt{\log20}$, obtained by solving $e^{-(h/a)^2}=0.05$.
