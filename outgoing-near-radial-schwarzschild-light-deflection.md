# Outgoing near-radial Schwarzschild light deflection

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild light deflection](schwarzschild-light-deflection.md)

A [Schwarzschild static observer](schwarzschild-static-observer.md) at [areal radius](areal-radius.md) $R$ emits an outward [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) at a small angle $\psi$ to the radial direction. Its [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) is $b=R\sin\psi/\sqrt f$, and the accumulated azimuth to infinity is $\int_R^\infty b\,dr/[r^2\sqrt{1-f(r)b^2/r^2}]=b/R+O(b^3/R^3)$. Thus weak [curvature](curvature.md) magnifies a narrow outward beam angle by $1/\sqrt{f(R)}$, rather than producing the full infinity-to-infinity deflection $4GM/(bc^2)$.
