# Gravitational collapse of a settled particle layer

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

A concentrated thin layer of solids can become self-gravitating if particle random velocities and stellar shear do not support it. [Gas](gas.md) turbulence, drag and settling change both concentration and velocity dispersion. Collapse into solid planetesimals supplies seeds for [core accretion](core-accretion.md) and is distinct from direct fragmentation of a gaseous disk patch.
