# Heliocentric model

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomy](astronomy-split.md)

An astronomical model in which Earth and the other planets orbit the Sun. Its acceptance and detailed mathematical form have distinct histories; heliocentrism alone does not imply [elliptic orbits](elliptic-orbit.md).
