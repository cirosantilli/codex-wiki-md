# Gamma trace of a vector-spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Rarita-Schwinger field](rarita-schwinger-field.md)

The gamma trace contracts the vector index of a [Rarita-Schwinger field](rarita-schwinger-field.md) with a [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md), producing an ordinary spinor. In the massive free theory it vanishes by the field equations. In the massless theory a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) changes it by $\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\epsilon$, allowing a gamma-traceless gauge for propagating modes. Together with transversality this removes the unwanted spin-one-half sector.
