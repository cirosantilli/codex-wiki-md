# Undecidability of beta equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md)

[Beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md) is [semidecidable](recursively-enumerable-set.md): enumerate finite conversion proofs. It is not decidable. A proposed total numeral-valued classifier of two nonempty disjoint [beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md) classes can be reversed by a [Church Boolean](church-boolean.md) selector; a [self-quoting fixed point](self-quoting-fixed-point.md) of this reversal then belongs to the opposite class from the classifier output.
