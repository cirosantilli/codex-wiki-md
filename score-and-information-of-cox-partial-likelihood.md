# Score and information of Cox partial likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Cox partial likelihood](cox-partial-likelihood.md)

At an untied event time, the [Cox partial likelihood](cox-partial-likelihood.md) weights the current [risk set](risk-set.md) by $w_{jk}=e^{\beta^Tz_k}/\sum_{\ell\in R_j}e^{\beta^Tz_\ell}$. Differentiation gives the displayed [score function](informant-function.md) and the negative Hessian as a sum of weighted covariances. The score is the sum of [Schoenfeld functions](schoenfeld-function.md), so [Schoenfeld residuals](schoenfeld-residual.md) sum to zero at a finite interior unpenalized fit. At arbitrary trial coefficients or boundary fits, a zero sum is not a general identity.
