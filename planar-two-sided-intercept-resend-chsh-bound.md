# Planar two-sided intercept-resend CHSH bound

↑ **Parent:** [Intercept-resend attack](intercept-resend-attack.md)

Use Alice's CHSH axes $\hat x,\hat y$ and Bob's $(\hat x+\hat y)/\sqrt2,(-\hat x+\hat y)/\sqrt2$, with sign pattern $S=C_{11}-C_{13}+C_{31}+C_{33}$. If Eve measures both singlet particles along planar angles $\alpha,\beta$, the [intercept-resend attack](intercept-resend-attack.md) correlation gives $S=-\sqrt2\cos^2(\alpha-\beta)$. Consequently $-\sqrt2\le S\le0$. Randomly mixing axes independently of the users' secret settings preserves this interval by averaging. The bound is specific to these settings and this attack, rather than a general security theorem.
