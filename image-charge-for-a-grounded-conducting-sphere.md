# Image charge for a grounded conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge](image-charge.md)

For a charge $q$ at $\mathbf p$, $R=|\mathbf p|>a$, outside a grounded sphere of radius $a$, the displayed image gives zero potential on the sphere and decay at infinity. On $|\mathbf x|=a$, one has $|\mathbf x-\mathbf p'|=(a/R)|\mathbf x-\mathbf p|$, proving boundary cancellation. The induced force is inward, of magnitude $q^2aR/[4\pi\varepsilon_0(R^2-a^2)^2]$, from [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) applied to the image field. The self-field is excluded.
