# Compressibility

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compressibility)

Compressibility measures fractional volume reduction per increase in [pressure](pressure.md), with another thermodynamic variable held fixed. Holding [temperature](temperature.md) fixed gives isothermal compressibility; holding [entropy](entropy.md) fixed gives adiabatic compressibility for a reversible process. Its reciprocal is the bulk modulus. The constraint matters: these responses need not agree.
