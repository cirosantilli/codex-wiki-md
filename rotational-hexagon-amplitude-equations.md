# Rotational hexagon amplitude equations

↑ **Parent:** [Hexagonal convection amplitude equations](hexagonal-convection-amplitude-equations.md)

For a [resonant three-wave triad](resonant-three-wave-triad.md) with rotational and translational [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) but no spatial [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md), the cubic [amplitude equations](amplitude-equation.md) have the cyclic form $\dot A=\mu A+\overline B\,\overline C-\nu_1|A|^2A-(\nu_2+\delta)|B|^2A-(\nu_2-\delta)|C|^2A$. A half-turn induces [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md), so all coefficients are real. The coefficient $\delta$ distinguishes the two cyclic cross-couplings that a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) would interchange.
