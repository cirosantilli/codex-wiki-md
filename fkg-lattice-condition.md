# FKG lattice condition

↑ **Parent:** [FKG inequality](fkg-inequality.md)

This lattice condition on configuration masses is the hypothesis of the [FKG inequality](fkg-inequality.md). A [product measure](product-measure.md) on a finite Boolean lattice satisfies it with equality, since in each coordinate the minimum and maximum retain the original two coordinate values. It implies [positive association of random variables](positive-association-of-random-variables.md) for increasing functions.
