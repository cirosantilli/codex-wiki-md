# Good-solvent brush height

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer brush](polymer-brush.md)

For an overlapping layer of long flexible grafted [polymers](polymer-split.md), [concentration blobs](concentration-blob.md) of width $D\sim\sigma^{-1/2}$ contain $g\sim(D/a)^{5/3}$ statistical units under the three-dimensional [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md). A chain of $N/g$ blobs has height $h\sim ND/g$, giving the displayed [good solvent](good-solvent.md) scaling. Numerical prefactors are not supplied by this argument; extension must remain below the [polymer contour length](polymer-contour-length.md).
