# Correlation-length critical exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Critical exponent](critical-exponent.md)

The correlation-length critical exponent is defined by $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$. At a [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md), its reciprocal is the positive eigenvalue of the temperature-like scaling direction.
