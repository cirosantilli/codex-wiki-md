# Boussinesq top-hat plume in a stratified ambient

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md)

With common factors of $\pi$ removed, steady kinematic fluxes satisfy $Q_z=2\alpha\sqrt M$, $M_z=FQ/M$ and $F_z=-N^2Q$. The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) supplies volume, the integrated [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) force supplies momentum, and the environmental [mass density](density.md) gradient reduces [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md). This uses the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md).
