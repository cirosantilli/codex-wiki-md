# Cassegrain reflector

↑ **Parent:** [Reflecting telescope](reflecting-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassegrain_reflector)

A Cassegrain arrangement places a secondary before the primary focus and returns light through a hole in the primary to a focus behind it. In the classical design the primary is parabolic and the secondary convex hyperbolic. The compact arrangement has a long effective [focal length](focal-length.md); its classical implementation retains off-axis [coma](coma-optics.md).
