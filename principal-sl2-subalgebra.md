# Principal sl2 subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A principal sl2 subalgebra of a semisimple [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has semisimple generator $h_{\mathrm{pr}}=2\rho^\vee$. Restriction to it packages a representation's weights into ordinary $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ weight strings.

The infinitesimal embedding is a subalgebra of the [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md); integrating it to a Lie-group homomorphism is a separate group-level construction.
