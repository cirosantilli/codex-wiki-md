# Cellular free resolution from a contractible universal cover

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For a connected [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $X$ with contractible [universal cover](universal-cover.md), let $\Gamma=\pi_1(X)$ and $R=\mathbb Z\Gamma$. Lift each cell once and transport its orientation by [deck transformations](deck-transformation.md). Its translates form an $R$-basis of the cellular chains of the cover. The augmentation sends every vertex to one, and contractibility makes the augmented chain complex an exact [free resolution](free-resolution.md) of the trivial module $\mathbb Z$. An $R$-linear cochain with trivial coefficient action is constant on each orbit of lifts, identifying $\operatorname{Hom}_R(C_*(\widetilde X),\mathbb Z)$ with the [cellular cohomology](cellular-cohomology.md) complex of $X$. Consequently $H^i(X;\mathbb Z)=\operatorname{Ext}_R^i(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z)$. The same argument with singular simplex lifts works for spaces with a contractible universal cover.
