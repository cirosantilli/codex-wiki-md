# Exact mean integrated squared error of a kernel density estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel density estimation](kernel-density-estimation.md)

For independent observations with common density f, the [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md) has mean $K_h*f$. Its pointwise variance is $n^{-1}[(K_h^2*f)-(K_h*f)^2]$. Integrate the variance and add integrated squared bias to get the displayed exact [mean integrated squared error](integrated-mean-squared-error.md), assuming the integrals are finite. Since $R(K_h)=R(K)/h$, this also separates the smoothing bias from the stochastic variance.
