# Compactly supported dual of a compact submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare dual](poincare-dual.md)

For a compact oriented submanifold without boundary in an oriented [manifold](topological-manifold.md), the normal [Thom class](thom-class.md) gives a [compactly supported cohomology](compactly-supported-cohomology.md) class dual to its pushed-forward [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md). It is independent of the [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) and its ordinary-cohomology image vanishes on the complement of the submanifold. A noncompact submanifold closed only as a subset generally has its dual in ordinary cohomology, using locally finite homology instead.
