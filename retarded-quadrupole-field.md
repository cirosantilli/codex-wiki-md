# Retarded quadrupole field

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md)

For a compact slowly moving source in the [weak-field approximation](weak-field-approximation.md), with no incoming homogeneous wave and $G=c=1$, the retarded [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md) give this leading far-zone [trace-reversed metric perturbation](trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md). The key consequence of [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) is $\ddot I_{ij}=2\int T_{ij}\,d^3x$. The physical [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md) is obtained by the [transverse-traceless projector](transverse-traceless-projector.md).
