<h1 id="guided-search-6-0">Guided Search 6.0</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Guided search](guided-search.md)

A 2021 modeling development by [Jeremy M. Wolfe](jeremy-m-wolfe.md), combining feature-based guidance, selection history, reward and scene information with selection, recognition and quitting processes. It models serial deployment with overlapping processing rather than assuming that only one complete operation occurs at a time. Model timings are hypotheses tested by simulation, not direct anatomical measurements.[Abstract and model architecture, published February 2021](guided-search-6-0-an-updated-model-of-visual-search-by-jeremy-m-wolfe.md)
