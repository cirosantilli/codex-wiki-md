# Proline

↑ **Parent:** [Proteinogenic amino acid](proteinogenic-amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proline)

[Proline](proline.md) is a cyclic [alpha-amino acid](alpha-amino-acid.md) whose [amine](amine.md) [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) is joined to two [carbon](carbon.md) positions within a five-membered ring. This explains the exception to $\mathrm{H_2N{-}CH}(R)\mathrm{{-}COOH}$ in [amino acid](amino-acid.md).
