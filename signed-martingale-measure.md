# Signed martingale measure

↑ **Parent:** [One-period quadratic hedge](one-period-quadratic-hedge.md)

A finite [signed measure](signed-measure.md) with total mass one under which discounted asset gains have zero integrals. Its density can be negative, so it need not be a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) or give arbitrage-free prices to every nonnegative [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md). A square-integrable signed density is useful in [one-period least-squares hedging](one-period-quadratic-hedge.md), even when it is not an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md).
