# Isobaric recombination-cooling stability with constant volumetric heating

↑ **Parent:** [Isobaric thermal stability of a heated ideal gas](isobaric-thermal-stability-of-a-heated-ideal-gas.md)

Take fully ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) with electron and proton [number densities](number-density.md) both $n$, fixed local heat input $\Gamma$ per unit volume, and volumetric cooling $3n^2\alpha_B(T)k_BT/2$. The [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) is $P=2nk_BT$. At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md), net cooling becomes $\mathcal L=3P^2\alpha_B(T)/(8k_BT)-\Gamma$. At an equilibrium $T_0$, differentiation gives the displayed derivative.

The linearized thermal equation is $\rho_0c_p\dot{\delta T}=-\mathcal L_T\delta T$, so equilibrium is stable exactly when the logarithmic slope of $\alpha_B$ exceeds one. A constant or decreasing coefficient gives instability. A different heating prescription must be differentiated too; constant incident radiation does not generally imply a density-independent local heating rate.
