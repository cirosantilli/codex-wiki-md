# Virial contraction under stellar evaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evaporation](stellar-evaporation.md)

For an isolated quasistatic cluster with near-zero escape energies, the bound negative energy is approximately fixed. The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) and [gravitational radius](gravitational-radius.md) then imply $R_g\propto M^2$. A homologous mean density scales as $M^{-5}$ and increases as mass evaporates. Tidal limitation or finite positive escape energies modifies this simple model.
