# Magnetic subcriticality of a razor-thin disk

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk](gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk-split.md)

For an isolated cold initially resting razor-thin disk, the displayed pointwise bound makes the absolute [gravity-equivalent magnetic surface density](gravity-equivalent-magnetic-surface-density.md) smaller than its actual [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). The [positive-kernel comparison of thin-disk fields](positive-kernel-comparison-of-thin-disk-fields.md) then makes the integrand in the [horizontal virial balance of a cold magnetized fluid](horizontal-virial-balance-of-a-cold-magnetized-fluid.md) negative above and below the disk. Consequently its radial second moment begins to decrease. This establishes initial contraction in the global virial sense, not inward acceleration of every individual fluid element or unlimited subsequent collapse.
