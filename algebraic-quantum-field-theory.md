# Algebraic quantum field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_quantum_field_theory)

[Algebraic quantum field theory](algebraic-quantum-field-theory.md) assigns an observable algebra $\mathcal A(O)$ to each suitable spacetime region $O$. Inclusion of regions implies inclusion of algebras, and spacelike-separated algebras commute under [microcausality](microcausality.md). A state is a positive normalized functional on the observable algebra, so its restriction to a region specifies expectation values and correlations for that algebra. [Spectrum conditions](spectrum-condition.md) restrict translation representations, while [primitive causality](primitive-causality.md) and [time-slice axioms](time-slice-axiom.md) constrain the dynamics; these are different assumptions.
