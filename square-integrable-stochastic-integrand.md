# Square-integrable stochastic integrand

↑ **Parent:** [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md)

For a zero-starting L2-bounded continuous martingale $M$, the space $L^2(M)$ consists of predictable $H$ with $\mathbb E\int_0^\infty H_s^2\,d[M]_s<\infty$, identifying processes equal almost everywhere for the quadratic-variation measure. The finite-horizon version stops the integral at $T$. The [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) is the isometric extension from elementary predictable integrands.
