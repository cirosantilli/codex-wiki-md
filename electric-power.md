# Electric power

↑ **Parent:** [Power](power.md)

The instantaneous rate of [energy](energy-split.md) transfer through a circuit port is $P(t)=V(t)I(t)$, with positive [electric current](electric-current.md) entering the positive [voltage](voltage.md) terminal for the passive sign convention. Peak [phasors](phasor.md) in sinusoidal steady state give $\langle P\rangle=\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde V\widetilde I^*)$. The factor $1/2$ is absent for [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) [phasors](phasor.md).
