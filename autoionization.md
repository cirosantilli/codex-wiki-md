# Autoionization

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

An excited atom or ion above an electron-emission threshold can decay without a photon by ejecting an electron and leaving a more highly charged ion. This is autoionization. Its rate competes with [radiative stabilization](radiative-stabilization.md) in [dielectronic recombination](dielectronic-recombination.md); the inverse electron-capture channel is [dielectronic capture](dielectronic-capture.md).
