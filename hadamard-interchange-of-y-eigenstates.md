# Hadamard interchange of Y eigenstates

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md)

For $|\pm i\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm i|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, direct [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) by the [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) gives coefficients $(1\pm i,1\mp i)/2$, proving the displayed identities. This exchanges the two [Pauli eigenstates](pauli-eigenstate.md) of the [Pauli Y gate](pauli-y-gate.md) and agrees with $HYH=-Y$. The phases in the formula are [global phases](global-phase.md) for each separate state; the [relative phase](relative-quantum-phase.md) inside each state is essential.
