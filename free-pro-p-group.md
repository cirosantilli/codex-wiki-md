# Free pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

For finite $X$, this is the [pro-p completion](pro-p-completion.md) of the [free group](free-group.md) on $X$. Every function from $X$ to a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) extends uniquely to a continuous [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md). More generally one can require a basis tending to the identity and impose the universal property for maps with that convergence condition; alternatively the pro-$p$ completion of the abstract free group represents unrestricted maps from an arbitrary set. These infinite-rank conventions should not be conflated when interpreting generator cardinalities.
