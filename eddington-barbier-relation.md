# Eddington-Barbier relation

↑ **Parent:** [Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md)

When the source function varies approximately linearly with optical depth, the emergent intensity obeys $I_\nu(0,\mu)\simeq S_\nu(\tau_\nu=\mu)$.

The relation is an approximation within [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), not the entire transfer theory.
