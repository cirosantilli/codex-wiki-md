# 2024

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the advanced quantum field theory for particle physics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source PDF: [https://mmathphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/sites/g/files/vzsiac2991/files/mmathphys/documents/media/aqft_for_particle_physics_2024.pdf](https://mmathphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/sites/g/files/vzsiac2991/files/mmathphys/documents/media/aqft_for_particle_physics_2024.pdf)

Solutions to all seventeen lettered parts. Use [natural units](../natural-units.md), the [Minkowski metric](../minkowski-metric.md) $g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, [Feynman slash notation](../feynman-slash-notation.md) $\not p=\gamma^\mu p_\mu$, and the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) in internal [propagators](../propagator.md). Define the [scattering amplitude](../scattering-amplitude.md) by $S_{fi}=\delta_{fi}+i(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p_f-p_i)\mathcal M_{fi}$. The [gauge covariant derivative](../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) convention in question 3 is specified there to match the printed three-boson [Feynman rule](../feynman-rule.md).
