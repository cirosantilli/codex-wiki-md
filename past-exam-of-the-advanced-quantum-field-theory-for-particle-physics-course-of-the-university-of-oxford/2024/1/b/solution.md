<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the local variation $\delta\psi=i\alpha(x)\psi$, $\delta\bar\psi=-i\alpha(x)\bar\psi$, the terms proportional to $\alpha$ cancel and the differentiated phase gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta\mathcal L
&=i(-i\alpha\bar\psi)\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi
+i\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu(i\alpha\psi)
-m\,\delta(\bar\psi\psi)\\
&=-\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi\,\partial_\mu\alpha.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, for a compactly supported $\alpha$, [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\boxed{j^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi,\qquad
\delta S=-\int d^4x\,j^\mu\partial_\mu\alpha
=\int d^4x\,\alpha\partial_\mu j^\mu.}
$$

This [Dirac current](../../../../dirac-current.md) is the [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) in the convention $\delta S=-\int j^\mu\partial_\mu\alpha$. Using the convention with a plus sign reverses the [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) and the [conserved charge](../../../../conserved-charge.md) together. There is one independent [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md), with four spacetime components of its [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md). The associated [conserved charge](../../../../conserved-charge.md) is $Q=\int d^3x\,j^0$, with vacuum subtraction when required.
