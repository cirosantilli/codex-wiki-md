<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [Dirac field](../../../../dirac-field.md), the [path integral](../../../../path-integral.md) is a [Berezin integral](../../../../berezin-integral.md) over independent [Grassmann fields](../../../../grassmann-field.md) $\psi$ and $\bar\psi$:

$$
\boxed{Z=\mathcal N\int\mathcal D\bar\psi\,\mathcal D\psi\,
\exp\left[i\int d^4x\,\bar\psi(i\not\partial-m)\psi\right].}
$$

The vacuum [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) and the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../../../../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) are understood; $\mathcal N$ fixes the vacuum normalization. This is the vacuum [generating functional](../../../../generating-functional.md) without sources, rather than a finite-temperature [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md).

The [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) transforms as $\bar\psi\mapsto\bar\psi e^{-i\alpha}$. For constant $\alpha$, $\partial_\mu(e^{i\alpha}\psi)=e^{i\alpha}\partial_\mu\psi$. Both the kinetic term and the [Dirac mass term](../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) therefore retain their values. This proves the [global phase symmetry](../../../../global-phase-symmetry.md), whose [group](../../../../group-split.md) is the [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md) $U(1)$.
