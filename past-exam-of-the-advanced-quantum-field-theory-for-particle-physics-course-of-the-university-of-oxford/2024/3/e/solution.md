<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The non-Abelian counterpart of the photon [Ward identity](../../../../ward-identity.md) is a [Slavnov-Taylor identity](../../../../slavnov-taylor-identity.md), derived from [BRST symmetry](../../../../brst-symmetry.md). A [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) with all other external states physical vanishes when one external massless [gauge boson](../../../../gauge-boson.md) [polarization vector](../../../../polarization-vector.md) is replaced by its [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md). An amputated tensor with the remaining indices open need not be transverse: part [d](../d.md) gives its explicit nonzero longitudinal remainder.

Consequently, amplitudes involving several unphysical covariant [polarization vectors](../../../../polarization-vector.md) need not vanish. For example, contracting the result of part [d](../d.md) with a timelike vector $n_\mu$ gives $ig^2\epsilon^{abc}(J_c\cdot k_2)(n\cdot k_1)/s$, nonzero in the explicit configuration there for $n=(1,0,0,0)$. Even with other states physical, the pure-gauge vector $k$ is the relevant null combination; separate timelike and spatial longitudinal basis vectors are not themselves pure gauge, and their separate amplitudes need not be zero.

These auxiliary components do **not** define separately observable positive probabilities. Physical states are classes in [BRST cohomology](../../../../brst-cohomology.md), and the [S-matrix](../../../../s-matrix.md) preserves that physical state space in this anomaly-free [vectorlike gauge spectrum](../../../../vectorlike-gauge-spectrum.md). There is no physical transition from a physical state to a nonphysical state. The incorrect stronger statement would be that every covariant component called “unphysical” has a zero matrix element.

For [loop Feynman diagrams](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) or [unitarity cuts](../../../../unitarity-cut.md), summing covariant gauge indices with $-g_{\mu\nu}$ propagates those auxiliary components. In a [Non-Abelian gauge theory](../../../../yang-mills-theory.md), their cancellation requires the interacting [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) as well as the [gauge boson](../../../../gauge-boson.md) diagrams. Ghost contributions carry their [Grassmann field](../../../../grassmann-field.md) loop signs and restore the [Slavnov-Taylor identities](../../../../slavnov-taylor-identity.md) and physical [unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md). One may instead use a formulation with only physical propagating [polarization vectors](../../../../polarization-vector.md), but one cannot simply discard the ghost diagrams while retaining the [Feynman gauge](../../../../feynman-gauge.md) rules from part [a](../a.md).
