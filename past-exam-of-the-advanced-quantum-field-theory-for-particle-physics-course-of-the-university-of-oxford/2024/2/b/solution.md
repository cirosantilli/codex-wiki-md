<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $S(p)=i(\not p+m)/(p^2-m^2+i0)$ for the [Dirac propagator](../../../../dirac-propagator.md), and $\int_\ell=\mu_R^{2\epsilon}\int d^{4-2\epsilon}\ell/(2\pi)^{4-2\epsilon}$ for a translation-invariant [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) of the [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) integral. The two oriented [scattering amplitudes](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
i\mathcal M_A^{\mu\nu\rho}
&=-\int_\ell\operatorname{tr}\left[
(-ie\gamma^\mu)S(\ell+k_1)
(-ie\gamma^\nu)S(\ell-k_3)
(-ie\gamma^\rho)S(\ell)\right],\\
i\mathcal M_B^{\mu\nu\rho}
&=-\int_\ell\operatorname{tr}\left[
(-ie\gamma^\mu)S(\ell+k_1)
(-ie\gamma^\rho)S(\ell-k_2)
(-ie\gamma^\nu)S(\ell)\right].
\end{aligned}}
$$

The common minus sign is the [closed fermion loop sign](../../../../closed-fermion-loop-sign.md). There is no additional [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) for either labelled orientation. Each expression is a [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) over spinor indices; all external photon [propagators](../../../../propagator.md) are amputated. If $K(p)=(\not p+m)/(p^2-m^2+i0)$, the common factor is $-(-ie)^3i^3=-e^3$, so, for example, $i\mathcal M_A=-e^3\int_\ell\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu K(\ell+k_1)\gamma^\nu K(\ell-k_3)\gamma^\rho K(\ell)]$.
