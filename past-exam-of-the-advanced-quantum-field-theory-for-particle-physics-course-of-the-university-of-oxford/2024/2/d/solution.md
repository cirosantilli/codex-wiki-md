<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For an $n$-vertex [fermion loop](../../../../fermion-loop.md), reversing the [fermion flow](../../../../fermion-flow.md) reverses the ordered product inside its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md). The same [charge-conjugation matrix](../../../../charge-conjugation-matrix.md) calculation transforms every [Dirac propagator](../../../../dirac-propagator.md) by $p\mapsto-p$ and contributes $-1$ for each vector photon [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md). Relabel the [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) so that the external labels are fixed. The reversed [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) therefore obeys

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\rm reversed}=(-1)^n\mathcal M_{\rm original}.}
$$

For odd $n\geq3$, the $(n-1)!$ cyclic orders pair into opposite orientations and cancel. For $n=1$, the single photon tadpole maps into itself with a minus sign and is zero. The common [closed fermion loop sign](../../../../closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) is present on both sides and does not supply the factor $(-1)^n$. This proves [Furry's theorem](../../../../furry-s-theorem.md) for all the requested [one-loop Feynman diagrams](../../../../one-loop-feynman-diagram.md). The [charge conjugation](../../../../charge-conjugation.md) argument also gives vanishing odd-photon [correlation functions](../../../../correlation-function.md) to all orders in vacuum [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md), provided no [charge conjugation symmetry](../../../../charge-conjugation-symmetry.md) breaking background or charged external state is introduced.
