<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

At large [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md), each [Dirac propagator](../../../../dirac-propagator.md) scales as $\ell^{-1}$. One four-dimensional loop and four internal [Dirac propagators](../../../../dirac-propagator.md) therefore give the [superficial degree of divergence](../../../../superficial-degree-of-divergence.md)

$$
D=4-4=0.
$$

Thus a possible [ultraviolet divergence](../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md) in the sum is logarithmic. By locality its divergent coefficient is a degree-zero polynomial in the external [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md), namely a constant Lorentz tensor,

$$
\mathcal M_{\rm div}^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
=\frac1\epsilon\left(a\,g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}
+b\,g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+c\,g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}\right).
$$

The photon [Ward identities](../../../../ward-identity.md) apply to the complete sum for arbitrary external [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md), including [off shell](../../../../off-shell.md) ones: $k_{1\mu}\mathcal M^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0$, and likewise for the other legs. A constant tensor with $k_{1\mu}\mathcal M_{\rm div}^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0$ for every $k_1$ must be zero. Hence $a=b=c=0$ and

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\rm one\ loop}^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\ \text{is ultraviolet finite}.}
$$

Equivalently, a logarithmic divergence would require a local dimension-four $A^4$ [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md), forbidden by Abelian [gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md). A gauge-invariant four-photon [local operator](../../../../lawvere-tierney-topology.md) starts with four [gauge field strengths](../../../../gauge-field-strength.md), for example $(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})^2$, of [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) eight; it cannot be this [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) divergence. Individual boxes need not be finite; their forbidden divergent parts cancel in the sum. There are no divergent proper loop subgraphs at this [loop order](../../../../loop-order.md). With the massive [Dirac field](../../../../dirac-field.md) in the source, there is also no soft or collinear [infrared divergence](../../../../infrared-divergence.md) at generic external kinematics. The argument establishes ultraviolet finiteness, and does not claim the absence of physical threshold singularities.
