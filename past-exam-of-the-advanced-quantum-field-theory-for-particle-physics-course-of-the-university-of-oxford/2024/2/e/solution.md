<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The third panel of the figure in part [a](../a.md) is a representative four-photon [box diagram](../../../../box-diagram.md). Fix the external photon labelled 1 and orient the [fermion loop](../../../../fermion-loop.md). The six inequivalent cyclic orders of the labelled [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) are

$$
\boxed{(1234),\ (1243),\ (1324),\ (1342),\ (1423),\ (1432).}
$$

They are obtained by permuting labels $2,3,4$ on the displayed box, keeping label 1 fixed. Equivalently there are three undirected boxes, each with two opposite [fermion flows](../../../../fermion-flow.md). The reversal pairs are

$$
(1234)\leftrightarrow(1432),\qquad
(1243)\leftrightarrow(1342),\qquad
(1324)\leftrightarrow(1423).
$$

For four photon [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), the sign in part [d](../d.md) is positive, so the members of each pair add. They can be represented by three [box diagrams](../../../../box-diagram.md) multiplied by two. There is no tree photon self-coupling in [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md), and therefore no triangle or bubble with a photon self-vertex to add at this [loop order](../../../../loop-order.md). This is the one-loop [light-by-light scattering](../../../../light-by-light-scattering.md) contribution, of order $e^4$.
