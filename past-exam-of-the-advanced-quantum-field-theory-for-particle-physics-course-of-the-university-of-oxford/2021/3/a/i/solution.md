<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $r^2=\Phi^T\Phi$. The potential has its minimum at

$$
\frac{dV}{dr}=-m^2r+4\lambda r^3=0,\qquad \boxed{v^2=\frac{m^2}{4\lambda}}.
$$

Choose the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\Phi_0=(0,0,v)^T$. Since the [weak hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) is zero, $Q=T^3$ on this [real scalar triplet](../../../../../real-scalar-triplet.md), and $T^3\Phi_0=0$. Neither $T^1$ nor $T^2$ annihilates $\Phi_0$. The [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{SU(2)\times U(1)_Y\longrightarrow U(1)_{T^3}\times U(1)_Y.}
$$

In particular, [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) has not reduced the two neutral [gauge generators](../../../../../gauge-generator.md) to a single one: $Y$ annihilates every component and remains unbroken independently of $T^3$.

The [scalar mass matrix](../../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md) is

$$
\left.\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial\phi_i\partial\phi_j}\right|_{\Phi_0}
=(-m^2+4\lambda v^2)\delta_{ij}+8\lambda(\Phi_0)_i(\Phi_0)_j
=8\lambda v^2\delta_{i3}\delta_{j3}.
$$

With canonical kinetic normalization $\tfrac12(\partial h)^2$, the radial [Higgs boson](../../../../../higgs-boson.md) therefore has

$$
\boxed{m_h^2=8\lambda v^2=2m^2,\qquad m_h=\sqrt2\,m.}
$$

The two tangential zero [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are [Goldstone modes](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md), absorbed by the two massive [gauge bosons](../../../../../gauge-boson.md) through the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md).
