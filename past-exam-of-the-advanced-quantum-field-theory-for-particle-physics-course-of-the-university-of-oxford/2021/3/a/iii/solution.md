<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This cannot be the sole symmetry-breaking sector of the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md). Its [gauge-boson mass matrix](../../../../../gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) has two zero [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), so besides the electromagnetic [photon](../../../../../photon.md) it predicts an extra massless neutral [gauge boson](../../../../../gauge-boson.md). Every linear combination of $W^3$ and $B$, including the combination normally called $Z$, is massless. The observed massive [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) is a concrete contradiction. Choosing $g,g',m,\lambda$ differently cannot change these two zero [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).

A [real scalar triplet](../../../../../real-scalar-triplet.md) can occur as an additional sector, but a further [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md), for example that of a [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md), is then necessary to obtain the observed neutral [gauge boson](../../../../../gauge-boson.md) mass pattern. This does not rescue the triplet-only model posed here.
