<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

There are two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md), with intermediate [photon](../../../../../photon.md) and [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) respectively. Their [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) tensors are $\delta_{ji}\delta_{kl}$ and $\sum_AT^A_{ji}T^A_{kl}$. The pure [photon](../../../../../photon.md) factor is

$$
C_\gamma=\frac1{N_c^2}\sum_{i,j,k,l}|\delta_{ji}\delta_{kl}|^2=1.
$$

Their interference vanishes after the [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) sum because of [photon-gluon colour orthogonality](../../../../../photon-gluon-colour-orthogonality.md):

$$
C_{\gamma g}=\frac1{N_c^2}\sum_A\operatorname{tr}(T^A)\operatorname{tr}(T^A)=0.
$$

Thus there is no term proportional to $e^2g_s^2$, and with the $F_u$ of part (iii),

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M_\gamma+\mathcal M_g|^2}
=\frac{2F_u}{s^2}\left[e^4Q_u^2Q_t^2+\frac29g_s^4\right]
=\frac{F_u}{s^2}\left[\frac{32}{81}e^4+\frac49g_s^4\right].}
$$

Here $Q_u=Q_t=2/3$. The absence of interference concerns the specified averaged and summed result; a fixed choice of external [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) need not have zero interference.
