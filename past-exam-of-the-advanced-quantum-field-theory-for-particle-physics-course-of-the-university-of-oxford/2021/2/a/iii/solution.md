<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exchanged particle is now a [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md), and the incoming [up quark](../../../../../up-quark.md) mass replaces $m_e$. Define

$$
\Sigma_u=m_u^2+m_t^2,\qquad F_u=t^2+u^2+4s\Sigma_u-2\Sigma_u^2.
$$

The [Dirac trace](../../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md) and [spin average](../../../../../spin-average.md) are unchanged. Normalize the fundamental [gauge generators](../../../../../gauge-generator.md) by $\operatorname{tr}(T^AT^B)=\delta^{AB}/2$. Averaging over the $N_c^2$ initial [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) choices and summing the final [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) choices gives

$$
C_g=\frac1{N_c^2}\sum_{A,B}\operatorname{tr}(T^AT^B)\operatorname{tr}(T^AT^B)
=\frac{N_c^2-1}{4N_c^2}=\frac29.
$$

Replace the factor $N_ce^4Q_e^2Q_t^2$ from part (ii) by $g_s^4C_g$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M_g|^2}=\frac{4g_s^4}{9s^2}F_u.}
$$

Keeping $m_u$ is allowed; if the usual negligible [up quark](../../../../../up-quark.md) mass approximation is wanted, simply set $m_u=0$. The factor $1/N_c^2$ is essential: initial [quark](../../../../../quark.md) [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) states are averaged, whereas final ones are summed.
