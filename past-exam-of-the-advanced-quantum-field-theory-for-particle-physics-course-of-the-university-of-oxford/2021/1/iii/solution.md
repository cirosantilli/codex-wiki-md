The assertion about this individual [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) therefore cannot be checked against its actual integrand. In particular, one must not identify the missing panel with a convenient vanishing [seagull vertex](../../../../seagull-vertex.md) graph by inference.

A useful criterion, once the drawing is recovered, is that [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) annihilates a vector integral of the form

$$
\int\frac{d^dl}{(2\pi)^d}\,l^\mu f(l^2)=0
$$

by the change of variables $l\mapsto-l$. This would prove vanishing for every $\xi$ if all remaining factors were functions of $l^2$; a denominator involving $l+k$ must first be checked rather than discarded. Whether this criterion applies to panel (c) is precisely the unavailable information.
