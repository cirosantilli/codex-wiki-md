<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [scalar QED Ward identity](../../../../scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md) equates the [vertex renormalization](../../../../vertex-renormalization.md) and [wave-function renormalization](../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md) constants. Consequently, using part (iv),

$$
\boxed{Z_2=Z_1=1+\frac{e^2(3-\xi)}{16\pi^2\epsilon}+O(e^4).}
$$

The [renormalization constants](../../../../renormalization-constant.md) describe gauge-dependent fields and [off shell](../../../../off-shell.md) vertices, so their dependence on $\xi$ is permitted. A physical [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) includes all [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md), the [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md), and the external-state factors from [LSZ reduction](../../../../lsz-reduction-formula.md). The [Ward identities](../../../../ward-identity.md) ensure that the dependence on $\xi$ cancels for physical external states. Infrared-sensitive probabilities also require the usual inclusive treatment of unobserved soft [photons](../../../../photon.md). Thus no dependence on $\xi$ is implied for consistently calculated physical observables.
