<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [superficial degree of divergence](../../../../superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) of this [scalar electrodynamics three-point vertex](../../../../scalar-electrodynamics-three-point-vertex.md) is one. Its [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md) is consequently a local expression at most linear in the external [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md). The allowed [counterterm Lagrangian](../../../../counterterm-lagrangian.md), with the charge-conjugation and Hermiticity properties of [scalar electrodynamics](../../../../scalar-electrodynamics.md), contains a single one-derivative [scalar electrodynamics three-point vertex](../../../../scalar-electrodynamics-three-point-vertex.md):

$$
V_{\mathrm{ct}}^\mu(k,k')=-ie\delta_1(k+k')^\mu.
$$

Thus the coefficient $\delta_1$ is independent of the chosen external [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md). Setting $k'=0$ leaves $-ie\delta_1k^\mu$, so a nonzero generic $k$ still determines that coefficient. It does not determine the full finite [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) at other [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md).

Keep $m\ne0$ and generic [off shell](../../../../off-shell.md) $k$ while extracting the [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md). These choices avoid spurious [infrared divergences](../../../../infrared-divergence.md); setting all scales to zero would instead produce [scaleless integrals](../../../../scaleless-integral.md) that can conceal the [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md).
