<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At second order in the [interaction term](../../../../../interaction-term.md), attach the two external [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) fields to different vertices and contract the remaining two [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) fields with each other and the two vector fields with each other. The factor $1/2!$ in the expansion is cancelled by the two ways to assign the external ends. There is no closed [fermion loop](../../../../../fermion-loop.md), and hence no extra closed-loop [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md).

After removing the two external [Dirac propagators](../../../../../dirac-propagator.md), the internal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) assignment $l$ for the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) and $p+l$ for the [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) gives the one-loop insertion

$$
\boxed{\mathcal I(p)=(-ie\mu^{\epsilon/2})^2\int\frac{d^dl}{(2\pi)^d}
\gamma^\nu\widetilde S(p+l)\gamma^\mu\widetilde\Delta_{\mu\nu}(l)
=-e^2\mu^\epsilon\int\frac{d^dl}{(2\pi)^d}
\gamma^\nu\widetilde S(p+l)\gamma^\mu\widetilde\Delta_{\mu\nu}(l).}
$$

Thus the insertion is proportional to exactly the propagator product specified in the source. Define $\mathcal I=-i\Sigma$. With Fourier convention $\psi(x)\sim e^{ip\cdot x}$, the reduced free inverse [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $K_0(p)=-\not p-m$, since the printed [Dirac propagator](../../../../../dirac-propagator.md) equals $iK_0^{-1}$. Repeated insertions give

$$
S=i(K_0-\Sigma)^{-1},\qquad
\Gamma^{(2)}=K_0-\Sigma+K_{\rm ct}.
$$

The factors of $i$ and the sign of the named [self-energy](../../../../../self-energy.md) are convention dependent; the cancelling [counterterm](../../../../../counterterm.md) is fixed once this insertion convention is specified. The internal [photon](../../../../../photon.md) joins two vertices on one directed [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) line, as in the first panel of the figure in 1b.
