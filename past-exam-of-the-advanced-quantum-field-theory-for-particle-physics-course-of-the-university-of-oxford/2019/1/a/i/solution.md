<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are four topologies for the one-loop [gluon self-energy](../../../../../gluon-self-energy.md), shown in the upper row of the figure:

- A closed [quark](../../../../../quark.md) loop with the two external [gluons](../../../../../gluon.md) attached through quark-gluon [Feynman vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), summed over active [quark flavors](../../../../../quark-flavor.md).
- A loop of two internal [gluons](../../../../../gluon.md) joining two three-gluon [Feynman vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), each carrying one external [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md).
- A closed [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) loop with two ghost-gluon [Feynman vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md).
- A single four-gluon [Feynman vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) with two external [gluons](../../../../../gluon.md) and its remaining two legs contracted into a [tadpole diagram](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md).

The two internal-[gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) graphs have [symmetry factor](../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $1/2$. The directed [quark](../../../../../quark.md) and [ghost loops](../../../../../ghost-loop.md) have [symmetry factor](../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one and a minus sign for a closed anticommuting loop. The four-gluon [tadpole diagram](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md) must be included in the list even though its massless [Feynman integral](../../../../../feynman-integral.md) vanishes in [dimensional regularization](../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md). Local [counterterms](../../../../../counterterm.md) are excluded here.
