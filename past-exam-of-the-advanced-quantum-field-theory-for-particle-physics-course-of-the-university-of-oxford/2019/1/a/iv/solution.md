<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Slavnov-Taylor identity](../../../../../slavnov-taylor-identity.md) implies transversality of the full perturbative [gluon self-energy](../../../../../gluon-self-energy.md):

$$
k_\mu\left(\Pi_{3g}^{\mu\nu,ab}+\Pi_{4g}^{\mu\nu,ab}+\Pi_{\mathrm{gh}}^{\mu\nu,ab}+\Pi_q^{\mu\nu,ab}\right)=0.
$$

The [quark](../../../../../quark.md) loop is separately transverse. This follows without evaluating its [Feynman integral](../../../../../feynman-integral.md): contract its vector [Feynman vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) with $k_\mu$ and use $\not k=(\not l+\not k-m)-(\not l-m)$ between its two [Dirac propagators](../../../../../dirac-propagator.md). One obtains a difference of two loop integrals that cancel after a shift of $l$ in [dimensional regularization](../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md). The [colour factor](../../../../../color-factor.md) is proportional to $\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)$ and does not obstruct this argument.

The four-gluon [tadpole diagram](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md) vanishes after removal of $m_g$. Thus the nontransverse parts of the two-three-gluon-vertex loop and the [ghost loop](../../../../../ghost-loop.md) must cancel. Contracting the preceding [ghost-loop pole of the gluon self-energy](../../../../../ghost-loop-pole-of-the-gluon-self-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{k_\mu\Pi_{3g}^{\mu\nu,ab}\big|_{\mathrm{UV}}=-k_\mu\Pi_{\mathrm{gh}}^{\mu\nu,ab}\big|_{\mathrm{UV}}=-\frac{g^2C_A\delta^{ab}}{32\pi^2\epsilon}k^2k^\nu.}
$$

For [QCD](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md), set $C_A=3$. No evaluation of the remaining three-gluon [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) is required. Its uncontracted transverse part is not fixed by this identity.
