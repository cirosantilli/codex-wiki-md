<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Label the [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) as $e^-(p_1)\mu^-(p_2)\to e^-(p_3)\mu^-(p_4)$. There is one [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md), the $t$-channel [photon](../../../../../photon.md) exchange shown in the lower-left panel. The two lepton species are distinct, so there is no exchanged-final-lepton diagram. With $s=(p_1+p_2)^2$, $t=(p_1-p_3)^2$ and $u=(p_1-p_4)^2$, the [Feynman rules](../../../../../feynman-rules.md) give

$$
i\mathcal M=\frac{ie^2}{t}[\bar u(p_3)\gamma^\mu u(p_1)][\bar u(p_4)\gamma_\mu u(p_2)].
$$

The massless [fermion spin sum](../../../../../fermion-spin-sum.md) is $\sum_ru_r(p)\bar u_r(p)=\not p$. Averaging the two initial [spins](../../../../../spin.md) of each lepton supplies $1/4$, so

$$
\langle|\mathcal M|^2\rangle=\frac{e^4}{4t^2}\operatorname{tr}(\not p_3\gamma^\mu\not p_1\gamma^\nu)\operatorname{tr}(\not p_4\gamma_\mu\not p_2\gamma_\nu).
$$

The [gamma matrix trace identities](../../../../../gamma-matrix-trace-identities.md) imply, for arbitrary null external [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md),

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\not a\gamma^\mu\not b\gamma^\nu)\operatorname{tr}(\not c\gamma_\mu\not d\gamma_\nu)=32[(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)].
$$

To see the contraction, each trace is $4(a^\mu b^\nu+a^\nu b^\mu-g^{\mu\nu}a\cdot b)$; the terms proportional to $(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)$ cancel in four dimensions, leaving the displayed two products. Here $p_1\cdot p_2=p_3\cdot p_4=s/2$ and $p_1\cdot p_4=p_2\cdot p_3=-u/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle|\mathcal M|^2\rangle=2e^4\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}.}
$$

The requested proportionality constant is $2e^4=32\pi^2\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}^2$, where $\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}$ is the [fine-structure constant](../../../../../fine-structure-constant.md). Both numerator terms are required by the [spin sum](../../../../../spin-sum.md); no final-state statistical factor enters a squared [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) for distinct leptons.
