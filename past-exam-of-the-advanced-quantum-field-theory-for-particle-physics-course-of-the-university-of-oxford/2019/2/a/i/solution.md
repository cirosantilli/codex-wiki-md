<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a real local parameter $\alpha(x)$, the [Dirac adjoint](../../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) varies as $\delta\bar\psi=ie\alpha\bar\psi$. The matter kinetic term varies by

$$
\delta(\bar\psi i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi)=e(\partial_\mu\alpha)\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi.
$$

The [QED](../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) interaction varies by $-e(\partial_\mu\alpha)\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$, since the phases in $\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$ cancel. These two variations cancel pointwise. The [mass](../../../../../mass.md) term is invariant for the same reason, and $\delta F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\alpha-\partial_\nu\partial_\mu\alpha=0$.

Only the [gauge fixing](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) term varies. Put $B=\partial_\mu A^\mu$; then $\delta B=\partial^2\alpha$, giving

$$
\boxed{\delta\mathcal L_{\mathrm{QED}}=-\frac1\xi(\partial_\mu A^\mu)\partial^2\alpha.}
$$

For arbitrary local $\alpha$, the [gauge-fixed action](../../../../../gauge-fixed-action.md) is therefore not invariant under this transformation of its dynamical fields. The restricted parameters satisfying $\partial^2\alpha=0$ give a residual invariance of the displayed density.
