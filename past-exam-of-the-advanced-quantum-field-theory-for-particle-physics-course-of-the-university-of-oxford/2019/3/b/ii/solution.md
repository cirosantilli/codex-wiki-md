<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $X=\Phi^\dagger\Phi$. Under the global transformation of the [chiral matrix scalar model](../../../../../chiral-matrix-scalar-model.md),

$$
\Phi'=e^{i\theta}U_L\Phi U_R^\dagger,\qquad X'=U_RXU_R^\dagger.
$$

The phase and $U_L^\dagger U_L$ cancel, and $U_R^\dagger U_R=\mathbf1$. The [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) implies

$$
\operatorname{tr}X'=\operatorname{tr}X,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(X'^2)=\operatorname{tr}(U_RX^2U_R^\dagger)=\operatorname{tr}X^2.
$$

Therefore each of the three terms in the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is invariant separately. Since $\theta,U_L,U_R$ are constant, $\partial_\mu\Phi'=e^{i\theta}U_L(\partial_\mu\Phi)U_R^\dagger$, giving invariance of the kinetic [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) by the same argument. Hence

$$
\boxed{G=SU(N)_L\times SU(N)_R\times U(1)}
$$

is a global symmetry group of the [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md). Its action has a finite central kernel, which does not affect its [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) or the broken-generator counting below. For special parameter choices the symmetry can be larger; the displayed group is a symmetry for the stated potential without such extra assumptions.
