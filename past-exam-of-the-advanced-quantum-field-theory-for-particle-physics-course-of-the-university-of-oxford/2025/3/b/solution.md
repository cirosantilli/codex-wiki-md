<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+ieA_\mu$. Write $\rho=f+h$, $r=1+h/f$ and $B_\mu=\partial_\mu\pi/f+eA_\mu$. The [polar parametrization of the Abelian Higgs field](../../../../polar-parametrization-of-the-abelian-higgs-field.md) gives

$$
D_\mu\phi=\frac{e^{i\pi/f}}{\sqrt2}\left[\partial_\mu h+i(f+h)B_\mu\right],
\qquad
\phi^\dagger\phi-\frac{f^2}{2}=fh+\frac{h^2}{2}.
$$

Hence the full [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
+\frac12(\partial h)^2+\frac12\left(1+\frac hf\right)^2(\partial_\mu\pi+efA_\mu)^2
-\frac\lambda2f^2h^2-\frac\lambda2fh^3-\frac\lambda8h^4.}
$$

Assume $f>0$ and $\lambda>0$, so the minimum has $|\phi|=f/\sqrt2$. In this convention the local [gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) acts as

$$
\boxed{\phi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\phi,\qquad
A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu-\frac1e\partial_\mu\alpha,\qquad
h\mapsto h,\qquad\pi\mapsto\pi+f\alpha.}
$$

The combination $\partial_\mu\pi+efA_\mu$, the radial field $h$, and the [gauge field strength](../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) are each invariant, proving invariance of the displayed [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md).

This parametrization follows the circle of classical minima: $h$ measures distance away from the [vacuum manifold](../../../../vacuum-manifold.md), whereas $\pi$ moves along a [gauge orbit](../../../../gauge-orbit.md). It separates the massive radial [Higgs mode](../../../../higgs-mode.md) from the would-be [Goldstone boson](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) and makes its removal by the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) transparent. At quadratic order,

$$
\mathcal L_2=-\frac14F^2+\frac12(\partial h)^2-\frac12\lambda f^2h^2
+\frac12(\partial\pi)^2+efA\cdot\partial\pi+\frac12e^2f^2A^2,
\qquad m_h^2=\lambda f^2,\quad m_A^2=e^2f^2.
$$

The [polar parametrization of the Abelian Higgs field](../../../../polar-parametrization-of-the-abelian-higgs-field.md) is a local description near the chosen nonzero [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md); it is singular at $\phi=0$ and is not a global coordinate system for configurations with zeros or winding.
