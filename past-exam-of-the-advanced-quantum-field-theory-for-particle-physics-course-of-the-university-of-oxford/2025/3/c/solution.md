<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Unitary gauge](../../../../unitary-gauge.md) fixes the scalar phase to zero. Locally choose $\alpha=-\pi/f$ in part [b](../b.md); then $\pi'=0$ and $A'_\mu=A_\mu+\partial_\mu\pi/(ef)$. The remaining fields are the radial [Higgs mode](../../../../higgs-mode.md) and a massive vector. Its quadratic [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is $-F^2/4+m_A^2A^2/2$ and its [gauge-boson propagator](../../../../gauge-boson-propagator.md) is

$$
\boxed{D^U_{\mu\nu}(k)=\frac{-i}{k^2-m_A^2+i0}
\left(g_{\mu\nu}-\frac{k_\mu k_\nu}{m_A^2}\right).}
$$

For $k^2\ne0$, introduce the transverse and longitudinal [orthogonal projectors](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $P^T_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}-k_\mu k_\nu/k^2$ and $P^L_{\mu\nu}=k_\mu k_\nu/k^2$. Omitting the infinitesimal in this algebraic decomposition,

$$
D^U=-\frac{iP^T}{k^2-m_A^2}+\frac{iP^L}{m_A^2}.
$$

At large [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) in [Euclidean space](../../../../euclidean-norm.md), the transverse coefficient falls as $k^{-2}$ but the longitudinal coefficient approaches a constant of order $m_A^{-2}$. Thus [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) applied directly to [unitary gauge](../../../../unitary-gauge.md) [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) suggests increasingly severe [ultraviolet divergences](../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md). This hides the [renormalizability](../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) of physical [scattering amplitudes](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md); it does not disprove it. A finite-$\xi$ [R-xi gauge](../../../../r-xi-gauge.md) exposes the better [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) scaling while preserving the fields needed to track the cancellations.
