<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In $d=4-2\epsilon$, the [modified minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) subtracts $\Delta_{\overline{\rm MS}}=1/\epsilon-\gamma_E+\log(4\pi)$ for each one-loop pole, with no further finite, kinematics-dependent subtraction. Equivalently, use a rescaled [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md) $\overline\mu^2=4\pi e^{-\gamma_E}\mu_R^2$ and subtract only $1/\epsilon$ in that convention. This differs from the [minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) by a fixed scale convention.

Let $C=e^2\Delta_{\overline{\rm MS}}/(16\pi^2)$. With the source's [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) insertion signs, cancellation proceeds as follows. The vertex has $-ie\gamma^\mu C$ from the loop and $-ie\gamma^\mu\delta_1$ from its [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md), giving $\delta_1=-C$. The kinetic part of the [fermion self-energy](../../../../fermion-self-energy.md) insertion is $+i\not p C$ and its [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is $+i\not p\delta_2$, giving $\delta_2=-C$. Finally the [photon vacuum polarization](../../../../photon-vacuum-polarization.md) insertion is $-i(q^2g_{\mu\nu}-q_\mu q_\nu)(4C/3)$ and its [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is $-i(q^2g_{\mu\nu}-q_\mu q_\nu)\delta_3$, giving

$$
\boxed{\delta_1=\delta_2=-\frac{e^2}{16\pi^2}\Delta_{\overline{\rm MS}},\qquad
\delta_3=-\frac{e^2}{12\pi^2}\Delta_{\overline{\rm MS}}.}
$$

Writing $Z_i=1+\delta_i$ therefore verifies the [Ward identity for QED renormalization constants](../../../../ward-identity-for-qed-renormalization-constants.md), $Z_1=Z_2$, at one loop. The [proper-vertex Ward-Takahashi identity in QED](../../../../proper-vertex-ward-takahashi-identity-in-qed.md) independently gives $\Lambda^\mu(p,p)=-\partial\Sigma(p)/\partial p_\mu$, whose divergent part reproduces the opposite signs of $F_1^{(1)}$ and $S$ found above. The equality holds in a common [gauge fixing](../../../../gauge-fixing.md) and subtraction prescription; the individual constants $Z_1,Z_2$ are gauge dependent.
