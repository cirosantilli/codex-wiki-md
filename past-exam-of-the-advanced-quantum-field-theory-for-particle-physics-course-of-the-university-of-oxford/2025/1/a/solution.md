<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [scalar field path integral](../../../../scalar-field-path-integral.md) integrates over real field histories with vacuum [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md). With $S_0[\phi]=\int d^4x\,\mathcal L_0$, the [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{Z=\mathcal N\int\mathcal D\phi\,
\exp\left\{i\int d^4x\left[\frac12\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi-\frac12(m^2-i0)\phi^2-\frac\lambda{4!}\phi^4\right]\right\}.}
$$

The constant $\mathcal N$ fixes the desired vacuum normalization. A [generating functional](../../../../generating-functional.md) adds $\int d^4x\,J\phi$ to the [action](../../../../action.md); the required [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is its zero-source value. Equivalently, a [Wick rotation](../../../../wick-rotation.md) gives $Z_E=\mathcal N_E\int\mathcal D\phi\,e^{-S_E[\phi]}$, with the positive-sign kinetic and potential terms in the [Euclidean action](../../../../euclidean-action.md) when $m^2\geq0$ and $\lambda>0$.
