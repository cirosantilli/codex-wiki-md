<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [action](../../../../action.md) is dimensionless, so in four dimensions the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) four. Its kinetic term fixes $[\phi]=1$ and $[\partial_\mu]=1$. An interaction $g_D\mathcal O_D$ with $[\mathcal O_D]=D$ has $[g_D]=4-D$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{D\leq4\ \text{is renormalizable by power counting};\qquad D>4\ \text{has a negative-dimensional coupling}.}
$$

In an [effective field theory](../../../../effective-field-theory.md) with heavy scale $\Lambda$, a higher-dimensional [local operator](../../../../lawvere-tierney-topology.md) enters as $c_D\mathcal O_D/\Lambda^{D-4}$. For characteristic scales $E,m\ll\Lambda$ and coefficients compatible with this expansion, its effects are suppressed by powers of the small ratios. Omitting these terms defines the leading approximation and its [truncation error in effective field theory](../../../../truncation-error-in-effective-field-theory.md). It is not a fundamental prohibition: such terms must be retained if that accuracy is required. A continuum [renormalizable quantum field theory](../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) can instead be defined by the closed set of [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md) of dimension at most four.

Up to [total derivatives](../../../../total-derivative.md), all [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) terms of dimension at most four built from one [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) are

$$
1,\quad\phi,\quad\phi^2,\quad\phi^3,\quad\phi^4,\quad
\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi.
$$

The term $\phi\Box\phi$ is equivalent to the kinetic term by [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md); $\Box\phi$ and $\Box(\phi^2)$ contribute only at the boundary. The displayed [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) omits the linear and cubic interactions and a field-independent constant. The exact [Z2 symmetry](../../../../z2-symmetry.md) $\phi\mapsto-\phi$ forbids both odd interactions, and [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md) preserving this [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) cannot generate them. The constant shifts the vacuum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and the normalization of $Z$ and can be dropped when gravity and absolute vacuum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) are not being studied. A constant vacuum [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) may still be used when renormalizing unnormalized vacuum diagrams. The kinetic coefficient has already been fixed by field normalization.
