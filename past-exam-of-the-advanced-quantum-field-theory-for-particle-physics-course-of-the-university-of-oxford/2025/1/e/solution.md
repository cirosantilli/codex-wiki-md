<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Write $X\simeq Y$ when the two densities differ by a [total derivative](../../../../total-derivative.md). Under the [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) used in the [action](../../../../action.md), they have the same integral. The following identities establish the [integration-by-parts reduction of scalar operators](../../../../integration-by-parts-reduction-of-scalar-operators.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\partial_\mu(\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi)&=3\phi^2(\partial\phi)^2+\phi^3\Box\phi,
&\mathcal O_2&\simeq-\frac13\mathcal O_3,\\
\partial_\mu(\phi\partial^\mu\Box\phi)&=\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\Box\phi+\phi\Box^2\phi,
&\mathcal O_4&\simeq-\mathcal O_5,\\
\partial_\mu(\partial^\mu\phi\Box\phi)&=(\Box\phi)^2+\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\Box\phi,
&\mathcal O_5&\simeq-\mathcal O_6,\\
\partial_\mu(\partial_\nu\phi\partial^\mu\partial^\nu\phi)&=
(\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\phi)^2+\partial_\nu\phi\partial^\nu\Box\phi,
&\mathcal O_7&\simeq-\mathcal O_5.
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\{\phi^6,\ \phi^3\Box\phi,\ (\Box\phi)^2\}}
$$

spans the [operator basis in effective field theory](../../../../operator-basis-in-effective-field-theory.md) before [equation-of-motion reduction of scalar operators](../../../../equation-of-motion-reduction-of-scalar-operators.md). These four independent relations reduce the seven representatives to three; the three sectors have different field or derivative content, so [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) cannot identify them further.
