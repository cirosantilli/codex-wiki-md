<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Vary the [action](../../../../action.md), taking the field variation to vanish at the boundary. [Integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\delta S_0=\int d^4x\left[\partial^\mu\phi\,\partial_\mu\delta\phi-m^2\phi\delta\phi-\frac\lambda6\phi^3\delta\phi\right]
=-\int d^4x\left(\Box\phi+m^2\phi+\frac\lambda6\phi^3\right)\delta\phi.
$$

Thus the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Box\phi+m^2\phi+\frac\lambda6\phi^3=0.}
$$

For the [field redefinition](../../../../field-redefinition.md) in part [f](../f.md), define the [functional derivative](../../../../functional-derivative.md) $E(\phi)=\delta S_0/\delta\phi=-(\Box\phi+m^2\phi+\lambda\phi^3/6)$. The sign does not affect the [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md), but it fixes the sign of an added [redundant operator](../../../../redundant-operator.md).
