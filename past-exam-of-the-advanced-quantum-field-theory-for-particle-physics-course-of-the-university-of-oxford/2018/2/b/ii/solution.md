<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Contract the numerator of the quoted [light cone gauge](../../../../../light-cone-gauge.md) [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md) with $n_\mu$:

$$
n_\mu\left[g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{n^\mu p^\nu+n^\nu p^\mu}{n\cdot p}\right]
=n^\nu-\frac{n^2p^\nu+(n\cdot p)n^\nu}{n\cdot p}
=-\frac{n^2p^\nu}{n\cdot p}=0.
$$

Thus every internal [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) emerging from a [ghost-gluon vertex](../../../../../ghost-gluon-vertex.md) contributes a factor $n_\mu\widetilde\Delta^{\mu\nu}=0$. Every ghost-dependent contribution to a physical [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) or matter amplitude vanishes. Equivalently, the strict condition $n\cdot A=0$ removes the [Faddeev-Popov ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) interaction in [i](../i.md), leaving the field-independent determinant $\det(n\cdot\partial)$, which cancels from normalized correlators.

This is [ghost decoupling in strict axial gauge](../../../../../ghost-decoupling-in-strict-axial-gauge.md), not a statement that the free [Faddeev-Popov ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md) itself is zero. The algebra assumes a consistent [axial-gauge pole prescription](../../../../../axial-gauge-pole-prescription.md) for $n\cdot p=0$ and treatment of residual gauge zero modes. The overall sign assigned to the displayed vector [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md) does not enter the contraction proof. With the mostly-minus convention and the usual $-F^2/4$ action, its conventional prefactor is $-i/(p^2+i0)$; the source prints $+i$. Either prefactor gives the same zero contraction, so no sign correction is needed to obtain the requested ghost-decoupling result.
