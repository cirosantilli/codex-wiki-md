<h1 id="3/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Using $\mu^2=\lambda v^2$, expand the potential around the chosen [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md):

$$
V=V_{\min}+\lambda v^2h^2+\lambda vh(h^2+\pi^2)
+\frac\lambda4(h^2+\pi^2)^2.
$$

The linear term vanishes at the minimum. With canonical real-field kinetic terms, comparison with $m_h^2h^2/2+m_\pi^2\pi^2/2$ gives

$$
\boxed{m_h^2=2\lambda v^2=2\mu^2,\qquad m_\pi^2=0.}
$$

The massive radial scalar changes the magnitude of the [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) field; the massless [Goldstone boson](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) changes its orientation. The symmetry is global here, so no gauge field eats that mode.

The genuinely new scalar cubic interactions are $-\lambda v h^3-\lambda v h\pi^2$. Including identical-field factorials, their [Feynman vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) factors are

$$
\boxed{hhh:\ -6i\lambda v,\qquad h\pi\pi:\ -2i\lambda v.}
$$

The quartic interactions remain $-\lambda h^4/4-\lambda h^2\pi^2/2-\lambda\pi^4/4$, with respective vertex factors $-6i\lambda,-2i\lambda,-6i\lambda$. The shifted Yukawa terms from [iv](../iv.md) give $h\bar\psi\psi$ vertex $-ig$ and $\pi\bar\psi\psi$ vertex $g\gamma_5$. These Yukawa structures were present before the shift; the new [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) bilinear is the [mass term](../../../../../mass-term.md), not an additional independent interaction coupling. There are no derivative interactions from the linear Cartesian field shift.
