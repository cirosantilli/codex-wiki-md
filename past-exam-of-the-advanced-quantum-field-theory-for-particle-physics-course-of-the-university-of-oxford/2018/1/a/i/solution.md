<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\phi_0=\sqrt{Z_2}\phi$, $A_{0\mu}=\sqrt{Z_3}A_\mu$, and define the [renormalized coupling](../../../../../renormalized-coupling.md) by $e_0=e_DZ_1/(Z_2\sqrt{Z_3})$. Write $Z_2m_0^2=m^2+\delta m^2$ and $Z_2^2\lambda_0=\mu^\epsilon(\lambda+\delta\lambda)$. The complete [renormalized](../../../../../renormalization.md) [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md), with its [counterterms](../../../../../counterterm.md) already included, is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L={}&Z_2\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-(m^2+\delta m^2)\phi^*\phi
-\frac{\mu^\epsilon(\lambda+\delta\lambda)}4(\phi^*\phi)^2-\frac{Z_3}4F^2\\
&-ie_DZ_1A_\mu[\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-(\partial^\mu\phi^*)\phi]
+e_D^2Z_4A^2\phi^*\phi+\mathcal L_{\rm gf}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Separating off the classical renormalized terms leaves the [counterterm Lagrangian](../../../../../counterterm-lagrangian.md)

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\rm ct}={}&\delta_2\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\delta m^2\phi^*\phi
-\frac{\mu^\epsilon\delta\lambda}{4}(\phi^*\phi)^2-\frac{\delta_3}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\\
&-ie_D\delta_1 A_\mu\big[\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-(\partial^\mu\phi^*)\phi\big]
+e_D^2\delta_4 A_\mu A^\mu\phi^*\phi,
\qquad Z_i=1+\delta_i.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [seagull vertex](../../../../../seagull-vertex.md) factor is thus $2ie_D^2Z_4g^{\mu\nu}$. The bare [covariant derivative](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) fixes $Z_4=Z_1^2/Z_2$, rather than supplying an unrelated fourth charge parameter. The [scalar QED Ward identity](../../../../../scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md), for a symmetry-preserving [regularization](../../../../../regularization.md), gives $Z_1=Z_2$ and hence $Z_4=Z_2$.

For an invertible quadratic [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) operator, also include $\mathcal L_{\rm gf}=-(\partial\cdot A)^2/(2\xi)$. One may choose $\xi_0=Z_3\xi$, so this term needs no independent [counterterm](../../../../../counterterm.md). The strict transverse limit is $\xi\to0$. Even if the renormalized quartic coupling is set to zero at one scale, its [counterterm](../../../../../counterterm.md) need not vanish: [photon](../../../../../photon.md) graphs generate a four-scalar interaction. The [renormalized](../../../../../renormalization.md) [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) must retain this allowed operator.
