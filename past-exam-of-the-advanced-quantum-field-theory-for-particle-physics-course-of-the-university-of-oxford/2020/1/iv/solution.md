<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

With $q=p'-p$ and the routing just described, multiplication of the three [QED Feynman rules](../../../../qed-feynman-rules.md) for the [Feynman vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), the two [Dirac propagators](../../../../dirac-propagator.md) and the [photon propagator](../../../../photon-propagator.md) gives $(-ie_D)^3i^2(-i)=-e_D^3$. Hence the unrenormalized amputated [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) through this order is

$$
\boxed{iV^\mu(p,p')=-ie_D\gamma^\mu-e_D^3\int\frac{d^Dk}{(2\pi)^D}\frac{\gamma^\nu(\not p'-\not k+m)\gamma^\mu(\not p-\not k+m)\gamma_\nu}{(k^2+i0)[(p'-k)^2-m^2+i0][(p-k)^2-m^2+i0]}+O(e_D^5).}
$$

For the renormalized [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), add $-ie_D\delta_1\gamma^\mu$ to this expression. Contributions from replacing parameters inside the one-loop term by their bare counterparts enter at higher [loop order](../../../../loop-order.md).
