<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There is one [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram](../../../../one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md): an internal [photon](../../../../photon.md) joins two points of the open [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) line, with the external [photon](../../../../photon.md) attached to that line between them. It is the upper-left panel of the figure. Route internal [photon](../../../../photon.md) [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) $k$ from the incoming side to the outgoing side; the adjacent internal [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) are $p-k$ and $p'-k$. The [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) is one.

The [vertex renormalization](../../../../vertex-renormalization.md) [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is an additional local contribution when forming the renormalized [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md). External-leg [self-energy](../../../../self-energy.md) insertions belong to the complete three-point [correlation function](../../../../correlation-function.md), rather than this amputated [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram](../../../../one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md).
