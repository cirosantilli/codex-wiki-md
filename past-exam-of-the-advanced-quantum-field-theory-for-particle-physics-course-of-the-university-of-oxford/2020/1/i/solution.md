<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the bare fields obey the [wave-function renormalization](../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md) definitions $\psi_0=Z_2^{1/2}\psi$, $A_{0\mu}=Z_3^{1/2}A_\mu$, and put $m_0=Z_m m$. Define the [vertex renormalization](../../../../vertex-renormalization.md) factor by $e_0=\mu^{\epsilon/2}eZ_1Z_2^{-1}Z_3^{-1/2}$. Substitution into the bare [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=Z_2\bar\psi i\not\partial\psi-Z_2Z_m m\bar\psi\psi-\frac{Z_3}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}-e_DZ_1\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu.}
$$

Equivalently, write $Z_i=1+\delta_i$ and $\delta_m=Z_2Z_m-1$. The [counterterm Lagrangian](../../../../counterterm-lagrangian.md) is

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ct}}=\delta_2\bar\psi i\not\partial\psi-\delta_m m\bar\psi\psi-\frac{\delta_3}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}-e_D\delta_1\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu.
$$

For the subsequent [Feynman gauge](../../../../feynman-gauge.md) calculation include $-(\partial\cdot A)^2/2$ in the [gauge-fixed action](../../../../gauge-fixed-action.md). The [Ward identity for QED renormalization constants](../../../../ward-identity-for-qed-renormalization-constants.md) implies $Z_1=Z_2$ when the [regularization](../../../../regularization.md) preserves [gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md); this is a consequence, rather than an extra independent definition of the [renormalization constants](../../../../renormalization-constant.md).
