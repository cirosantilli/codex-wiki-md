<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Keep $m>0$ and set $p=p'=0$, which is an [off shell](../../../../off-shell.md) choice. The numerator is

$$
N^\mu=\gamma^\nu(-\not k+m)\gamma^\mu(-\not k+m)\gamma_\nu.
$$

Terms odd in $k$ integrate to zero. [Lorentz covariance](../../../../lorentz-covariance.md) and [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) give, inside this integral,

$$
k_\rho k_\sigma\longmapsto\frac{k^2}{D}g_{\rho\sigma},\qquad
\not k\gamma^\mu\not k\longmapsto\frac{2-D}{D}k^2\gamma^\mu.
$$

Applying the [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) identity from part (ii) once more yields

$$
N^\mu\longmapsto\left[\frac{(D-2)^2}{D}k^2+(2-D)m^2\right]\gamma^\mu.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{iV^\mu_{\mathrm{loop}}(0,0)=-e_D^3\gamma^\mu\int\frac{d^Dk}{(2\pi)^D}\frac{\alpha k^2+\beta m^2}{(k^2+i0)(k^2-m^2+i0)^2},\quad\alpha=\frac{(2-\epsilon)^2}{4-\epsilon},\quad\beta=\epsilon-2.}
$$

This [massive zero-momentum QED vertex integral](../../../../massive-zero-momentum-qed-vertex-integral.md) uses an integrated tensor identity for the replacement of $N^\mu$, not an equality for each fixed $k$. The physical [photon](../../../../photon.md) remains massless. The retained [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) supplies a scale and makes this zero-external-[four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) calculation suitable for extracting the [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md) without a [scaleless integral](../../../../scaleless-integral.md).
