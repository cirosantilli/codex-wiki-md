<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Clifford algebra](../../../../clifford-algebra.md) of the [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) gives $\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu=2g^{\nu\mu}-\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu$ and $\gamma^\nu\gamma_\nu=D\mathbf1$. Therefore

$$
\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu\gamma_\nu=2g^{\nu\mu}\gamma_\nu-\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma_\nu=2\gamma^\mu-D\gamma^\mu,
\qquad\boxed{\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu\gamma_\nu=(2-D)\gamma^\mu.}
$$

The contraction $g^\nu{}_{\nu}=D$ must be retained until the [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) calculation is complete; replacing it prematurely by four can change finite terms multiplying a [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md).
