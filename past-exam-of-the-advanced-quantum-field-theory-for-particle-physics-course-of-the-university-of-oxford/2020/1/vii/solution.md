<h1 id="1/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Only the term proportional to $\alpha$ has an [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md). Its $k^2$ cancels the massless denominator, leaving the standard [Feynman integral](../../../../feynman-integral.md)

$$
J_D=\int\frac{d^Dk}{(2\pi)^D}\frac1{(k^2-m^2+i0)^2}=\frac{i}{(4\pi)^{D/2}}\Gamma(2-D/2)(m^2)^{D/2-2}.
$$

In the present [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) convention, $\Gamma(\epsilon/2)=2/\epsilon+O(1)$ and $\alpha=1+O(\epsilon)$. Therefore

$$
iV^\mu_{\mathrm{loop}}\big|_{\mathrm{UV}}=-ie_D\gamma^\mu\frac{e^2}{8\pi^2\epsilon}.
$$

The [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) contribution is $-ie_D\delta_1\gamma^\mu$. Cancellation of the [ultraviolet pole](../../../../ultraviolet-pole.md) fixes

$$
\boxed{Z_1=1+\delta_1=1-\frac{e^2}{8\pi^2\epsilon}+O(e^4)\quad(D=4-\epsilon),}
$$

where the displayed expression uses the [minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../minimal-subtraction-scheme.md); a different [renormalization scheme](../../../../renormalization-scheme.md) may add finite $O(e^2)$ terms. In the frequently used convention $D=4-2\varepsilon$, the same result is $Z_1=1-e^2/(16\pi^2\varepsilon)+O(e^4)$. The sign agrees with $Z_1=Z_2$ from the [Ward identity for QED renormalization constants](../../../../ward-identity-for-qed-renormalization-constants.md) in [Feynman gauge](../../../../feynman-gauge.md).
