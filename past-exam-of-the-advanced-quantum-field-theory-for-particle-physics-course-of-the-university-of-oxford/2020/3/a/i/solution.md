<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Goldstone's theorem](../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) states that in a relativistic [quantum field theory](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md), spontaneous breaking of an exact continuous global internal [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) produces a massless [Goldstone boson](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) for each independent broken generator. More precisely, a conserved global current whose charge acts nontrivially on the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) has a massless pole in an appropriate current-field [correlation function](../../../../../correlation-function.md). The usual counting assumes a [Lorentz invariant](../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [vacuum state](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) and the standard locality and positivity hypotheses.

A local [gauge symmetry](../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) is a [gauge redundancy](../../../../../gauge-redundancy.md), so it does not by itself imply massless physical [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). In the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md), the corresponding scalar directions provide the longitudinal [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) of massive [gauge bosons](../../../../../gauge-boson.md).
