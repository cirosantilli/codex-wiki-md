<h1 id="3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Expand the [gauge fixing](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) square:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{gf}}=-\frac1{2\xi}(\partial\cdot A)^2-\frac\lambda\xi(\partial\cdot A)A_\mu A^\mu-\frac{\lambda^2}{2\xi}(A_\mu A^\mu)^2.}
$$

The second term is a derivative cubic [photon](../../../../../photon.md) interaction; the third is a quartic [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [contact interaction](../../../../../contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md). Both arise from [nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing](../../../../../nonlinear-abelian-gauge-fixing.md), rather than an additional physical electromagnetic force.

For $\xi=1$, differentiating the quartic term with respect to four identical [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md) gives eight copies for each pairing, hence

$$
V_4^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}=-4i\lambda^2(g^{\alpha\beta}g^{\gamma\delta}+g^{\alpha\gamma}g^{\beta\delta}+g^{\alpha\delta}g^{\beta\gamma}).
$$

With the Fourier convention $A(x)=\int d^4p\,(2\pi)^{-4}e^{ipx}A(p)$ and all [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) incoming, the differentiated cubic term gives

$$
V_3^{\alpha\beta\gamma}=2\lambda(g^{\beta\gamma}p_1^\alpha+g^{\alpha\gamma}p_2^\beta+g^{\alpha\beta}p_3^\gamma),
$$

matching the supplied [Feynman rules](../../../../../feynman-rules.md). Reversing the Fourier convention reverses this cubic rule's sign; each exchange graph contains two such [Feynman vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), so its contribution is unchanged.
