<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For fixed $A$, the delta functional sets $\sigma(x)=F[A](x)$. Therefore its weighted [functional integral](../../../../../functional-measure.md) is

$$
\int\mathcal D\sigma\,\delta(F[A]-\sigma)\exp\left[-\frac{i}{2\xi}\int d^4x\,\sigma^2\right]
=\exp\left[-\frac{i}{2\xi}\int d^4x\,F[A]^2\right].
$$

Since the action enters as $e^{iS}$, the [gauge fixing](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) contribution is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{gf}}=-\frac1{2\xi}\left(\partial_\mu A^\mu+\lambda A_\mu A^\mu\right)^2.}
$$

The [Faddeev-Popov determinant](../../../../../faddeev-popov-determinant.md) does not depend on $\sigma$ and remains in the [path integral](../../../../../path-integral.md). The limit $\xi\to0$ enforces the original condition sharply; for nonzero $\xi$ this is a weighted average of the gauge slices.
