<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $Q_u=2/3$, $s=q_s^2$, $u=q_u^2$, and suppress the common [colour factor](../../../../../color-factor.md) $\delta_{ji}$. Define $i\mathcal M$ by the product of [Feynman rules](../../../../../feynman-rules.md). With massless external [Dirac spinors](../../../../../dirac-spinor.md) the two graphs give

$$
\mathcal M_{\mu\nu}=-e^2Q_u^2\bar u(p_3)\left[\gamma_\nu\frac{\not q_s}{s}\gamma_\mu+\gamma_\mu\frac{\not q_u}{u}\gamma_\nu\right]u(p_1).
$$

The massless [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) gives $\not p_1u(p_1)=0$ and $\bar u(p_3)\not p_3=0$. Thus

$$
\frac{\not q_s\not p_2}{s}u(p_1)=\frac{\not q_s(\not q_s-\not p_1)}s u(p_1)=u(p_1).
$$

By [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), $p_2=p_3-q_u$, so

$$
\bar u(p_3)\frac{\not p_2\not q_u}{u}=\bar u(p_3)\frac{(\not p_3-\not q_u)\not q_u}{u}=-\bar u(p_3).
$$

The two terms cancel:

$$
\boxed{p_2^\mu\mathcal M_{\mu\nu}=-e^2Q_u^2\bar u(p_3)(\gamma_\nu-\gamma_\nu)u(p_1)=0.}
$$

This is the [Ward identity](../../../../../ward-identity.md) for the incoming [photon](../../../../../photon.md); replacing its [photon polarization vector](../../../../../photon-polarization-vector.md) by its [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) produces zero. The outgoing [photon](../../../../../photon.md) obeys the analogous identity. Each exchange graph separately fails this test, so the cancellation requires their sum. Consequently the physical [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) is invariant under $\varepsilon^\mu(p_2)\mapsto\varepsilon^\mu(p_2)+c p_2^\mu$.
