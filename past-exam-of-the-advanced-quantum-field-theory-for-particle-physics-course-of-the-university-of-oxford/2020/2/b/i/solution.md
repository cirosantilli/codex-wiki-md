The two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) are the $s$-channel and $u$-channel [quark](../../../../../quark.md) exchange graphs, drawn in the middle row of the figure. Along the directed [quark](../../../../../quark.md) line the incoming [photon](../../../../../photon.md) is absorbed before the outgoing [photon](../../../../../photon.md) is emitted in the first graph; those two insertions are reversed in the second. Their internal [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) are $q_s=p_1+p_2$ and $q_u=p_1-p_4$, respectively. There is no three-photon [Feynman vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) in ordinary [QED](../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md), and no two-photon-two-quark [contact interaction](../../../../../contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) in the [Dirac field](../../../../../dirac-field.md) [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md).
