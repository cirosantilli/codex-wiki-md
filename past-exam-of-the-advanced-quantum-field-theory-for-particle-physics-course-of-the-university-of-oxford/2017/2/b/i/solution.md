<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [different-flavour quark annihilation into a massive pair](../../../../../different-flavour-quark-annihilation-into-a-massive-pair.md) has only an $s$-channel [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) graph at tree level when the two [quark flavours](../../../../../quark-flavor.md) differ. Its [amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) contains one incoming and one outgoing [colour current](../../../../../color-charge.md), with common magnitude factor $g_s^2/s$. [On-shell](../../../../../on-shell.md) current conservation removes the gauge-parameter-dependent longitudinal part of the [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) propagator. To display the [Dirac](../../../../../dirac-field.md) signs transparently, evaluate [spin traces](../../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md) with the auxiliary [mostly-minus](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) [metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=-g$ and $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2\eta^{\mu\nu}$; the invariants $s,t$ are then the usual positive-energy [Mandelstam invariants](../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md), numerically identical to those defined in the paper.

The incoming [colour average](../../../../../color-factor.md) and outgoing [colour sum](../../../../../color-factor.md) give

$$
C_{\mathrm{col}}=\frac1{3^2}\sum_{a,b}\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)
=\frac19\frac14\delta^{ab}\delta^{ab}=\frac29.
$$

Averaging over the two [spins](../../../../../spin.md) of each incident [particle](../../../../../particle-physics.md) supplies an additional $1/4$. The physical [spin sums](../../../../../spin-sum.md) are $\sum u\bar u=\not p+M$ and $\sum v\bar v=\not p-M$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline{|T|^2}=\frac{g_s^4}{18s^2}
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma_\mu(\not p_2-M)\gamma_\alpha(\not p_1+M)]\,
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\not k_1\gamma^\alpha\not k_2].}
$$

The coefficient is equivalently $8\pi^2\alpha_s^2/(9s^2)$, with $\alpha_s=g_s^2/(4\pi)$.

**The two top projectors cannot both contain $-M$.** The printed expression has that error. It is not repaired by changing the [metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) convention consistently: a [particle](../../../../../particle-physics.md) and [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) still require the appropriate distinct projectors. In the auxiliary convention, its outgoing [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) would obey $q_\mu H_{\mathrm{printed}}^{\mu\alpha}=8M^2q^\alpha$, rather than zero for $q=p_1+p_2$. At [threshold](../../../../../particle-production-threshold.md) it gives a zero contracted [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md), while the correctly averaged vector-current production [amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) squared has a nonzero limit $4g_s^4/9$. The [phase-space](../../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) still vanishes at [threshold](../../../../../particle-production-threshold.md) because the final [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) tends to zero. The [colour](../../../../../color-charge.md) and [spin](../../../../../spin.md) prefactor above is consequently the prefactor for the corrected [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md); no overall constant can make the misprinted massive [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) the physical answer for all kinematics.
