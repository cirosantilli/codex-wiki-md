<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the source term $\int d^4x[J\phi+\bar\varsigma\psi+\bar\psi\varsigma]$ and left [Grassmann functional derivatives](../../../../../grassmann-derivative.md). Differentiating its exponential inserts fields according to

$$
\boxed{\phi\ \longleftrightarrow\ \frac1i\frac\delta{\delta J},\qquad
\bar\psi\ \longleftrightarrow\ i\frac\delta{\delta\varsigma},\qquad
\psi\ \longleftrightarrow\ \frac1i\frac\delta{\delta\bar\varsigma}.}
$$

The sign for $\bar\psi$ follows because a [left derivative](../../../../../left-grassmann-derivative.md) with respect to $\varsigma$ must cross $\bar\psi$. Keep the order $\bar\psi\gamma_5\psi\phi$ when replacing these fields. Expand $e^{iS_{\mathrm{int}}}$ in the original [path integral](../../../../../path-integral.md); each field monomial can now be generated by these ordered derivatives acting on the [free functional](../../../../../generating-functional.md). Resumming gives

$$
\boxed{Z[J,\bar\varsigma,\varsigma]=\mathcal N\exp\left\{i\int d^4x\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}}\left(\frac1i\frac\delta{\delta J},\ i\frac\delta{\delta\varsigma},\ \frac1i\frac\delta{\delta\bar\varsigma}\right)\right\}Z_0[J,\bar\varsigma,\varsigma].}
$$

The arguments refer respectively to $\phi,\bar\psi,\psi$ at $x$. The [free functional](../../../../../generating-functional.md) arises by performing the free [Gaussian](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) field integrals, and $\mathcal N$ may be chosen so that $Z[0]=1$.

**No additional field-integration measure remains in this differential-operator formula.** Such a measure is printed outside the operator in the source even though $Z_0$ has already integrated out those fields. At $\lambda=0$, that literal expression would contain $\int\mathcal D\phi\mathcal D\bar\psi\mathcal D\psi\,1$ rather than simply $Z_0$, which is not the [free generating functional](../../../../../generating-functional.md). Also, when $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}}$ denotes a density, the spacetime integral in the exponent is required.
