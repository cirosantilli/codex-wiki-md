<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Gauge invariance](../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) of the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) implies the infinitesimal identity

$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\phi_i}(T^a)_{ij}\phi_j=0.
$$

The irrelevant common factor $i$ from the real representation's infinitesimal rotation has been divided out. Differentiate with respect to $\phi_k$ and evaluate at the [stationary](../../../../../stationary-point.md) [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) $v$:

$$
\left.\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial\phi_k\partial\phi_i}\right|_v(T^a)_{ij}v_j
+\left.\frac{\partial V}{\partial\phi_i}\right|_v(T^a)_{ik}=0.
$$

The second term vanishes by [stationarity](../../../../../stationary-point.md), leaving

$$
\boxed{(M_\phi^2)_{ki}(T^av)_i=0.}
$$

Whenever $T^av\ne0$, this is a nonzero vector in the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md) [mass-squared matrix](../../../../../mass-matrix.md), so it is a zero-eigenvalue direction. It is tangent to the degenerate [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) orbit. In a global theory it is a [Goldstone direction](../../../../../goldstone-directions-in-the-scalar-mass-matrix.md); in the gauged theory the corresponding would-be [Goldstone field](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) supplies a [longitudinal polarization](../../../../../longitudinal-polarization-of-a-massive-vector-boson.md) of a massive vector.

For the specific [potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md), [stationarity](../../../../../stationary-point.md) at a nonzero [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) gives $v^2=m^2/(4\lambda)$, assuming $m^2>0$ and $\lambda>0$. Its [Hessian](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
(M_\phi^2)_{ij}=(-m^2+4\lambda v^2)\delta_{ij}+8\lambda v_iv_j=8\lambda v_iv_j.
$$

The antisymmetry of the [triplet generators](../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) gives $v_i(T^av)_i=0$, verifying the claimed [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) directly.
