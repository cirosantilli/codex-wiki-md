<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since the [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) is zero, [electric neutrality](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) means $T^3v=0$. Choose the real-triplet [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) $v_i=v\delta_{i3}$, with $v^2=m^2/(4\lambda)$. With [canonical](../../../../../canonical-field-normalization.md) real-scalar kinetic density $-\tfrac12(D_\mu\Phi)^T D^\mu\Phi$, insert this [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md) into the [covariant derivative](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md). The [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) field $B_\mu$ has no coupling to this [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md), while only $T^1v,T^2v$ are nonzero. Thus

$$
\boxed{M_V^2=g^2v^2\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0,0)\quad\text{in }(W^1,W^2,W^3,B).}
$$

The [charged](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) combinations $W^\pm=(W^1\mp iW^2)/\sqrt2$ have

$$
\boxed{m_{W^\pm}^2=g^2v^2=\frac{g^2m^2}{4\lambda},\qquad m_{W^3}=m_B=0.}
$$

Both $T^3$ and the independent [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) generator annihilate the [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum-state.md). The connected [unbroken subgroup](../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{U(1)_{T^3}\times U(1)_Y.}
$$

The electromagnetic generator $Q=T^3+Y/2$ belongs to it, but it is only one linear combination of two surviving neutral generators. This model has two massless [neutral gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md), rather than the single massless [photon](../../../../../photon.md) and massive neutral vector of the usual scalar-doublet electroweak model. A neutral-field rotation does not create a massive [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) from this zero block.

The two transverse triplet components are eaten, leaving one real, electrically neutral radial [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md) $H=\phi_3-v$. The radial [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the [Hessian](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) found in part i is

$$
\boxed{m_H^2=8\lambda v^2=2m^2,\qquad m_H=\sqrt2\,m.}
$$

Indeed the radial [potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is a constant plus $4\lambda v^2H^2+4\lambda vH^3+\lambda H^4$, fixing the factor of two between its quadratic coefficient and its [mass squared](../../../../../mass.md). The physical degree-of-freedom count is preserved: three real [scalars](../../../../../scalar-field.md) plus four massless vectors give $3+4\times2=11$, while one [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md), two massive vectors and two massless vectors give $1+2\times3+2\times2=11$.
