<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The gauge-invariant [functional integral](../../../../../path-integral.md) counts every [gauge orbit](../../../../../gauge-orbit.md) repeatedly. Insert a gauge-orbit identity

$$
1=\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}[A,\phi]\int\mathcal D\theta\,\delta(G[A^\theta,\phi^\theta]-f),\qquad
\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}=\det\left(\frac{\delta G(x)}{\delta\theta(y)}\right).
$$

Dividing by the [gauge-group](../../../../../gauge-group.md) volume and averaging $f$ with a [Gaussian](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) weight produces the term $-G^2/(2\xi)$. The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is represented by a pair of independent [anticommuting scalar fields](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) $c,\bar c$, giving the prescribed [ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) density $-\bar c\mathcal M c$, where $\mathcal M=\delta G/\delta\theta$. The [Grassmann integral](../../../../../berezin-integral.md) gives a [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) rather than the inverse [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of a [commuting](../../../../../commutator.md) [Gaussian integral](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md); field-independent constants and overall [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) signs are absorbed in [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md).

Although the [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md) is [Abelian](../../../../../abelian-group.md), the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) here is field dependent because the [gauge condition](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) contains the [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md) fluctuation $b$. Thus [Abelian](../../../../../abelian-group.md) [ghosts](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) need not decouple in a broken-phase $R_\xi$ gauge. The fields implement gauge-orbit counting and are not physical external [particles](../../../../../particle-physics.md).
