<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [ghost coupling in a linear Cartesian Abelian Higgs gauge](../../../../../ghost-coupling-in-a-linear-cartesian-abelian-higgs-gauge.md), the common factor in the [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md) parametrization cancels from the [gauge transformation](../../../../../gauge-transformation.md). Separating real and imaginary parts gives

$$
\delta h=g\theta b,\qquad \delta b=-g\theta(v+h),\qquad \delta(\partial\cdot A)=-\Box\theta.
$$

Consequently

$$
\mathcal M=-\Box+\xi g^2v(v+h),\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{gh}}=\bar c\Box c-\xi g^2v^2\bar cc-\xi g^2v h\bar cc.}
$$

For the variables and [gauge function](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) actually printed, the [ghost mass](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) and [Higgs-ghost](../../../../../ghost-coupling-in-a-linear-cartesian-abelian-higgs-gauge.md) [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{m_c^2=\xi g^2v^2,\qquad V_{h\bar cc}=-i\xi g^2v.}
$$

For the usual nonnegative [gauge parameter](../../../../../gauge-fixing-parameter.md), $m_c=\sqrt\xi\,g|v|$. The momentum-space [ghost propagator](../../../../../ghost-propagator.md) is $-i/(k^2+m_c^2-i0)$ in this kinetic convention. There is no direct [ghost-photon](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) or ghost-$b$ [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). At $\xi=0$ the [ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) becomes massless and decouples.

**The [scalar](../../../../../scalar-field.md) prefactor $1/4$ is incompatible with the stated [canonical](../../../../../canonical-field-normalization.md) broken-vacuum interpretation.** At $h=b=0$ it gives $|\phi|^2=v^2/16$, whereas the [potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is minimized at $|\phi|^2=v^2/2$. In fact the printed parametrization gives

$$
\left.\frac{\partial V}{\partial h}\right|_{h=b=0}=-\frac{7\lambda v^3}{256}\ne0.
$$

Its $h,b$ [kinetic terms](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) have coefficient $-1/16$, not $-1/2$, and its vector [mass parameter](../../../../../mass.md) is $g^2v^2/8$. The given [gauge function](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) consequently does not cancel the vector-scalar mixing in those literal variables. The [ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) operator computed above nevertheless follows algebraically from that [gauge function](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) without changing the printed fields.

With a [canonical Abelian Higgs shift](../../../../../canonical-abelian-higgs-shift.md) $\phi=(v+h+ib)/\sqrt2$, the expansion is about a true [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), the vector [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is $m_A=g|v|$, the [gauge fixing](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) cancels the mixing, and the same [ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) formulas acquire the usual interpretation $m_c^2=\xi m_A^2$. For the literal $1/4$ parametrization, a [canonical](../../../../../canonical-field-normalization.md) fluctuation is $h_{\mathrm{can}}=h/\sqrt8$, so its [ghost](../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) would instead be $-i\sqrt8\,\xi g^2v$; the non-vacuum background problem still remains. These two interpretations must not be silently mixed.
