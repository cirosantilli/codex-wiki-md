<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) and [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) permit the same local operators as in the original [renormalizable action](../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md). With renormalized fields, a convenient [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) parametrization is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ct}}=-\delta Z_\phi(D_\mu\phi)^\dagger D^\mu\phi-\delta M^2\phi^\dagger\phi-\frac{\mu^\epsilon\delta\lambda}{4}(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2-\frac{\delta Z_A}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}.}
$$

Here $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-i\mu^{\epsilon/2}eA_\mu$. Expanding the first term supplies kinetic, scalar-scalar-photon, and [seagull](../../../../seagull-vertex.md) [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md) with the relative coefficients required by the [Ward identity](../../../../ward-identity.md). Equivalently introduce separate [vertex](../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) constants first and then impose that identity. For $\phi_0=Z_\phi^{1/2}\phi$, $A_0=Z_A^{1/2}A$, and $e_0=\mu^{\epsilon/2}Z_e e$, it gives $Z_eZ_A^{1/2}=1$. Thus [charge renormalization](../../../../charge-renormalization.md) can be represented through the [photon](../../../../photon.md) field factor, rather than adding an independent gauge-noninvariant [interaction](../../../../interaction-term.md).

The [mass](../../../../mass.md) coefficient defined here is $\delta M^2=Z_\phi m_0^2-m^2$; it must be distinguished from $m_0^2-m^2$. [Gauge fixing](../../../../gauge-fixing.md) may require the corresponding [gauge-parameter](../../../../gauge-fixing-parameter.md) redefinition, and [vacuum diagrams](../../../../vacuum-feynman-diagram.md) can require an additive [vacuum-energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md) [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md). No [photon](../../../../photon.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is allowed by the [unbroken gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md). The [quartic](../../../../quartic-interaction.md) [scalar](../../../../scalar-field.md) [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) remains necessary for the full theory even at a scale where the renormalized $\lambda$ is zero, because gauge [interactions](../../../../interaction-term.md) generate [scalar](../../../../scalar-field.md) [four-point](../../../../four-point-correlation-function.md) divergences.
