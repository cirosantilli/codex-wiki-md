↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the advanced quantum field theory for particle physics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source PDF: [https://mmathphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/sites/g/files/vzsiac2991/files/mmathphys/documents/media/a15272h1corrected.pdf](https://mmathphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/sites/g/files/vzsiac2991/files/mmathphys/documents/media/a15272h1corrected.pdf)

Solutions to all seventeen lettered parts. The source is the version publicly labelled _corrected_; no claim about the contents of an earlier version is needed. Use [natural units](../natural-units.md), the [Minkowski metric](../minkowski-metric.md) $g=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, and $\Box=\partial_\mu\partial^\mu$ for the [d'Alembert operator](../d-alembert-operator.md). In question 2, every denominator has its [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) $+i0$, and $\int_k=\mu_R^{2\epsilon}\int d^{4-2\epsilon}k/(2\pi)^{4-2\epsilon}$. Here $\epsilon$ is the [dimensional regularization](../dimensional-regularization.md) parameter; the small parameter in 1(f) is denoted $\eta$ to distinguish it. Signs follow the insertion [Feynman rules](../feynman-rules.md) printed on source page 4.
