# B411 contribution to the one-loop matter bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop matter bispectrum](one-loop-matter-bispectrum.md)

The connected correlation of one fourth-order and two first-order [density contrasts](density-contrast.md) contracts a pair of inputs at the fourth-order vertex into a loop. For each choice of the fourth-order external field there are twelve [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md). In one dimension,

$$
B_{411}=12P_L(k_1)P_L(k_2)\int\frac{dq}{2\pi}F_4(-k_1,-k_2,q,-q)P_L(q)+\text{two cyclic terms}.
$$

For the [one-dimensional cosmological density kernel](one-dimensional-cosmological-density-kernel.md), $F_4=-k_3^4/(24k_1k_2q^2)$ exactly. Its leading cutoff dependence is therefore proportional to $k_3^2F_2(k_1,k_2)P_L(k_1)P_L(k_2)\int dq\,P_L(q)/q^2$.
