# Reduced Heegaard Floer homology

↑ **Parent:** [Variants of Heegaard Floer homology](variants-of-heegaard-floer-homology.md)

The reduced minus and plus groups are

$$
HF^-_{\mathrm{red}}=\ker(HF^-\to HF^\infty),\qquad HF^+_{\mathrm{red}}=\operatorname{coker}(HF^\infty\to HF^+).
$$

The connecting homomorphism of the fundamental [long exact sequence](long-exact-sequence.md) induces an isomorphism $\delta:HF^+_{\mathrm{red}}\to HF^-_{\mathrm{red}}$ of degree $-1$. This follows immediately from exactness: its image is the indicated kernel, and its kernel is precisely the image quotiented out on the plus side. These groups isolate the information lost by inverting $U$.
