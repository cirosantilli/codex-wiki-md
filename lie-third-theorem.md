# Lie third theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

Every finite-dimensional real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of a connected [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [Lie group](lie-group.md). One construction first uses the [Ado theorem](ado-s-theorem.md) to embed it into a matrix [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), integrates the corresponding left-invariant distribution by the [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md) to a connected immersed [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md), and then takes a [universal covering Lie group](universal-covering-lie-group.md). The immersed [subgroup](subgroup.md) need not be closed; taking its ambient closure may change its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
