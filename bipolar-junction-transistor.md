# Bipolar junction transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_junction_transistor)

A [bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md) has two [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md) sharing a thin base region, arranged n-p-n or p-n-p. In its forward amplification regime, the emitter junction injects [minority carriers](minority-carrier.md); many cross the base before [electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md) and reach the reverse-biased collector junction.[Printed pp. 368–370, Figs. 11–12](transistor-technology-evokes-new-physics-by-william-shockley.md)
