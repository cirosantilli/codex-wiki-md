# Constraint graph

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md)

For a binary [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md), the [undirected graph](undirected-graph.md) with one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) per variable and an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) for each variable pair sharing a [binary constraint](binary-constraint.md). Unary conditions restrict [constraint domains](constraint-domain.md) but add no [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). A more general constraint can be represented in a primal [constraint graph](constraint-graph.md) by joining every pair of variables in its scope; such [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) do not make a genuinely many-variable constraint binary.

After restricting [constraint domains](constraint-domain.md), a [binary constraint](binary-constraint.md) can become automatically satisfied for every remaining pair of values. Removing such constraints can reveal a [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) and enable [tree-structured constraint satisfaction](tree-structured-constraint-satisfaction.md).
