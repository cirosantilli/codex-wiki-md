# Attempt of a Theory of Electrical and Optical Phenomena in Moving Bodies by Hendrik Antoon Lorentz

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Hendrik Antoon Lorentz](work-by-hendrik-antoon-lorentz.md)

Primary text in English translation: [https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation:Attempt_of_a_Theory_of_Electrical_and_Optical_Phenomena_in_Moving_Bodies](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation:Attempt_of_a_Theory_of_Electrical_and_Optical_Phenomena_in_Moving_Bodies)

Original-language facsimile record: [https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/eb/2021/0037/](https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/eb/2021/0037/)

Introduction and sections II and IV formulate moving-body electromagnetism relative to a stationary ether and introduce transformations of the equations. The 2021 date in the facsimile repository is the digitization date, not the publication date. The source is used to distinguish nineteenth-century ether dynamics from [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md)'s 1905 principle-based treatment.
