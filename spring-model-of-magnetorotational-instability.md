# Spring model of magnetorotational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md)

For an incompressible vertical-field mode, frozen-in [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) supplies a spring of frequency $\omega_A$. Its displacement satisfies $\ddot\xi_x-2\Omega\dot\xi_y-4\Omega A\xi_x=-\omega_A^2\xi_x$ and $\ddot\xi_y+2\Omega\dot\xi_x=-\omega_A^2\xi_y$. Their [determinant](determinant.md) gives the [ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md). A weak spring transfers [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) from the faster inner element to the slower outer element, causing inward and outward migration that increase their separation when angular velocity decreases outward. A sufficiently strong spring instead supplies a restoring force.
