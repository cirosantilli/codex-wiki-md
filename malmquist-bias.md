# Malmquist bias

↑ **Parent:** [Standard candle](standard-candle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malmquist_bias)

A flux-limited sample preferentially includes intrinsically brighter objects at a given distance. Calibrating [standard candles](standard-candle.md) from that selected sample can bias inferred distances.
