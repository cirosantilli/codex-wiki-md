# Oikopleura dioica

↑ **Parent:** [Tunicate](tunicate.md)

A larvacean [tunicate](tunicate.md) with dispersed [Hox genes](hox-gene.md) whose expression still shows aspects of anteroposterior ordering. The derived dispersed state is a counterexample to obligatory linkage for all spatial Hox regulation.
