# Noether current

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory.md)

A continuous variational symmetry gives a current satisfying $\partial_\mu j^\mu=0$ [on shell](on-shell.md). Integrating $j^0$ over space gives a conserved charge when boundary flux vanishes.
