# Mean recurrence time

↑ **Parent:** [First return time](first-return-time.md)

The mean recurrence time of $i$ is $\mathbb E_iT_i^+$. In a finite irreducible [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) with [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$, it equals $1/\pi_i$.
