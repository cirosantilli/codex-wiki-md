# Momentum-space Poisson structure for a uniform magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

A uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) defines a constant [Poisson bivector](poisson-bivector.md) on momentum space by the displayed components. For the Hamiltonian $E(\mathbf p)$ and convention $\dot f=\{f,E\}$, it gives $\dot{\mathbf p}=e\nabla E\times\mathbf B$. Constancy makes every term in the coordinate [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) vanish. If $e\mathbf B\ne0$, the [symplectic leaves](symplectic-leaf.md) are the planes $\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf p=c$, and energy is conserved along the flow. The whole three-dimensional momentum space is not symplectic, and an energy surface need not itself be a Poisson submanifold.
