# Radiative-equilibrium obstruction in a rotating barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly rotating stellar hydrostatic equilibrium](uniformly-rotating-stellar-hydrostatic-equilibrium.md)

On a regular [equipotential surface](equipotential-surface.md), $f,f',\rho$ and $\nabla^2\phi$ are constant, but $|\nabla\phi|^2$ generally varies. Consequently a flux $\mathbf F=f(\phi)\nabla\phi$ cannot ordinarily satisfy local [radiative equilibrium](radiative-equilibrium.md) $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\rho\epsilon$ at all latitudes. Special cases such as $f'=0$ or spherical symmetry evade this obstruction. [Stellar meridional circulation](meridional-circulation-in-a-star.md) supplies heat advection in the leading hydrostatic treatment.
