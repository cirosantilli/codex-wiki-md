# CCD clocked charge transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md)

For an [electron](electron.md) packet, a sufficiently positive gate [electric potential](electric-potential.md) attracts [electrons](electron.md) beneath the insulating layer of a [MOS capacitor](mos-capacitor.md). The corresponding [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $U=-e\phi$, where $e>0$ is the magnitude of [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md): a larger [electric potential](electric-potential.md) gives a deeper [potential well](potential-well.md) for [electrons](electron.md). A drawing of [potential energy](potential-energy.md) must therefore reverse the vertical sense of a drawing of [electric potential](electric-potential.md).

A simplified three-phase transfer raises the adjacent gate's [electric potential](electric-potential.md) before lowering the old holding gate. This overlaps the accessible regions, then leaves the packet under the new gate. Repeat with the next gate to move it forward. Isolated wells with an intervening barrier would store the packet without transferring it.[Boyle and Smith, 1970, p. 587, neighboring capacitors and manipulated electrode potentials](charge-coupled-semiconductor-devices-by-willard-sterling-boyle-and-george-elwood-smith.md)
