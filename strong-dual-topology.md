# Strong dual topology

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

For a [locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md) $E$, the [strong dual topology](strong-dual-topology.md) on its [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) $E'$ is generated by seminorms $p_B(T)=\sup_{\varphi\in B}|\langle T,\varphi\rangle|$, where $B$ runs through the [bounded sets in a topological vector space](bounded-set-in-a-topological-vector-space.md) $E$. Convergence therefore means uniform convergence of pairings on every such set. For the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md), the defining boundedness is boundedness of every Schwartz seminorm, rather than one norm or one common compact support.
