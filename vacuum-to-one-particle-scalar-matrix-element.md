# Vacuum-to-one-particle scalar matrix element

↑ **Parent:** [One-particle state](one-particle-state.md)

For a massive spin-zero [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) with covariant normalization and a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) field, [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md) makes $F=\langle\Omega|\phi(0)|p\rangle$ independent of the on-shell [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) within a fixed species. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) to the [rest frame](rest-frame.md), invariance of the [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md) and scalar transformation at the origin prove this. [Translation covariance of a quantum field](translation-covariance-of-a-quantum-field.md) then supplies the plane-wave factor. With noncovariant normalization, the boost law and the displayed momentum independence acquire energy factors.
