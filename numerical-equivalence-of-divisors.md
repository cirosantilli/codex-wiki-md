# Numerical equivalence of divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Positivity of divisors](positivity-of-divisors.md)

Two [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md) are numerically equivalent when they have equal [intersection numbers](intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) with every integral complete curve. A numerically trivial divisor has zero intersection with every such curve. Unlike [linear equivalence of Cartier divisors](linear-equivalence-of-cartier-divisors.md), numerical equivalence does not require the same [line bundle](line-bundle.md).
