# Linear span

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_span)

The linear span of a subset $S$ of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is the set of all finite [linear combinations](linear-combination.md) of elements of $S$.
