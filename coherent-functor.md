# Coherent functor

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent category](coherent-category.md)

A [functor](functor.md) between [coherent categories](coherent-category.md) is coherent when it preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md), image factorizations and finite unions of [subobjects](subobject.md), including the empty union. It consequently preserves the interpretation of every [coherent formula](coherent-formula.md). A [coherent functor](coherent-functor.md) from a [coherent syntactic category](coherent-syntactic-category.md) to a [topos](elementary-topos.md) is equivalent to a model of the corresponding [coherent theory](coherent-theory.md); [natural transformations](natural-transformation.md) correspond to ordinary model homomorphisms.
