# Synthetic antiferromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md)

Two or more ferromagnetic layers coupled to favour antiparallel alignment, often through a thin spacer. Compensating their magnetic moments reduces net stray fields while retaining a useful reference [magnetization](magnetization.md) in spintronic structures.
