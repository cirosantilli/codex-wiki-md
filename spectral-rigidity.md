# Spectral rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md)

A class of metrics is spectrally rigid if equality of their specified [spectra](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) forces the metrics to be isometric, or, in a deformation version, if continuous isospectral deformations are trivial. The [spectrum of a flat torus](spectrum-of-a-flat-torus.md) determines every two-dimensional [flat torus](flat-torus.md) up to [isometry](isometry.md). The [Wolpert generic spectral rigidity theorem](wolpert-generic-spectral-rigidity-theorem.md) is a generic, rather than universal, uniqueness statement.
