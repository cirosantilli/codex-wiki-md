# Leptonic pion decay

↑ **Parent:** [Pion](pion.md)

The [pion decay constant](pion-decay-constant.md) gives $\langle0|J_A^\mu|\pi(p)\rangle\propto f_\pi p^\mu$. Contracting the momentum with the leptonic [weak charged current](charged-current.md) and applying the external Dirac equations makes the amplitude proportional to the charged-lepton mass. This is an explicit example of [helicity suppression](helicity-suppression.md).
