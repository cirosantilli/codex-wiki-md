# Sign-reversal symmetry in Landau theory

↑ **Parent:** [Landau theory](landau-theory.md)

A scalar [order parameter](order-parameter.md) with symmetry $q\mapsto-q$ has only even powers in its zero-field [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). A conjugate field contributes $-hq$. Without this symmetry, odd terms can be allowed and change the transition structure.
