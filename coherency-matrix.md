# Coherency matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization (waves)](polarization-waves.md)

The optical [coherency matrix](coherency-matrix.md) records the second moments of transverse [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md). Normalize its trace to [light intensity](light-intensity.md). Under a deterministic [Jones matrix](jones-matrix.md) $M$, $C\mapsto MCM^\dagger$. [Unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md) has $C=(I_0/2)I_2$, so its output intensity is $(I_0/2)\operatorname{tr}(MM^\dagger)$; it cannot be represented by one fixed [Jones vector](jones-vector.md).
