# Circular ladder operators of a two-dimensional oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For Cartesian [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) $a_x,a_y$, define

$$
a_+^\dagger=\frac{a_x^\dagger+ia_y^\dagger}{\sqrt2},\qquad
a_-^\dagger=\frac{a_x^\dagger-ia_y^\dagger}{\sqrt2},\qquad
N_\pm=a_\pm^\dagger a_\pm.
$$

Their [Hermitian adjoints](adjoint-operator.md) define $a_\pm$. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) imply $[a_\pm,a_\pm^\dagger]=1$, with mixed [commutators](commutator.md) zero. Direct expansion gives

$$
H=\hbar\omega(N_++N_-+1),\qquad
L_z=-i\hbar(a_x^\dagger a_y-a_y^\dagger a_x)=\hbar(N_+-N_-).
$$

Each circular creation adds one [energy](energy-split.md) quantum and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $+\hbar$ or $-\hbar$. At total occupation $N$, the allowed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) values are $N\hbar,(N-2)\hbar,\ldots,-N\hbar$, each once in this [energy eigenspace](energy-eigenspace.md).
