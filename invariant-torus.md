# Invariant torus

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

An invariant torus is a subset homeomorphic to a [torus](torus.md) and preserved by a [flow](flow-map.md). In [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), an integrable Hamiltonian flow moves linearly in the angular coordinates of each regular invariant torus.

The torus is a particular [invariant manifold](invariant-manifold.md); its toroidal topology is an extra condition beyond invariance.
