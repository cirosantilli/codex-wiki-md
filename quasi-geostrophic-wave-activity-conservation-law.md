# Quasi-geostrophic wave-activity conservation law

↑ **Parent:** [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md)

For linear perturbations about a uniform zonal flow, multiplying the forced quasi-geostrophic potential-vorticity equation by the PV anomaly gives a local conservation law for quadratic wave activity. Its flux is the [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md), and forcing or damping appears as a wave-activity source or sink.
