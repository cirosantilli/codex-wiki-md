# Weighted permutation unitarity criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation matrix](permutation-matrix.md)

For a [permutation](permutation.md) $\pi$ of an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), the weighted image columns are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) and have squared [norms](norm.md) $|w_j|^2$. Hence $U^\dagger U=\operatorname{diag}(|w_1|^2,\ldots,|w_d|^2)$ and the displayed criterion follows. All nonzero weights suffice for invertibility, but [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) requires unit-modulus weights. This describes [basis](basis.md) permutations decorated by phases without introducing unwanted changes of amplitude.
