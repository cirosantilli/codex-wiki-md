# Buckley-Leverett equation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-phase porous-media flow](two-phase-porous-media-flow.md)

The one-dimensional [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) equation for saturation when total [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) is constant and capillary pressure and gravity are neglected. It is a [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) with dimensional characteristic speed $(Q/\phi)F'(s)$. Shock speeds obey a [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md); physically admissible solutions satisfy an entropy selection. [Capillary diffusion](capillary-diffusion.md) regularizes the saturation transition.
