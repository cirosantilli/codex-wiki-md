# Thickness fringe

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction contrast](diffraction-contrast.md)

An oscillation of transmitted or diffracted intensity with crystal thickness caused by coupled-wave [electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md). A uniform chemical composition can still show [thickness fringes](thickness-fringe.md).
