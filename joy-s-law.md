<h1 id="joy-s-law">Joy's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sunspot](sunspot.md)

The axis of a bipolar solar active region is statistically tilted relative to the local east-west direction, with its following polarity typically farther from the equator and a mean tilt that grows with latitude. Scatter is substantial. The separation in latitude of the two polarities allows their surface dispersal to generate a net poloidal field through the [Babcock-Leighton mechanism](babcock-leighton-mechanism.md).
