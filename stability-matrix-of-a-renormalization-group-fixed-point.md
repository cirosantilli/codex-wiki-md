# Stability matrix of a renormalization-group fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md)

The stability matrix is the Jacobian of the beta functions at a [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md). Its eigenvalues classify perturbations as relevant, irrelevant, or marginal.
