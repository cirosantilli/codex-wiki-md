# Harmonic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)

A quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) about a stable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). In one dimension $k>0$ is the restoring stiffness and the [force](force-split.md) is $-dV/dx=-kx$. A [mass](mass.md) $m$ undergoing undamped motion has [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\sqrt{k/m}$. A smooth potential with a nondegenerate local minimum is approximately harmonic for sufficiently small [displacements](displacement.md); higher-order terms describe departures from [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md).
