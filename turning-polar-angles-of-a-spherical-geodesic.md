# Turning polar angles of a spherical geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-geodesic first integral](spherical-geodesic-first-integral.md)

With [polar angle](polar-angle.md) $0\le\theta\le\pi$ and $0<|ah|\le1$, the [spherical-geodesic first integral](spherical-geodesic-first-integral.md) gives $\theta_{\min}=\arcsin|ah|$ and $\theta_{\max}=\pi-\arcsin|ah|$. The maximum absolute latitude is the different angle $\arccos|ah|$. At $|ah|=1$ the [great circle](great-circle.md) is the equator; at $h=0$ it is a meridian reaching both poles.
