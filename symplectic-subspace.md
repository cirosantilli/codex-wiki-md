# Symplectic subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md)

A subspace of a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) is symplectic if the restricted [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md). It has a [symplectic orthogonal complement](symplectic-orthogonal-complement.md) and the ambient space is the direct sum of those two subspaces.
