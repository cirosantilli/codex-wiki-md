# Monadic length

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md)

When each comparison functor has a left adjoint, iterating the [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md) produces the monadic tower. Its monadic length is the least number of comparison steps needed to reach an equivalence; an equivalence has length zero and a non-equivalence that is already monadic has length one.
