# Generalized weight space of a Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Weight space](weight-space.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) $\rho$ of a [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) $H$, a generalized weight space is the simultaneous generalized [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) displayed above, where $\lambda:H\to\mathbb C$ is a [linear functional](linear-functional.md). Every occurring $\lambda$ vanishes on the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) of $H$. Ordinary [weight vectors](weight-vector.md) satisfy the same equations without the powers.
