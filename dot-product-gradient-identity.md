# Dot-product gradient identity

↑ **Parent:** [Vector calculus](vector-calculus.md)

For continuously differentiable [vector fields](vector-field.md), the [gradient](gradient.md) of their [dot product](dot-product.md) expands as displayed. The [contraction of two Levi-Civita symbols](contraction-of-two-levi-civita-symbols.md) gives $[F\times(\nabla\times G)]_i=F_j\partial_iG_j-F_j\partial_jG_i$; adding the directional derivative cancels the second term. Interchanging the fields and using the [product rule](product-rule.md) proves the identity. It does not require either field to be irrotational.
