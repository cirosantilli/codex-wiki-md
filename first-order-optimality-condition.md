# First-order optimality condition

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

At an interior local extremum of a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md), its [gradient](gradient.md) is zero: restricting the function to every line through the point shows that all directional derivatives vanish. This is only a necessary condition in general. For a [strictly concave function](strictly-concave-function.md), an interior stationary point is the unique global maximizer on its convex domain. Constrained or boundary optima instead need conditions adapted to their feasible directions.
