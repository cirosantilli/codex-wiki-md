# Archimedean spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_spiral)

An Archimedean spiral has radius linear in polar angle, $r=a+b\theta$. For the special parametrization $(u\cos u,u\sin u)$, the [curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md) is $(u^2+2)/(1+u^2)^{3/2}$. Its parameter speed is $\sqrt{1+u^2}$, so it remains a [regular curve](regular-curve.md) at the origin.
