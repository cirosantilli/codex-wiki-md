# Retroviral life cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Virus](virus.md)

The [retroviral life cycle](retroviral-life-cycle.md) includes entry, RNA-to-DNA copying by reverse transcriptase, a DNA intermediate and gene expression followed by particle production. Integration as a provirus can make the genetic change persistent across cell divisions.
