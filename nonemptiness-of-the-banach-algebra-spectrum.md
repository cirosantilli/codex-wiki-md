# Nonemptiness of the Banach-algebra spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

Every element of a nonzero complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) has a nonempty compact [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md). If the [resolvent of an element](resolvent-of-an-element.md) existed everywhere, composing it with any [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) would give a bounded [entire function](entire-function.md) tending to zero at infinity. The [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md) and point separation by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) would force the resolvent to be zero, contradicting the inverse equation. Nonunital algebras are treated by [unitization](unitization-of-an-algebra.md).
