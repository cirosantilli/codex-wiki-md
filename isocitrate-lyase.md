# Isocitrate lyase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[isocitrate lyase](isocitrate-lyase.md) cleaves [isocitrate](isocitrate.md) into succinate and [glyoxylate](glyoxylate.md). It competes with [isocitrate](isocitrate.md) dehydrogenases for the branchpoint substrate and initiates the [glyoxylate shunt](glyoxylate-shunt.md).
