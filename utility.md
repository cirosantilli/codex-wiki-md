# Utility

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical economics](mathematical-economics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility)

[Utility](utility.md) represents how a decision maker ranks outcomes. A [utility function](utility-function-split.md) assigns numbers to alternatives consistently with those [preference relations](preference-relation.md); ordinal comparisons depend on the ordering rather than the particular numerical scale.
