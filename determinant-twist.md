# Determinant twist

↑ **Parent:** [Rational representation of the general linear group](rational-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md)

Tensoring a [rational representation](rational-representation.md) of the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) with a [determinant](determinant.md) power shifts every highest-weight coordinate by the same integer $r$. Positive twists can clear [determinant](determinant.md) denominators; negative twists supply [rational representations](rational-representation.md) that do not extend to singular matrices. Twisting preserves irreducibility and dimension.
