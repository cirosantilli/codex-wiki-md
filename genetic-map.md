# Genetic map

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md)

A genetic map orders [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) by [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md) and describes distances inferred from [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md). A [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) is a probability of a recombinant transmission; map distances count expected exchanges and can be added along a chromosome, whereas recombination fractions generally cannot. Whole-chromosome identity requires the linkage and recombination model, whereas single-locus [kinship coefficients](kinship-coefficient.md) depend only on descent at that locus.
