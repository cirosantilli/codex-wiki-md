# Area and length of a cycloid arch

↑ **Parent:** [Cycloid](cycloid.md)

For the [cycloid](cycloid.md) $x=a(t-\sin t)$, $y=a(1-\cos t)$, $a>0$, $0\leq t\leq2\pi$, the area between the arch and its baseline is

$$
\mathcal A=\int y\,dx=a^2\int_0^{2\pi}(1-\cos t)^2dt=3\pi a^2.
$$

Its [arc length](arc-length.md) is

$$
L=\int_0^{2\pi}\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}\,dt
=2a\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(t/2)dt=8a.
$$

The positive sign of $\sin(t/2)$ inside this parameter interval removes the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) in the speed. Omitting the two endpoint cusps does not change either [integral](integral-split.md).
