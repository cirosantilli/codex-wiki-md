# Astrocentric osculating-element oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Osculating orbital element](osculating-orbital-element.md)

A circular distant barycentric [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) around a circular star–planet binary need not have constant star-centred [osculating orbital elements](osculating-orbital-element.md). Let $\mu$ be the planet's fraction of the binary [mass](mass.md), $\alpha=a_{\rm pl}/a\ll1$, and $\varepsilon=\mu/\sqrt\alpha\ll1$. Subtracting the stellar reflex [velocity](velocity.md) produces radial and tangential perturbations $(-v_0\varepsilon\sin\Delta,v_0\varepsilon\cos\Delta)$, where $\Delta$ is the planet's longitude minus the particle's longitude. The [vis-viva equation](vis-viva-equation.md) gives the displayed [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) variation. The [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) components follow from $e_r=rv_\theta^2/(GM_\star)-1$ and $e_\theta=-rv_rv_\theta/(GM_\star)$. The errors are $O(\mu+\varepsilon^2)$; the spatial translation itself is only $O(\mu\alpha)$. These are changes of instantaneous orbital description, even when the assumed barycentric trajectory is fixed.
