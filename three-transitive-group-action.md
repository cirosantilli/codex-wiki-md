# Three-transitive group action

↑ **Parent:** [Multiply transitive group action](multiply-transitive-group-action.md)

Transitivity on ordered triples of distinct points. It implies a [two-transitive group action](two-transitive-group-action.md), but is stronger: in projective dimension at least two, collinear and noncollinear triples distinguish two orbits, even though the projective action is two-transitive.
