# GCH preservation by a finite-function collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-function collapse to countable size](finite-function-collapse-to-countable-size.md)

If the ground model satisfies the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), a [finite-function collapse to countable size](finite-function-collapse-to-countable-size.md) of $\kappa$ preserves it. The old $\kappa^+$ becomes the new $\aleph_1$, and there are at most $2^\kappa=\kappa^+$ names for subsets of $\omega$. For every old cardinal $\lambda\geq\kappa^+$ there are at most $2^{\lambda\cdot\kappa}=2^\lambda=\lambda^+$ names for subsets of $\lambda$. [Cardinal preservation by chain-condition forcing](cardinal-preservation-by-chain-condition-forcing.md) and [Cantor theorem](cantor-s-theorem.md) turn these upper bounds into the required equalities.
