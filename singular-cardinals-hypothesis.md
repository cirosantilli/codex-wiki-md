# Singular cardinals hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md)

The singular cardinals hypothesis asserts that every infinite [singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$ satisfies

$$
2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}<\kappa\ \Longrightarrow\ \gimel(\kappa)=\kappa^+.
$$

Equivalently, $\gimel(\kappa)=\max\{\kappa^+,2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}\}$ for every infinite [singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md). Here $\gimel$ is the [gimel function](gimel-function.md). The [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) implies this principle, but the principle only constrains the indicated singular-cardinal exponentiation.
