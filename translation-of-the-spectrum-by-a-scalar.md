# Translation of the spectrum by a scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

The identity $(x+\alpha e)-\lambda e=x-(\lambda-\alpha)e$ proves the formula directly from invertibility. In a nonzero complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), the [nonemptiness of the Banach-algebra spectrum](nonemptiness-of-the-banach-algebra-spectrum.md) implies that if $x$ is a [quasinilpotent element](quasinilpotent-element.md), then $\rho(x+e)=1$. Consequently every nonscalar element cannot simultaneously be quasinilpotent: adding the identity preserves nonscalarity while translating its spectrum.
