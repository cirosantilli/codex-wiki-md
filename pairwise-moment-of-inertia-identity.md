# Pairwise moment-of-inertia identity

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

For positive [masses](mass.md) in their [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame, $\sum_{j<k}m_jm_k|\boldsymbol r_j-\boldsymbol r_k|^2=M\sum_km_kr_k^2$. Thus the scalar inertia $I=\tfrac12\sum_km_kr_k^2$ equals $(2M)^{-1}\sum_{j<k}m_jm_kr_{jk}^2$. The identity compares total spatial extent with pairwise separations.
