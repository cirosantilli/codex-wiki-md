# Dew point

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dew_point)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) at which an air parcel with a specified [water vapor](water-vapor.md) [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) first becomes saturated on cooling at fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md). It solves $E(T_D)=e_v$ with [saturation pressure](vapor-pressure.md) $E$. A falling [dew point](dew-point.md) during ascent does not by itself imply loss of [water vapor](water-vapor.md).
