# Nonloop of a matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

A [nonloop](nonloop-of-a-matroid.md) is an element whose [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) is a [matroid independent set](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md). It is precisely an element that is not a [loop of a matroid](loop-of-a-matroid.md), and its [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) has [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) one.
