# Duad

↑ **Parent:** [Six-point matching geometry](six-point-matching-geometry.md)

A duad is an unordered pair of distinct points. On a six-point set there are $\binom62=15$ duads. They are the edges of the complete graph and are grouped into [synthemes](syntheme.md).
