# Saturable absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturable_absorption)

Absorption that decreases with increasing illumination intensity as the absorber's population distribution changes. It can provide an intensity-dependent loss for passive [Q-switching](q-switching.md).
