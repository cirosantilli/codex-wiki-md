# Face-pair orbits of cube rotations

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

The [rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) has three [group orbits](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) on ordered pairs of faces: equal faces, opposite faces, and adjacent faces. Their sizes are respectively $6$, $6$ and $24$. Fixing one face leaves its cyclic group of four quarter-turns, which acts transitively on the four adjacent faces; this proves transitivity in the last orbit rather than merely counting its points.
