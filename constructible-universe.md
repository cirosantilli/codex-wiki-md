# Constructible universe

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible hierarchy](constructible-hierarchy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructible_universe)

The constructible universe is the [inner model](inner-model.md) $L=\bigcup_{\alpha\in\operatorname{Ord}}L_\alpha$ obtained by iterating the [definable power set](definable-power-set-split.md). It satisfies [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) and the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md).
