# Second-order backward differentiation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Backward differentiation formula](backward-differentiation-formula.md)

The displayed implicit [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md) is second order and [A-stable](a-stability.md). It is the $\alpha=4/3$ member of the [trapezoidal-BDF two-step family](trapezoidal-bdf-two-step-family.md), whose order and boundary-locus proof establish both properties. Its derivative formula uses three equally spaced values; changing the steps requires different coefficients.
