# B-spline differentiation formula

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

For the partition-of-unity normalization, the derivative of a degree-$d$ [B-spline](b-spline.md) is the displayed difference of two overlapping degree-$(d-1)$ [B-splines](b-spline.md). A zero denominator denotes a term with collapsed support, assigned zero. For simple knots the divided-difference representation $N_{i,d}(t)=(t_{i+d+1}-t_i)[t_i,\ldots,t_{i+d+1}](\tau-t)_+^d$ has the required support, smoothness and integral normalization. Differentiate the truncated power and apply the last-step [divided difference](divided-difference.md) recursion to obtain the formula. Repeated-knot versions follow by confluent limits, with one-sided interpretation where necessary.
