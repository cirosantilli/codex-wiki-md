# Periodic convolution operator

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

A [periodic convolution operator](periodic-convolution-operator.md) with continuous periodic kernel is compact on $L^2[0,2\pi]$, since its kernel is square integrable. In the normalized [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) basis it is diagonal, with multipliers $c_n=\int_0^{2\pi}K(x)e^{-inx}dx$. These multipliers include the full integral rather than its normalized [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md).
