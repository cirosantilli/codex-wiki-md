# Modular form on a finite-index subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)

A [modular form](modular-form.md) on an arbitrary [finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md) $\Gamma\leq SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), invariant under the weight-$k$ [slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md), and [holomorphic at a cusp](holomorphic-at-a-cusp.md) at every [cusp of a modular group](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) for $\Gamma$. If $\sigma\in SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ maps infinity to a cusp, choose a genuine translation period $h>0$ with $\sigma T^h\sigma^{-1}\in\Gamma$. Then $f|_k\sigma$ has a convergent series in $e^{2\pi iz/h}$ with nonnegative exponents. A [cusp form](cusp-form.md) has zero constant term at every cusp. This definition allows noncongruence subgroups and avoids sign ambiguities in odd weights when a smaller width is defined only modulo the center.
