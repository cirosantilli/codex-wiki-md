# Susceptible individual

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

In a compartmental epidemic model, a [susceptible individual](susceptible-individual.md) is one who can acquire the modeled [infection](infectious-disease.md). The [susceptible](susceptible-individual.md) [compartment](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md) counts these individuals. An exposed but not yet infectious individual belongs to a separate latent class in an SEIR model; models that retain such individuals in a not-yet-infective [compartment](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md) must state that bookkeeping convention explicitly.
