# Laser transition

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

The transition between the upper and lower working [energy levels](energy-level.md) that supplies stimulated laser emission. It is one part of the pumping cycle, distinct from the complete [laser](laser-split.md) device and from an auxiliary pump transition.
