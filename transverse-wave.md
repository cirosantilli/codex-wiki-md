# Transverse wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transverse_wave)

A [transverse wave](transverse-wave.md) has its oscillating [vector](vector.md) perpendicular to the direction of propagation. In a plane [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) in vacuum, the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) are transverse; their orientation is described by [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md).
