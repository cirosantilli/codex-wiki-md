# Brownian increment

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

An increment of a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) between deterministic times $s<t$ is $W_t-W_s$. It has [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $N(0,t-s)$ and is independent of the filtration up to time $s$. Increments on disjoint time intervals are independent. Their [covariance](covariance.md) is the length of overlap of the corresponding intervals; this also follows from the [Brownian covariance kernel](brownian-covariance-kernel.md).
