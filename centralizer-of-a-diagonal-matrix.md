# Centralizer of a diagonal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonal matrix](diagonal-matrix.md)

For a [diagonal matrix](diagonal-matrix.md) $D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)$ over a [field](field.md), [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) gives $(BD-DB)_{ij}=(d_j-d_i)b_{ij}$. Hence a [matrix](matrix.md) $B$ [commutes](commuting-elements.md) with $D$ exactly when $b_{ij}=0$ wherever $d_i\ne d_j$. If all diagonal entries are distinct, the [centralizer of a diagonal matrix](centralizer-of-a-diagonal-matrix.md) consists exactly of [diagonal matrices](diagonal-matrix.md). When entries repeat, the allowed entries form unrestricted blocks on the corresponding [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md).
