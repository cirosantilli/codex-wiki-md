# Finite ramification support for radical extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer theory](kummer-theory.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) and finite subgroup $\Delta\subset K^*/K^{*n}$, adjoining all the indicated nth roots gives an extension [unramified](unramified-extension.md) outside the displayed finite set. The valuations modulo $n$ are independent of representatives. At a finite place outside $S$, divide each radicand by a local nth power of a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) to obtain a unit. Its polynomial has separable reduction because $n$ is invertible. A finite [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) containing all residue roots contains all lifted roots by [Hensel's lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). This proof does not require $\mu_n\subset K$.
