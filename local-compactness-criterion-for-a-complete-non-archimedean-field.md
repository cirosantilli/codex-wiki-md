# Local compactness criterion for a complete non-Archimedean field

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

A complete field with a nontrivial [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md) is locally compact exactly when its [valuation](valuation.md) is discrete and its [residue field](residue-field.md) is finite. In that case its [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) is the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of the finite rings $\mathcal O/\mathfrak m^n$ and is compact. The [nontrivial valuation in the local compactness criterion](nontrivial-valuation-in-the-local-compactness-criterion.md) is essential.
