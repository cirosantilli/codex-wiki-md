# Temperature-dependent cubic-quartic Landau free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For $\phi\geq0$ and positive $c,B,\lambda,T_0$, define $\gamma=2B^2/(\lambda c)$. Completing the square gives

$$
F=\phi^2\left\{\frac\lambda4\left(\phi-\frac{2BT}{\lambda}\right)^2+\frac c2[(1-\gamma)T^2-T_0^2]\right\}.
$$

For $0<\gamma<1$, [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) occurs at $T^*=T_0/\sqrt{1-\gamma}$ with a discontinuous minimum at $\phi^*=2BT^*/\lambda$. For $\gamma\geq1$, a nonzero [global minimum](global-minimum.md) has lower [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) at every finite [temperature](temperature-split.md). Following the global equilibrium branch then does not force a restoration transition.
