# Finite-ring low-temperature expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic Potts chain](periodic-potts-chain.md)

At fixed finite $L$, the [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) of a [periodic Potts chain](periodic-potts-chain.md) receives its first thermal correction from a pair of [domain walls](domain-wall.md). The [two-domain-wall degeneracy of a Potts ring](two-domain-wall-degeneracy-of-a-potts-ring.md) divided by the [ground state](ground-state.md) [degeneracy factor](degeneracy-factor.md) supplies the coefficient. Expanding the exact [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) proves the formula. The expansion is not uniform in $L$: taking the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) first gives $F/L=-J-\beta^{-1}\log[1+(Q-1)e^{-\beta J}]$, whose leading correction is of order $e^{-\beta J}$.
