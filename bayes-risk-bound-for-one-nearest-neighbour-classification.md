# Bayes risk bound for one-nearest-neighbour classification

↑ **Parent:** [One-nearest-neighbour asymptotic risk](one-nearest-neighbour-asymptotic-risk.md)

Under the hypotheses for the [one-nearest-neighbour asymptotic risk](one-nearest-neighbour-asymptotic-risk.md), the limiting risk satisfies $R^*\leq R_\infty\leq2R^*$. Put $a=\min(\eta,1-\eta)$: then $a\leq2a(1-a)\leq2a$ since $0\leq a\leq1/2$. Integrate, using the definition of [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md). Constant $\eta=1/4$ gives limiting risk $3/8$ and [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md) $1/4$, so the lower comparison is not generally equality.
