# Mean lifetime (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

For an unstable [particle](particle-physics.md) undergoing [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) in its [rest frame](rest-frame.md), the decay waiting time has an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) with [mean](expected-value-split.md) $\tau$. Its probability of surviving to time $t\geq0$ is $e^{-t/\tau}$, and its total [decay width](decay-width.md) is $\Gamma=\hbar/\tau$. In units $\hbar=1$, $\tau=1/\Gamma$.
