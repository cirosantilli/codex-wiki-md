# Constant-power rotation against dry friction

↑ **Parent:** [Torque](torque.md)

For positive [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), a motor delivering constant mechanical [power](power.md) $P$ produces [torque](torque.md) $P/\omega$. With constant opposing [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) torque $\tau_f$, [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) balance gives the displayed equation and a steady speed $\omega_*=P/\tau_f$. A small increase in speed reduces the driving torque below the friction torque, making this equilibrium stable within the model. The constant-power idealization is singular at exact rest and requires a separate starting regime in a real motor.
