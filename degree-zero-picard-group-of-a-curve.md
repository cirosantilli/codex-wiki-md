# Degree-zero Picard group of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)

For a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), the degree-zero Picard group consists of [line bundles](line-bundle.md) of degree zero up to isomorphism, with tensor product as addition. A nonzero [rational section of a line bundle](rational-section-of-a-line-bundle.md) identifies each [line bundle](line-bundle.md) with $\mathcal O(D)$ for a [divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md); degree is the sum of multiplicities weighted by residue-field degrees. Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), every degree-zero [divisor](divisor.md) is a sum of differences of points.
