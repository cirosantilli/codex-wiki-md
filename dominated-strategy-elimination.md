# Dominated strategy elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Dominated strategy](dominated-strategy.md)

In a finite [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md), removing a [strictly dominated strategy](strict-dominance.md) preserves the [value of a zero-sum game](value-of-a-zero-sum-game.md): replace any probability on it by the dominating [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md). This increases every payoff for the maximizing player, or decreases every payoff for the minimizing player. Repeat on the reduced game; new domination relations may appear after a deletion.
