# Jacobi elliptic sine

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi elliptic functions](jacobi-elliptic-functions.md)

The Jacobi elliptic sine is the local inverse of the [elliptic integral of the first kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md): if

$$
u=\int_0^z\frac{dt}{\sqrt{(1-t^2)(1-k^2t^2)}},
$$

then $z=\operatorname{sn}(u,k)$. Its analytic continuation is a meromorphic doubly periodic function.

It is the sn member of the [Jacobi elliptic functions](jacobi-elliptic-functions.md).
