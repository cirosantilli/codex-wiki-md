# Free for substitution

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A [first-order term](first-order-term.md) $\tau$ is [free for substitution](free-for-substitution.md) for a variable $z$ in a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) when no [free variable](free-variable.md) of $\tau$ becomes bound after replacing a free occurrence of $z$. Equivalently, at every free occurrence to be replaced, none of its enclosing [quantifiers](quantifier.md) binds a variable appearing in $\tau$. The test depends on occurrences and scopes, not merely on which variable names appear anywhere in the formula.
