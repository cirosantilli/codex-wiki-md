# History of completing the square

↑ **Parent:** [Completing the square](completing-the-square.md)

[Al-Khwarizmi](al-khwarizmi.md) presented geometric completions of [squares](square.md) in his treatment of [quadratic equations](quadratic-equation.md). His exposition used verbal instructions, positive lengths and [areas](surface-area.md), rather than the modern [algebraic notation](algebraic-notation.md) used here. The [area](surface-area.md) assembly above illustrates the same principle with a different expression and arrangement. The modern [polynomial identity](polynomial-identity.md) also covers negative values of the [variable](variable-mathematics.md) and [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) in the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), which need no interpretation as positive side lengths.[MacTutor, Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Musa Al-Khwarizmi: six standard equation forms, verbal algebra and geometric square completion](al-khwarizmi-by-john-joseph-o-connor-and-edmund-frederick-robertson.md)
