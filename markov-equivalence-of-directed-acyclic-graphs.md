# Markov equivalence of directed acyclic graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md)

Two [Directed acyclic graphs](directed-acyclic-graph.md) are Markov equivalent when they encode the same [D-separations](d-separation.md) for all disjoint sets of vertices. They consequently impose the same [global Markov property for a directed acyclic graph](global-markov-property-for-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md), although their causal interpretations can differ.
