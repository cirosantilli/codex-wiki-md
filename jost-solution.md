# Jost solution

↑ **Parent:** [KdV Schrodinger spectral problem](kdv-schrodinger-spectral-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jost_solution)

A Jost solution of a one-dimensional [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) is specified by plane-wave asymptotics at one spatial infinity. Comparing the left and right Jost solutions defines transmission and reflection coefficients.
