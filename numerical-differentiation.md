# Numerical differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

[Numerical differentiation](numerical-differentiation.md) estimates [derivatives](derivative.md) from finitely many [function](function-split.md) values, often by differentiating an [interpolation polynomial](interpolation-polynomial.md) or using a [finite difference](finite-difference-split.md). Its error combines [truncation error](truncation-error.md) from the formula with errors already present in the sampled values and additional [roundoff error](round-off-error.md). Decreasing the sample spacing can decrease [truncation error](truncation-error.md) while increasing the effect of uncertain values. This tradeoff is explained by the [ill-posedness of numerical differentiation](ill-posedness-of-numerical-differentiation.md) when only an absolute value-error bound is available.
