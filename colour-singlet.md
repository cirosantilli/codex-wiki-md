# Colour singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Color charge](color-charge.md)

A colour singlet transforms in the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) of the [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) colour group. Global colour rotations leave it invariant, and its colour generators annihilate it. A [three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md) uses the completely antisymmetric colour tensor; a quark–antiquark singlet contracts their colour indices.
