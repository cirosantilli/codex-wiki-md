# Pairing gap

↑ **Parent:** [Cooper pair](cooper-pair.md)

A [pairing gap](pairing-gap.md) is an [energy](energy-split.md) scale for fermionic excitations above a paired [superfluid](superfluid.md) or [superconductor](superconductor.md) [ground state](ground-state.md). In a simple isotropic paired system the [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) dispersion has the form $E_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\xi_{\mathbf k}^2+|\Delta|^2}$, where $\xi_{\mathbf k}$ measures the unpaired single-particle [energy](energy-split.md) relative to the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). A nonzero minimum gap gives exponentially small fermionic thermal occupations when $k_BT\ll|\Delta|$. If the gap varies with direction and has nodes, gapless directions can instead produce power-law thermodynamics. Collective excitations must be counted separately from the gapped fermionic [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md).
