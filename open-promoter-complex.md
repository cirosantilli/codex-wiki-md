# Open promoter complex

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription initiation](transcription-initiation.md)

An open promoter complex is a promoter-bound [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) assembly in which a short region of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is unwound and the template strand is accessible to the active site. This distinguishes it from the initially bound closed complex; it need not yet have escaped into productive elongation.
