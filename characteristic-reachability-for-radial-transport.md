# Characteristic reachability for radial transport

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md)

The radial [transport equation](transport-equation-split.md) has projected [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) $(x,y)=e^s(x_0,y_0)$, which remain on positive rays. Data on $x+y=1$ reach precisely the half-plane $x+y>0$, since $x+y=e^s$. A datum $u(a,1-a)=h(a)$ therefore gives $u(x,y)=(x+y)^k h(x/(x+y))$ in that half-plane. The opposite half-plane is not determined by these data. Extending a characteristic line through the origin would join distinct flow orbits across a point where the projected vector field vanishes.
