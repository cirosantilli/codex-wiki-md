# Principal bundle trivialization by a global section

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

A [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) is trivial exactly when it has a global [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md). Given such a section, the displayed map is a smooth equivariant isomorphism: the free transitive action on each fiber gives the unique inverse group coordinate, and local trivializations make it smooth. Conversely, a product trivialization has the section $x\mapsto(x,e)$.
