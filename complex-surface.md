# Complex surface

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A complex surface is a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) of complex dimension two, hence real dimension four. Compact complex surfaces need not admit a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md); a [Hopf surface](hopf-surface.md) is an example. Every [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md) on a compact complex surface is closed, and the [Betti and Hodge bounds for compact complex surfaces](betti-and-hodge-bounds-for-compact-complex-surfaces.md) still apply without Kähler assumptions.
