# Conditional bias-corrected normal mean estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional selection bias after futility continuation](conditional-selection-bias-after-futility-continuation.md)

In a two-stage normal trial, invert the pooled [estimator](estimator.md)'s conditional mean after continuation to correct its selection shift. The root is unique when the pooled stage-1 weight satisfies $0<w<1$, because its conditional-mean map has derivative between $1-w$ and one. This root also maximizes the corresponding conditional [likelihood](likelihood-function.md). It is distinct from exact conditional unbiasedness, which is supplied by a suitable [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md) construction.
