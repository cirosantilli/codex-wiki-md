# Radiocarbon age

↑ **Parent:** [Radiocarbon dating](radiocarbon-dating.md)

A radiocarbon age estimates elapsed time from a measured [carbon-14](carbon-14.md) ratio $R$, an assumed initial ratio $R_0$ and a specified [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md) $\lambda$. The displayed [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) inversion gives the age on that model's timescale. A conventional radiocarbon age uses a prescribed reference and decay convention; it is not generally a calendar age. [Radiocarbon calibration](radiocarbon-calibration.md) accounts for changes in the atmospheric isotope ratio and maps the measurement to a calendar-age estimate.
