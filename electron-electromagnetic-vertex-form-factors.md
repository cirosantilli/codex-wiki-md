# Electron electromagnetic vertex form factors

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

For external [electrons](electron.md) [on shell](on-shell.md), factor out signed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in the parity-preserving [interaction vertex](interaction-vertex.md):

$$
\bar u(p')\Gamma^\mu u(p)
=\bar u(p')\left[\gamma^\mu F_1(q^2)
+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}q_\nu}{2m}F_2(q^2)\right]u(p),
\qquad q=p'-p,\quad p^2=p'^2=m^2,
\quad\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac i2[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu].
$$

The $u$ are [Dirac spinors](dirac-spinor.md); $\gamma^\mu$ are [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md). With [on-shell renormalization scheme](on-shell-renormalization-scheme.md) normalization $F_1(0)=1$, the [Gordon identity](gordon-identity.md) gives $g_s/2=F_1(0)+F_2(0)$ and $a_e=F_2(0)$.[Eqs. (3), (36)–(38)](qed-vertex-correction-vadim-kaplunovsky-course-notes.md)

This explains a subtlety: the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) already predicts a [spin](spin.md) [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) with $F_2=0$. Rewriting its [Dirac electromagnetic current](dirac-electromagnetic-current.md) with the [Gordon identity](gordon-identity.md) produces a spin-dependent term even before any [loop order](loop-order.md) correction. $F_2$ measures the additional response after that baseline is included. The limit $q\to0$ is taken after extracting its coefficient; setting $q=0$ in the [interaction vertex](interaction-vertex.md) at the outset would hide it. This electromagnetic [anomalous magnetic moment](anomalous-magnetic-moment.md) is not a failure of [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) of the kind called a [quantum anomaly](anomaly-physics.md).
