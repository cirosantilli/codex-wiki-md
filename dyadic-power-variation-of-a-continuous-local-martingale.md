# Dyadic power variation of a continuous local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

Along the dyadic [partitions of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) of $[0,1]$, the sums $\sum_i|\Delta_iM|^p$ of a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) tend to zero in [probability](probability.md) for $p>2$. If $0<p<2$ and these sums are eventually bounded [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), then $M$ is constant on $[0,1]$: compare them with the sums defining [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md), using the maximum increment, which tends to zero by [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md).
