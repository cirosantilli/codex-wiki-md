# Sine-Gordon threshold kink-antikink solution

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon kink-antikink scattering solution](sine-gordon-kink-antikink-scattering-solution.md)

The zero-relative-speed limit of the [Sine-Gordon kink-antikink scattering solution](sine-gordon-kink-antikink-scattering-solution.md) is a [separatrix](separatrix.md) at twice the rest-kink energy. A [Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation](sine-gordon-backlund-transformation.md) of the static [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) with equal parameter gives $p_x=-p\tanh x$, $p_t=\operatorname{sech}x$ for $p=\tan(\phi/4)$, hence the displayed solution. The two asymptotic transitions separate logarithmically in time and their speeds tend to zero; this is not a finite-period [Sine-Gordon breather](sine-gordon-breather.md).
