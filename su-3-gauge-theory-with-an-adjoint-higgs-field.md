<h1 id="su-3-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field">SU(3) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(3) gauge theory](su-3-gauge-theory.md)

A traceless Hermitian $3\times3$ [adjoint scalar field](adjoint-scalar-field.md) transforms by $\Phi\mapsto U\Phi U^\dagger$. A diagonal vacuum with eigenvalues $(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)$, summing to zero, leaves its [centralizer](centralizer.md) unbroken. The [adjoint scalar gauge-boson mass matrix](adjoint-scalar-gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) assigns [mass](mass.md) $g|\lambda_i-\lambda_j|$ to the vector fields linking the $i$th and $j$th eigenspaces. Coincident eigenvalues preserve non-Abelian rotations of their eigenspace; distinct eigenvalues preserve only a [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md).
