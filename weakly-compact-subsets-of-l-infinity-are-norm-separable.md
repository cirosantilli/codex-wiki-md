# Weakly compact subsets of l-infinity are norm separable

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly compact set](weakly-compact-set.md)

Coordinate evaluations on the [l-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md) form a countable separating family, so a [weakly compact set](weakly-compact-set.md) $K$ is metrizable by [countable separating family metrizes a weakly compact set](countable-separating-family-metrizes-a-weakly-compact-set.md). Choose a countable weakly dense subset $D$. Then $K$ lies in the weak closure of $\operatorname{conv}D$, which equals its norm closure by [Mazur theorem](mazur-theorem.md). Rational convex combinations make that norm closure separable, and every subset of a separable metric space is separable. The weakly dense subset itself need not be norm dense in a nonconvex $K$.
