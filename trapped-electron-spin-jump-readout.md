# Trapped-electron spin-jump readout

↑ **Parent:** [Penning trap](penning-trap.md)

[Hans Georg Dehmelt](hans-georg-dehmelt.md) describes a $4\ \mathrm K$, $5\ \mathrm T$ [Penning trap](penning-trap.md), with positive ring and negative endcaps $8\ \mathrm{mm}$ apart. Axial RF emission tracked an injected seven-[electron](electron.md) bunch losing particles; reducing the drive retained the last [electron](electron.md). A weak magnetic bottle converted [spin](spin.md) changes into axial-[frequency](frequency.md) changes. At successive probe [frequencies](frequency.md), counted spin flips traced a resonance.[Dehmelt, pp. 584–589, Figs. 1, 3, 6–7; Columbia scan](experiments-with-an-isolated-subatomic-particle-at-rest-by-hans-georg-dehmelt.md)

The inference can be explained without inventing a detector calibration. Let a weak bottle have on-axis field $B_z\simeq B_0+bz^2$. The [magnetic dipole potential energy](magnetic-dipole-potential-energy.md) adds $-\mu_zbz^2$ to the axial [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) [potential energy](potential-energy.md), so

$$
\omega_{z,\mu}^2\simeq\omega_z^2-\frac{2\mu_zb}{m}.
$$

A change in the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) therefore shifts the monitored axial [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). This is a simplified readout model, not a quantitative reconstruction of the historical bottle. It illustrates how a transition in one degree of freedom becomes a detectable change in another.

For the positive [electron spin g-factor](electron-spin-g-factor.md) convention, uniform-field [spin](spin.md) precession and free [cyclotron](cyclotron.md) motion give

$$
\omega_s=\frac{g_s}{2}\frac{eB}{m},\qquad
\omega_c=\frac{eB}{m},\qquad
a_e=\frac{g_s-2}{2}=\frac{\omega_s-\omega_c}{\omega_c},\qquad e>0.
$$

Thus an [anomalous magnetic moment](anomalous-magnetic-moment.md) measurement compares [frequencies](frequency.md) rather than requiring an equally precise absolute [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) calibration. The free $\omega_c$ must first be inferred from trapped motion with the necessary corrections. A spin-flip count versus probe [frequency](frequency.md) supplies evidence for a transition resonance; it does not directly measure a spatial [electron](electron.md) radius. Nor is this radio readout an optical [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) record: different experiments require different preparations and detectors. In a precision comparison, repeat scans with changed drive strength and monitor [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) drift, motional excitation and detector back-action; these are experimental-design checks, not additional historical observations asserted here.
