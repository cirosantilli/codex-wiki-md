# Roots of unity in the p-adic numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Roots of unity in a p-adic field](roots-of-unity-in-a-p-adic-field.md)

For odd $p$, [Teichmuller representatives](teichmuller-representative.md) provide the $p-1$ prime-to-$p$ roots, while the [logarithm isomorphism on deep principal units](logarithm-isomorphism-on-deep-principal-units.md) makes $1+p\mathbb Z_p$ torsion-free. At two, the decomposition $\mathbb Z_2^\times=\{1,-1\}\times(1+4\mathbb Z_2)$ leaves exactly two roots.
