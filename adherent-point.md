# Adherent point

↑ **Parent:** [Closure (topology)](closure-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adherent_point)

An adherent point of $A$ is a point whose every [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) meets $A$. The set of adherent points is the [closure](closure-topology.md) of $A$. This includes all points of $A$, even isolated ones; a [limit point](limit-point.md) in the accumulation-point convention instead requires each neighbourhood to meet $A\setminus\{x\}$.
