# Three-point amputation in the connected-action convention

↑ **Parent:** [One-particle-irreducible vertex](one-particle-irreducible-vertex.md)

Use the Minkowski [generating functional](generating-functional.md) $Z=e^{iW}$, source $+J\phi$, mean field $\varphi=\delta W/\delta J$, and [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) $\Gamma=W-\int J\varphi$. Then $W^{(2)}=iG_2^c$, $W^{(3)}=-G_3^c$, and $\Gamma^{(2)}=-(W^{(2)})^{-1}$. Differentiating this inverse gives $\Gamma^{(3)}=(W^{(2)})^{-1\otimes3}W^{(3)}=-i(G_2^c)^{-1\otimes3}G_3^c$, with integration over the repeated spacetime arguments. Thus [amputation of external propagators](amputation-of-external-propagators.md) gives $i\Gamma^{(3)}$ in this convention. The full connected two-point functions, rather than bare propagators, perform the exact amputation.
