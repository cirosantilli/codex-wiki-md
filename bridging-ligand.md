# Bridging ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md) attaches to two or more metal centers. The symbol $\mu$ records bridging; its [electron](electron.md) donation must be apportioned consistently between the metals in [electron counting](electron-counting.md).
