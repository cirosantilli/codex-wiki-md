# Klein j-invariant classifies complex lattice homothety

↑ **Parent:** [Klein j-invariant](klein-j-invariant.md)

The [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md) shows that $\Delta=(E_4^3-E_6^2)/1728$ has no zeros in the half-plane. For every $a\in\mathbb C$, the weight-twelve form $E_4^3-a\Delta$ has total weighted zero order one. At $a=0$ its sole zero is the order-three zero at the cubic elliptic orbit; at $a=1728$ its sole zero is the order-two zero at the quadratic elliptic orbit. Otherwise it has one simple ordinary zero orbit. Thus each value of the [Klein j-invariant](klein-j-invariant.md) occurs on exactly one modular orbit and classifies one [homothety of complex lattices](homothety-of-complex-lattices.md).
