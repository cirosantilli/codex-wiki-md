# Radial distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Liquid](liquid.md)

In a homogeneous isotropic one-component fluid, $g(r)$ is the conditional [number density](number-density.md) at separation $r$ from a tagged particle divided by the bulk number density $n$. Thus the displayed shell contains the mean number of neighbours. For an uncorrelated ideal gas $g(r)=1$ away from the tagged particle; repulsive cores suppress small separations, while liquid coordination shells can produce damped oscillations approaching one at large distance. This is a pair correlation, not a normalized one-particle radial probability density. The [static structure factor](static-structure-factor.md) measures its Fourier-space counterpart.
