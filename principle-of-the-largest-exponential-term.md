# Principle of the largest exponential term

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

For a fixed finite number of nonnegative terms and $a_L\to\infty$, use $\max_jp_{j,L}\le\sum_jp_{j,L}\le r\max_jp_{j,L}$. The [logarithm](logarithm.md) of $r$, divided by the [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md), tends to zero, and a finite maximum commutes with the [limit superior](limit-superior.md). If all individual logarithmic limits exist, the limit of the sum is their maximum.
