# Great-circle distance

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)

For points on a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$, write their position [vectors](vector.md) relative to the centre as $Ru,Rv$ with $u,v$ [unit vectors](unit-vector.md). The [great-circle distance](great-circle-distance.md) is the shorter [great circle](great-circle.md) arc length, $R$ times its central angle. Hence it equals $R\arccos(u\cdot v)$, including zero for identical points and $\pi R$ for antipodal points. At antipodal points the shortest arc is not unique, but its length is.
