# Flat dust-vacuum Hubble parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

For [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), a positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), flat spatial geometry and negligible [radiation](radiation.md), the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho_m\propto a^{-3}$. Substitution in the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) yields the displayed [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md), with $\Omega_{m0}+\Omega_{\Lambda0}=1$. [radiation](radiation.md) must be restored at early epochs; the formula does not require matter to be the largest present-day density component.
