# Autocorrelation of a square-integrable function

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-correlation](cross-correlation.md)

This deterministic, unnormalized [cross-correlation](cross-correlation.md) of a function with itself exists for every shift when $f\in L^2$, by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). It satisfies $h(0)=\|f\|_2^2$ and $h(-x)=\overline{h(x)}$. With [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $\widehat f(k)=\int e^{-ikx}f(x)dx$, its transform is $|\widehat f|^2$, in the [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) sense when the correlation is not integrable. [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) at zero gives the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md), first for [Schwartz functions](schwartz-function.md) and then by approximation in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Unlike the normalized statistical [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md), its value at zero is not generally one.
