# Alternating-access carrier transport

↑ **Parent:** [Facilitated diffusion](facilitated-diffusion.md)

In an alternating-access model a [protein binding site](protein-binding-site.md) faces either side of a [membrane](cell-membrane-split.md); both empty and substrate-bound carriers can switch orientation. Mass-action binding on each side and conformational transition rates give a four-state carrier cycle coupled to two free-substrate pools. Conservation counts all four carrier states as one [protein](protein-split.md) each and counts bound [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) with the free-substrate pools. Equal source and sink fluxes preserve the total [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) while maintaining throughput.
