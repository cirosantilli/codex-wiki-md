# Anti-instanton

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton](instanton.md)

The orientation-reversed Euclidean trajectory of an [instanton](instanton.md). In a symmetric double well it goes from the right vacuum to the left vacuum and has the same positive action as the forward crossing. Its topological orientation changes sign, not its Euclidean action.
