# Treatment contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Linear contrast of cell means](linear-contrast-of-cell-means.md)

A [treatment contrast](treatment-contrast.md) is a [linear contrast of cell means](linear-contrast-of-cell-means.md) comparing treatment responses, with coefficients adding to zero. Pairwise differences and marginal factorial differences are examples. Its [variance](variance-split.md) depends on the true allocation units and their [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md).
