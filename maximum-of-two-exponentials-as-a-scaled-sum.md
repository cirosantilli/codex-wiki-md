# Maximum of two exponentials as a scaled sum

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md)

For independent [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md) $X,Y$ with the same rate $\lambda>0$, the [convolution](convolution.md) density of $X+Y/2$ is $2\lambda(e^{-\lambda v}-e^{-2\lambda v})$ for $v>0$. The [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) of $\max(X,Y)$ is $(1-e^{-\lambda v})^2$, whose derivative is the same density. This is equality of [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md), not an almost-sure equality of the two expressions formed from the same sample.
