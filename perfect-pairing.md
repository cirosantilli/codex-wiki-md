# Perfect pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space.md)

For finite-dimensional [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) $V,W$ over a [field](field.md) $F$, a bilinear pairing $B:V\times W\to F$ is perfect when the induced [linear maps](linear-map.md) $V\to W^*$ and $W\to V^*$ are [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md). Equivalently, both dimensions agree and the matrix of the pairing in any two bases is invertible. The evaluation pairing between a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) and its [dual space](dual-space.md) is perfect. This notion allows two different spaces; a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) is the case $V=W$.
