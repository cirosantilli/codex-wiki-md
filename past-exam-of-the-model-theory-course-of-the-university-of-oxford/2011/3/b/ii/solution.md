<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the [ultrafilter](../../../../../ultrafilter.md) as $\mathcal U$ to distinguish it from [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) domains. On the direct product $\prod_{i\in I}A_i$, put

$$
\mathbf a\sim\mathbf b\iff\{i\in I:a_i=b_i\}\in\mathcal U.
$$

The domain of the [ultraproduct](../../../../../ultraproduct.md) is the quotient $(\prod_iA_i)/{\sim}$. Its elements are classes $[\mathbf a]$. A constant $c$ is interpreted by $[(c^{\mathcal A_i})_i]$, and a $k$-ary [function](../../../../../function-split.md) symbol by

$$
f^{\mathcal A}([\mathbf a^1],\ldots,[\mathbf a^k])=\left[(f^{\mathcal A_i}(a_i^1,\ldots,a_i^k))_{i\in I}\right].
$$

For a $k$-ary relation symbol,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models R([\mathbf a^1],\ldots,[\mathbf a^k])\iff\{i:\mathcal A_i\models R(a_i^1,\ldots,a_i^k)\}\in\mathcal U.}
$$

These definitions are independent of representatives: if finitely many input representatives are changed, the coordinates where all old and new inputs agree form a set in the filter. At those coordinates the interpreted outputs and relation truth values agree. Filter closure and upward closure therefore preserve the resulting quotient elements and truth conditions.
