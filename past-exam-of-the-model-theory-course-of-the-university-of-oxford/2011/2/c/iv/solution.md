↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The inclusion $\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal D$ is a [structure embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) between [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of the same [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md), but it is not an [elementary embedding](../../../../../elementary-embedding.md), as the existential partner [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) at $u$ shows. Therefore **the [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) is not model-complete**. The facts $\mathcal B\preceq\mathcal C$ and $\mathcal B\preceq\mathcal D$ do not make the intervening inclusion elementary.
