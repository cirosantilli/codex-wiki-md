<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $T$ is [model-complete](../../../../../model-complete-theory.md), the inclusion of a [substructure of a first-order structure](../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal B$, both [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$, is a [structure embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) and hence elementary. Therefore $\mathcal A\preceq\mathcal B$.

Conversely, assume every such inclusion is elementary, and take any [structure embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) $f:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ between [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$. Its image $f[A]$, with the induced [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md), is a substructure of $\mathcal B$ isomorphic to $\mathcal A$, so it is itself a [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$. By hypothesis $f[A]\preceq\mathcal B$. The [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) $\mathcal A\to f[A]$ preserves all [first-order formulas](../../../../../first-order-formula.md), and composing it with this elementary inclusion shows that $f$ is an [elementary embedding](../../../../../elementary-embedding.md). Thus $\boxed{T\text{ is model-complete}\iff(\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal B\models T\Rightarrow\mathcal A\preceq\mathcal B)}$.
