<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [structure embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) preserves and reflects all [atomic formulas](../../../../../atomic-formula.md), hence all [quantifier-free formulas](../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) by induction on Boolean combinations. If $T$ has [quantifier elimination](../../../../../quantifier-elimination.md), replace any $\varphi(\mathbf x)$ by a quantifier-free $\theta(\mathbf x)$ equivalent over $T$. For $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal B\models T$ and a tuple $\mathbf a$ from $A$,

$$
\mathcal A\models\varphi(\mathbf a)\iff\mathcal A\models\theta(\mathbf a)\iff\mathcal B\models\theta(\mathbf a)\iff\mathcal B\models\varphi(\mathbf a).
$$

Thus $\mathcal A\preceq\mathcal B$, and the preceding criterion proves $\boxed{\text{quantifier elimination implies model completeness}.}$
