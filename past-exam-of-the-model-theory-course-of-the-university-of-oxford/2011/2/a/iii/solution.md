A [model-complete theory](../../../../../model-complete-theory.md) is a [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) for which every [structure embedding](../../../../../structure-embedding.md) between two of its [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) is an [elementary embedding](../../../../../elementary-embedding.md). Thus, if $f:\mathcal A\hookrightarrow\mathcal B$ is such an embedding, then for every [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi(\mathbf x)$ and tuple $\mathbf a$ from $A$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models\varphi(\mathbf a)\iff\mathcal B\models\varphi(f\mathbf a).}
$$

[first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) completeness does not assert that every two [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) are elementarily equivalent: it constrains embeddings that exist between [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md), whereas completeness constrains all [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md).
