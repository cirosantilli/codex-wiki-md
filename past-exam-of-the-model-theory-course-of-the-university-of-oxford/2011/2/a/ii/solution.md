<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) has [quantifier elimination](../../../../../quantifier-elimination.md) if every [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) is equivalent over the [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) to a [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) with the same free variables:

$$
\boxed{\text{for each }\varphi(\mathbf x)\text{ there is quantifier-free }\theta(\mathbf x)\text{ with }T\models\forall\mathbf x(\varphi\leftrightarrow\theta).}
$$

Logical truth and falsity may be treated as the empty Boolean combinations when considering sentences in a language without closed terms. The assertion concerns [first-order formulas](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) with free variables, not merely sentences.
