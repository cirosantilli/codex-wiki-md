<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [aleph-zero-categorical theory](../../../../../omega-categorical-theory.md) has, up to [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md), exactly one [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of cardinality $\aleph_0$.

The [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](../../../../../ryll-nardzewski-theorem.md) says that, for a [complete theory](../../../../../complete-theory.md) $T$ in a countable [first-order language](../../../../../first-order-language.md), with infinite [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md), the following conditions are equivalent: $T$ is aleph-zero-categorical; for every positive integer $n$, its [type space](../../../../../type-space.md) $S_n(T)$ of complete types over the empty set is finite; and, for every $n$, there are only finitely many $L$-formulas in $n$ free variables modulo equivalence over $T$. Here $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are equivalent over $T$ when $T\models\forall\mathbf x(\varphi\leftrightarrow\psi)$.
