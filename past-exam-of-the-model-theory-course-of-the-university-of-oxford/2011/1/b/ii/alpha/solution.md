↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

**Yes.** Zero is the unique absorbing element of the multiplicative [first-order structure](../../../../../../first-order-structure.md). It is defined by

$$
\boxed{\forall y\,(x\cdot y=x).}
$$

Zero satisfies this [universal formula](../../../../../../universal-formula.md). Conversely, substituting the actual integer zero for $y$ in the semantic verification forces $x=0$. This use of zero in the verification does not insert a constant symbol into the [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md).
