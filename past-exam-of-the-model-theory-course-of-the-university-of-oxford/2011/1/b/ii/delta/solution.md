**No.** The [prime-sign automorphism of the multiplicative integers](../../../../../../prime-sign-automorphism-of-the-multiplicative-integers.md) from the preceding part sends the positive integer two to minus two. It therefore does not preserve the nonnegative integers, contradicting the necessary [automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability](../../../../../../automorphism-invariance-of-parameter-free-definability.md). This also excludes definability of the strictly positive integers if that convention for the printed notation is used.
