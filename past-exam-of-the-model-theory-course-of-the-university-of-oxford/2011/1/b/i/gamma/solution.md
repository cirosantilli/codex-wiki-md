↑ **Parent:** [Γ](../gamma.md)

**No.** Definability of a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) means definability of its graph. The same additive [structure automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $s(x)=-x$ would have to preserve the graph of multiplication. It sends the graph point $(1,1,1)$ to $(-1,-1,-1)$, which is not a multiplication-graph point because $(-1)(-1)=1\ne-1$. This contradicts [automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability](../../../../../../automorphism-invariance-of-parameter-free-definability.md).
