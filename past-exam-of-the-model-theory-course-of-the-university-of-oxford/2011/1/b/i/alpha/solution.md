↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

**Yes.** In the additive [first-order structure](../../../../../../first-order-structure.md), the [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{x+x=x}
$$

holds exactly at zero, because cancellation in the additive [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) gives $x=0$. This [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) uses only the permitted [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) symbol and equality; it does not require a constant symbol for zero.
