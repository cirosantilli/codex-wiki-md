**No.** Interpreting the printed $\mathbb Z^{\geqslant}$ as the nonnegative integers, negation is an additive [structure automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) carrying the positive integer one outside that set. The set is therefore not invariant under [structure automorphisms](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) and cannot have [parameter-free definability](../../../../../../parameter-free-definability.md). The same argument would apply if the notation were taken to mean the strictly positive integers.
