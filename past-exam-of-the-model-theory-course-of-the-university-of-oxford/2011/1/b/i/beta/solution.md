**No.** The map $s(x)=-x$ is an [automorphism of a first-order structure](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) for the additive integers, but it sends one to minus one. By [automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability](../../../../../../automorphism-invariance-of-parameter-free-definability.md), every parameter-free definable singleton is fixed by every [structure automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md). The singleton containing one is not fixed, so it is not definable.
