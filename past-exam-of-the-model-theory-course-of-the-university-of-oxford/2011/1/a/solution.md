↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Here [parameter-free definability](../../../../parameter-free-definability.md) is intended: parameters are not silently added to the [first-order language](../../../../first-order-language.md). A subset $D\subseteq A^n$ is a [definable set](../../../../definable-set.md) when some $L$-[first-order formula](../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{D=\{\mathbf a\in A^n:\mathcal A\models\varphi(\mathbf a)\}.}
$$

A [definable function](../../../../definable-function.md) is one whose graph is a definable set. Thus $f:A^n\to A$ is definable when an $L$-formula $\theta(\mathbf x,y)$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models\theta(\mathbf a,b)\iff b=f(\mathbf a).}
$$

In particular $\mathcal A\models\forall\mathbf x\exists!y\,\theta(\mathbf x,y)$. Definability over a parameter set $B$ permits parameters from $B$ in these [first-order formulas](../../../../first-order-formula.md), equivalently new constants naming those parameters. That is a different requirement: allowing a parameter naming an element would immediately define its singleton.
