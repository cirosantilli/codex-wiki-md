# Approximation property of an infimum

↑ **Parent:** [Infimum](infimum.md)

Let $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ be nonempty and bounded below. A [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) $\ell$ equals $\inf A$ exactly when every $\varepsilon>0$ admits $a\in A$ with $\ell\leq a<\ell+\varepsilon$. Otherwise $\ell+\varepsilon$ would be a larger [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Taking tolerances $1/n$ gives a [sequence](sequence.md) of members converging to $\ell$. For a real-valued objective with finite [infimum](infimum.md), this supplies a [minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md) without assuming that a [global minimizer](global-minimizer.md) exists.
