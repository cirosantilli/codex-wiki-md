# Advected constant-volume porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Porous gravity current with background flow](porous-gravity-current-with-background-flow.md)

An instantaneous point release of actual volume $V$ per unit width in a [porous gravity current with background flow](porous-gravity-current-with-background-flow.md) has the [Barenblatt solution](barenblatt-solution.md) $h=[R(t)^2-(x-ut)^2]_+/(6at)$, where $R^3=9aVt/(2\phi)$. Its centre translates at pore speed $u=U/\phi$ and its radius grows as $t^{1/3}$. The conserved physical volume is $\phi\int h\,dx=V$.
