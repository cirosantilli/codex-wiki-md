# Symmetry and positivity of a rigid-body resistance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md)

Here $\mathbf F,\mathbf G$ are forces and torques exerted by the body on the fluid, or the external force and torque needed to maintain its motion. The [Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md) makes the six-dimensional [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md) symmetric. The power identity $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf U+\mathbf G\cdot\boldsymbol\Omega=2\mu\int e:e\,dV$ makes it a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) for a body moving in otherwise stationary unbounded fluid.
