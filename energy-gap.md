# Energy gap

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral gap](spectral-gap.md)

An [energy gap](energy-gap.md) is a positive separation between a ground-state sector and its lowest excitations, with the sector, [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) specified. A local positive curvature of an auxiliary field is not alone a proof of a dynamical gap: the kinetic terms and conjugate variables determine the excitation poles.
