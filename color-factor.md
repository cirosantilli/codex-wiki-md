# Color factor

↑ **Parent:** [Color charge](color-charge.md)

The group-theoretic tensor multiplying the kinematic part of a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) involving [color charge](color-charge.md). It is obtained by contracting internal indices among [gauge generators](gauge-generator.md), invariant tensors and [structure constants of a Lie algebra](structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md). For instance, one [gluon](gluon.md) exchange on a single [quark](quark.md) line contributes $\sum_aT^aT^a=C_F\mathbf1$; averaging or summing the squared tensor over external colour labels gives the corresponding [color average](color-average.md) in a squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md).
