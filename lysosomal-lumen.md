# Lysosomal lumen

↑ **Parent:** [Lysosome](lysosome.md)

The [lysosomal lumen](lysosomal-lumen.md) is the aqueous interior of a [lysosome](lysosome.md), separated from [cytosol](cytosol.md) by its surrounding [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). A membrane pump couples [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) to inward transport of [hydrogen ions](hydrogen-ion.md), maintaining an acidic environment for [acid hydrolases](acid-hydrolase.md). [Membrane transport proteins](membrane-transport-protein.md) export digestion products, including [amino acids](amino-acid.md) and [sugars](sugar.md), for reuse in [metabolism](metabolism.md). Compartmentalization combines restricted access with controlled exchange.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Lysosomes Are the Principal Sites of Intracellular Digestion, Figure 13-31: ATP-driven acidification and export of digestion products](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/transport-from-the-trans-golgi-network-to-lysosomes.md)
