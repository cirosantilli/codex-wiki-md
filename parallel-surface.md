# Parallel surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth surface](smooth-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_surface)

A [parallel surface](parallel-surface.md) is a constant-distance normal displacement $P_d=P+d n$ of a [regular surface](smooth-surface.md) with unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $n$. With [shape operator](shape-operator.md) $W=-Dn$, its differential is $(I-dW)DP$; it can become singular where $1-d\kappa_i=0$ for a [principal curvature](principal-curvature.md) $\kappa_i$.
