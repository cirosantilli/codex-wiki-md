# Bosonic coherent-state path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

In a [bosonic coherent-state path integral](bosonic-coherent-state-path-integral.md), coherent-state resolutions between imaginary-time evolution factors turn a normal-ordered grand [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $K=H-\mu N$ into its complex symbol. Coherent-state overlaps supply the first-order temporal term. A [thermal trace](thermal-trace.md) makes the fields periodic in imaginary [time](time-in-physics.md). The [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) couples to number, and interactions retain their normal-ordering convention.
