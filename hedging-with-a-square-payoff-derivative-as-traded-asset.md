# Hedging with a square-payoff derivative as traded asset

↑ **Parent:** [Power option](power-option.md)

In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), the claim paying $S_T^2$ has the displayed positive price $U$ and dynamics $dU/U=(2\mu-r)\,dt+2\sigma\,dW$. Its nonzero volatility lets it replace the stock as a risky hedging asset. A claim with ordinary stock-price function $F(t,s)$ is replicated by $\Gamma=sF_s/(2U)$ units of $U$ and bank-account value $F-sF_s/2$. Substitution in the self-financing equation proves the hedge.
