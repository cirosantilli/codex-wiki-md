# Local Maxwellian

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

A Gaussian velocity profile at each position, with coefficients encoding local density, mean velocity and temperature. Its logarithm is a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of [collision invariants](collision-invariant.md), so its collision term vanishes pointwise in position. Transport need not vanish: the coefficients must also satisfy their transport equations for a [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md) to solve the full [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md).
