# Middle-mass-independent mode of a fixed three-mass spring chain

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Three consecutive [masses](mass.md) $m,M,m$ joined by four identical [springs](spring.md) of constant $k$ between fixed walls have an antisymmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) with zero central [displacement](displacement.md). The two adjacent spring forces cancel on the central mass, while each outer mass experiences restoring force $-2kx$. This proves that the displayed [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is independent of $M$ for every positive central mass. The two symmetric modes satisfy $(2k-m\omega^2)(2k-M\omega^2)-2k^2=0$ and generally do depend on $M$. For $M=m$, all three [mode shapes](mode-shape.md) are $(1,\sqrt2,1)$, $(1,0,-1)$ and $(1,-\sqrt2,1)$, with squared frequencies $(2-\sqrt2)k/m$, $2k/m$, $(2+\sqrt2)k/m$.
