# Missing transverse momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md)

[Missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md) is the negative [vector sum](vector-sum.md) of reconstructed visible [transverse momenta](transverse-momentum.md), with detector corrections specified. Under the ideal hypotheses of zero total incoming [transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md), complete visible reconstruction and exactly one invisible [neutrino](neutrino.md), [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
\mathbf p_T^{\rm miss}=\mathbf p_{T\nu}.
$$

The [neutrino](neutrino.md) is **not directly seen**. Even under these ideal hypotheses, its longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md) $p_{z\nu}$ is not measured. Beam remnants can carry substantial unobserved longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md); the colliding [quarks](quark.md)' longitudinal [momenta](momentum-split.md) are also unknown event by event. Imposing an assumed parent [mass](mass.md) can constrain $p_{z\nu}$, but that imports a hypothesis rather than measuring it. Finite coverage, poorly reconstructed [particle jets](jet-particle-physics.md) and other invisible [particles](particle-physics.md) can also generate or modify [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md).
