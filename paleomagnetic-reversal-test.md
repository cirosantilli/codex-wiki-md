# Paleomagnetic reversal test

↑ **Parent:** [Paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md)

Test whether normal- and reversed-polarity [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) directions are statistically antipodal after suitable structural correction. Agreement supports recording of opposite polarities of the same large-scale [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); failure can reveal overprints or other complications. Passing is not, by itself, proof of primary acquisition.
