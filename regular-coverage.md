# Regular coverage

↑ **Parent:** [Regular category](regular-category.md)

The regular coverage on a small [regular category](regular-category.md) takes each regular epimorphism as a one-arrow cover. Pullback and composition stability make these a coverage basis. It is subcanonical because maps constant on the kernel pair of a regular epimorphism descend uniquely to its quotient.
