# Smooth projective surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth algebraic surface](smooth-algebraic-surface.md)

A [smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md) is a two-dimensional [smooth variety](smooth-algebraic-variety.md) admitting a closed embedding in [projective space](projective-space-split.md). Its integral curves are [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md), and their intersections, arithmetic adjunction and point blowups control its birational geometry.
