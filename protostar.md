# Protostar

↑ **Parent:** [Star formation](star-formation.md)

A protostar is a forming [star](star.md) whose energy comes chiefly from gravitational contraction and [accretion](accretion.md), before sustained core [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md) supplies its [luminosity](luminosity.md). A fixed-mass contraction model approximates the stage after most accretion has ended; an actively accreting protostar need not have fixed [mass](mass.md) or the same [Hayashi track](hayashi-track.md).
