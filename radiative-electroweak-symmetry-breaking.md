# Radiative electroweak symmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

Renormalization-group evolution can change a Higgs squared-mass parameter from positive at a high input scale to negative at lower scales, making a symmetry-breaking scalar expectation energetically favorable. In the [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), the large top Yukawa coupling can drive the up-type Higgs soft squared mass downwards as the scale is lowered. Actual breaking also requires the other Higgs-potential parameters to give a stable suitable minimum; the running of one mass alone is not sufficient.
