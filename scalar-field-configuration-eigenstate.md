# Scalar field configuration eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

A [scalar field configuration eigenstate](scalar-field-configuration-eigenstate.md) assigns eigenvalues of the equal-time field operator at every spatial point. These generalized states play the role of position eigenstates for a system of infinitely many coordinates. Inserting their regulated completeness relations on time slices constructs the [scalar field path integral](scalar-field-path-integral.md).
