# Strictly convex normed space

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)

A [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is strictly convex when distinct unit vectors have their midpoint strictly inside the unit ball. The [Lp space](lp-space.md) is strictly convex for $1<p<\infty$: strict convexity of $|z|^p$ gives a strict integrated midpoint inequality for distinct unit functions. The endpoint spaces $L^1$ and $L^\infty$ need not have this property.
