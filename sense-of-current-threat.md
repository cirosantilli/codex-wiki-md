# Sense of current threat

↑ **Parent:** [Post-traumatic stress disorder](post-traumatic-stress-disorder.md)

The experience that danger is present now, even when a reminder refers to a past event. The cognitive model of [PTSD](post-traumatic-stress-disorder.md) links this to [appraisals](cognitive-appraisal.md) and trauma-memory properties rather than simply the historical severity of the event.[Behaviour Research and Therapy 38, 319–345; current threat, trauma-memory properties and maintaining strategies](a-cognitive-model-of-posttraumatic-stress-disorder-by-anke-ehlers-and-david-m-clark.md)
