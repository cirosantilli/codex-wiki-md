# Soft scalar-mass sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Soft supersymmetry breaking](soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md)

[Soft supersymmetry breaking](soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md) removes quadratic ultraviolet sensitivity but can leave scalar-mass corrections proportional to the soft squared masses and logarithms of scale ratios. The high-momentum difference of a paired scalar and fermion propagator falls as their squared-mass splitting divided by momentum to the fourth power. Heavy soft partners can therefore still create a tuning problem through logarithmic and finite thresholds even though the original quadratic [hierarchy problem](hierarchy-problem.md) has been controlled.
