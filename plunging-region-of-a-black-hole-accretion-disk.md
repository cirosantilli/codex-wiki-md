# Plunging region of a black-hole accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md)

The plunging region begins near the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), where nearly circular motion becomes unstable and radial inflow becomes rapid. If the inflow time is shorter than the stress-transport time, the gas approximately conserves its [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) there.
