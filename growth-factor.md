# Growth factor

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric mean](geometric-mean.md)

For positive quantities measured at successive steps, the growth factor $g_i=Q_i/Q_{i-1}$ is a [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [ratio](ratio.md). It can be below one when the quantity shrinks. Repeated [multiplication](multiplication.md) gives $Q_n=Q_0\prod_{i=1}^n g_i$, so the common factor that reproduces the endpoint after $n$ identical steps is their [geometric mean](geometric-mean.md). A factor $g$ corresponds to a relative change $g-1$; factors must be averaged before subtracting one when summarizing compounded change.
