# Dynamic topography

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)

[Dynamic topography](dynamic-topography.md) is surface or internal-boundary relief supported by stresses from interior flow. Inferring it requires separating effects of the thickness of the [crust](planetary-crust.md), thermal subsidence and other loads, and depends on the assumed interior [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and [density](density.md) structure.
