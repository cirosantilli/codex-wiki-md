# Odd-parity S-matrix of a symmetric double-delta potential

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric double-delta potential](symmetric-double-delta-potential.md)

For positive [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, the odd channel of the [symmetric double-delta potential](symmetric-double-delta-potential.md) has

$$
S_{--}(k)=e^{-2ika}
\frac{k e^{ika}+U_0\sin(ka)}{k e^{-ika}+U_0\sin(ka)}.
$$

For real $k$ its numerator and denominator are complex conjugates, making the channel [unitary](unitary-operator.md). Its poles are the zeros of $k e^{-ika}+U_0\sin(ka)$.
