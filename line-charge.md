# Line charge

↑ **Parent:** [Charge density](charge-density.md)

A line charge is a charge distribution idealized as lying on a curve, described by charge per unit length. An infinite straight line with constant density $\mu$ produces the radial [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $E_r=\mu/(2\pi\varepsilon_0r)$ by [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md), and a logarithmic [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md).
