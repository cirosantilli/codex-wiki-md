# Exchange contribution to the second virial coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md)

For a single internal species of noninteracting particles with quadratic dispersion in three dimensions, expand the [grand canonical partition function](grand-canonical-partition-function.md) at small [quantum gas fugacity](fugacity-statistical-mechanics.md). With $\eta=+1$ for [bosons](boson.md) and $\eta=-1$ for [fermions](fermion.md), $n\lambda^3=z+\eta z^2/2^{3/2}+O(z^3)$ and $\beta p\lambda^3=z+\eta z^2/2^{5/2}+O(z^3)$. Eliminating $z$ gives $p=nk_BT[1-\eta n\lambda^3/2^{5/2}+\cdots]$. Bosonic bunching lowers the [pressure](pressure-split.md) at fixed density; fermionic exclusion raises it. This correction needs no interaction potential. For $g$ equally populated internal species, the coefficient in terms of total [number density](number-density.md) is reduced by $g$. In the dilute one-species [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) the equal-time [pair detection correlation](pair-detection-correlation.md) is $g^{(2)}(r)=1+\eta e^{-2\pi r^2/\lambda^2}$. The exchange contribution to the two-particle cluster [integral](integral-split.md) is therefore $-\tfrac12\int[g^{(2)}(r)-1]d^3r=-\eta\lambda^3/2^{5/2}$, providing a direct pair-counting check of the coefficient.
