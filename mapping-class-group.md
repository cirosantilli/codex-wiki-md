# Mapping class group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological surface](topological-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_class_group)

The mapping class group $\operatorname{Mod}(S)$ of an oriented surface $S$ is the group of orientation-preserving self-homeomorphisms of $S$ modulo [isotopy](isotopy.md). It acts on isotopy classes of curves and arcs.
