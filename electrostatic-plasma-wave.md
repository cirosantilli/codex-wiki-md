# Electrostatic plasma wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

A [plasma](plasma-physics.md) [wave](wave-split.md) dominated by a longitudinal [electric field](electric-field-split.md). The leading [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon\cdot\mathbf k=0$. In a [cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md), this becomes $k_\perp^2S+k_\parallel^2P=0$. An exactly longitudinal finite-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) solution must also satisfy the full [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md); the oblique electrostatic condition is usually an asymptotic large-[refractive index](refractive-index.md) approximation.
