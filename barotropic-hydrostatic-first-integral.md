# Barotropic hydrostatic first integral

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic equilibrium along a Killing vector](hydrostatic-equilibrium-along-a-killing-vector.md)

For a [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) in [hydrostatic equilibrium along a Killing vector](hydrostatic-equilibrium-along-a-killing-vector.md), assume $\rho+p>0$. Integrating $dp/(\rho+p)=-d\log N$ gives the displayed [first integral](first-integral.md), constant on each connected fluid region. For the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p=w\rho$ with constant $w>0$, it gives $\rho N^{(1+w)/w}=C_1$.
