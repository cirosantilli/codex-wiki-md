# Surfactant stabilization of film rupture

↑ **Parent:** [Surfactant](surfactant.md)

Outward interfacial flow from a thinning region dilutes surfactant there and raises its [surface tension](surface-tension.md). The resulting [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) pulls fluid back toward the thinning region, opposes further stretching, and slows rupture.
