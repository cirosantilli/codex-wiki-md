# Rheometry

↑ **Parent:** [Rheology](rheology-split.md)

Measurement of [stress](stress.md)–deformation response using a calibrated [rheometer](rheometer.md). Geometry, [temperature](temperature-split.md), sample history and possible [wall slip](wall-slip.md) must be controlled before interpreting a measured [force](force-split.md) or torque as a bulk constitutive response.
