# NLS ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Schrödinger blowup analysis](nonlinear-schrodinger-blowup-analysis.md)

For the [mass-critical focusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation](mass-critical-focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) in dimension $d$, the NLS ground state is the positive radial solution of

$$
-\Delta Q+Q-Q^{1+4/d}=0.
$$

Its symmetry orbit consists of the optimizers of the sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality.
