<h1 id="finson-probstein-model">Finson–Probstein model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Interplanetary dust](interplanetary-dust.md)

The Finson–Probstein model follows cometary dust after release with negligible ejection speed under stellar gravity reduced by [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md). A grid of [synchrones](synchrone.md) and [syndynes](syndyne.md) organizes the resulting dust-tail morphology.
