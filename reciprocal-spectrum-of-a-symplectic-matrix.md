# Reciprocal spectrum of a symplectic matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic matrix](symplectic-matrix.md)

For a [symplectic matrix](symplectic-matrix.md) $M$ of size $2m$ with determinant one, the defining identity $M^TJM=J$ gives $M^{-1}=J^{-1}M^TJ$. Since similarity and transposition preserve the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md), $\chi_{M^{-1}}=\chi_M$. Factoring $xI-M=M(xM^{-1}-I)$ then proves the displayed reciprocal identity for $x\ne0$. If $\chi_M(z)=(z-\lambda)^dR(z)$ with $R(\lambda)\ne0$, this identity gives $\chi_M(x)=x^{2m-d}(1-\lambda x)^dR(1/x)$ near $x=\lambda^{-1}$. Thus reciprocal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have the same [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md). The fixed points $\pm1$ require the stronger parity argument in [even multiplicities at fixed points of reciprocal root pairing](even-multiplicities-at-fixed-points-of-reciprocal-root-pairing.md).
