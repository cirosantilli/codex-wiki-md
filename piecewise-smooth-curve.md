# Piecewise smooth curve

↑ **Parent:** [Complex contour](complex-contour.md)

A piecewise smooth curve has a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) as a parametrization on a [compact](compact-space.md) [interval](interval-mathematics.md), with a finite partition into subintervals on each of which the parametrization is [continuously differentiable](continuously-differentiable-function.md). One-sided derivatives may differ at joining points, so polygonal [contours](complex-integration-contour.md) are allowed. A [contour integral](contour-integral.md) is the sum of the integrals over those smooth pieces.
