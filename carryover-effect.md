# Carryover effect

↑ **Parent:** [Crossover design](crossover-design.md)

A [carryover effect](carryover-effect.md) is an effect of a previous [treatment](treatment.md) on a later response. Balanced transitions help prevent a treatment from being preceded disproportionately by one particular alternative, but do not ensure that [carryover effects](carryover-effect.md) vanish or are separately identifiable in every model.
