# Power normalization of Fresnel amplitudes

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md)

For real [wave impedances](wave-impedance.md) at normal incidence, the electric-field transmission coefficient $t$ gives transmitted power fraction $T=(Z_1/Z_2)|t|^2$. Reflection gives $R=|r|^2$. Enforcing both tangential electric and magnetic [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) gives $r=(Z_2-Z_1)/(Z_2+Z_1)$ and $t=2Z_2/(Z_1+Z_2)$, which satisfy $R+T=1$.
