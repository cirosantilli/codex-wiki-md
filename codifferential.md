# Codifferential

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md)

The codifferential is the formal $L^2$ adjoint of the exterior derivative. On $p$-forms in dimension $n$, one convention is

$$
\delta=(-1)^{n(p+1)+1}*d*.
$$
