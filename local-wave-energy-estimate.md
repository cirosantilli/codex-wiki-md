# Local wave energy estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Wave energy estimate](wave-energy-estimate.md)

For the unit-speed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), the integral of $(u_t^2+|\nabla u|^2)/2$ over $B_r(x_0)$ at time $T\geq0$ is at most its initial integral over $B_{r+T}(x_0)$. On a shrinking ball the outward energy flux minus the loss from its moving boundary is $-((u_t-\partial_nu)^2+|\nabla_{\mathrm{tan}}u|^2)/2$, hence nonpositive. This estimate establishes the [domain of dependence](domain-of-dependence.md) and [finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md) without any assumptions at spatial infinity.
