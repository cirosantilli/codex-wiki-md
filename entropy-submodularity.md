# Entropy submodularity

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual information](mutual-information.md)

For discrete [random variables](random-variable-split.md) $X,Y,Z$,

$$
H(X,Y)+H(Y,Z)\geq H(Y)+H(X,Y,Z).
$$

The difference between the left- and right-hand sides is the nonnegative [conditional mutual information](conditional-mutual-information.md) $I(X;Z\mid Y)$.
