# Compton scattering experiment of 1923

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

In [Arthur Holly Compton](arthur-holly-compton.md)'s [A Quantum Theory of the Scattering of X-rays by Light Elements](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.21.483) (1923), pp. 494–495 and Figure 4 compare [X-rays](x-ray.md) from a [molybdenum](molybdenum.md) target with rays scattered by [graphite](graphite.md) at right angles. Slits select the rays; a [calcite](calcite.md) crystal analyzes their [wavelengths](wavelength.md) by [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md). The plotted readout is scattered [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) against the crystal's glancing angle, distinct from the fixed $90^\circ$ [photon](photon.md) deflection. The primary-beam comparison uses the same slits, crystal, and tube potential. First-order [diffraction](diffraction.md) lines on both sides establish angular zero. The displaced [spectral line](spectral-line.md) supports a longer outgoing [wavelength](wavelength.md) and hence [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) transfer. The two curves use different vertical scales, so their heights cannot directly compare absolute [wave intensities](intensity-physics.md). The paper postpones a fuller apparatus description; it also states on p. 496 that recoil [electrons](electron.md) from ordinary [X-rays](x-ray.md) had not been observed. Thus this spectral test infers recoil through the conservation model rather than directly tracking an [electron](electron.md).[Compton, Physical Review 21 (1923), pp. 494–496, Experimental Test, Figure 4 and opening of Velocity of recoil of the scattering electrons](a-quantum-theory-of-the-scattering-of-x-rays-by-light-elements-by-arthur-holly-compton.md)
