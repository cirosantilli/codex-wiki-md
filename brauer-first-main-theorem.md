# Brauer first main theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer correspondence](brauer-correspondence.md)

Brauer's first main theorem gives a bijection between the blocks of $kG$ with defect group $D$ and the blocks of $kN_G(D)$ with defect group $D$. Corresponding blocks are related by their images under the [Brauer morphism](brauer-morphism.md).
