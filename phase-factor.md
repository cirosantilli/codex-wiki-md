# Phase factor

↑ **Parent:** [Phase (waves)](phase-waves.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_factor)

A [phase factor](phase-factor.md) is a unit-modulus [complex number](complex-number.md) $e^{i\phi}$. Multiplying a [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) by it changes its [wave phase](phase-waves.md) by $\phi$ while preserving its squared [modulus](modulus.md). A common [phase factor](phase-factor.md) does not change [light intensity](light-intensity.md), while different [phase factors](phase-factor.md) of contributions can change their [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md).
