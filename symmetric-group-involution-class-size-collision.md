# Symmetric-group involution class-size collision

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphisms of the symmetric group](automorphisms-of-the-symmetric-group.md)

A [permutation](permutation.md) of order two is a product of $k$ disjoint [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md). Its [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) has the displayed size. For $k\geq2$, equality with the [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) class requires $(n-2)!/(n-2k)!=2^{k-1}k!$. The left side grows strictly with $n$. At $n=2k$, its ratio to the right side is one only for $k=3$; at $k=2$ it jumps from below to above one between $n=4$ and $n=5$, and for $k\geq4$ it is already greater than one. Thus degree six is the only possible exceptional degree for an [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) not preserving [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md).
