# Evaluation onto the dual of a reflexive dual subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md)

If $Z$ is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) and $X$ is a norm-closed reflexive [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of $Z^*$, evaluation gives the displayed surjection. Restriction $R:Z^{**}\to X^*$ is [weak-star](weak-star-topology.md) continuous and maps the closed [unit ball](unit-ball.md) onto the target ball by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md). The [Goldstine theorem](goldstine-theorem.md) then makes $T(B_Z)$ weak-star dense in $B_{X^*}$, where $T=R J_Z$. Reflexivity of $X$ makes this target [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) equal to its [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md). Convexity and the [Hahn-Banach separation theorem](hahn-banach-separation-theorem.md) convert weak density of this ball image to [norm](norm.md) density.

To pass from density to surjectivity, approximate a desired $f$ by $Tz_1$ with $\|z_1\|\leq\|f\|$ and residual at most $\|f\|/2$. Repeat on each residual. The chosen [vectors](vector.md) have summable [norms](norm.md), with sum at most $2\|f\|$, and the residuals tend to zero geometrically. Completeness of $Z$ produces $z=\sum_nz_n$ with $Tz=f$. Using any smaller residual ratio proves the infimum lifting [norm](norm.md) equals $\|f\|$, although that infimum need not be attained. This argument proves onto-ness, rather than merely density of the evaluation functionals.
