# Nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)

For an [Abelian gauge theory](abelian-gauge-theory.md) with $A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\partial_\mu\alpha$, a nonlinear gauge functional $F[A]$ has [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) $\det(\delta F[A+\partial\alpha]/\delta\alpha)$. This operator can depend on $A$ even though the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of $U(1)$ is trivial. Consequently [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) need not decouple in a nonlinear Abelian gauge, unlike a field-independent linear gauge condition.
