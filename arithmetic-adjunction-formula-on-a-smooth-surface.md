# Arithmetic adjunction formula on a smooth surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth algebraic surface](smooth-algebraic-surface.md)

For an integral curve $C$ on a [smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md), $2p_a(C)-2=C\cdot(C+K_X)$. Apply [Riemann–Roch theorem for algebraic surfaces](riemann-roch-theorem-for-algebraic-surfaces.md) to the [divisor restriction exact sequence](divisor-restriction-exact-sequence.md) $0\to\mathcal O_X(-C)\to\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal O_C\to0$ to derive it. This arithmetic version applies also to singular curves and in arbitrary characteristic.
