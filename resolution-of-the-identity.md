# Resolution of the identity

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md)

A complete [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) resolves the identity as $\sum_j|j\rangle\langle j|=I$. In infinite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) the partial sums converge strongly: their [action](action.md) on each fixed vector converges in norm. Generalized [position](position.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) eigenstates instead give distributional [integral](integral-split.md) resolutions, such as $\int dx\,|x\rangle\langle x|=I$, with their normalization interpreted through the [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md).
