# Neutron drip line

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear drip line](nuclear-drip-line.md)

The [neutron drip line](neutron-drip-line.md) is the neutron-rich boundary at which one- or two-neutron emission becomes energetically possible. [Neutrons](neutron-split.md) beyond that boundary are not stably bound; the absence of a Coulomb barrier often makes their emission prompt, subject to angular-momentum barriers.
