# Red blood cell

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell.md)

A red blood cell is a specialized [biological cell](biological-cell.md) that transports [oxygen](oxygen.md) in blood. Its deformability lets it pass through vessels narrower than its unstressed size. A compliant-body [lubrication](lubrication-theory.md) model isolates the coupling between its deformation, the surrounding viscous film and a driving [pressure](pressure.md) difference; it is an idealization rather than a complete membrane or cytoplasm model.
