# Stellar radiative temperature gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative stellar structure](radiative-stellar-structure.md)

The [radiative temperature gradient](radiative-temperature-gradient.md) needed to carry a spherical stellar [luminosity](luminosity.md) follows by dividing [radiative diffusion in a star](radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) by the [hydrostatic pressure support equation](hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md). It is a logarithmic [pressure](pressure.md) gradient, not $dT/dr$ itself. Comparing it with the [adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md) by the [Schwarzschild criterion](schwarzschild-criterion.md) determines whether purely radiative transport is locally unstable at uniform composition.
