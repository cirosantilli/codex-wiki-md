# Squared uniform distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md)

The square of a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) variable on $(0,1)$ has [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $\sqrt{x}$ and an integrable inverse-square-root [probability density function](probability-density-function.md). Reflection replaces $x$ by $1-x$ in that density.
