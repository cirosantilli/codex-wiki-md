# Secondary cascade below a removal band

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion feedback on a collision timescale](depletion-feedback-on-a-collision-timescale.md)

When a loss mechanism becomes slow again at very small diameters, a depleted intermediate band can feed a lower-normalization [collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md). For size-independent strength the asymptotic collision-dominated slope returns to $-7/2$. In the local two-limit [Yarkovsky removal](yarkovsky-removal.md) model, matching gives $D_-\sim D_0^{8/5}D_+^{-3/5}$ and normalization ratio $K_-/K\sim(D_0/D_+)^{2/5}$. Coefficients depend on the collision kernel; a sharp join is only an asymptotic sketch.
