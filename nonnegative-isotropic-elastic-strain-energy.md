# Nonnegative isotropic elastic strain energy

↑ **Parent:** [Strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md)

In three-dimensional isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), decompose the symmetric strain as $e=pI+d$ with $p=\operatorname{tr}(e)/3$ and $\operatorname{tr}d=0$. The stored energy is $E=\mu\sum_{ij}d_{ij}^2+\tfrac32(3\lambda+2\mu)p^2$. Its nonnegativity for every strain is equivalent to the displayed inequalities: the traceless and purely volumetric test strains prove necessity, and the sum of squares proves sufficiency.
