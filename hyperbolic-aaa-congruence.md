# Hyperbolic AAA congruence

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic law of cosines](hyperbolic-law-of-cosines.md)

A hyperbolic triangle is determined up to isometry by its three angles. Indeed, the angle form of the [hyperbolic law of cosines](hyperbolic-law-of-cosines.md) determines every side length from the angles, after which hyperbolic side-side-side congruence determines the triangle. This differs from Euclidean geometry because hyperbolic space has an intrinsic length scale.
