# Flying focus

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pulse](optical-pulse.md)

A [flying focus](flying-focus.md) is an [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md) whose position of maximum focusing varies with time because different [frequencies](frequency.md) focus at different axial locations and arrive at different times. For a broad incident [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md), $f(\omega)=f_0+\beta(\omega-\omega_0)$ and [spectral phase](spectral-phase.md) $\psi=\eta(\omega-\omega_0)^2$ give $z=f(\omega)$ and $t=\psi'(\omega)+z/c$, hence $v_f/c=[1+2\eta c/\beta]^{-1}$. This is the velocity of a focusing pattern, which can exceed $c$ or be negative without individual portions of the field propagating faster than [light](light.md).
