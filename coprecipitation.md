# Coprecipitation

↑ **Parent:** [Precipitation (chemistry)](precipitation-chemistry.md)

Incorporation or removal of an otherwise soluble component during [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) of another material. Surface adsorption, occlusion of solution and incorporation in the growing solid can make [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) less selective than a solubility-product calculation alone suggests.
