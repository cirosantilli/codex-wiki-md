# Sacrificial layer

↑ **Parent:** [Surface micromachining](surface-micromachining.md)

A temporary material supporting another structure during fabrication, then removed selectively. In an oxide-supported [polysilicon](polysilicon.md) beam, selective oxide removal makes an air gap while protected substrate contacts retain anchors. Removal must reach beneath the structure without destroying it; a purely vertical etch may not release a lateral overhang.
