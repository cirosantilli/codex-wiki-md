# Mercury cadmium telluride

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_cadmium_telluride)

Mercury cadmium telluride is a semiconductor whose composition tunes its [band gap](band-gap.md) and hence its long-wavelength absorption cutoff. It is widely used as the absorbing material in infrared detector arrays.
