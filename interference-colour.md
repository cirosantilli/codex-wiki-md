# Interference colour

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

Colour produced when wavelength-dependent [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) selectively transmits or reflects part of white light. Between crossed [polarizers](polarizer.md), a uniform birefringent plate gives a factor $\sin^2(\pi\Gamma/\lambda)$, where $\Gamma$ is its [optical path difference](optical-path-difference.md). The observed colour also depends on illumination and the spectral response of the observer.
