# Asymptotic normality of the Watterson estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Watterson estimator](watterson-estimator.md)

For fixed $\theta>0$, a [geometric mutation count in a coalescent epoch](geometric-mutation-count-in-a-coalescent-epoch.md) with mean $\mu_i=\theta/i$ has third [moment](moment.md) $\mu_i+6\mu_i^2+6\mu_i^3$. The centered absolute third moment is at most $4\mathbb E[Y_i^3]+4\mu_i^3$, so its sum is $O(\log n)$. The total [variance](variance-split.md) is asymptotic to $\theta\log n$, making the [Lyapunov condition](lyapunov-condition.md) with $\delta=1$ hold. Rescaling by the [harmonic sum](harmonic-sum.md) gives the displayed limit. At $\theta=0$ the estimator is identically zero and the limit is degenerate. The [variance of the Watterson estimator](variance-of-the-watterson-estimator.md) decreases only logarithmically with sample size at a fixed nonrecombining locus.
