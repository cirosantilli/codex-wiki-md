# Split resolvent criterion for a Klein four Galois group

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic resolvent of a quartic](cubic-resolvent-of-a-quartic.md)

Let a [separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md) $f$ of degree four be irreducible over a [field](field.md) $F$. If all three pair sums $r_1=\alpha_1\alpha_2+\alpha_3\alpha_4$, $r_2=\alpha_1\alpha_3+\alpha_2\alpha_4$, and $r_3=\alpha_1\alpha_4+\alpha_2\alpha_3$ lie in $F$, its [Galois group](galois-group.md) is the [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md). Indeed $r_1-r_2=(\alpha_1-\alpha_4)(\alpha_2-\alpha_3)\ne0$, and similarly the other pair sums differ. Every [Galois automorphism](automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md) therefore fixes each of the three partitions into pairs. The kernel of this action of $S_4$ on three partitions is $V_4=\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}$. Irreducibility gives a [transitive group action](transitive-group-action.md) on the four roots, so the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) forces $4$ to divide the group order. Hence the [Galois group](galois-group.md) equals $V_4$. The argument also works in characteristic two.
