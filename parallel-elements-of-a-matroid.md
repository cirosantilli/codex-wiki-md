# Parallel elements of a matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

Distinct [nonloops](nonloop-of-a-matroid.md) $x,y$ are [parallel elements of a matroid](parallel-elements-of-a-matroid.md) when their pair is dependent. Declaring each [nonloop](nonloop-of-a-matroid.md) parallel to itself makes parallelism an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) on the [nonloops](nonloop-of-a-matroid.md). Transitivity follows directly from augmentation: if $\{a,b\}$ and $\{b,c\}$ are dependent but $\{a,c\}$ independent, the independent [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) $\{b\}$ could not be augmented from the independent pair $\{a,c\}$, contradicting the [matroid independence axioms](matroid-independence-axioms.md). In a [linear matroid](linear-matroid.md), nonzero proportional [vectors](vector.md) give [parallel elements of a matroid](parallel-elements-of-a-matroid.md).
