# Solution (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture](mixture.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solution_(chemistry))

A [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) is a homogeneous [mixture](mixture.md) in which designated [solutes](solute.md) are distributed in a [solvent](solvent.md). The distinction between [solute](solute.md) and [solvent](solvent.md) is part of the description; [water](water.md) is the [solvent](solvent.md) in the aqueous [pH](ph.md) examples.
