# Nonsymmorphic space group

↑ **Parent:** [Space group](space-group.md)

A [space group](space-group.md) whose point-group operations cannot all be represented without essential fractional translations for any origin. [screw axes](screw-axis.md) and [glide planes](glide-plane.md) are characteristic examples. Its [point group](point-group.md) alone omits these translational data.
