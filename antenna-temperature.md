# Antenna temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna (radio)](antenna-radio.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antenna_temperature)

The [antenna temperature](antenna-temperature.md) expresses the noise [power](power.md) delivered by a receiving [antenna](antenna-radio.md) as an equivalent input [noise temperature](noise-temperature.md). It depends on the sky and surroundings weighted by the [antenna beam pattern](antenna-beam-pattern.md), as well as losses in the [antenna](antenna-radio.md); it need not be the physical [temperature](temperature-split.md) of either the [antenna](antenna-radio.md) or a single astronomical source.
