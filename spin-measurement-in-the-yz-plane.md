# Spin measurement in the yz plane

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform-field spin precession](uniform-field-spin-precession.md)

For a state initially $z$-up and precessing about $y$, measurement along $\mathbf n=(0,\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$ has the displayed [probabilities](probability.md). [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) can be chosen as $(\cos(\theta/2),i\sin(\theta/2))^T$ and $(i\sin(\theta/2),\cos(\theta/2))^T$. Their [phases](phase-waves.md) must be retained when taking [inner products](inner-product-split.md). Full $2\pi$ rotation changes their signs but leaves isolated-state [probabilities](probability.md) unchanged.
