# Benzene dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Intermolecular force](intermolecular-force.md)

Two [benzene](benzene.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) associated by dispersion and electrostatic interactions. T-shaped and parallel-displaced geometries are important low-energy arrangements; a single permanent dipole–dipole model is inadequate.
