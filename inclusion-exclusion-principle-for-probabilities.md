# Inclusion-exclusion principle for probabilities

↑ **Parent:** [Inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md)

For a finite family of [events](event.md), the [probability](probability.md) of their [union](set-union.md) is the alternating sum of their [intersection](set-intersection.md) [probabilities](probability.md), with the same pattern as the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md). Expand the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of the [union](set-union.md) and apply [linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md); this does not require [independent events](independent-events.md).
