# D wave

↑ **Parent:** [Partial wave](partial-wave.md)

A partial wave with [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $\ell=2$. Its orbital [parity](parity.md) is positive. Coupling it to a spin-one pair can include total $J=1$, allowing a [D wave](d-wave.md) component in the [deuteron](deuteron.md).
