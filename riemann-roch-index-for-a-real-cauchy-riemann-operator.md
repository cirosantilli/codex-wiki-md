# Riemann-Roch index for a real Cauchy-Riemann operator

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy–Riemann operator](cauchy-riemann-operator.md)

A real-linear [Cauchy–Riemann operator](cauchy-riemann-operator.md) on a complex rank-$n$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) over a closed genus-$g$ [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) is a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md) between the usual [Sobolev spaces](sobolev-space-split.md) and has the displayed index. Zero-order real-linear terms do not change this index, since they form a continuous deformation of [Fredholm operators](fredholm-operator.md). For the bundle pulled back by a sphere, subtracting the six-dimensional reparametrization group gives the [dimension formula for regular J-holomorphic spheres](dimension-formula-for-regular-j-holomorphic-spheres.md).
