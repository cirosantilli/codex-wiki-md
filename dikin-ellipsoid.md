# Dikin ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Self-concordant barrier](self-concordant-barrier.md)

For a nondegenerate [self-concordant barrier](self-concordant-barrier.md), the open local Hessian ball

$$
\{x+h:h^\top\nabla^2F(x)h<1\}
$$

is contained in its barrier domain. This is the Dikin ellipsoid. It supplies a quantitative way to ensure that a damped [Newton method](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) stays interior, without relying on Euclidean distance to a possibly curved boundary.
