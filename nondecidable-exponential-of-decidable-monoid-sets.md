# Nondecidable exponential of decidable monoid sets

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential of monoid sets](exponential-of-monoid-sets.md)

For the free monoid on $x,y$, act on $\mathbb N$ by adding word length and on $\{0,1\}$ trivially. Both actions are injective. The equivariant function $f(w,n)$ indicating words of length strictly greater than $n$ ending in $x$ is nonzero, but $y\cdot f=0$. Thus the [exponential of monoid sets](exponential-of-monoid-sets.md) is not decidable. Strict inequality ensures invariance when the original word is empty.
