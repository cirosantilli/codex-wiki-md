# Matter parity allows Majorana neutrino masses

↑ **Parent:** [Matter parity](matter-parity.md)

[Matter parity](matter-parity.md) assigns $-1$ to every matter [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md), including a singlet $N$ describing a conjugate [right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md), and $+1$ to Higgs [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md). Thus the quadratic [superpotential](superpotential.md) term $\frac12M_{ij}N_iN_j$ is even, although it changes [lepton number](lepton-number.md) by two. The higher-degree operators $QQQL$ and $(LH_u)^2$ are also even and violate [baryon number](baryon-number.md) or [lepton number](lepton-number.md). Exact [R-parity](r-parity.md) therefore forbids the renormalizable trilinear violations $LLE$, $LQD$, $UDD$, $NH_dH_u$, $NNN$, but does not imply conservation of these continuous charges in every allowed interaction.
