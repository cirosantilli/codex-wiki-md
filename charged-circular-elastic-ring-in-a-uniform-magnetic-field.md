# Charged circular elastic ring in a uniform magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

A uniformly charged circular ring has

$$
L=\frac m2(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2)-2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2+\frac{qBr^2}{2}\dot\phi.
$$

Here $k$ is the [spring constant](spring-constant.md) for extension of its full circumference and the [symmetric gauge](symmetric-gauge.md) is used. The canonical [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J=mr^2\dot\phi+qBr^2/2$ is conserved. Eliminating the angle gives $m\ddot r=J^2/(mr^3)-q^2B^2r/(4m)-4\pi^2k(r-r_0)$. This model neglects electric self-interaction and noncircular deformation.
