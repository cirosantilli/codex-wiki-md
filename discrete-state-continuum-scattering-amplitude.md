# Discrete-state continuum scattering amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

For a real point coupling $g$ between a three-dimensional continuum and one discrete state, $A=mg^2/(2\pi\hbar^2)$. After the short-distance level shift is absorbed, the isotropic amplitude has the displayed form. The imaginary part is fixed by the outgoing [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md), and $\operatorname{Im}(1/f_k)=-k$ supplies elastic [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).
