# Prime filter of a distributive lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice filter](lattice-filter.md)

A prime filter is a proper [lattice filter](lattice-filter.md) $P$ such that $a\vee b\in P$ implies $a\in P$ or $b\in P$.
