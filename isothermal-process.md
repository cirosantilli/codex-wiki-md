# Isothermal process

↑ **Parent:** [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isothermal_process)

An isothermal process is a thermodynamic change at constant [temperature](temperature.md). In a reversible isothermal step, the working substance remains in thermal equilibrium with a reservoir while exchanging [heat](heat.md).
