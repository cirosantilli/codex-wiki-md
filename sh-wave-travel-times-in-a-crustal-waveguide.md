# SH-wave travel times in a crustal waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [SH-wave](sh-wave.md)

For a flat layer of thickness $h$ and shear [speed](speed.md) $\beta_1$ above a faster half-space of [speed](speed.md) $\beta_2$, unfolding $m$ down-and-up traversals gives the displayed reflected-ray [travel time](travel-time.md). Its slope is the horizontal ray [slowness](slowness.md). At $x=2mh\tan i_c$, with $\sin i_c=\beta_1/\beta_2$, the corresponding head-wave branch $t=x/\beta_2+2mh\sqrt{\beta_1^{-2}-\beta_2^{-2}}$ is tangent to this curve.
