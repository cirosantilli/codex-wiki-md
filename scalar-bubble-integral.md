# Scalar bubble integral

↑ **Parent:** [Bubble diagram](bubble-diagram.md)

The [loop momentum](loop-momentum.md) integral with two massive [quantum field theory propagators](propagator.md) is

$$
B(P)=\int\frac{d^dq}{(2\pi)^d}\frac{1}{(q^2+m^2)((q+P)^2+m^2)}.
$$

Its momentum dependence gives a one-loop [self-energy](self-energy.md) in [cubic scalar field theory](phi-cubed-theory.md) and a quartic effective vertex in [quartic scalar field theory](quartic-interaction.md).
