# Contributory-causal treatment development

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

A research approach that identifies a potentially modifiable maintaining process, changes it experimentally and tests consequences for a clinical difficulty. Changing one contributory cause need not eliminate the difficulty, and a multicomponent manipulation does not identify one exclusive pathway.[Lancet Psychiatry 2, 305–313; 150 patients, six worry sessions plus usual care versus usual care, eight-/24-week outcomes](effects-of-cognitive-behaviour-therapy-for-worry-on-persecutory-delusions-in-patients-with-psychosis-wit-a-parallel-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-with-a-mediation-analysis-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
