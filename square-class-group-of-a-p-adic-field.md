# Square-class group of a p-adic field

↑ **Parent:** [Square class](square-class.md)

For odd $p$, parity of [valuation](valuation.md) and the [square class](square-class.md) of the unit residue give a [group](group-split.md) $C_2\times C_2$, represented by $1,u,p,pu$ for a unit $u$ with nonsquare residue. For $p=2$, [valuation](valuation.md) parity and unit residue modulo eight give $C_2^3$, with generators $-1,5,2$. The unit criteria follow from the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md), using its strong derivative version for $p=2$.
