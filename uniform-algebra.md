# Uniform algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_algebra)

A [uniform algebra](uniform-algebra.md) on a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md) $K$ is a closed complex subalgebra of $C(K)$, with the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), containing the constants and separating points. Evaluation embeds $K$ continuously and injectively in the [character space of an algebra](character-space-of-an-algebra.md). The embedding is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto a closed subset because its domain is compact and its target is Hausdorff. Extra characters need not be point evaluations on the specified $K$.
