# Derivation into a bimodule

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md)

A $k$-linear derivation from a $k$-algebra $R$ to an $R$-$R$-[bimodule](bimodule.md) $M$ is a map $d:R\to M$ satisfying

$$
d(rs)=r d(s)+d(r)s.
$$
