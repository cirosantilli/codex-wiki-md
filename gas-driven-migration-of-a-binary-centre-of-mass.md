# Gas-driven migration of a binary centre of mass

↑ **Parent:** [Gas drag on a planetesimal binary](gas-drag-on-a-planetesimal-binary.md)

For a nearly circular stellar orbit of radius $a_b$ in gas moving at $(1-\eta)$ times the local circular speed, the averaged tangential [gas drag](gas-drag.md) on the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is $-\eta\kappa_bv_K$. [Orbit-averaged drag work](orbit-averaged-drag-work.md) gives

$$
\dot a_b=-2\eta\kappa_ba_b,\qquad \tau_b=\frac1{2\eta\kappa_b}.
$$

If $m_2/m_1=\epsilon\ll1$ and [aerodynamic stopping time](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md) scales as $m^k$, $0<k<1$, then $\tau/\tau_b\simeq\eta\epsilon^k\ll1$. Internal contraction can outpace stellar migration. Tightly gas-coupled drift requires solving radial and azimuthal motion together instead of this weak-drag approximation.
