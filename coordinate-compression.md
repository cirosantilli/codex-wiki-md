# Coordinate compression

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

Coordinate compression replaces coordinates by a smaller representation while retaining the order or distance information needed by an [algorithm](algorithm-split.md). Rank compression retains only order; geometry-specific transformations may instead remove known gaps or widths.
