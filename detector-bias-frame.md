# Detector bias frame

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

A [photodetector](photodetector.md) bias frame is an exposure of effectively zero integration [time](time-in-physics.md) used to estimate the electronic offset and [read noise](read-noise.md). For independent identical bias reads, the [variance](variance-split.md) of their difference is twice the single-read [variance](variance-split.md); subtracting an estimated mean bias removes the offset but adds the uncertainty of that estimate.
