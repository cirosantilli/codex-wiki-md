# Ring-closing metathesis

↑ **Parent:** [Olefin metathesis](olefin-metathesis.md)

Intramolecular [olefin metathesis](olefin-metathesis.md) connecting two alkene sites into a ring. Terminal-alkene substrates can release [ethene](ethylene.md); concentration and preorganization influence competition with chain growth.
