# Three-ion axial normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Three identical repelling charges in a one-dimensional harmonic trap have a translation mode $(1,1,1)$, a breathing mode $(1,0,-1)$, and a symmetric middle-against-outer mode $(1,-2,1)$. Their [frequencies](frequency.md) are $\omega$, $\sqrt3\omega$, and $\sqrt{29/5}\omega$. The translation [frequency](frequency.md) is unchanged by internal [Coulomb forces](coulomb-force.md). Linearizing each pair potential produces a positive spring coefficient $2q^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0d^3)$.
