# Probability simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplex](simplex.md)

The probability simplex consists of vectors of nonnegative weights summing to one. It is the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the coordinate vectors. A [linear function](linear-function.md) $r^Tx$ has maximum $\max_i r_i$ on this simplex; equality holds exactly when the weights are supported on maximizing indices.
