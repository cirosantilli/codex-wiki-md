# Modulus in modular arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic.md)

The positive [integer](integer.md) $m$ in $a\equiv b\pmod m$ is the modulus. It specifies which differences are ignored: exactly the [integer](integer.md) multiples of $m$. It is distinct from the [modulus of a complex number](complex-modulus.md), which measures magnitude.
