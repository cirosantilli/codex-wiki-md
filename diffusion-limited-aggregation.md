# Diffusion-limited aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle aggregation](particle-aggregation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion-limited_aggregation)

In the standard single-seed model, individual particles execute [random walks](random-walk-split.md) until first contact with a growing cluster, where they stick irreversibly. Exposed tips intercept more particles than screened inner regions, producing branched clusters. This differs from [diffusion-limited cluster aggregation](diffusion-limited-cluster-aggregation.md), in which whole existing clusters move and join one another. The two models need not share the same [mass fractal dimension](mass-fractal-dimension.md).
