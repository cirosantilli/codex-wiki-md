# Free particle spectrum on a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Particle in a ring](particle-in-a-ring.md)

For a [particle on a circle](particle-in-a-ring.md) with arc length $x\sim x+2\pi R$ and [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md), the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $P^2/(2m)$ has [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $\phi_n=e^{inx/R}/\sqrt{2\pi R}$, $n\in\mathbb Z$, and [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $E_n=\hbar^2n^2/(2mR^2)$. To see this, periodicity forces the [wave number](wavenumber.md) to be $n/R$, and applying the [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md) $-i\hbar\partial_x$ gives $\hbar n/R$. The integral of $|\phi_n|^2$ over one turn is one. The zero [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is simple; each positive [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) has [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) two, from $n$ and $-n$.
