# Initial object criterion for a covariant local topos

↑ **Parent:** [Local topos](local-topos.md)

The constant singleton [functor](functor.md) must be a [retract in a category](retract-in-a-category.md) of a covariant [representable functor](representable-functor.md). Necessity follows by applying a colimit-preserving global-sections [functor](functor.md) to the canonical [coproduct](coproduct.md) of representables onto the terminal [functor](functor.md). Conversely its Hom [functor](functor.md) is a retract of evaluation, preserves [colimits](colimit.md) and has a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md). Such a retract is represented by an [initial object](initial-object.md) in the [idempotent completion](karoubi-envelope.md). For an idempotent-complete category an actual [initial object](initial-object.md) suffices; without this hypothesis an absorbing-zero [monoid](monoid.md) gives a counterexample to the unqualified claim.
