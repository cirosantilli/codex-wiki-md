# Conditional split of two exponential times

↑ **Parent:** [Hypoexponential distribution](hypoexponential-distribution.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md) with rates $\lambda_1>\lambda_2>0$, let $T=S_1+S_2$. Dividing the joint density of $(S_1,T)$ by the [hypoexponential distribution](hypoexponential-distribution.md) density of $T$ gives a [truncated exponential distribution](truncated-exponential-distribution.md) on $(0,t)$ with rate $\lambda_1-\lambda_2$. Its displayed [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) supplies an E-step when only pair totals are observed. At equal rates the conditional split is uniform, with mean $t/2$.
