# Rank-one spectral-factor Gram matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix representation of a trigonometric polynomial](gram-matrix-representation-of-a-trigonometric-polynomial.md)

For [coefficient](coefficient.md) column $q=(q_0,\ldots,q_d)^T$, $M_{ij}=q_i\overline{q_j}$ is a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) and a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) because $x^*Mx=|q^*x|^2$. Expanding $|q(z)|^2$ on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) gives [coefficient](coefficient.md) $p_k=\sum_{i-j=k}M_{ij}$. Thus $M$ has [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) one when $q\ne0$ and zero otherwise. General feasible [Gram matrices](gram-matrix.md) need not have [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) one.
