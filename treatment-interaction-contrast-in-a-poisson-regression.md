# Treatment interaction contrast in a Poisson regression

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md)

In a [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md) with $\log\mu=\beta_0+\beta_TT+\beta_Bb+\beta_{TB}Tb$, the treatment [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the mean ratio at baseline $b$ is $\beta_T+b\beta_{TB}$. The main treatment coefficient tests the contrast at $b=0$. An overall no-treatment test requires both $\beta_T=0$ and $\beta_{TB}=0$, with their joint [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) for a [Wald test](wald-test.md).
