# Meiotic DNA content

↑ **Parent:** [Meiosis](meiosis.md)

[Ploidy](ploidy.md) counts sets of [chromosomes](chromosome.md); [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount counts material. Let $n$ be the number of [chromosomes](chromosome.md) in one complete set and $C$ the [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount of one unreplicated set. For the ordinary [diploid](diploid.md) model, read each row per completed [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), not as a total across all products:

| Stage | [Ploidy](ploidy.md) | [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount | [Chromosome](chromosome.md) organization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) | $2n$ | $2C$ | Two unreplicated sets |
| After [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) | $2n$ | $4C$ | Two sets; each [chromosome](chromosome.md) has two [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) |
| After [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) | $n$ | $2C$ | One replicated set |
| After [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md) | $n$ | $C$ | One unreplicated set |

In the illustrated $n=2$ example, the starting [biological cell](biological-cell.md) has four [chromosomes](chromosome.md). [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) gives eight [chromatids](chromatid.md), organized into four replicated [chromosomes](chromosome.md). Each product of [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) has two replicated [chromosomes](chromosome.md), hence four [chromatids](chromatid.md); each final product has two unreplicated [chromosomes](chromosome.md). Across completed products, $2(2C)=4C$ after [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) and $4(C)=4C$ after [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md): division redistributes the replicated [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). A [haploid](haploid.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) can therefore have $2C$ without becoming [diploid](diploid.md).
