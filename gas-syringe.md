# Gas syringe

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal gas syringe experiment](isothermal-gas-syringe-experiment.md)

A [gas syringe](gas-syringe.md) traps a [gas](gas.md) in a graduated barrel with a movable plunger, allowing its [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) to be varied and read. In an [isothermal gas syringe experiment](isothermal-gas-syringe-experiment.md), the relevant total [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) also includes any connected tubing and sensor cavity containing the same [gas](gas.md).
