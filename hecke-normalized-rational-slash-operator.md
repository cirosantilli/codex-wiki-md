# Hecke-normalized rational slash operator

↑ **Parent:** [Slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md)

For a rational [matrix](matrix.md) of positive determinant, this slash normalization differs from the [determinant-normalized slash operator](determinant-normalized-slash-operator.md) by $(\det\alpha)^{k/2-1}$. Both are right actions, by the [automorphy factor](automorphy-factor.md) identity. For a positive scalar $t$, the displayed action of $tI$ is multiplication by $t^{k-2}$. On determinant-one matrices it agrees with the ordinary [slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md). This normalization makes a good-prime [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md) a plain sum over its double-coset representatives.
