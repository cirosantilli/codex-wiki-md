# Silanolate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Silanol](silanol.md)

A [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) containing deprotonated [silanol](silanol.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md), $\mathrm{Si-O^-}$, which binds a [metal](metal.md) through [oxygen](oxygen.md). Three [silanol](silanol.md) groups on a corner-deficient [silsesquioxane](silsesquioxane.md) can give a facial tridentate trianionic donor set.
