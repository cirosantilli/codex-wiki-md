# Principal-unit logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Principal unit](principal-unit.md)

For a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$ and all sufficiently large $r$, the [p-adic logarithm](p-adic-logarithm.md) and [p-adic exponential](p-adic-exponential-function.md) are inverse group isomorphisms

$$
1+\mathfrak m_K^r\cong(\mathfrak m_K^r,+).
$$
