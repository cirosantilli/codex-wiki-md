# Third covariant derivatives of a scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric Hessian of a scalar field](symmetric-hessian-of-a-scalar-field.md)

Use the sequential convention $\varphi_{;lmn}=\nabla_n\nabla_m\nabla_l\varphi$ and define $R$ by $X^a{}_{;mn}-X^a{}_{;nm}=R^a{}_{bmn}X^b$. The [symmetric Hessian of a scalar field](symmetric-hessian-of-a-scalar-field.md) gives symmetry in $l,m$, but the [curvature commutator on a covariant tensor](curvature-commutator-on-a-covariant-tensor.md) gives $\varphi_{;l[mn]}=-R^p{}_{lmn}\varphi_{;p}/2$. Averaging the three distinct permutations yields $\varphi_{;(lm)n}=\varphi_{;(lmn)}-(R^p{}_{lmn}+R^p{}_{mln})\varphi_{;p}/3$. Thus a scalar's third covariant derivative is not generally totally symmetric.
