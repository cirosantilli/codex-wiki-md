# Stability of a cubic-quartic scalar potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field vacuum](scalar-field-vacuum.md)

For $V(\phi)=m^2\phi^2/2-\mu\phi^3/6+\lambda\phi^4/24$, positive $\lambda$ makes the classical potential bounded below. For $m^2\ge0$, completing the square gives

$$
V=\phi^2\left[\frac{\lambda}{24}\left(\phi-\frac{2\mu}{\lambda}\right)^2+\frac{m^2}{2}-\frac{\mu^2}{6\lambda}\right].
$$

Thus zero is a global minimum precisely when $\mu^2\le3\lambda m^2$. At a nontrivial equality there are degenerate minima at zero and $2\mu/\lambda$. If $m^2>0$ and the inequality fails, zero is a [false vacuum](false-vacuum.md): its Hessian is positive but another minimum has lower energy. A negative quartic coupling, or a nonzero cubic term without a quartic term, is unbounded below. These are classical or tree-level criteria.
