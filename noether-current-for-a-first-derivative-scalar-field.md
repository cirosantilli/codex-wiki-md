# Noether current for a first-derivative scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

For $\delta\phi=\varepsilon X$ and a [variational symmetry of a Lagrangian density](variational-symmetry-of-a-lagrangian-density.md), write $\Pi^\mu=\partial\mathcal L/\partial(\partial_\mu\phi)$ and $\mathcal E=\mathcal L_\phi-\partial_\mu\Pi^\mu$. The product rule gives $\delta\mathcal L/\varepsilon=\mathcal E X+\partial_\mu(\Pi^\mu X)$. Therefore $j^\mu=\Pi^\mu X-K^\mu$ obeys $\partial_\mu j^\mu=-\mathcal E X$, so it is a [conserved current](conserved-current.md) [on shell](on-shell.md). Spatial integration gives a conserved [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) only when the boundary flux vanishes. The formula extends to several components by summation.
