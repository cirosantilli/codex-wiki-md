<h1 id="s16/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For total nuclear [density](../../../../../density.md) $n$, $n_e=n\zeta$ and $n_H=n(1-\zeta)$. Saha's equation becomes $n\zeta^2/(1-\zeta)=C T^{3/2}e^{-R/(k_BT)}$. At $\zeta\ll1$,

$$
\boxed{\zeta\simeq\sqrt{C/n}\,T^{3/4}e^{-T_H/T},\quad m=\tfrac34,\quad T_H=\frac R{2k_B}\simeq7.89\times10^4\ \mathrm K.}
$$

The factor two in the exponent follows from taking a [square root](../../../../../square-root.md); using the full ionization [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) would double the predicted logarithmic slope.
