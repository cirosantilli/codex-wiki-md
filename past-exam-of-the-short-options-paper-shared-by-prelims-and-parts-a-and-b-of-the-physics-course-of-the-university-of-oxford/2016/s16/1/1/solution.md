<h1 id="s16/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Assume dilute, nondegenerate ideal [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), [protons](../../../../../proton.md) and neutral [atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) in thermal and [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md), with [charge neutrality](../../../../../charge-neutrality.md) $n_p=n_e$. Set $\mu_H=\mu_p+\mu_e$, using each ideal species' translational factor $n\lambda_{\mathrm{th}}^3/g$ and [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) ground-state [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) $-R$. Neglect [excited states](../../../../../excited-state.md) and take the heavy [proton](../../../../../proton.md)/[atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md) thermal-wavelength ratio as one. With [electron spin](../../../../../electron-spin.md) and neutral-atom degeneracies treated in the convention of the source, the remaining factor is

$$
\boxed{\frac{n_e^2}{n_H}=\left(\frac{2\pi m_ek_BT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}e^{-R/(k_BT)}.}
$$

Different partition-degeneracy conventions must be propagated consistently. Screening, dense-plasma level dissolution and departures from [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) lie outside this derivation.
