<h1 id="s16/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Differentiate the approximate dispersion: $v_g=3kv_{th}^2/\omega$, while $v_\phi=\omega/k$. Therefore $\boxed{v_gv_\phi=3v_{th}^2}$. In the high-phase-speed regime that justified the approximation, the group [speed](../../../../../speed.md) is correspondingly small. Trying to make $v_g$ much larger than $v_{th}$ would put the [phase speed](../../../../../phase-speed.md) into the thermally populated distribution, where resonant [Landau damping](../../../../../landau-damping.md) is strong and the undamped-fluid estimate becomes unreliable. This explains the stated tendency for these [electron](../../../../../electron.md) electrostatic [waves](../../../../../wave-split.md), not a universal prohibition on fast [electromagnetic waves](../../../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md) in [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md).
