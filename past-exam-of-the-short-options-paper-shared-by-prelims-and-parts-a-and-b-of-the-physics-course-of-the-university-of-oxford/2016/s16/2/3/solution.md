<h1 id="s16/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In the cold solid, [phonon scattering](../../../../../phonon-scattering.md) makes $\tau\propto T^{-1}$ and therefore conductivity falls as [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) rises, at roughly fixed [carrier density](../../../../../carrier-density.md). After melting and ionization, the character of scattering changes; a hot Coulomb [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) eventually follows an increasing $T^{3/2}$ branch. A minimum in the crossover region is natural, but a weakly coupled [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) formula is not quantitatively justified throughout the warm dense regime.
