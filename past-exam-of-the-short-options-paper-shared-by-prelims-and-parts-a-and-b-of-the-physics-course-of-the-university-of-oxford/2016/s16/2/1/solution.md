<h1 id="s16/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For weak potentials, Boltzmann [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) give $\delta n_e=n_ee\phi/(k_BT)$ against fixed [ions](../../../../../ion.md). [Poisson's equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) becomes $\nabla^2\phi=\phi/\lambda_D^2$ outside a test [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md), giving a [Yukawa potential](../../../../../yukawa-potential.md) with $\boxed{\lambda_D=\sqrt{\epsilon_0k_BT/(n_ee^2)}}$. For a small [electron](../../../../../electron.md) displacement $x$ relative to fixed [ions](../../../../../ion.md), [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) separation produces restoring field $E=n_eex/\epsilon_0$, so $m\ddot x=-n_ee^2x/\epsilon_0$. Hence $\boxed{\omega_p=\sqrt{n_ee^2/(\epsilon_0m_e)}}$. The two formulas respectively encode thermal screening and [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md).
