↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Choose $N_D=(4\pi/3)n_e\lambda_D^3$. The stipulated estimate then gives

$$
\tau\simeq\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0^2\sqrt{m_e}(k_BT)^{3/2}}{3Z n_ee^4},\qquad
\boxed{\sigma\simeq\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0^2(k_BT)^{3/2}}{3Z e^2\sqrt{m_e}}.}
$$

Thus [density](../../../../../density.md) cancels and conductivity grows as $T^{3/2}$ at fixed [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md). Physically faster [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) are less effectively deflected by Coulomb encounters. Numerical factors depend on the Debye-volume convention, and a realistic collision calculation introduces a [Coulomb logarithm](../../../../../coulomb-logarithm.md) and evolving ionization; the cancellation is approximate rather than exact for every real [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md).
