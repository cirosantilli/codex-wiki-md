↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

“Never” cannot be established by one present price comparison. A useful comparison is levelized cost with common financing, capacity and system boundaries. For illustration, solar capital cost 1000 currency units/kW, annual capital-plus-maintenance charge 8%, and [capacity factor](../../../../../capacity-factor.md) 0.15 imply about 61 units/MWh. A coal plant with fuel, maintenance and capital costs of the same order is not separated by an immutable physical barrier. These are illustrative inputs, not current price quotations. Carbon costs and air pollution can worsen coal's comparison; intermittency, land and balancing costs can worsen solar's. Compare delivered services as well as panel-level [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The defensible conclusion is contingent competition, not a universal impossibility.
