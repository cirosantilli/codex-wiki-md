<h1 id="s4/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Electricity is a strong early decarbonization target because large stationary sources are comparatively accessible and a low-carbon grid enables cleaner vehicles, [heat pumps](../../../../../heat-pump.md) and industry. For example a [heat pump](../../../../../heat-pump.md) with [coefficient of performance](../../../../../coefficient-of-performance.md) three divides electricity-related [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) emissions by three per unit useful [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). But grid decarbonization does not automatically outrank every efficiency, methane, land-use or industrial intervention. Marginal abatement cost, cumulative emissions, construction time and available technology determine priorities. A very coal-intensive grid makes electrification alone less effective; a clean grid does not remove aviation or process emissions. The statement is a plausible strategic emphasis, not a deduction valid for every country and timescale.
