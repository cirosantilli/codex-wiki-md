↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Storage has multiple performance measures: [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and power [density](../../../../../density.md), efficiency, cycle/calendar life, duration, cost, materials, safety and siting. Lithium-ion batteries offer high [round-trip efficiency](../../../../../round-trip-efficiency.md), fast response and compactness, making them useful for vehicles and short-duration grid services. That does not make them optimal for every duty. Pumped hydro can suit large stationary [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) stores where geography permits; thermal stores can serve [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) directly; [chemical fuels](../../../../../chemical-fuel.md) or other long-duration systems can trade efficiency for cheap [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) capacity. A technology that is excellent per kilogram may be poor for seasonal storage. “Best” requires a specified service and life-cycle comparison.
