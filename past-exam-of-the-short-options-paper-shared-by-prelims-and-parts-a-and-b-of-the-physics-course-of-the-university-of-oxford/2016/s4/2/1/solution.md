<h1 id="s4/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Neglect upstream [speed](../../../../../speed.md) and hydraulic losses. If $H$ is the upstream free-surface height above the crest, the [specific energy](../../../../../specific-energy.md) at the crest is $H=y+v^2/(2g)$ and flow per unit width is $Q=yv$. At the critical broad-crested control section, extremizing $Q^2=2gy^2(H-y)$ gives $y=2H/3$ and $v^2=gy$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{Q=\sqrt g\left(\frac{2H}{3}\right)^{3/2}.}
$$

Finite approach [speed](../../../../../speed.md), [friction](../../../../../friction.md) and contraction introduce measured [discharge coefficients](../../../../../discharge-coefficient.md); the ideal result assumes a long enough crest for the near-hydrostatic critical-flow approximation.
