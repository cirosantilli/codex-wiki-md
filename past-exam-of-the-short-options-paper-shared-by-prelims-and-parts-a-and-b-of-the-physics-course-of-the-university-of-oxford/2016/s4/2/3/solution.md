<h1 id="s4/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Hydropower can convert a renewable water flux efficiently, has low operating fuel requirements, and reservoir schemes can provide rapid dispatch, [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) and storage. Long plant lifetimes can make capital expenditure worthwhile, while [pumped storage](../../../../../pumped-storage-hydroelectricity.md) shifts [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) rather than producing a new primary resource.

Limitations include finite favorable sites, seasonal drought, sedimentation, [habitat fragmentation](../../../../../habitat-fragmentation.md), blocked fish migration, displaced populations and major civil-engineering/failure risks. Reservoir evaporation and methane emissions can matter, especially in warm flooded landscapes, so life-cycle emissions are not uniformly zero. Run-of-river plants avoid some reservoir impacts but offer less controllable output. A project assessment must compare useful annual [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and firm capacity, ecological and social costs, and the counterfactual electricity system.
