A tokamak confines a hot, approximately toroidal [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) using a strong [toroidal magnetic field](../../../../../toroidal-magnetic-field.md) and a poloidal field associated with [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) current and external coils. Helical field lines and suitable magnetic surfaces reduce rapid transverse escape. D-T fusion releases 17.6 MeV, mostly in an uncharged [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) that escapes magnetic confinement and transfers [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to a surrounding blanket. A [heat exchanger](../../../../../heat-exchanger.md) and thermal power cycle convert some of that [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) into electricity.

Essential systems include the vacuum vessel, toroidal and poloidal magnets, current-drive/transformer and auxiliary heating equipment, fueling and exhaust systems, divertor for concentrated [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and particle removal, neutron-absorbing/breeding blanket and shielding. [Alpha particles](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) retained in the [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) contribute self-heating. Confinement and controlled exhaust are as important as reaching a high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) does not confine the [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) or make material [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) loads vanish.
