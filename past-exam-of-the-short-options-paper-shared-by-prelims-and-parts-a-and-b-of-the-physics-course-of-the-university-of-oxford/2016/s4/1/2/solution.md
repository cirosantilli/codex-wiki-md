<h1 id="s4/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Lithium in the blanket breeds tritium through [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) reactions, notably $^6\mathrm{Li}+n\to{}^4\mathrm{He}+T$. Tritium is not abundant enough in nature to supply a large reactor fleet without breeding. The blanket must also transfer [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and manage [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) damage and losses, and the breeding ratio must exceed consumption after accounting for processing and inventory. Lithium is therefore a fuel-cycle resource rather than the primary charged [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) reactant in the D-T core.
