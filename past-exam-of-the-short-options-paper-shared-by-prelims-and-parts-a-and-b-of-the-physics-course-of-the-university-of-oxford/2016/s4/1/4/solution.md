<h1 id="s4/1/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Mean [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $3k_BT/2=12.9$ keV means $T=8.6$ keV, not 12.9 keV. For fully ionized D-T with equal [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), $p=2n_i k_BT$ gives $n_i\simeq4.90\times10^{19}$ m$^{-3}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{n_i\tau_ET\simeq1.05\times10^{21}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}\,s\,keV},}
$$

only about one third of the stated requirement. The specified [operating point](../../../../../operating-point.md) therefore does not meet that criterion and requires improved confinement/[pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or continuing external heating. If the criterion's $n$ counts all thermal particles instead of [ions](../../../../../ion.md), the product doubles but remains below its given threshold. A precise fusion gain or [duty cycle](../../../../../duty-cycle.md) cannot be inferred from the [triple product](../../../../../triple-product.md) alone.
