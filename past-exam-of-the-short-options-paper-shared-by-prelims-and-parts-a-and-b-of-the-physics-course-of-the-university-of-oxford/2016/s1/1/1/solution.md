↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [simple poles](../../../../../simple-pole.md) occur at $z=n-\alpha$, $n\in\mathbb Z$, since the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the sine there is $\pi(-1)^n$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_{n-\alpha}f=\frac{(-1)^n}{\pi(n-\alpha)^2}.}
$$

There is also a [double pole](../../../../../double-pole.md) at zero, not included in that list because $0<\alpha<1$. Its [residue](../../../../../residue.md), needed for the next [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) calculation, is the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $\csc\pi(z+\alpha)$ at zero, namely $-\pi\csc(\pi\alpha)\cot(\pi\alpha)$.
