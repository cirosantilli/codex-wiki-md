<h1 id="s1/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For real $x\ne-1$, $w=1-2/(x+1)$ is real. Its finite image is the entire [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md) except $w=1$, which is approached only as $x\to\pm\infty$. The point $x=-1$ maps to infinity. On the extended [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md) the extended [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md) maps onto itself, with $z=\infty$ mapping to one.
