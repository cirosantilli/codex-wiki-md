↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) preserves local oriented angles and has a nonzero local [scale factor](../../../../../geometric-scale-factor.md). For an orientation-preserving complex map this is equivalent to [holomorphy](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) with $f'(z)\ne0$ at the point concerned. Infinitesimal figures are rotated and uniformly scaled there, but finite shapes need not be preserved: [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) magnitude and direction can vary across a region. [Antiholomorphic maps](../../../../../antiholomorphic-function.md) preserve unoriented angles while reversing orientation; the convention being used must be stated.
