<h1 id="s1/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For $z=e^{i\theta}$, $w=i\tan(\theta/2)$, which is purely imaginary; $z=-1$ supplies the point at infinity. Conversely, the [inverse map](../../../../../inverse-function.md) is $z=(1+w)/(1-w)$. Requiring $|w|=1$ is equivalent to $|z-1|=|z+1|$, hence $\boxed{\operatorname{Re}z=0}$, including the appropriate infinite point in the extended-plane formulation. The [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) and [imaginary axis](../../../../../imaginary-axis.md) are exchanged, but the [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md) is not.
