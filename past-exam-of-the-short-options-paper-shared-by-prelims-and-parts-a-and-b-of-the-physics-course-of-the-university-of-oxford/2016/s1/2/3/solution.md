<h1 id="s1/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The identity $w-1=-2/(z+1)$ gives $\boxed{|w-1|=2/r}$ on $|z+1|=r$. Thus the image circle has center one and [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $2/r$. Points strictly inside the original circle satisfy $|w-1|>2/r$; the center itself maps to infinity. The interior therefore becomes the exterior together with infinity on the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../riemann-sphere.md), rather than the interior of the image circle. This inversion is a useful warning against assuming conformality preserves global inside/outside assignments.
