<h1 id="s1/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $z=e^{i\theta}$. The [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) becomes

$$
\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{dz}{i[(1-i/2)z^2+3z+(1+i/2)]}.
$$

The roots are $z=(-3\pm2)/(2-i)$, only $z_+=-1/(2-i)$ lying inside. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the quadratic at $z_+$ is two, so the [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is $1/(2i)$. Consequently $\boxed{\int_0^{2\pi}d\theta/(3+2\cos\theta+\sin\theta)=\pi}$. The denominator is positive since $3>\sqrt5$, so no principal-value qualification is required.
