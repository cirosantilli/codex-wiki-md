<h1 id="s3/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The equation is $i\hbar\partial_t\psi=[-\hbar^2\partial_x^2/(2m)+V(x)]\psi$. The generator of [time evolution](../../../../../time-evolution.md) is the sum of kinetic and [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) operators. For a sharp stationary [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), insert $\psi(x,t)=\phi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$ to obtain $[-\hbar^2\phi''/(2m)+V\phi]=E\phi$. The [probability](../../../../../probability.md) in $dx$ is $|\psi|^2dx$, normalized to one; hence a normalized one-dimensional [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) has dimensions length$^{-1/2}$. Its overall phase is unobservable, but [relative phase](../../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md) affects interference.
