<h1 id="s3/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Time evolution](../../../../../time-evolution.md) gives $e^{-iE_0t/\hbar}[\phi_0+e^{-i\omega t}\phi_1]/\sqrt2$. It becomes the opposite relative-sign [superposition](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) when $e^{-i\omega t}=-1$, first at $\boxed{t_{\min}=\pi/\omega}$. The overlap with the initial state is proportional to $1+e^{-i\omega t}$ and vanishes then; [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md) is immaterial.
