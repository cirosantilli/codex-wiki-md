↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Blackbody radiation](../../../../../black-body-radiation.md) defeated classical equipartition because it predicts excessive short-wavelength emission. Quantized oscillator [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) exchange $h\nu$, and ultimately the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) occupation law, gives the [Planck spectrum](../../../../../blackbody-spectrum.md). In the [photoelectric effect](../../../../../photoelectric-effect.md), [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) follows light [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) with a threshold, rather than simply increasing with beam intensity; individual [photons](../../../../../photon.md) supply $h\nu$ and the maximum [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $h\nu-W$. Intensity chiefly changes the event rate at a fixed [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). These explain two distinct failures of a purely continuous classical wave-plus-matter model, while retaining [wave propagation](../../../../../wave-propagation.md) and interference.
