<h1 id="s3/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Approximate [helium](../../../../../helium.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) by $2m_p+2m_n\simeq6.69\times10^{-27}$ kg. Nonrelativistic [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is $p=\sqrt{2mT}$, giving $\boxed{\lambda=h/p\simeq0.203\ \mathrm{nm}}$. A transverse width of one micrometre implies a characteristic [position uncertainty](../../../../../position-uncertainty.md) of that order and a minimum [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) uncertainty of order $\hbar/a\sim10^{-28}$ kg m s$^{-1}$, with factors depending on profile convention. For a uniform interval $\Delta x=a/\sqrt{12}$, but an exactly abrupt rectangular [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) actually has infinite rms [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) spread. Width alone does not uniquely specify both rms uncertainties; a smooth near-minimum-uncertainty beam is needed for a finite order-of-magnitude estimate.
