↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A heated cathode emits [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) into low-pressure atomic vapor, and a variable accelerating [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) gives them [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). A grid and small retarding region precede the collecting anode. Below the first excitation threshold most collisions are elastic; above it, [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) can transfer a definite [excitation energy](../../../../../excitation-energy.md) to [atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) and may then fail to overcome the collector's retarding field. Collector current develops repeated minima as accelerating [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) increases, separated by roughly the same excitation [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md), approximately 4.9 V for mercury. Contact potentials and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) spreads shift or smear the minima without removing their characteristic spacing. A smooth loss mechanism would not produce these repeated fixed-energy thresholds.[1914, accelerated-electron energy losses in mercury](../../../../../uber-zusammenstosse-zwischen-elektronen-und-den-molekulen-des-quecksilberdampfes-und-die-ionisierungsspannung-desselben-by-james-franck-and-gustav-ludwig-hertz.md)
