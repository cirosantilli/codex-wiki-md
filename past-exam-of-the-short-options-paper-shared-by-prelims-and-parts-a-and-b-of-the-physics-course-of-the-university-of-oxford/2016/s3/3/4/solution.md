<h1 id="s3/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The relative [orbital speed](../../../../../orbital-speed.md) is $Ze^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar n)$. Divide the circumference by this [speed](../../../../../speed.md) to find

$$
\boxed{T_n=\frac{4\epsilon_0^2h^3n^3}{Z^2\mu e^4}.}
$$

Positronium has twice the [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) period at the same [principal quantum number](../../../../../principal-quantum-number.md). The symbol $n$ here labels principal levels with $n=1$ the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md); if “nth [excited state](../../../../../excited-state.md)” is read literally as excitation count, replace $n$ by $n+1$.
