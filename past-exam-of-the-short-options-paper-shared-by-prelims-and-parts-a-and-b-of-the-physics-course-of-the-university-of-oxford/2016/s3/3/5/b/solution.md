↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Positronium has $Z=1$ and half the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) reduced-mass [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) scale, so $\boxed{\lambda\simeq243\ \mathrm{nm}}$. The large contrast with lithium is mainly $Z^2$ Coulomb scaling, with an additional factor two from the moving positron's equal [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). It is not explained by a different [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) propagation law.
