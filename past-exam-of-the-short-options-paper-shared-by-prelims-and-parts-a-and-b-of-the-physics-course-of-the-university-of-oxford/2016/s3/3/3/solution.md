<h1 id="s3/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Coulomb centripetal balance and $\mu vr=n\hbar$ give

$$
\boxed{r_n=\frac{m_e}{\mu}\frac{a_0n^2}{Z},\qquad E_n=-\frac{\mu}{m_e}\frac{Z^2\mathrm{Ry}}{n^2}.}
$$

The [radius](../../../../../radius.md) is the electron-nucleus separation. For positronium, $Z=1$ and $\mu=m_e/2$, so $r_n=2a_0n^2$ and $E_n=-\mathrm{Ry}/(2n^2)$. Each constituent's [radius](../../../../../radius.md) about the [center of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) is half the separation; calling both that [radius](../../../../../radius.md) and $r_n$ the [orbital radius](../../../../../orbital-radius.md) without distinction would create another factor-two error.
