<h1 id="s26/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a thin [spherical shell](../../../../../spherical-shell.md), outward [pressure force](../../../../../pressure-force.md) is $-4\pi r^2(dP/dr)dr$ and inward weight is $GM(r)\rho(4\pi r^2dr)/r^2$. Balance gives $\boxed{dP/dr=-GM(r)\rho/r^2}$. This assumes approximately spherical, slowly evolving structure without important rotational or magnetic support.
