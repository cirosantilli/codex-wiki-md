Hot bright stars lie toward the upper left: smaller $B-V$ means bluer/hotter, and smaller numerical $V$ means brighter. The vertical axis is inverted in the original diagram. The plot shows [apparent magnitudes](../../../../../../apparent-magnitude.md), but cluster members at a common distance permit relative [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) comparisons.
