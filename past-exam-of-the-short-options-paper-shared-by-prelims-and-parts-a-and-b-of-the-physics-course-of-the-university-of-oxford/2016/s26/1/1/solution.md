↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Dust condenses in cool stellar outflows and ejecta and is processed and grown in interstellar clouds. It absorbs and scatters starlight, usually more efficiently at blue wavelengths, making a background star fainter and redder. RR Lyrae variables are old horizontal-branch pulsators with approximately calibrated absolute luminosities, so their recognizable [light curves](../../../../../light-curve.md) and standard-candle relation provide distances after metallicity and extinction corrections. They are not exactly identical-luminosity objects in every population.
