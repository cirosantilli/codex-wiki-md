<h1 id="s26/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use an explicit Tully-Fisher-type scaling $L\propto v_{\max}^4$ for comparable spiral populations and photometric band. Relative to the preceding galaxy,

$$
\boxed{L_2\sim10^{10}(100/282)^4L_\odot\sim1.6\times10^8L_\odot.}
$$

The exponent and [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md) are empirical and have scatter; one galaxy alone does not establish the relation. Different [stellar populations](../../../../../stellar-population.md), inclination errors and extinction change the photometric inference.
