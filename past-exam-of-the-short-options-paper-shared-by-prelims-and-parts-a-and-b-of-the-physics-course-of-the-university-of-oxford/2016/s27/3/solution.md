<h1 id="s27/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

[Relativity of simultaneity](../../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md) concerns separated events. Under Einstein-synchronized inertial coordinates, $\Delta t'=\gamma(\Delta t-v\Delta x/c^2)$. Two events with $\Delta t=0$ and $\Delta x\ne0$ are therefore generally nonsimultaneous in a relatively moving frame. The distinction is compatible with invariant coincidence at one event and with invariant temporal order for timelike-separated events. It is [spacelike separation](../../../../spacelike-separation.md) that permits differing coordinate order.

Conventionality enters when defining distant [clock](../../../../clock.md) [synchronization](../../../../einstein-synchronisation.md). A send-and-return signal experiment measures a round-trip duration, but assigning a time to reflection at the distant [clock](../../../../clock.md) requires a rule. Einstein's convention assigns equal outward and return times. Alternative admissible [synchronizations](../../../../einstein-synchronisation.md) can reassign the one-way coordinate [speeds](../../../../speed.md) while preserving the observed two-way timings and local [clock](../../../../clock.md) readings. These alternatives do not permit changing measured [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) at will or making any two causally ordered events operationally interchangeable.

The physical conclusion and convention claim should therefore be separated. No preferred inertial simultaneity relation is selected by the ordinary [Lorentz-invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) structure. Once the standard [synchronization](../../../../einstein-synchronisation.md) is fixed, relativity between [inertial frames](../../../../inertial-frame.md) follows quantitatively. Reparameterizing distant times shows a conventional element in their coordinate representation; it does not eliminate every empirical constraint from relativity. Matter dynamics, causal cones and [clock](../../../../clock.md) transport still restrict what counts as an equivalent description.

A claim that simultaneity is simply unreal overlooks its well-defined local and frame-relative uses. A claim that it is entirely conventional overlooks the invariant difference between causal and [spacelike separation](../../../../spacelike-separation.md). The defensible account distinguishes those uses rather than expecting one slogan to settle them all.[1905, sections 1–3, clock synchronization and coordinate transformation](../../../../on-the-electrodynamics-of-moving-bodies-by-albert-einstein.md)
