<h1 id="s2/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Hydrostatic balance](../../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) is $dP/dr=-GM(r)\rho/r^2$. A free-fall estimate gives $t_{\mathrm{dyn}}\sim\sqrt{R_\odot^3/(GM_\odot)}\simeq1.6\times10^3$ s; a [test particle](../../../../../test-particle.md) falling in a fixed point-mass field adds factor $\pi/(2\sqrt2)$, giving about 30 minutes.

For an ideal nonrelativistic gas with negligible boundary [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), the [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) is $2U+\Omega=0$, so total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\Omega/2$. Since $\Omega=-\int_0^M Gm\,dm/r(m)$ and $r\leq R$, $|\Omega|\geq GM^2/(2R)$. This is a _minimum magnitude_, not a [lower bound](../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) on the negative [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md): unrestricted central concentration can make the latter arbitrarily negative. A uniform-density estimate gives $\Omega=-3GM^2/(5R)$ and available contraction [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $3GM^2/(10R)$. Its release at present solar [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) lasts $\boxed{t\sim10^7\ \mathrm{yr}}$ (order $10^7$–$10^8$ years for alternative structural estimates), much less than a nuclear-burning lifetime.
