↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Using the supplied atomic masses consistently includes the net [electron](../../../../../electron.md) bookkeeping after [positron annihilation](../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md). The released fraction is $\eta=(4m_H-m_{He})/(4m_H)\simeq0.0071$, corresponding to about 26.7 MeV per [helium](../../../../../helium.md) nucleus. If 10% of the [solar mass](../../../../../solar-mass.md) is processed and [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) stays constant, $t\simeq0.1\eta M_\odot c^2/L_\odot\simeq10^{10}$ years.

At separation one femtometre the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) Coulomb [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is 1.44 MeV. At $2\times10^7$ K, mean thermal [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $3k_BT/2\simeq2.59$ keV, making their ratio about $5.6\times10^2$. Classical over-barrier collisions are therefore extremely improbable. [Quantum tunnelling](../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) through the [Coulomb barrier](../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md) and the high-energy distribution tail allow fusion, with the slow weak-interaction step regulating the proton-proton chain. The reaction summary is a net [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md)/matter balance, not one simultaneous four-proton collision.
