↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The first law gives elliptical relative [orbits](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) with the center of [force](../../../../../force-split.md) at a focus; the second gives equal swept areas in equal times, expressing angular-momentum conservation. For circular binary motion, let $r_1+r_2=a$ and $M_1r_1=M_2r_2$. The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) equation $M_1(2\pi/P)^2r_1=GM_1M_2/a^2$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{a^3}{P^2}=\frac{G(M_1+M_2)}{4\pi^2}.}
$$

The corresponding relation for ellipses uses the relative [semimajor axis](../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) rather than instantaneous separation.
