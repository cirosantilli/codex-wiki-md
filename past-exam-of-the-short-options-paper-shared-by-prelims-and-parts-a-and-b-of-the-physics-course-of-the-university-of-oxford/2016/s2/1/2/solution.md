<h1 id="s2/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Eclipses justify an approximately [edge-on orbit](../../../../../edge-on-orbit.md), and equal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) imply equal masses. The relative [speed](../../../../../speed.md) is 200 km s$^{-1}$, so $a=(v_1+v_2)P/(2\pi)\simeq1.50\times10^{10}$ m, or $\boxed{a\simeq0.100\ \mathrm{AU}}$. Substitution in the binary period law gives $M_1+M_2\simeq4.52M_\odot$, hence $\boxed{M_1=M_2\simeq2.26M_\odot}$. Inclination corrections scale the dynamical masses as $\sin^{-3}i$ if exact edge-on viewing is relaxed.
