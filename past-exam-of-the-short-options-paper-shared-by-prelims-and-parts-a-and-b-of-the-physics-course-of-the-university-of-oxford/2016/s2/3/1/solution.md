<h1 id="s2/3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Expansion redshifts [photon](../../../../../photon.md) energies and preserves a [blackbody spectrum](../../../../../blackbody-spectrum.md), so a [comoving observer](../../../../../comoving-observer.md) at $z=2$ sees $T=T_0(1+z)\simeq8.18$ K. A one-sided dipole excess of 0.105% means $v/c=0.00105$, hence $\boxed{v\simeq315\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}}$. Motion is toward the hotter region. The peak-to-peak contrast is twice this, so using 0.210% for $v/c$ would double the inferred [speed](../../../../../speed.md) incorrectly.
