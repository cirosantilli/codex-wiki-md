<h1 id="s2/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Motion relative to an isotropic [radiation field](../../../../../radiation-field.md) produces a dipole: one broad hot hemisphere and an opposite cool hemisphere. To first order $T(\theta)=T_0[1+(v/c)\cos\theta]$, where $\theta=0$ is the forward direction. This is not a narrow Galactic-plane feature and is not the intrinsic small-scale CMB pattern.
