↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The given calibration yields $M_V=-3.5\log_{10}50\simeq-5.946$. The [distance modulus](../../../../../distance-modulus.md) is $31.446$, giving $\boxed{d\simeq19.5\ \mathrm{Mpc}}$ if extinction is negligible. [Hubble's law](../../../../../hubble-s-law.md) predicts $v_H\simeq1400$ km s$^{-1}$, compared with 1150 km s$^{-1}$ observed. The approximately $-250$ km s$^{-1}$ difference can be a line-of-sight [peculiar velocity](../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) of the cluster and observer, together with distance-calibration uncertainties. The [local flow](../../../../../local-flow.md) need not follow a perfectly smooth expansion law.
