↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

After foreground and dipole removal, primordial CMB anisotropies are small hot and cold patches across the sky, with characteristic correlated angular scales and [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) of order $10^{-5}$ of the mean [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). They trace perturbations at [photon decoupling](../../../../../photon-decoupling.md) and subsequent propagation, not simply the current distribution of [Milky Way](../../../../../milky-way.md) dust.
