<h1 id="s12/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For independent equiprobable bases there are $4^3=64$ [codons](../../../../../codon.md), three of them stops, so $p_{\mathrm{stop}}=3/64$. In a fixed [reading frame](../../../../../reading-frame.md), and conditional on starting [translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), surviving at least $L$ successive non-stop [codons](../../../../../codon.md) has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $\boxed{(61/64)^L}$. For 300 it is about $5.6\times10^{-7}$. Strictly “exceeding 300” requires 301 non-stop [codons](../../../../../codon.md), giving $(61/64)^{301}$. A specified [start codon](../../../../../start-codon.md), [codon](../../../../../codon.md) bias, correlations or looking for a long sequence anywhere in a large genome changes the sampling problem and cannot be silently folded into this single-frame [probability](../../../../../probability.md).
