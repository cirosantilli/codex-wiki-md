<h1 id="s12/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The chemical mismatch types are A–C and G–T, with either strand acting as template. In the fixed ring alignment shown, A–C has no correct [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) while G–T retains one. Relative probabilities must specify the _template_: template A loses two bonds versus A–T, giving $e^{-10}$; template G loses two versus G–C, giving $e^{-10}$; template C loses three versus C–G, giving $e^{-15}$; template T loses one versus T–A, giving $e^{-5}$. Equal incoming activities and $5k_BT$ per bond give approximate relative weights $4.5\times10^{-5},4.5\times10^{-5},3.1\times10^{-7},6.7\times10^{-3}$ respectively.

For roughly equal template frequencies the mean error [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is order $1.7\times10^{-3}$, or several million errors across $3\times10^9$ copied [base pairs](../../../../../base-pair.md). Restricting the estimate to [purine](../../../../../purine.md) templates instead gives order $10^5$ errors; neither is an accurate [replication](../../../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md) model. Both greatly exceed the source's order-one surviving [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) input. Polymerase kinetic discrimination, proofreading and mismatch repair suppress errors, and a transient mismatch need not become an inherited [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md). Ring relaxation, stacking, solvent and rare tautomers are missing from this simple hydrogen-bond calculation. Real G–T wobble geometry can form additional bonds. The source's [mutation rate](../../../../../mutation-rate.md) is illustrative, not a universal measured human rate.[Chapter 5, DNA Replication Mechanisms: multiple proofreading mechanisms](../../../../../molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)
