[Natural selection](../../../../../natural-selection.md) requires variation among individuals, heritable contributions to that variation, and differences in [reproductive success](../../../../../reproductive-success.md) associated with it. Given those conditions, variant frequencies can change across generations. Competition and finite resources help make [reproductive success](../../../../../reproductive-success.md) unequal, but selection does not require every interaction to be direct combat. [Mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) and [recombination](../../../../../genetic-recombination.md) supply variation; they do not generally arise because an organism needs a particular adaptation. Drift also changes frequencies, and common ancestry and branching descent connect population changes to the evolution of species. Selection is a mechanism within evolution, not a synonym for every evolutionary change.[Chapters III–IV, struggle for existence and natural selection](../../../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md)
