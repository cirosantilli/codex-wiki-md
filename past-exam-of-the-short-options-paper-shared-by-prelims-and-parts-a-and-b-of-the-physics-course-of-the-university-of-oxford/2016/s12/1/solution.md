↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Nucleic acids](../../../../nucleic-acid.md) are directional sugar-phosphate polymers whose base sequences store information. Complementary antiparallel [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) strands provide templates for [replication](../../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md) and repair, while [base stacking](../../../../base-stacking.md) and pairing stabilize the usual double helix. [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence also specifies recognition sites that regulate when information is used. [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) generally has [ribose](../../../../ribose.md) and [uracil](../../../../uracil.md) and can fold through internal pairing into functional structures: [messenger RNA](../../../../messenger-rna.md) carries coding information, [transfer RNA](../../../../transfer-rna.md) links [codons](../../../../codon.md) with [amino acids](../../../../amino-acid.md), and [ribosomal RNA](../../../../ribosomal-rna.md) contributes catalytic machinery. Not all [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) is a passive messenger.

[Proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) have peptide-linked amino-acid sequences with chemically diverse [side chains](../../../../side-chain.md). Their primary sequence constrains secondary structures, tertiary folding and multi-subunit assembly, producing binding pockets, catalytic sites, structural fibers, channels and [molecular motors](../../../../molecular-motor.md). Function depends on conformational dynamics, chemical context and interactions; a linear sequence is not itself a complete account of an active structure. Folding errors and modifications illustrate the need to connect multiple organizational levels.

The central dogma concerns directional transfer of _sequence information_. [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) can be copied, transcribed into [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), and coding [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) translated into [protein](../../../../protein-split.md). [Reverse transcription](../../../../reverse-transcription.md) is compatible with its careful formulation: RNA-to-DNA transfer does not entail extracting an arbitrary [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)'s amino-acid sequence back into a nucleic-acid template. [RNA replication](../../../../rna-replication.md) also occurs. The simple slogan “[DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) makes [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) makes [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)” is useful but incomplete if taken to prohibit those transfers or to deny [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) catalysis, regulatory feedback or environmental effects on expression.

Amino-acid sequence is encoded through a triplet [genetic code](../../../../genetic-code.md), with tRNAs and [ribosomes](../../../../ribosome.md) mediating [translation](../../../../translation-biology.md) rather than direct chemical affinity between each [codon](../../../../codon.md) and its [amino acid](../../../../amino-acid.md). Complementary copying gives inherited continuity, while [mutation](../../../../mutation.md) and selection allow sequence and functional changes. Thus molecular structure supports both stable heredity and adaptable biochemical machinery without making every cellular process a one-way causal chain.[Chapters 3–6, protein structure, nucleic acids and sequence-information transfer](../../../../molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)
