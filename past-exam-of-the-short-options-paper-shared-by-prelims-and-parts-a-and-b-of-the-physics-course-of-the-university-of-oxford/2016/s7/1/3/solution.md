<h1 id="s7/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The floor contributes [gravitational potential](../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $mgz=m\omega^2(x_1^2+y_1^2)/2$. Neglecting [vertical velocity](../../../../../vertical-velocity.md) as specified, for the nonrotating cup orientation $\phi=-\Omega$ the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{L=\frac m2[(\dot x_1-R\Omega\sin\Omega t)^2+(\dot y_1+R\Omega\cos\Omega t)^2]-\frac{m\omega^2}{2}(x_1^2+y_1^2).}
$$

For general $\alpha=\Omega+\phi$, replace the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components by $\dot x_1-\alpha y_1+R\Omega\sin\phi t$ and $\dot y_1+\alpha x_1+R\Omega\cos\phi t$. The potential remains the same because the paraboloid is axisymmetric.
