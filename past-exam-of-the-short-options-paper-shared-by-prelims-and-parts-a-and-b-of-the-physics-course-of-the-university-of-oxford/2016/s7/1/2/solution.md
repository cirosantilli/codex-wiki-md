<h1 id="s7/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The cup's rotation relative to the radial basis has [angular speed](../../../../../angular-speed.md) $\phi$, so its absolute orientation has [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $\alpha=\Omega+\phi$. With $\mathcal R(\theta)$ the [planar rotation](../../../../../planar-rotation.md) matrix,

$$
\boxed{\binom{x}{y}=R\binom{\cos\Omega t}{\sin\Omega t}+\mathcal R(\alpha t)\binom{x_1}{y_1}.}
$$

At $\phi=-\Omega$, $\alpha=0$: the cup translates on a circle while its axes remain parallel to fixed inertial axes. Thus $x=R\cos\Omega t+x_1$, $y=R\sin\Omega t+y_1$. Cancellation of orientation rotation does not remove [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) of the cup's center.
