<h1 id="s7/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In a frame rotating with [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\Omega$, the [Coriolis force](../../../../../coriolis-force.md) is $\mathbf F_C=-2m\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf v_{\mathrm{rot}}$. In the northern hemisphere horizontal flow is deflected to the right: balanced circulation is counterclockwise about a low-pressure center and clockwise about a high-pressure center. The deflection reverses in the southern hemisphere. Jupiter's counterclockwise southern Great Red Spot is consequently anticyclonic, a high-pressure system. These statements assume a large-scale rotating atmosphere; they do not explain tiny vortices dominated by local [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
