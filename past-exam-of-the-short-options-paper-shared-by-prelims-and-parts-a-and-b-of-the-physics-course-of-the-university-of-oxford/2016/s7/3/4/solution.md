<h1 id="s7/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Lossless [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) focusing can reduce a beam's transverse position width at the expense of [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) spread, but cannot reduce its occupied canonical phase volume. Coupling to the longitudinal direction can redistribute projected emittance without reducing total emittance. True cooling requires exporting [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) to additional [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) or discarding particles: stochastic feedback, [electron cooling](../../../../../electron-cooling.md) or radiative damping coupled to an external environment provide possible mechanisms, with very different efficiency for high-energy [protons](../../../../../proton.md). Collimation shrinks the accepted distribution at the cost of intensity. [Acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) also reduces angular divergence at fixed [normalized emittance](../../../../../normalized-emittance.md), an apparent geometrical cooling rather than a violation of [Liouville's theorem](../../../../../liouville-theorem.md). The apparatus and radiation cannot be omitted from the accounting when the bunch is no longer an isolated [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) subsystem.
