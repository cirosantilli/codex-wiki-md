↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) combines all independent [canonical coordinates](../../../../../canonical-variables.md) and [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md). Its [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) has divergence $\sum_i[\partial_{q_i}H_{p_i}-\partial_{p_i}H_{q_i}]=0$ by equality of mixed [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md). Thus an infinitesimal material phase volume has zero fractional rate of change. This is [Liouville's theorem](../../../../../liouville-theorem.md), valid also for explicitly time-dependent [Hamiltonians](../../../../../hamiltonian.md). It preserves fine-grained volume, not necessarily the area of any projected coordinate pair.
