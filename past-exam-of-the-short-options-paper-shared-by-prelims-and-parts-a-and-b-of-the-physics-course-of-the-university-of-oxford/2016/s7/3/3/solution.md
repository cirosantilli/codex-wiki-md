↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The oscillator flow is

$$
\binom{x(t)}{p(t)}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\omega t&\sin\omega t/(m\omega)\\-m\omega\sin\omega t&\cos\omega t\end{pmatrix}\binom{x(0)}{p(0)}.
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $\cos^2\omega t+\sin^2\omega t=1$, explicitly preserving area. The [orbits](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) are [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) ellipses; stretching one coordinate during transport is compensated by the conjugate direction.
