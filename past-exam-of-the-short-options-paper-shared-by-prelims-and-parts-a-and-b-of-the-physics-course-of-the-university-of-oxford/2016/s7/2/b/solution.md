When $m_1=m_2$, the cosine becomes one and the previously driven $y$ relative subsystem becomes autonomous. Its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is one new generic independent time-independent [integral](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md); equivalently total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) becomes conserved because the remaining subsystem energies already were. Making $m_3$ equal as well introduces **no further generic [continuous symmetry](../../../../../continuous-symmetry.md)** in the printed model. The arbitrary $U_{13}$ and $V_{12}$ distinguish particle labels and directions, so equal masses alone do not create a permutation or physical-rotation invariance.

A finite “number of conserved quantities” must mean independent generators/[integrals](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md): arbitrary functions of any [integral](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) give infinitely many. Mass-weighted rotations and free-coordinate symmetries identified in part a already exist at unequal masses. Thus the justified generic increment is one at $m_1=m_2$, and zero further at complete equality. Special potentials or $\lambda=0$ can change the count, but were not stipulated.
