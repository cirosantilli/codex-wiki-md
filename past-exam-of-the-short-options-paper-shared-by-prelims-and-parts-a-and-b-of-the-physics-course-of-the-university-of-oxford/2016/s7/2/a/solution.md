<h1 id="s7/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The separability of the printed [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is important. Every $x_i$ is an independent oscillator of the same [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), irrespective of [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Define $X_i=\sqrt{m_i}x_i$, $P_i=p_{x_i}/\sqrt{m_i}$. Then the complete constant matrix $Q_{ij}=P_iP_j+\omega^2X_iX_j$, and angular quantities $J_{ij}=X_iP_j-X_jP_i$, include each oscillator [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and the rotational symmetries of this mass-weighted oscillator space. Alternatively its explicitly time-dependent constants are $X_i\cos\omega t-P_i\sin\omega t/\omega$ and $X_i\sin\omega t+P_i\cos\omega t/\omega$.

[Translations](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) in all $y_i$ give conserved $P_y=\sum p_{y_i}$. [Translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) of $z_1,z_2$ together gives $P_{z12}=p_{z_1}+p_{z_2}$; $z_3$ is free and gives $p_{z_3}$. Their center-of-mass positions minus the associated constant velocities times $t$ are boost constants. The decoupled relative $z_1-z_2$ motion conserves $E_{z,\mathrm{rel}}=(p_{z_1}/m_1-p_{z_2}/m_2)^2\mu_{12}/2+V(z_1-z_2)$, where $\mu_{12}=m_1m_2/(m_1+m_2)$. In mass-weighted versions, the three free center coordinates also have conserved mutual [angular momenta](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). These families are not all [functionally independent](../../../../../functionally-independent-functions.md).

The $y$ relative subsystem alone is driven through $\cos[\lambda(m_1-m_2)t]$. For generic nonzero $\lambda(m_1-m_2)$ its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), and hence the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), is not conserved. Omitting the [mass-weighted oscillator symmetries](../../../../../mass-weighted-oscillator-symmetries.md) simply because the physical masses differ would miss genuine symmetries of this particular model.
