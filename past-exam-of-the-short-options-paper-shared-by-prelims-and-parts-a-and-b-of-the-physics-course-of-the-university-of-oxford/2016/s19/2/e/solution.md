<h1 id="s19/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the same lattice radiation-integral ratio, natural [geometric emittance](../../../../../geometric-emittance.md) scales as $\gamma^2$. A factor-ten decrease therefore requires $\boxed{E'=3/\sqrt{10}\simeq0.949\ \mathrm{GeV}}$. Keeping coupling ratio fixed reduces the [vertical emittance](../../../../../vertical-emittance.md) by the same factor, within that ideal scaling model.
