<h1 id="s19/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Emittance reduction has little effect when radiation dominates every source width and divergence. At fixed $\beta=L=2$ m, the strongest condition is $\lambda L/(16\pi^2)\gg\epsilon_x\beta$, or $\boxed{\lambda\gg4.7\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm m}$. The divergence condition only requires $\lambda\gg\epsilon_xL/\beta=3$ nm. There is no sharp “negligible” cutoff without an allowed percentage. If the [optics](../../../../../optics-split.md) were reoptimized to match diffraction instead, the weaker comparison $\lambda/(4\pi)\gg\epsilon_x$ would set the relevant scale; it is not the same fixed-beta problem.
