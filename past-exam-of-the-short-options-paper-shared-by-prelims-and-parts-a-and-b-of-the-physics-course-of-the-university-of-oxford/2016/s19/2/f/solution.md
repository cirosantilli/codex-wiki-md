<h1 id="s19/2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Lower [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) changes the source [spectrum](../../../../../frequency-spectrum.md) as well as emittance. At fixed bending geometry, bending-radiation critical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scales as $\gamma^3$, so it falls by $10^{3/2}$; for fixed undulator period and deflection parameter the fundamental wavelength grows as $\gamma^{-2}$, by ten. A smaller [electron](../../../../../electron.md) emittance therefore does not automatically improve brightness at the original 10 nm wavelength: the device may no longer radiate there on its fundamental, flux and [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) operation change, and diffraction remains. Lattice redesign can retain beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and target [spectrum](../../../../../frequency-spectrum.md) while reducing emittance, which is why [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reduction alone is not a generally satisfactory substitute.
