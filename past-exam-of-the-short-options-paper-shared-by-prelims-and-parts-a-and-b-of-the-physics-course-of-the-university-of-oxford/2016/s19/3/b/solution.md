↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The required [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) increment is 9.9 TeV. If the usable longitudinal [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $\eta E_0$, with $E_0=m_ec\omega_p/e$, the channel length is

$$
\boxed{L_p=\frac{9.9\ \mathrm{TeV}}{\eta m_ec\omega_p}.}
$$

Under the _illustrative_ preceding $\sigma_{zf}=20\ \mu$m and $k_p\sigma_{zf}=1$ choices, $E_0\simeq25.6$ GV m$^{-1}$ and $L_p\simeq390/\eta$ m. **TODO — a unique numerical length inherits the missing compression/[density](../../../../../density.md) specification and additionally requires a usable wake [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md)/loading factor $\eta$ or driver information.** A driver [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per [proton](../../../../../proton.md) alone does not fix its bunch population or accelerating [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md).
