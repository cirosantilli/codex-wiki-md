<h1 id="s19/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Propagation through an already formed cold [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) requires $\omega_L>\omega_p$, hence $\boxed{\lambda_L<\lambda_p=2\pi c/\omega_p}$. For the illustrative [density](../../../../../density.md) above, that is a cutoff near 126 micrometres. It is a propagation ceiling, not a sufficient condition for producing ionization.
