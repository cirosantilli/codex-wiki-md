<h1 id="s29/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [point mass](../../../../../point-mass.md) at the rotation center has zero perpendicular distance to the axis, so $\boxed{C=\int r_\perp^2dm=0}$. More generally, moving a fixed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) inward lowers its moment even if its total gravitational monopole and outer [radius](../../../../../radius.md) remain fixed.
