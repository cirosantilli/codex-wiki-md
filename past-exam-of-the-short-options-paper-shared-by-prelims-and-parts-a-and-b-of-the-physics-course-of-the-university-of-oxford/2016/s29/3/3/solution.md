↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

All three measured factors are below the uniform-sphere value 0.4, indicating central concentration. Earth at 0.331 is consistent with a dense core and less dense mantle; Jupiter at 0.264 and Saturn at 0.21 have still more centrally concentrated normalized distributions, with Saturn especially concentrated relative to its outer [radius](../../../../../radius.md). These numbers do not uniquely determine core masses, chemical boundaries or [density profiles](../../../../../density-profile.md).
