<h1 id="s29/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a thin uniform [spherical shell](../../../../../spherical-shell.md), $dC=(R\sin\theta)^2dm$ and angular averaging gives $\langle\sin^2\theta\rangle=2/3$. Thus $\boxed{C=2MR^2/3}$. The printed formula requires a negligibly thin outer shell. For a _finite-thickness_ hollow sphere of [uniform density](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) between $r_0$ and $R$,

$$
C=\frac25M\frac{R^5-r_0^5}{R^3-r_0^3}.
$$

For example $r_0=R/2$ gives $C/(MR^2)=31/70$, not $2/3$. The thin-shell limit $r_0\to R$ restores the printed result. A uniform outer layer of unspecified thickness does not by itself establish it.
