<h1 id="s29/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The formula assumes [spherical geometry](../../../../../spherical-geometry.md), an effective uniform emission [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), steady balance, specified bolometric emissivity, no important internal heating and appropriate redistribution or averaging. An atmosphere is not mathematically required: for any surface-temperature pattern the same global balance holds with $T_{eq}^4=\langle T^4\rangle$ if emissivity is uniform. Without redistribution on an ideal airless surface, local dayside [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) instead gives $T(\zeta)=[F_S(1-A)\cos\zeta/(\epsilon\sigma R_{AU}^2)]^{1/4}$ and the instantaneous night side would approach zero in the no-storage limit. Atmospheric absence alone does not justify replacing the factor four by one for a _global_ [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
