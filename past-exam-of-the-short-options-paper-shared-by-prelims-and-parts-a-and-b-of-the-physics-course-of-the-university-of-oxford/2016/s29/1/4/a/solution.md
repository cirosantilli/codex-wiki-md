<h1 id="s29/1/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The Moon's mean equatorial [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) near 206 K is well below the approximately 270 K global effective radiative [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). The surface has a large day-night range and finite [thermal inertia](../../../../../../thermal-inertia.md); $\langle T^4\rangle^{1/4}$ exceeds $\langle T\rangle$, so comparing those two differently averaged quantities as though they were equal is invalid. The discrepancy need not imply a missing atmospheric [greenhouse effect](../../../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) on an airless Moon.
