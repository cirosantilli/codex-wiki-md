↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Jupiter emits at about 130 K rather than the solar-only estimate around 111 K. Intrinsic [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) contributes additional outgoing flux, so $\sigma T_{\mathrm{emit}}^4=F_{\mathrm{absorbed}}+F_{\mathrm{internal}}$. The difference of [fourth powers](../../../../../../fourth-power.md) is the relevant [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) excess, not their direct [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference. Cooling and contraction of the interior provide the main physical motivation for including this extra term.
