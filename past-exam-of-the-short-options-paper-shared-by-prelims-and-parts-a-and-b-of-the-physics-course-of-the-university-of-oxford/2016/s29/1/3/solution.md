↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For $F_S=1360$ W m$^{-2}$ and emissivity one, the estimates are

$$
\boxed{T_{eq,\oplus}\simeq254\ \mathrm K,\quad T_{eq,\mathrm{Moon}}\simeq270\ \mathrm K,\quad T_{eq,\mathrm{Io}}\simeq99\ \mathrm K,\quad T_{eq,\mathrm{Jupiter}}\simeq111\ \mathrm K.}
$$

These are effective fourth-power averages set by absorbed sunlight, not automatic surface [thermometer](../../../../../thermometer.md) readings. Uncertainty in actual albedo, emissivity and redistribution changes a more detailed model.
