<h1 id="s29/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Radiative equilibrium](../../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) balances absorbed solar power against emitted thermal power, with no net [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) accumulation. A spherical body's [projected area](../../../../../projected-area.md) absorbs $\pi r^2F_S(1-A)/R_{AU}^2$; its full area emits $4\pi r^2\epsilon\sigma T^4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T_{eq}=\left[\frac{F_S(1-A)}{4\epsilon\sigma R_{AU}^2}\right]^{1/4}.}
$$

Here $A$ is bolometric [Bond albedo](../../../../../bond-albedo.md), not a [geometric albedo](../../../../../geometric-albedo.md) measured at one phase or wavelength. The [radius](../../../../../radius.md) cancels because the absorption and emission areas scale together.
