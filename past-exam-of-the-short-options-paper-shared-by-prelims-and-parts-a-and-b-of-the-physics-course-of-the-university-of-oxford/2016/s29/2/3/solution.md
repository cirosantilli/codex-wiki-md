<h1 id="s29/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In a gravity-dominated excavation model, displaced [mass](../../../../../mass.md) scales as $\rho D^3$ and lifting it through a distance of order $D$ costs order $\rho gD^4$. If a comparable fraction of impact [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) supplies this [work](../../../../../work.md) across similar impacts, $\boxed{D\propto E_{\mathrm{impact}}^{1/4}}$. This is a scaling justification, not a universal crater law: strength-dominated small craters or varying coupling efficiencies can have different [exponents](../../../../../exponent.md).
