↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Impact cratering begins with contact and compression, in which a shock rapidly [heats](../../../../../heat-split.md) and compresses target and impactor. During excavation, material moves outward and upward, producing a transient cavity and ejecta. Modification then involves [gravitational collapse](../../../../../gravitational-collapse.md), wall slumping, fallback and, for large structures, rebound and central features. The stages overlap in time; crater size and morphology depend on gravity, impact conditions and target strength, not just the projectile's geometric hole size.
