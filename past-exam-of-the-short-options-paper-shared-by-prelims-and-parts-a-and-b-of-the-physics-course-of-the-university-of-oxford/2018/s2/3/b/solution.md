<h1 id="s2/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For conserved nonrelativistic matter, $\rho_M=\rho_{M0}a^{-3}$, where $a=R/R_0$ and $a_0=1$. Substitution into the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives

$$
H^2=H_0^2\left(\Omega_Ma^{-3}+\Omega_ka^{-2}+\Omega_\Lambda\right),
$$

with

$$
\boxed{\Omega_M=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{M0}}{3H_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_k=-\frac{kc^2}{H_0^2R_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda}{3H_0^2}.}
$$

The source writes the dimensionless normalized factor as $R$ in its final expression; this is equivalent to setting $R_0=1$ consistently in the curvature coefficient. Evaluating today gives $\Omega_M+\Omega_k+\Omega_\Lambda=1$. Here the printed $\Lambda$ already has dimensions of inverse [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) squared. If the [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) is instead defined geometrically with inverse-length-squared units, replace this $\Lambda$ by $\Lambda_{\rm geom}c^2$.
