<h1 id="s2/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If radiation dominates the [central pressure](../../../../../central-pressure.md), equate $P_c\sim GM_*^2/R_*^4$ to $aT_c^4/3$. This gives

$$
\boxed{R_*\sim\left(\frac{3G}{a}\right)^{1/4}\frac{M_*^{1/2}}{T_c}.}
$$

At roughly fixed burning [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the corresponding mass-radius scaling is $R_*\propto M_*^{1/2}$, rather than the gas-pressure estimate $R_*\propto M_*$. [Radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) alone does not determine a unique [radius](../../../../../radius.md) at a specified [mass](../../../../../mass.md): a thermal or nuclear-burning condition is still needed to specify $T_c$. The relation is a homology estimate, not a universal exact law for all massive [stars](../../../../../star.md).
