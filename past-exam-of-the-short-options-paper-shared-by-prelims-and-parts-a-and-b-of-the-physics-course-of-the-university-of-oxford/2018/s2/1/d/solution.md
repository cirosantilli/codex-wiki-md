↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The numbered features in the original [colour-magnitude diagram](../../../../../colour-magnitude-diagram.md) have the following interpretation.

- **1:** the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md), where core [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) fusion supplies the [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md); [stars](../../../../../star.md) span a range of [masses](../../../../../mass.md) and [effective temperatures](../../../../../effective-temperature.md).
- **2:** the [subgiant](../../../../../subgiant.md) region, following core [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) exhaustion, with a contracting helium-rich core and increasing hydrogen-shell burning.
- **3:** the [red giant branch](../../../../../red-giant-branch.md), with an extended cool envelope and a compact [helium](../../../../../helium.md) core fed by hydrogen-shell burning.
- **4:** the roughly horizontal core-helium-burning branch, or its red-clump part, reached after [helium](../../../../../helium.md) ignition; hydrogen-shell burning can continue outside the core.
- **5:** the white-dwarf cooling sequence, consisting of compact remnants supported mainly by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) and radiating stored [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md).

Continuous [star](../../../../../star.md) formation explains the coexistence of newly formed massive main-sequence [stars](../../../../../star.md) with evolved low-mass remnants; interpreting a single sharp turnoff as the age of a coeval cluster would be inappropriate here.
