<h1 id="s2/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [distance modulus](../../../../../distance-modulus.md) at $100\,\mathrm{pc}$ is five magnitudes. Thus the two approximate main-sequence endpoints read from the original figure, $m_V\simeq0,20$, correspond to $M_V\simeq-5,15$. With the given solar reference,

$$
\frac{L_V}{L_{V,\odot}}=10^{-0.4(M_V-5)}\simeq10^4,10^{-4}.
$$

Use the figure's approximate endpoint [effective temperatures](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) $25{,}000$ K and $2500$ K. If $V$-band [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) is used as a rough bolometric proxy, the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives

$$
\frac{R_*}{R_\odot}\simeq
\left(\frac L{L_\odot}\right)^{1/2}\left(\frac{T_\odot}{T_{\rm eff}}\right)^2.
$$

Taking $T_\odot\simeq5800$ K gives

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm small}\simeq0.054R_\odot,\qquad
R_{\rm large}\simeq5.4R_\odot.}
$$

These are schematic estimates, not precision [radii](../../../../../radius.md). The diagram supplies $V$ magnitudes rather than bolometric [luminosities](../../../../../luminosity.md), and hot and cool endpoints need different [bolometric corrections](../../../../../bolometric-correction.md). The exact range cannot be recovered without those corrections; the quoted values state the intended approximation explicitly.
