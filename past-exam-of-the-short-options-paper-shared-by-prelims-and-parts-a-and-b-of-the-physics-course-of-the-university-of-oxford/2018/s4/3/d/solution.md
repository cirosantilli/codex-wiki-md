A [battery](../../../../../electric-battery.md) stores electrochemical free [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Representative pack specific [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is of order $0.1$-$0.25$ kWh/kg, with electrical [round-trip efficiency](../../../../../round-trip-efficiency.md) roughly $85$-$95\%$. It responds rapidly and can be modular, but [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) capacity requires more active material, and ageing depends on [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md), [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and cycling.

[Pumped storage](../../../../../pumped-storage-hydroelectricity.md) stores [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../gravitational-energy.md). At a $400$ m head with $89\%$ generation efficiency, useful [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\eta gh\simeq0.97$ Wh/kg of water. Its low mass-specific [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is acceptable when inexpensive water and large reservoirs are available. Storage [volume](../../../../../volume.md) and generator rating can be chosen substantially independently, making multi-hour service practical, but suitable sites and civil works are major constraints.

[Hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) stores chemical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), with a lower heating value around $33$ kWh/kg of [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) before allowing for tank [mass](../../../../../mass.md). [Electrolysis](../../../../../electrolysis.md), compression or other storage processing, and conversion back to electricity can reduce electrical [round-trip efficiency](../../../../../round-trip-efficiency.md) to roughly $25$-$45\%$, depending on the route. Separate tanks or caverns can make long-duration [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) capacity less expensive than adding electrochemical cells, but containment, infrastructure, leakage and conversion equipment matter.

The appropriate comparison includes [power](../../../../../power.md) rating, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) duration, cycle count, system [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or area, and whether [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) or electricity is the final useful product. A large fuel heating value does not imply a high electricity-storage efficiency.
