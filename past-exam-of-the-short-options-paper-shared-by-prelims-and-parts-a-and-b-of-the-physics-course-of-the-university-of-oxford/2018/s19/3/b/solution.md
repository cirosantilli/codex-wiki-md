<h1 id="s19/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The target collision [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) requires $175$ GeV per lepton beam. Its initial [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) after the half-linac is $62.5$ GeV, so each lepton must gain $112.5$ GeV. Define the [transformer ratio](../../../../../transformer-ratio.md) as accelerating field experienced by the witness divided by decelerating field experienced by the driver. If the useful stage [length](../../../../../length.md) exhausts the driver's $62.5$ GeV while delivering the required gain, then

$$
\boxed{k_T=\frac{112.5}{62.5}=1.8.}
$$

This matches depletion to [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) and avoids leaving unused driver [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) at the witness target. It is an ideal energy-transfer estimate, assuming suitable loading and longitudinal field profiles; a field ratio alone does not specify a practical wake or its efficiency.
