<h1 id="s19/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the bunch tail have the higher [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) so that it catches the head. For an ultrarelativistic [proton](../../../../../proton.md), $\delta v/v\simeq(\delta E/E)/(\beta^2\gamma^2)$. A drift of [length](../../../../../length.md) $S$ then changes the bunch [length](../../../../../length.md) by approximately $S\delta v/v$. Requiring that to match the initial [length](../../../../../length.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta E}{E}\simeq\beta^2\gamma^2\frac{\ell_p}{S_{\rm prop}}.}
$$

Taking $\gamma\simeq62.5/0.938\simeq66.6$, $\beta\simeq1$, and the given [lengths](../../../../../length.md) yields $\Delta E/E\simeq0.023$, about $2.3\%$. Treating $62.5$ GeV as kinetic rather than total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) changes that by only a few percent.

This is the linear ballistic compression requirement; a real finite-emittance bunch, nonlinear velocity-energy relation, uncorrelated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) spread and collective fields prevent exact zero [length](../../../../../length.md). The chirp is a correlated full head-to-tail spread, not automatically an rms [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) spread.
