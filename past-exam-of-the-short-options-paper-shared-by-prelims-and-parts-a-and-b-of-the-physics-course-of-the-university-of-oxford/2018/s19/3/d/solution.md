<h1 id="s19/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The mean witness gradient must be $112.5\,\mathrm{GeV}/1.1\,\mathrm m\simeq102\,\mathrm{GV\,m^{-1}}$. To turn this into a [density](../../../../../density.md) estimate, assume an accelerating wake amplitude of order the cold [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) wave-breaking scale,

$$
E_{\rm w}\sim\frac{m_ec\omega_p}{e},\qquad
\omega_p^2=\frac{n_0e^2}{\varepsilon_0m_e}.
$$

Eliminating $\omega_p$ gives

$$
\boxed{n_0\sim\frac{\varepsilon_0E_{\rm w}^2}{m_ec^2}
\simeq1.1\times10^{24}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}
\simeq1.1\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}.}
$$

The associated [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) wavelength is about $31$ micrometres, illustrating why a short driver structure is required. The given [length](../../../../../length.md) and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) gain do not uniquely fix [density](../../../../../density.md) without an assumption about wake amplitude relative to the [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) scale. Driver shape, beam loading, relativistic wave-breaking corrections and matching to a [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) witness are substantive additional issues; the estimate is a scale calculation, not a completed accelerator design.
