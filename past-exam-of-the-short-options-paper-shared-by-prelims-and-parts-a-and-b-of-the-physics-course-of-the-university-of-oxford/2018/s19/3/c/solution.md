<h1 id="s19/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For ideal transfer of the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) bunch's [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) to the witness bunch,

$$
N_p(62.5\,\mathrm{GeV})=N_e(112.5\,\mathrm{GeV}).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{N_p/N_e=1.8.}
$$

This is a minimum driver population for unit efficiency and full depletion under the stated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget. If only a fraction $\eta$ is usefully transferred, the required ratio becomes $1.8/\eta$. The equality to the [transformer ratio](../../../../../transformer-ratio.md) uses the same ideal matching assumption as part [b](../b.md).
