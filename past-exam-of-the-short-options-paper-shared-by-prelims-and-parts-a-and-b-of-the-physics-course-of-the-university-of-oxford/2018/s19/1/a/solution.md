<h1 id="s19/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For geometrically similar FODO optics at fixed beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and damping partition, the natural [horizontal emittance](../../../../../horizontal-emittance.md) scales approximately as the cube of the bending angle per cell, $\varepsilon_{x0}\propto\gamma^2\theta_b^3$. This follows from the radiation-integral expression $\varepsilon_{x0}=C_q\gamma^2I_5/(J_xI_2)$: at fixed optical [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) advance the dispersion-invariant scaling makes the ratio proportional to the cube of the cell bend.

Doubling the number of cells around a fixed circumference halves the bending angle per cell, giving

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_{x0,\rm new}/\varepsilon_{x0,\rm old}\simeq1/8.}
$$

This estimate assumes the redesigned magnets actually preserve the relevant dimensionless optical structure, the same [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and circumference, and no dominant insertion-device emittance contribution. Merely counting twice as many quadrupoles without specifying the lattice would not guarantee the cubic improvement.
