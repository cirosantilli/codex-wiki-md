<h1 id="s19/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Far from the [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) limit, and with negligible dispersion contribution, the source phase-space area scales as $\varepsilon_x\varepsilon_y$. At fixed photon flux, source-point optical functions and spectral conditions, brightness is inversely proportional to that product. The leading estimates therefore give

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathcal B_{\rm new}}{\mathcal B_{\rm old}}\simeq8\times40=320.}
$$

Using the coupling-partition refinement in part [b](../b.md) consistently gives about $275$ instead. Both estimates assume comparable current, photon flux and insertion devices. They would cease to apply once photon [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) emittance or other source-size contributions become important; the question specifically excludes that regime.
