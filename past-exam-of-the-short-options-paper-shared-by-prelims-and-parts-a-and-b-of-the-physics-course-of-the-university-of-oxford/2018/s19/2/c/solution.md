<h1 id="s19/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Express [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) as energies $\Theta=k_BT$. A longitudinal cathode-energy spread of order $\Theta_c$ remains of that order during an ideal common electrostatic [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md). At final mean [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $v_0$, a small [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) spread produces $\delta v_\parallel\simeq\delta E/(m_ev_0)$. In the accompanying beam frame, the corresponding random kinetic-energy scale is

$$
\Theta_\parallel\sim\frac{m_e\delta v_\parallel^2}{2}
\sim\frac{\Theta_c^2}{2m_ev_0^2}
=\boxed{\frac{\Theta_c^2}{4E_e}}.
$$

The last equality uses the nonrelativistic [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_e=m_ev_0^2/2$. This is the approximate [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) convention supplied in the hint; exact numerical factors depend on the emitted distribution and the definition of spread. At $50$ keV, relativistic corrections are modest but not strictly zero.

Electrostatic [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) narrows longitudinal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) spread relative to the fast mean beam even though it does not remove each [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s transverse cathode motion. In ideal magnetized cooling, slow [proton](../../../../../proton.md) motion couples to the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) guiding-centre dynamics, making this very small longitudinal spread relevant to the attainable [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Practical voltage ripple, space charge, alignment errors and [ion](../../../../../ion.md) heating can set much larger limits.
