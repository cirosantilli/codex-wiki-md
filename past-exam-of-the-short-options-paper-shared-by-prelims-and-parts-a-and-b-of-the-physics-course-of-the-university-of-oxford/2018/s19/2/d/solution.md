<h1 id="s19/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The stated conversion gives $\Theta_c\simeq0.10$ eV. Hence the ideal longitudinal bath [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Theta_\parallel\simeq\frac{(0.10\,\mathrm{eV})^2}{4(5.0\times10^4\,\mathrm{eV})}
=5.0\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad
T_{p,\rm eq}\sim5.8\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This estimate equates the cooled [proton](../../../../../proton.md) thermal-energy scale with the effective [electron](../../../../../electron.md) bath scale, not the two particles' random [speeds](../../../../../speed.md). The [proton](../../../../../proton.md) root-mean-square [speed](../../../../../speed.md) for a one-component convention $m_p\sigma_v^2=k_BT$ would be about $2.2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

The result is an ideal floor under the hinted cooling model. It is not a promise of a sub-millikelvin stored beam in a real ring, where noise, beam self-fields, intrabeam scattering and other [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) sources enter the balance.
