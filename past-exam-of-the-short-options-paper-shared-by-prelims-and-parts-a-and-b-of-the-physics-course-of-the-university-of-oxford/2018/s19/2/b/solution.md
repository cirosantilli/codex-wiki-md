↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Regenerative braking in an electric vehicle converts part of its mechanical [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) back into electrical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) stored in a [battery](../../../../../electric-battery.md), rather than dissipating it all in friction brakes. An industrial recuperative [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) exchanger transfers [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from a hot exhaust stream to incoming process fluid, reducing the required new [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) input.

Both recover [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) that would otherwise be rejected, while remaining subject to conversion losses and limits on the usefulness of the recovered form. Neither is a source of net [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) over a complete cycle.
