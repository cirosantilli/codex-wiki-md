<h1 id="s19/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A longitudinal accelerating [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) changes longitudinal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), but exerts no direct transverse [force](../../../../../force-split.md). With a constant guiding [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and adiabatic transport, the transverse gyro-energy is consequently retained from the cathode:

$$
\boxed{\Theta_\perp\simeq\Theta_c.}
$$

More generally the [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) of the orbit gives $\Theta_\perp/B\simeq\text{constant}$, so the equality assumes the same relevant field strength, not merely that a field is present all along the line.

In the strong-magnetization regime, many [electron](../../../../../electron.md) gyro-orbits occur during an important long-range electron-ion interaction and the gyroradius is small. The [ion](../../../../../ion.md) then interacts predominantly with a [guiding centre](../../../../../guiding-center.md); rapid transverse cyclotron motion does not act like an unmagnetized bath at the full transverse [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). This allows a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) equilibrium scale far below $T_c$ despite the hot transverse gyro-motion.[magnetized-electron enhancement and equilibrium below transverse [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md)](../../../../../theory-of-electron-cooling-by-yaroslav-s-derbenev.md)

That conclusion is conditional on magnetization and collision scales. A finite gyroradius, nonadiabatic fields or transverse beam errors can restore heating and degrade cooling; it is not generally true that transverse [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) can never affect the cooled beam.
