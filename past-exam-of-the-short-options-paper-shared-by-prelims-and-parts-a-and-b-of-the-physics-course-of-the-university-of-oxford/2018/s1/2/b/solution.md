<h1 id="s1/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $z=e^{i\theta}$, so $\sin\theta=(z-z^{-1})/(2i)$ and $d\theta=dz/(iz)$. The [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) becomes

$$
\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{dz}{6z^2+13iz-6}.
$$

The poles are $-2i/3$ and $-3i/2$; only the first is inside the unit circle. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the denominator there is $12(-2i/3)+13i=5i$. The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{13+12\sin\theta}=\frac{2\pi}{5}.}
$$

There are no poles on the contour because the real denominator is at least one.
