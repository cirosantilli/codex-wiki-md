<h1 id="s1/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Integrate $e^{iz}/z$ around an upper semicircle, indenting the origin by a small upper semicircle traversed clockwise. The integrand is holomorphic inside the indented contour. The large arc vanishes by [Jordan's lemma](../../../../../jordan-s-lemma.md), and the small arc contributes $-i\pi$, so

$$
\operatorname{PV}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ix}}x\,dx=i\pi.
$$

Taking imaginary and real parts yields

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx=\pi,\qquad
\operatorname{PV}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\cos x}{x}\,dx=0.}
$$

The first integrand has a removable singularity at zero and its tails converge conditionally by [Dirichlet test for improper integrals](../../../../../dirichlet-test-for-improper-integrals.md). The second [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) needs a [principal value](../../../../../cauchy-principal-value.md) at zero; its zero also follows from oddness with symmetric exclusion. One must not assign an ordinary convergent [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) to its singularity.
