<h1 id="s18/3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For any one of the four anticommuting matrices $A$, choose another $B$. Since $B^{-1}=B$ and $BAB=-A$, cyclic invariance of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}A=\operatorname{Tr}(B^{-1}AB)=\operatorname{Tr}(-A).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}\alpha_i=0,\qquad\operatorname{Tr}\beta=0.}
$$

Equivalently, the paired positive and negative unit [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) cancel. This argument does not depend on a particular matrix representation.
