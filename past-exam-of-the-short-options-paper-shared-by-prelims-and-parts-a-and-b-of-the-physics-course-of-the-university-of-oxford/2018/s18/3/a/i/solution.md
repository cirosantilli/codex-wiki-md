<h1 id="s18/3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a free particle the [Dirac matrices](../../../../../../dirac-matrices.md) are constant and the momenta commute. Squaring the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) cancels all mixed terms by their [anticommutators](../../../../../../anticommutator.md):

$$
H_D^2=c^2\mathbf p^2+m^2c^4.
$$

Applying another [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) to the [Dirac equation](../../../../../../dirac-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac1{c^2}\partial_t^2-\nabla^2+
\frac{m^2c^2}{\hbar^2}\right)\psi_\alpha=0}
$$

for every component. This is the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md). Its componentwise satisfaction is a necessary consequence of the first-order [Dirac equation](../../../../../../dirac-equation.md), not a converse selecting all spinor solutions of the squared equation.
