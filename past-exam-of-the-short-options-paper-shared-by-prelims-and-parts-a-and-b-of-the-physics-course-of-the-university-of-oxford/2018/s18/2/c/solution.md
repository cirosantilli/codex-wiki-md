<h1 id="s18/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Factorizing the projector found in part [b](../b.md) gives the normalized [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md)

$$
\boxed{\varphi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\frac{n\pi(x+a/2)}a,
\quad -a/2<x<a/2,\quad n\ge1,}
$$

with zero continuation outside. Their squared [integrals](../../../../../integral-split.md) are one and different positive integers give orthogonal functions. These are the familiar infinite-square-well states shifted so that the well centre is at zero. Each [eigenfunction](../../../../../eigenfunction.md) has an arbitrary overall constant [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md), which cancels from the projector.
