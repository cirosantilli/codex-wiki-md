<h1 id="s18/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Away from $x=x'$, the free equation is $G''+k^2G=0$. The [outgoing boundary condition](../../../../../outgoing-boundary-condition.md) selects $G=Ae^{ik|x-x'|}$, continuous at its source. Integrating the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) across the source gives the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jump

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left[G'(x'^+,x')-G'(x'^-,x')\right]=1.
$$

The jump for the proposed form is $2ikA$, so $A=im/(k\hbar^2)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{G(x,x')=\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}e^{ik|x-x'|}.}
$$

For $x$ far to either side, the $x'$-dependent [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) is absorbed into the outgoing amplitude. The outgoing prescription is equivalently the boundary value of the [resolvent](../../../../../resolvent-of-an-operator.md) with $E\to E+i0$; changing that prescription would give a different [Green function](../../../../../green-s-function-split.md).
