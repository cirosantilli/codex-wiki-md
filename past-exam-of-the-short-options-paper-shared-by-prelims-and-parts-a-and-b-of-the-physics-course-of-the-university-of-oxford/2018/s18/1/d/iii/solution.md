<h1 id="s18/1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $\nu=m\gamma/\hbar^2$, so $t(k)\simeq k/(k+i\nu)$. Its nontrivial pole is

$$
\boxed{k_*=-i\nu=-\frac{im\gamma}{\hbar^2},\qquad
E_*=-\frac{m\gamma^2}{2\hbar^2}.}
$$

For negative $\gamma$ this is on the positive imaginary $k$ axis, the physical bound-state sheet. For positive $\gamma$ it lies on the negative imaginary axis and is a virtual-state pole reached by [analytic continuation](../../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) through the continuum cut. These different sheets can have the same negative numerical [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md); specifying only $E_*$ would conceal the distinction. As a function of $E$, the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) also introduces the continuum threshold [branch point](../../../../../../branch-point.md) at zero. If $\gamma=0$, numerator and denominator cancel at this order and no pole is predicted by this leading approximation.
