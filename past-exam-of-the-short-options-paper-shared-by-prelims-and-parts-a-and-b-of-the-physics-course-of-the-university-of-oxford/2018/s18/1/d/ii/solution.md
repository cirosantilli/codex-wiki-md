<h1 id="s18/1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Replace the slowly varying interior wave by $C$ and $e^{ik|x-x'|}$ by one when both arguments lie within the range. The [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) equation gives

$$
C=1-\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}\gamma C,\qquad
\gamma=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}U(x)\,dx.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{C=t\simeq\frac1{1+im\gamma/(k\hbar^2)},\qquad r\simeq C-1.}
$$

The integrated strength has dimensions of [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) [times](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) [length](../../../../../../length.md). For $U=-\alpha\delta(x)$, $\gamma=-\alpha$ and this is exactly the result in part [c](../../c.md). For a regular weak potential the approximation sums the important ratio $m\gamma/(k\hbar^2)$ without requiring it to be small, which is why it remains useful beyond the naive first Born expansion at small [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md).
