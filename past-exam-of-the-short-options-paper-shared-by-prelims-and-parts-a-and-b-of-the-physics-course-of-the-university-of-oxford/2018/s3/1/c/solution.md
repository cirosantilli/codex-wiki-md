<h1 id="s3/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $\lambda\simeq d$ with the convention of part [b](../b.md). Then

$$
\boxed{T_e\simeq\frac{h^2}{m_ek_Bd^2}\simeq3.9\times10^5\,\mathrm K.}
$$

Thus ordinary solid-state [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) are far below this rough overlap scale. The [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) need [quantum statistics](../../../../../quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md) and [quantum states](../../../../../quantum-state.md) throughout that regime. The number is an order-of-magnitude wavelength criterion, not an exact [Fermi temperature](../../../../../fermi-temperature.md): [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [density](../../../../../density.md), [electronic band structure](../../../../../electronic-band-structure.md), [effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) and the numerical convention all matter.
