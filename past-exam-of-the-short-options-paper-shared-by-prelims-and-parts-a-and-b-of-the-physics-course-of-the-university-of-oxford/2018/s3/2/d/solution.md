<h1 id="s3/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both first moments vanish, so the [standard deviations](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) follow directly from the second moments:

$$
\boxed{\Delta x\Delta p=\sqrt{\frac{a^2}{7}\frac{5\hbar^2}{2a^2}}
=\hbar\sqrt{\frac5{14}}\simeq0.598\hbar>\frac\hbar2.}
$$

The [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) is satisfied and is not saturated. A [Gaussian wave packet](../../../../../gaussian-wave-packet.md) can saturate the position-momentum bound; this compactly supported polynomial is not [Gaussian](../../../../../normal-distribution.md).
