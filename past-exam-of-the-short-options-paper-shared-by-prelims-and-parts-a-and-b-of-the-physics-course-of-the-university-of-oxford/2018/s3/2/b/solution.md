↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is even, so its first moment vanishes. Its second moment follows by integrating the polynomial:

$$
\langle x^2\rangle=A^2\left[\frac{2a^7}{3}-\frac{4a^7}{5}+\frac{2a^7}{7}\right].
$$

Using the [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md) from part [a](../a.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle=0,\qquad\langle x^2\rangle=\frac{a^2}{7}.}
$$

The [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) in [position](../../../../../position.md) is consequently $a^2/7$.
