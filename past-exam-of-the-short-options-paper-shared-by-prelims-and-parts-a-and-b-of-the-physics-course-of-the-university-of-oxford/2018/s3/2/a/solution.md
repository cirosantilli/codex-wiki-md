<h1 id="s3/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the overall [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) so that $A$ is positive. [Normalization](../../../../../normalization.md) requires

$$
1=|A|^2\int_{-a}^a(a^2-x^2)^2dx
=|A|^2\frac{16a^5}{15}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4a^{5/2}}.}
$$

An arbitrary constant [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) multiplying this answer has no effect on any expectation value.
