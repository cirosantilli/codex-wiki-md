<h1 id="s3/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Inside a rectangular barrier with $E<V$, the stationary [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives

$$
\Psi''=\frac{2m(V-E)}{\hbar^2}\Psi.
$$

Its independent solutions are $e^{\pm\alpha x}$ with

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2m(V-E)}}\hbar.}
$$

For an opaque barrier the amplitude is suppressed by approximately $e^{-\alpha L}$ and the probability by $e^{-2\alpha L}$. Matching contributes a prefactor, so this exponential approximation is not an exact [transmission coefficient](../../../../../transmission-coefficient.md) for a transparent or very thin barrier. For two moving particles the relative-coordinate [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is their [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md).
