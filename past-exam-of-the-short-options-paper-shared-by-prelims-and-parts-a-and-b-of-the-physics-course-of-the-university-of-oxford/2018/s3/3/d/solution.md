↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Two major suppressions are missing from the one-femtometre rectangular estimate. First, the [Coulomb barrier](../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md) extends far beyond nuclear distances and is not rectangular. A semiclassical [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) through it produces the [Gamow factor](../../../../../gamow-factor.md) $\exp(-2\pi\eta)$, with $\eta=\alpha_{\rm fs}\sqrt{\mu c^2/(2E)}$; at the illustrative thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) it is already of order $10^{-11}$. A thermal average additionally weights the high-energy tail and is not the same as evaluating a single thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).

Second, two [protons](../../../../../proton.md) cannot simply form a stable bound diproton. The [proton–proton chain](../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) starts with a weak-interaction conversion producing a [deuteron](../../../../../deuteron.md), a [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) and a [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md). The weak transition probability supplies a further very large suppression. Thus the quoted tiny fusion probability is not a pure one-dimensional barrier transmission probability with universal energy-independent meaning.

The Sun contains of order $10^{57}$ nucleons in a hot dense core and offers enormously many encounters over astronomical [times](../../../../../time-in-physics.md). A very small reaction probability per encounter can therefore supply its [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) while consuming only a small fraction of the fuel per unit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md). The small weak reaction rate is also crucial for the long stellar lifetime; a larger core [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) increases burning and triggers expansion and cooling.
