↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Quantum tunnelling](../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) is transmission through a region where the particle's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is below the classical [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md). The stationary [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) decays within that region but need not vanish at its far boundary, so matching can leave a nonzero transmitted [probability current](../../../../../probability-current.md).

This does not require a particle to borrow [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) temporarily. [Energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is conserved for a time-independent barrier; the classically forbidden region is described by an evanescent wave, with the same stationary [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) as the incident and transmitted waves.
