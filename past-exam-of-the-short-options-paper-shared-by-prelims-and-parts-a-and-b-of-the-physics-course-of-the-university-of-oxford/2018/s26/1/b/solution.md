<h1 id="s26/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) by $4\pi r^3dr$ and integrate from centre to surface. [Integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), neglecting external surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), gives

$$
-3\int P\,dV=-\int_0^M\frac{GM_r}{r}\,dM_r=\Omega.
$$

For an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) with a constant [adiabatic index](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md), $U=\int P\,dV/(\gamma-1)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{3(\gamma-1)U+\Omega=0.}
$$

A fully ionized, nonrelativistic monatomic [gas](../../../../../gas.md) has $\gamma=5/3$, provided [gas](../../../../../gas.md) rather than radiation or degeneracy dominates the thermal equation of state. It then obeys $\boxed{2U+\Omega=0}$ and $E=U+\Omega=-U=\Omega/2$.

Added nuclear [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) initially raises [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and causes expansion. The expansion makes gravitational [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) less negative and can lower the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) of the self-gravitating [gas](../../../../../gas.md), reducing the temperature-sensitive fusion rate. Conversely, a small contraction can raise the core [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and increase burning. This negative feedback explains stable main-sequence burning in an ordinary nondegenerate stellar core; a degenerate core need not respond the same way.

Before [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) ignition, a cloud loses [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by radiation and contracts. Its gravitational [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) decreases; for the ideal-gas virial relation, roughly half the released binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [heats](../../../../../heat-split.md) the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) and half is radiated. Heating continues until [nuclear reactions](../../../../../nuclear-reaction.md) can balance the [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md), explaining the Kelvin-Helmholtz [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) of pre-main-sequence evolution.

The effective adiabatic response varies with [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), molecular dissociation, [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) and degeneracy. Partial [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) can absorb compression [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and lower the local first adiabatic exponent below $4/3$, encouraging compressibility, convection and pulsational instability. A local outer layer with $\gamma\le4/3$ does **not** by itself prove global collapse: ordinary [stars](../../../../../star.md) have [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) layers of that kind. The dynamical stability criterion concerns a suitable pressure-weighted global response and its coupling to the eigenmode; an extended [star](../../../../../star.md) with an effectively global exponent below $4/3$ can be dynamically unstable.
