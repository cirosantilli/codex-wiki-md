<h1 id="s26/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A thin spherical shell of thickness $dr$ has [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $4\pi r^2\rho\,dr$. Balancing its inward gravitational [force](../../../../../force-split.md) with the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) difference across its faces gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dP}{dr}=-\frac{GM_r\rho}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dM_r}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho.}
$$

For a transparent estimate take constant [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho=3M_\odot/(4\pi R_\odot^3)$ and negligible surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). Then $M_r=M_\odot r^3/R_\odot^3$, so integration gives

$$
\boxed{P_c=\frac{3GM_\odot^2}{8\pi R_\odot^4}\simeq1.3\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Pa}.}
$$

Combining with the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) and $\mu\simeq0.6$ yields $T_c\simeq\mu m_HGM_\odot/(2k_BR_\odot)\simeq7\times10^6$ K. The less specific virial scaling gives $T_c\sim\mu m_HGM_\odot/(k_BR_\odot)\sim10^7$ K.

The real Sun is strongly centrally concentrated: a uniform-density model substantially underestimates [central pressure](../../../../../central-pressure.md) and is not a detailed solar model. Its value is a justified scale estimate, while more realistic structure produces [central temperatures](../../../../../central-stellar-temperature.md) of order $1.5\times10^7$ K and [central pressures](../../../../../central-pressure.md) of order $10^{16}$ Pa. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) alone does not uniquely determine both profiles without an equation of state and a [density](../../../../../density.md)/thermal model.
