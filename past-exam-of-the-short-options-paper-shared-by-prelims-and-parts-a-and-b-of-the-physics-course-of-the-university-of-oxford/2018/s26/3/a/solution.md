↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sequence primarily orders [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) rather than chemical composition. Hot O spectra show strong ionized-helium features and highly ionized metals, with relatively weak neutral-hydrogen lines. B [stars](../../../../../star.md) show neutral [helium](../../../../../helium.md) and increasingly prominent [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). A [stars](../../../../../star.md) have the strongest Balmer absorption. Through F and G, lower-ionization metal lines and calcium features become prominent. K [stars](../../../../../star.md) have strong low-ionization metal absorption and cool-star features; M [stars](../../../../../star.md) develop strong molecular bands such as TiO, while Balmer absorption is weak.

Line [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md) depends on how many [atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) occupy both the relevant [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) stage and the particular lower level. Boltzmann excitation controls the latter and Saha [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) the former, so equal elemental abundances need not give equal line strengths. [Electron](../../../../../electron.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), [surface gravity](../../../../../surface-gravity.md), broadening and atmospheric [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gradients also affect the spectrum.

For Balmer absorption the lower state is $n=2$, $10.2$ eV above the [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) ground state. In cool atmospheres its population is exponentially small. At higher [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) it rises, but at still higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) removes neutral [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). The competition gives the maximum in A-type [stars](../../../../../star.md), with [effective temperatures](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) around $9000$-$10{,}000$ K. This is an excitation-and-ionization effect, not evidence that A [stars](../../../../../star.md) have the largest [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) abundance.
