<h1 id="s26/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Core-helium-burning [stars](../../../../../star.md) populate the [horizontal branch](../../../../../horizontal-branch.md) and [red clump](../../../../../red-clump.md); [hot subdwarf](../../../../../hot-subdwarf.md) B [stars](../../../../../star.md) provide examples with a helium-burning core of roughly half a [solar mass](../../../../../solar-mass.md) and a very small remaining [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) envelope. Their high [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) relative to [mass](../../../../../mass.md), hot spectra and locations away from an ordinary hydrogen-burning [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) are observational evidence for such evolved configurations.

A plausible history starts with a hydrogen-burning [star](../../../../../star.md). After central [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) exhaustion, hydrogen-shell burning builds a compact [helium](../../../../../helium.md) core and the envelope expands on the [red giant branch](../../../../../red-giant-branch.md). Sufficient core [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) ignites [helium](../../../../../helium.md), often by a [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md) for a low-mass degenerate core. Strong [mass](../../../../../mass.md) loss or binary interaction can strip most of the [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) envelope, exposing a hot helium-burning remnant; retaining a substantial envelope instead gives an ordinary horizontal-branch or red-clump [star](../../../../../star.md).

The simple radiative Kramers scaling estimates a [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) contrast but does not uniquely identify one evolutionary state or predict a real subdwarf [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) precisely. A similarly low-mass [helium](../../../../../helium.md) [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) would be a cooling, degenerate object rather than the sustained high-temperature helium-burning configuration assumed here.
