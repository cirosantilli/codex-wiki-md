<h1 id="s26/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use characteristic stellar values in the supplied radiative [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gradient: $T_c/R\sim\kappa\rho L/(R^2T_c^3)$. Hence $L\propto T_c^4R/(\kappa\rho)$. With Kramers [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md), $\kappa=\kappa_0\rho T_c^{-3.5}$,

$$
L\propto\kappa_0^{-1}\frac{T_c^{7.5}R}{\rho^2}
\propto\kappa_0^{-1}\frac{T_c^{7.5}R^7}{M_s^2}.
$$

Hydrostatic support by an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) gives $T_c\propto\mu M_s/R$, or $R\propto\mu M_s/T_c$. Substitution then yields

$$
\boxed{L\propto\kappa_0^{-1}\mu^7M_s^5T_c^{1/2}.}
$$

The result assumes homologous [density](../../../../../density.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) profiles, comparable structural constants, gas-pressure support and radiative transport throughout. It is not simultaneously an electron-scattering-opacity result; replacing Kramers [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md) changes the exponents. Convection, degenerate cores or a different [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md) regime limit quantitative application to real evolved [helium](../../../../../helium.md) [stars](../../../../../star.md).
