<h1 id="s7/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{p_\theta=I_1\dot\theta,\quad
p_\psi=I_3(\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta),\quad
p_\phi=I_1\dot\phi\sin^2\theta+p_\psi\cos\theta.}
$$

The angles $\phi$ and $\psi$ are cyclic, so their canonical momenta are conserved. The [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is autonomous, so the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is also conserved. The [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_\theta$ is not generally constant because [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) and the angular kinetic terms depend on $\theta$.
