↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each canonical coordinate and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) are

$$
\boxed{\dot q_i=\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i},\qquad
\dot p_i=-\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_i}.}
$$

They use canonical rather than arbitrary mechanical momenta, a distinction important in a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). The bracket convention printed in the source is consistent with $\dot F=[F,H]$ for a function with no explicit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) dependence.
