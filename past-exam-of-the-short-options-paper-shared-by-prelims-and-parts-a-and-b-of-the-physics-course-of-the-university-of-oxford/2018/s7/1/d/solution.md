<h1 id="s7/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) cancels the term linear in [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) and gives

$$
\boxed{H=\frac{p_x^2+p_y^2+(p_z+ax^2)^2}{2m}.}
$$

It equals the mechanical [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) has no explicit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) dependence, so $H$ is conserved. This is also a direct consequence of the magnetic [force](../../../../../force-split.md) doing no work: $q\mathbf v\cdot(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)=0$.
