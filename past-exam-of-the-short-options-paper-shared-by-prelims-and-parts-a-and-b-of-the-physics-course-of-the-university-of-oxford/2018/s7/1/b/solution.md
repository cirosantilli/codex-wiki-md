<h1 id="s7/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With zero scalar potential, the nonrelativistic charged-particle [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) in SI units is [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) plus $q\mathbf A\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac m2(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)
-\frac{qB_0}{2L}x^2\dot z.}
$$

The velocity-dependent magnetic term changes [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) but does not contribute to the [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md).
