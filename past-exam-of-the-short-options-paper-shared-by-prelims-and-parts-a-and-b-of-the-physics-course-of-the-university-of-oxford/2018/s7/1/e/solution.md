<h1 id="s7/1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Since $p_y=0$ initially and remains constant, the motion stays in a plane of constant $y$. Setting $P=\sqrt{2mH_0}$ and $p=p_{z0}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\dot z=\frac{p+ax^2}{m},\qquad
\dot x=\pm\frac{\sqrt{P^2-(p+ax^2)^2}}m.}
$$

The allowed interval obeys $|p+ax^2|\le P$. The sign of $\dot x$ is set by the initial direction and changes at a turning point. The specified $x_0$ fixes the starting [position](../../../../../position.md) on the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) orbit, but the data do not specify which sign of the initial transverse [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is chosen.
