<h1 id="s7/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Only $A_z$ depends on [position](../../../../../position.md). Taking the [curl](../../../../../curl.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A=\frac{B_0x}{L}\,\hat{\mathbf y}.}
$$

The field reverses across $x=0$. Its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is zero, as required for a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md); the vanishing field on the midplane is important for the crossing and [separatrix](../../../../../separatrix.md) [trajectories](../../../../../trajectory.md) below.
