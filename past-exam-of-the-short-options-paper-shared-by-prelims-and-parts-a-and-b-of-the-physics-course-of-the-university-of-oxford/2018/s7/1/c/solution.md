<h1 id="s7/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define $a=qB_0/(2L)>0$. Differentiating the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) with respect to each generalized [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p_x=m\dot x,\qquad p_y=m\dot y,\qquad p_z=m\dot z-a x^2.}
$$

The coordinates $y$ and $z$ are cyclic, so $p_y$ and $p_z$ are conserved. The [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_x$ is generally not conserved: its equation is $\dot p_x=-2ax\dot z$. In particular, conserved canonical $p_z$ is not the same as conserved mechanical $m\dot z$, which changes as the particle moves across the magnetic-field gradient.
