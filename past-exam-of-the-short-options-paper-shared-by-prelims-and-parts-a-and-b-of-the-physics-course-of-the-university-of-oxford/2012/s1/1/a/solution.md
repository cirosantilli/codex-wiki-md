↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) is

$$
e^{-1/z}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{n!}z^{-n}=1-\frac1z+\frac1{2z^2}-\frac1{6z^3}+\cdots,
\qquad z\ne0.
$$

Its [principal part](../../../../../principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) contains infinitely many nonzero negative powers, so **zero is an essential singularity**. The first three displayed terms are not an asymptotic truncation as $z\to0$; they are the beginning of the convergent [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) in powers of $1/z$.
