<h1 id="s1/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The coefficient of $z^{-1}$ in the [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) is $-1$. The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) therefore gives, for the standard positive orientation,

$$
\boxed{\oint_Ce^{-1/z}\,dz=2\pi i\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}e^{-1/z}=-2\pi i.}
$$

The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) applies to an isolated [essential singularity](../../../../../essential-singularity.md) as well as to a [pole](../../../../../pole.md).
