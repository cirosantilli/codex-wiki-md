<h1 id="s1/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $z=1+\zeta$. Expanding the [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) near its [critical point](../../../../../critical-point.md) gives

$$
f(1+\zeta)=2+\zeta^2-\zeta^3+O(\zeta^4).
$$

For a small ray $\zeta=re^{i\theta}$, the leading image displacement is $r^2e^{2i\theta}$. Thus **oriented ray angles are doubled modulo $2\pi$** at $z=1$. This is [angle multiplication at a holomorphic critical point](../../../../../angle-multiplication-at-a-holomorphic-critical-point.md) of multiplicity two; an unoriented angle between full tangent lines must also be interpreted modulo $\pi$. The [map](../../../../../function-class.md) is not locally one-to-one there.
