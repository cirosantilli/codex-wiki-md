<h1 id="s1/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $z=e^{i\theta}$ the image is $w=2\cos\theta$, a real number. Therefore **each semicircle maps onto the real interval $[-2,2]$**, with endpoints included for closed semicircles. Under positive traversal of the full unit [circle](../../../../../circle.md), the upper half runs from $2$ to $-2$ and the lower half returns from $-2$ to $2$. Every interior point has two unit-circle preimages. The [Joukowski circle-to-ellipse mapping](../../../../../joukowski-circle-to-ellipse-mapping.md) degenerates to a doubled line segment at this radius.
