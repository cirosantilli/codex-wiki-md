<h1 id="s1/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Joukowski map](../../../../../joukowski-map.md) $f(z)=z+z^{-1}$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$. A [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) is locally [conformal](../../../../../conformal-map.md) where its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is nonzero. Here $f'(z)=1-z^{-2}$ vanishes at $z=\pm1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{holomorphic on }\mathbb C\setminus\{0\};\qquad
\text{conformal at every point except }0,1,-1,}
$$

where the exclusion of zero is because the function is undefined there. Holomorphy alone does not supply conformality at the two [critical points](../../../../../critical-point.md).
