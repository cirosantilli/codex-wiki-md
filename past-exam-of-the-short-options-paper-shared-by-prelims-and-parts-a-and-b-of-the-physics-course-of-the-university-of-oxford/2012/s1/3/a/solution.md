↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [square-root branch points](../../../../../square-root-branch-point.md) are **zero and infinity**. At zero the local behavior is $z^{-1/2}$; in the coordinate $u=1/z$ near infinity it is $u^{1/2}$. In either case one circuit reverses the sign and two restore it. Thus the [ramification index](../../../../../ramification-index.md) is $2$: these are two-sheeted algebraic [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md), sometimes described as branch order one when order means ramification index minus one. The decay to zero at infinity does not remove the branching.
