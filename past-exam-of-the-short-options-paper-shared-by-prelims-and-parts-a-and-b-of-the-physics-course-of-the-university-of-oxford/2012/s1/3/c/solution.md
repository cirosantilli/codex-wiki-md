<h1 id="s1/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Gamma function](../../../../../gamma-function.md) identity $\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt\pi$ gives the [Bromwich integral of an inverse square root](../../../../../bromwich-integral-of-an-inverse-square-root.md) directly:

$$
\boxed{I=2i\Gamma(1/2)=2i\sqrt\pi.}
$$

Equivalently, put $x=u^2$ in the real [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). It becomes $2\int_0^\infty e^{-u^2}du=\sqrt\pi$ by the [Gaussian integral](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md).
