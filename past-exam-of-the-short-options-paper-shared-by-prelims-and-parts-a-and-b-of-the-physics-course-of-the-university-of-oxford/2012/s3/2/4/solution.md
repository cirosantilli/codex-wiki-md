<h1 id="s3/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The emitted photon carries the decrease in atomic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), so $hc/\lambda=E_n-E_m$ for $n>m$. Using the preceding energy levels gives

$$
\boxed{\frac1\lambda=R_MZ^2\left(\frac1{m^2}-\frac1{n^2}\right).}
$$

The lower state's energy is more negative, making the right-hand side positive. This is the hydrogenic [Rydberg formula](../../../../../rydberg-formula.md), with the finite-nuclear-mass factor included in $R_M$.
