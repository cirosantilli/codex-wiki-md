<h1 id="s3/3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

An ideal projective measurement yielding the antisymmetric state prepares that state at $t_1$. Starting from it, the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of reaching the symmetric state after an additional interval $s$ is again $\sin^2((E_2-E_1)s/(2\hbar))$. At the full recurrence interval found above,

$$
\boxed{P\bigl(+\text{ at }t_1+\tau\mid-\text{ at }t_1\bigr)=\sin^2\pi=0.}
$$

The full-period propagator is proportional to the identity on the two-dimensional subspace, so it returns the antisymmetric state to itself, not to its orthogonal partner. The result assumes the stated time-independent evolution continues after the measurement.
