<h1 id="s3/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Projection onto the antisymmetric [quantum superposition](../../../../../quantum-superposition.md) gives amplitude $\tfrac12(e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}-e^{-iE_2t/\hbar})$. Its squared modulus is

$$
\boxed{P_{+\to-}(t)=\sin^2\left(\frac{(E_2-E_1)t}{2\hbar}\right).}
$$

It vanishes initially, reaches one at a half-recurrence time, and returns to zero after $\tau$. This interference depends on a relative phase even though each energy-eigenstate population and the mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) remain constant.
