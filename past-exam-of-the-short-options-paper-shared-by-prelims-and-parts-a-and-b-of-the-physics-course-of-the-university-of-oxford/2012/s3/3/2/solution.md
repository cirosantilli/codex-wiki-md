↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is a [probability](../../../../../probability.md) amplitude: by the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md), $|\psi(x)|^2dx$ is the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of a position measurement in an interval of width $dx$. For a normalized bound state $\int|\psi|^2dx=1$, the [expected values](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle=\int x|\psi(x)|^2\,dx,\qquad
\langle x^2\rangle=\int x^2|\psi(x)|^2\,dx.}
$$

The integrals run over the allowed domain. If the given state is not normalized, divide both by $\int|\psi|^2dx$. Its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is $\langle x^2\rangle-\langle x\rangle^2$; neither $\psi$ itself nor its overall phase is a directly measured position [probability](../../../../../probability.md).
