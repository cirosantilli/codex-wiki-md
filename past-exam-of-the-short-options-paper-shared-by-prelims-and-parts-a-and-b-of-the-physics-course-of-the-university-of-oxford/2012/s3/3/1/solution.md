<h1 id="s3/3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The one-dimensional [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}+V(x)\psi(x)=E\psi(x).}
$$

The differential term is the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) operator acting on the [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md), $V(x)$ supplies the multiplicative [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), and their sum is the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). The eigenvalue $E$ is the stationary state's total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). This form assumes a nonrelativistic particle, a time-independent scalar potential, and no additional internal degrees of freedom in the stated model.
