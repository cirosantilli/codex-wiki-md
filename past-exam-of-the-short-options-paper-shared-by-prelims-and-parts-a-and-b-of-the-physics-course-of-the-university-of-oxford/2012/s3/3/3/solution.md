<h1 id="s3/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Each [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) acquires its own phase under the time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md):

$$
|\psi(t)\rangle=\frac{e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}|1\rangle+e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}|2\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Orthonormality removes all off-diagonal terms from the [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), giving

$$
\boxed{\langle E\rangle=\frac{E_1+E_2}{2},\quad\text{independent of time}.}
$$

Return up to an overall phase requires the relative phase to return to one. For $E_1\ne E_2$, the shortest positive recurrence time is

$$
\boxed{\tau=\frac{2\pi\hbar}{|E_2-E_1|}.}
$$

If the two energies coincide, the whole superposition is stationary up to a common phase for every time; there is then no smallest strictly positive recurrence time. A half-period changes the relative sign and produces the orthogonal antisymmetric state rather than the original symmetric state.
