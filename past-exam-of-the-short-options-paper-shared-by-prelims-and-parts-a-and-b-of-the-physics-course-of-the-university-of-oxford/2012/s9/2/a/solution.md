<h1 id="s9/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the driving signs $a_t=\alpha(t)$ to be independent across times, with $\mathbb Ea_t=0$ and $a_t^2=1$. Temporal independence of the drivers is an assumption needed in addition to their stated equal one-time [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md). For $X_t=\delta y(t)=a_{t-2}a_{t-1}+a_t$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EX_t=\mathbb Ea_{t-2}\,\mathbb Ea_{t-1}+\mathbb Ea_t=0.}
$$

As a useful scale check, $X_t$ takes values $-2,0,2$ with [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) $1/4,1/2,1/4$. Its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is therefore $\boxed{\mathbb EX_t^2=2}$, rather than the unit [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) of a single driving sign.
