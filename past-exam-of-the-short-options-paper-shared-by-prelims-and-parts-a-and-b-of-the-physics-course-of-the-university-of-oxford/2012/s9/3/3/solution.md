<h1 id="s9/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

At strike $K=108$, the European put payoff is $(108-S_3)^+$. In the ascending-price order $72.9,89.1,108.9,133.1$, its values are

$$
\boxed{35.1,\quad18.9,\quad0,\quad0.}
$$

Risk-neutral expectation discounted for three steps gives

$$
\boxed{P_0=\frac{(0.3)^3(35.1)+3(0.7)(0.3)^2(18.9)}{(1.04)^3}
=4.01809.}
$$

The physical [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $p$ drops out because the complete two-state tree admits a replicating hedge. The strike is $108$, so the $108.9$ endpoint is out of the money; replacing it by a positive payoff would materially change the price.
