<h1 id="s9/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The one-step expected price multiplier under the stated physical [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is $1.1p+0.9(1-p)=0.9+0.2p$. Independence lets the expectation of the product factorize, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_p[S_3]=100(0.9+0.2p)^3.}
$$

The same expression follows by summing the four binomial endpoint [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) times their prices. This is a physical-measure expectation; option replication below uses a separately determined risk-neutral [probability](../../../../../probability.md).
