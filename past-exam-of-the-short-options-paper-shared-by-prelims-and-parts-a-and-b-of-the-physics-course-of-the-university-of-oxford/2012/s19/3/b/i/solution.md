<h1 id="s19/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The instantaneous [beam lifetime](../../../../../../beam-lifetime.md) is

$$
\boxed{\tau=-\frac{N}{dN/dt},}
$$

where losses make $dN/dt<0$. If the fractional loss rate is constant, $N(t)=N_0e^{-t/\tau}$ and $\tau$ is the time to fall to $1/e$ of the starting population. At fixed revolution frequency, the circulating current has the same exponential decay. Density-dependent loss mechanisms can make the instantaneous lifetime vary, so a single exponential need not fit every fill.
