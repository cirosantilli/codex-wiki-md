<h1 id="s19/3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use a calibrated, nonintercepting [DC beam current transformer](../../../../../../dc-beam-current-transformer.md) to measure the circulating current repeatedly during a fill, with top-up injection absent or separately recorded. At fixed revolution frequency, $I=eNf_{\rm rev}$, so a fit to $\log I$ versus time gives slope $-1/\tau$ for exponential decay. The transformer's zero offset and long-term drift must be controlled, especially at low current. An AC current transformer or a calibrated bunch pickup can additionally resolve bunch charge, whereas a DC transformer provides the total circulating intensity needed here.
