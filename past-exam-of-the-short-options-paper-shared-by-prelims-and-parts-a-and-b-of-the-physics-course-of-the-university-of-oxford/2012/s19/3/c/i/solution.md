<h1 id="s19/3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both machines are proposed electron–positron [linear colliders](../../../../../../linear-collider.md), using sources, damping rings, bunch compressors, two opposing main linacs, and final-focus and beam-delivery sections that bring very small bunches to an interaction point. The spent beams go to extraction lines and beam dumps rather than making repeated collision turns.

In the exam-era [International Linear Collider](../../../../../../international-linear-collider.md) reference design, polarized electrons and an undulator-based positron source feed damping rings and two superconducting main linacs; the full $500$ GeV centre-of-mass facility is about $31$ km long. The [Compact Linear Collider](../../../../../../compact-linear-collider.md) adds high-current drive-beam generation and deceleration lines parallel to its main linacs; its $3$ TeV concept is roughly $48$ km long. The linear layout avoids the repeated synchrotron-radiation losses that would be severe for multi-hundred-GeV electrons in a storage ring. [ILC Reference Design Report, Volume 3—Accelerator (2007), abstract and overall layout](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2361) [CLIC Conceptual Design Report, CERN-2012-007, accelerator concept and layout](https://cds.cern.ch/record/1500095)
