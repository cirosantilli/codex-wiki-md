Thermal [interstellar dust](../../../../../interstellar-dust.md) produces a bright [galactic foreground](../../../../../galactic-foreground.md) concentrated toward the [Milky Way](../../../../../milky-way.md) plane, with additional cloud structure. Its emission spectrum differs from the approximately $2.73\,\mathrm K$ [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) spectrum of the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md), so comparisons across [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) help separate it. In the sketch below this is the bright equatorial band.
