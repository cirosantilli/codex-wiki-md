<h1 id="s2/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Adiabatic expansion preserves the [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) form while each photon's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) redshifts as $a^{-1}$. Thus the [cosmic microwave background temperature](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-temperature.md) obeys $T(z)=T_0(1+z)$, giving

$$
\boxed{T(z=3)=4T_0\simeq10.9\,\mathrm K.}
$$
