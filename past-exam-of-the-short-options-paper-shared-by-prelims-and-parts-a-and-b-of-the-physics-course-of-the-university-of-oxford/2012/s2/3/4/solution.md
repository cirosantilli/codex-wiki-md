<h1 id="s2/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Convert both inputs to [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md). The matter contribution, including rest energy, is $u_{M,0}=\rho_{M,0}c^2=2.25\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$. The given photon value is $u_{\gamma,0}=5\times10^5(1.602\times10^{-19})=8.01\times10^{-14}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{u_{M,0}}{u_{\gamma,0}}\simeq2.80\times10^3.}
$$

Matter [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) scales as $(1+z)^3$, whereas the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) scales as $(1+z)^4$. The [photon-only matter-radiation equality](../../../../../photon-only-matter-radiation-equality.md) condition is therefore

$$
1+z_{\rm eq}=\frac{u_{M,0}}{u_{\gamma,0}},\qquad
\boxed{z_{\rm eq}\simeq2.8\times10^3.}
$$

This uses the supplied CMB photon density. Additional relativistic species change equality for total radiation.
