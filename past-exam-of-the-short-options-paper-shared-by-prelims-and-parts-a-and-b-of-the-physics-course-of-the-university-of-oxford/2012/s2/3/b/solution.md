↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Observer motion produces the [cosmic microwave background dipole](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-dipole.md): to first order in $\beta=v/c$,

$$
\frac{\Delta T(\theta)}{T_0}=\beta\cos\theta.
$$

The direction of motion is hotter and the opposite direction cooler. Its full-sky pattern has one broad positive hemisphere and one broad negative hemisphere; it is not concentrated in the [galactic plane](../../../../../galactic-plane.md).
