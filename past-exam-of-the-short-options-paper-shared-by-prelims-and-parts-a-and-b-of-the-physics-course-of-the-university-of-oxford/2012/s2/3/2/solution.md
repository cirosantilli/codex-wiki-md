<h1 id="s2/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The stated one-sided temperature contrast is $\beta=0.00105$, so the [Doppler dipole](../../../../../doppler-dipole.md) gives

$$
\boxed{v=0.00105c\simeq3.15\times10^5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}=315\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The motion is **toward the hotter region**. The contrast already gives one side relative to the mean; the hot-to-cold difference would be twice as large.
