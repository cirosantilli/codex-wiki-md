<h1 id="s2/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For two bodies of masses $M_1,M_2$, the relative [Kepler orbit](../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) has semimajor axis $a$ and orbital [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) $P$ satisfying [Kepler's third law](../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md)

$$
\boxed{P^2=\frac{4\pi^2a^3}{G(M_1+M_2)}.}
$$

Here $a$ is the semimajor axis of the separation vector, not either star's individual barycentric orbit. In conventional stellar units the same relation is approximately $(P/\mathrm{yr})^2=(a/\mathrm{AU})^3/[(M_1+M_2)/M_\odot]$, with year, [astronomical unit](../../../../../astronomical-unit.md) and [solar mass](../../../../../solar-mass.md) as the units.
