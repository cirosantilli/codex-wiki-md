<h1 id="s2/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Use the supplied atomic masses consistently. Their [mass defect](../../../../../mass-defect.md) per four hydrogen atoms is $4(1.0078)-4.0026=0.0286$ atomic mass units. The conversion efficiency is

$$
\boxed{\epsilon=\frac{4m_H-m_{\rm He}}{4m_H}=0.007095\simeq0.71\%.}
$$

The atomic-mass bookkeeping includes the associated positron-annihilation energy; an exact photon-heating budget also subtracts energy carried away by [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md). With the stated ten-percent fuel fraction and constant present [solar luminosity](../../../../../solar-luminosity.md), the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) lifetime estimate is

$$
\boxed{\tau_{\rm MS}\simeq\frac{0.1\epsilon M_\odot c^2}{L_\odot}\simeq1.05\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

The much longer timescale explains why [stellar nuclear fusion](../../../../../stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) can power the Sun throughout its long main-sequence history.
