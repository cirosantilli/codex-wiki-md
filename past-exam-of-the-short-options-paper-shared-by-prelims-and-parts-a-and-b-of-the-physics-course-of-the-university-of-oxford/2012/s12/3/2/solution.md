<h1 id="s12/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Let $C(x)$ be the particle [number density](../../../../../number-density.md), $U(x)$ the conservative [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) and $F=-dU/dx$ the applied [force](../../../../../force-split.md). In the overdamped regime a viscous [drag coefficient](../../../../../drag-coefficient.md) $\zeta$ gives drift [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $v_d=F/\zeta$. Combining this drift with [Fick's first law](../../../../../fick-s-first-law.md) gives the number flux

$$
J=-D\frac{dC}{dx}+\frac F\zeta C.
$$

At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) is $C_{\rm eq}\propto e^{-U/(k_{\rm B}T)}$, so $dC_{\rm eq}/dx=FC_{\rm eq}/(k_{\rm B}T)$. Setting its net flux to zero yields

$$
0=FC_{\rm eq}\left(\frac1\zeta-\frac D{k_{\rm B}T}\right),\qquad
\boxed{D=\frac{k_{\rm B}T}{\zeta}=\mu k_{\rm B}T,\quad\mu=\zeta^{-1}.}
$$

This is the [Einstein diffusion relation](../../../../../einstein-diffusion-relation.md). The derivation assumes a dilute equilibrium suspension, uniform [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), linear drift response and a well-defined long-time [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md). With a nonzero conservative [force](../../../../../force-split.md), the full transport equation includes drift; the force-free equation in the next section describes spreading without that drift.
