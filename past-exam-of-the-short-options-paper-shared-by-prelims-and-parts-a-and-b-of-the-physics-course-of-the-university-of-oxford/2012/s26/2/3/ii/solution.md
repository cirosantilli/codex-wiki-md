↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An old [globular cluster](../../../../../../globular-cluster.md) has lost its original massive main-sequence members. Its [main-sequence turnoff](../../../../../../main-sequence-turnoff.md) lies near a low stellar mass, typically around $0.8M_\odot$ for an age of order $10^{10}$ years. The diagram shows a lower [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md), a [subgiant branch](../../../../../../subgiant-branch.md), a prominent [red giant branch](../../../../../../red-giant-branch.md), a [horizontal branch](../../../../../../horizontal-branch.md) of core-helium-burning stars, an [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) and a faint [white dwarf](../../../../../../white-dwarf.md) cooling sequence. The preceding schematic labels these features. A few [blue stragglers](../../../../../../blue-straggler.md) may lie above the old turnoff because interaction or merger rejuvenated them; they do not imply a young age for the bulk population.
