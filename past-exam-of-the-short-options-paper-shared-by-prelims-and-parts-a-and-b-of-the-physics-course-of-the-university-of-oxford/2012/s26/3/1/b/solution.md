↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Edge-on, the bright [thin galactic disk](../../../../../../thin-galactic-disk.md) forms a narrow band, with a central bulge protruding above and below it and a thinner gas-and-dust lane along the midplane. Young stars and [H II regions](../../../../../../h-ii-region.md) occupy a vertical scale of a few tenths of a kiloparsec or less; a broader old [thick disk](../../../../../../thick-disk.md) has scale of order a kiloparsec. These are approximate scale heights, not sharp boundaries. The old [stellar halo](../../../../../../stellar-halo.md) and [globular clusters](../../../../../../globular-cluster.md) form a much larger, low-surface-brightness spheroidal component, extending to many tens of kiloparsecs or more. The [dark matter halo](../../../../../../dark-matter-halo.md) is inferred dynamically rather than directly visible in such a sketch. The preceding figure is a structural diagram, not an external photograph or a reconstruction of a particular survey.
