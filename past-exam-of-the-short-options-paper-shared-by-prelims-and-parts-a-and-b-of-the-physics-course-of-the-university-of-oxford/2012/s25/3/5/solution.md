<h1 id="s25/3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The real [carbon cycle](../../../../../carbon-cycle.md) has multiple reservoirs and adjustment times, rather than one universally exponential removal process. Air-sea exchange, ocean mixing and carbonate chemistry give a long-lived atmospheric tail; uptake also changes with [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), circulation and carbon loading. For example, a warming ocean can absorb less additional [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), so a fixed $b_0$ omits a potentially positive climate-carbon feedback. Terrestrial vegetation and soil responses provide further examples. **One missing feature is the dependence of carbon uptake on the changing state of the ocean and climate.**
