<h1 id="s25/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Put $A=b_1E_0/b_0$. The proposed solution can be written

$$
C-C_0=\frac A{g+b_0}\left(ge^{-b_0t}+b_0e^{gt}\right).
$$

It gives $C(0)-C_0=A$. Differentiation and substitution give, respectively,

$$
\dot C=\frac{Agb_0}{g+b_0}\left(e^{gt}-e^{-b_0t}\right),
$$



$$
b_1E_0e^{gt}-b_0(C-C_0)=\frac{Agb_0}{g+b_0}\left(e^{gt}-e^{-b_0t}\right),
$$

so it satisfies both the differential equation and the initial condition. At $t=200$ years,

$$
\boxed{C(2000)=279+\frac{0.03e^{-1}+0.005e^6}{0.035}\simeq336.95\,\mathrm{ppmv},}
$$

with $E(2000)=0.02e^6\simeq8.069\,\mathrm{GtC\,yr^{-1}}$. The earlier emissions grow on the $g^{-1}=33.3$ year timescale, much faster than the model's 200-year removal time.
