<h1 id="s19/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an ultrarelativistic electron of [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=E/(m_ec^2)=3000/0.511=5870.84$, the [synchrotron critical frequency](../../../../../synchrotron-critical-frequency.md) gives $\varepsilon_c=\hbar\omega_c=3\hbar c\gamma^3/(2\rho)$. Thus, using the supplied constants,

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{3\hbar c\gamma^3}{2\varepsilon_c}=7.47\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The angle-integrated spectral power per unit photon energy is proportional to $x\int_x^\infty K_{5/3}(u)du$, with $x=\varepsilon/\varepsilon_c$. It peaks at $x\simeq0.286$, so

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_{\rm peak}\simeq2.29\,\mathrm{keV}.}
$$

This is a power spectrum per linear energy interval; power per logarithmic interval and photon-count spectra have different peaks. The [magnetic rigidity](../../../../../magnetic-rigidity.md) condition is $p=|e|B\rho$, with $pc\simeq E$, giving

$$
\boxed{B\simeq\frac{E}{|e|c\rho}=1.34\,\mathrm T.}
$$

The critical energy sets a broad spectral scale, not a maximum allowed photon energy.[critical energy and angle-integrated synchrotron spectrum](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation-in-the-geant4-physics-reference-manual-by-geant4-collaboration.md)
