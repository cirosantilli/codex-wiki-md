<h1 id="s19/1/c/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a real uncoupled, lossless transverse map, $\det M=1$. Bounded nondegenerate oscillatory motion requires complex-conjugate [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $e^{\pm i\mu}$, equivalently

$$
\boxed{|\operatorname{tr}M|<2.}
$$

Its [Courant-Snyder parameters](../../../../../../courant-snyder-parameters.md) give

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\mu+\alpha_T\sin\mu&\beta_T\sin\mu\\
-\gamma_T\sin\mu&\cos\mu-\alpha_T\sin\mu
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\beta_T>0,\quad\beta_T\gamma_T-\alpha_T^2=1.
$$

The positive quadratic form $\gamma_Tx^2+2\alpha_Txx'+\beta_Tx'^2$ is invariant, so repeating the cell cannot produce secular growth. At trace $\pm2$ a nontrivial Jordan block instead produces linear growth; only the exceptional maps $M=\pm I$ remain bounded at those exact boundaries. The criterion must hold in both transverse planes. For the symmetric [FODO cell](../../../../../../fodo-cell.md), both traces are $2-d^2/f^2$, giving

$$
\boxed{0<d/|f|<2,\qquad \sin(\mu/2)=d/(2|f|).}
$$
