<h1 id="s7/1/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

In $D$ dimensions the antisymmetric [angular momentum tensor](../../../../../angular-momentum-tensor.md) has components

$$
L_{ab}=\sum_{s=1}^2(q_{s,a}p_{s,b}-q_{s,b}p_{s,a}),\qquad a<b.
$$

There are $D(D-1)/2$ independent rotation generators. Together with $D$ translation generators and one time-translation generator, the standard [Noether charge](../../../../../noether-charge.md) enumeration is therefore

$$
\boxed{1+D+\frac{D(D-1)}2=1+\frac{D(D+1)}2.}
$$

The word independent here counts symmetry generators and tensor components; it needs qualification if it is meant as functional independence of all charges for only two particles in arbitrarily high dimension. In [centre-of-mass coordinates](../../../../../center-of-mass-coordinates.md), $L=R\wedge P+r\wedge p$. Consequently $P\wedge L\wedge L=0$, a nontrivial identity from dimension five onwards, since $(r\wedge p)\wedge(r\wedge p)=0$. Thus the displayed number is the symmetry-charge count; it is not an assertion that these functions have unrestricted values for a two-particle realization in every $D$.
