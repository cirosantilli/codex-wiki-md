<h1 id="s7/1/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The central pair potential is invariant under simultaneous spatial translations, simultaneous rotations and time translations. The corresponding time-independent [Noether charges](../../../../../noether-charge.md) are total [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $P=p_1+p_2$, total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) about a fixed origin, and total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $H$.

In one dimension this supplies **$P$ and $H$: two charges**. In two dimensions it supplies **$P_x,P_y,L_{xy},H$: four charges**. In three dimensions it supplies **three momentum components, three angular-momentum components and $H$: seven charges**. For generic states these listed charges are independent in those dimensions. Galilean boosts can also be expressed by explicitly time-dependent conserved combinations, but are not part of the requested translation-rotation-energy count.
