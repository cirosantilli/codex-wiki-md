↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [Jacobi identity](../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) is $\{f,\{g,h\}\}+\{g,\{h,f\}\}+\{h,\{f,g\}\}=0$. If $f,g$ have no explicit time dependence and are conserved, $\{f,H\}=\{g,H\}=0$. Taking $h=H$ in that identity yields $\{H,\{f,g\}\}=0$, or

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}\{f,g\}=\{\{f,g\},H\}=0.}
$$

Thus the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) of the two conserved functions is another conserved function; it need not be independent of the original ones.
