<h1 id="s7/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For a phase-space function with no explicit time dependence, the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) and [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give

$$
\frac{df}{dt}=\sum_i(\partial_{q_i}f\,\dot q_i+\partial_{p_i}f\,\dot p_i)=\sum_i(\partial_{q_i}f\,\partial_{p_i}H-\partial_{p_i}f\,\partial_{q_i}H)=\boxed{\{f,H\}}.
$$

An explicitly time-dependent function would instead satisfy $df/dt=\partial_tf+\{f,H\}$.
