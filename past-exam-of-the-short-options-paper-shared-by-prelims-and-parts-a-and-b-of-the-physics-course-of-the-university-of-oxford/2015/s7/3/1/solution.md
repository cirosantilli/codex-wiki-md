↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The canonical [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is

$$
\{f,g\}=\sum_k\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_k}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_k}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_k}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_k}\right).
$$

The coordinate derivatives are [Kronecker deltas](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md), so direct substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\{q_i,q_j\}=0,\qquad\{p_i,p_j\}=0,\qquad\{q_i,p_j\}=\delta_{ij}.}
$$
