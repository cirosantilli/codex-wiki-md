<h1 id="s7/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Let $\mathcal E=\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2\pi^2}$. [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give

$$
\boxed{\dot r_i=\frac{c^2\pi_i}{\mathcal E},\qquad\dot p_i=q\sum_j\dot r_j\partial_iA_j-q\partial_i\phi.}
$$

Subtracting $q\,dA_i/dt$ from the second equation gives the familiar [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md), $\dot\pi=q(E+\dot r\times B)$, with $E=-\nabla\phi-\partial_tA$ and $B=\nabla\times A$. This also checks the signs of both potential terms.
