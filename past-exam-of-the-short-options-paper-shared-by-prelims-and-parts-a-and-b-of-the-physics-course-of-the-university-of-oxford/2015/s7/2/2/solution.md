<h1 id="s7/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $p=\gamma m\dot r$, where $\gamma=(1-\dot r^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Eliminating velocity gives

$$
\boxed{H=\gamma mc^2=\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2p^2}.}
$$

Its [Hamilton equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) are $\dot r=c^2p/H$ and $\dot p=0$. In the limit $|p|\ll mc$, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is $H=mc^2+p^2/(2m)+O(p^4/(m^3c^2))$, and $\dot r\simeq p/m$. The additive rest energy does not change the classical free-particle equations.
