<h1 id="s7/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Minimal electromagnetic coupling changes the [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) to $p=\gamma m\dot r+qA$. The [mechanical momentum](../../../../../mechanical-momentum.md) is $\pi=p-qA$. The [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) yields

$$
\boxed{H(r,p,t)=\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2[p-qA(r,t)]^2}+q\phi(r,t).}
$$

The cancellation of $q\dot r\cdot A$ in the Legendre transform does not remove magnetic effects: $A$ remains in the relation between canonical and mechanical momenta. The expression is the relativistic kinetic-plus-rest energy with an electrostatic potential-energy term, expressed in canonical variables.
