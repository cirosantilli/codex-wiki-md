↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**No region maps onto the entire finite complex plane:** $e^z$ is never zero. Its image on $\mathbb C$ is $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$. A fundamental strip such as

$$
\boxed{\{x+iy:x\in\mathbb R,\ -\pi<y\leq\pi\}}
$$

maps bijectively onto that punctured plane, because $e^{x+iy}=e^xe^{iy}$. The half-open strip supplies one representative of each angle; it is not an open [domain](../../../../../domain-mathematical-analysis.md). An open strip of height $2\pi$ omits a ray as well. The missing zero is approached only in the limit $x\to-\infty$.
