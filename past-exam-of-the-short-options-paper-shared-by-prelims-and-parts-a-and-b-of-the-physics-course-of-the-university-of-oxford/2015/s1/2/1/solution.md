<h1 id="s1/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) preserves the magnitude and orientation of angles between intersecting smooth curves locally. For a complex [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md), the condition at $z_0$ is $f'(z_0)\ne0$: the first-order change $\delta w=f'(z_0)\delta z$ rotates and uniformly scales every tangent vector by the same complex factor. This local statement does not require a globally one-to-one map.
