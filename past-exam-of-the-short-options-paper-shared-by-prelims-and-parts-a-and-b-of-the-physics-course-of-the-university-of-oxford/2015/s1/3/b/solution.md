<h1 id="s1/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $I$ denote the requested [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). Symmetry and the period of $\sin^2\theta$ make the corresponding integral over $0\leq\theta\leq2\pi$ equal to $4I$. With $z=e^{i\theta}$, this becomes

$$
4I=\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{z\,dz}{i(-2z^4+5z^2-2)}.
$$

The denominator factors as $-2(z^2-1/2)(z^2-2)$. The two poles inside the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) are $z=\pm1/\sqrt2$. Each [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is $1/(6i)$, because the derivative of the polynomial denominator there is $6z$. The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) gives $4I=2\pi/3$, so

$$
\boxed{I=\frac\pi6.}
$$
