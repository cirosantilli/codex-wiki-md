<h1 id="s16/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Seek a constant transverse [guiding-centre drift](../../../../../guiding-centre-drift.md) $\mathbf v_d$ whose magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) balances the applied force. Define

$$
\mathbf v_d=\frac{\mathbf F\times\mathbf B}{qB^2}.
$$

Since $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf B=0$, the vector triple-product identity gives $q\mathbf v_d\times\mathbf B=-\mathbf F$. Writing $\mathbf v=\mathbf v_d+\mathbf u$ therefore reduces the full equation to $m\dot{\mathbf u}=q\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$, the ordinary gyromotion equation. Its transverse orbit is circular in the drifting frame, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v_{\rm gc}=\mathbf v_d=\frac1q\frac{\mathbf F\times\mathbf B}{B^2}.}
$$

Averaging over a gyroperiod removes the transverse oscillatory velocity and leaves this drift, together with any constant parallel velocity. For an electric force $\mathbf F=q\mathbf E$, this becomes the charge-independent $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$ drift; a charge-independent force instead gives opposite drift directions for opposite charge signs.
