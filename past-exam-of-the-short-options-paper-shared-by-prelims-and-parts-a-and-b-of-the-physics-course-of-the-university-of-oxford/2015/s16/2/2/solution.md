Define the [plasma parameter](../../../../../plasma-parameter.md) here as the number of electrons in an electron Debye sphere,

$$
N_D=\frac{4\pi}{3}n_e\lambda_D^3.
$$

Some texts instead use $n_e\lambda_D^3$, or its inverse as a coupling parameter. A weakly coupled collective plasma has $N_D\gg1$; fluctuations from individual nearby particles are small compared with the collective description.

A Coulomb collision means the cumulative deflection or relaxation produced by many small-angle encounters, not necessarily a hard contact between two particles. The [Coulomb logarithm](../../../../../coulomb-logarithm.md) $\ln\Lambda_C\simeq\ln(b_{\max}/b_{\min})$ summarizes the impact-parameter range, with $b_{\max}$ of order $\lambda_D$. A process is collisionless when its timescale is much shorter than the appropriate collisional relaxation time, or its length scale is much smaller than a corresponding mean free path. It still contains collective interactions.

For fully ionized ions of charge $Ze$, a thermal electron–ion momentum-relaxation rate has the scaling

$$
\nu_{ei}\sim\frac{Zn_ee^4\ln\Lambda_C}{\epsilon_0^2m_e^{1/2}(k_BT)^{3/2}},\qquad \boxed{t_{\rm col}\sim\frac{N_D}{Z\ln\Lambda_C\,\omega_p}},
$$

up to numerical factors depending on the Debye-sphere and collision-time conventions. More explicitly, the Maxwellian rate used below gives $\nu_{ei}/\omega_p\simeq0.089\,Z\ln\Lambda_C/N_D$, or $t_{\rm col}\omega_p\simeq11.3N_D/(Z\ln\Lambda_C)$. Specifying this factor prevents mixing a small coupling parameter with a large Debye-sphere population.[Fundamental Plasma Parameters and Coulomb Collisions: electron thermal and collision-rate conventions](../../../../../nrl-plasma-formulary-2019.md)
