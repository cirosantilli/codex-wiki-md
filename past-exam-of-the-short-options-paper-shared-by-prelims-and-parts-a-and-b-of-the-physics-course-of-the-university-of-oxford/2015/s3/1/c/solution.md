<h1 id="s3/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The wave-function amplitude decays as $e^{-\kappa x}$, so its e-folding penetration depth is

$$
\boxed{\Delta x=\kappa^{-1}=\frac\hbar{\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}}.}
$$

The [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) decays as $e^{-2\kappa x}$ and has the smaller e-folding depth $1/(2\kappa)$. Specifying which quantity decays avoids a factor-of-two ambiguity in the term penetration depth.
