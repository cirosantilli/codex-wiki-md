<h1 id="s3/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The reflection-one result describes a semi-infinite [potential step](../../../../../step-potential.md), rather than a finite-width barrier. Define $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. For $E>0$, take

$$
\phi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+r e^{-ikx},&x<0,\\c e^{-\kappa x},&x>0.\end{cases}
$$

The growing exponential is excluded by boundedness as $x\to\infty$. Continuity of $\phi$ and $\phi'$ at the finite step gives $1+r=c$ and $ik(1-r)=-\kappa c$, hence

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{k-i\kappa}{k+i\kappa},\qquad c=\frac{2k}{k+i\kappa},\qquad R=|r|^2=1.}
$$

The [probability current](../../../../../probability-current.md) of the decaying tail is zero; the reflected and incident currents have equal magnitude. This agrees with the classical reflection coefficient even though the spatial wave function differs from a classical trajectory.
