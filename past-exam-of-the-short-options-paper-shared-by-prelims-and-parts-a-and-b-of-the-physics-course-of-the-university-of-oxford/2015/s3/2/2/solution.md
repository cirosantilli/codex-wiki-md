↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For the macroscopic mass,

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac h{mv}=\frac{6.626\times10^{-34}}{2(33)}\simeq1.00\times10^{-35}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The ideal fixed-momentum expression tends to infinity as $v\to0$. An everyday localized object is not a perfectly coherent momentum eigenstate, however: its thermal and environmental [momentum uncertainty](../../../../../momentum-uncertainty.md) remains nonzero, and [decoherence](../../../../../quantum-decoherence.md) rapidly suppresses spatially separated coherent alternatives. Merely setting the mean velocity to zero does not prepare an object with a macroscopic [coherence length](../../../../../optical-coherence-length.md).
