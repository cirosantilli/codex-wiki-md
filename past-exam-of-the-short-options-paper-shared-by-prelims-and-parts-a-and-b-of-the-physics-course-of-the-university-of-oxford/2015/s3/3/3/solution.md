<h1 id="s3/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The classical mixture has [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) $\rho=\tfrac12|HH\rangle\langle HH|+\tfrac12|VV\rangle\langle VV|$. Average the probabilities of its two alternatives:

$$
\boxed{p(A,B)=\frac12[\cos^2\theta_A\cos^2\theta_B+\sin^2\theta_A\sin^2\theta_B].}
$$

Alice's single-detector transmission probability is $1/2$ at every angle. Dividing by it gives

$$
\boxed{p(B\mid A,\theta_A=45^\circ)=\frac12,\qquad p(B\mid A,\theta_A=0)=\cos^2\theta_B.}
$$

At $45^\circ$, Alice's click does not distinguish the two classical alternatives. At zero, it selects the horizontally polarized alternative.
