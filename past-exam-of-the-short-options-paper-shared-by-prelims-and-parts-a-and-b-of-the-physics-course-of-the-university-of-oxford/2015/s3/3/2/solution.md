↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For the [product state](../../../../../product-state.md) $|H\rangle_A|H\rangle_B$, the two projection amplitudes multiply. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p(\theta_A,\theta_B)=\cos^2\theta_A\cos^2\theta_B.}
$$

The independent factors arise from this product input; they cannot be assumed for a general correlated pair state.
