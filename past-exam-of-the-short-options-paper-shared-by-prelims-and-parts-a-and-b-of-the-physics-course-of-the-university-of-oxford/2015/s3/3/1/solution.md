<h1 id="s3/3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The normalized linear [polarization state](../../../../../polarization-state.md) is $|\theta\rangle=\cos\theta|H\rangle+\sin\theta|V\rangle$. A transmitted-photon count implements the [projector](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) $|\theta\rangle\langle\theta|$. The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p(\theta)=|\langle\theta|H\rangle|^2=\cos^2\theta,}
$$

the single-photon form of [Malus's law](../../../../../malus-s-law.md), assuming ideal polarizers and detection.
