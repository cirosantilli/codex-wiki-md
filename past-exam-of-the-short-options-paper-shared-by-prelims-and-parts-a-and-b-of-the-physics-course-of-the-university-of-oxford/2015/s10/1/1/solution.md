<h1 id="s10/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a narrow, well-collimated [photon](../../../../../photon.md) beam, scattered [photons](../../../../../photon.md) are rejected and the uncollided [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) obeys the [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../../beer-lambert-law.md), $I(x)=I(0)e^{-\mu x}$. With water [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho\simeq1\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$, the [linear attenuation coefficient](../../../../../linear-attenuation-coefficient.md) is $\mu=\rho(\mu/\rho)$. The [half-value layer](../../../../../half-value-layer.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{x_{1/2}=\frac{\ln2}{\mu},\qquad x_{1/2}(140\,\mathrm{keV})=4.51\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad x_{1/2}(511\,\mathrm{keV})=7.22\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

Thus the [annihilation photons](../../../../../annihilation-photon.md) associated with the fluorine-18 source require about $1.60$ times as much water for the same attenuation as the technetium-99m [photons](../../../../../photon.md). The $511\,\mathrm{keV}$ here is the [annihilation photon](../../../../../annihilation-photon.md) energy, not the variable kinetic energy of an emitted [Positron](../../../../../positron.md). A broad-beam measurement admitting scattered [photons](../../../../../photon.md) would require a buildup correction and would not have this simple transmission interpretation.[§2, equations 1–3: uncollided transmission and mass thickness](../../../../../tables-of-x-ray-mass-attenuation-coefficients-and-mass-energy-absorption-coefficients-by-john-h-hubbell-and-stephen-m-seltzer.md)
