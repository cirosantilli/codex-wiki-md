<h1 id="s10/1/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

In the ideal unscattered coincidence model, let a [positron annihilation](../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) occur at position $s$ on a [coincidence line of response](../../../../../coincidence-line-of-response.md) connecting detectors $A$ and $B$. Its two oppositely directed [photons](../../../../../photon.md) must both survive. Their independent attenuation factors multiply:

$$
P_{\rm coinc}(s)=\exp\left[-\int_A^s\mu(\ell)\,d\ell\right]\exp\left[-\int_s^B\mu(\ell)\,d\ell\right]=\boxed{\exp\left[-\int_A^B\mu(\ell)\,d\ell\right].}
$$

The source position cancels because the complementary path integrals span the whole chord. In a uniform slab of chord length $L$, the same argument is $e^{-\mu s}e^{-\mu(L-s)}=e^{-\mu L}$. Therefore attenuation correction requires the full [linear attenuation coefficient](../../../../../linear-attenuation-coefficient.md) map along the chord, but not the depth of the particular annihilation along that chord. This argument addresses attenuation of true [PET coincidences](../../../../../pet-coincidence.md); scatter, random coincidences and detector efficiency need their own corrections.
