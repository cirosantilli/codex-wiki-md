A passively scattered [proton therapy](../../../../../proton-therapy.md) beam uses distinct devices to control lateral spread, range and distal shape. The lower panel of the figure gives a functional schematic; the exact ordering varies among beamlines. An energy selector or range shifter sets the maximum range. A stepped range-modulation wheel or ridge filter gives a weighted collection of proton energies, forming the [spread-out Bragg peak](../../../../../spread-out-bragg-peak.md). One or more scattering foils broaden the narrow beam; a double-scattering system can improve lateral uniformity. [Ionization chambers](../../../../../ionization-chamber.md) monitor delivered fluence and position. A patient-specific aperture or collimator blocks unwanted lateral regions. A shaped range compensator makes the remaining range vary across the field, matching the distal target boundary. Their effects must be combined: lateral collimation alone cannot flatten the depth distribution, and an energy modulator alone cannot set the lateral contour.[original experiments and design: blocking the central peak followed by rescattering to flatten a large field](../../../../../flattening-of-proton-dose-distributions-for-large-field-radiotherapy-by-andreas-m-koehler-and-others.md)
