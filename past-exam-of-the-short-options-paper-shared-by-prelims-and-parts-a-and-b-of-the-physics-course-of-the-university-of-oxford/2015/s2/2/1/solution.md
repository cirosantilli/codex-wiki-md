The [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) is defined by the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) applied to a star's total luminosity:

$$
\boxed{L=4\pi R^2\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm eff}^4.}
$$

Here $R$ is the adopted photospheric radius and $\sigma_{\rm SB}$ is the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md). This defines the temperature of a black body radiating the same total flux per unit surface area; it does not assert that every wavelength of the stellar spectrum follows a perfect [Planck spectrum](../../../../../blackbody-spectrum.md).
