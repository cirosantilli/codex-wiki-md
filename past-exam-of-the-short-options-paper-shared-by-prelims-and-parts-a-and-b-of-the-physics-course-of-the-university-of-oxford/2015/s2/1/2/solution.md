<h1 id="s2/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Kepler's third law](../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) is $P^2=4\pi^2a^3/[G(M_S+M_P)]$, with $a$ the semimajor axis of the relative [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). For a circular edge-on [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), the star's speed is $V_S=2\pi a_S/P$, and the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) relation gives $a_S=aM_P/(M_S+M_P)$. Cubing the speed expression and eliminating $a$ yields

$$
\frac{M_P^3}{(M_S+M_P)^2}=\frac{PV_S^3}{2\pi G}.
$$

For $M_P\ll M_S$, this reduces to

$$
\boxed{M_P^3\simeq\frac{V_S^3PM_S^2}{2\pi G}.}
$$

Without the edge-on assumption, a factor $\sin^3i$ multiplies the numerator, explaining the minimum-mass degeneracy of the [radial-velocity method](../../../../../doppler-spectroscopy.md).
