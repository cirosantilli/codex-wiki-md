<h1 id="s2/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the [two-body problem](../../../../../two-body-problem.md), the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) lies a distance $aM_J/(M_\odot+M_J)=a/1048$ from the Sun. With $a=5.2\,\mathrm{AU}$,

$$
\boxed{r_\odot\simeq7.42\times10^8\,\mathrm m\simeq1.07R_\odot.}
$$

The relative circular-orbit period is $P=2\pi\sqrt{a^3/[G(M_\odot+M_J)]}\simeq11.85$ years. The Sun's orbital [speed](../../../../../speed.md) about that [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{v_\odot=2\pi r_\odot/P\simeq12.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

This is the space-speed amplitude; the observed [radial velocity](../../../../../radial-velocity.md) amplitude also includes the orbital inclination.
