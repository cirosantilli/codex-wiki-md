↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) for a stationary isolated system with inverse-square gravity is

$$
\boxed{2\langle K\rangle+\langle W\rangle=0,}
$$

where $K$ is total kinetic energy in the system's [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) and $W$ is its negative gravitational potential energy. The brackets denote a suitable time or ensemble average. Surface pressure, rapid evolution or mass fluxes add terms, so equilibrium and an appropriate boundary are substantive assumptions.
