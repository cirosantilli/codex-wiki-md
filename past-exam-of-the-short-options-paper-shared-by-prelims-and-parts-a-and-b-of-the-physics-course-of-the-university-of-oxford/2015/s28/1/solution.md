<h1 id="s28/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In [Bohmian mechanics](../../../../de-broglie-bohm-theory.md), the corpuscles supply an actual configuration of matter, not a further branch of the [wave function](../../../../wave-function-split.md). Their positions and the guiding wave together specify the physical state. In a common nonrelativistic spinless formulation, the wave obeys the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) while the actual configuration $Q=(Q_1,\ldots,Q_N)$ obeys

$$
\dot Q_k=\frac{\hbar}{m_k}\operatorname{Im}\frac{\nabla_k\Psi}{\Psi}(Q,t),
$$

where the expression is applied away from nodes of $\Psi$. These are coupled in the sense that the wave guides the configuration; in the standard theory the wave's Schrödinger evolution does not receive a reciprocal source term from the actual particle positions. This is not a model of classical little balls carrying every ordinary observable as a pre-existing intrinsic attribute.

The point of adding a configuration is especially clear in a measurement. A wave may evolve into several macroscopically separated pointer packets. The apparatus's actual particle configuration lies in one packet, so there is a definite pointer record even though the full wave has not undergone fundamental collapse. The corpuscles thereby connect the mathematical description with the location of actual apparatus, records and observers. [David Bohm](../../../../david-bohm.md)'s original papers provide a theory of individual processes and analyse measurement in this spirit.[1952, Physical Review 85, pages 166–179: additional variables and continuous individual dynamics](../../../../a-suggested-interpretation-of-the-quantum-theory-in-terms-of-hidden-variables-i-by-david-bohm.md)[1952, Physical Review 85, pages 180–193: measurement analysis and correlations between separated systems](../../../../a-suggested-interpretation-of-the-quantum-theory-in-terms-of-hidden-variables-ii-by-david-bohm.md)

For a subsystem, inserting the actual environment configuration into the universal wave gives a [conditional wave function](../../../../conditional-wave-function.md), schematically $\psi(x,t)\propto\Psi(x,Y(t),t)$. If the branches are suitably disjoint in the environment's configuration coordinates and remain dynamically isolated, the occupied branch supplies an [effective collapse in Bohmian mechanics](../../../../effective-collapse-in-bohmian-mechanics.md). The unoccupied branches are not simply erased by definition: before permanent separation, wave packets can recombine and affect interference. The conditional and effective wave-function constructions make this distinction precise.[sections 2.1–2.3, especially Conditional and Effective Wave Functions; conditional wave functions need not always obey an autonomous subsystem Schrödinger equation](../../../../quantum-equilibrium-and-the-role-of-operators-as-observables-in-quantum-theory-by-detlef-durr-and-others.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../born-rule.md) also needs an account. The guidance current is $j_k=(\hbar/m_k)\operatorname{Im}(\Psi^*\nabla_k\Psi)$, and both $|\Psi|^2$ and an ensemble of guided configurations satisfy the associated [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md). Hence an initial [quantum equilibrium](../../../../quantum-equilibrium.md) distribution $\rho=|\Psi|^2$ is preserved. Equivariance supports the ordinary statistical predictions, but the guidance law alone does not prove that an arbitrarily prepared ensemble has that distribution.

The corpuscles are therefore neither detectors that a conscious observer adds to the theory nor dispensable labels attached after the statistics have been calculated. They constitute a [primitive ontology](../../../../primitive-ontology.md) that provides definite configurations, trajectories and records. Whether this is preferable to a wave-only ontology is a further explanatory judgement; its empirical equivalence in quantum equilibrium does not make the two accounts identical in their claims about what exists.
