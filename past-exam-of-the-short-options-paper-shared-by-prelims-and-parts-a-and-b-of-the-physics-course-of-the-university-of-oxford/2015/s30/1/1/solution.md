<h1 id="s30/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use the relative star–planet separation and specific [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit reduced mass,

$$
\boxed{J=r^2\dot\theta.}
$$

For $r=l/(1+e\cos\theta)$, differentiation gives $\dot r=Je\sin\theta/l$, while the transverse speed is $r\dot\theta=J/r=J(1+e\cos\theta)/l$. Adding the squared orthogonal components yields

$$
\boxed{v^2=\frac{J^2}{l^2}(1+2e\cos\theta+e^2).}
$$

The [Binet equation](../../../../../binet-equation.md) for the relative two-body [inverse-square force](../../../../../inverse-square-force.md) is $u''+u=G(M+m)/J^2$, with $u=1/r$. The conic has $u''+u=1/l$, so

$$
\boxed{J^2=G(M+m)l.}
$$

The printed division by $l$ is an error: $J^2$ has dimensions $\mathrm{length^4/time^2}$, while $G(M+m)/l$ has dimensions of speed squared. A circular orbit independently checks the correction: $J^2=r^2v^2=G(M+m)r$, with $l=r$.

For a closed ellipse, $l=a(1-e^2)$ and $1+2e\cos\theta+e^2=2l/r-(1-e^2)$. Substitution now gives the [vis-viva equation](../../../../../vis-viva-equation.md),

$$
\boxed{v^2=G(M+m)\left(\frac2r-\frac1a\right).}
$$

This is relative orbital speed; either body's speed about the barycentre follows by the corresponding mass-ratio factor.
