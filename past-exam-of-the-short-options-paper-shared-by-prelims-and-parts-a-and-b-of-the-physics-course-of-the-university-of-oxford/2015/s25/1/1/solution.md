The [tropopause](../../../../../tropopause.md) is the transition between the lower, often convecting [troposphere](../../../../../troposphere.md) and the more stably stratified atmosphere above, where the decrease of temperature with altitude weakens or reverses. Its definition concerns the thermal profile, not a fixed universal altitude. An [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) is a dimensionless path-integrated extinction, $\tau_\nu=\int\kappa_\nu\rho\,ds$ for mass extinction coefficient $\kappa_\nu$; without emission into the ray its direct intensity is attenuated by $e^{-\tau_\nu}$.

In [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), the upward pressure force balances weight:

$$
\boxed{\frac{dp}{dz}=-\rho g=-\frac{gp}{R_aT(z)}.}
$$

The second equality uses the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) with specific gas constant $R_a$. For constant $T$ and $g$,

$$
p(z)=p(z_0)e^{-(z-z_0)/H},\qquad \boxed{H=R_aT/g.}
$$

For variable temperature the corresponding result is $p(z)=p(z_0)\exp[-\int_{z_0}^z g/(R_aT(z'))dz']$. The fixed-temperature derivative does not require the entire column to be isothermal.
