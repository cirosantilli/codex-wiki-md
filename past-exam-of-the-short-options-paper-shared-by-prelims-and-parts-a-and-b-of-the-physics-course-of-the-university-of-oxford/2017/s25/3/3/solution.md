<h1 id="s25/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For constant marginal annual damage $D'$ and positive [discount rate](../../../../../discount-rate.md) $r$, use the [Laplace transform](../../../../../laplace-transform.md) of the preceding equations:

$$
\widetilde C(r)=\frac{r+k}{r(r+2k)},\qquad
\boxed{\mathrm{SC\!-CO_2}=\frac{D'b_0(r+k)}{r(r+2k)(r+b_1)}
\simeq\frac{D'b_0}{2b_1}\left(\frac1r+\frac1{r+2k}\right).}
$$

The approximation uses $r\ll b_1$ but does not require choosing an order between $r$ and $k$. At zero [discount rate](../../../../../discount-rate.md) the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) diverges in this model because the pulse leaves permanent positive warming and damages remain constant per degree.
