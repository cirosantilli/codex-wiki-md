[Temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is continuous through an instantaneous carbon injection. With $T(0)=0$ and $C(0^+)=1$, $\boxed{\dot T(0^+)=b_0}$. Its initial slope is finite; the emission pulse makes the carbon burden jump but does not give this first-order thermal model an instantaneous [temperature jump](../../../../../../temperature-jump.md).
