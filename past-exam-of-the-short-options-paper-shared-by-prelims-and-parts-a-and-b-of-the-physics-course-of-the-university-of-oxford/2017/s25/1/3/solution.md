<h1 id="s25/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

[Hydrostatic balance](../../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) and the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) give $dp/dz=-p g/(R_aT)$, hence approximately $p=p_0e^{-z/H}$ with $H=R_aT/g$ for weak [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) variation. For well-mixed absorber abundance, $\rho_{\mathrm{abs}}\propto p/T$, while [pressure broadening](../../../../../pressure-broadening.md) gives $k\propto p$. Thus $k\rho_{\mathrm{abs}}\propto e^{-2z/H}$ and its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) above height $z$ has the same scale. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\tau(z)\propto e^{-z/H_\tau},\qquad H_\tau=R_aT/(2g).}
$$

The factor two depends on both [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) dependence of absorption and constant [mixing ratio](../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md); it is not the ordinary [density](../../../../../density.md) [scale height](../../../../../scale-height.md).
