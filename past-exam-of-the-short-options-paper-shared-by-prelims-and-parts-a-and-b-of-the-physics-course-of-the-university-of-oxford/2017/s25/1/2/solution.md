<h1 id="s25/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Multiplying $I'-I=-\pi B$ by $e^{-\tau}$ gives $(I e^{-\tau})'=-\pi B e^{-\tau}$. Integrating to depth $T$ yields $I(0)=I(T)e^{-T}+\pi\int_0^TB(t)e^{-t}dt$. If the first term vanishes as $T\to\infty$,

$$
\boxed{I_0=\pi\int_0^\infty B(\tau)e^{-\tau}d\tau.}
$$

Each layer emits in proportion to $B\,d\tau$ and is attenuated by its overlying [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md). The normalized exponential weighting emphasizes [optical depths](../../../../../optical-depth.md) of order unity, while retaining contributions from a range of heights.
