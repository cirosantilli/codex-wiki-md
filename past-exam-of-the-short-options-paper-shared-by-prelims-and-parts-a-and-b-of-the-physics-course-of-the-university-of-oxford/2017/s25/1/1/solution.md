↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) increases downward because $d\tau=-k\rho dz$. Along an upward ray the beam loses irradiance by absorption, as in Beer-Lambert attenuation, and gains it from local thermal emission. Kirchhoff's law equates emissivity and absorptivity in [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), giving source term $\pi B$. Thus $-dI/d\tau=-I+\pi B$, equivalently the given equation. The factor $\pi$ converts isotropic radiance into hemispheric irradiance in the stipulated diffuse approximation.
