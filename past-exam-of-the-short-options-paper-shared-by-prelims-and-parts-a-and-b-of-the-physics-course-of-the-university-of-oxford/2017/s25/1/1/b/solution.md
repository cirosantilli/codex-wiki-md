<h1 id="s25/1/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an infinitely deep [isothermal atmosphere](../../../../../../isothermal-atmosphere.md) the bounded solution is $\boxed{I(\tau)=\pi B}$. A more general formal solution contains $Ce^\tau$, excluded by the bounded deep [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md). Emission exactly replenishes absorption, so the emergent [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-topology.md) is blackbody in this idealized wavelength-by-wavelength thermal model.
