<h1 id="s25/1/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With negligible atmospheric emission, $dI/d\tau=I$. If the warm surface is at $\tau_s$, $\boxed{I(\tau)=I_s e^{-(\tau_s-\tau)}}$. Irradiance decreases as the beam travels upward to smaller $\tau$, and $I(0)=I_s e^{-\tau_s}$. The positive [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) with respect to downward [optical depth](../../../../../../optical-depth.md) is consistent with attenuation, not amplification along propagation.
