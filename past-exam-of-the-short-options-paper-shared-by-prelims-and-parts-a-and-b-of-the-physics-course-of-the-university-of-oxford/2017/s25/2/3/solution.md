<h1 id="s25/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

At a fixed surface warming, a smaller [lapse rate](../../../../../lapse-rate-atmosphere.md) makes the upper troposphere warmer than in an unchanged-lapse profile. Its emission to space therefore increases, giving a [negative feedback](../../../../../negative-feedback.md). [Water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md) has the opposite effect under the given coefficients. The net restoring coefficient is $\lambda_P-2+0.5\simeq2.24$ W m$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$, so $\boxed{\Delta T\simeq1.65\ \mathrm K}$. This calculation uses the same $S_0$ and linearized Planck model as the preceding part.
