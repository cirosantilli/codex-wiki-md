<h1 id="s25/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Effective emission [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) satisfies $\sigma T_e^4=S_0(1-\alpha)/4$: absorbed sunlight over the planetary disc balances radiation over the whole sphere. Thus $T_e=[S_0(1-\alpha)/(4\sigma)]^{1/4}$. Taking $S_0=1360$ W m$^{-2}$ gives about 254.5 K. If only a uniform atmospheric/[surface temperature](../../../../../surface-temperature.md) shift is allowed, the blackbody response coefficient is $\lambda_P=4\sigma T_e^3\simeq3.74$ W m$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta T\simeq3.7/\lambda_P\simeq0.99\ \mathrm K.}
$$

The surface and troposphere shift together under this fixed-profile approximation; their absolute [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) need not equal $T_e$. A more detailed spectral [Planck feedback](../../../../../planck-feedback.md) requires additional information not supplied by the simple model.
