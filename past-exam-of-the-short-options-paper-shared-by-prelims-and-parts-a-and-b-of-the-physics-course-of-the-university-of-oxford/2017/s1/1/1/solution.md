<h1 id="s1/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Complex differentiability](../../../../../complex-differentiability.md) gives $u_x=v_y$, $u_y=-v_x$. Differentiating once more, for [continuously differentiable](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) first [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) with the needed [second derivatives](../../../../../second-derivative.md), yields $u_{xx}+u_{yy}=v_{yx}-v_{xy}=0$ and $v_{xx}+v_{yy}=-u_{yx}+u_{xy}=0$. Thus both components satisfy [Laplace's equation](../../../../../laplace-equation.md). The result is local on the domain of [holomorphy](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md); it makes no assertion across a [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md).
