<h1 id="s1/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) use $0<\theta<\pi$, and interpret the source's [arctangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) as this [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md). Then $V=(V_0/\pi)\operatorname{Im}(z\operatorname{Log}z)$ is [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md), so $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$ has zero curl and divergence where there is no [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md). On the positive boundary $\theta\to0$ and $y\log r\to0$; on the negative boundary $\theta\to\pi$. Hence the two [boundary values](../../../../../boundary-value.md) are respectively zero and $V_0x$.

**Branch qualification:** the principal real [arctangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) alone gives zero on _both_ sides of $y=0$, contradicting the stated negative-axis [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), for example at $x=-1$. The continuous upper-half-plane [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) repairs this notation; it is not an optional [additive constant](../../../../../additive-constant.md).
