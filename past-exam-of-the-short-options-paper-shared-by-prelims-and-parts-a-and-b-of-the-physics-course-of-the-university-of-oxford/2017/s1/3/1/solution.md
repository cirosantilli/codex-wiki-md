↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a [meromorphic function](../../../../../meromorphic-function.md) with no [poles](../../../../../pole.md) on a positively oriented closed [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md), the [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) equals $2\pi i$ times the sum of enclosed [residues](../../../../../residue.md), weighted by [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md) for a general [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md). At a [simple pole](../../../../../simple-pole.md) $a$, the [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is $\lim_{z\to a}(z-a)f(z)$. [Analyticity](../../../../../analyticity.md) on the [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) and the absence of other interior singularities are necessary hypotheses.
