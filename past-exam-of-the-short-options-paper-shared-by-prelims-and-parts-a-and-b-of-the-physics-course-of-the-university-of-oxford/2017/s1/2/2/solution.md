↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The large circle contributes $O((\log R)^n/R)$ and the small circle contributes $O(\epsilon|\log\epsilon|^n)$, so both disappear. The enclosed [simple poles](../../../../../simple-pole.md) are $i$ and $-i$, with [logarithms](../../../../../logarithm.md) $i\pi/2$ and $3i\pi/2$. Their [residues](../../../../../residue.md) are $(i\pi/2)^n/(2i)$ and $-(3i\pi/2)^n/(2i)$. The positive orientation of the keyhole therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_\Gamma\frac{(\operatorname{Log}z)^n}{1+z^2}\,dz
=\pi^{n+1}(i/2)^n(1-3^n).}
$$

The upper bank runs outward and the lower bank inward. Their [logarithms](../../../../../logarithm.md) differ by $2\pi i$; keeping that orientation is essential for the sign.
