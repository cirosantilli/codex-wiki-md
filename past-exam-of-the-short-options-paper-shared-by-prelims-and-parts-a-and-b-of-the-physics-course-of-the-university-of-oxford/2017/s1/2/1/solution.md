A [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md) obstructs a single-valued [analytic continuation](../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) around a closed circuit. Continuing $\log z=\log|z|+i\theta$ once around zero increases its value by $2\pi i$. A [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) removes a curve joining [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md) or extending to infinity so that a continuous [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) can be chosen on the remainder. Zero and infinity are [logarithmic branch points](../../../../../logarithmic-branch-point.md); the positive-axis cut used here selects $0<\arg z<2\pi$. The cut is a choice of representation, not a line of [poles](../../../../../pole.md) of the multivalued [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md).
