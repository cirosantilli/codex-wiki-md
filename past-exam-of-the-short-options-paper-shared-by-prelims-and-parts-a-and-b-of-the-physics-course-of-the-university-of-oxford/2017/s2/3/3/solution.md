↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The matter rest-energy [density](../../../../../density.md) is $\rho_m c^2\simeq2.25\times10^{-10}$ J m$^{-3}$, whereas the given [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) is $8.01\times10^{-14}$ J m$^{-3}$. Their present ratio is about $2.8\times10^3$. Matter scales as $(1+z)^3$ and freely redshifting radiation as $(1+z)^4$, so

$$
\boxed{1+z_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{\rho_{m0}c^2}{u_{\gamma0}}\simeq2.8\times10^3.}
$$

This compares matter specifically with the supplied CMB [photons](../../../../../photon.md). Adding relativistic neutrinos changes the total radiation equality redshift; it is not included in these data.
