<h1 id="s2/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Set the spatial-curvature constant to zero in the stipulated matter-only [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) to get $\rho_c=3H^2/(8\pi G)$. Conservation of [pressureless matter](../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) with $R(t_0)=1$ gives $\rho=\rho_0/R^3$, and therefore

$$
\dot R^2=\frac{8\pi G\rho_0}{3R},\qquad R^{3/2}=\frac32\sqrt{\frac{8\pi G\rho_0}{3}}\,t.
$$

Choosing the [Big Bang](../../../../../big-bang.md) as $t=0$ yields $R=(t/t_0)^{2/3}$ and $H=2/(3t)$. Consequently $\boxed{t_0=2/(3H_0)\simeq9.3\ \mathrm{Gyr}}$ for the supplied $H_0$. This is the age of the flat pressureless model without a [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), not a determination in the current multi-component cosmological model.
