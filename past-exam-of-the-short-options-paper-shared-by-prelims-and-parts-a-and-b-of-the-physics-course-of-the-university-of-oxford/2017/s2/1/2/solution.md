<h1 id="s2/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Circular edge-on motion gives $V_s=2\pi a_s/P$ and center-of-mass balance gives $a_s=aM_p/(M_s+M_p)$. Eliminate $a$ using the preceding period relation:

$$
\frac{M_p^3}{(M_s+M_p)^2}=\frac{PV_s^3}{2\pi G}.
$$

Thus $\boxed{M_p^3\simeq PV_s^3M_s^2/(2\pi G)}$ when $M_p\ll M_s$. For a non-edge-on [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) the measured [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) instead determines $M_p\sin i$ to this accuracy; neglecting eccentricity is also part of the stipulated model.
