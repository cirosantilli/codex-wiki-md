The [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) is defined by $\boxed{L=4\pi R_*^2\sigma T_{\mathrm{eff}}^4}$, with [bolometric luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) $L$, photospheric [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $R_*$ and [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md) $\sigma$. It is the blackbody [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) giving the same total [surface flux](../../../../../flux-integral.md), not a claim that each [spectral line](../../../../../spectral-line.md) forms at that [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
