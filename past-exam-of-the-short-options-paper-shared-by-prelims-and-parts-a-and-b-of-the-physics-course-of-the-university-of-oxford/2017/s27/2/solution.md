<h1 id="s27/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Special relativity](../../../../special-relativity-split.md) supplies a tenseless mathematical representation: events have [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) relations, proper-time intervals and causal order whether or not a coordinate labels one event “now.” For [spacelike separated](../../../../spacelike-separation.md) events, different [inertial frames](../../../../inertial-frame.md) disagree about simultaneity and temporal order. The invariant structure therefore does not select a global present consisting of all and only events simultaneous with an observer's current event.

That supports an [argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) for [eternalism](../../../../eternalism-philosophy-of-time.md) if one adds that what exists should not depend on an arbitrary [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) and that no extra preferred foliation is available. On those assumptions, restricting existence to a frame-dependent simultaneity plane looks unjustified. A [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) account can instead represent an observer's temporal location as it represents spatial location, without introducing an objective moving present.

The conclusion is not a formal derivation of metaphysics from the [Lorentz transformations](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) alone. Relativity establishes the metric and its symmetries, not a complete semantics for existence, becoming or experience. A presentist could add a preferred but empirically hidden slicing, though this adds structure unsupported by ordinary relativistic predictions. Local becoming along a worldline or causal [partial orders](../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) are other possibilities, and do not reinstate a unique invariant global simultaneity relation.

A tenseless description also does not erase every temporal distinction. Timelike-separated events retain invariant order, [light cones](../../../../light-cone.md) distinguish causal possibilities, and clocks measure nonzero proper durations. The absence of a global privileged “now” does not identify past and future dynamically or explain the thermodynamic arrow. Relativity strongly constrains a global present and makes a block representation natural; whether that representation exhausts temporal reality requires additional philosophical premises.
