<h1 id="s27/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [coordinate transformation](../../../../../smooth-transition-map.md) considered solely as a relabeling of events has no independent observable outcome: changing labels changes neither a detector record nor a [clock](../../../../../clock.md) comparison. But the [Lorentz transformations](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) also express substantive relations between operationally realized inertial coordinates. With clocks and rulers specified, their velocity-dependent [time dilation](../../../../../time-dilation.md), length relations and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) composition make testable claims about how material systems and electromagnetic signals behave.

The distinction matters for [synchronization](../../../../../synchronization-computing.md). One-way coordinate light [speed](../../../../../speed.md) depends on how distant clocks are synchronized; a conventional alteration changes the displayed transformation law while leaving two-way timing observations unchanged. It would therefore be incorrect to regard every coefficient in an unspecified coordinate formula as a separately measurable physical fact. Testable content comes from the transformation together with the protocol and dynamical behavior of the systems implementing the coordinates.

Tests can compare [clock](../../../../../clock.md) [proper times](../../../../../proper-time.md), decay lifetimes or [resonance](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) behavior across [relative motion](../../../../../relative-motion.md) and orientation. Agreement supports [Lorentz-invariant](../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) kinematics and the associated matter dynamics over the range tested; it does not prove exact invariance at every possible scale. A rival theory with an undetectable preferred frame can reproduce the same observations by changing its explanatory commitments. [Empirical equivalence](../../../../../empirical-equivalence.md) of that rival does not make the observable [clock](../../../../../clock.md) relations untestable.

Thus the answer is qualified: transformations as passive mathematical changes are not experiments, while Lorentzian relations among specified measuring procedures have substantial empirical consequences. Separating these roles avoids both treating coordinate convention as a measured [force](../../../../../force-split.md) and declaring the entire relativistic structure immune to evidence.
