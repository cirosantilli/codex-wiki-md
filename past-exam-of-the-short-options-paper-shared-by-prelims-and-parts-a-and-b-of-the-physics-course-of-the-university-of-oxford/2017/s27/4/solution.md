<h1 id="s27/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[Newton's first law](../../../../newton-s-first-law.md) says that an unforced body maintains uniform straight-line motion in an [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md). It can function partly as a criterion for identifying [inertial frames](../../../../inertial-frame.md), but that does not automatically empty it of empirical content. The substantive claim is that there is a common class of frames in which independently isolated bodies have this behavior, rather than needing a different accelerated frame for each trajectory.

Testing requires auxiliary judgments about [forces](../../../../force-split.md) and measurement. If every unexpected [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) is redescribed as an unknown [force](../../../../force-split.md), or if a new [coordinate system](../../../../coordinate-system.md) is tailored to every body, the law becomes insulated by construction. In practice [forces](../../../../force-split.md) are investigated independently through interactions, shielding, environmental changes and comparison among bodies. Reducing [drag](../../../../drag-physics.md) and varying external influences allow extrapolation toward the ideal. Mutually consistent force-free trajectories and the success of the broader [dynamical system](../../../../dynamical-system.md) then support the inertial-frame hypothesis.

A simple counter to the claim that any coordinate choice suffices is a rotating laboratory: free bodies generally have velocity-dependent [Coriolis accelerations](../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) and cannot all be made uniformly rectilinear by calling that [rotating frame](../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md) inertial while retaining the same [force](../../../../force-split.md) account. Conversely, no finite experiment establishes complete isolation or exact uniform motion forever. The law is tested as part of a network of [force laws](../../../../force-law.md), geometrical assumptions and [clock](../../../../clock.md)/ruler procedures, and may have local rather than global validity in a gravitational theory.

The first law is therefore neither an independently observed slogan with no definitions nor a mere tautology. Its definition-like role fixes a useful framework, while its applicability and the consistency of that framework across systems are answerable to experience.[Axioms or Laws of Motion, Law I and Corollary V](../../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)
