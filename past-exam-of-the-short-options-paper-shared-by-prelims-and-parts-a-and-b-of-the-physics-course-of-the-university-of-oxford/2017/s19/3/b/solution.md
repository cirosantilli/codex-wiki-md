<h1 id="s19/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The supplied [density](../../../../../density.md) is $4\times10^{24}$ m$^{-3}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_p\simeq1.13\times10^{14}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\quad \lambda_p=2\pi c/\omega_p\simeq16.7\ \mu\mathrm m.}
$$

The cold relativistic field scale is $E_0=m_ec\omega_p/e\simeq1.92\times10^{11}$ V m$^{-1}$, corresponding to an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) $\boxed{eE_0\simeq192\ \mathrm{GeV\,m^{-1}}}$. It is an upper scale for the stipulated large-amplitude [wave](../../../../../wave-split.md), not proof that the particular weak driver produces that field.
