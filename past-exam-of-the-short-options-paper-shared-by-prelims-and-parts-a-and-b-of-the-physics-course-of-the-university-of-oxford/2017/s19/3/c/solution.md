<h1 id="s19/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The laser driver should occupy at most roughly half a [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) period to excite the accelerating [density](../../../../../density.md) response effectively. Interpreting pulse length as full significant spatial duration gives $\boxed{\ell_{\max}\sim\lambda_p/2\simeq8.35\ \mu\mathrm m,\quad\tau_{\max}\sim27.8\ \mathrm{fs}}$. If the source's $\sigma$ denotes the rms width of a Gaussian envelope instead, the resonant scale is commonly $k_p\sigma\sim1$, or $\sigma\sim2.66\ \mu$m and $8.9$ fs; full duration and rms width cannot be assigned the same numerical value without a pulse-shape convention. The robust condition is a sub-plasma-period pulse.
