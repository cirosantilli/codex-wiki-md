<h1 id="s19/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md), $a_0=eE_L/(m_ec\omega_0)$ and $I=\epsilon_0cE_L^2/2$. Substitution gives $a_0\simeq0.855\lambda_{\mu\mathrm m}\sqrt{I/(10^{18}\ \mathrm{W\,cm^{-2}})}\simeq0.64$. Thus the specified peak [electron](../../../../../electron.md) quiver motion is at most mildly relativistic, not highly relativistic. Its quiver [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is of order $\sqrt{1+a_0^2}\simeq1.19$. The supplied 40 TW fixes a focal area through $P/I$, but does not increase $a_0$ once the intensity is specified. This exposes a limitation of assuming a strongly nonlinear, fully wavebreaking wake in the preceding estimates.
