<h1 id="s19/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a periodic uncoupled lattice, $\operatorname{tr}M=2\cos\mu$ in the Twiss representation. Here $\cos\mu=1-L_1L_2q^2/2$, so bounded nondegenerate cell motion requires

$$
\boxed{0<L_1L_2q^2<4,\qquad |\!\int G\,ds|<\frac{2B\rho}{\sqrt{L_1L_2}}.}
$$

The zero-strength endpoint is only marginal and usually gives a drifting [Jordan block](../../../../../jordan-block.md). The phase advance increases from zero to $\pi$ on the positive-strength stable branch. Repeating the cell $N$ times gives ring [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $e^{\pm iN\mu}$ and tune $N\mu/(2\pi)$; it does not enlarge the stable strength interval. Integer ring phase advances can be resonance-sensitive in the presence of errors even when the ideal repeated matrix is bounded.
