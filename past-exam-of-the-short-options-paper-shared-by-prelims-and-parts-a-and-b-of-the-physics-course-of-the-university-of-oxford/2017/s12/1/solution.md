[DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is a directional polymer with a sugar-phosphate backbone linked by [phosphodiester bonds](../../../../phosphodiester-bond.md). Its [deoxyribose](../../../../deoxyribose.md) sugars carry [adenine](../../../../adenine.md), [thymine](../../../../thymine.md), [guanine](../../../../guanine.md) or [cytosine](../../../../cytosine.md). In the usual cellular duplex, antiparallel strands form a double helix with complementary A–T and G–C pairing, stabilized by [base stacking](../../../../base-stacking.md) as well as [hydrogen bonding](../../../../hydrogen-bonding.md). The sequence can vary without changing the basic backbone, allowing a large information capacity. Complementarity provides a template for copying and repair; the two strands are not merely two redundant lists stored without a copying mechanism. Major and [minor grooves](../../../../minor-groove.md) permit [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) to recognize sequence-dependent chemical patterns. Eukaryotic [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md) package [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) around [histones](../../../../histone.md), while bacterial [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md) are usually supercoiled and associated with other [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md); not all cellular [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is nuclear or linear.

[RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) uses [ribose](../../../../ribose.md) with a reactive 2-prime hydroxyl and usually [uracil](../../../../uracil.md) in place of [thymine](../../../../thymine.md). It is often a single chain, but intramolecular complementary segments form stems, loops and elaborate three-dimensional folds. [Messenger RNA](../../../../messenger-rna.md) conveys coding information to [ribosomes](../../../../ribosome.md); [transfer RNA](../../../../transfer-rna.md) combines an [anticodon](../../../../anticodon.md) with an amino-acid attachment site; [ribosomal RNA](../../../../ribosomal-rna.md) has structural and catalytic roles in [translation](../../../../translation-geometry.md). Regulatory and catalytic [RNAs](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) show why it is inadequate to describe [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) solely as a transient [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) copy. [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)'s local pairing and folding let sequence encode both information and molecular machinery. [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)'s chemical stability and duplex repair make it especially suitable for long-term inheritance.

[Replication](../../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md) is highly faithful but imperfect. Heritable sequence changes, [recombination](../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) and [gene duplication](../../../../gene-duplication.md) create variation, and selection, [Genetic drift](../../../../genetic-drift.md) and population history determine its spread. Thus the same chemistry explains both continuity of lineages and their divergence; [mutation](../../../../mutation.md) is not directed toward what an organism needs. Conserved genes, homologous sequences and the widely shared [genetic code](../../../../genetic-code.md) support common ancestry. [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) catalysis makes an early RNA-based stage of [evolution](../../../../evolution-split.md) a plausible hypothesis, not a direct historical observation or a deduction that all modern [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) preceded [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in a demonstrated sequence. Structure constrains function and evolutionary possibilities without uniquely determining a species' history.[Chapter 4, The Structure and Function of DNA; Chapter 6, RNA structure and translation](../../../../molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)
