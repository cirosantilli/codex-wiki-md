<h1 id="s12/3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Cells store usable [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) in chemical disequilibria: reduced carbon compounds, [redox](../../../../../redox.md) carriers such as [NADH](../../../../../nadh.md), [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) relative to [ADP](../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md) and [inorganic phosphate](../../../../../inorganic-phosphate.md), [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [concentration gradients](../../../../../concentration-gradient.md) and [membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md). These reservoirs are maintained away from [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md); the useful quantity is the chemical or [electrochemical potential](../../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) difference, not simply a bond's isolated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Some ATP-consuming reactions can be driven because a coupled overall reaction decreases [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), even when the uncoupled synthesis would not.

Oxidation of nutrients transfers reducing equivalents to [electron carriers](../../../../../electron-carrier.md) and then to an [electron-transport chain](../../../../../electron-transport-chain.md). Coupled [proton](../../../../../proton.md) pumping converts part of the [redox](../../../../../redox.md) free-energy drop into a [protonmotive force](../../../../../proton-motive-force.md). [ATP synthase](../../../../../atp-synthase.md) can convert that [force](../../../../../force-split.md) into [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), and [ATP hydrolysis](../../../../../atp-hydrolysis.md) drives [biosynthesis](../../../../../biosynthesis.md), transport and mechanical motion. [Substrate-level phosphorylation](../../../../../substrate-level-phosphorylation.md) is another [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) source; photosynthetic cells can initially supply the disequilibrium with absorbed light. These are flows with unavoidable dissipation and [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) release, not independent energy-creation processes. A reservoir is depleted unless metabolism replenishes it.
