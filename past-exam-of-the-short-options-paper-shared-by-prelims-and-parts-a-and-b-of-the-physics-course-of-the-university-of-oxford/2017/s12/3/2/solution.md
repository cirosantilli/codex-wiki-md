<h1 id="s12/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The cylindrical part has length 2 micrometres and [radius](../../../../../radius.md) 0.5 micrometres. Including both hemispherical ends gives area $A=2\pi rL+4\pi r^2=3\pi\times10^{-12}$ m$^2$. The [membrane capacitance](../../../../../membrane-capacitance.md) is $C=\epsilon_0\epsilon_rA/d\simeq4.17\times10^{-14}$ F. Define the signed inward electrochemical drop per unit positive [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) as

$$
\boxed{\Delta p=(\psi_{\mathrm{in}}-\psi_{\mathrm{out}})-\frac{2.303k_BT}{e}(\mathrm{pH}_{\mathrm{in}}-\mathrm{pH}_{\mathrm{out}}).}
$$

There is a factor-of-two ambiguity in the wording of the [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) data. If the stated excess means the inner [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $Q=-40000e$, then $\Delta\psi=Q/C=-0.154$ V and $\Delta p\simeq-0.183$ V at 298 K. If “inside minus outside” is taken literally as $Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}}=-40000e$ for opposite [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) [charges](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md), the inner plate has $-20000e$, giving $\Delta\psi=-0.0768$ V and $\Delta p\simeq-0.106$ V. The latter is the literal charge-difference interpretation; the former is the commonly intended excess-charge convention. In either case inward [proton](../../../../../proton.md) motion is favorable, and the positive available driving magnitude is $-\Delta p$.
