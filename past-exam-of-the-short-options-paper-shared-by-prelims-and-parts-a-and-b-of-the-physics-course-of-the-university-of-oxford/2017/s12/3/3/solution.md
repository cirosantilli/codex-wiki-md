<h1 id="s12/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

An [electron-transport chain](../../../../../electron-transport-chain.md) couples favorable [redox](../../../../../redox.md) transfers to pumping [protons](../../../../../proton.md) outward, producing the [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) and concentration terms of the [protonmotive force](../../../../../proton-motive-force.md). The [membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md)'s low [proton](../../../../../proton.md) permeability prevents immediate equilibration. The membrane-embedded $F_o$ part of [ATP synthase](../../../../../atp-synthase.md) provides a controlled return pathway: protonation and deprotonation of rotor sites couple [proton](../../../../../proton.md) flow to rotation of the c-ring and central shaft. The soluble $F_1$ catalytic head has sites that change affinity and conformation as this shaft turns. [ADP](../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md) and [phosphate](../../../../../phosphate.md) bind, [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) forms, and the conformational cycle permits [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) release; the mechanical coupling is essential because [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) synthesis is thermodynamically uphill under cellular concentrations.

If $n_H$ [protons](../../../../../proton.md) traverse the machine per [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md), reversibly available [work](../../../../../work.md) requires $n_He(-\Delta p)\geq\Delta G_{\mathrm{ATP}}$, with extra dissipation and [transport costs](../../../../../transport-cost-function.md) in a real cell. Ring stoichiometry makes $n_H$ organism dependent, rather than a universal integer inferred from the [membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) alone. [ATP synthase](../../../../../atp-synthase.md) is reversible: sufficient [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) can drive reverse rotation and [proton](../../../../../proton.md) pumping. Uncoupling agents dissipate the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) without corresponding [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) production, illustrating that respiration alone does not guarantee phosphorylation.[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps: chemiosmotic coupling and ATP synthase](../../../../../molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)
