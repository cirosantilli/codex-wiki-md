<h1 id="s12/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Let $U(x)$ be the static potential and $\gamma$ the [drag coefficient](../../../../../drag-coefficient.md), so terminal [Genetic drift](../../../../../genetic-drift.md) under [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $-U'$ is $v=-U'/\gamma$. The [probability current](../../../../../probability-flux.md) is $J=-DU'p/(k_BT)$ only after imposing the desired relation; instead start independently from $J=-Dp'-U'p/\gamma$. At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) $p\propto e^{-U/(k_BT)}$ and [detailed balance](../../../../../detailed-balance.md) requires $J=0$. Substitution yields

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{k_BT}{\gamma}=\frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta a}.}
$$

At 298 K with $a=0.5\ \mu$m, this gives approximately $4.37\times10^{-13}$ m$^2$ s$^{-1}$. The [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) assumes an overdamped particle in an [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) thermal bath, rather than arbitrary driven [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md).
