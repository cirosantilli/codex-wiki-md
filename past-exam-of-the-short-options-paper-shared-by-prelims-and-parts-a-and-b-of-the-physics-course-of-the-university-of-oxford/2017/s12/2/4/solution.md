<h1 id="s12/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

In a sufficiently [uniform flow](../../../../../uniform-flow.md) the [Stokes force](../../../../../stokes-s-law.md) is $F=\gamma v$ toward trap 2. Its [work](../../../../../work.md) over separation $d$ lowers the second effective well by $Fd$. Equal effective free energies require $\gamma vd=k_BT\log2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{v=\frac{k_BT\log2}{6\pi\eta a d}\simeq3.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}
$$

at 298 K. The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) was not specified, so the numerical result scales as $T/298\ \mathrm K$. This approximation treats a weak uniform [drag](../../../../../drag-physics.md) as a constant tilt and neglects changes in barrier shape and [hydrodynamic interactions](../../../../../hydrodynamic-interaction.md) with boundaries.
