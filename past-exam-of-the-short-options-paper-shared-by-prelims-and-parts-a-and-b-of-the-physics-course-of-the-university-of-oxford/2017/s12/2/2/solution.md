<h1 id="s12/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Write the displacement after $m$ independent isotropic zero-mean steps as $\mathbf R=\sum_j\mathbf s_j$. Cross terms vanish, giving $\boxed{\langle R^2\rangle=m\Delta}$ when $\Delta=\langle|\mathbf s|^2\rangle$. [Isotropy](../../../../../isotropy.md) makes each Cartesian [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) one third of this. Since $m\simeq kt$ and $\langle R^2\rangle=6Dt$, $\boxed{D=k\Delta/6}$. If “[step size](../../../../../step-size.md)” were instead defined as a single-coordinate mean square, the corresponding expression would be $k\Delta/2$; the factor of three is purely the stated three-dimensional convention.
