<h1 id="s3/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Inside the well, $\psi''+k^2\psi=0$. Vanishing at zero removes the cosine term; vanishing at $L$ requires $k_nL=n\pi$. The nonzero states have [positive integer](../../../../../positive-integer.md) $n$ because $n=0$ gives the [zero function](../../../../../zero-function.md) and negative $n$ only repeats a state up to sign. [Normalization](../../../../../normalization.md) follows from $\int_0^L\sin^2(n\pi x/L)dx=L/2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\psi_n=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(n\pi x/L),\qquad E_n=\hbar^2\pi^2n^2/(2mL^2).}
$$

The [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanishes outside the infinite barriers, so there is no additional contribution to its [norm](../../../../../norm.md).
