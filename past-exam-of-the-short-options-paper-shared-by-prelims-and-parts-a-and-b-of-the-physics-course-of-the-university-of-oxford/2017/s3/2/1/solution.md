↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a stationary nonrelativistic state, $\boxed{[-\hbar^2\nabla^2/(2m)+V(\mathbf r)]\psi=E\psi}$. The [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\psi$ is a [probability amplitude](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md), $m$ the particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and $E$ its [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md); the [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) $V$ is measured in the same [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) units. In one dimension the [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md) becomes $d^2/dx^2$.
