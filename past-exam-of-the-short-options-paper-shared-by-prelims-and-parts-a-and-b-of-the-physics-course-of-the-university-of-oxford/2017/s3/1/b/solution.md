<h1 id="s3/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The emitted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_3-E_2=\mathrm{Ry}(1/4-1/9)=5\mathrm{Ry}/36$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{36hc}{5\mathrm{Ry}}\simeq656\ \mathrm{nm}.}
$$

This is the Balmer-alpha transition in this approximation. Absorption in the reverse direction has the same [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md); [fine structure](../../../../../fine-structure.md) and [radiative corrections](../../../../../radiative-correction.md) are beyond the model.
