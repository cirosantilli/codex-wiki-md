<h1 id="s3/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitute the quantized [radius](../../../../../radius.md) in $mvr=n\hbar$ to get $v_n=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar n)$. [Coulomb potential energy](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) is $-e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r_n)$ and the stipulated virial relation makes the total one half of this value. Hence

$$
\boxed{v_n=\frac{\alpha c}{n},\qquad E_n=-\frac{me^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0)^2\hbar^2n^2}=-\frac{\mathrm{Ry}}{n^2}.}
$$

The negative sign means a [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) relative to a separated stationary [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and [electron](../../../../../electron.md).
