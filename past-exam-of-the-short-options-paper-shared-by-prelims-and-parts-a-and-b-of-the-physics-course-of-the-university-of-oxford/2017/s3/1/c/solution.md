<h1 id="s3/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Separate [center-of-mass motion](../../../../../center-of-mass-motion.md) and replace $m$ by the [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) $\mu=mM/(m+M)$ in the relative-coordinate formulas. Then $r_n$ is the proton-electron separation, [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) and the Rydberg constant scale by $\mu/m$, and transition wavelengths increase by $m/\mu$. The [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s own [orbital radius](../../../../../orbital-radius.md) about the [center of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) is $Mr_n/(M+m)$; the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) [radius](../../../../../radius.md) is $mr_n/(M+m)$. The relative [speed](../../../../../speed.md) retains $\alpha c/n$, while the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s individual [speed](../../../../../speed.md) acquires $M/(M+m)$. Replacing the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) in the [radius](../../../../../radius.md) alone but leaving energies unchanged would be inconsistent.
