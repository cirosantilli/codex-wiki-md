<h1 id="s3/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Choose the upper route for horizontal [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), the lower route for vertical [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), and absorb fixed optical phases into the path kets. The normalized output is

$$
\boxed{\frac{|H\rangle|\uparrow_1\rangle+|V\rangle|\downarrow_1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

This is [entanglement](../../../../../entangled-state.md) between two [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) of one [photon](../../../../../photon.md). Interchanging the path convention interchanges the labels but not any probabilities.
