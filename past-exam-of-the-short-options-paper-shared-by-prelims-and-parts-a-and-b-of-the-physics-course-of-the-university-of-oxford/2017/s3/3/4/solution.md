<h1 id="s3/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A [quarter-wave plate](../../../../../waveplate.md) needs a specified axis angle. If its fast axis makes angle $\eta$ to horizontal, choose its [Jones matrix](../../../../../jones-matrix.md) $Q=R(\eta)\operatorname{diag}(1,i)R(-\eta)$. Then

$$
Q|V\rangle=a|H\rangle+b|V\rangle,\quad a=(1-i)\sin\eta\cos\eta,\quad b=\sin^2\eta+i\cos^2\eta.
$$

After the preceding successful lower-path detection the total state is $(aH+bV)|\downarrow_1\rangle$. The next crystal sends it to

$$
\boxed{a|H,\uparrow_2\rangle+b|V,\downarrow_2\rangle,\quad P(\downarrow_2\mid\downarrow_1)=|b|^2.}
$$

For axes at $45^\circ$, the state before splitting is circular up to phase, $a=(1-i)/2$, $b=(1+i)/2$, and the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is $1/2$. Detection in $\downarrow_2$ always leaves vertical [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md). If no earlier lower-path measurement was actually performed, retain the untouched upper [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md): the final state is $(|H,\uparrow_1\rangle+a|H,\uparrow_2\rangle+b|V,\downarrow_2\rangle)/\sqrt2$, and the last [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is $|b|^2/2$, or $1/4$ at $45^\circ$. At axis angle zero the [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is instead one, a concrete demonstration that an unspecified orientation does not determine a unique number.
