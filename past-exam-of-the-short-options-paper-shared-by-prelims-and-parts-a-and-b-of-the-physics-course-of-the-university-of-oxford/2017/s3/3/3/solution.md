<h1 id="s3/3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Orthogonal path projectors give an upper-path [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. A successful non-destructive lower-path detection projects onto $|V\rangle|\downarrow_1\rangle$ and its conditional [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) is therefore pure vertical. Retaining the detection record removes the upper [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) for that run; it would be incorrect to continue evolving the original coherent two-path state as though the detection had not occurred.
