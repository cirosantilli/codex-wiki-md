↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a fixed propagation direction and spatial mode, a [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has two independent [transverse polarization](../../../../../transverse-polarization.md) [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Orthogonal horizontal and vertical states span its two-dimensional [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md): $\alpha|H\rangle+\beta|V\rangle$, with $|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1$. [Polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) analyzers measure bases in this space, and [phase plates](../../../../../phase-plate.md) implement transformations of its relative [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Additional spatial or [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) modes enlarge the space and must not be silently discarded.
