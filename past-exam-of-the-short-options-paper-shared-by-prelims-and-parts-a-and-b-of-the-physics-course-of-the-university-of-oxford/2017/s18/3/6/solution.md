A plausible extension **assuming [gyromagnetic ratio](../../../../../gyromagnetic-ratio.md) two** is

$$
E^2=m^2c^4+p_z^2c^2+|e|\hbar|B|c(2n+1-2m_s),\qquad m_s=-s,\ldots,s,
$$

where the spin projection is aligned with the charge-field sign. For spin one the lowest aligned mode has $E^2=m^2c^4-|e|\hbar|B|c$ at $p_z=0$. Above $|eB|>m^2c^3/\hbar$, this is negative: [linear time](../../../../../linear-time.md) [evolution](../../../../../evolution-split.md) has exponentially growing/decaying modes, signaling an instability of the assumed background.

Spin alone does not fix a [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The scalar and Dirac examples cannot establish this formula for every higher-spin particle; the stated instability belongs to the specified $g=2$ field model and its regime of applicability. Interactions and backreaction are needed to describe its eventual outcome.
