<h1 id="s18/3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Eliminate the lower component to get $(E^2-m^2c^4)\varphi=c^2(\sigma\cdot\Pi)^2\varphi$. Since $[\Pi_x,\Pi_y]=ie\hbar B/c$, the Pauli identity gives $(\sigma\cdot\Pi)^2=\Pi^2-e\hbar B\sigma_3/c$. Substitute the three [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) and rearrange:

$$
\boxed{\left[\frac{d^2}{dy^2}-\frac{(p_xc+eBy)^2}{\hbar^2c^2}+\frac{E^2-m^2c^4}{\hbar^2c^2}-\frac{p_z^2}{\hbar^2}+\frac{eB}{\hbar c}\sigma_3\right]\varphi=0.}
$$

The spin-field sign follows from the [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) convention and commutator, not an independent assignment.
