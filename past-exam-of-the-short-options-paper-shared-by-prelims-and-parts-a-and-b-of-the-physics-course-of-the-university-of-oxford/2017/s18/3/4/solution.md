<h1 id="s18/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Using $\varepsilon=\hbar\omega_c(n+1/2)$ gives the charge-explicit result

$$
\boxed{E^2=m^2c^4+p_z^2c^2+|e|\hbar|B|c(2n+1)-e\hbar Bc\,s_z.}
$$

For $B>0$ the two spin signs are equivalently $m^2c^4+2mc^2[p_z^2/(2m)+\mu B(2n+1)\pm\mu B]$. Both positive and negative [relativistic energy](../../../../../relativistic-energy.md) branches arise from this squared equation; an actual [Dirac spinor](../../../../../dirac-spinor.md) must still be reconstructed from the coupled equations, especially at exceptional values where division by $E+mc^2$ is unavailable.
