<h1 id="s18/3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Separate $x,z$ [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) in the spinless equation. The transverse operator is $-\hbar^2d^2/dy^2+(p_x+eBy/c)^2$, the same shifted oscillator without a [Pauli term](../../../../../pauli-term.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $|e|\hbar|B|(2n+1)/c$. Thus

$$
\boxed{E^2=m^2c^4+p_z^2c^2+|e|\hbar|B|c(2n+1),}
$$

which is the printed formula for $B>0$. The absence of the spin splitting is the substantive difference from the Dirac result.
