<h1 id="s18/2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The full equation rearranges to $L\psi=-U\psi$. Since a [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) has no incoming free [wave](../../../../../wave-split.md), convolution with the decaying inverse gives $\boxed{\psi(x)=-m\int e^{-\kappa|x-x'|}U(x')\psi(x')dx'/(\kappa\hbar^2)}$. Applying $L$ to this identity recovers the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) and the decay [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
