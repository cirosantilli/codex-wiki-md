<h1 id="s18/2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For $E<0$, impose decay and continuity on $G=C e^{-\kappa|x-x'|}$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jump is $-2\kappa C$, so $-\hbar^2(G'_+-G'_-)/(2m)=1$ requires

$$
\boxed{G(x,x')=\frac m{\kappa\hbar^2}e^{-\kappa|x-x'|},\quad\kappa=\sqrt{-2mE}/\hbar.}
$$

There is no nonzero free solution decaying at both infinities that can be added without spoiling the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) condition.
