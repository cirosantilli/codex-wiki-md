<h1 id="s18/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Continuity has already been built into these forms. At $b$, [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) continuity requires $-q\sin(qb)=-\kappa\cos(qb)$ for even parity and $q\cos(qb)=-\kappa\sin(qb)$ for odd parity. Thus

$$
\boxed{q\tan(qb)=\kappa\quad\text{or}\quad-q\cot(qb)=\kappa.}
$$

Use $q/\kappa=\sqrt{U_0/|E|-1}$ to obtain the two printed equations. The right side is positive, so only the corresponding allowed tangent/cotangent branches can describe a [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md); squaring without retaining this sign restriction adds spurious roots.
