<h1 id="s18/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Rewrite the real-energy [probability](../../../../../probability.md) as $T=[1+U_0^2\sin^2(\zeta a)/(4E(E+U_0))]^{-1}$. Thus $\boxed{T_{\max}=1}$ at $\zeta a=j\pi$, namely $E_j=\hbar^2j^2\pi^2/(2ma^2)-U_0\geq0$, with a strictly positive [resonance](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in the ordinary scattering problem. Between [resonances](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) it is less than one and its [oscillations](../../../../../oscillation.md) approach one as [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) increases. Generically $T(0^+)=0$; a well tuned to a zero-energy [resonance](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) instead has $T(0^+)=1$. The original transmission plot in S1 illustrates these maxima for a chosen well, not a universal specified depth.
