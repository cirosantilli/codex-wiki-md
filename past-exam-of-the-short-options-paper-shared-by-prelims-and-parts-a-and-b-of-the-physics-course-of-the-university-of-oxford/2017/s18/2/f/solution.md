<h1 id="s18/2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

On the physical sheet the bound-state [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) [poles](../../../../../pole.md) lie at $k=i\kappa_j$, $\kappa_j>0$, and map to real negative $E_j=-\hbar^2\kappa_j^2/(2m)$. The square-root map has two sheets meeting at the continuum threshold. [Analytic continuation](../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) can also have virtual-state [poles](../../../../../pole.md) on the negative imaginary $k$ axis and [resonance poles](../../../../../resonance-pole.md) in the [lower half-plane](../../../../../lower-half-plane.md); these are not normalizable [bound states](../../../../../bound-state.md). In the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) plane the [resonances](../../../../../resonance-chemistry.md) belong to the nonphysical sheet, rather than additional negative-axis bound levels.

For the algebraically continued _[probability](../../../../../probability.md)_, include the mirror [poles](../../../../../pole.md) contributed by $D(-k)$ as explained in part d; the two $k$ signs map to the same numerical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) but need not impose the same [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md). A sketch must label the sheet or the [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) being plotted, rather than treating every singularity as a [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md).
