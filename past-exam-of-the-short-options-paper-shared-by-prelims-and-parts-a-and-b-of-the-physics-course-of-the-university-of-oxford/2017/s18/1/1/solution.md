<h1 id="s18/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Away from $x=x'$, an outgoing solution has form $G=C e^{ik|x-x'|}$. Integrate the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) across $x'$ to get $G'_+-G'_-=-2m/\hbar^2$. Since the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jump is $2ikC$, $C=im/(k\hbar^2)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{G^+(x,x')=\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}e^{ik|x-x'|}.}
$$

Continuity excludes a derivative-of-delta contribution. The outgoing condition, equivalently the appropriate limiting resolvent, removes the arbitrary incoming [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md).
