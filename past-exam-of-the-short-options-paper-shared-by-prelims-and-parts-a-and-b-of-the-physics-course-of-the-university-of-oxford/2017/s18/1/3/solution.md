<h1 id="s18/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

As $x\to-\infty$, the scattered kernel is $e^{-ikx}e^{ikx'}$; as $x\to+\infty$, it is $e^{ikx}e^{-ikx'}$. Therefore $\psi_s\sim e^{ikx}+r e^{-ikx}$ on the left and $t e^{ikx}$ on the right, with

$$
r=-\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}\int e^{ikx'}U(x')\psi_s(x')dx',\quad
 t=1-\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}\int e^{-ikx'}U(x')\psi_s(x')dx'.
$$

Equal asymptotic wavenumbers give $R=|r|^2$, $T=|t|^2$. Squaring the first [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) proves the requested expression. These are current ratios, which would require a [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) factor if the two asymptotic potentials differed.
