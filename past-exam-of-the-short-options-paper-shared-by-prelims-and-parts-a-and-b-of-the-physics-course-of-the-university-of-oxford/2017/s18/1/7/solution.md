<h1 id="s18/1/7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

At the [delta potential](../../../../../delta-potential.md), the bound equation becomes $\psi(x)=\beta\psi(0)e^{-\kappa|x|}/\kappa$. A nonzero solution therefore requires $\kappa=\beta$, giving

$$
\boxed{E_b=-\frac{m\alpha^2}{2\hbar^2},\qquad\psi_b(x)=\sqrt\beta e^{-\beta|x|}.}
$$

There is one bound level. The transmission _[amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md)_ $t(k)$ has its physical-sheet [pole](../../../../../pole.md) at $k=i\beta$, which produces exactly this decaying state. The real-axis [probability](../../../../../probability.md)'s rational continuation $T(k)=k^2/(k^2+\beta^2)$ has both $+i\beta$ and $-i\beta$ [poles](../../../../../pole.md); the lower [pole](../../../../../pole.md) is not an additional [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md). It is essential to distinguish the analytic [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) from an even continuation of its modulus squared.
