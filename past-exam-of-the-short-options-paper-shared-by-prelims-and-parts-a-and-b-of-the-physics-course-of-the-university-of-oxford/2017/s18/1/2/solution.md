<h1 id="s18/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Write the full equation as $L\psi_s=-U\psi_s$. Apply the outgoing inverse of $L$ and add the incident [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) with unit left-incident [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). This gives

$$
\boxed{\psi_s(x)=e^{ikx}-\frac{im}{k\hbar^2}\int e^{ik|x-x'|}U(x')\psi_s(x')dx'.}
$$

For sufficiently short-range potentials this is the outgoing [Lippmann-Schwinger equation](../../../../../lippmann-schwinger-equation.md). The minus sign follows from the definition $L=H_0-E$, rather than $E-H_0$.
