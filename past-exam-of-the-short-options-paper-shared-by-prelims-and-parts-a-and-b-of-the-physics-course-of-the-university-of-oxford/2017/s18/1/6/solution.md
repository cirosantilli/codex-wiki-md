<h1 id="s18/1/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

For $E=-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)$, the decaying [Green's function](../../../../../green-s-function-split.md) is $m e^{-\kappa|x-x'|}/(\kappa\hbar^2)$, with the same [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jump as before. A normalizable negative-energy state has no incident homogeneous part. Applying the inverse to $L\psi=-U\psi$ gives

$$
\boxed{\psi(x)=-\frac m{\kappa\hbar^2}\int e^{-\kappa|x-x'|}U(x')\psi(x')dx'.}
$$

The condition $\kappa>0$ is necessary for decay on both sides.
