<h1 id="s16/2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

[Hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md)'s $n=2$ [excitation energy](../../../../../excitation-energy.md) is $3R/4=10.2$ eV. With electronic degeneracy ratio $g_2/g_1=4$, its relative population is $4e^{-10.2/1.60}\simeq6.8\times10^{-3}$. Thus the first excited manifold contributes below one percent of the ground population at the inferred [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), explaining the leading ground-state approximation.

There is a qualification to extending this [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) to infinitely many Rydberg states: the isolated-atom partition sum with degeneracy $2n^2$ formally diverges. [Plasma screening](../../../../../plasma-screening.md) and level dissolution impose a physical cutoff. The small low-lying populations justify the simple approximation in its intended regime, not an unrestricted infinite partition sum.
