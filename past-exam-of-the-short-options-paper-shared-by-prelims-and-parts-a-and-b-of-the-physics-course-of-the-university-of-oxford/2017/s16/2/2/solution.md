↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The measured ratio is ten, so $R/(k_BT')=8.5$. Taking $R=13.6$ eV gives $k_BT'=1.60$ eV or $T'\simeq1.86\times10^4$ K. Substitute into Saha's equation:

$$
\boxed{n_0=2\frac{(2\pi m_ek_BT')^{3/2}}{h^3}e^{-8.5}\simeq2.49\times10^{24}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}.}
$$

Higher [density](../../../../../density.md) favors [recombination](../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) and reduces the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) advantage of free [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) at a fixed ionized fraction, so a higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is required. The ratio 8.5 means half ionization does not require $k_BT$ to equal the [ionization energy](../../../../../ionization-energy.md): translational [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) and the exponential tail are important.
