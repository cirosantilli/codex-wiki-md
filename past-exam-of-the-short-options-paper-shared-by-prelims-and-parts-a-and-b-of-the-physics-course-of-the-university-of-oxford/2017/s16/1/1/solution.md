<h1 id="s16/1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A weak positive potential perturbs Boltzmann [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) as $n_e\simeq n_0[1+e\phi/(k_BT)]$ against nearly fixed [ions](../../../../../ion.md). [Poisson's equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) outside the test [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) becomes $\nabla^2\phi=\phi/\lambda_D^2$, with

$$
\boxed{\lambda_D=\sqrt{\epsilon_0k_BT/(n_ee^2)}.}
$$

The resulting potential is proportional to $e^{-r/\lambda_D}/r$. The [Debye length](../../../../../debye-length.md) is the screening distance over which [charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) imbalance is suppressed, within this classical weak-perturbation model.
