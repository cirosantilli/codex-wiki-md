The inferred parameters give $\lambda_D\simeq6.46\times10^{-11}$ m and $\boxed{N_D\simeq0.28}$, or $n_e\lambda_D^3\simeq0.067$ without the spherical factor. Neither is much larger than one. Thus a good classical weakly coupled [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) approximation is not justified. Correlations, collisions and potentially degeneracy matter at this [density](../../../../../density.md); treating the preceding 18.7 eV as a reliable [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) measurement would overstate what this simple model establishes.
