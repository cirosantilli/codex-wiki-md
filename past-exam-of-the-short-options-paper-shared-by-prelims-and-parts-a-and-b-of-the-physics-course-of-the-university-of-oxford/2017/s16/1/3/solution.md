<h1 id="s16/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Rapid longitudinal [oscillations](../../../../../oscillation.md) in a weakly collisional [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) compress the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) distribution primarily along one direction. There is insufficient time for collisions to redistribute that [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) among three directions; the effective one-dimensional [adiabatic exponent](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) is $1+2/1=3$. Ordinary monatomic-gas sound with local collisional [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has three translational [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md), giving $1+2/3=5/3$. Thus $\Gamma=3$ belongs to the stated kinetic/time-scale limit, not to every [thermodynamic process](../../../../../thermodynamic-process.md) involving ionized gas.
