<h1 id="s16/1/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) is $\rho/(9m_u)$ and double ionization gives $n_e\simeq2.48\times10^{29}$ m$^{-3}$. Therefore $\hbar\omega_p\simeq18.5$ eV. The [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md) loss gives $\hbar\omega=30$ eV, while [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
k=\frac{\sqrt{E_i^2+E_f^2-2E_iE_f\cos(\pi/4)}}{\hbar c}\simeq1.14\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$

Inverting the stipulated dispersion law gives

$$
\boxed{k_BT=\frac{m_e(\omega^2-\omega_p^2)}{3k^2}\simeq18.7\ \mathrm{eV}.}
$$

An [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) expressed “in eV” means $\hbar\omega$, not the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) itself. The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is a formal inference conditional on the dispersion model, whose validity is tested next.
