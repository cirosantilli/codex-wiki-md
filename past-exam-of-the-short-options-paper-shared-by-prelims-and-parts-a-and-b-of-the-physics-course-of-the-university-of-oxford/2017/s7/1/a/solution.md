<h1 id="s7/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) the orthogonal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components are $\dot r$, $r\dot\theta$, $\dot z$. Their squared magnitudes add, and $\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf A=\dot z A(r)$. Thus $\boxed{\mathcal L=m(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+\dot z^2)/2+q\dot z A(r)}$. Taking the curl gives $B_\theta=-A'(r)$, consistent with the prescribed field.
