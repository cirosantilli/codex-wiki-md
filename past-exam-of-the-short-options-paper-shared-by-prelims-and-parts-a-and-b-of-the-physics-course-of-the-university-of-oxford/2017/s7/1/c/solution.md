<h1 id="s7/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) cancels the velocity-linear magnetic term. Expressing velocities in terms of [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{H=\frac{p_r^2}{2m}+\frac{p_\theta^2}{2mr^2}+\frac{[p_z-qA(r)]^2}{2m}.}
$$

The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is time independent and hence conserved. It equals [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) because a static [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) does no [work](../../../../../work.md); its dependence on $A$ is through the [mechanical momentum](../../../../../mechanical-momentum.md) combination.
