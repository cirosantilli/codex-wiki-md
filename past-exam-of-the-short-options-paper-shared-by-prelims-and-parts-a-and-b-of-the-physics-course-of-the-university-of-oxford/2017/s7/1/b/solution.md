<h1 id="s7/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $\boxed{p_r=m\dot r,\ p_\theta=mr^2\dot\theta,\ p_z=m\dot z+qA(r)}$. The coordinates $\theta$ and $z$ are cyclic, so $p_\theta,p_z$ are conserved. The radial [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) generally changes. In particular the conserved axial [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) differs from the axial [mechanical momentum](../../../../../mechanical-momentum.md) by $qA$.
