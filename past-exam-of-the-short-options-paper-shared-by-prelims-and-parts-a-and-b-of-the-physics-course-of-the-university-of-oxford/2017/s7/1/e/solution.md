<h1 id="s7/1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Expand $A(r)=A(r_0)+A'(r_0)s+O(s^2)$ about $qA(r_0)=p_z$. Then $p_z-qA\simeq-qA'(r_0)s$ and [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot s^2+\Omega^2s^2=\frac{2H}{m}=\Omega^2\rho^2,\quad \rho=\frac{\sqrt{2H/m}}{|\Omega|},\quad s(t)=\rho\cos(|\Omega|t+\phi).}
$$

Here $\Omega=qA'(r_0)/m$ can be signed, but the physical [oscillation](../../../../../oscillation.md) [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) are nonnegative. The small-energy condition ensures that this local expansion samples only a narrow range about $r_0$, provided the field varies smoothly there.
