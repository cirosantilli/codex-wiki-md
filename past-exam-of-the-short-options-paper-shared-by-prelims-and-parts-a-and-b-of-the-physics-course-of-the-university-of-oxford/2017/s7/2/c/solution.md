<h1 id="s7/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substitution of the exponential ansatz gives the homogeneous [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) system

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\omega_1^2-\omega^2&2i\Omega\omega\\-2i\Omega\omega&\omega_2^2-\omega^2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\bar x_1\\\bar x_2\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

A nonzero mode requires zero [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md). Thus $\boxed{(\omega_1^2-\omega^2)(\omega_2^2-\omega^2)=4\Omega^2\omega^2}$ with the stated two effective stiffnesses. The off-diagonal product is positive, which determines the sign on the right.
