<h1 id="s7/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use rotating [basis vectors](../../../../../basis-vector.md) with $\dot{\mathbf e}_1=\Omega\mathbf e_2$, $\dot{\mathbf e}_2=-\Omega\mathbf e_1$. The inertial [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $(\dot x_1-\Omega x_2)\mathbf e_1+(\dot x_2+\Omega x_1)\mathbf e_2$. The two fixed rotating anchors are $(0,\pm a)$, so the spring lengths are $\ell_\pm=\sqrt{x_1^2+(x_2\mp a)^2}$. Combining kinetic and elastic energies gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac m2[(\dot x_1-\Omega x_2)^2+(\dot x_2+\Omega x_1)^2]-\frac k2[(\ell_+-l_0)^2+(\ell_--l_0)^2].}
$$

A constant elastic offset has no dynamical effect.
