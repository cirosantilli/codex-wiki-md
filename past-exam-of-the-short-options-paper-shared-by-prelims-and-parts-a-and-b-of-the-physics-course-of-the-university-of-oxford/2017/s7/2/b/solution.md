<h1 id="s7/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the [quadratic Lagrangian](../../../../../quadratic-lagrangian.md) with respect to coordinates and velocities. The equations are

$$
\boxed{\ddot x_1-2\Omega\dot x_2+(2k'/m-\Omega^2)x_1=0,\qquad
\ddot x_2+2\Omega\dot x_1+(2k/m-\Omega^2)x_2=0.}
$$

The velocity-cross terms are [Coriolis accelerations](../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md); moving $-\Omega^2x_i$ to the [force](../../../../../force-split.md) side identifies the outward [centrifugal acceleration](../../../../../centrifugal-acceleration.md). The remaining terms are elastic [restoring forces](../../../../../restoring-force.md). The sign pair is fixed by the rotating-basis convention in part a.
