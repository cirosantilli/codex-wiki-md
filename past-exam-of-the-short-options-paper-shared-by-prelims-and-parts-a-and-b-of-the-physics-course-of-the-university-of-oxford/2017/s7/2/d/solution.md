<h1 id="s7/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $X=\omega^2$ and $S=\omega_1^2+\omega_2^2+4\Omega^2$. Its equation is $X^2-SX+\omega_1^2\omega_2^2=0$. If both effective stiffnesses are positive, the sum and product of roots are positive and $S^2-4\omega_1^2\omega_2^2\geq0$, so both roots are positive. They can coincide in a degenerate zero-rotation isotropic case; “two” need not mean distinct.

If $\omega_1^2<0<\omega_2^2$, the product is negative, so one root is negative. Its imaginary [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) gives a growing exponential displacement: [centrifugal](../../../../../centrifugal-force.md) softening has overwhelmed one elastic restoring direction, making the central configuration unstable. [Coriolis](../../../../../coriolis-force.md) coupling mixes the two coordinates but does not remove this negative-product instability.
