↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $f=F(H)$ with no explicit time dependence, $\partial_t f=0$ and $[H,F(H)]=F'(H)[H,H]=0$. It therefore solves the stationary [transport equation](../../../../../transport-equation-split.md). Normalizability and the accessible [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) region still have to be checked for a proposed [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md).
