<h1 id="s7/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) define phase-space [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $(\dot q_i,\dot p_i)=(H_{p_i},-H_{q_i})$. Its divergence is

$$
\sum_i(\partial_{q_i}\dot q_i+\partial_{p_i}\dot p_i)=\sum_i(H_{q_ip_i}-H_{p_iq_i})=0.
$$

Thus infinitesimal [phase-space volumes](../../../../../symplectic-volume-form.md) are preserved, assuming sufficient differentiability. This is [Liouville's theorem](../../../../../liouville-theorem.md); spatial [density](../../../../../density.md) alone need not be incompressible because the statement concerns the combined coordinate-momentum space.
