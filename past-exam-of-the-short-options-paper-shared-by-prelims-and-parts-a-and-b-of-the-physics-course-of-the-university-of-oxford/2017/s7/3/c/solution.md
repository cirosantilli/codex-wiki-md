<h1 id="s7/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Conservation of [probability](../../../../../probability.md) obeys $\partial_t f+\nabla_{q,p}\cdot(f\mathbf v)=0$. Expand the divergence and use incompressibility to obtain $\partial_t f+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla_{q,p}f=0$, or $\boxed{df/dt=0}$. This is fine-grained transport for [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) trajectories without sources or collisions. Coarse graining can obscure this constancy without contradicting it.
