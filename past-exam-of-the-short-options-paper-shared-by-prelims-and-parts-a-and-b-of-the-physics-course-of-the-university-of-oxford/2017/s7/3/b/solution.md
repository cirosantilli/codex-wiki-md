<h1 id="s7/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

To match the source's order define $[a,b]=\sum_i(a_{p_i}b_{q_i}-a_{q_i}b_{p_i})$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) and [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) then give

$$
\boxed{\frac{dg}{dt}=g_t+[H,g].}
$$

Antisymmetry implies $[H,H]=0$, hence $dH/dt=H_t$, which vanishes for a time-independent [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md). With the alternative common convention $\{a,b\}=\sum_i(a_{q_i}b_{p_i}-a_{p_i}b_{q_i})$, the same law is $dg/dt=g_t+\{g,H\}$; mixing the two orders would reverse the physical [time evolution](../../../../../time-evolution.md).
