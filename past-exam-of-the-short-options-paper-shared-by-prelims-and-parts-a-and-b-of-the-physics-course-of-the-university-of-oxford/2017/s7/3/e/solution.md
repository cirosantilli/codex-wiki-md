<h1 id="s7/3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Axisymmetry supplies conserved $p_\theta$ in addition to conserved [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Consequently $\boxed{f=F(H,p_\theta)}$ is a more general [stationary solution](../../../../../stationary-solution.md), since both [arguments](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) are constant on every trajectory. Additional independent [integrals](../../../../../integral-split.md) may permit still more general distributions. This is a sufficient construction, not a claim that every [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) must have exactly these two [arguments](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md).
