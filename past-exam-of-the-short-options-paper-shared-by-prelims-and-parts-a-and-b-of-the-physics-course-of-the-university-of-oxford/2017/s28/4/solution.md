<h1 id="s28/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[Quantum mechanics](../../../../quantum-mechanics-split.md) has a highly successful predictive calculus, but its standard informal presentation does not transparently supply one consistent physical description of all processes. An [isolated system](../../../../isolated-system.md) evolves unitarily, while a measurement is often said to select one outcome stochastically. If both the apparatus and the system are physical and governed by the same [unitary](../../../../unitary-connection.md) dynamics, their interaction generally produces an entangled [superposition](../../../../superposition-principle.md) of records. Explaining how this relates to an experienced definite result is the [measurement problem](../../../../measurement-problem.md).

For a limited laboratory task, an operational rule can assign probabilities to recorded outcomes without first resolving every ontological question. In that pragmatic sense, a calculation need not await agreement on interpretation. But specifying what counts as an intervention, a record and an outcome still supplies a relation between the mathematics and practice; calling this “no interpretation” may merely leave it implicit.

The need becomes sharper in cosmology or a universal theory, where an external classical observer cannot simply be assumed. Distinct responses change what is physically asserted: collapse theories alter dynamics, [Bohmian mechanics](../../../../de-broglie-bohm-theory.md) adds configurations and guidance, and many-worlds accounts keep [unitary](../../../../unitary-connection.md) dynamics and branch-relative records. Some proposals are [empirically equivalent](../../../../empirical-equivalence.md) in standard regimes, whereas modified collapse models can have additional testable noise or heating. Interpretation is therefore not always an idle choice among verbal paraphrases of one unchanged theory.

The appropriate conclusion distinguishes applications from completeness. One can make reliable conditional predictions using a modest operational account, but a claim to describe the physical world requires saying what the formal state and its alternatives represent and how definite evidence arises. That need does not prove that any particular realist interpretation is correct, or that every philosophical dispute must be settled before doing useful physics.
