<h1 id="s28/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

A dynamical collapse law specifies stochastic changes of the [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md), including a rate, localization scale and probabilities. Such changes can suppress macroscopic [superpositions](../../../../superposition-principle.md), but a law for a mathematical state does not automatically identify the stuff composing a macroscopic table. If the [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) is on [configuration space](../../../../mechanical-configuration-space.md), one must still explain how it represents a three-dimensional distribution of matter and records.

One option defines [mass density](../../../../density.md) in ordinary space by $m(\mathbf x,t)=\sum_i m_i\int dq\,|\psi(q,t)|^2\delta(\mathbf x-\mathbf q_i)$. A localization then changes this [density](../../../../density.md) so that a single dominant macroscopic pattern remains. The formula is additional interpretive structure, even if it introduces no independent evolving variable beyond the [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md). Another option takes discrete [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) localization events as the primitive ontology. These two proposals can accompany closely related [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) dynamics while telling different stories about what matter is.

This is why [mass density](../../../../density.md) in particular is not logically mandatory, but some account of the relation between collapse and ordinary physical objects is required if the theory is offered as a complete ontology. A wavefunction-only view may argue that no further primitive stuff is necessary, yet it owes an explanation of spatial localization and records on that basis. Merely asserting that interference terms become small is not that explanation.

Additional posits can also have costs: small [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) tails survive finite localization, and the ontology must say what their tiny matter components or corresponding events mean. Choosing primitive ontology and handling such tails are distinct from specifying the stochastic dynamical law. A defensible collapse interpretation therefore makes both commitments explicit rather than suggesting that adding randomness alone solves every part of the [measurement problem](../../../../measurement-problem.md).[1986, stochastic localization dynamics](../../../../unified-dynamics-for-microscopic-and-macroscopic-systems-by-giancarlo-ghirardi-and-others.md)[primitive ontology and alternative mass-density/event descriptions](../../../../on-the-common-structure-of-bohmian-mechanics-and-the-ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory-by-valia-allori-and-others.md)
