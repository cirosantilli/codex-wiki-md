[Isaac Newton](../../../../isaac-newton.md)'s distinctive achievement was a tightly organized mathematical connection between terrestrial mechanics and celestial motions. Three laws of motion supplied a general dynamical framework, and universal gravitation made the same inverse-square interaction relevant to falling bodies, satellites and planets. Instead of merely fitting planetary positions, the theory related observed motions to [forces](../../../../force-split.md) and showed what consequences followed for [orbits](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), tides and perturbations.

The novelty was not that mathematics, [inertia](../../../../inertia.md) or heliocentrism appeared for the first time. Earlier [work](../../../../work.md) had supplied essential components. Newton's synthesis made interaction quantitative through [mass](../../../../mass.md), [force](../../../../force-split.md) and [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md), treated mutual action rather than merely a prescribed planetary path, and offered comparisons extending across phenomena. An ideal two-body inverse-square system has conic-section relative [orbits](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), while systematic departures can indicate perturbing bodies or inadequate approximations. This made residual discrepancies informative rather than simply inconvenient.

His account of light also connected experiment and theoretical inference. Prism experiments distinguished the refrangibility of rays and explained the colors of white light through their composition. The claim depended on controlling the geometry and interpretation of [refraction](../../../../refraction.md), not just producing an attractive [spectrum](../../../../spectrum-topology.md). In both mechanics and [optics](../../../../optics-split.md), idealized mathematical objects were related to imperfect observations by explicit [arguments](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md).

There were limits to the unification. An [inverse-square law](../../../../inverse-square-law.md) specified the gravitational interaction without furnishing an accepted mechanical medium that produced it. Absolute time and space were conceptual commitments, and the empirical success of a [force law](../../../../force-law.md) did not automatically settle every metaphysical question about them. The significant novelty was a powerful structure for deriving and testing relations among motions, rather than a completed explanation of every physical cause.[Axioms or Laws of Motion; Book III on the system of the world](../../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)[Book I, prism experiments and different refrangibility](../../../../opticks-by-isaac-newton.md)
