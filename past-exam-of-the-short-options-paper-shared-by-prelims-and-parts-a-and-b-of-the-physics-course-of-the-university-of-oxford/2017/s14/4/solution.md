<h1 id="s14/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[William Thomson](../../../../william-thomson-1st-baron-kelvin.md) had to reconcile a successful constraint on engine performance with evidence that mechanical [work](../../../../work.md) could be converted into [heat](../../../../heat-split.md). [Count Rumford](../../../../benjamin-thompson.md)'s cannon-boring observations challenged a finite store of [caloric](../../../../caloric.md) by showing sustained production of [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) through mechanical action. [James Prescott Joule](../../../../james-prescott-joule.md) made the connection quantitative through controlled comparisons of mechanical or electrical input and thermal output. Neither result alone supplied the general limit on converting [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) back into useful [work](../../../../work.md).

Carnot's reversible-engine reasoning supplied that second ingredient, but its conservation-of-caloric interpretation could not simply coexist unchanged with the mechanical equivalent of [heat](../../../../heat-split.md). The synthesis retains [energy conservation](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) while replacing [caloric](../../../../caloric.md) conservation: in a cycle, the [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) received equals [work](../../../../work.md) produced plus [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) rejected. A second principle restricts what transformations of that conserved [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) are possible. In the Thomson formulation, a cyclic device cannot have as its sole result the extraction of [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) from one reservoir and its complete conversion into [work](../../../../work.md). A reversible engine between two [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md) sets the [upper bound](../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md); an irreversible one performs worse.

This separation explains why the first law does not imply the second. An imagined perfect converter of [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) from a single reservoir could conserve total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and still violate the required directionality. Conversely, observing [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) from [friction](../../../../friction.md) does not prove that every reverse conversion can occur without additional change. Absolute [thermodynamic temperature](../../../../thermodynamic-temperature.md) gives the reversible comparison an operational scale independent of any particular [thermometer](../../../../thermometer.md) substance.

Thomson's contribution was a developing reconciliation, not the immediate acceptance of a finished package. Experimental [work](../../../../work.md), revised theoretical commitments and collaboration and disagreement with contemporaries all mattered. The resulting laws harmonized work-heat equivalence with limits on cyclic conversion while preserving the useful core, rather than every premise, of Carnot's [argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md).[1850, mechanical work and measured heating](../../../../on-the-mechanical-equivalent-of-heat-by-james-prescott-joule.md)[1851, fundamental propositions and their relation to Carnot's principle](../../../../dynamical-theory-of-heat-by-william-thomson.md)
