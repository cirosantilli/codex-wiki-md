# Euclidean division

↑ **Parent:** [Integer](integer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_division)

For an [integer](integer.md) $a$ and positive [integer](integer.md) $b$, Euclidean division writes $a=bq+r$ with unique integer quotient $q$ and remainder $0\le r<b$. For $a\ge0$, repeated quotient-and-remainder steps yield the digits of a [base-p expansion](base-p-expansion.md) when $b=p$. The uniqueness follows because the difference of two permitted remainders cannot be a nonzero multiple of $b$.
