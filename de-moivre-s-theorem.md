<h1 id="de-moivre-s-theorem">De Moivre's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complex exponentiation](complex-exponentiation.md)

For every [integer](integer.md) $n$ and real $\theta$,

$$
(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta).
$$

It converts powers of a [complex number](complex-number.md) in polar form into multiplication of its [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md).
