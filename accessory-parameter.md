# Accessory parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Fuchsian differential equation](fuchsian-differential-equation.md)

An accessory parameter is a coefficient not determined by the singular points and their [indicial exponents](indicial-exponent.md). A second-order [Fuchsian differential equation](fuchsian-differential-equation.md) with exactly three regular singular points has no such independent parameter.
