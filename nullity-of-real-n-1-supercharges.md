<h1 id="nullity-of-real-n-1-supercharges">Nullity of real N=1 supercharges</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Energy positivity in global supersymmetry](energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md)

For a normalized state in a [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) of the ordinary four-dimensional [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md), define $M_{ab}=\langle\{Q_a,Q_b\}\rangle/2$ in a real Hermitian charge basis. Then $\|u\cdot Q\,\psi\|^2=u^TMu$, so its annihilator is precisely the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) represented by the positive matrix $M$. In a suitable real basis its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the two [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $\langle P^0\rangle I+\langle\mathbf P\rangle\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, each repeated twice. A nonzero kernel therefore has dimension two at nonzero null momentum expectation or four at zero expectation. Complex non-Hermitian lowering operators and algebras with domain-wall extensions are not covered by this argument.
