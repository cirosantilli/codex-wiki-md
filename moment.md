# Moment

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_(mathematics))

The $n$th raw moment of a random variable $X$ is $\mathbb E[X^n]$ when this expectation exists.

More generally, a [moment](moment.md) is an integral weighted by a power of a coordinate against a measure or density; raw, central, and standardized moments are distinguished by the coordinate and normalization used.
