# Fundamental even mode of a symmetric dielectric slab

↑ **Parent:** [Planar dielectric waveguide](planar-dielectric-waveguide.md)

Set $u=Kd$, $w=K'd$, and $\mathcal V=(\omega d/c)\sqrt{\epsilon_r-1}$. The [circle](circle.md) in the first quadrant falls from $w=\mathcal V$, while $u\tan u$ rises from zero to its first pole. A unique first-branch intersection exists for every $\mathcal V>0$. It yields a nodeless even bound field. At exactly zero index contrast there is no confined mode; as contrast tends to zero from above its tail extends without bound.
