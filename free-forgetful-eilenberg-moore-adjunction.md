# Free-forgetful Eilenberg-Moore adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md)

For a [monad](monad.md) $(T,\eta,\mu)$, the [free algebra functor](free-algebra-functor.md) sends $X$ to $(TX,\mu_X)$ and is a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to the [forgetful functor](forgetful-functor.md) from the [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md). The transpose [bijection](bijection.md) sends an [monad algebra morphism](morphism-of-algebras-for-a-monad.md) $h:TX\to A$ to $h\eta_X$; its inverse sends $k:X\to A$ to $aT(k)$, where $a:TA\to A$ is the algebra structure. The [monad](monad.md) and algebra laws make these inverse and natural. The [adjunction unit](unit-of-an-adjunction.md) is $\eta$ and the [adjunction counit](counit-of-an-adjunction.md) at $(A,a)$ is $a$, so the induced [monad](monad.md) has exactly the original endofunctor, unit and multiplication.
