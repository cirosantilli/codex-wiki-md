# Preemptive scheduling

↑ **Parent:** [Scheduling preemption](scheduling-preemption.md)

A [process scheduling](process-scheduling.md) policy that can replace a running, still-runnable task with another task. Time-sliced [round-robin scheduling](round-robin-scheduling.md) uses a [hardware timer](hardware-timer.md) [interrupt](interrupt.md) to regain control when a [scheduling quantum](scheduling-quantum.md) expires.
