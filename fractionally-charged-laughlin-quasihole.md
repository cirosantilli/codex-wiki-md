# Fractionally charged Laughlin quasihole

↑ **Parent:** [Laughlin wavefunction](laughlin-wavefunction.md)

A quasihole adds one zero at $\eta$ per [electron](electron.md); its local deficit carries positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $e/m$.[pp. 1396–1397, Eqs. (13), (15) and screening argument](anomalous-quantum-hall-effect-an-incompressible-quantum-fluid-with-fractionally-charged-excitations-by-robert-laughlin.md)

Here is a transport counting outline, conditional on an [incompressible quantum Hall fluid](incompressible-quantum-hall-fluid.md). Inserting one [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) quantum $\Delta\Phi=h/e$ slowly compared with $\hbar/\Delta$, where $\Delta$ is the excitation [energy](energy-split.md) gap, produces a circulating [electric field](electric-field-split.md) by [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). The transverse [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) sends a radial [electric current](electric-current.md) whose time integral has magnitude

$$
|\Delta Q|=|\sigma_{xy}|\Delta\Phi
=\frac{e^2}{mh}\frac he=\frac em.
$$

The opposite [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) moves to the boundary, so the total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) remains conserved. The counted deficit belongs to a collective [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md); no individual [electron](electron.md) has been cut into pieces. The argument assumes the gapped fluid and its Hall response, and is not a consequence of the finite two-[electron](electron.md) normalization integral.
