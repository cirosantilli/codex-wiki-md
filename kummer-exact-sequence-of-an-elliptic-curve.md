# Kummer exact sequence of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer-theoretic proof of the weak Mordell-Weil theorem](kummer-theoretic-proof-of-the-weak-mordell-weil-theorem.md)

The exact sequence of Galois modules

$$
0\to E[n]\to E(\overline K)\xrightarrow{[n]}E(\overline K)\to0
$$

induces the [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) from $E(K)/nE(K)$ into $H^1(K,E[n])$.
