# Dimension of image of a morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md)

For an irreducible source, regard the [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) as dominant onto the closure of its image, with generic point $\eta$. The [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md) gives the displayed formula. A [proper morphism](proper-morphism.md) has closed image, so the closure can then be omitted.
