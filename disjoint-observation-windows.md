# Disjoint observation windows

↑ **Parent:** [Window count of a point process](window-count-of-a-point-process.md)

Observation windows are [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) used to count points of a [point process](point-process.md). They are pairwise disjoint when $W_i\cap W_j=\varnothing$ for every $i\ne j$. Half-open intervals such as $[0,1)$ and $[1,2)$ meet at a boundary without sharing any point, whereas the closed intervals $[0,1]$ and $[1,2]$ both contain one. A [disjoint union](disjoint-union.md) makes window counts additive; it does not make their [random variables](random-variable-split.md) [independent](independent-random-variables.md).
