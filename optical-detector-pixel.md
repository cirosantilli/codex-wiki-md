# Optical detector pixel

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

An optical [photodetector](photodetector.md) [detector pixel](optical-detector-pixel.md) is a spatially sampled photosensitive element. Counts from distinct [detector pixels](optical-detector-pixel.md) have independent [photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md) in the ideal model, but charge transfer, electrical coupling and resampling can produce [covariance](covariance.md).
