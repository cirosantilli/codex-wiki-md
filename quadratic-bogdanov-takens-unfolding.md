<h1 id="quadratic-bogdanov-takens-unfolding">Quadratic Bogdanov–Takens unfolding</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation](bogdanov-takens-bifurcation.md)

For $\lambda>0$, the [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $x=\pm\sqrt\lambda$, $y=0$. The positive branch is a saddle; the negative branch has a subcritical [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) at $\mu=\sqrt\lambda$. A [homoclinic orbit](homoclinic-orbit.md) occurs at $\mu=(5/7)\sqrt\lambda+O(\lambda)$ near the double-zero point. Between this homoclinic curve and the Hopf curve, an unstable [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) surrounds a stable [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). The factor $5/7$ follows from a [Melnikov energy-balance method](melnikov-energy-balance-method.md), using the integrals of $v^2$ and $uv^2$ along the leading conservative saddle loop.
