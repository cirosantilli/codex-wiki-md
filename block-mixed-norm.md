# Block mixed norm

↑ **Parent:** [Norm](norm.md)

The block mixed [norm](norm.md) sums the $\ell^q$ [norm](norm.md) of each block, for $q\ge1$. Its [dual norm](dual-norm.md) is $\|p\|_{q',\infty}=\max_i\|p_i\|_{q'}$, where $q'=q/(q-1)$ for $q>1$ and $q'=\infty$ for $q=1$. Apply [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) blockwise and choose a norming block to attain equality. [Absolute values](absolute-value.md) are essential for signed components.
