# Isothermal displacement energy of a self-gravitating slab

↑ **Parent:** [Self-gravitating isothermal slab](self-gravitating-isothermal-slab.md)

For an isothermal gas slab, a horizontal [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) has $\delta\rho=-ik\rho\xi_x-(\rho\xi_z)_z$, $W=c_s^2\delta\rho/\rho+\phi$, $\omega^2\xi_x=ikW$, $\omega^2\xi_z=W_z$ and $(\partial_z^2-k^2)\phi=4\pi G\delta\rho$. Multiply momentum by $\rho\boldsymbol\xi^*$ and integrate by parts, then use the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md). Vanishing [pressure](pressure.md)-work and gravitational boundary terms give the displayed real quadratic identity, with $I=\int\rho|\boldsymbol\xi|^2dz>0$. Thus every admissible squared [eigenfrequency](eigenfrequency.md) is real; a negative value gives gravitational growth rather than an oscillatory instability.
