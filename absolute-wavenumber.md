# Absolute wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md)

An [absolute wavenumber](absolute-wavenumber.md) is a complex [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) at the selected zero-[group velocity](group-velocity.md) saddle of a [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). For the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md), $k_0=-U/[2(c_d-i)]$; the positive real part of the diffusion coefficient makes the Gaussian saddle accessible from the real Fourier contour.
