# Photoelectric effect

↑ **Parent:** [Photon absorption](photon-absorption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoelectric_effect)

The [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) is the release of [electrons](electron.md) from matter following [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md). Here the focus is emission from a metal surface into [vacuum](vacuum.md): a [photon](photon.md) supplies [energy](energy-split.md), the [work function](work-function.md) pays the escape cost, and the remainder can become the emitted [electron](electron.md)'s [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). A [photocurrent](photocurrent.md) counts collected [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); a [stopping potential](stopping-potential.md) probes the upper [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) limit. These are different measurements.

In 1905, [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md) proposed that an individual light quantum could transfer its whole [energy](energy-split.md) to one [electron](electron.md). His §8 allowed losses before escape, distinguished the fastest emitted [electrons](electron.md) from the rest, and predicted a straight [stopping potential](stopping-potential.md) versus [frequency](frequency.md) relation with a material-independent slope. He separately predicted that changing [light intensity](light-intensity.md) changes the number emitted without changing their [velocity](velocity.md) distribution under otherwise identical conditions. He also discussed incomplete transfer and limits to the account.[Einstein's Miraculous Year; §8, printed pp. 193–196; full 21-page English translation of the 1905 paper](einstein-s-miraculous-year/on-a-heuristic-point-of-view-concerning-the-production-and-transformation-of-light-by-albert-einstein.md)

The useful modern starting model is a clean, homogeneous metal with independent single-[photon](photon.md) events, negligible initial thermal broadening, negligible lowering of the surface barrier by an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md), and negligible [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) from the emitted [electrons](electron.md) themselves. Specify the surface and its [work function](work-function.md); it is not enough to name the bulk material. The [Einstein photoelectric equation](einstein-photoelectric-equation.md) describes the upper edge of the emission [energy](energy-split.md) distribution. It neither predicts that every absorbed [photon](photon.md) liberates an [electron](electron.md) nor supplies the whole [photocurrent](photocurrent.md) curve. Its appeal is that a complicated surface process still exposes the universal [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) through a simple slope.
