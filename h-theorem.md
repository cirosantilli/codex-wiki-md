# H-theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann H functional](boltzmann-h-functional.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H-theorem)

For the normalized isotropic [Maxwell molecule collision operator](maxwell-molecule-collision-operator.md), put $a=f'f_*'$ and $b=ff_*$. The [integral](integral.md) is over position, both velocities and collision direction. The symmetrized weak identity yields the displayed [derivative](derivative.md) of the [Boltzmann H functional](boltzmann-h-functional.md). It is nonpositive because $(a-b)\log(a/b)\geq0$ for positive $a,b$. Spatial transport contributes only a boundary flux, which must vanish for the total-space inequality.
