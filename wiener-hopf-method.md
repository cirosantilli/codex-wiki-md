# Wiener-Hopf method

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem-split.md)

The Wiener-Hopf method solves a [boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem-split.md) with complementary half-line traces by [Wiener-Hopf factorization](wiener-hopf-factorization.md) and additive splitting into upper- and lower-half-plane analytic functions. Analytic continuation gives a common entire function, whose growth and edge conditions determine its admissible value.
