# Fourier weight

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md)

Fourier weight is the squared [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md) coefficient mass on specified levels or a specified family of subsets. [Parseval's identity](parseval-identity.md) identifies the total weight with $\mathbb E|f|^2$, and the nonconstant weight with [variance](variance-split.md). A decision tree of depth at most $s$ has no weight above level $s$.
