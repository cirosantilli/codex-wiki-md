# GaN blue LED development

↑ **Parent:** [Blue light-emitting diode](blue-light-emitting-diode.md)

[Isamu Akasaki](isamu-akasaki.md) chose [metalorganic vapour-phase epitaxy](metalorganic-vapour-phase-epitaxy.md) and [sapphire](sapphire.md), which tolerated hot [ammonia](ammonia.md). Large [lattice mismatch](lattice-mismatch.md) hindered film growth. In 1985, [Hiroshi Amano](hiroshi-amano.md) and [Isamu Akasaki](isamu-akasaki.md)'s group developed an [aluminium nitride](aluminium-nitride.md) [epitaxial buffer layer](epitaxial-buffer-layer.md) deposited before hotter [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) growth. The buffer supplies initial growth sites; growing islands join into a continuous film. It improves growth without making the lattices identical. [Isamu Akasaki](isamu-akasaki.md) separately credits [Shuji Nakamura](shuji-nakamura.md)'s 1991 [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) buffer.[§§3.2–4.2, pp. 13–20, Figs. 7–11: growth sequence and microscopy](https://www.kapitza.ras.ru/~glazkov/students/Nobel-akasaki-lecture.pdf)

The 1986 growth paper reports a vertical [metalorganic vapour-phase epitaxy](metalorganic-vapour-phase-epitaxy.md) reactor, source gases mixed just before delivery to slanted [sapphire](sapphire.md), and subsequent [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) growth over [aluminium nitride](aluminium-nitride.md). Surface microscopy, [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) and [photoluminescence](photoluminescence.md) tested different aspects of the result. Buffered and unbuffered growth supplied the comparison. Its reported preparation conditions should not be replaced by one universal buffer [temperature](temperature-split.md): the later lecture's account of the first low-temperature trial and the paper's parameter study describe different stages of development.[p. 353, Fig. 1 and Table I; p. 354, surface and diffraction comparisons](metalorganic-vapor-phase-epitaxial-growth-of-a-high-quality-gan-film-using-an-aln-buffer-layer-by-hiroshi-amano-and-others.md)

A [magnesium](magnesium.md) [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) in [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) accepts a [valence band](valence-band.md) [electron](electron.md), leaving a mobile [electron hole](electron-hole.md). [Hydrogen passivation of magnesium acceptors in gallium nitride](hydrogen-passivation-of-magnesium-acceptors-in-gallium-nitride.md) suppresses this activity. Post-growth activation restores existing impurities' electrical activity; ionization of active [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md) supplies mobile [electron holes](electron-hole.md). Adding [magnesium](magnesium.md) alone is insufficient.[p. 1258, abstract and §1: passivation and activation](hole-compensation-mechanism-of-p-type-gan-films-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)

In 1989, [Hiroshi Amano](hiroshi-amano.md), [Masahiro Kito](masahiro-kito.md), [Kazumasa Hiramatsu](kazumasa-hiramatsu.md) and [Isamu Akasaki](isamu-akasaki.md) obtained [p-type semiconductor](p-type-semiconductor.md) conduction in [magnesium](magnesium.md)-doped [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) using low-energy [electron](electron.md) beam irradiation. [Hiroshi Amano](hiroshi-amano.md) recalls initially resistive samples, an unreliable hot-probe test, then [Masahiro Kito](masahiro-kito.md)'s [Hall effect](hall-effect.md) confirmation. A junction irradiated in an M-shaped region emitted in that pattern. Treatment, carrier-sign measurement and spatial emission supplied different evidence. Earlier [zinc](zinc.md)-doped samples brightened under irradiation without becoming [p-type semiconductors](p-type-semiconductor.md): brightness alone did not prove the carrier sign.[§V, pp. 1135–1136, Figs. 3–4; authors of 1989 paper in references, p. 1137](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/pdf/10.1103/RevModPhys.87.1133)

[Shuji Nakamura](shuji-nakamura.md), [Takashi Mukai](takashi-mukai.md), [Masayuki Senoh](masayuki-senoh.md) and [Naruhito Iwasa](naruhito-iwasa.md)'s 1992 thermal-activation paper established another route using [annealing](annealing-materials-science.md) in [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). Their companion study compared already conducting films heated in [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) with films heated in [ammonia](ammonia.md): the latter became highly resistive again. Its [hydrogen](hydrogen.md)-complex explanation connected this atmosphere dependence to [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) passivation. These are thermal treatment and atmosphere comparisons, distinct from the earlier [electron](electron.md) beam intervention; neither observation alone images an individual [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) complex.[abstract: nitrogen annealing above 700 degrees Celsius and electrical changes](thermal-annealing-effects-on-p-type-mg-doped-gan-films-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)[p. 1258, abstract and §§1–2: activation history, nitrogen/ammonia comparison and hydrogenation hypothesis](hole-compensation-mechanism-of-p-type-gan-films-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)

[Shuji Nakamura](shuji-nakamura.md)'s two-flow [metalorganic chemical vapor deposition](metalorganic-vapour-phase-epitaxy.md) reactor, [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) buffer, thermal activation and improved [indium gallium nitride](indium-gallium-nitride.md) growth enabled bright [double heterostructure](double-heterostructure.md) devices, followed by thinner [quantum well](quantum-well.md) devices.[§§III–IV.A, pp. 1140–1144; Figs. 9–11](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/pdf/10.1103/RevModPhys.87.1139)

The original 1994 paper by [Shuji Nakamura](shuji-nakamura.md), [Takashi Mukai](takashi-mukai.md) and [Masayuki Senoh](masayuki-senoh.md) describes a [sapphire](sapphire.md)/[gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md)-buffer device with [zinc](zinc.md)-doped [indium gallium nitride](indium-gallium-nitride.md) emission and [aluminium gallium nitride](aluminium-gallium-nitride.md) confinement. After [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [annealing](annealing-materials-science.md), etching exposed the n-side for a separate contact. At room [temperature](temperature-split.md), varied forward [electric currents](electric-current.md) gave emission [spectra](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and optical-output curves alongside a [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md). At $20\,\mathrm{mA}$: $1.5\,\mathrm{mW}$ output, $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ peak [wavelength](wavelength.md), $3.6\,\mathrm V$ terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). The peak stayed nearly fixed across the current series. The paper omits a complete calibrated collection apparatus; comparisons with earlier devices change several preparation details.[pp. 1687–1688, fabrication and Figs. 1–4](candela-class-high-brightness-ingan%E2%81%84algan-double-heterostructure-blue-light-emitting-diodes-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)
