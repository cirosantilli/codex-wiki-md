# Global obstruction to Wick rotation of conformal compactifications

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal compactification](conformal-compactification.md)

The [Projective Minkowski compactification](projective-minkowski-compactification.md) has infinite cyclic fundamental group, while the [conformal compactification of Euclidean space](conformal-compactification-of-euclidean-space.md) is $S^4$ and simply connected. Thus the affine substitution $t\mapsto it$ cannot extend to an invertible smooth identification of these real compactifications. Both are real slices of the same complex [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md), which permits local analytic continuation without equating their global real topologies.

In affine cone coordinates $[U,V,t,\boldsymbol x]$ with $2UV+t^2-|\boldsymbol x|^2=0$ and $U=1$, the substitution is represented on the complex quadric by the rational map $[U,V,t,\boldsymbol x]\mapsto[U^2,UV+t^2,iUt,U\boldsymbol x]$. It preserves the quadric equation but is indeterminate at some infinity points, including $[0,1,0,\boldsymbol0]$. This is a direct algebraic manifestation of the obstruction to a global regular Wick map.
