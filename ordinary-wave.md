# Ordinary wave

↑ **Parent:** [Uniaxial optical medium](uniaxial-optical-medium.md)

The [ordinary wave](ordinary-wave.md)'s [electric field](electric-field.md) is perpendicular to the plane containing the [wavevector](wavevector.md) and [optic axis](optic-axis.md). Its [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n_o$ is direction independent at fixed [frequency](frequency.md).
