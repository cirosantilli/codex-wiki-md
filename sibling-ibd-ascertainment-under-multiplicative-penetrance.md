# Sibling IBD ascertainment under multiplicative penetrance

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

For [full siblings](full-sibling.md) in an [outbred pedigree](outbred-pedigree.md), condition on both being affected under [multiplicative diallelic penetrance](multiplicative-diallelic-penetrance.md) and on residual disease outcomes being independent given their [genotypes](genotype.md). The usual [Mendelian IBD sharing of full siblings](mendelian-ibd-sharing-of-full-siblings.md) probabilities $(1/4,1/2,1/4)$ are weighted by $p^2\alpha^4,p^2\alpha^2\beta,p^2\beta^2$, respectively, giving the displayed conditional sharing probabilities. Since $\beta-\alpha^2=\phi(1-\phi)(\theta-1)^2\geq0$, $z_1\leq1/2$, strictly for a polymorphic locus with a nontrivial effect.
