# Gas-globe density measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Gas density](gas-density.md)

In an idealized rigid-globe weighing, subtract the evacuated assembly's [mass](mass.md) from the filled assembly's [mass](mass.md), then divide by the internal [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). Establish the filled sample's [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) before comparing gases. The subtraction removes the container's large contribution, making a small gas contribution accessible. A real measurement also needs corrections or bounds for residual [gas](gas.md), [adsorption](adsorption.md), leaks, changing vessel volume and external [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). The [Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment](rayleigh-ramsay-argon-density-experiment.md) shows why preparation-route comparisons add information beyond repeated weighings of one sample.
