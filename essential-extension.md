# Essential extension

↑ **Parent:** [Essential submodule](essential-submodule.md)

An extension $N\subseteq M$ is essential when $N$ is an [essential submodule](essential-submodule.md) of $M$. Essentiality is transitive. It is an intersection condition, not a statement that the quotient is small in dimension or finitely generated.
