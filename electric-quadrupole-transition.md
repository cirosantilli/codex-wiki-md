# Electric quadrupole transition

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma ray](gamma-ray.md)

An [electric quadrupole transition](electric-quadrupole-transition.md) is a radiative transition of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) with electric multipole order two. The emitted [photon](photon.md) carries two units of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and positive [parity](parity.md), so the [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) has unchanged [parity](parity.md).[Multipolarity, E2 entry](nudat-3-glossary-by-national-nuclear-data-center.md)

[Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) imposes $|I_i-I_f|\le2\le I_i+I_f$. Thus $2^+\to0^+$ and $2^+\to2^+$ can be E2, whereas $0^+\to0^+$ cannot emit one E2 [photon](photon.md). On the even-$I$, $K=0$ [nuclear rotational band](nuclear-rotational-band.md), the adjacent downward transitions have $\Delta I=2$; that band pattern is more restrictive than the general E2 [selection rule](selection-rule.md).
