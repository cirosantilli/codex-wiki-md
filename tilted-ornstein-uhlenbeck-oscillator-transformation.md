# Tilted Ornstein-Uhlenbeck oscillator transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck multiplicative amplification](ornstein-uhlenbeck-multiplicative-amplification.md)

The weighted strain density in [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck multiplicative amplification](ornstein-uhlenbeck-multiplicative-amplification.md) evolves under $\mathcal L_np=(\kappa/2)p''+\tau^{-1}(\sigma p)'+n\sigma p$. Setting $p=e^{-\sigma^2/(2\kappa\tau)}\psi$ eliminates its first derivative. Shifting and scaling to $x=(\sigma-\kappa n\tau^2)/\sqrt{\kappa\tau}$ gives $\psi_{xx}+[1+\kappa n^2\tau^3-2\gamma\tau-x^2]\psi=0$. The [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) levels yield the displayed [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Its [ground state](ground-state.md) is the dominant positive mode; higher sign-changing modes may occur in a transient expansion.
