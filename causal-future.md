# Causal future

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

The [causal future](causal-future.md) of $S$ consists of points reached from $S$ by future-directed causal curves, including points of $S$ itself. In a [globally hyperbolic spacetime](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md), the [causal future](causal-future.md) of a [compact](compact-space.md) set is [closed](closed-set.md).
