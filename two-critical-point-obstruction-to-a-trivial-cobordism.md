# Two-critical-point obstruction to a trivial cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism Morse function](cobordism-morse-function.md)

If a [cobordism Morse function](cobordism-morse-function.md) on a [trivial cobordism](trivial-cobordism.md) has exactly two [critical points](critical-point.md), its relative [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md) has two generators and must be acyclic. Their indices therefore differ by one. The only differential is multiplication by the signed intersection of the lower point's [belt sphere](belt-sphere.md) with the higher point's [attaching sphere](attaching-sphere.md) in a common regular level. A two-term complex of copies of $\mathbb Z$ is acyclic exactly when that integer is a unit. Algebraic intersection is therefore a necessary condition; it is not by itself a geometric handle-cancellation statement.
