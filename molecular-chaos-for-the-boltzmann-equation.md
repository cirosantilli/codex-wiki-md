# Molecular chaos for the Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

The incoming two-particle distribution at a collision is approximated by the product of one-particle distributions. This closes the binary collision term into the quadratic [nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md). It does not assert exact independence after collisions: outgoing pairs can be correlated, and time reversal generally converts those correlations into incoming correlations. A rigorous kinetic limit needs propagation and convergence estimates rather than a bare independence assertion.
