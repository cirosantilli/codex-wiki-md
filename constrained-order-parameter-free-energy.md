# Constrained order-parameter free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

Fix the spatial mean of a scalar [order parameter](order-parameter.md) to $M$ and sum the [Boltzmann weights](boltzmann-factor.md) of all configurations with that mean. The resulting constrained [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z_M$ defines this [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md), with an additive normalization fixed consistently. For an applied [conjugate field](field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) $h$, $Z(h)=\int dM\,e^{-\beta_TV[F_c(M)-hM]}$, up to the chosen measure normalization. The [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) gives $f(h)=\inf_M[F_c(M)-hM]$. A homogeneous [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) is an approximation to this constrained potential; phase-separated configurations can convexify it in the full [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md).
