# Compact embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)

A [compact embedding](compact-embedding.md) of [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) $X,Y$ is an injective [continuous linear map](continuous-linear-operator.md) $i:X\to Y$ that sends every [bounded set](bounded-set.md) to a [relatively compact subset](relatively-compact-subset.md). Equivalently, every bounded [sequence](sequence.md) in $X$ has a [subsequence](subsequence.md) converging in $Y$. One identifies $X$ with its image when $i$ is the natural inclusion. The [Rellich-Kondrachov theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) supplies many [compact embeddings](compact-embedding.md) between [Sobolev spaces](sobolev-space-split.md) and [Lp spaces](lp-space.md) on bounded [domains](domain-mathematical-analysis.md).
