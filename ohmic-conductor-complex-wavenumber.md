# Ohmic-conductor complex wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Skin effect](skin-effect.md)

With fields proportional to $e^{i(kz-\omega t)}$, a homogeneous Ohmic medium obeys the displayed relation by combining the two [curl](curl.md) [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md). The physical passive branch has $\operatorname{Im}k\geq0$. For real positive conductivity much larger than $\omega\epsilon$, it reduces to $k=(1+i)/\delta$ with [skin depth](skin-depth.md) $\delta=\sqrt{2/(\mu\sigma\omega)}$. Field [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and [intensity](intensity-physics.md) then decay with respective [lengths](length.md) $\delta$ and $\delta/2$. A frequency-dependent conductivity must be retained when charge-carrier inertia matters.
