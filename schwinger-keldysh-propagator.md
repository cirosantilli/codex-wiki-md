# Schwinger-Keldysh propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Keldysh formalism](keldysh-formalism.md)

The closed time contour of the [in-in formalism](keldysh-formalism.md) gives four bulk propagators: time-ordered $G_{RR}$, anti-time-ordered $G_{LL}$, and the two Wightman functions $G_{LR}$ and $G_{RL}$. Vertices on the two contour branches carry opposite factors of $i$.
