# Baeyer-Villiger oxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Insertion of [oxygen](oxygen.md) beside an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) or [ketone](ketone.md) [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md), commonly with a [peroxy acid](peroxy-acid.md). Cyclic [ketones](ketone.md) give ring-expanded [lactones](lactone.md). The migrating group retains its configuration, but competing migrations can give different constitutional products.
