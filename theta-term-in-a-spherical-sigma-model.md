# Theta term in a spherical sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-deformed spherical sigma model](mass-deformed-spherical-sigma-model.md)

Locally this density is a total derivative, so it changes neither the bulk field equations nor the [energy](energy.md). Its contribution to the angular [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is a spatial boundary derivative. With $\epsilon^{01}=+1$, it produces the [theta-angle shift of a sigma-model kink charge](theta-angle-shift-of-a-sigma-model-kink-charge.md).
