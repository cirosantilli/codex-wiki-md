# Smooth exponential sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md)

On a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), the sequence of [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md)

$$
0\to\underline{\mathbb Z}\to\mathcal C^\infty_{\mathbb C}\xrightarrow{\exp(2\pi i\,\cdot)}\mathcal C^{\infty,*}_{\mathbb C}\to1
$$

is exact. The additive smooth-function sheaf is a [fine sheaf](fine-sheaf.md), so the connecting map identifies $H^1(X,\mathcal C^{\infty,*}_{\mathbb C})$ with $H^2(X,\mathbb Z)$. The former classifies smooth [complex line bundles](complex-line-bundle.md) and the connecting class is their [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md).
