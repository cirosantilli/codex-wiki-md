# Yang-Mills vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

A classical zero-energy Yang-Mills vacuum has vanishing spatial gauge curvature. On simply connected space it is pure gauge, $A=h^{-1}dh$. If gauge transformations approach the identity at spatial infinity, the boundary-normalized gauge maps on compactified space $S^3$ have integer winding, recorded by the [Chern-Simons number of a gauge field](chern-simons-number-of-a-gauge-field.md). Transformations of nonzero winding relate these representatives; quantum theta states encode their tunnelling and large-gauge-transformation phases.
