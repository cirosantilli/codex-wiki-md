# Lunar laser ranging

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-square potential](inverse-square-potential.md)

[Lunar laser ranging](lunar-laser-ranging.md) gives a more discriminating [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) readout. An observatory directs short [laser](laser-split.md) pulses at a [retroreflector](retroreflector.md) array on the [Moon](moon.md), and a detector records returning [photons](photon.md). Round-trip [time](time-in-physics.md), statistically combined over several returns, constrains distance; [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) accompany the readout. The fitted model includes [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md), terrestrial and lunar rotation, geophysical effects, and corrections from [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md). Comparing [orbit determination](orbit-determination.md) residuals across stations and model choices checks consistency. The inspected study reports $1.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ weighted [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) residuals for its 2005–2011 ranges, rather than for a bare isolated [inverse-square potential](inverse-square-potential.md). It tests the [equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md) using the [Earth](earth.md) and [Moon](moon.md)'s response to the [Sun](sun.md); it is not a direct [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md) measurement. Uneven coverage near new and full [Moon](moon.md), [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md), and thermal expansion of reflector mounts limit that inference.[§§2–3, pp. 3–6: pulse timing, normal points, model and figure 1; §4, pp. 7–8: consistency checks and radiation/thermal effects](lunar-laser-ranging-tests-of-the-equivalence-principle-by-james-g-williams-and-others.md)
