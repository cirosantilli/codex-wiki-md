# Flow cytometry

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_cytometry)

[Flow cytometry](flow-cytometry.md) records signals from suspended particles passing an illuminated interrogation region. For a [flow cytometric DNA-content assay](flow-cytometric-dna-content-assay.md), a [laser](laser.md) excites a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)-binding dye and a detector records its [fluorescence](fluorescence.md). An event is a recorded passage, not a guarantee that exactly one intact [biological cell](biological-cell.md) was measured.
