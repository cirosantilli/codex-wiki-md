# Constant-shell BCS gap equation

↑ **Parent:** [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md)

An attractive interaction $V_{k,k'}=-V/\mathcal V$ within $|\xi_k|,|\xi_{k'}|<\Omega_D$ gives a constant gap inside that energy shell and zero outside. With approximately constant [single-spin density of states](single-spin-density-of-states.md) $\nu_s$ per volume,

$$
1=\nu_sV\int_0^{\Omega_D}\frac{d\xi}{\sqrt{\xi^2+\Delta^2}}=\nu_sV\operatorname{arsinh}(\Omega_D/\Delta),\qquad \Delta=\frac{\Omega_D}{\sinh(1/(\nu_sV))}.
$$

The [weak coupling](weak-coupling.md) limit is $\Delta\simeq2\Omega_De^{-1/(\nu_sV)}$. A density counting both spin species is twice $\nu_s$ and must be divided by two. The shell cutoff is a relative-energy condition, even when every [Cooper pair](cooper-pair.md) has zero total momentum.
