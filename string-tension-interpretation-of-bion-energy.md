# String-tension interpretation of BIon energy

↑ **Parent:** [Born–Infeld soliton](born-infeld-soliton.md)

For $X-X_\infty=c/r$, subtracting the planar [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) energy leaves $T_3\int|\nabla X|^2d^3x=4\pi T_3c^2/r_0$ outside a cutoff. Since $X_{\rm cut}-X_\infty=c/r_0$, its coefficient is the carried string tension. Electric charge quantization gives $c=\pi g_s|N|\alpha'$ and coefficient $|N|T_{F1}$; magnetic charge gives $c=\pi|N|\alpha'$ and coefficient $|N|T_{D1}$.
