# Dislocation glide

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

Conservative motion within a plane containing both the dislocation line and its [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md). For a nonscrew line their [cross product](cross-product.md) determines the plane normal; a screw line can belong to more than one such plane.
