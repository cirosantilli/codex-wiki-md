# Real closed field

↑ **Parent:** [Formally real field](formally-real-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_closed_field)

A real closed field is a [formally real field](formally-real-field.md) with no proper formally real algebraic extension. Equivalently every positive element in its unique compatible order is a square and every odd-degree polynomial has a root. Its positive cone is definable in the field language as the nonzero squares. The [Theory of real closed fields](theory-of-real-closed-fields.md) is model-complete.
