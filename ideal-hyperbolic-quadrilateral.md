# Ideal hyperbolic quadrilateral

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md)

An ideal hyperbolic quadrilateral is the region bounded by four [hyperbolic geodesics](geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md) whose four consecutive endpoints are distinct points on the ideal boundary. The ideal vertices lie outside the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md). In the [hyperbolic disk](hyperbolic-disc.md), the four vertices $1,i,-1,-i$ give a regular example: its sides are the interior arcs of the circles of radius one centered at $\pm1\pm i$. Although the sides have infinite length, the quadrilateral has finite [hyperbolic area](hyperbolic-area.md) $2\pi$ in curvature $-1$.
