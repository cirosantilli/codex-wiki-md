# Uniform asymptotic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

An approximation is uniform on a specified domain when its error bound holds throughout that domain as the small parameter tends to zero. In a [matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md), adding the inner and outer approximations and subtracting their common overlap produces a composite approximation. Checking only a fixed point away from a [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) does not establish uniformity.
