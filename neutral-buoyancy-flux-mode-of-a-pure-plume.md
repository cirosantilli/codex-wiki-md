# Neutral buoyancy-flux mode of a pure plume

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric pure plume](axisymmetric-pure-plume.md)

A constant surviving [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) sets the amplitudes of a [pure plume](pure-plume.md): $Q\propto F^{1/3}$ and $M\propto F^{2/3}$. Relative-flux perturbations have the neutral ratio $(\delta Q/Q,\delta M/M,\delta F/F)\propto(1,2,3)$. This is the zero-eigenvalue limit of [growing mode of power-law plume similarity](growing-mode-of-power-law-plume-similarity.md); nonzero endpoint stratification can still create cumulative logarithmic flux loss.
