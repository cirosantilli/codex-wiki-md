<h1 id="ado-s-theorem">Ado's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Faithful Lie algebra representation](faithful-lie-algebra-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ado's_theorem)

Every finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md) has a finite-dimensional faithful [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md). Thus it embeds as a Lie subalgebra of a [matrix algebra](matrix-algebra.md), using the [matrix commutator](commutator.md) as bracket. Faithfulness is crucial for detecting central elements: the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) alone kills the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
