# Double-coset Hecke algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Double coset](double-coset.md)

For a [group](group-split.md) $G$ and subgroup $\Gamma$ such that every [double coset](double-coset.md) has finitely many orbits under left multiplication by $\Gamma$, this algebra consists of complex $\Gamma$-bi-invariant [functions](function-split.md) on $G$ supported on finitely many [double cosets](double-coset.md). Its convolution is

$$
(h_1*h_2)(g)=\sum_{\Gamma x\in\Gamma\backslash G}h_1(gx^{-1})h_2(x).
$$

The [double coset](double-coset.md) indicator functions form a basis. On invariant modular [functions](function-split.md) it acts on the right by $f*h=\sum_{\Gamma x}h(x)f|_kx$. For $G=GL_2(\mathbb Q)^+$ and $\Gamma=SL_2(\mathbb Z)$, [rational conjugation of finite-index modular subgroups](rational-conjugation-of-finite-index-modular-subgroups.md) verifies the finiteness condition. The determinant-$n$ [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md) uses the indicator of all integral determinant-$n$ [matrices](matrix.md), not only a single [double coset](double-coset.md) when $n$ is composite.
