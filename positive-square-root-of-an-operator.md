# Positive square root of an operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

Every bounded [positive operator](positive-operator.md) $T$ has a unique positive operator $T^{1/2}$ satisfying $(T^{1/2})^2=T$. The [spectral theorem for normal operators on a separable Hilbert space](spectral-theorem-for-normal-operators-on-a-separable-hilbert-space.md) constructs it by applying the scalar square-root function to the spectrum.
