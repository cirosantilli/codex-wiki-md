# Infrared photometric band

↑ **Parent:** [Photometric passband](photometric-passband.md)

The traditional near- and thermal-infrared photometric bands in the 1–5 micrometre region are $J,H,K,L,M$, with approximate centers $1.25,1.65,2.2,3.5,4.8$ micrometres. Variants such as $K_s$, $L'$ and $M'$ have different detailed responses. They largely follow [atmospheric windows](atmospheric-window.md); transmission, [sky brightness](sky-brightness.md), and detector response determine practical band edges. The [ISAAC filter documentation](https://www.eso.org/instruments/isaac/doc/VLT-MAN-ESO-14100-0841_v76.pdf) records instrument-specific variants, including an $L$ filter closer to $L'$ near 3.8 micrometres.
