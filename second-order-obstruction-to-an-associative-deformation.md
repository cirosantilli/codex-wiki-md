# Second-order obstruction to an associative deformation

↑ **Parent:** [First-order associative deformation](first-order-associative-deformation.md)

Expanding [associativity](associative-property.md) for $a*b=ab+tf(a,b)+t^2\mu_2(a,b)$ modulo $t^3$ gives the displayed equation. If $\delta f=0$, its right side is a degree-three [Hochschild cocycle](hochschild-cocycle.md). Its class in $HH^3(R,R)$ must vanish to extend the first-order [formal associative deformation](formal-associative-deformation.md) to second order. A nonzero class in $HH^2$ therefore need not integrate to a full [formal associative deformation](formal-associative-deformation.md).
