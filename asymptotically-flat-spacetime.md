# Asymptotically flat spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotically_flat_spacetime)

An asymptotically flat spacetime approaches [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) sufficiently far from isolated sources. At null infinity this is stated invariantly by requiring a smooth [conformal completion](conformal-completion.md) whose boundary contains future and past null infinity.
