# Cohomological obstruction to a Lagrangian section

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted Lagrangian graph criterion](twisted-lagrangian-graph-criterion.md)

If $[\sigma]\ne0$ in [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md), a [twisted cotangent symplectic form](twisted-cotangent-symplectic-form.md) has no [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) whose projection is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) onto the entire base. Such a submanifold would be a global [one-form graph](graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md), and the [twisted Lagrangian graph criterion](twisted-lagrangian-graph-criterion.md) would make $\sigma$ exact.
