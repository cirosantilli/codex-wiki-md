# Multiwire proportional chamber experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Multiwire proportional chamber](multiwire-proportional-chamber.md)

The original apparatus used $40\,\mu\mathrm m$ stainless-steel wires between meshes $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ away, flowing [argon](argon.md) with organic [vapor](vapor.md), negative mesh [voltage](voltage.md) and individual [electronic amplifiers](electronic-amplifier.md). A $5.9\,\mathrm{keV}$ [X-ray](x-ray.md) source calibrated pulse height while [voltage](voltage.md) was varied.[CERN manuscript §§1–2.1, Fig. 3](the-use-of-multiwire-proportional-counters-to-select-and-localize-charged-particles-by-georges-charpak-and-others.md)

A [coincidence circuit](coincidence-circuit.md) defined a narrow [proton](proton.md) or [pion](pion.md) beam. Scanning it across wires tested localization; the response edge matched the beam width, limiting the inference. Distant counts were attributed to [accidental coincidences](accidental-coincidence.md). Separate [electron](electron.md) scans measured pulse delay versus position, comparing [gas](gas.md) mixtures and pulse-height-dependent electronics.[CERN manuscript §§2.3–2.4, Figs. 8–11](the-use-of-multiwire-proportional-counters-to-select-and-localize-charged-particles-by-georges-charpak-and-others.md)

Thresholds rejected small pulses, reducing [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md). Wire-independence tests did not establish unlimited separation of overlapping tracks.[CERN manuscript §§2.2–2.4](the-use-of-multiwire-proportional-counters-to-select-and-localize-charged-particles-by-georges-charpak-and-others.md)
