# Collisionless stellar system

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md)

A [collisionless stellar system](collisionless-stellar-system.md) is one for which discrete gravitational encounters change its distribution negligibly over the [time](time-in-physics.md) under study, usually $T\ll t_{\rm rel}$. [Stars](star.md) can [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) and respond collectively to a varying smooth [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) even in this regime; collisionless does not mean force-free or stable against collective gravitational instabilities. Its smooth distribution follows the [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md).
