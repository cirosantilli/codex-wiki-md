# Binary operation

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic operation](algebraic-operation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_operation)

A [binary operation](binary-operation.md) on a [set](set-split.md) $S$ is a [function](function-split.md) $\ast:S\times S\to S$. It takes two ordered [inputs](input-computer-science.md) from the same [set](set-split.md) and returns exactly one [output](output-computing.md) in that [set](set-split.md). The [codomain](codomain.md) is part of the definition: ordinary [subtraction](subtraction.md) is a [binary operation](binary-operation.md) on the [integers](integer-split.md), but not on the positive [integers](integer-split.md), since $2-3=-1$ leaves that [set](set-split.md). Ordinary [division](division-mathematics.md) is not a total [binary operation](binary-operation.md) on the [real numbers](real-number.md), because division by zero is undefined; it is one on the nonzero [real numbers](real-number.md).

The same [set](set-split.md) can carry several different [binary operations](binary-operation.md). On $S=\{0,1,2\}$, both $a\ast b=(a+b)\bmod3$ and $a\diamond b=(a-b)\bmod3$ return an element of $S$, using [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md). Yet the [addition](addition.md) operation is [commutative](commutativity.md) and [associative](associative-property.md), while the [subtraction](subtraction.md) operation is neither. Specifying the allowed [inputs](input-computer-science.md) and [outputs](output-computing.md) tells us what can be evaluated; additional laws tell us how expressions may be transformed. [Grassl and Mingus, Some Abstract Algebra: A Primer and Interactive Workbook, binary operations](https://openmathbooks.org/someabstract/pdf/primer.pdf).

For a [finite set](finite-set.md), a [Cayley table](cayley-table.md) records every ordered pair and its result. This turns abstract laws into checkable examples. It also makes the order of the [inputs](input-computer-science.md) explicit: the row gives the first [input](input-computer-science.md), and the column gives the second.
