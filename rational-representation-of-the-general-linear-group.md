# Rational representation of the general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Rational representation](rational-representation.md)

The matrix coefficients of a rational $GL_m$ action belong to its [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $\mathbb C[g_{ab},\det(g)^{-1}]$. Multiplying the action by a sufficiently large [determinant twist](determinant-twist.md) clears all [determinant](determinant.md) denominators and produces a [polynomial representation](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md). Irreducible rational actions are indexed by decreasing integer highest weights, with negative coordinates allowed.
