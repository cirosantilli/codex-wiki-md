# Inverse-cube central force

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)

A radial [force](force-split.md) proportional to $r^{-3}$. For potential $V=B/r^2$, its outward radial component is $2B/r^3$: positive $B$ is repulsive and negative $B$ is attractive.
