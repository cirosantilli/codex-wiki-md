# AGB envelope-loss and core-growth timescales

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md)

The fate of an [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md) star depends on whether its envelope is removed before its core reaches the next ignition or instability threshold. The approximate envelope-loss time is $t_{\rm env}=M_{\rm env}/|\dot M|$, while the time to add a required core [mass](mass.md) $\Delta M_c$ is $t_{\rm grow}=\Delta M_c/\dot M_c$. Shell [stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) grows the core; [third dredge-up](third-dredge-up.md) can reduce the net growth. If $t_{\rm env}\ll t_{\rm grow}$, shell fuel disappears first and a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) remnant is favored. The opposite ordering permits further core growth but does not by itself specify whether [carbon burning](carbon-burning.md) is stable, explosive, or followed by further evolution. Both rates change with time, so the estimates guide rather than replace a stellar model.
