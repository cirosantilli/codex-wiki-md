# Finite additive group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)

A finite additive group is a [finite group](finite-group.md) whose operation is written as [addition](addition.md). Its [identity element](identity-element.md) is denoted $0$, the [inverse element](inverse-element.md) of $x$ is denoted $-x$, and [translation](translation-in-a-group.md) $y\mapsto x+y$ is a [bijection](bijection.md).
