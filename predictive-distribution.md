# Predictive distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

A probability law for a future observation conditional on currently available information. A [posterior predictive distribution](posterior-predictive-distribution.md) integrates the observation model over the posterior distribution of unknown parameters; a plug-in predictive law instead fixes an estimate of those parameters.
