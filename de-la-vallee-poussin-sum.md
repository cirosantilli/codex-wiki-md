<h1 id="de-la-vallee-poussin-sum">de la Vallée Poussin sum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md)

This average of [Fourier partial sums](fourier-partial-sum.md) reproduces every degree-at-most-$n$ [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md). With $\sigma_r=r^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}s_j$,

$$
v_{n,m}=\frac{(n+m)\sigma_{n+m}-n\sigma_n}{m},\qquad
\|v_{n,m}\|_\infty\le1+\frac{2n}{m}.
$$

The bound follows because [Fejér summation is a uniform-norm contraction](fejer-summation-is-a-uniform-norm-contraction.md). For $n=0$, the term involving $\sigma_0$ is omitted.
