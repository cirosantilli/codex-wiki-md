# Cache hit rate

↑ **Parent:** [Cache hit](cache-hit.md)

The fraction of $R>0$ [memory references](memory-reference.md) yielding [cache hits](cache-hit.md), with $H$ their count: $h=H/R$. The two-slot [fully associative cache](fully-associative-cache.md) example has $h=5/12$. Count the same reference unit and consulted level for both [cache hit rate](cache-hit-rate.md) and [cache miss rate](cache-miss-rate.md).
