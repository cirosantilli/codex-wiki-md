# Gradient wind

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md)

The [gradient wind](gradient-wind.md) retains the centripetal [acceleration](acceleration.md) of a curved flow as well as the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) and horizontal [pressure gradient force](pressure-gradient-force.md). For cyclonic circular flow in the northern hemisphere, $v^2/r+fv=\rho^{-1}dp/dr$, with $r$ increasing away from the low-pressure centre.
