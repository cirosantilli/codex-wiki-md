# Mixed micelle

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid digestion](lipid-digestion.md)

An aggregate containing amphipathic molecules such as [bile salts](bile-salt.md) and products of [lipid digestion](lipid-digestion.md), helping deliver poorly water-soluble lipids to intestinal absorptive surfaces.
