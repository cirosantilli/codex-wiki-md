# Nonparametrizability of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

An [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) in characteristic zero admits no nonconstant [rational map of projective varieties](rational-map-of-projective-varieties.md) from the [projective line](projective-line.md). Over $\mathbb C$, write its equation as $y^2=\prod_{j=1}^3(x-e_j)$ with distinct $e_j$. Substituting $x=u/v$ with [coprime polynomials](coprime-polynomials.md) gives $(yv^2)^2=v\prod_j(u-e_jv)$. These four pairwise coprime factors must all be squares. The [polynomial pencil with four square members](polynomial-pencil-with-four-square-members.md) forces $u,v$ constant, ruling out a nonconstant [rational parametrization of an algebraic curve](rational-parametrization-of-an-algebraic-curve.md).
