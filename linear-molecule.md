# Linear molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

A [linear molecule](linear-molecule.md) has collinear [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) in its idealized [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md). Both a [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) and [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) can supply [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) examples, although their [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) need not give an observable permanent-[electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) spectrum.
