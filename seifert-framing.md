# Seifert framing

↑ **Parent:** [Framed link](framed-link.md)

The Seifert framing pushes a knot in the direction tangent to a [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md) and normal to its boundary. The knot and its push-off then have [linking number](linking-number.md) zero.
