# A group of prime order is cyclic

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic group](cyclic-group.md)

Let $G$ have [group order](order-of-a-finite-group.md) equal to a [prime number](prime-number-split.md) $p$. Choose any $g\ne e$. By [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md), the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) $g$ divides $p$, and it is greater than one. It is therefore $p$, so the [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) generated by $g$ is all of $G$. This proves that every nonidentity element generates $G$, and $G$ is isomorphic to the additive group of integers modulo $p$.
