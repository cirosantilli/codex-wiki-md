<h1 id="lubin-tate-formal-group">Lubin–Tate formal group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubin–Tate_formal_group)

A [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md) associated with a [Lubin–Tate series](lubin-tate-series.md) $f$ is the unique one-dimensional commutative [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) $\mathcal F_f$ over the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O_K$ for which $f$ is an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) with linear coefficient the chosen [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$. The [Lubin–Tate functional equation lemma](lubin-tate-functional-equation-lemma.md) constructs the law and the [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) $[a]_f$ for every $a\in\mathcal O_K$, with linear term $aX$. Their addition is given by the law, and their multiplication by composition. Thus the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) acts on the group; $[\pi]_f=f$.
