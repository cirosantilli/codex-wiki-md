# Brewster reflection from an ionospheric interface

↑ **Parent:** [Brewster's angle](brewster-s-angle.md)

In the sharp planar, nonmagnetic [ionosphere](ionosphere.md) model with frequency above the [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md), the [refractive index](refractive-index.md) is $n=\sqrt{1-\omega_p^2/\omega^2}<1$. From air, the [Brewster angle](brewster-s-angle.md) satisfies $\tan\theta_B=n$. At that angle the reflected p-polarized wave vanishes, while s polarization retains a nonzero reflected component. A ground-to-ground single reflection from height $h$ spans $D=2h\tan\theta_B=2hn$. This is below the [critical angle for total internal reflection](critical-angle-for-total-internal-reflection.md) for [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md), which reflects both polarizations in this lossless isotropic model. The distinction diagnoses ordinary Brewster reflection rather than total internal reflection.
