# Vaccination status

↑ **Parent:** [Vaccination](vaccination.md)

A record of which [vaccinations](vaccination.md) a person has received at a specified time. A binary [categorical variable](categorical-variable.md) may collapse this into two recorded categories; the categories alone do not specify number of doses, timing, protective [immunity](immunity-medical.md) or willingness to receive a [vaccine](vaccine.md).
