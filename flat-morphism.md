# Flat morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_morphism)

A morphism $f:X\to Y$ is flat at $x\in X$ when the local ring $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a [flat module](flat-module.md) over $\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}$. It is flat when it is flat at every point.
