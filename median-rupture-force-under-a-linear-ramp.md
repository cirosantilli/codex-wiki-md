# Median rupture force under a linear ramp

↑ **Parent:** [Survival under a linear force ramp](survival-under-a-linear-force-ramp.md)

Solving $p(F_{1/2})=1/2$ for [survival under a linear force ramp](survival-under-a-linear-force-ramp.md) gives the displayed expression. At large $f/(k_0F_0)$, its slope against $\log f$ approaches $F_0$; at low loading rate the [median](median.md) is approximately $f\log2/k_0$. A full fit can infer activation distance $k_BT/F_0$ and zero-force escape rate $k_0$. An absolute barrier additionally needs an attempt prefactor, and equilibrium binding strength additionally needs reverse-state information.
