# Uncountability by interleaving prescribed binary coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Bitstream](bitstream.md)

Partition the positive [natural numbers](natural-number.md) into three infinite coordinate [sets](set-split.md). Fix the coordinates on the first two [sets](set-split.md) to any prescribed binary values, and leave those on the third [set](set-split.md) free. Reading the third coordinate [set](set-split.md) embeds all infinite [binary sequences](bitstream.md) in the resulting family, so the family is an [uncountable set](uncountable-set.md) by [Cantor's diagonal argument](cantor-s-diagonal-argument.md). For example, fixing $x_{3k-2}=0$ and $x_{3k-1}=a_{3k-1}$ while leaving $x_{3k}$ free guarantees infinitely many zeros and infinitely many agreements with any prescribed [binary sequence](bitstream.md) $(a_n)$. Infinite free coordinates remain even after both requirements are enforced.
