# Rank-two Killing tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Killing tensor](killing-tensor.md)

A symmetric two-tensor satisfying this equation gives a [quadratic geodesic first integral](quadratic-geodesic-first-integral.md). Examples include the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), symmetric products of metric-dual [Killing vector fields](killing-vector-field.md), and the [square of a Killing-Yano two-form](square-of-a-killing-yano-two-form.md).
