# Axisymmetric high-frequency capacitor field

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement current](displacement-current.md)

If a thin [capacitor](capacitor.md) interior is modeled with axially uniform axisymmetric fields, Maxwell's equations give $r^{-1}(rE_z')'+(\omega/c)^2E_z=0$. The solution regular at the axis is proportional to $J_0(\omega r/c)$. Normalizing to a central sinusoidal [voltage](voltage.md) gives $E_z=-(V_0/L)J_0(\omega r/c)\sin\omega t$ in this ideal interior model. It can change sign across the radius when propagation matters. Feed, edge and radiation [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) are needed for a full driven-device solution.
