# Coherent topology

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent category](coherent-category.md)

The coherent topology on a small [coherent category](coherent-category.md) is generated by finite families of arrows whose image subobjects have union equal to the whole target. Pullback stability of images and unions and closure under composition make these a coverage. On a [coherent syntactic category](coherent-syntactic-category.md), this is the site topology used to construct the [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md) of its [coherent theory](coherent-theory.md).
