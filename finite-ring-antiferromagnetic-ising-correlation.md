# Finite-ring antiferromagnetic Ising correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md)

The exact zero-field [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md) $C_n=\langle S_1S_{n+1}\rangle$ in the finite [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) of an [antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md). The [spin-chain transfer matrix](transfer-matrix-for-a-classical-spin-chain.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_+=2\cosh(\beta J)$ and $\lambda_-=-2\sinh(\beta J)$. In their [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md), an [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) insertion exchanges the two [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). The [trace](matrix-trace.md) with two insertions is therefore $\lambda_-^n\lambda_+^{N-n}+\lambda_+^n\lambda_-^{N-n}$, while the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $\lambda_+^N+\lambda_-^N$, proving the formula. Its two terms retain both paths around the ring. It has $C_0=1$ and $C_n=C_{N-n}$. At fixed $n$ and finite positive [inverse temperature](inverse-temperature.md) its [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) is $(-\tanh\beta J)^n$.
