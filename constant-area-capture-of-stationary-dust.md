# Constant-area capture of stationary dust

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md)

A body of initial [mass](mass.md) $M_0$ and [speed](speed.md) $v_0$ collects all stationary dust swept out by constant area $A$ in a uniform cloud of [mass density](density.md) $\rho$. Then $M=M_0+\rho Ax$ and $Mv=M_0v_0$ by [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md). Integration gives the displayed [mass](mass.md) law, $v=M_0v_0/M$ and $x=(M-M_0)/(\rho A)$. Sticking dissipates [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) at rate $\dot Mv^2/2$. The model assumes no external longitudinal [force](force-split.md) and negligible relativistic corrections.
