# Cutoff surface term for a Sine-Gordon kink

↑ **Parent:** [Mode-number regularization of soliton masses](mode-number-regularization-of-soliton-masses.md)

The [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md) $\delta(k)=2\arctan(m/k)$ of the [Sine-Gordon kink fluctuation operator](sine-gordon-kink-fluctuation-operator.md) gives a continuum frequency sum. In [mode-number regularization of soliton masses](mode-number-regularization-of-soliton-masses.md), the zero-frequency translation mode replaces the vacuum oscillator at $k=0$. The bare difference is $-m/2-(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^\Lambda\delta(k)k/\sqrt{k^2+m^2}\,dk$. Integration by parts cancels the lower endpoint against $-m/2$ and produces the displayed upper surface term. Although $\delta(\Lambda)$ vanishes, its product with $\omega(\Lambda)$ tends to $2m$. Vacuum [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) cancels the logarithmic bulk [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md), leaving this finite [one-loop soliton mass correction](one-loop-soliton-mass-correction.md). Parameters and finite [counterterms](counterterm.md) must be fixed by a stated [renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md).
