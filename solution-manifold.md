# Solution manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Jet space of a scalar ordinary differential equation](jet-space-of-a-scalar-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

An ordinary differential equation $\Delta(x,u,u',\ldots,u^{(n)})=0$ defines a solution manifold in [jet space](jet-space-of-a-scalar-ordinary-differential-equation.md). The prolonged graph of every classical solution lies in this manifold.
