# Independent censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Censoring (statistics)](censoring-statistics.md)

A simple sufficient [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) assumption is that the potential observation-ending time $C$ and [survival time](survival-time.md) $T$ are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md). More generally, appropriate [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) given the [covariates](covariate.md) used in the analysis can suffice for analysis within those conditions. The requirement concerns the potential times, rather than [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of the observed duration $Y=\min(T,C)$ and event indicator $\delta$: those recorded quantities ordinarily depend on each other.

For an ordinary pooled [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md), the observed [risk set](risk-set.md) must represent the future event experience of everyone still event-free at each time. [Conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) given a [covariate](covariate.md) does not alone justify ignoring that [covariate](covariate.md) in a pooled calculation. Also, there must be a positive [probability](probability.md) of remaining observed through the horizons being estimated. If follow-up ends at a common closing time, it supplies no observations to identify the later [survival function](survival-function.md). Independent subjects with a common [survival distribution](survival-distribution.md) and [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) form the basic model in [Lu Tian's STAT331 notes, Unit 3, pages 1–4](https://web.stanford.edu/~lutian/coursepdf/STAT331unit3.pdf).

For example, subjects who stop attending because their condition is deteriorating can produce [informative censoring](informative-censoring.md). A predetermined common closing date can instead give [administrative censoring](administrative-censoring.md), provided calendar entry and other relevant conditions do not select future [survival times](survival-time.md). The reason for stopping observation must be justified from study design and context; the recorded event indicators alone cannot verify [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md).
