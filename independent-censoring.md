# Independent censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Censoring (statistics)](censoring-statistics.md)

Independent censoring means that, conditional on the variables used in the analysis, the censoring time carries no further information about the event time. It lets the observed [risk set](risk-set.md) represent those who would remain at risk without censoring.
