# DNA damage checkpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

A control system that changes cell-cycle progression in response to damaged [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Checkpoint activation is a response to damage rather than a guarantee that every lesion will be repaired.
