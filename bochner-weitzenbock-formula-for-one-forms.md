# Bochner-Weitzenbock formula for one-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md)

For a real one-form $\alpha$, the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) differs from the [rough Laplacian](rough-laplacian.md) by the action of [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md): $(\operatorname{Ric}\cdot\alpha)(X)=\alpha(\operatorname{Ric}^{\sharp}X)$, where $g(\operatorname{Ric}^{\sharp}X,Y)=\operatorname{Ric}(X,Y)$. Equivalently, with the nonnegative scalar Laplacian convention,

$$
\tfrac12\Delta|\alpha|^2=\langle\Delta\alpha,\alpha\rangle-|\nabla\alpha|^2-\operatorname{Ric}(\alpha^{\sharp},\alpha^{\sharp}).
$$
