# Resolution of the identity in an orthonormal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md)

For a real [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $u_1,\ldots,u_n$, each [vector](vector.md) has expansion $x=\sum_j u_j(u_j^Tx)$. Since this holds for every $x$, the sum of the rank-one [orthogonal projection matrices](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) $u_ju_j^T$ is the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md). Their pairwise products vanish for distinct basis vectors. For a complex [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), replace the [transpose](transpose.md) by the [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md).
