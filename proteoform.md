# Proteoform

↑ **Parent:** [Proteomics](proteomics.md)

A molecular form of a [protein](protein-split.md) defined by its [amino-acid](amino-acid.md) sequence and covalent modifications, including processing. Different conformations and mass-spectrometric charge states do not by themselves define different [proteoforms](proteoform.md).
