# Homogenisation on a uniform layer

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md)

On the [uniform layer of the Boolean cube](uniform-layer-of-the-boolean-cube.md) $\Omega_r$, every [multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $s\leq r$ is in the [span](linear-span.md) of the degree-$s$ square-free [monomials](monomial.md). For $T\subseteq[n]$, $|T|=j\leq s$, and $x_T=\prod_{i\in T}x_i$, the identity is

$$
x_T=\binom{r-j}{s-j}^{-1}\sum_{\substack{S\supseteq T\\|S|=s}}x_S\quad\text{on }\Omega_r.
$$

At the [characteristic vector of a set](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) $A$ of size $r$, both sides vanish if $T\nsubseteq A$. Otherwise exactly $\binom{r-j}{s-j}$ summands are $1$. Hence the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the restricted polynomial space is at most $\binom ns$. No assertion of independence of the spanning [monomials](monomial.md) is needed, so no condition $r+s\leq n$ is required. Unlike ordinary [homogenization](homogenization-algebra.md), this operation preserves degree bounds by using the fixed-weight evaluation domain.
