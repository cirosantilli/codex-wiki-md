# Force-extension of an intrinsically curved filament

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic curvature of an elastic filament](intrinsic-curvature-of-an-elastic-filament.md)

For a planar [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) under positive constant extension force $F$, the quadratic force-controlled energy is

$$
\mathcal H=\frac12\int\left[B(\zeta''-\kappa_0)^2+F(\zeta')^2\right]dx.
$$

The [higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation](higher-order-euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $B\zeta''''-F\zeta''=B\kappa_0''$. On a periodic interval or in a bulk [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) treatment, every nonzero mode obeys the displayed transfer relation. The length deficit is $\frac12\int(\zeta')^2dx$, so its ensemble value is determined by the [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md) of the [quenched intrinsic curvature](quenched-intrinsic-curvature.md). Boundary conditions must be specified for a finite filament.
