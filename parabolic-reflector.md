# Parabolic reflector

↑ **Parent:** [Mirror](mirror.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabolic_reflector)

A [parabolic reflector](parabolic-reflector.md) has a paraboloidal reflecting [surface](topological-surface-split.md). The [law of reflection](law-of-reflection.md) makes all incident [light rays](light-ray.md) parallel to its [optical axis](optical-axis.md) pass through one focus in ideal [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md). This removes on-axis [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md) and explains its use as a receiving [antenna](antenna-radio.md) for distant sources.
