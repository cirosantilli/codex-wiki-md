# Depolarization field

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization density](polarization-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depolarization_field)

The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) produced by the [bound charge](bound-charge.md) of a polarized body is its [depolarization field](depolarization-field.md). For a uniformly polarized [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md), the internal [depolarization field](depolarization-field.md) is uniform and opposes the relevant component of the [electric polarization](polarization-density.md): $E_{{\rm dep},i}=-N_iP_i/\epsilon_0$. Its dimensionless geometric factors obey $N_x+N_y+N_z=1$.
