# Killed Brownian transition density

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian transition density](brownian-transition-density.md)

The area density of $\mathbb P_z(B_t\in dw,\ t<T_D)$ for [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) killed on its first exit from $D$. Its total mass is the survival probability, not generally one. Integrating this density over time gives the [Green function of killed planar Brownian motion](green-function-of-killed-planar-brownian-motion.md).
