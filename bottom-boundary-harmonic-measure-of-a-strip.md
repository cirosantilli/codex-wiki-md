# Bottom-boundary harmonic measure of a strip

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started at $x+iy$ in $0<y<\pi$, this is its unconditional exit density on the bottom boundary. Its mass is $1-y/\pi$; the remaining mass $y/\pi$ lies on the top boundary. The [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) $z\mapsto e^z$, the [Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) and the boundary [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) derive the formula. Conditional bottom-boundary density requires division by its mass.
