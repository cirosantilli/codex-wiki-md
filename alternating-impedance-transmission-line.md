# Alternating-impedance transmission line

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)

A periodic lossless line can consist of equal-length sections with impedances $Z_1,Z_2$. Its semi-infinite input is a fixed point of the two-section impedance transformation. The physical solution selects a forward-energy or decaying [Bloch wave](bloch-state.md). At special [frequencies](frequency.md) where the cell [matrix](matrix.md) is the identity, the fixed-point equation alone loses uniqueness; limiting absorption or a termination specifies the response.
