# Unique lifting property of a discrete fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete fibration](discrete-fibration.md)

An incoming [morphism](morphism.md) into the image of a specified object has exactly one lift with that specified codomain. Both its source object and its lifted [morphism](morphism.md) are determined. Applying uniqueness to identity and composite [morphisms](morphism.md) gives the functoriality of lifting.
