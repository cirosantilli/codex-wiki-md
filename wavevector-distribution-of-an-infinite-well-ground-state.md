# Wavevector distribution of an infinite-well ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

A centred width-$L$ [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) has [ground state](ground-state.md) [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) $\sqrt{2/L}\cos(\pi x/L)$ inside and zero outside. Its [momentum representation in quantum mechanics](momentum-representation-in-quantum-mechanics.md) follows from the [Fourier transform of a truncated cosine](fourier-transform-of-a-truncated-cosine.md). The [probability density](probability-density.md) in the title has removable singularities at $k=\pm\pi/L$, with limit $L/(4\pi)$, and an oscillatory $k^{-4}$ tail envelope. The distribution is even, with $\langle k\rangle=0$ and $\langle k^2\rangle=\pi^2/L^2$.
