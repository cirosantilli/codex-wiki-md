# Neutron capture resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron activation](neutron-activation.md)

A [neutron capture resonance](neutron-capture-resonance.md) is an [energy](energy-split.md) interval where a matching excited state of the combined [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) enhances capture. Its [collision cross-section](collision-cross-section.md) can have a peak rather than a monotone dependence on [energy](energy-split.md). The [resonance pole](resonance-pole.md) description connects this to [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md). Slowing a [neutron](neutron-split.md) through such a region can first increase and then decrease a particular reaction response. The capture probability also competes with other reaction channels.
