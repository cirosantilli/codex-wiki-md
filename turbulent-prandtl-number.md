# Turbulent Prandtl number

↑ **Parent:** [Eddy diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md)

The ratio of turbulent momentum diffusivity to buoyancy or heat diffusivity. Their ratio determines how the [gradient Richardson number](gradient-richardson-number.md) is related to the energy-budget [Flux Richardson number](flux-richardson-number.md). It is generally a closure-dependent function of the flow, not necessarily one.
