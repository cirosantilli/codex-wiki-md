# Perpendicular magnetohydrodynamic shock

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic shock](magnetohydrodynamic-shock.md)

For a planar [magnetohydrodynamic shock](magnetohydrodynamic-shock.md) with normal [velocity](velocity.md) and tangential [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho u=m$ and the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) gives $uB$ constant across the front. Hence $B/\rho$ is unchanged, expressing [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md). Normal [momentum](momentum.md) flux is $\rho u^2+p+B^2/(2\mu_0)$, and energy flux per unit mass flux is $u^2/2+\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]+B^2/(\mu_0\rho)$, apart from a smooth common [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). The amplified tangential field contributes both [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) and [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) transport.
