# Brouwer inward-pointing zero lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md)

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let $F:V\to V$ be continuous. If $\langle F(x),x\rangle<0$ on the sphere $\|x\|=R$, then $F$ has a zero in the open ball. Otherwise the radial projection of $F$ gives a map of the ball that contradicts the [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md).
