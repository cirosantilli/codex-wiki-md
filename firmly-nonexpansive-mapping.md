# Firmly nonexpansive mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

A mapping $T$ on an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) is firmly nonexpansive when

$$
\|T(v)-T(w)\|^2
\leq\langle T(v)-T(w),v-w\rangle.
$$

Every firmly nonexpansive mapping is [nonexpansive](nonexpansive-mapping.md), and the resolvent of a maximal [monotone operator](monotone-operator.md) is firmly nonexpansive.
