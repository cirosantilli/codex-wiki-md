# Reflection at a string junction carrying a point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection at a density step on a string](reflection-at-a-density-step-on-a-string.md)

Two semi-infinite strings with common [tension](tension.md) $T$ and a point mass $M$ at their join have continuous transverse [displacement](displacement.md), $A+B=C$. With time factor $e^{i\omega t}$ and a right-moving incident factor $e^{-ik_1x}$, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) at the join gives $k_1T(A-B)=(k_2T+iM\omega^2)C$. Solving gives the displayed [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) and $t=C/A=2k_1T/[T(k_1+k_2)+iM\omega^2]$. Their power fractions obey $|r|^2+(k_2/k_1)|t|^2=1$. Reversing the time convention complex-conjugates the coefficients. The limits $M=0$ and $M\to\infty$ recover a massless join and a fixed boundary, respectively.
