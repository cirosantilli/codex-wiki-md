# Gluon-fusion Higgs production

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

Production of a [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) from two [gluons](gluon.md), mediated at leading order in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) by a quark loop. The large [top quark](top-quark.md) [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) makes the top loop important. There is no direct renormalizable tree-level coupling of the colorless [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) to two gluons.
