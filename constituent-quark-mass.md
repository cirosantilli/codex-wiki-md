# Constituent quark mass

↑ **Parent:** [Quark mass](quark-mass.md)

A [constituent quark mass](constituent-quark-mass.md) is an effective mass parameter in a low-energy [constituent-quark model](constituent-quark-model.md). It accounts approximately for strong-interaction dressing and some spin-independent bound-state dynamics; it is not identical to a renormalized [quark mass](quark-mass.md) in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md).
