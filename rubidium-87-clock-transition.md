# Rubidium-87 clock transition

↑ **Parent:** [Clock transition](clock-transition.md)

The ground-hyperfine magnetic-dipole transition between $F=1,m_F=0$ and $F=2,m_F=0$ has vanishing first-order Zeeman shift near zero field. It obeys $\Delta F=1,\Delta m_F=0$. Its residual quadratic shift follows from the [Breit-Rabi formula](breit-rabi-formula.md), rather than from assigning both states permanently zero [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md).
