# Cardinality of continuous maps determined on a countable dense set

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

Continuous [real-valued functions](real-valued-function.md) on a space with a [countable](countable-set.md) [dense subset](dense-set.md) are determined by their restrictions to that [subset](subset.md). The equality [set](set-split.md) of two such [functions](function-split.md) is closed and contains the [dense subset](dense-set.md). Hence there are at most $|\mathbb R|^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}$ such [functions](function-split.md). If the domain is nonempty, distinct [constant functions](constant-function.md) give the matching [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md).
