# Magnetic-tension response to an incompressible displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md)

In [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md), a small displacement of a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) obeys $\delta\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\xi\times\mathbf B_0)$. For $\mathbf B_0=B_0\mathbf e_z$, a horizontal [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) proportional to $e^{ikz}$ with $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi=0$ gives the displayed field and force perturbations. The linear [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) perturbation vanishes because $\delta B_z=0$. The remaining [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) acts as a restoring spring, with [Alfvén frequency](alfven-frequency.md) $kv_A$.
