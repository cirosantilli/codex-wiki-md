# Permafrost carbon feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Permafrost](permafrost.md)

Thawing [permafrost](permafrost.md) makes previously frozen organic matter available for decomposition, releasing [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) and, in waterlogged oxygen-poor conditions, [methane](methane.md). These [greenhouse gases](greenhouse-gas.md) amplify warming, which can cause further thaw. The amount reaching the atmosphere depends on decomposition, transport and oxidation; this feedback does not imply an inevitable abrupt release of an entire carbon reservoir.
