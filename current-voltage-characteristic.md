# Current-voltage characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

A [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md) describes the relation between terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md) for specified physical conditions. A [resistor](resistor.md) obeying [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) gives a [straight line](straight-line.md); a [diode](diode.md) has an asymmetric relation.
