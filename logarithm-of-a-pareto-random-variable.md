# Logarithm of a Pareto random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md)

If $X$ has a [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) with positive scale $x_m$ and positive shape $\alpha$, then for $z\geq0$,

$$
\mathbb P(\log(X/x_m)>z)=\mathbb P(X>x_m e^z)=e^{-\alpha z}.
$$

Thus the rescaled [logarithm](logarithm.md) has an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\alpha$. The [tail probability](tail-probability.md) calculation also identifies the support as $z\geq0$.
