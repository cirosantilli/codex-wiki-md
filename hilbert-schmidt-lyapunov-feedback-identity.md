# Hilbert-Schmidt Lyapunov feedback identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov quantum control](lyapunov-quantum-control.md)

For $V=\|\rho-\rho_d\|_{\rm HS}^2$, with both states evolving under the same drift and only $\rho$ receiving $fH_1$, cyclicity of the [trace](matrix-trace.md) cancels the drift terms and gives the displayed identity. The scalar $C$ is real because $-i[H_1,\rho]$ is Hermitian. Choosing $f=kC$, $k>0$, makes $\dot V=-2kC^2\leq0$. This is nonincrease, not by itself convergence to the target: additional invariant-set and reachability conditions are needed.
