# Field-independent stationary threshold in oblique magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Oblique-field plane-wave magnetoconvection](oblique-field-plane-wave-magnetoconvection.md)

At inclination $\alpha=\arctan\sqrt2$ and vertical wavenumber one, the nonmagnetic minimizing wavenumber $k=-1/\sqrt2$ also satisfies $\mathbf c\cdot\mathbf k=0$. The stationary threshold is the sum of $F(k)\geq27/4$ and a nonnegative magnetic penalty that vanishes there. Therefore $k_c=-1/\sqrt2$ and $R_c=27/4$ for every $Q\geq0$. The selected disturbance is constant along the imposed field, so the linear [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md) produces no magnetic perturbation and there is no field-line tension penalty.
