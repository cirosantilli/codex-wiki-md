# Local-unitary invariance of entanglement entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Local unitary operation](local-unitary-operation.md)

A local unitary sends a [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) to a unitary conjugate on the acted-on subsystem and leaves the other subsystem unchanged. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and von Neumann entropy therefore do not change. This distinguishes a deterministic change of local basis from a selective measurement or nonunitary local filter.
