# Five-qubit stabilizer syndrome table

↑ **Parent:** [Five-qubit error correcting code](five-qubit-error-correcting-code.md)

The four commuting independent [stabilizer generators](stabilizer-generator.md) define a two-dimensional [stabilizer code](stabilizer-code.md). The identity and fifteen single-site [Pauli errors](pauli-operator.md) have sixteen distinct four-bit [error syndromes](error-syndrome.md), so their two-dimensional images form an [orthogonal direct sum](orthogonal-direct-sum.md) of the physical space. This proves nondegenerate single-site correction and the [perfect quantum error-correcting code](perfect-quantum-error-correcting-code.md) property. The operators $X^{\otimes5}$ and $Z^{\otimes5}$ commute with the stabilizer and anticommute with each other. Multiplying $X^{\otimes5}$ by $XZZXI$ gives, up to phase, $IYYIX$, a nontrivial weight-three logical operator, proving distance exactly three.
