# Mass threshold after photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

A stationary particle of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$ absorbing a [photon](photon.md) of [energy](energy.md) $E$ acquires total [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md)

$$
M=\sqrt{m^2+2mE/c^2}.
$$

This follows from [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md), since the incoming [photon](photon.md) has [momentum](momentum.md) magnitude $E/c$. If the system splits into two equal particles, the largest kinematically permitted daughter [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) is $M/2$, attained when both are at rest in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md). Their common laboratory [speed](speed.md) at threshold is $cE/(mc^2+E)$. Above this daughter-mass threshold there is insufficient [centre-of-momentum energy](centre-of-momentum-energy.md); below it, relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is available.
