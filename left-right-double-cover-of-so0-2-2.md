<h1 id="left-right-double-cover-of-so0-2-2">Left-right double cover of SO0(2,2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix model of anti-de Sitter three-space](matrix-model-of-anti-de-sitter-three-space.md)

The action $(A,B):M\mapsto AMB^{-1}$ preserves the [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $-\det M$ on real two-by-two matrices. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) consists of the simultaneous scalar pairs $(I,I)$ and $(-I,-I)$. The differential $(a,b):M\mapsto aM-Mb$ is injective on the two traceless [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md), and both source and target have dimension six. Its image is therefore open and, since the source is connected, equals the [identity component of a Lie group](identity-component-of-a-lie-group.md) $SO_0(2,2)$. This gives a two-sheeted [covering map](covering-space.md) onto the identity component, not onto the disconnected full [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) $SO(2,2)$.
