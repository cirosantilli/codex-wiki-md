# Nonclassical state of light

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

A state of light is nonclassical when it is not a [classical mixture of coherent states](classical-mixture-of-coherent-states.md). The [Glauber-Sudarshan representation](glauber-sudarshan-representation.md) states this criterion in phase space. Examples include nonvacuum [number states](number-state.md) and [quadrature squeezing](quadrature-squeezing.md) states. A coherent state is on the classical boundary despite its vacuum fluctuations; [thermal light](thermal-light.md) is a classical mixture despite its non-Poisson fluctuations. None of these [nonclassicality witnesses](nonclassicality-witnesses.md) is a necessary condition for nonclassicality.
