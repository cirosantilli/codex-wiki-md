# Engineering dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization group](renormalization-group.md)

The engineering dimension of a field, parameter or operator is its dimension under the rescaling that leaves the quadratic theory invariant. Interactions can change it into a full [scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md).
