# Bruhat decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Reductive group](reductive-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruhat_decomposition)

For a connected reductive algebraic group, a [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) $B$, and a maximal torus $T\subseteq B$, the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) $W=N_G(T)/T$ indexes the double cosets:

$$
G=\bigsqcup_{w\in W}B\dot wB.
$$
