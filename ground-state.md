# Ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_state)

A ground state is a normalized [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) that attains the lowest possible [energy](energy.md) of a [quantum system](quantum-system.md). Precisely, let the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$ be a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) bounded below on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), and write $E_*=\inf\sigma(H)$ for the [infimum](infimum.md) of its [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md). A [ground state](ground-state.md) is a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\psi_0$ in the [operator domain](operator-domain.md) $D(H)$ satisfying

$$
H\psi_0=E_*\psi_0,\qquad \|\psi_0\|=1.
$$

Thus a [ground state](ground-state.md) exists exactly when the spectral bottom $E_*$ is an [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue.md). The lowest [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue.md) among some listed [bound states](bound-state.md) is insufficient if other parts of the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) lie below it. The [ground-state subspace](ground-state-subspace.md) collects all minimizing [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md); it can have [ground-state degeneracy](ground-state-degeneracy.md). The [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md) characterizes $E_*$ as the [infimum](infimum.md) of normalized [energy](energy.md) [expectation values](expectation-value.md). [Gerald Teschl, Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics, Theorem 2.19](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-schroe/schroe.pdf).

For the one-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), the [ground state](ground-state.md) has [energy](energy.md) $\hbar\omega/2$ above the minimum of the [potential energy](potential-energy.md). This [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) is compatible with being the lowest allowed [energy](energy.md): a localized [wavefunction](wave-function.md) cannot simultaneously have zero [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). For a [free particle](free-particle.md) on the whole line, the spectral bottom is zero but is unattained by any [normalizable wavefunction](normalizable-wavefunction.md); see [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md). A lower bound therefore need not describe a physically preparable [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md).

The [ground state](ground-state.md) supplies a reference for excitation costs and for the [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md). A [ground state](ground-state.md) is also a [stationary state](stationary-state.md) under a time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md): its [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) changes, while its position [probability density](probability-density.md) stays fixed. A nonzero [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) does not imply motion along a classical trajectory, and [ground-state degeneracy](ground-state-degeneracy.md) means there need not be a unique lowest [quantum state](quantum-state.md).
