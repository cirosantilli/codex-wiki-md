# Subgroup distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometrically embedded subgroup](quasi-isometrically-embedded-subgroup.md)

For a finitely generated subgroup $H\leq G$, subgroup distortion compares the intrinsic word length $|h|_H$ with the ambient word length $|h|_G$. The subgroup is undistorted exactly when its inclusion is a [quasi-isometric embedding](quasi-isometric-embedding.md).

This is the subgroup word-metric instance of [distortion](distortion-mathematics.md).
