# Higgs boson coupling to Z bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

The neutral vector mass term is $\frac12m_Z^2Z_\mu Z^\mu(1+H/v)^2$. Expanding it gives the cubic $HZZ$ and quartic $HHZZ$ interactions in [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md). Differentiating the action with respect to identical fields gives [Feynman rules](feynman-rule.md) $2im_Z^2g_{\mu\nu}/v$ and $2im_Z^2g_{\mu\nu}/v^2$. A single $Z$ does not couple to two copies of the real neutral radial [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) through an elementary vertex.
