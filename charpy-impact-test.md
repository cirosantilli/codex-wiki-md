# Charpy impact test

↑ **Parent:** [Toughness](toughness.md)

A pendulum impact test on a specified notched specimen, commonly used to measure absorbed energy as a function of [temperature](temperature-split.md). A transition curve characterizes a [ductile-to-brittle transition](ductile-to-brittle-transition.md), but its energy is not itself a [fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md) value.[§2, Background: measured transition quantities and their dependence on temperature](nist-software-package-for-obtaining-charpy-transition-curves-by-enrico-lucon-and-others.md)
