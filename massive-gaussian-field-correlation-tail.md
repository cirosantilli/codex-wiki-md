# Massive Gaussian field correlation tail

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian field theory](gaussian-field-theory.md)

For the positive quadratic kernel $\widetilde\Delta(p)=\kappa^{-1}p^2+m^2$, $\kappa,m>0$, the [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) is its inverse. Fourier inversion gives $G(r)=\kappa(\sqrt\kappa m/r)^{D/2-1}K_{D/2-1}(\sqrt\kappa mr)/(2\pi)^{D/2}$. The [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md) has an exponentially decaying large-argument tail, so the [correlation length](correlation-length.md) is $1/(\sqrt\kappa m)$ at fixed positive [gradient](gradient.md) coefficient. In three dimensions this becomes $\kappa e^{-\sqrt\kappa mr}/(4\pi r)$. The statement concerns the continuum long-distance theory; regulator-dependent contact terms do not define the physical [correlation length](correlation-length.md).
