# Fourier sine basis

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md)

The functions $\sqrt{2/\pi}\sin(nx)$, $n\geq1$, form a complete [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $L^2(0,\pi)$. An odd extension to $(-\pi,\pi)$ reduces completeness to that of the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md). Equality of all sine coefficients therefore implies equality almost everywhere; if both functions are continuous, it implies equality everywhere.
