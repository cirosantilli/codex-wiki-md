# Group I catalytic intron

↑ **Parent:** [Intron](intron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_I_catalytic_intron)

A class of [introns](intron.md) with a conserved [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) catalytic core and a two-step [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md) mechanism initiated by external [guanosine](guanosine.md). Some [group I introns](group-i-catalytic-intron.md) self-splice under suitable conditions; others require [protein](protein-split.md) assistance. The [ribozyme](ribozyme.md) body explains the Tetrahymena example.
