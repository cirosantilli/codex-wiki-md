# Partition-regular system forcing an ordered Schur relation

↑ **Parent:** [Rado's theorem](rado-s-theorem.md)

The positive-variable system $3x+y=3z$, $x-2y=w$ is [partition regular](partition-regular-matrix.md). Its columns for $x,z,w$ sum to zero, and the remaining $y$ column lies in their rational span, satisfying the [columns condition](columns-property.md). Thus every finite colouring has monochromatic positive $x,y,z,w$ with $2y<x$. A triangular [m-p-c set](m-p-c-set.md) gives the explicit solution $x=3u$, $y=3v$, $z=3u+v$, $w=3u-6v$, with positivity guaranteed by membership in that set.
