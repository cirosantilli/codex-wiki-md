# Frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequency)

Frequency is the number of repetitions of a periodic phenomenon per unit time. For a [wave](wave.md) of period $T$, its frequency is $\nu=1/T$, measured in hertz. The [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\omega=2\pi\nu$.
