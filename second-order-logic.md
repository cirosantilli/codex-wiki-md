# Second-order logic

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-order_logic)

A logic allowing quantification over relations or [subsets](subset.md) of the object domain as well as over its individual objects. The range of relation quantifiers depends on the chosen semantics.
