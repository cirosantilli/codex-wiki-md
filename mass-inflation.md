# Mass inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md)

[Mass inflation](mass-inflation.md) is the growth of the interior effective mass and curvature near an inner [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md) under suitably perturbed black-hole data. It can obstruct regular spacetime extensions that exist for an exact [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md). The extension regularity matters: curvature blowup alone does not rule out every continuous-metric extension.
