# Conformal map of a segment complement to a punctured disk

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

On $\mathbb C\setminus[-1,1]$, take the [principal square root](principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) with positive real part. The [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $(z-1)/(z+1)$ has image $\bigl(\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,0]\bigr)\setminus\{1\}$. The [principal square root](principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) therefore maps to the right half-plane with $1$ omitted, and the final [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) gives exactly $0<|w|<1$. The inverse is $z=-(w+w^{-1})/2$. Adding $\infty$ to the source on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) fills the missing value $w=0$ and gives a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) onto the full disk. Without $\infty$, a loop surrounding the segment has nonzero [winding number](winding-number.md), so the source is not a [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md) and cannot be [conformally equivalent](conformal-equivalence.md) to a disk.
