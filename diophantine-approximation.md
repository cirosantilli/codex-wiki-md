# Diophantine approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantine_approximation)

Diophantine approximation studies how closely numbers can be approximated by [rational](rational-number.md) or [algebraic numbers](algebraic-number.md) subject to bounds on their arithmetic complexity.
