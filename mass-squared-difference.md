# Mass-squared difference

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

The difference between the squared [masses](mass.md) of two propagation states. In vacuum its contribution to a [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) [probability](probability.md) has phase $\Delta m_{ij}^2L/(4E)$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). The exact identity $\Delta m_{13}^2=\Delta m_{12}^2+\Delta m_{23}^2$ constrains any proposed three-state numerical inputs.
