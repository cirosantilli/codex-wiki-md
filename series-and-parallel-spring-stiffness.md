# Series and parallel spring stiffness

↑ **Parent:** [Spring](spring.md)

Identical linear [springs](spring.md) of stiffness $k$ have equivalent stiffness $2k$ in parallel and $k/2$ in series. Parallel [springs](spring.md) have equal [displacement](displacement.md) and add [forces](force-split.md); series [springs](spring.md) carry equal [force](force-split.md) and add extensions. A [mass](mass.md) $m$ attached to an equivalent massless [spring](spring.md) has [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\sqrt{k_{\rm eff}/m}$.
