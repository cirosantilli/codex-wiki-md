# Proto-oncogene

↑ **Parent:** [Gene](gene.md)

A [proto-oncogene](proto-oncogene.md) is a normal cellular gene that can contribute to oncogenic transformation when its expression or activity is altered. Its normal presence is not evidence that a cell is malignant.
