<h1 id="first-massive-gso-projected-neveu-schwarz-states">First massive GSO-projected Neveu–Schwarz states</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [First massive level of a chiral RNS sector](first-massive-level-of-a-chiral-rns-sector.md)

At NS level $3/2$, the light-cone families are $b_{-3/2}^i|0\rangle$, $\alpha_{-1}^ib_{-1/2}^j|0\rangle$, and three distinct $b_{-1/2}$ oscillators. Their dimensions are $8+64+\binom83=128$. All have odd fermion-excitation parity. They assemble into the massive [Spin group](spin-group.md) representations $44+84$, a symmetric traceless tensor and a three-form of Spin(9).
