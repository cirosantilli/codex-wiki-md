# Survey completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical selection function](astronomical-selection-function.md)

[survey completeness](survey-completeness.md) is the fraction of a specified true source population recovered by a survey, conditional on properties such as flux, size and sky position. Injecting simulated sources into realistic data and applying the actual detection pipeline measures recovery probability. Completeness differs from purity: recovered objects can include false detections or incorrectly identified sources.
