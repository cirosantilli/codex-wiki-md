# Bardeen-Brattain point-contact transistor experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Point-contact transistor](point-contact-transistor.md)

In December 1947, [John Bardeen](john-bardeen.md) and [Walter Houser Brattain](walter-houser-brattain.md) obtained transistor action at Bell Telephone Laboratories. [William Shockley](william-shockley.md) proposed the distinct [bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md) within the following month.[Printed pp. 78–79](walter-houser-brattain-by-john-bardeen.md)

Their 1948 paper describes ground, etched [germanium](germanium.md), initially also anodized and rinsed. Surface contacts separated by $50$–$250\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ share a broad base contact. Positive emitter [biasing](biasing.md) injects [electron holes](electron-hole.md); negative collector [biasing](biasing.md) draws them toward the collector. An input [signal](signal.md) changes emitter [electric current](electric-current.md), and a collector [electrical load](electrical-load.md) converts the resulting change into voltage.[pp. 230–231, Fig. 1](the-transistor-a-semi-conductor-triode-by-john-bardeen-and-walter-houser-brattain.md)

Early [electric field](electric-field-split.md) control experiments gave unexpectedly weak responses. [John Bardeen](john-bardeen.md) proposed that [surface states](surface-state.md) screened the interior. Surface charge could create a barrier and a hole-rich [semiconductor inversion layer](semiconductor-inversion-layer.md). Replacing an [electrolyte](electrolyte.md) control with a [gold](gold.md) contact reversed the response sign, suggesting [electron hole](electron-hole.md) [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md). Closely spaced contacts made from a [gold](gold.md)-coated wedge split with a razor then enabled [power gain](power-gain.md). The relative importance of surface and bulk transport depended on preparation and collector forming.[Printed pp. 333–340, Figs. 6–12](semiconductor-research-leading-to-the-point-contact-transistor-by-john-bardeen.md)

The 1948 authors found that collector-current increments could exceed emitter-current increments; their [current gain](current-gain.md) depended on [biasing](biasing.md).[p. 231, amplification-factor definition](the-transistor-a-semi-conductor-triode-by-john-bardeen-and-walter-houser-brattain.md) A terminal-current ratio alone cannot identify the microscopic path. [Surface states](surface-state.md), the local barrier, [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md) and contact preparation prevent a single ideal modern [bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md) model from explaining every [point-contact transistor](point-contact-transistor.md).

For the conventional [electric current](electric-current.md) directions drawn in the figure, $I_E$ enters the emitter, $I_C$ leaves the collector and signed $I_B$ enters the base. [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) requires

$$
I_E+I_B=I_C,\qquad
\alpha=\left(\frac{\partial I_C}{\partial I_E}\right)_{V_C},\qquad
\delta I_B=(\alpha-1)\delta I_E\quad\text{at fixed }V_C.
$$

Thus a local [current gain](current-gain.md) $\alpha>1$ requires an accompanying base-current change. The collector increment is not simply a count of injected [electron holes](electron-hole.md), and it does not violate [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) conservation.

For an experiment following the functional arrangement, first establish each contact's separate [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md) relative to the base. Then hold collector [biasing](biasing.md) fixed, vary a small emitter [signal](signal.md), and compare the collector [electrical load](electrical-load.md) voltage with the input. A decisive [power gain](power-gain.md) check measures both input [electric current](electric-current.md) and voltage and the output [power](power.md) in a known [electrical load](electrical-load.md); voltage enlargement alone is insufficient. These are proposed measurement controls, not additional claims about the 1947 laboratory record.

Repeating a setting tests drift; reversing or removing emitter [biasing](biasing.md) tests whether the response requires [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md). Contact motion, surface contamination, [temperature](temperature-split.md), direct electrical coupling and [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) can all affect the inference. A [small-signal model](small-signal-model.md) describes measured local terminal response without deciding every microscopic mechanism.
