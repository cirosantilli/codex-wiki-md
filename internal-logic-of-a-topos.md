# Internal logic of a topos

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary topos](elementary-topos.md)

The [subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md) supplies intuitionistic truth values. [Subobjects](subobject.md) interpret predicates, [finite limits](finite-limit.md) interpret finite conjunctions and equality, and suitable image and adjoint constructions interpret quantifiers. One must distinguish internal intuitionistic arguments from classical reasoning in the external category of sets.
