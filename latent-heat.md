# Latent heat

↑ **Parent:** [Phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_heat)

Latent heat is the [heat](heat-split.md) absorbed or released in a [first-order phase transition](first-order-phase-transition.md) through its [entropy](entropy-split.md) jump. For an equilibrium conversion from [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) $\alpha$ to [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) $\beta$ at fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md), evaluate both [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) on their [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md):

$$
L=T_{\rm tr}(S_\beta-S_\alpha)=H_\beta-H_\alpha.
$$

Use one normalization throughout: $L$, $S$ and $H$ may all be per [mole](mole-unit.md), giving $L$ in $\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}$, or all per unit [mass](mass.md), giving $L$ in $\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}$. Here $S$ is [entropy](entropy-split.md) and $H$ is [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) on that basis. Equal [Gibbs free energies](gibbs-free-energy.md) of the coexisting [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) give $\Delta H=T_{\rm tr}\Delta S$. Identifying this [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) difference with supplied [heat](heat-split.md) assumes a [closed system](closed-system.md) and an equilibrium conversion with only [pressure](pressure-split.md)-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) [work](work.md). [Tong, Statistical Physics, phase coexistence and latent heat](statistical-physics-by-david-tong.md)

For a pure substance with positive [latent heat](latent-heat.md) per unit [mass](mass.md) $L$, let $x$ be the [phase fraction](phase-fraction.md) of the higher-[enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md), measured by [mass](mass.md). While both [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) coexist at the fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md),

$$
Q=mL(x_2-x_1),\qquad T=T_{\rm tr}.
$$

Adding [heat](heat-split.md) changes $x$ rather than the [temperature](temperature-split.md); this creates the horizontal portion of a [heating curve](heating-curve.md). Once $x=1$, additional [heat](heat-split.md) raises the [temperature](temperature-split.md) according to [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md). Reversing the conversion releases [heat](heat-split.md). The plateau represents changing [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md), rather than a finite single-[thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) satisfying $Q=C_p\Delta T$. [1.5 Phase Changes](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/1-5-phase-changes.md)

For a constructed fixed-[pressure](pressure-split.md) model, take $m=1\,\mathrm{kg}$, initial [temperature](temperature-split.md) $280\,\mathrm K$, transition [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_{\rm tr}=300\,\mathrm K$, [solid](solid.md) [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) $c_s=2\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, [latent heat](latent-heat.md) $L=100\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}}$, and [liquid](liquid.md) [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) $c_l=3\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. These are stipulated model parameters, not empirical properties of a named material. Heating the [solid](solid.md) to $300\,\mathrm K$ takes $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$ of [sensible heat](sensible-heat.md); converting it fully to [liquid](liquid.md) takes another $100\,\mathrm{kJ}$ of [heat](heat-split.md); heating the [liquid](liquid.md) to $320\,\mathrm K$ takes $60\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The total is $200\,\mathrm{kJ}$. After a total [heat](heat-split.md) input of $90\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $x=(90-40)/100=1/2$ and the [temperature](temperature-split.md) is still $300\,\mathrm K$. The corresponding per-unit-[mass](mass.md) [entropy](entropy-split.md) jump is $L/T_{\rm tr}=\frac13\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$.

**[Figure 5](#image-sensible-heat-raises-temperature-latent-heat-changes-phase-fraction). Sensible heat raises temperature; latent heat changes phase fraction**. Constructed $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ fixed-[pressure](pressure-split.md) model with total supplied [heat](heat-split.md) starting at zero at $280\,\mathrm K$. The plateau runs from $40$ to $140\,\mathrm{kJ}$, while the [liquid](liquid.md) [phase fraction](phase-fraction.md) by [mass](mass.md) rises from zero to one. The horizontal axis measures $Q=\Delta H$, rather than absolute [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md).

A [continuous phase transition](continuous-phase-transition.md) has no [latent heat](latent-heat.md). Its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) can have a jump or a singularity without a finite interval in which added [heat](heat-split.md) converts coexisting [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) at a constant [temperature](temperature-split.md). A heating plateau therefore requires the stated [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) conditions; it is not a feature of every [phase transition](phase-transition.md).
