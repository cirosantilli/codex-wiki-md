# Eddy turnover time

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The eddy turnover time estimates how long a velocity difference $\delta u_l$ takes to distort a structure of size $l$. In a local strong [energy cascade](energy-cascade.md), the transfer time is assumed comparable to it. A [weak wave cascade](weak-wave-cascade.md) can transfer energy much more slowly because a single encounter makes only a small fractional change.
