# Graph Laplacian eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md)

An [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md)'s [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md). For a finite undirected graph, these eigenvalues are nonnegative, and the multiplicity of zero equals the number of [connected components](connected-component.md).
