# Order-parameter mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Conserved order-parameter dynamics](conserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md)

The positive order-parameter mobility $M$ relates the diffusive transport flux to the [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) gradient: $\mathbf J=-M\nabla\mu$. Combining this law with local conservation gives the [Cahn--Hilliard equation](cahn-hilliard-equation.md) when $\mu$ is the [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) of a composition [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
