# Fluctuation-dissipation relation for a conserved flux

↑ **Parent:** [Conserved order-parameter dynamics](conserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md)

For the flux $W_{ij}=-M\partial_i\mu_j+N_{ij}$ of a [vector order parameter](vector-order-parameter.md), [microscopic reversibility](microscopic-reversibility.md) requires independent [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) components with covariance

$$
\langle N_{ij}(\mathbf r,t)N_{kl}(\mathbf r',t')\rangle
=2Mk_BT\,\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\delta(t-t').
$$

The current changes sign under time reversal. The resulting path-probability ratio contributes $-(k_BT)^{-1}\int W_{ij}\partial_i\mu_j$ to its logarithm; [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) converts this to the free-energy loss from the conserved dynamics.
