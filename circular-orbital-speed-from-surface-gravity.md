# Circular orbital speed from surface gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Circular orbit](circular-orbit.md)

For a spherical central body of radius $R$ with [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) $g_s=GM/R^2$, a circular test-particle orbit at radius $r$ has $v=\sqrt{g_sR^2/r}$. [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) is used to infer $GM$, not incorrectly kept constant at orbital height.
