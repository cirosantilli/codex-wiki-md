# Weyl dimension formula for B2

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of B2](fundamental-representations-of-b2.md)

When $\alpha_1$ is the short simple root, $\omega_1$ is the spin fundamental weight and $\omega_2$ is the vector fundamental weight. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) gives

$$
\dim V(a\omega_1+b\omega_2)
=\frac{(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)(a+2b+3)}6.
$$
