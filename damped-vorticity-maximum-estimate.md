# Damped-vorticity maximum estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Damped-driven Navier-Stokes equation](damped-driven-navier-stokes-equation.md)

For a steady damped vorticity equation with $\gamma>0$, divergence-free transport, and bounded forcing, multiplication by $\omega|\omega|^{q-2}$ gives $\gamma\|\omega\|_q\leq\|g\|_q$. Sending $q$ to infinity yields

$$
\gamma\|\omega\|_\infty\leq\|g\|_\infty.
$$
