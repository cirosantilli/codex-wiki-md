# Density of diagonalizable complex matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md)

Perturb each diagonal position of a [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md) matrix by a different arbitrarily small scalar, avoiding the finitely many collisions between diagonal entries. The resulting upper triangular matrix has distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), hence is [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md). Conjugate back to approximate any complex matrix. Continuous identities proved for [diagonalizable matrices](diagonalizable-matrix.md) can therefore extend to all matrices.
