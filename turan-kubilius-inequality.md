<h1 id="turan-kubilius-inequality">Turán-Kubilius inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic number theory](probabilistic-number-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turán-Kubilius_inequality)

For an [additive arithmetic function](additive-function-number-theory.md) $f$, $N^{-1}\sum_{n\le N}|f(n)-A_f(N)|^2\ll B_f(N)^2$, where $A_f$ is the [mean of an additive arithmetic function](mean-of-an-additive-arithmetic-function.md) and $B_f(N)^2=\sum_{p^k\le N}|f(p^k)|^2/p^k$. The implied constant is absolute.
