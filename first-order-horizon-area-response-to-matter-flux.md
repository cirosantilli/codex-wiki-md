# First-order horizon-area response to matter flux

↑ **Parent:** [Physical-process first law of black-hole mechanics](physical-process-first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)

For a small perturbation of a stationary horizon, the linearized [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) gives $\delta\theta^{\prime}=-8\pi\delta T_{kk}$. With vanishing boundary term $[\lambda\delta\theta]$, integration by parts gives $\delta A=8\pi\int\lambda\delta T_{kk}\,d\lambda\,dA_0$. Combining this with [affine horizon-generator scaling](affine-horizon-generator-scaling.md) converts area response to horizon Killing-energy flux.
