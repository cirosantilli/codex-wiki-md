# Tetrahedron stabilizer in the cube rotation group

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

For cube vertices $(\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)$, the four vertices of coordinate product $+1$ and the four of product $-1$ form the two inscribed [regular tetrahedra](regular-tetrahedron.md). A cube [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) is a [signed permutation matrix](signed-permutation-matrix.md) of [determinant](determinant.md) one and maps each product class to one class. A quarter-turn about a coordinate axis exchanges the classes. The [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) of either tetrahedron therefore has size two, while the [cube rotation group](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) has order 24. The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives its setwise [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) order 12; this is the tetrahedron's proper [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) [group](group-split.md).
