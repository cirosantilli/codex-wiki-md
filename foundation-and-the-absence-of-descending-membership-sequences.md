# Foundation and the absence of descending membership sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Descending membership sequence](descending-membership-sequence.md)

[Foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) implies that no [descending membership sequence](descending-membership-sequence.md) exists. Conversely, with [dependent choice](axiom-of-dependent-choice.md), failure of [foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) gives a nonempty [set](set-split.md) in which every element contains another element of that [set](set-split.md). Successively selecting such members produces a [descending membership sequence](descending-membership-sequence.md). Thus the two conditions are equivalent with [dependent choice](axiom-of-dependent-choice.md); full [choice](axiom-of-choice.md) is sufficient.
