# Radiometric dating

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiometric_dating)

[Radiometric dating](radiometric-dating.md) estimates elapsed time from changes in [radionuclide](radionuclide.md) populations, using a known [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md) and assumptions about the initial composition and subsequent closure to matter exchange.
