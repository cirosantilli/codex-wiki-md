# Singly ionized calcium

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium](calcium.md)

A [calcium](calcium.md) ion with one electron removed, leaving a closed 18-electron core and one [valence electron](valence-electron.md). It is distinct from the divalent [calcium ion](calcium-ion.md) used in much biological terminology. Its bound electronic levels and [ionization continuum](ionization-continuum.md) support the Ca II optical series.
