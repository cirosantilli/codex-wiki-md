# Glyoxylate shunt

↑ **Parent:** [Citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md)

The [glyoxylate shunt](glyoxylate-shunt.md) bypasses oxidative decarboxylations using [isocitrate lyase](isocitrate-lyase.md) and [malate synthase](malate-synthase.md). It permits net conservation of acetyl-unit carbon in four-carbon precursors, at a different balance of reducing-equivalent production from the oxidative route.
