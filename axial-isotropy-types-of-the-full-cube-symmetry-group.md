# Axial isotropy types of the full cube symmetry group

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

The full [symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) acts as all signed permutation [matrices](matrix.md). The [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $(1,0,0)$, $(1,1,0)$ and $(1,1,1)$ have orders eight, four and six, respectively, and their [fixed-point subspaces of a group action](fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) are the lines through those vectors. The three [group orbits](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) have sizes six, twelve and eight. They therefore give distinct axial types for the [equivariant branching lemma](equivariant-branching-lemma.md). More generally the fixed-space dimension of a vector [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) equals the number of distinct nonzero absolute coordinate values, so these are precisely its one-dimensional types.
