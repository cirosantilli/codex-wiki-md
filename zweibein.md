# Zweibein

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal coframe in spacetime](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md)

A zweibein is a two-dimensional [orthonormal coframe in spacetime](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md): its components obey $h_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{AB}e^A{}_{\mu}e^B{}_{\nu}$. It converts worldsheet coordinate indices into local Lorentz-frame indices and defines curved gamma matrices $\gamma^\mu=e_A{}^\mu\gamma^A$. The associated [spin connection](spin-connection.md) supplies the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) of worldsheet spinors. In flat [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md) the coframe can be constant.
