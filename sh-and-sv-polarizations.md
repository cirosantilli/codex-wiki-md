# SH and SV polarizations

↑ **Parent:** [S wave](s-wave.md)

For an [isotropic](isotropy.md) layered medium, SH [displacement](displacement.md) is perpendicular to the vertical propagation plane and SV [displacement](displacement.md) lies within it. P-SV coupling occurs at interfaces, while SH remains a separate [polarization](polarization-waves.md) sector.
