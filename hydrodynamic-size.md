# Hydrodynamic size

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)

[Hydrodynamic size](hydrodynamic-size.md) describes the effective dimensions of a dissolved [molecule](molecule-split.md) moving with its surrounding [solvent](solvent.md). It depends on [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md), internal shape, and [solvent](solvent.md) conditions, rather than just [molar mass](molar-mass.md). [SEC pore access](sec-pore-access.md) probes size-dependent accessibility of the packing.
