# Energy-biased detection probability

↑ **Parent:** [Detector dead time](detector-dead-time.md)

If source class $i$ has prior fraction $p_i$ and [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md) $\eta_i$, its fraction among detected events is proportional to $p_i\eta_i$. [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) normalizes these products. Class-dependent losses therefore bias the detected composition even when the incident fractions are known exactly.
