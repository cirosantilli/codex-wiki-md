# Conjugation Jacobian for a compact Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl integration formula](weyl-integration-formula.md)

For a connected [compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md) with [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md) $T$, the map $q:G/T\times T\to G$, $q(xT,t)=xtx^{-1}$, has the displayed real [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). Choose an invariant [inner product](inner-product.md) and identify the tangent space of $G/T$ with $\mathfrak t^\perp$. Translation of the derivative at $(T,t)$ to the identity gives $(X,Y)\mapsto(\operatorname{Ad}(t^{-1})-I)X+Y$. Each pair of opposite [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) corresponds to a real two-plane on which the determinant is $|1-\alpha(t)|^2$. The torus direction has determinant one. Normalized quotient and [Haar measures](haar-measure.md) have matching volume constants because the Riemannian submersion gives $\operatorname{vol}G=\operatorname{vol}(G/T)\operatorname{vol}T$. The map covers regular elements $|W|$ times, giving the [Weyl integration formula](weyl-integration-formula.md) with conjugacy averaging for general functions.
