# Relativistic momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum](momentum-split.md)

For a massive particle, its spatial [momentum](momentum-split.md) in an inertial frame is $\gamma m\mathbf v$, with m its invariant [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) and $\gamma$ the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md). Together with its [relativistic energy](relativistic-energy.md) it forms the [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). In a magnetic quadrupole's [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md), replacing $mv$ by the longitudinal momentum extends the transverse focusing scale to relativistic beams.
