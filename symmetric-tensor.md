# Symmetric tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_tensor)

A symmetric [tensor](tensor.md) is unchanged by every [permutation](permutation.md) of its slots. A [symmetric second-rank tensor](symmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) is the rank-two case; symmetry for higher rank requires invariance under every such slot exchange.
