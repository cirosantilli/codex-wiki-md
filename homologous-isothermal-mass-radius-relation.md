# Homologous isothermal mass-radius relation

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md)

Use the [isothermal Lane-Emden equation](isothermal-lane-emden-equation.md) variables $r=a\xi$, $a^2=c_T^2/(4\pi G\rho_c)$ and $\rho=\rho_ce^{-\Psi}$. Truncation at fixed dimensionless radius $\xi_b$ gives $R=a\xi_b$ and $M=4\pi\rho_ca^3\xi_b^2\Psi'(\xi_b)$. Division proves the displayed relation. A common [temperature](temperature-split.md), composition and dimensionless truncation therefore imply $M\propto R$, while the external [pressure](pressure-split.md) generally varies along that homologous sequence. Arbitrary changes of truncation at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) do not retain a common coefficient. A finite regular cloud needs an outer boundary such as that of a [pressure-confined isothermal sphere](pressure-confined-isothermal-sphere.md).
