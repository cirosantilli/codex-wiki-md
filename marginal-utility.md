# Marginal utility

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marginal_utility)

The additional [utility function](utility-function-split.md) obtained per additional unit of [consumption](consumption.md) or [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md). For a differentiable [utility function](utility-function-split.md) it is its [derivative](derivative.md); for a [concave](concave-function.md) [utility function](utility-function-split.md) it is nonincreasing. In a complete-market allocation, equating marginal utility to a multiplier times the [state-price density](state-price-density.md) compares the utility benefit of a payoff with its financing cost. The [concave supporting-tangent inequality](concave-supporting-tangent-inequality.md) makes this comparison a global optimality certificate rather than only a local first-order condition.
