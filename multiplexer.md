# Multiplexer

↑ **Parent:** [Combinational logic](combinational-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplexer)

A selector that routes one of several inputs to its output according to control [bits](bit.md). The displayed two-input version selects $a$ when $s=0$ and $b$ when $s=1$. A word selector applies the same control [bit](bit.md) to parallel one-[bit](bit.md) [multiplexers](multiplexer.md); its [circuit size](circuit-size.md) grows with the word width while its added [circuit depth](depth-of-a-boolean-circuit.md) remains constant.
