# Riesz-Fischer theorem for an orthonormal system

↑ **Parent:** [Riesz-Fischer theorem](riesz-fischer-theorem.md)

For an orthonormal family in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the squared [norm](norm.md) of every finite partial sum is the sum of squared coefficients. Square summability therefore makes the net of finite partial sums Cauchy, proving unconditional [convergence](convergence.md) by [completeness](completeness.md). If the orthonormal family is complete, [Bessel's inequality](bessel-s-inequality.md) makes the coefficients of each vector square summable, and the residual after summing is orthogonal to the whole family, hence zero. This recovers the orthonormal expansion and [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md).
