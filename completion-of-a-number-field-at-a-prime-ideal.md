# Completion of a number field at a prime ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Local factorization and extended absolute values](local-factorization-and-extended-absolute-values.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ and a nonzero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p\subset\mathcal O_K$, the completion $K_{\mathfrak p}$ is the completion for the $\mathfrak p$-adic absolute value. It is a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$, where $p\mathbb Z=\mathfrak p\cap\mathbb Z$.
