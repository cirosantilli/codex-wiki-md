# Radiative energy of virialized gravitational collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

For an isolated final [stellar population](stellar-population.md) with isotropic one-dimensional [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma$, kinetic energy is $K=3M\sigma^2/2$. The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $W=-2K$ and total energy $E=-K$. Collapse from a diffuse nearly unbound gas reservoir therefore requires removal of approximately $K$ of energy. The radiated fraction can differ if there are outflows, appreciable initial binding energy, or boundary stresses.
