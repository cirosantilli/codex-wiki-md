# Line segment

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_segment)

The line segment joining vectors $x$ and $y$ is

$$
\{(1-t)x+ty:0\leq t\leq1\}.
$$
