# Mantel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal number](extremal-number.md)

Every triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices has at most $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges.

This is the triangle-free case of [Turán's theorem](turan-s-theorem.md).
