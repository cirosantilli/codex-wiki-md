# Defect sink

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

A feature that absorbs mobile [point defects](point-defect.md), such as a free surface, grain boundary, [dislocation](dislocation.md) or cavity. Capture efficiency and relative preference for defect types are as important as visible size or number.
