# Universal stiffness jump

↑ **Parent:** [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md)

The renormalized [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) has this discontinuity at the [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md). The algebraic [correlation function](correlation-function.md) has exponent $1/4$ at the transition from the ordered side.

The stiffness here is an energy in two dimensions, not an areal mass density. For a neutral [superfluid](superfluid.md) of particles of mass $m$, let $n_s$ be the [superfluid number density](superfluid-number-density.md) per unit area. Substituting the [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md) $\mathbf v_s=(\hbar/m)\nabla\theta$ into the kinetic energy gives $\rho_s=\hbar^2n_s/m$. Consequently the jump can also be written

$$
n_s(T_{\rm BKT}^-)\lambda_T^2=4,
\qquad \lambda_T^2=\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{mk_BT},
$$

where $\lambda_T$ is the [thermal wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md). It is the large-distance [superfluid number density](superfluid-number-density.md), rather than the total density or a locally inferred condensate density, that enters this relation.

Within the [vortex-pair renormalization flow](vortex-pair-renormalization-flow.md), $y$ changes from irrelevant to relevant at $\pi K_R=2$, with $K_R$ the large-distance dimensionless [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md). Combining this with the [spin wave](spin-wave.md) result $\eta=1/(2\pi K_R)$ gives $\eta=1/4$. Above the transition the homogeneous equilibrium [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) vanishes at infinite observation scale. A discontinuous response coefficient is compatible with a diverging [correlation length](correlation-length.md); it does not imply a [latent heat](latent-heat.md).[Section 3, pp. 92–94: fugacity flow and renormalized superfluid-density jump](topological-defects-and-phase-transitions-by-john-michael-kosterlitz.md)
