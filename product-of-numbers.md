# Product of numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication](multiplication.md)

The product of numbers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ is the result of their iterated [multiplication](multiplication.md). The empty product is $1$, the multiplicative identity. For positive factors, $\log(\prod_i a_i)=\sum_i\log a_i$, turning a multiplicative [conservation law](conservation-law.md) into an additive derivative identity.
