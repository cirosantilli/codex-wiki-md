# Minimum inhibitory concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Antibiotic](antibiotic.md)

The lowest tested antimicrobial concentration preventing defined visible growth under specified conditions. It measures an organism-level response, not just the catalytic efficiency of an isolated resistance [enzyme](enzyme-split.md).
