# Chelation

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

[Chelation](chelation.md) binds multiple donor sites of one ligand to the same central atom, forming a [chelate ring](chelate-ring.md). It can retain a donor near a metal even when one attachment temporarily opens.
