# Second moment bound for spectral truncation

↑ **Parent:** [Centered truncation of a Wigner matrix](centered-truncation-of-a-wigner-matrix.md)

For a fixed mean-zero variance-one real entry law, [centered truncation of a Wigner matrix](centered-truncation-of-a-wigner-matrix.md) satisfies $\mathbb E[N^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(X-\widehat X)^2]\leq t(C)$, where $t(C)=\mathbb E[Y^2\mathbf1_{|Y|\geq C}]\to0$. The [spectral Lipschitz bound from Frobenius distance](spectral-lipschitz-bound-from-frobenius-distance.md) and [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) give probability at most $t(C)/\varepsilon^2$ for a spectral test-function discrepancy exceeding $\varepsilon$. Thus $t(C)<\varepsilon^3$ works uniformly in [matrix](matrix.md) size. A varying entry-law family requires uniform second-moment tail decay instead.
