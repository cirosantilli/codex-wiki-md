# Reflexive-module second-syzygy criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive module](reflexive-module.md)

Let $A$ be a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) that is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md). If

$$
0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow P\longrightarrow0
$$

is [exact](exact-sequence.md), $N$ is a finitely generated [free module](free-module.md), and $P$ is a [torsion-free module](torsion-free-module.md), then $M$ is a [reflexive module](reflexive-module.md). In particular, the dual of every finitely generated $A$-module is reflexive.
