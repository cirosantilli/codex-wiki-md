# Effective bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic network](stochastic-network.md)

Effective bandwidth describes the exponential-moment traffic rate at a specified time scale and tail parameter. Independent sources have additive effective bandwidths. The [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) connects their sum to overflow bounds. For a single time unit, the small-parameter limit is the [expected value](expected-value.md) and the large-parameter limit is the [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md), with appropriate moment or extended-value conventions.
