# IgG chain reduction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Immunoglobulin G](immunoglobulin-g.md)

[Gerald M. Edelman](gerald-maurice-edelman.md) reduced [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) [disulfide bonds](disulfide-bond.md) and used dissociating [chemical solutions](solution-chemistry.md), including $6\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ [urea](urea.md). Ultracentrifugal analysis showed a decrease in [molar mass](molar-mass.md), supporting a multichain [protein](protein-split.md). In the comparison reproduced in his lecture, [starch gel electrophoresis](starch-gel-electrophoresis.md) in [urea](urea.md) separated serum [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) and a urinary [protein](protein-split.md) from the same patient, alongside their reduced and [alkylation](alkylation.md)-treated preparations. The untreated samples provided the treatment comparison; the urinary material provided a chain-identity comparison. The treated serum sample revealed heavy- and light-chain bands. Two-dimensional [peptide mapping](peptide-mapping.md) compared the urinary [protein](protein-split.md) with the isolated [immunoglobulin light chain](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md). Together these readouts supported a shared chain identity.[Gerald M. Edelman, Antibody Structure and Molecular Immunology, Nobel Lecture dated 12 December 1972, printed pp. 33–34 and Figure 2a–b: dissociation, four comparison lanes and peptide maps](antibody-structure-and-molecular-immunology-by-gerald-m-edelman.md)

The controls address distinct alternatives. Treatment-dependent separation supports chains joined in the starting [protein](protein-split.md); a matching [gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) position alone would not uniquely identify a chain. Agreement of [peptide mapping](peptide-mapping.md) adds an independent chemical comparison. Neither readout gives atomic coordinates, and this patient's preparation does not establish a common [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) for all [antibodies](antibody.md).

[Reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) targets [protein disulfide bonds](protein-disulfide-bond.md), while [protein denaturation](denaturation-biochemistry.md) weakens the folded assembly. Neither operation is the [proteolysis](proteolysis.md) that cuts [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). This explains why chain separation and [Papain digestion of IgG](papain-digestion-of-igg.md) give different inventories: four intact chain backbones in the first case, three assembled fragments in the second.
