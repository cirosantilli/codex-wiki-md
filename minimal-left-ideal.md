# Minimal left ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Left ideal](left-ideal.md)

A minimal [left ideal](left-ideal.md) is a nonzero [left ideal](left-ideal.md) containing no smaller nonzero [left ideal](left-ideal.md). Equivalently, it is a [simple module](irreducible-module.md) inside the [left regular module](left-regular-module.md). Every nonzero [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) of finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) has one: choose a nonzero [left ideal](left-ideal.md) of least positive [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md).
