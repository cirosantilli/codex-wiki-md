# Boundary monotonicity of the random-cluster measure

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster boundary condition](random-cluster-boundary-condition.md)

Here $\xi\preceq\zeta$ means each block of $\xi$ lies in a block of $\zeta$, and the measure order is [stochastic domination of probability measures](stochastic-domination-of-probability-measures.md). The [random-cluster single-edge conditional probability](random-cluster-single-edge-conditional-probability.md) is increasing in both exterior open edges and wiring for $q\geq1$. Couple two [heat-bath Markov chains](heat-bath-markov-chain.md) by identical update edges and uniform random variables, starting from ordered states. Order persists, and convergence of the finite chains to their stationary laws proves the displayed inequality. At $q=1$, the law does not depend on the boundary partition.
