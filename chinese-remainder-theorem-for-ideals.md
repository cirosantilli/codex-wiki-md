# Chinese remainder theorem for ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md)

For pairwise comaximal [ideals](ideal.md) in a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), reduction has the displayed [ring isomorphism](ring-isomorphism.md). The kernel is their intersection, equal to their product. Choose $e_i\in\prod_{j\ne i}I_j$ with $e_i\equiv1\pmod{I_i}$; then $\sum_i a_ie_i$ lifts any prescribed residue tuple. The equality between intersection and product follows from $1=u+v$, $u\in I$, $v\in J$, which writes each $x\in I\cap J$ as $xu+xv\in IJ$.
