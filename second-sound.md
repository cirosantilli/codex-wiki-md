# Second sound

↑ **Parent:** [Two-fluid model of superfluidity](two-fluid-model-of-superfluidity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_sound)

[Second sound](second-sound.md) is an [entropy](entropy-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) wave. At negligible [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md), it has almost zero net [mass](mass.md) flux. [Lev Davidovich Landau](lev-davidovich-landau.md) predicted two sound modes in 1941.[Landau, §8, pp.515–516](the-theory-of-superfluidity-of-helium-ii-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)
