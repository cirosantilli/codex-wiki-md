# FIFO semaphore

↑ **Parent:** [Semaphore (programming)](semaphore-programming.md)

A [semaphore](semaphore-programming.md) that satisfies registered down requests in [FIFO queue](fifo-queue.md) order. Direct handoff reserves a released permit for the oldest waiter rather than exposing it to competing newcomers. The ordering of truly concurrent registrations is determined by the registration mechanism.
