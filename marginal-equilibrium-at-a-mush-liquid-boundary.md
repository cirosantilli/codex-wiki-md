# Marginal equilibrium at a mush-liquid boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Stagnant mushy-layer model](stagnant-mushy-layer-model.md)

At a sharp mush–liquid edge with linear [liquidus](liquidus.md) $T_L=T_m-mC$, marginal equilibrium makes the liquid [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) tangent to the local [liquidus](liquidus.md): $T_z=-mC_z$. Together with $T=T_L$ at the edge and $T\ge T_L$ in the exterior liquid, it supplies the usual absence-of-[constitutional supercooling](constitutional-supercooling.md) closure. When solute [diffusion](diffusion.md) is neglected inside the [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) but retained outside it, interfacial solute conservation can require a small [solid fraction](solid-fraction.md) jump; imposing unit [porosity](porosity.md) as an additional finite-diffusivity condition generally overdetermines this sharp-edge reduction.
