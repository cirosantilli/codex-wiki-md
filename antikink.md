# Antikink

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field kink](scalar-field-kink.md)

An [antikink](antikink.md) is a [scalar-field kink](scalar-field-kink.md) with the opposite orientation of its vacuum boundary values. If $\phi_K(x)$ joins vacua $\phi_-$ to $\phi_+$, then $\phi_K(-x)$ joins $\phi_+$ to $\phi_-$. For an even double-well potential and an odd centered [phi-four kink](phi-four-kink.md), the centered [antikink](antikink.md) is $-\phi_K(x)$. With $Q=[\phi(+\infty)-\phi(-\infty)]/(\phi_+-\phi_-)$, a [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) has $Q=1$ and an [antikink](antikink.md) has $Q=-1$. Their localized energies coincide by spatial reflection.
