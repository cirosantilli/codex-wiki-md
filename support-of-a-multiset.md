# Support of a multiset

↑ **Parent:** [Multiset](multiset.md)

The [set](set-split.md) of keys with positive multiplicity. Its [cardinality](cardinality.md) counts distinct keys, whereas summing the multiplicities counts all occurrences. A count-[array](array-data-structure.md) implementation can maintain the support [cardinality](cardinality.md) by tracking only zero-to-positive and positive-to-zero transitions.
