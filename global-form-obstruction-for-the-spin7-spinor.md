# Global form obstruction for the Spin7 spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Spin representation](spin-representation.md)

The [Spin group](spin-group.md) $\operatorname{Spin}(7)$ double covers [SO(7)](so-7-group.md). Its central kernel element $-1$ acts trivially in the vector [representation](group-representation.md) and as $-I$ in the spinor [representation](group-representation.md). Thus a spinor [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) does not define an ordinary [SO(7)](so-7-group.md) [group representation](group-representation.md). Under $\operatorname{Spin}(6)\cong SU(4)$ the same obstruction is visible because $-I_4$ acts trivially on $\Lambda^2\mathbf4$ but negatively on $\mathbf4\oplus\overline{\mathbf4}$.
