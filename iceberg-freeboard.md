# Iceberg freeboard

↑ **Parent:** [Iceberg](iceberg.md)

For a vertical-sided floating [iceberg](iceberg.md) of height $H$, [Archimedes' principle](archimedes-principle.md) equates displaced water mass to ice mass. Its draft is $\rho_w^{-1}\int_0^H\rho_i(z)\,dz$ and its freeboard is the remaining height above water. A depth-dependent [mass density](density.md) must be integrated; using the densest layer throughout overestimates the draft.
