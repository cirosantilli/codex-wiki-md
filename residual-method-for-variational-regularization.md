# Residual method for variational regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Variational regularization](variational-regularization.md)

Given $\|f_\delta-f\|\leq\delta$, the residual method minimizes a penalty $J(u)$ subject to $\|Au-f_\delta\|\leq\delta$. Every exact solution is feasible, so a reconstruction $u_\delta$ has penalty no larger than that of any [J-minimizing solution](j-minimizing-solution.md).
