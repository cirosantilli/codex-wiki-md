# Squared-population near-invariant for stochastic attrition

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric stochastic attrition process](symmetric-stochastic-attrition-process.md)

In the [jump chain](jump-chain.md) of the [symmetric stochastic attrition process](symmetric-stochastic-attrition-process.md), the two possible increments of $D$ are $-2A+1$ and $2B-1$. Their mean is the displayed quantity, bounded by one in absolute value. Subtracting these conditional means produces a [martingale](martingale-split.md). If both initial populations are at most $N$, it has at most $2N$ nonzero increments, each bounded by $4N$. Thus [Azuma-Hoeffding inequality](azuma-s-inequality.md) controls changes of order $N^2$ with exponentially small probability of order $e^{-cN}$.
