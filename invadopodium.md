# Invadopodium

↑ **Parent:** [Cancer invasion](cancer-invasion.md)

An actin-rich cellular protrusion associated with localized [extracellular matrix](extracellular-matrix.md) degradation during invasion. Formation, matrix digestion and whole-cell invasion are related but distinct experimental endpoints.
