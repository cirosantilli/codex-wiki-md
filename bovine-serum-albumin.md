# Bovine serum albumin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bovine_serum_albumin)

A [protein](protein-split.md) obtained from cattle serum and widely used as a reference sample in [biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md). It serves as a [protein](protein-split.md) standard for assays such as the [bicinchoninic acid assay](bicinchoninic-acid-assay.md), and as a model [protein](protein-split.md) in [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md) experiments.
