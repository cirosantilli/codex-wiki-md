# Continuous lift

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

For [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) $p:E\to B$ and $f:X\to B$, a [continuous lift](continuous-lift.md) of $f$ through $p$ is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $\widetilde f:X\to E$ with $p\widetilde f=f$. Existence and uniqueness require additional hypotheses; the [lifting criterion for a covering space](lifting-criterion-for-a-covering-space.md) states a useful existence criterion when $p$ is a [covering map](covering-space.md) and $X$ is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md) and [locally path-connected](locally-path-connected-space.md).
