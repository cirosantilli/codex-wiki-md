# Sphingomyelin

↑ **Parent:** [Phospholipid](phospholipid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphingomyelin)

[Sphingomyelin](sphingomyelin.md) is a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md) whose backbone is based on [sphingosine](sphingosine.md), rather than [glycerol](glycerol.md). One long [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) region belongs to that backbone and another comes from an attached [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md). It is a counterexample to defining every [phospholipid](phospholipid.md) by the illustrated [glycerol](glycerol.md) scaffold. See [Cooper, The Cell: the molecular composition of cells](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9879/).
