# Ergosphere

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergosphere)

An ergosphere is the region outside a rotating black hole horizon in which the stationary time-translation [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md) is spacelike. Observers cannot remain stationary relative to infinity there. The [Kerr ergoregion](kerr-ergoregion.md) is its Kerr-metric instance.
