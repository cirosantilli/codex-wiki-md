# Equilibrium stage

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md)

An [equilibrium stage](equilibrium-stage.md) models a contact whose outgoing [liquid](liquid.md) and [vapor](vapor.md) satisfy [vapor-liquid equilibrium](vapor-liquid-equilibrium.md) at the stage's [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). It is a composition model, not a claim that each piece of packing reaches [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md).[Hyper-TVT by ETH Zurich; §5.6 Efficiency, opening equilibrium-plate assumption](hyper-tvt-by-eth-zurich/distillation-efficiency.md)
