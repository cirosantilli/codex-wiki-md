# Higher principal-unit group

↑ **Parent:** [Principal unit](principal-unit.md)

For a non-Archimedean local field $K$, the $n$th higher principal-unit group is $1+\mathfrak m_K^n$. These groups form a decreasing filtration of the [principal units](principal-unit.md).
