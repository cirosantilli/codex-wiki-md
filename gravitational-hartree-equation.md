# Gravitational Hartree equation

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear analysis](nonlinear-analysis-split.md)

This attractive mean-field equation is a gravitational version of the [Hartree equations](hartree-equations.md). The gravitational Hartree equation couples a wave function to its attractive Newtonian potential:

$$
iu_t+\Delta u-\phi u=0,
\qquad
\Delta\phi=|u|^2,
\qquad
\phi=-\frac1{C_N|x|^{N-2}}*|u|^2.
$$
