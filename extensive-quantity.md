# Extensive quantity

↑ **Parent:** [Intensive and extensive thermodynamic quantities](intensive-and-extensive-thermodynamic-quantities.md)

An extensive quantity scales in proportion to the size of a thermodynamic system: combining $n$ independent identical copies multiplies it by $n$. [Energy](energy.md), [entropy](entropy.md) and [volume](volume.md) are examples.
