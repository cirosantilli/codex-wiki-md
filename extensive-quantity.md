# Extensive quantity

↑ **Parent:** [Intensive and extensive thermodynamic quantities](intensive-and-extensive-thermodynamic-quantities.md)

Two related properties of an [extensive quantity](extensive-quantity.md) must be distinguished. Additivity says that a quantity $X$ for independent subsystems A and B obeys

$$
X_{A\cup B}=X_A+X_B.
$$

The subsystems may be in different states. Extensivity expressed through a [homogeneous function](homogeneous-function.md) says that the equilibrium fundamental relation obeys

$$
U(\lambda S,\lambda V,\lambda n)=\lambda U(S,V,n),\qquad \lambda>0,
$$

with composition held fixed. This is degree-one [homogeneity](homogeneity.md) along a replication ray; it does not say that [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is a [linear function](linear-function.md) of arbitrary independent changes of [entropy](entropy-split.md), [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md).

Additivity gives integer replication by repeated addition. Subdividing a uniform bulk sample into $b$ equivalent parts gives rational scaling $a/b$; continuity then extends it to positive real factors. This argument requires negligible boundary contributions and a consistently prepared state. Conversely, a degree-one [homogeneous function](homogeneous-function.md) need not be additive between different rays: $f(x,y)=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ has $f(\lambda x,\lambda y)=\lambda f(x,y)$, but $f(1,0)+f(0,1)=2\ne\sqrt2=f(1,1)$. Thus a scaling identity alone cannot justify summing arbitrary states.

For finite samples, the [surface correction to thermodynamic free energy](surface-correction-to-thermodynamic-free-energy.md) can matter. Under geometric enlargement with [volume](volume.md) multiplied by $\lambda$, [surface area](surface-area.md) grows as $\lambda^{2/3}$, so a [surface energy](surface-energy.md) term is not a bulk [extensive quantity](extensive-quantity.md). Replicating separated droplets retains every original surface, whereas merging them changes the total [surface area](surface-area.md). Likewise, appreciable mutual [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) makes interacting subsystems fail the independent-copy premise. The [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) explains when bulk scaling becomes accurate; it does not remove these qualifications for every small or strongly interacting system.
