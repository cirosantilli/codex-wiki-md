# Represented homology theory

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized homology theory](generalized-homology-theory.md)

A [topological spectrum](spectrum-topology.md) $E$ represents the reduced [generalized homology theory](generalized-homology-theory.md) $\widetilde E_k(X)=\pi_k(E\wedge\Sigma^\infty X)$ on [based spaces](based-space.md), where the [smash product of spectra](smash-product-of-spectra.md) is derived. It is covariant in $X$.
