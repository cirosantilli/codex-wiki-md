# Orthonormal wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Wavelet](wavelet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonormal_wavelet)

An orthonormal wavelet $\psi$ generates $\{2^{j/2}\psi(2^jx-k)\}_{j,k\in\mathbb Z}$ as an orthonormal basis of $L^2(\mathbb R)$.
