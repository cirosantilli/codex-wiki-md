# Thermodynamic phase

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

A thermodynamic phase is a form of macroscopic equilibrium characterized by its structure, symmetries and thermodynamic response. Away from [phase transitions](phase-transition.md), its [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md) varies smoothly with the control parameters. Different phases can share a [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) boundary. An ordered magnetic phase is distinguished from a disordered phase by its spontaneous [order parameter](order-parameter.md); positive and negative ordered states are different [pure thermodynamic phases](pure-thermodynamic-phase.md) related by [spin inversion symmetry](spin-inversion-symmetry.md).
