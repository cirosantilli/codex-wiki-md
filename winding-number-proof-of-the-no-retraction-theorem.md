# Winding-number proof of the no-retraction theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy invariance of winding number](homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md)

If a continuous retraction from a closed disc to its boundary existed, applying it to a contraction of the boundary circle inside the disc would give a homotopy in $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$ from a loop of winding number one to a constant loop of winding number zero. [Homotopy invariance of winding number](homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md) rules this out.
