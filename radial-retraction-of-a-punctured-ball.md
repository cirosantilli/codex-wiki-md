# Radial retraction of a punctured ball

↑ **Parent:** [Retraction](retraction.md)

Removing any interior point $a$ from the Euclidean [closed unit ball](closed-unit-ball.md) allows a [retraction](retraction.md) onto its [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md). For $v=x-a\ne0$, the positive root of $|a+tv|^2=1$ is

$$
\tau(x)=\frac{-a\cdot v+\sqrt{(a\cdot v)^2+(1-|a|^2)|v|^2}}{|v|^2}.
$$

Then $r(x)=a+\tau(x)v$ is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) on the punctured [closed unit ball](closed-unit-ball.md), has [norm](norm.md) one, and fixes its [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) because $\tau(x)=1$ there. The [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md) has one positive and one negative root since $|a|<1$, so the chosen root is unambiguous.
