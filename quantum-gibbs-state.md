# Quantum Gibbs state

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

A [quantum Gibbs state](quantum-gibbs-state.md) is the [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) of a [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) with inverse [temperature](temperature-split.md) $\beta=1/(k_BT)$. It exists when the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z=\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H})$ is finite. The [spectral theorem](spectral-theorem.md) assigns [probabilities](probability.md) $e^{-\beta E_j}/Z$ to an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) of the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). An additive constant in the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) cancels between numerator and denominator. For $H=-\mu B\sigma_z$, the [exponential of a Pauli vector](exponential-of-a-pauli-vector.md), continued to an imaginary argument, gives $\rho_\beta=[I+\tanh(\beta\mu B)\sigma_z]/2$.
