# BTZ black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTZ_black_hole)

The BTZ black hole is a quotient of three-dimensional [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md). In lapse-and-shift coordinates its outer and inner horizon radii are $r_+$ and $r_-$, and its outer-horizon surface gravity is

$$
\kappa=\frac{r_+^2-r_-^2}{L^2r_+}.
$$
