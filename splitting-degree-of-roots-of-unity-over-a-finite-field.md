# Splitting degree of roots of unity over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity over a finite field](root-of-unity-over-a-finite-field.md)

With $m$ the characteristic-prime-free part of $n$, all [roots of unity over a finite field](root-of-unity-over-a-finite-field.md) lie in $\mathbb F_{q^s}$ exactly when $m\mid q^s-1$. This follows from the cyclicity of the [multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md). For $m=1$ the least positive degree is one. A primitive root of order $m$ has the same [field](field.md) degree, because its Frobenius orbit closes exactly when $m\mid q^s-1$.
