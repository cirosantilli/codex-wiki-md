# London dispersion force

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals force](van-der-waals-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_dispersion_force)

Named after [Fritz London](fritz-london.md), this is an attractive interaction between fluctuating [electric dipoles](electric-dipole.md) of atoms or molecules, present even without a permanent dipole. At large separation compared with atomic size in the nonretarded regime, the dipole interaction scales as $r^{-3}$ and its second-order ground-state energy correction is negative and scales as $r^{-6}$. Differentiating the energy gives an attractive [force](force-split.md) of magnitude $6C_6/r^7$. The short-distance overlap regime and the very-long-distance retarded regime require different descriptions.
