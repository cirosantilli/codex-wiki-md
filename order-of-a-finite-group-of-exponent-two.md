# Order of a finite group of exponent two

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md)

A [group of exponent two is abelian](group-of-exponent-two-is-abelian.md). Give such a finite group the additive notation of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over $\mathbb F_2$, with zero acting as zero and one as the identity scalar. The group axioms and $g+g=0$ verify the vector-space laws. A [basis](basis.md) with $d$ vectors then gives exactly $2^d$ elements. Thus a finite group whose elements all have order at most two must have power-of-two order.
