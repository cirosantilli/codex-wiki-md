# Pentalenide

↑ **Parent:** [Pentalene](pentalene.md)

The dianion $\mathrm{C_8H_6^{2-}}$, with ten pi [electrons](electron.md) over the fused framework. The two overlapping five-membered rings must not be counted as two independent cyclopentadienyl donors.
