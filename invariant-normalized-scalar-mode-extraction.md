# Invariant-normalized scalar mode extraction

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md)

For a free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) expanded with measure $d^3p/[(2\pi)^3 2E_p]$ and $f_p=e^{-ip\cdot x}$, the displayed [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md) extracts its [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md). Indeed $(f_p,f_q)=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$ and $(f_p,f_q^*)=0$; spatial [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) removes the measure. Taking the [Hermitian adjoint](adjoint-operator.md) gives $a^\dagger(\mathbf p)=-i\int d^3x\,f_p\overleftrightarrow{\partial_t}\phi$. The equal-time [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) then gives $[a(\mathbf p),a^\dagger(\mathbf q)]=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$.
