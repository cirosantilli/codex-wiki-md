# Hopf thresholds in a two-pool calcium model

↑ **Parent:** [Two-pool calcium model](two-pool-calcium-model.md)

The unique [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has $u=\mu$ and $f=0$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is $-f_v/\epsilon>0$ and [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $-1+(f_v-\gamma f_u)/\epsilon$. Thresholds are obtained from the two displayed [equations](equation.md), and a positive-trace interval exists only if the fast normal [derivative](derivative.md) rises above $\epsilon$. At a nondegenerate [trace](matrix-trace.md) crossing, the linear [oscillation](oscillation.md) [frequency](frequency.md) is $\sqrt{-f_v/\epsilon}$. The sign of a nonlinear Hopf coefficient is needed to classify the local periodic branch; in a bounded positive planar flow, a unique [repelling equilibrium](source-equilibrium.md) also allows a Poincaré-Bendixson existence argument for a [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md).
