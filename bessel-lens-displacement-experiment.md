# Bessel lens displacement experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel lens displacement method](bessel-lens-displacement-method.md)

KIT's teaching apparatus uses a glass spoke-wheel or test negative as the object, a halogen lamp with an illuminating [optical lens](lens-optics.md), an imaging [optical lens](lens-optics.md), a screen and a triangular rail with a position scale. The illuminating [optical lens](lens-optics.md) concentrates the lamp filament's image on the imaging [optical lens](lens-optics.md)'s centre; translucent paper checks that illumination. This reduces the contribution of peripheral [light rays](light-ray.md) and their [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md). The instructions require fixed object-screen separation exceeding $4f$, at least ten displacement measurements, and separate red- and blue-filter measurements to test [chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md).[Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Brennweite dünner Linsen, Durchführung and Aufgaben 1a and 2: target, illumination check, rail readout, repeated Bessel measurements and colour controls](brennweite-dunner-linsen-by-karlsruhe-institute-of-technology-faculty-of-physics-teaching-laboratory.md)

The following measurement protocol elaborates that apparatus rather than reporting additional KIT results. Align the target centre, [optical lens](lens-optics.md) and screen normal along one [optical axis](optical-axis.md); measure $L$ between the target pattern plane and receiving screen face. Lock the target and screen. Slide the imaging [optical lens](lens-optics.md) to obtain the enlarged [real image](real-image.md), then the reduced [real image](real-image.md), reading the same carrier reference mark at each setting. Record $r_-,r_+$ and $d=r_+-r_-$ for each pair. Record corresponding target and image intervals to test the reciprocal [transverse magnifications](transverse-magnification.md) independently; merely observing different image sizes is a weaker test.

Use the same central target detail to judge best [optical focus](focus-optics.md). Bracket each setting by moving through both visibly blurred sides, repeat from both travel directions, and record the accepted position range as well as repeat scatter. Rail divisions set a readout limit but do not alone specify focusing uncertainty. Check scale calibration, carrier play and drift; a common additive carrier offset cancels in $d$, whereas scale-factor error remains. Keep illumination, [aperture](aperture.md), target, screen and [optical lens](lens-optics.md) orientation fixed within a series. Change the [aperture](aperture.md) and colour filter in separate control series: [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md) and [chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md) can shift the apparent best [optical focus](focus-optics.md), while a very small [aperture](aperture.md) introduces stronger [diffraction](diffraction.md) and makes focus discrimination harder. Record these settings alongside the inferred [focal length](focal-length.md), rather than interpreting all disagreement as random [measurement error](measurement-error.md).

For a rigid [thick lens](thick-lens.md) in air, let $h=x_{H'}-x_H$ be the signed separation of its rear and front [principal planes](principal-plane.md) along the direction of propagation. Then the positive object and image distances measured from the [principal planes](principal-plane.md) obey $u+v=L-h$. Translating the whole assembly without reversing it moves both [principal planes](principal-plane.md) equally, so the measured carrier displacement still equals the difference of the two solutions for $u$. The same algebra therefore gives

$$
d^2=(L-h)^2-4f(L-h),\qquad f=\frac{(L-h)^2-d^2}{4(L-h)},\qquad L-h>4f.
$$

Here $f$ is the effective [focal length](focal-length.md) measured from the [principal planes](principal-plane.md). This deduction uses the principal-plane imaging equation, not the uncorrected [thin lens](thin-lens.md) formula.[Refracting systems (details), principal-plane definition and final paragraph on object/image distances](refracting-systems-details-university-of-tennessee-physics-teaching-resource.md) Mechanical centre thickness is not generally $h$. Record thickness and [optical lens](lens-optics.md) orientation, but use a justified bound or model for $h$ if it matters at the claimed precision. The two readings $L,d$ alone cannot determine both $f$ and unknown $h$; cancelling a carrier offset does not cancel this limitation.
