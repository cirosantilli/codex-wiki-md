<h1 id="taylor-proudman-theorem">Taylor–Proudman theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating fluid](rotating-fluid.md)

For homogeneous, incompressible, inviscid flow with constant rotation vector and negligible inertia, taking the [curl](curl.md) of $2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u=-\nabla p$ gives $(\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=0$. Thus a [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) is independent of distance along the rotation axis. Body forces modify the relation by their [curl](curl.md); it applies unchanged in unforced exterior regions. A nontrivial exterior column cannot both obey this leading balance and decay along the rotation axis.
