# Drift chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)

A [drift chamber](drift-chamber.md) uses the drift times of [ionization](ionization.md) [electrons](electron.md) in a [gas](gas.md) to infer positions along [charged particle](charged-particle.md) tracks. In a known [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), [track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md) can estimate [momentum](momentum-split.md) and the sign of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
