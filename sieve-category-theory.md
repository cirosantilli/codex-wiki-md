# Sieve (category theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Presheaf category](presheaf-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieve_(category_theory))

A sieve on $U$ is a subfunctor of the [representable functor](representable-functor.md) $yU$, equivalently a family of arrows into $U$ closed under precomposition. For $f:V\to U$, the pullback sieve consists of arrows $g$ into $V$ for which $fg$ belongs to the original sieve.
