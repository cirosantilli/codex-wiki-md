# Projective equivalence of affine connections

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

Two [torsion-free connections](torsion-free-connection.md) are [projectively equivalent](projective-equivalence-of-affine-connections.md) if they have the same unparametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md). Their [difference of affine connections is a tensor](difference-of-affine-connections-is-a-tensor.md) $S$. A change of parameter adds only tangential acceleration, so equality of curves requires $S(v,v)$ to be parallel to $v$ for every tangent vector. The [symmetric bilinear diagonal-parallel lemma](symmetric-bilinear-diagonal-parallel-lemma.md) gives exactly the displayed condition. Conversely the [geodesic reparametrization under projective equivalence](geodesic-reparametrization-under-projective-equivalence.md) absorbs this tangential term. Agreement with the same [affine parameters](affine-parameter.md) instead forces $S=0$.
