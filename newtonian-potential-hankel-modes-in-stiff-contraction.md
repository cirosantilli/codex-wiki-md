# Newtonian-potential Hankel modes in stiff contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological contraction with a free homogeneous scalar](cosmological-contraction-with-a-free-homogeneous-scalar.md)

In [conformal Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md), a free scalar has no linear anisotropic stress. Eliminating its perturbation with the momentum constraint gives $\Phi_k''+3\Phi_k'/\tau+k^2\Phi_k=0$. Substitution of $\Phi_k=(-\tau)^{-1}y(-k\tau)$ gives the order-one [Bessel equation](bessel-differential-equation.md). The displayed [Hankel function](hankel-function.md) has positive incoming frequency $e^{-ik\tau}$ at early times.
