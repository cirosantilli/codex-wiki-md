# Quasienergy

↑ **Parent:** [Floquet operator](floquet-operator.md)

A [quasienergy](quasienergy.md) is defined by an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the [Floquet operator](floquet-operator.md). It is determined modulo $2\pi\hbar/T$, because adding an integer multiple of that quantity leaves the [complex exponential](complex-exponential-function.md) unchanged. A time-independent effective [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) can be defined by a branch of the [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md); its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the corresponding chosen [quasienergies](quasienergy.md).
