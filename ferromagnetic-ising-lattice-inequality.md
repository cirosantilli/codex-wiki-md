# Ferromagnetic Ising lattice inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

For $H(\sigma)=\sum_{xy}J_{xy}\sigma_x\sigma_y+\sum_xh_x\sigma_x$ on a finite graph, assume $J_{xy}\ge0$. Let $U=\{x:\sigma_x=1,\tau_x=-1\}$ and $W=\{x:\sigma_x=-1,\tau_x=1\}$. The displayed difference is $4\sum_{xy:\,x\in U,y\in W\text{ or conversely}}J_{xy}$: all other edge terms and all field terms cancel. Exponentiating proves the [FKG lattice condition](fkg-lattice-condition.md) and hence [positive association of random variables](positive-association-of-random-variables.md) for this [Ising model](ising-model.md).
