# Transport port

↑ **Parent:** [Transport protocol](transport-protocol.md)

A [transport port](transport-port.md) is a numbered identifier used by a [transport protocol](transport-protocol.md) to distinguish communication [endpoints](communication-endpoint.md) at a host. [TCP](transmission-control-protocol.md) and [UDP](user-datagram-protocol.md) have separate port spaces.
