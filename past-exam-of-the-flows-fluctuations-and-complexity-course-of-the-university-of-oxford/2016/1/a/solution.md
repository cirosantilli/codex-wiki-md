<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [irrotational flow](../../../../irrotational-flow.md) satisfies $\nabla\times\mathbf u=0$. On a simply connected region, or locally on any small regular region, this gives a [velocity potential](../../../../velocity-potential.md) $\phi$ with $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$. The [incompressibility condition](../../../../incompressible-flow.md) is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\phi=0.}
$$

Thus the potential is a [harmonic function](../../../../harmonic-function.md). The locality qualification matters: a curl-free circulation around an excluded vortex core need not admit a globally single-valued potential. No such circulation is needed for the sphere flow below.
