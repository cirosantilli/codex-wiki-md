<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On the surface, $u_r=0$ and $u_\theta=(3v/2)\sin\theta$. The only surface [stagnation points](../../../../stagnation-point.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{(0,0,R)\quad\text{and}\quad(0,0,-R).}
$$

There are no other [stagnation points](../../../../stagnation-point.md) in the exterior: away from the axis the polar component is nonzero, and on the axis the radial component vanishes only at $r=R$.

Near the front point put $(X,Y,Z)=(x,y,z-R)$. Expanding the potential from [solution](../c/solution.md) gives

$$
\phi=-\frac{3vR}{2}+\frac{3v}{4R}(X^2+Y^2-2Z^2)+O(|\mathbf X|^3/R^2),
$$

so the linear [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) field is $(3vX/(2R),3vY/(2R),-3vZ/R)$. This agrees with [solution](../b/solution.md) for $W=3v/(4R)$.

At the rear point, using $Z=z+R$ instead, the quadratic term has the opposite sign: $W=-3v/(4R)$. Incoming axial flow and tangential outflow at the front become tangential inflow and axial outflow at the back. The local [streamline](../../../../streamline.md) geometry is unchanged while its direction reverses.
