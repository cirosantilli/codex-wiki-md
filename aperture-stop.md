# Aperture stop

↑ **Parent:** [Aperture](aperture.md)

An [aperture stop](aperture-stop.md) is the physical opening that limits the angular cone of rays from an on-axis object point. Its images through preceding and following optics are the entrance and exit [optical pupils](optical-pupil.md); it differs from a field stop, which limits the extent of the imaged field.
