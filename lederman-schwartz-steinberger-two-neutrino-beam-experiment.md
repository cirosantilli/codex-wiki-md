# Lederman-Schwartz-Steinberger two-neutrino beam experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)

In 1962, [Leon Max Lederman](leon-max-lederman.md), [Melvin Schwartz](melvin-schwartz.md), [Jack Steinberger](jack-steinberger.md) and collaborators distinguished [muon neutrinos](muon-neutrino.md) from [electron neutrinos](electron-neutrino.md) using a [neutrino beam](neutrino-beam.md).[Discovery of the Muon Neutrino](1988-nobel-prize-by-brookhaven-national-laboratory.md)

The [Alternating Gradient Synchrotron](alternating-gradient-synchrotron.md) delivered $15\,\mathrm{GeV}$ [protons](proton.md) onto [beryllium](beryllium.md). [Particle decay](particle-decay.md) of [pions](pion.md) and [kaons](kaon.md) supplied [neutrinos](neutrino.md); a $13.5\,\mathrm m$ [iron](iron.md) shield preceded a ten-ton [aluminium](aluminium.md) [spark chamber](spark-chamber.md).[pp. 37–38, Figs. 1–3](observation-of-high-energy-neutrino-reactions-and-the-existence-of-two-kinds-of-neutrinos-by-g-danby-and-others.md)

Production and detection are separate. In modern [neutrino flavour](neutrino-flavor.md) notation,

$$
\pi^+\longrightarrow\mu^++\nu_\mu.
$$

Shielding suppresses the daughter [muon](muon.md); a newly produced [muon](muon.md) inside the [spark chamber](spark-chamber.md)'s [aluminium](aluminium.md) marks an interaction.[Decay and aluminium readout](1988-nobel-prize-by-brookhaven-national-laboratory.md)

Beam-timed [coincidence circuits](coincidence-circuit.md) and veto counters rejected entering tracks. Machine-off runs measured [cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md) contamination; shield-thickness changes tested [neutrons](neutron-split.md). Placing [lead](lead.md) near the target shortened the [meson](meson.md) decay path and reduced events. Cosmotron [electron](electron.md) calibration established [particle shower](particle-shower.md) recognition.[pp. 38–42, Table II, Figs. 8–9](observation-of-high-energy-neutrino-reactions-and-the-existence-of-two-kinds-of-neutrinos-by-g-danby-and-others.md)

The paper compared $34$ single-[muon](muon.md) candidates, including $5$ estimated [cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md) events, with six shower candidates unlike calibrated [electron](electron.md) showers. [Kaon](kaon.md) decays could supply genuine [electron neutrinos](electron-neutrino.md). The deficit favored distinct [neutrino flavours](neutrino-flavor.md) under [lepton universality](lepton-universality.md).[p. 42, Figs. 8–9](observation-of-high-energy-neutrino-reactions-and-the-existence-of-two-kinds-of-neutrinos-by-g-danby-and-others.md)

The inference concerns production and detection channels. It does not measure [neutrino](neutrino.md) [mass](mass.md) or establish permanently fixed [neutrino flavour](neutrino-flavor.md). Later [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) experiments address changes during propagation.[Discovery of the Muon Neutrino; Super-Kamiokande](brookhaven-neutrino-research-by-brookhaven-national-laboratory.md)
