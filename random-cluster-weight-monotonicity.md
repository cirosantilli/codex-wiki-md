# Random-cluster weight monotonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md)

For fixed $p\in(0,1)$ and $1\le q_1\le q_2$, the [random-cluster measure](random-cluster-model.md) at $q_1$ [stochastically dominates](stochastic-domination-of-probability-measures.md) that at $q_2$. In [Holley's condition](holley-condition.md), Bernoulli factors cancel. Put $a=k(S)-k(S\cup T)$ and $b=k(S\cap T)-k(T)$; [supermodularity of graph component count](supermodularity-of-graph-component-count.md) gives $b\ge a\ge0$, and the weight ratio is $(q_2/q_1)^a q_2^{b-a}\ge1$. The comparison also holds with the same boundary wiring in both measures.
