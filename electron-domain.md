# Electron domain

↑ **Parent:** [Valence shell electron pair repulsion theory](valence-shell-electron-pair-repulsion-theory.md)

An [electron domain](electron-domain.md) is a region of [electron density](electron-density.md) counted around a central [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) in the [VSEPR model](valence-shell-electron-pair-repulsion-theory.md). One [lone pair](lone-pair.md) counts as one domain; a bond to one neighbour counts as one domain even when its [bond order](bond-order.md) is two or three. Domain count is therefore different from simply counting [electron pairs](electron-pair.md).
