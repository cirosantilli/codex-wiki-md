# Petersen graph

↑ **Parent:** [Regular graph](regular-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersen_graph)

The [graph](graph-split.md) whose vertices are two-element subsets of a five-element set, with two vertices adjacent when the subsets are disjoint. It has ten vertices, degree three, zero common neighbours for adjacent pairs and one for nonadjacent pairs. Its [adjacency matrix of a graph](adjacency-matrix.md) satisfies $A^2+A-2I=J$ and has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $3,1,-2$ of multiplicities $1,5,4$.
