# Fixed-point congruence for an involution on an odd-sphere connected sum

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz-Hopf fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-hopf-fixed-point-theorem.md)

Let $W_g$ be a [connected sum of oriented manifolds](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) consisting of $g$ copies of $S^r\times S^r$ with odd $r\geq1$. Suppose an orientation-preserving smooth [involution](involution.md) $f$ has finitely many fixed points, all nondegenerate with positive [determinant](determinant.md) $\det(I-Df_x)$. The [Lefschetz-Hopf fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-hopf-fixed-point-theorem.md) and the [cohomology ring of a connected sum of odd-dimensional sphere products](cohomology-ring-of-a-connected-sum-of-odd-dimensional-sphere-products.md) give

$$
\#\operatorname{Fix}(f)=L(f)=2-\operatorname{tr}(f^*|_{H^r(W_g;\mathbb R)}).
$$

This middle group has dimension $2g$ and its [Poincare duality pairing](poincare-duality-pairing.md) is alternating. The induced map preserves the pairing and squares to the identity. By [eigenspaces of a symplectic involution](eigenspaces-of-a-symplectic-involution.md), its trace is $2g-4b$ for an integer $b$. Therefore $\#\operatorname{Fix}(f)\equiv2-2g\pmod4$.
