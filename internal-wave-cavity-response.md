# Internal-wave cavity response

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave transmission across a buoyancy-frequency jump](internal-wave-transmission-across-a-buoyancy-frequency-jump.md)

A reflecting bottom beneath a jump in [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) creates a standing field from repeated transmitted and reflected [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md). For ray angles $\theta_1=\pi/3$ and $\theta_2=\pi/6$, the downward particle-displacement amplitude has magnitude $\sqrt3|\eta_i|/[1+8\sin^2(m_2H)]^{1/2}$. It reaches $\sqrt3|\eta_i|$ when $m_2H$ is an integer multiple of $\pi$. The upper reflected wave has the same amplitude magnitude as the incident wave because the lossless closed cavity admits no net downward energy flux.
