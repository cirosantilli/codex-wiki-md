# Jacobi integral of a rigidly rotating potential

↑ **Parent:** [Integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md)

For $\Phi(R,\phi-\Omega t,z)$ with constant pattern angular velocity $\Omega$, ordinary specific [energy](energy.md) satisfies $\dot E=\partial_t\Phi=-\Omega\partial_\phi\Phi$ and specific axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) satisfies $\dot L_z=-\partial_\phi\Phi$. Their combination $E_J$ is conserved. This is the rotating-frame [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md); in the [circular restricted three-body problem](circular-restricted-three-body-problem.md), the usual [Jacobi constant](jacobi-constant.md) is an affine rescaling of it. Thus time dependence in an inertial frame does not alone imply the absence of all integrals.
