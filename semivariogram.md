# Semivariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsically stationary random field](intrinsically-stationary-random-field.md)

For an [intrinsically stationary random field](intrinsically-stationary-random-field.md), the [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) is $\gamma(h)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Var}[Z(s+h)-Z(s)]$. It is even and vanishes at zero. With constant mean, a second-order stationary field has $\gamma(h)=C(0)-C(h)$. An isotropic model writes it as a function of nonnegative distance.
