# Degeneracy onset from thermal wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md)

Equating [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $h/\sqrt{2\pi mk_BT}$ to mean particle spacing $n^{-1/3}$ gives the displayed quantum-degeneracy scale. It is an onset estimate rather than a universal phase-transition [temperature](temperature-split.md). Different typical-momentum conventions change its order-one factor; [density](density.md), [mass](mass.md), spin degeneracy and interactions determine the detailed crossover or condensation criterion.
