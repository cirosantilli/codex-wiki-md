# Paired logarithmic series with zero sum

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional convergence](conditional-convergence.md)

The terms at $n=2j$ and $n=2j+1$ are $\ln[(2j+1)/(2j)]$ and its negative. Thus the [partial sums](partial-sum.md) ending at an odd index are zero, while those ending at $2m$ equal $\ln[(2m+1)/(2m)]\to0$. Considering both subsequences proves convergence of the full sequence of [partial sums](partial-sum.md), not merely of grouped terms.

The paired sum of absolute values is $2\ln[1+1/(2j)]$. Since $\ln(1+u)\geq u/(1+u)$ for $u\geq0$, these pairs dominate $2/(2j+1)$, whose series diverges by comparison with the [harmonic series](harmonic-series.md). The original [series](series-mathematics.md) therefore has [conditional convergence](conditional-convergence.md) with the displayed sum.
