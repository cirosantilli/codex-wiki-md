# Baryon-density consistency between nucleosynthesis and the microwave background

↑ **Parent:** [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md)

For the measured mean [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md) temperature, the [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) and [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) obey the displayed conversion. [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), especially deuterium, measures this density during the first minutes, while acoustic peak ratios measure it near recombination. Agreement tests the thermal history and approximate conservation of baryon number and photon entropy between these epochs. It is not a claim that every observed light-element abundance has no residual tension.
