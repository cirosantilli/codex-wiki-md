# Time-dependent quantum perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md)

An additional time-dependent operator in a quantum [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). It may couple stationary states or shift their phases. It is the physical operator being treated, whereas [time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md) is a method for approximating the response.
