# Bouguer slab

↑ **Parent:** [Bouguer gravity anomaly](bouguer-gravity-anomaly.md)

An ideal laterally infinite horizontal slab, whose vertical [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md) is proportional to its thickness $h$ and [geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) $\Delta\rho$ with replaced material. A negative [geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) gives a negative anomaly in a downward-positive convention. The slab's field above it does not specify burial depth; applying it to finite bodies is an approximation.
