# Counting cycles in an interval covering graph

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic orbit from a closed interval-covering walk](periodic-orbit-from-a-closed-interval-covering-walk.md)

If $A$ is the [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix-of-a-directed-graph.md) of a directed covering graph, then $\operatorname{tr}(A^n)$ counts its pointed closed walks of length $n$. For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, subtracting the $\operatorname{tr}(A)$ constant walks and identifying the $p$ cyclic choices of starting point gives

$$
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A^p)-\operatorname{tr}(A)}p
$$

primitive closed itineraries of length $p$.
