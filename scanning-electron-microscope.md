# Scanning electron microscope

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scanning_electron_microscope)

A [scanning electron microscope](scanning-electron-microscope.md) scans a focused [electron](electron.md) probe across a specimen and assigns a detector signal to each probe position. [Secondary electrons](secondary-electron.md) leaving the irradiated region often reveal surface relief; [backscattered electrons](backscattered-electron.md) can provide composition-dependent contrast. Image brightness records the selected signal, rather than a transmitted projection enlarged by two lenses. Display enlargement is therefore set by the ratio of display width to scanned specimen width. Surface geometry and composition both affect brightness.[JEOL, Why can a specimen be observed with an electron? and Various Function of SEM: secondary and backscattered signals](scanning-electron-microscopes-sem-by-jeol.md)
