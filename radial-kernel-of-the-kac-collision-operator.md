# Radial kernel of the Kac collision operator

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet form of the Kac collision operator](dirichlet-form-of-the-kac-collision-operator.md)

Zero [Dirichlet form of the Kac collision operator](dirichlet-form-of-the-kac-collision-operator.md) forces invariance under every pair rotation; strong continuity upgrades almost every angle to every angle. Coordinate-plane rotations generate the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md), whose action is transitive on each sphere for $N\geq2$. Averaging over normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) therefore identifies the invariant $L^2$ functions with [radial functions](radial-function.md). Conversely radial functions are fixed by every pair rotation.
