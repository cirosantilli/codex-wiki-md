# Scheduling quantum

↑ **Parent:** [Round-robin scheduling](round-robin-scheduling.md)

The maximum amount of [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) execution allotted in one turn by [round-robin scheduling](round-robin-scheduling.md). A task can finish before consuming its [scheduling quantum](scheduling-quantum.md). When other tasks remain runnable, a shorter [scheduling quantum](scheduling-quantum.md) provides more frequent turns at the cost of more frequent [context switches](context-switch.md).
