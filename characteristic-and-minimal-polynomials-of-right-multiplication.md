# Characteristic and minimal polynomials of right multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md)

On two-by-two complex matrices let $\rho_A(X)=XA$. In row-entry coordinates, its matrix is $\operatorname{diag}(A^T,A^T)$, so its [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) is the square of that of $A$. For every polynomial $p$, $p(\rho_A)(X)=Xp(A)$. Thus $p(A)=0$ implies $p(\rho_A)=0$, and the reverse implication follows by taking $X=I$. The operators have exactly the same annihilating polynomials and hence the same [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md). In general, right multiplication on $r$-by-$n$ matrices has characteristic polynomial $\chi_A^r$ and the same minimal polynomial.
