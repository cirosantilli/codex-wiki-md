# Gaussian Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

The first Gaussian Sobolev space consists of functions and their weak first [derivatives](derivative.md) square-integrable for standard [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md). If $f=\sum c_nh_n$ in the normalized [Probabilists' Hermite polynomial](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) basis, [Gaussian integration by parts](stein-s-lemma-probability.md) gives $\langle f',h_{n-1}\rangle=\sqrt n c_n$. Thus [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) identifies it with $\sum(1+n)|c_n|^2<\infty$, and polynomial truncations converge in its norm. It is the form domain of the [Gaussian number operator](gaussian-number-operator.md), with [Gaussian Dirichlet energy](gaussian-dirichlet-energy.md) $\sum n|c_n|^2$.
