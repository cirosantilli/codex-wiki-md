# Cytoskeleton

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytoskeleton)

A network of protein filaments within a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) that contributes to shape, mechanical support and motion. Its coupling to the [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) adds mechanical responses, including shear elasticity, absent from an isolated fluid bilayer.
