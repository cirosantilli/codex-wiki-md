# Quotient of functions

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

For [functions](function-split.md) $f$ and $g$ taking values in the same [field](field.md), their [quotient of functions](quotient-of-functions.md) is defined by $(f/g)(x)=f(x)/g(x)$ on the common [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) restricted to points where $g(x)\ne0$. Thus $f(x)=1$ and $g(x)=x$ on the [real line](real-line.md) give a [quotient of functions](quotient-of-functions.md) defined only for $x\ne0$.
