# Helix-turn-helix

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)

A [helix-turn-helix](helix-turn-helix.md) is a structural DNA-binding motif in which an intervening turn relates two helices, one often contributing major-groove recognition contacts. The motif alone does not specify which DNA sequence is recognized.
