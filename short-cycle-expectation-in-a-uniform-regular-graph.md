# Short-cycle expectation in a uniform regular graph

↑ **Parent:** [Regular graph](regular-graph.md)

For fixed $r,\ell\geq3$ and $rn$ even, a prescribed [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) in the [configuration model](configuration-model.md) has two ordered distinct half-edges at each of its vertices. Therefore its expected count is $(n)_\ell[r(r-1)]^\ell/[2\ell\prod_{j=0}^{\ell-1}(rn-2j-1)]$. Conditioning on the chosen cycle leaves degrees $r-2$ at its vertices and $r$ elsewhere; forbid further edges along that cycle. The [bounded-degree pairing avoidance estimate](bounded-degree-pairing-avoidance-estimate.md) changes by $o(1)$, so the simplicity probability conditional on the cycle divided by the unconditional probability tends to one. The displayed expectation therefore holds for the uniform [simple graph](simple-graph.md), not just the unconditioned pairing model.
