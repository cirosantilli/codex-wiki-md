# Reflection group of a root system

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl reflection](weyl-reflection.md)

The reflection group of a root system $\Phi$ is the subgroup of the orthogonal group generated by the reflections $s_\alpha$ for $\alpha\in\Phi$. It permutes the roots and acts freely and transitively on the fundamental systems.
