# Gysin map in complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Complex orientation of a smooth map](complex-orientation-of-a-smooth-map.md)

For a proper map with fixed complex normal orientation, apply the [Thom isomorphism in complex cobordism](thom-isomorphism-in-complex-cobordism.md), pull back by the collapse of a tubular neighborhood of a graph embedding, and desuspend the auxiliary Euclidean coordinates. The degree increase is the relative real codimension. Changing the orientation can change this map.
