# Krull dimension of rings of algebraic integers

↑ **Parent:** [Integral extensions preserve Krull dimension](integral-extensions-preserve-krull-dimension.md)

For any algebraic field extension $K/\mathbb Q$, including an infinite one, its ring $\mathcal O_K$ of elements integral over $\mathbb Z$ is an [integral extension](integral-extension.md) of $\mathbb Z$. The [Lying-over theorem](lying-over-theorem.md), [going-up theorem](going-up-theorem.md) and [incomparability theorem for integral extensions](incomparability-theorem-for-integral-extensions.md) give $\dim\mathcal O_K=\dim\mathbb Z=1$. The argument does not require that $\mathcal O_K$ be Noetherian or finitely generated over $\mathbb Z$.
