# Sakharov conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md)

The standard conditions for [baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md) from a symmetric initial ensemble are [baryon number](baryon-number.md) violation, both [C violation](violation-of-charge-conjugation.md) and [CP violation](cp-violation.md), and departure from [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md). Conserved [baryon number](baryon-number.md) cannot develop a net value from zero; an exact conjugation symmetry pairs production with equal antiproduction. In [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md), [CPT symmetry](cpt-symmetry.md) makes the equilibrium expectation of net [baryon number](baryon-number.md) vanish for zero imposed [chemical potentials](chemical-potential.md) for charges odd under [CPT symmetry](cpt-symmetry.md). These conditions assume the usual local quantum-field framework and do not alone guarantee a large asymmetry.
