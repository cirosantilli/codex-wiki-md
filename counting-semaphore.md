# Counting semaphore

↑ **Parent:** [Semaphore (programming)](semaphore-programming.md)

A [semaphore](semaphore-programming.md) with a nonnegative number of available permits. Its contract must state initial availability and whether unmatched releases beyond an initial or configured bound are allowed.
