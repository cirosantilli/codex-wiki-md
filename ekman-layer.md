# Ekman layer

↑ **Parent:** [Wind stress](wind-stress.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekman_layer)

An Ekman layer is a viscous boundary layer in a rotating fluid. For constant [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$ and [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) $f$, its characteristic thickness is $\delta_E=\sqrt{2\nu/|f|}$, and its velocity turns with distance from the boundary.
