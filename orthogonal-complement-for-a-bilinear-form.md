# Orthogonal complement for a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

For a [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), the displayed right [bilinear orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement-for-a-bilinear-form.md) is a subspace. Nondegeneracy identifies the ambient space with its dual and gives $\dim W^\perp=\dim V-\dim W$. If the form is symmetric or alternating, orthogonality is reciprocal and $(W^\perp)^\perp=W$. For a general nonsymmetric form, left and right complements must be distinguished. A restricted symmetric or alternating form has [radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) $W\cap W^\perp$; quotienting by that [radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) gives a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) induced form.
