# Vortex fugacity

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex Coulomb gas](vortex-coulomb-gas.md)

A dimensionless statistical weight per microscopic [phase vortex](phase-vortex.md) core. In a dilute convention it is $y_0\simeq\exp[-E_c/(k_BT)]$, with core energy $E_c$ and a specified microscopic integration measure. Its numerical normalization fixes coefficients in the [vortex-pair renormalization flow](vortex-pair-renormalization-flow.md).
