# Executive function

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive psychology](cognitive-psychology.md)

Processes supporting goal-directed control, including maintaining and updating relevant information, inhibiting competing responses and changing task sets. Their importance depends on what a task requires: an initially effortful procedure may need less executive control after practice. The unity-and-diversity framework distinguishes correlated components without equating a single test score with a pure executive capacity.[Cognitive Psychology 41, 49–100; latent-variable analyses](the-unity-and-diversity-of-executive-functions-and-their-contributions-to-complex-frontal-lobe-tasks-by-akira-miyake-and-others.md)
