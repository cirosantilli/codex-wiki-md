# Tangent circles

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

Two distinct [circles](circle.md) are tangent when they meet at exactly one point. For external tangency the distance between centers is the sum of the [radii](radius.md); for internal tangency it is their absolute difference. These relations turn many [circle packing](circle-packing.md) problems into exact distance equations.
