# Rational-linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

A [rational-linear map](rational-linear-map.md) is a [linear map](linear-map.md) when its domain and codomain are regarded as [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md). An additive map on a rational [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of the [real numbers](real-number.md) is automatically rational-linear: [integer](integer-split.md) homogeneity follows by repeated [addition](addition.md), and division by a positive [integer](integer-split.md) gives [rational homogeneity of an additive function](rational-homogeneity-of-an-additive-function.md).
