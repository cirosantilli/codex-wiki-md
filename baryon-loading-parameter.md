# Baryon loading parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md)

The inertia ratio of the [photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md) is $R=3\bar\rho_b/(4\bar\rho_\gamma)$. Photon momentum is weighted by the radiation enthalpy $4\bar\rho_\gamma/3$, so this ratio controls momentum exchange, the common sound speed and the offset of the acoustic oscillator. Background conservation gives $R\propto a$ and $\dot R=\mathcal H R$.
