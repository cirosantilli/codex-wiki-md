# Phase-change volume source in a rigid mush

↑ **Parent:** [Mushy layer](mushy-layer.md)

For constant phase [mass densities](density.md) $\rho_s,\rho_l$, a fixed solid framework has mixture density $\bar\rho=\rho_s\varphi+\rho_l(1-\varphi)$. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives

$$
\partial_t\bar\rho+\rho_l\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0,\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf u=(1-r)\varphi_t,\qquad r=\rho_s/\rho_l,
$$

where $\mathbf u$ is [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md). Equal phase densities give a divergence-free flux. Unequal densities require expansion or contraction flow during freezing or melting.
