# Cataclysmic-variable period gap

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic-variable orbital-period distribution](cataclysmic-variable-orbital-period-distribution.md)

The cataclysmic-variable period gap is the observed deficit of accreting, ordinary hydrogen-rich [cataclysmic variables](cataclysmic-variable.md) with periods around two to three hours. The [disrupted magnetic braking model](disrupted-magnetic-braking-model.md) interprets it as a detached phase after the donor contracts inside its [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md).
