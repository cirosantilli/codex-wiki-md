# Rational normal curve

↑ **Parent:** [Projective curve](projective-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_normal_curve)

A rational normal curve in $\mathbb P^n$ is the image of the degree-$n$ [Veronese map](veronese-map.md) from $\mathbb P^1$. After projective changes of coordinates it has parametrization

$$
[S:T]\longmapsto[S^n:S^{n-1}T:\cdots:T^n].
$$
