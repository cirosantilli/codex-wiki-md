# Hyperbolic derivative density

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare metric on a Riemann surface](poincare-metric-on-a-riemann-surface.md)

For a nonconstant [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) into the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), the displayed density pulls the target [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) back to the source Euclidean coordinate. It is not the operator norm between two hyperbolic tangent spaces, which additionally divides by the source metric density. Away from [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $\Delta\log H_g=H_g^2$; a [ramification point](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) of [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) $m$ adds $2\pi m$ times its point mass to the distributional [Laplacian](laplacian.md).
