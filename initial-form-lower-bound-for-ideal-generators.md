# Initial-form lower bound for ideal generators

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal number of generators](minimal-number-of-generators.md)

Let $\mathfrak m$ be a [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of a [ring](ring.md) $A$, $k=A/\mathfrak m$, and $I\subseteq\mathfrak m^d$. Since $\mathfrak mI\subseteq\mathfrak m^{d+1}$, elements of $I$ with [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) classes in $\mathfrak m^d/\mathfrak m^{d+1}$ have independent classes in $I/\mathfrak mI$. Any generating set must span this latter [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Thus $r$ independent degree-$d$ initial forms give a lower bound of $r$ generators, without assuming that height equals the generator count.
