# Dunoyer molecular-beam experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular beam](molecular-beam.md)

In 1911, [Louis Dunoyer de Segonzac](louis-dunoyer-de-segonzac.md) formed a [sodium](sodium.md) beam in [vacuum](vacuum.md) and collected a deposit on a surface cooled with liquid air. Its shape agreed with straight paths through the slits.[The method of molecular rays, lecture 12 December 1946, pp. 8–9: Dunoyer's sodium beam, collimation and cooled collector](the-method-of-molecular-rays-by-otto-stern.md) A deposit tests the geometry of the surviving paths. A clock or a calibrated deflection is needed to turn their travel into a [speed](speed.md) measurement.
