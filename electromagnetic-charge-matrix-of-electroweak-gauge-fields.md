# Electromagnetic charge matrix of electroweak gauge fields

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak gauge boson](electroweak-gauge-boson.md)

In the $(A^1,A^2,A^3,B)$ basis with [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md) $U=e^{i\alpha Q}$ and $Q=T_3+Y$, the non-Abelian connection transforms by conjugation. The [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md) $[T_3,T_1]=iT_2$ and $[T_3,T_2]=-iT_1$ give $\delta A^1=\alpha A^2$, $\delta A^2=-\alpha A^1$, with neutral $A^3,B$. Thus $\delta A=i\alpha Q_{\rm gauge}A$ gives the displayed Hermitian matrix. The fields $W^\pm=(A^1\mp iA^2)/\sqrt2$ transform as $e^{\pm i\alpha}W^\pm$, while both neutral combinations are uncharged. This separates the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) on the gauge fields from the doublet charge matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ on the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md).
