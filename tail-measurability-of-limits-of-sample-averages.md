# Tail measurability of limits of sample averages

↑ **Parent:** [Tail sigma-algebra](tail-sigma-algebra.md)

For a sequence of finite real [random variables](random-variable-split.md), $\limsup_n n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^nX_j$ is measurable with respect to its [tail sigma-algebra](tail-sigma-algebra.md): deleting finitely many summands changes each [sample mean](sample-mean.md) by a quantity tending to zero. When the [random variables](random-variable-split.md) are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md), the [Kolmogorov zero-one law](kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) makes every finite such limit constant [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md).
