# Homotopy retract

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md)

In the Oxford course's convention, a [homotopy retract](homotopy-retract.md) is a [retraction](retraction.md) $r:X\to A$ such that the [inclusion map](inclusion-map.md) $i:A\hookrightarrow X$ also satisfies $ir\simeq\operatorname{id}_X$. It gives a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) between $A$ and $X$. Unlike the [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) convention used here, the [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) need not fix $A$ at intermediate times. [Topology and Groups, Definition II.11, printed p. 16](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lackenby/tg11o17.pdf#page=16)
