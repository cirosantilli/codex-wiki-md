# Indecomposable representation

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)

An indecomposable representation is nonzero and cannot be expressed as a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of two nonzero invariant subspaces. It is an [indecomposable module](indecomposable-module.md) over the associated [group algebra](group-algebra.md) or representing algebra.
