# Normal form (abstract rewriting)

↑ **Parent:** [Lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_form_(abstract_rewriting))

An expression in [normal form](normal-form-abstract-rewriting.md) admits no further reductions. A term is strongly normalizing when every reduction sequence from it terminates; weak normalization requires only one terminating sequence. [Strong normalization theorem for simply typed lambda calculus](strong-normalization-theorem-for-simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md) establishes the stronger property for well-typed terms.
