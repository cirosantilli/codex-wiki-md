# Regular outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Outer measure](outer-measure.md)

An [outer measure](outer-measure.md) is regular in this sense when every [set](set-split.md) admits a measurable hull of equal [outer measure](outer-measure.md). This is different from merely specifying inner or outer approximation of already [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) by compact or open sets. [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md) has a hull that is a [Borel set](borel-set.md): for finite [outer measure](outer-measure.md) choose open covers of total volumes within $1/j$ of its value and intersect their unions. [Monotonicity](monotonic-function.md) gives equality of the hull's [outer measure](outer-measure.md) with that of the original [set](set-split.md). For infinite [outer measure](outer-measure.md) the whole Euclidean space is a hull. This regularity is useful for turning numerical equality of inner and outer sizes into [measurability](measurability.md).
