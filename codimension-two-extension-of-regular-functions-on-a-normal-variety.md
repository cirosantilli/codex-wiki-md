# Codimension-two extension of regular functions on a normal variety

↑ **Parent:** [Normal scheme](normal-scheme.md)

For an integral [normal variety](normal-variety.md) that is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md), removing a closed subset of codimension at least two does not create new [regular functions](regular-function.md). Affine-locally, a normal Noetherian domain is the intersection of its localizations at height-one primes inside its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md). The complement still contains every height-one point, so a [regular function](regular-function.md) on it lies in each such [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) and therefore in the original ring. On projective space this also follows directly by excluding [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) factor of the denominators on each polynomial [affine chart](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md).
