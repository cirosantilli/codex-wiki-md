↑ **Parent:** [Phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md)

[phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate](phosphatidylinositol-3-5-bisphosphate.md) is a doubly phosphorylated [phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md) involved in later endolysosomal organization and function. Perturbing it need not imply that initial surface uptake is defective.
