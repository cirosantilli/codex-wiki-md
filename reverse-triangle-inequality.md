# Reverse triangle inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md)

In a [metric space](metric-space.md), applying the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) in both directions gives $|d(x,z)-d(y,z)|\leq d(x,y)$. In a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md), its corresponding form is $|\|x\|-\|y\||\leq\|x-y\|$. In particular, distance to a fixed point is a [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) real function.
