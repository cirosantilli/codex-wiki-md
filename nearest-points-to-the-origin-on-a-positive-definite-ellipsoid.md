# Nearest points to the origin on a positive-definite ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

For a [positive-definite symmetric matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $A$, the [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) $x^TAx=1$ has nearest points to the origin exactly at $x=\lambda_{\max}^{-1/2}u$, where $u$ ranges over [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) in the largest-[eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). Indeed, the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md) gives $x^TAx\leq\lambda_{\max}|x|^2$, with equality precisely in that [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). A simple largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) gives two opposite nearest points; multiplicity $m>1$ gives a scaled $(m-1)$-sphere of nearest points.
