# Charged sheet between grounded parallel plates

↑ **Parent:** [Conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md)

Place an infinite sheet of [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma$ at $z=0$ between grounded conducting planes $z=\pm d/2$, in [vacuum](vacuum.md). Reflection symmetry and [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) give $E_z=\sigma\operatorname{sgn}(z)/(2\varepsilon_0)$ in the two gaps. Integrating the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) with zero [electric potential](electric-potential.md) at the conductors gives $V(z)=\sigma(d/2-|z|)/(2\varepsilon_0)$. Each facing conducting surface carries induced [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $-\sigma/2$. For $\sigma>0$, an [electron](electron.md) released from rest at a plate reaches the sheet with [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $e\sigma d/(4\varepsilon_0)$; its nonrelativistic [speed](speed.md) follows from $m_ev^2/2$.
