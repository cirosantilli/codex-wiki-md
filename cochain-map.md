# Cochain map

↑ **Parent:** [Cochain complex](cochain-complex.md)

A [cochain map](cochain-map.md) $f:E\to F$ consists of degreewise maps $f^i:E^i\to F^i$ satisfying $d_F^i f^i=f^{i+1}d_E^i$. This identity sends cocycles to cocycles and coboundaries to coboundaries, hence induces maps on [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). For complexes of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) the same identity holds as an identity of [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) morphisms.
