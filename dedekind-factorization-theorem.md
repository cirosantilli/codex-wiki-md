# Dedekind factorization theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Unique factorization of ideals in a number field](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-number-field.md)

If $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\theta]$ and the minimal polynomial of $\theta$ factors modulo a rational prime $p$ as

$$
\overline f=\prod_i\overline g_i^{e_i},
$$

then

$$
(p)=\prod_i(p,g_i(\theta))^{e_i},
$$

and the prime ideal $(p,g_i(\theta))$ has norm $p^{\deg g_i}$.

This gives an explicit instance of [unique factorization of ideals in a number field](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-number-field.md).
