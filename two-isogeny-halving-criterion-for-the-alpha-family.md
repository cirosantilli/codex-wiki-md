# Two-isogeny halving criterion for the alpha family

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

For $C:y^2=x^3+\alpha x^2-\alpha x$, $\alpha\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0,-4\}$, the [two-isogeny formula](two-isogeny-formula.md) companion is $D:v^2=u(u^2-2\alpha u+\alpha(\alpha+4))$. With the positive-sign standard isogenies, the two preimages of $(1,1)$ under the dual are $(\alpha,-2\alpha)$ and $(\alpha+4,2(\alpha+4))$. The [two-torsion square-class homomorphism](two-torsion-square-class-homomorphism.md) on $D$ detects membership in the image of the forward [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) by the class of $u$. Thus either preimage lifts precisely when its first coordinate is a rational square. Their being [integers](integer-split.md) turns rational squareness into [integer](integer-split.md) squareness.
