# Resonant antidot backscattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall antidot](quantum-hall-antidot.md)

A closed [antidot](quantum-hall-antidot.md) edge orbit can tunnel-couple extended channels on opposite edges. When its electrochemical energy aligns with the reservoirs, resonant backscattering reduces conductance from an otherwise transmitting integer plateau. Different connectivity can instead add a transmission path and produce peaks. Gate [voltage](voltage.md) changes both the orbit and tunnel couplings; charging and [spin](spin.md) alter the simplest fixed-area Aharonov-Bohm resonance sequence.
