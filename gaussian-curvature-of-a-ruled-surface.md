# Gaussian curvature of a ruled surface

↑ **Parent:** [Ruled surface](ruled-surface.md)

For the regular $C^2$ [ruled surface](ruled-surface.md) $\sigma(x,y)=\mathbf a(x)+y\mathbf b(x)$, put $W=|(\mathbf a'+y\mathbf b')\times\mathbf b|>0$. The second-form coefficient in the ruling direction is zero, and the mixed coefficient is $\mathbf a'\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf b')/W$. The determinant formula for [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) therefore gives the displayed nonpositive expression. Its vanishing is equivalent to the zero scalar triple product, only at regular points.
