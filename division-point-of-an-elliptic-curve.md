# Division point of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

A division point above $P$ is a point $Q$ over an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md) satisfying $[N]Q=P$, for a specified nonzero integer $N$. Two such points differ by an $N$-[torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). If the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) does not divide $N$, there are $N^2$ distinct division points; in other characteristics the scheme fibre still has length $N^2$, but its geometric points can be fewer. The [unramified division torsors at good primes](unramified-division-torsors-at-good-primes.md) control their coordinate fields over [local fields](local-field.md).
