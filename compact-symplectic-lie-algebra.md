# Compact symplectic Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

The [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of the [compact symplectic group](compact-symplectic-group.md) consists of $X^\dagger=-X$ and $XJ+JX^T=0$. Equivalently $X=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\-\overline B&\overline A\end{pmatrix}$ with $A^\dagger=-A$ and $B^T=B$. Its real dimension is $n(2n+1)$, and its complexification is the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md).
