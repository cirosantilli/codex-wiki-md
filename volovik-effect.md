# Volovik effect

↑ **Parent:** [D-wave superconductivity](d-wave-superconductivity.md)

[Vortex](phase-vortex.md) superflow Doppler-shifts low-energy [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md) in a nodal superconductor. For a circular cell of radius $R\propto B^{-1/2}$ and flow magnitude proportional to $1/r$, its spatially averaged magnitude is proportional to $1/R$. A linearly vanishing nodal [density of states](density-of-states.md) then acquires a zero-energy contribution proportional to $\sqrt B$, producing a low-temperature linear heat-capacity coefficient with that scaling. This is a clean semiclassical mixed-state estimate; the vector flow average can be zero, and disorder, screening, core states and other field effects change its range of validity.
