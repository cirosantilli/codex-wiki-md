# Primordial atomic cooling curve

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical cooling function](astrophysical-cooling-function.md)

The [primordial atomic cooling curve](primordial-atomic-cooling-curve.md) describes hydrogen-helium gas without heavy elements. Near the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) excitation threshold it rises sharply, then shows [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [helium](helium.md) excitation/[ionization](ionization.md) features. Bound-state losses weaken when both elements become highly ionized. At sufficiently high temperatures [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) gives a slowly rising tail. Composition, density convention and [ionization](ionization.md) assumptions fix the numerical normalization.
