# Causal vertical wavenumber branch

↑ **Parent:** [Shear-horizontal wave](shear-horizontal-wave.md)

For real horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, positive real [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $\beta$ and $\omega=u+iv$ with $v>0$, write $k_\beta=a+ib$ with $k_\beta^2=\omega^2/\beta^2-k^2$. Its imaginary part gives $ab=uv/\beta^2$. The number $b$ cannot vanish, and $\operatorname{Re}(k_\beta/\omega)=v[b+u^2/(\beta^2b)]/|\omega|^2$ has the sign of $b$. The branch $b>0$ makes $e^{ik_\beta z}$ decay towards increasing depth and analytically continues the outgoing propagating or evanescent branch to the upper [frequency](frequency.md) half-plane.
