<h1 id="landauer-buttiker-formula">Landauer-Büttiker formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Landauer formula](landauer-formula.md)

A multi-reservoir [quantum transport](quantum-transport.md) relation. Here $I_i$ is conventional current injected from reservoir $i$, $N_i$ counts outgoing spin-resolved modes and $T_{ij}$ counts transmission from reservoir $j$ into $i$. Ideal voltage probes draw zero net current and equilibrate incident carriers. On a chiral integer Hall edge with $N$ modes, $I_i=(Ne^2/h)(V_i-V_{i-1})$ for the stated circulation order.
