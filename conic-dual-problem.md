# Conic dual problem

↑ **Parent:** [Conic optimization](conic-optimization.md)

For $\min_x c^\top x$ subject to $Ax-b\in K$, the [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md) is $\max_y b^\top y$ subject to $A^\top y=c$ and $y\in K^*$. At feasible primal-dual points, the gap is $\langle Ax-b,y\rangle\geq0$. A self-dual cone has $K^*=K$, but self-duality alone does not imply feasibility or [strong duality](strong-duality.md).
