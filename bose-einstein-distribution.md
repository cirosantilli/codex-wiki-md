# Bose-Einstein distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md)

The mean occupation of a bosonic one-particle state of energy $E$ is

$$
n(E)=\frac1{e^{\beta(E-\mu)}-1},
\qquad \beta=(k_BT)^{-1}.
$$
