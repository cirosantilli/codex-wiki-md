# Partial autocorrelation function

↑ **Parent:** [Autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md)

The partial [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) at lag $k$ is the correlation of $X_t$ and $X_{t-k}$ after their linear projections onto the intervening observations are removed. A causal [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md) of order $p$ has zero partial correlations after lag $p$. This complements the finite cutoff of the [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) for a [moving-average model](moving-average-model.md).
