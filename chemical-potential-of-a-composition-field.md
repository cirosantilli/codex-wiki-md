# Chemical potential of a composition field

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential.md)

For a composition [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $F=\int[f(\phi)+\kappa|\nabla\phi|^2/2]$, the local chemical potential is the [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md)

$$
\mu=f'(\phi)-\kappa\nabla^2\phi.
$$

At [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md), exchanges between the bulk phases require equal [chemical potentials](chemical-potential.md). Mechanical equilibrium additionally requires equal [pressure](pressure.md) for a flat interface, or a [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) pressure jump for a curved interface.
