# Ideal vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal triangle](ideal-triangle.md)

An [ideal vertex](ideal-vertex.md) is a vertex at infinity of an [ideal triangle](ideal-triangle.md) or another ideal hyperbolic polygon. An ideal vertex lies on the boundary at infinity and has internal angle zero.
