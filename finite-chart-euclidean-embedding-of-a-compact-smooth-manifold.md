# Finite-chart Euclidean embedding of a compact smooth manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md)

Choose finitely many coordinate charts on a [compact manifold](compact-manifold.md) and smooth functions supported in them, positive on a cover. Extend the displayed chart products by zero. A positive first coordinate and its matching chart coordinates separate points, while their derivatives detect every nonzero tangent vector. Thus the map is an injective [immersion](immersion.md); compactness makes it a [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md). Extra zero coordinates make its codimension positive if required.
