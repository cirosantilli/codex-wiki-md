# Bovine spongiform encephalopathy

↑ **Parent:** [Prion disease](prion-disease.md)

A [prion disease](prion-disease.md) of cattle. A positive surveillance test is an observed diagnostic outcome, whose frequency need not equal true infection [prevalence](prevalence.md) unless test performance and sampling are accounted for.
