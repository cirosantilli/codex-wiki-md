# Steady states of an affine Bloch equation

↑ **Parent:** [Affine Bloch equation](affine-bloch-equation.md)

An arbitrary real affine equation has [steady states](steady-state.md) exactly when $\operatorname{rank}A=\operatorname{rank}[A\mid-c]$. Its solution set is $s_*+\ker A$. For a finite-dimensional time-independent [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md), physical stationary states always exist by time averaging trajectories. If $A$ is invertible the [steady state](steady-state.md) is unique. Conversely, a nonzero traceless Hermitian stationary direction can be decomposed into the difference of two equally weighted [density operators](density-matrix.md); time averaging their trajectories yields two distinct stationary [density operators](density-matrix.md). Thus singularity of the physical Bloch matrix also obstructs physical uniqueness.
