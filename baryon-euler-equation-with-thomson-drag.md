# Baryon Euler equation with Thomson drag

↑ **Parent:** [Photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md)

For the velocity convention $v_\gamma=-\Theta_1$ and $\dot\tau<0$, momentum-conserving [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) gives $\dot v_b+\mathcal H v_b+k\psi=(\dot\tau/R)(v_b-v_\gamma)$. Here $R$ is the [baryon loading parameter](baryon-loading-parameter.md). Its collision force is opposite to the photon force when both are weighted by their enthalpies.
