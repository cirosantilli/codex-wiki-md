# Peptide backbone dihedral angles

↑ **Parent:** [Protein backbone](protein-backbone.md)

The [peptide backbone dihedral angles](peptide-backbone-dihedral-angles.md) are $\phi=\angle(C^{\prime}_{i-1},N_i,C_{\alpha i},C^{\prime}_i)$ and $\psi=\angle(N_i,C_{\alpha i},C^{\prime}_i,N_{i+1})$. Rotation is restricted by ring closure, [peptide](peptide.md) geometry and steric contacts.
