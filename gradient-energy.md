# Gradient energy

↑ **Parent:** [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md)

A gradient energy penalizes spatial variation of an [order parameter](order-parameter.md). Its leading isotropic form is usually proportional to the squared spatial gradient.
