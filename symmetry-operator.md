# Symmetry operator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

An operator implementing a symmetry of quantum states and observables. The ordinary spatial symmetries considered in molecular spectroscopy are unitary and commute with the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). More generally, an antiunitary symmetry such as [time reversal](t-symmetry.md) needs its corresponding treatment.
