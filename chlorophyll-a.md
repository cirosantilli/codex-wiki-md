# Chlorophyll a

↑ **Parent:** [Chlorophyll](chlorophyll.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlorophyll_a)

A widespread [photosynthetic pigment](photosynthetic-pigment.md) with prominent blue and red absorption bands. Its concentration is a useful index of [phytoplankton biomass](phytoplankton-biomass.md), but pigment per unit carbon varies with light acclimation and physiological condition.
