# Sobolev duality

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

The continuous [dual space](dual-space.md) of $H^s(\mathbb R^d)$ is $H^{-s}(\mathbb R^d)$ under the extension of the [L2 inner product](l2-inner-product.md). The [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) definition $\|f\|_{H^s}^2=\int(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\widehat f(\xi)|^2d\xi$ proves boundedness of the pairing by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), and the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) proves that every continuous functional has this form. Consequently a bounded [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $K:H^r\to H^{r+a}$ has an [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md), relative to this pairing, from $H^{-r-a}$ to $H^{-r}$. For an operator that smooths by two orders for every $r$, this yields $K^*:H^2\to H^4$ by taking $r=-4$.
