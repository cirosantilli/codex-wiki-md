# Extremal epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Epimorphism](epimorphism.md)

An [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) $e$ is extremal when a factorization $e=mh$ with $m$ a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) forces $m$ to be an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Every [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) is extremal: its coequalizer property factors $h$ back through $e$, giving an inverse to $m$. In a [regular category](regular-category.md), the converse follows by applying extremality to the regular-epi/mono image factorization.
