# Colloidal gel

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A [colloidal gel](colloidal-gel.md) is a mechanically persistent, space-spanning particle network arrested on the observation timescale. Attractions can produce such a network during aggregation or arrested phase separation. A gel is a dynamical, often nonequilibrium state; its appearance does not establish an additional equilibrium [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md).
