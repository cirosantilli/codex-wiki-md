# Supremum bound for a complete function-algebra norm

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

For an algebra of complex-valued functions with a complete [algebra norm](algebra-norm.md), every point value $f(t)$ belongs to the [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) of $f$ in the artificial [unitization](unitization-of-an-algebra.md). Indeed $(h,\lambda)\mapsto h(t)+\lambda$ is an algebraic unital [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md), and it vanishes on $f-f(t)1$. The [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) bound gives $|f(t)|\le\|f\|$ without first assuming that evaluation is continuous.
