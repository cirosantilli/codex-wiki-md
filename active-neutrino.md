# Active neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

A [neutrino](neutrino.md) participating in the weak interactions of the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), in contrast to a sterile state. Its thermal cosmological abundance and momentum distribution depend on weak decoupling and subsequent cosmic history.
