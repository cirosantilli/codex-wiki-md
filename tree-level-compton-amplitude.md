# Tree-level Compton amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

For a charged Dirac particle, incoming momenta $p,k$ and outgoing momenta $p',k'$ obey $p+k=p'+k'$. The two [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) diagrams have internal [fermion](fermion.md) momenta $p+k$ and $p-k'$, giving $s$ and $u$ channels. The amplitude is proportional to $\bar u(p')[\not\epsilon'^*R(p+k)\not\epsilon+\not\epsilon R(p-k')\not\epsilon'^*]u(p)$, where $R(q)=(\not q+m)/(q^2-m^2+i0)$. It is related to particle-[antiparticle](antiparticle.md) two-[photon](photon.md) annihilation by [crossing symmetry](crossing-symmetry.md), with appropriate changes to external wavefunctions and conjugations.
