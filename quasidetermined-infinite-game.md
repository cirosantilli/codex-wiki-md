# Quasidetermined infinite game

↑ **Parent:** [Quasistrategy](quasistrategy.md)

An [infinite game of perfect information](infinite-game-of-perfect-information.md) is quasidetermined when one of the players has a winning [quasistrategy](quasistrategy.md). Choosing one allowed move at every relevant position refines a winning quasistrategy to a [winning strategy in an infinite game](winning-strategy-in-an-infinite-game.md) whenever the required restricted choice principle holds.
