# Free-electron count from a Hall coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md)

In a one-carrier [Drude model](drude-model.md), the [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md) is $R_H=1/(nq)$. Combining its inferred [electron density](electron-density.md) with atomic density $\rho_mN_A/M$ gives the displayed number of mobile [electrons](electron.md) per atom. The [molar mass](molar-mass.md) and [mass density](density.md) must be supplied independently. Multiple bands, anisotropic carrier motion and Hall factors limit this simple interpretation; field reversal helps distinguish the transverse response from voltage offsets.
