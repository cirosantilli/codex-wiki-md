# Boundary approach forces a small SLE Bessel gap

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-point Bessel flow for SLE](boundary-point-bessel-flow-for-sle.md)

For $0<\kappa<4$ and $x>0$, stop the simple chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) trace on first entering the radius-$r$ half-disc about $x$, with $r<x/4$. A crosscut from its tip to $x$ separates the positive-side boundary arc containing $x/2$ from infinity. [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) comparison with the half-disc bounds the length of that arc's mapped interval, $g_t(x/2)-\xi_t$, by a universal constant times $r$. This centered boundary image is a positive [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension $1+4/\kappa>2$ and has a strictly positive all-time minimum. Hence the full trace stays a positive distance from each fixed nonzero real point. Reflection gives the negative-side version.
