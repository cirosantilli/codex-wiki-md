# Endpoint approximation for a rigid-backed shear mode

↑ **Parent:** [Singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md)

In the [rigid-backed shear-wave dispersion](rigid-backed-shear-wave-dispersion.md) equation $\tan\theta=-\epsilon\theta/\sqrt{A^2-\theta^2}$, take fixed $(n-1/2)\pi<A<n\pi$ and let $\epsilon\to0^+$. The highest intersection approaches the endpoint $A$. Setting $\delta=A-\theta$ and using $A^2-\theta^2\sim2A\delta$ gives the displayed [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion-split.md). At $A=n\pi$ the appropriate balance is instead $\delta\sim(\epsilon^2n\pi/2)^{1/3}$. Near that transition, with $\Delta=A-n\pi$, the leading equation is $\delta^2(\Delta+\delta)=\epsilon^2n\pi/2$, where now $\theta=n\pi-\delta$ and $\Delta+\delta>0$.
