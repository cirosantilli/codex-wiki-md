# Transmission conditions for a delta coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Point interaction in a second-order differential equation](point-interaction-in-a-second-order-differential-equation.md)

For a [point interaction in a second-order differential equation](point-interaction-in-a-second-order-differential-equation.md), the equation $y''+\alpha y(c)\delta_c=f+J\delta_c$, with nonsingular continuous $f$, requires

$$
[y]_c=0,\qquad [y']_c+\alpha y(c)=J.
$$

The [distributional second derivative across a jump](distributional-second-derivative-across-a-jump.md) explains both conditions: there is no derivative-of-delta source to balance a value jump, and the coefficients of the delta source must agree. In particular, without a point source, $[y']_c=-\alpha y(c)$. With a unit point source at the same location, the condition is $[y']_c=1-\alpha y(c)$; one must combine the two contributions rather than impose two incompatible jump conditions.
