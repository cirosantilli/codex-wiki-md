# Albert-Brauer-Hasse-Noether theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer group](brauer-group.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, localization of the [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) is injective. Its image is exactly the finite-support tuples of local classes whose [local Brauer invariants](local-brauer-invariant.md) sum to zero. The non-Archimedean summands have invariant [group](group-split.md) $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$, real summands have $\{0,1/2\}$, and complex summands are zero. For a cyclic character $\chi$ and parameter $a$, the invariant of the local [cyclic algebra](cyclic-algebra.md) is $\chi(\operatorname{Art}_{K_v}(a))$. Thus the zero-sum condition on principal parameters is the additive form of [Artin reciprocity](artin-reciprocity-law.md).
