# Zero-framed Hopf-link handle decomposition of the sphere product

↑ **Parent:** [Handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md)

Write each [sphere](sphere.md) as two [disks](disk-mathematics.md), $S^2=D_-^2\cup D_+^2$. In their product, $D_-^2\times D_-^2$ is a [zero-handle](zero-handle.md); $D_+^2\times D_-^2$ and $D_-^2\times D_+^2$ are [two-handles](two-handle.md). Their attaching circles are the core circles of the complementary solid tori in $\partial(D^2\times D^2)$, hence a [Hopf link](hopf-link.md). Constant normal directions from the disk factors give both components the zero [Seifert framing](seifert-framing.md). The remaining $D_+^2\times D_+^2$ is a four-handle. This product construction establishes the actual [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) type, rather than relying on its [intersection form](intersection-form.md) alone. The resulting intersection matrix is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
