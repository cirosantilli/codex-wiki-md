# R-xi gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)

For the Abelian [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) with scalar phase $\chi$ and vector mass $m$, take $\mathcal L_{\rm gf}=-(\partial\cdot A+\xi m\chi)^2/(2\xi)$ when the scalar kinetic mixing is $-mA_\mu\partial^\mu\chi$. The cross term cancels this mixing after integration by parts. The resulting vector [propagator](propagator.md) is

$$
D_{\mu\nu}(k)=\frac{-i}{k^2-m^2+i0}\left(g_{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)\frac{k_\mu k_\nu}{k^2-\xi m^2+i0}\right).
$$

At fixed finite $\xi$ it decays as $1/k^2$. Would-be [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) and the required [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) remain in intermediate calculations; [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md) identifies the physical states and ensures cancellation of unphysical modes. The limit $\xi\to\infty$ gives the [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) propagator and is not uniform in ultraviolet momentum.
