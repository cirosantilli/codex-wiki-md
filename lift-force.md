# Lift (force)

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lift_(force))

Lift is the component of the fluid force on a body perpendicular to its far-field relative [velocity](velocity.md); the parallel component is [drag](drag-physics.md). In a two-dimensional ideal circulatory flow, the [Kutta–Joukowski theorem](kutta-joukowski-theorem.md) gives the lift per unit span from the far-field speed and [circulation](circulation-physics.md).
