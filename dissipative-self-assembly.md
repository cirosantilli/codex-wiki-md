# Dissipative self-assembly

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

Assembly maintained or transiently created by consumption of a [chemical fuel](chemical-fuel.md). A fuel-triggered state can decay as the fuel is irreversibly converted to waste, even though rapid binding equilibria hold within each instantaneous composition.
