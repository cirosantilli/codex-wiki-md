# Shift epimorphism of the natural-number presheaf classifier

↑ **Parent:** [Subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md)

In the covariant [functor category](functor-category.md) $[\mathbb N,\mathbf{Set}]$, the classifier at $n$ consists of thresholds $k\geq n$ and the empty threshold $\infty$. Its transition to $n+1$ sends $k$ to $\max(k,n+1)$. The displayed maps commute with transitions and are all surjective, so define an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md). They identify $n$ and $n+1$, so the endomorphism is not an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
