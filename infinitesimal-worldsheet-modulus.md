# Infinitesimal worldsheet modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet moduli](worldsheet-moduli.md)

After quotienting infinitesimal [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) and [Weyl transformations](weyl-transformation.md), choose a metric variation orthogonal to the gauge directions. Orthogonality to Weyl scaling makes its trace zero; the [formal adjoint of the worldsheet conformal Killing operator](formal-adjoint-of-the-worldsheet-conformal-killing-operator.md) makes its divergence zero. These are transverse-traceless variations of the two-dimensional metric. In a local conformal complex coordinate, their nonzero components satisfy $\partial_{\bar z}t_{zz}=0$ and its conjugate, linking them to [holomorphic quadratic differentials](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md).
