# Supertranslation

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

A [supertranslation](supertranslation.md) is generated by translations and [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md), excluding the [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md). For flat Majorana [superspace](superspace.md) with $\Pi^m=dX^m+i\bar\theta\gamma^m d\theta$, the rigid odd transformation $\delta\theta=\epsilon$, $\delta X^m=-i\bar\epsilon\gamma^m\theta$ leaves $\Pi^m$ invariant. Its commutator gives an ordinary translation.
