# Linkage equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md)

For two [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) with two [alleles](allele.md) each, [linkage equilibrium](linkage-equilibrium.md) is [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of their states on sampled [haplotypes](haplotype.md). It concerns association between [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md); [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](hardy-weinberg-principle.md) concerns pairing of [alleles](allele.md) into [diploid](diploid.md) [genotypes](genotype.md).
