# Holomorphic and canonically normalized superpotential couplings

↑ **Parent:** [Non-renormalization theorem](non-renormalization-theorem.md)

Local Wilsonian [superpotential](superpotential.md) coefficients can have no perturbative vertex corrections while the [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) undergoes [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md). Canonical normalization then converts a quadratic coefficient $m$ and cubic coefficient $g$ to the displayed running coefficients. Non-renormalization of a holomorphic F-term is therefore compatible with running physical masses and interactions. The distinction requires keeping a local [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md) separate from infrared-singular one-particle-irreducible effects and from eliminating entire massive fields.
