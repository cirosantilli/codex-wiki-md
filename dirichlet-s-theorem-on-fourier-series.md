<h1 id="dirichlet-s-theorem-on-fourier-series">Dirichlet's theorem on Fourier series</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

One standard sufficient form states that the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of a piecewise continuously differentiable periodic [function](function-split.md) converges at $x$ to $[f(x-)+f(x+)]/2$. At a continuity point it equals $f(x)$. This modern sufficient form should be distinguished from the exact hypotheses of the 1829 original.[Sur la convergence des séries trigonométriques by Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet](sur-la-convergence-des-series-trigonometriques-qui-servent-a-representer-une-fonction-arbitraire-entre-des-limites-donnees-by-peter-gustav-lejeune-dirichlet.md)
