# Flux summation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolic control analysis](metabolic-control-analysis.md)

Under the scaling assumptions of [metabolic control analysis](metabolic-control-analysis.md), the complete set of independently scaled reaction-capacity [flux control coefficients](flux-control-coefficient.md) for one steady-state flux sums to one. Uniformly scaling all capacities scales the flux, and differentiation with respect to the scale factor gives the sum.
