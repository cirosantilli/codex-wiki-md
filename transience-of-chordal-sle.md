# Transience of chordal SLE

↑ **Parent:** [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

The chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) eventually leaves every bounded subset of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). For $0<\kappa<4$, its time-one [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) is a simple slit and does not contain a half-disc. Instead, the [boundary approach forces a small SLE Bessel gap](boundary-approach-forces-a-small-sle-bessel-gap.md) estimate shows that every fixed nonzero real point is outside the closure of the full [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md). After mapping out the initial simple slit, the starting point has two distinct real [prime end](prime-end.md) images. A fresh independent [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) avoids approaching either image, so the original future stays a positive distance from its starting point. [Scaling invariance of SLE](scaling-invariance-of-sle.md) gives $\inf_{t\geq T}|\gamma(t)|\overset d=\sqrt T\inf_{t\geq1}|\gamma(t)|$, and decreasing-event continuity proves escape from every fixed disc. The separate argument based on a swallowed half-disc at positive time applies in the nonsimple regime $\kappa>4$; it must not be applied to a simple slit. The critical parameters require their own limiting or trace-existence arguments.
