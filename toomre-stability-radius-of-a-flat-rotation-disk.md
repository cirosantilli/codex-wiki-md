# Toomre stability radius of a flat-rotation disk

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md)

For constant circular speed $v_0$, [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) is $v_0/r$ and [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) is $\sqrt2v_0/r$. Constant effective random speed and [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) then give $Q=r_Q/r$. The local axisymmetric fluid model is stable inside $r_Q$ and unstable outside it. This statement is conditional on the thin-disk local approximation, rather than a proof of global or collisionless stability.
