# Triadobatrachus massinoti

↑ **Parent:** [Salientia](salientia.md)

An Early [Triassic](triassic.md) stem [frog](frog.md) from Madagascar with an anteriorly elongated [ilium](ilium.md), a retained short tail and incompletely transformed [limbs](vertebrate-limb.md). CT redescription identified 15 [presacral vertebrae](presacral-vertebra.md) rather than the traditional count of 14.
