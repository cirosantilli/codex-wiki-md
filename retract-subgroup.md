# Retract subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometrically embedded subgroup](quasi-isometrically-embedded-subgroup.md)

A subgroup $H\leq G$ is a retract when there is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $r:G\to H$ whose restriction to $H$ is the identity. If both groups are finitely generated, the inclusion of $H$ is a [quasi-isometric embedding](quasi-isometric-embedding.md): the inclusion and retraction are both Lipschitz in their word metrics.
