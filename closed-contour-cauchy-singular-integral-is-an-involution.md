# Closed-contour Cauchy singular integral is an involution

↑ **Parent:** [Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem](sokhotski-plemelj-theorem.md)

On a positively oriented smooth simple closed contour, set $\mathcal Cf=(\pi i)^{-1}\operatorname{PV}\int_\Gamma f(t)/(t-z)\,dt$. The [Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem](sokhotski-plemelj-theorem.md) gives the sum and difference of the two Cauchy boundary values. Changing the sign of the exterior branch converts the equation $\mathcal Cf=g$ into a normalized additive [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md) with jump $g$. Applying the boundary formula again yields $f=\mathcal Cg$, proving the involution on a suitable smooth or Hölder function class.
