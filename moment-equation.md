# Moment equation

↑ **Parent:** [Moment hierarchy](moment-hierarchy.md)

For a [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md) $L$, differentiating an integrable observable gives $d\mathbb E[f(X_t)]/dt=\mathbb E[Lf(X_t)]$, when the generator identity and interchange are justified. Choosing powers gives equations for [moments](moment.md). These need not form a closed finite system.
