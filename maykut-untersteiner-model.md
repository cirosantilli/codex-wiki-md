# Maykut-Untersteiner model

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice thermodynamics](sea-ice-thermodynamics.md)

The Maykut-Untersteiner model is a seasonally forced one-dimensional [sea ice thermodynamics](sea-ice-thermodynamics.md) model with snow and saline ice layers. It couples radiative and turbulent surface fluxes, [heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md), interior shortwave absorption, brine-dependent effective heat capacity and conductivity, snowfall, and a basal [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) driven by prescribed [ocean heat flux](ocean-heat-flux.md). Its [annual sea-ice thermodynamic equilibrium](annual-sea-ice-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) is a periodic cycle with zero net yearly thickness change. The [snow insulation and summer ice protection](snow-insulation-and-summer-ice-protection.md) mechanisms compete, so additional snow need not reduce annual equilibrium thickness. The transient temperature profile need not be linear.
