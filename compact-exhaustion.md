# Compact exhaustion

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

A compact exhaustion of an open subset $U$ of a finite-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) is an increasing sequence of compact subsets $K_n\subset U$ with $K_n\subset\operatorname{int}K_{n+1}$ and $\bigcup_nK_n=U$. For example, choose smoothed bounds or suitable nested levels of distance from the complement and distance from the origin. Exit times from these sets express the lifetime of a [maximal local solution of a stochastic differential equation](maximal-local-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md); every finite-time continuous path lying in $U$ has compact image and is contained in some $K_n$.
