<h1 id="kahler-laplacian-identity">Kähler Laplacian identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md)

On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), expanding $d=\partial+\bar\partial$ and using the [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md) makes the mixed anticommutators vanish and gives $\Delta_\partial=\Delta_{\bar\partial}$. Therefore

$$
\Delta_d=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}=2\Delta_\partial.
$$
