# Albiez double-well Josephson experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic Josephson junction](bosonic-josephson-junction.md)

Two weakly coupled condensates in a double-well potential realize one [bosonic Josephson junction](bosonic-josephson-junction.md). The 2005 experiment measured the density distribution in situ and inferred the evolving relative phase from [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) fringes. It distinguished tunneling oscillations from nonlinear suppression of large-amplitude transfer, called macroscopic quantum self-trapping.[2005 abstract: apparatus and the two measurement channels](direct-observation-of-tunneling-and-nonlinear-self-trapping-in-a-single-bosonic-josephson-junction-by-michael-albiez-and-others.md)
