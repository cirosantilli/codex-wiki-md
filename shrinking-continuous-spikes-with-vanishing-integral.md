# Shrinking continuous spikes with vanishing integral

↑ **Parent:** [Pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md)

For $n\ge2$, these [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) on $[0,1]$ have maximum one and integral $1/n^2$. Their supports shrink towards zero while their value at zero remains zero. They converge pointwise to zero and their integrals converge to zero, but their convergence is not [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md). Thus convergence of integrals and continuity of the pointwise limit do not imply [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md).
