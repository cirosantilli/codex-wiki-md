# Right-angled Artin group

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-angled_Artin_group)

For a [graph](graph-split.md) $\Gamma$, the right-angled Artin group is

$$
A_\Gamma=\left\langle v\in V(\Gamma)\mathrel{\middle|}[v,w]=1\text{ whenever }\{v,w\}\in E(\Gamma)\right\rangle.
$$
