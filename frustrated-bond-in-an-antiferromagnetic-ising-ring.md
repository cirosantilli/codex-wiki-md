# Frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md)

A neighboring parallel pair in an [antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md). It costs $2J$ relative to a satisfied antiparallel pair. If there are $m$ such pairs, the [energy](energy-split.md) is $-JN+2Jm$, and the [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) force $N-m$ to be even because $\prod_jS_jS_{j+1}=1$. Thus an odd ring has $2N$ [ground states](ground-state.md), obtained by locating one such pair and choosing one overall [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) orientation. The even ring has two [ground states](ground-state.md) with no such pair. This definition uses the local preferred ordering; a parallel pair is a defect in staggered order rather than a wall between uniformly magnetized domains.
