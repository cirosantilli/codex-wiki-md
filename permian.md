# Permian

↑ **Parent:** [Paleozoic](paleozoic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permian)

The final period of the [Paleozoic](paleozoic.md), immediately preceding the [Triassic](triassic.md). A geological age does not by itself specify mechanical strength or [seismic velocity](seismic-velocity.md).
