# Geometric quotient theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric theory](geometric-theory.md)

A geometric quotient adds geometric sequents over the same signature. Quotients are compared modulo provable equivalence. In a syntactic [site](site-category-theory.md) the extra sequents become additional covers, giving a [subtopos](subtopos.md) of the original [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md). Stronger axioms correspond to a larger [site](site-category-theory.md) topology and a smaller [subtopos](subtopos.md).
