# Boltzmann-Grad limit

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

For $N$ hard particles in a fixed macroscopic volume, shrink their diameter $a$ while keeping $Na^{d-1}$ of order one. The occupied volume fraction tends to zero, but the [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) stays macroscopic. Deriving the [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md) in this dilute limit requires control of recollisions and propagation of an incoming independence hypothesis related to [molecular chaos for the Boltzmann equation](molecular-chaos-for-the-boltzmann-equation.md). Its validity is not an automatic all-time consequence of small particle size.
