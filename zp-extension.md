# Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa theory](iwasawa-theory.md)

A Zp-extension is an infinite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of a [number field](number-field.md) whose [Galois group](galois-group.md) is topologically isomorphic to the additive [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md). There is a unique intermediate field of degree $p^n$ for every $n\geq0$, corresponding to $p^n\mathbb Z_p$. The finite layers form a tower with cyclic successive degree $p$ extensions.
