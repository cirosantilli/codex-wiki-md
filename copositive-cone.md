# Copositive cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cone](convex-cone.md)

The real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) that are [copositive matrices](copositive-matrix.md) form a closed [convex cone](convex-cone.md):

$$
\operatorname{COP}_n=\{A:x^TAx\geq0\text{ for every }x\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^n\}.
$$

Each fixed $x$ gives a closed linear inequality in $A$. Their intersection is therefore closed and [convex](convex-function.md), and it is preserved by nonnegative scaling.
