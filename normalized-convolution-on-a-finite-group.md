# Normalized convolution on a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis on a finite group](fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-group.md)

For scalar functions on a [finite group](finite-group.md), [normalized convolution on a finite group](normalized-convolution-on-a-finite-group.md) is $(f*g)(x)=|G|^{-1}\sum_y f(y)g(y^{-1}x)$. It is associative and need not commute. Its identity is $|G|1_{\{e\}}$, rather than the unscaled [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of the identity element. The [Fourier analysis on a finite group](fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-group.md) convention $\widehat f(\rho)=\mathbb E f(x)\rho(x)$ turns it into matrix multiplication in the same order.
