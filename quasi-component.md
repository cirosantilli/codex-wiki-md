# Quasi-component

↑ **Parent:** [Connected component](connected-component.md)

The [quasi-component](quasi-component.md) of a point is the [intersection](set-intersection.md) of its [clopen](clopen-set.md) neighborhoods. Its [connected component](connected-component.md) is always contained in it: a [connected set](connected-set.md) meeting a [clopen](clopen-set.md) set cannot also meet its complement. Equality need not hold in noncompact [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) spaces. The [Šura-Bura lemma](sura-bura-lemma.md) proves equality in [compact Hausdorff spaces](compact-hausdorff-space.md).
