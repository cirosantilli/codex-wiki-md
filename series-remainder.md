# Series remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Partial sum](partial-sum.md)

For a [convergent series](convergent-series.md) with [sum](sum.md) $S$ and $N$-th [partial sum](partial-sum.md) $S_N$, its [series remainder](series-remainder.md) is $R_N=S-S_N$. It equals the [sum](sum.md) of the terms after position $N$. Its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) measures the error of replacing $S$ by $S_N$; it need not be positive when the [series](series-mathematics.md) has signed [sequence terms](sequence-term.md).
