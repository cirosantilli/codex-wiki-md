# Maximum aggregate loss in a classical risk model

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

Starting from capital $u$, ruin occurs exactly when the aggregate loss exceeds $u$. Positive [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) and the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) make $S(t)-ct$ tend to minus infinity, so its supremum is finite almost surely. For [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) claims of rate $\alpha$, $L$ has an atom $1-\lambda/(\alpha c)$ at zero, and, conditional on $L>0$, has [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\alpha-\lambda/c$. The [ultimate ruin probability](ultimate-ruin-probability.md) determines this mixture directly.
