# Whitehead product

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy group](homotopy-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehead_product)

The geometric [Whitehead product](whitehead-product.md) of based maps $\alpha:S^p\to Y$, $\beta:S^q\to Y$ is their [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md) map composed with the attaching map $S^{p+q-1}\to S^p\vee S^q$ of the top cell of $S^p\times S^q$. With the usual geometric signs it satisfies $[\alpha,\beta]_{W}=(-1)^{pq}[\beta,\alpha]_{W}$. Regrading $\pi_pY$ to degree $p-1$ and setting $\{\alpha,\beta\}=(-1)^p[\alpha,\beta]_{W}$ gives the [graded Lie bracket](graded-lie-bracket.md) transported from the [Samelson product](samelson-product.md) on the loop space. In degrees $p,q\ge2$ it is bilinear; fundamental-group inputs instead record [commutators](commutator.md) and the action of $\pi_1$ on higher [homotopy](homotopy.md).
