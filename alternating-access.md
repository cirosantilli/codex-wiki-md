# Alternating access

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)

[alternating access](alternating-access.md) is a transport mechanism in which a substrate-binding region becomes accessible to opposite sides of a membrane in different states. A continuously open pore is avoided; coupling the state sequence to a driving process permits [active transport](active-transport.md).
