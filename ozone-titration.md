# Ozone titration

↑ **Parent:** [Tropospheric ozone](tropospheric-ozone.md)

Local [ozone](ozone.md) consumption by the reaction $\mathrm{NO+O_3\to NO_2+O_2}$. Reducing fresh NO emissions can raise local measured [ozone](ozone.md) even while decreasing regional [ozone](ozone.md) production. This distinction matters when interpreting precursor-control experiments and observations.
