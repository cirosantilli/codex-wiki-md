# Soliton order

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal optical soliton](temporal-optical-soliton.md)

A dimensionless comparison of dispersion and [self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md) scales. For the focusing cubic fibre equation with a sech input, $N_s=1$ gives a [fundamental temporal bright soliton](fundamental-temporal-bright-soliton.md), and integer $N_s>1$ gives an ideal [higher-order temporal soliton](higher-order-temporal-soliton.md). It is a scale parameter for an arbitrary initial pulse, not a theorem that the pulse contains exactly that many separated [solitons](soliton.md).
