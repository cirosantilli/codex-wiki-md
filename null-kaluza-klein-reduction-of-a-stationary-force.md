# Null Kaluza-Klein reduction of a stationary force

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenhart-Duval lift](eisenhart-duval-lift.md)

For an [Eisenhart-Duval lift](eisenhart-duval-lift.md), reduction along the null isometry fixes a nonzero conserved $p_u=t\prime$ and projects [geodesics](geodesic.md) to $(t,\mathbf x)$. One never divides by $g_{uu}=0$. When $\mathbf E=-\nabla U$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf A=\mathbf B$, the reduced equation is $\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf E+2\mathbf B\times\dot{\mathbf x}$. Every trajectory admits a null lift by choosing $\dot u=U+2\mathbf A\cdot\dot{\mathbf x}-\tfrac12|\dot{\mathbf x}|^2$.
