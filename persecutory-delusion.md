# Persecutory delusion

↑ **Parent:** [Delusion](delusion.md)

A [belief](belief.md) that others intend harm, assessed as a [delusion](delusion.md) in its clinical and contextual setting. Psychological formulations examine processes such as [worry](worry.md), felt vulnerability and [safety behaviors](safety-behavior.md) without assuming a single cause of every presentation.
