# Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Laser spectroscopy](laser-spectroscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturated_absorption_spectroscopy)

[Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md) pairs a strong pump with an opposed weak probe. Shared populations produce a narrow [Lamb dip](lamb-dip.md) within the [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md) of a [spectral line](spectral-line.md).
