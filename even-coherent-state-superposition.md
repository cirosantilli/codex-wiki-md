# Even coherent-state superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger cat state](schrodinger-cat-state.md)

The normalized sum $N_+(|\alpha\rangle+|-\alpha\rangle)$ contains only even [photon numbers](photon-number.md), since the two coherent-state expansions cancel every odd term. It tends to vacuum at small amplitude. With real $\alpha$, its second quadrature can be squeezed below the vacuum [variance](variance-split.md).
