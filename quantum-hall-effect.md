# Quantum Hall effect

↑ **Parent:** [Hall effect](hall-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Hall_effect)

The [quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md) connects an electrical measurement to a quantum counting rule: a [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) in a perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can exhibit plateaus of transverse [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). The integer case supplies a resistance scale built from the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), rather than the sample's dimensions. The [fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md) involves additional many-[electron](electron.md) physics.
