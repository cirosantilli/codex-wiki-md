# Character expansion of the identity delta

↑ **Parent:** [Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md), the [regular representation](regular-representation.md) contains $d_\rho$ copies of each [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) $\rho$. Its [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) is $|G|$ at the identity and zero elsewhere. Thus $\sum_\rho d_\rho\chi_\rho(x)=|G|1_{\{x=e\}}$, including the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). This formula converts independent uniform [expectations](expected-value.md) into constrained configuration counts.
