# Order dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_dimension)

The order dimension of a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) $(X,<)$ is the least cardinality of a family of [total orders](total-order.md) on $X$ whose intersection is $<$. Such a family is called a realizer of the partial order.
