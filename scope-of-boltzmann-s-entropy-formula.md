<h1 id="scope-of-boltzmann-s-entropy-formula">Scope of Boltzmann's entropy formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann's entropy formula](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md)

The strings in [Boltzmann entropy of a two-box occupancy](boltzmann-entropy-of-a-two-box-occupancy.md) are complete [microstates](microstate.md) only within the binary toy model. They retain a [coarse-grained variable](coarse-grained-variable.md) for each particle and omit its exact [position](position.md) within a box and its [momentum](momentum.md). An actual classical [gas](gas.md) instead occupies continuous [phase space](phase-space.md); obtaining a dimensionless count from a [phase space](phase-space.md) volume requires a specified cell convention and the appropriate treatment of particle identity. A [quantum state](quantum-state.md) count must respect the allowed [quantum states](quantum-state.md) and [quantum statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md). Thus $2^N$ is not a general count of [microstates](microstate.md) for a [gas](gas.md) obeying [quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md). [Kardar, MIT 8.333 (2013), lecture 13, §§IV.D–E: gas phase space and particle counting](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/0cdb670a825c6aaea7e229ac40776cbf_MIT8_333F13_Lec13.pdf)

For a discrete [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) with unequal weights, use the existing [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md) article; its equal-weight case gives [Boltzmann's entropy formula](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md). [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) uses the corresponding information measure, with the logarithm base setting its units. A count alone cannot replace the specified [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md), as the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) examples demonstrate. [MIT, §7.3: statistical entropy for general microstate probabilities](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node56.html)

Overwhelming multiplicity explains why [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) is overwhelmingly probable in macroscopic systems under the appropriate statistical assumptions. It does not make low-[entropy](entropy.md) [thermal fluctuations](thermal-fluctuation.md) logically impossible. Nor does counting alone derive irreversible macroscopic evolution: one also needs dynamics, an initial preparation, and a reason the system samples the allowed [microstates](microstate.md) on the timescale of interest. [Kardar, lecture 12, §§IV.A–B: statistical equilibrium and the limits of counting arguments](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/0cb99318c2ed889316cdcff839759e8d_MIT8_333F13_Lec12.pdf)

Historically, Boltzmann's 1877 paper counted microscopic arrangements, called complexions, and related the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of their number to [entropy](entropy.md). The Sharp and Matschinsky translation appeared in _Entropy_ 17 (2015), pages 1971–2009, DOI 10.3390/e17041971. Its notation differs from ours: Boltzmann's permutability measure $\Omega$ is already logarithmic, whereas here $\Omega$ is the count. [Boltzmann (1877), translated by Kim Sharp and Franz Matschinsky (2015), §III after equation 34 and §V](https://gilles.montambaux.com/files/histoire-physique/Boltzmann-1877-entropy.pdf) [Josiah Willard Gibbs](josiah-willard-gibbs.md)'s _Elementary Principles in Statistical Mechanics_ (1902) developed the [statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md) framework; Chapter XIV compares several statistical definitions of [entropy](entropy.md) with [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md). [Gibbs (1902), title page and preface](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Elementary_Principles_in_Statistical_Mechanics) [Gibbs (1902), Chapter XIV: discussion of thermodynamic analogies](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Elementary_Principles_in_Statistical_Mechanics/Chapter_XIV)
