# Euler criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre symbol](legendre-symbol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_criterion)

For an odd prime $p$,

$$
a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p.
$$

For $p\nmid a$, write $a=g^m$ using a [primitive root](primitive-root-modulo-n.md) $g$; then the left side is $(-1)^m$, which is one exactly when $m$ is even and $a$ is a square.
