# Preference relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)

A weak [preference relation](preference-relation.md) records that one alternative is at least as desirable as another. Rational preferences are complete and [transitive](transitive-relation.md). Their [strict preference](strict-preference.md) and [indifference relation](indifference-relation.md) are the asymmetric and symmetric parts. On a consumption space, continuity is expressed by a [closed set](closed-set.md) of weakly ranked pairs.
