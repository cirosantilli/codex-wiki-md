# Critical layer in a shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

A critical layer is a region where the leading wave phase speed coincides with the local shear-flow [velocity](velocity.md) and an outer approximation degenerates. Small viscosity or other corrections must then be retained on a distinguished transverse scale. The width depends on the order of vanishing of $U-c_0$ and on the governing mode equation; simple-shear and stationary-profile layers have different balances.
