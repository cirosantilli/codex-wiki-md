# Enzyme inhibitor

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzyme_inhibitor)

An [enzyme inhibitor](enzyme-inhibitor.md) lowers an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)'s [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md). In [reversible enzyme inhibition](reversible-enzyme-inhibition.md), reducing free inhibitor [concentration](concentration.md) can restore activity; slow release can make this difficult to distinguish experimentally from irreversible inactivation. [Competitive inhibition](competitive-inhibition.md) and [mixed inhibition](mixed-inhibition.md) describe different changes in [enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md); [pure noncompetitive inhibition](pure-noncompetitive-inhibition.md) is a special case of [mixed inhibition](mixed-inhibition.md), not a name for every inhibitor that binds away from an [active site](active-site.md).[Symbolism and Terminology in Enzyme Kinetics, recommendation 1981, sections 6.1 and 6.4: reversibility and the ambiguity of non-competitive](symbolism-and-terminology-in-enzyme-kinetics-recommendation-1981-by-nomenclature-committee-of-the-international-union-of-biochemistry.md)

The useful question is how adding [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) changes the inhibition. The model below connects binding to a measurable [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md), predicts when more [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) can overcome inhibition, and supplies examples for comparison with an [enzyme inhibition initial-rate assay](enzyme-inhibition-initial-rate-assay.md). Its [inhibition constants](inhibition-constant.md) are explicitly defined; neither a binding cartoon nor one reduced [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) by itself determines a mechanism.
