# Earth crust

↑ **Parent:** [Earth](earth.md)

The outer compositional shell above the mantle. Crustal thickness and [density](density.md) variations contribute to [surface topography](surface-topography.md) and [gravity](gravity.md); crust and mechanically defined [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) are different boundaries.
