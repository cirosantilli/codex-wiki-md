# Quartic gradient monotonicity inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Strictly monotone operator](strictly-monotone-operator.md)

For real [vectors](vector.md) $\xi,\eta$, put $m=(\xi+\eta)/2$ and $d=\xi-\eta$. Expansion gives

$$
(|\xi|^2\xi-|\eta|^2\eta)\cdot(\xi-\eta)
=|m|^2|d|^2+2(m\cdot d)^2+\frac14|d|^4
\geq\frac14|d|^4.
$$

This proves strict [operator monotonicity](operator-monotonicity.md) of the [gradient](gradient.md) of $\xi\mapsto|\xi|^4/4$. Integrating the inequality controls the difference of two [weak gradients](weak-gradient.md) in the fourth power of their [Lp norm](lp-norm.md), which yields uniqueness for suitable nonlinear [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
