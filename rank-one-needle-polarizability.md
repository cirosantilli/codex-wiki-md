# Rank-one needle polarizability

↑ **Parent:** [Polarizability tensor](polarizability-tensor.md)

A slender high-permittivity particle responds mainly along its axis $\mathbf u$, giving $\boldsymbol\alpha\simeq\alpha_\parallel\mathbf u\mathbf u^{\mathsf T}$. Far-field projection into an observation plane leaves one real [polarization](polarization-waves.md) direction. Every nonzero output is therefore linearly polarized in this leading approximation, even for circular or unpolarized input.
