# Bell-clapper loss of contact

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

Let a bell have axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $Mk^2$ and [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) h from its axle. A clapper of [point mass](point-mass.md) m at radius l touches the bell at fixed relative angle $\beta$. While they remain in contact, the clapper angle $\theta$ from the upward vertical obeys $(Mk^2+ml^2)\ddot\theta=Mgh\sin(\theta-\beta)+mgl\sin\theta$. The contact [force](force-split.md) opposing increasing angle is $N=mg\sin\theta-ml\ddot\theta$. Eliminating the [acceleration](acceleration.md) gives

$$
N=\frac{mMg}{Mk^2+ml^2}[k^2\sin\theta-lh\sin(\theta-\beta)].
$$

The first zero on the physical trajectory marks detachment, because a nonadhesive contact cannot support negative [normal force](normal-force.md). Selecting the correct trigonometric branch is part of the contact problem.
