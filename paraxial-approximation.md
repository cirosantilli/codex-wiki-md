# Paraxial approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraxial_approximation)

The paraxial approximation describes waves whose propagation directions make small angles with a preferred axis. Factoring out a carrier $e^{ikx}$ and neglecting the envelope's second longitudinal derivative turns the [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) into a [parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md) and suppresses backward propagation.
