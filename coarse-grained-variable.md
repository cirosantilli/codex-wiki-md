# Coarse-grained variable

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

A coarse-grained variable retains selected macroscopic information while averaging over unresolved microscopic states. At [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md), the statistical multiplicity of those states contributes [entropy](entropy.md) to the effective [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) of the retained variable.
