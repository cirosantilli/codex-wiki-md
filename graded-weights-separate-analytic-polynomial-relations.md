# Graded weights separate analytic polynomial relations

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring of level-one modular forms](polynomial-ring-of-level-one-modular-forms.md)

Let $P_w(E_4,E_6)$ be weighted homogeneous of [modular weight](weight-of-a-modular-form.md) $w$, and suppose $\sum_wP_w(z)=0$ as a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). Apply the [modular group](modular-group.md) [matrices](matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\n&1\end{pmatrix}$. At a fixed point $z$ in the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), this gives $\sum_w(nz+1)^wP_w(z)=0$ for every integer $n$. A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with infinitely many roots vanishes identically, so every $P_w(z)=0$. Within one [modular weight](weight-of-a-modular-form.md), the monomials reduce to powers of $E_4^3/E_6^2$, after taking out a common factor. This ratio is nonconstant, as its [Fourier expansion](fourier-series-split.md) begins $1+1728q+\cdots$, so its powers are linearly independent. This proves [algebraic independence](algebraic-independence.md) even when the generators are regarded as analytic functions rather than elements of a formal [graded ring](graded-ring.md).
