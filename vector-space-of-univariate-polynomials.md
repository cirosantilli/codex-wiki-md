# Vector space of univariate polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The real univariate polynomials form a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with the countable [Hamel basis](basis.md)

$$
1,X,X^2,\ldots.
$$

The subspace of polynomials of degree at most $n$ has dimension $n+1$.
