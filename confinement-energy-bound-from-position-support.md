# Confinement energy bound from position support

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md)

Suppose each Cartesian coordinate of a particle of mass $m$ is supported in an interval of length $L$, in $d$ dimensions. [Popoviciu's inequality on variances](popoviciu-s-inequality-on-variances.md) gives $\Delta x_i\leq L/2$, and the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) gives $\Delta p_i\geq\hbar/L$. Since $\langle p_i^2\rangle=(\Delta p_i)^2+\langle p_i\rangle^2$,

$$
\boxed{\langle K\rangle\geq\frac{d\hbar^2}{2mL^2}.}
$$

This is a rigorous but non-sharp confinement estimate. For a cubical [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md), separation of variables gives the stronger exact ground-state value $d\pi^2\hbar^2/(2mL^2)$. One must not identify a support length with a uniquely prescribed [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) or claim that the uncertainty estimate is the exact ground-state energy.
