# Nilradical of a commutative Artinian ring

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md)

The [nilradical](nilradical.md) $N$ of a commutative [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md) is a [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md). Its powers stabilize, say $N^r=N^{r+1}=I$. If $I\ne0$, choose an ideal $J$ minimal subject to $IJ\ne0$, and then $x\in J$ with $Ix\ne0$. Minimality and $I^2=I$ give $Rx=Ix$, so $x=ax$ for some $a\in I$. Since $a$ is nilpotent, $1-a$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md), contradicting $(1-a)x=0$ and $x\ne0$.
