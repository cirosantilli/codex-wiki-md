# Best linear prediction from an infinite past

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

The best mean-square linear predictor from a semi-infinite past is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the closed linear span of past observations in the random-variable [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). For a causal invertible [ARMA](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) representation, past observations and past innovations generate the same closed span. The next [linear innovation](linear-innovation-process.md) is orthogonal to it and is therefore the prediction error.
