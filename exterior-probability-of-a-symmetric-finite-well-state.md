# Exterior probability of a symmetric finite-well state

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

If a normalized [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) has exterior tails $Ce^{-q|x|}$ outside $|x|<a/2$, integration over both tails gives the title [probability](probability.md). For an even [cosine](cosine.md) interior $A\cos(kx)$, the remaining normalization contribution is $A^2[a/2+\sin(ka)/(2k)]$. Tail [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) defined at the edge rather than at the origin absorb the factor $e^{-qa/2}$, so their [probability](probability.md) formula looks different despite describing the same state.
