# Quaternionic left-invariant frame on the three-sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Parallelizable sphere](parallelizable-sphere.md)

For $q=(q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)\in S^3$, the vectors $(-q_1,q_0,q_3,-q_2)$, $(-q_2,-q_3,q_0,q_1)$ and $(-q_3,q_2,-q_1,q_0)$ are tangent and orthonormal. Under [SU(2) as the three-sphere](su-2-as-the-three-sphere.md) they are the [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md) obtained by translating $-i$ times the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). This explicitly realizes the [parallelization of a Lie group by left translations](parallelization-of-a-lie-group-by-left-translations.md).
