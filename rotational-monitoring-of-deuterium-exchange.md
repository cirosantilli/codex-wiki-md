# Rotational monitoring of deuterium exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform microwave spectroscopy](fourier-transform-microwave-spectroscopy.md)

A distinct experiment in [An Automated, Highly Selective Reaction Monitoring Instrument Using Molecular Rotational Resonance Spectroscopy by Ann Adele Byars and others](an-automated-highly-selective-reaction-monitoring-instrument-using-molecular-rotational-resonance-spectroscopy-by-ann-adele-byars-and-others.md) (Figure 1 and Figure 3) tracked exchange of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) for [deuterium](deuterium.md) in [ethyl acetoacetate](ethyl-acetoacetate.md) mixed with deuterated [methanol](methanol.md). A pump circulated [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) through a valve; a calibrated loop injected aliquots into a temperature-controlled inlet, held at room [temperature](temperature.md) for this exchange experiment. [Neon](neon.md) carried the vaporized sample to a cavity [spectrometer](spectrometer.md). Separately recorded broadband spectra and fitted rotational [Hamiltonians](hamiltonian.md) identified transitions for the four isotopic forms before targeted monitoring.[apparatus and assignment paragraphs around Figure 1; deuterium-exchange method and Figure 3](an-automated-highly-selective-reaction-monitoring-instrument-using-molecular-rotational-resonance-spectroscopy-by-ann-adele-byars-and-others.md)

The readout was each assigned line's signal over reaction time, normalized by the sum of the four signals. Transitions with the same [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md) reduced response-factor differences. A solvent wash addressed observed sample carryover. The signals distinguished the unsubstituted, two singly substituted, and doubly substituted forms. Only one gas-phase structural form was detected; the authors explicitly caution that their readout does not recover the structural [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) in [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md).[deuterium-exchange paragraphs surrounding Figure 3; sample carryover and wash paragraph](an-automated-highly-selective-reaction-monitoring-instrument-using-molecular-rotational-resonance-spectroscopy-by-ann-adele-byars-and-others.md)

This example uses nonlinear [molecules](molecule.md), so neither the single-constant [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) nor the synthetic comb above is a fit to its spectrum. Its connection to the model is the sensitivity of [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) to [isotope](isotope.md) placement. Line position helps assign a species; signal size additionally depends on sample transfer, initial [quantum state](quantum-state.md) populations and instrument response. Matching [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md) is a useful control, not a proof of identical response factors. These distinctions explain why resolving species and determining their [concentrations](concentration.md) are separate inference steps.
