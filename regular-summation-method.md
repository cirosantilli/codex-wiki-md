# Regular summation method

↑ **Parent:** [Summation method](summation-method.md)

A [summation method](summation-method.md) that includes every ordinarily convergent [series](series-mathematics.md) and assigns it its ordinary sum. The [Cesàro theorem for convergent sequences](cesaro-theorem-for-convergent-sequences.md) makes first-order [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md) regular. Regularity alone does not justify every rearrangement or multiplication of a [divergent series](divergent-series.md).
