# Real affine plane scheme points

↑ **Parent:** [Affine plane](affine-plane.md)

The points of the real [affine plane](affine-plane.md) as a [scheme](scheme.md) are its [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md): the zero ideal, the principal ideals of irreducible real polynomials, and its [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md). The latter have [residue field](residue-field.md) $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, by the [Zariski lemma](zariski-s-lemma.md) and the [real closed field](real-closed-field.md) property. They correspond respectively to real coordinate pairs and conjugate pairs of nonreal complex coordinate pairs. Thus the scheme contains both nonclosed [generic points](generic-point.md) and closed points absent from the real locus. Its topology is the [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md), and its [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) has sections $\mathbb R[x,y]_h$ on each [principal open subscheme](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(h)$.
