# Imperfect information

↑ **Parent:** [Information set](information-set.md)

In an [extensive-form game](extensive-form-game.md), a player has imperfect information when some decision [information set](information-set.md) contains more than one node. The player cannot distinguish those histories at the time of acting. This does not itself imply failure of [perfect recall](perfect-recall.md): a player can remember all their own earlier information and actions while never having observed an opponent's hidden choice. Conversely, merging histories in which the player's own earlier actions differ violates [perfect recall](perfect-recall.md).
