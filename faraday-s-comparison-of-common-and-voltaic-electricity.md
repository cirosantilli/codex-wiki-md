<h1 id="faraday-s-comparison-of-common-and-voltaic-electricity">Faraday's comparison of common and voltaic electricity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Frictional electricity](frictional-electricity.md)

In 1833 [Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md) compared electrical effects from different sources and then their quantitative delivery. His galvanometer test discharged a machine-charged jar battery through a wet thread and the detector, connecting frictionally produced charge to magnetic action.[Third Series, paragraphs 296 and 361–378](experimental-researches-in-electricity-volume-1-by-michael-faraday.md)
