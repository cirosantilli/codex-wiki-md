# Random constant Gaussian interest-rate mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md)

Average constant-rate [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) prices of a fixed terminal payoff over $\rho\sim N(\rho_0,\tau^2)$. Tilting this Gaussian by the discount factor shifts its mean by $-\tau^2T$; combining the resulting log-price variance with stock variance gives the displayed effective parameters. Thus the average equals the Black-Scholes price with those parameters, whenever the expectation is well defined. This is an average of constant-parameter prices, rather than a specification of an adapted stochastic short-rate market.
