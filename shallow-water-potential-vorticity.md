# Shallow-water potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md)

Shallow-water potential vorticity is materially conserved by inviscid, unforced [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md). Linearizing about rest with depth $H_0$ gives an anomaly proportional to

$$
\zeta-\frac{f\eta}{H_0}.
$$
