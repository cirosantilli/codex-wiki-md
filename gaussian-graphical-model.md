# Gaussian graphical model

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional independence graph](conditional-independence-graph.md)

For a nondegenerate [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), the [conditional independence graph](conditional-independence-graph.md) is read from zeros in its [precision matrix](precision-matrix.md). Conditional independence of two components given all others is equivalent to a zero cross term in their conditional Gaussian quadratic density, hence to $\Omega_{jk}=0$. It does not generally correspond to a zero in the [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md).
