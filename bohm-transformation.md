<h1 id="bohm-transformation">Böhm transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Böhm tree](bohm-tree.md)

A Böhm transformation is a finite composition of [capture-avoiding substitutions](capture-avoiding-substitution.md) and [lambda applications](lambda-application.md) to chosen auxiliary terms. It can extract a finite-address subtree of a [Böhm tree](bohm-tree.md) as a substitution instance of the corresponding argument term.
