# Transient protein-count variance in the instantaneous-burst model

↑ **Parent:** [Translational burst size](translational-burst-size.md)

For independent [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md) arrivals of geometric translational bursts with mean $b$, arrival rate $\lambda$, and unit protein removal rate $\alpha N$, the stationary mean is $\bar N=\lambda b/\alpha$. Starting with zero molecules, the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is $m(t)=\bar N(1-e^{-\alpha t})$. The [variance](variance-split.md) satisfies $v'=\lambda b(1+2b)+\alpha m-2\alpha v$. An integrating factor gives the displayed expression, and its stationary limit is $\bar N(1+b)$. Concentration fluctuations require a specified cell volume to convert concentrations into molecule counts.
