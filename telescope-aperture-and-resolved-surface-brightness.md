# Telescope aperture and resolved surface brightness

↑ **Parent:** [Surface brightness](surface-brightness.md)

For a diffraction-limited [telescope](telescope.md), collecting area scales as $D^2$ while a native [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md) element scales as $(\lambda/D)^2$. Counts from a smooth resolved source per native resolution element therefore do not increase with aperture, at fixed radiance, exposure and throughput. Counts in a fixed angular bin instead grow as $D^2$, and the photon-noise-limited [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) grows as $D\sqrt{t}$. [Conservation of etendue](conservation-of-etendue.md) and [cosmological surface-brightness dimming](cosmological-surface-brightness-dimming.md) do not forbid this latter gain.
