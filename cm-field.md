# CM-field

↑ **Parent:** [Totally imaginary number field](totally-imaginary-number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CM-field)

A CM-field is a [totally imaginary number field](totally-imaginary-number-field.md) that is a quadratic extension of a [totally real number field](totally-real-number-field.md). Its nontrivial automorphism over that real subfield acts as [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) in every complex embedding. Imaginary [quadratic number fields](quadratic-field.md) are examples. An arbitrary [totally imaginary number field](totally-imaginary-number-field.md) need not have such a real subfield of index two.
