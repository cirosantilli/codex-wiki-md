# Rational torsion of a congruent number curve

↑ **Parent:** [Congruent number elliptic curve](congruent-number-elliptic-curve.md)

For nonzero integral $D$, the [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) $E_D:y^2=x^3-D^2x$ has rational torsion precisely $O,(0,0),(D,0),(-D,0)$. At every good [prime](prime-number.md) $p\equiv3\pmod4$, cancellation of the [Legendre symbols](legendre-symbol.md) at $x$ and $-x$ gives $\#E_D(\mathbb F_p)=p+1$. The [torsion-freeness of the formal group over Qp for odd p](torsion-freeness-of-the-formal-group-over-qp-for-odd-p.md) makes the rational [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md) inject into this group. The [Dirichlet theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-arithmetic-progressions.md) excludes odd factors of its order and bounds its two-primary part by four. The four displayed rational [2-torsion](2-torsion.md) points then give equality.
