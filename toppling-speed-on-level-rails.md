# Toppling speed on level rails

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

A vehicle with [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) midway between level rails separated by d, at height h, follows a turn of [radius](radius.md) R. Vertical [force](force-split.md) balance and cross-sectional [moment balance](moment-balance.md) give inner rail reaction $mg/2-mv^2h/(Rd)$. Its first zero occurs at the displayed speed. This is a toppling criterion under maintained lateral contact, not a guarantee against sliding or suspension instability.
