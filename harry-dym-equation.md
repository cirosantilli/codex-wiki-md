# Harry Dym equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable partial differential equation](integrable-partial-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Dym_equation)

The Harry Dym equation is the [integrable partial differential equation](integrable-partial-differential-equation.md) $p_t=p^3p_{xxx}$. It admits a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md) based on the second-order operator $L=-\partial_xp^2\partial_x$.
