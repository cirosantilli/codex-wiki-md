# Outgoing analytic branch of Klein-Gordon step momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Klein paradox](klein-paradox.md)

Set $z=E-V_0+i0$ and continue both roots from the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The right-hand wavenumber is positive above the positive-energy threshold, positive imaginary inside the gap, and negative below the negative-energy threshold. Its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) in the last region remains rightward because both [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) are negative. Choosing a positive real root there instead specifies an incoming wavepacket from the right.
