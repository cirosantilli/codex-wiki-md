# JK flip-flop

↑ **Parent:** [Flip-flop](flip-flop.md)

At the active [clock edge](clock-edge.md), the next state is $Q^+=J\neg Q\lor\neg K Q$: inputs $00$ hold, $01$ reset, $10$ set, and $11$ toggle. In particular, $K=0$ gives $Q^+=J\lor Q$, whereas $J=K=1$ gives $Q^+=\neg Q$. The standard function table is illustrated by a positive-edge device in the [Section 8.2, p. 9](cd4027b-cmos-dual-j-k-flip-flop-datasheet-by-texas-instruments.md). Its asynchronous reset is active high; another circuit may specify an active-low clear.
