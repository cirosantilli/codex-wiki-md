# Skyscraper sheaf of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf (mathematics)](sheaf-mathematics.md)

For inclusion of a point $i:\{x\}\to X$, this is the direct-image sheaf of a set. It is [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to the [stalk functor](stalk-functor-for-presheaves-of-sets.md). It uses the singleton terminal set on opens omitting $x$, rather than the zero [group](group-split.md) used for abelian-group skyscraper sheaves.
