# Backbone hydrogen bond

↑ **Parent:** [Protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md)

A [backbone hydrogen bond](backbone-hydrogen-bond.md) is a [hydrogen bond](hydrogen-bond.md) involving groups of the [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md), typically a backbone [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) and a [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) already joined by a [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md) to backbone [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). It can connect positions that are not adjacent along the [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md). The [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md) still determine which [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) are consecutive. See the original structural argument cited under [alpha helix](alpha-helix.md).
