# Mean-lifetime survival energy estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic-ray muon time-dilation experiment](cosmic-ray-muon-time-dilation-experiment.md)

An unstable particle of rest lifetime $\tau_0$ traversing distance $H$ at constant [speed](speed.md) $v$ has [survival probability](survival-probability.md) $p=\exp[-H/(\gamma v\tau_0)]$. Requiring a flight time no greater than one dilated [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) selects $p\ge e^{-1}$. In the ultrarelativistic approximation $v\simeq c$, this gives $\gamma\gtrsim H/(c\tau_0)$ and $E\gtrsim mc^2H/(c\tau_0)$. It is a specified survival-probability criterion, not a sharp cutoff for individual random decays. Continuous [energy](energy-split.md) loss requires integrating the decay probability along the path.
