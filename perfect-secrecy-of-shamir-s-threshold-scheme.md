<h1 id="perfect-secrecy-of-shamir-s-threshold-scheme">Perfect secrecy of Shamir's threshold scheme</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Shamir's secret sharing](shamir-s-secret-sharing.md)

Conditioned on any $r-1$ shares in [Shamir's secret sharing](shamir-s-secret-sharing.md), each candidate secret in $\mathbb F_p$ remains equally likely. Appending $(0,N)$ to those shares determines exactly one degree-at-most-$(r-1)$ polynomial for every candidate $N$, by the nonzero [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md).
