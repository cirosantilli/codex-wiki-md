# Chirality (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chirality_(physics))

Chirality distinguishes the two irreducible Weyl representations of the proper Lorentz group. For a massless particle it agrees with helicity up to the particle-antiparticle convention, while a mass term couples opposite chiralities.

The [chiral projectors](chiral-projector.md) $P_{L,R}=(1\mp\gamma^5)/2$ separate the components of a [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md). The [weak charged current](charged-current.md) selects one chirality. A massive fixed-[helicity](helicity.md) state generally contains both chiral components; the simple helicity-chirality identification requires the massless limit and a specified particle-antiparticle convention.
