<h1 id="littlewood-richardson-rule">Littlewood–Richardson rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Specht filtration](specht-filtration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littlewood–Richardson_rule)

For induction of $S^\mu\boxtimes S^\lambda$ to the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) on $|\mu|+|\lambda|$ letters, the [Specht filtration](specht-filtration.md) multiplicity of $S^\nu$ is the number of [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md) of [skew shape](skew-young-diagram.md) $\nu/\mu$, content $\lambda$, and [lattice word](lattice-word.md) read from right to left in each row, taking rows from top to bottom. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) zero these are [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) constituents; in positive [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) they remain Specht factors rather than simple constituents. Content $(1)$ gives the add-one-cell induction rule.
