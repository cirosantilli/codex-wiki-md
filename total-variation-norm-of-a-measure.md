# Total variation norm of a measure

↑ **Parent:** [Variation measure](variation-measure.md)

The total variation norm of a finite signed or complex [measure](measure.md) is the total mass of its [variation measure](variation-measure.md). Under the [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md), it equals the norm of the corresponding member of $C(K)^*$.
