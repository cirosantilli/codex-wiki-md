# Global projected virial estimate of a mass-to-light ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Projected spherical Jeans residual](projected-spherical-jeans-residual.md)

For an isolated spherical stellar system in equilibrium with constant [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md), the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $2K+W=0$. Spherical averaging gives $K=3\pi\Upsilon\int R\mu\sigma_p^2\,dR$, while $W=-4\pi G\Upsilon^2\int r\lambda L\,dr$. Dividing yields the displayed estimator, independent of the radial [velocity-anisotropy parameter](velocity-anisotropy-parameter.md). It requires the full aperture and negligible boundary stress. A radially varying [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) or an independent [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) prevents interpreting this expression as a general reconstruction of the mass profile.
