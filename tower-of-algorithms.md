# Tower of algorithms

↑ **Parent:** [Solvability complexity index](solvability-complexity-index.md)

A tower of algorithms of height $k$ is a family $\Gamma_{n_k,\ldots,n_1}$ of finite-information algorithms such that

$$
\Xi(A)=\lim_{n_k\to\infty}\cdots\lim_{n_1\to\infty}\Gamma_{n_k,\ldots,n_1}(A)
$$

for every input $A$. The [Solvability complexity index](solvability-complexity-index.md) is the least possible height.
