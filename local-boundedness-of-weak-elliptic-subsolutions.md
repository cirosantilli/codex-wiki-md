# Local boundedness of weak elliptic subsolutions

↑ **Parent:** [Weak subsolution of a divergence-form elliptic equation](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md)

For a [uniformly elliptic](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) [divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md) with lower-order coefficients in the supercritical [Lebesgue spaces](lp-space.md), truncation tests and [Moser iteration](moser-iteration.md) bound the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) of a [weak subsolution](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) on an interior ball by its [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) on a larger ball and the forcing norms. In particular, every [weak subsolution](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) of the homogeneous equation is locally bounded above. This is the preliminary estimate needed when a [strong maximum principle](strong-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) is phrased using [essential suprema](essential-supremum.md).
