# Covolume-one theta function of a fractional ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian theta sum](gaussian-theta-sum.md)

Let $C=\sqrt{|D_K|}N(\mathfrak b)$ and $n=[K:\mathbb Q]$. Normalize the [Minkowski embedding of a number field](minkowski-embedding-of-a-number-field.md) to the [covolume](covolume.md)-one [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) $\Lambda_{\mathfrak b}=C^{-1/n}j(\mathfrak b)$, using the metric with complex coordinates weighted by $2$. Its theta function is

$$
\Theta(y,\mathfrak b)=\sum_{a\in\mathfrak b}\exp\left(-\pi C^{-2/n}\sum_v[K_v:\mathbb R]y_v|\sigma_v(a)|^2\right).
$$

Writing $\|y\|=\prod_v y_v^{[K_v:\mathbb R]}$ and using the [trace dual of a fractional ideal](trace-dual-of-a-fractional-ideal.md) gives $\Theta(y,\mathfrak b)=\|y\|^{-1/2}\Theta(y^{-1},\mathfrak b^\vee)$. Its [small-parameter asymptotic of a lattice theta sum](small-parameter-asymptotic-of-a-lattice-theta-sum.md) has leading coefficient one. For the unscaled ideal lattice, the leading coefficient is instead $C^{-1}$; one must specify the normalization when quoting this limit.
