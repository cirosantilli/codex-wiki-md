<h1 id="gauss-codazzi-equations">Gauss–Codazzi equations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss–Codazzi_equations)

The Gauss–Codazzi equations relate the intrinsic curvature and connection of an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) to its [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) and the ambient curvature. The [Gauss equation](gauss-equation.md) controls tangential curvature; the [Codazzi equation](codazzi-equation.md) controls the covariant derivative of the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md). The [Gauss formula](gauss-formula.md) is the tangential-normal splitting used to derive these relations.
