# Saturable absorber

↑ **Parent:** [Saturable absorption](saturable-absorption.md)

An element whose transmission increases as its absorption saturates. In a passive [Q-switched](q-switching.md) laser it initially blocks oscillation, then becomes more transparent when the growing intracavity field bleaches the loss.
