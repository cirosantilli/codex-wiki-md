# Standard Model one-loop gauge coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function (physics)](beta-function-physics.md)

For three [quark generations](quark-generation.md), their corresponding [leptons](lepton.md), and one complex [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md), use $Q=T_3+Y$ and $D_\mu\supset ig_Y YB_\mu$. In $\mu\,dg_i/d\mu=b_i g_i^3/(16\pi^2)$ the coefficients are the displayed values. Per generation, the fermionic [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md) sums are two for both non-Abelian factors and the sum of squared hypercharges is $10/3$. The Higgs index for $SU(2)$ and its sum of squared hypercharges are each $1/2$. Gauge terms are $-22/3,-11$ for $SU(2),SU(3)$, respectively. For the grand-unified normalization $g_1=\sqrt{5/3}\,g_Y$, the Abelian coefficient instead is $41/10$.
