# Divergence-form elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly elliptic operator](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md)

A divergence-form elliptic operator is $Lu=D_i(a^{ij}D_j u)$, with bounded measurable coefficients whose symmetric part has a uniform positive lower bound. Its [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) involves only first [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md), so differentiability of the coefficients is unnecessary.
