# Falkner-Skan boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkner–Skan_boundary_layer)

A Falkner-Skan boundary layer is a similarity boundary-layer flow driven by an outer velocity proportional to a power of distance along the wall. Its streamfunction reduction gives the [Falkner-Skan equation](falkner-skan-equation.md), with no-slip wall and outer-velocity matching conditions. It generalizes the Blasius flat-plate boundary layer.
