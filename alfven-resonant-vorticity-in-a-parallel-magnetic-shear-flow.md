<h1 id="alfven-resonant-vorticity-in-a-parallel-magnetic-shear-flow">Alfvén-resonant vorticity in a parallel magnetic shear flow</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

For an incompressible [shear flow](shear-flow.md) $U(z)\mathbf e_x$ with uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $B\mathbf e_x$, define $S=\sigma+k_xU$ for time dependence $e^{i\sigma t}$ and $\zeta=ik_xv-ik_yu$. Eliminating the magnetic perturbations from the horizontal [momentum](momentum.md) equations gives the displayed product for $S\ne0$. Away from the [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) resonance, it yields $\zeta=k_yU'w/S$. At resonance one must retain the original system: for constant $U$, $k_y=0$, $S^2=k_x^2B^2/\rho$, any smooth transverse amplitude $v(z)$ with $b_y=k_xBv/S$ and all other amplitudes zero is an [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md). Its nonzero [vorticity](vorticity.md) disproves the unrestricted zero-vorticity reduction. Exponentially growing modes with real background coefficients cannot encounter these real-frequency resonances.
