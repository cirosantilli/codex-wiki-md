# Multiple-image caustic of a softened isothermal lensing potential

↑ **Parent:** [Softened isothermal lensing potential](softened-isothermal-lensing-potential.md)

For $0<\theta_c<\theta_E$, the signed circular lens mapping $\beta=\theta[1-\theta_E/\sqrt{\theta^2+\theta_c^2}]$ has radial [stationary points](stationary-point.md) at $\theta_r^2=(\theta_E\theta_c^2)^{2/3}-\theta_c^2$. Substituting them gives the displayed caustic radius. An offset [point-like astronomical source](point-like-astronomical-source.md) has three images when $0<|\beta|<\beta_c$, a merging pair plus a separate outer image on the fold, and one image outside. At alignment, the two off-centre images form a ring while a central image remains. For $\theta_c\geq\theta_E$ the mapping is monotone and there is no finite multiple-image region. The zero-core singular limit loses the central image and has a different image-count exception.
