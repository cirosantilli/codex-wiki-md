# Linear interpolant

↑ **Parent:** [Linear interpolation](linear-interpolation.md)

A [linear interpolant](linear-interpolant.md) is the unique [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most one taking prescribed values $y_a,y_b$ at distinct [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md) $a,b$. It is an [affine function](affine-function.md), including the [constant function](constant-function.md) case. For $a\leq x\leq b$, its two displayed [coefficients](coefficient.md) are nonnegative and sum to one, so changing each sampled value by at most $\delta$ changes every interpolated value by at most $\delta$. Differentiation instead uses the difference of the two samples divided by $b-a$, causing [noise amplification by differentiation of a linear interpolant](noise-amplification-by-differentiation-of-a-linear-interpolant.md).
