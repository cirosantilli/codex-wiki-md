# Multiplicity of a parametrized surface integral

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

For a regular [parametric surface](parametric-surface.md) $\mathbf r:U\to X$, the displayed [surface integral](surface-integral.md) counts each image point as many times as its preimages in $U$, apart from sets of zero [surface area](surface-area.md). It equals the ordinary integral over the image when the parametrization is [injective](injective-function.md) except on such sets. A periodic parametrization integrated over more than one period can give a different answer from integration over its image set. For example, $(u,v)\mapsto(\cos u,\sin u,v)$ with $0\leq u\leq4\pi$ and $0\leq v\leq1$ gives parameter area $4\pi$, whereas the image [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) has [surface area](surface-area.md) $2\pi$.
