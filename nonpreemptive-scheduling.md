# Nonpreemptive scheduling

↑ **Parent:** [Process scheduling](process-scheduling.md)

A policy under which a running [operating-system process](process-computing.md) keeps the [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) until its current [CPU burst](cpu-burst.md) ends, it blocks or it voluntarily yields. A newly ready process waits instead of interrupting the current burst.
