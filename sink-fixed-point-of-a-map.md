# Sink fixed point of a map

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point stability for an iteration](fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md)

A hyperbolic sink [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of a smooth finite-dimensional map has every Jacobian [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) inside the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). Nearby iterates converge to it, and zero multipliers are allowed for a [noninvertible map](noninvertible-map.md). Unit-modulus multipliers require nonlinear or exact analysis rather than this strict linear criterion. A complex pair gives spiral contraction, while negative real multipliers alternate directions.
