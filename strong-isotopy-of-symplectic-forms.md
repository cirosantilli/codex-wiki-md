# Strong isotopy of symplectic forms

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic isotopy](symplectic-isotopy.md)

A strong isotopy of symplectic forms is a smooth path of symplectic forms whose de Rham cohomology class is constant, so that Moser's method can seek an ambient isotopy relating its endpoints.
