# Smooth-window Fourier coefficients of a periodic distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

A smooth compactly supported window with $\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}\chi(x+j)=1$ permits multiplying every periodic [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) unambiguously. Its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) sampled at integer harmonics gives the periodic coefficients. The sharp window $H(x)-H(x-1)$ works for locally integrable functions but can be undefined at singular period endpoints, such as delta derivatives.
