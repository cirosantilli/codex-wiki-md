# Momentum-winding duality of a quantum rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Flux-threaded quantum rotor](flux-threaded-quantum-rotor.md)

The thermal [trace](matrix-trace.md) can be expanded in angular-momentum eigenstates or in lifted paths whose endpoints differ by $2\pi m$. A winding saddle is linear in imaginary time, and its flux weight is the [Aharonov-Bohm phase](aharonov-bohm-effect.md) $e^{2\pi ifm}$. The common fixed-endpoint fluctuation factor is $\sqrt{I/(2\pi\hbar^2\beta)}$; integrating the initial angle supplies $2\pi$. The [Poisson summation formula](poisson-summation-formula.md) converts the resulting winding Gaussian sum into the [momentum](momentum-split.md) sum with exactly cancelling prefactors. The Gaussian evaluation is exact for this free rotor.
