# Low-mass X-ray binary

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md)

In a low-mass [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md), the [donor star](donor-star.md) is relatively low mass. [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) usually feeds an [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) around a [neutron star](neutron-star.md) or [black hole](black-hole.md). The low-mass label concerns the donor rather than the total binary mass. Accumulated fuel on a [neutron star](neutron-star.md) can produce a [Type I X-ray burst](type-i-x-ray-burst.md).
