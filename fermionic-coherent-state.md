# Fermionic coherent state

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

For a single [fermionic annihilation operator](fermionic-annihilation-operator.md) $a$ and an independent odd [Grassmann variable](grassmann-variable.md) $\theta$, a [fermionic coherent state](fermionic-coherent-state.md) is an extended state obeying $a|\theta\rangle=\theta|\theta\rangle$. With $\{a,\theta\}=0$ one may take $|\theta\rangle=\exp(-\theta a^\dagger)|0\rangle$. Its overlap is exponential in the Grassmann labels. A resolution of identity with a [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) gives the time-slice construction of a [fermionic path integral](fermionic-path-integral.md). These formal eigenstates use Grassmann-valued coefficients.
