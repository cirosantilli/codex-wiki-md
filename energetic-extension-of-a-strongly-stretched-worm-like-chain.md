# Energetic extension of a strongly stretched worm-like chain

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

An inextensible [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md) gains extension by reducing configurational fluctuations. At high force its fractional entropic extension approaches $1-\tfrac12\sqrt{k_BT/(fL_p)}$. A finite axial stretch modulus $K_s$ adds the leading energetic extension $f/K_s$. This approximation distinguishes suppressed bending fluctuations from actual stretching of the chain backbone; it does not describe an unfolding event that changes contour length.
