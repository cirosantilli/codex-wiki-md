# Double-well oscillator scale in a linear magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic-gradient quartic confinement](magnetic-gradient-quartic-confinement.md)

For $q\alpha k>0$, let $x_0=\sqrt{2\hbar k/(q\alpha)}$. Expansion of the [magnetic-gradient quartic confinement](magnetic-gradient-quartic-confinement.md) at either minimum gives $V(x_0+\delta x)=(q\alpha)^2x_0^2(\delta x)^2/(2m)+\cdots$. The local oscillator frequency is therefore $|q\alpha|x_0/m$. Both lowest parity states have leading energy $\hbar\omega_d/2$ if $x_0$ greatly exceeds the oscillator width. Local anharmonicity changes their common energy, while [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) gives their much smaller splitting.
