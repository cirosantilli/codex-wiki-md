# Guidance equation

↑ **Parent:** [de Broglie-Bohm theory](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md)

For a spinless particle without a magnetic vector potential, [Bohmian mechanics](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md) sets $\dot{\mathbf X}=\hbar\nabla S/m$, where $S$ is the dimensionless [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md). Equivalently the velocity is the [probability current](probability-current.md) divided by the [probability density](probability-density.md). This formula is local to nonzero [wavefunction](wave-function.md) regions.
