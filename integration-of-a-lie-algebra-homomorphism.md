# Integration of a Lie algebra homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md)

For a connected [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) source [Lie group](lie-group.md), every [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) integrates uniquely to a [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md). Along a path $\gamma$ from the identity, transport its left logarithmic velocity by $\theta$ and solve the corresponding left-invariant differential equation in the target. The [Maurer-Cartan equation](maurer-cartan-equation.md) and preservation of brackets make the endpoint invariant under fixed-endpoint [homotopies](homotopy.md). Simple connectedness removes path dependence, and path concatenation proves multiplicativity.
