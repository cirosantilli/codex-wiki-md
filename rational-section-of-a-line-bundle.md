# Rational section of a line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Line bundle](line-bundle.md)

On an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md) with [generic point](generic-point.md) $\eta$ and function field $K$, a rational section of a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $\mathcal L$ is an element of the one-dimensional $K$-space $\mathcal L_\eta$. A nonzero rational section writes as $a_ie_i$ in local trivializations, with $a_i\in K^*$. Ratios of these coefficients are regular units, and therefore define a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md).
