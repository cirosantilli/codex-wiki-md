# Frobenius norm

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md)

The Frobenius norm is the finite-matrix [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md). It equals $\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(A^*A)}$, is unchanged by unitary multiplication on either side, and is the square root of the sum of squared singular values. For unit vectors $u,v$, $\|uu^*-vv^*\|_F^2=2(1-|u^*v|^2)$, a useful sign-invariant measure of distance between rank-one [orthogonal projection matrices](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md).
