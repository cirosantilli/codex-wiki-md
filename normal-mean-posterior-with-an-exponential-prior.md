# Normal mean posterior with an exponential prior

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

For [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) observations from a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) with known [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) prior of rate $\lambda$ on their nonnegative mean gives this [truncated normal distribution](truncated-normal-distribution.md) [posterior](bayesian-posterior.md). Complete the square in $-n(\mu-\bar x)^2/(2\sigma^2)-\lambda\mu$; the truncation remains essential even when the untruncated normal mean is negative.
