# RNA splicing

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_splicing)

Processing of a precursor [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) that removes an [intron](intron.md) and joins the flanking [exons](exon.md). The [ribozyme](ribozyme.md) experiments show one self-splicing mechanism; [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md) need not always be autonomous.
