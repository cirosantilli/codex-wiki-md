# Strong normalization theorem for simply typed lambda calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form (abstract rewriting)](normal-form-abstract-rewriting.md)

This theorem establishes strong normalization in the [normal form](normal-form-abstract-rewriting.md) sense. Every well-typed simply typed lambda term admits no infinite beta-reduction sequence.
