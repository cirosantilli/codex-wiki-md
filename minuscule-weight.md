# Minuscule weight

↑ **Parent:** [Minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md)

A [dominant integral weight](dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$ is minuscule when its irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) is a [minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md). Equivalently, $\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle\in\{0,1\}$ for every [positive root](positive-root.md) $\alpha$. This convention includes the zero weight and its [trivial Lie algebra representation](trivial-lie-algebra-representation.md).
