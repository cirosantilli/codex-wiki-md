# Potential dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

A potential dipole in three dimensions is the [potential flow](potential-flow.md) $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$ with $\phi=\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf x/r^3$. Away from the origin its potential is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md), so it is also a constant-[pressure](pressure.md) [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md). Its [velocity](velocity.md) decays as $r^{-3}$ and its [vorticity](vorticity.md) is zero. It is distinct from a [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md), whose leading [velocity](velocity.md) decays as $r^{-2}$.
