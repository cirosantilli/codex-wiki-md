# Nucleolus

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

When the [imputation](imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md) set is nonempty, the [nucleolus](nucleolus.md) is the unique [imputation](imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md) lexicographically minimizing the vector of [excesses of a coalition](excess-of-a-coalition.md) sorted decreasingly. It minimizes the largest complaint first, then subsequent complaints among ties. It can be computed through successive [linear programs](linear-programming.md) that minimize the current maximum excess and retain constraints fixed by prior stages. Its uniqueness is the standard [nucleolus](nucleolus.md) theorem. If the [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md) is nonempty, the [nucleolus](nucleolus.md) belongs to it; an empty core does not preclude a [nucleolus](nucleolus.md).
