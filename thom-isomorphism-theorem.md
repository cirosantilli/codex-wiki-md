# Thom isomorphism theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Thom class](thom-class.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_isomorphism_theorem)

For an $R$-oriented rank-$d$ vector bundle, multiplication by the [Thom class](thom-class.md) gives isomorphisms

$$
H^q(B;R)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }H^{q+d}(D(E),S(E);R),
\qquad a\longmapsto\pi^*a\smile u_E.
$$
