# Canonical pseudometric of a Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)

The canonical distance is the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) of an increment of a [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md). It is a [pseudometric](pseudometric.md), since distinct parameters may represent equal [random variables](random-variable-split.md) almost surely. Its [metric covering number](metric-covering-number.md) appears in the [Dudley entropy integral](dudley-entropy-integral.md). For standard [fractional Brownian motion](fractional-brownian-motion.md), $d(s,t)=|s-t|^H$; a normalization with twice the [covariance function](covariance-function.md) multiplies this distance by $\sqrt2$.
