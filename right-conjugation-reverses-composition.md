# Right conjugation reverses composition

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugation action](conjugation-action.md)

For a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $K$ of $G$, each $c_h:k\mapsto h^{-1}kh$ is a [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) of $K$. Its inverse is $c_{h^{-1}}$, but the dependence on $h$ is a [group antihomomorphism](group-antihomomorphism.md) under ordinary [function composition](function-composition.md), because $(gh)^{-1}k(gh)=h^{-1}(g^{-1}kg)h$. The assignment $h\mapsto(k\mapsto hkh^{-1})$ instead is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) to the [automorphism group](automorphism-group.md). Equivalently the first convention is a right [group action](group-action-split.md), while the second is a left [group action](group-action-split.md).
