# Physical dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)

The physical dimension of a quantity records its powers of chosen base dimensions, independently of units. For example, [velocity](velocity.md) has dimension $LT^{-1}$ and [force](force.md) has dimension $MLT^{-2}$. [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) compares these expressions in physical equations.
