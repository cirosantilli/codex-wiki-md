# Upper-ray topology on the real line

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

These sets form a [topology](topology-split.md): a nonempty union of upper rays is another upper ray, or all of $\mathbb R$ when their endpoints have no lower bound; a finite intersection of rays has endpoint the maximum of their endpoints. The whole line must be included. The [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $n\mapsto n$ from $\mathbb N$ with the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) to this space is nonconstant, because each upper ray has cofinite inverse image.
