# Critical micelle concentration from standard chemical potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Critical micelle concentration](critical-micelle-concentration.md)

With $\mu=\mu_1^0+k_BT\ln x_1$, large aggregates become abundant when monomer [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) approaches the favorable aggregate standard value $\mu_*^0$. This gives $x_{1,*}\simeq\exp[(\mu_*^0-\mu_1^0)/(k_BT)]$ in the large-aggregation approximation. For one finite preferred size $n$, equal molecular populations $x_n=x_1$ instead give $\ln x_{1,*}=[n(\mu_n^0-\mu_1^0)/(k_BT)-\ln n]/(n-1)$.
