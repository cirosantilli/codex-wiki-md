# Typewriter sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

On $(0,1)$, for $k\geq0$ and $0\leq j<2^k$, the displayed [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) form a sequence that converges to zero in every finite [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) while failing to converge pointwise anywhere. Indeed, for $1\leq p<\infty$,

$$
\|f_{2^k+j}\|_p^p=2^{-k}.
$$

Every point belongs to exactly one interval in each dyadic block, and for $k\geq1$ it misses at least one other interval in that block. Thus every point sees infinitely many ones and infinitely many zeros. Half-open intervals specify the behaviour at dyadic endpoints. This separates [norm convergence](norm-convergence.md) in an [Lp space](lp-space.md) from [pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md) and convergence [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md).
