# Poleward heat transport by equatorward iceberg drift

↑ **Parent:** [Iceberg melt heat budget](iceberg-melt-heat-budget.md)

An [iceberg](iceberg.md) moving toward the equator carries a thermal deficit into warmer water. Melting extracts heat there, equivalent to transport of heat toward the pole. For an annual number of similar bergs, multiply their individual [iceberg melt heat budget](iceberg-melt-heat-budget.md) by the rate per second. Freshwater effects on [density stratification](density-stratification.md) and [ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md) are separate from this heat accounting.
