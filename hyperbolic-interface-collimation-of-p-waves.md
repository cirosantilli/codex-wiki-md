# Hyperbolic interface collimation of P-waves

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic ray](elastic-ray.md)

A solid-to-fluid interface can turn spherical source [P waves](p-wave.md) into parallel vertical rays if its height $Z(R)$ satisfies the displayed travel-time identity, with $r=\sqrt{R^2+Z^2}$. The positive branch through $Z(0)=z_0$ is a hyperboloid when $\alpha>\alpha_f$. Differentiating the travel-time identity enforces [Snell law for elastic and acoustic waves](snell-law-for-elastic-and-acoustic-waves.md). Travel time above the interface is then $z_0/\alpha+(z-z_0)/\alpha_f$, independent of cylindrical radius. The leading [wavefront](wavefront.md) is planar although its [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) need not be uniform across that plane.
