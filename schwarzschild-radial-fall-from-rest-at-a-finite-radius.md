# Schwarzschild radial fall from rest at a finite radius

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

For radial timelike [geodesic](geodesic.md) fall released from rest at $r_0>2M$, the [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) is $E=\sqrt{1-2M/r_0}$ and the [proper time](proper-time.md) radial equation is $(dr/ds)^2=2M/r-2M/r_0$. The displayed parameterization integrates its inward branch, with $s=0$ at release. The [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) is reached at $\eta_H=\arccos(4M/r_0-1)$ in finite proper time, even though [Schwarzschild time](schwarzschild-time.md) diverges.
