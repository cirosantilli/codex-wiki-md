<h1 id="weber-s-law">Weber's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

An approximate regime in which a threshold increment is proportional to background [intensity](intensity-physics.md), so $\Delta I/I$ is roughly constant. It is a task- and range-dependent empirical relation, not a universal law at photon-limited levels or under all adaptation states.
