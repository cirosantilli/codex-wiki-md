# Neumann point source on a half-space boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann Poisson problem](neumann-poisson-problem.md)

In the upper half-space $z>0$, the displayed decaying [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) has outward normal derivative $\partial_n\Phi=-\partial_z\Phi=z/[2\pi(x^2+y^2+z^2)^{3/2}]$. This is the [Poisson kernel for the upper half-space](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-space.md), whose integral on a horizontal plane is one and whose limit as $z\downarrow0$ is the two-dimensional [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md). Thus $\partial_n\Phi=\delta(x)\delta(y)$ in the distributional boundary sense. The compensating flux through a large hemisphere is minus one. This illustrates why a nonzero total boundary source is possible on an unbounded [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) even when the interior source is zero.
