# Adiabatic acoustic-peak spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background acoustic peak](cosmic-microwave-background-acoustic-peak.md)

[Adiabatic initial conditions](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) select coherent cosine phases of the [photon-baryon acoustic oscillator](photon-baryon-acoustic-oscillator.md). Its extrema at last scattering satisfy $kr_s\simeq n\pi$ and project to $\ell\simeq k\chi_*$. Thus $\ell_n\simeq n\pi\chi_*/r_s(\eta_*)$, with comoving [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md) $r_s=\int d\eta/\sqrt{3(1+R)}$. Projection, driving and finite last-scattering width modify the exact peak locations.
