# Brewer-Dobson circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Stratosphere](stratosphere.md)

The wave-driven mean stratospheric circulation with tropical ascent and extratropical descent. Changes in wave propagation and momentum deposition can alter its strength and transport times. Its response redistributes [ozone](ozone.md) and trace gases; local chemistry and transport must be considered together.
