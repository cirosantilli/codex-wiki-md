# One-sided spectral density of a real stationary time series

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

For real stationary processes, the one-sided density on $[0,\pi]$ is twice the usual two-sided density. Its integral is the [variance](variance-split.md) and $\gamma_k=\int_0^\pi f(\omega)\cos(k\omega)\,d\omega$. When [covariances](covariance.md) are absolutely summable, $f(\omega)=\pi^{-1}(\gamma_0+2\sum_{k\ge1}\gamma_k\cos(k\omega))$.
