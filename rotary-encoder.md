# Rotary encoder

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational inertia measurement with a falling mass](rotational-inertia-measurement-with-a-falling-mass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_encoder)

A [rotary encoder](rotary-encoder.md) converts shaft rotation into a readout of [angle](angle.md) or changes in [angle](angle.md). The PS-2120A uses interruptions of a [light](light.md) beam to encode shaft motion; its product specification gives 4000 divisions per revolution. That count is a resolution specification, not a guarantee of the precision of an inferred [angular acceleration](angular-acceleration.md) or [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md).
