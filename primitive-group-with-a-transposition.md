# Primitive group with a transposition

↑ **Parent:** [Primitive group action](primitive-group-action.md)

If a primitive subgroup of $S_n$ contains a [transposition](transposition-permutation.md), make a graph whose edges are the supports of its conjugate [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md). Connected components form blocks for the group. An edge exists, so primitivity forces the graph to be connected. Its edge [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) generate all of $S_n$, proving that the original group is $S_n$.
