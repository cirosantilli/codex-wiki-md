# Bragg point

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

A Bragg point is a [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) at which two plane waves differing by a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector are degenerate. A periodic potential mixes the waves there, producing an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md) and opening a [band gap](band-gap.md).
