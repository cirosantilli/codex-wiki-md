# Waveguide cutoff frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md)

For a [transverse electric mode of a rectangular waveguide](transverse-electric-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md), the dispersion relation is $\omega^2=c^2(k^2+\kappa_{mn}^2)$, with $\kappa_{mn}^2=(m\pi/a)^2+(n\pi/b)^2$. Below $\omega_{mn}=c\kappa_{mn}$, the longitudinal wave number is imaginary and the mode is evanescent. If $a\ge b$, the lowest propagating cutoff is $\omega_{10}=\pi c/a$, or ordinary frequency $c/(2a)$.
