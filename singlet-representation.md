# Singlet representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A one-dimensional group representation. For connected semisimple groups such as SU(2) or SU(3), its continuous irreducible [singlet](singlet-representation.md) is trivial, so all Lie-algebra generators act as zero. A [singlet](singlet-representation.md) of one subgroup need not be a [singlet](singlet-representation.md) of a larger group.
