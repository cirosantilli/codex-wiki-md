# Knot cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism](cobordism.md)

An oriented knot cobordism is a compact oriented smooth surface properly embedded in $S^3\times[0,1]$ with prescribed [knots](knot.md) at its incoming and outgoing boundary. A connected genus-zero [knot cobordism](knot-cobordism.md) between knots is a [knot concordance](knot-concordance.md). Composing [knot cobordisms](knot-cobordism.md) adds their [genera](genus-of-a-surface.md).
