# Shadow ray

↑ **Parent:** [Ray tracing (computer graphics)](ray-tracing-computer-graphics.md)

A visibility [ray](ray-geometry.md) or finite segment from a surface toward a light source. An opaque blocker before the source removes that source's direct illumination at the surface. Intersections beyond a finite light source must not count.
