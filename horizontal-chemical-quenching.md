# Horizontal chemical quenching

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical quench level](chemical-quench-level.md)

[Atmospheric advection time](atmospheric-advection-time.md) shorter than the [chemical relaxation time](chemical-relaxation-time.md) allows winds to carry a composition into regions where it differs from local [thermochemical equilibrium](thermochemical-equilibrium.md). In a [hot Jupiter](hot-jupiter.md), this can transport dayside carbon chemistry into the cooler nightside.
