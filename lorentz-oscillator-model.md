# Lorentz oscillator model

↑ **Parent:** [Electric susceptibility](electric-susceptibility.md)

A bound charge obeying $m\ddot u+m\gamma\dot u+m\omega_0^2u=qE$ gives a resonant [electric susceptibility](electric-susceptibility.md). For [carrier density](carrier-density.md) $n$ and the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention,

$$
\epsilon_r(\omega)=1+\frac{\omega_p^2}{\omega_0^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega},\qquad
\omega_p^2=\frac{nq^2}{m\epsilon_0}.
$$

Positive damping gives $\operatorname{Im}\epsilon_r\geq0$ for $\omega>0$, expressing passive absorption. Independent resonances add to the [electric susceptibility](electric-susceptibility.md). Setting $\omega_0=0$ gives the [Drude frequency response](drude-frequency-response.md).
