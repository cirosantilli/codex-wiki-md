# Purkinje cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron-split.md)

Named for [Jan Evangelista Purkyně](jan-evangelista-purkyne.md), a cerebellar [neuron](neuron-split.md) receiving many [parallel fiber](parallel-fiber.md) inputs and a powerful [climbing fiber](climbing-fiber.md) input. Simple-spike and complex-spike responses provide distinct physiological readouts in studies of motor learning.
