<h1 id="schramm-loewner-evolution">Schramm–Loewner evolution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schramm–Loewner_evolution)

Chordal Schramm–Loewner evolution in the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) from $0$ to $\infty$ is the random [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) driven by $U_t=\sqrt\kappa B_t$, where $B$ is standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and $\kappa\geq0$.
