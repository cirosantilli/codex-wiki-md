# Second group cohomology classifies group extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Group extension](group-extension.md)

Equivalence classes of [group extensions](group-extension.md) of $G$ by an abelian $G$-module $A$ correspond to $H^2(G,A)$. A section $s:G\to E$ produces the [extension cocycle](extension-cocycle.md)

$$
c(g,h)=s(g)s(h)s(gh)^{-1},
$$

and changing the section changes $c$ by a [group coboundary](group-coboundary.md).
