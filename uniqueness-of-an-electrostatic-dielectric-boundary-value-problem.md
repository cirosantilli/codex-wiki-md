# Uniqueness of an electrostatic dielectric boundary-value problem

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

For positive scalar [permittivities](permittivity-split.md) and specified free charges, fixed conductor potentials, [dielectric](dielectric.md) matching conditions and decay at infinity, the electrostatic field is unique. Subtract two proposed potentials and integrate $\nabla\cdot(\epsilon\,\delta\phi\nabla\delta\phi)$. The boundary terms vanish or cancel across [dielectric](dielectric.md) interfaces, leaving $\int\epsilon|\nabla\delta\phi|^2dV=0$. Hence the difference field is zero, with any potential constant fixed by the boundary convention.
