# Negation normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)

A [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) or [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) is in [negation normal form](negation-normal-form.md) when its [negations](negation.md) occur only directly before [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md), and its other [logical connectives](logical-connective.md) are conjunction and disjunction, together with [quantifiers](quantifier.md) in the first-order case. Remove implications using $P\to Q\equiv\neg P\lor Q$, then push [negations](negation.md) inward using [De Morgan's laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md), double-negation cancellation, and

$$
\neg\forall x\,\psi\equiv\exists x\,\neg\psi,\qquad
\neg\exists x\,\psi\equiv\forall x\,\neg\psi.
$$

In particular, negating an implication gives $P\land\neg Q$, so its antecedent retains its original sign after the outer [negation](negation.md) has been pushed through.
