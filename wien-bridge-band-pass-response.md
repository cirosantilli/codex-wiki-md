# Wien bridge band-pass response

↑ **Parent:** [Wien bridge](wien-bridge.md)

Use the equal-component [Wien bridge](wien-bridge.md) with upper-left parallel arm and lower-left series arm. Let $A$ be the left arm junction, $C$ the upper input node, and $V_i$ the upper-minus-lower input [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). With $u=\omega RC$, the unloaded transfer ratio $H=(V_A-V_C)/V_i$ is the displayed expression. Its [band-pass filter](band-pass-filter.md) magnitude is $[9+(u-u^{-1})^2]^{-1/2}$, maximal at $u=1$ with $H=-1/3$. One continuous [wave phase](phase-waves.md) is $\phi=-\pi-\arctan[(u-u^{-1})/3]$, tending from $-\pi/2$ at low positive [frequency](frequency.md) to $-3\pi/2$ at high [frequency](frequency.md). Reversing the measured output polarity multiplies $H$ by $-1$ and shifts its phase by $\pi$ without changing its magnitude.
