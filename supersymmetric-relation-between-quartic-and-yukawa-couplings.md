# Supersymmetric relation between quartic and Yukawa couplings

↑ **Parent:** [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md)

In a canonical [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md) with cubic [superpotential](superpotential.md) $g\Phi^3/3$, eliminating the [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) gives $V\supset|g|^2|\varphi|^4$ and $\mathcal L\supset-g\varphi\psi\psi+\mathrm{h.c.}$. Thus the coefficient of the quartic [scalar field](scalar-field.md) interaction is the squared modulus of this convention for the [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md).
