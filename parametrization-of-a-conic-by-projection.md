# Parametrization of a conic by projection

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

For a nonsingular [conic](conic-section.md) with a point $p$ and a [projective line](projective-line.md) $L$ not containing $p$, send $q\in L$ to the second intersection of $pq$ with the [conic](conic-section.md), counted with multiplicity. The tangent direction gives $p$ itself. The inverse sends any other [conic](conic-section.md) point to its joining line's intersection with $L$, and sends $p$ to the tangent's intersection. The [Bézout theorem](bezout-s-theorem.md) makes these maps inverse. With data over a [field](field.md), the construction preserves rationality: a known rational root of the restricted quadratic equation leaves its other root rational too. A [conic](conic-section.md) with a rational point is therefore rationally parametrized by a [projective line](projective-line.md).
