# Dispositional attribution

↑ **Parent:** [Attribution theory](attribution-theory.md)

An explanation of conduct in terms of a relatively enduring characteristic of the actor, such as an attitude or tendency. A [dispositional attribution](dispositional-attribution.md) can be accurate; [correspondence bias](correspondence-bias.md) concerns excessive inference from conduct whose circumstances make it weak evidence.
