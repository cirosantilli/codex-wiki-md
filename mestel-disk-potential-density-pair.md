# Mestel disk potential-density pair

↑ **Parent:** [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md)

The reflection-symmetric [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) $\Phi=v_0^2\log[(\sqrt{R^2+z^2}+|z|)/R_*]$ is harmonic off the plane. Its normal-derivative jump is $2v_0^2/R$, so the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) gives [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $S=v_0^2/(2\pi GR)$ and volume [mass density](density.md) $\rho=S\delta(z)$. The midplane [circular speed](circular-speed.md) is constant. The scale-free model has infinite total [mass](mass.md) and does not use a finite-mass boundary condition at infinity.
