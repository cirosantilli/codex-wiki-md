# Criminal behavior

↑ **Parent:** [Social psychology](social-psychology.md)

Actions classified as offences in a specified legal setting. Their causes and consequences are heterogeneous; a legal classification is not itself a psychiatric diagnosis. Behavioural incidence, arrests, convictions and severity of harm are different outcomes.
