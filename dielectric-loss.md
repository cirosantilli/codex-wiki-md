# Dielectric loss

↑ **Parent:** [Complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dielectric_loss)

Conversion of electrical [energy](energy-split.md) into heat in a [dielectric](dielectric.md) driven by an [electric field](electric-field-split.md). In the stated harmonic convention, a passive scalar response has $\epsilon''\geq0$ for positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). A [dielectric capacitance measurement](dielectric-capacitance-measurement.md) measures a dissipative component as well as [capacitance](capacitance.md); leakage from [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) can contribute to the inferred loss.
