# Local volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_volatility)

A [local volatility model](local-volatility-model.md) uses a deterministic function of current time and spot as its diffusion volatility, $\sigma(t,S_t)$. It can reproduce a surface of [European call option](european-call-option.md) prices through the [Dupire equation](dupire-equation.md).
