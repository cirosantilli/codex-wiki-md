# Iodoetherification

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Intramolecular capture of an alkene-derived iodonium [ion](ion.md) by an [alcohol](alcohol.md) produces a cyclic [ether](ether.md) and a [carbon](carbon.md)–iodine bond. Backside opening gives an anti relationship between the new C–O and C–I bonds.
