# Minimax search

↑ **Parent:** [Game tree](game-tree.md)

[Backward induction](backward-induction.md) on a finite deterministic [game tree](game-tree.md) of a two-player [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md). Terminal [payoffs](payoff.md) are propagated by maximization at one player's decision nodes and minimization at the other's. With full information, the resulting choices give an optimal strategy against the opposing player's best response. A cyclic state [graph](graph-split.md) requires a winning condition on infinite plays and a [graph](graph-split.md) algorithm rather than unrestricted recursive unfolding.
