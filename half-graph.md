# Half graph

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_graph)

A half graph has two [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) classes $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$ and $Y=\{y_1,\ldots,y_n\}$, with $x_i$ adjacent to $y_j$ exactly when $i\leq j$. Its nested [vertex neighbourhoods](vertex-neighbourhood.md) give a useful obstruction to partitions in which every pair is a [regular pair of vertex sets](regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md).
