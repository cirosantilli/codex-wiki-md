# Plane of incidence

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md)

For a ray meeting an interface at nonzero incidence angle, the plane spanned by the incoming wavevector and the surface normal. [transverse electric polarization](transverse-electric-polarization.md) has electric field perpendicular to this plane, while [transverse magnetic polarization](transverse-magnetic-polarization.md) has electric field in it. At exact normal incidence, the surface normal and ray alone do not select a unique plane.
