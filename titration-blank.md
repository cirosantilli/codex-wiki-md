# Titration blank

↑ **Parent:** [Titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md)

A [titration blank](titration-blank.md) runs the background procedure without the intended reacting sample, matching relevant [water](water.md), indicator quantity and [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md) conditions. Its [titrant](titrant.md) demand estimates a background contribution to subtract. The inspected EPA procedure uses such a [phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md) blank; this is a distinct control, rather than an instruction present in Columbia's Part 3.[Printed pp. 26–27, §1.4.4.2, indicator-blank paragraph and steps 1–3: water replaces acid, indicator quantity matches, blank volume is subtracted](process-measurement-procedures-sulfuric-acid-emissions-by-r-maddalone-and-n-garner.md) A blank must match the relevant background to justify subtraction; it does not remove sample-specific side reactions or a mismatch between [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md) and [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md).
