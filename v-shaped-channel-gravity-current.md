# V-shaped channel gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

For a fixed half-angle $0<\alpha<\pi/2$, a slow slender [gravity current](gravity-current.md) in a V-shaped channel has an almost horizontal [free surface](free-surface.md) across each section. Its area is $h^2\tan\alpha$, and its axial [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is $-gQ(\alpha)h^4h_x/\nu$. Here $Q$ is the integral of the dimensionless cross-sectional [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) solution: $\Delta\phi=-1$, with zero velocity on the two rigid slopes and zero normal velocity derivative on the horizontal shear-free surface. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $C=gQ/(\nu\tan\alpha)$. This treats the full two-dimensional section and does not require a shallow V angle.
