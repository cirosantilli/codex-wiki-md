# Equal-rapidity chain of collinear Lorentz boosts

↑ **Parent:** [Rapidity](rapidity.md)

If each successive [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) moves at the same signed [velocity](velocity.md) $u$, with $|u|<c$, relative to the preceding frame along a common axis, the [rapidity](rapidity.md) adds. The final [velocity](velocity.md) relative to the first frame is therefore the displayed expression. When $u=c/N$ and $N\ge2$, the limit is $c\tanh1$, since $N\operatorname{artanh}(1/N)\to1$.
