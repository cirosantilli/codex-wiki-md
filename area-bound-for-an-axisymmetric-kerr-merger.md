# Area bound for an axisymmetric Kerr merger

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible mass](irreducible-mass.md)

For initially well-separated [Kerr black holes](kerr-black-hole.md) with no orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), exact [axisymmetry](axisymmetric-vector-field.md) gives $J_f=J_1+J_2$. Put $X=(A_1+A_2)/(8\pi)$. The [black-hole area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md) and the [irreducible mass](irreducible-mass.md) identity imply

$$
M_f^2\geq\frac12\left(X+\frac{(J_1+J_2)^2}{X}\right).
$$

Indeed, $X\geq|J_1|+|J_2|\geq|J_f|$, and $\tfrac12(Y+J_f^2/Y)$ is nondecreasing for $Y\geq X$. Opposite spin signs lower $J_f^2$ at fixed initial areas and hence raise the allowed radiated-energy bound; this need not be achievable in an actual merger.
