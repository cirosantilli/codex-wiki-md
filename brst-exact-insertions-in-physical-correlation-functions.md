# BRST-exact insertions in physical correlation functions

↑ **Parent:** [BRST-exact operator](brst-exact-operator.md)

When separated operators $G_i$ are [BRST-closed](brst-closed-operator.md), the [graded BRST Ward identity](graded-brst-ward-identity.md) makes an exact insertion vanish. This is the correlator form of identifying closed representatives that differ by exact terms in [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md). The statement presupposes an invariant measure and suitable boundary conditions.
