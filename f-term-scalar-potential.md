# F-term scalar potential

↑ **Parent:** [Superpotential](superpotential.md)

For canonical global supersymmetry, eliminating the auxiliary fields gives

$$
V_F=\sum_i\left|\frac{\partial W}{\partial\phi_i}\right|^2.
$$
