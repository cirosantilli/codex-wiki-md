# CP parity of the Yang-Mills theta density

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theta term](yang-mills-theta-term.md)

The color-contracted density $\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}F^a_{\mu\nu}F^a_{\rho\sigma}$ is even under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) and odd under [parity](parity.md). In matrix form the two charge-conjugation signs cancel and transposition leaves the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the product unchanged. [Parity](parity.md) contributes its orientation-reversing determinant to the four-index [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md). Thus the density is [CP](cp-symmetry.md) odd. A generic fixed coefficient violates [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md); being a local total derivative does not eliminate effects from nontrivial gauge topology.
