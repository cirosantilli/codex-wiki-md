# Jones polynomial skein relation

↑ **Parent:** [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md)

Resolve the two crossings in a [skein relation](skein-relation.md) using the [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md), then correct by the [writhe of a link diagram](writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md). The other smoothing cancels, leaving the displayed coefficient of the [oriented smoothing](oriented-smoothing.md). This relation and the value one on the [unknot](unknot.md) determine the [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md): crossing changes give descending diagrams, which are [unlinks](unlink.md), while the smoothing terms have fewer crossings.
