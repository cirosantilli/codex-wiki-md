# Unit of a Laurent polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md)

For an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $R$, the [breadth of a Laurent polynomial](breadth-of-a-laurent-polynomial.md) adds under multiplication. If two Laurent [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) multiply to one, both therefore have breadth zero: they are [monomials](monomial.md), and their coefficients must be [units](unit-in-a-ring.md) of $R$. Conversely every displayed monomial is invertible. For $R=\mathbb Z$ the units are precisely $\pm t^k$, explaining the normalization ambiguity of an [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md).
