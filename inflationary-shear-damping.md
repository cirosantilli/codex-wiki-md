# Inflationary shear damping

↑ **Parent:** [Long-wavelength approximation in cosmology](long-wavelength-approximation-in-cosmology.md)

The traceless mixed [extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md) can have leading solution $\widetilde K^i{}_j=C^i{}_j(\mathbf x)a_{\mathrm{loc}}^{-3}$. [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) suppresses this anisotropic expansion. An anisotropic spatial shape can nevertheless freeze to a nonzero constant, so shear damping does not prove the absence of [tensor cosmological perturbations](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md).
