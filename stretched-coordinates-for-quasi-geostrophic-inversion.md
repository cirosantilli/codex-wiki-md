# Stretched coordinates for quasi-geostrophic inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Stratified quasi-geostrophic inversion](stratified-quasi-geostrophic-inversion.md)

For constant nonzero [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) and positive constant [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) [frequency](frequency.md), the anisotropic [quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) operator becomes the ordinary three-dimensional [Laplacian](laplacian.md) in the displayed coordinates. Its decaying whole-space inverse is $\psi(\mathbf X)=-\int[Q(\mathbf X')-f]/(4\pi|\mathbf X-\mathbf X'|)\,d^3X'$. Boundary or far-field data are needed to exclude arbitrary harmonic additions.
