# Pinned-square surface displacement mode

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

A surface displacement constrained to vanish on all four sides of an ideal square has sine factors $\sin(n\pi x/L)\sin(m\pi y/L)$, with $n,m\geq1$. The fundamental therefore has [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $\sqrt2\pi/L$. This differs from the impermeable-wall condition in a closed tank, which permits cosine modes with a zero index. For an elastic plate a simply supported edge also supplies its bending [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md); clamping imposes a different problem.
