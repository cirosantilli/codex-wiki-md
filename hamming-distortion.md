# Hamming distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Rate-distortion function](rate-distortion-function.md)

[Hamming distortion](hamming-distortion.md) is zero for a correctly reconstructed symbol and one for a mismatch. Its [expected distortion](expected-distortion.md) is the symbol-error [probability](probability.md):

$$
\mathbb E[d(X,\widehat X)]=\mathbb P(X\ne\widehat X).
$$

For strings of length $n$, [separable distortion](separable-distortion.md) divides their [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md) by $n$. Thus $001$ reconstructed as $000$ has [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md) one and average [Hamming distortion](hamming-distortion.md) $1/3$. Counting only whether the whole string is correct would give a different criterion.
