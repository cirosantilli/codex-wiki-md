# Systematic encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Code encoding](code-encoding.md)

A systematic encoder retains the message symbols unchanged in designated coordinates of its [codeword](codeword.md) and calculates the remaining check symbols. For a [linear code](linear-code.md), choosing an invertible set of $k$ columns of a [generator matrix](generator-matrix.md) and changing its row [basis](basis.md) gives this form.
