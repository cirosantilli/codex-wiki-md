# Nonleptonic baryon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

A [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) decay of a [baryon](baryon.md) into final [hadrons](hadron.md) without final [leptons](lepton.md). A [weak charged current](charged-current.md) changes [quark flavour](quark-flavor.md), while [spectator quarks](spectator-quark.md) and the produced [quarks](quark.md) undergo [hadronization](hadronization.md). A quark-flow [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) specifies a possible weak topology; it does not calculate the full hadronic [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md).
