# Monotonicity energy estimate for a semilinear Dirichlet problem

↑ **Parent:** [Energy method](energy-method.md)

Suppose $-u''+u+q(u)=f$ and $-v''+v+q(v)=g$ on a bounded interval, the solutions have the same [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), and $q$ is a [nondecreasing function](nondecreasing-function.md). Multiplying the difference equation by $w=u-v$ and using [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\|w'\|_2^2+\|w\|_2^2+\int(q(u)-q(v))w=\int(f-g)w.
$$

The nonlinear [integral](integral-split.md) is nonnegative. [Young's inequality for products](young-s-inequality-for-products.md) gives the displayed [energy estimate](energy-estimate.md). It proves [uniqueness](uniqueness.md) and [continuous dependence on forcing](continuous-dependence-on-forcing.md). The same argument holds for nodal values when [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) is replaced by [discrete summation by parts](discrete-summation-by-parts.md).
