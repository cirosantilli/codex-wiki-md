# Restriction of a measure to a measurable subset

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure-split.md)

For a [measure space](measure-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mu)$ and $E\in\mathcal F$, the [restriction of a measure to a measurable subset](restriction-of-a-measure-to-a-measurable-subset.md) is the [measure](measure-split.md) on $(E,\mathcal F|_E)$ given by the displayed formula. The [trace sigma-algebra](trace-sigma-algebra.md) consists of the [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) contained in $E$. The empty-set axiom and [countable additivity](countable-additivity.md) are inherited directly from $\mu$. An associated [measure](measure-split.md) on the original [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) is $\nu(A)=\mu(A\cap E)$ for $A\in\mathcal F$; the two constructions have different underlying [measurable spaces](measurable-space.md).
