# Prime-sign automorphism of the multiplicative integers

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism of a first-order structure](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md)

For a fixed prime $p$, put $s_p(0)=0$ and $s_p(n)=(-1)^{v_p(n)}n$ for $n\ne0$, where $v_p(n)$ is its [P-adic valuation](p-adic-valuation.md). Additivity of the valuation on products proves $s_p(mn)=s_p(m)s_p(n)$. Changing sign does not change the valuation, so $s_p^2$ is the identity. Thus this is a [structure automorphism](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of $(\mathbb Z,\cdot)$. At $p=2$ it fixes one and sends two to minus two, so it does not preserve the addition graph or the nonnegative integers. [Automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability](automorphism-invariance-of-parameter-free-definability.md) therefore excludes both from definability in this multiplicative language.
