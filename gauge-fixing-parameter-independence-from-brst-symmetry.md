# Gauge-fixing parameter independence from BRST symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [BRST Ward identity](brst-ward-identity.md)

For auxiliary-field covariant gauge fixing, varying the parameter inserts a [BRST-exact operator](brst-exact-operator.md). Its correlations with separated physical [BRST-closed](brst-closed-operator.md) operators vanish by the [BRST Ward identity](brst-ward-identity.md). Normalized physical correlators are therefore independent of the parameter under the invariant-measure and boundary assumptions.
