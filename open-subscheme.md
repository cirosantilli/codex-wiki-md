# Open subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

An open subscheme of a [scheme](scheme.md) $X$ is an [open subset](open-set.md) $U$ equipped with the restriction of its [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). Its inclusion is an [open immersion](open-immersion.md). Every scheme is covered by [affine open subschemes](affine-open-subscheme.md). Any nonempty open subscheme of an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md) is integral and has the same [generic point](generic-point.md) and [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md).
