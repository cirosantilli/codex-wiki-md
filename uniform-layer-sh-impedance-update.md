# Uniform-layer SH impedance update

↑ **Parent:** [SH impedance Riccati equation](sh-impedance-riccati-equation.md)

In a uniform layer, put $q=\mu p_\beta$, $\kappa=\omega p_\beta$ and $d=z-z_r$. Initial impedance $Z_r$ gives the displayed fractional-linear solution and $V(z)=V_r[\cos\kappa d+i(Z_r/q)\sin\kappa d]$. Expanding into exponentials yields forward and backward [shear-horizontal waves](shear-horizontal-wave.md). Negative $d$ propagates a bottom load upward. If the denominator vanishes, the nonsingular pair $(V,T)$ must be propagated through the impedance pole.
