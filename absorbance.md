# Absorbance

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absorbance)

[Absorbance](absorbance.md) is the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) quantity $A=\log_{10}(P_0/P)$, where $P_0$ and $P$ are reference and transmitted radiant [powers](power.md) at the chosen [wavelength](wavelength.md). For example, $A=1$ means one tenth of the reference [power](power.md) is transmitted. Under the [Beer-Lambert law](beer-lambert-law.md), [absorbance](absorbance.md) can determine an absorbing species' [concentration](concentration.md); attenuation by cloudiness or unrelated colored [solutes](solute.md) needs separate controls. [IUPAC Gold Book: absorbance and its concentration relation](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/B00626)
