# Energy-loss straggling

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping power](stopping-power.md)

The stochastic spread of particle energy losses around their mean after traversal of matter. It broadens the endpoint of a particle beam and is distinct from the mean [stopping power](stopping-power.md).
