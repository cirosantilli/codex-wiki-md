# Convex combination

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_combination)

A convex combination of points $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ is a sum

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i x_i,
\qquad \lambda_i\geq0,
\qquad \sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i=1.
$$
