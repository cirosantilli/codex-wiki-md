# Joint probability mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Random vector](random-vector.md)

For [discrete random variables](discrete-random-variable.md) $X,Y$, their joint probability mass function assigns a [probability](probability.md) to each possible ordered pair. Its entries are nonnegative and sum to one. [Marginalization](marginalization.md) gives the [probability mass functions](probability-mass-function.md) $p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y)$ and $p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y)$. In a table with rows indexed by $x$ and columns by $y$, these are the row and column totals. The [joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md) records how the coordinates occur together, while the [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) describe each separately.
