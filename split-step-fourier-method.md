# Split-step Fourier method

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split-step_Fourier_method)

The split-step Fourier method alternates local phase accumulation with free diffraction. For $E_x=(D+S)E$ over a step $\Delta x$, [Lie-Trotter splitting](lie-product-formula.md) gives

$$
E(x+\Delta x)\simeq e^{\Delta xD}e^{\Delta xS}E(x),
$$

and a transverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) applies the constant-coefficient diffraction exponential efficiently.
