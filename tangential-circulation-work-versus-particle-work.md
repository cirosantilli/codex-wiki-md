# Tangential circulation work versus particle work

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic forces do no work](magnetic-forces-do-no-work.md)

A tangential [force](force-split.md) [integral](integral-split.md) around an instantaneous moving loop is not generally the [force](force-split.md) [work](work.md) along the actual particle trajectory. For an expanding circular guide, the particle has both radial and tangential [velocity](velocity.md); the magnetic radial and tangential [powers](power.md) cancel exactly even when the tangential [circulation](circulation-physics.md) is nonzero.
