# Lemniscatic integral

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic integral of the first kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md)

Choose the [holomorphic square root](holomorphic-square-root.md) equal to one at zero on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md). The resulting [elliptic integral](elliptic-integral.md) maps that disc conformally onto the [square](square.md) with vertices $\pm B,\pm iB$, where $B=\int_0^1dt/\sqrt{1-t^4}$. For the inverse [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md) $h(z)=i(1+z)/(1-z)$, the square of the [derivative](derivative.md) of $F_1\circ h$, with $F_1'(w)=1/\sqrt{w(1-w^2)}$, is $2i/(1-z^4)$. Thus the two [square](square.md) maps differ by an affine change of coordinate.
