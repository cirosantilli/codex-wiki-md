# Ceiling effect (statistics)

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceiling_effect_(statistics))

Clustering at the upper limit of a measurement instrument can prevent it from distinguishing higher underlying levels. A bounded score need not exhibit a [ceiling effect](ceiling-effect-statistics.md), and a sample maximum alone does not establish one. The issue concerns what variation the instrument can measure, rather than a proof that a treatment has no effect.
