# Quantum electrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_electrodynamics)

[QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) predicts how [electrons](electron.md), [Positrons](positron.md) and [photons](photon.md) interact by coupling a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) to an Abelian [gauge field](gauge-field.md). For a field of signed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q_e$, the interaction is $\mathcal L_{\rm int}=-q_e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu$; an [electron](electron.md) has $q_e=-e$, $e>0$. Quantizing both fields lets an [electron magnetic moment](electron-magnetic-moment.md) receive [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) beyond the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) value. The small [fine-structure constant](fine-structure-constant.md) organizes these corrections into a useful [perturbation series](perturbation-series.md).

A motivating question is concrete: once [mass](mass.md) and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) are fixed, can the theory predict a small excess in the [electron magnetic moment](electron-magnetic-moment.md)? The [Kusch-Foley magnetic moment experiment](kusch-foley-magnetic-moment-experiment.md) compared [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) frequencies in a constant [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), using different atomic states to separate [spin](spin.md) and orbital contributions. Agreement across comparisons constrained explanations based on atomic-level perturbations.[Abstract: states, frequency ratios and perturbation control](the-magnetic-moment-of-the-electron-by-polykarp-kusch-and-henry-michael-foley-1948.md) The [Lamb-Retherford experiment](lamb-retherford-experiment.md) posed a complementary [energy level](energy-level.md) problem. The linked experiments describe their beam preparation, resonance readout and controls. Together they motivate asking whether [renormalized](renormalization.md) [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) predicts more than its input [mass](mass.md) and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
