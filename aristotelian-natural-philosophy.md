# Aristotelian natural philosophy

↑ **Parent:** [Natural philosophy](natural-philosophy.md)

A tradition of explanations of natural change drawing on [Aristotle](aristotle.md)'s distinctions among matter, form, capacities and causes. Its medieval and early modern versions were interpreted traditions, not identical endorsements of every ancient conclusion.[book II, chapters 1–3](physics-by-aristotle.md)
