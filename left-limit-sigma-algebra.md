# Left-limit sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration (probability theory)](filtration-probability-theory.md)

For a [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$, the information strictly before time $t$ is

$$
\mathcal F_{t-}=\sigma\!\left(\bigcup_{s<t}\mathcal F_s\right).
$$

A [predictable process](predictable-process.md) evaluated at a deterministic positive time $t$ is $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable.
