# Manning condensed fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Counterion condensation](counterion-condensation.md)

In the mean-field infinite-dilution cylinder model, the fraction of neutralizing charge that remains localized is $f_c=\max(1-1/\eta,0)$, where $\eta$ is the [Manning parameter](manning-parameter.md). For $\eta>1$, integrating the [cylindrical counterion-only Poisson-Boltzmann profile](cylindrical-counterion-only-poisson-boltzmann-profile.md) gives $Q_c=\lambda-q/\ell_B$. Below threshold, the local limiting density vanishes even though every finite neutral cell contains the full [counterion](counterion.md) charge.
