# Left coset

↑ **Parent:** [Coset](coset.md)

For a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of a [group](group-split.md) $G$ and $g\in G$, its left coset is $gH=\{gh:h\in H\}$. Left multiplication $h\mapsto gh$ is a [bijection](bijection.md) from $H$ onto $gH$. Left and [right cosets](right-coset.md) agree for every $g$ exactly when $H$ is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md).
