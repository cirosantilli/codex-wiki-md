# Backtracking

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

Build a candidate incrementally, abandoning a partial candidate as soon as no valid completion is possible. A [depth-first search](depth-first-search.md) explores one choice, restores the previous state, and tries the next. Constraint propagation and bounds can greatly reduce the visited [state space](state-space.md), although the worst-case [time complexity](time-complexity.md) may remain exponential.
