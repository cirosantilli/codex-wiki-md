# Smooth point of a variety

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth locus of a variety](smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md)

Over a [perfect field](perfect-field.md), a point of a [variety](algebraic-variety.md) is smooth when its [local ring](local-ring.md) is a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md). For an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md) of dimension $n$ at a [closed point](closed-point.md), this is equivalent to its [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md) having dimension $n$. The [Jacobian criterion](jacobian-criterion.md) expresses the equality as a matrix-rank condition.
