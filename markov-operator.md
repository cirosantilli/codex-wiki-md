# Markov operator

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_operator)

A positive [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on bounded [measurable functions](measurable-function.md) that preserves the constant function one. A [Markov semigroup](markov-semigroup.md) is a time-indexed family of such operators with the semigroup law.
