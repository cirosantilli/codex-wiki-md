# Outdegree orientation criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation of a graph](orientation-of-a-graph.md)

A finite undirected [graph](graph-split.md) can be oriented with [outdegree](outdegree.md) at least an integer $k\geq0$ at every vertex if and only if every vertex subset $U$ is incident to at least $k|U|$ distinct edges. The [max-flow min-cut theorem](max-flow-min-cut-theorem.md) and [integral max-flow theorem](integral-max-flow-theorem.md) prove sufficiency by assigning distinct edges to vertices through an incidence [flow network](flow-network.md); each assigned edge is oriented away from that vertex. Necessity follows by counting outgoing edges from $U$.
