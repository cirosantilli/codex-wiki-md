# Yield stress

↑ **Parent:** [Rheology](rheology-split.md)

The [yield stress](yield-stress.md) is the stress threshold beyond which a material sustains irreversible flow under the specified loading conditions. Time-dependent materials require an operational measurement protocol; a single elastic modulus does not determine their [yield stress](yield-stress.md).
