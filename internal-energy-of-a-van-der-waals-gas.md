# Internal energy of a van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

For fixed [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md), thermodynamic consistency gives

$$
\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_T
=T\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_V-P.
$$

Indeed, the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives $U_V|_T=T S_V|_T-P$, and the [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) gives $S_V|_T=P_T|_V$. For the fixed-amount convention $P=RT/(V-b)-a/V^2$ with temperature-independent $a,b$, substitution gives $U_V|_T=a/V^2$. Integration yields $U=h(T)-a/V$. If the dilute-limit reference is $U\to cT$ as $V\to\infty$ for every $T$, then $h(T)=cT$.

The same formula may be interpreted molarly if $V,U,a,b$ all use that convention. Mixing molar and total quantities changes the amount factors. The attraction controls the volume correction to [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), while excluded volume alone does not contribute to this derivative when $a,b$ are independent of temperature.
