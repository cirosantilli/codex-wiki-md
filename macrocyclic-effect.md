# Macrocyclic effect

↑ **Parent:** [Chelation](chelation.md)

Enhanced complex stability associated with donor sites being held in a [macrocycle](macrocycle.md), relative to comparable open-chain ligands. The comparison depends on donor geometry, solvation and strain.
