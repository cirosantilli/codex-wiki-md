# Mixed Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_Poisson_distribution)

A mixture of [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) over a nonnegative random intensity. With exposure $n>0$ and [prior density](prior-density.md) $\pi(\theta)$, its [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) is $p_n(s)=\int_0^\infty e^{-n\theta}(n\theta)^s\pi(\theta)\,d\theta/s!$. A gamma mixing distribution gives a [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md). Counts sharing the same latent intensity are [conditionally independent](conditional-independence.md), but generally dependent after mixing.
