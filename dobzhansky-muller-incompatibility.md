# Dobzhansky-Muller incompatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md)

A harmful interaction between [alleles](allele.md) that evolved in separate lineages and are compatible with their own [genetic backgrounds](genetic-background.md). For example, substitutions yielding $Ab$ and $aB$ can each be tolerated, while the combination $AB$ is harmful in a [hybrid](hybrid-biology.md). This is a form of [epistasis](epistasis.md).
