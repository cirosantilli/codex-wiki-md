# Analytical concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid mass and charge balances](weak-acid-mass-and-charge-balances.md)

An [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md) records the total conserved inventory of a specified component per [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), summed over its relevant [chemical species](chemical-species.md). Here $C_T=[\mathrm{HA}]+[\mathrm A^-]$; it is not generally the [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) [concentration](concentration.md) $[\mathrm{HA}]$ of the undissociated [weak acid](weak-acid.md).
