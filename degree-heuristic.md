# Degree heuristic

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md)

Choose an unassigned [variable](variable-mathematics.md) constrained to the greatest number of other unassigned [variables](variable-mathematics.md). This is commonly a tie-breaker after [minimum remaining values](minimum-remaining-values.md). The relevant [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) is computed in the remaining [constraint graph](constraint-graph.md), rather than counting neighbors already assigned. It aims to make each decision constrain many future decisions without changing the solution [set](set-split.md).
