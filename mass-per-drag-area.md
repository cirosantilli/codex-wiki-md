# Mass per drag area

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic-drag turning angle](quadratic-drag-turning-angle.md)

The normalization $\mu=m/A_w$ has units $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}}$. It converts the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) into a force per water-drag area and allows comparison with wind and water stresses. [Icebergs](iceberg.md) generally have much larger $\mu$ than thin [ice floes](ice-floe.md), but their effective air-to-water drag-area ratio also matters. A geometric depth becomes a mass per area only after multiplication by density.
