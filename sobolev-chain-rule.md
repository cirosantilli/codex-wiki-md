# Sobolev chain rule

↑ **Parent:** [Weak derivative](weak-derivative.md)

If $u\in W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and $\Phi$ is continuously differentiable with bounded derivative on the range in use, then $D(\Phi(u))=\Phi^{\prime}(u)Du$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). One proves this first for [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) and then uses [density of smooth functions in a Sobolev space](density-of-smooth-functions-in-a-sobolev-space.md) and boundedness of $\Phi^{\prime}$. On compact sets one may truncate $\Phi$ away from the range of a bounded $u$.
