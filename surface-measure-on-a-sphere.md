# Surface measure on a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

Surface area is the two-dimensional measure induced on the unit [sphere](sphere.md) in three dimensions. It is invariant under orthogonal rotations. It is not the restriction of ambient three-dimensional [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), under which the [sphere](sphere.md) has measure zero. Normalizing by the [sphere](sphere.md) area gives a [probability measure](probability-measure.md).
