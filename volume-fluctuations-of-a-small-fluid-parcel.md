# Volume fluctuations of a small fluid parcel

↑ **Parent:** [Availability curvature and equilibrium fluctuations](availability-curvature-and-equilibrium-fluctuations.md)

For a material parcel of fixed amount inside a much larger thermal and mechanical environment, [availability](exergy.md) is $A(V)=F(V,T)+p_{\rm bath}V$. Since $F_V=-p$, its curvature is $A_{VV}=-p_V=1/(V\kappa_T)$. The [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md) gives $\langle\delta V^2\rangle=k_BT\kappa_T\bar V$. This describes parcel-volume fluctuations, not the geometric [volume](volume.md) of a fixed observation box or a globally rigid vessel. A fixed spatial box instead has number fluctuations related to the same compressibility.
