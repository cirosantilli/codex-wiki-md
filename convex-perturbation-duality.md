# Convex perturbation duality

↑ **Parent:** [Convex perturbation function](convex-perturbation-function.md)

For a [convex perturbation function](convex-perturbation-function.md), define

$$
\varphi(x)=f(x,0),\qquad\psi(y)=-f^*(0,y),\qquad p(z)=\inf_x f(x,z),\qquad q(v)=\sup_y[-f^*(v,y)].
$$

The primal and dual values are $p(0)$ and $q(0)$; the signed dual marginal $q$ is concave. The identity $p^*(y)=f^*(0,y)$ gives $q(0)=p^{**}(0)$. If $p$ is proper and finite near zero, [subgradients](subgradient.md) at zero prove [strong duality](strong-duality.md) with dual attainment. In finite dimensions the relative-interior condition $0\in\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom}p)$ also suffices, provided $p(0)$ is finite. This does not itself prove primal attainment.
