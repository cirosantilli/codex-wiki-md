# Normal-ordered Hamiltonian of a free complex scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a complex scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

Spatial integration of the free [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) cancels particle-[antiparticle](antiparticle.md) pair terms through $E_p^2-\mathbf p^2-m^2=0$. The raw [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $\int d\Pi_p E_p(a^\dagger a+bb^\dagger)$. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) removes the field-independent [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) and gives $H=\int d\Pi_p E_p(a^\dagger a+b^\dagger b)$. Both [creation operators](creation-operator.md) increase the energy by $E_p$, so a negative-frequency field mode represents a positive-energy [antiparticle](antiparticle.md), not a negative-energy state.
