# Alkyl bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md)

An alkyl group attached across metal centres. A bridging methyl can form a [three-centre two-electron bond](three-centre-two-electron-bond.md), rather than two full independent two-electron metal–[carbon](carbon.md) bonds.
