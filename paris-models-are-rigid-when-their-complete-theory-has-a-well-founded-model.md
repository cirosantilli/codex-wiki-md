# Paris models are rigid when their complete theory has a well-founded model

↑ **Parent:** [Paris model](paris-model.md)

Under the hypothesis of [Paris models are well-founded when their complete theory has a well-founded model](paris-models-are-well-founded-when-their-complete-theory-has-a-well-founded-model.md), a Paris model $M$ is well-founded. Every automorphism of $M$ is then the identity by external [epsilon induction](epsilon-induction.md): once all members of $x$ are fixed, extensionality gives $\pi(x)=\{\pi(y):y\in^M x\}=x$. Hence $M$ is a [rigid first-order structure](rigid-first-order-structure.md).
