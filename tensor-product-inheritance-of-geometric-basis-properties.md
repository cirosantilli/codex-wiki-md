# Tensor-product inheritance of geometric basis properties

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor-product surface basis](tensor-product-surface-basis.md)

Nonnegative [tensor-product surface basis](tensor-product-surface-basis.md) factors yield nonnegative product weights. Their two [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) sums multiply to one. If the factors are $C^r$ and $C^s$, mixed [derivatives](derivative.md) through separate orders $r,s$ are continuous, so the resulting finite or locally finite surface representation is jointly $C^{\min(r,s)}$. Control coefficients can cancel derivative jumps and yield more smoothness; inherited continuity is a guaranteed lower bound, not a claim that every surface has exactly the basis's worst regularity.
