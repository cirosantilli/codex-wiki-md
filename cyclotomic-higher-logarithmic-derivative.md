# Cyclotomic higher logarithmic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

For a norm-compatible sequence of local units, its [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md) $f_u\in\mathbb Z_p[[T]]^\times$ defines these integral additive homomorphisms. In the formal coordinate $z=\log(1+T)$ they are the derivatives of $\log f_u(e^z-1)$; the constant term of the logarithm is irrelevant. A Galois element with cyclotomic value $a$ multiplies the kth derivative by $a^k$. This is the multiplicative-formal-group version of the [Coates–Wiles homomorphism](coates-wiles-homomorphism.md).
