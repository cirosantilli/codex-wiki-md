# Signature of a knot

↑ **Parent:** [Levine-Tristram signature](levine-tristram-signature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signature_of_a_knot)

The knot signature is the [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) of the symmetrized [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md). Equivalently it is the [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) of the double [branched covering](branched-covering.md) of $B^4$ over a pushed-in [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md). At $\omega=-1$ it agrees with the [Levine-Tristram signature](levine-tristram-signature.md). It is additive under [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md), changes sign under reflection, and obeys $|\sigma(K)|\leq2g_4(K)$. State the ambient [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) and push-off convention when assigning a sign.
