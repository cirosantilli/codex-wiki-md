# Microconstituent

↑ **Parent:** [Microstructure](microstructure.md)

A recognizable constituent of a [microstructure](microstructure.md), which can itself contain more than one thermodynamic phase. Primary Bi-rich crystals and a two-phase [eutectic microstructure](eutectic-microstructure.md) are different [microconstituents](microconstituent.md); counting them differs from counting the total Bi-rich and Sn-rich phase amounts.
