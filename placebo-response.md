# Placebo response

↑ **Parent:** [Placebo effect](placebo-effect.md)

The observed change after placebo administration, including possible contextual effects, spontaneous change, [regression to the mean](regression-to-the-mean.md) and concurrent care. A placebo group's improvement is not by itself an estimate of the causal [placebo effect](placebo-effect.md).
