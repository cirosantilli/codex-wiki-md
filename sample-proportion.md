# Sample proportion

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

For independent Bernoulli observations with success probability $p$, the sample proportion is the success count divided by the sample size. It is an unbiased [estimator](estimator.md) with variance $p(1-p)/n$. It supplies the arm-specific risks in a [risk ratio](risk-ratio.md) and the mean difference in a two-proportion comparison.
