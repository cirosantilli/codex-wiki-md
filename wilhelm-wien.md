# Wilhelm Wien

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Wien)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md).[Independent biographical identity and professional specialization](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/uniarchiv/persoenlichkeiten/gelehrtentafeln/wilhelm-wien/)

[Wien's displacement law](wien-s-displacement-law.md) connects a thermal spectrum's peak position to [temperature](temperature-split.md). Specifying whether the spectrum is expressed per unit [wavelength](wavelength.md) or per unit [frequency](frequency.md) is essential to stating which peak is meant.
