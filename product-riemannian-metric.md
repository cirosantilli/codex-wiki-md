# Product Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

The product of [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) has metric

$$
g=p_1^*g_1+p_2^*g_2,\qquad g((u_1,u_2),(v_1,v_2))=g_1(u_1,v_1)+g_2(u_2,v_2).
$$

The tangent summands are orthogonal, and positivity follows because a nonzero product tangent vector has a nonzero component. Its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is the [product affine connection](product-affine-connection.md) of the two factor [Levi-Civita connections](levi-civita-connection.md): mixed torsion is zero and metric compatibility follows from independence of the two coordinate sets.
