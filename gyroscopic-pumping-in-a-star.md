# Gyroscopic pumping in a star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar rotation](stellar-rotation.md)

In a steady axisymmetric [star](star.md), a torque due to [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md), [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md) or [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) must balance advection of [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) by [stellar meridional circulation](meridional-circulation-in-a-star.md). Writing the nonadvective [angular momentum transport](angular-momentum-transport.md) flux as $\mathbf F_J$ gives the displayed balance. Thus stresses that maintain [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md) also drive a meridional response. The [stellar thermal-wind balance](stellar-thermal-wind-balance.md) supplies a separate leading force constraint, not a replacement for this torque equation.
