# Air track

↑ **Parent:** [Damping measurement from free decay](damping-measurement-from-free-decay.md)

An [air track](air-track.md) supports a cart on a cushion of air to reduce contact [friction](friction.md). It provides a nearly one-dimensional platform for a [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md); residual [drag](drag-physics.md) remains part of the baseline [damping](damping.md).
