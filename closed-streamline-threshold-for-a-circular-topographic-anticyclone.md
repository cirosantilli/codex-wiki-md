# Closed-streamline threshold for a circular topographic anticyclone

↑ **Parent:** [Circular topographic anticyclone](circular-topographic-anticyclone.md)

For the [circular topographic anticyclone](circular-topographic-anticyclone.md) in a positive uniform current $U$, the opposing disturbance speed is at most $Ca=f\epsilon a/2$. Below the displayed threshold $u$ remains positive everywhere, so no [streamline](streamline.md) can close. Above it an interior [stagnation point](stagnation-point.md) at $(0,-U/C)$ is a center, while the exterior point $(0,-Ca^2/U)$ is a saddle. At equality the two meet at the rim. This is the onset criterion; once closed contours form, the [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) of trapped fluid is not determined solely by its present upstream conditions.
