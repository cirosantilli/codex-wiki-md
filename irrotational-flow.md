# Irrotational flow

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrotational_flow)

Use [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) to identify where a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) can describe the [velocity field](velocity-field.md) locally. An [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) has $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u=\mathbf0$ throughout the fluid region. For a continuously differentiable [velocity field](velocity-field.md), the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) supplies a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) on sufficiently small neighborhoods. A global single-valued [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) requires zero [circulation](circulation-physics.md) around every [closed curve](closed-curve.md); a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) is a sufficient domain assumption.

The potential [line vortex](line-vortex.md) $\mathbf u=K\mathbf e_\theta/r$ is an [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) on the punctured plane, or on an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $a<r<b$ with $a>0$, even though its [pathlines](pathline.md) are circular. Every sufficiently small loop bounding a disk in that fluid has zero [circulation](circulation-physics.md), by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). A loop winding once counterclockwise around the excluded origin has [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $2\pi K$. Thus local [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) and nonzero [circulation](circulation-physics.md) around a hole are compatible: the enclosing disk passes outside the domain of the [velocity field](velocity-field.md).

An [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) is a kinematic condition on a [velocity field](velocity-field.md), independent of whether a particular model neglects [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) or assumes [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md). Adding [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) makes the local [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) satisfy the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md). This reduces the number of unknowns and makes [potential flow](potential-flow.md) a powerful starting point for flow around bodies and for [gravity waves](gravity-wave-split.md). [A. H. Techet, MIT Hydrodynamics reading 4, pp. 1–2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-016-hydrodynamics-13-012-fall-2005/c472432debcf6ee250209b68cf18cc12_2005reading4.pdf)
