# Fill-in

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse Gaussian elimination](sparse-gaussian-elimination.md)

[Fill-in](fill-in.md) consists of factor entries that become nonzero despite being zero in the original [sparse matrix](sparse-matrix.md). In symmetric elimination these are the added edges of the [elimination graph](elimination-graph.md). Removing a high-degree vertex can create many fill entries, whereas a [perfect elimination ordering](perfect-elimination-ordering.md) creates none. Symbolic fill predicts possible nonzeros before floating-point arithmetic; special cancellation can reduce the numerical pattern.
