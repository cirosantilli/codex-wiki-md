# Universal formula

↑ **Parent:** [Universal quantification](universal-quantification.md)

A [universal formula](universal-formula.md) has a block of [universal quantifiers](universal-quantification.md) followed by a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md). Empty blocks are usually permitted. Truth transfers from a [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) to a [substructure of a first-order structure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) for parameters in that substructure: every tuple in the smaller domain is available in the larger domain, and quantifier-free truth is preserved and reflected by the inclusion. This is the dual preservation direction to the forward preservation of an [existential formula](existential-formula.md) under a [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md).
