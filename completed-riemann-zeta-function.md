# Completed Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

The completed zeta function $\Lambda(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)$ satisfies $\Lambda(s)=\Lambda(1-s)$ and has poles at zero and one. It is also sometimes denoted $\xi(s)$; this differs from the entire xi function obtained by multiplying by $s(s-1)/2$. The distinction matters in the [constant term of a nonholomorphic Eisenstein series](constant-term-of-a-nonholomorphic-eisenstein-series.md).
