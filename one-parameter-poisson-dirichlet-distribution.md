# One-parameter Poisson-Dirichlet distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Dirichlet distribution](poisson-dirichlet-distribution.md)

Take a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) on $(0,\infty)$ with intensity $\theta e^{-u}du/u$ and arrange its atoms as $J_1>J_2>\cdots$. The sum $T=\sum_iJ_i$ is finite and positive [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), has [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with shape $\theta$ and rate one, and the law of $(J_i/T)$ is $PD(\theta)$. The order is decreasing, so the first component is the largest; a size-biased selection of a component has a different law.
