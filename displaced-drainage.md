# Displaced drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

A channel system whose correlated reaches have been separated by [fault slip](fault-slip.md). A fault-parallel connecting reach, abandoned reach or abrupt bend can reveal repeated [strike-slip faulting](strike-slip-faulting.md). Reliable displacement measurement requires correlation of the same original channel rather than assigning every bend to tectonic displacement.
