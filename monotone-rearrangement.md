# Monotone rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

For an [atomless measure](non-atomic-measure.md) $\mu$ on $\mathbb R$ with [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$, the monotone transport to a target with [quantile function](quantile-function.md) $G^{-1}$ is $T=G^{-1}\circ F$, defined $\mu$-almost everywhere. It minimizes well-defined costs $d(x-y)$ for [convex](convex-function.md) continuous $d$. With atoms in the source, the common-quantile [transport plan](transport-plan.md) $(F^{-1},G^{-1})_\#\mathcal U(0,1)$ remains available but need not be induced by a map.
