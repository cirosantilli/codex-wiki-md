# Migratory insertion

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

In [migratory insertion](migratory-insertion.md), adjacent ligands combine while remaining attached to the [metal](metal.md) in the product. In [alkene](alkene.md) insertion into M–R, R forms a bond to one [alkene](alkene.md) [carbon](carbon.md) while the other [carbon](carbon.md) acquires the M–C bond. [metal](metal.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) need not change.
