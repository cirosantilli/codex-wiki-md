# Reading comprehension

↑ **Parent:** [Reading](reading.md)

Constructing an integrated interpretation of a text, including its explicit content and relevant inferences. Comprehension can depend on [word decoding](word-decoding.md), vocabulary, [working memory](working-memory.md) and monitoring; the contribution of one should not be inferred by ignoring the others.[Journal of Educational Psychology 96, 31–42; longitudinal assessments at eight, nine and eleven years, concurrent prediction models](children-s-reading-comprehension-ability-concurrent-prediction-by-working-memory-verbal-ability-and-component-skills-by-kate-cain-and-others.md)
