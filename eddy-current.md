# Eddy current

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddy_current)

An [eddy current](eddy-current.md) is a circulating [electric current](electric-current.md) induced within an [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) by changing [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), as described by [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). Its [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) opposes the inducing change according to [Lenz's law](lenz-s-law.md); [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) converts electrical [energy](energy-split.md) into [heat](heat-split.md).
