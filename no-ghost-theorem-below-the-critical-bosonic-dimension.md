# No-ghost theorem below the critical bosonic dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant quantization of the bosonic string](covariant-quantization-of-the-bosonic-string.md)

For integer target dimension $2\le d\le26$, intercept $a=1$ and ordinary nonzero momentum, the [old covariant string quantization](old-covariant-string-quantization.md) conditions leave a positive-semidefinite physical inner product. Quotienting its physical [null string states](null-string-state.md) removes gauge degeneracy. A [DDF operator](ddf-operator.md) basis displays $d-2$ positive transverse oscillators; below dimension 26 the remaining physical longitudinal sector can be organized as a unitary [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) sector with central charge $26-d$ and highest weight zero. At $d=26$ this extra sector becomes null and the quotient reduces to the 24 transverse oscillators. The result concerns negative-norm states, not the removal of the bosonic [tachyon](tachyon.md), and it does not itself cancel the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) of a subcritical matter theory.
