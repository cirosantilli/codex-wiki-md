# Muscle moment arm

↑ **Parent:** [Muscle force](muscle-force.md)

The perpendicular distance from a [muscle force](muscle-force.md)'s line of action to the relevant axis of an [anatomical joint](anatomical-joint.md). It can change with posture; multiplying it by the [muscle force](muscle-force.md) gives the corresponding [torque](torque.md).
