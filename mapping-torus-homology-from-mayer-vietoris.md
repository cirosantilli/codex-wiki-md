<h1 id="mapping-torus-homology-from-mayer-vietoris">Mapping-torus homology from Mayer–Vietoris</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md)

For a continuous self-map $f:X\to X$, the [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) has the [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md) even when $f$ is not a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). Cover the time circle by a seam neighborhood and an interval away from the seam. Their inverse images retract to $X$: the seam neighborhood is a [mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md), and the other is a product. Their intersection retracts to two copies of $X$. The [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) map on [homology](homology-split.md) is, after consistent choices of signs,

$$
(a,b)\longmapsto(a+f_*b,-a-b).
$$

Invertible changes of coordinates reduce it to the direct sum of $\operatorname{id}-f_*$ and an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Canceling the isomorphism summands gives the [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md). This proof does not assume a [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) structure for a noninvertible $f$.
