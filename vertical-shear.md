# Vertical shear

↑ **Parent:** [Shear flow](shear-flow.md)

Variation of horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) with height. In a rotating balanced [stratification](density-stratification.md), [thermal-wind balance](thermal-wind.md) relates this [vertical shear](vertical-shear.md) to a horizontal [buoyancy gradient](buoyancy-gradient.md). The sign reverses when changing from upward height to downward-increasing [pressure coordinate](pressure-coordinate.md).
