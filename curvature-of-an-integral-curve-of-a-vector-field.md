# Curvature of an integral curve of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Integral curve of a vector field](integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md)

For a [continuously differentiable](continuously-differentiable-function.md) nonzero [vector field](vector-field.md) $F$, the [curvature of a space curve](curvature-of-a-space-curve.md) following its direction is

$$
\kappa=\frac{|F\times(F\cdot\nabla)F|}{|F|^3}.
$$

Take the [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) $t=\pm F/|F|$ and differentiate with respect to [arc length](arc-length.md). The derivative of $|F|^{-1}$ is parallel to $F$ and disappears from $t\times t'$. Since $t\cdot t'=0$, we have $|t\times t'|=|t'|=\kappa$, proving the formula. It remains valid with zero curvature, even where the usual [Frenet frame](frenet-frame.md) is undefined.
