# Z-selective Wittig olefination

↑ **Parent:** [Wittig reaction](wittig-reaction.md)

Conditions favouring a Z alkene in a [Wittig reaction](wittig-reaction.md), often using a nonstabilized [ylide](ylide.md) under kinetic, salt-controlled conditions. Isolation or verification is still required when complete stereoselectivity is not established.
