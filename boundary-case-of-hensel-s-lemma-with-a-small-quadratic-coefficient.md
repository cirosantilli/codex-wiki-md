<h1 id="boundary-case-of-hensel-s-lemma-with-a-small-quadratic-coefficient">Boundary case of Hensel's lemma with a small quadratic coefficient</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Strong form of Hensel lemma](strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md)

Let $R$ be a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md), $f\in R[X]$, and $d=|f'(a)|$ satisfy $0<d<1$ and $|f(a)|=d^2$. Suppose the second Taylor coefficient of $f$ at every point of $R$ has norm below one. One [Newton iteration over a valued field](newton-iteration-over-a-valued-field.md) step has correction $h=-f(a)/f'(a)$ of norm $d$. Its residual has quadratic term of norm strictly below $d^2$, and all higher terms have norm at most $d^3<d^2$. The derivative changes by a term of norm below $d$, so its norm remains $d$. The [strong form of Hensel lemma](strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md) therefore applies from the new point, producing a root within distance $d$ of the original point. The second Taylor coefficient is $f''/2$ when that quotient is meaningful, or the division-free binomial-coefficient Taylor expression in arbitrary characteristic. A Newton sequence that reaches a root is thereafter constant; residuals decrease strictly only before that event.
