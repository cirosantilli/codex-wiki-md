# Induced worldvolume metric

↑ **Parent:** [Worldvolume](worldvolume.md)

The induced worldvolume metric is the pullback of the target [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) by a [brane](brane.md) embedding. For a nondegenerate timelike embedding, $\sqrt{-\det h}$ is its invariant [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) volume density. The $p=1$ specialization is the [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md).
