# Crossed random effects

↑ **Parent:** [Random effect](random-effect.md)

Random effects are crossed when an observation can belong to levels of two grouping factors without one grouping factor being nested within the other. Independent location intercepts and incubator slopes, for example, contribute separate terms to the observation [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md).
