# Vector-bundle-valued differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

A smooth section of $\bigwedge^k(T^*X\otimes\mathbb C)\otimes E$ is a differential form with values in the complex [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$. A [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) extends to these forms by the graded Leibniz rule.
