# Mass-to-light ratio of a power-law stellar population

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md)

Write $x=m/M_\odot$, the [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) as $dN=A x^{-\alpha}dx$, and a main-sequence [mass-luminosity relation](mass-luminosity-relation.md) as $L/L_\odot=x^\beta$. On $a<x<b$, the [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) in solar units is

$$
\frac{\Upsilon}{M_\odot/L_\odot}=\frac{\int_a^b x^{1-\alpha}dx}{\int_a^b x^{\beta-\alpha}dx}.
$$

For $\alpha=\beta=3$ this is exactly $1/(ab)$. The result concerns the specified unevolved [stellar population](stellar-population.md); [stellar remnants](stellar-remnant.md), evolved luminous stars and cooling substellar objects require different accounting.
