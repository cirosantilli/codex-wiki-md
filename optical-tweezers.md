# Optical tweezers

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

An [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) uses the forces from a focused light field to confine or manipulate a small particle. In an overdamped one-dimensional model, a translating localized potential exerts $F(x-v_Tt)$ and balances [linear drag](linear-drag.md).
