<h1 id="hilbert-samuel-multiplicity">Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert–Samuel function](hilbert-samuel-function.md)

If $M$ has [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) $d$, the coefficient of $n^d$ in its [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md) is $e_I(M)/d!$. The positive integer $e_I(M)$ is the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity of $M$ with respect to $I$.
