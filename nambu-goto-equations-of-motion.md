<h1 id="nambu-goto-equations-of-motion">Nambu–Goto equations of motion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md)

For a nondegenerate [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md) $g_{ab}=\partial_aX\cdot\partial_bX$, variation gives $\partial_a(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{ab}\partial_bX^m)=0$. In [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md) this is the two-dimensional [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), supplemented by the [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md).
