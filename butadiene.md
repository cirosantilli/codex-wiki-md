# Butadiene

↑ **Parent:** [Optical material](optical-material.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butadiene)

Here [butadiene](butadiene.md) denotes the 1,3-butadiene [molecule](molecule.md), with formula $\mathrm{C}_4\mathrm{H}_6$. It supplies a concrete [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) absorption example: a [photon](photon.md) can promote an [Electron](electron.md) between [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md) with the appropriate [energy](energy.md) separation. [John McMurry, ultraviolet absorption by butadiene](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/14-7-ultraviolet-spectroscopy).
