# Resource congestion price

↑ **Parent:** [Congestion control](congestion-control.md)

A resource congestion price signals scarcity at a capacity-constrained link. Under [multiplicative resource-price dynamics](multiplicative-resource-price-dynamics.md), its increase indicates excess demand and its decrease indicates spare capacity. A route pays the sum of these prices, its [route congestion price](route-congestion-price.md). A zero resource congestion price at an optimum satisfies [complementary slackness](complementary-slackness.md) with possible spare capacity.
