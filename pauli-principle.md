# Pauli principle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion](fermion.md)

The complete [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) of identical [fermions](fermion.md) changes sign on exchange of two particles, including their spatial and internal coordinates. The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) follows because an antisymmetrized product with two identical complete [spin orbitals](spin-orbital.md) is zero. Antisymmetry concerns the total state; a spin-antisymmetric [singlet state](singlet-state.md) can have a symmetric spatial factor.
