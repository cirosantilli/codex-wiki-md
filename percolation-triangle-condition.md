# Percolation triangle condition

↑ **Parent:** [Bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md)

Here $\tau(x,y)=P_{p_c}(x\leftrightarrow y)$ is critical two-point connectivity. The condition controls the intersection effects that spoil a branching approximation. It is a sufficient criterion for important [mean-field percolation exponents](mean-field-percolation-exponents.md), including the order-parameter, [percolation susceptibility](percolation-susceptibility.md) and cluster-tail exponents. Establishing detailed spatial asymptotics is a further task, commonly addressed by [lace expansion](lace-expansion.md).
