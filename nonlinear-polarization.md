# Nonlinear polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

[Nonlinear polarization](nonlinear-polarization.md) is the part of an induced electric [polarization](polarization-waves.md) nonlinear in the driving [electric field](electric-field-split.md). A quadratic response multiplies field components, creating sums and differences of their [frequencies](frequency.md); [second-harmonic generation](second-harmonic-generation.md) is the equal-frequency sum case. Conversion strength also depends on [phase matching](phase-matching.md) and the material response.
