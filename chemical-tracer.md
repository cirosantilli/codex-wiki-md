# Chemical tracer

↑ **Parent:** [Analytical chemistry](analytical-chemistry.md)

A [chemical tracer](chemical-tracer.md) is a constituent whose measured [concentration](concentration.md) reveals transport or other processes. An ocean [box model](box-model.md) can follow a [chemical tracer](chemical-tracer.md) through [ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md), with additional source and sink terms if the constituent is produced or removed internally. A passive, conservative [chemical tracer](chemical-tracer.md) changes only by transport and mixing.
