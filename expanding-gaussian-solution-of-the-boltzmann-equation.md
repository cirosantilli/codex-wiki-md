# Expanding Gaussian solution of the Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md)

This spatially nonconstant [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md) is constant along free [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md). Its collision term vanishes by the [collision invariants](collision-invariant.md), so it solves the full [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md). It is positive, rapidly decaying and integrable in [phase space](phase-space.md). Completing its velocity square gives local mean $\alpha tx/(\beta+\alpha t^2)$ and local variance proportional to $(\beta+\alpha t^2)^{-1}$, while its spatial profile broadens with time.
