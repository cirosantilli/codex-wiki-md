# GRW flash ontology

↑ **Parent:** [Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber theory](ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory.md)

A version of the [GRW model](ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory.md) whose material events are collapse centres in [spacetime](spacetime.md). A macroscopic object is represented by a pattern of such events, not by particle [trajectories](trajectory.md) between flashes. Exact flash locations coexist with the absence of a position assigned at every instant.
