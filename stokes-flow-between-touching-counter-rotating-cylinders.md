# Stokes flow between touching counter-rotating cylinders

↑ **Parent:** [Biharmonic stream function for planar Stokes flow](biharmonic-stream-function-for-planar-stokes-flow.md)

An inner cylinder of radius $a$ centered at $(0,a)$ and an outer cylinder of radius $2a$ centered at $(0,2a)$ have polar boundaries $r=2a\sin\theta$ and $r=4a\sin\theta$. If their angular velocities are $\Omega$ and $-\Omega/4$, respectively, the no-slip [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) between them has streamfunction

$$
\psi=\frac{a\Omega\sin\theta}{r}
(r-2a\sin\theta)(r-4a\sin\theta).
$$

Its unique interior stagnation point is $(x,y)=(0,2\sqrt2a)$, and its nonzero streamlines are closed curves around that point.
