# First Hopf boundary of scalar delayed negative feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Delay differential equation](delay-differential-equation.md)

For nonnegative instantaneous damping $\beta$ and positive delayed gain $\gamma$, [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) roots obey $\lambda+\beta+\gamma e^{-\lambda}=0$. There is no nonnegative real root. The first imaginary crossing has $\omega\in[\pi/2,\pi)$ and $\beta=-\omega\cot\omega$, $\gamma=\omega/\sin\omega$. In particular stability is retained below this first boundary and $\gamma=\pi/2$ is exact only at $\beta=0$. For small positive $\beta$, the critical gain is $\pi/2+2\beta/\pi+O(\beta^2)$ and the scaled [period](period-of-a-function.md) tends to four. If the [time](time-in-physics.md) delay is dimensional $\tau$, that [period](period-of-a-function.md) is multiplied by $\tau$.
