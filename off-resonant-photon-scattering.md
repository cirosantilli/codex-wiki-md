# Off-resonant photon scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md)

A detuned optical drive still admixes a decaying [excited state](excited-state.md). Its spontaneous scattering rate is of order $\Gamma|\Omega|^2/(4\Delta^2)$ for weak far-detuned driving with coupling $\hbar\Omega/2$. Increasing detuning at fixed optical power reduces both unwanted scattering and useful coherent coupling.
