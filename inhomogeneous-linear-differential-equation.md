# Inhomogeneous linear differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md)

An [inhomogeneous linear differential equation](inhomogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) has a nonzero [forcing term](forcing-term.md). Its [general solution](general-solution.md) is one [particular solution](particular-solution.md) plus the [general solution](general-solution.md) of the associated [homogeneous linear differential equation](homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md).
