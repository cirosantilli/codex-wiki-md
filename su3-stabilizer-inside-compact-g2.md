# SU3 stabilizer inside compact G2

↑ **Parent:** [G2 (mathematics)](g2-mathematics.md)

On $\mathbb C^3\oplus\mathbb R$, take $\omega=(i/2)\sum_jdz_j\wedge d\bar z_j$ and $\Omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$. The [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) $SU(3)$ preserves the [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) $\omega$ by [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) and preserves $\Omega$ by its unit [determinant](determinant.md). Fixing $y$ therefore embeds it in the compact [G2](g2-mathematics.md) stabilizer of $\phi$. The complexified seven-dimensional [representation](group-representation.md) restricts as $\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1$.
