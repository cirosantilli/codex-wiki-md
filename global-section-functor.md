# Global section functor

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

The global section functor sends a sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$ to $\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$. It is left exact, and its right derived functors are [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md).
