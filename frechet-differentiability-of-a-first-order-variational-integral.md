<h1 id="frechet-differentiability-of-a-first-order-variational-integral">Fréchet differentiability of a first-order variational integral</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md)

For $I(u)=\int_a^bf(x,u,u')dx$ on $W^{1,\infty}$, [continuity](continuous-function.md) of $f,f_u,f_v$ on $[a,b]\times\mathbb R^2$ gives a bounded [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md) in the displayed form. Fixing $u$ and restricting $\|w\|_{W^{1,\infty}}$ to a bounded neighborhood confines all intermediate pairs $(u+tw,u'+tw')$ to a compact range up to null sets. [Uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) of the [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) bounds the integrated first-order remainder by $(b-a)\omega(\|w\|)(\|w\|_\infty+\|w'\|_\infty)$, where $\omega(s)\to0$. This is little-oh of the Sobolev [norm](norm.md). The result needs no continuous derivative representative for $u$; essential boundedness suffices.
