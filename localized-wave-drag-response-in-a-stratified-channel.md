# Localized wave-drag response in a stratified channel

↑ **Parent:** [Eliassen equation for residual circulation](eliassen-equation-for-residual-circulation.md)

For a sinusoidal [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md) whose vertical divergence is constant in $-D<z<D$ and zero elsewhere, the [residual mean circulation](residual-mean-circulation.md) is determined by a forced one-dimensional modified Helmholtz equation. With $v^*=C'(z)\sin(\pi y/L)$ and $w^*=-(\pi/L)C(z)\cos(\pi y/L)$, a forcing proportional to $-G_0\sin(\pi y/L)/(2D)$ gives

$$
C(z)=\frac{G_0}{4f_0Dm}\left[e^{-m|z-D|}-e^{-m|z+D|}\right].
$$

The response is uniquely selected by decay of the residual circulation at both vertical infinities. An [Eulerian mean](eulerian-mean-flow.md) vertical flow can persist in the undamped wave region even where this residual response vanishes.
