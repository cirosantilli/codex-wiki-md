# Standard maximum form of a linear program

↑ **Parent:** [Linear programming](linear-programming-split.md)

This convention for [standard maximum form of a linear program](standard-maximum-form-of-a-linear-program.md) requires a maximizing [linear function](linear-function.md), upper-bound [linear inequalities](linear-inequality.md), and nonnegative variables. An unrestricted variable becomes the difference of two nonnegative variables; a lower-bound inequality changes sign. The corresponding [dual linear program](dual-linear-program.md) is $\min\{b^Ty:A^Ty\geq c,\ y\geq0\}$. Adding nonnegative [slack variables](slack-variable.md) instead gives the equivalent equality-constraint standard form. Since standard-form terminology varies, state the chosen convention.
