# Minimal prime over an ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal prime ideal](minimal-prime-ideal.md)

A [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P$ is minimal over an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ if $I\subseteq P$ and no smaller [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) contains $I$. The definition also applies to two-sided [prime ideals of a noncommutative ring](prime-ideal-of-a-noncommutative-ring.md).
