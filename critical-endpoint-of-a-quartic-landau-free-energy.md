# Critical endpoint of a quartic Landau free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For the stable scalar [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) $f=b\lambda^4+c\lambda^3+a\lambda^2+d\lambda$, $b>0$, a stable zero-curvature equilibrium must have $f'=f''=f'''=0$. Solving these equations gives

$$
\lambda_c=-\frac c{4b},\qquad a_c=\frac{3c^2}{8b},\qquad d_c=\frac{c^3}{16b^2}.
$$

At this point $f=f(\lambda_c)+b(\lambda-\lambda_c)^4$. Varying two control parameters allows a line of [first-order phase transitions](first-order-phase-transition.md) to terminate here. A [spinodal point](spinodal-point.md) satisfies zero curvature but need not satisfy the third-derivative condition or remain a local minimum.
