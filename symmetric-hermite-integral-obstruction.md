# Symmetric Hermite integral obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence theory](transcendence-theory.md)

Suppose nonzero [algebraic numbers](algebraic-number.md) $\beta_j$ form a Galois-stable set, with Galois-invariant [integer](integer.md) weights $b_j$, and $k\ne0$ is an [integer](integer.md). Choose $P\in\mathbb Z[X]$ vanishing at all $\beta_j$ with $P(0)\ne0$, and a positive [integer](integer.md) $c$ making all $c\beta_j$ [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md). For a large prime $p$, put $D=(1+\deg P)p-1$ and $f_p(X)=c^DX^{p-1}P(X)^p/(p-1)!$, and let $F_p$ be the sum of its derivatives. The weighted sum $kF_p(0)+\sum b_jF_p(\beta_j)$ is an [integer](integer.md), nonzero modulo $p$ when $p\nmid kcP(0)$. If the displayed exponential expression vanished, integrating $(e^{-z}F_p(z))'=-e^{-z}f_p(z)$ along the finitely many segments to $\beta_j$ would bound that nonzero [integer](integer.md) by $C^p/(p-1)!\to0$. This contradiction proves the obstruction and supplies direct proofs for $e$ and $\pi$.
