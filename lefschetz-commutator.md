# Lefschetz commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

In complex dimension $n$, the [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md) and [adjoint Lefschetz operator](adjoint-lefschetz-operator.md) satisfy $[\Lambda,L]=(n-k)\operatorname{id}$ on degree $k$. In middle degree, this yields $\|L\alpha\|^2=\|\Lambda\alpha\|^2$.
