# Acute tryptophan depletion

↑ **Parent:** [Monoamine hypothesis of depression](monoamine-hypothesis-of-depression.md)

An experimental manipulation intended to reduce the availability of the [serotonin](serotonin.md) precursor [tryptophan](tryptophan.md). Effects in a previously treated remitted sample test dependence of that state on precursor availability; they do not directly measure a universal baseline serotonin deficit.
