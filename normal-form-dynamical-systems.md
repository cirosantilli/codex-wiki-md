# Normal form (dynamical systems)

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

A simplified local vector field obtained by smooth changes of state variables, and sometimes time or parameter, retaining the terms that cannot be removed at the required order. Near a simple zero eigenvalue, reduction to a [centre manifold](center-manifold.md) can give the transcritical form $\dot v=\epsilon v-v^2$ or a reflection-symmetric pitchfork form $\dot x=\epsilon x\pm x^3$. The nonzero leading coefficients and permitted coordinate changes are part of the classification.
