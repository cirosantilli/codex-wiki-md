# Exponential Chebyshev system

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev system](chebyshev-system.md)

Exponentials with distinct real exponents form a [Chebyshev system](chebyshev-system.md) on every nondegenerate real interval. For an inductive proof, multiply a candidate combination by $e^{-\lambda_0x}$. If it has $n+1$ zeros, [Rolle's theorem](rolle-theorem.md) gives at least $n$ zeros of its derivative, a combination of the $n$ exponentials with exponents $\lambda_i-\lambda_0$, $i>0$. The inductive zero bound forces all those coefficients to vanish. The remaining constant also vanishes, a contradiction.
