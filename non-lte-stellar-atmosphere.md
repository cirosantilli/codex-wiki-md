# Non-LTE stellar atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md)

When radiative rates compete with or dominate collisions, level populations need statistical equilibrium coupled to [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), rather than thermal population laws at the local [temperature](temperature.md). Radiation from other depths can make the local radiation field differ from the [Planck function](planck-function.md). This changes ionization and spectral-line strengths, so a geometrically thin atmosphere may still require non-LTE abundance modelling.
