# Fixed-charge Reissner-Nordstrom temperature maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom Hawking temperature](reissner-nordstrom-hawking-temperature.md)

Set $y=r_+/|Q|\geq1$. Then $M/|Q|=(y+y^{-1})/2$ and $|Q|T=(y^{-1}-y^{-3})/(4\pi)$. Differentiation gives a unique maximum at $y=\sqrt3$, proving the displayed [mass](mass.md) and [temperature](temperature.md). The curve is zero at extremality and asymptotic to $1/(8\pi M)$ at large [mass](mass.md).
