<h1 id="brun-s-theorem">Brun's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Twin prime](twin-prime.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brun's_theorem)

The sum of reciprocals of [twin primes](twin-prime.md) converges, whether or not there are infinitely many [twin primes](twin-prime.md). The [twin-prime upper bound from a quadratic sieve](twin-prime-upper-bound-from-a-quadratic-sieve.md) and [reciprocal-sum convergence from a counting bound](reciprocal-sum-convergence-from-a-counting-bound.md) prove it. This contrasts with the [Euler proof that the sum of reciprocals of primes diverges](euler-proof-that-the-sum-of-reciprocals-of-primes-diverges.md).
