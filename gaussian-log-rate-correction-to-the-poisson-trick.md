# Gaussian log-rate correction to the Poisson trick

↑ **Parent:** [Flat log-rate marginalization gives the multinomial likelihood](flat-log-rate-marginalization-gives-the-multinomial-likelihood.md)

A mean-zero [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) prior of variance $s^2$ on the log baseline multiplies the multinomial kernel by the displayed correction, up to a constant independent of coefficients. The factor is not constant in $S$, so the posterior equivalence is approximate. [Dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) makes it tend to one as $s^2$ tends to infinity.
