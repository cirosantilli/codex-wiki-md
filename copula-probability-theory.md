# Copula (probability theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Joint distribution function](joint-distribution-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copula_(probability_theory))

A copula is a joint [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) on a unit cube whose one-dimensional [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) are [uniform distributions](continuous-uniform-distribution.md). Applying each continuous marginal distribution to its random variable produces uniform marginals by the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md); their copula records the remaining dependence. Independent uniform coordinates and one common uniform coordinate give different copulas with the same marginals.
