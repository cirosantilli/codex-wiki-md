# Thiol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiol)

A [thiol](thiol.md) is a [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) of the form $\mathrm{R-SH}$, with an organic group $R$ attached to [sulfur](sulfur.md). The [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) is $\mathrm{R-S^-}$; [cysteine](cysteine.md) and [mercaptoethanol](2-mercaptoethanol.md) supply examples of [thiol](thiol.md) groups. Two [thiol](thiol.md) groups can undergo [oxidation](oxidation.md) to form a [disulfide bond](disulfide-bond.md). [IUPAC Gold Book: thiols](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/T06359)
