# Rayleigh centrifugal stability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

For inviscid cylindrical rotation without a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), an axisymmetric radial interchange conserves each parcel's [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $l=r^2\Omega$. Its restoring coefficient is $\kappa^2=r^{-3}d(l^2)/dr$. Increasing $l^2$ gives centrifugal stability; decreasing $l^2$ gives an exponentially growing interchange mode when radial displacement is allowed by the boundaries. A vanishing derivative is marginal, not a strictly restoring condition.
