# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the history of mathematics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Answers to all six extract commentaries and all three essay alternatives in the [history of mathematics course of the University of Oxford](../history-of-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 6 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 245–248; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=245). Questions 1–6 belong to Section A and questions 7–9 to Section B; the examination required a selection, but every alternative is answered here. The commentaries identify and analyse the sources without reproducing the extracts. Modern notation below explains the historical arguments; it is not attributed to the original authors.
