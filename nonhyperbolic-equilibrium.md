# Nonhyperbolic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

An equilibrium is nonhyperbolic when its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has at least one [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) on the [imaginary axis](imaginary-axis.md). Linearization alone then need not determine the local dynamics, and a [center manifold](center-manifold.md) often supplies the relevant reduction.
