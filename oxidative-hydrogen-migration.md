# Oxidative hydrogen migration

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidative addition](oxidative-addition.md)

A metal-assisted pathway in which cleavage of a substrate C-H bond transfers H to an electrophilic ligand while new metal-carbon bonding forms. In directed Fischer-carbene insertion, it can precede carbon-carbon [reductive elimination](reductive-elimination.md).
