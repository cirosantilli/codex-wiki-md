# Compressed supersymmetric spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

A spectrum with small [mass](mass.md) splittings among particles in a decay chain. Small available decay [energy](energy-split.md) makes visible daughters soft, motivating recoil triggers, soft-lepton reconstruction and lifetime-sensitive searches rather than a universal hard-object cut.
