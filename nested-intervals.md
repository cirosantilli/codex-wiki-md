# Nested intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Interval (mathematics)](interval-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nested_intervals)

A nested family of [real intervals](interval-mathematics.md) satisfies $I_{n+1}\subseteq I_n$. The [nested interval theorem](nested-interval-theorem.md) ensures a common point for nonempty closed bounded members; open intervals can instead have empty intersection.
