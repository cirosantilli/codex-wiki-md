# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md)

The one-dimensional semigroup associated with the standard [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) has invariant standard [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md) and generator $Lf=f''-xf'$. Its [Mehler formula for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](mehler-formula-for-the-ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md) makes [positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) and invariance explicit. The normalized [Probabilists' Hermite polynomials](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) diagonalize it, with $P_th_n=e^{-nt}h_n$. It has [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) one and satisfies the sharp [Gaussian Poincaré inequality](gaussian-poincare-inequality.md).
