<h1 id="global-potential-for-cohomologous-kahler-forms">Global potential for cohomologous Kähler forms</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the difference of cohomologous [Kähler forms](kahler-form.md) is a real, [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) exact $(1,1)$-form. The [ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md) gives $\widetilde\omega-\omega=\partial\bar\partial h$. Reality implies $\partial\bar\partial\bar h=-(\widetilde\omega-\omega)$, so $f=(h-\bar h)/(2i)$ is real and has the displayed potential equation. The difference of two real potentials is a [pluriharmonic function](pluriharmonic-function.md), hence constant on each connected component. On a connected manifold the only ambiguity is one additive constant.
