# Photodissociation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photodissociation)

[Photodissociation](photodissociation.md) separates a [molecule](molecule.md) into fragments after [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md). For an isolated parent with a single absorbed [photon](photon.md), [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
h\nu+E_{\rm initial,int}=D_0+E_{\rm products,int}+E_{\rm recoil}.
$$

Internal excitation [energies](energy.md) here are relative to the parent and product ground states used to define $D_0$; $E_{\rm recoil}$ is the total product translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in the parent's [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame. Measuring one product's [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is insufficient by itself. For two fragments of [masses](mass.md) $m_A,m_B$, [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) gives $E_{\rm recoil}=E_A(m_A+m_B)/m_B$ when $A$ is detected. Unknown product excitation can conceal part of the available [energy](energy.md).
