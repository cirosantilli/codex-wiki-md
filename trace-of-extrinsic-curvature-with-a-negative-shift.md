# Trace of extrinsic curvature with a negative shift

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature with a negative shift](extrinsic-curvature-with-a-negative-shift.md)

For the threading convention $dx^i-N^i dt$ and $K_{ij}=-(\dot\gamma_{ij}+D_iN_j+D_jN_i)/(2N)$, contract with $\gamma^{ij}$. [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) gives $\gamma^{ij}D_iN_j=D_iN^i$, and the [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) gives $\gamma^{ij}\dot\gamma_{ij}=\partial_t\log\det\gamma$. With zero shift, $-K/3=(3N)^{-1}\partial_t\log\sqrt\gamma$ is the [local volume Hubble parameter](local-volume-hubble-parameter.md). Reversing the shift convention reverses both the normal's spatial component and the shift terms; the two conventions must not be mixed.
