# Lagrange multiplier for antisymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md)

To vary an [antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md) without treating its entries as independent physical variables, temporarily allow an unrestricted array $M^{\mu\nu}$ and add $\lambda_{\mu\nu}(M^{\mu\nu}+M^{\nu\mu})/2$ to the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md), with symmetric [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) $\lambda$. Variation of $\lambda$ enforces antisymmetry. The antisymmetric part of the unrestricted $M$ equation eliminates $\lambda$ and gives the physical [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md). Equivalently, vary only the six independent entries in four spacetime dimensions. Quadratic contractions must be differentiated before applying antisymmetry, or their factors and signs can be lost.
