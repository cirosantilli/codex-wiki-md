# Chain rule

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_rule)

If $f$ is differentiable at $a$ and $g$ is differentiable at $f(a)$, then

$$
(g\circ f)'(a)=g'(f(a))f'(a).
$$
