# Rarefied-gas nanoparticle drag

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md)

Drag on a small particle when the gas [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) is not negligible relative to its size. Continuum no-slip Stokes friction then needs a slip or kinetic correction depending on pressure and gas-surface interactions.
