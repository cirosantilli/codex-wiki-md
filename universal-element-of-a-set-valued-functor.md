# Universal element of a set-valued functor

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a functor](representation-of-a-functor.md)

The universal element in a [representation of a functor](representation-of-a-functor.md) is the image of $1_A$ under the representing natural isomorphism. Any two universal elements induce unique mutually inverse maps between their representing objects, so representations are unique up to unique compatible isomorphism.
