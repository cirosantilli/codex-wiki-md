# Radial decreasing kernel domination by the maximal function

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md)

If $k(x)=\phi(|x|)\geq0$ is decreasing in radius and has integral one, then $|k_t*f(x)|\leq M f(x)$, where $k_t(x)=t^{-d}k(x/t)$ and $M$ is the centered [Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md) of $|f|\,dx$. The [layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md) expresses $k_t$ as a positive mixture of ball indicators; each corresponding integral is bounded by its ball volume times $Mf(x)$. Integrating these volumes gives exactly one. This applies to the [Poisson kernel for the upper half-space](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-space.md) and supplies a [weak type (1,1)](weak-type-1-1.md) domination for its boundary convergence.
