# Semantic forcing relation

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing theorem](forcing-theorem.md)

For a [countable transitive model](countable-transitive-model.md) $M$, semantic forcing declares $p\Vdash\varphi$ when every [generic filter](generic-filter.md) $G$ over $M$ containing $p$ gives $M[G]\models\varphi$.
