# Cylindrical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md)

The curved [surface](topological-surface-split.md) of a [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md), excluding any flat end caps. A [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) of a radius-$a$ [cylindrical surface](cylindrical-surface.md) is $\mathbf R(\theta,z)=(a\cos\theta,a\sin\theta,z)$, with [surface area element](surface-area-element.md) $a\,d\theta\,dz$.
