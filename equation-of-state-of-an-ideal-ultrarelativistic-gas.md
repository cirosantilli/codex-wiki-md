# Equation of state of an ideal ultrarelativistic gas

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of state](equation-of-state.md)

An isotropic ideal gas whose particles obey the ultrarelativistic dispersion $\varepsilon=pc$ satisfies

$$
pV=\frac E3.
$$
