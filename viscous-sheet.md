# Viscous sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

A viscous sheet is a thin fluid layer whose motion is controlled by [viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md) and whose two broad faces can move freely. Unlike a no-slip wall film, an extensional sheet with shear-free faces has leading tangential velocity nearly uniform across its thickness. The in-plane [stresses](stress.md) depend on extension in both horizontal directions.
