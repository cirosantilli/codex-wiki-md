# Redshift shell volume at the present epoch

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving volume](comoving-volume.md)

On a present FLRW spatial slice, a shell has volume $R_0^3S^2(\chi)d\chi\,d\Omega$. The radial light-ray relation gives $d\chi/dz=c/(R_0H)$, yielding the displayed expression in terms of [transverse comoving distance](transverse-comoving-distance.md). At emission the proper volume is smaller by $(1+z)^3$. For a conserved comoving population its proper [density](density.md) is greater by the same factor, so either epoch gives the same count.
