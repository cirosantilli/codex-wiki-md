# Critical energy of an electromagnetic shower

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic shower](electromagnetic-shower.md)

The approximate [electron](electron.md) [energy](energy-split.md) at which radiative and collisional energy-loss rates are equal in a given material. Below it, [ionization](ionization.md) increasingly dominates and multiplication of the [electromagnetic shower](electromagnetic-shower.md) ends. It is material dependent, not a universal particle constant.
