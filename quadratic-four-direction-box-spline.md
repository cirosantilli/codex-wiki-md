# Quadratic four-direction box spline

↑ **Parent:** [Box spline](box-spline.md)

This [box spline](box-spline.md) is the density of the image of the uniform unit four-cube under the indicated direction [matrix](matrix.md). Its [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) pieces have total degree $4-2=2$. Removing three directions is necessary to leave a nonspanning set, giving $C^1$ [continuity](continuous-function.md). Its knot arrangement is a criss-cross triangulation of a square lattice. Its refinement mask is $\frac14(1+z)(1+w)(1+zw)(1+z/w)$, up to the monomial that fixes the origin.
