# Totally imaginary number field

↑ **Parent:** [Number field](number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_imaginary_number_field)

A [number field](number-field.md) is totally imaginary if it has no embedding into the real numbers. Its embeddings into the complex numbers occur entirely in conjugate pairs. This is also called totally complex; it does not assert that every element of the field is nonreal.
