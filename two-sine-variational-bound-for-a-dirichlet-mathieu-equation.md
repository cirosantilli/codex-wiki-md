# Two-sine variational bound for a Dirichlet Mathieu equation

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md)

For $-\psi''+\cos(\pi x)\psi=\lambda\psi$ on $(0,1)$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), use $\psi=\sin(\pi x)+\alpha\sin(2\pi x)$. Its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) is $(\pi^2+\alpha+4\pi^2\alpha^2)/(1+\alpha^2)$. In the normalized sine basis the restricted operator has [matrix](matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}\pi^2&1/2\\1/2&4\pi^2\end{pmatrix}$. Its smaller [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) gives the displayed upper bound, attained within this trial space at $\alpha=3\pi^2-\sqrt{9\pi^4+1}$. The other stationary parameter gives the larger restricted [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). This is a finite-dimensional [Rayleigh-Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md) for a regular [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md) with a sign-changing potential.
