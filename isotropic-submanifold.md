# Isotropic submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

An isotropic submanifold has every [tangent space](tangent-space.md) an [isotropic subspace of a symplectic vector space](isotropic-subspace-of-a-symplectic-vector-space.md). Its dimension is at most half the ambient dimension, and equality makes it a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md).
