# Trace of a permuted tensor power

↑ **Parent:** [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md)

If $\sigma$ has $\alpha_r$ cycles of length $r$, contraction of matrix entries around its cycles gives $\operatorname{tr}(\sigma\xi^{\otimes n})=\prod_r\operatorname{tr}(\xi^r)^{\alpha_r}$. The formula holds for every [endomorphism](endomorphism.md), not only diagonalizable ones. The [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md) decomposition equates it with a sum of products of Specht and Schur [characters](character-of-a-representation.md).
