# Psychosis continuum hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Psychotic experience](psychotic-experience.md)

The hypothesis that some [psychosis](psychosis.md)-related experiences vary dimensionally across clinical and nonclinical populations. Overlap in a dimension does not imply that all people share the same cause, prognosis or [need for care](need-for-care.md).
