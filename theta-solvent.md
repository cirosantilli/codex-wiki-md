# Theta solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

A [solvent](solvent.md) under conditions where the leading effective pair [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) of a long dilute [polymer](polymer-split.md) vanishes. In the simplest [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) this means $\chi=1/2$. The chain is approximately an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md), while three-segment interactions can still affect the [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) of a concentrated solution. A particular polymer-solvent pair often meets this condition only near its [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md).
