# Mean velocity of a kinetic distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

The mass-normalized first velocity moment of a distribution on [phase space](phase-space.md). For a velocity-only distribution, omit the position integral. Nonzero finite [mass](mass.md) and a finite first moment are required. For a probability distribution this is its [expected value](expected-value.md); its unnormalized counterpart is the [momentum](momentum.md).
