# Drainage volume decay in a dipole porous current

↑ **Parent:** [Dipole similarity solution of a draining porous current](dipole-similarity-solution-of-a-draining-porous-current.md)

For the [dipole similarity solution of a draining porous current](dipole-similarity-solution-of-a-draining-porous-current.md), $V=\phi\int h\,dx=\phi ad\,t^{-1/4}/18$. Thus $\dot V/V=-1/(4t)$. A shifted similarity origin replaces $t$ by $t+t_0$. Neither a positive rate nor a rate $-1/(2t)$ is compatible with this two-dimensional first-moment-conserving profile.
