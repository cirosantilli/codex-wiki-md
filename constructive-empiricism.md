# Constructive empiricism

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_empiricism)

Acceptance of a scientific theory involves belief in its [empirical adequacy](empirical-adequacy.md) and practical commitment to its use, without requiring belief in its full truth about unobservables. This concerns epistemic attitudes, whereas the distinction between a [constructive theory](constructive-theory.md) and a [principle theory](principle-theory.md) concerns explanatory organization.[section III](constructive-empiricism-now-by-bas-van-fraassen.md)
