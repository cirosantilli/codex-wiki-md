# Rational principal part

↑ **Parent:** [Rational section of a sheaf of modules](rational-section-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md)

For a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) on an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md), the stalk inclusion identifies sections regular near $P$ inside its [rational sections](rational-section-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md). The quotient records the obstruction to regularity at that point. This definition does not assume a nonsingular point or a discrete valuation parameter.
