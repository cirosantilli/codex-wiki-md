# Spin qubit in a gated quantum dot

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatically defined quantum dot](electrostatically-defined-quantum-dot.md)

A single confined [electron](electron.md)'s two [spin](spin.md) states form a [qubit](qubit.md) when initialization, coherent rotations and measurement are controlled. Local driving rotates one [spin](spin.md); gate-controlled exchange can entangle neighboring dot [spins](spin.md). [Spin-to-charge conversion](spin-to-charge-conversion.md) permits electrical readout. Observing charge blockade alone does not demonstrate coherent [qubit](qubit.md) operation.
