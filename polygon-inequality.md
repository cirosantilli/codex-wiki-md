# Polygon inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Polygon](polygon.md)

Positive [real number](real-number.md) lengths $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, with $k\ge3$, are the sides of a nondegenerate [polygon](polygon.md) exactly when $2\max_i a_i<\sum_i a_i$. Necessity follows from the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md): the longest side must be shorter than the path formed by the remaining sides.
