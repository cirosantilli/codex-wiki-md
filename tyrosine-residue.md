# Tyrosine residue

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

A [tyrosine residue](tyrosine-residue.md) is an [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) whose [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) can accept a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group during [receptor tyrosine kinase](receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md) signaling. The conventional one-letter sequence symbol is $Y$.
