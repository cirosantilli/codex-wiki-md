# Gaussian fluctuation approximation

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop scalar effective action](one-loop-scalar-effective-action.md)

Expanding an [action](action.md) about a classical stationary field and retaining its [second variation](second-variation.md) gives a Gaussian [path integral](path-integral.md). Its nonzero fluctuation modes determine a [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md) and a [one-loop soliton mass correction](one-loop-soliton-mass-correction.md). Symmetry-generated [zero modes in field theory](zero-mode-in-field-theory.md) must be removed from that determinant and integrated as [collective coordinates](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md).
