# Sigma-donor ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [sigma-donor ligand](sigma-donor-ligand.md) supplies an occupied orbital for [sigma donation](sigma-donation.md). Additional occupied or empty orbitals can make it a [pi-donor ligand](pi-donor-ligand.md) or a [pi-acceptor ligand](pi-acceptor-ligand.md) as well.
