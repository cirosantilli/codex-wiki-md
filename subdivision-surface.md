# Subdivision surface

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivision_surface)

A [subdivision surface](subdivision-surface.md) is the limit of repeated mesh refinement and vertex averaging. Exact interrogation evaluates this limit and, where defined, its [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md) and [normal vectors](normal-vector.md); a finite refined control mesh is generally only an approximation to the limit.
