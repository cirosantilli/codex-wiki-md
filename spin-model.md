# Spin model

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_model)

A spin model assigns a discrete or continuous spin variable to each site of a [lattice](lattice.md) or vertex of a graph and gives interacting configurations a [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md). Spin models describe collective order and phase transitions in magnetic and other many-body systems.
