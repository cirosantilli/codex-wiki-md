# Cataclasite

↑ **Parent:** [Fault rock](fault-rock.md)

A cohesive [fault rock](fault-rock.md) produced predominantly by mechanical grain fragmentation and granular rearrangement. It differs from a loose [fault breccia](fault-breccia.md) or [fault gouge](fault-gouge.md) in cohesion, while sharing a brittle crushing mechanism rather than requiring bulk melting.
