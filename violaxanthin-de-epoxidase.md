# Violaxanthin de-epoxidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Violaxanthin de-epoxidase](violaxanthin-de-epoxidase.md) catalyses steps of conversion from violaxanthin towards zeaxanthin under suitable thylakoid-lumen conditions. Its activity links lumen acidification to a change in antenna pigment composition.
