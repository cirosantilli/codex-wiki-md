# Accretion-disk boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

An accretion-disk boundary layer connects nearly [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md) to a stellar surface rotating at a different angular speed. For a slowly rotating star, dissipation of the incoming orbital [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives $L_{\rm BL}\simeq GM\dot M/(2r_*)$, comparable to the [standard thin-disk luminosity](standard-thin-disk-luminosity.md). If the emitting area scales as $r_*H$, its [blackbody](blackbody.md) [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) scales as $T_{\rm BL}\sim(r_*/H)^{1/4}T_{\rm in}$. The coefficient depends on geometry, and stellar rotation reduces the available energy.
