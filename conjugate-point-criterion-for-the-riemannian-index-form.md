# Conjugate-point criterion for the Riemannian index form

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md)

On continuous piecewise smooth perpendicular fields with zero endpoints, the [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md) is nonnegative exactly when no point strictly inside the [geodesic](geodesic.md) segment is conjugate to its initial point. It is positive on every nonzero field exactly when no [conjugate point](conjugate-point.md) occurs up to and including the final endpoint. At a first conjugate endpoint its nullspace consists of the endpoint-vanishing [Jacobi fields](jacobi-field.md). These statements distinguish nonnegative quadratic forms from strictly positive ones and do not assume global minimization.
