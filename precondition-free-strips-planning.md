# Precondition-free STRIPS planning

↑ **Parent:** [STRIPS](strips.md)

[Automated planning](automated-planning.md) with [ground actions](ground-action.md) that are always applicable because their [precondition](precondition.md) sets are empty. Delete effects are still permitted, so taking the union of all add effects does not decide reachability. With a positive conjunctive goal, the [last-achiever algorithm for precondition-free planning](last-achiever-algorithm-for-precondition-free-planning.md) gives a [polynomial-time algorithm](polynomial-time-algorithm.md).
