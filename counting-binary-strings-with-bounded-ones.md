# Counting binary strings with bounded ones

↑ **Parent:** [Binary string](binary-string.md)

A [binary string](binary-string.md) with $n$ zeroes and $j$ ones is determined by its $j$ one-positions, giving $\binom{n+j}j$ possibilities. Sum for $0\le j\le m$ and apply the [hockey-stick identity](hockey-stick-identity.md). Equivalently, distribute at most $m$ ones among the $n+1$ gaps around the zeroes and add a slack count; [stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) counts $n+2$ nonnegative counts summing to $m$.
