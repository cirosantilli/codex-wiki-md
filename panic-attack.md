# Panic attack

↑ **Parent:** [Panic disorder](panic-disorder.md)

An abrupt episode of intense [fear](fear.md) or discomfort with associated symptoms. A [panic attack](panic-attack.md) is an event; [panic disorder](panic-disorder.md) additionally involves a diagnostic pattern of recurrent unexpected attacks and persistent concern or behavioural change.
