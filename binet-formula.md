# Binet formula

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binet_formula)

For $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ and $\psi=(1-\sqrt5)/2$, the Fibonacci numbers satisfy

$$
F_n=\frac{\phi^n-\psi^n}{\sqrt5}.
$$
