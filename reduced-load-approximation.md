# Reduced-load approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Erlang fixed point approximation](erlang-fixed-point-approximation.md)

For unit resource requirements in a [loss network](loss-network.md), the effective [offered traffic](offered-traffic.md) to resource $j$ is $a_j=\sum_{r:j\in r}\alpha_r\prod_{k\in r\setminus\{j\}}(1-B_k)$, where $B_k$ are approximate resource blocking probabilities. The resource being modeled is excluded from its own screening product.
