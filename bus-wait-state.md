# Bus wait state

↑ **Parent:** [Bus transaction](bus-transaction.md)

An extension while a [bus transaction](bus-transaction.md) awaits readiness. A [bus wait state](bus-wait-state.md) spends time without completing the next payload transfer. In a blocking design it also postpones another initiator's access; completion and [bus ownership](bus-ownership.md) changes must follow the protocol.[AMBA Specification, revision 2.0, section 3.11.3, pp. 3-30–3-31, figures 3-16–3-17: readiness and ownership handover](amba-specification-rev-2-0-by-arm-limited.md)
