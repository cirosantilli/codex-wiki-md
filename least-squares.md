# Least squares

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_squares)

The [least squares](least-squares.md) approach chooses parameters by minimizing the sum of squared residuals. A [linear least-squares problem](linear-least-squares-problem.md) has affine residuals, whereas [nonlinear least squares](non-linear-least-squares.md) permits nonlinear dependence on the parameters. The [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) makes the objective $\tfrac12\|R(x)\|_2^2$; the factor one-half simplifies its derivatives without changing its minimizers.
