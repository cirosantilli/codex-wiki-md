# Reduced plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

A [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md) is reduced when its defining [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) is a [squarefree polynomial](square-free-polynomial.md), so no [irreducible component](irreducible-component.md) is repeated. Its degree is the sum of the degrees of its distinct [irreducible components](irreducible-component.md). This convention is essential when bounding the number of [singular points](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md): the nonreduced equation $L^2=0$ has vanishing first [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) at every point of its supporting line.
