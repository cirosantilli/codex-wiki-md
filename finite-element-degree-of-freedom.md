# Finite element degree of freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Finite element](finite-element.md)

A [finite element degree of freedom](finite-element-degree-of-freedom.md) is one of the [linear functionals](linear-functional.md) chosen to specify a local shape function in a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $P$. Examples are [point evaluation functionals](point-evaluation-functional.md) and edge or cell [integrals](integral-split.md). The [finite element](finite-element.md) is an [unisolvent finite element](unisolvent-finite-element.md) when the complete list gives a [bijection](bijection.md) from $P$ to the corresponding list of real values.
