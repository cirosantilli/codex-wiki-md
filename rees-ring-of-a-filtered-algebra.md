# Rees ring of a filtered algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered algebra](filtered-algebra.md)

For an ascending multiplicative filtration with $1\in F_0A$, its Rees ring contains the central element $h$. The quotient by $h$ is the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md), and the quotient by $h-1$ is the original algebra. If $[F_iA,F_jA]\subseteq F_{i+j-1}A$, commutators of lifts are divisible by $h$. Their first-order term defines the [Poisson bracket from a central deformation parameter](poisson-bracket-from-a-central-deformation-parameter.md) on the associated graded ring.
