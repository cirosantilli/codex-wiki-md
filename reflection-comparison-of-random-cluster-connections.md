# Reflection comparison of random-cluster connections

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster inverse correlation length](random-cluster-inverse-correlation-length.md)

Choose a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) $x=(n,x_2,\ldots,x_d)$ on the boundary of a [lattice](lattice.md) box. Translation and reflection invariance identify the [probabilities](probability.md) of $0\leftrightarrow x$ and $x\leftrightarrow2ne_1$. Their intersection implies $0\leftrightarrow2ne_1$, so [positive association of random variables](positive-association-of-random-variables.md) gives $C_{2n}\ge\phi(0\leftrightarrow x)^2$. A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) and [lattice](lattice.md) symmetries choose such an $x$ with [probability](probability.md) at least the box-boundary connection [probability](probability.md) divided by the number of boundary [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). This polynomial factor disappears in the exponential rate, identifying boundary and axial inverse correlation lengths.
