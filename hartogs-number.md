# Hartogs number

↑ **Parent:** [Hartogs theorem](hartogs-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartogs_number)

The least [ordinal](ordinal.md) that does not admit an [injection](injective-function.md) into $X$. Its existence, supplied by [Hartogs theorem](hartogs-theorem.md), does not require the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md).
