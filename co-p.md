# co-P

↑ **Parent:** [P (complexity)](p-complexity.md)

The [complexity class](complexity-class.md) of [complements](complement-of-a-set.md) of [formal languages](formal-language.md) in [P](p-complexity.md). A deterministic [polynomial-time algorithm](polynomial-time-algorithm.md) deciding a [formal language](formal-language.md) can have its accepting and rejecting outcomes interchanged, so [P](p-complexity.md) is closed under [complement](complement-of-a-set.md) and equals [co-P](co-p.md).
