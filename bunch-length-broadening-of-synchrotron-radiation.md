# Bunch-length broadening of synchrotron radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Synchrotron pulse compression](synchrotron-pulse-compression.md)

In a fixed tangent viewing geometry, an [electron](electron.md) bunch of longitudinal [length](length.md) $L_b$ passes the same radiating point over approximately $L_b/u$. Different [electrons](electron.md) then emit from nearly the same point at different times. The incoherent pulse envelope is the [convolution](convolution.md) of the single-[electron](electron.md) intensity profile with the bunch's passage-time distribution, assuming equal particle trajectories and a linear detector. Its width generally has scale at least $\max\{L_b/u,r/(c\gamma^3)\}$. A bunch short compared with the orbit need not be short compared with the intrinsic single-[electron](electron.md) pulse.
