<h1 id="air-gap-optical-path-difference-in-a-fabry-perot-interferometer">Air-gap optical path difference in a Fabry–Pérot interferometer</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](fabry-perot-interferometer.md)

For an air gap of thickness $d$, an extra internal round trip has length $2d/\cos\theta$, but consecutive transmitted rays emerge at different positions. Comparing them at the same outgoing [wavefront](wavefront.md) subtracts $2d\tan\theta\sin\theta$, leaving [optical path difference](optical-path-difference.md) $2d\cos\theta$. Reflection-coating [wave phases](phase-waves.md) are separate from this geometric path calculation.
