# Laurent series field

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

The Laurent series field $k((t))$ consists of [formal series](formal-power-series.md) $\sum_{n\geq N}a_nt^n$ with finitely many negative-degree terms. Its $t$-adic valuation is the least exponent with nonzero coefficient.
