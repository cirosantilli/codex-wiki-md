# Left radical of a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md)

The [left radical](left-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) is $\{v\in V:B(v,w)=0\text{ for every }w\in V\}$. If $A$ is the [matrix of a bilinear form](matrix-of-a-bilinear-form.md), this condition is $v^TA=0$, so its [coordinate vectors](coordinate-vector.md) form $\ker A^T$. For $A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, the [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) $B(v,w)=v_1w_2$ has [left radical](left-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) $\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)\}$.
