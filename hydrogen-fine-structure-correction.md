# Hydrogen fine-structure correction

↑ **Parent:** [Fine structure](fine-structure.md)

For a point, fixed proton, the sum of the [relativistic kinetic-energy correction](relativistic-kinetic-energy-correction.md), [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) including the [Thomas precession](thomas-precession.md) factor, and [Darwin term](darwin-term.md) has the displayed leading correction. It depends on n and total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) j, but not separately on l. Thus $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ remain degenerate while $2P_{3/2}$ lies above them. Radiative corrections, recoil, nuclear size and [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) require additional terms. The fine-structure constant is $\alpha_{\rm fs}=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar c)$.
