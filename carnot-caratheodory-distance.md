<h1 id="carnot-caratheodory-distance">Carnot-Carathéodory distance</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)

On a graded nilpotent [Lie group](lie-group.md) whose first layer generates its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), a horizontal curve obeys $\dot\gamma_t=(L_{\gamma_t})_*\dot x_t$ for a control in the first layer. The displayed infimum is over horizontal curves with prescribed endpoints. The distance is left invariant and homogeneous under the graded dilations. On a free nilpotent [Lie group](lie-group.md) it measures the least Euclidean length of a path realizing a prescribed truncated [path signature](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md).
