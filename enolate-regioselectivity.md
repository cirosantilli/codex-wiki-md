# Enolate regioselectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Enolate](enolate.md)

Preference for forming or reacting one [enolate](enolate.md) of a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound with more than one alpha site. A less substituted [kinetic enolate](kinetic-enolate.md) and an equilibrated more substituted [enolate](enolate.md) can give different [carbon](carbon.md) skeletons.
