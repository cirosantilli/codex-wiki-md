# Spectral Hopf condition for a cubic characteristic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

A real [cubic](cubic-polynomial.md) $p(\lambda)=\lambda^3+A\lambda^2+B\lambda+C$ has [roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $-A,\pm i\sqrt B$ when $B>0$ and $AB=C$. For $A>0$ this gives a [stable](lyapunov-stability.md) [transverse](transversality-of-a-map-to-a-submanifold.md) third direction. To conclude a generic [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) as a [parameter](mathematical-model-parameter.md) varies, also require a [transverse crossing](transversality-of-a-map-to-a-submanifold.md) of the imaginary pair and a nonzero [first Lyapunov coefficient](first-lyapunov-coefficient.md). The algebraic imaginary-root condition alone is not sufficient.
