# Discrete Dupire equation

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

For a [martingale](martingale-split.md) with increments in $\{-1,0,1\}$, [European call option](european-call-option.md) prices satisfy $C(T+1,K)-C(T,K)=\frac12\sigma^2(T,K)(C(T,K+1)-2C(T,K)+C(T,K-1))$. At positive-probability states, the [variance](variance-split.md) and the upward/downward conditional transitions are determined by this identity; transitions at zero-probability states cannot be recovered.
