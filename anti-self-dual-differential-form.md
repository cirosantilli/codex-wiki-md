# Anti-self-dual differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md)

On an oriented Riemannian four-manifold, a two-form $\alpha$ is anti-self-dual when $*\alpha=-\alpha$.

On an oriented Riemannian four-manifold, a [two-form](2-form.md) is anti-self-dual when $*\alpha=-\alpha$. Here $*$ is the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md).
