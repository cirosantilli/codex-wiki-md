# DNA ladder

↑ **Parent:** [Agarose gel electrophoresis](agarose-gel-electrophoresis.md)

A [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md) contains [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) fragments of known lengths in [base pairs](base-pair.md). Its [gel bands](gel-band.md) supply size references. Match the sample's strandedness and shape, and use the specified lengths for that [DNA ladder](dna-ladder.md). A size reference need not provide a quantitative [mass](mass.md) standard.[Step 2, Nucleic acid ladder selection guidelines: fragment structure, conformation, intended use and manufacturer-specific composition](five-main-nucleic-acid-gel-electrophoresis-steps-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
