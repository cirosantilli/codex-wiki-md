# Second-difference integral formula

↑ **Parent:** [Second modulus of smoothness](second-modulus-of-smoothness.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable function and $h\geq0$, the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives

$$
f(x+h)-2f(x)+f(x-h)=\int_{-h}^h(h-|u|)f''(x+u)\,du.
$$

The nonnegative triangular weight has integral $h^2$, so its [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) estimate directly controls the [second modulus of smoothness](second-modulus-of-smoothness.md).
