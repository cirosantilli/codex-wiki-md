# Cross-Sperner family

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)

Two [set families](set-family.md) $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ are cross-Sperner when no member of either is a [subset](subset.md) of a member of the other, including equality. Thus $\mathcal A\cap\mathcal B=\varnothing$. Neither family need itself be an [antichain](antichain.md). On the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) of [subsets](subset.md) of $[n]$, the [Cross-Sperner inequality](cross-sperner-inequality.md) bounds $\sqrt{|\mathcal A|}+\sqrt{|\mathcal B|}$ by $2^{n/2}$.
