# Voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical network](electrical-network.md)

A voltage is a scalar potential on the vertices of an [electrical network](electrical-network.md). Away from prescribed boundary vertices it is harmonic, and its difference across an edge drives current through that edge.
