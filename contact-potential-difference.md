# Contact potential difference

↑ **Parent:** [Millikan photoelectric experiment](millikan-photoelectric-experiment.md)

A [contact potential difference](contact-potential-difference.md) is an electrostatic [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) associated with different conducting surfaces brought into electrical equilibrium. Surface condition and different [work functions](work-function.md) can create a [vacuum](vacuum.md) gap field even when an external [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) reads zero. Connecting the metals establishes equilibrium of their electronic [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) including electrostatic [energy](energy-split.md); it need not equalize the electrostatic [electric potential](electric-potential.md) in the gap.
