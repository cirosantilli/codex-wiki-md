# Gauge covariance of the charged Dirac equation

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal electromagnetic coupling of a Dirac field](minimal-electromagnetic-coupling-of-a-dirac-field.md)

The derivative of the local phase in $\Psi\mapsto e^{-iq\alpha}\Psi$ cancels the added gradient in the electromagnetic potential. Thus $D'_\mu\Psi'=e^{-iq\alpha}D_\mu\Psi$, and the charged [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) maps solutions to solutions. The same cancellation makes its action gauge invariant.
