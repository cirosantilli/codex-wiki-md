# Kohn singularity of static Lindhard screening

↑ **Parent:** [Static Lindhard dielectric function](static-lindhard-dielectric-function.md)

The three-dimensional static screening function is finite at $q=2k_F$, but its [derivative](derivative.md) diverges logarithmically. Expanding the explicit [static Lindhard dielectric function](static-lindhard-dielectric-function.md) gives the title's nonanalytic term. The singularity comes from particle–hole excitations connecting opposite parts of the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). It produces a [Friedel density tail from a derivative singularity](friedel-density-tail-from-a-derivative-singularity.md); analogous electronic response can cause a [Kohn anomaly](kohn-anomaly.md) in a coupled lattice vibration.
