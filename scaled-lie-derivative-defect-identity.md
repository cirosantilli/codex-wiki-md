# Scaled Lie derivative defect identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

For a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) $f$ and [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$,

$$
f\mathcal L_X-\mathcal L_{fX}=D_{X\otimes df}
$$

on every [tensor field](tensor-field.md), with $X\otimes df$ interpreted as the [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) $Y\mapsto Y(f)X$. Both sides vanish on functions; on [vector fields](vector-field.md) this follows from $[fX,Y]=f[X,Y]-Y(f)X$. Both are contraction-compatible [tensor derivations](tensor-derivation.md), so agreement on functions and [vector fields](vector-field.md) proves equality on all tensor types. In particular $\mathcal L_{fX}\omega=f\mathcal L_X\omega+\omega(X)df$.
