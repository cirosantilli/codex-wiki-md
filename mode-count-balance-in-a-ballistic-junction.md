# Mode-count balance in a ballistic junction

↑ **Parent:** [Landauer-Büttiker formula](landauer-buttiker-formula.md)

Let $T_{ij}$ count transmitted [modes](mode-statistics.md) from injector $j$ to collector $i$, excluding reflection $R_{ii}$. Scattering conservation gives $\sum_{i\ne j}T_{ij}=N_j-R_{jj}$; [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) also gives the corresponding row balance. The conductance matrix has zero row sum, making equal lead potentials carry no current, and zero column sum, conserving total current.
