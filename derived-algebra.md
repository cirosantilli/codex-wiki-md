# Derived algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Derived series of a Lie algebra](derived-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

The derived algebra is the linear span of all [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md) $[x,y]$ in a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) makes it an [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Its quotient is the largest [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) quotient of $\mathfrak g$.
