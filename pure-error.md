# Pure error

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)

With repeated responses at an identical predictor configuration, pure error is their within-configuration variability. Its [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) can estimate measurement variance independently of a candidate regression mean function. Without replication, a fitted residual can combine pure error and [lack of fit](lack-of-fit.md).
