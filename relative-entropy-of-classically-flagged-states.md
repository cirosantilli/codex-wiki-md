# Relative entropy of classically flagged states

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)

For common classical weights, the [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md) on each positive-weight block is $\log(p_i\rho_i)=(\log p_i)I+\log\rho_i$ on its support. The two logarithms of $p_i$ cancel in the [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md), giving the displayed identity. Zero-weight blocks are omitted, including if their unweighted relative entropy is infinite. If a positive-weight block violates support inclusion, both sides are infinite. Applying the [data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy](data-processing-inequality-for-quantum-relative-entropy.md) to the [partial trace](partial-trace.md) over the flag gives [joint convexity of quantum relative entropy](joint-convexity-of-quantum-relative-entropy.md).
