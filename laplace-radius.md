# Laplace radius

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace plane](laplace-plane.md)

The Laplace radius is the satellite distance where the small-tilt [nodal precession](nodal-precession.md) contributions from a planet's axisymmetric quadrupole and the stellar tide have equal magnitudes. In the circular hierarchical limit those rates are $3GM_pJ_2R_p^2/(2na^5)$ and $3GM_\star/(4na_p^3)$. Equating them proves the displayed fifth-root expression. Inside this radius the [planetary quadrupole coefficient](planetary-quadrupole-coefficient.md) controls the preferred plane; outside it the [double-averaged circular tidal potential](double-averaged-circular-tidal-potential.md) dominates. When equator and planetary orbit are inclined to one another, their balance determines the local [Laplace plane](laplace-plane.md).
