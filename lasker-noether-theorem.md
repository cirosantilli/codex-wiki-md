<h1 id="lasker-noether-theorem">Lasker–Noether theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Primary decomposition](primary-decomposition.md)

Every proper [ideal](ideal.md) in a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) is a finite intersection of [primary ideals](primary-ideal.md). This existence theorem does not give uniqueness of the individual components: [embedded primary components](embedded-primary-component.md) may vary.
