# Loop Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A connected [loop Feynman diagram](loop-feynman-diagram.md) has at least one independent internal [loop momentum](loop-momentum.md). With $I$ internal lines and $V$ vertices, the number of independent loops is $L=I-V+1$. A [tree-level Feynman diagram](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) has $L=0$. A multi-leg amplitude generated by a loop is not an extra fundamental [interaction vertex](interaction-vertex.md) of the original [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md).
