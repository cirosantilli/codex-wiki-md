# Wave speed

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For the homogeneous [one-dimensional wave equation](one-dimensional-wave-equation.md) $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$ with constant $c>0$, [wave speed](wave-speed.md) means the speed at which a [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) profile translates. In $F(x-ct)$, a feature initially at $x=s$ follows $x=s+ct$, giving signed [velocity](velocity.md) $+c$; in $G(x+ct)$ it follows $x=s-ct$, giving signed [velocity](velocity.md) $-c$. The [characteristic lines for the one-dimensional wave equation](characteristic-line-for-the-one-dimensional-wave-equation.md) therefore have slopes $dx/dt=\pm c$. The speed of either profile is $c$, even though the [displacement](displacement.md) at a fixed point can change sign or pass through zero.[The 1-D Wave Equation](18-303-linear-partial-differential-equations-fall-2006-by-matthew-j-hancock/the-1-d-wave-equation.md)

The same $c$ bounds the [causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation](causal-cone-for-the-constant-speed-wave-equation.md). For a [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md), $c=\sqrt{T/\mu}$ depends on [tension](tension.md) and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md); for small [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md) in stationary uniform air it is the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md). This interpretation assumes the stated ideal [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md). In media with [wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md), [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), [group velocity](group-velocity.md) and the earliest signal arrival have different meanings: a measured [wave crest](wave-crest.md) speed need not be the speed of a [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md), and neither alone establishes a [causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation](causal-cone-for-the-constant-speed-wave-equation.md).
