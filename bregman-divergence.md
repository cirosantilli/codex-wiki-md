# Bregman divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Variational regularization](variational-regularization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bregman_divergence)

For a [convex functional](convex-function.md) $J$ and $p\in\partial J(v)$, the Bregman divergence is

$$
D_J^p(u,v)=J(u)-J(v)-\langle p,u-v\rangle.
$$

It is nonnegative but need not be symmetric or satisfy the triangle inequality.
