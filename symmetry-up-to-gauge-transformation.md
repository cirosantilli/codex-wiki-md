# Symmetry up to gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md)

A spatial transformation is a symmetry of a gauge-field configuration when its pullback lies in the [gauge orbit](gauge-orbit.md) of the original configuration. It need not fix a particular representative. For a planar vortex, rotation changes its winding phase and a constant $U(1)$ transformation compensates. For a [hedgehog ansatz for a monopole](hedgehog-ansatz-for-a-monopole.md), a spatial rotation is compensated by the corresponding internal adjoint $SU(2)$ rotation. Gauge-invariant densities remain invariant without choosing a special gauge.
