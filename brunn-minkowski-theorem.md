# Brunn-Minkowski theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski addition](minkowski-addition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunn–Minkowski_theorem)

For nonempty [open sets](open-set.md) $A,B\subset\mathbb R^n$, the [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) of their [Minkowski sum](minkowski-addition.md) satisfies

$$
\lambda_n(A+B)^{1/n}\geq\lambda_n(A)^{1/n}+\lambda_n(B)^{1/n}.
$$

It also holds for compact sets and in standard measurable-set formulations with the appropriate measurability qualification. Normalize both volumes to one and apply the [Prékopa–Leindler inequality](prekopa-leindler-inequality.md) to [indicator functions](indicator-function.md).
