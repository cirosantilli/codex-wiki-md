# Exact anisotropic radial limit cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md)

For $\dot r=r[1-r^2(2-\cos2\theta)]$, $\dot\theta=2$, the substitution $w=r^{-2}$ gives $dw/d\theta+w=2-\cos2\theta$. Its positive periodic solution yields the displayed [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md), with full planar period $\pi$. Every other solution at the same phase has $w-w_p=Ce^{-2t}$, proving uniqueness and attraction. The limiting circles $r=1/\sqrt3$ and $r=1$ give a trapping [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md), with tangencies at their extremal angles.
