# Variance identity for independent copies

↑ **Parent:** [Variance](variance-split.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) identically distributed [random vectors](random-vector.md) $Q,Q'$ with finite second [absolute moments](absolute-moment.md), expand $|Q-Q'|^2=|Q|^2+|Q'|^2-2Q\cdot Q'$. Their [independence](independent-random-variables.md) makes $\mathbb E(Q\cdot Q')=|\mathbb EQ|^2$, proving the identity. The right side is the sum of the coordinate [variances](variance-split.md). Applied to the displacement in an optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md), it separates motion of the [barycenter of a measure](barycenter-of-a-measure-on-a-banach-space.md) from relative particle motion.
