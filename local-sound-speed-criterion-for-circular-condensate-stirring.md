# Local sound-speed criterion for circular condensate stirring

↑ **Parent:** [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md)

In a parabolic condensate in the [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md), the local sound [speed](speed.md) is $c(D)=\omega_r\sqrt{R^2-D^2}/\sqrt2$. Equating it to the obstacle [speed](speed.md) $\Omega D$ gives the displayed threshold. This is the stipulated local [phonon](phonon.md) estimate; a finite strongly depleting obstacle can modify flow and nucleate [vortices](phase-vortex.md), so the inequality is not a universal exact onset law for every stirring experiment.
