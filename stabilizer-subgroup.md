# Stabilizer subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

For a [group action](group-action.md) of a [group](group-split.md) $G$ on a [set](set-split.md) $X$, the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $x\in X$ consists of the transformations that leave $x$ fixed:

$$
G_x=\operatorname{Stab}_G(x)=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}\leq G.
$$

It is a [subgroup](subgroup.md): the [identity element](identity-element.md) fixes $x$, products of transformations fixing $x$ fix $x$, and the [inverse element](inverse-element.md) of a transformation fixing $x$ also fixes $x$. A [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) is a [subset](subset.md) of $G$, whereas the [orbit of a group action](orbit-group-theory.md) is a [subset](subset.md) of $X$. See [Ackerman's algebra notes, definition 0.4.1.2](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~nate/teaching/UPenn/2007/fall/math_371/lectures/week_1/lecture_1/lecture_1.pdf).

For the [square](square.md) [group actions](group-action.md) defined under [group action](group-action.md), direct calculation gives

$$
(D_4)_A=\{e,s\},\qquad (D_4)_{AB}=\{e,rs\},\qquad (D_4)_{AC}=\{e,r^2,s,r^2s\},\qquad (D_4)_O=D_4.
$$

The [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) $s$ fixes $A$ and $C$ individually. The [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) $rs$ preserves the unoriented [side of a polygon](side-of-a-polygon.md) $AB$ by exchanging its two [vertices of a polygon](vertex-of-a-polygon.md). The half-turn [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) $r^2$ exchanges the two ends of the unoriented [diagonal of a polygon](diagonal-of-a-polygon.md) $AC$ while preserving that [diagonal of a polygon](diagonal-of-a-polygon.md). Thus fixing a [side of a polygon](side-of-a-polygon.md) or [diagonal of a polygon](diagonal-of-a-polygon.md) as an object does not mean fixing every point on it; the latter condition uses a [pointwise stabilizer](pointwise-stabilizer.md). The four [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) have [group orders](order-of-a-finite-group.md) $2,2,4,8$, matching [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md) sizes $4,4,2,1$ through the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md).

A [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) records the transformations still available after imposing a constraint. Fixing a [vertex of a polygon](vertex-of-a-polygon.md) of the [square](square.md) leaves two choices; fixing its [centroid](centroid.md) leaves all eight. In a [faithful group action](faithful-group-action.md), a [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) can still contain a nonidentity transformation, as $s\in(D_4)_A$ shows: a [faithful group action](faithful-group-action.md) requires that only the [identity element](identity-element.md) fix every point of $X$ simultaneously, not that every individual [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) be trivial. The two-point [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) example under [group action](group-action.md) has both [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) equal to the [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $\{0,2\}$ of its associated [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md).
