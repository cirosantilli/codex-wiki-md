# Conditional modus ponens

↑ **Parent:** [Deduction theorem for propositional logic](deduction-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md)

[Conditional modus ponens](conditional-modus-ponens.md) derives $A\to C$ from $A\to B$ and $A\to(B\to C)$. It is the step which translates a [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) inference when discharging a common assumption. In the [case-elimination Hilbert calculus](case-elimination-hilbert-calculus.md), the third scheme first gives $A\to(\neg B\to C)$; the second scheme combines the two conditionals to give $A\to C$.
