# Chain union of partial group operations

↑ **Parent:** [Group operation](group-operation.md)

For a nonempty inclusion chain of [group](group-split.md) operations on [subsets](subset.md) of a fixed [set](set-split.md), the union is a [group operation](group-operation.md) on the union of their carriers. Any finite list of carrier elements lies in one chain member, so its multiplication and [associativity](associative-property.md) agree there. Nested identities coincide because the only [idempotent](idempotent.md) of a [group](group-split.md) is its identity; inverses remain valid in larger members. Thus the union supplies the chain [upper bound](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) required by [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md).
