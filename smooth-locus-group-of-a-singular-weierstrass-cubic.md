# Smooth-locus group of a singular Weierstrass cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md)

For a singular projective Weierstrass cubic, its [smooth locus of a variety](smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md) contains the point at infinity and carries the [chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md). The singular point is excluded. Over an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md), a node gives the [multiplicative algebraic group](multiplicative-algebraic-group.md) and a cusp gives the [additive group](additive-group.md). A node whose branches are not defined over the base [field](field.md) gives a nonsplit one-dimensional [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md). This group is distinct from an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), whose projective model is smooth.
