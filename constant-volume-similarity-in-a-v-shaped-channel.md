# Constant-volume similarity in a V-shaped channel

↑ **Parent:** [V-shaped channel gravity current](v-shaped-channel-gravity-current.md)

The symmetric [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) for a localized fixed volume $V$ in a [V-shaped channel gravity current](v-shaped-channel-gravity-current.md) has $h(x,t)=[3(X^2-x^2)/(7Ct)]_+^{1/3}$. Integrating the similarity [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) equation once gives $Ch^4h_x=-2xh^2/(7t)$, and vanishing thickness at the two noses gives the profile. Its volume fixes $X=[V/(I\tan\alpha)]^{3/7}(7Ct/3)^{2/7}$, where $I=\int_{-1}^1(1-s^2)^{2/3}\,ds$. At any fixed nonzero position, the thickness first rises after arrival and then falls; its maximum occurs when $X^2=7x^2/3$.
