# Formal group exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md)

The formal group exponential is the compositional inverse of the [formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md). On a sufficiently small neighborhood over a characteristic-zero local field, both series converge and identify the formal group with the additive group.
