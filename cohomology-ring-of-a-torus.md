# Cohomology ring of a torus

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)

Since $T^r$ is a product of circles, the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) identifies its [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) with the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) on the coordinate degree-one classes. Inversion negates each integral degree-one class, so it acts as $(-1)^q$ in degree $q$. Even in characteristic two, each degree-one class has square zero.
