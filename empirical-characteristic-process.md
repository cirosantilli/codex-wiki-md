# Empirical characteristic process

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical characteristic function](empirical-characteristic-function.md)

The centered process $\mathcal C_n(u)=\sqrt n(\varphi_n(u)-\varphi(u))$ describes fluctuations of the [empirical characteristic function](empirical-characteristic-function.md). At a fixed frequency, its real and imaginary parts satisfy a [multivariate central limit theorem](multivariate-central-limit-theorem.md). A process-level limit requires further control over the frequency index.
