# Regulated complex Fresnel integral

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md)

For $\alpha>0$, polar coordinates reduce the integral to $2\pi\int_0^\infty r e^{-(\alpha-i\lambda)r^2}dr$. The result is $\pi/(\alpha-i\lambda)$. Its damped limit for real $\lambda>0$ is $i\pi/\lambda$, which fixes the sign of the oscillatory complex [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md). The [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md) convention can be compared with [NIST definitions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/7.2).
