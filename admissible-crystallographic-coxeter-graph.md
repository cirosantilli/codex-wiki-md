# Admissible crystallographic Coxeter graph

↑ **Parent:** [Coxeter graph](coxeter-graph.md)

In the crystallographic convention, a [Coxeter graph](coxeter-graph.md) is admissible when it is realized by linearly independent unit [vectors](vector.md) $v_i$ in a [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), with nonpositive mutual [inner products](inner-product.md) and $4(v_i,v_j)^2=n_{ij}\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ for distinct vertices. The number $n_{ij}$ records a single, double or triple connection; equivalently, the displayed [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) is [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md). In edge-label notation the nonzero connections have labels $3,4,6$. Forgetting multiplicity, the underlying [graph](graph-split.md) is a [forest](forest.md): for any cycle of $k\geq3$ distinct vertices, the sum of the corresponding unit [vectors](vector.md) would have squared [norm](norm.md) at most $k-k=0$, contradicting their linear independence. Multiple lines on one connection are labels and are not length-two cycles. This is the convention of [Wehler's Lie algebra notes, section 6.3](https://www.mathematik.uni-muenchen.de/~wehler/LieAlgebrasScript.pdf).
