<h1 id="gustaf-dalen">Gustaf Dalén</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of mechanical engineers](list-of-mechanical-engineers.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustaf_Dalén)

A [mechanical engineer](mechanical-engineer.md) whose work connected gas lighting to automatic operation. Chalmers records his mechanical-engineering qualification in 1896.[Gustaf Dalén, Mechanical Engineering 1896: qualification and engineering career](https://www.chalmers.se/en/about-chalmers/alumni/wall-of-fame/) After an accident left him blind in 1912, he returned to AGA in 1913 and continued leading the company.[The accident; Willpower and optimism: loss of sight, return and subsequent engineering work](gustaf-dalen-beacon-lighting-by-tekniska-museet.md)

The [sun valve](sun-valve.md) links [radiometric absorptance](radiometric-absorptance.md), [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) and mechanical motion to a useful decision: whether a beacon should receive its main gas supply. The [Dalén light](dalen-light.md) combines this daylight response with repeated flashes. The [Nobel Prize in Physics in 1912](nobel-prize-in-physics-in-1912.md) recognized his automatic regulators for lighthouse and buoy lighting.
