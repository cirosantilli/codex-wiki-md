# Lie group homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A smooth homomorphism of [Lie groups](lie-group.md) has differential $D_eF$ at the identity. Differentiating $F\circ L_g=L_{F(g)}\circ F$ makes the corresponding [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md) $F$-related. Their commutator action on pulled-back functions shows that the [differential of a Lie group homomorphism preserves Lie brackets](differential-of-a-lie-group-homomorphism-preserves-lie-brackets.md). The map need not be injective or surjective.
