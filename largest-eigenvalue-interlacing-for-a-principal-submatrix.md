# Largest-eigenvalue interlacing for a principal submatrix

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue interlacing](eigenvalue-interlacing.md)

Let a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $M$ have ordered [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n$, with $n\ge2$. If $A$ is obtained by deleting one matching row and column, its largest eigenvalue $\mu_1$ satisfies

$$
\lambda_1\ge\mu_1\ge\lambda_2.
$$

The [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) of $A$ is that of $M$ restricted to vectors whose deleted coordinate is zero. This gives the upper bound. Some nonzero linear combination of the top two orthonormal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) has that coordinate zero; normalizing it gives a quotient at least $\lambda_2$. The argument does not require distinct eigenvalues.
