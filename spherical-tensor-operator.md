# Spherical tensor operator

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md)

A rank-$k$ [spherical tensor operator](spherical-tensor-operator.md) has components $T_q^{(k)}$, $-k\leq q\leq k$, that transform under [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) like an [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $k$. Equivalently, their [commutators](commutator.md) with the [angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md) satisfy

$$
[J_z,T_q^{(k)}]=\hbar qT_q^{(k)},\qquad
[J_\pm,T_q^{(k)}]=\hbar\sqrt{k(k+1)-q(q\pm1)}\,T_{q\pm1}^{(k)}.
$$

For a vector operator, the rank is one and a conventional choice is $T_0=V_z$, $T_{\pm1}=\mp(V_x\pm iV_y)/\sqrt2$. The [Wigner-Eckart theorem](wigner-eckart-theorem.md) separates the [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) of a [spherical tensor operator](spherical-tensor-operator.md) into [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) and a [reduced matrix element](reduced-matrix-element.md).
