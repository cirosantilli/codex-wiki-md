# Primitive three-cycle criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Primitive group action](primitive-group-action.md)

Conjugate the three-cycle throughout the group. Connected components of the support hypergraph form an invariant [block system](block-system.md). Primitivity makes it connected, and [connected triple supports generate an alternating group](connected-triple-supports-generate-an-alternating-group.md) gives the conclusion.
