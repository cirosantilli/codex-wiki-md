# Fringe tracking

↑ **Parent:** [Optical interferometry](optical-interferometry.md)

[fringe tracking](fringe-tracking.md) estimates changing optical delay or phase and adjusts delay compensation to keep an [astronomical interferometer](astronomical-interferometer.md) near a useful interference condition. The delay must first lie inside the finite-bandwidth coherence envelope; rapid tracking then controls atmospheric piston. This is optical-path stabilization, with sensitivity and reference-source limitations.
