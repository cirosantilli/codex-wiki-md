# Principal argument

↑ **Parent:** [Argument (complex analysis)](argument-complex-analysis.md)

The [principal argument](principal-argument.md) selects a single [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) of a nonzero [complex number](complex-number.md), conventionally in $(-\pi,\pi]$. It is the [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) of the [principal logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md). The alternative interval $[-\pi,\pi)$ changes the convention on the negative [real axis](real-axis.md). Neither convention makes the selected [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) continuous across that axis.
