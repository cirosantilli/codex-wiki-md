# Spin ladder operator

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The operators $S_\pm=S_x\pm iS_y$ satisfy

$$
S_\pm|s,m\rangle
=\hbar\sqrt{s(s+1)-m(m\pm1)}|s,m\pm1\rangle.
$$
