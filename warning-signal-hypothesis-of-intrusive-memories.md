# Warning-signal hypothesis of intrusive memories

↑ **Parent:** [Intrusive memory](intrusive-memory.md)

The proposal that sensory impressions preceding the worst moments of a [trauma](psychological-trauma.md) acquire predictive significance and are later reexperienced as warning signals. Descriptive clinical content supports investigation of the account, while causal conditioning and retrospective reconstruction require separate tests.[Behaviour Research and Therapy 40, 995–1002; descriptions of intrusion content, sensory qualities and moments preceding worst impact](the-nature-of-intrusive-memories-after-trauma-the-warning-signal-hypothesis-by-anke-ehlers-and-others.md)
