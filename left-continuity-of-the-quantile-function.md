# Left continuity of the quantile function

↑ **Parent:** [Quantile function](quantile-function.md)

For $Q(u)=\inf\{x:F(x)\geq u\}$, $0<u<1$, the [quantile function](quantile-function.md) is a [monotone function](monotonic-function.md) and is left-continuous. The [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is right-continuous, giving $Q(u)\leq x$ exactly when $u\leq F(x)$. If $\sup_{v<u}Q(v)<Q(u)$, a point between them has $F(x)<u$ and contradicts this equivalence for some $v\in(F(x),u)$. A jump just to the right of $u$ is possible when $F$ is flat on an interval.
