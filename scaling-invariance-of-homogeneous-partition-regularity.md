# Scaling invariance of homogeneous partition regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Partition regular matrix](partition-regular-matrix.md)

For any positive integer $c$, a homogeneous rational matrix is [partition regular](partition-regular-matrix.md) over $c\mathbb N$ if and only if it is [partition regular](partition-regular-matrix.md) over $\mathbb N$. Pull a colouring of $c\mathbb N$ back along $n\mapsto cn$ and scale the resulting solution. Conversely, restrict a colouring of all positive integers to $c\mathbb N$. The argument depends on homogeneity and does not automatically extend to inhomogeneous equations or prescribed distinctness conditions.
