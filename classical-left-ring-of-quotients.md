# Classical left ring of quotients

↑ **Parent:** [Ore localization](ore-localization.md)

This is the localization of a [ring](ring.md) at all its [regular elements of a ring](regular-element-of-a-ring.md), with left fractions. It exists precisely when those denominators satisfy the left [Ore condition](ore-condition.md). If $R$ embeds in a ring $Q$, all regular elements become units in $Q$, and every element of $Q$ is such a fraction, the Ore condition follows by writing $rc^{-1}=s^{-1}b$, which gives $sr=bc$.
