# Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For an incompressible irrotational two-dimensional [potential flow](potential-flow.md), an analytic [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) combines the velocity potential $\Phi$ and [streamfunction](stream-function.md) $\Psi$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $u=\Phi_x=\Psi_y$ and $v=\Phi_y=-\Psi_x$, hence $W'(z)=u-iv$. Curves of constant $\Psi$ are [streamlines](streamline.md). An analytic coordinate change $z=f(\zeta)$ transforms the complex velocity by $dW/dz=(dW/d\zeta)/f'(\zeta)$ wherever $f'\neq0$.
