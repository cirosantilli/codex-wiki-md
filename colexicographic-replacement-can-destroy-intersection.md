# Colexicographic replacement can destroy intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Intersecting family](intersecting-family.md)

On five points, the four sets $12,13,14,15$ are an [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md). The first four two-element sets in [colexicographic order](colexicographic-order.md) are $12,13,23,14$, containing the disjoint pair $23,14$. Thus replacing a family by an equally large [colexicographic initial segment](colexicographic-initial-segment.md) can destroy intersection, even though it minimizes the [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md).
