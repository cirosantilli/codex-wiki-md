# Gene

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene)

A [gene](gene.md) is an inherited functional unit of genetic material. In cellular [organisms](organism-split.md), a [gene](gene.md) is a region of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) whose information produces a functional [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) or contributes to a [protein](protein-split.md). This connects inheritance to cellular chemistry: a change in a [gene](gene.md) can alter the amount or activity of a [protein](protein-split.md), and thereby a [phenotype](phenotype.md). See [NHGRI: gene](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Gene).

Alternative inherited versions at a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) are [alleles](allele.md). The distinction matters: two parents can carry the same [gene](gene.md) but different [alleles](allele.md), and a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) can have variation outside a [gene](gene.md). Neither the word [gene](gene.md) nor a change in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) alone specifies the resulting [phenotype](phenotype.md).
