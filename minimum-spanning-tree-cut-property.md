# Minimum spanning tree cut property

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md)

The unique lightest [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) across a [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md) belongs to every [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md). If a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) omits it, inserting it creates a [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) with another [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) across that [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md). Removing that heavier [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) gives a lighter [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md), a contradiction. This proves the correctness of [Prim algorithm](prim-s-algorithm.md) and [Kruskal algorithm](kruskal-s-algorithm.md) for distinct weights.
