# Coordinatively unsaturated complex

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination complex](coordination-complex.md)

A complex with an accessible coordination site or low [electron](electron.md) count that permits [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) capture. [electron](electron.md) deficiency and geometric accessibility must both be assessed.
