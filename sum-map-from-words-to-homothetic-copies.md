# Sum map from words to homothetic copies

↑ **Parent:** [Gallai theorem for an integer lattice](gallai-theorem-for-an-integer-lattice.md)

For a finite pattern $\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}$ in a [real vector space](real-vector-space.md), pull a [finite colouring](finite-coloring.md) back to words through the displayed sum map. A [combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md) with active coordinates J maps to $a+|J|\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}$. The [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md) therefore forces a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) [homothetic copy](homothetic-copy-of-a-finite-configuration.md). [Integer](integer.md) patterns remain in the [integer lattice](integer-lattice.md); choosing b sufficiently large gives the positive-lattice version.
