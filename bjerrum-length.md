# Bjerrum length

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bjerrum_length)

For a chosen reference charge $q$, the separation at which its Coulomb interaction energy equals $k_BT$. The charge-$q$ convention is $\ell_B=q^2/(4\pi\epsilon_{\rm abs}k_BT)$ in SI, or $\ell_B=\beta q^2/\epsilon$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md). The commonly tabulated value uses the elementary charge; changing [counterion](counterion.md) valence requires tracking that reference convention. It sets the strength of [counterion condensation](counterion-condensation.md).
