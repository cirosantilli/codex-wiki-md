# Meniscus (liquid surface)

↑ **Parent:** [Solution preparation and volumetric calibration](solution-preparation-and-volumetric-calibration.md)

A [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md) is the curved boundary where a [liquid](liquid.md) meets a vessel wall. In the transparent [water](water.md) examples above, the bottom of this curve is aligned with the calibration mark at eye level. Reading from above or below changes the apparent alignment without changing the contained [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
