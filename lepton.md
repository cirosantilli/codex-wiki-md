# Lepton

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model fermion](standard-model-fermion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lepton)

A lepton is a [Standard Model fermion](standard-model-fermion.md) without [color charge](color-charge.md). The charged leptons have [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-1$ and their corresponding [neutrinos](neutrino.md) have charge zero. There are three generations; their [antiparticles](antiparticle.md) carry opposite [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [lepton number](lepton-number.md). Charged leptons and neutrinos have [lepton number](lepton-number.md) $+1$, with the corresponding antiparticles carrying $-1$.
