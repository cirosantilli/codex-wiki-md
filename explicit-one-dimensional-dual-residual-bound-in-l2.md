# Explicit one-dimensional dual residual bound in L2

↑ **Parent:** [Aubin–Nitsche duality argument](aubin-nitsche-duality-argument.md)

For $-(pu')'+qu=f$ with one homogeneous Neumann condition and one fixed Dirichlet value, the dual problem has error forcing and homogeneous corresponding boundary data. [Galerkin orthogonality](galerkin-orthogonality.md) with its nodal interpolant yields an element-residual identity with no jump terms, because the interpolation error vanishes at every node. With $p_*=\min p>0$, $q_*=\min q>0$, $q^*=\max q$, one reliable constant is

$$
C=\frac1{\pi^2p_*}\left(1+\frac{q^*}{q_*}
+\frac{\|p'\|_\infty}{\sqrt{p_*q_*}}\right).
$$

The proof combines a dual $H^2$ estimate with two interval Poincaré inequalities. Constants are independent of the nonuniform mesh.
