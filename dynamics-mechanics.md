# Dynamics (mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanics](mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamics_(mechanics))

The study of physical evolution through [dynamical equations](dynamical-equation.md), including how [forces](force-split.md) affect [motion](motion.md). A law relating [force](force-split.md) to [acceleration](acceleration.md) must be combined with an interaction law and [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) to predict a history.
