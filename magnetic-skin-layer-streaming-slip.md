# Magnetic skin-layer streaming slip

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic skin layer](magnetic-skin-layer.md)

In a thin [magnetic skin layer](magnetic-skin-layer.md) next to a rigid [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md), vertical [pressure](pressure.md) balance gives $p=P-C^2e^{-2y/\delta}/(4\mu_0)$. With negligible inertia in the layer, the tangential [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) balance is $\rho\nu u_{yy}=p_x$. Matching zero leading shear outside the layer gives $u=U(1-e^{-2y/\delta})$ and the displayed effective tangential slip. The physical wall still has zero [velocity](velocity.md); the slip is a boundary condition for the outer flow extrapolated through the unresolved layer. It points toward larger squared boundary-field amplitude.
