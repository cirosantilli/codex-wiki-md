# Mnemonic reinstatement

↑ **Parent:** [Memory retrieval](memory-retrieval.md)

Reactivation during retrieval of neural patterns or cells associated with a previous experience. It can be selective without reproducing every detail of the original sensory response. Correlated reinstatement and successful recall are not automatically a proof of causation.
