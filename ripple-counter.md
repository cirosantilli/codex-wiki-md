# Ripple counter

↑ **Parent:** [Binary counter](binary-counter.md)

A cascade in which an output transition of one [flip-flop](flip-flop.md) clocks the next. With positive-edge-triggered toggle stages and uncomplemented $Q$ connections, it decrements modulo $2^n$: a zero [bit](bit.md) rises to one and propagates a borrow, while a one [bit](bit.md) falls to zero and stops propagation. Ripple propagation takes time, so intermediate transient states are not the settled counter state.
