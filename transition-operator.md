# Transition operator

↑ **Parent:** [Markov property](markov-property.md)

The operator that averages a function over the next-state conditional law of a time-homogeneous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md). For a [random walk](random-walk.md) with increments of law $\nu$ independent of the past including the initial state, $(Ph)(s)=\int h(s+y)\nu(dy)$. This additive operator preserves [convexity](convex-function.md) when the integrals exist.
