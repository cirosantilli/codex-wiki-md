# Magnetic field-line curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md)

The curvature [vector](vector.md) of a [magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md) parametrized by arc length. For circular lines, it points towards their center and has magnitude $1/R$. The curvature contribution to [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) is $B^2\boldsymbol\kappa/(4\pi)$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md).
