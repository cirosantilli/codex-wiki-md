# Hamiltonian constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar Gauss equation](scalar-gauss-equation.md)

The Hamiltonian constraint is the normal-normal projection of the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) onto a spatial hypersurface. With the extrinsic-curvature convention used here,

$$
{}^{(3)}R+K^2-K_{ij}K^{ij}=16\pi\rho.
$$
