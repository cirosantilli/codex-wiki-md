# Supercritical pitchfork bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Pitchfork bifurcation normal form](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md)

In the displayed [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) normal form, zero is stable for $\mu<0$ and unstable for $\mu>0$. At $\mu=0$, a symmetric stable pair $x=\pm\sqrt\mu$ appears on the side where zero is unstable. Reversing the parameter orientation changes the side, while retaining the supercritical relationship between the new stable branches and the destabilized central branch.
