# SL2 over F2 as a permutation group

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group over a finite field](special-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

A determinant-one matrix over $\mathbb F_2$ permutes the three nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_2^2$. This [group action](group-action.md) is faithful because fixing two basis vectors forces the matrix to be the identity. There are $(4-1)(4-2)=6$ invertible matrices, all of determinant one since the only nonzero field element is one, so the action identifies the group with the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_3$. Reduction of integer determinant-one matrices modulo two is onto: $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ induce two distinct transpositions, which generate $S_3$. Its kernel consists of matrices congruent to the identity modulo two, the level-two [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md).
