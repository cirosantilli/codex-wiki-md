# Two abundance crossings of the Press-Schechter mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter abundance growth at fixed mass](press-schechter-abundance-growth-at-fixed-mass.md)

At fixed mass, the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) has abundance per logarithmic interval proportional to $\nu e^{-\nu^2/2}$. This increases as $\nu$ falls from infinity to one and then decreases as $\nu$ falls toward zero, reflecting incorporation of small [dark-matter haloes](dark-matter-halo.md) into larger ones. A target below the maximum therefore has two mathematical crossings if the [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md) can increase without limit. The large-$\nu$ crossing describes first appearance; the small-$\nu$ crossing is late depletion. Late vacuum domination limits growth, so a formal future crossing need not be reached physically.
