# Cartan-Ambrose-Hicks theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan–Ambrose–Hicks_theorem)

A linear [isometry](isometry.md) between tangent spaces of complete simply connected [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) extends to a global [isometry](isometry.md) when parallel transport along corresponding broken [geodesics](geodesic.md) identifies their [Riemann curvature tensors](riemann-curvature-tensor.md). In particular, complete simply connected manifolds of the same dimension and constant [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) are isometric: their curvature tensors are determined by that common constant and their [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md). This is the constant-curvature form often called Cartan's theorem.
