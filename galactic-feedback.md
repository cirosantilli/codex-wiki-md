# Galactic feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)

[galactic feedback](galactic-feedback.md) couples [star formation](star-formation.md) or [black-hole accretion](black-hole-accretion.md) to the surrounding [gas](gas.md) through [energy](energy-split.md), [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [mass](mass.md) exchange. It includes [stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md) and [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md). A feedback loop may heat, stir, expel or compress [gas](gas.md), so its sign depends on how the [gas](gas.md) supply and cooling respond; [energy](energy-split.md) injection alone does not establish a lasting reduction of [star formation](star-formation.md).
