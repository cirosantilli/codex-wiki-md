# Discrete-time Markov chain martingale characterization

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

An adapted process on a finite or countable state space has the [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) transition matrix $P$ exactly when the displayed process is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) for every bounded test function $f$. The forward implication follows by taking the [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) of its increment. Conversely, the martingale property gives $\mathbb E[f(X_{n+1})\mid\mathcal F_n]=(Pf)(X_n)$; testing individual-state indicators recovers the entire conditional transition law. Stopping these martingales turns equations $(P-I)h=0$ and $(P-I)v=-1$ into hitting probabilities and expected hitting times.
