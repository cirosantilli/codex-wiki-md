# Formal transpose of a differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Differential operator](differential-operator.md)

The formal transpose is defined by the bilinear [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) identity $\int(Lf)g=\int f(L^tg)$ for [test functions](test-function.md). For $L=\sum_j b_j\partial_j$, it is $L^tg=-\sum_j\partial_j(b_jg)$; unlike the Hermitian [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md), it does not conjugate coefficients.
