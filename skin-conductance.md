# Skin conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrodermal activity](electrodermal-activity.md)

The [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) of skin, often measured between two recording electrodes. [Sweating](sweating.md) changes the conductive properties of the skin. Baseline, movement and recording conditions must be considered before interpreting an event-related response as evidence about [emotion](emotion.md).
