# Pipette

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid dissociation measurement](weak-acid-dissociation-measurement.md)

A [pipette](pipette.md) transfers a measured portion of a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md).

For an [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md), a [volumetric pipette](volumetric-pipette.md) selects an [aliquot](aliquot.md) whose [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) is $n=cV$ from a homogeneous sample. The delivered [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) depends on the instrument's calibration and drainage convention; the residual liquid of a gravity-draining [volumetric pipette](volumetric-pipette.md) is allowed for in that calibration. Its mark is read using the [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md). A volume calibration alone does not establish that the sampled [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) has the intended [concentration](concentration.md).[Laboratory Experiment 1. Calibration of Volumetric Glassware, pp. 1–2, Calibrating 25 mL pipette: eye-level meniscus, gravity drainage, retained liquid](calibration-of-volumetric-glassware-buffalo-state-laboratory-handout.md)
