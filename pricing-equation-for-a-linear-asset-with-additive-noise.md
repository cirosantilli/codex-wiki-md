# Pricing equation for a linear asset with additive noise

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral pricing](risk-neutral-pricing.md)

For a non-dividend-paying traded asset $dS=\mu Sdt+\sigma dW$ and bank account $dB=rBdt$, [delta hedging](delta-hedge.md) sets the share holding to $V_s$. Its bond value is $V-sV_s$. [Itô's formula](ito-s-lemma.md) and the [self-financing condition](self-financing-condition.md) give the displayed pricing equation on the whole [real line](real-line.md). The [linear drift change of measure](linear-drift-change-of-measure.md) supplies risk-neutral dynamics $dS=rSdt+\sigma dW^{\mathbb Q}$, so the same price is the discounted conditional payoff expectation under the usual admissibility and integrability hypotheses.
