# Porous medium equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous_medium_equation)

The porous medium equation $u_t=\Delta(u^m)$ with $m>1$ is a [nonlinear partial differential equation](nonlinear-partial-differential-equation.md) whose diffusivity vanishes at $u=0$. Unlike the [heat equation](heat-equation.md), it has compactly supported solutions with finite propagation speed.
