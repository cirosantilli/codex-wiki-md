# Binary relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

The binary relative entropy is the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) between Bernoulli distributions:

$$
\operatorname{kl}(a,b)
=a\log\frac ab+(1-a)\log\frac{1-a}{1-b}.
$$

It is the exact exponent obtained by optimizing the Chernoff bound for a Bernoulli mean.
