# Subdifferential of the L1 norm

↑ **Parent:** [Subdifferential](subdifferential.md)

The [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) of the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) consists of vectors $z$ with $z_j=\operatorname{sgn}(\beta_j)$ when $\beta_j\ne0$ and $z_j\in[-1,1]$ when $\beta_j=0$. These coordinate conditions give the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) for [Lasso](lasso.md) and [Square-root Lasso](square-root-lasso.md).
