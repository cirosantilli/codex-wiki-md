# Synthesized beam

↑ **Parent:** [Aperture synthesis](aperture-synthesis.md)

The [synthesized beam](synthesized-beam.md) is the angular response produced by the sampled and weighted [complex visibilities](complex-visibility.md). Its characteristic width is set by the longest well-weighted projected [interferometric baselines](interferometric-baseline.md), of order $\lambda/b_{\max}$, while sidelobes reflect incomplete Fourier coverage. It differs from the individual [antenna](antenna-radio.md) [primary beam](primary-beam.md), which limits the field of view.
