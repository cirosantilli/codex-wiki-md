<h1 id="santiago-ramon-y-cajal">Santiago Ramón y Cajal</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of neuroanatomists](list-of-neuroanatomists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Ramón_y_Cajal)

His drawings of [neurons](neuron-split.md) made their branching structure visible with extraordinary clarity. The historical illustration under [Purkinje cell](purkinje-cell.md) shows how different [neurons](neuron-split.md) can occupy the same part of the [cerebellum](cerebellum.md) while having strikingly different shapes.
