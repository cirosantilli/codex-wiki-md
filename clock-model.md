# Clock model

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clock_model)

The clock model places a planar [unit vector](unit-vector.md) at every [lattice](lattice.md) site but restricts its angle to finitely many equally spaced values. Nearest-neighbour spins usually interact through the cosine of their angular difference.
