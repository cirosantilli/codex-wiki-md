# p-adic integer

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic valuation](p-adic-valuation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/p-adic_integer)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, the ring of p-adic integers is the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md)

$$
\mathbb Z_p=\varprojlim_n\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z.
$$

Every p-adic integer has a unique convergent expansion $\sum_{j\geq0}b_jp^j$ with $0\leq b_j<p$. It is a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) with [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $p$ and [residue field](residue-field.md) $\mathbb F_p$.
