# Ground wave

↑ **Parent:** [Ionosphere](ionosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_wave)

A ground wave is a [radio wave](radio-wave.md) propagating near and along the surface of [Earth](earth.md). In a short-baseline comparison with a [skywave](skywave.md), it supplies a reference path. Approximating its distance by the station separation and its speed by the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) is a modeling choice; ground properties and [frequency](frequency.md) can also affect its attenuation and [wave phase](phase-waves.md).
