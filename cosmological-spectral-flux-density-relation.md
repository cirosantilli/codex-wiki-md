# Cosmological spectral flux-density relation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological flux dimming](cosmological-flux-dimming.md)

A comoving source's [spectral luminosity](spectral-luminosity.md) $L_{\nu_e}$ specifies emitted power per unit emitted [frequency](frequency.md). The [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) reduces photon [energy](energy.md) and arrival rate by two factors of $1+z$, but also narrows the received frequency interval by one factor. Consequently its observed [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md) is $L_{\nu_e}/[4\pi a_0^2r^2(1+z)]$, with $\nu_e=(1+z)\nu_0$. Substituting [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) $d_L=(1+z)a_0r$ gives the displayed relation. Integrating over received [frequency](frequency.md) recovers the bolometric [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) $L/(4\pi d_L^2)$. A fixed-frequency comparison must evaluate the source spectrum at the shifted emitted [frequency](frequency.md).
