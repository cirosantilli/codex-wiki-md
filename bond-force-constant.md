# Bond force constant

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic bond stretch](diatomic-bond-stretch.md)

The [bond force constant](bond-force-constant.md) is the curvature of the effective [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at the equilibrium [bond length](bond-length.md). For a [diatomic bond stretch](diatomic-bond-stretch.md), it plays the role of a [spring constant](spring-constant.md). Its definition is local: two wells with the same curvature can have different [bond-dissociation energies](bond-dissociation-energy.md).
