# Lamb dip

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md)

The narrow reduction in [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) near the common resonance of opposed beams. The [Doppler-free absorption line model](doppler-free-absorption-line-model.md) illustrates its sign and contrast; the same feature appears as increased probe transmission.
