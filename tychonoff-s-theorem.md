<h1 id="tychonoff-s-theorem">Tychonoff's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonoff's_theorem)

An arbitrary product of [compact spaces](compact-space.md) is compact in the [product topology](product-topology.md). The theorem is equivalent to the axiom of choice.
