# Boron trifluoride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An isolated [boron trifluoride](boron-trifluoride.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) has [trigonal planar molecular geometry](trigonal-planar-molecular-geometry.md) and the [D3h molecular point group](d3h-molecular-point-group.md). Its three equal fluorine contributions give $I_\parallel=3m_{\rm F}R^2$ and $I_\perp=3m_{\rm F}R^2/2$ in the [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) approximation.
