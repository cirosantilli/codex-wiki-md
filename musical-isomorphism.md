# Musical isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_isomorphism)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) gives inverse [vector bundle isomorphisms](vector-bundle-isomorphism.md)

$$
\flat_g:TM\to T^*M,\quad v\mapsto g(v,\cdot),\qquad
\sharp_g:T^*M\to TM.
$$

In coordinates they have [matrices](matrix.md) $g_{ij}$ and $g^{ij}$. Every [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) admits a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) by a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md), so its tangent and [cotangent bundles](cotangent-bundle.md) are isomorphic as real smooth [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md). This isomorphism depends on the metric, not merely on the manifold.
