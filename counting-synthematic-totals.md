# Counting synthematic totals

↑ **Parent:** [Total of synthemes](total-of-synthemes.md)

Two disjoint [synthemes](syntheme.md) form a six-cycle, whose complement is a triangular prism. The prism has a unique partition into three perfect matchings, so the pair extends uniquely to a [total of synthemes](total-of-synthemes.md). Eight synthemes are disjoint from a fixed syntheme; grouping them four per total yields two totals through it, and double counting gives six totals in all.
