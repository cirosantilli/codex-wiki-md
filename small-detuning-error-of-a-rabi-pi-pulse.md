# Small-detuning error of a Rabi pi pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Rabi pi pulse](rabi-pi-pulse.md)

For the [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md) at fixed resonant pulse duration $T_\pi=\pi/\Omega$, put $x=\delta/\Omega$. Its transfer probability is

$$
P_\pi(x)=\frac{\sin^2[(\pi/2)\sqrt{1+x^2}]}{1+x^2}.
$$

The sine-squared numerator differs from one only at order $x^4$, while the denominator gives $1-x^2+O(x^4)$. Thus the leading population-transfer error is $x^2$. For a target transfer error $\epsilon$, the frequency mismatch in cycles per second should satisfy $|\delta\nu|\lesssim\Omega\sqrt\epsilon/(2\pi)$ in this small-detuning regime. This is not a complete characterization of every coherent gate error.
