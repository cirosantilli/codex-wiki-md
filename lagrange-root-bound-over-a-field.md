# Lagrange root bound over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md)

A nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $d$ over a [field](field.md) has at most $d$ distinct roots. Indeed, each root supplies a linear factor by [polynomial division](polynomial-division.md), and induction on $d$ gives the bound.
