# Fermi function

↑ **Parent:** [Allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md)

The electron-nucleus Coulomb-distortion factor multiplying an otherwise plane-wave [beta decay](beta-decay.md) spectrum. It depends on daughter charge, [lepton](lepton.md) charge and [lepton](lepton.md) [energy](energy-split.md). Its [energy](energy-split.md) dependence means that a pure fifth-power phase-space rule is not generally exact.
