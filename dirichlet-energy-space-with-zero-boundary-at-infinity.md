# Dirichlet energy space with zero boundary at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md)

For a transient weighted graph, $H_0$ is the completion of the finitely supported functions in the norm $\lVert f\rVert_{H_0}=\mathcal E(f,f)^{1/2}$. Its elements have finite [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) and vanish at infinity in the energy sense.
