# Terminal velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity](velocity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_velocity)

Terminal velocity is the constant limiting velocity reached when a velocity-dependent resistive force balances the applied force.
