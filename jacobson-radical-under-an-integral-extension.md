# Jacobson radical under an integral extension

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md)

For an [integral extension](integral-extension.md) $A\subseteq B$ of commutative rings,

$$
J(A)=J(B)\cap A.
$$

Contraction sends maximal ideals of $B$ to maximal ideals of $A$, while the [Lying-over theorem](lying-over-theorem.md) puts a maximal ideal of $B$ over every maximal ideal of $A$.
