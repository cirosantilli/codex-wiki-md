# Pressurized water reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A water-cooled and water-moderated [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md) whose primary coolant is pressurized to suppress bulk boiling in the core. Heat is transferred to a secondary circuit through steam generators.
