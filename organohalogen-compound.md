# Organohalogen compound

↑ **Parent:** [Organic compound](organic-compound.md)

An [organic compound](organic-compound.md) containing a carbon-halogen bond. The bonding environment matters: a [vinylic halide](vinylic-halide.md) has the halogen directly attached to a double-bonded carbon, whereas an [allylic halide](allylic-halide.md) has it attached to the adjacent saturated carbon.
