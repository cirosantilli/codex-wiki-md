# Kepler-harmonic epicyclic precession

↑ **Parent:** [Radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

For $\Phi=-GM/r+\alpha\omega^2r^2/2$, nearly circular motion has $\Omega^2=GM/r^3+\alpha\omega^2$ and $\kappa^2=GM/r^3+4\alpha\omega^2$. Their generally nonrational ratio makes small-eccentricity orbits precess even if $\alpha$ was added slowly. This illustrates [adiabatic actions do not preserve orbital closure](adiabatic-actions-do-not-preserve-orbital-closure.md).
