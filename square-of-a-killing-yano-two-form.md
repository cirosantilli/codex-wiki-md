# Square of a Killing-Yano two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Killing-Yano two-form](killing-yano-two-form.md)

The square is a [rank-two Killing tensor](rank-two-killing-tensor.md). It is symmetric because it pairs the covectors $Y_{a\cdot}$ and $Y_{b\cdot}$ using the metric. Along every affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md), its contraction $K(v,v)$ is the squared norm of the carried by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) covector $Y(v,\cdot)$. [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) makes that norm constant, proving the [Killing tensor](killing-tensor.md) equation. On a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) this tensor is positive semidefinite.
