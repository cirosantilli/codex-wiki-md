# Surface diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Insoluble surfactant](insoluble-surfactant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_diffusion)

Surface diffusion transports particles along an interface down a [concentration](concentration.md) gradient. On an isotropic smooth interface with constant diffusivity $D_s$, its flux is $-D_s\nabla_sC$ and its concentration source is $D_s\Delta_sC$, using the [surface gradient](surface-gradient.md) and [surface Laplacian](surface-laplacian.md).
