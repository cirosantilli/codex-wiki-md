# Primitive Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

A [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) modulo $N$ is primitive if its character on $(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times$ does not factor through reduction to the unit group for any proper divisor of $N$. Its least inducing modulus is called its conductor. Primitivity is essential in the usual formula expressing its finite Fourier transform as a conjugate character times a [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md).
