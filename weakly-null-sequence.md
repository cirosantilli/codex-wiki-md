# Weakly null sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

A sequence $(x_n)$ is weakly null when $f(x_n)\to0$ for every member $f$ of the [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md). It may remain bounded away from zero in norm.
