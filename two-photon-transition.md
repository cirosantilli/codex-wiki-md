# Two-photon transition

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole transition](electric-dipole-transition.md)

A coherent transition involving two atom-field interactions. [two-photon absorption](two-photon-absorption.md) uses a sum of [photon](photon.md) [energies](energy-split.md); stimulated absorption-emission uses their difference. A second-order intermediate contribution need not represent real population in a stationary atomic state.
