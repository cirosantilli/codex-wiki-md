# Alpha particle

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_particle)

The helium-four [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md), containing two [protons](proton.md) and two [neutrons](neutron.md). Its ground-state [spin](spin.md) and [isospin](isospin.md) are zero. A quantized [cubic four-Skyrmion](cubic-four-skyrmion.md) supplies a corresponding nuclear state in the soliton description.
