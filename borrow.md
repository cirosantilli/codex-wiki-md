# Borrow

↑ **Parent:** [Subtraction](subtraction.md)

In positional [subtraction](subtraction.md), a higher place supplies one unit of its place value when the current place cannot subtract the requested digit. In [binary representation](binary-number.md), [subtraction](subtraction.md) of one, a low [bit](bit.md) toggles at every operation and the borrow continues through each zero [bit](bit.md), stopping at the first one [bit](bit.md). Thus decrementing a [binary counter](binary-counter.md) toggles position $i$ exactly when all less significant [bits](bit.md) were zero. This is the downward-counting counterpart of a [carry](carry-arithmetic.md) propagating through one [bits](bit.md) during incrementing.
