# Protein domain

↑ **Parent:** [Protein structure](protein-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_domain)

A [protein domain](protein-domain.md) is a distinguishable structural and functional region of a [protein](protein.md). A catalytic [protein domain](protein-domain.md) and a binding [protein domain](protein-domain.md) can carry out different tasks within one chain.
