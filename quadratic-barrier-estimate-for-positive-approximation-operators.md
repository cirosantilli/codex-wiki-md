# Quadratic barrier estimate for positive approximation operators

↑ **Parent:** [Korovkin theorem](korovkin-theorem.md)

For [positive linear operators on continuous functions](positive-linear-operator-on-continuous-functions.md) on $[0,1]$, put $e_j=U_n(x^j)-x^j$. Uniform continuity supplies the barriers $|f(x)-f(t)|\le\varepsilon+\gamma(x-t)^2$. Applying positivity and evaluating at $t$ gives the displayed estimate because $U_n((x-t)^2)(t)=e_2(t)-2te_1(t)+t^2e_0(t)$. The constant $\gamma$ is independent of $t$ and $n$. Thus uniform reproduction of $1,x,x^2$ proves [Korovkin theorem](korovkin-theorem.md) convergence on every continuous function; exact reproduction forces the operator to be the identity.
