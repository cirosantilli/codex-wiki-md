# Framed cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism](cobordism.md)

Framed cobordism is the [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md) of stable cohomotopy. Its geometric maps have a [stable framing of a vector bundle](stable-framing-of-a-vector-bundle.md) on their normal data. A finite covering has canonically framed zero-dimensional relative tangent data; its Gysin class of $1$ is the stable transfer of the cover. A trivial $d$-sheeted cover gives $d\cdot1$ in degree zero.
