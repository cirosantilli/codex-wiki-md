# Cancellation of vacuum bubbles

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum Feynman diagram](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md)

A perturbative correlator factors into an exponential of connected [Vacuum Feynman diagrams](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md) times a sum of [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) attached to external insertions. Dividing its [path integral](path-integral.md) by the vacuum [generating functional](generating-functional.md) at zero source cancels that exponential, including all disconnected products of vacuum components. The corresponding [operator formalism](operator-formalism.md) uses the same denominator in the vacuum expectation of the [Dyson series](dyson-series.md). This leaves externally disconnected correlators intact; the [connected generating functional](connected-generating-functional.md) removes their products.
