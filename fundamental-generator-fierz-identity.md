# Fundamental-generator Fierz identity

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

For the [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) of SU(N), take traceless Hermitian generators with $\operatorname{Tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$. Along with the identity matrix, they form an [orthogonal basis](orthogonal-basis.md) of all complex matrices. Expanding a matrix in this basis and subtracting its identity component proves the displayed completeness relation. It separates exchanged-index and unchanged-index contributions to color amplitudes.
