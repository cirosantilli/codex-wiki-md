# Category of injective functions

↑ **Parent:** [Arrow category](arrow-category.md)

The category of injective functions is the full subcategory of $[\mathbf 2,\mathbf{Set}]$ whose objects are [injective functions](injective-function.md). It is cartesian closed: products are computed pointwise, and exponentiating an injection by any arrow again gives an injection.
