# Redshifting of peculiar momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md)

A freely moving massive test particle in an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) has physical [momentum](momentum-split.md) $p=m\gamma v$ relative to a [comoving observer](comoving-observer.md), where $v$ is its [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md). For a radial [geodesic](geodesic.md), the [geodesic Lagrangian](geodesic-lagrangian.md) gives $C=a^2d\chi/d\tau$ constant, and the local radial [momentum](momentum-split.md) is $p=ma\,d\chi/d\tau=mC/a$. The [proper-time normalization](proper-time-normalization.md) gives $dt/d\tau=\sqrt{1+C^2/(a^2c^2)}$, so

$$
v=\frac{C/a}{\sqrt{1+C^2/(a^2c^2)}}.
$$

Consequently nonrelativistic [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) scales as $a^{-1}$, while the exact relativistic law keeps $|v|<c$. The [momentum](momentum-split.md) magnitude of a free [photon](photon.md) also scales as $a^{-1}$, giving [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) through $E=cp$. These statements concern force-free propagation; a bound system need not follow the cosmological expansion.
