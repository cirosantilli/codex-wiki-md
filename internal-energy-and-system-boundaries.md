# Internal energy and system boundaries

↑ **Parent:** [Internal energy](internal-energy.md)

For an ordinary stationary laboratory sample, a useful convention is

$$
E_{\rm total}=U+K_{\rm bulk}+\Phi_{\rm ext},\qquad
\Delta U+\Delta K_{\rm bulk}+\Delta\Phi_{\rm ext}
=Q_{\rm in}+W_{\rm on}.
$$

The bulk [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $K_{\rm bulk}$ includes organized whole-body motion; for pure translation it is $Mv_{\rm cm}^2/2$, with total [mass](mass.md) $M$ and [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) speed $v_{\rm cm}$. The external [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $\Phi_{\rm ext}$ includes elevation in an imposed [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md), approximately $Mgz$ in a uniform field. Accelerating a sealed bottle or lifting it without otherwise changing its state changes these mechanical terms, rather than its [internal energy](internal-energy.md). Molecular [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is measured relative to the chosen bulk motion.[§3.2, “Internal Energy”: molecular speeds relative to the center of mass; moving-train and roof examples](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/3-2-work-heat-and-internal-energy.md)

State the convention rather than applying this separation blindly. If an experiment separately tracks a rotating apparatus or an organized flow, its mechanical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) must not also be counted in $U$. In [Joule's paddle-wheel experiment](joule-s-paddle-wheel-experiment.md), waiting for the apparatus and fluid to come to rest removes such an endpoint ambiguity. Including the vessel and paddle changes whose [internal energy](internal-energy.md) is measured; including the falling weights additionally puts their changing [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) inside the enlarged system's total [energy](energy.md) balance.
