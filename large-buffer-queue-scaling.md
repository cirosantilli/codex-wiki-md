# Large-buffer queue scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling limit of a queue](scaling-limit-of-a-queue.md)

Hold a stable input law and [service rate of a queue](service-rate-of-a-queue.md) fixed while the [buffer size of a queue](buffer-size-of-a-queue.md) grows. Under interior exponential-moment and root hypotheses, the [Cramér-Lundberg workload exponent](cramer-lundberg-workload-exponent.md) gives an exponential tail linear in buffer size. A likely overflow accumulates during a lookback horizon proportional to the buffer size.
