# Existence theorem of global class field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Class field theory](class-field-theory.md)

Finite abelian extensions of $K$ correspond contravariantly to congruence subgroups $H$ with $P_K(\mathfrak m)\subseteq H\subseteq I_K(\mathfrak m)$, and the Artin map identifies $I_K(\mathfrak m)/H$ with the Galois group.
