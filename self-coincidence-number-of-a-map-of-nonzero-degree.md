# Self-coincidence number of a map of nonzero degree

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz coincidence number](lefschetz-coincidence-number.md)

For $f:M\to N$ between closed connected oriented manifolds of equal dimension, cyclic invariance of trace and $f^!f^*=(\deg f)\mathrm{id}$ give the formula. Thus if both the degree and the target [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) are nonzero, $f$ coincides somewhere with every map homotopic to it, by the [Lefschetz coincidence theorem](lefschetz-coincidence-theorem.md). For target $\mathbb{CP}^{2k}$ the Euler characteristic is $2k+1$.
