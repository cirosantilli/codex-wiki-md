# Equalizer submonad of a monad

↑ **Parent:** [Monad](monad.md)

In a [category](category-split.md) with [equalizers](equaliser.md), the equalizer of $\eta_{TA},T\eta_A$ defines a [functor](functor.md) $R$ by $\beta_BRf=Tf\beta_A$. It has unit $\alpha$ and multiplication $\theta$ characterized by

$$
\beta_A\alpha_A=\eta_A,\qquad \beta_A\theta_A=\mu_AT\beta_A\beta_{RA}.
$$

Cancelling the monomorphisms $\beta_A$ reduces its unit and associativity laws to those of $T$, so $\beta:R\Rightarrow T$ is a [monad morphism](monad-morphism.md). The [split equalizers associated with a monad](split-equalizers-associated-with-a-monad.md) show that $\alpha_{TA}$ is invertible. If every $T\beta_A$ is monic, then every $T\alpha_A$ is invertible, the naturality square of $\beta$ at $\beta_A$ is a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md), and $R$ is an [idempotent monad](idempotent-monad.md). This monicity hypothesis is essential to this last argument; arbitrary [functors](functor.md) need not preserve monomorphisms.
