# Transpose of a matrix product

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose](transpose.md)

For compatible finite [matrices](matrix.md), the [transpose](transpose.md) reverses the factor order. Entrywise, $[(AB)^T]_{ij}=\sum_kA_{jk}B_{ki}=\sum_k(B^T)_{ik}(A^T)_{kj}$, proving the identity. The analogous formula for [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md) is $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$. Commutativity of scalar entries permits the last rearrangement; the matrix products themselves are not assumed to [commute](commuting-elements.md).
