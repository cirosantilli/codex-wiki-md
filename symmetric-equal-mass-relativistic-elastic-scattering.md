# Symmetric equal-mass relativistic elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

A particle of total incident [energy](energy-split.md) $E$ and [rest energy](rest-energy.md) $E_0$ strikes an identical stationary particle. If the two outgoing paths make equal and opposite nonzero angles $\theta$ to the incoming direction in the [laboratory frame](laboratory-frame.md), transverse [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) makes their outgoing [energies](energy-split.md) equal to $(E+E_0)/2$. Longitudinal [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) and the [mass shell](mass-shell.md) relation imply $\tan^2\theta=2E_0/(E+E_0)$. The nonrelativistic limit is $\theta=45^\circ$; at large incident [energy](energy-split.md) both paths become forward directed.
