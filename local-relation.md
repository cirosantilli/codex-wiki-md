# Local relation

↑ **Parent:** [Fokas method](fokas-method.md)

A spectral-parameter-dependent divergence identity equivalent to a partial differential equation. Integrating it over a space-time region gives a [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) between transformed initial and boundary data. For the half-line free [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md), take $P=e^{-ikx+ik^2t}q$ and $Q=e^{-ikx+ik^2t}(-kq+iq_x)$.
