# Classification of real line bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Real line bundle](real-line-bundle.md)

Real line bundles over a paracompact space are classified up to isomorphism by their first [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md) in $H^1(X;\mathbb F_2)$. Tensor product corresponds to addition of classes.
