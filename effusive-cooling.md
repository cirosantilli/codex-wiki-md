# Effusive cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Effusion](effusion.md)

If a classical monatomic gas re-equilibrates by collisions while losing atoms by [effusion](effusion.md), the escaping atoms carry mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $2k_BT$. Without wall heating, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives $d(3Nk_BT/2)=2k_BT\,dN$ and therefore $T\propto N^{1/3}$. This assumes a fixed container [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and a small aperture in the molecular-flow regime.
