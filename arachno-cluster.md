# Arachno cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedral cluster](polyhedral-cluster.md)

An open cluster framework formally obtained by removing two vertices from a [closo](closo-cluster.md) parent. Its usual [electron](electron.md) count is $n+3$ skeletal pairs; [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) positions and cage distortions require additional structural information.
