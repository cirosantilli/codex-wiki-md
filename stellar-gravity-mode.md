# Stellar gravity mode

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md)

A stellar gravity mode is a nonradial [stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md) restored by [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) in stable stratification. In the local interior approximation it propagates where $\omega^2<N^2,S_\ell^2$. Such modes are distinct from [stellar acoustic modes](stellar-acoustic-mode.md) and do not have a radial $\ell=0$ gravity-wave branch.
