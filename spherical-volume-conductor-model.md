# Spherical volume conductor model

↑ **Parent:** [Volume conductor](volume-conductor.md)

A [spherical volume conductor model](spherical-volume-conductor-model.md) approximates a conducting body by a sphere, usually with uniform or radially stratified conductivity. It provides explicit forward models for [electroencephalography](electroencephalography.md) and [magnetoencephalography](magnetoencephalography.md). Its symmetry makes a [radial current dipole](radial-current-dipole.md) magnetically silent outside the sphere; departures from spherical symmetry need not retain that property.
