# Kelvin-Helmholtz dispersion relation with gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md)

Two deep layers of [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) with upper [mass density](density.md) $\rho_1$, lower [mass density](density.md) $\rho_2$, and tangential [velocities](velocity.md) $U_1,U_2$ have this [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) when [surface tension](surface-tension.md) is absent. Decaying [velocity potentials](velocity-potential.md) and the linearized [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) determine their amplitudes; the [dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface](dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md) gives the displayed relation. For $k>0$ the squared departure of the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c$ from the density-weighted mean [velocity](velocity.md) is $g(\rho_2-\rho_1)/[k(\rho_1+\rho_2)]-\rho_1\rho_2(U_1-U_2)^2/(\rho_1+\rho_2)^2$. A negative value gives [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) despite stable [density stratification](density-stratification.md).
