# Euclidean distance

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_distance)

The [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) between $x,y\in\mathbb R^d$ is

$$
\|x-y\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^d(x_i-y_i)^2}.
$$
