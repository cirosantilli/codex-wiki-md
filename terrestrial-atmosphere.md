# Terrestrial atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Earth](earth.md)

The terrestrial atmosphere has regions with opposite signs of the [atmospheric lapse rate](lapse-rate-atmosphere.md): the [troposphere](troposphere.md) usually cools upward, the [stratosphere](stratosphere.md) warms upward through ultraviolet heating, the [mesosphere](mesosphere.md) cools, and the [thermosphere](thermosphere.md) warms. A monotone [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md) can approximate one such interval, but cannot describe all their transitions with one fixed branch.
