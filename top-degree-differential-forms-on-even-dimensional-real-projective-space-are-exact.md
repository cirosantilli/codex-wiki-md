# Top-degree differential forms on even-dimensional real projective space are exact

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant primitive under a finite group action](invariant-primitive-under-a-finite-group-action.md)

For the double covering $\pi:S^{2n}\to\mathbb{RP}^{2n}$ and the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) $a$, every pulled-back top form satisfies $a^*\pi^*\omega=\pi^*\omega$. Since $\deg a=-1$, its integral over the sphere is its own negative and hence vanishes. It is therefore exact on the sphere. Averaging a primitive under $a$ and descending it proves that $\omega$ is exact on real projective space.
