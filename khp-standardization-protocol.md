# KHP standardization protocol

↑ **Parent:** [Primary standard](primary-standard.md)

Columbia University's institutional laboratory manual, Experiment 1, Glassware Calibration, Primary and Secondary Standards, and Manual Titrations, Part 3, specifies two weighed $0.2$–$0.3\,\mathrm g$ portions of [potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md), transferred completely into separate $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ [Erlenmeyer flasks](erlenmeyer-flask.md). Dissolve each in approximately $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ distilled [water](water.md); any warmed sample cools to room [temperature](temperature-split.md) before titration. Add two or three drops of [phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md). Rinse and condition a clamped [burette](burette.md) with the diluted [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md), then titrate while swirling to faint pink lasting about $20$ seconds. Calculate [concentration](concentration.md) from each portion's own [mass](mass.md) and delivered [titre](titre.md); repeat if results scatter. Part 1 identifies a Mettler balance, and Part 2 includes paired weighings of exposed [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) and [potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md). These are prescribed procedures, not reported measurements.[Experiment 1; Part 1, balance capacity note; Part 2, steps 1–3; Part 3, steps 1–6 and results table](glassware-calibration-primary-and-secondary-standards-and-manual-titrations-columbia-university-experiment-1.md)

The same manual directs keeping the diluted [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) covered and limiting air exposure after [titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md).[Experiment 1, Part 3, introductory paragraph and step 1](glassware-calibration-primary-and-secondary-standards-and-manual-titrations-columbia-university-experiment-1.md) The MIT handout explains the associated [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) problem: carbonate contamination of [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) is controlled during preparation, bottles are promptly capped, and atmospheric [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) makes the pink [phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md) endpoint fade.[Printed Titration-5, §IV.1 and footnote 3; Titration-6, footnote 6](the-potentiometric-titration-of-an-acid-mixture-mit-5-310-laboratory-handout.md) Consequently, a standardized [concentration](concentration.md) applies to the stored [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) under its handling conditions; continued uptake of [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) can change the reacting inventory per delivered [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).

The color is the readout, and [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) supplies the inference. [Phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md) indicates a [pH](ph.md) range, so its first reproducible pale pink is an operational [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md), rather than a direct observation of equal reacting inventories. A [potentiometric titration](potentiometric-titration.md) can locate an endpoint from a [pH](ph.md) versus [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) curve, but [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) calibration alone still does not determine [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) [concentration](concentration.md). Repeat portions test scatter; incomplete dissolution, an unmatched [titration blank](titration-blank.md), or a shared endpoint error can bias all repeats together.
