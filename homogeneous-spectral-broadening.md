# Homogeneous spectral broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line broadening](spectral-line-broadening.md)

Broadening shared by otherwise identical emitters, often described by a common [optical coherence time](optical-coherence-time.md) $T_2$. Exponential field-coherence decay produces a [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) with full angular-frequency width $2/T_2$. Population decay, [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) and collisions can all contribute, with their time constants distinguished.
