# Direct limit of abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Direct system of abelian groups](direct-system-of-abelian-groups.md)

The direct limit of a [direct system of abelian groups](direct-system-of-abelian-groups.md) is the quotient of $\bigoplus_aG_a$ by the relations identifying $g\in G_a$ with $\phi_{ab}(g)\in G_b$ whenever $a\leq b$.
