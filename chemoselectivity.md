# Chemoselectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Selectivity](selectivity.md)

Preferential reaction of one functional group or bond type in a [molecule](molecule-split.md) containing alternatives. It depends on reagents, conditions and reaction time rather than being an absolute property of a functional group.
