# Canonical-height lattice-point growth

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical height of an elliptic curve](canonical-height-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

If an elliptic curve over $\mathbb Q$ has [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md) of rank $r$, then its [canonical height of an elliptic curve](canonical-height-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) is a positive-definite [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) on the free lattice. Consequently

$$
\#\{P:\widehat h(P)\leq B\}=O(B^{r/2}).
$$
