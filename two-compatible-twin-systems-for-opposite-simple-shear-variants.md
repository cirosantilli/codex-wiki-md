# Two compatible twin systems for opposite simple-shear variants

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-one connection](rank-one-connection.md)

The unrotated variants differ by $2\delta e_3\otimes e_1$, giving [normal](normal-distribution.md) $e_1$. The [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) about $e_2$ with $\cos\theta=(1-\delta^2)/(1+\delta^2)$ and $\sin\theta=2\delta/(1+\delta^2)$ gives $U_+-\overline R U_-=[2\delta/(1+\delta^2)](e_1+\delta e_3)\otimes e_3$, giving the second [normal](normal-distribution.md) $e_3$. If these are the only two compatible [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md), the only gradients in $SO(3)U_+$ compatible with $\overline R U_-$ are $U_+$ and $\overline R U_+$. Left multiplication by a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) preserves [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md), so the second classification follows directly from the first.
