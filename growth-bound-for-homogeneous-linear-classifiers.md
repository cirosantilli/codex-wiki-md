# Growth bound for homogeneous linear classifiers

↑ **Parent:** [VC dimension of a vector space](vc-dimension-of-a-vector-space.md)

The signs of homogeneous linear forms $x\mapsto x^T\beta$ on $\mathbb R^p$ have VC dimension at most $p$. The [Sauer-Shelah growth bound](sauer-shelah-growth-bound.md) therefore gives

$$
s(\mathcal H,n)\leq(n+1)^p.
$$
