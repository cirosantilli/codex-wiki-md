# Ecliptic longitude

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital observations behind the area law](orbital-observations-behind-the-area-law.md)

An angular coordinate around the [celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md) measured along the [Earth](earth.md)'s orbital reference [plane](plane.md). An Earth-based [ecliptic longitude](ecliptic-longitude.md) specifies a viewing direction; it does not by itself give a body's distance or its Sun-based [position](position.md).
