# Exterior square of the defining orthogonal representation

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)

For a complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) $Q$, the map $u\wedge v\mapsto[w\mapsto Q(v,w)u-Q(u,w)v]$ identifies the [exterior square](exterior-square.md) of the defining representation with the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of $\mathfrak{so}(V)$. Equivariance follows from invariance of $Q$; an orthogonal basis verifies bijectivity. It is irreducible when $\mathfrak{so}(V)$ is simple. In dimension four it splits into the adjoint modules of the two commuting [sl2 Lie algebras](sl2-lie-algebra.md).
