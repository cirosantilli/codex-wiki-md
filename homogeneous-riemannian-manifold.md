# Homogeneous Riemannian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)

A [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is homogeneous when its [isometry](isometry.md) group acts transitively on points. For example, a [Lie group](lie-group.md) with a [left-invariant metric](left-invariant-metric.md) is homogeneous under left translations. Homogeneity gives the same local metric-ball geometry at every point, which yields the [completeness of homogeneous Riemannian manifolds](completeness-of-homogeneous-riemannian-manifolds.md). It does not imply two-point homogeneity: that stronger property also controls directions and pairs at equal distance.
