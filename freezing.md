# Freezing

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing)

[Freezing](freezing.md) is a liquid-to-solid [phase transition](phase-transition.md). A moving [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md) releases [latent heat](latent-heat.md); its rate is constrained by heat transport and the [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md). In a solution the [liquidus](liquidus.md) depends on composition, so being below the pure solvent's [melting](melting.md) [temperature](temperature.md) does not imply that the solution must freeze.
