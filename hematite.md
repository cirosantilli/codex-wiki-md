# Hematite

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An iron oxide, $\alpha\text{-}\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$. It typically has weaker induced [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) than [magnetite](magnetite.md) but can preserve stable [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md).
