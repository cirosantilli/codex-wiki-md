# WKB spectrum of a square-root half-line well

↑ **Parent:** [Hard-wall and soft-turning-point WKB quantization](hard-wall-and-soft-turning-point-wkb-quantization.md)

For the [half-line square-root potential well](half-line-square-root-potential-well.md), the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [action integral](action-integral.md) is

$$
\int_0^{(E/a)^2}\frac{\sqrt{2m(E-a\sqrt x)}}{\hbar}\,dx
=\frac{8\sqrt{2m}E^{5/2}}{15\hbar a^2}.
$$

Hence $E_n/E_*=[15\pi(n+3/4)/8]^{2/5}$. The ground-state estimate is $1.811694E_*$. This is a semiclassical estimate, not a variational upper bound.
