# Ising coupling of two qubits

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

An [Ising coupling](ising-coupling-of-two-qubits.md) generates $e^{-i\phi Z\otimes Z}$ with $\phi=\int J(t)dt$ in units $\hbar=1$. It is entangling precisely when $\phi$ is not an integer multiple of $\pi/2$. A $\pi$ rotation on one qubit reverses its sign, permitting an ideal echo to cancel an always-on coupling.
