# Extension of an order automorphism from a dense subset

↑ **Parent:** [Order automorphism](order-automorphism.md)

An [order automorphism](order-automorphism.md) of an [order-dense subset](order-dense-subset.md) $D$ of the real line extends uniquely by $\widetilde\varphi(x)=\sup\{\varphi(d):d\in D,d<x\}$. [Order completeness](order-completeness.md) supplies the extension, and the lower cuts in $D$ give uniqueness.
