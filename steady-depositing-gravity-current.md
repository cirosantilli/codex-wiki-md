# Steady depositing gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

A dilute, vertically mixed [particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md) with no [fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md), bed erosion or carrier-fluid leakage obeys [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) $q=uh=\text{constant}$. With downward [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) magnitude $v_s$, an absorbing bed gives $q\,dg'/dx=-v_sg'$. The [variable-buoyancy shallow water equations](variable-buoyancy-shallow-water-equations.md) conserve the kinematic momentum flux including [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md),

$$
g'(x)=g'_0e^{-v_sx/q},\qquad K=\frac{q^2}{h}+\frac12g'h^2.
$$

These equations describe a steady interior supplied continuously from upstream, not the moving nose of a finite-volume release. The depth follows one of the [depth branches of a steady depositing gravity current](depth-branches-of-a-steady-depositing-gravity-current.md).
