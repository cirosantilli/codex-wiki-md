# Contact inhibition of locomotion

↑ **Parent:** [Collective cell migration](collective-cell-migration.md)

[Contact inhibition of locomotion](contact-inhibition-of-locomotion.md) is a contact-triggered change of protrusions and movement direction. It is distinct from contact inhibition of proliferation. In [neural crest](neural-crest.md) experiments, cell collisions caused protrusion collapse and repolarization; blocking noncanonical [Wnt signalling](wnt-signalling.md) disrupted the response.[Collision and signalling-perturbation experiments](contact-inhibition-of-locomotion-in-vivo-controls-neural-crest-directional-migration-by-carlos-carmona-fontaine-and-others.md)
