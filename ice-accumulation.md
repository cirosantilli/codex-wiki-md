# Ice accumulation

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)

Ice accumulation is the local gain of glacier or ice-sheet material, expressed in thickness per unit time after converting to ice-equivalent volume. The net accumulation rate subtracts [ice ablation](ice-ablation.md).
