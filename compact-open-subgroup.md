# Compact-open subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Locally profinite group](locally-profinite-group.md)

A [compact-open subgroup](compact-open-subgroup.md) is both a [compact subset](compact-space.md) and an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md). Its coset space is discrete, and compact subsets meet only finitely many cosets. Finite intersections of [compact-open subgroups](compact-open-subgroup.md) are again compact-open, making them useful common stabilizers for finitely many vectors.
