# Risk-enriched developmental cohort

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal study](longitudinal-study.md)

A sample selected to include elevated familial or early developmental risk. It can increase information about pathways but its [comorbidity](comorbidity.md) and prevalence estimates require qualification before generalising to the population.
