# Exterior square realization of the orthogonal adjoint representation

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)

For a real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) $V$, the displayed map identifies $\Lambda^2V$ with its [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md). It sends a wedge of distinct orthonormal basis vectors to the corresponding elementary [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md), so it is an isomorphism. For an [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md) $g$, invariance of the [inner product](inner-product.md) gives $\Phi(gu\wedge gv)=g\Phi(u\wedge v)g^{-1}$. Thus the [exterior square](exterior-square.md) of the defining representation is the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of the orthogonal group. Complexification identifies their [weight spaces](weight-space.md), allowing the [root system](root-system.md) to be read from the exterior-square weights and the zero-weight multiplicity.
