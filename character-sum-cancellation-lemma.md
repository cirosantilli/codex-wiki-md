# Character-sum cancellation lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Character group of a finite abelian group](character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md)

For a [linear character](linear-character.md) $\chi$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $K$,

$$
\sum_{k\in K}\chi(k)=\begin{cases}|K|,&\chi=1,\\0,&\chi\ne1.\end{cases}
$$

Indeed, if $\chi(h)\ne1$, the [bijection](bijection.md) $k\mapsto hk$ gives $\sum_k\chi(k)=\chi(h)\sum_k\chi(k)$, forcing the sum to vanish. The trivial character gives $|K|$ directly. The same argument applies to the restriction of a [linear character](linear-character.md) of a larger group to a [subgroup](subgroup.md).
