# Fano scheme of lines

↑ **Parent:** [Fano scheme](fano-scheme.md)

The [Fano scheme](fano-scheme.md) for projective lines. For $X\subseteq\mathbb P^3$, it is a closed subscheme of $\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)$ and hence is projective. Requiring the defining homogeneous polynomials of $X$ to vanish along a line gives its closed defining conditions.
