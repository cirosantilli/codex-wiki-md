# Superlinear functional

↑ **Parent:** [Sublinear function](sublinear-function.md)

In the functional-analytic sense used here, a superlinear functional is real valued, positively homogeneous and superadditive. Equivalently $-q$ is a [sublinear functional](sublinear-function.md). This definition concerns functional inequalities, rather than a comparison of asymptotic growth rates. Such a $q$ is concave, satisfies $q(0)=0$, and can serve as a lower support in the [sandwich form of the Hahn-Banach theorem](sandwich-form-of-the-hahn-banach-theorem.md).
