# Scaled right Maurer-Cartan gauge potential

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md)

Write $B_i=(\partial_i g)g^{-1}$ and use $D=d+A$. The right [Maurer-Cartan equation](maurer-cartan-equation.md) gives $\partial_xB_y-\partial_yB_x=[B_x,B_y]$. Thus $A_i=\alpha B_i$ has the displayed [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md). Both $\alpha=0$ and $\alpha=-1$ are universally flat; the latter is a [pure gauge potential](pure-gauge-potential.md). Commuting $B_x,B_y$ give additional flat cases. For the opposite convention $D=d-A$, the corresponding component formula instead has $\alpha(1-\alpha)$.
