# Two-isogeny index formula over a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

For the [two-isogeny formula](two-isogeny-formula.md) $\phi:E\to E'$ over a [number field](number-field.md), the [two-torsion square-class homomorphisms](two-torsion-square-class-homomorphism.md) have kernels $\widehat\phi E'(K)$ and $\phi E(K)$. Consequently

$$
[E(K):2E(K)]=\frac{\#\alpha(E(K))\#\alpha'(E'(K))}{\delta},\qquad
\delta=[\ker\widehat\phi:\ker\widehat\phi\cap\phi E(K)].
$$

Indeed $2E(K)=\widehat\phi\phi E(K)$, and the surjection from $E'(K)/\phi E(K)$ to $\widehat\phi E'(K)/2E(K)$ has kernel $\ker\widehat\phi/(\ker\widehat\phi\cap\phi E(K))$. Combined with the [Mordell-Weil theorem](mordell-weil-group.md), the index is $2^r\#E(K)[2]$, where $r$ is the [rank of an abelian group](rank-of-an-abelian-group.md). This formula prevents an erroneous extra factor of two in an isogeny descent.
