# Linearized Einstein equations

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

In terms of the [trace-reversed metric perturbation](trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md) and in the [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md), the linearized Einstein equations on Minkowski spacetime are

$$
\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-16\pi T_{\mu\nu}.
$$
