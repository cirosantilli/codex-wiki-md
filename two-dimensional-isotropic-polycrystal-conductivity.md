# Two-dimensional isotropic polycrystal conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Hashin-Shtrikman conductivity variational principle](hashin-shtrikman-conductivity-variational-principle.md)

For uniformly distributed rotations of a two-dimensional conductivity [tensor](tensor.md) with positive principal values $a\le b$, the [Hashin-Shtrikman conductivity variational principle](hashin-shtrikman-conductivity-variational-principle.md) with isotropic comparison $cI$ yields $H(c)I$. Averaging a rotated diagonal [tensor](tensor.md) replaces it by half its trace times the identity. Since $H'(c)=(a-b)^2/(a+b+2c)^2$, the admissible comparison endpoints give $a(a+3b)/(3a+b)\le C_{\mathrm{eff}}\le b(3a+b)/(a+3b)$ within the statistically isotropic polycrystal model.
