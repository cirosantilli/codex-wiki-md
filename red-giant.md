# Red giant

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_giant)

A red giant is an evolved star with a large radius and low [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md). On the first giant branch, a low- or intermediate-mass star is powered primarily by shell [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md) around a helium-rich core.
