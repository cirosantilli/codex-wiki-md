# Fermi-Dirac distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md)

The [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) follows from [Fermi-Dirac statistics](fermi-dirac-statistics.md) and is $n(E)=[e^{\beta(E-\mu)}+1]^{-1}$.

The mean occupation of a fermionic one-particle state of energy $E$ is

$$
f(E)=\frac{1}{e^{\beta(E-\mu)}+1},
\qquad \beta=(k_BT)^{-1}.
$$

At zero temperature this becomes the step function $\mathbf 1_{E<E_F}$.
