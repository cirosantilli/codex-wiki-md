# Siegel zero

↑ **Parent:** [Classical zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions](classical-zero-free-region-for-dirichlet-l-functions.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_zero)

An exceptional zero is the possible real simple zero $\beta$ omitted from the otherwise uniform [classical zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions](classical-zero-free-region-for-dirichlet-l-functions.md). It belongs to a real nonprincipal primitive [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) and satisfies $\beta<1$.
