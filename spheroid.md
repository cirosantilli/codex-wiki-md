# Spheroid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spheroid)

A [spheroid](spheroid.md) is an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) of revolution obtained by rotating an [ellipse](ellipse.md) about a principal axis. Rotation about the shorter axis gives an [oblate spheroid](oblate-spheroid.md); rotation about the longer axis gives a [prolate spheroid](prolate-spheroid.md). Thus the oblate form is a member of the general family.
