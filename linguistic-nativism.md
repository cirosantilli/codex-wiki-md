# Linguistic nativism

↑ **Parent:** [Language acquisition](language-acquisition.md)

The position that some language-relevant structure or [learning](learning.md) constraints are present prior to the relevant linguistic experience. It is not equivalent to denying all [learning](learning.md); the specificity and content of the proposed initial endowment require evidence.
