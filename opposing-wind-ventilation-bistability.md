# Opposing-wind ventilation bistability

↑ **Parent:** [Well-mixed ventilation temperature balance](well-mixed-ventilation-temperature-balance.md)

For $0<\varepsilon<2/(3\sqrt3)$, the [well-mixed ventilation temperature balance](well-mixed-ventilation-temperature-balance.md) has one stable upward-flow [temperature](temperature.md) and two downward-flow temperatures. The heat-removal curve on the latter interval is $\sqrt{K_A}\theta\sqrt{W-G_T\theta}$, maximal at $2W/(3G_T)$. Its lower-temperature root is stable and its higher-temperature root unstable. The two meet at a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md). The unstable root separates the basins of the stable wind-dominated and buoyancy-dominated states.
