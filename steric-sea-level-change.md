# Steric sea level change

↑ **Parent:** [Sea level rise](sea-level-rise.md)

Steric sea level change results from changes of seawater [mass density](density.md) at fixed ocean mass. To first order, [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) gives $\Delta\eta_{\rm thermal}=\int\alpha\Delta T\,dz$; changes in [salinity](salinity.md) give a halosteric contribution. The reference column, pressure and equation of state must be specified.
