# Supermembranes

↑ **Parent:** [M2-brane](m2-brane.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermembranes)

The supersymmetric [M2-brane](m2-brane.md) worldvolume theory includes embedding bosons and fermions and has [kappa symmetry](kappa-symmetry.md). In eleven dimensions its fermionic embedding coordinate is a thirty-two-component [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md).
