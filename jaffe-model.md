# Jaffe model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A spherical stellar model with total [mass](mass.md) $M$, scale radius $a$, inner density proportional to $r^{-2}$ and outer density proportional to $r^{-4}$. Integrating its [mass density](density.md) gives $M(r)=Mr/(r+a)$, hence [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) $\Phi=(GM/a)\log[r/(r+a)]$, normalized to zero at infinity. Its [circular speed](circular-speed.md) satisfies $v_c^2=GM/(r+a)$: it tends to a constant inside the scale radius and becomes Keplerian outside. The central density cusp has vanishing enclosed [mass](mass.md) as $r\to0$ and does not imply a central point mass.
