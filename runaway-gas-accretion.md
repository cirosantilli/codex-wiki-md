# Runaway gas accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Core accretion](core-accretion.md)

Rapid envelope growth after cooling and envelope self-gravity allow gas capture to accelerate. It requires an available disk gas supply; eventually disk transport, gap formation or [protoplanetary disk dispersal](protoplanetary-disk-dispersal.md) can limit growth. It differs from solid-body [runaway planetesimal growth](runaway-planetesimal-growth.md).
