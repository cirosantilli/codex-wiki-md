# Pressure lobes in a symmetric translating lubrication gap

↑ **Parent:** [Flux average for a translating lubrication gap](flux-average-for-a-translating-lubrication-gap.md)

For a symmetric gap $h(x)=\sqrt{1+x^2}$ on $[-1,1]$, unit lower-wall speed and equal zero gauge pressures at the ends, the [flux average for a translating lubrication gap](flux-average-for-a-translating-lubrication-gap.md) gives $Q=\pi/(4\sqrt2)$. Integrating $p_x=6(h-2Q)/h^3$ gives the displayed odd [pressure](pressure-split.md). It has a positive maximum at $x=-\sqrt{\pi^2/8-1}$ and a negative minimum at the opposite point. The converging and diverging portions of a symmetric gap therefore have opposite [pressure](pressure-split.md) lobes even though the geometry itself is even: the directed wall motion breaks reflection symmetry of the flow.
