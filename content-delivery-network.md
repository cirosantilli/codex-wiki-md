# Content delivery network

↑ **Parent:** [Internet](internet.md)

A [content delivery network](content-delivery-network.md) uses delivery infrastructure, often at multiple locations, to supply content and direct requests to suitable delivery [endpoints](communication-endpoint.md). It can retain objects through [caching](caching.md) or retrieve them from an origin. Distribution across sites does not imply that those sites have independent administrative control.[§1.2 and §2.1: reference model and request redirection](framework-for-content-distribution-network-interconnection-rfc-7336.md)
