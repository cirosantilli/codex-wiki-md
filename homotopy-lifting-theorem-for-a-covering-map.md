# Homotopy lifting theorem for a covering map

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

A homotopy into the base of a [covering space](covering-space.md) has a unique continuous lift once its initial map is continuously lifted. Local inverse sheets construct the lift along each time interval, and compactness of that interval permits a finite subdivision locally around each point of the homotopy's parameter space. Uniqueness on overlapping intervals follows from [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md) uniqueness. In a homotopy of paths with endpoints fixed, a chosen lifted endpoint remains fixed because it varies continuously in a discrete fiber.
