# Dehn surgery

↑ **Parent:** [Knot theory](knot-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehn_surgery)

[Dehn surgery](dehn-surgery.md) removes tubular neighborhoods of a [link](link.md) in a three-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md) and performs [Dehn filling](dehn-filling.md) on their boundary tori with specified primitive meridian slopes. [Dehn surgery on a framed link](dehn-surgery-on-a-framed-link.md) is the integral-slope special case.
