# Resolution principle for sheaf cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

If $0\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal A^0\to\mathcal A^1\to\cdots$ is an [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) of [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) on $X$ and $H^q(X,\mathcal A^j)=0$ for every $q>0$ and $j\geq0$, then [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) is computed by the [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) of global sections:

$$
H^i(X,\mathcal F)\cong H^i\bigl(\Gamma(X,\mathcal A^\bullet)\bigr).
$$

In particular a [flasque resolution](flasque-resolution.md) computes sheaf cohomology. Acyclicity is required on the space on which cohomology is being computed.
