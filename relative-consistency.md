# Relative consistency

↑ **Parent:** [Consistency-strength preorder](consistency-strength-preorder.md)

A relative-consistency result constructs a [model of a first-order theory](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) $S$, or interprets its proofs, from the assumed consistency of a background theory $T$. It establishes $\operatorname{Con}(T)\Rightarrow\operatorname{Con}(S)$ in the stated metatheory, not an unconditional proof of consistency. For independence of an axiom $\varphi$ from $S$, obtain consistent models of both $S+\varphi$ and $S+\neg\varphi$, relative to the background assumption.
