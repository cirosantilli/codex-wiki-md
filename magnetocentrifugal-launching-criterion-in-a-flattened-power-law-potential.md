# Magnetocentrifugal launching criterion in a flattened power-law potential

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetocentrifugal acceleration](magnetocentrifugal-acceleration.md)

In a [flattened power-law gravitational potential](flattened-power-law-gravitational-potential.md) $\Phi=\Phi_0R_0^\beta(R^2+\lambda^2z^2)^{-\beta/2}$, a magnetic surface corotating with its disk footpoint has an [effective potential](effective-potential.md) with meridional [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) $\Omega_f^2\operatorname{diag}[-(\beta+2),\lambda^2]$ there. A cold outward displacement along a field inclined by $\alpha$ to the vertical is downhill when $\tan^2\alpha>\lambda^2/(\beta+2)$. Equality is marginal and can depend on higher derivatives and field-line curvature; this is a local launching criterion, not a guarantee of a global escaping wind.
