# Octahedral bis-chelate isomerism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical isomerism](geometrical-isomerism.md)

For ideal $\mathrm{M(AA)_2a_2}$ with equivalent ends of each [chelate](chelate.md), the monodentate donors can be trans or cis. The cis form has two mirror-related configurations; the trans form is achiral when the [chelate](chelate.md) backbones introduce no additional [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md).
