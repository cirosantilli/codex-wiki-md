# f-morphism of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md)

For a continuous map $f:X\to Y$, an f-morphism from a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) on $Y$ to a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) on $X$ is a family of the displayed maps commuting with restrictions. It is equivalently a [morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md) $\mathcal G\to f_*\mathcal F$. For each $x\in X$ it induces a map $\mathcal G_{f(x)}\to\mathcal F_x$ on [stalks](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md), by pulling back the neighbourhood representing a [germ](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md).
