# Surface-gravity-wave energy

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

A linear [deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md) of surface-displacement amplitude $a$ has equal mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md), and total mean [energy](energy.md) per horizontal area $\mathcal E=\rho_wga^2/2$. Its energy transport speed is the [group velocity](group-velocity.md). This amplitude is half the crest-to-trough height.
