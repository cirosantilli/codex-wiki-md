# Characteristic representation of a measure-valued continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md)

Let $b(t,x)$ be continuous in time and continuously differentiable in space, with a uniform spatial [Lipschitz constant](lipschitz-constant.md) and at most linear growth on each compact time interval. Its [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) exists forward and backward over every bounded time interval. For each initial [probability measure](probability-measure.md) with finite first [absolute moment](absolute-moment.md), the displayed [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) is the unique [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) $\partial_t\rho+\operatorname{div}(b\rho)=0$ continuous in the first [Wasserstein distance](wasserstein-distance.md).

The [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) bounds the flow by $C_T(1+|x|)$ and therefore preserves finite first [absolute moments](absolute-moment.md). The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) establishes the weak equation. For uniqueness, transport a compactly supported terminal [test function](test-function.md) backward along the flow; it solves $\partial_t\varphi+b\cdot\nabla\varphi=0$ and remains compactly supported on a bounded time interval. Inserting it into the weak identity identifies every terminal integral with that of the claimed [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md). A zero [divergence](divergence.md) makes the corresponding scalar [transport equation](transport-equation-split.md) and the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) coincide.
