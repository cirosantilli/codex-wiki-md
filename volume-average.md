# Volume average

↑ **Parent:** [Integral average](integral-average.md)

A [volume average](volume-average.md) is the normalized [integral average](integral-average.md) of a field over a three-dimensional region of finite positive [volume](volume.md). For a fixed region, differentiating in time commutes with this average under the usual regularity conditions. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) converts the average of a divergence into its net boundary flux divided by volume.
