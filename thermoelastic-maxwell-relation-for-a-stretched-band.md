# Thermoelastic Maxwell relation for a stretched band

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelasticity](thermoelasticity.md)

Let $f\,dx$ be reversible work supplied to stretch a band. Its [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) obeys $dF=-S\,dT+f\,dx$. Equality of mixed [derivatives](derivative.md) gives the displayed relation. An [entropy](entropy-split.md) decrease on stretching therefore corresponds to increasing tensile [force](force-split.md) on heating at fixed extension. The sign follows the [force](force-split.md)/work convention; a restoring [force](force-split.md) exerted by the band has the opposite sign.
