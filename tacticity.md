# Tacticity

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

[Tacticity](tacticity.md) describes the stereochemical ordering of configurational units along a [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain. It is distinct from the head-to-tail or head-to-head connectivity described by [regioselectivity](regioselectivity.md).
