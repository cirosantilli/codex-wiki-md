# Activity-dependent ocular dominance segregation

↑ **Parent:** [Ocular dominance column](ocular-dominance-column.md)

Two eye-specific afferent populations compete under [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md) and [synaptic normalization](synaptic-normalization.md). In a simplified symmetric two-eye model, the eye-difference mode $d_a=w_{aL}-w_{aR}$ evolves with a linear operator containing $K_{ab}(C_{\rm same}-C_{\rm cross})_{ab}$. Dominant spatial modes can produce alternating eye preference, while saturation sets finite contrast. The interaction width, arbor constraints and correlation structure influence the column scale. This is a mechanistic hypothesis whose predictions can be tested, not a unique inference from the existence of columns.
