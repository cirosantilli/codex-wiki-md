# Digital elevation model

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

A numerical representation of surface elevation sampled over a horizontal grid or another spatial mesh. A hillshaded rendering highlights slope geometry but depends on artificial illumination direction. A [digital elevation model](digital-elevation-model.md) also supplies the topographic phase needed in [interferometric synthetic-aperture radar](interferometric-synthetic-aperture-radar.md) processing.
