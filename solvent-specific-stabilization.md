# Solvent-specific stabilization

↑ **Parent:** [Solvation free energy](solvation-free-energy.md)

[Solvent-specific stabilization](solvent-specific-stabilization.md) means that the same species need not have the same [solvation free energy](solvation-free-energy.md) in different [solvents](solvent.md). [Chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md), [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md), and the cost of reorganizing each [solvent](solvent.md) all matter. [Water](water.md) can stabilize [ions](ion.md) by [ion-dipole attractions](ion-dipole-attraction.md); a favorable isolated attraction alone still cannot decide the total [dissolution](dissolution.md) balance.[solvation energy](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/solvation-energy.md)

With a common gaseous reference and compatible dissolved [standard states](standard-state.md), subtraction removes that reference:

$$
\Delta G_{\mathrm{transfer}}^{\circ}(A\to B)
=\mu_X^{\circ}(B)-\mu_X^{\circ}(A)
=\Delta G_{\mathrm{solv}}^{\circ}(B)-\Delta G_{\mathrm{solv}}^{\circ}(A).
$$

A negative result favors [solvent](solvent.md) B under those reference conventions. This comparison assumes the same species $X$: if [protonation](protonation.md), [ionization](ionization.md), or formation of [ion pairs](ion-pair.md) changes its identity, include a [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) for each corresponding [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md).
