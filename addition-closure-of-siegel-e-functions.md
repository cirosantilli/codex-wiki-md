# Addition closure of Siegel E-functions

↑ **Parent:** [E-function](e-function.md)

Work in the [compositum](field-compositum.md) of the two coefficient fields. Conjugate coefficient bounds add, while multiplying the common denominators clears both coefficient lists. For Siegel's original bounds $O_\epsilon(n^{\epsilon n})$, use $\epsilon/2$ in each denominator estimate. A nonzero [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) for the sum follows because its derivatives lie in the sum of the two finite-dimensional derivative spaces over the [rational function](rational-function.md) [field](field.md). The stricter exponential arithmetic bounds are preserved as well.
