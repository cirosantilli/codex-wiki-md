# Weak acid dissociation measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid](weak-acid.md)

A [pH](ph.md) measurement becomes evidence for a [weak acid](weak-acid.md) [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) when paired with a known [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md) and a calibrated [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md). For the pure-[weak acid](weak-acid.md) model, measured [pH](ph.md) estimates $h=10^{-\mathrm{pH}}$ only in the ideal [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) approximation. Then $o=K_w/h$, $x=h-o$, $y=c-x$, and $K_a=hx/y$. Require $0<x<c$; values outside that range signal inconsistent measurements or an inadequate model. Repeating this reconstruction across a [dilution](dilution.md) series tests whether one [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) describes the data, rather than merely fitting one point.

The Analytical Sciences Digital Library's [Potentiometric Titration of an Unknown Weak Monoprotic Acid](analytical-electrochemistry-potentiometry/potentiometric-titration-of-an-unknown-weak-monoprotic-acid.md) provides a complementary [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md) experiment. Its apparatus includes a [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) and meter, a stirring magnet, a [volumetric flask](volumetric-flask.md), [pipettes](pipette.md), and a [burette](burette.md) containing standardized [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md). Prepare an aqueous acid stock of known [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md), transfer a measured portion, and stir gently with the [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) clear of the magnet. Record added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and stable [pH](ph.md) after each addition; use a preliminary run to locate the steep region and smaller additions there on a fresh portion. The readout is the [pH](ph.md) versus added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) curve, not a visual count of dissociated [molecules](molecule-split.md).

Calibrate with fresh standard [buffer solutions](buffer-solution.md) bracketing the [pH](ph.md) range being interpreted. Use their assigned values at the actual [temperature](temperature-split.md); a pair near [pH](ph.md) $4$ and $7$ can cover the midpoint of many [weak acid](weak-acid.md) examples, but does not cover an entire alkaline endpoint region. Rinse between standards and samples to limit carryover, keep the sensing bulb and [reference electrode](reference-electrode.md) junction immersed, and wait for a stable reading. These instrument practices are documented in [HI5221 & HI5222 Instruction Manual: pH and mV and ISE and Temperature Bench Meters by Hanna Instruments](hi5221-and-hi5222-instruction-manual-ph-and-mv-and-ise-and-temperature-bench-meters-by-hanna-instruments.md) (pp. 45–47, 49–50 and 73–74: calibration, measurement, rinsing and electrode conditioning).

Locate the [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md) equivalence region from the steepest change of [pH](ph.md) with added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). For one-to-one [neutralization](neutralization.md), standardized [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) [concentration](concentration.md) times equivalence [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) estimates the initial [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of acid. Half that [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) gives approximately equal $[\mathrm{HA}]$ and $[\mathrm A^-]$ only when subsequent [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) redistribution is small. The [Henderson-Hasselbalch equation](henderson-hasselbalch-equation.md) then gives $\mathrm{pH}\approx\mathrm pK_a$. Unequal [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md) or appreciable [hydronium](hydronium.md) or [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) inventories can shift this estimate. The full [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) curve supplies a stronger consistency check than its midpoint alone.

Useful controls for this model test are a fresh independently prepared acid portion, a [water](water.md) blank, a check standard after measurement to detect instrument drift, and fixed recorded [temperature](temperature-split.md). A blank diagnoses contamination but should not be subtracted directly from [pH](ph.md), which is a [logarithm](logarithm.md). Keep exposure to atmospheric [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) comparable, especially at high [dilution](dilution.md), and record any background salt that changes [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md). These are experimental-design controls suggested by the model's assumptions. The observed curve and its uncertainty determine whether the model is adequate; the diagrams here contain no experimental readouts. An aqueous [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) cannot be inferred unchanged from a sample requiring a different [solvent](solvent.md) mixture, and a nearly neutral dilute sample makes the difference $h-K_w/h$ particularly sensitive to [pH](ph.md) error.
