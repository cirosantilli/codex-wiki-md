# Arctic methane feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)

Warming can mobilize frozen organic material or change sediment conditions, increasing [methane](methane.md) emissions and strengthening the [greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md). Production, transport and atmospheric release must be distinguished: methane can dissolve or be oxidized before reaching the atmosphere. Observed seabed bubbles alone do not establish the magnitude or timing of a global atmospheric feedback.
