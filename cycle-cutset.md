# Cycle cutset

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint graph](constraint-graph.md)

A [set](set-split.md) of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) whose deletion leaves a [forest](forest.md). For a binary [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md), fixing these [variables](variable-mathematics.md) and filtering the remaining [constraint domains](constraint-domain.md) leaves independent instances of [tree-structured constraint satisfaction](tree-structured-constraint-satisfaction.md). A valid cutset need not be minimum; smaller cutsets reduce the exponential enumeration in [cutset conditioning](cutset-conditioning.md).
