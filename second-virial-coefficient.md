# Second virial coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Virial expansion](virial-expansion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_virial_coefficient)

For particles with pair potential $U$, the second virial coefficient is

$$
B_2=\frac12\int \left[1-e^{-U(\mathbf r)/(k_BT)}\right]d\mathbf r,
$$

with the integration measure adapted to the available relative configurations. Repulsion contributes positively and attraction negatively.
