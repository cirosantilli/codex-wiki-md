# Linearized free-surface boundary conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

For a small-amplitude [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) described by [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) $\phi$ and [surface elevation](surface-elevation.md) $\eta$, linearizing the [kinematic boundary condition for a free-surface graph](kinematic-boundary-condition-for-a-free-surface-graph.md) and the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) at the mean surface $z=0$ gives

$$
\eta_t=\phi_z,
\qquad
\phi_t+g\eta=-\frac{p}{\rho}
\qquad(z=0).
$$

Here $p$ is the applied excess [pressure](pressure-split.md) above constant atmospheric pressure. With $p=0$, differentiate the second equation in time and use the first to obtain $\phi_{tt}+g\phi_z=0$. These surface conditions apply at finite depth as well as in the [deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md) limit; depth enters through the bottom condition. The quadratic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) term is omitted at this order, and [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) is neglected.
