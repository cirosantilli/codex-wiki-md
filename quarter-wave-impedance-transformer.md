# Quarter-wave impedance transformer

↑ **Parent:** [Impedance matching](impedance-matching.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarter-wave_impedance_transformer)

A lossless intermediate section of positive real [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) $Z_M$ matches unequal real exterior [characteristic impedances](characteristic-impedance.md) $Z_L,Z_R$ when $Z_M^2=Z_LZ_R$ and its propagation [wave phase](phase-waves.md) is an odd multiple of $\pi/2$. The length is an odd quarter of the internal [wavelength](wavelength.md). This principle also applies to the [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md).

For a uniform intermediate string of length $l$, put $\delta=k_Ml$. Continuity of displacement and transverse [force](force-split.md) gives the displacement [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md)

$$
r=\frac{(Z_L-Z_R)\cos\delta+i(Z_M-Z_LZ_R/Z_M)\sin\delta}{(Z_L+Z_R)\cos\delta-i(Z_M+Z_LZ_R/Z_M)\sin\delta}.
$$

At real positive [mechanical impedances](mechanical-impedance.md), the denominator never vanishes. Its numerator vanishes only if $(Z_L-Z_R)\cos\delta=0$ and $(Z_M^2-Z_LZ_R)\sin\delta=0$. Thus unequal exterior strings require the quarter-wave condition. Equal exterior strings with a different middle string instead transmit perfectly at integer half-[wavelengths](wavelength.md); if all three [mechanical impedances](mechanical-impedance.md) agree, every [frequency](frequency.md) is matched.
