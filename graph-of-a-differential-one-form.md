# Graph of a differential one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

A [differential one-form](one-form.md) $\theta$ is a smooth [section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) $T^*X\to X$. Its graph is its image as a section, and the projection restricts to a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) onto $X$. Pullback of the [Liouville one-form](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) by that section equals $\theta$, making the [twisted Lagrangian graph criterion](twisted-lagrangian-graph-criterion.md) immediate.
