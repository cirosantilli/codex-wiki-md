# Commutation of iterated categorical limits

↑ **Parent:** [Commutation of limits and colimits](commutation-of-limits-and-colimits.md)

For small $J,K$ and a [functor](functor.md) $D:J\times K\to\mathcal C$ into a [complete category](complete-category.md), the two iterated [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) are canonically isomorphic. They have the same projections to every $D(j,k)$ and the same [universal property](universal-property.md). The result follows also from preservation of [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) by the [limit functor](limit-functor.md).
