# Strong cosmic censorship conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md)

The [strong cosmic censorship conjecture](strong-cosmic-censorship-conjecture.md) asserts that the [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md) of generic admissible [initial data](initial-data-in-general-relativity.md) is inextendible in a specified regularity class. The matter model, topology on initial data, and extension regularity are part of the assertion. An exceptional exact solution with a smooth [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md) need not violate this generic claim.
