# Particle on a uniformly rotating inclined plane

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

For a smooth plane at inclination $\theta$ rotating about a vertical axis at constant [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$, choose rotating coordinates $\xi$ horizontally and $\eta$ uphill, with their cross product the upward [normal vector](normal-vector.md). A sliding particle under gravity satisfies

$$
\ddot\xi=\omega^2\xi+2\omega\dot\eta\cos\theta,\qquad\ddot\eta=\omega^2\eta\cos^2\theta-2\omega\dot\xi\cos\theta-g\sin\theta.
$$

The [normal reaction](normal-force.md) is $N=m(g\cos\theta-2\omega\dot\xi\sin\theta+\omega^2\eta\sin\theta\cos\theta)$ while contact is maintained. The [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) does no work in the rotating frame, giving the conserved specific [energy](energy.md)

$$
\frac12(\dot\xi^2+\dot\eta^2)+g\eta\sin\theta-\frac{\omega^2}{2}(\xi^2+\eta^2\cos^2\theta).
$$

The last term is the [centrifugal potential](centrifugal-potential.md); this quantity need not equal the inertial [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md).
