# Ploidy

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ploidy)

[Ploidy](ploidy.md) counts complete sets of [chromosomes](chromosome.md) in a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). Write $n$ for the number of [chromosomes](chromosome.md) in one set: [haploid](haploid.md) means one set, [diploid](diploid.md) two. [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) produces [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) without changing [ploidy](ploidy.md).[Biology 2e, §10.1, Genomic DNA; Eukaryotic Chromosomal Structure and Compaction, final paragraph](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/cell-division.md)

Consider an illustrative [species](species.md) with $2n=6$, hence $n=3$. One set contains three distinguishable [chromosome](chromosome.md) types, $A,B,D$; the [diploid](diploid.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) contains two [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md) of each type. Labels describe [chromosome](chromosome.md) identity, not particular [alleles](allele.md). Let $C$ be the [Nuclear DNA content](nuclear-dna-content.md) of one unreplicated set. All entries below refer to one completed product of division, or the starting [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), rather than the sum over products. Here “[chromatid](chromatid.md) count” includes the single [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) copy in each unreplicated [chromosome](chromosome.md).

| State | [Ploidy](ploidy.md) | [Chromosomes](chromosome.md) | [Chromatid](chromatid.md) copies | [Nuclear DNA content](nuclear-dna-content.md) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [G1 phase](g1-phase.md), before [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) | [Diploid](diploid.md), $2n$ | 6 | 6 | $2C$ |
| After [S phase](s-phase.md), in [G2 phase](g2-phase.md) | [Diploid](diploid.md), $2n$ | 6 | 12 | $4C$ |
| Each product of [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) | [Haploid](haploid.md), $n$ | 3 | 6 | $2C$ |
| Each product of [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md) | [Haploid](haploid.md), $n$ | 3 | 3 | $1C$ |

**[Figure 1](#image-illustrative-chromosome-and-chromatid-bookkeeping-for-a-stipulated-2n-6-species). Illustrative chromosome and chromatid bookkeeping for a stipulated $2n=6$ species**. Original counting model, not a microscopy image or measured dataset. Colors distinguish parental [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md); lengths distinguish three [chromosome](chromosome.md) types. Paired dots on [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) represent their two copied [centromere](centromere.md) regions, counted together as one replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) until separation. One representative product is shown after each division; the model omits [chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md).

The arithmetic follows the allocation rules. [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) replaces each of six single copies with two associated [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md): $6\times2=12$ copies still grouped as six [chromosomes](chromosome.md). [Meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) allocates one [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md) of every type to each product: three replicated [chromosomes](chromosome.md), with $3\times2=6$ copies. [Meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md) allocates one copy from each pair of [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) to each final product: three single-copy [chromosomes](chromosome.md). Compare [meiotic DNA content](meiotic-dna-content.md) for the general symbolic bookkeeping. [Chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md) can change the [alleles](allele.md) on a [chromatid](chromatid.md) without changing these counts when exchanges preserve material.

Keeping [ploidy](ploidy.md), [chromosome](chromosome.md) count and [Nuclear DNA content](nuclear-dna-content.md) separate makes the beauty of [meiosis](meiosis.md) explicit: copying supplies material for two divisions, while sorting determines which complete sets each product inherits. It also prevents interpreting a doubled [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) signal as evidence of an extra [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md) set.
