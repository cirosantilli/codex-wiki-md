# Exterior derivative of a one-form evaluated on vector fields

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md)

For a smooth one-form $\alpha$ and smooth [vector fields](vector-field.md) $X,Y$,

$$
d\alpha(X,Y)=X(\alpha(Y))-Y(\alpha(X))-\alpha([X,Y]).
$$

In coordinates the derivatives of the components of $X,Y$ cancel against the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md), leaving $(\partial_i\alpha_j-\partial_j\alpha_i)X^iY^j$. This formula extends the coordinate definition of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) to arbitrary frames.
