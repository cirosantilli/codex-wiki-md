# Quasi-likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-likelihood)

[Quasi-likelihood](quasi-likelihood.md) specifies a mean and a [variance function](variance-function.md) without necessarily specifying a full response distribution. For independent responses, the estimating equation is $\sum_i (\partial\mu_i/\partial\beta)(y_i-\mu_i)/(\phi V(\mu_i))=0$. A common scalar [dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md) rescales coefficient [covariance](covariance.md) while leaving the roots of the [quasi-score equation](quasi-score-equation.md) unchanged.
