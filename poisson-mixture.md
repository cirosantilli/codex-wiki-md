# Poisson mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

A model with $Y\mid\Lambda\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda)$ for a nonnegative random intensity. [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) and total [variance](variance-split.md) give $\mathbb E Y=\mathbb E\Lambda$ and $\operatorname{Var}Y=\mathbb E\Lambda+\operatorname{Var}\Lambda$, explaining [overdispersion](overdispersion.md). A [Poisson-gamma mixture](poisson-gamma-mixture.md) gives the [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md).
