# Degree of an isogeny

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

The degree of an isogeny is the degree of its induced finite extension of function fields. On $\operatorname{End}(E)$ it is a quadratic form: $\deg(n\phi)=n^2\deg\phi$, and

$$
\deg(\phi+\psi)+\deg(\phi-\psi)=2\deg\phi+2\deg\psi.
$$
