# Euclidean embedding by finite cell attachment

↑ **Parent:** [Finite CW complex](finite-cw-complex.md)

Suppose a [compact](compact-space.md) skeleton $Y$ embeds as $e(Y)\subset\mathbb R^m$. Attach a disk by $a:S^{k-1}\to Y$ and extend its boundary coordinate map radially by $G(ru)=r e(a(u))$. Map the disk to $(G(x),(1-|x|)x,1-|x|)$ and $Y$ to $(e(y),0,0)$. The boundary identifications agree; a positive last coordinate recovers each interior point. This defines a continuous injection of the [compact](compact-space.md) quotient into [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), hence an [embedding](embedding.md). Repeating proves Hausdorffness and Euclidean embeddability for every finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md), without assuming triangulability of arbitrary attaching maps.
