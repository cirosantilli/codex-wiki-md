# Kastler-Brossel optical pumping experiments

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pumping](optical-pumping.md)

[Alfred Kastler](alfred-kastler.md)'s 1966 lecture recalls his 1950 proposal and distinguishes the early $^{23}\mathrm{Na}$ [atomic beam](atomic-beam.md) work from later sealed-vapour experiments. Its Fig. 5 shows subsequent [sodium](sodium.md) [radio wave](radio-wave.md) resonances, including multiple-[photon](photon.md) transitions.[1966 lecture, pp. 188–189, 193–194, Fig. 5; inspected authored transcription](optical-methods-for-studying-hertzian-resonances-by-alfred-kastler.md)

The 1952 demonstration by [Jean Brossel](jean-brossel.md), [Alfred Kastler](alfred-kastler.md) and [Jacques Winter](jacques-winter.md) used a [sodium](sodium.md) [atomic beam](atomic-beam.md), lamp [light](light.md) with [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) and [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) readout. [Jean Brossel](jean-brossel.md) was a central experimental contributor; [Alfred Kastler](alfred-kastler.md) received the 1966 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) alone.[Direction du laboratoire, 1952 sodium-beam demonstration and Brossel's experimental role](jean-brossel-by-laboratoire-kastler-brossel.md)[Alfred Kastler, prize share 1/1](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1966-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

The lecture also describes a $^{199}\mathrm{Hg}$ experiment: a pumping beam with [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) prepares the vapour, while a perpendicular probe converts transverse [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md) into modulated absorption at a [photodetector](photodetector.md). Interrupting pump and RF together exposes decaying free [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md). Its envelope measures transverse relaxation under that model; it is distinct from the [sodium](sodium.md) [atomic beam](atomic-beam.md) [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) signal.[pp. 196–199, Figs. 9–12: crossed-beam detection and interruption experiment](optical-methods-for-studying-hertzian-resonances-by-alfred-kastler.md)

MIT's later [rubidium](rubidium.md) experiment pumps natural $^{85}\mathrm{Rb}$/$^{87}\mathrm{Rb}$ with a discharge lamp filtered to D1 ($5S_{1/2}\to5P_{1/2}$, near $795\ \mathrm{nm}$). A [polarizer](polarizer.md) and [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) precede a heated [rubidium](rubidium.md)/[neon](neon.md) cell. [Helmholtz coils](helmholtz-coil.md) set a weak static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Separate transverse RF mixing increases absorption, measured by [photodiode](photodiode.md) transmission.[2011 guide, abstract, §V, Fig. 4, §VI.1](optical-pumping-by-mit-department-of-physics.md)

Compare resonant and detuned RF with the optical beam fixed, then change the calibrated static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md): a true [magnetic sublevel](magnetic-sublevel.md) resonance should move with its [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md). Lamp drift, stray [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md), imperfect [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md), optical attenuation and RF power broadening can change the observed signal. Consequently, a transmission dip alone does not determine every sublevel population. These are measurement-design considerations, not additional reported historical observations.
