# Closed combinatorial surface

↑ **Parent:** [Topological surface](topological-surface-split.md)

A closed combinatorial surface is a finite [connected](connected-space.md) [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) in which every [simplex](simplex.md) is a face of a triangle and the [link of a simplex](link-of-a-simplex.md) at every vertex is a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md). Every edge then lies in exactly two triangles, and the triangles at a vertex form a single cyclic fan. The edge-incidence condition alone is insufficient: gluing two triangulated [spheres](sphere.md) only at one vertex gives two disjoint circles in that vertex's link.

Its [geometric realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) is a [compact](compact-space.md) [surface](topological-surface-split.md) without boundary. Triangle interiors have disc [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md), edge interiors have such [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) from the two incident triangles, and the cyclic fan gives one at each vertex. A finite realization is a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) with a countable [basis of a topology](basis-of-a-topology.md), being a finite union of geometric [simplexes](simplex.md) in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). If [connectedness](connected-space.md) is omitted by convention, require these properties separately on each [connected component](connected-component.md).
