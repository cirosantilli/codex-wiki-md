# Morse index theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Morse theory](morse-theory.md)

The [Morse index](morse-index.md) of a fixed-endpoint [geodesic](geodesic.md) equals the number of [conjugate points](conjugate-points.md) strictly between its endpoints, counted with multiplicity. Its nullity equals the multiplicity of the terminal conjugate point. A conjugate multiplicity is the dimension of the space of [Jacobi fields](jacobi-field.md) vanishing at the initial point and at the point in question.
