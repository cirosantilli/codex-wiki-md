# Oriented surface element of an ellipsoid of revolution

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

Parametrize the [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) by $\mathbf x=(b\sin\theta\cos\phi,b\sin\theta\sin\phi,a\cos\theta)$, with positive semiaxes $a,b$, $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$ and $0\leq\phi<2\pi$. The [cross product](cross-product.md) $\mathbf x_\theta\times\mathbf x_\phi$ gives the displayed outward vector [area element](area-element-of-a-surface.md). For $0<\theta<\pi$, it is the positive multiple $ab^2\sin\theta$ of the outward normal direction $(x/b^2,y/b^2,z/a^2)$, fixing its orientation. A [flux](flux.md) integral can therefore be evaluated directly as $\int\int\mathbf F(\mathbf x)\cdot(\mathbf x_\theta\times\mathbf x_\phi)d\theta d\phi$, without computing the length of the normal separately. The scalar [surface area](surface-area.md) element is the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) of this vector element.
