# Hermitian parts of a unitary matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md)

Every [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) has a unique decomposition $U=A+iB$ into [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md). The formulas follow by adding and subtracting $U$ and its [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md). Expanding $U^\dagger U=UU^\dagger=I$ gives $A^2+B^2+i(AB-BA)=I$ and $A^2+B^2-i(AB-BA)=I$. Therefore the parts commute and $A^2+B^2=I$.
