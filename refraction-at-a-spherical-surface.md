# Refraction at a spherical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Refraction](refraction.md)

With incident [light](light.md) travelling to the right, use positive object distance $u$ to the left, image distance $d$ to the right, and curvature radius $R$ toward a right-hand centre. For a ray hitting at small height $Y$, its incoming inclination, outgoing downward inclination and inward-normal downward inclination are $\alpha\simeq Y/u$, $\beta\simeq Y/d$ and $\theta\simeq Y/R$. The [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md) to [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) gives $n_1(\alpha+\theta)=n_2(\theta-\beta)$. Cancelling $Y$ proves the title equation. Virtual conjugates and other centre orientations require corresponding signed distances. Applying the relation at both faces of a [thin lens](thin-lens.md) proves the [lensmaker's equation](lensmaker-s-equation.md).
