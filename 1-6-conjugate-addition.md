<h1 id="1-6-conjugate-addition">1,6-Conjugate addition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate addition](conjugate-addition.md)

A [1,6-Conjugate addition](1-6-conjugate-addition.md) attacks the delta [carbon](carbon.md) of an extended conjugated [electrophile](electrophile.md). The resulting extended [enolate](enolate.md) can be protonated at different sites; [proton](proton.md) transfer or equilibration can favor a product retaining [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md)–[alkene](alkene.md) conjugation.
