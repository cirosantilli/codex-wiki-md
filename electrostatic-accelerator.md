# Electrostatic accelerator

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatic_accelerator)

An electrostatic accelerator gives a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) by taking it through a sustained [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). Its [ion source](ion-source.md), accelerating tube and target form a particularly direct chain: prepare a charge, let an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) do [work](work.md), and measure what arrives or what the target emits. A [vacuum](vacuum.md) reduces collisions with gas before the beam reaches its target. The [Cockcroft-Walton generator](cockcroft-walton-generator.md) supplies the potential by accumulating [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) on [capacitors](capacitor.md).
