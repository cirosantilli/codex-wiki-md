# Multivariate normal density

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md)

For a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) $\Sigma$, the distribution $N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$ has density

$$
f(x)=\frac{
\exp\!\left[-\frac12(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\right]
}{(2\pi)^{p/2}(\det\Sigma)^{1/2}}.
$$
