# Head-socle identity for a symmetric algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric algebra (Frobenius algebra)](symmetric-algebra-frobenius-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional [symmetric algebra](symmetric-algebra-frobenius-algebra.md) over any [field](field.md), an indecomposable [projective module](projective-module.md) $P=Ae$ has isomorphic simple [head of a module](head-of-a-module.md) and [socle](socle-mathematics.md). A symmetrizing functional pairs $eA$ with $Ae$ nondegenerately, identifying $Ae$ with the left [dual module](dual-module.md) $D(eA)$. Taking its [socle](socle-mathematics.md) gives $D(eA/eAJ(A))$. This dual of the right simple top and the left simple top $Ae/J(A)e$ belong to the same matrix block of the [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md) $A/J(A)$, so they are isomorphic. The result does not require an algebraically closed ground [field](field.md).
