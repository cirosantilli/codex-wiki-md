# Coprime factor lifting from valuation-extension uniqueness

↑ **Parent:** [Hensel's lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md)

Suppose a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md) on $K$ has a unique [extension of an absolute value](extension-of-an-absolute-value.md) to every finite extension. Factor a [monic](monic-polynomial.md) $g\in\mathcal O_K[X]$ into [monic](monic-polynomial.md) irreducible factors over $K$. All factors have integral coefficients because their roots have absolute value at most one. By [pure-power reduction of a monic irreducible polynomial](pure-power-reduction-of-a-monic-irreducible-polynomial.md), each factor reduces to a power of a single irreducible [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). If $\overline g=\overline g_1\overline g_2$ is a [coprime](coprime-integers.md) [monic](monic-polynomial.md) factorization, assign each whole irreducible factor of $g$, with its multiplicity, to the unique side containing its residue factor. The resulting [monic](monic-polynomial.md) $g_1,g_2\in\mathcal O_K[X]$ satisfy $g=g_1g_2$ and reduce to the prescribed factors. Completeness of $K$ is not required for this proof.
