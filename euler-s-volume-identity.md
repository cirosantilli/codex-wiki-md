<h1 id="euler-s-volume-identity">Euler's volume identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian evolution of a smooth flow](jacobian-evolution-of-a-smooth-flow.md)

For a smooth, locally invertible material map $\mathbf x=\mathbf X(\mathbf a,t)$ with $\mathbf X_t=\mathbf u(\mathbf X,t)$ and positive [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $J=\det(\partial\mathbf X/\partial\mathbf a)$, [Euler's volume identity](euler-s-volume-identity.md) states that $J_t=J\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$. Indeed, $F=\partial\mathbf X/\partial\mathbf a$ obeys $F_t=(\nabla\mathbf u)F$, so the [determinant](determinant.md) derivative is $J_t=J\operatorname{tr}(F^{-1}F_t)=J\operatorname{tr}(\nabla\mathbf u)$. This form of the [Jacobian evolution of a smooth flow](jacobian-evolution-of-a-smooth-flow.md) applies also to explicitly time-dependent [velocity fields](velocity-field.md). It gives the material-volume [Reynolds transport theorem](reynolds-transport-theorem.md) by [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) to the fixed particle labels. It is distinct from the complex-exponential [Euler's formula](euler-s-formula.md).
