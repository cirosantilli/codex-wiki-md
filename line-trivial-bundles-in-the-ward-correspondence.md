# Line-trivial bundles in the Ward correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose-Ward correspondence](penrose-ward-correspondence.md)

On a twistor domain containing the lines $L_x$ for $x$ in a space-time domain $U$, the Ward construction uses a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $E$ with $E|_{L_x}\cong\mathcal O^{\oplus r}$ for every $x\in U$. A degree-zero restriction is not sufficient: $\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(-1)$ has degree zero but is not holomorphically trivial. A trivial restriction has $r$ independent global [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) sections; their evaluations give a linewise [bundle frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) and hence a factorization of its patching [matrix](matrix.md). Nearby factors can be chosen holomorphically because $H^1(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O)^{r^2}=0$. Lines on which the splitting type is nontrivial are called jumping lines and obstruct this regular reconstruction there. For a globally regular field on affine [complexified Minkowski space](complexified-minkowski-spacetime.md) the condition is needed on all its incidence lines; on a smaller domain only those lines are required.
