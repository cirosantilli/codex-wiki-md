# Ocean carbonate chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

The linked equilibria among dissolved [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), bicarbonate, carbonate and hydrogen ions in seawater. At a fixed alkalinity, added carbon changes speciation and acidity, limiting how much extra carbon can be held at a given atmospheric [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md). Exchange and chemical partitioning therefore do not behave like irreversible destruction by a single first-order sink.
