# Cosmic distance ladder

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological distance measure](cosmological-distance-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_distance_ladder)

Geometrical distances to nearby calibrators determine the luminosities of indicators that can be observed farther away. Those indicators calibrate still more luminous indicators in turn. A ladder may connect parallax or maser distances to [Cepheid variables](cepheid-variable.md) and then to [Type Ia supernovae](type-ia-supernova.md). Each step propagates its calibration uncertainty to the final distance scale and the inferred [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md).
