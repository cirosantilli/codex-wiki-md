# Travelling front

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)

A [travelling front](travelling-front.md) is a [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) joining distinct limiting states ahead of and behind a moving interface. Its spatial profile depends on a coordinate such as $x-ct$.
