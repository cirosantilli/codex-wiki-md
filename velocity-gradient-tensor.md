# Velocity gradient tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity field](velocity-field.md)

The [velocity gradient tensor](velocity-gradient-tensor.md) is $A_{ij}=\partial_j u_i$. Its symmetric part is the [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) $S$ and its antisymmetric part describes local rotation. For [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), $\operatorname{tr}A=\operatorname{tr}S=0$. The sign convention for a cubic invariant must be specified explicitly.
