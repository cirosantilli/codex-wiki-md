# SLE boundary-point logarithmic separation diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-point Bessel flow for SLE](boundary-point-bessel-flow-for-sle.md)

For two boundary points $1<r$, let $V_t^x=g_t(x)-U_t$ and set $Z_t=\log(V_t^r-V_t^1)-\log V_t^1$. After the clock $q(u)=\int_0^u(V_s^1)^{-2}ds$, the [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) gives

$$
d\widetilde Z_t=\sqrt\kappa\,dW_t+\left(\frac{\kappa-4}{2}-\frac2{1+e^{\widetilde Z_t}}\right)dt.
$$

This one-dimensional [diffusion process](markov-diffusion.md) compares the swallowing times of nearby boundary points.
