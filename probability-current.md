# Probability current

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_current)

For a nonrelativistic particle of mass $m$, the probability current is

$$
J=-\frac{i\hbar}{2m}
\left(\psi^*\nabla\psi-\psi\nabla\psi^*\right).
$$
