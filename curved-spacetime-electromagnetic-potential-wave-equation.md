# Curved-spacetime electromagnetic potential wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Lorenz gauge condition](lorenz-gauge-condition.md)

With convention $\nabla^\mu F_{\mu\nu}=-\mu_0J_\nu$, write the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) as $F_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu A_\nu-\nabla_\nu A_\mu$. Commuting the contracted [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) gives $\nabla^\mu\nabla_\nu A_\mu=\nabla_\nu(\nabla^\mu A_\mu)+R^\alpha{}_\nu A_\alpha$. The [Lorenz gauge condition](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) removes the gradient-of-divergence term, leaving the displayed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) for a [covector field](one-form.md). A locally inertial coordinate system does not eliminate the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) term.
