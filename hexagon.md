# Hexagon

↑ **Parent:** [Polygon](polygon.md)

A [polygon](polygon.md) with six sides and six vertices. A regular [hexagon](hexagon.md) has equal sides and angles, with each interior angle equal to $2\pi/3$. Its [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md) consists of six rotations and six reflections, forming a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order twelve.
