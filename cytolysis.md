# Cytolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytolysis)

[Osmotic lysis](cytolysis.md) is rupture after excessive [water](water.md) entry and swelling. It is an outcome for susceptible [biological cells](biological-cell.md), not an inevitable response of every [biological cell](biological-cell.md) to a [hypotonic solution](hypotonic-solution.md). [Cell walls](cell-wall.md) can bear the resulting load, and cellular mechanisms can regulate [water](water.md) balance. A [protoplast](protoplast.md) challenge tests a particular preparation under particular conditions.
