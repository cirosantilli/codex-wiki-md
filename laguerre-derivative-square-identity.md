# Laguerre derivative square identity

↑ **Parent:** [Laguerre polynomial](laguerre-polynomial.md)

For [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) solutions of the [Laguerre differential equation](laguerre-differential-equation.md), put $D_n=L_n'-L_n$. The equation gives $D_n+tD_n'=-(n+1)L_n$. Integrating $\int tD_n(L_m'-L_m)e^{-t}$ by parts gives the displayed identity; the factor $t$ kills the endpoint at zero and exponential decay kills the endpoint at infinity. Its left side is symmetric in $n,m$, so subtracting the swapped identities proves [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) for distinct degrees. This provides a direct energy proof of [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) for the [Laguerre polynomials](laguerre-polynomial.md) without assuming their [norm](norm.md) formula.
