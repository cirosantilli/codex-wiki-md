<h1 id="so-3-noether-current-of-a-real-scalar-triplet">SO(3) Noether current of a real scalar triplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar triplet](real-scalar-triplet.md)

A real triplet with a rotationally invariant common [mass](mass.md) term has three conserved internal currents. With the displayed sign convention, the current for $\delta\boldsymbol\phi=\theta\mathbf n\times\boldsymbol\phi$ is the negative of the conventional canonical current contracted with $\mathbf n$. Overall current sign does not affect conservation. Integrated charges require convergent integrals and vanishing net boundary flux; a local [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) alone does not guarantee conservation in an open region.
