# Diffraction intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md)

For coherent elastic [diffraction](diffraction.md), the [intensity](intensity-physics.md) associated with a given [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is proportional to its squared modulus. Integrated [powder diffraction](powder-diffraction.md) intensities additionally include the [powder diffraction multiplicity](powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md), [Lorentz-polarization factor](lorentz-polarization-factor.md), [Debye–Waller factor](debye-waller-factor.md), absorption and instrument corrections. A peak's height is not its integrated [intensity](intensity-physics.md) unless its profile width and shape are also specified.
