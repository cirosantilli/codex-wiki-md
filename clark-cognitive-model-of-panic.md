# Clark cognitive model of panic

↑ **Parent:** [Panic disorder](panic-disorder.md)

[David M. Clark](david-m-clark.md)'s account proposes that catastrophic interpretations of bodily sensations increase [fear](fear.md) and [arousal](physiological-arousal.md), intensifying sensations available for further threatening interpretation. [Safety behaviors](safety-behavior.md) and avoidance can prevent disconfirmation. The theory distinguishes the presence of a sensation from the meaning assigned to it; a test should manipulate interpretation while measuring physiological input and subsequent [fear](fear.md).[Behaviour Research and Therapy 24, 461–470; original model of catastrophic misinterpretation](a-cognitive-approach-to-panic-by-david-m-clark.md)
