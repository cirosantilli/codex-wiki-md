# Fourier transform over a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

For a [local field](local-field.md) $F$, an [additive character](additive-character.md) $\psi$, and a [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) $dx$, the Fourier transform is

$$
\widehat f(y)=\int_Ff(x)\psi(xy)\,dx.
$$

It maps the [Schwartz-Bruhat space](schwartz-bruhat-space.md) to itself.
