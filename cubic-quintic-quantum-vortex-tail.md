# Cubic-quintic quantum vortex tail

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

For a [cubic-quintic Gross–Pitaevskii equation](cubic-quintic-gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) with normalized bulk amplitude one, a straight [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) of integer [winding number](winding-number.md) $\mathcal N$ has radial equation $R''+R'/r-\mathcal N^2R/r^2+(1-\alpha R^2-\beta R^4)R=0$. With $\alpha+\beta=1$, the derivative of the nonlinear term at $R=1$ is $-2(1+\beta)$. Balancing the order-$r^{-2}$ centrifugal term with the bulk amplitude restoring term proves the displayed tail for a stable nondegenerate bulk state, $1+\beta>0$. A vortex's amplitude tends to one; its full complex field tends to $e^{i\mathcal N\theta}$, not a direction-independent constant. At $\beta=-1$ the stated inverse-square expansion cannot cancel the centrifugal term.
