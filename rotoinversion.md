# Rotoinversion

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic point group](crystallographic-point-group.md)

The composition of a rotation about an axis with inversion through a point on the axis. A $\bar4$ operation rotates by a quarter turn and reverses all coordinates through the inversion center. It can preserve a basis whose height pattern is not preserved by a pure fourfold rotation.
