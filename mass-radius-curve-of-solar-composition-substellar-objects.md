# Mass-radius curve of solar-composition substellar objects

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-radius relation](mass-radius-relation.md)

For a specified age and irradiation, a [hydrogen](hydrogen.md)–[helium](helium.md) sequence rises at low mass, has radii near Jupiter's across giant planets and [brown dwarfs](brown-dwarf.md), and declines as [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) becomes important. Above the [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md), sustained fusion supports a rising low-mass stellar branch.
