# Tensor product of chain complexes

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

The [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of homologically graded [chain complexes](chain-complex.md) has differential $D(x_p\otimes y)=dx_p\otimes y+(-1)^p x_p\otimes dy$. The sign makes the two mixed terms in $D^2$ cancel. Total degree is the sum of the two degrees.
