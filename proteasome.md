# Proteasome

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteasome)

A [proteasome](proteasome.md) is a [protein](protein-split.md) complex that carries out [proteolysis](proteolysis.md); unlike a [lysosome](lysosome.md), it has no surrounding [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md).
