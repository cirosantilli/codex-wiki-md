# Low-temperature heat capacity of a three-dimensional Fermi gas

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

For a three-dimensional [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with quadratic dispersion and fixed particle number, the total [density of states](density-of-states.md) at the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) is $g(E_F)=3N/(2E_F)$. The [Sommerfeld coefficient](electronic-heat-capacity-coefficient.md) then gives $C_V=(\pi^2/2)Nk_B(T/T_F)$ to leading order, where $T_F=E_F/k_B$. The fixed-number [energy](energy-split.md) correction is $\Delta U=(\pi^2/4)N(k_BT)^2/E_F$. Only states near the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) change occupation, explaining the small active fraction.
