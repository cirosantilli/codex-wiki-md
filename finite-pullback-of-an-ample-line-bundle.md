# Finite pullback of an ample line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md)

For a [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md) $f:X\to Y$, the pullback of an [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md). If $f$ is also surjective, [ampleness](ample-cartier-divisor.md) is equivalent in both directions. These results hold for proper schemes over a field, including nonreduced schemes. Cohomological [ampleness](ample-cartier-divisor.md) and the exact finite pushforward prove the forward direction; coherent-sheaf devissage gives finite-surjective descent. In particular, a finite map to projective space makes the pulled-back hyperplane bundle [ample](ample-line-bundle.md).
