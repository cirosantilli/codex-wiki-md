# Spectroscopic velocity drift

↑ **Parent:** [Redshift drift](redshift-drift.md)

For a transition with fixed rest wavelength, $\lambda_{\rm obs}=(1+z)\lambda_{\rm rest}$. The apparent spectroscopic velocity shift is $dv=c\,d\lambda_{\rm obs}/\lambda_{\rm obs}=c\,dz/(1+z)$. It is a convenient wavelength-shift unit, not necessarily a change in a source's [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md). Omitting the denominator $1+z$ overestimates the signal at high [redshift](redshift.md).
