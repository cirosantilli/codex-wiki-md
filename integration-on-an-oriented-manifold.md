# Integration on an oriented manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

A compactly supported top-degree [differential form](differential-form-split.md) on an oriented smooth [manifold](topological-manifold.md) is integrated using orientation-preserving coordinates and a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md). The coordinate [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) formula makes the result independent of the choices. More generally, a form is integrated over a lower-dimensional oriented submanifold by its [pullback](pullback-category-theory.md). The [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) states $\int_M d\beta=\int_{\partial M}\beta$ with the induced boundary orientation. Integrating a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) over a closed cycle depends only on its [homology class](homology-class.md), since its integral over a boundary vanishes.
