# Contracting Euclidean similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similarity](self-similarity.md)

A [contracting Euclidean similarity](contracting-euclidean-similarity.md) multiplies every [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) by a constant $c<1$; equivalently it has the form $cRx+b$ for an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $R$ and translation vector $b$. Finite families of such maps generate compact sets with [self-similarity](self-similarity.md).
