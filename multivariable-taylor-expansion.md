# Multivariable Taylor expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md)

For a scalar function with continuous third derivatives near $a$, apply the one-dimensional [Taylor theorem](taylor-s-theorem.md) to $g(t)=f(a+th)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $g'(0)=Df(a)h$ and $g''(0)=h^TH_f(a)h$, proving the displayed formula. Here $H_f$ is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). The finite polynomial part is the [Multivariate Taylor polynomial](multivariate-taylor-polynomial.md); the remainder controls its accuracy.
