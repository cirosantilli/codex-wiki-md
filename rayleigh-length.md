# Rayleigh length

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayleigh_length)

The [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) of a fundamental [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md) is the positive axial distance from the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) at which its [radius](radius.md) grows from $w_0$ to $\sqrt2w_0$. Thus its contour [area](surface-area.md) doubles and its peak [irradiance](irradiance.md) halves. Within $|z|\leq z_R$, the [radius](radius.md) stays at most $\sqrt2w_0$; for $|z|\gg z_R$, [beam divergence](beam-divergence.md) dominates. At fixed [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md), the [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) scales quadratically with the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) [radius](radius.md). The formula here uses the [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md), not an unlabelled [wavelength](wavelength.md) in a material.
