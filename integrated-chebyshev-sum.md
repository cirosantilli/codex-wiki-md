# Integrated Chebyshev sum

↑ **Parent:** [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md)

For $\psi(t)=\sum_{m\leq t}\Lambda(m)$, finite rearrangement and the [Von Mangoldt divisor identity](von-mangoldt-divisor-identity.md) give

$$
T(x)=\sum_{n\leq x}\psi(x/n)=\sum_{m\leq x}\log m=\log(\lfloor x\rfloor!).
$$

The [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md), or integral comparison for the [logarithm](logarithm.md), gives $T(x)=x\log x-x+O(\log(x+1))$ for real $x\geq1$.
