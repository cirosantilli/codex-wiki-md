# Compound binomial distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

For an independent [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) count $N\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)$, the aggregate can also be represented as $\sum_{j=1}^n B_jX_j$, with independent parameter-$p$ [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) $B_j$ and independent claim sizes. Its [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $n\log(1-p+pM_X(t))$. Its third [cumulant](cumulant.md) is $n(pm_3-3p^2m_1m_2+2p^3m_1^3)$ in severity [moments](moment.md). Constant positive claims $d$ give $np(1-p)(1-2p)d^3$, which is negative when $p>1/2$. Thus positive claim sizes do not by themselves force a positive third [central moment](central-moment.md).
