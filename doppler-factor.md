# Doppler factor

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic beaming](relativistic-beaming.md)

For velocity $\beta c$ at angle $\theta$ toward the observer, $\delta_D=[\Gamma(1-\beta\cos\theta)]^{-1}$. Frequencies transform as $\nu=\delta_D\nu^\prime$ before cosmological [redshift](redshift.md). Specific-intensity transformation follows invariance of $I_\nu/\nu^3$.
