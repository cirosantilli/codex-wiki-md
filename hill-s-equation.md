<h1 id="hill-s-equation">Hill's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

A linear second-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) with periodic coefficient. For accelerator [beam optics](beam-optics.md), $s$ measures distance along the reference orbit and $k(s)$ is the local transverse focusing strength.
