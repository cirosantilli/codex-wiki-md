<h1 id="p-x-phi-scalar-field-theory">P(X, phi) scalar field theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Field theory in cosmology](field-theory-in-cosmology.md)

A $P(X,\phi)$ scalar theory has Lagrangian density $P$ depending on the field and $X=-g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi/2$. It includes canonical scalar theory and models with noncanonical kinetic interactions.

A [k-essence](k-essence.md) model uses noncanonical kinetic terms for cosmological acceleration; a general [P(X, phi) scalar field theory](p-x-phi-scalar-field-theory.md) need not have that application.
