# Spectrum homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Map of topological spectra](map-of-topological-spectra.md)

A [spectrum homotopy](spectrum-homotopy.md) between compatible maps $D\to E$ is a map from the cylinder spectrum $D\wedge[0,1]_+$ restricting to them at the endpoints. Thus the homotopies at all levels must themselves respect the structure maps.
