# Ghost propagator for a negative derivative kinetic term

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md)

With signature $(+---)$ and action $-\int\partial^\mu\bar c\,\partial_\mu c$, integration by parts gives the quadratic kernel $K_c=\Box$, or $-p^2$ in Fourier space. The [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) with odd sources gives $Z=\exp[-i\bar\eta K_{c,F}^{-1}\eta]$. Left and right source derivatives, including their insertion factors, yield $\langle Tc\bar c\rangle=iK_{c,F}^{-1}$ and hence the displayed sign. Replacing $\bar c$ by $-\bar c$ changes this propagator and the [ghost-gluon vertex](ghost-gluon-vertex.md) together; it is a convention change, not a physical discrepancy.
