# Generic isolation of lifted simple geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection test for nonisometric finite covers](intersection-test-for-nonisometric-finite-covers.md)

In a finite hyperbolic covering, primitive lifts of a primitive closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) correspond to cycles of its [monodromy permutation](monodromy-permutation.md); an $r$-cycle gives length $r\ell(\gamma)$. In a real-analytic family of compact [hyperbolic surfaces](hyperbolic-surface.md) or hyperbolic cone orbifolds, a prescribed multiple of a simple curve's length can be isolated generically from unrelated lifted lengths. To exclude an identity of length functions, pinch the simple curve: only its peripheral powers can have length tending to zero, while intersecting curves are long by the [collar lemma](collar-lemma.md). The countably many remaining length equalities have empty interior, so the [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md) supplies a parameter avoiding them. Only finitely many closed geodesics lie below a fixed local length bound; thus the isolation persists in an open parameter neighbourhood. This makes cycle-intersection information an intrinsic obstruction to an [isometry](isometry.md), rather than an invariant of a chosen covering map alone.
