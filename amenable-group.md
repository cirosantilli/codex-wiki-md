# Amenable group

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amenable_group)

A discrete group $G$ is amenable when it admits a left-invariant [finitely additive probability measure](finitely-additive-probability-measure.md) on all subsets of $G$. Equivalently, it admits an [invariant mean](invariant-mean.md) on the bounded real-valued functions on $G$.
