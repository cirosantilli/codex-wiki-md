# Very general surface of degree at least four has no smooth rational curve

↑ **Parent:** [Noether–Lefschetz theorem](noether-lefschetz-theorem.md)

The [Noether–Lefschetz theorem](noether-lefschetz-theorem.md) makes any curve class a positive multiple of the hyperplane class, with positive self-intersection. But the [smooth rational-curve self-intersection on a projective hypersurface](smooth-rational-curve-self-intersection-on-a-projective-hypersurface.md) formula makes every smooth rational curve have self-intersection at most $-2$ when the surface degree is at least four. Thus none exists. The smoothness condition is essential; this does not prohibit singular curves whose normalizations are rational.
