# Unions of subobjects are pushouts

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent category](coherent-category.md)

In a [coherent category](coherent-category.md), maps $A\to Y$ and $B\to Y$ agreeing on the intersection define a relation from $A\cup B$ to $Y$ by taking the union of their graphs. It is total because the two subobjects cover their union, and single-valued because the maps agree on overlap. [Total functional relations define morphisms](total-functional-relations-define-morphisms.md), giving a unique glued map. This is the [pushout](pushout.md) universal property without assuming a general supply of pushouts.
