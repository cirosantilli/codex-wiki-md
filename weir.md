# Weir

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic control](hydraulic-control.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weir)

A weir is an obstruction across a channel that can regulate [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) by creating a hydraulic control. In a [shallow water](shallow-water-approximation.md) model a sufficiently smooth crest has a critical section, with upstream flow subcritical and downstream flow supercritical under free discharge. Losses, submergence and geometry determine whether this ideal control applies.
