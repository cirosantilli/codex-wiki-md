# Meridional-wave instability of a two-layer Sverdrup flow

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a meridional two-layer current](linear-stability-of-a-meridional-two-layer-current.md)

For purely meridional [wavevectors](wavevector.md), $k=0$, $l\ne0$, the growth rate of a uniform meridional upper-layer flow $V$ over a resting lower layer is

$$
\gamma=\frac{|lV|}{2}\sqrt{\frac{2F-l^2}{l^2+2F}}.
$$

Growth requires $V\ne0$ and $0<l^2<2F$. The perturbation has no northward velocity, so [planetary vorticity](planetary-vorticity.md) advection does not explicitly stabilize it. At fixed normalized [wind stress curl](wind-stress-curl.md), $V=W/\beta$ means stronger $|\beta|$ nevertheless reduces the shear and growth rate.
