# Radial uncertainty scale in a uniform sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md)

For uniform probability per volume in a sphere of radius $R$, the radial [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $3r^2/R^3$. Its root-mean-square radius is $\sqrt{3/5}R$, whereas its scalar-radius standard deviation is $\sqrt{3/80}R$. These are distinct from a fully specified quantum momentum uncertainty.
