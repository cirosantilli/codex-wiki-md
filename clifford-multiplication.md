# Clifford multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md)

Clifford multiplication is the action of a [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) on a [module](module-mathematics.md). The displayed relation uses the negative [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) convention; in a compatible [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-form.md), $c(v)^*=-c(v)$ for real $v$. Another convention writes $C(v)=ic(v)$, giving self-adjoint generators with positive anticommutator. Switching between these conventions switches a geometric [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md) between self-adjoint and skew-adjoint realizations.
