# Extended Euclidean algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean algorithm](euclidean-algorithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_Euclidean_algorithm)

The extended Euclidean algorithm also computes integers $x,y$ satisfying $ax+by=\gcd(a,b)$. When $a$ and $b$ are coprime, either coefficient gives the corresponding modular multiplicative inverse.
