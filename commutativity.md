# Commutativity

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutativity)

A [binary operation](binary-operation.md) is commutative if $a*b=b*a$ for every pair in its domain. A particular pair may commute even when the operation is not commutative. For example, [ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md) and [ordinal multiplication](ordinal-multiplication.md) are not generally commutative.
