# Two independent exponential variables conditioned on their sum

↑ **Parent:** [Beta-gamma independence](beta-gamma-independence.md)

If $X,Y$ are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md) with one common rate $\lambda>0$, put $U=X+Y$. The [change of variables for probability densities](change-of-variables-for-probability-densities.md) $(x,y)=(u-y,y)$ gives $f_{U,Y}(u,y)=\lambda^2e^{-\lambda u}$ on $0<y<u$. Its [marginal density](marginal-density.md) is $\lambda^2u e^{-\lambda u}$, so the [conditional probability density](conditional-density.md) of $Y$ given $U=u>0$ is $1/u$ on $(0,u)$. This is the shape-$(1,1)$ case of [beta-gamma independence](beta-gamma-independence.md). In particular $Y/U$ is uniform on $(0,1)$ and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of $U$. Equality of the rates is essential: different rates leave an exponential tilt across the conditional interval.
