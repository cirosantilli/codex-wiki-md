# Right-invariant vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Left and right translation on a Lie group](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md)

The right-invariant [vector field](vector-field.md) associated with a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) element $\xi$ is obtained by right translation of its value at the identity. Its flow is $g\mapsto\exp(t\xi)g$, hence consists of [left translations on a Lie group](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md). Right invariance follows because left and right multiplication commute.
