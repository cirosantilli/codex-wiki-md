# Navier-slip Taylor swimming sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor swimming sheet](taylor-swimming-sheet.md)

A transversely waving [Taylor swimming sheet](taylor-swimming-sheet.md) can have a [Navier slip boundary condition](navier-slip-boundary-condition.md) relating surface tangential velocity to shear and a [slip length](slip-length.md) $\gamma$. The small-amplitude parameters are $\epsilon=ky_0$ and $\delta=k\gamma$. The first-order velocity is unchanged by slip, but evaluation of shear at the displaced surface changes the second-order mean velocity.
