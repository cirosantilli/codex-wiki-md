# Insurance exposure

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate claims model](aggregate-claims-model.md)

[Insurance exposure](insurance-exposure.md) scales the opportunity for claims: $w_j$ policy-years produce expected count $w_j\Theta$ at common annual frequency $\Theta$. Conditionally [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [insurance exposures](insurance-exposure.md) add, so the total count and total [insurance exposure](insurance-exposure.md) are [sufficient statistics](sufficient-statistic.md) in a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) frequency model. A yearly claim average has [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of the number of policies, but its [conditional variance](conditional-variance.md) falls with that number.
