# Adjacent sums of a cyclic basis

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

For a [basis](basis.md) $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ with cyclic indexing, the vectors $e_j+e_{j+1}$ have coefficient matrix $I+S$, where $S$ cyclically shifts coordinates. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is $1-(-1)^n$. They therefore form a [basis](basis.md) exactly when $n$ is odd over a field of characteristic different from two. For even $n$, alternating coefficients produce a nontrivial [linear dependence](linear-dependence.md).
