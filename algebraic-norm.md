# Algebraic norm

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)

The algebraic norm of an element of a finite field extension is the determinant of multiplication by that element. In a quadratic subring of $\mathbb C$, it is often $N(\alpha)=\alpha\overline\alpha$.
