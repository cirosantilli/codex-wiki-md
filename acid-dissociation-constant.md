# Acid dissociation constant

↑ **Parent:** [Acid (chemistry)](acid-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acid_dissociation_constant)

The [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) quantifies the [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) for loss of one [proton](proton.md) from a [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md). In [water](water.md), write the actual proton transfer as $\mathrm{HA}+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O\rightleftharpoons\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^++\mathrm A^-$. Its full [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) is

$$
K_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\frac{a_{\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^+}a_{\mathrm A^-}}{a_{\mathrm{HA}}a_{\mathrm H_2\mathrm O}}.
$$

The usual aqueous [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) uses the [solvent](solvent.md) convention with [water](water.md) [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) approximated by one:

$$
K_a=\frac{a_{\mathrm H^+}a_{\mathrm A^-}}{a_{\mathrm{HA}}},\qquad \mathrm pK_a=-\log_{10}K_a.
$$

Here hydrated [hydrogen ions](hydrogen-ion.md) are understood; $\mathrm H^+$ is shorthand, not an isolated bare [proton](proton.md) in the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). All [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md) and $K_a$ are [dimensionless quantities](dimensionless-quantity.md), using consistent [standard states](standard-state.md). If [water](water.md) [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) differs appreciably from one, retain it in the full [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) instead of silently mixing conventions.

With [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md) $\gamma_i$ and reference [concentration](concentration.md) $c^\circ=1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, put $a_i=\gamma_i[i]/c^\circ$ for each [solute](solute.md). Then

$$
K_a=\frac{\gamma_{\mathrm H^+}\gamma_{\mathrm A^-}}{\gamma_{\mathrm{HA}}}
\frac{[\mathrm H^+][\mathrm A^-]}{c^\circ[\mathrm{HA}]}
\approx\frac{[\mathrm H^+][\mathrm A^-]}{c^\circ[\mathrm{HA}]}.
$$

The last expression is the dilute ideal [concentration](concentration.md) approximation, not the definition in a nonideal [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). Omitting $c^\circ$ instead produces a quotient with units of [concentration](concentration.md); its numerical value depends on the chosen units. Do not take a [logarithm](logarithm.md) of that dimensional quotient. Conditional [concentration](concentration.md) quotients can change with [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) even at fixed [temperature](temperature.md), whereas the thermodynamic [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) retains the [activity coefficient](activity-coefficient.md) corrections. The [acid dissociation constant](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/acid-dissociation-constant.md) entry distinguishes these conventions.

At the same [temperature](temperature.md), [solvent](solvent.md) and [standard states](standard-state.md), a larger [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md), equivalently a smaller [pKa](pka.md), indicates a stronger tendency to donate a [proton](proton.md). It does not specify an [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md), a [pH](ph.md), or a [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md). Composition and the [weak acid mass and charge balances](weak-acid-mass-and-charge-balances.md) are needed to predict [pH](ph.md); [chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics.md) addresses how quickly the [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) is approached.
