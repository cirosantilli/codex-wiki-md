# Resolution of a fractional factorial design

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional factorial design](fractional-factorial-design.md)

The resolution of a regular fraction is the shortest nonidentity word in its [defining contrast subgroup](defining-contrast-subgroup.md). Two effects alias only when their word product belongs to that subgroup. Thus resolution VI separates all [main effects](main-effect.md) and [two-factor interactions](two-factor-interaction.md) from one another and from three-factor interactions, though some three-factor interactions alias in complementary pairs.
