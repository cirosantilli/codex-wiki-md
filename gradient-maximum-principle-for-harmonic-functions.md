# Gradient maximum principle for harmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum principle for subharmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-subharmonic-functions.md)

For a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $w$ smooth inside a bounded domain and $C^1$ on its closure, $\Delta|Dw|^2=2|D^2w|^2\geq0$. The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) and continuity of the [gradient](gradient.md) imply $\sup_\Omega|Dw|=\sup_{\partial\Omega}|Dw|$.
