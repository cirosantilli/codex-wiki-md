# Synchronous photon momentum redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous photon brightness equation](synchronous-photon-brightness-equation.md)

For a photon in $ds^2=a^2[-d\tau^2+(\delta_{ij}+h_{ij})dx^idx^j]$, the [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) condition cancels the homogeneous $2\mathcal H p^0$ term in the derivative of $q=a^2p^0$. The remaining first-order [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) gives the displayed momentum change. Its direction is constant at zeroth order, so first-order redshift and brightness sources can be evaluated on the unperturbed ray; direction deflection multiplied by an already first-order anisotropy is second order.
