# Structure theorem for compactly supported distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md)

Every compactly supported distribution is a finite sum of distributional derivatives of bounded continuous functions. One proof convolves it with a sufficiently high-order [Bessel potential](bessel-potential.md) and then applies a power of $1-\Delta$.
