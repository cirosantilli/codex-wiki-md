# Hydrogen binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md)

For $p+e^-\leftrightarrow H+\gamma$, the hydrogen binding energy is the rest-energy defect

$$
E_{\mathrm{bind}}=(m_p+m_e-m_H)c^2.
$$
