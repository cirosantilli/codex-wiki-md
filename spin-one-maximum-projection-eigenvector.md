# Spin-one maximum-projection eigenvector

↑ **Parent:** [Spin coherent state](spin-coherent-state.md)

In the ordered z-projection basis $|1,1\rangle,|1,0\rangle,|1,-1\rangle$, the normalized [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $J_x\sin\theta+J_z\cos\theta$ with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $+\hbar$ is the displayed vector. It follows by a y-axis [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) of the maximum-weight state, or direct solution of the tridiagonal [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) equation. Squaring its entries gives the three z-projection measurement [probabilities](probability.md).
