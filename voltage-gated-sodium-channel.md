# Voltage-gated sodium channel

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

A [voltage-gated sodium channel](voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) couples voltage-sensor movement to a sodium-permeable pore. Activation and inactivation are distinct transitions, and loss of current must be separated from altered expression or membrane delivery.
