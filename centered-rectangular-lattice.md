# Centered rectangular lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional Bravais lattice](two-dimensional-bravais-lattice.md)

A lattice represented by a rectangular conventional cell with corner points and one center point. For conventional sides $2a,2b$, primitive vectors can be $(a,b)$ and $(-a,b)$. The special case $a=b$ is a [square lattice](square-lattice.md) with a rotated primitive basis.
