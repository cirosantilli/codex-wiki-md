# Viscous shock

↑ **Parent:** [Shock wave](shock-wave.md)

A [viscous shock](viscous-shock.md) is a smooth travelling transition produced when positive [diffusion](diffusion.md) resolves a [shock](shock-wave.md) of a [conservation law](conservation-law.md). For $u_t+q(u)_x=\epsilon[D(u)u_x]_x$ with $D>0$, an inner coordinate $X=(x-ct)/\epsilon$ gives $D(U)U'=q(U)-q(u_-)-c(U-u_-)$. The endpoint conditions impose the Rankine-Hugoniot speed. If $q$ is strictly concave, the right side is positive between its roots, so the admissible profile increases from its rear state to its front state. This supplies the entropy selection directly from a regularizing mechanism.
