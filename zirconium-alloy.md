# Zirconium alloy

↑ **Parent:** [Alloy](alloy.md)

An alloy based on [zirconium](zirconium.md). Low [neutron](neutron-split.md) absorption and [corrosion](corrosion.md) behaviour motivate [nuclear fuel cladding](nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) applications; [irradiation growth](irradiation-growth.md), [creep](creep-deformation.md) and hydrogen uptake remain constraints.
