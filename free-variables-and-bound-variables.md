# Free variables and bound variables

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_variables_and_bound_variables)

A variable occurrence is bound when it falls within the scope of a binding operation that binds that occurrence; otherwise it is free. Quantifiers and lambda abstractions are common binders. In first-order formulas, a [free variable](free-variable.md) occurrence is outside the scope of a corresponding quantifier.
