# Skin-temperature perturbation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoregulation](thermoregulation.md)

Twelve resting volunteers wore a water-perfused suit. Tubes covered the torso, upper arms and legs; the right forearm remained uncovered for laser Doppler blood-flow measurement. Six [thermocouples](thermocouple.md) estimated mean [skin](skin.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md); a sublingual [thermocouple](thermocouple.md) measured oral [temperature](temperature-split.md). A dry-air capsule measured local sweat output from outgoing air moisture. [Methods, Temperature-Control Procedures: garment, uncovered forearm, blood-flow probe, ventilated capsule and thermocouples](cortical-thalamic-and-hypothalamic-responses-to-cooling-and-warming-the-skin-in-awake-humans-a-positron-emission-tomography-study-by-gary-f-egan-and-others.md)

After two control scans near $34\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ [skin](skin.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md), the suit cooled and then warmed the [skin](skin.md). Mean oral [temperature](temperature-split.md) was $36.6\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ during both cooling and early warming; mean [skin](skin.md) readings were $30.5$ and $38.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Cutaneous vascular conductance fell during cooling and rose during warming. The inference is that compensatory blood-flow responses can precede an appreciable internal [temperature](temperature-split.md) change. [Methods, Experimental Protocol; Results, Body Temperature and Hemodynamic Responses; Table 1, SC1 and H1](cortical-thalamic-and-hypothalamic-responses-to-cooling-and-warming-the-skin-in-awake-humans-a-positron-emission-tomography-study-by-gary-f-egan-and-others.md)

Repeated baselines controlled comparison within participants; closed mouths reduced oral-probe cooling artifacts. A later control with reduced oral [temperature](temperature-split.md) was excluded. Oral [temperature](temperature-split.md) is a proxy, local flow is not whole-body [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md), and the fixed condition order leaves time effects possible. Brain imaging measured regional blood flow, not direct [neuron](neuron.md) firing or causal necessity.
