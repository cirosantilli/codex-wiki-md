# Fine structure

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

Fine structure splits atomic levels through relativistic corrections and interactions involving spin, such as spin-orbit coupling. Different total angular momenta can then have distinct energies. Even a small ground-term splitting can matter for density-sensitive [metastable atomic levels](metastable-atomic-level.md), because weak radiative decay between the levels competes with collisional mixing.
