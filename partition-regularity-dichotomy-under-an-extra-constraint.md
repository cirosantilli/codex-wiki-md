# Partition regularity dichotomy under an extra constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Partition regular matrix](partition-regular-matrix.md)

For a [partition regular matrix](partition-regular-matrix.md) $A$ and any property $P$ of its positive solution vectors, either every [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) admits a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) solution with $P$, or every [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) admits one without $P$. If the first alternative fails, take a witnessing coloring and pair it with any other coloring. Partition regularity of the product coloring forces a solution avoiding $P$ in the other coloring. The alternatives need not be exclusive.
