# Multinomial likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md)

For category counts $y_1,\ldots,y_k$ with fixed total $n$ and probabilities $p_1,\ldots,p_k$, the multinomial likelihood is

$$
L(p_1,\ldots,p_k)
=\frac{n!}{\prod_jy_j!}\prod_jp_j^{y_j},
\qquad \sum_jp_j=1.
$$

Its [maximum-likelihood fitted probabilities](maximum-likelihood-fitted-probability.md) are $\widehat p_j=y_j/n$.
