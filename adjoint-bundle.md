# Adjoint bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Associated vector bundle](associated-vector-bundle.md)

The adjoint bundle is the [associated vector bundle](associated-vector-bundle.md) for the [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) on its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). The [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) of a [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md) is globally a two-form with values in this bundle. Differences of [principal connections](connection-principal-bundle.md) and their infinitesimal variations are one-forms with values in the same bundle.
