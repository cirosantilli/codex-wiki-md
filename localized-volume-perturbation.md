# Localized volume perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

For a nonnegative smooth [bump function](bump-function.md) supported in an open set, multiplying a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) by $1+t\eta$ increases volume there for $t>0$ while leaving the metric elsewhere fixed. For distinct regular compact domains a perturbation supported in the interior of one outside the other breaks equal volume, and therefore breaks any [isometry](isometry.md) between the domains. The perturbations tend to the original metric in the smooth topology.
