# Matrix polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

Evaluate a scalar [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) on a square [matrix](matrix.md) by replacing its variable with that [matrix](matrix.md) and its constant term with a multiple of the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md). Powers of the same [matrix](matrix.md) [commute](commuting-elements.md), so $p(A)q(A)=(pq)(A)$. For an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $Av=\lambda v$, $p(A)v=p(\lambda)v$.
