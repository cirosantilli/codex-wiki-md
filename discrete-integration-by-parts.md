# Discrete integration by parts

↑ **Parent:** [Difference quotient](difference-quotient.md)

With $\delta_h f(x)=[f(x+he_\ell)-f(x)]/h$, a change of variables gives

$$
\int g\,\delta_h f=-\int(\delta_{-h}g)f.
$$

The [compact support](compact-support.md) of $f$ and its translates must remain inside the integration domain, or one must integrate over the whole space with convergent integrals. In particular $\delta_{-h}g(x)=[g(x)-g(x-he_\ell)]/h$: the denominator's sign matters. This is the [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) analogue of [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md).
