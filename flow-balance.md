# Flow balance

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-cost flow problem](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md)

A flow balance prescribes net outgoing minus incoming flow at a vertex. Positive $b_i$ is supply and negative $b_i$ is demand; zero is ordinary [flow conservation](flow-conservation.md). Summing all balances requires total supply to equal total demand. In a [directed graph](directed-graph.md), these equations are represented by the [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence-matrix.md).
