# Dynamin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[dynamin](dynamin.md) is a large [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md)-dependent membrane-remodeling [protein](protein-split.md). In several endocytic pathways it contributes to constriction and [membrane fission](membrane-fission.md); its role is distinct from initial cargo capture.
