# Modular multiplication eigenstates

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum order finding](quantum-order-finding.md)

For $\gcd(x,n)=1$ and order $r$, multiplication by $x$ modulo $n$ is a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md). On the orbit of one it cyclically permutes $r$ basis states. The displayed orthonormal [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) have [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $e^{2\pi is/r}$, and $|1\rangle=r^{-1/2}\sum_s|u_s\rangle$. Thus [quantum phase estimation](quantum-phase-estimation.md) on this efficiently prepared state samples a uniform $s$ without knowing $r$. Controlled powers are implemented by [quantum modular exponentiation](quantum-modular-exponentiation.md). Sufficient phase resolution allows [continued-fraction recovery in quantum order finding](continued-fraction-recovery-in-quantum-order-finding.md); the reduced denominator equals $r$ only when $s$ is coprime to $r$.
