# Overspill lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Non-standard model of arithmetic](non-standard-model-of-arithmetic.md)

If a definable property in a [nonstandard model of Peano arithmetic](non-standard-model-of-arithmetic.md) holds of every standard natural number, then it holds of some nonstandard element. Equivalently, a definable set containing the entire [standard cut of a nonstandard model of arithmetic](standard-cut-of-a-nonstandard-model-of-arithmetic.md) must overspill beyond it.

This is the arithmetic case of [overspill](overspill.md), which also applies to internal subsets of nonstandard extensions in analysis.
