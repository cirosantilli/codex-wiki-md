# Temperature ratio of an early-decoupled neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md)

A relativistic species decoupling before ordinary [neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md) cools as $T_X\propto a^{-1}$. [Cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) in the remaining bath gives $T_X/T_\nu=[10.75/g_{*s,D}]^{1/3}$, where $g_{*s,D}$ counts that bath just after the early species has left it. Subsequent [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md) gives $T_\nu/T_\gamma=(4/11)^{1/3}$. For the ideal high-temperature [relativistic entropy content of the Standard Model](relativistic-entropy-content-of-the-standard-model.md), the resulting ratio is about $0.332$.
