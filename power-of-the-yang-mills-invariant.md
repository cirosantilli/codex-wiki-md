# Power of the Yang-Mills invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md)

For a semisimple gauge algebra and positive integer $p$, this [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) density yields $D_\mu(s^{p-1}F^{\mu\nu})=0$. The curvature variation $\delta F_{\mu\nu}=D_\mu\delta A_\nu-D_\nu\delta A_\mu$ gives $\delta s=4\kappa(D_\mu\delta A_\nu,F^{\mu\nu})$, and [gauge-covariant integration by parts](gauge-covariant-integration-by-parts.md) proves the equation. More generally a density $f(s)$ gives $D_\mu(f'(s)F^{\mu\nu})=0$. The product form retains the equation at zeros of $s$; dividing by $s$ can discard solutions.
