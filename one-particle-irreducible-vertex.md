# One-particle-irreducible vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

A [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) of the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) gives an amputated [one-particle-irreducible correlation function](one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md). Its perturbative graphs stay connected after cutting any single internal propagator. A free quadratic effective [action](action.md) has no vertices of order greater than two; interactions generate higher vertices.
