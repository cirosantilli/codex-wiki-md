# Localic Tychonoff theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Compact locale](compact-locale.md)

An arbitrary product of [compact locales](compact-locale.md) is compact. Finite products correspond to [preframe tensor products](preframe-tensor-product.md), whose compactness follows by tensoring the top tests. An arbitrary product is obtained by the filtered colimit of finite [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) coproducts; the eventual-top test proves that filtered colimits preserve compactness. No choice of points is required.
