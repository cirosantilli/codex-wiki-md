# Lattice plane

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

A [lattice plane](lattice-plane.md) is a [plane](plane.md) containing a two-dimensional periodic array of [lattice points](lattice-point.md). Parallel [lattice planes](lattice-plane.md) form equally spaced geometrical sheets; an [atomic basis](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) can place [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) between them.

With [primitive lattice translation vectors](primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) $a_i$, write $r=xa_1+ya_2+za_3$. For [integers](integer-split.md) $h,k,l$ with [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) one, the [plane equations](cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md)

$$
hx+ky+lz=m,\qquad m\in\mathbb Z,
$$

give a complete family labeled by [Miller indices](miller-index.md) $(hkl)$. In a [primitive cubic lattice](primitive-cubic-lattice.md), $(110)$ means $x+y=m$, parallel to $a_3$.[Online Dictionary of Crystallography by International Union of Crystallography; Definition, Direct space: intercepts, plane equation, and the restriction to relatively prime indices when primitive axes are used](online-dictionary-of-crystallography-by-international-union-of-crystallography/miller-indices.md)
