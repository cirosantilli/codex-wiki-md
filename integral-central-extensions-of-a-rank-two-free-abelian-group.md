# Integral central extensions of a rank-two free abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Second group cohomology classifies group extensions](second-group-cohomology-classifies-group-extensions.md)

With a fixed identification of the kernel with $\mathbb Z$ and quotient with $\mathbb Z^2$, these central [group extensions](group-extension.md) are classified by $n\in\mathbb Z$. Choose lifts $x,y$ of the quotient generators; their commutator is $z^n$ and is unchanged by central changes of lifts. Every element has unique normal form $z^my^bx^a$, proving this invariant is complete. A cocycle representative is $c((a,b),(c,d))=nad$, whose cocycle identity follows from bilinearity. Its extension multiplication is $(a,b,m)(c,d,l)=(a+c,b+d,m+l+nad)$. The zero parameter gives $\mathbb Z^3$ and parameter one gives the integer Heisenberg group. The [Koszul resolution for a rank-two free abelian group](koszul-resolution-for-a-rank-two-free-abelian-group.md) independently computes $H^2(\mathbb Z^2,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$.
