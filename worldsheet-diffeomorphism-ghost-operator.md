# Worldsheet diffeomorphism ghost operator

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet ghost action](worldsheet-ghost-action.md)

The [trace](matrix-trace.md)-free metric variation is $(P_1c)_{ab}=\nabla_ac_b+\nabla_bc_a-\gamma_{ab}\nabla_dc^d$. A symmetric [trace](matrix-trace.md)-free antighost exponentiates the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md). In light-cone worldsheet coordinates its two chiral kinetic terms are $b_{++}\partial_-c^+$ and $b_{--}\partial_+c^-$.
