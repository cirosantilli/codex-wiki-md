# Conserved deviation energy in constant curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md)

In [constant sectional curvature](constant-sectional-curvature.md) $\kappa$, a [deviation vector](deviation-vector.md) orthogonal to a unit [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) obeys $D^2S=\kappa S$, where $D=\nabla_T$. Therefore $K=g(DS,DS)-\kappa g(S,S)$ is constant, since its derivative is $2g(DS,D^2S)-2\kappa g(S,DS)=0$. Its squared length $f=g(S,S)$ satisfies $\ddot f=2K+4\kappa f$. In four spacetime dimensions, $\kappa=R/12$.
