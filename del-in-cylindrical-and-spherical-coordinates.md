# Del in cylindrical and spherical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Del_in_cylindrical_and_spherical_coordinates)

This collects the [gradient](gradient.md), [divergence](divergence.md), [curl](curl.md) and [Laplacian](laplacian.md) formulas in cylindrical and [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md). Coordinate [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) vary with position, so taking their derivatives is essential when translating [vector calculus](vector-calculus.md) operators from Cartesian coordinates. [Curl in spherical coordinates](curl-in-spherical-coordinates.md) is one of these formulas.
