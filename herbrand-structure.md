# Herbrand structure

↑ **Parent:** [Herbrand universe](herbrand-universe.md)

A [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) whose domain is the [Herbrand universe](herbrand-universe.md), with constants denoting themselves and function symbols acting by syntactic term formation. Its predicates are specified by assigning [truth values](truth-value.md) to ground atoms. With [first-order equality](logical-equality.md), ordinary equality of syntactic terms imposes additional restrictions; more general term models may require quotienting by a congruence.

Equivalently, the predicate meanings of a [Herbrand interpretation](herbrand-structure.md) correspond to a subset of its [Herbrand base](herbrand-base.md).
