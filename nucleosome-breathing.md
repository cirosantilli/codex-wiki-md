# Nucleosome breathing

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleosome](nucleosome.md)

Transient local unwrapping and rewrapping of nucleosomal [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). This changes the accessibility of DNA and histone surfaces without requiring complete and irreversible loss of the [histone octamer](histone-octamer.md).
