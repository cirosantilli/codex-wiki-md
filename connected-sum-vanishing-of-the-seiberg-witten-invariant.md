<h1 id="connected-sum-vanishing-of-the-seiberg-witten-invariant">Connected-sum vanishing of the Seiberg–Witten invariant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Seiberg–Witten invariant of a four-manifold](seiberg-witten-invariant-of-a-four-manifold.md)

If a closed oriented [smooth four-manifold](smooth-four-manifold.md) splits as a [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) $X_1\#X_2$ with both $b_2^+(X_i)>0$, its ordinary [Seiberg–Witten invariants of a four-manifold](seiberg-witten-invariant-of-a-four-manifold.md) vanish. Combined with [Taubes nonvanishing theorem](taubes-nonvanishing-theorem.md), this obstructs a symplectic structure in that orientation.
