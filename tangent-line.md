# Tangent line

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)

The [tangent line](tangent-line.md) to a differentiable [function](function-split.md) at $a$ is its affine first-order approximation $f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$. For a differentiable [concave function](concave-function.md), it is an upper bound throughout a convex interval: the secant-slope inequality and passage to the [derivative](derivative.md) give $f(x)\leq f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$. This upper bound constructs valid envelopes in [adaptive rejection sampling](adaptive-rejection-sampling.md).
