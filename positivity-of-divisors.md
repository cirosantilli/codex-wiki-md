# Positivity of divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

The positivity of a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) can be measured by sections, [intersection numbers](intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md), or its [numerical class](real-numerical-divisor-classes.md). [Ample divisors](ample-cartier-divisor.md) give projective embeddings after taking a multiple; [nef divisors](nef-line-bundle.md) are their numerical limits; [big divisors](big-divisor.md) have the maximum possible order of section growth. Nefness and bigness are different conditions.
