# Magnetic-energy lower bound from helicity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md)

In a bounded regular domain, fix the global extension or circulation convention for the [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md). A bounded inverse of [curl](curl.md) on tangent [solenoidal vector fields](solenoidal-vector-field.md) gives $\|\mathbf A\|_2\leq C_D\|\mathbf B\|_2$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) then gives $|H_M|\leq C_D\|\mathbf B\|_2^2=2\mu_0 C_DE_B$. One construction extends the tangent field by zero outside the domain and uses the whole-space [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md); its kernel of size $|\mathbf x-\mathbf y|^{-2}$ is integrable over a bounded region, giving the required $L^2$ bound. Nonzero conserved [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) therefore prevents complete decay of [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md).
