# Primary beam

↑ **Parent:** [Complex visibility](complex-visibility.md)

The primary beam is the individual [antenna](antenna-radio.md) pair's directional sensitivity envelope in the [complex visibility](complex-visibility.md) equation. For identical, identically pointed elements it is the individual element's power pattern. Its angular width sets the useful field of view; the much longer [interferometric baselines](interferometric-baseline.md) set finer measured scales. The [primary beam](primary-beam.md) and the [dirty beam](dirty-beam.md) describe different responses.
