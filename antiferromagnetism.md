# Antiferromagnetism

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiferromagnetism)

[Antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md) is ordered compensation of [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). In the collinear two-sublattice model, $\mathbf M_A=-\mathbf M_B\ne0$: net [magnetization](magnetization.md) vanishes but [staggered magnetization](staggered-magnetization.md) remains. [Louis Néel](louis-neel.md) developed this possibility.[§5, pp.324–326: opposing ordered sublattices](magnetism-and-the-local-molecular-field-by-louis-neel.md)

For an original local [energy](energy-split.md) check, perturb the antiparallel pair in [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) by writing $\theta=\pi-\delta$. Then

$$
E=-JS^2\cos\delta=-JS^2+\frac12JS^2\delta^2+O(\delta^4).
$$

For $J>0$, a small relative rotation costs positive [energy](energy-split.md). This verifies a pair's preference; it does not prove a bulk ordering [temperature](temperature-split.md). A whole [crystal](crystal.md) must satisfy many bonds simultaneously, and [thermal fluctuations](thermal-fluctuation.md) and quantum effects matter. The [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md) and [antiferromagnetic Ising model](antiferromagnetic-ising-model.md) specify different assumptions about those collective degrees of freedom.

Why is the distinction useful? A measurement of zero [magnetization](magnetization.md) cannot decide between an ordered alternating arrangement, a disordered ensemble, and oppositely magnetized [magnetic domains](magnetic-domain.md). A spatial probe such as [magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md) tests the pattern directly. The [manganese monoxide neutron diffraction experiment](manganese-monoxide-neutron-diffraction-experiment.md) is a particularly clear example: the order adds a new periodic scattering signal even though the net [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) cancels.
