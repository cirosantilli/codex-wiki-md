# Positive unfolding of a cusp-form square

↑ **Parent:** [Rankin–Selberg integral for holomorphic cusp forms](rankin-selberg-integral-for-holomorphic-cusp-forms.md)

For real $w>1$, the finite [Rankin–Selberg integral](rankin-selberg-integral-for-holomorphic-cusp-forms.md) $I(f,f,w)$ has a nonnegative integrand. [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) unfolds it without an a priori bound on the [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md). Integration over $x$ gives $\int_0^1|f(x+iy)|^2dx=\sum_{n\geq1}|a_n|^2e^{-4\pi ny}$. A second application of [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) gives the finite sum in the displayed identity with $s=w+k-1$. Positivity, rather than analytic continuation alone, proves absolute convergence.
