# Time reversal of a continuous-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

For an equilibrium chain with strictly positive stationary probabilities, reversed jump rates are $q^*_{ij}=\pi_jq_{ji}/\pi_i$. Under [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) they equal the original rates, making the stationary path law reversible. Holding times and jump directions reverse together; this is more than symmetry of one-time distributions.
