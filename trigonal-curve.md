# Trigonal curve

↑ **Parent:** [Gonality](gonality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonal_curve)

A [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) of gonality three has a basepoint-free pencil of degree three. For a nonhyperelliptic curve, a fibre of this pencil spans a line in its [canonical map](canonical-map.md): [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $h^0(K_C-D)=g-2$. The union of these lines is a scroll of degree $g-2$ in $\mathbb P^{g-1}$. Its smooth ruled model may map to a cone in low genus.
