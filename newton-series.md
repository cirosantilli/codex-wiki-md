# Newton series

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference](finite-difference-split.md)

A Newton series expands a function in the basis of [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient-split.md): $f(x)=\sum_{k\ge0}\binom{x}{k}\Delta^k f(0)$. For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), the sum terminates.
