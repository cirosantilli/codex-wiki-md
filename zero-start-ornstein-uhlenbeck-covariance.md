# Zero-start Ornstein-Uhlenbeck covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Explicit Ornstein-Uhlenbeck solution](explicit-ornstein-uhlenbeck-solution.md)

For $d\sigma=-\sigma\,dt/\tau+\sqrt\kappa\,dW$ with $\sigma_0=0$, the explicit [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) is $\sigma_t=\sqrt\kappa\int_0^te^{-(t-u)/\tau}dW_u$. The [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) on the overlap of the two integration intervals gives the displayed [covariance](covariance.md). The second exponential records the zero initial condition; removing it gives the stationary [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) covariance.
