# Force-free cylinder in confined Couette flow

↑ **Parent:** [Torque-free cylinder in a lubrication gap](torque-free-cylinder-in-a-lubrication-gap.md)

A nearly confined cylinder in [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) is driven by the unequal lubrication resistances of its two gaps. If their minimum widths are proportional to $1+\lambda$ and $1-\lambda$, force balance weights the relative wall velocities by the inverse square roots of those widths.
