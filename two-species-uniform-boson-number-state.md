# Two-species uniform boson number state

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

Two independent [boson](boson.md) species occupy uniform single-particle modes on $L$ sites, with fixed [occupation numbers](occupation-number.md) $n_A,n_B$. This differs from a [fixed-number two-mode condensate](fixed-number-two-mode-condensate.md) whose particles all occupy one superposition of the two modes. Here $\langle a_i^\dagger a_j\rangle=n_A/L$, $\langle b_i^\dagger b_j\rangle=n_B/L$, and the local total [number operator](number-operator.md) has [variance](variance-split.md) $(n_A+n_B)L^{-1}(1-L^{-1})$. The latter follows from two independent [binomial distributions](binomial-distribution.md) with success probability $1/L$. This state satisfies a prescribed [Schwinger boson constraint](local-spin-constraint-in-the-schwinger-boson-representation.md) only in the mean unless the local [variance](variance-split.md) vanishes.
