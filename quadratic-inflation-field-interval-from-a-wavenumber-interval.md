# Quadratic-inflation field interval from a wavenumber interval

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution](quadratic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

For a quadratic [inflaton](inflaton.md) potential ending at the leading estimate $\epsilon_V=1$, $\Phi/M_{\rm Pl}=\sqrt{4N+2}$. [Cosmological horizon exit](cosmological-horizon-exit.md) gives $d\log k/dN=-1+\epsilon_H$. A narrow logarithmic interval therefore probes a field span $2\Delta\log k/(\sqrt{4N+2}(1-\epsilon_H))$, to the accuracy of the [slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md). At sixty e-folds, five logarithmic wavenumber units probe about $0.64M_{\rm Pl}$ around a background field near $15.6M_{\rm Pl}$.
