# Absence of a local gravitational stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md)

The [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md) allows [Riemann normal coordinates](normal-coordinates.md) in which the first derivatives of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) vanish at a chosen point. A purported local gravitational [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) built from those first derivatives would vanish there in that chart and therefore, if tensorial, in all charts. Such an expression cannot encode the gravitational energy assigned by a coordinate-dependent field description. This rules out an ordinary universal local covariant gravitational energy density, not the construction of local curvature tensors or boundary and averaged notions of gravitational energy. The [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) still describes invariant tidal gravity.
