# Lefschetz-Dolbeault curvature commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Bundle-valued Kähler identities](bundle-valued-kahler-identities.md)

For a Hermitian holomorphic bundle over a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), let $\Theta$ act by exterior multiplication. The [bundle-valued Kähler identities](bundle-valued-kahler-identities.md) give $[\Lambda,\Delta_{\bar\partial}]=-i(\Theta\wedge-)^*$, and taking adjoints yields $[\Delta_{\bar\partial},L]=i\Theta\wedge-$. This measures the failure of the bundle-valued [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) to commute with the Lefschetz operator.
