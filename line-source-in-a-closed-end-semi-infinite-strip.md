# Line source in a closed-end semi-infinite strip

↑ **Parent:** [Line source](line-source.md)

In the impermeable strip $x>0$, $0<y<a$, a [line source](line-source.md) of strength $Q$ at an interior point $c$ has the displayed [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md), with locally consistent [complex logarithm](complex-logarithm.md) and $f(z)=\cosh(\pi z/a)$. The [hyperbolic-cosine map of a half-strip](hyperbolic-cosine-map-of-a-half-strip.md) sends the walls to the [real axis](real-axis.md). An equal image [line source](line-source.md) at $\overline{f(c)}$ makes that axis a [streamline](streamline.md). Since $f'(c)\ne0$, the local singular part remains $Q\log(z-c)/(2\pi)$, proving preservation of strength. With no extra imposed flow, the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) tends to $Q/a$ as $x\to\infty$, so the outgoing [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is $Q$. The source position $c$ has positive imaginary part; assuming it real would place it on a wall.
