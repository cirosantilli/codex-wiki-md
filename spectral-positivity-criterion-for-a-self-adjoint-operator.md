# Spectral positivity criterion for a self-adjoint operator

↑ **Parent:** [Unbounded self-adjoint operator](unbounded-self-adjoint-operator.md)

[Positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) makes $A-z$ bounded below with dense closed range for every $z<0$, excluding negative spectral values. Conversely if the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is nonnegative, $R=(I+A)^{-1}$ has [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) in $[0,1]$ by the [resolvent spectral mapping identity](resolvent-spectral-mapping-identity.md). The [numerical-range endpoint approximate-eigenvalue theorem](numerical-range-endpoint-approximate-eigenvalue-theorem.md) gives $0\leq R\leq I$, and positive-form [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\|Rf\|^2\leq\langle Rf,f\rangle$. For $u=Rf$, $\langle Au,u\rangle=\langle Rf,f\rangle-\|Rf\|^2\geq0$.
