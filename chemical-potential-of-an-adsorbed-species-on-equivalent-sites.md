# Chemical potential of an adsorbed species on equivalent sites

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption-site grand partition function](adsorption-site-grand-partition-function.md)

The occupied-to-vacant probability ratio of the [adsorption-site grand partition function](adsorption-site-grand-partition-function.md) is $\theta/(1-\theta)=z_s e^{\beta(\mu_s+\epsilon)}$. Taking its [logarithm](logarithm.md) yields the displayed [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). [binding energy](binding-energy.md) and vibrational [energies](energy-split.md) must share a consistent reference so that [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) is not counted twice.
