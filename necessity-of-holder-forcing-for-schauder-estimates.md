<h1 id="necessity-of-holder-forcing-for-schauder-estimates">Necessity of Hölder forcing for Schauder estimates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Interior Schauder estimate](interior-schauder-estimate.md)

The bounded-forcing norm alone cannot control the [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md) of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). On a unit [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) take $u_m=m^{-2}\sin(mx_1)$ and $f_m=\Delta u_m=-\sin(mx_1)$. The [derivative supremum norm](derivative-supremum-norm.md) $|u_m|_2$ and $\|f_m\|_\infty$ stay bounded, but the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) difference between zero and $\pi/(2m)e_1$ gives $[D^2u_m]_\alpha\geq(2m/\pi)^\alpha$ on $B_{1/2}$.
