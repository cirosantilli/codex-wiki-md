# Real de Rham intersection form in dimension four

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

On a compact oriented [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $N$ of dimension four without boundary, set $Q([\alpha],[\beta])=\int_N\alpha\wedge\beta$ for closed real two-forms. The [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) makes the expression independent of representatives, and the even degrees make it symmetric. It is the real [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) version of the [intersection form](intersection-form.md). The [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) and [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) express it as $Q(h,k)=(h,*k)_{L^2}$ on harmonic representatives.
