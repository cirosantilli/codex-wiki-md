# Parallel postulate

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_postulate)

In the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md), precisely one line through a point outside a given line is parallel to it. This formulation is equivalent to Euclid's fifth postulate within the usual axioms of plane geometry.
