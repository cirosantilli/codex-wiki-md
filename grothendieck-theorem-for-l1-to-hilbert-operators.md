# Grothendieck theorem for L1 to Hilbert operators

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck inequality](grothendieck-inequality.md)

Every [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:L^1(\mu)\to H$ into a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is an [absolutely summing operator](absolutely-summing-operator.md), with $\pi_1(T)\le K_G\|T\|$. For simple functions on a common disjoint partition, apply the [Grothendieck inequality](grothendieck-inequality.md) to the vectors $T(1_A/\mu(A))$ and norming vectors of the images. The scalar sign or phase supremum is the weak 1-norm of the family. Approximation by simple functions proves the general result.
