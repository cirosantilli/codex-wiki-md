# Doublet-triplet splitting

↑ **Parent:** [SU(5) grand unified theory](su-5-grand-unified-theory.md)

In a minimal SU(5) scalar multiplet, the weak doublet and a colored triplet share a unified representation. The doublet must remain near the electroweak scale while the triplet is much heavier, notably to suppress [proton decay](proton-decay.md). Arranging this splitting without an unprotected cancellation is a model-building problem distinct from merely embedding the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) gauge group.
