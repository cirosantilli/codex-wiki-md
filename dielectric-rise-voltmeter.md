# Dielectric-rise voltmeter

↑ **Parent:** [Partially filled coaxial capacitor](partially-filled-coaxial-capacitor.md)

At fixed applied [voltage](voltage.md), the mechanical potential of a [capacitor](capacitor.md) and its source includes $-CV^2/2$. Increasing the liquid overlap of a [partially filled coaxial capacitor](partially-filled-coaxial-capacitor.md) therefore pulls the liquid upward. Balancing $V^2(dC/dh)/2$ against the excess [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) times the annular area gives the title formula for a wide external reservoir. Its differential sensitivity is $2\Delta h/V$; it vanishes at zero bias and measures the magnitude, rather than the sign, of [voltage](voltage.md).
