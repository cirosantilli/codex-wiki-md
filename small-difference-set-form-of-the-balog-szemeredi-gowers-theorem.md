<h1 id="small-difference-set-form-of-the-balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem">Small-difference-set form of the Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md)

If $E(A)\geq\theta|A|^3$, a subset $B\subseteq A$ satisfies $|B|\geq c_\theta|A|$ and $|B-B|\leq K_\theta|B|$. A [dependent random choice](dependent-random-choice.md) argument in the [popular sum](popular-sum.md) graph finds many four-edge paths representing each member of the [difference set](difference-set.md). The [Petridis minimal-growth lemma](petridis-minimal-growth-lemma.md) then bounds all higher [iterated sumsets](iterated-sumset.md) and [difference sets](difference-set.md).
