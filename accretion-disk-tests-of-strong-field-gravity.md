# Accretion-disk tests of strong-field gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

Radiating material close to a compact object probes relativistic orbital motion, [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) and light propagation. A thin disk's inferred inner edge can be compared with the predicted [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), while iron-line transfer and thermal-continuum fitting probe complementary aspects of the same geometry. Agreement tests a combined spacetime-and-disk model. A [black-hole spin](black-hole-spin.md) measurement made after imposing a [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) is not alone an independent verification of that [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md); geometry tests must vary gravitational predictions and astrophysical nuisance parameters together.
