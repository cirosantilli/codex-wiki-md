# Chromatographic mobile phase

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatography](chromatography-split.md)

The [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md) carries [solutes](solute.md) through the apparatus. In [liquid chromatography](liquid-chromatography.md) it is a liquid [solvent](solvent.md) or [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md).

The [eluent](eluent.md) is the supplied [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md). Its composition can alter both [solute](solute.md) [solubility](solubility.md) and competition for [adsorption](adsorption.md) sites, so changing the [eluent](eluent.md) can change retention even with the same [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md).
