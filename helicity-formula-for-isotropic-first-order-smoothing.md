# Helicity formula for isotropic first-order smoothing

↑ **Parent:** [First-order smoothing approximation](first-order-smoothing-approximation.md)

For a locally uniform mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), [first-order smoothing](first-order-smoothing-approximation.md) solves $\partial_t\mathbf b-\eta\Delta\mathbf b=(\overline{\mathbf B}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$. Inserting its causal diffusion propagator into the [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md) and taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the isotropic response tensor gives the displayed relation to [kinetic helicity](hydrodynamical-helicity.md). When diffusion during a [correlation time](correlation-time.md) is negligible, it reduces to $\alpha=-\tau_H\langle\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf u\rangle/3$. At small [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md), a quasistatic monochromatic velocity instead gives $\alpha=-\langle\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf u\rangle/(3\eta k_0^2)$. The effective [correlation time](correlation-time.md) and diffusion weighting should not be silently discarded.
