# Normality assumption

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)

A model assumption specifying a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) for the relevant random quantities, often errors conditional on predictors. In an [analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md), it concerns within-group errors; in a [paired t-test](paired-t-test.md), it concerns the differences. It does not generally require pooled outcomes from distinct groups to be normal.
