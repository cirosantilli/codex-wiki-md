# Evanescent amplitude versus transmitted current

↑ **Parent:** [Step potential](step-potential.md)

For a real positive decay coefficient alpha, a stationary exponential tail has zero [probability current](probability-current.md) because $\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\partial_x\psi)=0$. Matching a subthreshold [potential step](step-potential.md) can nevertheless give a nonzero coefficient T with $|T|^2>1$ relative to a unit incident plane-wave amplitude. This measures the density just beyond the step, not a flux-transmission probability. It describes penetration without a propagating outgoing channel in the semi-infinite high-potential region.
