# Relativistic kinetic theory

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

Relativistic kinetic theory describes particles by a scalar [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) on the future mass shell. Its [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md) uses geodesic streaming and a collision operator. Integrating its momentum moments supplies a [kinetic stress-energy tensor](kinetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) and particle-number currents. An [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) makes the local momentum integrals identical in form to those of [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md).
