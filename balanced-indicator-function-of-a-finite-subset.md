# Balanced indicator function of a finite subset

↑ **Parent:** [Density of a finite subset](density-of-a-finite-subset.md)

For nonempty [finite set](finite-set.md) $I$ and $A\subseteq I$, let $\alpha=|A|/|I|$ be the [density of a finite subset](density-of-a-finite-subset.md). The balanced [indicator function](indicator-function.md) $f=1_A-\alpha1_I$, extended by zero to an ambient finite [group](group-split.md), has sum zero and vanishing zero [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md). The sum of $f$ on a subset $P\subseteq I$ is $|A\cap P|-\alpha|P|$, measuring the excess [density of a finite subset](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) there.
