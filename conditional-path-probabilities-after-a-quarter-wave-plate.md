# Conditional path probabilities after a quarter-wave plate

↑ **Parent:** [Waveplate](waveplate.md)

A detected lower-path [photon](photon.md) with vertical [polarization](polarization-waves.md) evolves under an angle-dependent [Jones matrix](jones-matrix.md). For fast-axis angle $\eta$, its horizontal [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is $(1-i)\sin\eta\cos\eta$ and its vertical [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is $\sin^2\eta+i\cos^2\eta$. A later vertical-path [probability](probability.md) therefore depends on the plate orientation and on whether the earlier lower-path event has been conditioned on.
