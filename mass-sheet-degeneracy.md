# Mass-sheet degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md)

For a single source plane, adding a uniform sheet and rescaling the original projected lens mass as shown leaves the reduced shear $g=\gamma/(1-\kappa)$ unchanged. Rescaling source coordinates by $\lambda$ also preserves the image positions and shapes. Thus image geometry alone need not determine absolute lens mass normalization. Absolute magnification, independently known source sizes, time-delay information with a fixed distance scale, or additional geometric/dynamical constraints can break or restrict this [mass-sheet degeneracy](mass-sheet-degeneracy.md).
