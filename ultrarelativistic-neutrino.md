# Ultrarelativistic neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

A [neutrino](neutrino.md) with [energy](energy-split.md) much larger than its [mass](mass.md). At fixed [momentum](momentum-split.md), expanding the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md) gives $E_i\simeq p+m_i^2/(2p)$, so differences of propagation phases are governed by $\Delta m^2/(2E)$.
