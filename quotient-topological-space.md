# Quotient topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

A quotient topological space identifies points of a [topological space](topological-space.md) according to an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) and equips the set of equivalence classes with the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md). A subset is open exactly when its inverse image under the canonical [quotient map](quotient-map.md) is open. Even a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) can have a non-Hausdorff quotient; identifying real numbers differing by a rational number gives a multi-point quotient with the [indiscrete topology](indiscrete-topology.md).
