# Gradient maximum principle for a minimal graph

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal surface equation for a graph](minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md)

For a $C^2$ solution $u$ of the [minimal surface equation for a graph](minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md) on the closure of a bounded domain,

$$
\sup_\Omega|Du|=\sup_{\partial\Omega}|Du|.
$$

Write $a^{ij}(Du)u_{ij}=0$ with $a^{ij}(p)=\delta_{ij}-p_ip_j/(1+|p|^2)$. Its differentiated operator $\mathcal L=a^{ij}D_{ij}+b^\ell D_\ell$, where $b^\ell=(\partial a^{ij}/\partial p_\ell)u_{ij}$, satisfies

$$
\mathcal L|Du|^2=2\sum_k a^{ij}u_{ki}u_{kj}\geq0.
$$

The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) gives the result. Interior [Schauder estimates](schauder-estimates.md) justify differentiating a solution initially assumed only $C^2$.
