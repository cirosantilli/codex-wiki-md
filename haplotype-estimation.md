# Haplotype estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Haplotype](haplotype.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplotype_estimation)

[Haplotype estimation](haplotype-estimation.md) reconstructs which [alleles](allele.md) share a [chromosome](chromosome.md) from observed [genotypes](genotype.md) and other information, such as [pedigrees](pedigree.md). A doubly [heterozygous](heterozygosity.md) [genotype](genotype.md) can be $AB/ab$ or $Ab/aB$; a [phase-known genotype](phase-known-genotype.md) distinguishes them. Uncertainty in that reconstruction must propagate to estimated [haplotype frequencies](haplotype-frequency.md) and [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md).
