# Arithmetical comprehension theory

↑ **Parent:** [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md)

This subsystem of second-order arithmetic allows comprehension for arithmetical formulas and induction in its standard restricted presentation. Its first-order arithmetical consequences are those of [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md). It is a useful base for comparing [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md) principles with [ordinal](ordinal.md) notation systems without assuming their global well-foundedness.
