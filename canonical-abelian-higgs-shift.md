# Canonical Abelian Higgs shift

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical field normalization](canonical-field-normalization.md)

For $\phi=\kappa(v+h+ib)$, the real fluctuation [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md) are $-\kappa^2[(\partial h)^2+(\partial b)^2]$. The same $v$ labels a [Minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) of $V=\lambda(|\phi|^2-v^2/2)^2/4$ and gives [canonically normalized](canonical-field-normalization.md) real fields exactly when $\kappa^2=1/2$. This condition also makes $G=\partial\cdot A-\xi gvb$ cancel the gauge-scalar derivative mixing: its coefficient is $(2\kappa^2-1)gvA\cdot\partial b$. Changing the [normalization](normalization.md) of the fields without changing the [gauge function](gauge-fixing.md) need not be a conventional diagonal $R_\xi$ gauge.
