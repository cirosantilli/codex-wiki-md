# Cofinality of a continuous cardinal hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Cofinality](cofinality.md)

At a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) $\delta$, a strictly increasing continuous sequence of [cardinals](cardinal-number.md) $(\kappa_\alpha)_{\alpha\le\delta}$ satisfies $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa_\delta)=\operatorname{cf}(\delta)$. A cofinal sequence of indices gives the upper bound. Conversely, bounds for a cofinal [set](set-split.md) of values must have cofinal indices, giving the lower bound. In particular the assertion applies to [Beth numbers](beth-number.md).
