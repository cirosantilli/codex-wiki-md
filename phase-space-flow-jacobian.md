# Phase-space flow Jacobian

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md)

The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) of a differentiable [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) measures its change of volume. The variational equation and the [Liouville formula for a fundamental matrix](liouville-formula-for-a-fundamental-matrix.md) give the displayed formula for a complete smooth field $b$. For kinetic advection with $b=(v,F)$, its [divergence](divergence.md) is $\nabla_v\cdot F$. A zero [divergence](divergence.md) preserves phase-space [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md).
