# Random obstruction to larger clique blow-ups

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic Erdős-Stone theorem](logarithmic-erdos-stone-theorem.md)

In a [binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md) with fixed [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) probability below one, the displayed first-moment bound tends to zero for $t=C\log n$ and sufficiently large fixed $C$. At the same time the [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) density concentrates near $p$. Thus a positive fixed density cannot force balanced complete multipartite subgraphs whose part size grows faster than logarithmically.
