# Homozygote

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype](genotype.md)

A homozygote carries two identical [alleles](allele.md) at a locus; a [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) carries different alleles. Homozygosity of an observed [haplotype](haplotype.md) is identity by state, and need not be [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md).
