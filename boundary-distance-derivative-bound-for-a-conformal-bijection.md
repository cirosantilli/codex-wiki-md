# Boundary-distance derivative bound for a conformal bijection

↑ **Parent:** [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md)

For a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) $f:D\to\widetilde D$ between proper planar [domains](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), put $d=\operatorname{dist}(z,\partial D)$ and $\widetilde d=\operatorname{dist}(f(z),\partial\widetilde D)$. Apply the [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md) to $(f(z+dw)-f(z))/(df'(z))$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) to obtain $d|f'(z)|/4\leq\widetilde d$. Apply it again to $f^{-1}$ to get the opposite bound. Thus $\boxed{\widetilde d/(4d)\leq|f'(z)|\leq4\widetilde d/d}$. The [domains](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) need not be [simply connected domains](simply-connected-domain.md). For the whole plane, the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) distances are infinite and these ratios are undefined.
