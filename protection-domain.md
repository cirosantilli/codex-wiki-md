# Protection domain

↑ **Parent:** [Operating system](operating-system-split.md)

A boundary within which code exercises a particular set of permissions. An [address space](address-space.md) describes accessible [virtual addresses](virtual-address.md); a [protection domain](protection-domain.md) also concerns authority over other objects, such as [software threads](thread-computing.md) and [IPC endpoints](ipc-endpoint.md). Two [address spaces](address-space.md) may deliberately share a writable [memory page](memory-page.md), so separation of [address spaces](address-space.md) alone does not make their contents independent.
