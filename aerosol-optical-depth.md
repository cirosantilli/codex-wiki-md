# Aerosol optical depth

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

The dimensionless column [optical depth](optical-depth.md) from [aerosol](aerosol.md) extinction measures attenuation by absorption and scattering at a stated wavelength. It depends on particle size, composition, humidity and vertical distribution; it is not a direct count of [cloud condensation nuclei](cloud-condensation-nucleus.md). Using it as a proxy for droplet-number forcing requires control of meteorological covariates and cloud-contaminated retrievals.
