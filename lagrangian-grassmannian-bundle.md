# Lagrangian Grassmannian bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian Grassmannian](lagrangian-grassmannian.md)

For a rank-$2n$ symplectic [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E\to B$, the Lagrangian Grassmannian bundle has fiber $\operatorname{LGr}(E_b)$ over $b$. A symplectic trivialization of $E$ induces a trivialization $\mathcal LGr(E)\cong B\times\operatorname{LGr}(\mathbb R^{2n})$.
