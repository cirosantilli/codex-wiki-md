# Dirichlet Helmholtz Green function on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For $\partial_x^2+k^2$ on $(0,\ell)$ with zero endpoint values and $\sin(k\ell)\ne0$, let $x_<=\min(x,\xi)$ and $x_>=\max(x,\xi)$. The displayed [Green function](green-s-function.md) is continuous at $x=\xi$ and its first derivative has jump one, giving unit [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) forcing. The formula follows from the left solution $\sin(kx)$ and right solution $\sin(k(\ell-x))$, whose [Wronskian](wronskian.md) is $-k\sin(k\ell)$. At $k=0$ its limit is $-x_<(\ell-x_>)/\ell$, although zero is excluded by a condition that $k$ is not an integer in the length-$\pi$ version.
