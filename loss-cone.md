# Loss cone

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A loss cone consists of low-[angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) stellar trajectories whose next pericentre enters a central absorbing region. For capture radius $r_c$ in a [black hole](black-hole.md)-dominated potential, the Newtonian near-parabolic estimate is $J_{\rm lc}^2\simeq2GM_Hr_c$. At radius $r$ and speed $v$, its angular half-width is $J_{\rm lc}/(rv)$. [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md) can refill this depleted part of phase space.
