# Mazur-Wiles theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md)

The Mazur-Wiles theorem proves the classical [Iwasawa main conjecture](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md) for abelian extensions of $\mathbb Q$. Its construction of class fields uses Galois representations associated with [modular forms](modular-form.md), congruences with Eisenstein series and the [Eisenstein ideal](eisenstein-ideal.md).
