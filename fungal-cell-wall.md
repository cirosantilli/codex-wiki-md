# Fungal cell wall

↑ **Parent:** [Cell wall](cell-wall.md)

A [fungal cell wall](fungal-cell-wall.md) contains structural [polysaccharides](polysaccharide.md) including [chitin](chitin.md) and [glucans](glucan.md). Its composition and organization vary among [fungi](fungus.md); the diagram illustrates a composite rather than a pure [chitin](chitin.md) shell. A [fungus](fungus.md) sharing a [cell wall](cell-wall.md) with a [plant](plant.md) does not imply shared wall chemistry.[Biology 2e, §24.1, Cell Structure and Function: structural wall polysaccharides](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/24-1-characteristics-of-fungi.md)
