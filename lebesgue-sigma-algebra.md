# Lebesgue sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md)

The [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) of all [Lebesgue measurable sets](lebesgue-measurable-set.md), equivalently the [completion of a measure](completion-of-a-measure.md) of the [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md) under [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). It contains every [subset](subset.md) of every [Lebesgue null set](lebesgue-null-set.md). Applying [Carathéodory's criterion](caratheodory-s-criterion.md) to [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md) gives another construction of this same sigma-algebra.
