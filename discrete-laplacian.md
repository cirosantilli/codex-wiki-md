# Discrete Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference](finite-difference-split.md)

A finite-dimensional approximation of the [Laplacian](laplacian.md). On a periodic one-dimensional grid the centered operator has Fourier eigenvalue $-4\sin^2(\theta/2)/h^2$ and a constant nullspace.
