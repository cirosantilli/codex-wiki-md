# Single bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A bond with formal [bond order](bond-order.md) one, conventionally represented by one line. Delocalization can make the actual [bond order](bond-order.md) differ from the integer in one Lewis structure.
