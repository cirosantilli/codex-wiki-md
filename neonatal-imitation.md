# Neonatal imitation

↑ **Parent:** [Imitation](imitation.md)

Model-specific action matching during the neonatal period. Early positive reports and subsequent disputed [replication](scientific-replication.md) and reanalysis make this a useful case for distinguishing whether an action occurs, whether it is selectively elicited by its matching model, and whether it supports a broad theory of [imitation](imitation.md).[Science 198, 75–78; modelled facial/manual actions and coded infant responses](imitation-of-facial-and-manual-gestures-by-human-neonates-by-andrew-n-meltzoff-and-m-keith-moore.md)
