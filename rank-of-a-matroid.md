# Rank of a matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

For a [subset](subset.md) $A$ of the [ground set](ground-set.md), the [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) on $A$ is the maximum [cardinality](cardinality.md) of a [matroid independent set](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) contained in $A$:

$$
r_M(A)=\max\{\,|I|: I\subseteq A,\ I\in\mathcal I\,\}.
$$

Thus $r_M(E)$ is the common size of the [matroid bases](basis-of-a-matroid.md), and $r_M(\varnothing)=0$. The [weighted three-vector matroid](weighted-three-vector-matroid.md) has $r_M(E)=2$, even though its [ground set](ground-set.md) has three labels.
