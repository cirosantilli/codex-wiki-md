# K combinator

↑ **Parent:** [Combinator](combinator.md)

In [lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md), the closed [lambda term](lambda-term.md) $K\equiv\lambda xy.x$ discards its second argument. In [combinatory logic](combinatory-logic.md), its primitive constant satisfies $KAB=A$. The corresponding [principal type](principal-type.md) in the [simply typed lambda calculus](simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md) is $\alpha\to(\beta\to\alpha)$.
