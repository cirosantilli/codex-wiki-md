<h1 id="adm-momentum-constraint-for-a-p-x-phi-scalar-field">ADM momentum constraint for a P(X, phi) scalar field</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [P(X, phi) scalar field theory](p-x-phi-scalar-field-theory.md)

Varying the shift in the [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md) gives a nondynamical momentum constraint. For a $P(X,\phi)$ field it equates the spatial divergence of $K_i{}^j-\delta_i^jK$ to the scalar momentum density.
