# Full conditional distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md)

A full conditional distribution is the conditional law of one component of a joint model given all the other components and the observed data. A [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md) updates components by drawing from these laws.
