# Double divergence of a contravariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

For a [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), a contravariant rank-two [tensor](tensor.md) satisfies $T^{ab}{}_{;ab}=T^{ab}{}_{;ba}$ without itself being symmetric. The [Ricci identity](curvature-commutator-on-a-covariant-tensor.md) gives their difference as $R_{pb}(T^{pb}-T^{bp})$, which vanishes because the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) is symmetric. Zero torsion alone for a general affine connection does not ensure this symmetry.
