# Orthogonal centered quadratic regression design

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

At symmetric design points, the columns $1$, $x$ and $x^2-\overline{x^2}$ are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). Their squared lengths give the diagonal entries of the normal-equation [matrix](matrix.md) and hence separate least-squares coefficient variances. At the seven [integer](integer-split.md) points from minus three to three, these squared lengths are $7,28,84$.
