# Negative part of a Hermitian operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-negative decomposition of a Hermitian operator](positive-negative-decomposition-of-a-hermitian-operator.md)

The negative part of a [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) is $X_-=\sum_i\max(-\lambda_i,0)P_i=(|X|-X)/2$. In this convention $X_-$ is itself a [positive operator](positive-operator.md), and $X=X_+-X_-$. Its support is orthogonal to that of the [positive part of a Hermitian operator](positive-part-of-a-hermitian-operator.md).
