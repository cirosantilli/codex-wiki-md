# Compact convergence of locally bounded harmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

A locally uniformly bounded sequence of [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) with a pointwise limit converges uniformly on compact subsets, and its limit is harmonic. A radial [mollifier](mollifier.md) of fixed local radius reproduces each harmonic function by the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md). Differentiating that convolution gives uniform interior gradient bounds, hence equicontinuity. A finite covering of a compact set converts pointwise convergence to uniform convergence. Passing to the limit in the radial convolution identity proves smoothness; passing to the limit against the Laplacian of each [test function](test-function.md) proves harmonicity.
