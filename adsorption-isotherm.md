# Adsorption isotherm

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)

An [adsorption isotherm](adsorption-isotherm.md) relates equilibrium uptake to surrounding [pressure](pressure-split.md) or [concentration](concentration.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md). For a [gas](gas.md), specify the [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) of the adsorbing [chemical species](chemical-species.md). An uptake trace during incomplete equilibration need not be an [adsorption isotherm](adsorption-isotherm.md).
