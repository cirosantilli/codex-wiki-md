# Connectedness of adjacent-level incidence in a Boolean lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Local LYM inequality](local-lym-inequality.md)

The bipartite inclusion [graph](graph-split.md) between consecutive nonempty ranks of the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) is connected. Two rank-$r$ sets differing by one exchanged element share their rank-$(r+1)$ union as a neighbour; successive exchanges connect any two rank-$r$ sets. Every upper vertex has a lower neighbour. For the two middle ranks of an odd-dimensional [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) the [graph](graph-split.md) is regular, so equality in the [Local LYM inequality](local-lym-inequality.md) for a nonempty upper family forces the whole upper rank: equality admits no [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) from its [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) to the remaining upper [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
