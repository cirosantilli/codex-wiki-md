# Coordinate projection

↑ **Parent:** [Product of projective varieties](product-of-projective-varieties.md)

The coordinate projections from a product send $(x,y)$ to $x$ and $y$, respectively. For products of [projective varieties](projective-variety.md), they are [morphisms](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md).
