# Cohomology cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md)

For classes on $X$ and $Y$, apply the [induced maps on cohomology](induced-map-on-cohomology.md) of the coordinate projections and take their [cup product](cup-product.md) on $X\times Y$. The result has degree $|a|+|b|$. On a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of graded rings, the sign rule $(a\otimes b)(c\otimes d)=(-1)^{|b||c|}ac\otimes bd$ makes this a ring homomorphism.
