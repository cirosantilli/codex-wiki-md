# Principle theory

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md)

A physical theory organized around general empirical constraints on admissible processes, rather than a specified microscopic mechanism. [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md) used [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md) and [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) as examples. This classification concerns the explanatory strategy; a [principle theory](principle-theory.md) can constrain possible [constructive theories](constructive-theory.md).[1919 discussion of constructive theories and principle-theories](what-is-the-theory-of-relativity-by-albert-einstein.md)
