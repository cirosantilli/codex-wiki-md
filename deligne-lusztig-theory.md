# Deligne-Lusztig theory

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group of Lie type](finite-group-of-lie-type.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deligne–Lusztig_theory)

Deligne-Lusztig theory constructs [virtual characters](virtual-character.md) of [finite groups of Lie type](finite-group-of-lie-type.md) from compactly supported [étale cohomology](etale-cohomology.md) of varieties associated with Frobenius and flags. [Rational maximal tori](rational-maximal-torus.md) and their finite-group [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) provide induction data, while the geometry explains character orthogonality and cuspidality.
