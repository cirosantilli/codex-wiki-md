# Positive-definite matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Definite matrix](definite-matrix.md)

A real symmetric matrix $A$ is positive definite when

$$
x^TAx>0
$$

for every nonzero real [vector](vector.md) $x$. It therefore defines the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\langle x,y\rangle_A=x^TAy$ and is invertible.
