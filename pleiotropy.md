# Pleiotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

An effect of one [gene](gene.md) or [genetic variant](genetic-variant.md) on multiple distinguishable [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md). It can constrain evolution when effects oppose simultaneous improvement, or facilitate coordinated improvement when their directions align with [natural selection](natural-selection.md). Distinguish a single causal change from several linked changes.
