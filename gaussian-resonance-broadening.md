# Gaussian resonance broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear diffusion in action space](quasilinear-diffusion-in-action-space.md)

For a perturbation with amplitude envelope $e^{-(t-t_0)^2/(2\tau^2)}$, the squared [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) is proportional to $e^{-\omega^2\tau^2}$. Hence pulse-integrated transport weights orbital detuning with a normalized [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) of width $\Gamma=(\sqrt2\tau)^{-1}$. A longer pulse selects a narrower band of [orbital resonances](orbital-resonance.md). This spectral broadening does not by itself assume stochastic temporal forcing.
