# Spectral-line emission from an atmospheric thermal inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Inversion (meteorology)](inversion-meteorology.md)

A strong-opacity line forms higher than the nearby continuum. Under the Eddington-Barbier approximation, an outward-decreasing temperature therefore makes an absorption line, while an [atmospheric thermal inversion](inversion-meteorology.md) makes the line-forming layer hotter and reverses it into emission.
