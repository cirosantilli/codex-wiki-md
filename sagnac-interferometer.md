# Sagnac interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Sagnac effect](sagnac-effect.md)

A [Sagnac interferometer](sagnac-interferometer.md) uses a [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) to send [light](light.md) with [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md) around a common closed optical path in opposite directions, then recombines the beams to read their [phase difference](phase-difference.md). [Mirrors](mirror.md) or a guiding path define the loop; the source, [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md), loop and readout rotate together in the basic experiment. A small relative beam tilt makes spatial [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md) whose displacement reveals a change of [phase difference](phase-difference.md). At one fixed output, a [photodetector](photodetector.md) can instead record the corresponding change in [light intensity](light-intensity.md).
