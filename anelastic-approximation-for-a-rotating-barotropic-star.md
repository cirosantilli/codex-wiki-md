# Anelastic approximation for a rotating barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure equation for inertial oscillations of a barotropic star](pressure-equation-for-inertial-oscillations-of-a-barotropic-star.md)

For slow rotation and low-frequency inertial motion, neglect the time derivative of the Eulerian [mass density](density.md) perturbation relative to the mass-weighted [velocity](velocity.md) divergence. This filters acoustic compressibility while retaining background [mass density](density.md) variation. A spherical background [mass density](density.md) is often sufficient at leading order in rotation.
