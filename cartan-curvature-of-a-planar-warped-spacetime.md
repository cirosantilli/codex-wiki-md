# Cartan curvature of a planar warped spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Planar warped spacetime](planar-warped-spacetime.md)

For the [orthonormal coframe](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) of a [planar warped spacetime](planar-warped-spacetime.md), [Cartan's first structure equation](cartan-s-first-structure-equation.md) gives $\omega^i{}_3=a e^i$ and $\omega^3{}_i=-s_i a e^i$, where $i=0,1,2$ and $(s_0,s_1,s_2)=(-1,1,1)$. All other [connection 1-forms](connection-1-form-split.md) vanish. [Cartan's second structure equation](cartan-s-second-structure-equation.md) gives $\Theta^i{}_3=-q e^i\wedge e^3$ and $\Theta^i{}_j=-s_j a^2e^i\wedge e^j$ for $i\ne j$. Lowering the first index makes both the [connection 1-forms](connection-1-form-split.md) and the [curvature 2-forms](curvature-2-form.md) antisymmetric. Contraction yields $R_{00}=q+2a^2$, $R_{11}=R_{22}=-q-2a^2$, and $R_{33}=-3q$.
