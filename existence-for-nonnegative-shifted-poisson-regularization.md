# Existence for nonnegative shifted Poisson regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Shifted Poisson data fidelity](shifted-poisson-data-fidelity.md)

Let $\Omega$ be a bounded connected [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), $\alpha>0$, $g\ge0$ bounded, and $T:L^1\to L^1$ bounded and positivity preserving with $T1\ne0$. Minimize $\alpha\operatorname{TV}(u)+D_g(u)$ over nonnegative [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) functions. The logarithmic [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) bounds $\|Tu\|_1$ and variation on sublevels; [mean control for positive imaging operators](mean-control-for-positive-imaging-operators.md) then bounds the full $BV$ [norm](norm.md). [Bounded-variation compactness](bounded-variation-compactness.md) gives strong $L^1$ convergence, including nonnegativity. The scalar fidelity has bounded [derivative](derivative.md) on $s\ge0$, so [continuity](continuous-function.md) of $T$ makes its integral [continuous](continuous-function.md) in that [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). [Lower semicontinuity](lower-semicontinuity.md) of variation proves existence by the [direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md). If $g>0$ almost everywhere and $T$ is [injective](injective-function.md), strict [convexity](convex-function.md) of the fidelity gives uniqueness. For $0<g<1$, zero is the unique [minimizer](global-minimizer.md) even without injectivity, since zero energy forces both $Tu=0$ and a constant nonnegative $u$.
