# Singular consumption control

↑ **Parent:** [Consumption satisfaction stock](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md)

In [singular stochastic control](singular-stochastic-control.md), an unrestricted [consumption](consumption.md) rate can approximate instantaneous transfers from [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) into a [consumption satisfaction stock](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md). In the relaxed problem a nondecreasing control $C$ records these transfers. A transfer $\Delta C$ decreases [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) and increases [consumption satisfaction](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md) by the same amount. Rate controls need not attain the supremum when the relaxed optimum has a jump.
