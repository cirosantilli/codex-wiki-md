# Massive compact halo object

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

A compact astronomical object proposed as a constituent of a galactic [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md). It can be sought through [gravitational microlensing](gravitational-microlensing.md) even if its emitted light is undetectable. Stellar objects and remnants consume the cosmological [baryon](baryon.md) budget; primordial [black holes](black-hole-split.md) do not obey that particular formation-budget argument. Exclusion bounds depend on the object's [mass](mass.md), the halo model and survey selection.
