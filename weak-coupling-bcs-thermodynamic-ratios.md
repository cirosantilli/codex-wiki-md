# Weak-coupling BCS thermodynamic ratios

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The isotropic weak-coupling [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) predicts the gap ratio approximately $3.528$ and the electronic heat-capacity jump approximately $1.426$ in units of the normal-state [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) at the transition. These compare distinct measurements of the same paired state. They are conditional predictions of the weak-coupling fully gapped model, rather than universal numbers for all [superconductors](superconductor.md).
