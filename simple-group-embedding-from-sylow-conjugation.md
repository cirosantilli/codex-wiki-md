# Simple group embedding from Sylow conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugation action on Sylow subgroups](conjugation-action-on-sylow-subgroups.md)

A [finite nonabelian simple group](finite-nonabelian-simple-group.md) embeds in $A_{n_p}$, where $n_p$ is its number of [Sylow subgroups](sylow-subgroup.md) for a prime dividing its order. It is not a [p-group](p-group.md) by the nontrivial-centre property. Thus its Sylow subgroups are proper and cannot be normal, so $n_p>1$. The conjugation action is nontrivial, and its normal [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) must therefore be trivial. The sign of this faithful permutation action must be trivial, since a nontrivial sign homomorphism would inject the group into a group of order two. [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md) consequently gives $|G|\mid n_p!/2$.
