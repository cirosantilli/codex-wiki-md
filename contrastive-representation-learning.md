# Contrastive representation learning

↑ **Parent:** [Self-supervised learning](self-supervised-learning.md)

Learning a representation by relating selected matching examples or views and contrasting them with other examples. The augmentation, matching and sampling rules determine which distinctions are retained. Brain-like prediction in a trained model does not establish the exact objective or plasticity mechanism in cortex.[Abstract and Results; PNAS 118, e2014196118: contrastive learning, multi-area prediction and child-view [training data](training-data.md)](unsupervised-neural-network-models-of-the-ventral-visual-stream-by-chengxu-zhuang-and-others.md)
