<h1 id="yang-baxter-operator">Yang–Baxter operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

An invertible [morphism](morphism.md) $y:X\otimes X\to X\otimes X$ satisfying $(y\otimes1)(1\otimes y)(y\otimes1)=(1\otimes y)(y\otimes1)(1\otimes y)$ with coherent parentheses. It produces representations of the [braid groups](braid-group.md); a self-[braiding](braiding.md) is an example.
