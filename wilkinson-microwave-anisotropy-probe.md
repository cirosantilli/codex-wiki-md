# Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md)

A space mission that mapped the temperature and polarization of the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md). Its nine-year analysis separates foreground emission from the microwave sky signal and fits cosmological models to the resulting maps. Combined-data fits additionally use other observations; they are not measurements from microwave maps alone.[Version 1 abstract, maps, foreground separation and combined-data fits](nine-year-wilkinson-microwave-anisotropy-probe-wmap-observations-final-maps-and-results-by-c-l-bennett-and-others.md)
