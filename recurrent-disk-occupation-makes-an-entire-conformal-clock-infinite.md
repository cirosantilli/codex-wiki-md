# Recurrent disk occupation makes an entire conformal clock infinite

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

For a nonconstant [entire function](entire-function.md) choose a point with nonzero derivative and a small closed disk around it where $|f'|^2$ has a positive lower bound. [recurrence of planar Brownian motion](recurrence-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) gives infinitely many successive crossings from a concentric inner circle to the disk boundary. Their durations are positive iid variables by strong Markov and [rotational invariance of Brownian motion](rotational-invariance-of-brownian-motion.md). The strong law applied to their bounded positive truncations makes their sum infinite. Hence the conformal clock is infinite and the analytic Brownian image, under [time change of a continuous process](time-change-of-a-continuous-process.md), has full infinite Brownian lifetime. Centering the disk at a noncritical point is essential.
