# Rotating calipers

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

A family of algorithms that exploit monotonically changing geometric directions while traversing sorted points or the boundary of a [convex polygon](convex-polygon.md). For example, after sorting rays by [polar angle](polar-angle.md) around a point, a second index can advance monotonically to identify all rays lying in each open half-plane. Collinear rays require consistent boundary handling.
