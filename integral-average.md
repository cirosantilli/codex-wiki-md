# Integral average

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue integral](lebesgue-integral.md)

The [integral average](integral-average.md) of an integrable function over a measurable set $E$ of finite positive measure is $\langle v\rangle_E=|E|^{-1}\int_Ev$. Normalization removes the volume factor when comparing estimates on balls of different radii.
