# Meridional ray

↑ **Parent:** [Light ray](light-ray.md)

A [light ray](light-ray.md) whose path remains in a plane containing the [optical axis](optical-axis.md). In a straight [step-index optical fiber](step-index-optical-fiber.md), this geometry gives the complementary axis and interface-normal [angles](angle.md) used to derive the [optical fiber acceptance angle](optical-fiber-acceptance-angle.md).
