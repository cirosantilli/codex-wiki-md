# Oblique total reflection at an unmagnetized plasma

↑ **Parent:** [Cold collisionless plasma dielectric response](cold-collisionless-plasma-dielectric-response.md)

For vacuum incidence on a sharp planar, collisionless [plasma](plasma-physics.md) interface, the transmitted normal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) obeys $k_z^2=(\omega^2/c^2)(\cos^2\theta-\omega_p^2/\omega^2)$. Negative $k_z^2$ produces an evanescent field and total reflection in this lossless model.
