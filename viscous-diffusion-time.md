# Viscous diffusion time

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

Momentum diffuses across a distance $L$ in the characteristic time $L^2/\nu$, where $\nu$ is the [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md).
