<h1 id="decollement">Décollement</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thrust fault](thrust-fault.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Décollement)

A mechanically weak detachment along which an overlying package can move relative to its substrate. A [décollement](decollement.md) can follow weak bedding and connect to steeper thrust ramps, so its orientation need not equal the optimum dip for a new fracture in homogeneous [rock](rock-geology.md).
