# Cyclic phase of a spin in a rotating field

↑ **Parent:** [Berry phase](berry-phase.md)

An [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) of the [rotating-frame Rabi Hamiltonian](rotating-frame-rabi-hamiltonian.md) returns to the same laboratory ray after a full drive period. Its [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) includes both the rotating-frame [energy](energy-split.md) and the sign of a full [spin](spin.md) rotation. For [spin](spin.md) $s$ and magnetic label $\mu$, the factor is $(-1)^{2s}e^{-i\mu\Omega T}$. Expanding the rotating-frame splitting to first order in the drive [frequency](frequency.md) and subtracting the adiabatic [dynamical phase](dynamical-phase.md) leaves $-\mu$ times the oriented [solid angle](solid-angle.md) of the field loop. Dropping the full-rotation sign gives a spurious half-integer-spin offset.
