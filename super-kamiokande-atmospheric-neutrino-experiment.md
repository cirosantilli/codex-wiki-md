# Super-Kamiokande atmospheric-neutrino experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Kamiokande](super-kamiokande.md)

Super-Kamiokande used $535$ days ($33.0$ kiloton-years) and $22.5$ fiducial kilotons of [water](water.md). Inner [photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md) times and charges reconstructed interactions; the outer [anticoincidence detector](anticoincidence-detector.md) vetoed entering [particles](particle-physics.md) and marked exits. Contained single-ring events used [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) classification: [electron](electron.md) [particle showers](particle-shower.md) blur rings; [muons](muon.md) give sharper edges. The $1.33\,\mathrm{GeV}$ visible-[energy](energy-split.md) sample division is not a universal detection threshold. Simulation estimated classification efficiency above about $98\%$, distinct from total [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md); stopping [muons](muon.md) and decay [electrons](electron.md) checked classification.[PDF pp. 2–3, Table I: apparatus and classification](evidence-for-oscillation-of-atmospheric-neutrinos-by-y-fukuda-and-others.md)

Downward [neutrinos](neutrino.md) travel about $15\,\mathrm{km}$; upward ones about $13000\,\mathrm{km}$. The multi-GeV [muon](muon.md)-like up/down asymmetry was $-0.296\pm0.048\pm0.010$; [electron](electron.md)-like events lacked a comparable effect. Calibration constrained directional bias. This was disappearance evidence; [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md) appearance was untagged and sterile conversion remained possible.[PDF pp. 2–5, Fig. 1: zenith test and interpretation](evidence-for-oscillation-of-atmospheric-neutrinos-by-y-fukuda-and-others.md)

Writing $U$ and $D$ for the selected upward and downward populations, the asymmetry is $A=(U-D)/(U+D)$: a negative value means fewer upward events. An absolute source-normalization error alone rescales both populations and leaves this ratio unchanged. A direction-dependent response error need not cancel.
