# Parity conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Parity](parity.md)

An interaction commuting with the [parity operator](parity-operator.md) preserves total parity. A two-body final state has the product of its particles' intrinsic parities times the orbital factor $(-1)^\ell$, giving the [parity selection rule for a two-body decay](parity-selection-rule-for-a-two-body-decay.md).
