# Internal-wave ray tracing

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection of an internal-wave ray](reflection-of-an-internal-wave-ray.md)

Internal-wave ray tracing follows the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) while preserving the wave frequency. In a uniformly stratified fluid, each straight ray keeps the angle $\sin^{-1}(\omega/N)$ to the horizontal until reflection or a change in the medium.
