# Lagrange optical invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md)

For paraxial rays, the [Lagrange optical invariant](lagrange-optical-invariant.md) is $H=n(y_1u_2-y_2u_1)$. Lossless paraxial ray transfer preserves this quantity: in coordinates $(y,nu)$, free propagation and a [thin lens](thin-lens.md) act by [matrices](matrix.md) with [determinant](determinant.md) one, which preserve the oriented area of two ray vectors. Consequently a full angular [optical slit](optical-slit.md) width $\theta$ at an [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) [diameter](diameter.md) $D$ satisfies $D\theta=A_{\rm cam}p/f_{\rm cam}$ for its image width $p$ and [optical camera](optical-camera.md) [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) width $A_{\rm cam}$ in the same meridional plane.
