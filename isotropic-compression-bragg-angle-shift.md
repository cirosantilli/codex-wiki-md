# Isotropic-compression Bragg-angle shift

↑ **Parent:** [Bragg angle](bragg-angle.md)

For an isotropically compressed cubic [crystal](crystal.md), $d\ln a/dp=-\kappa/3$. Differentiating [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) at fixed [wavelength](wavelength.md) gives the displayed derivative. Constant [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md) gives $a(p)=a_0e^{-\kappa p/3}$, not an exactly linear lattice parameter. The integrated prediction presumes no intervening structural phase change.
