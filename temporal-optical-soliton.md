# Temporal optical soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Optical soliton](optical-soliton.md)

A localized temporal [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md) supported by a balance of [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md) and [self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md). Bright [solitons](soliton.md) decay to zero background; [dark solitons](dark-soliton.md) are dips on a nonzero background. This temporal balance differs from the [diffraction](diffraction.md) balance of a [spatial optical soliton](spatial-optical-soliton.md).
