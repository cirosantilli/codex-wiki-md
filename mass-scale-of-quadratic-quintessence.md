# Mass scale of quadratic quintessence

↑ **Parent:** [Quintessence (physics)](quintessence-physics.md)

A homogeneous scalar with $V=m^2\phi^2/2$ obeys $\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+m^2\phi=0$. If it is to provide potential-dominated [dark energy](dark-energy.md) today rather than rapidly oscillating matter, $m\lesssim H_0\sim10^{-33}\,\mathrm{eV}$ is required. Its present density gives $\phi_0\simeq\sqrt{6\Omega_X}M_{\rm Pl}H_0/m$. Taking $m\sim H_0$ gives a Planck-scale field; a much smaller mass requires a larger field value, so density alone does not fix a unique mass.
