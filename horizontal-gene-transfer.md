# Horizontal gene transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_gene_transfer)

[Horizontal gene transfer](horizontal-gene-transfer.md) moves genetic material between lineages through routes other than ordinary inheritance from parent to offspring. [Endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md) is a specialized case involving an internal partner or its descendant [organelle](organelle.md).
