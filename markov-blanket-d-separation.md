# Markov blanket D-separation

↑ **Parent:** [Markov blanket](markov-blanket.md)

In a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md), let $B$ contain the parents, children, and other parents of children of vertex $k$. Then $k$ is [D-separated](d-separation.md) from all remaining vertices by $B$. Every path out of $k$ meets a conditioned noncollider either at a parent, at a child, or at another parent just after a conditioned child-collider. The [global Markov property for a directed acyclic graph](global-markov-property-for-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md) gives $Z_k\perp Z_{(B\cup\{k\})^c}\mid Z_B$.
