# Spectral energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Energy density](energy-density.md)

[Energy density](energy-density.md) per unit spectral coordinate, such as $U_\nu$ per [frequency](frequency.md) or $U_\lambda$ per [wavelength](wavelength.md). Conservation of energy in corresponding bins requires $U_\lambda=U_\nu|d\nu/d\lambda|$; therefore their peak positions need not map by $\lambda=c/\nu$.
