# Gamma prior for an inverse-gamma scale

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-gamma distribution](inverse-gamma-distribution.md)

For [inverse-gamma distribution](inverse-gamma-distribution.md) observations of known shape $k$ and scale $\Theta$, their [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) in $\Theta$ is proportional to $\Theta^{nk}\exp(-\Theta\sum_i x_i^{-1})$. A shape-$\alpha$, rate-$\lambda$ [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) [conjugate prior](conjugate-prior.md) therefore gives the displayed [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md). The [Bayes estimator under squared error loss](bayes-estimator-under-squared-error-loss.md) of the conditional claim [expected value](expected-value.md) $\Theta/(k-1)$ is $(\alpha+nk)/[(k-1)(\lambda+\sum_i x_i^{-1})]$. It depends on reciprocals, rather than the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) of the claims.
