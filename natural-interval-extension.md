# Natural interval extension

↑ **Parent:** [Interval arithmetic operations](interval-arithmetic-operations.md)

A [natural interval extension](natural-interval-extension.md) evaluates a chosen expression by replacing its [arithmetic operations](arithmetic-operation.md) with [interval arithmetic operations](interval-arithmetic-operations.md), its [constants](constant-mathematics.md) with enclosing [closed intervals](closed-real-interval.md), and any additional [functions](function-split.md) with valid [interval enclosures](interval-enclosure.md). Each intermediate result must remain defined on its inputs. The resulting $F(X)$ encloses the actual [image of a function](image-of-a-function.md) $f(X)$, but its width depends on the expression used. Rump's INTLAB tutorial describes this inclusion principle and warns that repeated inputs can produce severe overestimation.[Siegfried M. Rump, Interval computations in INTLAB, Overestimation of interval operations and A common misuse of interval arithmetic](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/rump/intlab/demos/html/dintval.html)
