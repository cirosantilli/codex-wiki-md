# Primary metabolic byproduct

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)

A product released by reactions needed for energy acquisition, growth or biomass construction. [Methane](methane.md) from energy-yielding microbial reactions and [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) from oxygenic [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) are examples. Abiotic chemistry can make some of the same molecules, so production mechanism and environmental abundance matter.
