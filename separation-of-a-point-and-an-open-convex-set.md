# Separation of a point and an open convex set

↑ **Parent:** [Locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md)

If $C$ is a nonempty open convex subset of a real locally convex space and $x_0\notin C$, a continuous linear functional strictly separates them: after choosing a sign, $f(c)<f(x_0)$ for every $c\in C$. Apply the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) to the [Minkowski functional](minkowski-functional.md) of a translate of $C$.
