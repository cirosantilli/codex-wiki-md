# Thermal normalization of a fermion determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic one-level thermal determinant](fermionic-one-level-thermal-determinant.md)

The formal product over fermionic [Matsubara frequencies](matsubara-frequency.md) is divergent before its normalization and time-slice prescription are fixed. The coherent-state thermal trace fixes the single-level factor to $1+e^{-\beta\xi}$, counting its empty and occupied states. A symmetrically regularized product can instead expose $2\cosh(\beta\xi/2)$; the additional factor $e^{-\beta\xi/2}$ restores the physical empty-state energy convention. Dropping that factor can incorrectly shift the [grand potential](grand-potential.md) and particle occupation.
