# Dirichlet energy of a map

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet energy on a Riemannian manifold](dirichlet-energy-on-a-riemannian-manifold.md)

For a smooth map between [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md), its Dirichlet energy is half the integral of the squared [norm](norm.md) of its differential, using the source and target metrics. When the source is two-dimensional, a conformal change of its metric leaves this energy unchanged. For a [J-holomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) into a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) with a [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md), the [Energy identity for a J-holomorphic curve](energy-identity-for-a-j-holomorphic-curve.md) identifies it with the pulled-back [symplectic area](symplectic-area.md). The norm of a differential here is the full tensor norm, summing its squared values on an orthonormal source frame.
