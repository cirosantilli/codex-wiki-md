# Boltzmann ion densities in a symmetric electrolyte

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

For point [ions](ion.md) of charge $\pm e$ in potential $\psi$, equilibrium electrochemical potentials give $n_\pm=n_0\exp[\mp e\psi/(k_BT)]$, with $n_0$ the reservoir [density](density.md) of each species. The charge [density](density.md) is $-2en_0\sinh[e\psi/(k_BT)]$. A negative wall enriches positive [counterions](counterion.md) and depletes negative [co-ions](co-ion.md).
