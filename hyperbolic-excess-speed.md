# Hyperbolic excess speed

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic Kepler orbit](hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md)

The [hyperbolic excess speed](hyperbolic-excess-speed.md) is the limiting relative [speed](speed.md) far from an attracting body on an unbound [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md). Its [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) is $\varepsilon=v_\infty^2/2>0$. At radius $r$ from a body of [gravitational parameter](standard-gravitational-parameter.md) $\mu$, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives $v^2=v_\infty^2+2\mu/r$. The outgoing velocity direction as well as its magnitude matters when this orbit is matched to another attracting body's frame in a [patched conic approximation](patched-conic-approximation.md).
