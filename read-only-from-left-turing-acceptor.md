# Read-only-from-left Turing acceptor

↑ **Parent:** [Turing machine](turing-machine.md)

A deterministic [single-tape Turing machine](single-tape-turing-machine.md) that leaves every scanned symbol unchanged and moves one cell right after every nonhalting transition. Such machines decide exactly the [regular languages](regular-language.md). A [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) simulates the finite control, with accepting states determined by the blank-only continuation after the [input](input-computer-science.md); conversely, a machine simulating a [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) halts on the first blank.
