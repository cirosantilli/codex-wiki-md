<h1 id="particle-brst-charge-and-klein-gordon-constraint">Particle BRST charge and Klein–Gordon constraint</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [BRST charge](brst-charge.md)

The [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) square vanishes, so this [BRST charge](brst-charge.md) is nilpotent. On a ghost-number-zero wavefunction, [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md) implements the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md). Closure of an unrestricted ghost-extended wavefunction does not independently constrain every component: a pure [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) component can lie in the kernel without satisfying the same scalar equation. The [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) sector is part of the physical prescription.
