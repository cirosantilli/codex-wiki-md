# Nonhydrostatic stress in ruby pressure measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Ruby pressure scale](ruby-pressure-scale.md)

A single optical shift represents [pressure](pressure-split.md) only to the extent that the calibration's mechanical and thermal conditions apply. Under [hydrostatic stress](hydrostatic-stress.md), all directions have the same compressive normal [stress](stress.md). Under [deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md), differently oriented [ruby](ruby.md) grains can respond differently; spatially varying [pressure](pressure-split.md) can also superpose shifted lines. These mechanisms can produce different optical signals at equal mean [pressure](pressure-split.md).[Sections 1–2: stress-sensitive fluorescence shifts and crystallographic stress components](stress-measurements-in-alumina-by-optical-fluorescence-revisited-by-robert-f-cook-and-chris-a-michaels.md)

The 2001 account describes scanning local line positions and measuring line width over an extended region. The former resolves spatial differences; the latter supplies a rapid warning.[Section 4.2, Methods I and II](high-pressure-x-ray-crystallography-with-the-diamond-cell-at-nist%E2%81%84nbs-by-gasper-j-piermarini.md)

Neither a broadened line nor a narrow one uniquely determines the [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). Instrument resolution, [temperature](temperature-split.md) variation and unresolved overlapping signals can also affect width. A small observed region can have a narrow line while differing from another region. Compare locations, reference traces and instrument settings before treating width as evidence for a particular mechanical explanation. These are proposed diagnostic checks, not additional historical controls attributed to the 1975 experiment.

[Temperature](temperature-split.md) is another independent input. A useful local expansion is $\Delta\lambda\simeq s_p p+s_T(T-T_0)$: one measured shift cannot determine both unknowns. Measure or control [temperature](temperature-split.md), or supply a second independently calibrated observable. The coefficients need not remain constant across a large range. Even when a pressure sensitivity transfers over some [temperatures](temperature-split.md), the reference line, line width and practical readout quality can change. 

The historical high-temperature account reports degrading [pressure measurement](pressure-measurement.md) quality above about $300\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.[Section 4.1, high-temperature cell and readout-quality discussion](high-pressure-x-ray-crystallography-with-the-diamond-cell-at-nist%E2%81%84nbs-by-gasper-j-piermarini.md)
