# Isentropic mass density

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic potential vorticity](isentropic-potential-vorticity.md)

$\sigma_\theta=-(1/g)\partial p/\partial\theta$ measures mass per horizontal area per unit potential-temperature interval under hydrostatic balance. It is positive in a statically stable atmosphere.
