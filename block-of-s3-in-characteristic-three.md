# Block of S3 in characteristic three

↑ **Parent:** [Block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md)

Over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) three, the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) $A=kS_3$ has a single [block of an Artinian algebra](block-of-an-artinian-algebra.md). Put $r=(123)$, $s=(12)$ and $a=r-1$. Then $a^3=0$, $sas=-a+a^2$, and $J(A)=aA$: the latter is a [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md), with [semisimple ring](semisimple-ring.md) quotient $kC_2$. The [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md) is $k1\oplus ka^2\oplus ka^2s$, whose last two summands form a [square-zero ideal](square-zero-ideal.md). A [central idempotent](central-idempotent.md) $d1+n$ satisfies $d^2=d$ and $(2d-1)n=0$, hence is zero or one. Thus no nontrivial central block decomposition exists.
