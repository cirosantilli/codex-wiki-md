# Compact-support comparison with a one-point compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported cohomology](compactly-supported-cohomology.md)

If the [one-point compactification](alexandroff-extension.md) $X^+$ is Hausdorff and has a basis of contractible neighbourhoods at the added point, then

$$
H_c^*(X;A)\cong\widetilde H^*(X^+;A).
$$

The [Excision theorem](excision-theorem.md) identifies $H^*(X,X\setminus K)$ with $H^*(X^+,X^+\setminus K)$. The contractible neighbourhoods form a cofinal family of the complements of compact $K$, and their pair [long exact sequences](long-exact-sequence.md) identify each relative group naturally with [reduced cohomology](reduced-cohomology.md). Passing to the [direct limit](direct-limit-of-abelian-groups.md) gives the comparison. Local contractibility matters for [singular cohomology](singular-cohomology.md); the [Hawaiian earring](hawaiian-earring.md) illustrates its failure.
