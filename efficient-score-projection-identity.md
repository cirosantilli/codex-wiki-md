# Efficient-score projection identity

↑ **Parent:** [Efficient score](efficient-score.md)

The decomposition $\dot\ell=\Pi_{\mathcal N}\dot\ell+\widetilde\ell$ is orthogonal. Taking the [inner product](inner-product.md) with the [efficient score](efficient-score.md) gives $P(\dot\ell\widetilde\ell)=\widetilde I$. Centering of the [efficient score](efficient-score.md) follows because the [nuisance tangent space](nuisance-tangent-space.md) and the parametric [score function](informant-function.md) lie in the closed [mean-zero L2 space](mean-zero-l2-space.md).
