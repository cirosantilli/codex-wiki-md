# Preparedness in psychology

↑ **Parent:** [Learning](learning.md)

A proposed biological bias that makes some associations easier to acquire than others under comparable experience. Prepared [learning](learning.md) does not mean that the resulting response is already present before [learning](learning.md), nor that it is unchangeable.
