# Bipyrimidine

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

Two linked pyrimidine rings; the 2,2′-linked isomer has two N,N donor pockets. It can bind one metal as a [bidentate ligand](bidentate-ligand.md), or bridge two metals by using one pocket at each end. In the latter mode it is a bis-bidentate [bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md).
