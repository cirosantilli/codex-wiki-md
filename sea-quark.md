# Sea quark

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A [quark](quark.md) or [antiquark](antiquark.md) in the nonvalence component of a hadron's [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md). Such pairs do not change the net valence flavor numbers. Approximately equal light-flavor sea distributions can dominate small-x [deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md), but exact equality is not required by their definition.
