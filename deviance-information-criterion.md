# Deviance information criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Model selection](model-selection.md)

For [Bayesian deviance](bayesian-deviance.md) $D(\theta)=-2\log L(\theta)$ under a common likelihood convention, define $\overline D=\mathbb E[D(\theta)\mid y]$ and $p_D=\overline D-D(\mathbb E[\theta\mid y])$. Then $\operatorname{DIC}=\overline D+p_D$ trades average fit against effective complexity. Lower values suggest better penalized predictive fit in comparable models; differences are not [Bayes factors](bayes-factor.md) or model [posterior probabilities](posterior-probability.md). The choice of stochastic parameters and latent-variable representation matters, especially in a [hierarchical Bayesian model](hierarchical-bayesian-model.md).
