# Degree-one maps between closed oriented surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

A [degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md) equal to one makes the pullback on degree-one [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) injective by the [Poincare duality pairing](poincare-duality-pairing.md). Thus a degree-one map of closed oriented surfaces requires $2h\leq2g$. Conversely, collapse the extra handles in $\Sigma_h\#\Sigma_{g-h}$ onto a point. The retained oriented disc shows that this map has degree one.
