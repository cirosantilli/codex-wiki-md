# Newton iteration over a valued field

↑ **Parent:** [Strong form of Hensel lemma](strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md)

Starting from $a_0$, Newton iteration sets $a_{n+1}=a_n-f(a_n)/f'(a_n)$. Under the [strong form of Hensel lemma](strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md) inequality, the correction valuations grow at least geometrically, so $(a_n)$ is a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) converging to a root.
