# Heterotic Calabi-Yau compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Heterotic string](heterotic-string.md)

Compactification of a heterotic string on a [Calabi-Yau threefold](calabi-yau-threefold.md) with a consistent [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md). Suitable bundle and geometry choices preserve four-dimensional N=1 [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and generate chiral matter from internal Dirac zero modes. The index controls net chirality. In the standard embedding, the net generation index is half the internal [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md), up to chirality orientation conventions.
