# Proper rational transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer function](transfer-function.md)

A [proper rational transfer function](proper-rational-transfer-function.md) is a [rational function](rational-function.md) whose numerator [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) no greater than its denominator [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), after common factors are canceled. Every finite-dimensional continuous-time [linear time-invariant system](linear-time-invariant-system.md) of the form $\dot x=Ax+Bu$, $y=Cx+Du$ has such a [transfer function](transfer-function.md) $G(s)=C(sI-A)^{-1}B+D$. [Dahleh, Dahleh and Verghese, Lectures on Dynamic Systems and Control, section 15.3.1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-241j-dynamic-systems-and-control-spring-2011/996025f6db0d90b00f11c44fc49b85f9_MIT6_241JS11_textbook.pdf)
