# Fractional quantum Hall effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_quantum_Hall_effect)

The [fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md) is quantized transverse [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) at fractional [filling factors](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md), produced by interacting [electrons](electron.md). The [Laughlin wavefunction](laughlin-wavefunction.md) describes the sequence $\nu=1/m$, with odd $m$, including $\nu=1/3$.[pp. 1395–1396, ground-state construction](anomalous-quantum-hall-effect-an-incompressible-quantum-fluid-with-fractionally-charged-excitations-by-robert-laughlin.md)

For an original ideal readout example, take a [Hall bar](hall-bar.md) on the $\nu=1/3$ plateau with negligible longitudinal [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md). The [quantum Hall conductivity tensor inversion](quantum-hall-conductivity-tensor-inversion.md) gives

$$
R_H=\frac{|V_H|}{I}=\frac{h}{\nu e^2}=3R_K,
\qquad I=10\,\mathrm{nA}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |V_H|\simeq0.774384\,\mathrm{mV}.
$$

Here $R_K$ is the [von Klitzing constant](von-klitzing-constant.md); $R_H$ denotes transverse [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), rather than the bulk [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md). Thus a fractional [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) corresponds to a larger transverse [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). The fraction labels a collective [quantum state](quantum-state.md): it does not mean that each [electron](electron.md) has acquired one third of its usual [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
