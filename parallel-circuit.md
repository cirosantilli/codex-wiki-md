# Parallel circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

A connection in which branches share both terminal nodes and therefore the same [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). Branch [electric currents](electric-current.md) add. For sinusoidal signals, the total [electrical admittance](electrical-admittance.md) is the sum of branch [electrical admittances](electrical-admittance.md); ideal [capacitances](capacitance.md) add.
