# Minuscule weights in the root lattice are zero

↑ **Parent:** [Minuscule weight](minuscule-weight.md)

By [dominant root-lattice highest weights have zero weight](dominant-root-lattice-highest-weights-have-zero-weight.md), a [minuscule weight](minuscule-weight.md) in the [root lattice](root-lattice.md) has zero in its [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) orbit. Since every element of the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) is invertible, the highest weight must itself be zero. Thus equality of the [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md) and [root lattice](root-lattice.md) rules out every nontrivial [minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md) without requiring a classification of [root systems](root-system.md).
