# Low-energy charged-scalar scattering through a neutral scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md)

For interaction $-g\varphi^\dagger\varphi\phi$ with a real mediator of mass $m$, fixed-charge particle–antiparticle scattering has $s$- and $t$-channel exchange. At $|s|,|t|\ll m^2$, their amplitudes add to $2g^2/m^2$. Distinguishable massless final particles then give $\sigma\simeq g^4/(4\pi m^4s)$. There is no extra $u$ channel for the same fixed assignment of final charges.
