# Clinical syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopathology](psychopathology.md)

A recognizable pattern of associated experiences or signs with clinical significance. A coherent [clinical syndrome](clinical-syndrome.md) need not have a single cause, and covariance among its indicators does not alone establish their causal organization.
