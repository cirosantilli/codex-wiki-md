# Potential function

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)

A potential function $V$ assigns a [scalar](scalar.md) to each point of a configuration or spatial domain. In mechanics it represents [potential energy](potential-energy.md), and its negative [gradient](gradient.md) gives the corresponding [conservative force](conservative-force.md).
