# Two-row alternating character cancellation

↑ **Parent:** [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md)

For even $n$, the [virtual character](virtual-character.md)

$$
F_n=\sum_{r=0}^{n/2}(-1)^r\chi^{(n-r,r)}
$$

vanishes on every permutation having an odd cycle. Under the [Frobenius characteristic map](frobenius-characteristic-map.md), the [Jacobi–Trudi identity](jacobi-trudi-identity.md) identifies its characteristic with the degree-$n$ part of $H(t)H(-t)$, which contains only products of even-indexed power sums.
