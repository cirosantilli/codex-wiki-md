# Chelate effect

↑ **Parent:** [Chelation](chelation.md)

The [chelate effect](chelate-effect.md) is the enhanced stability often associated with binding several donor sites from one ligand compared with comparable independent donors. Solvent release, effective local concentration and enthalpic contributions all matter; it is not simply a consequence of satisfying an electron-count rule.
