# Minimally dependent vector family

↑ **Parent:** [Linear independence](linear-independence.md)

A finite [vector](vector.md) family is minimally dependent if it is [linearly dependent](linear-dependence.md) and every proper subfamily is [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). For $n\geq2$, the family $e_1,\ldots,e_{n-1},\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}e_j$ in $\mathbb R^n$ is an example. The sum supplies a nonzero dependence. If the sum [vector](vector.md) is omitted, the remaining [vectors](vector.md) are a coordinate basis; if $e_j$ is omitted, its coordinate in any proposed relation forces the coefficient of the sum [vector](vector.md) to vanish, then all other coefficients vanish. Every smaller proper subfamily lies in one of these independent $(n-1)$-element subfamilies.
