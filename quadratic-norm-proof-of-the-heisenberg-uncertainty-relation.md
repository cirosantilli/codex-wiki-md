# Quadratic-norm proof of the Heisenberg uncertainty relation

↑ **Parent:** [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md)

For an admissible [quantum state](quantum-state.md) with zero [position](position.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) means, positivity of the squared [norm](norm.md) $\lVert(p-isx)\psi\rVert^2$ for every real $s$ gives

$$
(\Delta p)^2+s^2(\Delta x)^2-s\hbar\geq0.
$$

The [discriminant](discriminant.md) is nonpositive, hence $\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. Subtracting the [expectation values](expectation-value.md) from the [position operator](position-operator.md) and [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md) extends this outline to nonzero means.
