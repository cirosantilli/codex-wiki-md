# Quasi-Newton Methods by NEOS Guide

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/quasi-newton/](https://neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/quasi-newton/)

The NEOS Guide's mathematical account of [quasi-Newton methods](quasi-newton-method.md), deriving the secant condition from an integrated [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) and describing low-rank updating, including [BFGS](bfgs-method.md). The related nonlinear-equation account is [https://neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/broyden/](https://neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/broyden/) and discusses [Broyden's method](broyden-s-method.md).
