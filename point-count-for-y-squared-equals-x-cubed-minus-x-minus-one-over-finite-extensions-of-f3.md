# Point count for y squared equals x cubed minus x minus one over finite extensions of F3

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md)

For $E:y^2=x^3-x-1$ over $\mathbb F_3$, the [Trace of Frobenius](trace-of-frobenius.md) is three and

$$
\#E(\mathbb F_{3^r})
=3^r+1-2\cdot3^{r/2}\cos(r\pi/6).
$$

In particular, $\#E(\mathbb F_{3^r})=3^r+1$ exactly when $r\equiv3\pmod6$.
