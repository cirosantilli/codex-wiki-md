# Real closure theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Real closure](real-closure.md)

Every ordered [field](field.md) has an ordered algebraic [real closure](real-closure.md). Combined with the [Artin-Schreier ordering criterion](artin-schreier-ordering-criterion.md), every [formally real field](formally-real-field.md) embeds into a [real closed field](real-closed-field.md). This is one embedding direction in the [model companion](model-companion.md) relation between their theories.
