# Pushforward of a contravariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md)

The [pushforward of a contravariant tensor](pushforward-of-a-contravariant-tensor.md) applies the [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) to each vector factor of a type $(r,0)$ tensor. It is defined pointwise at a specified source point. A field generally gives a field along the map, not necessarily a unique tensor field on the image when several points have the same image. A [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) allows the usual transport of arbitrary mixed tensors through its inverse as well.
