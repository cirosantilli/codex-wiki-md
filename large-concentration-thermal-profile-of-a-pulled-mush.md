# Large-concentration thermal profile of a pulled mush

↑ **Parent:** [Directional solidification](directional-solidification.md)

For $\mathcal C\gg1$ and [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md) $S\gg1$ with $S/\mathcal C=O(1)$, put $\Omega=1+S/(r\mathcal C)$, $\zeta=Vz/\kappa$, and $H=Vh/\kappa$. The steady [heat equation](heat-equation.md) becomes $\theta''+r\Omega\theta'=0$ in the mush and $\theta''+r\theta'=0$ in the liquid. Matching temperature and [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) at $\zeta=H$, with $\theta(0)=-1$ and liquid temperature $\theta_\infty>0$ far above, gives

$$
\theta_m=\frac{\theta_\infty}{\Omega}-(1+\theta_\infty/\Omega)e^{-r\Omega\zeta},\qquad \theta_l=\theta_\infty[1-e^{-r(\zeta-H)}],\qquad H=\frac1{r\Omega}\log(1+\Omega/\theta_\infty).
$$

The latent heat modifies the mush's effective thermal decay rate and therefore its thickness.
