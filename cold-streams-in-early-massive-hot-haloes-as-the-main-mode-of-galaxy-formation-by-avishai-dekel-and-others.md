# Cold streams in early massive hot haloes as the main mode of galaxy formation by Avishai Dekel and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Avishai Dekel](work-by-avishai-dekel.md)

The study uses cosmological simulations and clustering theory to investigate cold gas supply to rapidly star-forming early galaxies. It compares stream-fed formation with observed galaxy abundance. These are model calculations informed by observations, not a direct image of every predicted gas stream.

[https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.0553](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.0553)

[https://doi.org/10.1038/nature07648](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature07648)
