# Contraction of a topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Contractible space](contractible-space.md)

A contraction is a [homotopy](homotopy.md) from the identity map of $X$ to a constant map. Its existence says that $X$ is a [contractible space](contractible-space.md). For a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md), choosing a root and moving each point along its unique finite path to that root gives a contraction, with continuity understood in the [weak topology of a CW complex](weak-topology-of-a-cw-complex.md).
