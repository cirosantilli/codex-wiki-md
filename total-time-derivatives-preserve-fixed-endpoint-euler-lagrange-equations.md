# Total time derivatives preserve fixed-endpoint Euler-Lagrange equations

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md)

Adding a [total derivative](total-derivative.md) $dg(q(t))/dt$ to a [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) changes its [action](action.md) only by $g(q(T))-g(q(0))$. Under fixed-endpoint [variations](variation.md), this [boundary term](boundary-term.md) is constant, so the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) and their minimizing paths are unchanged. Componentwise, $d[\partial(dg/dt)/\partial\dot q_i]/dt=\sum_jg_{ij}\dot q_j=\partial(dg/dt)/\partial q_i$ by equality of mixed [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md). Endpoint freedom would require reconsidering the resulting [natural boundary conditions for a free endpoint](natural-boundary-conditions-for-a-free-endpoint.md).
