# Bounded-degree bipartite Ramsey bound

↑ **Parent:** [Dependent random choice](dependent-random-choice.md)

If a bipartite graph $H$ has $k$ vertices and [maximum degree](maximum-degree-of-a-graph.md) $d$, then

$$
R(H)=O(d^{2d}k).
$$

A [dependent random choice](dependent-random-choice.md) argument finds, in one colour, enough vertices whose every subset of at most $d$ vertices has a large common neighbourhood; a greedy embedding then places $H$.
