# Electron magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

[Electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) neglects [ion](ion.md) motion and [Electron](electron.md) inertia while retaining transport of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) by the [Electron](electron.md) current. At constant [Electron](electron.md) [number density](number-density.md), $\mathbf u_e=-\mathbf J/(en)=-c\nabla\times\mathbf B/(4\pi en)$. Flux freezing in this [velocity](velocity.md) gives

$$
\partial_t\mathbf B=-\frac c{4\pi en}\nabla\times[(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B].
$$

For a uniform guide field, the [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) linear equation implies $\omega^2=(cB_0/4\pi en)^2k_\parallel^2k^2$. With $v_A=B_0/\sqrt{4\pi nm_i}$ and $d_i=c\sqrt{m_i/(4\pi e^2n)}$, the coefficient is $cB_0/(4\pi en)=v_Ad_i$.
