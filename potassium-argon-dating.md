# Potassium-argon dating

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometric dating](radiometric-dating.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potassium–argon_dating)

With no initial radiogenic [argon-40](argon-40.md) and complete daughter retention, the surviving [potassium-40](potassium-40.md) population is $N_K=N_{K,0}e^{-\lambda t}$ and the accumulated daughter population is $N_{\mathrm{Ar}}=b(N_{K,0}-N_K)$. Here $\lambda$ is the total [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md) and $b$ is the argon-producing [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md). Their ratio gives the displayed age. The result measures closure of the mineral's isotope system under those assumptions.
