<h1 id="kahler-potential-complex-geometry">Kähler potential (complex geometry)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md)

A [geometric Kähler potential](kahler-potential-complex-geometry.md) is locally a real smooth function $\varphi$ with $\omega=i\partial\bar\partial\varphi$ for the [Kähler form](kahler-form.md). Its complex Hessian must be positive definite to define a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md). Conventions sometimes absorb a factor of $1/2$ into $\varphi$. Potentials differing by the real part of a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) determine the same form. This topic concerns ordinary [complex geometry](complex-geometry-split.md); the existing undisambiguated [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) entry concerns superfields.
