# Compact H-hull

↑ **Parent:** [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

A compact H-hull is a bounded relatively closed subset $A$ of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) $\mathbb H$ such that $\mathbb H\setminus A$ is a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md).
