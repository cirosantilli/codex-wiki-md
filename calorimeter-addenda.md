# Calorimeter addenda

↑ **Parent:** [Low-temperature heat capacity measurement](low-temperature-heat-capacity-measurement.md)

[Calorimeter addenda](calorimeter-addenda.md) are the participating [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) of everything in the measured thermal assembly except the sample: platform, heater, [thermometer](thermometer.md), mounting material and the effective part of the leads. They depend on [temperature](temperature-split.md) and assembly details. [Calorimeter calibration](calorimeter-calibration.md) can determine them using an empty platform or a reference sample. Subtract $C_a(T)$ from the loaded assembly at matching [temperature](temperature-split.md) and applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); replacing it with a constant can imitate a sample anomaly.
