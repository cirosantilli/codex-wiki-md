# Coefficient of restitution

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient_of_restitution)

The restitution law applies to velocities of the actual contact points, including any rotational velocities of rigid bodies. A perfectly [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) has $e=1$; $e=0$ gives zero immediate normal separation speed, but does not by itself require all parts of colliding bodies to share the same velocity.
