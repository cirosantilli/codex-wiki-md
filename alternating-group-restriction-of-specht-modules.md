# Alternating-group restriction of Specht modules

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

An ordinary [Specht module](specht-module.md) of non-self-conjugate shape stays [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) on the [alternating group](alternating-group.md), and conjugate shapes give the same restriction. A self-conjugate shape splits into two [irreducibles](irreducible-representation.md) of equal degree, interchanged by an odd [permutation](permutation.md). The index-two inner-product identity is $\langle\chi^\lambda\downarrow,\chi^\mu\downarrow\rangle=\delta_{\lambda\mu}+\delta_{\lambda'\mu}$, and [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md) proves exhaustion. The statement applies for $n\ge2$; $A_1$ is trivial.
