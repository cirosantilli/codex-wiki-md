<h1 id="risk-neutral-valuation-in-the-cox-ross-rubinstein-model">Risk-neutral valuation in the Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral probability in the Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model](risk-neutral-probability-in-the-cox-ross-rubinstein-model.md)

For a discounted claim $H$, the arbitrage-free value process is $V_t=\mathbb E_Q[H\mid\mathcal F_t]$. A terminal payoff $h(S_T)$ has value

$$
V_t=\sum_{k=0}^{T-t}\binom{T-t}{k}q^k(1-q)^{T-t-k}
h(S_tu^kd^{T-t-k}).
$$
