# Composite-fermion approach for the fractional quantum Hall effect by J. K. Jain

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.63.199](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.63.199)

Physical Review Letters 63, 199–202. The abstract proposes understanding electronic fractional Hall states as integer Hall states of composite objects with an even number of attached flux quanta.
