# Essential supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Lp inclusion on a finite measure space](lp-inclusion-on-a-finite-measure-space.md)

The [essential infimum and essential supremum](essential-infimum-and-essential-supremum.md) are the two almost-everywhere order bounds; this section defines the upper bound.

The essential supremum of a measurable function is the least number $M$ such that $f\leq M$ almost everywhere. It equals the $L^\infty$ norm for a nonnegative function and, on a finite measure space, $\|f\|_p\to\|f\|_\infty$ as $p\to\infty$.
