<h1 id="bezier-derivative-control-polygon">Bézier derivative control polygon</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md)

For a degree-$n$ [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md), the $r$th [derivative](derivative.md) has degree at most $n-r$ and [control points](control-point.md) $n!\Delta^rP_j/(n-r)!$. The first [derivative](derivative.md) follows by differentiating the [Bernstein basis](bernstein-basis.md) and shifting one summation index; iteration proves the general formula. A parameter interval of length $h$ multiplies these [derivative](derivative.md) controls by $h^{-r}$.
