# Significant figures

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Significant_figures)

In [numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md), [significant figures](significant-figures.md) record how many meaningful digits are retained in a written numerical value. Leading zeros locate the decimal point rather than adding precision: $0.0079$ and $79$ each have two [significant figures](significant-figures.md). Trailing zeros may require an explicit convention to distinguish measured precision from positional notation.
