# Tau (particle)

↑ **Parent:** [Lepton](lepton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_(particle))

The tau is the charged [lepton](lepton.md) of the third [fermion generation](fermion-generation.md). In the massless approximation its charged-current [W boson](w-boson.md) partial [decay width](decay-width.md) equals those of the [Electron](electron.md) and [muon](muon.md), reflecting lepton universality.
