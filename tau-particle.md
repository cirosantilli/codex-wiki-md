# Tau (particle)

↑ **Parent:** [Lepton](lepton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_(particle))

The tau is the charged [lepton](lepton.md) of the third [fermion generation](fermion-generation.md). In the massless approximation its charged-current [W boson](w-boson.md) partial [decay width](decay-width.md) equals those of the [electron](electron.md) and [muon](muon.md), reflecting lepton universality.

The [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) has [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-1$ in units of the positive elementary [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); $\tau^+$ is its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md). Its [weak charged current](charged-current.md) decays make the [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) useful for testing [lepton universality](lepton-universality.md). They also illustrate how [particle detection](particle-detection-split.md) can establish a short-lived parent through visible daughters and a consistent invisible recoil.
