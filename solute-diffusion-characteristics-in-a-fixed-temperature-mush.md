# Solute-diffusion characteristics in a fixed-temperature mush

↑ **Parent:** [Mushy layer](mushy-layer.md)

In a stationary [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) with fixed $T=T_E+Gz$, pure solid and local [liquidus](liquidus.md) $C=-T/m$, solute conservation is $\partial_t[(1-\phi)C]=D\partial_z[(1-\phi)C_z]$. Since $C_z=-G/m$ is constant, it reduces to $(z+T_E/G)\phi_t=D\phi_z$. The [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) makes the displayed $\eta$ constant and gives $\phi=f(\eta)$ within the region that remains mush. For negative $T_E$, the characteristics move upward where the equilibrium concentration is positive. Extrapolating a zero-solid-fraction level is not sufficient to determine a boundary when additional flux constraints create a liquid cap or a solid-fraction jump.
