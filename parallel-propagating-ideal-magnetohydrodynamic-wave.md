# Parallel-propagating ideal magnetohydrodynamic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md)

In a uniform ideal-MHD [plasma](plasma-physics.md) at rest, take the [wavevector](wavevector.md) parallel to a nonzero background field. Projecting the linear [velocity](velocity.md) equation along and perpendicular to the field gives two transverse Alfvén [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) at speed $v_A$ and one longitudinal acoustic [polarization](polarization-waves.md) at speed $c_s$. Compression does not bend field lines for the longitudinal branch. The general oblique fast/slow magnetosonic modes reduce to the corresponding faster/slower parallel branches, with degeneracies when their speeds agree.
