# Baryon transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological transfer function](cosmological-transfer-function.md)

The baryon transfer function retains acoustic oscillations generated while baryons belonged to the [photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md). After recombination, baryons fall into cold-dark-matter potential wells, reducing the relative perturbation while leaving a small oscillatory imprint.
