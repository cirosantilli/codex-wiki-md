# Worldsheet Weyl anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-model beta function](sigma-model-beta-function.md)

A worldsheet Weyl anomaly is the quantum failure of the gauge-fixed string path integral to remain invariant under a [Weyl transformation](weyl-transformation.md). Its local coefficients are the [sigma-model beta functions](sigma-model-beta-function.md), while its constant part is controlled by the total [central charge](central-charge.md).
