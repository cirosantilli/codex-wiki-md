# Fermionic creation operator

↑ **Parent:** [Mode expansion of a Dirac field](mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md)

A fermionic creation operator is the adjoint of a [fermionic annihilation operator](fermionic-annihilation-operator.md) and adds one fermion to a mode. Its square vanishes, implementing the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md).
