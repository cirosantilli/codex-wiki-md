# Contact-angle hysteresis

↑ **Parent:** [Contact angle](contact-angle.md)

A contact line can remain pinned while the observed [contact angle](contact-angle.md) changes between receding and advancing limits. Surface roughness and chemical heterogeneity can produce this hysteresis. Slowly adding or removing liquid from a sessile drop measures advancing and receding angles separately. An observed pinned angle therefore need not be the single equilibrium angle predicted by [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md) for a perfectly homogeneous surface.
