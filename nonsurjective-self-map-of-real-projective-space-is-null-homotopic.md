# Nonsurjective self-map of real projective space is null-homotopic

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

For $n\ge2$, a nonsurjective self-map of [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^n$ has zero [mod-two degree of a map between closed manifolds](mod-two-degree-of-a-map-between-closed-manifolds.md). In the [mod-two cohomology ring of real projective space](mod-two-cohomology-ring-of-real-projective-space.md), the nonzero degree-one class $a$ has $a^n\ne0$. If pullback fixed $a$, it would fix $a^n$, contradicting zero degree. Thus the map is trivial on the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) and lifts to $S^n$. A point missed by the original map has both its inverse images missed by the lift. The lift therefore takes values in a punctured sphere, which is contractible, and is null-homotopic. Composing its contraction with the covering projection proves the conclusion.
