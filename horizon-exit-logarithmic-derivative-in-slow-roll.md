# Horizon-exit logarithmic derivative in slow roll

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)

Along [cosmological horizon exit](cosmological-horizon-exit.md) $k\propto aH$, differentiation gives $d\log k/d\log a=1-\epsilon_H$, where $\epsilon_H=-\dot H/H^2$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the first expression. In [reduced Planck units](reduced-planck-units.md), the [slow-roll equations](slow-roll-approximation.md) $3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'$ and $3H^2\simeq V$ give the second expression to leading order. Applied to a quantity already of slow-roll order, the omitted factor changes the answer only at second order.
