# Infinite-dimensional real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

This is the [CW complex](cw-complex.md) obtained as the increasing union of finite [Real projective spaces](real-projective-space.md) under their standard inclusions. It has one cell in each nonnegative dimension. Its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) has boundary multiplication by two in positive even dimensions and zero in odd dimensions. Consequently its integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is $\mathbb Z$ in degree zero, $\mathbb Z/2$ in positive even degrees and zero in odd degrees. Its mod-two [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is $\mathbb Z_2$ in every nonnegative degree.
