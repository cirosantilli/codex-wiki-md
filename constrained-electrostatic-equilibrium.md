# Constrained electrostatic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential](electric-potential.md)

A particle constrained to a surface can have a stable minimum of [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) restricted to that surface even when the unrestricted equilibrium is unstable. Away from source charges, [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md) makes the full potential [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) trace zero, which rules out a positive-definite three-dimensional Hessian but does not rule out a positive-definite restriction to two dimensions. Equal positive charges at the corners of a square give a planar minimum at its centre and an unstable perpendicular direction.
