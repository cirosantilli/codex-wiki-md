# Carbonyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md) is coordinated [carbon monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md), usually attached through [carbon](carbon.md). [Sigma donation](sigma-donation.md) and [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) cooperate in the metal–[carbon](carbon.md) bond. Terminal and bridging forms have different electron-counting descriptions.
