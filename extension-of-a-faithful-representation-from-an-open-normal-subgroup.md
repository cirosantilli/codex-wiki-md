# Extension of a faithful representation from an open normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Induced representation](induced-representation.md)

Let $H$ be an [open normal subgroup](open-normal-subgroup.md) of a [compact topological group](compact-group.md) $G$, and let $\tau:H\to\operatorname{GL}_m(\mathbb Z_p)$ be a continuous [faithful representation](faithful-representation.md). Choose representatives $t_j$ for the finite left [cosets](coset.md) $G/H$. Write $gt_j=t_i h_{ij}$ and act on the $j$-th summand of $\bigoplus_j\mathbb Z_p^m$ by $\tau(h_{ij})$ into the $i$-th summand. The cocycle identity proves this is a continuous [group representation](group-representation.md). An element outside $H$ has a nontrivial coset permutation, while an element of $H$ acting trivially has $\tau(g)=1$ on the identity-coset summand. Hence the representation of $G$ is faithful and integral.
