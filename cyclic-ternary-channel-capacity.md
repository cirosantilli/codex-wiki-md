# Cyclic ternary channel capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly symmetric channel capacity](weakly-symmetric-channel-capacity.md)

A [discrete memoryless channel](discrete-memoryless-channel.md) on the additive [group](group-split.md) $\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$ maps $x$ to $x$ with [probability](probability.md) $1-q$ and to $x+1$ with [probability](probability.md) $q$. Every input gives the same [conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md) $h_2(q)$, whereas the output [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) is at most $\log_2 3$. A uniform input produces a uniform output since every column of the transition [matrix](matrix.md) sums to one. Hence the [channel capacity](channel-capacity.md) is $\log_2 3-h_2(q)$ [bits](bit.md) per use. This cyclic-error channel differs from a channel distributing its error probability equally over both other output symbols.
