# Bulk-density upper limit from a transit and an RV nondetection

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md)

A transit [radius](radius.md) $R_p$ and an inclination-constrained [mass](mass.md) upper limit $M_{\max}$ imply $\bar\rho_p<M_{\max}/(4\pi R_p^3/3)$. The inferred [density](density.md) bound depends on the adopted stellar [radius](radius.md), transit dilution, orbital model and how the velocity detection threshold is defined. A nondetection threshold without a likelihood model does not by itself specify a confidence level.
