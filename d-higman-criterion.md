# D. Higman criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Relative trace](relative-trace.md)

D. Higman's criterion says that an $RG$-module $M$ is [relative projective module](relative-projective-module.md) for $H$ exactly when there is $\alpha\in\operatorname{End}_{RH}(M)$ satisfying

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_H^G(\alpha)=\operatorname{id}_M.
$$
