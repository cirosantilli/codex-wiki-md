<h1 id="schoenberg-whitney-theorem">Schoenberg–Whitney theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline collocation matrix](b-spline-collocation-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoenberg–Whitney_theorem)

For increasing interpolation sites $x_i$ and an order-$k$ B-spline basis with knots $t_i$, the [B-spline collocation matrix](b-spline-collocation-matrix.md) is invertible exactly when

$$
t_i<x_i<t_{i+k}
$$

for every $i$, equivalently when $N_i(x_i)>0$ for every $i$.
