# Transport of a Dirac point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Linear transport equation](linear-transport-equation.md)

For the constant-velocity [linear transport equation](linear-transport-equation.md) $u_t+c\cdot\nabla_xu=0$, a [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) initially at zero moves along $x=ct$. Its spacetime [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) pairs by $\langle u,\varphi\rangle=\int\varphi(ct,t)\,dt$, and its initial trace is obtained by [weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md). The [Fourier representation of the Dirac delta function](fourier-representation-of-the-dirac-delta-function.md) writes it as an [oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md) with [phase function](phase-function.md) $(x-ct)\cdot\theta$. Its [singular support](singular-support.md) is precisely the trajectory; it has [order-zero distribution](order-zero-distribution.md) regularity as a measure, without becoming a [smooth function](smooth-function.md).
