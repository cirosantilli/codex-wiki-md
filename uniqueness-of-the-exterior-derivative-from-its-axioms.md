# Uniqueness of the exterior derivative from its axioms

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md)

An $\mathbb R$-linear degree-one [graded derivation](graded-derivation.md) of the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) of smooth [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) that equals the differential on functions and squares to zero is unique. A [smooth bump function](smooth-bump-function.md) and the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) first establish locality. Then $d(dx^i)=0$, and applying the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) to $\alpha=\sum_Ia_Idx^I$ gives the displayed coordinate formula. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) makes that formula coordinate-independent.
