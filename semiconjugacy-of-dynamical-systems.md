# Semiconjugacy of dynamical systems

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

A semiconjugacy from $f:X\to X$ to $g:Y\to Y$ is a map $h:X\to Y$ satisfying $h\circ f=g\circ h$. It transports forward orbits but need not be invertible, A [bijection](bijection.md) instead gives [conjugate functions](conjugate-functions.md).
