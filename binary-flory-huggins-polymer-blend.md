# Binary Flory-Huggins polymer blend

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

An incompressible, equal-site-volume [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) for two [polymers](polymer-split.md) of degrees of polymerization $N_A,N_B$. Here $f$ is mixing [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per site and $\phi$ is the [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) of species $A$. For a composition-independent [Flory interaction parameter](flory-interaction-parameter.md), local stability requires $1/(N_A\phi)+1/[N_B(1-\phi)]-2\chi>0$. The [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md) is $\phi_c=\sqrt{N_B}/(\sqrt{N_A}+\sqrt{N_B})$ and $\chi_c=(N_A^{-1/2}+N_B^{-1/2})^2/2$.
