<h1 id="bochner-s-theorem">Bochner's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-definite function](positive-definite-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochner's_theorem)

For a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group, a continuous [positive-definite function](positive-definite-function.md) $p$ is uniquely representable as $p(x)=\int_{\widehat G}\chi(x)\,d\mu_p(\chi)$ for a finite positive [Radon measure](radon-measure.md) on the [Pontryagin dual group](pontryagin-dual-group.md). Its total mass is $p(e)$. Conversely every such finite positive measure defines a continuous positive-definite function.
