# Formal expansion at a smooth point

↑ **Parent:** [Regular system of parameters](regular-system-of-parameters.md)

A chosen [regular system of parameters](regular-system-of-parameters.md) gives the displayed completion isomorphism by sending $T_i$ to $t_i$. The [associated graded ring of a regular local ring](associated-graded-ring-of-a-regular-local-ring.md) is the polynomial ring on their initial forms. Successively removing the homogeneous initial form of each remainder gives a unique formal series for every local function. The [Krull intersection theorem](krull-intersection-theorem.md) embeds the local ring in its completion. The power-series characterization is also given in [Stacks Project Lemma 10.160.10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C0S).
