# Icicle ripple

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

An icicle ripple is a corrugation of the ice-water interface produced while a thin flowing water film freezes. Coupling [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md), [surface tension](surface-tension.md), [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md), and the [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) determines its growth and migration.
