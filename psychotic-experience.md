# Psychotic experience

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopathology](psychopathology.md)

An experience resembling phenomena assessed in [psychosis](psychosis.md), such as a hallucination or unusual conviction. [Psychotic experiences](psychotic-experience.md) can occur with or without a clinical disorder; a research definition must specify persistence, context and consequences.
