# Psychological resilience trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement of psychological resilience](measurement-of-psychological-resilience.md)

A pattern of functioning across time around [adversity](adversity.md). Stable adaptation and [recovery after adversity](recovery-after-adversity.md) can produce similar scores at a late follow-up while reflecting different histories. Estimated trajectory classes depend on measurement and statistical assumptions; they should not automatically be treated as discrete natural types.
