# Regular sequence on a module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

For central elements, each multiplication map on the preceding quotient must be injective; a usual convention also requires the final quotient to be nonzero. The augmented [Koszul complex on central ring elements](koszul-complex-on-central-ring-elements.md) tensored with $M$ is then a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) to the final quotient in degree zero.
