# Observations on a certain theorem of Fermat and on others concerning prime numbers by Leonhard Euler

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Jordan Bell](work-by-jordan-bell.md), [Work by Leonhard Euler](work-by-leonhard-euler.md)

By [Leonhard Euler](leonhard-euler.md); English translation by [Jordan Bell](jordan-bell.md). Presented to the St Petersburg Academy on 26 September 1732; the original Latin paper, "Observationes de theoremate quodam Fermatiano aliisque ad numeros primos spectantibus", appeared in Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae 6 (1738), pp. 103–107. The publication note on page one of the translation distinguishes presentation from publication. The linked translation is version three, dated 15 April 2008.

English translation: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0501118](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0501118)

Catalogue record: [https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0501118](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0501118)

Cited passages: pp. 1–2, Euler's account of Fermat's proposed prime pattern and the factor $641$ of $2^{32}+1$; p. 1 publication and translator note. The short modular check in [Fermat number](fermat-number.md) is presented as a modern verification, not a historical reconstruction of Euler's search.
