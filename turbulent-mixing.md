# Turbulent mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

Turbulent mixing redistributes a transported scalar, such as temperature or particle [concentration](concentration.md), through irregular fluid motions and eventual molecular diffusion. A [top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md) assumes this mixing keeps the scalar approximately uniform within a selected cross section; that uniformity is an additional closure, not a consequence of thin geometry alone.
