# Small-angle width of nematic orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md)

For a narrow [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md) orientation distribution about its unoriented [nematic director](nematic-director.md), expand $S=\langle(3\cos^2\theta-1)/2\rangle=1-3\langle\theta^2\rangle/2+O(\langle\theta^4\rangle)$. This proves the displayed root-mean-square width. It does not determine the full distribution or a full width at half maximum. If the two small transverse tilt components are independent equal-variance Gaussians with variance $\sigma^2$, then $\langle\theta^2\rangle\simeq2\sigma^2$, so $\sigma\simeq\sqrt{(1-S)/3}$.
