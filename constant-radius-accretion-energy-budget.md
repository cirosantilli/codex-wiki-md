# Constant-radius accretion energy budget

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion luminosity](accretion-luminosity.md)

Integrating $L_{\rm acc}=GM\dot M/r_0$ at fixed radius gives this result. With negligible seed [mass](mass.md) it supplies the radiative requirement of monatomic cloud collapse only if $\Omega_f=-GM^2/r_0$ under the adopted zero-initial-energy approximation. A different density-profile coefficient, retained [accretion](accretion.md) heat, a wind or a changing radius modifies the budget. Positive binding-energy magnitude and negative potential [energy](energy-split.md) must not be interchanged silently.
