# Positive-frequency spectral normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

For a real [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md), restricting its even angular-frequency density $f$ to $[0,\pi]$ gives $\gamma(k)=2\int_0^\pi f(\omega)\cos(k\omega)\,d\omega$. Folding both frequency halves into one density gives $g=2f$ and removes that factor two. Consequently [white noise](white-noise.md) of variance $v$ has restricted density $v/(2\pi)$ and folded density $v/\pi$.
