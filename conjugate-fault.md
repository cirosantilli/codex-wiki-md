# Conjugate fault

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

One of a pair of [fault](geological-fault.md) orientations that can form under a common [principal stress](principal-stress.md) field with opposite senses of resolved [shear stress](shear-stress.md). For newly formed, optimally oriented [faults](geological-fault.md) obeying the same [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md), their acute intersection angle is $90^\circ-\phi$, where $\phi$ is the [friction angle](friction-angle.md). The maximum compressive [principal stress](principal-stress.md) bisects that acute angle. This permits an estimate $\mu=\tan\phi$ only under the common-formation and optimal-orientation assumptions, rather than for arbitrary inherited [faults](geological-fault.md).
