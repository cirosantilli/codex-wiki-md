<h1 id="bright-s-disease">Bright's disease</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nephritis](nephritis.md)

Historical category of [kidney](kidney.md) disease associated with [albuminuria](albuminuria.md) and often [oedema](oedema.md), named after [Richard Bright](richard-bright.md).
