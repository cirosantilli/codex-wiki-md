# Divergence of a curl is zero

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable [vector field](vector-field.md), commuting partial derivatives and antisymmetry of the Levi-Civita symbol give

$$
\nabla\mathbin\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)=0.
$$
