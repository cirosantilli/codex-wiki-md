# Knaster forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md)

Every uncountable [subset](subset.md) of a Knaster [forcing](forcing-split.md) has an uncountable pairwise compatible [subset](subset.md). The property implies the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md). Finite [Cohen forcing](cohen-forcing.md) has this property by the [delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md) and agreement on the finite root. A Knaster [forcing](forcing-split.md) times any [CCC](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) [forcing](forcing-split.md) is [CCC](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md)
