# Roche-lobe-filling period-density relation

↑ **Parent:** [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md)

If $R_2=R_L\simeq Ca(M_2/M)^{1/3}$, [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) gives

$$
P=\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt{GC^3}}\left(\frac{R_2^3}{M_2}\right)^{1/2}.
$$

Thus a [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md)-filling star's period measures its inverse square-root mean [mass density](density.md), largely independently of the companion mass in this approximation. A local [mass-radius relation](mass-radius-relation.md) $R_2\propto M_2^\zeta$ gives $P\propto M_2^{(3\zeta-1)/2}$.
