# Semistandard Young tableau

↑ **Parent:** [Young tableau](young-tableau.md)

A semistandard Young tableau fills a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) with positive [integers](integer.md), permitting repeated entries, so that entries weakly increase along rows and strictly increase down columns. Its content records the number of occurrences of each entry. The [Kostka number](kostka-number.md) $K_{\lambda\mu}$ counts such fillings of shape $\lambda$ and content $\mu$.
