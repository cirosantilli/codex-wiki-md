# Direct summand

↑ **Parent:** [Direct sum](direct-sum.md)

A [submodule](submodule.md) $N$ of a [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ is a direct summand if some [submodule](submodule.md) $L$ gives $M=N\oplus L$. Equivalently, the inclusion has a left inverse [R-module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md), or $N$ is the image of an [idempotent](idempotent.md) [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of $M$.
