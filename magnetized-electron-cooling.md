# Magnetized electron cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Electron cooling](electron-cooling.md)

Strong longitudinal magnetic guidance can average rapid [electron](electron.md) gyro-motion during relevant ion-electron interactions. The effective [ion](ion.md) equilibrium scale can then be much lower than the transverse [electron](electron.md) gyro-temperature, subject to gyroradius, collision-scale and beam-quality limits.
