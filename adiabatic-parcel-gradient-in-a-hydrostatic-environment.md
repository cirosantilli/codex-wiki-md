# Adiabatic parcel gradient in a hydrostatic environment

↑ **Parent:** [Dry adiabatic lapse rate](dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md)

A dry parcel in rapid pressure balance with an ambient [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) has $dT_g/dP=T_g\mathcal R/(PC_p)$. Ambient [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $dP/dz=-Pg/(\mathcal RT_{\rm env})$, hence $dT_g/dz=-(g/C_p)T_g/T_{\rm env}$. At a temperature-matched launch point this equals the [dry adiabatic lapse rate](dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md), supplying the local linear stability test. A substantially hotter parcel does not exactly follow the ambient density's hydrostatic adiabat.
