# Inverse-energy stopping-power model

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping power](stopping-power.md)

For an ideal particle energy-loss law $dE/dx=-k/E$, integration gives $E(x)=\sqrt{E_0^2-2kx}$ up to the formal range. [Stopping power](stopping-power.md) grows as the endpoint is approached but its integral remains finite. This simple law does not describe endpoint broadening, scattering or every low-energy stopping mechanism.
