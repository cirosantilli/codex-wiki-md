# Gibbs free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbs_free_energy)

The Gibbs free energy is $G=E-TS+pV$. For a simple system of fixed composition,

$$
dG=-S\,dT+V\,dp.
$$
