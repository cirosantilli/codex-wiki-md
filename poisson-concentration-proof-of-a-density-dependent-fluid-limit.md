# Poisson concentration proof of a density-dependent fluid limit

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid limit](fluid-limit.md)

If the rate functions are bounded and [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) on an invariant compact state space, the [Poisson time-change representation of a Markov chain](poisson-time-change-representation-of-a-markov-chain.md) has drift $F=\sum_jv_j\beta_j$. Each centered Poisson term is uniformly small after division by $n$. The [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) then bounds the distance to $\dot z=F(z)$ by the initial error and the centered counting error, multiplied by $e^{LT}$. A Poisson maximal concentration bound gives exponentially decreasing error probabilities for a fixed horizon and fixed error tolerance.
