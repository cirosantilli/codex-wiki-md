# Higman group as an amalgam of iterated HNN extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Higman group](higman-group.md)

The two three-generator components on $\{a,b,c\}$ and $\{c,d,a\}$ are iterated [HNN extensions](hnn-extension.md) of infinite cyclic groups. The subgroup on $\{a,c\}$ is free of rank two in each component by [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md). Amalgamating over these free subgroups gives the displayed infinite [finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md). Its cyclic squaring relations forbid every nontrivial finite quotient by the least-prime multiplicative-order argument.
