# Centralizer of a Lie subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md)

The centralizer of a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak a$ is the intersection of the [centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra](centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md) as that element ranges over $\mathfrak a$. It is a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) and a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md): for $x,y$ commuting with $\mathfrak a$, the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) gives $[[x,y],a]=[x,[y,a]]-[y,[x,a]]=0$. It is contained in the [normalizer of a Lie subalgebra](normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). If $\mathfrak a$ is an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md), it is contained in its own centralizer.
