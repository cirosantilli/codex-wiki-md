# Singular integral equation

↑ **Parent:** [Singular integral operator](singular-integral-operator.md)

An integral equation with a kernel singular on the integration [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) needs a prescribed limiting interpretation such as a [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md). For the Cauchy kernel, the [Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem](sokhotski-plemelj-theorem.md) converts a linear scalar equation into a [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md) for its two analytic boundary values. Normalization, endpoint regularity and the index of that jump determine homogeneous freedom and solvability.
