# Skolem hull

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolem_hull)

After choosing a Skolem function for each existential formula, the Skolem hull of $A\subseteq N$ is the smallest subset containing $A$ and closed under those functions. The [Tarski-Vaught test](tarski-vaught-test.md) makes it an [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md) of $N$, and in a countable language an infinite hull has cardinality at most $|A|+\aleph_0$.
