# Six-point matching geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

The complete graph on six points has fifteen edges and fifteen perfect matchings. Its six one-factorizations organize a dual incidence geometry exchanging points with factorizations and edges with matchings. The classical names are [duads](duad.md), [synthemes](syntheme.md) and [totals of synthemes](total-of-synthemes.md). This geometry supports [duad-syntheme duality on six points](duad-syntheme-duality-on-six-points.md) and a construction of the [small Witt design](small-witt-design.md).
