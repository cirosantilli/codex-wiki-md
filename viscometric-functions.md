# Viscometric functions

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

The three viscometric functions describe steady homogeneous [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md): its [shear viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) and the first and second [normal-stress differences](normal-stress-difference.md) per squared shear rate. Take x as the flow direction, y as the gradient direction and z as the vorticity direction, so $N_1=\sigma_{xx}-\sigma_{yy}$ and $N_2=\sigma_{yy}-\sigma_{zz}$. Isotropic pressure drops out of these differences.
