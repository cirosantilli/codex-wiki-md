# Phytochrome

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytochrome)

A family of light-sensing [proteins](protein-split.md) with interconvertible red-absorbing and far-red-absorbing forms. [Phytochrome](phytochrome.md) links [light](light.md) perception to [plant](plant.md) development, including the [lettuce red and far-red germination experiment](lettuce-red-and-far-red-germination-experiment.md).[Biology 2e, §30.6 The Phytochrome System and the Red/Far-Red Response, Figure 30.38](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
