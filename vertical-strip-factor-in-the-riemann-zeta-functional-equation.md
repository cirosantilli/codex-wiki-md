# Vertical-strip factor in the Riemann zeta functional equation

↑ **Parent:** [Functional equation of the Riemann zeta function](functional-equation-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md)

Writing

$$
\zeta(s)=\chi(s)\zeta(1-s),
\qquad
\chi(s)=2^s\pi^{s-1}\sin(\pi s/2)\Gamma(1-s),
$$

the [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md) gives, uniformly on every fixed vertical strip and for large $|t|$,

$$
|\chi(\sigma+it)|\asymp |t|^{1/2-\sigma}.
$$
