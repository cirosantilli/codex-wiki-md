# Unique factorization of ideals in a Dedekind domain

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md)

Every nonzero proper [ideal](ideal.md) of a [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md) has a unique factorization, up to reordering, as a finite product of positive powers of distinct nonzero [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md). The unit [ideal](ideal.md) is the empty product. This is distinct from unique factorization of elements.
