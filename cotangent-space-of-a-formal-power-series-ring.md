# Cotangent space of a formal power series ring

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series over a local ring](formal-power-series-over-a-local-ring.md)

For a [local ring](local-ring.md) $(A,\mathfrak m)$ with [residue field](residue-field.md) $k$, put $B=A[[x]]$ and $I=\mathfrak mB+xB$. There is a natural $k$-[vector space](vector-space-split.md) [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) $I/I^2\cong(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2)\oplus k$. The inverse sends $(\bar a,\bar b)$ to the class of $a+bx$. It is well-defined because changing $a$ by $\mathfrak m^2$ or $b$ by $\mathfrak m$ changes the result by $I^2$. It is [surjective](surjective-function.md) since every $f\in I$ is congruent modulo $x^2B\subseteq I^2$ to its constant and linear terms. As $I^2=\mathfrak m^2B+x\mathfrak mB+x^2B$, those coefficients of an element of $I^2$ lie in $\mathfrak m^2$ and $\mathfrak m$, respectively. This proves injectivity. Thus adjoining one formal variable increases finite [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) by one.
