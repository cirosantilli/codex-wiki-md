# Isotherm

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal process](isothermal-process.md)

An [isotherm](isotherm.md) is a curve of equilibrium states at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), often drawn in the pressure-volume plane. For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) of fixed amount it obeys $pV=\mathrm{constant}$. The curve is not itself a specification of the rate, reversibility or experimental mechanism by which the states are visited.
