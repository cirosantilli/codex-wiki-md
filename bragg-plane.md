# Bragg plane

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md)

For free-electron dispersion proportional to $k^2$, states at $\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf k-\mathbf G$ are degenerate on this plane, obtained by equating their squared norms. A nonzero periodic-potential Fourier component can mix them and open an avoided crossing in the [nearly-free electron model](nearly-free-electron-model.md). First-zone boundaries are selected planes of this kind.
