# Low-degree Fourier mass of a sparse Boolean set

↑ **Parent:** [Beckner's inequality](beckner-s-inequality.md)

For a subset of the unbiased [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) of density $\alpha$, use normalized [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md) coefficients. Applying [Beckner's inequality](beckner-s-inequality.md) with $\rho=1/\sqrt3$ gives $\|T_\rho1_A\|_2^2\leq\|1_A\|_{4/3}^2=\alpha^{3/2}$. On levels at most two the weights $\rho^{2|S|}$ are at least $1/9$, giving the displayed bound. [Parseval's identity](parseval-identity.md) gives total Fourier mass $\alpha$, so when $9\sqrt\alpha<1/2$ the higher levels contain strictly more than the lower levels.
