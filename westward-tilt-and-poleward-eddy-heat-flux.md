# Westward tilt and poleward eddy heat flux

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md)

For a disturbance proportional to $\operatorname{Re}[a(z)e^{i\phi(z)}e^{ik(x-ct)}]$, westward phase tilt means $\phi_z>0$ for $k>0$. With $v'=\psi'_x$ and $b'=f_0\psi'_z$, a zonal average gives the displayed positive flux in the northern hemisphere, up to any meridional amplitude and time-growth factors. When the background buoyancy decreases northward, this flux extracts mean [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md) and supports growing eddies.
