# Comparison of stable maps and cohomology operations

↑ **Parent:** [Stable cohomology operation](stable-cohomology-operation.md)

For cellular [Omega-spectra](omega-spectrum.md) $D,E$, natural degree-preserving additive operations $D^*\to E^*$ commuting with suspension are compatible families $[D_j,E_j]_*$ by the [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md). Since $D\simeq\operatorname{hocolim}_j\Sigma^{-j}\Sigma^\infty D_j$, the [Milnor exact sequence for maps of spectra](milnor-exact-sequence-for-maps-of-spectra.md) realizes every such family. Its kernel consists precisely of [hyperphantom maps of spectra](hyperphantom-map-of-spectra.md). Thus operations determine stable maps only modulo that kernel.
