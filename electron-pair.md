# Electron pair

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_pair)

An [electron pair](electron-pair.md) consists of two [electrons](electron.md) with opposite [spins](spin.md) sharing the same spatial part of their [quantum state](quantum-state.md). The [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) distinction between a [bond pair](bond-pair.md) and a [lone pair](lone-pair.md) records whether that pair is assigned to a connection between [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) or to one [chemical atom](atom-physics.md).
