# Surface current density

↑ **Parent:** [Current density](current-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_current_density)

A surface current density is electric current per unit transverse length confined to a surface. Integrating a thin layer's [current density](current-density.md) through its thickness gives $\mathbf K$.
