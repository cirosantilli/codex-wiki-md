# Nonrelativistic particle

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

A massive particle is nonrelativistic when its speed is small compared with the speed of light. Its energy expansion is $E=mc^2+p^2/(2m)+O(p^4/(m^3c^2))$. The leading momentum-dependent term is the classical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
