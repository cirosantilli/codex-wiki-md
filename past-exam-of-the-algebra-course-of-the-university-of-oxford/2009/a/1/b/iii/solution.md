<h1 id="a/1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For equal irreducible [group representations](../../../../../../group-representation.md), the intertwining identity and the complex [Schur's lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) give $T=cI_n$. Conjugation preserves [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md), so

$$
\operatorname{tr}T=\sum_{g\in G}\operatorname{tr}(D_1(g)BD_1(g)^{-1})=|G|\operatorname{tr}B.
$$

Since $\operatorname{tr}(cI_n)=nc$, we obtain

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{|G|\operatorname{tr}B}{n}I_n.}
$$
