↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The preceding intertwining identity makes $T$ a homomorphism between the corresponding [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md). By [Schur's lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md), a nonzero such homomorphism would be an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md). The representations are inequivalent, so this is impossible. Therefore

$$
\boxed{T=0.}
$$
