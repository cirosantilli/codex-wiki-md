↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $h\in G$, right-multiplying by $D_2(h)^{-1}$ gives

$$
D_1(h)TD_2(h)^{-1}=\sum_{g\in G}D_1(hg)B D_2(g^{-1}h^{-1}).
$$

Make the bijective substitution $k=hg$, so $g^{-1}h^{-1}=k^{-1}$. The sum becomes $\sum_kD_1(k)BD_2(k^{-1})=T$. Hence

$$
\boxed{D_1(h)T=TD_2(h)\quad(h\in G).}
$$

Thus $T$ is an [intertwining operator](../../../../../../intertwining-operator.md) from the second [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) to the first. This is the [Schur averaging of rectangular matrices](../../../../../../schur-averaging-of-rectangular-matrices.md) construction.
