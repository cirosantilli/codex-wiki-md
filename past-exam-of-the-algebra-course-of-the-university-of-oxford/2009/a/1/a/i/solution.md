<h1 id="a/1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a finite-dimensional [algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md), every [simple module](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) $S$ is finite-dimensional: choose $s\ne0$; simplicity makes $A\to S$, $a\mapsto as$, surjective, so $\dim S\leq\dim A$.

The general [Schur's lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) says that a homomorphism $f:M\to N$ between [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) over any unital [ring](../../../../../../ring.md) is either zero or an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md). Indeed, $\ker f$ is a [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) of $M$ and $\operatorname{im}f$ is a [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) of $N$. If $f\ne0$, simplicity gives $\ker f=0$ and $\operatorname{im}f=N$. In particular, $\operatorname{End}_R(M)$ is a [division ring](../../../../../../division-ring.md).

For a complex [algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md) and a finite-dimensional simple [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $M$, the stronger conclusion is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{End}_R(M)=\mathbb C\,\mathrm{id}_M.}
$$

For $f\in\operatorname{End}_R(M)$, the [fundamental theorem of algebra](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md) gives an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda\in\mathbb C$. The endomorphism $f-\lambda I$ has nonzero [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), so the general [Schur's lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) forces it to be zero. Consequently a homomorphism between two such simple [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is zero if they are nonisomorphic, and their homomorphism space is one-dimensional if they are isomorphic.
