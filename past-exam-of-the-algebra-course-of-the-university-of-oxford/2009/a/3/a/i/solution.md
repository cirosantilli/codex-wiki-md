A [simple module](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) is nonzero and has no proper nonzero [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md). A [composition series of a module](../../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md) is a finite strict chain $0=V_0<V_1<\cdots<V_n=V$ of [submodules](../../../../../../submodule.md) with simple quotients $V_i/V_{i-1}$. These quotients are the [composition factors](../../../../../../composition-factor.md) of the series.

The [Jordan–Hölder theorem for modules](../../../../../../jordan-holder-theorem-for-modules.md) says that if a [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has a composition series, then any two such series have the same length and the same simple factors up to [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) and reordering. In particular, every finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has a well-defined multiset of composition factors.
