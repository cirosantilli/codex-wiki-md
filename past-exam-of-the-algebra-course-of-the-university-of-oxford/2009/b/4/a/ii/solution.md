<h1 id="b/4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The map $g\mapsto g^{-1}$ partitions $S$ into disjoint two-element sets $\{g,g^{-1}\}$, so $|S|$ is even. Since $|G|$ is even, $|G\setminus S|$ is even as well. This complement contains the identity and consists of elements with $g^2=e$. It therefore contains at least one further element $h\ne e$, and that element has order two. Thus **every finite group of even order contains an involution**, without using [Cauchy theorem for groups](../../../../../../cauchy-theorem-for-groups.md).
