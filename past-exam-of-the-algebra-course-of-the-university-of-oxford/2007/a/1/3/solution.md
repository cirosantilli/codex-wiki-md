<h1 id="a/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Coordinatewise multiplication gives $V=A(1,1,0)$, so **the third [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is cyclic**.

A [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) of a [cyclic module](../../../../../cyclic-module.md) need not be cyclic. In the second example the first-row space is a [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md) and hence a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) of the regular [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $A$. The regular [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is cyclic, generated by $1_A$, whereas its first-row [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) has just been shown not to be cyclic. Thus **the proposed inheritance property fails even for a finite-dimensional [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md)**.
