↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A left [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) over the unital $K$-[algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md) $A$ is a $K$-[vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ with a $K$-bilinear action $A\times V\to V$ satisfying $(ab)v=a(bv)$ and $1v=v$. For a vector $v$, the map $A\to V$, $a\mapsto av$, is an $A$-[module homomorphism](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md). Its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\operatorname{Ann}(v)$; explicitly this is a [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) and $b(av)=(ba)v=0$ for $a\in\operatorname{Ann}(v)$, so it is a [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md).

If $V=Av$, this homomorphism is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), and the [module isomorphism](../../../../../module-isomorphism.md) theorem gives $V\cong A/\operatorname{Ann}(v)$. Conversely the quotient by a [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md) is generated by $1+I$. Thus **[cyclic modules](../../../../../cyclic-module.md) are exactly the quotients $A/I$ by [left ideals](../../../../../left-ideal.md)**.

For the first example set $e=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$. Right multiplication by $e$ keeps the first two columns and zeroes the third. Every [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) with third column zero equals its product with $e$. Hence **$V=Ae$ is cyclic**, generated by $e$.
