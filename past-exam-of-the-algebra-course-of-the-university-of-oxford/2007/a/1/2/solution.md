<h1 id="a/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For a [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $v$ supported in its first row and any [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) $a$, multiplication gives $av=a_{11}v$. Thus $Av=Kv$ has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) at most one for every $v$, whereas the entire [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) three. Hence **this [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is not cyclic**, over every [field](../../../../../field.md) $K$.
