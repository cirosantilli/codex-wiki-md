<h1 id="a/4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the cyclic quotient $R/(d)$, the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is formed by residue classes of [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $h$ such that $d\mid Xh$. If $X\nmid d$, coprimality forces $d\mid h$, so the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is zero. If $d=Xe$, divisibility is equivalent to $e\mid h$, and the residue classes of multiples of $e$ modulo $Xe$ form a one-dimensional $K$-space. Hence

$$
\boxed{\dim\ker\alpha=\begin{cases}0,&d(0)\ne0,\\1,&d(0)=0.\end{cases}}
$$

This too is immediately readable in the cyclic case, even if zero is a repeated root. The zero-dimensional [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) zero separately.
