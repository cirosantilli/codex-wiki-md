<h1 id="a/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) in $K$ are precisely the roots in $K$ of the companion [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $d$; over an [algebraic closure](../../../../../algebraic-closure.md) take all its roots. Thus the form determines their root set, but **an explicit list generally requires factoring or solving $d$ and cannot be read off by inspection**. This is the relevant distinction in the qualification “with no further calculations”. Over a general [field](../../../../../field.md) the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) may have no roots in $K$ at all, and its diagonal entries are not its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). If “determinable” means only uniquely specified rather than immediately readable, all the information is of course fixed by the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md), including this root set.
