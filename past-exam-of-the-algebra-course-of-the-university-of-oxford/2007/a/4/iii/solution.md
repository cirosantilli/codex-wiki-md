<h1 id="a/4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $d(X)=X^n+a_{n-1}X^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0$, the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of its [companion matrix](../../../../../companion-matrix.md) is $(-1)^na_0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha\text{ invertible}\iff a_0\ne0.}
$$

Thus **invertibility is readable from the constant coefficient**, with no factorization or elimination.
