<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every finite-dimensional $\mathbb C[X]$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is a finitely generated torsion [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) over the [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) $\mathbb C[X]$. The [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](../../../../../structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md) gives

$$
M\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^r\mathbb C[X]/(p_j),\qquad
p_1\mid p_2\mid\cdots\mid p_r,
$$

where the $p_j$ are uniquely determined monic nonconstant [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Equivalently, factoring over $\mathbb C$ and using the [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{M\cong\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\mathbb C}\ \bigoplus_j
\mathbb C[X]/((X-\lambda)^{m_{\lambda j}}).}
$$

There are finitely many summands, and the multisets of positive integers $m_{\lambda j}$ are uniquely determined. Each displayed primary cyclic summand is an [indecomposable module](../../../../../indecomposable-module.md).
