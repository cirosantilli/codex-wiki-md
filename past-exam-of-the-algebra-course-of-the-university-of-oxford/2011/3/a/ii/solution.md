<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the decomposition to the [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) on which $X$ acts as $\alpha$. On $\mathbb C[X]/((X-\lambda)^m)$ put $t=X-\lambda$ and use the ordered [basis](../../../../../basis.md)

$$
t^{m-1},\ t^{m-2},\ldots,\ t,\ 1.
$$

Multiplication by $X=\lambda+t$ has diagonal entries $\lambda$ and superdiagonal entries $1$, so its [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is the [Jordan block](../../../../../jordan-block.md) $J_m(\lambda)$. Concatenating the bases of the [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md) gives a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $V$ in which

$$
\boxed{[\alpha]=\bigoplus_{\lambda,j}J_{m_{\lambda j}}(\lambda).}
$$

This proves existence of [Jordan normal form](../../../../../jordan-normal-form.md); uniqueness of the primary cyclic decomposition also proves uniqueness of its blocks up to order.
