<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the same [basis](../../../../../basis.md), $Y=D^2$ kills $v,u$ and sends $w$ to $16w$. Explicitly,

$$
D^2=\begin{pmatrix}7&4&5\\7&4&5\\7&4&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus all three [basis](../../../../../basis.md) lines are [submodules](../../../../../submodule.md) after [restriction of scalars](../../../../../restriction-of-scalars.md), and

$$
\boxed{{}_BM\cong B/(Y)\oplus B/(Y)\oplus B/(Y-16).}
$$

Each summand is a [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md), hence an [indecomposable module](../../../../../indecomposable-module.md). Squaring has destroyed the length-two [nilpotent](../../../../../nilpotent.md) [Jordan chain](../../../../../jordan-chain.md).
