<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The universal property of the [polynomial ring](../../../../polynomial-ring.md) gives the unique complex [algebra homomorphism](../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md)

$$
\iota:\mathbb C[Y]\longrightarrow\mathbb C[X],\qquad
\sum_jc_jY^j\longmapsto\sum_jc_jX^{2j}.
$$

Distinct monomials $X^{2j}$ are linearly independent, so $\iota$ is injective. [Restriction of scalars](../../../../restriction-of-scalars.md) defines $b\cdot m=\iota(b)m$ for $b\in B$ and $m\in M$; its [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) axioms follow from those of the original action and the [algebra homomorphism](../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md). In particular **$Y$ acts as the square of the operator defining the $X$ action**.
