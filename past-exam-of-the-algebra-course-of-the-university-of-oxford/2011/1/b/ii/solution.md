**No.** Take $A=M_2(K)$ and its diagonal [subalgebra](../../../../../subalgebra.md) $B$. The [algebra over a field](../../../../../algebra-over-a-field-split.md) $B$ is commutative, so $Z(B)=B$. But $e_{11}\in B$ does not commute with $e_{12}$:

$$
e_{11}e_{12}=e_{12},\qquad e_{12}e_{11}=0.
$$

Hence $e_{11}\notin Z(A)$, although $e_{11}\in Z(B)$. Commutation inside a [subalgebra](../../../../../subalgebra.md) places no condition on elements outside it.
