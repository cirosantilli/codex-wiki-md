↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the usual unital convention for an [algebra over a field](../../../../../algebra-over-a-field-split.md). For $a,b,x\in A$ and $c\in K$,

$$
\phi_{a+cb}(x)=\phi_a(x)+c\phi_b(x),\qquad
\phi_{ab}(x)=a(bx)=(\phi_a\circ\phi_b)(x),\qquad \phi_1(x)=x.
$$

Passing to matrices in one fixed [basis](../../../../../basis.md) preserves these identities: $M_{a+cb}=M_a+cM_b$, $M_{ab}=M_aM_b$ and $M_1=I$. Thus $\psi$ is an [algebra homomorphism](../../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md). If $M_a=0$, evaluating $\phi_a$ at $1_A$ gives $a=0$. Its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is zero, so **the left regular representation is faithful**.
