<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

All 63 [involutions](../../../../../involution.md) lie in [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) for $2$. Each such [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) has exactly seven nonidentity elements and distinct ones have no nonidentity overlap. Hence

$$
\boxed{n_2=63/7=9.}
$$
