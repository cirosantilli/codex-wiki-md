<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) $S,T$ share $t\ne1$, they are abelian by the preceding deduction, so $S,T\leq C_G(t)$. But $|C_G(t)|=8$ and both [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) have order eight. Thus $S=C_G(t)=T$. Consequently **distinct Sylow $2$-[subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) intersect only in the identity**.
