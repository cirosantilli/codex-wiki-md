↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) for $2$ has order eight. Every one of its nonidentity elements lies in the only even-order class, so has order two. In a [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of exponent two,

$$
xy=(xy)^{-1}=y^{-1}x^{-1}=yx.
$$

It is therefore an [elementary abelian group](../../../../../elementary-abelian-group.md) and $\boxed{S\cong C_2^3}$.
