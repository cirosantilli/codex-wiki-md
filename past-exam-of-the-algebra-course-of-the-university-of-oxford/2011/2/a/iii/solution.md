<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the [Artin–Wedderburn theorem](../../../../../artin-wedderburn-theorem.md), a finite-dimensional complex [simple algebra](../../../../../simple-algebra.md) is $M_d(\mathbb C)$, with a unique [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md) up to isomorphism, namely the column [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $S=\mathbb C^d$. Its [left regular module](../../../../../left-regular-module.md) is $S^{\oplus d}$. The [Schur lemma](../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) gives $\operatorname{End}_A(S)=\mathbb C$, so

$$
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}V_M=d=\dim_{\mathbb C}M=\sqrt{\dim_{\mathbb C}A}.}
$$

The right action can be seen explicitly. For a row vector $r$, the map $f_r(m)=mr$ is $A$-linear, and all such maps are obtained this way, since the row space and $V_M$ both have [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $d$. Moreover $f_r\cdot x=f_{rx}$. Any nonzero row generates the whole row space under right multiplication by matrices: if $r_j\ne0$, suitable [matrix units](../../../../../matrix-unit.md) send it to each coordinate row. Thus **$V_M$ is a simple right $A$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md)**.
