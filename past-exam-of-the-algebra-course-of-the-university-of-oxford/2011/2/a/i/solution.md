<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](../../../../../jordan-holder-theorem.md) applies to a [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) of finite length: if

$$
0=M_0\subsetneq M_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq M_r=M
$$

is a [composition series of a module](../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md), its successive quotients are [simple modules](../../../../../irreducible-module.md). Any two such [composition series of a module](../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md) have the same length $r$, and their [composition factors](../../../../../composition-factor.md) are pairwise isomorphic after a permutation, with multiplicities preserved. Every finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) over $A$ has finite length, since strict inclusions increase vector-space [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). A finite-dimensional [algebra over a field](../../../../../algebra-over-a-field-split.md) does not by itself make every possible [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) finite-dimensional, so the finite-length hypothesis should be retained in the statement.
