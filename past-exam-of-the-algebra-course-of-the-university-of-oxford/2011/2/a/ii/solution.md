<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $a\in A$, $m\in M$,

$$
(f\cdot x)(am)=f(am)x=af(m)x=a(f\cdot x)(m).
$$

Additivity and complex linearity follow from those of $f$ and multiplication in $A$, so $f\cdot x$ is an [module homomorphism](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md). In particular the action stays inside $V$. Associativity gives

$$
((f\cdot x)\cdot y)(m)=f(m)xy=(f\cdot(xy))(m),\qquad (f\cdot1)(m)=f(m).
$$

Both distributive laws hold pointwise. These are exactly the axioms for a [right module](../../../../../right-module.md), proving **$\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,A)$ is a right $A$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md)**.
