<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose bases $S_1=\mathbb Cs_1$, $S_2=\mathbb Cs_2$ and set

$$
\begin{pmatrix}r&s\\0&t\end{pmatrix}s_1=rs_1,
\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}r&s\\0&t\end{pmatrix}s_2=ts_2.
$$

Both are [simple modules](../../../../../irreducible-module.md). They are nonisomorphic because $e_{11}$ acts as the identity on $S_1$ and as zero on $S_2$. In the ascending [composition series of a module](../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md) above, the factors are, in order,

$$
\boxed{S_1,\ S_1,\ S_2.}
$$

The last factor is represented by $e_{22}$ modulo the span of $e_{11},e_{12}$.
