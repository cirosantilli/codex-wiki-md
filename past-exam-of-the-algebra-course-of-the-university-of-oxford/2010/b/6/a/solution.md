A complex [character of a representation](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of a finite [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ is the function

$$
\boxed{\chi(g)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(g)),}
$$

where $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ is a finite-dimensional complex [group representation](../../../../../group-representation.md). The [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is a [class function](../../../../../class-function.md) because [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is unchanged by conjugation. Its degree is $\chi(1)=\dim V$.
