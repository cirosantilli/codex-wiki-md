<h1 id="b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By the [Maschke theorem](../../../../../maschke-s-theorem.md), the restricted [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) is a direct sum of irreducible $H$-[modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md). The [row orthogonality relations for a character table](../../../../../row-orthogonality-relations-for-a-character-table.md) identify the multiplicity of $\psi_i$ as $d_i=\langle\chi\mathord\downarrow_H,\psi_i\rangle_H$. Therefore

$$
\chi\mathord\downarrow_H=\sum_i d_i\psi_i,\qquad d_i\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}.
$$

Using the preceding [character inner product](../../../../../character-inner-product.md) identity and the irreducibility of $\chi$,

$$
\sum_i d_i^2=\frac1{|H|}\sum_{h\in H}|\chi(h)|^2\leq\frac1{|H|}\sum_{g\in G}|\chi(g)|^2=\frac{|G|}{|H|}.
$$

This is the [character restriction norm bound](../../../../../character-restriction-norm-bound.md). When $[G:H]=2$, the nonzero sum of squares of integer multiplicities is either one or two. Hence **the restriction is irreducible, or is the sum of two distinct [irreducible characters](../../../../../irreducible-character.md)**, each with multiplicity one. This proves the requested [index-two character restriction dichotomy](../../../../../index-two-character-restriction-dichotomy.md) directly; normality of an index-two subgroup is a general [group theory](../../../../../group-theory-split.md) fact.
