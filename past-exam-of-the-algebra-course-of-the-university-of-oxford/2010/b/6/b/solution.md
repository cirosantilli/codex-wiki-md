↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [row orthogonality relations for a character table](../../../../../row-orthogonality-relations-for-a-character-table.md) assert that distinct complex [irreducible characters](../../../../../irreducible-character.md) are orthogonal and each has squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) one:

$$
\langle\chi_i,\chi_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}.
$$

Using the linearity and conjugate-linearity of the [character inner product](../../../../../character-inner-product.md),

$$
\langle\psi,\psi\rangle=\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\overline{\lambda_j}\langle\chi_i,\chi_j\rangle=\sum_i|\lambda_i|^2.
$$

Since the multiplicities are nonnegative integers, this is

$$
\boxed{\langle\psi,\psi\rangle=\sum_i\lambda_i^2.}
$$
