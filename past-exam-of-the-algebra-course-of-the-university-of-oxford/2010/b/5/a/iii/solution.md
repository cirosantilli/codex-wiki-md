<h1 id="b/5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the free [Abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md)

$$
\boxed{G=\mathbb Z^m.}
$$

Its coordinate flag $0<\mathbb Z<\mathbb Z^2<\cdots<\mathbb Z^m$ is a [subnormal series](../../../../../../subnormal-series.md) of length $m$, with every factor isomorphic to the infinite [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z$. Hence it has exactly $m$ infinite cyclic factors.
