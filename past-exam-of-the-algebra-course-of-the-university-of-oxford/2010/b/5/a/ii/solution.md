<h1 id="b/5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{G=S_3\times C_2^{m-2}\quad(m\geq2).}
$$

Start with $1<A_3<S_3$, whose factors are $C_3$ and $C_2$, and then append the $m-2$ coordinate factors of the elementary [Abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) $C_2^{m-2}$. More explicitly, append $S_3\times C_2^j$ for $1\leq j\leq m-2$. Each subgroup is normal in the next, and the factors consist of one $C_3$ and $m-1$ copies of $C_2$. Thus there are exactly $m$ finite cyclic factors. The [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is non-[Abelian](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) because it contains $S_3$.
