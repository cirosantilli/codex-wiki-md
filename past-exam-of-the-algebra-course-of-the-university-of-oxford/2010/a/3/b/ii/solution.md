<h1 id="a/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $w=\sum_{g\in G}g$. This is nonzero since its [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) coefficients are all one, and $gw=w$ for every $g\in G$. Hence $kw$ is a one-dimensional left ideal of $kG$.

If the regular [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is semisimple, $kw$ is a direct summand. Let $\pi:kG\to kw$ be the corresponding $kG$-linear projection, so $\pi(w)=w$. Write $\pi(1)=cw$ for $c\in k$. Then

$$
w=\pi(w)=\sum_{g\in G}\pi(g)=\sum_{g\in G}g\pi(1)=c|G|w.
$$

Because $w\ne0$, $c|G|=1$ in $k$. Thus

$$
\boxed{|G|\ne0\text{ in }k.}
$$

Together with the [Maschke theorem](../../../../../../maschke-s-theorem.md), this gives the exact [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md) criterion for a [group algebra](../../../../../../group-algebra.md).
