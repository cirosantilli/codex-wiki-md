A [semisimple module](../../../../../../semisimple-module.md) is a direct sum of [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md). A finite-dimensional [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md) is one whose left regular [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is semisimple; equivalently, all its finite-dimensional [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) are semisimple, or every [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) of a finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has a complementary [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md).

The [Maschke theorem](../../../../../../maschke-s-theorem.md) states that, for a finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ and a [field](../../../../../../field.md) $k$ with $|G|\ne0$ in $k$, every finite-dimensional $kG$-[module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is semisimple, so $kG$ is a [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md). The assumption is equivalent to saying that the [characteristic of a field](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) does not divide $|G|$. The averaging construction makes a projection onto a [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) equivariant by replacing $P$ with $|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}gPg^{-1}$.
