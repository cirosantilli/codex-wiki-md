<h1 id="a/3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** The polynomial $X^7-1$ has seven distinct roots $\zeta_7^j$ in $\mathbb C$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives

$$
\mathbb C[X]/(X^7-1)\cong\prod_{j=0}^6\mathbb C.
$$

The simple [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) are exactly the seven one-dimensional evaluation [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md), on which $X$ acts by $\zeta_7^j$. Thus there are **seven** up to [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md).
