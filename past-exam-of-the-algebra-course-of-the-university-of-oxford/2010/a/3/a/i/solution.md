The [group algebra](../../../../../../group-algebra.md) $kG$ is the [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) with [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $G$, whose elements are formal sums $\sum_{g\in G}a_gg$ with $a_g\in k$. Extend the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) multiplication bilinearly:

$$
\left(\sum_ga_gg\right)\left(\sum_hb_hh\right)=\sum_{g,h}a_gb_h(gh).
$$

This is an associative unital $k$-[algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md), with multiplicative identity the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) identity, and $\dim_k kG=|G|$.
