<h1 id="a/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $M$ be a nonzero [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md) for $A=k[X]/(X^3)$ and let $N$ be the action of $X$. Since $X$ is central, $NM$ is a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md), and $N^3=0$. If $NM=M$, iterating would give $N^3M=M$, impossible. Thus $NM=0$.

The action therefore factors through $A/(X)\cong k$. A simple [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md) is one-dimensional: the span of any nonzero vector is already a nonzero [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md). Conversely, $k$ with $X$ acting as zero is simple. Hence **there is exactly one simple [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) up to [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md)**, namely

$$
\boxed{A/(X)\cong k,\qquad X\cdot v=0.}
$$

This argument covers all simple [modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md), without assuming their finite dimensionality in advance.
