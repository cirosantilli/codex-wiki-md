A [composition series of a module](../../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md) is a finite chain

$$
0=M_0\subsetneq M_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq M_r=M
$$

of [submodules](../../../../../../submodule.md) whose factors $M_j/M_{j-1}$ are [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md). A finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) has such a series by induction on its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), choosing a maximal proper [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) at each step.

The [Jordan–Hölder theorem for modules](../../../../../../jordan-holder-theorem-for-modules.md) states that **any two [composition series of a module](../../../../../../composition-series-of-a-module.md) have the same length and the same simple factors up to [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) and reordering**. Thus the multiset of [composition factors](../../../../../../composition-factor.md) is an invariant of the [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md), even when the series itself is not unique.
