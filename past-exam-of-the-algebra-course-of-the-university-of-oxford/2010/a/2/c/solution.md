<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On a finite-dimensional $A$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $M$, the actions of $X$ and $Y$ are endomorphisms $P,Q$ satisfying $PQ-QP=I_M$. Taking [traces](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and using $\operatorname{tr}(PQ)=\operatorname{tr}(QP)$ gives

$$
0=\operatorname{tr}(PQ-QP)=\operatorname{tr}(I_M)=\dim_kM\quad\text{in }k.
$$

Since $k$ has [characteristic zero](../../../../../characteristic-zero.md), this forces $\dim_kM=0$. Thus **the zero [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is the only finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md)** for this [Weyl algebra](../../../../../weyl-algebra.md). The obstruction is the [Weyl algebra has no nonzero finite-dimensional modules](../../../../../weyl-algebra-has-no-nonzero-finite-dimensional-modules.md).
