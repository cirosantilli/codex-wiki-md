↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) factors over $\mathbb C$ as $(t-3)(t-3i)(t+3i)$ with distinct roots. The [diagonalizability criterion using the minimal polynomial](../../../../../../diagonalizability-criterion-using-the-minimal-polynomial.md) therefore makes $\alpha$ diagonalizable. Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) gives multiplicities two, one and one, so its [Jordan normal form](../../../../../../jordan-normal-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{diag}(3,3,3i,-3i),}
$$

up to reordering the one-dimensional [Jordan blocks](../../../../../../jordan-block.md).
