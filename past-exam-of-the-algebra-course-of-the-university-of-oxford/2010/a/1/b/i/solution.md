↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first block is the [companion matrix](../../../../../../companion-matrix.md) of $t-3$. The last block is the [companion matrix](../../../../../../companion-matrix.md) of $f(t)=t^3-3t^2+9t-27=(t-3)(t^2+9)$. Since $t-3$ divides $f$, these are the ordered [invariant factors of a linear operator](../../../../../../invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{m_\alpha(t)=(t-3)(t^2+9),\qquad\chi_\alpha(t)=(t-3)^2(t^2+9).}
$$

The [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is the largest invariant factor, and the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is their product.
