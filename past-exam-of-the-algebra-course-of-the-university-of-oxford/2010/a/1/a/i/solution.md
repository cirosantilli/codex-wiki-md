An $R$-[module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $M$ is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) if there are $m_1,\ldots,m_s\in M$ such that every element of $M$ has the form $r_1m_1+\cdots+r_sm_s$ with $r_i\in R$. Equivalently, **$M=Rm_1+\cdots+Rm_s$**, or $M$ is a [quotient module](../../../../../../quotient-module.md) of $R^s$.
