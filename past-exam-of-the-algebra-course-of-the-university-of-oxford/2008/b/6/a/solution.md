A [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) for the [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$ is a maximal $p$-[subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md); in a [finite group](../../../../../finite-group.md) of order $p^am$ with $p\nmid m$ it has order $p^a$. [Sylow theorems](../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) assert existence, containment of every $p$-[subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) in a Sylow $p$-[subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), conjugacy of all Sylow $p$-subgroups, and the counting conditions $n_p\mid m$, $n_p\equiv1\pmod p$.

Since $N$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), $NP$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) and

$$
[N:N\cap P]=[NP:P]\mid[G:P].
$$

The final index is [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) to $p$. Since $N\cap P$ is a $p$-[subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), its index in $N$ being [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) to $p$ means it contains the entire $p$-part of $|N|$. Hence **$N\cap P$ is Sylow in $N$**.
