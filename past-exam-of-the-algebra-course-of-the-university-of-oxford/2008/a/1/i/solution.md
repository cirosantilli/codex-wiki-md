An [algebra homomorphism](../../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) is a $K$-[linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) preserving multiplication, and preserving the unit under the unital convention. An [algebra isomorphism](../../../../../algebra-isomorphism.md) is a bijective [algebra homomorphism](../../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md); its inverse then preserves the same operations.

**A [commutative](../../../../../commutativity.md) domain [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md) may map into a [noncommutative](../../../../../noncommutativity.md) target:** take $A=K$, $B=K\times M_2(K)$, and $\varphi(\lambda)=(\lambda,\lambda I_2)$. This is even an injective unital homomorphism. The matrix factor makes the target [noncommutative](../../../../../noncommutativity.md) over every [field](../../../../../field.md), since $E_{12}E_{21}\ne E_{21}E_{12}$.
