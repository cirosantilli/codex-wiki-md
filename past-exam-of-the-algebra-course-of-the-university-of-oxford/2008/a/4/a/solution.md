↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of a finite-dimensional complex representation is $\chi_\rho(g)=\operatorname{Tr}\rho(g)$; it is irreducible if the representation is irreducible. The [character inner product](../../../../../character-inner-product.md) is

$$
(\alpha,\beta)=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}\overline{\alpha(g)}\beta(g).
$$

Distinct irreducible characters are orthogonal and each has norm one. For a [permutation representation](../../../../../permutation-representation.md), the diagonal entry of the permutation matrix at a basis vector is one exactly when that vector is fixed. Its [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) therefore counts fixed points.

Let $r$ be rotation through $\pi/2$. Let $s_x,s_y$ be reflections in the coordinate axes, and $s_d,s_{d'}$ reflections in the two diagonals. The eight values are

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
g&e&r&r^2&r^3&s_x&s_y&s_d&s_{d'}\\\hline
\chi_\Omega(g)&4&0&0&0&0&0&2&2.
\end{array}
$$

Only diagonal reflections fix vertices, two each. Hence

$$
\boxed{(1,\chi_\Omega)=\frac{4+2+2}{8}=1,\qquad(\chi_\Omega,\chi_\Omega)=\frac{16+4+4}{8}=3.}
$$
