# de Sitter horizon temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Static patch of de Sitter spacetime](static-patch-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

In units $\hbar=c=k_B=1$, the central inertial observer has temperature $1/(2\pi R)$. The [Tolman temperature law](tolman-ehrenfest-relation.md) redshifts it to the displayed static-observer value. Its acceleration satisfies $4\pi^2T^2=a^2+R^{-2}$. Near the horizon the temperature approaches $a/(2\pi)$, as predicted by the [Unruh effect](unruh-effect.md); at the center temperature remains nonzero despite vanishing acceleration.
