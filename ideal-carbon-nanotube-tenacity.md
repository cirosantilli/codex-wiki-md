# Ideal carbon nanotube tenacity

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md)

A strength estimate formed by dividing the maximum axial [force](force-split.md) supported by load-bearing carbon bonds by the tube [mass](mass.md) per length. Equal bond loading and a defect-free, fully participating shell are strong assumptions; network fibres can be limited by intertube shear and sliding.
