# Radial kick from a circular Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Radial impulse](radial-impulse.md)

A radial [impulse](impulse.md) that multiplies the initial circular [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) by $\beta>1$ adds radial [velocity](velocity.md) $\pm\sqrt{GM/r_0}\sqrt{\beta-1}$ and leaves specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $j^2=GMr_0$. The new specific [orbital energy](orbital-energy.md) is $GM(\beta-2)/(2r_0)$, yielding [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) $e=\sqrt{\beta-1}$ and [semilatus rectum](semilatus-rectum.md) $p=r_0$. For $1<\beta<2$, the apsidal radii are $r_0/(1\pm e)$; for $\beta\geq2$ there is no finite apocentre. A nonzero radial kick occurs away from an apsis, so the old circular radius is not automatically the new periapsis.
