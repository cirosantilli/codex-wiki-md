# Leading exponential term determines a ruin adjustment coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic](cramer-lundberg-ruin-asymptotic.md)

If the [Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic](cramer-lundberg-ruin-asymptotic.md) has a finite positive constant, compare it with the leading nonzero term of a finite exponential representation. An [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) smaller than that term's decay rate would give limit zero; a larger coefficient would give infinity. Thus the two rates agree and the prefactor is the leading coefficient. Terms with zero coefficient are absent and do not determine a decay rate.
