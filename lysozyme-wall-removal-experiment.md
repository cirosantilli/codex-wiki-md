# Lysozyme wall-removal experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Protoplast](protoplast.md)

The 1953 paper [The isolation of protoplasts from Bacillus megaterium by controlled treatment with lysozyme by Claes Weibull](the-isolation-of-protoplasts-from-bacillus-megaterium-by-controlled-treatment-with-lysozyme-by-claes-weibull.md) (pp. 688–690, Figures 2–3) tested [Bacillus megaterium](bacillus-megaterium.md) strain KM. Apparatus included a Klett-Summerson colorimeter with a red filter and a Spencer no. 8 [phase-contrast microscope](phase-contrast-microscope.md). [Lysozyme](lysozyme.md) in phosphate buffer produced empty residual bodies; adding $0.2\,\mathrm{M}$ [sucrose](sucrose.md) preserved granular spheres. Diluting their surroundings caused rapid [osmotic lysis](cytolysis.md).

The same study compared untreated, boiled, [lysozyme](lysozyme.md)-exposed and [sucrose](sucrose.md)-protected samples. Filled, stoppered tubes reduced damage from air exposure. After [centrifugation](centrifugation.md), a Beckman DU spectrophotometer measured ultraviolet [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md) of the separated liquid. Protected samples resembled untreated controls in releasing little ultraviolet-absorbing material; boiled and unprotected samples released much more. Later-growth cultures reduced spontaneous breakdown as a confounder.[pp. 688–689, Materials and Methods; pp. 692–694, release comparison and Figure 6](the-isolation-of-protoplasts-from-bacillus-megaterium-by-controlled-treatment-with-lysozyme-by-claes-weibull.md)

Together, the shape and release readouts support removal of mechanical protection while a permeability barrier persists. They do not establish unchanged [selective permeability](selective-permeability.md) to every [solute](solute.md), long-term viability, or universal susceptibility.

An instructive extension of this experimental logic is a matched comparison: [lysozyme](lysozyme.md) present or absent, crossed with osmotic support present or absent. Keep starting material, buffer, observation interval and handling comparable; include a blank to distinguish optical effects of reagents from changes in the [biological cells](biological-cell.md). Then dilute already formed [protoplasts](protoplast.md) as a separate intervention. This separates access and wall removal from failure after removal. These are proposed controls for interpreting the mechanism, not additional reported results from the historical paper. A fall in optical signal alone cannot establish rupture: shape changes also alter [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md).
