# Second protostellar collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Protostar](protostar.md)

An accelerated contraction phase associated with [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) dissociation absorbing compression energy and reducing effective pressure support. A compact stellar core forms when the gas can again oppose contraction. Its timing and geometry depend on the [accretion](accretion.md) and thermodynamic history.
