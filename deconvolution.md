# Deconvolution

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

[Deconvolution](deconvolution.md) is the [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md) of recovering an input $f$ from a known response kernel $h$ and measured output $g=h*f$. The [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md) turns it into $G=HF$, but division by a small $H$ amplifies [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) and a zero of $H$ loses information in the corresponding mode. A unique stable inverse requires assumptions on the operator and admissible inputs; a formal [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) quotient alone does not establish either property.
