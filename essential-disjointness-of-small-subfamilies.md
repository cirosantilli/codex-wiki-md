# Essential disjointness of small subfamilies

↑ **Parent:** [Almost disjoint family on a regular cardinal](almost-disjoint-family-on-a-regular-cardinal.md)

Any at-most-$\kappa$ subfamily of an [almost disjoint family on a regular cardinal](almost-disjoint-family-on-a-regular-cardinal.md) becomes pairwise disjoint after deleting fewer than $\kappa$ points from each member. Enumerate it in length at most $\kappa$ and apply the displayed trimming. Each removed part is a union of fewer than $\kappa$ small intersections, so [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) arithmetic bounds it below $\kappa$.
