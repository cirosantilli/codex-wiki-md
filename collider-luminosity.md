# Collider luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [SPEAR Mark I tau discovery experiment](spear-mark-i-tau-discovery-experiment.md)

The beam-overlap factor $L$ relating a reaction's [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) $\sigma$ to its ideal production rate $dN/dt$. Integrated [collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md) $\mathcal L=\int L\,dt$ has units of inverse area and gives the expected production count $\mathcal L\sigma$ when $\sigma$ is constant.
