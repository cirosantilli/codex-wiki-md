# Square completion for a one-dimensional kink

↑ **Parent:** [Bogomolny bound](bogomolny-bound.md)

For the static energy $E=\int[\phi'^2+W'(\phi)^2]dx/2$, [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) gives

$$
E=\frac12\int(\phi'\mp W')^2dx\pm[W(\phi_+)-W(\phi_-)].
$$

The [Bogomolny bound](bogomolny-bound.md) is the absolute boundary difference. Equality requires the [Bogomolny equation](bogomolny-equations.md) $\phi'=\pm W'(\phi)$ with the sign selected by that difference. Differentiating it gives the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) $\phi''=W'W''$. A stationary solution of that second-order equation need not saturate the bound in a general field theory.
