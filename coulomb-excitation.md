# Coulomb excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Collective nuclear model](collective-nuclear-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coulomb_excitation)

Excitation of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) by the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) of a passing charged projectile. For suitable collision [energies](energy-split.md) and separations, the [electromagnetic interaction](electromagnetic-interaction.md) can populate collective states without requiring contact through the [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md). [Electric quadrupole transition](electric-quadrupole-transition.md) strengths connect excitation yields with collective charge motion; inferring a shape additionally assumes a nuclear model. Contributions from the [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md), competing transitions and the collision kinematics must be controlled before identifying a yield with a single quadrupole strength.
