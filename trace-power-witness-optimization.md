# Trace-power witness optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Witness acceptance operator](witness-acceptance-operator.md)

For a positive [witness acceptance operator](witness-acceptance-operator.md) on dimension $D$, large trace powers approximate its largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). With an $m$-qubit [QMA](qma.md) [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) and $d=2m+2$, the usual NO instances give $\operatorname{Tr}(F^d)<2^{-d}$ and YES instances give a value above $2^{-d}$. A closed product-index expansion computes the trace by recomputation with polynomial storage, avoiding logarithms and roots in a real-arithmetic implementation.
