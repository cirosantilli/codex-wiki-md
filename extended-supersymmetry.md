# Extended supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

In four-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), extended [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) has more than one independent [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md), together with their adjoints. The index $A=1,\ldots,\mathcal N$ distinguishes these [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md).
