# Electronegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronegativity)

[Electronegativity](electronegativity.md) describes a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md)'s tendency to attract [electron density](electron-density.md) towards itself in a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md). It helps answer a useful question: when two different [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) share [Electrons](electron.md), which end of the [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md) tends to be richer in [electron density](electron-density.md)? For [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md), the answer is [chlorine](chlorine.md): the [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) is a [polar covalent bond](polar-covalent-bond.md), conventionally written $\mathrm H^{\delta+}-\mathrm{Cl}^{\delta-}$. The symbols indicate [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md), not isolated [ions](ion.md) carrying whole elementary [electric charges](electric-charge.md).[§7.2, “Pure vs. Polar Covalent Bonds”, HCl example](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

[Electronegativity](electronegativity.md) is a comparison scheme, not the number of [Electrons](electron.md) on a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). A [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md) depends on how the [electron density](electron-density.md) is partitioned; [formal charge](formal-charge.md) instead follows an integer allocation in a [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md). Neither is numerically equal to [electronegativity](electronegativity.md). Nor is [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) identical to [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md), which concerns adding an [Electron](electron.md) to an isolated species. Different [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) scales use different measured inputs and conventions; compare numbers only after specifying the scale.[electronegativity](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/electronegativity.md)

For a concrete comparison, the Royal Society of Chemistry lists [Pauling electronegativities](pauling-electronegativity.md) $\chi_{\rm P}(\mathrm H)=2.20$ and $\chi_{\rm P}(\mathrm{Cl})=3.16$. Their difference is $0.96$, so the [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) rule predicts [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md) towards [chlorine](chlorine.md) in [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md). An isolated [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) [molecule](molecule.md) has identical ends and zero [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) difference. These examples explain a direction of unequal sharing, not a quantitative [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md).[Hydrogen — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/hydrogen-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)[Chlorine — Element information, properties and uses](periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/chlorine-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)

A larger [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) difference often suggests stronger [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md), but there is no universal numerical boundary where a [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) suddenly becomes [ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md). Tables of cutoffs are classification aids with exceptions. [Hydrogen fluoride](hydrogen-fluoride.md), for example, is described by a [polar covalent bond](polar-covalent-bond.md) even when its [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) difference exceeds a textbook's nominal ionic threshold. The actual [electron density](electron-density.md), physical setting, and [chemical substance](chemical-substance.md)'s structure matter.[§7.2, “Electronegativity and Bond Type”, general-guide warning and HF exception](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

The distinction from [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) is equally useful: an [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) measures the cost of removing an [Electron](electron.md) from a specified isolated species, whereas [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) compares attraction for [electron density](electron-density.md) in a bonded environment. Neither the removal cost alone nor the attachment release alone supplies a unique bonded-atom [electronegativity](electronegativity.md). A scale combines selected measured inputs with a model and a convention.[E01990: multiple definitions; Mulliken average versus Pauling bond-energy differences](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/electronegativity.md)
