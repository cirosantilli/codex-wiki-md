# Stratified internal-wave guide

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

A stratified layer between unstratified regions can trap [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md), because the exterior disturbance is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md). Its discrete [normal modes](normal-mode.md) obey [Robin boundary conditions](robin-boundary-condition.md) representing the exterior layers. Periodic boundary forcing at a trapped [normal mode](normal-mode.md) [frequency](frequency.md) produces [resonance](resonance.md) in the ideal undamped model.
