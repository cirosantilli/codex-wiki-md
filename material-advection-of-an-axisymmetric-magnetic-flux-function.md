# Material advection of an axisymmetric magnetic flux function

↑ **Parent:** [Poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md)

For an axisymmetric [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B_p=\nabla\Psi\times\nabla\phi$, the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) gives $\partial_t\Psi+\mathbf u_p\cdot\nabla\Psi=0$, after fixing the time-dependent additive constant in $\Psi$. A regular axial reference makes $2\pi\Psi$ the enclosed [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md). This scalar statement of [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) holds even when rotation also produces a [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md).
