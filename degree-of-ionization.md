# Degree of ionization

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

The fraction of a specified population of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) that has become ionized. For singly ionized atoms with no external charge source, [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) gives equal free-[electron](electron.md) and ion [number densities](number-density.md). Its equilibrium value depends on both [temperature](temperature-split.md) and total [number density](number-density.md) through the [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md).
