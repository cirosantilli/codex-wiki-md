# Cardinal product

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal arithmetic](cardinal-arithmetic.md)

The cardinal product is the [cardinality](cardinality.md) of the set of functions $f$ on the index set with $f(i)<\kappa_i$ at every coordinate. The empty product is one, because it contains the [empty function](empty-function.md). A zero factor makes a nonempty-index product zero. In ZFC, a family of nonzero cardinals has nonzero product by the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md).
