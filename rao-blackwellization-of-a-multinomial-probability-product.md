# Rao-Blackwellization of a multinomial probability product

↑ **Parent:** [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md)

For $n\geq3$ independent labels with three type probabilities, conditioning the indicator of an ordered $(0,1,2)$ initial triple on the [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md) type counts gives the displayed unbiased [estimator](estimator.md). The [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md) reduces its [variance](variance-split.md) strictly when all three probabilities are positive. At a boundary parameter with a missing type, both [variances](variance-split.md) vanish.
