# Three-error syndrome decoding of extended Golay code

↑ **Parent:** [Extended binary Golay code](extended-binary-golay-code.md)

For a binary [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ with $A^2=I$, the [syndrome](syndrome.md) of an error $(p,q)$ relative to the column [parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md) $(I;A)$ is $s=p+qA$. An error of total [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) at most three has at most one nonzero bit in one half. Test $s$, each $s+A_i$, $sA$, and each $sA+A_i$ for weights at most three, two, three and two respectively. These identify $(s,0)$, $(s+A_i,e_i)$, $(0,sA)$ and $(e_i,sA+A_i)$. For the [extended binary Golay code](extended-binary-golay-code.md), [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) eight guarantees that at most one such error exists.
