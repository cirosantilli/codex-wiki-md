# Convection roll

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)

A [convection roll](convection-roll.md) is a circulating cell pattern whose ideal [velocity](velocity.md) and [temperature](temperature.md) fields are periodic across a chosen horizontal direction and independent of the coordinate along the [convection roll](convection-roll.md) axis. In a two-dimensional [stream function](stream-function.md) representation, its [velocity field](velocity-field.md) is $(-\psi_z,0,\psi_x)$. [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md) can select stationary or oscillatory [convection rolls](convection-roll.md); a two-dimensional [convection roll](convection-roll.md) may be unstable to three-dimensional disturbances, a different [convection roll](convection-roll.md) orientation, or slow amplitude and phase modulations. Existence of a saturated [Landau amplitude equation](landau-amplitude-equation.md) branch does not alone establish its stability.
