# Generalized estimating equation

↑ **Parent:** [Estimating equation](estimating-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_estimating_equation)

For independent clusters with mean vector $m_i(\theta)$ and positive definite working [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $V_i$, a [generalized estimating equation](generalized-estimating-equation.md) solves

$$
\sum_iD_i^TV_i^{-1}(Y_i-m_i)=0,\qquad D_i=\partial m_i/\partial\theta^T.
$$

Correct mean specification and regularity can give a [consistent estimator](consistency-statistics.md) of identifiable mean coefficients despite a misspecified working [covariance](covariance.md). A [sandwich covariance matrix](sandwich-covariance-matrix.md) accounts for actual cluster variation. Mean equations alone cannot identify two parameters that always enter through the same combination.
