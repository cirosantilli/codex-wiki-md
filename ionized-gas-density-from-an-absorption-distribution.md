# Ionized gas density from an absorption distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution](neutral-hydrogen-column-density-distribution.md)

A neutral-hydrogen [column density](column-density.md) $N$ traces total hydrogen column $N/x_{\rm HI}$ only after specifying its neutral fraction $x_{\rm HI}$. Integrating the latter against the distribution per [absorption distance](absorption-distance.md) gives the comoving gas [mass density](density.md) in the displayed expression. The factor $\mu_H$ includes helium if desired. In [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) for optically thin, almost fully ionized hydrogen, $x_{\rm HI}\simeq\alpha(T)n_H/\Gamma$, so gas density, [temperature](temperature.md), [photoionization rate](photoionization-rate.md) and absorber size are required. An observed distribution alone does not fix the total baryon inventory.
