# Holevo-capacity additivity for entanglement-breaking channels

↑ **Parent:** [Additivity of Holevo capacity](additivity-of-holevo-capacity.md)

Every [entanglement-breaking channel](entanglement-breaking-channel.md) $\mathcal E$ has additive [Holevo capacity](holevo-capacity.md) with every finite-dimensional [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) $\mathcal N$. A [measure-and-prepare channel](measure-and-prepare-channel.md) factorization exposes a classical measurement outcome. The [quantum mutual information balance identity](quantum-mutual-information-balance-identity.md) and [data processing for quantum mutual information](data-processing-for-quantum-mutual-information.md) then bound the joint output [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md) by the sum of the individual capacities, using the [conditional input ensemble after a local measurement](conditional-input-ensemble-after-a-local-measurement.md) for the other channel. Independent product ensembles attain the reverse inequality.
