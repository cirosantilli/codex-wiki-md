# Sediment mass determines final deposit thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md)

Particle [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $h_c=[\phi_1(H-z_m)+\phi_2z_m]/\phi_{\max}$. Averaging concentration over the entire original container gives $\bar\phi=\phi_{\max}h_c/H$. This average differs from the concentration inside the deposit itself.
