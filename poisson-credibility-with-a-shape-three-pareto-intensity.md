# Poisson credibility with a shape-three Pareto intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann credibility premium](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md)

Let a fixed policy intensity have [prior density](prior-density.md) $3\theta^{-4}$ on $\theta>1$, and let $n$ annual [claim counts](claim-count.md) be conditionally independent with [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) mean $\theta$. The [expected process variance](expected-process-variance.md) is $3/2$ and the [variance of hypothetical means](variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) is $3/4$, so the [Bühlmann credibility factor](credibility-factor.md) is $n/(n+2)$. A total count $k$ gives the displayed [credibility premium](credibility-estimate.md). By contrast, the [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) density is proportional to $\theta^{k-4}e^{-n\theta}$ on $\theta>1$. Its mean is the ratio $\int_1^\infty\theta^{k-3}e^{-n\theta}\,d\theta/\int_1^\infty\theta^{k-4}e^{-n\theta}\,d\theta$. For $n=2,k=5$, that [Bayesian credibility](bayesian-credibility.md) estimate is $5/3$, whereas the affine estimate is $2$.
