# Atomic fine-structure level

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic term](atomic-term.md)

A level of an [atomic term](atomic-term.md) with specified total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J$. Without an external field, its $2J+1$ magnetic states remain degenerate in a rotationally invariant [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md).
