# Lie group action

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A Lie group action is a smooth [group action](group-action.md) of a Lie group on a smooth manifold. Differentiating the action gives an infinitesimal action of its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) by vector fields.
