# Block induction

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer correspondence](brauer-correspondence.md)

Let $b$ be a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) of $kH$ with [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$, where $DC_G(D)\le H\le G$. Apply [Brauer first main theorem](brauer-first-main-theorem.md) inside $H$ to obtain its correspondent in $N_H(D)$. Composing that correspondent's [central character of a block](central-character-of-a-block.md) with the [Brauer morphism](brauer-morphism.md) from $Z(kG)$ selects a unique [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) $b^G$. It is the unique [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) whose restriction to $H\times H$ contains the block bimodule $b$ as a [direct summand](direct-summand.md). Its [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) can be larger than $D$ when $H$ does not contain $N_G(D)$.
