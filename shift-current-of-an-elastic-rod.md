# Shift current of an elastic rod

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

For a longitudinal displacement field $\psi(z,t)$ and [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) $\mathcal L=A[\rho\dot\psi^2-K\psi'^2]/2$, uniform field shifts give [Noether current](noether-current.md) $j^0=A\rho\dot\psi$, $j^z=-AK\psi'$. Its spatial integral is the rod's longitudinal mechanical [momentum](momentum-split.md). A prescribed position-dependent [mass density](density.md) changes $j^0$ to $A\rho(z)\dot\psi$ but does not break this internal shift symmetry. Boundary traction can carry momentum into or out of a finite rod, so action symmetry alone does not guarantee conservation of the integrated charge.
