# Noether formula

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann–Roch theorem for algebraic surfaces](riemann-roch-theorem-for-algebraic-surfaces.md)

For a smooth compact complex surface, the degree-four part of its Todd class is $(c_1^2+c_2)/12$. Applying Riemann–Roch to its structure sheaf gives the formula. For a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md), $c_1=0$ and $\chi(\mathcal O_X)=2$, so its topological Euler characteristic is $c_2=24$.
