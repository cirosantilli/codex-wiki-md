# Mestel disk

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestel_disk)

A Mestel disk is a thin axisymmetric [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md) with [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) inversely proportional to radius. Its [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) is exactly flat, $v_c=v_M$, and $v_c^2=GM(R)/R$ despite its nonspherical geometry. In the [Legendre expansion of thin-disk gravity](legendre-expansion-of-thin-disk-gravity.md), all positive-degree interior and exterior terms cancel. Its total mass is infinite, so only potential differences, rather than a potential zero at infinity, are defined.
