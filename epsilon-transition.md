# Epsilon transition

↑ **Parent:** [Epsilon-NFA](epsilon-nfa.md)

An [automaton transition](automaton-transition.md) that consumes no input symbol. The [epsilon closure](epsilon-closure.md) of a [set](set-split.md) of [automaton states](automaton-state.md) includes all [automaton states](automaton-state.md) reachable through finitely many such moves. [Pushdown automata](pushdown-automaton.md) can also use moves consuming no input, while still changing their [stack](stack-data-structure.md).
