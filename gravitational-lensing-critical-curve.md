# Gravitational-lensing critical curve

↑ **Parent:** [Strong gravitational lensing](strong-gravitational-lensing.md)

A critical curve is a locus in the image plane where the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the [thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md) becomes singular. Its formal point-source [lensing magnification](lensing-magnification.md) diverges, while finite source size regularizes the measured flux. Its mapped locus in the source plane is a [gravitational-lensing caustic](gravitational-lensing-caustic.md).
