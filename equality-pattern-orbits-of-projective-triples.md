# Equality-pattern orbits of projective triples

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md)

The [general linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md) acting diagonally on projective triples has five [orbits of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md): all equal, three different positions for the repeated coordinate when exactly two are equal, and all distinct. The corresponding representative [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) are upper triangular, diagonal, and [scalar matrices](scalar-matrix.md). Their orders are $p(p-1)^2$, $(p-1)^2$, and $p-1$, respectively; the orbit sizes are $p+1$, $p(p+1)$, and $p(p+1)(p-1)$. Transitivity follows from the action being [sharply three-transitive on a projective line](sharply-three-transitive-on-a-projective-line.md).
