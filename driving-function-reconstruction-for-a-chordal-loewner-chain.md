# Driving-function reconstruction for a chordal Loewner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md)

The domains of a capacity-parametrized [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) determine their hydrodynamically normalized [mapping-out functions of compact H-hulls](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md). At a surviving point, the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) gives $U_t=g_t(z)-2/\partial_tg_t(z)$. For a continuous driver and $t>0$, the left derivative uses only the hull past. High points $in$, $n\in\mathbb N$, survive up to any fixed finite time for sufficiently large $n$, making this a countable local reconstruction of the driver and its past filtration.
