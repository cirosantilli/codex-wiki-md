# Local criterion for injectivity over a Noetherian ring

↑ **Parent:** [Injective module](injective-module.md)

For a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), injectivity of a [module](module-mathematics.md) can be tested at all prime localizations. By the [Baer criterion](baer-criterion.md), test $\operatorname{Ext}^1_R(R/J,M)$ for every [ideal](ideal.md) $J$. [Localization of Ext over a Noetherian ring](localization-of-ext-over-a-noetherian-ring.md) identifies its localization with the corresponding [ideal](ideal.md) test over $R_P$. Every [ideal](ideal.md) of $R_P$ is extended from its contraction, and [localization detects zero elements](localization-detects-zero-elements.md) applies to the resulting Ext [module](module-mathematics.md) even when it is not finitely generated. These observations prove both directions.
