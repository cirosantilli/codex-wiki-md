# Universal property of an inverse image sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse image sheaf](inverse-image-sheaf.md)

Every [f-morphism of sheaves](f-morphism-of-sheaves.md) from $\mathcal G$ to $\mathcal F$ factors uniquely through the canonical f-morphism from $\mathcal G$ to its [inverse image sheaf](inverse-image-sheaf.md). A local inverse-image section is represented by sections of $\mathcal G$ on image neighbourhoods; their prescribed images glue in $\mathcal F$.
