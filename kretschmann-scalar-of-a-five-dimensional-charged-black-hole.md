# Kretschmann scalar of a five-dimensional charged black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom black hole](five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-black-hole.md)

For $V=1-2M/r^2+Q^2/r^4$, an orthonormal-frame curvature calculation gives $(V'')^2+6(V'/r)^2+12((1-V)/r^2)^2$. Expansion gives the displayed invariant, diverging at zero radius. It is finite at every positive root of V. Thus horizon coordinate singularities are distinct from the central [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md).
