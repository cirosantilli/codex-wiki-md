# Elliptic integral of the first kind

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic integral](elliptic-integral.md)

For a parameter $k$, one form of the incomplete elliptic integral of the first kind is

$$
F(z,k)=\int_0^z\frac{dt}{\sqrt{(1-t^2)(1-k^2t^2)}}.
$$

Its value depends on the choices of square-root branches and, under [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) around the [branch points](branch-point.md), on the integration path.
