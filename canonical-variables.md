# Canonical variables

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md)

[Phase space](phase-space.md) coordinates are canonical when their [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md) have the form $\{q^I,p_J\}=\delta^I_J$, with the position-position and momentum-momentum brackets zero.
