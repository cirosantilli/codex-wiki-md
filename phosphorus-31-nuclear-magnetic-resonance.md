# Phosphorus-31 nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[Phosphorus-31 nuclear magnetic resonance](phosphorus-31-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) observes spin-one-half phosphorus-31. An $A_2X$ system can show a two-phosphorus doublet and a one-phosphorus triplet; three inequivalent mutually coupled phosphorus sites can each give a doublet of doublets in the first-order limit.
