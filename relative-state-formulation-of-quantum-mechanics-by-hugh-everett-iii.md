# Relative State Formulation of Quantum Mechanics by Hugh Everett III

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Hugh Everett III](work-by-hugh-everett-iii.md)

[https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454)

The original paper treats [quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md) through correlations in a [unitary](unitary-connection.md) relative-state description. Its statistical [argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) must be distinguished from a postulated dynamical collapse.
