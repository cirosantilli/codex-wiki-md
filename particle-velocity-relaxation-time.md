# Particle velocity-relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md)

The exponential inertial relaxation time $\tau_p=m/\zeta$ for linear drag. A no-slip sphere has $\tau_p=\rho_pd^2/(18\eta)$; particle [density](density.md) enters this time, whereas fluid [density](density.md) enters the fluid [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md).
