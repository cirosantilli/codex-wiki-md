# Golod-Shafarevich infinitude criterion for pro-p groups

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

If a pro-$p$ presentation on $d$ generators and possibly infinitely many relators $r_i$ satisfies

$$
1-d\tau+\sum_i\tau^{D(r_i)}<0\qquad(0<\tau<1),
$$

with the sum convergent and $D$ the [Magnus degree of a free pro-p group element](magnus-degree-of-a-free-pro-p-group-element.md), then the presented [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) is infinite. The Golod-Shafarevich inequality forces its completed group algebra to be infinite-dimensional, which would be impossible for a finite group. This formulation applies to relators whose degrees tend to infinity and is suitable for enumerated power-relation constructions.
