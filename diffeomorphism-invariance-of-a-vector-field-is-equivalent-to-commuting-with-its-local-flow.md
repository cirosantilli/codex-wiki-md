# Diffeomorphism invariance of a vector field is equivalent to commuting with its local flow

↑ **Parent:** [Pushforward of a vector field](pushforward-of-a-vector-field.md)

The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) shows that $\alpha\phi_t\alpha^{-1}$ is the [local flow](local-flow.md) of the [pushforward of a vector field](pushforward-of-a-vector-field.md) $\alpha_*X$. If this equals $X$, uniqueness of [integral curves of a vector field](integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) gives commutation. Conversely, differentiate the commuting identity at $t=0$ to obtain $d\alpha_pX_p=X_{\alpha(p)}$. All identities hold on their common domains; the [vector field](vector-field.md) need not be complete.
