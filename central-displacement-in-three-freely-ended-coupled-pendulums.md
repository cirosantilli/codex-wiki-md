# Central displacement in three freely ended coupled pendulums

↑ **Parent:** [Normal modes of three freely ended coupled pendulums](normal-modes-of-three-freely-ended-coupled-pendulums.md)

Releasing the central bob of three identical spring-coupled [simple pendulums](simple-pendulum-split.md) from displacement $d$ excites only the symmetric [normal modes](normal-mode.md). In displacement ratios, $(0,d,0)=(d/3)(1,1,1)-(d/3)(1,-2,1)$. Thus the outer coordinates are $(d/3)(\cos\omega_1t-\cos\omega_3t)$ and the central coordinate is $(d/3)(\cos\omega_1t+2\cos\omega_3t)$, where $\omega_1^2=g/l$ and $\omega_3^2=g/l+3k/m$. The antisymmetric mode is absent by reflection symmetry. The central modulation envelope is $|d|\sqrt{5+4\cos[(\omega_3-\omega_1)t]}/3$, so it never vanishes completely. Slow [beats](beat.md) require a small frequency difference; the decomposition itself is exact in the linear model.
