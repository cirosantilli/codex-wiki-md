# Kerr stationary-limit surface is generically timelike

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr ergoregion](kerr-ergoregion.md)

For subextreme [Kerr spacetime](kerr-black-hole.md), the outer stationary-limit surface is $s=r-r_e(\theta)=0$, where $r_e=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2\cos^2\theta}$. Away from the rotation axis, $r_e>r_+$ and $\Delta(r_e)=a^2\sin^2\theta>0$. Its normal is therefore spacelike and the surface timelike. Although the stationary [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) has zero norm on it, it is not normal to it, and the surface is not a [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md). The actual outer Killing horizon is $r=r_+$, generated by $K+\Omega_H\Phi$.
