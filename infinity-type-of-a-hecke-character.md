# Infinity type of a Hecke character

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke character](hecke-character.md)

For an algebraic [Hecke character](hecke-character.md), the character on archimedean ideles is a product of integral powers of embeddings and their complex conjugates. In the convention $\psi((a))=a$ for primary $a$ over an imaginary quadratic field, the ideal character has type $(1,0)$ and the idele character has archimedean component $z^{-1}$. Distinguishing these conventions avoids inverse errors in CM [Galois representations](galois-representation.md).
