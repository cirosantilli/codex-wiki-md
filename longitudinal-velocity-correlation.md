# Longitudinal velocity correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md)

For a separation parallel to a unit vector $\mathbf n$, the [longitudinal velocity correlation](longitudinal-velocity-correlation.md) is $Q_{LL}=\langle u_L(\mathbf x)u_L(\mathbf x+r\mathbf n)\rangle=u^2f(r)$. In isotropic incompressible [turbulence](turbulence-split.md) the transverse normalized correlation is $g=f+rf\prime/2$ and the trace is $C=u^2(3f+rf\prime)$.
