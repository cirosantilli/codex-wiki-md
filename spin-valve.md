# Spin valve

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoresistance](magnetoresistance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_valve)

A device with a reference magnetic layer whose [magnetization](magnetization.md) is comparatively fixed and another layer whose [magnetization](magnetization.md) responds more readily to an applied field. The resistance reads their relative alignment. Fert's account describes how such [giant magnetoresistance](giant-magnetoresistance.md) sensors entered hard-disk read heads in 1997.[p.1519, spin-valve structure and historical read-head adoption](nobel-lecture-origin-development-and-future-of-spintronics-by-albert-fert.md)
