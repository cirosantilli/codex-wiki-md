# Proper-power language is not regular

↑ **Parent:** [Pumping lemma for regular languages](pumping-lemma-for-regular-languages.md)

Over an alphabet containing $a,b$, the language $\{w^n:n\geq2\}$ is not [regular language](regular-language.md). Intersecting it with the regular language $a^+ba^+b$ gives exactly

$$
\{a^mba^mb:m\geq1\},
$$

which fails the [pumping lemma for regular languages](pumping-lemma-for-regular-languages.md).
