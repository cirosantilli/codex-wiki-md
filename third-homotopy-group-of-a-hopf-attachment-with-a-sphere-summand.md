# Third homotopy group of a Hopf attachment with a sphere summand

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring of a Hopf attachment with a sphere summand](cohomology-ring-of-a-hopf-attachment-with-a-sphere-summand.md)

Map $X_{d,f}$ to $K(\mathbb Z,2)=\mathbb{CP}^\infty$ using its degree-two generator. The [homotopy fibre](homotopy-fiber.md) is the total space of the corresponding [circle bundle](circle-bundle.md), is 2-connected, and has $\pi_3$ equal to that of $X_{d,f}$. Over $S^2\vee S^3$ its total space is $P=S^3\cup_{S^1}(S^1\times S^3)$, with $H_3(P)=\mathbb Z A\oplus\mathbb Z B$. The four-cell bundle is trivial; its relative degree-four generator has boundary $dA+fB$, the lifted attaching map. The [long exact sequence in relative homology](long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md) and [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) therefore give $\pi_3(X_{d,f})=\mathbb Z^2/\langle(d,f)\rangle$. [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md) gives the displayed expression when $d>0$, including $f=0$.
