# Boundary extension of the inverse map for a continuous Loewner trace

↑ **Parent:** [Trace of a Loewner chain](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md)

For a continuous [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) generating its hulls, the inverse [mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) $g_t^{-1}$ extends continuously to the closed [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The finite trace is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) image of an interval, and the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of its unbounded complementary component has [local connectedness](locally-connected-space.md); the [Caratheodory boundary extension theorem](caratheodory-boundary-extension-theorem.md) gives the extension. In particular $g_t^{-1}(U_t)=\gamma(t)$, while $g_t^{-1}$ maps the open [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) into $D_t$. This is the deterministic boundary-extension input of [Rohde and Schramm, Theorem 4.1](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v161-n2-p07.pdf). Under a [Conformal Markov property of SLE](conformal-markov-property-of-sle.md) restart, this relates [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) contacts of the mapped future to contacts with the old hull, without assuming the old trace is simple.
