# Dependency graph of events

↑ **Parent:** [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md)

A [dependency graph of events](dependency-graph-of-events.md) joins potentially dependent events. Each event must be independent of the [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) generated by all its nonneighbors, a joint condition stronger than pairwise independence. In a product probability space, joining events whose sets of underlying independent variables overlap gives such a graph. This supplies the hypothesis of the [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md).
