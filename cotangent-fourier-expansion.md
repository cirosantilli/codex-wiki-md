# Cotangent Fourier expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent partial-fraction expansion](cotangent-partial-fraction-expansion.md)

For $\operatorname{Im}z>0$, writing [cotangent](cotangent.md) as $i(e^{2\pi iz}+1)/(e^{2\pi iz}-1)$ and expanding a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives this [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md). Differentiating the [cotangent partial-fraction expansion](cotangent-partial-fraction-expansion.md) and this [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) $k-1$ times yields

$$
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}(z+n)^{-k}=\frac{(-2\pi i)^k}{(k-1)!}\sum_{r\geq1}r^{k-1}e^{2\pi irz},\qquad k\geq2.
$$
