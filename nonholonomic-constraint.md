# Nonholonomic constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

A [nonholonomic constraint](nonholonomic-constraint.md) is a nonintegrable restriction on allowed [velocities](velocity.md): it cannot locally be replaced by relations among [generalized coordinates](generalized-coordinate.md) and time. In contrast, a velocity equation $r\dot\phi-R\dot\theta=0$ with constant radii integrates to a [holonomic constraint](holonomic-constraint.md) $r\phi-R\theta=C$. [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) is therefore not automatically nonholonomic: planar rolling on a prescribed curve can be holonomic, whereas rolling with additional directional freedoms can impose nonintegrable velocity constraints. Unwrapped angles may be needed to record the winding of a rolling trajectory.
