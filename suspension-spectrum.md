# Suspension spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (topology)](spectrum-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_spectrum)

For a [based space](based-space.md) $X$, its [suspension spectrum](suspension-spectrum.md) has level $j$ equal to $S^j\wedge X$. Its derived adjunction with the infinite [loop space](loop-space.md) is $[\Sigma^\infty X,E]_{\mathrm{st}}=[X,\Omega^\infty E]_*$.
