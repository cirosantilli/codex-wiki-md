# Pitch

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

A perceptual attribute allowing sounds to be ordered as relatively high or low. It is related to, but not identical with, acoustic frequency or the location of the largest component in a [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md). Complex periodic signals require consideration of temporal and spectral structure.
