# Borel transform of a factorially divergent series

↑ **Parent:** [Borel summation](borel-summation.md)

Removing factorial growth from the coefficients of a formal inverse-power series produces its Borel transform. When continuation and exponential growth bounds allow it, $\int_{\mathcal C}e^{-zt}\mathcal B(t)\,dt$ reconstructs the series by [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md). A singularity on the integration direction requires a lateral contour prescription. A [simple pole](simple-pole.md) contributes a [residue](residue.md) when that prescription is changed, explaining how [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md) arises from otherwise identical algebraic expansions.
