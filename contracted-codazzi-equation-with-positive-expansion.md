# Contracted Codazzi equation with positive expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss–Codazzi equations for a spatial hypersurface](gauss-codazzi-equations-for-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

For the [positive-expansion convention for extrinsic curvature](positive-expansion-convention-for-extrinsic-curvature.md), write $\nabla_\mu n_\nu=K_{\mu\nu}-n_\mu a_\nu$ with [normal acceleration](normal-acceleration.md) $a_\nu=n^\rho\nabla_\rho n_\nu$. The Ricci commutator applied to $n$ gives the difference of its two second-derivative contractions. Projecting tangentially, the acceleration term in that commutator cancels exactly the acceleration term converting a spacetime divergence of $K$ into its fully projected [spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md). Einstein's equations then identify the displayed equation as the [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md).
