# Transverse electric-dipole pair energy

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-dipole-interaction.md)

The point-dipole [energy](energy-split.md) is $[\mathbf p_1\cdot\mathbf p_2-3(\mathbf p_1\cdot\hat r)(\mathbf p_2\cdot\hat r)]/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$. If the separation is perpendicular to the first dipole, the second term vanishes. Parallel dipoles then have positive [energy](energy-split.md) $p_1p_2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$, while mutually perpendicular dipoles give zero in this approximation. Real molecular interactions can include multipoles, induction and short-range contributions beyond this point-dipole term.
