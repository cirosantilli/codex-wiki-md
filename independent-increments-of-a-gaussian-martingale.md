# Independent increments of a Gaussian martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)

A centered continuous [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) that is also a [martingale](martingale-split.md) has independent increments. Every future increment has zero [covariance](covariance.md) with every finite vector of past values by the martingale property; [uncorrelated jointly normal variables are independent](uncorrelated-jointly-normal-variables-are-independent.md).
