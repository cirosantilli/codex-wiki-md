# Full-density sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Natural density](natural-density.md)

An increasing sequence of integers has full density when its range has [natural density](natural-density.md) one. [Convergence in density of a sequence](convergence-in-density-of-a-sequence.md) means that, for each fixed tolerance, the exceptional indices have [natural density](natural-density.md) zero.
