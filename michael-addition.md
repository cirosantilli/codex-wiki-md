# Michael addition

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_addition)

A [Michael addition](michael-addition.md) makes a bond at the beta carbon of a conjugated electrophile through conjugate addition. An [enamine](enamine.md) can supply a carbon nucleophile; subsequent hydrolysis restores its original carbonyl group.
