# Zero-diagonal Jacobi Lax pair for the finite Volterra lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Finite Volterra lattice](finite-volterra-lattice.md)

Let $L$ be the symmetric zero-diagonal [Jacobi matrix](jacobi-matrix.md) with $L_{j,j+1}=L_{j+1,j}=a_j$, and let the [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) $B$ have

$$
B_{j,j+2}=-B_{j+2,j}=a_ja_{j+1}.
$$

Then $\dot L=[B,L]$ is equivalent to the [Finite Volterra lattice](finite-volterra-lattice.md). The corresponding similarity flow makes the eigenvalues of $L$, the coefficients of its [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md), and its [trace invariants](trace-invariants-of-a-lax-equation.md) first integrals.
