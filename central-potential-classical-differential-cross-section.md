# Central-potential classical differential cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md)

For an azimuthally symmetric classical scattering map from [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b$ to scattering angle $\theta$,

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}
=\frac{b}{\sin\theta}\left|\frac{db}{d\theta}\right|.
$$

When several impact parameters produce the same angle, their nonnegative contributions are summed.
