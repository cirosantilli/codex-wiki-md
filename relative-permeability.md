# Relative permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md)

A dimensionless reduction of a porous formation's intrinsic [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) for one fluid phase in multiphase flow. Its effective phase permeability is $Kk_{ri}$, and its [phase mobility](phase-mobility.md) is $k_{ri}/\mu_i$. It depends on saturation and pore-scale phase configuration; it is not the magnetic use of relative permeability.
