# Pseudoscalar decay constant

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md)

The vacuum-to-meson axial-current normalization defined by $\langle0|\bar q_1\gamma^\mu\gamma^5q_2|P(p)\rangle=if_Pp^\mu$. It controls exclusive weak annihilation rates. Ratios of [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) decays into [kaons](kaon.md) and [pions](pion.md) determine $f_K|V_{us}|/(f_\pi|V_{ud}|)$; extracting the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) ratio alone requires independent hadronic input.
