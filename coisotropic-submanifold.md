# Coisotropic submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coisotropic_submanifold)

A coisotropic submanifold $N$ satisfies $(T_pN)^\omega\subset T_pN$, where the superscript denotes the [symplectic orthogonal complement](symplectic-orthogonal-complement.md). Every hypersurface of a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) is coisotropic.
