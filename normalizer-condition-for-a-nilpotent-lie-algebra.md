# Normalizer condition for a nilpotent Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md)

Every proper [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $H$ of a finite-dimensional [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) is properly contained in its [normalizer](normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). Choose the first term of the lower central series contained in $H$; the preceding term contains an element outside $H$ that normalizes it.
