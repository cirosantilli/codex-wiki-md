# Schwarzschild launch-orbit recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild perihelion precession](schwarzschild-perihelion-precession.md)

For a small radial excursion about a stable circular [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md), the inverse radius oscillates with azimuthal frequency $\sqrt{1-6GM/(c^2R)}$. A satellite launched tangentially returns to its launch radius once per radial period. A later exact encounter with a point station at the launch azimuth requires the exact radial-to-azimuthal rotation ratio to be rational. An irrational ratio instead gives dense, arbitrarily close recurrence, which still threatens objects of finite size. The linearized rotation ratio is an approximation; exact orbital closure uses the actual orbit's radial advance.
