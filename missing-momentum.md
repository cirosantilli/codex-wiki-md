# Missing momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

The initial [momentum](momentum-split.md) minus the vector sum of reconstructed final visible [momenta](momentum-split.md). At an [electron-positron collider](electron-positron-collider.md) the full initial [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is approximately known, unlike the hard partonic longitudinal momentum at a hadron collider. Nonzero missing [momentum](momentum-split.md) may indicate [neutrinos](neutrino.md), other invisible particles, or a detector loss; it is not alone proof of an invisible decay.
