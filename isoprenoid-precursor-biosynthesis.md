# Isoprenoid precursor biosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolic pathway](metabolic-pathway.md)

[isoprenoid precursor biosynthesis](isoprenoid-precursor-biosynthesis.md) supplies activated building blocks for diverse isoprenoid products. In blood-stage [Plasmodium falciparum](plasmodium-falciparum.md) culture, supplementation with IPP can bypass the essential output of the [apicoplast](apicoplast.md) under the tested conditions.
