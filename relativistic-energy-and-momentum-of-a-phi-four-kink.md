# Relativistic energy and momentum of a phi-four kink

↑ **Parent:** [Phi-four kink](phi-four-kink.md)

For the real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with metric $(+,-)$ and potential $U=\kappa^2(\phi^2-1)^2/2$, the static [phi-four kink](phi-four-kink.md) is $\phi_0(x)=\tanh(\kappa x)$ and satisfies $\phi_0'^2=2U(\phi_0)$. Its mass is $M=\int\phi_0'^2dx=4\kappa/3$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) gives $\phi(t,x)=\phi_0(\gamma(x-vt))$. The [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) gives $P=-\int\phi_t\phi_xdx=\gamma v\int\phi_0'^2dx=\gamma Mv$. Similarly, $E=\tfrac12[\gamma(1+v^2)+\gamma^{-1}]M=\gamma M$, proving the [energy–momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md) $E^2-P^2=M^2$.
