# Lebedev ice-growth law

↑ **Parent:** [Freezing degree-day](freezing-degree-day.md)

This empirical [sea ice](sea-ice.md) growth relation gives thickness $H$ in metres from accumulated [freezing degree-days](freezing-degree-day.md) $N$. Its coefficient includes the specified units and calibration. It is not a complete energy-balance model: snow, ocean heat and changing forcing can alter its applicability. Inversion gives $N=(H/0.0133)^{1/0.58}$.
