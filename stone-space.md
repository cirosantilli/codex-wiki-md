# Stone space

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_space)

A [Stone space](stone-space.md) is a [compact](compact-space.md) [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) that is [totally disconnected](totally-disconnected-space.md), equivalently a compact Hausdorff space with a basis of clopen sets. The topology on a [type space](type-space.md) gives an important example.
