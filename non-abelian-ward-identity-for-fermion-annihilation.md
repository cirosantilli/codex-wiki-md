# Non-Abelian Ward identity for fermion annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Slavnov-Taylor identity](slavnov-taylor-identity.md)

For a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) annihilating with its antiparticle into two massless [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md), the two ordered fermion-exchange diagrams leave a colour [matrix commutator](commutator.md) when one outgoing momentum contracts the [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). The three-boson exchange diagram cancels this current term but leaves a term proportional to the other outgoing momentum. With $D=\partial-igA$, $J_c^\lambda=\bar v\gamma^\lambda t^cu$, $q=k_1+k_2$, and the source's positive-sign three-boson vertex, the tree result is $k_{2\nu}\mathcal M^{ab\mu\nu}=ig^2f^{abc}(J_c\cdot k_2)k_1^\mu/q^2$. It vanishes after contraction with a physical [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md) on leg 1, although the open-index tensor is generally nonzero.
