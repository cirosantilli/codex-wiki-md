# Small GTPase

↑ **Parent:** [G protein](g-protein.md)

A [small GTPase](small-gtpase.md) is a relatively small nucleotide-switch [protein](protein-split.md) that cycles between [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md)- and [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md)-bound states. [Ras](ras.md) family proteins illustrate signaling mechanisms distinct from the three-subunit organization of a [heterotrimeric G protein](heterotrimeric-g-protein.md).
