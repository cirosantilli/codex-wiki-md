# Mass-loaded rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

When material sticks to a rotor, the incoming [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) flux $\dot L_{\rm in}$ and external [torque](torque.md) $\tau_{\rm ext}$ obey $d(I\omega)/dt=\dot L_{\rm in}+\tau_{\rm ext}$. With zero incoming axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and zero external axial [torque](torque.md), $I\omega$ is constant. For deposition at fixed radius $a$ at [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) $\alpha$, $\dot I=\alpha a^2$ and the [rotational kinetic energy](rotational-kinetic-energy.md) satisfies $\dot K=-\alpha a^2\omega^2/2$. This loss is the irreversible cost of bringing the incoming material up to tangential [speed](speed.md); it does not violate [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md).
