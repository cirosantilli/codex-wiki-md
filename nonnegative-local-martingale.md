# Nonnegative local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Local martingale](local-martingale.md)

Every nonnegative [local martingale](local-martingale.md) is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md). In particular, one that starts from zero is identically zero: its nonnegative value at every time has expectation at most zero.
