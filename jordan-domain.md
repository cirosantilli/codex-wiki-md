# Jordan domain

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan curve theorem](jordan-curve-theorem.md)

A Jordan domain is the bounded complementary component of a closed [simple curve](simple-curve.md) in the plane. The [Jordan curve theorem](jordan-curve-theorem.md) separates the plane into this inside component and one unbounded outside component. A [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) from the disc or [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) to this [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) domain extends to a homeomorphism of their closed conformal boundaries by the [Caratheodory boundary extension theorem](caratheodory-boundary-extension-theorem.md).
