# Modulus of continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulus_of_continuity)

The modulus of continuity of a function $f$ is

$$
\omega(f,\delta)=\sup\{|f(x)-f(y)|:d(x,y)\leq\delta\}.
$$

It quantifies [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) by recording the largest oscillation of $f$ across pairs of points at distance at most $\delta$.
