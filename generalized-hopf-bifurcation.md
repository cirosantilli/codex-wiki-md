# Generalized Hopf bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

A generalized [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) has a vanishing cubic radial coefficient and nonzero quintic coefficient. The two parameters unfold the [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) real part and cubic coefficient. With $R=r^2$, [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md) satisfy $\tau+aR+bR^2=0$. Their [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) curve is $\tau=a^2/(4b)$ with $R=-a/(2b)>0$, hence $ab<0$. For $b>0$ the smaller-radius [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md) is stable and the larger one unstable where both exist; their collision explains why the criticality change of a [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) curve brings a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) of [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md).
