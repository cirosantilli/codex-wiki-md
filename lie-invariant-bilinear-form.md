# Lie-invariant bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

A [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) is invariant when it satisfies the displayed identity. Equivalently, $T_B(v)=B(v,-)$ is an [intertwining operator](intertwining-operator.md) from the representation to its [dual Lie algebra representation](dual-lie-algebra-representation.md). For a finite-dimensional [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), any nonzero such form is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), and the [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) makes all invariant forms proportional. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, transposing twice then proves that the form is a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) or an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md).
