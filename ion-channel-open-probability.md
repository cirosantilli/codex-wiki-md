# Ion-channel open probability

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

The [probability](probability.md) that an [ion channel](ion-channel.md) is in a conducting open state under specified conditions. For N equivalent channels, each carrying open-state [electric current](electric-current.md) i, the mean total [electric current](electric-current.md) is $NP_Oi$. An ensemble open fraction and the fraction of time one channel remains open coincide under suitable stationary and ergodic assumptions. A [two-state ion-channel gating](two-state-ion-channel-gating.md) model is one model of this probability, not its definition.
