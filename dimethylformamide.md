# Dimethylformamide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Dimethylformamide](dimethylformamide.md) is a polar organic solvent, abbreviated DMF. Enzyme performance in concentrated DMF can depend on folding, stability and catalytic chemistry, making it useful for separating several goals in protein engineering.
