# Cell cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_cycle)

The [cell cycle](cell-cycle.md) is the sequence of events by which a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) prepares for and completes division, including [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) and distribution of [chromosomes](chromosome.md). In [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md), changing bud appearance provides an accessible readout; see the Burgess, Powers and Mell account and Hartwell, Culotti and Reid's primary study cited there.

For a typical cycling [diploid](diploid.md) [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) [biological cell](biological-cell.md), the order is [G1 phase](g1-phase.md), [S phase](s-phase.md), [G2 phase](g2-phase.md), then [M phase](m-phase.md). [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) doubles nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount during [S phase](s-phase.md); [mitosis](mitosis.md) distributes the copies, and [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md) separates the daughters. The useful distinction is between copying a library and allocating its copies: [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) changes how much material exists, whereas division changes which [biological cell](biological-cell.md) contains it.

**[Figure 64](#image-the-cell-cycle-copying-nuclear-dna-once-then-distributing-it-to-two-daughters). The cell cycle: copying nuclear DNA once, then distributing it to two daughters**. Original schematic for a typical cycling [diploid](diploid.md) [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) [biological cell](biological-cell.md). Each box states nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) content and [chromosome](chromosome.md) count; [M phase](m-phase.md) is summarized by its completed daughters. Arrow lengths do not represent phase durations. The [G0 phase](g0-phase.md) branch shows reversible withdrawal where re-entry is possible, rather than claiming that all nondividing [biological cells](biological-cell.md) can return.

[Gene expression](gene-expression.md) continues to supply [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) and [proteins](protein.md) as the [biological cell](biological-cell.md) grows; it is different from duplicating the [genome](genome.md). In the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md), restored growth requires successful repeated [cell cycles](cell-cycle.md), but growth alone does not locate a defect within a phase. Bud appearance in [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md) provides another readout, while a [flow cytometric DNA-content assay](flow-cytometric-dna-content-assay.md) asks how much nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is present and [DNA pulse labeling](dna-pulse-labeling.md) asks whether new [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) was synthesized during an exposure.
