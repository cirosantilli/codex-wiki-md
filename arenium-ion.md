# Arenium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophilic aromatic substitution](electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenium_ion)

An [arenium ion](arenium-ion.md) is a positively charged intermediate in which electrophilic addition has interrupted the original aromatic delocalization. Lone-pair donors can stabilize contributors that place positive charge at adjacent positions.
