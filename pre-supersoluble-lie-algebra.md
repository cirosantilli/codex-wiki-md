# Pre-supersoluble Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

Call a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) pre-supersoluble if it is abelian or its quotient by its centre has a one-dimensional ideal. Every nonzero such algebra has nonzero abelianization over every field. To prove this, choose $x$ outside the centre with $Z(L)+Fx$ an ideal and write $[a,x]=\lambda(a)x+z(a)$ with $z(a)$ central. The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) gives $\lambda([a,b])=0$. If $\lambda\ne0$, the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) lies in its proper kernel. If $\lambda=0$, Jacobi gives $[[a,b],x]=0$, so the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) lies in the proper [centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra](centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of $x$. The abelian case is immediate. This condition does not imply solvability: $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$ direct sum the [affine Lie algebra of the line](affine-lie-algebra-of-the-line.md) has zero centre and a one-dimensional ideal in the affine factor, but is not solvable.
