# Bias spectroscopy of one-dimensional subbands

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear conductance of a quantum point contact](nonlinear-conductance-of-a-quantum-point-contact.md)

In a rigid spectrum, source and drain threshold lines in a bias-versus-gate map satisfy $E_n(V_g)=\mu_S$ or $\mu_D$. Where adjacent thresholds align with opposite edges of the bias window, their spacing equals the window width $e|V|$. For symmetric bias and [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md), neighboring channel counts at those edges give a differential [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) halfway between integer $2e^2/h$ plateaus. The dc ratio $I/V$ is a different observable; barrier motion and heating can modify the interpretation.
