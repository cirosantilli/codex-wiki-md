# Virial mass of a dark-matter halo

↑ **Parent:** [Virial radius of a dark-matter halo](virial-radius-of-a-dark-matter-halo.md)

If the virial radius is defined relative to critical density $\rho_c$, the virial mass is

$$
M_v=\frac{4\pi}{3}v\rho_cr_v^3.
$$
