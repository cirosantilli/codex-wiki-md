# Sedimentation length

↑ **Parent:** [Colloidal stability](colloidal-stability.md)

For noninteracting particles with positive buoyant mass $m_b$ in a gravitational field, a dilute [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) profile is proportional to $e^{-z/\ell_g}$ with $\ell_g=k_BT/(m_bg)$. Uniformity over a vessel height $H$ requires $H\ll\ell_g$. Requiring only $\ell_g$ to exceed a particle radius is a weaker microscopic gravity-versus-diffusion condition.
