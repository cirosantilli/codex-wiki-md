# Scanning gate microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Microscopy](microscopy.md)

A conducting scanning-probe tip acts as a movable electrostatic gate. Mapping conductance changes as the tip perturbs a [quantum point contact](quantum-point-contact.md) or surrounding electron gas probes [quantum transport](quantum-transport.md). The image is a response to the tip, not automatically a noninvasive map of the unperturbed local current density.
