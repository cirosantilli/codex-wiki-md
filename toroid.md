# Toroid

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toroid)

A [toroid](toroid.md) is a ring-shaped [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md) obtained by rotating a closed planar profile about an axis in its plane that stays outside the profile. A circular profile gives a [torus](torus.md); a rectangular profile gives a ring with rectangular cross-section. The enclosed solid supplies the core geometry in a toroidal winding, while the mathematical surface is its boundary. The distinction matters when computing a [surface area](surface-area.md) versus a [volume](volume.md).
