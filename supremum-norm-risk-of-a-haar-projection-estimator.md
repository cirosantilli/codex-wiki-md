# Supremum norm risk of a Haar projection estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Haar projection estimator in Gaussian white noise](haar-projection-estimator-in-gaussian-white-noise.md)

The cellwise error of the [Haar projection estimator in Gaussian white noise](haar-projection-estimator-in-gaussian-white-noise.md) is $\sqrt{2^J/n}$ times a standard normal variable. Its [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) is therefore exactly that factor times the [Gaussian maximum](gaussian-maximum.md) over $2^J$ cells. The [Gaussian maximum bound without independence](gaussian-maximum-bound-without-independence.md) gives expected error at most $\sqrt{2^J(2J+2)\log2/n}$. This concerns the stochastic error about the projection; approximation bias must be added when estimating the full drift.
