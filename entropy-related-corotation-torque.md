# Entropy-related corotation torque

↑ **Parent:** [Corotation torque](corotation-torque.md)

An entropy contrast carried by [gas](gas.md) executing a horseshoe turn creates a [density](density.md) asymmetry around an embedded [planet](planet.md). The resulting torque can oppose the usual negative wave torque. Its persistence depends on thermal transport and the replenishment of the background entropy gradient.
