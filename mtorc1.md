# mTORC1

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md)

Mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1, a nutrient-responsive [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) complex. Its activity affects initiation through [4E-BP proteins](4e-bp-protein.md) and other substrates, and also regulates [autophagy](autophagy.md) and growth investment.
