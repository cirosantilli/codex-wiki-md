# Motion blur

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

[Motion blur](motion-blur.md) arises when a detector averages changing image positions during a finite exposure rather than recording an instantaneous position. For a stationary harmonic optical trap with position variance $k_BT/k$ and relaxation time $\tau$, rectangular exposure of duration $W$ reduces that variance by the factor $2[\alpha-1+e^{-\alpha}]/\alpha^2$, where $\alpha=W/\tau$. Integrating the exponential position covariance over both exposure times proves this formula. Ignoring the reduction makes a naive equipartition stiffness estimate too large.[§II, equations (12)–(15): exposure-averaged variance](the-effect-of-integration-time-on-fluctuation-measurements-calibrating-an-optical-trap-in-the-presence-of-motion-blur-by-wesley-p-wong-and-others.md)
