# Generalized finite automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-state machine](finite-state-machine.md)

A finite directed automaton whose [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) labels are [regular expressions](regular-expression.md) rather than individual letters. A path accepts concatenations of words in its successive labels, with choices over all accepting paths combined by [union](set-union.md).
