# Grain boundary strengthening

↑ **Parent:** [Grain boundary](grain-boundary.md)

Resistance to plastic flow associated with [grain boundaries](grain-boundary.md) obstructing or transmitting [dislocations](dislocation.md). In the ordinary Hall-Petch regime, smaller grains raise the yield [stress](stress.md), but at high [temperature](temperature-split.md) the same boundaries can accelerate [diffusion creep](diffusion-creep.md).
