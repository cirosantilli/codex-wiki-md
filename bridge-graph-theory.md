# Bridge (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_(graph_theory))

A bridge is an edge whose deletion increases the number of components of a graph. Equivalently, it belongs to no cycle; within its original component, deleting it makes the [connected graph](connected-graph.md) disconnected.
