# Conformal invariance of the two-dimensional Gaussian free field

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum Gaussian free field](continuum-gaussian-free-field.md)

If $\phi:D\to\widetilde D$ is a [conformal map](conformal-map.md), pulling back a zero-boundary Gaussian free field on $\widetilde D$ gives a zero-boundary Gaussian free field on $D$. This follows from the conformal invariance of the planar Dirichlet Green function.
