# Polynomial growth of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Growth function of a finitely generated group](growth-function-of-a-finitely-generated-group.md)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) has polynomial growth when, for some constants $C,D$, its [growth function of a finitely generated group](growth-function-of-a-finitely-generated-group.md) satisfies

$$
\beta_{G,S}(r)\leq C(1+r)^D.
$$

This property is independent of the finite generating set $S$.
