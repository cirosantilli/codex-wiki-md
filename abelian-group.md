# Abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_group)

An abelian group is a [group](group-split.md) whose operation is commutative: $gh=hg$ for every $g,h$ in the group.
