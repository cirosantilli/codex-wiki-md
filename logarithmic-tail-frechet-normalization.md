<h1 id="logarithmic-tail-frechet-normalization">Logarithmic-tail Fréchet normalization</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md)

For a [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) with tail $1-F(x)=1/(x\log x)$, let $a_n>1$ solve $a_n\log a_n=n$. Then $n(1-F(a_nx))\to1/x$ for $x>0$, so the normalized [sample maximum](sample-maximum.md) tends to the shape-one [Fréchet distribution](frechet-distribution.md). The inequalities $n/\log n<a_n<n/(\log n-\log\log n)$ for large $n$ show $a_n\sim n/\log n$, allowing that simpler scale too.
