# Odd-momentum fermionic Bogoliubov angle

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

For a spinless [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) $c_p=\cos\theta_p\,\alpha_p+i\sin\theta_p\,\alpha_{-p}^\dagger$, consistency of the doubled [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md) requires $\cos\theta_{-p}=\cos\theta_p$ and $\sin\theta_{-p}=-\sin\theta_p$. Thus choose $\theta_{-p}=-\theta_p$ modulo $2\pi$. The cross [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) from the first-row formulas is $\{c_p,c_{-p}\}=i\sin(\theta_p+\theta_{-p})$; a nontrivial even angle would violate the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). A self-paired zero mode must instead have vanishing mixing in this convention. Real-angle [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) within each two-by-two block alone does not enforce consistency between duplicated momentum labels.
