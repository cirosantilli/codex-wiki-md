# Tree-level electroweak gauge-boson masses

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

For the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) vacuum $(0,v)^T/\sqrt2$, the neutral [gauge-boson mass matrix](gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) in $(W^3,B)$ is $(v^2/4)\begin{pmatrix}g^2&-gg'\\-gg'&g'^2\end{pmatrix}$. Its zero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) is proportional to $(g',g)$, the [photon](photon.md); the perpendicular vector is the [Z boson](z-boson.md). The charged fields $W^\pm=(W^1\mp iW^2)/\sqrt2$ have the stated [W boson](w-boson.md) mass. Thus $m_W=m_Z\cos\theta_W$, where $\theta_W$ is the [Weinberg angle](weinberg-angle.md). The unbroken charge generator annihilates the vacuum and explains the zero eigenvalue.
