# Nuclear magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_magnetic_moment)

The [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). Its relation to nuclear [spin](spin.md) is expressed by the [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md); its interaction with an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) underlies [nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md).
