# Trace form of a Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) $\rho:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V)$, its trace form is the symmetric bilinear form

$$
(x,y)_V=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(x)\rho(y)).
$$

It is invariant: $([x,y],z)_V=(x,[y,z])_V$.
