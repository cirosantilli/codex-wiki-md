# Claim size

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate claims model](aggregate-claims-model.md)

The monetary amount of an individual insurance claim. Its [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) describes loss severity, separately from the [claim count](claim-count.md). In an independent [random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md), severity mean $\mu$ and expected count combine to give aggregate mean $\mathbb EN\,\mu$.
