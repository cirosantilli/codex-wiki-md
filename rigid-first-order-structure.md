# Rigid first-order structure

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism of a first-order structure](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md)

A first-order structure is rigid when its identity map is its only automorphism. Every finite linear order is rigid, whereas $(\mathbb Z,<)$ is nonrigid because every translation is an order automorphism.

Rigidity here means absence of nonidentity [automorphisms of a first-order structure](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md); it does not assert the mechanical rigidity of a rod-and-hinge framework.
