# International Temperature Scale of 1990

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature-split.md)

The [International Temperature Scale of 1990](international-temperature-scale-of-1990.md) is a practical scale for reproducible [temperature](temperature-split.md) measurements. Its assigned reference points and interpolation procedures approximate [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md). A calibrated [standard platinum resistance thermometer](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) transfers this scale over specified intervals; it does not define the modern [kelvin](kelvin.md).[§2.3.1: practical temperature scales, ITS-90, and thermodynamic temperature](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
