# Effective population size

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_population_size)

Effective population size is the size of an idealized population giving the relevant rate of [genetic drift](genetic-drift.md) or ancestral coalescence in the population being modeled. In the standard neutral diploid coalescent, time is measured in $2N_e$ generations and the population-scaled [mutation](mutation.md) parameter is $\theta=4N_e\mu$.
