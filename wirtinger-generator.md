# Wirtinger generator

↑ **Parent:** [Wirtinger presentation](wirtinger-presentation.md)

A Wirtinger generator is the [meridian of a knot](meridian-of-a-knot.md) associated with an arc of a [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md) between successive undercrossings. The overpassing meridian conjugates the incoming meridian to the outgoing meridian, with exponent determined by the crossing sign.
