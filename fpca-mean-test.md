# FPCA mean test

↑ **Parent:** [Functional mean test](functional-mean-test.md)

For covariance eigenpairs $(\lambda_k,\phi_k)$, the $K$-coordinate FPCA mean statistic is $n\sum_{k=1}^K\langle\overline X,\phi_k\rangle^2/\lambda_k$. Under a zero-mean null and standard estimation conditions, its plug-in version converges to a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $K$ degrees of freedom.
