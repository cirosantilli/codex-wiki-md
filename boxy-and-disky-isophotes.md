# Boxy and disky isophotes

↑ **Parent:** [Isophote](isophote.md)

Writing an [isophote](isophote.md)'s radial residual about its fitted ellipse as $\delta r=a_4\cos4\theta+\cdots$, positive $a_4$ extends the major and minor axes and gives a pointed, disky shape; negative $a_4$ extends diagonal directions and gives a boxy shape. The sign stated here is for radial residuals, not an unconverted brightness residual. Massive slowly rotating [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md) commonly have boxy [isophotes](isophote.md), while embedded rotating disks commonly make lower-mass [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md) disky.
