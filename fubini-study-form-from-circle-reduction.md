# Fubini-Study form from circle reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Fubini-Study form](fubini-study-form.md)

The scalar [circle group](circle-group.md) action on $\mathbb C^{n+1}$ with the [standard symplectic form](standard-symplectic-form.md) has [moment map](moment-map.md) $\mu=-|z|^2/2$ in the convention $\iota_{X_H}\omega=dH$. Reduction of the radius-$R$ sphere gives [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) and the form $\omega_R=(iR^2/2)\partial\bar\partial\log(1+|w|^2)$. Its area on a [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) is $\pi R^2$. Thus $R=\sqrt2$ yields the [Fubini-Study form](fubini-study-form.md) normalized to projective-line area $2\pi$, while $R=1$ yields half that form. The [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md) identifies the quotient.
