# Epitope

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epitope)

An [epitope](epitope.md) is the molecular feature recognized by an [antibody](antibody.md). [Protein denaturation](denaturation-biochemistry.md) can expose an [epitope](epitope.md) or destroy the spatial arrangement required for its recognition; an [antibody](antibody.md) that works on a folded [protein](protein-split.md) need not work in [SDS-PAGE](sds-page.md)-based [Western blot antibody detection](western-blot-antibody-detection.md).
