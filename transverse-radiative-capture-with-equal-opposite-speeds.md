# Transverse radiative capture with equal opposite speeds

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

Two particles with unequal rest energies $r,R$ and equal opposite [velocities](velocity.md) have total [energy](energy-split.md) $\gamma(r+R)$ but nonzero total [momentum](momentum-split.md). If they form a composite while emitting a photon transverse to their initial axis, the final composite rest energy $Q$ satisfies the displayed identity. It follows by squaring the residual [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). Neglecting binding, put $Q=r+R$; the minimum incoming energy is $(r+R)[\chi+\sqrt{1+\chi^2}]$, where $\chi=\varepsilon(r+R)/(4rR)$. An exact threshold requires the composite rest mass, not just the incoming constituent masses.
