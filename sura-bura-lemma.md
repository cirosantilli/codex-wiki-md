<h1 id="sura-bura-lemma">Šura-Bura lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-component](quasi-component.md)

In a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md), each [connected component](connected-component.md) is its [quasi-component](quasi-component.md) and has a neighborhood basis of [clopen](clopen-set.md) sets. Let $Q$ be the [intersection](set-intersection.md) of [clopen](clopen-set.md) neighborhoods of $x$. It is [compact](compact-space.md). If it splits into two nonempty [compact](compact-space.md) sets, normality separates them by disjoint [open neighborhoods](open-neighbourhood.md). The [finite intersection characterization of compactness](finite-intersection-characterization-of-compactness.md) gives a finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) of [clopen](clopen-set.md) neighborhoods lying in their [union](set-union.md). Its [intersection](set-intersection.md) with the neighborhood containing $x$ is then [clopen](clopen-set.md) and excludes part of $Q$, a contradiction. Thus $Q$ is [connected](connected-space.md) and equals the component. Applying the same finite-intersection argument to any neighborhood of $Q$ proves the neighborhood-basis assertion. [Formalized Šura-Bura results, including the compact Euclidean case](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/hvg/Isabelle/dist/Isabelle/browser_info/HOL/HOL-Analysis/Homotopy.html).
