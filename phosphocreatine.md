# Phosphocreatine

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Phosphocreatine](phosphocreatine.md) buffers ATP supply through reversible phosphate transfer catalysed by creatine kinase. Its depletion and recovery during muscle exercise can be followed using phosphorus NMR spectroscopy.
