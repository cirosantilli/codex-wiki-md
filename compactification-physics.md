# Compactification (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical physics](mathematical-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactification_(physics))

Compactification makes some spatial dimensions compact, so modes along them acquire discrete momenta. At energies below the first nonzero compact-mode mass, a [dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md) to the zero modes gives a lower-dimensional [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md). The compact volume fixes the gravitational kinetic coefficient in an unwarped product background.
