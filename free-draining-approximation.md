# Free-draining approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic interaction](hydrodynamic-interaction.md)

In the free-draining approximation, each separated component of a body experiences its isolated viscous drag, and [hydrodynamic interactions](hydrodynamic-interaction.md) between components are neglected. For widely separated spheres of radii $a_i$ in an unbounded [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) of [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\mu$, [Stokes law](stokes-s-law.md) gives $\mathbf f_i=-\zeta_i\mathbf v_i$, with $\zeta_i=6\pi\mu a_i$. This is a leading approximation in sphere size divided by separation. Any connecting rod must also have negligible drag; a small diameter alone does not guarantee this for an arbitrarily long rod.
