# FOXP2

↑ **Parent:** [Gene](gene.md)

The human [gene](gene.md) encoding a [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) implicated in [speech](speech.md) and [language](language.md) development through rare pathogenic variants. Related [genes](gene.md) in other vertebrates provide mechanistic models, but the human [phenotype](phenotype.md) is not equivalent to any single animal motor task. A gene-to-phenotype relationship does not by itself isolate one affected [brain](brain.md) structure.[Nature 413, 519–523; KE-family cosegregation and independent chromosome-translocation case](a-forkhead-domain-gene-is-mutated-in-a-severe-speech-and-language-disorder-by-cecilia-s-l-lai-and-others.md)
