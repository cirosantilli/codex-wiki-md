# Ray class field of Q of square root minus three modulo seven

↑ **Parent:** [Ray class field](ray-class-field.md)

The [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) is $\mathbb Z[\omega]$, where $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$, and its [class number](class-number.md) is one by the [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md). Modulo seven it is $\mathbb F_7\times\mathbb F_7$. The six [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) reduce to the kernel of the product map on the two residue [unit groups](unit-group.md), so the [ray class group](ray-class-group.md) is $\mathbb F_7^\times$, of order six. The relative [Artin symbol](artin-symbol.md) on $K(\zeta_7)$ is exponentiation by the [norm of a fractional ideal](norm-of-a-fractional-ideal.md) modulo seven. It kills the principal ray subgroup, and $[K(\zeta_7):K]=6$, proving that this is the [ray class field](ray-class-field.md).
