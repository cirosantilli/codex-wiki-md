# Constant-force spatially gated motor

↑ **Parent:** [Spatially gated myosin attachment](spatially-gated-myosin-attachment.md)

A prescribed-speed [molecular motor](molecular-motor.md) model in which a head attaches only within a phase window of width $x_1$, stays bound to phase $x_2$, and then detaches at a constant rate before the next period $\Delta$. If the binding window and detachment tail are short relative to the bound plateau, the phase-average [force](force-split.md) is the plateau fraction multiplied by its attachment [probability](probability.md) and bound-head [force](force-split.md) $f_0$.
