# Statistical homogeneity

↑ **Parent:** [Power spectrum](power-spectrum.md)

A random field is statistically homogeneous when all of its joint distributions are invariant under a common spatial translation of their arguments. Its momentum-space correlation functions consequently contain a [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) imposing total-momentum conservation.
