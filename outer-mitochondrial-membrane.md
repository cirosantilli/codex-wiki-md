# Outer mitochondrial membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

The [outer mitochondrial membrane](outer-mitochondrial-membrane.md) separates the surrounding [cytosol](cytosol.md) from the [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md). Channels permit passage of many small [molecules](molecule-split.md), while the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) supplies the main selective barrier between them and the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md).[Chapter 14, The Mitochondrion Contains an Outer Membrane, an Inner Membrane, and Two Internal Compartments: outer-membrane channels and inner-membrane selectivity](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

Small-solute access does not imply unrestricted access for [proteins](protein-split.md). The [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md) addition in the [isolated mitochondria respiration experiment](isolated-mitochondria-respiration-experiment.md) tests a consequence of damage to this boundary. [Organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) describes purposeful delivery across mitochondrial boundaries.
