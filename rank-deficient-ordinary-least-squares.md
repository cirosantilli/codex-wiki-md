# Rank-deficient ordinary least squares

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md)

If a [design matrix](design-matrix.md) $X$ has deficient [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md), every [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) minimizer has the form $X^+Y+z$ with $z\in\ker X$, where $X^+$ is the [Moore-Penrose inverse](moore-penrose-inverse.md). Hence the fitted values are unique but the coefficients are not: the minimizers form an [affine subspace](affine-subspace.md) of dimension $p-\operatorname{rank}X$.
