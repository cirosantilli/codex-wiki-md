# Banach norm rigidity from continuous point evaluations

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent norms](equivalent-norms.md)

On $C(X)$ for compact Hausdorff $X$, any complete norm making every [point evaluation functional](point-evaluation-functional.md) continuous is equivalent to the [uniform norm](supremum-norm.md). If $f_n$ converges in the new norm to $f$ and uniformly to $g$, evaluation gives $f(x)=g(x)$ for every $x$, so the identity map between the two [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) has closed graph. The [closed graph theorem](closed-graph-theorem.md) and [open mapping theorem](open-mapping-theorem-functional-analysis.md) make the identity and its inverse bounded. Completeness of the proposed norm is essential to this argument.
