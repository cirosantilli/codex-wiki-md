# Occupation-times formula

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian occupation time](brownian-occupation-time.md)

For a continuous semimartingale $X$ with [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) $L_t^a(X)$,

$$
\int_0^tf(X_s)d[X]_s=\int_{\mathbb R}f(a)L_t^a(X)da
$$

for every nonnegative measurable function $f$.
