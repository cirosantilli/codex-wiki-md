# Two-qubit gate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum logic gate](quantum-logic-gate.md)

A two-qubit gate is a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) on two [qubit](qubit.md) lines, extended by the identity on the others. It may be entangling, as a [CNOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) is, or a product of local operations. In a [Hamiltonian simulation](hamiltonian-simulation.md) model allowing arbitrary two-qubit gates, the exponential of a Hermitian term supported on two lines counts as one such gate. Compiling it into a fixed discrete [universal quantum gate set](universal-quantum-gate-set.md) introduces a separate approximation cost.
