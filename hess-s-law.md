<h1 id="hess-s-law">Hess's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemistry](thermochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess's_law)

[Hess's law](hess-s-law.md) states that compatible [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) [enthalpies](enthalpy.md) add when their equations add. Reversing a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) reverses its [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) change; multiplying the entire [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) equation by a factor multiplies that change by the same factor. The reason is that [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) is a [state function](state-function.md): intermediate states cancel from the differences. A cycle supplies one independent balance relation; it cannot determine two unknown inputs without further information.
