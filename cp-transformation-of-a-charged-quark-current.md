# CP transformation of a charged quark current

↑ **Parent:** [Quark rephasing and CP conservation](quark-rephasing-and-cp-conservation.md)

Under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md), $\bar u\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma^5)d$ becomes $-\bar d\gamma^\mu(1+\gamma^5)u$. [Parity](parity.md) reverses the chirality again and applies the Lorentz parity matrix. The minus sign cancels that of charge conjugation on the charged vector field. Thus each charged-current operator is exchanged with its Hermitian conjugate under [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md), giving the displayed transformation for the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) $V$. For nondegenerate quark masses, physical CP conservation means that allowed quark rephasings can make $V$ real. A nonzero [Jarlskog invariant](jarlskog-invariant.md) obstructs this. A merely complex phase convention is not proof of physical CP violation, and $V=I$ is a CP-conserving counterexample to unconditional noninvariance.
