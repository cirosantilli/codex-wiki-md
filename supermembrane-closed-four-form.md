# Supermembrane closed four-form

↑ **Parent:** [Four-dimensional supermembrane](four-dimensional-supermembrane.md)

The four-dimensional membrane [Fierz rearrangement](fierz-identity.md) identity $(C\gamma_{mn})_{(\alpha\beta}(C\gamma^n)_{\gamma\delta)}=0$ makes the displayed supertranslation-invariant four-form closed. It is locally the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) of a three-form potential, which can change by an exact form under target [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). Pulling the potential back to a closed [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) gives an invariant [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md). The charge magnitude and normalization of this form are fixed together by cancellation of the area variation under [kappa symmetry](kappa-symmetry.md).
