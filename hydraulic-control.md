# Hydraulic control

↑ **Parent:** [Froude number](froude-number.md)

A [hydraulic control](hydraulic-control.md) is a critical section where a [shallow water](shallow-water-approximation.md) flow passes smoothly between [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md) and [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md). One long-wave [characteristic speed](characteristic-speed.md) relative to the bed vanishes there. Regularity supplies an extra relation between discharge, depth and channel geometry, allowing the control to set the discharge or upstream state. A critical depth alone is not sufficient for a regular transcritical solution: the channel forcing must also be compatible there.
