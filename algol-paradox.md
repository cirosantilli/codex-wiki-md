# Algol paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Algol binary](algol-binary.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algol_paradox)

The Algol paradox is the apparently inconsistent evolutionary ordering in an [Algol binary](algol-binary.md): the less massive star is more evolved, whereas higher-mass isolated [main sequence](main-sequence.md) stars normally exhaust core fuel sooner. [Binary mass-ratio reversal](binary-mass-ratio-reversal.md) resolves it by identifying the present donor as the initially more massive star.
