# Dust settling in an astrophysical disk

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

Dust settling is the vertical drift of solid particles toward a disk's midplane under gravity and [drag force](drag-physics.md). In the strong-drag limit, a particle with [aerodynamic stopping time](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md) $\tau$ settles through a linear vertical gravitational field at rate $\Omega_z^2\tau$.
