# Volumetric strain

↑ **Parent:** [Strain](strain.md)

The first-order relative change of volume for a small [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md). It is the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md). For a radial [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) $u(r)\mathbf e_r$ in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), $e=u'+2u/r$.
