# Attractive and repulsive inverse-square closest approaches

↑ **Parent:** [Central-force radial turning point](central-force-radial-turning-point.md)

For unit [mass](mass.md), incoming [speed](speed.md) $v>0$, [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b>0$, and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $\mp\kappa/r$, put $q=\kappa/v^2>0$. At closest approach, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) and [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) give the displayed radii and tangential [speeds](speed.md) $u_\mp=bv/p_\mp$. Consequently $p_-p_+=b^2$ and $u_-u_+=v^2$. These relations compare equal force magnitudes. The attractive radial case $b=0$ is a singular collision, so the speed-product statement does not extend to that endpoint.
