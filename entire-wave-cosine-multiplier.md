# Entire wave cosine multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform method for the wave equation](fourier-transform-method-for-the-wave-equation.md)

For real wave speed $c$ and $t\ge0$, define the complex-frequency cosine through the entire series $C_t(z)=\sum_{j\ge0}(-1)^j(ct)^{2j}(z\cdot z)^j/(2j)!$. The expression is independent of the square-root branch even though the square root itself need not be entire. Writing $z=a+ib$ gives $|\operatorname{Im}\sqrt{z\cdot z}|\le|b|$, and hence $|C_t(z)|\le e^{|c|t|\operatorname{Im}z|}$. The [Paley–Wiener–Schwartz theorem](paley-wiener-schwartz-theorem.md) therefore makes its inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) supported in the radius-$|c|t$ ball. Multiplication by $C_t$ evolves initial displacement with zero initial velocity in the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), giving [finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md) for compactly supported initial [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
