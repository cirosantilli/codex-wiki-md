# Siletzia

↑ **Parent:** [Cascadia subduction zone](cascadia-subduction-zone.md)

An accreted basaltic terrane forming important parts of the forearc basement in the Pacific Northwest. Spatial variations in its thickness, stiffness and permeability can affect the interpretation of [gravity anomalies](gravity-anomaly.md) and the fluid/stress environment above the [megathrust](megathrust.md). A proposed relation to central Cascadia creep uses gravity, geodetic and paleoseismic observations, rather than lithology alone.[Discussion and conclusions, Siletzia and fluid-pressure interpretation](central-cascadia-subduction-zone-creep-by-gina-m-schmalzle-and-others.md)
