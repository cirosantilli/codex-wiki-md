# Unboundedness of derivative evaluation in the supremum norm

↑ **Parent:** [Supremum norm](supremum-norm.md)

On a nondegenerate interval, point evaluation of the [derivative](derivative.md) is unbounded for the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) on smooth functions. The functions $f_N(x)=\sin(N(x-x_0))$ have [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) at most one but $f_N'(x_0)=N$. Adding the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of the [derivative](derivative.md) makes this evaluation a [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md).
