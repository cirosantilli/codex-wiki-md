# Workload Chernoff bound for independent increments

↑ **Parent:** [Effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md)

For independent identically distributed traffic increments and constant service $C$, let $a(s)=\mathbb E[e^{s(X-C)}]$. If $a(s)<1$, apply the [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) at each candidate workload window and sum a geometric series. This bounds the stationary random-walk supremum. It explains the [effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md) criterion $\alpha(s)<C$ and distinguishes a queue's infinite-horizon overflow event from a single-window traffic event.
