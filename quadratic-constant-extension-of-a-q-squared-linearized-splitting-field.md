# Quadratic constant extension of a q-squared linearized splitting field

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized polynomial over a finite field](linearized-polynomial-over-a-finite-field.md)

For even $d=2n$, the roots of $X^{q^d}+X^{q^2}+TX$ form an $n$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over $\mathbb F_{q^2}$. Ratios $(c\alpha)/\alpha$ for a nonzero root $\alpha$ show that the [splitting field](splitting-field.md) contains every $c\in\mathbb F_{q^2}$. The subgroup fixing that quadratic constant extension is normal of index two and lies in $GL(n,q^2)$; applying projective transitivity over $\mathbb F_{q^2}(T)$ makes it contain $SL(n,q^2)$. It therefore normalizes that [special linear group](special-linear-group.md), while the full group acts semilinearly.
