<h1 id="traction-of-translating-sphere-papkovich-neuber-potentials">Traction of translating-sphere Papkovich–Neuber potentials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unscaled Papkovich–Neuber representation](unscaled-papkovich-neuber-representation.md)

For $\Phi=U+\alpha aU/r$ and $\chi=\beta a^3U\cdot\nabla(1/r)$ in the [Unscaled Papkovich–Neuber representation](unscaled-papkovich-neuber-representation.md), the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) gives the displayed traction at $r=a$. The $\alpha$ contribution is the [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) stress; the $\beta$ contribution is twice the viscosity times the Hessian of its harmonic scalar potential. The prefactor is $6\mu/a$, not $12\mu/a$. The rigid translating-sphere limit is a direct normalization check.
