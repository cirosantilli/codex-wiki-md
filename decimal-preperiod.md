# Decimal preperiod

↑ **Parent:** [Repeating decimal](repeating-decimal.md)

The shortest prefix before the repeating tail of a [decimal representation](decimal-representation.md). For a [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md) with [denominator](denominator.md) $2^u5^v m$, where $m>1$ is [coprime](coprime-integers.md) to ten, its length is $\max(u,v)$; the tail length is given by [decimal period from multiplicative order](decimal-period-from-multiplicative-order.md).
