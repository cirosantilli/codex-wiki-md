# Phase-speed bound for unstable stratified shear modes

↑ **Parent:** [Power-transformed Taylor–Goldstein energy identity](power-transformed-taylor-goldstein-energy-identity.md)

For an unstable [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) mode in a finite channel, take $a=1$ in the [power-transformed Taylor–Goldstein energy identity](power-transformed-taylor-goldstein-energy-identity.md). Its [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) gives

$$
c_r=\frac{\int U(|q'|^2+k^2|q|^2)dz}{\int(|q'|^2+k^2|q|^2)dz}.
$$

Thus the real [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) lies strictly between the extremes of a nonconstant smooth shear profile. Equality would force the regular [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) to vanish on an interval, hence everywhere by uniqueness for its [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md).
