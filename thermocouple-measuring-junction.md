# Thermocouple measuring junction

↑ **Parent:** [Thermocouple](thermocouple.md)

The [thermocouple measuring junction](thermocouple-measuring-junction.md) joins A and B at the location whose [temperature](temperature-split.md) is to be inferred. Its own [temperature](temperature-split.md) is what the electrical measurement estimates. To infer a surrounding object's [temperature](temperature-split.md), the junction must approach [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with that object: insufficient immersion, [heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md) along the wires, or a changing environment can keep the junction at another [temperature](temperature-split.md). A stable electrical display alone does not establish that this thermal condition holds. The two-[heat bath](heat-bath.md) procedure and terminal-reversal controls under [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md) provide the starting experiment; the practical issue here is the reference and conversion chain.
