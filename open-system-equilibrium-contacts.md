# Open-system equilibrium contacts

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential equality for particle exchange](chemical-potential-equality-for-particle-exchange.md)

Unconstrained thermal, mechanical and particle-exchange contacts require equality of [temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) of each exchangeable species. Walls, external fields, interfaces and chemical reactions change which variations are allowed.
