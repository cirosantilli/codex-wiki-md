# Bounded vertical modes of a sech-squared magnetized disk

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-thickness magnetorotational instability criterion](finite-thickness-magnetorotational-instability-criterion.md)

For density proportional to $\operatorname{sech}^2(z/H)$, the magnetic vertical equation $F''+k^2\operatorname{sech}^2(z/H)F=0$ becomes the [Legendre differential equation](legendre-differential-equation.md) under $\xi=\tanh(z/H)$. Bounded solutions at both surfaces are [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md), with $k_n=\sqrt{n(n+1)}/H$. The $n=0$ constant function has no magnetically coupled finite wavenumber. The smallest MRI-relevant eigenvalue is $k_1^2=2/H^2$, which makes sufficiently strong [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) stabilize all allowed modes.
