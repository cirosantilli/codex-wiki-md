# Bogoliubov mode

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md)

A normal mode of linearized condensate dynamics with particle and hole components. Quantizing a suitably normalized mode gives a [Bogoliubov quasiparticle](bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md). The density and phase components of one mode share a frequency; that fact alone does not establish a twofold excitation degeneracy.
