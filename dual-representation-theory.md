# Dual representation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Post-traumatic stress disorder](post-traumatic-stress-disorder.md)

The original 1996 account distinguishes verbally accessible [memories](memory-in-animals.md) from representations accessed automatically through situational cues. It aims to explain differences between deliberate narrative retrieval and sensory reliving. This functional distinction does not by itself prove two anatomically discrete [memory](memory-in-animals.md) stores.[Psychological Review 103, 670–686; original verbally/situationally accessible memory distinction](a-dual-representation-theory-of-posttraumatic-stress-disorder-by-chris-r-brewin-and-others.md)
