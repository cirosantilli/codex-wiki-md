<h1 id="jensen-s-inequality">Jensen's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen's_inequality)

For a [convex function](convex-function.md) $f$ and an integrable [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$,

$$
f(\mathbb E X)\leq\mathbb E[f(X)].
$$

The inequality is reversed when $f$ is [concave](concave-function.md).
