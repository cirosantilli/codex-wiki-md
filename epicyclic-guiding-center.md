# Epicyclic guiding center

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

The epicyclic guiding center is the circular-orbit location about which a particle performs [epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md). In a [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), the conserved [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is $p_y=\dot y+2\Omega x=\Omega x_0/2$. The guiding center therefore labels the particle's first-order [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) offset, even while its instantaneous radial coordinate oscillates.
