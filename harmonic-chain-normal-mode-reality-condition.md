# Harmonic-chain normal-mode reality condition

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic chain](quantum-harmonic-chain.md)

The [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) of real displacement operators produces complex coordinates obeying $Q_k^\dagger=Q_{-k}$. They are not independent real oscillators. The canonical expansion $Q_k=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega_k)}(a_k+a_{-k}^\dagger)$ and $P_k=-i\sqrt{m\hbar\omega_k/2}(a_k-a_{-k}^\dagger)$ has independent occupation operators for all nonzero wavevectors, with the uniform free coordinate handled separately. At self-inverse zone-boundary wavevectors the same formulas reduce to one ordinary real oscillator.
