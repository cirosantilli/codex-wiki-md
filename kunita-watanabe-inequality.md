# Kunita-Watanabe inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

For continuous local martingales, the [total-variation process](total-variation-process.md) of their [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md) satisfies

$$
V_t([M,N])\leq[M]_t^{1/2}[N]_t^{1/2}.
$$

It is the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) for the matrix-valued measure formed by their [quadratic variations](quadratic-variation.md) and [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md).
