# Orientation of a simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

This simplicial notion is induced by an [orientation of a vector space](orientation-of-a-vector-space.md) on the affine span of the simplex.

An orientation of a simplex is an ordering of its vertices modulo even permutations.
