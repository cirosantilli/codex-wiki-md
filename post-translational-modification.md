# Post-translational modification

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A [post-translational modification](post-translational-modification.md) is a chemical change to a [protein](protein-split.md) after its synthesis, such as [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) or [protein lipidation](protein-lipidation.md). It can change activity, stability, localization or partner binding without changing the encoded sequence.
