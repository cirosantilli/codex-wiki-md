# Alpha-decay angular-momentum and parity selection rule

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha decay](alpha-decay.md)

The [ground state](ground-state.md) of an [even-even nucleus](even-even-nucleus.md) has $J^P=0^+$, and the emitted [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) also has $0^+$. In an [alpha decay](alpha-decay.md) to daughter [spin](spin.md) $J_Y$, [addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) permits total zero only when the relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) is $L=J_Y$. [Parity conservation](parity-conservation.md) gives $+1=\pi_Y(-1)^L$, hence the daughter has [natural parity](natural-parity.md), $0^+,1^-,2^+,3^-,\ldots$. This rule does not itself restrict $J_Y$ to even integers. An even-$J$ sequence such as $0^+,2^+,4^+$ requires additional nuclear structure, for example a positive-parity ground-state [nuclear rotational band](nuclear-rotational-band.md).
