<h1 id="stratification-rotation-analogy">Stratification–rotation analogy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md)

Uniformly stratified, nonrotating, two-dimensional ideal [Boussinesq equations](boussinesq-equations.md) in coordinates $(x,z)$ are equivalent to unstratified rotating equations independent of $Y$, with $(X,Y,Z)=(z,y,-x)$, rotation $2\boldsymbol\Omega=N\mathbf e_Z$, velocity $(U,V,W)=(w,\sigma/N,-u)$, and the same reduced pressure. The transverse rotating velocity represents [buoyancy perturbation](buoyancy-perturbation.md), while the rotation-axis velocity represents the stratified horizontal velocity. Matching prescribed forces may have components in the invariant plane, but no transverse component. Physical [free surface](free-surface.md) conditions are not preserved: the physical vertical direction and its normal velocity are different under the mapping.
