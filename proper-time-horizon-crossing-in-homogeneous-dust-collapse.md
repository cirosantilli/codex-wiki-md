# Proper-time horizon crossing in homogeneous dust collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Oppenheimer-Snyder model](oppenheimer-snyder-model.md)

A boundary clock in [Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse](oppenheimer-snyder-model.md) reads $\tau=a_{\max}(\eta+\sin\eta)/2$. Its [event horizon](event-horizon.md) crossing occurs at $R=2M$, giving the displayed angle, and the final [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) occurs at $\eta=\pi$. The remaining [proper time](proper-time.md) is $a_{\max}(\pi-\eta_H-\sin\eta_H)/2$. For $R_0\gg2M$ this approaches $4M/3$, but it is not exactly that value for finite release radius.
