# Massless supermultiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md)

A massless [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md) is a positive-energy [unitary irreducible representation](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md) with null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). For finite [helicity](helicity.md) states in four dimensions, half the [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) act trivially and the others form a [fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md).
