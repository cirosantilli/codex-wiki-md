# Self-intersection number

↑ **Parent:** [Normal bundle](normal-bundle.md)

For an oriented submanifold of half the dimension of its ambient manifold, the self-intersection number is the intersection number with a transverse perturbation. It equals the Euler number of the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md).
