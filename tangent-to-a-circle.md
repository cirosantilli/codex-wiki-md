# Tangent to a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

At a point $T$ on a [circle](circle.md) with centre $O$, the [tangent line](tangent-line.md) is perpendicular to the [radius](radius.md) $OT$. For example, differentiating $|\mathbf r(s)-O|^2=R^2$ along a [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) on the [circle](circle.md) gives $(\mathbf r-O)\cdot\mathbf r'=0$, showing that the radial [vector](vector.md) and tangent direction are [orthogonal vectors](orthogonal-vectors.md). Conversely, the perpendicular [straight line](straight-line.md) through $T$ has only that point in common with the [circle](circle.md): for a nonzero displacement $v$ along it, $|T+v-O|^2=R^2+|v|^2>R^2$.
