# Estimation lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estimation_lemma)

If $|f(z)|\leq M$ along a contour of length $L$, then

$$
\left|\int_C f(z)\,dz\right|\leq ML.
$$
