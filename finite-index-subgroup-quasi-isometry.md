# Finite-index subgroup quasi-isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)

If $H$ is a finite-index subgroup of a finitely generated group $G$, then $H$ is finitely generated and its inclusion into $G$ is a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md). Finite generation follows from [Schreier's lemma](schreier-s-lemma.md), while finitely many coset representatives give coarse surjectivity and uniformly bounded distortion.
