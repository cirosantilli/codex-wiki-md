# Photosynthetic state transition

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

A [photosynthetic state transition](photosynthetic-state-transition.md) redistributes excitation between photosystems in response to their relative activity. In plants, regulated phosphorylation of a mobile LHCII fraction helps alter its functional association with the two photosystems.
