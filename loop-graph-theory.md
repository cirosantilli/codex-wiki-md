# Loop (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_(graph_theory))

A self-loop is an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) whose two endpoints are the same [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). In undirected total-degree counting it contributes two to the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md), since both endpoints are incident to that [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Simple [graphs](graph-split.md) exclude self-loops; the [linearized chord diagram model](linearized-chord-diagram-model.md) retains them and starts from one self-loop.
