# Cohomological dimension bound from an affine cover

↑ **Parent:** [Leray's theorem](leray-s-theorem.md)

If a [separated scheme](separated-scheme.md) has an acyclic affine cover by $r$ open sets, then every [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) on it has vanishing [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) in degrees at least $r$. The corresponding [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md) has no terms in those degrees.
