# Chapman family lifetime versus relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Chapman cycle](chapman-cycle.md)

Under fast [Chapman cycle](chapman-cycle.md) partitioning, an [odd oxygen](odd-oxygen.md) family abundance $X$ can satisfy $\dot X=P-kX^2$. At equilibrium $P=kX_e^2$, its turnover lifetime is $X_e/P=1/(kX_e)$, while linearizing gives $\delta\dot X=-2kX_e\delta X$ and relaxation time $1/(2kX_e)$. A chemical lifetime is therefore not automatically an eigenmode relaxation time. The often-used expression $1/(2k_3[\mathrm O])$ also assumes ozone dominates the family abundance.
