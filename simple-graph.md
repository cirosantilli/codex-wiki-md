# Simple graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A simple [graph](graph-split.md) has no [self-loops](loop-graph-theory.md) and at most one [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between each pair of distinct [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). An undirected simple [graph](graph-split.md) is determined by a [set](set-split.md) of unordered [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) pairs.
