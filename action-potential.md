# Action potential

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_potential)

A regenerative transient of [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) caused by changing membrane conductances. Its roughly stereotyped waveform permits signals to propagate along an [axon](axon.md); information can depend on the number and timing of these events rather than their amplitude.
