# Action potential

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_potential)

An [action potential](action-potential.md) is a brief regenerative excursion of [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md), produced by changing [electrical resistance and conductance](electrical-resistance-and-conductance.md) through [voltage-gated ion channels](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md). In a typical fast neuronal [action potential](action-potential.md), [sodium](sodium.md) entry raises [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md), activating further [sodium](sodium.md) channels. This feedback produces the rapid rising phase. Inactivation of [sodium](sodium.md) channels and increased conductance to [potassium](potassium.md) then lower [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) again; persisting [potassium](potassium.md) conductance can produce an undershoot below the resting level. See [Purves and colleagues: the ionic basis of action potentials](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10897/).

For an illustrative electrical calculation, write inward [sodium](sodium.md) [electric current](electric-current.md) as $I_{\mathrm{Na}}=g_{\mathrm{Na}}(E_{\mathrm{Na}}-V)$. At $V=-50\,\mathrm{mV}$, taking $E_{\mathrm{Na}}=60\,\mathrm{mV}$ and $g_{\mathrm{Na}}=10\,\mathrm{nS}$ gives $I_{\mathrm{Na}}=1.1\,\mathrm{nA}$ inward. A [potassium](potassium.md) current $I_{\mathrm{K}}=g_{\mathrm{K}}(E_{\mathrm{K}}-V)$ with $E_{\mathrm{K}}=-80\,\mathrm{mV}$ and $g_{\mathrm{K}}=20\,\mathrm{nS}$ gives $-0.6\,\mathrm{nA}$, outward. These illustrative values expose opposing driving forces; a waveform requires the conductances to evolve with [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) and time, as in the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md).

An [action potential](action-potential.md) propagates along an [axon](axon.md) because local [electric currents](electric-current.md) bring neighboring regions of [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) into the regime where their [voltage-gated ion channels](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md) regenerate the signal. It is a traveling pattern of excitation, not a packet of [sodium](sodium.md) [ions](ion.md) traversing the entire [axon](axon.md). Regeneration allows an [action potential](action-potential.md) to travel without the passive attenuation of a small local voltage change. See [Ion Channels and the Electrical Properties of Membranes](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/ion-channels-and-the-electrical-properties-of-membranes.md).

The [neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md) constrains how soon another [action potential](action-potential.md) can follow. Under comparable conditions, successive [action potentials](action-potential.md) have roughly stereotyped shapes, making event count and timing useful for [neural coding](neural-coding.md); waveform details can nevertheless change with channel state and conditions. The [leaky integrate-and-fire model](leaky-integrate-and-fire-model.md) abstracts each [action potential](action-potential.md) to a threshold event and reset. Its reset jump is a mathematical rule, not the actual regenerative waveform produced by [sodium](sodium.md) and [potassium](potassium.md) currents.
