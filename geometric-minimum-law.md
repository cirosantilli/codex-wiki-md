# Geometric minimum law

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md)

For independent positive-support [geometric random variables](geometric-distribution.md) with success probabilities $p_i$, all must exceed $k$ for their minimum to exceed $k$. The resulting [survival function](survival-function.md) is $\prod_i(1-p_i)^k$. This is the positive-support [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) with the displayed success probability. Independence is needed for multiplying the survival probabilities.
