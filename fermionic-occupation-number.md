# Fermionic occupation number

↑ **Parent:** [Occupation number](occupation-number.md)

For $\{f,f^\dagger\}_+=1$ and $f^2=0$, the [occupation number](occupation-number.md) operator $f^\dagger f$ has eigenvalues zero and one. This gives the single-mode exclusion principle.
