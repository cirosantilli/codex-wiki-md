# Yang-Mills Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

In a Minkowski-space convention with metric $(+---)$ and canonically normalized [gauge fields](gauge-field.md), the displayed [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) supplies their [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md). The [Yang-Mills field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) contains a quadratic gauge-potential term, which generates [cubic and quartic Yang-Mills self-interactions](cubic-and-quartic-yang-mills-self-interactions.md) when the field-strength square is expanded. The coupling can instead be placed as an overall prefactor by rescaling the gauge potential; the normalization convention must be fixed before comparing coefficients.
