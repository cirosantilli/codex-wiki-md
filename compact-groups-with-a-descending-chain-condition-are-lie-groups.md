# Compact groups with a descending chain condition are Lie groups

↑ **Parent:** [Compact group](compact-group.md)

The [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md) makes finite-dimensional [unitary representations](unitary-representation.md) separate elements of a [compact group](compact-group.md). Intersecting their closed kernels gives the identity [subgroup](subgroup.md). If the [descending chain condition](descending-chain-condition.md) holds for closed [subgroups](subgroup.md), choose finitely many representations whose kernel intersection is already trivial: otherwise repeatedly exclude a surviving nonidentity element to obtain an infinite strict chain. Their direct sum is a [faithful representation](faithful-representation.md) into a finite-dimensional [unitary group](unitary-group.md). Compactness makes its image closed, and the [closed-subgroup theorem](closed-subgroup-theorem.md) gives the original [group](group-split.md) a compatible [Lie group](lie-group.md) structure.
