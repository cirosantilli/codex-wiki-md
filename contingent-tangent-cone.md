# Contingent tangent cone

↑ **Parent:** [Feasible region](feasible-region.md)

This cone records first-order limiting velocities of feasible points approaching $x$. The defining limits are required to exist, and the point sequences must be feasible. A local minimizer of a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) satisfies $\nabla f(x)^Td\geq0$ for every $d$ in the cone: expand $f(x_n)-f(x)$ to first order and divide by $t_n>0$. A tangent direction need not itself generate an exactly feasible straight segment. Linearizing active constraints always gives a containing [linearized tangent cone](linearized-tangent-cone.md); equality requires a suitable [constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md).
