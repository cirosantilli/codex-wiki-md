# Rosser provability predicate

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)

A Rosser provability predicate adds a bounded proof-order test to an [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md): a proof of the sentence must occur before every proof of its [negation](negation.md). For a consistent theory it has the same standard extension as ordinary provability. Internally its behavior can differ: [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) proves $\neg R_T(\ulcorner\bot\urcorner)$ by splitting proof codes before and after a fixed proof of $\neg\bot$. This does not establish the usual consistency statement for ordinary provability.
