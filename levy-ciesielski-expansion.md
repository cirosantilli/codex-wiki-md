<h1 id="levy-ciesielski-expansion">Lévy-Ciesielski expansion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy construction of Brownian motion](levy-construction-of-brownian-motion.md)

Let $H_{n,k}(s)=2^{n/2}(\mathbf1_{(0,1/2)}-\mathbf1_{(1/2,1)})(2^ns-k)$ for $n\geq0$ and $0\leq k<2^n$. Integrating these [Haar wavelets](haar-wavelet.md) gives tents of height $2^{-n/2-1}$ supported on the corresponding dyadic intervals. For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md) $Z_0,Z_{n,k}$, the displayed series, summed level by level, is exactly the succession of midpoint displacements in the [Lévy construction of Brownian motion](levy-construction-of-brownian-motion.md). Its [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) is uniform, and its limit is standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). This gives a continuous function from Gaussian coefficients even though the unintegrated unit-variance [Haar wavelet](haar-wavelet.md) series diverges in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md).
