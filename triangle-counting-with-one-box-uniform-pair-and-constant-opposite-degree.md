# Triangle counting with one box-uniform pair and constant opposite degree

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear correlation bound for the box norm](bilinear-correlation-bound-for-the-box-norm.md)

Let a [tripartite graph](tripartite-graph.md) have nonempty parts $X,Y,Z$, pair [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) values $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ on $XY,YZ,XZ$, and $\|G(X,Y)-\alpha\|_{\square}\leq c$. If every $z\in Z$ has exactly $\beta|Y|$ neighbors in $Y$, its normalized [triangle count](triangle-count.md) $\tau$ obeys

$$
|\tau-\alpha\beta\gamma|\leq c\sqrt{\beta\gamma}.
$$

The constant-degree assumption makes the contribution of the constant $\alpha$ exactly $\alpha\beta\gamma$. For each fixed $z$, apply the [bilinear correlation bound for the box norm](bilinear-correlation-bound-for-the-box-norm.md) to the [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) of its two [vertex neighbourhoods](vertex-neighbourhood.md). Their squared $L^2$ [norms](norm.md) are $\beta$ and the relative $X$-degree of $z$. Average over $z$ and use the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to bound the mean square root of that degree by $\sqrt\gamma$.
