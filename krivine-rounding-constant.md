# Krivine rounding constant

↑ **Parent:** [Krivine rounding scheme](krivine-rounding-scheme.md)

The [Krivine rounding scheme](krivine-rounding-scheme.md) yields $c_K=0.56109985\ldots$ as its guaranteed objective factor. It is obtained by normalizing $\sinh t=1$, so $t=\operatorname{arsinh}(1)$ and $c_K=2t/\pi$. This proof gives a valid universal factor for the bipartite sign problem, not a proof that it is optimal.
