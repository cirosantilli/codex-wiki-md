# Cylinder (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylinder)

A [cylinder](cylinder-geometry.md) is generated by translating a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) along parallel lines transverse to its plane. A [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) uses a [circle](circle.md) as its generating [plane curve](plane-curve.md); it is right when the generators are perpendicular to that plane. The term can denote the lateral surface or, for a bounded portion with end caps, the enclosed solid. The [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) curvature formulas describe the smooth lateral surface.
