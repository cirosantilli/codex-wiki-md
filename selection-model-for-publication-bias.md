# Selection model for publication bias

↑ **Parent:** [Publication bias](publication-bias.md)

A selection model combines a distribution for all study results with a [probability](probability.md) $\pi(y)$ that a result becomes available. The observed distribution is reweighted by this [probability](probability.md) and renormalized. Assumed or estimated selection mechanisms permit [sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md) of a [meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md), but unavailable results generally prevent a fully assumption-free correction.
