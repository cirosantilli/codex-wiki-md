# Spectral emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Emissivity](emissivity.md)

[Spectral emissivity](spectral-emissivity.md) compares emission with an ideal [blackbody](blackbody.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) at a specified [wavelength](wavelength.md), or [frequency](frequency.md) under a consistently transformed convention. A hemispherical [spectral emissivity](spectral-emissivity.md) uses spectral [radiant exitance](radiant-exitance.md); a directional [spectral emissivity](spectral-emissivity.md) uses [spectral radiance](spectral-radiance.md). Always state which angular convention applies. A wavelength-selective coating can emit strongly in one band while emitting weakly elsewhere, so one band measurement does not determine [total hemispherical emissivity](total-hemispherical-emissivity.md).
