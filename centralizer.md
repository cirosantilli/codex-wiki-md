# Centralizer

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer and normalizer](centralizer-and-normalizer.md)

The centralizer of $x\in G$ is the stabilizer of $x$ under the [conjugation action](conjugation-action.md):

$$
C_G(x)=\{g\in G:gx=xg\}.
$$
