# Degenerate critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

A [critical point](critical-point.md) whose [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is a [singular matrix](singular-matrix.md). The [second-derivative test](second-derivative-test.md) does not completely classify such a point. Higher-order terms can produce a [local minimum](local-minimum.md), a [local maximum](local-maximum.md), or a [saddle point of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md).
