# Cylindrical magnetostatic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md)

A cylindrical magnetostatic equilibrium is an [axisymmetric vector field](axisymmetric-vector-field.md) and [pressure](pressure.md) configuration independent of the axial coordinate. Its radial [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) balances the [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md), as expressed by [cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md).
