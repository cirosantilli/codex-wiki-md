# Area of a regular octagon

↑ **Parent:** [Regular polygon](regular-polygon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_of_a_regular_octagon)

A regular octagon of side length s has area 2(1+sqrt(2))s^2.
