# Bloch stability operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sideband instability](sideband-instability.md)

For a spatially periodic nonlinear pattern, a [linear stability](linear-stability.md) disturbance is written $e^{\lambda t+iq\cdot x}v(x)$ with $v$ periodic on the pattern cell. The conjugated operator $L(q)$ acts on that fixed cell and determines the sideband [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Translation invariance supplies zero [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) at $q=0$, namely spatial derivatives of the base pattern. Their small-$q$ [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) determine long-wavelength [phase](phase-waves.md) stability; other parts of the spectrum can give independent instabilities.
