# Formal multiplicative group

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group law](formal-group-law.md)

The formal multiplicative group has law $F_m(X,Y)=X+Y+XY$, obtained by writing a multiplicative coordinate as $1+X$. Its multiplication-by-$n$ series is

$$
[n]_m(X)=(1+X)^n-1.
$$
