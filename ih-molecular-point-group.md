# Ih molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The full icosahedral molecular [point group](point-group.md), including inversion, with 120 operations. Its single-valued orbital [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) have dimensions 1, 3, 3, 4 and 5 in each inversion parity.
