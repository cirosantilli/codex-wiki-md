# Mass conversion in global warming potential

↑ **Parent:** [Global warming potential](global-warming-potential.md)

A [global warming potential](global-warming-potential.md) compares equal-mass pulses. If $\epsilon_i$ is the [greenhouse gas radiative efficiency](greenhouse-gas-radiative-efficiency.md) per ppb, an emitted [mass](mass.md) produces a mixing-ratio increment inversely proportional to [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M_i$. For two gases with exponential pulse removal, their GWP ratio at horizon $H$ is $(\epsilon_i/\epsilon_j)(M_j/M_i)\tau_i(1-e^{-H/\tau_i})/[\tau_j(1-e^{-H/\tau_j})]$. The common carbon-dioxide reference integral cancels. Comparing only the per-ppb coefficients would instead compare unequal emitted masses.
