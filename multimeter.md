# Multimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical measurement](electrical-measurement.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multimeter)

A [multimeter](multimeter.md) combines measurements such as [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), [electric current](electric-current.md) and [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) in one instrument. Some models also measure [capacitance](capacitance.md); the supported functions and test conditions depend on the instrument. The [dielectric](dielectric.md) laboratory example uses a [multimeter](multimeter.md) in capacitance mode.
