<h1 id="poincare-polynomial">Poincaré polynomial</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Betti number](betti-number.md)

For a space with finite-dimensional [homology](homology-split.md) over a specified field and only finitely many nonzero [Betti numbers](betti-number.md), this polynomial records their graded dimensions. Its value at one is the total [Betti number](betti-number.md), and its value at minus one is the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). The [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives $P_{M\times N}(t)=P_M(t)P_N(t)$ over a field. For integer [homology](homology-split.md) ranks, use the rational [Betti numbers](betti-number.md).
