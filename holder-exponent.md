<h1 id="holder-exponent">Hölder exponent</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder condition](holder-condition.md)

The Hölder exponent in [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) measures the power in the bound $|f(x)-f(y)|\leq C|x-y|^\alpha$. For $0<\alpha\leq1$, a larger exponent imposes stronger local regularity on bounded distance ranges. Exponent one is [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md).
