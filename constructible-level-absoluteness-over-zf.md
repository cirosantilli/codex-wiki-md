# Constructible-level absoluteness over ZF

↑ **Parent:** [Absoluteness of constructible levels](absoluteness-of-constructible-levels.md)

If $M$ is a [transitive model](transitive-model.md) of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), its internally computed $L_\alpha$ is the actual $L_\alpha$ for each [ordinal](ordinal.md) $\alpha\in M$. At a successor stage use absoluteness of satisfaction for the same [set](set-split.md) structure, formula codes and parameters; at a limit stage take the union of the already identical earlier stages. Choice is not required.
