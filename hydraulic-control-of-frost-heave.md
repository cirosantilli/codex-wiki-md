# Hydraulic control of frost heave

↑ **Parent:** [Frost heave](frost-heave.md)

A porous sheet of thickness $d$ and [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $\Pi$ supplies a thin [premelting](premelting.md) film. The [Darcy flow law](darcy-law.md) equates water supply to [ice](ice.md) growth for equal densities. With equal film and [ice](ice.md) conductivities, negligible latent heat relative to the through-going conductive flux, and $a\ll h$, phase equilibrium gives $p_T=(\rho L\Delta T/T_m)^{3/4}(A/(6\pi h^3))^{1/4}$. The increasing gravitational load limits growth. A quasi-steady [temperature](temperature.md) field alone does not imply the negligible-latent-heat approximation.
