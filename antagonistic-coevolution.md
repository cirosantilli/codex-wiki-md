# Antagonistic coevolution

↑ **Parent:** [Coevolution](coevolution.md)

Reciprocal evolutionary change driven by opposed fitness effects, including some host–[parasite](parasite.md) and sexual interactions. Correlated traits or temporal change alone do not establish reciprocal selection.
