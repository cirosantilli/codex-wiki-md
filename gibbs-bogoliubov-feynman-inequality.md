<h1 id="gibbs-bogoliubov-feynman-inequality">Gibbs--Bogoliubov--Feynman inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian variational approximation](gaussian-variational-approximation.md)

For an exact dimensionless Hamiltonian $H$ and trial Hamiltonian $H_0$, the free energies obey

$$
F\leq F_0+\langle H-H_0\rangle_0,
$$

where $F_0=-\log Z_0$ and the expectation uses the trial Gibbs distribution $Z_0^{-1}e^{-H_0}$.

The identity $e^{-F}=e^{-F_0}\langle e^{-(H-H_0)}\rangle_0$ and the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) for the convex [exponential function](exponential-function.md) give $\log\langle e^{-(H-H_0)}\rangle_0\geq-\langle H-H_0\rangle_0$. Taking minus the logarithm proves the bound.
