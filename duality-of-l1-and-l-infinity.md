# Duality of l1 and l infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Duality of sequence spaces](duality-of-sequence-spaces.md)

For $y\in\ell^\infty$, the formula $\phi_y(x)=\sum_nx_ny_n$ defines a bounded functional on $\ell^1$ with $\|\phi_y\|=\|y\|_\infty$. Conversely, every $\phi\in(\ell^1)^*$ is represented this way by the bounded sequence $y_n=\phi(e_n)$. This is an [isometric isomorphism of normed spaces](isometric-isomorphism-of-normed-spaces.md).
