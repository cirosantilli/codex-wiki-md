# Module isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Module (mathematics)](module-mathematics.md)

A [module isomorphism](module-isomorphism.md) is a bijection of [modules](module-mathematics.md) over the same [ring](ring.md) preserving addition and scalar multiplication. Its inverse preserves these operations too. When a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$ gives a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) an $F[t]$-[module](module-mathematics.md) structure by $t\cdot v=T(v)$, a [module isomorphism](module-isomorphism.md) is exactly an invertible [linear map](linear-map.md) intertwining the two operators. This translates classification of [modules](module-mathematics.md) into [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md) and [rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md).
