# Solid electrolyte

↑ **Parent:** [Physical properties of salts](physical-properties-of-salts.md)

A [solid electrolyte](solid-electrolyte.md) carries [electric current](electric-current.md) through mobile [ions](ion.md) while remaining a [solid](solid.md). The distinction between ionic and electronic [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) matters: different charge carriers serve different roles in [electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md).
