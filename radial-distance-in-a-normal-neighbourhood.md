# Radial distance in a normal neighbourhood

↑ **Parent:** [Normal neighbourhood](normal-neighbourhood.md)

In a [normal neighbourhood](normal-neighbourhood.md) $U=\exp_p(V)$ with $V$ star-shaped, define $r(q)=|\exp_p^{-1}(q)|$. Away from $p$, the [Gauss lemma](gauss-s-lemma-riemannian-geometry.md) gives $\nabla r=(d\exp_p)_v(v/|v|)$ for $v=\exp_p^{-1}(q)$. Thus $|\nabla r|=1$ and this unit [vector field](vector-field.md) is tangent to unit-speed radial [geodesics](geodesic.md). The function $r$ is not smooth at $p$, but $r^2/2$ is smooth and $\nabla(r^2/2)=(d\exp_p)_v v$ extends by zero. On a sufficiently small normal ball, $r$ equals the [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) from $p$.
