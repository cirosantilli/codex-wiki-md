# Taylor formula for a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor theorem](taylor-theorem.md)

For a polynomial $p$ of degree at most $m$, the Taylor formula is exact:

$$
p(t)=\sum_{j=0}^m\frac{p^{(j)}(x)}{j!}(t-x)^j.
$$
