# Neyman-Scott process

↑ **Parent:** [Point process](point-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neyman–Scott_process)

A [Neyman-Scott process](neyman-scott-process.md) places [independent](independent-random-variables.md) offspring clusters around points of a Poisson parent process, with an independently drawn offspring count and displacements. Poisson offspring counts make the offspring process conditionally Poisson given the parents, and therefore give a [Cox process](cox-process.md). Arbitrary offspring counts need not have that property.
