# Nonnormal Fourier amplification matrix

↑ **Parent:** [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md)

For a system or a multilevel method, each [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) evolves through an amplification matrix $G(\theta)$. Stability requires bounds on $G(\theta)^n$ uniform in frequencies and mesh parameters. Eigenvalue moduli alone do not suffice: $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has unit eigenvalues but $G^n$ has off-diagonal entry $n$. Uniformly bounded eigenvector matrices, or a uniformly positive energy symmetrizer, can supply the missing power bound.
