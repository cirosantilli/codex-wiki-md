# Cauchy-complete category

↑ **Parent:** [Idempotent morphism](idempotent-morphism.md)

A category is Cauchy-complete when every [idempotent morphism](idempotent-morphism.md) splits: for $e:A\to A$ with $e^2=e$, there are $r:A\to B$ and $i:B\to A$ with $ir=e$ and $ri=1_B$. Its [Karoubi envelope](karoubi-envelope.md) freely supplies these splittings. For a [small category](small-category.md), this condition makes every retract of a [representable functor](representable-functor.md) representable.
