# Oscillatory drift of an attenuated internal-wave beam

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous attenuation of an internal-wave beam](viscous-attenuation-of-an-internal-wave-beam.md)

In the prescribed along-beam scalar model $U=A(\xi)\cos\omega t$ with $A=U_0e^{-\lambda\xi}$, the first-order [fluid displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) is $A\sin\omega t/\omega$. Its leading sampled-velocity correction is $-\lambda A^2\sin(2\omega t)/(2\omega)$, whose [period average](period-average.md) vanishes. This result concerns the supplied component model and does not determine an Eulerian mean streaming flow.
