# Borel sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Borel set](borel-set.md)

The Borel sigma-algebra of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is the [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) generated by its [open sets](open-set.md). Its members are exactly the [Borel sets](borel-set.md).
