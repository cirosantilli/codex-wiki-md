# Transit-radius contribution of a gaseous envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary mass-radius relation](planetary-mass-radius-relation.md)

A low-[mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) hydrogen-helium envelope can add a large [radius](radius.md) while adding a small fraction of the [mass](mass.md). The [exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md) radius is the altitude where tangential [optical depth](optical-depth.md) becomes order unity, so [temperature](temperature.md), [opacity](opacity.md), and [exoplanet clouds](exoplanet-cloud.md) can move the measured radius above the deep material radius. Atmospheric loss and cooling change this contribution over time.
