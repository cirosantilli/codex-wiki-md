# Image registration

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_registration)

Estimating a coordinate transformation that aligns corresponding content in images. The transformation family, similarity objective, [interpolation](interpolation-split.md) and [regularization](regularization.md) are separate modeling choices. Intensity agreement alone does not guarantee physically correct correspondence.
