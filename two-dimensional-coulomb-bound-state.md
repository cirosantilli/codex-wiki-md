# Two-dimensional Coulomb bound state

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb bound state](coulomb-bound-state.md)

For motion confined to a plane but retaining [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $-\kappa/r$, separation in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) gives angular [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) $e^{im\phi}$ with integer $m$. The regular radial factor behaves as $r^{|m|}$ at the origin. Removing this power and the decaying [exponential function](exponential-function.md) leaves an [Generalized Laguerre polynomial](generalized-laguerre-polynomial.md) differential equation. [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) termination requires $N=n_r+|m|+1/2$, yielding the displayed [energy](energy-split.md). At fixed $q=n_r+|m|$, the spinless [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) is $2q+1$. This is not the logarithmic potential of [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md) formulated entirely in two spatial dimensions.
