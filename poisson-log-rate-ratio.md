# Poisson log-rate ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md)

For independent samples of $n$ observations from [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) with positive means $a$ and $b$, the [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) of $\nu=\log(b/a)$ is the displayed difference when both totals are positive. Its asymptotic variance is $(a^{-1}+b^{-1})/n$, obtained by the [delta method](delta-method.md) or by inverting the two-parameter [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md). A zero group total makes its log-mean estimate a boundary value; finite normal intervals require nonzero totals.
