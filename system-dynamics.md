# System dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_dynamics)

A modeling approach using stocks, flows and feedback to investigate coupled systems over [time](time-in-physics.md). [Computer simulations](computer-simulation.md) can explore delayed responses and the consequences of different model assumptions.
