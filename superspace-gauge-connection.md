# Superspace gauge connection

↑ **Parent:** [Vector superfield](vector-superfield.md)

A gauge connection on [superspace](superspace.md) has both vector and spinor components. Subtracting the flat-frame torsion from the graded commutator of its [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) defines its supercurvature. The conventional four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ spinor-curvature constraints leave one real [vector superfield](vector-superfield.md) as a prepotential. Its [chiral field-strength superfield](chiral-field-strength-superfield.md) contains the ordinary gauge curvature, [gaugino](gaugino.md) and real [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md).
