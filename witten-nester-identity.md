# Witten-Nester identity

↑ **Parent:** [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md)

In mostly-plus signature orient the commuting [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) to the future by $\bar\epsilon=-\epsilon^\dagger\gamma^{\hat0}$. For the [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md), put $b^i=n_\mu B^{\mu i}$. Its covariant divergence has a spinor-gradient term and the [Einstein tensor contraction of spin curvature](einstein-tensor-contraction-of-spin-curvature.md). Projecting onto the hypersurface gives the displayed identity with positive spinor norm. The [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md) removes the negative square, and the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) makes the matter term nonnegative after the Einstein constraints are imposed.
