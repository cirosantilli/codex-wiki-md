# Series spring and Voigt-branch creep

↑ **Parent:** [Standard linear solid model](standard-linear-solid-model.md)

A [spring](spring.md) $E_0$ in series with a parallel spring-dashpot branch $E_1,\eta$ has creep compliance $J(t)=1/E_0+(1/E_1)(1-e^{-E_1t/\eta})$. It includes immediate and delayed recoverable [strain](strain.md), but no unbounded viscous flow. Large [strain](strain.md), damage or [anisotropy](anisotropy.md) require additional constitutive structure.
