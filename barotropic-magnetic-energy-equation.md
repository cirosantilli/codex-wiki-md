# Barotropic magnetic energy equation

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

For a [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md), an energy per mass $e$ can be defined by $de/d\rho=p/\rho^2$. For the [isothermal equation of state](globally-isothermal-equation-of-state.md), $e=c_s^2\ln(\rho/\rho_0)$. Adding kinetic, gravitational and [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) gives the ideal magnetic energy flux $\mathbf u(E+p+B^2/(2\mu_0))-\mathbf B(\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf B)/\mu_0$. This is a mechanical barotropic energy identity; it does not say that an isothermal gas is thermally isolated.
