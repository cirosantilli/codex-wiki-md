# Killing tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_tensor)

A Killing tensor is a symmetric covariant tensor satisfying the displayed equation for the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). Its contraction with $r$ copies of a [geodesic](geodesic.md) velocity is constant along every affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md). The converse follows because the resulting symmetric derivative is determined by its homogeneous polynomial on tangent vectors. Rank one recovers the [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) after metric duality.
