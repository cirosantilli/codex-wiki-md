# Capillary depression

↑ **Parent:** [Jurin's law](jurin-s-law.md)

A negative signed capillary rise for a nonwetting [liquid](liquid.md): the equilibrium [contact angle](contact-angle.md) exceeds a right angle, giving $\cos\theta<0$ in [Jurin's law](jurin-s-law.md). Correcting a mercury-column measurement requires a consistent [meniscus](meniscus-liquid.md) and surface condition.
