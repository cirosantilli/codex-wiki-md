# Commuting symmetric matrices have a common orthonormal eigenbasis

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md)

For commuting real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) $A,B$, each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $A$ is invariant under $B$: $A(Bv)=B(Av)=\lambda Bv$. The restriction of $B$ to that [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) is again symmetric. The [spectral theorem](spectral-theorem.md) therefore supplies an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) for $B$ inside each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $A$. Combining these [bases](basis.md) gives a common [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) of the whole space. Conversely, two [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) diagonal in the same [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) [commute](commuting-elements.md).
