# Ingroup favoritism

↑ **Parent:** [Social categorization](social-categorization.md)

More favorable evaluation or treatment of an [ingroup](ingroup.md) than an [outgroup](social-outgroup.md). It should be distinguished from absolute hostility toward an [outgroup](social-outgroup.md) and measured separately from consequential [discrimination](discrimination.md).
