# Even cross-intersection bound

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

If every cross-intersection of two [set families](set-family.md) on $[n]$ has even size, the spans of their [indicator vectors](indicator-vector.md) are orthogonal in $\mathbb F_2^n$. The [orthogonal complement over the binary field](orthogonal-complement-over-the-binary-field.md) dimension formula bounds the sum of their dimensions by $n$, giving the displayed cardinality bound.
