# Copy-number trace-distance obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum cloning](quantum-cloning.md)

Let distinct nonorthogonal [pure states](pure-state.md) have overlap magnitude $0<c<1$, and let one [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) take $n$ identical inputs to $m$ outputs. Let $\epsilon_u,\epsilon_v$ be the respective [probabilities](probability.md) that a test for the ideal $m$-copy [pure state](pure-state.md) fails. [Trace distance](trace-distance.md) contraction, its triangle inequality and the [pure-target upper bound on trace distance](pure-target-upper-bound-on-trace-distance.md) give $\sqrt{1-c^{2m}}\leq\sqrt{\epsilon_u}+\sqrt{1-c^{2n}}+\sqrt{\epsilon_v}$. Squaring with the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives the displayed strategy-independent positive bound. It establishes both a worst-case bound and an equal-prior average bound. Averaging rotated pairs establishes a positive Haar-average bound for unknown qubit inputs, without assuming independent clone errors.
