# Perfect-conductor sphere dipole interference

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md)

An electrically small [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) sphere has both an induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) and [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The latter contributes half the electric scattering [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) in magnitude. The two [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) add in the backward direction and partially cancel in the forward direction. Thus the backward intensity is $9/4$ of the electric-only value and its [radar cross section](radar-cross-section.md) is $9\pi k^4a^6$. The correction persists at leading order as $ka\to0$; discarding the magnetic term is a separate response approximation.[Scattering by a Small Perfectly Conducting Sphere](scattering-of-em-waves-by-michael-fowler.md)
