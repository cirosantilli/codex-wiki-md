# Singular locus

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth locus of a variety](smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md)

The closed subset of points where an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) is not smooth. Over a [perfect field](perfect-field.md) it is equivalently the locus whose [local rings](local-ring.md) are not [regular local rings](regular-local-ring.md). A [normal variety](normal-variety.md) has no singular points in codimension zero or one.
