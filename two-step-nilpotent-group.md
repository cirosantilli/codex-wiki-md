# Two-step nilpotent group

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md)

A group $G$ is two-step nilpotent when

$$
[[G,G],G]=\{1\}.
$$

Equivalently, its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is contained in the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md) of $G$. This allows every word to be collected into powers of generators followed by powers of their pairwise commutators.
