# Twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor theory](twistor-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twistor_space)

In flat [twistor theory](twistor-theory.md), a twistor is a pair $Z=(\omega^A,\pi_{A'})$ of two-component spinors. The [twistor incidence relation](twistor-incidence-relation.md) with a spacetime point is $\omega^A=ix^{AA'}\pi_{A'}$. The nonzero vector $Z$ and its projective class play different roles: the latter belongs to [projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md).
