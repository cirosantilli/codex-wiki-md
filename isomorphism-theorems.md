# Isomorphism theorems

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphism_theorems)

The [isomorphism theorems](isomorphism-theorems.md) relate [homomorphisms](homomorphism.md), their kernels and images, and quotient constructions in [algebra](algebra-split.md). For [groups](group-split.md), the [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) identifies a homomorphism's image with the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) by its [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md). Analogous statements include the [first isomorphism theorem for rings](first-isomorphism-theorem-for-rings.md) and the [First isomorphism theorem for vector spaces](first-isomorphism-theorem-for-vector-spaces.md): identifying precisely the inputs with the same output preserves the remaining algebraic structure.
