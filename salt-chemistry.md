# Salt (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_(chemistry))

A [salt](salt-chemistry.md) is an assembly of [cations](cation.md) and [anions](anion.md) with zero total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). Its constituents can be single [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) or groups joined internally by [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md). Thus [ionic bonding](ionic-bonding-split.md) and [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) can occur in the same [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md), at different structural levels.[§2.6, polyatomic ions and ionic compounds](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
