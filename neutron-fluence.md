# Neutron fluence

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron flux](neutron-flux.md)

The time integral of scalar [neutron flux](neutron-flux.md), with inverse-area units. Multiplication by a constant microscopic reaction [collision cross-section](collision-cross-section.md) gives a dimensionless exposure parameter. A single integrated exposure does not specify how the neutron energy distribution varied during irradiation.
