# Finite-limit-preserving functor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A finite-limit-preserving functor preserves a terminal object and pullbacks, equivalently all finite limits. It is also called left exact. Such functors from a [Cartesian syntactic category](cartesian-syntactic-category.md) to a finite-limit category correspond to models of the associated [Cartesian theory](cartesian-theory.md).
