# Phase-space area

↑ **Parent:** [Phase space](phase-space.md)

The symplectic area enclosed by a closed orbit in a two-dimensional phase plane. With the orientation of Hamiltonian motion it is $\oint p\,dq=2\pi I$, where $I$ is the [action variable](action-variable.md).
