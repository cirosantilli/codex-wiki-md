# Cross-sectional area

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

The [area](surface-area.md) of a planar section through a body. A homogeneous diagonal deformation with transverse [stretch ratios](stretch-ratio.md) $\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_2$ changes the area of a transverse section from $\mathcal A_0$ to $\mathcal A=\lambda_1\lambda_2\mathcal A_0$. [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) components use current [cross-sectional area](cross-sectional-area.md), whereas nominal [tractions](traction.md) use reference [cross-sectional area](cross-sectional-area.md).
