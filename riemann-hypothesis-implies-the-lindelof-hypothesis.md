<h1 id="riemann-hypothesis-implies-the-lindelof-hypothesis">Riemann hypothesis implies the Lindelöf hypothesis</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md)

The [subpower zeta bound to the right of the critical line](subpower-zeta-bound-to-the-right-of-the-critical-line.md) gives an arbitrarily small exponent at $1/2+\delta$. The [functional equation](functional-equation.md) gives exponent $\delta+\varepsilon$ at $1/2-\delta$. The [Phragmén–Lindelöf principle](phragmen-lindelof-principle.md) gives exponent $\delta/2+\varepsilon$ at the midpoint. Letting these fixed positive parameters be arbitrarily small proves the [Lindelöf hypothesis](lindelof-hypothesis.md).
