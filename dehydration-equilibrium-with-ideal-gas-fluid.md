# Dehydration equilibrium with ideal-gas fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

For a [mineral](mineral.md) reaction producing one mole of water, let $H^\circ,S^\circ$ refer to the $P^\circ$ [standard state](standard-state.md) and let the solids’ volume change $\Delta V_s$ be constant. If $A_s$ is the product/reactant solid-activity ratio, the [Gibbs energy of reaction](gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) is

$$
\Delta_rG=H^\circ-TS^\circ+\Delta V_s(P-P^\circ)
+RT\ln\left(A_s\frac{f_{\mathrm{H_2O}}}{P^\circ}\right).
$$

For temperature-independent specified activities and fugacity, equilibrium gives

$$
T=\frac{H^\circ+\Delta V_s(P-P^\circ)}
{S^\circ-R\ln(A_sf_{\mathrm{H_2O}}/P^\circ)}.
$$

The gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) work is already represented by the fugacity term; adding a second constant water-volume term double counts it. For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), $f=P$; a given [fugacity coefficient](fugacity-coefficient.md) gives $f=\phi_fP$. If these quantities depend on [temperature](temperature-split.md), the equilibrium equation must instead be solved implicitly. [Pressure](pressure-split.md) units in the solid volume correction must match those used for $\Delta V_s$.
