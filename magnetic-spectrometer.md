# Magnetic spectrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrometer](spectrometer.md)

A [spectrometer](spectrometer.md) that selects [charged particles](charged-particle.md) using the bending of their trajectories in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). For a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of magnitude $B$, the perpendicular [momentum](momentum-split.md) component is $p_\perp=|q|Br$, where $q$ is the [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and $r$ is the curvature [radius](radius.md) of the projected trajectory.
