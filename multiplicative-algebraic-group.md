# Multiplicative algebraic group

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic group](algebraic-group.md)

The multiplicative [algebraic group](algebraic-group.md) has $\mathbb G_m(K)=K^{\times}$, with multiplication as its [group operation](group-operation.md). Its coordinate ring is $K[z,z^{-1}]$. The [formal multiplicative group](formal-multiplicative-group.md) describes its law near the identity $z=1$.
