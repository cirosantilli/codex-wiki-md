# Cawthorne-Fulton observation of voids in irradiated steel

↑ **Parent:** [Void swelling](void-swelling.md)

In 1967, [C. Cawthorne](c-cawthorne.md) and [E. J. Fulton](e-j-fulton.md) reported electron-microscope thin-foil examinations of type316 stainless-steel fuel cladding and mechanical-test specimens irradiated in the Dounreay Fast Reactor. The work helped identify microscopic cavities in the dimensional-instability problem.[Opening description of the specimens, reactor and thin-foil examination](voids-in-irradiated-stainless-steel-by-c-cawthorne-and-e-j-fulton.md)
