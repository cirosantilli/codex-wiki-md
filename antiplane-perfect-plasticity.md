# Antiplane perfect plasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect plasticity](perfect-plasticity.md)

In [antiplane perfect plasticity](antiplane-perfect-plasticity.md), [velocity](velocity.md) $w=v_3$ and shear [stresses](stress.md) $a=\sigma_{13},b=\sigma_{23}$ depend only on $x_1,x_2$. [Force balance](force-balance.md) is $a_{,1}+b_{,2}=0$, the fixed [yield surface](yield-surface.md) is $f(a,b)=k$, and the [associated flow rule](associated-flow-rule.md) is $\nabla w=2\dot\lambda(f_a,f_b)$ with $\dot\lambda\geq0$. Differentiating the yield equation in each coordinate and combining it with equilibrium shows that $a,b,w$ are constant along [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) tangent to $(-f_b,f_a)$. For example $-f_ba_{,1}+f_aa_{,2}=f_bb_{,2}+f_aa_{,2}=0$. Nonzero regular yield gradient is needed for a characteristic direction.
