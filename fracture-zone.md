# Fracture zone

↑ **Parent:** [Transform fault](transform-fault.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fracture_zone)

The seafloor continuation beyond the actively slipping ridge-to-ridge [transform fault](transform-fault.md). Its two sides can differ in age without continuing relative plate-boundary slip.
