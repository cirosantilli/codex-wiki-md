# Plummer model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The spherical [Plummer model](plummer-model.md) has $\psi=GM/\sqrt{a^2+r^2}$ and $\rho=3Ma^2/[4\pi(a^2+r^2)^{5/2}]$. It is the $p=2$ [hypervirial model](hypervirial-model.md). In unit scales its isotropic distribution is $24\sqrt2E^{7/2}/(7\pi^3)$ for positive [relative energy](relative-energy.md).
