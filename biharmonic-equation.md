# Biharmonic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Biharmonic operator](biharmonic-operator.md)

The [biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md) is $\nabla^4f=0$, where the [biharmonic operator](biharmonic-operator.md) is the square of the [Laplacian](laplacian.md). In planar [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md), taking the curl of the momentum equation and using a [Cartesian streamfunction](cartesian-streamfunction.md) produces this equation. Its decaying spatial [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) modes have form $(A+By)e^{-|k|y}e^{ikx}$.
