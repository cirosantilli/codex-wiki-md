# Measurement zero offset

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md)

A constant additive contribution $b$ to an instrument reading is a zero offset. A blank or zero-signal reading can estimate it, and a [systematic-error cancellation by reversal](systematic-error-cancellation-by-reversal.md) can cancel it if it remains unchanged while the desired signal changes sign. The residual correction uncertainty remains part of the [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md).
