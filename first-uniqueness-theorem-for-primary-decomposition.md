# First uniqueness theorem for primary decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal primary decomposition](minimal-primary-decomposition.md)

The distinct radicals in a [minimal primary decomposition](minimal-primary-decomposition.md) are exactly the [associated primes of a module](associated-prime-of-a-module.md) $R/I$, and hence are invariant. The injection into the sum of primary quotients proves one inclusion; an element supported on one component, supplied by irredundancy, proves the other. This does not imply uniqueness of [embedded primary components](embedded-primary-component.md).
