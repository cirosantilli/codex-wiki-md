# Self-dual cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cone](convex-cone.md)

A [self-dual cone](self-dual-cone.md) equals its [dual cone](dual-cone.md) under the specified inner product. The [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md), [second-order cone](second-order-cone.md) and [positive semidefinite cone](positive-semidefinite-cone.md) are standard examples. Self-duality of a set alone does not establish primal or dual feasibility.
