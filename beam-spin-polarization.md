# Beam spin polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Spin polarization](spin-polarization.md)

A beam's unequal population of [spin](spin.md) projections along a specified axis. For spin-one-half particles the displayed [longitudinal polarization](longitudinal-polarization.md) uses populations parallel and antiparallel to the [momentum](momentum-split.md). An unpolarized beam has equal populations and requires an initial-spin average in a [cross section](cross-section-physics.md); a polarized beam weights the corresponding [helicity](helicity.md) amplitudes differently. This differs from [photon](photon.md) linear polarization.
