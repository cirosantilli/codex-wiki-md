# Two-block quantified Boolean formula

↑ **Parent:** [Second level of the polynomial hierarchy](second-level-of-the-polynomial-hierarchy.md)

A [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) with two blocks of quantified [Boolean variables](boolean-variable.md). Determining the truth of an existential-then-universal instance is a canonical complete problem for the existential [second level of the polynomial hierarchy](second-level-of-the-polynomial-hierarchy.md), $\Sigma_2^{\mathrm P}$. For completeness one may restrict the matrix $\psi$ to a disjunction of terms with at most three literals. The order of the quantifiers matters: the assignment to $X$ must work for every assignment to $Y$.
