<h1 id="kamerlingh-onnes-s-1908-helium-liquefaction-experiment">Kamerlingh Onnes's 1908 helium liquefaction experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Helium liquefaction](helium-liquefaction.md)

On 10 July 1908, [Heike Kamerlingh Onnes](heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)'s Leiden laboratory circulated purified [helium](helium.md) through [liquid air](liquid-air.md) and evaporatively cooled liquid-[hydrogen](hydrogen.md) precooling, a regenerative [heat exchanger](heat-exchanger.md), and an expansion valve. Returning [helium](helium.md) cooled incoming [helium](helium.md); [liquid](liquid.md) collected in a transparent vacuum vessel. Reflected light revealed its [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md), crossed by [thermocouple](thermocouple.md) wires. Subsequent [mass density](density.md) and [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) checks tested contamination.[Communication 108, §§2, 4–5, pp. 175–183](the-liquefaction-of-helium-by-heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)

A constant-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) [helium](helium.md) [gas thermometer](gas-thermometer.md) below the valve supplied the [temperature](temperature-split.md) indication. An [oxygen](oxygen.md) [boiling](boiling.md) reference check disagreed with its expected reading; the [real gas](real-gas.md) correction to [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) remained provisional. A [temperature](temperature-split.md) plateau suggested immersion before the [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md) was recognized.[§4, pp. 180–181; §§5–6, pp. 183–184](the-liquefaction-of-helium-by-heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)

The measurement principles separate two questions. A visible [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md) establishes an interface; a [gas thermometer](gas-thermometer.md) estimates [temperature](temperature-split.md) through an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md). A stationary [temperature](temperature-split.md) trace can also result from a [steady state](steady-state.md) without [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md), so it cannot replace the interface observation. Conversely, seeing a [liquid](liquid.md) does not calibrate a [thermometer](thermometer.md). [Thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md), the [gas thermometer dead-space correction](gas-thermometer-dead-space-correction.md), departures from the [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) law and incomplete [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) all matter when assigning an absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md). A check far from the intended measurement range tests the instrument but does not by itself bound every low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) error.
