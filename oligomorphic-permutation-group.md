# Oligomorphic permutation group

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation group](permutation-group.md)

A permutation group on a set is oligomorphic when its diagonal action on ordered $n$-tuples has finitely many orbits for every positive integer $n$. For a countable model of a complete theory, an oligomorphic automorphism group makes every finite-arity [type space](type-space.md) finite: realized types are dense, and only finitely many are possible because types are constant on orbits.
