# Normal equation for coercive Tikhonov regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization with a coercive penalty operator](tikhonov-regularization-with-a-coercive-penalty-operator.md)

For [bounded linear operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) $K:U\to V$ and $B:U\to W$ between real [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md), $\alpha>0$ and $\|Bu\|\geq\beta\|u\|$ with $\beta>0$, the unique minimizer of $\|Ku-f\|^2/2+\alpha\|Bu\|^2/2$ satisfies $(K^*K+\alpha B^*B)u_\alpha=K^*f$. Differentiate the objective in every direction and use the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) to obtain this equation. The coercive quadratic penalty makes its left-hand operator boundedly invertible. Setting $B=I$ gives the [Tikhonov normal equation](tikhonov-normal-equation.md); omitting the penalty instead gives the [normal equation for a linear inverse problem](normal-equation-for-a-linear-inverse-problem.md).
