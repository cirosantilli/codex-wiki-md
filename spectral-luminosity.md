# Spectral luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity](luminosity.md)

Spectral luminosity is energy radiated per unit time per unit frequency. Its frequency integral is the total [luminosity](luminosity.md). For an isotropic source at small distance $r$, neglecting attenuation and cosmological effects, the [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md) is $L_\nu/(4\pi r^2)$.
