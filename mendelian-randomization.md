# Mendelian randomization

↑ **Parent:** [Instrumental variable](instrumental-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendelian_randomization)

Mendelian randomization uses inherited [genetic variants](genetic-variant.md) associated with an [exposure](exposure.md) as [instrumental variables](instrumental-variable.md). Its causal interpretation requires [instrument relevance](instrument-relevance.md), [instrumental-variable independence](instrumental-variable-independence.md), and the [exclusion restriction](exclusion-restriction.md).
