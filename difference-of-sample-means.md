# Difference of sample means

↑ **Parent:** [Sample mean](sample-mean.md)

For independent samples of sizes $n,m$ from populations with [means](expected-value-split.md) $\mu_X,\mu_Y$ and finite [variances](variance-split.md) $\sigma_X^2,\sigma_Y^2$, the difference of their [sample means](sample-mean.md) has [expected value](expected-value-split.md) $\mu_X-\mu_Y$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma_X^2/n+\sigma_Y^2/m$. This uses independence between and within the samples. With dependent samples, a [covariance](covariance.md) term is required instead.
