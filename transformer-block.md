# Transformer block

↑ **Parent:** [Transformer model](transformer-model.md)

A standard transformer block combines [multi-head attention](multi-head-attention.md) with a positionwise [feedforward neural network](feedforward-neural-network.md), wrapping each sublayer in a [residual connection](residual-connection.md) and [layer normalization](layer-normalization.md). An encoder-decoder transformer's decoder also has a cross-attention sublayer. Pre-normalization and post-normalization variants differ in where [layer normalization](layer-normalization.md) occurs relative to the sublayer and [residual connection](residual-connection.md).
