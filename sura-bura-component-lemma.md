<h1 id="sura-bura-component-lemma">Šura-Bura component lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quasicomponent](quasicomponent.md)

In a compact Hausdorff space, the [quasicomponent](quasicomponent.md) $Q_x$ is connected and equals the [connected component](connected-component.md) of $x$. If $Q_x=A\cup B$ were a nontrivial separation, disjoint open neighborhoods $U,V$ of its compact pieces leave a compact complement missing $Q_x$. Finitely many clopen neighborhoods of $x$ intersect to a clopen $N$ containing $Q_x$ inside $U\cup V$. The piece $N\cap U$ is then clopen in the whole space, contains $x$, and misses the nonempty other piece, contradicting the definition of $Q_x$. This also proves that any neighborhood of a component contains a clopen neighborhood of that component, by compactness of its complement.
