# Q-switching

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-switching)

[Q-switching](q-switching.md) creates a short high-energy [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md) by first storing [population inversion](population-inversion.md) while an [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) has high loss, then increasing its [quality factor](q-factor.md) so that [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) rapidly extracts the stored excitation. Its duration depends on [photon lifetime](photon-lifetime.md), excess [optical gain](optical-gain.md), inversion depletion and switching time; it is not fixed by a universal time-bandwidth equality.
