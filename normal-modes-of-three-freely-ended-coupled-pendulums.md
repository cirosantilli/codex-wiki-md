# Normal modes of three freely ended coupled pendulums

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

For three equal [simple pendulums](simple-pendulum-split.md) in a straight open chain with springs only between neighbours, the spring stiffness matrix in displacement coordinates is $k\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix}$. Its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $(1,1,1)$, $(1,0,-1)$ and $(1,-2,1)$ have respective dimensionless [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $0,1,3$. Adding the gravitational restoring term yields the displayed squared [eigenfrequencies](eigenfrequency.md). These are free spring ends: fixed wall springs would change the matrix and mode shapes.
