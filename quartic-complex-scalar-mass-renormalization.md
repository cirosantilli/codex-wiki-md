# Quartic complex-scalar mass renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Complex scalar quartic contact vertex](complex-scalar-quartic-contact-vertex.md)

For $\mathcal L=|\partial\phi|^2-m^2|\phi|^2-\mu^\epsilon g|\phi|^4/4$ in $d=4-\epsilon$, the directed [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) has [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one. Its divergent Feynman contribution to $i\Gamma^{(2)}$ is $igm^2/(8\pi^2\epsilon)$. A [mass](mass.md) insertion $-i\delta_{m^2}$ therefore gives the displayed [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) [counterterm](counterterm.md). Differentiating at fixed [bare mass](bare-mass.md) and [coupling](coupling.md) gives the displayed $\gamma_m=\tfrac12\mu\,d\log m^2/d\mu$. The equivalent two-real-field formulation has vertex coefficients $3g/2$ for a pure species and $g/2$ for two of each, giving total tadpole weight $(3g/2+g/2)/2=g$.
