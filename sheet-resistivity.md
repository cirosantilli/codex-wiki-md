# Sheet resistivity

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md)

The [sheet resistivity](sheet-resistivity.md) [tensor](tensor.md) relates tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) to [surface current density](surface-current-density.md): $\mathbf E_\parallel=\boldsymbol\rho_s\mathbf K$. It is the [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse-split.md) of the [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) [tensor](tensor.md), when that inverse exists. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is the [ohm](ohm.md), rather than the [ohm](ohm.md) times [metre](metre.md) used for bulk [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md). With a transverse response, individual [tensor](tensor.md) components are not generally reciprocals of the corresponding [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) components.
