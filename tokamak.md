# Tokamak

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokamak)

A toroidal magnetic-confinement system combining an externally imposed toroidal field with a poloidal field supplied largely by toroidal plasma current. The resulting helical field-line winding supports nested [magnetic flux surfaces](magnetic-flux-surface.md) and improves orbit confinement compared with a purely toroidal field. Confinement remains limited by orbit effects, collisions and instabilities.
