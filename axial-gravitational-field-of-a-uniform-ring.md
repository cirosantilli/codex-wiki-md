# Axial gravitational field of a uniform ring

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

A uniform thin ring of radius $a$ and [mass](mass.md) $M$ has axial [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) $g_z=-GMz/(a^2+z^2)^{3/2}$. Every ring element is the same distance from the axial point; transverse contributions cancel and axial projection supplies the factor $z/\sqrt{a^2+z^2}$. This also follows by differentiating the axial potential $\Phi=-GM/\sqrt{a^2+z^2}$. Near the centre $g_z\simeq-GMz/a^3$, whereas far away it approaches the point-mass field.
