<h1 id="a/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The convex-combination formula proved in part (i) gives $0\leq U_j^{m+1}\leq\sup_\ell U_\ell^m$ for every $j$. Taking the supremum and using nonnegativity gives

$$
\boxed{\|U^{m+1}\|_{\ell^\infty}\leq\|U^m\|_{\ell^\infty}\leq\|U^0\|_{\ell^\infty}.}
$$

This is the [discrete maximum principle](../../../../../../discrete-maximum-principle.md) for the nonlinear upwind step and proves the requested conditional $\ell^\infty$ stability under the propagated [Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy condition](../../../../../../courant-friedrichs-lewy-condition.md).
