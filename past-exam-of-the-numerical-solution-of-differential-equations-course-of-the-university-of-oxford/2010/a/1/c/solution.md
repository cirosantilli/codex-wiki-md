↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A method has [numerical convergence](../../../../../convergence-of-a-numerical-method.md) if it approximates the exact solution uniformly at mesh points on every fixed finite interval as $h\to0$, whenever its starting approximations converge to the corresponding exact starting values, for sufficiently smooth solutions of a well-posed initial-value problem.

The [Dahlquist equivalence theorem](../../../../../dahlquist-equivalence-theorem.md) states that a fixed-coefficient [linear multistep method](../../../../../linear-multistep-method-split.md) is convergent if and only if it is consistent and zero-stable, under the usual [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) on the differential equation and convergent starting data. In this family [numerical consistency](../../../../../consistency-of-a-numerical-method.md) forces the parameters found in part (a), and part (b) proves that choice is not zero-stable. All other parameter choices are inconsistent. Therefore **no member of the family is convergent**.
