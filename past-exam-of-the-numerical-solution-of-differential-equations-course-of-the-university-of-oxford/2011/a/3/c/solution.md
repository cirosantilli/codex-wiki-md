<h1 id="a/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For nonnegative initial data, $U_{\min}=0$. The proved [Crank-Nicolson maximum principle under a step restriction](../../../../../crank-nicolson-maximum-principle-under-a-step-restriction.md) immediately gives $\boxed{U_{ij}^m\geq0}$ for all indices. For nonpositive initial data, $U_{\max}=0$ and the same bound gives $\boxed{U_{ij}^m\leq0}$. Equivalently, apply positivity to $-U$, since the [finite difference method](../../../../../finite-difference-method.md) is linear and has homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). Both conclusions use $\mu_x+\mu_y\leq1$.
