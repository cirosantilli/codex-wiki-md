# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical solution of differential equations course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to both sections and all six questions of the Numerical Solution of Differential Equations paper sat on 22 June 2011. Source: [released Mathematics papers](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), `Part B 2007 11.pdf`. The real-triangularization assertion in question 6 is corrected to complex triangularization. Statements are not reproduced.
