# Fermi sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

For an isotropic three-dimensional [free electron gas](free-electron-model.md) at zero temperature, occupied wavevectors fill a sphere of radius $k_F$. Including two spin states, $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$. The volume in reciprocal space counts occupied states; folding an extended-zone sphere into a first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) does not remove states outside that first zone.
