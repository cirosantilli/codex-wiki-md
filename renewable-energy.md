# Renewable energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewable_energy)

Energy obtained from naturally replenished flows or stocks on a human timescale. Replenishment does not by itself prove low life-cycle emissions or negligible environmental impact. Variability, geographic availability and conversion equipment matter when a source is integrated into an energy system.
