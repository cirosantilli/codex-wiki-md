# Natural units

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_units)

Natural units choose units so that selected fundamental constants have numerical value one. In relativistic [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) the standard choice is $\hbar=c=1$: [energy](energy.md), [mass](mass.md) and inverse length then have the same units. This is a convention, not a physical change of the constants; other natural-unit systems may also normalize other constants.
