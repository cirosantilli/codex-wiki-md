# Dirac valley

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac cone](dirac-cone.md)

A region of momentum space surrounding a [Dirac cone](dirac-cone.md). The two inequivalent [Dirac valleys in graphene](dirac-valleys-in-graphene.md) provide a familiar example; a valley labels a region and is not the electron spin.
