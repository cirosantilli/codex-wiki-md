# Dirac spinor pseudo-unitarity

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

The generators in the [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md) satisfy $M^\dagger\gamma^0+\gamma^0M=0$, so the finite transformation preserves the indefinite form $S^\dagger\gamma^0S=\gamma^0$. This makes $\bar\psi\psi$ a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) through the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md). Boost generators are Hermitian rather than anti-Hermitian: a boost has eigenvalues $e^{\pm\eta/2}$ and is not unitary in the ordinary positive-definite component norm. Rotations are unitary. This component representation is distinct from the unitary [action](action.md) on the physical state space.
