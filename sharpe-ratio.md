# Sharpe ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Investment portfolio](investment-portfolio.md)

The expected excess return of a portfolio divided by its return standard deviation. Under the [capital asset pricing model](capital-asset-pricing-model.md), the ratio for one asset equals its correlation with the market times the market's ratio.
