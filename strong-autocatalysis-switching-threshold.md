# Strong-autocatalysis switching threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Saturating autocatalytic switch](saturating-autocatalytic-switch.md)

For $k\gg1$, the low-concentration saddle node of the [saturating autocatalytic switch](saturating-autocatalytic-switch.md) has

$$
g_c\sim\frac1{2k},
\qquad
s_c\sim\frac1{4k}.
$$
