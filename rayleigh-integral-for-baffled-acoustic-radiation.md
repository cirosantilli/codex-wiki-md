# Rayleigh integral for baffled acoustic radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

An aperture in a rigid plane with prescribed harmonic normal displacement $f(x')e^{i\omega t}$ radiates into a half-space. Superposing [acoustic monopoles](acoustic-monopole.md), each doubled by its same-sign rigid-wall image, gives the stated integral, where $R$ is the distance from aperture element to observer. In the [acoustic far field](acoustic-far-field.md), expanding $R$ to first order across the aperture identifies the angular radiation amplitude with the aperture's [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md).
