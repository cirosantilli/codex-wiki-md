# Quantum well

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor heterostructure](semiconductor-heterostructure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_well)

A [quantum well](quantum-well.md) confines a [particle](particle-physics.md) in one spatial direction while allowing motion along the layer. In a [semiconductor heterostructure](semiconductor-heterostructure.md), [band offsets](band-offset.md) can create that confinement. The following [finite square well](finite-square-well.md) model makes the resulting discrete transverse [energy levels](energy-level.md) explicit.
