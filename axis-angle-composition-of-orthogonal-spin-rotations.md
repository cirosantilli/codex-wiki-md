# Axis-angle composition of orthogonal spin rotations

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential of a Pauli vector](exponential-of-a-pauli-vector.md)

For $A=\Omega T/2$, $B=\Phi/2$, the ordered product of a $z$ [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) after an $x$ [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) has $C=\cos A\cos B$ and $\mathbf v=(\cos A\sin B,\sin A\sin B,\sin A\cos B)$. Since $C^2+|\mathbf v|^2=1$, its angle is $2\operatorname{atan2}(|\mathbf v|,C)$ and its axis is $\mathbf v/|\mathbf v|$. At $\mathbf v=0$ the group element is central and its axis is undetermined.
