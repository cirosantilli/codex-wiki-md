# Equal likelihoods preserve model odds

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md)

For two models with finite positive [prior odds](prior-odds.md), a sequence of data with identical conditional [likelihoods](likelihood-function.md) under both models has [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md) one. The [posterior odds](posterior-odds.md) therefore equal the [prior odds](prior-odds.md). This requires equality of the [probability](probability.md) model for all the data considered, not merely that both models fit a selected data set.
