# Nearest least-squares solution

↑ **Parent:** [Least-squares solution of a linear inverse problem](least-squares-solution-of-a-linear-inverse-problem.md)

The [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of a prescribed point $u_0$ onto the [affine subspace](affine-subspace.md) of [least-squares solutions](least-squares-solution-of-a-linear-inverse-problem.md) is unique. It retains $u_0$'s [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) component and chooses the determined component from the [Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator](moore-penrose-inverse-of-an-operator.md).
