# Grand-canonical spin susceptibility of a half-filled single level

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

One noninteracting spatial orbital with two [spin](spin.md) energies $\epsilon\pm\mu_BB$, in the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md) with mean occupation one, has [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) $\mu=\epsilon$ for every positive [temperature](temperature-split.md). Its [spin](spin.md) population difference is $-\tanh[\mu_BB/(2k_BT)]$, giving total-moment response $\chi=\mu_B^2/(2k_BT)$ at zero field. Empty and doubly occupied orbital states are permitted. Fixing exactly one [electron](electron.md) instead leaves only the two singly occupied states and gives $\chi=\mu_B^2/(k_BT)$. The factor of two is an ensemble effect in this small system.
