# Local Lagrangian argument for CPT invariance

↑ **Parent:** [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md)

Under [CPT](cpt-symmetry.md), a Hermitian local tensor monomial picks up a minus sign for each Lorentz vector index, including derivative indices. A [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) contracts all such indices, so the total sign is positive; charged terms are exchanged with their [Hermitian conjugates](hermitian-conjugation.md). Therefore a local Hermitian [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) obeys $\Theta\mathcal L(x)\Theta^{-1}=\mathcal L(-x)$, leaving its [action](action.md) invariant. This field-level argument illustrates the [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md) for ordinary scalar, spinor and vector theories; the axiomatic theorem also uses positive energy and [microcausality](microcausality.md).
