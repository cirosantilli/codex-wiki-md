# Vehicle rollover threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

For a rigid vehicle turning on a level road, with [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) height $h$, half track width $a$ and turn radius $R_b$, sufficient [static friction](static-friction.md) supplies [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md) $v^2/R_b$. The inner and outer wheel [normal forces](normal-force.md) obey $N_i+N_o=Mg$ and $a(N_o-N_i)=Mhv^2/R_b$. The inner contact force first vanishes at the displayed [speed](speed.md). The approximation neglects suspension movement and extra [torques](torque.md); inadequate [static friction](static-friction.md) can cause sliding before rollover.
