# Hoeffding lower-tail bound for Hamming balls

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming ball](hamming-ball.md)

For a uniform vertex of the [Hamming cube](hamming-cube.md), distance from a fixed vertex is the sum of $n$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) with parameter $1/2$. The lower-tail [Hoeffding inequality](hoeffding-inequality.md) implies that the fraction of vertices at distance at most $an$, $0<a<1/2$, is at most $e^{-2n(1/2-a)^2}$. Strict-radius balls satisfy the same upper bound.
