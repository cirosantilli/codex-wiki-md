# Paleomagnetic fold test

↑ **Parent:** [Paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md)

Compare the scatter of [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) directions before and after correcting for [folding](folding.md). Improved agreement after restoring bedding supports acquisition before [folding](folding.md); agreement only in present coordinates supports a younger overprint. Partial unfolding can indicate acquisition during deformation or more complicated histories. The test constrains relative timing, rather than supplying an absolute age.
