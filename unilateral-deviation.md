# Unilateral deviation

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-form game](normal-form-game.md)

A [unilateral deviation](unilateral-deviation.md) changes only one player's strategy, holding the other players' strategies fixed. It is profitable if it strictly increases that player's [payoff](payoff.md), or [expected payoff](expected-payoff.md) for [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md). The comparison does not allow the opponents to adjust in response to the deviation. In a sequential game, however, holding an opponent's complete [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) fixed still allows their prescribed actions to differ after different observed histories.
