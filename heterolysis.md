# Heterolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

[Heterolysis](heterolysis.md) assigns both bonding [electrons](electron.md) to one fragment. For an initially neutral [molecule](molecule-split.md), the resulting [cation](cation.md) and [anion](anion.md) define a different dissociation channel from [homolysis](homolysis.md); their isolated gas-phase [energy](energy-split.md) differs from that of solvated [ions](ion.md).
