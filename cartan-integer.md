# Cartan integer

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The Cartan integer associated with two roots is

$$
\langle\beta,\alpha^\vee\rangle
=\frac{2(\beta,\alpha)}{(\alpha,\alpha)}.
$$

A crystallographic root system requires every Cartan integer to be an [integer](integer.md).
