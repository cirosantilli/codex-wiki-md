# Gaussian curvature of a graph surface

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental forms of a graph surface](fundamental-forms-of-a-graph-surface.md)

For the graph of $h$,

$$
K=\frac{h_{xx}h_{yy}-h_{xy}^2}{(1+h_x^2+h_y^2)^2}.
$$
