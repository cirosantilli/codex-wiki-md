# Classical ideal-gas entropy limits

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

For constant positive [molar heat capacity](molar-heat-capacity.md), the [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) [entropy](entropy-split.md) grows without bound on isothermal dilution to unbounded [volume](volume.md), corresponding to $p\to0$. This is a meaningful limiting configuration count, not an infinite [entropy](entropy-split.md) in a finite container. Extrapolation to $T\to0$ at fixed positive [pressure](pressure-split.md) instead predicts negative infinite [entropy](entropy-split.md). That extrapolation fails: phase changes, [quantum statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md) and temperature-dependent [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) prevent a real equilibrium substance from retaining the same classical formula down to zero [temperature](temperature-split.md).
