# Four-gamete test

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-gamete_test)

Under an [infinite sites mutation model](infinite-sites-mutation-model.md), two variable sites on one unrecombined rooted tree have nested or disjoint derived-allele clades. At most three of the four biallelic [haplotypes](haplotype.md) can then occur. Observation of all four haplotypes proves that the single-tree, single-mutation-per-site model cannot hold: some ancestral [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) is necessary if recurrent mutation is excluded. The test gives no proof of absence of recombination when only three haplotypes occur.
