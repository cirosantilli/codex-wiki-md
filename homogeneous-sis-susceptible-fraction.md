# Homogeneous SIS susceptible fraction

↑ **Parent:** [SIS model](sis-model.md)

For $\dot i=\beta i(1-i)-\gamma i$, a positive [endemic equilibrium](endemic-equilibrium.md) requires $R_0=\beta/\gamma>1$ and gives susceptible fraction $s_*=\gamma/\beta$. Below threshold the disease-free susceptible fraction is one. The reciprocal relation is a property of this homogeneous scalar [SIS model](sis-model.md), not a universal identity for heterogeneous populations.
