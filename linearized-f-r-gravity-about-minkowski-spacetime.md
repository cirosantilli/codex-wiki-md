<h1 id="linearized-f-r-gravity-about-minkowski-spacetime">Linearized f(R) gravity about Minkowski spacetime</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [f(R) gravity](f-r-gravity.md)

If $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$, and $f''(0)=0$, then $f(R)=R+O(R^3)$ near zero. The field equations linearized about [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) are therefore the [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md); deviations first appear beyond linear order.
