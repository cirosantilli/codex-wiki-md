# Simple closed curve

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous path](continuous-path.md)

A [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md) $\gamma:S^1\to X$ of a [circle](circle.md) into a [topological space](topological-space.md). On an oriented surface, an essential such curve is noncontractible; on a punctured surface, nonperipheral means that it does not surround only one puncture.
