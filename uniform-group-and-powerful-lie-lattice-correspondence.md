# Uniform group and powerful Lie lattice correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md)

The [Lie algebra of a uniform pro-p group](lie-algebra-of-a-uniform-pro-p-group.md) construction and [BCH convergence on a powerful Lie lattice](bch-convergence-on-a-powerful-lie-lattice.md) are mutually inverse on objects and morphisms. On the group side morphisms are continuous [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md); on the lattice side they are linear [Lie algebra homomorphisms](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) over the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md). A group homomorphism respects powers, roots and limits, and a Lie homomorphism respects every convergent [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) term.
