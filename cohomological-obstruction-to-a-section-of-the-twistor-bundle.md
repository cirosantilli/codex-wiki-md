# Cohomological obstruction to a section of the twistor bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Section (fiber bundle)](section-fiber-bundle.md)

If the displayed [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) had a section $s$, its projection $p$ would satisfy $s^*p^*=1$. The [cohomology ring of complex projective space](cohomology-ring-of-complex-projective-space.md) is $\mathbb Z[h]/(h^4)$ with $|h|=2$. For a generator $u\in H^4(S^4;\mathbb Z)$, write $p^*u=mh^2$. But $s^*h=0$ since $H^2(S^4;\mathbb Z)=0$, giving $u=s^*p^*u=0$. This contradiction proves that no section exists, without needing the value of $m$.
