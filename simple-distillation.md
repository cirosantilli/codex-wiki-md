# Simple distillation

↑ **Parent:** [Distillation](distillation.md)

In [simple distillation](simple-distillation.md), pot [vapor](vapor.md) passes directly to the [laboratory condenser](laboratory-condenser.md). An initial small condensate sample can approximate [vapor-liquid equilibrium](vapor-liquid-equilibrium.md) with the current pot [liquid](liquid.md). Removing enriched distillate depletes the pot's more volatile component. A collected fraction averages the changing condensate composition, rather than preserving the first-drop [mole fraction](mole-fraction.md).[Chem2O06 Laboratory Manual by McMaster University; A Mixture of Ideal Liquids, discussion of first and later fractions](chem2o06-laboratory-manual-by-mcmaster-university/distillation-introduction.md)
