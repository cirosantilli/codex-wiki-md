# Convective cooling under the four-thirds heat-transfer law

↑ **Parent:** [Four-thirds convective heat-transfer law](four-thirds-convective-heat-transfer-law.md)

A well-mixed layer of depth $h$ losing heat through an unstable cold upper boundary has bulk excess temperature $\theta=T-T_0$. With [four-thirds convective heat-transfer law](four-thirds-convective-heat-transfer-law.md) flux $q=Ck[g\beta/(\nu\kappa)]^{1/3}\theta^{4/3}$, its heat capacity per area is $kh/\kappa$. Thus $\dot\theta=-K\theta^{4/3}$ with $K=C\kappa[g\beta/(\nu\kappa)]^{1/3}/h$, giving the displayed cooling law. It excludes the initial conductive transient and the late low-Rayleigh conduction regime.
