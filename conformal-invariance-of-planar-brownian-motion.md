# Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md)

If $B$ is planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) in a domain $D$ and $f:D\to D'$ is a nonconstant [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md), then $f(B)$, run with the clock $\int_0^t|f'(B_s)|^2ds$, is planar Brownian motion in $D'$ until the relevant exit time. In particular, [conformal maps](conformal-map.md) transport [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) between planar domains.
