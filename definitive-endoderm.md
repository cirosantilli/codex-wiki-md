# Definitive endoderm

↑ **Parent:** [Endoderm](endoderm.md)

The embryonic [endoderm](endoderm.md) population specified during [gastrulation](gastrulation.md), distinguished from earlier extraembryonic [primitive endoderm](hypoblast.md). In mouse, resulting gut [endoderm](endoderm.md) can include intercalated [cells](biological-cell-split.md) from distinct histories.
