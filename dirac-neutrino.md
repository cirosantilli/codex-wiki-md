# Dirac neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

A [neutrino](neutrino.md) with distinct particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) states and a Dirac [mass](mass.md) term connecting left- and right-chiral fields. If conventional [lepton number](lepton-number.md) is conserved, such a [mass](mass.md) does not cause [neutrinoless double beta decay](neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md). Oscillation evidence for nonzero [neutrino masses](neutrino-mass.md) does not alone distinguish the Dirac and Majorana possibilities.
