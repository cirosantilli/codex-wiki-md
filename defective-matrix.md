# Defective matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md)

A [matrix](matrix.md) is defective when an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) has [geometric multiplicity](geometric-multiplicity.md) smaller than its [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md). Over a [field](field.md) in which the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) splits, this is precisely the obstruction to an [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md): at least one [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) has size greater than one, and [generalized eigenvectors](generalized-eigenvector.md) are needed to complete a [basis](basis.md). The [shear mapping](shear-mapping.md) in [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md) is the simplest example.
