# Dirac mass domain wall

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md)

A spatial change of the sign of a Dirac mass coefficient. In the two-dimensional model, a wall along one direction can support a mode exponentially confined across the wall and propagating chirally along it. The existence and orientation can be obtained directly from the first-order wave equation without assigning a bulk invariant to an unregularized continuum model.
