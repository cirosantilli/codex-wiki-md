# Holomorph

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism group](automorphism-group.md)

The holomorph acts on the underlying set of a [group](group-split.md) by $x\mapsto g\alpha(x)$. Its multiplication is $(g,\alpha)(h,\beta)=(g\alpha(h),\alpha\beta)$. This is exactly the [normalizer](normalizer.md) of the left regular [permutation](permutation.md) representation: a normalizing [permutation](permutation.md) fixing the identity is an [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md), and every normalizing [permutation](permutation.md) is a left translation followed by one. The embedded copy of $G$ is normal and the identity stabilizer is its [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) [group](group-split.md).
