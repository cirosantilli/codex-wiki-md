# Nambu superconductivity and chiral symmetry analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)

[Yoichiro Nambu](yoichiro-nambu.md)'s [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) work led to the [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) ideas recognized by his share of the 2008 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md).[Biographical Note, superconductivity origins and 2008 award](guide-to-the-yoichiro-nambu-papers-1917-2009-by-university-of-chicago-library.md) His 1960 treatment made the [Meissner effect](meissner-effect.md) response consistent with [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) by including collective pair contributions alongside the [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md). The resulting [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) relates the interaction correction to the current and the [self-energy](self-energy.md); retaining the [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md) alone omits part of the response.[Abstract, paragraphs 2–4: current corrections, Ward identity, Meissner response and collective pair excitations](quasi-particles-and-gauge-invariance-in-the-theory-of-superconductivity-by-yoichiro-nambu.md)

The individual Nobel lecture explains the bridge to [physics](particle-physics.md): the [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) gap suggested a dynamically generated [fermion](fermion.md) [mass](mass.md), and the accompanying collective excitation suggested a light [pion](pion.md). It also notes that the long-range [Coulomb interaction](coulomb-interaction.md) turns the charged phase response into a [plasmon](plasmon.md). A neutral global phase mode and a charged material therefore need different low-[momentum](momentum-split.md) descriptions.[p. 1016, Eqs. (1)–(7): quasiparticles, collective current, plasma frequency and pion analogy](nobel-lecture-spontaneous-symmetry-breaking-in-particle-physics-a-case-of-cross-fertilization-by-yoichiro-nambu.md)

In 1961, [Yoichiro Nambu](yoichiro-nambu.md) and [Giovanni Jona-Lasinio](giovanni-jona-lasinio.md) supplied a four-[fermion](fermion.md) model with a self-consistent [mass](mass.md) and a massless [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md) bound state: an idealized [pion](pion.md). Their original constituents were nucleon fields. Their introduction explicitly distinguishes the zero-[mass](mass.md) neutral collective excitation from its charged [plasma](plasma-physics.md) counterpart and allows approximate [chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md) as the explanation of a nonzero [pion](pion.md) [mass](mass.md). This is a model of dynamical [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) breaking, rather than a microscopic derivation of [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md).[§I, pp. 345–346: gap analogy, collective pairs, idealized pion, Coulomb interaction and approximate chirality](dynamical-model-of-elementary-particles-based-on-an-analogy-with-superconductivity-i-by-yoichiro-nambu-and-giovanni-jona-lasinio.md)

In the modern two-light-[quark](quark.md) account, [chiral symmetry breaking](chiral-symmetry-breaking.md) leaves [isospin](isospin.md) intact and produces three [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) in the exact non-anomalous chiral limit. Small [quark masses](quark-mass.md) explicitly break that [chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md), so the observed [pions](pion.md) are [pseudo-Goldstone bosons](pseudo-goldstone-boson.md). The [chiral protection of a fermion mass](chiral-protection-of-a-fermion-mass.md) identifies the distinct obstruction to inserting an explicit [fermion](fermion.md) [mass](mass.md) into a chirally symmetric equation. The useful lesson is that massive individual excitations and much lighter collective excitations can coexist because they describe different motions of an interacting state.

A concrete experimental motivation for treating the electromagnetic response carefully is the [Field-cooled Meissner experiment](field-cooled-meissner-experiment.md). MIT's realization cools [lead](lead.md) in a [solenoid](solenoid.md) field using helium gas. A nearby [thermometer](thermometer.md) follows the cooling; a test coil feeds an integrator and [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md). Expulsion gives a pulse, warming reverses it, and switching off the field while the sample is normal checks its sign. A change in alternating-field response alone can reflect normal [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md). This readout tests [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) expulsion, not the spectrum or existence of a neutral [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md).[Probe I, §4.2 and Fig. 6, pp. 5–6; §5.1, pp. 8–9; §5.2.2, p. 10: field cooling and sign control](superconductivity-the-meissner-effect-persistent-currents-and-the-josephson-effects-by-mit-department-of-physics.md)

For a [global symmetry in field theory](global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md), different orientations can be physically distinct states. A local [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) instead includes redundant descriptions of the same physical state. A gauge-dependent field expectation alone is consequently insufficient as a physical criterion for [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). The [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) changes the physical mode content when the [gauge field](gauge-field.md) is dynamical; the global [Goldstone theorem](goldstone-theorem.md) alone does not prove that mechanism. In particular, the massless direction of a gauge-fixed [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) need not appear as a separately observable massless [particle](particle-physics.md).
