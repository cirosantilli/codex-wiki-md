# Radiative shift of an isothermal breeze maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Subsonic isothermal breeze](subsonic-isothermal-breeze.md)

For unattenuated isotropic radiation from a point source, the [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) force on each absorbing particle is $\sigma L/(4\pi cr^2)$. Dividing by its [mass](mass.md) $\bar m$ changes the effective inverse-square inward acceleration to $[GM-\sigma L/(4\pi\bar m c)]/r^2$. The wind equation therefore places the maximum of a [subsonic isothermal breeze](subsonic-isothermal-breeze.md) at the displayed radius, provided the bracket is positive. If it is nonpositive, the positive-temperature subsonic outflow decreases in speed throughout its domain and has no interior speed maximum. A cross section per particle requires that particle's mass; an [opacity](opacity.md) per unit mass instead absorbs this conversion.
