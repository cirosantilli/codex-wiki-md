# Positive stationary Hamiltonian selects a particle splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum state in a stationary spacetime](vacuum-state-in-a-stationary-spacetime.md)

For a stable free [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md) with a complete timelike Killing flow and a positive self-adjoint one-particle energy generator, its positive spectral subspace selects [positive-frequency solutions](positive-frequency-solution.md) of positive [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md). Their annihilation operators define the ground-state [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md). Degenerate modes can be mixed by a unitary matrix without changing this splitting or vacuum. Merely being stationary near infinity is insufficient if the Killing field becomes spacelike in an [ergoregion of a stationary spacetime](ergoregion-of-a-stationary-spacetime.md); zero modes, instability or additional boundary conditions also require separate treatment. The choice of a ground state does not prevent other states, such as thermal states, from using the same particle operators.
