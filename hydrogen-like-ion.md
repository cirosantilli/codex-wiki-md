# Hydrogen-like ion

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

A monatomic ion with exactly one [electron](electron.md). Its nonrelativistic [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) energy levels scale as $Z^2$ at fixed [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md), apart from [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) corrections.
