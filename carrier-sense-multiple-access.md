# Carrier-sense multiple access

↑ **Parent:** [Random access network](random-access-network.md)

Carrier-sense multiple access lets a station attempt transmission only when interfering neighbors are inactive. An ideal continuous-time model gives inactive station $r$ an exponential attempt rate $e^{\theta_r}$ and unit-rate exponential transmission completions. [Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) then gives activation weights $e^{\theta\cdot n}$ on the [independent sets](independent-set-graph-theory.md) of the [interference graph](interference-graph.md).
