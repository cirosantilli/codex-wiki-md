# Constructive solid geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_solid_geometry)

[Constructive solid geometry](constructive-solid-geometry.md) describes a solid by combining primitive regions with Boolean [set union](set-union.md), [set intersection](set-intersection.md) and difference. With primitive defining [functions](function-split.md) nonpositive inside, [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) implements union and [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) implements intersection. These formulas encode membership rather than signed [distance functions](metric.md). Solid modellers often regularize Boolean operations to remove isolated lower-dimensional remnants.
