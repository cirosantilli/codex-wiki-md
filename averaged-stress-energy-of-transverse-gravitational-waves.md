# Averaged stress-energy of transverse gravitational waves

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md)

In the weak-field, short-wavelength averaging regime, a [transverse-traceless tensor](transverse-traceless-tensor.md) strain $H_{ij}$ carries the displayed effective [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) in units $c=1$. The average spans several wave periods while remaining small compared with the background [curvature](curvature.md) scale. The stress is second order in amplitude, so it is absent from a first-order vacuum [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md) calculation. For a plane wave, summing the two equal transverse diagonal contributions gives flux $(16\pi G)^{-1}\langle\dot H_+^2+\dot H_\times^2\rangle$. This effective averaged stress is not an arbitrary pointwise gravitational energy [tensor](tensor.md).
