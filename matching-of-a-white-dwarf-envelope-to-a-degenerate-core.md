# Matching of a white-dwarf envelope to a degenerate core

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope](radiative-zero-white-dwarf-envelope.md)

Continuity of [temperature](temperature.md), [pressure](pressure.md) and [mass density](density.md) matches the [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) envelope [pressure](pressure.md) $\mathcal R\rho_bT_c$ to the nonrelativistic [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) $K_{\rm NR}\rho_b^{5/3}$. Substituting this base [mass density](density.md) into the [radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope](radiative-zero-white-dwarf-envelope.md) law gives the luminosity-temperature scaling behind [Mestel's cooling law](mestel-s-cooling-law.md). The matched point is a transition approximation; the cold degeneracy law does not represent finite-temperature corrections there exactly.
