# Translated vector-space structure

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

Choosing a new origin $a$ in a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) transports its operations along $T(x)=x-a$. Since $T(x\oplus y)=T(x)+T(y)$ and $T(k\odot x)=kT(x)$, every [vector space](vector-space-split.md) axiom transfers through this [bijection](bijection.md). The new additive identity is $a$ and the additive inverse of $x$ is $2a-x$. This illustrates why an [affine space](affine-space.md) has no preferred origin even though choosing one produces a [vector space](vector-space-split.md).
