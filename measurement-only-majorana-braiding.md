# Measurement-only Majorana braiding

↑ **Parent:** [Majorana braiding operator](majorana-braiding-operator.md)

Pairwise [fermion-parity measurements](fermion-parity-measurement.md) involving an ancillary Majorana mode can reproduce a [Majorana braiding operator](majorana-braiding-operator.md) without physically moving the zero modes. Projecting the ancilla back to its initial parity sector turns the product of projectors into the braid unitary up to normalization and known outcome-dependent signs.
