# Fatty acid elongase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [fatty acid elongase](fatty-acid-elongase.md) participates in acyl-chain extension. An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) acting on an acyl-CoA substrate may not directly access a chain held in a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md).
