# Reaction propensity function

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic chemical kinetics](stochastic-chemical-kinetics.md)

The reaction propensity $a_r(x)$ is the instantaneous rate at which reaction $r$ fires when the copy-number state is $x$. Conditional on the present state, its reaction clock has an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $a_r(x)$.
