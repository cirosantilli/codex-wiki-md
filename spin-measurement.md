# Spin measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Spin one-half](spin-one-half.md)

For a [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) system, an ideal [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) along a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$ measures $\sigma_{\mathbf n}=\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\pm1$, corresponding to physical [spin angular momentum](spin.md) components $\pm\hbar/2$, and its [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md) are $\Pi_\pm=(I\pm\sigma_{\mathbf n})/2$. For [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) $\rho=(I+\mathbf r\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$, the [Born rule](born-rule.md) and the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) give the displayed [probabilities](probability.md). Thus a [spin measurement](spin-measurement.md) along one direction tests only that component of the [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md); measurements along three independent directions determine the entire [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md).
