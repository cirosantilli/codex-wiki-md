<h1 id="neimark-sacker-bifurcation">Neimark–Sacker bifurcation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

A Neimark–Sacker bifurcation of a smooth planar map occurs when a simple complex-conjugate pair of fixed-point [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md) crosses the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) away from low-order resonances. If its radial [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) is $r'=r(1+\eta+dr^2)+\cdots$ with $d\ne0$, a small invariant closed curve has $r^2=-\eta/d+\cdots$. For $d<0$ it appears on $\eta>0$, where the [fixed point](fixed-point.md) has become unstable, and its radial [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md) is $1-2\eta+\cdots$, so the bifurcation is supercritical. For $d>0$ the curve lies on $\eta<0$ and is unstable, giving a subcritical bifurcation. [Area-preserving maps](area-preserving-map.md) have a vanishing cubic radial drift at an elliptic point and do not meet this dissipative nondegeneracy condition.
