# Inner models with all reals preserve omega-one

↑ **Parent:** [Relative constructible universe](relative-constructible-universe.md)

An [inner model](inner-model.md) $W$ of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) containing all ambient [subsets](subset.md) of $\omega$ computes the same [countable ordinals](countable-ordinal.md) as the ambient universe. Every ambient countably infinite [ordinal](ordinal.md) has a [well-order code](well-order-code.md) coded by a [subset](subset.md) of $\omega$, hence that code belongs to $W$; finite [ordinals](ordinal.md) are already shared. The [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md) inside $W$ recovers the actual [ordinal](ordinal.md) and its countability witness. Conversely any countability witness in $W$ remains one outside. If $W$ also contains the full [power set](power-set.md) of $\omega$ as a [set](set-split.md), its [Continuum hypothesis](continuum-hypothesis.md) would give an ambient [bijection](bijection.md) from $\omega_1$ onto that same [power set](power-set.md). Thus an ambient failure of this well-orderable formulation of the [Continuum hypothesis](continuum-hypothesis.md) is preserved in such an inner model. No internal [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) is required for this argument.
