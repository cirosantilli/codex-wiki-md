# Charm (quantum number)

↑ **Parent:** [Quark flavor](quark-flavor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charm_(quantum_number))

The net number of [charm quarks](charm-quark.md) minus [charm antiquarks](charm-antiquark.md). It is conserved by the [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) and [electromagnetic interaction](electromagnetic-interaction.md), but can change through [weak charged currents](charged-current.md).
