<h1 id="so-3-invariant-quartic-scalar-potential">SO(3) invariant quartic scalar potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md)

The [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) $V(\Phi)=\lambda(\Phi^T\Phi-v^2)^2/4$, with $\lambda>0$, is invariant under the [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md). For $v>0$, its vacuum manifold is a sphere. Canonical scalar normalization gives radial mass squared $2\lambda v^2$.
