# Stable projective point in geometric invariant theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric invariant theory](geometric-invariant-theory.md)

A point is stable when a positive-degree homogeneous invariant is nonzero there and its orbit is closed in that invariant's nonvanishing affine open set, with finite [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md). This is stronger than merely having a closed orbit or a nonzero invariant. For [binary forms](binary-form.md) of degree four, the stable points have four distinct projective roots.
