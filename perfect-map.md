# Perfect map

↑ **Parent:** [Closed map](closed-map.md)

A perfect map is a continuous closed map whose [fibers](fiber-of-a-function.md) are [compact](compact-space.md). Surjectivity is sometimes an additional convention; here it is not imposed, and empty [fibers](fiber-of-a-function.md) are [compact](compact-space.md). For maps between [Tychonoff spaces](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md), the [perfect-map characterization by Stone-Čech remainders](perfect-map-characterization-by-stone-cech-remainders.md) identifies this property using the unique extension to their compactifications.
