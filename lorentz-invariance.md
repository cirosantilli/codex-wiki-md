# Lorentz invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md)

A physical quantity is Lorentz invariant when its value is unchanged under [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md). Tensor equations can be [Lorentz covariant](lorentz-covariance.md) while their components transform. Fixed-vector gauge choices need not make covariance manifest, although physical gauge-invariant predictions retain the symmetry.
