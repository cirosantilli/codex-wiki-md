# Ductile-to-brittle transition

↑ **Parent:** [Fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md)

A change from predominantly ductile deformation and fracture to predominantly brittle fracture as [temperature](temperature-split.md) decreases under specified loading and specimen conditions. A quoted transition [temperature](temperature-split.md) depends on its criterion: absorbed impact energy, fracture appearance and [fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md) need not give the same number. In ferritic [steel](steel.md), [irradiation hardening](irradiation-hardening.md) can raise the transition [temperature](temperature-split.md).
