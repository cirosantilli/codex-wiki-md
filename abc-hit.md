# Abc-hit

↑ **Parent:** [abc conjecture](abc-conjecture.md)

A triple of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $(a,b,c)$ with $a<b$, $a+b=c$, $\gcd(a,b)=1$, and $\operatorname{rad}(abc)<c$, where $\operatorname{rad}$ is the [radical of an integer](radical-of-an-integer.md). The example $(1,8,9)$ has $\operatorname{rad}(72)=6<9$.
