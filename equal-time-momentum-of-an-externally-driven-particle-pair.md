# Equal-time momentum of an externally driven particle pair

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

A sum of particle [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) at specified events is a [four-vector](four-vector.md), but sums recomputed at simultaneous events in different [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) need not correspond to that same [four-vector](four-vector.md). For opposite equal [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) initially at rest a distance $d$ apart, a longitudinal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) switched on simultaneously in the laboratory produces zero total laboratory [three-momentum](three-momentum.md). In a frame moving at $v>0$, the switch-on events are separated by $\gamma_vvd/c^2$; during that interval only one force acts. The late equal-time total [three-momentum](three-momentum.md) is $-2\gamma_vmv+\gamma_veEvd/c^2$, while the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) of an equal-laboratory-time sum varies as the laboratory pair's [energy](energy-split.md) grows. Closed-system [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) requires including the field and external apparatus.
