# Heterotrophic respiration

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md)

Respiratory carbon loss by organisms using organic matter produced by others. It includes important decomposer fluxes and must be distinguished from [autotrophic respiration](autotrophic-respiration.md).
