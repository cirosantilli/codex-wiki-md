# Polar equation of a conic

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

A [conic section](conic-section.md) with a [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) at the origin has the [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) [equation](equation.md) $r=\ell/(1+e\cos\theta)$, where $\ell>0$ is the [semilatus rectum](semilatus-rectum.md) and $e\geq0$ the [eccentricity](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md). The [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) definition $r=e(d-r\cos\theta)$ gives the formula with $\ell=ed$ when $e>0$; $e=0$ is the [circle](circle.md) limit. The cases $0<e<1$, $e=1$, and $e>1$ give an [ellipse](ellipse.md), [parabola](parabola.md), and [hyperbola](hyperbola.md). The physical [radius](radius.md) requires a positive [denominator](denominator.md). For an attractive inverse-square [central force](central-force.md), the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) gives $\ell=mh^2/k$ and $e=A/(mk)$.
