# Quasirandom bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Biregular graph](biregular-graph.md)

For a [biregular graph](biregular-graph.md) of [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) $\gamma$, small rectangular discrepancy, a [box norm](box-norm.md) fourth power close to $\gamma^4$, and a small second [singular value](singular-value.md) of the [normalized adjacency operator of a bipartite graph](normalized-adjacency-operator-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) are equivalent notions of quasirandomness, with quantitative bounds independent of the part sizes. Centering the adjacency function removes the constant singular component.
