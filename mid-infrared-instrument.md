# Mid-Infrared Instrument

↑ **Parent:** [James Webb Space Telescope](james-webb-space-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Infrared_Instrument)

MIRI's low-resolution spectroscopy includes a slitless time-series mode for [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) observations. Its useful coverage includes the $5$–$12\,\mu\mathrm m$ region, with longer-wavelength limits dependent on calibration and sensitivity. See [the instrument guide](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/jwst-mid-infrared-instrument/miri-observing-modes/miri-low-resolution-spectroscopy).
