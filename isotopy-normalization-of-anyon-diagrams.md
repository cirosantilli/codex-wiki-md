# Isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion tree](anyon-fusion-tree.md)

The convention in which the value of an unknotted, untwisted charge-$a$ loop is its [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md) $d_a$. For multiplicity-free [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md), a split-fuse bubble with input charges $a,b$ and total charge $c$ gives $\sqrt{d_ad_b/d_c}$ times the identity strand; the corresponding completeness resolution has coefficient $\sqrt{d_c/(d_ad_b)}$.
