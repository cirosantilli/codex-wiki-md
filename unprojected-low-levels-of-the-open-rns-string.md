# Unprojected low levels of the open RNS string

↑ **Parent:** [Spinning string](spinning-string.md)

Before the [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), the [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) has [string oscillator](string-oscillator.md) levels $0,1/2,1,3/2$, with physical multiplicities $1,8,36,128$ in ten-dimensional [light-cone gauge in string theory](light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md). The eight transverse [worldsheet Majorana fermions](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) supply eight single $\psi_{-1/2}$ states. Level one has eight $\alpha_{-1}$ states and $\binom82=28$ pairs of distinct $\psi_{-1/2}$ modes. Level $3/2$ has $8$ single $\psi_{-3/2}$ modes, $8\cdot8$ mixed [boson](boson.md)–[fermion](fermion.md) modes and $\binom83=56$ triples. Their squared masses are respectively $-1/(2\alpha'),0,1/(2\alpha'),1/\alpha'$. In the [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md), the [Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra](ramond-zero-mode-clifford-algebra.md) gives a sixteen-dimensional ground space. At level one, $\alpha_{-1}^i$ and $\psi_{-1}^i$ each provide $8\cdot16$ states, hence multiplicities $16,256$ at squared masses $0,1/\alpha'$. No half-integer Ramond levels occur. The [GSO projection](gso-projection.md) keeps odd NS [fermion](fermion.md) number and one total Ramond [chirality](chirality-physics.md), giving $8$ [bosons](boson.md) and $8$ [fermions](fermion.md) at zero mass, then $128$ of each at the first massive level.
