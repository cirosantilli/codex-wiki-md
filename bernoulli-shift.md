# Bernoulli shift

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodicity](ergodicity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_shift)

On a one-sided sequence space $E^{\mathbb N}$ with [product measure](product-measure.md), the Bernoulli shift is

$$
S(x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots)=(x_1,x_2,x_3,\ldots).
$$

It preserves the product measure. Every invariant event lies in the [tail sigma-algebra](tail-sigma-algebra.md) of the coordinate process, so the [Kolmogorov zero-one law](kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) makes the shift an [ergodic transformation](ergodicity.md).
