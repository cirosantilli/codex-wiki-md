# Orthogonal unitary-gauge slice near a scalar vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md)

For a real orthogonal [gauge group representation](gauge-group-representation.md), the tangent vectors to the vacuum orbit are $v_a=T_a\phi_0$. Choose an independent basis of these vectors. Under an infinitesimal [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md), the derivative of the displayed slice conditions with respect to the broken gauge parameters is the [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $v_a^Tv_b$. It is invertible on the independent tangent directions, so the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) selects this [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) locally near the vacuum. The remaining scalar fluctuations are orthogonal to the gauge orbit, and their derivative has no quadratic mixing with the massive [gauge fields](gauge-field.md). The statement is local and does not assert a global gauge slice through zeros of the scalar field.
