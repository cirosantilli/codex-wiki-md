# Spacelike geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A spacelike geodesic has positive tangent norm in signature $(-+++)$ and satisfies the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) with an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) preserves that norm along the curve. In [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) these are straight lines whose spatial tangent is larger in magnitude than the time component; their two ends approach [spacelike infinity](spacelike-infinity.md) in a [conformal completion](conformal-completion.md).
