# Sphere theorem for a Stokes streamfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes streamfunction equation for irrotational flow](stokes-streamfunction-equation-for-irrotational-flow.md)

Suppose the incident [axisymmetric flow](axisymmetric-flow.md) is an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) and an [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md), with [Stokes streamfunction](stokes-streamfunction.md) $\Psi$ regular inside $r<a$. The displayed transformation adds an impermeable [sphere](sphere.md) at $r=a$ without new finite exterior singularities. Writing $s=a^2/r$ and $G=(r/a)\Psi(s,\theta)$, direct differentiation shows

$$
\left(r^2\partial_r^2+\partial_\theta^2-\cot\theta\,\partial_\theta\right)G
=\frac r a\left(s^2\Psi_{ss}+\Psi_{\theta\theta}-\cot\theta\,\Psi_\theta\right)=0.
$$

Hence the [Stokes streamfunction equation for irrotational flow](stokes-streamfunction-equation-for-irrotational-flow.md) is preserved. At $r=a$, $\widetilde\Psi=0$, making the radial [velocity](velocity.md) zero. Exterior points sample only the regular interior under inversion. If $\Psi$ vanishes at the origin and is $O(r)$ there with smooth regular derivatives, the induced [velocity](velocity.md) decays at infinity. This is an inversion construction for an axisymmetric streamfunction, distinct from a scalar [Kelvin transform](kelvin-transform.md).
