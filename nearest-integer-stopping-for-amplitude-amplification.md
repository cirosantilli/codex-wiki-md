# Nearest-integer stopping for amplitude amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude amplification](amplitude-amplification.md)

For known initial success probability $p\in(0,1)$, this choice of iteration count ensures $|(2r+1)\theta-\pi/2|\leq\theta$. Hence the success probability after [amplitude amplification](amplitude-amplification.md) is at least $\cos^2\theta=1-p$. Counting initial state preparation and the forward and inverse preparation in each iterate gives $2r+1$ preparation-unitary applications, asymptotic to $\pi/(2\sqrt p)$ as $p\to0$.
