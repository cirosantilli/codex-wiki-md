# Trial wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md)

An admissible [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) chosen from a tractable family for the [variational method](variational-method.md). Its [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) expectation gives an upper bound on the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md); parameters can be adjusted to lower that bound.
