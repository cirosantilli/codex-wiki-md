# Finite-box ground-energy convention in Bose condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous ideal Bose gas condensation](homogeneous-ideal-bose-gas-condensation.md)

A hard-wall cube has positive one-particle ground [energy](energy-split.md) $E_0=3\pi^2\hbar^2/(2mL^2)$. Condensation corresponds to the absolute [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) approaching $E_0$, not zero. Shifting the [energy](energy-split.md) reference makes the limiting [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) zero, while the exact shifted excited-state [density of states](density-of-states.md) also shifts. Keeping its bulk square-root form is a large-system approximation valid when $k_BT\gg E_0$. A periodic cube instead has zero ground [energy](energy-split.md).
