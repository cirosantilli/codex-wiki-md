# Antipodal stereographic coordinate relation

↑ **Parent:** [Stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md)

For north-pole [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) $p=(X+iY)/(1-Z)$ on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md), the [antipodal pair](antipodal-pair.md) point has coordinate $q=-(X+iY)/(1+Z)$. Since $X^2+Y^2=1-Z^2$, this gives $p\overline q=-1$ when both coordinates are finite. On the whole [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), write $q=-1/\overline p$, pairing zero with infinity. The two axis endpoints fixed by a sphere [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) obey this relation.
