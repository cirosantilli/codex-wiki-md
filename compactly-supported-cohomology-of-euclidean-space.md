# Compactly supported cohomology of Euclidean space

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported cohomology](compactly-supported-cohomology.md)

For a nonnegative integer $d$ and an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$,

$$
H_c^q(\mathbb R^d;A)\cong\begin{cases}A,&q=d,\\0,&q\ne d.\end{cases}
$$

The [one-point compactification](alexandroff-extension.md) is the [sphere](sphere.md) $S^d$, and it is locally contractible at the added point. Apply the [compact-support comparison with a one-point compactification](compact-support-comparison-with-a-one-point-compactification.md) and the [reduced cohomology](reduced-cohomology.md) of a sphere. In dimension zero the compactification is $S^0$, giving the same formula.
