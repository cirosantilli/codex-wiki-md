# Permutation cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric group](symmetric-group.md)

A permutation cycle $(a_1\,a_2\,\ldots\,a_k)$ sends $a_i$ to $a_{i+1}$, sends $a_k$ to $a_1$, and fixes every other element. Its [order](order-of-a-group-element.md) is $k$.
