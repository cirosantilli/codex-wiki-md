# Star-shaped domain

↑ **Parent:** [Star-shaped set](star-shaped-set.md)

An open [star-shaped set](star-shaped-set.md): there is a point $p$ such that every line segment from $p$ to another point of the set lies entirely in the set. In the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), this geometric condition supplies the star-domain form of the [Cauchy integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md). The positive-axis slit plane is star-shaped about $-1$, even though it is not convex.
