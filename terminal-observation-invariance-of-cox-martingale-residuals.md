# Terminal-observation invariance of Cox martingale residuals

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale residual](martingale-residual.md)

In an untied [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md) with a single subject remaining after all other observed exit times, that subject's event contributes the constant factor one to [Cox partial likelihood](cox-partial-likelihood.md). Replacing its terminal censoring time by a later event therefore preserves the fitted coefficients and every earlier [Breslow estimator](breslow-estimator.md) increment. At its new event, the subject's fitted cumulative hazard increases by one, cancelling the increase of one in its event indicator. Every [martingale residual](martingale-residual.md) is unchanged. This property relies on the subject being alone in the terminal [risk set](risk-set.md) and the same covariate history being retained.
