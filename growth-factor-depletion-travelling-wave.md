# Growth-factor depletion travelling wave

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)

The [dimensionless](nondimensionalization.md) [bacterial](bacteria.md) growth and substrate depletion system $b_t=b_{xx}+gb$, $g_t=-gb$ has [travelling-wave equations](travelling-wave.md) $b\prime\prime+cb\prime+gb=0$, $cg\prime=gb$ in coordinate $y=x-ct$. For a [front](travelling-wave.md) with $(b,g)\to(b_0,0)$ behind and $(b,g)\to(0,1)$ ahead, its [first integral](first-integral-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md) is $b\prime+c(b+g)=c$, forcing $b_0=1$. Positivity of the leading edge requires $c\ge2$.
