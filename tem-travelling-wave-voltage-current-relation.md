# TEM travelling-wave voltage-current relation

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md)

A lossless uniform [transmission line](transmission-line.md) has a forward travelling-wave [voltage](voltage.md) and current related by $V_0=Z_0I_0$ with $Z_0=\sqrt{L/C}$. Arbitrary independent DC [voltage](voltage.md) and current fields cannot simply acquire the same forward [wave](wave-split.md) factor while retaining Maxwell consistency.
