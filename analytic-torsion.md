# Analytic torsion

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_torsion)

Analytic torsion is a spectral invariant constructed from regularized determinants of the Laplacians on differential forms with suitable flat coefficients. It is the analytic counterpart of combinatorial Reidemeister torsion, whose refinements include [Turaev torsion](turaev-torsion.md); equality theorems relate the spectral and combinatorial invariants under appropriate hypotheses on the manifold and coefficients.
