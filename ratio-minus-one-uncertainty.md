# Ratio-minus-one uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For $c=a/b-1$, the sensitivity coefficients are $1/b$ and $-a/b^2$. The first-order [uncertainty propagation](propagation-of-uncertainty.md) law gives the displayed variance; independent inputs remove the covariance term. Subtracting a known constant does not change absolute uncertainty, but can greatly change relative uncertainty when the resulting value is small. The result is a standard uncertainty estimate, not automatically a worst-case error bound or a confidence interval.
