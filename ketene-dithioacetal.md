# Ketene dithioacetal

↑ **Parent:** [Alkene](alkene.md)

An [alkene](alkene.md) bearing two thioalkoxy groups on one [carbon](carbon.md), $\mathrm{R_2C=C(SR\prime)_2}$. [pi conjugation](pi-conjugation.md) to a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) can allow addition–elimination at that beta [carbon](carbon.md) with [thiolate](thiolate.md) departure.
