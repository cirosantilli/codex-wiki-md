# Antenna directivity

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna (radio)](antenna-radio.md)

The [antenna directivity](antenna-directivity.md) compares angular radiated power $U=dP_{\rm rad}/d\Omega$ with its average over directions. Its integral is $4\pi$. Unlike [antenna gain](antenna-gain.md), it uses radiated power rather than accepted input power, so it omits dissipative losses. A narrow beam can have high directivity without a larger total radiated power.
