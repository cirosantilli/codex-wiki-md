# Fractional-part continuation formula for the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

[Abel summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) applied to the counting function $\lfloor w\rfloor$ gives the displayed identity for $\Re s>1$ and every $x>0$. Since $0\le\{w\}<1$, the integral is [locally uniformly convergent](locally-uniform-convergence.md) and [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) for $\Re s>0$. This supplies a [meromorphic continuation](meromorphic-continuation.md) of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) to that half-plane, with its sole [pole](pole.md) at one and [residue](residue.md) one. Keeping the fractional endpoint term makes the formula valid at noninteger cutoffs.
