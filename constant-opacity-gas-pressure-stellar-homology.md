# Constant-opacity gas-pressure stellar homology

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

For homologous gas-pressure-supported stars, [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $T_c\propto\mu M/R$ and $\rho_c\propto M/R^3$. [Radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) gives $L\propto RT_c^4/(\kappa\rho_c)$, so the radius cancels and yields the displayed cubic mass scaling. Composition and opacity are held fixed. Strong radiation pressure alters this gas-pressure relation towards the [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md) scaling.
