# Desilylation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

[Desilylation](desilylation.md) removes a silyl group. Attack by a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) at [silicon](silicon.md) can pass through a pentacoordinate [silicon](silicon.md) arrangement before Si–O cleavage; the liberated [oxygen](oxygen.md) may be trapped by a new [electrophile](electrophile.md).
