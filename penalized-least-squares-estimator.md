# Penalized least-squares estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Penalized least squares](penalized-least-squares.md)

A penalized least-squares estimator balances squared error with a nonnegative penalty $J$. The [Lasso](lasso.md) uses the coefficient $\ell_1$ [norm](norm.md); [graph total variation denoising](graph-total-variation-denoising.md) uses the $\ell_1$ [norm](norm.md) of endpoint differences. The choice of normalization of the squared loss changes the numerical tuning parameter.
