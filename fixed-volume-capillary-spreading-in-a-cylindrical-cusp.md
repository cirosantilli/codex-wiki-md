# Fixed-volume capillary spreading in a cylindrical cusp

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp flow between touching cylinders](cusp-flow-between-touching-cylinders.md)

For fixed volume $V$ and symmetric spreading, [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) and the [cusp flow between touching cylinders](cusp-flow-between-touching-cylinders.md) equation give width proportional to $t^{-1/8}$ and support length proportional to $t^{3/8}$. The source-type [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has the displayed profile and $z_N=(8Va/\pi)^{1/4}(8\gamma t/(21\mu a))^{3/8}$. It is a formal outer lubrication solution; its microscopic nose requires a local description when the axial slope becomes large.
