# Low Earth orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)

An [Earth](earth.md)-centred [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) at relatively low altitude, commonly within roughly 2000 km of the surface. It supports frequent orbital revolutions and relatively fine spatial sampling for [remote sensing](remote-sensing.md).
