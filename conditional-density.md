# Conditional density

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md)

Where the [marginal density](marginal-density.md) $f_X(x)$ is positive, a [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) determines the conditional density

$$
f_{Y\mid X=x}(y)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_X(x)}.
$$

It integrates to one in $y$, and supplies [conditional expectations](conditional-expectation.md) by integrating against it. Its values on a probability-zero set of conditioning points are not fixed by the [joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md).
