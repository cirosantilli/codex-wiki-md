# Boundary-pressure correction to the virial theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

An external [pressure](pressure.md) changes the balance even if the system remains confined. For a material boundary acted on by spatially uniform inward [pressure](pressure.md) $P_s$, the surface [force](force.md) contributes $-P_s\int_{\partial\mathcal V}\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=-3P_s\mathcal V$, by the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md). For [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md), the finite-window identity becomes

$$
\frac{G(T)-G(0)}T=2\langle K\rangle_T+\langle U\rangle_T-3\langle P_s\mathcal V\rangle_T.
$$

Here $\mathcal V$ is the enclosed [volume](volume.md), $\mathbf n$ the outward [unit normal](unit-normal.md), and $K$ includes the microscopic translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of the confined particles. Do not also add the same random motions as a separate internal [pressure](pressure.md) term. A nonuniform external stress needs its surface integral; a fixed observational boundary crossed by matter also needs flux terms.[Negative Specific Heat in Astronomy, Physics and Chemistry by D. Lynden-Bell, p. 2, §1: external-pressure virial relation](negative-specific-heat-in-astronomy-physics-and-chemistry-by-d-lynden-bell.md)

With negligible surface [pressure](pressure.md), the [negative heat capacity of a virialized gravitational system](negative-heat-capacity-of-a-virialized-gravitational-system.md) follows from $E=-\langle K\rangle$: after a slow loss of total [energy](energy.md) and readjustment to [virial equilibrium](virial-equilibrium.md), the average [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) rises. For fixed $N$ classical particles with three translational [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md), define kinetic [temperature](temperature.md) $\Theta$ by $\langle K\rangle=3Nk_B\Theta/2$. Along this isolated equilibrium sequence, $dE/d\Theta=-3Nk_B/2$. The system contracts and its binding [potential energy](potential-energy.md) becomes more negative, supplying both the outgoing [energy](energy.md) and the increased [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). This is an endpoint comparison, not a claim that every transient contraction obeys the average relation. A [collisionless stellar system](collisionless-stellar-system.md) need not have a common thermodynamic [temperature](temperature.md); a pressure-confined system instead has $E=-\langle K\rangle+3\langle P_s\mathcal V\rangle$. A stable [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) in contact with a large [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) does not support this negative [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) branch.[Negative Specific Heat in Astronomy, Physics and Chemistry by D. Lynden-Bell, pp. 2-3, §1: isolated-system energy, negative heat capacity and instability in a large heat bath](negative-specific-heat-in-astronomy-physics-and-chemistry-by-d-lynden-bell.md)
