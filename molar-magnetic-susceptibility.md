# Molar magnetic susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

The product $\chi_{\rm mol}=V_m\chi_v$ of [volume magnetic susceptibility](volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md) and molar volume. In SI it has units $\mathrm{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$. For one localized magnetic [ion](ion.md) per formula unit, its [Curie–Weiss law](curie-weiss-law.md) is $\chi_{\rm mol}=C_{\rm mol}/(T-\Theta)$, with $C_{\rm mol}=\mu_0N_A\mu_{\rm eff}^2/(3k_B)$. Normalization per mole of formula units and per mole of magnetic [ions](ion.md) differ when there is more than one such [ion](ion.md) per formula unit.
