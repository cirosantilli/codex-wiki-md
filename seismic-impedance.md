# Seismic impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

For a one-dimensional shear [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md), the [seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md) is the magnitude of the ratio of [stress](stress.md) to particle [velocity](velocity.md) in a progressive wave. With the positive coordinate aligned with [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) propagation and tension-positive [stress](stress.md), $\sigma=-Zv$ for the forward wave and $\sigma=Zv$ for the backward wave. Its instantaneous [elastic-wave energy flux](elastic-wave-energy-flux.md) is $-v\sigma$.
