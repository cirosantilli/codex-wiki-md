<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-level-operator">Neveu–Schwarz level operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md)

Before the [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), $N=\sum_{k>0}\alpha_{-k}\cdot\alpha_k+\sum_{r>0}r\,b_{-r}\cdot b_r$ takes nonnegative half-integer values. The first vector at $N=1/2$ is massless for [string intercept](normal-ordering-constant-of-a-string.md) $a_{\mathrm{NS}}=1/2$.
