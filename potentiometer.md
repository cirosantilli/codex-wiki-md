# Potentiometer

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potentiometer)

A [resistor](resistor.md) with a movable contact that selects a point along its resistive path. In the [Thévenin measurement experiment](thevenin-measurement-experiment.md), a contact and one end provide an adjustable resistive [electrical load](electrical-load.md).
