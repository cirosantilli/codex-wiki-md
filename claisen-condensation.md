# Claisen condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

[carbon](carbon.md) acylation of an [ester](ester.md) [enolate](enolate.md) followed by [deprotonation](deprotonation.md) of the beta-dicarbonyl product. [ketone](ketone.md)-[enolate acylation](enolate-acylation.md) by an [acid](acid-chemistry.md) [chloride](chloride.md) is a closely related acyl-substitution strategy, although it is not the classical [ester](ester.md) self-condensation.
