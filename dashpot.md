# Dashpot

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

An ideal [dashpot](dashpot.md) resists relative motion with force proportional to velocity. Maintaining a displacement rate $\dot x$ requires force $\mu\dot x$ and dissipates power $\mu\dot x^2$ for $\mu>0$. Connecting it in parallel or series with a [spring](spring.md) gives different constitutive laws.
