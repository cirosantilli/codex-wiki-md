# Step number

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical digit](numerical-digit.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) whose adjacent decimal [numerical digits](numerical-digit.md) differ by exactly 1. Its digits form a walk on the [path graph](path-graph.md) $0-1-\cdots-9$, with a nonzero starting digit. Counting [pandigital numbers](pandigital-number.md) of this type can use [digit dynamic programming](digit-dynamic-programming.md) with the final digit and a [bit mask](bit-mask.md) of visited digits as the state.
