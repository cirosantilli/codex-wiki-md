# Conventional unit cell

↑ **Parent:** [Unit cell](unit-cell.md)

A crystallographic [unit cell](unit-cell.md) chosen to display the symmetry or a standard setting of a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). It tiles the periodic arrangement but need not be primitive. A conventional FCC cube contains four [lattice points](lattice-point.md) and a BCC cube contains two; a [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) contains one. These are lattice-point counts, not a universal count of atoms in a chemical motif.
