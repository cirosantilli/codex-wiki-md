# Einstein-Hilbert action

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein-Hilbert_action)

The Einstein-Hilbert action is the integral of the [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) against the metric volume form. Varying it with respect to the metric gives the vacuum [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md), up to a boundary term.
