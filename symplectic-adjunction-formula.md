# Symplectic adjunction formula

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic submanifold](symplectic-submanifold.md)

For a connected embedded symplectic surface $C$ in a symplectic four-manifold,

$$
2g(C)-2=C^2+K_\omega\mathbin{\cdot}C,
$$

where $K_\omega=-c_1(TM,J)$ is the symplectic canonical class for any [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md).
