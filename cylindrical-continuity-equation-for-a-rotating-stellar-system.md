# Cylindrical continuity equation for a rotating stellar system

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating-frame collisionless Boltzmann equation](rotating-frame-collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md)

The zeroth velocity moment gives $\partial_t\rho+R^{-1}\partial_R(R\rho\langle v_R\rangle)+R^{-1}\partial_\phi(\rho\langle v_\phi\rangle)=0$. In the [rotating-frame collisionless Boltzmann equation](rotating-frame-collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md), velocity integration by parts supplies the geometric $\rho\langle v_R\rangle/R$ term. This equation expresses conservation of planar mass.
