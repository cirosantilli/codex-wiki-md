# Stationary convection

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)

[stationary convection](stationary-convection.md) has a time-independent [velocity](velocity.md) and [temperature](temperature.md) field, in the chosen fixed frame. Its onset from a conduction state corresponds to a real growth rate passing through zero. Stability of a resulting steady [convection roll](convection-roll.md) is a separate nonlinear question.
