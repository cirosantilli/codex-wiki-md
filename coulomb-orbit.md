# Coulomb orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

A classical two-body trajectory in an inverse-distance [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md). With an effectively fixed centre and coupling $C$, a circular orbit has $v^2=C/(mr)$ and mechanical energy $-C/(2r)$.
