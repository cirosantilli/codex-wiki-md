# List reversal

↑ **Parent:** [List (abstract data type)](list-abstract-data-type.md)

Reverse the order of a finite [list](list-abstract-data-type.md). Reversal is an [involution](involution.md) and satisfies $(AB)^R=B^RA^R$ for [list concatenation](list-concatenation.md). In an [array](array-data-structure.md) segment of length $n$, exchanging symmetric offsets $j$ and $n-1-j$ for $0\leq j<\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ realizes reversal in constant auxiliary space.
