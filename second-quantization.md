# Second quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_quantization)

Second quantization describes identical-particle [quantum states](quantum-state.md) in [Fock space](fock-space.md) using [creation operators](creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). Relative to an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [one-particle states](one-particle-state.md), a one-body operator becomes $\sum_{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}a_\alpha^\dagger a_\beta$. The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) implements [bosonic](boson.md) statistics, while the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) implement [fermionic](fermion.md) statistics. Expanding the [quantized field](quantized-field.md) in a complete basis gives a delta-function equal-time bracket; restricting the expansion to finitely many modes replaces that delta function by the corresponding [projection operator](projection-linear-algebra.md) kernel.
