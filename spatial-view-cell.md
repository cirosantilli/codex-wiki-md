# Spatial view cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A [neuron](neuron-split.md) selective for a viewed location in external space. In the primate recordings motivating the term, eye-position and locomotion measurements distinguished the looked-at part of the environment from the animal’s self-location and head direction.[Abstract; pp. 1789–1794, active locomotion, eye position and allocentric spatial-view responses](spatial-view-cells-in-the-primate-hippocampus-by-edmund-t-rolls-and-others.md)
