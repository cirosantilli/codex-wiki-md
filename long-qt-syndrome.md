# Long QT syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-channel disease](ion-channel-disease.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_QT_syndrome)

A disorder characterized by delayed ventricular repolarization and susceptibility to dangerous cardiac rhythms. Different molecular subtypes affect different [ion channels](ion-channel.md); type 3 can involve persistent inward sodium current caused by impaired fast [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md).
