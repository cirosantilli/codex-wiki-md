# Reduced magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

[Reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md) is the leading ideal-fluid description of small, anisotropic fluctuations about a strong uniform guide field, with $k_\parallel/k_\perp\sim\delta B_\perp/B_0\sim\delta u_\perp/v_A\ll1$. Its [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) perpendicular [Elsässer variables](elsasser-variable.md) obey

$$
\partial_t\mathbf z^\pm_\perp\mp v_A\partial_z\mathbf z^\pm_\perp+(\mathbf z^\mp_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp)\mathbf z^\pm_\perp=-\nabla_\perp\Pi.
$$

The parallel magnetic derivative includes bending of field lines: $\nabla_\parallel=\partial_z+(\delta\mathbf B_\perp/B_0)\cdot\nabla_\perp$. Slow compressive and [entropy mode](entropy-mode.md) fluctuations are passively transported at this order. The system excludes the rapidly propagating [fast magnetosonic wave](fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) sector rather than describing all possible [MHD](magnetohydrodynamics.md) perturbations.
