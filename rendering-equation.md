# Rendering equation

↑ **Parent:** [Computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendering_equation)

A balance of emitted and reflected [radiance](radiance.md) at a surface. A [bidirectional reflectance distribution function](bidirectional-reflectance-distribution-function.md) weights incident [radiance](radiance.md) from each direction, and a cosine accounts for projected [area](surface-area.md). Indirect illumination makes the incident field depend on other surfaces.
