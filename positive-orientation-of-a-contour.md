# Positive orientation of a contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

For a simple [closed curve](closed-curve.md) in the standard oriented [complex plane](complex-plane.md), positive orientation keeps the bounded interior on the left while traversing the curve. A [circle](circle.md) is therefore traversed counterclockwise; reversing direction changes the sign of its [contour integral](contour-integral.md).
