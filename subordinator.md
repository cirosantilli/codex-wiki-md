# Subordinator

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

A subordinator is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with nondecreasing paths. Its nonnegative [stationary increments](stationary-increments.md) have a [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) of the form

$$
\mathbb E e^{-\lambda T_t}=e^{-t\Phi(\lambda)},\qquad\lambda\geq0,
$$

where $\Phi$ is called its Laplace exponent. Its standard path convention is [càdlàg](cadlag.md). Subordinators provide random clocks for [subordination of a Lévy process](subordination-of-a-levy-process.md).
