# Isothermal fragmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

During isothermal collapse, $T$ is constant and the [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md) decreases as $\rho^{-1/2}$. Successively denser regions can therefore become independently unstable and fragment to smaller masses.
