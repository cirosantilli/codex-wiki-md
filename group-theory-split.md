# Group theory

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_theory)

A [group](group-split.md) records reversible operations that can be combined consistently. Group theory studies their [subgroups](subgroup.md), [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md), [group actions](group-action-split.md), and [quotient groups](quotient-group.md). A [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md) makes the abstraction visible: transformations may change individual points while preserving a specified object or its structure. An [orbit of a group action](orbit-group-theory.md) records which positions can be exchanged, while a [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) records which operations leave a selected feature fixed.
