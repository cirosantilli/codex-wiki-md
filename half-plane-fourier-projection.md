# Half-plane Fourier projection

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier.md)

The half-plane Fourier projection is $(I-iH_v)/2$, where $H_v$ is the [directional Hilbert transform](directional-hilbert-transform.md). A disk of radius $R$, modulated to put a boundary point at the origin, has symbol $\mathbf1_{\{|\xi+Rv|\le R\}}$, which converges almost everywhere to this half-plane symbol. The [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md) makes this an $L^2$ multiplier limit.
