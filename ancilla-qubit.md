# Ancilla qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ancilla](quantum-ancilla.md)

An [ancilla qubit](ancilla-qubit.md) is a two-dimensional [quantum ancilla](quantum-ancilla.md). It is the quantum version of an [ancilla bit](ancilla-bit.md); an auxiliary quantum register can contain several such qubits.
