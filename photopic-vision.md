# Photopic vision

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopic_vision)

[Photopic vision](photopic-vision.md) means vision under light-adapted conditions. Standard [photometry](photometry-optics.md) here uses the [photopic luminous efficiency function](photopic-luminous-efficiency-function.md); [scotopic vision](scotopic-vision.md) uses a different weighting for dark-adapted conditions. The response function and adaptation conditions must be stated when comparing [luminous fluxes](luminous-flux.md).[§3.1, printed p. 3: photopic and scotopic luminous efficiency functions](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure/mise-en-pratique-for-the-definition-of-the-candela-and-associated-derived-units-for-photometric-and-radiometric-quantities-in-the-si.md)
