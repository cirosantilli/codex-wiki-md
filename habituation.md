# Habituation

↑ **Parent:** [Learning](learning.md)

A reduction in a response following repeated stimulation, distinguished from sensory adaptation or motor fatigue. A decrease in [anxiety](anxiety.md) during exposure can occur, but neither defines all therapeutic [learning](learning.md) nor guarantees its transfer to another context.
