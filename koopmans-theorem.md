# Koopmans' theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

In frozen-orbital Hartree-Fock theory, removing an [electron](electron.md) from occupied orbital $i$ changes the energy by approximately $-\epsilon_i$. Orbital relaxation and correlation are omitted, so a measured [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) is not an exact orbital-energy measurement.
