# Sequential-enzyme Hill limit

↑ **Parent:** [Hill equation](hill-equation.md)

For sequential [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) binding, a [quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) gives occupied-state weights $\phi_jS^j$, $\phi_j=\prod_{i=1}^jK_i^{-1}$, relative to free [enzyme](enzyme-split.md). If all intermediate weights tend to zero while the terminal weight remains nonzero, [enzyme conservation](enzyme-conservation.md) leaves only unbound and fully bound states, and the production rate becomes the displayed [Hill equation](hill-equation.md). One realization holds other rates fixed while the first binding rate tends to zero and the last tends to infinity with positive finite product. A statement merely bounding that product does not by itself specify this nontrivial [distinguished limit](distinguished-limit.md).
