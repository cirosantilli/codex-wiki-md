# Lyman-alpha line

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyman-alpha_line)

The [Lyman-alpha line](lyman-alpha-line.md) is the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) $2p\leftrightarrow1s$ electric-dipole transition, with photon energy about $10.2\,\mathrm{eV}$ and vacuum wavelength about $1216$ angstroms. Its emission cools collisionally excited gas, while absorption traces neutral [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) along a background-source sightline.
