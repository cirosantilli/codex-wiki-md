# Magnetic flux transport for a moving circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md)

For a smoothly moving circuit and a divergence-free [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) derivative is $d\Phi/dt=\int_S\partial_t\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S-\oint_C(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\mathbf r$. The minus sign follows by orienting the swept side surface compatibly with the two spanning surfaces. Combining this transport identity with [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) yields the moving-loop electromotive-force law.
