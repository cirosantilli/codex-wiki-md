# Alternating-group polynomial invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

For the [permutation](permutation.md) action on $n$ coordinates with $n\geq2$, every alternating-group invariant is a [symmetric polynomial](symmetric-polynomial.md) plus the Vandermonde product $\Delta$ times a [symmetric polynomial](symmetric-polynomial.md). Thus the invariant ring is $\mathbb C[e_1,\ldots,e_n,Z]/(Z^2-D(e_1,\ldots,e_n))$, where $D$ is the discriminant [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). The two summands are independent over the [symmetric polynomial](symmetric-polynomial.md) ring.
