# Riemannian volume density

↑ **Parent:** [Density bundle](density-bundle.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) gives a positive section of the [density bundle](density-bundle.md), locally $d\mu_g=\sqrt{\det(g_{ij})}|dx^1\cdots dx^n|$. The absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) makes this independent of coordinates without requiring orientability. With an [orientation of a smooth manifold](orientation-of-a-smooth-manifold.md), it corresponds to the [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md).
