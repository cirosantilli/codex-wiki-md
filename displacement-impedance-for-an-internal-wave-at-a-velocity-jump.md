# Displacement impedance for an internal wave at a velocity jump

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave transmission across a velocity jump](internal-wave-transmission-across-a-velocity-jump.md)

At a material velocity jump with continuous background [mass density](density.md), vertical displacement $\eta$ and [fluid pressure](fluid-pressure.md) are continuous, while $w=ikU\eta$ need not be. For a stationary component, $p=U^2\eta_z$ and the signed upward-wave impedance is $Z=U^2m$. Reflection and vertical-displacement transmission are $R=(Z_1-Z_2)/(Z_1+Z_2)$ and $T=2Z_1/(Z_1+Z_2)$.
