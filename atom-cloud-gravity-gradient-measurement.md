# Atom-cloud gravity-gradient measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity gradiometry](gravity-gradiometry.md)

A laboratory [gravity gradiometry](gravity-gradiometry.md) experiment used two vertically separated clouds of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md), launched into [free fall](free-fall.md). Shared [laser](laser.md) beams applied three pulses ($\pi/2,\pi,\pi/2$), splitting and recombining [matter waves](matter-wave.md). Balanced [photodiodes](photodiode.md) measured probe-light absorption to infer internal-state populations; scanning the last pulse's [wave phase](phase-waves.md) produced an [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) fringe.[§I, pp. 033608-1–2; §II E 1, p. 033608-6; §II F 1, p. 033608-7](sensitive-absolute-gravity-gradiometry-using-atom-interferometry-by-j-m-mcguirk-and-others.md)

The difference of fringe [wave phases](phase-waves.md) obeys $\delta\phi=\mathbf k_{\mathrm{eff}}\cdot\Delta\mathbf g\,\tau^2$, with effective [wave vector](wavevector.md) $\mathbf k_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and pulse spacing $\tau$: shared reference motion cancels, leaving differential [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). Eight lead bricks, each approximately $12.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, were placed symmetrically around the lower chamber, about $0.2\,\mathrm m$ from the fountain apex. The measured signal was $(8.1\pm2.1)\times10^{-9}g$, versus a calculated $8.2\times10^{-9}g$, where $g$ denotes terrestrial [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md).[§I, p. 033608-2; §III A 2, p. 033608-10, mass detection](sensitive-absolute-gravity-gradiometry-using-atom-interferometry-by-j-m-mcguirk-and-others.md)

Controls deliberately shook the shared reference reflector and compared actively isolated and unisolated operation. Tilting tests exposed sensitivity to [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) and [centrifugal acceleration](centrifugal-acceleration.md). Thus shared translation is rejectable, while rotation and finite measurement uncertainty remain limitations. The brick signal detects spatially varying [gravity](gravity.md); it does not establish a precision value of the [gravitational constant](gravitational-constant.md).[§III A, p. 033608-9; §III B, pp. 033608-10–11](sensitive-absolute-gravity-gradiometry-using-atom-interferometry-by-j-m-mcguirk-and-others.md)
