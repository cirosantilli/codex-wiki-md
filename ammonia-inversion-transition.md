# Ammonia inversion transition

↑ **Parent:** [Ammonia maser](ammonia-maser.md)

In [ammonia](ammonia.md), the [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) can lie on either side of the plane of the three [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) couples these configurations; the resulting pair of [energy levels](energy-level.md) permits an [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) transition. This geometrical inversion is distinct from [population inversion](population-inversion.md), which concerns how many [molecules](molecule-split.md) occupy each level.[§9–1, ammonia configurations and inversion doublet](chapter-9-the-ammonia-maser.md)

For an idealized pair of [energy levels](energy-level.md) with equal [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md), the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) gives $N_u/N_l=\exp[-h\nu_0/(k_{\mathrm B}T)]<1$ at positive [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$. A warm source therefore supplies upper-state [molecules](molecule-split.md) together with an unwanted lower-state population. Selecting the former can produce [population inversion](population-inversion.md) in the transmitted beam without exciting each transmitted [molecule](molecule-split.md) during selection. The [energy](energy-split.md) per net downward transition is

$$
\epsilon=h\nu_0.
$$

Here $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). Taking the reported $\nu_0=23.87\,\mathrm{GHz}$ as a rounded input gives $\epsilon\approx1.582\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm J$. This calculation is a conversion of the reported [frequency](frequency.md), not a new measurement of the [ammonia inversion transition](ammonia-inversion-transition.md).
