# Quotient topology of a cofinite space

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

A [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) of a space carrying the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) has a [topology](topology-split.md) contained in the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) on its underlying [quotient set](quotient-set.md). If $q:X\to Y$ is the [quotient map](quotient-map.md) and $U$ is a nonempty [open subset](open-set.md) of $Y$, its [preimage](preimage.md) has finite complement. Since $q$ is [surjective](surjective-function.md), the complement of $U$ is the image of that finite complement, hence is finite. Equality need not hold: partition $\mathbb Z$ into its even and odd integers. Both classes are infinite, so their two-point [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) has the [indiscrete topology](indiscrete-topology.md), whereas the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) on a finite set is the [discrete topology](discrete-space.md).
