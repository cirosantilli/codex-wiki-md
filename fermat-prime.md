# Fermat prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime)

A Fermat prime is a [prime number](prime-number.md) of the form

$$
F_k=2^{2^k}+1.
$$

For $k\geq1$, it is congruent to $1$ modulo $4$ and to $2$ modulo $3$.
