# Fermat prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)

A [Fermat prime](fermat-prime.md) is a [Fermat number](fermat-number.md) that is also a [prime number](prime-number.md):

$$
F_k=2^{2^k}+1,\qquad k\geq0.
$$

The defining form alone does not imply [primality](primality.md). For example, $65537$ is a [Fermat prime](fermat-prime.md), while $4294967297$ is a [Fermat number](fermat-number.md) with proper [divisors](divisor.md). For $k\geq1$, every [Fermat prime](fermat-prime.md) is one modulo four and two modulo three. [Pépin's test](pepin-s-test.md) gives an exact [primality testing](primality-testing.md) criterion for these candidates, rather than relying on their form.
