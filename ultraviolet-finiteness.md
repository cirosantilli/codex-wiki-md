# Ultraviolet finiteness

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md)

An [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is [ultraviolet finite](ultraviolet-finiteness.md) if its regulated high-[loop momentum](loop-momentum.md) contribution has a finite regulator-removal limit after whatever lower-order subtractions are specified. A zero [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) does not force divergence: [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) can cancel the candidate local term. Conversely, superficial convergence alone does not exclude divergent subgraphs. Ultraviolet finiteness makes no claim about [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md) or physical threshold singularities.
