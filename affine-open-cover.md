# Affine open cover

↑ **Parent:** [Open cover](open-cover.md)

An affine open cover of a [scheme](scheme.md) is an [open cover](open-cover.md) by [affine open subsets](affine-open-subscheme.md). Every [scheme](scheme.md) has such a cover by definition. A [quasi-compact scheme](quasi-compact-scheme.md) has a finite affine open cover. Whether the intersections are affine is a further condition, supplied in particular by separatedness over an affine base.
