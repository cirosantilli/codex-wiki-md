# Compact-preimage theorem for closed maps

↑ **Parent:** [Closed map](closed-map.md)

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) that is a [closed map](closed-map.md) and whose fibres are [compact](compact-space.md). Then $f^{-1}(K)$ is [compact](compact-space.md) for every [compact set](compact-space.md) $K\subseteq Y$, without a [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) hypothesis. Given an [open cover](open-cover.md), cover each fibre by a finite union $V_y$ of its members. The set $W_y=Y\setminus f(X\setminus V_y)$ is open, contains $y$, and has $f^{-1}(W_y)\subseteq V_y$. A finite subcover of $K$ by the $W_y$ uses only finitely many original cover members and covers $f^{-1}(K)$. Empty fibres cause no exception.
