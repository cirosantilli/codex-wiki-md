The three [elementary row operations](../../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) on an $m\times n$ [real matrix](../../../../../../../real-matrix.md) are:

- Interchange two distinct rows: $R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$.
- Multiply one row by a nonzero [real number](../../../../../../../real-number.md): $R_i\leftarrow cR_i$, with $c\ne0$.
- Add a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of one row to a different row: $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$, with $i\ne j$.

Each operation changes whole rows, so it acts on every column by the same rule. Each can be undone by an [elementary row operation](../../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md).
