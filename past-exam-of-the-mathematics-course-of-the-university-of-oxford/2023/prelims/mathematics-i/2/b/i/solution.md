<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Use [basis extension](../../../../../../../basis-extension.md) to choose a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $(v,v_2,\ldots,v_n)$ of the [vector space](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ over the [finite field](../../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_p=\mathbb Z_p$. The condition $T(v)=0$ fixes the first value of a [linear functional](../../../../../../../linear-functional.md); its remaining $n-1$ values are arbitrary. There are $p^{n-1}$ such [linear maps](../../../../../../../linear-map.md), exactly one of which is zero. Hence the required number is

$$
\boxed{p^{n-1}-1.}
$$

For $n=1$ this is zero, as it should be: vanishing at a [basis vector](../../../../../../../basis-vector.md) then forces the entire [linear functional](../../../../../../../linear-functional.md) to vanish.
