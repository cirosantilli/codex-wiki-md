A [vector space](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over a [field](../../../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb F$ is a set $V$ with [vector addition](../../../../../../../vector-addition.md) $V\times V\to V$ and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) $\mathbb F\times V\to V$ satisfying the following [vector space axioms](../../../../../../../vector-space-axioms.md). Under [vector addition](../../../../../../../vector-addition.md), $V$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md): addition is associative and commutative, there is a [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md) $0_V$, and each $v$ has an additive inverse $-v$. For all $a,b\in\mathbb F$ and $u,v\in V$,

$$
a(u+v)=au+av,\qquad (a+b)v=av+bv,\qquad
(ab)v=a(bv),\qquad 1_{\mathbb F}v=v.
$$

The operations are defined for every indicated pair and take their values in $V$.
