↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

A map $T:V\to W$ between [vector spaces](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over the same [field](../../../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb F$ is a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) precisely when it preserves [vector addition](../../../../../../../vector-addition.md) and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md):

$$
T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v),\qquad T(av)=aT(v)
\quad(u,v\in V,\ a\in\mathbb F).
$$

Equivalently, $T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)$ for all $u,v\in V$ and $a,b\in\mathbb F$.
