<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Every $v\in V$ has a unique [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) expansion $v=\sum_{i=1}^n a_iv_i$. Define the [linear functional](../../../../../../../linear-functional.md)

$$
\boxed{T\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_iv_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^n a_i\mu_i.}
$$

Uniqueness of the [coordinates](../../../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) makes this definition well defined. The [coordinates](../../../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) of a sum or a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) are the corresponding sums or [scalar multiples](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of the [coordinates](../../../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md), so this formula defines a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md). It has $T(v_i)=\mu_i$. Conversely, any [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) with those values must satisfy the same formula by [linearity](../../../../../../../linearity.md), proving uniqueness.

If the [finite field](../../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F$ has $q$ elements, each of the $n$ values $\mu_i$ can be chosen independently in $q$ ways. The number of [linear maps](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) $V\to\mathbb F$ is therefore $\boxed{q^n}$, including the zero [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md).
