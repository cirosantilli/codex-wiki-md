<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Since $Ae_i=r_ie_i$, the [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $e_i$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) with nonzero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $r_i$. The assumption $r_i\ne0$ also makes $r\ne0$, so the [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) has [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $n-1$. Choose its [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $u_1,\ldots,u_{n-1}$.

The [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $e_i$ is outside this [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). Hence $(u_1,\ldots,u_{n-1},e_i)$ is a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^n$ consisting of [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md), with corresponding [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $0,\ldots,0,r_i$. In that [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md), $A$ is the [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{diag}(0,\ldots,0,r_i).}
$$

This proves that $A$ is a [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md), including the case $n=1$.
