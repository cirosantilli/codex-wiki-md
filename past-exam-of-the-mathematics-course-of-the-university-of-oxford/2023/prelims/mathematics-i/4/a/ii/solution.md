<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $A$ be a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). For every real [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $x$, the real [scalar](../../../../../../../scalar.md) $x^TAx$ equals its own [transpose](../../../../../../../transpose.md), but

$$
x^TAx=(x^TAx)^T=x^TA^Tx=-x^TAx.
$$

Hence $x^TAx=0$. If $Av=\lambda v$ is a real [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) equation with $v\ne0$, it follows that

$$
0=v^TAv=\lambda v^Tv,
$$

so $\lambda=0$. If $A$ is a [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over $\mathbb R$, it has a real [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md), all with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero. Thus $A$ vanishes on a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) and $\boxed{A=0_n}$.

The [field](../../../../../../../field.md) matters: a nonzero real [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) can be a [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over the [complex numbers](../../../../../../../complex-number.md), for example $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $i,-i$.
