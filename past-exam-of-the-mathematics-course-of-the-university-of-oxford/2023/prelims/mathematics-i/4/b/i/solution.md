The [matrix units](../../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) $E_{ij}$, $1\le i,j\le n$, form a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $M_n(\mathbb R)$: each [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) has the unique expression $A=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}E_{ij}$. There are $n^2$ of them, so

$$
\boxed{\dim M_n(\mathbb R)=n^2.}
$$
