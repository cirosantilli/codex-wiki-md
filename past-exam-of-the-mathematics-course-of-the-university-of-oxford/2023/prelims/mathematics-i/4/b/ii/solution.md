<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Take the [vector subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of [skew-symmetric matrices](../../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md),

$$
K=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb R):A^T=-A\}.
$$

It is closed under [vector addition](../../../../../../../vector-addition.md) and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md), and a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) is

$$
\{E_{ij}-E_{ji}:1\le i<j\le n\}.
$$

Indeed, its diagonal entries vanish, and its entries below the diagonal are forced by those above it. Hence $\boxed{\dim K=\binom n2}$. Part (a)(ii) proves that the only [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) in $K$ is $0_n$.
