The identity is the [identity matrix](../../../../../../../identity-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{e=h(0,0,0)=I_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Left or right [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) by it preserves every entry, as is also immediate from the product formula above.
