↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

If $G$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md), its elements commute, so for all $x,y\in G$,

$$
T(xy)=(xy)^3=xyxyxy=x^3y^3=T(x)T(y).
$$

Thus $\boxed{T\text{ is a group homomorphism}}$. Also $T(e)=e^3=e$, as required.
