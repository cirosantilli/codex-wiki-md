<h1 id="5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

If $T$ is a surjective [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), then $G=T(G)$, which is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md) by the preceding part.

If $T$ is an injective [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), then for all $a,b\in G$,

$$
T(ab)=T(a)T(b)=T(b)T(a)=T(ba),
$$

because $T(a),T(b)$ lie in the [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md) $T(G)$. Injectivity gives $ab=ba$. Hence $\boxed{G\text{ is abelian in either case}}$.
