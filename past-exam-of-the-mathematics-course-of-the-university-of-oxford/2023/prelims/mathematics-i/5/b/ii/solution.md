<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Use the [symmetric group](../../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_3$, composing [permutations](../../../../../../../permutation.md) from right to left. Set $x=(12)$ and $y=(23)$. Each is a [transposition](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md), so $x^3=x$ and $y^3=y$, whereas $xy=(123)$ has [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) three. Consequently

$$
T(xy)=(123)^3=e,\qquad T(x)T(y)=(12)(23)=(123)\ne e.
$$

Thus the cubing map on $S_3$ is not a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md).
