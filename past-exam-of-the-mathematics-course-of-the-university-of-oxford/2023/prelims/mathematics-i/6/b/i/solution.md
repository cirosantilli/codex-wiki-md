<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

For every $g\in G$, left multiplication gives a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md)

$$
H\longrightarrow gH,\qquad h\longmapsto gh,
$$

whose inverse is $x\mapsto g^{-1}x$. Hence every [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) has the same [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) as $H$. If $H$ is an [infinite set](../../../../../../../infinite-set.md), every [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) is infinite; if $H$ is a [finite set](../../../../../../../finite-set.md), every [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) is finite. These are the only possibilities.
