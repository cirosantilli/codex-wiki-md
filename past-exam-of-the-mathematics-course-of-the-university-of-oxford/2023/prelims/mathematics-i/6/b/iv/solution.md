<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

Define a map between sets of [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) by

$$
\Phi:G/(H\cap K)\longrightarrow(G/H)\times(G/K),
\qquad g(H\cap K)\longmapsto(gH,gK).
$$

It is well defined: replacing $g$ by $gu$ for $u\in H\cap K$ changes neither component. It is injective because

$$
gH=g'H\text{ and }gK=g'K
\iff g'^{-1}g\in H\cap K
\iff g(H\cap K)=g'(H\cap K).
$$

The [Cartesian product](../../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) on the right has at most $nm$ elements. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|G/(H\cap K)|\le|G/H|\,|G/K|\le nm.}
$$

This proves the basic bound for a [finite intersection of finite-index subgroups](../../../../../../../finite-intersection-of-finite-index-subgroups.md).
