<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Every [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) $kH$ with $k\in K$ is also a [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) of $H$ in $G$. Moreover equality of two such [cosets](../../../../../../../coset.md) means the same thing in $K$ and in $G$:

$$
kH=k'H\iff k'^{-1}k\in H.
$$

Thus the map from the set of [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) of $H$ in $K$ to the set of [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) of $H$ in $G$, taking $kH$ to itself, is injective. Consequently the [index of a subgroup](../../../../../../../index-of-a-subgroup.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{|K/H|\le|G/H|\le n.}
$$

These are sets of [cosets](../../../../../../../coset.md); no [normal subgroup](../../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) assumption is needed.
