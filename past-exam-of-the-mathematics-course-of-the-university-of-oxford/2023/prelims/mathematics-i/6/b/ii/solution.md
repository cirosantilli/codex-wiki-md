<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $H$ were finite, say $|H|=r$. There are at most $n$ [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md), each with $r$ elements by part (i), and they partition $G$. Hence $G$ would be a [finite set](../../../../../../../finite-set.md) with at most $nr$ elements. This contradicts the assumption that $G$ is an [infinite group](../../../../../../../infinite-group.md). Therefore $\boxed{H\text{ is infinite}}$.
