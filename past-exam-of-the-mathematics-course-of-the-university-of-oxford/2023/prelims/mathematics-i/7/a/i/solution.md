<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Conjugating a [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) simply relabels its entries. With [permutations](../../../../../../../permutation.md) composed from right to left,

$$
\rho(a_1\ a_2\ \cdots\ a_\ell)\rho^{-1}
=(\rho(a_1)\ \rho(a_2)\ \cdots\ \rho(a_\ell)).
$$

This follows by applying each side to $\rho(a_j)$ and to points outside the displayed [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md). Consequently [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) preserves the lengths in the [disjoint cycle decomposition](../../../../../../../disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md), including fixed points, and therefore preserves [cycle type](../../../../../../../cycle-type.md).

Conversely, suppose two [permutations](../../../../../../../permutation.md) have the same [cycle type](../../../../../../../cycle-type.md). Pair their [cycles](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) of equal length, including their one-cycles. Define a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) $\rho$ by mapping the entries of each [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) of the first [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md), in their cyclic order, to the entries of the paired [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) of the second. Because the [cycles](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) partition $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, these assignments combine into a [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md). The displayed identity then conjugates the first [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) to the second. Thus they are [conjugate group elements](../../../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md) exactly when their [cycle types](../../../../../../../cycle-type.md) agree.
