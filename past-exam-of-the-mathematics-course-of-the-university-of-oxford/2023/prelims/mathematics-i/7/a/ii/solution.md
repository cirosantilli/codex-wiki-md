<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let the lengths in the [disjoint cycle decomposition](../../../../../../../disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md) of $\tau$ be $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_s$, with fixed points contributing length one. Disjoint [cycles](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) commute, and $\tau^m=e$ if and only if every [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) raised to $m$ is the identity, equivalently $\ell_j$ divides $m$ for every $j$. Hence the [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(\tau)=\operatorname{lcm}(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_s).}
$$

In particular, the identity has order one.
