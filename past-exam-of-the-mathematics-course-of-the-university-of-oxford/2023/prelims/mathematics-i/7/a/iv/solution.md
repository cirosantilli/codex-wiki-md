<h1 id="7/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

For distinct $i,j\ne1$, direct [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) gives

$$
(ij)=(1i)(1j)(1i).
$$

Thus the given star [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) generate every [transposition](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md). Every [cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) can in turn be expressed as

$$
(a_1a_2\cdots a_\ell)
=(a_1a_\ell)(a_1a_{\ell-1})\cdots(a_1a_2).
$$

By the [disjoint cycle decomposition](../../../../../../../disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md), every [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) is a product of [cycles](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md), hence of [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md), hence of the given star [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle(12),(13),\ldots,(1n)\rangle=S_n.}
$$

For $n=1$, the empty [generating set of a group](../../../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md) generates the [trivial group](../../../../../../../trivial-group.md) $S_1$.
