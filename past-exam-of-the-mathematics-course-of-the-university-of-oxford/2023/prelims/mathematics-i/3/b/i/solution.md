<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Take the nonzero [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)^T$. Its $i$th image entry under [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) is the sum of row $i$:

$$
(A\mathbf1)_i=\sum_{j=1}^n A_{ij}=\lambda.
$$

Thus $A\mathbf1=\lambda\mathbf1$, exhibiting $\mathbf1$ as an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\boxed{\lambda}$.
