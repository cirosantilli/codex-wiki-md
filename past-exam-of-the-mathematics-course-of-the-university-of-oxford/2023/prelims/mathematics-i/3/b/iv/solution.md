↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

The same [matrix similarity](../../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) preserves being a [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md). If $S^{-1}(AB)S=D$ is a [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md), then

$$
(A^{-1}S)^{-1}(BA)(A^{-1}S)
=S^{-1}A(BA)A^{-1}S
=S^{-1}(AB)S=D.
$$

Therefore $BA$ is a [diagonalisable matrix](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) whenever $AB$ is, and the converse follows by interchanging $A,B$. Thus they are either both [diagonalisable matrices](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) or neither is. In fact the previous part shows that one invertible factor suffices.
