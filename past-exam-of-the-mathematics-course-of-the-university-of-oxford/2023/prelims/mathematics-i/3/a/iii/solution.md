<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

A [scalar](../../../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$ if and only if there is a nonzero [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $v$ with $Av=\lambda v$. By the preceding [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) criterion,

$$
Av=\lambda v\text{ for some }v\ne0
\iff (\lambda I_n-A)v=0\text{ for some }v\ne0
\iff\det(\lambda I_n-A)=0.
$$

The last condition is $\chi_A(\lambda)=0$, exactly the definition of a [polynomial root](../../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). For a [real matrix](../../../../../../../real-matrix.md), this proves the assertion for real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md); the same proof over the [complex numbers](../../../../../../../complex-number.md) gives the usual complex version.
