<h1 id="3/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [v](../v.md)

We have $D(I_n)=\det A$. Define a function of the columns of $B$ by

$$
F(B)=D(B)+(1-\det A)\det B.
$$

It is an [alternating multilinear map](../../../../../../../alternating-multilinear-map.md), because both summands are, and $F(I_n)=\det A+1-\det A=1$. Hence $F$ is a normalized [determinantal function](../../../../../../../determinantal-function.md). By the asserted uniqueness of the [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md), $F(B)=\det B$ for every $B$. Rearranging yields

$$
\boxed{\det(AB)=\det A\det B.}
$$

This argument also covers $\det A=0$ without dividing by it.
