↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

For the [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) $T_A:x\mapsto Ax$ on the [finite-dimensional vector space](../../../../../../../finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) $\mathbb R^n$, the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) implies that injectivity and surjectivity are equivalent. Consequently $T_A$ is invertible if and only if $\ker T_A=\{0\}$.

Using the permitted equivalence between invertibility and a nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md), we obtain

$$
\det A=0
\iff T_A\text{ is not invertible}
\iff\ker T_A\ne\{0\}
\iff \exists v\ne0:\ Av=0.
$$
