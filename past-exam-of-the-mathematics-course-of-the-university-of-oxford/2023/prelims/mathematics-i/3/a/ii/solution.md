↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

We use the [monic polynomial](../../../../../../../monic-polynomial.md) convention for the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of an $n\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md):

$$
\boxed{\chi_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A).}
$$

It has degree $n$, since the only contribution to the coefficient of $t^n$ in the [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) is the product of the $n$ diagonal terms $t$. The alternative convention $\det(A-tI_n)=(-1)^n\chi_A(t)$ has the same [polynomial roots](../../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md).
