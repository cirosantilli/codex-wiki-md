<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

Write $B=[b_1,\ldots,b_n]$. Since $AB=[Ab_1,\ldots,Ab_n]$ and $b\mapsto Ab$ is a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md), the [multilinearity](../../../../../../../multilinearity.md) of the [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) gives, for any column position $j$,

$$
D(b_1,\ldots,\alpha u+\beta w,\ldots,b_n)
=\alpha D(b_1,\ldots,u,\ldots,b_n)
+\beta D(b_1,\ldots,w,\ldots,b_n).
$$

If $b_i=b_j$ for $i\ne j$, the corresponding columns $Ab_i,Ab_j$ also agree, so $D(B)=0$. Thus $D$ is an [alternating multilinear map](../../../../../../../alternating-multilinear-map.md) of the columns of $B$, even when $A$ is not invertible.
