<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Inward [heat flux](../../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) at the left end points in the positive $x$ direction, so the [boundary conditions](../../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) are

$$
-kT_x(0,t)=Q,\qquad T(2\ell,t)=0.
$$

For uniform [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), the steady [heat equation](../../../../../../../heat-equation.md) gives $T_s''=0$. The [heat flux](../../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) is constant and equal to $Q$, so $T_s'=-Q/k$. Imposing the right-end temperature gives

$$
\boxed{T_s(x)=\frac Qk(2\ell-x).}
$$
