<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a continuous [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) along an oriented piecewise continuously differentiable [parametric curve](../../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) $\gamma:[a,b]\to\mathbb R^3$, define its [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) by

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_a^b\mathbf F(\gamma(t))\cdot\gamma'(t)\,dt,}
$$

adding the integrals over the smooth pieces when necessary. The value refers to the specified traversal: going around a curve twice counts twice.
