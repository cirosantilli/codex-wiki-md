<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take counterclockwise traversal as viewed from $z>0$. In the plane $z=0$, the [work](../../../../../../work.md) is the planar [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) of $P=2x-y$ and $Q=x+y$. [Green's theorem](../../../../../../green-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{W=\oint_C(P\,dx+Q\,dy)
=\iint_{x^2+y^2\leq9}(1-(-1))\,dx\,dy
=2\pi(3^2)=18\pi.}
$$

The $z$ component contributes nothing because $dz=0$. The question specifies one circuit without specifying its direction: clockwise traversal gives $\boxed{-18\pi}$.
