↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [parametric curve](../../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t^2,t^3)$, $0\leq t\leq1$, the [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) and the tangent vector are

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(9t^2,-14t^5,20t^7),\qquad
\mathbf r'(t)=(1,2t,3t^2).
$$

Consequently the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\int_0^1(9t^2-28t^6+60t^9)\,dt
=3-4+6=5.}
$$
