<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Parametrize the straight segment by $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,t)$, $0\leq t\leq1$. Then the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
&=\int_0^1\bigl(3t^2+6t-14t^2+20t^3\bigr)\,dt\\
&=3-\frac{11}{3}+5=\boxed{\frac{13}{3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The three different answers are possible because this [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) is not a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md); for example $\partial_yF_x=6$ whereas $\partial_xF_y=0$.
