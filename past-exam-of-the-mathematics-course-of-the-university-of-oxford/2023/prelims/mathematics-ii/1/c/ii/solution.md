<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [sine](../../../../../../../sine.md) has values $1,0,-1,0$ on the four residue classes of $n$ modulo four. For $n=4k+1$, the [exponential function](../../../../../../../exponential-function.md) gives $b_n=e^{1-1/n}\to e$; the other residue classes tend to $1,e^{-1},1$. All terms satisfy $b_n\leq e$, and the first class approaches $e$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\limsup_{n\to\infty}e^{(1-1/n)\sin(n\pi/2)}=e.}
$$
