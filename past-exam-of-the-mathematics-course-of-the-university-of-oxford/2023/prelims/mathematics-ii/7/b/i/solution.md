<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $f_n$ be the [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) triangular bump

$$
f_n(x)=\begin{cases}
 n^2x,&0\leq x\leq1/n,\\
 n(2-nx),&1/n\leq x\leq2/n,\\
 0,&2/n\leq x\leq1,
\end{cases}\qquad n\geq2,
$$

and set $f_1=0$. At $x=0$ every term is zero, and each fixed $x>0$ eventually lies outside the support, so $f_n\to0$ [pointwise](../../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md). The triangle has base $2/n$ and height $n$, hence

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1 f_n(x)\,dx=1\quad(n\geq2),\qquad \int_0^1 0\,dx=0.}
$$

This [concentrating triangular bump](../../../../../../../concentrating-triangular-bump.md) shows that [pointwise convergence](../../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md) does not preserve integrals, even for [continuous functions](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on a [compact interval](../../../../../../../compact-interval.md).
