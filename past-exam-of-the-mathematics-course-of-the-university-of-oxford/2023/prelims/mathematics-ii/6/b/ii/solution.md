<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every real $t$, $f(t)-t=1/(1+e^t)>0$, so **there is no [fixed point](../../../../../../../fixed-point.md)**. Direct [differentiation](../../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
f'(t)=1-\frac{e^t}{(1+e^t)^2}.
$$

Writing $u=e^t>0$, the inequality $(1+u)^2\geq4u$ shows

$$
\boxed{\frac34\leq f'(t)<1.}
$$

In particular $0<f'(t)<1$, but $f'(t)\to1$ as $t\to\pm\infty$. There is no common [contraction constant](../../../../../../../contraction-constant.md) smaller than one. Strict pointwise bounds on the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) therefore do not suffice for the [contraction mapping theorem](../../../../../../../contraction-mapping-theorem.md).
