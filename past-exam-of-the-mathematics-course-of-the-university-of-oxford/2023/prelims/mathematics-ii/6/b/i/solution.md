<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose distinct [fixed points](../../../../../../../fixed-point.md) $x<y$ existed. Then $h(t)=f(t)-t$ satisfies $h(x)=h(y)=0$. [Rolle's theorem](../../../../../../../rolle-theorem.md) supplies $c\in(x,y)$ with $h'(c)=0$, hence $f'(c)=1$, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus **there is at most one [fixed point](../../../../../../../fixed-point.md)**. The argument does not require $f'$ to be [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md).
