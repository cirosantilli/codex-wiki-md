The [mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) states that if $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on $[a,b]$ and [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(a,b)$, where $a<b$, there is $c\in(a,b)$ such that

$$
\boxed{f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a).}
$$

No [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) assumption on the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is needed.
