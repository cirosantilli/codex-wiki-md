<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of $h(x)=f(x)-\sin x$ vanishes. By the [constancy theorem](../../../../../../../constancy-theorem.md) on the [interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $I$,

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\sin x+C\quad(x\in I).}
$$

**This need not hold on a disconnected [domain](../../../../../../../domain-mathematical-analysis.md).** On $I=(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$, define $f(x)=\sin x$ for $x<0$ and $f(x)=\sin x+1$ for $x>0$. The [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) throughout $I$ with $f'(x)=\cos x$, but no single $C$ works on both components.
