↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../../../stirling-formula.md),

$$
(n!)^{1/n}\sim\frac ne(2\pi n)^{1/(2n)},\qquad
|a_n|^{1/n}=\frac{n^{1+1/n^2}}{(n!)^{1/n}}\longrightarrow e.
$$

Here $(2\pi n)^{1/(2n)}\to1$ and $n^{1/n^2}\to1$, as is seen by taking the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md). The [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](../../../../../../../cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) gives $\boxed{R=e^{-1}}$.
