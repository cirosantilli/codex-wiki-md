<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) to choose $N$ with $|f_n(x)-f_N(x)|\leq1$ for all $n\geq N$ and $x\in E$; this follows, for example, from the [Uniform Cauchy criterion](../../../../../../../uniform-cauchy-criterion.md). Since each $f_j$ is a [bounded function](../../../../../../../bounded-function.md), put $M_j=\sup_E|f_j|<\infty$ for $1\leq j\leq N$. Then

$$
|f_n(x)|\leq M:=1+\max_{1\leq j\leq N}M_j
$$

for every $n$ and $x$. Thus $(f_n)$ is a [uniformly bounded family of functions](../../../../../../../uniformly-bounded-family-of-functions.md). The finite initial segment is essential: [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) directly controls only a [tail of a sequence](../../../../../../../tail-of-a-sequence.md).
