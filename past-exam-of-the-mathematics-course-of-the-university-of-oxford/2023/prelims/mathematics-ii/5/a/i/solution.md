<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [sequence of functions](../../../../../../../sequence-of-functions.md) $(f_n)$ [converges uniformly](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) to $f$ on $E$ if

$$
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\ \forall n\geq N\ \forall x\in E:\quad |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.
$$

The same $N$ must work for every $x$. Equivalently, the [supremum norm](../../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) of $f_n-f$ tends to zero. This is stronger than [pointwise convergence](../../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md), where $N$ may depend on $x$.
