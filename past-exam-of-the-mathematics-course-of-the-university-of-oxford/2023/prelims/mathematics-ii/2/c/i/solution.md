↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [binomial theorem](../../../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) gives $\binom{2n}{n}\leq\sum_{k=0}^{2n}\binom{2n}{k}=4^n$. Therefore

$$
\left|\binom{2n}{n}3^{-2n}\sin n\right|\leq(4/9)^n.
$$

The [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) with ratio $4/9$ converges. By the [comparison test](../../../../../../../direct-comparison-test.md), the given [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) is **absolutely convergent**.
