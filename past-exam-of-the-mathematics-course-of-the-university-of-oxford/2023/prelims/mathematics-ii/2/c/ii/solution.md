<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $t\geq0$, $|\sin t|\leq t$, so

$$
0\leq\frac{n\sin^2(1/n)}{(\log n)^2}\leq\frac1{n(\log n)^2}.
$$

The [integral test](../../../../../../../integral-test-for-convergence.md) applies to the decreasing positive comparison function, and

$$
\int_2^\infty\frac{dx}{x(\log x)^2}=\frac1{\log2}<\infty.
$$

The [comparison test](../../../../../../../direct-comparison-test.md) proves that the [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) **converges**; since all terms are nonnegative, this is also [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md).
