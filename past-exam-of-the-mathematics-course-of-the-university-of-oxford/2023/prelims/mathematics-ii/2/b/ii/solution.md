<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The implication is false.** Set

$$
a_n=\frac1{n\log(n+1)}.
$$

Then $na_n=1/\log(n+1)\to0$. For $n\geq2$, $\log(n+1)\leq\log(2n)\leq2\log n$, so $a_n\geq1/(2n\log n)$. The [integral test](../../../../../../../integral-test-for-convergence.md) gives

$$
\int_2^R\frac{dx}{x\log x}=\log\log R-\log\log2\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

Thus the [comparison test](../../../../../../../direct-comparison-test.md) shows that $\sum a_n$ diverges.
