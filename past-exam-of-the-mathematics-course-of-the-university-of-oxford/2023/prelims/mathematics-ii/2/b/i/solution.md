↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The implication is true, with [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md).** Since $n^{3/2}a_n\to0$, there is $N$ such that $|a_n|\leq n^{-3/2}$ for $n\geq N$. The [p-series](../../../../../../../p-series.md) $\sum n^{-3/2}$ converges because its exponent exceeds one. The [comparison test](../../../../../../../direct-comparison-test.md) proves $\sum|a_n|<\infty$, and [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) implies convergence of $\sum a_n$.
