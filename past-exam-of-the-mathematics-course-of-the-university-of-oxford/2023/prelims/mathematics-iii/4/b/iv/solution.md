<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply the [law of total expectation](../../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) to the [partition of a sample space](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-sample-space.md) given by the possible values of $R_k$. Since the conditional mean is affine,

$$
\boxed{r_{k+1}=\mathbb E[\mathbb E[R_{k+1}\mid R_k]]=1+\left(1-\frac2n\right)r_k.}
$$

Here $r_0=n$. This uses [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and does not assert independence of successive urn counts.
