<h1 id="4/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The equilibrium of the [first-order linear recurrence](../../../../../../../first-order-linear-recurrence.md) is $n/2$. Subtracting it gives

$$
r_{k+1}-n/2=(1-2/n)(r_k-n/2).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{r_k=\frac n2+\frac n2\left(1-\frac2n\right)^k,\qquad k\geq0.}
$$

For $n=2$, interpret the formula at $k=0$ through the initial condition: $r_0=2$, while $r_k=1$ for $k\geq1$. For every $n\geq2$, $r_k\to n/2$.
