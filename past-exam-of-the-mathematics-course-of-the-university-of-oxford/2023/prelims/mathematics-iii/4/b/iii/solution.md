<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Only upward and downward moves change the value, so the [conditional expectation](../../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[R_{k+1}\mid R_k=r]=r+\frac{(n-r)^2-r^2}{n^2}
=1+\left(1-\frac2n\right)r.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\alpha=1,\quad\beta=1-2/n}$.
