The [joint probability mass function](../../../../../../../joint-probability-mass-function.md) is

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=\mathbb P(X=x,Y=y),\qquad x,y\in S.
$$

It is nonnegative and its sum over $S\times S$ is one. Summing over one coordinate gives the other [marginal probability mass function](../../../../../../../marginal-probability-mass-function.md).
