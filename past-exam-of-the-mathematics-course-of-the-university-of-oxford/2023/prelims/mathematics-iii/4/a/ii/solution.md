For $\mathbb P(X=x)>0$, the [conditional probability mass function](../../../../../../../conditional-probability-mass-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{p_{Y\mid X=x}(y)=\frac{\mathbb P(X=x,Y=y)}{\mathbb P(X=x)}.}
$$

The denominator is the sum of the numerator over all possible $y$, so these [conditional probabilities](../../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) sum to one.
