↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [principal component scores](../../../../../../../principal-component-score.md) are the projections of centered observations onto the loading directions:

$$
\boxed{Z=X_cV,\qquad Z_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^p(X_{ik}-\overline X_k)V_{kj}.}
$$

If the symbol $X$ already denotes centered data, this simplifies to $Z=XV$. The [principal component loadings](../../../../../../../principal-component-loading.md) are directions; the [principal component scores](../../../../../../../principal-component-score.md) are coordinates of individual observations in those directions.
