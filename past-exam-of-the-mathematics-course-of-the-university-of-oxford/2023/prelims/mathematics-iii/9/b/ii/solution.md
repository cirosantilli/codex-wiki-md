<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $X_c$ be the column-centered [data matrix](../../../../../../../data-matrix.md). Its sample [covariance matrix](../../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) is $S=X_c^TX_c/(n-1)$. The [loadings matrix](../../../../../../../loadings-matrix.md) $V=(v_1,\ldots,v_p)$ has orthonormal [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $S$ as columns, ordered by nonincreasing [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md):

$$
Sv_j=\lambda_jv_j,\qquad V^TV=I.
$$

Each [principal component loading](../../../../../../../principal-component-loading.md) specifies the coefficients of a principal direction in the original variables. Signs are arbitrary, and equal [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) permit orthogonal changes of basis within their [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md).
