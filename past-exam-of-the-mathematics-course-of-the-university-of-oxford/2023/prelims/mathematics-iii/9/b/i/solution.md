[Principal component analysis](../../../../../../../principal-component-analysis.md) finds orthogonal directions of greatest sample variation, ordered by the variance explained. It can summarize correlated variables, reveal lower-dimensional structure, and provide a reduced representation by retaining only the first few [principal components](../../../../../../../principal-component.md). Centering is standard; scaling may also be appropriate when the variables have incomparable units.
