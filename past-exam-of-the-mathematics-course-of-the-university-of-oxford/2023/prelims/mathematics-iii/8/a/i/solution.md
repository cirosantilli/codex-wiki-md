<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For known $\sigma^2>0$, maximizing the [normal likelihood](../../../../../../../normal-likelihood.md) over $\mu$ is equivalent to minimizing $\sum_i(X_i-\mu)^2$. The identity

$$
\sum_i(X_i-\mu)^2=\sum_i(X_i-\overline X)^2+n(\mu-\overline X)^2
$$

shows that the unique minimum occurs at $\boxed{\hat\mu=\overline X}$. Equivalently, the [log-likelihood](../../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) has second [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) $-n/\sigma^2<0$.
