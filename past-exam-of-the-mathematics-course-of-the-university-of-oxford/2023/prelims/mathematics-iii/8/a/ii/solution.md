<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [sample mean](../../../../../../../sample-mean.md) is exactly $N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$, so $(\overline X-\mu)/(\sigma/\sqrt n)$ has the [standard normal distribution](../../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md). The given [quantile](../../../../../../../quantile-function.md) gives probability $0.95$ to the interval $[-1.96,1.96]$. Inverting this [pivot](../../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) yields the exact $95\%$ [confidence interval](../../../../../../../confidence-interval.md)

$$
\boxed{\left[\overline X-1.96\frac\sigma{\sqrt n},\quad\overline X+1.96\frac\sigma{\sqrt n}\right].}
$$
