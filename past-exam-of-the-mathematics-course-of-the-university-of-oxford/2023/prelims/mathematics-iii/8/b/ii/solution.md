<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each residual has mean zero and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2(1-1/n)$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md), without needing independence of residuals,

$$
\mathbb E[S^2]=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E[D_i^2]
=\frac n{n-1}\sigma^2(1-1/n)=\boxed{\sigma^2}.
$$

Hence $S^2$ is an [unbiased estimator](../../../../../../../unbiased-estimator.md) of $\sigma^2$.
