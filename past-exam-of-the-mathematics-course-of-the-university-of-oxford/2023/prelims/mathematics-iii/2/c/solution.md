<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is $U(x,y)=x^3y+y^4$, since $\nabla U=(3x^2y,x^3+4y^3)$. The [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) therefore reduces the [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) to its endpoints. The curve starts at $(0,1)$ and ends at $(2,1)$, so

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=U(2,1)-U(0,1)=9-1=8.}
$$
