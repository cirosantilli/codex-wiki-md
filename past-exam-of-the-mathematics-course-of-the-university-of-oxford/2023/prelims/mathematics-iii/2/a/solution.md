<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives $u_s=2su_x+2tu_y$ and $u_t=-2tu_x+2su_y$. Therefore

$$
su_s-tu_t=2(s^2+t^2)u_x,
$$

so $\boxed{m=2}$. The $u_y$ terms cancel identically, independently of the particular [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $u$.
