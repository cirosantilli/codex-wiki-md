↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [tangent addition formula](../../../../../../../tangent-addition-formula.md) gives

$$
\tan(\arctan x+\arctan y)=\frac{x+y}{1-xy}=u.
$$

Hence $\boxed{f(v)=\tan v}$. The restriction $xy\ne1$ excludes the poles of [tangent](../../../../../../../tangent.md) that can arise in the possible range $-\pi<v<\pi$. One should not replace this identity by $v=\arctan u$ globally: the principal [inverse tangent](../../../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) can differ from $v$ by an integer multiple of $\pi$.
