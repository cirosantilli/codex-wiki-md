The [partial derivatives](../../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are

$$
u_x=\frac{1+y^2}{(1-xy)^2},\quad u_y=\frac{1+x^2}{(1-xy)^2},\quad
v_x=\frac1{1+x^2},\quad v_y=\frac1{1+y^2}.
$$

Thus the [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial(u,v)}{\partial(x,y)}=u_xv_y-u_yv_x=0.}
$$

This vanishing reflects the functional dependence found next.
