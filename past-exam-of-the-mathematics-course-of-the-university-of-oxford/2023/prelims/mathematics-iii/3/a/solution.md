↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Taylor expansions](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) $\sin t=t+O(t^3)$ and $e^t=1+t+t^2/2+O(t^3)$ give

$$
f(x,y)=-3+6x+3y+\frac12(x+2y)^2+O((|x|+|y|)^3).
$$

Hence the second-order [Taylor polynomial](../../../../../../taylor-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_2(x,y)=-3+6x+3y+\frac12x^2+2xy+2y^2.}
$$

The [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) contributes no quadratic term at zero.
