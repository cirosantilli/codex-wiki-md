<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the [constraint](../../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) $x^2-z^2=1$, substitute $x^2=1+z^2$ to obtain

$$
f=1+z^2+y^2+z=\left(z+\frac12\right)^2+y^2+\frac34.
$$

Therefore the two [global minima](../../../../../../../global-minimum.md) are

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=\left(\pm\frac{\sqrt5}{2},0,-\frac12\right),\qquad f_{\min}=\frac34.}
$$

Every pair $(y,z)$ is allowed on either smooth branch $x=\pm\sqrt{1+z^2}$, so these are also the only [local extrema](../../../../../../../local-extremum.md). There is **no [local maximum](../../../../../../../local-maximum.md) or [global maximum](../../../../../../../global-maximum.md)**: the displayed positive quadratic in $(y,z)$ has no other stationary point and is unbounded above. At the minima $F_z=-2z=1$, consistent with the hypothesis in part [i](../i.md).
