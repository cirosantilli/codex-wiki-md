<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take independent [Bernoulli random variables](../../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md) $X_i$ of success probability $r$, independently of $N$. On the event $N=n$, the [random sum](../../../../../../../random-sum.md) $\sum_{i=1}^N X_i$ has the [binomial distribution](../../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) with parameters $(n,r)$, including the empty sum zero at $n=0$. It therefore has exactly the specified joint distribution with $N$.

Thus one may realize $\boxed{Z=\sum_{i=1}^N X_i}$ on a suitable probability space. This is a distributional construction; it does not require a pre-existing $Z$ to be independent of $N$.
