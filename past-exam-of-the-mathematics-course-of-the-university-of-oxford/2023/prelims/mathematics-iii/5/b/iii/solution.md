<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $p_*=p/[p+(1-p)r]$. Then

$$
G_Z(s)=\frac{p_*}{1-(1-p_*)s}.
$$

Uniqueness of the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{Z\sim\operatorname{Geom}_0\!\left(\frac p{p+(1-p)r}\right).}
$$

This is [Bernoulli thinning of a geometric count](../../../../../../../geometric-count-under-bernoulli-thinning.md), using the convention of counting failures before success.
