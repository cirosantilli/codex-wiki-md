<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiate the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) inside its [radius of convergence](../../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md):

$$
G_N'(1)=\frac qp,\qquad G_N''(1)=\frac{2q^2}{p^2}.
$$

Since these are $\mathbb E[N]$ and $\mathbb E[N(N-1)]$, respectively,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[N]=\frac{1-p}{p},\qquad\operatorname{Var}(N)=G_N''(1)+G_N'(1)-G_N'(1)^2=\frac{1-p}{p^2}.}
$$
