↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Writing $q=1-p$, the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is a [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md):

$$
\boxed{G_N(s)=\mathbb E[s^N]=\sum_{n=0}^\infty p(qs)^n=\frac p{1-qs},\qquad |s|<q^{-1}.}
$$

The displayed restriction ensures [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md).
