↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With independent [Bernoulli trials](../../../../../../../bernoulli-trial.md) of success probability $p$, $N$ counts the failures before the first success. There are $n$ failures followed by one success with probability $(1-p)^np$, giving the stated [geometric distribution](../../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. It counts failures, rather than the total number of trials including the success.
