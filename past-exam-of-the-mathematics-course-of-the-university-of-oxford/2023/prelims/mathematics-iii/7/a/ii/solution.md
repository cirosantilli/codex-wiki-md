<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an [estimator](../../../../../../../estimator.md) $T$ of $\theta$, its [bias](../../../../../../../bias-of-an-estimator.md) and [mean squared error](../../../../../../../mean-squared-error.md) are

$$
\boxed{b_\theta(T)=\mathbb E_\theta[T]-\theta,\qquad
\operatorname{MSE}_\theta(T)=\mathbb E_\theta[(T-\theta)^2].}
$$

When the second moment exists, expanding around $\mathbb E_\theta[T]$ gives the [bias-variance decomposition](../../../../../../../bias-variance-decomposition-of-mean-squared-error.md) $\operatorname{MSE}_\theta(T)=\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)+b_\theta(T)^2$.
