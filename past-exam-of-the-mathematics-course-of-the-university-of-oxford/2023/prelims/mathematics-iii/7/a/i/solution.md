↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\theta$, the [change of variables](../../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $u=\theta x$ gives

$$
\mathbb E[X]=\frac1\theta\int_0^\infty ue^{-u}\,du=\frac1\theta,\qquad
\mathbb E[X^2]=\frac1{\theta^2}\int_0^\infty u^2e^{-u}\,du=\frac2{\theta^2}.
$$

The integrals are one and two by [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md). Hence $\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=1/\theta,\quad\operatorname{Var}(X)=1/\theta^2}$.
