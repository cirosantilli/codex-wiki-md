<h1 id="6/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

By part [iii](../../a/iii.md), $X$ is an [integer](../../../../../../../integer-split.md) with probability zero. Thus $\lceil X\rceil=\lfloor X\rfloor+1$ almost surely, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[\lceil X\rceil]=\zeta(t-1)+1.}
$$

The pointwise inequalities $\lfloor X\rfloor\leq X\leq\lceil X\rceil$ imply

$$
\zeta(t-1)\leq\frac{t-1}{t-2}\leq\zeta(t-1)+1.
$$

Putting $s=t-1>1$ and rearranging yields

$$
\boxed{\frac1{s-1}\leq\zeta(s)\leq\frac s{s-1}.}
$$

This extracts bounds for an infinite [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) from simple rounding inequalities.
