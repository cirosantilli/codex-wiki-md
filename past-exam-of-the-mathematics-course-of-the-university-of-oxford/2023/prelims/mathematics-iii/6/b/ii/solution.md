<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The condition $t>2$ guarantees a finite first moment. Direct integration gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=(t-1)\int_1^\infty x^{1-t}\,dx=\frac{t-1}{t-2}.}
$$
