<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an [integer](../../../../../../../integer-split.md) $k\geq1$, $\lfloor X\rfloor\geq k$ is the same event as $X\geq k$. Integration for the [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\lfloor X\rfloor\geq k)=k^{1-t},\qquad
\mathbb P(\lfloor X\rfloor=k)=k^{1-t}-(k+1)^{1-t}.}
$$

The second identity subtracts two successive tail probabilities, equivalently integrating over $[k,k+1)$. If $\mathbb N$ includes zero, the extra case is $\mathbb P(\lfloor X\rfloor\geq0)=1$ and $\mathbb P(\lfloor X\rfloor=0)=0$, since $X\geq1$ almost surely.
