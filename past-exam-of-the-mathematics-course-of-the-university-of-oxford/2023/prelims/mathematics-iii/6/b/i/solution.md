<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) requires

$$
1=c\int_1^\infty x^{-t}\,dx=\frac c{t-1}.
$$

Hence $\boxed{c=t-1}$. The distribution is a [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) with minimum one and shape parameter $t-1$.
