The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\mathbb P(X\leq x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f(t)\,dt.}
$$

The total integral of the nonnegative [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is one.
