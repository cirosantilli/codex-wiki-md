<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The initial transverse [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) gives $h=a(L/a)=L$. From $\dot r=-hu'$, the [initial conditions](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md) are $u(0)=1/a$ and $u'(0)=1/a$. Define

$$
\nu=\sqrt{1+L^{-2}}>1.
$$

The [Binet equation](../../../../../../../binet-equation.md) becomes the constant-coefficient [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

$$
u''+\nu^2u=\frac1{aL^2}.
$$

Its [particular solution](../../../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $(\nu^2-1)/(a\nu^2)$. Applying the two [initial conditions](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md) to the [complementary solution](../../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac1r=\frac{\nu^2-1+\cos(\nu\theta)+\nu\sin(\nu\theta)}{a\nu^2}.}
$$

The physical branch is the connected range containing $\theta=0$ on which the right-hand side is positive. A zero corresponds to escape to infinite [radius](../../../../../../../radius.md), rather than continuation to negative $r$.

The source calls $F$ an inward force magnitude, but here $F(r)=(r-a)/(ar^3)$ changes sign at $r=a$. The stated [initial condition](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md) has inward radial [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md), so the particle immediately enters $r<a$, where the [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) is outward. The consistent interpretation is that $F$ is the **signed inward radial component** of the [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md).
