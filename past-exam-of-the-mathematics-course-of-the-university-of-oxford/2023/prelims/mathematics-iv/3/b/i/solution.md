<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parametrize the [function graph](../../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) by $\mathbf R(x,y)=(x,y,f(x,y))$. Its two tangent [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) are $\mathbf R_x=(1,0,f_x)$ and $\mathbf R_y=(0,1,f_y)$. A small parameter [rectangle](../../../../../../../rectangle.md) maps, to first order, to a tangent [parallelogram](../../../../../../../parallelogram.md) with [area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md) $|\mathbf R_x\times\mathbf R_y|\,dx\,dy$. Since

$$
\mathbf R_x\times\mathbf R_y=(-f_x,-f_y,1),
$$

the [surface area of a graph](../../../../../../../surface-area-of-a-graph.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=\iint_S\sqrt{1+f_x^2+f_y^2}\,dx\,dy.}
$$

For example, $f\in C^1$ ensures a [regular surface](../../../../../../../smooth-surface.md) chart; more general domains can be handled by the corresponding convergent [improper integral](../../../../../../../improper-integral.md).
