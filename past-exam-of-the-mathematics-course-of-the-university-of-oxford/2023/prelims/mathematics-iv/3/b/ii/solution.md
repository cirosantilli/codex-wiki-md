↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) of the [surface of revolution](../../../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md) is

$$
\mathbf R(x,\phi)=(x,f(x)\cos\phi,f(x)\sin\phi),\qquad a<x<b,\quad0\leq\phi<2\pi.
$$

Its tangent [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) are orthogonal, with [norms](../../../../../../../norm.md) $\sqrt{1+f'(x)^2}$ and $|f(x)|$. Thus

$$
\boxed{A=2\pi\int_a^b|f(x)|\sqrt{1+f'(x)^2}\,dx.}
$$

The [absolute value](../../../../../../../absolute-value.md) is necessary if the [function graph](../../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) passes below the axis. The formula gives the curved [surface area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md); it adds no end discs. Zeros of $f$ can be treated by splitting the integral into regular portions and taking limits.
