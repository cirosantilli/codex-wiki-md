<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a piecewise [continuously differentiable](../../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) [parametric curve](../../../../../../../parametric-curve.md), the [arc length](../../../../../../../arc-length.md) is the integral of [speed](../../../../../../../speed.md):

$$
\boxed{s=\int_a^b\sqrt{[x'(t)]^2+[y'(t)]^2}\,dt.}
$$

The [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives the length of an infinitesimal displacement as $\sqrt{dx^2+dy^2}$. Integration counts traversal length, including repeated portions if the [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) retraces them. Piecewise continuous [derivatives](../../../../../../../derivative.md), or suitable [absolute continuity](../../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-function.md), justify the formula; mere differentiability as stated in the source is not by itself the usual sufficient regularity hypothesis.
