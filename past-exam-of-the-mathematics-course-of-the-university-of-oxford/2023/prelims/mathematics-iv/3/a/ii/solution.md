<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [polar coordinates](../../../../../../../polar-coordinates.md), $x=r(\theta)\cos\theta$ and $y=r(\theta)\sin\theta$. The [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
x'=r'\cos\theta-r\sin\theta,\qquad y'=r'\sin\theta+r\cos\theta.
$$

Squaring and adding cancels the mixed terms and gives $(x')^2+(y')^2=(r')^2+r^2$. Hence the [arc length](../../../../../../../arc-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{s=\int_{\theta_1}^{\theta_2}\sqrt{r(\theta)^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2}\,d\theta.}
$$
