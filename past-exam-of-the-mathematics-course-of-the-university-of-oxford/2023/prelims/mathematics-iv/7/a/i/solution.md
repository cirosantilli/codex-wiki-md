↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
497&=259+238,\\
259&=238+21,\\
238&=11\cdot21+7,\\
21&=3\cdot7.
\end{aligned}
$$

The last nonzero remainder is the [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md), so $\boxed{\gcd(259,497)=7}$.
