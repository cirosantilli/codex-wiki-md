<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathbf N$ be the [normal reaction](../../../../../../normal-force.md) of the stationary smooth [surface of revolution](../../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md). Its normal lies in the span of $\mathbf e_r,\mathbf e_z$, so it has no $\mathbf e_\theta$ component. The transverse component of [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md), $m\ddot{\mathbf r}=\mathbf N-mg\mathbf e_z$, is therefore

$$
2\dot r\dot\theta+r\ddot\theta=0,
$$

which gives $\boxed{h=r^2\dot\theta=\text{constant}}$.

The [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is tangent to the stationary [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md), so $\mathbf N\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}=0$. Take the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) of [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) with $\dot{\mathbf r}$:

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(\frac12m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2\right)=-mg\dot z.
$$

Using $z=f(r)$ proves [conservation of mechanical energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md):

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2+gf(r)=\text{constant}.}
$$

The ideal [constraint](../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) does no [work](../../../../../../work.md). These equations describe motion while the particle remains constrained to the [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md).
