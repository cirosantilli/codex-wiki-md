<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the rotated [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md)

$$
X=\frac{x+y}{\sqrt2},\qquad Y=\frac{y-x}{\sqrt2},\qquad Z=z.
$$

Their inverse is $x=(X-Y)/\sqrt2$, $y=(X+Y)/\sqrt2$. Consequently $x^2+y^2=X^2+Y^2$ and $xy=(X^2-Y^2)/2$, giving the [canonical form of a quadric](../../../../../../canonical-form-of-a-quadric.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(1+\frac a2\right)X^2+\left(1-\frac a2\right)Y^2+Z^2=1.}
$$

The [orthogonal diagonalization](../../../../../../orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md) identifies the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1+a/2,1-a/2,1$ of the [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) of the [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md).

For $|a|<2$, all three coefficients are positive: the surface is an [ellipsoid](../../../../../../ellipsoid.md) with semiaxes $(1+a/2)^{-1/2},(1-a/2)^{-1/2},1$. At $a=0$ it is a [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md).

For $a=2$, the equation is $2X^2+Z^2=1$, an [elliptic cylinder](../../../../../../elliptic-cylinder.md) parallel to the $Y$ axis. For $a=-2$, it is $2Y^2+Z^2=1$, an [elliptic cylinder](../../../../../../elliptic-cylinder.md) parallel to the $X$ axis. The absent coordinate is unrestricted; its disappearance does not turn the [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) into an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md).

For $a>2$, the negative coefficient is that of $Y^2$: this is a [one-sheet hyperboloid](../../../../../../one-sheet-hyperboloid.md) with axis $Y$. For $a<-2$, the negative coefficient is that of $X^2$: it is a [one-sheet hyperboloid](../../../../../../one-sheet-hyperboloid.md) with axis $X$. This gives the [signature transition from an ellipsoid to an elliptic cylinder and a one-sheet hyperboloid](../../../../../../signature-transition-from-an-ellipsoid-to-an-elliptic-cylinder-and-a-one-sheet-hyperboloid.md), including both transition values.
