<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $V=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)\ne0$. The [scalar triple product](../../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is invariant under a cyclic permutation, so the definitions of the [reciprocal basis](../../../../../../../reciprocal-basis.md) give

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a'\cdot\mathbf a=\frac VV=1,\qquad\mathbf b'\cdot\mathbf b=\frac VV=1,\qquad\mathbf c'\cdot\mathbf c=\frac VV=1.}
$$
