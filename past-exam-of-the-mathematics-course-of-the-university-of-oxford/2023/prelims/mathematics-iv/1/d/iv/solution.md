↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Since $V\ne0$, [iii](../iii.md) gives $\mathbf a'\cdot(\mathbf b'\times\mathbf c')=1/V\ne0$. The criterion in [a](../../a.md) therefore proves that **$\mathbf a',\mathbf b',\mathbf c'$ are non-coplanar**. In particular they form a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^3$.
