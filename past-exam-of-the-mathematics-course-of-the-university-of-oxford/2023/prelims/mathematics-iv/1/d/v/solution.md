<h1 id="1/d/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives

$$
\mathbf b'\times\mathbf c'
=\frac{(\mathbf c\times\mathbf a)\times(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)}{V^2}
=\frac{V\mathbf a}{V^2}=\frac{\mathbf a}{V}.
$$

Eliminate $V$ with [iii](../iii.md) to obtain the [reciprocal basis involution](../../../../../../../reciprocal-basis-involution.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a=\frac{\mathbf b'\times\mathbf c'}{\mathbf a'\cdot(\mathbf b'\times\mathbf c')}.}
$$

This formula actually holds without the unit-length assumptions. Under those assumptions it also says $|\mathbf b'\times\mathbf c'|=1/|V|$; normalizing this [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) alone would lose the sign of $V$ and could return $-\mathbf a$.
