↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c$ is perpendicular to both factors. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a'\cdot\mathbf b=\frac{(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)\cdot\mathbf b}{V}=0,\qquad\mathbf a'\cdot\mathbf c=\frac{(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)\cdot\mathbf c}{V}=0.}
$$

Cyclic permutations give all the off-diagonal [dot products](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) between the original [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) and its [reciprocal basis](../../../../../../../reciprocal-basis.md).
