<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\times$ for the paper's $\wedge$. In a right-handed [Cartesian coordinate system](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=(a_2b_3-a_3b_2,\ a_3b_1-a_1b_3,\ a_1b_2-a_2b_1).}
$$

It is perpendicular to both [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), has [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\sin\phi$, where $\phi$ is their [angle](../../../../../../angle.md), and has direction given by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) when nonzero. Its [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) is the [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) of the [parallelogram](../../../../../../parallelogram.md) spanned by the two [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md).

If $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c\ne0$, use that [parallelogram](../../../../../../parallelogram.md) as the base of the [parallelepiped](../../../../../../parallelepiped.md). Its perpendicular height is $|\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n|$, where $\mathbf n=(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)/|\mathbf b\times\mathbf c|$. Thus its [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal V=|\mathbf b\times\mathbf c|\,|\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n|=|\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)|.}
$$

If $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c=0$, both the [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) and this [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) vanish. If the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is nonzero, the [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) vanishes exactly when $\mathbf a$ lies in the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) spanned by $\mathbf b,\mathbf c$. If it is zero, $\mathbf b,\mathbf c$ are [linearly dependent](../../../../../../linear-dependence.md), and adjoining $\mathbf a$ still spans at most a [plane](../../../../../../plane.md). Consequently **the three [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are coplanar if and only if $\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)=0$**, including cases involving a zero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md).
