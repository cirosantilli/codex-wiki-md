<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The rod and disc contribute restoring [torques](../../../../../../../torque.md) $-mg(\ell/2)\sin\theta$ and $-Mgd\sin\theta$, respectively. Using [i](../i.md) in the [rotational equation of motion](../../../../../../../rotational-equation-of-motion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot\theta=-\frac g\ell\,
\frac{\frac12+\mu\delta}{\frac13+\mu(\delta^2+\alpha^2/2)}\sin\theta.}
$$

Only the disc's [center of mass](../../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) enters its gravitational [torque](../../../../../../../torque.md); its finite [radius](../../../../../../../radius.md) enters its [moment of inertia](../../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md). The formula is also regular at $M=m$, although the source happens to exclude that value.
