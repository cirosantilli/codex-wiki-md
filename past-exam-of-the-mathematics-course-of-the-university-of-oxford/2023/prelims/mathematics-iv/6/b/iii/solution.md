<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [small-angle approximation](../../../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md) $\sin\theta=\theta+O(\theta^3)$ reduces [ii](../ii.md) to $\ddot\theta+\omega^2\theta=0$, where

$$
\boxed{\omega=\sqrt{\frac g\ell\,
\frac{\frac12+\mu\delta}{\frac13+\mu(\delta^2+\alpha^2/2)}}.}
$$

The coefficient is positive, so the solution is [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), $\theta(t)=C\cos\omega t+D\sin\omega t$. Here $\omega$ is the [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md); the ordinary [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md) and [period](../../../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) are

$$
\boxed{f=\frac\omega{2\pi},\qquad T=\frac{2\pi}{\omega}.}
$$
