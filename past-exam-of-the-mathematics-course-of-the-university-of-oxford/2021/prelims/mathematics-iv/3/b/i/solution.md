<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Interpolate the [radius](../../../../../../../radius.md) and height by

$$
\rho(t)=a+(c-a)t,\qquad z(t)=b+(d-b)t,\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
$$

A [parametrized surface](../../../../../../../parametrized-surface.md) for the [conical frustum](../../../../../../../conical-frustum.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf R(t,\theta)=\bigl(\rho(t)\cos\theta,\rho(t)\sin\theta,b+(d-b)t\bigr),\quad 0\leq t\leq1,\quad0\leq\theta<2\pi.}
$$

The strict positivity of $a,c$ gives $\rho(t)>0$. Since $d>b$, the height identifies $t$ uniquely; the [angle](../../../../../../../angle.md) then identifies a [point](../../../../../../../point-geometry.md) of each [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) uniquely modulo $2\pi$. Hence this covers the lateral [surface of revolution](../../../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md) once. If $a=c$, it is the lateral surface of a [circular cylinder](../../../../../../../circular-cylinder.md).
