<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

For the [parametrized surface](../../../../../../../parametrized-surface.md) in [i](../i.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf R_t&=((c-a)\cos\theta,(c-a)\sin\theta,d-b),\\
\mathbf R_\theta&=(-\rho\sin\theta,\rho\cos\theta,0).
\end{aligned}
$$

These [tangent vectors](../../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), and their [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $\sqrt{(c-a)^2+(d-b)^2}$ and $\rho$, respectively. Their [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) therefore has [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\rho\sqrt{(c-a)^2+(d-b)^2}$. Integration gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma_1)
&=2\pi\sqrt{(c-a)^2+(d-b)^2}\int_0^1(a+(c-a)t)\,dt\\
&=\boxed{\pi(a+c)\sqrt{(a-c)^2+(b-d)^2}.}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the lateral [surface area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md) of a [conical frustum](../../../../../../../conical-frustum.md), with no end disks included.
