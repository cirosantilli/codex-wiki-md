<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [a](../a.md)

For a regular [parametrized surface](../../../../../../parametrized-surface.md) that covers its image once, up to sets of zero [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md), its [surface area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Area}(S)=\iint_U\left\|\frac{\partial\mathbf r}{\partial u}\times\frac{\partial\mathbf r}{\partial v}\right\|\,du\,dv.}
$$

The integrand is the [area element of a surface](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md), the [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) of the small [parallelogram](../../../../../../parallelogram.md) spanned by the two [tangent vectors](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md). A [parametrization](../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) that covers a region more than once counts that multiplicity; the geometric [surface area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) requires a single covering or a division into nonoverlapping patches.
