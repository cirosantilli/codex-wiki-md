<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [a](../a.md)

All time [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) in this part are taken relative to the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md), so its [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) is held fixed during [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md). Put $f(\phi)=p(\phi)\sin\phi$ and $q(\phi)=p(\phi)\cos\phi$. The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) give

$$
\boxed{\ddot{\mathbf r}=\ddot\phi(f'\mathbf e_1-q'\mathbf e_3)+\dot\phi^2(f''\mathbf e_1-q''\mathbf e_3).}
$$

Explicitly,

$$
\begin{aligned}
f'&=p'\sin\phi+p\cos\phi,&
f''&=p''\sin\phi+2p'\cos\phi-p\sin\phi,\\
q'&=p'\cos\phi-p\sin\phi,&
q''&=p''\cos\phi-2p'\sin\phi-p\cos\phi.
\end{aligned}
$$

The separate [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) and [centrifugal force](../../../../../../centrifugal-force.md) terms in the stated [equation](../../../../../../equation.md) account for the motion of the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) relative to an inertial observer.
