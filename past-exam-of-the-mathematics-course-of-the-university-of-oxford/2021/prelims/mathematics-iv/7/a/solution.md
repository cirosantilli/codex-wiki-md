<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [a](../a.md)

The [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) of $54$ and $21$ is $3$, which divides $906$. Divide the [linear Diophantine equation](../../../../../../linear-diophantine-equation.md) by $3$ to obtain

$$
18x+7y=302.
$$

Reducing modulo $7$ gives $4x\equiv1\pmod7$, so $x\equiv2\pmod7$. Substitution gives the complete family of [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) solutions

$$
\boxed{x=2+7t,\qquad y=38-18t,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Every such pair satisfies the [equation](../../../../../../equation.md), and every solution must have this residue for $x$, so none are omitted. Positivity requires $2+7t>0$ and $38-18t>0$. For [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $t$ these are exactly $0\leq t\leq2$. Thus the pairs with both entries positive are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(2,38),\ (9,20),\ (16,2).}
$$
