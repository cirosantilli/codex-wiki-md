<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [b](../b.md)

Eliminate $z$ using the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) [equation](../../../../../../equation.md). The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) consists exactly of the [points](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) satisfying

$$
x^2+xy+y^2+x+y=1,
$$

since each such $(x,y)$ lifts uniquely to $(x,y,1-x-y)$ on the intersection. Translating to the [center of an ellipse](../../../../../../center-of-an-ellipse.md) with $X=x+1/3$ and $Y=y+1/3$ gives

$$
X^2+XY+Y^2=\frac43.
$$

The [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) of this [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) is $M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3/2$ and $1/2$. Thus it is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md), and the projected curve is an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md). The [quadratic-form ellipse area](../../../../../../quadratic-form-ellipse-area.md), with the right-hand side scaled by $4/3$, is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A(C')=\frac{(4/3)\pi}{\sqrt{3/4}}=\frac{8\pi}{3\sqrt3}.}
$$

For example, the [orthogonal change of coordinates](../../../../../../orthogonal-change-of-coordinates.md) $u=(X+Y)/\sqrt2$, $v=(X-Y)/\sqrt2$ gives semiaxes $2\sqrt2/3$ and $2\sqrt6/3$, with the same product.
