<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [c](../c.md)

Write $z=x+iy$, with $y\ne0$ because the [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) is nondegenerate. Let $w=u+iv$ be its [circumcenter](../../../../../../circumcenter.md). Equality of the [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) values $|w|=|w-1|$ gives $u=1/2$. Equality of $|w|=|w-z|$, after expanding the squares, gives

$$
2\operatorname{Re}(w\bar z)=|z|^2,\qquad x+2vy=x^2+y^2.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) determine

$$
w=\frac12+i\frac{x^2+y^2-x}{2y}.
$$

Using the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $\bar z=x-iy$, we also have

$$
\frac{z(1-\bar z)}{z-\bar z}
=\frac{x-x^2-y^2+iy}{2iy}
=\frac12+i\frac{x^2+y^2-x}{2y}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{w=\frac{z(1-\bar z)}{z-\bar z}.}
$$

The nonzero [denominator](../../../../../../denominator.md) is exactly the nondegeneracy condition. If $z$ is real, the three distinct collinear [points](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) do not have a finite [circumcenter](../../../../../../circumcenter.md).
