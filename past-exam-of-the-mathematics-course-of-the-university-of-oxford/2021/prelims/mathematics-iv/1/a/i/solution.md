<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Write $\mathbf u=\mathbf b-\mathbf a$. Since the [triangle](../../../../../../../triangle.md) is nondegenerate, $\mathbf u\ne0$. Every [point](../../../../../../../point-geometry.md) on the [line](../../../../../../../straight-line.md) $AB$ has a unique [position vector](../../../../../../../position-vector.md) $\mathbf p=\mathbf a+t\mathbf u$. The condition that $CP$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $AB$ is the [dot product](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) [equation](../../../../../../../equation.md)

$$
(\mathbf c-\mathbf a-t\mathbf u)\cdot\mathbf u=0.
$$

Its [coefficient](../../../../../../../coefficient.md) of $t$ is $-\|\mathbf u\|^2\ne0$, so it has exactly one solution. The [foot of a perpendicular](../../../../../../../foot-of-a-perpendicular.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf p=\mathbf a+\frac{(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\cdot(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)}{\|\mathbf b-\mathbf a\|^2}(\mathbf b-\mathbf a).}
$$

This is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of $C$ onto the [line](../../../../../../../straight-line.md) $AB$, expressed using the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md).
