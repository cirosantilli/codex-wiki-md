<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

With $t$ as in [i](../i.md), the [foot of a perpendicular](../../../../../../../foot-of-a-perpendicular.md) lies strictly between the endpoints precisely when $0<t<1$. Since the [denominator](../../../../../../../denominator.md) is positive, this gives

$$
\boxed{0<(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\cdot(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)<\|\mathbf b-\mathbf a\|^2.}
$$

Equivalently, both [dot products](../../../../../../../dot-product.md)

$$
(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\cdot(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)>0,\qquad
(\mathbf c-\mathbf b)\cdot(\mathbf a-\mathbf b)>0
$$

are positive: the [angles](../../../../../../../angle.md) of the [triangle](../../../../../../../triangle.md) at $A$ and $B$ are both less than $\pi/2$. If “between” includes the endpoints, replace both strict [inequalities](../../../../../../../inequality-mathematics.md) by non-strict ones; equality gives $P=A$ or $P=B$.
