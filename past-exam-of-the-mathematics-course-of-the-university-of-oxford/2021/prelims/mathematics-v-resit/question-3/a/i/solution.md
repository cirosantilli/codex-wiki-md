<h1 id="question-3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Let $\Sigma$ be a compact, piecewise smooth [oriented surface](../../../../../../../oriented-surface.md) with piecewise smooth boundary, and let $\mathbf F$ be continuously differentiable on an open neighbourhood of $\Sigma$. The [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\partial\Sigma}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\iint_\Sigma(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS}.
$$

The [boundary orientation](../../../../../../../boundary-orientation.md) must agree with the chosen [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). For an upward [orientation](../../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) on a horizontal [disk](../../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md), its boundary is traversed counterclockwise when viewed from above. Reversing the [orientation](../../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) reverses both the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) and the [surface integral](../../../../../../../surface-integral.md).
