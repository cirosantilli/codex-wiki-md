<h1 id="question-3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Choose the [scalar field](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md)

$$
f(x,y,z)=\frac{x^2+y^2+z^2}{2},\qquad\nabla f=\mathbf r.
$$

The allowed [vector-valued Stokes identity](../../../../../../../vector-valued-stokes-identity.md) gives

$$
\oint_{\partial\Sigma}f\,d\mathbf r
=\iint_\Sigma\mathbf n\times\mathbf r\,dS
=-\iint_\Sigma\mathbf r\times d\mathbf S.
$$

Every boundary point lies on the unit [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) in the $xy$-plane, so $f=1/2$ throughout the boundary. Since the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) of $d\mathbf r$ around a closed [curve](../../../../../../../curve.md) is the zero displacement,

$$
\boxed{\iint_\Sigma\mathbf r\times d\mathbf S
=-\frac12\oint_{\partial\Sigma}d\mathbf r=\mathbf0}.
$$

Only the boundary value of this [scalar field](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md) mattered: the interior of the [surface](../../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) need not be part of a [sphere](../../../../../../../sphere.md). Either consistent choice of [orientation](../../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) gives the same zero answer.
