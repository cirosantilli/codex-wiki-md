<h1 id="question-2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

For each of the three constant Cartesian [unit vectors](../../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\mathbf e_j$, apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to the [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\phi\mathbf e_j$. Its [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) is $\partial_j\phi$, so

$$
\iiint_R\partial_j\phi\,dV
=\iint_{\partial R}\phi n_j\,dS
\qquad(j=1,2,3).
$$

These are precisely the three components of

$$
\boxed{\iiint_R\nabla\phi\,dV
=\iint_{\partial R}\phi\mathbf n\,dS
=\iint_{\partial R}\phi\,d\mathbf S}.
$$

Thus the [volume integral](../../../../../../../volume-integral.md) of the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) is the boundary [surface integral](../../../../../../../surface-integral.md) weighted by the [vector surface element](../../../../../../../vector-area-element.md) $d\mathbf S=\mathbf n\,dS$. The regularity needed here is that $\phi$ be continuously differentiable on a neighbourhood of the [closure](../../../../../../../closure-topology.md) of $R$.
