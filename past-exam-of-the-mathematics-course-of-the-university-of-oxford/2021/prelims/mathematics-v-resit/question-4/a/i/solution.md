<h1 id="question-4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [Fourier cosine series](../../../../../../../fourier-cosine-series.md) and [Fourier sine series](../../../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) use the respective forms

$$
C_f(x)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\cos(nx),\qquad
S_f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n\sin(nx).
$$

Their [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md), obtained from [Fourier orthogonality](../../../../../../../fourier-orthogonality.md), are

$$
\boxed{a_n=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi f(t)\cos(nt)\,dt\quad(n\geq0)},
\qquad
\boxed{b_n=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi f(t)\sin(nt)\,dt\quad(n\geq1)}.
$$

In particular the constant term is $a_0/2=\pi^{-1}\int_0^\pi f(t)\,dt$. The [Fourier cosine series](../../../../../../../fourier-cosine-series.md) is the [Fourier series](../../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) of the [even extension](../../../../../../../even-extension.md) $f(|x|)$, made $2\pi$-periodic. The [Fourier sine series](../../../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) is the [Fourier series](../../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) of the [odd extension](../../../../../../../odd-extension.md), equal to $f(x)$ for $0<x<\pi$ and $-f(-x)$ for $-\pi<x<0$, again made $2\pi$-periodic.
