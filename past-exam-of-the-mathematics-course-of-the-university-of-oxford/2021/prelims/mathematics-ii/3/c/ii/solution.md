<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Place the single term in $N_0$ first. For $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ in order, append $2^{n+1}$ copies of the pair

$$
\left(2^{-(n+1)},\ -2^{-(n+1)}\right).
$$

This uses all of $P_n$ and all of $N_{n+1}$ exactly once, so it is an explicit [rearrangement of a series](../../../../../../../rearrangement-of-a-series.md). Its beginning is

$$
-1,\quad \frac12,-\frac12,\frac12,-\frac12,\quad
\frac14,-\frac14,\frac14,-\frac14,\frac14,-\frac14,\frac14,-\frac14,\quad\ldots.
$$

Every completed pair leaves the [partial sum](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) at $-1$. Within a level-$n$ pair the [partial sum](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) differs from $-1$ by at most $2^{-(n+1)}$. As $n\to\infty$ this tends to zero, proving that the rearranged [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) has sum $\boxed{-1}$.
