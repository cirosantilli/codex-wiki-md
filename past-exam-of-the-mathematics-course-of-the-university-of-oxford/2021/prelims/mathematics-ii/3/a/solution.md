↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ **converges** if its [partial sums](../../../../../../partial-sum.md) $s_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n$ have a finite [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) $S$ as $N\to\infty$; its sum is then $S$. It has **[absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md)** if the [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$ converges, where $|a_n|$ is the [absolute value](../../../../../../absolute-value.md) of the term.
