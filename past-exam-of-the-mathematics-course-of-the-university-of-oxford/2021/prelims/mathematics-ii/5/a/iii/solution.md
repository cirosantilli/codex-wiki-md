<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An explicit [piecewise linear function](../../../../../../../piecewise-linear-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(t)=\begin{cases}
-3t,&0\leq t\leq1/3,\\
6t-3,&1/3\leq t\leq2/3,\\
3-3t,&2/3\leq t\leq1.
\end{cases}}
$$

The adjacent formulas agree at $1/3$ and $2/3$, so the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md), and $f(0)=f(1)=0$. Any pair of points at distance $2/3$ can be written $t,t+2/3$ with $0\leq t\leq1/3$. For every such $t$,

$$
f(t+2/3)-f(t)=\bigl(1-3t\bigr)-(-3t)=1.
$$

Thus their values are never equal. The [horizontal chord of reciprocal-integer length](../../../../../../../horizontal-chord-of-reciprocal-integer-length.md) conclusion does not extend to this distance.
