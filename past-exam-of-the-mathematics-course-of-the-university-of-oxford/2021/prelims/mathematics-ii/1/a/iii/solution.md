**False.** Take the finite [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) $A=B=\{-2,-1\}$. Their [suprema](../../../../../../../supremum.md) are both $-1$, whereas the [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) of pairwise products is $A\cdot B=\{1,2,4\}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\sup(A\cdot B)=4\ne1=(\sup A)(\sup B).}
$$

Negative factors can reverse an [inequality](../../../../../../../inequality-mathematics.md), which prevents the proposed argument with separate [suprema](../../../../../../../supremum.md).
