<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the symmetric [difference quotient](../../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) as the average of two ordinary [difference quotients](../../../../../../../difference-quotient.md):

$$
\frac{f(x+h)-f(x-h)}{2h}
=\frac12\left(\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h+\frac{f(x-h)-f(x)}{-h}\right).
$$

If $f$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $x$, both terms tend to the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) $f'(x)$. Hence the [symmetric derivative](../../../../../../../symmetric-derivative.md) satisfies $\boxed{f^s(x)=f'(x)}$.

The [absolute value function](../../../../../../../absolute-value.md) $f(x)=|x|$ is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md), and $f^s(0)=0$ because $|h|=|-h|$. Its right [one-sided derivative](../../../../../../../one-sided-derivative.md) at zero is $1$ and its left [one-sided derivative](../../../../../../../one-sided-derivative.md) is $-1$, so it is not [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at zero. Away from zero it is [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md), hence has a [symmetric derivative](../../../../../../../symmetric-derivative.md) there as well. This supplies an example meeting the hypotheses on, for example, $(-1,1)$.
