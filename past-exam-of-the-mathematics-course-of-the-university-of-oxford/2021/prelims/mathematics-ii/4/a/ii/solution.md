The **[mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md)** states: if a real-valued [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on a closed [interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[u,v]$, where $u<v$, and [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(u,v)$, then there is $c\in(u,v)$ with

$$
\boxed{f(v)-f(u)=f'(c)(v-u).}
$$
