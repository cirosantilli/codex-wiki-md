<h1 id="4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For any $u<v$ in the [interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), the [mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md), applicable by [differentiability implies continuity](../../../../../../../differentiability-implies-continuity.md), gives

$$
f(v)-f(u)=f'(c)(v-u)=0.
$$

All pairs of values are equal. Thus $\boxed{f\text{ is a constant function}}$, the [constancy theorem](../../../../../../../constancy-theorem.md).
