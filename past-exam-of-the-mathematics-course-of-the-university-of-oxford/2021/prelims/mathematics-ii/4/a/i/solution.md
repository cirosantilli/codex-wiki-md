↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R$ is **[differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(a,b)$** if, for every $x\in(a,b)$, the finite [limit of a function](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md)

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h}
$$

exists. Here $h\ne0$ and is sufficiently small that $x+h\in(a,b)$. This [limit of a function](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of $f$ at $x$.
