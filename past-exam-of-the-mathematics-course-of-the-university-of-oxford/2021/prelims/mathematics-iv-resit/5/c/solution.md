<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

All [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) on the first particle lie in the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) spanned by $\mathbf e_r$ and $\mathbf e_z$: this holds for [gravity](../../../../../../gravity.md), [tension](../../../../../../tension.md), and the [normal reaction](../../../../../../normal-force.md). Hence the azimuthal component of [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) vanishes:

$$
m_1(r_1\ddot\theta+2\dot r_1\dot\theta)=0.
$$

Using the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot h=\frac{d}{dt}(r_1^2\dot\theta)=r_1(r_1\ddot\theta+2\dot r_1\dot\theta)=0.}
$$

Strictly, $h$ is the axial [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md); the actual axial [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $m_1h$, which is conserved as well.

Differentiate the stated total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) using the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and then substitute the two [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot E&=m_1\dot{\mathbf r}_1\cdot\ddot{\mathbf r}_1+m_1g\dot z_1+m_2\dot{\mathbf r}_2\cdot\ddot{\mathbf r}_2+m_2g\dot z_2\\
&=\mathbf N\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_1+\mathbf T_1\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_1+\mathbf T_2\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

The [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) terms have canceled the [power](../../../../../../power.md) of [gravity](../../../../../../gravity.md). Also $\mathbf N$ is proportional to $\mathbf e_r+\mathbf e_z$, whose [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\dot{\mathbf r}_1$ is $\dot r_1+\dot z_1=0$. Thus the stationary [smooth mechanical constraint](../../../../../../smooth-mechanical-constraint.md) does no [work](../../../../../../work.md). The remaining [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) [powers](../../../../../../power.md) cancel by part (b), proving **$\dot E=0$ and conservation of total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md)** while the [massless inextensible string](../../../../../../massless-inextensible-string.md) remains taut and contact is maintained.
