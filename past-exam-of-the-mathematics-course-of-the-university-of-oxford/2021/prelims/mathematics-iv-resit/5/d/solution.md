<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Initially $r_1=a$ and $z_2=\sqrt2a-l$, so the total [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) is

$$
V_0=-m_1ga+m_2g(\sqrt2a-l).
$$

If the given [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is understood as a full tangential [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf v$, put $v_z=\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf e_z$. The [string length constraint](../../../../../../string-length-constraint.md) gives $\dot z_2(0)=-\sqrt2v_z$, and hence

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12m_1|\mathbf v|^2+m_2v_z^2-m_1ga+m_2g(\sqrt2a-l).}
$$

This formula accounts for both particles' [kinetic energies](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), including that of the hanging particle.

If $v$ denotes only the initial [speed](../../../../../../speed.md), the printed wording does not specify its direction within the [tangent plane](../../../../../../tangent-plane.md). To make that dependence explicit, let $\beta$ be the [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) from the downward generator of the [cone](../../../../../../cone-geometry.md) towards the azimuthal [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md), so

$$
\mathbf v=v\cos\beta\,\frac{\mathbf e_r-\mathbf e_z}{\sqrt2}+v\sin\beta\,\mathbf e_\theta.
$$

Then $v_z=-v\cos\beta/\sqrt2$, and

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12(m_1+m_2\cos^2\beta)v^2-m_1ga+m_2g(\sqrt2a-l).}
$$

For a launch along a generator, $\beta=0$ or $\pi$, this reduces to $E=\tfrac12(m_1+m_2)v^2-m_1ga+m_2g(\sqrt2a-l)$. For a horizontal azimuthal launch, $\beta=\pi/2$, it instead gives $E=\tfrac12m_1v^2-m_1ga+m_2g(\sqrt2a-l)$. **A scalar [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) alone does not determine a unique [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) here.** Both directions are tangent to the [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md); “along the surface” does not distinguish them. The assumed configuration also requires $l>\sqrt2a$ so the second particle hangs below the apex.
