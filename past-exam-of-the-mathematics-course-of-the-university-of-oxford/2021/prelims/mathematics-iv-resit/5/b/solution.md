<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [massless inextensible string](../../../../../../massless-inextensible-string.md) has a common [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) magnitude $T$. Since $\mathbf r_1=r_1(\mathbf e_r-\mathbf e_z)$, the [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) from the first particle towards the apex is $(-\mathbf e_r+\mathbf e_z)/\sqrt2$. The second particle lies below the apex, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf T_1=\frac{T}{\sqrt2}(-\mathbf e_r+\mathbf e_z),\qquad \mathbf T_2=T\mathbf e_z.}
$$

The [string length constraint](../../../../../../string-length-constraint.md) is

$$
l=\sqrt2r_1-z_2=-\sqrt2z_1-z_2,\qquad \dot r_1=-\dot z_1,\qquad \dot z_2=-\sqrt2\dot z_1.
$$

The [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) of the first particle in [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) is $\dot r_1\mathbf e_r+r_1\dot\theta\mathbf e_\theta+\dot z_1\mathbf e_z$. Therefore the [powers](../../../../../../power.md) delivered by the two [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) are

$$
\mathbf T_1\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_1=\frac T{\sqrt2}(-\dot r_1+\dot z_1)=\sqrt2T\dot z_1,\qquad \mathbf T_2\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_2=T\dot z_2=-\sqrt2T\dot z_1.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf T_1\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_1=-\mathbf T_2\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}_2.}
$$

The [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) can transfer [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) between the particles, but its total [power](../../../../../../power.md) on the pair is zero.
