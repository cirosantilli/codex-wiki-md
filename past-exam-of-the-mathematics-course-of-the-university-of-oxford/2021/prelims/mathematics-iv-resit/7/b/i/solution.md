The successive [division with remainder](../../../../../../../euclidean-division.md) steps of the [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
657&=2\cdot306+45,\\
306&=6\cdot45+36,\\
45&=1\cdot36+9,\\
36&=4\cdot9+0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each step preserves the [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) by part (a). The final nonzero [remainder](../../../../../../../remainder.md) is $9$, so

$$
\boxed{\gcd(306,657)=9.}
$$
