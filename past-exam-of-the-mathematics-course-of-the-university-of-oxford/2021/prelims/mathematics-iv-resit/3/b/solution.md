<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) is

$$
5x^2+6xy+5y^2=\begin{pmatrix}x&y\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}5&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2,8$, with respective [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,-1)^T,(1,1)^T$. Normalize them to obtain the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf e_1=\frac{\mathbf i-\mathbf j}{\sqrt2},\qquad \mathbf e_2=\frac{\mathbf i+\mathbf j}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The given [change of basis](../../../../../../change-of-basis.md) means

$$
x=\frac{X+Y}{\sqrt2},\qquad y=\frac{-X+Y}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Substitution, or [orthogonal diagonalization of a real symmetric matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md), gives

$$
2X^2+8Y^2=16,\qquad \boxed{\frac{X^2}{8}+\frac{Y^2}{2}=1.}
$$

Thus the [semi-major and semi-minor axes](../../../../../../semi-major-and-semi-minor-axes.md) have lengths $2\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt2$. The [eccentricity](../../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) and enclosed [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) are

$$
\boxed{e=\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\qquad A=4\pi.}
$$

The [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) [change of basis](../../../../../../change-of-basis.md) preserves [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md), so this is also the [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) in the original [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md).
