# Mathematics V

↑ **Parent:** [Prelims](split.md)

Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 279–284](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_prelims_and_part/response/2784209/attach/3/202409%20817%20annex.pdf#page=279).

Solutions to all six questions and all optional parts. The surface-[volume](../../../volume.md) formula in Question 2(b) requires an appropriate enclosing surface and orientation. The symmetry claim in Question 6(a), as printed, also needs the coefficient $b(x,t)$ to be even in $x$; a counterexample to the unrestricted claim is given.
