↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $f(t)=\sin2t$, the positive-$t$ branch is above the negative one, so the orientation in part [i](../i.md) is outward. Consequently

$$
\boxed{V=\pi\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\sin^22t\,dt=\frac{\pi^2}{2}.}
$$

The [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) follows from $\sin^22t=(1-\cos4t)/2$.
