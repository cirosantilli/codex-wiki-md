<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a bounded region $R\subseteq\mathbb R^3$ with piecewise smooth boundary and a [continuously differentiable vector field](../../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ on a neighborhood of its closure, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) says

$$
\boxed{\iiint_R\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=\iint_{\partial R}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS,}
$$

where $\mathbf n$ is the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md). The left side integrates [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) over the [volume](../../../../../../../volume.md); the right side is the outward [flux](../../../../../../../flux.md).
