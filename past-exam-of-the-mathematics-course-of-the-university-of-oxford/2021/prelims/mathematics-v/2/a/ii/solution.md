<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to $\mathbf F=u\nabla v-v\nabla u$. The [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=u\nabla^2v-v\nabla^2u,
$$

because the two [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) [inner products](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) cancel. Also $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n=u\partial_nv-v\partial_nu$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\iiint_R(u\nabla^2v-v\nabla^2u)\,dV
=\iint_{\partial R}(u\partial_nv-v\partial_nu)\,dS.}
$$

This is [Green's second identity](../../../../../../../green-second-identity.md), under the usual regularity assumptions permitting these derivatives and boundary [integrals](../../../../../../../integral-split.md).
