<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md) is $\nabla\times\mathbf F=(x,y,-2z)$. For the upward orientation of the paraboloid, the [vector surface area element](../../../../../../../vector-surface-area-element.md) is $(2x,2y,1)\,dx\,dy$. Therefore [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
&=\iint_{x,y\geq0,\ x^2+y^2\leq4}\{2x^2+2y^2-2(4-x^2-y^2)\}\,dx\,dy\\
&=\int_0^{\pi/2}\int_0^2(4r^2-8)r\,dr\,d\theta
=\boxed{0}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The upward normal gives the prescribed anticlockwise boundary orientation viewed from above.
