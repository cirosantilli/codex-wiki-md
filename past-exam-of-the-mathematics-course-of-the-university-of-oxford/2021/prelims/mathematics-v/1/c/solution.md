<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [parametrization](../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) of the cylindrical patch is

$$
\mathbf r(x,\theta)=(x,2\cos\theta,2\sin\theta),\qquad
0<\theta<\pi/2,\quad0<x<2\cos\theta.
$$

The last range uses the planes $x=0$ and $x=y$. The [surface area element](../../../../../../surface-area-element.md) is $|\mathbf r_x\times\mathbf r_\theta|\,dx\,d\theta=2\,dx\,d\theta$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A=\int_0^{\pi/2}\int_0^{2\cos\theta}2\,dx\,d\theta=4.}
$$

Including or excluding the boundary curves does not change the [surface area](../../../../../../surface-area.md).
