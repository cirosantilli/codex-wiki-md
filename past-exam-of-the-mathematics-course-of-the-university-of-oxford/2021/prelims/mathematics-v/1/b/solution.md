<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Green's theorem](../../../../../../green-theorem.md) for a positively oriented simple closed planar curve states

$$
\oint_C(P\,dx+Q\,dy)=\iint_D(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\,dy.
$$

Here $Q_x-P_y=1-(-1)=2$. The [trapezium](../../../../../../trapezoid.md) has parallel sides of lengths two and one, separated by height one, so its [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) is $3/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=2\cdot\frac32=3.}
$$

**Every congruent [trapezium](../../../../../../trapezoid.md) gives the same answer when its boundary is traversed anticlockwise**, since the scalar [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is constant and [geometric congruence](../../../../../../congruence-geometry.md) preserves [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md). Reversing the traversal would instead reverse the sign.
