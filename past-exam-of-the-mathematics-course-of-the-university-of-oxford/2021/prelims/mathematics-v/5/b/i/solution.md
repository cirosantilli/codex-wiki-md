↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives $\partial_x=\partial_\xi+\partial_\eta$ and $\partial_t=\alpha\partial_\xi+\beta\partial_\eta$. Substitution into the [differential equation](../../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) yields

$$
\boxed{(\alpha^2+2B\alpha+C)y_{\xi\xi}
+2\{\alpha\beta+B(\alpha+\beta)+C\}y_{\xi\eta}
+(\beta^2+2B\beta+C)y_{\eta\eta}=0.}
$$

The [coordinate transformation](../../../../../../../smooth-transition-map.md) is invertible exactly when $\alpha\ne\beta$.
