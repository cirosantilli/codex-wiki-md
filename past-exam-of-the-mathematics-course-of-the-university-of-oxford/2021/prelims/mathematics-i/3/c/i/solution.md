<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Take any finite selection of distinct indices $i_1,\ldots,i_r$ and a relation

$$
\alpha_1e^{(i_1)}+\cdots+\alpha_re^{(i_r)}=0.
$$

The coordinate at $i_j$ on the left is exactly $\alpha_j$, while the [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md) has coordinate zero. Hence $\alpha_j=0$ for each $j$. Every finite [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $E$ is therefore [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md), which is the definition of [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) for an infinite [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md). Thus **$E$ is [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md)** in the [vector space](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of [finitely supported sequences](../../../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md).
