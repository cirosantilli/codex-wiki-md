<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Every $v\in V$ is a [finitely supported sequence](../../../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md), so for a finite [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) of indices $S$,

$$
v=\sum_{i\in S}v_i e^{(i)}.
$$

Thus $E$ is a [spanning set](../../../../../../../spanning-set.md). If $F$ contained $v\notin E$, the displayed expression would give a nontrivial relation

$$
v-\sum_{i\in S}v_i e^{(i)}=0
$$

among the distinct [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) of the finite [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $\{v\}\cup\{e^{(i)}:i\in S\}$ of $F$: the [coefficient](../../../../../../../coefficient.md) of $v$ is $1$. This contradicts the [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) of $F$. It also covers $v=0$, whose singleton is not [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Hence **$F=E$**. In particular, $E$ is both a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) and a maximal [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $V$.
