<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $\sum_{i=1}^{k+1}\alpha_i u_i=0$. If $\alpha_{k+1}\ne0$, then

$$
u_{k+1}=-\sum_{i=1}^k\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_{k+1}}u_i\in\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k\rangle,
$$

contrary to the hypothesis. Hence $\alpha_{k+1}=0$. The assumed [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) of $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ then gives $\alpha_1=\cdots=\alpha_k=0$. Therefore **$u_1,\ldots,u_{k+1}$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md)**. Adding a [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) outside the previous [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) creates no nontrivial relation.
