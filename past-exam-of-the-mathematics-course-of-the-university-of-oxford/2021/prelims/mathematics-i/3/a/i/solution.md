<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Put $U=\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k\rangle$. Taking all [coefficients](../../../../../../../coefficient.md) zero puts the [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md) in $U$. If $s=\sum_i\alpha_i u_i$, $t=\sum_i\beta_i u_i$ lie in $U$ and $a,b\in\mathbb R$, then

$$
as+bt=\sum_{i=1}^k(a\alpha_i+b\beta_i)u_i\in U.
$$

Thus $U$ is closed under [vector addition](../../../../../../../vector-addition.md) and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md), contains the [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md), and is therefore **a [linear subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $V$**. This is precisely why the [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) is a [linear subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md).
