<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Write $v=\sum_{i=1}^{k+1}\alpha_i u_i$. Since $v\notin\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k\rangle$, necessarily $\alpha_{k+1}\ne0$. Rearranging gives

$$
u_{k+1}=\frac1{\alpha_{k+1}}v-\sum_{i=1}^k\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_{k+1}}u_i
\in\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k,v\rangle.
$$

All generators of $\langle u_1,\ldots,u_{k+1}\rangle$ therefore belong to $\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k,v\rangle$, proving one [set inclusion](../../../../../../../set-inclusion.md). Conversely, $u_1,\ldots,u_k,v$ all belong to the [linear subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $\langle u_1,\ldots,u_{k+1}\rangle$, proving the other [set inclusion](../../../../../../../set-inclusion.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\langle u_1,\ldots,u_k,v\rangle=\langle u_1,\ldots,u_{k+1}\rangle.}
$$

This [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) exchange does not require [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) of the original [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md).
