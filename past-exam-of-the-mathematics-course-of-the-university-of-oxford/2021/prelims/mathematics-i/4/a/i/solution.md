<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The hypothesis implies $v_1,v_2\ne0$, since the [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md) is a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of every [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md). Set

$$
t=\frac{\langle v_1,v_2\rangle}{\|v_2\|^2},\qquad w=v_1-tv_2.
$$

Since $v_1$ is not a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of $v_2$, $w\ne0$. [Positive definiteness](../../../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) and [bilinearity](../../../../../../../bilinearity.md) of the real [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) give

$$
0<\langle w,w\rangle
=\|v_1\|^2-2t\langle v_1,v_2\rangle+t^2\|v_2\|^2
=\|v_1\|^2-\frac{\langle v_1,v_2\rangle^2}{\|v_2\|^2}.
$$

Multiplying by $\|v_2\|^2>0$ and taking [square roots](../../../../../../../square-root.md) proves the strict [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md)

$$
|\langle v_1,v_2\rangle|<\|v_1\|\,\|v_2\|.
$$

Since every [real number](../../../../../../../real-number.md) $s$ satisfies $s\leq|s|$, this proves, in particular,

$$
\boxed{\langle v_1,v_2\rangle<\|v_1\|\,\|v_2\|.}
$$

The argument derives the required [inequality](../../../../../../../inequality-mathematics.md) directly from the [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) axioms.
