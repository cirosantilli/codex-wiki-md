<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

If every $\|v_i\|=1$, then each diagonal entry of the [Gram matrix](../../../../../../../gram-matrix.md) is $A_{ii}=\langle v_i,v_i\rangle=1$, so its [matrix trace](../../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is $n$. Using [iii](../iii.md) and the permitted [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md),

$$
0\leq\det A=\prod_{i=1}^n\lambda_i
\leq\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i}{n}\right)^n
=\left(\frac{\operatorname{tr}A}{n}\right)^n
=\boxed{1}.
$$

The bound is attained by any [orthonormal](../../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) family, whose [Gram matrix](../../../../../../../gram-matrix.md) is the [identity matrix](../../../../../../../identity-matrix.md).
