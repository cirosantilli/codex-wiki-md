↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

[Bilinearity](../../../../../../../bilinearity.md) of the real [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives, for the specified [linear combinations](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle u,v\rangle
&=\left\langle\sum_{i=1}^n x_iv_i,\sum_{j=1}^n y_jv_j\right\rangle\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n x_i y_j\langle v_i,v_j\rangle
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n x_iA_{ij}y_j
=\boxed{x^TAy}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The last equality is the entrywise rule for [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md), with $x^T$ the [transpose](../../../../../../../transpose.md) of the column [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $x$.
