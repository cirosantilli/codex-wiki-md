<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Write $g\cdot x=gxg^{-1}$. If $e$ is the [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) of $G$, then $e\cdot x=exe^{-1}=x$. Also, for $g,h,x\in G$,

$$
g\cdot(h\cdot x)=g(hxh^{-1})g^{-1}
=(gh)x(h^{-1}g^{-1})=(gh)x(gh)^{-1}
=(gh)\cdot x.
$$

The middle equality uses [associativity](../../../../../../../associative-property.md) and the [inverse element](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md) rule $(gh)^{-1}=h^{-1}g^{-1}$. Thus **this is a left [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md)**, the [conjugation action](../../../../../../../conjugation-action.md) of $G$ on itself.
