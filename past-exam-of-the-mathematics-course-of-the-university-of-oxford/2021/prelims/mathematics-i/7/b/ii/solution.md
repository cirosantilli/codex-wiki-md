<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Suppose the [quotient group](../../../../../../../quotient-group.md) $G/A$ is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md), generated by $gA$. For any $x,y\in G$, there are [integers](../../../../../../../integer-split.md) $r,s$ and $a,b\in A$ such that

$$
x=g^ra,\qquad y=g^sb.
$$

Since $a,b$ lie in the [center of a group](../../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md), they [commute](../../../../../../../commuting-elements.md) with every element, in particular with each other and with the [integer powers of a group element](../../../../../../../integer-power-of-a-group-element.md) $g$. Therefore

$$
xy=g^rag^sb=g^{r+s}ab=g^{s+r}ba=g^sbg^ra=yx.
$$

As $x,y$ were arbitrary, **$G$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md)**. This is the [cyclic quotient by the center](../../../../../../../cyclic-quotient-by-the-center.md).
