<h1 id="7/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

If $G$ is not [Abelian](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md), the contrapositive of [ii](../ii.md) says that $G/A$ is not a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md). The [quotient group](../../../../../../../quotient-group.md) is finite because $G$ is finite. By [iii](../iii.md), every [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) with fewer than four elements is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{|G/A|\geq4.}
$$

Equivalently, the [center of a group](../../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) satisfies $|A|\leq|G|/4$, by the [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md) count in [iii](../../../6/a/iii.md).
