<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The specified [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) $A$ is the [center of a group](../../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) $Z(G)$. It contains $e$. If $a,b\in A$ and $y\in G$, then

$$
(ab)y=a(by)=a(yb)=(ay)b=y(ab),
$$

so $ab\in A$. From $ay=ya$, multiplying on the left and right by $a^{-1}$ gives $ya^{-1}=a^{-1}y$, so $a^{-1}\in A$. Hence $A$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md).

For $g\in G$ and $a\in A$, the defining [commutativity](../../../../../../../commutativity.md) gives $gag^{-1}=agg^{-1}=a$. Thus the image of each element of $A$ under [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) is in $A$, and indeed $gAg^{-1}=A$. Consequently **$A=Z(G)$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of $G$**.
