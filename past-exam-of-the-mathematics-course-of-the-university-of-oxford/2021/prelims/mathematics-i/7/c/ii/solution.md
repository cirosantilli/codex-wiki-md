<h1 id="7/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

For a [nonabelian group](../../../../../../../non-abelian-group.md), [iv](../../b/iv.md) and [iii](../../../6/a/iii.md) imply $|G|=|G/A|\,|A|\geq4|A|$. Substituting $|A|\leq|G|/4$ into [i](../i.md) yields

$$
\boxed{m(G)\leq\frac12\left(|G|+\frac{|G|}{4}\right)=\frac58|G|.}
$$

The constant is sharp: the [quaternion group](../../../../../../../quaternion-group.md) has eight elements and the five [conjugacy classes](../../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) $\{1\}$, $\{-1\}$, $\{i,-i\}$, $\{j,-j\}$, $\{k,-k\}$. To verify this directly, $\pm1$ are [central elements](../../../../../../../central-element-of-a-group.md), [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) by $j$ sends $i$ to $-i$, and similarly for $j,k$; its relations $i^2=j^2=k^2=-1$ and $ij=k=-ji$, $jk=i=-kj$, $ki=j=-ik$ show that conjugating $i,j,k$ by any of $\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k$ changes only its sign. Hence $m(G)=5=(5/8)|G|$.
