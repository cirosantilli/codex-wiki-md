<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Suppose $y*y=y$. Choose $z$ with $y*z=e$, using the given right [inverse element](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md) property. Multiply the idempotent relation on the right by $z$. By [associativity](../../../../../../../associative-property.md) and the given right [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) property,

$$
e=y*z=(y*y)*z=y*(y*z)=y*e=y.
$$

Thus **the only [idempotent element of a semigroup](../../../../../../../idempotent-element-of-a-semigroup.md) is $e$**. This argument uses no cancellation and does not assume a left [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md).
