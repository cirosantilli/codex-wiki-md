<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

For any $x$, choose $z$ with $x*z=e$. By [ii](../ii.md), $z*x=e$ as well. [Associativity](../../../../../../../associative-property.md) and the right [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) property now give

$$
e*x=(x*z)*x=x*(z*x)=x*e=x.
$$

Thus $e$ is also a left [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md). The given [binary operation](../../../../../../../binary-operation.md) is closed on $X$ and [associative](../../../../../../../associative-property.md), $e$ is a two-sided [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md), and each $x$ has the two-sided [inverse element](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md) $z$. All [group axioms](../../../../../../../group-axioms.md) hold, so **$(X,*)$ is a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md)**. This is the [one-sided group axioms criterion](../../../../../../../one-sided-group-axioms-criterion.md).
