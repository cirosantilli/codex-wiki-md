<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $x*z=e$. Using only [associativity](../../../../../../../associative-property.md) and the given right [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) property,

$$
(z*x)*(z*x)=(z*(x*z))*x=(z*e)*x=z*x.
$$

Thus $z*x$ is an [idempotent element of a semigroup](../../../../../../../idempotent-element-of-a-semigroup.md). By [i](../i.md) it equals $e$, proving

$$
\boxed{z*x=e.}
$$

No left [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) or cancellation law is needed for this deduction.
