<h1 id="5/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

The shift $s:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$, $s(n)=n+1$, is [injective](../../../../../../../injective-function.md), so $s\in I(\mathbb N)$. It is not [surjective](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md): the least [natural number](../../../../../../../natural-number.md) is missing from its [image of a function](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md), whether $\mathbb N$ starts at zero or one. For every $t:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$, the [image of a function](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $s\circ t$ is contained in the [image of a function](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $s$, so $s\circ t\ne\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb N}$.

The [identity function](../../../../../../../identity-function.md) belongs to $I(\mathbb N)$ and is its [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md) under [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md); any [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) structure using that operation would have this same [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md). Since $s$ has no right [inverse element](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md), **$I(\mathbb N)$ is not a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) under [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md)**. It is a [monoid](../../../../../../../monoid.md), since [injectivity](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) is preserved by [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md).
