<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md) of two [bijections](../../../../../../../bijection.md) $f,g:X\to X$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), with [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $(f\circ g)^{-1}=g^{-1}\circ f^{-1}$. Thus [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md) is a [binary operation](../../../../../../../binary-operation.md) on $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$. It is [associative](../../../../../../../associative-property.md), since both $(f\circ g)\circ h$ and $f\circ(g\circ h)$ send $x$ to $f(g(h(x)))$.

The [identity function](../../../../../../../identity-function.md) $\operatorname{id}_X$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) and satisfies $f\circ\operatorname{id}_X=\operatorname{id}_X\circ f=f$. Every [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) $f$ has an [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $f^{-1}:X\to X$, itself a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), satisfying $f\circ f^{-1}=f^{-1}\circ f=\operatorname{id}_X$. These verify all [group axioms](../../../../../../../group-axioms.md), so **$\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ is a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md)**, the [symmetric group](../../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) of $X$.
