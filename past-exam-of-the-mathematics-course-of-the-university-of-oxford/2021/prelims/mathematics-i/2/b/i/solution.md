<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [monomials](../../../../../../../monomial.md) $1,x,\ldots,x^n$ form a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $V_n$: they [span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) every [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$, and equality of [polynomials](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) forces equality of every coefficient, proving [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}V_n=n+1.}
$$

The [zero polynomial](../../../../../../../zero-polynomial.md) is included in $V_n$ even though its [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) need not be assigned an ordinary nonnegative value.
