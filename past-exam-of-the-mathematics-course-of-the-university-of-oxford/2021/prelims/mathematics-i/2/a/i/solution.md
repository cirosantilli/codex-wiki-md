The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) for a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) $T:V\to W$, with $V$ a [finite-dimensional vector space](../../../../../../../finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) over a [field](../../../../../../../field.md) $F$, is

$$
\boxed{\dim_F V=\dim_F\ker T+\dim_F\operatorname{im}T.}
$$

Here the [nullity](../../../../../../../nullity-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), and the [rank of a linear map](../../../../../../../rank-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md).
