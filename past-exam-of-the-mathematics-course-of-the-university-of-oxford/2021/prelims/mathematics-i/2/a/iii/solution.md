<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Over the [finite field](../../../../../../../finite-field.md) $F=\mathbb Z_p$, a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of the [hyperplane](../../../../../../../hyperplane.md) has $n-1$ [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) by [ii](../ii.md). Each [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) on the [hyperplane](../../../../../../../hyperplane.md) has a unique expression as a [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of that [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md), and each [coefficient](../../../../../../../coefficient.md) has $p$ possibilities. Thus

$$
\boxed{|H|=p^{\,n-1}.}
$$

Equivalently, choose freely all coordinates except $x_j$, where $a_j\ne0$, and solve uniquely for $x_j$. For $n=1$ this counts just the [zero vector](../../../../../../../zero-vector.md), consistently with $p^0=1$.
