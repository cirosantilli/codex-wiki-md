<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Define the [linear functional](../../../../../../../linear-functional.md) $L:F^n\to F$ by $L(x)=\sum_{i=1}^n a_ix_i$. The given [hyperplane](../../../../../../../hyperplane.md) is $H=\ker L$. Choose $j$ with $a_j\ne0$. For any $t\in F$, the [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) whose only nonzero coordinate is $x_j=t/a_j$ satisfies $L(x)=t$. Hence $L$ is [surjective](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md) and has [rank of a linear map](../../../../../../../rank-of-a-linear-map.md) equal to $\dim_F F=1$.

Applying the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) to this particular [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dim_F H=n-1.}
$$

The nonzero-[coefficient](../../../../../../../coefficient.md) hypothesis is essential: if all $a_i$ were zero, the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) would instead be all of $F^n$.
