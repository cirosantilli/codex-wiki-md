[Matrix similarity](../../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) transports [commuting operators](../../../../../../../commuting-operators.md): with $M=PDP^{-1}$,

$$
CM=MC\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(P^{-1}CP)D=D(P^{-1}CP).
$$

By [ii](../ii.md), all such [matrices](../../../../../../../matrix.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{C=P\operatorname{diag}(a,b,c)P^{-1}\quad(a,b,c\in\mathbb R).}
$$

For completeness, [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
C=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}
a+c&-a+c&a-c\\
-b+c&b+c&b-c\\
a-b&-a+b&a+b
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The parameterization is [injective](../../../../../../../injective-function.md), since [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) by $P^{-1}$ and $P$ recovers the parameter [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md). Thus there are **infinitely many** choices. Already the distinct [scalar matrices](../../../../../../../scalar-matrix.md) $tI_3$, $t\in\mathbb R$, give an infinite family.
