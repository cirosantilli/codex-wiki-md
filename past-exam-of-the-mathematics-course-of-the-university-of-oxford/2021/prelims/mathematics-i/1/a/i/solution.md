↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Write $A=(a_{ij})$ as an $m\times r$ [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) and $B$ as an $r\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md), with rows $b_1,\ldots,b_r$. The rule for [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{row}_i(AB)=\sum_{j=1}^r a_{ij}b_j.
$$

Every row of $AB$ is therefore a [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of rows of $B$. Taking [linear spans](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rowspace}(AB)\subseteq\operatorname{rowspace}(B).}
$$
