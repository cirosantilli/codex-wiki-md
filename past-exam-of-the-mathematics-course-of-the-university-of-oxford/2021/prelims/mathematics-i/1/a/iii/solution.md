<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

For any [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $Y$ and compatible [invertible matrix](../../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) $P$, the [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) $r\mapsto rP$ maps $\operatorname{rowspace}(Y)$ onto $\operatorname{rowspace}(YP)$: a [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of rows of $Y$ is sent to the same [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of rows of $YP$. Its [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md) is $s\mapsto sP^{-1}$, so these [row spaces](../../../../../../../row-space.md) have equal [dimensions](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Hence right [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) by $P$ also preserves [row rank](../../../../../../../row-rank.md).

If $X=P^{-1}YP$, [ii](../ii.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rowrank}X=\operatorname{rowrank}(YP)=\operatorname{rowrank}Y.}
$$

This proves the required [matrix similarity](../../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) invariant without needing to assume equality of [row rank](../../../../../../../row-rank.md) and [column rank](../../../../../../../column-rank.md).
