<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

When $A$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), $B=A^{-1}(AB)$. Apply [i](../i.md) first to $AB$ and then to $A^{-1}(AB)$:

$$
\operatorname{rowspace}(AB)\subseteq\operatorname{rowspace}(B)
=\operatorname{rowspace}(A^{-1}AB)\subseteq\operatorname{rowspace}(AB).
$$

Thus **the two [row spaces](../../../../../../../row-space.md) are equal**. In particular, invertible left [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) preserves [row rank](../../../../../../../row-rank.md).
