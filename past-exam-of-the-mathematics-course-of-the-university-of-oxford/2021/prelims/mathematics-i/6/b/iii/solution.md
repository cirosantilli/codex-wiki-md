<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

If $G=\{e\}$, choose ordinary [addition](../../../../../../../addition.md) on $\mathbb Z$, giving the [additive group of integers](../../../../../../../additive-group-of-integers.md). Every power [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is the unique [identity function](../../../../../../../identity-function.md) on the [singleton](../../../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) $G$, so the [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md) axioms hold:

$$
\rho(0,e)=e,\qquad
\rho(z+w,e)=e=\rho(z,\rho(w,e)).
$$

Thus **ordinary [addition](../../../../../../../addition.md) supplies the required [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) operation on $\mathbb Z$**. Together with [ii](../ii.md), existence is equivalent to $G$ being the [trivial group](../../../../../../../trivial-group.md).
