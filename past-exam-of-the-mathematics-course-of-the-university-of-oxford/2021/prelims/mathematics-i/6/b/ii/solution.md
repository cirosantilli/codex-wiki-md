<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Suppose an arbitrary [binary operation](../../../../../../../binary-operation.md) $\star$ makes $\mathbb Z$ a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) and makes $\rho$ a left [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md) on $G$. For any [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md), each acting element induces a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md): the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) induced by its [inverse element](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md) is its [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md).

In particular, the acting element whose underlying integer is $0$ must induce a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) $G\to G$. But its power [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is

$$
x\longmapsto\rho(0,x)=x^0=e,
$$

a constant [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md). This is [injective](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) only when all elements of $G$ equal $e$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G=\{e\}.}
$$

This proves the [power-map group action obstruction](../../../../../../../power-map-group-action-obstruction.md) for every possible $\star$; it never assumes that $\star$ is ordinary integer [multiplication](../../../../../../../multiplication.md), or that the integer $0$ is the acting [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md).
