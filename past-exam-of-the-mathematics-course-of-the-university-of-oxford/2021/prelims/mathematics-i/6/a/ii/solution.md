<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

For $W=aH$ and $W'=bH$, define

$$
f:W\to W',\qquad f(w)=ba^{-1}w.
$$

If $w=ah$ then $f(w)=bh\in bH$, so this [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is well defined. Its [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md) is $w'\mapsto ab^{-1}w'$, since successive left [translation in a group](../../../../../../../translation-in-a-group.md) by $ba^{-1}$ and $ab^{-1}$ cancels. Thus

$$
\boxed{f:aH\xrightarrow{\sim}bH\text{ is a bijection}.}
$$

In particular every left [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md) has the same [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) as $H$.
