<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Each left [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md) $gH$ is nonempty, since it contains $g=ge$, and every $g\in G$ lies in its own left [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md). Suppose $aH$ and $bH$ intersect, say $ah_1=bh_2$ with $h_1,h_2\in H$. Then

$$
a=bh_2h_1^{-1}.
$$

Since $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md), $h_2h_1^{-1}\in H$, and $aH\subseteq bH$. The reversed relation $b=ah_1h_2^{-1}$ gives $bH\subseteq aH$. Thus any two left [cosets](../../../../../../../coset.md) are either equal or disjoint, and together they cover $G$. Therefore **$G/H$ is a [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $G$**. No finiteness or [normal subgroup](../../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) assumption is needed.
