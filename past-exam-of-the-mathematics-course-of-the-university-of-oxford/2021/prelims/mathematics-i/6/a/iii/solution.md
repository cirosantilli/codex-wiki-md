<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Let the [finite set](../../../../../../../finite-set.md) $G/H$ contain $r$ distinct left [cosets](../../../../../../../coset.md). By [i](../i.md), they form a disjoint [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $G$, and by [ii](../ii.md) each has $|H|$ elements. If $H$ is also finite, counting this finite disjoint [set union](../../../../../../../set-union.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|G|=\sum_{W\in G/H}|W|=r|H|=|G/H|\,|H|.}
$$

Thus $G$ is a [finite group](../../../../../../../finite-group.md). This is the counting argument behind [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md).
