<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

The [multiplicative group of a finite field](../../../../../../../multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) $p$ has [group order](../../../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) $p-1$: its elements are exactly the nonzero residue classes. For any [prime factor](../../../../../../../prime-factor.md) $p$ of $2^q-1$, [i](../i.md) and [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) therefore imply

$$
q=\operatorname{ord}_p(2)\mid p-1.
$$

Since $q$ and $p-1$ are positive [integers](../../../../../../../integer-split.md), $q\leq p-1$, and hence

$$
\boxed{p>q.}
$$

This applies to every [prime factor](../../../../../../../prime-factor.md), including repeated [prime factors](../../../../../../../prime-factor.md) viewed as the same [prime number](../../../../../../../prime-number-split.md).
