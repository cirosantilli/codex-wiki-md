<h1 id="5/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Absolute [integrability](../../../../../../../integrability.md) now requires

$$
\mathbb E|Ze^{\alpha Z^4}|
=\frac2{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^\infty z e^{\alpha z^4-z^2/2}\,dz<\infty.
$$

For $\alpha\le0$, the [integrand](../../../../../../../integrand.md) is bounded by $ze^{-z^2/2}$, whose [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) is finite. For $\alpha>0$, once $z^2\ge1/\alpha$, the exponent is at least $\alpha z^4/2$, so the [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) diverges. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[Ze^{\alpha Z^4}]\text{ exists exactly for }\alpha\le0,
\quad\text{and then equals }0.}
$$

Again the zero follows from the [odd function](../../../../../../../odd-function.md) and [symmetry](../../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md); outside the [integrable](../../../../../../../integrability.md) range both signed parts diverge, so the [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is undefined.
