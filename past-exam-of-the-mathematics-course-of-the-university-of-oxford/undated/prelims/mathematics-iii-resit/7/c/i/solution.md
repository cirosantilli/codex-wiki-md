↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [random vector](../../../../../../../random-vector.md) $\boldsymbol\epsilon=(\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_n)^\top$, its [covariance matrix](../../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Sigma=\mathbb E[(\boldsymbol\epsilon-\mathbb E\boldsymbol\epsilon)
(\boldsymbol\epsilon-\mathbb E\boldsymbol\epsilon)^\top],\qquad
\Sigma_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(\epsilon_i,\epsilon_j).}
$$

Its diagonal entries are [variances](../../../../../../../variance-split.md), and its off-diagonal entries measure pairwise [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md). Finite [second moments](../../../../../../../second-moment.md) make every entry well defined.
