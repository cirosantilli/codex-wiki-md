↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [least-squares estimators](../../../../../../../ordinary-least-squares-estimators.md) depend only on the observed responses and design, so the formulas in [i](../../b/i.md) still hold. Their [variances](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) and [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md) need only the error [covariance matrix](../../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md), now $I_n$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\beta)=\frac1{S_{xx}},\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\widehat\alpha,\widehat\beta)=0.}
$$

For example the first formula follows from $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_i x_i\epsilon_i)=\sum_i x_i^2$, and the second from $\sum_i x_i=0$. The formulas remain the same as before with $\sigma^2=1$; this calculation does not establish [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) or a [normal distribution](../../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) for the [estimators](../../../../../../../estimator.md).
