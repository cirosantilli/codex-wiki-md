<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The centered design gives $\widehat\beta=\beta+S_{xx}^{-1}\sum_i x_i\epsilon_i$. By [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the errors, their off-diagonal [covariances](../../../../../../../covariance.md) vanish. Thus [variance additivity for independent random variables](../../../../../../../variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\beta)
=\frac{\sum_i x_i^2\sigma^2}{S_{xx}^2}
=\frac{\sigma^2}{S_{xx}}.}
$$

A larger spread of predictor values decreases the slope [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) for fixed error [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md).
