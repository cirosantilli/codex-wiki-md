Here $\widehat\alpha=\alpha+n^{-1}\sum_i\epsilon_i$. By [linearity of covariance](../../../../../../../linearity-of-covariance.md) and [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(\widehat\alpha,\widehat\beta)
=\frac1{nS_{xx}}\sum_{i,j}x_j\operatorname{Cov}(\epsilon_i,\epsilon_j)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{nS_{xx}}\sum_i x_i=0.}
$$

Thus the two [estimators](../../../../../../../estimator.md) are [uncorrelated random variables](../../../../../../../uncorrelated-random-variables.md).
