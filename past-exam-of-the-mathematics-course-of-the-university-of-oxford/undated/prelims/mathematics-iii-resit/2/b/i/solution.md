↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial(u,v)}{\partial(x,y)}
=\det\begin{pmatrix}-y/x^2&1/x\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
=-\frac{x+y}{x^2}.}
$$

The [change of variables](../../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) is defined only for $x\ne0$. On the interior of the integration region, $x>0$ and $y>0$, so this [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is negative and nonzero.
