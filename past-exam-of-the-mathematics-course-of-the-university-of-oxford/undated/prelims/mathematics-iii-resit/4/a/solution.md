A [discrete random variable](../../../../../../discrete-random-variable.md) on a [probability space](../../../../../../probability-space-split.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ is a real-valued [measurable function](../../../../../../measurable-function.md) $X:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ whose [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) is concentrated on some finite or countable set $S$:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\in S)=1,\qquad S\text{ countable}.}
$$

[Measurability](../../../../../../measurability.md) means $\{\omega:X(\omega)\le t\}\in\mathcal F$ for every real $t$. Its [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) is $p_X(x)=\mathbb P(X=x)$, and $\sum_{x\in S}p_X(x)=1$. Requiring a countable image everywhere, rather than outside a [null set](../../../../../../null-set.md), is an equivalent convention after harmless modification on a [null set](../../../../../../null-set.md).
