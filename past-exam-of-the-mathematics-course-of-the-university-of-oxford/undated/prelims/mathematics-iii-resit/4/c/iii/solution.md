<h1 id="4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Summing over the last digit yields the [marginal probability mass function](../../../../../../../marginal-probability-mass-function.md)

$$
p_Y(y)=(1-q)q^{10y}\sum_{x=0}^9q^x=(1-q^{10})q^{10y},\qquad y\ge0.
$$

Together with the other [marginal probability mass function](../../../../../../../marginal-probability-mass-function.md), this gives

$$
\boxed{p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)p_Y(y).}
$$

The factorization holds on the whole support and with zeros elsewhere, proving that **$X$ and $Y$ are [independent](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md)**. The [conditional distribution](../../../../../../../conditional-distribution.md) in [ii](../ii.md) likewise does not depend on the permissible value $x$.
