<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the ordered outcomes of the two rolls as the [sample space](../../../../../../../sample-space.md):

$$
\boxed{\Omega=\{1,\ldots,6\}^2,\quad\mathcal F=2^\Omega,\quad
\mathbb P(E)=\frac{|E|}{36}\quad(E\subseteq\Omega).}
$$

Thus each elementary outcome has [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) $1/36$. This is the usual [probability space](../../../../../../../probability-space-split.md) for two independent fair dice.
