<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are three even faces, so $\mathbb P(A)=1/2$. The sum is even precisely when both faces have the same [parity](../../../../../../../parity.md), giving $9+9=18$ outcomes and $\mathbb P(B)=1/2$. Both $A$ and $B$ occur precisely when both faces are even, giving nine outcomes. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A\cap B)=\frac9{36}=\frac14
=\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B).}
$$

Thus $A$ and $B$ are [independent events](../../../../../../../independent-events.md).
