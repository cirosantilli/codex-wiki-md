<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every [Euclidean distance](../../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) obeys the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md). The three recorded entries $d_{14}=0.67$, $d_{16}=2.88$ and $d_{46}=4.37$ violate it:

$$
\boxed{d_{46}=4.37>0.67+2.88=3.55=d_{41}+d_{16}.}
$$

**At least one of $0.67,\ 2.88,\ 4.37$ must be incorrect.** Two are in the final row as required. The gap is far larger than any possible rounding of entries to two decimal places.
