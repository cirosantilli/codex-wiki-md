<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the [law of total probability](../../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md),

$$
\mathbb P(T=1)=\frac16\sum_{r=1}^6\frac{7-r}{6}=\frac7{12}.
$$

[Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) therefore gives the [conditional probability mass function](../../../../../../../conditional-probability-mass-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(R=r\mid T=1)
=\frac{(1/6)(7-r)/6}{7/12}=\frac{7-r}{21},\quad r=1,\ldots,6.}
$$

The six [probabilities](../../../../../../../probability.md) sum to one. A small red outcome is more likely after an immediate success because more white faces can meet its threshold.
