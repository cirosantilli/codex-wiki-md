<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [joint probability density function](../../../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) integrates to one. Here

$$
1=C\int_0^1\int_0^1u(1+2v)\,dv\,du
=C\left(\frac12\right)(2),
$$

so

$$
\boxed{C=1.}
$$

This value also makes the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) nonnegative everywhere.
