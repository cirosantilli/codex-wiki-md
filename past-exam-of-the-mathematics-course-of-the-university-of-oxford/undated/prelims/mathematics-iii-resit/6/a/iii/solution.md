<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

On the product support $[0,1]^2$, the [joint probability density function](../../../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) factors as

$$
\boxed{f_{U,V}(u,v)=u(1+2v)=(2u)(1/2+v)=f_U(u)f_V(v).}
$$

The equality also holds outside the support with both sides zero. The [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) factorization criterion proves that **$U$ and $V$ are [independent random variables](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md)**.
