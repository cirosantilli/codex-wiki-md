↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Obtain each [marginal density](../../../../../../../marginal-density.md) by integrating the [joint probability density function](../../../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) over the other variable:

$$
\boxed{f_U(u)=\int_0^1u(1+2v)\,dv=2u,\quad0\le u\le1,}
$$



$$
\boxed{f_V(v)=\int_0^1u(1+2v)\,du=\frac12+v,\quad0\le v\le1.}
$$

Each [marginal density](../../../../../../../marginal-density.md) is zero off its displayed [interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) and has total [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) one.
