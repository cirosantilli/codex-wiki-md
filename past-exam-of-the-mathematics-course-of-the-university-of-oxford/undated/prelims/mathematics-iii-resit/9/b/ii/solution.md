↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), the [likelihood function](../../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) is the product of the individual [probability density functions](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md):

$$
\boxed{L(\mu,\lambda;x_1,\ldots,x_n)
=\left(\frac\lambda2\right)^n
\exp\!\left[-\lambda\sum_{i=1}^n|x_i-\mu|\right],\quad
\mu\in\mathbb R,\ \lambda>0.}
$$

Writing $A(\mu)=\sum_i|x_i-\mu|$, the [log-likelihood function](../../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) is $\ell=n\ln\lambda-n\ln2-\lambda A(\mu)$. Here $\lambda$ is a rate, the reciprocal of the scale in the usual [Laplace distribution](../../../../../../../laplace-distribution.md) notation.
