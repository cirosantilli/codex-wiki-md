↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The joint [maximum-likelihood estimators](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) are functions of the observations selecting a maximizing parameter pair:

$$
\boxed{(\widehat\mu,\widehat\lambda)\in
\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\mu\in\mathbb R,\lambda>0}
L(\mu,\lambda;X_1,\ldots,X_n).}
$$

The [estimates](../../../../../../../statistical-estimate.md) after observation maximize the same [likelihood function](../../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) with $X_i=x_i$. Maximization may be nonunique, as for an even [sample size](../../../../../../../sample-size.md), or may fail to yield a finite admissible pair, as in the one-observation case below. The definition does not turn a boundary supremum into an attained [maximum](../../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md).
