<h1 id="9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The independent uniform choices from the student list describe [sampling with replacement](../../../../../../../sampling-with-replacement.md). Under the stated independent [normal distribution](../../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) model for the heights, the population [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) is unknown and the given $8.5$ cm is the [sample standard deviation](../../../../../../../sample-standard-deviation.md) $s$. The exact [pivotal quantity](../../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) is

$$
\frac{\overline X-\mu}{S/\sqrt{100}}\sim t_{99}.
$$

Thus the exact 95% [Student t confidence interval](../../../../../../../student-t-confidence-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{171.7\pm t_{99,0.975}\frac{8.5}{10}\ \mathrm{cm}
\ \approx\ [170.01,173.39]\ \mathrm{cm},}
$$

using $t_{99,0.975}\approx1.9842$. The supplied approximation $\Phi(2)=0.975$ instead gives a [normal approximation](../../../../../../../normal-approximation.md) for large [sample size](../../../../../../../sample-size.md)

$$
\boxed{171.7\pm2(0.85)=[170.0,173.4]\ \mathrm{cm}.}
$$

This latter [confidence interval](../../../../../../../confidence-interval.md) is approximate even under [normality](../../../../../../../normality-statistics.md) because it substitutes the [sample standard deviation](../../../../../../../sample-standard-deviation.md) for the unknown population value. There is no [finite population correction](../../../../../../../finite-population-correction.md) under the specified independent selections. Interpreting the finite student list literally as a discrete population makes [normality](../../../../../../../normality-statistics.md) an idealized model; it does not justify changing the stated sampling design.
