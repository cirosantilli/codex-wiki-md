<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Evaluate the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) separately on the three oriented [line segments](../../../../../../../line-segment.md). Their [parametrizations](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) and contributions are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\mathbf r(t),\ 0\le t\le1&\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)&\text{contribution}\\\hline
(t,0,0)&0&0\\
(1,t,0)&0&0\\
(1,1,t)&t&1/2
\end{array}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\frac12.}
$$

The different value in [i](../i.md) demonstrates [path dependence](../../../../../../../path-dependence.md). The [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) in this part is different from the [conservative vector field](../../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) in part (a).
