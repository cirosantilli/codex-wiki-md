<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) of the straight [line segment](../../../../../../../line-segment.md) is $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,t)$, $0\le t\le1$. Then $\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(t,t^2,t)$ and $\mathbf r'(t)=(1,1,1)$. By the [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) formula,

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\int_0^1(2t+t^2)\,dt=\frac43.}
$$
