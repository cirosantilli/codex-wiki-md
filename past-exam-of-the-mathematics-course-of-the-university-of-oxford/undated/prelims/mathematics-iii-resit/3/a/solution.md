<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The middle component suggests a [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) with $-\phi_y=2y\cosh^2(xz)$, so $\phi=-y^2\cosh^2(xz)+h(x,z)$. The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and $2\sinh t\cosh t=\sinh(2t)$ give

$$
-\phi_x=y^2z\sinh(2xz)-h_x,\qquad
-\phi_z=y^2x\sinh(2xz)-h_z.
$$

Matching the other two components requires $h_x=h_z=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi(x,y,z)=-y^2\cosh^2(xz)+C.}
$$

Its negative [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is the given [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) in all three components; the additive constant has no effect on that [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md).
