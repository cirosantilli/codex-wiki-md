<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a uniform solid [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md), set $a=0$ in the [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) formula, giving

$$
\ddot x_{\mathrm{solid}}=\frac23g\sin\varphi.
$$

For the hollow [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md), $a>0$ makes the denominator larger:

$$
\ddot x_{\mathrm{hollow}}=\frac{2b^2}{3b^2+a^2}g\sin\varphi
<\frac23g\sin\varphi
$$

on a downward incline with $\sin\varphi>0$. Thus **the solid [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) accelerates faster**, assuming [rolling without slipping](../../../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) for both. More of the hollow [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md)'s [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) lies far from the rotation axis, increasing its [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) relative to $Mb^2$.
