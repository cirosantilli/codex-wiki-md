↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [scalar product](../../../../../../dot-product.md), also called the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md), and the [vector product](../../../../../../cross-product.md), also called the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md), are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3,\qquad
\mathbf a\wedge\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}a_2b_3-a_3b_2\\a_3b_1-a_1b_3\\a_1b_2-a_2b_1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For nonzero [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), let $\vartheta\in[0,\pi]$ be their [angle](../../../../../../angle.md). Their [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) satisfy

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\vartheta,\qquad
|\mathbf a\wedge\mathbf b|=|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\sin\vartheta.}
$$

The [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to both [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), with its orientation determined by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). If either [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is zero, both products vanish, although the [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) is undefined.
