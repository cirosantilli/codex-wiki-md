<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At a regular point of the [parametrized surface](../../../../../../../parametrized-surface.md), the [partial derivatives](../../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) $\mathbf r_s$ and $\mathbf r_t$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) [tangent vectors](../../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md). Their [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) gives a nonzero [normal vector](../../../../../../../normal-vector.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\mathbf r_s\times\mathbf r_t.}
$$

Dividing by its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) gives a [unit normal](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md); changing the sign chooses the opposite orientation. If the [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) vanishes, this [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) is singular there and cannot determine a [normal vector](../../../../../../../normal-vector.md) by this formula.
