<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An infinitesimal parameter rectangle becomes a [parallelogram](../../../../../../../parallelogram.md) with edges $\mathbf r_s\,ds$ and $\mathbf r_t\,dt$. Its area is the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) of their [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md), giving the [surface area element](../../../../../../../surface-area-element.md)

$$
dS=|\mathbf r_s\times\mathbf r_t|\,ds\,dt.
$$

Thus, for a regular [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) covering the [surface](../../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) once except possibly on sets of area zero,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{area}(S)=\iint_U|\mathbf r_s\times\mathbf r_t|\,ds\,dt.}
$$

For several patches, add their [surface integrals](../../../../../../../surface-integral.md) while avoiding overlap; repeated coverage would otherwise count area with multiplicity.
