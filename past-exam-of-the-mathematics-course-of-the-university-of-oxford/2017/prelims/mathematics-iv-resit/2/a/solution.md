<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [hyperbola](../../../../../../hyperbola.md) with [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) $F$, [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) $D$ not containing $F$, and [eccentricity](../../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) $e>1$ is the [locus](../../../../../../locus-mathematics.md)

$$
\{P:\operatorname{dist}(P,F)=e\operatorname{dist}(P,D)\}.
$$

Put $c=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ and $e=c/a>1$. For $F=(c,0)$ and $D=\{x=a^2/c\}$, the defining equality becomes

$$
\sqrt{(x-c)^2+y^2}=\frac ca\left|x-\frac{a^2}{c}\right|.
$$

Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring is reversible. Expanding and cancelling the linear terms gives

$$
x^2-2cx+c^2+y^2=\frac{c^2}{a^2}x^2-2cx+a^2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1.
$$

Thus the two matched [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) and [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) choices, related by reflection, are

$$
\boxed{F_\pm=(\pm\sqrt{a^2+b^2},0),\qquad
D_\pm:\ x=\pm\frac{a^2}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}},\qquad
e=\sqrt{1+\frac{b^2}{a^2}}.}
$$

The signs in each pair must agree.

On the [hyperbola](../../../../../../hyperbola.md), $y=\pm(b/a)\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$. As $|x|\to\infty$,

$$
|x|-\sqrt{x^2-a^2}=\frac{a^2}{|x|+\sqrt{x^2-a^2}}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Hence the perpendicular [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) to one of the two [straight lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md) tends to zero on each end of a branch. The [asymptotes](../../../../../../asymptote.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{y=\frac ba x\quad\text{and}\quad y=-\frac ba x.}
$$
