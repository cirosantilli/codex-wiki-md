<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In terms of the positive [cylindrical radius](../../../../../../cylindrical-radius.md) $r=-z$, the conserved [mechanical energy](../../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) becomes

$$
(1+\sqrt2)\dot r^2+U(r)=E,\qquad
U(r)=gr+\frac{a^2v^2}{2r^2},\qquad E=ga+\frac{v^2}{2}.
$$

The [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) $U$ tends to infinity at both ends of $(0,\infty)$ for $v>0$, and $U''(r)=3a^2v^2/r^4>0$. Thus its two [turning points](../../../../../../turning-point.md) enclose the allowed radial motion. At a [turning point](../../../../../../turning-point.md), $\dot r=0$, so

$$
2gr^3-(v^2+2ga)r^2+a^2v^2
=(r-a)(2gr^2-v^2r-av^2)=0.
$$

Besides $r=a$, the positive [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) is

$$
r_*=\frac{v^2+\sqrt{v^4+8gav^2}}{4g};
$$

the other [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) is negative. The [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) $Q(r)=2gr^2-v^2r-av^2$ is negative at zero and has just one positive [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). Since $Q(a)=2a(ga-v^2)$, we have $r_*<a$ when $v^2<ga$, and $r_*>a$ when $v^2>ga$.

Height is $z=-r$, so its [maximum](../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) occurs at the smaller positive [turning point](../../../../../../turning-point.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{z_{\max}=\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{v^2+\sqrt{v^4+8gav^2}}{4g},&0<v<\sqrt{ga},\\[6pt]
-a,&v>\sqrt{ga}.
\end{cases}}
$$

Indeed, the initial vertical [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) is

$$
\ddot z(0)=\frac{g-v^2/a}{2(1+\sqrt2)}.
$$

Below the critical speed the particle first rises to $z=-r_*$; above it the particle initially descends, and its initial height is already its greatest height. At $v=\sqrt{ga}$ the [turning points](../../../../../../turning-point.md) merge and the particle follows a horizontal [circular orbit](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md) at $z=-a$. For zero initial speed the radial [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives motion to the apex level $z=0$; if the hole excludes the apex itself, zero is the supremum of the heights before arrival at the hole.

These are the heights under the maintained [mechanical constraint](../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) used in part (a). A unilateral contact with only one side of a cone would additionally require checking the sign of the [normal reaction](../../../../../../normal-force.md); the ideal constrained equations alone do not impose that condition.
