<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md) satisfy

$$
f_x=2x+y=0,\qquad f_y=3y^2+x=0.
$$

Substitute $x=-y/2$ to obtain $y(3y-1/2)=0$. Thus the only [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md) are $(0,0)$ and $(-1/12,1/6)$.

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) and [Hessian determinant](../../../../../../hessian-determinant.md) are

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&6y\end{pmatrix},\qquad D=12y-1.
$$

At $(0,0)$, $D=-1$, so this is a [saddle point](../../../../../../saddle-point.md). At $(-1/12,1/6)$, $D=1$ and $f_{xx}=2>0$, so this is a strict [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{(0,0):\text{ saddle};\qquad(-1/12,1/6):\text{ strict local minimum}.}
$$

The value at the [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) is $-1/432$; there is no [local maximum](../../../../../../local-maximum.md).
