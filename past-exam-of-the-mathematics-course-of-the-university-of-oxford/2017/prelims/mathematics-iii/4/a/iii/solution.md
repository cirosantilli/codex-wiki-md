↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) at one of the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E X=G'_X(1)=\frac{p}{p^2}=\frac1p.}
$$

Evaluation at one is valid here because the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) has radius of convergence $1/(1-p)>1$.
