↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using the [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md), the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is

$$
G_X(s)=\mathbb E(s^X)=\sum_{k\ge1}pq^{k-1}s^k
=\boxed{\frac{ps}{1-qs}},\qquad |qs|<1.
$$

Differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{G'_X(s)=\frac{p(1-qs)+pqs}{(1-qs)^2}=\frac{p}{(1-s+ps)^2}.}
$$
