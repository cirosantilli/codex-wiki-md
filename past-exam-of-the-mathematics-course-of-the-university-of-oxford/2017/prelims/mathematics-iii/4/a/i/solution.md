<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $q=1-p$. Since $X$ takes positive integer values, $\mathbb P(X>0)=1=q^0$. Taking differences of the given tail [probabilities](../../../../../../../probability.md) therefore yields, also for $k=1$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X=k)=\mathbb P(X>k-1)-\mathbb P(X>k)=pq^{k-1},\qquad k\ge1.}
$$

This is the [probability mass function](../../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) of the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on $\{1,2,\ldots\}$.
