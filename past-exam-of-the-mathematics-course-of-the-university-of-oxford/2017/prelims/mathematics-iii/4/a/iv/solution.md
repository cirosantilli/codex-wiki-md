<h1 id="4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The event $W>k$ occurs exactly when both $X_1>k$ and $X_2>k$. By [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md),

$$
\mathbb P(W>k)=q^kq^k=q^{2k}=\left(1-(2p-p^2)\right)^k.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{W\sim\operatorname{Geom}(2p-p^2).}
$$

The new success [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) $2p-p^2$ is the chance that at least one of the two trials succeeds.
