<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the definition of a [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md),

$$
G_{\widetilde Y}(s)=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{kp_k}{\mu}s^k
=\boxed{\frac{s}{\mu}G'_Y(s)}.
$$

Termwise differentiation of the [power series](../../../../../../../power-series.md) is valid for $|s|<1$. At $s=1$, the equality extends by the finite [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) assumption, and both sides equal one.
