<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $Y\ge1$, its finite [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) satisfies $1\le\mu<\infty$. Every $q_k=kp_k/\mu$ is nonnegative, and

$$
\sum_{k\ge1}q_k=\frac1\mu\sum_{k\ge1}kp_k=\frac{\mathbb E Y}{\mu}=1.
$$

Thus $(q_k)$ is a **[probability mass function](../../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md)**. It defines the [size-biased distribution](../../../../../../../size-biased-distribution.md) of $Y$: an outcome of size $k$ receives $k$ times its original relative weight.
