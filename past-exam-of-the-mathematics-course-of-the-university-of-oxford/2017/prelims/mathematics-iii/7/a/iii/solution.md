<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiating the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) in part (ii) gives the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $nt^{n-1}$ on $(0,1)$. Hence

$$
\mathbb E\left(\frac M\theta\right)=\int_0^1t\,nt^{n-1}\,dt=\frac n{n+1},\qquad
\mathbb E M=\frac n{n+1}\theta.
$$

Thus an [unbiased estimator](../../../../../../../unbiased-estimator.md) based on the sample maximum is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\theta=\frac{n+1}{n}\widehat\theta.}
$$
