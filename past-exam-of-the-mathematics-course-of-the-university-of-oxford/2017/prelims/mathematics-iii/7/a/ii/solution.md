<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $0\le t\le1$, [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) give

$$
\mathbb P\left(\frac M\theta\le t\right)=\mathbb P(X_1\le\theta t,\ldots,X_n\le\theta t)=t^n.
$$

Therefore the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_{M/\theta}(t)=\begin{cases}0,&t<0,\\t^n,&0\le t\le1,\\1,&t>1.\end{cases}}
$$

It contains no $\theta$, so $M/\theta$ is a [pivotal quantity](../../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md).
