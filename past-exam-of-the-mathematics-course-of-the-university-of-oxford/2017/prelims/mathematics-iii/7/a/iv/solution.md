<h1 id="7/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Each observation from the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) has [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\theta/2$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md), $\mathbb E\sum_iX_i=n\theta/2$, so another [unbiased estimator](../../../../../../../unbiased-estimator.md) is

$$
\boxed{\theta^*=\frac2n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i=2\overline X.}
$$
