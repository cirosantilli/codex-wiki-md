<h1 id="7/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [pivotal quantity](../../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) $U=M/\theta$ lies in $[0,1]$ and satisfies $\mathbb P(U\le t)=t^n$. Thus

$$
\mathbb P\left(\alpha^{1/n}\le\frac M\theta\le1\right)=1-\alpha.
$$

Rearranging these inequalities yields the exact [confidence interval](../../../../../../../confidence-interval.md)

$$
\boxed{\left[\widehat\theta,\frac{\widehat\theta}{\alpha^{1/n}}\right].}
$$

For every $\theta>0$, its coverage [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) is exactly $1-\alpha$; the result uses the finite-sample [probability distribution](../../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md), with no [normal approximation](../../../../../../../normal-approximation.md).
