<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are two paths from $X$ back to $X$ in two steps: $X\to X\to X$ and $X\to Y\to X$. By the [markov property](../../../../../../../markov-property.md), their [probabilities](../../../../../../../probability.md) multiply along each path. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_2=\frac12\cdot\frac12+\frac12\cdot\frac38=\frac14+\frac3{16}=\frac7{16}.}
$$
