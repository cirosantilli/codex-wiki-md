A [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $\Omega$ is a collection of nonempty [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) whose union is $\Omega$ and which are pairwise disjoint:

$$
\bigcup_{i\in I}B_i=\Omega,\qquad B_i\cap B_j=\varnothing\quad(i\ne j).
$$

For use with [probabilities](../../../../../../../probability.md), the $B_i$ must also be [events](../../../../../../../event.md) in the [sample space](../../../../../../../sample-space.md).
