<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a finite or countable [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) into [events](../../../../../../../event.md) $B_i$, the [law of total probability](../../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) states

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A)=\sum_{i:\,\mathbb P(B_i)>0}\mathbb P(A\mid B_i)\mathbb P(B_i).}
$$

The [events](../../../../../../../event.md) $A\cap B_i$ are pairwise disjoint and have union $A$, so countable additivity proves the formula. Terms with $\mathbb P(B_i)=0$ contribute zero and are omitted to avoid undefined [conditional probabilities](../../../../../../../conditional-probability.md).
