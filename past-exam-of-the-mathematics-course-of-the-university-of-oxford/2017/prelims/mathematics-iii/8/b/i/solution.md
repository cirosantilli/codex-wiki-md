The [central limit theorem](../../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) states that if $W_1,W_2,\ldots$ are [independent and identically distributed](../../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with finite [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\mu$ and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) $0<\tau^2<\infty$, then

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^nW_i-n\mu}{\tau\sqrt n}\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1).}
$$

Here $\xrightarrow d$ denotes [convergence in distribution](../../../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md).
