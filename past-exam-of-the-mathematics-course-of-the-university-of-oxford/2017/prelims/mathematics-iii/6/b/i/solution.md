<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume finite second moments and write $Z_i=X_i-\mathbb E X_i$. Expanding the square in the definition of [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)=\mathbb E[(Z_1+Z_2)^2]
=\mathbb E Z_1^2+\mathbb E Z_2^2+2\mathbb E(Z_1Z_2).
$$

Recognizing the two [variances](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) and the [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md), we obtain

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)=\operatorname{Var}(X_1)+\operatorname{Var}(X_2)+2\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2).}
$$
