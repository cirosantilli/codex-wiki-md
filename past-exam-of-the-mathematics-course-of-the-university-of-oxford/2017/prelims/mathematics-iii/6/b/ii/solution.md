↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [random variables](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) with finite second moments, the [variance of a sum](../../../../../../../variance-of-a-sum.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)+2\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).}
$$

No [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) assumption is needed.
