<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the rate-one [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md), the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is $e^{-t}$ for $t\ge0$. By [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md),

$$
\mathbb E T=\int_0^\infty te^{-t}\,dt=1,\qquad
\mathbb E(T^2)=\int_0^\infty t^2e^{-t}\,dt=2.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E T=1,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(T)=2-1^2=1.}
$$
