<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) and part (i),

$$
0\le\mathbb P\left(\frac{S_n}{n}>1+\epsilon\right)
\le\mathbb P\left(\left|\frac{S_n}{n}-1\right|\ge\epsilon\right)
\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}(S_n)}{n^2\epsilon^2}
=\frac{5n-2}{n^2\epsilon^2}\longrightarrow0.
$$

This actually proves the stronger statement $\boxed{S_n/n\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}1}$, namely [convergence in probability](../../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md), despite the [statistical dependence](../../../../../../../statistical-dependence.md) between adjacent products.
