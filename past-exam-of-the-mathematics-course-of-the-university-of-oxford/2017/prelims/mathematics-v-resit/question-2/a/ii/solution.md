<h1 id="question-2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

For $r>0$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\partial r/\partial x_i=x_i/r$. Applying the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) to the components of the [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf u
&=\sum_{i=1}^3\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}(x_i r^{-\alpha})\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^3\left(r^{-\alpha}-\alpha x_i^2r^{-\alpha-2}\right)\\
&=3r^{-\alpha}-\alpha r^{-\alpha-2}\sum_{i=1}^3x_i^2
=\boxed{\frac{3-\alpha}{r^\alpha}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The calculation is pointwise away from the origin; any extension of the [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) to the origin must be considered separately.
