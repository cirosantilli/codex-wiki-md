<h1 id="question-2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Let $V$ be a bounded region with piecewise smooth closed boundary $\partial V$, and let $\mathbf F$ be a [continuously differentiable vector field](../../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-vector-field.md) on an open neighbourhood of its [closure](../../../../../../../closure-topology.md). The [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV
=\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS},
$$

where $\mathbf n$ is the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md). Thus the [volume integral](../../../../../../../volume-integral.md) of the [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) equals the outward boundary [flux integral](../../../../../../../flux-integral.md). For a region containing a hole, the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) on its inner boundary points into that hole.
