<h1 id="question-3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [b](../b.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) inside the Cartesian expression for the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\times(\zeta\mathbf v)
&=\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\partial_i(\zeta\mathbf v)\\
&=\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\bigl((\partial_i\zeta)\mathbf v
+\zeta\partial_i\mathbf v\bigr)\\
&=\left(\sum_i(\partial_i\zeta)\mathbf e_i\right)\times\mathbf v
+\zeta\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\partial_i\mathbf v.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first sum is the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) of the [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) and the second is the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) of the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md). Thus the [curl product rule for a scalar field](../../../../../../curl-product-rule-for-a-scalar-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times(\zeta\mathbf v)
=\zeta\nabla\times\mathbf v+\nabla\zeta\times\mathbf v}.
$$
