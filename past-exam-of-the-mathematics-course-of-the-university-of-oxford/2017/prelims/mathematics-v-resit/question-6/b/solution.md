<h1 id="question-6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [b](../b.md)

A temperature on the full unit [disk](../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md) must be single valued, so its angular factor and angular [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) are $2\pi$-periodic. For positive [separation constant](../../../../../../separation-constant.md) $\lambda=k^2$, this requires $k=n$ to be a positive integer. For $\lambda=0$, [periodicity](../../../../../../periodicity.md) removes $D\theta$. For $\lambda<0$, no nonzero linear combination of $e^{k\theta}$ and $e^{-k\theta}$ is $2\pi$-periodic, so that case disappears.

The origin belongs to the [disk](../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md), so a classical [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) must remain bounded and extend smoothly there. This excludes $r^{-n}$ and $\log r$. The remaining individual separated solutions are constants and $r^n(A_n\cos(n\theta)+B_n\sin(n\theta))$. By [superposition](../../../../../../superposition-principle.md), the regular [harmonic expansion on a disk](../../../../../../harmonic-expansion-on-a-disk.md) is

$$
\boxed{T(r,\theta)=A_0+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}r^n
\bigl(A_n\cos(n\theta)+B_n\sin(n\theta)\bigr)}.
$$

The admissible [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) are fixed by the [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md). Smoothness at the origin can also be seen from $r^n\cos(n\theta)$ and $r^n\sin(n\theta)$ being the real and imaginary parts of $(x+iy)^n$, hence [harmonic polynomials](../../../../../../harmonic-polynomial.md).
