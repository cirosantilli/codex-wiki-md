<h1 id="question-6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

The boundary data consist of the third angular [Fourier mode](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md). Its regular radial factor in the [harmonic expansion on a disk](../../../../../../../harmonic-expansion-on-a-disk.md) is $r^3$, so

$$
\boxed{T(r,\theta)=r^3\sin(3\theta)}.
$$

For a direct check of [Laplace's equation](../../../../../../../laplace-equation.md),

$$
T_{rr}+\frac1rT_r+\frac1{r^2}T_{\theta\theta}
=6r\sin(3\theta)+3r\sin(3\theta)-9r\sin(3\theta)=0.
$$

Also $T(1,\theta)=\sin(3\theta)$. At the origin the apparent coordinate singularity is harmless: this is the [harmonic polynomial](../../../../../../../harmonic-polynomial.md) $3x^2y-y^3$. The allowed uniqueness for the [Dirichlet problem](../../../../../../../dirichlet-problem.md) completes the identification.
