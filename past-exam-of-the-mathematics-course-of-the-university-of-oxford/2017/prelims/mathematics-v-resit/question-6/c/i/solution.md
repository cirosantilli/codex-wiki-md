The constant [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) satisfies [Laplace's equation](../../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) and the prescribed [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). In the [harmonic expansion on a disk](../../../../../../../harmonic-expansion-on-a-disk.md), its only nonzero coefficient is $A_0=1$. By the allowed uniqueness for the [Dirichlet problem](../../../../../../../dirichlet-problem.md),

$$
\boxed{T(r,\theta)=1}.
$$
