<h1 id="question-4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [c](../c.md)

Set $p=3$ and $x=0$ in the [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) from [b](../b.md). Since the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is continuous there, [pointwise convergence of a piecewise smooth Fourier series](../../../../../../pointwise-convergence-of-a-piecewise-smooth-fourier-series.md) gives

$$
0=\frac13-\frac16-6\!\sum_{\substack{n\geq1\\n\ne3}}
\frac{(-1)^n}{n^2-9}.
$$

It follows that

$$
\sum_{\substack{n\geq1\\n\ne3}}\frac{(-1)^n}{n^2-9}=\frac1{36}.
$$

The $n=1$ and $n=2$ terms are $1/8$ and $-1/5$, respectively. Subtracting them gives the required absolutely convergent [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md):

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=4}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n}{n^2-9}
=\frac1{36}-\frac18+\frac15=\frac{37}{360}}.
$$
