↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Yes.** A [surjective](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md) $T_A$ has $\operatorname{im}T_A=\mathbb R^n$, so every $\mathbf b$ is attained. Its [rank of a linear map](../../../../../../../rank-of-a-linear-map.md) is $n$, and the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives

$$
\dim\ker T_A=m-n>0.
$$

The [solution set of a linear system](../../../../../../../solution-set-of-a-linear-system.md) is therefore a translate of a nonzero [vector subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md), containing infinitely many solutions as in [iii](../../a/iii.md).
