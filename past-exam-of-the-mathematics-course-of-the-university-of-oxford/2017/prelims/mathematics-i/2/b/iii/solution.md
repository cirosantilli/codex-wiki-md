↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives

$$
\dim\ker T_A=m-\operatorname{rank}T_A\geq m-n>0,
$$

but this guarantees infinitely many solutions only when the [linear system](../../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) is consistent. For a counterexample take

$$
n=1,\quad m=2,\quad A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\mathbf b=(1).
$$

There is no solution, since the left side always vanishes.
