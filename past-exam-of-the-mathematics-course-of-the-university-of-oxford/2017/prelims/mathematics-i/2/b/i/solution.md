**No.** Here $A$ has more rows than columns, which does not force a nonzero [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). For example, with $m=1$, $n=2$,

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$

the [linear system](../../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) has the unique solution $x=0$. With the same $A$ and $\mathbf b=(0,1)^T$ it has no solution at all.
