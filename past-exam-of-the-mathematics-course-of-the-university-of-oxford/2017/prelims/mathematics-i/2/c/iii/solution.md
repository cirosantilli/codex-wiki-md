↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By [i](../i.md), a free choice of $x_3$ is possible precisely when $\lambda=5$ and $\mu=2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{infinitely many solutions}\iff(\lambda,\mu)=(5,2).}
$$

The complete [solution set of a linear system](../../../../../../../solution-set-of-a-linear-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\0\end{pmatrix}
+t\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-5\\1\end{pmatrix},\quad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The direction [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) spans the [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), and the displayed [straight line](../../../../../../../straight-line.md) is the entire [solution set of a linear system](../../../../../../../solution-set-of-a-linear-system.md).
