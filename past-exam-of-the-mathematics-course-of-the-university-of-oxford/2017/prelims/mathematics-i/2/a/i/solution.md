<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By definition, the [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $T_A$ is

$$
\operatorname{im}T_A=\{A\mathbf x:\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^m\}.
$$

Consequently $\mathbf b\in\operatorname{im}T_A$ means exactly that some $\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^m$ satisfies $A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$. This is precisely the existence of a solution of the [linear system](../../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md).
