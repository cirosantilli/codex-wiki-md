<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $\mathbf b$ is outside the [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $T_A$, there is no solution. Otherwise choose $\mathbf x_0$ with $A\mathbf x_0=\mathbf b$. By [ii](../ii.md), a zero [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) gives a unique solution. If the [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) contains a nonzero [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf z$, all

$$
\mathbf x_0+t\mathbf z,\qquad t\in\mathbb R,
$$

are solutions. They are distinct, since $(t-s)\mathbf z=0$ forces $t=s$. Hence there are infinitely many. These alternatives exhaust all possibilities.
