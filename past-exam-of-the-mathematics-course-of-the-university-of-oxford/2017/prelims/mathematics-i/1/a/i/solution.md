↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) if

$$
\sum_{j=1}^n a_jv_j=0,\quad a_j\in\mathbb R
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_1=\cdots=a_n=0.
$$

They [span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) $V$ if every $v\in V$ is a [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) $v=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jv_j$ with real [coefficients](../../../../../../../coefficient.md). They form a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $V$ if they are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) and [span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) $V$. Equivalently, every $v\in V$ has exactly one such representation: existence is the [spanning set](../../../../../../../spanning-set.md) condition, and subtracting two representations shows that uniqueness is precisely [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md).
