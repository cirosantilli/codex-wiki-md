<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $w_1=v_1$ and $w_j=v_j-v_1$ for $j\geq2$. Since $v_1=w_1$ and $v_j=w_j+w_1$, the two families have the same [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md). Moreover,

$$
\sum_{j=1}^n a_jw_j
=\left(a_1-\sum_{j=2}^n a_j\right)v_1+\sum_{j=2}^n a_jv_j.
$$

If the $v_j$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md), a vanishing expression forces $a_j=0$ for $j\geq2$ and then $a_1=0$. Conversely,

$$
\sum_{j=1}^n b_jv_j
=\left(\sum_{j=1}^n b_j\right)w_1+\sum_{j=2}^n b_jw_j,
$$

so [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) of the $w_j$ forces all $b_j=0$. Thus both the [spanning set](../../../../../../../spanning-set.md) condition and [linear independence](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) are equivalent for the two families, proving the desired equivalence of [bases](../../../../../../../basis.md).
