<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose an [ordered basis](../../../../../../../ordered-basis.md) $\mathcal B$ of $V$ and let $A=[T]_{\mathcal B}$. Define the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) by

$$
\boxed{\chi_T(x)=\det(xI-A).}
$$

If a different [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) gives $B=P^{-1}AP$ for an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) $P$, multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\det(xI-B)=\det\bigl(P^{-1}(xI-A)P\bigr)
=\det(P^{-1})\det(xI-A)\det P
=\det(xI-A).
$$

Thus the definition is independent of the chosen [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md). The alternate convention $\det(A-xI)$ differs by the constant factor $(-1)^{\dim V}$ and gives the same roots.
