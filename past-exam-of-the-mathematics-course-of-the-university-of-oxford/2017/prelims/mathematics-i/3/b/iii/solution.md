<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By [i](../i.md) and [ii](../ii.md),

$$
r_{ST}+n_{ST}=r_{S'}+n_{S'}+n_T.
$$

The [domain of a function](../../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) of $S'$ is $\operatorname{im}T$. Its [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $r_{S'}+n_{S'}=\dim\operatorname{im}T=r_T$. Applying the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) for $T$ now gives

$$
\boxed{r_{ST}+n_{ST}=r_T+n_T=\dim U,}
$$

which is exactly the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) for the [function composition](../../../../../../../function-composition.md) $ST$.
