<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $u\in U$, the [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) $Tu$ already belongs to $\operatorname{im}T$, so

$$
STu=0\iff S'(Tu)=0\iff Tu\in\ker S'.
$$

Therefore $\ker(ST)=T^{-1}(\ker S')$. Since $\ker S'=\ker S\cap\operatorname{im}T$, its intersection with $\operatorname{im}T$ is itself. Applying [a](../../a.md) with $X=\ker S'$ yields

$$
\boxed{n_{ST}=n_{S'}+n_T\leq n_S+n_T.}
$$

The inequality follows from the inclusion $\ker S'\subseteq\ker S$ and monotonicity of [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) for [vector subspaces](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md).
