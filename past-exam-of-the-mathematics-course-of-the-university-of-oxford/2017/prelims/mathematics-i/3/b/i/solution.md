↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $S'$ is the [restriction](../../../../../../../restriction-mathematics.md) of $S$ to $\operatorname{im}T$,

$$
\operatorname{im}(ST)=\{S(Tu):u\in U\}
=\{S(v):v\in\operatorname{im}T\}=\operatorname{im}S'.
$$

This [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) is contained in $\operatorname{im}S$, so $r_{ST}\leq r_S$. Also, applying $S$ to a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $\operatorname{im}T$ gives a [spanning set](../../../../../../../spanning-set.md) for $\operatorname{im}S'$, of size $r_T$. Thus $r_{ST}\leq r_T$, and

$$
\boxed{r_{ST}\leq\min(r_S,r_T).}
$$
