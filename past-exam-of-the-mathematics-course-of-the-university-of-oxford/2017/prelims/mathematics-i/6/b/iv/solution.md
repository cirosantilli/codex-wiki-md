<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $H$ for this [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md) is its [normalizer](../../../../../../../normalizer.md)

$$
\operatorname{Stab}(H)=N_G(H)=\{g\in G:gHg^{-1}=H\}.
$$

If it is all of $G$, the equality $gHg^{-1}=H$ holds for every $g\in G$, which is precisely the definition of a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md).

If instead $\operatorname{Stab}(H)=H$, the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) says that the [group orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) of $H$ has size $[G:H]$. Every conjugate $gHg^{-1}$ is isomorphic to $H$, with [group isomorphism](../../../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) $h\mapsto ghg^{-1}$, so this [group orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) is a [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $\Omega$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|\Omega|\geq[G:H].}
$$

The [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md) need not be transitive on all of $\Omega$; this one [group orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) supplies the lower bound.
