<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $C_p=\langle g\rangle$. A [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from this [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) is determined by the image of $g$, because $\phi(g^j)=\phi(g)^j$. Each choice $\phi(g)=g^a$, with $a=0,\ldots,p-1$, gives a well-defined [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md). It is an [group automorphism](../../../../../../../group-automorphism.md) exactly when $g^a$ generates $C_p$.

For a [prime number](../../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$, every $a=1,\ldots,p-1$ is [coprime](../../../../../../../coprime-integers.md) to $p$, so the powers of $g^a$ run through all of $C_p$. The choice $a=0$ gives the trivial [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) and fails to be bijective. Consequently

$$
\boxed{|\operatorname{Aut}(C_p)|=p-1.}
$$
