<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $v\in S$. Choose a [unit vector](../../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $u$ perpendicular to $v$, and put $w=v\times u$. The [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) identities show that $(v,u,w)$ is an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) with positive orientation: $u\times(v\times u)=v$, so

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}v&u&w\end{pmatrix}
=v\cdot(u\times w)=v\cdot v=1.
$$

Thus the [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $A_v$ with these columns belongs to the [special orthogonal group](../../../../../../../special-orthogonal-group.md) $G$ and satisfies $A_vN=v$. For any $v,z\in S$, the element $A_zA_v^{-1}$ sends $v$ to $z$. Therefore the [group action](../../../../../../../group-action-split.md) on the [unit sphere](../../../../../../../unit-sphere.md) is **transitive**.
