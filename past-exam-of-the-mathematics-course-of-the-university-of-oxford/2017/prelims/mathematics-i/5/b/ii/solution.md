<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With products acting from right to left, a [permutation cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) satisfies

$$
(a_1\ a_2\ \cdots\ a_k)
=(a_1\ a_k)(a_1\ a_{k-1})\cdots(a_1\ a_2).
$$

The right side sends $a_1$ to $a_2$, each $a_j$ to $a_{j+1}$ for $2\leq j<k$, and $a_k$ to $a_1$, while fixing all other symbols. This verifies the formula. Applying it to every cycle in the [disjoint cycle decomposition](../../../../../../../disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md) expresses any [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) as a product of [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md). The identity is represented by the empty product.
