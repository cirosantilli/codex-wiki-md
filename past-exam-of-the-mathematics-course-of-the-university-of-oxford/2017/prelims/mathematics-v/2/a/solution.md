<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a bounded region with a piecewise smooth closed boundary, and let $\mathbf F$ be a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) on an open neighbourhood of its closure. With $\mathbf n$ the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../unit-normal.md), the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\iiint_\Omega\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=\iint_{\partial\Omega}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dA.}
$$

The boundary [surface integral](../../../../../../surface-integral.md) is the total outward [flux](../../../../../../flux.md). All boundary components are included; on the boundary of a cavity, the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) points into the cavity.
