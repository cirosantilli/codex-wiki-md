<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Integrate the [steady state](../../../../../../../steady-state.md) equation over $R$ and use the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md):

$$
0=\iint_{\partial R}k\nabla T\cdot\mathbf n\,dA+\rho\iiint_R S(\mathbf x)\,dV.
$$

The zero [heat flux](../../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) condition makes the boundary term vanish. But $\rho>0$ and $S>0$ throughout a region of positive [volume](../../../../../../../volume.md), so

$$
\rho\iiint_R S\,dV>0,
$$

a contradiction. Thus **no steady-state solution exists**. This is the [insulated steady heat balance](../../../../../../../insulated-steady-heat-balance.md): positive internal [heat](../../../../../../../heat-split.md) generation continually increases the stored thermal [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) when no [heat](../../../../../../../heat-split.md) can escape.
