<h1 id="5/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The finite [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) identity gives, for each $|x|<1$,

$$
f_n(x)=\frac{x(1-x^n)}{1-x}\longrightarrow f(x)=\frac{x}{1-x},\qquad f(x)-f_n(x)=\frac{x^{n+1}}{1-x}.
$$

For every fixed $n$, the [absolute value](../../../../../../../absolute-value.md) of this error tends to infinity as $x\to1^-$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\sup_{-1<x<1}|f(x)-f_n(x)|=\infty,}
$$

and there is **no [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md)** on $(-1,1)$.
