↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Set $l=\inf A$ and $v=\inf\{\inf R_m:m\geq1\}$. Because $R_m\subseteq A$, every row [infimum](../../../../../../../infimum.md) satisfies $\inf R_m\geq l$, so $v\geq l$. But $x_{m,n}\geq\inf R_m\geq v$ for all $m,n$, making $v$ a [lower bound](../../../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of $A$. Therefore $v\leq l$, and

$$
\boxed{\inf A=\inf_{m\geq1}\inf_{n\geq1}x_{m,n}.}
$$
