↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Write $s=\sup A$ and $u=\sup\{\sup R_m:m\geq1\}$. All these [suprema](../../../../../../../supremum.md) exist in $[0,1]$. Since $R_m\subseteq A$, we have $\sup R_m\leq s$ for every $m$, hence $u\leq s$. Conversely, every $x_{m,n}\leq\sup R_m\leq u$, so $u$ is an [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of $A$ and $s\leq u$. Thus the [iterated extrema of an array](../../../../../../../iterated-extrema-of-an-array.md) satisfy

$$
\boxed{\sup A=\sup_{m\geq1}\sup_{n\geq1}x_{m,n}.}
$$
