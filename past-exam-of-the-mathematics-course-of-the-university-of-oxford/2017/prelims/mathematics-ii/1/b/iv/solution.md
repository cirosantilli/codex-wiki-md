**False.** Take $x_{1,n}=0$ for every $n$, and $x_{m,n}=1$ for every $m\geq2$ and every $n$. Each row is constant, so its [infimum](../../../../../../../infimum.md) and [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) coincide. Nevertheless,

$$
\boxed{\inf_m\sup R_m=0<1=\sup_m\inf R_m.}
$$

The two outer [extrema](../../../../../../../maximum-and-minimum.md) can select different rows.
