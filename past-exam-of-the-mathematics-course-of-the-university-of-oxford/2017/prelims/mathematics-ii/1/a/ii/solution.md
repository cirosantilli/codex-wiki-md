<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $M=\max\{\sup A,\sup B\}$. Every element of the [union](../../../../../../../set-union.md) $A\cup B$ is at most $M$, so $M$ is an [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Conversely, every [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of $A\cup B$ is an [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of each of $A$ and $B$, hence is at least both [suprema](../../../../../../../supremum.md) and therefore at least $M$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\sup(A\cup B)=\max\{\sup A,\sup B\}.}
$$
