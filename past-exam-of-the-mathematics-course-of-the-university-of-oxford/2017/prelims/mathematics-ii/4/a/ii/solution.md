<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $u<v$ lie in $f([a,b])$, and put $x=g(u)$ and $y=g(v)$. Equality $x=y$ would imply $u=v$. If $x>y$, strict [monotonicity](../../../../../../../monotonic-function.md) of $f$ would give $u=f(x)>f(y)=v$. Both are impossible, so $x<y$. Therefore the [inverse function](../../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $g$ is **[strictly increasing](../../../../../../../strictly-increasing-function.md)**.
