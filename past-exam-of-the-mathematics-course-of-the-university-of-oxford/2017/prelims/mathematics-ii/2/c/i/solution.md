↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Only (ii) is necessarily true.** Every real $a_n$ satisfies $a_n^2\geq0>c$, so (ii) holds automatically. For a single counterexample to (i) and (iii), choose $c=-3$ and $a_0=1$. The [recurrence relation](../../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) then gives the nonzero periodic [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md)

$$
a_{2j}=1,\qquad a_{2j+1}=-1\qquad(j\geq0).
$$

Here $a_1<0$, disproving (i), and $a_2>a_1$, disproving (iii). This counterexample satisfies the requirement that no iterate is zero.
