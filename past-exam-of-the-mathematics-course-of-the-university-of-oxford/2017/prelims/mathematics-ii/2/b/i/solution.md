<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Rewrite the [recurrence relation](../../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) as

$$
a_{n+1}=\frac12\left(a_n+\frac c{a_n}\right).
$$

Since $a_0=1>0$ and $c>0$, a positive $a_n$ gives a positive $a_{n+1}$. [Mathematical induction](../../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) therefore proves $\boxed{a_n>0\text{ for all }n\geq0}$, and also shows that every division in the [recurrence relation](../../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) is defined.
