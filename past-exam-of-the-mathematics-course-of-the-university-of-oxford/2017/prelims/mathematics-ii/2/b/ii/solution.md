<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every $n\geq0$, direct algebra gives

$$
a_{n+1}^2-c=\frac{(a_n^2+c)^2-4ca_n^2}{4a_n^2}=\frac{(a_n^2-c)^2}{4a_n^2}\geq0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{a_n^2\geq c\text{ for every }n\geq1}$. This identity is the key estimate in [Newton iteration for a square root](../../../../../../../newton-iteration-for-a-square-root.md).
