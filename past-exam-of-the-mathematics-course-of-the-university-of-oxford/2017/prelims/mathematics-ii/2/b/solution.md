<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The tail $(a_n)_{n\geq1}$ is a [monotone sequence](../../../../../../monotone-sequence.md) bounded below by zero. The [bounded monotone sequence theorem](../../../../../../bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md) therefore gives $a_n\to L\geq0$. Taking the [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) in $a_n^2\geq c$ yields $L^2\geq c>0$, so $L>0$. The reciprocal rule from part (a) permits passage to the [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) in the [recurrence relation](../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md):

$$
L=\frac12\left(L+\frac cL\right).
$$

Multiplication by $2L$ gives

$$
\boxed{L>0,\qquad L^2=c.}
$$

This proves existence of the positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) without assuming that existence when constructing the [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md).
