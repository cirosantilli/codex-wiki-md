<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $(a_n)$ has [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) $L$ if

$$
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\ \forall n\geq N:\quad |a_n-L|<\varepsilon.
$$

For the reciprocal rule, the required condition is $\boxed{L\ne0}$. Indeed, eventually $|a_n-L|<|L|/2$, so $|a_n|\geq|L|/2>0$. The reciprocals are then defined on this tail, and

$$
\left|\frac1{a_n}-\frac1L\right|=\frac{|a_n-L|}{|a_n||L|}\leq\frac{2|a_n-L|}{|L|^2}\longrightarrow0.
$$

A finite number of zero initial terms does not affect this tail [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md); if a reciprocal [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) is required at every index, those terms must be excluded or assigned arbitrary values separately.
