<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [b](../b.md)

Use the [point](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) $\mathbf p_0=(-2,-2,4)$ and the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of directions in the cutting [plane](../../../../../../plane.md)

$$
\mathbf e_1=\frac{(1,-1,0)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\mathbf e_2=\frac{(1,1,-1)}{\sqrt3}.
$$

Both [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to the [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) $(1,1,2)$, so every [point](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) of the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) has unique [coordinates](../../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md)

$$
(x,y,z)=\mathbf p_0+s\mathbf e_1+t\mathbf e_2.
$$

Substituting into the [cone](../../../../../../cone-geometry.md) [equation](../../../../../../equation.md) gives

$$
x^2+y^2-z^2=-8+s^2+\frac{t^2}{3}.
$$

Consequently the intersection has [equation](../../../../../../equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{t^2}{24}+\frac{s^2}{8}=1.}
$$

Because these are [orthonormal coordinates](../../../../../../orthonormal-coordinates.md), their lengths are the actual lengths in the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md). Thus this is an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) with [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) $\sqrt{24}$ and [semi-minor axis](../../../../../../semi-minor-axis.md) $\sqrt8$, not merely a projected [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md). By [a](../a.md), its [eccentricity](../../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) is

$$
\boxed{e=\sqrt{1-\frac8{24}}=\sqrt{\frac23}.}
$$
