<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [a](../a.md)

For a fixed [point](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) $F$ not on a fixed [line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) $D$, the [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) with [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) $F$, [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) $D$ and [eccentricity](../../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) $0<e<1$ is the [locus](../../../../../../locus-mathematics.md) of [points](../../../../../../point-geometry.md) $P$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{dist}(P,F)=e\operatorname{dist}(P,D).}
$$

Set $c=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}$ and $e=c/a$. One matched [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) and [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) pair is

$$
\boxed{F=(c,0),\qquad D:\ x=\frac{a^2}{c},\qquad e=\sqrt{1-\frac{b^2}{a^2}}.}
$$

Indeed the squared [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) condition is

$$
(x-c)^2+y^2=\frac{c^2}{a^2}\left(x-\frac{a^2}{c}\right)^2.
$$

Expanding and cancelling the terms linear in $x$ gives

$$
\left(1-\frac{c^2}{a^2}\right)x^2+y^2=a^2-c^2=b^2,
$$

which is exactly $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2=1$. Both sides of the unsquared [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) condition are nonnegative, so squaring introduces no extra [points](../../../../../../point-geometry.md). Reflecting across the $y$-axis gives the other matched pair: $F=(-c,0)$ and $D:x=-a^2/c$, with the same [eccentricity](../../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md).
