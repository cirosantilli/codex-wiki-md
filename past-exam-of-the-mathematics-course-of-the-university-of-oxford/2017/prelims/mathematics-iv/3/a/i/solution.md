↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [ellipsoid](../../../../../../../ellipsoid.md) is the [level set](../../../../../../../level-set.md) $F(x,y,z)=2x^2+y^2+z^2=1$. Its [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) is $(4x,2y,2z)$, which never vanishes on the [ellipsoid](../../../../../../../ellipsoid.md). Half this [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) gives the outward [normal vector](../../../../../../../normal-vector.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n(\theta,\phi)=(\sqrt2\cos\theta,\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi).}
$$

This [normal vector](../../../../../../../normal-vector.md) remains valid at $\theta=0,\pi$, where the given [parametrization](../../../../../../../parametric-equation.md) is singular.
