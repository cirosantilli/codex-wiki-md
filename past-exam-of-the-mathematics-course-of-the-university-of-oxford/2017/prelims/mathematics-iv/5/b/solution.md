<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [b](../b.md)

Far from the planet, the magnitude of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is the magnitude of the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) of [position vector](../../../../../../position-vector.md) and [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). The perpendicular distance from the incoming [asymptote](../../../../../../asymptote.md) is the [impact parameter](../../../../../../impact-parameter.md) $d$, so

$$
\boxed{h=dv.}
$$

Choose $\theta=0$ at closest approach. The general solution of the [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) in [a](../a.md), with $u'(0)=0$, is

$$
u=\frac\mu{h^2}(1+e\cos\theta).
$$

The [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is

$$
\varepsilon=\frac12(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2)-\frac\mu r
=\frac{h^2}2(u'^2+u^2)-\mu u
=\frac{\mu^2}{2h^2}(e^2-1).
$$

At infinity $\varepsilon=v^2/2$, giving $e=\sqrt{1+h^2v^2/\mu^2}>1$. Thus the [hyperbolic orbit](../../../../../../hyperbolic-orbit.md) has

$$
\boxed{u(\theta)=\frac{GM}{d^2v^2}\left[1+\sqrt{1+\frac{d^2v^4}{G^2M^2}}\cos\theta\right].}
$$

A different zero of [polar angle](../../../../../../polar-angle.md) simply replaces $\theta$ by $\theta-\theta_0$.

The incoming and outgoing [asymptotes](../../../../../../asymptote.md) have radial directions $-\theta_\infty$ and $\theta_\infty$, where $\cos\theta_\infty=-1/e$ and $\pi/2<\theta_\infty<\pi$. The incoming [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) points opposite its radial direction, so the [deflection angle](../../../../../../deflection-angle.md) between incoming and outgoing [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md) is $\delta=2\theta_\infty-\pi$. Hence

$$
\sin\frac\delta2=\frac1e,\qquad
\tan\frac\delta2=\frac{GM}{dv^2}.
$$

Putting $\delta=\pi/2$ gives

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{GM}{v^2}.}
$$
