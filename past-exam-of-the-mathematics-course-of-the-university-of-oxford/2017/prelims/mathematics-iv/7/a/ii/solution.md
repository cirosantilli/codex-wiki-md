<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $P=3x^4-5x^3+2x^2-3x+2$ and $Q=3x^3+x^2+x-2$. Applying [polynomial long division](../../../../../../../polynomial-long-division.md) gives the [euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md)

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=(x-2)Q+(3x^2+x-2),\\
Q&=x(3x^2+x-2)+(3x-2),\\
3x^2+x-2&=(x+1)(3x-2).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus their [polynomial greatest common divisor](../../../../../../../polynomial-greatest-common-divisor.md) is $3x-2$, up to a nonzero constant factor. Their unique common [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac23.}
$$
