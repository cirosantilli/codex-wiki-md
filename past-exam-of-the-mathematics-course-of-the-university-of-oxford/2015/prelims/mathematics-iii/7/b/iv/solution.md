<h1 id="7/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [recurrence relation](../../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) at $k=3$ gives $I_5=2\theta I_3=2\theta^2$. Consequently

$$
\operatorname{var}(X_i^2)=\mathbb E[X_i^4]-\bigl(\mathbb E[X_i^2]\bigr)^2=I_5-I_3^2=2\theta^2-\theta^2=\theta^2.
$$

By [variance additivity for independent random variables](../../../../../../../variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md), the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) of their [sample mean](../../../../../../../sample-mean.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(\widehat\theta)=\frac{n\theta^2}{n^2}=\frac{\theta^2}{n}.}
$$
