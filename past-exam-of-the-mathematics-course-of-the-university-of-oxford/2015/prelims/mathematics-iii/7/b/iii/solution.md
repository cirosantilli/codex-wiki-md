↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Be careful about the index: the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) already contains a factor $x$, so $\mathbb E[X_i^r]=I_{r+1}$. The [recurrence relation](../../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) at $k=1$ yields $I_3=\theta I_1=\theta$. Therefore [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[\widehat\theta]=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E[X_i^2]=I_3=\theta.}
$$

Thus **$\widehat\theta$ is an [unbiased estimator](../../../../../../../unbiased-estimator.md)**.
