For the specified [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md), the [exponential series](../../../../../../../exponential-series.md) gives the [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md)

$$
G(s)=e^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{s^j}{j!}=\boxed{e^{s-1},\qquad s\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Every [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of $G$ is again $e^{s-1}$, so the [factorial moments](../../../../../../../factorial-moment.md) and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[(X)_k]=1\quad(k\geq1),\qquad \operatorname{var}(X)=1+1-1=1.}
$$

Also $\mathbb E[X]=1$.
