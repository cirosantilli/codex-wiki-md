<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $X^2=X(X-1)+X$, the second [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) is $\mathbb E[X^2]=G''(1)+G'(1)$. Subtracting the squared [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=G''(1)+G'(1)-\bigl(G'(1)\bigr)^2.}
$$
