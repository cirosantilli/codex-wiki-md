<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a nonnegative integer-valued [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md), its [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{G(s)=\mathbb E[s^X]=\sum_{j=0}^\infty p(j)s^j.}
$$

The convention $s^0=1$, including at $s=0$, gives $G(0)=p(0)$. The [power series](../../../../../../../power-series.md) has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) for every real $|s|\leq1$, since $\sum_jp(j)|s|^j\leq1$. More generally its real domain as a finite [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is $\{s\in\mathbb R:\sum_jp(j)|s|^j<\infty\}$, which may extend beyond $[-1,1]$. No extension beyond that guaranteed [interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) is automatic.
