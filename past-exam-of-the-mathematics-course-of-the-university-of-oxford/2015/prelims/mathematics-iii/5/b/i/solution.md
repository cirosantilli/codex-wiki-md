<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each of the $n$ distinct elements can receive any of the $x$ labelled colours. The [multiplication principle](../../../../../../../rule-of-product.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{x^n}
$$

colour assignments; colours are allowed to remain unused.
