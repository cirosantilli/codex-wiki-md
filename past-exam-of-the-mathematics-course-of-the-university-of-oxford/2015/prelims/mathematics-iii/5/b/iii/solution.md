<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Substitute $X$ into the [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) identity and apply [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) to its finite sum. The [factorial moments](../../../../../../../factorial-moment.md) of the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) in part (a)(iv) are all one, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X^n]=\sum_{k=1}^n A(n,k)\mathbb E[(X)_k]=\sum_{k=1}^n A(n,k)=B(n).}
$$

Thus its $n$th [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) counts [set partitions](../../../../../../../set-partition.md): it equals the $n$th [Bell number](../../../../../../../bell-number.md).
