<h1 id="5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Computing the same [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) directly from the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) instead gives

$$
\boxed{B(n)=\mathbb E[X^n]=\sum_{j=0}^\infty j^n\frac{e^{-1}}{j!}=\frac1e\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{j^n}{j!}.}
$$

For each fixed positive integer $n$, the [ratio test](../../../../../../../ratio-test.md) shows that this sum converges. This identity is [Dobiński's formula](../../../../../../../dobinski-s-formula.md). The case $n=0$ also gives $B(0)=1$ if $0^0$ is interpreted as one in this particular [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) formula.
