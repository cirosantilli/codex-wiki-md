<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) has [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) zero and second [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) $2/3$, so its [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) is $2/3$. For $S_m=\sum_{i=1}^mX_i$, [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and [variance additivity for independent random variables](../../../../../../../variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md) give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[S_m]=0,\qquad \operatorname{var}(S_m)=\frac{2m}{3}.}
$$
