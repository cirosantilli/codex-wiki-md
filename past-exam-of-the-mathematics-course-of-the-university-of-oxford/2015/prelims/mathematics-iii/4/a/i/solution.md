↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [events](../../../../../../../event.md) $B_1,B_2,\ldots$ form a [partition of a sample space](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-sample-space.md) $\Omega$ when they are pairwise [disjoint sets](../../../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) and cover the whole [sample space](../../../../../../../sample-space.md):

$$
\boxed{B_i\cap B_j=\varnothing\ (i\ne j),\qquad \bigcup_{i\geq1}B_i=\Omega.}
$$

Thus exactly one of these [events](../../../../../../../event.md) occurs on every outcome. Under the usual [set partition](../../../../../../../set-partition.md) convention the parts are nonempty; empty parts can simply be discarded.
