<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) for $y>0$, using $\Phi'(z)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-z^2/2}$ and $d\sqrt{2y}/dy=1/\sqrt{2y}$. The [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_Y(y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{e^{-y}}{\sqrt{\pi y}},&y>0,\\0,&y<0.\end{cases}}
$$

Its value at zero can be assigned arbitrarily without changing any [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md). Although this [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) diverges as $y\downarrow0$, $F_Y(0)=0$ and there is no [point mass](../../../../../../../point-mass.md) at zero. Equivalently, $Y$ has a [gamma distribution](../../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md) with shape $1/2$ and rate one.
