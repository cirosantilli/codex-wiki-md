↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The second [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[X^2]=\int_0^b ax^3\,dx=\frac{ab^4}{4}=\frac{b^2}{2}=\frac98.
$$

Using the [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\mathbb E[X]=1$, the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=\frac98-1=\frac18.}
$$
