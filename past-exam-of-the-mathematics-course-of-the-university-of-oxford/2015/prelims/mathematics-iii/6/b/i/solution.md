↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) and the specified [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) give two equations:

$$
1=\int_0^b ax\,dx=\frac{ab^2}{2},\qquad 1=\mathbb E[X]=\int_0^b ax^2\,dx=\frac{ab^3}{3}.
$$

Dividing the second by the first gives $2b/3=1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{b=\frac32,\qquad a=\frac{2}{b^2}=\frac89.}
$$

Both are positive, as required for the stated [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md).
