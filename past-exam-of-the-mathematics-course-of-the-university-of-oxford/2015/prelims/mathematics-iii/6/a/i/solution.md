↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) with a [continuous probability distribution](../../../../../../../continuous-probability-distribution-split.md) and [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f_X$, the [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mu=\mathbb E[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x f_X(x)\,dx,\qquad \operatorname{var}(X)=\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^2]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(x-\mu)^2f_X(x)\,dx.}
$$

A finite [mean](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) requires $\int |x|f_X(x)\,dx<\infty$. A finite [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) additionally requires the second [moment](../../../../../../../moment.md) to be finite; without that condition the nonnegative integral for the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) may be infinite.
