<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an [oriented surface](../../../../../../oriented-surface.md) $\Sigma$ with piecewise smooth closed [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) $\partial\Sigma$, and a [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ that is [continuously differentiable](../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) on an open neighborhood of $\Sigma$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\iint_\Sigma(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\oint_{\partial\Sigma}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r.}
$$

Here $\mathbf n$ is the chosen [unit normal vector](../../../../../../unit-normal.md). The [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of $\partial\Sigma$ follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md): with the right thumb pointing along $\mathbf n$, the fingers curl in the positive direction. Equivalently, walking along the [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) with one's head along $\mathbf n$ leaves the [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) on the left. Each [boundary component](../../../../../../boundary-component.md) receives this induced [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
