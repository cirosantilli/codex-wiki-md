<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

When $\alpha=n$, the reduced [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) is $F''=0$. The [initial conditions](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md) $F(0)=0$, $F'(0)=1$ give

$$
\boxed{F(t)=t,\qquad y(x,t)=te^{-nt}\sin(nx).}
$$

This is the [critically damped](../../../../../../../critical-damping.md) [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md), also obtained by taking $\nu\to0$ in $\sin(\nu t)/\nu$.
