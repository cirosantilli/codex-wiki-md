<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the same difference $w$ and [wave energy](../../../../../../../wave-energy.md) as in part (i). The [damped wave equation](../../../../../../../damped-wave-equation.md) now gives the [energy dissipation identity for a linearly damped string](../../../../../../../energy-dissipation-identity-for-a-linearly-damped-string.md)

$$
\boxed{E'(t)=\int_0^\pi w_t(w_{tt}-w_{xx})\,dx
=-2\alpha\int_0^\pi w_t^2\,dx\leq0.}
$$

Because $E(0)=0$ and $E(t)\geq0$, the [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) remains zero. Hence $w_t=w_x=0$, and the homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) force $w=0$. Therefore the [solution of a differential equation](../../../../../../../solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) is unique also for $\boxed{\alpha>0}$; positive [damping](../../../../../../../damping.md) can only decrease this [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md).
