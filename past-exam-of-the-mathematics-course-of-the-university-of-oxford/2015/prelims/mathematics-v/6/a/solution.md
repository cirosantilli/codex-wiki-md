<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Conservation of [thermal energy](../../../../../../thermal-energy.md) and [Fourier's law](../../../../../../fourier-s-law.md) give the [heat equations](../../../../../../heat-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho_1c_1\,T_{1t}=k_1T_{1xx}\quad(-L<x<0),\qquad
\rho_2c_2\,T_{2t}=k_2T_{2xx}\quad(0<x<L).}
$$

The [density](../../../../../../density.md) times the [specific heat capacity](../../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md), $\rho_i c_i$, is the [volumetric heat capacity](../../../../../../volumetric-heat-capacity.md), and $k_i$ is the [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md).

The condition $T_1(-L,t)=0$ holds the left exterior face at a fixed zero reference [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md); $T_2(L,t)=0$ does the same for the right exterior face. The condition $T_1(0_-,t)=T_2(0_+,t)$ expresses perfect [thermal contact](../../../../../../thermal-contact.md), with no [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) jump at the interface. Finally,

$$
-k_1T_{1x}(0_-,t)=-k_2T_{2x}(0_+,t)
$$

expresses continuity of the signed [heat flux](../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) in the positive $x$ direction: the interface neither stores nor produces [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md).
