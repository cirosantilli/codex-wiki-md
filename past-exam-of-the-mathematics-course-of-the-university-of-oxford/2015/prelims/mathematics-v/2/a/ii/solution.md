<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) to each [vector component](../../../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) and sum:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot(\phi\mathbf F)
&=\sum_{j=1}^3\partial_{x_j}(\phi F_j)\\
&=\phi\sum_{j=1}^3\partial_{x_j}F_j+\sum_{j=1}^3(\partial_{x_j}\phi)F_j\\
&=\boxed{\phi\nabla\cdot\mathbf F+\nabla\phi\cdot\mathbf F.}
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) of a [scalar field](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md) times a [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) contains a [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) term and a [dot product](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) of the [scalar field](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md).
