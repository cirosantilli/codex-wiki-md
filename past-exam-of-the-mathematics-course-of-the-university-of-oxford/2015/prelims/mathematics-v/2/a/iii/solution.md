<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose $\mathbf F=\nabla\psi$ in the preceding [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) and apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md). Since $\nabla\cdot\nabla\psi=\Delta\psi$, this gives the first of [Green's identities](../../../../../../../green-s-identities.md):

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V\bigl(\phi\Delta\psi+\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\psi\bigr),dV
=\iint_{\partial V}\phi\,\partial_n\psi\,dS.}
$$

Here $\partial_n\psi=\mathbf n\cdot\nabla\psi$ is the outward [normal derivative](../../../../../../../normal-derivative.md). Interchange $\phi$ and $\psi$, then subtract. The [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) [dot products](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) cancel, yielding the second of [Green's identities](../../../../../../../green-s-identities.md):

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V(\phi\Delta\psi-\psi\Delta\phi)\,dV
=\iint_{\partial V}(\phi\partial_n\psi-\psi\partial_n\phi)\,dS.}
$$

For example, $\phi,\psi\in C^2(\overline V)$ with the needed extensions suffice for these applications of the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md).
