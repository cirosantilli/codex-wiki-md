↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [continuously differentiable vector field](../../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ on a neighborhood of the closure of $V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.}
$$

Here $\mathbf n$ is the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md); the right side is the outward [flux](../../../../../../../flux.md) through the [boundary](../../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md).
