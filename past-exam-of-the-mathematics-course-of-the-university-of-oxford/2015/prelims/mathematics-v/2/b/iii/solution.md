<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** Take $V=\{x^2+y^2+z^2<1\}$, the open [unit ball](../../../../../../../unit-ball.md), and $u(x,y,z)=x$. This is a nonzero [harmonic function](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md). On the [unit sphere](../../../../../../../unit-sphere.md), the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) is $\mathbf n=(x,y,z)$, so

$$
\partial_nu=\mathbf n\cdot(1,0,0)=x=u.
$$

Thus the prescribed [Robin boundary condition](../../../../../../../robin-boundary-condition.md) holds. Unlike the preceding case, [Green's identities](../../../../../../../green-s-identities.md) now give $\int_V|\nabla u|^2=\int_{\partial V}u^2$, which permits nonzero solutions of the [differential equation](../../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md).
