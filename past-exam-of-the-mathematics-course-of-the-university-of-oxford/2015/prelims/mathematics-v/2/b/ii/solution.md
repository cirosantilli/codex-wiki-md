<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** The first of [Green's identities](../../../../../../../green-s-identities.md) and the [Robin boundary condition](../../../../../../../robin-boundary-condition.md) $\partial_nu=-u$ give

$$
\iiint_V|\nabla u|^2\,dV=-\iint_{\partial V}u^2\,dS,
\qquad
\boxed{\iiint_V|\nabla u|^2\,dV+\iint_{\partial V}u^2\,dS=0.}
$$

Both [integrands](../../../../../../../integrand.md) are nonnegative, so $\nabla u=0$ in $V$ and $u=0$ on the [boundary](../../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md). By [path connectedness](../../../../../../../path-connected-space.md), $u$ is a [constant function](../../../../../../../constant-function.md), and its [boundary value](../../../../../../../boundary-value.md) makes that constant zero. Therefore $\boxed{u=0}$.
