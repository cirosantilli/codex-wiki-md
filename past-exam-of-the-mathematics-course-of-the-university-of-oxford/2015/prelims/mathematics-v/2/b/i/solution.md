↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** The [constant function](../../../../../../../constant-function.md) $u=1$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) and has zero [normal derivative](../../../../../../../normal-derivative.md) on every smooth [boundary](../../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md), but it is nonzero in $V$.

In fact, the first of [Green's identities](../../../../../../../green-s-identities.md) gives

$$
\iiint_V|\nabla u|^2\,dV=\iint_{\partial V}u\partial_nu\,dS-\iiint_Vu\Delta u\,dV=0.
$$

Hence $\nabla u=0$; [path connectedness](../../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) makes $u$ constant throughout $V$. Thus this homogeneous [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) determines a [harmonic function](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) only up to an additive constant.
