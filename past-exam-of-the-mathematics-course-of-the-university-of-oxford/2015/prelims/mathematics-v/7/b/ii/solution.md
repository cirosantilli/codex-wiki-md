<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $r=R$ in the enclosed [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) formula:

$$
\boxed{Q=4\pi\rho_0a^3\left[\frac13+\frac{h(1+\delta)-h(1)}{\delta}\right].}
$$

Outside the entire [charge distribution](../../../../../../../charge-distribution.md), [Gauss's law](../../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $E_r(r)=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$. Integrating $E_r=-\varphi'$ with $\varphi(\infty)=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\varphi(r)=\int_r^\infty\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0s^2}\,ds
=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r},\qquad r>R.}
$$

Thus the exterior [electric potential](../../../../../../../electric-potential.md) and [electric field](../../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) are exactly those of a [point charge](../../../../../../../point-charge.md) $Q$ placed at the center. This is the [electrostatic shell theorem](../../../../../../../electrostatic-shell-theorem.md): outside a [spherically symmetric charge distribution](../../../../../../../spherically-symmetric-charge-distribution.md), only its total [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) affects the [electric field](../../../../../../../electric-field-split.md), not how that [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is distributed radially. If positive and negative [electric charges](../../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) cancel to give $Q=0$, both exterior quantities vanish.
