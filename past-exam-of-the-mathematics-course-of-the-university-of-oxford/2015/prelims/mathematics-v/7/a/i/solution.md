<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For any bounded [volume](../../../../../../../volume.md) $V$ with piecewise smooth closed [boundary](../../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) and outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$, [Gauss's law](../../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) in integral form is

$$
\boxed{\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\frac{Q_V}{\epsilon_0},\qquad Q_V=\iiint_V\rho\,dV.}
$$

Thus the outward [electric flux](../../../../../../../electric-flux.md) equals the enclosed [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) divided by the [vacuum permittivity](../../../../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md). To prove it, apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to the [electric field](../../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and use its given [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md):

$$
\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf E\,dV
=\frac1{\epsilon_0}\iiint_V\rho\,dV.
$$

The classical proof assumes the requisite differentiability of the [electric field](../../../../../../../electric-field-split.md). For piecewise smooth [charge densities](../../../../../../../charge-density.md), one may apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) piece by piece and cancel the internal [fluxes](../../../../../../../flux.md).
