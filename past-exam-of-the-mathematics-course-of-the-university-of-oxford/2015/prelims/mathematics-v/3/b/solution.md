<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At a fixed $z\in[-1,1]$, both [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) inequalities become

$$
|x|\leq\sqrt{1-z^2},\qquad |y|\leq\sqrt{1-z^2}.
$$

Thus the [cross section](../../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) is a [square](../../../../../../square.md) of side $2\sqrt{1-z^2}$ and [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) $4(1-z^2)$. Integrating these [cross-sectional areas](../../../../../../cross-sectional-area.md) gives the [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) of this [bicylinder](../../../../../../bicylinder.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Vol}(R_1)=\int_{-1}^{1}4(1-z^2)\,dz
=4\left[z-\frac{z^3}{3}\right]_{-1}^{1}=\frac{16}{3}.}
$$
