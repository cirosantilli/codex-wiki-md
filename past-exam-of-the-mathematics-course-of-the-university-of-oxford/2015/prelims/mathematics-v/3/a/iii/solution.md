<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Parametrize the finite [cylindrical surface](../../../../../../../cylindrical-surface.md) patch by

$$
\mathbf R(t,z)=(\cos t,\sin t,z),\qquad-\pi\leq t\leq\pi,\quad0\leq z\leq\cosh t.
$$

The [surface area element](../../../../../../../surface-area-element.md) is $|\mathbf R_t\times\mathbf R_z|\,dt\,dz=dt\,dz$. The seam $t=\pm\pi$ has zero [surface area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md), so it causes no overcounting. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\int_0^{\cosh t}dz\,dt=2\sinh\pi.}
$$

Unrolling the unit [circular cylinder](../../../../../../../circular-cylinder.md) preserves [surface area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md), which explains the equality with the [area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md) under the [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) in the figure. The patch consists of the [cylindrical surface](../../../../../../../cylindrical-surface.md) alone; no flat base is included.
