↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [velocity vector](../../../../../../../velocity-vector.md) of the [parametric curve](../../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) is

$$
\mathbf r'(t)=(-\sin t,\cos t,\sinh t).
$$

Its [speed](../../../../../../../speed.md) is

$$
|\mathbf r'(t)|=\sqrt{1+\sinh^2t}=\cosh t,
$$

where $\cosh t>0$ fixes the sign of the square root. Hence the [arc length](../../../../../../../arc-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ell(C)=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\cosh t\,dt=2\sinh\pi.}
$$

The coincidence of the endpoints does not duplicate any positive [arc length](../../../../../../../arc-length.md).
