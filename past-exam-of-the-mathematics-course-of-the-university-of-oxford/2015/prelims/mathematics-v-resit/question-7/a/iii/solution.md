<h1 id="question-7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a volume source of constant [mass density](../../../../../../../density.md) $\rho$, use $dm=\rho\,dV'$. Its [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) is the [volume integral](../../../../../../../volume-integral.md)

$$
\boxed{\phi(\mathbf r)=-G\rho\iiint_V\frac{dV'}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|},\qquad\phi(\infty)=0.}
$$

The source and observation [position vectors](../../../../../../../position-vector.md) are $\mathbf r'$ and $\mathbf r$, respectively; $G$ is the [gravitational constant](../../../../../../../gravitational-constant.md). With this sign convention positive source [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md) gives negative [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md).
