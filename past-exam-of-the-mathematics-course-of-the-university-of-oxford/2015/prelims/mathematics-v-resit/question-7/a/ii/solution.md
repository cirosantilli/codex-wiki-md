<h1 id="question-7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the surface, a source element has [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md) $dm=\sigma\,dS'$. Superposing its [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) gives the [surface integral](../../../../../../../surface-integral.md)

$$
\boxed{\phi(\mathbf r)=-G\sigma\iint_S\frac{dS'}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}.}
$$

Here $dS'$ is the source [surface area element](../../../../../../../surface-area-element.md), $\mathbf r'$ the source [position vector](../../../../../../../position-vector.md), and $G$ the [gravitational constant](../../../../../../../gravitational-constant.md). The same zero-at-infinity convention applies.
