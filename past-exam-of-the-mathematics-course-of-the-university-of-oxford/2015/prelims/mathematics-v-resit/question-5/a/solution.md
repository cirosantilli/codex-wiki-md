<h1 id="question-5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume $\rho$ is [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) per unit equilibrium [length](../../../../../../length.md), the [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) has constant magnitude $T$, the string has negligible bending stiffness, and there is no damping or external transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). Assume small slopes $|y_x|\ll1$ and purely transverse motion. Then a short segment has [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $\rho\Delta x$ to first order. If $\alpha$ is the angle of its tangent, the small-slope approximation gives $\sin\alpha\simeq\tan\alpha=y_x$.

The vertical components of [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) at the two ends give net transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) $T[y_x(x+\Delta x,t)-y_x(x,t)]$. [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) therefore gives

$$
\rho\Delta x\,y_{tt}
=T\bigl[y_x(x+\Delta x,t)-y_x(x,t)\bigr]
$$

to first order. Dividing by $\Delta x$ and taking its [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) to zero yields

$$
\boxed{y_{tt}=c^2y_{xx},\qquad c=\sqrt{\frac{T}{\rho}}.}
$$

Thus $c$ is the propagation [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) of small transverse [waves](../../../../../../wave-split.md).
