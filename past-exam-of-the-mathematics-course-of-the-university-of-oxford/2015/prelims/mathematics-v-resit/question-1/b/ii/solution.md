<h1 id="question-1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Here a [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) means a single-valued [scalar field](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md) $\psi$ with $\nabla\psi=\mathbf F$. By the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md), its [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) around every closed [curve](../../../../../../../curve.md) must vanish. Here the [angular circulation obstruction to a cylindrical potential](../../../../../../../angular-circulation-obstruction-to-a-cylindrical-potential.md) lets us test this using a [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) about the axis.

On $V_1$, the [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) from [i](../i.md) lies entirely in the region and has [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) $4\sqrt2$. Therefore **there is no [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) on $V_1$**.

On $V_2$, choose the continuous angular branch $-\pi<\theta<\pi$. By [a](../../a.md), the equation for a [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) reduces to $\psi_r=\psi_z=0$ and $\psi_\theta=\sqrt2\cos(\theta/2)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\psi=2\sqrt2\sin(\theta/2)+C
=\frac{2y}{\sqrt{r(r+x)}}+C,\qquad r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\quad\text{on }V_2.}
$$

The denominator is positive throughout $V_2$, including when $y=0$ and $x>0$, so this is a well-defined differentiable [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) on the entire region.

For $V_3$, choose the particular [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) $x=2\cos\theta$, $y=2\sin\theta$, $z=0$. Its only point on the removed half-plane's supporting cut is $(-2,0,0)$, where $(x+2)^2+z^2=0<1$; this point is retained by $V_3$. Thus the whole [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) lies in $V_3$ and again has [line integral](../../../../../../../line-integral.md) $4\sqrt2$. Consequently **there is no [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) on $V_3$**. The opening in the cut allows a closed [curve](../../../../../../../curve.md) to go around the excluded axis.

Finally $V_4\subset V_2$. Restricting the [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) already constructed on $V_2$ gives

$$
\boxed{\psi=\frac{2y}{\sqrt{r(r+x)}}+C\quad\text{on }V_4.}
$$

The extra excluded [cylinder](../../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) does not invalidate an existing [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md). Thus being [simply connected](../../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) is a sufficient condition in the usual [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md)-free criterion, not a necessary condition for a particular [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) to have a [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md).
