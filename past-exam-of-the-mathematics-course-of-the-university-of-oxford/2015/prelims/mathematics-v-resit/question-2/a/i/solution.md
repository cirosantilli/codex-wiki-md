<h1 id="question-2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a bounded region $V\subset\mathbb R^3$ with piecewise smooth boundary, suppose $\mathbf F$ is a [continuously differentiable vector field](../../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-vector-field.md) on an open neighbourhood of its [closure](../../../../../../../closure-topology.md). The [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md), with outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$, is

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV
=\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.}
$$

The [surface integral](../../../../../../../surface-integral.md) measures total outward [flux](../../../../../../../flux.md). Edges and isolated vertices have zero [surface area](../../../../../../../surface-area.md) and do not affect the [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md).
