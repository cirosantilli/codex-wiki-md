<h1 id="question-2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), write the constant [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) as $\mathbf c=(c_1,c_2,c_3)$. The [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) and constancy of its components give

$$
\nabla\cdot(\phi\mathbf c)
=\partial_x(c_1\phi)+\partial_y(c_2\phi)+\partial_z(c_3\phi)
=c_1\phi_x+c_2\phi_y+c_3\phi_z.
$$

The last expression is the [dot product](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md), so

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(\phi\mathbf c)=\mathbf c\cdot\nabla\phi.}
$$
