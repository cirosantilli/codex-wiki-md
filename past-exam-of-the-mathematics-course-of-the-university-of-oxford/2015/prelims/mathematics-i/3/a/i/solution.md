<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose the [rotation](../../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) taking $e_1$ to $e_2$, $e_2$ to $-e_1$ and $e_3$ to itself. The columns of the [matrix of a linear map](../../../../../../../matrix-representation-of-a-linear-map.md) are these images of the [standard basis](../../../../../../../standard-basis.md), so

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This fixes the orientation used throughout this question.
