<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using the convention $\chi_T(x)=\det(xI-A)$, the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi_T(x)=(x-1)\det\begin{pmatrix}x&1\\-1&x\end{pmatrix}=(x-1)(x^2+1).}
$$

Since $x^2+1>0$ for every $x\in\mathbb R$, the only [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) over the [real numbers](../../../../../../../real-number.md) is $1$. Solving $(A-I)v=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{E_1=\ker(A-I)=\operatorname{span}\{(0,0,1)\}.}
$$

Thus the only [eigenspace](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) over the [real numbers](../../../../../../../real-number.md) is the axis of the [rotation](../../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md).
