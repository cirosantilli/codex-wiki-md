Here $\dim V=2$. By the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md), $r(T)+n(T)=2$, and a [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is a nonnegative integer. All three possibilities occur:

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
(r(T),n(T))&T(x,y)\\\hline
(0,2)&(0,0,0)\\
(1,1)&(x,0,0)\\
(2,0)&(x,y,0)
\end{array}}
$$

The [images of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) in these examples have [dimensions](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $0,1,2$, respectively, while the corresponding [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\mathbb R^2$, $\{(0,y):y\in\mathbb R\}$ and $\{0\}$. The identity above excludes all other pairs.
