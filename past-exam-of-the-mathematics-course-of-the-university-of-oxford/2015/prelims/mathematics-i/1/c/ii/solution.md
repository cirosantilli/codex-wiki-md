<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $TS=\operatorname{id}_W$, every $w\in W$ would satisfy $w=T(Sw)$, so $T$ would be a [surjective linear map](../../../../../../../surjective-linear-map.md). But its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) has [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) at most $\dim V=2$, whereas $\dim W=3$. Therefore **no such $S$ exists**. This is the obstruction to a [right inverse](../../../../../../../right-inverse.md): $T$ cannot reach every target [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md).
