<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $T:V\to W$, its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\},\qquad\ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0\}.}
$$

Both contain the [zero vector](../../../../../../zero-vector.md), since $T(0)=0$. If $y_1=T(v_1)$ and $y_2=T(v_2)$ belong to the [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md), then for any [real numbers](../../../../../../real-number.md) $a,b$,

$$
ay_1+by_2=T(av_1+bv_2)\in\operatorname{im}T.
$$

Thus $\operatorname{im}T$ is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $W$. If $u_1,u_2\in\ker T$, then

$$
T(au_1+bu_2)=aT(u_1)+bT(u_2)=0,
$$

so $\ker T$ is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $V$.
