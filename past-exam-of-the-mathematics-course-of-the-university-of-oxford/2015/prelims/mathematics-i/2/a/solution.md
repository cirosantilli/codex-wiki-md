<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [elementary row operation](../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) is one of three reversible changes: interchange two rows; multiply one row by a nonzero [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md); or add a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of one row to a different row. Their inverses are, respectively, the same interchange, multiplication by the reciprocal, and addition of the negative [scalar multiple](../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md).

Applied to an [augmented matrix](../../../../../../augmented-matrix.md), these changes interchange [linear equations](../../../../../../linear-equation.md), rescale an equation by a nonzero factor, or add a multiple of one equation to another. Every old solution satisfies the new equations. Applying the inverse [elementary row operation](../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) gives the converse, so **the solution set is unchanged** at every step. Equivalently, all the operations together give

$$
[\widetilde A\mid\widetilde b]=E[A\mid b],\qquad\widetilde Ax=\widetilde b\iff EAx=Eb\iff Ax=b,
$$

where $E$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), a product of [elementary matrices](../../../../../../elementary-matrix.md).
