<h1 id="2/c/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every [elementary column operation](../../../../../../../../elementary-column-operation.md) preserves the [linear span](../../../../../../../../linear-span.md) of the columns: each new column is a [linear combination](../../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the old columns, and the inverse operation supplies the reverse inclusion. Thus the [column space](../../../../../../../../column-space.md) of $A''$ equals that of $A$, which is precisely the [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $T_\alpha$. Consequently **(B) is true for every $\alpha\in\mathbb R$**.
