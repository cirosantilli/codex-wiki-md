↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $\alpha\ne2$, $A'=I_3$, whose [row space](../../../../../../../../row-space.md) is $\mathbb R^3=\operatorname{im}T_\alpha$. For $\alpha=2$, [elementary row operations](../../../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) give the [reduced row echelon form](../../../../../../../../reduced-row-echelon-form.md)

$$
A'=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-1\\0&1&-1\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [row space](../../../../../../../../row-space.md) is $\{(u,v,w):u+v+w=0\}$, whereas the [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) is $\{(u,v,w):u-v+w=0\}$. These differ: $(0,1,-1)$ belongs to the former but not the latter. Thus **(A) is true exactly when $\alpha\ne2$**. Rows are being identified with [vectors](../../../../../../../../vector.md) in $\mathbb R^3$ in this comparison.
