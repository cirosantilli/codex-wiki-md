<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [orbits of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) form a [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $S$: every element lies in its own [orbit of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md), and two intersecting [orbits of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) coincide because each point can be moved back and then forward using [inverse elements](../../../../../../../inverse-element.md). By the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md), each [orbit of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) has [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) dividing $8$, so its size is $1,2,4$ or $8$.

If every [orbit of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) had size $2,4$ or $8$, their sizes would add to an even number, contrary to $|S|=17$. Therefore there is a one-element [orbit of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md), say $\{s'\}$. Every $g\in G$ sends $s'$ into this same singleton, so

$$
\boxed{g\cdot s'=s'\quad\text{for every }g\in G.}
$$

Thus $s'$ is a [fixed point of a group action](../../../../../../../fixed-point-of-a-group-action.md). In fact the number of [fixed points of a group action](../../../../../../../fixed-point-of-a-group-action.md) is odd, since all other [orbits of a group action](../../../../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) have even [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md).
