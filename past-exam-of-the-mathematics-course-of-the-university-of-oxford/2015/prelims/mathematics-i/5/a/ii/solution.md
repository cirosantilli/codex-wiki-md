<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) states that if $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) of a [finite group](../../../../../../../finite-group.md) $G$, then

$$
\boxed{|G|=[G:H]|H|,\qquad |H|\mid|G|,}
$$

where the [index of a subgroup](../../../../../../../index-of-a-subgroup.md) $[G:H]$ is the number of distinct [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md).

Every $g\in G$ lies in $gH$, since $g=ge$. By [i](../i.md), distinct [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) are disjoint, so these [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) form a [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $G$. Each has [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) $|H|$: the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $h\mapsto gh$ from $H$ to $gH$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), with inverse $gh\mapsto h$, by cancellation in a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md). Counting the elements in this finite [partition of a set](../../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) gives $|G|=[G:H]|H|$, proving the theorem.
