<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ of $G$, the [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) represented by $g$ is

$$
\boxed{gH=\{gh:h\in H\}.}
$$

Suppose two [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) intersect. There are $h_1,h_2\in H$ such that $g_1h_1=g_2h_2$, so

$$
g_1=g_2h_2h_1^{-1}.
$$

Because $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md), $h_2h_1^{-1}\in H$. Thus, for every $h\in H$, $g_1h=g_2(h_2h_1^{-1}h)\in g_2H$, proving $g_1H\subseteq g_2H$. Interchanging the two representatives gives the reverse inclusion. Therefore **two [left cosets](../../../../../../../left-coset.md) are equal or disjoint**.
