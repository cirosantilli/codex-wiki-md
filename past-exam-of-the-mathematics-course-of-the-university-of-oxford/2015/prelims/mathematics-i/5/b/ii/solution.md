<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $b_0\in B$ and put $H=b_0^{-1}B$. Then $e\in H$. For any $g_1,g_2\in G$,

$$
g_1H=(g_1b_0^{-1})B,\qquad g_2H=(g_2b_0^{-1})B,
$$

so these translates are equal or disjoint by the assumed property of $B$. Applying [i](../i.md) shows that $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{B=b_0H,\qquad H=b_0^{-1}B\leq G.}
$$

This proves that $B$ is a [left coset](../../../../../../../left-coset.md). Together with [i](../../a/i.md), it gives the [equal-or-disjoint translates characterize cosets](../../../../../../../equal-or-disjoint-translates-characterize-cosets.md) criterion.
