<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The hypothesis implies $x^{-1}=x$ for every $x\in G$, including $e$. For $a,b\in G$,

$$
ab=(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}=ba.
$$

Thus **$G$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md)**. This is the [group of exponent two is abelian](../../../../../../../group-of-exponent-two-is-abelian.md) argument.

The converse is false: the [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $C_3$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md), but its two nonidentity elements have [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) three.
