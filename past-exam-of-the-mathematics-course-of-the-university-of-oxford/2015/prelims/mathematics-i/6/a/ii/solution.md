<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $G=\langle g\rangle$, any two elements are $g^a,g^b$, and

$$
g^ag^b=g^{a+b}=g^{b+a}=g^bg^a.
$$

Thus **every [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md)**.

For the converse, the [Klein four-group](../../../../../../../klein-four-group.md) $C_2\times C_2$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md), with componentwise multiplication. Each nonidentity element has [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) two, so none generates its four elements. It is therefore not a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md).
