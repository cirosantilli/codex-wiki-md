<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $|G|=p$, with $p$ a [prime number](../../../../../../../prime-number-split.md), and choose $g\ne e$. By [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md), the [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) $g$ divides $p$ and is greater than one. It is therefore $p$, and $G=\langle g\rangle$. Hence **a [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) of prime [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md)**.

The converse is false: the [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $C_4$ has four elements, and $4$ is not a [prime number](../../../../../../../prime-number-split.md).
