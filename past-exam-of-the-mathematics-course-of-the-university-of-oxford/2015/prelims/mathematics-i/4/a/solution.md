<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The prescribed form is a sum of products of real [polynomial evaluations](../../../../../../polynomial-evaluation.md). It is a [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md), since each evaluation is a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md). Also

$$
\langle f,f\rangle=\frac{f(-1)^2+f(0)^2+f(1)^2}{3}\geq0.
$$

Equality forces $f(-1)=f(0)=f(1)=0$. A nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most two cannot have three distinct [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), by the [root bound for a polynomial](../../../../../../lagrange-root-bound-over-a-field.md). Thus equality holds only for the zero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md), establishing [positive definiteness](../../../../../../positive-definiteness.md). Therefore **the form is a real [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md)**, and $V$ is an [inner product space](../../../../../../inner-product-space.md). This is a [discrete sampling inner product on polynomial spaces](../../../../../../discrete-sampling-inner-product-on-polynomial-spaces.md).
