<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For any [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $f,g$ of $U$, [bilinearity](../../../../../../../bilinearity.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle f,h\rangle
&=\langle f,x^2\rangle-\langle f,x^2\rangle\langle f,f\rangle-\langle g,x^2\rangle\langle f,g\rangle=0,\\
\langle g,h\rangle
&=\langle g,x^2\rangle-\langle f,x^2\rangle\langle g,f\rangle-\langle g,x^2\rangle\langle g,g\rangle=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Together with $\langle f,g\rangle=0$, these identities prove **pairwise [orthogonality](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md)**. The construction subtracts the [orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional subspace](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md) $U$ from $x^2$, leaving the [orthogonal projection residual](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-residual.md).
