↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The relevant [inner products](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) are

$$
\langle1,x\rangle=\frac{-1+0+1}{3}=0,\qquad
\langle1,1\rangle=1,\qquad\langle x,x\rangle=\frac23.
$$

Hence $1$ and $x$ are [orthogonal vectors](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). Dividing by their [norms](../../../../../../../norm.md) gives the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md)

$$
\boxed{f=1,\qquad g=\sqrt{\frac32}\,x}
$$

of their [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) $U$.
