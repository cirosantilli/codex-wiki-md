<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use $f,g$ from [i](../i.md). Their [inner products](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with $x^2$ are

$$
\langle f,x^2\rangle=\frac23,\qquad
\langle g,x^2\rangle=\sqrt{\frac32}\,\frac{-1+0+1}{3}=0.
$$

Thus $h=x^2-2/3$. Its values at $-1,0,1$ are $1/3,-2/3,1/3$, so

$$
\|h\|^2=\frac13\left(\frac19+\frac49+\frac19\right)=\frac29.
$$

By [ii](../ii.md) the three [polynomials](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) are [orthogonal vectors](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), and normalizing $h$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B=\left(1,\sqrt{\frac32}\,x,\frac{3x^2-2}{\sqrt2}\right).}
$$

This [orthonormal set](../../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) is a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of the three-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$. It is the result of the [Gram-Schmidt process](../../../../../../../gram-schmidt-process.md) applied to $1,x,x^2$.
