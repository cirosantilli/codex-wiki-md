<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $T$ is an [orthogonal transformation](../../../../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md) and $e_1,e_2,e_3$ is an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), then

$$
\langle Te_i,Te_j\rangle=\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}.
$$

The images form an [orthonormal set](../../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md), hence are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md) and form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) in [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) three.

Conversely, suppose the images $Te_1,Te_2,Te_3$ of one chosen [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Write $u=\sum_i a_ie_i$ and $v=\sum_j b_je_j$. By [linearity](../../../../../../../linearity.md) of $T$ and [bilinearity](../../../../../../../bilinearity.md),

$$
\langle Tu,Tv\rangle=\sum_{i,j}a_ib_j\langle Te_i,Te_j\rangle
=\sum_i a_ib_i=\langle u,v\rangle.
$$

Thus **mapping one [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) to an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) is equivalent to preserving every [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md)**. In the converse, a single chosen [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) suffices.
