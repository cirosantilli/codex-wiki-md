<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $p=(3x^2-2)/\sqrt2$ and use the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\mathcal B=(1,\sqrt{3/2}\,x,p)$ from [iii](../../b/iii.md). The columns of the given [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) give

$$
T(1)=-\sqrt2+\frac3{\sqrt2}x^2=p,\qquad T(x)=x,\qquad T(x^2)=-\sqrt2+\sqrt2x^2.
$$

Consequently

$$
T(p)=\frac{3T(x^2)-2T(1)}{\sqrt2}=-1.
$$

The images of $\mathcal B$ are therefore $(p,\sqrt{3/2}\,x,-1)$, again an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). By [i](../i.md), **$T$ is an [orthogonal transformation](../../../../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md)**. Its [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) in this [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) is

$$
\boxed{[T]_{\mathcal B}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&-1\\0&1&0\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
