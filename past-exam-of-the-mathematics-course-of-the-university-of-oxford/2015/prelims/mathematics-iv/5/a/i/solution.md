<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $A$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns, attach a nonnegative [dual variable](../../../../../../../dual-variable.md) $y_i$ to each of the $m$ upper-bound constraints. The [dual linear program](../../../../../../../dual-linear-program.md) is

$$
\boxed{\text{minimise }\mathbf b^T\mathbf y
\quad\text{subject to }A^T\mathbf y\ge\mathbf c,\quad\mathbf y\ge\mathbf0.}
$$

Here $A^T$ is the [transpose](../../../../../../../transpose.md) of $A$, and all [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) inequalities hold componentwise. Nonnegative [dual variables](../../../../../../../dual-variable.md) preserve the direction of the original inequalities when taking weighted sums.
