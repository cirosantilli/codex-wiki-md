<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [feasible solutions](../../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $\mathbf x,\mathbf y$, nonnegativity gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c^T\mathbf x
\le (A^T\mathbf y)^T\mathbf x
=\mathbf y^TA\mathbf x
\le\mathbf y^T\mathbf b
=\mathbf b^T\mathbf y.}
$$

The first inequality uses $A^T\mathbf y\ge\mathbf c$ and $\mathbf x\ge\mathbf0$; the second uses $A\mathbf x\le\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf y\ge\mathbf0$. This is [weak duality](../../../../../../../weak-duality.md).
