<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For every primal [feasible solution](../../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $\mathbf x$, [weak duality](../../../../../../../weak-duality.md) gives

$$
\mathbf c^T\mathbf x\le\mathbf b^T\mathbf y^*=\mathbf c^T\mathbf x^*.
$$

Thus no primal [feasible solution](../../../../../../../feasible-point.md) improves on $\mathbf x^*$. Similarly, for every dual [feasible solution](../../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $\mathbf y$,

$$
\mathbf b^T\mathbf y\ge\mathbf c^T\mathbf x^*=\mathbf b^T\mathbf y^*.
$$

Therefore **both candidates are optimal**, with the same [objective function](../../../../../../../objective-function.md) value. Equality is a [linear programming optimality certificate](../../../../../../../linear-programming-optimality-certificate.md) and does not require a separate existence argument.
