<h1 id="5/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [complementary slackness](../../../../../../../complementary-slackness.md) conditions are

$$
\boxed{y_i\bigl(b_i-(A\mathbf x)_i\bigr)=0\quad(1\le i\le m),\qquad
x_j\bigl((A^T\mathbf y)_j-c_j\bigr)=0\quad(1\le j\le n).}
$$

To see their role, decompose the [duality gap](../../../../../../../duality-gap.md) as

$$
\mathbf b^T\mathbf y-\mathbf c^T\mathbf x
=\sum_i y_i\bigl(b_i-(A\mathbf x)_i\bigr)
+\sum_j x_j\bigl((A^T\mathbf y)_j-c_j\bigr).
$$

Every summand is nonnegative for [feasible solutions](../../../../../../../feasible-point.md), so the gap is zero exactly when every displayed product vanishes. Zero gap implies optimality by [weak duality](../../../../../../../weak-duality.md). Conversely, [strong duality](../../../../../../../strong-duality.md) states that primal and dual optima, when both are attained, have equal values, and hence satisfy these conditions.
