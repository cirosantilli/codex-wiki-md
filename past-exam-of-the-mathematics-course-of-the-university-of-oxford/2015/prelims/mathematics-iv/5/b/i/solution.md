<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are two primal inequalities, so there are two nonnegative [dual variables](../../../../../../../dual-variable.md). The five primal [variables](../../../../../../../variable-mathematics.md) yield five dual inequalities:

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\text{minimise }2y_1+y_2,\\
y_1+y_2\ge1,\qquad -y_1+y_2\ge-1,\\
-y_1+3y_2\ge-5,\qquad 3y_1+4y_2\ge12,\\
3y_1+y_2\ge6,\qquad y_1,y_2\ge0.
\end{gathered}}
$$

Each coefficient column of the primal [linear program](../../../../../../../linear-programming-split.md) becomes one row of this [dual linear program](../../../../../../../dual-linear-program.md).
