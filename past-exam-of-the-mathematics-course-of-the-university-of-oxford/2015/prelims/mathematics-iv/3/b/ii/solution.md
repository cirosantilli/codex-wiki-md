<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $gf''(a)<\omega^2$, put

$$
\lambda^2=\frac{\omega^2-gf''(a)}{1+f'(a)^2}>0.
$$

The [linearized equation](../../../../../../../linearized-equation.md) becomes $\ddot\epsilon-\lambda^2\epsilon=0$, so

$$
\epsilon(t)=C_1e^{\lambda t}+C_2e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

Arbitrarily small initial perturbations can have $C_1\ne0$ and grow exponentially. Hence the strict [unstable equilibrium](../../../../../../../unstable-equilibrium.md) condition is

$$
\boxed{f''(a)<\frac{\omega^2}{g}.}
$$

For the particular wire, this occurs precisely at $a=2j\pi b$, $j=1,2,\ldots$; the alternating radii $a=(2j-1)\pi b$ satisfy the [stable equilibrium](../../../../../../../stable-equilibrium.md) condition.
