<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $gf''(a)>\omega^2$, define

$$
\nu^2=\frac{gf''(a)-\omega^2}{1+f'(a)^2}>0.
$$

The [linearized equation](../../../../../../../linearized-equation.md) is $\ddot\epsilon+\nu^2\epsilon=0$, with bounded [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md)

$$
\epsilon(t)=\epsilon(0)\cos(\nu t)+\frac{\dot\epsilon(0)}\nu\sin(\nu t).
$$

Thus the strict [stable equilibrium](../../../../../../../stable-equilibrium.md) condition is

$$
\boxed{f''(a)>\frac{\omega^2}{g}.}
$$

This is also a sufficient condition for local nonlinear [Lyapunov stability](../../../../../../../lyapunov-stability.md). Indeed the equation for the [bead on a uniformly rotating wire](../../../../../../../bead-on-a-uniformly-rotating-wire.md) has the [conserved quantity](../../../../../../../conserved-quantity.md)

$$
\mathcal H=\frac12\left(1+f'(r)^2\right)\dot r^2+gf(r)-\frac12\omega^2r^2.
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is $\dot r$ times the governing equation moved to one side, so it vanishes. At $a$, the last two terms have zero first [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) and positive second [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) $gf''(a)-\omega^2$. Their strict local minimum traps sufficiently small perturbations. The motion is bounded rather than asymptotically returning to rest.
