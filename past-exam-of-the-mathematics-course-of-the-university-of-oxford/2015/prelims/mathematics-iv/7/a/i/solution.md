<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $0\le x\le7$, we have $2\le2+x\le9$. Taking the nonnegative [square root](../../../../../../../square-root.md) gives

$$
\boxed{G([0,7])=[\sqrt2,3]\subseteq[0,7].}
$$

The [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
G'(x)=\frac1{2\sqrt{2+x}},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{|G'(x)|\le\frac1{2\sqrt2}<1\qquad(0\le x\le7).}
$$

In particular, the [mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) makes $G$ a [contraction mapping](../../../../../../../contraction-mapping.md) on this interval.
