↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The divisions in the [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
323&=1\cdot187+136,\\
187&=1\cdot136+51,\\
136&=2\cdot51+34,\\
51&=1\cdot34+17,\\
34&=2\cdot17+0.
\end{aligned}
$$

The last nonzero [remainder](../../../../../../../remainder.md) is $17$, so

$$
\boxed{\gcd(323,187)=17.}
$$

As a check, $323=19\cdot17$ and $187=11\cdot17$; back-substitution also gives $17=-4\cdot323+7\cdot187$.
