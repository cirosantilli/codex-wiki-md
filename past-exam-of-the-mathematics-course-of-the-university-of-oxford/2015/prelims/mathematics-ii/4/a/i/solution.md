↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:E\to\mathbb R$ is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on $E$ if it is [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at every $p\in E$, meaning

$$
\boxed{\forall p\in E\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x\in E:\quad |x-p|<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\ |f(x)-f(p)|<\varepsilon.}
$$

The [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) has [uniform continuity](../../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) on $E$ when

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x,y\in E:\quad |x-y|<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\ |f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.}
$$

For [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md), $\delta$ may depend on both $p$ and $\varepsilon$. For [uniform continuity](../../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md), one $\delta$ must work for every pair of points in $E$.
