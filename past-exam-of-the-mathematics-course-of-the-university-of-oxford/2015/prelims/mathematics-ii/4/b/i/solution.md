<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For any $x,y\in\mathbb R$, rationalizing the difference of [square roots](../../../../../../../square-root.md) gives

$$
|g(x)-g(y)|=\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{\sqrt{f(x)}+\sqrt{f(y)}}\leq\frac12|f(x)-f(y)|,
$$

since $f(x),f(y)\geq1$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, use [uniform continuity](../../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) of $f$ with tolerance $2\varepsilon$ to obtain $\delta>0$. Whenever $|x-y|<\delta$, the displayed estimate yields $|g(x)-g(y)|<\varepsilon$. Thus **$g$ has [uniform continuity](../../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) on $\mathbb R$.** The [square root](../../../../../../../square-root.md) [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is in fact [Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant $1/2$ on $[1,\infty)$.
