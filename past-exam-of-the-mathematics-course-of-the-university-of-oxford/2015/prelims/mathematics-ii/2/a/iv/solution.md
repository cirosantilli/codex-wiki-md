<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Given $\varepsilon>0$, the two assumed [limits of sequences](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) supply thresholds $N_1,N_2$ such that $|a_n-\ell|<\varepsilon$ for $n\geq N_1$ and $|c_n-\ell|<\varepsilon$ for $n\geq N_2$. For $n\geq\max\{N_1,N_2\}$,

$$
\ell-\varepsilon<a_n\leq b_n\leq c_n<\ell+\varepsilon.
$$

Thus $|b_n-\ell|<\varepsilon$ eventually, proving the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{b_n\longrightarrow\ell.}
$$
