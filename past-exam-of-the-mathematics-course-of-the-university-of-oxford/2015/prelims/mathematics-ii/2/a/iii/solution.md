<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $u=c^{-1}-1>0$, so $c=(1+u)^{-1}$. By [Bernoulli's inequality](../../../../../../../bernoulli-s-inequality.md), $(1+u)^n\geq1+nu$, and hence

$$
\boxed{0<c^n\leq\frac1{1+nu}\longrightarrow0.}
$$

More explicitly, for $\varepsilon>0$ choose an [integer](../../../../../../../integer-split.md) $N>1/(u\varepsilon)$. For $n\geq N$, the right-hand side is less than $\varepsilon$. Thus the [geometric sequence](../../../../../../../geometric-progression.md) has [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) zero, either directly by the definition or by the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md).
