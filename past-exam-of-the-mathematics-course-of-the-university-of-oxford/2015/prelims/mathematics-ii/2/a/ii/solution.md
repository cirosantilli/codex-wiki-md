<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $a_n\to\ell$. With tolerance $1$, choose $N$ such that $|a_n-\ell|<1$ whenever $n\geq N$. The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives $|a_n|\leq|a_n-\ell|+|\ell|<1+|\ell|$ for these terms. Set

$$
\boxed{B=1+|\ell|+\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}|a_j|.}
$$

An empty sum is zero. This finite $B$ satisfies $|a_n|\leq B$ for every $n$, so every [convergent sequence](../../../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) is a [bounded sequence](../../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md).
