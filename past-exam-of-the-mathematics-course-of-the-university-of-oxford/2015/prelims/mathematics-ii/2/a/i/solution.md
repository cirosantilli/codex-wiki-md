↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md) $(a_n)$ of [real numbers](../../../../../../../real-number.md) is a [convergent sequence](../../../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) with [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) $\ell$ when

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\ \forall n\geq N:\quad |a_n-\ell|<\varepsilon.}
$$

Divergence to positive [infinity](../../../../../../../infinity.md) means

$$
\boxed{\forall M\in\mathbb R\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\ \forall n\geq N:\quad a_n>M.}
$$

In both definitions the same $N$ must control every later term. The second condition is not convergence to a [real number](../../../../../../../real-number.md).
