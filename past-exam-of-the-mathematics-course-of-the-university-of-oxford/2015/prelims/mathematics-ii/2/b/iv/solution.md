<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** Choose $N$ such that $\ell/2<a_n<3\ell/2$ for every $n\geq N$. All the finitely many preceding terms are also positive. Define the positive [minimum](../../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) and finite [maximum](../../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md)

$$
\eta=\min\bigl(\{\ell/2\}\cup\{a_j:1\leq j<N\}\bigr)>0,
\qquad
B=\max\bigl(\{3\ell/2\}\cup\{a_j:1\leq j<N\}\bigr)<\infty.
$$

Every term lies between $\eta$ and $B$, so every ratio is at most $B/\eta$. Consequently the required [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\sup\{a_n/a_m:m,n\in\mathbb N\}\leq B/\eta<\infty.}
$$
