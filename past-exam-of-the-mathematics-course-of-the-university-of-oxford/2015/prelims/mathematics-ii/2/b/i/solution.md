↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** Take the positive [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md) $\boxed{a_n=n}$. Then

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\frac{n+1}{n}=1+\frac1n\longrightarrow1,
$$

but $a_n\to+\infty$, so it has no finite [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md). Successive ratios close to one do not prevent cumulative growth.
