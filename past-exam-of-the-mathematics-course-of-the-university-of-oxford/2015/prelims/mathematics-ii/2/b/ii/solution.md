↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Take the positive [geometric sequence](../../../../../../../geometric-progression.md) $\boxed{a_n=2^{-n}}$. It tends to zero, while

$$
\boxed{\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\frac12\not\longrightarrow1.}
$$

A positive [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) would make the claim true since $a_{n+1}/a_n$ would then tend to $\ell/\ell=1$, but positivity of each individual term does not exclude a zero [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md).
