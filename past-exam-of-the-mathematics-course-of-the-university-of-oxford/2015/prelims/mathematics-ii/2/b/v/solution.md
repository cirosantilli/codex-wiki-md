↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**False.** Choose the positive [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md)

$$
\boxed{a_n=1+\frac1{\sqrt n}.}
$$

It has [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) one, but [Bernoulli's inequality](../../../../../../../bernoulli-s-inequality.md) gives

$$
a_n^n=\left(1+\frac1{\sqrt n}\right)^n\geq1+\sqrt n\longrightarrow+\infty.
$$

Thus $(a_n^n)$ is not a [bounded sequence](../../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md).
