<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(a,b)$ when, at each $c\in(a,b)$, its [difference quotient](../../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) has a finite [limit of a function](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md):

$$
\boxed{f'(c)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(c+h)-f(c)}h\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Here $h\ne0$ and $c+h\in(a,b)$.

For sufficiently small nonzero $h$, the [difference quotient](../../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) has [absolute value](../../../../../../../absolute-value.md) at most $|f'(c)|+1$, because it tends to $f'(c)$. Consequently

$$
|f(c+h)-f(c)|\leq|h|\bigl(|f'(c)|+1\bigr)\longrightarrow0.
$$

This is [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $c$. Since $c$ was arbitrary, **[differentiability](../../../../../../../differentiability.md) implies [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on $(a,b)$.**
