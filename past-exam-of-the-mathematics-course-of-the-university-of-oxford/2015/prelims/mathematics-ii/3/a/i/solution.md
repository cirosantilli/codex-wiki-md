<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) of [complex numbers](../../../../../../../complex-number.md), form the [partial sums](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n$. The [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converges when these [partial sums](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) have a finite [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) $S\in\mathbb C$:

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N_0\ \forall N\geq N_0:\quad |S_N-S|<\varepsilon.}
$$

The [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) when $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$ converges as a [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) of nonnegative [real numbers](../../../../../../../real-number.md). A [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) beginning at $n=0$ uses the same definitions with that starting index.
