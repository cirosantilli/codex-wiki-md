↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The finite [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) identity, obtained by subtracting $t$ times the sum from the sum itself, gives

$$
S_N=\sum_{n=0}^Nt^n=\frac{1-t^{N+1}}{1-t}.
$$

For $0<t<1$, the [geometric sequence](../../../../../../../geometric-progression.md) $t^{N+1}$ tends to zero by Question 2(a)(iii). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}t^n=\frac1{1-t}.}
$$
