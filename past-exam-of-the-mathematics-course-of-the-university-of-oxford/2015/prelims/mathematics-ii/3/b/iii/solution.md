<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $z_0=0$, there is no $z$ with $|z|<|z_0|$, so the claim is vacuous. Suppose $z_0\ne0$. Convergence of $\sum a_nz_0^n$ implies $a_nz_0^n\to0$, because [terms of a convergent series tend to zero](../../../../../../../terms-of-a-convergent-series-tend-to-zero.md). In particular this is a [bounded sequence](../../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md), so $|a_nz_0^n|\leq C$ for some finite $C$ and every $n$.

For $|z|<|z_0|$, put $q=|z|/|z_0|<1$. Then

$$
|a_nz^n|=|a_nz_0^n|q^n\leq Cq^n.
$$

The [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) and [comparison test for series](../../../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) prove

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}|a_nz^n|<\infty.}
$$

Thus convergence at one point gives [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) at every point strictly closer to the centre.
