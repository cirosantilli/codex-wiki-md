<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [comparison test for series](../../../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) states that, for $0\leq u_n\leq v_n$ eventually, convergence of $\sum v_n$ implies convergence of $\sum u_n$. Equivalently, divergence of $\sum u_n$ implies divergence of $\sum v_n$.

For $|z|<1$, the coefficient bound gives

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_nz^n|\leq M\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|z|^n<\infty
$$

by the [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) formula and the [comparison test for series](../../../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md). The single term $a_0$ does not affect convergence. For $|z|\geq1$,

$$
|a_nz^n|>m|z|^n\geq m>0\qquad(n\geq1),
$$

so the terms do not tend to zero. The fact that [terms of a convergent series tend to zero](../../../../../../../terms-of-a-convergent-series-tend-to-zero.md) excludes convergence there. Thus

$$
\boxed{R=1.}
$$
