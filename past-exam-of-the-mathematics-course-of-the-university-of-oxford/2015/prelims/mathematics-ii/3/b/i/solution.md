↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [radius of convergence](../../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) of a [power series](../../../../../../../power-series.md) $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ is the number $R\in[0,\infty]$ such that the [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) for $|z|<R$ and diverges for $|z|>R$. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{R=\sup\left\{r\geq0:\ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}|a_n|r^n<\infty\right\}.}
$$

Use $r^0=1$, including at $r=0$. The [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) may be $+\infty$. There is no general conclusion at $|z|=R$: boundary convergence depends on the [coefficients](../../../../../../../coefficient.md). If $R=0$, only the centre is guaranteed; if $R=\infty$, every finite $z$ lies inside the [disk of convergence](../../../../../../../disk-of-convergence.md).
