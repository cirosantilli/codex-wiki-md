<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Nf_n$ and let $s$ be its [uniform limit](../../../../../../../uniform-limit.md). Choose a finite $B\geq0$ with $|g(x)|\leq B$ for all $x\in E$. The [partial sums](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) of the multiplied [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) are $gS_N$, and

$$
|g(x)S_N(x)-g(x)s(x)|\leq B|S_N(x)-s(x)|.
$$

For any $\varepsilon>0$, [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) supplies $N_0$ making $|S_N(x)-s(x)|<\varepsilon/(B+1)$ for all $N\geq N_0$ and $x\in E$. The displayed estimate then gives an error less than $\varepsilon$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n(x)g(x)=g(x)s(x)\quad\text{uniformly on }E.}
$$
