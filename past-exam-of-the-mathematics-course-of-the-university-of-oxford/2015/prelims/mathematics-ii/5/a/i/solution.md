<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [partial sums](../../../../../../../partial-sum.md) as $S_N(x)=\sum_{n=1}^Nf_n(x)$. The [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) has [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) to $s:E\to\mathbb R$ if

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N_0\in\mathbb N\ \forall N\geq N_0\ \forall x\in E:\quad |S_N(x)-s(x)|<\varepsilon.}
$$

The same threshold $N_0$ must control every point of $E$. Equivalently, the [supremum error](../../../../../../../supremum-error.md) $\sup_{x\in E}|S_N(x)-s(x)|$ tends to zero, for nonempty $E$.
