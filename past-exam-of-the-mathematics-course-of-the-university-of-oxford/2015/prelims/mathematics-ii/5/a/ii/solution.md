<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Weierstrass M-test](../../../../../../../weierstrass-m-test.md) states: if [functions](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f_n:E\to\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ satisfy

$$
|f_n(x)|\leq M_n\quad\text{for every }x\in E,
\qquad M_n\geq0,
\qquad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}M_n<\infty,
$$

then $\sum f_n$ has [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) and [absolute convergence](../../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) on $E$. In fact $\sum|f_n|$ also has [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md), since every tail is bounded by the corresponding tail of $\sum M_n$:

$$
\boxed{\sup_{x\in E}\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}|f_n(x)|\leq\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}M_n\longrightarrow0.}
$$
