↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose the [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md)

$$
\boxed{a_n=(-1)^n\qquad(n\geq1).}
$$

Every even index is a [peak of a sequence](../../../../../../../peak-of-a-sequence.md) $(a_n)$, since its value is $1$ and all later values are at most $1$. Every odd index is a [peak of a sequence](../../../../../../../peak-of-a-sequence.md) $(-a_n)$ for the same reason. Both [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) of indices are infinite.
