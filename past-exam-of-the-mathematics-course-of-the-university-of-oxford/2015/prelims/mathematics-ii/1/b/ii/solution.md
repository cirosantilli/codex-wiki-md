Choose the [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md)

$$
\boxed{b_n=(-1)^n n\qquad(n\geq1).}
$$

After every index there are positive terms arbitrarily large and negative terms arbitrarily small. Thus every term has both a larger later term and a smaller later term. The former excludes a [peak of a sequence](../../../../../../../peak-of-a-sequence.md) $(b_n)$, and the latter excludes a [peak of a sequence](../../../../../../../peak-of-a-sequence.md) $(-b_n)$.
