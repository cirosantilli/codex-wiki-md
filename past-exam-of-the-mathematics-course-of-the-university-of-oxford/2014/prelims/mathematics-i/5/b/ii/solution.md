<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from an additive [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z_n$ is determined by the image $a$ of $1$, subject exactly to $a^n=e$ in multiplicative notation, or $na=0$ in an additive target.

For $\mathbb Z_{18}\to\mathbb Z_{15}$, the relation is $18a\equiv0\pmod{15}$, equivalently $3a\equiv0\pmod{15}$. The possibilities are $a=0,5,10$, and each defines a [homomorphism between cyclic groups](../../../../../../../homomorphism-between-cyclic-groups.md). Thus there are **three** such maps, an instance of the [number of homomorphisms between finite cyclic groups](../../../../../../../number-of-homomorphisms-between-finite-cyclic-groups.md).

For $\mathbb Z_9\to S_4$, the image of $1$ must be a [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) whose [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) divides $9$. A [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) on four symbols can have order $1,2,3$ or $4$, so the allowed images are the identity and the [three-cycles](../../../../../../../three-cycle.md). There are $\binom43(3-1)!=8$ [three-cycles](../../../../../../../three-cycle.md): choose their three symbols and one of the two cyclic orientations. Hence there are **nine** maps.

Finally, each [transposition](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) in $S_5$ has order $2$, so its image in $\mathbb Z_5$ must have order dividing both $2$ and $5$, and is therefore the identity. Every [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) is a product of [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md), so every element must map to the identity. There is **one** map. In the order asked, the counts are

$$
\boxed{3,\qquad9,\qquad1.}
$$
