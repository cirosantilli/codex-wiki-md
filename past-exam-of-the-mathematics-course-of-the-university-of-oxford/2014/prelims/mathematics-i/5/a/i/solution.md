<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Among the $|G|+1$ powers $e,g,g^2,\ldots,g^{|G|}$ in the finite [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$, the [pigeonhole principle](../../../../../../../pigeonhole-principle.md) forces two to be equal: $g^i=g^j$ with $0\leq i<j\leq|G|$. Multiply by $g^{-i}$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{g^{j-i}=e,\qquad j-i>0.}
$$

This proves the existence of a positive power equal to the [identity element](../../../../../../../identity-element.md), without presupposing the [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md).
