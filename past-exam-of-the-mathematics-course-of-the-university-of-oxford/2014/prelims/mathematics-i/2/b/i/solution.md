<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Restrict $S$ to the [vector subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $W=\operatorname{im}T$. This restriction has [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $W\cap\ker S$ and [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $S(W)=\operatorname{im}(ST)$. The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) on $W$ therefore gives

$$
r(ST)=r(T)-\dim(\operatorname{im}T\cap\ker S).
$$

Apply the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) to $T$ and $ST$ on $V$ to obtain the exact [nullity of a composition](../../../../../../../nullity-of-a-composition.md) identity:

$$
n(ST)=n(T)+\dim(\operatorname{im}T\cap\ker S).
$$

Since the intersection is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\ker S$, its [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is at most $n(S)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{n(ST)\leq n(T)+n(S).}
$$
