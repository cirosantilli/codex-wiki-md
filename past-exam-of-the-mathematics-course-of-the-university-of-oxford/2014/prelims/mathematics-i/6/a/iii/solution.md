<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $g\in Z(G)$, it commutes with each $x\in G$, so $x^{-1}gx=g$. Its [conjugacy class](../../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) is therefore $C(g)=\{g\}$.

Conversely, if $C(g)=\{g\}$, then $x^{-1}gx=g$ for every $x$. Multiplication by $x$ on the left gives $gx=xg$ for every $x$, which is exactly membership in the [center of a group](../../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{g\in Z(G)\iff C(g)=\{g\}.}
$$
