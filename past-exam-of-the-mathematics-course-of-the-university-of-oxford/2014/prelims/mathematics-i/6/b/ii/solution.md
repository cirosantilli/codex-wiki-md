↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the preceding relabelling formula, every conjugate of $(123)$ is a [three-cycle](../../../../../../../three-cycle.md), and any [three-cycle](../../../../../../../three-cycle.md) on four symbols can be obtained by a suitable relabelling. Therefore

$$
\boxed{C((123))=\{(123),(132),(124),(142),(134),(143),(234),(243)\}.}
$$

There are eight elements, agreeing with the count $\binom43\cdot2$.

Likewise, conjugation preserves the pattern of two disjoint [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md), and every partition of four symbols into two pairs is realized by relabelling. Thus

$$
\boxed{C((12)(34))=\{(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}.}
$$

These have the respective [cycle types](../../../../../../../cycle-type.md) $(3,1)$ and $(2,2)$ in the [symmetric group](../../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_4$.
