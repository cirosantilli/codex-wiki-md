<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) of a [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) relabels each symbol by the inverse conjugating permutation. Specifically,

$$
\boxed{\tau^{-1}(a_1a_2\cdots a_n)\tau
=(\tau^{-1}(a_1)\ \tau^{-1}(a_2)\ \cdots\ \tau^{-1}(a_n)).}
$$

To verify the direction of the inverse, apply the left side to $\tau^{-1}(a_j)$: $\tau$ first takes it to $a_j$, the original cycle takes it to $a_{j+1}$, and $\tau^{-1}$ takes it to $\tau^{-1}(a_{j+1})$, with cyclic indexing. Any symbols fixed by the original [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) are similarly relabelled and remain fixed. More generally, conjugate every cycle of a disjoint-cycle decomposition in this way; disjointness is preserved because $\tau^{-1}$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md).
