<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose $\sigma\ne e$. Choose a symbol $i$ moved by $\sigma$ and write $j=\sigma(i)\ne i$. Since $n\geq3$, choose a third symbol $k$ distinct from $i,j$. Let $\tau=(jk)$, a [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) that fixes $i$. Then

$$
(\tau^{-1}\sigma\tau)(i)=\tau^{-1}(\sigma(i))=\tau^{-1}(j)=k\ne j=\sigma(i).
$$

Thus $\tau^{-1}\sigma\tau\ne\sigma$, proving that the [conjugacy class](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) of any nonidentity [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) has more than one element. By part (a)(iii), no such [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) lies in the [center of a group](../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md). The identity always does, so

$$
\boxed{Z(S_n)=\{e\}\quad(n\geq3).}
$$

The restriction on $n$ is necessary: $S_2$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) and equals its own center.
