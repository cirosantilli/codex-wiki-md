# Mathematics I

↑ **Parent:** [Prelims](split.md)

Solutions to all seven questions of the [University of Oxford](../../../university-of-oxford-split.md) undergraduate Prelims Mathematics I examination, sat on 23 June 2014. The cover also identifies the shared mathematics and computer science paper as M1. Source: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_papers_2/response/1850333/attach/3/FOI%2020210708%201%20Data.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `FOI-20210708-1 - Data/4243.pdf`, printed pages 1–4. Question statements are omitted. A bounded exact-paper availability check found no advertised full or partial answers; the published even-year policy explicitly establishes coverage back to at least 2016, rather than establishing this 2014 paper's availability.
