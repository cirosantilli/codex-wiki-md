# Fan-out

↑ **Parent:** [Logic gate](logic-gate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan-out)

The number of load inputs driven by one output of a [logic gate](logic-gate.md). A large fan-out increases the capacitive load and can increase [propagation delay](propagation-delay.md); the allowable number also depends on the logic technology and input characteristics.
