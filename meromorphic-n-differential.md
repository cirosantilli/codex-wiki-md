# Meromorphic n-differential

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

On a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), a meromorphic n-differential is a meromorphic section of the $n$th power of the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md). In a coordinate it is $F(z)(dz)^n$, with coefficients transforming by the $n$th power of the derivative. At a ramification coordinate $w=t^e$, its pullback has order $e\operatorname{ord}_w\omega+n(e-1)$. At a [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), the exponential coordinate gives $dz=(h/(2\pi i))dq/q$, so a weight-$2n$ form of cusp order $v$ produces a differential of order $v-n$.
