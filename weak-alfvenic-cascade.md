<h1 id="weak-alfvenic-cascade">Weak Alfvénic cascade</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md)

For balanced anisotropic [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md), suppose $\tau_A=l_\parallel/v_A\ll\tau_{\rm nl}=l_\perp/\delta z$ and resonant interactions preserve the parallel scale during the perpendicular cascade. Random encounter accumulation gives $\tau_{\rm cas}=v_Al_\perp^2/(\delta z^2l_\parallel)$. Constant [energy flux](energy-flux.md) then gives $\delta z\propto l_\perp^{1/2}$ and the displayed perpendicular [turbulent energy spectrum](turbulent-energy-spectrum.md). Unlike isotropic phenomenology, the fixed parallel scale is independent of the decreasing perpendicular scale.
