# Radiative homology with fifth-power hydrogen burning and inverse-cubic opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md)

For gas-pressure-supported [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) with [opacity](opacity.md) $\kappa=\kappa_0Z\rho T^{-3}$ and [stellar energy-generation rate](stellar-energy-generation-rate.md) $\epsilon=\epsilon_0X^2\rho T^5$, [radiative diffusion in a star](radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) gives $L\propto RT_c^7/(Z\rho_c^2)\propto\mu^7M^5/Z$. Integrating the heating rate instead gives $L\propto X^2\mu^5M^7/R^8$. Equality in [stellar thermal equilibrium](stellar-thermal-equilibrium.md) yields the displayed [radius](radius.md) law and $T_c\propto\mu^{5/4}M^{3/4}X^{-1/4}Z^{-1/8}$. This fixes the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) profiles and coefficients, and neglects [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md); it is a model comparison rather than a universal law for all [main sequence](main-sequence.md) stars. At fixed [mass](mass.md) and [metal mass fraction](metal-mass-fraction.md), homogeneous [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md) gives the [homogeneous fuel-depletion luminosity feedback](homogeneous-fuel-depletion-luminosity-feedback.md) through $L\propto\mu^7$.
