# Plane domain with two omitted points is hyperbolic

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformization theorem](uniformization-theorem.md)

Every plane domain whose complement contains at least two points is uniformized by the unit disc. A plane universal cover would give a nonconstant entire function omitting two values, contrary to the [Little Picard theorem](little-picard-theorem.md).
