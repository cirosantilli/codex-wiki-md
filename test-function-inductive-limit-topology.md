# Test-function inductive limit topology

↑ **Parent:** [Space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md)

The [space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md) has the locally convex inductive-limit topology of the spaces of [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) supported in a fixed [compact set](compact-space.md) $K$. Each $\mathcal D_K$ uses [seminorms](seminorm.md) $p_m(\varphi)=\max_{|\alpha|\leq m}\|\partial^\alpha\varphi\|_\infty$. Sequential convergence means a common [compact support](compact-support.md) and [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) of every derivative. A [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on this space is a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) exactly when its restriction to each $\mathcal D_K$ satisfies a finite [order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md) estimate; the order and constant may depend on $K$.
