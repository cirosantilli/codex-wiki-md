# Spin one-half

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

In units $\hbar=1$, spin one-half is the two-dimensional irreducible [spin angular momentum](spin.md) representation with components $S_i=\sigma_i/2$. The [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) give [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm1/2$ along every unit axis. The normalized $x$-axis states are $(|\uparrow_z\rangle\pm|\downarrow_z\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
