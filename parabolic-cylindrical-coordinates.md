# Parabolic cylindrical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md)

These [orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md) extend two confocal families of [parabolas](parabola.md) parallel to an axis. In the displayed scaling their [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) are $h_u=h_v=2\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$ and $h_w=1$, so the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is $\Delta\phi=(\phi_{uu}+\phi_{vv})/[4(u^2+v^2)]+\phi_{ww}$ away from $u=v=0$. With $u\geq0$, signed $v$ distinguishes the upper and lower half-planes; the two signs at $u=0$ describe the same negative $x$ half-axis. Fixed positive $u^2$ or $v^2$ describes a [parabolic cylinder](parabolic-cylinder.md). This differs from the rotational three-dimensional [parabolic coordinates](parabolic-coordinates.md), whose coordinate surfaces are [elliptic paraboloids](elliptic-paraboloid.md).
