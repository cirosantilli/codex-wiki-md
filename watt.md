# Watt

↑ **Parent:** [Joule heating](joule-heating.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watt)

The [watt](watt.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [power](power.md), one [joule](joule.md) per second. [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) at $12.5\,\mathrm W$ transfers $125\,\mathrm J$ of [energy](energy.md) over a [time](time-in-physics.md) of $10\,\mathrm s$ if that [power](power.md) stays constant. [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.5: power in watts](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/9-5-electrical-energy-and-power.md).
