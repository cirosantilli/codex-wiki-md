# Cotangent coordinate transition

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

For coordinates $x,y$ on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), fibre coefficients of a [cotangent space](cotangent-space.md) element obey $b_j=\sum_i a_i\partial x^i/\partial y^j$. Thus induced [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) charts change by $(x,a)\mapsto(y(x),b)$ with smooth, invertible, fibre-linear changes. The [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) satisfies $\sum_i a_i dx^i=\sum_j b_jdy^j$, and differentiating proves invariance of its [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md).
