# Fault-plane ambiguity

↑ **Parent:** [Focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md)

Interchanging the slip direction and fault normal leaves a double-couple moment tensor unchanged. First-motion polarities therefore cannot identify the physical fault plane without information such as mapped rupture, [aftershock](aftershock.md) geometry or geodetic [displacement](displacement.md).
