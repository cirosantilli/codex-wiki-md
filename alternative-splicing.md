# Alternative splicing

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)

Production of different mature [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) sequences from the same precursor by changing which [splice sites](splice-site.md) or [exons](exon.md) are used. A cassette [exon](exon.md) can be included in one [transcript isoform](transcript-isoform.md) and skipped in another.
