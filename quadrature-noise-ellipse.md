# Quadrature noise ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md)

For a single-mode Gaussian state with no quadrature covariance, the covariance contour has semiaxes $\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}X}$ and $\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}P}$. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md) has both equal to $1/2$ in the convention $[X,P]=i/2$. A pure [squeezed vacuum state](squeezed-vacuum-state.md) has semiaxes $e^{-r}/2$ and $e^r/2$; the uncertainty area remains fixed while one noise width decreases. An ellipse of means alone does not specify a general non-Gaussian state.[Slides9–12, physical PDF pp.5–6: minimum-uncertainty and squeezed states](quantum-optical-communication-lecture-6-by-jeffrey-h-shapiro.md)
