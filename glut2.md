# GLUT2

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

[GLUT2](glut2.md) is a facilitative glucose transporter with important roles in hepatocytes and in the classical rodent beta-cell model. It supports transport down a concentration gradient and should not be assumed to be the predominant human beta-cell glucose transporter.
