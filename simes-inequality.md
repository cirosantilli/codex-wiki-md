# Simes inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple hypothesis testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md)

For $m\ge1$ independent valid null [p-values](p-value.md), the displayed inequality follows by applying the [Benjamini-Hochberg procedure](benjamini-hochberg-procedure.md) with all nulls true. Its [false discovery rate](false-discovery-rate.md) then equals the [probability](probability.md) of at least one rejection. Valid means $\Pr(p_i\le t)\le t$ for $0\le t\le1$; independence is a sufficient hypothesis, and arbitrary dependence is not covered by this proof.
