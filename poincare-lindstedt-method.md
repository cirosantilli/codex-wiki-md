<h1 id="poincare-lindstedt-method">Poincaré-Lindstedt method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md)

For a weakly perturbed [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md), expand both its periodic solution and its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), using the stretched time $\tau=\omega t$. At each order, choose the frequency correction to remove fundamental-harmonic forcing that would produce a [secular term](secular-term.md). For a self-excited [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md), cancellation of the independent sine and cosine terms can determine both the leading [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and the frequency correction. Actual existence follows when the associated [Poincaré return map](poincare-map.md) has a simple displacement zero, by the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md).
