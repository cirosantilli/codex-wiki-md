# Proper maps extend to one-point compactifications

↑ **Parent:** [Alexandroff extension](alexandroff-extension.md)

For locally [compact](compact-space.md) noncompact [Hausdorff spaces](hausdorff-space.md), a [continuous](continuous-function.md) [proper map](proper-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ extends continuously to their [one-point compactifications](alexandroff-extension.md) by the displayed rule. The inverse image of $\alpha Y\setminus K$ is $\alpha X\setminus f^{-1}(K)$, a [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of infinity because the inverse image of a [compact set](compact-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md). The extension itself is proper, since it is [continuous](continuous-function.md) from a [compact space](compact-space.md) to a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md). A nonproper map can fail to admit any [continuous](continuous-function.md) extension: projection $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ sends two vertical sequences escaping to the same [point](point-geometry.md) at infinity to two distinct constant sequences, incompatible with [continuity](continuous-function.md) at that [point](point-geometry.md).
