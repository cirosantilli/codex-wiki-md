# Spin four-vector

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

The spin of a small torque-free test [gyroscope](gyroscope.md) is represented by a [spacelike vector](spacelike-vector.md) orthogonal to its [four-velocity](four-velocity.md), $s^\mu u_\mu=0$. Its temporal component vanishes in its own [rest frame](rest-frame.md). Along a [geodesic](geodesic.md), $Ds^\mu/d\tau=0$: [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) preserves the orthogonality and the invariant spin magnitude. For an accelerated observer the corresponding nonrotating transport is [Fermi-Walker transport](fermi-walker-transport.md).
