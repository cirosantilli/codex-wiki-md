# Supermartingale control of deflated consumption gains

↑ **Parent:** [Deflated wealth equation with consumption](deflated-wealth-equation-with-consumption.md)

If wealth and [consumption](consumption.md) are nonnegative, this [local martingale](local-martingale.md) is bounded below by minus the finite initial deflated capital. Adding that capital gives a nonnegative [local martingale](local-martingale.md), hence a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) by the [Conditional Fatou lemma](conditional-fatou-lemma.md). Consequently expected discounted [consumption](consumption.md) cannot exceed initial deflated wealth. The [consumption](consumption.md) sign and [predictable](predictable-process.md) stochastic [integrability](integrability.md) are part of the hypotheses.
