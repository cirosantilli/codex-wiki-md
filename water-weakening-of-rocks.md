# Water weakening of rocks

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

[Water weakening of rocks](water-weakening-of-rocks.md) denotes the reduction of mechanical strength or increase of [material creep](creep-deformation.md) rate produced by water or hydrogen-related defects under specified conditions. The effect depends on mineralogy, water activity, [temperature](temperature-split.md) and deformation mechanism; atmospheric dryness alone does not measure a planet's interior water content.
