# Finiteness of coherent cohomology on projective varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent cohomology](coherent-cohomology.md)

For a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ on a [projective variety](projective-variety.md) over a field, every $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ is finite-dimensional, and [Grothendieck vanishing](grothendieck-vanishing.md) gives zero for $q>\dim X$. A closed embedding in projective space and a [finite twisting resolution of a coherent sheaf on projective space](finite-twisting-resolution-of-a-coherent-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) reduce finiteness to the explicit [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md). The same resolution proves [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md) after sufficiently positive twisting.
