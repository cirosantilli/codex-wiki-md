# Trace norm

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_norm)

The trace norm of a finite-dimensional operator $A$ is the sum of its [singular values](singular-value.md):

$$
\lVert A\rVert_1=\operatorname{Tr}\sqrt{A^*A}.
$$

For a [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) $A=A_+-A_-$, it equals $\operatorname{Tr}A_++\operatorname{Tr}A_-$.
