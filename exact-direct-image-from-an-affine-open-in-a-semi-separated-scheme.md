# Exact direct image from an affine open in a semi-separated scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-separated scheme](semi-separated-scheme.md)

If $U$ is an [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) of a [semi-separated scheme](semi-separated-scheme.md), its [open immersion](open-immersion.md) $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ has exact [direct image](direct-image-sheaf.md) on [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md). Test on an affine open $V$ of $X$: the intersection $U\cap V$ is affine, and the [affine module-sheaf equivalence](affine-module-sheaf-equivalence.md) makes sections on this intersection exact. The direct image is also quasi-coherent, since this open immersion is an [affine morphism](affine-morphism.md).
