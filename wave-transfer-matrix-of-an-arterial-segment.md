# Wave transfer matrix of an arterial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Arterial pressure wave](arterial-pressure-wave.md)

For time dependence $e^{i\omega t}$ and a segment of length $\ell$, let $\beta=\omega\ell/c$. Adding the forward and backward [arterial pressure waves](arterial-pressure-wave.md) gives the displayed upstream-to-downstream matrix. The flow orientation is downstream at both ends. At a junction, [pressure](pressure.md) is continuous and incoming [volumetric flow rates](volumetric-flow-rate.md) balance outgoing ones.
