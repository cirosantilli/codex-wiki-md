# Skipping orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md)

A [skipping orbit](skipping-orbit.md) is the semiclassical path of a charged particle whose cyclotron arcs are repeatedly interrupted by a reflecting boundary. The centre of successive arcs advances along the edge. For [electrons](electron.md) in a perpendicular positive $B_z$, the opposite sides of a strip carry opposite directed motion. A smooth boundary replaces hard reflections by guiding-centre drift; the quantum counterpart is a [quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md).
