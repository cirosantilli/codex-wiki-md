# Quantum tunnelling

↑ **Parent:** [Potential barrier](potential-barrier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_tunnelling)

Quantum tunnelling is transmission through a [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md) even when the particle energy is below the barrier height.

The [wavefunction](wave-function.md) penetrates the classically inaccessible region; matching it to an outgoing [scattering state](scattering-state.md) on the far side gives a nonzero [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md). For a real, time-independent [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md), the transmitted [energy](energy.md) equals the incident [energy](energy.md): [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) does not require a temporary violation of [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md). A [tunnel junction](tunnel-junction.md) turns this small [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) into a measurable [electric current](electric-current.md), whose sensitivity to separation makes the [scanning tunnelling microscope](scanning-tunneling-microscope.md) possible.
