# Betatron oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

Small transverse oscillation about an accelerator's reference orbit. In a linear periodic lattice the displacement obeys [Hill's equation](hill-s-equation.md) $z''+k(s)z=0$.
