# Trapped surface

↑ **Parent:** [Null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trapped_surface)

A trapped surface is a closed spacelike codimension-two surface whose two future-directed orthogonal null congruences both have negative expansion.

In four-dimensional [spacetime](spacetime.md), “closed” here means [compact](compact-space.md) without [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md); “spacelike” means that the induced [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is positive definite. Let $k_+,k_-$ be the two future [null vector](null-vector.md) normals and $q_{ab}$ the induced [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). Their [null expansions](null-expansion.md) are

$$
\theta_\pm=q^{ab}\nabla_a(k_\pm)_b
=\frac{1}{dA}\frac{d(dA)}{d\lambda_\pm}.
$$

Here $dA$ is an infinitesimal transverse area element and $\lambda_\pm$ an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) along the respective normal [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md). A future [trapped surface](trapped-surface.md) has $\theta_+<0$ and $\theta_-<0$ everywhere: even the outward-directed light front initially loses area. Multiplying either future normal by a positive [function](function-split.md) multiplies its initial [null expansion](null-expansion.md) by that [function](function-split.md), preserving the sign. A small inward-converging patch is insufficient: the entire closed surface must satisfy both strict inequalities.
