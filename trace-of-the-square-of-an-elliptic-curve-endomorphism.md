# Trace of the square of an elliptic-curve endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Trace of an elliptic-curve endomorphism](trace-of-an-elliptic-curve-endomorphism.md)

The characteristic equation $\phi^2-[\operatorname{tr}\phi]\phi+[\deg\phi]=0$ gives

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\phi^2)=\operatorname{tr}(\phi)^2-2\deg\phi.
$$
