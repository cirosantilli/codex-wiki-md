# Spherical free-fall accretion rate

↑ **Parent:** [Mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md)

A steady full-sphere radial inflow has mass flux $\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho|v_r|$ by [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md). With Newtonian [free-fall speed](free-fall-speed.md) from rest at infinity, $|v_r|=\sqrt{2GM/r}$, this becomes the displayed expression. The assumed velocity then requires $\rho\propto r^{-3/2}$ along the steady flow. A local density and speed do not determine the total [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) of a general nonspherical inflow, and this estimate does not impose the sonic boundary condition of [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md).
