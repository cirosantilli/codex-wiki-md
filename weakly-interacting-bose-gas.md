# Weakly interacting Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

A dilute condensed Bose system whose interaction is sufficiently weak for a small-depletion expansion. Its low-energy excitations are described by a [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) rather than free particles. The homogeneous quadratic theory is controlled only while noncondensate occupation remains small compared with the condensate and its excitation energies are stable.
