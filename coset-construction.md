# Coset construction

↑ **Parent:** [Sugawara construction](sugawara-construction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coset_construction)

For a current subalgebra $\mathfrak h\subset\mathfrak g$, its currents have conformal weight one under both [Sugawara construction](sugawara-construction.md) stress tensors. Their difference therefore commutes with all $\mathfrak h$ currents and with the $\mathfrak h$ stress tensor. It obeys the [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) with [central charge](central-charge.md) $c_{\mathfrak g}-c_{\mathfrak h}$. For a positive-level compact unitary vacuum representation, $\|L_{-2}^{\mathfrak g/\mathfrak h}|0\rangle\|^2=(c_{\mathfrak g}-c_{\mathfrak h})/2\ge0$. A [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) has $c_{\mathfrak h}=\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak g$, giving the lower bound on $c_{\mathfrak g}$ under these hypotheses.
