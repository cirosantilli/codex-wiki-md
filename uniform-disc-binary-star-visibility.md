# Uniform-disc binary-star visibility

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-brightness binary-star visibility](equal-brightness-binary-star-visibility.md)

Two identical uniform circular discs of [angular diameter](angular-diameter.md) $\alpha$ and equal [flux](flux.md) have $g_\star(Y)=2J_1(x)/x$, $x=\pi\alpha Y/\lambda$, with limiting value one at $Y=0$. Multiplication by $\cos(\pi\beta Y/\lambda)$ gives their signed binary coherence. Its first single-disc envelope zero is $Y=1.21967\lambda/\alpha$, while the binary modulation has a separate scale $\lambda/\beta$. [Limb darkening](limb-darkening.md) and other nonuniform profiles change the envelope, not the elementary Fourier shift factor.
