# Centered discrete advection is skew-adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

With zero endpoints, the centered first-difference matrix has opposite off-diagonal entries and is a [skew-adjoint operator](skew-adjoint-generator.md) in the mesh-weighted Euclidean inner product. Adding a real multiple of it to the negative symmetric second-difference matrix does not change the real energy form. The semidiscrete convection-diffusion solution therefore obeys a mesh-independent [energy estimate](energy-estimate.md), even when its drift is large compared with its diffusion coefficient.
