# Logical qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum error correction](quantum-error-correction-split.md)

A [logical qubit](logical-qubit.md) is a two-dimensional encoded quantum subsystem with chosen orthonormal logical basis states $|0_L\rangle,|1_L\rangle$. An encoding [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) sends $\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$ to $\alpha|0_L\rangle+\beta|1_L\rangle$. In a [concatenated quantum error-correcting code](concatenated-quantum-error-correcting-code.md), one level's [logical qubit](logical-qubit.md) supplies one [qubit](qubit.md) of the next outer code.
