# Localized interchange criterion for a magnetized atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic buoyancy instability](magnetic-buoyancy-instability.md)

For disturbances constant along the horizontal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the minimized energy [density](density.md) is $\rho g[-(\ln\rho)'-\rho g/(\gamma p+C)]|\xi_z|^2$. If its coefficient is nonnegative everywhere, the full energy is nonnegative. If it is strictly negative at a point of a smooth equilibrium, a compactly supported vertical displacement in a neighbouring interval gives negative energy. These interchange motions compress gas and [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) together.
