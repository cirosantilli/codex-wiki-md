# Laplacian identity for the squared modulus of a holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Subharmonic function](subharmonic-function.md)

Write a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) as $f=u+iv$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) make $u,v$ [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) and give $u_x=v_y$, $u_y=-v_x$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) therefore gives $\Delta(u^2+v^2)=2(|\nabla u|^2+|\nabla v|^2)=4|f'|^2$. Thus its squared [modulus](modulus.md) is [subharmonic](subharmonic-function.md) and is harmonic only where $f'$ vanishes.
