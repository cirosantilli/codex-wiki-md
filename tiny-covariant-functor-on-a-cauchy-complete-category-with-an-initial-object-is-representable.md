# Tiny covariant functor on a Cauchy-complete category with an initial object is representable

↑ **Parent:** [Tiny object](tiny-object.md)

Let $\mathcal C$ be a small [Cauchy-complete category](cauchy-complete-category.md) with [initial object](initial-object.md) $0$. The terminal object of $[\mathcal C,\mathbf{Set}]$ is $h_0$, so $\operatorname{Nat}(P,F)\cong(F^P)(0)$. If $P$ is tiny, exponentiation by $P$ is itself a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) and preserves all colimits. Evaluation at zero also preserves colimits. Consequently $\operatorname{Nat}(P,-)$ preserves coproducts and epimorphisms, since every epimorphism in a set-valued functor category is the coequalizer of its [kernel pair](kernel-pair.md). Thus $P$ is an [irreducible projective in a set-valued functor category](irreducible-projective-in-a-set-valued-functor-category.md), hence [representable](representable-functor.md).
