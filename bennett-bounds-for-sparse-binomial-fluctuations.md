# Bennett bounds for sparse binomial fluctuations

↑ **Parent:** [Bennett inequality](bennett-inequality.md)

For a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) with $p_n\to0$ and $np_n\to\infty$, the [Bennett inequality](bennett-inequality.md) at $C$ standard deviations tends to $2e^{-C^2/2}$, whereas the [Hoeffding inequality](hoeffding-inequality.md) tends to two. The raw [Bennett inequality](bennett-inequality.md) bound is eventually smaller for any fixed $C>0$; after clipping at one it is nontrivial in the limit only when $C>\sqrt{2\log2}$.
