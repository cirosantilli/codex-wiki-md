# Allene

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allene)

An [allene](allene.md) contains the unit $\mathrm{C=C=C}$ with two consecutive [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) of order two. Suitable terminal [substituents](substituent.md) give [axial chirality](axial-chirality.md).
