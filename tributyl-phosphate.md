# Tributyl phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A neutral oxygen-donor extractant, $\mathrm{OP(OBu)_3}$, often abbreviated TBP. It can solvate metal nitrate species in [solvent extraction](solvent-extraction.md). Selectivity depends on acidity, metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md), diluent and subsequent stripping conditions.
