# Occupancy-preserving second-order bosonic hopping

↑ **Parent:** [Occupation-dependent virtual hopping](occupation-dependent-virtual-hopping.md)

With $X=\sum_s a_s^\dagger b_s$, $Y=X^\dagger$ and a real coefficient $f$, the fixed-occupancy second-order operator is $t[f(\Delta)XY-f(\Delta+2)YX]$. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) gives $XY=N_a+P$ and $YX=N_b+P$, where $P=\sum_{ss^\prime}a_s^\dagger a_{s^\prime}b_{s^\prime}^\dagger b_s$ exchanges internal states without changing site totals.
