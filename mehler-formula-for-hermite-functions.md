# Mehler formula for Hermite functions

↑ **Parent:** [Hermite function](hermite-function.md)

For $|t|<1$, this identity sums the [Hermite function](hermite-function.md) expansion into a Gaussian. One proof takes the [Bargmann-Fock space](bargmann-fock-space.md) inner product of the generating functions $\pi^{-1/4}\exp(-x^2/2+\sqrt2 xz-z^2/2)$ at $\sqrt t\,z$. Writing $z=u+iv$, the two resulting real [Gaussian integrals](gaussian-integral.md) have quadratic coefficients $1+t$ and $1-t$ and linear coefficients $\sqrt{2t}(x+y)$ and $i\sqrt{2t}(x-y)$. Evaluation gives the formula. Setting $t=e^{-2s}$ and multiplying by $e^{-s}$ gives the [harmonic oscillator transition kernel](harmonic-oscillator-transition-kernel.md) of $e^{-s(-d^2/dx^2+x^2)}$.
