# First fundamental form of a surface of revolution

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)

For

$$
X(u,v)=(f(v)\cos u,f(v)\sin u,g(v)),
$$

the coordinate tangent vectors are orthogonal and the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) is

$$
I=f(v)^2\,du^2+\bigl(f'(v)^2+g'(v)^2\bigr)\,dv^2.
$$
