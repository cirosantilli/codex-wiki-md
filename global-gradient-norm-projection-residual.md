# Global gradient-norm projection residual

↑ **Parent:** [Global discrete gradient-norm denoising](global-discrete-gradient-norm-denoising.md)

Let $C=A^*B_\alpha$ with one global dual ball. Its [support function](support-function.md) is $\alpha\|Au\|_2$, and the [proximal operator of a support function](proximal-operator-of-a-support-function.md) gives $u=g-P_Cg$. The removed component is the [convex projection](euclidean-projection-onto-a-convex-set.md), while the reconstructed [image signal](image-signal.md) is its residual. [Projected gradient descent](projected-gradient-descent.md) on $\tfrac12\|g-A^*p\|_2^2$, $p\in B_\alpha$, converges for $0<\tau<2/\|A\|^2$.
