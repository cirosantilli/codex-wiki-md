# Gaussian coordinates of deterministic orthonormal Wiener integrands

↑ **Parent:** [Itô integral](ito-integral.md)

Deterministic Itô integrals are jointly centered Gaussian by approximation from step functions and the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md). Their covariance is the L2 inner product of their integrands. Orthonormal integrands therefore produce independent standard-normal coordinates, with independence of a countable family understood through all finite subfamilies.
