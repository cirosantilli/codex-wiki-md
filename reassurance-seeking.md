# Reassurance seeking

↑ **Parent:** [Safety behavior](safety-behavior.md)

Seeking another person's confirmation of safety or certainty. In an [anxiety](anxiety.md)-maintaining cycle, immediate relief can reinforce repeated requests without changing the underlying interpretation. This function should be distinguished from appropriate information seeking or clinical assessment.
