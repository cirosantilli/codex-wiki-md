# Roton

↑ **Parent:** [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roton)

A [roton](roton.md) is an excitation near a local minimum of a [superfluid](superfluid.md)'s [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) at nonzero [momentum](momentum-split.md) $p_0$. The displayed local approximation defines a positive [energy](energy-split.md) gap $\Delta$ and positive curvature [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) $m_r$; it is not a free helium atom or a macroscopic [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md).[Landau, On the Theory of Superfluidity of Helium II (1947), p.91, Fig.1](on-the-theory-of-superfluidity-of-helium-ii-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md) In the stipulated plot, these correspond to $p_0=2p_*$, $\Delta=0.6E_*$ and $m_r=p_*^2/(4E_*)$. The plot illustrates how a low [energy](energy-split.md) at appreciable [momentum](momentum-split.md) can lower the [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md) below the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md), without assigning any measured parameter to real [helium](helium.md).

Landau's 1947 revision joined the low-[momentum](momentum-split.md) linear part to a spectrum with a finite-[momentum](momentum-split.md) minimum, retaining the macroscopic [superfluid](superfluid.md) theory while changing its excitation model.[Landau (1947), p.91, spectrum revision](on-the-theory-of-superfluidity-of-helium-ii-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)
