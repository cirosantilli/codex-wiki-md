# Unimodular intersection pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

An integral [intersection form](intersection-form.md) on a finite-rank free [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $L$ is unimodular if its adjoint $x\mapsto\lambda(x,-)$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) to the integral dual. In an integral basis, this is equivalent to the pairing matrix having determinant $1$ or $-1$, not merely nonzero determinant. For example, [projective lines generate a unimodular intersection form](projective-lines-generate-a-unimodular-intersection-form.md) on the [Complex projective plane](complex-projective-plane.md), with matrix $(1)$ in its complex [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
