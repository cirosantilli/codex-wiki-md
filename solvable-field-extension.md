# Solvable field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)

A finite separable extension is solvable when its normal closure has a [solvable group](solvable-group.md) as [Galois group](galois-group.md). In characteristic zero it is contained in a radical tower after adjoining suitable [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md); containment need not mean it is itself a tower of radical adjunctions.
