# Causal reachability with periodic time

↑ **Parent:** [Closed timelike curve](closed-timelike-curve.md)

In the flat quotient $t\equiv t+P_t$ with noncompact space, choose any sufficiently large positive or negative temporal winding to make a lifted displacement timelike. Every event is in both the [causal future](causal-future.md) and [causal past](causal-past.md) of every other event. Constant-position curves are [closed timelike curves](closed-timelike-curve.md). The quotient is a valid [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md), but it violates chronology and cannot support arbitrary globally consistent initial-value evolution.
