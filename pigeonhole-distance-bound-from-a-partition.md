# Pigeonhole distance bound from a partition

↑ **Parent:** [Pigeonhole principle](pigeonhole-principle.md)

Suppose a [subset](subset.md) of a [metric space](metric-space.md) is covered by $m$ [sets](set-split.md), each of [diameter](diameter.md) at most $D$. Assign each point to one covering [set](set-split.md) containing it, choosing a single [set](set-split.md) at shared boundaries. Among $m+1$ points, the [pigeonhole principle](pigeonhole-principle.md) puts two in a common [set](set-split.md), and their [distance](distance.md) is at most $D$. The pieces need not be equal or congruent. This freedom permits better [distance](distance.md) bounds than dividing a region only into equal [rectangles](rectangle.md).
