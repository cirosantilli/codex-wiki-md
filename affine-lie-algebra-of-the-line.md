# Affine Lie algebra of the line

↑ **Parent:** [Solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md)

The two-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of infinitesimal dilations and translations of a line has a [basis](basis.md) $h,e$ with $[h,e]=e$. Its [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) is the abelian line spanned by $e$, but every nontrivial term of its [lower central series of a Lie algebra](lower-central-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is that same line. It is solvable and not nilpotent.
