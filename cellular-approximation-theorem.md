# Cellular approximation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular map](cellular-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_approximation_theorem)

A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) between [CW complexes](cw-complex.md) is homotopic to a [cellular map](cellular-map.md). If the map is already cellular on a subcomplex, the homotopy can be taken relative to that subcomplex. In particular, a map from a $d$-dimensional [CW complex](cw-complex.md) can be deformed into the $d$-skeleton of the target. This permits finite [cellular chain complexes](cellular-chain-complex.md) to compute induced [homology](homology-split.md) maps, and turns maps to [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) into maps to an appropriate finite-dimensional projective skeleton.
