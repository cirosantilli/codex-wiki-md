# Marginal fragmentation mode of an isothermal slab

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal displacement energy of a self-gravitating slab](isothermal-displacement-energy-of-a-self-gravitating-slab.md)

For [mass density](density.md) $\rho=\rho_0\operatorname{sech}^2(z/H)$ with $H^2=c_s^2/(2\pi G\rho_0)$, a zero-frequency gravitational mode with $k\ne0$ obeys $W=0$. Its potential satisfies $\phi_{zz}+[2\operatorname{sech}^2(z/H)/H^2-k^2]\phi=0$. The substitution $\tau=\tanh(z/H)$ gives the [associated Legendre differential equation](associated-legendre-differential-equation.md) of degree one and order $|k|H$. Solutions decaying at both infinities exist only at $|k|H=1$, when $\phi\propto\operatorname{sech}(z/H)$. Longer horizontal wavelengths are unstable. Neutral mass-preserving relabeling displacements and the uniform vertical translation at $k=0$ are distinct from this fragmentation threshold.
