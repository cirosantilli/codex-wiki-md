# Depletion region

↑ **Parent:** [P-n junction](p-n-junction.md)

The region around a [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) with reduced mobile [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) density.

An illustrative abrupt-junction depletion approximation assumes uniform, fully ionized [donor impurities](donor-impurity.md) and [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md), negligible mobile space charge in the depleted layer, and [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\varepsilon_s$. For area $A$, doping [number densities](number-density.md) $N_A,N_D$ and built-in [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V_{\mathrm{bi}}$, it gives

$$
W\simeq\sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s}{q}\left(\frac1{N_A}+\frac1{N_D}\right)(V_{\mathrm{bi}}-V)},\qquad C_j\simeq\frac{\varepsilon_s A}{W}.
$$

Here $q>0$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) and $V$ is positive for [forward bias](forward-bias.md). Increasing both doping densities reduces $W$ in this estimate. That explains the preparation strategy for a [tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md); it does not make this sharp-depletion approximation a precise microscopic model of a [degenerate semiconductor](degenerate-semiconductor.md).
