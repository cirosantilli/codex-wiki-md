# Thin airfoil theory

↑ **Parent:** [Airfoil](airfoil.md)

A small-disturbance, two-dimensional [potential flow](potential-flow.md) model for a thin [airfoil](airfoil.md), using surface tangency and smooth flow departure at its trailing edge to determine [circulation](circulation-physics.md). Its [lift coefficient](lift-coefficient.md) slope is $2\pi$ per [radian](radian.md), with camber shifting the zero-lift [angle of attack](angle-of-attack.md). See [airfoil](airfoil.md) for assumptions, examples and the [aerodynamic stall](aerodynamic-stall.md) limit.
