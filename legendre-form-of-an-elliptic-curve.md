# Legendre form of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of characteristic different from two, a cubic [Weierstrass model](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) with distinct roots becomes this form by sending two roots to zero and one, then scaling $Y$ by a square root of the resulting nonzero coefficient. The third root becomes $\lambda\notin\{0,1\}$. The point $[0:1:0]$ is a [flex](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md) and the curve is a [genus one curve](genus-one-curve.md).
