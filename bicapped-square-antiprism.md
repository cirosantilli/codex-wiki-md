# Bicapped square antiprism

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

A ten-vertex cage formed by adding one cap over each square face of a square antiprism. Each axial cap is joined to four vertices in its nearer square; adjacent squares are rotated relative to each other. It is the usual idealized ten-vertex [closo cluster](closo-cluster.md) skeleton.
