# Voltage multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Rectifier](rectifier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_multiplier)

An [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) that uses [diodes](diode.md) and [capacitors](capacitor.md) to produce an output [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) larger in magnitude than the input peak. The attainable output depends on the circuit arrangement, [electric current](electric-current.md) drawn by the load, [capacitance](capacitance.md) and input [frequency](frequency.md); an ideal unloaded result does not specify the output under load.
