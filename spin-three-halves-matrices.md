# Spin-three-halves matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

In descending $S_z$ order, the spin-$3/2$ raising operator has superdiagonal entries $\hbar\sqrt3,2\hbar,\hbar\sqrt3$; $S_x$ and $S_y$ are its Hermitian real and imaginary combinations.
