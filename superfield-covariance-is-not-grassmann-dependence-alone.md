# Superfield covariance is not Grassmann dependence alone

↑ **Parent:** [Superfield](superfield.md)

A [superfield](superfield.md) is a field on [superspace](superspace.md) with a specified [Super-Poincaré group](super-poincare-group.md) transformation law. Being a polynomial in anticommuting coordinates alone does not specify this law or make coefficients a [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md). Conversely, a suitably graded function on full [superspace](superspace.md), acted on by coordinate pullback, is an unconstrained scalar superfield. A polynomial with spacetime-independent coefficients can be a special constant-component [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md); dependence on spacetime is not itself the defining criterion. Chirality and reality impose additional conditions.
