# Horizon of a convex polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polyhedron](convex-polyhedron.md)

For an external viewpoint in general position, the horizon is the cycle of edges separating visible and nonvisible faces of a [convex polyhedron](convex-polyhedron.md). In the [incremental three-dimensional convex hull algorithm](incremental-three-dimensional-convex-hull-algorithm.md), retain these edges, remove visible faces, and join the viewpoint to each horizon edge to form the new faces. Edges incident with two removed faces disappear.
