# Planetary differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

Separation of a planet's materials into compositionally different reservoirs, such as metal-rich core, silicate [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) and [crust](planetary-crust.md). Melting, gravitational segregation, crystallization and transport can all contribute. The conversion of gravitational energy during segregation is part of an early planet's thermal history.
