# Closed diagonal of a metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum product metric](maximum-product-metric.md)

For a [metric space](metric-space.md) with the same metric on both factors, the diagonal is a [closed subset](closed-set.md) of its product. At an off-diagonal point $(x,y)$ let $\delta=d(x,y)>0$. A maximum-metric ball of radius $\delta/3$ misses the diagonal, since a common coordinate $z$ would give $d(x,y)\le d(x,z)+d(z,y)<2\delta/3$. Equality of underlying sets without equality or compatibility of the metrics is a different assertion.
