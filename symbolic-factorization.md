# Symbolic factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse Gaussian elimination](sparse-gaussian-elimination.md)

[Symbolic factorization](symbolic-factorization.md) computes possible nonzero positions and update dependencies from a [sparse matrix](sparse-matrix.md) pattern and ordering before calculating numerical factor values. The [elimination graph](elimination-graph.md) predicts fill and the [elimination tree](elimination-tree.md) organizes dependencies. Numerical pivoting or exceptional cancellation can make the computed factor pattern differ from this structural prediction.
