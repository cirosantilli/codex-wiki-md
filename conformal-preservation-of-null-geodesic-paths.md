# Conformal preservation of null geodesic paths

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal factor](conformal-factor.md)

The difference of the two [Levi-Civita connections](levi-civita-connection.md) is $\widetilde\Gamma^a{}_{bc}-\Gamma^a{}_{bc}=\delta^a_b\partial_c\log\Omega+\delta^a_c\partial_b\log\Omega-g_{bc}g^{ad}\partial_d\log\Omega$. Along a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) with tangent $k$, the last term vanishes and the remaining acceleration is $2k(\log\Omega)k$. It is parallel to the tangent, so changing the [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) removes it. Thus a positive [conformal factor](conformal-factor.md) preserves unparametrized [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md), although it generally does not preserve timelike or spacelike geodesics.
