# Glycerophospholipid

↑ **Parent:** [Phospholipid](phospholipid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycerophospholipid)

[Glycerophospholipids](glycerophospholipid.md) are [phospholipids](phospholipid.md) built on a [glycerol](glycerol.md)-[phosphate](phosphate.md) scaffold. The illustrated two-[acyl group](acyl-group.md) form is common, but the subclass also includes other substitutions and one-chain derivatives. See [IUPAC–IUB lipid nomenclature, Lip-2.2 and Lip-2.6](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/lipid/lip1n2.html).
