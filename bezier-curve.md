<h1 id="bezier-curve">Bézier curve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bézier_curve)

A degree-$p$ [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md) with control points $B_j$ is $\sum_{j=0}^p\binom pj(1-t)^{p-j}t^jB_j$. Its [Bernstein polynomial](bernstein-polynomial.md) basis gives affine invariance and the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) property.
