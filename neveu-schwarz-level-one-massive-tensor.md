<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-level-one-massive-tensor">Neveu–Schwarz level-one massive tensor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md)

The unprojected level-one states form a transverse [vector representation](vector-representation.md) and an [exterior square](exterior-square.md). Fermionic anticommutation makes the two-fermion states antisymmetric. Together they restrict from an antisymmetric rank-two tensor of $SO(D-1)$, not a [massive spin-two field](massive-spin-two-field.md).
