# Finite-thickness disk relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md)

For a disk with thickness $z_0$, random relative speeds $u=\lambda v$ and small-impact cutoff $b_0\ll z_0\ll R$, the leading three-dimensional logarithmic encounter term has upper cutoff of order $z_0$. At fixed [mass](mass.md) and radius, its number [volume](volume.md) density exceeds the spherical density by order $R/z_0$. Hence $t_{\rm disk}/t_{\rm sphere}\sim\lambda^3(z_0/R)\log(R/b_0)/\log(z_0/b_0)$ when the same cutoff is used for comparison. Different speed-dependent cutoffs must be distinguished in numerical applications. Distant planar encounters contribute a convergent, non-logarithmic correction. This scaling ceases to apply if thickness is comparable to the cutoff.
