# Mathieu equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

The Mathieu equation is the displayed [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) with periodic coefficients. [Floquet theory](floquet-theory.md) describes its bounded and exponentially growing solutions, while its periodic [Mathieu characteristic values](mathieu-characteristic-value.md) mark the edges of [parametric resonance](parametric-resonance.md) gaps. The forcing normalization matters: replacing $t$ by $t/2$ rescales both the constant and periodic coefficients.
