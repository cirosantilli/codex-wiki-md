# Poor solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

A [poor solvent](poor-solvent.md) favors effective attraction between [polymer](polymer-split.md) segments relative to solvent contacts. A dilute flexible chain tends to contract, and sufficiently unfavorable conditions produce a compact [polymer globule](polymer-globule.md). Solvent quality depends on the polymer, solvent and [temperature](temperature-split.md). The simple [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) identifies the poor-solvent side of the theta condition with $\chi>1/2$; this does not guarantee that every finite-density mixture is inside its spinodal.
