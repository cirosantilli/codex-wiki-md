# Degree as a sum of local degrees

↑ **Parent:** [Local degree of a continuous map](local-degree-of-a-continuous-map.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:M\to N$ of closed connected oriented $d$-dimensional [manifolds](topological-manifold.md), suppose $f^{-1}(y)=\{x_1,\ldots,x_s\}$ is finite. Then

$$
\deg f=\sum_{j=1}^s\deg_{x_j}f.
$$

By [excision](excision-theorem.md), the degree-$d$ [relative homology](relative-homology.md) group $H_d(M,M\setminus f^{-1}(y);\mathbb Z)$ is the direct sum of the local groups at $x_j$. The [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) maps to the tuple of local orientation generators. Mapping this tuple to $H_d(N,N\setminus\{y\};\mathbb Z)$ adds their local degrees. Naturality identifies the result with the image of $(\deg f)[N]$. If the fiber is empty, the same argument gives degree zero.
