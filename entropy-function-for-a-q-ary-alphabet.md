# Entropy function for a q-ary alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy.md)

This is the entropy in base $q$ of the distribution with one symbol of probability $1-x$ and each of the other $q-1$ symbols of probability $x/(q-1)$. It increases on $[0,1-1/q]$ from zero to one, and governs the exponential volume of a [Hamming ball](hamming-ball.md). For $q=2$ it is the [binary entropy](binary-entropy.md) function.
