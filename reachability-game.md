# Reachability game

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A turn-based game on a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) in which a player tries to force a designated target set. Its winning region is a least [fixed point](fixed-point.md): add that player's [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with some winning successor and the opponent's [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with all successors winning. The finite-[graph](graph-split.md) version uses the same construction as a [winning-position attractor in an infinite game](winning-position-attractor-in-an-infinite-game.md).

With disjoint terminal wins for two players and infinite plays valued as draws, compute both reachability attractors while keeping terminals fixed. Entry ranks give terminating [winning strategies](winning-strategy-in-an-infinite-game.md); positions outside both regions admit defence avoiding a forced loss. A cycle alone is not a certificate of a draw if it also offers a forceable winning exit.
