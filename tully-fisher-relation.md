# Tully-Fisher relation

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

An empirical relation between the [luminosity](luminosity.md) of a disk [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) and its [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) [speed](speed.md). A simple virial and surface-brightness argument gives $L\propto V^4/(\Upsilon^2I)$, so changes in [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) or [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) change the slope.
