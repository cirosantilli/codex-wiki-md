# Single-field inflation squeezed-limit consistency relation

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

For a single-clock adiabatic inflationary background, a long [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) acts on short modes as a spatial dilation. The squeezed bispectrum therefore obeys

$$
\lim_{q\to0}B_{\mathcal R}(q,k,|\mathbf k+\mathbf q|)
=-(n_s-1)P_{\mathcal R}(q)P_{\mathcal R}(k)
$$

at leading order in $q/k$.
