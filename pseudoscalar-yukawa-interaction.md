# Pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

A [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md) [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md) couples a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) bilinear containing the [chirality](chirality-physics.md) matrix to a spin-zero field. In the standard mostly-minus [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) convention with $\gamma^5=i\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$ and the usual [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md), $ig\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi\phi$ is Hermitian for real $g$ and real $\phi$. If $\phi$ is a pseudoscalar, the interaction also preserves [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md); if $\phi$ is parity even, it breaks that [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). Moving the factor $i$ into the definition of $\gamma^5$ changes the displayed vertex coefficient but not the interaction. Each vertex has one bosonic leg and two oppositely oriented fermion legs, and preserves [Dirac fermion number conservation](dirac-fermion-number-conservation.md).
