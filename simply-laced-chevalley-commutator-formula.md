# Simply laced Chevalley commutator formula

↑ **Parent:** [Chevalley basis](chevalley-basis.md)

For equal-length [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) $r,s$ with a root sum, their angle is $2\pi/3$. The vectors $2r+s$ and $r+2s$ have squared length three times a root's squared length, so neither is a root. Thus $[e_r,e_{r+s}]=[e_s,e_{r+s}]=0$. Apply the [central-commutator exponential identity](central-commutator-exponential-identity.md) to $A=t\operatorname{ad}e_r$ and $B=u\operatorname{ad}e_s$, whose commutator is $tuN_{r,s}\operatorname{ad}e_{r+s}$, to obtain the displayed identity over the complex numbers.
