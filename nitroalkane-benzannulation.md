# Nitroalkane benzannulation

↑ **Parent:** [Benzannulation](benzannulation.md)

A nitroalkane [carbon](carbon.md) can join the two ends of a five-[carbon](carbon.md) conjugated [ketone](ketone.md) framework. [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond formation, [thiolate](thiolate.md) displacement and [nitrite](nitrite.md) elimination can build a substituted [phenol](phenol.md). The nitro-bearing [carbon](carbon.md), rather than the nitro [nitrogen](nitrogen.md), enters the [aromatic ring](aromatic-ring.md).
