# Perspectivity from a point

↑ **Parent:** [Desargues's theorem](desargues-s-theorem.md)

Two [projective triangles](projective-triangle.md) are in perspectivity from a point when the three [projective lines](projective-line.md) joining corresponding vertices meet in one [projective point](projective-point.md), called their center of perspectivity.
