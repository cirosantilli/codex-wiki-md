# SEC protein conformation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [SEC light-scattering detection](sec-light-scattering-detection.md)

An authored manufacturer experiment used an Agilent 1260 Infinity Bio-inert Quaternary LC with Bio SEC-3 $300\,\mathrm{\mathring A}$ columns, $7.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ diameter and $150$ or $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ length. Four pump channels mixed [water](water.md), [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md), and two [phosphate](phosphate.md) solutions into $20\,\mathrm{mmol\,L^{-1}}$ [phosphate](phosphate.md) at [pH](ph.md) $7.0$, varying salt from $0.2$ to $0.6\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. At $1.0\,\mathrm{mL\,min^{-1}}$ and $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, the readouts were diode-array [absorbance](absorbance.md), differential [refractive index](refractive-index.md), and scattering at $15^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. A supplied [protein](protein-split.md) standard mixture calibrated retention; bovine serum albumin calibrated scattering constants and inter-detector delay. Increasing salt improved separation of two α-chymotrypsinogen A peaks; two $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ columns also resolved peaks that one column did not. At $0.5\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ salt, the two scattering-derived estimates were about $26{,}100$ and $25{,}800\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. The author inferred a possible size-changing conformation difference, rather than doubling of [molar mass](molar-mass.md). The same poster shows delayed, tailing [lysozyme](lysozyme.md) peaks from unwanted interactions.[Andrew Coffey, Size Exclusion Chromatography of Biomolecules – Detector Combinations to Obtain Maximum Information with Minimum Effort, HPLC 2013, poster 901: Experimental; Figures 3–7; Table 1](size-exclusion-chromatography-of-biomolecules-detector-combinations-to-obtain-maximum-information-with-minimum-effort-by-andrew-coffey.md)

The salt comparison and the independent mass readout constrain interpretation, but do not uniquely prove a particular [protein structure](protein-structure.md). Salt can change [protein folding](protein-folding.md), [protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md), and packing interactions together. In ideal [SEC](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) there is no [adsorption](adsorption.md); attraction from the [hydrophobic effect](hydrophobic-effect.md) or forces described by [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md) can cause additional retention, tailing, and loss of recovered sample. Repulsion can instead reduce pore entry. A peak beyond $V_t$ contradicts the simple nonbinding model, rather than extending its size calibration.

Useful proposed controls compare a [solvent](solvent.md) blank, fresh calibration standards bracketing the sample, several injection amounts and volumes, and recovered sample quantity with injected quantity using an appropriately calibrated detector. Keep [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md) fixed while testing [pH](ph.md) or [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md). Compare an independent mass readout before declaring an early peak an aggregate. Loss of an insoluble species during filtration, on-column association or dissociation, overloading, and broad unresolved peaks can all hide the original sample population. These are interpretation checks and limitations, not measurements invented for the figures.
