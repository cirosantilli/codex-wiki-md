# ER lumen

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

The [ER lumen](er-lumen.md) is the internal aqueous compartment of the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md). A [ribosome](ribosome.md) attached to its membrane sits on the [cytosol](cytosol.md) side, not inside the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md). This distinction separates the machinery making a [protein](protein-split.md) from the compartment receiving it.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Membrane-bound Ribosomes Define the Rough ER, Figure 12-36: cytosolic ribosomes and lumenal delivery](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)
