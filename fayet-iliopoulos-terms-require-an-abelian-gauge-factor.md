<h1 id="fayet-iliopoulos-terms-require-an-abelian-gauge-factor">Fayet–Iliopoulos terms require an Abelian gauge factor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md)

A constant linear auxiliary-field term $\xi_aD^a$ is [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) only when $\xi$ is an invariant vector in the dual adjoint representation. Equivalently, it annihilates all Lie brackets. A simple non-Abelian [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) equals its commutator algebra and has no such nonzero vector. Abelian factors do admit the term. Identifying a Fayet–Iliopoulos parameter in a simple non-Abelian theory therefore requires setting it to zero, rather than treating it as an arbitrary coupling.
