# Truncated first moment bound

↑ **Parent:** [Paley-Zygmund inequality](paley-zygmund-inequality.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with finite mean $\mu$ and $0\leq\theta\leq1$, the part of the [expectation](expected-value.md) on $X\leq\theta\mu$ is at most $\theta\mu$. Subtracting gives $\mathbb E[X\mathbf1_{\{X>\theta\mu\}}]\geq(1-\theta)\mu$. If the [second moment](second-moment.md) is positive and finite, applying the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to $X$ and the [indicator variable](indicator-variable.md) gives the [Paley-Zygmund inequality](paley-zygmund-inequality.md) $\mathbb P(X>\theta\mu)\geq(1-\theta)^2\mu^2/\mathbb E[X^2]$.
