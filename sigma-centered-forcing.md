# Sigma-centered forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Centered subset of a forcing order](centered-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md)

A forcing order is sigma-centered when it is the union of countably many [centered subsets](centered-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md). Every sigma-centered forcing has the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md).
