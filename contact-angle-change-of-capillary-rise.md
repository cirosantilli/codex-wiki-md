# Contact-angle change of capillary rise

↑ **Parent:** [Jurin's law](jurin-s-law.md)

For unchanged [fluid](fluid.md), [gravity](gravity.md) and capillary geometry, [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) rise is proportional to the cosine of the [contact angle](contact-angle.md) measured through the liquid. Changing from angle $\theta$ to [complete wetting](complete-wetting.md) therefore multiplies the height by $1/\cos\theta$. If contamination also changes liquid [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) or [density](density.md), the contact-angle ratio alone does not fix the new height.
