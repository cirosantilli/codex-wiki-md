# M-M-s-K queue

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death process](birth-death-process.md)

An $M/M/s/K$ queue has arrivals forming a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of rate $\lambda$, $s$ servers with independent service times having an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\mu$, and total system capacity $K\geq s$. Its occupancy is the finite [birth-death process](birth-death-process.md) on $\{0,\ldots,K\}$ with

$$
b_n=\lambda\mathbf1_{\{n<K\}},
\qquad
d_n=\mu\min(n,s).
$$
