# Three-quark flavour singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Eightfold way](eightfold-way.md)

The antisymmetric flavour representation $\bigwedge^3\mathbf3$ has dimension one and consists of the alternating $uds$ combination. Its sole weight is $(I_3,Y)=(0,0)$, distinct as a representation from the octet $\Lambda$ at the same weight. The [Pauli constraint on three-quark flavour multiplets](pauli-constraint-on-three-quark-flavour-multiplets.md) requires orbital excitation for a three-quark colour-singlet realization.
