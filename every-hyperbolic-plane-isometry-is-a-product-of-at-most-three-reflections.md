# Every hyperbolic-plane isometry is a product of at most three reflections

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic reflection](hyperbolic-reflection.md)

A reflection in the perpendicular bisector of a point and its image corrects that point. A second bisector reflection through the corrected point can correct another point without moving the first. An [isometry](isometry.md) fixing two distinct points is either the identity or reflection in their joining geodesic: points with prescribed distances from both centres have at most two possibilities, exchanged by that reflection. Fixing a third noncollinear point then fixes every point. This constructs a factorization with at most three [hyperbolic reflections](hyperbolic-reflection.md).
