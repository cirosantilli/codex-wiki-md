<h1 id="finite-bohm-approximant">Finite Böhm approximant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Böhm tree](bohm-tree.md)

A finite Böhm approximant is $\bot$ or a finite [lambda term](lambda-term.md) in the grammar $\lambda x_1\cdots x_n.yA_1\cdots A_r$, obtained by pruning a [Böhm tree](bohm-tree.md). Equivalently it is a beta-bottom normal form obtained from a beta-equivalent term by replacing subterms by $\bot$; bottom reduction contracts $\bot N$ and $\lambda x.\bot$ to $\bot$. Its interpretation in the [graph model of lambda calculus](graph-model-of-lambda-calculus.md) is included in that of the original term.
