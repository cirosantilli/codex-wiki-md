# Boundary virial term of a singular isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

A finite spherical subvolume has radial-pressure virial surface term $4\pi R^3\rho(R)\sigma_r^2$. The infinite [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) has divergent total mass and energies, so a finite isolated [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) cannot omit this term. Different orbital distributions with the same density can have different radial boundary pressures.
