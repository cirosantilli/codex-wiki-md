# Lorentz invariance of optical cavity resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Optical cavity](optical-cavity.md)

A [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) of a resonant [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) transforms both the electromagnetic field and the mirror worldlines. Boundary conditions and event-by-event phase matching remain satisfied. For a longitudinal boost, a cavity of rest length $L$ has length $L/\gamma$ and unequal forward/backward frequencies $\gamma\omega_0(1\mp\beta)$. The coordinate round-trip time is $2\gamma L/c$, while the phase rate on a moving mirror is $\omega_0/\gamma$, so the round-trip phase is still $2\omega_0L/c$. A stationary-mirror resonance formula with only length contraction inserted describes the wrong boundary-value problem.
