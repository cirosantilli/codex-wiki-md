# Augmented chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

An augmented [chain complex](chain-complex.md) includes an augmentation $\varepsilon:C_0\to\mathbb Z$ with $\varepsilon d_1=0$. For a nonempty [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md), the augmentation sends every vertex to $1$ and the augmented homology in degree zero is its [reduced homology](reduced-homology.md).
