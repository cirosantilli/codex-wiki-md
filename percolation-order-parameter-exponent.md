# Percolation order-parameter exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation critical exponents](percolation-critical-exponents.md)

The leading power $\beta$ of the [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md) approached from above the [percolation critical probability](percolation-critical-probability.md). It quantifies how the density of the [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) first becomes positive.
