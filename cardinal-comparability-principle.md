# Cardinal comparability principle

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md)

For any sets $X,Y$, either $X$ injects into $Y$ or $Y$ injects into $X$. Applying this to $X$ and its Hartogs ordinal proves the [well-ordering theorem](well-ordering-theorem.md), so over ZF cardinal comparability is equivalent to choice.
