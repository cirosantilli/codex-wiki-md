# Kappa-tree

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md)

For a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, a [set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md) of height $\kappa$ with nonempty levels of [cardinality](cardinality.md) less than $\kappa$. The regular-height convention is important for the [unbounded-extension kernel of a regular tree](unbounded-extension-kernel-of-a-regular-tree.md).
