# Cooperation

↑ **Parent:** [Behavioural ecology](behavioural-ecology.md)

A social interaction in which a participant provides a benefit to another; classifications distinguish immediate actor benefits from evolutionary [altruism](altruism.md). Group benefit alone does not explain persistence of an individually costly contribution.
