# Uniform-pressure friction torque on a circular contact

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md)

A flat circular contact of radius $a$ carrying total normal [force](force-split.md) $N$ has uniform [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p=N/(\pi a^2)$. In steady relative rotation, a ring of radius $s$ and thickness $ds$ experiences tangential [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) $dF=\mu p\,2\pi s\,ds$. Its resisting [torque](torque.md) is $d\tau=s\,dF$. [Integration](integral-split.md) from zero to $a$ gives $\tau_f=2\mu Na/3$. A different pressure distribution changes the result; for an axisymmetric distribution use $\tau_f=2\pi\mu\int_0^a p(s)s^2ds$.
