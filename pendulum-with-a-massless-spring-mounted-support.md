# Pendulum with a massless spring-mounted support

↑ **Parent:** [Normal modes of a spring-mounted pendulum cart](normal-modes-of-a-spring-mounted-pendulum-cart.md)

For a bob of mass $m$ on a massless rod of length $\ell$, let a massless support slide horizontally against a [spring](spring.md) of stiffness $k$. With support displacement $s=\ell\eta$ and angle $\theta$ from downward vertical, the quadratic [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md), divided by $m\ell^2$, is $(\dot\eta+\dot\theta)^2/2-(k/m)\eta^2/2-(g/\ell)\theta^2/2$. Subtracting its two [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $\eta=mg\theta/(k\ell)$. There is one finite [normal mode](normal-mode.md), with the displayed frequency. A stiff spring fixes the support and recovers the [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md) frequency. The missing second finite frequency reflects a [singular kinetic matrix in small oscillations](singular-kinetic-matrix-in-small-oscillations.md), not an omitted oscillation branch.
