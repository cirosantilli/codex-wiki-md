# Stellar luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity](luminosity.md)

The bolometric luminosity of a [star](star.md) is its total radiated [power](power.md) integrated over all [wavelengths](wavelength.md). A band or spectral [luminosity](luminosity.md) instead specifies a restricted spectral range. The [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) compares total luminosity with [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md).
