# Look-and-say sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

Each term is obtained by reading the lengths and values of consecutive runs in the previous term. Starting from $1$ gives $1,11,21,1211,111221,\ldots$: for example, $21$ contains one $2$ followed by one $1$, so its successor is $1211$.
