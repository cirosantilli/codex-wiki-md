# Square of a number

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation](exponentiation.md)

The square of a number $a$ is the product $a\cdot a$. For a [real number](real-number.md), it is nonnegative. The [sum of squares](sum-of-squares.md) of real coordinates is the squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) of the corresponding [vector](vector.md).
