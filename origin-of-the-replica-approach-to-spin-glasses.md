# Origin of the replica approach to spin glasses

↑ **Parent:** [Spin glass](spin-glass.md)

In his 1985 recollection, [Sam Edwards](sam-edwards.md) traced his work with [Philip Warren Anderson](philip-warren-anderson.md) to Saturday discussions and an analogy with randomly crosslinked polymers. He adapted methods from rubber theory during train journeys while chairing the Science Research Council.[Edwards, commentary dated 17 July 1985, published 16 September 1985, collaboration and train journeys](citation-classic-commentary-on-theory-of-spin-glasses-by-sam-edwards.md)
