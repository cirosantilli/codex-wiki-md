# Optical pumping preparation and readout

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pumping](optical-pumping.md)

[Circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) selects the sign of the absorbed [photon](photon.md)'s [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) along the beam. Choose that direction as $z$ and define $\sigma^+$ by the absorption rule $m'=m+1$, where $m$ is the [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md). [Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) can return an atom to a different [magnetic sublevel](magnetic-sublevel.md), so absorption followed by emission is a population-transfer cycle. A sublevel that the chosen beam cannot excite is dark to that beam. Darkness is relative to the selected transition and [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md), rather than a claim that the atom cannot interact with any [light](light.md).

A static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) along $z$ defines a stable axis and gives [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md). A transverse oscillating [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can redistribute [magnetic sublevel](magnetic-sublevel.md) populations when its [frequency](frequency.md) matches an allowed splitting. With the appropriate optical transition, that redistribution restores [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md): transmitted intensity decreases, or collected [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) increases. These are alternative readouts whose detailed sensitivity depends on the transition, viewing direction and [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md).
