# Hyperpolarized magnetic resonance imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic resonance imaging](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md)

[Magnetic resonance imaging](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) with a prepared nuclear population imbalance exceeding ordinary thermal polarization under the imaging conditions. [Dynamic nuclear polarization](dynamic-nuclear-polarization.md), optical spin transfer and spin-order transfer offer different preparation routes. Preparation and acquisition must account for [spin-lattice relaxation](spin-lattice-relaxation.md), which erases the excess polarization.
