# Burst kinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md)

A time course with rapid initial formation of a measured [reaction product](product-chemistry.md), followed by slower steady-state turnover. It can reveal fast initial chemistry followed by slow regeneration of competent [enzyme](enzyme-split.md); the monitored product and mixing time resolution are essential to interpretation.
