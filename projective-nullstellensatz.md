# Projective Nullstellensatz

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md)

Let $I\subseteq k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ be a [homogeneous ideal](homogeneous-ideal.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), and let

$$
\mathfrak m=(x_0,\ldots,x_n)
$$

be the irrelevant ideal. Then $V_+(I)=\varnothing$ exactly when either $I=(1)$ or $\sqrt I=\mathfrak m$. For a proper homogeneous ideal this is also equivalent to $\mathfrak m^N\subseteq I$ for some $N$.

It is the homogeneous projective version of the [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md).
