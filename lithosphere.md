# Lithosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Solid Earth](solid-earth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithosphere)

The mechanically strong outer shell of a rocky planet, including crust and the colder upper mantle. It can form mobile plates in [plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md) or a relatively stagnant lid. Its temperature, water content and damage affect its ability to yield.
