# Extended centre-manifold reductions of the 2019 Cambridge reflection-symmetric system

↑ **Parent:** [Center manifold](center-manifold.md)

For

$$
\dot x=x+y^2-a,
\qquad
\dot y=y(4x-x^2-a),
$$

the [extended centre manifold for a parameter](extended-centre-manifold-for-a-parameter.md) gives the local reduced equations

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
(a,x,y)&\text{local parameter and centre coordinate}&\text{reduced equation}\\ \hline
(0,0,0)&\mu=a,\ y&\dot y=3\mu y-4y^3+O(4),\\
(3,3,0)&\mu=a-3,\ y&\dot y=-3\mu y+2y^3+O(4),\\
(4,2,y_0)&\mu=a-4,\ v=y-y_0&\dot v=-y_0(\mu+8v^2)+O(v^3,\mu v),
\end{array}
$$

where $y_0=\pm\sqrt2$. Thus the first two points undergo respectively supercritical and subcritical [symmetry-forced pitchfork bifurcations](symmetry-forced-pitchfork-bifurcation.md), while each of the last two points undergoes a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md).
