# Phosphatidylserine

↑ **Parent:** [Glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md)

[phosphatidylserine](phosphatidylserine.md) is a [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md) with a serine-containing headgroup that is normally net negatively charged near physiological [pH](ph.md). Its leaflet distribution contributes to membrane surface properties and protein recruitment.
