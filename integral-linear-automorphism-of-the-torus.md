# Integral linear automorphism of the torus

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)

Every $A\in GL_2(\mathbb Z)$ induces a based homeomorphism

$$
f_A:\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2\to\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2,
\qquad
f_A([r])=[Ar].
$$

Its inverse is $f_{A^{-1}}$, and under the [lift-endpoint description of the fundamental group of the torus](lift-endpoint-description-of-the-fundamental-group-of-the-torus.md), its induced map on $\pi_1(T^2)$ is multiplication by $A$.
