# L-function of a cusp form

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp form](cusp-form.md)

For $f=\sum_{n\geq1}a_nq^n$, define $L(f,s)=\sum_{n\geq1}a_nn^{-s}$ in a right half-plane. Its completion $(2\pi)^{-s}\Gamma(s)L(f,s)$ is the [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) of $f(iy)$. The modular inversion and exponential cusp decay continue this completion to an entire function and give its reflection $s\mapsto k-s$. A normalized [Hecke eigenform](hecke-eigenform.md) also has an [Euler product of a Hecke eigenform](euler-product-of-a-hecke-eigenform.md).
