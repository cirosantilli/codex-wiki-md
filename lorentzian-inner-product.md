# Lorentzian inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md)

A [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md) is a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form with one negative and the remaining positive eigenvalues in the mostly-plus convention. Two nonzero future causal vectors have nonpositive inner product; if either is timelike, it is strictly negative.
