# Local-count independence in a linear flow network

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary law of a linear flow network](stationary-law-of-a-linear-flow-network.md)

Summing the [stationary law of a linear flow network](stationary-law-of-a-linear-flow-network.md) over its through-flow count gives independent local [geometric distributions](geometric-distribution.md) with ratios $q_i=\rho_i/(1-\rho_0)$. This gives the displayed means. The local counts are independent of one another in this marginal distribution; the through count is generally dependent on them. The [independence](independent-random-variables.md) is a stationary distributional fact, not an assertion that their dynamics evolve independently.
