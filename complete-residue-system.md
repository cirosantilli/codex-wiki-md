# Complete residue system

↑ **Parent:** [Congruence class](congruence-class.md)

A complete residue system modulo a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $m$ contains exactly one representative of every [congruence class](congruence-class.md) modulo $m$. Both $\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$ and $\{1,\ldots,m\}$ are examples. Counting the representatives that are [coprime](coprime-integers.md) to $m$ gives [Euler's totient function](euler-totient-function.md); those representatives form a reduced residue system.
