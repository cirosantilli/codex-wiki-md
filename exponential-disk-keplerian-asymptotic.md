# Exponential-disk Keplerian asymptotic

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential galactic disk](exponential-galactic-disk.md)

For an [exponential galactic disk](exponential-galactic-disk.md), the first nonspherical term in the [Legendre expansion of thin-disk gravity](legendre-expansion-of-thin-disk-gravity.md) is positive and proportional to $R^{-3}$ in $v_c^2$. The missing enclosed mass and exterior-ring terms are exponentially small at large radius. Hence the [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) approaches the point-mass Keplerian curve from above.
