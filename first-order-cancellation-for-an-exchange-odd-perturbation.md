# First-order cancellation for an exchange-odd perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md)

Let $X$ be a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) with $X^2=I$, and let $P$ be the orthogonal [projection operator](projection-linear-algebra.md) onto an unperturbed [energy eigenspace](energy-eigenspace.md). Suppose $X$ acts as the same sign $\varepsilon=\pm1$ throughout that [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) and $XVX=-V$. For any $u,v$ in the [eigenspace](eigenspace.md), preservation of the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) gives $\langle u,Vv\rangle=\langle Xu,XVv\rangle=-\langle\varepsilon u,V\varepsilon v\rangle=-\langle u,Vv\rangle$. Hence every projected [matrix element](matrix-element.md) vanishes, so $PVP=0$ and [degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) gives zero first-order [energy](energy-split.md) corrections. A zero diagonal alone would be insufficient. For equal [spins](spin.md), the [exchange parity of two coupled identical spins](exchange-parity-of-two-coupled-identical-spins.md) is constant on each total-[spin](spin.md) sector, while their spin-projection difference changes sign under exchange.
