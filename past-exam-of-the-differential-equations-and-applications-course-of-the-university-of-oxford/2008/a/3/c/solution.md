The adjoint also leaves $E$ invariant, with $L^*a=5a-15b$ and $L^*b=3a-9b$. By [adjoint in weighted coordinates](../../../../../adjoint-in-weighted-coordinates.md), its [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) in the same [basis](../../../../../basis.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}5&3\\-15&-9\end{pmatrix}$. Solving the two [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) equations gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda=0:\ z_0=3e^{-x}-5e^{-2x};\qquad\lambda=-4:\ z_{-4}=e^{-x}-3e^{-2x}.}
$$

The [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $a,b$ is not [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md), so one must not simply transpose the previous numerical [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) without accounting for its [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md). Directly transposing the integral [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) avoids that error.
