<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) different from one lies in $E=\operatorname{span}\{a,b\}$, because $(\lambda-1)y=Ky\in E$. The elementary overlaps are $\langle a,a\rangle=1/2$, $\langle a,b\rangle=1/3$, $\langle b,b\rangle=1/4$. Thus

$$
La=-9a+6b,\quad Lb=-\tfrac{15}2a+5b,\quad
[L]_E=\begin{pmatrix}-9&-15/2\\6&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda(\lambda+4)$, so the finite-[multiplicity](../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) eigenpairs are

$$
\boxed{\lambda=0:\ y_0=5e^{-x}-6e^{-2x};\qquad\lambda=-4:\ y_{-4}=3e^{-x}-2e^{-2x}.}
$$

Each [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. On $E^\perp$, both integrals defining $K$ vanish, so $Ly=y$. Thus $\boxed{\lambda=1}$ is also an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), of infinite [multiplicity](../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md), and there are no others.
