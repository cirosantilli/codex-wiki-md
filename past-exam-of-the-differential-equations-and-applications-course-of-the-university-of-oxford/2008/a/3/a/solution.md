<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the integral part as $Ky=12b\langle a,y\rangle-30a\langle b,y\rangle$, where $a(x)=e^{-x}$ and $b(x)=e^{-2x}$. The [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is real, so its adjoint transposes the two arguments. Hence

$$
\boxed{L^*z(x)=z(x)+\int_0^\infty[12e^{-x-2t}-30e^{-2x-t}]z(t)\,dt.}
$$

This follows by exchanging the two [integrations](../../../../../integral-split.md) in the $L^2(0,\infty)$ [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md); the exponential factors make the [finite-rank operators](../../../../../finite-rank-operator.md) bounded.
