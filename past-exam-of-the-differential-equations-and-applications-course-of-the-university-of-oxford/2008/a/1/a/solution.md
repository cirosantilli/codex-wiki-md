<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $y=\dot x$. The [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md)-plane system is $\dot x=y$, $\dot y=-9x+\epsilon(1-4x^2)y$. The origin is its only [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), and its [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-9&\epsilon\end{pmatrix}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=\frac\epsilon2\pm i\sqrt{9-\epsilon^2/4}.}
$$

For the stated small positive $\epsilon$, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) have positive real part and nonzero imaginary part: the origin is an **[unstable focus](../../../../../unstable-spiral.md)**.
