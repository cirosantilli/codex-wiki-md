<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [method of multiple scales](../../../../../method-of-multiple-scales.md) with independent fast and slow variables $t$ and $\tau=\epsilon t$, and write $x_0=R(\tau)\cos\Theta$, $\Theta=3t+\Phi(\tau)$. At the next order, $x_{1,tt}+9x_1=-2x_{0,t\tau}-(4x_0^2-1)x_{0,t}$. Its resonant terms are

$$
[6R'+3R(R^2-1)]\sin\Theta+6R\Phi'\cos\Theta.
$$

Suppressing [secular terms](../../../../../secular-term.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{dR_0}{d\tau}=\frac12R_0(1-R_0^2),\qquad\frac{d\Phi_0}{d\tau}=0.}
$$

The leading initial data are $\boxed{R_0(0)=4,\ \Phi_0(0)=0}$, up to adding an integer multiple of $2\pi$ to the [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md). Thus $x(t)=R_0(\epsilon t)\cos[3t+\Phi_0(\epsilon t)]+O(\epsilon)$. The [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) decreases from four towards the stable value one, consistent with the selected [limit cycle](../../../../../limit-cycle.md); the zero-[amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) state is unstable. Order-$\epsilon$ corrections enforce the exact initial [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) after differentiating the slow [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) term.
