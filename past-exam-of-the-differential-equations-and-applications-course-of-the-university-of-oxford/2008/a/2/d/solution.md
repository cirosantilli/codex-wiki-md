<h1 id="a/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Expand a forcing in the complete [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $\phi_n$. Since $L\phi_n=-\lambda_n\phi_n$, its inverse multiplies the $n$th coefficient by $-1/\lambda_n$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{g(x,t)=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{\phi_n(x)\phi_n(t)}{\lambda_n}=-2\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{\sin(k_nx)\sin(k_nt)}{k_n^2-1}.}
$$

The negative sign follows from the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) convention $Ly=-\lambda y$. This [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) satisfies the mixed endpoint conditions in its first argument and $L_xg=\delta(x-t)$.
