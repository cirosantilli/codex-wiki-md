<h1 id="a/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The equation is $y''+(1+\lambda)y=0$. If $1+\lambda\leq0$, the [linear](../../../../../linearity.md) or [hyperbolic](../../../../../hyperbolic-equilibrium-point.md) branches cannot meet both homogeneous endpoint conditions nontrivially. Otherwise $y=C\sin(kx)$, $k=\sqrt{1+\lambda}$, and $y'(1)=Ck\cos k=0$ gives $k_n=(n+1/2)\pi$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda_n=(n+\tfrac12)^2\pi^2-1,\qquad\phi_n(x)=\sqrt2\sin[(n+\tfrac12)\pi x],\quad n=0,1,\ldots.}
$$

The factor $\sqrt2$ makes the [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md) unit normalized. All the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are positive, in particular none is zero.
