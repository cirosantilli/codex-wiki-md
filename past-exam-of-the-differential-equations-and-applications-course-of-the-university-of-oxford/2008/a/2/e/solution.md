<h1 id="a/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [integral equation](../../../../../integral-equation.md) says precisely $h=L^{-1}z$ with the homogeneous mixed endpoint conditions. Since the supplied $h$ satisfies those conditions, apply $L$ directly:

$$
\boxed{z(x)=Lh=h''+h=x^2-2x+2.}
$$

No [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) is required. The absence of a zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) established in (b)–(c) also proves uniqueness of this recovered forcing.
