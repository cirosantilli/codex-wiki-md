<h1 id="a/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $Ly_j=-\lambda_jy_j$, then $\langle y_1,Ly_2\rangle=\langle Ly_1,y_2\rangle$ gives $(\lambda_1-\lambda_2)\langle y_1,y_2\rangle=0$. Thus [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) belonging to distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are **[orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md)**. [Normalization](../../../../../../normalization.md) can make them [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md). Equality of [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone would not force arbitrary [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) to be [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), although in this particular scalar separated problem each [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) is one-dimensional.
