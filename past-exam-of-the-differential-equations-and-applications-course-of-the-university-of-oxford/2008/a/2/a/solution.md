<h1 id="a/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $L=D^2+1$ on the mixed endpoint domain $y(0)=0$, $y'(1)=0$ in $L^2(0,1)$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives the boundary form

$$
\langle z,Ly\rangle-\langle Lz,y\rangle=[\overline z y'-\overline{z'}y]_0^1.
$$

At zero, $y'(0)$ is free, forcing $z(0)=0$; at one, $y(1)$ is free, forcing $z'(1)=0$. Therefore the [formal adjoint](../../../../../formal-adjoint.md) expression and full adjoint endpoint conditions coincide with the original ones:

$$
\boxed{L^*=D^2+1,\qquad z(0)=0,\quad z'(1)=0.}
$$

With the usual second-order operator domain this is a [self-adjoint](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) problem. The consequences for its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and [eigenfunctions](../../../../../eigenfunction.md) follow below.
