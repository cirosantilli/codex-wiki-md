↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $Ly=-\lambda y$, [self-adjointness](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) gives $-\lambda\|y\|^2=\langle y,Ly\rangle=\overline{\langle y,Ly\rangle}$. Since an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) is nonzero, $\boxed{\lambda\in\mathbb R}$. One may also see reality directly from $\lambda\int|y|^2=\int|y'|^2-\int|y|^2$, whose boundary term vanishes under the mixed conditions.
