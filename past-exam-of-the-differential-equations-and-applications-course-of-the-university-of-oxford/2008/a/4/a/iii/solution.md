<h1 id="a/4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The first fold occurs when $t^2\max_{\xi>0}\xi e^{-\xi^2/2}=1$. [Differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $(\xi e^{-\xi^2/2})'=e^{-\xi^2/2}(1-\xi^2)$, so the [maximum](../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) is $e^{-1/2}$ at $\xi=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{t_c=e^{1/4}.}
$$

At that point the first singular projection is at $x_c=1+t_c^2e^{-1/2}=2$, with density $e^{-1/2}$.
