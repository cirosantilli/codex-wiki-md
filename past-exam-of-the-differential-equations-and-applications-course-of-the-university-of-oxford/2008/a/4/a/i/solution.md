<h1 id="a/4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Integrate the [conservation law](../../../../../../conservation-law.md) over $[-R,R]$: $d\int_{-R}^R\rho\,dx/dt=-t[\rho^2]_{-R}^R$. The Gaussian data and their finite-time [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md)/entropy evolution have vanishing far-field flux. Taking $R\to\infty$ yields $\boxed{M'(t)=0}$ and hence $\boxed{M(t)=\sqrt{2\pi}}$. After a [shock](../../../../../../shock-wave.md) forms, its [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) preserves the same integral [mass](../../../../../../mass.md); the formal multivalued [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) surface itself must be replaced by the conservative [entropy solution](../../../../../../entropy-solution.md).
