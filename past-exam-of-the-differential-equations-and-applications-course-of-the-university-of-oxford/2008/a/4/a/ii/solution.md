<h1 id="a/4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding the flux gives $\rho_t+2t\rho\rho_x=0$. Along [characteristics](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md), $\dot\rho=0$ and $\dot x=2t\rho$. Labeling the initial point by $\xi$ gives

$$
\boxed{\rho=e^{-\xi^2/2},\qquad x=\xi+t^2e^{-\xi^2/2}.}
$$

Equivalently, [time reparametrization for a separable conservation-law flux](../../../../../../time-reparametrization-for-a-separable-conservation-law-flux.md) uses $T=t^2/2$. The projection [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $x_\xi=1-t^2\xi e^{-\xi^2/2}$. For large enough $t$ it becomes negative on an interval of positive $\xi$, although it tends to one at both far ends. The projection then folds and assigns several density values to one position. This establishes multivaluedness of the formal [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) continuation, not of the admissible [shock](../../../../../../shock-wave.md) solution.
