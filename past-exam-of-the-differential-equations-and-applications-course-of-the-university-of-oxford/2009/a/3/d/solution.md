<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Mass](../../../../../mass.md) preservation of a nonnegative finite-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) profile requires $\alpha+\beta=0$. Balancing $\rho_t$ and $\partial_x(\rho^4/4)$ requires $\alpha-1=4\alpha-\beta$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha=-\tfrac14,\qquad\beta=\tfrac14.}
$$

The similarity ODE is

$$
-\tfrac14(f+\eta f')+f^3f'=0,\qquad (f^4-\eta f)'=0.
$$

For a source-type profile adjoining vacuum, the integration constant is zero. The positive branch is $f=\eta^{1/3}$ for positive $\eta$, terminated by a [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) into zero. Normalize the [mass](../../../../../mass.md):

$$
1=\int_0^{\eta_F}\eta^{1/3}\,d\eta=\frac34\eta_F^{4/3},\qquad \eta_F=(4/3)^{3/4}.
$$

Thus a unit-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) [similarity solution](../../../../../similarity-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\rho(x,t)=t^{-1/4}\begin{cases}(x/t^{1/4})^{1/3},&0<x<\eta_Ft^{1/4},\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}}
$$

At the moving right edge, $S'=\eta_Ft^{-3/4}/4$ equals $\rho_L^3/4$, so the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) is satisfied. The [characteristic speeds](../../../../../characteristic-speed.md) satisfy $0<S'<\rho_L^3$, making it an entropy [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md). The left edge meets vacuum continuously. As $t\downarrow0$ the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) concentrates at the origin; this is a source solution, not a claim that every finite-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) initial profile equals it at finite time.
