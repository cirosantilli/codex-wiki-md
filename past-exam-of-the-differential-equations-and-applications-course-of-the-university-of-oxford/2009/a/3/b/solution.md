<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In smooth regions the characteristic equations are $\dot x=q'(\rho)=\rho^3$ and $\dot\rho=0$. If $\xi$ labels the initial point, their parametrization is

$$
x=\xi+\rho_0(\xi)^3t,\qquad\rho=\rho_0(\xi).
$$

For the two constant states, characteristics from the left have speed one and those from the right speed zero; they would overlap. The [entropy solution](../../../../../entropy-solution.md) replaces the overlap by a compressive [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md). Its speed is

$$
c=\frac{q(1)-q(0)}{1-0}=\frac14.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\rho(x,t)=\begin{cases}1,&x<t/4,\\0,&x>t/4,\end{cases}\qquad S(t)=t/4.}
$$

The left parametrization $x=\xi+t$ is retained only for $x<t/4$, and the right parametrization $x=\xi$ only for $x>t/4$. The [characteristic speeds](../../../../../characteristic-speed.md) obey $0<c<1$, so characteristics enter the [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) from both sides.
