<h1 id="a/2/b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $L$ are $\pi/\sqrt2$, $-\pi/\sqrt2$ and zero, none equal to one. On $E=\operatorname{span}\{1,\cos2x\}$ the map $L-I$ is an invertible two-dimensional operator; on $E^\perp$ it is $-I$. Hence it is invertible on all of $L^2(0,\pi)$. Thus $(L-I)y=\sin x$ has **exactly one solution**. This is the [finite-rank Fredholm alternative](../../../../../../finite-rank-fredholm-alternative.md); no range obstruction survives the nonresonant identity term.
