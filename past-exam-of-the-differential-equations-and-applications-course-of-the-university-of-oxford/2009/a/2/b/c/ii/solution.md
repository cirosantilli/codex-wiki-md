↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Here $\sin^2x=(1-\cos2x)/2$ belongs to the [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md). Matching the two coefficients using $L(A+B\cos2x)=B\pi/2+A\pi\cos2x$ gives $A=-1/(2\pi)$, $B=1/\pi$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{\cos2x-1/2}{\pi}+z(x),\qquad z\perp1,\quad z\perp\cos2x.}
$$

There are **infinitely many solutions**, since any element of the infinite-dimensional [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) may be added.
