<h1 id="a/2/b/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $L$ consists only of $a+b\cos2x$. If $\sin x$ were such a function, its endpoint values would give $a+b=0$ and its midpoint value $a-b=1$, forcing $a=1/2$, $b=-1/2$. That combination is $\sin^2x$, which differs from $\sin x$, for example at $x=\pi/6$. [Continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) makes the same argument valid for equality almost everywhere. Thus the forcing is outside the [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) and **no solution exists**.
