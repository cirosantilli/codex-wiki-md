↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Symmetry $k(x,t)=k(t,x)$ makes the adjoint [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) equal to the original [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), so $L^*=L$. For a nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) $Ly=\lambda y$,

$$
\lambda\|y\|^2=\langle y,Ly\rangle=\langle Ly,y\rangle=\overline\lambda\|y\|^2.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\lambda\in\mathbb R}$. The argument includes the zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and does not require normalizing the [eigenfunction](../../../../../../../eigenfunction.md).
