# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the differential equations and applications course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all six questions and nineteen numbered terminal parts of the Differential Equations and Applications paper sat on 14 June 2011. Source: [released Mathematics papers](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), `Part B 2007 11.pdf`. Question 3 distinguishes the conflicting printed Laguerre normalizations. Statements are not reproduced.
