↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) is a **[continuous linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) on the space of [test functions](../../../../../../test-function.md)** $\mathcal D(\mathbb R)=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$. A precise useful form of [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) is: for every compact set $K\subset\mathbb R$ there exist an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $m\geq0$ and a constant $C_K$ such that

$$
\boxed{|\langle T,\phi\rangle|\leq C_K\max_{0\leq j\leq m}\sup_{x\in K}|\phi^{(j)}(x)|\quad\text{when }\operatorname{supp}\phi\subset K.}
$$

Equivalently, if a sequence of [test functions](../../../../../../test-function.md) has supports in one fixed compact set and every [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) tends uniformly to zero, then its images under $T$ tend to zero. Keeping a common [compact support](../../../../../../compact-support.md) is part of the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) topology; mere pointwise convergence is insufficient.
