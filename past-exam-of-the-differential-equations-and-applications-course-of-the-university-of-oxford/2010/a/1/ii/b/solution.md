↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Linearity](../../../../../../linearity.md) follows from [linearity](../../../../../../linearity.md) of differentiation and point evaluation. For a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) supported in a fixed compact set $K$, its value and [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanish at any point outside $K$. Therefore

$$
|\phi'(0)+\phi(1)|\leq\sup_K|\phi'|+\sup_K|\phi|\leq2\max_{0\leq j\leq1}\sup_K|\phi^{(j)}|.
$$

This proves the [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) estimate with $m=1$ and $C_K=2$ for every $K$. Thus the stated mapping is a [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) of order at most one, supported in $\{0,1\}$. In particular, a sequence tending to zero in the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) topology has both $\phi_n'(0)\to0$ and $\phi_n(1)\to0$.
