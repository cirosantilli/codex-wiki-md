↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) is defined by moving the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) onto a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) and changing the sign:

$$
\boxed{\langle S',\phi\rangle=-\langle S,\phi'\rangle.}
$$

Since $\langle\delta_a,\phi\rangle=\phi(a)$, one has $\langle\delta_0',\phi\rangle=-\phi'(0)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T=-\delta_0'+\delta_1=-\delta'(x)+\delta(x-1).}
$$

The minus sign in front of $\delta_0'$ is essential: it makes its action the positive [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) evaluation required here.
