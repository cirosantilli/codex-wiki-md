<h1 id="a/1/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $r^2+2r+2=(r+1)^2+1$, with distinct complex roots $r=-1\pm i$. Taking the real and imaginary parts of the corresponding [exponential functions](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) gives the real [fundamental system of solutions](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-solutions.md) $e^{-x}\cos x$ and $e^{-x}\sin x$. Consequently the general real solution is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-x}(C\cos x+D\sin x),\qquad C,D\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Their [Wronskian](../../../../../../wronskian.md) is $e^{-2x}\ne0$, so they span the two-dimensional real solution space.
