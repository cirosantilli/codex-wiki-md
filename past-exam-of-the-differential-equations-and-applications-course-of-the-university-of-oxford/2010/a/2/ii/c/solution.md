<h1 id="a/2/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Rewrite the equation as $(L-I)y=\cos x$. On $E=\operatorname{span}\{\cos2x,\sin2x\}$, the operator $L-I$ has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\pi/2-1$ and $\pi/4-1$, both nonzero. On $E^\perp$ it equals $-I$. The [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) decomposition $L^2(0,\pi)=E\oplus E^\perp$ therefore makes $L-I$ bijective with [bounded inverse](../../../../../../bounded-inverse.md). In particular **there is exactly one solution** for this forcing. This is also the [finite-rank Fredholm alternative](../../../../../../finite-rank-fredholm-alternative.md): $1$ is not an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $L$, so adding the identity term removes the [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) obstruction present in $Ly=f$.
