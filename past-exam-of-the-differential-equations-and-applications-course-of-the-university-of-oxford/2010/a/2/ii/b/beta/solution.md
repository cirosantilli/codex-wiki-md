<h1 id="a/2/ii/b/beta/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Β](../beta.md)

Every image of $L$ is a [linear combination](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) $a\cos2x+b\sin2x$. The function $\cos x$ is not such a combination. Indeed these functions are continuous, so equality almost everywhere would imply equality everywhere; evaluation at the endpoints would require simultaneously $a=1$ and $a=-1$. Therefore the forcing is outside the [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) and **there is no solution**. [Orthogonality](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to the [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is the equivalent [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) test for this [self-adjoint](../../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) [finite-rank operator](../../../../../../../finite-rank-operator.md).
