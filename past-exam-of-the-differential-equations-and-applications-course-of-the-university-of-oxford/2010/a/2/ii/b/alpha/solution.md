<h1 id="a/2/ii/b/alpha/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

The forcing is $\tfrac12\sin2x$, which lies in the [image](../../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $L$. Since the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) on $\sin2x$ is $\pi/4$, a particular solution is $y_p=(2/\pi)\sin2x$. The full family is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac2\pi\sin2x+z(x),\qquad z\perp\cos2x,\quad z\perp\sin2x.}
$$

Thus **solutions exist and are not unique**: adding any element of the infinite-dimensional [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) leaves $Ly$ unchanged. For example every constant can be added.
