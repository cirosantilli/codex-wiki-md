<h1 id="a/1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The function $\phi-\phi'$ is continuous at zero, so its average over $[-1/n,1/n]$ tends to its value there. Consequently

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\langle T_n,\phi\rangle=\phi(0)-\phi'(0).
$$

The [Dirac delta](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) satisfies $\langle\delta,\phi\rangle=\phi(0)$, and its [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) satisfies $\langle\delta',\phi\rangle=-\phi'(0)$. Hence the [distributional convergence](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-distributions.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_n\longrightarrow\delta+\delta'.}
$$
