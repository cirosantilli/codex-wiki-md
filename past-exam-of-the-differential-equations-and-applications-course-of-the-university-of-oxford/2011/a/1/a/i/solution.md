<h1 id="a/1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) is a linear functional on the [space of test functions](../../../../../../space-of-test-functions.md) that is continuous in its test-function topology. Equivalently, for every compact $K\subset\mathbb R$ there are an integer $m$ and a constant $C_K$ such that $|\langle T,\phi\rangle|\leq C_K\max_{0\leq j\leq m}\|\phi^{(j)}\|_\infty$ whenever $\phi$ has support in $K$.

Linearity of $T_n$ follows from linearity of the [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) and [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md). Since the integration interval has length $2/n$,

$$
|\langle T_n,\phi\rangle|\leq\frac n2\int_{-1/n}^{1/n}(|\phi|+|\phi'|)\,dx
\leq\|\phi\|_\infty+\|\phi'\|_\infty.
$$

This is a test-function [seminorm](../../../../../../seminorm.md) estimate of order one, valid for every $K$ and even uniformly in $n$. It proves that **each $T_n$ is a distribution**.
