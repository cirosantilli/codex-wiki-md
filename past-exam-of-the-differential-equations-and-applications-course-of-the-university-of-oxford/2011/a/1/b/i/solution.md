↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) after multiplication by four is $r^2+4r+4+\pi^2$, with roots $-2\pm i\pi$. Thus the general real solution of this constant-coefficient [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-2x}[A\cos(\pi x)+B\sin(\pi x)],\qquad A,B\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Equivalently the [change of dependent variable](../../../../../../change-of-dependent-variable.md) $y=e^{-2x}v$ gives $Ly=\tfrac14e^{-2x}(v''+\pi^2v)$.
