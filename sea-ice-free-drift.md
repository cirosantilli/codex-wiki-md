# Sea-ice free drift

↑ **Parent:** [Ice floe](ice-floe.md)

[Sea-ice free drift](sea-ice-free-drift.md) neglects internal stress transmitted from neighboring ice. An isolated floe is governed by air and water [drag forces](drag-physics.md), the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) and sea-surface tilt; steady drift also neglects acceleration.
