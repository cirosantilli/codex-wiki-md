# Q factor

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance](resonance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_factor)

For a resonator, the quality factor is $2\pi$ times the stored [energy](energy.md) divided by the [energy](energy.md) dissipated per cycle, using a specified steady-state stored-energy convention. A [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) at [resonance](resonance.md) has $Q=L\omega_0/R$, because its maximum magnetic [energy](energy.md) is $LI_M^2/2$ and one cycle of [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) dissipates $\pi RI_M^2/\omega_0$.
