# Transplantation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md)

For two assemblies of congruent Euclidean tiles, let $M_s,N_s$ encode the gluing or boundary reflection at each labelled face. An invertible constant matrix $C$ satisfying $CM_s=N_sC$ carries tile restrictions of [Laplacian eigenfunctions](laplacian-eigenfunction.md) bijectively to those on the second assembly. Boundary values satisfy $M_su=u$ and outward [normal derivatives](normal-derivative.md) satisfy $M_sv=-v$, so the intertwining identities preserve matching and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Use diagonal $-1$ for a [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) and $+1$ for a [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md).
