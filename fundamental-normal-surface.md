# Fundamental normal surface

↑ **Parent:** [Normal surface coordinates](normal-surface-coordinates.md)

A fundamental [normal surface](normal-surface.md) has a nonzero admissible coordinate vector which is not the sum of two nonzero admissible vectors. Fixing the allowed quadrilateral type in every [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) reduces the problem to the [Hilbert basis of a rational cone](hilbert-basis-of-a-rational-cone.md). There are finitely many choices of quadrilateral types and finitely many indecomposable vectors for each choice.
