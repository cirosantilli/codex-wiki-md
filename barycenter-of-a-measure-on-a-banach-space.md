# Barycenter of a measure on a Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md)

For a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) whose identity map is strongly measurable with integrable norm, its barycenter is the [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md) of that map. It is characterized by $\varphi(b(\mu))=\int\varphi(x)\,d\mu(x)$ for every [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md). For a measure on a [weakly compact set](weakly-compact-set.md) in a [separable Banach space](separable-banach-space.md), its barycenter belongs to the norm-closed [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of that set.
