# Viscous stirring of planetesimals

↑ **Parent:** [Oligarchic growth](oligarchic-growth.md)

Growth of planetesimal random orbital motions through gravitational encounters. The word viscous does not denote molecular fluid viscosity here. Stirring competes with gas [drag](drag-physics.md), collisions and [dynamical friction](dynamical-friction.md) in setting the encounter speeds that control [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md).
