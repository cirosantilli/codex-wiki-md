# Steady friction-dominated grounding-line thickness relation

↑ **Parent:** [Friction-dominated grounding-line evolution law](friction-dominated-grounding-line-evolution-law.md)

With constant [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) $Hu=q_0>0$, define $\beta=l/\lambda$, $\mathcal H=H/\beta$, and $\mathcal Q=\nu q_0/(g\beta^3)$. The friction closure gives $H_x=\alpha-q_0\nu/(\beta gH^2)$. Differentiating constant flux and applying the [unbuttressed Newtonian grounding-line stress](unbuttressed-newtonian-grounding-line-stress.md) condition gives $q_0H_x/H+g'H^2/(8\nu)=0$. Substitution proves the displayed relation. For $\alpha\ge0$, $g'>0$ and $\mathcal Q>0$, the polynomial $(g'/8g)\mathcal H^5+\alpha\mathcal Q\mathcal H^2-\mathcal Q^2$ increases strictly on the positive half-line, begins negative and tends to infinity; hence it has exactly one positive root. Its root satisfies $\mathcal Q>\alpha\mathcal H^2$, so the thickness decreases seaward at that steady grounding line.
