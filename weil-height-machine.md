# Weil height machine

↑ **Parent:** [Height function](height-function.md)

On a projective variety over a [number field](number-field.md), a very ample [line bundle](line-bundle.md) gives a logarithmic [projective height](projective-height.md) by an embedding. Tensor products correspond to sums by the Segre embedding, and a sufficiently ample twist expresses any line bundle as a difference of very ample ones. This defines its height modulo bounded functions. It is additive and functorial under morphism pullback. An ample bundle has the [Northcott theorem](northcott-theorem.md) finiteness property for bounded height and bounded point degree; a trivial bundle need not. The [product formula](product-formula.md) makes these absolute heights independent of scalar coordinates and of the number field used to compute them.
