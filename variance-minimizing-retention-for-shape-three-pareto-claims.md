# Variance-minimizing retention for shape-three Pareto claims

↑ **Parent:** [Total variance stationary condition for excess of loss](total-variance-stationary-condition-for-excess-of-loss.md)

For a [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) of shape three and minimum $d>0$ with count rate $\lambda>0$, the sum of insurer and reinsurer aggregate [variances](variance-split.md) is $V(M)=\lambda(3d^2-3dM+2M^2)$ for $0\le M\le d$, and $\lambda(3d^2-d^3/M)$ for $M\ge d$. The first branch has its unique minimum at $3d/4$; the second is increasing. The branches agree in value and slope at $d$, giving the displayed global minimum. This criterion minimizes the sum of party [variances](variance-split.md); the total portfolio [variance](variance-split.md) includes their [covariance](covariance.md) and remains $3\lambda d^2$.
