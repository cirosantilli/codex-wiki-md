# Three-branch-point regular surface cover

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)

For a finite group generated by $A,B$, glue sheets labeled by the group over the three-punctured [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) using left monodromy multiplication. Fill the punctures by the local power charts dictated by the orders $m,n,r$ of $A,B,AB$. Right multiplication by inverses defines the commuting deck action. Its nontrivial stabilizers are conjugates of the corresponding cyclic [subgroups](subgroup.md). The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives the displayed [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). A compatible metric can be averaged over the group; in genus at least two the invariant hyperbolic metric is canonical by the [uniformization theorem](uniformization-theorem.md).
