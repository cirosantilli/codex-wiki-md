# Rank bounding for sets in the cumulative hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md)

If every member of a [set](set-split.md) $a$ belongs to some cumulative-hierarchy stage, [replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) collects the least such stages. Their [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) [supremum](supremum.md) gives a common stage $V_\beta$ containing every element of $a$, so $a\in V_{\beta+1}$. The argument also works without [foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) and bounds a [set](set-split.md) of well-founded elements inside the hierarchy.
