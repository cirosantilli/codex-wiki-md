# 2-core of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

The 2-core is the unique largest induced [subgraph](subgraph.md) with [minimum degree](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) at least two. It is obtained by repeatedly deleting vertices of degree zero or one. Every subgraph with [minimum degree](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) two survives each deletion, which proves maximality and order independence. In a connected graph with a nonempty 2-core, the deleted vertices form rooted trees attached individually to the core vertices.
