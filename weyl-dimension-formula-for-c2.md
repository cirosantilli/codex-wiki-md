# Weyl dimension formula for C2

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md)

For the [C2 root system](c2-root-system.md) with its first [simple root](simple-root.md) short, the irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $a\omega_1+b\omega_2$, where $a,b\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$, has dimension

$$
\frac{(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)(a+2b+3)}6.
$$
