# Affine-time scaling of a nonradial Schwarzschild photon plunge

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-azimuth Schwarzschild photon plunge](finite-azimuth-schwarzschild-photon-plunge.md)

For nonzero angular constant $h$, the interior null radial equation gives $\dot r^2\sim2\mu h^2/r^3$. Its inward branch integrates to $r^{5/2}\sim\tfrac52\sqrt{2\mu}|h|(\lambda_*-\lambda)$. Thus the singularity is at finite [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) and $r\propto(\lambda_*-\lambda)^{2/5}$. The zero-angular-momentum case has a different affine scaling.
