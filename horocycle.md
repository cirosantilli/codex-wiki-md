# Horocycle

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horocycle)

A horocycle in the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) is a Euclidean [circle](circle.md) internally tangent to the boundary [circle](circle.md). In the [upper half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md), a horocycle is a Euclidean [circle](circle.md) tangent to the real axis from above, or a horizontal line $y=h>0$. The common geometric feature is a single [ideal centre of a horocycle](ideal-centre-of-a-horocycle.md).
