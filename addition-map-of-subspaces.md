# Addition map of subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of vector subspaces](sum-of-vector-subspaces.md)

For [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) $U,W$ of a common [vector space](vector-space-split.md), this is a [surjective linear map](surjective-linear-map.md). Its [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\{(x,-x):x\in U\cap W\}$, which has a [linear isomorphism](linear-isomorphism.md) to $U\cap W$ by $x\mapsto(x,-x)$. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) and the [direct sum basis](direct-sum-basis.md) give the [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md). The paired domain keeps the two contributions separate even when their underlying [vectors](vector.md) overlap.
