# Average pure-source fidelity after typical projection

↑ **Parent:** [Typical-subspace compression with a failure flag](typical-subspace-compression-with-a-failure-flag.md)

For a [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md) of pure vectors and an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P$, measure $\{P,I-P\}$, preserve the successful subspace, and decode failure to a fixed normalized vector $\varphi$. The resulting [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is $\Lambda(X)=PXP+\operatorname{Tr}[(I-P)X]|\varphi\rangle\langle\varphi|$. For $\alpha_k=\|P\psi_k\|$, its [squared quantum fidelity](squared-quantum-fidelity.md) on the $k$th source vector is $\alpha_k^4+(1-\alpha_k^2)|\langle\psi_k|\varphi\rangle|^2$. The inequality $a^2\geq2a-1$ on $[0,1]$ proves the displayed average bound. It depends only on the source density operator's weight in $P$ and does not require the emitted vectors to be orthogonal.
