# Variance of the Watterson estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Watterson estimator](watterson-estimator.md)

The [moments of the mutation count on a neutral coalescent tree](moments-of-the-mutation-count-on-a-neutral-coalescent-tree.md) give this [variance](variance-split.md) for $\widehat\theta_W=S/H_{n-1}$. Since $H_{n-1}\sim\log n$ and the sum of inverse squares is bounded, it tends to zero. Together with unbiasedness, this gives [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md) to the mutation parameter, so it is a [consistent estimator](consistency-statistics.md).
