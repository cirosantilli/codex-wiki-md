# Multiplicative group scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic group](algebraic-group.md)

The multiplicative group [scheme](scheme.md) represents [units](unit-in-a-ring.md): its $R$-points are $R^\times$. Multiplication is specified by $t\mapsto t\otimes t$ on the coordinate [ring](ring.md). It is the [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(t)$ of the [affine line](affine-line.md) and the one-dimensional split [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md).
