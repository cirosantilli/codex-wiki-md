# Joint of a line collection

↑ **Parent:** [Incidence geometry](incidence-geometry.md)

For distinct real affine lines in $\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, a [joint of a line collection](joint-of-a-line-collection.md) is a point lying on $n$ lines with [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) direction vectors. In three dimensions the directions must not all lie in one plane. Extra incident lines are allowed; existence of one spanning choice is enough. This counts [joint of a line collection](joint-of-a-line-collection.md) points, rather than tuples of lines or incidence multiplicities.
