# Oriented manifold retract for the Lefschetz theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md)

A closed smooth manifold X embeds in Euclidean space. The double M of a closed tubular neighbourhood is a closed oriented [manifold](topological-manifold.md) retracting onto X. If i is its inclusion and r its retraction, the fixed points of F correspond to those of f, and cyclicity of finite-dimensional trace gives $L(F)=L(f)$ from $i^*r^*=1$. Thus the oriented diagonal-class proof of the [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md) implies the theorem for possibly nonorientable X, without incorrectly treating its diagonal as cooriented.
