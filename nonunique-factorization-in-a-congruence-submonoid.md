# Nonunique factorization in a congruence submonoid

↑ **Parent:** [Congruence submonoid of the positive integers](congruence-submonoid-of-the-positive-integers.md)

The [congruence submonoid of the positive integers](congruence-submonoid-of-the-positive-integers.md) with modulus ten has four distinct [irreducible elements of a commutative monoid](irreducible-element-of-a-commutative-monoid.md) $21,221,51,91$ with equal pairwise products. Their ordinary prime factors are respectively $(3,7),(13,17),(3,17),(7,13)$. None of these proper prime factors is congruent to one modulo ten. The [Euclid lemma](euclid-lemma.md) proves that a product of two distinct primes has no other proper positive divisors, so all four are irreducible in this monoid. This gives nonunique factorization without assuming the fundamental theorem of arithmetic.
