# Tensor product of central simple algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md)

The [tensor product](tensor-product.md) over $k$ of two central simple $k$-algebras is central simple. After extending scalars to an algebraic closure, both factors and their tensor product become full matrix algebras; faithful flatness then descends simplicity and the center.
