# Algol mass-ratio stability limit

↑ **Parent:** [Algol binary](algol-binary.md)

A simplified conservative model with a fully convective donor gives long-lived stable [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) only for donor-to-accretor mass ratio $q<2/3$. This follows from the [Roche-lobe radius response exponent](roche-lobe-radius-response-exponent.md) and the donor's adiabatic [stellar radius response exponent](stellar-radius-response-exponent.md) $-1/3$. The boundary is model-dependent; radiative donors, evolved core structure and mass or angular-momentum loss change it. [Van Rensbergen and collaborators' Algol population study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2620) includes reported Algol mass ratios above $0.7$ and discusses observational uncertainties.
