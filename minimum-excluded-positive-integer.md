# Minimum excluded positive integer

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum excludant](minimum-excludant.md)

For a set of positive integers, its minimum excluded positive integer is the least positive integer absent from the set. This is the positive-indexed version of the [minimum excludant](minimum-excludant.md), which normally allows zero.
