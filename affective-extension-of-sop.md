# Affective extension of SOP

↑ **Parent:** [SOP model](sop-model.md)

A development of the model by [Allan R. Wagner](allan-r-wagner.md) and [Susan E. Brandon](susan-e-brandon.md) distinguishing sensory-specific and emotive components of outcome representations, with potentially different activation time courses and response consequences. It allows one cue to influence multiple response systems differently without requiring contradictory values in one undifferentiated representation.
