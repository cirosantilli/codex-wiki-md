# Gibbs energy of activation

↑ **Parent:** [Transition state theory](transition-state-theory.md)

The [Gibbs energy of activation](gibbs-energy-of-activation.md) is the standard molar [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) change associated with going from the starting [reactants](reactant.md) to the [transition state](transition-state.md) in [transition state theory](transition-state-theory.md). It specifies the directional barrier, rather than the overall endpoint change in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md). Use consistent [standard states](standard-state.md) when comparing [Gibbs energies of activation](gibbs-energy-of-activation.md) and [rate constants](rate-constant.md). See the [IUPAC Gold Book: Gibbs energy of activation](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/G02631).
