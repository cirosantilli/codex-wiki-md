↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

The [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) TCF-family [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md). Its action depends on nuclear abundance and availability of [SYS-1](sys-1.md), so it can repress or activate [endoderm](endoderm.md) targets in different daughter [cells](biological-cell-split.md).
