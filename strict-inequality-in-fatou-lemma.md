# Strict inequality in Fatou lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md)

On $([0,1],\lambda)$, the functions

$$
f_n=n\mathbf1_{(0,1/n)}
$$

converge pointwise to zero but satisfy $\int f_n\,d\lambda=1$. Hence the two sides of [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) can be zero and one.
