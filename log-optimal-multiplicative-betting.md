# Log-optimal multiplicative betting

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs inequality](gibbs-inequality.md)

For independent outcomes with positive [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) $p$, allocations $a_j>0$ summing to one and return multipliers $a_j/u_j$, the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) gives asymptotic logarithmic fortune growth $g(a)=\sum_jp_j\log(a_j/u_j)$. [Gibbs inequality](gibbs-inequality.md) gives $g(a)=g(p)-D(p\Vert a)$, so the unique optimum is $a=p$. Writing $U=\sum_ju_j$ gives $g(p)=D(p\Vert u/U)-\log U$. Thus $U<1$ guarantees positive optimal growth, whereas $U>1$ allows the house to force negative growth by choosing $u_j=Up_j$, but does not guarantee decline for every choice of $u$.
