# Reflexive transitive closure

↑ **Parent:** [Transitive closure](transitive-closure.md)

The least [reflexive relation](reflexive-relation.md) and [transitive relation](transitive-relation.md) containing $R$. Here $R^0$ is the identity relation. Membership means that a finite sequence of zero or more $R$-steps connects the ordered pair. Adding paths can create reverse connections that were absent in $R$, so initial [antisymmetry](antisymmetry.md) need not be preserved.
