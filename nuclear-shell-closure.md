# Nuclear shell closure

↑ **Parent:** [Magic number](magic-number.md)

Filling the accessible one-species [nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md) states below a substantial [energy](energy-split.md) gap. A closed spherical $j$ level has all $m_j$ values occupied, so their sum is zero. In the independent-particle picture, a closed core therefore contributes zero total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md); a single particle outside it, or a single missing particle inside it, supplies the leading [spin](spin.md) assignment. Residual interactions can change this simple picture.

The [three-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](three-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md) alone has one-species shell capacities $(q+1)(q+2)$ for oscillator index $q=0,1,\ldots$, including [spin](spin.md). Summing gives closures $2,8,20,40,70,112,\ldots$, rather than the complete traditional [magic number](magic-number.md) sequence. Strong [nuclear spin-orbit splitting](nuclear-spin-orbit-splitting.md) can separate a high-$j$ level from its former neighbors. Realistic radial [potential energies](potential-energy.md) and residual interactions also matter: a chosen oscillator plus an arbitrary single constant is not a demonstrated explanation of every real nuclear gap.

Traditional [nuclear shell closures](nuclear-shell-closure.md) can weaken along [isotope](isotope.md) chains far from stability, as in neutron-rich [magnesium](magnesium.md).[20 November 2013, magnesium shell weakening](magic-numbers-disappear-and-expand-area-of-nuclear-deformation-by-riken.md)

The relevant test is the actual [energy](energy-split.md) gap and the correlated nuclear [quantum states](quantum-state.md), rather than membership in an immutable numerical list.
