# Continuity equation for a superbubble similarity solution

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic superbubble similarity solution](adiabatic-superbubble-similarity-solution.md)

Substitution of the [adiabatic superbubble similarity solution](adiabatic-superbubble-similarity-solution.md) into spherical [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives

$$
-\eta f'+\eta^{-2}(\eta^2fh)'=0,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad(h-\eta)f'+fh'+2fh/\eta=0.
$$
