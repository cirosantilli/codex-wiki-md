# Continuous unitary character

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A continuous unitary character is a continuous [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) to the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) with multiplication. For Abelian groups, multiplication of characters is pointwise. Such characters must be distinguished from the traces of higher-dimensional group representations and from [characters of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md).
