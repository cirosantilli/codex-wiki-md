# Continuous unitary character

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A [continuous unitary character](continuous-unitary-character.md) is a continuous [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) to the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) with multiplication. For [abelian groups](abelian-group.md), multiplication of [continuous unitary characters](continuous-unitary-character.md) is pointwise. Such [continuous unitary characters](continuous-unitary-character.md) must be distinguished from the [traces](matrix-trace.md) of higher-dimensional [group representations](group-representation.md), called [characters of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md), and from [characters of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md).

For every [integer](integer.md) $n$, the map $\chi_n(z)=z^n$ is a [continuous unitary character](continuous-unitary-character.md) of the [circle group](circle-group.md): $\chi_n(zw)=\chi_n(z)\chi_n(w)$ and $|\chi_n(z)|=1$. By contrast, the two-dimensional [group representation](group-representation.md) $\rho(z)=\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1})$ has [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) $\operatorname{tr}\rho(e^{i\theta})=2\cos\theta$. Its value at the [identity element](identity-element.md) is $2$, so this [trace](matrix-trace.md) is not a [continuous unitary character](continuous-unitary-character.md).
