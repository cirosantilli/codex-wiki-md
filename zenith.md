↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal coordinate system](horizontal-coordinate-system.md)

The [zenith](zenith.md) is the direction of the local upward vertical on the [celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md). [Azimuth](azimuth.md) is undefined there.
