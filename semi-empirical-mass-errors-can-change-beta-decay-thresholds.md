# Semi-empirical mass errors can change beta-decay thresholds

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md)

[Beta decay](beta-decay.md) [Q values](q-value.md) depend on small differences between much larger atomic [masses](mass.md). A smooth [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) can therefore approximate absolute masses reasonably while predicting the wrong sign for a MeV-scale decay threshold. [Nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) explains offset even-even and odd-odd mass branches, but does not guarantee that every specific even-even neighbor lies below the intervening odd-odd nucleus. Measured masses or additional [nuclear shell](nuclear-shell.md) corrections are needed for quantitative threshold tests near a model's residual error scale.
