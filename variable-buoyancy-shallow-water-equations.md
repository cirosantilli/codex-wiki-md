# Variable-buoyancy shallow water equations

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

A vertically mixed [gravity current](gravity-current.md) with horizontally varying [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $b$ obeys $h_t+(hu)_x=0$ and $(hu)_t+(hu^2+bh^2/2)_x=0$. Consequently the [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md) is $Du/Dt=-b h_x-hb_x/2$. Advected density variables add repeated contact [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) of speed $u$ to the gravity-wave [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) of speeds $u\pm\sqrt{bh}$.
