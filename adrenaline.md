# Adrenaline

↑ **Parent:** [Hormone](hormone.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrenaline)

[Adrenaline](adrenaline.md), also called [epinephrine](adrenaline.md), is an endogenous [catecholamine](catecholamine.md) messenger: the body makes it and can release it into blood as a [hormone](hormone.md). The [adrenal medulla](adrenal-medulla.md), the inner region of an [adrenal gland](adrenal-gland.md), secretes [adrenaline](adrenaline.md) in response to [sympathetic nervous system](sympathetic-nervous-system.md) stimulation. A circulating [hormone](hormone.md) broadcasts a signal, but each receiving [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) interprets it through its own [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md) and intracellular machinery. [Adrenaline](adrenaline.md) activates both [alpha-adrenergic receptors](alpha-adrenergic-receptor.md) and [beta-adrenergic receptors](beta-adrenergic-receptor.md); it does not command one identical response everywhere.[Hormones of the Adrenal Medulla: secretion in response to sympathetic stimulation](adrenal-gland-by-national-cancer-institute.md)[Epinephrine, D004837: Scope Note, entry term Adrenaline and Catecholamines tree placement](epinephrine-mesh-descriptor-by-national-library-of-medicine.md)

Fuel mobilization is a useful example. A [beta-adrenergic receptor](beta-adrenergic-receptor.md) can increase [adenylyl cyclase](adenylyl-cyclase.md) activity through a stimulatory [G protein](g-protein.md), increasing the intracellular [second messenger](second-messenger.md) [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md). [Protein kinase A](protein-kinase-a.md) responds to [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md); a [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) relay through [phosphorylase kinase](phosphorylase-kinase.md) activates [glycogen phosphorylase](glycogen-phosphorylase.md), favoring [glycogenolysis](glycogenolysis.md). Here [adrenaline](adrenaline.md) is the extracellular message, [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) is the intracellular message, and [glycogen](glycogen.md) is the reserve being mobilized. These are three different roles.[Abstract, properties 2-4: purified guanine-nucleotide regulatory component reconstitutes hormone-stimulated adenylyl cyclase](the-regulatory-component-of-adenylate-cyclase-purification-and-properties-by-p-c-sternweis-and-others.md)[Walsh, Perkins and Krebs, 1968, printed p. 3763 Summary and introductory paragraphs: cAMP-dependent kinase and phosphorylase kinase activation](an-adenosine-3%E2%80%B2-5%E2%80%B2-monophosphate-dependant-protein-kinase-from-rabbit-skeletal-muscle-by-d-a-walsh-and-others.md)[Abstract, second paragraph: positive beta-receptor coupling to adenylyl cyclase](pharmacological-characterizations-of-adrenergic-receptors-in-human-adipocytes-by-thomas-w-burns-and-others.md)

Solid arrows show a selected activating relay to [glycogenolysis](glycogenolysis.md); the dashed branch summarizes promotion of [lipolysis](lipolysis.md) in responsive [adipocytes](adipocyte.md), omitting its intermediate steps. Arrows denote causal influences, not transport of [adrenaline](adrenaline.md) into the [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md), reaction rates or measured yields.

The two reserves provide different material. [Glycogenolysis](glycogenolysis.md) draws on [glycogen](glycogen.md); [lipolysis](lipolysis.md) breaks down stored [triglycerides](triglyceride.md), releasing [fatty acids](fatty-acid.md) and [glycerol](glycerol.md). A [skeletal muscle](skeletal-muscle.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)'s own reserve and fuel released by [adipose tissue](adipose-tissue.md) are different supplies. Increasing a signal cannot mobilize an empty reserve: receptor activation changes the use of available material rather than creating fuel. The [adrenergic adipocyte assay](adrenergic-adipocyte-assay.md) below gives direct human evidence for the [lipolysis](lipolysis.md) branch.[Abstract: epinephrine-stimulated glycerol release as an index of lipolysis](pharmacological-characterizations-of-adrenergic-receptors-in-human-adipocytes-by-thomas-w-burns-and-others.md)

Receptor context can even oppose this branch. In the human [adipocyte](adipocyte.md) preparation below, [beta-adrenergic receptors](beta-adrenergic-receptor.md) promoted [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) accumulation, whereas [alpha-2 adrenergic receptors](alpha-2-adrenergic-receptor.md) opposed it. [Alpha-1 adrenergic receptors](alpha-1-adrenergic-receptor.md) were associated with a different [phospholipid](phospholipid.md) readout. The balance of these responses matters more than the name of the messenger alone.[Abstract: opposing beta and alpha-2 coupling; separate alpha-1 phospholipid response](pharmacological-characterizations-of-adrenergic-receptors-in-human-adipocytes-by-thomas-w-burns-and-others.md)

This separation is useful when explaining [signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md): ask which [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) is present, which relay it controls, which reserve or target is available, and which response was actually measured. [Receptor binding occupancy](receptor-binding-occupancy.md), [second messenger](second-messenger.md) production and final fuel release are distinct observations. A stronger [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) signal need not imply an equally large change in every downstream process; substrate availability and the rest of the receiving [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) still matter.

The extract-transfer experiment under [second messenger](second-messenger.md) supplies the earlier experimental separation between [hormone](hormone.md) reception and an intracellular intermediary. Its existing account includes the preparations and controls. The complementary [adrenergic adipocyte assay](adrenergic-adipocyte-assay.md) asks a different question: can the same [adrenaline](adrenaline.md) stimulus engage opposing [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md) within a human preparation?
