# Thermal radiation shield

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md)

A [thermal radiation shield](thermal-radiation-shield.md) interposes a surface to reduce radiative heat exchange between an apparatus and its surroundings. [temperature](temperature-split.md), [emissivity](emissivity.md) and view factors determine its effectiveness; conductive supports and gas transport remain separate heat paths. A [thermal radiation shield](thermal-radiation-shield.md) is not automatically an ionizing-radiation shield.
