# Magnetohydrodynamic-viscoelastic analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

For component-first [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) $L_{ij}=\partial_j u_i$, ideal incompressible [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) stretching gives the displayed evolution of $M_{ij}=B_iB_j$. It has the same [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md) as a [polymer](polymer-split.md) [conformation tensor](conformation-tensor.md) with negligible relaxation. Finite [polymer relaxation time](polymer-relaxation-time.md) introduces a local return toward thermal equilibrium, whereas [magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md) acts through spatial gradients and preserves the [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md). The analogy does not identify those dissipative mechanisms.
