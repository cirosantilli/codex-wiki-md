# Primitive Pythagorean triple

↑ **Parent:** [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md)

A [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md) $(a,b,c)$ whose coordinates have [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) 1. Its two legs have opposite [integer parity](parity-mathematics.md), and every [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md) is a unique [positive integer](positive-integer.md) multiple of a [primitive Pythagorean triple](primitive-pythagorean-triple.md) up to the ordering of its legs.
