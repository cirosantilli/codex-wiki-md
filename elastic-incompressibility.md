# Elastic incompressibility

↑ **Parent:** [Finite strain theory](finite-strain-theory.md)

Volume preservation by the elastic accommodation in [morphoelasticity](morphoelasticity.md). If $\mathbf F=\mathbf A\mathbf G$, [elastic incompressibility](elastic-incompressibility.md) imposes $\det\mathbf A=1$, and hence $\det\mathbf F=\det\mathbf G$. Total volume can increase through growth while the elastic response remains incompressible. A [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) acts as a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) enforcing this constraint in the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md).
