# Diffuse thermal emitter

↑ **Parent:** [Emissivity](emissivity.md)

A [diffuse thermal emitter](diffuse-thermal-emitter.md) has emitted [radiance](radiance.md) independent of outgoing direction over the outward [hemisphere](hemisphere.md). Its emitted [radiant intensity](radiant-intensity.md) from a planar patch still varies with viewing [angle](angle.md) through [projected area](projected-area.md), as described by [Lambert's cosine law](lambert-s-cosine-law.md). Diffuse emission does not mean equal [power](power.md) per [solid angle](solid-angle.md) from the patch. This approximation does not by itself impose a [grey body](grey-body.md) spectrum or specify how incident radiation is reflected.
