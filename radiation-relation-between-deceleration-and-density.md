# Radiation relation between deceleration and density

↑ **Parent:** [Deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md)

For [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), $p=\rho/3$. The [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives $\ddot a/a=-8\pi G\rho/3$, so the [deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md) equals the [cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md), regardless of spatial curvature. This is a relation for a pure radiation fluid, not a general identity for a mixture.
