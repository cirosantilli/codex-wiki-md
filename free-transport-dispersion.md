# Free transport dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Free transport equation](free-transport-equation.md)

For the [free transport equation](free-transport-equation.md), substitute $y=x-tv$ into $\int|f_0(x-tv,v)|\,dv$. The resulting factor $|t|^{-d}$ and the bound by $\sup_v|f_0(y,v)|$ prove this [mixed Lebesgue norm](mixed-lebesgue-norm.md) estimate. The right side contains initial data. The same-time mixed norm is not a conserved replacement: prescribing a product $\psi(x)\chi(v)$ at a later time and dilating $\chi$ disproves such a claim.
