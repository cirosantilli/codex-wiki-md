# Statistical reflection symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical isotropy](statistical-isotropy.md)

Statistical reflection symmetry means that the joint distributions of a [random field](random-field.md) are unchanged by a simultaneous spatial reflection, with each field component transformed according to its vector or pseudovector character. For an isotropic [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), it excludes a parity-odd [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) contribution to the two-point [spectral tensor](spectral-tensor.md).
