# Rational homotopy type

↑ **Parent:** [Rational homotopy equivalence](rational-homotopy-equivalence.md)

The [rational homotopy type](rational-homotopy-type.md) of a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) space is its equivalence class under zigzags of [rational homotopy equivalences](rational-homotopy-equivalence.md), or equivalently the [homotopy](homotopy.md) type of its [rationalization of a topological space](rationalization-of-a-topological-space.md). For the usual finite-type spaces, it is encoded contravariantly by the isomorphism class of the [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md). Isomorphic [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) rings determine this type only when additional information, such as [formality of a topological space](formal-space.md), makes the cochain algebra equivalent to the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) algebra.
