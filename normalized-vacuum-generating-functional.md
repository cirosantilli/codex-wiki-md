# Normalized vacuum generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Generating functional](generating-functional.md)

For a vacuum [generating functional](generating-functional.md) $Z[J]$, normalization gives $\widehat Z[J]=Z[J]/Z[0]$ and $\widehat Z[0]=1$. [Functional derivatives](functional-derivative.md) of $\widehat Z$ give normalized [vacuum expectation values](vacuum-expectation-value.md). The [linked-cluster theorem](linked-cluster-theorem.md) shows that this ratio cancels [Vacuum Feynman diagrams](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md), while $-i\log\widehat Z[J]$ gives connected correlators. Components attached to external insertions are not removed merely by the normalization.
