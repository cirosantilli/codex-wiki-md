# Bounded exceptional primes for least quadratic nonresidues

↑ **Parent:** [Least quadratic nonresidue](least-quadratic-nonresidue.md)

If, for every $\eta>0$, the [smooth numbers](smooth-number.md) up to $T$ with all [prime factors](prime-factor.md) at most $T^\eta$ have density at least $\kappa(\eta)>0$, then for fixed $\varepsilon>0$ there are only $O_\varepsilon(1)$ [primes](prime-number.md) $p\leq N$ with $n(p)>N^\varepsilon$. Apply the [variance form of the large sieve](variance-form-of-the-large-sieve.md) to the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of the $N^\varepsilon$-smooth numbers up to $N^2$. For every exceptional [prime](prime-number.md), all these numbers are nonzero [quadratic residues](quadratic-residue.md), so half its nonzero [residue classes](residue-class.md) are empty. Their variance contribution is at least one third of the square of the total count. The large sieve bounds the sum of contributions by a constant times $N^2$ times that count. The density assumption with $\eta=\varepsilon/2$ finishes the argument.
