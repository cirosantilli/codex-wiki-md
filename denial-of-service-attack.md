# Denial-of-service attack

↑ **Parent:** [Computer security](computer-security.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denial-of-service_attack)

An attack on [service availability](service-availability.md) by exhausting resources or preventing useful work. Requiring inexpensive unauthenticated messages to trigger expensive private-key operations creates an amplification opportunity.
