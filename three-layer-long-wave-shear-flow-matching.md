# Three-layer long-wave shear-flow matching

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

A high-[Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) long-wave mode near a wall has a viscous layer of thickness $\delta=(\alpha R)^{-1/3}$, an order-one region following the base profile, and a pressure-decay region of thickness $1/\alpha$. The lower [Airy function](airy-function.md) solution fixes velocity displacement and [fluid pressure](fluid-pressure.md). The middle [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md) transports these to the outer constant-velocity region. Matching its decaying pressure produces an [Airy wall-layer matching determinant](airy-wall-layer-matching-determinant.md). For order-one amplitudes, constant leading middle pressure requires $\alpha^2\ll\delta$; the distinguished balance $\alpha\sim\delta$ gives $\alpha\sim R^{-1/4}$.
