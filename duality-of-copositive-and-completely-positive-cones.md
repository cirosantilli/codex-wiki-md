# Duality of copositive and completely positive cones

↑ **Parent:** [Copositive cone](copositive-cone.md)

Under the nonnegative-pairing convention for the [dual cone](dual-cone.md), $\operatorname{CP}_n^*=\operatorname{COP}_n$ follows from $\langle A,xx^T\rangle_F=x^TAx$. Conversely, if $B\notin\operatorname{CP}_n$, [separation from a closed convex cone](separation-from-a-closed-convex-cone.md) gives a separating $A$ nonnegative on all generators and negative on $B$. That $A$ is a [copositive matrix](copositive-matrix.md), so $B\notin\operatorname{COP}_n^*$. This proves the displayed equality using [closedness of the completely positive cone](closedness-of-the-completely-positive-cone.md).
