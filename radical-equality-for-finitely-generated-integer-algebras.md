# Radical equality for finitely generated integer algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobson ring](jacobson-ring.md)

For a [finite-type integer algebra](finite-type-integer-algebra.md), a nonnilpotent element $r$ gives a nonzero [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) $R[1/r]$. A [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) there has finite [residue field](residue-field.md) by the [finite-field theorem for finitely generated integer algebras](finite-field-theorem-for-finitely-generated-integer-algebras.md). The image of $R$ in that [field](field.md) is a finite [integral domain](integral-domain.md), hence a [field](field.md). Its kernel is therefore maximal in $R$ and avoids $r$. Nilpotents lie in every [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md), proving the radical equality. Applying the same argument to every prime quotient shows that these algebras are [Jacobson rings](jacobson-ring.md). Merely contracting a localized [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) without the finite-image argument would not prove maximality.
