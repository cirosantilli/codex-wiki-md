# Spherical volume element

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical coordinate system](spherical-coordinate-system.md)

With polar [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ measured from positive $z$ and azimuth $\phi$, the Cartesian formulas $x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi$, $y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi$, $z=r\cos\theta$ have [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $r^2\sin\theta$. For $r\geq0$ and $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$ this gives the unsigned [volume element](volume-element.md) shown. The inverse determinant is $1/(r^2\sin\theta)$ off the origin and polar axis.
