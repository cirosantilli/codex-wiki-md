# Universal property of Cartesian products

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md)

Given [functions](function-split.md) $f_i:Y\to X_i$, there is a unique [function](function-split.md) $g:Y\to X_1\times X_2$ whose coordinate [projection maps](projection-map.md) are $f_1,f_2$. It is the displayed pointwise pairing. Equality of ordered pairs is equality of both coordinates, which proves uniqueness and makes the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) a categorical product in the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md).
