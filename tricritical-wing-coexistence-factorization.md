# Tricritical wing coexistence factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Tricritical wing](tricritical-wing.md)

Take two nonnegative coexisting minima $a\leq b$, put $s=a+b$ and $p=ab$, and fix $v>0$. For the sextic [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) with quadratic, quartic, sextic and linear terms, equal stationary minimum values occur at

$$
u=\frac{2v}{3}(-2s^2+3p),\qquad
r=\frac v3(s^4-s^2p+3p^2),\qquad h=\frac v3s^3p.
$$

At these coefficients,

$$
f(M)-f(a)=\frac v6(M-a)^2(M-b)^2[(M+s)^2+p]\geq0.
$$

Thus both are [global minima](global-minimum.md). The case $a=0$ gives the zero-field three-phase line, including the negative minimum; the limit $a=b$ gives the [tricritical wing critical edge](tricritical-wing-critical-edge.md). Reflecting $M,h$ gives the negative-field wing.
