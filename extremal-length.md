# Extremal length

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller theory](teichmuller-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremal_length)

For a [path family](path-family.md) $\Gamma$ on a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces-split.md), take the supremum of $L_\rho(\Gamma)^2/A_\rho$ over measurable [conformal metrics](conformal-metric.md) of finite positive area. This is unchanged under [conformal equivalence](conformal-equivalence.md), and a [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md) of [maximal dilatation](maximal-dilatation.md) $K$ changes it by a factor between $K^{-1}$ and $K$.
