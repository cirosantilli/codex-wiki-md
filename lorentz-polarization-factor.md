# Lorentz-polarization factor

↑ **Parent:** [Powder diffraction](powder-diffraction.md)

An angular factor in measured integrated [diffraction intensity](diffraction-intensity.md) combining scattering geometry and the incident beam's [polarization](polarization-waves.md). For an unpolarized beam in a common symmetric powder-diffractometer geometry, it is proportional to $(1+\cos^2 2\theta)/(\sin^2\theta\cos\theta)$, with convention-dependent overall constants. Other geometries or incident [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) require the appropriate factor rather than this particular expression.
