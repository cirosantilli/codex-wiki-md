# Quasi-geostrophic wave pseudomomentum

↑ **Parent:** [Wave activity](wave-activity.md)

For small [quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md) waves on a locally uniform basic zonal flow with nonzero meridional [potential-vorticity gradient](potential-vorticity-gradient.md) $Q_y$, this signed quadratic [wave activity](wave-activity.md) obeys $\mathcal A_t+\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\overline{q'\mathcal D}/Q_y$, where $\mathbf F$ is the [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md) and $\mathcal D$ is a PV source. Multiply the linear PV equation by $q'/Q_y$ and use the [Taylor identity for quasi-geostrophic flux](taylor-identity-for-quasi-geostrophic-flux.md) to obtain it. In a locally uniform conservative wave packet, $\mathbf F=\mathcal A\mathbf c_g$, so the flux follows the [group velocity](group-velocity.md), with its sign determined by $Q_y$.
