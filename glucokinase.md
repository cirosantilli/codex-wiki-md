# Glucokinase

↑ **Parent:** [Hexokinase](hexokinase.md)

[Glucokinase](glucokinase.md) phosphorylates glucose and has kinetic properties suited to sensing changing glucose availability in liver and pancreatic beta cells. Its activity is not strongly inhibited by glucose-6-phosphate, and tissue-specific regulation determines how that flux is used.
