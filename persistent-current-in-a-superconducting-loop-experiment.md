# Persistent current in a superconducting loop experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

The [persistent current in a superconducting loop experiment](persistent-current-in-a-superconducting-loop-experiment.md) prepares a closed cold path, changes the externally linked [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), and follows the resulting [superconducting persistent current](persistent-current-superconductivity.md) through its [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The essential measurement starts after the inducing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) has stopped changing.

**[Figure 1](#image-preparing-a-closed-superconducting-loop-and-sensing-its-magnetic-field-with-a-compass). Preparing a closed superconducting loop and sensing its magnetic field with a compass**. Original schematic of field preparation and magnetic compensation. Shapes, winding counts and distances are illustrative. The [magnetic compass](compass.md) senses a static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); it is not a stationary voltage-reading [pickup coil](pickup-coil.md).

[Heike Kamerlingh Onnes](heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)'s 1914 Communication 140c describes a [lead](lead.md) coil closed by fused [lead](lead.md) connections. Cooling in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and removing the inducing magnet prepared the circulation. A [magnetic compass](compass.md) sensed it without contacts; a separate [copper](copper.md) coil, cooled in liquid air, supplied a measured balancing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Reversing orientation and compensation reduced offsets. [Communication 140c (1914), §5, pp. 278–280; §7, pp. 280–281](further-experiments-with-liquid-helium-j-continued-by-heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)

The liquid-[helium](helium.md) [cryostat](cryostat.md) maintained low [temperature](temperature-split.md), but refilling temporarily warmed the apparatus and changed the inferred [electric current](electric-current.md). A later run used a smaller initial [electric current](electric-current.md). Apparatus deformation and needle-zero drift limited comparison; cutting the closure still left a smaller magnetic signal, warning against assigning every signal to circulation around the whole loop. The report consequently gave an upper bound under a residual [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) hypothesis. [Communication 140c, §5, pp. 279–280; §6, p. 280; §8, pp. 281–283](further-experiments-with-liquid-helium-j-continued-by-heike-kamerlingh-onnes.md)

For interpreting such a measurement, write a locally calibrated magnetic signal as

$$
S(t)=k(t)I(t)+b(t).
$$

Here $k$ is the coupling and gain, while $b$ contains the background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and readout offset. Only after establishing stable $k$ and correcting $b$ does a ratio of signals determine a ratio of [electric currents](electric-current.md). A gradual change in geometry, a drifting background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), or a change of [temperature](temperature-split.md) can imitate or conceal a decay. A [pickup coil](pickup-coil.md) connected only to a voltage meter obeys [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and detects a changing linked [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md); its zero steady output cannot demonstrate that the underlying static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) has disappeared. A [magnetic compass](compass.md) can respond to that static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).

Opening the path, reversing the preparation, and repeating with smaller initial [electric current](electric-current.md) are useful controls for separating whole-loop circulation, magnetic background and a [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md). Keep the [temperature](temperature-split.md) and external [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) stable during the interval used to infer [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). A sudden reduction near a [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md) is not evidence for the constant-$R$ [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) model at smaller [electric current](electric-current.md). Persistence also does not replace the [Field-cooled Meissner experiment](field-cooled-meissner-experiment.md): transport around a hole and magnetic expulsion from the material test different properties.
