# Rotational symmetry number

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md)

The [rotational symmetry number](rotational-symmetry-number.md) corrects a rotational phase-space count for indistinguishable molecular orientations. A linear molecule with identical ends, such as carbon dioxide with identical oxygen isotopes, has $\sigma=2$; making its ends isotopically distinct gives $\sigma=1$. In the high-temperature linear [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) approximation, $q_{\rm rot}=8\pi^2IkT/(\sigma h^2)$. Holding the spectrum otherwise fixed, doubling $\sigma$ lowers molar rotational [entropy](entropy-split.md) by $R\log2$.
