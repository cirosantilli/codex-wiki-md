# Stress transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

Resolution of a [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) onto differently oriented planes. For two compression-positive [principal stresses](principal-stress.md), the resolved [normal stress](normal-stress.md) and [shear stress](shear-stress.md) trace a [Mohr circle](mohr-s-circle.md) as the plane normal rotates, with double the physical rotation angle.
