# Effective paramagnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

The [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) inferred from the weak-field [Curie constant](curie-constant.md) of a localized angular-momentum multiplet. Uniform zero-field occupation of its $2J+1$ substates gives $\langle m_J^2\rangle=J(J+1)/3$, yielding $\mu_{\rm eff}=|g_J|\mu_B\sqrt{J(J+1)}$. Here $g_J$ is the [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) and $\mu_B$ the [Bohr magneton](bohr-magneton.md). The dimensionless number $m_{\rm eff}=\mu_{\rm eff}/\mu_B$ differs from the saturated projection $|g_J|J$.
