# Theory of formally real fields

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

The theory of formally real fields consists of the [field](field.md) axioms and all sentences asserting that $-1$ is not a sum of $m$ squares, one for each positive integer $m$. The [real closure theorem](real-closure-theorem.md) and model completeness make the [Theory of real closed fields](theory-of-real-closed-fields.md) its [model companion](model-companion.md).
