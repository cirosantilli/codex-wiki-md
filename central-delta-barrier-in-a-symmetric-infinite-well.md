# Central delta barrier in a symmetric infinite well

↑ **Parent:** [Delta potential](delta-potential.md)

Inside walls at $\pm a$, a central [delta potential](delta-potential.md) of strength $\kappa$ leaves odd [parity operator](parity-operator.md) eigenstates unchanged because their wavefunctions vanish at zero. For even states, continuity and the derivative jump $\psi'(0+)-\psi'(0-)=2m\kappa\psi(0)/\hbar^2$ give the displayed quantization equation. For $\kappa>0$, one root lies between $(n+1/2)\pi/a$ and $(n+1)\pi/a$ for every $n\ge0$.
