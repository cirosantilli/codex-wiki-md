# Lubrication equation for a bubble with viscous exterior

↑ **Parent:** [Slender viscous bubble](slender-viscous-bubble.md)

Internal [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) of viscosity $\lambda\mu$ has axial volume flux $Q=-\pi a^4P_z/(8\lambda\mu)$ when the interfacial axial speed is negligible to leading order. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $(\pi a^2)_t+Q_z=0$. Combining with the exterior radial normal-stress balance gives the displayed implicit evolution equation. The radial viscous normal stress and capillary pressure both matter in its local pinch-off similarity.
