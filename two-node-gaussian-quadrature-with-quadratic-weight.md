# Two-node Gaussian quadrature with quadratic weight

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

The even weight $1-x^2$ has moments $m_0=4/3$ and $m_2=4/15$. Its monic quadratic orthogonal polynomial is $x^2-1/5$, so the two [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md) nodes are $\pm1/\sqrt5$. Symmetry and constant exactness give weights $2/3$ each. These nodes and weights integrate every polynomial of degree at most three exactly. Degree four fails: the true integral is $4/35$, whereas the rule gives $4/75$.
