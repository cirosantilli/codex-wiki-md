# Harmonic conjugate points

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

Distinct [projective points](projective-point.md) $C,D$ on the [projective line](projective-line.md) are harmonic conjugates relative to distinct $A,B$ when $(AB:CD)=-1$. Use the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md) convention $(AB:CD)=[a,c][b,d]/([a,d][b,c])$, where the brackets are two-by-two [determinants](determinant.md). In an affine coordinate with $A=0$, $B=\infty$, the condition is $D=-C$. A [complete quadrangle](complete-quadrangle.md) constructs the fourth point using joins and intersections only. Characteristic two is excluded because then $-1=1$, and the four points could not be distinct.
