# Magnetoelastic wave in an isotropic magnetic tangle

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

A long-wavelength transverse disturbance of an initially isotropic [magnetic second-moment tensor](magnetic-second-moment-tensor.md) behaves as an elastic shear [wave](wave-split.md) when small-scale rearrangements of the tangle are omitted. For $v_A^2=\langle B^2\rangle/3$, its [fluid displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) obeys $\partial_t^2\boldsymbol\xi=v_A^2\nabla^2\boldsymbol\xi$ and $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi=0$ on the oscillatory branch. The tensor perturbation is $\delta M_{ij}=v_A^2(\partial_i\xi_j+\partial_j\xi_i)$. A zero mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) does not imply zero restoring [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md).
