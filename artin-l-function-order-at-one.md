# Artin L-function order at one

↑ **Parent:** [Artin L-function](artin-l-function.md)

Assume that every nontrivial one-dimensional [Artin L-function](artin-l-function.md) over the intermediate fields is holomorphic and nonzero at one, while the trivial one is the base [Dedekind zeta function](dedekind-zeta-function.md) with a simple pole. By [Brauer induction](brauer-induction.md), $\chi=\sum_jn_j\operatorname{Ind}_{H_j}^G\psi_j$ with integers $n_j$ and [linear characters](linear-character.md) $\psi_j$. Compatibility of [Artin L-functions](artin-l-function.md) with [induced representations](induced-representation.md) expresses $L_K(\chi,s)$ as the corresponding product of $L_{L^{H_j}}(\psi_j,s)^{n_j}$. Its order at one is $-\sum_jn_j\langle\psi_j,1_{H_j}\rangle$. [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md) turns this sum into $-\langle\chi,1_G\rangle$. Hence every nontrivial [irreducible character](irreducible-character.md) gives order zero and a holomorphic nonzero function near one. This local conclusion does not assert global Artin holomorphy.
