# Neutrino helicity

↑ **Parent:** [Helicity](helicity.md)

For a relativistic [neutrino](neutrino.md), the normalized [helicity](helicity.md) is approximately $-1$ in ordinary weak production. For a massive particle, [helicity](helicity.md) and [fermion chirality](chirality-physics.md) differ; suppressed opposite-helicity amplitudes and frame dependence prevent promoting the massless selection rule to an exact statement about arbitrary boosts.
