# Tensorization lower bound for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For a product reference $P=\bigotimes_iP_i$ and arbitrary $Q$,

$$
D(Q\Vert P)\geq\sum_iD(Q_i\Vert P_i).
$$

It follows from the [chain rule for relative entropy](chain-rule-for-relative-entropy.md) and convexity of relative entropy in its first argument.
