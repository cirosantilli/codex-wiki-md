# Finite presentation of an algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated algebra](finitely-generated-algebra.md)

A presentation of an [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) with finitely many generators and relations. The [Hilbert basis theorem](hilbert-basis-theorem.md) ensures that every [finitely generated algebra](finitely-generated-algebra.md) over a field admits such a presentation. Its [module of Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md) is presented by the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the relations.
