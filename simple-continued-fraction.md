# Simple continued fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Continued fraction](continued-fraction.md)

A continued fraction $[a_0;a_1,a_2,\ldots]$ with integer $a_0$ and positive integer $a_j$ for $j\geq1$. Its partial numerators are all one. The [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md) have the standard two-term numerator and denominator recurrences.
