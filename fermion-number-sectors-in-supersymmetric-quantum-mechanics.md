# Fermion-number sectors in supersymmetric quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric quantum mechanics](supersymmetric-quantum-mechanics.md)

For $d$ [fermionic creation operators](fermionic-creation-operator.md) with the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md), the [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $L^2(\mathbb R^d)\otimes\Lambda^\bullet\mathbb C^d$ splits into $\mathcal H_n=L^2(\mathbb R^d)\otimes\Lambda^n\mathbb C^d$, $0\leq n\leq d$. The [fermion number operator](fermion-number-operator.md) $N=\sum_a\psi_a^\dagger\psi_a$ has value $n$ on $\mathcal H_n$; its internal multiplicity is $\binom dn$. An odd [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) $Q=q+q^\dagger$, with $q$ raising $n$ and $q^2=0$, gives $H=\{q,q^\dagger\}$ and preserves these sectors.

On an [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) with $E>0$, $Q/\sqrt E$ is an [odd involution pairing bosonic and fermionic states](odd-involution-pairing-bosonic-and-fermionic-states.md). More precisely,

$$
v=E^{-1}(qq^\dagger+q^\dagger q)v
$$

gives an [orthogonal decomposition](orthogonal-decomposition-by-a-closed-subspace.md) of each state into the images of $q$ and $q^\dagger$: their [inner product](inner-product.md) vanishes because $(q^\dagger)^2=0$. If $u\in\operatorname{im}q^\dagger$, then $q^\dagger u=0$, $q^\dagger qu=Eu$ and $\|qu\|^2=E\|u\|^2$. Thus $q/\sqrt E$ pairs such a state in degree $n$ with one in degree $n+1$, and $q^\dagger/\sqrt E$ is the inverse. States with zero energy are killed by both operators and may remain unpaired. The pairing applies to positive spectral subspaces as well; discreteness is not implied.
