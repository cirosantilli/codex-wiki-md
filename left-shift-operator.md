# Left shift operator

↑ **Parent:** [Unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md)

On the [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $\ell^2(\mathbb N)$, the left shift deletes the first coordinate. It is the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) of the [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) $S$. The identities $LS=I$ and $SLf=f-f_1e_1$ show that it is also the [Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator](moore-penrose-inverse-of-an-operator.md) for $S$; both maps have [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one.
