# Winning coalition

↑ **Parent:** [Simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md)

A [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) of value $1$ in a [simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md). Monotonicity makes every superset of a [winning coalition](winning-coalition.md) winning. An inclusion-minimal [winning coalition](winning-coalition.md) has no winning proper [subset](subset.md).
