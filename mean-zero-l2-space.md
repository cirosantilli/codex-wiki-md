# Mean-zero L2 space

↑ **Parent:** [L2 space is a Hilbert space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md)

The mean-zero L2 space is the kernel of the [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) $h\mapsto Ph$ on [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) for a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) $P$. It is a [closed subspace of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md), with [inner product](inner-product.md) $P(hk)$, and is the natural ambient space for [score functions](informant-function.md) and [canonical gradients](canonical-gradient.md).
