# Heavy-hole and light-hole bands

↑ **Parent:** [Valence band](valence-band.md)

Near the zone centre of many cubic [semiconductors](semiconductor-split.md), the upper [valence bands](valence-band.md) have different curvatures and hence different [band effective masses](band-effective-mass.md). In the isotropic approximation, the $j=3/2$ states with angular-momentum projection magnitude $3/2$ along the propagation direction form the heavy-hole branch; projection magnitude $1/2$ gives the light-hole branch. A flatter downward electron dispersion corresponds to a larger positive [electron hole](electron-hole.md) effective mass. Crystal anisotropy, strain and confinement can mix these branches, so the labels do not imply direction-independent exact masses.
