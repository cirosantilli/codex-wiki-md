# Projective hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)

A degree-$d$ projective hypersurface $X_d\subseteq\mathbb P_k^n$ is the [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) cut out by one nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree $d$.

This is a homogeneous-polynomial [hypersurface](hypersurface.md) in projective space.
