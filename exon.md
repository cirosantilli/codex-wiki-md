# Exon

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exon)

A segment retained in the mature [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) after [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md), or the corresponding segment of a [gene](gene.md). An [exon](exon.md) need not encode a [protein](protein.md): the [ribosomal RNA](ribosomal-rna.md) [exons](exon.md) in the Tetrahymena [ribozyme](ribozyme.md) experiment are an example.
