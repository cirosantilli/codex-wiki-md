# Weyl Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)

The isotropic [Weyl Hamiltonian](weyl-hamiltonian.md) acts on a two-component [spinor](spinor.md) as $H=v\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p$. For a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) of momentum $\hbar\mathbf k$, its two [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are $E_\pm=\pm\hbar|v||\mathbf k|$. For $v>0$ the corresponding [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) point along and opposite $\mathbf k$, respectively. If the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) describe physical [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the mean spin is $\pm(\hbar/2)\widehat{\mathbf k}$; in an effective two-band model the same algebra can describe an internal pseudospin instead. The bands meet linearly at the origin. [David Tong, Gauge Theory, §4.3.2, continuum Hamiltonian near equation (4.26)](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gaugetheory/4lattice.pdf)
