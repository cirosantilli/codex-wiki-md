# Steady accretion disk with arbitrary rotation law

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

For inward [accretion rate](accretion-rate.md) $\dot M>0$, the steady [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) equation is $\mathcal G=\dot M(h-h_{\rm in})$ when the inner torque vanishes. The [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) is $\mathcal G=2\pi qh\bar\nu\Sigma$, where $h=r^2\Omega$ and $q$ is the [orbital shear parameter](orbital-shear-parameter.md). Thus

$$
\bar\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot M}{2\pi q}\left(1-\frac{h_{\rm in}}h\right).
$$

The familiar [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) follows when $q=3/2$ and $h\propto r^{1/2}$. The general form also applies to the non-Keplerian [Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit](paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit.md) rotation law.
