# Perigee

↑ **Parent:** [Periapsis](periapsis.md)

The [periapsis](periapsis.md) of an Earth-centred [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). For an [elliptic orbit](elliptic-orbit.md) its distance from the Earth's centre is $a(1-e)$. Both this distance and the [apogee](apogee.md) distance are measured from the force centre, rather than as heights above Earth's surface.
