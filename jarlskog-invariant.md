# Jarlskog invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarlskog_invariant)

The Jarlskog invariant is a rephasing-invariant measure of CP violation in a three-generation quark mixing matrix. It is the common magnitude of imaginary quartic products such as $\operatorname{Im}(V_{ij}V_{k\ell}V_{i\ell}^*V_{kj}^*)$ for distinct rows and columns.
