# Jarlskog invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarlskog_invariant)

The [Jarlskog invariant](jarlskog-invariant.md) is a measure of [CP violation](cp-violation.md) that survives [quantum field rephasings](quantum-field-rephasing.md). Fix its sign by the quartet

$$
J=\operatorname{Im}(V_{ud}V_{cs}V_{us}^*V_{cd}^*).
$$

For the exact three-generation [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), direct multiplication gives

$$
J=c_{12}c_{23}c_{13}^2s_{12}s_{23}s_{13}\sin\delta.
$$

The [CKM unitarity triangle](ckm-unitarity-triangle.md)'s illustrative parameters give $J=89991\sqrt{55}/3200000000\simeq2.085597\times10^{-4}$. Setting $s_{13}=0$ or $\delta=0$ makes $J=0$: the existence of three [quark generations](quark-generation.md) is necessary for this mechanism, but not sufficient for [CP violation](cp-violation.md).

To see the invariance directly, the phases multiplying $V_{ud}V_{cs}V_{us}^*V_{cd}^*$ sum to

$$
(-\alpha_u+\beta_d)+(-\alpha_c+\beta_s)+(\alpha_u-\beta_s)+(\alpha_c-\beta_d)=0.
$$

Under [CP](cp-symmetry.md), the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) is complex conjugated and $J$ changes sign. In an exactly [unitary](unitary-connection.md) three-generation [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), quartets formed from any distinct pair of rows and columns have imaginary parts equal to $J$ up to sign. This follows by multiplying a row [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) relation by a suitable pair of entries and taking its imaginary part: its diagonal product is real, forcing the two remaining imaginary parts to cancel. The same [Jarlskog invariant](jarlskog-invariant.md) therefore determines the areas of all six unscaled [CKM unitarity triangles](ckm-unitarity-triangle.md).
