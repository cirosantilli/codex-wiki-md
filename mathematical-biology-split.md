# Mathematical biology

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology)

[Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md) uses [mathematical models](mathematical-model.md) to connect biological mechanisms with quantitative predictions. [Differential equations](differential-equation-split.md) can track changing quantities, while [population dynamics](population-dynamics.md), [enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md) and [gene regulatory networks](gene-regulatory-network.md) ask how growth, reactions and feedback shape living systems. A useful [mathematical model](mathematical-model.md) states its assumptions and units: a simple [chemostat](chemostat.md), for example, reveals how nutrient supply and removal jointly determine whether a culture persists.
