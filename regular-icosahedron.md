# Regular icosahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Platonic solid](platonic-solid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_icosahedron)

A [regular icosahedron](regular-icosahedron.md) is a [Platonic solid](platonic-solid.md) with twenty equilateral triangular faces, twelve vertices and thirty edges. Five faces meet at each vertex. Its orientation-preserving [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) has order sixty: the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives twenty faces times the three [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) stabilizing a chosen face.
