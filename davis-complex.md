# Davis complex

↑ **Parent:** [Standard parabolic subgroup](standard-parabolic-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_complex)

The Davis complex is the geometric realization of the poset of cosets $wW_T$ for spherical subsets $T\subseteq S$. Equivalently, it is the [basic construction of a Coxeter group](basic-construction-of-a-coxeter-group.md) obtained by gluing copies of the Davis chamber along mirrors. It is a contractible proper $W$-CW complex.
