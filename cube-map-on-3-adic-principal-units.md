# Cube map on 3-adic principal units

↑ **Parent:** [Higher principal-unit group](higher-principal-unit-group.md)

The cube of $1+3t$ is $1+9(t+3t^2+3t^3)$. Conversely the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $T+3T^2+3T^3-a$ has a unique simple residue root modulo three for each $a\in\mathbb Z_3$, so [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) produces a cube root of every $1+9a$. Thus the [principal units](principal-unit.md) modulo their cubes have order three. The change of variable removes the derivative obstruction that prevents direct simple-root lifting for $X^3-u$.
