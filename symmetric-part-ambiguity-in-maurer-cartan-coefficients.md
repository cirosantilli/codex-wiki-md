# Symmetric-part ambiguity in Maurer-Cartan coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Maurer-Cartan equation in a Lie-algebra basis](maurer-cartan-equation-in-a-lie-algebra-basis.md)

When $d\sigma^\gamma=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}f^\gamma{}_{\alpha\beta}\sigma^\alpha\wedge\sigma^\beta$ sums over ordered pairs, the [Maurer-Cartan equation](maurer-cartan-equation.md) determines only $f^\gamma{}_{[\alpha\beta]}=-c^\gamma{}_{\alpha\beta}/2$. Symmetric additions vanish in the [exterior product](exterior-product.md). The usual coefficients are the unique antisymmetric representatives; summing instead over $\alpha<\beta$ removes the factor one half.
