# Exponentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication](multiplication.md)

For a nonnegative integer exponent, [exponentiation](exponentiation.md) iterates [multiplication](multiplication.md): $a^0=1$ and $a^{b+1}=a\,a^b$. This convention defines $0^0=1$ in [primitive recursive](primitive-recursive-function.md) arithmetic. Extensions to other exponents may use different domains and conventions.
