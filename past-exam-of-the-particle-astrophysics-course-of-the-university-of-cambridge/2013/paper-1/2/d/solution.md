<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) is relic thermal [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) from the hot early universe. Its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) anisotropy is expanded in [spherical harmonics](../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md), with angular [power](../../../../../power.md) spectrum $C_\ell^{TT}$. [Thomson scattering](../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) of a local [radiation quadrupole](../../../../../radiation-quadrupole.md) generates [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) around last scattering and again during [reionization](../../../../../reionization.md). Polarization maps are decomposed into [E-mode polarization](../../../../../e-mode-polarization.md) and [B-mode polarization](../../../../../b-mode-polarization.md), giving spectra such as $EE$, $TE$ and $BB$ in addition to [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) information.

[Cosmic microwave background acoustic peak](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-acoustic-peak.md) positions compare the [sound horizon](../../../../../sound-horizon.md) with the angular-diameter distance to last scattering, constraining geometry and expansion history. Relative peak heights constrain [baryon](../../../../../baryon.md) loading and [matter-radiation equality](../../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md); the damping tail constrains diffusion and other early-universe physics. The [scalar spectral index](../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md) and [primordial scalar amplitude](../../../../../primordial-scalar-amplitude.md) constrain inflationary perturbations. Coherent acoustic structure and temperature-polarization correlations support predominantly adiabatic initial perturbations. Large-angle [E-mode polarization](../../../../../e-mode-polarization.md) measures the [reionization](../../../../../reionization.md) [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md), helping break its degeneracy with the scalar amplitude inferred from small-scale [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) anisotropy.

Under a specified cosmological model, these data constrain ordinary and [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) densities, the [Hubble constant](../../../../../hubble-constant.md), spatial curvature and cosmic age. Complementary distance information helps break the geometrical degeneracy: [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) peaks alone do not uniquely establish a [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md). The Planck 2013 parameter analysis used Planck [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) data and, in important combinations, [WMAP](../../../../../wilkinson-microwave-anisotropy-probe.md) low-multipole polarization; it should not be described as a full Planck polarization release.[Abstract and data-combination definitions, including WMAP polarization](../../../../../planck-2013-results-xvi-cosmological-parameters-by-planck-collaboration.md)

Linear [scalar cosmological perturbations](../../../../../scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md) generate [E-mode polarization](../../../../../e-mode-polarization.md) but no primordial [B-mode polarization](../../../../../b-mode-polarization.md) in the simplest treatment. [tensor cosmological perturbations](../../../../../tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md) can generate [B-mode polarization](../../../../../b-mode-polarization.md), making them a possible probe of inflationary [gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md). [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) converts some E-mode [power](../../../../../power.md) into [B-mode polarization](../../../../../b-mode-polarization.md), however, and Galactic dust and [synchrotron radiation](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) also contribute polarized foregrounds. Multifrequency observations, foreground modelling, beam [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) and control of [temperature-to-polarization leakage](../../../../../temperature-to-polarization-leakage.md) are therefore necessary. Polarization does not by itself establish a primordial tensor signal. **[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and polarization jointly test the expansion history and primordial perturbations, while complementary data and foreground control determine how uniquely parameters can be inferred.**
