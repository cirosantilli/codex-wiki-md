<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [grand unified theory](../../../../../grand-unified-theory.md) embeds the strong and electroweak [gauge groups](../../../../../gauge-group.md) in a larger group, for example $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\subset SU(5)$. [quarks](../../../../../quark.md) and [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) can belong to common representations, while heavy extra gauge or scalar [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) connect them. The resulting interactions can violate [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md), unlike ordinary perturbative Standard Model reactions. Running gauge couplings and proton-decay searches test such ideas; unification is a theoretical possibility, not an established observation.[Abstract, early-universe baryon-number-violating reactions in unified theories; later erratum noted in source record](../../../../../unified-gauge-theories-and-the-baryon-number-of-the-universe-by-motohiko-yoshimura.md)

[baryogenesis](../../../../../baryogenesis.md) must explain why a nearly matter-antimatter-symmetric early state evolves into a surviving [baryon asymmetry](../../../../../baryon-asymmetry.md). In the usual local CPT-invariant framework, the [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md) require baryon-number violation, C and [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md), and departure from [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md). [baryon](../../../../../baryon.md) conservation would forbid creating a net excess; exact conjugation symmetries would pair baryon-producing processes with equal antibaryon production; equilibrium with no imposed asymmetry would erase a source term through [detailed balance](../../../../../detailed-balance.md).

For heavy particles $X$ and $\bar X$, let decay channels carry different [baryon numbers](../../../../../baryon-number.md). CP-violating interference can make their [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) differ, even though [CPT symmetry](../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) requires equal total widths. Defining the mean [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) produced per decay as $\epsilon$, an out-of-equilibrium abundance can generate $n_B/s$ of order $\epsilon$ times its initial number per [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md), reduced by inefficient production and washout. A rough condition is that decays become effective after inverse production has ceased, with the relevant reaction rate compared with the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../hubble-parameter.md). Complex couplings alone do not guarantee a nonzero rate asymmetry: a relative [absorptive phase](../../../../../absorptive-phase-of-a-decay-amplitude.md) is also needed.

Survival matters as much as production. Electroweak [sphalerons](../../../../../sphaleron.md) violate $B+L$ while conserving [baryon-minus-lepton number](../../../../../baryon-minus-lepton-number.md) $B-L$, so a minimal GUT-generated asymmetry with $B-L=0$ can be washed out if [sphalerons](../../../../../sphaleron.md) and the other relevant reactions equilibrate. Generating nonzero $B-L$, as in suitable heavy-Majorana-neutrino [leptogenesis](../../../../../leptogenesis.md), can avoid this problem. **A viable GUT [baryogenesis](../../../../../baryogenesis.md) model must generate an asymmetry and demonstrate that subsequent reactions do not erase it.** The [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md) are necessary ingredients under these assumptions, not a numerical prediction of the observed abundance.
