↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [gravitational wave](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) produces a time-dependent tidal distortion. For a normally incident plus-polarized wave in the long-wavelength limit, two perpendicular freely suspended arms change by $\delta L_x/L=h_+/2$ and $\delta L_y/L=-h_+/2$. Their differential length is therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta L=Lh_+},\qquad
\delta\Phi=\frac{4\pi\Delta L}{\lambda}
$$

for a simple round-trip [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md). [LIGO](../../../../../ligo.md) uses kilometre-scale orthogonal arms with [optical cavities](../../../../../optical-cavity.md) that enhance the phase response, a stabilized [laser](../../../../../laser-split.md) and suspended mirrors in [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md). As an illustrative scale, $h=10^{-21}$ across a 4 km arm corresponds to differential length $4\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm m$. The measured quantity is returning light phase, not mirror displacement against a rigid external ruler. Optical storage and control loops give a frequency-dependent calibrated response.

Seismic and terrestrial disturbances dominate low [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md), thermal motion of suspensions and mirror coatings contributes at intermediate [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md), and [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [shot noise](../../../../../shot-noise.md) limits high-frequency readout. Isolation and suspension systems reduce ground coupling; low-loss materials reduce [thermal noise](../../../../../thermal-noise.md); [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) suppresses gas scattering and [forces](../../../../../force-split.md). Greater optical [power](../../../../../power.md) reduces [shot noise](../../../../../shot-noise.md) but increases radiation-pressure fluctuations and thermal demands. Stable optical alignment and feedback are essential, yet their own sensor and actuator noise must be controlled. These are engineering requirements because the desired [strain](../../../../../strain.md) is extraordinarily small.[Instrument configuration and detector-noise discussion; primary initial-LIGO account](../../../../../ligo-the-laser-interferometer-gravitational-wave-observatory-by-b-p-abbott-and-others.md)

A candidate should appear coherently at separated observatories with consistent propagation delay and antenna response. Auxiliary environmental monitors, [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) injections and time-shifted coincidence backgrounds test instrumental explanations. Matched filtering is powerful for modelled compact-binary chirps; coherent burst searches allow less predictable signals. Binary-pulsar orbital [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss already provided indirect evidence for [gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) before 2013, but it is distinct from directly measuring passing waves.[Binary-pulsar orbital-decay test of relativistic gravity](../../../../../binary-pulsars-and-relativistic-gravity-by-joseph-hooton-taylor-jr.md) At the examination date, direct detection remained a search objective. **Reliable detection requires both sufficient [strain](../../../../../strain.md) sensitivity and a demonstrated astrophysical signal rather than correlated or accidental noise.**
