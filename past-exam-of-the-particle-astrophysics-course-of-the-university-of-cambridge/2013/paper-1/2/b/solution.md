<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) pairs bosonic and fermionic degrees of freedom; broken [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) permits partners heavier than known particles. At the [Large Hadron Collider](../../../../../large-hadron-collider.md), strong production of [squarks](../../../../../squark.md) and [gluinos](../../../../../gluino.md) is an important channel. If [R-parity](../../../../../r-parity.md) is conserved, partners are produced in pairs and their cascades end in a stable [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md). A neutral weakly interacting [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md) escapes, giving [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) together with jets and possibly [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md). Conservation is tested transversely because the initial [parton](../../../../../parton.md) longitudinal momenta are unknown.

The experimental observable is

$$
\boldsymbol p_T^{\rm miss}=-\sum_{\rm reconstructed}\boldsymbol p_T.
$$

[neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) produce genuine [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md), and [calorimeter](../../../../../calorimeter.md) mismeasurement or detector defects can imitate it. Searches use jet multiplicity, transverse momenta, missing [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), angular separation cuts and a [collider effective mass](../../../../../collider-effective-mass.md) such as $m_{\rm eff}=p_T^{\rm miss}+\sum p_T^{\rm jet}$. Isolated-lepton selections explore complementary cascades. Trigger efficiency, jet [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scale, [collision pileup](../../../../../collision-pileup.md) and event cleaning affect acceptance.

A credible search estimates backgrounds from [particle-physics control regions](../../../../../particle-physics-control-region.md) before inspecting the [signal region](../../../../../signal-region.md). Invisible $Z\to\nu\bar\nu$ plus jets can be constrained using photon-plus-jets or visible $Z\to\ell^+\ell^-$ samples with [control-region transfer factors](../../../../../control-region-transfer-factor.md). Lepton-enriched samples constrain $W$ and top backgrounds with lost [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md); multijet samples constrain fake missing [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). A joint [likelihood](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) includes these observations and [nuisance parameters](../../../../../nuisance-parameter.md) for [systematic uncertainties](../../../../../systematic-uncertainty.md). Agreement with background gives upper bounds on signal rate and conditional [mass](../../../../../mass.md) exclusions. The pre-examination ATLAS 7 TeV analysis is a concrete example of this strategy and reported no significant excess.[Sections III, IX and XI, control-region likelihood and background estimates; abstract for the null result](../../../../../search-for-squarks-and-gluinos-with-the-atlas-detector-in-final-states-with-jets-and-missing-transverse-momentum-using-4-7-fb-1-of-sqrt-s-7-tev-proton-proton-collision-data-by-atlas-collaboration.md)

Electroweak production motivates multilepton searches; compressed spectra can give soft visible decay products and require special selections such as recoil against [initial-state radiation](../../../../../initial-state-radiation.md). [R-parity](../../../../../r-parity.md) violation can instead remove the stable missing-particle signature, requiring other searches. **An exclusion always depends on [masses](../../../../../mass.md), [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md), production rates and decay assumptions; a null jets-plus-missing-momentum result does not exclude [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) as a whole.**
