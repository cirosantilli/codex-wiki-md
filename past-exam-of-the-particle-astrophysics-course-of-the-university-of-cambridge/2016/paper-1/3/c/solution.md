<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [topological defect](../../../../../topological-defect.md) can remain after [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) when the field cannot be continuously put into the vacuum everywhere. If the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) is $\mathcal M=G/H$, its [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) classifies possible obstructions. Causally separated regions can settle into different local order-parameter configurations; joining them during a [phase transition](../../../../../phase-transition.md) can trap defects. This is a formation mechanism, not an assertion that every transition creates every possible defect.

In three spatial dimensions the [homotopy classification of cosmological defects](../../../../../homotopy-classification-of-cosmological-defects.md) gives are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{vacuum topology}&\text{configuration}&\text{spatial character}\\ \hline
\pi_0(\mathcal M)\ne0&\text{domain walls}&\text{surfaces}\\
\pi_1(\mathcal M)\ne0&\text{strings}&\text{lines}\\
\pi_2(\mathcal M)\ne0&\text{monopoles}&\text{points}\\
\pi_3(\mathcal M)\ne0&\text{textures}&\text{extended winding}
\end{array}
$$

Disconnected vacua can meet across a wall; winding around a line can obstruct filling its core; a sphere around a point can carry monopole charge. Textures differ from permanent localized cores: they can collapse and unwind when the field leaves the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md). Local gauge and global symmetry breaking also differ in their long-range fields and [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md). The original cosmological analysis connects vacuum [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) with domain and string formation and their subsequent evolution.[J. Phys. A 9, pp. 1387–1398 (1976), vacuum topology and domain/string evolution](../../../../../topology-of-cosmic-domains-and-strings-by-thomas-walter-bannerman-kibble.md)

Original schematic vacuum spaces, not pictures of astronomical objects. The main text specifies the physical-space loops or spheres whose mappings classify walls, strings and monopoles.

The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget determines cosmological importance. Nonrelativistic monopoles dilute approximately as $a^{-3}$, so a large primordial abundance can become unacceptable as [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) falls faster. Inflation can dilute an earlier population, provided the same defect-producing transition is not repeated afterwards. A slowly evolving wall network dilutes especially slowly and can dominate the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget or create excessive anisotropy.

Strings can lose [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by intercommutation and loop formation, with loops radiating [gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) or field quanta. A [horizon-scaling cosmic string network](../../../../../horizon-scaling-cosmic-string-network.md) whose characteristic separation is of order the horizon can have density $\rho_{\rm string}\sim\mu/t^2$ in natural units, keeping a limited fraction of the total rather than accumulating indefinitely. This differs from an ideal frozen noninteracting network's simple $a^{-2}$ dilution law. Similarly, a [horizon-scaling domain wall network](../../../../../horizon-scaling-domain-wall-network.md) has $\rho_{\rm wall}\sim\sigma/t$, while a frozen wall network follows $a^{-1}$. The evolutionary assumptions must accompany any quoted scaling.

Defects can seed density wakes and other inhomogeneities, perturb the microwave background and generate non-Gaussian signatures. A sufficiently straight ideal [cosmic string](../../../../../cosmic-string.md) has conical spacetime and can create doubled background images; a cosmic-string network can generate gravitational-wave bursts and a stochastic background. Textures can produce localized microwave-background [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) features while collapsing. These signatures constrain defect abundance and symmetry-breaking scale; an isolated suggestive feature is not a unique defect detection.

The predictions depend on network evolution, reconnection, loop sizes, [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) channels and the relation between symmetry breaking and inflation. **[topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) identifies which configurations are possible; formation and evolution determine whether they survive and whether their gravitational or radiative effects are observable.** A defect's unseen [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) does not automatically make it the dominant [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md).
