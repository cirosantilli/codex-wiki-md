<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Extra spatial dimensions offer mechanisms for unification and for the large separation between weak and gravitational scales. They are hypotheses about geometry and fields, not observations established by the [hierarchy problem](../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md) itself.

In a [Kaluza-Klein theory](../../../../../kaluza-klein-theory.md) construction, a compact extra coordinate can turn components of a higher-dimensional metric into four-dimensional gauge and [scalar fields](../../../../../scalar-field.md). For a field periodic on a circle of radius $R$, [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) in that direction is quantized. In units $\hbar=c=1$,

$$
m_n^2=m_0^2+\frac{n^2}{R^2},\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The resulting tower explains why a small compact dimension can be hard to see at low [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md): nonzero modes need [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of order $1/R$. A realistic model must also explain its matter representations, [chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md), internal geometry and stabilization of [Lorentz scalar](../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) moduli; a compact coordinate alone does not reproduce the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md).

Large-extra-dimension models instead use volume to weaken effective gravity. With a fixed unwarped compact space of volume $V_d$, integrating the higher-dimensional [Einstein-Hilbert action](../../../../../einstein-hilbert-action.md) gives, in a consistent reduced-Planck-mass convention,

$$
\boxed{M_{\rm Pl}^2=M_D^{d+2}V_d.}
$$

The [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) can be localized on a [brane](../../../../../brane.md) while gravity propagates in the bulk. A large $V_d$ makes the observed four-dimensional gravitational scale large even if the fundamental higher-dimensional scale is much smaller. The proposal identifies gravitational dilution as a possible hierarchy mechanism, with deviations from ordinary gravity at sufficiently short distance.[arXiv:hep-ph/9803315, abstract and volume-dilution mechanism](../../../../../the-hierarchy-problem-and-new-dimensions-at-a-millimeter-by-nima-arkani-hamed-and-others.md) For approximately flat dimensions of common size $R$, gravity at distances much smaller than $R$ spreads through more directions and the force scales as $r^{-(2+d)}$, returning to $r^{-2}$ at large distance.

Warped models use a different mechanism. A five-dimensional metric

$$
ds^2=e^{-2k|y|}\eta_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu+dy^2
$$

redshifts a [mass](../../../../../mass.md) on a distant [brane](../../../../../brane.md) by $m_{\rm IR}=e^{-kL}m_{\rm UV}$. An exponential hierarchy can therefore arise without a large flat compact volume. The original Randall–Sundrum construction makes this distinction explicit and predicts characteristic massive spin-two excitations.[arXiv:hep-ph/9905221, abstract and warped hierarchy mechanism](../../../../../a-large-mass-hierarchy-from-a-small-extra-dimension-by-lisa-randall-and-raman-sundrum.md) It still needs a consistent stabilization and phenomenology; the flat-volume formula and the warped redshift should not be treated as interchangeable explanations.

Extra dimensions also occur in string-theory constructions, where consistency and [compactification](../../../../../compactification-physics.md) can organize gravitational and gauge degrees of freedom. That is a framework motivation, not proof that a particular [compactification](../../../../../compactification-physics.md) describes nature. Laboratory inverse-square-law tests, collider missing-momentum channels from escaping bulk modes, massive-mode resonances, and astrophysical or cosmological energy-loss constraints probe different parameter ranges. Background estimates and dependence on field localization matter: a [brane](../../../../../brane.md) model with invisible bulk emission need not share the signatures of a model with visible resonant decays. **The useful claim is a testable mechanism for a specified hierarchy or unification problem, with [compactification](../../../../../compactification-physics.md) and stabilization assumptions stated.**
