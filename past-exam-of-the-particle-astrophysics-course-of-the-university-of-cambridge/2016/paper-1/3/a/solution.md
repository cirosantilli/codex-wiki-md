↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) measures the gravitational influence of matter without requiring that matter to shine. In [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) a [point mass](../../../../../point-mass.md) produces small-angle deflection

$$
\hat\alpha=\frac{4GM}{bc^2}.
$$

For a thin lens, with angular-diameter distances $D_d,D_s,D_{ds}$,

$$
\boldsymbol\beta=\boldsymbol\theta-
\frac{D_{ds}}{D_s}\hat{\boldsymbol\alpha}(D_d\boldsymbol\theta).
$$

Image multiplicity, distortion and magnification therefore constrain projected gravitating [mass](../../../../../mass.md), with source and lens geometry measured separately.

Strong lensing gives multiple images, arcs and [Einstein rings](../../../../../einstein-ring.md). Their positions and, where available, time delays constrain a central [mass](../../../../../mass.md) distribution. Weak lensing measures correlated small image distortions over a larger field. Using the [critical surface density for gravitational lensing](../../../../../critical-surface-density-for-gravitational-lensing.md) and [reduced gravitational shear](../../../../../reduced-gravitational-shear.md), the convergence and shear satisfy

$$
\kappa=\frac{\Sigma}{\Sigma_{\rm crit}},\qquad
\Sigma_{\rm crit}=\frac{c^2}{4\pi G}\frac{D_s}{D_dD_{ds}},\qquad
g_{\rm red}=\frac{\gamma}{1-\kappa}.
$$

Because intrinsic galaxy shapes are unknown, weak shear is inferred statistically using many sources. Point-spread-function anisotropy, shear calibration, photometric redshifts, intrinsic alignments and foreground contamination must be controlled.

An inferred lensing [mass](../../../../../mass.md) can be compared with the independently estimated stellar and hot-gas [mass](../../../../../mass.md). The [bullet Cluster dark-matter separation](../../../../../bullet-cluster-dark-matter-separation.md) provides a particularly informative separation of components: the collisional X-ray-emitting gas is displaced from the galaxies, while lensing [mass](../../../../../mass.md) peaks approximately follow the less-collisional galaxy component. The primary analysis combined wide-field and Hubble imaging rather than inferring lensing [mass](../../../../../mass.md) from X-ray hydrostatic equilibrium.[arXiv:astro-ph/0608407, abstract and lensing/X-ray component comparison](../../../../../a-direct-empirical-proof-of-the-existence-of-dark-matter-by-douglas-clowe-and-others.md) This favors a dominant relatively collisionless unseen component. Translating a merger offset into a particle self-interaction [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) also requires a merger model and projection uncertainties; lensing does not directly reveal a [WIMP](../../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md)'s [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or quantum numbers.

Microlensing tests a different possibility: [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) in compact objects. A compact foreground lens can create an unresolved, time-dependent magnification of a background star. Event frequency and duration constrain the abundance and [mass](../../../../../mass.md) range of compact lenses only after survey cadence, detection efficiency, blending, source sizes and halo geometry are included. Achromaticity and the expected light-curve shape help distinguish simple lensing from intrinsic variability. A null survey result does not exclude every compact-object [mass](../../../../../mass.md) at every location.

Weak-lensing correlations and small-scale strong-lens structure also test the [matter power spectrum](../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) and halo substructure, potentially distinguishing cold from warm free-streaming behavior. Baryonic feedback can change the same small-scale [mass](../../../../../mass.md) distribution. There are intrinsic inversion ambiguities: the mass-sheet transformation

$$
\kappa\mapsto\lambda\kappa+(1-\lambda),\qquad
\gamma\mapsto\lambda\gamma
$$

leaves [reduced gravitational shear](../../../../../reduced-gravitational-shear.md) unchanged. Source-redshift information, absolute magnification or other data can restrict such degeneracies. **Lensing establishes where gravitating matter is and tests its distribution and interactions; identification of its particle species requires complementary evidence.**
