[supersymmetry](../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) relates [bosons](../../../../boson.md) and [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) through fermionic symmetry generators. A main motivation is the [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) [hierarchy problem](../../../../hierarchy-problem.md): related [boson](../../../../boson.md) and [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) loop contributions cancel particular ultraviolet sensitivities in an exactly supersymmetric theory. [soft supersymmetry breaking](../../../../soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md) leaves residual sensitivity to the [superpartner](../../../../sparticle.md) scale, so a very heavy spectrum does not automatically solve naturalness. Supersymmetric matter content can also improve gauge-coupling unification, and a stable neutral lightest [superpartner](../../../../sparticle.md) can be a dark-matter candidate. None of these motivations is itself experimental evidence; [supersymmetry](../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) must be broken if ordinary particles and their partners have different observed [masses](../../../../mass.md).

If [R-parity](../../../../r-parity.md) is conserved, [superpartners](../../../../sparticle.md) are produced in pairs and cascades end in a stable [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md). A [neutralino](../../../../neutralino.md) LSP can escape the detector, giving [missing transverse momentum](../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md). Colored [squarks](../../../../squark.md) and [gluinos](../../../../gluino.md) can generate energetic [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md), sometimes b-tagged [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) and [leptons](../../../../lepton.md), plus that imbalance. An [inclusive supersymmetry search](../../../../inclusive-supersymmetry-search.md) classifies broad event samples by [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) and [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) multiplicities, [transverse momentum](../../../../transverse-momentum.md) sums and missing [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), without requiring a fully reconstructed decay chain.

Background controls are part of the physics argument. Lost-lepton W and top events can be constrained using [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) [collider control regions](../../../../collider-control-region.md); invisible-Z production can be related to visible-Z or photon samples with appropriate transfer factors. Instrumental imbalance in multijet events needs detector-quality checks and data sidebands. An excess in a [collider signal region](../../../../collider-signal-region.md) should survive uncertainty in those extrapolations, efficiencies and [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md), with independently defined validation regions and attention to the number of searches performed.

An [exclusive supersymmetry search](../../../../exclusive-supersymmetry-search.md) targets a more specific production mode and decay pattern. Electroweak chargino-neutralino production can yield three [leptons](../../../../lepton.md) plus missing [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md); a cascade through a [slepton](../../../../slepton.md) can yield a dilepton invariant-mass edge that constrains [mass](../../../../mass.md) combinations. Such searches often have cleaner backgrounds but depend more strongly on branching fractions and intermediate [masses](../../../../mass.md). An endpoint does not necessarily determine every absolute [mass](../../../../mass.md) without additional observables.

Different spectra require different strategies. For a [compressed supersymmetric spectrum](../../../../compressed-supersymmetric-spectrum.md), small parent-daughter [mass](../../../../mass.md) differences make the visible decay products soft. [initial-state radiation](../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) can recoil against the pair and provide a trigger, supplemented by soft-lepton reconstruction. The ATLAS compressed-spectrum study explicitly uses small [neutralino](../../../../neutralino.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) differences and [initial-state radiation](../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) to define a dedicated search rather than applying a hard-lepton selection indiscriminately.[arXiv:1712.08119, abstract and compressed-spectrum search strategy; methodological illustration published after this exam](../../../../search-for-electroweak-production-of-supersymmetric-states-in-scenarios-with-compressed-mass-spectra-at-13-tev-with-the-atlas-detector-by-the-atlas-collaboration.md) If [R-parity](../../../../r-parity.md) is violated, the LSP can decay visibly, so high-multiplicity [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) or [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) channels may matter more than large missing [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). CMS separately studied hadronic and leptonic final states for this reason.[CMS-SUS-14-003, abstract and independent lepton-multiplicity analyses](../../../../searches-for-r-parity-violating-supersymmetry-in-pp-collisions-at-8-tev-in-final-states-with-0-4-leptons-by-the-cms-collaboration.md) Long-lived particles instead motivate displaced vertices, disappearing tracks, abnormal [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) or time-of-flight measurements. Thus **there is no single model-independent missing-energy cut that covers every supersymmetric spectrum**.

For the hypothetical relic-density calculation, use the supplied distribution and retain its prefactor exactly. Put $M=mc^2$, $\Theta=k_BT$ and $\Theta_0=k_BT_0$. Then

$$
n_f=6\left[\frac{M\Theta_f}{(\hbar c)^2}\right]^{3/2}e^{-P},\qquad
\Theta_f=\frac{M}{P}.
$$

[number density](../../../../number-density.md), denoted $N$ in the paper, is written $n$ here to distinguish it from a total particle count. The [cosmological particle freeze-out](../../../../cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) in [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) units is

$$
\boxed{\Theta_f=\frac{38}{36}\,\mathrm{GeV}=1.0556\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

Assume a stable nonrelativistic relic, negligible further [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) and $aT=\mathrm{constant}$ after [cosmological particle freeze-out](../../../../cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md), using [sharp-freeze-out relic dilution](../../../../sharp-freeze-out-relic-dilution.md). Its comoving particle number is conserved, so

$$
n_0=n_f\left(\frac{a_f}{a_0}\right)^3
=n_f\left(\frac{\Theta_0}{\Theta_f}\right)^3
=\boxed{6\left(\frac{\Theta_0}{\hbar c}\right)^3P^{3/2}e^{-P}.}
$$

The particle [mass](../../../../mass.md) cancels from this present [number density](../../../../number-density.md) under the stated approximation. Its present rest-energy density is $Mn_0$, not merely $n_0$:

$$
n_f=4.4193\times10^{34}\,\mathrm{m}^{-3},\qquad
n_0=4.8766\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}^{-3},
$$



$$
\boxed{\rho_0=38n_0=1.8531\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{GeV\,m}^{-3},\qquad
\frac{\rho_0}{\rho_c}=3.7062\times10^{-3}.}
$$

This is only **0.371% of the [critical density](../../../../critical-density.md)**, not a universe-closing component. The correct further comparison is with the measured dark-matter fraction, not an assumption that all [critical density](../../../../critical-density.md) must be [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md). In standard [cosmology](../../../../cosmology-split.md) this abundance would be a small subcomponent, despite a possible collider discovery.

The [exponential freeze-out sensitivity](../../../../exponential-freeze-out-sensitivity.md) makes the result highly sensitive to $P$. Also, thermal [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) release means $aT$ is not exactly constant through the whole cosmic history. If total comoving [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) is conserved, the above [number density](../../../../number-density.md) acquires a factor $g_{*s,0}/g_{*s,f}$, involving [entropy degrees of freedom](../../../../entropy-degrees-of-freedom.md). Those degrees of freedom and a full [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) history are not given, so no precise correction is justified from the supplied data.

Establishing the nature of [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md) would require more than measuring a missing particle's collider [mass](../../../../mass.md). Measure its spin, charges, interactions, decay lifetime or stability limits, and [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) or coannihilation processes; compare the resulting relic calculation with cosmological observations. Seek compatible direct nuclear-recoil signals and indirect [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) or decay products, while controlling astrophysical and detector backgrounds. A collider-invisible lifetime need not be cosmologically long, and nonthermal production could change the abundance. **A consistent particle identity, cosmological abundance and independent non-collider evidence are needed to connect the hypothetical discovery to the [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md).**
