For a fixed comoving cell, take physical volume $V=R^3$ and energy density $u=\rho c^2$. With negligible chemical potentials and fixed relativistic species, $u=AT^4$ and $P=u/3$. The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives

$$
T\,dS=d(uR^3)+\frac u3d(R^3),\qquad dS=d\!\left(\frac{4AR^3T^3}{3}\right).
$$

Choosing zero entropy at zero temperature yields **$S=4R^3u/(3T)$**. Reversible expansion conserves this entropy; when species disappear, the effective entropy degrees of freedom must be changed rather than keeping $A$ fixed.

Before electron-positron annihilation, photons contribute two states, electrons plus positrons four, and three neutrino/antineutrino species six. Thus

$$
g_*=2+\frac78(4+6)=\frac{43}{4},\qquad\boxed{u=\frac a2g_*T^4=\frac{43}{8}aT^4.}
$$

After neutrinos decouple, the electromagnetic entropy alone is conserved through annihilation: $(2+7\times4/8)T_i^3R_i^3=2T_\gamma^3R_f^3$, while $T_\nu R_f=T_iR_i$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_\nu}{T_\gamma}=\left(\frac4{11}\right)^{1/3}\simeq0.714.}
$$

This assumes instantaneous decoupling, negligible neutrino masses/chemical potentials and complete electromagnetic thermalization. Small noninstantaneous corrections are outside this approximation.

In the [Pati-Salam model](../../../../pati-salam-model.md), right-handed quarks and leptons fill a $(4,1,2)$ multiplet. The neutrino component has no colour, $T_L=0$ and hypercharge $Y=T_{3R}+(B-L)/2=1/2-1/2=0$. Hence $\nu_R$ is a [Standard Model gauge singlet neutrino](../../../../standard-model-gauge-singlet-neutrino.md), with no ordinary weak or electromagnetic gauge coupling. It is **sterile under the low-energy Standard Model**, not a singlet of the full unbroken Pati-Salam group. A heavy Majorana mass can arise after symmetry breaking. The representation alone fixes neither its mass nor the stability, mixing or relic abundance needed for dark matter.[1974 gauge embedding and right-handed neutrino](../../../../lepton-number-as-the-fourth-color-by-jogesh-pati-and-abdus-salam.md)

**Space example: [AMS-02](../../../../ams-02.md)**, a magnetic spectrometer on the International Space Station. Tracker curvature determines rigidity and charge sign; transition-radiation detection and an electromagnetic calorimeter distinguish electrons/positrons from the much larger proton background. It measures charged cosmic-ray spectra, including positrons that could arise from halo-particle annihilation. This is an **indirect dark-matter search**, despite directly recording the charged products: it does not measure scattering of neutral halo WIMPs, and astrophysical sources can mimic a positron excess. Calling it direct WIMP detection would misstate the technique.[2013 positron fraction and detector discrimination](../../../../first-result-from-the-alpha-magnetic-spectrometer-on-the-international-space-station-by-ams-collaboration.md)

**Underground example: [XENON100](../../../../xenon100.md)**, a [dual-phase xenon detector](../../../../dual-phase-xenon-detector.md). A candidate WIMP scatters from a xenon nucleus, producing prompt liquid scintillation S1 and ionization electrons. Drift and extraction into gas produce delayed proportional scintillation S2. Their delay and light patterns reconstruct the interaction position; S2/S1 discriminates nuclear recoils from electron-recoil backgrounds. Underground shielding, fiducial cuts and calibration reduce backgrounds, though neutrons can imitate nuclear recoils. This is direct recoil detection; a null excess constrains cross sections for an assumed halo and interaction model.[Dual-phase time-projection chamber, scintillation and ionization](../../../../the-xenon100-dark-matter-experiment-by-the-xenon100-collaboration.md)[2012 search found no significant dark-matter excess](../../../../dark-matter-results-from-225-live-days-of-xenon100-data-by-the-xenon100-collaboration.md)
