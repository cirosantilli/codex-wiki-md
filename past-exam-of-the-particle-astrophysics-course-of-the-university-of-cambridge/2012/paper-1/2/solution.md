<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Gravitational evidence requires much more nonbaryonic matter than can be supplied by ordinary luminous material or by the baryon abundance permitted by [nucleosynthesis](../../../../nucleosynthesis.md). A stable, electrically neutral massive particle is a plausible cold component. Thermal production followed by [cosmological particle freeze-out](../../../../cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md) gives an additional motivation: an [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) rate near a weak-interaction scale can leave an appreciable relic abundance. This is a mechanism and search hypothesis, not proof that the [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md) must be a [WIMP](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) or have any particular elastic-scattering [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md).

The plotted regions are conditional [WIMP](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) interpretations of different measurements. The experiments use different targets and distinguish signal from background differently:

- [DAMA experiment](../../../../dama-experiment.md) uses NaI(Tl) [particle scintillation](../../../../scintillation-physics.md) and seeks an annual variation of low-energy single-hit counts as the Earth's velocity through the halo changes. Multiple-hit events and other [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) ranges provide controls, but [particle scintillation](../../../../scintillation-physics.md) alone does not identify every event as an [electron](../../../../electron.md) or [nuclear recoil](../../../../nuclear-recoil.md). Converting the modulation into [mass](../../../../mass.md) and [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) requires a [halo model](../../../../halo-model.md) and [nuclear-recoil quenching factor](../../../../nuclear-recoil-quenching-factor.md) assumptions.[abstract and single-hit annual-modulation analysis](../../../../new-results-from-dama-libra-by-r-bernabei-and-others.md)
- [CoGeNT experiment](../../../../cogent-experiment.md) uses a low-capacitance p-type point-contact [germanium](../../../../germanium.md) diode to reach low [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) thresholds. Low-energy event [radiation spectrum](../../../../radiation-spectrum.md) and a possible annual modulation can suggest light [dark matter](../../../../dark-matter.md), but surface charge collection, internal x-ray lines and threshold efficiency must be distinguished from bulk recoil events. Its 2011 modulation paper described a modulated component of unknown origin rather than an unambiguous particle identification.[abstract and pulse/energy-event analysis](../../../../search-for-an-annual-modulation-in-a-p-type-point-contact-germanium-dark-matter-detector-by-c-e-aalseth-and-others.md)
- [CDMS experiment](../../../../cdms-experiment.md) measures [phonons](../../../../phonon.md) and [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) in cryogenic [germanium](../../../../germanium.md) or [silicon](../../../../silicon.md). The smaller [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) yield of [nuclear recoils](../../../../nuclear-recoil.md) and [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) timing help reject [electron](../../../../electron.md) and surface events. The lowered-threshold [germanium](../../../../germanium.md) analysis improves low-mass sensitivity, at the cost of more difficult background discrimination near threshold; this explains why distinct 2010 and 2011 curves appear.[abstract; low-threshold analysis and background discussion](../../../../results-from-a-low-energy-analysis-of-the-cdms-ii-germanium-data-by-cdms-collaboration.md)
- [XENON100](../../../../xenon100.md) uses a [dual-phase xenon detector](../../../../dual-phase-xenon-detector.md). A recoil produces prompt liquid [particle scintillation](../../../../scintillation-physics.md), S1, and [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) [electrons](../../../../electron.md). A drift field transports the charge to the surface; extracted [electrons](../../../../electron.md) produce gas [electroluminescence](../../../../electroluminescence.md), S2. Drift delay and the S2 light pattern reconstruct position, while S2/S1 statistically separates [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [nuclear recoils](../../../../nuclear-recoil.md). An inner [fiducial volume](../../../../fiducial-volume.md) suppresses external radiation. Neutron and gamma [calibrations](../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) establish recoil response and leakage, and low-light-yield [calibration](../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) is particularly important for light [WIMPS](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md). Neutrons can themselves mimic [nuclear recoils](../../../../nuclear-recoil.md).[detector principle, signal reconstruction and calibration sections](../../../../the-xenon100-dark-matter-experiment-by-the-xenon100-collaboration.md)

The plot also includes [CRESST experiment](../../../../cresst-experiment.md), whose cryogenic calcium-tungstate modules combine [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) and scintillation-light signals. Different nuclei contribute to its recoil interpretation. Its excess region is another conditional candidate region, not an extra confirmed detection.[abstract and detector/recoil-population analysis](../../../../results-from-730-kg-days-of-the-cresst-ii-dark-matter-search-by-g-angloher-and-others.md)

Around ten GeV, the shaded regions favour [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) roughly $10^{-41}$–$10^{-40}\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, while the [XENON100](../../../../xenon100.md) solid curve and the improved low-mass CDMS curve cut into or exclude much of those regions under the common elastic, [spin-independent WIMP-nucleus scattering](../../../../spin-independent-wimp-nucleus-scattering.md) interpretation expressed per [nucleon](../../../../nucleon.md). **There is tension within that shared model; the plot does not show mutually confirmed discoveries.** [electron](../../../../electron.md) backgrounds, threshold response, [nuclear-recoil quenching factors](../../../../nuclear-recoil-quenching-factor.md), channeling assumptions and the halo velocity distribution can shift interpretations. Changing the interaction model can also alter target comparisons, but cannot establish a [WIMP](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) by assertion. The 100-live-day [XENON100](../../../../xenon100.md) result itself reported candidates compatible with estimated background.[abstract; background-compatible candidates and elastic spin-independent limit](../../../../dark-matter-results-from-100-live-days-of-xenon100-data-by-xenon100-collaboration.md)

For a rough dimensional event estimate, $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives

$$
10^{-41}\text{--}10^{-40}\,\mathrm{cm^2}=0.01\text{--}0.1\,\mathrm{fb},
\qquad \mathcal L\sigma\sim20(0.01\text{--}0.1)=\boxed{0.2\text{--}2\ \text{events}}.
$$

If a collider production [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) had this value, that small yield, before acceptance and backgrounds, would not support a likely discovery. **The plotted nuclear-scattering [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) is not the LHC production [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md)**, however. Converting between them needs a mediator, couplings, parton distributions and production kinematics; a light stable [WIMP](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) would normally be inferred from [missing transverse momentum](../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) with a visible recoil object, not a visible [WIMP](../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) track. The [mass](../../../../mass.md) makes [pair production](../../../../pair-production.md) kinematically accessible, but the plot and [integrated collider luminosity](../../../../integrated-collider-luminosity.md) alone do not decide discovery probability.

For the relic estimate, define $M=mc^2$ and $\theta=k_BT$. The [sharp-freeze-out relic dilution](../../../../sharp-freeze-out-relic-dilution.md) model gives

$$
\theta_f=\frac{M}{P}=\frac{10}{30}\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
n_f=6\left[\frac{M\theta_f}{(\hbar c)^2}\right]^{3/2}e^{-P}
=4.27\times10^{35}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

Assume stable particles, no subsequent [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md), and $RT=\mathrm{constant}$. Conserved comoving particle number then gives

$$
n_0=n_f\left(\frac{T_0}{T_f}\right)^3
=6P^{3/2}\left(\frac{k_BT_0}{\hbar c}\right)^3e^{-P}
=\boxed{0.1497\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}}.
$$

The present rest-energy density and its critical fraction are

$$
\boxed{\rho_{\chi,0}=Mn_0\simeq1.50\,\mathrm{GeV\,m^{-3}},\qquad
\Omega_\chi\simeq\frac{1.50}{5}=0.30}.
$$

Thus the supplied toy model yields a cosmologically substantial density, of the right broad scale to motivate a dark-matter candidate. It is not an independent precision prediction: $P$ is imposed and the abundance depends exponentially on it. If [entropy degrees of freedom](../../../../entropy-degrees-of-freedom.md) change, [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) conservation instead gives an extra factor $g_{*s,0}/g_{*s,f}$ in $n_0$ when using the [photon](../../../../photon.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The species content needed to evaluate that correction is not supplied. A realistic calculation also follows residual [annihilations](../../../../annihilation.md) with the abundance [Boltzmann equation](../../../../boltzmann-equation-split.md) rather than stopping exactly at equilibrium freeze-out.
