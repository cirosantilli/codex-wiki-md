<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [Majorana neutrino](../../../../../majorana-neutrino.md) is its own [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md), with a [mass](../../../../../mass.md) term that can violate [lepton number](../../../../../lepton-number.md) by two units. It is not thereby a state of both readily observable [helicities](../../../../../helicity.md) with equal weak rates: charged-current vertices still select [chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md). In light-neutrino exchange, the needed [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) connection between two beta vertices supplies a neutrino-mass factor.

Ordinary two-neutrino [double beta decay](../../../../../double-beta-decay.md) is $(A,Z)\to(A,Z+2)+2e^-+2\bar\nu_e$ and conserves [lepton number](../../../../../lepton-number.md). It can occur when the direct single-beta route is energetically forbidden or highly suppressed but the double change is allowed. [neutrinoless double beta decay](../../../../../neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md), $(A,Z)\to(A,Z+2)+2e^-$, violates [lepton number](../../../../../lepton-number.md) by two. For the conventional [light-Majorana exchange in neutrinoless double beta decay](../../../../../light-majorana-exchange-in-neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md) mechanism,

$$
[T_{1/2}^{0\nu}]^{-1}=G_{0\nu}|\mathcal M_{0\nu}|^2
\left|\frac{m_{\beta\beta}}{m_e}\right|^2,\qquad
m_{\beta\beta}=\left|\sum_iU_{ei}^2m_i\right|.
$$

Here $U$ is the [lepton mixing matrix](../../../../../lepton-mixing-matrix.md), $G_{0\nu}$ the phase-space factor in this convention and $\mathcal M_{0\nu}$ a [nuclear matrix element](../../../../../nuclear-matrix-element.md). Complex phases can cancel contributions to the [effective Majorana mass](../../../../../effective-majorana-mass.md); oscillation [mass](../../../../../mass.md) differences alone do not fix it. Other lepton-number-violating mechanisms can also cause the process, so a rate is not a model-independent measurement of $m_{\beta\beta}$.

The experimental signature is a peak in the summed [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) at the nuclear Q-value, apart from small recoil and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) losses. Two-neutrino decay instead gives a continuous sum-energy [radiation spectrum](../../../../../radiation-spectrum.md) because [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) carry away variable [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Isotope enrichment, large exposure, good [energy resolution](../../../../../energy-resolution.md) and radiopure shielding improve sensitivity; gamma backgrounds, [radon](../../../../../radon.md), surface events and the two-neutrino tail require [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) and sideband controls. Coincidence or track information can test whether the topology is compatible with two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). A later concrete example is GERDA's 2013 germanium-76 source-detector search, which used pulse-shape discrimination and background controls rather than identifying any isolated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) deposit as a decay.[experimental method and pulse-shape/background analysis; published after this exam](../../../../../results-on-neutrinoless-double-beta-decay-of-76ge-from-gerda-phase-i-by-the-gerda-collaboration.md)

**Observation would establish lepton-number violation and support a Majorana [mass](../../../../../mass.md) component; a null search constrains mechanisms and does not prove [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) are Dirac particles.**
