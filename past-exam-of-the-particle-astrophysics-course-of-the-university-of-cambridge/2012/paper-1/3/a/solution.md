<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) can produce [gamma rays](../../../../../gamma-ray.md), charged [antiparticles](../../../../../antiparticle.md) and [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md), directly or through decay and [hadronization](../../../../../hadronization.md) of the primary products. For self-conjugate [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m_\chi$, the [annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) rate per unit volume is $\langle\sigma v\rangle\rho_\chi^2/(2m_\chi^2)$. For a [photon](../../../../../photon.md) yield $dN_\gamma/dE$, the optically thin flux from a chosen solid angle is

$$
\frac{d\Phi_\gamma}{dE}=\frac{\langle\sigma v\rangle}{8\pi m_\chi^2}\frac{dN_\gamma}{dE}\,
\underbrace{\int_{\Delta\Omega}d\Omega\int_{\rm los}\rho_\chi^2\,dl}_{J}.
$$

Use consistent mass-energy units. The factor $1/2$ assumes identical self-conjugate particles; distinct particle-antiparticle populations require their separate [number densities](../../../../../number-density.md). A gamma-ray continuum with an endpoint near the particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is generic for many channels, whereas a narrow line from $\chi\chi\to\gamma\gamma$ or $\gamma Z$ would be especially distinctive but often loop suppressed. [dwarf spheroidal galaxies](../../../../../dwarf-spheroidal-galaxy.md) offer high dark-matter content with limited conventional emission; the [Galactic centre](../../../../../galactic-center.md) has a potentially larger signal and much more complicated foregrounds.

Charged [Positrons](../../../../../positron.md) and [antiprotons](../../../../../antiproton.md) are redistributed by [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and lose [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), so their [radiation spectrum](../../../../../radiation-spectrum.md) depend on propagation and cannot usually point directly to the source. [annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) can also produce synchrotron and inverse-Compton radiation. [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) captured in the Sun could produce escaping high-energy [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md), with the signal depending on capture, [annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) equilibrium and particle interactions. Early [annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) injection can alter recombination and [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) anisotropies.

**A persuasive interpretation joins particle-like spectral structure with a consistent density-dependent morphology and other channels.** Pulsars, cosmic-ray interactions and unresolved sources can imitate continuum excesses. Density-profile uncertainties, substructure and velocity-dependent [annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) modify the predicted signal; a lack of a detectable line does not exclude all annihilating [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md).
