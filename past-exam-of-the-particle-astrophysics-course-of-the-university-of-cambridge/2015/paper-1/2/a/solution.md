<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The primordial-element abundances from [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), together with the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md), constrain the total baryonic density. [massive compact halo objects](../../../../../massive-compact-halo-object.md) such as ordinary faint stars, [brown dwarfs](../../../../../brown-dwarf.md) and [stellar remnants](../../../../../stellar-remnant.md) are baryonic whether or not they shine. With the stipulated density fractions, **the entire baryonic budget is only one fifth of the required dark-matter density**, and much of it is already accounted for by ordinary gas and stars.

A second test does not require a compact object to emit light: [gravitational microlensing](../../../../../gravitational-microlensing.md) temporarily magnifies a background star as the object crosses the line of sight. Monitoring many Magellanic-Cloud stars constrains the [microlensing optical depth](../../../../../microlensing-optical-depth.md) and event-duration distribution. Surveys such as EROS-2 found too few suitable events for a standard halo predominantly composed of compact objects over their tested [mass](../../../../../mass.md) range.[Abstract and halo-fraction limits; mass-dependent detection efficiency](../../../../../limits-on-the-macho-content-of-the-galactic-halo-from-the-eros-2-survey-of-the-magellanic-clouds-by-the-eros-2-collaboration.md) Artificial-event injection, cadence and blending corrections are needed because very short or long events are easily missed; lensing by stars within the Clouds must also be separated from halo lensing. **These are mass-range and halo-model constraints, not a proof excluding every compact-object [mass](../../../../../mass.md).** Primordial [black holes](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) are not limited by the ordinary-baryon budget in the same way as [stellar remnants](../../../../../stellar-remnant.md); their viability requires their own observational bounds.
