<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) active [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) decouple while relativistic and retain a thermal momentum distribution. They therefore constitute [hot dark matter](../../../../../hot-dark-matter.md) for the light masses compatible with the standard cosmological picture. Their [collisionless free streaming](../../../../../free-streaming.md) erases [linear cosmological density perturbations](../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) below a characteristic scale. Making them the dominant gravitating matter would delay small-scale structure formation and suppress the observed [matter power spectrum](../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md).

For standard thermal abundances and nonrelativistic [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) today,

$$
\Omega_\nu h^2\simeq\frac{\sum_i m_{\nu_i}}{93\ \mathrm{eV}/c^2},
\qquad
h=\frac{H_0}{100\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}}.
$$

Increasing the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) sum enough to provide all the stipulated [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) conflicts with the successful [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) description of cosmological structure. Independently, the [Tremaine-Gunn bound](../../../../../tremaine-gunn-bound.md) on the fermionic phase-space occupation limits the density light [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) can supply in small galactic haloes.[Pages 407–410, collisionless phase-space bound](../../../../../dynamical-role-of-light-neutral-leptons-in-cosmology-by-scott-tremaine-and-james-edward-gunn.md) Oscillations establish nonzero [neutrino mass](../../../../../neutrino-mass.md) differences, but do not make the relic [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) cold. **Active [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) can contribute some matter; they cannot supply the dominant cold component.** A heavier [sterile neutrino](../../../../../sterile-neutrino.md) candidate would be a different hypothesis, not an ordinary [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) relic.
