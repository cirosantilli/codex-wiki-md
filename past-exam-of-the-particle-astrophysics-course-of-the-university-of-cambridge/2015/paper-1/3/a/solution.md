<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

When a cosmological [phase transition](../../../../../phase-transition.md) breaks a [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $G$ to a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$, the degenerate vacuum configurations form a [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) $\mathcal M=G/H$. Causally separated regions need not choose the same vacuum orientation. The [Kibble mechanism](../../../../../kibble-mechanism.md) explains how joining those choices can leave a field configuration that cannot be continuously deformed to a uniform vacuum without leaving $\mathcal M$. The field then stores [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in a defect core, gradients or [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md).[Sections on vacuum topology, domains and strings](../../../../../topology-of-cosmic-domains-and-strings-by-thomas-walter-bannerman-kibble.md)

The relevant [homotopy classification of cosmological defects](../../../../../homotopy-classification-of-cosmological-defects.md) uses the vacuum map on a surface surrounding a proposed defect:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{nontrivial topology}&\text{defect in three spatial dimensions}&\text{example}\\
\hline
\pi_0(\mathcal M)&\text{domain wall}&\mathcal M=\{+v,-v\}\\
\pi_1(\mathcal M)&\text{string}&\mathcal M=S^1\\
\pi_2(\mathcal M)&\text{monopole}&\mathcal M=S^2
\end{array}
$$

A [domain wall](../../../../../domain-wall.md) separates different disconnected vacua. A [cosmic string](../../../../../cosmic-string.md) has nonzero [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md) on a loop around its core. A [magnetic monopole](../../../../../magnetic-monopole.md) has a nontrivial vacuum map on an enclosing sphere. Nontrivial $\pi_3(\mathcal M)$ can instead support [cosmological texture](../../../../../cosmological-texture.md) configurations extending over a volume; [cosmological textures](../../../../../cosmological-texture.md) can unwind dynamically by leaving the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) and need not possess a permanently localized stable core. Nontrivial topology permits a defect; formation efficiency, interactions and the cosmological history still determine its abundance.

For example, a complex scalar with a circular set of minima can wind by $2\pi n$ around a [cosmic string](../../../../../cosmic-string.md). Continuity forces the field away from its vacuum value near the core. A local [gauge symmetry](../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) allows [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md) to screen much of the exterior gradient and trap flux, whereas a global [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) leaves long-range Goldstone-field gradients. Thus local and global [cosmic strings](../../../../../cosmic-string.md) have different energy-loss channels; they should not be treated as identical networks.

Relic defects can be tightly constrained because their [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) dilutes differently from [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md). Nonrelativistic [magnetic monopoles](../../../../../magnetic-monopole.md) approximately dilute as $a^{-3}$, so excessive production causes the [cosmological monopole problem](../../../../../cosmological-monopole-problem.md). A roughly frozen wall network has [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) proportional to $a^{-1}$, even slower dilution. An evolving [cosmic string network](../../../../../cosmic-string-network.md) can approach a scaling regime with characteristic separation proportional to [cosmic time](../../../../../cosmic-time.md) and density of order $\mu/t^2$ in $c=1$ units, losing [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) into loops and [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md). That is a dynamical network statement, not the dilution law of a fixed collection of [cosmic strings](../../../../../cosmic-string.md).

[cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) can dilute defects made before it; [reheating](../../../../../reheating.md) and later [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) breaking must not regenerate an unacceptable abundance. Observational tests include microwave-background anisotropy, [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) by [cosmic strings](../../../../../cosmic-string.md), [gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) from string loops, and searches for [magnetic monopoles](../../../../../magnetic-monopole.md). Each is sensitive to model-dependent tensions, couplings and evolution. **Defects are possible relics of [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) breaking, not an inevitable dominant dark-matter population.** Their absence constrains a transition and its subsequent history rather than every theory possessing a nontrivial vacuum topology.
