<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The inference of [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) uses a gravitational law to turn observed motions and lensing into a [mass](../../../../../mass.md) distribution. [modified gravity](../../../../../modified-gravity.md) asks whether some discrepancies instead reveal a breakdown of that law. The strongest motivation at galactic scales is the regular relation between baryonic structure and [galaxy rotation curves](../../../../../galaxy-rotation-curve.md), including approximately flat outer rotation speeds and the [baryonic Tully-Fisher relation](../../../../../baryonic-tully-fisher-relation.md).

In [modified Newtonian dynamics](../../../../../modified-newtonian-dynamics.md), the low-acceleration spherical relation is $g\,\mu(g/a_0)=g_N$, with $\mu(x)\to1$ at high acceleration and $\mu(x)\to x$ at low acceleration. For an isolated baryonic [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M_b$ in the latter regime,

$$
\frac{v_c^4}{r^2a_0}=\frac{GM_b}{r^2},
\qquad
\boxed{v_c^4=GM_ba_0}.
$$

Thus an asymptotically flat rotation curve and a fourth-power [mass](../../../../../mass.md)–speed relation arise from a common acceleration scale, without assigning an independent halo profile to every [galaxy](../../../../../galaxy-split.md). This is a substantive phenomenological motivation. Distances, stellar [mass-to-light ratios](../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md), gas distributions and the external gravitational environment must still be controlled.[Astrophysical Journal 270, pp. 365–370, low-acceleration law and asymptotic rotation-speed prediction](../../../../../a-modification-of-the-newtonian-dynamics-as-a-possible-alternative-to-the-hidden-mass-hypothesis-by-mordehai-milgrom.md)

A nonrelativistic acceleration prescription does not itself specify light bending or cosmology. A relativistic completion must determine the [metric](../../../../../metric.md) followed by photons and matter. [tensor-vector-scalar gravity](../../../../../tensor-vector-scalar-gravity.md) illustrates the need for additional dynamical fields and a specified physical [metric](../../../../../metric.md); lensing predictions then have to be calculated within that theory rather than imported unchanged from [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md).[Abstract and relativistic field construction, physical metric and lensing](../../../../../relativistic-gravitation-theory-for-the-mond-paradigm-by-jacob-bekenstein.md)

Several observations challenge a simple replacement of [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) by a single galactic acceleration law. [galaxy clusters](../../../../../galaxy-cluster.md) retain substantial [mass](../../../../../mass.md) discrepancies even after such a modification. In the [bullet Cluster dark-matter separation](../../../../../bullet-cluster-dark-matter-separation.md), the dominant X-ray-emitting baryonic gas is displaced from the galaxies, whereas weak-lensing [mass](../../../../../mass.md) concentrations lie nearer the galaxies. Collisionless [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) accompanying the galaxies provides a natural account of this separation. The inference uses a lensing model and does not constitute a theorem excluding every conceivable modified-gravity theory, but a successful alternative must reproduce the displaced lensing signal quantitatively.[L109–L113, comparison of optical galaxies, X-ray gas and weak-lensing mass map](../../../../../a-direct-empirical-proof-of-the-existence-of-dark-matter-by-douglas-clowe-and-others.md)

The [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md), acoustic structure, expansion history and growth of cosmic structure provide additional simultaneous tests. A theory that fits [galaxy rotation curves](../../../../../galaxy-rotation-curve.md) must also supply the appropriate cosmological perturbation evolution and lensing. Adding extra clustering fields can help, but may reintroduce an effective unseen gravitating component. Solar-system and binary-pulsar agreement with [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) constrain alternatives in their tested regimes, although a modification designed to act only at very low acceleration is not automatically ruled out by a high-acceleration test.

A discriminating programme compares dynamics, which probes the potential accelerating nonrelativistic matter, with lensing, which probes the sum of the two scalar [metric](../../../../../metric.md) potentials. Scale-dependent discrepancies, [gravitational slip](../../../../../gravitational-slip.md) and environmental screening can then be tested with controlled [galaxy bias](../../../../../galaxy-bias.md) and selection effects. Galaxy-scale tensions in a dark-matter model must likewise be separated from uncertain baryonic astrophysics. **Regular galactic scaling relations motivate [modified gravity](../../../../../modified-gravity.md); clusters and the combined cosmological evidence strongly challenge its simplest dark-matter-free forms.** Failure to detect a particular [WIMP](../../../../../weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) is not positive evidence for a particular modified-gravity theory.
