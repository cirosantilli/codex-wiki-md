<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) predicts partners with [spins](../../../../../spin.md) differing by one half. At a proton collider, strongly interacting [squarks](../../../../../squark.md) and [gluinos](../../../../../gluino.md) can be pair produced if kinematically accessible. With conserved [R-parity](../../../../../r-parity.md), their cascade decays end in a stable [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md), often a [neutralino](../../../../../neutralino.md). The generic signature is energetic [particle jets](../../../../../jet-particle-physics.md), possibly [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) or heavy-flavour jets, together with [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) from invisible particles. Longitudinal missing momentum is not an equally clean observable because the initial [parton](../../../../../parton.md) momentum fractions are unknown.

A practical inclusive search defines [collider signal regions](../../../../../collider-signal-region.md) using jet multiplicity, [transverse momenta](../../../../../transverse-momentum.md), [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) and an event scale such as

$$
m_{\rm eff}=|\mathbf p_T^{\rm miss}|+\sum_{\rm selected\ jets}p_{T,j}.
$$

Leptonic and heavy-flavour selections probe other decay chains. For a [compressed supersymmetric spectrum](../../../../../compressed-supersymmetric-spectrum.md), the daughter particles can be soft, making a hard [initial-state radiation](../../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) jet useful for triggering and recoil. Direct [chargino](../../../../../chargino.md) or [neutralino](../../../../../neutralino.md) production instead motivates multilepton searches. Decays with [R-parity violation](../../../../../r-parity-violation.md) can yield high jet or [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) multiplicities with little missing momentum, and long-lived particles motivate displaced vertices, disappearing tracks or anomalous [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md). No single selection covers all these cases.

The difficult issue is background inference. Invisible $Z$ decay with jets is a real missing-momentum background: a photon-plus-jets sample or reconstructed $Z$ decays to charged [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) constrains its normalization and recoil shape through modeled transfer factors. A single-lepton [collider control region](../../../../../collider-control-region.md) constrains $W$ and top backgrounds whose charged [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) is missed in the [collider signal region](../../../../../collider-signal-region.md). Jet mismeasurement in ordinary multijet events gives instrumental missing momentum; angular cuts, detector-quality checks and control samples test that tail. Validation regions check these extrapolations without tuning on the final signal count.

[detector acceptance](../../../../../detector-acceptance.md), trigger efficiency, jet [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md), [collider luminosity](../../../../../collider-luminosity.md), theoretical production rates and transfer factors enter a likelihood as [nuisance parameters](../../../../../nuisance-parameter.md). One compares the observed signal-region distribution with background-only and signal-plus-background hypotheses, using a [profile likelihood](../../../../../profile-likelihood.md) and reporting exclusions for specified masses, [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) and spectra. As a pre-examination example, the 2014 ATLAS zero-lepton jets-plus-missing-momentum search used $20.3\ \mathrm{fb^{-1}}$ at $8\ \mathrm{TeV}$ and found no significant excess. Its interpretation depended on [R-parity](../../../../../r-parity.md) conservation and a [neutralino](../../../../../neutralino.md) lightest state; different simplified models gave different [mass](../../../../../mass.md) exclusions.[Sections 4–8, event selection, background control, statistical analysis and model-dependent interpretation](../../../../../search-for-squarks-and-gluinos-with-the-atlas-detector-in-final-states-with-jets-and-missing-transverse-momentum-using-8-tev-proton-proton-collision-data-by-the-atlas-collaboration.md)

**A null search excludes the tested parameter region, not [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) as a whole.** Conversely, a missing-momentum excess alone would not establish [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md): one would need consistent [masses](../../../../../mass.md), decay chains, couplings and eventually [spin](../../../../../spin.md) information. A stable particle on detector flight-time scales is not thereby proved stable for the age of the universe.
