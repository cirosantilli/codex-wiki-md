# Polariton

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polariton)

A [polariton](polariton.md) is a hybrid light–matter excitation formed by strong coupling between a [photon](photon.md) and a material excitation. For an exciton-polariton, the material component is an [exciton](exciton.md).
