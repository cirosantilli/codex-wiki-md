# Uniform-value multi-prize all-pay effort formula

↑ **Parent:** [Expected effort in a rank-order contest](expected-effort-in-a-rank-order-contest.md)

With $n$ independent uniform types, $m<n$ equal prizes of scale $w(m/n)$ and unit effort costs, a symmetric [Bayesian Nash equilibrium](bayesian-nash-equilibrium.md) has total expected effort $R_m=m(n-m)w(m/n)/(n+1)$. The [all-pay effort identity](all-pay-effort-identity.md) yields $b(v)=w(m/n)\int_0^v t q_m'(t)dt$. The allocation derivative is a [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) density with parameters $n-m,m$, so integration gives the formula.
