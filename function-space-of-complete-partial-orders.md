# Function space of complete partial orders

↑ **Parent:** [Pointed complete partial order](pointed-complete-partial-order.md)

The [Scott continuous maps](scott-continuous-map.md) from $D$ to $E$, ordered pointwise. A nonempty directed family has pointwise supremum, and the constant-bottom function is least. Joint evaluation is Scott continuous: for a directed family of pairs $(f_i,x_i)$, directedness makes diagonal values cofinal among all $f_i(x_j)$. Evaluation and [currying](currying.md) make the category of pointed complete partial orders and non-strict Scott continuous maps a [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md).
