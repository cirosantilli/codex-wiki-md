# Dual integer-height representation of the planar Villain model

↑ **Parent:** [Integer-current representation of the Villain model](integer-current-representation-of-the-villain-model.md)

On a finite simply connected planar graph, fix the height of the exterior face to zero. Every divergence-free integer bond current is uniquely the oriented difference of the two neighboring face heights. The positive [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md) $L$ on the remaining faces gives its energy. Applying the [Poisson summation formula](poisson-summation-formula.md) to these heights and completing the square gives

$$
Z_V=Z_{\rm sw}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z^{\rm faces}}e^{-2\pi^2\beta q^TL^{-1}q}.
$$

The Gaussian prefactor $Z_{\rm sw}$ equals the [spin wave](spin-wave.md) determinant of the original graph, up to angle zero-mode normalization. One way to verify this is that the [matrix-tree theorem](kirchhoff-s-theorem.md) equates the spanning-tree counts of a planar graph and its dual. Boundaries can admit nonzero total vortex charge; neutrality is an additional periodic or finite-energy condition.
