# Primitive elements of a completed rational Iwasawa algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lazard logarithm of a p-saturated group](lazard-logarithm-of-a-p-saturated-group.md)

If $(g_1,\ldots,g_d)$ is an ordered basis of a finite-rank p-saturated group, then

$$
P(\widehat{\mathbb Q_pG})
=\bigoplus_{i=1}^d\mathbb Q_p\log(g_i),
$$

where a primitive element $x$ satisfies $\Delta(x)=x\otimes1+1\otimes x$ for the completed [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) coproduct.
