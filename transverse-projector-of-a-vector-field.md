# Transverse projector of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson propagator](gauge-boson-propagator.md)

At a nonzero Euclidean [momentum](momentum.md) $k$, the [transverse projector of a vector field](transverse-projector-of-a-vector-field.md) is $P_T^{ab}=\delta^{ab}-k^ak^b/k^2$. In a Lorentzian [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), its mixed-index form is $(P_T)^a{}_b=\delta^a_b-p^ap_b/p^2$, defined for $p^2\ne0$. It obeys $P_Tp=0$ and $P_T^2=P_T$. A nonzero null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is insufficient for this particular decomposition because the denominator vanishes.
