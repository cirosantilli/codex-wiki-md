# Full-film journal bearing

↑ **Parent:** [Journal bearing](journal-bearing.md)

A full-film journal bearing model assumes that liquid fills the entire annular gap and that [pressure](pressure.md) is single-valued around a complete circumference. No [cavitation](cavitation.md) boundary is imposed. The [full-film eccentric journal-bearing rotation](full-film-eccentric-journal-bearing-rotation.md) and [full-film journal-bearing whirl](full-film-journal-bearing-whirl.md) formulas use this assumption; their predicted motion depends on keeping it.
