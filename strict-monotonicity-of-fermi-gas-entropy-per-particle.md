# Strict monotonicity of Fermi gas entropy per particle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

For [density of states](density-of-states.md) proportional to $\epsilon^a$, $a>-1$, and positive temperature, set $q=\mu/(kT)$, $n(x)=(1+e^{x-q})^{-1}$, $b=a+1$ and $M_j=\int_0^\infty x^{a+j}n(1-n)\,dx$. Differentiation and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
\frac d{dq}\frac S{kN}=b\left(1-\frac{M_0M_2}{M_1^2}\right)<0.
$$

The inequality is strict [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), since $x$ is not constant under this positive weight. Thus fixed entropy per particle forces fixed $\mu/T$. This is the injectivity step behind the adiabatic scaling of an ideal [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md).
