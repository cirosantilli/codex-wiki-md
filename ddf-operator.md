# DDF operator

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant quantization of the bosonic string](covariant-quantization-of-the-bosonic-string.md)

DDF operators are transverse physical [string oscillators](string-oscillator.md) constructed as contour integrals of weight-one [string vertex operators](string-vertex-operator.md). Choose a null reference momentum $q$ and transverse polarizations $\epsilon_i$ orthogonal to it and to a reference on-shell momentum. The integrand is proportional to $\epsilon_i\cdot\partial X\,e^{inq\cdot X}$; its normalization and $q\cdot p$ are fixed so the operators obey the oscillator algebra in the title and commute with the positive-mode [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md). For an open-string tachyon seed, one can use $2\alpha' q\cdot p=1$. Acting with negative modes shifts momentum and raises oscillator level while preserving the physical mass-shell condition. Their transverse [Fock space](fock-space.md) has positive norm and makes the physical content of the [no-ghost theorem for the critical bosonic string](no-ghost-theorem-for-the-critical-bosonic-string.md) explicit.
