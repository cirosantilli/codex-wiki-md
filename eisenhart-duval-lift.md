# Eisenhart-Duval lift

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

A mechanical Lagrangian quadratic in velocities can be represented by [geodesics](geodesic.md) of a [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md) with an extra null coordinate. For $L=\tfrac12|\dot{\mathbf x}|^2-2\mathbf A\cdot\dot{\mathbf x}-U$, use $g=d\mathbf x^2+2dt(du-2\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf x-Udt)$. The null [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $\partial_u$ gives the conserved momentum $p_u=t\prime$. Fixing $p_u=1$ projects the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) to the mechanical [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md). This is useful for relating mechanical forces to spacetime geometry.
