# Bacillus virus phi29

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacillus_virus_phi29)

A double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) of [Bacillus subtilis](bacillus-subtilis.md), widely discussed as a model of active genome packaging. A portal-associated [molecular motor](molecular-motor.md) uses [ATP hydrolysis](atp-hydrolysis.md) to oppose the physical costs of dense [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) confinement.[Introduction identifies the host and the packaging motor](experimental-test-of-connector-rotation-during-dna-packaging-into-bacteriophage-phi29-capsids-by-thorsten-hugel-and-others.md)
