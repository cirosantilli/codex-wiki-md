# Unit equation over a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md)

For fixed nonzero $\alpha,\beta$ in a [number field](number-field.md), this equation has finitely many unit solutions. By the [Dirichlet unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md), a large unit-exponent vector makes one unit exponentially small at some archimedean embedding. The equation makes the other fixed multiple exponentially close to one. Its principal logarithm is a nonzero linear form in fixed algebraic logarithms, with coefficients bounded by the exponent size; a polynomial lower bound contradicts the exponential upper bound. Finite torsion and finitely many embeddings allow uniform constants.
