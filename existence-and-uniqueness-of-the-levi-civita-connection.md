# Existence and uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection

↑ **Parent:** [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md)

The [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md) uniquely determines $g(\nabla_XY,Z)$ for all [vector fields](vector-field.md) $X,Y,Z$, so nondegeneracy of the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) determines $\nabla_XY$. The resulting operation is $C^\infty$-linear in $X$, obeys the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) in $Y$, is compatible with the metric, and is [torsion-free](torsion-free-connection.md). It is therefore the unique [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md).
