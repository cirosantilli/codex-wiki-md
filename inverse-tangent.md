# Inverse tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse trigonometric functions](inverse-trigonometric-functions.md)

The inverse of the [tangent](tangent.md) restricted to $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$, taking real values in that interval. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is $(1+x^2)^{-1}$. A formula involving an inverse tangent of a ratio may need a continuous [branch of a multivalued function](branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) to describe a smooth field through a pole of that ratio.
