# Negative curves of a big real divisor lie in finitely many divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Big real divisor](big-real-divisor.md)

Write a big real [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) on an integral [projective variety](projective-variety.md) as $D\sim_{\mathbb R}B+E$, with $B$ [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) and $E$ effective real Cartier. Every curve outside $\operatorname{Supp}E$ has $D\cdot C=B\cdot C+E\cdot C>0$. Therefore curves negative against $D$ lie in finitely many support components of codimension one. On a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md) use [componentwise bigness on a projective scheme](componentwise-bigness-on-a-projective-scheme.md) and collect the supports on its finitely many integral components; codimension one is taken in the relevant component.
