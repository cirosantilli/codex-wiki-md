# Frequency-dependent transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous mixing](homogeneous-mixing.md)

For [frequency-dependent transmission](frequency-dependent-transmission.md) with [homogeneous mixing](homogeneous-mixing.md), each member of the [susceptible compartment](susceptible-compartment.md) experiences [force of infection](force-of-infection.md) $\lambda=\beta I/N=\beta i$. Consequently the total flow into the [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md) is $\beta SI/N$ people per unit time. The constant $\beta$ combines the contact frequency and the chance that a relevant contact causes [infection](infection.md), and has units of inverse time. Holding the [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md)'s [fraction](fraction.md) fixed holds each person's [force of infection](force-of-infection.md) fixed when $N$ changes. An unnormalized [mass-action infection](mass-action-infection.md) convention instead writes the total flow as $bSI$, where $b=\beta/N$ has units of inverse person-time; these two coefficients must not be confused.
