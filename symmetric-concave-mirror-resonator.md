# Symmetric concave-mirror resonator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical cavity](optical-cavity.md)

Two identical concave mirrors of curvature radius R separated by L have half-round-trip ray matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&L\\-2/R&1-2L/R\end{pmatrix}$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is one and its trace is $2(1-L/R)$. The [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are a conjugate unit-modulus pair for the displayed interval. At the confocal point $L=R$, $A^2=-I$, so it is bounded despite the full-round-trip trace reaching minus two. Matching a [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)'s mirror wavefront gives $z_R^2=(L/2)(R-L/2)$ and $\pi w_0^2=\lambda z_R$. The endpoints are degenerate limiting geometries, not finite confined Gaussian modes.
