# Arterial reservoir

↑ **Parent:** [Artery](artery.md)

A lumped [arterial reservoir](arterial-reservoir.md) stores [blood](blood.md) by elastic expansion. Its [vascular compliance](vascular-compliance.md) relates volume and [pressure](pressure-split.md) changes, while [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $C_a\dot p_a=Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}}$. This storage smooths intermittent ventricular input into continuing peripheral flow.
