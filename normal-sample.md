# Normal sample

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical sample](statistical-sample.md)

A collection of independent identically distributed [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md). Orthogonal coordinates in the standardized [Gaussian vector](gaussian-random-vector.md) separate the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) from the residual sum of squares, proving their independence and giving the normal and [chi-squared distributions](chi-squared-distribution.md) used for exact inference.
