# Semiquinonate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Dioxolene ligand](dioxolene-ligand.md)

A [free radical](radical-chemistry.md) monoanion intermediate in redox state between a quinone and a catecholate; an isolated ligand has [spin](spin.md) $S=1/2$. Its [spin](spin.md) may couple to a [metal](metal.md) [spin](spin.md) in a complex.
