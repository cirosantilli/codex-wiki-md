# Hyperbolic metric on the punctured disk

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md)

The [universal covering map](universal-cover.md) $\tau\mapsto e^{i\tau}$ from the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) to the punctured [unit disc](unit-disc.md) pulls back the metric $|dz|/(|z|\log(1/|z|))$ to $|d\tau|/\operatorname{Im}\tau$. This is its complete curvature-minus-one [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md), which is different from restricting the ordinary disk metric to the punctured disk.
