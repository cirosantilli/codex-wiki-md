# Real-virtual infrared cancellation in inclusive QCD

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

For an [infrared-safe observable](infrared-safe-observable.md), loop corrections and integrated unresolved real emissions have compensating [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md) and [collinear divergences](collinear-divergence.md). Keep a common regulator until they are added. At the first QCD correction to photon-mediated quark production, virtual gluon exchange corrects the two-parton state and real emission supplies the three-parton state. Their inclusive sum is finite after ultraviolet renormalization; resolved three-jet rates additionally depend on an infrared-safe resolution prescription.
