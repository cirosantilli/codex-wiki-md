# Partially directed self-avoiding walk

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)

On the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), a [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) using north, east and west steps but no south steps. At each horizontal height, self-avoidance forces one monotone horizontal run. Conversely, such runs separated by north steps always give [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md), because every new run is at a previously unvisited height.
