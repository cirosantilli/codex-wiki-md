# De Boor point

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

A [de Boor point](de-boor-point.md) is a [control point](control-point.md) in a [B-spline](b-spline.md) representation. For a degree-two uniform segment with three active [de Boor points](de-boor-point.md) $D_0,D_1,D_2$, the equivalent [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md) handles are $(D_0+D_1)/2,D_1,(D_1+D_2)/2$. The next segment shares $D_1,D_2$ and introduces one new handle. This formula assumes simple equally spaced [spline knots](spline-knot.md); clamped or repeated [spline knots](spline-knot.md) have different endpoint conversions.
