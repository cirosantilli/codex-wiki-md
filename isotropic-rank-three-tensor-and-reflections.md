# Isotropic rank-three tensor and reflections

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

In three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), a rank-three [tensor](tensor.md) invariant under every proper [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) is a scalar multiple of the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md). The distinction between proper [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) and all [orthogonal transformations](orthogonal-transformation.md) matters: $R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}\epsilon_{abc}=(\det R)\epsilon_{ijk}$. A polar [tensor](tensor.md) of odd rank invariant under every [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md) must vanish, since the transformation $R=-I$ changes its sign. A nonzero invariant rank-three object under reflections is instead a [pseudotensor](pseudotensor.md), whose transformation law includes the extra determinant factor.
