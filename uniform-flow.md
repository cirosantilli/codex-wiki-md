# Uniform flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A uniform flow has the same [velocity](velocity.md) at every position. A constant velocity field has zero [divergence](divergence.md) and zero [vorticity](vorticity.md); a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) is $\phi(\mathbf x)=\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf x$. It describes the remote ambient flow in idealized obstacle and [line vortex](line-vortex.md) problems.
