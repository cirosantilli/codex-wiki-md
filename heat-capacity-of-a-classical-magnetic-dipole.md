# Heat capacity of a classical magnetic dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Independent-moment magnetic response](independent-moment-magnetic-response.md)

A freely orientable classical [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) of fixed magnitude $m$ in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $B$ has angular [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z=4\pi\sinh x/x$, where $x=mB/(k_BT)$. Its [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is $U=-mB\coth x+k_BT$, and its fixed-field [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $C_B=k_B[1-x^2/\sinh^2x]$. At low [temperature](temperature-split.md), two locally Cartesian tangent coordinates on the orientation [sphere](sphere.md) each contribute $k_BT/2$ by the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md), giving $C_B\to k_B$. A discrete quantum [spin](spin.md) in a nonzero [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) instead has a [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) and its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) vanishes exponentially at sufficiently low [temperature](temperature-split.md).
