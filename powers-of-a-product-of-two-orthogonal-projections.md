# Powers of a product of two orthogonal projections

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md)

For [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md) $P,Q$ with $\|PQ\|\leq q<1$, the identity $(PQ)^r=PQ(QPQ)^{r-1}$ and $\|QPQ\|=\|PQ\|^2$ imply $\|(PQ)^r\|\leq q^{2r-1}$ for $r\geq1$. When the projections have a common fixed subspace, first remove its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). This estimate is stronger than generic submultiplicativity and is useful for alternating local projection layers.
