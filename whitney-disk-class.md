# Whitney disk class

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard Floer chain complex](heegaard-floer-chain-complex.md)

For intersection generators $\mathbf x,\mathbf y$, a Whitney disk is a map of a disk into the [symmetric product](symmetric-product.md), with its two boundary semicircles mapped to the two product tori and its endpoints mapped to $\mathbf x,\mathbf y$. Its homotopy class belongs to $\pi_2(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)$. Projecting it to the [Heegaard surface](heegaard-surface.md) gives an integral domain. With the surface oriented, the convention is $\partial_\alpha D=\mathbf y-\mathbf x$. The obstruction to a disk is the class $\epsilon(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\in H_1(Y)$ obtained by joining the points along alpha arcs and returning along beta arcs. In the Heegaard Floer setup, its vanishing is equivalent to equality of the generators' [Spin-c structures](spin-c-structure.md); for the rational homology sphere diagrams this is equivalent to $\pi_2(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\ne\varnothing$.
