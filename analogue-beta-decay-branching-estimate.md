# Analogue beta-decay branching estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Isobaric analog state](isobaric-analog-state.md)

Compare an unknown allowed branch of parent $P$ with a reference allowed decay of mean lifetime $\tau_R$ and unit branching fraction. Its [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) is the parent mean lifetime $\tau_P$ times the inferred partial rate. The phase-space ratio $f_X/f_R$ and the total vector-plus-axial strengths $S_X/S_R$ enter separately. Approximating both strengths by their [Fermi transition](fermi-transition.md) contributions and both phase-space integrals by $Q^5$ gives a useful scale, but does not establish a unique branch fraction if the axial strengths are unknown.
