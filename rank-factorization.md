# Rank factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_factorization)

For a [matrix](matrix.md) of [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) $r$, choose an ordered [basis](basis.md) of its [column space](column-space.md) as the columns of $B$. Express every column of $A$ in that basis and put its coordinates in $C$. Then $A=BC$, $B$ has [full column rank](full-column-rank.md) and $C$ has [full row rank](full-row-rank.md). Choosing independent original columns makes the corresponding columns of $C$ the standard basis [vectors](vector.md), directly proving its rank.

The basis choice is not unique: for any invertible $r\times r$ [matrix](matrix.md) $S$, the factors $BS$ and $S^{-1}C$ have the same product. Once $B$ is fixed, its coordinate [matrix](matrix.md) is unique. For rank zero, the empty factors of the displayed shapes represent the zero [matrix](matrix.md).
