# Electron spin g-factor

↑ **Parent:** [Electron magnetic moment](electron-magnetic-moment.md)

The dimensionless coefficient in the [spin](spin.md) part of the [electron magnetic moment](electron-magnetic-moment.md). It is distinct from a [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md), which has units: in the angular convention, $\gamma_e=-g_se/(2m_e)$. It is also distinct from the [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) $g_J$ of a combined atomic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and from a nuclear or hyperfine [g-factor](g-factor-physics.md).

For [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the largest magnitude of a spin-component [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is $g_s\mu_B/2$, not $g_s\mu_B$. Thus the ideal value $g_s=2$ gives one [Bohr magneton](bohr-magneton.md). This follows directly from the [spin angular momentum](spin.md) projections $S_z=\pm\hbar/2$.
