# Cholesteric liquid crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)

A cholesteric liquid crystal is a chiral [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md) whose local [nematic director](nematic-director.md) rotates helically in space. The pitch is the distance over which the director completes a full turn. It retains locally nematic orientational order without smectic density layering; the helical modulation should not be mistaken for a layer lattice.
