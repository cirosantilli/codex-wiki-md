# Narain moduli space

↑ **Parent:** [Modulus of a string compactification](modulus-of-a-string-compactification.md)

For a toroidally compactified [heterotic string](heterotic-string.md), the torus metric, antisymmetric tensor and gauge [Wilson lines](wilson-line.md) determine a positive $d$-plane in a charge space of signature $(d,d+16)$, modulo lattice identifications. The continuous dimension is $d(d+16)$; the coupling is an additional modulus. The three-torus case has fifty-seven shape and Wilson-line parameters.
