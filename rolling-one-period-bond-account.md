# Rolling one-period bond account

↑ **Parent:** [Bank account](bank-account.md)

Reinvest each maturity payment in the next unit-principal [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md). Positive bond prices make the resulting [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) strictly positive, so it can be used as a [numéraire](numeraire.md). Its one-step growth is the reciprocal of the current one-period bond price.
