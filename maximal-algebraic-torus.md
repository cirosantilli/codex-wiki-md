# Maximal algebraic torus

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md)

A maximal algebraic torus is an [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md) maximal among the tori contained in an [algebraic group](algebraic-group.md). In a connected [reductive algebraic group](reductive-group.md), all maximal algebraic tori are conjugate. A [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) contains maximal algebraic tori, and its [unipotent radical](unipotent-radical.md) gives a decomposition $B=T\ltimes U$.
