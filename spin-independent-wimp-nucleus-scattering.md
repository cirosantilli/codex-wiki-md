# Spin-independent WIMP-nucleus scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

An elastic recoil interaction whose leading coherent nuclear amplitude does not depend on nuclear spin. At low [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer, [proton](proton.md) and neutron amplitudes add; equal couplings give an $A$-fold amplitude and the displayed cross-section scaling, with reduced [masses](mass.md) $\mu_A$ and $\mu_p$. A finite-momentum nuclear form factor reduces coherence. Unequal [nucleon](nucleon.md) couplings or other interactions change how experiments on different targets should be compared.
