# Nuclear rotational inertia relative to a rigid sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear rotational band](nuclear-rotational-band.md)

The excitation [energy](energy-split.md) in an ideal $K=0$ [nuclear rotational band](nuclear-rotational-band.md) determines an effective [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) through the displayed equation. Comparing it with $2MR^2/5$ tests the rigid-sphere scale. A sequence of approximately constant $E_J/[J(J+1)]$ supports the rotational interpretation; systematic variation indicates that the effective inertia changes along the band. Collective rotation need not have a rigid-body inertia, notably when [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) is important.
