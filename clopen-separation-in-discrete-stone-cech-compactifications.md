<h1 id="clopen-separation-in-discrete-stone-cech-compactifications">Clopen separation in discrete Stone-Čech compactifications</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md)

In the [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md) of a [discrete space](discrete-space.md), the closure of each subset of $X$ is [clopen](clopen-set.md), because its indicator extends to a continuous zero-one-valued function. For disjoint closed sets $A,B\subseteq\beta X$, choose $u=0$ on $A$ and $u=1$ on $B$ by [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md). Then $U=\overline{\{x\in X:u(x)<1/2\}}$ and $V=\beta X\setminus U$ are disjoint clopen sets containing $A$ and $B$, respectively. Density proves these containments.
