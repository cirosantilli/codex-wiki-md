# Easy lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Unsolvable lambda term](unsolvable-lambda-term.md)

A closed [lambda term](lambda-term.md) $E$ is easy if adjoining $E=M$ to the equations of [beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md) remains nontrivial for every closed [lambda term](lambda-term.md) $M$. If $E$ is easy, then $ET$ is easy for every closed $T$: use the consistent equation $E=\lambda x.M$ to obtain $ET=M$. Since $I$ is not easy and solvability produces $I$ by applications, easy terms are unsolvable. The converse need not hold.
