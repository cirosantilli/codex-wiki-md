# Structure formation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_formation)

[Structure formation](structure-formation.md) is the growth of cosmological matter inhomogeneities into structures such as [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) through [gravitational instability](gravitational-instability.md) and gas dynamics. [Linear cosmological density perturbations](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) describe the early, small-amplitude regime; nonlinear evolution is required once the [density contrast](density-contrast.md) becomes large.
