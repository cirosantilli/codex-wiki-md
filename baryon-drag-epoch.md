# Baryon drag epoch

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon acoustic oscillation](baryon-acoustic-oscillation.md)

The epoch at which momentum exchange with photons ceases to drag [baryons](baryon.md) efficiently. The [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md) at this epoch calibrates the late-time [baryon acoustic oscillation](baryon-acoustic-oscillation.md) ruler. It is related to, but not identical with, photon last scattering.
