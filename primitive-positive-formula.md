# Primitive positive formula

↑ **Parent:** [First-order formula](first-order-formula.md)

A primitive positive formula is generated from [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md) using only [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) and [existential quantification](existential-quantification.md). It can be put in the form $\exists\bar y\,\bigwedge_j\theta_j(\bar x,\bar y)$ with each $\theta_j$ atomic. The exam terminology tame formula refers to this fragment. Its [reduced product](reduced-product.md) transfer needs only filter intersection and upward closure, together with coordinatewise choices of witnesses.
