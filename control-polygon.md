# Control polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Control point](control-point.md)

A [control polygon](control-polygon.md) joins successive [control points](control-point.md) by [line segments](line-segment.md). It gives an editable geometric skeleton, not usually the exact curve. Positive partition-of-unity basis functions place the curve inside the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of its active [control points](control-point.md); subdivision can refine this polygon to approximate the limit curve with a certified error.
