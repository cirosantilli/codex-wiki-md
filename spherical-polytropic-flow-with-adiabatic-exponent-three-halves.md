# Spherical polytropic flow with adiabatic exponent three halves

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

For steady spherical [isentropic flow](isentropic-flow.md) with $p=K\rho^{3/2}$ around a point mass, put $j=r^2\rho v>0$ and $\mathcal E=v^2/2+2c^2-GM/r>0$. Eliminating the [mass density](density.md) gives $c^2=[j(3K/2)^2]^{2/5}r^{-4/5}\mathcal M^{-2/5}$. With $r_s=GM/(4\mathcal E)$, $x=(r/r_s)^{1/5}$, $y=\mathcal M^{2/5}$ and $\lambda=[j(3K/2)^2]^{2/5}/(2\mathcal E r_s^{4/5})$, the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) reduces to $\lambda F(y)=F(x)$, where $F(t)=t^4+4/t$. Since $F$ has its unique minimum at $t=1$, a regular [sonic point](sonic-point.md) requires $\lambda=1$ and $r=r_s$. One [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) is $y=x$, which has $v=\sqrt{2\mathcal E}$, $\rho\propto r^{-2}$ and $p\propto r^{-3}$. The decreasing branch has inner [free fall](free-fall.md) and an outer nearly static reservoir, giving [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md) for inward [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md). A nonradiative stationary [shock wave](shock-wave.md) keeps $j,\mathcal E,r_s$ fixed but raises $K$, so $\lambda$ increases by the factor $(K_2/K_1)^{4/5}$. The inward transonic solution is the spherical $n=2$, $\gamma=3/2$ specialization of [transonic accretion in a power-law tube](transonic-accretion-in-a-power-law-tube.md).
