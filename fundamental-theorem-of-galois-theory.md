# Fundamental theorem of Galois theory

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_theorem_of_Galois_theory)

For a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) $L/K$, intermediate fields correspond inclusion-reversingly to subgroups of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Normal subgroups correspond to Galois intermediate extensions.
