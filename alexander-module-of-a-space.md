# Alexander module of a space

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cyclic cover associated to an epimorphism](infinite-cyclic-cover-associated-to-an-epimorphism.md)

Given an integral epimorphism $\alpha:\pi_1(X)\to\mathbb Z$, the Alexander module is the first integral [homology](homology-split.md) of the corresponding [infinite cyclic cover associated to an epimorphism](infinite-cyclic-cover-associated-to-an-epimorphism.md), with $t$ acting by its chosen [deck transformation](deck-transformation.md). Its order is the greatest common divisor of maximal presentation [matrix minors](minor-linear-algebra.md) when it is finitely presented and torsion. For the meridional epimorphism of a [knot exterior](knot-exterior.md), this is the [Alexander module of a knot](alexander-module-of-a-knot.md).
