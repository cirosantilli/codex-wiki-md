# Phylogenetic species concept

↑ **Parent:** [Species concept](species-concept.md)

[Phylogenetic species concepts](phylogenetic-species-concept.md) use ancestry and diagnostic inherited differences; versions differ over requiring a [clade](clade.md), an exclusive ancestry pattern at [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md), or a distinguishable character combination. State which criterion is intended. [p. 880, Table 1: distinct monophyletic, genealogical and diagnosable versions of phylogenetic criteria](species-concepts-and-species-delimitation-by-kevin-de-queiroz.md)

For an original operational example, suppose a [phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md) inferred from one [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) separates two sampled [biological populations](population-biology.md). Compare further [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) and additional local samples before interpreting that branch as a [species](species.md) boundary. Otherwise the test may merely identify the particular ancestry of the sampled [alleles](allele.md). Adding more sites from the same [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) improves that locus's evidence without creating an independent ancestry test.
