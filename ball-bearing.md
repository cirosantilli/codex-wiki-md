# Ball bearing

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A [ball bearing](ball-bearing.md) transfers loads through rolling spherical elements between two races. The no-slip conditions at both contacts determine the translation and spin of each ball; these are generally not the same [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md).
