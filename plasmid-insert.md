# Plasmid insert

↑ **Parent:** [Plasmid](plasmid.md)

A [plasmid insert](plasmid-insert.md) is the [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) fragment added to a carrier [plasmid](plasmid.md) during construction. In the [plasmid feature map](plasmid-feature-map.md), its stipulated boundaries are 1000 and 2500, giving 1500 [base pairs](base-pair.md). The label identifies a construction role; it does not establish [gene expression](gene-expression.md) or [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md).
