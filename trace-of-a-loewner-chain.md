# Trace of a Loewner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md)

When the indicated limit exists continuously and the hulls are generated by its past, it defines the trace of the [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md). The hull $K_t$ includes the curve up to time $t$ and any regions it disconnects from infinity. Therefore swallowing a point by $K_t$ is different from visiting that point by the [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md). For [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md), the continuous trace is part of its basic existence theory.
