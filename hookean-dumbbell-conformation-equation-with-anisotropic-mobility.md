# Hookean dumbbell conformation equation with anisotropic mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Hookean dumbbell conformation equation](hookean-dumbbell-conformation-equation.md)

Let $C=\langle\mathbf R\mathbf R^T\rangle$ be the unnormalized [conformation tensor](conformation-tensor.md), $G_{ij}=\partial_j u_i$ the [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md), and $M/\zeta$ a symmetric positive [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md) independent of the individual connector. The connector [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md) has drift $G\mathbf R-(2H/\zeta)M\mathbf R$ and [diffusion](diffusion.md) matrix $(2k_BT/\zeta)M$. Integrating its second moment twice by parts gives the displayed [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md), $C^{\nabla}=D_tC-GC-CG^T$. If $M$ is a polynomial in $C$, the two matrices commute and the relaxation term simplifies to $-(4H/\zeta)M(C-k_BT I/H)$. No [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) assumption is required for this [moment closure](moment-closure.md).
