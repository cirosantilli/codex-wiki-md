# Collapse time of a spherical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh collapse of a spherical cavity](rayleigh-collapse-of-a-spherical-cavity.md)

For a spherical vacuum cavity initially at rest with radius $a$, in an inviscid liquid of constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ and positive constant far-field [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_\infty$, the [Rayleigh equation for an inviscid spherical bubble](rayleigh-equation-for-an-inviscid-spherical-bubble.md) has the [first integral](first-integral.md) $R^3\dot R^2=2p_\infty(a^3-R^3)/(3\rho)$. To obtain it, multiply the radius equation by $2R^2\dot R$ and integrate the resulting exact derivative. Choosing the negative square root and integrating $dt=dR/\dot R$ gives

$$
k=\sqrt{\frac32}\int_0^1\frac{s^{3/2}}{\sqrt{1-s^3}}\,ds.
$$

This [improper integral](improper-integral.md) converges at both endpoints, proving finite-time [Rayleigh collapse of a spherical cavity](rayleigh-collapse-of-a-spherical-cavity.md) in the ideal model. It has numerical value approximately $0.914681$. The prediction neglects [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md), [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) and liquid compressibility.
