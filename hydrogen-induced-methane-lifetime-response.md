# Hydrogen-induced methane lifetime response

↑ **Parent:** [Tropospheric OH budget](tropospheric-oh-budget.md)

In a fixed-production [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) box, adding hydrogen by $\Delta[\mathrm H_2]$ changes $K$ to $K+k_{\mathrm H_2+OH}\Delta[\mathrm H_2]$. [Methane](methane.md) lifetime scales as $K/P$ when its [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) reaction coefficient is held fixed. The pre-existing hydrogen contribution is already in $K$ and must not be counted again.
