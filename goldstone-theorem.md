# Goldstone theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldstone_theorem)

In a local [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) obeying [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md), with a positive physical state space, a conserved continuous internal [global symmetry in field theory](global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) and a selected Lorentz-invariant [ground state](ground-state.md), a broken generator requires a massless [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md). For $G\to H$, there are $\dim G-\dim H$ symmetry-required [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) in this setting; additional accidental massless excitations are possible. [Jeffrey Goldstone](jeffrey-goldstone.md)'s institutional biography identifies his work with [Abdus Salam](abdus-salam.md) and [Steven Weinberg](steven-weinberg.md) on the zero-[mass](mass.md) theorem.[Biographical Sketch, paragraph 2: spontaneously broken relativistic field theories and the zero-mass theorem](jeffrey-goldstone-by-mit-department-of-physics.md)

The [Goldstone directions in the scalar mass matrix](goldstone-directions-in-the-scalar-mass-matrix.md) give a short classical proof at a smooth minimum. The quantum argument uses a [conserved current](conserved-current.md) $j^\mu$ and a [scalar](scalar.md) [local operator](lawvere-tierney-topology.md) $O$ whose expectation changes under the broken generator. The [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) for the current–[local operator](lawvere-tierney-topology.md) [correlation function](correlation-function.md) then has a nonzero contact term. After a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md), its divergence remains nonzero as four-[momentum](momentum-split.md) tends to zero. [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md) forces the vector–scalar correlator to have the form $p^\mu F(p^2)$, so $F$ must have a pole at $p^2=0$. The [spectral representation](spectral-decomposition.md) associates that pole with a physical zero-[mass](mass.md) excitation. A properly regulated spatial integral of the [conserved current](conserved-current.md) implements the broken generator; a well-defined global charge acting on the broken [ground state](ground-state.md) cannot simply be assumed.

The hypotheses explain the boundaries of the result. A discrete [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) has no infinitesimal generator and need not give a [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md). A gauge redundancy does not supply the physical global generator assumed here. In nonrelativistic matter the [type-B Goldstone boson](type-b-goldstone-boson.md) shows why one cannot universally count one independent mode per broken generator. Long-wavelength fluctuations can also prevent an assumed broken state from existing, as in the [Mermin-Wagner theorem](mermin-wagner-theorem.md).
