# Goldstone theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldstone_theorem)

In a relativistic quantum field theory, each spontaneously broken generator of a continuous internal symmetry produces a massless scalar excitation. For $G\to H$, the standard setting therefore has $\dim G-\dim H$ Goldstone bosons.
