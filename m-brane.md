# M-brane

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory](m-theory.md)

An [M-brane](m-brane.md) is a [brane](brane.md) in [M-theory](m-theory.md). Its elementary membrane and five-brane carry electric and magnetic charges for the three-form potential of [eleven-dimensional supergravity](eleven-dimensional-supergravity.md). Compactification and wrapping relate them to strings and [D-branes](d-brane.md).
