# Ultrasonic motion sensor

↑ **Parent:** [Damping measurement from free decay](damping-measurement-from-free-decay.md)

An [ultrasonic motion sensor](ultrasonic-motion-sensor.md) estimates distance from the return delay of an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) pulse reflected by a target. The teaching setup places a flat flag on the cart facing the detector above the [air track](air-track.md), and specifies a minimum range of about $0.3\,\mathrm m$ for its detector. That apparatus-specific limit should not be assumed for every sensor model. [Motion Sensor Setup](motion-sensor-setup.md)
