# Circular birefringence

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

[Circular birefringence](circular-birefringence.md) means different [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) for opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md). In a transparent isotropic medium, their relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md) changes rotate the direction of transmitted [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md); [optical activity](optical-activity.md) gives the solution measurement and convention.
