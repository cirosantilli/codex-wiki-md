# Power transmission coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Wave impedance](wave-impedance.md)

The ratio of transmitted to incident normal time-averaged [power](power.md) for a specified wave and interface. Converting a field-amplitude transmission coefficient into power requires the appropriate impedances or flux normalization. For a displacement wave on a string with impedance $Z$, $\mathcal T=(Z_2/Z_1)|t|^2$; using an electric-field amplitude in electromagnetic waves instead gives the corresponding electromagnetic flux ratio. Amplitude squared alone is sufficient only when the relevant incident and transmitted normalizations agree.
