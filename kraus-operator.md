# Kraus operator

↑ **Parent:** [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md)

The operators $K_j$ in a [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md) are Kraus operators. For a measurement outcome $i$, its associated Kraus operators determine both the outcome probability and the conditional post-measurement state.
