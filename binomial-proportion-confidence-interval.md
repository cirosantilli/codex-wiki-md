# Binomial proportion confidence interval

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence interval](confidence-interval.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_proportion_confidence_interval)

A [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) for the success [probability](probability.md) of a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md), based on the observed success count and number of trials. A nominal confidence level need not equal the actual finite-sample [coverage probability](coverage-probability.md); the distinction matters especially for small samples and proportions near zero or one.
