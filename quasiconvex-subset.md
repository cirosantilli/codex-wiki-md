# Quasiconvex subset

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)

A subset $Y$ of a [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md) is $K$-quasiconvex when every [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) between points of $Y$ lies in the closed $K$-neighborhood of $Y$.
