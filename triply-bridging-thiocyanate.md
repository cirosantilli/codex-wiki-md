# Triply bridging thiocyanate

↑ **Parent:** [Thiocyanate coordination mode](thiocyanate-coordination-mode.md)

A $\mu_3$ [thiocyanate](thiocyanate.md) connecting three [metal](metal.md) centres. In layered first-row divalent [metal](metal.md) [thiocyanates](thiocyanate.md), its [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) coordinates one [metal](metal.md) and its [sulfur](sulfur.md) coordinates two others, giving an $\mathrm{MN_2S_4}$ octahedral coordination environment.
