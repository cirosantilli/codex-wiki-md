# Recovery of a continuous Brownian integrand from short increments

↑ **Parent:** [Itô integral](ito-integral.md)

For a bounded continuous [adapted process](adapted-process.md) $H$ and a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $B$, $\int_t^{t+h}H_s\,dB_s/(B_{t+h}-B_t)$ converges in [probability](probability.md) to $H_t$ as $h\downarrow0$. The [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) makes the error numerator small relative to $\sqrt h$; the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) of the denominator controls its chance of being too close to zero.
