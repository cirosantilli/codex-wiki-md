# Red-Al

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Red-Al](red-al.md) is sodium bis(2-methoxyethoxy)aluminium hydride. Suitable [propargylic alcohols](propargylic-alcohol.md) undergo [oxygen](oxygen.md)-directed trans [hydroalumination](hydroalumination.md) to cyclic [alkenyl aluminate](alkenyl-aluminate.md) species; aqueous workup gives E allylic alcohols.
