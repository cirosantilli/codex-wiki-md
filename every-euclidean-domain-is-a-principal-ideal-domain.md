# Every Euclidean domain is a principal ideal domain

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean domain](euclidean-domain.md)

Let $R$ be a [Euclidean domain](euclidean-domain.md) with [Euclidean function](euclidean-function.md) $\nu$, and let $I$ be a nonzero [ideal](ideal.md). Choose $b\in I\setminus\{0\}$ minimizing the nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md) $\nu(b)$. For $a\in I$, divide $a=qb+r$ with $r=0$ or $\nu(r)<\nu(b)$. Since $r=a-qb\in I$, the latter case contradicts minimality. Hence every $a$ is a multiple of $b$, giving $I=(b)$. The zero [ideal](ideal.md) is $(0)$, so every ideal is a [principal ideal](principal-ideal.md) and $R$ is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md).
