# Positive root of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md)

A positive root of a real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$ is a real number $r>0$ for which $p(r)=0$. Zero is excluded. A count of distinct positive roots counts each such number once; a count with [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md) counts a root of order $m$ exactly $m$ times. Multiplication by a power of $x$ does not change either positive-root count.
