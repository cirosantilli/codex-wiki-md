# Parallelepiped

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallelepiped)

A [parallelepiped](parallelepiped.md) is a [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) with six [parallelogram](parallelogram.md) faces in three opposite pairs. Three generating [vectors](vector.md) determine its edges; the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of their [determinant](determinant.md) gives its volume in orthonormal Cartesian coordinates.
