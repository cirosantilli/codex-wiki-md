# Magnetic energy spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md)

The shell-integrated magnetic energy spectrum distributes [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) over [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md). With a [solenoidal isotropic spectral tensor](solenoidal-isotropic-spectral-tensor.md) normalized to $\widehat C_{ii}=2H(k)$ and the inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) measure $d^3k/(2\pi)^3$, one has $\mathbb E|\mathbf B|^2=\pi^{-2}\int k^2H(k)dk$. In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), $E_B(k)=k^2H(k)/(8\pi^3)$ integrates to the energy density $\mathbb E|\mathbf B|^2/(8\pi)$. A spectrum per unit mass or normalized to $B^2/2$ has different constant factors. Scale-local fluctuation estimates give $\delta B_l^2$ proportional to $kE_B(k)$ at $k\sim l^{-1}$ when the spectrum is normalized consistently.
