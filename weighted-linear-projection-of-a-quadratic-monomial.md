# Weighted linear projection of a quadratic monomial

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional subspace](orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md)

In the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) $\langle f,g\rangle=\int_0^1xf(x)g(x)\,dx$, the [best approximation](best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md) to $x^2$ in the span of $1,x$ is the displayed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). The [normal equations](normal-equation.md) are $1/4-A/2-B/3=0$ and $1/5-A/3-B/4=0$. Their solution gives $A=-3/10$, $B=6/5$. The residual is orthogonal to that span, so the [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md) proves minimality and uniqueness. Its squared [norm](norm.md) is $1/600$. A weight vanishing at a single endpoint still gives a [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) inner product on continuous functions: a nonzero continuous function remains nonzero on an interval where the weight is positive.
