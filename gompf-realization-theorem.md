# Gompf realization theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

Every finitely presented group is the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a closed symplectic four-manifold. The construction starts from products of surfaces, realizes relator words by embedded loops after stabilization, and takes [symplectic sums](symplectic-sum.md) with copies of the [rational elliptic surface](rational-elliptic-surface.md) along square-zero tori. Since the complement of a regular elliptic fiber in the rational elliptic surface is simply connected, van Kampen's theorem imposes the desired generators and relators.
