# Projective determinacy

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of determinacy](axiom-of-determinacy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_determinacy)

Projective determinacy states that every [infinite game of perfect information](infinite-game-of-perfect-information.md) whose payoff is a [projective set](projective-set.md) is determined.
