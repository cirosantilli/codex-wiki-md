# Mesylate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A [mesylate](mesylate.md) is a methanesulfonate, $\mathrm{ROSO_2Me}$. Converting an alcohol to its [mesylate](mesylate.md) improves leaving-group ability without initially cleaving the original [carbon](carbon.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bond.
