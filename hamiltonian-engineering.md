# Hamiltonian engineering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

[Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md) designs accessible interactions or their effective time average to implement desired [quantum states](quantum-state.md) or [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md). The available [control inputs](control-input.md), [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) generators and laboratory constraints determine which transformations can actually be achieved. Model-based [open-loop control](open-loop-control.md) computes a waveform before running it; adaptive experiments instead evaluate its performance on the physical system.
