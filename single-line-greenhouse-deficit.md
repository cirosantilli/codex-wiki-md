# Single-line greenhouse deficit

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md)

For a black surface thermally coupled to a dry convective atmosphere, define $E(p,q)=\int[1-\mathfrak T_\nu(p)]d\nu$, the line [equivalent width](equivalent-width.md) between pressure $p$ and space. If the line is sufficiently narrow to evaluate the [Planck function](planck-function.md) at its centre, the outgoing flux is $\sigma T_s^4-D$. Integration by parts of the formal [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) solution gives the displayed deficit, nonnegative because temperature and the [Planck function](planck-function.md) increase downward. In the weak-line limit with constant integrated strength, $E\simeq qSp/(g\mu_d)$ and the leading deficit is independent of normalized line shape.
