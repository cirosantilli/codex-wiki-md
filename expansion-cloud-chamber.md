# Expansion cloud chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md)

The central mechanism is a competition between two falling [pressures](pressure-split.md): expansion reduces the actual [water](water.md) [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md), but cooling can reduce the equilibrium [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) still faster. The ratio can therefore rise above one even though the [gas](gas.md) becomes more dilute. The [cloud-chamber expansion model](cloud-chamber-expansion-model.md) below makes that comparison explicit.
