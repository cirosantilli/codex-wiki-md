# Gamma scale inverse-gamma conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate prior](conjugate-prior.md)

For conditionally independent [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) observations of known shape $\alpha$ and unknown scale $\Theta$, an [inverse-gamma distribution](inverse-gamma-distribution.md) prior with shape $k$ and scale $\lambda$ gives an [inverse-gamma distribution](inverse-gamma-distribution.md) posterior with shape $k+n\alpha$ and scale $\lambda+\sum_jx_j$. The [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) of the conditional claim [expected value](expected-value.md) $\alpha\Theta$ is $\alpha(\lambda+\sum_jx_j)/(k+n\alpha-1)$ when the denominator is positive.
