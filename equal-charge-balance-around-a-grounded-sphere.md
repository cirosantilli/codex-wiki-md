# Equal-charge balance around a grounded sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge for a grounded conducting sphere](image-charge-for-a-grounded-conducting-sphere.md)

Place equal positive [point charges](point-charge.md) at distances $d$ on opposite sides of a grounded conducting sphere of radius $a$, and set $x=d/a>1$. Each physical charge has an image $-qa/d$ at distance $a^2/d$ from the centre on its own side. The repulsion from the other physical charge balances attraction to both images precisely when the displayed equation holds. At large $x$, the two sides behave as $1/(4x^2)$ and $2/x^3$, explaining the approximate root $x\simeq8$. A constant nonzero sphere potential adds a central image and changes the force balance.
