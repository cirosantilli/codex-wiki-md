<h1 id="lindelof-hypothesis">Lindelöf hypothesis</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindelöf_hypothesis)

The hypothesis asks for growth smaller than every fixed positive power on the [critical line](critical-line.md). The implied constant may depend on the chosen exponent. The [Riemann hypothesis implies the Lindelöf hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis-implies-the-lindelof-hypothesis.md) through a smoothed explicit formula and strip convexity.
