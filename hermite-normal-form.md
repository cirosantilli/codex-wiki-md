# Hermite normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_normal_form)

The Hermite normal form is a canonical triangular form for an integer [matrix](matrix.md) under integer elementary row operations or, in the column convention, column operations. In the row convention for a nonsingular rank-two [matrix](matrix.md), positive diagonal entries and reduction of the upper-right entry modulo the lower diagonal entry give the [row Hermite normal form in rank two](row-hermite-normal-form-in-rank-two.md).
