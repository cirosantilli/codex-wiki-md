# Logarithmic conjugacy of a repeated-root Euler operator

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Euler equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md)

For $x>0$, write $t=\log x$ and $y=xv(t)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $y'=v+v_t$, $y''=x^{-1}(v_t+v_{tt})$, and therefore

$$
(x^2D^2-xD+1)y=xv_{tt}.
$$

On $1<x<e^\ell$ with homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are $x\sin(n\pi\log x/\ell)$ and the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $-(n\pi/\ell)^2$. The [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) with weight $x^{-3}$ becomes $\int_0^\ell\overline v w\,dt$, so their squared [norms](norm.md) are $\ell/2$ and their [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) is exactly that of the [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md). The weight is the factor that makes this conjugacy preserve the [inner product](inner-product-split.md).
