# Fusion closure of transparent anyons

↑ **Parent:** [Transparent anyon](transparent-anyon.md)

For [transparent anyons](transparent-anyon.md) $a,b$, the [hexagon identities for braiding](hexagon-identity-for-braiding.md) for [braiding](braiding.md) express double exchange of $a\otimes b$ with any charge $x$ as the successive double exchanges of $a$ and $b$ with $x$. Both are identities. Naturality restricts the resulting identity to every simple [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md) summand $c$ of $a\otimes b$, proving that $c$ is transparent.
