# Airy wave theory

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airy_wave_theory)

[Airy wave theory](airy-wave-theory.md) linearizes the motion of small-amplitude [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md) in an inviscid, incompressible, irrotational fluid. At depth $h$, its dispersion relation is $\omega^2=gk\tanh(kh)$; deep water is the limit $kh\to\infty$.
