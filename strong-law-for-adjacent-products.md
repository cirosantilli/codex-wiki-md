# Strong law for adjacent products

↑ **Parent:** [Strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md)

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_j$ with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$ and $m=\mathbb EX_1$, the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) of $X_jX_{j+1}$ tends to $m^2$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Split into odd and even indexed products; each subsequence consists of [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) that are [integrable random variables](integrable-random-variable.md). Apply the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) separately.
