# Potential and Hubble slow-roll parameter relation

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble slow-roll parameter](hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md)

For a canonical [inflaton](inflaton.md) under the [slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md), $\dot\phi/H\simeq-M_{\rm Pl}^2V'/V$ and $\epsilon\simeq\epsilon_V=(M_{\rm Pl}^2/2)(V'/V)^2$. Differentiating $\log\epsilon_V$ along this trajectory gives

$$
\eta\simeq2\left(\frac{V''}{V'}-\frac{V'}V\right)\frac{\dot\phi}{H}=4\epsilon_V-2\eta_V.
$$

Here $\eta=d\log\epsilon/d\log a$, not the alternate convention $-\ddot\phi/(H\dot\phi)$. The logarithmic definition requires $\epsilon>0$; exact de Sitter expansion is treated as a limit.
