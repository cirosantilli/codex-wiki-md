# Velocity kick on a circular Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

For an instantaneous in-plane change of [velocity](velocity.md) of magnitude $\gamma v_K$ on a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md), with angle $\theta$ measured from the forward tangential direction, the new [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) gives $a'/a=(1-2\gamma\cos\theta-\gamma^2)^{-1}$. The [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) gives $e'^2=1-(1-2\gamma\cos\theta-\gamma^2)(1+\gamma\cos\theta)^2$. The signed [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) becomes negative for an unbound [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md).
