# Lagrange remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder](taylor-theorem-with-lagrange-remainder.md)

The [Taylor remainder](taylor-remainder.md) in this form evaluates one higher [derivative](derivative.md) at an intermediate point $\xi$ between the expansion point $a$ and $x$. If $|f^{(n+1)}|\leq M$ on that interval, then $|R_n(x)|\leq M|x-a|^{n+1}/(n+1)!$. This bounds truncation error without knowing the particular intermediate point. The differentiability hypotheses are those of the [Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder](taylor-theorem-with-lagrange-remainder.md).
