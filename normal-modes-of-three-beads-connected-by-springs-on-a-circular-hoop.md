# Normal modes of three beads connected by springs on a circular hoop

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

Consider three beads of [masses](mass.md) $m,m,M$ on a horizontal hoop, connected in cyclic order by identical [springs](spring.md) constrained to follow the arcs. Their common natural length affects only a constant in the [potential energy](potential-energy.md), since the sum of the three arc lengths is fixed. About the equally spaced configuration, angular displacements $q_i$ have

$$
T=\frac{R^2}{2}(m\dot q_1^2+m\dot q_2^2+M\dot q_3^2),\qquad
V-V_0=\frac{kR^2}{2}\big[(q_2-q_1)^2+(q_3-q_2)^2+(q_1-q_3)^2\big].
$$

The [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) has the rigid [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) $(1,1,1)$ as a [zero mode](zero-mode.md). Exchanging the equal [masses](mass.md) separates an antisymmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $(1,-1,0)$, with squared [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $3k/m$, from a symmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $(1,1,-2m/M)$, with squared [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $k/m+2k/M$. The last [mode shape](mode-shape.md) has zero total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The hoop radius cancels from both the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and the restoring quadratic form. These formulas use arc-constrained [springs](spring.md); straight chord [springs](spring.md) have a different [potential energy](potential-energy.md).
