# Deoxyribose

↑ **Parent:** [Sugar](sugar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deoxyribose)

In the usual [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) context, [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md) means 2-deoxy-D-[ribose](ribose.md), a five-[carbon](carbon.md) [monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md) with [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) $\mathrm C_5\mathrm H_{10}\mathrm O_4$. Relative to D-[ribose](ribose.md), the [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) at C2 is replaced by [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). The locant 2 matters: it identifies which position loses its [oxygen](oxygen.md). See [Favre and Powell, IUPAC Blue Book, P-102.5.3: deoxy sugars](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P10.html#P-102.5.3) and [IUPAC: nucleic acids and their component sugars](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/class/nucle.html).

The paired [Fischer projections](fischer-projection.md) in [ribose](ribose.md) make the change concrete. C2 has [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) on both sides, while C3 and C4 retain their [hydroxy groups](hydroxy-group.md) on the right. C2 is therefore not a [stereocenter](stereocenter.md): exchanging its two identical [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) substituents cannot produce a distinct configuration. The unchanged C3 and C4 still carry [stereochemical](stereochemistry-split.md) information.

Replacing $\mathrm{OH}$ by $\mathrm H$ removes one [oxygen](oxygen.md) but leaves the total [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) count unchanged: the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) belonging to the removed [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) is replaced by a [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) bonded directly to [carbon](carbon.md). Thus $\mathrm C_5\mathrm H_{10}\mathrm O_5$ becomes $\mathrm C_5\mathrm H_{10}\mathrm O_4$. This comparison describes structures, rather than a complete [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) or its mechanism.

In [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md) is incorporated as a ring in each [nucleotide](nucleotide.md), with [phosphate](phosphate.md) connections building the backbone. Its C2′ bears [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) where [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md)'s [ribose](ribose.md) bears a [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md). This small local difference lets the [sugar](sugar.md) structure identify the two kinds of [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md); the [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md) supply their sequence information. See [Alberts et al.: nucleotides as the subunits of DNA and RNA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26883/).
