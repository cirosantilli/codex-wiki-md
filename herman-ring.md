# Herman ring

↑ **Parent:** [Fatou component](fatou-component.md)

A Herman ring is a doubly connected periodic [Fatou component](fatou-component.md) on which the first-return map is holomorphically conjugate to an irrational rotation of an annulus $r<|w|<1$. Compactness of the circle of rotation factors proves normality of the return iterates, and the finitely many intervening iterates preserve normality. It is the annular rotation-domain counterpart of a [Siegel disc](siegel-disc.md).
