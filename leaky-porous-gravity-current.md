# Leaky porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Porous gravity current](porous-gravity-current.md)

A leaky porous gravity current loses flux through a localized fracture or a distributed permeable boundary. At a point fracture, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) makes the horizontal Darcy flux discontinuous by exactly the leakage rate.
