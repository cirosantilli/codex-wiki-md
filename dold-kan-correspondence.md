ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dold–Kan_correspondence)

The normalized-chain functor gives an equivalence between [simplicial abelian groups](simplicial-abelian-group.md) and nonnegatively graded [chain complexes](chain-complex.md) of abelian groups. Its inverse is the Eilenberg–MacLane denormalization functor $K$.
