# Sheaf of rings

↑ **Parent:** [Ringed space](ringed-space-split.md)

A sheaf of rings assigns a ring $\mathcal O(U)$ to every open set, restriction homomorphisms to inclusions, and satisfies local identity and gluing. It supplies the local functions on a [ringed space](ringed-space-split.md).

This is a ring-valued [Sheaf (mathematics)](sheaf-mathematics.md), with ring homomorphisms as restriction maps.
