<h1 id="suslin-tree-obstruction-to-martin-s-axiom">Suslin-tree obstruction to Martin's axiom</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Suslin tree](suslin-tree.md)

A [well-pruned set-theoretic tree](well-pruned-set-theoretic-tree.md) that is an [Aronszajn tree](aronszajn-tree.md) and a [Suslin tree](suslin-tree.md) gives a [forcing](forcing-split.md) with the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md): stronger nodes extend weaker ones. The $\omega_1$ [dense subsets of a forcing order](dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md) of nodes at or above each level cannot all be met by a [filter in an ordered set](filter-mathematics.md), since that would produce a [cofinal branch](cofinal-branch.md). Thus $\mathrm{MA}_{\aleph_1}$ fails. When the continuum exceeds $\aleph_1$, full [Martin axiom](martin-s-axiom.md) includes this instance.
