# Photoemission spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoemission_spectroscopy)

[Photoelectron spectroscopy](photoemission-spectroscopy.md) measures the [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) of [photoelectrons](photoelectron.md) produced by illuminating matter with [photons](photon.md) of known [energy](energy-split.md). The [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) supplies the emission mechanism; [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) turns the distribution of emitted [electron](electron.md) energies into information about occupied [energy levels](energy-level.md) and [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md). A broad survey helps identify [chemical elements](chemical-element.md), while small shifts within one element's lines can distinguish different chemical surroundings. These are different questions: finding [carbon](carbon.md) does not establish which [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) its [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) form.

The useful inversion is an [energy](energy-split.md) balance. A known incoming [photon](photon.md) energy pays for removal of an [electron](electron.md) and for its outgoing [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). To apply that balance to a [solid](solid.md), specify the zero of [energy](energy-split.md) and the [work function](work-function.md) of the instrument. [Photoelectron binding energy reference](photoelectron-binding-energy-reference.md) derives the conversion for a conducting sample in electrical contact with its analyzer. A free [molecule](molecule-split.md) instead has an [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) referenced to an [electron](electron.md) at rest in [vacuum](vacuum.md); it need not share a [Fermi level](fermi-level.md) with the analyzer.
