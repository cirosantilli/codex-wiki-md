# Non-colliding gravitational velocity-kick bound

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational scattering angle](gravitational-scattering-angle.md)

For a local two-body hyperbolic encounter with central parameter $\mu$ and allowed centre separation $r_{\min}\geq r_0$, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives equal asymptotic speeds. The [gravitational scattering angle](gravitational-scattering-angle.md) therefore implies

$$
|\Delta\boldsymbol v|=\frac{2v_\infty}{1+r_{\min}v_\infty^2/\mu}
\leq\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r_0}}=\frac{v_{\rm esc}(r_0)}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Put $z=v_\infty\sqrt{r_{\min}/\mu}$ and use $2z/(1+z^2)\leq1$. The optimizing grazing [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) has $z=1$, [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) two and deflection $\pi/3$. For finite spherical bodies $r_0$ is the sum of their radii; excluding actual contact makes the upper bound a supremum. Without a finite exclusion radius the ideal point-mass problem has no finite bound.
