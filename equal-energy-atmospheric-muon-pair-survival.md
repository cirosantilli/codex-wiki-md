# Equal-energy atmospheric muon-pair survival

↑ **Parent:** [Muon lifetime](muon-lifetime.md)

A vertically incident [particle](particle-physics.md) annihilating with a stationary target into two equal-energy [muons](muon.md) gives each half the incident longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md), while their transverse [momenta](momentum-split.md) are opposite. At height $H$ above a locally flat surface, each proper flight time is $2Hm_\mu c^2/[c(p_{\rm in}c)]$, since $\gamma v_z=p_z/m_\mu$. Independent exponential [particle decays](particle-decay.md) then give the displayed joint survival [probability](probability.md). Atmospheric [energy loss](energy-loss.md), scattering and curvature are omitted; replacing both trajectories by vertical paths would miss the two-body angular correction.
