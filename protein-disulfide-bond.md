# Protein disulfide bond

↑ **Parent:** [Protein tertiary structure](protein-tertiary-structure.md)

A [protein disulfide bond](protein-disulfide-bond.md) is a [disulfide bond](disulfide-bond.md) linking two [sulfur](sulfur.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) carried by suitable [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md). It can connect distant portions of a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) or different chains. See [Cooper: sulfur-containing residues and disulfide crosslinks](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9879/). The four internal [protein disulfide bonds](protein-disulfide-bond.md) of [ribonuclease A](ribonuclease-a.md) are central to the [ribonuclease A refolding experiment](ribonuclease-a-refolding-experiment.md); breaking these crosslinks need not break the [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md) along its [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md).
