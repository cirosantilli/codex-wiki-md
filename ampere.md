# Ampere

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampere)

The [ampere](ampere.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [electric current](electric-current.md). Since 20 May 2019 its definition fixes the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) exactly at $e=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, with $\mathrm C=\mathrm{A\,s}$. This gives electrical measurements a reference based on a fixed [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). [NIST, Ampere: introduction to the 2019 definition](https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/ampere-introduction); [NIST, CODATA value of the elementary charge](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Value/e.html).
