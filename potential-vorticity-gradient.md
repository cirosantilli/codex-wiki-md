# Potential-vorticity gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md)

The spatial [gradient](gradient.md) of [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) controls the anomaly created when a parcel is displaced. The linear [potential-vorticity equation](potential-vorticity-evolution-equation.md) contains $\mathbf u'\cdot\nabla\bar q$. A gradient of the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) contributes [planetary vorticity](planetary-vorticity.md), but gradients of relative [vorticity](vorticity.md) and layer stretching can be equally important.
