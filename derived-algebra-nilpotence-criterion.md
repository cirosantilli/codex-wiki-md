# Derived algebra nilpotence criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Derived algebra](derived-algebra.md)

A finite-dimensional complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md) exactly when its [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) is a [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md). The forward direction follows from the [Lie theorem](lie-s-theorem.md) in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) and lifting nilpotence through its central kernel. The reverse direction follows because the [derived series of a Lie algebra](derived-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) after its first term is the [derived series of a Lie algebra](derived-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md).
