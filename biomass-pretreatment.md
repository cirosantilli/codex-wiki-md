# Biomass pretreatment

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)

[biomass pretreatment](biomass-pretreatment.md) changes a feedstock before subsequent conversion, often to improve [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) access. Increased accessibility must be balanced against sugar loss, inhibitor formation, energy cost and altered [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) adsorption.
