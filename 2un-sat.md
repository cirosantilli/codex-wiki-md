# 2UN-SAT

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

Satisfiability of [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) formulas whose [clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) have at most two unnegated variables. It is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md): binary AND/OR and unary NOT gate equivalences in a [Tseitin transformation](tseytin-transformation.md) already satisfy that restriction. [Clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) may contain arbitrarily many negative [literals](boolean-literal.md); the total [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) width is not restricted to two.
