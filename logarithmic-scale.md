# Logarithmic scale

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_scale)

A scale whose coordinate is a [logarithm](logarithm.md) of a positive quantity. Equal ratios occupy equal coordinate intervals; for example, $1,2,4,8$ are equally spaced under the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) because every successive difference is $\ln2$.
