# Rotating-frame magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md)

For a rigid frame with constant [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\Omega$, add the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) and [centrifugal force](centrifugal-force.md) to the inviscid [magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md). A constant [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) and [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md) split the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) into [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) and [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md). This nonrelativistic conducting-fluid approximation neglects the [displacement current](displacement-current.md), net electric-charge force and viscosity. [Differential rotation](differential-rotation.md) belongs to the fluid's [velocity field](velocity-field.md), not to a spatially varying rigid-frame rotation vector. A time-dependent frame requires the additional [Euler force](euler-force.md).
