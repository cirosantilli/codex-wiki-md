# CNO-I cycle

↑ **Parent:** [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md)

The principal [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) follows ${}^{12}\mathrm C(p,\gamma){}^{13}\mathrm N$, [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) to carbon-13, proton capture to nitrogen-14, proton capture to oxygen-15, [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) to nitrogen-15, and ${}^{15}\mathrm N(p,\alpha){}^{12}\mathrm C$. The carbon seed is regenerated. The ordinary bottleneck is ${}^{14}\mathrm N(p,\gamma){}^{15}\mathrm O$, so nitrogen-14 accumulates in cycle equilibrium.
