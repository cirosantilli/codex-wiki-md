# Bedrock

↑ **Parent:** [Rock (geology)](rock-geology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedrock)

Relatively consolidated [rock](rock-geology.md) beneath loose cover. A geophysically defined bedrock interface can be an operational [seismic velocity](seismic-velocity.md) or stiffness boundary and need not coincide with crystalline basement.
