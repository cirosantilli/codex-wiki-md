# Torsion and tilt stiffness of a bonded elastic disk

↑ **Parent:** [Perfectly bonded elastic interface](perfectly-bonded-elastic-interface.md)

A circular elastic layer of thickness $h\ll a$ bonded between rigid faces develops [shear strain](shear-strain.md) $r\phi/h$ under a relative twist $\phi$. Integrating the [shear stress](shear-stress.md) times its moment arm gives $K_V=GJ_p/h$. A small relative tilt produces longitudinal [strain](strain.md) $y\theta/h$; when lateral deformation is constrained, the corresponding normal traction gives $K_H=MI/h$. The [polar second moment of area](polar-second-moment-of-area.md) is $J_p=\pi a^4/2$ and the diametric [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md) is $I=\pi a^4/4$. Edge relaxation limits this thin-layer approximation, especially in a nearly incompressible material.
