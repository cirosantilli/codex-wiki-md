# Graph of a closed one-form is Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

The graph of a one-form $\beta$ in $T^*X$ is Lagrangian exactly when $d\beta=0$. Its intersections with the zero section are the zeros of $\beta$; if $\beta=df$, they are the critical points of $f$.
