# Volume enclosed by a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

The solid ball inside a radius-$a$ [sphere](sphere.md) has [volume](volume.md) $4\pi a^3/3$. One derivation integrates concentric shell area: $V=\int_0^a4\pi r^2dr$. The sphere itself is the boundary surface; its area is $4\pi a^2$, a different dimensional quantity.
