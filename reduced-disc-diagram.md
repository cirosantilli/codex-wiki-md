# Reduced disc diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Disc diagram](disc-diagram.md)

A [disc diagram](disc-diagram.md) is reduced if it has no adjacent pair of two-cells that can be folded together across their common boundary and removed while preserving the outside boundary. A minimum-area diagram is reduced. Among diagrams of minimum area, minimizing the number of edges removes unnecessary tree portions. Internal common arcs in a reduced diagram satisfy the applicable [small cancellation theory](small-cancellation-theory.md) piece bounds.
