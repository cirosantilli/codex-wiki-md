# Integrable partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

An integrable partial differential equation has enough exact structure, such as a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md), infinitely many [conserved quantities](conserved-quantity.md), or an [inverse scattering transform](inverse-scattering-transform.md), to reduce its evolution to auxiliary linear or finite-dimensional data.
