# Orthogonal local velocities in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Relative speed from four-velocity contraction](relative-speed-from-four-velocity-contraction.md)

Compare two observers at one event using a common static [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) outside a Schwarzschild horizon. If one observer moves tangentially at speed $u$ and another radially at speed $w$, their [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) contraction is $\gamma_u\gamma_w c^2$. Their relative speed therefore has the displayed form. The radial component measured by the tangential observer is $w/\gamma_u$, so Euclidean subtraction of the two original three-velocities fails. For a circular [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) and radial fall from rest at infinity at radius $R$, the relative speed is $c\sqrt{3\mu/R}$, where $\mu=GM/c^2$ and $R>3\mu$.
