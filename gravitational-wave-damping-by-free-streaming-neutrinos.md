# Gravitational-wave damping by free-streaming neutrinos

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino tensor anisotropic stress](neutrino-tensor-anisotropic-stress.md)

For either tensor [helicity](helicity.md), [neutrino free streaming](neutrino-free-streaming.md) produces the memory equation

$$
\ddot h+2\mathcal H\dot h+k^2h
=-24\mathcal H^2f_\nu\int_0^\eta
K(k(\eta-\eta'))\dot h(\eta')\,d\eta',
\qquad
f_\nu=\frac{\bar\rho_\nu}{\bar\rho_{\rm tot}}.
$$

The [neutrino tensor free-streaming kernel](neutrino-tensor-free-streaming-kernel.md) encodes the angular propagation of the perturbation. Energy transferred to neutrino directional anisotropy damps the oscillation amplitude of a [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md) after horizon entry. This effect was calculated by [Weinberg](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0306304).
