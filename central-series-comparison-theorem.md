# Central-series comparison theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Central series](central-series.md)

For a length-$r$ [central series](central-series.md) $1=G_0\le\cdots\le G_r=G$, induction from the top gives $\Gamma_{r-i+1}(G)\le G_i$, and induction from the bottom gives $G_i\le Z_i(G)$. Consequently the [lower central series](lower-central-series.md) terminates at $\Gamma_{c+1}=1$ exactly when the [upper central series](upper-central-series.md) reaches $Z_c=G$.
