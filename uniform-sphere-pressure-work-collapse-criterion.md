# Uniform-sphere pressure-work collapse criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

For a uniform [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) sphere, set $\rho=mn$, $p=nk_BT$ and $M=4\pi\rho R^3/3$. Building the sphere by concentric shells gives [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) $W=-3GM^2/(5R)$. During a small homologous contraction, released gravitational [energy](energy-split.md) balances the [work](work.md) against gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) when $3GM^2|dR|/(5R^2)=4\pi R^2p|dR|$, or $GMm/R=5k_BT$. Eliminating $R$ gives the displayed mass. At the same initial [mass density](density.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md), larger masses have stronger gravitational contraction than pressure support in this estimate. Its numerical coefficient belongs to the uniform-sphere pressure-work model; the infinite-medium [Jeans instability](jeans-instability.md) and a [pressure-confined isothermal sphere](pressure-confined-isothermal-sphere.md) use different equilibrium and boundary assumptions.
