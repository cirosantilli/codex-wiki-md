# Bonding trends in second-period single bonds

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

Compact valence orbitals favor strong carbon-carbon bonding, but neighboring lone-pair repulsion can weaken nitrogen-nitrogen bonds. Bond strengths depend on [orbital overlap](orbital-overlap.md), polarity and electronic occupancy together; atom size alone is insufficient.
