<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the negative test [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md), use the same change in the fixed [point charge](../../../../../point-charge.md)'s [electric potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md):

$$
\boxed{W_{-}=(-q)\,[\phi(d)-\phi(2d)]=-\frac{k_eq^2}{2d}=-\frac{q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0d}.}
$$

The external agent extracts [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), since the final [Coulomb potential energy](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) is lower. Again the path must avoid the fixed [point charge](../../../../../point-charge.md), and the [work](../../../../../work.md) done by the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is $-W_{-}$.
