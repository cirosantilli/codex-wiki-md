<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) have independent [interference](../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) patterns. Their [interference fringe](../../../../interference-fringe.md) spacings are

$$
\boxed{\beta_g=\frac{546\times10^{-9}\times0.5}{100\times10^{-6}}=2.730\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad
\beta_b=\frac{437\times10^{-9}\times0.5}{100\times10^{-6}}=2.185\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

Different optical [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) do not maintain a fixed mutual [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) over the detector's integration time, so their [intensities](../../../../intensity-physics.md) add. For narrow equal slits, suppressing the slowly varying [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) envelopes, one may write

$$
I(y)=I_{g,\max}\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\pi y}{\beta_g}\right)+I_{b,\max}\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\pi y}{\beta_b}\right).
$$

The relative line strengths are not supplied; the figure illustrates equal peak [intensities](../../../../intensity-physics.md) rather than inferring a quantitative brightness ratio. At $y=0$ both colours are bright, producing a blue-green mixture. Away from the centre the green and blue [interference fringes](../../../../interference-fringe.md) progressively separate, creating alternating colour and brightness variations; places where one line is dark can still be bright in the other. Neither two-line mixing nor its central maximum is generally white.

Across $-15\,\mathrm{mm}\leq y\leq15\,\mathrm{mm}$, the green maxima occur at $y=2.730m\,\mathrm{mm}$ for $m=-5,\ldots,5$, and the blue maxima at $y=2.185n\,\mathrm{mm}$ for $n=-6,\ldots,6$. Near $y=\pm10.92\,\mathrm{mm}$, the fourth green and fifth blue maxima nearly coincide: $4\beta_g=10.920\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $5\beta_b=10.925\,\mathrm{mm}$. The $5\,\mu\mathrm m$ mismatch shows that these are near coincidences, not exact ones for the given [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md). The plotted central interval includes all these maxima.
