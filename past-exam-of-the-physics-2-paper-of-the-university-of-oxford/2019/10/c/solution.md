<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The passive [RC low-pass filter](../../../../rc-low-pass-filter.md) is simple, inexpensive, requires no power supply, and has no [operational amplifier](../../../../operational-amplifier.md) saturation or finite [bandwidth](../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) limitation. It offers no [voltage gain](../../../../voltage-gain.md); its [output impedance](../../../../output-impedance.md) is finite, and both source [electrical resistance](../../../../electrical-resistance.md) and loading can change the observed [frequency response](../../../../frequency-response.md). Its [resistor](../../../../resistor.md) also produces [thermal noise](../../../../thermal-noise.md).

The active [inverting low-pass amplifier](../../../../inverting-low-pass-amplifier.md) provides a low-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) [voltage gain](../../../../voltage-gain.md) of magnitude five and, within the usable [bandwidth](../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md), a small [output impedance](../../../../output-impedance.md), so it can drive a load with much less change in [frequency response](../../../../frequency-response.md). The input [electrical resistance](../../../../electrical-resistance.md) is approximately $R_1=1\,\mathrm{k}\Omega$ and may itself load the source. It needs a power supply and adds [electrical noise](../../../../electronic-noise.md) and offset; finite [operational amplifier](../../../../operational-amplifier.md) gain, [bandwidth](../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md), [slew rate](../../../../slew-rate.md), available output swing, and output [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) limit the ideal formula. Its inversion must also suit the application. The best choice depends on required [voltage gain](../../../../voltage-gain.md), source and load [electrical impedances](../../../../electrical-impedance.md), and the signal's [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) and [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md).
