<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [electron](../../../../electron.md) follows a semicircle under the transverse [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md). Parallel, oppositely directed incoming and outgoing paths are separated by a diameter, so the orbit radius is $r=1.00\,\mathrm{cm}$. Acceleration from rest through the [electric potential difference](../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ gives [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $m_ev^2/2=eV$, where $e>0$ is the elementary [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md). Equating the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) magnitude to the required [centripetal force](../../../../centripetal-force.md) gives

$$
e v B=\frac{m_ev^2}{r},\qquad
\boxed{B=\frac1r\sqrt{\frac{2m_eV}{e}}\simeq3.372\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm T.}
$$

The [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) must be perpendicular to the plane of the turn, with its sign chosen for the [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s negative [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md). In the illustration the [electron](../../../../electron.md) enters rightwards at the upper endpoint, so a [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) into the page bends it downwards.

The nonrelativistic estimate has $v\simeq0.198c$. If the small [special relativity](../../../../special-relativity-split.md) correction is retained, use $pc=\sqrt{K(K+2m_ec^2)}$ with $K=eV$ and $B=p/(er)$. This gives $B\simeq3.389\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm T$, about $0.49\%$ larger. The leading answer is adequate at the precision implicit in the classical treatment.
