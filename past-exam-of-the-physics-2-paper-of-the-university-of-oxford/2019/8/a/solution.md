<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a circular [coil](../../../../electromagnetic-coil.md), use the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../biot-savart-law.md)

$$
d\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf r}{r^3}.
$$

At an axial position $x$, every wire element is at distance $r=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$. The transverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) contributions cancel by rotational [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). The axial part of $d\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf r$ is $a\,d\ell$, and $\oint d\ell=2\pi a$. Multiplying by the $N$ turns gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B(x)=\frac{\mu_0NIa^2}{2(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}}\,\hat{\mathbf x}.}
$$

The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md)'s sense fixes the axial direction by the right-hand rule.
