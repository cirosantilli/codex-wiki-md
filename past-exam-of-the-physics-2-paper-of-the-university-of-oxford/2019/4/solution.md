<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Let $\theta=\omega t+\theta_0$ be the angle between the [coil](../../../electromagnetic-coil.md)'s area normal and the [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Its [magnetic flux linkage](../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) is $\Lambda=NB\pi r^2\cos\theta$. Consequently [Faraday's law of induction](../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E(t)=-\frac{d\Lambda}{dt}=NB\pi r^2\omega\sin(\omega t+\theta_0).}
$$

The sign refers to the winding orientation used to define the normal. The initial [phase](../../../phase-waves.md) $\theta_0$ depends on the chosen time origin; one can set it to zero when the normal first points along $+x$. The minimum [absolute value](../../../absolute-value.md) is zero, attained at $\theta=m\pi$: the [coil](../../../electromagnetic-coil.md)'s normal is parallel or antiparallel to the $x$-axis, and the plane of the [coil](../../../electromagnetic-coil.md) is perpendicular to that axis. At this orientation the [magnetic flux](../../../magnetic-flux.md) is extremal and its [derivative](../../../derivative.md) with respect to time vanishes.
