<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

A [wirewound resistor](../../../wirewound-resistor.md) uses a metal alloy chosen for stable [electrical resistivity](../../../electrical-resistivity.md). Compared with a typical [carbon composition resistor](../../../carbon-composition-resistor.md), it can offer tighter [resistor tolerance](../../../resistor-tolerance.md), a smaller [temperature coefficient of resistance](../../../temperature-coefficient-of-resistance.md), lower excess [electrical noise](../../../electronic-noise.md), and greater continuous [electrical dissipation](../../../joule-heating.md) when its construction provides an adequate heat path. These are design-dependent advantages, rather than guarantees for every alloy or package.

The winding has parasitic [inductance](../../../inductance.md) and interturn [capacitance](../../../capacitance.md). Its [electrical impedance](../../../electrical-impedance.md) therefore ceases to be a nearly real [electrical resistance](../../../electrical-resistance.md) at sufficiently high [frequencies](../../../frequency.md); an approximate model is a [series circuit](../../../series-circuit.md) $R+i\omega L$ shunted by a small parasitic [capacitance](../../../capacitance.md). A [resonance](../../../resonance.md) can occur, making a [wirewound resistor](../../../wirewound-resistor.md) unsuitable where a nearly frequency-independent [electrical resistance](../../../electrical-resistance.md) is required. Noninductive winding patterns reduce this problem. For comparable small-signal duties it is usually larger and more expensive. A [carbon composition resistor](../../../carbon-composition-resistor.md) is compact and has relatively small parasitic [inductance](../../../inductance.md), but commonly has poorer stability, larger [resistor tolerance](../../../resistor-tolerance.md), and more excess [electrical noise](../../../electronic-noise.md).
