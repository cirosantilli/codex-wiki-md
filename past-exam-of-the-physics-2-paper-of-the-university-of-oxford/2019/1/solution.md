<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Take an [electric current](../../../electric-current.md) $I_1$ in the long [solenoid](../../../solenoid.md). Away from its ends, [Ampère's circuital law](../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives the approximately uniform [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md)

$$
B_1=\mu_0\frac{N_1}{L}I_1.
$$

Here $\mu_0$ is the [vacuum permeability](../../../vacuum-permeability.md); an air core is understood. Each turn of the short surrounding [coil](../../../electromagnetic-coil.md) encloses the [magnetic flux](../../../magnetic-flux.md) $B_1\pi R^2$. Thus its [magnetic flux linkage](../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) is $\Lambda_2=N_2B_1\pi R^2$, and the [mutual inductance](../../../mutual-inductance.md) is

$$
\boxed{M=\frac{\Lambda_2}{I_1}=\mu_0\frac{N_1N_2\pi R^2}{L}.}
$$

The short [coil](../../../electromagnetic-coil.md) lies in the nearly uniform central [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md); its own axial length therefore does not enter. With consistent winding orientations, [Faraday's law of induction](../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives $\mathcal E_2=-M\,dI_1/dt$. Reversing a winding reverses this signed coupling, while the stated magnitude is unchanged.
