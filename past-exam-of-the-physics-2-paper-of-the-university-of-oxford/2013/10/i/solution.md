<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the wire lie along $z$, and observe at perpendicular distance $r$. In the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../biot-savart-law.md), the azimuthal contributions from line elements add with magnitude

$$
B(r)=\frac{\mu_0I_0}{4\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{r\,dz}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{\mu_0I_0}{2\pi r}.
$$

Independently, cylindrical symmetry makes the field constant and azimuthal on a circular [Amperian loop](../../../../amperian-loop.md), so [Ampère's law](../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives $2\pi rB=\mu_0I_0$. Both yield

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0I_0}{2\pi r}\hat{\boldsymbol\phi},}
$$

with direction fixed by the right-hand rule.
