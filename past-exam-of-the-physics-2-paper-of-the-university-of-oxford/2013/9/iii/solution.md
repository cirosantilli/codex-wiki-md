<h1 id="9/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For near-normal viewing through the flat top surface, the refracted ray appears to originate at an apparent depth $h_{\rm app}$. A ray at horizontal offset $x$ has $x=h\tan\theta_w=h_{\rm app}\tan\theta_a$, and [Snell's law](../../../../snell-s-law.md) gives $n\sin\theta_w=\sin\theta_a$. In the paraxial limit,

$$
\boxed{h_{\rm app}=\frac hn=\frac{20\,\mathrm{cm}}{1.33}\simeq15.0\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

At an oblique viewing angle the exact ray-based apparent depth is $h\tan\theta_w/\tan\theta_a$; the simple ratio presumes the usual near-normal top view.
