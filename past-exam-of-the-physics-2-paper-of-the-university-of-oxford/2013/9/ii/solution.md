↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The result need not be the same. [Optical dispersion](../../../../dispersion-optics.md) changes the [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) with wavelength. Under [normal dispersion](../../../../normal-dispersion.md), the blue-light index is larger, changing the refracted rays and generally reducing the visible fraction in the one-interface model. The blue indices of both water and glass are not specified; they may also differ from each other, introducing refraction at their interface. Thus no unique numerical blue-light fraction follows from the red-light value alone.
