<h1 id="8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Ground the non-inverting input and take the ideal [operational amplifier](../../../../operational-amplifier.md) in stable, unsaturated [negative feedback](../../../../negative-feedback.md). Its inverting input is a [virtual ground](../../../../virtual-ground.md) and draws no current. [Kirchhoff's current law](../../../../kirchhoff-node-law.md) there gives

$$
\frac{V_i}{R_i}+\frac{V_o}{R_f}=0,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{V_o}{V_i}=-\frac{R_f}{R_i},\quad |A_v|=\frac{R_f}{R_i}.}
$$

The minus sign is the phase inversion of the [inverting amplifier](../../../../inverting-amplifier.md).
