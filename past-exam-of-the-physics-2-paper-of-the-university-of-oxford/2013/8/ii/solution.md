<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The input source supplies $I_i=V_i/R_i$ into the [virtual ground](../../../../virtual-ground.md), so the complete circuit's input resistance is $R_i$. The ideal output is a zero-resistance controlled voltage source:

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm in}=R_i,\qquad R_{\rm out}=0.}
$$

These are properties of the closed-loop circuit, distinct from the infinite input resistance of the amplifier terminals themselves.
