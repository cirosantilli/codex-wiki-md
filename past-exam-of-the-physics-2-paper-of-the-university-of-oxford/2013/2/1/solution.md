↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is force per unit positive stationary test charge, in the limit that the test charge does not disturb the source:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F_E=q\mathbf E.}
$$

The [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is defined by the velocity-dependent part of the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md), $\mathbf F_B=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$. Equivalently, a straight current element in a uniform field experiences

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=I\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf B,}
$$

which fixes its magnitude and right-hand-rule direction by force measurements with different current orientations.
