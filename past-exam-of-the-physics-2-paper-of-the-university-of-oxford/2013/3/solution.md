<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In classical [Maxwell's equations](../../../maxwell-equations-split.md), electric charge is a source or sink of the [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md), so in vacuum $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho/\epsilon_0$ can be nonzero. There is no magnetic-charge source in the same theory: [Gauss's law for magnetism](../../../gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$. Magnetic flux entering a closed surface equals the flux leaving it. A magnetic dipole has both polarities; it is not an isolated magnetic monopole.
