<h1 id="7/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

In uniform fields, a rigid neutral [electric dipole](../../../../electric-dipole.md) with both charges moving at the selected common velocity has zero net translational force. To leading nonrelativistic order its torque is $\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf p\times(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$, which also vanishes at the velocity-selector condition. Thus it continues undeflected and, initially nonrotating, does not start rotating in the ideal balanced fields. An unbalanced effective field could exert a torque; the dipole orientation is not otherwise specified.
