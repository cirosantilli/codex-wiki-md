<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For one wire, [Ampère's circuital law](../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) on a concentric [circle](../../../circle.md) of radius $r$ gives

$$
2\pi r B(r)=\mu_0I,\qquad B(r)=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}.
$$

The other wire experiences the [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md) on its [electric current](../../../electric-current.md), $d\mathbf F=I\,d\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf B$. Its direction is perpendicular to this [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md), so the [force per unit length](../../../force-per-unit-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac FL=\frac{\mu_0I^2}{2\pi r}.}
$$

The [right-hand rule](../../../right-hand-rule.md) gives attraction for parallel [electric currents](../../../electric-current.md) in the same direction and repulsion for opposite directions.

For the upper wire, the upward magnetic [force per unit length](../../../force-per-unit-length.md) balances its weight per unit length, $\lambda g$, where $g$ is the [gravitational acceleration](../../../gravitational-acceleration.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{r_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{\mu_0I^2}{2\pi\lambda g}.}
$$

This assumes the lower wire is held fixed and both wires remain straight. Within the vertical-motion model, increasing the separation weakens the upward [magnetic force](../../../magnetic-force.md), so this [equilibrium](../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is vertically restoring.
