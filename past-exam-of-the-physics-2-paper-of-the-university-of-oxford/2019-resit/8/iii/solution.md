<h1 id="8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Project [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) down the slope. The normal reaction has no component along the rails, while the magnetic term opposes motion:

$$
\boxed{m\frac{dv}{dt}=mg\sin\phi-IBs\cos\phi
=mg\sin\phi-\frac{B^2s^2\cos^2\phi}{R}v.}
$$

This first-order linear [differential equation](../../../../differential-equation-split.md) has the release-from-rest solution

$$
v(t)=\frac{mgR\sin\phi}{B^2s^2\cos^2\phi}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_m}\right),\qquad
\tau_m=\frac{mR}{B^2s^2\cos^2\phi}.
$$

The electrical [self-inductance](../../../../self-inductance.md) has been neglected; retaining it introduces a coupled [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) equation rather than this instantaneous resistive response.
