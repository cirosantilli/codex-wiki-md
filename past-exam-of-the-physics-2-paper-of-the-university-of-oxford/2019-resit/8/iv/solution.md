<h1 id="8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At the [terminal velocity](../../../../terminal-velocity.md), the acceleration in [iii](../iii.md) vanishes. Thus

$$
\boxed{v_{\mathrm t}=\frac{mgR\sin\phi}{B^2s^2\cos^2\phi}.}
$$

For $0<\phi<\pi/2$ and nonzero $B$, the magnetic drag increases linearly with speed and this terminal state is stable. Horizontal rails have no gravitational drive. Vertical rails have no linked vertical [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md) change, so the model gives free fall rather than a finite [terminal velocity](../../../../terminal-velocity.md).
