<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The phrase “image” does not specify whether a [real image](../../../../real-image.md) or a [virtual image](../../../../virtual-image.md) is required. The later mention of an image plane makes a projected [real image](../../../../real-image.md) a natural interpretation. Let $u>0$ be object distance and $v>0$ real-image distance from a converging [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md). A [transverse magnification](../../../../transverse-magnification.md) of magnitude $15$ gives $v=15u$, and the object-to-image separation gives $u+v=200\,\mathrm{mm}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u=12.5\,\mathrm{mm},\quad v=187.5\,\mathrm{mm},\quad
f=\frac{uv}{u+v}=11.71875\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

Place the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) $12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond the object along the illuminating beam. The [real image](../../../../real-image.md) is inverted and has [transverse magnification](../../../../transverse-magnification.md) $-15$.

For the alternative single-[optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) [magnifying glass](../../../../magnifying-glass.md) interpretation, the [virtual image](../../../../virtual-image.md) is upright and lies on the object's side, so signed $v=-15u$. Its separation from the object is $|v|-u=14u=200\,\mathrm{mm}$. The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation then gives

$$
\boxed{u=\frac{100}{7}\,\mathrm{mm}=14.286\,\mathrm{mm},\quad
v=-\frac{1500}{7}\,\mathrm{mm},\quad
f=\frac{750}{49}\,\mathrm{mm}=15.306\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

The source does not explicitly select between these two geometries; they should not be conflated with a $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ lens-to-image distance.
