<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the principle of [dark-field microscopy](../../../../dark-field-microscopy.md): block the unscattered beam where it is concentrated, while transmitting most [light scattering](../../../../scattering-of-light.md) at nonzero angles. All direct [light rays](../../../../light-ray.md) of the collimated illumination are parallel, so the [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) focuses them to one point in its [back focal plane](../../../../back-focal-plane.md). Put a small opaque circular stop centred on that point, one [focal length](../../../../focal-length.md) behind the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md). In the [real image](../../../../real-image.md) geometry above this is $11.719\,\mathrm{mm}$ behind the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md), well before the [real image](../../../../real-image.md) at $187.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

A [light ray](../../../../light-ray.md) leaving object coordinate $y$ with a small initial direction $\theta$ reaches the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) at $y+u\theta$. The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) changes its direction to $\theta-(y+u\theta)/f$. At the [back focal plane](../../../../back-focal-plane.md) its coordinate is therefore

$$
y_f=y+u\theta+f\left(\theta-\frac{y+u\theta}{f}\right)=f\theta.
$$

Thus direct illumination with $\theta=0$ is concentrated at the centre, whereas scattered [light](../../../../light.md) at different directions is distributed away from it, independently of $y$. A stop of [radius](../../../../radius.md) $r_s$ removes approximately $|\theta|\lesssim r_s/f$; choose it just large enough to cover the direct beam's finite [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) spot, illumination divergence and alignment errors. Scattered [light](../../../../light.md) inside that small angular cone is also lost, so negligible loss requires most useful scattered [light](../../../../light.md) to lie outside it. A stop in the object's [real image](../../../../real-image.md) plane instead removes object detail and does not generally separate scattered and direct [light](../../../../light.md). For the [virtual image](../../../../virtual-image.md) interpretation, the same physical [back focal plane](../../../../back-focal-plane.md) filtering requires a downstream observation arrangement that actually passes through this plane.
