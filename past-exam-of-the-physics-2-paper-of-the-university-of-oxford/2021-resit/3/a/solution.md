<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $U>0$ be the drop in [electric potential](../../../../electric-potential.md) along the positive [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md)'s path. By [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md), $qU=mv^2/2$, so the nonrelativistic exit [speed](../../../../speed.md) is $v=\sqrt{2qU/m}$. Apply a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) perpendicular to the incident [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) and to the desired orbital plane. The magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to the [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), does no [work](../../../../work.md), and supplies the [centripetal force](../../../../centripetal-force.md):

$$
qvB=\frac{mv^2}{R},\qquad
\boxed{R=\frac{mv}{qB}=\frac1B\sqrt{\frac{2U}{q/m}}.}
$$

For example, $\mathbf v=v\widehat{\mathbf x}$ and $\mathbf B=B\widehat{\mathbf z}$ give initial deflection along $-y$ for positive $q$. Reversing the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) reverses the bending. A uniform [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) alone would give constant [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) and a parabolic path, not uniform [circular motion](../../../../circular-motion.md). The displayed result assumes $qU\ll mc^2$; the exact relativistic [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) relation would give $R=\sqrt{q^2U^2/c^2+2mqU}/(qB)$.
