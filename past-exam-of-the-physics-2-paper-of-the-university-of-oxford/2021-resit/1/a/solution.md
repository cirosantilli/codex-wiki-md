<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md), [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) states that the outward [electric flux](../../../../electric-flux.md) through a closed [surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is the enclosed [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) divided by the [vacuum permittivity](../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md):

$$
\oint_S\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A=\frac{Q_{\rm enc}}{\epsilon_0}.
$$

Take opposite [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $\pm Q$ on the two [electrical conductors](../../../../electrical-conductor.md), with [surface charge densities](../../../../surface-charge-density.md) $\pm\sigma=\pm Q/A$. A [Gaussian pillbox](../../../../gaussian-pillbox.md) crossing one isolated large sheet gives $2ES=\sigma S/\epsilon_0$, so each sheet contributes $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ on either side. By the [superposition principle](../../../../superposition-principle.md), the [electric fields](../../../../electric-field-split.md) add between the sheets and cancel outside. Ignoring the [fringing field of a capacitor](../../../../fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md), the gap [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$. The [electric potential difference](../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) and [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md) of this [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md) are consequently

$$
V=\int_0^d E\,dx=\frac{Qd}{\epsilon_0A},\qquad
\boxed{C=\frac QV=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}.}
$$

The approximation requires $d$ much smaller than both transverse sheet dimensions. A uniform filling with [permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon$ replaces $\epsilon_0$ by $\epsilon$.
