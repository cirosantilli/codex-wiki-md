<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use opposite [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $\pm Q$ over a common [length](../../../../length.md) $L$, and write the inner [linear charge density](../../../../linear-charge-density.md) as $\lambda=Q/L$. Away from the ends, [axisymmetry](../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) and [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) on a coaxial cylindrical [Gaussian surface](../../../../gaussian-surface.md) of [length](../../../../length.md) $\ell$ give

$$
E(r)\,2\pi r\ell=\frac{\lambda\ell}{\epsilon_0},\qquad
E(r)=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0r},\qquad a<r<b.
$$

The [electric potential difference](../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) and [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md) of the [coaxial cylindrical capacitor](../../../../coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) are

$$
V=\int_a^bE(r)\,dr=\frac Q{2\pi\epsilon_0L}\log\frac ba,
\qquad\boxed{C=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\log(b/a)}.}
$$

This neglects the [fringing field of a capacitor](../../../../fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) at the ends, as justified when $L\gg b$.
