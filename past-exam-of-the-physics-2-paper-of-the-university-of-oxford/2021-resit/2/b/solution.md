<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The image is upright vertically when viewed down from behind, but left and right are interchanged.** To track the [image formation](../../../../image-formation.md), let $+x$ point forwards and $+z$ upwards. Incoming [light](../../../../light.md) travels along $-x$. The $45^\circ$ flip [plane mirror](../../../../plane-mirror.md) sends it along $+z$. Before this [reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md), the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) has inverted the scene's top into the $-z$ direction; the [law of reflection](../../../../law-of-reflection.md) maps that direction into $+x$ on the horizontal screen. The screen's far edge is the top of the picture for someone looking down from behind. The horizontal inversion from the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) remains, since this [plane mirror](../../../../plane-mirror.md) does not change the lateral coordinate.
