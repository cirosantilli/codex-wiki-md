<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) at $12\,\mathrm{GHz}$ is $\lambda=c/f\simeq0.0250\,\mathrm m$. For an ideal circular [aperture](../../../../aperture.md) of [diameter](../../../../diameter.md) $D=0.850\,\mathrm m$, the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) places one source at the first minimum of the other's [Airy disk](../../../../airy-disk.md):

$$
\boxed{\theta_R=1.22\frac\lambda D=0.03588\,\mathrm{rad}=2.056^\circ.}
$$

The customary full-ring packing estimate for the number of resolvable positions in a [geostationary orbit](../../../../geostationary-orbit.md) is

$$
\boxed{N_{\max}\simeq\left\lfloor\frac{2\pi}{\theta_R}\right\rfloor=175.}
$$

This is an ideal angular-packing estimate. It identifies apparent angular spacing with spacing around the orbital ring and disregards Earth's occultation and station location. An individual ground station cannot see the whole ring; exact conversion from orbital longitude to sky direction requires its location and the orbital [radius](../../../../radius.md). Also, a practical tapered [antenna](../../../../antenna-radio.md) illumination changes the ideal circular-[aperture](../../../../aperture.md) response.
