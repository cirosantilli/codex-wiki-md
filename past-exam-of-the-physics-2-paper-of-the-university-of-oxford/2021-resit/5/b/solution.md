<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a sinusoidal [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) in [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md), write the maximum [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) as $E_0$. The [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) maximum is $B_0=E_0/c$, so the cycle-averaged [Poynting vector](../../../../poynting-vector.md) magnitude, or [light intensity](../../../../light-intensity.md), is

$$
I=\frac{E_0B_0}{\mu_0}\langle\cos^2\omega t\rangle
=\frac12\epsilon_0cE_0^2,\qquad
\boxed{E_0=\sqrt{\frac{2I}{\epsilon_0c}}.}
$$

Each [photon](../../../../photon.md) carries [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $hf$, where $h$ is the [Planck constant](../../../../planck-constant.md). Therefore the [photon number flux density](../../../../photon-number-flux-density.md), namely the number per unit normal [area](../../../../surface-area.md) per unit time, is

$$
\boxed{\Phi_N=\frac I{hf}=\frac{\epsilon_0cE_0^2}{2hf}.}
$$

Multiplying by an illuminated normal [area](../../../../surface-area.md) gives the [photon](../../../../photon.md) arrival rate. Each absorbed [photon](../../../../photon.md) transfers [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $hf/c$ at [normal incidence](../../../../normal-incidence.md), giving the [radiation pressure on a perfectly absorbing surface](../../../../radiation-pressure-on-a-perfectly-absorbing-surface.md)

$$
\boxed{p=\frac Ic=\frac12\epsilon_0E_0^2.}
$$

These are time-averaged quantities. At oblique [angle of incidence](../../../../angle-of-incidence.md) $\theta$, the normal [radiation pressure](../../../../radiation-pressure.md) is $I\cos^2\theta/c$; the simple displayed expression assumes [normal incidence](../../../../normal-incidence.md).
