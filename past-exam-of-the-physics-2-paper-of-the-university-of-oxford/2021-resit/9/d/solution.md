<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A simple converging [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) bends different [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) through different [angles](../../../../angle.md) because of [optical dispersion](../../../../dispersion-optics.md). For a fixed shape in air, the [lensmaker's equation](../../../../lensmaker-s-equation.md) gives $1/f\propto n(\lambda)-1$: the larger short-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) gives a shorter [focal length](../../../../focal-length.md). Different colours therefore form sharp images at different axial positions, causing [longitudinal chromatic aberration](../../../../axial-chromatic-aberration.md), blur and coloured fringes in a white-light image. Off the [optical axis](../../../../optical-axis.md), colour-dependent [transverse magnification](../../../../transverse-magnification.md) can also give [lateral chromatic aberration](../../../../lateral-chromatic-aberration.md). An [achromatic doublet](../../../../achromatic-doublet.md) combines suitable [optical glasses](../../../../optical-glass.md) and curvatures to reduce this wavelength dependence.
