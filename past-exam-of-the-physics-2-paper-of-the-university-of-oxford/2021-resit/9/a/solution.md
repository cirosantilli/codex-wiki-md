<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Huygens-Fresnel principle](../../../../huygens-fresnel-principle.md) treats every point of an advancing [wavefront](../../../../wavefront.md) as a source of secondary [spherical waves](../../../../spherical-wave.md); their forward envelope constructs the next [wavefront](../../../../wavefront.md). Consider two points where an oblique plane [wavefront](../../../../wavefront.md) meets a plane interface a distance $AB$ apart. After its first point reaches $A$, let time $t$ elapse until the second reaches $B$. In medium 1, the incident travel distance is $v_1t=AB\sin i$. Meanwhile, a secondary [wave](../../../../wave-split.md) from $A$ has travelled distance $v_2t$ in medium 2. The tangent from $B$ to that secondary [wavefront](../../../../wavefront.md) gives $v_2t=AB\sin r$. Therefore

$$
\frac{\sin i}{\sin r}=\frac{v_1}{v_2}=\frac{n_2}{n_1},
\qquad\boxed{n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r.}
$$

Here $i,r$ are the [angle of incidence](../../../../angle-of-incidence.md) and [angle of refraction](../../../../angle-of-refraction.md), measured from the interface [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md), and $n_j=c/v_j$ are the [refractive indices](../../../../refractive-index.md) at the common [frequency](../../../../frequency.md). This is [Snell's law](../../../../snell-s-law.md).
