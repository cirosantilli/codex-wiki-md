<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let each slit alone give local [light intensity](../../../../light-intensity.md) $I_s$, and assume equal coherent illumination. In [Fraunhofer diffraction](../../../../fraunhofer-diffraction.md), the two [wave amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) differ in [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) by $\delta=2\pi s\sin\theta/\lambda$. Adding the [wave amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) before squaring gives

$$
I(\theta)=I_s|1+e^{i\delta}|^2=4I_s\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi s\sin\theta}{\lambda}\right).
$$

For a flat screen, $\sin\theta=x/\sqrt{d^2+x^2}$. In the paraxial region $|x|\ll d$, the [double-slit interference](../../../../double-slit-interference.md) profile is therefore

$$
\boxed{I(x)=4I_s\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi sx}{\lambda d}\right),\qquad \Delta x=\frac{\lambda d}{s}.}
$$

The given $d\gg s^2/\lambda$ justifies the far-field expansion. Slit widths have been neglected; a finite width multiplies this profile by the single-slit [diffraction envelope](../../../../diffraction-envelope.md). At large angles the one-slit [light intensity](../../../../light-intensity.md) and geometric spreading must also be retained, rather than treating $I_s$ as constant.
