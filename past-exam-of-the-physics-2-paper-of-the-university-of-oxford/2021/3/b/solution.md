<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) with [linear polarization](../../../../linear-polarization.md), write $\mathbf E=E_0\widehat{\mathbf x}\cos(kz-\omega t)$ and $\mathbf H=E_0\widehat{\mathbf y}\cos(kz-\omega t)/(\mu_0c)$. The [Poynting vector](../../../../poynting-vector.md) is instantaneous [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) flux:

$$
\mathbf S=\frac{E_0^2}{\mu_0c}\cos^2(kz-\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf z}.
$$

The [light intensity](../../../../light-intensity.md) is its cycle average, not its peak:

$$
\boxed{I=\langle|\mathbf S|\rangle=\frac{E_0^2}{2\mu_0c}=\frac12\epsilon_0cE_0^2.}
$$

An incident [electromagnetic wave](../../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md) transports longitudinal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathcal E/c$ per [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\mathcal E$. Perfect reflection back along the incident direction reverses that [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), transferring $2\mathcal E/c$ to the mirror. Dividing by illuminated [area](../../../../surface-area.md) and time gives the mean [radiation pressure](../../../../radiation-pressure.md)

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{2I}{c}=\frac{2\langle|\mathbf S|\rangle}{c}.}
$$

Here $\mathbf S$ denotes the **incident travelling wave**. The average net [Poynting vector](../../../../poynting-vector.md) of the incident-plus-reflected [standing wave](../../../../standing-wave.md) is zero, although its [radiation pressure](../../../../radiation-pressure.md) is nonzero.
