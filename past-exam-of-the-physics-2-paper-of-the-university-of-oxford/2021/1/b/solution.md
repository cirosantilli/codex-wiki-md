<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A large [electrical conductor](../../../../electrical-conductor.md) between the [point charges](../../../../point-charge.md) provides [electrostatic shielding](../../../../electrostatic-shielding.md): one must not retain their original mutual [Coulomb force](../../../../coulomb-force.md) through an ideal infinite plate. In the usual broad-plate approximation, let $\sigma$ denote the unperturbed [surface charge density](../../../../surface-charge-density.md) **on each face**. The [electrostatic boundary condition](../../../../electrostatic-boundary-conditions-at-a-conductor.md) gives an outward [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) $E_0=\sigma/\epsilon_0$. Neglecting local [electrostatic induction](../../../../electrostatic-induction.md), the rod's [tension](../../../../tension.md) then satisfies $mg\tan\phi=QE_0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\phi\simeq\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{Q\sigma}{\epsilon_0mg}\right)=1.98^\circ.}
$$

There are two physical qualifications. First, if $\sigma$ means the **total charge per projected plate area**, shared equally between the two faces, then $E_0=\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ and the same approximation gives $\phi\simeq0.989^\circ$. The factor of two is a convention about the specified [surface charge density](../../../../surface-charge-density.md), not a different [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md).

Second, a [point charge](../../../../point-charge.md) near an [electrical conductor](../../../../electrical-conductor.md) makes its [surface charge density](../../../../surface-charge-density.md) nonuniform. For an infinite plane with prescribed background [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) $E_0$, the [method of images](../../../../method-of-images.md) adds an attractive [force](../../../../force-split.md) $kQ^2/(4x^2)$, where $x=l\sin\phi$. The more accurate [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) equation is

$$
mg\tan\phi=QE_0-\frac{Q^2}{16\pi\epsilon_0l^2\sin^2\phi}.
$$

Its larger, stable root is approximately $1.96^\circ$ for the per-face convention, or $0.877^\circ$ for the total-area convention. This ideal-plane refinement is not an exact answer for an unspecified finite plate: its dimensions and the meaning of the quoted [surface charge density](../../../../surface-charge-density.md) would be needed. In particular, adding a uniform-sheet [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) to the original mutual repulsion would describe a different, nonshielding model.
