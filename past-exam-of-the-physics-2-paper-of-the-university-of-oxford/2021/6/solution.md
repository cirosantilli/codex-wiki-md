<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Neglect the entrance and exit [capacitor fringing fields](../../../fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) and use the coaxial [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) between the cylindrical plates. Let $U=V(R_1)-V(R_2)$, with $R_2>R_1$. The radial [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) is

$$
E_r(r)=\frac{U}{r\ln(R_2/R_1)}.
$$

For an [electron](../../../electron.md), of [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) $-e$, to follow the circular path $R=(R_1+R_2)/2$, its inward [centripetal force](../../../centripetal-force.md) must be $m_ev_0^2/R$. Therefore

$$
e\frac{U}{R\ln(R_2/R_1)}=\frac{m_ev_0^2}{R},\qquad
\boxed{U=\frac{m_ev_0^2}{e}\ln\frac{R_2}{R_1}.}
$$

The **inner plate must be at higher [electric potential](../../../electric-potential.md)**: the [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) points outward and the [electron](../../../electron.md)'s [electric force](../../../electric-force.md) inward. The [electric force](../../../electric-force.md) is perpendicular to the [velocity](../../../velocity.md), so no [work](../../../work.md) is done and the speed remains $v_0$. The required [voltage](../../../voltage.md) is independent of the opening angle $\phi$, which only sets the transit time $R\phi/v_0$. For a narrow gap, $\ln(R_2/R_1)\simeq(R_2-R_1)/R$. This is a nonrelativistic, negligible-[capacitor fringing field](../../../fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) answer; finite open cylindrical sections do not have an exactly radial [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) at their ends.
