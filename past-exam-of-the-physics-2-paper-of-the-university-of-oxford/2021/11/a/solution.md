<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Faraday's law of induction](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) states that the induced [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) around an oriented closed circuit equals the negative rate of change of linked [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md). For a stationary circuit,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=-\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt},\qquad \Phi_B=\int_A\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf A.}
$$

Here $\mathbf E$ is [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md), $\mathbf B$ is [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), and the oriented surface $A$ has the circuit as its boundary. For $N$ identical series turns, replace $\Phi_B$ by the total [flux linkage](../../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) $N\Phi_B$. [Lenz's law](../../../../lenz-s-law.md) supplies the sign: an induced [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) opposes the **change** of linked [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md), in agreement with [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).

If the wire moves with local [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf u$, the more general [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) includes the magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) per unit [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md):

$$
\mathcal E=\oint(\mathbf E+\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=-\frac{d}{dt}\int_{A(t)}\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf A.
$$

The boundary and surface orientations in both expressions are related by the [right-hand rule](../../../../right-hand-rule.md).
