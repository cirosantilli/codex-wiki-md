<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [long-solenoid approximation](../../../../long-solenoid-approximation.md), the internal [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is uniform, $B=\mu_0nI$, and the external [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is negligible. This follows from the [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) applied to a rectangular loop running inside and outside the [solenoid](../../../../solenoid.md). Its number of turns is $N=nD$ and its cross-sectional [area](../../../../surface-area.md) is $A=\pi a^2$. Each turn links [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md) $\Phi_B=BA=\mu_0nI\pi a^2$. Therefore the [flux linkage](../../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) is

$$
\Lambda=N\Phi_B=\mu_0n^2D\pi a^2I.
$$

Using the definition of [self-inductance](../../../../self-inductance.md), $\Lambda=LI$, gives

$$
\boxed{L=\mu_0n^2\pi a^2D.}
$$

This neglects end leakage and winding thickness and uses [vacuum permeability](../../../../vacuum-permeability.md) for the air core.
