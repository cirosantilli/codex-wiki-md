<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the imposed [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) $I=I_0\cos\omega t$, [Faraday's law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives the self-induced [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E_{\rm self}=-L\frac{dI}{dt}=\omega LI_0\sin\omega t.}
$$

The externally applied terminal [voltage](../../../../voltage.md), with the [passive sign convention](../../../../passive-sign-convention.md), has the opposite sign:

$$
v_L=L\frac{dI}{dt}=-\omega LI_0\sin\omega t.
$$

Thus the terminal [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) leads the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) by $\pi/2$. The instantaneous input [power](../../../../power.md) is

$$
p=v_LI=-\frac12\omega LI_0^2\sin2\omega t=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac12LI^2\right),\qquad \boxed{\langle p\rangle=0.}
$$

An ideal [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) alternately stores [magnetic energy](../../../../magnetic-energy.md) and returns it to the source. Real winding [electrical resistance](../../../../electrical-resistance.md) would add mean [Joule heating](../../../../joule-heating.md) $I_0^2R_w/2$; the ideal zero result assumes that loss is neglected.
