<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use positive real object and image distances $u,v$ for a [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md). The required absolute [magnification](../../../../magnification.md) is $M=1000/15=200/3$, and the specified LCD-to-screen separation gives $u+v=2000\,\mathrm{mm}$, not $v=2000\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since $v/u=M$,

$$
u=\frac{2000}{1+200/3}=29.5567\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad v=1970.4433\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation yields

$$
\boxed{f=\frac{uv}{u+v}=29.1198\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

Place the projection [lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) $29.56\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond the LCD towards the screen. Its real image is inverted; the signed [magnification](../../../../magnification.md) is $-200/3$.
