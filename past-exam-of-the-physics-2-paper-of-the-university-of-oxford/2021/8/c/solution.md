<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\widehat{\mathbf e}_r$ point from the intersection to the real [point charge](../../../../point-charge.md). Reflection symmetry cancels the component of [electrostatic force](../../../../electrostatic-force.md) perpendicular to the wedge bisector. An [image charge](../../../../image-charge.md) $q_j$ at angular separation $\Delta$ from the real [point charge](../../../../point-charge.md) gives radial [force](../../../../force-split.md)

$$
F_{r,j}=kqq_j\frac{d(1-\cos\Delta)}{[2d^2(1-\cos\Delta)]^{3/2}}.
$$

The two adjacent negative [image charges](../../../../image-charge.md) have $\Delta=\pm60^\circ$, distance $d$, and combined radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) $-kq^2/d^2$. The two positive [image charges](../../../../image-charge.md) have $\Delta=\pm120^\circ$, distance $\sqrt3d$, and combined radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) $+kq^2/(\sqrt3d^2)$. The opposite negative [image charge](../../../../image-charge.md) is at distance $2d$ and contributes $-kq^2/(4d^2)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=-\frac{q^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0d^2}\left(\frac54-\frac1{\sqrt3}\right)\widehat{\mathbf e}_r.}
$$

The [electrostatic force](../../../../electrostatic-force.md) is towards the intersection, independently of the sign of $q$. One sums the five [image charge](../../../../image-charge.md) [electric fields](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and multiplies by $q$; the factor $1/2$ used in induced-charge [electrostatic energy](../../../../electrostatic-energy.md) must not be inserted into this direct [force](../../../../force-split.md) calculation.
