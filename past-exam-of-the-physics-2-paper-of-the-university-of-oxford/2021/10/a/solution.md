<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With [phasors](../../../../phasor.md) proportional to $e^{i\omega t}$, the [resistor](../../../../resistor.md), [inductor](../../../../inductor.md), and [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) have [complex impedances](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) $R$, $i\omega L$, and $1/(i\omega C)$. In a [series circuit](../../../../series-circuit.md) they carry the same [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md), so their [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) drops add:

$$
\boxed{Z(\omega)=R+i\left(\omega L-\frac1{\omega C}\right).}
$$

The real part describes [Joule heating](../../../../joule-heating.md); the imaginary part changes sign between capacitive and inductive response, passing through zero at $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$.
