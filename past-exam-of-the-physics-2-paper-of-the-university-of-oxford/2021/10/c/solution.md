<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$ the inductive and capacitive [electrical reactances](../../../../electrical-reactance.md) cancel, so the [complex impedance](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) is $R$ and the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) amplitude is $V_0/R$. The [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) amplitude is $V_0/(R\omega_0C)$. Thus the specified [quality factor](../../../../q-factor.md) is

$$
\boxed{Q=|H(\omega_0)|=\frac1{R\omega_0C}=\frac{\omega_0L}{R}=\frac1R\sqrt{\frac LC}.}
$$

A [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) output exceeding the input [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) is consistent with a passive [electrical circuit](../../../../electrical-circuit-split.md): the [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) and [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) [voltages](../../../../voltage.md) largely cancel in their sum. The expression assumes positive $R$; the ideal undamped limit $R=0$ has no finite steady resonant response.
