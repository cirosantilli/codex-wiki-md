<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $x=\omega/\omega_0$. Maximizing the output [wave amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is equivalent to minimizing

$$
D(x)=|H|^{-2}=(1-x^2)^2+\frac{x^2}{Q^2}.
$$

Its derivative is $D'(x)=2x[2x^2-2+Q^{-2}]$. An interior minimum at positive [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) therefore requires $Q>1/\sqrt2$, and occurs at

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\rm res}=\omega_0\sqrt{1-\frac1{2Q^2}},\qquad Q>\frac1{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Indeed $D''(x_{\rm res})=8x_{\rm res}^2>0$, and the peak value is $|H|_{\max}=Q/\sqrt{1-1/(4Q^2)}$. For $0<Q\leq1/\sqrt2$, $D$ increases for $x>0$ and the largest output is the DC value $|H(0)|=1$. At equality the putative peak has reached zero [frequency](../../../../frequency.md); there is no distinct nonzero-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) [resonance](../../../../resonance.md). This threshold concerns [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) peaking: the [series RLC circuit](../../../../series-rlc-circuit.md)'s [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) still has its maximum at $\omega_0$ for every positive $R$.
