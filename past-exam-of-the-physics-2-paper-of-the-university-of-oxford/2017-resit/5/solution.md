<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For normal incidence on a [diffraction grating](../../../diffraction-grating.md), adjacent grooves contribute a path difference $d\sin\theta$. [Constructive interference](../../../constructive-interference.md) occurs when the [grating equation](../../../grating-equation.md) $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$ holds. Thus the groove spacing is

$$
d=\frac{3(589\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm m)}{\sin32^\circ}
=3.334\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm m,
\qquad
\boxed{N=\frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm m}{d}=299.9\ \text{lines per mm}\simeq300\ \text{lines per mm}.}
$$

An allowed propagating [diffraction order](../../../diffraction-order.md) must have $|\sin\theta|\leq1$. Therefore

$$
|m|\leq\frac d\lambda=\frac3{\sin32^\circ}=5.6615,
\qquad
\boxed{|m|_{\max}=5.}
$$

The fifth [diffraction order](../../../diffraction-order.md) is the highest geometrically permitted order for this [wavelength](../../../wavelength.md). Its actual brightness depends on groove shape and the [single-slit diffraction](../../../single-slit-diffraction.md) envelope, which the source does not specify; geometric permission alone does not guarantee a bright observable maximum.
