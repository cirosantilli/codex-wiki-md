<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [phasors](../../../../phasor.md) with time dependence $e^{i\omega t}$. The [electrical impedance](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) $Z=\widetilde V/\widetilde I$ is the [complex number](../../../../complex-number.md) relating the sinusoidal [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) and [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md): $|Z|$ is their [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) ratio and $\arg Z$ is the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) relative to the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md). The component laws give

$$
V=RI\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{Z_R=R},\qquad
I=C\frac{dV}{dt}\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{Z_C=\frac1{i\omega C}},\qquad
V=L\frac{dI}{dt}\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{Z_L=i\omega L}.
$$

Thus the [resistor](../../../../resistor.md) has zero relative [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md), the [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) lags its [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) by $\pi/2$, and the [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) leads its [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) by $\pi/2$. The ideal [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) and [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) store and return [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the real [electrical impedance](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) of the [resistor](../../../../resistor.md) describes dissipation.
