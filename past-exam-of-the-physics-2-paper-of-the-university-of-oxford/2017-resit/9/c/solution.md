<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substituting the stated [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) and thickness coefficient, $\alpha=2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, gives

$$
\boxed{f=\frac1{2(1.5-1)(2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})}=0.50\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation then gives

$$
\frac1v=\frac1f-\frac1u=2-\frac1{0.7}=\frac47\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},
\qquad
\boxed{v=1.75\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The positive image distance places a [real image](../../../../real-image.md) on the outgoing side of the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md), at $x=+1.75\,\mathrm m$. The object is beyond the [focal point](../../../../focal-point-of-a-hypersurface.md), so outgoing [light rays](../../../../light-ray.md) actually converge there rather than only their backward extensions meeting.
