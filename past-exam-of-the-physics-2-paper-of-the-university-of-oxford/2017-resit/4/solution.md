<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [current density](../../../current-density.md) is the moving [charge density](../../../charge-density.md) times its [velocity](../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf J=\rho\mathbf v=\rho v_0\hat{\mathbf z}\quad(r<R),\qquad \mathbf J=\mathbf0\quad(r>R).}
$$

For a cylindrical [Gaussian surface](../../../gaussian-surface.md) of radius $r<R$ and length $\ell$, cylindrical symmetry makes the [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) radial. [Gauss's law](../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $E(2\pi r\ell)=\rho\pi r^2\ell/\epsilon_0$. A circular [Ampère's circuital law](../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) contour encloses [electric current](../../../electric-current.md) $I(r)=\rho v_0\pi r^2$, giving $B(2\pi r)=\mu_0I(r)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E(r)=\frac{\rho r}{2\epsilon_0}\hat{\mathbf r},\qquad
\mathbf B(r)=\frac{\mu_0\rho v_0r}{2}\hat{\boldsymbol\phi}
=\mu_0\epsilon_0\,\mathbf v\times\mathbf E(r),\quad r<R.}
$$

The [right-hand rule](../../../right-hand-rule.md) fixes the positive azimuthal direction of the [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) for positive [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) moving along $+z$. Both the [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) and the [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) vanish on the axis. These are the steady long-cylinder [electric field](../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md); end effects and changes of the prescribed beam distribution are excluded.
