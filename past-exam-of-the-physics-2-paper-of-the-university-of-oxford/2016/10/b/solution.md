<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a real image, $u>f$ and $v=fu/(u-f)>0$. The object-to-image separation is

$$
D=u+v=\frac{u^2}{u-f}.
$$

Its excess above $4f$ factors into a nonnegative expression:

$$
D-4f=\frac{u^2-4f(u-f)}{u-f}=\frac{(u-2f)^2}{u-f}\ge0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{\min}=4f,\qquad u=v=2f}.
$$

At this minimum the image is inverted and has unit absolute [magnification](../../../../magnification.md). The restriction to a real object and a real image matters: the positive separation used here is not the same quantity as a signed distance to a virtual image.
