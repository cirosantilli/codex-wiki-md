<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)}.
$$

For $\mathbf E=0$, the magnetic part is perpendicular to the [velocity](../../../velocity.md), so its instantaneous [power](../../../power.md) $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v$ vanishes. The [speed](../../../speed.md) and [kinetic energy](../../../kinetic-energy.md) therefore remain constant. Decompose the initial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) into $v_\parallel$ along the uniform [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and $v_\perp$ perpendicular to it. The parallel component is unchanged, while the perpendicular component rotates uniformly.

For a nonrelativistic particle with $qB\ne0$, the resulting helical motion has

$$
\boxed{r_L=\frac{mv_\perp}{|q|B},\qquad\omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m},\qquad T=\frac{2\pi m}{|q|B},\qquad h=v_\parallel T}.
$$

Here $r_L$ is the [Larmor radius](../../../gyroradius.md), $\omega_c$ the [cyclotron frequency](../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) in angular units, and $h$ the axial advance per turn. Opposite signs of [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) give opposite senses of rotation. Pure perpendicular motion gives a circle; pure parallel motion gives a straight line. If $q=0$ or $B=0$, there is no magnetic deflection. At relativistic [speeds](../../../speed.md), replace $m$ in the radius and period by $\gamma m$; $\gamma$ stays constant because the magnetic [force](../../../force-split.md) does no work.
