<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the [electrostatic potential energy](../../../../electrostatic-energy.md) to vanish for infinitely separated [point charges](../../../../point-charge.md), and write $k_e=1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$. The first charge costs no work to place. Bringing the second to distance $a$ costs $k_eq^2/a$; the third is distance $a$ from both existing charges and costs $2k_eq^2/a$. Thus the external quasistatic assembly work is

$$
\boxed{W=U=\frac{3q^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0a}}.
$$

Equivalently, sum over the three distinct pairs in the [equilateral triangle](../../../../equilateral-triangle.md). Counting both ordered pairs without a compensating factor of one half would double the answer. The positive result applies to either sign of $q$, since all three [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) have the same sign.
