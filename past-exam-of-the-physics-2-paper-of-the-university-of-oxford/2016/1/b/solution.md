<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By symmetry, the resultant [Coulomb force](../../../../coulomb-force.md) on each charge points along the line from the [centroid](../../../../centroid.md) to its vertex. Their [accelerations](../../../../acceleration.md) and subsequent [speeds](../../../../speed.md) are equal, so the [centroid](../../../../centroid.md) remains fixed and the [equilateral triangle](../../../../equilateral-triangle.md) expands without rotating. Each charge follows a straight radial trajectory; the separation grows and the repulsive [force](../../../../force-split.md) decreases.

For example, if the instantaneous side is $s$ and the centroid-to-charge distance is $r=s/\sqrt3$, the two pair forces have a $60^\circ$ angle between them. Their resultant is $\sqrt3k_eq^2/s^2=k_eq^2/(\sqrt3r^2)$, along the outward radius. This confirms that the symmetric shape persists.

Assuming release from rest, nonrelativistic motion and negligible radiation losses, [energy conservation](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\frac32mv_\infty^2=\frac{3k_eq^2}{a},\qquad\boxed{v_\infty=\sqrt{\frac{2k_eq^2}{ma}}=\sqrt{\frac{q^2}{2\pi\epsilon_0ma}}}.
$$

The three velocity vectors sum to zero, consistent with [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md). Their magnitudes approach the displayed value as the remaining [electrostatic potential energy](../../../../electrostatic-energy.md) tends to zero.
