<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Spherical symmetry and the vacuum [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) imply $\mathbf E=A\hat{\mathbf r}/r^2$ in the gap, where $A$ is constant. Integrating the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) between the two [equipotentials](../../../../equipotential.md) gives

$$
V_1=V(a)-V(b)=\int_a^b\frac{A}{r^2}\,dr=A\left(\frac1a-\frac1b\right).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E(r)=\frac{V_1ab}{(b-a)r^2}\hat{\mathbf r},\qquad a<r<b}.
$$

The inner charge is $Q=4\pi\epsilon_0A$, which reproduces the [spherical capacitor](../../../../spherical-capacitor.md) [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md) $4\pi\epsilon_0ab/(b-a)$. The radial direction is outward for positive $V_1$ and inward for negative $V_1$; the formula represents the signed field, not just its magnitude.
