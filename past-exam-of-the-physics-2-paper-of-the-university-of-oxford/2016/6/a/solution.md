<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md), two equally bright, mutually incoherent [point sources](../../../../point-source.md) are just resolved when the central maximum of one [diffraction pattern](../../../../diffraction-pattern.md) lies at the first minimum of the other. Their [intensities](../../../../intensity-physics.md), rather than their electric-field amplitudes, add. For an ideal circular aperture of diameter $D$, the first dark ring of the [Airy disk](../../../../airy-disk.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\theta_R\simeq1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}}.
$$

The angle is in radians in the small-angle limit. This is a conventional resolution criterion for a specified aperture and [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md); it is not an absolute prohibition on estimating a source separation by fitting noisy data under additional assumptions.
