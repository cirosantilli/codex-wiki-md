<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $A=\pi r^2$ and choose the rotating area normal to coincide initially with $+z$. At time $t$ its angle to the applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is $\omega t$, so the external [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is $\Phi_B=BA\cos\omega t$. [Faraday's law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E(t)=-\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}=B\pi r^2\omega\sin\omega t}.
$$

In the high-[electrical resistance](../../../../electrical-resistance.md) approximation, neglecting the loop's [self-inductance](../../../../self-inductance.md), [Ohm's law](../../../../ohm-s-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I(t)=\frac{B\pi r^2\omega}{R}\sin\omega t}.
$$

The displayed signs specify positive circulation by the chosen normal; reversing the reference circulation reverses both signs. The high-[electrical resistance](../../../../electrical-resistance.md) assumption is quantitatively $\omega L\ll R$ if the loop has [self-inductance](../../../../self-inductance.md) $L$. Otherwise the current lags the induced [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) and must be obtained from $L\dot I+RI=\mathcal E$.
