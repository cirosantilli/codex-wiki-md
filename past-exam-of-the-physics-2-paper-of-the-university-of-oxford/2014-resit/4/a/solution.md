<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\psi=kx-\omega t$ and $v_p=\omega/k$. The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is

$$
\mathbf E=A\sin\psi\,(\hat{\mathbf y}+6\hat{\mathbf z}).
$$

It has [linear polarization](../../../../linear-polarization.md), along the fixed line $E_z=6E_y$, at angle $\tan^{-1}6\simeq80.54^\circ$ from $+y$ toward $+z$. For the associated [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md), [Faraday's law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives $\mathbf B=v_p^{-1}\hat{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf E$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{A}{v_p}\sin\psi\,(-6\hat{\mathbf y}+\hat{\mathbf z}),\qquad B_x=0.}
$$

In vacuum $v_p=c$. Both fields are transverse, and $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ points along $+x$.
