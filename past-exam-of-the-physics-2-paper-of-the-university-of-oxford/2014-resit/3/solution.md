<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let $\theta=\omega t+\theta_0$ be the angle between the coil's area normal and the uniform [magnetic flux density](../../../magnetic-field-split.md). The linked [magnetic flux](../../../magnetic-flux.md) is $\Phi_N=NBA\cos\theta$. [Faraday's law](../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E(t)=-\frac{d\Phi_N}{dt}=NBA\omega\sin(\omega t+\theta_0).}
$$

The sign uses the winding direction associated with the chosen normal; reversing the terminal convention changes it. The magnitude is largest when $\cos\theta=0$: the coil's normal is perpendicular to the [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md), so the plane of the coil is parallel to it. At that orientation the instantaneous [magnetic flux](../../../magnetic-flux.md) is zero but its rate of change has maximum magnitude.
