<h1 id="9/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use the [phasor](../../../../phasor.md) convention $v(t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde V e^{i\omega t})$. The [complex impedance](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) is $Z=\widetilde V/\widetilde I$ in sinusoidal steady state. From $v_R=Ri$, $i_C=C\,dv_C/dt$, and $v_L=L\,di_L/dt$,

$$
\boxed{Z_R=R,\qquad Z_C=\frac1{i\omega C}=-\frac{i}{\omega C},\qquad Z_L=i\omega L.}
$$

For this convention an ideal [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) has voltage leading current by $\pi/2$, while an ideal [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) has current leading voltage by $\pi/2$.
