↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

In [wave optics](../../../../physical-optics-split.md), a finite lens aperture produces [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md). Contributions interfere constructively near the geometric focus but form a finite [point spread function](../../../../point-spread-function-split.md), rather than a point of infinite intensity. For a uniformly illuminated circular aperture of diameter $D$, the paraxial focal pattern is an [Airy disk](../../../../airy-disk.md), with first dark-ring radius approximately $1.22\lambda f/D$. Aberrations can broaden or distort it further; even an ideal lens retains the finite diffraction scale.
