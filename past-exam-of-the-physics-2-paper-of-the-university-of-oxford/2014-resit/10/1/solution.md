[Fermat's principle](../../../../fermat-principle.md) states that the actual [ray](../../../../ray-geometry.md) makes the travel time stationary under small variations of its path with endpoints fixed. Equivalently, for a stationary medium it makes the [optical path length](../../../../optical-path-length.md) $\int n\,ds$ stationary. “Stationary” allows minima, maxima, or other stationary paths; it does not require a strict minimum in every optical system.
