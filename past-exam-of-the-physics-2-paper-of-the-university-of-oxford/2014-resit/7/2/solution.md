<h1 id="7/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Take the initial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) perpendicular to a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). The magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to the motion, has constant magnitude $|q|vB$, and supplies the [centripetal force](../../../../centripetal-force.md):

$$
\frac{mv^2}{r}=|q|vB,\qquad\boxed{r=\frac{mv}{|q|B},\quad\omega_c=\frac vr=\frac{|q|B}{m}.}
$$

The rotation sense is fixed by $q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$. A component of [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) parallel to the field remains constant and produces a [helical motion in a uniform magnetic field](../../../../helical-motion-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md); a circle requires that component to vanish. The speed-independent [cyclotron frequency](../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) here assumes nonrelativistic motion.
