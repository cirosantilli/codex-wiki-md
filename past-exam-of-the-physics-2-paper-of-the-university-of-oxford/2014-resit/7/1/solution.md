<h1 id="7/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Lorentz force law](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B).}
$$

Its rate of work is $dT/dt=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$, because $\mathbf v\cdot(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)=0$. Thus, with $\mathbf E=0$, the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) cannot increase: a static [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) changes the direction of motion but does no work.
