<h1 id="8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For normal incidence, adjacent grating lines have [optical path difference](../../../../optical-path-difference.md) $s\sin\theta$. Constructive interference at order $m$ requires $s\sin\theta_m=m\lambda$. Differentiating at fixed $m$ yields the [angular dispersion](../../../../angular-dispersion.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\theta_m}{d\lambda}=\frac{m}{s\cos\theta_m}.}
$$

It is zero in the zero order and increases in magnitude toward grazing diffraction. An order exists only if $|m\lambda|\le s$. The number of illuminated lines controls peak width and resolving power, but does not alter this angle-versus-wavelength relation.
