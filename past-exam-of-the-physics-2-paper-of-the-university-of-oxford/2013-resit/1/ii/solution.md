<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The instantaneous power delivered by the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is

$$
\frac{dW}{dt}=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v=q(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf v=0.
$$

Therefore $\boxed{W=0}$ over any part of the orbit. The magnetic force changes direction of motion but leaves the speed and [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) constant.
