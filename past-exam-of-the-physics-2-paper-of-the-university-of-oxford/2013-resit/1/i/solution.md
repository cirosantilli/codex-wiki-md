↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B.}
$$

Since the given [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is perpendicular to the [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), its magnitude is $|q|vB$. Its direction follows the vector product for positive charge and reverses for negative charge.
