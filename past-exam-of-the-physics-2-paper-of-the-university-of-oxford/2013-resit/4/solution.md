<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Assume a converging [thin lens](../../../thin-lens.md) with $f>0$, a real object, and a real image on the detector. For positive object and image distances $u,v$, the [thin lens](../../../thin-lens.md) equation gives $uv=f(u+v)$. Put $S=u+v$. Then

$$
(u-v)^2=S^2-4uv=S(S-4f)\ge0.
$$

Since $S>0$, $S\ge4f$, with equality at $u=v=2f$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S_{\min}=4f,\qquad u=v=2f.}
$$

Below this separation no real conjugate pair exists for one ideal thin lens; a diverging lens alone cannot form a real image of a real object on the detector.
