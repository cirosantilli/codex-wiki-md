<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the loop normal along $+z$. Rotation about $x$ gives $B_z=B\cos\omega t$, so the linked [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is $\Phi_B=Ba^2\cos\omega t$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E(t)=Ba^2\omega\sin\omega t.}
$$

The sign uses the contour orientation associated with $+z$. For the small open gap, this is the corresponding open-circuit terminal emf, with the stated terminal ordering. The tangential induced [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is nonconservative, so its circulation is not the integral of a single-valued electrostatic potential gradient.
