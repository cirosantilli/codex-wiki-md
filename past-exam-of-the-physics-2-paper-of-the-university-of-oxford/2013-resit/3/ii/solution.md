<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An external source sees the input resistor between its terminal and the [virtual ground](../../../../virtual-ground.md). The input current is $I_i=V_i/R_i$, so

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm in}=R_i=1\,\mathrm{k\Omega},\qquad R_{\rm out}=0\quad\text{for an ideal amplifier}.}
$$

The infinite input resistance of the ideal [operational amplifier](../../../../operational-amplifier.md) itself is distinct from the finite input resistance of the complete [inverting amplifier](../../../../inverting-amplifier.md). Negative feedback makes the output approach an ideal [voltage source](../../../../voltage-source.md); a real amplifier retains a small nonzero output resistance.
