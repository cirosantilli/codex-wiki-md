<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the linear [inverting amplifier](../../../../inverting-amplifier.md), the non-inverting terminal is grounded and [negative feedback](../../../../negative-feedback.md) connects the output to the inverting terminal. For large open-loop gain $A$, $V_o=A(V_+-V_-)$; a finite unsaturated output therefore requires $V_-\simeq V_+=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{V_-\simeq0\quad\text{under stable negative feedback}.}
$$

This is a [virtual ground](../../../../virtual-ground.md): it has approximately ground potential, but it is not a conducting connection to ground. The ideal [operational amplifier](../../../../operational-amplifier.md) draws no input current, so input current flows through the feedback resistor. Saturation, loss of feedback, or insufficient bandwidth invalidates the approximation.
