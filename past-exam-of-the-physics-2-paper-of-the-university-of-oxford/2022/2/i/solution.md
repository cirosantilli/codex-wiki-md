<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [ideal operational amplifier](../../../../ideal-operational-amplifier.md) has **infinite [input impedance](../../../../input-impedance.md)**, so neither input draws [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md), and **zero [output impedance](../../../../output-impedance.md)**, so its prescribed output [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) is independent of the output [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) within the ideal model. These are the two additional assumptions needed here.
