<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Position A disconnects the [battery](../../../../electric-battery.md) and closes the [resistor](../../../../resistor.md)-[inductor](../../../../inductor.md) loop. Taking clockwise [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) as positive, [Kirchhoff's voltage law](../../../../kirchhoff-cycle-law.md) for this [RL circuit](../../../../rl-circuit.md) gives

$$
L\frac{dI}{dt}+RI=0.
$$

The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) through an ideal [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) remains continuous across a switch with no impulsive [voltage](../../../../voltage.md). Thus $I(0^+)=I_0$, and [separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I(t)=I_0e^{-Rt/L}\quad(0<t<\Delta t).}
$$

If the switch remains at A, the same result holds for every $t>0$. The [time constant](../../../../time-constant.md) is $L/R$; the stored [magnetic energy](../../../../magnetic-energy.md) is dissipated by [Joule heating](../../../../joule-heating.md).
