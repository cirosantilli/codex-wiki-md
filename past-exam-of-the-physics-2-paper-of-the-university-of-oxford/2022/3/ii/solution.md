<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the axial [current density](../../../../current-density.md) as $j(r)=Kr$. Normalize it using the total [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md):

$$
I=\int_0^a Kr\,2\pi r\,dr=\frac{2\pi Ka^3}{3},\qquad K=\frac{3I}{2\pi a^3}.
$$

The enclosed [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) at radius $r$ is $I_{\rm enc}(r)=2\pi Kr^3/3=I(r/a)^3$. The [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) now yields

$$
\boxed{B(r)=\frac{\mu_0 I r^2}{2\pi a^3}\quad(0\leq r\leq a).}
$$

Both profiles join the exterior [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) $\mu_0 I/(2\pi a)$ at the surface, but the second vanishes quadratically at the axis because less [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is concentrated near it.
