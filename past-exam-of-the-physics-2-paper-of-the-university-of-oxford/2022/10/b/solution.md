<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The elemental [torque](../../../../torque.md) about the origin is $d\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf r\times d\mathbf F$. Substitution of part (a) gives

$$
d\boldsymbol\tau=Ia^2\bigl(-B_y\sin^2\theta-B_x\sin\theta\cos\theta,\ B_y\sin\theta\cos\theta+B_x\cos^2\theta,\ 0\bigr)\,d\theta.
$$

Only its $x$ component drives the allowed rotation about the $x$ axis. Using $\int_0^{2\pi}\sin^2\theta\,d\theta=\pi$ and $\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta=0$, the projected [torque](../../../../torque.md) is

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\tau_{\text{about }x}=-I\pi a^2B_y\hat{\mathbf x}.}
$$

The [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is $\mathbf m=I\pi a^2\hat{\mathbf z}$, so the full electromagnetic [torque](../../../../torque.md) is

$$
\mathbf m\times\mathbf B=I\pi a^2(-B_y\hat{\mathbf x}+B_x\hat{\mathbf y}).
$$

Its $x$ component agrees exactly. The $y$ component is a real electromagnetic [torque](../../../../torque.md), but the bearing constraining motion to the $x$ axis supplies a cancelling reaction [torque](../../../../torque.md). For an allowed infinitesimal rotation $d\boldsymbol\alpha=\hat{\mathbf x}\,d\alpha$, only $\boldsymbol\tau\cdot d\boldsymbol\alpha=\tau_xd\alpha$ does mechanical [work](../../../../work.md).
