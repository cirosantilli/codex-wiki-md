<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the source orientation, anticlockwise viewed from $+z$. The position and oriented line element are

$$
\mathbf r=a(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0),\qquad
 d\mathbf l=a(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0)\,d\theta.
$$

The [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) on the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) element is $d\mathbf F=I\,d\mathbf l\times\mathbf B$. Evaluating the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) gives

$$
\boxed{d\mathbf F=Ia\bigl(B_z\cos\theta,\ B_z\sin\theta,\ -B_y\sin\theta-B_x\cos\theta\bigr)\,d\theta.}
$$

Each component integrates to zero around $0\leq\theta<2\pi$, so $\mathbf F_{\rm net}=0$. Equivalently, uniform [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) permits $I\oint d\mathbf l\times\mathbf B=I(\oint d\mathbf l)\times\mathbf B=0$. Uniformity is essential; a nonuniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) can exert a net [force](../../../../force-split.md) on a [magnetic dipole](../../../../magnetic-dipole.md).
