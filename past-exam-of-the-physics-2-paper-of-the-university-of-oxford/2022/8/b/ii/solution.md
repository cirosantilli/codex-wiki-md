<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the balance [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md), $Z=W$ and the two [voltage dividers](../../../../../voltage-divider.md) have the same ratio. Thus

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm oc}(\omega_0)=0,\qquad V_A(\omega_0)=0,\qquad I_A(\omega_0)=0.}
$$

This zero detector [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) is the operational condition used to find $R_v$ and $\omega_0$.
