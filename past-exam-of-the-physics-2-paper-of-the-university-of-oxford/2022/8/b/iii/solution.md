<h1 id="8/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

As $\omega\to\infty$, both [capacitors](../../../../../capacitor.md) approach [short circuits](../../../../../short-circuit.md). Thus $Z\to0$, $W\to R_v>0$, and the unloaded right junction tends to the source's top-terminal [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\lim_{\omega\to\infty}V_{\rm oc}=\frac{V_0}{2},\qquad V_A=0\ \text{for an ideal ammeter}.}
$$

The connected ideal [ammeter](../../../../../ammeter.md) carries limiting [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) $V_0/R$ from right to left because $Z_{\rm th}\to R/2$. Reversing the output polarity reverses both nonzero open-circuit answers, without changing their magnitudes.
