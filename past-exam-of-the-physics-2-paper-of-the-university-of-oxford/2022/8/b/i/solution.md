<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At zero [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md), the lower right [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) is an [open circuit](../../../../../open-circuit.md), while the upper right [electrical impedance](../../../../../electrical-impedance.md) tends to $R_x$. With the detector removed, no [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) flows down that branch, so $V_R=V_0$ and $V_L=V_0/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm oc}(0)=\frac{V_0}{2},\qquad V_A(0)=0\ \text{for an ideal ammeter}.}
$$

For the connected ideal [ammeter](../../../../../ammeter.md), the resulting [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) from right to left is $V_0/(R+2R_x)$, despite its zero terminal [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md). This follows from $I_A=V_{\rm oc}/Z_{\rm th}$ with $Z_{\rm th}=R/2+R_x$.
