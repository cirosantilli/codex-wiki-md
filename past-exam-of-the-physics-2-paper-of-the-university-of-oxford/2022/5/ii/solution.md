<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using $\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B$ directly with the specified [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) gives

$$
-ik\hat{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf E_0=-i\omega\mathbf B_0,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf B_0=\frac{E_0}{c}\hat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md), [magnetic flux density](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and propagation direction form a right-handed triad, and for a nonzero [wave amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{|\mathbf E_0|}{|\mathbf B_0|}=c.}
$$

The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) have the same [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) in vacuum.
