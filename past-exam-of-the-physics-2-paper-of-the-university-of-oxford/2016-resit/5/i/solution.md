<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a steady uniform [current density](../../../../current-density.md) in a nonmagnetic wire, the enclosed [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) at radius $r<a$ is $I_{\mathrm{enc}}=Ir^2/a^2$. A circular [Amperian loop](../../../../amperian-loop.md) and [Ampère's law](../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) then give

$$
\boxed{B(r)=\frac{\mu_0Ir}{2\pi a^2},\qquad 0\leq r<a.}
$$

The [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) circles the wire according to the [right-hand rule](../../../../right-hand-rule.md) relative to the current direction. Its magnitude increases linearly from zero on the axis. The uniform-current assumption excludes [skin effect](../../../../skin-effect.md).
