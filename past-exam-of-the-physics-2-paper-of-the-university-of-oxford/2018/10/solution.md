<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [Huygens-Fresnel principle](../../../huygens-fresnel-principle.md) constructs a later wavefront as the envelope of secondary wavelets emitted from the earlier one. At a planar interface, take a time interval during which one point on the incoming front travels distance $v_1\Delta t$ before reaching the interface, while the point that reached it first generates a wavelet of radius $v_2\Delta t$. The two right triangles share the interface segment, giving $\sin i/\sin r=v_1/v_2$. Since the [refractive index](../../../refractive-index.md) is $n=c/v$, this yields [Snell's law](../../../snell-s-law.md):

$$
\boxed{n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r.}
$$

The angles are measured from the interface [normal](../../../normal-distribution.md). This geometric construction assumes homogeneous media and uses the propagation speeds at the given [frequency](../../../frequency.md).
