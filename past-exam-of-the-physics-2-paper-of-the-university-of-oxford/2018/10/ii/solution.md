<h1 id="10/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $n=1.5$, the [critical angle](../../../../critical-angle-for-total-internal-reflection.md) is $i_c=\arcsin(1/1.5)\simeq41.8103^\circ$. Here $0\leq r<i_c<\alpha=60^\circ$, so the condition at B is $\alpha-r>i_c$. At the threshold,

$$
r_*=60^\circ-i_c\simeq18.1897^\circ,\qquad
\theta_*=\arcsin(1.5\sin r_*)\simeq27.9205^\circ.
$$

Thus the requested positive-angle interval for strict [total internal reflection](../../../../total-internal-reflection.md) is

$$
\boxed{0<\theta<27.9205^\circ.}
$$

At the upper equality the transmitted ray is grazing; normal incidence $\theta=0$ also gives [total internal reflection](../../../../total-internal-reflection.md) at B if the endpoint is included. The distinction follows the question's positive-angle convention.
