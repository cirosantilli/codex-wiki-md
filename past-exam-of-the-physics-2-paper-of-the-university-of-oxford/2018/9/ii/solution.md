<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md), $I_C=C\,dV/dt$, so

$$
\boxed{Z_C=\frac1{i\omega C},\qquad I_C(t)=-\omega CV_0\sin\omega t=\omega CV_0\cos(\omega t+\pi/2).}
$$

The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) **leads the voltage by $\pi/2$**.
