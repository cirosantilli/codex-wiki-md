↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [resistor](../../../../resistor.md), [Ohm's law](../../../../ohm-s-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{Z_R=R,\qquad I_R(t)=\frac{V_0}{R}\cos\omega t.}
$$

The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) and [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) are **in phase**.
