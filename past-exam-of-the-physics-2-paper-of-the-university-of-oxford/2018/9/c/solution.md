<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [RC low-pass filter](../../../../rc-low-pass-filter.md) satisfies $RC\,\dot V_{\mathrm{out}}+V_{\mathrm{out}}=V$ for $t>0$. With zero initial capacitor voltage, the [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V_{\mathrm{out}}(t)=V\bigl(1-e^{-t/(RC)}\bigr),\quad t\geq0.}
$$

The output is continuous at switching and approaches $V$ with [time constant](../../../../time-constant.md) $RC$.
