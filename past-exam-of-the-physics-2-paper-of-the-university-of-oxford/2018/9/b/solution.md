<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The half-power amplitude condition for the [RC low-pass filter](../../../../rc-low-pass-filter.md) gives $1+(\omega RC)^2=2$. Since [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is positive,

$$
\boxed{\omega_c=\frac1{RC}.}
$$

At this [cutoff angular frequency](../../../../cutoff-angular-frequency.md), the output amplitude is smaller by $1/\sqrt2$ and the [phase shift](../../../../phase-shift.md) is $-\pi/4$.
