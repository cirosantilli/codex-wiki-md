<h1 id="9/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an [inductor](../../../../inductor.md), $V=L\,dI_L/dt$. In the steady AC solution with no added constant [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md),

$$
\boxed{Z_L=i\omega L,\qquad I_L(t)=\frac{V_0}{\omega L}\sin\omega t=\frac{V_0}{\omega L}\cos(\omega t-\pi/2).}
$$

The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) **lags the voltage by $\pi/2$**. An ideal [inductor](../../../../inductor.md) can also sustain an arbitrary DC current, fixed by initial conditions; it is excluded from this steady sinusoidal comparison.
