<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $b<r<a$, a coaxial [Gauss surface](../../../../gaussian-surface.md) of length $l$ encloses net charge $Q$: the sphere and the cavity-wall charge cancel, leaving $+Q$ on the outside of the inner conductor. In the long-cylinder approximation,

$$
E_r(r)\,2\pi rl=\frac Q{\epsilon_0},\qquad
\boxed{E_r(r)=\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0lr}=\frac{2RV}{lr}.}
$$

The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) points radially outward for $V>0$. End effects and axial nonuniformity near the openings are excluded by the ideal [coaxial cylindrical capacitor](../../../../coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) approximation.
