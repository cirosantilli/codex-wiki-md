<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The grounded outer cylinder fixes $\Phi(a)=0$. Integrating the annular [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) inward gives

$$
\boxed{\Phi(b)=\int_b^aE_r(r)\,dr=\frac{2RV}{l}\log\frac ab.}
$$

Equivalently, the [coaxial cylindrical capacitor](../../../../coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) has $C_{\mathrm{cyl}}=2\pi\epsilon_0l/\log(a/b)$ and carries charge $Q=4\pi\epsilon_0RV$, so $\Phi(b)=Q/C_{\mathrm{cyl}}$. The original sphere potential $V$ is used to infer its conserved charge; it is not the final potential of the sphere or of the inner cylinder.
