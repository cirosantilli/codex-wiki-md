<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For steady currents in vacuum, [Ampère's circuital law](../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) is

$$
\oint_C B\cdot d\ell=\mu_0I_{\mathrm{enc}}.
$$

Here $C$ is an oriented closed [curve](../../../curve.md), $d\ell$ its tangent line element, and $I_{\mathrm{enc}}$ the signed [electric current](../../../electric-current.md) through a spanning [surface](../../../topological-surface-split.md) oriented by the [right-hand rule](../../../right-hand-rule.md). A circular contour about a straight infinite wire has constant tangential [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md), so $B(r)=\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$. The [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md) on a length $L$ of the other wire is $IL\hat\ell\times B$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac FL=\frac{\mu_0I^2}{2\pi r},\qquad\text{the force is attractive}.}
$$

The [right-hand rule](../../../right-hand-rule.md) makes the force on each parallel, same-direction current point toward the other wire.
