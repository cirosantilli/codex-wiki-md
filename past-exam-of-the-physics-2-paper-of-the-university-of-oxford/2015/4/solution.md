<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Choose the entry point as $(0,0)$, the initial [velocity](../../../velocity.md) as $v\hat{\mathbf x}$, and the slit as $(d,a)$, with $a,d>0$. A [magnetic field](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) perpendicular to the page changes the direction of the [velocity](../../../velocity.md), while the [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md) does no [work](../../../work.md). For positive $q$, an upward initial [force](../../../force-split.md) requires the [magnetic flux density](../../../magnetic-field-split.md) to point into the page. The resulting [circular motion](../../../circular-motion.md) has centre $(0,r)$, since its initial tangent is horizontal.

The slit must lie on the [circle](../../../circle.md), giving $d^2+(a-r)^2=r^2$ and hence $r=(d^2+a^2)/(2a)$. Equating the [centripetal force](../../../centripetal-force.md) to the magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](../../../lorentz-force.md), $mv^2/r=|q|vB$, gives

$$
\boxed{|B|=\frac{2mva}{|q|(d^2+a^2)},\qquad \mathbf B=-\frac{2mva}{q(d^2+a^2)}\hat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

A negative [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) requires the opposite direction of [magnetic flux density](../../../magnetic-field-split.md). These expressions assume nonrelativistic [motion](../../../motion.md) and negligible energy loss through [electromagnetic radiation](../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md).

For an opaque screen, reaching the slit must be the _first_ encounter with $x=d$. The [velocity](../../../velocity.md) there has $v_x/v=(d^2-a^2)/(d^2+a^2)$, so passage from left to right requires $a<d$, as in the drawn arrangement. At $a=d$ the [trajectory](../../../trajectory.md) merely touches the screen. If $a>d$, the same [circle](../../../circle.md) first meets the screen at height $d^2/a<a$, where the particle is blocked; its later passage through $(d,a)$ does not solve the opaque-screen problem. This qualification distinguishes a geometric [circle](../../../circle.md) through two points from an accessible slit [trajectory](../../../trajectory.md).
