<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Follow the branch of a [light ray](../../../../light-ray.md) that undergoes one internal [specular reflection](../../../../specular-reflection.md) in the spherical drop. At entry, [refraction](../../../../refraction.md) turns its direction by $\alpha-\beta$. The internal [specular reflection](../../../../specular-reflection.md) turns it by $\pi-2\beta$, because the radius is the [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md) and the incidence and reflection angles are both $\beta$. At exit the second [refraction](../../../../refraction.md) contributes another $\alpha-\beta$. Thus the total [deviation angle](../../../../deviation-angle.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta=2(\alpha-\beta)+(\pi-2\beta)=\pi+2\alpha-4\beta.}
$$

[Snell's law](../../../../snell-s-law.md) at entry gives $\sin\alpha=n\sin\beta$, taking the surrounding air's [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) as one. The same internal angle at successive encounters follows from the [isosceles triangles](../../../../isosceles-triangle.md) formed by each chord and the two radii. Internal [specular reflection](../../../../specular-reflection.md) here need not be [total internal reflection](../../../../total-internal-reflection.md); the transmitted branch at that encounter does not belong to the one-reflection [trajectory](../../../../trajectory.md).
