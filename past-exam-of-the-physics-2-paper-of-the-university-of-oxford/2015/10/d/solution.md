<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At [terminal velocity](../../../../terminal-velocity.md), the upward magnetic [force](../../../../force-split.md) balances the [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). Substitute the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) from the preceding solution:

$$
Mg=IaB=\frac{B^2a^2}{R}v_s,\qquad M=\rho_m\pi a d_w^2,\qquad R=\frac{16\rho_ca}{\pi d_w^2}.
$$

The geometric factors cancel, giving

$$
\boxed{v_s=\frac{MgR}{B^2a^2}=\frac{16\rho_m\rho_cg}{B^2}=2.39\times10^{-2}\ {\rm m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

Here $g=9.81\ {\rm m\,s^{-2}}$, $B=1\ {\rm T}$, $\rho_m=8960\ {\rm kg\,m^{-3}}$ and $\rho_c=1.7\times10^{-8}\ {\rm \Omega\,m}$. As numerical checks, the specified $a=0.08\ {\rm m}$ and $d_w=0.001\ {\rm m}$ give $M=2.252\times10^{-3}\ {\rm kg}$, $R=6.926\times10^{-3}\ {\rm \Omega}$ and $I_s=0.276\ {\rm A}$.

The gravitational [power](../../../../power.md) lost, $Mgv_s$, equals the [Joule heating](../../../../joule-heating.md) $I_s^2R$, consistent with [energy conservation](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md). This [terminal velocity](../../../../terminal-velocity.md) applies only while the top arm remains in the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md); after it exits, the magnetic braking ceases, apart from any brief [inductive transient](../../../../inductive-transient.md).
