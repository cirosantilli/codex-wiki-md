<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume a passive linear [electrical circuit](../../../../electrical-circuit-split.md) in sinusoidal steady state, with transients absent and $V_0$ the _peak_ [voltage](../../../../voltage.md). Write $Z=|Z|e^{i\phi}=R+iX$. The [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is $I(t)=I_0\cos(\omega t-\phi)$, with $I_0=V_0/|Z|$. Average the instantaneous [electric power](../../../../electric-power.md) $V(t)I(t)$ over a full period. The double-frequency term averages to zero, leaving

$$
\langle P\rangle=\frac{V_0I_0}{2}\cos\phi,\qquad |Z|^2=R^2+X^2,\qquad \cos\phi=\frac R{|Z|}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle P\rangle=\frac{V_0^2R}{2(R^2+X^2)}=\frac12 I_0^2R.}
$$

Equivalently, [root mean square](../../../../root-mean-square.md) values give $\langle P\rangle=V_{\rm rms}I_{\rm rms}\cos\phi$. The [reactance](../../../../electrical-reactance.md) exchanges stored [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) or [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) energy with the source, whereas the real part produces [Joule heating](../../../../joule-heating.md). A purely imaginary [complex impedance](../../../../electrical-impedance.md) dissipates no mean [electric power](../../../../electric-power.md) under these ideal assumptions.
