<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) states that the outward [electric flux](../../../../electric-flux.md) through a closed [surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is the enclosed [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) divided by the [vacuum permittivity](../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md):

$$
\oint_S\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf S=\frac{Q_{\rm enc}}{\epsilon_0},\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}.
$$

In [electrostatics](../../../../electrostatics-split.md), the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is the negative [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) of the [electrostatic potential](../../../../electric-potential.md), $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$. Substitution into the [differential equation](../../../../differential-equation-split.md) form of [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives [Poisson's equation](../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) $\nabla^2V=-\rho/\epsilon_0$. Consequently, in a region containing no [charge density](../../../../charge-density.md),

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2V=0,}
$$

which is [Laplace's equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md). Charges on the bounding [conductors](../../../../electrical-conductor.md) enter through the [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md); they need not vanish for [Laplace's equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md) to hold inside the empty gap.
