<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Neglect [fringing field](../../../../fringing-field.md) near the edges of the [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md). Put the plates at $z=0,d$, with prescribed [electric potentials](../../../../electric-potential.md) $V_A,V_B$. [Translational symmetry parallel to a plane](../../../../translational-symmetry-parallel-to-a-plane.md) makes the [electrostatic potential](../../../../electric-potential.md) independent of the coordinates parallel to the plates. Thus [Laplace's equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md) reduces to $d^2V/dz^2=0$. Its [general solution](../../../../general-solution.md) is $V=A+Bz$, and the two [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) determine both constants:

$$
\boxed{V(z)=V_A+\frac{V_B-V_A}{d}z,\qquad \mathbf E=\frac{V_A-V_B}{d}\hat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is uniform and points toward decreasing [electric potential](../../../../electric-potential.md). No plate [voltages](../../../../voltage.md) are specified, so a numerical [electric potential](../../../../electric-potential.md) cannot be fixed; choosing one plate as the reference only fixes the arbitrary additive constant.
