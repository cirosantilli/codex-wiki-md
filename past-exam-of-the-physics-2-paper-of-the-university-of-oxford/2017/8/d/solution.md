<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With $V=+20\,\mathrm V$ in the outer-minus-inner convention, $\eta=\ln1.5=0.405465$ and the radial [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) at the orbit is $E_r=-986.52\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}$. The signed roots are

$$
u_\pm=\frac{-eBr\pm\sqrt{e^2B^2r^2+4meV/\eta}}{2m}.
$$

Using the [proton](../../../../proton.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) and charge gives

$$
\boxed{u_+=4.88\times10^4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad
u_-=-9.67\times10^4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The [speeds](../../../../speed.md) are therefore $48.8$ and $96.7\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, in opposite senses of rotation. Both are nonrelativistic. The slower counterclockwise orbit has an outward magnetic [force](../../../../force-split.md); the faster clockwise orbit has an inward magnetic [force](../../../../force-split.md). Reversing $\mathbf B$ interchanges the senses, while reversing the electrode polarity changes the quadratic and may eliminate real orbits; its sign must therefore be specified.
