<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [electromagnetic energy density](../../../../electromagnetic-energy-density.md) in [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) is $u_E=\epsilon_0E^2/2$. For a length $\ell_z$ of the coaxial [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md), integrate over annular shells:

$$
U=\int_{r_1}^{r_2}\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\frac{V^2}{\eta^2r^2}(2\pi r\ell_z)\,dr
=\frac{\pi\epsilon_0\ell_zV^2}{\eta^2}\ln\frac{r_2}{r_1}.
$$

Since $C=C'\ell_z=2\pi\epsilon_0\ell_z/\eta$, this becomes

$$
\boxed{U=\frac12CV^2.}
$$

The integral is over the inter-cylinder [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md), and gives the stored [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), independently of which electrode is assigned zero [electric potential](../../../../electric-potential.md).
