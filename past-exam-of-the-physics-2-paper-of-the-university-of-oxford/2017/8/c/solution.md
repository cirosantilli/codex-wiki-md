<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $\mathbf B=B\hat{\mathbf z}$ and a signed tangential [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v=u\hat{\boldsymbol\phi}$, with $u>0$ counterclockwise viewed from $+z$. The magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is $euB\hat{\mathbf r}$, while the electric part is $-eV\hat{\mathbf r}/(\eta r)$. The radial [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) for a [circular orbit](../../../../circular-orbit.md) is $-u^2/r$, regardless of the sign of $u$. Consequently

$$
-\frac{mu^2}{r}=e\left(-\frac{V}{\eta r}+uB\right),\qquad
\boxed{mu^2+eBr\,u-\frac{eV}{\eta}=0.}
$$

One valid signed-velocity convention therefore has $a=m$, $b=eBr$, $c=-eV/\ln(r_2/r_1)$. If $v$ is required to be a nonnegative [speed](../../../../speed.md), the two senses of rotation instead satisfy

$$
\boxed{mv^2\pm eBr\,v-\frac{eV}{\eta}=0,}
$$

with the plus sign for counterclockwise motion. For positive $V$, the electric [force](../../../../force-split.md) is inward, so it can supply [circular motion](../../../../circular-motion.md) in either direction; in one direction the magnetic [force](../../../../force-split.md) opposes it, while in the other it helps. A signed negative root must not be discarded as an unphysical [speed](../../../../speed.md); it denotes the opposite orbit direction.
