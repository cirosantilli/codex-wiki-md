<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) declares two equal incoherent point images just resolved when the central maximum of one lies at the first minimum of the other's [point spread function](../../../../point-spread-function-split.md). For a single rectangular slit, the first minimum occurs at $w\sin\theta_1=\lambda$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Delta\theta_R=\sin^{-1}(\lambda/w)\simeq\lambda/w.}
$$

This is a resolution in the direction across the slit width, and the last approximation requires $\lambda\ll w$. The central maximum's full width between its two first minima is approximately $2\lambda/w$; it is not the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md)'s separation. Two coherent sources can interfere with one another, so a resolution statement for them also needs their relative [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md).
