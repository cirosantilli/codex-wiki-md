<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the slit run over $-w/2<x<w/2$ and let its coherent illumination have constant complex [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $A_0$. In a direction making angle $\theta$ to the normal, the relative propagation [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) from position $x$ is $-kx\sin\theta$, with $k=2\pi/\lambda$. Summing the secondary [amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) gives

$$
\mathcal A(\theta)\propto A_0\int_{-w/2}^{w/2}e^{-ikx\sin\theta}\,dx
=A_0w\frac{\sin\beta}{\beta},\qquad
\beta=\frac{\pi w\sin\theta}{\lambda}.
$$

The [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) is proportional to the squared modulus, hence within the usual scalar [Fraunhofer diffraction](../../../../fraunhofer-diffraction.md) approximation

$$
\boxed{I(\theta)=I(0)\left(\frac{\sin\beta}{\beta}\right)^2.}
$$

The central value is defined by the continuous limit $\sin\beta/\beta\to1$. The nonzero minima satisfy $w\sin\theta=m\lambda$ for integer $m\ne0$. This expression neglects the slowly varying obliquity factor and uses a uniform slit; it describes the narrow-angle [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) pattern for $w\gg\lambda$.
