<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The six faces subtend equal [solid angles](../../../../solid-angle.md) at the central [point charge](../../../../point-charge.md). [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) fixes their total outward [electric flux](../../../../electric-flux.md) at $q/\epsilon_0$. By the cube's [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md),

$$
\boxed{\Phi_{\rm each\ face}=\frac{q}{6\epsilon_0}.}
$$

For negative $q$ the same signed expression represents inward [electric flux](../../../../electric-flux.md). The result is independent of the side length.
