<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a long air-cored [solenoid](../../../../solenoid.md), [Ampère's law](../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) applied to a rectangular loop running inside and outside the winding gives $B\ell\simeq\mu_0NI$, with the external [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) negligible away from the ends. Hence $B=\mu_0nI$ along the winding axis, where $n=N/\ell$. Each turn links [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md) $BA$ with $A=\pi a^2$, so the total [magnetic flux linkage](../../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) is $NBA=LI$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{B=\mu_0\frac N\ell I,\qquad
L=\mu_0\frac{N^2\pi a^2}{\ell}=\mu_0n^2\pi a^2\ell.}
$$

Here $n=2000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, $a=0.010\,\mathrm m$ and $\ell=0.50\,\mathrm m$, so $N=1000$ and

$$
\boxed{L=7.90\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm H=0.790\,\mathrm{mH}.}
$$

The value uses the stated long-solenoid approximation and ignores end corrections and winding resistance.
