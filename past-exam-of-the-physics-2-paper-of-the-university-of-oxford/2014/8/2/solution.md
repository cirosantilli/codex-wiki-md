<h1 id="8/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Put charges $+Q$ and $-Q$ on the concentric conductors. [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $E(r)=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$ between them, $a<r<2a$. The potential difference is

$$
\Delta V=\int_a^{2a}\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}\,dr
=\frac{Q}{8\pi\epsilon_0a}.
$$

The [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md) of this vacuum [spherical capacitor](../../../../spherical-capacitor.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=8\pi\epsilon_0a.}
$$

The conductors are equipotentials; a uniform dielectric filling would replace $\epsilon_0$ by its [permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md).
