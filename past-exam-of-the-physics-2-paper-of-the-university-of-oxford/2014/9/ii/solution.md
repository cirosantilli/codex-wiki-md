<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The constant particular solution is $A=V/(4R)$, and the homogeneous equation has decay rate $2R/L$. Imposing $I_1(0)=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{I_1(t)=\frac{V}{4R}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad A=\frac V{4R},\quad\tau=\frac L{2R}.}
$$

As a separate check, the [Thévenin equivalent](../../../../thevenin-equivalent.md) seen by $L$ has open-circuit voltage $V/2$ and resistance $R+(2R\parallel2R)=2R$, again giving $\tau=L/(2R)$.
