↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Faraday's law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) states that the induced [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) equals minus the rate of change of linked [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=-\frac{d(N\Phi)}{dt}.}
$$

The minus sign expresses [Lenz's law](../../../../lenz-s-law.md): the induced [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) acts to oppose the change in [magnetic flux](../../../../magnetic-flux.md). For a fixed contour this is equivalent to $\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=-d\int\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S/dt$.
