<h1 id="10/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The phase $kz-\omega t$ describes propagation along $+z$. With $\psi=kz-\omega t$, the vacuum [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) has

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac1c\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf E=\frac Ac(-3\hat{\mathbf x}+2\hat{\mathbf y})\sin\psi.}
$$

Thus $B_x=-3A\sin\psi/c$, $B_y=2A\sin\psi/c$, and $B_z=0$. The field amplitudes are $E_0=\sqrt{13}|A|$ and $B_0=\sqrt{13}|A|/c$, giving

$$
\boxed{E_0/B_0=c.}
$$

The instantaneous magnitude ratio also equals $c$ wherever the fields are nonzero; at a node both vanish together. Their [Poynting vector](../../../../poynting-vector.md) points along $+z$, checking the relative component signs.
