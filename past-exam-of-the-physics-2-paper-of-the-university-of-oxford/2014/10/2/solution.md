<h1 id="10/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For a charging [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md) of area $A$, neglect fringing. The field in the gap is $E=Q/(\epsilon_0A)$, so the [displacement current](../../../../displacement-current.md) through the gap is

$$
\boxed{I_D=\epsilon_0\frac{d}{dt}\int\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf S=\epsilon_0A\frac{dE}{dt}=\frac{dQ}{dt}=I.}
$$

An [Amperian loop](../../../../amperian-loop.md) around the feed wire can bound a surface cutting the wire, which has conduction current $I$, or a bulging surface passing through the dielectric gap, which has no conduction current. Including displacement current makes both surfaces give the same magnetic circulation. It is also required by [charge conservation](../../../../charge-conservation.md): the divergence of the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) and [Gauss's law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) yields $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J+\partial\rho/\partial t=0$, the [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md).
