<h1 id="4/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

After the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is removed, the uniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) preserves the speed. Decompose the instantaneous initial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) as $\mathbf v_0=\mathbf v_\parallel+\mathbf v_\perp$, with $\hat{\mathbf b}=\mathbf B/B$ and signed $\Omega=qB/m$. Then

$$
\mathbf v(t)=\mathbf v_\parallel+\mathbf v_\perp\cos\Omega t+(\mathbf v_\perp\times\hat{\mathbf b})\sin\Omega t,
$$

and, integrating,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r(t)=\mathbf r_0+\mathbf v_\parallel t+\frac{\mathbf v_\perp}{\Omega}\sin\Omega t+\frac{\mathbf v_\perp\times\hat{\mathbf b}}{\Omega}(1-\cos\Omega t).}
$$

This is a [helical motion in a uniform magnetic field](../../../../helical-motion-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) of radius $m|\mathbf v_\perp|/(|q|B)$ and angular frequency $|q|B/m$. It is a circle if $\mathbf v_\parallel=0$, and a straight line if $\mathbf v_\perp=0$. The formula assumes a uniform, nonzero field and nonrelativistic motion.
