# Scanning tunneling microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Tunnel junction](tunnel-junction.md)

[Scanning tunneling microscopy](scanning-tunneling-microscopy.md) uses a [scanning tunneling microscope](scanning-tunneling-microscope.md) to map a surface from the [electric current](electric-current.md) across a [tunnel junction](tunnel-junction.md). In constant-current operation, [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) adjusts the tip height to maintain a chosen [electric current](electric-current.md). The [current-distance test of vacuum tunnelling](current-distance-test-of-vacuum-tunnelling.md) explains the underlying distance sensitivity and its measurement limits.

The image records the response of a [tunnel junction](tunnel-junction.md), rather than photographing [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). Its apparent height can reveal both surface structure and the [local density of states](local-density-of-states.md); distinguishing them is part of interpreting the measurement.[p. 391, Fig. 2; p. 396, Fig. 5](scanning-tunneling-microscopy-from-birth-to-adolescence-by-gerd-binnig-and-others.md)
