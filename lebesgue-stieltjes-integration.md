<h1 id="lebesgue-stieltjes-integration">Lebesgue–Stieltjes integration</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue-Stieltjes measure](lebesgue-stieltjes-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue–Stieltjes_integration)

The Lebesgue–Stieltjes integral $\int f\,dF$ integrates $f$ against the [Lebesgue-Stieltjes measure](lebesgue-stieltjes-measure.md) generated by an increasing right-continuous function $F$. An atom at $x$ contributes $f(x)\Delta F(x)$.
