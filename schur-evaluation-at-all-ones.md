# Schur evaluation at all ones

↑ **Parent:** [Bialternant formula](bialternant-formula.md)

Put $b_i=\lambda_i+N-i$. In the [bialternant formula](bialternant-formula.md), substitute $x_j=e^{ty_j}$ with distinct $y_j$ and compare the first nonzero [determinant](determinant.md) coefficients as $t\to0$. They arise from the distinct degrees $0,\ldots,N-1$ in the exponential expansions. The common factor in the $y_j$ and the factorials cancel, leaving the product of differences of the $b_i$ divided by the differences of $N-i$. This proves the displayed integral specialization without dividing by a vanishing Vandermonde value.
