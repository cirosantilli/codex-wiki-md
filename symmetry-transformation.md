# Symmetry transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group](symmetry-group.md)

A symmetry transformation is a [bijection](bijection.md) preserving the data that define the object's [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md). Its individual [group elements](group-element.md) are [symmetry transformations](symmetry-transformation.md); the [group operation](group-operation.md) combines them by [function composition](function-composition.md).
