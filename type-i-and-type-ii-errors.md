# Type I and type II errors

↑ **Parent:** [Binary hypothesis testing in information theory](binary-hypothesis-testing-in-information-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_and_type_II_errors)

For a [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) $H_0$, a [type I error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) rejects $H_0$ when it is true, while a [type II error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) does not reject $H_0$ when the alternative is true. If a test does not reject $H_0$ on an event $A$, their [conditional probabilities](conditional-probability.md) are $\alpha=P_0(A^c)$ and $\beta=P_1(A)$. Nonrejection does not establish the truth of the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md).
