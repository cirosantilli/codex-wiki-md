# Charge conjugation of a non-Abelian gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

Write a matrix-valued [gauge field](gauge-field.md) $\mathcal A_\mu=A_\mu^aT^a$ in the [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md). [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) sends it to $-\mathcal A_\mu^T$ because reordering the fermions transposes both the spinor and internal matrices. The identity $[A^T,B^T]=-[A,B]^T$ then sends the [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) to $-\mathcal F_{\mu\nu}^T$. This distinction prevents treating all color components as individually C-odd.
