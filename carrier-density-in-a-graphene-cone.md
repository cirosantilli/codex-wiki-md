# Carrier density in a graphene cone

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac valleys in graphene](dirac-valleys-in-graphene.md)

Assume zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), the linear [Dirac cone](dirac-cone.md), and both real-[spin](spin.md) states and both [electronic valleys](electronic-valley.md). Define $n$ as excess [electron](electron.md) number per unit [area](surface-area.md), negative for an excess of [electron holes](electron-hole.md), relative to neutrality. A filled circular region of radius $k_F$ in [wave vector](wavevector.md) space contains

$$
|n|=4\frac{\pi k_F^2}{(2\pi)^2}=\frac{k_F^2}{\pi},\qquad |E_F|=\hbar v_F k_F.
$$

Thus the excess [carrier density](carrier-density.md) grows quadratically with the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), unlike the constant [density of states](density-of-states.md) of a parabolic two-dimensional band. Differentiating gives the positive [density of states](density-of-states.md) per unit [area](surface-area.md), $D(E)=2|E|/[\pi(\hbar v_F)^2]$. Both the geometry of occupied states and the fourfold degeneracy matter to the coefficient.
