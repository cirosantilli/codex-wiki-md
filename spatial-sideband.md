# Spatial sideband

↑ **Parent:** [Sideband instability](sideband-instability.md)

A spatial sideband is a Fourier perturbation with [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) close to a pattern's carrier [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), such as $k_c+p$ or $k_c-p$. A real physical field also includes the conjugate Fourier component. Linearizing a nonlinear complex [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md) can couple the two displaced components, so testing only a single uniform amplitude misses their joint [linear stability](linear-stability.md) problem.
