# Pullback of a covariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md)

The [pullback of a covariant tensor](pullback-of-a-covariant-tensor.md) composes each of its vector arguments with the [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md). It takes a type $(0,s)$ tensor on the target to one on the source. Antisymmetry is not required; the [pullback of a differential form](pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) is the alternating special case. If the target vectors are projected onto the image tangent space first, evaluating the pullback is unchanged.
