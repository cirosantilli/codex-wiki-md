# Influence function of a U-statistic

↑ **Parent:** [U-statistic](u-statistic.md)

For a symmetric degree-$m$ [U-statistic kernel](kernel-of-a-u-statistic.md), the population functional is $T(F)=\int h\,dF^m$. Contaminating each of the $m$ identical distribution arguments and differentiating gives the displayed [influence function](influence-function.md). It equals $m$ times the first [Hoeffding projection](hoeffding-projection.md) and gives leading [asymptotic variance](asymptotic-variance.md) $m^2\operatorname{Var}(h_1(X))/n$. If that projection vanishes, the usual root-$n$ normal limit degenerates and higher-order projections determine the limiting law.
