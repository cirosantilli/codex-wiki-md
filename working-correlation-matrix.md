# Working correlation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized estimating equation](generalized-estimating-equation.md)

A [working correlation matrix](working-correlation-matrix.md) $R_i(\alpha)$ is a positive-definite proposed correlation structure used to weight a [generalized estimating equation](generalized-estimating-equation.md), with $A_i$ the diagonal matrix of marginal [variances](variance-split.md). It need not equal the actual correlation matrix. Correct marginal means and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) sampling clusters keep the estimating equations unbiased; a cluster-level [sandwich covariance matrix](sandwich-covariance-matrix.md) estimates their actual large-sample [covariance](covariance.md). For three exchangeable observations, positive definiteness requires $-1/2<\rho<1$.
