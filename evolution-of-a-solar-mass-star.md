# Evolution of a solar-mass star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

Core hydrogen fusion supports the [main sequence](main-sequence.md). Hydrogen-shell fusion around an inert helium core then produces the [subgiant](subgiant.md) and [red giant](red-giant.md) phases. Core helium fusion gives the [red clump](red-clump.md), followed by hydrogen- and helium-shell burning on the [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md). Envelope loss can expose a hot [planetary nebula](planetary-nebula.md) nucleus before a carbon-oxygen [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) cools. This sequence concerns a roughly solar-mass star; initial [mass](mass.md), composition and mass loss affect detailed tracks and timescales.
