# Units of Q of square root two

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)

The unit $\varepsilon=1+\sqrt2$ has norm minus one and is the least unit greater than one in the positive real embedding. [Dirichlet unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]^\times
=\{\mathord\pm(1+\sqrt2)^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
$$
