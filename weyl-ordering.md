# Weyl ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization](canonical-quantization.md)

Weyl ordering symmetrizes position and momentum factors in a classical polynomial before promoting them to an operator. Its phase-space symbol corresponds to midpoint [time slicing of a phase-space path integral](time-slicing-of-a-phase-space-path-integral.md). In particular, the Weyl symbol of $a^\dagger a$ is $\bar\psi\psi-1/2$, whereas its [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) symbol is $\bar\psi\psi$.
