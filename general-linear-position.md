# General linear position

↑ **Parent:** [Projective space](projective-space-split.md)

Points in $\mathbb P^n$ are in general linear position when no $k+1$ of them lie in a projective subspace of dimension less than $k$ for $k\leq n$. Equivalently, every selection of at most $n+1$ representing vectors is [linearly independent](linear-independence.md).
