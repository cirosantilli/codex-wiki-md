# Root hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The hyperplane perpendicular to a [root of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) is its root hyperplane, interpreted as the kernel of the associated linear functional on the Cartan space. The corresponding [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) is $s_\alpha(v)=v-2\langle v,\alpha\rangle\alpha/\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle$. For the [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md), the four distinct hyperplanes are the two coordinate axes and the two diagonals; their reflections generate the signed-permutation [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) of order eight.
