# Weber (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weber_(unit))

The [weber](weber-unit.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), with $1\,\mathrm{Wb}=1\,\mathrm{T\,m^2}$. A uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) of $1\,\mathrm T$ crossing a flat [surface](topological-surface.md) of [area](surface-area.md) $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$ along its chosen [unit normal](unit-normal.md) gives $1\,\mathrm{Wb}$. [OpenStax, magnetic-flux units](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-1-faradays-law).
