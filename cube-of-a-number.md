# Cube of a number

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation](exponentiation.md)

The [cube of a number](cube-of-a-number.md) $x$ is $x^3=x\cdot x\cdot x$, using the [exponent](exponent.md) three. For example, $2^3=8$ and $(-2)^3=-8$.
