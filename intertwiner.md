# Intertwiner

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

An intertwiner between [group representations](group-representation.md) $\rho$ on $V$ and $\sigma$ on $W$ is a [linear map](linear-map.md) $A:V\to W$ satisfying $A\rho(x)=\sigma(x)A$ for every group element. The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) controls these maps between [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md).
