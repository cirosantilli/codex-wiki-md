# Past null infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Null infinity](null-infinity.md)

[Past null infinity](past-null-infinity.md) is the ideal boundary from which incoming [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) arrive in an [asymptotically flat spacetime](asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md). In the [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md) it is $T-R=-\pi$, with the retarded coordinate tending to $-\infty$ and the advanced coordinate finite.
