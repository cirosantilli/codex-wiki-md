# Rate equation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction network](chemical-reaction-network.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_equation)

A reaction-rate equation is an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) for continuously varying species concentrations. Under the [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md), its reaction rates are monomials in those concentrations.
