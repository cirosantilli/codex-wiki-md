# Hermite-Hadamard inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

For a [continuous](continuous-function.md) [convex function](convex-function.md) on $[a,b]$, where $a<b$, its integral average lies between its midpoint value and the average of its endpoint values. For a [concave function](concave-function.md) both [inequalities](inequality-mathematics.md) reverse. The bound involving endpoints follows by comparison with the [affine function](affine-function.md) joining them and [monotonicity of the Riemann integral](monotonicity-of-the-riemann-integral.md). The midpoint bound follows from the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) on equal-weight midpoint tags and the [Riemann sum convergence theorem](riemann-sum-convergence-theorem.md).
