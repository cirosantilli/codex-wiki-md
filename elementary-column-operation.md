# Elementary column operation

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

An [elementary column operation](elementary-column-operation.md) interchanges two columns of a [matrix](matrix.md), scales one by a nonzero scalar, or adds a multiple of another column. It is an [elementary row operation](elementary-row-operation.md) on the transpose. Hence it has the same three effects on the [determinant](determinant.md): changing sign, multiplying by the scale factor, or leaving the [determinant](determinant.md) unchanged.
