<h1 id="cartan-s-criterion">Cartan's criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan's_criterion)

For a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) over a field of characteristic zero, [Cartan's criterion](cartan-s-criterion.md) characterizes a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md) by vanishing of its [Killing form](killing-form.md) on $\mathfrak g\times[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]$, and a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) by nondegeneracy of that form. The [Cartan solvability criterion](cartan-solvability-criterion.md) and [Cartan criterion for semisimplicity](cartan-criterion-for-semisimplicity.md) state the two distinct tests.
