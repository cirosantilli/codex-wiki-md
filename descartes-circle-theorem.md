# Descartes' circle theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Circle packing](circle-packing.md)

Four mutually [tangent circles](tangent-circles.md) with signed curvatures $k_i$ satisfy $(k_1+k_2+k_3+k_4)^2=2(k_1^2+k_2^2+k_3^2+k_4^2)$. Curvature is the reciprocal of the [radius](radius.md), with an enclosing [circle](circle.md) assigned negative curvature. Given three curvatures, solving the [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md) gives $k_4=k_1+k_2+k_3\pm2\sqrt{k_1k_2+k_2k_3+k_3k_1}$, providing the two completions of the configuration.
