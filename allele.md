# Allele

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allele)

An [allele](allele.md) is one of the alternative inherited states at a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md), often distinguished by a difference in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence. A population can contain many [alleles](allele.md), although a two-copy [diploid](diploid.md) individual carries at most two at that [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md), one inherited from each parent. See [NHGRI: allele](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Allele).

Count copies, not just individuals, when computing [allele frequency](allele-frequency.md): in 100 individuals with [genotype](genotype.md) counts $49\ AA$, $42\ Aa$, $9\ aa$, there are $2(49)+42=140$ copies of $A$ among 200, so $p_A=0.70$. The [heterozygotes](heterozygote.md) contribute to both [allele](allele.md) counts. This converts observed [genotypes](genotype.md) into the population quantity needed by the [Hardy-Weinberg principle](hardy-weinberg-principle.md).
