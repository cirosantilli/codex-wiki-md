# Allele

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allele)

An allele is one of the alternative inherited states at a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md). A diploid individual normally carries two copies at an autosomal locus, one inherited from each parent.
