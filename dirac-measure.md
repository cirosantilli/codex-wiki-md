# Dirac measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_measure)

The Dirac [probability measure](probability-measure.md) at $x$ satisfies $\delta_x(A)=1$ if $x\in A$ and zero otherwise. Its [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) under a measurable map $T$ is $T_\#\delta_x=\delta_{T(x)}$.
