# Lowest levels of a fully transverse ND bosonic string

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann-Dirichlet string zero-point energy](neumann-dirichlet-string-zero-point-energy.md)

With 24 physical [half-integer open-string oscillators](half-integer-open-string-oscillator.md), the first three [string level operator](string-level-operator.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $0,1/2,1$. Their states are the vacuum, $a_{1/2}^{i\dagger}|0\rangle$, and $a_{1/2}^{i\dagger}a_{1/2}^{j\dagger}|0\rangle$. The [creation operators](creation-operator.md) commute, so the third level is the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) of the 24-dimensional transverse vector, of dimension $24\cdot25/2=300$. Its trace is a scalar and its trace-free part has dimension 299. Including the vacuum shift gives $\alpha'M^2=1/2,1,3/2$ when the two longitudinal directions are common directions with [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md). The representation labels refer to the surviving ND rotation group, not full unbroken target Lorentz symmetry.
