# Valuation of a rational differential

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md)

If $t$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) at a point $p$ of a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) and a nonzero rational differential is $\omega=f(t)\,dt$, then $\nu_p(\omega)=\nu_p(f)$. This integer is independent of the chosen uniformizer. Its positive and negative values are respectively the orders of the zero and pole of $\omega$ at $p$.
