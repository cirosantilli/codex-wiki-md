# Affine parameter change under conformal rescaling

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal preservation of null geodesic paths](conformal-preservation-of-null-geodesic-paths.md)

If $\widetilde g=\Lambda^2g$, a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) with [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) $\tau$ for $g$ has [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) $\widetilde\tau$ for $\widetilde g$ when $d\widetilde\tau/d\tau=C\Lambda^2$ along the curve. Indeed the [conformal change of the Levi-Civita connection](conformal-change-of-the-levi-civita-connection.md) gives $\widetilde\nabla_VV=2V(\log|\Lambda|)V$; rescaling $V$ by $d\tau/d\widetilde\tau$ cancels this tangential acceleration. Constant $C>0$ preserves the direction of parametrization. Agreement of unparametrized [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) does not imply agreement of their [affine parameters](affine-parameter.md) or completeness.
