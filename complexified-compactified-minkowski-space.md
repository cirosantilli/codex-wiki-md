# Complexified compactified Minkowski space

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md)

The four-complex-dimensional [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md) of two-planes in [twistor space](twistor-space.md). Its [Plücker embedding](plucker-embedding.md) is the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md). An affine chart consists of graphs $W_x=\{(ix\pi,\pi)\}$. A [Lorentzian reality structure on twistor space](lorentzian-reality-structure-on-twistor-space.md) or an [Euclidean reality structure on twistor space](euclidean-reality-structure-on-twistor-space.md) selects different four-real-dimensional compact spacetimes inside this same complex manifold.
