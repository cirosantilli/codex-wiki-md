<h1 id="randall-sundrum-model">Randall–Sundrum model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randall–Sundrum_model)

A warped five-dimensional geometry redshifts masses of fields localized on the distant [brane](brane.md): $m_{\rm IR}=e^{-kL}m_0$. For the usual doubled orbifold interval, integration of the [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md) gives $M_{\rm Pl}^2=M_5^3(1-e^{-2kL})/k$. Exponential redshift can generate a large separation of observable scales with a moderately sized dimensionless distance $kL$. Stabilizing the [radion](radion.md) remains a dynamical requirement.
