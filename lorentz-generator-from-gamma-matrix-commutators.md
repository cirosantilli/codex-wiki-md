# Lorentz generator from gamma-matrix commutators

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

The [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) gives $S^{\mu\nu}=\frac12\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu-\frac12\eta^{\mu\nu}I$. Reordering one more [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) gives $[S^{\mu\nu},\gamma^\rho]=\eta^{\nu\rho}\gamma^\mu-\eta^{\mu\rho}\gamma^\nu$. The [commutator derivation identity](commutator-derivation-identity.md) then gives

$$
[S^{\mu\nu},S^{\rho\sigma}]=\eta^{\nu\rho}S^{\mu\sigma}-\eta^{\mu\rho}S^{\nu\sigma}+\eta^{\nu\sigma}S^{\rho\mu}-\eta^{\mu\sigma}S^{\rho\nu},
$$

so these matrices represent the [Lorentz algebra](lorentz-algebra.md). This convention has no extra factor of $i$; the spatial generators are $-\frac i2\epsilon^{jkl}\operatorname{diag}(\sigma^l,\sigma^l)$.
