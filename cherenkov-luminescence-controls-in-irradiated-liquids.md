# Cherenkov luminescence controls in irradiated liquids

↑ **Parent:** [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md)

In his 1958 lecture, [Pavel Cherenkov](pavel-alekseyevich-cherenkov.md) recalls the 1934 reports by himself and [Sergey Vavilov](sergey-vavilov.md): [radium](radium.md) [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md) illuminated solutions and their solvents. Weak [light](light.md) was compared using dark-adapted human vision and threshold [photometry](photometry-optics.md). Heating and added [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) [quenching](quenching-fluorescence.md) agents left the solvent component and its [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) unchanged; parallel aqueous fluorescent controls visibly quenched. Its electric vibration direction was parallel to the exciting beam, unlike the compared polarized [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md). Further experiments attributed the emission to fast [electrons](electron.md) produced by [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md), rather than directly to [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md). These comparisons supported a mechanism distinct from ordinary [luminescence](luminescence.md).[1958 lecture, pp. 426–429: liquids, threshold photometry, parallel quenching and polarization controls; Columbia scan pp. 413–416](radiation-of-particles-moving-at-a-velocity-exceeding-that-of-light-and-some-of-the-possibilities-for-their-use-in-experimental-physics-by-pavel-alekseyevich-cherenkov.md)

The evidential step is the controlled comparison, rather than the mere presence of [light](light.md). A weak glow could include [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) from impurities; suppressing a known fluorescent control tests whether the intervention actually works. Visual threshold comparisons also require a stable observer and viewing conditions. These early controls do not themselves calibrate absolute [photon](photon.md) yield or supply a single-[electron](electron.md) angular scan. Its [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) comparison also does not by itself measure the [Cherenkov cone angle](cherenkov-cone-angle.md) of an individual [electron](electron.md).

[Ilya Frank](ilya-mikhailovich-frank.md) and [Igor Tamm](igor-yevgenyevich-tamm.md) supplied the theory in 1937; [Ilya Frank](ilya-mikhailovich-frank.md) also investigated [nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md) experimentally and later directed neutron research.[JINR, 23 October 2023, paragraphs on 1934 experiments, 1937 explanation, and neutron research](ilya-mikhailovich-frank-first-flnp-jinr-director-laureate-of-nobel-prize-by-joint-institute-for-nuclear-research.md) [Sergey Vavilov](sergey-vavilov.md), an [optics](optics-split.md) and [luminescence](luminescence.md) researcher and collaborator in the discovery, died in 1951. The 1958 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) went to [Pavel Cherenkov](pavel-alekseyevich-cherenkov.md), [Ilya Frank](ilya-mikhailovich-frank.md) and [Igor Tamm](igor-yevgenyevich-tamm.md); [Sergey Vavilov](sergey-vavilov.md) was not a recipient.[Russian Academy of Sciences, Sergey Ivanovich Vavilov: physical-optics and luminescence work; discovery and 1958 award after his death](sergey-ivanovich-vavilov-by-russian-academy-of-sciences.md)
