# Trit

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)

A ternary digit, taking one of three values. A [codeword](codeword.md) of length $l$ over a three-symbol [alphabet](alphabet.md) uses $l$ [trits](trit.md). If the trit is uniform its [information entropy](information-entropy-split.md) is $\log_2 3$ [bits](bit.md); an arbitrary ternary digit can have less [information entropy](information-entropy-split.md). [Expected codeword length](expected-codeword-length.md) in [trits](trit.md) must therefore be distinguished from source [information entropy](information-entropy-split.md) measured in [bits](bit.md).
