# Quadratic rotational Lie-Poisson dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md)

With the displayed signed [Lie-Poisson bracket](lie-poisson-bracket.md) and evolution $\dot F=\{F,H\}$, the equations are $\dot x_1=2(C-B)x_2x_3$, $\dot x_2=2(A-C)x_3x_1$, and $\dot x_3=2(B-A)x_1x_2$. The energy and the [Casimir function of a Poisson manifold](casimir-function-of-a-poisson-manifold.md) $x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2$ are conserved. For $A=1/(2I_1)$, $B=1/(2I_2)$, $C=1/(2I_3)$ with positive inertias, these are the [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md). Reversing the bracket while fixing $H$ reverses time orientation.
