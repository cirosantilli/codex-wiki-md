# Jacobi energy in a shearing sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Particle Lagrangian in a shearing sheet](particle-lagrangian-in-a-shearing-sheet.md)

The time-independent [particle Lagrangian in a shearing sheet](particle-lagrangian-in-a-shearing-sheet.md) has conserved rotating-frame energy

$$
\varepsilon_J=\sum_i\dot q_i\frac{\partial L_2}{\partial\dot q_i}-L_2
=\frac12(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)-q\Omega_0^2x^2+\frac12\Omega_z^2z^2.
$$

The velocity-linear [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) term cancels from this expression. Up to the reference-orbit constant, it is the second-order expansion of inertial [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) minus $\Omega_0$ times inertial [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md). Its negative radial tidal term allows [inelastic collisions](inelastic-collision.md) to lower the total energy while increasing the radial extent of a ring.
