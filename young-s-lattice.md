<h1 id="young-s-lattice">Young's lattice</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young's_lattice)

The Young lattice is a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) whose elements are integer partitions, including the empty partition. Each [partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md) is identified with its diagram, and the order is inclusion of [Young diagrams](young-diagram.md). A cover adds exactly one box. A saturated path from the empty diagram to $\lambda$ is equivalent to a [standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$.
