# Two-spin decoherence-free encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Decoherence-free subspace](decoherence-free-subspace.md)

The zero-total-$Z$ [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of two [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) systems stores one [logical qubit](logical-qubit.md). Both basis states satisfy $(ZI+IZ)|\psi\rangle=0$, so every [collective dephasing](collective-dephasing.md) rotation acts as the [identity operator](identity-operator.md) on their span. Also $ZZ|01\rangle=-|01\rangle$ and $ZZ|10\rangle=-|10\rangle$, so simultaneous [Pauli Z gates](pauli-z-gate.md) preserve every encoded [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md). For independent rotations, the logical amplitudes instead acquire phases $e^{-i(\phi_1-\phi_2)/2}$ and $e^{i(\phi_1-\phi_2)/2}$, which generally alter their [relative phase](relative-quantum-phase.md). The code is not a two-dimensional [decoherence-free subspace](decoherence-free-subspace.md) for arbitrary collective rotations about all axes; the invariant two-spin singlet alone is only one-dimensional.
