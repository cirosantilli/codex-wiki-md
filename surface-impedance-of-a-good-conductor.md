# Surface impedance of a good conductor

↑ **Parent:** [Skin effect](skin-effect.md)

For a wave penetrating a nonmagnetic good conductor in the $+z$ direction with time convention $e^{-i\omega t}$, its [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is $k=(1+i)/\delta$, with [skin depth](skin-depth.md) $\delta=\sqrt{2/(\mu_0\sigma\omega)}$. If $\mathbf E\parallel\widehat{\mathbf x}$, [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives $\mathbf H=[k/(\mu_0\omega)]E_x\widehat{\mathbf y}$. Thus the field ratio is $Z_s=E_x/H_y=(1-i)/(\sigma\delta)$, equal to the displayed expression. Magnetic and electric fields have equal exponential penetration lengths but a phasor phase difference $\pi/4$; $H_y$ has the positive phase relative to $E_x$ in this convention.
