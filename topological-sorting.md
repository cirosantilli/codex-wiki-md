# Topological sorting

↑ **Parent:** [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_sorting)

A [topological sorting](topological-sorting.md) constructs a [topological order](topological-order.md) of a finite [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md). Repeatedly remove a vertex of indegree zero and place it next in the ordering. Such a vertex exists in every nonempty finite [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md), since otherwise following predecessor edges eventually creates a directed cycle.
