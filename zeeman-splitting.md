# Zeeman splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeeman_effect)

Zeeman splitting is the separation of [energy](energy-split.md) levels or mode [frequencies](frequency.md) by a background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). In [atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md), the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) couples to a [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), lifting [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md). Transitions between the shifted [quantum states](quantum-state.md) can turn one [spectral line](spectral-line.md) into several. A split level and a split [spectral line](spectral-line.md) are different objects: the emitted [photon](photon.md) measures the difference between two level [energies](energy-split.md), and the [electric-dipole selection rule](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) determines which differences appear.

The [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md) also probes [magnetic dipole moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), through beam deflection in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) gradient. Here the readout is a [spectral line](spectral-line.md).

The pattern makes an invisible [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) visible through [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md). It also reveals [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [magnetic dipole moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) inside [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). [Zeeman slowers](zeeman-slower.md) exploit a changing resonance [frequency](frequency.md) to keep moving [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) absorbing [laser](laser-split.md) [photons](photon.md); a [magneto-optical trap](magneto-optical-trap.md) combines position-dependent shifts with [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) and [laser cooling](laser-cooling.md). Related mode shifts occur in [Zeeman splitting of an antiferromagnetic spin wave](zeeman-splitting-of-an-antiferromagnetic-spin-wave.md).
