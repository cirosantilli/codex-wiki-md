# One-dimensional twisted nematic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Distortion free energy density](distortion-free-energy-density.md)

For a laterally uniform [nematic director](nematic-director.md) $(\cos\theta\cos\phi,\cos\theta\sin\phi,\sin\theta)$, its [divergence](divergence.md) is $\cos\theta\,\theta'$ and its twist invariant is $-\cos^2\theta\,\phi'$. Thus $f=K_1\cos^2\theta+K_3\sin^2\theta$ and $g=K_2\cos^4\theta+K_3\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta$. A normal [electric field](electric-field.md) contributes $-c\sin^2\theta$. The [Euler-Lagrange equations for two fields](euler-lagrange-equations-for-two-fields.md) are $(g\phi')'=0$ and $f\theta''+f_\theta\theta'^2/2-g_\theta\phi'^2/2+2c\sin\theta\cos\theta=0$.
