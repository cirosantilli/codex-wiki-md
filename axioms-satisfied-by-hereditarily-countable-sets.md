# Axioms satisfied by hereditarily countable sets

↑ **Parent:** [Hereditarily countable set](hereditarily-countable-set.md)

The [transitive set](transitive-set.md) of [hereditarily countable sets](hereditarily-countable-set.md) satisfies every [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) axiom except the [Axiom of power set](axiom-of-power-set.md). For internal definable functions on a countable domain, ambient [Axiom schema of replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) gives a countable range of hereditarily countable sets, which belongs to the structure by [countable-subset closure](countable-subset-closure.md). The same closure proves [axiom schema of collection](axiom-schema-of-collection.md) and retains countable choice-function graphs. All subsets of $\omega$ are elements, but their uncountable full power set is not an element, so Power Set fails.
