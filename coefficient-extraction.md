# Coefficient extraction

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

Coefficient extraction is the linear operation selecting the coefficient of a specified [monomial](monomial.md) from a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) or [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md). Multiplying by the inverse of that monomial reduces the operation to taking a [constant term](constant-term.md). The coefficient form of the [Combinatorial Nullstellensatz](combinatorial-nullstellensatz.md) expresses certain coefficient extractions as weighted sums of evaluations on a finite product set.
