# Wolfenstein parametrization

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

The [Wolfenstein parametrization](wolfenstein-parametrization.md) organizes the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) in powers of a small parameter $\lambda=s_{12}$, defining $s_{23}=A\lambda^2$ and $s_{13}e^{i\delta}=A\lambda^3(\rho+i\eta)$. Substitution into the exact [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) gives the cubic truncation

$$
V^{(3)}=\begin{pmatrix}
1-\lambda^2/2&\lambda&A\lambda^3(\rho-i\eta)\\
-\lambda&1-\lambda^2/2&A\lambda^2\\
A\lambda^3(1-\rho-i\eta)&-A\lambda^2&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This is a useful truncation, with entrywise corrections starting at order $\lambda^4$, rather than an exactly [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md). For example, its first-row squared norm is $1+\lambda^4/4+A^2\lambda^6(\rho^2+\eta^2)$, already different from one. The exact definitions of the sines can instead be inserted into the exact [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), retaining every cosine, to obtain exact [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).

For the illustrative parameters of the [CKM unitarity triangle](ckm-unitarity-triangle.md), the exact definitions give $\lambda=1/4$, $A=8/5$, $\rho=1/5$ and $\eta=\sqrt3/5$. The first-row norm error in $V^{(3)}$ is $0.0010765625$, whereas the exact [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) has unit row norms. To leading order the [Jarlskog invariant](jarlskog-invariant.md) is $J\simeq A^2\lambda^6\eta$, but the exact expression includes cosine factors. The barred parameters $\bar\rho,\bar\eta$ are defined by the exact ratio in the [CKM unitarity triangle](ckm-unitarity-triangle.md); they differ from $\rho,\eta$ beyond leading order. This distinction matters when a geometrical closure test is used to test [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).
