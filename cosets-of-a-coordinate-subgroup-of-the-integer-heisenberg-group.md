# Cosets of a coordinate subgroup of the integer Heisenberg group

↑ **Parent:** [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md)

Write $M(a,b,c)=\begin{pmatrix}1&a&b\\0&1&c\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$ with integer coordinates. Multiplication in this parametrization is $M(a,b,c)M(A,B,C)=M(a+A,b+B+aC,c+C)$. The subgroup $H=\{M(0,0,t):t\in\mathbb Z\}$ has [right cosets](right-coset.md) obtained by multiplying on the left by its elements, so its right cosets are exactly the displayed sets with fixed $(a,b)$. In particular, right cosets are indexed by $\mathbb Z^2$. Left cosets differ: $M(a,b,c)H$ fixes $a$ and $b-ac$, since right multiplication changes $b$ by $at$ and $c$ by $t$. This also exhibits why $H$ is not a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of the [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md).
