# Nonvanishing of holomorphic differentials in genus one

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md)

Every nonzero [holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md) on a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) one is nowhere zero. Its [canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md) is an [effective divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) of degree $2g-2=0$, and hence has every coefficient zero. In particular, such a differential trivializes the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md). This assertion concerns the differential itself, not the individual factors in a representation $f\,dh$.
