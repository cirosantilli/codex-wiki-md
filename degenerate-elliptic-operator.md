# Degenerate elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic boundary value problem](elliptic-boundary-value-problem-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degenerate_elliptic_operator)

A degenerate elliptic operator has a positive semidefinite principal form that may lose positive definiteness at some points or jets. The [p-Laplacian](p-laplacian.md) with $p>2$ degenerates where its solution has zero gradient.
