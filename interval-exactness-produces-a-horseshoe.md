# Interval exactness produces a horseshoe

↑ **Parent:** [Horseshoe for an interval map](horseshoe-for-an-interval-map.md)

Suppose a [interval map](interval-map.md) $F:A\to A$ maps every nondegenerate subinterval onto $A$ after finitely many iterates. Choose two separated [closed intervals](closed-real-interval.md) inside the interior of $A$ and a common iterate mapping each onto $A$. Between first-passage endpoint levels in each interval, take an [open interval](open-interval.md) that maps exactly onto the interior of $A$. These two disjoint intervals are the branches of a [horseshoe for an interval map](horseshoe-for-an-interval-map.md) for that iterate.
