# Algebraic charge conjugation reverses chirality

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

The [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) of a chiral field has opposite projector placement because $\gamma_5$ anticommutes with $\gamma^0$: $\bar\psi\gamma_5=-\eta\bar\psi$. Since $C\gamma_5^TC^{-1}=\gamma_5$, the algebraic charge conjugate satisfies $\gamma_5 C\bar\psi^T=C(\bar\psi\gamma_5)^T=-\eta\psi^c$. This is conjugation of a projected spinor, distinguished from [operator conjugation of a chiral field component](operator-conjugation-of-a-chiral-field-component.md), where the numerical projector is kept outside the conjugation operator.
