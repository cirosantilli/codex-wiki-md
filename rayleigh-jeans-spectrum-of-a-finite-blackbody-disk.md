# Rayleigh-Jeans spectrum of a finite blackbody disk

↑ **Parent:** [Multitemperature blackbody disk](multitemperature-blackbody-disk.md)

For a finite [multitemperature blackbody disk](multitemperature-blackbody-disk.md), the low-[frequency](frequency.md) [Rayleigh-Jeans law](rayleigh-jeans-law.md) gives $F_\nu\propto\nu^2\int rT(r)\,dr$. If $T\propto r^{-3/4}$ away from the inner edge, outer annuli dominate because the weight per logarithmic radial interval grows as $r^{5/4}$. A measure-zero zero-temperature inner edge does not invalidate the leading law when $rT(r)$ is integrable.
