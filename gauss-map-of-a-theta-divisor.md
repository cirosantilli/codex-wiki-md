# Gauss map of a theta divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Theta divisor](theta-divisor.md)

Translate the tangent hyperplane at a smooth point of the [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) to the identity of the [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md). Its annihilator defines the Gauss map. At $L=\mathcal O_X(D)$ with $h^0(L)=1$, the [derivative of the Abelian sum map](derivative-of-the-abelian-sum-map.md) identifies this annihilator with the unique line $H^0(K_X(-D))$. Thus $\Gamma(L)=[\omega]$ implies $D\le\operatorname{div}(\omega)$. This connects the intrinsic geometry of the [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) to the [canonical map](canonical-map.md) of the curve.
