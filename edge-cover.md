# Edge cover

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

An [edge cover](edge-cover.md) of a finite [graph](graph-split.md) is a [subset](subset.md) of its [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) incident to every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). It exists if and only if there is no [isolated vertex](isolated-vertex.md), with the empty [graph](graph-split.md) covered by the empty [set](set-split.md). A minimum-[cardinality](cardinality.md) [edge cover](edge-cover.md) uses as few [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) as possible; a minimum-weight [edge cover](edge-cover.md) minimizes their total weight. These objectives are related to [maximum cardinality matching](maximum-cardinality-matching.md) and [maximum-weight matching](maximum-weight-matching.md), respectively, by the [edge cover and matching identity](edge-cover-and-matching-identity.md) and [minimum-weight edge cover by matching savings](minimum-weight-edge-cover-by-matching-savings.md).
