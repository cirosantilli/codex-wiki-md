# Upper-convected rate as a stress push-forward

↑ **Parent:** [Upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md)

Differentiate the [Kirchhoff stress tensor](kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) relation $\boldsymbol\tau=FSF^T$, using $\dot F=LF$ for the [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md). Subtracting the two deformation terms gives the displayed [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md). This removes the transport generated solely by deformation of a fixed material stress tensor.
