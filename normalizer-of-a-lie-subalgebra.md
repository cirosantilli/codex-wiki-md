# Normalizer of a Lie subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md)

The normalizer of a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $H\subseteq L$ is

$$
N_L(H)=\{x\in L:[x,H]\subseteq H\}.
$$

It is the largest Lie subalgebra of $L$ in which $H$ is an [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
