# Bertotti-Robinson spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

The simply connected Bertotti-Robinson geometry is the product of the [universal cover](universal-cover.md) of [two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) and a [two-sphere](two-sphere.md) with equal [radius](radius.md) $m>0$. In [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$,

$$
g=m^2\sec^2X(-dT^2+dX^2)+m^2d\Omega_2^2,\qquad T\in\mathbb R,\quad |X|<\pi/2.
$$

Its radial [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md) is an infinite [conformal strip of two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime](conformal-strip-of-two-dimensional-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md). The [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) $-\lambda^2dt^2+m^2\lambda^{-2}d\lambda^2+m^2d\Omega_2^2$ is a patch of it, with $\lambda=m(\sin T-\sin X)/\cos X$. Its [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field-split.md) $\partial_t$ extends across alternating [degenerate Killing horizons](degenerate-killing-horizon.md) $\lambda=0$. Both factors are [geodesically complete](geodesic-completeness.md), giving a [maximal analytic extension](maximal-analytic-extension.md) of this patch without periodic time identification.
