# Antiparticle modes and spacelike commutativity

↑ **Parent:** [Microcausality](microcausality.md)

For a free [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md), independent particle annihilators and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) creators give

$$
[\Phi(x),\Phi^\dagger(y)]=\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3\,2E_p}\left(e^{-ip(x-y)}-e^{ip(x-y)}\right).
$$

The invariant [mass shell](mass-shell.md) integral vanishes at spacelike separation: transform to equal time, then reverse the spatial momentum in one term. Retaining only the particle-annihilation term would leave a [Wightman function](wightman-function.md), which is generally nonzero even at spacelike separation. Thus both frequency branches are needed for a local relativistic field. For a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md), the creation term supplies the same cancellation but creates the same self-conjugate particle species.
