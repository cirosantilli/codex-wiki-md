# Change-of-rings tensor quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md)

For a homomorphism of [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md) $R\to A$ and two $A$-[modules](module-mathematics.md), the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) over $A$ is the quotient of the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) over $R$ imposing all relations $am\otimes n=m\otimes an$. The quotient map is $A$-linear when $A$ acts through the first factor on the source. This expresses the extra balancing imposed by [change of rings](change-of-rings.md).
