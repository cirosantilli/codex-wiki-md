# Random field

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

A random field is a family of [random variables](random-variable-split.md) indexed by points of a spatial domain, or more generally by a multi-dimensional parameter set. Its componentwise [covariance function](covariance-function.md) and [spectral tensor](spectral-tensor.md) describe second-order dependence. A [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md) has jointly Gaussian finite-dimensional distributions; a general random field need not be Gaussian.
