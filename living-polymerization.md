# Living polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_polymerization)

[Living polymerization](living-polymerization.md) is [chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md) without chain termination or irreversible chain transfer. A linear mean [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md) versus feed conversion additionally needs the fixed-chain assumptions in [polymerization](polymerization.md); reversibility of [monomer](monomer.md) addition is not required. See [Shipp, IUPAC workshop, slide 50](https://iupac.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/IUPAC_PolymEdu_EduWorkshop_PPT_Shipp2.pdf).
