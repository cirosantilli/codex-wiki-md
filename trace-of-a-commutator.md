# Trace of a commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

For [linear maps](linear-map.md) $A,B$ on a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md), the [cyclic property of the trace](cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(AB-BA)=\operatorname{tr}(AB)-\operatorname{tr}(BA)=0$. In [matrix](matrix.md) entries both traces are $\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}B_{ji}$ after exchanging indices. Thus the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) vanishes on the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md). In particular, for a [perfect Lie algebra](perfect-lie-algebra.md), every representing [matrix](matrix.md) has zero [trace](matrix-trace.md).
