# Shot noise

↑ **Parent:** [Noise (signal processing)](noise-signal-processing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_noise)

Shot noise is fluctuation associated with discrete transfer events. For an [electric current](electric-current.md), individual [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) contribute finite amounts of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), even when a meter displays a smooth mean. The simplest model is a stream of equal, independently arriving charges with constant rate: its [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md) predicts both the mean [electric current](electric-current.md) and the [variance](variance-split.md) around it. Discreteness alone does not imply a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md); the timing and correlations matter.

The useful surprise is that [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) can reveal something a mean hides. Two devices can carry the same mean [electric current](electric-current.md) yet have different fluctuations because one transfers charges independently and the other groups or regulates them. A calibrated [shot noise photocurrent measurement](shot-noise-photocurrent-measurement.md) can connect a macroscopic voltage [variance](variance-split.md) to the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). [Thermal electrical noise](thermal-electrical-noise.md) instead exists in an unbiased [resistor](resistor.md) at [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md); a measured background can contain both mechanisms.

[Walter Schottky](walter-schottky.md)'s 1918 paper _Über spontane Stromschwankungen in verschiedenen Elektrizitätsleitern_ treated [amplifier](amplifier.md) sensitivity limits. Part II considered independent discrete transfers, including [thermionic emission](thermionic-emission-split.md) through a [vacuum](vacuum.md). On p. 563 he proposed amplifying fluctuations from a separate discharge tube to determine the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md); those experiments were prospective, not reported results.[Schottky (1918), p. 541; Part II, pp. 547–552; p. 563, proposed experiment](uber-spontane-stromschwankungen-in-verschiedenen-elektrizitatsleitern-by-walter-schottky.md)
