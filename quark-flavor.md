# Quark flavor

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A quark flavor is one of the six [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) quark species: up, down, strange, charm, bottom and top. Each flavor occurs in three colour states and is counted as one Dirac species when both chiralities are included. The active flavor number in a low-energy [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) description excludes quarks heavier than the relevant scale, with matching of the [running coupling](running-coupling.md) when a heavy threshold is crossed.
