# Ordinal multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal](ordinal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_multiplication)

Ordinal multiplication is defined recursively by

$$
\alpha0=0,
\qquad
\alpha(\beta+1)=\alpha\beta+\alpha,
\qquad
\alpha\lambda=\sup_{\xi<\lambda}\alpha\xi
$$

for nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) $\lambda$. It is associative and left-distributive over [ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md), but generally neither commutative nor right-distributive.
