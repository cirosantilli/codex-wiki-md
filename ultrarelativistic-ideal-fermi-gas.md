# Ultrarelativistic ideal Fermi gas

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

For spin-one-half particles in three dimensions with dispersion $\varepsilon=pc$, the [density of states](density-of-states.md) is

$$
g(\varepsilon)=\frac{V\varepsilon^2}{\pi^2\hbar^3c^3}.
$$

At zero temperature,

$$
E_F=\hbar c\left(3\pi^2\frac NV\right)^{1/3},
\qquad
E=\frac34NE_F,
\qquad
p=\frac E{3V}.
$$
