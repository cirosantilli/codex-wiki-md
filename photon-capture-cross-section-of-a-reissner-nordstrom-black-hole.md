# Photon capture cross-section of a Reissner-Nordstrom black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Photon sphere](photon-sphere.md)

For a [Reissner-Nordstrom black hole](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) with $|Q|\le M$, the outer unstable [circular null geodesic](circular-null-geodesic.md) has radius $r_{\mathrm{ph}}=(3M+\sqrt{9M^2-8Q^2})/2$. Maximizing $f/r^2$ for $f=1-2M/r+Q^2/r^2$ gives the critical [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b_c^2=r_{\mathrm{ph}}^2/f(r_{\mathrm{ph}})=2r_{\mathrm{ph}}^3/(r_{\mathrm{ph}}-M)$. The [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md) capture cross-section is $\pi b_c^2$, reducing to $27\pi M^2$ in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) and $16\pi M^2$ in the extremal charged case. Finite-wavelength absorption need not equal this geometric limit.
