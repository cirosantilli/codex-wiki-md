# Right continuity of percolation probability

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation probability](percolation-probability.md)

For independent [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) or [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on a rooted [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md), $\theta(p)$ is right continuous on $[0,1)$. Finite-radius connection events have polynomial [probabilities](probability.md) in $p$ and decrease to infinite connection. Thus $\theta$ is their infimum by [continuity from above of a measure](continuity-from-above-of-a-measure.md). This infimum of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) is upper semicontinuous; parameter monotonicity from the [monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md) then gives right continuity.
