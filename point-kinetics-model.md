# Point kinetics model

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A reduced time-dependent model for a [neutron](neutron-split.md) population amplitude coupled to [delayed neutron precursor](delayed-neutron-precursor.md) populations. Spatial and spectral shape changes are neglected or represented through prescribed effective coefficients. Such a model illustrates timescale separation without establishing the spatial behaviour or operating limits of an actual reactor.
