# Calcium-dependent protein kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)

A kinase with an intrinsic calcium-sensing regulatory region. Calcium binding can relieve autoinhibition and couple [calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md) to substrate [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md).
