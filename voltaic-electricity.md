# Voltaic electricity

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

Historical terminology for electrical effects supplied by electrochemical cells. The source supplies energy through chemical changes; the resulting [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md) obey the same electrical laws as charge supplied by other means.
