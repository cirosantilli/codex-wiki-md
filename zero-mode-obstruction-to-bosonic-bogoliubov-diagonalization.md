# Zero-mode obstruction to bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md)

For $H=\alpha a^\dagger a+\Delta(a^2+a^{\dagger2})/2$, a finite real [squeeze](bogoliubov-transformation.md) requires $\alpha>|\Delta|$. At $\alpha=\Delta>0$, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) instead equals $\alpha Q^2-\alpha/2$, with $Q=(a+a^\dagger)/\sqrt2$. The formal oscillator frequency is zero, but this [operator](linear-operator.md) is not constant: $\langle2|H|0\rangle=\alpha/\sqrt2$. Thus a normalizable oscillator [ground state](ground-state.md) and finite canonical [squeeze](bogoliubov-transformation.md) cannot be obtained by simply substituting zero frequency into the positive-frequency formula.
