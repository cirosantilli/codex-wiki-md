# Delta-system lemma at a regular uncountable cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md)

If $\kappa$ is a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md), every family of $\kappa$ finite sets has a subfamily of cardinality $\kappa$ whose distinct members have one common pairwise intersection.
