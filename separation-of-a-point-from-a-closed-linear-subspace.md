# Separation of a point from a closed linear subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md)

If $Y$ is a [closed linear subspace](closed-vector-subspace.md) of a Hausdorff [locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md) and $x_0\notin Y$, choose a continuous [seminorm](seminorm.md) $q$ with $q(x_0-y)\geq1$ for all $y\in Y$, using a basic neighborhood disjoint from $Y$. The [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $g(y+tx_0)=t$ is bounded by $q$. The [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends it to a continuous [linear functional](linear-functional.md) with the displayed values. This is the linear-subspace form of separation, sharper than merely distinguishing the two sets.
