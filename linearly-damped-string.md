# Linearly damped string

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

With fixed endpoints, $y_{tt}-y_{xx}+y_t=0$ separates into sine modes whose amplitudes satisfy

$$
T_n''+T_n'+n^2\pi^2T_n=0.
$$
