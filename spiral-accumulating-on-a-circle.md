# Spiral accumulating on a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Connected space](connected-space.md)

The closure of the displayed [connected](connected-space.md) spiral adds the unit circle. Its union with the closed unit disk is therefore connected but not path-connected. A path from the spiral to the disk would have a first contact time. Before contact its reciprocal radial offset defines a continuous parameter tending to infinity. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) then forces sequences of path points with parameters integer and half-integer, approaching opposite circle points. This contradicts continuity at the first contact. Thus the spiral and disk form separate [path-connected components](path-component.md).
