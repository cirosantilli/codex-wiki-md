# Character table of the symmetry group of a square

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group of a square](symmetry-group-of-a-square.md)

In the order of the [conjugacy classes of the symmetry group of a square](conjugacy-classes-of-the-symmetry-group-of-a-square.md) displayed there, its irreducible complex [character table](character-table.md) is

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
&e&r^2&\{r,r^3\}&\{s,r^2s\}&\{rs,r^3s\}\\\hline
A_1&1&1&1&1&1\\A_2&1&1&1&-1&-1\\B_1&1&1&-1&1&-1\\B_2&1&1&-1&-1&1\\E&2&-2&0&0&0
\end{array}
$$

The four [one-dimensional representations](one-dimensional-representation.md) assign independent signs to $r$ and $s$. The remaining row is the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of the ordinary planar [representation](group-representation.md): a rotation has trace $2\cos\theta$ and a reflection has trace zero. Weighted by class sizes, these rows satisfy [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md). Their squared dimensions sum to eight, so they exhaust the [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md).
