# Falkner-Skan equation

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)

For $U=Ax^m$, $\psi=\sqrt{\nu xU}f(\eta)$ gives

$$
f'''+\frac{m+1}{2}ff''+m(1-f'^2)=0,
$$

with $f(0)=f'(0)=0$ and $f'(\infty)=1$.

This equation and its boundary conditions determine the [Falkner-Skan boundary layer](falkner-skan-boundary-layer.md).
