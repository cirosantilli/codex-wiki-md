<h1 id="maxwell-chern-simons-one-form-equation">Maxwell–Chern–Simons one-form equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)

For a [differential one-form](one-form.md) $A$ on an oriented three-dimensional spacetime, varying $\frac12\int(F\wedge*F+kA\wedge F)$, with $F=dA$, gives $d*F+kF=0$. With signature $(-,+,+)$, ${*}^2=-1$ on one- and two-forms; writing $B=*F$ gives $dB-k*B=0$ and the identity $d*B=0$. An Abelian [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) changes the action by $\frac k2\int_{\partial M}\Lambda F$. Thus the bulk field equation is invariant, while invariance of the integrated action depends on boundary conditions.
