# Kuhn length of a freely rotating chain

↑ **Parent:** [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md)

For fixed bond length $b$, fixed forward bond angle $\alpha$, and independently uniform torsion angles, the bond correlations are $\langle\boldsymbol b_i\cdot\boldsymbol b_{i+s}\rangle=b^2c^s$. Thus for $N$ bonds

$$
\langle R^2\rangle=b^2\left[N+2\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}(N-s)c^s\right].
$$

For fixed $|c|<1$ and a sufficiently long chain, divide by contour length $Nb$ and sum the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) to obtain the title formula. Positive forward persistence raises the [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md), and negative correlation lowers it relative to $b$. A chemical internal angle is supplementary to the forward-vector angle. The long-chain formula cannot be used uniformly as $c\to1$ at fixed $N$: the exact chain obeys $R\leq Nb$ and hence $l_K\leq Nb$, with equality in the rigid-rod limit.
