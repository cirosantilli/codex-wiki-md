# Ising-chain ground-state energy

↑ **Parent:** [Ising-chain Bogoliubov diagonalization](ising-chain-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md)

The vacuum of the diagonal [Bogoliubov quasiparticles](bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) has the displayed energy. In the thermodynamic bulk, $E_0/N=-J(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\sqrt{1+g^2-2g\cos k}\,dk$. The [Hellmann–Feynman theorem](hellmann-feynman-theorem.md) then gives the field [magnetization](magnetization.md) $\sum_n\langle S_n^z\rangle=\sum_kJ(g-\cos k)/\epsilon_k$. Sector-dependent finite-chain boundary terms must be restored when exact finite-size levels are needed.
