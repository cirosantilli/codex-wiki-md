# Time orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

A [time orientation](time-orientation.md) is a continuous choice of future cone throughout a time-orientable [spacetime](spacetime.md). A continuous nonzero [timelike vector field](timelike-vector-field.md) supplies such a choice. For [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, a nonzero causal vector $Z$ is future-directed relative to a future timelike $W$ exactly when $g(W,Z)<0$.
