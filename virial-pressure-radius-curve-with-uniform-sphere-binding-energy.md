# Virial pressure-radius curve with uniform-sphere binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-confined isothermal sphere](pressure-confined-isothermal-sphere.md)

For fixed cloud [mass](mass.md) $M$ and isothermal sound speed $c_s$, approximate the binding [energy](energy-split.md) by $-3GM^2/(5r)$. The boundary-pressure [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $A=3Mc_s^2/(4\pi)$ and $B=3GM^2/(20\pi)$. The required external [pressure](pressure-split.md) peaks at $r_c=4B/(3A)$. A homologous radial displacement is restoring on the branch $dp_{\rm ext}/dr<0$, or $r>r_c$; the smaller-radius positive-pressure branch is unstable. This is a one-radius virial model, not the exact hydrostatic isothermal-sphere sequence.
