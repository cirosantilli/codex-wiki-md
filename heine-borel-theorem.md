# Heine-Borel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heine–Borel_theorem)

A subset of a finite-dimensional real vector space is compact exactly when it is closed and bounded.
