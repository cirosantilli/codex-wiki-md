# Double beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_beta_decay)

[Double beta decay](double-beta-decay.md) changes the [atomic number](atomic-number.md) of a [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) by two while preserving its [mass number](mass-number.md). The ordinary two-neutrino process can connect even-even [nuclear isobars](isobar-nuclide.md) when the intermediate odd-odd [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) is too heavy for a single [beta decay](beta-decay.md). Neutrinoless variants would violate [lepton number](lepton-number.md) and require additional physics.
