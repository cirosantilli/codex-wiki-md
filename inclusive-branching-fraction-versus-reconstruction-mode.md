# Inclusive branching fraction versus reconstruction mode

↑ **Parent:** [Branching fraction](branching-fraction.md)

An inclusive [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) sums many exclusive final states. A clean reconstruction mode can be experimentally attractive without dominating the total decay rate. For example, the J/psi has useful electron-pair and muon-pair decays, but predominantly decays to hadrons inclusively. Detector acceptance, [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) and background rejection jointly determine the usefulness of a channel.[J/psi(1S) listing, p. 2, hadronic and charged-lepton decay fractions](review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
