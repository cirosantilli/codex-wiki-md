# Local compactness of a product with finitely many noncompact factors

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)

A [product topology](product-topology.md) on locally [compact](compact-space.md) factors is locally [compact](compact-space.md) if all but finitely many factors are [compact](compact-space.md). At a [point](point-geometry.md), choose a [compact](compact-space.md) [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) in each of the finitely many exceptional factors and take the full [compact space](compact-space.md) in every other coordinate. [Tychonoff's theorem](tychonoff-s-theorem.md) makes their product [compact](compact-space.md), and the product contains an open basic [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) because only finitely many coordinates were restricted. In contrast $\mathbb R^{\mathbb N}$ is not locally [compact](compact-space.md): a basic [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) has a free coordinate projecting onto all of the noncompact real line, so no [compact set](compact-space.md) can contain it.
