# Compact stellar object

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar remnant](stellar-remnant.md)

A dense stellar-scale object such as a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md), [neutron star](neutron-star.md) or stellar-mass [black hole](black-hole-split.md). [accretion](accretion.md) releases a large gravitational binding [energy](energy-split.md) per unit [mass](mass.md) because the accreting surface or inner orbit is small. A [black hole](black-hole-split.md) has no material surface, so its radiative yield depends on the [accretion](accretion.md) flow rather than surface impact.
