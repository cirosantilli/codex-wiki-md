# Tetrahedron removal lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph removal](hypergraph-removal.md)

For every $\eta>0$, some $\rho>0$ has this property: a three-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) on $v$ vertices with fewer than $\rho v^4$ copies of the [three-uniform tetrahedron](three-uniform-tetrahedron.md) can be made tetrahedron-free by removing fewer than $\eta v^3$ hyperedges. The [four-term progression hypergraph encoding](four-term-progression-hypergraph-encoding.md) converts this into the length-four case of the [Szemerédi theorem](szemeredi-s-theorem.md).
