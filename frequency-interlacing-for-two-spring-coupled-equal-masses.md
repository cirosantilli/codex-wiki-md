# Frequency interlacing for two spring-coupled equal masses

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled oscillator](coupled-oscillator.md)

For equal masses $m>0$, positive end [spring constants](spring-constant.md) $k_1>k_2$ and coupling [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $\kappa>0$, the stiffness [matrix](matrix.md) is $K=((k_1+\kappa,-\kappa),(-\kappa,k_2+\kappa))$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) divided by $m$ are the squared [normal mode](normal-mode.md) [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_\pm^2=(k_1+k_2+2\kappa\pm D)/(2m)$, where $D=\sqrt{(k_1-k_2)^2+4\kappa^2}$. Holding the opposite mass fixed gives $\omega_j^2=(k_j+\kappa)/m$. Since $D>k_1-k_2$, subtraction proves the title ordering. Holding a mass fixed retains the coupling stiffness; removing the coupling spring produces a different system.
