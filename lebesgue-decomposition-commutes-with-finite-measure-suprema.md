# Lebesgue decomposition commutes with finite measure suprema

↑ **Parent:** [Finite supremum of positive measures](finite-supremum-of-positive-measures.md)

Take [Lebesgue decompositions](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md) relative to a fixed finite positive [measure](measure-split.md) $\kappa$. The finite union $S$ of null supports of their singular components is still $\kappa$-null. On $S$ each original [measure](measure-split.md) equals its singular component; on $S^c$ it equals its absolutely continuous component. Restriction commutes with the partition formula defining the [finite supremum of positive measures](finite-supremum-of-positive-measures.md). Thus the supremum restricts to $\bigvee_i\mu_i^s$ on $S$ and to $\bigvee_i\mu_i^a$ off $S$. The latter is [absolutely continuous with respect to](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) $\kappa$ because it is bounded by the sum of the absolutely continuous components. Uniqueness of the [Lebesgue decomposition](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md) proves the displayed identities, and the [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md) of the absolutely continuous component is the pointwise maximum of the individual derivatives.
