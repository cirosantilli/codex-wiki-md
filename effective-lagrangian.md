# Effective Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

An effective Lagrangian encodes the interactions of retained degrees of freedom after others have been removed, often by a [path integral](path-integral.md). For external energies below a massive field's excitation threshold, its effects admit a local expansion in external [momenta](momentum-split.md) divided by its mass. Matching [scattering amplitudes](scattering-amplitude.md) fixes the coefficients of the resulting local operators; truncation has a specified order in that small ratio.
