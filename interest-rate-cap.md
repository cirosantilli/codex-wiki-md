# Interest-rate cap

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

A cap limits the floating rate on a notional principal by paying the excess over a strike for successive accrual periods. It is a sum of [caplets](caplet.md). For accrual fraction $\delta$ and reset at $T$ with payment at $U$, the unit-notional payoff is $\delta(L(T;T,U)-K)^+$ at $U$, where $L(T;T,U)=[P(T,U)^{-1}-1]/\delta$ and $P$ is a [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) price.
