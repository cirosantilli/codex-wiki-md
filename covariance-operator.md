# Covariance operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-space-valued random variable](hilbert-space-valued-random-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariance_operator)

For a square-integrable centered [Hilbert-space-valued random variable](hilbert-space-valued-random-variable.md) $X$, its covariance operator is

$$
C_Xh=\mathbb E[\langle X,h\rangle X]=\mathbb E[X\otimes X]h.
$$

It is a positive self-adjoint [trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md), and $\operatorname{tr}C_X=\mathbb E\lVert X\rVert^2$.
