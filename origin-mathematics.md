# Origin (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origin_(mathematics))

The [point](point-geometry.md) assigned all-zero coordinates in a chosen [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md). Choosing an origin identifies position displacements with [vectors](vector.md); a bare [affine space](affine-space.md) has no distinguished origin.
