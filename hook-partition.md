# Hook partition

↑ **Parent:** [Young diagram](young-diagram.md)

A hook partition has shape $(n-k,1^k)$, $0\leq k\leq n-1$. Equivalently its [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) has no cell $(2,2)$, so its only cell of content zero is $(1,1)$. Its [standard Young tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md) are counted by $\binom{n-1}{k}$: choose the $k$ entries below the initial $1$, then the column and remaining row orders are forced.
