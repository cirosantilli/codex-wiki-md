# Fault step-over

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

A lateral separation between adjacent or overlapping [fault](geological-fault.md) strands. Displacement transfer can be accommodated by distributed deformation, a tilted [relay ramp](relay-ramp.md), or a connecting [fault](geological-fault.md). Its releasing or restraining effect depends on both the stepping direction and the sense of [fault slip](fault-slip.md).
