# Euler potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)

Two scalar labels constant along a [magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md) are Euler potentials when their crossed [gradients](gradient.md) give the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) $\mathbf A=\alpha\nabla\beta$ proves that this representation is a [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md). Conversely, two independent local line labels yield $\mathbf B=f(\alpha,\beta)\nabla\alpha\times\nabla\beta$; replacing $\beta$ by $\int^\beta f(\alpha,b)\,db$ absorbs the factor. This is a local construction; a global regular single-valued pair requires compatible [magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md) topology. Fixing boundary labels preserves their paired-footpoint connections in an ideal variation.
