# Barometer

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)

An instrument measuring atmospheric [pressure](pressure-split.md). A mercury [barometer](barometer.md) compares the [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) of a measured mercury column with the pressure above it. Its equation is $p_{\rm atm}=p_t+\rho gh$, with $h$ the vertical separation of the liquid levels.
