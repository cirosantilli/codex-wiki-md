# Redshift evolution of the Lyman-alpha forest

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md)

The [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md) becomes denser and more opaque towards higher [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md), for a fixed absorption selection over the ordinary post-reionization range. The incidence is often parametrized as $d\mathcal N/dz=A(1+z)^\gamma$, with the index depending on the [column density](column-density.md) threshold and epoch. The evolution flattens at low redshift as the declining [cosmic ionizing background](cosmic-ionizing-background.md) partly offsets dilution of the [intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md). For optically thin gas at fixed overdensity, [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) gives approximately $n_{\rm HI}\propto\alpha(T)(1+z)^6/\Gamma$, illustrating why density, temperature and ionizing radiation all matter.
