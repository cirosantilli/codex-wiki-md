# Radiative-convective equilibrium model

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative-convective boundary](radiative-convective-boundary.md)

This model solves a vertical [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md) consistent with heating, [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) and a convective adjustment where the radiative gradient exceeds the [adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md). Irradiation, intrinsic flux, gravity and [opacity](opacity.md) determine the [radiative-convective boundary](radiative-convective-boundary.md). A one-dimensional model describes a column or average rather than resolving all atmospheric winds.
