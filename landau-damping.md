# Landau damping

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_damping)

Collisionless damping of a collective [plasma](plasma-physics.md) perturbation by phase mixing and resonant exchange with particles near its [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). In linear electrostatic response, the imaginary part of the [dielectric function](dielectric-function.md) at real [frequency](frequency.md) is proportional to the derivative of the equilibrium [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) at resonance. A [velocity plateau](velocity-plateau.md) removes this contribution on an interval; compact velocity support also permits undamped waves whose [phase velocities](phase-velocity.md) lie outside that support.
