# Wadati-Benioff zone

↑ **Parent:** [Seismicity](seismicity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadati–Benioff_zone)

An inclined distribution of [earthquake](earthquake.md) sources associated with a descending [slab](slab.md). Shallow interface [earthquakes](earthquake.md) and deeper intraslab [earthquakes](earthquake.md) need not share the same failure mechanism. The pattern helps trace [subduction](subduction.md), subject to the limits of source location and the distribution of seismically active material. Named for [Kiyoo Wadati](kiyoo-wadati.md) and [Hugo Benioff](hugo-benioff.md).
