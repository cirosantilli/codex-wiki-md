# Intramolecular aldol reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Aldol reaction](aldol-reaction.md)

An [aldol reaction](aldol-reaction.md) between two reactive sites of the same [molecule](molecule-split.md). A tethered [enol](enol.md) or [enolate](enolate.md) attacks a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) group to close a ring; the tether, [conformation](conformation.md) and competing [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md) influence the product.
