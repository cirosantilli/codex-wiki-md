# Nominal stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [First Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](first-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md)

Here the convention places the material index first: $P_{Ii}$. Its [stress](stress.md) power per reference volume is $P_{Ii}\dot F_{iI}$. It is the transpose of the [first Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](first-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md); some texts instead call that first tensor itself nominal stress.
