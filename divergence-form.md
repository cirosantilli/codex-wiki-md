# Divergence form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential operator](differential-operator.md)

A [differential operator](differential-operator.md) is in divergence form when its highest derivatives occur inside a [divergence](divergence.md), for example $Lu=\nabla\cdot(A\nabla u)$ or $(p(x)u')'$. This form makes [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) express the operator through a lower-order bilinear form and boundary flux.
