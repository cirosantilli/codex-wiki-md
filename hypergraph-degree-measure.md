# Hypergraph degree measure

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph vertex degree](hypergraph-vertex-degree.md)

For a [uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) with positive average [hypergraph vertex degree](hypergraph-vertex-degree.md) $d$, the degree measure is $\mu(S)=(nd)^{-1}\sum_{u\in S}d_H(u)$. It is a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) and equals $|S|/n$ in a regular hypergraph. Every [hypergraph independent set](hypergraph-independent-set.md) has degree measure at most $1-1/r$, even when its fraction of all vertices is close to one.
