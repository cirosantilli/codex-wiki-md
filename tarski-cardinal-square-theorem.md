# Tarski cardinal-square theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

In ZF, if $X\times X\cong X$ for every infinite $X$, apply this to $X\sqcup h(X)$. The [product-sum comparison lemma](product-sum-comparison-lemma.md) applied to $X$ and its Hartogs ordinal gives either an impossible injection $h(X)\to X$ or a surjection $h(X)\to X$. In the latter case, ordering each $x\in X$ by its least ordinal preimage well-orders $X$. The converse is the well-orderable-cardinal identity $\kappa^2=\kappa$.
