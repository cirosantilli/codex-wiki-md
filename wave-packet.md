# Wave packet

↑ **Parent:** [Phase velocity and group velocity](phase-velocity-and-group-velocity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_packet)

A [wave packet](wave-packet.md) is a localized [superposition](superposition-principle.md) of [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). In a narrow packet, nearby [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) interfere to produce a slowly varying [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md) around a rapid [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md). Its leading translation is governed by [group velocity](group-velocity.md); the carrier's [wave phase](phase-waves.md) follows [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). Localized does not necessarily mean [compact support](compact-support.md): a [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) decays rapidly but extends over the whole line. The [Gaussian envelope in a Klein-Gordon medium](gaussian-envelope-in-a-klein-gordon-medium.md) gives an original example with both translation and spreading.
