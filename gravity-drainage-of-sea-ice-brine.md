# Gravity drainage of sea-ice brine

↑ **Parent:** [Brine rejection](brine-rejection.md)

Dense saline liquid in connected pore space can drain from [sea ice](sea-ice.md) while fresher seawater enters. The process is controlled by [buoyancy](buoyancy.md), [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) and network connectivity, and is a major means of growing-ice desalination.
