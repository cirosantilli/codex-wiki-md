# Radial solar-wind magnetic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Solar wind](solar-wind.md)

[Magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) in a highly conducting [solar wind](solar-wind.md) can carry open [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) outward. A nearly radial [flux tube](flux-tube.md) occupying fixed solid angle has area proportional to $r^2$, so constant [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) gives $B_r\propto r^{-2}$. If a field is approximately dipolar inside an opening radius $r_o$ and radial beyond it, its radial component at $r\gg r_o$ is enhanced by order $r/r_o$ compared with continued dipolar $r^{-3}$ decay. Opposite polarities preserve zero net flux through a closed surface; this approximation does not introduce a magnetic monopole.
