# Cesium chloride density gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md)

A [cesium chloride density gradient](cesium-chloride-density-gradient.md) forms when the [salt](salt-chemistry.md) $\mathrm{CsCl}$ redistributes in a rotating [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). It can supply matching-density positions for [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) during [isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md).[Types of Density Gradients and Types of Gradient Material: self-forming CsCl gradients and nucleic-acid separation](https://www.beckman.com/resources/technologies/centrifugation/density-gradient-centrifugation/)
