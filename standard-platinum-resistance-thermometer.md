# Standard platinum resistance thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Platinum resistance thermometer](platinum-resistance-thermometer.md)

A [standard platinum resistance thermometer](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) uses very pure, annealed [platinum](platinum.md) with strain-limiting construction. An [industrial platinum resistance thermometer](industrial-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) commonly uses supported wire or a film. Their response curves differ.[§§1–2.2, printed pp. 5–11: industrial elements versus SPRTs and wire/film construction](guide-on-secondary-thermometry-industrial-platinum-resistance-thermometers-by-jonathan-pearce-and-others.md)

The word standard describes the instrument's metrological role. Calibrated [SPRTs](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) realize specified interpolation intervals of the [International Temperature Scale of 1990](international-temperature-scale-of-1990.md) and transfer reference [temperatures](temperature-split.md) to other [thermometers](thermometer.md).[Platinum Resistance Thermometers, opening explanation: SPRTs as ITS-90 interpolation tools](mercury-thermometer-alternatives-platinum-resistance-thermometers-prts-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) They are primary standards for realizing that practical scale, but their [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) alone does not determine absolute [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) without calibration. Primary thermometry such as the corrected [gas thermometer](gas-thermometer.md) obtains [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) from a physical law. A reference in a comparison bath may be an [SPRT](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md); an individually calibrated [industrial platinum resistance thermometer](industrial-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) can also serve as a working reference with an appropriate uncertainty.
