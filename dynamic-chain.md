# Dynamic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Call stack](call-stack.md)

The chain of active callers obtained by following [dynamic links](dynamic-link.md) from the current [stack frame](stack-frame.md). It records call history, whereas a [static chain](static-chain.md) records lexical environments.
