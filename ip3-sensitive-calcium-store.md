# IP3-sensitive calcium store

↑ **Parent:** [Inositol trisphosphate](inositol-trisphosphate.md)

An [IP3-sensitive calcium store](ip3-sensitive-calcium-store.md) contains releasable [calcium](calcium.md) behind a membrane bearing functional [IP3 receptors](inositol-trisphosphate-receptor.md). The [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) is an important example. Pumping [calcium](calcium.md) into it or exporting [calcium](calcium.md) across the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) lowers free cytosolic [calcium](calcium.md); intracellular redistribution and loss from the whole [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) are different balances.[Phospholipids and Ca2+, paragraph accompanying Figure 13.27: calcium pumps across plasma and ER membranes](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/pathways-of-intracellular-signal-transduction.md)

Finite availability matters: a release pathway cannot deliver more [calcium](calcium.md) than its accessible store contains unless replenishment occurs. A falling cytosolic [calcium](calcium.md) response can reflect reduced release, increased removal, or both. Store content, release rate and free cytosolic [concentration](concentration.md) therefore answer different questions. Binding to [proteins](protein-split.md) can also change the free fraction without changing the total number of [calcium](calcium.md) [ions](ion.md).
