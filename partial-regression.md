# Partial regression

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md)

Partial regression first projects a response and a selected predictor orthogonally off the remaining design columns. Regressing the residualized response on the residualized predictor yields its coefficient in the full [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) fit.
