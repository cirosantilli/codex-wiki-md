# Finite flat Noether normalization of an affine hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Noether normalization by weighted substitutions](noether-normalization-by-weighted-substitutions.md)

For a nonconstant polynomial over any [field](field.md), use triangular substitutions $x_i=y_i+y_n^{B^i}$, $x_n=y_n$, where $B$ exceeds every exponent in its support. Base-$B$ weights distinguish all original [monomials](monomial.md), so the transformed polynomial has a unique highest $y_n$ power with coefficient in $k^\times$. Rescale to make it monic. The [monic polynomial quotient is finite free](monic-polynomial-quotient-is-finite-free.md) over $k[y_1,\ldots,y_{n-1}]$, yielding a finite flat projection. The nonlinear substitution works over [finite fields](finite-field.md) as well as infinite ones; a generic linear-direction argument alone would not do so.
