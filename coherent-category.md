# Coherent category

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)

A coherent category has [finite limits](finite-limit.md), pullback-stable regular-epi/mono image factorizations, and finite unions of subobjects stable under pullback. Each subobject lattice is distributive. These structures interpret all constructors of [coherent logic](coherent-logic.md).
