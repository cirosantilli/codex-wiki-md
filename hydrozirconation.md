# Hydrozirconation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Addition of a [zirconium](zirconium.md)–[hydrogen bond](hydrogen-bond.md) across an [alkene](alkene.md) or [alkyne](alkyne.md). [Schwartz reagent](schwartz-reagent.md) commonly gives a syn addition and places [zirconium](zirconium.md) at the less substituted terminus; reversible insertion can influence regioselectivity.
