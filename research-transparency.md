# Research transparency

↑ **Parent:** [Open science](open-science.md)

Making the provenance, design, measurements and analytical choices underlying a research claim sufficiently inspectable for informed evaluation. It can support [computational reproducibility](computational-reproducibility.md) and [scientific replication](scientific-replication.md), but does not by itself establish that the measured variable represents the intended construct.
