# Silicate mineral

↑ **Parent:** [Mineral](mineral.md)

A [mineral](mineral.md) whose structure contains silicon–oxygen units. Different linkages give isolated groups, chains, sheets or extended frameworks. Refractory silicate grains are an important solid component of a [protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md).
