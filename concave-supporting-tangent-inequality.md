# Concave supporting-tangent inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Concave function](concave-function.md)

For a differentiable [concave](concave-function.md) function, secant slopes lie below the tangent slope at their left endpoint and above the tangent slope at their right endpoint. This gives the displayed inequality for either order of $x,y$. It converts a first-order optimality relation into a global utility bound. Strict [concavity](concave-function.md) makes equality possible only at $x=y$, on the differentiability domain.
