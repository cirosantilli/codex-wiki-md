# Bipartite lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md)

A lattice interaction graph is bipartite if its sites split into two sets such that every interaction bond joins opposite sets. An even nearest-neighbour ring has this property. It permits a two-sublattice [Néel state](neel-state.md) and a [bipartite spin rotation](bipartite-spin-rotation.md); an odd antiferromagnetic ring is frustrated.
