# Worldsheet chirality and propagation direction

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet Majorana fermion](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md)

In signature $(-,+)$, choose real [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) $\gamma^0=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, $\gamma^1=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ and $\gamma_*=-\gamma^0\gamma^1$. The [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) then gives $(\partial_\tau+\partial_\sigma)\psi_+=0$ and $(\partial_\tau-\partial_\sigma)\psi_-=0$. Positive [chirality](chirality-physics.md) is right-moving and negative [chirality](chirality-physics.md) is left-moving in this orientation. Reversing the definition of $\gamma_*$ exchanges the labels without changing the physics.
