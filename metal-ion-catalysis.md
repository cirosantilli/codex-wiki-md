# Metal ion catalysis

↑ **Parent:** [Catalysis](catalysis.md)

[Metal ion catalysis](metal-ion-catalysis.md) uses a coordinated [metal](metal.md) ion to activate a reagent, orient reactants or stabilize charge. Zinc can lower the acidity constant of coordinated [water](water.md) and support attack by the resulting [oxygen](oxygen.md) [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) without a [metal](metal.md) [redox](redox.md) step.
