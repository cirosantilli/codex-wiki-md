# Gyroscopic roll torque in a turning vehicle

↑ **Parent:** [Gyroscope](gyroscope.md)

A flywheel's spin [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf L_f$ must turn with the vehicle. The vehicle supplies [torque](torque.md) $\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf L_f$ to maintain this [precession](precession.md), and experiences the opposite reaction [torque](torque.md). With right-handed axes $x$ forward, $y$ left and $z$ up, a left turn has $\boldsymbol\Omega=+\Omega\widehat{\mathbf z}$. A transverse flywheel with $\mathbf L_f=-L_f\widehat{\mathbf y}$ then produces body [torque](torque.md) $-\Omega L_f\widehat{\mathbf x}$, opposing outward roll. Its magnitude is $L_fv/R_b$ for turn radius $R_b$. Reversing the flywheel reverses the effect.
