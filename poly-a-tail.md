<h1 id="poly-a-tail">Poly(A) tail</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md)

A stretch of [adenosine](adenosine.md)-containing residues at an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) end. Tail length and associated [proteins](protein-split.md) can influence [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) stability and [biological translation](translation-biology.md).
