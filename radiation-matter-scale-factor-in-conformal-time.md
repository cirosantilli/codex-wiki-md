# Radiation-matter scale factor in conformal time

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

In a flat universe containing separately conserved [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) and [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) becomes $(a')^2=H_0^2(\Omega_{r,0}+\Omega_{m,0}a)$ in [conformal time](conformal-time.md). Setting $a=0$ at $\tau=0$ gives the stated quadratic-plus-linear [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). The linear term controls [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) and the quadratic term controls [matter domination](matter-domination.md). The formula presumes $a_0=1$, $\Omega_{r,0}+\Omega_{m,0}=1$ and no [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md).
