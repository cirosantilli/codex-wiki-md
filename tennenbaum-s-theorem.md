<h1 id="tennenbaum-s-theorem">Tennenbaum's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennenbaum's_theorem)

Tennenbaum's theorem says that no countable nonstandard model of [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md) can have both addition and multiplication computable under a presentation of its domain by the natural numbers.
