# Antipodal cycle of a cubic swap map

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic swap-map two-cycle factorization](cubic-swap-map-two-cycle-factorization.md)

Along the antipodal [two-cycle](period-two-orbit.md) of $F(x,y)=(y,a(x-x^3/3)-y)$, the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) is $B=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-2a&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ at both points. Its two-step [periodic-orbit multipliers](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) are squares of the roots of $\lambda^2+\lambda+2a=0$. The Jury criterion makes the cycle attracting precisely in the displayed interval, and repelling for $a>1/2$. This stable family is distinct from the always unstable nonzero-sum cycles.
