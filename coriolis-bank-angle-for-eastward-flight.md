# Coriolis bank angle for eastward flight

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md)

For a locally eastward aircraft [velocity](velocity.md) at northern latitude $\lambda$, the horizontal [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) is southward with magnitude $2\Omega v\sin\lambda$. Banking to direct part of the lift northward cancels this [acceleration](acceleration.md). If vertical lift balances weight, the [force](force-split.md) ratio gives the displayed angle. Including vertical [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) replaces $g$ by $g-2\Omega v\cos\lambda$. This is a local rotational correction, not a full navigation model of motion along a curved latitude circle.
