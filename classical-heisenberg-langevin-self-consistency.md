# Classical Heisenberg Langevin self-consistency

↑ **Parent:** [Classical Heisenberg mean-field partition function](classical-heisenberg-mean-field-partition-function.md)

The single-site average in molecular field $z_cJS$ is $S=\coth x-1/x$, where $x=\beta z_cJS$. Thus $\tau x=\coth x-1/x$ with $\tau=k_BT/(z_cJ)$. The small-$x$ slope $1/3$ gives $k_BT_c^{\rm MF}=z_cJ/3$. On a [square lattice](square-lattice.md) $z_c=4$. This finite mean-field transition does not imply actual positive-temperature [magnetization](magnetization.md) for the ideal two-dimensional short-range isotropic model, where the [Mermin-Wagner theorem](mermin-wagner-theorem.md) applies.
