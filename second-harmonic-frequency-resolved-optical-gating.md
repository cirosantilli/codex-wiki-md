# Second-harmonic frequency-resolved optical gating

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency-resolved optical gating](frequency-resolved-optical-gating.md)

The self-gated second-harmonic [spectrogram](spectrogram.md) has the displayed ideal form. Its delay marginal is proportional to the [intensity autocorrelation](intensity-autocorrelation.md) by the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md). Absolute time and [wave phase](phase-waves.md) are undetermined; $A(t)$ and $A^*(-t)$ also give the same record. Finite crystal acceptance and [spatial walk-off](spatial-walk-off.md) modify the ideal gate.
