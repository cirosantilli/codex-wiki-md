# Shortwave infrared

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared](infrared.md)

An operational spectral region often taken as about 1–2.5 micrometres in [remote sensing](remote-sensing.md), with convention-dependent boundaries. Water absorption makes suitable bands sensitive to leaf and surface moisture.
