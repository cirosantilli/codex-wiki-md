# Trace-preserving and unital Kraus conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md)

For a [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md) $\Phi(X)=\sum_kA_kXA_k^\dagger$, trace preservation is equivalent to the first identity, by cyclicity of the trace. The second says $\Phi(I)=I$, namely unitality, and is a distinct constraint. For $0<\gamma<1$, the [amplitude damping channel](amplitude-damping-channel.md) has operators $A_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt{1-\gamma})$ and $A_1=\sqrt\gamma|0\rangle\langle1|$ and is trace-preserving but not unital. Taking the adjoints as Kraus operators instead gives a unital completely positive map whose output trace on $|0\rangle\langle0|$ is $1+\gamma$.
