# Volume integral of a curl

↑ **Parent:** [Curl](curl.md)

The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) applied to $G\times c$ for every constant vector $c$ gives $\int_V\nabla\times G\,dV=\int_{\partial V}n\times G\,dS$, with outward [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $n$. Indeed $\nabla\cdot(G\times c)=c\cdot(\nabla\times G)$, while $(G\times c)\cdot n=c\cdot(n\times G)$. Equality of the scalar projections for every $c$ proves the vector identity. Reversing the surface [cross product](cross-product.md) reverses the sign.
