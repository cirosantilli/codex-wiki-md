<h1 id="bott-samelson-theorem">Bott–Samelson theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [James reduced product](james-reduced-product.md)

For a connected based CW complex with free integral homology, the [Pontryagin ring](pontryagin-ring.md) of its looped reduced suspension is the indicated tensor algebra. The generator inclusion comes from $X\to\Omega\Sigma X$, and multiplication corresponds to concatenation of words in the [James reduced product](james-reduced-product.md).
