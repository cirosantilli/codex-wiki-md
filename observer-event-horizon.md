# Observer event horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

An observer's future event horizon bounds the events that can ever signal to its future-inextendible worldline. Its past event horizon bounds the events reachable by signals from its past-inextendible worldline. A complete inertial observer in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) has neither. The uniformly accelerated worldline $t=a^{-1}\sinh(a\tau)$, $x=a^{-1}\cosh(a\tau)$ has $I^-(\gamma)=\{t-x<0\}$ and $I^+(\gamma)=\{t+x>0\}$. Their boundaries are its two [Rindler horizons](rindler-horizon.md). This observer dependence is distinct from a black-hole horizon defined using [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md).
