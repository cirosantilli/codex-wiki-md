# Multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md)

Replace each positive power $x_i^a$ in a [monomial](monomial.md) by $x_i$ and combine equal [monomials](monomial.md). The result is a [multilinear polynomial](multilinear-polynomial.md) agreeing with the original [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) at every point of $\{0,1\}^n$, because $x_i^a=x_i$ there for $a\geq1$. Its [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) does not increase. This reduction identifies polynomial functions on the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) with their unique square-free [monomial](monomial.md) representations: uniqueness follows by successively evaluating at [characteristic vectors of sets](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) in increasing order of size.
