# Polyethylene glycol

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

[Polyethylene glycol](polyethylene-glycol.md) is a water-soluble [polymer](polymer-split.md) with repeating ethylene oxide units. Nonadsorbing dissolved chains can cause a [depletion force](depletion-force.md) between larger [colloids](colloid.md). Chains attached to a surface can instead give [steric stabilization](steric-stabilization.md). Which effect occurs depends on surface affinity, chain size and [concentration](concentration.md).
