# Pulse-and-precession spin one-half map

↑ **Parent:** [Floquet operator](floquet-operator.md)

Multiplying the two [matrix exponentials](matrix-exponential.md) with the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives

$$
U_F=CI-i\mathbf v\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,\qquad C=\cos a\cos b,\qquad
\mathbf v=(\cos a\sin b,-\sin a\sin b,-\sin a\cos b).
$$

One has $C^2+|\mathbf v|^2=1$. If $|\mathbf v|\ne0$, put $\theta=2\arccos C$ and $\mathbf n=\mathbf v/|\mathbf v|$. The [exponential of a Pauli vector](exponential-of-a-pauli-vector.md) gives $U_F^N=I\cos(N\theta/2)-i\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\sin(N\theta/2)$. Its action on a [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) is a rotation through $N\theta$ about $\mathbf n$, and its [quasienergies](quasienergy.md) are $\pm\hbar\theta/(2T)$ modulo $2\pi\hbar/T$. When $U_F=\pm I$, its axis is arbitrary and the [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) is unchanged.
