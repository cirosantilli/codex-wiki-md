# Golomb sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Integer sequence](integer-sequence.md)

The nondecreasing sequence of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) beginning with $G(1)=1$ in which the [integer](integer-split.md) $n$ occurs exactly $G(n)$ times. Its first terms are $1,2,2,3,3,4,4,4,5,5,5,\ldots$. The [prefix sum](prefix-sum.md) $\sum_{j=1}^kG(j)$ gives the last position occupied by $k$.
