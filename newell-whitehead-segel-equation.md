<h1 id="newell-whitehead-segel-equation">Newell–Whitehead–Segel equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

The Newell–Whitehead–Segel equation is an [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md) for slowly modulated stationary rolls in an isotropic pattern-forming medium. With the displayed sign convention, a roll $A=R e^{iqX}$ has $R^2=\mu-q^2$. A transverse [phase modulation](phase-modulation.md) has linear growth rate $qk^2-k^4/4$, so long-wave transverse instability occurs for $q>0$. Reversing the carrier-wave convention reverses the sign attached to its detuning $q$; one must derive the stability sign from the operator actually used.
