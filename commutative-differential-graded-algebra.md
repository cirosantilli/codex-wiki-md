# Commutative differential graded algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md)

A commutative [differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md) has multiplication $ab=(-1)^{|a||b|}ba$ and a degree-one differential with $d^2=0$ and $d(ab)=d(a)b+(-1)^{|a|}a\,d(b)$. Over $\mathbb Q$, its free algebra on graded generators is polynomial on even-degree generators and exterior on odd-degree generators. Rational [rational polynomial differential forms](rational-polynomial-differential-forms.md) on a space supply such an algebra, modelled up to [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) by a [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md).
