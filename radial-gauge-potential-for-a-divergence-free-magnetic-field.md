# Radial-gauge potential for a divergence-free magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)

For a smooth [divergence](divergence.md)-free field on $\mathbb R^3$, the [differential form](differential-form-split.md) $F=\iota_{\mathbf B}(dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3)$ is closed. The radial homotopy formula gives a global [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) $\mathbf A(\mathbf x)=\int_0^1s\,\mathbf B(s\mathbf x)\times\mathbf x\,ds$ with [curl](curl.md) $\mathbf B$. For constant $\mathbf B$, this reduces to $\mathbf A=\tfrac12\mathbf B\times\mathbf x$.
