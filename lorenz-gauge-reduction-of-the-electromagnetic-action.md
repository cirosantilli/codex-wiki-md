# Lorenz-gauge reduction of the electromagnetic action

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)

The [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) action differs from the component-gradient potential action by $(2\mu_0)^{-1}\int \partial^bA^a\partial_aA_b\,d^4x$. Integration by parts expresses this term as a boundary integral plus $(2\mu_0)^{-1}\int(\partial_aA^a)^2\,d^4x$. With the [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) and fixed or vanishing boundary data, the two actions therefore differ only by a boundary term. Varying the component-gradient action without restriction gives the potential wave equations; the Lorenz condition must be supplied to recover all [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md).
