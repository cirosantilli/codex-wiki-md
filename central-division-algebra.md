# Central division algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Division algebra](division-algebra.md)

A [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md) over $K$ is a finite-dimensional [division algebra](division-algebra.md) whose center is exactly $K$. It is a [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md). Every [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) class has a unique [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md) representative up to isomorphism.
