# Intersection of equivalence relations

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

The intersection of any family of [equivalence relations](equivalence-relation.md) on one [set](set-split.md) is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md). Reflexivity and symmetry hold in every component. A pair of consecutive related points belongs to every component, so each component's [transitivity](transitive-relation.md) supplies the required final pair. Its [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) refine the component partitions. The empty intersection is the universal relation.
