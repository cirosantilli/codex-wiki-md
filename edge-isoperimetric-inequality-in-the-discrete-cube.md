# Edge-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube

↑ **Parent:** [Edge boundary in a graph](edge-boundary-in-a-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-isoperimetric_inequality_in_the_discrete_cube)

For $A\subseteq\{0,1\}^n$,

$$
|\partial_eA|\geq|A|\log_2\frac{2^n}{|A|}.
$$

Equivalently, $A$ spans at most $\frac12|A|\log_2|A|$ cube edges. Induction on the dimension and concavity of [binary entropy](binary-entropy-function.md) prove the inequality.
