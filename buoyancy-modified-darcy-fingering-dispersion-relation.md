# Buoyancy-modified Darcy fingering dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Planar viscous-fingering dispersion relation](planar-viscous-fingering-dispersion-relation.md)

Let fluid 1 lie above fluid 2, with [mass densities](density.md) $\rho_1<\rho_2$ and [dynamic viscosities](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu_1<\mu_2$. Define $\Delta\rho=\rho_2-\rho_1>0$, $\Delta\mu=\mu_2-\mu_1>0$, [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$, [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $k$, and downward interface speed $W$. With [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) $\phi W$, no capillarity, and two unbounded layers, [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) gives

$$
\sigma(\alpha)=\frac{|\alpha|[\phi W\Delta\mu-kg\Delta\rho]}{\phi(\mu_1+\mu_2)}.
$$

For a [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) of interface displacement, the two perturbation [pressures](pressure.md) decay exponentially into the respective layers. The [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) makes their amplitudes proportional to $\phi\mu_1\sigma/(k|\alpha|)$ and $-\phi\mu_2\sigma/(k|\alpha|)$. Linearized [pressure continuity](pressure-continuity.md) adds the base-gradient difference $\phi W\Delta\mu/k-g\Delta\rho$. Eliminating the amplitudes proves the formula. The critical interface speed is $kg\Delta\rho/(\phi\Delta\mu)$; the critical [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) is $kg\Delta\rho/\Delta\mu$. Without [surface tension](surface-tension.md) this model has unbounded short-wave [growth rate](growth-rate.md) on its unstable side.
