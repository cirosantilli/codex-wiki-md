# Ring of invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

For a group acting on a [ring](ring.md) by [ring automorphisms](ring-automorphism.md), the elements fixed by every group element form a [subring](subring.md) containing $1$. For finite $G$ acting on a commutative [ring](ring.md), each $r$ satisfies the monic orbit equation $\prod_{g\in G}(T-g(r))$, whose coefficients are fixed. Thus $R$ is integral over its [invariant subring](ring-of-invariants.md). This uses an orbit product, not division by the group order.
