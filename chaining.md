# Chaining

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

A multiscale method for bounding the [supremum](supremum.md) of a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md). Approximate each index by points $\pi_k(t)$ in successively finer [metric nets](metric-net.md), expand its difference from an anchor as a [telescoping sum](telescoping-sum.md), and bound the largest increment at each scale. For a finite index set the finest net can be the entire set, making this decomposition exact. If $E_k$ is the set of approximation edges, then pointwise $\sup_t(X_t-X_{t_0})\leq\sum_k\max_{(u,v)\in E_k}(X_u-X_v)$. Finite maxima controlled by [moment-generating functions](moment-generating-function.md) turn the sizes of the nets into [metric entropy](metric-entropy.md) sums and integrals. The [Dudley entropy integral](dudley-entropy-integral.md) and [dyadic increment chaining](dyadic-increment-chaining.md) are related instances.
