# Discrete metric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric](metric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_metric)

The discrete metric sets $d(x,x)=0$ and $d(x,y)=1$ for distinct points. It induces the [discrete topology](discrete-space.md), and its Cauchy sequences are exactly the eventually constant sequences.
