# Turbulent energy spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

The [turbulent energy spectrum](turbulent-energy-spectrum.md) $E(k)$ distributes [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) over wavenumber shells, normalized by $\int_0^\infty E(k)\,dk=\langle|\mathbf u|^2\rangle/2$. For isotropic [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) its tensor form is $\Phi_{ij}=E(k)(\delta_{ij}-k_ik_j/k^2)/(4\pi k^2)$. Its low-wavenumber behavior records large-eddy [momentum](momentum.md) statistics.
