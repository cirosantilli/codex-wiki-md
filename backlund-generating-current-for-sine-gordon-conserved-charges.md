<h1 id="backlund-generating-current-for-sine-gordon-conserved-charges">Bäcklund generating current for sine-Gordon conserved charges</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation](sine-gordon-backlund-transformation.md)

Write the transformed angular field as $v=u+2d$. The [Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation](sine-gordon-backlund-transformation.md) gives $\sin d=a(u_\xi+d_\xi)$, determining $d$ as a formal local derivative expansion in $a$. The displayed exact current identity yields a [conservation law](conservation-law.md) at each order. Formal convergence is unnecessary because each coefficient obeys an exact identity on solutions.
