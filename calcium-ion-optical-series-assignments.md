# Calcium-ion optical series assignments

↑ **Parent:** [Singly ionized calcium](singly-ionized-calcium.md)

In [singly ionized calcium](singly-ionized-calcium.md), $np\,{}^2P\to4s\,{}^2S$ resonance doublets, $ns\,{}^2S\to4p\,{}^2P$ doublets and $np\,{}^2P\to3d\,{}^2D$ three-component groups approach the same ionization continuum from different lower terms. A three-line group need not have spin multiplicity three. The electric-dipole rule excludes $P_{1/2}\to D_{5/2}$.[4s,3d,4p and5s assignments](energy-levels-of-singly-ionized-calcium-ca-ii-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
