# Ion trap

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic measurement methods](atomic-measurement-methods.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_trap)

An ion trap confines an [ion](ion.md) using [electromagnetic fields](electromagnetic-field.md), allowing repeated interrogation of the same particle. The name also covers related traps for an [electron](electron.md). A [Paul trap](paul-trap.md) uses an oscillating [electric quadrupole](electric-quadrupole.md) field; a [Penning trap](penning-trap.md) combines a static [electric quadrupole](electric-quadrupole.md) field with a static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Confinement creates time to measure an [energy level](energy-level.md), [frequency](frequency.md) or [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md); it does not itself cool the particle or identify its internal [quantum state](quantum-state.md).

The obstacle is [Earnshaw's theorem](earnshaw-s-theorem.md). In a charge-free neighbourhood the static [electric potential](electric-potential.md) $\Phi$ obeys [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md), $\nabla^2\Phi=0$. For a particle of fixed nonzero [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $Q$, the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $Q\Phi$ is also a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). The [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) excludes a strict interior minimum. For a quadratic example, the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) has zero [trace](matrix-trace.md); it cannot have three positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). A static purely [electric field](electric-field-split.md) therefore cannot supply three-dimensional stable confinement of a free point [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). Time variation in a [Paul trap](paul-trap.md), and the [velocity](velocity.md)-dependent [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) in a [Penning trap](penning-trap.md), change the assumptions of this argument.
