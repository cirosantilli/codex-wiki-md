# Surgery linking matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Surgery trace](surgery-trace.md)

The surgery linking matrix has the framing coefficients on its diagonal and pairwise linking numbers off the diagonal. It represents the [intersection form](intersection-form.md) on the second homology of the surgery trace, presents the first homology of its boundary, and has kernel isomorphic to the boundary's second homology.
