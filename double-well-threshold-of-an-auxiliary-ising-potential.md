# Double-well threshold of an auxiliary Ising potential

↑ **Parent:** [Classical Ising chain as a quantum particle](classical-ising-chain-as-a-quantum-particle.md)

The zero-field [Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation](hubbard-stratonovich-transformation.md) of an exponentially coupled [Ising model](ising-model.md) produces $U(m)=Am^2-\log[2\cosh(2m)]$, where $A=\tanh(\kappa/2)/J>0$. Since $U''(0)=2A-4$ and $U$ tends to positive infinity, a symmetric double well exists exactly when $A<2$. Its nonzero stationary points solve $Am=\tanh(2m)$; the ratio $\tanh(2m)/m$ decreases from 2 to 0 for $m>0$, proving that there is exactly one positive minimum. At $A=2$ the minimum is a single quartic minimum. A small conjugate field adds $-h\tanh(2m)+O(h^2)$ and tilts the wells, while a sufficiently large field can remove the metastable well. A finite barrier permits [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md); two classical minima alone do not establish [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md).
