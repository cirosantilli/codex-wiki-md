# Buscher dual of a metric with vanishing two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Buscher rules](buscher-rules.md)

For a nondegenerate isometry direction $y$ and initially zero two-form, the local T-dual fields in alpha-prime-one units are $g'_{yy}=1/g_{yy}$, $g'_{yi}=0$, $g'_{ij}=g_{ij}-g_{yi}g_{yj}/g_{yy}$, $B'_{yi}=g_{yi}/g_{yy}$ and $B'_{ij}=0$. Gauging the isometry and eliminating its auxiliary connection derives these rules. The spectator metric is a [Schur complement](schur-complement.md). The sign of the mixed two-form follows the multiplier and dual-coordinate orientation convention.
