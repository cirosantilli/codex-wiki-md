# Linearly ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linearly_ordered_group)

A [group](group-split.md) with a two-sided invariant [total order](total-order.md). Thus it is a [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md) whose [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) choose the larger and smaller element. An order on only one side is not sufficient here. Every linearly ordered group is [torsion-free](torsion-free-connection.md): a positive element has strictly increasing positive powers, and a negative element has a positive inverse.
