# Ramsey spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey separated oscillatory fields experiment](ramsey-separated-oscillatory-fields-experiment.md)

[Ramsey spectroscopy](ramsey-spectroscopy.md) measures a transition [frequency](frequency.md) by comparing the relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) acquired between two coherent interactions with the reference [oscillator](oscillator.md). In a beam, a state selector and apertures admit particular internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md) and trajectories; an analyzer and detector measure state-dependent transmission. In an optically prepared [atomic clock](atomic-clock.md), [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) and [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) can perform those functions. The preparation and readout determine which part of the beam contributes; they are not themselves the two coherent interactions.

For an ideal accepted particle, the first resonant half-duration [Rabi pi pulse](rabi-pi-pulse.md) splits the [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) between $|g\rangle$ and $|e\rangle$. The second combines two indistinguishable alternatives: a transition in the first region followed by no transition in the second, or the reverse. Their relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) depends on the dark flight and the drive [oscillation phases](phase-waves.md). Adding [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) gives [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md); adding only the two alternatives' [probabilities](probability.md) would erase the fringe. “Dark” means that the transverse drive is absent: a static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and the internal [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) can still act.

Original functional [Ramsey spectroscopy](ramsey-spectroscopy.md) model, not a reconstruction of NIST-7 or a historical apparatus. Equal zone lengths $\ell$ and edge-to-edge gap $L$ give $\tau=\ell/v$ and $T=L/v$ for constant [speed](speed.md) $v$. Preparation and state-sensitive readout are abstract functions; the diagram does not claim perfectly pure preparation.

The useful separation is between pulse duration and phase-accumulation time. Short regions can share one coherent [oscillator](oscillator.md) while a long drive-free interval resolves small [frequency](frequency.md) differences. A scan over reference [frequency](frequency.md) locates a fringe pattern; controlled changes of the second pulse's [oscillation phase](phase-waves.md) distinguish that coherent signal from phase-insensitive background. A reference scan must also identify the central fringe before [atomic clock frequency lock](atomic-clock-frequency-lock.md), because neighboring fringes provide competing lock points.
