# Carbon-dioxide viewing-cell phase-transition experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md)

The institutional account [CO2 viewing cell](co2-viewing-cell.md) describes an optical cell at the University of Stuttgart: a fixed-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) sample cools from above its [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md) into [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md). The apparatus supplies a view through the cell and displayed [temperature](temperature.md) and [pressure](pressure.md) readings. Near roughly $31\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, the account reports [critical opalescence](critical-opalescence.md): enhanced [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) makes reflected light appear whitish and transmitted light dark. This readout reveals growing density [thermal fluctuations](thermal-fluctuation.md); it is not a direct count of molecules or a measurement of an infinite [correlation length](correlation-length.md). [CO2 viewing cell](co2-viewing-cell.md)

Fixed [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) constrains the experimental path; the [pressure](pressure.md) varies and is a readout, not an independently held control. The account explicitly identifies the clip as part of a longer cooldown and says its endpoints are not steady states. To test an equilibrium interpretation more closely, one would hold [temperature](temperature.md) until the readout settles, compare cooling and warming, and check optical background away from the [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md); these are proposed controls, not measurements reported on that page. The account does not provide cell dimensions, filling [number density](number-density.md), calibration uncertainties or such control runs. The sample is finite: its striking appearance motivates the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) as a model of macroscopic sharpness, rather than demonstrating a literal equilibrium singularity.
