<h1 id="cation-pi-interaction">Cation–π interaction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

A noncovalent attraction between a cation and an electron-rich aromatic system, with electrostatic and polarization contributions. Its solution free energy depends strongly on [solvation](solvation.md).
