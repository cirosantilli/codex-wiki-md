# Derivative-antighost gauge-fixing fermion

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-fixing fermion](gauge-fixing-fermion.md)

For the convention $s\bar c=-b$, the [action](action.md) $S_{\mathrm{YM}}-s\Psi$ contains $-b\,\partial\cdot A+\xi b^2/2-\bar c\,\partial\cdot Dc$ after [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). Integrating the [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) gives $-(\partial\cdot A)^2/(2\xi)$. The [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) represents the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md).
