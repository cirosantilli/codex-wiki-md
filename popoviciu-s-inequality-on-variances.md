<h1 id="popoviciu-s-inequality-on-variances">Popoviciu's inequality on variances</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Variance](variance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popoviciu's_inequality_on_variances)

If a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ takes values in $[m,M]$, then

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)\leq\frac{(M-m)^2}{4}.
$$
