# Orthogonality of integer translates

↑ **Parent:** [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md)

With the Fourier convention $\widehat f(t)=\int f(x)e^{-itx}\,dx$, translation by $k$ multiplies the transform by $e^{ikt}$. The [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md) gives the displayed correlation identity for every square-integrable function, by extension from integrable square-integrable functions. Thus distinct integer translates are orthogonal exactly when the nonzero integer Fourier [integrals](integral.md) of $|\widehat f|^2$ vanish. The products in both [integrals](integral.md) are integrable by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md).
