# Sphere rolling off a hemisphere

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A uniform solid sphere released from the top of a fixed spherical surface has $v^2=10g(R+r)(1-\cos\theta)/7$ under the ideal [rolling constraint](rolling-constraint.md). The [normal force](normal-force.md) is $Mg(17\cos\theta-10)/7$, giving the title's ideal detachment angle. The required [static friction](static-friction.md) is $2Mg\sin\theta/7$. Its ratio to the [normal force](normal-force.md) diverges at detachment, so any finite [coefficient of static friction](coefficient-of-static-friction.md) causes prior slipping; a physical subsequent trajectory requires a sliding-friction model.
