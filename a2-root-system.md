# A2 root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The $A_2$ [root system](root-system.md) has equal-length [simple roots](simple-root.md) $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ with angle $2\pi/3$, and roots $\pm\alpha_1$, $\pm\alpha_2$, $\pm(\alpha_1+\alpha_2)$. Its [Cartan matrix](cartan-matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$. The corresponding complex [simple Lie algebra](simple-lie-algebra.md) is $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)$, of dimension eight.
