# Finite-endpoint branch-cut jump integral

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

A pair of oppositely directed [contours](complex-integration-contour.md) on a [branch cut](branch-cut.md) produces the difference of the two boundary values integrated over their actual endpoint interval. Replacing finite endpoints by zero and infinity requires a limiting argument and convergence of the combined [improper integral](improper-integral.md). Individual side integrals may diverge even when their difference converges, so cancellation must precede separation of endpoint limits.
