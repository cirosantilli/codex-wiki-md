# Proof goal transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

Reading an [inference rule](inference-rule.md) backwards to replace a desired conclusion by sufficient subgoals. A conjunction goal can be replaced by its two conjuncts using the [conjunction introduction rule](conjunction-introduction-rule.md); an available disjunction can instead lead to two case subgoals using the [disjunction elimination rule](disjunction-elimination-rule.md). Different choices change the organization and sharing of [subproofs](subproof.md). Scope and [assumption discharge](assumption-discharge.md) conditions remain obligations even when a rule is applied backwards during proof search.
