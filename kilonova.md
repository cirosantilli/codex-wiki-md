# Kilonova

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron star merger](neutron-star-merger.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilonova)

An optical and infrared transient powered by radioactive decay in material ejected from a compact-object merger. Its evolving spectrum and light curve probe ejecta composition and [rapid neutron-capture process](r-process.md) production.
