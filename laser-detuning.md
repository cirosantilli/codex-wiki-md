# Laser detuning

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

[Laser detuning](laser-detuning.md) is the difference between a [laser](laser-split.md) [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and a specified atomic resonance [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). Red detuning has $\delta<0$; blue detuning has $\delta>0$. The sign matters in [laser cooling](laser-cooling.md): a moving atom can see a red-detuned [laser](laser-split.md) closer to resonance because of the [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md).
