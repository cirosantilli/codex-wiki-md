# Strong stationary time

↑ **Parent:** [Mixing time of a Markov chain](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md)

A randomized [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $\tau$ is a strong stationary time for a [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) with [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$ when $X_\tau$ has distribution $\pi$ and is [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of $\tau$. Equivalently,

$$
\mathbb P_x(X_\tau=y,\tau=t)=\pi(y)\mathbb P_x(\tau=t).
$$
