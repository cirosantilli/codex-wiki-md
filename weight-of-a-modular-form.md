# Weight of a modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)

The modular [modular weight](weight-of-a-modular-form.md) specifies the power $(cz+d)^k$ in a [modular form](modular-form.md)'s transformation law. Multiplication adds [modular weights](weight-of-a-modular-form.md). At level one, the central [matrix](matrix.md) $-I$ excludes nonzero odd-weight forms; a [nebentypus character](nebentypus-character.md) at higher level changes this condition to $\psi(-1)=(-1)^k$.
