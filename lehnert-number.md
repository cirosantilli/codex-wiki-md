# Lehnert number

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The Lehnert number compares an [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) to a rotation speed on length scale $L$. With $\mathrm{Le}\ll1$, magnetic-wave frequencies are small compared with the rotation frequency, allowing nearly [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md). Ideal weakly damped magnetic waves also require $\eta/L^2$ and $\nu/L^2$ to be small compared with their wave frequency; a small Lehnert number alone does not guarantee weak diffusion.
