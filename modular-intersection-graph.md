# Modular intersection graph

↑ **Parent:** [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are $k$-subsets of $[N]$. An [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) avoids the only off-diagonal intersection congruent to $k$ modulo $p$, so the [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md) bounds its size by $\binom N{p-1}$. A [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) has constant intersection $p-1$ and has size at most $N$, by the same theorem modulo a prime larger than $k$. This explicit [graph](graph-split.md) gives an asymmetric [Ramsey number](ramsey-number.md) lower bound and an exponential [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) lower bound.
