# Midpoint parity operator

↑ **Parent:** [Parity operator](parity-operator.md)

On $L^2(0,a)$, the [midpoint parity operator](midpoint-parity-operator.md) is $(R_a\psi)(x)=\psi(a-x)$. Substitution $y=a-x$ gives $\langle f,R_ag\rangle=\langle R_af,g\rangle$ and $\|R_af\|=\|f\|$, so it is a bounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) and a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md), with $R_a^2=I$. It is the [parity operator](parity-operator.md) in the centered coordinate $x-a/2$.

For an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md), $\psi_n=\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$ obeys $R_a\psi_n=(-1)^{n+1}\psi_n$. Reflection preserves the [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) and commutes with the second derivative, so $R_a$ commutes with the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) on its [operator domain](operator-domain.md). By contrast, differentiating once gives $PR_a=-R_aP$ for the [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md). This distinction permits conserved midpoint [parity](parity.md) with changing mean momentum.
