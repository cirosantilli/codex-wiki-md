# Magic basis

↑ **Parent:** [Bell state](bell-state-split.md)

A [Bell basis](bell-basis.md) with [phases](phase-waves.md) chosen so that local special-unitary transformations of the two [qubits](qubit.md) are represented by real four-dimensional rotations. For one standard ordering it is $\Phi_+,i\Phi_-,i\Psi_+,\Psi_-$. Permuting these vectors changes generator conventions but preserves the invariant [concurrence](concurrence.md) $|\sum_jb_j^2|$.[Eqs.(3)–(6), phased Bell basis and concurrence](entanglement-of-a-pair-of-quantum-bits-by-scott-hill-and-william-k-wootters.md)
