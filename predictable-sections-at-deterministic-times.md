# Predictable sections at deterministic times

↑ **Parent:** [Predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md)

For a [predictable process](predictable-process.md) $H$ and deterministic $t>0$, $H_t$ is measurable for the [left-limit sigma-algebra](left-limit-sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal F_{t-}$. This follows by taking sections of the generating predictable rectangles. At time zero use $\mathcal F_{0-}=\mathcal F_0$.
