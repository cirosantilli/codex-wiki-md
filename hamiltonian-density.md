# Hamiltonian density

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory-split.md)

A [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) is a local integrand for the field-theory [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). For canonical fields with invertible velocity-momentum relation it is the [Legendre transform in mechanics](legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) of the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md), summing over all conjugate field components. [Gauge theories](gauge-theory.md) require constraints and multiplier terms as well.
