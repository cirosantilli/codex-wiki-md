# Dirac-Born-Infeld action

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

The [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md) describes the leading derivative dynamics of one [D-brane](d-brane.md), including nonlinear dependence on slowly varying [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) field strengths. Its induced tensors are [D-brane worldvolume pullbacks](d-brane-worldvolume-pullbacks.md). The combination $P[B]+2\pi\alpha'F$ is invariant under the combined two-form and boundary gauge transformation. The [dilaton](dilaton.md) factor reflects a disk's [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). Expanding about flat space with small field strengths and embedding gradients gives the [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) gauge and scalar kinetic terms. A coincident stack has a non-Abelian low-energy Yang-Mills action; this does not imply that a naive traced determinant is exact at every order.
