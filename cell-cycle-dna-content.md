# Cell-cycle DNA content

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

Let $n$ count [chromosomes](chromosome.md) in one complete set, and let $C$ denote the nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount in one unreplicated [haploid](haploid.md) set of this [organism](organism-split.md). [Ploidy](ploidy.md) counts sets, whereas $C$ units count material; $C$ is not a universal mass shared by all [species](species.md). Here we count a paired replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) as one until its [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) separate. A copied [diploid](diploid.md) [genome](genome.md) is therefore still [diploid](diploid.md) through [S phase](s-phase.md): $2n$ persists even as $2C$ becomes $4C$.

An original bookkeeping example uses a hypothetical [diploid](diploid.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) with $n=3$. Name its three [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md) pairs $A,a$, $B,b$ and $D,d$. Assume ordinary nuclear [DNA replication](dna-replication.md), no [chromosome](chromosome.md) loss, and one continuous double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) per unreplicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) or replicated [chromatid](chromatid.md). Count nuclear material only, excluding [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).

| Stage and counting compartment | [Chromosomes](chromosome.md) | Double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) | Nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) | 6 unreplicated | 6 | $2C$ |
| One [G2 phase](g2-phase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) | 6 replicated | 12 in 6 pairs of [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) | $4C$ |
| Whole undivided [anaphase](anaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) | 12 separated daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md) | 12 | $4C$ total |
| Each completed daughter [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) | 6 unreplicated | 6 | $2C$ each |

For example, $A$ becomes the paired copies $A_1,A_2$ during [S phase](s-phase.md). They remain one replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) until separation. Each completed daughter receives one $A$ copy and one $a$ copy, and similarly for the other two pairs. Across both daughters, $2\times6=12$ double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) and $2(2C)=4C$: division allocates the copied material without another round of [DNA replication](dna-replication.md).

The whole undivided [anaphase](anaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) transiently contains $4n$ separately counted daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md). This counting change does not establish a four-set [ploidy](ploidy.md) in a completed daughter [genome](genome.md): each pole receives $2n$, and each completed daughter [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) is [diploid](diploid.md). Always state whether a number refers to one [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), one pole, or the entire undivided [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). Compare [meiotic DNA content](meiotic-dna-content.md): after [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md), a [haploid](haploid.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) can contain $2C$, so amount alone cannot determine [ploidy](ploidy.md).
