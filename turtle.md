# Turtle

↑ **Parent:** [Diapsida](diapsida.md)

A member of the [turtle](turtle.md) lineage, whose shell and [skull](skull.md) underwent substantial modification. Molecular and [fossil](fossil.md) evidence support a [diapsid](diapsida.md) origin, with living [turtles](turtle.md) commonly recovered as sister to [archosaurs](archosauria.md).
