<h1 id="first-stiefel-whitney-class-of-a-tensor-product-of-real-line-bundles">First Stiefel–Whitney class of a tensor product of real line bundles</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md)

Real line bundles are classified by $H^1(X;\mathbb F_2)$, and tensor product corresponds to addition:

$$
w_1(L\otimes L')=w_1(L)+w_1(L').
$$
