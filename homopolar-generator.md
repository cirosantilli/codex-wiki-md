# Homopolar generator

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md)

For a conducting disc of radius $a$ rotating in a uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $B$, the shaft-to-rim [motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md) is $\int_0^a\omega rB\,dr=\omega Ba^2/2$. A current $I$ between shaft and rim gives the opposing [torque](torque.md) $IBa^2/2$, making electromagnetic conversion [power](power.md) $\mathcal E I$ equal to mechanical [torque](torque.md) times [angular speed](angular-speed.md).
