# Dimension of an algebraic set

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of a topological space by irreducible chains](dimension-of-a-topological-space-by-irreducible-chains.md)

The supremum of the lengths of strict chains of nonempty [irreducible closed subsets](irreducible-closed-subset.md). It is the maximum of the dimensions of the [irreducible components](irreducible-component.md). For an [affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md) it equals the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of its [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md); for a [quasi-projective algebraic set](quasi-projective-algebraic-set.md) it is the supremum of these dimensions over [affine open subsets](affine-open-subscheme.md).
