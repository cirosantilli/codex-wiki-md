# Buoyancy exchange time of a cold-air enclosure

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md)

Cold air leaving an opened enclosure must displace warmer air, so its driving acceleration is set by [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md), rather than by unrestricted fall through vacuum. The [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) at approximately common [pressure](pressure-split.md) gives a fractional [mass density](density.md) contrast of order $\Delta T/T$. Balancing gravitational potential energy against [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives exchange speed $U\sim\sqrt{g'H}$ over height $H$, and time $H/U$. Geometry, entrainment and shelves change its coefficient and can lengthen the time for complete replacement. The estimate describes the initial [buoyancy](buoyancy.md)-driven exchange.
