<h1 id="foster-s-theorem">Foster's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

For a finite connected unweighted loopless [graph](graph-split.md) with $n$ [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md),

$$
\sum_{e\in E}R_{\mathrm{eff}}(e)=n-1.
$$

The [edge-inclusion formula for a uniform spanning tree](edge-inclusion-formula-for-a-uniform-spanning-tree.md) gives the left-hand side as $\mathbb E|T|$, and every [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) has $n-1$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). On the same finite [connected graph](connected-graph.md) with positive [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) conductances $c_e>0$, the corresponding identity is $\sum_e c_eR_{\mathrm{eff}}(e)=n-1$.
