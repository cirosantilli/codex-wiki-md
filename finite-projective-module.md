# Finite projective module

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

A finite projective module is a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) that is a [projective module](projective-module.md). Equivalently it is a direct summand of a finite [free module](free-module.md): a finite free surjection splits by projectivity. Its [dual module](dual-module.md) is finite projective and its evaluation map to its [double dual module](double-dual-module.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), since both properties hold for finite free modules and pass to direct summands. Finite generation is needed for this elementary duality argument.
