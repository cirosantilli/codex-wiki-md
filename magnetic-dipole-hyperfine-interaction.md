# Magnetic-dipole hyperfine interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md)

The interaction of a [nuclear magnetic moment](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) with the electronic magnetic field at the nucleus. Within an isolated electronic J manifold, rotational symmetry gives the displayed effective Hamiltonian for angular momenta in hbar units. Its adjacent F intervals obey the [Landé interval rule](lande-interval-rule.md).
