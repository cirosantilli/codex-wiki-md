# Smooth-data instability of the Cauchy-Riemann Cauchy problem

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Cauchy-Riemann operator](first-order-cauchy-riemann-operator.md)

For the [first-order Cauchy-Riemann operator](first-order-cauchy-riemann-operator.md), the equation $\phi_t-i\phi_x=0$ admits the [entire functions](entire-function.md) $\phi_n(x,t)=e^{-\sqrt n}\cos(n(x+it))$. Every fixed-order [derivative](derivative.md) of the initial data converges uniformly to zero, since $n^r e^{-\sqrt n}\to0$. Nevertheless $\sup_x|\phi_n(x,t)|=e^{-\sqrt n}\cosh(n|t|)\to\infty$ for each $t\ne0$. Thus even convergence of all initial [derivatives](derivative.md) does not give [continuous dependence on initial data](continuous-dependence-on-initial-data.md) away from the initial line. Analytic existence and smooth-data stability are different properties.
