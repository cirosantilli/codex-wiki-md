<h1 id="hardy-littlewood-twin-prime-asymptotic-conjecture">Hardy–Littlewood twin-prime asymptotic conjecture</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Twin prime conjecture](twin-prime-conjecture.md)

The local-density prediction for twin primes is the displayed asymptotic, with the [twin-prime constant](twin-prime-constant.md) $C_2$. It is stronger than infinitude alone. The [Selberg sieve](selberg-sieve.md) proves the correct order of magnitude as an upper bound, but the [parity problem in sieve theory](parity-problem.md) prevents its elementary majorant from proving this conjectured asymptotic.
