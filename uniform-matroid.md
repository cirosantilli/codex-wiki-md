# Uniform matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

For $0\leq r\leq n$, the [uniform matroid](uniform-matroid.md) $U_{r,n}$ on an $n$-element [ground set](ground-set.md) has as its [matroid independent sets](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) exactly the [subsets](subset.md) of [cardinality](cardinality.md) at most $r$. Heredity is immediate, and augmentation works because any smaller such [subset](subset.md) can acquire an element of a larger one without exceeding $r$. Its [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) on $A$ is $\min\{|A|,r\}$.
