# Reliable change index

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)

A change score scaled by its expected measurement error. With equal marginal standard deviation $s$, reliability $r$ and independent measurement errors at the two assessments, the difference has error standard deviation $s\sqrt{2(1-r)}$, giving

$$
\mathrm{RCI}=\frac{X_{\mathrm{post}}-X_{\mathrm{pre}}}{s\sqrt{2(1-r)}}.
$$

A conventional normal-error criterion compares its magnitude with 1.96. The assumptions and instrument-specific reliability matter; reliable change is not itself [diagnostic remission](diagnostic-remission.md) or proof that treatment caused the change.
