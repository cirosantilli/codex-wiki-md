# Pressure measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure_measurement)

A [pressure measurement](pressure-measurement.md) determines [pressure](pressure-split.md) relative to a specified reference using an instrument such as a [manometer](manometer.md) or [pressure transducer](pressure-transducer.md). [Gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md), [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md) and differential [pressure](pressure-split.md) require different references; [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md) and [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md) determine how the reading can be interpreted.
