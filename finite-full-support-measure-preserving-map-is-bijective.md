# Finite full-support measure-preserving map is bijective

↑ **Parent:** [Measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md)

Let $X$ be a [finite set](finite-set.md), let every singleton have positive probability, and let $T:X\to X$ preserve that probability measure. For every $x\in X$,

$$
\mu(T^{-1}\{x\})=\mu(\{x\})>0,
$$

so every $x$ has a preimage. Thus $T$ is a [surjection](surjective-function.md), and every surjection from a finite set to itself is a [bijection](bijection.md).
