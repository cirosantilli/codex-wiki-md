# Laboratory scattering circle

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)

When a particle of [mass](mass.md) $M_1$ and initial [speed](speed.md) $u_1$ strikes a stationary [mass](mass.md) $M_2$, the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) moves at $\mathbf V=M_1u_1\widehat{\mathbf x}/(M_1+M_2)$. If the [relative speed retention in an oblique collision](relative-speed-retention-in-an-oblique-collision.md) is $q$, its possible outgoing [velocity](velocity.md) vectors lie on the displayed circle in a scattering plane. Intersect a ray of fixed laboratory direction with that circle to obtain the quadratic formula for outgoing [speed](speed.md). A negative root is inadmissible, and the origin has no defined [scattering angle](scattering-angle.md). Tangency gives the maximum laboratory angle when the circle excludes the origin.
