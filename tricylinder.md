# Tricylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Steinmetz solid](steinmetz-solid.md)

The common intersection of three equal-radius [circular cylinders](circular-cylinder.md) with mutually perpendicular axes. The displayed inequalities describe a centered example of radius $a$. It is a [Steinmetz solid](steinmetz-solid.md); its [surface area](surface-area.md) is given by the [surface area of a tricylinder](surface-area-of-a-tricylinder.md) formula.
