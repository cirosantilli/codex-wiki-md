# Open dust linear growth mode

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

For [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) in a [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with negative [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) and no [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), set $x=(1-\Omega_{m0})a/\Omega_{m0}>0$. A decaying [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md) is $D_-(x)=\sqrt{(1+x)/x^3}$. [Reduction of order](reduction-of-order.md) gives the regular growing [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

$$
D_+(x)=D_-(x)\int_0^x\left(\frac{y}{1+y}\right)^{3/2}dy=1+\frac3x-3D_-(x)\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt x.
$$

Its [Wronskian](wronskian.md) with $D_-$ is $x^{-3/2}(1+x)^{-1/2}$, so the modes are independent. The limits are $D_+\sim2x/5$ during [matter domination](matter-domination.md) and $D_+\to1$ in a [curvature-dominated universe](curvature-dominated-universe.md). Multiplying by $5/2$ normalizes the early mode to $x$. Negative [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) thus stops late linear amplification even though the model contains no [dark energy](dark-energy.md).
