# Scientific confirmation

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)

Evidence supports a scientific hypothesis without necessarily proving it. Distinguish increasing its credibility from showing it highly credible in absolute terms; a low [prior probability](prior-probability.md) can rise substantially while remaining low.
