# Curvaton

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curvaton)

A curvaton is a light spectator scalar field whose energy density is negligible during inflation but whose fluctuations can later generate the primordial curvature perturbation.
