# Homotopy groups of the stunted projective space through degree nine

↑ **Parent:** [Stunted real projective space](stunted-real-projective-space.md)

For this quotient, homotopy groups through degree six and in degree eight vanish. The degree-seven and degree-nine groups are $\mathbb Z/2$. A map to $K(\mathbb Z/2,7)$ induces an isomorphism on degree-nine integral [homology](homology-split.md) because the relevant square of the bottom mod-two class is nonzero. Two relative Hurewicz steps then compute degrees eight and nine.
