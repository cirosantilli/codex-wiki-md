# Color charge

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_charge)

Color charge is the [gauge group](gauge-group.md) charge of [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md), associated with its $SU(3)$ symmetry. A [quark](quark.md) transforms in the fundamental three-dimensional representation, an [antiquark](antiquark.md) in its conjugate, and a [lepton](lepton.md) is a singlet. The word color is a label for this charge, unrelated to visible light.
