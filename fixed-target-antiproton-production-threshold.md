# Fixed-target antiproton production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Antiproton](antiproton.md)

The [fixed-target production threshold](fixed-target-production-threshold.md) concerns the incident accelerator beam, whereas [antiproton mass from momentum and velocity](antiproton-mass-from-momentum-and-velocity.md) concerns an outgoing secondary beam. To see the distinction, consider a free [proton](proton.md) hitting a stationary free [proton](proton.md), producing

$$
p+p\longrightarrow p+p+p+\bar p.
$$

The final [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is $+2e$ and final [baryon number](baryon-number.md) is $3-1=2$, as initially. Approximate all four final [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) by the same [proton](proton.md) [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$. With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, define the squared [centre-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md) by $W^2=c^2(P_1+P_2)^2$. The incident [proton](proton.md) has total [energy](energy-split.md) $E=mc^2+K$, and the target [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is $(mc,\mathbf0)$. [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) and the [mass shell](mass-shell.md) relation give

$$
W^2=2m^2c^4+2mc^2E=4m^2c^4+2mc^2K.
$$

At threshold the four final particles have zero relative [momentum](momentum-split.md) in their [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), so $W_{\mathrm{thr}}=4mc^2$. Hence

$$
16m^2c^4=4m^2c^4+2mc^2K_{\mathrm{thr}},\qquad K_{\mathrm{thr}}=6mc^2\simeq5.63\,\mathrm{GeV}.
$$

Only $2mc^2$ of new [rest energy](rest-energy.md) is created. The larger incident [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is required because the final system must also carry the initial laboratory [momentum](momentum-split.md). The incident total [energy](energy-split.md) at threshold is $7mc^2$; neither this nor $K_{\mathrm{thr}}$ is the selected secondary [momentum](momentum-split.md).

The 1955 authors explicitly qualified the stationary-nucleon threshold: target [nucleon](nucleon.md) motion inside an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) can lower the required incident [energy](energy-split.md).[UCRL-3172, p. 3](https://escholarship.org/content/qt46p0z8w7/qt46p0z8w7.pdf)

The kinematic reason is visible for two on-shell incoming [nucleons](nucleon.md):

$$
W^2=2m^2c^4+2\left(E_bE_t-c^2\mathbf p_b\cdot\mathbf p_t\right).
$$

A target [momentum](momentum-split.md) directed against the beam increases $W^2$. Applying this illustration to a bound [nucleon](nucleon.md) also requires [binding energy](binding-energy.md) and residual-[nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) recoil; the free stationary-target $6mc^2$ result is therefore not a universal sharp threshold for a [copper](copper.md) target. A low observed secondary [momentum](momentum-split.md) is entirely compatible with a much larger accelerator [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
