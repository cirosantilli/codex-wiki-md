# Receiver noise temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Noise temperature](noise-temperature.md)

The [receiver noise temperature](receiver-noise-temperature.md) represents noise added by the receiver as an equivalent [noise temperature](noise-temperature.md) referred to its input. Separating it from the [antenna temperature](antenna-temperature.md) prevents internal receiver noise from being mistaken for radiation from the sky.
