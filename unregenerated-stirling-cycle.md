# Unregenerated Stirling cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Stirling engine](stirling-engine.md)

For an ideal working gas and $r=V_2/V_1>1$, two isotherms at $T_h,T_c$ produce net [work](work.md) $nR(T_h-T_c)\ln r$. Without a regenerator, isochoric heating requires additional external [heat](heat-split.md) $C_V(T_h-T_c)$, giving the displayed [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md), strictly below [Carnot efficiency](carnot-efficiency.md). A perfect reversible regenerator recovers this heat and attains the bound instead. Reversing the unregenerated cycle gives cooling [coefficient of performance](coefficient-of-performance.md) $T_c/(T_h-T_c)-C_V/(nR\ln r)$; positive cooling requires a sufficiently large expansion ratio.
