# Schmidt subspace theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_subspace_theorem)

Let $L_1,\ldots,L_n$ be linearly independent [linear forms](linear-functional.md) in $n$ variables with algebraic coefficients. For every $\varepsilon>0$, the nonzero $\mathbf x\in\mathbb Z^n$ satisfying

$$
\prod_{i=1}^n|L_i(\mathbf x)|
<\|\mathbf x\|^{-\varepsilon}
$$

lie in finitely many proper [linear subspaces](vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb Q^n$. There are variants involving a finite set of places of a [number field](number-field.md).
