# Paraelectricity

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization density](polarization-density.md)

A phase without spontaneous switchable [electric polarization](polarization-density.md), although an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can induce [electric polarization](polarization-density.md). A material can be [paraelectric](paraelectricity.md) above a transition to a [ferroelectric](ferroelectricity.md) phase.
