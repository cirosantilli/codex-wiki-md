# Solid angle

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_angle)

A solid angle is the area cut out on a unit [two-sphere](two-sphere.md) by a cone of directions. Its SI unit is the steradian, and a full sphere subtends $4\pi$ steradians.
