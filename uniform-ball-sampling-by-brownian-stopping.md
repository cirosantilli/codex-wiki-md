# Uniform ball sampling by Brownian stopping

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero, choose an [independent](independent-random-variables.md) radius $R$ with $\mathbb P(R\leq s)=(s/r)^n$, $0\leq s\leq r$, and stop on first reaching that radius. [Rotational invariance of Brownian motion](rotational-invariance-of-brownian-motion.md) makes the conditional exit position uniform on each sphere. Mixing with radius density $ns^{n-1}/r^n$ gives the uniform [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) on the ball. In dimension one, the conditional sign has probabilities $1/2,1/2$. The stopped point samples volume measure even though each conditional exit law is supported on a boundary.
