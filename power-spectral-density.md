# Power spectral density

↑ **Parent:** [Signal](signal.md)

A [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) distributes the mean square of a [signal](signal.md) over [frequency](frequency.md). Its integral over the specified [frequency](frequency.md) domain gives that mean square. State whether the convention is one-sided or two-sided and whether [frequency](frequency.md) is measured in [hertz](hertz.md) or as [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). For a zero-mean stationary [signal](signal.md), the mean square is its [variance](variance-split.md); [spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) gives the discrete-time convention.
