# Signed area as a boundary integral

↑ **Parent:** [Green theorem](green-theorem.md)

For a counterclockwise, piecewise smooth simple closed planar curve $C$ enclosing $D$, [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md) with $P=-y/2$ and $Q=x/2$ gives $\frac12\oint_C(x\,dy-y\,dx)=\iint_D1\,dx\,dy$. The integral is the [area](surface-area.md) of $D$ with a sign determined by the traversal direction. For [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) $x=r\cos\theta$, $y=r\sin\theta$, differentiation gives $x\,dy-y\,dx=r^2d\theta$, yielding the familiar polar area formula when the curve is traversed once.
