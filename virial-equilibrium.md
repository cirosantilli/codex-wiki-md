# Virial equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

A bounded gravitational system in statistical equilibrium satisfies the time-averaged [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md). For inverse-distance gravity with negligible boundary stresses, $2K+W=0$. This relation does not require static particles or a homogeneous density profile, and reaching it does not erase the system's dynamical history.
