# Degenerate codon

↑ **Parent:** [Codon](codon.md)

A [codon](codon.md) position synthesized as a nucleotide mixture, producing several [codons](codon.md) across a [library](dna-library.md). Equal nucleotide mixture proportions do not generally produce equal [amino-acid](amino-acid.md) frequencies.
