# Self-duality of the Koszul complex

↑ **Parent:** [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md)

The perfect exterior pairing in complementary degrees identifies the dual of a [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md) with its degree reversal, with suitable differential signs. For a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md), its [Koszul resolution](koszul-resolution.md) consequently gives $\operatorname{Ext}_R^i(R/(f),R)=0$ except in degree $r$, where it is $R/(f)$.
