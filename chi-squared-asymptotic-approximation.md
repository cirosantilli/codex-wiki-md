# Chi-squared asymptotic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md)

Many quadratic and likelihood-ratio statistics converge under a regular null hypothesis to a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md). Using its quantiles for a finite sample is accurate when the sample is large relative to the model dimension and expected category counts are not too small.
