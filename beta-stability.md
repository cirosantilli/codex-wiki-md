# Beta stability

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)

A [nuclide](nuclide.md) is beta-stable if it has no energetically allowed single [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md), [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md), or [electron capture](electron-capture.md) to a lower-mass neighboring [nuclear isobar](isobar-nuclide.md), with electron thresholds treated consistently. Beta stability need not imply stability against [alpha decay](alpha-decay.md), [double beta decay](double-beta-decay.md), or [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md).
