# Gaussian error

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

A [Gaussian error](gaussian-error.md) is an error term with a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Independent [Gaussian errors](gaussian-error.md) of equal [variance](variance-split.md) turn [least squares](least-squares.md) into maximum likelihood and make standardized linear coefficient [estimators](estimator.md) exactly normal. Zero mean and common [variance](variance-split.md) alone do not imply [Gaussian errors](gaussian-error.md).
