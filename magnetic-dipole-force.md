# Magnetic dipole force

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole potential energy](magnetic-dipole-potential-energy.md)

For a small fixed [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $\mathbf m$ in a slowly varying external [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), $U=-\mathbf m\cdot\mathbf B$ gives the displayed [force](force-split.md). For a compact current loop, integrate $I\,d\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf B$ to first order in the field's spatial variation to get the same result. For a coaxial small loop with $m_z=NI\pi a^2$, this becomes $F_z=m_z\,dB_z/dz$. A spatially uniform field can exert [torque](torque.md) but gives zero net [force](force-split.md); a field [gradient](gradient.md) is essential. The approximation assumes the field does not appreciably vary over the dipole's dimensions.
