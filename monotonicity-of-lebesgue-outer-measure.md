# Monotonicity of Lebesgue outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md)

If $A\subseteq B$, every countable [interval](interval-mathematics.md) cover of $B$ is also a cover of $A$. Taking the [infimum](infimum.md) of the lengths over the larger family of covers can only decrease the result. Thus [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md) is monotone on all [subsets](subset.md), with no [measurability](measurability.md) assumption.
