# Supergravity auxiliary field

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

After eliminating the chiral [auxiliary fields](auxiliary-field.md) in Planck-unit [supergravity](supergravity.md), one convention gives $F^i=-e^{K/2}K^{i\bar j}\overline{D_jW}$. The upper-index inverse satisfies $K_{i\bar j}K^{k\bar j}=\delta_i^k$. Nonzero auxiliary expectation values diagnose [supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md); their common phase convention does not affect the vanishing condition.
