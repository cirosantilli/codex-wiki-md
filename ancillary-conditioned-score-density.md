# Ancillary-conditioned score density

↑ **Parent:** [P-star approximation](p-star-approximation.md)

Fix an [ancillary statistic](ancillary-statistic.md) $a$ and a parameter value at which the [score function](informant-function.md) is evaluated. Express that score as a function $u(v,a)$ of the varying [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) coordinate $v$. The ordinary density change-of-variables formula applied to the [P-star approximation](p-star-approximation.md) gives the displayed density; several monotone branches require a sum. Its derivative is a sample-coordinate derivative, not generally the parameter [observed information](observed-fisher-information.md).
