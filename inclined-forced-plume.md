# Inclined forced plume

↑ **Parent:** [Forced plume](forced-plume.md)

An inclined [forced plume](forced-plume.md) curves upward under [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). In a stationary homogeneous ambient, its horizontal [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) is conserved and its vertical [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) increases. For [kinematic plume fluxes](kinematic-plume-fluxes.md), $m_x'=0$ and $m_z'=fq/m$, where $m=\sqrt{m_x^2+m_z^2}$ and the derivative is with respect to centreline [arc length](arc-length.md).
