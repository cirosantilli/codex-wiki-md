# Log odds ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Odds ratio](odds-ratio.md)

The log odds ratio is the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of an [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md). For two [independent](independent-random-variables.md) binomial groups with positive event and non-event counts $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$, its estimate is $\log(ad/(bc))$, with first-order [variance](variance-split.md) $1/a+1/b+1/c+1/d$ by the [delta method](delta-method.md). Zero cells need a method that handles boundary data; the displayed normal approximation is not valid there.
