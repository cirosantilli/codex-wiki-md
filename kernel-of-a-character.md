# Kernel of a character

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

For a complex character $\chi$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md), its kernel is the kernel of an affording [group representation](group-representation.md). Averaging an inner product makes that representation unitary. If its degree is $d$, all eigenvalues of a group element have modulus one, so their sum equals $d$ only when every eigenvalue is one. Thus the displayed character-value criterion agrees with the representation kernel and defines a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md).
