# Hayashi relation with eighth-power temperature opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Hayashi track](hayashi-track.md)

Match a fully convective [stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md) of index $3/2$ to the [grey-atmosphere convection onset with eighth-power temperature opacity](grey-atmosphere-convection-onset-with-eighth-power-temperature-opacity.md). At the fixed matching optical depth, $T_b\propto T_e$ and $P_b\propto g^{2/3}T_e^{-16/3}$. The ideal-gas adiabat $P=K\rho^{5/3}$ gives $K\propto T_b^{5/3}P_b^{-2/3}\propto T_e^{47/9}g^{-4/9}$. The [polytropic mass-radius relation](polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md) gives $K\propto RM^{1/3}$; with $g\propto M/R^2$, this yields $T_e^{47}\propto RM^7$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) then gives the displayed luminosity. Composition and opacity normalization are held fixed between models.
