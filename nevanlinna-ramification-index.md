# Nevanlinna ramification index

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna theory](nevanlinna-theory.md)

The [Nevanlinna ramification index](nevanlinna-ramification-index.md) is the normalized lower limiting integrated excess [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) over a target value, with [pole](pole.md) orders used for infinity. It is an asymptotic value-distribution quantity, distinct from the integer [ramification index of a holomorphic map](ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) at one source point. It can be positive even when the target has positive [Nevanlinna deficiency](nevanlinna-deficiency.md).
