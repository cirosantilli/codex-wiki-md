# Scramblase

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

A [scramblase](scramblase.md) facilitates relatively nonselective bidirectional movement of [lipids](lipid.md) between [membrane leaflets](membrane-leaflet.md), tending to dissipate asymmetry when active. Its action differs from directional transport used to maintain asymmetry.
