# Stable complex structure on a real vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md)

For an even-rank real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $\xi$, a stable complex structure is a complex structure on $\xi\oplus\mathbb R^{2s}$, considered up to adding trivial complex bundles and homotopy. This gives a stable complex class but does not necessarily make $\xi$ itself a complex vector bundle. Stable Chern classes therefore need not recover the unstable [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md): $TS^4\oplus\mathbb R^2$ can be made trivial complex, while $e(TS^4)$ evaluates to $2$.
