# Free scalar four-point function

↑ **Parent:** [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md)

For a centered free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md), its vacuum [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) has expectation $\langle T\phi_1\phi_2\phi_3\phi_4\rangle=G_{12}G_{34}+G_{13}G_{24}+G_{14}G_{23}$, where $G_{ij}$ is the [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md) between insertions $i,j$. These are the three complete pairings in the [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md). All remaining [normal-ordered products](normal-ordered-product.md) have zero vacuum expectation. No fermionic interchange signs occur for a bosonic field.
