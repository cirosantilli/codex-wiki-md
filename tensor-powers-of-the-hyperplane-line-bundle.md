# Tensor powers of the hyperplane line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md)

For positive $k$ take the $k$th tensor power of the [hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md); for negative $k$ take the corresponding power of its dual; for $k=0$ take the trivial line bundle. On the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md), global sections for $k\geq0$ correspond to polynomials of degree at most $k$ and form a space of dimension $k+1$.
