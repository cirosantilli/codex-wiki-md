# Quantum plane

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)

For a [field](field.md) $k$ and $q\in k^\times$, the quantum plane is $k_q[X,Y]=k\langle X,Y\rangle/(YX-qXY)$. Its ordered [monomials](monomial.md) $X^iY^j$, $i,j\geq0$, are a [basis](basis.md), and their multiplication is $X^iY^jX^uY^v=q^{ju}X^{i+u}Y^{j+v}$. Leading [monomials](monomial.md) show it is a [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md). Some sources interchange $q$ with $q^{-1}$ in the defining relation.
