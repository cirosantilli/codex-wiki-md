# Regular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth curve](smooth-curve.md)

A smooth parametrized curve is regular when its velocity never vanishes.

Regularity is an additional differential condition on a [smooth curve](smooth-curve.md), not a synonym for an arbitrary [curve](curve.md).
