# Single-walled carbon nanotube

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md)

A [carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md) comprising one rolled [graphene](graphene.md) wall. The wrapping indices determine its allowed circumferential electronic wave vectors and therefore its metallic or semiconducting band structure.
