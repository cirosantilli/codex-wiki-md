# Caller-saved register

↑ **Parent:** [Calling convention](calling-convention.md)

A [processor register](processor-register.md) that a subroutine may overwrite. A caller retaining a live value in such a [processor register](processor-register.md) must preserve it elsewhere before calling. Argument and result [processor registers](processor-register.md) commonly have this role.
