# Fourier transform of a Gaussian

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

With $\widehat f(k)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ikx}dx$ and $a>0$,

$$
\widehat{e^{-ax^2+ibx}}(k)
=\sqrt{\frac\pi a}\exp\left(-\frac{(k-b)^2}{4a}\right).
$$
