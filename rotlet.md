# Rotlet

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating sphere in Stokes flow](rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotlet)

A rotlet is the singular [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) generated by a point torque $\mathbf G$:

$$
\mathbf u(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mathbf G\times\mathbf r}{8\pi\mu r^3},
\qquad p=0.
$$

It is the far field of a [rotating sphere in Stokes flow](rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md).
