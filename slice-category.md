# Slice category

↑ **Parent:** [Comma category](comma-category.md)

The slice over $A$ has [morphisms](morphism.md) $x:X\to A$ as objects and commuting triangles $yu=x$ as arrows $u:(X,x)\to(Y,y)$. Its [terminal object](terminal-object.md) is $1_A$, regardless of whether $\mathcal C$ has a [terminal object](terminal-object.md). It is the [comma category](comma-category.md) $(1_{\mathcal C}\downarrow A)$, viewing $A$ as a [functor](functor.md) from the [terminal category](terminal-category.md).
