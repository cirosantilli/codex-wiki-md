# Aluminium phosphide

↑ **Parent:** [III-V semiconductor](iii-v-semiconductor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium_phosphide)

A compound of [aluminium](aluminium.md) and [phosphorus](phosphorus.md), with two [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) per [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) in its usual [zinc blende structure](zinc-blende-structure.md). Its two inequivalent [atomic basis](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) sites give six [phonon branches](phonon-branch.md), three acoustic and three optical.
