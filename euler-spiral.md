# Euler spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Curve](curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_spiral)

The [Euler spiral](euler-spiral.md) is the planar [curve](curve.md) parametrized by the [Fresnel integrals](fresnel-integral.md). In the optical convention,

$$
C(u)+iS(u)=\int_0^u e^{i\pi t^2/2}\,dt.
$$

The tangent has unit length and argument $\pi u^2/2$, so its signed [curvature](curvature.md) is $\pi u$, proportional to signed [arc length](arc-length.md). In [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md), an interval integral is the directed chord joining its endpoint parameters on the [Cornu spiral](euler-spiral.md). Chord length gives the interval factor's [modulus](modulus.md), while chord direction gives its [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md).
