# Paired eigenspaces of the square of a real skew-symmetric matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md)

For a real [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) $M$, $M^2$ is symmetric and $x^TM^2x=-\|Mx\|^2$. On an [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-\mu^2<0$, every unit [vector](vector.md) $u$ has unit partner $v=Mu/\mu$, orthogonal to $u$, with $Mu=\mu v$ and $Mv=-\mu u$. Its plane and its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) are invariant under $M$. Repeating gives real orthogonal two-dimensional blocks $\begin{pmatrix}0&-\mu\\\mu&0\end{pmatrix}$ and a zero block. Thus every nonzero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $M^2$ has even [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md), the zero multiplicity has the parity of $n$, and the number of distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) is at most $n/2$ for even $n$ and $(n+1)/2$ for odd $n$. Distinct positive block parameters attain the bound, with one zero direction added when $n$ is odd.
