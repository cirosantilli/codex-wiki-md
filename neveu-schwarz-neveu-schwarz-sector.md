<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-neveu-schwarz-sector">Neveu–Schwarz–Neveu–Schwarz sector</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md)

The closed-string sector with [NS sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) excitations on both chiral sides. Its massless transverse tensor product is $8_v\otimes8_v=35_v+28+1$, giving the [graviton](graviton.md), [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md) and [dilaton](dilaton.md). These fields are common to the type II theories.
