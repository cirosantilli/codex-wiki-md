# Hot and cold starts of a giant planet

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

Accretion releases gravitational energy. Efficient radiation at an accretion shock leaves a lower-entropy cold start, while greater retained energy gives a higher-entropy hot start; intermediate outcomes are possible. Initial entropy changes a young planet's radius, luminosity and spectrum. Consequently an [exoplanet direct imaging](exoplanet-direct-imaging.md) luminosity and age need not uniquely fix mass without a formation/evolution model.
