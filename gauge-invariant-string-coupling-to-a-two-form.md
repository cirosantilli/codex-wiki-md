# Gauge-invariant string coupling to a two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Two-form gauge field](two-form-gauge-field.md)

The coupling $q\int_\Sigma X^*B$ integrates the pullback of the [two-form gauge field](two-form-gauge-field.md) over the [string worldsheet](worldsheet.md). Its gauge variation is $q\int_{\partial\Sigma}X^*\alpha$, by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). It therefore vanishes for a closed worldsheet, while an open surface requires suitable boundary fields or boundary-state transformations.
