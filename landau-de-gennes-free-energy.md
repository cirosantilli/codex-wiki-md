# Landau-de Gennes free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md)

The Landau-de Gennes free energy is a [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) preserving [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) of the [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md). One three-dimensional normalization through fourth order is

$$
f(Q)=\frac a2\operatorname{Tr}Q^2+\frac c3\operatorname{Tr}Q^3+\frac b4(\operatorname{Tr}Q^2)^2,
\qquad b>0.
$$

The cubic invariant generically produces a [first-order phase transition](first-order-phase-transition.md). Its absence in two dimensions is an algebraic identity, not a guarantee that a [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) captures all fluctuations.
