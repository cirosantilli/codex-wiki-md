# Constrained Poisson and multinomial likelihood equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md)

For cell means $\mu_j$ with $\sum_j\mu_j=n$, the independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) likelihood and the [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md) likelihood with probabilities $\mu_j/n$ differ by a parameter-independent factor. Thus a [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) for the unrestricted Poisson model whose fitted total is $n$ is also an estimator for the constrained multinomial model. Equality of named parameter estimates presumes identifiability and uniqueness; without these, the conclusion concerns the sets of maximizers. A free intercept in a log-linear Poisson model enforces the fitted-total equation.
