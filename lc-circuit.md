# LC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LC_circuit)

An ideal [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) exchanges [energy](energy-split.md) between an [inductor](inductor.md) and a [capacitor](capacitor.md). With no [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) or applied drive, the [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) obeys $Lq''+q/C=0$, giving [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$. A [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) adds dissipation and can include an external drive.
