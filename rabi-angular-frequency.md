# Rabi angular frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md)

The [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) is the on-resonance population oscillation rate in the specified [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md). The rotating-frame off-diagonal [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) entry has magnitude $\hbar\Omega/2$. Stating this convention avoids a factor-of-two ambiguity between drive coefficients and oscillation rates. The generalized rate off resonance is $\Omega_{\mathrm{eff}}=\sqrt{\Omega^2+\delta^2}$.
