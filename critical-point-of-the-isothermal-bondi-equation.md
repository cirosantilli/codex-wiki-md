# Critical point of the isothermal Bondi equation

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi sonic point](bondi-sonic-point.md)

At the critical point, both the coefficient of $du/dr$ and the remaining right-hand side of the [Isothermal Bondi equation](isothermal-bondi-equation.md) vanish, allowing a finite derivative through the sonic transition.
