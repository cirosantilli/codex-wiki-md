<h1 id="convex-poincare-inequality">Convex Poincaré inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality in probability theory](poincare-inequality-in-probability-theory.md)

For independent random variables supported on $[0,1]$ and a differentiable convex function $f$,

$$
\operatorname{Var}(f(X))\leq\mathbb E\lVert\nabla f(X)\rVert^2.
$$
