# Polynomial tails prevent exponential tilting

↑ **Parent:** [Saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md)

If a density has polynomially decaying tails on both half-lines, its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is infinite at every nonzero real parameter: exponential growth defeats polynomial decay on the positive tail for a positive parameter, and on the negative tail for a negative parameter. Thus no open real tilting domain exists for a [saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md), even when the distribution has finite mean and variance and a [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) applies.
