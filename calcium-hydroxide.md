# Calcium hydroxide

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium](calcium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_hydroxide)

[Calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md) supplies [calcium](calcium.md) [cations](cation.md) and [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) [anions](anion.md) on [dissolution](dissolution.md) in [water](water.md). The ideal one-to-two [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) relates [molar solubility](molar-solubility.md) to the dissolved [concentrations](concentration.md); [solubility product measurement by titration](solubility-product-measurement-by-titration.md) explains how the [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) inventory is measured.
