<h1 id="five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-black-hole">Five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom black hole</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

The static spherically symmetric electrically charged black hole in five spacetime dimensions has $f(r)=1-2M/r^2+Q^2/r^4$. When $M>Q$ it has outer and inner horizon radii $r_\pm^2=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2}$; for $M=Q$ it is an [extremal black hole](extremal-black-hole.md), while $M<Q$ exposes a [naked singularity](naked-singularity.md).
