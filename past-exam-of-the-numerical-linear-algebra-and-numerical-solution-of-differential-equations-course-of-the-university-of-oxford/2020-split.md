↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical linear algebra and numerical solution of differential equations course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source paper: [https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/suli/MMSC_2020.pdf](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/suli/MMSC_2020.pdf)

This is the B1 paper for the [MSc in mathematical modelling and scientific computing course of the University of Oxford](../msc-in-mathematical-modelling-and-scientific-computing-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md). Questions 1–2 have original solutions below. The [official revision page](https://courses.maths.ox.ac.uk/course/view.php?id=5523) identifies [public model solutions to questions 3–4](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/suli/MSc_2020_Solutions_to_Q3_and_Q4.pdf), so those questions are skipped. The programme also advertises a [student-only past-papers-and-solutions folder](https://courses.maths.ox.ac.uk/mod/folder/view.php?id=65499); its exact coverage of this paper has not been established.
