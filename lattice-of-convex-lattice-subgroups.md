# Lattice of convex lattice subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md)

The [convex lattice subgroups](convex-lattice-subgroup.md) of a [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md) form a distributive [lattice](lattice.md), with intersection as [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and the convex lattice subgroup generated by a union as [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). A positive element of a join is bounded by a finite product of positive elements from the generating subgroups. [Riesz decomposition in a lattice-ordered group](riesz-decomposition-in-a-lattice-ordered-group.md) splits such an element into positive factors from those subgroups. If the original element belongs to another convex subgroup $E$, every factor does too. Consequently $E\cap(C\vee D)=(E\cap C)\vee(E\cap D)$.
