# Inverse hyperbolic sine

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse hyperbolic functions](inverse-hyperbolic-functions.md)

The real inverse of the [hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md) is defined on all real numbers and has [derivative](derivative.md) $1/\sqrt{1+x^2}$. It appears in [arc length](arc-length.md) and in [integrals](integral-split.md) involving square roots of sums of squares, where a [hyperbolic substitution](hyperbolic-substitution.md) can simplify the expression.
