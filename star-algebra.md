# Star-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A complex [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) with a conjugate-linear involution satisfying the displayed product-reversal identities. An [operator algebra](operator-algebra.md) closed under the Hilbert-space adjoint is a [star-algebra](star-algebra.md). A [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) additionally has a complete [norm](norm.md) with $\|a^*a\|=\|a\|^2$.
