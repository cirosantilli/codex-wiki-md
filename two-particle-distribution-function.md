# Two-particle distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md)

For $N$ identical particles with symmetric, unit-normalized [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) $D_N$, the number-normalized two-particle marginal is $f_2(z_1,z_2)=N(N-1)\int D_N\,dz_3\cdots dz_N$. Its integral over $z_2$ is $(N-1)f(z_1)$ and its full integral is $N(N-1)$. The factorial convention counts ordered distinct pairs and absorbs the combinatorial coefficient in the first [BBGKY hierarchy](bbgky-hierarchy.md) equation. It does not imply statistical independence of the pair.
