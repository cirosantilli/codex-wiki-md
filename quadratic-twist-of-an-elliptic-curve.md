# Quadratic twist of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

For an elliptic curve $E:y^2=f(x)$ over a field of characteristic other than two and $d\in K^\times$, the curve $E^{(d)}:dy^2=f(x)$ is its quadratic twist by $d$. It becomes isomorphic to $E$ after adjoining $\sqrt d$; over a finite field its Frobenius trace is multiplied by the quadratic character of $d$.
