<h1 id="faraday-s-carbon-dioxide-and-ether-cooling-experiment">Faraday's carbon-dioxide and ether cooling experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Evaporative cooling (atomic physics)](evaporative-cooling-atomic-physics.md)

[Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md)'s 1845 experiment combined solid [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), [diethyl ether](diethyl-ether.md) and reduced [pressure](pressure-split.md). Removal of vapour cooled the bath further. He reported a low alcohol-thermometer indication near $-166\,{}^\circ\mathrm F$, while explicitly discussing lag and uncertainty in the graduation at low [temperature](temperature-split.md).[printed p. 158](on-the-liquefaction-and-solidification-of-bodies-generally-existing-as-gases-by-michael-faraday.md)
