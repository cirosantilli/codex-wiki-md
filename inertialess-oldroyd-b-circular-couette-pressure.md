# Inertialess Oldroyd-B circular Couette pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Oldroyd-B model](oldroyd-b-model.md)

For circular [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) with $v=Ar+B/r$, the [rotating-frame reduction of circular viscoelastic shear](rotating-frame-reduction-of-circular-viscoelastic-shear.md) gives shear rate $g=-2B/r^2$. The total [shear stress](shear-stress.md) is $\mu g$, and the azimuthal minus radial [normal-stress difference](normal-stress-difference.md) is $2(\mu-\mu_r)\tau g^2$ in the stress convention where $\sigma_{rr}=-p$. Radial [force balance](force-balance.md) without inertia is $p'=-2(\mu-\mu_r)\tau g^2/r$, which integrates to the displayed pressure. The pressure constant is undetermined by these equations.
