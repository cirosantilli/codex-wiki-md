# Tilted parallel-plate capacitor at fixed charge

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md)

For square plates with a linearly varying gap from $a-\delta$ to $a+\delta$, the local-strip approximation gives the displayed [capacitance](capacitance.md) for $|\delta|<a$. Its expansion is $C=(\epsilon_0s^2/a)[1+\delta^2/(3a^2)+O(\delta^4/a^4)]$. An isolated capacitor has fixed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), so $U=Q^2/(2C)$ decreases as $|\delta|$ increases. A sinusoidal small tilt therefore produces a leading energy variation at twice the tilt frequency.
