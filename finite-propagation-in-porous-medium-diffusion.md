# Finite propagation in porous-medium diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md)

In a [porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md) with exponent $m>1$, the effective [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) vanishes at zero density. A nonnegative compactly supported source profile can therefore retain a finite front for every finite positive time. For example, the [radial cubic-diffusion source profile](radial-cubic-diffusion-source-profile.md) has support radius proportional to $t^{1/6}$. This notion concerns a moving diffusion front; it does not require the constant characteristic cone used for a [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md).
