# Absolute hydrodynamic instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

An [absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md) makes the response to a localized impulse grow at a fixed spatial point in a specified frame. This differs from [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md), which amplifies a moving [wave packet](wave-packet.md) while its response decays at fixed positions. For analytic [dispersion relations](dispersion-relation.md), the relevant [absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md) is associated with an accessible zero-[group velocity](group-velocity.md) saddle, with the spatial-branch selection checked rather than assumed for an arbitrary dispersion relation.
