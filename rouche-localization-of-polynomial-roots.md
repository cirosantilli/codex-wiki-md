<h1 id="rouche-localization-of-polynomial-roots">Rouché localization of polynomial roots</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rouché's theorem](rouche-s-theorem.md)

Let $P(z)=\prod_\ell(z-r_\ell)$ have distinct roots. Choose a disc around $r_j$ with radius $\delta$ smaller than every separation from $r_j$. On its boundary, $|P(z)|\geq\delta\prod_{\ell\ne j}(|r_j-r_\ell|-\delta)$. If a [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) perturbation $H$ satisfies the displayed inequality, [Rouché's theorem](rouche-s-theorem.md) shows that $P+H$ has exactly one root in that disc, counted with multiplicity. Discs confined to specified half-planes or quadrants give direct geometric root-location proofs without needing a global root-continuation argument.
