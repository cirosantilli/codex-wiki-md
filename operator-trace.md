# Operator trace

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md)

For a trace-class operator, the series $\operatorname{tr}T=\sum_n\langle Te_n,e_n\rangle$ converges absolutely and is independent of the orthonormal basis. It satisfies $|\operatorname{tr}T|\leq\|T\|_1$.

The [trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md) condition makes this [operator trace](operator-trace.md) well-defined; the class of eligible operators and the trace functional are distinct concepts.
