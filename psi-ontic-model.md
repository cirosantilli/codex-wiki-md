# Psi-ontic model

↑ **Parent:** [Ontological model of a quantum system](ontological-model-of-a-quantum-system.md)

An [ontological model of a quantum system](ontological-model-of-a-quantum-system.md) is psi-ontic if distinct [pure states](pure-state.md) have mutually singular preparation distributions. The physical state then determines which pure [quantum state](quantum-state.md) was prepared, almost surely in the pairwise sense. Extra underlying variables may still exist. The [PBR theorem](pusey-barrett-rudolph-theorem.md) establishes this conclusion under [preparation independence](preparation-independence.md) and exact quantum predictions.
