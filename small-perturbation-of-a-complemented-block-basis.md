# Small perturbation of a complemented block basis

↑ **Parent:** [Block basic sequence](block-basic-sequence.md)

Suppose $(u_n)$ is a normalized [block basic sequence](block-basic-sequence.md) in the [l-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md), $1\le p<\infty$, and $\sum_n\|x_n-u_n\|_p<1$. Choose norm-one [linear functionals](linear-functional.md) $\phi_n$ supported on the respective blocks with $\phi_n(u_n)=1$. The [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $A=I+\sum_n\phi_n(\cdot)(x_n-u_n)$ is invertible by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md). The block projection $Qz=\sum_n\phi_n(z)u_n$ has norm at most one, and $AQA^{-1}$ projects onto the closed span of $(x_n)$. Thus that span is a [complemented subspace](complemented-subspace.md) isomorphic to the [l-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md).
