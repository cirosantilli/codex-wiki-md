# Genetic association

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_association)

A genetic association is a statistical dependence between a [genetic variant](genetic-variant.md) and a [phenotype](phenotype.md). An associated marker need not be causal: [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) can correlate it with a causal locus, and [population stratification](population-stratification.md) can produce an association without such a link.
