# Field-amplitude beating of coupled Klein-Gordon modes

↑ **Parent:** [Two coupled real Klein-Gordon fields](two-coupled-real-klein-gordon-fields.md)

A single spatial Fourier pattern initially displaced in one field excites two different normal [frequencies](frequency.md). Their sum and difference generate slowly varying amplitude envelopes in the original fields. Total coupled-field [energy](energy-split.md) is conserved, but the separate uncoupled [energies](energy-split.md) need not be, and zeros of one [displacement](displacement.md) envelope do not automatically imply exact complete [energy](energy-split.md) transfer.
