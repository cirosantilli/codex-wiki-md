# Jackson-type estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

A Jackson-type estimate bounds an approximation error by a smoothness quantity such as a [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md), with the smoothness evaluated at a scale comparable to the reciprocal of the approximating degree.
