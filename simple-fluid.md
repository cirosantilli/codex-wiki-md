# Simple fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Newtonian fluid](non-newtonian-fluid.md)

A simple fluid has local [stress](stress.md) determined by the history of deformation of a material element, rather than by spatial derivatives of that history. An isotropic incompressible simple fluid has an arbitrary isotropic [pressure](pressure.md) and an objective constitutive functional of the relative [deformation gradients](deformation-gradient.md). Internal-variable equations can represent such a functional when the initial material state is fixed by the past history.
