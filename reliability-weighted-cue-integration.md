# Reliability-weighted cue integration

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory cue integration](multisensory-cue-integration.md)

A cue-combination rule weighting more precise estimates more strongly. For independent unbiased Gaussian estimates of one quantity and a flat [prior](prior-probability.md), inverse-variance weights are both minimum-variance linear and maximum-likelihood weights. The assumptions exclude arbitrary averaging of complementary qualities such as odor identity and gustatory sweetness.
