# Gentzen ordinal reduction for arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md)

Gentzen's consistency argument associates finite arithmetic derivations with effective [ordinal](ordinal.md) notations below [epsilon zero](epsilon-zero.md). An alleged contradiction admits an effective proof reduction preserving the contradictory end-sequent and strictly decreasing the assigned ordinal. Termination would therefore produce a normal contradiction, excluded by inspection of the logical rules and true atomic axioms. This explains why the relevant [transfinite induction](transfinite-induction.md) principle implies [formal consistency statement](formal-consistency-statement.md). For a finite slow-well-ordering formulation one additionally needs the controlled-termination miniaturization theorem, rather than assuming that ordinary proof reductions have linearly growing size. The reduction program is discussed in [https://math.stanford.edu/~feferman/papers/reductive.pdf](https://math.stanford.edu/~feferman/papers/reductive.pdf) .
