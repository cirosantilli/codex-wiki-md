# Skyscraper sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper_sheaf)

For a point inclusion $i:\{x\}\hookrightarrow X$ and an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$, the skyscraper sheaf $i_*A$ has sections $A$ on opens containing $x$ and zero on other opens. Its restriction maps are identities or maps to zero. If $A\ne0$, its nonzero [stalks](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) occur at every point of the closure $\overline{\{x\}}$: exactly those points whose every neighbourhood contains $x$. Thus the familiar assertion that only the stalk at $x$ is nonzero requires $x$ to be a [closed point](closed-point.md). It is always [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md), giving the stated [skyscraper sheaf cohomology](skyscraper-sheaf-cohomology.md).
