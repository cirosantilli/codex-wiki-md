# Insulating boundary condition for the radial magnetic scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Radial magnetic induction scalar](radial-magnetic-induction-scalar.md)

At a spherical interface with an insulating exterior, equal [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) on both sides and no surface current, all components of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) are continuous. The [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md) then makes $P=\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf x$ and $\partial_rP$ continuous. A current-free exterior has $\Delta P=0$. For an isolated field decaying at infinity, its degree-$l$ [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) satisfies $\partial_rP_{lm}=-(l+1)P_{lm}/a$ at a sphere of radius $a$. This [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) incorporates the exterior field without setting the radial field to zero.
