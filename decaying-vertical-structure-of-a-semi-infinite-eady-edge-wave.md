# Decaying vertical structure of a semi-infinite Eady edge wave

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-infinite Eady edge wave](semi-infinite-eady-edge-wave.md)

A smooth zero-interior-[potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) mode has

$$
\widehat\psi''-m^2\widehat\psi=0,\qquad m=NK/|f_0|,\qquad K=\sqrt{k^2+l^2}.
$$

Decay for $z\to\infty$ gives $\widehat\psi=C e^{-mz}$. Its penetration depth $1/m$ increases with horizontal wavelength and decreases with [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md). Singular neutral interior sheets are a distinct continuous-spectrum class.
