# Invariant-mass resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant-mass selection of bubble-chamber events](invariant-mass-selection-of-bubble-chamber-events.md)

The simulation separates a stipulated physical distribution from measurement broadening. Its [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) is only a convenient resonance-shaped input, not a complete [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md) model. [Spatial resolution](spatial-resolution.md), [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) calibration, [Multiple Coulomb scattering](multiple-coulomb-scattering.md), [stopping power](stopping-power.md) and incorrect daughter identities all propagate into the measured [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md). Instrumental broadening must be understood before interpreting an observed width as a lifetime.

Selection also changes the shape. A [momentum](momentum-split.md) threshold can remove low-[invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) pairs; a fit that fixes the candidate parent's [mass](mass.md) would artificially concentrate that same [mass](mass.md) and cannot supply an independent search distribution. Compare accepted simulated events with the input distribution, vary justified selection criteria, and check neighboring [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) intervals. A peak supported only by one untested selection is weaker evidence than a structure that survives calibration and background checks. The smooth background in the figure is a stipulated control component, not a claim that every physical background is smooth.
