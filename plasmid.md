# Plasmid

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmid)

A [plasmid](plasmid.md) is an extrachromosomal [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [replicon](replicon.md): genetic material maintained by [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) separately from the host's main [chromosomes](chromosome.md). Separate copying does not mean independence of the host's [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) or resources. Many [plasmids](plasmid.md) are circular, but naturally occurring linear [plasmids](plasmid.md) also exist.[Stewart, Chaconas and Rosa (2003), abstract opening sentence: natural linear and circular plasmids](conservation-of-plasmid-maintenance-functions-between-linear-and-circular-plasmids-in-borrelia-burgdorferi-by-philip-e-stewart-and-others.md)

Engineered [plasmids](plasmid.md) can carry [genes](gene.md) for [genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md) and [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md). Typical laboratory [plasmids](plasmid.md) combine an [origin of replication](origin-of-replication.md), a [selectable marker](selectable-marker.md) and a [plasmid insert](plasmid-insert.md). These are engineering roles, not requirements that every natural [plasmid](plasmid.md) carry an experimentally chosen [selectable marker](selectable-marker.md) or [plasmid insert](plasmid-insert.md).[Plasmid Elements, Figure 2 and table: origin, antibiotic-resistance gene, insert and promoter; description applies to engineered vectors](molecular-biology-reference-by-addgene.md)

The useful separation is between keeping a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) fragment, finding its carriers and testing its function. [Molecular cloning](molecular-cloning.md) uses propagation to recover more copies for study. [Plasmid restriction mapping](plasmid-restriction-mapping.md) tests a proposed arrangement, while [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md) examines its letters. A successful [genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md) or a [biological colony](colony-biology.md) showing [antibiotic resistance](antibiotic-resistance.md) answers neither question completely. The [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md) goes a step further by separating [plasmid](plasmid.md) selection from a test of [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md).
