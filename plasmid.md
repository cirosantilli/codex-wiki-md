# Plasmid

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmid)

A [plasmid](plasmid.md) is a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) element capable of replication separately from the main [chromosomes](chromosome.md) of its host. Engineered [plasmids](plasmid.md) can carry [genes](gene.md) for [genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md) and [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md).
