# Topological conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Topological dynamics](topological-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_conjugacy)

Two [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) are topologically conjugate if a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) $H$ between their [state spaces](state-space.md) satisfies the displayed intertwining identity. It is [conjugate maps](conjugate-functions.md) with a topology-preserving conjugating map. On compact [metric spaces](metric-space.md), pulling back a [compatible metric](compatible-metric.md) through $H$ preserves every [Bowen metric](bowen-metric.md), and hence [topological entropy](topological-entropy.md).
