# Cup square after a diagonal sphere attachment

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring of a product of two spheres](cohomology-ring-of-a-product-of-two-spheres.md)

For even $n\ge2$, attach an $(n+1)$-cell to $S^n\times S^n$ along the diagonal. The [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) has boundary $(1,1)$ for the new cell. The degree-$n$ [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) generator restricts to $a-b$, and the top generator restricts to $ab$. Naturality of the [cup product](cup-product.md) therefore gives $u^2=-2v$, since $(a-b)^2=-2ab$. Thus the resulting [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) is $\mathbb Z[u,v]/(u^2+2v,uv,v^2)$, with $|u|=n$ and $|v|=2n$. This distinguishes the attachment from a wedge of spheres, whose positive-degree products vanish.
