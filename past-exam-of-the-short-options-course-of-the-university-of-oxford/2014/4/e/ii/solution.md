<h1 id="4/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) at the identity is the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) from part (i):

$$
\boxed{T_IG=\{H:H^TJ+JH=0\}.}
$$

This says precisely that $JH$ is [skew-symmetric](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Since $J^{-1}=J$, multiplication by $J$ identifies this tangent space with the three-dimensional space of skew-symmetric $3\times3$ [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md). In entries it is

$$
\boxed{H=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&0\\c&0&-b\\0&-c&-a\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b,c\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Substitution verifies the equation directly and displays its three independent parameters.
