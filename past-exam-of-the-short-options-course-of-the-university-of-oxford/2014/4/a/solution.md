<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Fréchet derivative](../../../../frechet-derivative.md) at a point $a$ is the unique [linear map](../../../../linear-map.md) $Df_a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that

$$
\boxed{f(a+h)=f(a)+Df_a(h)+o(\|h\|).}
$$

The error divided by $\|h\|$ tends to zero as $h\to0$. In standard coordinates this [linear map](../../../../linear-map.md) is represented by the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../jacobian-matrix.md), but the derivative itself is the map acting on increments, not merely a list of directional rates.
