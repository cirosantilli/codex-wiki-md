<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Apply the [regular level set theorem](../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) to $F(A)=\det A-1$ on the $n^2$-dimensional [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) of real [matrices](../../../../matrix.md). At a point with determinant one, part (b) gives $DF_A(H)=\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}H)$. Choosing $H=A/n$ gives $DF_A(H)=1$, so this derivative is [surjective](../../../../surjective-function.md).

Hence **the [special linear group](../../../../special-linear-group.md) $\operatorname{SL}(n,\mathbb R)$ is a smooth [submanifold](../../../../submanifold.md) of dimension $n^2-1$**. At the identity,

$$
\boxed{T_I\operatorname{SL}(n,\mathbb R)=\{H:\operatorname{tr}H=0\}.}
$$

This is the [special linear Lie algebra](../../../../special-linear-lie-algebra.md). When $n=1$, the group is a single point and the tangent space is zero, consistent with the same formula.
