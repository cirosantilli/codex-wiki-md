A **[simple group](../../../../simple-group.md)** is a nontrivial [group](../../../../group-split.md) whose only [normal subgroups](../../../../normal-subgroup.md) are itself and the trivial [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md).

A **[composition series](../../../../composition-series.md)** is a chain $G=G_0\triangleright G_1\triangleright\cdots\triangleright G_r=1$ in which each $G_{i+1}$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of $G_i$ and each [quotient group](../../../../quotient-group.md) $G_i/G_{i+1}$ is simple and nontrivial. Its length is $r$, and these quotients are its [composition factors](../../../../composition-factor.md). The individual terms need not all be normal in $G$.

A **[solvable group](../../../../solvable-group.md)** is a [group](../../../../group-split.md) whose [derived series](../../../../derived-series.md) reaches the trivial [group](../../../../group-split.md): starting with $G^{(0)}=G$, define $G^{(i+1)}=[G^{(i)},G^{(i)}]$. For finite [groups](../../../../group-split.md) this is equivalent to having a [composition series](../../../../composition-series.md) whose factors are all [cyclic groups](../../../../cyclic-group.md) of [prime](../../../../prime-number-split.md) order.
