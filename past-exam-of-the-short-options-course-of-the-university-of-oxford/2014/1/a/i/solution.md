<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [odd prime](../../../../../odd-prime.md) $p$, the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=\begin{cases}0,&p\mid a,\\1,&p\nmid a\text{ and }a\text{ is a square modulo }p,\\-1,&a\text{ is a nonsquare modulo }p.\end{cases}}
$$

It depends only on the [congruence class](../../../../../congruence-class.md) of the [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $a$. The values $1$ and $-1$ distinguish a nonzero [quadratic residue](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) from a [quadratic nonresidue](../../../../../quadratic-nonresidue.md); zero is a separate case.
