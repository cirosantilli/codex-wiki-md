A [Hamilton cycle](../../../../../hamilton-cycle.md) is a [cycle](../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) visiting every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of a finite [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) exactly once before returning to its starting point. A [Hamilton path](../../../../../hamiltonian-path.md) is a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) visiting every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) exactly once, with no requirement to return or to have its endpoints adjacent. Both conditions concern visiting vertices, rather than traversing every edge.
