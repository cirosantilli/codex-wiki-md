<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Ramsey number](../../../../../ramsey-number.md) $R(k,\ell)$ is the least positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $N$ such that every red-blue coloring of the edges of $K_N$ has a red $K_k$ or a blue $K_\ell$. Equivalently, every $N$-vertex [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) has a [clique](../../../../../clique-graph-theory.md) of size $k$ or an [independent set](../../../../../independent-set-graph-theory.md) of size $\ell$.

For $N=k$, either there is a blue edge or every edge is red, producing the red $K_k$. Conversely an entirely red $K_{k-1}$ has neither a red $K_k$ nor a blue edge. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R(k,2)=k.}
$$
