<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The sets of admissible [functions](../../../../function-split.md) are nested:

$$
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{clamped}}\subseteq\mathcal A_{\mathrm{pinned}}\subseteq\mathcal A_{\mathrm{free}}.
$$

All impose the same left conditions; the right height adds a restriction, and the right slope adds another. Minimizing the same [functional](../../../../functional.md) over a smaller set cannot lower its minimum. Therefore

$$
\boxed{E_{\mathrm{free}}\leq E_{\mathrm{pinned}}\leq E_{\mathrm{clamped}}.}
$$

**The doubly clamped beam has the greatest minimum energy, and the free-ended beam the smallest.** No energy integrals are needed. In fact the inequalities are strict: the positive quadratic bending term makes the minimizer unique on the left-clamped space, and the three solutions in part (b) are different. The relevant principle is [monotonicity of a constrained minimum under added constraints](../../../../monotonicity-of-a-constrained-minimum-under-added-constraints.md).
