<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take spacetime coordinates $X=(ct,x,y,z)$. Differentiating the initial segment gives the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{U=\frac{dX}{ds}=c(\cosh\omega s,\sinh\omega s,0,0).}
$$

The identity $\cosh^2r-\sinh^2r=1$ gives $g(U,U)=c^2$, and $dt/ds=\cosh\omega s>0$. The [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) element therefore satisfies $d\tau^2=g(dX,dX)/c^2=ds^2$, with the future orientation selecting $d\tau=ds$. Thus **$s$ is proper time** along this path.
