<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $s$ is [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md), the [four-acceleration](../../../../../four-acceleration.md) is

$$
A=\frac{dU}{ds}=c\omega(\sinh\omega s,\cosh\omega s,0,0).
$$

Its [Minkowski norm](../../../../../minkowski-norm.md) squared is $g(A,A)=-c^2\omega^2$, so the invariant magnitude of [proper acceleration](../../../../../proper-acceleration.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{-g(A,A)}=c\omega.}
$$

The vector is spacelike, which accounts for the minus sign under the square root.
