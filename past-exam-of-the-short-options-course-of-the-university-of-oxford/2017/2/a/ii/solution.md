<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) as $|G|=p^am$, where $p$ is a [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) and $p\nmid m$. A [Sylow p-subgroup](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of [group order](../../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) $p^a$, the largest power of $p$ dividing $|G|$.

The three [Sylow theorems](../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) assert:

- **Existence:** a [Sylow p-subgroup](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) exists, and every [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of [prime power](../../../../../prime-power.md) order a power of $p$ is contained in one.
- **Conjugacy:** any two [Sylow p-subgroups](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) are conjugate in $G$.
- **Counting:** their number $n_p$ satisfies $n_p\mid m$ and $n_p\equiv1\pmod p$.

In particular, a unique [Sylow p-subgroup](../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), since all its conjugates must equal it.
