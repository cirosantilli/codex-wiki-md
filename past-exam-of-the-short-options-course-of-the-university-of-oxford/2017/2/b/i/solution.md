<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Sylow theorems](../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) give $n_7\mid10$ and $n_7\equiv1\pmod7$. Among the [divisors](../../../../../divisor.md) $1,2,5,10$, only $1$ satisfies the [modular congruence](../../../../../modular-congruence.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{n_7=1,\qquad K\triangleleft G,\qquad |K|=7.}
$$

Uniqueness and the conjugacy assertion of the [Sylow theorems](../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) make $K$ a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md).
