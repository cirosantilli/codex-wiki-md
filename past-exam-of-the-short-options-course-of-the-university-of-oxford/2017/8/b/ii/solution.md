<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The laboratory flight time is $R/v_B$. By [time dilation](../../../../../time-dilation.md), the accumulated [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) is $\tau=R/(\gamma_Bv_B)$. The definition of the rest-frame [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) gives at least one-half survival when $\tau\leq T$, or $\gamma_Bv_B\geq R/T$. Equivalently,

$$
\frac{v_B^2}{1-v_B^2/c^2}\geq\frac{R^2}{T^2}.
$$

Solving for the minimum speed gives

$$
\boxed{v_{B,\min}=\frac{cR}{\sqrt{R^2+c^2T^2}}.}
$$

Under the usual exponential decay law, the survival probability is exactly $2^{-R/(\gamma_Bv_BT)}$. This is [relativistic decay survival to a detector](../../../../../relativistic-decay-survival-to-a-detector.md).
