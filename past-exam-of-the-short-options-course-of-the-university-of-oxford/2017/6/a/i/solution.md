<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply a constant [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) to the [isoperimetric constraint](../../../../../isoperimetric-constraint.md), using the augmented functional $J-\lambda(I-1)$. Its integrand is

$$
F(x,y,y')=p(x)y'^2-q(x)y^2-\lambda w(x)y^2.
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $-2qy-2\lambda wy-2(py')'=0$, hence

$$
\boxed{(py')'+qy+\lambda wy=0.}
$$

The multiplier rule applies because the constraint derivative in direction $y$ is $DI_y[y]=2I[y]=2$, so the constraint derivative is not zero. The prescribed endpoint values remove boundary variations.
