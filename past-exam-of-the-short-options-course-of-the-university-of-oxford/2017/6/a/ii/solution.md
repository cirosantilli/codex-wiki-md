<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiply the [Sturm-Liouville equation](../../../../../sturm-liouville-equation.md) by $y$ and integrate. The endpoint values and [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
0=\int_{x_0}^{x_1}\bigl[y(py')'+qy^2+\lambda wy^2\bigr]dx
=-\int_{x_0}^{x_1}(py'^2-qy^2)dx+\lambda I[y].
$$

Since $I[y]=1$,

$$
\boxed{J[y]=\lambda.}
$$

Thus the multiplier equals the stationary value of $J$ at the extremal, as in [stationary values of a quadratic functional with a quadratic constraint](../../../../../stationary-values-of-a-quadratic-functional-with-a-quadratic-constraint.md). It is also the corresponding [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md).
