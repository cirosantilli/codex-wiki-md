<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Multiply the equation for $y_n$ by $y_m$ and that for $y_m$ by $y_n$, then subtract. Integrating the resulting identity yields

$$
\bigl[p(y_my_n'-y_ny_m')\bigr]_{x_0}^{x_1}
+(\lambda_n-\lambda_m)\int_{x_0}^{x_1}wy_ny_m\,dx=0.
$$

All endpoint terms vanish because both functions satisfy the zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). Since the multipliers differ,

$$
\boxed{\int_{x_0}^{x_1}w(x)y_n(x)y_m(x)\,dx=0.}
$$

This is the weighted [orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions](../../../../../orthogonality-of-sturm-liouville-eigenfunctions.md).
