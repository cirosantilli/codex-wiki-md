<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The sine functions obey $\int_0^\pi\sin(nx)\sin(mx)dx=(\pi/2)\delta_{nm}$. The [Parseval identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md) for the [Fourier sine series](../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) gives

$$
1=\int_0^\pi y(x)^2\,dx=\frac\pi2\sum_{n\geq1}a_n^2.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n\geq1}a_n^2=\frac2\pi.}
$$
