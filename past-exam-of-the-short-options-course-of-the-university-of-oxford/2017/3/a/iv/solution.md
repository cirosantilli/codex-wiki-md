↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [dual projective space](../../../../../dual-projective-space.md) is $\mathbb P((\mathbb R^3)^*)$. A [projective line](../../../../../projective-line.md) with coefficients $[\alpha:\beta:\gamma]$ becomes a [projective point](../../../../../projective-point.md) in this dual plane; a [projective point](../../../../../projective-point.md) $[a:b:c]$ becomes the dual [projective line](../../../../../projective-line.md) $a\alpha+b\beta+c\gamma=0$.

The [point-line duality](../../../../../point-line-duality.md) reverses incidence:

$$
\boxed{P\in l\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad l^*\in P^*.}
$$

Thus **the dual statement is that the dual point $l^*$ lies on the dual line $P^*$**. A join of [projective points](../../../../../projective-point.md) becomes an intersection of dual [projective lines](../../../../../projective-line.md), and the converse holds too. In particular, [collinear](../../../../../collinearity.md) [projective points](../../../../../projective-point.md) correspond to concurrent dual [projective lines](../../../../../projective-line.md).

The dual of [Desargues's theorem](../../../../../desargues-s-theorem.md) is its converse: **if the intersections of the three pairs of corresponding sides are [collinear](../../../../../collinearity.md), then the three lines joining corresponding vertices are concurrent**. In the same nondegenerate setting, [perspectivity from a line](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-line.md) therefore implies [perspectivity from a point](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-point.md).
