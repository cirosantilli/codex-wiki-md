<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Two [projective triangles](../../../../../projective-triangle.md) are in [perspectivity from a point](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-point.md) $O$ when their three corresponding-vertex [projective lines](../../../../../projective-line.md) $PP'$, $QQ'$, and $RR'$ all pass through $O$.

**[Desargues's theorem](../../../../../desargues-s-theorem.md): [perspectivity from a point](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-point.md) implies [perspectivity from a line](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-line.md).** More explicitly, if $PP',QQ',RR'$ are concurrent, then the three corresponding-side intersections

$$
X=PQ\cap P'Q',\qquad Y=QR\cap Q'R',\qquad Z=RP\cap R'P'
$$

are [collinear](../../../../../collinearity.md). The usual statement assumes nondegenerate [projective triangles](../../../../../projective-triangle.md) and well-defined, distinct corresponding-side intersections. The [projective line](../../../../../projective-line.md) containing $X,Y,Z$ is the axis of [perspectivity from a line](../../../../../perspectivity-from-a-line.md). All intersections are projective: parallel lines in an [affine chart](../../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) meet on its [projective line at infinity](../../../../../projective-line-at-infinity.md).
