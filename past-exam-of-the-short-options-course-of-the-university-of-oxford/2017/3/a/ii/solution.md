<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For representatives $p=(a,b,c)$ and $p'=(a',b',c')$, a point $[x:y:z]$ belongs to their [projective line](../../../../../projective-line.md) exactly when these three vectors are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md). The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) criterion gives

$$
\begin{vmatrix}x&y&z\\a&b&c\\a'&b'&c'\end{vmatrix}=0.
$$

Expanding along the first row gives

$$
\boxed{(bc'-cb')x+(ca'-ac')y+(ab'-ba')z=0.}
$$

The coefficient vector is the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) $p\times p'$. It is nonzero because the [projective points](../../../../../projective-point.md) are distinct. Rescaling either representative merely rescales all three coefficients, leaving the [projective line](../../../../../projective-line.md) unchanged. This is the join formula in [projective joins and meets by cross products](../../../../../projective-joins-and-meets-by-cross-products.md).
