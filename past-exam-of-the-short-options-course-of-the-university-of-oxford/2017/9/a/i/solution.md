<h1 id="9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [susceptible compartment](../../../../../susceptible-compartment.md) retains $S_t$ individuals, gains $B$ recruits per generation and loses $\alpha S_tI_t$ through [infection](../../../../../infection.md). The [mass-action interaction](../../../../../mass-action-interaction.md) term represents new infections generated by encounters between susceptible and infectious individuals. Those new cases form the next [infectious compartment](../../../../../infectious-compartment.md), $I_{t+1}$; the current infectious generation leaves that compartment after one time step.

The initial conditions $S_0,I_0$ specify the starting susceptible and infectious counts. The parameter $\alpha$ is the transmission coefficient in this deterministic approximation, while $B$ supplies new susceptible individuals. This is the [discrete susceptible-infective model with recruitment](../../../../../discrete-susceptible-infective-model-with-recruitment.md).
