<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the increment of the [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md), using $A^T=A$:

$$
q(v+h)-q(v)=v^TAh+h^TAv+h^TAh=2v^TAh+h^TAh.
$$

The last term obeys $|h^TAh|\leq\|A\|\|h\|^2=o(\|h\|)$. Thus $q$ is differentiable and

$$
\boxed{Dq_v(h)=2v^TAh,\qquad\nabla q(v)=2Av.}
$$

This is the [quadratic form gradient](../../../../../quadratic-form-gradient.md). The derivative is a linear functional of the increment $h$, whereas the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is its representing vector under the Euclidean inner product.
