<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The map is [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $x$ if there is a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that

$$
\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\|f(x+h)-f(x)-Lh\|}{\|h\|}=0.
$$

Equivalently, $f(x+h)=f(x)+Lh+o(\|h\|)$. This linear map is unique and is the [Fréchet derivative](../../../../../frechet-derivative.md) $Df_x$. Its matrix in the standard bases is the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md), whose $(i,j)$ entry is $\partial f_i/\partial x_j$ at $x$. A function is differentiable on its domain if this condition holds at every point.
