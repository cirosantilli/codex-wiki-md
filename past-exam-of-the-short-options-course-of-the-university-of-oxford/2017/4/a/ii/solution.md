<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A sufficient condition is that the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $Df_x:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), equivalently that its [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) has rank $m$. Then the [implicit function theorem](../../../../../implicit-function-theorem.md) makes the zero set near $x$ a $C^1$ [embedded submanifold](../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) of dimension

$$
\boxed{n-m.}
$$

The rank condition persists nearby because the derivative is continuous. This is the local form of the [regular level set theorem](../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md); only the rank at the specified point is needed for this local conclusion.
