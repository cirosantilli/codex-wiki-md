A **[tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md)** is a connected [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) with no [cycles](../../../../../permutation-cycle.md). Equivalently, between any two of its vertices there is exactly one [graph path](../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md). A single vertex with no edges is also a tree.
