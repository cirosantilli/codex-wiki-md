<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $|G|=pq$ with $p<q$. The [Sylow theorems](../../../../sylow-theorems.md) give $n_q\mid p$ and $n_q\equiv1\pmod q$. Since $p<q$, necessarily $n_q=1$. Its unique [Sylow subgroup](../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) $N$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../normal-subgroup.md) and has prime order $q$, so

$$
\boxed{N\cong C_q\trianglelefteq G.}
$$

A [Sylow subgroup](../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) $H\cong C_p$ has trivial intersection with $N$, and $|NH|=|N||H|=pq$, so $NH=G$. Thus

$$
\boxed{G\cong C_q\rtimes C_p.}
$$

The [semidirect product](../../../../semidirect-product.md) action is a [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $C_p\to\operatorname{Aut}(C_q)\cong C_{q-1}$. If the action is trivial, $G\cong C_{pq}$ by the [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md); if it is nontrivial, $p\mid q-1$. In particular $G$ has both a cyclic normal subgroup and a cyclic quotient, making it a [metacyclic group](../../../../metacyclic-group.md) and hence a [solvable group](../../../../solvable-group.md).
