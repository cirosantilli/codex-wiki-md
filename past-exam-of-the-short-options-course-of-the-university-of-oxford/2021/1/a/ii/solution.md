<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Factor $Q=\prod_i p_i$ and $Q'=\prod_j q_j$, retaining repeated [prime factors](../../../../../prime-factor.md). If $P$ has a common [prime factor](../../../../../prime-factor.md) with $QQ'$, it has one with at least one of $Q,Q'$, and both sides of the desired equality are zero. Otherwise the definition of the [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac P{QQ'}\right)=\prod_i\left(\frac P{p_i}\right)\prod_j\left(\frac P{q_j}\right)=\left(\frac P Q\right)\left(\frac P{Q'}\right).}
$$

An empty product is $1$, so the argument includes either denominator being $1$.
