**No.** Take $P=2$, $Q=9$. The [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) is

$$
\left(\frac29\right)=\left(\frac23\right)^2=(-1)^2=1,
$$

but the squares modulo $3$ are $0,1$. A square root of $2$ modulo $9$ would reduce to one modulo $3$, which is impossible. A [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) of $1$ records a product of local signs; it does not assert that every local sign is positive, or that a [quadratic congruence](../../../../../quadratic-congruence.md) is soluble.
