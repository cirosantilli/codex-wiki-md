<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$, [multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol](../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-legendre-symbol.md) says $(PP'/p)=(P/p)(P'/p)$, including the case of a zero factor. Therefore, writing $Q=\prod_i p_i$ with repetitions,

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{PP'}Q\right)=\prod_i\left(\frac{PP'}{p_i}\right)=\prod_i\left(\frac P{p_i}\right)\prod_i\left(\frac{P'}{p_i}\right)=\left(\frac P Q\right)\left(\frac{P'}Q\right).}
$$

This product definition still agrees with the stipulated zero when the numerator and denominator are not [coprime](../../../../../coprime-integers.md). For $Q=1$ all three symbols are $1$.
