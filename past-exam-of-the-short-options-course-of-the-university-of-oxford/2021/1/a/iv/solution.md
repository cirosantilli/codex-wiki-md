<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $P\equiv P'\pmod Q$, then $P\equiv P'\pmod p$ for each [prime factor](../../../../../prime-factor.md) $p$ of $Q$. The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) depends only on the numerator's [residue class](../../../../../residue-class.md), so the corresponding factors agree. Also $\gcd(P,Q)=\gcd(P',Q)$, covering the zero cases. Multiplying gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac P Q\right)=\left(\frac{P'}Q\right).}
$$

The convention for denominator $1$ gives the same conclusion there.
