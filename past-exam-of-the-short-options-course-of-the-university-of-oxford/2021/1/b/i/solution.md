<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The supplementary law for the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is $(-1/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}$ for odd [prime numbers](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$. For odd integers $a,b$,

$$
\frac{ab-1}{2}=\frac{a-1}{2}+\frac{b-1}{2}+\frac{(a-1)(b-1)}2,
$$

and the last term is even. [Induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) over the [prime factors](../../../../../prime-factor.md) of $Q$ therefore gives $\sum_i(p_i-1)/2\equiv(Q-1)/2\pmod2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{-1}Q\right)=\prod_i(-1)^{(p_i-1)/2}=(-1)^{(Q-1)/2}.}
$$

For $Q=1$ the exponent is zero.
