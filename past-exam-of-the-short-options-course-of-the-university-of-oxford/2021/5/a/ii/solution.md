<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiplication of a [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) by a [smooth function](../../../../../smooth-function.md) is defined by moving that function onto the [test function](../../../../../test-function.md). The [Dirac delta multiplication identity](../../../../../dirac-delta-multiplication-identity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\langle x^2\delta,\phi\rangle=\langle\delta,x^2\phi\rangle=(x^2\phi(x))\big|_{x=0}=0.}
$$

In fact $x^2\delta$ is the zero [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), independently of the special positivity assumptions on $\phi$.
