<h1 id="5/a/i/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Exactly $a\ne0$ works.** For nonzero $a$, composition with $x\mapsto ax$ preserves smoothness, and the [support of a function](../../../../../../support.md) is $[-1/|a|,1/|a|]$, which is compact. For $a=0$ the result is the strictly positive constant $\phi(0)$ on all of $\mathbb R$, so it lacks [compact support](../../../../../../compact-support.md) and is not a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md).
