<h1 id="5/a/i/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Exactly $|a|>1$ works.** Composition with the [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md) is smooth for every $a$. If $|a|>1$, set $R=\operatorname{arctanh}(1/|a|)$. For $|x|\ge R$, $|a\tanh x|\ge1$, so the result vanishes; it is therefore a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) with [support of a function](../../../../../../support.md) $[-R,R]$.

If $|a|\le1$, every finite $x$ satisfies $|a\tanh x|<1$. The given positivity makes $\phi(|a|\tanh x)>0$ for every $x$, so its support is all of $\mathbb R$. In particular $|a|=1$ is excluded even though the function tends to zero at infinity: decay is not [compact support](../../../../../../compact-support.md).
