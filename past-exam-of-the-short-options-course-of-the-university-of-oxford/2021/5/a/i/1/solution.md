↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Every $a\in\mathbb R$ works.** Translation preserves a [smooth function](../../../../../../smooth-function.md), and the [support of a function](../../../../../../support.md) becomes the compact interval $[a-1,a+1]$. Hence $\phi(\,\cdot-a)\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$, the [space of test functions](../../../../../../space-of-test-functions.md).
