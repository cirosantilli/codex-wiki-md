↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [projective transformation](../../../../../projective-linear-transformation.md) of $\mathbb P(\mathbb F^{n+1})$ is induced by an invertible [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $A:\mathbb F^{n+1}\to\mathbb F^{n+1}$:

$$
\boxed{\tau([v])=[Av],\qquad A\in\operatorname{GL}_{n+1}(\mathbb F).}
$$

This is well defined because replacing $v$ by a nonzero scalar multiple does not change $[Av]$. Multiplying $A$ by a nonzero scalar also leaves $\tau$ unchanged. Conversely these are precisely the transformations in the [projective linear group](../../../../../projective-linear-group.md) $\operatorname{PGL}_{n+1}(\mathbb F)$. No field automorphism is included in this definition.
