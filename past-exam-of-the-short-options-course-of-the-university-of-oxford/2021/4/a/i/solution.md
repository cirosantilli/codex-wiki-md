<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $\Omega=\mathbb R^2$ and

$$
\boxed{F(s,t)=(e^s\cos t,e^s\sin t).}
$$

Its [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) and [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) are

$$
DF_{(s,t)}=e^s\begin{pmatrix}\cos t&-\sin t\\\sin t&\cos t\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det DF_{(s,t)}=e^{2s}>0.
$$

Thus its [total derivative](../../../../../total-derivative.md) is invertible everywhere, and the [inverse function theorem](../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) makes it locally invertible. But $F(s,t+2\pi)=F(s,t)$, so it is not [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) and has no global inverse on its image. The domain is open and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) as required.
