<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $a=\partial_1f_1=\partial_2f_2$ and $b=\partial_2f_1=-\partial_1f_2$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give

$$
DF_x=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\-b&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det DF_x=a^2+b^2.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{DF_x\text{ is invertible exactly when }(\partial_1f_1(x),\partial_2f_1(x))\ne(0,0).}
$$

Equivalently, it is singular exactly when all four [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) vanish. The sum of squares is over $\mathbb R$, so no cancellation is possible.
