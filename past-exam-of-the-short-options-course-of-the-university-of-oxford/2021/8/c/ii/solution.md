<h1 id="8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [photon](../../../../../photon.md), $m=0$ and $u=c$, so the result from (i) reduces to

$$
\boxed{V=c\cos\phi,\qquad \boldsymbol v_O=-c\cos\phi\,\mathbf e_x.}
$$

This is [relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md) for a photon initially transverse to the boost. It also follows directly from $p'_x=\gamma VE/c^2$ and $E'=\gamma E$: the observed direction cosine is $cp'_x/E'=V/c$. The transverse observed speed is $c/\gamma=c\sin\phi$, and the total photon speed is still $c$. If desired the observed photon energy is $E'=E/\sin\phi$ for $0<\phi\le\pi/2$.
