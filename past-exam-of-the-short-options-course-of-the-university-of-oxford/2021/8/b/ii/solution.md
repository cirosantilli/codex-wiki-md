<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By definition $v/c=\tanh\theta$ and $v'/c=\tanh\theta'$. Using the addition identity for the [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md) and the [rapidity](../../../../../rapidity.md) sum from (i),

$$
\frac{v''}c=\tanh(\theta+\theta')=\frac{v/c+v'/c}{1+vv'/c^2}.
$$

Thus the [velocity-addition formula](../../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{v''=\frac{v+v'}{1+vv'/c^2}=\frac{c^2(v+v')}{c^2+vv'}.}
$$

The denominator is positive for the permitted subluminal signed velocities. For the nonnegative speeds in (iii), the composite is also nonnegative and remains below $c$.
