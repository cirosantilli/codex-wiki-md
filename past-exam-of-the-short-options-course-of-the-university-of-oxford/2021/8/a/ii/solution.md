<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the fixed parameter interval the integrand tends to $\cos\theta$ as $\kappa\to0$ and is bounded by $\cos\theta$. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md), or [uniform convergence](../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) here, gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{\kappa\to0}\tau_A=2\sqrt2T\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos\theta\,d\theta=2T.}
$$

This equals Sam's time between departure and reunion. Physically, $dx/dt=-c\kappa\tan\theta$ tends uniformly to zero, so Alice's [worldline](../../../../../world-line.md) approaches Sam's, and the [time dilation](../../../../../time-dilation.md) disappears.
