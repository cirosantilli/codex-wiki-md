<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The signed [rapidity](../../../../../rapidity.md) corresponding to [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $u$ is

$$
\boxed{\phi=\operatorname{artanh}(u/c)=\frac12\log\frac{1+u/c}{1-u/c}.}
$$

Equivalently $u/c=\tanh\phi$, $\gamma=\cosh\phi$, and $\gamma u/c=\sinh\phi$. Thus the [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) from part (i) becomes

$$
B(\phi)=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh\phi&\sinh\phi\\\sinh\phi&\cosh\phi\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Multiplying two such [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) gives diagonal entries $\cosh\phi\cosh\psi+\sinh\phi\sinh\psi=\cosh(\phi+\psi)$ and off-diagonal entries $\cosh\phi\sinh\psi+\sinh\phi\cosh\psi=\sinh(\phi+\psi)$, by the addition identities for [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{B(\phi)B(\psi)=B(\phi+\psi).}
$$

The resulting [rapidity](../../../../../rapidity.md) is $\phi+\psi$. In [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) variables this is the [relativistic velocity-addition formula](../../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) $u\oplus v=(u+v)/(1+uv/c^2)$.
