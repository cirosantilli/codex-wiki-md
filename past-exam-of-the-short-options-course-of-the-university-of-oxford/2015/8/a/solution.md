<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use coordinates $X=(ct,x,y,z)^T$ and [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$. A real $4\times4$ [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) $L$ is a [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) precisely when it preserves the [Minkowski interval](../../../../minkowski-interval.md) for every $X$, equivalently

$$
\boxed{L^T\eta L=\eta.}
$$

It is proper when $\boxed{\det L=+1}$, and orthochronous when $\boxed{L^0{}_0>0}$, equivalently $L^0{}_0\ge1$. The latter selects the [orthochronous Lorentz group](../../../../orthochronous-lorentz-group.md), which preserves future-directed timelike [four-vectors](../../../../four-vector.md). Indeed the time-time entry of the preservation identity gives $(L^0{}_0)^2-\sum_{i=1}^3(L^i{}_0)^2=1$, so $|L^0{}_0|\ge1$. Imposing both conditions gives a [proper orthochronous Lorentz transformation](../../../../proper-orthochronous-lorentz-transformation.md).
