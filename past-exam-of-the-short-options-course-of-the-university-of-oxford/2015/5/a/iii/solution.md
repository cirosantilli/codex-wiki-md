<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The defining evaluation rule of the [Dirac delta](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L[\delta(x-a)](p)=e^{-ap},\qquad a>0.}
$$

The impulse lies strictly inside the integration domain, so there is no endpoint convention to settle. This [Laplace transform](../../../../../laplace-transform.md) exists for every complex $p$.
