<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the unilateral [Laplace transform](../../../../../laplace-transform.md) convention $\overline f(p)=\int_0^\infty e^{-px}f(x)\,dx$. The [Laplace transform of a derivative](../../../../../laplace-transform-of-a-derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L[f'](p)=p\overline f(p)-f(0).}
$$

The formula holds in the common [Laplace convergence region](../../../../../region-of-absolute-convergence-of-a-laplace-transform.md) under the usual endpoint and growth hypotheses. The initial value is the right-hand value at zero.
