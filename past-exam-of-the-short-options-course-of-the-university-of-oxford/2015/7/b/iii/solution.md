<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For every [matching in a graph](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) $M'$ and every [vertex cover](../../../../../vertex-cover.md) $C'$, the disjoint [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) of $M'$ need distinct covering [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). Therefore

$$
|M'|\le|C'|,\qquad\nu(G)\le\tau(G).
$$

Now assume the assertion in part (ii). It supplies a [matching in a graph](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) $M$ and a [vertex cover](../../../../../vertex-cover.md) $C$ formed by choosing one endpoint of each [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) of $M$. Because these [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) are disjoint, the chosen endpoints are distinct, so $|C|=|M|$. By the definitions of maximum and minimum,

$$
\tau(G)\le|C|=|M|\le\nu(G)\le\tau(G).
$$

Every inequality must be equality, proving [Kőnig's theorem for bipartite matching](../../../../../konig-s-theorem-graph-theory.md):

$$
\boxed{\nu(G)=\tau(G).}
$$
