<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For distinct odd [prime numbers](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p,q$, the law of [quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac pq\right)\left(\frac qp\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)/4}.}
$$

Thus the two [Legendre symbols](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) agree unless both [prime numbers](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) are congruent to $3$ modulo $4$, in which case they have opposite signs.
