<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) along a sufficiently smooth [extremal](../../../../extremal.md) of the [functional](../../../../functional.md). For $E=y'F_{y'}-F$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dE}{dx}
&=y''F_{y'}+y'\frac d{dx}F_{y'}
-F_x-F_yy'-F_{y'}y''\\
&=y'\left(\frac d{dx}F_{y'}-F_y\right)-F_x.
\end{aligned}
$$

The bracket vanishes by the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). When $F$ has no explicit $x$ dependence, $F_x=0$, giving the [Beltrami identity](../../../../beltrami-identity.md):

$$
\boxed{y'F_{y'}-F=\text{constant along the extremal}.}
$$
