<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $p\mid n$, the [field](../../../../../field.md) property forces $x=0$, giving exactly one solution. Otherwise, if $x_0$ is any solution, multiplication by $x_0$ is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) from the solutions of $z^k=1$ to those of $x^k=n$. Part (i) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{cases}1,&p\mid n,\\0\text{ or }\gcd(k,p-1),&p\nmid n.\end{cases}}
$$

The two possibilities in the second line distinguish an empty fiber from a [coset](../../../../../coset.md) of the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the power [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) on $\mathbb F_p^\times$.
