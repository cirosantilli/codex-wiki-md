<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $p>3$, [quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac3p\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\left(\frac p3\right).
$$

The second [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is $1$ when $p\equiv1\pmod3$ and $-1$ when $p\equiv2\pmod3$. Combining it with the sign determined by $p\pmod4$ shows that a [quadratic residue](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) occurs precisely for

$$
\boxed{p\equiv1\text{ or }11\pmod{12}}.
$$
