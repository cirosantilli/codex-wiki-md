<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The speed of one rocket in the other's rest frame is obtained by [relativistic velocity addition](../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md):

$$
u=\frac{c/2-(-c/2)}{1-(c/2)(-c/2)/c^2}=\frac{4c}{5}.
$$

The [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma=(1-u^2/c^2)^{-1/2}=5/3$. By [length contraction](../../../../length-contraction.md), the spatial length measured at simultaneous times in that rocket's rest frame is

$$
\boxed{L=L_0/\gamma=3L_0/5}.
$$

This is the inertial-frame length measurement; a visual image assembled from light arriving at one instant is a different measurement.
