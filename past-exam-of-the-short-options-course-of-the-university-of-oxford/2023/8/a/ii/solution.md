<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For increasing parameter $s$ along a future-directed [timelike curve](../../../../../timelike-curve.md), the elapsed [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\tau=\frac1c\int_{s_1}^{s_2}\sqrt{\eta_{ab}\frac{dx^a}{ds}\frac{dx^b}{ds}}\,ds}
$$

or $\int\sqrt{1-|\boldsymbol v|^2/c^2}\,dt$ when coordinate time is the parameter. A [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) satisfies $L^T\eta L=\eta$, so replacing $\dot x$ by $L\dot{\tilde x}$ leaves the expression under the square root unchanged. Hence the integral is invariant. A monotone change of path parameter also leaves it unchanged by the ordinary substitution rule.
