<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ and coordinates $x^0=ct$. At a point, a smooth path is timelike if its tangent $\dot x$ is a [timelike vector](../../../../../timelike-vector.md), here meaning $\eta_{ab}\dot x^a\dot x^b>0$. It is lightlike if its tangent is a [null vector](../../../../../null-vector.md): the same expression is zero and the tangent is nonzero. In these coordinates, a future-directed timelike tangent has spatial speed less than $c$; a lightlike tangent has speed $c$.
