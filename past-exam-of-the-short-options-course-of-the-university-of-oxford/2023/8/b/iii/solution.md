<h1 id="8/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

When $0\leq v,v'\ll c$, expand the denominator:

$$
v''=v+v'-\frac{vv'(v+v')}{c^2}+O\!\left(\frac{v^2v'^2(v+v')}{c^4}\right).
$$

The leading term is ordinary [Galilean velocity addition](../../../../../galilean-velocity-addition.md). When both $v,v'\to c$ from below, the exact formula instead gives $\boxed{v''\to c}$, rather than $2c$. Indeed, writing $a=v/c$, $b=v'/c$, we have $1-v''/c=(1-a)(1-b)/(1+ab)>0$ whenever $a,b<1$.
