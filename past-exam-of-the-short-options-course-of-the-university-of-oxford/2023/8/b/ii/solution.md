<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using $v/c=\tanh\theta$ and the [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md) addition identity,

$$
\frac{v''}{c}=\tanh(\theta+\theta')=\frac{v/c+v'/c}{1+vv'/c^2}.
$$

Consequently the [relativistic velocity addition](../../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) rule is

$$
\boxed{v''=\frac{v+v'}{1+vv'/c^2}=\frac{c^2(v+v')}{c^2+vv'}}.
$$
