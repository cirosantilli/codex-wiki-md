<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [distributional derivative](../../../../distributional-derivative.md) is defined by $\langle F',\phi\rangle=-\langle F,\phi'\rangle$ for every [test function](../../../../test-function.md). For the [Heaviside function](../../../../heaviside-step-function.md), compact support gives

$$
\langle\mathcal H',\phi\rangle=-\int_0^\infty\phi'(x)\,dx=\phi(0)=\langle\delta,\phi\rangle.
$$

Thus $\mathcal H'=\delta$. Splitting the integral for $|x|$ at zero and using [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives its first derivative as the [sign function](../../../../sign-function.md). The jump of that function at zero is two, so

$$
\boxed{D|x|=\operatorname{sgn}(x),\qquad D^2|x|=2\delta}
$$

as [distributions](../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). The value assigned to the [sign function](../../../../sign-function.md) at the single point zero does not affect its associated [distribution](../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
