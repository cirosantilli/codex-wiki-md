↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the space of [tempered distributions](../../../../../tempered-distribution.md), this [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention gives $\widehat1=2\pi\delta$ and $\widehat\delta=1$. Therefore

$$
\hat g=c+2\pi\delta,\qquad\lambda g=\lambda+\lambda c\delta.
$$

The constant [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) and the [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) are linearly independent: testing away from zero first determines the constant coefficient, and then testing at zero determines the delta coefficient. Hence $c=\lambda$ and $\lambda c=2\pi$, giving

$$
\boxed{(c,\lambda)=(\sqrt{2\pi},\sqrt{2\pi})\quad\text{or}\quad(-\sqrt{2\pi},-\sqrt{2\pi})}.
$$
