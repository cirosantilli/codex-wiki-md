<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assume $L\ne0$. The quadratics divisible by $L$ form $L\cdot\operatorname{span}\{x,y\}$, a two-dimensional [linear subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md); their projectivization is a [projective line](../../../../projective-line.md). Evaluating at the projective zero $[-\beta:\alpha]$ of $L$ gives its equation

$$
\boxed{\beta^2a-\alpha\beta b+\alpha^2c=0}.
$$

At the point $[L^2]=[\alpha^2:2\alpha\beta:\beta^2]$ of $C$, the tangent equation obtained from the [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) of $b^2-4ac$ is $-4\beta^2a+4\alpha\beta b-4\alpha^2c=0$. It is exactly the same line, establishing tangency.
