<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use coefficient coordinates $[a:b:c]$ on $\mathbb P(V)$. A nonzero binary quadratic has a repeated linear factor precisely when its [discriminant](../../../../discriminant.md) vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{C=\{[a:b:c]:b^2-4ac=0\}}.
$$

Its [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is $(-4c,2b,-4a)$, which cannot vanish at a projective point. Therefore $C$ is a nonsingular [projective conic](../../../../projective-conic.md). The parametrization $[\alpha:\beta]\mapsto[\alpha^2:2\alpha\beta:\beta^2]$ identifies it as a degree-two [Veronese map](../../../../veronese-map.md).
