<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Abbreviate $A=\alpha_{12}$, $B=\alpha_{21}$. The coordinate factors immediately give the three boundary [equilibria](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $S_1,S_2,S_3$. The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is

$$
J(u)=\begin{pmatrix}1-2u_1-Au_2&-Au_1\\-\rho Bu_2&\rho(\gamma-2u_2-Bu_1)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At $S_1$, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1,\rho\gamma>0$, so it is a repelling [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). At the other two points, triangularity gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}S_2:&\quad-1,\ \rho(\gamma-B),\\S_3:&\quad1-A\gamma,\ -\rho\gamma.\end{aligned}}
$$

Therefore $S_2$ is asymptotically stable for $\gamma<B$ and a [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) for $\gamma>B$; $S_3$ is asymptotically stable for $\gamma>1/A$ and a [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) for $\gamma<1/A$. Equality gives a zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), so linearization alone is inconclusive there.

For later use, the coexistence point is physically positive exactly when $B<\gamma<1/A$ if $AB<1$, or $1/A<\gamma<B$ if $AB>1$. Its stability is as supplied in the question. If $AB=1$ the displayed formula for $S_4$ does not apply: when also $\gamma=B=1/A$, the coincident non-axis [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md) give a whole segment of [equilibria](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
