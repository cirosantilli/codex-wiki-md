↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [shortest-path tree](../../../../../shortest-path-tree.md) rooted at $x$ is a [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) $T$ such that, for every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $v$, the unique $xv$ [graph path](../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) in $T$ has minimum total [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) length among all $xv$ [graph paths](../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) in $G$. The objective is the sum of the lengths along each route, not the sum of all the tree's [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) lengths.
