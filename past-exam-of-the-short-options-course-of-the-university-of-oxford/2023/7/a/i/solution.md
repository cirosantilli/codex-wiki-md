A [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) of the connected [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) $G$ is a subgraph containing every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md), connected and without [cycles in a graph](../../../../../cycle-in-a-graph.md). Equivalently, between every pair of [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) it has exactly one [graph path](../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md). For a finite [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) with $v$ vertices, it has $v-1$ edges.
