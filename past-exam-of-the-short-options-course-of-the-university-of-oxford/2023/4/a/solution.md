<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [operator norm](../../../../operator-norm.md), [submultiplicativity](../../../../submultiplicativity.md) bounds the remainder of the [matrix exponential](../../../../matrix-exponential.md) by

$$
\|\exp A-I-A\|\leq\sum_{j=2}^\infty\frac{\|A\|^j}{j!}=e^{\|A\|}-1-\|A\|=O(\|A\|^2).
$$

Therefore $\|\exp A-\exp0-A\|/\|A\|\to0$. This is precisely [Fréchet differentiability](../../../../frechet-differentiability.md) at zero, with

$$
\boxed{D\exp(0)[H]=H}.
$$
