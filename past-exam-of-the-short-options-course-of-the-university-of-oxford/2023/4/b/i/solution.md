<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The curve-based [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) at $x$ consists of velocities $\gamma'(0)$ of smooth curves $\gamma:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to M$ satisfying $\gamma(0)=x$. For an arbitrary subset these velocities need not form a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md); for a smooth [submanifold](../../../../../submanifold.md) they do, and this is its usual [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md). This definition suffices for the direct computation at the identity.
