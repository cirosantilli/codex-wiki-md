<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $Q(t)$ is a smooth curve of [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) with $Q(0)=I$, differentiating $Q(t)^TQ(t)=I$ gives $Q'(0)^T+Q'(0)=0$. Thus each tangent velocity is a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md).

Conversely, if $A^T=-A$, the curve $Q(t)=\exp(tA)$ satisfies

$$
Q(t)^TQ(t)=\exp(-tA)\exp(tA)=I,
$$

using commutation of $-tA$ and $tA$. By part (a), $Q'(0)=A$. Both inclusions prove

$$
\boxed{T_IO(n)=\{A:A^T=-A\}=\mathcal A}.
$$
