↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Part (b) already shows $\exp(U\cap\mathcal A)\subset V\cap O(n)$. Conversely, if $Q\in V\cap O(n)$ and $A=L(Q)\in U$, then

$$
\exp(A^T)=Q^T=Q^{-1}=\exp(-A).
$$

Both $A^T$ and $-A$ lie in $U$, where the [matrix exponential](../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) is injective. Therefore $A^T=-A$, proving the reverse inclusion. The restricted map is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md), indeed a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) between these local sets.
