↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [group presentation](../../../../group-presentation.md) $\langle X\mid R\rangle$ denotes the quotient $F(X)/\langle\!\langle R\rangle\!\rangle$ of the [free group](../../../../free-group.md) on the generator symbols by the [normal closure](../../../../normal-closure.md) of the relator words. The symbols generate the quotient, and each relator becomes the identity. The phrase also asserts that no additional relations are imposed except consequences of these relators and the [group axioms](../../../../group-axioms.md). Equivalently, an assignment of the generators to a [group](../../../../group-split.md) extends to a [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from the presented [group](../../../../group-split.md) exactly when all relators evaluate to the identity; this is the [universal property of a group presentation](../../../../universal-property-of-a-group-presentation.md).
