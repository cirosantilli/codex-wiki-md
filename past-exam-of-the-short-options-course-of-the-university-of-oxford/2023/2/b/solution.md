<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplication, followed by [induction](../../../../mathematical-induction.md) and inversion for negative powers, gives

$$
\boxed{T^n=\begin{pmatrix}1&n\\0&1\end{pmatrix}},\qquad
T^nX=\begin{pmatrix}a+nc&b+nd\\c&d\end{pmatrix}.
$$

If $c\ne0$, apply [Euclidean division](../../../../euclidean-division.md) to write $a=q|c|+r$ with $0\leq r<|c|$. Choose $n=-q\operatorname{sgn}(c)$, so $a+nc=r$. This is one step of the [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) implemented by a determinant-one row operation.
