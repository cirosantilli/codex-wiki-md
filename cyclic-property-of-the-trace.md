# Cyclic property of the trace

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

For square matrices whose product is defined, $\operatorname{tr}(A_1A_2\cdots A_n)$ is unchanged by a cyclic permutation of the factors. In particular, every [commutator](commutator.md) has trace zero.
