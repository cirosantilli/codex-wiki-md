# Fourier integral of a rational function with simple nonreal poles

↑ **Parent:** [Residue theorem](residue-theorem.md)

For monic $q$ with distinct nonreal zeros and $\deg p\leq\deg q-1$, close the contour in the upper half-plane. The rational factor is $O(R^{-1})$ on the large semicircle, whose exponential damping makes the arc integral $O(R^{-1})$. The two one-sided real integrals also converge by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), since the rational factor tends to zero and its derivative is $O(x^{-2})$. Thus the displayed formula is an ordinary improper integral, not merely a principal value.
