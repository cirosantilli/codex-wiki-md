# A nowhere-zero section annihilates generalized Euler classes

↑ **Parent:** [Euler class in a generalized cohomology theory](euler-class-in-a-generalized-cohomology-theory.md)

Normalize the section to the [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md). Scaling it from zero to unit length gives a [homotopy](homotopy.md) within the [disk bundle](disk-bundle.md) from the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) to this sphere-valued section. The image of the relative [Thom class](thom-class.md) in absolute cohomology restricts to zero on the [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md). [Homotopy](homotopy.md) invariance therefore makes its zero-section pullback vanish.
