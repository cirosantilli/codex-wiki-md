# Eddington bias

↑ **Parent:** [Selection bias](selection-bias.md)

Measurement scatter acting on a steep source-count distribution changes the observed abundance near a threshold. If faint objects are much more numerous, more scatter into a bright selected sample than scatter out of it. This is distinct from the accessible-volume weighting of [Malmquist bias](malmquist-bias.md), which can occur without measurement noise. Forward convolution of the population with the error model and [astronomical selection function](astronomical-selection-function.md) treats both effects consistently.
