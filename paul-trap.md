# Paul trap

↑ **Parent:** [Ion trap](ion-trap.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_trap)

The Paul trap alternates focusing and defocusing [electric forces](electric-force.md). Their time average along a moving trajectory need not vanish, even when the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) averages to zero at every fixed point. A spatial [gradient](gradient.md) makes the fast displacement correlate with the driving [electric field](electric-field-split.md); the [RF-averaged trapping potential](rf-averaged-trapping-potential.md) calculates the resulting restoring [force](force-split.md).

[Wolfgang Paul](wolfgang-paul.md)'s lecture describes hyperbolic ring/endcap electrodes, loading by [electron](electron.md)-impact [ionization](ionization.md) of low-pressure gas, and resonant ejection through an endcap hole into an [electron multiplier](electron-multiplier.md). Sweeping RF voltage produces an air [mass spectrum](mass-spectrum.md).[Paul, pp. 608–614, Figs. 6, 11; Columbia scan](electromagnetic-traps-for-charged-and-neutral-particles-by-wolfgang-paul.md)
