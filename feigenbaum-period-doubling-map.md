# Feigenbaum period-doubling map

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

The smooth quadratic-critical fixed point of period-doubling renormalization is normalized by $g(0)=1$ and $\beta=g(1)<0$. It has $g'(0)=0$ and $g''(0)\ne0$. Differentiating its displayed functional equation twice at zero gives $g'(1)=1/\beta$. Its restrictive intervals generate a Cantor attractor described by [Feigenbaum geometric potential](feigenbaum-geometric-potential.md) weights.
