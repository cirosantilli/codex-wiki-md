# Hydrodynamic radius

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic size](hydrodynamic-size.md)

The [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md) is the radius of a hypothetical no-slip [sphere](sphere.md) with the same translational [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) as the dissolved species. In the dilute continuum regime the [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md) gives

$$
R_h=\frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta D},
$$

where $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md), $T$ the absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md), $\eta$ the [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) of the [solvent](solvent.md), and $D$ the translational [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md). This effective radius need not equal the [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) or a geometric molecular radius. It is a useful descriptor for [SEC molar mass calibration](sec-molar-mass-calibration.md), rather than a complete specification of how every shape enters every pore.
