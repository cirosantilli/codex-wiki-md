# SYS-1

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A divergent [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) [beta-catenin](beta-catenin.md) coactivator that binds [POP-1](pop-1.md). A high [SYS-1](sys-1.md)-to-[POP-1](pop-1.md) ratio supports transcriptional activation in the appropriate posterior daughter.
