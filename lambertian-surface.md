# Lambertian surface

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md)

This idealized surface follows [Lambertian reflectance](lambertian-reflectance.md), with projected-area dependence governed by [Lambert's cosine law](lambert-s-cosine-law.md).

A Lambertian surface has direction-independent radiance. A perfectly reflecting Lambertian patch receiving normal flux $F$ emits specific intensity $I=F/\pi$ into its outward hemisphere.
