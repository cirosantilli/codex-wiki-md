# Spin eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A spin eigenstate along a specified axis is a simultaneous [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) of the squared [spin](spin.md) operator $S^2$ and its component $S_{\hat n}$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\hbar^2s(s+1)$ and $\hbar m$, where $m=-s,-s+1,\ldots,s$. The axis must be specified because components along different axes generally do not commute. For [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the two field-axis eigenstates have projections $\pm\hbar/2$ and form the branches of a [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md).
