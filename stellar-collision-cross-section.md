# Stellar collision cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

For two approximately spherical [stars](star.md) approaching with speed $v_\infty$, contact at separation $R_1+R_2$ has gravitationally focused [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) $\pi(R_1+R_2)^2[1+2G(m_1+m_2)/((R_1+R_2)v_\infty^2)]$. This measures direct stellar encounters, not the probability of a collective encounter between their host [galaxies](galaxy-split.md).
