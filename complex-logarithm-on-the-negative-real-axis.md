# Complex logarithm on the negative real axis

↑ **Parent:** [Complex logarithm](complex-logarithm.md)

A continuous choice of [complex logarithm](complex-logarithm.md) on a negative real interval has a fixed integer $k$ in the displayed expression. The choice with argument $\pi$ is the upper-bank boundary value of the [principal logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md); the usual analytic principal-branch domain itself excludes the negative axis. Consequently, integrating a real weight times that logarithm adds $(2k+1)i\pi$ times the integral of the weight to the real logarithmic integral. The branch choice must be stated.
