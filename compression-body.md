# Compression body

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compression_body)

A compression body is obtained from a product $F\times I$ by attaching [one-handles](one-handle.md) to $F\times\{1\}$, allowing three-ball components as well. Its negative boundary is the retained copy of $F$ and its positive boundary is the other boundary component. A [handlebody](handlebody.md) has empty negative boundary.
