# Extendible geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal geodesic](maximal-geodesic.md)

An [extendible geodesic](extendible-geodesic.md) is a [geodesic](geodesic.md) segment that can be continued beyond at least one endpoint within the same manifold after retaining an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). An artificial truncation at a regular event is extendible even when its continuation eventually reaches a singularity.
