# System of ordinary differential equations

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A system of ordinary differential equations specifies derivatives of several unknown [functions](function-split.md) of one independent variable. A vector-valued form $\mathbf y'=\mathbf f(t,\mathbf y)$ bundles its scalar equations. A scalar second-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) $y''=F(t,y,y')$ becomes the first-order system $(y_1',y_2')=(y_2,F(t,y_1,y_2))$ by introducing position and velocity variables. Conversely the first equation identifies $y_2=y_1'$ and the second recovers the original equation. Its [initial condition](initial-condition.md) must specify both variables.
