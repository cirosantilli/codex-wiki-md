# Pion G-parity

↑ **Parent:** [G parity](g-parity.md)

The neutral [pion](pion.md) has positive [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and its spin-one [isospin](isospin.md) half-turn has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) minus one. Hence the [G parity](g-parity.md) [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is minus one. Since G parity commutes with [isospin](isospin.md), both charged members have the same value. Charged-state phase changes alter individual charge-conjugation and rotation formulas together, leaving this [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) unchanged.
