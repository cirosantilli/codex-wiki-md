# Phonemic restoration

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

[Perception](perception.md) of a speech sequence as containing a segment whose acoustic information has been replaced by another sound. The classic contrast with a silent gap shows the importance of the replacement sound as plausible masking evidence. The perceived continuity does not mean that the missing waveform has been reconstructed from the ear signal alone.[Replacement-sound versus silence comparison, Science 167, pp. 392–393](perceptual-restoration-of-missing-speech-sounds-by-richard-m-warren.md)
