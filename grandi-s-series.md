<h1 id="grandi-s-series">Grandi's series</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandi's_series)

The [series](series-mathematics.md) with terms $a_n=(-1)^n$, indexed from zero, named for [Guido Grandi](luigi-guido-grandi.md). Its even-indexed [partial sums](partial-sum.md) are one and its odd-indexed [partial sums](partial-sum.md) are zero, so it has no ordinary sum. First-order [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md) gives $1/2$. Grouping adjacent pairs would obscure this distinction: the [partial sums](partial-sum.md) of the grouped expressions select only one of the original [subsequences](subsequence.md).
