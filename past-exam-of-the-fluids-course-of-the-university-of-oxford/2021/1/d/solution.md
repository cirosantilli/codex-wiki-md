<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take outward radial [volume flux per unit width](../../../../volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) as positive. The [streamfunction in polar coordinates](../../../../streamfunction-in-polar-coordinates.md) immediately gives

$$
q(r)=\int_0^\alpha v_r(r,\theta)r\,d\theta
=\psi(r,\alpha)-\psi(r,0)
=\boxed{-\frac12r^2\dot\alpha.}
$$

For spanwise width $W$, the [volumetric flow rate](../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) is $Q=Wq$. The wedge inside radius $r$ has cross-sectional [area](../../../../surface-area.md) $\alpha r^2/2$, whose rate of increase is $r^2\dot\alpha/2$. Opening therefore draws in exactly the [volume](../../../../volume.md) needed to fill the expanding wedge; closing expels it. This is also a direct check of [conservation of mass](../../../../mass-conservation.md) for the moving [control volume](../../../../control-volume.md). It follows from the wall [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) and remains true even though the printed interior [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md) is erroneous.
