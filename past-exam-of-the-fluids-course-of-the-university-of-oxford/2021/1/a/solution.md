<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md) compares [convective acceleration](../../../../convective-acceleration.md) with viscous [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) in the [Navier-Stokes equation](../../../../navier-stokes-equation.md):

$$
\mathrm{Re}=\frac{UL}{\nu}=\frac{\rho UL}{\mu}.
$$

At large [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md), [inertia](../../../../inertia.md) dominates in the bulk; [viscous boundary layers](../../../../viscous-boundary-layer.md) can still control wall [shear stress](../../../../shear-stress.md), separation and [drag](../../../../drag-physics.md). [Turbulence](../../../../turbulence-split.md) is possible but does not follow from large [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md) alone. At small [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md), [viscous stress](../../../../viscous-stress-tensor.md) balances the [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md) and applied [body forces](../../../../body-force.md). In the [quasi-steady Stokes approximation](../../../../quasi-steady-stokes-approximation.md), the [velocity field](../../../../velocity-field.md) responds instantaneously to the boundary motion and has [kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](../../../../kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md). Small [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md) alone does not suppress rapid local [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md).

For a swimmer, take a characteristic [length](../../../../length.md) $L\sim1\,\mathrm m$ and [speed](../../../../speed.md) $U\sim1\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. The supplied [kinematic viscosity](../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Re}\sim\frac{1\times1}{10^{-6}}=10^6\gg1.}
$$

Human swimming is therefore an [inertia](../../../../inertia.md)-dominated [fluid flow](../../../../fluid-flow.md), rather than [Stokes flow](../../../../stokes-flow-split.md).
