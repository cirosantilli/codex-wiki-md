<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [simple shear flow](../../../../simple-shear-flow.md), the vertical [vorticity](../../../../vorticity-split.md) is

$$
\zeta=\partial_xv_y-\partial_yv_x=\boxed{-\beta}.
$$

For $\beta\ne0$, this is not an [irrotational flow](../../../../irrotational-flow.md). A [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $w=\phi+i\psi$, analytic in the flow region, has [complex velocity](../../../../complex-velocity.md) $w'=v_x-iv_y$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) imply both [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and zero [vorticity](../../../../vorticity-split.md), so this shear has no such [complex potential](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md). It does have a [Cartesian streamfunction](../../../../cartesian-streamfunction.md), $\psi=\beta y^2/2$. In the degenerate case $\beta=0$, the resting fluid has a constant [complex potential](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md).
