# Fresnel tail expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Finite Fresnel integrals](finite-fresnel-integrals.md)

For $A=\sqrt{\pi/8}$, define real tail functions by $Q+iP=e^{-i\lambda^2}\int_\lambda^\infty e^{it^2}dt$. Then $C=A+P\sin\lambda^2-Q\cos\lambda^2$ and $S=A-P\cos\lambda^2-Q\sin\lambda^2$. Repeated [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
P\sim\sum_{m\ge0}\frac{(-1)^m(4m-1)!!}{2^{2m+1}\lambda^{4m+1}},\qquad Q\sim\sum_{m\ge0}\frac{(-1)^m(4m+1)!!}{2^{2m+2}\lambda^{4m+3}},
$$

where $(-1)!!=1$. These are [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion.md), rather than convergent infinite sums at a fixed argument.
