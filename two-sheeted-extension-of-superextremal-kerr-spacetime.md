# Two-sheeted extension of superextremal Kerr spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic extension of a spacetime](analytic-extension-of-a-spacetime.md)

For $0<M<a$, $\Delta=(r-M)^2+a^2-M^2>0$, so no horizon blocks need adjoining. The oblate Cartesian coordinates of the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) satisfy the displayed relation, where $\rho^2=x^2+y^2$. Keeping only the positive root $r$ leaves a cut along the disk $z=0$, $\rho<a$. Continue $r$ through zero away from its boundary, the [Kerr ring singularity](kerr-ring-singularity.md), onto a negative-radius sheet. Equivalently cut two Cartesian sheets along the disk and join opposite banks, so $r$ changes sign continuously. The metric is analytic at the disk interior in signed oblate coordinates since $\Sigma=a^2\cos^2\theta\ne0$ there. The extension has asymptotically flat ends at $r\to+\infty$ and $r\to-\infty$; only the singular ring remains inextendible by an analytic Lorentz metric.
