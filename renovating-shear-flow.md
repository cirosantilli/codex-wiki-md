# Renovating shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Shear flow](shear-flow.md)

A renovating shear flow holds a planar [shear flow](shear-flow.md) fixed for an interval $T$, then chooses a fresh orientation independently of preceding intervals. Each interval acts on a [material line](material-curve.md) by a rotated copy of the displayed [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md). The orientation law must be specified: independent uniformly distributed orientations define the isotropic model, whereas a biased orientation law generally gives another [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md).
