# Quartic dispersion relation for a three-layer stratified shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Three-layer stratified piecewise-linear shear flow](three-layer-stratified-piecewise-linear-shear-flow.md)

For the bounded-shear three-layer profile with density contrasts minus one, zero and plus one, the localized-mode matching determinant is $[a(1-c)^2-(1-c)-J][a(1+c)^2-(1+c)-J]-b^2(1-c^2)^2=0$, where $a=\alpha[1+\coth(2\alpha)]$ and $b=\alpha\operatorname{csch}(2\alpha)$. Dividing by $a^2-b^2=2\alpha a$ gives a quartic in $c$ and a quadratic in $c^2$. Its constant term is $\{[2\alpha-(1+J)]^2-e^{-4\alpha}(1+J)^2\}/(4\alpha^2)$, whose negative sign proves the [unstable band of a three-layer stratified shear flow](unstable-band-of-a-three-layer-stratified-shear-flow.md).
