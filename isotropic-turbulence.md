# Isotropic turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md)

[Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md) has statistics invariant under rotations as well as translations. With no mean velocity, its two-point [velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md) depends on separation length and on longitudinal/transverse directions, and its one-component velocity [variances](variance-split.md) are equal.
