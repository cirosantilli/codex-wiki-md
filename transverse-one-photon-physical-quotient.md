# Transverse one-photon physical quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Gupta-Bleuler null-state quotient](gupta-bleuler-null-state-quotient.md)

Choose a temporal [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md), two transverse [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md) and a spatial longitudinal [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md). The [Gupta-Bleuler quantization](gupta-bleuler-formalism.md) condition is $(a^0-a^3)|\chi\rangle=0$. Applied to $\sum_\lambda c_\lambda a^{\lambda\dagger}|0\rangle$, it gives $c_3=-c_0$. The remaining temporal-longitudinal excitation $a^{0\dagger}-a^{3\dagger}$ has zero norm and is orthogonal to every constrained state. Its field wavefunction is proportional to the null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md), hence is pure gauge. Quotienting this direction leaves the two positive transverse [photon](photon.md) states. Use [wave packets](wave-packet.md) or a box normalization to avoid treating continuum momentum eigenstates as normalizable vectors.
