# Hopf-type transition with a nonsmooth radial response

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

For $\dot r=r(a-g(r))$, $\dot\theta=1$ and $g(r)=1+[1+(r-1)^2]^{-1}$, the origin loses [stability](lyapunov-stability.md) at $a=3/2$ and a [stable](lyapunov-stability.md) small [circular orbit](limit-cycle.md) appears for larger $a$. Its radius is $1-\sqrt{(2-a)/(a-1)}\sim2(a-3/2)$. The [Cartesian](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) [vector field](vector-field.md) is continuously differentiable but not generally twice differentiable at the origin, so the usual [smooth Hopf normal form](hopf-bifurcation.md) and its square-root amplitude law do not apply directly. The explicit radial equation proves the transition.
