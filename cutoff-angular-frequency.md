# Cutoff angular frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Cutoff frequency](cutoff-frequency.md)

The [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) corresponding to a specified filter [cutoff frequency](cutoff-frequency.md). For a first-order [RC low-pass filter](rc-low-pass-filter.md), the half-power convention gives $\omega_c=1/(RC)$ and $f_c=1/(2\pi RC)$.
