# Optical homodyne detection

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

Interference of a signal with a coherent reference at the same optical carrier frequency to measure phase-sensitive field information. The reference fixes an [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md). The inferred noise depends on reference strength, detector efficiency and the detection arrangement.
