# Laser gain medium

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

The material in which pumping produces [optical gain](optical-gain.md) through [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md). Its inversion density, mode-overlap volume and emission cross section determine whether the gain overcomes [cavity](optical-cavity.md) losses.
