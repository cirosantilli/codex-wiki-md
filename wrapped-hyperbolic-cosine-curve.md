# Wrapped hyperbolic cosine curve

↑ **Parent:** [Circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md)

For $-\pi\leq t\leq\pi$, this [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) closes with a corner on a unit [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md). Its [speed](speed.md) is $\cosh t$, so its [arc length](arc-length.md) is $2\sinh\pi$. The [cylindrical surface](cylindrical-surface.md) between the [curve](curve.md) and $z=0$ has the same [surface area](surface-area.md), because unrolling gives the [integral](integral-split.md) $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\cosh t\,dt$.
