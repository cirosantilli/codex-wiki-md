# Hydrostatic lower bound for a travelling-pressure response

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

For the subcritical [travelling-pressure response of finite-depth surface gravity waves](travelling-pressure-response-of-finite-depth-surface-gravity-waves.md), put $c_*^2=g\tanh(kh)/k$. When $0\leq c<c_*$, the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is

$$
a(c)=\frac{p_1}{\rho g}\frac1{1-(c/c_*)^2}\geq\frac{p_1}{\rho g}.
$$

The inequality follows immediately because the denominator belongs to $(0,1]$. Equality holds precisely at $c=0$, where [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $\rho g\eta=-p_1\cos(kx)$. If only strictly positive $c$ is allowed, $p_1/(\rho g)$ is the [infimum](infimum.md), approached as $c\downarrow0$, and is not an attained [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md). This bound applies to the travelling forced component, rather than an arbitrary superposition with free [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md).
