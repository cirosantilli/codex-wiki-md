# Schmidt basis

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)

An orthonormal subsystem basis appearing in a [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md). Its vectors diagonalize the corresponding [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md). Degenerate [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) permit a freedom to rotate the paired bases together; zero-weight basis vectors complete the subsystem space without contributing to the state.
