# RNA interference

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

Sequence-directed regulation guided by small [RNAs](ribonucleic-acid.md), often through [Argonaute](argonaute.md). Its effects can include transcript destruction, repression of [biological translation](translation-biology.md) or recruitment of repressive [chromatin](chromatin.md), depending on the pathway.
