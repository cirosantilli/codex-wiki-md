# Min strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Counting-on strategy](counting-on-strategy.md)

For [addition](addition.md) of nonnegative [natural numbers](natural-number.md) $a$ and $b$, start at $\max(a,b)$ and count $\min(a,b)$ further units. [Commutativity](commutativity.md) guarantees the same sum while minimizing the increments among the two possible choices of starting addend.
