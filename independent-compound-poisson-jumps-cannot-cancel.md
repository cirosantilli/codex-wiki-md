# Independent compound Poisson jumps cannot cancel

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md)

Let $Y$ be a [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md) with nonzero jump marks and positive rate, [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of a [càdlàg](cadlag.md) process $Z$. Conditional on the path of $Z$, its discontinuities form a [countable set](countable-set.md). The Poisson jump times have continuous distributions and avoid that set almost surely. At every jump of $Y$, the sum $Y+Z$ therefore has the same nonzero jump. In particular a [continuous Lévy process](continuous-levy-process.md) cannot have a positive-rate [independent](independent-random-variables.md) compound Poisson component. Applying this to each truncation of its [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md) proves that its jump measure is zero.
