# Delayed logistic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Delay differential equation](delay-differential-equation.md)

This [delay differential equation](delay-differential-equation.md) replaces instantaneous logistic inhibition by a population value one delay earlier. A positive [history function of a delay differential equation](history-function-of-a-delay-differential-equation.md) gives positive solutions while they exist. The equilibria are zero and one. [Linearization](linearization.md) at one gives $x'(t)=-x(t-\tau)$, with characteristic equation $\lambda=-e^{-\lambda\tau}$. Delayed sign reversal permits overshoots around one and, at the [delayed logistic Hopf threshold](delayed-logistic-hopf-threshold.md), a periodic branch.
