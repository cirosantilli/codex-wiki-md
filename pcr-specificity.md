# PCR specificity

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)

[PCR specificity](pcr-specificity.md) concerns whether the accumulated [amplicon](amplicon.md) comes from the intended interval. Binding [temperature](temperature-split.md), [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) and their possible alternative binding sites matter. Too permissive binding can support competing products; excessively restrictive binding can prevent the intended product. A band of the expected length is useful evidence, but unrelated [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) can have the same length. [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md) tests identity more directly, subject to mixtures and copying errors.[Primer Design for PCR: specificity, paired melting temperatures and complementary primer sequences](how-to-design-a-primer-by-addgene.md)
