# Single-molecule force spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Single-molecule experiment](single-molecule-experiment.md)

Measure the [force](force-split.md) and extension of an individual molecular tether under controlled displacement or load. A calibrated cantilever or [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) converts deflection to [force](force-split.md). Synthetic [polymer](polymer-split.md) stretching provides a nonbiological application; the observed curve also contains instrument and attachment compliance, and its loading-rate dependence can distinguish [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) elasticity from activated events.
