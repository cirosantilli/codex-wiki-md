# Critical density

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

At Hubble parameter $H$, the critical energy density is

$$
\rho_{\rm crit}=\frac{3c^2H^2}{8\pi G}.
$$

This is the density threshold in the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) for spatial flatness; thermodynamic and plasma critical densities are different concepts.
