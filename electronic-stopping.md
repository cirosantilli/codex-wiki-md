# Electronic stopping

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping power](stopping-power.md)

Energy loss of a moving particle to [electronic excitation](electronic-excitation.md) and [ionization](ionization.md). It is distinct from direct [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer to target nuclei; electronic energy deposition can also couple to atomic motion in a material-dependent way.
