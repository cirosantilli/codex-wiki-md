# Tetrahedral intermediate

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction intermediate](reaction-intermediate.md)

A tetrahedral intermediate has a formerly trigonal [carbonyl group](carbonyl-group.md) carbon bonded to four groups after [nucleophilic addition](nucleophilic-addition.md). Its subsequent elimination can restore a [carbonyl group](carbonyl-group.md).
