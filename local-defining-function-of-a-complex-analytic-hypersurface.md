# Local defining function of a complex analytic hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analytic hypersurface](complex-analytic-hypersurface.md)

A local defining function for a complex analytic hypersurface $Y$ near $x$ is a holomorphic function $f$ on a neighbourhood $U$ such that $Y\cap U=\{f=0\}$. It may be chosen reduced, with each local irreducible factor occurring once. The [local ring](local-ring.md) $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ of a complex manifold is a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md) and hence a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md); each height-one prime of the hypersurface germ is principal, and the product of generators for its finitely many local branches gives $f$.
