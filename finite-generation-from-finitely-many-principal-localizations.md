# Finite generation from finitely many principal localizations

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated algebra](finitely-generated-algebra.md)

Suppose a $k$-algebra $A$ has elements $f_i,g_i$ with $\sum_i g_if_i=1$, and each $A_{f_i}$ is a [finitely generated algebra](finitely-generated-algebra.md). Let $B\subseteq A$ contain the $f_i,g_i$ and numerators of finite generating sets of all $A_{f_i}$. Then $B$ is finitely generated and $B_{f_i}=A_{f_i}$. For $a\in A$, clear denominators so that all $f_i^Na$ belong to $B$. A [unit-ideal identity for powers](unit-ideal-identity-for-powers.md) gives $1=\sum_i c_if_i^N$ in $B$, hence $a=\sum_i c_i f_i^Na\in B$. Thus $A=B$.
