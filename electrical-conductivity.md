# Electrical conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md)

Electrical [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) relates [current density](current-density.md) to the local [electric field](electric-field.md) in the rest frame of an isotropic ohmic medium: $\mathbf J=\sigma\mathbf E$. It is a bulk material coefficient, distinct from a sheet surface [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md). A moving conductor instead uses the [moving-conductor Ohm law](moving-conductor-ohm-law.md).
