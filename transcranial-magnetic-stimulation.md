# Transcranial magnetic stimulation

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscientific methods](neuroscientific-methods.md)

Noninvasive stimulation in which a changing magnetic field induces electrical effects in neural tissue. Task-specific perturbation and control conditions can test causal hypotheses, but stimulation can affect connected regions and does not isolate a unique cellular mechanism.
