# Stellar activity Rossby number

↑ **Parent:** [Rossby number](rossby-number.md)

For a convective star, the activity convention compares its rotation period with a convective turnover time. It differs by fixed factors from the fluid [Rossby number](rossby-number.md) estimated from speed, length and angular velocity. Small $\operatorname{Ro}_*$ denotes strong rotational influence on [convection](convection.md). Magnetic activity usually rises as this ratio decreases in the unsaturated regime, but levels off in a saturated rapid-rotation regime.
