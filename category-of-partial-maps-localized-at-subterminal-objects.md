# Category of partial maps localized at subterminal objects

↑ **Parent:** [Congruence on a category](congruence-on-a-category.md)

Given a finite-product category and a product-closed upward filter $\Phi$ of [subobjects](subobject.md) of its [terminal object](terminal-object.md), a morphism $A\to B$ in $\mathcal C_\Phi$ is represented by a map $A\times U\to B$ for $U\in\Phi$, with two representatives identified when they agree after restriction to some $W\in\Phi$ below both domains. This construction preserves finite products and, when $\mathcal C$ is [Cartesian closed](cartesian-closed-category.md), exponentials.
