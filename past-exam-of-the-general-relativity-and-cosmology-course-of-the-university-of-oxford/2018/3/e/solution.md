<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Spatial flatness constrains the total density, not matter alone. A flat model can have

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{m0}+\Omega_{\Lambda0}+\Omega_{r0}=1}
$$

with a substantial [dark energy](../../../../dark-energy.md) component. A positive [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md) has [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) $p_\Lambda=-\rho_\Lambda c^2$ and does not dilute like matter; it can produce the observed accelerated expansion.

If positive $\Lambda$ persists, $a(t)$ becomes exponential and the future [conformal time](../../../../conformal-time.md) interval

$$
\Delta\eta_{\rm future}=\int_{t_0}^{\infty}\frac{dt}{a(t)}
$$

is finite. For two comoving civilisations separated by $\chi$, each one-way signal consumes $\chi/c$. Thus a message completing $n$ legs at finite time requires

$$
\boxed{n\frac\chi c<\Delta\eta_{\rm future}.}
$$

Only finitely many legs, and hence finitely many full exchanges, can then occur. A reply to even the first new message requires $2\chi/c<\Delta\eta_{\rm future}$, the [cosmological round-trip communication horizon](../../../../cosmological-round-trip-communication-horizon.md) condition. Depending on the density parameters, the initial outward message might arrive while its reply can never reach us, as in the right-hand panel of part [d](../d.md).

The source gives no matter and dark-energy fractions, so it does not specify an exact count for the $z=1$ pair in the real Universe. Flatness and lack of matter dominance alone do not prove a finite horizon: a radiation-dominated flat universe, for example, has divergent future [conformal time](../../../../conformal-time.md) if that power-law expansion persists. The finite-message conclusion uses persistent accelerated expansion such as positive constant $\Lambda$.
