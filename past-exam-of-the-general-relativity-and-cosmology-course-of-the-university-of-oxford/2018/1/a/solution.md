<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [strong equivalence principle](../../../../strong-equivalence-principle.md) extends universality of [free fall](../../../../free-fall.md) to bodies with appreciable gravitational self-energy, and states that the outcome of any local experiment, including a gravitational experiment, in a freely falling laboratory is independent of its location and [velocity](../../../../velocity.md). In a sufficiently small freely falling laboratory the external gravitational influence can be removed to first order by choosing a [local inertial frame](../../../../local-inertial-frame.md).

This does not remove [tidal forces](../../../../tidal-force.md) or the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md). The laboratory must be small compared with the length scale over which the external gravitational field varies. These qualifications distinguish a local equivalence statement from the incorrect assertion that gravity can be transformed away throughout an extended curved region.
