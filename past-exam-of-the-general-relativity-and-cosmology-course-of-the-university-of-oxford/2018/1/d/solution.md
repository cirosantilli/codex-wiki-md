<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $F(r)=1-r_s/r$ and $r_s=2GM/c^2$. For radial motion $u^\theta=u^\phi=0$. The given [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) imply

$$
\boxed{a^0=\frac{du^0}{d\tau}
+\frac{2GM}{c^2r^2F}u^0u^r,}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{a^r=\frac{du^r}{d\tau}
+\frac{GM}{c^2r^2}\left[F(u^0)^2-F^{-1}(u^r)^2\right].}
$$

The [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) normalization of part [c](../c.md) is

$$
-F(u^0)^2+F^{-1}(u^r)^2=-c^2.
$$

Its bracketed combination is therefore exactly $c^2$, independently of the instantaneous [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) or the thrust history. Consequently

$$
\boxed{a^r=\frac{du^r}{d\tau}+\frac{GM}{r^2}.}
$$

The derivative here is with respect to [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md). This expression does not say that the ship measures a gravitational acceleration $GM/r^2$ during [free fall](../../../../free-fall.md); setting $a^r=0$ gives its radial proper-time equation of motion in [Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md).
