<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In general coordinates the basis changes along the [world line](../../../../world-line.md). Differentiating only the components of a [vector](../../../../vector.md) omits that basis change, and does not transform as a [vector](../../../../vector.md) under a position-dependent coordinate transformation. The appropriate rate is the [covariant derivative along a curve](../../../../covariant-derivative-along-a-curve.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{DV^\alpha}{d\lambda}
=\frac{dV^\alpha}{d\lambda}
+\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}\frac{dx^\beta}{d\lambda}V^\gamma.}
$$

For the metric-compatible torsion-free connection, the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are determined by the [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) and its first derivatives:

$$
\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}
=\frac12g^{\alpha\delta}(\partial_\beta g_{\delta\gamma}
+\partial_\gamma g_{\delta\beta}-\partial_\delta g_{\beta\gamma}).
$$

Thus the ship's [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md) is

$$
\boxed{a^\alpha=\frac{Du^\alpha}{d\tau}
=\frac{du^\alpha}{d\tau}+\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}u^\beta u^\gamma.}
$$

In Cartesian coordinates of an [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) in flat [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md), the connection coefficients vanish and the ordinary component derivative happens to suffice. In curved [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) it suffices at a chosen event in a [local inertial frame](../../../../local-inertial-frame.md), but not generally along an extended trajectory.
