<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For zero [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), each species contributes $S_i=(U_i+P_iV)/T=4U_i/(3T)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_B}{V}=\frac{2\pi^2}{45}g_Bk_{\rm B}
\left(\frac{k_{\rm B}T}{\hbar c}\right)^3,\qquad
\frac{S_F}{V}=\frac78\frac{2\pi^2}{45}g_Fk_{\rm B}
\left(\frac{k_{\rm B}T}{\hbar c}\right)^3.}
$$

[Entropy density](../../../../entropy-density.md) scales as $T^3$ at fixed relativistic particle content; total [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) also includes the changing proper volume. An isolated adiabatically expanding comoving gas has conserved total [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) and $aT$ constant, rather than its total [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) falling as $T^3$.

At equal [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), single [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [photons](../../../../photon.md) each have two spin/polarization states. In the [ultrarelativistic](../../../../ultrarelativistic-particle.md) approximation,

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_{e^-}}{S_\gamma}=\frac78.}
$$

[Positrons](../../../../positron.md) are a separate species with the same contribution, so $(S_{e^-}+S_{e^+})/S_\gamma=7/4$. This distinction is needed for the heating count in part [d](../d.md). At the exact supplied [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) the [electron](../../../../electron.md) rest mass gives corrections to the ideal $7/8$ ratio; it is the limiting relativistic value requested by the problem.

There is also a sign convention issue in the introductory [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) formula. With the chemical-potential convention used in the supplied distribution, the Euler relation is $U=TS-PV+\sum_i\mu_iN_i$, so $S=(U+PV-\sum_i\mu_iN_i)/T$. Its chemical-potential term has the opposite sign to the printed one. For a concrete counterexample, a dilute monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) with one internal state and $n\lambda_T^3=e^{-1}$, where $\lambda_T$ is the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md), has $\mu=-k_{\rm B}T$, $U=3Nk_{\rm B}T/2$ and $PV=Nk_{\rm B}T$. Its [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) is $7Nk_{\rm B}/2$; the printed plus sign would instead give $3Nk_{\rm B}/2$. All [chemical potentials](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) have been neglected here, so this source error does not change any of the above results.
