<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [photon gas](../../../../photon-gas.md) in thermal and [chemical equilibrium](../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md), [photon](../../../../photon.md) number is not a conserved thermodynamic charge. Number-changing processes can create and absorb [photons](../../../../photon.md) without a corresponding conserved-number constraint, so the equilibrium [photon chemical potential](../../../../photon-chemical-potential.md) is zero.

At $T=5\times10^9\,\mathrm K$, $k_{\rm B}T\simeq0.431\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The other light species are [electrons](../../../../electron.md), [Positrons](../../../../positron.md), three [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) species and their antiparticles. [Neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) are [ultrarelativistic](../../../../ultrarelativistic-particle.md); [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [Positrons](../../../../positron.md) are only moderately relativistic at this particular [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) because $m_ec^2=0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The later simple counting formulas use the intended [ultrarelativistic](../../../../ultrarelativistic-particle.md) approximation for them. Baryons, muons and heavier particles are not an abundant relativistic bath at this [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md).

Pair equilibrium implies $\mu_{e^-}+\mu_{e^+}=2\mu_\gamma=0$. Approximate charge symmetry, with the net [electron](../../../../electron.md) excess tied to the much smaller baryon asymmetry, then makes both [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../positron.md) [chemical potentials](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) small compared with $k_{\rm B}T$. For [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) one additionally assumes negligible lepton asymmetry. Their near-zero [chemical potentials](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) are not implied merely by their statistics. After [neutrino decoupling](../../../../neutrino-decoupling.md), a distribution initially having negligible [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) retains a redshifted [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md), with its own [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md).
