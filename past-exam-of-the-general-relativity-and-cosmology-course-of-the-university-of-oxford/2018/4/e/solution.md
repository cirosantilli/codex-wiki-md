<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use the observed present [photon](../../../../photon.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_{\gamma0}\simeq2.725\,\mathrm K$. This is an additional observational input, consistent with [abstract: combined CMB-temperature determination](../../../../the-temperature-of-the-cosmic-microwave-background-by-dale-j-fixsen.md). Standard [neutrino decoupling](../../../../neutrino-decoupling.md) and electron-positron [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) transfer give

$$
\boxed{T_{\nu0}=\left(\frac4{11}\right)^{1/3}T_{\gamma0}\simeq1.945\,\mathrm K.}
$$

For nonrelativistic relic [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) this is the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) parameter of the redshifted momentum distribution, not the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) of a newly thermalized gas of massive particles.

The [photon number density](../../../../photon-number-density.md) from part [b](../b.md) is

$$
n_{\gamma0}=\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\pi^2}
\left(\frac{k_{\rm B}T_{\gamma0}}{\hbar c}\right)^3\simeq410.5\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}.
$$

One [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) mass eigenstate and its antineutrino have two populated [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) states. Thus

$$
\boxed{n_{\nu_i}+n_{\bar\nu_i}
=\frac34\left(\frac{T_\nu}{T_\gamma}\right)^3n_\gamma
=\frac3{11}n_\gamma\simeq112\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}.}
$$

The total over three species is about $336\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, including antineutrinos. Comoving number conservation preserves these abundances after the particles become nonrelativistic.

For the supplied $H_0=72\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}=2.33\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$,

$$
\rho_c=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G}\simeq9.74\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}.
$$

Assuming nonnegative other energy components, relic [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) rest-mass density cannot exceed the near-critical total density. With the common per-species abundance $n_*=n_{\nu_i}+n_{\bar\nu_i}$,

$$
\rho_{\nu,\rm rest}=n_*\sum_{i=1}^3m_i\le\rho_c,
\qquad
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^3m_i\lesssim\frac{\rho_c}{n_*}
\simeq48.8\,\mathrm{eV}/c^2\approx50\,\mathrm{eV}/c^2.}
$$

Using the total $336\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$ would instead bound the mean mass, not the sum. This [critical-density bound on the sum of relic neutrino masses](../../../../critical-density-bound-on-the-sum-of-relic-neutrino-masses.md) is deliberately conservative: baryons, dark matter and [dark energy](../../../../dark-energy.md) leave less density available. It assumes the standard thermal abundance, negligible [chemical potentials](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), no later dilution or extra populated states, and the idealized decoupling count. Small non-instantaneous-decoupling corrections do not matter for this estimate.
