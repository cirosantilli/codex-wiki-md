<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

As the plasma cools through electron-positron annihilation, the pairs transfer their [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) to the coupled [photons](../../../../photon.md). [Neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) have largely decoupled and do not share that heating in the instantaneous-decoupling approximation. Before annihilation the electromagnetic [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) count is

$$
g_{*s,\rm before}=2+\frac78(2+2)=\frac{11}{2},
$$

while after it $g_{*s,\rm after}=2$. Conservation of electromagnetic [comoving entropy](../../../../comoving-entropy.md), $g_{*s}T_\gamma^3a^3=\text{constant}$, gives the relative [photon](../../../../photon.md) heating factor

$$
\boxed{\frac{(aT_\gamma)_{\rm after}}{(aT_\gamma)_{\rm before}}
=\left(\frac{11}{4}\right)^{1/3}\simeq1.401.}
$$

Equivalently $T_\gamma/T_\nu=(11/4)^{1/3}$ afterward. The exact $11/4$ count compares an asymptotic relativistic pair bath with the pair-free bath; at the supplied [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) some finite-electron-mass [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) loss has already begun.

The word “rises” requires care. The [photons](../../../../photon.md) become hotter than an otherwise freely redshifting bath at the same [scale factor](../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md); their absolute [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) need not rise during the expansion. In the smooth equilibrium process,

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\ln T_\gamma}{d\ln a}
=-\frac{3}{3+d\ln g_{*s}/d\ln T_\gamma}<0,}
$$

since the pair contribution grows with [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). This [monotonic photon cooling during pair annihilation](../../../../monotonic-photon-cooling-during-pair-annihilation.md) is slower than $a^{-1}$ while $aT_\gamma$ increases. The factor $1.401$ compares the [photon](../../../../photon.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) with the freely redshifting [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) bath, not two consecutive absolute temperatures along the expanding history.
