<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Throughout this question use [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md) with $c=1$. For zero potential and nonzero [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), the homogeneous canonical [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) has $P=\rho=\dot\varphi^2/2$, so $\boxed{w=1}$. Its [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) becomes $d(a^3\dot\varphi)/dt=0$, giving $\dot\varphi\propto a^{-3}$ and therefore

$$
\boxed{\rho\propto a^{-6}.}
$$

The same scaling follows from [cosmological energy conservation](../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) with constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w=1$. If the field is exactly constant, both $P$ and $\rho$ vanish and their ratio is undefined; that is the separate empty solution.
