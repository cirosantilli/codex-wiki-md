<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At maximum expansion, $a_{\max}=A/k$. With $a_0=1$, the present [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $kc^2=H_0^2(\Omega_{M,0}-1)$, so positive curvature requires $\Omega_{M,0}>1$. Taking $H_0>0$ for the presently expanding branch gives

$$
\boxed{a_{\max}=\frac{\Omega_{M,0}}{\Omega_{M,0}-1},\qquad
t_{\rm life}=\frac{\pi\Omega_{M,0}}{H_0(\Omega_{M,0}-1)^{3/2}},\qquad
q_0=\frac{\Omega_{M,0}}2.}
$$

The [lifetime of a closed dust universe](../../../../lifetime-of-a-closed-dust-universe.md) follows by setting $\theta=2\pi$. The [deceleration parameter](../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) follows from the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md), $\ddot a/a=-4\pi G\rho_M/3$. Thus this model always decelerates while expanding. Its total lifetime includes the elapsed age; it is not the remaining lifetime.

The [supernova discovery of accelerating expansion](../../../../supernova-discovery-of-accelerating-expansion.md) does not support this dust-only recollapse model as a description of the observed expansion history. Already the 1998 and 1999 [Type Ia supernova](../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) analyses favoured [accelerating expansion](../../../../accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md), requiring ingredients beyond this zero-[cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md), positive-curvature dust model within the usual [FLRW metric](../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) interpretation.[Abstract: evidence for present acceleration](../../../../observational-evidence-from-supernovae-for-an-accelerating-universe-and-a-cosmological-constant-by-adam-riess-and-others.md)[Abstract: positive cosmological constant preferred](../../../../measurements-of-omega-and-lambda-from-42-high-redshift-supernovae-by-saul-perlmutter-and-others.md)
