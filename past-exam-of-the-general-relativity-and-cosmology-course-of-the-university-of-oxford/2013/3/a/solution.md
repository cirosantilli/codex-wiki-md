<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [mass density](../../../../density.md) units for the $\rho$ appearing in the [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md); an actual [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) is $\rho c^2$. Conservation, $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P/c^2)=0$, gives $\boxed{\rho_M=\rho_{M,0}a^{-3},\quad\rho_R=\rho_{R,0}a^{-4}}$ for [pressureless matter](../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), respectively, with $a_0=1$. The first scaling counts dilution of particle number, while the second includes the [cosmological redshift](../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) of each photon's [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).

Define the present [cosmological density parameters](../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) by $\Omega_{X,0}=8\pi G\rho_{X,0}/(3H_0^2)$. Then

$$
\frac{H^2}{H_0^2}=\Omega_{M,0}a^{-3}+\Omega_{R,0}a^{-4}+\Omega_{k,0}a^{-2},\qquad
\Omega_{k,0}=-\frac{kc^2}{H_0^2}=1-\Omega_{M,0}-\Omega_{R,0}.
$$

For $k=0$, [conformal time](../../../../conformal-time.md) gives $da/d\eta=a^2H$, so the expanding branch obeys $(da/d\eta)^2=H_0^2(\Omega_{M,0}a+\Omega_{R,0})$. Set $\eta=0$ at the [Big Bang](../../../../big-bang.md). Integrating gives

$$
\sqrt{\Omega_{M,0}a+\Omega_{R,0}}=\sqrt{\Omega_{R,0}}+\frac12\Omega_{M,0}H_0\eta,
$$

and hence the [flat matter-radiation conformal solution](../../../../flat-matter-radiation-conformal-solution.md)

$$
\boxed{a(\eta)=\sqrt{\Omega_{R,0}}H_0\eta+\frac14\Omega_{M,0}(H_0\eta)^2.}
$$

The limiting pure-radiation and pure-matter cases follow continuously.
