<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At [matter-radiation equality](../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md), $\rho_{M,0}a^{-3}=\rho_{R,0}a^{-4}$, so $a_{\rm eq}=\Omega_{R,0}/\Omega_{M,0}$. Evaluating the integrated square-root relation gives

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\rm eq}=\frac{2(\sqrt2-1)\sqrt{\Omega_{R,0}}}{\Omega_{M,0}H_0}.}
$$

This assumes both present densities are positive and uses the same [Big Bang](../../../../big-bang.md) origin for [conformal time](../../../../conformal-time.md).
