<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $\mathcal H=a'/a$, with the prime denoting $\partial_0=c^{-1}\partial_\eta$. The [metric](../../../../metric.md) and [inverse metric](../../../../inverse-metric.md) are $g_{\mu\nu}=a^2\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and $g^{\mu\nu}=a^{-2}\eta^{\mu\nu}$. Since $a$ depends only on $x^0$, the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) reduce to

$$
\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}
=\mathcal H\left(\delta^\alpha_\beta\delta^0_\gamma+\delta^\alpha_\gamma\delta^0_\beta-\eta_{\beta\gamma}\eta^{\alpha0}\right).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^0{}_{00}=\mathcal H,\quad\Gamma^0{}_{0i}=0,\quad\Gamma^0{}_{ij}=\mathcal H\delta_{ij},\quad
\Gamma^k{}_{00}=0,\quad\Gamma^k{}_{0i}=\mathcal H\delta^k_i,\quad\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=0.}
$$

For the stated [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md) scale factor, $\mathcal H=-1/(c\eta)$. The factor $c^{-1}$ is required because the prime is not differentiation with respect to $\eta$ itself.
