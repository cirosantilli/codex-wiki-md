<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Einstein tensor](../../../../einstein-tensor.md) is $G^\mu{}_\nu=R^\mu{}_\nu-\tfrac12\delta^\mu_\nu R$. Thus $G^0{}_0=-R_{00}-R/2$, where the time raising introduces the minus sign. Substitution cancels the $\Phi$ and time-derivative terms in that component and gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
G^0{}_0&=-\frac{2\nabla^2\Psi}{c^2}=-\frac{8\pi G\rho}{c^2},\\
G^i{}_j&=\frac{2\partial_0^2\Psi\delta^i_j+\nabla^2(\Phi-\Psi)\delta^i_j+\partial^i\partial_j(\Psi-\Phi)}{c^2}=0.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The right sides follow from the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md) $G^\mu{}_\nu=(8\pi G/c^4)T^\mu{}_\nu$ with zero [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md). The full equations also contain the momentum constraint $G^0{}_i=-2\partial_0\partial_i\Psi/c^2=0$ for dust at rest to this order. Specifying arbitrary time-dependent potentials without that constraint would not give a solution of all the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md).
