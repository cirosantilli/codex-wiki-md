<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [metric signature](../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, $x^0=ct$, and retain terms linear in $\Phi/c^2$ or $\Psi/c^2$. The [inverse metric](../../../../inverse-metric.md) is $g^{00}=-(1-2\Phi/c^2)$ and $g^{ij}=(1+2\Psi/c^2)\delta^{ij}$. In the [Christoffel symbol](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) formula

$$
\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}=\frac12g^{\alpha\delta}(\partial_\beta g_{\delta\gamma}+\partial_\gamma g_{\delta\beta}-\partial_\delta g_{\beta\gamma}),
$$

the differentiated [metric perturbation](../../../../linearized-gravity-split.md) is already first order, so the prefactor can be replaced by the flat [inverse metric](../../../../inverse-metric.md). For example $\Gamma^0{}_{ij}=\tfrac12\partial_0g_{ij}=-\delta_{ij}\partial_0\Psi/c^2$ and $\Gamma^k{}_{00}=-\tfrac12\partial_kg_{00}=\partial_k\Phi/c^2$. Evaluating the remaining components gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Gamma^0{}_{00}&=\frac{\partial_0\Phi}{c^2},&
\Gamma^0{}_{0i}&=\frac{\partial_i\Phi}{c^2},&
\Gamma^0{}_{ij}&=-\frac{\partial_0\Psi}{c^2}\delta_{ij},\\
\Gamma^k{}_{00}&=\frac{\partial_k\Phi}{c^2},&
\Gamma^k{}_{0i}&=-\frac{\partial_0\Psi}{c^2}\delta^k_i,&
\Gamma^k{}_{ij}&=-\frac{\partial_i\Psi\delta^k_j+\partial_j\Psi\delta^k_i-\partial^k\Psi\delta_{ij}}{c^2}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The lower-index symmetry follows from the [torsion-free connection](../../../../torsion-free-connection.md). Products of these first-order [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are beyond the retained [weak-field approximation](../../../../weak-field-approximation.md).
