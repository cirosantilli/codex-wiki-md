<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [pressureless relativistic fluid](../../../../relativistic-dust.md) has [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md) $T^{\mu\nu}=\rho u^\mu u^\nu$, with [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) normalized by $u^\mu u_\mu=-c^2$. In its [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md), $u^i=0$, so $\boxed{T^0{}_0=-\rho c^2,\ T^i{}_j=0}$. Here $\rho$ is rest-frame [mass density](../../../../density.md), or [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) divided by $c^2$.

For the [linearized Ricci tensor and scalar](../../../../linearized-ricci-tensor-and-scalar.md), discard the quadratic connection terms. The contracted connection is $\Gamma^\mu{}_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\nu(\Phi-3\Psi)/c^2$. Differentiation then gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
R_{00}&=\frac{\nabla^2\Phi+3\partial_0^2\Psi}{c^2},\\
R_{0i}&=\frac{2\partial_0\partial_i\Psi}{c^2},\\
R_{ij}&=\frac{\partial_i\partial_j(\Psi-\Phi)+\delta_{ij}(\nabla^2\Psi-\partial_0^2\Psi)}{c^2},\\
R&=\frac{4\nabla^2\Psi-2\nabla^2\Phi-6\partial_0^2\Psi}{c^2}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [Ricci scalar](../../../../ricci-scalar.md) uses the zeroth-order [inverse metric](../../../../inverse-metric.md), $R=-R_{00}+\delta^{ij}R_{ij}$, because multiplying first-order [curvature](../../../../curvature.md) by another [metric perturbation](../../../../linearized-gravity-split.md) would again be second order. These are the [linearized curvature of a two-potential weak-field metric](../../../../linearized-curvature-of-a-two-potential-weak-field-metric.md) in the paper's [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) sign convention.
