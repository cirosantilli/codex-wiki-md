<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The additional invariant is the squared [norm](../../../../norm.md) of the affine tangent. [Metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md) and the [geodesic equation](../../../../geodesic-equation.md) make it constant, and a photon has the [null condition](../../../../null-condition.md)

$$
g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu=0.
$$

On the equatorial plane this gives

$$
0=-fc^2\dot t^2+f^{-1}\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2,
$$

and the [conserved quantities](../../../../conserved-quantity.md) of part (a) yield

$$
\boxed{
\dot r^2=c^2\varepsilon^2-\frac{\ell^2}{r^2}\left(1-\frac{2m}{r}\right).
}
$$

For a nonradial ray $\ell\ne0$, put $u=1/r$. Since

$$
\frac{du}{d\phi}
=\frac{-\dot r/r^2}{\ell/r^2}
=-\frac{\dot r}{\ell},
$$

the orbit equation becomes

$$
\boxed{
\left(\frac{du}{d\phi}\right)^2+u^2(1-2mu)
=C,\qquad C=\frac{c^2\varepsilon^2}{\ell^2}=\frac1{b^2}.
}
$$

Here $b=|\ell|/(c\varepsilon)$ is the positive [impact parameter](../../../../impact-parameter.md) for a future-directed ray arriving from infinity. A strictly radial ray has $\ell=0$ and must be described by the radial equation rather than by $\phi$ as parameter.
