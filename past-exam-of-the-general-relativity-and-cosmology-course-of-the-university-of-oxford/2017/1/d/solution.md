<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For nonrelativistic particles the [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) is dominated by rest energy, while their isotropic kinetic [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is approximately $p_m=n m\langle v^2\rangle/3$. Therefore

$$
w_m=\frac{p_m}{\rho_mc^2}
\simeq\frac{\langle v^2\rangle}{3c^2}\ll1,
\qquad
\boxed{w_m\simeq0.}
$$

The matter [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is negligible in this approximation, including in the gravitational combination $\rho_m+3p_m/c^2$. For isotropic [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), $E=pc$ implies one third of the [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) in each diagonal spatial stress:

$$
p_r=\frac13\rho_rc^2,\qquad \boxed{w_r=\frac13.}
$$

Because the two fluids do not exchange energy or momentum, they are [separately conserved cosmological fluids](../../../../separately-conserved-cosmological-fluids.md): $\nabla_\alpha T_i^{\alpha\beta}=0$ for each, rather than merely conservation of their sum. In a comoving volume proportional to $a^3$, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives

$$
d(\rho_i c^2a^3)=-p_i\,d(a^3).
$$

Equivalently this follows by projecting the individual [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) equation along the common comoving [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md). Differentiating and dividing by $\rho_i c^2a^3$ yields

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\dot\rho_i}{\rho_i}
+3(1+w_i)\frac{\dot a}{a}=0.
}
$$

For constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w_i$,

$$
\rho_i\propto a^{-3(1+w_i)},\qquad
\boxed{\rho_m\propto a^{-3},\quad \rho_r\propto a^{-4}.}
$$

The extra power for [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) represents the [cosmological redshift](../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) of photon energies in addition to number dilution. Differentiating the total [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives only the total continuity equation; the separate equations also use the stated absence of interactions between components.
