# Local extremum

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum and minimum](maximum-and-minimum.md)

A local extremum of a real-valued [function](function-split.md) is either a [local maximum](local-maximum.md) or a [local minimum](local-minimum.md), with comparisons restricted to a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) in its domain. A differentiable interior local extremum is a [stationary point](stationary-point.md), but the converse can fail, as $f(x)=x^3$ at zero shows.
