<h1 id="preconditioned-crank-nicolson-algorithm">Preconditioned Crank–Nicolson algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian autoregressive proposal reversible with respect to a standard normal distribution](gaussian-autoregressive-proposal-reversible-with-respect-to-a-standard-normal-distribution.md)

The preconditioned Crank–Nicolson algorithm, or pCN algorithm, uses a [Gaussian autoregressive proposal reversible with respect to a standard normal distribution](gaussian-autoregressive-proposal-reversible-with-respect-to-a-standard-normal-distribution.md) in a [Metropolis–Hastings algorithm](metropolis-hastings-algorithm.md). When the target density is a likelihood times the proposal's invariant Gaussian prior density, the Gaussian factors cancel from the [Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability](metropolis-hastings-acceptance-probability.md), leaving the likelihood ratio.
