# Conway polynomial (knot theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

The Conway polynomial is normalized by $\nabla_U=1$ for the [unknot](unknot.md), zero for a split [unlink](unlink.md) with more than one component, and the [skein relation](skein-relation.md) $\nabla_{L_+}-\nabla_{L_-}=z\nabla_{L_0}$. For a [knot](knot.md), the symmetric [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md) with value one at $t=1$ is $\Delta_K(t)=\nabla_K(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})$. This fixes the Laurent-unit ambiguity of the [Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module](seifert-matrix-presentation-of-the-alexander-module.md).
