# Lie product formula

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_product_formula)

For bounded [matrices](matrix.md) $A_j$, the Lie product formula gives [operator norm](operator-norm.md) convergence

$$
e^{t\sum_jA_j}=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left(\prod_je^{tA_j/k}\right)^k.
$$

For two summands, expanding their [matrix exponentials](matrix-exponential.md) gives the one-step difference below. For [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) multiplied by $-i$, the factors are [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) and the [first-order unitary product-formula error bound](first-order-unitary-product-formula-error-bound.md) gives an explicit error estimate.
