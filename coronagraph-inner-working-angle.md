# Coronagraph inner working angle

↑ **Parent:** [Coronagraph](coronagraph.md)

The [coronagraph inner working angle](coronagraph-inner-working-angle.md) describes how close to the blocked source a specified [coronagraph](coronagraph.md) can usefully transmit or detect a faint neighbor. It requires a criterion, such as a chosen fraction of the far off-axis [coronagraph throughput](coronagraph-throughput.md), and is often expressed in units of $\lambda/D$. It is not automatically the [coronagraphic focal plane mask](coronagraphic-focal-plane-mask.md) [radius](radius.md) or a guarantee of detection. Small separations make useful source transmission and accurate control of [wavefront errors](wavefront-error.md) particularly demanding. [Olivier Guyon, Low order aberrations and high performance coronagraphy](https://subarutelescope.org/staff/guyon/04research.web/01adaptiveoptics.web/lowfs.web/content_nomenu.html).
