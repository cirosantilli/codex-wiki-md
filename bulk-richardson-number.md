# Bulk Richardson number

↑ **Parent:** [Richardson number](richardson-number.md)

A [bulk Richardson number](bulk-richardson-number.md) compares a characteristic buoyancy difference across a layer with the squared characteristic flow speed. It uses large-scale differences rather than the pointwise [gradient Richardson number](gradient-richardson-number.md). Its normalization depends on the chosen velocity, density and length scales.
