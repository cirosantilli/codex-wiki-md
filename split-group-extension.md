# Split group extension

↑ **Parent:** [Group extension](group-extension.md)

A [group extension](group-extension.md) is split when its quotient homomorphism admits a group-homomorphic section. Equivalently, the middle group is a [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) of the kernel by the quotient.
