# Degree-two even elliptic function

↑ **Parent:** [Even elliptic functions are rational in the Weierstrass function](even-elliptic-functions-are-rational-in-the-weierstrass-function.md)

An even [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) descends through the degree-two quotient map $\wp$ to a [rational function](rational-function.md) $R$ on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). If the original function also has degree two, composition of map degrees gives $2=2\deg R$, so $R$ has degree one. Consequently it is a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) with $ad-bc\ne0$. The shift $\operatorname{sn}(z+K,k)$ is even and has degree two, providing an example for the common [period lattice](period-lattice.md) of the [Jacobi elliptic sine](jacobi-elliptic-sine.md) and $\wp$.
