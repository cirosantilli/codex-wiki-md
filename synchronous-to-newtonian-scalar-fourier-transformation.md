# Synchronous-to-Newtonian scalar Fourier transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory](gauge-transformation-in-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md)

For a nonzero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) in [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md), write $h_{ij}=h\delta_{ij}/3+h_s(\widehat k_i\widehat k_j-\delta_{ij}/3)$. Under the passive displacement $\xi^\mu=(T,i\widehat k_iL)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$, cancellation of spatial scalar shear requires $h_s+2kL=0$, and cancellation of the shift requires $kT-L'=0$. The resulting [cosmological Newtonian potential](cosmological-newtonian-potential.md) is $\Phi=(h_s''+\mathcal Hh_s')/(2k^2)$ and the spatial potential is $\Psi=-(h-h_s)/6-\mathcal Hh_s'/(2k^2)$. Both remain unchanged under [residual synchronous-gauge freedom](residual-synchronous-gauge-freedom.md), for which $T'+\mathcal HT=0$ and $L'=kT$.
