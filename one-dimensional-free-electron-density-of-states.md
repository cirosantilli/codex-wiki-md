# One-dimensional free-electron density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

For a length $L$ and parabolic free-electron [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$, the displayed total [density of states](density-of-states.md) includes both signs of $k$ and both [spin](spin.md) states. Per spin it is half as large. Its $E^{-1/2}$ dependence follows by transforming the constant signed-$k$ density $L/(2\pi)$ to [energy](energy-split.md) and including both momenta. The continuum singularity at the band bottom is integrable; a finite specimen still has discrete levels.
