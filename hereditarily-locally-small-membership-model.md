# Hereditarily locally small membership model

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

For a strong-limit cardinal $\lambda$ of [countable](countable-set.md) [cofinality](cofinality.md), define

$$
B_\lambda=\{x:\forall y\in\operatorname{TC}(\{x\})\ (|y|<\lambda)\}.
$$

Here smallness bounds every individual membership extension; it does not bound the [cardinality](cardinality.md) of the entire [transitive closure](transitive-closure.md). The [transitive class](transitive-class.md) $B_\lambda$ satisfies all [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) axioms except possibly the [Axiom of union](axiom-of-union.md): functional images have fewer than $\lambda$ elements, and the [power set](power-set.md) of a [set](set-split.md) of size $\mu<\lambda$ has size $2^\mu<\lambda$. These facts give [Axiom schema of replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) and [Axiom of power set](axiom-of-power-set.md); pairs and definable [subsets](subset.md) also remain locally small. For $\lambda=\beth_\omega$, the [countable](countable-set.md) [set](set-split.md) $\{V_{\omega+n}:n<\omega\}$ belongs to $B_\lambda$, but its [union](set-union.md) $V_{\omega+\omega}$ has size $\lambda$ and does not. This gives an explicit relative-independence model for the [Axiom of union](axiom-of-union.md).
