# Prime representation via an ideal class

↑ **Parent:** [Binary quadratic form](binary-quadratic-form.md)

Let $I$ be an [integral ideal](integral-ideal.md) of an imaginary quadratic [number field](number-field.md) and $(e_1,e_2)$ an [integral basis](integral-basis.md) of $I$. Its normalized norm is a positive definite [binary quadratic form](binary-quadratic-form.md). An unramified rational prime $p$ is represented by this form exactly when there is an integral [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P$ of norm $p$ with $[P]=[I]^{-1}$. Indeed, a representation gives an element $\alpha\in I$ with $N\alpha=pN(I)$, so $P=(\alpha)I^{-1}$ is integral of norm $p$. Conversely, $IP=(\alpha)$ with $\alpha\in I$ produces a representation by expressing $\alpha$ in the basis. [Artin reciprocity](artin-reciprocity-law.md) then detects this [ideal class](ideal-class.md) by Frobenius in the [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md).
