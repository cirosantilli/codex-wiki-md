# Polar decomposition in continuum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

For an orientation-preserving invertible [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md), the [polar decomposition of an invertible real matrix](polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-real-matrix.md) separates a rotation $R$ from the [right stretch tensor](right-stretch-tensor.md) $U$ or the [left stretch tensor](left-stretch-tensor.md) $V$. The rotation is shared by the two factorizations, with $V=RUR^T$.
