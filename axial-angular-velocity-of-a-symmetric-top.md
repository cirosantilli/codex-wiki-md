# Axial angular velocity of a symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric top](symmetric-top.md)

For a [symmetric top](symmetric-top.md) with axial [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf e$ and [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega$, the classical axial spin is the scalar $n=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf e$. The identity $\dot{\mathbf e}=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e$ implies $\boldsymbol\omega=\mathbf e\times\dot{\mathbf e}+n\mathbf e$. If the transverse and axial [principal moments of inertia](principal-moment-of-inertia.md) are $A$ and $C$, then the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) about the fixed vertex is $\mathbf L=A\mathbf e\times\dot{\mathbf e}+Cn\mathbf e$. A [torque](torque.md) perpendicular to $\mathbf e$ conserves $n$. During steady [precession](precession.md) at rate $\Omega$ and inclination $\alpha$, the general [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) is $\Omega\mathbf k+(n-\Omega\cos\alpha)\mathbf e$; the [precession](precession.md) rate by itself does not fix $n$.
