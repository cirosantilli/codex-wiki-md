# TE wave equation in a stratified magnetodielectric

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse electric polarization](transverse-electric-polarization.md)

With permittivity and permeability depending only on $z$ and time dependence $e^{i\omega t}$, a [transverse electric polarization](transverse-electric-polarization.md) $\mathbf E=\widehat{\mathbf y}E_y(x,z)$ obeys the displayed equation. [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-equations.md) give $H_x=E_{y,z}/(i\omega\mu)$ and $H_z=-E_{y,x}/(i\omega\mu)$. The latter satisfies $H_{z,xx}+H_{z,zz}+(\mu'/\mu)H_{z,z}+[\omega^2\mu\varepsilon+(\log\mu)'']H_z=0$. At normal incidence $H_z=0$; the nonzero magnetic component is $H_x$.
