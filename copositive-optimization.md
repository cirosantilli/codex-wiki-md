# Copositive optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Conic optimization](conic-optimization.md)

[Conic optimization](conic-optimization.md) using the [copositive cone](copositive-cone.md). The constraint $Q-\lambda I\in\operatorname{COP}_n$ supplies an exact reformulation of a [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) minimum on the [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md). Exact conic formulation does not itself provide an efficient membership algorithm.
