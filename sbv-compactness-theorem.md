# SBV compactness theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Special bounded-variation space](special-bounded-variation-space.md)

For uniformly bounded values, an $L^p$ [gradient](gradient.md) bound with $p>1$, and bounded $(n-1)$-dimensional jump [measure](measure.md), a sequence in the [SBV space](special-bounded-variation-space.md) admits an $L^1$-convergent subsequence whose limit remains special. [Gradients](gradient.md) converge weakly in $L^p$, and jump [measure](measure.md) is [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md). These hypotheses prevent diffuse singular [derivatives](derivative.md) from replacing the controlled interfaces in a minimizing limit.
