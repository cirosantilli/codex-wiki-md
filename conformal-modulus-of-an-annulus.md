# Conformal modulus of an annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal length](extremal-length.md)

The height divided by circumference in a conformal Euclidean cylinder model of an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md). For $r<|z|<R$, it is $(2\pi)^{-1}\log(R/r)$. The [extremal length](extremal-length.md) of winding-one core curves is $1/M$, whereas that of curves joining the two boundary components is $M$.
