# Linear-nonlinear-Poisson cascade model

↑ **Parent:** [Computational neuroscience](computational-neuroscience.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear-nonlinear-Poisson_cascade_model)

A stimulus passes through a linear temporal or spatial filter, then a nonnegative nonlinear rate function. A conditionally [Inhomogeneous Poisson process](inhomogeneous-poisson-process.md) generates spikes at that rate. The filter and nonlinearity can be fitted from data and tested on held-out trials. Refractoriness and spike-history correlations require extensions beyond this basic cascade.
