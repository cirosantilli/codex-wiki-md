# Annealing ratios for a matching generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Matching generating function](matching-generating-function.md)

For $0<a\leq b$, expand the [expectation](expected-value.md) under the [weighted matching Markov chain](weighted-matching-markov-chain.md) stationary weights to obtain the displayed identity. If the largest [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) has $n$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and $b/a\leq1+1/n$, the observable lies in $[1,e]$. Its sample mean has controlled error by the [Hoeffding inequality](hoeffding-inequality.md). A product of $J$ ratios, each estimated to relative accuracy $O(\varepsilon/J)$, approximates the full ratio to relative accuracy $O(\varepsilon)$.
