# Discrete subsets of the real line are countable

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete subset](discrete-subset.md)

Enumerate all intervals with rational endpoints, using the countability of the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) and listing pairs of enumeration indices by increasing sum. If every point $b$ of $B$ is an [isolated point](isolated-point.md), some such interval contains $b$ and no other point of $B$. Assign to $b$ the least index of such an interval. Distinct points cannot have the same index, so this gives an [injective function](injective-function.md) into $\mathbb N$ and proves that $B$ is a [countable set](countable-set.md). The argument uses isolation in the [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md), not closedness: the [discrete subset](discrete-subset.md) $\{1/n:n\ge1\}$ illustrates that an accumulation point outside $B$ is allowed.
