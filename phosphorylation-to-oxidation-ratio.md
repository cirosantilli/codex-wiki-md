# Phosphorylation-to-oxidation ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md)

The amount of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formed per oxygen atom reduced during coupled respiration. Oxygen molecules contain two atoms, so the ATP:$\mathrm O_2$ ratio is twice the conventional [phosphorylation-to-oxidation ratio](phosphorylation-to-oxidation-ratio.md) for the same measurements.
