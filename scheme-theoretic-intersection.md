# Scheme-theoretic intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber product of schemes](fiber-product-of-schemes.md)

For two [closed subschemes](closed-subscheme.md) of a [scheme](scheme.md) $X$, their intersection is their [fiber product of schemes](fiber-product-of-schemes.md) over $X$. On an affine chart, ideals $I,J$ give the quotient by $I+J$. This retains nonreduced structure and [intersection multiplicities](intersection-multiplicity.md) which a set-theoretic intersection would lose.
