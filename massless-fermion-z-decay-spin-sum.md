# Massless fermion Z decay spin sum

↑ **Parent:** [Massless leptonic Z decay width](massless-leptonic-z-decay-width.md)

For the [Z boson](z-boson.md) vertex $g\gamma^\mu(c_v-c_a\gamma^5)/(2c_W)$, sum over the three initial polarizations and final [fermion](fermion.md) spins. The symmetric [Dirac trace](gamma-matrix-trace.md) is $4(c_v^2+c_a^2)(k_1^\mu k_2^\nu+k_1^\nu k_2^\mu-g^{\mu\nu}k_1\cdot k_2)$. The vector-axial interference is antisymmetric and drops out of the [massive vector polarization sum](polarization-sum-for-a-massive-vector-boson.md). The longitudinal term drops out by current conservation for massless final particles. Contracting the remaining trace gives the displayed result. The [massive-vector spin average](massive-vector-spin-average.md) divides it by three; [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) then gives $\Gamma=g^2M_Z(c_v^2+c_a^2)/(48\pi c_W^2)$ per color. These coefficients are twice those used with a vertex prefactor $g/c_W$.
