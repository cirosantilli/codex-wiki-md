# Compositional convection

↑ **Parent:** [Convection](convection.md)

Compositional convection is buoyancy-driven motion caused by composition-dependent density variations. In a planetary core, freezing can expel light elements into the surrounding liquid, making that liquid buoyant and supplying mechanical power for a [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md). It can operate alongside thermally driven [convection](convection.md) and release of [latent heat](latent-heat.md).
