# Adversarial search

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

Exploration of a [game tree](game-tree.md) containing choices by competing decision makers. A maximizing node chooses the largest successor value; a minimizing opponent chooses the smallest. Known random outcomes instead require chance nodes and [expected values](expected-value-split.md), as in [expectimax](expectimax.md). A [game tree](game-tree.md) representation can combine controlled choices and chance without assuming that chance maliciously chooses its worst outcome.
