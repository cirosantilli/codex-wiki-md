# Nash bargaining problem

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

The essential two-person bargaining domain consists of a [compact convex set](compact-convex-set.md) of feasible utility vectors $F$, a [disagreement point](disagreement-point.md) $d\in F$, and at least one feasible vector strictly exceeding $d$ coordinatewise. The [Nash bargaining solution](nash-bargaining-solution.md) selects a jointly feasible improvement using a rule characterized by [Pareto efficiency](pareto-efficiency.md), [bargaining symmetry](bargaining-symmetry.md), [positive affine invariance in bargaining](positive-affine-invariance-in-bargaining.md) and [bargaining independence of irrelevant alternatives](bargaining-independence-of-irrelevant-alternatives.md).
