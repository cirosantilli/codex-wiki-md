# Odd-odd nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

An [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) with odd [proton](proton.md) number and odd [neutron](neutron-split.md) number. Both unpaired species can contribute to the total [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md); [addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives possible couplings, while the [nuclear residual interaction](nuclear-residual-interaction.md) determines their ordering.
