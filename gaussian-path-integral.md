# Gaussian path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

A quadratic [path integral](path-integral.md) becomes a product of [Gaussian integrals](gaussian-integral.md) after diagonalizing its fluctuation operator. One real coordinate per mode gives an inverse square root of the [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md); one complex coordinate, or two real coordinates, gives its inverse. A regulator and normalization are essential: [determinant](determinant.md) ratios are often meaningful before an absolute [determinant](determinant.md) is defined.
