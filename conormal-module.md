# Conormal module

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

For an [ideal](ideal.md) $I\subseteq R$, its conormal module is $I/I^2$, naturally an $R/I$-[module](module-mathematics.md). It records generators and relations to first order along the quotient $R/I$. If $I$ is the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of a [local ring](local-ring.md), it is the [cotangent space of a local ring](cotangent-space-of-a-local-ring.md) over the [residue field](residue-field.md). For the augmentation ideal $(X_1-1,\ldots,X_r-1)$ of $A[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_r^{\pm1}]$, reduction modulo $I^2$ gives $I/I^2\cong A^r$, with basis the classes of $X_i-1$.
