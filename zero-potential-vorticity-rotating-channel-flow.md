# Zero-potential-vorticity rotating channel flow

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md)

For a steady, slowly varying rotating channel, negligible transverse velocity and zero [shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md) give $u_y=f$. Cross-channel [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md) gives $gh_y=-fu$, producing the displayed profiles. The specific energy $E=h+u^2/(2g)$ is constant across the channel, and $E+H$ is constant along it. Zero potential vorticity is not the value in an ordinary finite-depth reservoir at rest in the rotating frame; it requires vanishing absolute vorticity or a deep-reservoir approximation.
