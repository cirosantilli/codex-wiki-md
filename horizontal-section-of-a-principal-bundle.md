# Horizontal section of a principal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal distribution of a principal connection](horizontal-distribution-of-a-principal-connection.md)

A local section $s:U\to P$ is horizontal when $ds(TU)\subseteq H$, equivalently when $s^*\mathcal A=0$. A [flat principal connection](flat-principal-connection.md) has horizontal sections locally, while its [holonomy](holonomy.md) can obstruct a global horizontal section.
