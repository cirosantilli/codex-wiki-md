# Coincident-point vacuum field variance

↑ **Parent:** [Mode expansion of a free field](mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md)

For a free field in the annihilation-operator vacuum, the coincident two-point function is its mode-power integral. Unsmeared vacuum products generally have an ultraviolet divergence. A finite band or spatial smearing defines a regulated variance without altering the meaning of a mode-by-mode [dimensionless power spectrum](dimensionless-cosmological-power-spectrum.md).
