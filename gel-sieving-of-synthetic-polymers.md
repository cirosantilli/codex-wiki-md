# Gel sieving of synthetic polymers

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md)

A porous gel changes the [electrophoretic mobility](electrophoretic-mobility.md) of charged synthetic [polymers](polymer-split.md) through confinement, deformation and pore-to-pore dynamics. Free-draining charge and friction can both grow proportionally to chain [length](length.md), leaving free-solution [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md) nearly [length](length.md) independent; matrix obstruction adds size sensitivity. [reptation](reptation.md) supplies one weak-field long-chain regime, not a universal calibration. Matrix [concentration](concentration.md), effective charge, [solvent](solvent.md) conditions, conformation, [topology](topology-split.md), [adsorption](adsorption.md), electro-osmotic drift and heating can all alter migration. Size standards should match the relevant chemistry and operating regime.
