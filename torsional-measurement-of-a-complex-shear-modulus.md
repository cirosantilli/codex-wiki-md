# Torsional measurement of a complex shear modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md)

A thin uniform circular rod of radius $r$ and length $\ell$ undergoing a small end twist of complex amplitude $\Theta$ has shear-strain amplitude $\gamma(\rho)=\rho\Theta/\ell$. A [complex viscoelastic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md) $G^*(\omega)$ gives shear-stress amplitude $G^*\rho\Theta/\ell$. Integrating the restoring [torque](torque.md) gives

$$
\mathcal T=-\frac{G^*\Theta}{\ell}\int_A\rho^2\,dA=-\frac{\pi r^4G^*}{2\ell}\Theta.
$$

The area integral is the polar second moment of the cross-section, not a mass [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). For a disc of moment of inertia $I$ and negligible rod inertia, harmonic angular motion satisfies $[-I\omega^2+K^*(\omega)]\Theta=0$ in a free-mode calculation, or equals the external torque amplitude in a driven measurement. The complex stiffness is a frequency-domain constitutive representation; a general real-time response has memory and cannot be described by putting an arbitrary frequency-dependent complex number into an instantaneous real equation.
