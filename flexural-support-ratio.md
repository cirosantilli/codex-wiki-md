# Flexural support ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md)

In the linear flat-[elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) model, let downward deflection be $w$, applied downward [pressure](pressure-split.md) be $q$, [flexural rigidity](bending-stiffness.md) be $D$, and the restoring [geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) be $\Delta\rho>0$. The static balance is

$$
D\nabla^4w+\Delta\rho g w=q.
$$

For a sinusoidal [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) of horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) magnitude $k$, applying the fourth spatial derivative multiplies its amplitude by $k^4$. Hence

$$
\widehat w=\frac{\widehat q}{Dk^4+\Delta\rho g},\qquad
\frac{\widehat w}{\widehat w_{\rm local}}=\frac{1}{1+\chi},\qquad
\chi=\frac{Dk^4}{\Delta\rho g}.
$$

The displayed dimensionless ratio compares bending resistance with buoyant restoration. Large $\chi$ means substantial elastic support and suppressed deflection. Small $\chi$ gives nearly local [isostasy](isostasy.md). The associated length $L_f=(D/\Delta\rho g)^{1/4}$ makes the load-width dependence explicit; some conventions define a flexural parameter with an additional factor $4^{1/4}$. At zero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), bending contributes nothing: total support remains buoyant even when the spatial distribution of subsidence changes.
