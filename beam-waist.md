# Beam waist

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)

The [beam waist](beam-waist.md) is the narrowest cross-section of a freely propagating [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md). Its [radius](radius.md) $w_0$ uses the [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $1/e$, or [light intensity](light-intensity.md) $1/e^2$, convention; its [diameter](diameter.md) is $2w_0$. Specifying a “spot size” without saying [radius](radius.md) or [diameter](diameter.md) can introduce a factor-of-two error. Within the scalar [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md), the [wavefront](wavefront.md) at the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) is planar. The minimum [radius](radius.md) sets both the [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) and the far-field [beam divergence](beam-divergence.md).
