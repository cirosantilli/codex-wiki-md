# Rectangular hyperbola

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbola](hyperbola.md)

A [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) whose two asymptotes are perpendicular. After a suitable choice of coordinates it has equation $xy=c$ for a nonzero constant $c$. In the form $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$, it is rectangular exactly when $a=b$.
