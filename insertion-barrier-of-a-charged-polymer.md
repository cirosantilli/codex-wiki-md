# Insertion barrier of a charged polymer

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

If only the inserted [length](length.md) $x$ feels a uniform inward [electric field](electric-field-split.md), its charge experiences [force](force-split.md) $qEx$, where $q$ is charge per contour [length](length.md). Integrating gives electric energy $-qEx^2/2$. Adding a constant entropic confinement [force](force-split.md) gives the displayed free-energy profile. Its interior maximum is at $x=f/(qE)$ and has height $f^2/(2qE)$; entry waiting and subsequent directed insertion are distinct times.
