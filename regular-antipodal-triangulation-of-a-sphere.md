# Regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

A [triangulation](triangulation.md) of $S^k$ is regular in this antipodal-combinatorics convention when the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) preserves its simplices and its nested coordinate equators $S^0\subset S^1\subset\cdots\subset S^{k-1}$ are subcomplexes. The two closed [hemispheres](hemisphere.md) at each stage are then triangulated balls. [Barycentric subdivision](barycentric-subdivision.md) preserves these conditions while making the [mesh of a simplicial complex](mesh-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) arbitrarily small. This convention is distinct from other meanings of regular triangulation in polyhedral geometry. [Cambridge extremal-combinatorics notes](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~par31/notes/extcomb.pdf) use this convention.
