# Symmetric Feller semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md)

A [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md) symmetric in the space associated with an invariant measure has a self-adjoint contraction realization in $L^2(\mu)$. Its two-point kernel measure $\mu(dx)P_t(x,dy)$ is symmetric. This reversibility expresses its joint energy as an integral of products of increments.
