# Pre-steady-state burst

↑ **Parent:** [Pre-steady-state kinetics](pre-steady-state-kinetics.md)

An initial rapid accumulation of product followed by slower turnover. It can reveal a fast first conversion followed by a slower recycling step; converting burst amplitude to active-enzyme concentration requires the appropriate mechanistic assumptions.
