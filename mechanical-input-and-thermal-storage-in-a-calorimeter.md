# Mechanical input and thermal storage in a calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [State function](state-function.md)

The UCSB demonstration uses an [aluminum](aluminium.md) cylinder turned by a crank, with a wrapped rope supporting a hanging [mass](mass.md). An embedded electrical [thermometer](thermometer.md) connects through sliding contacts to an [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) meter; a supplied [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)–[temperature](temperature-split.md) table or fitted equation converts the readout. A counter records turns. Record the initial [temperature](temperature-split.md), crank steadily, then stop and convert the minimum recorded [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) to the final [temperature](temperature-split.md).[UCSB Physics Lecture Demonstrations; UCSB demonstration 52.33, opening apparatus paragraphs and “Operating the demonstration”: cylinder, rope, turn counter and resistance calibration](ucsb-physics-lecture-demonstrations/52-33-mechanical-equivalent-of-heat.md)

The proposed control is to start below room [temperature](temperature-split.md) and finish comparably above it, approximately balancing incoming and outgoing environmental [heat](heat-split.md). Neglected accessory [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) and rough [temperature](temperature-split.md) estimates limit accuracy. The mechanical input and thermal response test an [energy](energy-split.md) balance; warming alone does not establish quantitative equivalence.[UCSB Physics Lecture Demonstrations; UCSB demonstration 52.33, final operating paragraphs: symmetric temperature preparation, environmental heat exchange and omitted apparatus capacities](ucsb-physics-lecture-demonstrations/52-33-mechanical-equivalent-of-heat.md)

This is a modern companion to [Joule's paddle-wheel experiment](joule-s-paddle-wheel-experiment.md), with a different apparatus. Choose the cylinder as the [closed system](closed-system.md). When endpoint bulk mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) changes are negligible, a model for its thermal storage is

$$
\Delta U\simeq C_{\rm eff}(T_f-T_i)=W_{\rm on}+Q_{\rm room},\qquad W_{\rm by}=-W_{\rm on},
$$

where $C_{\rm eff}$ is its participating [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), approximately constant over the run, and $Q_{\rm room}$ is signed environmental [heat](heat-split.md). The cylinder is not an [isolated system](isolated-system.md), since mechanical [work](work.md) enters. The [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise estimates a change in [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), rather than an amount of externally supplied [heat](heat-split.md). This boundary distinction connects a measured thermal response to the same stored quantity used in [two reversible ideal-gas paths between the same states](two-reversible-ideal-gas-paths-between-the-same-states.md).
