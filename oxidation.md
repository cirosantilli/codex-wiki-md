# Oxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

An [oxidation](oxidation.md) increases an [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md), recording loss from the formal [electron](electron.md) assignment. For $\mathrm{Zn}\to\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}+2e^-$, the increase is $0\to+2$. [Oxidation](oxidation.md) need not involve [oxygen](oxygen.md).[§4.2, “Oxidation-Reduction Reactions”: oxidation as increasing oxidation number](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

In the [electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md) examples here, [oxidation](oxidation.md) is loss of [electrons](electron.md) by a [chemical species](chemical-species.md); it occurs at the [anode](anode.md). That [electrode](electrode.md) definition holds in both a [galvanic cell](galvanic-cell.md) and an [electrolytic cell](electrolytic-cell.md); it does not fix terminal polarity. The oxidized [chemical species](chemical-species.md) serves as the [reducing agent](reducing-agent.md) in the coupled [redox](redox.md).
