# Logarithmic conical helix

↑ **Parent:** [Helix](helix.md)

The displayed [space curve](space-curve.md), $s>0$, lies on a cone and is parametrized by [arc length](arc-length.md). It has a constant tangent angle with its axis, while the turn spacing shrinks geometrically toward the limiting apex. Its [curvature](curvature.md) is $1/(\sqrt2s)$: differentiate its unit tangent with respect to $s$. Infinitely many turns accumulate at the apex with finite [arc length](arc-length.md); the apex itself is not a regular point of the curve.
