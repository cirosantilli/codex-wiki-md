# Poisson arrival time

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md)

The time of the $n$-th event of a [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md). At positive rate $\lambda$, the [Poisson interarrival times](poisson-interarrival-times.md) $T_j-T_{j-1}$, with $T_0=0$, are [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with the rate-$\lambda$ [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md). In particular, $T_n$ has [expected value](expected-value-split.md) $n/\lambda$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $n/\lambda^2$. The inverse-count identity is $\{T_n\leq t\}=\{N_t\geq n\}$.
