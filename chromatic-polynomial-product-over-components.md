# Chromatic polynomial product over components

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

For [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) $G_1,\ldots,G_t$ of a finite [graph](graph-split.md) $G$, choices of [proper vertex colourings](proper-vertex-coloring.md) on different [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) are independent. The [multiplication principle](rule-of-product.md) gives $p_G(x)=\prod_jp_{G_j}(x)$. Each nonempty factor is divisible by $x$, hence $x^t$ divides the [chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md). The vanishing constant term follows by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) from the [deletion-contraction recurrence for the chromatic polynomial](deletion-contraction-recurrence-for-the-chromatic-polynomial.md), starting with $p_H(x)=x^{|V(H)|}$ for a nonempty [graph](graph-split.md) without [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md).
