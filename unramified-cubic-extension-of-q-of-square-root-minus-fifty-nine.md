# Unramified cubic extension of Q of square root minus fifty-nine

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

The splitting field has [Galois group](galois-group.md) $S_3$ and quadratic subfield $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-59})$. The extension over that subfield is abelian of degree three and unramified everywhere. The only possible rational ramified prime is $59$; at that prime a simple residue root lifts, so the local splitting field is quadratic and coincides with the quadratic subfield's completion. It follows from the [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md) degree that $3\mid h_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-59})}$.
