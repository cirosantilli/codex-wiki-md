<h1 id="cartan-s-magic-formula">Cartan's magic formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a differential form](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan's_magic_formula)

The [Lie derivative of a differential form](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) satisfies $\mathcal L_X=d\iota_X+\iota_Xd$. Both sides agree on [functions](function-split.md). On a [differential 1-form](one-form.md) $\eta$, evaluation at a [vector field](vector-field.md) $Y$ gives $X(\eta(Y))-\eta([X,Y])$ on both sides, by the definition of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) and the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md). Both operators are degree-zero [derivations of an algebra](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) on the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md). Agreement on [functions](function-split.md) and coordinate [differential 1-forms](one-form.md) therefore proves agreement on every [differential form](differential-form-split.md). Since $d^2=0$, the formula also gives $\mathcal L_Xd=d\mathcal L_X$, so the [Lie derivative of a differential form](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) commutes with the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md).
