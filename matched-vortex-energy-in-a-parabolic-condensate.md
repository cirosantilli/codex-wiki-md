# Matched vortex energy in a parabolic condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

For a centered [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) in a [two-dimensional Thomas–Fermi condensate](two-dimensional-thomas-fermi-condensate.md), use an intermediate radius $\xi_{\rm core}\ll L\ll R$. The approximately uniform core contributes $\pi n_c\hbar^2\mathcal N^2[\log(L/\xi)+L_{0|\mathcal N|}]/m$. The outer [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md) is $\hbar\mathcal N/(mr)$, so its kinetic contribution is $(\pi\hbar^2\mathcal N^2/m)\int_L^R n_c(1-r^2/R^2)dr/r$. Integration cancels $L$ and produces the constant $-1/2$. The displayed result is the matched small-core approximation at fixed particle number, not a formula for arbitrarily large winding at fixed cloud size. The angular momentum is exactly $L_z=N\hbar\mathcal N$ for a normalized axisymmetric single-winding state.
