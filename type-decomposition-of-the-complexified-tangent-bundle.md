# Type decomposition of the complexified tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Almost complex manifold](almost-complex-manifold.md)

An [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md) $J$ gives the $+i$ and $-i$ [eigenbundles](eigenbundle.md) of its complex-linear extension. Their projections are $(I-iJ)/2$ and $(I+iJ)/2$. For an integrable structure the first is the [holomorphic tangent bundle](holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md).
