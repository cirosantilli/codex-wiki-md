# Elliptic paraboloid

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)

An elliptic paraboloid is a smooth bowl-shaped quadratic surface with elliptical horizontal sections. In the circular case $A=B$, it is a [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md). For $z=x^2+y^2$, polar coordinates give the parametrization $(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,r^2)$ and upward [vector area element](vector-area-element.md) $(-2r^2\cos\theta,-2r^2\sin\theta,r)\,dr\,d\theta$. A band between two positive heights has two boundary circles and no cap.
