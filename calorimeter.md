# Calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimetry](calorimetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calorimeter)

A calorimeter infers transferred [heat](heat.md) from a measured change, such as a [temperature](temperature.md) change and a calibrated [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). The [heat-engine calorimetry](heat-engine-calorimetry.md) example requires accounting for the vessel and [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) to the surroundings as well as the [water](water.md).

A practical receiver has an inner vessel, insulation or an outer shield, a lid, a [thermometer](thermometer.md) and a stirrer. Its participating [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) includes the immersed parts that follow the [water](water.md)'s [temperature](temperature.md). Insulation reduces [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) to the room; stirring makes a single reading more representative of the contents. These serve different purposes: a well-insulated vessel can still have substantial [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), and a uniform [temperature](temperature.md) does not imply zero external [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md).

The University of Toronto's [Heat Capacity (University of Toronto laboratory handout)](heat-capacity-university-of-toronto-laboratory-handout.md) describes a [copper](copper.md) inner vessel, outer shield, [thermometer](thermometer.md) and heated metal block. Weigh the block and receiving [water](water.md), heat the block in a bath, then transfer it quickly into enough [water](water.md) to cover it. The observed [temperature](temperature.md) rises, reaches a maximum, then falls. The inferred [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) comes from the block's cooling and the warming of both [water](water.md) and vessel. In its cooling exercise the handout deliberately exposes the inner vessel to room air, recommends readings every $15\,\mathrm s$, and uses the later common cooling to estimate the earlier loss. Starting below room [temperature](temperature.md) reduces exchange without guaranteeing cancellation. The handout notes limited time and thermometry for small corrections; it supplies no dataset.[pp. 1–3, The Method of Mixtures and Cooling Correction, Figure 1](heat-capacity-university-of-toronto-laboratory-handout.md)

For a proposed modern version, independently perform [thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md) and [calorimeter calibration](calorimeter-calibration.md), record bath and room [temperatures](temperature.md), and keep immersion depth, lid position and stirring rate fixed. Ensure the block actually reaches bath [temperature](temperature.md); minimize and account for adhering hot bath [water](water.md). Compare repeated transfers with different delays and a reference sample of known [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md). A resting run tests environmental [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md), while a stirred run with no added sample checks mechanical input. These are proposed controls, not claimed observations from the handout. Sensor delay, changing [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), incomplete [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) and material escaping the boundary limit the resulting estimate.
