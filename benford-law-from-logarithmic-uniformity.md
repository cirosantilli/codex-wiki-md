# Benford law from logarithmic uniformity

↑ **Parent:** [Benford law](benford-law.md)

The leading decimal digit is determined by the fractional part of $\log_{10}X$. A uniform fractional logarithm gives [Benford law](benford-law.md) by the lengths of the intervals $[\log_{10}i,\log_{10}(i+1))$. A [log-uniform distribution](log-uniform-distribution.md) whose logarithmic span is an integer has this property. Arbitrary partial-decade truncations generally do not.
