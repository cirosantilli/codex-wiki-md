# Birkhoff decomposition by support matchings

↑ **Parent:** [Birkhoff-von Neumann theorem](birkhoff-von-neumann-theorem.md)

For a [doubly stochastic matrix](doubly-stochastic-matrix.md), the [Hall marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) supplies a [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) in its positive-entry support. Subtract the smallest matched entry times the corresponding [permutation matrix](permutation-matrix.md). The residual has equal row and column sums and smaller support unless it vanishes. Repeating gives a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of [permutation matrices](permutation-matrix.md). With $r$ initially positive entries, there are at most $r-n+1$ terms, since a positive-mass residual has at least $n$ positive entries. Elementary augmenting-path matching gives $O(n^5)$ arithmetic operations.
