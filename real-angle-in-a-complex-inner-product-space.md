# Real angle in a complex inner product space

↑ **Parent:** [Angle between vectors](angle-between-vectors.md)

For nonzero [vectors](vector.md) in a complex [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), the displayed quotient defines the ordinary real [angle](angle.md) in the underlying real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), whose [inner product](inner-product-split.md) is $\operatorname{Re}\langle u,v\rangle$. The complex [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bounds it between $-1$ and $1$. This convention depends on the vectors' [phase factors](phase-factor.md). The different phase-invariant convention $|\langle u,v\rangle|/(\|u\|\|v\|)$ measures an angle between complex one-dimensional subspaces; the two conventions should not be interchanged.
