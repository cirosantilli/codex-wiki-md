# Vertical Jeans equation

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md)

For a steady axisymmetric [stellar population](stellar-population.md) with no mean vertical motion, the vertical [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md) balances the gradient of the vertical random-motion stress and the radial gradient of the tilted [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md) against the vertical [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). Neglecting the mixed moment gives $\partial_z(n\sigma_z^2)=-n\partial_z\Phi$. A tracer density $n$ need not equal the density sourcing the [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md).
