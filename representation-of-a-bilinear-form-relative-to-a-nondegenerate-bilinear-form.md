# Representation of a bilinear form relative to a nondegenerate bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md)

If $B_1$ is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) on a finite-dimensional vector space and $B_2$ is any bilinear form, there is a unique [linear map](linear-map.md) $\alpha$ such that

$$
B_2(v,w)=B_1(v,\alpha w).
$$

The right radical of $B_2$ is $\ker\alpha$.
