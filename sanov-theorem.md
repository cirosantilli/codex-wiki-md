# Sanov theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Method of types](method-of-types.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanov_theorem)

Sanov's theorem is the [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) for empirical distributions of independent identically distributed observations. On a finite alphabet with sampling law $Q$, its rate function is $D_e(R\Vert Q)$.
