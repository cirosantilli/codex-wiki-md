# Mafic rock

↑ **Parent:** [Rock (geology)](rock-geology.md)

A [rock](rock-geology.md) relatively rich in magnesium- and iron-bearing silicate [minerals](mineral.md). [Basalt](basalt.md) and [gabbro](gabbro.md) are common examples; their [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) depends particularly on magnetic-mineral abundance.
