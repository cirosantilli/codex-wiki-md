# Poisson-gamma mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md)

If $Y\mid\Lambda\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda)$ and $\Lambda$ has a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md), then $Y$ has a [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md). For gamma shape $\nu$ and scale $\alpha$,

$$
\mathbb EY=\alpha\nu,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\alpha\nu+\alpha^2\nu.
$$
