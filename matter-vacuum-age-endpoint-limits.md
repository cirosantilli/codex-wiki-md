# Matter-vacuum age endpoint limits

↑ **Parent:** [Age of a flat matter-Lambda universe](age-of-a-flat-matter-lambda-universe.md)

The [age of a flat matter-Lambda universe](age-of-a-flat-matter-lambda-universe.md) is $H_0t_0=2\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{(1-\Omega_m)/\Omega_m}/(3\sqrt{1-\Omega_m})$. The large-argument expansion $\operatorname{arsinh}z=\log(2z)+O(z^{-2})$ gives the first limit. Its small-argument expansion $\operatorname{arsinh}z=z+O(z^3)$ gives the second. The divergence at the pure-vacuum endpoint reflects the absence of a finite [Big Bang](big-bang.md) time in the expanding flat [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) patch.
