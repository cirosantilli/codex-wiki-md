# Dynkin formula for a diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion generator](diffusion-generator.md)

For a [diffusion process](markov-diffusion.md) with [diffusion generator](diffusion-generator.md) $L$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) makes $\varphi(X_t)-\varphi(X_0)-\int_0^tL\varphi(X_s)ds$ a local martingale. Under stopping and integrability conditions making it a true martingale, taking expectations gives the displayed identity. For [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), $L=\tfrac12\Delta$, recovering the usual Brownian formula.
