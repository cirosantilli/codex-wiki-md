# Gauge group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge group](gauge-group.md)

A gauge group representation specifies how a matter field transforms under a [gauge group](gauge-group.md). It fixes the gauge charges and whether gauge-invariant mass terms can pair a field with a field in the conjugate [group representation](group-representation.md).
