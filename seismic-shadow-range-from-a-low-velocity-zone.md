# Seismic shadow range from a low-velocity zone

↑ **Parent:** [Low-velocity zone](low-velocity-zone.md)

A seismic shadow range is an interval of receiver distances missing a specified family of geometrical rays. A [velocity](velocity.md) decrease can separate shallow-turning and deep-turning arrivals; [diffraction](diffraction.md) or other wave families can still deliver [energy](energy-split.md) there.
