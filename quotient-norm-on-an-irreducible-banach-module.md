# Quotient norm on an irreducible Banach module

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraically irreducible representation of a Banach algebra](algebraically-irreducible-representation-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

For a [unital](unital-algebra.md) irreducible action and $\xi\ne0$, the annihilator $L_\xi=\{a:\pi(a)\xi=0\}$ is a [maximal left ideal](maximal-left-ideal.md) and is [closed](closed-set.md). Thus $A/L_\xi$ supplies the displayed complete [norm](norm.md) on the module, with contractive action. Every [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md) is bounded for this quotient [norm](norm.md): its value at the cyclic [vector](vector.md) is $a\xi$, so it is induced by bounded right multiplication by $a$ on the quotient. The [Gelfand-Mazur theorem](gelfand-mazur-theorem.md) then makes the module's commutant consist of scalar operators. This constructs one compatible [norm](norm.md); it does not by itself establish [continuity](continuous-function.md) in a different preassigned [norm](norm.md).
