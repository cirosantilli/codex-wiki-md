# Quark field

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A [quark field](quark-field.md) is a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) or a chiral [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) carrying the colour [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) of $SU(3)$. Its three colour components transform as $\psi^i\mapsto U^i{}_j\psi^j$ under local [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md). The colour index is independent of the Lorentz spinor index, and the weak [representations](group-representation.md) of its left and right chiral components differ in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md).
