# Prandtl normal-mode equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized unsteady Prandtl equation](linearized-unsteady-prandtl-equation.md)

For a frozen real parallel profile and modes proportional to $e^{i\alpha(x-ct)}$, continuity eliminates tangential [velocity](velocity.md) and gives this third-order equation. Wall conditions are $v=v'=0$; outer tangential matching gives $v'\to0$, with bounded $v$. Real coefficients give the spectral symmetry $(\alpha,c)\mapsto(-\alpha,c^*)$. For positive real [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), growth means $\operatorname{Im}c>0$.
