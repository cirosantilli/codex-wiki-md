# Divisor class group of the three-dimensional affine quadric cone

↑ **Parent:** [Nagata theorem for divisor class groups](nagata-theorem-for-divisor-class-groups.md)

For the three-dimensional affine quadric cone

$$
X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y,z,w]/(xy-zw),
$$

the [divisor class group](divisor-class-group.md) is infinite cyclic. A generator is either ruling plane $V(x,z)$ or $V(x,w)$, and the [principal divisor](principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) of $x$ gives the relation $[V(x,z)]+[V(x,w)]=0$.
