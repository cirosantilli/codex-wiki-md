# Exponential density-dependent birth model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

If a population $N$ has per-capita birth rate $be^{-aN}$ and per-capita death rate $d$, then

$$
\dot N=N\left(be^{-aN}-d\right).
$$

When $b>d$, its positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md) is

$$
N^*=\frac1a\log\frac bd,
$$

and the derivative of the scalar vector field there is $-adN^*<0$, so the equilibrium is locally [asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md).
