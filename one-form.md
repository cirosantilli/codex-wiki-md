# One-form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-form)

A differential 1-form is a smooth section of the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md); it assigns a [covector](covector.md) to every point of a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md).
