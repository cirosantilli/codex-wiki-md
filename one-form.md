# One-form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-form)

A differential 1-form is a smooth section of the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md); it assigns a [covector](covector.md) to every point of a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md). In planar coordinates, $\alpha=P(x,y)\,dx+Q(x,y)\,dy$ evaluates a [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) $v$ as $P(x,y)v_x+Q(x,y)v_y$.

Along a [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$, its [line integral](line-integral.md) is

$$
\int_\gamma\alpha=\int_a^b\left[P(x(t),y(t))x'(t)+Q(x(t),y(t))y'(t)\right]dt.
$$

Reversing the direction reverses the integral. A [force](force.md) expressed as a [one-form](one-form.md) therefore accumulates [work](work.md) along a directed path. In Euclidean coordinates its components can be displayed as arrows, but identifying a [one-form](one-form.md) with a [vector field](vector-field.md) uses an [inner product](inner-product.md); the [covector field](one-form.md) itself does not require a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md).
