# Photon momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

In [vacuum](vacuum.md), a [photon](photon.md) of [wave vector](wavevector.md) $\mathbf k$ carries [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p=\hbar\mathbf k$ and [energy](energy-split.md) $E=\hbar c|\mathbf k|$. In a direct optical interband transition this [momentum](momentum-split.md) is generally small compared with relevant crystal [momenta](momentum-split.md), motivating the nearly vertical-transition approximation in [wave vector](wavevector.md) space. Electromagnetic momentum in matter requires additional conventions.
