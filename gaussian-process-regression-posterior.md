# Gaussian process regression posterior

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional multivariate normal distribution](conditional-multivariate-normal-distribution.md)

For a zero-mean [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) observed with independent [Gaussian noise](gaussian-noise.md), the latent value at a new input is conditionally normal. Its mean is the kernel cross-covariance times the noisy kernel-matrix inverse times the observations, and its variance is the prior variance minus the corresponding quadratic form.
