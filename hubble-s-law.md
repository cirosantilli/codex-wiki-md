<h1 id="hubble-s-law">Hubble's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubble's_law)

[Hubble's law](hubble-s-law.md) relates recession velocity and distance in homogeneous cosmic expansion. The associated [Hubble flow](hubble-flow.md) is the velocity field; peculiar velocities are deviations from that idealized expansion.
