# Gaussian log-response model

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic transformation](logarithmic-transformation.md)

For a positive response, the model $\log Y=\eta+\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$ gives conditional [median](median.md) $e^\eta$ and conditional mean $e^{\eta+\sigma^2/2}$. The latter follows from the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Additive [factor](regression-factor.md) effects in $\eta$ become multiplicative effects on these response summaries. Its constant log-scale [variance](variance-split.md) corresponds to original-scale [variance](variance-split.md) proportional to the square of the mean; direct exponentiation of fitted log means incurs [retransformation bias](retransformation-bias.md).
