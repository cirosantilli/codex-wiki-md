# Quantum asymmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum rotor](quantum-rotor.md)

The [quantum asymmetric top](quantum-asymmetric-top.md) has different rotational-energy coefficients on its principal axes. Its [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) commutes with $L^2$ but generally not with an individual component, so diagonalization proceeds within fixed total-angular-momentum multiplets. For a mechanical rigid rotor the positive coefficients are reciprocals of twice its principal [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). The component sign convention for body-fixed generators does not affect this squared-component [energy](energy-split.md) expression.
