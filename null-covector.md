# Null covector

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md)

A nonzero [covector](covector.md) $\ell$ is null when its squared dual [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md) is zero: $g^{\alpha\beta}\ell_\alpha\ell_\beta=0$. Raising its index gives a [null vector](null-vector.md). Null covectors describe normals to characteristic [light cones](light-cone.md) and are the test directions in the [classical null condition for wave equations](classical-null-condition-for-wave-equations.md).
