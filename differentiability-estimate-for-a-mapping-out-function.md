# Differentiability estimate for a mapping-out function

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md)

If a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) lies in a disc of radius $r$ centred at $\xi\in\mathbb R$, the displayed estimate holds for $|z-\xi|>2r$, with an absolute constant $C$. It refines the Laurent expansion uniformly in the hull and supplies the first-order increment needed to derive the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) from the [Loewner local growth property](loewner-local-growth-property.md).
