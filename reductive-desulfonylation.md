# Reductive desulfonylation

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction (chemistry)](reduction-chemistry.md)

Removal of a sulfone substituent by a reducing process, replacing its [carbon](carbon.md)–[sulfur](sulfur.md) connection with a [carbon](carbon.md)–[hydrogen](hydrogen.md) connection under an appropriate work-up. Compatibility with other reducible groups must be considered.
