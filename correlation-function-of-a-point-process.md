# Correlation function of a point process

↑ **Parent:** [Point process](point-process.md)

The $k$th correlation [function](function-split.md) is the density of the $k$th [factorial](factorial.md) moment measure: integrating a nonnegative test [function](function-split.md) against $R_k$ gives the [expectation](expected-value.md) of its sum over ordered $k$-tuples of distinct points. For exactly $m$ exchangeably labelled points with symmetric joint density $f_m$, it is $m!/(m-k)!$ times the marginal density of $k$ labels. It is not normalized to have total mass one.
