# Steady-state approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

An approximation setting the time derivative of a transient population or intermediate concentration to zero when production and loss balance on the timescale considered. It differs from [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md).
