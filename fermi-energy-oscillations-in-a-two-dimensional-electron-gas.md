# Fermi-energy oscillations in a two-dimensional electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Landau level](landau-level.md)

At fixed sheet density $n_s$, each resolved [Landau level](landau-level.md) gains degeneracy $eB/h$ per unit [area](surface-area.md) as the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) grows. A partly filled ideal level has [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) at its [energy](energy-split.md); when it empties into lower levels, the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) drops. This produces an inverse-field-periodic sawtooth in the clean zero-temperature model. At fixed field and increasing density the corresponding [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is an upward staircase with density increment $eB/h$. Fixed reservoir [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is a different boundary condition, in which the density responds instead. [spin](spin.md) counting, disorder and [temperature](temperature-split.md) qualify these ideal pictures.
