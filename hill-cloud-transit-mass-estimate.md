# Hill-cloud transit mass estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Transit duration of a small planet](transit-duration-of-a-small-planet.md)

For an opaque or detectably extinguishing spherical debris cloud of radius $R_c=\eta R_H$ much larger than the stellar radius, a central [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md) lasts approximately $2R_c/(na)$. Combining this with the [Hill radius](hill-radius.md) gives the displayed mass estimate. For cloud impact distance $s$, replace $R_c$ by $\sqrt{R_c^2-s^2}$: the central formula then gives a lower mass estimate unless the chord geometry is known.
