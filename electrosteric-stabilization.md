# Electrosteric stabilization

↑ **Parent:** [Steric stabilization](steric-stabilization.md)

Stabilization by a surface-bound [polymer](polymer-split.md) layer that supplies both conformational/osmotic repulsion and electrostatic repulsion. Chain coverage, solvent quality and screening determine the resulting interaction barrier.
