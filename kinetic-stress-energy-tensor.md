# Kinetic stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic kinetic theory](relativistic-kinetic-theory.md)

The invariant future mass-shell measure is $d^3p/E$, up to fixed normalization factors absorbed in $f$. Its relation to the [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md) follows by integrating $2\theta(p^0)\delta(p_\mu p^\mu+m^2)\,d^4p$ over $p^0$ in a local [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md). A particle carries [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) $p^\mu$ and crosses a local spatial surface with velocity $p^i/p^0$. Integrating the associated [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) gives the displayed [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md). For [photons](photon.md), $p^{\hat a}=E(1,\mathbf e)$ and $|\mathbf e|=1$.
