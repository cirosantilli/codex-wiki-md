# Apperceptive agnosia

↑ **Parent:** [Visual agnosia](visual-agnosia.md)

A visual-recognition disorder in which constructing or integrating an adequate perceptual representation is impaired. The specific perceptual demands must be established rather than inferred from recognition failure alone.
