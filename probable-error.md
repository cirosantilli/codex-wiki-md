# Probable error

↑ **Parent:** [Quantile function](quantile-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probable_error)

The [probable error](probable-error.md) is the half-width of an interval around a specified central value containing [probability](probability.md) $1/2$. For a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma>0$, its [quantile function](quantile-function.md) gives

$$
r=\sigma\Phi^{-1}(3/4)\approx0.67448975\,\sigma,
$$

where $\Phi$ is the [standard normal cumulative distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md) and $\sigma$ is the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). The factor is specific to the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). The centered [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) with parameter $\gamma>0$ has [probable error](probable-error.md) $\gamma$, despite having no finite [variance](variance-split.md). Thus a central probability width need not require a finite [second moment](second-moment.md).
