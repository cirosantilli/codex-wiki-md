# Inverse-square weighted Dirichlet spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md)

For $y''+\lambda x^{-2}y=0$ on $0<a<x<b$ with zero endpoint values, write $t=\log(x/a)$ and $y=\sqrt{x}\,w(t)$. The [Euler-Cauchy equation](euler-cauchy-equation.md) becomes $w''+(\lambda-1/4)w=0$. Thus $y_n=\sqrt{x}\sin[n\pi\log(x/a)/\log(b/a)]$ and the exact spectrum is displayed above. Leading [WKB quantization](wkb-quantization-condition.md) omits the constant $1/4$, while producing the same leading eigenfunction shape after quantization.
