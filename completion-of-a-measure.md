# Completion of a measure

↑ **Parent:** [Complete measure](complete-measure.md)

The completion of a measure space adjoins every [subset](subset.md) of every measurable [null set](null-set.md) to its [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md). A statement that two events or σ-algebras agree modulo null sets becomes literal after passing to the corresponding measure-algebra completion.
