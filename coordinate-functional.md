# Coordinate functional

↑ **Parent:** [Dual basis](dual-basis.md)

A [coordinate functional](coordinate-functional.md) extracts one coefficient in a specified finite [basis](basis.md). It is an element of that [basis](basis.md)'s [dual basis](dual-basis.md), rather than a coordinate rule independent of the [basis](basis.md). For the [standard basis](standard-basis.md) of $F^n$, the $i$th [coordinate functional](coordinate-functional.md) sends $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ to $a_i$.
