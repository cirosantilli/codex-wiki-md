# Van der Waals law of corresponding states

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

The [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) becomes independent of its material parameters when [pressure](pressure.md), [temperature](temperature.md) and molecular [volume](volume.md) are divided by their critical values: $\bar p=8\bar T/(3\bar v-1)-3/\bar v^2$. This explains the common reduced isotherms within this model, without claiming exact agreement for all real fluids.
