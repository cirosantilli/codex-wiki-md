# Modulus of the complex exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md)

For real $x,y$, the [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) has $e^{x+iy}=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)$. Since the trigonometric factor lies on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md),

$$
|e^z|=e^{\operatorname{Re}z}.
$$

Thus the [real part](real-part.md) of the exponent controls its [modulus](modulus.md), while the [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) controls its direction on the complex plane.
