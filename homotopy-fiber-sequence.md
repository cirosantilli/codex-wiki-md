# Homotopy fiber sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)

A [homotopy fiber sequence](homotopy-fiber-sequence.md) identifies a fibre after replacing a map by a suitable [fibration](fibration.md).

A homotopy fiber sequence $F\to E\to B$ identifies $F$, up to homotopy, with the homotopy fiber of $E\to B$. It induces a long exact sequence of [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md).
