# Hole pocket

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

A [hole pocket](hole-pocket.md) surrounds unoccupied states near a band maximum. Its [hole](electron-hole.md) [carrier density](carrier-density.md) counts missing states in the nearly filled band, not all occupied electrons. In two dimensions the count is $gA_h/(2\pi)^2$. Four corner pieces of a square-zone plot can be one pocket on the periodically identified zone.
