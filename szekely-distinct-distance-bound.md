<h1 id="szekely-distinct-distance-bound">Székely distinct-distance bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Distinct-distance set](distinct-distance-set.md)

For a finite set of at least two points in the plane, some point determines the displayed number of different positive [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md). The [circular-arc graph for distinct distances](circular-arc-graph-for-distinct-distances.md) has quadratically many [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). The [rich-bisector deletion bound](rich-bisector-deletion-bound.md) removes high-multiplicity [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) while retaining that order of growth. The [crossing lemma for multigraphs](crossing-lemma-for-multigraphs.md) then compares its lower crossing bound with the quadratic count of pairs of [circles](circle.md). This strengthens the corresponding bound for the whole [distinct-distance set](distinct-distance-set.md). The original result is Theorem11 of [Székely's 1997 paper](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~michas/szekely.pdf).
