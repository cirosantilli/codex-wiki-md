# Cyclic multiplication model for a normal operator

↑ **Parent:** [Projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md)

The [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md) of a bounded [normal operator](normal-operator.md) $T$ is a representation of $C(K)$, with $K=\sigma(T)$. The [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) represents $f\mapsto\langle f(T)v,v\rangle$ by a positive regular measure $\mu_v$. The assignment $f\mapsto f(T)v$ is isometric for the $L^2(\mu_v)$ [norm](norm.md), and extends to a unitary map onto the [closed](closed-set.md) cyclic subspace it generates. Multiplication by $z$ and $\overline z$ represent $T$ and $T^*$. Orthogonal decomposition into cyclic [reducing subspaces](reducing-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space-operator.md), allowing an uncountable family, constructs the [Borel functional calculus for a normal operator](borel-functional-calculus-for-a-normal-operator.md) without requiring separability.
