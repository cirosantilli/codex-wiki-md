# Mean-estimation minimax lower bound for continuous densities

↑ **Parent:** [Metric squared-loss two-point bound](metric-squared-loss-two-point-bound.md)

For estimating the [expected value](expected-value.md) over all continuous [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md) on $[0,1]$, compare $f=1$ and $g_n(u)=1+(u-1/2)/\sqrt n$. Their means differ by $1/(12\sqrt n)$, and $\chi^2(P_{g_n}\Vert P_f)=1/(12n)$. The [chi-squared divergence of product measures](chi-squared-divergence-of-product-measures.md) bounds joint [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) by $1/2$. The [metric squared-loss two-point bound](metric-squared-loss-two-point-bound.md) then gives the displayed uniform [minimax risk](minimax-risk.md) lower bound.
