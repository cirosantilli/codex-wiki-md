# Neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence-split.md)

Approaches combining learned [neural representations](neural-representation.md) or computations with explicit structured representations and reasoning. A useful account specifies the interface, uncertainty and learning responsibilities of the components rather than assuming their combination automatically supplies general intelligence.
