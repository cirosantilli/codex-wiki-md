# Three power summands over a large prime field

↑ **Parent:** [Power Gauss sum over a prime field](power-gauss-sum-over-a-prime-field.md)

For $S(a)=\sum_u e(au^k/p)$, [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) counts representations of $x$ by $p^{-1}\sum_a S(a)^3e(-ax/p)$. Its zero-frequency term is $p^2$. The [power Gauss sum over a prime field](power-gauss-sum-over-a-prime-field.md) bounds the other terms in total by $k^3p^{3/2}$, strictly less than $p^2$ when $p>k^6$. Every [residue class](residue-class.md) therefore has a representation.
