# Expansion-wave mass budget

↑ **Parent:** [Inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere](inside-out-collapse-of-a-singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

For a [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) with $\rho=a_s^2/(2\pi Gr^2)$, the initial [mass](mass.md) inside the outward front $r_{\rm ew}=a_st$ is $M_{\rm ew}=2a_s^3t/G$. This is not all immediately accreted by the central object. The [inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere](inside-out-collapse-of-a-singular-isothermal-sphere.md) instead has $\dot M_*=m_0a_s^3/G$, with $m_0$ fixed by its dynamical similarity solution; the remainder can stay in the infalling envelope. Equating front sweeping with central [accretion](accretion.md) loses that [mass](mass.md) budget.
