# Transitivity and strict growth of the cumulative hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md)

Each stage of the [cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md) is a [transitive set](transitive-set.md), and the stages increase strictly. If $A$ is transitive, then $A\subseteq\mathcal P(A)$ and $\mathcal P(A)$ is transitive: $x\in y\subseteq A$ gives $x\in A$ and then $x\subseteq A$. Unions of increasing [transitive sets](transitive-set.md) are transitive. These observations prove the assertions by [transfinite induction](transfinite-induction.md), with strictness witnessed by $V_\alpha\in V_{\alpha+1}$ and $V_\alpha\notin V_\alpha$ from the [Axiom of foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md).
