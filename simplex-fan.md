# Simplex fan

↑ **Parent:** [Complete fan](complete-fan.md)

In dimension $d$, the fan whose rays are generated by $e_1,\ldots,e_d,-\sum_ie_i$ and whose cones are generated by proper subsets of those rays is complete. It is the fan of [projective space](projective-space-split.md) $\mathbb P^d$.
