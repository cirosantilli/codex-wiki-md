# Heat-capacity activation estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal activation](thermal-activation.md)

For gapped excitations with $C(T)\simeq A\exp[-\Delta/(k_BT)]$ and approximately constant prefactor, two [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) measurements at $T_2>T_1$ give the displayed activation-energy estimate. A more general prefactor $AT^p$ changes its numerator to $\ln[C(T_2)/C(T_1)]-p\ln(T_2/T_1)$. The two-point estimate measures an effective exponential scale; it does not independently determine the prefactor or establish a detailed excitation [density of states](density-of-states.md).
