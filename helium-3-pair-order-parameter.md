# Helium-3 pair order parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-triplet p-wave pairing](spin-triplet-p-wave-pairing.md)

For a simple [spin-triplet p-wave pairing](spin-triplet-p-wave-pairing.md) description, let the $3\times3$ [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, with [complex number](complex-number.md) entries, map an orbital direction $\widehat{\mathbf k}$ on the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) into a triplet gap vector $\mathbf d=A\widehat{\mathbf k}$. The indices refer to spin and orbital components. A scalar amplitude cannot retain all these relative orientations. [Anthony James Leggett](anthony-james-leggett.md)'s account connects that extra structure to the magnetic responses of the different phases.[pp.1004–1008, broken spin-orbit symmetry and pairing-state response](nobel-lecture-superfluid-3he-the-early-days-as-seen-by-a-theorist-by-anthony-james-leggett.md)

Here are two explicit idealizations, with a common positive scale $\Delta$ and an overall [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) omitted. They show how odd orbital pairing can have either nodes or an isotropic gap. They do not calculate which state has the lower [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md).
