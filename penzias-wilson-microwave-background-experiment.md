# Penzias-Wilson microwave background experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md)

In 1965, [Arno Penzias](arno-allan-penzias.md) and [Robert Woodrow Wilson](robert-woodrow-wilson.md) reported measurements with a 20-foot horn-reflector [antenna](antenna-radio.md) at $4.080\,\mathrm{GHz}$. They manually switched a traveling-wave [maser](maser-split.md) receiver between the horn and a helium-cooled reference. From $6.7\,\mathrm K$ zenith [antenna temperature](antenna-temperature.md), atmospheric and antenna/ground contributions left an excess $3.5\pm1.0\,\mathrm K$.[pp.419–420: apparatus, temperature contributions and residual](a-measurement-of-excess-antenna-temperature-at-4080-mc-per-second-by-arno-allan-penzias-and-robert-woodrow-wilson.md)

Elevation scans estimated the atmospheric term. Taping horn seams produced no measurable change; a nearby transmitter tested ground response. These controls addressed distinct possible contaminations.[p.420: elevation, seam and back-lobe tests](a-measurement-of-excess-antenna-temperature-at-4080-mc-per-second-by-arno-allan-penzias-and-robert-woodrow-wilson.md)

**[Figure 1](#image-comparing-a-sky-horn-with-a-cold-reference-separates-receiver-response-from-incident-radiation). Comparing a sky horn with a cold reference separates receiver response from incident radiation**. Original functional diagram inspired by the reported comparison, not a reconstruction or photograph of the historical apparatus. The added warm reference illustrates a generic [two-load radiometer calibration](two-load-radiometer-calibration.md); it is not claimed to have been switched in this form in the original experiment. Radiation from the sky, atmosphere, ground and antenna can contribute at the receiver input.

The comparison does not make all nuisance contributions disappear. A reference can establish a response scale, but atmospheric emission and pickup through unwanted [antenna](antenna-radio.md) directions are also real incident radiation. In his later account, [Robert Woodrow Wilson](robert-woodrow-wilson.md) describes these competing contributions as central to a [radio astronomy](radio-astronomy.md) measurement.[1978 lecture, introduction and sectionII: sky, ground, atmosphere and instrument contributions](the-cosmic-microwave-background-radiation-by-robert-woodrow-wilson.md)

The Princeton group proposed primordial thermal radiation as an interpretation and called for more [wavelength](wavelength.md) measurements. Their separate $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ [radiometer](radiometer.md) had not yet produced results.[pp.415–416: interpretation, Princeton apparatus and need for further wavelengths](cosmic-black-body-radiation-by-r-h-dicke-and-others.md) A single-frequency excess therefore does not by itself establish a complete [Planck spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md). Reproducibility across independent instruments, observing directions and frequencies tests the interpretation further.
