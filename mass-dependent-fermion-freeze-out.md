# Mass-dependent fermion freeze-out

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md)

In a radiation-dominated toy model with $\Gamma=\alpha_Y^4T^5/m_Y^4$, setting the interaction rate equal to the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) gives the displayed decoupling scale. The transition $T_D=m_Y$ defines $m_{\rm nr}$. For smaller masses, $x_D=m_Y/T_D=(m_{\rm nr}/m_Y)^{1/3}\gg1$. An instantaneous chemically equilibrated [cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md) estimate gives $n_Y/s=[45g_Y/(2\pi^2g_{*s})(2\pi)^{-3/2}]x_D^{3/2}e^{-x_D}$. This estimate presupposes number-changing [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md), [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) and the assumed rate law, rather than following from elastic thermalization alone.
