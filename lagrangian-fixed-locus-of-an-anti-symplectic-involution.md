# Lagrangian fixed locus of an anti-symplectic involution

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-symplectic involution](anti-symplectic-involution.md)

A nonempty [fixed-point set](fixed-point-set.md) of an [anti-symplectic involution](anti-symplectic-involution.md) is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md). Local linearization identifies its [tangent space](tangent-space.md) with the $+1$ [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of the derivative.
