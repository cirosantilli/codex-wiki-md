# Interchange instability

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interchange_instability)

An instability in which neighboring [flux tubes](flux-tube.md) exchange positions across a [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) or other stratification, reducing the appropriate potential [energy](energy-split.md). Unfavorable [magnetic field-line curvature](magnetic-field-line-curvature.md) can drive the exchange; parallel variation bends the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and introduces stabilizing [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md). In a steep-gradient low-[plasma beta](plasma-beta.md) local [Z-pinch](z-pinch.md), $\omega^2=k_\parallel^2v_A^2-2p_0/(\rho_0RL_p)$.
