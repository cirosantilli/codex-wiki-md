# Specific competitor DNA

↑ **Parent:** [DNA competition assay](dna-competition-assay.md)

Competition can draw that [protein](protein-split.md) away from labelled [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) without necessarily removing other [proteins](protein-split.md) from the labelled assembly.
