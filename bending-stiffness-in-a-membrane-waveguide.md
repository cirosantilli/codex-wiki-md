# Bending stiffness in a membrane waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane waveguide](membrane-waveguide.md)

For a plate under uniform [tension](tension.md) per length $T$, with [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) $\sigma$ and [flexural rigidity](bending-stiffness.md) $D_b>0$, the small-displacement equation is $\sigma z_{tt}=T\nabla^2z-D_b\nabla^4z$. If the edge conditions admit a transverse sine mode of [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q_n$, its longitudinal [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is

$$
\omega^2=\frac T\sigma(k^2+q_n^2)+\frac{D_b}\sigma(k^2+q_n^2)^2.
$$

Bending raises mode [cutoff frequencies of a membrane waveguide](cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md) and changes large-$k$ behaviour from $\omega\propto|k|$ to $\omega\propto k^2$. A genuinely clamped plate also fixes the edge slope, so its transverse mode shapes need not remain simple sine functions. The qualitative increase in restoring [force](force-split.md) survives that change of [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
