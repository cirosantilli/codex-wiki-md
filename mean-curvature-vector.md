# Mean curvature vector

↑ **Parent:** [Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md)

For an $n$-dimensional [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md), let $A$ denote its vector-valued [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) and $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of its [tangent space](tangent-space.md). This branch uses the trace [mean curvature vector](mean-curvature-vector.md)

$$
\mathbf H=\sum_{i=1}^n A(e_i,e_i).
$$

An alternative averaged [mean curvature vector](mean-curvature-vector.md) is $\mathbf H/n$; on a [smooth surface](smooth-surface.md) in three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), these are $2HN$ and $HN$, respectively. Reversing $N$ reverses $H$, so their product is independent of the chosen [orientation of a surface](orientation-of-a-surface.md). With the trace convention, the [first variation of area formula](first-variation-of-area-formula.md) has no additional factor $n$. For an outward-oriented [sphere](sphere.md) of [radius](radius.md) $R$, $\mathbf H=-2N/R$: it points toward the centre even though the scalar's sign depends on conventions.
