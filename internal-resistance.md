# Internal resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

An effective source resistance in a specified circuit model: a source with [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) $\mathcal E$ drives $I=\mathcal E/(R_s+R_L)$ through a resistive load $R_L$. A biological source or electrochemical interface can require a frequency-dependent or nonlinear model instead.
