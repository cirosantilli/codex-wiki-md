# Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem](freiman-ruzsa-theorem.md)

If $A\subseteq\mathbb F_p^n$ and $|A+A|\leq K|A|$, then $A$ is contained in a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $H$ satisfying

$$
|H|\leq K^2p^{K^4}|A|.
$$
