# Holomorphic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $F$ on a larger [open set](open-set.md) $V$ whose restriction to $U$ is the original [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $f$. A [removable singularity](removable-singularity.md) is a point across which such an extension exists. On a [connected](connected-space.md) larger [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), a [holomorphic extension](holomorphic-extension.md) is unique by the [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md), provided $U$ is nonempty.
