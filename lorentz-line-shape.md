# Lorentz line shape

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line profile](spectral-line-profile.md)

A normalized collisional line model $\kappa_\nu=(S/\pi)\Delta/[(\nu-\nu_0)^2+\Delta^2]$, with integrated strength $S$ and half-width $\Delta$. The centre scales as $S/\Delta$ and far wings as $S\Delta/(\nu-\nu_0)^2$. In a dilute pressure-broadened gas $\Delta$ scales approximately with [pressure](pressure-split.md).
