# Distance duality relation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological distance measure](cosmological-distance-measure.md)

In metric light propagation with [photon](photon.md) conservation and the usual reciprocity assumptions, [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) and [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md) satisfy $D_L=(1+z)^2D_A$. Comparing received flux with apparent angular area then gives the bolometric [cosmological surface-brightness dimming](cosmological-surface-brightness-dimming.md) factor $(1+z)^{-4}$. Absorption, [photon](photon.md) conversion or an inconsistent definition of emitted/observed bands must be treated separately.
