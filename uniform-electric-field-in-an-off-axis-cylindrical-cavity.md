# Uniform electric field in an off-axis cylindrical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field of a uniformly charged infinite cylinder](electric-field-of-a-uniformly-charged-infinite-cylinder.md)

Remove an axial cylindrical hole, wholly contained in an infinite insulating cylinder with uniform fixed [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$. Let $\mathbf a$ point from the original axis to the hole axis. The [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) replaces the removed material by a cylinder of [charge density](charge-density.md) $-\rho$. At any point $\mathbf r$ inside the hole, the two interior [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) add to $\rho[\mathbf r-(\mathbf r-\mathbf a)]/(2\epsilon_0)=\rho\mathbf a/(2\epsilon_0)$. The result is uniform throughout the hole, even when the hole is not concentric; it does not apply to a hole in an [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) whose [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) can redistribute.
