# Causal reachability on a spatial circle

↑ **Parent:** [Causal structure](causal-structure.md)

For flat [spacetime](spacetime.md) with $x$ periodic of circumference $2\pi R$, lift endpoints of [causal curves](causal-curve.md) to the covering [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). A signal can choose any winding, so the [causal future](causal-future.md) and [causal past](causal-past.md) use the shortest circular distance rather than a fixed coordinate separation. After a duration $\pi R/c$, every point on the circle is reachable. A constant tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) has a nonzero loop [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md); maintaining it while a charge gains [energy](energy-split.md) each lap requires an energy reservoir, not a single-valued electrostatic potential.
