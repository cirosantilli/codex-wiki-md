# Norm projection formula for cyclic Brauer pairings

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic algebra](cyclic-algebra.md)

For a finite extension $L/K$, an unramified or general cyclic character $\chi$ over $K$, and $a\in L^{\times}$, transfer satisfies $\operatorname{Cor}_{L/K}(\operatorname{Res}\chi,a)=(\chi,N_{L/K}a)$. It is the cup-product projection formula combined with the [field norm](field-norm.md) on multiplicative degree-zero coefficients. With the norm definition of Brauer transfer it also covers purely inseparable steps.
