# Cocoercivity

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

An operator $G$ is $\beta$-cocoercive when $\langle Gz-Gw,z-w\rangle\geq\beta\|Gz-Gw\|^2$ with $\beta>0$. This strengthens monotonicity and implies a Lipschitz constant $1/\beta$ by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Gradient maps of smooth [convex functions](convex-function.md) provide an important converse through the [Baillon–Haddad theorem](baillon-haddad-theorem.md).
