# Rainwater

↑ **Parent:** [Water](water.md)

[Water](water.md) condensed and precipitated from the atmosphere. It normally has little dissolved [calcium](calcium.md) or [magnesium](magnesium.md) before contamination or contact with mineral-bearing surfaces; softness does not imply purity.
