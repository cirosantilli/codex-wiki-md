<h1 id="caratheodory-derivative-bound-for-the-right-half-plane">Carathéodory derivative bound for the right half-plane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md)

If a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $F$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) has positive real part, the [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md) $\phi=(F-F(0))/(F+\overline{F(0)})$ maps the disc into itself and vanishes at zero. The [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md) gives the displayed derivative bound. Equality holds exactly when $\phi(z)=\omega z$ with $|\omega|=1$, equivalently $F(z)=i\operatorname{Im}F(0)+\operatorname{Re}F(0)(1+\omega z)/(1-\omega z)$.
