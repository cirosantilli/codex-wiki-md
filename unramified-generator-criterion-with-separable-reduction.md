# Unramified generator criterion with separable reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

A finite extension of complete discretely valued fields is [unramified](unramified-extension.md) exactly when it has an integral generator whose reduced [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) is separable. In one direction, lift a residue-field primitive element. In the other, the factorization form of [Hensel's lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) makes the separable reduction irreducible, forcing residue degree to equal the entire extension degree.
