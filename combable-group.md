# Combable group

↑ **Parent:** [Combing of a group](combing-of-a-group.md)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) is synchronously combable if it admits a [combing of a group](combing-of-a-group.md) with the [synchronous fellow-traveller property](synchronous-fellow-traveller-property.md). Without a regular-language assumption, this is more general than an [automatic group](automatic-group.md). A bounded-rung strip argument gives a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md), and finite-state row shortening gives a recursive exponential filling bound and a soluble [word problem for a group](word-problem-for-groups.md).
