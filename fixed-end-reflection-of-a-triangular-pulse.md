# Fixed-end reflection of a triangular pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For a [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) on $x\le0$ with fixed endpoint $y(0,t)=0$, take an initially incident symmetric [triangular pulse](triangular-function.md) $HT(x/L)$, where $T(s)=\max(0,1-|2s+1|)$. The [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) gives the title formula. Both terms satisfy the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) away from the corners and satisfy it weakly everywhere; their values cancel at the endpoint. At $ct=L/2$ their [displacements](displacement.md) cancel on the whole half-line, but the [velocity](velocity.md) is nonzero on $-L/2<x<0$. Hence a flat instantaneous shape need not mean zero [wave energy](wave-energy.md).
