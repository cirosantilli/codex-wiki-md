# Differential of the exponential map at zero

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map (Riemannian geometry)](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md)

For the [Riemannian exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) $\exp_p(v)=\gamma_v(1)$, uniqueness of the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) gives $\exp_p(tv)=\gamma_v(t)$. Differentiation at $t=0$ yields $(d\exp_p)_0v=v$. Smooth ODE dependence and the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) therefore make $\exp_p$ a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) on a neighborhood of zero, producing [geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md). This is a local assertion and requires no geodesic completeness.
