# Beltrami-Klein model

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic geometry](hyperbolic-geometry.md)

The Beltrami-Klein model realizes the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) inside an open Euclidean disk, with [geodesics](geodesic.md) given by straight chords. Its metric differs from the Euclidean metric, and angles are generally distorted. The straight chords make [geodesic convexity](geodesic-convexity.md) especially easy to see.
