# Frayne embedding lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Model-theoretic ultralimit](model-theoretic-ultralimit.md)

If $A\equiv B$, the elementary diagram of $B$ is finitely satisfiable by interpreting its finitely many named elements in $A$. Choose those finite realizations as coordinates, extend the filter of diagram cones to an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md), and use the [Łoś theorem](los-theorem.md) to embed $B$ elementarily into an [ultrapower](ultrapower.md) of $A$. If $A\prec B$, work over the named elements of $A$ and keep their coordinates constant. The resulting embedding extends the diagonal map of $A$. This relative assertion allows an alternating chain of ultrapowers to keep every previously embedded substructure fixed.
