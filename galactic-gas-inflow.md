# Galactic gas inflow

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)

Galactic gas inflow supplies a galaxy from its circumgalactic medium, cooling halo, or intergalactic filaments. It can sustain [star formation](star-formation.md) and dilute the metallicity of the existing interstellar gas.
