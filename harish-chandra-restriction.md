# Harish-Chandra restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Harish-Chandra induction](harish-chandra-induction.md)

Restriction first views a $G^F$-module as a $P^F$-module, then takes the subspace fixed by the unipotent radical. The resulting $L^F$-module is adjoint to [Harish-Chandra induction](harish-chandra-induction.md) by ordinary [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md). Over characteristic zero, averaging makes invariants exact and identifies them with coinvariants.
