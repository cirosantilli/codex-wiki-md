# Monotonicity of a convex subdifferential

↑ **Parent:** [Subdifferential](subdifferential.md)

Adding the two [subgradient inequalities](subgradient-inequality.md) for $p\in\partial f(x)$ and $q\in\partial f(y)$ gives

$$
\langle p-q,x-y\rangle\geq0.
$$

Thus the [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) is a [monotone operator](monotone-operator.md). This inequality proves uniqueness of its resolvent: two solutions of $z\in x+\tau\partial f(x)$ must coincide when $\tau>0$.
