# Cas9

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md)

A guide-directed [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) nuclease. Target recognition depends on a [guide RNA](guide-rna.md) and an appropriate [protospacer adjacent motif](protospacer-adjacent-motif.md); cleavage is followed by cellular repair, so cleavage alone does not specify the final allele.
