# Vanishing absolute logarithmic mean characterization of Blaschke products

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md)

For a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), this boundary condition characterizes [Blaschke products](blaschke-product.md), including unimodular constants. The nondecreasing radial means of the nonnegative [subharmonic function](subharmonic-function.md) $\log^+|f|$ must vanish, so $|f|\le1$. In the [Blaschke factorization of a bounded holomorphic function](blaschke-factorization-of-a-bounded-holomorphic-function.md) $f=Bg$, both logarithmic means tend to zero. The [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) applied to $\log|g|$ gives $|g(0)|=1$, and the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) makes $g$ unimodular constant. The converse follows from the [radial logarithmic mean of a Blaschke product](radial-logarithmic-mean-of-a-blaschke-product.md). Omitting the outer absolute value changes the condition: $e^z$ has zero mean logarithmic modulus, but is not a [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md).
