# Self-weight deflection of a square cantilever

↑ **Parent:** [Cantilever](cantilever.md)

A uniform square section has [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md) $a^4/12$ and gravitational load per length $\rho g a^2$. Euler-Bernoulli bending therefore gives $y''''=12\rho g/(Ea^2)$, with zero displacement and slope at the clamp and zero moment and shear at the free end. Integrating gives the displayed tip deflection and $y(x)=\rho g x^2(x^2-4lx+6l^2)/(2Ea^2)$. Small slope and [strain](strain.md) are required.
