# Spherical shell

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

A spherical shell is the region between two concentric [spheres](sphere.md). A thin mass shell at radius $r$, thickness $dr$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho(r)$ has mass $4\pi r^2\rho(r)\,dr$ to first order. Decomposing a spherical body into such shells makes the [spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) applicable to its [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md).
