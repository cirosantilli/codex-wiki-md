# Enantiomer

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (chemistry)](chirality-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomer)

[Enantiomers](enantiomer.md) are a pair of nonsuperposable mirror-image [molecules](molecule.md). The D and L configurations of [alanine](alanine.md) are an example; [glycine](glycine.md) has no such pair because its [amino acid alpha carbon](amino-acid-alpha-carbon.md) carries two identical [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md).
