# Observed Fisher information

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md)

The observed Fisher information is the negative Hessian of the realized [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md). Its expectation, under standard regularity conditions, is the [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md).
