# Fast fission

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)

[Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) induced before the incident [neutron](neutron-split.md) has slowed to thermal energies. It allows otherwise non-thermal-fissile fuel constituents such as [uranium-238](uranium-238.md) to contribute to fission power. A change in their fission fraction need not mean a change in total power.
