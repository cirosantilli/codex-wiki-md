# Frobenius reciprocity for compact groups

↑ **Parent:** [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md)

For a [compact group](compact-group.md) $G$, closed subgroup $K$ and finite-dimensional unitary $K$-representation $W$, induced sections are functions satisfying $f(gk)=k^{-1}f(g)$. A $K$-intertwiner $\phi:E\to W$ produces the $G$-intertwiner $v\mapsto(g\mapsto\phi(g^{-1}v))$. Evaluation at the identity reverses this construction on each finite-dimensional continuous representation summand. Thus the multiplicity of an irreducible $E$ in the induced section space is $\dim\operatorname{Hom}_K(E|_K,W)$.
