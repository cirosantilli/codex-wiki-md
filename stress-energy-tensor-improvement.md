# Stress-energy tensor improvement

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md)

Adding $\partial_\rho B^{\rho\mu\nu}$ with $B^{\rho\mu\nu}=-B^{\mu\rho\nu}$ to a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) preserves its divergence identically, since commuting partial derivatives annihilates the antisymmetric pair. The total [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) changes only by a spatial boundary term. Improvements can make local densities symmetric or [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md); equality of integrated charges requires the chosen [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Equation-of-motion terms can also relate representatives [on shell](on-shell.md).
