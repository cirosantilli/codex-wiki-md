# Goeppert Mayer and Jensen nuclear shell model history

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

The 1948 empirical synthesis preceded the 1949 strong [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) proposals. [Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen](johannes-hans-daniel-jensen.md) recalled developing that idea in a discussion with colleagues, independently of [Maria Goeppert Mayer](maria-goeppert-mayer.md)'s work; their shared problem was to account for the observed [magic numbers](magic-number.md) and [spins](spin.md).[pp. 46–48, empirical evidence and strong spin-orbit proposal](glimpses-at-the-history-of-the-nuclear-structure-theory-by-johannes-hans-daniel-jensen.md)

[Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen](johannes-hans-daniel-jensen.md) emphasized that even nuclear [ground states](ground-state.md) retain correlations and that independent-particle motion becomes less adequate at higher excitation. This helps explain why the [nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md) needs residual interactions and complementary collective descriptions.[pp. 48–49, correlations and configuration mixing](glimpses-at-the-history-of-the-nuclear-structure-theory-by-johannes-hans-daniel-jensen.md)

The 1963 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) recognized [Maria Goeppert Mayer](maria-goeppert-mayer.md) and [Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen](johannes-hans-daniel-jensen.md) for nuclear shell structure. [Eugene Paul Wigner](eugene-paul-wigner.md)'s share recognized his contributions to nuclear and elementary-particle theory, especially fundamental [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) principles, a distinct award motivation.[1963 Physics: separate motivations for Wigner and for Goeppert Mayer and Jensen](all-nobel-prizes-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
