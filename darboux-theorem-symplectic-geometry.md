# Darboux theorem (symplectic geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

The Darboux theorem says that every point of a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) has local coordinates in which the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is $\sum_jdx_j\wedge dy_j$. Thus symplectic manifolds have no local invariants beyond dimension.
