# One-period marginal-utility certificate of optimality

↑ **Parent:** [Utility duality with martingale deflators](utility-duality-with-martingale-deflators.md)

For a positive [state-price density](state-price-density.md) $Z$ pricing every attainable payoff, a feasible positive terminal payoff $W^*$ is optimal at its budget if its marginal utility is $yZ$ for some scalar $y>0$. The pointwise conjugate inequality gives $\mathbb EU(W)\leq\mathbb E\widehat U(yZ)+yx$ for every competitor; the marginal-utility identity makes equality hold for $W^*$. Finite states make integrability straightforward. In a [complete market](complete-market.md) an interior optimum also necessarily has this proportionality, by variations preserving the state-price budget.
