<h1 id="arzela-ascoli-theorem">Arzelà-Ascoli theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Equicontinuity](equicontinuity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arzelà–Ascoli_theorem)

For a compact Hausdorff space $K$, a subset of $C(K)$ is relatively compact in the uniform norm exactly when it is [equicontinuous](equicontinuity.md) and pointwise bounded. On a compact metric space, this says that every uniformly bounded equicontinuous sequence has a uniformly convergent subsequence.
