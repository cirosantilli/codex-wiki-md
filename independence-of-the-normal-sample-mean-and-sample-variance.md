# Independence of the normal sample mean and sample variance

↑ **Parent:** [Normal sample](normal-sample.md)

For an independent [normal sample](normal-sample.md) with common mean $\mu$ and positive [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, its [sample mean](sample-mean.md) and [sample variance](sample-variance.md) are independent. Indeed, the mean and residual vector $(X_i-\overline X)_i$ are jointly [multivariate normal](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), and $\operatorname{Cov}(\overline X,X_i-\overline X)=\sigma^2/n-\sigma^2/n=0$. Vanishing cross-covariance between jointly Gaussian blocks proves independence of the mean from the entire residual vector. Since the [sample variance](sample-variance.md) is a measurable [function](function-split.md) of that vector, independence follows. Independence from each coordinate separately would not suffice without the joint Gaussian argument.
