# Finite sum

↑ **Parent:** [Summation](summation.md)

A finite sum adds finitely many terms, for example $\sum_{i=1}^n a_i=a_1+\cdots+a_n$ for a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$. The empty sum is the [additive identity](additive-identity.md). In an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), the [associative property](associative-property.md) and commutativity of the [abelian group](abelian-group.md) make the result independent of grouping and order.
