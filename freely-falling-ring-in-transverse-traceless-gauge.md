# Freely falling ring in transverse-traceless gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-traceless gauge](transverse-traceless-gauge.md)

In a weak plus-polarized [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave-split.md), initially stationary test particles have constant spatial coordinates in [transverse-traceless gauge](transverse-traceless-gauge.md) because $\Gamma^i{}_{00}=0$. Their physical separations nevertheless change. For a small transverse ring, $X=(1+h_+/2)x$ and $Y=(1-h_+/2)y$, so its principal semiaxes oscillate as $a(1\pm h_+/2)$. The radial response has the displayed angular dependence. These are local distance measurements in the long-wavelength limit; finite-arm light-travel times require the full detector response.
