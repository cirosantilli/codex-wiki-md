# Angular displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Circular motion](circular-motion.md)

The signed accumulated rotation about a specified fixed axis. Unlike an angle specified only modulo $2\pi$, [angular displacement](angular-displacement.md) counts complete revolutions as well as the final orientation. Its [derivative](derivative.md) with respect to [time](time-in-physics.md) is [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). The displayed formula therefore follows from the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md).
