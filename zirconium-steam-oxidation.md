# Zirconium-steam oxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidation](oxidation.md)

Reaction of [zirconium](zirconium.md) with steam to form zirconium oxide and hydrogen. Its rate increases strongly with temperature; oxide formation and oxygen uptake alter heat transfer and mechanical behavior.
