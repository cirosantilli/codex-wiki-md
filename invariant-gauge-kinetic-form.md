# Invariant gauge kinetic form

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

In $\mathcal L=-g_{ab}F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{b\mu\nu}/4$, only the symmetric part of $g$ contributes. A constant symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) gives local [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) precisely when it is an [invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md), because the [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) transforms in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). A nondegenerate kinetic term requires a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md); positivity of the Hamiltonian is an additional condition. For a compact simple real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), $-\kappa$ is positive definite, and a positive multiple supplies the usual positive-energy convention with Minkowski signature $(+---)$.
