# Minority carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

The less abundant mobile [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) species: [electron holes](electron-hole.md) in an [n-type semiconductor](n-type-semiconductor.md), or [electrons](electron.md) in a [p-type semiconductor](p-type-semiconductor.md). A small equilibrium population does not imply an unimportant role in an operating [transistor](transistor.md).
