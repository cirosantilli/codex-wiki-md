# Weighted projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Toric variety](toric-variety.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_projective_space)

The weighted projective space is $\operatorname{Proj}k[z_0,\ldots,z_n]$ with $\deg z_i=w_i>0$. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero it is the [geometric quotient](geometric-quotient.md) of punctured affine space by $\lambda\cdot z_i=\lambda^{w_i}z_i$. For well-formed weights, these weights also grade its [Cox ring](cox-ring.md).
