# Collisional quenching of a metastable line

↑ **Parent:** [Critical density of an atomic transition](critical-density-of-an-atomic-transition.md)

A schematic statistical equilibrium is $n_ln_eq_{lu}=n_u(A_{ul}+n_eq_{ul})$. The radiative yield per excitation is $A_{ul}/(A_{ul}+n_eq_{ul})$. It is near unity below critical density $A_{ul}/q_{ul}$ and decreases above it. Quenching suppresses emission relative to naive low-density excitation scaling; it need not make the absolute [emissivity](emissivity.md) decrease when density rises.
