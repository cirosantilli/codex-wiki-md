# D6h molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The 24-operation molecular [point group](point-group.md) of a regular planar hexagon with equivalent sites. Its [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) have inversion labels $g,u$; polar-vector components transform as $A_{2u}$ for $z$ and $E_{1u}$ for $(x,y)$.
