# Dipolar velocity field of a localized eddy

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic impulse](hydrodynamic-impulse.md)

A localized [vorticity](vorticity.md) distribution with nonzero [hydrodynamic impulse](hydrodynamic-impulse.md) induces $\mathbf u\sim[3\mathbf n(\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf n)-\mathbf I]/(4\pi r^3)$. This [curl](curl.md)-free exterior velocity can remain long-ranged even when the [vorticity](vorticity.md) is localized. In a random ensemble it produces dipolar longitudinal and transverse correlation tails.
