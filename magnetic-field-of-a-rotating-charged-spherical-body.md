# Magnetic field of a rotating charged spherical body

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment of a rigidly rotating charge distribution](magnetic-dipole-moment-of-a-rigidly-rotating-charge-distribution.md)

A uniformly charged solid sphere of total charge $Q$, radius $R$ and [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega$ has [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

$$
\mathbf m=\frac12\int\mathbf r\times(\rho\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r)\,d^3r=\frac{QR^2}{5}\boldsymbol\omega.
$$

For a uniformly charged thin spherical shell the coefficient is $1/3$ instead of $1/5$. At large distances the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) is a [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md), $\mathbf B=\mu_0[3(\mathbf m\cdot\hat{\mathbf r})\hat{\mathbf r}-\mathbf m]/(4\pi r^3)$. Its angularly averaged magnitude and [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) magnitude both scale as $r^{-3}$; its angularly averaged vector is zero. Steady rotation of this axisymmetric charge distribution produces [magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md), not radiation.
