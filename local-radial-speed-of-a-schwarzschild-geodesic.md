# Local radial speed of a Schwarzschild geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild static observer](schwarzschild-static-observer.md)

For a radial [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) of specific [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) $k=f\,dt/d\tau$, the static observer measures $v_{\rm loc}=(dr/dt)/f=\dot r/k$. Its relative [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) is $k/\sqrt f$, giving $v_{\rm loc}^2=c^2(1-f/k^2)$.
