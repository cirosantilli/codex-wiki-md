# Population III star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar population](stellar-population.md)

A Population III star is a first-generation star formed from essentially metal-free primordial gas. Limited cooling generally raises its characteristic fragmentation mass and favors a more top-heavy [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) than in enriched star formation.
