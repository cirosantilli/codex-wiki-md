# Affine group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_group)

For a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$, the affine group consists of invertible [affine maps](affine-map.md) $x\mapsto Ax+b$, where $A$ belongs to the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) and $b\in V$. Its product is $(A,b)(A',b')=(AA',b+Ab')$, making it the [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) of the translation vector space and its general linear group. On the real line the positive-dilation subgroup is the [real affine group](orientation-preserving-affine-group-of-the-real-line.md) used in the adjacent article.
