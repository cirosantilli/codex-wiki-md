# Link exterior

↑ **Parent:** [Link](link.md)

The exterior of a [link](link.md) is the complement of the interior of a closed [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) of the entire [link](link.md). Its boundary consists of one [torus](torus.md) per component; its [first homology](first-homology.md) has the component meridians as a free basis.
