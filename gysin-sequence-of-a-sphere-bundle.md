# Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Euler class of a vector bundle](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

A [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) is the [long exact sequence](long-exact-sequence.md) induced by the [Gysin homomorphism](gysin-homomorphism.md) and the [Euler class of a vector bundle](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md).

For the unit sphere bundle $S(E)\to B$ of an oriented rank-$d$ vector bundle, the Gysin sequence contains

$$
\cdots\to H^{q-d}(B)\xrightarrow{\smile e(E)}H^q(B)\to H^q(S(E))\to H^{q-d+1}(B)\xrightarrow{\smile e(E)}H^{q+1}(B)\to\cdots.
$$
