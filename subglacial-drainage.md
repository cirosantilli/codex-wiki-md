# Subglacial drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)

Subglacial drainage transports [water](water.md) beneath a [glacier](glacier.md). The [water pressure](fluid-pressure.md) affects [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md) through the [effective pressure in subglacial drainage](effective-pressure-in-subglacial-drainage.md). [Röthlisberger channel drainage](rothlisberger-channel-drainage.md) concentrates discharge in melt-enlarged conduits; a [linked cavity drainage system](linked-cavity-drainage-system.md) distributes it through connected spaces maintained by sliding and creep.
