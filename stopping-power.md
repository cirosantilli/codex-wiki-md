# Stopping power

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stopping_power_(particle_radiation))

The mean [energy](energy-split.md) lost by a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) per unit path [length](length.md) through a material, with the [particle](particle-physics.md) species, material and [energy](energy-split.md) specified. It is distinct from the [particle range](particle-range.md) obtained by following the [particle](particle-physics.md) until it stops.
