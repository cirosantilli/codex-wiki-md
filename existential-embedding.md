# Existential embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Simple closure](simple-closure.md)

An embedding of [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) is existential when every existential formula with parameters from the source has the same truth value in the source and target. Forward preservation follows from being a [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md); reflection is the additional requirement. Applying [compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) to the target's [diagram of a structure](diagram-mathematical-logic.md) and the source's [elementary diagram](elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md) places the target inside an elementary extension of the source.
