# Intramolecular ligand trapping

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand substitution](ligand-substitution.md)

A donor tethered to a complex captures a vacant coordination site created by [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) loss. Its effective proximity can compete favourably with weakly coordinating bulk solvent, provided its geometry allows it to reach the [metal](metal.md).
