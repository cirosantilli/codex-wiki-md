# Planetary effective radiating temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md)

Define a planet's effective radiating temperature by its actual outgoing longwave flux, regardless of the surface temperature. In radiative equilibrium with solar irradiance $S_0$ and [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) A, $\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4=(1-A)S_0/4$. A [greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md) changes the surface temperature needed to emit that flux. An effective emissivity relating OLR to surface temperature is a diagnostic of the atmosphere–surface system, not necessarily a material emissivity. Away from equilibrium, the outgoing-flux definition and the temperature inferred from absorbed sunlight need not agree.
