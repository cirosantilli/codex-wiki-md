# Burgers vector

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

The lattice translation represented by closure failure of a circuit around a [dislocation](dislocation.md), with a consistent circuit convention. It is a vector, so dislocation reactions conserve its direction as well as its magnitude.
