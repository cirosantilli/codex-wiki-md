# Euler transform of a sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Generating function](generating-function.md)

The Euler transform of a sequence $(b_n)_{n\ge1}$ has coefficients $a_n$ defined by $\sum_{n\ge0}a_nx^n=\prod_{n\ge1}(1-x^n)^{-b_n}$. In unlabelled counting, $b_n$ may count connected components of size $n$, while $a_n$ counts [multisets](multiset.md) of components.
