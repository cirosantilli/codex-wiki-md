# Vertex operator algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_operator_algebra)

A [vertex operator algebra](vertex-operator-algebra.md) is an integer-graded [vector space](vector-space-split.md) equipped with a state-field correspondence $a\mapsto Y(a,z)$, a vacuum, translation operator and conformal vector satisfying vacuum, translation, locality and compatibility axioms. It packages the local [operator product expansions](operator-product-expansion.md) of a chiral [conformal field theory](conformal-field-theory.md). A [string vertex operator](string-vertex-operator.md) is an individual state insertion, rather than this complete [algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md).
