# Galaxy stellar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

Galaxy stellar mass is the total mass in a galaxy's stars and stellar remnants. It is usually inferred from luminosity and colour or a spectral-energy-distribution fit, so it depends on stellar-population models, dust treatment, and the adopted [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md).
