# Pseudoscalar

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoscalar)

A pseudoscalar is invariant under proper rotations but changes sign under a [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md). In three dimensions the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) is a pseudoscalar.
