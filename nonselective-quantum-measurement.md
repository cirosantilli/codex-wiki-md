# Nonselective quantum measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized measurement postulate](generalized-measurement-postulate.md)

A nonselective [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) averages over outcomes that are not read or not communicated. Its [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is $\rho\mapsto\sum_iA_i\rho A_i^\dagger$, with $\sum_iA_i^\dagger A_i=I$. The completeness condition makes the channel trace preserving. The average equals $\sum_i p_i\rho_i$, where $\rho_i$ are the conditional states from a [selective quantum measurement](selective-quantum-measurement.md). A [nonselective projective measurement](nonselective-projective-measurement.md) is the special case in which the operators are orthogonal projectors.
