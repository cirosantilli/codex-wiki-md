# Dissipation identity for a homogeneous Squire mode

↑ **Parent:** [Squire mode](squire-mode.md)

For real base-flow velocity and positive [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md), multiply the homogeneous [Squire equation](squire-equation.md) by the conjugate mode and integrate with zero endpoint values. Taking real parts gives the displayed negative temporal [growth rate](growth-rate.md). This proves decay of homogeneous Squire modes; the coupled shear-flow system can still be unstable or show [transient growth](transient-growth.md) because wall-normal velocity can force the Squire variable.
