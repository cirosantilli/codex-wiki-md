# NIRSpec

↑ **Parent:** [James Webb Space Telescope](james-webb-space-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIRSpec)

NIRSpec provides multiple spectroscopic modes within $0.6$–$5.3\,\mu\mathrm m$. Its time-series modes can measure [exoplanet transmission spectra](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) and [exoplanet secondary eclipses](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md); individual settings have narrower coverage. See [NASA's instrument overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/nirspec/).
