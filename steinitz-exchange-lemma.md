# Steinitz exchange lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinitz_exchange_lemma)

If a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is spanned by $n$ vectors, every [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) family in it has at most $n$ members. Consequently, any two finite [bases](basis.md) have the same number of vectors.
