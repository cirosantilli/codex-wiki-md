# Strictly stable distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Stable distribution](stable-distribution.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) is strictly stable with index $\alpha$ if $n^{-1/\alpha}(T^{(1)}+\cdots+T^{(n)})\overset d=T$ for every positive integer $n$, for [independent](independent-random-variables.md) copies. Here no centering term is allowed, unlike in the general definition of a [stable distribution](stable-distribution.md).
