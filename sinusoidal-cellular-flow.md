# Sinusoidal cellular flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stream function](stream-function.md)

Using $\mathbf u=(-\psi_y,\psi_x)$, the displayed [stream function](stream-function.md) gives $\mathbf u_0=(\sin x\cos y,-\cos x\sin y)$. Its [streamlines](streamline.md) are level sets of $\psi_0$. At $(n\pi,m\pi)$, the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) is $\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{n+m},-(-1)^{n+m})$, so the points are [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md). At $(\pi/2+n\pi,\pi/2+m\pi)$ the eigenvalues are $\pm i$, giving [center equilibria](center-equilibrium.md) surrounded by closed [streamlines](streamline.md). The grid lines $x=n\pi$ and $y=m\pi$ form [heteroclinic orbits](heteroclinic-orbit.md) between adjacent saddles; circulation alternates between cells.
