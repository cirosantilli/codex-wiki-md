# Acoustic WKB amplitude in an expanding universe

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

For pressure-dominated [linear cosmological density perturbations](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md), $\ddot\delta_k+2H\dot\delta_k+\omega^2\delta_k=0$. Writing $\delta_k=\chi/a$ gives $\ddot\chi+(\omega^2-\ddot a/a)\chi=0$. In the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md), the oscillation amplitude is $1/(a\sqrt\omega)$. For constant sound speed it is proportional to $a^{-1/2}$; the local frozen-frequency amplitude damping time $H^{-1}$ does not imply a universal global envelope $a^{-1}$.
