# Fourier shell correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

[Fourier shell correlation](fourier-shell-correlation.md) compares two three-dimensional maps using their complex [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) coefficients in successive reciprocal-space shells. Independent half-map refinement helps distinguish reproducible signal from overfitting; masking and correlated inputs can bias the result.
