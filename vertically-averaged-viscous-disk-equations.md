# Vertically averaged viscous disk equations

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

In an axisymmetric [thin disc](thin-disk.md) with no vertical mass or [torque](torque.md) flux, integrate mass and [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) over height. With fixed height-independent $l=r^2\Omega$, the equations are $\partial_t\Sigma+r^{-1}\partial_r(r\Sigma v_r)=0$ and $\partial_t(\Sigma l)+r^{-1}\partial_r(r\Sigma v_rl-r^3\bar\nu\Sigma\Omega')=0$. Their difference gives $r\Sigma v_rl'=(r^3\bar\nu\Sigma\Omega')'$. The [density-weighted viscosity of a disk](density-weighted-viscosity-of-a-disk.md) supplies the integrated stress.
