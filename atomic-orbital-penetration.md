# Atomic-orbital penetration

↑ **Parent:** [Effective nuclear charge](effective-nuclear-charge.md)

Probability density within the inner [electron](electron.md) shells. Greater penetration can reduce screening and lower an orbital energy; this helps distinguish many-electron s and p levels with the same principal quantum number.
