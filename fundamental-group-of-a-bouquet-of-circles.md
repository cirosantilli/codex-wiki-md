# Fundamental group of a bouquet of circles

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)

The [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a bouquet of $r$ circles is the [free group](free-group.md) on $r$ generators, represented by the oriented circles. For finite $r$, iterated [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) proves the claim by attaching one circle at a time. Nontrivial [reduced words in a free group](reduced-word-in-a-free-group.md) distinguish based loops, while [free homotopy](free-homotopy.md) classes correspond to conjugacy classes.
