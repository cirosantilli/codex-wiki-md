# First-cousin inbreeding coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Inbreeding coefficient](inbreeding-coefficient.md)

For an otherwise [outbred pedigree](outbred-pedigree.md), each of the two shared grandparents contributes $(1/2)^{2+2+1}=1/32$ to the [kinship coefficient](kinship-coefficient.md) of first cousins. Thus their child has [inbreeding coefficient](inbreeding-coefficient.md) $1/16$. Equivalently, cousin kinship is one quarter of the $1/4$ kinship of their sibling parents.
