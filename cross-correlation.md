# Cross-correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

For this sign convention, cross-correlation compares a function with translated copies of another, conjugating the first factor. If $f,g\in L^1(\mathbb R)$, [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) gives [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $\widehat{f\star g}(k)=\overline{\widehat f(k)}\widehat g(k)$. Indeed, the substitution $s=x+y$ factors the double integral into $\int\overline{f(y)}e^{iky}dy$ and $\int g(s)e^{-iks}ds$. The zero-lag value for $f=g\in L^2$ is the squared [L2 norm](l2-norm.md). [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) applied to this correlation proves the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md), first for [Schwartz functions](schwartz-function.md) and then by density for [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md).
