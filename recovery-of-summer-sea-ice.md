# Recovery of summer sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Arctic sea ice decline](arctic-sea-ice-decline.md)

Loss of summer [sea ice](sea-ice.md) need not be irreversible at fixed external forcing. Open water can release excess [heat](heat.md) in autumn, and thin ice grows faster than thick ice during winter because it offers less resistance to [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md). Continued [climate change](climate-change.md) can nevertheless shift the long-term ice cover downward. A one-summer perturbation and persistent forcing are different physical experiments.
