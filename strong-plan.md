# Strong plan

↑ **Parent:** [Nondeterministic planning](nondeterministic-planning.md)

A plan or policy that reaches a goal after finitely many steps under every permitted outcome sequence. In a finite [state space](state-space.md) with finite outcome [sets](set-split.md), a terminating finitely branching plan tree has bounded depth. [Strong planning winning-set iteration](strong-planning-winning-set-iteration.md) constructs exactly the states from which such finite guaranteed-success plans exist. A repeated state cannot be accepted merely because repeated attempts might eventually succeed: without a fairness or [probability](probability.md) assumption, an unfavorable outcome can recur forever.
