# Topological transition map

↑ **Parent:** [Topological atlas](topological-atlas.md)

For two [coordinate charts](manifold-chart.md) in a [topological atlas](topological-atlas.md), the topological transition map is the [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md)

$$
g_{ji}:\varphi_i(U_i\cap U_j)\longrightarrow\varphi_j(U_i\cap U_j),\qquad g_{ji}=\varphi_j\circ\varphi_i^{-1}.
$$

Both domains are [open sets](open-set.md) in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). [Function composition](function-composition.md) gives $g_{ij}=g_{ji}^{-1}$ and $g_{ki}=g_{kj}\circ g_{ji}$ wherever all three [coordinate charts](manifold-chart.md) overlap. These identities express consistency of the coordinate descriptions; imposing [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) as the coordinate changes gives a [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md).
