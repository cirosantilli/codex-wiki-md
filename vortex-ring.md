# Vortex ring

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex tube](vortex-tube.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex_ring)

A [vortex ring](vortex-ring.md) is a [vortex tube](vortex-tube.md) bent into a closed loop, often approximately a [circle](circle.md). Its [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) runs around the loop, while the induced [velocity field](velocity-field.md) passes through the centre and returns outside. For a thin ring of radius $r$ and [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$, the [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md) for incompressible [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) gives an axial [velocity](velocity.md) $u_z(z)=\Gamma r^2/[2(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}]$, measured relative to the ring centre, with its sign set by the [circulation](circulation-physics.md) orientation. A [vortex ring](vortex-ring.md) is not a loop of [fluid](fluid.md) whose [velocity](velocity.md) everywhere merely follows the circular [vortex line](vortex-line.md).
