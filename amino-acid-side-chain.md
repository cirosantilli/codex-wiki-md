# Amino acid side chain

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

An [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) is the variable group attached to an [amino acid alpha carbon](amino-acid-alpha-carbon.md), conventionally denoted $R$. For [alanine](alanine.md) it is $\mathrm{CH_3}$: a [carbon](carbon.md) bonded to three [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). In a [beta strand](beta-strand.md), successive [amino acid side chains](amino-acid-side-chain.md) alternate between the two faces in the idealized arrangement. See [Clark, Douglas and Choi: amino-acid R groups](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins).
