# Floor function

↑ **Parent:** [Floor and ceiling functions](floor-and-ceiling-functions.md)

The floor $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest [integer](integer.md) not exceeding the [real number](real-number.md) $x$. Equivalently, $\lfloor x\rfloor=m$ exactly when $m\leq x<m+1$, for an [integer](integer.md) $m$. The [floor function](floor-function.md) is constant on each such half-open [interval](interval-mathematics.md) and has a [discontinuity of a function](discontinuity-of-a-function.md) at each [integer](integer.md).

For nonnegative [integers](integer.md) $N$ and positive [integers](integer.md) $k$, $\lfloor N/k\rfloor$ is the number of complete groups of size $k$ that fit into $N$ items. With $N=102$ and $k=20$, five complete groups use 100 items. [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md) writes $102=20\cdot5+2$, retaining the two-item [remainder](remainder.md) rather than treating the [quotient](quotient.md) $102/20=5.1$ as a number of complete groups.
