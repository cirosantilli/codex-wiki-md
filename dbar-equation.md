# Dbar equation

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Cauchy-Riemann operator](first-order-cauchy-riemann-operator.md)

A prescribed inhomogeneous [Wirtinger derivative](wirtinger-derivatives.md) equation. The distributional identity $\partial_{\bar z}(1/(\pi z))=\delta_0$ gives its whole-plane right inverse through the [Cauchy-Green operator](cauchy-green-operator.md). Boundary or decay conditions select a solution from the freedom to add a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md).
