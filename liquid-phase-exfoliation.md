# Liquid-phase exfoliation

↑ **Parent:** [Graphene](graphene.md)

Separating a layered material into dispersed thin flakes by mechanical processing in a liquid. For [graphite](graphite.md), liquid choice, shear or sonication, classification and recovery jointly control the [graphene](graphene.md) flake distribution. Preserving the lattice does not guarantee a high monolayer yield or a clean final electrode.[2008, preparation and characterization](high-yield-production-of-graphene-by-liquid-phase-exfoliation-of-graphite-by-yenny-hernandez-and-others.md)
