# Cosmological lithium problem

↑ **Parent:** [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmological_lithium_problem)

At the [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) favored by deuterium and the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md), standard [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) predicts more lithium-7 than is directly inferred from the [Spite plateau](spite-plateau.md), typically by a factor of a few. Stellar depletion, atmosphere systematics and possible new microscopic physics must be distinguished before interpreting this discrepancy as a failure of the cosmological framework.
