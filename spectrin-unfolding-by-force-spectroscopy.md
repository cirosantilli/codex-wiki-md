# Spectrin unfolding by force spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrin](spectrin.md)

A modular [protein](protein-split.md) can release additional contour length when a folded domain crosses a mechanical unfolding barrier. At almost fixed end separation this lowers fractional extension and tension, producing a saw-tooth force record. Successive [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md) fits can share backbone [persistence length](persistence-length.md) while their contour lengths increase. Rupture force and hysteresis depend on loading history; peak force alone is not an equilibrium unfolding free energy.
