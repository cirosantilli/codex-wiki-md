# Dobson unit

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

A unit of atmospheric column abundance. One [Dobson unit](dobson-unit.md) corresponds to approximately $2.69\times10^{16}$ [molecules](molecule-split.md) per square centimetre. Multiplying a column decrement by an [absorption cross section](absorption-cross-section.md) gives its contribution to a monochromatic optical-depth decrement.
