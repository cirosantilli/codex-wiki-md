# Numeric widening

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit conversion](implicit-conversion.md)

A language-defined conversion from a numeric type to another admitted numeric type, such as [Scala](scala.md)'s conversion of an `Int` argument to `Double`. It can make an overloaded method applicable even when the argument's [static type](static-type.md) differs from the parameter type. It does not imply a [subtyping](subtyping.md) relationship between every pair of numeric types or exact representation of every source value; sufficiently large integers can lose precision in a floating-point target.
