# Signal region

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

A pre-specified region of reconstructed event space selected for sensitivity to a hypothesized signal. Observed events are compared with a background prediction constrained by [particle-physics control regions](particle-physics-control-region.md) and other measurements. A [blind analysis](blind-analysis.md) can hide this region while choices and uncertainty estimates are finalized.
