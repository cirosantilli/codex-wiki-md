# Hemispherical capillary imbibition

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary imbibition](capillary-imbibition.md)

Radial [capillary imbibition](capillary-imbibition.md) into a half-space has hemispherical flow area $2\pi r^2$. Its radial hydraulic resistance is proportional to $1/R_s-1/R$, so its inlet [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) approaches $2\pi k\Delta P R_s/\mu$. The growing front area gives late-time $R\propto t^{1/3}$, unlike the planar $t^{1/2}$ law. Both results assume capillary-dominated geometry with negligible directional gravity effects.
