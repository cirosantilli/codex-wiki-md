# Wavevector selection rule for equivariant monomials

↑ **Parent:** [Translation invariants of Fourier-mode phases](translation-invariants-of-fourier-mode-phases.md)

An [equivariant dynamical system](equivariant-dynamical-system.md) can contain the [monomial](monomial.md) $\prod_jA_j^{p_j}\overline A_j^{q_j}$ in its $\ell$th [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) equation only if its total [wavevector](wavevector.md) equals the output [wavevector](wavevector.md). Its degree is $\sum_j(p_j+q_j)$. Thus an integer vector $d=p-q$ contributes at degree $N$ exactly when the [wavevector](wavevector.md) equation holds, $\|d\|_1\leq N$, and $N-\|d\|_1$ is even. The extra even degree is supplied by factors $|A_j|^2$. This finite integer test distinguishes regular saturation terms from phase-sensitive [resonance](resonance.md) terms.
