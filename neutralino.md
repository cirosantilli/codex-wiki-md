# Neutralino

↑ **Parent:** [Sparticle](sparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutralino)

In the [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md) mixes the neutral [bino](bino.md), [wino](wino.md) and two neutral [higgsinos](higgsino.md) into four neutral [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md) mass eigenstates, the [neutralinos](neutralino.md). A neutralino can be the [lightest supersymmetric particle](lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md); with conserved [R-parity](r-parity.md) it then provides a stable possible [dark matter](dark-matter.md) candidate.
