# Attachment theory

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental psychology](developmental-psychology.md)

A framework concerning enduring caregiver relationships and their contribution to security and development. Attachment-related predictions require measures of relationship organization and subsequent functioning, not simply a count of separations.
