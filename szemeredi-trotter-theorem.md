<h1 id="szemeredi-trotter-theorem">Szemerédi–Trotter theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Incidence geometry](incidence-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szemerédi–Trotter_theorem)

For $m$ lines and $n$ points in the real plane, the number of point-line incidences is

$$
O(m+n+m^{2/3}n^{2/3}).
$$

Connecting consecutive incidence points on each line and applying the [Crossing lemma](crossing-lemma.md) proves the bound.
