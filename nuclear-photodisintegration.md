# Nuclear photodisintegration

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

A sufficiently energetic photon can break up a nucleus, consuming energy for an endothermic breakup. In [neon burning](neon-burning.md) and [silicon burning](silicon-burning.md), such steps combine with captures to form a larger reaction network whose net energy balance can be positive.
