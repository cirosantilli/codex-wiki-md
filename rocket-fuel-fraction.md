# Rocket fuel fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md)

In an ideal [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) calculation where all expelled [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md) is called fuel, the [rocket fuel fraction](rocket-fuel-fraction.md) is its initial [mass](mass.md) divided by the total initial [mass](mass.md), including payload and structure. Complete consumption leaves [rocket dry mass](rocket-dry-mass.md) $m_f=(1-f)m_0$ and [rocket mass ratio](rocket-mass-ratio.md) $m_0/m_f=1/(1-f)$. The payload is one component of $m_f$, not an additional [mass](mass.md) to add to an already inclusive $m_0$.
