# Exner equation

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exner_equation)

The [Exner equation](exner-equation.md) is local sediment [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) expressed in terms of solid volume: accumulation raises the bed and flux divergence lowers it. Here $\phi_b$ is the solid volume fraction of the packed bed and $q$ is solid volume flux per unit width.
