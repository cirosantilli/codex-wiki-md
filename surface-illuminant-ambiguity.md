# Surface-illuminant ambiguity

↑ **Parent:** [Color constancy](color-constancy.md)

In a diffuse spectral model $N(\lambda)=E(\lambda)R(\lambda)$, choose any positive $g$ for which $R/g$ remains an admissible [reflectance](reflectance.md). Then $E'=Eg$ and $R'=R/g$ preserve $N$ exactly. Thus outgoing spectral information alone does not uniquely identify [reflectance](reflectance.md) and illumination; three-channel receptor sampling introduces further ambiguity.
