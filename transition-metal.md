# Transition metal

↑ **Parent:** [Metal](metal.md)

A [transition metal](transition-metal.md) has an incompletely filled d subshell in its atom or a relevant cation. Its [metal](metal.md)–ligand orbital interactions support varied [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md), magnetic states and coordination geometries.
