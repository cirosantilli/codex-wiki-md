# Generator square inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Carré du champ operator](carre-du-champ-operator.md)

If real $f$ and $f^2$ belong to the [generator domain](generator-domain.md) of a [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md), [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives $P_t(f^2)-(P_tf)^2\geq0$. This expression is zero at $t=0$, so its right [derivative](derivative.md) is nonnegative, proving the inequality. It is equivalent to [positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) of the [carré du champ](carre-du-champ-operator.md).
