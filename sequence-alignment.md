# Sequence alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence_alignment)

A [sequence alignment](sequence-alignment.md) places [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) or [protein](protein-split.md) sequences in correspondence for comparison. The choice of corresponding positions affects evolutionary inference; matching a short stretch alone does not establish [common descent](common-descent.md) of entire [organisms](organism-split.md).
