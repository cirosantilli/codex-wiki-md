# Generating cofibrations for nonnegative chain complexes

↑ **Parent:** [Projective model structure on nonnegative chain complexes](projective-model-structure-on-nonnegative-chain-complexes.md)

The [sphere chain complex](sphere-chain-complex.md) inclusions into [disk chain complexes](disk-chain-complex.md), together with the separate degree-zero cell, generate the [model cofibrations](model-cofibration.md) by cell attachment and retracts. Their right-injective class is exactly the [acyclic fibration in the projective chain-complex model structure](acyclic-fibration-in-the-projective-chain-complex-model-structure.md).
