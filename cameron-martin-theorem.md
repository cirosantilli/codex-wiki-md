# Cameron-Martin theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron-Martin_theorem)

Translation of [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md) by a path $h$ is equivalent to Wiener measure exactly when $h$ belongs to the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md). In that case the density is

$$
\exp\left(\int_0^Th'(s)\,dB_s-\frac12\int_0^T|h'(s)|^2ds\right).
$$

Translation by any continuous path outside that space produces a singular measure.
