# Laguerre unitary ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Wishart matrix](complex-wishart-matrix.md)

The Laguerre unitary ensemble is the positive-eigenvalue ensemble with joint density proportional to $\prod_{i<j}(x_j-x_i)^2\prod_i x_i^a e^{-x_i}$, for $a>-1$. Integer $a=n-m\geq0$ is realized by a [complex Wishart matrix](complex-wishart-matrix.md). The [Laguerre projection kernel](laguerre-projection-kernel.md) expresses its [correlation functions of a point process](correlation-function-of-a-point-process.md) as determinants.
