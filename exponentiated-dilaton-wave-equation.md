# Exponentiated dilaton wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dilaton equation from contracted Bianchi identity](dilaton-equation-from-contracted-bianchi-identity.md)

For the [dilaton equation from contracted Bianchi identity](dilaton-equation-from-contracted-bianchi-identity.md), put $F=e^{-2\varphi}$. The chain rule gives $\Box_gF=F(-2\Box_g\varphi+4|\nabla\varphi|^2)=-2CF$. Thus the exponential obeys a linear [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) on the given background, although the original [dilaton](dilaton.md) equation is nonlinear and the [string-frame metric](string-frame-metric.md) remains coupled to the [dilaton](dilaton.md). With the opposite named-field convention $\varphi=-\Phi$, this variable is $F=e^{2\Phi}$.
