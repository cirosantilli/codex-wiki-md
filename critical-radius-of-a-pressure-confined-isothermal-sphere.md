# Critical radius of a pressure-confined isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-mass turning point of an isothermal sphere](fixed-mass-turning-point-of-an-isothermal-sphere.md)

For the regular [isothermal Lane-Emden equation](isothermal-lane-emden-equation.md), the first fixed-mass pressure turning point occurs at $\xi_{\rm crit}\simeq6.451$. Thus the boundary radius there is $R_{\rm crit}\simeq1.820\,c_s/\sqrt{G\rho_c}$, where $c_s^2=K$ is the squared [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md). The approximate choice $\xi_{\rm crit}=6.5$ gives coefficient $1.83$. Comparing with the [Jeans length](jeans-length.md) requires specifying the reference density and remembering that the latter is a wavelength rather than a boundary radius.
