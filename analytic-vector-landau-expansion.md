# Analytic vector Landau expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Landau theory](landau-theory.md)

For an isotropic [vector](vector.md) [order parameter](order-parameter.md), an analytic scalar [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) near the origin contains powers of $\mathbf m\cdot\mathbf m$. Odd powers of its magnitude are rotationally invariant but nonanalytic as functions of the Cartesian components at zero; excluding them requires analyticity, not merely writing the magnitude as a nonnegative scalar. With $d>0$, changing $b$ from positive to negative continuously creates minima at $|\mathbf m|=\sqrt{-b/(2d)}$, with an arbitrary direction.[§4.2, rotationally invariant vector expansion](statistical-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
