# Sturm-Liouville equation

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville theory](sturm-liouville-theory-split.md)

A second-order differential equation in the displayed divergence form. Under the usual regular spectral assumptions, $p>0$, $w>0$, the coefficients have suitable regularity, and separated self-adjoint boundary conditions specify a [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md). Multiplying by $y$ and using zero endpoint values gives the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) $\lambda=\int(py'^2+qy^2)dx/\int wy^2dx$ for a nonzero real [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md). Sign conventions for the potential and eigenvalue vary; they must be read from the stated equation.
