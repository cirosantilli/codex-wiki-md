# Calcium-induced calcium release

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md)

[Calcium-induced calcium release](calcium-induced-calcium-release.md) is activation of release from an intracellular [calcium](calcium.md) store by cytoplasmic [calcium](calcium.md) itself. A two-pool model can represent the release [flux](flux.md) as a product of [saturating](hill-function.md) [functions](function-split.md) of store and cytoplasmic [concentrations](concentration.md). Together with uptake, leakage and external [calcium](calcium.md) balance, this [positive feedback](positive-feedback.md) can generate excitability or sustained [oscillation](oscillation.md); the existence of [oscillation](oscillation.md) still depends on the full dynamical parameters.
