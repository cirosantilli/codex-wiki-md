# Tropospheric ozone

↑ **Parent:** [Ozone](ozone.md)

[Ozone](ozone.md) in the [troposphere](troposphere.md) is a secondary chemical pollutant and a radiatively active gas. Its abundance reflects photochemical production, chemical loss, [dry deposition](dry-deposition.md) and transport. The [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) of [nitrogen dioxide](nitrogen-dioxide.md) alone is part of a null [ozone](ozone.md) cycle unless [peroxy radicals](peroxy-radical.md) regenerate [nitrogen dioxide](nitrogen-dioxide.md) without consuming [ozone](ozone.md).
