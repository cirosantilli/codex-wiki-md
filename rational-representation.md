# Rational representation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_representation)

A [rational representation](rational-representation.md) is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) whose matrix coefficients are [regular functions](regular-function.md) on the [affine algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md). For the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md), those functions are [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in matrix entries and the inverse [determinant](determinant.md). The word rational does not permit poles at points of the group.
