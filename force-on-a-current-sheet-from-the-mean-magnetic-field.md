# Force on a current sheet from the mean magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Current sheet](current-sheet.md)

The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) per unit area on an ideal [current sheet](current-sheet.md) is computed using the mean of its limiting [magnetic fields](magnetic-field.md). The sheet's own local [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is equal and opposite on its two sides and cancels in this mean. Equivalently, integrate the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) through a thin regularized layer: its self-field contribution averages to zero, leaving the external field acting on the [surface current density](surface-current-density.md).
