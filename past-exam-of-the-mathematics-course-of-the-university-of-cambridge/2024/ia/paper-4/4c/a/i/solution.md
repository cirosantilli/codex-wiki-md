↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $c=1$. If $B$ is in the future lightcone of $A$ and $C$ in that of $B$, then

$$
t_B-t_A\geq|x_B-x_A|,
\qquad
t_C-t_B\geq|x_C-x_B|.
$$

Adding and using the triangle inequality gives

$$
t_C-t_A\geq|x_B-x_A|+|x_C-x_B|
\geq|x_C-x_A|.
$$

**Thus the statement is true.**
