<h1 id="10c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [central force](../../../../../../central-force.md) conserves [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md)

$$
\ell=mr^2\dot\theta.
$$

Put $u=1/r$. Since $\dot\theta=\ell u^2/m$,

$$
\dot r=-\frac{\ell}{m}u',
\qquad
\ddot r=-\frac{\ell^2}{m^2}u^2u'',
\qquad
r\dot\theta^2=\frac{\ell^2}{m^2}u^3,
$$

where primes now denote [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) with respect to $\theta$. Substitution into the radial equation gives the [Binet equation](../../../../../../binet-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{
u''+u
=\frac{m}{\ell^2u^2}
\frac{dV}{dr}\bigg|_{r=1/u}}.
$$
