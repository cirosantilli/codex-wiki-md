<h1 id="11c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each freely moving particle has position, relative to its own initial radial and tangential unit [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md),

$$
x_i(t)=R,e_{r,i}+vt,e_{\theta,i}.
$$

Thus all particles lie on a circle of radius

$$
\boxed{R(t)=\sqrt{R^2+v^2t^2}}.
$$

Their [angular momenta](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) are constant and sum to

$$
\boxed{L=mRv,\hat z}.
$$

Since $I(t)=mR(t)^2$, the angular [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) is

$$
\dot\theta=\frac{L}{I(t)}
=\frac{Rv}{R^2+v^2t^2}.
$$

With $\theta(0)=0$,

$$
\boxed{\theta(t)=\arctan\frac{vt}{R}}.
$$

This is the geometry of a [freely expanding rotating particle ring](../../../../../../../freely-expanding-rotating-particle-ring.md).
