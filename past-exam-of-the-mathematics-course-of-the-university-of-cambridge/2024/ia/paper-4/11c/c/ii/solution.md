<h1 id="11c/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At time $t$, the remaining water has [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md)

$$
L=\frac12mR^2\omega.
$$

During $dt$, the ejected mass is $-dm$, and its signed tangential speed in the inertial frame is $R\omega+u$. Conservation of [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) gives, to first order,

$$
0=d\left(\frac12mR^2\omega\right)
+(-dm)R(R\omega+u).
$$

After simplification, the [angular rocket equation](../../../../../../../angular-rocket-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{m\dot\omega
=\dot m\left(\omega+\frac{2u}{R}\right)}.
$$

Here $u$ is signed relative to the direction of rotation; reversing the spray changes its sign.
