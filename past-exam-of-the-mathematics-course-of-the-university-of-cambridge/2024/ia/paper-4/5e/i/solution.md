<h1 id="5e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A decimal integer is congruent modulo $3$ to its digit sum. Cyclic permutation does not change that sum. Hence every rotation of a multiple of $3$ is again divisible by $3$.
