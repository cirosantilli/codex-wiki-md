↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No, even when every element of $S$ is positive.** Take

$$
S=\left\{\frac12\right\},\qquad T=\{1,2\}.
$$

Then $S^T=\{1/2,1/4\}$, so

$$
\sup(S^T)=\frac12,
\qquad
(\sup S)^{\sup T}=\left(\frac12\right)^2=\frac14.
$$
