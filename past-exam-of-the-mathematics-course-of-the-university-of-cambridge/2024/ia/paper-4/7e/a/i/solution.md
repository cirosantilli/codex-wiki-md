<h1 id="7e/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every convergent [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md) is bounded. Conversely, let $(x_n)$ be increasing and bounded above, and set $L=\sup\{x_n:n\geq1\}$. For any $\varepsilon>0$, $L-\varepsilon$ is not an upper bound, so some $x_N>L-\varepsilon$. Monotonicity then gives

$$
L-\varepsilon<x_N\leq x_n\leq L
$$

for all $n\geq N$. Hence $|x_n-L|<\varepsilon$, proving the [monotone bounded sequence](../../../../../../../monotone-bounded-sequence.md) criterion.
