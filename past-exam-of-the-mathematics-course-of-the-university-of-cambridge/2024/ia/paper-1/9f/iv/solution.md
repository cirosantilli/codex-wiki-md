For the [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in part (iii), part (ii) gives

$$
\left(\frac{n!}{n^n}\right)^{1/n}\longrightarrow e^{-1}.
$$

Taking reciprocals yields the [factorial-over-power series](../../../../../../factorial-over-power-series.md) [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n}{(n!)^{1/n}}=e}.
$$
