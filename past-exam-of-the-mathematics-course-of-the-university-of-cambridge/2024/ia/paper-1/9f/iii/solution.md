<h1 id="9f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set

$$
a_n=\frac{n!}{n^n}.
$$

Then

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}
=\frac{(n+1)!}{(n+1)^{n+1}}
\frac{n^n}{n!}
=\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^n
\longrightarrow e^{-1}<1.
$$

The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) therefore proves

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n!}{n^n}<\infty}.
$$
