<h1 id="2c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The $j$th column of $B$ is

$$
c_j=v+aj\mathbf1,
\qquad
v=(1^2+b,2^2+b,\ldots,n^2+b)^T.
$$

Thus the image is contained in $\operatorname{span}\{v,\mathbf1\}$. Since $n>1$, $v$ is not constant; since $m>1$ and $a\ne0$, two columns have a nonzero constant difference. The two spanning [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) therefore both lie in the image, so $\operatorname{rank}B=2$. By [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{nullity}B=m-2}.
$$
