The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) states that for a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) with finite-dimensional domain,

$$
\dim(\text{domain})
=\operatorname{rank}+\operatorname{nullity}.
$$

Here the domain is $\mathbb R^3$, and the preceding parts give

$$
3=2+1,
$$

which verifies the theorem.
