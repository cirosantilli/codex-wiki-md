<h1 id="2c/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $j$th column is

$$
c_j=v+aj\mathbf1,
\qquad
v=(1+b,2+b,3+b)^T.
$$

All three columns therefore lie in the two-dimensional span of $v$ and $\mathbf1$, so

$$
\boxed{\det A=0}.
$$
