<h1 id="3f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every $N$, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\sum_{n=1}^N\sqrt{a_nb_n}
\leq
\left(\sum_{n=1}^Na_n\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_{n=1}^Nb_n\right)^{1/2}.
$$

The right-hand side is bounded independently of $N$. Since the summands are positive, the partial sums increase to a finite [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md), proving the [geometric means of two summable positive sequences](../../../../../../geometric-means-of-two-summable-positive-sequences.md) result.
