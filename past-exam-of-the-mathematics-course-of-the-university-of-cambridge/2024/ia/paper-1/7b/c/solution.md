<h1 id="7b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Consider the real [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md)

$$
p(t)=\det(A+tB).
$$

The hypothesis says $p(i)\ne0$, so $p$ is not identically zero. A nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has only finitely many roots, whereas $\mathbb R$ is infinite. Hence there is a real $\lambda$ with

$$
p(\lambda)=\det(A+\lambda B)\ne0.
$$

**Thus $A+\lambda B$ is invertible, as summarized by the [real parameter avoiding a singular matrix pencil](../../../../../../real-parameter-avoiding-a-singular-matrix-pencil.md).**
