The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of $A$ is

$$
\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A).
$$

A [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda\in\mathbb C$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) when there is a nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $v$ such that

$$
Av=\lambda v.
$$

Such a $v$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) belonging to $\lambda$. Equivalently, $\lambda$ is a root of $\chi_A$ and the corresponding [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are the nonzero elements of $\ker(A-\lambda I)$.
