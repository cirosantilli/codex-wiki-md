<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(A)=\sum_{i=1}^nA_{ii}.
$$

Using [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) multiplication and interchanging dummy indices,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(AB)
=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}B_{ji}
=\sum_{j,i}B_{ji}A_{ij}
=\operatorname{Tr}(BA).
$$

The proposed noncyclic interchange is false. With [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) units,

$$
A=E_{11},\qquad B=E_{12},\qquad C=E_{21},
$$

one has

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(ABC)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}(ACB)=0.
$$

Trace is invariant under cyclic permutations, but an arbitrary swap need not be cyclic.
