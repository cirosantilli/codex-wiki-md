<h1 id="7b/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) belonging to distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are linearly independent. Indeed, suppose

$$
\sum_{j=1}^m c_jv_j=0
$$

is a shortest nontrivial relation, where $Av_j=\lambda_jv_j$ and the $\lambda_j$ are distinct. Applying $A-\lambda_mI$ gives

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}c_j(\lambda_j-\lambda_m)v_j=0,
$$

a shorter nontrivial relation, a contradiction.

Thus $n$ distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) provide a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $n$ [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), proving that $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb C$. If $A$ is real and all its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real, each real singular [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A-\lambda I$ has a nonzero real null [vector](../../../../../../vector.md). Choosing one for each [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) gives a real eigenbasis. Hence the real [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$. This is the [distinct eigenvalues imply diagonalizability](../../../../../../distinct-eigenvalues-imply-diagonalizability.md) criterion.
