<h1 id="1a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The fixed-point equation is

$$
\frac{az+b}{z+1}=z
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
z^2+(1-a)z-b=0.
$$

A unique solution at $z=i$ means that this [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is

$$
(z-i)^2=z^2-2iz-1.
$$

Comparing coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{a=1+2i,\qquad b=1}.
$$

This is the repeated-root case of [prescribed fixed points of a Möbius transformation](../../../../../../../prescribed-fixed-points-of-a-mobius-transformation.md).
