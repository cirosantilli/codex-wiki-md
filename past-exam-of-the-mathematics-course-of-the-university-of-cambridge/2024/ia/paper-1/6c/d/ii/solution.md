<h1 id="6c/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The recurrence becomes

$$
d_n=-2d_{n-1}-d_{n-2},
\qquad d_0=1,\quad d_1=-2.
$$

Its characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is

$$
r^2+2r+1=(r+1)^2.
$$

Therefore $d_n=(\alpha+\beta n)(-1)^n$. The initial conditions give $\alpha=1$ and $\beta=1$, so

$$
\boxed{d_n=(-1)^n(n+1)}.
$$
