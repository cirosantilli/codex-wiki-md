<h1 id="6c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)^T$, the [cross-product matrix](../../../../../../cross-product-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-a_3&a_2\\
a_3&0&-a_1\\
-a_2&a_1&0
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Its diagonal is zero, so $\operatorname{tr}A=0$. Also $Aa=a\times a=0$, and $a\ne0$, so $A$ has a nontrivial kernel. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}A=0,\qquad\det A=0}.
$$
