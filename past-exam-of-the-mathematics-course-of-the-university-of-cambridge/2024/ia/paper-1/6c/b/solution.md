<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $a=(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)^T$. The [reflection in a hyperplane](../../../../../../reflection-in-a-hyperplane.md) orthogonal to $a$ sends

$$
x\longmapsto x-2\frac{a^Tx}{a^Ta}a,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{M=I_4-\frac{2aa^T}{a^Ta}}.
$$

It acts as $-1$ on $\operatorname{span}\{a\}$ and as $1$ on the three-dimensional hyperplane $a^\perp$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\det M=-1}.
$$
