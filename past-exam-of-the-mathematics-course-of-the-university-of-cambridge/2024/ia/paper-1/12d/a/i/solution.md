<h1 id="12d/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a partition

$$
P:\quad 0=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_m=1,
$$

put

$$
M_j=\sup_{[x_{j-1},x_j]}f,
\qquad
m_j=\inf_{[x_{j-1},x_j]}f.
$$

The upper and lower Darboux sums are

$$
U(f,P)=\sum_{j=1}^mM_j(x_j-x_{j-1}),
\qquad
L(f,P)=\sum_{j=1}^mm_j(x_j-x_{j-1}).
$$

The [upper and lower Darboux integrals](../../../../../../../upper-and-lower-darboux-integrals.md) are

$$
\overline{\int_0^1}f=\inf_PU(f,P),
\qquad
\underline{\int_0^1}f=\sup_PL(f,P).
$$

The [bounded function](../../../../../../../bounded-function.md) is Riemann integrable when these two numbers agree; their common value is its [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md).
