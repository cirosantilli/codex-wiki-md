<h1 id="10e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(a+h)g(a+h)-f(a)g(a)
={}&[f(a+h)-f(a)]g(a+h)\\
&+f(a)[g(a+h)-g(a)].
\end{aligned}
$$

Divide by $h$ and let $h\to0$. Differentiability implies continuity, so $g(a+h)\to g(a)$. Therefore the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{(fg)'(a)=f'(a)g(a)+f(a)g'(a)}.
$$
