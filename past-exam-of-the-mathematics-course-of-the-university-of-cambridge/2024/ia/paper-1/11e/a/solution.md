<h1 id="11e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose $z_+,z_-\in\mathbb C$ with

$$
f(z_+)>0,\qquad f(z_-)<0.
$$

Restrict $f$ to the [line segment](../../../../../../line-segment.md) between them:

$$
h(t)=f\bigl((1-t)z_-+tz_+\bigr),
\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
$$

This is continuous, with $h(0)<0<h(1)$. The [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) gives a $t_0$ with $h(t_0)=0$. Therefore

$$
z=(1-t_0)z_-+t_0z_+
$$

is the required [zero on a line segment](../../../../../../zero-on-a-line-segment.md).
