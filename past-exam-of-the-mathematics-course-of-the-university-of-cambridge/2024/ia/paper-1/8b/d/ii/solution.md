<h1 id="8b/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let

$$
U=(e_3,e_1,e_2)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&1\\
1&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This permutation [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) is unitary. The actions

$$
Ae_3=e_3,\qquad
Ae_1=2e_3+4e_1,\qquad
Ae_2=3e_3+5e_1+6e_2
$$

give

$$
\boxed{
U^\dagger AU=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3\\
0&4&5\\
0&0&6
\end{pmatrix}},
$$

which is upper triangular.
