↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A complex [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $U$ is [unitary matrix](../../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) when

$$
U^\dagger U=UU^\dagger=I.
$$

If $Uv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, unitarity preserves norms, so

$$
\|v\|=\|Uv\|=\|\lambda v\|=|\lambda|\,\|v\|.
$$

Therefore every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{|\lambda|=1}.
$$
