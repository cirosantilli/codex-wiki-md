The [adjoint matrix](../../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of $A$ is its conjugate transpose:

$$
\boxed{A^\dagger=\overline A^T},
\qquad
(A^\dagger)_{ij}=\overline{A_{ji}}.
$$
