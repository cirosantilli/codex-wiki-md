↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose $A$ is diagonalized by a unitary [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md):

$$
A=UDU^\dagger,
$$

where $D$ is diagonal. Then

$$
A^\dagger=UD^\dagger U^\dagger.
$$

Since the diagonal [matrices](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $D$ and $D^\dagger$ commute,

$$
AA^\dagger=UDD^\dagger U^\dagger
=UD^\dagger DU^\dagger=A^\dagger A.
$$

**Thus $A$ is a [normal matrix](../../../../../../../normal-matrix.md).**
