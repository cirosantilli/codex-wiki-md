<h1 id="8b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$ has degree $n$. By the [fundamental theorem of algebra](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md), it has a complex root $\lambda$. Hence

$$
\det(\lambda I-A)=0,
$$

so $\lambda I-A$ has a nonzero kernel. Any nonzero $v$ in that kernel satisfies

$$
Av=\lambda v.
$$

**Thus $\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and $v$ is a corresponding [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md).**
