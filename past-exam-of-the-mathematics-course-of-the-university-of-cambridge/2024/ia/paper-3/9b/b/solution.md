<h1 id="9b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For any rotation $R$ about the centre, $\phi_R(x)=\phi(Rx)$ satisfies the same equation and the same Dirichlet data because $V,f,g$ are rotationally invariant. Uniqueness from part (a) gives $\phi_R=\phi$ for every $R$. Thus $\phi$ is constant on spheres and is a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of $r$ alone.
