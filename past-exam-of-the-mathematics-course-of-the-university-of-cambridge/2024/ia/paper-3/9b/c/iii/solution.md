<h1 id="9b/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Regularity at zero gives

$$
r^2\phi'(r)=\int_0^r\frac{s^2}{1+s^2},ds
=r-\arctan r.
$$

Integrating once more,

$$
\phi(r)=\frac12\log(1+r^2)+\frac{\arctan r}{r}+C,
$$

where $\arctan r/r$ has removable [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) one at zero. At $r=1$, the nonconstant terms sum to $\tfrac12\log2+\tfrac\pi4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{
\phi(r)=\frac12\log(1+r^2)+\frac{\arctan r}{r}
-1-\frac12\log2-\frac\pi4}.
$$
