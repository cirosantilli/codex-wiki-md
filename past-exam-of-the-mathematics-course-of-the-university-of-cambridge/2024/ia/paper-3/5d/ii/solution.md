<h1 id="5d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The conjugacy classes in $A_5$ consist of the identity, the $15$ double transpositions, the $20$ three-cycles, and two classes of $12$ five-cycles. Thus their sizes are

$$
1,\quad15,\quad20,\quad12,\quad12.
$$

A normal [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) is a union of conjugacy classes containing the identity, and its order must divide $60$ by Lagrange's theorem. Checking sums of a subset of $15,20,12,12$ shows that no number

$$
1+\text{a nonempty proper subset sum}
$$

divides $60$. Hence the only possible normal-subgroup orders are $1$ and $60$. This proves the [Simplicity of the alternating group A5](../../../../../../simplicity-of-the-alternating-group-a5.md).
