<h1 id="3b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $F=(x,y,z)$,

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot F=1+1+1=3}
$$

and all cross-partial terms in the curl vanish, so

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times F=0}.
$$
