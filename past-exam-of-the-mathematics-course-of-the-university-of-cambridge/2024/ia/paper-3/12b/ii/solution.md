↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A tensor is [totally antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../../totally-antisymmetric-tensor.md) if interchanging any two indices changes its sign. Consequently every component with repeated indices vanishes. In $\mathbb R^3$:

- for $n=2$, the general tensor is $A_{ij}=a_k\epsilon_{kij}$, with three free components;
- for $n=3$, it is $A_{ijk}=a\epsilon_{ijk}$;
- for $n>3$, every component repeats an index, so the tensor is zero.

These are all possibilities.
