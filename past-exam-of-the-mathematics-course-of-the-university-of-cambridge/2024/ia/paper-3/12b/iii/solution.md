↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A tensor is [isotropic tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) if its components are unchanged under every proper orthogonal change of [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). The general isotropic rank-four tensor is

$$
\boxed{T_{ijkl}
=a\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}
+b\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}
+c\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}},
$$

where $a,b,c$ are [scalars](../../../../../../scalar.md).
