Similarly,

$$
\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times A)
=\partial_i(\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_jA_k)
=\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_i\partial_jA_k=0,
$$

since the last [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are symmetric in $i,j$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times A)=0}.
$$
