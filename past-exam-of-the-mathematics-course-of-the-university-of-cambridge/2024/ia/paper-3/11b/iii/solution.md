<h1 id="11b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states that for an oriented smooth surface $S$ with positively oriented boundary $\partial S$,

$$
\int_S(\nabla\times F)\cdot n\,dS
=\oint_{\partial S}F\cdot dr.
$$

A field is [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) if its line [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) is path-independent, equivalently if every closed line [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) vanishes. If $F=\nabla\phi$, the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) gives

$$
\int_\gamma F\cdot dr=\phi(\gamma(1))-\phi(\gamma(0)).
$$

This is zero for every closed curve, so every globally defined [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) field is conservative.
