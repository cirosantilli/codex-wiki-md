<h1 id="11b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In components,

$$
[\nabla\times\nabla\phi]_i
=\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_j\partial_k\phi=0,
$$

because the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is antisymmetric in $j,k$ while equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) makes $\partial_j\partial_k\phi$ symmetric. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\nabla\phi=0}.
$$
