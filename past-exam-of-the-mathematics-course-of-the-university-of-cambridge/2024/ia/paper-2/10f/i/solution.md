<h1 id="10f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $n$ possible matching events are independent, each with probability $1/n$. Hence

$$
\boxed{p_n=1-\left(1-\frac1n\right)^n},
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}p_n=1-e^{-1}}.
$$
