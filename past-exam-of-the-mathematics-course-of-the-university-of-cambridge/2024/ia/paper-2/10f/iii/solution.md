Using the mean $1/p$ of a [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) and the decomposition in part (ii),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(T_n)
=\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{n}{j}
=n\sum_{j=1}^n\frac1j
=nH_n}.
$$
