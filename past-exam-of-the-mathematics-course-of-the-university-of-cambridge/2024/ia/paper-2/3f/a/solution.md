↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-inequality.md) states that for a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Y$ and $a>0$,

$$
\Pr(Y\geq a)\leq\frac{\mathbb EY}{a}.
$$

For $t>0$, the event $X\geq x$ is the same as $e^{tX}\geq e^{tx}$. Applying Markov's inequality to the nonnegative variable $e^{tX}$ gives the [exponential Markov bound](../../../../../../exponential-markov-bound.md)

$$
\boxed{\Pr(X\geq x)\leq e^{-tx}M_X(t)}.
$$
