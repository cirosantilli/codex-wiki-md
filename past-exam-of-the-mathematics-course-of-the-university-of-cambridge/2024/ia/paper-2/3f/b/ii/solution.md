↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The exponential Markov bound gives, for every $t>0$,

$$
\Pr(S\geq2n)
\leq\exp\{-2nt+n(e^t-1)\}.
$$

The exponent is minimized when $e^t=2$, so take $t=\log2$. Then

$$
\Pr(S\geq2n)
\leq\exp\{n(1-2\log2)\}
=\boxed{\left(\frac e4\right)^n}.
$$
