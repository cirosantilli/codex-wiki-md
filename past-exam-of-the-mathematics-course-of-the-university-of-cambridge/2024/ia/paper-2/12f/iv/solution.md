<h1 id="12f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $N=0$, then $|N-\mathbb EN|=\mathbb EN$. Hence the [Chebyshev inequality](../../../../../../chebyshev-inequality.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(N=0)
\leq
\mathbb P\bigl(|N-\mathbb EN|\geq\mathbb EN\bigr)
\leq
\frac{\operatorname{var}(N)}{(\mathbb EN)^2}.
$$

The assumed [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) therefore proves

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N=0)\longrightarrow0}.
$$

This is the [second moment method](../../../../../../second-moment-method.md) in its simplest form.
