↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Setting $t=0$ in part (i) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(K=k)=\frac{q_k}{Q}}.
$$

Also

$$
\mathbb P(T\geq t)
=\prod_{j=1}^ne^{-q_jt}
=e^{-Qt},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{T\sim\operatorname{Exp}(Q)}.
$$

Part (i) now factors as

$$
\mathbb P(K=k,T\geq t)
=\mathbb P(K=k)\mathbb P(T\geq t),
$$

which proves that $K$ and $T$ are independent.
