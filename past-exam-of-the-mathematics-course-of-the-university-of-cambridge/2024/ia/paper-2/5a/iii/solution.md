<h1 id="5a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take

$$
q_1(x)=1,\qquad
\varphi_1(x)=\sin(x+1).
$$

Its consecutive zeros are $-1$ and $\pi-1$. For the stated equation,

$$
q_2(x)=2+\cos3x\geq1=q_1(x),
$$

and $q_2$ is not identically equal to $1$ on the interval. The [Sturm comparison theorem](../../../../../../sturm-comparison-theorem.md) therefore shows that every nontrivial solution has at least one zero in

$$
\boxed{[-1,\pi-1]}.
$$
