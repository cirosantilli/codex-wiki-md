↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The preceding representation and the [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) give

$$
\frac{B_n-np}{\sqrt{npq}}
\ \xrightarrow{\ d\ }\ N(0,1).
$$

Since

$$
S_n-n(p-q)=2(B_n-np),
$$

one may take

$$
\boxed{b_n=n(p-q),\qquad c_n=2\sqrt{npq}}.
$$

For $0<p<1$ this gives the required convergence to $\Phi(x)$ at every real $x$.
