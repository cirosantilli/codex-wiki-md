<h1 id="8a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a stationary gas with constant phase, $\psi=a(x)$ is real and time independent. The field equation reduces to

$$
\boxed{a''+(1-a^2)a=0}.
$$

A positive constant solution obeys $d(1-d^2)=0$, so

$$
\boxed{d=1}.
$$
