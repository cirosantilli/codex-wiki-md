<h1 id="2a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $x=y=0$ in the functional equation:

$$
f(0)=\frac{2f(0)}{1-f(0)^2}.
$$

The denominator is defined. If $f(0)\ne0$, cancellation would give $f(0)^2=-1$, impossible for a real-valued [function](../../../../../../function-split.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{f(0)=0}.
$$
