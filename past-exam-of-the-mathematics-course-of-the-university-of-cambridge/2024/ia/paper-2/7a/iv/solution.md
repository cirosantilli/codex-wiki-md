<h1 id="7a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the modified system, $F=x^2+y^2$ satisfies

$$
\dot F=2F-4F^2=2F(1-2F).
$$

With $F(0)=1$, the logistic solution is

$$
\boxed{x(t)^2+y(t)^2
=\frac1{2-e^{-2t}}}.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\lim_{t\to\infty}(x(t)^2+y(t)^2)=\frac12}.
$$
