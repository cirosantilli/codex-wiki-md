<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This is the [Gibbs--Bogoliubov--Feynman inequality](../../../../../../gibbs-bogoliubov-feynman-inequality.md). In units with $k_BT=1$, $H$ is the exact dimensionless Hamiltonian, here the full interacting free-energy functional, and $H_0$ is a freely chosen trial Hamiltonian. Its [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) and free energy are

$$
Z_0=\int\mathcal D\phi\,e^{-H_0[\phi]},
\qquad
F_0=-\log Z_0.
$$

The notation

$$
\langle X\rangle_0
=Z_0^{-1}\int\mathcal D\phi\,X[\phi]e^{-H_0[\phi]}
$$

means an expectation in the trial Gibbs ensemble. The bound is therefore

$$
\boxed{F=-\log\int\mathcal D\phi\,e^{-H}
\leq F_0+\langle H-H_0\rangle_0.}
$$
