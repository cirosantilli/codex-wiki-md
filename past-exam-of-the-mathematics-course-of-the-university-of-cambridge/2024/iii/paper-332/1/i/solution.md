<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the given pressure-dependent ratio of [permeability of a porous medium](../../../../../../permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) to sponge thickness as

$$
\frac{k(p)}{h(p)}=Kp^{-\beta},
$$

where $K$ is constant. The [fluid pressure](../../../../../../fluid-pressure.md) drop across the sponge is the [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) of the current,

$$
p=\rho gH,
$$

because the air pressures above and below are equal. Therefore [Darcy's law](../../../../../../darcy-law.md) gives the downward [volume flux](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit horizontal area as

$$
w=\frac{k(p)}{\mu h(p)}p
=\frac K\mu p^{1-\beta}
=\boxed{D H^{1-\beta}},
\qquad
D=\frac K\mu(\rho g)^{1-\beta}.
$$

**Thus the sponge drainage obeys a [power law](../../../../../../power-law.md) in the current thickness, as described by [pressure-dependent Darcy drainage](../../../../../../pressure-dependent-darcy-drainage.md).**
