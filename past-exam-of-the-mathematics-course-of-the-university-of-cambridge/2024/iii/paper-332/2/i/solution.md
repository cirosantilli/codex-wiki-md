<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [density anomaly of water](../../../../../../density-anomaly-of-water.md) makes [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) increase with temperature up to $T_m$ and decrease above $T_m$. Since temperature increases with depth in the stated layer, the density profile first increases to its maximum $\rho_m$ at the $T=T_m$ isotherm and then decreases toward the warm bottom.

The upper region has denser water below lighter water and hence [stable density stratification](../../../../../../stable-density-stratification.md). Below the density maximum, density decreases downward, so lighter water lies beneath denser water and gives [unstable density stratification](../../../../../../unstable-density-stratification.md). The lower region therefore overturns by [thermal convection](../../../../../../thermal-convection.md), while the cold upper region remains a stagnant cap. This coexistence is [penetrative convection](../../../../../../penetrative-convection.md).
