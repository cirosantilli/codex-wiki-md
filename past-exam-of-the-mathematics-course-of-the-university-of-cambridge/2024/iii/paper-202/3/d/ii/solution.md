<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No such function exists.** Continuity on the compact closed unit disk makes $u$ bounded near the origin. The [removable singularity for a bounded harmonic function](../../../../../../../removable-singularity-for-a-bounded-harmonic-function.md) extends $u$ harmonically across the origin. The extended function is continuous on the closed disk and vanishes on its boundary, so the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../../../maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md), applied to both $u$ and $-u$, forces $u=0$ throughout the disk. This contradicts $u(0,0)=1$.
