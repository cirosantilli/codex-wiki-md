<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function $z\mapsto\log|z|$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) on the [annulus](../../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md) $r_1<|z|<r_2$. The [Itô formula](../../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) therefore makes $\log|B_{t\wedge\tau}|$ a bounded [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md). The [optional sampling theorem for a supermartingale](../../../../../../../optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) gives

$$
\log r=\mathbb E[\log|B_\tau|]=p\log r_1+(1-p)\log r_2,
$$

where $p=\mathbb P(|B_\tau|=r_1)$. Solving this [linear equation](../../../../../../../linear-equation.md) yields the [planar Brownian annulus hitting probability](../../../../../../../planar-brownian-annulus-hitting-probability.md)

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{\log r_2-\log r}{\log r_2-\log r_1}.}
$$
