↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Kunita-Watanabe inequality](../../../../../../../kunita-watanabe-inequality.md) applied to the [continuous local martingales](../../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) $M,N$ gives directly

$$
V_t([M,N])\leq[M]_t^{1/2}[N]_t^{1/2}.
$$

Equivalently, with the clock and densities from part (i), positivity of the covariance matrix gives $|c|\leq\sqrt{ab}$, and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V_t=\int_0^t|c|dC\leq\int_0^t\sqrt{ab}\,dC\leq\left(\int_0^ta\,dC\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_0^tb\,dC\right)^{1/2}=[M]_t^{1/2}[N]_t^{1/2}.}
$$
