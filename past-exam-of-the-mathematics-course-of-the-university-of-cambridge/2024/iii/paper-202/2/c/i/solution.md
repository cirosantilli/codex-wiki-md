↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [martingale product identity](../../../../../../../martingale-product-identity.md) says that $N_tK_t-[N,K]_t$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md). Passing to the terminal values of the square-integrable martingales and using $N_0=K_0=0$ gives

$$
\mathbb E[N_\infty K_\infty]=\mathbb E[N,K]_\infty.
$$

The [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md) identity for a [stochastic integral](../../../../../../../stochastic-integral.md) is

$$
[H\mathbin\cdot M,K]_t=\int_0^tH_s\,d[M,K]_s.
$$

Applying the same product identity to $H\mathbin\cdot M$ and $K$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[(H\mathbin\cdot M)_\infty K_\infty]=\mathbb E[H\mathbin\cdot M,K]_\infty.}
$$
