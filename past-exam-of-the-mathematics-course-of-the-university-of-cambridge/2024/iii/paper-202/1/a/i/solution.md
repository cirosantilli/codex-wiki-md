↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The integrand $\operatorname{sign}(\beta_s)$ is a bounded [previsible process](../../../../../../../predictable-process.md), so $B$ is a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md). The [quadratic variation of a stochastic integral](../../../../../../../quadratic-variation-of-a-stochastic-integral.md) is

$$
[B]_t=\int_0^t\operatorname{sign}(\beta_s)^2ds=t,
$$

because the [Brownian zero set](../../../../../../../brownian-zero-set.md) has zero [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md). Since $B_0=0$, the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../../../levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) shows that $B$ is a standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md).
