<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Because independent [Brownian motions](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) have zero [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md), the [Itô product rule](../../../../../../ito-product-rule.md) gives

$$
d(B_t^1B_t^2)=B_t^1\,dB_t^2+B_t^2\,dB_t^1.
$$

After integration, the random variable in the question is $B_t^1B_t^2$. Writing $B_t^j=\sqrt t\,Z_j$ for independent standard [Gaussian random variables](../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md) $Z_1,Z_2$, its distribution is

$$
tZ_1Z_2,
$$

the scaled [product of two independent standard normal random variables](../../../../../../product-of-two-independent-standard-normal-random-variables.md).
