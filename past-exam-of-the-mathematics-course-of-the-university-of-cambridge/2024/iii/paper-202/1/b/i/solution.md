The [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](../../../../../../../dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) states that if $M$ is a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) with $M_0=0$ and $[M]_\infty=\infty$, then, for

$$
\tau_s=\inf\{t\geq0:[M]_t>s\},
$$

the process $W_s=M_{\tau_s}$ is a standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and $M_t=W_{[M]_t}$. If $[M]_\infty<\infty$, one obtains the same representation after enlarging the [probability space](../../../../../../../probability-space.md) and continuing $W$ independently beyond $[M]_\infty$.
