↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $M_t=\int_0^tH_s\,dB_s$. Its [quadratic variation](../../../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) is $[M]_t=\int_0^tH_s^2ds$, which is continuous and tends to infinity almost surely by assumption. The stated [stopping time](../../../../../../../stopping-time.md) is the inverse clock at level one, so $[M]_\tau=1$. The [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](../../../../../../../dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\tau H_s\,dB_s=M_\tau=W_{[M]_\tau}=W_1\sim N(0,1).}
$$
