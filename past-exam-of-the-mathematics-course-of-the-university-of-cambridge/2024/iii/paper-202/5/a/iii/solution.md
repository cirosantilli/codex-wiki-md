One continuous form of the [Girsanov theorem](../../../../../../../girsanov-theorem.md) is as follows. Let $M$ be a continuous local martingale under $P$, and suppose $Z=\mathcal E(M)$ is a true martingale on $[0,T]$. Define $Q$ by $dQ=Z_TdP$. Then every continuous $P$-local martingale $N$ becomes the continuous $Q$-local martingale

$$
\widetilde N_t=N_t-[N,M]_t.
$$

In particular, if $M_t=\int_0^t\theta_s\,dW_s$, then $\widetilde W_t=W_t-\int_0^t\theta_sds$ is a $Q$-Brownian motion.
