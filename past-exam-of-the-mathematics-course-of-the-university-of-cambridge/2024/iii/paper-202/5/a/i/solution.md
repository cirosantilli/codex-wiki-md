↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The relation $P\ll\widetilde P$ is an instance of [absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md): it means that every $\widetilde P$-null [event](../../../../../../../event.md) is also $P$-null,

$$
\widetilde P(A)=0\implies P(A)=0.
$$

By the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md), this is equivalent to the existence of a nonnegative [Radon-Nikodym derivative](../../../../../../../radon-nikodym-derivative.md) $dP/d\widetilde P$ whose $\widetilde P$-expectation is one.
