↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False for a general continuous $f$.** For example, take $f(t)=t^{1/3}$. The [Brownian Hölder regularity](../../../../../../../brownian-holder-regularity.md) gives $B_t/t^{1/3}\to0$ almost surely as $t\downarrow0$, while

$$
\frac{B_t+f(t)}{t^{1/3}}\longrightarrow1
$$

almost surely. The original and translated path laws therefore concentrate on disjoint measurable events and are [mutually singular measures](../../../../../../../mutually-singular-measures.md).

More generally, the [Cameron-Martin theorem](../../../../../../../cameron-martin-theorem.md) gives the exact criterion: translation by $f$ is absolutely continuous precisely when $f$ is an [absolutely continuous function](../../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-function.md), $f(0)=0$, and $f'\in L^2[0,1]$.
