↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Since $f\in C^1[0,1]$, it lies in the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](../../../../../../../cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md). The [Cameron-Martin theorem](../../../../../../../cameron-martin-theorem.md) says that the translated law is equivalent, and in particular absolutely continuous, with respect to [Wiener measure](../../../../../../../wiener-measure.md). Its [Radon-Nikodym derivative](../../../../../../../radon-nikodym-derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\exp\!\left(\int_0^1f'(s)dB_s-\frac12\int_0^1f'(s)^2ds\right).}
$$
