<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Over $K=\mathbb Q_p$, consider the two [Lubin–Tate series](../../../../../../lubin-tate-series.md)

$$
f(X)=(1+X)^p-1,
\qquad
g(X)=X^p+pX.
$$

Both have linear term $pX$ and reduce to $X^p$ modulo $p$. The nonzero roots of $f$ are $\zeta_p^i-1$, while the nonzero roots of $g$ satisfy

$$
X^{p-1}=-p.
$$

The [Lubin–Tate change of series](../../../../../../lubin-tate-change-of-series.md) $h:\mathcal F_f\to\mathcal F_g$ and its inverse have coefficients in $\mathbb Z_p$ and converge on the maximal ideal. They therefore give mutually inverse bijections between the first torsion sets without changing the fields generated by them. Hence the [first Lubin–Tate torsion fields for the p-adic numbers](../../../../../../first-lubin-tate-torsion-fields-for-the-p-adic-numbers.md) satisfy

$$
\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p-1)
=\mathbb Q_p\left(\sqrt[p-1]{-p}\right).
$$

Since $\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p-1)=\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)$, the required equality follows.
