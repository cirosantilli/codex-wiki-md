<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $\Delta\mathbf u=(U,W,0)$. The background shear gives

$$
\mathbf u_0\mathbin\cdot\nabla\mathbf u_0
=-\frac32\Omega_KU\mathbf e_y.
$$

The constant radial and azimuthal components of the dust equation are therefore

$$
2\Omega_KW-\frac U\tau=0,
\qquad
\frac12\Omega_KU+\frac{W-V}{\tau}=0.
$$

With the [Stokes number](../../../../../../../stokes-number.md) $\mathrm{St}=\tau\Omega_K$, their solution is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\mathbf u
=\frac{2\mathrm{St}\,V}{1+\mathrm{St}^2}\mathbf e_x
+\frac{V}{1+\mathrm{St}^2}\mathbf e_y}.
$$

The gas is slower than a Keplerian particle, so the particle feels an aerodynamic headwind. [Drag force](../../../../../../../drag-physics.md) removes its [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), and it drifts radially inward because $U<0$. Its radial speed is

$$
|U|=2|V|\frac{\mathrm{St}}{1+\mathrm{St}^2}.
$$

Differentiation with respect to $\mathrm{St}>0$ shows that the unique maximum occurs at

$$
\boxed{\tau\Omega_K=1},
$$

where $|U|=|V|$.
