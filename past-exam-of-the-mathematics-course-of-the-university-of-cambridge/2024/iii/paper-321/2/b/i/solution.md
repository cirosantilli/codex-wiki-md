<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The linear term $-(3/2)\Omega_Kx\mathbf e_y$ is the local [Keplerian shear](../../../../../../../keplerian-shear.md) in the [shearing sheet](../../../../../../../shearing-sheet.md). The remaining constant

$$
V=r_0[\Omega(r_0)-\Omega_K(r_0)]
$$

is the gas's azimuthal velocity relative to the local Keplerian frame. The outwardly decreasing midplane pressure found in part (a)(iii) makes the gas [Sub-Keplerian](../../../../../../../sub-keplerian-rotation.md), so

$$
\boxed{V<0},
\qquad
|V|\sim\frac{c_s^2}{r_0\Omega_K}
\sim\Omega_Kr_0\left(\frac H{r_0}\right)^2.
$$
