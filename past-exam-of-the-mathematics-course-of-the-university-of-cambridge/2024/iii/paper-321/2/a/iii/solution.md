<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the midplane, radial [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives

$$
r\Omega^2=\frac{GM_\star}{r^2}
+\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{dP_0}{dr}
=r\Omega_K^2+\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{dP_0}{dr}.
$$

If $dP_0/dr<0$, then $\Omega<\Omega_K$: the pressure gradient provides part of the inward force and the gas is [Sub-Keplerian](../../../../../../../sub-keplerian-rotation.md). For radial pressure scale comparable to $r$,

$$
\Omega_K^2-\Omega^2
\sim\frac{c_s^2}{r^2}.
$$

Using $H\sim c_s/\Omega_K$ and $\Omega_K-\Omega\simeq(\Omega_K^2-\Omega^2)/(2\Omega_K)$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Omega_K-\Omega}{\Omega_K}
\sim\left(\frac Hr\right)^2}.
$$
