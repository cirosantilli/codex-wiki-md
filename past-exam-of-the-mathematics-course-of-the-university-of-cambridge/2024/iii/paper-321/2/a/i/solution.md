<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $|z|\ll r$, the [gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) has the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md)

$$
\Phi(r,z)=-\frac{GM_\star}{r}
+\frac{GM_\star z^2}{2r^3}+O(z^4/r^5).
$$

Hence the vertical gravity is $-\partial_z\Phi\simeq-\Omega_K^2z$, where $\Omega_K^2=GM_\star/r^3$. Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) balances a pressure gradient of order $P/H$ against $\rho\Omega_K^2H$. Since $c_s^2\sim P/\rho$,

$$
\boxed{H\sim\frac{c_s}{\Omega_K}\simeq\frac{c_s}{\Omega}}.
$$

The [thin disk](../../../../../../../thin-disk.md) condition $H/r\ll1$ is therefore equivalent to a highly supersonic orbital speed.
