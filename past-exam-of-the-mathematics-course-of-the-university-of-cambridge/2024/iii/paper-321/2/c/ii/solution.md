<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the forcing $e^{-i\omega t}$,

$$
\mathcal D=-i(\omega-\mathbf k\mathbin\cdot\Delta\mathbf u).
$$

The other three factors have roots with real decay rate $-1/\tau$, so a real-frequency gas wave can resonate only with the undamped factor $\mathcal D$. The [resonance condition](../../../../../../../resonance-condition.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega=\mathbf k\mathbin\cdot\Delta\mathbf u}.
$$

At resonance, $\mathcal D$ annihilates the forcing, so a particular solution acquires one power of time:

$$
\sigma'(t)=Ct\,e^{-i\omega t}+O(1),
\qquad
\boxed{|\sigma'(t)|\sim|C|t}.
$$

This is a [streaming instability resonance](../../../../../../../streaming-instability-resonance.md): the gas wave's [phase velocity](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) along $\mathbf k$ matches the dust drift projected along the same wavevector.
