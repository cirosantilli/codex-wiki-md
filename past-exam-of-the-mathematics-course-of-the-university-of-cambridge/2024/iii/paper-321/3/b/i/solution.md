<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $u_x=0$ and $u_y=u_y(x)$, the material acceleration vanishes. The radial component of force balance is

$$
0=2\Omega u_y-c_s^2\frac{d\sigma}{dx}+3\Omega^2x.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{u_y(x)=-\frac32\Omega x
+\frac{c_s^2}{2\Omega}\frac{d\sigma}{dx}}.
$$

The first term is the background [Keplerian shear](../../../../../../../keplerian-shear.md); the second is the [geostrophic balance](../../../../../../../geostrophic-balance.md) correction produced by the surface-density gradient.
