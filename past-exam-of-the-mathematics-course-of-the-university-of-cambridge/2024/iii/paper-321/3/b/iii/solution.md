<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Linearizing $e^\sigma=1+\sigma+O(\sigma^2)$ gives

$$
\sigma''-\sigma=0\quad(|\xi|>d),
\qquad
\sigma''+1=0\quad(|\xi|<d).
$$

The prescribed $q$ and the decay conditions are even in $\xi$, so the localized solution is even. Continuity of $\sigma$ and $\sigma'$ at $\xi=\pm d$ determines all constants:

$$
\boxed{
\sigma(\xi)=
\begin{cases}
d+\dfrac{d^2}{2}-\dfrac{\xi^2}{2},&|\xi|\leq d,\\
d\exp(d-|\xi|),&|\xi|\geq d.
\end{cases}}
$$

Thus $\Sigma=\Sigma_0e^\sigma\simeq\Sigma_0(1+\sigma)$ is a symmetric density bump: it has a concave parabolic core, reaches its maximum at $\xi=0$, and has exponential tails approaching $\Sigma_0$. The [linearization](../../../../../../../linearization.md) is self-consistent when $d+d^2/2\ll1$.
