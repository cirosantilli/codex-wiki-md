<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
W=2\Omega+(\nabla\times\mathbf u)\mathbin\cdot\mathbf e_z
$$

be the vertical [absolute vorticity](../../../../../../absolute-vorticity.md). Taking the vertical curl of the momentum equation removes both the tidal potential and the isothermal pressure force, because each is a [gradient field](../../../../../../gradient-field.md). The two-dimensional [vorticity equation](../../../../../../vorticity-equation.md) is

$$
D_tW=-W\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u.
$$

The surface-density equation is

$$
D_t\Sigma=-\Sigma\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u.
$$

The [quotient rule](../../../../../../quotient-rule.md) now gives

$$
D_t\left(\frac W\Sigma\right)
=\frac{D_tW}{\Sigma}-\frac W{\Sigma^2}D_t\Sigma=0.
$$

Since $\zeta=W/\Sigma$ is the [vortensity](../../../../../../vortensity.md),

$$
\boxed{D_t\zeta=0}.
$$
