<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\delta=\Sigma'/\Sigma_0$ and define the derivative following the background [Keplerian shear](../../../../../../../keplerian-shear.md) by

$$
\mathcal D_0=\partial_t-\frac32\Omega x\partial_y.
$$

The linearized continuity and momentum equations are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal D_0\delta+\partial_xu_x'+\partial_yu_y'=0},
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathcal D_0u_x'-2\Omega u_y'=-c_s^2\partial_x\delta},
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathcal D_0u_y'+\frac12\Omega u_x'=-c_s^2\partial_y\delta}.
$$

The coefficient $\Omega/2$ combines the background shear with the [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md).
