<h1 id="3/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

When $k_y=0$, $k_x$ is constant and the system becomes

$$
\dot{\widetilde u}_x
=2\left(\Omega+\frac{c_s^2k_x^2}{\Omega}\right)\widetilde u_y,
\qquad
\dot{\widetilde u}_y=-\frac\Omega2\widetilde u_x.
$$

Thus

$$
\ddot{\widetilde u}_x
+(\Omega^2+c_s^2k_x^2)\widetilde u_x=0.
$$

For time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\Omega^2+c_s^2k_x^2}.
$$

Explicitly,

$$
\widetilde u_x=C_1\cos(\omega t)+C_2\sin(\omega t),
\qquad
\widetilde u_y=
\frac{\dot{\widetilde u}_x}
{2(\Omega+c_s^2k_x^2/\Omega)},
$$

and $\widetilde\delta=2ik_x\widetilde u_y/\Omega$. This is an axisymmetric [inertial-acoustic wave](../../../../../../../inertial-acoustic-wave.md): [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) supplies the $c_s^2k_x^2$ restoring term and [epicyclic motion](../../../../../../../epicyclic-motion.md) supplies the $\Omega^2$ term.
