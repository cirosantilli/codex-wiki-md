<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $y=\nu\Sigma$. A steady inward [accretion rate](../../../../../../../accretion-rate.md) has $F_M=-\dot M$, so the integrated angular-momentum equation is

$$
\partial_r\mathcal G-r\mathcal T=\dot M\frac{dh}{dr}.
$$

For $\beta=0$, $\mathcal T=0$ and therefore

$$
\mathcal G=\dot M(h-h_{\rm in})
$$

after imposing the [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) at $r_{\rm in}$. In a [Keplerian accretion disk](../../../../../../../keplerian-accretion-disk.md), $h\propto r^{1/2}$ and $\mathcal G=3\pi hy$, so

$$
\boxed{\nu\Sigma
=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi}
\left[1-\left(\frac{r_{\rm in}}r\right)^{1/2}\right]}.
$$
