<h1 id="3/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write

$$
a=e^{\lambda_1t},
\qquad
b=\frac{e^{\lambda_1t}-e^{\lambda_2t}}
{\lambda_1-\lambda_2},
\qquad
c=e^{\lambda_2t}.
$$

The extremal squared amplification factors are the largest and smallest [singular values](../../../../../../../singular-value.md) of $A$ squared, equivalently the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $G$ of

$$
A^TA=\begin{pmatrix}a^2+b^2&bc\\bc&c^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Since its trace is $a^2+b^2+c^2$ and its determinant is $a^2c^2$, the requested quadratic is

$$
\boxed{
G^2-(a^2+b^2+c^2)G+a^2c^2=0}.
$$
