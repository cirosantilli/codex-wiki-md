<h1 id="1/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The displaced sheet is the material surface $r=R_0+\xi(\theta,t)$. Its [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) equates the radial velocity on each side to the material velocity of the sheet. To linear order,

$$
\boxed{
\xi_t=\left.\frac{\partial\phi_1}{\partial r}\right|_{R_0}},
$$



$$
\boxed{
\xi_t+\Omega_0\xi_\theta
=\left.\frac{\partial\phi_2}{\partial r}\right|_{R_0}},
\qquad
\Omega_0=\frac{\Gamma_0}{R_0^2}.
$$

For a mode $e^{\sigma t+im\theta}$ these become

$$
\boxed{\sigma\xi=\phi_1'(R_0),
\qquad
(\sigma+im\Omega_0)\xi=\phi_2'(R_0).}
$$
