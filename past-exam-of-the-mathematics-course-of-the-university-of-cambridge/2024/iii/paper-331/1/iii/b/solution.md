<h1 id="1/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The inner base flow is at rest, so its linearized [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) gives $p_1'=-\sigma\phi_1$. The outer base potential is $\Gamma_0\theta$, hence its cross term with the perturbation gives

$$
p_2'=-\sigma\phi_2
-\frac{im\Gamma_0}{R_0^2}\phi_2.
$$

The base outer pressure satisfies $dp_{20}/dr=\Gamma_0^2/r^3$, whereas the inner base pressure is constant. Expanding [pressure continuity](../../../../../../../pressure-continuity.md) on the displaced sheet therefore gives

$$
p_1'-p_2'-\frac{\Gamma_0^2}{R_0^3}\xi=0.
$$

After multiplication by $-1$ this is

$$
\boxed{
\sigma(\phi_1-\phi_2)
-\frac{im\Gamma_0}{R_0^2}\phi_2
+\frac{\Gamma_0^2}{R_0^3}\xi=0}.
$$

An additive function of time in Bernoulli appears as the stated constant; it vanishes for every nonaxisymmetric Fourier mode.
