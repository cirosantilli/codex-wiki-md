<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $S(\alpha)=\sum_yQ(y)^\alpha$. Both $\log S(\alpha)$ and $1-\alpha$ vanish at one. By [L'Hopital rule](../../../../../../l-hopital-s-rule.md), with logarithms to base two,

$$
\lim_{\alpha\to1}H_\alpha(Y)
=-\left.\frac{d}{d\alpha}\log_2S(\alpha)\right|_{\alpha=1}
=-\sum_yQ(y)\log_2Q(y)
=H(Y).
$$

Terms with $Q(y)=0$ contribute zero by continuity.
