<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This symmetric finite-rank [integral operator](../../../../../../../integral-operator.md) is not positive. For $f(t)=1-\tfrac32t$, set $A_j=\int_0^1t^jf(t)dt$. Directly,

$$
A_0=\frac14,
\qquad
A_1=0,
\qquad
A_2=-\frac1{24}.
$$

The associated [quadratic form](../../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) is

$$
\langle Uf,f\rangle
=\int_0^1\!\int_0^1(s+t)^2f(s)f(t)dsdt
=2A_0A_2+2A_1^2
=-\frac1{48}<0.
$$

Every [covariance operator](../../../../../../../covariance-operator.md) is a [positive operator](../../../../../../../positive-operator.md), so this operator is not a covariance operator.
