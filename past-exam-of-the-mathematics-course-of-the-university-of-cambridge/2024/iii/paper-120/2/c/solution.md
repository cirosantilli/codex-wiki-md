<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $p\neq q$, completeness gives a formula $\varphi$ with $\varphi\in p$ and $\neg\varphi\in q$. Then

$$
p\in[\varphi],\qquad q\in[\neg\varphi],
$$

and these two disjoint open sets cover $S_n^M(X)$. This proves the [total disconnectedness of a type space](../../../../../../total-disconnectedness-of-a-type-space.md).
