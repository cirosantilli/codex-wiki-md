<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write the [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski model](../../../../../../ehrenfeucht-mostowski-model.md) as the Skolem hull of its order-indiscernible skeleton $(a_i)_{i\in\eta}$. Every element is $t(a_{i_1},\ldots,a_{i_r})$ for a Skolem term $t$. Choose supports for the elements of $X$ and let $J\subseteq\eta$ be their union. Then

$$
|J|\leq |L|+|X|.
$$

When $\eta$ is well ordered, the type over $X$ of $t(a_{i_1},\ldots,a_{i_r})$ is determined by $t$ and the finite order pattern of the indices $i_k$ relative to $J$. There are at most $|L|$ terms and at most $|J|$ such finite patterns. Therefore the number of realized complete one-types is at most

$$
\boxed{|L|+|X|}.
$$
