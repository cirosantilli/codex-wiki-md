<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand $L$ to $L(M)$ by a constant $c_m$ for each $m\in M$. The [diagram of a structure](../../../../../../diagram-mathematical-logic.md) $\operatorname{Diag}(M)$ contains all atomic and negated atomic $L(M)$-sentences true in $M$. The elementary diagram $\operatorname{ElDiag}(M)$ contains every $L(M)$-sentence true in $M$.

The [method of diagrams](../../../../../../method-of-diagrams.md) combines one of these sets with another theory and applies the [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md). A model of $\operatorname{Diag}(M)$ yields an embedding of $M$ by $m\mapsto c_m$, while a model of $\operatorname{ElDiag}(M)$ yields an [elementary embedding](../../../../../../elementary-embedding.md).
