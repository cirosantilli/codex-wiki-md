<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By part (a), such a term would prove

$$
((\sigma\to\tau)\to\sigma)\to\sigma
$$

in intuitionistic propositional logic. Consider the two-world [Kripke model for intuitionistic propositional logic](../../../../../../kripke-model-for-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md) $r<s$. Let $\sigma$ hold only at $s$ and let $\tau$ hold nowhere. At both worlds $\sigma\to\tau$ fails, so $(\sigma\to\tau)\to\sigma$ holds at $r$ vacuously, while $\sigma$ does not hold at $r$. The displayed formula therefore fails at $r$. By the [Kripke completeness theorem for intuitionistic propositional logic](../../../../../../kripke-completeness-theorem-for-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md), it is not derivable, so no simply typed lambda term inhabits that type.
