↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The defining relation for $P_{\mathcal U}$ gives

$$
(f-P_{\mathcal U}f,v)=0
\qquad\forall v\in\mathcal U_n.
$$

Part (a) therefore shows that $P_{\mathcal U}f$ is the best approximation. Moreover, $P_{\mathcal U}f$ and $f-P_{\mathcal U}f$ are orthogonal, so

$$
\|f\|^2=\|P_{\mathcal U}f\|^2
+\|f-P_{\mathcal U}f\|^2.
$$

Thus [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) is a contraction:

$$
\boxed{\|P_{\mathcal U}f\|\leq\|f\|}.
$$
