<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $I=A+C\mu$, the angular moments are

$$
J=\frac1{4\pi}\int I\,d\Omega=A,
$$



$$
F=\int\mu I\,d\Omega=\frac{4\pi}{3}C,
$$

and

$$
P_r=\frac1c\int\mu^2I\,d\Omega
=\frac{4\pi}{3c}A=\frac{4\pi}{3c}J.
$$

Thus this angular form satisfies the [Eddington closure approximation](../../../../../../eddington-closure-approximation.md), $cP_r=4\pi J/3$, and

$$
\boxed{C=\frac{3F}{4\pi}}.
$$

Part (i) gives $S=J=A$. Substitution in $\mu\,dI/d\tau=I-S$ yields

$$
\mu(A'+C'\mu)=C\mu.
$$

Matching powers of $\mu$ gives $A'=C$ and $C'=0$, the latter being constant flux. Hence $A=C\tau+A_0$.

At the surface there is no incoming intensity. Applying this condition to the outgoing hemisphere in the moment approximation,

$$
F=2\pi\int_0^1(A_0+C\mu)\mu\,d\mu
=\pi A_0+\frac{2\pi C}{3},
$$

so $A_0=F/(2\pi)=2C/3$. Consequently

$$
A=\frac{3F}{4\pi}\left(\tau+\frac23\right).
$$

Now $A=J=j/\kappa=\sigma T^4/\pi$ and the [effective temperature](../../../../../../effective-temperature.md) is defined by $F=\sigma T_e^4$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{T^4=\frac34T_e^4\left(\tau+\frac23\right)}.
$$

At the top of the [grey atmosphere](../../../../../../grey-atmosphere.md),

$$
\boxed{T_0^4=\frac12T_e^4},
\qquad
\boxed{T_0=2^{-1/4}T_e}.
$$
