↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The one-dimensional [Donsker invariance principle](../../../../../../donsker-s-theorem.md) says that if $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ are [IID random variables](../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with mean zero and variance one, then the linearly interpolated process

$$
W_n(t)=\frac1{\sqrt n}\left(S_{\lfloor nt\rfloor}+(nt-\lfloor nt\rfloor)X_{\lfloor nt\rfloor+1}\right),
\qquad0\leq t\leq1,
$$

converges weakly in $C([0,1])$ with the uniform norm to standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md).
