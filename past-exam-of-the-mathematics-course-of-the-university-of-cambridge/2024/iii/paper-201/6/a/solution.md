The [martingale convergence theorem](../../../../../../martingale-convergence-theorem.md) states that a discrete-time martingale $(X_n)$ with [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) has an integrable random variable $X_\infty$ such that

$$
X_n\longrightarrow X_\infty
$$

almost surely and in $L^1$. Moreover, the martingale is closed by its limit:

$$
\boxed{X_n=\mathbb E[X_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n].}
$$
