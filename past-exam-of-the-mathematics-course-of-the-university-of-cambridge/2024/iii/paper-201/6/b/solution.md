<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For bounded stopping times $S\wedge n\leq T\wedge n$, the [optional sampling theorem for a supermartingale](../../../../../../optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E[X_{T\wedge n}]=\mathbb E[X_{S\wedge n}]=\mathbb E[X_0].
$$

A stopped family drawn from a uniformly integrable martingale is uniformly integrable. Since $X_{T\wedge n}\to X_T$ and $X_{S\wedge n}\to X_S$ almost surely, [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) upgrades both convergences to $L^1$. Passing to the limit yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X_T]=\mathbb E[X_S].}
$$
