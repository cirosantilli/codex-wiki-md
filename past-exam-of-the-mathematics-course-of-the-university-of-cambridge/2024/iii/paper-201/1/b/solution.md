↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [law of the iterated logarithm for a simple symmetric random walk](../../../../../../law-of-the-iterated-logarithm-for-a-simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) states that almost surely

$$
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{|S_n|}{\sqrt{2n\log\log n}}=1.
$$

Since $\sqrt{2\log\log n}$ is unbounded, $|S_n|/\sqrt n$ exceeds every fixed $c>0$ at some finite time. Hence

$$
T_c=\inf\{n\geq0:|S_n|>c\sqrt n\}<\infty
$$

almost surely.
