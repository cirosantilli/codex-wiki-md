[Weak convergence of probability measures](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) $\mu_n\to\mu$ on a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) means that

$$
\int f\,d\mu_n\longrightarrow\int f\,d\mu
$$

for every bounded continuous real function $f$. [Weak convergence of random variables](../../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md) means weak convergence of their [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md); equivalently,

$$
\mathbb E[f(X_n)]\longrightarrow\mathbb E[f(X)]
$$

for every bounded continuous $f$.
