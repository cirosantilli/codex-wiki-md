<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ be the primitive lattice generators of the rays of the strongly convex rational cone $\sigma\subseteq N_{\mathbb R}$. The [smoothness criterion for a toric variety](../../../../../../smoothness-criterion-for-a-toric-variety.md) says that the [affine toric variety](../../../../../../affine-toric-variety.md) $U_\sigma$ is smooth exactly when $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ form part of a $\mathbb Z$-basis of the [cocharacter lattice of an algebraic torus](../../../../../../cocharacter-lattice-of-an-algebraic-torus.md) $N$. Equivalently, there is a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $N$ such that

$$
\sigma=\operatorname{Cone}(e_1,\ldots,e_r).
$$

In particular, the cone must be a [simplicial polyhedral cone](../../../../../../simplicial-polyhedral-cone.md) and its ray generators must be primitive; for a full-dimensional cone the criterion says that those generators form a lattice basis.
