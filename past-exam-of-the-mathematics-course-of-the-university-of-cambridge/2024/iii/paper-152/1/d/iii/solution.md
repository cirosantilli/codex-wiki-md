<h1 id="1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $D_1$ and $D_2$ be the [toric divisors](../../../../../../../toric-divisor.md) corresponding respectively to the primitive ray generators $v_1=(3,-2)$ and $v_2=(0,1)$ of the original cone. The [principal divisor on a toric variety](../../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-a-toric-variety.md) formula applied to the characters $x=\chi^{e_1^*}$ and $y=\chi^{e_2^*}$ gives

$$
\operatorname{div}(x)=3D_1,
\qquad
\operatorname{div}(y)=-2D_1+D_2.
$$

The [toric divisor class sequence](../../../../../../../toric-divisor-class-sequence.md) therefore presents the [divisor class group](../../../../../../../divisor-class-group.md) as

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(U_\sigma)
\cong\frac{\mathbb ZD_1\oplus\mathbb ZD_2}
{\langle3D_1,-2D_1+D_2\rangle}
\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z.
$$

The class of $D_1$ is a generator and $[D_2]=2[D_1]$.
