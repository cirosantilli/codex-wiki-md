The [orbit-cone correspondence](../../../../../../orbit-cone-correspondence.md) is an inclusion-reversing bijection

$$
\{\sigma\in\Sigma\}\longleftrightarrow
\{T_N\text{-orbits in }X_\Sigma\},
\qquad \sigma\longmapsto O(\sigma).
$$

If $n=\operatorname{rank}N$, then

$$
\dim O(\sigma)=n-\dim\sigma,
\qquad
O(\sigma)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(M\cap\sigma^\perp,\mathbb C^\times).
$$

The affine chart and [orbit closure in a toric variety](../../../../../../orbit-closure-in-a-toric-variety.md) are

$$
U_\sigma=\bigcup_{\tau\preceq\sigma}O(\tau),
\qquad
V(\sigma)=\overline{O(\sigma)}
=\bigcup_{\tau\supseteq\sigma}O(\tau).
$$

In particular, the zero cone corresponds to the dense [algebraic torus](../../../../../../algebraic-torus.md), while maximal cones correspond to torus-fixed points.
