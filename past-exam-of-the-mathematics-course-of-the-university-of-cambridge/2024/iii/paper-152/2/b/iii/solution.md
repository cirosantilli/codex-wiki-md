<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let

$$
v=e_1+e_2+e_3
$$

and let $\widetilde\Sigma$ be the [star subdivision](../../../../../../../star-subdivision.md) of $\sigma$ through the ray $\mathbb R_{\geq0}v$. Its maximal cones are

$$
\operatorname{Cone}(v,e_2,e_3),\qquad
\operatorname{Cone}(e_1,v,e_3),\qquad
\operatorname{Cone}(e_1,e_2,v).
$$

Since $\widehat\varphi(v)=0$, their images lie respectively in

$$
\operatorname{Cone}(f_2,-f_1-f_2),\qquad
\operatorname{Cone}(f_1,-f_1-f_2),\qquad
\operatorname{Cone}(f_1,f_2).
$$

Thus $\widehat\varphi$ is a morphism of fans from $\widetilde\Sigma$ to $\Sigma$.

The [toric blowup at a torus-fixed point](../../../../../../../toric-blowup-at-a-torus-fixed-point.md) identifies

$$
X_{\widetilde\Sigma}\cong\operatorname{Bl}_0\mathbb A^3.
$$

More explicitly, the [blowup of affine space at the origin](../../../../../../../blowup-of-affine-space-at-the-origin.md) is the total space of $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-1)$. The induced [toric morphism](../../../../../../../toric-morphism.md)

$$
g:X_{\widetilde\Sigma}\longrightarrow X_\Sigma\cong\mathbb P^2
$$

is its bundle projection: it sends a nonzero vector to its direction $[x_1:x_2:x_3]$ and restricts to the identity on the [exceptional divisor](../../../../../../../exceptional-divisor.md) $\mathbb P^2$. Therefore $g$ resolves the [indeterminacy locus](../../../../../../../indeterminacy-locus.md) of $f$.
