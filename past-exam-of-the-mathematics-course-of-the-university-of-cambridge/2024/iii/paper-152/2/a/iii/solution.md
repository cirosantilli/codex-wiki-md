<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Before blowing up, the fiber over zero is the smooth curve

$$
F_0=\mathbb P^1\times\{0\},
$$

and the blowup center $p$ lies on it. Its total transform is the union of the [strict transform](../../../../../../../strict-transform.md) $\widetilde F_0$ and the [exceptional divisor](../../../../../../../exceptional-divisor.md) $E$. Blowing up a smooth point of a smooth curve does not change the curve, so $\widetilde F_0\cong\mathbb P^1$, while $E\cong\mathbb P^1$. They meet transversely in the single point recording the tangent direction of $F_0$ at $p$. Therefore

$$
f^{-1}(0)=\widetilde F_0\cup E
$$

is two copies of the [complex projective line](../../../../../../../complex-projective-line.md) meeting at one point.
