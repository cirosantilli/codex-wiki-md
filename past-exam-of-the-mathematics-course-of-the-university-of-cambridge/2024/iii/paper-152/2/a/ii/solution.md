The [fan of the affine line](../../../../../../../fan-of-the-affine-line.md) consists of the zero cone and $\mathbb R_{\geq0}e'$. Under $\varphi(e_1)=0$ and $\varphi(e_2)=e'$, the four primitive source rays map as

$$
e_1+e_2\mapsto e',\qquad
e_1\mapsto0,\qquad
e_2\mapsto e',\qquad
-e_1\mapsto0.
$$

Every cone of the source fan consequently maps into $\mathbb R_{\geq0}e'$. The defining criterion for a [toric morphism](../../../../../../../toric-morphism.md) is satisfied, so $\varphi$ induces

$$
f:X_\Sigma\longrightarrow\mathbb A^1.
$$

Under the blowup description from part (i), this is the blowup map followed by the projection $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb A^1\to\mathbb A^1$.
