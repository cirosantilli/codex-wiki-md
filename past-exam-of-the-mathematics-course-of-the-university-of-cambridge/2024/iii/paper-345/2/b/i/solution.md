<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\theta$ be the angle made by the [group velocity](../../../../../../../group-velocity.md) ray with the horizontal. The [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../../internal-wave.md) dispersion relation gives

$$
\sin\theta=\frac{|\omega|}{N},
\qquad
\tan\theta=\frac{|k|}{|m|}
=\frac{|\omega|}{\sqrt{N^2-\omega^2}}.
$$

Each sawtooth face changes height by $2h_0$ over horizontal distance $\lambda_T/2$, so its slope magnitude is

$$
s_T=\frac{4h_0}{\lambda_T}.
$$

The [internal-wave slope criticality](../../../../../../../internal-wave-slope-criticality.md) criterion is $s_T<\tan\theta$. Hence reflection is subcritical for

$$
\boxed{
\frac{Ns_T}{\sqrt{1+s_T^2}}<|\omega|<N
}.
$$

In the corresponding [internal-wave ray tracing](../../../../../../../internal-wave-ray-tracing.md) sketch, every incident ray meets one planar face and leaves it into the fluid at the same angle $\theta$ to the horizontal. The reflected ray is steeper than either face, so it clears the sawtooth rather than running into an adjacent corner.
