<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Measure downward distance from the [plume virtual origin](../../../../../../plume-virtual-origin.md) by

$$
s=z_o-z>0,
$$

and let $\mathcal B$ be the magnitude of the cold plume's [buoyancy flux](../../../../../../buoyancy-flux.md) per unit span. A one-sided [wall line plume](../../../../../../wall-line-plume.md) with width $b$, downward speed $W>0$, and reduced-gravity magnitude $g'$ satisfies

$$
\frac d{ds}(bW)=\alpha W,
\qquad
\frac d{ds}(bW^2)=bg'=\frac{\mathcal B}{W},
\qquad
bWg'=\mathcal B.
$$

The [pure plume](../../../../../../pure-plume.md) solution is

$$
b(z)=\alpha(z_o-z),
\qquad
W(z)=\left(\frac{\mathcal B}{\alpha}\right)^{1/3},
\qquad
g'(z)=
\left(\frac{\mathcal B}{\alpha}\right)^{2/3}
\frac1{z_o-z}.
$$

Imposing $b(z_V)=H'/2$ fixes

$$
\boxed{z_o=z_V+\frac{H'}{2\alpha}}.
$$

The constant speed is a special feature of a pure top-hat line plume; its width and [volumetric flow rate](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) grow linearly with downward distance while entrainment dilutes its density anomaly like $(z_o-z)^{-1}$.
