<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $W_f$ be the vertical velocity of the carrier fluid. Each species has its isolated [Stokes settling velocity](../../../../../../stokes-settling-velocity.md) relative to that fluid, so $W_i=W_f+\widehat W_i$. The mixture has no imposed vertical [volumetric flow rate](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md). Using the [particle volume fractions](../../../../../../particle-volume-fraction.md), its zero-volume-flux condition is

$$
(1-\phi_1-\phi_2)W_f+\phi_1W_1+\phi_2W_2=0.
$$

Substitution of $W_i=W_f+\widehat W_i$ gives the common carrier-fluid backflow

$$
W_f=-\phi_1\widehat W_1-\phi_2\widehat W_2.
$$

Consequently the [hindered settling](../../../../../../hindered-settling.md) velocities are

$$
W_1=(1-\phi_1)\widehat W_1-\phi_2\widehat W_2,
\qquad
W_2=(1-\phi_2)\widehat W_2-\phi_1\widehat W_1.
$$

The same $W_f$ appears for both populations precisely because every particle is assumed to feel the same volume-averaged backflow.
