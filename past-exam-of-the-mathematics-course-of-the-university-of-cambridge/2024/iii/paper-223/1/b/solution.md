<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $X=\sigma Z$ with $Z\sim F_1$. Then

$$
Y=\log X^2=\log\sigma^2+\log Z^2=\theta+W,
$$

where the distribution of $W=\log Z^2$ does not depend on $\sigma$. Consequently

$$
G_\theta(y)=G_0(y-\theta),
$$

which is a [location family](../../../../../../location-family.md). Since $\sigma>0$, the transformed parameter $\theta=\log\sigma^2$ ranges over all of $\mathbb R$; the paper's restriction $\theta>0$ is unnecessary.
