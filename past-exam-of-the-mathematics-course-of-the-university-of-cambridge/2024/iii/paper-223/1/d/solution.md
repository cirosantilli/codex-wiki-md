The transformation $g(t)=e^{t/2}$ satisfies $g(\theta)=\sigma$ and $g'(\theta)=\sigma/2$. Applying the [delta method](../../../../../../delta-method.md) to part c gives

$$
\sqrt n\left(e^{T_n/2}-\sigma\right)
\xrightarrow d
N\!\left(0,
\frac{\sigma^2}{4}
\frac{\mathbb E_{G_0}[\psi(W)^2]}
{\{\mathbb E_{G_0}[\psi'(W)]\}^2}
\right).
$$

**Thus exponentiating half the robust location estimate produces an asymptotically normal [scale estimator](../../../../../../scale-estimator.md).**
