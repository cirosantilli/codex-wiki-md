↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $\Sigma=I_d$, $\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma)=d$ and $\|\Sigma\|_2=1$. Taking $k$ of order $\max\{n\epsilon,1\}$ in part d gives

$$
\|\widehat\mu-\mu_0\|_2
=O_p\!\left(\sqrt{\frac dn}+\sqrt\epsilon\right).
$$

For Gaussian data under [Huber contamination](../../../../../../huber-contamination.md), the [Tukey median](../../../../../../tukey-median.md) has the sharper dependence

$$
O_p\!\left(\sqrt{\frac dn}+\epsilon\right)
$$

up to universal constants in its valid contamination range. Both have the same sampling term $\sqrt{d/n}$, but the Tukey median is linear rather than square-root in the contamination fraction.
