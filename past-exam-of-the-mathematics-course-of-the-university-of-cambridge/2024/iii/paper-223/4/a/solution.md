↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

After the replacement, the objective is

$$
\sup_{\|v\|_2\leq1}|(\mu_0-\mu)^Tv|
=\|\mu-\mu_0\|_2
$$

by [Euclidean norm duality](../../../../../../euclidean-norm-duality.md). It is nonnegative and equals zero exactly at $\mu=\mu_0$, so $\mu_0$ is the unique minimizer.
