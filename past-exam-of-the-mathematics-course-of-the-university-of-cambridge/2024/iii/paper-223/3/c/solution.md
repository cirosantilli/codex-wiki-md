<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a randomized test $\varphi(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\in[0,1]$, level $\alpha$ over the null neighborhood means

$$
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\epsilon(P_0)}\mathbb E_{P^n}\varphi\leq\alpha.
$$

A [maximin test](../../../../../../maximin-test.md) maximizes its worst-case power

$$
\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_\epsilon(P_1)}\mathbb E_{P^n}\varphi
$$

among all tests satisfying that robust level constraint.
