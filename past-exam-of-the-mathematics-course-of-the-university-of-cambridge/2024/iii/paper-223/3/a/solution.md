<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a location M-functional defined by

$$
\int\psi(x-T(F))\,dF(x)=0,
$$

its [influence function](../../../../../../influence-function.md) is

$$
\operatorname{IF}(x;T,F)
=\frac{\psi(x-T(F))}
{\mathbb E_F[\psi'(X-T(F))]},
$$

provided the denominator is nonzero. A bounded score therefore gives a bounded influence function.
