<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Only $h-1$ original observations remain in $Z_{\gamma,\tau}$, so the $h$ selected order statistics must include at least one moved observation. If $\|\theta\|_2\leq\gamma-1$, then $|\theta_1|\leq\gamma-1$, and its residual obeys

$$
|\gamma\tau-\tau\theta_1|
=\tau|\gamma-\theta_1|\geq\tau.
$$

Monotonicity of the symmetric loss, together with nonnegativity of the other loss and penalty terms, gives

$$
\boxed{L_{Z_{\gamma,\tau}}(\theta)\geq\rho(\tau).}
$$
