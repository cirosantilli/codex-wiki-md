<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Changing at most $n-h$ observations leaves at least $h$ original observations unchanged. At $\theta=0$, each unchanged observation has residual $y_i$ and loss at most $\rho(M_y)$. The sum of the $h$ smallest losses cannot exceed the sum over any chosen $h$ observations, so

$$
L_{Z'}(0)\leq h\rho(M_y).
$$

The $L^1$ regularization term vanishes at zero.
