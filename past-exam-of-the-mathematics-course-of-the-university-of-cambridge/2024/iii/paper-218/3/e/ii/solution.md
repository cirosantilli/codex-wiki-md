<h1 id="3/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An unpruned maximal [CART](../../../../../../../classification-and-regression-tree.md) classifier repeatedly cuts with vertical or horizontal lines until every terminal rectangle is pure or contains observations that cannot be separated by an axis-aligned split. Here the distinct grid points can be isolated into pure rectangles, producing a step-shaped, axis-aligned [decision boundary](../../../../../../../decision-boundary.md) and zero training error.
