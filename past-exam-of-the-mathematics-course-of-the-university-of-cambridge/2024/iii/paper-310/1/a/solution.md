<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a component with $P=w\rho$ and constant $w$, the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) becomes

$$
\frac{\dot\rho}{\rho}=-3(1+w)\frac{\dot a}{a}.
$$

Integration gives the [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho_i(a)=\rho_{i,0}a^{-3(1+w_i)}
=\rho_{i,0}(1+z)^{3(1+w_i)}}.
$$

Define the present [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) by

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{i,0}=\frac{\rho_{i,0}}{\rho_{\rm crit,0}},
\qquad \rho_{\rm crit,0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G}}.
$$

Substitution into the spatially flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives the [Hubble parameter for constant-equation-of-state components](../../../../../../hubble-parameter-for-constant-equation-of-state-components.md)

$$
\boxed{H(z)=H_0\left[
\sum_i\Omega_{i,0}(1+z)^{3(1+w_i)}
\right]^{1/2}}.
$$

Spatial flatness is what allows the expression to contain only the listed density components, with $\sum_i\Omega_{i,0}=1$.
