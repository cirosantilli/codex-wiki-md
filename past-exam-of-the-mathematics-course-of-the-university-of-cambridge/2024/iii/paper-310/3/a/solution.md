<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

During matter domination, pressureless matter has $\bar P_m=\delta P_m=0$. On subhorizon scales the time derivative of the [Newtonian gauge in cosmology](../../../../../../newtonian-gauge.md) potential is negligible, so the continuity and Euler equations reduce to

$$
\delta_m'=-\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf v,
\qquad
\mathbf v'+\mathcal H\mathbf v=-\nabla\Phi.
$$

Taking the divergence of the second equation, differentiating the first, and using the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) gives

$$
\delta_m''+\mathcal H\delta_m'
-4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho_m\delta_m=0.
$$

Since $d/d\tau=a,d/dt$, one has

$$
\delta_m'=a\dot\delta_m,
\qquad
\delta_m''=a^2(\ddot\delta_m+H\dot\delta_m),
\qquad
\mathcal H=aH.
$$

Division by $a^2$ yields the standard [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) equation

$$
\boxed{\ddot\delta_m+2H\dot\delta_m
-4\pi G\bar\rho_m\delta_m=0}.
$$
