<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

During matter domination, $a\propto t^{2/3}$ and $4\pi G\bar\rho_m=2/(3t^2)$. Trying $\delta_m\propto t^p$ gives

$$
p(p-1)+\frac43p-\frac23=0,
$$

whose roots are $p=2/3$ and $p=-1$. Thus the [cosmic-time matter density modes](../../../../../../cosmic-time-matter-density-modes.md) are

$$
\boxed{\delta_m=C_+a+C_-a^{-3/2}}.
$$

During asymptotic [cosmological constant energy](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) domination, $H$ is constant and $\bar\rho_m$ is negligible, so

$$
\ddot\delta_m+2H\dot\delta_m=0.
$$

The [matter density modes during cosmological-constant domination](../../../../../../matter-density-modes-during-cosmological-constant-domination.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\delta_m=C_1+C_2e^{-2Ht}=C_1+C_2a^{-2}}.
$$

The growing matter-era solution freezes to a constant.
