↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At horizon exit, the [primordial tensor power spectrum](../../../../../../primordial-tensor-power-spectrum.md) is proportional to $H^2$. Therefore

$$
n_T=\frac{d\log\Delta_t^2}{d\log k}
=\frac{-2\epsilon}{1-\epsilon}
=-2\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{C=2}.
$$

The ratio of the stated tensor and scalar spectra is

$$
r=\frac{8M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}(H/2\pi)^2}
{(2\epsilon M_{\rm Pl}^2)^{-1}(H/2\pi)^2}
=16\epsilon.
$$

Eliminating $\epsilon$ gives the [single-field inflation consistency relation](../../../../../../single-field-inflation-consistency-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{r=-8n_T}.
$$
