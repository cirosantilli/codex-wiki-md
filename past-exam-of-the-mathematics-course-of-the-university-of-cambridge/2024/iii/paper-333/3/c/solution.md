<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md)

$$
w'=\widehat w(z)e^{ikx-i\omega t},
\qquad
c=\frac\omega k,
$$

the linear material derivative becomes

$$
D_t\longmapsto ik(\overline u-c).
$$

Substitution into the equation from part b and division by $-k^2(\overline u-c)^2$ gives the [Taylor–Goldstein equation](../../../../../../taylor-goldstein-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{
\widehat w_{zz}+m^2(z)\widehat w=0},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{m^2(z)=l^2(z)-k^2},
$$

and the [Scorer parameter](../../../../../../scorer-parameter.md) is

$$
\boxed{
l^2(z)=
\frac{N^2(z)}{[\overline u(z)-c]^2}
-\frac{\overline u_{zz}(z)}{\overline u(z)-c}}.
$$
