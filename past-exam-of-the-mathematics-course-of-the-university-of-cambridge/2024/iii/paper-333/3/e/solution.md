<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Multiply

$$
\widehat w_{zz}+(l^2-k^2)\widehat w=0
$$

by $\widehat w$ and integrate from the rigid bottom to an upper endpoint $z$ at which $\widehat w\widehat w_z=0$. The bottom term also vanishes because $\widehat w(0)=0$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
-\int_0^z\widehat w_{z'}^2\,dz'
+\int_0^z(l^2-k^2)\widehat w^2\,dz'=0.
$$

Therefore the horizontal [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) has the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md)

$$
\boxed{
k^2=
\frac{\displaystyle
\int_0^z
\left(l^2\widehat w^2-\widehat w_{z'}^2\right)dz'}
{\displaystyle\int_0^z\widehat w^2\,dz'}}.
$$

For a trapped wave the upper endpoint may be taken to infinity because the eigenfunction decays.
