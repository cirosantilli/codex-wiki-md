<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Define the residual-circulation [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) by

$$
\boxed{
(\overline v_a^*,\overline w_a^*)
=(\overline\chi_{a,z}^*,-\overline\chi_{a,y}^*)}.
$$

This satisfies residual [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) identically. Let

$$
G=\nabla\mathbin\cdot\overline{\mathbf F},
\qquad
N^2=-\frac g{\rho_0}\frac{d\rho_s}{dz}.
$$

The momentum equation is

$$
\overline u_t-f_0\overline\chi_{a,z}^*=G.
$$

Differentiate [geostrophic balance](../../../../../../geostrophic-balance.md) vertically and [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) meridionally to obtain [thermal-wind balance](../../../../../../thermal-wind.md)

$$
f_0\overline u_z
=\frac g{\rho_0}\overline\rho_y.
$$

After a time derivative, the transformed density equation gives

$$
f_0\overline u_{tz}
=\frac g{\rho_0}\overline\rho_{ty}
=-N^2\overline\chi_{a,yy}^*.
$$

On the other hand, the $z$ derivative of momentum gives

$$
\overline u_{tz}
-f_0\overline\chi_{a,zz}^*=G_z.
$$

Eliminating $\overline u_{tz}$ yields the [Eliassen equation for residual circulation](../../../../../../eliassen-equation-for-residual-circulation.md)

$$
\boxed{
f_0^2\overline\chi_{a,zz}^*
+N^2\overline\chi_{a,yy}^*
=-f_0(\nabla\mathbin\cdot\overline{\mathbf F})_z}.
$$
