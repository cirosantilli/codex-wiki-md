<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In Euclidean worldsheet signature, the [string nonlinear sigma model](../../../../../../string-nonlinear-sigma-model.md) action can be written

$$
\boxed{
S=\frac1{4\pi\alpha'}\int_\Sigma d^2\sigma\sqrt h\,
\left[h^{ab}G_{\mu\nu}(X)\partial_aX^\mu\partial_bX^\nu
+i\epsilon^{ab}B_{\mu\nu}(X)\partial_aX^\mu\partial_bX^\nu
+\alpha'\Phi(X)R^{(2)}(h)\right]}.
$$

The factor of $i$ in the B-field term is absent in Lorentzian signature. Here $G$ is the target metric, $B$ the [Kalb–Ramond field](../../../../../../kalb-ramond-field.md), and $\Phi$ the [dilaton](../../../../../../dilaton.md).
