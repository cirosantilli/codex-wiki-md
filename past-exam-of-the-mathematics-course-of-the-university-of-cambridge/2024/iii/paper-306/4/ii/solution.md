<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The worldsheet metric is a gauge variable, so quantum consistency requires the [Weyl transformation](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md) to remain non-anomalous. The background fields are couplings of a two-dimensional quantum field theory, and their [sigma-model beta functions](../../../../../../sigma-model-beta-function.md) multiply the trace of the worldsheet stress tensor. Requiring every beta function to vanish gives, at the first nontrivial order in the derivative or $\alpha'$ expansion, precisely the stated metric, B-field and dilaton equations. They are also the Euler-Lagrange equations of the leading spacetime string-frame effective action

$$
\boxed{S_{\rm eff}\propto\int d^{26}x\sqrt{-G}\,e^{-2\Phi}
\left[R+4(\nabla\Phi)^2-\frac1{12}H^2+O(\alpha')\right].}
$$
