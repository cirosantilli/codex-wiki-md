<h1 id="2/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The momentum integral is replaced by the sum over [momentum and winding modes](../../../../../../momentum-and-winding-modes.md). Combining the zero modes with the oscillator trace gives

$$
\boxed{
Z_R(\tau,\bar\tau)=\frac1{|\eta(\tau)|^2}
\sum_{n,w\in\mathbb Z}
\exp\!\left[-\pi\tau_2\left(\frac{n^2}{R^2}+w^2R^2\right)
+2\pi i\tau_1nw\right]}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
Z_R=\frac1{|\eta|^2}\sum_{n,w}
q^{p_L^2/4}\bar q^{p_R^2/4}
$$

with the left/right convention of part (iv). The answer is invariant under the [T-duality](../../../../../../t-duality.md) $R\leftrightarrow1/R$ accompanied by $n\leftrightarrow w$ in these units.
