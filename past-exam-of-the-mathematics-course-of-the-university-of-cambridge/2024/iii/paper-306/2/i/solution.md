↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term $\widehat p^{,2}/2$ is the target-space zero-mode kinetic energy. The constant $-1/12$ is the [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) of the left- and right-moving oscillators: each chiral boson contributes $-1/24$. Finally,

$$
\sum_{n\geq1}\alpha_{-n}\alpha_n,
\qquad
\sum_{n\geq1}\widetilde\alpha_{-n}\widetilde\alpha_n
$$

count the energies of the two independent sets of [string oscillators](../../../../../../string-oscillator.md); one excitation of mode $n$ has energy $n$.
