<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The free equations of motion are $\bar\partial\psi=0$ and $\partial\bar\partial X=0$, hence

$$
\bar\partial G=i(\bar\partial\psi)\partial X+i\psi\bar\partial\partial X=0.
$$

Thus the [superconformal current](../../../../../../superconformal-current.md) $G=i\psi\partial X$ is holomorphic. Differentiating the boson OPE gives $\partial X(z)\partial X(w)\sim-1/(z-w)^2$. The double contraction in $G(z)G(w)$ is consequently $1/(z-w)^3$, while the single contractions combine into twice the full stress tensor. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G(z)G(w)\sim
\frac1{(z-w)^3}+\frac{2T(w)}{z-w}}.
$$

The leading coefficient equals $2c/3=1$ for $c=3/2$.
