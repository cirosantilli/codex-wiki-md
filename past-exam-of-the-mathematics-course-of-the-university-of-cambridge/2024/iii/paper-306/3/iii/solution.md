<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Only the fermionic part of $T$ contracts with $\psi$. Applying [Wick theorem](../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) to $T_\psi(z)=-\tfrac12{}:\!\psi\partial\psi\!:(z)$ and using $\psi(z)\psi(w)\sim1/(z-w)$ gives

$$
T(z)\psi(w)\sim
\frac{\frac12\psi(w)}{(z-w)^2}
+\frac{\partial\psi(w)}{z-w}.
$$

There is no singular OPE with $\bar T(\bar z)$. Thus $\psi$ is a [primary operator](../../../../../../primary-field.md) with

$$
\boxed{(h,\widetilde h)=\left(\frac12,0\right)}.
$$
