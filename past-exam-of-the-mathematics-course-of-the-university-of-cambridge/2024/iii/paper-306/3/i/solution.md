<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An operator $\mathcal O(w,\bar w)$ is a [primary operator](../../../../../../primary-field.md) of [conformal weights](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) $(h,\widetilde h)$ when its OPEs with the stress tensors are

$$
T(z)\mathcal O(w,\bar w)\sim
\frac{h\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{(z-w)^2}
+\frac{\partial_w\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{z-w},
$$



$$
\bar T(\bar z)\mathcal O(w,\bar w)\sim
\frac{\widetilde h\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{(\bar z-\bar w)^2}
+\frac{\partial_{\bar w}\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{\bar z-\bar w},
$$

with no more singular terms. Its [scaling dimension](../../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) and two-dimensional spin are

$$
\boxed{\Delta=h+\widetilde h,
\qquad s=h-\widetilde h}.
$$
