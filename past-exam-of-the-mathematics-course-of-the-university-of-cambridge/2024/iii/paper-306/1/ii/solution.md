<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The quadratic worldsheet action makes each $X^\mu$ a free boson. Its [Green function](../../../../../../green-s-function.md) in the complex plane inverts the operator in the action and uses $\partial\bar\partial\log|z-w|^2=2\pi\delta^{(2)}(z-w)$ with the corresponding convention. The result is

$$
\boxed{\left\langle X^\mu(z,\bar z)X^\nu(w,\bar w)\right\rangle
=-\frac{\alpha'}2\eta^{\mu\nu}\log|z-w|^2}.
$$

An additive constant is physically irrelevant because it can be absorbed into the zero mode.
