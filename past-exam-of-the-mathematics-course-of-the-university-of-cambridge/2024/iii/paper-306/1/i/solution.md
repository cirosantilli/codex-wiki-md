<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) contains the dilaton coupling

$$
S_\Phi=\frac1{4\pi}\int_\Sigma\sqrt h\,\Phi(X)R^{(2)}.
$$

For a constant [dilaton](../../../../../../dilaton.md) $\Phi_0$, the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) gives $S_\Phi=\Phi_0\chi(\Sigma)$, so the path-integral weight contributes $e^{-\Phi_0\chi}=g_s^{-\chi}$ with [string coupling](../../../../../../string-coupling.md) $g_s=e^{\Phi_0}$. A connected closed oriented genus-$g$ worldsheet has $\chi=2-2g$. Including one conventional factor of $g_s$ for each of $n$ external closed-string vertices gives the [string genus expansion](../../../../../../string-genus-expansion.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_{g,n}\propto g_s^{,2g-2+n}}.
$$

For four external states on the sphere, this is $g_s^2$.
