<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $\sqrt p=\sin\theta$ and $\sqrt{1-p}=\cos\theta$, where $0<\theta<\pi/2$. In the ordered basis $(|b\rangle,|g\rangle)$, the product of the two reflections is

$$
(UR_0U^\dagger)(I_{m-1}\otimes Z)
=-\begin{pmatrix}
\cos2\theta&-\sin2\theta\\
\sin2\theta&\cos2\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The leading minus sign is a physically irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../../../global-phase.md). The remaining [rotation matrix](../../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) rotates the good-bad plane through $2\theta$, where

$$
\boxed{\sin^2\theta=p}.
$$

Equivalently, one iterate changes the initial angle $\theta$ to $3\theta$.
