<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [spectral norm](../../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) of an operator $O$ is the induced [operator norm](../../../../../../operator-norm.md)

$$
\|O\|=\sup_{\|\psi\|=1}\|O\psi\|.
$$

Every [Pauli X gate](../../../../../../pauli-x-gate.md) $X_i$ is [unitary](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md), so $\|X_i\|=1$. The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) for the norm therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\|J_X\|
\leq\sum_{i=1}^n\|X_i\|=n}.
$$

In fact equality holds, as the product of $+1$ [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of the $X_i$ has eigenvalue $n$ under $J_X$.
