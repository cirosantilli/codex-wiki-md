<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each fixed $\alpha$, right multiplication by $x^{-\alpha}$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) of $G$, whose inverse is right multiplication by $x^\alpha$. Therefore $D_x$ permutes the displayed [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the [tensor-product space](../../../../../../../tensor-product.md). It is consequently a [unitary operator](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md), with

$$
\boxed{D_x^\dagger|\alpha\rangle|y\rangle
=|\alpha\rangle|yx^\alpha\rangle.}
$$
