The [hidden subgroup problem](../../../../../../hidden-subgroup-problem.md) for a [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ is specified by an oracle $f:G\to S$ that hides an unknown [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H\leq G$: it is constant on each left [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) of $H$ and takes distinct values on distinct cosets. The task is to determine $H$, usually by finding a [generating set](../../../../../../generating-set.md) for it.
