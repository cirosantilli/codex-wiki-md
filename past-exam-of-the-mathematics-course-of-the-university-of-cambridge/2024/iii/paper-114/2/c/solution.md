<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A closed manifold of odd dimension has Euler characteristic zero by the [Euler characteristic of an odd-dimensional closed manifold](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-of-an-odd-dimensional-closed-manifold.md). Decomposing $M$ into $P$ and the removed closed ball along $S^{2k}$ gives

$$
0=\chi(M)=\chi(P)+\chi(D^{2k+1})-\chi(S^{2k})
=\chi(P)+1-2,
$$

so $\chi(P)=1$.

If the antipodal boundary map extended to a fixed-point-free involution of $P$, the quotient map $P\to P/\langle\tau\rangle$ would be a double covering. The formula for [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md) would imply

$$
1=\chi(P)=2\chi(P/\langle\tau\rangle),
$$

which is impossible. Hence no such extension exists.
