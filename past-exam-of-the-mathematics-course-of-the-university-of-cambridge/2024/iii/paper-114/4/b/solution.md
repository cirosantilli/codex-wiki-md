<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Fibrewise evaluation identifies

$$
L^*\otimes_\mathbb C L\cong\operatorname{End}_\mathbb C(L).
$$

The identity endomorphism is a nowhere-zero continuous section, giving the [canonical trivialization of a line bundle tensored with its dual](../../../../../../canonical-trivialization-of-a-line-bundle-tensored-with-its-dual.md).

Give a complex line bundle its natural orientation as a real plane bundle. Then its [Euler class](../../../../../../euler-class-of-a-complex-line-bundle.md) equals its [First Chern class](../../../../../../first-chern-class.md). If $a,b$ are the standard generators of $H^2(S^2\times S^2;\mathbb Z)$, choose complex line bundles $L_1,L_2$ pulled back from the [Hopf fibration](../../../../../../hopf-fibration.md) on the two factors, with $e(L_1)=a$ and $e(L_2)=b$. The triviality of $L_i^*\otimes L_i$ gives $e(L_i^*)=-e(L_i)$. Hence, for any

$$
\alpha=pa+qb,
$$

the tensor product $L_1^{\otimes p}\otimes L_2^{\otimes q}$, with negative powers interpreted using [dual bundles](../../../../../../dual-bundle.md), has Euler class $\alpha$. Its underlying oriented real rank-two bundle is the required $E_\alpha$.
