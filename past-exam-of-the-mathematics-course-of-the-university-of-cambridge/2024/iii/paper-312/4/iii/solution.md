<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $\mathbf x_j=\mathbf x_0+(\eta_0-\eta_j)\widehat{\mathbf n}$. The delta functions in $g$ evaluate the local last-scattering sources, while the piecewise $e^{-\tau}$ weights the integrated gravitational source. Thus

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
(\Theta+\Psi)_0={}&p(\Theta_0+\Psi-\widehat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf v_e)(\eta_*,\mathbf x_*)\\
&+(1-p)(\Theta_0+\Psi-\widehat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf v_e)(\eta_1,\mathbf x_1)\\
&+p\int_{\eta_*}^{\eta_1}d\eta,(\Phi'+\Psi')(\eta,\mathbf x(\eta))\\
&+\int_{\eta_1}^{\eta_0}d\eta,(\Phi'+\Psi')(\eta,\mathbf x(\eta)).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The first two lines are weighted [Sachs-Wolfe combination](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-combination.md) and Doppler sources at the two last-scattering surfaces; the final lines are the corresponding [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md).

To predict these quantities from [primordial perturbations](../../../../../../primordial-perturbation.md), one solves the coupled linear [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) and [Boltzmann equation](../../../../../../boltzmann-equation.md) hierarchy for photons, baryons, cold dark matter, and neutrinos, together with the ionization history that determines $\Gamma$ and hence $g$. The solutions provide $\Theta_0$, $\mathbf v_e$, $\Phi$, and $\Psi$ at both surfaces and along the line of sight.
