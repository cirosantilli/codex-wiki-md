<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The second Friedmann equation gives the [first Hubble slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../first-hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md)

$$
\boxed{\epsilon=-\frac{\dot H}{H^2}
=\frac{\dot{\bar\phi}^{,2}}{2M_{\rm Pl}^2H^2}}.
$$

Because $\dot{\bar\phi}\propto a^{-3}$,

$$
\frac{\dot\epsilon}{\epsilon H}
=2\frac{\ddot{\bar\phi}}{H\dot{\bar\phi}}
-2\frac{\dot H}{H^2}
=-6+2\epsilon.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\rm SR}=-6+2\epsilon}.
$$

As $a\to\infty$, the kinetic energy decays as $a^{-6}$ and the constant $\Lambda$ dominates. Hence $H$ approaches a constant,

$$
\boxed{aH\simeq-\frac1\eta,
\qquad\epsilon\simeq0,
\qquad\eta_{\rm SR}\simeq-6}.
$$

This non-attractor background is [ultra-slow-roll inflation](../../../../../../ultra-slow-roll-inflation.md).
