<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The massless field equation is

$$
\boxed{\phi''+2\mathcal H\phi'-\nabla^2\phi=0},
\qquad \mathcal H=\frac{a'}a.
$$

For a homogeneous solution this becomes

$$
(a^2\bar\phi')'=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\bar\phi'\propto a^{-2}},
\qquad \boxed{p=-2}.
$$

Since $\dot{\bar\phi}=\bar\phi'/a$, the cosmic-time velocity decays as $a^{-3}$.
