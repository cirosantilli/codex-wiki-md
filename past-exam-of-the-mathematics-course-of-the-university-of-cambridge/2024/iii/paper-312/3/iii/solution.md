<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For Gaussian linear perturbations, a connected tree on five external density insertions has four Wick-contraction edges and therefore contains

$$
\boxed{P_{\rm lin}^4}.
$$

Thus $n=4$. The three unlabeled [tree-level connected matter five-point function](../../../../../../tree-level-connected-matter-five-point-function.md) topologies correspond to the perturbative-order partitions

$$
\boxed{4+1+1+1+1,
\qquad3+2+1+1+1,
\qquad2+2+2+1+1}.
$$

They are respectively an $F_4$ star with four $F_1$ leaves, an $F_3$ vertex joined to an $F_2$ vertex with three $F_1$ leaves, and a chain with three $F_2$ vertices and two endpoint $F_1$ vertices.

For one labeling of the last topology, let $\mathbf k_{12}=\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2$ and $\mathbf k_{123}=\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2+\mathbf k_3$. Its algebraic contribution is

$$
\boxed{8,F_2(-\mathbf k_1,\mathbf k_{12})
F_2(-\mathbf k_{12},\mathbf k_{123})
F_2(-\mathbf k_{123},-\mathbf k_5)
P_{\rm lin}(k_1)P_{\rm lin}(k_{12})
P_{\rm lin}(k_{123})P_{\rm lin}(k_5)}.
$$

The factor $2^3=8$ counts the interchange of the two linear legs at each symmetric $F_2$ vertex. The full correlator adds all distinct permutations of external labels and the overall momentum-conserving delta function.
