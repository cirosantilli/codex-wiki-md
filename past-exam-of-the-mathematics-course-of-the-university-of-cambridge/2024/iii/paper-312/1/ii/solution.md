<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For

$$
f(k,\eta)=\frac{H\eta}{\sqrt{2k}}e^{-ik\eta},
$$

the specified [cosmological bulk-to-boundary propagator](../../../../../../cosmological-bulk-to-boundary-propagator.md) is

$$
\boxed{G_R(\eta,p)=f(p,\eta_0)f^*(p,\eta)
=\frac{H^2\eta_0\eta}{2p}e^{-ip(\eta_0-\eta)}}.
$$

The right-right [cosmological bulk-to-bulk propagator](../../../../../../cosmological-bulk-to-bulk-propagator.md) is time ordered:

$$
\boxed{G_{RR}(\eta_1,\eta_2,p)
=\frac{H^2\eta_1\eta_2}{2p}
e^{-ip|\eta_1-\eta_2|}}.
$$

The mixed [Wightman function](../../../../../../wightman-function.md) in the order stated in the question is

$$
\boxed{G_{LR}(\eta_1,\eta_2,p)
=f(p,\eta_1)f^*(p,\eta_2)
=\frac{H^2\eta_1\eta_2}{2p}
e^{-ip(\eta_1-\eta_2)}}.
$$

The suppressed factor in each expression is $(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf p+\mathbf p')$.
