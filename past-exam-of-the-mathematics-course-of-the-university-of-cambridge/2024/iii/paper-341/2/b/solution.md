<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A two-node collocation method already has order at least two. It has order at least three exactly when its node polynomial is orthogonal to constants on $[0,1]$:

$$
0=\int_0^1\omega(x)dx
=\frac13-\frac{c_1+c_2}{2}+c_1c_2.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{2-3(c_1+c_2)+6c_1c_2=0}.
$$

This is the additional quadrature order condition $\sum_i b_ic_i^2=1/3$.
