<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $h=0$ the first characteristic polynomial is

$$
\rho(\xi)=\xi^2-\frac45\xi-\frac15
=(\xi-1)\left(\xi+\frac15\right).
$$

Its roots satisfy the [root condition for a multistep method](../../../../../../root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md): the only unit-modulus root is the simple root $1$, and the other root has modulus $1/5$. The method is consistent because part a gives positive order. The [Dahlquist equivalence theorem](../../../../../../dahlquist-equivalence-theorem.md), in the form allowed by the question for this derivative-augmented method, therefore proves convergence.
