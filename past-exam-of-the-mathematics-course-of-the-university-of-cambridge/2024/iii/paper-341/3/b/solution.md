<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Insert the Fourier mode $u_m^n=G^ne^{im\theta}$ into the scheme. [von Neumann stability analysis](../../../../../../von-neumann-stability-analysis.md) gives

$$
G(\theta)=\frac1{1+2\mu-2\mu\cos\theta}
=\frac1{1+4\mu\sin^2(\theta/2)}.
$$

For every physical [Courant number](../../../../../../courant-number.md) $\mu\geq0$, one has $0<G(\theta)\leq1$. Thus the method is unconditionally stable: the range is $\boxed{\mu\geq0}$.
