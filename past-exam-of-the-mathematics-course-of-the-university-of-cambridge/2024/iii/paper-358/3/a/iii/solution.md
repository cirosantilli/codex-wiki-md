<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The map is invertible with respect to $\omega$ when there is a measurable $G:X\to X$ such that

$$
G\circ F=\operatorname{id},
\qquad
F\circ G=\operatorname{id}
$$

almost everywhere. For an [invertible measure-preserving system](../../../../../../../invertible-measure-preserving-system.md), $K_G=K_F^{-1}=K_F^*$, so its [Koopman operator](../../../../../../../koopman-operator.md) is unitary.
