<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every $a>0$, define

$$
\widehat X_t=a^{-1}X_{a^2t},
\qquad
\widehat Y_t=a^{-1}Y_{a^2t},
\qquad
\widehat B_t=a^{-1}B_{a^2t}.
$$

The [Brownian scaling](../../../../../../brownian-scaling.md) theorem makes $\widehat B$ a standard Brownian motion, and substitution shows that $(\widehat X,\widehat Y)$ satisfies the same coupled [Bessel process](../../../../../../bessel-process.md) equations from initial values $(x/a,y/a)$. Both hitting times are divided by $a^2$, so their order is unchanged. Taking $a=x$ or comparing any two pairs with the same ratio proves that $\mathbb P(\tau_x>\tau_y)$ depends only on $x/y$.
