<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $\kappa=4/(d-1)>4$. For chordal [Schramm–Loewner evolution](../../../../../../schramm-loewner-evolution.md), the centered image of a real boundary point, divided by $\sqrt\kappa$, follows the [Boundary-point Bessel flow for SLE](../../../../../../boundary-point-bessel-flow-for-sle.md); changing $B$ to $-B$ matches the sign convention in the question. Thus $\tau_x$ and $\tau_y$ are the times at which the marked boundary points $x$ and $y$ are swallowed, or equivalently disconnected from infinity, by the [Loewner chain](../../../../../../loewner-chain.md).

Consequently

$$
\{\tau_x>\tau_y\}=\{\text{the SLE trace reaches }(-\infty,y]\text{ before }[x,\infty)\}.
$$

It is the event that the negative marked point is swallowed before the positive marked point.
