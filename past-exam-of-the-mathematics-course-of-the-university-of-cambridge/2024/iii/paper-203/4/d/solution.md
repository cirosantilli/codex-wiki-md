<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Part (c), iterated over disjoint nested annuli, gives [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md), and the law $X_s-X_t\overset d=X_{s-t}$ gives [stationary increments](../../../../../../stationary-increments.md). Every finite vector is jointly Gaussian by the definition of the [Zero-boundary Gaussian free field](../../../../../../zero-boundary-gaussian-free-field.md), and a continuous version was assumed. Moreover $X_0=0$, because the field has zero boundary values.

Thus $X$ is a continuous centered [Gaussian process](../../../../../../gaussian-process.md) with [stationary increments](../../../../../../stationary-increments.md) and [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md). Its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is a [continuous additive function on the nonnegative real numbers](../../../../../../continuous-additive-function-on-the-nonnegative-real-numbers.md), so $\operatorname{Var}(X_t)=\sigma^2t$ for some $\sigma^2\geq0$. The [Gaussian-process characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../../gaussian-process-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) now gives

$$
(X_t)_{t\geq0}\overset d=(\sigma B_t)_{t\geq0}
$$

for standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) $B$. This is the [Circle-average process of the Gaussian free field is Brownian motion](../../../../../../circle-average-process-of-the-gaussian-free-field-is-brownian-motion.md).
