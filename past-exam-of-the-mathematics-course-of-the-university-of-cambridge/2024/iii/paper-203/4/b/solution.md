<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $U=B(0,e^{-t})$ in the [Domain Markov property of the Gaussian free field](../../../../../../domain-markov-property-of-the-gaussian-free-field.md). The field $h^{\mathbb D\setminus U}$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) throughout $U$. If $s>t$, the circle $\partial B(0,e^{-s})$ lies inside $U$, so the [mean value property for harmonic functions](../../../../../../mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) gives

$$
\boxed{(h^{\mathbb D\setminus U},\rho_s)=h^{\mathbb D\setminus U}(0).}
$$
