<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The parameterization in part (c) gives the exact self-similarity

$$
K_{\lambda^2t}=\lambda K_t
$$

for every $\lambda>0$. The [Loewner local growth property](../../../../../../loewner-local-growth-property.md) supplies a continuous [Loewner driving function](../../../../../../loewner-driving-function.md) $U$. Under this scaling of the hulls, the deterministic scaling rule for the [Chordal Loewner equation](../../../../../../chordal-loewner-equation.md) gives

$$
U_{\lambda^2t}=\lambda U_t.
$$

Taking $t=1$ and $\lambda=\sqrt t$ shows that

$$
U_t=U_1\sqrt t=c\sqrt t
$$

for the real constant $c=U_1$.
