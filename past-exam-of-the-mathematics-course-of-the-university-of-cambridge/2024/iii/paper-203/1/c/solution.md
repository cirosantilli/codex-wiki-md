<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $S_r=\{se^{i\theta}:0<s\leq r\}$. Since $S_r=rS_1$, the [scaling and translation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../scaling-and-translation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(S_r)=r^2\operatorname{hcap}(S_1)=ar^2.
$$

The [half-plane-capacity parameterization](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-parameterization.md) requires $ar(t)^2=2t$, so

$$
r(t)=\sqrt{\frac{2t}{a}},
\qquad
\gamma(t)=\sqrt{\frac{2t}{a}}e^{i\theta}.
$$
