<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The trace of the [electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) in four [spacetime dimensions](../../../../../../../spacetime-dimension.md) is

$$
T^a{}_a=F^{ac}F_{ac}-\frac14\delta^a_aF_{cd}F^{cd}=0.
$$

For its [covariant divergence](../../../../../../../covariant-divergence.md), the source-free [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) eliminate the derivative of the first factor. Contracting the [Bianchi identity](../../../../../../../bianchi-identity.md) $\nabla_{[a}F_{bc]}=0$ with $F^{ac}$ gives

$$
F^{ac}\nabla_aF_{bc}=\frac14\nabla_b(F_{cd}F^{cd}),
$$

which cancels the derivative of the trace term. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nabla_aT^a{}_b=0,
\qquad T^a{}_a=0}.
$$

For $J^a=T^{ab}V_b$, [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) and symmetry of $T^{ab}$ now imply

$$
\nabla_aJ^a=T^{ab}\nabla_aV_b
=\frac12T^{ab}(\nabla_aV_b+\nabla_bV_a)
=\boxed{\frac12T^{ab}(\mathcal L_Vg)_{ab}}.
$$
