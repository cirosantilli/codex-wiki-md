<h1 id="2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The integrand defining $\varepsilon(t)$ is nonnegative by the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md), hence

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon(t)\geq0}.
$$

Apply the spacetime [divergence theorem](../../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to $J^a=T^{ab}V_b$ on the slab $S$. The flux through the cylinder at $r\to\infty$ vanishes by the stated decay. With the Lorentzian boundary orientation, the two spacelike boundary contributions give

$$
\varepsilon(t_0)-\varepsilon(t_1)
=\int_S\nabla_aJ^a\,d\operatorname{vol}_g.
$$

Part (ii) makes the right-hand side nonnegative, so for $t_1\geq t_0$,

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon(t_1)\leq\varepsilon(t_0)}.
$$

**Therefore $\varepsilon$ is a nonnegative monotone decreasing energy functional.**
