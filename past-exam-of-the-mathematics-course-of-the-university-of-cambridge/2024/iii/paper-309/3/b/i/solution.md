↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Vary the gravitational volume factor and use part (a)(ii):

$$
\delta(f(R)d\operatorname{vol}_g)
=\left[f'(R)\delta R+\frac12f(R)g^{ab}h_{ab}\right]d\operatorname{vol}_g.
$$

Twice applying [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) moves both derivatives in $\delta R$ from the compactly supported $h_{ab}$ onto $f'(R)$. Combining the result with the stated matter variation and requiring every coefficient of $h_{ab}$ to vanish gives the [f(R) gravity](../../../../../../../f-r-gravity.md) equation

$$
\boxed{f'(R)R_{ab}-\frac12g_{ab}f(R)
+(g_{ab}\nabla_c\nabla^c-\nabla_a\nabla_b)f'(R)
=8\pi T_{ab}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha'=1,
\qquad\beta'=-1}.
$$
