<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Varying the [Ricci tensor](../../../../../../../ricci-tensor.md) and contracting gives

$$
\delta R=-R^{ab}h_{ab}
+\nabla_a\!\left(g^{cb}\delta\Gamma^a{}_{cb}
-g^{ab}\delta\Gamma^c{}_{cb}\right).
$$

Substitution of part (i), followed by [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md), reduces the divergence to

$$
\nabla_a\nabla_bh^{ab}-\nabla^a\nabla_ah.
$$

Thus the [metric variation of scalar curvature](../../../../../../../metric-variation-of-scalar-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta R=-R^{ab}\delta g_{ab}
-g^{ab}\nabla_c\nabla^c\delta g_{ab}
+\nabla^a\nabla^b\delta g_{ab}},
$$

and consequently

$$
\boxed{\alpha=-1,
\qquad \beta=1}.
$$
