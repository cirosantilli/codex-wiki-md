<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [curvature 2-forms](../../../../../../curvature-2-form.md) show that orthonormal curvature components grow as $m/r^3$. The [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) equation converts them into the relative acceleration of nearby parts of an observer. As $r\to0$, the [Schwarzschild tidal force](../../../../../../schwarzschild-tidal-force.md) stretches radial separations and compresses transverse separations with unbounded magnitude. An extended body therefore undergoes destructive tidal deformation before reaching the [Schwarzschild singularity](../../../../../../schwarzschild-singularity.md).
