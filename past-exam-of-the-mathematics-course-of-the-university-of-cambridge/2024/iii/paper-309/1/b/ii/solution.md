<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $u=t-z$, the quadratic [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md) is

$$
L=\frac12(-\dot t^2+\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)
+xyA(u)\dot u^2.
$$

Its transverse [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\ddot x=yA(u)\dot u^2,
\qquad \ddot y=xA(u)\dot u^2}.
$$

The longitudinal equations are

$$
\boxed{\ddot t=\ddot z
=2A(u)(\dot x,y+x\dot y)\dot u+xyA'(u)\dot u^2},
$$

after using the difference of those same equations. In particular,

$$
\ddot u=\ddot t-\ddot z=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\dot t-\dot z=\dot u=\text{constant}}.
$$

This conserved quantity also follows from the [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $\partial_t+\partial_z$ and the [geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector](../../../../../../../geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md).
