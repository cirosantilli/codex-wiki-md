<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $u=t-z$. Wherever $|u|>U$, the profile $A(u)$ vanishes and the displayed metric is exactly the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md). Thus the two flat regions in the $(t,z)$-plane are the half-planes $t-z>U$ and $t-z<-U$, separated by the strip

$$
\boxed{|t-z|\leq U}.
$$

At fixed $t$ this strip is $t-U\leq z\leq t+U$, so it has longitudinal width $2U$. A surface $u=\text{constant}$ obeys $z=t-u$ and travels in the positive $z$ direction at the [speed of light](../../../../../../../speed-of-light.md). The curvature is confined to that moving strip, identifying the solution as a [gravitational-wave pulse](../../../../../../../gravitational-wave-pulse.md) represented by a [plane-fronted gravitational wave](../../../../../../../plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md).
