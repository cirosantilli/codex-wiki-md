↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Now $A_0=0$, so every mass is again at rest after the pulse and $z=0+O(\epsilon^2)$. Put $k=\epsilon A_1>0$. The final transverse coordinates are

$$
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}_{\!\rm after}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&-k\\-k&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_0\\y_0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The unit vectors along $x=y$ and $x=-y$ are [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1-k$ and $1+k$, respectively. Consequently an initial [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) becomes, to first order, an [ellipse](../../../../../../../ellipse.md) compressed along the line $x=y$ and stretched along $x=-y$. This persistent deformation is [displacement memory](../../../../../../../displacement-memory.md).
