The [proper time](../../../../../../../proper-time.md) elapsed along the smooth [timelike curve](../../../../../../../timelike-curve.md) is

$$
\boxed{T[\gamma]=\int_0^1
\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}(x(s))\frac{dx^\mu}{ds}\frac{dx^\nu}{ds}}\,ds}.
$$

The expression is invariant under every orientation-preserving [reparametrization](../../../../../../../reparametrization.md) of the curve.
