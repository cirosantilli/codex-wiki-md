<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $\dot x^\mu=dx^\mu/ds$ and $L=\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu}$. Varying $T=\int L\,ds$ with fixed endpoints and then choosing [proper time](../../../../../../../proper-time.md) as parameter, for which $L=1$, gives the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\frac d{d\tau}(g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\nu)
-\frac12\partial_\mu g_{\alpha\beta}\dot x^\alpha\dot x^\beta=0.
$$

Expanding the derivative, raising the free index with the [inverse metric](../../../../../../../inverse-metric.md), and using the symmetry of $\dot x^\alpha\dot x^\beta$ gives the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot x^\rho+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\alpha\beta}
\dot x^\alpha\dot x^\beta=0},
\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma^\rho{}_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac12g^{\rho\mu}
(\partial_\alpha g_{\mu\beta}+\partial_\beta g_{\mu\alpha}
-\partial_\mu g_{\alpha\beta})}.
$$

These are the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) of the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md).
