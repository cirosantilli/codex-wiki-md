<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the quadratic [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md)

$$
L_E=\frac12g_{\mu\nu}(x)\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu,
$$

the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
g_{\mu\nu}\ddot x^\nu
+\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\rho\dot x^\nu
-\frac12\partial_\mu g_{\alpha\beta}\dot x^\alpha\dot x^\beta=0.
$$

Raising $\mu$ and symmetrizing the coefficient of the two velocities produces exactly

$$
\ddot x^\rho+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\alpha\beta}
\dot x^\alpha\dot x^\beta=0.
$$

The quadratic [action](../../../../../../../action.md) fixes an [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md); proper time is an affine parameter for a timelike geodesic.
