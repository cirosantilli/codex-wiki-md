<h1 id="2/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A positive density factorizes according to an [undirected graphical model](../../../../../../../markov-random-field.md) $G^u=(\mathcal V,\mathcal E)$ when there are nonnegative [clique potentials](../../../../../../../clique-potential.md) $\psi_C$ such that

$$
f(v)=\frac1Z\prod_{C\in\mathcal C}\psi_C(v_C),
$$

where $\mathcal C$ may be taken as the maximal cliques and $Z$ normalizes the density. Equivalently, each potential involves only variables in one complete subgraph.
