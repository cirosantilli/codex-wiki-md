↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under [complete randomization](../../../../../../../complete-randomization.md), every assignment vector with exactly $n_z$ patients receiving treatment $z$ is equally likely. Thus, for $z=(z_1,\ldots,z_n)$ and $N_r(z)=\sum_i\mathbf1_{\{z_i=r\}}$,

$$
\mathbb P(Z=z)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{n_0!n_1!n_2!}{n!},&N_r(z)=n_r\quad(r=0,1,2),\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

The reciprocal probability $n!/(n_0!n_1!n_2!)$ is the [multinomial coefficient](../../../../../../../multinomial-coefficient.md) counting assignments with those arm sizes.
