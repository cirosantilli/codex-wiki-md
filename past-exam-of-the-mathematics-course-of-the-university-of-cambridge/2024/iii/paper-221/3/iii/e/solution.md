This conditional independence is implied. In the graph for $M(a)$, the incoming causal value of $A$ has been fixed, so the path $A\to M$ no longer transmits association to $M(a)$. The only possible route through $Y$ contains a collider, and conditioning on $X$ blocks the common-cause path through $X$. Hence [m-separation](../../../../../../../m-separation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A\mathrel{\perp\!\!\!\perp}M(a)\mid X.}
$$
