<h1 id="3/iii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

This conditional independence is implied. Fixing the mediator at $m$ removes the directed edge $M\to Y$ in the relevant counterfactual graph. Conditioning on $A$ blocks $M\leftarrow A\leftrightarrow Y(m)$, while conditioning on $X$ blocks $M\leftarrow X\to Y(m)$. Every remaining path is blocked by the [m-separation](../../../../../../../m-separation.md) criterion, so

$$
\boxed{M\mathrel{\perp\!\!\!\perp}Y(m)\mid A,X.}
$$
