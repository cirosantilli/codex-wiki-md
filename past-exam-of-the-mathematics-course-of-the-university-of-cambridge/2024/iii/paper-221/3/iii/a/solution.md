↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The claim is not implied. On the path $A\leftrightarrow Y\leftarrow X$, $Y$ is a [collider](../../../../../../../collider.md), and conditioning on $Y$ opens that path. Equivalently, conditioning on the common effect $Y$ can induce [collider bias](../../../../../../../collider-bias.md) between $A$ and $X$.
