<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

This is the covariate-conditional [front-door adjustment](../../../../../../front-door-adjustment.md). The paths from $A$ to $M$ have no unblocked backdoor path after conditioning on $X$, and all backdoor paths from $M$ to $Y$ are blocked by $(A,X)$. Therefore

$$
\mathbb P(Y(a)=y)
=\sum_{x,m}\mathbb P(x)\mathbb P(m\mid a,x)
\sum_{a'}\mathbb P(y\mid m,a',x)\mathbb P(a'\mid x).
$$

The inner sum identifies the effect of setting $M=m$ at covariate value $x$ by adjusting for $A$; the outer sum transports this through the mediator distribution generated by setting $A=a$ and then averages over $X$.
