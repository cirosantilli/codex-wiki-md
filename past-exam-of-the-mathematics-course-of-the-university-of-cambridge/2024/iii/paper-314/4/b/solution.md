<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) give the compression ratio

$$
\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_0}=\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}=4
$$

for $\gamma=5/3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\rho(R(t),t)=4\rho_0}.
$$

In the shock frame, [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) says

$$
\rho_0\dot R=\rho_2(\dot R-u_0).
$$

Using $\rho_2=4\rho_0$ gives

$$
\boxed{u_0(t)=\frac34\dot R(t)}.
$$
