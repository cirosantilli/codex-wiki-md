<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the wing frame the unperturbed plasma moves with velocity $-u_0\mathbf e_x$. The [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) condition $\mathbf E+\mathbf u\times\mathbf B/c=0$ therefore produces the [motional electric field](../../../../../../motional-electric-field.md)

$$
\mathbf E=-\frac{(-u_0\mathbf e_x)\times(B_0\mathbf e_z)}c
=-\frac{u_0B_0}{c}\mathbf e_y.
$$

The wing's [surface conductivity](../../../../../../surface-conductivity.md) then gives the [surface current density](../../../../../../surface-current-density.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf J=\Sigma\mathbf E
=-\frac{\Sigma u_0B_0}{c}\mathbf e_y}.
$$

Its direction also follows directly from the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) on the wing's mobile charges.
