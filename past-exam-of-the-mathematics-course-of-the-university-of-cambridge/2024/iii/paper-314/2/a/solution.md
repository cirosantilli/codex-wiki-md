<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $u(r)>0$ denote the inward [radial velocity](../../../../../../radial-velocity.md). Steady [spherical symmetry](../../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md) and [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) give

$$
\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho u=\text{constant}.
$$

The radial [Euler momentum equation](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) for the [globally isothermal equation of state](../../../../../../globally-isothermal-equation-of-state.md) $p=c_s^2\rho$ is

$$
u\frac{du}{dr}=-c_s^2\frac1\rho\frac{d\rho}{dr}-\frac{GM}{r^2}.
$$

The logarithmic derivative of mass conservation is $\rho'/\rho=-u'/u-2/r$. Substitution gives the [Isothermal Bondi equation](../../../../../../isothermal-bondi-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(u-\frac{c_s^2}{u}\right)\frac{du}{dr}
=\frac{2c_s^2}{r}-\frac{GM}{r^2}}.
$$

At a smooth [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md), $u=c_s$ makes the coefficient of $u'$ vanish, so the right-hand side must vanish too. Therefore

$$
\boxed{r_s=\frac{GM}{2c_s^2}}.
$$

The solution that crosses this [critical point of the isothermal Bondi equation](../../../../../../critical-point-of-the-isothermal-bondi-equation.md) continuously is the transonic accretion solution.
