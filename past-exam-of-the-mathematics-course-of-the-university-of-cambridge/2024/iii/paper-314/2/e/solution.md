<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $Y=y^2$. On the inner supersonic branch, the equation from part (d) is

$$
Y-\ln Y=\frac4x+4\ln x-3.
$$

Its [asymptotic expansion](../../../../../../asymptotic-expansion.md) as $x\to0^+$ begins

$$
Y=\frac4x+3\ln x+\ln4-3+O(x\ln x).
$$

Taking the positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) gives

$$
y=\frac2{\sqrt x}
\left[1+\frac x8\left(3\ln x+\ln4-3\right)
+O\!\left(x^2\ln^2x\right)\right].
$$

Since $x=r/r_s$ and $y=u/c_s$,

$$
\boxed{u(r)=2c_s\sqrt{\frac{r_s}{r}}
\left[1+\frac{r}{8r_s}
\left(3\ln\frac r{r_s}+\ln4-3\right)
+O\!\left(\frac{r^2}{r_s^2}\ln^2\frac r{r_s}\right)\right]}.
$$

The leading term is the [free-fall speed](../../../../../../free-fall-speed.md) $\sqrt{2GM/r}$; the logarithmic term is the first pressure correction to the inner [Isothermal Bondi accretion](../../../../../../isothermal-bondi-accretion.md) flow.
