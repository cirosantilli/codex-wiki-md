<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With the [dimensionless variables](../../../../../../dimensionless-variable.md) $x=r/r_s$ and $y=u/c_s$, [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) and part (c) give

$$
\frac\rho{\rho_0}=\frac{e^{3/2}}{x^2y}.
$$

Substitution into the [Bernoulli function](../../../../../../bernoulli-function.md), together with $GM/r=2c_s^2/x$, eliminates every dimensional parameter and gives the [transcendental equation](../../../../../../transcendental-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{y^2}{2}-\ln y-2\ln x+\frac32-\frac2x=0}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{y^2-\ln(y^2)=\frac4x+4\ln x-3},
$$

or

$$
\boxed{y^2e^{-y^2}=x^{-4}e^{3-4/x}}.
$$

The [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md) passes through $(x,y)=(1,1)$.
