<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the [Bondi sonic point](../../../../../../bondi-sonic-point.md), $u_s=c_s$ and $GM/r_s=2c_s^2$. Evaluating the [Bernoulli function](../../../../../../bernoulli-function.md) there gives

$$
\frac{c_s^2}{2}+c_s^2\ln\rho_s-2c_s^2
=c_s^2\ln\rho_0,
$$

and hence

$$
\rho_s=e^{3/2}\rho_0.
$$

The conserved [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) is therefore

$$
\dot M=4\pi r_s^2\rho_sc_s
=\pi e^{3/2}\rho_0\frac{(GM)^2}{c_s^3}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{A=\pi e^{3/2}}.
$$
