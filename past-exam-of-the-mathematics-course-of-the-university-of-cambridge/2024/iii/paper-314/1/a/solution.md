<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [magnetostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../magnetostatic-equilibrium.md), inertia is absent and all forces balance. A [force-free magnetic field](../../../../../../force-free-magnetic-field.md) is the limiting case in which the [Lorentz force density](../../../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) vanishes:

$$
\frac1c\mathbf j\times\mathbf B=0.
$$

Thus the [current density](../../../../../../current-density.md) is parallel to the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), so for some scalar [force-free parameter](../../../../../../force-free-parameter.md) $\alpha(\mathbf r)$,

$$
\mathbf j=\alpha\mathbf B.
$$

Using [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) in magnetostatics gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf B=\frac{4\pi}{c}\alpha\mathbf B}.
$$

Taking the [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) and using both the [divergence of a curl is zero](../../../../../../divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md) and $\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf B=0$ yields

$$
0=\nabla\mathbin\cdot(\alpha\mathbf B)
=\mathbf B\mathbin\cdot\nabla\alpha,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B\mathbin\cdot\nabla\alpha=0}.
$$

The [force-free parameter](../../../../../../force-free-parameter.md) is therefore constant along every [magnetic field line](../../../../../../magnetic-field-line.md).
