<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [affine morphism](../../../../../../affine-morphism.md) $f:X\to Y$ and a [quasi-coherent sheaf](../../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$, every inverse image of an affine open $V\subseteq Y$ is affine. Higher cohomology of a quasi-coherent sheaf on an affine scheme vanishes, so

$$
(R^qf_*\mathcal F)|_V=H^q(f^{-1}V,\mathcal F)=0\qquad(q>0).
$$

The [Leray spectral sequence](../../../../../../leray-spectral-sequence.md) therefore has only its zeroth row, and its edge maps give

$$
\boxed{H^p(X,\mathcal F)\simeq H^p(Y,f_*\mathcal F)\quad(p\ge0).}
$$
