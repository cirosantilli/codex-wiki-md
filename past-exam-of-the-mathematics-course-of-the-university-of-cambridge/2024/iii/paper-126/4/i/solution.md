The [Theorem of the square](../../../../../../theorem-of-the-square.md) says that for an [abelian variety](../../../../../../abelian-variety-split.md) $X$, a [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) $L$, and $x,y\in X(k)$,

$$
\boxed{T_{x+y}^*L\otimes L\simeq T_x^*L\otimes T_y^*L,}
$$

where $T_x$ denotes translation by $x$.
