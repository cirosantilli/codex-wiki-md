<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $H\le\mathbb F_2^n$ have dimension $k$. The value

$$
(1_B*\mu_H)(x)=\frac{|B\cap(x+H)|}{|H|}
$$

is a multiple of $|H|^{-1}=2^{-k}$, and its average over $x$ is the density $\beta$ of $B$. If every coset of $H$ met $B$, then every value would be at least $2^{-k}$, forcing $\beta\ge2^{-k}$. Thus $\beta<2^{-k}$ implies that some coset $x+H$ is disjoint from $B$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{x+H\subseteq A+A.}
$$
