One normalized form of the [Croot-Sisask almost-periodicity theorem](../../../../../../croot-sisask-almost-periodicity-theorem.md) is as follows. If finite sets $A,S$ in a group satisfy $|A+S|\le K|A|$, $p\ge2$, and $0<\epsilon<1$, then for every function $f$ there is $T\subseteq S$ with

$$
|T|\ge(2K)^{-O(p/\epsilon^2)}|S|
$$

such that

$$
\boxed{\|\tau_t(f*\mu_A)-f*\mu_A\|_p\le\epsilon\|f\|_p\qquad(t\in T-T).}
$$
