The [Finite-field Szemerédi theorem for four-term arithmetic progressions](../../../../../../finite-field-szemeredi-theorem-for-four-term-arithmetic-progressions.md) says that for every $\delta>0$ there is $N=N(\delta)$ such that, for every prime $p\ge5$, whenever $|\mathbb F_p^n|\ge N$ and $A\subseteq\mathbb F_p^n$ has density at least $\delta$, there exist $x,d\in\mathbb F_p^n$ with $d\ne0$ and

$$
\boxed{x,x+d,x+2d,x+3d\in A.}
$$
