<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Part (iii) gives at least $\eta^2|A|^3$ [additive quadruples](../../../../../../additive-quadruple.md). By the [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](../../../../../../balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md), there is $A'\subseteq A$ with

$$
|A'|\ge c(\eta)|A|,
\qquad
|A'+A'|\le K(\eta)|A'|.
$$

The [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem](../../../../../../freiman-ruzsa-theorem.md) places $A'$ in a coset progression $P$ whose rank is bounded in terms of $\eta$ and which satisfies

$$
|P|\le C_1(\eta)|A'|.
$$

Thus $A'$ has density at least $C_1(\eta)^{-1}$ inside $P$.

The [Szemerédi theorem in a bounded-rank coset progression](../../../../../../szemeredi-theorem-in-a-bounded-rank-coset-progression.md) says that, for fixed rank and density, every sufficiently large such progression has a nontrivial four-term progression in every subset of that density. If $|A|\ge C(\eta)$ for a sufficiently large $C(\eta)$, then $|P|\ge|A'|\ge c(\eta)|A|$ crosses this threshold. It gives a nontrivial four-term progression in $A'$, which is also contained in $A$. This proves the claim with a constant depending only on $\eta$.
