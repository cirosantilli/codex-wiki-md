<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At fixed radiative efficiency, the [Eddington ratio](../../../../../../eddington-ratio.md) implies $\dot m\propto f_{\rm Edd}M$. Far outside the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), part (a) gives $\Sigma\propto\dot m/\nu$. The supplied viscosity law therefore gives, in physical radius,

$$
\Sigma
\propto
f_{\rm Edd}^{7/10}
\left(\frac\alpha{0.1}\right)^{-4/5}
M^{19/20}R^{-3/4}.
$$

The disk mass follows by radial integration:

$$
M_d(R)=2\pi\int^{R}\Sigma(R')R'\,dR'
\propto
f_{\rm Edd}^{7/10}
\left(\frac\alpha{0.1}\right)^{-4/5}
M^{19/20}R^{5/4}.
$$

Since the [Schwarzschild radius](../../../../../../schwarzschild-radius.md) satisfies $R_S\propto M$, replacing $R$ by $(R/R_S)R_S$ contributes another factor $M^{5/4}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{
M_d(R)=C_1
f_{\rm Edd}^{7/10}
\left(\frac\alpha{0.1}\right)^{-4/5}
\left(\frac{M}{10^6M_\odot}\right)^{11/5}
\left(\frac R{R_S}\right)^{5/4}
},
$$

so

$$
(k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4)=
\left(\frac7{10},-\frac45,\frac{11}5,\frac54\right).
$$
