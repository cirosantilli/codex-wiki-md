<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Krull intersection theorem](../../../../../../krull-intersection-theorem.md) gives

$$
\bigcap_{r\geq0}(x^r)=0
$$

because $(R,\mathfrak m)$ is a [Noetherian local ring](../../../../../../noetherian-local-ring.md) and $x\in\mathfrak m$. Consequently every nonzero $a\in R$ has a largest $x$-adic order: one can write

$$
a=x^ra',\qquad a'\notin(x).
$$

Suppose nonzero elements $a,b$ satisfy $ab=0$. Write $a=x^ra'$ and $b=x^sb'$ with $a',b'\notin(x)$. Since $x$ is a [non-zero-divisor](../../../../../../non-zero-divisor.md), cancellation of $x^{r+s}$ gives $a'b'=0$. Reducing modulo $(x)$ now gives a product of two nonzero elements equal to zero in $R/(x)$, contradicting that this quotient is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md). Hence $R$ is an integral domain.
