<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose $a\in K\setminus F$. The multiplication homomorphism

$$
\mu:K\otimes_FK\longrightarrow K,
\qquad x\otimes y\longmapsto xy
$$

has

$$
a\otimes1-1\otimes a\in\ker\mu.
$$

This element is nonzero. Since $1,a$ are linearly independent over $F$, there is an $F$-linear functional $\lambda:K\to F$ with $\lambda(1)=0$ and $\lambda(a)=1$. Applying $\lambda\otimes\operatorname{id}_K$ sends the displayed tensor to $1$.

**Thus the unital ring homomorphism $\mu$ has a nonzero kernel. A unital homomorphism from a [field](../../../../../../field.md) is injective, so $K\otimes_FK$ is not a field.**
