<h1 id="4/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take

$$
f=T_1T_2-1.
$$

Then

$$
A=\mathbb R[T_1,T_2]/(T_1T_2-1)
\cong\mathbb R[t_1,t_1^{-1}].
$$

Every prime ideal in the image of

$$
\operatorname{Spec}A\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb R[t_1]
$$

is disjoint from the multiplicative set $\{1,t_1,t_1^2,\ldots\}$. In particular, the prime ideal $(t_1)$ is not in the image, so the contraction map is not surjective.
