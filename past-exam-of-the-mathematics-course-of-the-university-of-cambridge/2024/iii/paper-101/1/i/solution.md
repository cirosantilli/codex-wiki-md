An $R$-module $M$ is [Noetherian](../../../../../../noetherian-module.md) when every [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) is finitely generated, equivalently when every ascending chain of submodules stabilizes.

A [free module](../../../../../../free-module.md) is an $R$-module with a [basis](../../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md): every element has a unique expression as a finite linear combination of basis elements.

A [flat module](../../../../../../flat-module.md) $M$ is one for which the [tensor functor](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-modules.md) $-\otimes_RM$ is exact. Since tensor products are always right exact, it is equivalent to require that tensoring with $M$ preserve injections.

A [projective module](../../../../../../projective-module.md) $P$ has the lifting property: for every surjection $E\twoheadrightarrow F$ and every map $P\to F$, there is a map $P\to E$ making the resulting triangle commute. Equivalently, $P$ is a direct summand of a [free module](../../../../../../free-module.md).
