<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At leading order,

$$
u=R\cos\theta,
\qquad
u_t=-R\sin\theta,
$$

so

$$
f=-u(u_t)^2
=-R^3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta.
$$

The required period averages are

$$
\langle\cos\theta\sin^3\theta\rangle=0,
\qquad
\langle\cos^2\theta\sin^2\theta\rangle=\frac18.
$$

Hence

$$
R'=0,
\qquad
R\phi'=\frac{R^3}{8}.
$$

The initial data give $R(0)=1$ and $\phi(0)=0$, so

$$
\boxed{R(T)=1,
\qquad
\phi(T)=\frac T8}.
$$

Thus the perturbation produces a slow frequency shift but no leading amplitude decay:

$$
\boxed{u(t)\sim\cos\left(t+\frac{\epsilon t}{8}\right).}
$$
