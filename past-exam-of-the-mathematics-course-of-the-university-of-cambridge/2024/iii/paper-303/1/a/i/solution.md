<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The equilibrium magnetization is a [global minimum](../../../../../../../global-minimum.md) of the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). Its stationary values solve the [polynomial equation](../../../../../../../polynomial-equation.md)

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial m}=4a_4m^3+6a_6m^5-B=0,
$$

and a local minimum must satisfy

$$
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial m^2}=12a_4m^2+30a_6m^4\geq0.
$$

One compares the value of $f$ at every such local minimum and chooses the smallest. Since $a_6>0$, the [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) tends to positive infinity as $|m|$ tends to infinity, so a global minimum exists.
