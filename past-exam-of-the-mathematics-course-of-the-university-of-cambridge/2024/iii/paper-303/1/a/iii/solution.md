<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For every $T<T_c$, the two zero-field minima $m_+$ and $m_-$ coexist. A positive field selects $m_+$ and a negative field selects $m_-$, so crossing $B=0$ makes the equilibrium magnetization jump between nonzero values. Hence $B=0$, $T<T_c$, is a line of [first-order phase transitions](../../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md), ending at the continuous critical point $(T_c,0)$.
