Under $x'=x/\zeta$ and the Gaussian field rescaling $\phi'(x')=\zeta^{(d-2)/2}\phi(x)$, a mass squared has [engineering dimension](../../../../../../../engineering-dimension.md) two and a quartic coupling has dimension $4-d$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A=2,\qquad B=4-d}.
$$
