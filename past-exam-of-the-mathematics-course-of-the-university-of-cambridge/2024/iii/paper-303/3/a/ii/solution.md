<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The leading mass corrections are one-vertex [tadpole diagrams](../../../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md). In index notation, one contraction closes a freely summed component loop and is proportional to $N$; the two exchange contractions force the internal component to equal the external one and are not proportional to $N$. Including their multiplicities gives

$$
\Delta\mu^2=4(N+2)g_0
\int_{\Lambda/\zeta}^{\Lambda}\frac{d^dq}{(2\pi)^d}\frac1{q^2+\mu_0^2}.
$$

**Thus the $N$ in $N+2$ comes from the closed index loop, while the $2$ comes from the two same-component contractions.**
