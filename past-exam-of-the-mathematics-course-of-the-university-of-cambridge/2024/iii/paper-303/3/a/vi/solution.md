<h1 id="3/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For $d=4-\epsilon$, define the dimensionless couplings $r=\mu^2/\Lambda^2$ and $u=\widetilde g=\Lambda^{-\epsilon}g$. Since $K_4=1/(8\pi^2)$, the leading [epsilon expansion](../../../../../../../epsilon-expansion.md) of the flow is

$$
\frac{dr}{ds}=2r+4(N+2)K_4\frac{u}{1+r},
\qquad
\frac{du}{ds}=\epsilon u-4(N+8)K_4\frac{u^2}{(1+r)^2}.
$$

There is a [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) $(r_*,u_*)=(0,0)$. Its thermal eigenvalue is $y_t=2$, so its [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md) is $\boxed{\nu_{\rm G}=1/2}$.

The interacting [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](../../../../../../../wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) is

$$
\boxed{u_*=\frac{2\pi^2}{N+8}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2),
\qquad
r_*=-\frac{N+2}{2(N+8)}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)}.
$$

Linearizing the [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md) gives the thermal eigenvalue

$$
y_t=2-\frac{N+2}{N+8}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Taking its reciprocal gives

$$
\boxed{\nu_{\rm WF}=\frac12+\frac{N+2}{4(N+8)}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)}.
$$
