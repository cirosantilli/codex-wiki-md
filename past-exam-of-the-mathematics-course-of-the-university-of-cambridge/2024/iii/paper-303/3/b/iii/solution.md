**No.** Even when the two masses differ, the free energy retains an independent [Z2 symmetry](../../../../../../../z2-symmetry.md) for each component: $\phi_1\mapsto-\phi_1$ and $\phi_2\mapsto-\phi_2$. The bilinear $\phi_1\phi_2$ is odd under either transformation, so integrating out modes cannot generate it.
