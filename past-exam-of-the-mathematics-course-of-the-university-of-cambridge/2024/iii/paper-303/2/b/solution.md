<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The free energy is dimensionless in units with $k_BT=1$, so the integrand has momentum dimension $d$. Since a [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) has dimension one, the kinetic term gives

$$
2+2[\phi]_{\rm eng}=d,
\qquad
\boxed{[\phi]_{\rm eng}=\frac{d-2}{2}}.
$$

The mass term then gives

$$
[\mu_0^2]+2[\phi]_{\rm eng}=d,
\qquad
\boxed{[\mu_0^2]=2}.
$$

These are [engineering dimensions](../../../../../../engineering-dimension.md).
