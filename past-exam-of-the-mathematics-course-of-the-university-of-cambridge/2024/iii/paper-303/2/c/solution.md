<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The engineering value follows from the Gaussian kinetic term. At an interacting [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md), momentum-dependent self-energy diagrams change the kinetic coefficient, and restoring its normalization requires [wave-function renormalization](../../../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md). The resulting [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md) $\eta$ changes the full [scaling dimension](../../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) to

$$
\boxed{\Delta_\phi=\frac{d-2+\eta}{2}}.
$$
