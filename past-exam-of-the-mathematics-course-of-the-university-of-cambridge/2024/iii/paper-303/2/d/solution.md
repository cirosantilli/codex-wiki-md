<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The operator $\nabla^{2m}\phi^{2n}$ has [engineering dimension](../../../../../../engineering-dimension.md)

$$
2m+2n\frac{d-2}{2}=2m+n(d-2).
$$

The action integral is dimensionless, so

$$
\boxed{[\alpha]=d-2m-n(d-2)=2n-2m-(n-1)d}.
$$

Thus $\alpha$ is a [relevant coupling](../../../../../../relevant-coupling.md), [marginal coupling](../../../../../../marginal-coupling.md), or [irrelevant coupling](../../../../../../irrelevant-coupling.md) according as

$$
\boxed{
d<\frac{2(n-m)}{n-1},\qquad
d=\frac{2(n-m)}{n-1},\qquad
d>\frac{2(n-m)}{n-1}}
$$

respectively.
