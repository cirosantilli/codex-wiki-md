<h1 id="2/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At order $g_0^2$, use the two-point [sunset diagram](../../../../../../../sunset-diagram.md): two quartic vertices are joined by three internal propagators, with one external line attached to each vertex. Unlike the one-vertex [tadpole diagram](../../../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md), its self-energy $\Sigma(p)$ depends nontrivially on the external momentum $p$. The coefficient of $p^2$ in the expansion of $\Sigma(p)$ changes the kinetic term, so normalizing that term requires [wave-function renormalization](../../../../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md) and gives a nonzero field [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md).
