<h1 id="2/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose four of the six fields at one sextic vertex to be slow and contract the remaining two fast fields into a [tadpole diagram](../../../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md). There are $\binom64=15$ choices. If

$$
I_1(\zeta)=\int_{\Lambda/\zeta<|q|<\Lambda}
\frac{d^dq}{(2\pi)^d}\,G_0(q),
\qquad G_0(q)=\frac1{q^2+\mu_0^2},
$$

then the first term of the [cumulant expansion](../../../../../../../cumulant-expansion.md) contributes

$$
15\lambda_0I_1(\zeta)\int d^dx\,\phi_<^4.
$$

After the canonical coordinate and field rescaling, its contribution to the quartic coupling is

$$
\boxed{\Delta g(\zeta)=15\,\zeta^{4-d}\lambda_0I_1(\zeta)}.
$$
