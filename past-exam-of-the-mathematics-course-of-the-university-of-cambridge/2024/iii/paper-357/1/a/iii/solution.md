<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The inverse spacetime metric decomposes as

$$
g^{\mu\nu}=\perp^{\mu\nu}-n^\mu n^\nu.
$$

Contracting the [covariant derivative](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of $Z_\nu$ and recognizing the fully projected contraction as its [spatial covariant derivative](../../../../../../../spatial-covariant-derivative.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
\nabla^\mu Z_\mu
=-n^\mu n^\nu\nabla_\mu Z_\nu+D^\mu Z_\mu.
}
$$
