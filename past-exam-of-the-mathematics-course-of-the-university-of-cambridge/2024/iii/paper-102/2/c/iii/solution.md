<h1 id="2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Relative to $\mathfrak g\oplus V$, the adjoint action has block form

$$
\operatorname{ad}_{(x,v)}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\operatorname{ad}_x&0\\
(y\mapsto-yv)&\phi(x)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Multiplying two such block-triangular matrices and taking the trace gives

$$
\boxed{
K((x,v),(y,w))
=\kappa(x,y)+\operatorname{tr}(\phi(x)\phi(y))},
$$

the [Killing form of a semidirect product with a module](../../../../../../../killing-form-of-a-semidirect-product-with-a-module.md). It does not depend on $v$ or $w$, so $V$ lies in its radical. Therefore $K$ can be nondegenerate only if $V=0$. In that case $K=\kappa$, which is nondegenerate exactly when $\mathfrak g$ is semisimple by the [Cartan criterion for semisimplicity](../../../../../../../cartan-criterion-for-semisimplicity.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{K\text{ is nondegenerate iff }V=0\text{ and }\mathfrak g\text{ is semisimple}.}
$$
