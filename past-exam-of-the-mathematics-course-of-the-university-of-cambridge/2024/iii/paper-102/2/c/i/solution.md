<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Bilinearity and alternatingness of

$$
[(x,v),(y,w)]=([x,y],xw-yv)
$$

are immediate. For three elements, the $\mathfrak g$ component of the Jacobi sum vanishes by the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) in $\mathfrak g$. In the $V$ component, the coefficient of a vector such as $u$ is

$$
[x,y]u-x(yu)+y(xu)=0
$$

because the action is a [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md); the other terms cancel cyclically in the same way. Hence the bracket satisfies Jacobi and defines the [semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module](../../../../../../../semidirect-product-of-a-lie-algebra-and-a-module.md) $\mathfrak g\ltimes V$.
