<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, the Verma module has basis

$$
v,,fv,,f^2v,\ldots
$$

by the [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](../../../../../../../poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md). If $hv=\lambda v$, then

$$
e f^rv=r(\lambda-r+1)f^{r-1}v.
$$

For $\lambda=-d$ with $d>0$, the coefficient is

$$
r(-d-r+1)\ne0
$$

for every $r\geq1$. Thus no $f^rv$ with $r>0$ is a [singular vector](../../../../../../../singular-vector.md).

Any nonzero submodule contains a weight vector $f^rv$ because the $h$-weights are distinct. Applying $e^r$ gives a nonzero multiple of $v$, after which applying powers of $f$ generates all of $M(-d)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{M(-d)\text{ is irreducible and infinite-dimensional}.}
$$

This is also the negative-highest-weight case of [Reducibility of an sl2 Verma module](../../../../../../../reducibility-of-an-sl2-verma-module.md).
