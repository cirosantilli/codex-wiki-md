<h1 id="3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The linear map

$$
\varepsilon_1\mapsto\varepsilon_1,
\qquad
\varepsilon_2\mapsto\varepsilon_2,
\qquad
\varepsilon_3\mapsto-\varepsilon_3
$$

preserves $\Phi$ and exchanges $\alpha_2$ with $\alpha_3$ while fixing $\alpha_1$. It is not in the $D_3$ [Weyl group](../../../../../../../weyl-group.md), whose signed permutations change an even number of signs. Thus it is an outer automorphism of the root system.
