The preceding [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) identifies the root system $D_3$ with $A_3$. The classification of finite-dimensional complex [simple Lie algebras](../../../../../../../simple-lie-algebra.md) by connected Dynkin diagrams therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{so}_6(\mathbb C)\cong\mathfrak{sl}_4(\mathbb C)}.
$$

This is the [Isomorphism between so6 and sl4](../../../../../../../isomorphism-between-so6-and-sl4.md). Both algebras have dimension $15$, consistently with the classification.
