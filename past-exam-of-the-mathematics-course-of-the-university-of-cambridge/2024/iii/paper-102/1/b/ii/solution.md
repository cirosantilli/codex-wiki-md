<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $0\ne U\subseteq W$ be a subrepresentation and choose

$$
0\ne u=\sum_{r=p}^q a_rw_r\in U
$$

with finite support. The $h$-eigenvalues $a+2r$ are pairwise distinct. By [Lagrange interpolation](../../../../../../../lagrange-polynomial.md), there is a polynomial $P$ which is one at one chosen eigenvalue appearing in $u$ and zero at all the others. Then $P(h)u$ is a nonzero scalar multiple of one basis vector $w_i$. Since $U$ is invariant under $h$, it contains $w_i$.
