<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The conserved [number operator](../../../../../../number-operator.md) is

$$
N=\int d^dx\,\Psi^\dagger\Psi=\int d^dx\,\rho.
$$

Substitution of $\Psi=\sqrt\rho e^{-i\theta}$ into the [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) gives

$$
i\Psi^\dagger\dot\Psi=\rho\dot\theta+\frac i2\dot\rho,
\qquad
|\nabla\Psi|^2=\frac{(\nabla\rho)^2}{4\rho}+\rho(\nabla\theta)^2.
$$

The imaginary term is a [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md), so

$$
\mathcal L=\rho\dot\theta-
\frac{(\nabla\rho)^2}{8m\rho}-
\frac{\rho}{2m}(\nabla\theta)^2-r\rho-\lambda\rho^2+\cdots.
$$

Thus $\rho$ is the [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) and is the [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) conjugate to $\theta$. The [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) is $[\theta(\mathbf x),\rho(\mathbf y)]=i\delta^{(d)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y)$. Consequently, for $\theta_0=\int d^dx\,\theta$ in volume $V$,

$$
[\theta_0,N]=iV,
\qquad
\Delta\theta_0\,\Delta N\geq\frac V2.
$$

Equivalently, the averaged phase $\bar\theta=\theta_0/V$ obeys $\Delta\bar\theta\,\Delta N\geq1/2$. This [number-phase conjugacy](../../../../../../number-phase-conjugacy.md) means that a state of sharp $N$ has no sharp phase, whereas a phase-selected state exhibiting [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) must superpose different number sectors. Such sectors become effectively degenerate in the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md).
