<h1 id="2/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

Write $D=\rho_s/s$, so $\omega_k=Dk^2$, and take $\Gamma_k=\gamma|k|$. The small-wavenumber [static structure factor](../../../../../../static-structure-factor.md) behaves as

$$
S(k)\propto
\frac{TD\gamma}{|k|(\gamma^2+D^2k^2)^2}
\sim\frac{TD}{\gamma^3|k|}.
$$

The spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) of $|k|^{-1}$ in $d$ dimensions scales as $|x|^{1-d}$. Thus

$$
C(x)\sim \text{constant}\times |x|^{1-d},
$$

and in three dimensions $C(x)\sim |x|^{-2}$. Since this [two-point correlation function](../../../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md) tends to zero rather than to a nonzero constant at large separation, it is incompatible with true long-range ferromagnetic order and hence with [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) at this higher temperature.
