<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $z=(e^{-i\phi}\cos(\theta/2),\sin(\theta/2))^T$,

$$
z^\dagger\dot z=-i\cos^2\left(\frac\theta2\right)\dot\phi.
$$

Thus $i\kappa z^\dagger\dot z=(\kappa/2)(1+\cos\theta)\dot\phi$. Dropping the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) $(\kappa/2)\dot\phi$ and writing $s=\kappa/2$ gives

$$
\mathcal L_{
m WZ}=s\cos\theta\,\dot\phi.
$$

The leading rotationally invariant [gradient energy](../../../../../../gradient-energy.md) is $(\rho_s/2)(\nabla\mathbf n)^2$, so an effective Lagrangian is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=s\cos\theta\,\dot\phi-
\frac{\rho_s}{2}\left[(\nabla\theta)^2+sin^2\theta(\nabla\phi)^2\right].}
$$
