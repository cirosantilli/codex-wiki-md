<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [CP1 spinor representation](../../../../../../cp1-spinor-representation.md),

$$
n_i=z^\dagger\sigma_i z,
\qquad z^\dagger z=1,
$$

where the $\sigma_i$ are [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md). The transformation $z\mapsto e^{i\alpha(x,t)}z$ leaves $\mathbf n$ unchanged. For $U\in SU(2)$,

$$
U^\dagger\sigma_iU=R_{ij}\sigma_j
$$

with $R$ in the [special orthogonal group](../../../../../../special-orthogonal-group.md) $SO(3)$, so $z\mapsto Uz$ induces $n_i\mapsto R_{ij}n_j$.

A rotationally invariant first-order term is the [Ferromagnetic Wess–Zumino term](../../../../../../ferromagnetic-wess-zumino-term.md)

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm WZ}=i\kappa z^\dagger\dot z.
$$

Under the phase redundancy it changes as $\mathcal L_{\rm WZ}\mapsto\mathcal L_{\rm WZ}-\kappa\dot\alpha$. The change is a [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md), so the action has the required invariance.
