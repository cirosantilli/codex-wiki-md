<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For compactly supported smooth variations $(\phi,\psi)$, differentiating at $t=0$ gives

$$
0=\frac12\frac d{dt}E[X+t\phi,Y+t\psi]\bigg|_{t=0}
=\int_\Omega\left[
\frac{\nabla X\cdot\nabla\phi+\nabla Y\cdot\nabla\psi}{X^2}
-\frac{(|\nabla X|^2+|\nabla Y|^2)\phi}{X^3}
\right].
$$

This is the [weak formulation](../../../../../../weak-formulation.md). Taking $\psi=0$ or $\phi=0$ separates its two equations. When $X,Y$ are smooth, [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) transfers each derivative from the test function; the [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) then recovers exactly the two pointwise equations in part (a).
