<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The Lagrangian is

$$
F(X,Y,\nabla X,\nabla Y)=X^{-2}(|\nabla X|^2+|\nabla Y|^2).
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $X$ is

$$
-2X^{-3}(|\nabla X|^2+|\nabla Y|^2)
-\operatorname{div}(2X^{-2}\nabla X)=0.
$$

Expanding the divergence and simplifying gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta X=\frac{|\nabla X|^2-|\nabla Y|^2}{X}}.
$$

The equation for $Y$ is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{\nabla Y}{X^2}\right)=0}.
$$
