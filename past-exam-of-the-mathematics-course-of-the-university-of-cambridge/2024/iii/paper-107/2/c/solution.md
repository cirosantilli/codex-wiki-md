<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take a minimizing sequence $(X_k,Y_k)$ in the affine Sobolev class $W$. The standing bounds $C^{-1}\leq X_k\leq C$ make the energy uniformly equivalent to

$$
\|\nabla X_k\|_2^2+\|\nabla Y_k\|_2^2.
$$

The fixed boundary values and the [Poincaré inequality](../../../../../../poincare-inequality.md) therefore bound the sequence in $H^1(\Omega;\mathbb R^2)$. By weak compactness in a [reflexive Banach space](../../../../../../reflexive-banach-space.md), a subsequence converges weakly to $(X,Y)$. The assumed weak closedness keeps the limit in $W$, and the assumed [weak lower semicontinuity](../../../../../../weak-lower-semicontinuity.md) gives

$$
E[X,Y]\leq\liminf_kE[X_k,Y_k]=\inf_WE.
$$

**Thus the [direct method in the calculus of variations](../../../../../../direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md) produces a minimizer. Its first variation vanishes in every compactly supported direction, so part (b) makes it a weak solution of the system.**
