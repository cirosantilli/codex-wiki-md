↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first variation of the graph area functional is

$$
\frac d{dt}A(u+t\phi)\bigg|_{t=0}
=\int_{B_1}\frac{Du\cdot D\phi}{\sqrt{1+|Du|^2}}.
$$

Thus the [minimal surface equation for a graph](../../../../../../../minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md) is

$$
\operatorname{div}\frac{Du}{\sqrt{1+|Du|^2}}=0,
$$

or, in nondivergence form,

$$
\boxed{\left(\delta_{ij}-\frac{D_iuD_ju}{1+|Du|^2}\right)D_{ij}u=0.}
$$

Differentiate the divergence equation with respect to $x_k$. Then $w=D_ku$ satisfies

$$
D_i(a^{ij}(Du)D_jw)=0,
$$

where

$$
\boxed{a^{ij}(p)=\frac{\delta_{ij}}{\sqrt{1+|p|^2}}
-\frac{p_ip_j}{{(1+|p|^2)}^{3/2}}.}
$$
