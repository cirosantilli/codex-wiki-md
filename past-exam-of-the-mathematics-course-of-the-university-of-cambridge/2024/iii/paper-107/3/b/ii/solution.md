<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $|Du|\leq L$, the eigenvalues of $a^{ij}(Du)$ lie between $(1+L^2)^{-3/2}$ and $1$, so the differentiated equations are uniformly elliptic with bounded measurable coefficients depending only on $L$. Apply the [De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theorem](../../../../../../../de-giorgi-nash-moser-theorem.md) to each $D_ku$. Since $|D_ku|\leq L$, for every $\theta<1$,

$$
[Du]_{C^{0,\alpha}(B_\theta)}\leq C(n,L,\theta)
$$

for some $\alpha=\alpha(n,L)\in(0,1)$. The map

$$
p\longmapsto\delta_{ij}-\frac{p_ip_j}{1+|p|^2}
$$

is smooth with bounded derivative on $|p|\leq L$. Composition therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\|b^{ij}\|_{C^{0,\alpha}(B_\theta)}\leq\beta(n,L,\theta).}
$$
