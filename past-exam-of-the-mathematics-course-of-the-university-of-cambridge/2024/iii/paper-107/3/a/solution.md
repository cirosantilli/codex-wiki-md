<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theorem](../../../../../../de-giorgi-nash-moser-theorem.md) gives numbers $\alpha=\alpha(n,\lambda,\Lambda)\in(0,1)$ and $C=C(n,\lambda,\Lambda,\theta)$ such that every weak solution satisfies

$$
\boxed{\|u\|_{C^{0,\alpha}(B_\theta)}
\leq C\|u\|_{L^2(B_1)}}
$$

for each fixed $0<\theta<1$. In particular, every such weak solution has a locally Hölder-continuous representative despite the coefficients being only bounded and measurable.
