↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The operator is [strictly elliptic](../../../../../../strictly-elliptic-operator.md) when its symmetric principal matrix $A(x)=(a^{ij}(x))$ is positive definite at every point:

$$
a^{ij}(x)\xi_i\xi_j>0
\qquad(\xi\ne0).
$$

It is a [uniformly elliptic operator](../../../../../../uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) when some $\lambda>0$ satisfies

$$
a^{ij}(x)\xi_i\xi_j\geq\lambda|\xi|^2
$$

for every $x$ and $\xi$. Here the least eigenvalue of the continuous matrix $A(x)$ is a positive continuous function on the compact set $\overline B_1$. It therefore has a positive minimum, which supplies $\lambda$ and proves uniform ellipticity.
