↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Yes.** Since $|q|\leq\mu$ and $u\geq0$,

$$
(\Delta-\mu)u=(\Delta u+qu)-(q+\mu)u\leq0.
$$

The operator $\Delta-\mu$ is uniformly elliptic and has nonpositive zeroth-order coefficient. If $u(y)=0$ at an interior point, $u$ attains its nonnegative minimum there, so the [strong minimum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../strong-minimum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) gives $u\equiv0$ on the connected ball $B_1$.
