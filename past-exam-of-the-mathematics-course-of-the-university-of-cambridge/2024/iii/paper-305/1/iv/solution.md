<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In four spacetime dimensions a [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) has [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $3/2$, so each chiral current $J_L^\mu=\bar\psi_L\bar\sigma^\mu\psi_L$ or $J_R^\mu=\bar\psi_R\sigma^\mu\psi_R$ has dimension three. Since the interaction is $(gJ_L+g'J_R)^2$, dimensional homogeneity of the Lagrangian gives

$$
\boxed{[g]=[g']=-1}.
$$

Equivalently, the expanded [four-fermion interaction](../../../../../../four-fermion-interaction.md) has coefficients $g^2,gg',g'^2$ of mass dimension minus two.

Parity exchanges $J_L$ and $J_R$. The square is invariant precisely when the two same-chirality coefficients agree, $g^2=g'^2$, while the mixed coefficient is already symmetric. Therefore

$$
\boxed{g'=g\quad\text{or}\quad g'=-g}.
$$
