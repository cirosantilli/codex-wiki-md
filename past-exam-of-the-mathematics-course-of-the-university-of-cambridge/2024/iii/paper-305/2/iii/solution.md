<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $SU(3)_c$, one generation contains two color triplets in the quark doublet and the two right-handed color triplets, so $N_f=4N_g$ and there is no colored scalar. Thus

$$
b_s=33-4N_g>0,
$$

which permits at most

$$
\boxed{N_g=8}.
$$

For $SU(2)_L$, each generation contains three colored copies of the quark doublet and one lepton doublet, again giving $N_f=4N_g$. The one complex [Higgs doublet](../../../../../../higgs-field.md) gives $N_s=1$, so

$$
b_w=22-4N_g-\frac12>0.
$$

Hence the maximum is

$$
\boxed{N_g=5}.
$$
