↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Asymptotic freedom](../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) means that the gauge [running coupling](../../../../../../running-coupling.md) tends to zero as the renormalization scale tends to infinity. In the stated convention this occurs when $b_0>0$, namely

$$
\boxed{11N_c-N_f-\frac12N_s>0}.
$$

The high-energy theory then approaches the [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) and perturbation theory becomes increasingly accurate.
