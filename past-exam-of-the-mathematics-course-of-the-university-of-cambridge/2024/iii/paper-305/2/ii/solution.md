↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [strong-coupling scale](../../../../../../strong-coupling-scale.md) $\Lambda$ is defined as the scale at which the one-loop inverse coupling vanishes. Setting $g^{-2}(\Lambda)=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Lambda=\Lambda_{UV}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{3(4\pi)^2}{2b_0g_0^2}\right]}.
$$

Equivalently, in terms of the coupling measured at any scale $\mu$,

$$
\boxed{\Lambda=\mu
\exp\!\left[-\frac{3(4\pi)^2}{2b_0g^2(\mu)}\right]}.
$$

This [dimensional transmutation](../../../../../../dimensional-transmutation.md) defines $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$ for [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md).
