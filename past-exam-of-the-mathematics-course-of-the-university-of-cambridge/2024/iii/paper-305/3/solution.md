<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) breaks a redundancy required to remove unphysical polarizations. It violates the Ward identities and makes the quantum gauge theory inconsistent, so all gauge anomalies must cancel. A [chiral anomaly](../../../../../chiral-anomaly.md), or [Adler-Bell-Jackiw anomaly](../../../../../chiral-anomaly.md), instead breaks a classically conserved global axial symmetry; it is physically allowed and explains effects such as anomalous pseudoscalar decays and instanton-induced charge violation. A ['t Hooft anomaly](../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md) is an obstruction to gauging a global symmetry. It is invariant under [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md), so ['t Hooft anomaly matching](../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly-matching.md) constrains the infrared theory to reproduce it through massless fields, symmetry breaking, or a topological sector.
