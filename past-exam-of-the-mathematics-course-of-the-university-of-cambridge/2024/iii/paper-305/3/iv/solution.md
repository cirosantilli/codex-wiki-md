<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $q\ne0$, divide the cubic equation by $q^3$ and define the [rational numbers](../../../../../../rational-number.md)

$$
\widetilde y=\frac yq,
\qquad
\widetilde x=\frac xq.
$$

Then every integer anomaly-free assignment supplies a rational solution of

$$
\boxed{54+18\widetilde y^{,2}+\widetilde x^{,3}=0,
\qquad \widetilde x,\widetilde y\in\mathbb Q}.
$$
