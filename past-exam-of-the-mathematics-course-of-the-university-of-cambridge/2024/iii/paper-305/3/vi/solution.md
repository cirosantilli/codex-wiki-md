<h1 id="3/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The coefficient of the [mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly](../../../../../../mixed-gauge-gravitational-anomaly.md) is the sum of all left-handed $U(1)$ charges, with right-handed fields counted as conjugates:

$$
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{grav}^2U(1)}=6q+2l-3u-3d-x.
$$

For the solution above,

$$
6+2(-3)-3(4)-3(-2)-(-6)=0.
$$

More invariantly, $l=-3q$, $u+d=2q$, and $x=-6q$ make the expression vanish identically. Thus the unique cubic-anomaly solution automatically cancels the mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly.
