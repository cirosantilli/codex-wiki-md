<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A single quintic vertex has five half-edges, so [Wick theorem](../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) cannot contract all of them in pairs. The leading connected [Vacuum Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../vacuum-feynman-diagram.md) therefore have two quintic vertices and are of order $g^2$. If $c$ propagators join the two vertices, each vertex has $5-c$ half-edges left for tadpoles, so $c$ must be odd. This gives exactly three topologies:

- $c=1$: one line joins the vertices and each vertex carries two tadpole loops.
- $c=3$: three lines join the vertices and each vertex carries one tadpole loop.
- $c=5$: all five lines join the vertices.

The three diagrams are connected; every other pairing is either isomorphic to one of them or disconnected.
