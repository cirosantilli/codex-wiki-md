<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Writing the source interaction as $\operatorname{Tr}(J^\mu A_\mu)$, gauge invariance requires the matrix current to transform in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md),

$$
\boxed{J^\mu\mapsto UJ^\mu U^\dagger}.
$$

The fermion bilinear with this transformation law is

$$
\boxed{J^{\mu a}=g\,\bar\psi_i\gamma^\mu(T^a)_{ij}\psi_j},
\qquad
J^\mu=J^{\mu a}T_a,
$$

up to a convention-dependent overall coupling or generator normalization. It is the [Noether current](../../../../../../noether-current.md) that appears when the free derivative is replaced by $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$.
