<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define

$$
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu-ig[A_\mu,A_\nu].
$$

The gauge-field transformation is precisely the one for which the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$ transforms by $D_\mu' =UD_\mu U^\dagger$. Since $[D_\mu,D_\nu]=-igF_{\mu\nu}$, it follows immediately that

$$
\boxed{F_{\mu\nu}'=UF_{\mu\nu}U^\dagger}.
$$

For $U=1+i\alpha^cT_c+O(\alpha^2)$,

$$
\delta F_{\mu\nu}=i[\alpha^cT_c,F_{\mu\nu}^bT_b].
$$

Define the [structure constant of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md) by $[T_b,T_c]=if_{bc}{}^aT_a$. Antisymmetry then gives

$$
\boxed{\delta F_{\mu\nu}^a=f_{bc}{}^aF_{\mu\nu}^b\alpha^c}.
$$
