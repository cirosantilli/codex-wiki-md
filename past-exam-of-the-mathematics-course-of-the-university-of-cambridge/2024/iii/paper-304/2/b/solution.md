<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The amputated connected four-point function through one loop contains the tree quartic vertex, the quartic-counterterm vertex, and three bubble diagrams in the $s$, $t$, and $u$ channels. The unamputated connected function also has one-loop self-energy and two-point-counterterm insertions on each external propagator, which do not determine $\delta g$.

For channel momentum $P$, the bubble has symmetry factor $1/2$ and the [Feynman parameter](../../../../../../feynman-parameter.md) identity gives an integral proportional to

$$
\frac{g^2}{2}\mu^\epsilon
\int_0^1dx\int\frac{d^d\ell}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac1{[\ell^2+\Delta(x,P)]^2}.
$$

In [dimensional regularization](../../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md), $\Gamma(\epsilon/2)=2/\epsilon+O(1)$, so the pole from one channel is $g^2/(16\pi^2\epsilon)$. Summing the three crossing channels gives $3g^2/(16\pi^2\epsilon)$. The [minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../../../minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) cancels only this pole, hence

$$
\boxed{\delta g=\frac{3g^2}{16\pi^2\epsilon}}.
$$
