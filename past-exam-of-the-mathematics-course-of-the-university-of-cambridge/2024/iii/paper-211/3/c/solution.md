<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The explicit [stochastic exponential](../../../../../../doleans-dade-exponential.md) solutions satisfy

$$
S_T=\widetilde S_T
\exp\left\{-\frac12(1-\rho^2)Y_T
+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\int_0^T\sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t^\perp\right\}.
$$

Conditionally on the path generated by $W$, the last stochastic integral is a centered [Gaussian random variable](../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md) with variance $Y_T$, because $W^\perp$ is independent of $W$. The conditional expectation of $g(S_T)$ is therefore

$$
G\bigl(\widetilde S_T,(1-\rho^2)Y_T\bigr).
$$

Taking expectations and using part b proves the result.
