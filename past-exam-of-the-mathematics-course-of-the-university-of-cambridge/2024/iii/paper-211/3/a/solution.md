<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The cash-discounted stock is a positive [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md), because its dynamics contain no drift. Applying [Itô formula](../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) to $\pi_t=U(t,v_t,S_t)$, the displayed partial differential equation cancels its drift exactly, leaving another local martingale. Since $U$ is bounded, $\pi$ is in fact a true martingale.

Thus the physical measure itself is an [equivalent local martingale measure](../../../../../../equivalent-local-martingale-measure.md) relative to cash for all three traded assets. The continuous-time [fundamental theorem of asset pricing](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-asset-pricing.md) rules out arbitrage, more precisely no free lunch with vanishing risk, in the usual admissible class.
