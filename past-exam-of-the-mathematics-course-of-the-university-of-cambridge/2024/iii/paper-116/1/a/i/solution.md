A [strongly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../../strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md) is an uncountable [regular cardinal](../../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$ that is also a [strong limit cardinal](../../../../../../../strong-limit-cardinal.md):

$$
\boxed{\lambda<\kappa\quad\Longrightarrow\quad2^\lambda<\kappa.}
$$
