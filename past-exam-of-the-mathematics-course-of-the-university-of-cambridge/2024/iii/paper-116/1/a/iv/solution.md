An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is a [strongly compact cardinal](../../../../../../../strongly-compact-cardinal.md) when every $\kappa$-satisfiable theory in any $L_{\kappa,\kappa}$ language is satisfiable. Unlike weak compactness, there is no cardinality bound on the language.
