A filter $U$ is [$\kappa$-complete](../../../../../../../kappa-complete-filter.md) when intersections of fewer than $\kappa$ members remain in $U$. An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is a [measurable cardinal](../../../../../../../measurable-cardinal.md) when it carries a $\kappa$-complete [nonprincipal ultrafilter](../../../../../../../nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md).
