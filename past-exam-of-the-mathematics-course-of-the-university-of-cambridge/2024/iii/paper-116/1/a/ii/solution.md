A theory is [$\kappa$-satisfiable](../../../../../../../kappa-satisfiable-theory.md) when every subtheory of size below $\kappa$ has a model. An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is [weakly compact](../../../../../../../weakly-compact-cardinal.md) when every $\kappa$-satisfiable theory in an infinitary language $L_{\kappa,\kappa}$ with at most $\kappa$ nonlogical symbols is satisfiable.

Equivalently, $\kappa\to(\kappa)^2_2$: every coloring $c:[\kappa]^2\to2$ has a homogeneous subset of cardinality $\kappa$.
