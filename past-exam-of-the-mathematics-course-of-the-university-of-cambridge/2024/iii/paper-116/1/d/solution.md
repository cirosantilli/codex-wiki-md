<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For regular $\kappa$, the [cobounded filter on a regular cardinal](../../../../../../cobounded-filter-on-a-regular-cardinal.md) is $\kappa$-complete. Part (c) extends it to a $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter $U$. Since $\kappa\setminus\{\alpha\}$ is cobounded for every $\alpha<\kappa$, no singleton belongs to $U$; hence $U$ is nonprincipal. Thus every strongly compact cardinal is [measurable](../../../../../../measurable-cardinal.md).
