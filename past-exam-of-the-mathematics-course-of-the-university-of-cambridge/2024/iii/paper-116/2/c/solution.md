<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let

$$
\widehat\kappa=\sup_{n<\omega}j^n(\kappa)
$$

be the supremum of the [Kunen critical sequence](../../../../../../kunen-critical-sequence.md), and put

$$
X=j^{\prime\prime}\widehat\kappa
=\{j(\xi):\xi<\widehat\kappa\}.
$$

The required choices are

$$
\boxed{\beta=\widehat\kappa+2,\qquad
\gamma=\widehat\kappa+1,\qquad
X=j^{\prime\prime}\widehat\kappa.}
$$

Indeed $X$ has rank $\widehat\kappa$, so $X\in V_{\widehat\kappa+1}$, while the [Kunen lemma](../../../../../../kunen-lemma.md) gives $X\notin M$ once the domain contains the omega-Jonsson function required by the proof, which is ensured by $\alpha\geq\widehat\kappa+2$.
