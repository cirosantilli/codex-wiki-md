<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The standard closure lemma for the [ultrapower embedding](../../../../../../../ultrapower-embedding.md) says that every $\kappa$-sequence of members of $M$ which belongs to $V_\lambda$ is itself in $M$. If $\kappa<\alpha<\kappa^+$, choose in $V_\lambda$ a surjection

$$
s:\kappa\longrightarrow\alpha.
$$

All ordinal values of $s$ belong to the transitive model $M$, so closure gives $s\in M$. Therefore

$$
M\models|\alpha|\leq\kappa<\alpha,
$$

and $M$ does not regard $\alpha$ as a cardinal.
