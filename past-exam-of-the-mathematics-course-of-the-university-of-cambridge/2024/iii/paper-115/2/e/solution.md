The [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is the commutator of the corresponding derivations:

$$
[X,Y]f=X(Yf)-Y(Xf).
$$

For $X=X^i\partial_i$ and $Y=Y^i\partial_i$,

$$
\boxed{[X,Y]=\left(X^i\partial_iY^k-Y^i\partial_iX^k\right)\partial_k.}
$$
