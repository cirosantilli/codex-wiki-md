The [equivalent formulations of orientability of a smooth manifold](../../../../../../equivalent-formulations-of-orientability-of-a-smooth-manifold.md) are:

- an atlas whose transition maps have positive [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md);
- a smooth choice of one of the two orientations of every [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md);
- a nowhere-zero smooth top-degree [differential form](../../../../../../differential-form-split.md).

A positive chart orients its coordinate frame. Conversely, smoothly oriented frames determine local positive coordinate volume forms. A [partition of unity](../../../../../../partition-of-unity.md) subordinate to an oriented atlas glues these positive forms: at each point they are positive multiples of one another, so their weighted sum cannot vanish. Finally, a nowhere-zero top form $\omega$ declares $(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ positive exactly when $\omega(v_1,\ldots,v_n)>0$. These constructions are inverse at the level of orientations.
