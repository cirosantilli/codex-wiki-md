<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $v$ near $0\in T_pM$, let $\gamma_v$ be the unique geodesic with $\gamma_v(0)=p$ and $\dot\gamma_v(0)=v$. The [exponential map](../../../../../../exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) is $\exp_p(v)=\gamma_v(1)$.

The constant initial velocity $0$ gives $\exp_p(0)=p$. Varying the initial velocity through $sv$ yields $\exp_p(sv)=\gamma_v(s)$, so

$$
(d\exp_p)_0(v)=\left.\frac d{ds}\right|_{0}\exp_p(sv)=v.
$$

**Thus $(d\exp_p)_0$ is the identity. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) makes $\exp_p$ a diffeomorphism from a neighborhood of $0$ onto a neighborhood of $p$. Coordinates from an orthonormal basis of $T_pM$ are the [geodesic normal coordinates](../../../../../../geodesic-normal-coordinates.md).**
