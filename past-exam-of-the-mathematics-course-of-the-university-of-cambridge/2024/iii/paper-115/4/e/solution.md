<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $\alpha=\delta\eta$. The hypothesis says that the exact two-form $\beta=d\alpha$ is [anti-self-dual](../../../../../../anti-self-dual-differential-form.md). Since $M$ is compact without boundary, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) gives

$$
0=\int_Md(\alpha\wedge d\alpha)
=\int_M\beta\wedge\beta
=-\int_M\beta\wedge *\beta
=-\|\beta\|_{L^2}^2.
$$

Hence $d\alpha=0$ by the [exact anti-self-dual form on a compact four-manifold](../../../../../../exact-anti-self-dual-form-on-a-compact-four-manifold.md) argument. Since $\delta$ is the formal $L^2$ adjoint of $d$,

$$
\|\delta\eta\|_{L^2}^2
=\langle\delta\eta,\alpha\rangle_{L^2}
=\langle\eta,d\alpha\rangle_{L^2}=0.
$$

Therefore $\boxed{\delta\eta=0}$.
