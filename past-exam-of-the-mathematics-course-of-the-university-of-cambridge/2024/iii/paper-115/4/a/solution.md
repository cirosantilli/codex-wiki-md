<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For local coordinates $x^1,\ldots,x^n$, a curve is an affinely parametrized [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) exactly when it satisfies the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot x^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}(x)\dot x^i\dot x^j=0,\qquad k=1,\ldots,n,}
$$

where the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) of the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^k_{ij}=\frac12g^{k\ell}
(\partial_i g_{j\ell}+\partial_jg_{i\ell}-\partial_\ell g_{ij}).}
$$
