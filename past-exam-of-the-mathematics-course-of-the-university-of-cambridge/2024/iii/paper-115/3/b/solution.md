<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $b=\pi(p)$ and $u=(d\pi)_p v$. Choose a vector field $X$ near $b$ with $X(b)=u$, multiply it by a [bump function](../../../../../../smooth-bump-function.md), and extend it by zero to $B$. At each $q\in E$, the restriction

$$
(d\pi)_q:H_qE\longrightarrow T_{\pi(q)}B
$$

is an isomorphism. Define $V(q)$ as the unique horizontal lift of $X(\pi(q))$. Smoothness of the horizontal distribution makes $V$ smooth, and uniqueness gives $V(p)=v$.
