<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s$. The assumed coefficient bounds give

$$
|a_n\overline{b_n}n^{-s}|
\leq C^2n^{(k+l)/2-\sigma}.
$$

The comparison [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) converges exactly when

$$
\frac{k+l}{2}-\sigma<-1,
$$

or $\operatorname{Re}s>1+(k+l)/2$. Therefore the [Rankin–Selberg convolution](../../../../../../rankin-selberg-convolution.md) $L(f,g,s)$ converges absolutely in the stated half-plane.
