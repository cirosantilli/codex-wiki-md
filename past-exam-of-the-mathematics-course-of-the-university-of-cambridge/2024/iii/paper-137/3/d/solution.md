<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The specialization of the [Jacobi triple product](../../../../../../jacobi-triple-product.md) to the [Jacobi theta function](../../../../../../jacobi-theta-function.md) is

$$
\theta(\tau)=\prod_{n\geq1}(1-q^{2n})(1+q^{2n-1})^2,
\qquad q=e^{\pi i\tau}.
$$

Since $\tau\in\mathfrak h$, one has $|q|<1$, so every displayed factor is nonzero. Moreover

$$
\sum_{n\geq1}\bigl(|q|^{2n}+2|q|^{2n-1}\bigr)<\infty.
$$

The standard convergence criterion for [infinite products](../../../../../../infinite-product.md) therefore shows that the product converges to a nonzero value. This proves the [nonvanishing of the Jacobi theta function](../../../../../../nonvanishing-of-the-jacobi-theta-function.md) throughout $\mathfrak h$.
