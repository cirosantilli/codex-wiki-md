<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [dimension of level-one cusp forms](../../../../../../dimension-of-level-one-cusp-forms.md) is zero when $k$ is odd or $k<12$. For even $k\geq12$ it is

$$
\dim S_k(\Gamma(1))=
\begin{cases}
\left\lfloor k/12\right\rfloor-1,&k\equiv2\pmod{12},\\
\left\lfloor k/12\right\rfloor,&k\not\equiv2\pmod{12}.
\end{cases}
$$

Equivalently, multiplication by the [modular discriminant](../../../../../../modular-discriminant.md) gives $S_k(\Gamma(1))=\Delta M_{k-12}(\Gamma(1))$, and the displayed formula follows from the standard dimension formula for $M_{k-12}(\Gamma(1))$.
