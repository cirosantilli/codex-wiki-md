↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathcal L$ be the set of [lattices](../../../../../../../lattice.md) in $\mathbb C$. The [lattice model of a modular form](../../../../../../../lattice-model-of-a-modular-form.md) is

$$
V_k=\{F:\mathcal L\to\mathbb C:F(\lambda\Lambda)=\lambda^{-k}F(\Lambda)
\text{ for every }\lambda\in\mathbb C^\times\}.
$$

For the normalization used here, the $n$th [Hecke operator on lattice functions](../../../../../../../hecke-operator-on-lattice-functions.md) is

$$
(T_nF)(\Lambda)
=n^{k-1}\sum_{\Lambda'\supset\Lambda\atop[\Lambda':\Lambda]=n}F(\Lambda').
$$

There are finitely many such superlattices, and scaling gives a bijection between those above $\Lambda$ and those above $\lambda\Lambda$, so $T_nF$ again has weight $k$.
