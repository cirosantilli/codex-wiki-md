↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [connected Feynman diagram](../../../../../../connected-feynman-diagram.md) that is a cubic tree with five external legs has three vertices and two internal lines. Its unique unlabeled topology is a chain: two external legs meet at each end vertex and one external leg attaches to the middle vertex. The [five-point tree amplitude in phi cubed theory](../../../../../../five-point-tree-amplitude-in-phi-cubed-theory.md) therefore has fifteen labeled [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md): choose the middle external leg in five ways, then partition the remaining four legs into two unordered pairs in three ways.
