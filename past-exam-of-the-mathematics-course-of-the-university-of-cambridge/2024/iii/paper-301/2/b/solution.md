The momentum-space [Feynman rules](../../../../../../feynman-rule.md) for [Phi cubed theory](../../../../../../phi-cubed-theory.md) are

$$
\text{internal scalar line: }\frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i\epsilon},
\qquad
\text{cubic vertex: }-ig.
$$

Each vertex carries $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}$ of momentum conservation, each independent loop momentum is integrated with $d^4\ell/(2\pi)^4$, and a graph is divided by its [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). External propagators are omitted from an amputated [scattering amplitude](../../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md).
