↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In four spacetime dimensions the action is dimensionless, so the [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) of the Lagrangian density is $[\mathcal L]=4$. The kinetic term gives $[\phi]=1$, and $g\phi^3$ then gives

$$
[g]=4-3[\phi]=1.
$$

**Thus $g$ has mass dimension one and is a [relevant coupling](../../../../../../relevant-coupling.md) under [power counting in quantum field theory](../../../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md).**
