<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md), $\phi\leftrightarrow\phi^*$ must be accompanied by

$$
A_\mu\mapsto-A_\mu.
$$

Then $D_\mu\phi$ is exchanged with $(D_\mu\phi)^*$, while $F_{\mu\nu}\mapsto-F_{\mu\nu}$. The scalar kinetic and mass terms are therefore exchanged with themselves and $F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ is unchanged, proving invariance of [scalar quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../../scalar-electrodynamics.md).
