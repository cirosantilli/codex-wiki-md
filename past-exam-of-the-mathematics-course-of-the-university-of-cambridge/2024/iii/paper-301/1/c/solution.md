<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) $A^\mu(x)=\epsilon^\mu(k)e^{-ik\cdot x}$, the equations from part a become

$$
k^2=m^2,
\qquad
k_\mu\epsilon^\mu=0.
$$

In the rest frame $k^\mu=(m,\mathbf0)$, transversality sets $\epsilon^0=0$ while the three spatial components remain independent. Hence a [Proca field](../../../../../../proca-field.md) has three polarization states, as required for a massive spin-one particle. A covariant orthonormal choice obeys

$$
\boxed{\sum_{\lambda=1}^3\epsilon_\mu^{(\lambda)}(k)epsilon_\nu^{(\lambda)}(k)^*
=-\eta_{\mu\nu}+\frac{k_\mu k_\nu}{m^2}.}
$$
