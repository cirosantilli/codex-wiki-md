↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The symmetric [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) obtained by metric variation is

$$
T^{\mu\nu}=-F^{\mu\rho}F^\nu{}_{\rho}
+\frac14\eta^{\mu\nu}F^{\rho\sigma}F_{\rho\sigma}
+m^2\left(A^\mu A^\nu-\frac12\eta^{\mu\nu}A^\rho A_\rho\right).
$$

It is conserved on shell. With signature $(+---)$ its energy density is

$$
T^{00}=\frac12(\mathbf E^2+\mathbf B^2)
+\frac{m^2}{2}\{(A^0)^2+\mathbf A^2\}\geq0.
$$

The [canonical stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md) differs from this symmetric tensor by the divergence of $F^{\mu\rho}A^\nu$ plus terms proportional to the field equation. Thus its integrated energy agrees after discarding the corresponding total spatial derivative, and positivity holds on shell.
