<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Keep $b=t^{-1}at$. Repeatedly applying $bab^{-1}=a^2$ gives

$$
b^na b^{-n}=a^{2^n}.
$$

In the [word metric](../../../../../../word-metric.md) on $G$ from the finite generating set $\{a,t\}$, the right-hand side therefore has length at most $6n+1$, because $b$ is represented by the word $t^{-1}at$. In the intrinsic word metric on the [infinite cyclic group](../../../../../../infinite-cyclic-group.md) $\langle a\rangle$ generated by $a$, however,

$$
|a^{2^n}|_{\langle a\rangle}=2^n.
$$

A [quasi-isometric embedding](../../../../../../quasi-isometric-embedding.md) would bound the latter by an affine function of the former. The exponential sequence above violates every such bound, so the inclusion $\langle a\rangle\hookrightarrow G$ is not a quasi-isometric embedding.
