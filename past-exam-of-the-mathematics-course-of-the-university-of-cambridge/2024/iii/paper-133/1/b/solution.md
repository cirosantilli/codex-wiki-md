<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Introduce $b=t^{-1}at$. The presentation may be written as the [HNN extension](../../../../../../hnn-extension.md)

$$
G=\left\langle a,b,t\mathrel{\middle|}bab^{-1}=a^2, t^{-1}at=b\right\rangle
$$

of the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md) $\operatorname{BS}(1,2)=\langle a,b\mid bab^{-1}=a^2\rangle$, with stable letter $t$ identifying the infinite cyclic subgroups $\langle a\rangle$ and $\langle b\rangle$. [Britton's lemma](../../../../../../britton-s-lemma.md) embeds the base group in the HNN extension. In particular, $a$ has [infinite order](../../../../../../infinite-order.md), so $G$ is infinite. It is also nonabelian because $bab^{-1}=a^2\ne a$.
