<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\bar a=f(a)$, $\bar t=f(t)$, and $\bar b=\bar a^{\bar t}=\bar t^{-1}\bar a\bar t$. The elements $\bar a$ and $\bar b$ are [conjugate group elements](../../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md), so they have the same [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md), say $n$. The defining relation becomes

$$
\bar b\bar a\bar b^{-1}=\bar a^2.
$$

Iterating [conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) gives $\bar b^j\bar a\bar b^{-j}=\bar a^{2^j}$. Since $\bar b^n=1$, taking $j=n$ yields $\bar a=\bar a^{2^n}$, and hence

$$
n\mid 2^n-1.
$$

The stated consequence of [Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) implies $n=1$. Thus $\bar a=1$, and the [surjective group homomorphism](../../../../../../surjective-group-homomorphism.md) $f$ shows that $Q$ is generated by $\bar t$. Therefore $Q$ is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md).
