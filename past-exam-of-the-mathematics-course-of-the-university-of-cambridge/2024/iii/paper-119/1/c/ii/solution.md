<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume every category of elements of a representable presheaf is a preorder. Let

$$
P=\coprod_{A\in\operatorname{ob}\mathcal C}(1\downarrow\mathcal C(-,A)).
$$

A disjoint union of preorders is a preorder. The category-of-elements projection $P\to\mathcal C$ is a [discrete fibration](../../../../../../../discrete-fibration.md). It is surjective on objects because $B\in\mathcal C$ is the image of the object $1_B:B\to B$ in the summand indexed by $B$.
