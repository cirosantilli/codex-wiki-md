<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose a preorder $P$ admits a discrete fibration $q:P\to\mathcal C$ that is surjective on objects. Given $f:B\to A$ in $\mathcal C$, choose $a\in P$ above $A$. The discrete-fibration property lifts $f$ to $\widetilde f:b\to a$. Every arrow in a preorder is monic, and a [discrete fibration](../../../../../../../discrete-fibration.md) preserves monomorphisms by part (b), so $f=q(\widetilde f)$ is monic. This proves the remaining implication and hence the equivalence.
