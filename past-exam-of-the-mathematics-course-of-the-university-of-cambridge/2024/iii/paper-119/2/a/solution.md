<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of $D:\mathcal J\to\mathcal C$ is a terminal cone $(L,(p_j))$: every cone $(X,(x_j))$ has a unique map $X\to L$ commuting with all legs.

Assume all small products and [equalizers](../../../../../../equaliser.md) exist. Put

$$
P=\prod_{j\in\mathcal J}D(j),
\qquad
Q=\prod_{u:i\to j}D(j).
$$

Define $s,t:P\rightrightarrows Q$ so that the $u:i\to j$ components are

$$
s_u=D(u)\pi_i,\qquad t_u=\pi_j.
$$

A map $X\to P$ equalizes $s,t$ exactly when its components form a cone over $D$. Therefore $\operatorname{eq}(s,t)$ represents cones and is the limit. This is the [construction of small limits from products and equalizers](../../../../../../construction-of-small-limits-from-products-and-equalizers.md).
