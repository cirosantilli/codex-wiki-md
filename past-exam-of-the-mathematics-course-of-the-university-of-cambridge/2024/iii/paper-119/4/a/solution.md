For an adjunction $F\dashv U:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$ with induced monad $T=UF$, the [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](../../../../../../eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md) is

$$
K:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C^T,\qquad
K(D)=(UD,U\varepsilon_D).
$$

The adjunction is [monadic](../../../../../../monadic-adjunction.md) when $K$ is an equivalence.

The [Crude monadicity theorem](../../../../../../crude-monadicity-theorem.md) states that a right adjoint is monadic if it reflects isomorphisms, its source has coequalizers of reflexive pairs, and it preserves those coequalizers. The dual statement is the crude comonadicity theorem.
