<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Identities are self-adjoint. If $f\dashv g$ and $f'\dashv g'$, then

$$
f'f\,gg'=f'(fg)g'\leq f'g'\leq1,
\qquad
1\leq gf\leq gg'f'f,
$$

with the second inequality written after inserting the two units in the appropriate order. Hence $f'f\dashv gg'$, so left adjoints form a subcategory.

In $\mathbf{Poset}$, these are exactly monotone maps possessing right adjoints, equivalently [lower adjoints](../../../../../../galois-connection.md); when all joins exist, they are precisely the arbitrary-join-preserving maps.

In the inclusion-ordered [category of relations](../../../../../../category-of-relations.md), a relation $R:A\rightsquigarrow B$ is left adjoint exactly when it is total and single-valued. It is therefore the graph of a function, and its right adjoint is $R^{\mathrm{op}}$. This is the [left adjoint relation is a function](../../../../../../left-adjoint-relation-is-a-function.md) criterion.
