<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The discriminant is $1909=23\cdot83$, so $3,5,7$ are primes of [good reduction of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). The [reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](../../../../../../../reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) injects the prime-to-$p$ torsion into $\widetilde E(\mathbb F_p)$.

If a prime $\ell$ divided the order of $E(\mathbb Q)_{\rm tors}$, its primary subgroup would inject at every one of $3,5,7$ except possibly when $p=\ell$. For $\ell=3$, the counts at $5$ and $7$ have greatest common divisor one; for $\ell=5$ use the counts at $3$ and $7$; for $\ell=7$ use those at $3$ and $5$; every other $\ell$ would divide all three counts. Each possibility is excluded. Hence

$$
\boxed{E(\mathbb Q)_{\rm tors}=0.}
$$
