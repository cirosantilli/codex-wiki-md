<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a minimal integral Weierstrass equation, let $\widetilde E$ be the reduced cubic and $\widetilde E_{\rm ns}(k)$ its nonsingular points, with their induced group law. Define the [filtration of elliptic-curve points over a local field](../../../../../../filtration-of-elliptic-curve-points-over-a-local-field.md) by

$$
E_0(K)=\{P\in E(K):\widetilde P\in\widetilde E_{\rm ns}(k)\},
$$

and

$$
E_1(K)=\ker\left(E_0(K)\longrightarrow\widetilde E_{\rm ns}(k)\right).
$$

The parameter $z=-x/y$ identifies $E_1(K)$ with the [formal group of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) on $\pi\mathcal O_K$. Part (a) therefore gives $E_1(K)[n]=0$. Reduction restricts to the exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow E_1(K)\longrightarrow E_0(K)
\longrightarrow\widetilde E_{\rm ns}(k)\longrightarrow0.
$$

Its restriction to $n$-torsion has trivial kernel, yielding the injection

$$
\boxed{E_0(K)[n]\hookrightarrow\widetilde E_{\rm ns}(k).}
$$
