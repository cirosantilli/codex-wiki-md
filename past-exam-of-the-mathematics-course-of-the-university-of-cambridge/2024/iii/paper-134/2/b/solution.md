<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Fix $x\in S$ and let $\sigma_x$ be its [principal vertex of a dual cube complex](../../../../../../principal-vertex-of-a-dual-cube-complex.md). The combinatorial distance in the dual complex is the [wall metric](../../../../../../wall-metric.md):

$$
d_C(\sigma_x,g\sigma_x)
=d_{\mathcal W}(x,gx)
=\#\{W\in\mathcal W:W\text{ separates }x\text{ from }gx\}.
$$

Consequently the [wall-metric properness criterion](../../../../../../wall-metric-properness-criterion.md) says that the action on $C$ is metrically proper provided this number tends to infinity as $g$ leaves every finite subset of $G$. Equivalently, for every $R$, only finitely many $g$ separate $x$ from $gx$ by at most $R$ walls.
