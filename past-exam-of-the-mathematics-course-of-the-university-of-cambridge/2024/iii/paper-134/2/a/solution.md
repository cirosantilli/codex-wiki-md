<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A group is cubulated when it admits a [cubulation of a group](../../../../../../cubulation-of-a-group.md), namely a [metrically proper group action](../../../../../../metrically-proper-group-action.md) by cubical automorphisms on a [CAT(0) cube complex](../../../../../../cat-0-cube-complex.md). It is cocompactly cubulated when that action is also a [cocompact group action](../../../../../../cocompact-group-action.md). Thus a cocompact cubulation is a proper, cocompact cubical action.
