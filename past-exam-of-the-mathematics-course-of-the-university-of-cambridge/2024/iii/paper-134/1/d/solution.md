<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [hyperplane of a Salvetti complex](../../../../../../hyperplane-of-a-salvetti-complex.md) dual to the loop labelled $v$ is itself the Salvetti complex of the [induced subgraph](../../../../../../induced-subgraph.md) on the neighbours of $v$. If this hyperplane were homeomorphic to a closed surface, its vertex link would be a cycle. Part (c), applied to that induced graph, says that the graph must be one edge. Its Salvetti complex is therefore the two-dimensional [torus](../../../../../../torus.md), a surface of genus one. It cannot be a [closed orientable surface](../../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md) of genus two.
