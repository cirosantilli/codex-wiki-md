<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $x=\sqrt{2\nu t}+ct$ and $y=c^2t/\nu$. Then

$$
\frac{cx}{\nu}=\sqrt{2y}+y,
\qquad
\sqrt{1+2(\sqrt{2y}+y)}=1+\sqrt{2y}.
$$

Consequently

$$
h\left(\frac{cx}{\nu}\right)
=1+\sqrt{2y}+y-(1+\sqrt{2y})=y.
$$

The tail bound from part c therefore has exponent $-(\nu/c^2)y=-t$, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\left(X\geq\sqrt{2\nu t}+ct\right)\leq e^{-t}.}
$$
