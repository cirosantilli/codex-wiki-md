<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) is finite in a neighborhood to the right of zero and $\mathbb EX=0$, its [cumulant-generating function](../../../../../../cumulant-generating-function.md) satisfies

$$
\psi_X(\lambda)=\frac{\lambda^2}{2}\operatorname{Var}(X)+o(\lambda^2)
\qquad(\lambda\downarrow0).
$$

Divide the defining [Sub-Gamma random variable in the right tail](../../../../../../sub-gamma-random-variable-in-the-right-tail.md) inequality by $\lambda^2/2$ and let $\lambda\downarrow0$. The right side converges to $\nu$, proving $\operatorname{Var}(X)\leq\nu$.
