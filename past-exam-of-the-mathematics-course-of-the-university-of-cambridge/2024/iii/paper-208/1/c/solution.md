<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Chernoff bound](../../../../../../chernoff-bound.md) gives, for $0<\lambda<1/c$,

$$
\mathbb P(X\geq t)
\leq\exp\left\{-\lambda t+
\frac{\nu\lambda^2}{2(1-c\lambda)}\right\}.
$$

Writing $u=ct/\nu$, elementary differentiation shows that the exponent is minimized at

$$
\lambda_*=\frac1c\left(1-\frac1{\sqrt{1+2u}}\right).
$$

Substitution gives

$$
\mathbb P(X\geq t)\leq
\exp\left\{-\frac{\nu}{c^2}h(u)\right\},
\qquad
h(u)=1+u-\sqrt{1+2u}.
$$

**Thus $h$ is the [Legendre transform](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) generated by the sub-Gamma cumulant bound after its natural rescaling.**
