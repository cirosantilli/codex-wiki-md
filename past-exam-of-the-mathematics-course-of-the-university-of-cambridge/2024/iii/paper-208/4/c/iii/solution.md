↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The function $-g$ is convex and $1$-Lipschitz. Applying part ii to $-g$ turns its lower-tail event into the upper-tail event for $g$:

$$
\mathbb P(W-\mathbb EW\geq t)
=\mathbb P((-W)-\mathbb E(-W)\leq-t)
\leq e^{-t^2/2}.
$$

This proves probability (iii).
