The [Chebotarev density theorem](../../../../../../chebotarev-density-theorem.md) states that for a finite Galois extension $L/K$ and a conjugacy class $C\subseteq\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, the unramified primes whose Frobenius conjugacy class is $C$ have Dirichlet density

$$
\boxed{\frac{|C|}{|\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)|}}.
$$
