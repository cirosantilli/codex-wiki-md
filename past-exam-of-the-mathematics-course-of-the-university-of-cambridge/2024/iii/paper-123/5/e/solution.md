<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A prime ideal of $K$ is principal exactly when its Artin symbol in

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(H_K/K)\cong\operatorname{Cl}(K)
$$

is the identity. The group has order two. Applying the [Chebotarev density theorem](../../../../../../chebotarev-density-theorem.md) to the identity conjugacy class gives density

$$
\boxed{\frac12}.
$$
