<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a set $S$ of prime ideals, its [Dirichlet density](../../../../../../dirichlet-density.md) is

$$
\delta(S)=\lim_{s\to1^+}
\frac{\sum_{\mathfrak p\in S}N\mathfrak p^{-s}}
{\log(1/(s-1))}
$$

when this limit exists.
