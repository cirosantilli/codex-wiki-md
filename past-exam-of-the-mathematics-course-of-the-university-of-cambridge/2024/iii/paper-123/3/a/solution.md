↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Dirichlet character](../../../../../../dirichlet-character.md) modulo $m$ is a homomorphism

$$
\chi:(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mathbb C^\times.
$$

Extend it periodically to $\mathbb Z$ by setting $\chi(n)=0$ when $(n,m)>1$. Its [Dirichlet L-function](../../../../../../dirichlet-l-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{\chi(n)}{n^s}
=\prod_p(1-\chi(p)p^{-s})^{-1}
\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s>1).}
$$
