<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [Kronecker–Weber theorem](../../../../../../kronecker-weber-theorem.md) says that every finite abelian extension of $\mathbb Q$ lies in some $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$.

Indeed, global class field theory assigns to a finite abelian $L/\mathbb Q$ the open [norm group of an abelian extension](../../../../../../norm-group-of-an-abelian-extension.md) $N_{L/\mathbb Q}C_L$. It contains a congruence subgroup $C_{\mathbb Q}(m\infty)$. By part (e), this is the norm group of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$. The inclusion-reversing class-field correspondence therefore gives

$$
\boxed{L\subseteq\mathbb Q(\zeta_m).}
$$
