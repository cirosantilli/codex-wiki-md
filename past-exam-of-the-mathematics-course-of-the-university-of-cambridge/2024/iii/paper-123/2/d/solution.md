↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The ideal-theoretic [existence theorem of global class field theory](../../../../../../existence-theorem-of-global-class-field-theory.md) gives an inclusion-reversing correspondence between finite abelian extensions $L/K$ and congruence subgroups

$$
P_K(\mathfrak m)\subseteq H\subseteq I_K(\mathfrak m).
$$

The corresponding field satisfies

$$
\boxed{H=\ker\!\left(I_K(\mathfrak m)
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Art}_{L/K}}
\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\right),
\qquad
I_K(\mathfrak m)/H\cong\operatorname{Gal}(L/K).}
$$
