↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

On either open half-line, part (i) says $\widehat u=-v'$. Differentiating the formula in part (iii) makes all elementary terms cancel except the [logarithm](../../../../../../../logarithm.md) and the bracket. Therefore

$$
\widehat u(\lambda)=-\log|\lambda|+f_+(\lambda),
\qquad \lambda>0,
$$

where

$$
f_+(\lambda)=
\int_\lambda^1\frac{e^{-ix}-1}{x}\,dx
+\int_1^\infty\frac{e^{-ix}}x\,dx,
$$

and

$$
\widehat u(\lambda)=-\log|\lambda|+f_-(\lambda),
\qquad \lambda<0,
$$

where

$$
f_-(\lambda)=
\int_{|\lambda|}^1\frac{e^{ix}-1}{x}\,dx
+\int_1^\infty\frac{e^{ix}}x\,dx.
$$

Both functions are [smooth](../../../../../../../smooth-function.md) on their respective half-lines, proving the required assertion.
