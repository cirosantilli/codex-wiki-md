↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) writes every derivative of $a_1a_2$ as a finite sum of products of derivatives of the two factors. Multiplying the corresponding symbol estimates adds their orders, so

$$
\boxed{a_1a_2\in
\operatorname{Sym}(X,\mathbb R^k;N_1+N_2)}.
$$
