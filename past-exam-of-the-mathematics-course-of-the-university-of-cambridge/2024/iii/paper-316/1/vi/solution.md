<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Let $\Delta=\Omega-\phi_d$. From part (iii), $\phi-\phi_d=\Delta+(\phi-\Omega)$ and $\tan(\phi-\Omega)=\cos I\tan f$. The [tangent addition formula](../../../../../../tangent-addition-formula.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
\tan(\phi-\phi_d)
=\frac{\tan\Delta+\cos I\tan f}
{1-\cos I\tan f\tan\Delta}}
$$

or, without singular coordinate tangents,

$$
\boxed{
\tan(\phi-\phi_d)=
\frac{\sin\Delta\cos f+\cos\Delta\cos I\sin f}
{\cos\Delta\cos f-\sin\Delta\cos I\sin f}}.
$$

For $I=\pi/2-I'$ with $I'\ll1$, smooth choice of the angular branch gives

$$
\boxed{\phi-\phi_d\simeq\Omega-\phi_d+I'\tan f\pmod\pi}.
$$
