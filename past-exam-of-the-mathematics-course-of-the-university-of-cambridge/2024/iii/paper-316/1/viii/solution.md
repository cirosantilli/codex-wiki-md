<h1 id="1/viii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Viii](../viii.md)

An exterior misaligned planet exerts an orbit-averaged [secular torque](../../../../../../secular-torque.md) on each belt orbit. In the hierarchical limit $a_d\ll a$, its leading [quadrupole approximation](../../../../../../quadrupole-approximation.md) drives [nodal precession](../../../../../../nodal-precession.md) about the planet's orbital angular-momentum axis at a rate of order

$$
|\dot\Omega_d|\sim n_d\frac{M_p}{M_*}
\left(\frac{a_d}{a}\right)^3|\cos I_m|,
$$

up to a coefficient of order unity. Because this rate varies across a radially extended belt, [differential nodal precession](../../../../../../differential-nodal-precession.md) winds an initially flat belt into a warp and eventually a vertically thick, phase-mixed structure. Collisions can damp the free inclinations and make material approach a forced or [Laplace plane](../../../../../../laplace-plane.md); at sufficiently large [mutual inclination](../../../../../../mutual-inclination.md), coupled eccentricity-inclination evolution such as the [Kozai–Lidov mechanism](../../../../../../kozai-lidov-mechanism.md) may also become important.
