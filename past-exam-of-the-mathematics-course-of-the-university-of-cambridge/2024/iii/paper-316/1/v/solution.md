<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The oriented [normal vectors](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the planet and dust-belt planes are

$$
\widehat{\mathbf h}
=(\sin\Omega\sin I,-\cos\Omega\sin I,\cos I),
$$



$$
\widehat{\mathbf h}_d
=(\sin\phi_d\sin I_d,-\cos\phi_d\sin I_d,\cos I_d).
$$

Their [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) is the cosine of the [mutual inclination](../../../../../../mutual-inclination.md), so

$$
\boxed{\cos I_m
=\cos I\cos I_d+\sin I\sin I_d\cos(\Omega-\phi_d)}.
$$

If $\phi_d=\Omega$, this reduces to $\cos I_m=\cos(I-I_d)$; equal inclinations then give $I_m=0$. If both planes are face-on, it likewise gives $I_m=0$, independently of their undefined nodal longitudes. Reversing an observationally unidentified ascending node by $\pi$ produces the familiar [orbital-plane orientation degeneracy](../../../../../../orbital-plane-orientation-degeneracy.md) for an axisymmetric belt.
