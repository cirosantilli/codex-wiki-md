<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the nearly [edge-on orbit](../../../../../../edge-on-orbit.md) $I=\pi/2-I'$ with $I'\ll1$, the [small-angle approximation](../../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md) gives $\cos I=\sin I'=I'+O(I'^3)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\tan(\phi-\Omega)=I'\tan f+O(I'^3)}.
$$

Also,

$$
R_{\rm sky}=a|\cos f|\sqrt{1+\cos^2I\tan^2f}
=a|\cos f|\left[1+\frac12(I'\tan f)^2+O(I'^4)\right].
$$

Thus, on a branch where $\cos f\gt0$ and with separation measured in units of $a$,

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm sky}\simeq\left[1+\frac12(I'\tan f)^2\right]\cos f},
$$

as stated. The absolute value is required when $R_{\rm sky}$ denotes the nonnegative separation over the whole orbit.
