<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $g_E$ solve the trapped stationary equation in the question and use

$$
\phi(x,t)=g_E(x-X(t))
e^{i\theta(t)+iu(t)(x-X(t))}.
$$

Writing $y=x-X$, the harmonic potential splits as $(y+X)^2/2=y^2/2+Xy+X^2/2$. Matching the coefficient of $g_E'$ and then the linear and constant coefficients of $g_E$ gives

$$
\boxed{\dot X=u,
\qquad\dot u=-X,
\qquad
\dot\theta=-E+\frac12u^2-\frac12X^2}.
$$

Thus the soliton center obeys $\ddot X=-X$. These are exactly [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) for the classical Hamiltonian

$$
H(X,p)=\frac12p^2+\frac12X^2
$$

with $p=u$, while the extra phase evolves by $-E$ plus the classical Lagrangian $(u^2-X^2)/2$.
