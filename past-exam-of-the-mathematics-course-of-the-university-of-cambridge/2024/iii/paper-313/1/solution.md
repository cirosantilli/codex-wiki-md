↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Treat $\phi$ and $\bar\phi$ as independent fields. The [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=\frac i2(\phi\dot{\bar\phi}-\bar\phi\dot\phi)
+\frac12\phi_x\bar\phi_x-\frac12(\phi\bar\phi)^2
+\epsilon V(\epsilon x)\phi\bar\phi.
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $\bar\phi$ is

$$
-i\phi_t-\frac12\phi_{xx}-|\phi|^2\phi
+\epsilon V(\epsilon x)\phi=0,
$$

or

$$
\boxed{i\phi_t=-\frac12\phi_{xx}-|\phi|^2\phi
+\epsilon V(\epsilon x)\phi}.
$$

The equation from variation of $\phi$ is its complex conjugate.
