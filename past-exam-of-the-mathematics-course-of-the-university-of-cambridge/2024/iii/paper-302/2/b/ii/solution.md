<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating $\operatorname{Ad}_{\exp(tX)}Y$ at zero gives the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

$$
\operatorname{ad}_X(Y)=[X,Y].
$$

The Jacobi identity implies $[\operatorname{ad}_X,\operatorname{ad}_Y]=\operatorname{ad}_{[X,Y]}$, proving that it is a Lie-algebra representation.
