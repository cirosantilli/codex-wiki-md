<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [classification of rank-two root systems](../../../../../../classification-of-rank-two-root-systems.md) gives $A_1\times A_1$, $A_2$, $B_2$, and $G_2$. The first is reducible and corresponds to a semisimple but nonsimple algebra. Hence the complex simple rank-two algebras are $A_2$, $B_2=C_2$, and $G_2$, so $N=3$.
