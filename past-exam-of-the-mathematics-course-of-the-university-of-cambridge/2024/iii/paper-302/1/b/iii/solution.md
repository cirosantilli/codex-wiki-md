<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $X^3=0$,

$$
\exp(qQ+rR+tS)=I+qQ+rR+(t+qr/2)S.
$$

Every group element has the unique preimage $t=s-qr/2$, so the [Exponential map of a Lie group](../../../../../../../exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) is bijective. It is a diffeomorphism from $\mathbb R^3$ onto $G$, proving that $G$ is connected and simply connected.
