The line $\mathbb RS$ is a nonzero central ideal, so the [Heisenberg Lie algebra](../../../../../../../heisenberg-lie-algebra.md) is not simple. It is two-step nilpotent and hence solvable; a nonzero solvable Lie algebra is not semisimple. It is therefore neither simple nor semisimple.
