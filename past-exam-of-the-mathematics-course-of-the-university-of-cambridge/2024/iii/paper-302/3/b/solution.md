↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With cyclic spatial indices,

$$
J_1=M^{23},\quad J_2=M^{31},\quad J_3=M^{12},
\qquad K_i=M^{0i}.
$$

Substitution in the given [Poincare algebra](../../../../../../poincare-algebra.md) brackets yields

$$
\boxed{[J_1,J_2]=J_3,
\qquad [J_1,K_2]=K_3,
\qquad [K_1,K_2]=-J_3.}
$$
