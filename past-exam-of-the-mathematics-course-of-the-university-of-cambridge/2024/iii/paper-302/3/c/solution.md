<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Because the translations commute, $P^\sigma P^\mu$ is symmetric in $(\sigma,\mu)$, whereas the Levi-Civita tensor in the [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](../../../../../../pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md) is antisymmetric, so $W_\mu P^\mu=0$. Moreover,

$$
[W_\mu,P^\tau]
=\frac12\epsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
(\eta^{\rho\tau}P^\nu-\eta^{\nu\tau}P^\rho)P^\sigma=0,
$$

because the two terms cancel after relabeling and each remaining momentum product is symmetric.
