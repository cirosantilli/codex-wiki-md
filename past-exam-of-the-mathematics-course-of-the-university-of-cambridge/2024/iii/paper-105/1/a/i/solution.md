The [principal symbol](../../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) of the equation is

$$
P(t,x;\tau,\xi)=tx\tau^2-\xi^2.
$$

In the standard notation $Au_{tt}+2Bu_{tx}+Cu_{xx}$, we have $A=tx$, $B=0$, and $C=-1$, so

$$
B^2-AC=tx.
$$

A second-order equation is a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](../../../../../../../hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) exactly where this discriminant is positive. Hence the hyperbolic set is

$$
\boxed{\{(t,x):tx>0\}},
$$

the union of the first and third open quadrants.
