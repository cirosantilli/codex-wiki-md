<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The initial line is $t=t_0$, whose conormal is $dt$. Evaluating the [principal symbol](../../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) on it gives

$$
P(t_0,x_0;1,0)=t_0x_0.
$$

It is therefore a [non-characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../non-characteristic-hypersurface.md) at $(t_0,x_0)$ exactly when $t_0x_0\ne0$. At such a point the equation can be written

$$
u_{tt}=\frac{u_{xx}-u_t^2}{tx},
$$

whose right-hand side is [real analytic](../../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) locally, and the prescribed [Cauchy data](../../../../../../../cauchy-data.md) are also real analytic. The [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](../../../../../../../cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md) consequently gives a unique local analytic solution exactly at the points

$$
\boxed{t_0x_0\ne0}.
$$
