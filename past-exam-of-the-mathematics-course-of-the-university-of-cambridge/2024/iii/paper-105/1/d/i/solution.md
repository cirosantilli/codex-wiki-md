The [Fredholm alternative for an elliptic Dirichlet problem](../../../../../../../fredholm-alternative-for-an-elliptic-dirichlet-problem.md) says that either the homogeneous adjoint problem has only the zero solution, in which case $Lu=f$ has a unique solution for every admissible $f$, or the homogeneous kernels are nontrivial and finite-dimensional. In the latter case,

$$
Lu=f
$$

is solvable exactly when

$$
\langle f,v\rangle=0
\qquad\text{for every }v\in\ker L^*,
$$

and any two solutions differ by an element of $\ker L$. Moreover $\dim\ker L=\dim\ker L^*$.
