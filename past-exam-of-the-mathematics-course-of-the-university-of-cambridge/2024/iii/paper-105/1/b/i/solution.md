<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $u\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R)$, a function $g\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R)$ is its [weak derivative](../../../../../../../weak-derivative.md) when

$$
\int_{\mathbb R}u(x)\varphi'(x)\,dx
=-\int_{\mathbb R}g(x)\varphi(x)\,dx
$$

for every [test function](../../../../../../../test-function.md) $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$. Equivalently, the [distributional derivative](../../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) of $u$ is represented by the locally integrable function $g$.
