↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $1\leq p<n$, define the [Sobolev conjugate exponent](../../../../../../../sobolev-conjugate-exponent.md)

$$
p^*=\frac{np}{n-p},
\qquad
\frac1{p^*}=\frac1p-\frac1n.
$$

The [Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../../sobolev-inequality.md), also called the Gagliardo--Nirenberg--Sobolev inequality, states that there is a constant $C=C(n,p)$ such that

$$
\boxed{\|u\|_{L^{p^*}(\mathbb R^n)}\leq C\|Du\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}}
$$

for every $u\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$, and hence by completion for every $u\in W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n)$ for which the right formulation applies.
