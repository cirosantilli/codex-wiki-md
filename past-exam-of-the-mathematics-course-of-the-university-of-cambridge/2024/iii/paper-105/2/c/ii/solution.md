<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $u\in H$, the trace $u(0)$ vanishes. Apply the [Hardy inequality on an interval](../../../../../../../hardy-inequality-on-an-interval.md) with $p=2$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\left\|\frac ux\right\|_{L^2(0,1)}\leq2\|u'\|_{L^2(0,1)}}.
$$

**Thus $u/x\in L^2(0,1)$.**
