<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [one-dimensional Sobolev representative](../../../../../../one-dimensional-sobolev-representative.md) of $u$ is absolutely continuous, and its zero [trace](../../../../../../sobolev-trace-theorem.md) gives

$$
u(x)=\int_0^xu'(t)\,dt.
$$

Consequently $u(x)/x=A(u')(x)$ for the [Hardy operator](../../../../../../hardy-operator.md). Applying the [Hardy averaging inequality](../../../../../../hardy-averaging-inequality.md) to $u'$ gives the [Hardy inequality on an interval](../../../../../../hardy-inequality-on-an-interval.md):

$$
\boxed{\left\|\frac ux\right\|_{L^p(0,1)}
\leq\frac p{p-1}\|u'\|_{L^p(0,1)}}.
$$
