[Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) turns the [categorical distribution](../../../../../../../categorical-distribution.md) prior probabilities and the component [multivariate normal densities](../../../../../../../multivariate-normal-density.md) into

$$
\mathbb P(c_i=m\mid y_i,\pi,f)
=\frac{\pi_m\phi(y_i;f_m,\sigma^2I_n)}
{\sum_{\ell=1}^3\pi_\ell\phi(y_i;f_\ell,\sigma^2I_n)},
\qquad m=1,2,3.
$$

These probabilities define the label update in the [Gibbs sampler](../../../../../../../gibbs-sampler.md).
