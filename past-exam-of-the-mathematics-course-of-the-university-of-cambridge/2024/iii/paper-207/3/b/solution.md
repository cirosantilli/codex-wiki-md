<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $x_*=0.5$ in the [Gaussian process regression posterior](../../../../../../gaussian-process-regression-posterior.md) and compute $m_*$ and $v_*$ as in part a. [Standardization of a normal random variable](../../../../../../standardization-of-a-normal-random-variable.md) then gives

$$
\mathbb P\bigl(f(0.5)<1.5\mid y,X\bigr)
=\Phi\!\left(\frac{1.5-m_*}{\sqrt{v_*}}\right),
$$

where $\Phi$ is the [standard normal cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution-function.md).
