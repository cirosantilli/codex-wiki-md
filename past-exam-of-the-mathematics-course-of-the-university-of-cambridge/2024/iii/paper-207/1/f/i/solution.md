The [transition probability matrix](../../../../../../../transition-semigroup-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) has entries

$$
P_{rs}(u)=\mathbb P(X(t+u)=s\mid X(t)=r)
$$

under the [time-homogeneous Markov property](../../../../../../../time-homogeneous-markov-property.md). For the finite-state model in part d, the [transition semigroup of a continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../../transition-semigroup-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is

$$
\boxed{P(u)=e^{uQ}.}
$$
