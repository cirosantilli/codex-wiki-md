<h1 id="1/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The model assumes a [time-homogeneous Markov property](../../../../../../time-homogeneous-markov-property.md): transition intensities depend only on the current state, not on calendar time, infection age, or earlier history. A [Semi-Markov multi-state model](../../../../../../semi-markov-multi-state-model.md) could let recovery and death hazards depend on time since infection or entry into the current state. Weekly tests reveal recovery only by [interval censoring](../../../../../../interval-censoring.md), however, so the relevant entry and transition times are latent. Fitting the less restrictive model then requires integrating over unobserved paths, and the available data may contain too little information to identify a flexible duration-dependent hazard.
