↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two event clocks have independent [exponential distributions](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rates $\mu$ and $\gamma$. The [competing exponential clocks](../../../../../../../competing-exponential-clocks.md) identity gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_I(D\text{ before }R)=\frac{\mu}{\mu+\gamma}.}
$$
