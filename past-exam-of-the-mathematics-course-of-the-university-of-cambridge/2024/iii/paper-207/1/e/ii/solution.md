<h1 id="1/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The minimum of the recovery and death clocks has an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\mu+\gamma$. Its [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_I[T_{\{R,D\}}]=\frac1{\mu+\gamma}.}
$$
