<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first subscript in $\beta_{t,\tau}$ is the [calendar time](../../../../../../calendar-time.md) $t$, and the second is the [infection age](../../../../../../infection-age.md) $\tau$, the time elapsed since the source individual became infected. Thus $\beta_{t,\tau}$ is the rate at which an individual of infection age $\tau$ generates infections at time $t$. The corresponding discrete [infectious disease renewal equation](../../../../../../infectious-disease-renewal-equation.md) is

$$
\Delta_t=\sum_{\tau=1}^{t}\beta_{t,\tau}\Delta_{t-\tau},
$$

up to a separately modelled term for [imported infection](../../../../../../imported-infection.md). The upper limit may instead be a fixed maximal infectious age, with unavailable terms set to zero.
