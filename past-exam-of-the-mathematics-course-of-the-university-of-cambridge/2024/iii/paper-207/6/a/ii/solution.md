↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An exact death at 42 months contributes the probability mass at 42,

$$
\mathbb P(T=42)=F(42)-F(42^-)=S(42^-)-S(42).
$$

For a continuous model this is represented by the event density $f(42)$ rather than a point mass, but [empirical likelihood](../../../../../../../empirical-likelihood.md) permits discrete masses.
