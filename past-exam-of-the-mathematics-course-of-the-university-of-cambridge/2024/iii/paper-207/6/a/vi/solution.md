<h1 id="6/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Measure time in years after age 70. Residence in the care home begins at time 3, so inclusion is conditional on $T>3$: this is [left truncation](../../../../../../../left-truncation.md), also called delayed entry. Exact death at time 13 contributes

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(T=13\mid T>3)
=\frac{F(13)-F(13^-)}{S(3)}
=\frac{S(13^-)-S(13)}{S(3)}.}
$$
