A [proportional hazards family](../../../../../../proportional-hazards-family.md) has [hazard functions](../../../../../../hazard-function.md) related by

$$
h(t\mid z)=c(z)h_0(t),
$$

where the [hazard ratio](../../../../../../hazard-ratio.md) $c(z)$ is positive and independent of time. Equivalently, its [cumulative hazard functions](../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) satisfy $H(t\mid z)=c(z)H_0(t)$ and its [survivor functions](../../../../../../survival-function.md) satisfy $S(t\mid z)=S_0(t)^{c(z)}$.
