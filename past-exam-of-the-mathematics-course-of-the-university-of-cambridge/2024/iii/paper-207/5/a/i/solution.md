↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing the two functions in the question as survivor functions, $S_2(t)=S_1(t)^\lambda$. Since $H_k(t)=-\log S_k(t)$,

$$
H_2(t)=-\log S_2(t)=-\lambda\log S_1(t)=\lambda H_1(t).
$$

Differentiating at times where the [hazard functions](../../../../../../../hazard-function.md) exist gives $h_2(t)=\lambda h_1(t)$. Their [hazard ratio](../../../../../../../hazard-ratio.md) is therefore the constant $\lambda$, so they form a [proportional hazards family](../../../../../../../proportional-hazards-family.md).
