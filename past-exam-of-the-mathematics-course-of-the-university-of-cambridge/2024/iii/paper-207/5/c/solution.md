A [semiparametric proportional hazards model](../../../../../../semiparametric-proportional-hazards-model.md) specifies

$$
h(t\mid z)=h_0(t)e^{\beta z}
$$

with finite-dimensional parameter $\beta$ but an unspecified baseline hazard $h_0$. A [partial likelihood](../../../../../../partial-likelihood.md) uses a component of the data likelihood that depends on $\beta$ while eliminating the nuisance function. In the [Cox proportional-hazards model](../../../../../../cox-proportional-hazards-model.md), conditioning on which member of each [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md) experiences the event produces the [Cox partial likelihood](../../../../../../cox-partial-likelihood.md).
