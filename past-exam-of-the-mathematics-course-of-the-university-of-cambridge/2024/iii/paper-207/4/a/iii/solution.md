<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The quantity

$$
u_j-\mathbb E_0U_j
=u_j-\frac{r_j^{(1)}}{r_j^{(0)}+r_j^{(1)}}
$$

is the observed-minus-expected group-1 event count at time $a_j$. A positive value is local evidence that group 1 has the greater [hazard function](../../../../../../../hazard-function.md); a negative value points toward group 0.
