Summing the observed-minus-expected contributions gives the unstandardized [log-rank statistic](../../../../../../../log-rank-statistic.md)

$$
T_0=\sum_{j=1}^m
\left\{u_j-
\frac{r_j^{(1)}}{r_j^{(0)}+r_j^{(1)}}
\right\}.
$$

Under the null it is centered at zero. A two-sided [log-rank test](../../../../../../../log-rank-test.md) compares its magnitude with the square root of its null variance.
