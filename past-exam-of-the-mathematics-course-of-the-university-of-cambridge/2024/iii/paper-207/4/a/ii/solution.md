<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) of equal event-time distributions, every member of the combined [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) has the same instantaneous chance of being the next event. Conditional on one event at $a_j$ and on the two risk-set sizes,

$$
U_j\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}\!\left(
\frac{r_j^{(1)}}{r_j^{(0)}+r_j^{(1)}}
\right),
$$

so

$$
\mathbb E_0(U_j\mid r_j^{(0)},r_j^{(1)})
=\frac{r_j^{(1)}}{r_j^{(0)}+r_j^{(1)}}.
$$
