<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $u_j^{(1)}=u_j$ and $u_j^{(0)}=1-u_j$. Applying the [Nelson–Aalen estimator](../../../../../../../nelson-aalen-estimator.md) separately to group $k$ gives

$$
\widehat H_j^{(k)}
=\sum_{\ell=1}^{j}\frac{u_\ell^{(k)}}{r_\ell^{(k)}},
$$

with a zero contribution when the event at $a_\ell$ occurs in the other group.
