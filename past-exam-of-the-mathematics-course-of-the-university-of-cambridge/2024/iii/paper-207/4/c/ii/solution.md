<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The group-specific estimated [cumulative hazard](../../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) jumps only when that group experiences the event, so

$$
\Delta\widehat H_j^{(1)}=\frac{u_j}{r_j^{(1)}},
\qquad
\Delta\widehat H_j^{(0)}=\frac{1-u_j}{r_j^{(0)}}.
$$
