[Response-adaptive randomization](../../../../../../response-adaptive-randomization.md) can assign a larger proportion of later participants to the treatment currently estimated to be better, improving outcomes for participants within the trial. Its allocation probabilities depend on earlier outcomes, which complicates [statistical inference](../../../../../../statistical-inference-split.md); delayed responses and calendar-time trends can also make adaptation ineffective or biased, and an allocation aimed at patient benefit need not maximize power.
