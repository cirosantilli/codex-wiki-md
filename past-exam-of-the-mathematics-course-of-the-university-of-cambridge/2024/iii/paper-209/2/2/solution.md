<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [Russo-Seymour-Welsh theorem](../../../../../../russo-seymour-welsh-theorem.md) and self-duality give a scale-independent $c>0$ such that every annulus

$$
A_k=B(3^{k+1})\setminus B(3^k)
$$

contains a closed circuit surrounding the origin with probability at least $c$. Choose every second annulus so that the corresponding site sets are disjoint. Their circuit events are then independent, and the [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) say that infinitely many of them occur almost surely.

Every such closed circuit separates the origin from infinity, so the origin cannot belong to an infinite open cluster. By translation invariance, the same holds for every site. Since the triangular lattice is countable, a countable union shows that almost surely no infinite open cluster exists at $p=1/2$.
