<h1 id="4/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Sample a [uniform spanning tree](../../../../../../uniform-spanning-tree.md) $T$. Rooting Wilson's algorithm at $a$ shows that the path in $T$ oriented from $x_1$ to $a$ has the law of $\operatorname{LE}(x_1\to a)$. Rooting the same uniform law at $x_1$ shows that the same undirected path with its orientation reversed has the law of $\operatorname{LE}(a\to x_1)$. Consequently

$$
(L(\tau),L(\tau-1),\ldots,L(0))
\overset d=\operatorname{LE}(a\to x_1).
$$

This is [reversibility of loop-erased random walk](../../../../../../reversibility-of-loop-erased-random-walk.md).
