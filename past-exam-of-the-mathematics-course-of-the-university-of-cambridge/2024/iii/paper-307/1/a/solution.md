<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Coleman–Mandula theorem](../../../../../../coleman-mandula-theorem.md) says that, under its assumptions on a nontrivial analytic relativistic S-matrix and the particle spectrum, every continuous bosonic symmetry algebra is a direct sum of the Poincare algebra and an internal symmetry algebra. Supersymmetry becomes possible by weakening the assumption that the symmetry algebra is an ordinary [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md): a Z2-graded [Lie superalgebra](../../../../../../lie-superalgebra.md) admits odd generators whose bracket is an anticommutator. The [Haag–Łopuszański–Sohnius theorem](../../../../../../haag-lopuszanski-sohnius-theorem.md) then classifies the allowed extension and leads to the [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md).
