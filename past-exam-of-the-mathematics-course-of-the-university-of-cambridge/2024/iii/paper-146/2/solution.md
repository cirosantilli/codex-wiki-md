The [Weinstein neighborhood theorem](../../../../../weinstein-neighborhood-theorem.md) states that if $L$ is a compact [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) of $(M,\omega)$, then neighborhoods of $L$ in $M$ and of the zero section in its [cotangent bundle](../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md) $T^*L$ are [symplectomorphic](../../../../../symplectomorphism.md). The symplectomorphism restricts to the identity on $L$, and the canonical form on $T^*L$ is taken with the sign matching the chosen convention.
