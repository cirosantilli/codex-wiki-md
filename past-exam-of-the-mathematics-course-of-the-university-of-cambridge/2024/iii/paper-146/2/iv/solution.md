<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Again take a symplectic two-sphere, but let $L$ be a small latitude bounding a cap of area strictly below half the total area. A rotation carrying that cap to a disjoint cap carries $L$ to a disjoint Lagrangian circle. Rotations of the symplectic sphere are flows of [Hamiltonian vector fields](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md); for rotation about an axis, a height function is a [Hamiltonian function](../../../../../../hamiltonian-function.md). Thus this rotation is a [Hamiltonian isotopy](../../../../../../hamiltonian-isotopy.md), and $L$ is Hamiltonian displaceable.
