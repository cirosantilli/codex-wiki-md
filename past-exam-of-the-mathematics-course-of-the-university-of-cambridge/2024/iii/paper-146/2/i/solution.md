<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $L=S^2$. For any [Lagrangian embedding](../../../../../../lagrangian-embedding.md) of $S^2$ into a symplectic four-manifold, the symplectic form identifies the [normal bundle](../../../../../../normal-bundle.md) with $T^*S^2$. The [self-intersection formula](../../../../../../self-intersection-formula.md) and the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) give

$$
[L]\cdot[L]
=\langle e(NL),[L]\rangle
=\langle e(T^*S^2),[S^2]\rangle
=2
$$

up to the harmless orientation sign. If a [smooth isotopy](../../../../../../smooth-isotopy.md) displaced $L$, its final image would represent the same homology class but have intersection number zero with $L$, a contradiction. This is the [smooth non-displaceability from self-intersection](../../../../../../smooth-non-displaceability-from-self-intersection.md).

For a compact ambient example, equip $S^2\times S^2$ with $\omega\oplus(-\omega)$. Its diagonal

$$
\Delta=\{(x,x):x\in S^2\}
$$

is Lagrangian because the two summands cancel on $T\Delta$, and the preceding argument shows that it is not smoothly displaceable.
