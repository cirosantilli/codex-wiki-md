<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Take the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md)

$$
M=T^{2n}=\mathbb R^{2n}/\mathbb Z^{2n}
$$

with the translation-invariant [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md)

$$
\omega=\sum_{j=1}^n dx_j\wedge dy_j.
$$

The coordinate vector fields give a global symplectic frame of $TM$, so $TM$ is symplectically trivial. Passing fiberwise to the [Lagrangian Grassmannian bundle](../../../../../../lagrangian-grassmannian-bundle.md) gives

$$
\mathcal LGr(TM)\cong
T^{2n}\times\operatorname{LGr}(\mathbb R^{2n}),
$$

which is a smooth trivialization over the compact symplectic manifold $T^{2n}$.
