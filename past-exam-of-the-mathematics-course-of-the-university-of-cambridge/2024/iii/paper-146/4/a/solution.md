↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [symplectic neighborhood theorem](../../../../../../symplectic-neighborhood-theorem.md) says that a neighborhood of a compact [symplectic submanifold](../../../../../../symplectic-submanifold.md) is determined, up to symplectomorphism, by the restricted symplectic form and its [symplectic normal bundle](../../../../../../symplectic-normal-bundle.md). More precisely, if $f:S_0\to S_1$ is a symplectomorphism and an isomorphism of symplectic normal bundles covers $f$, then that bundle isomorphism extends to a symplectomorphism between neighborhoods of $S_0$ and $S_1$.
