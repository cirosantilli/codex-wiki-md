<h1 id="9e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $P$ be free with [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $(e_i)_{i\in I}$. Given a surjection $f:M\to N$ and a map $g:P\to N$, choose $m_i\in M$ satisfying $f(m_i)=g(e_i)$. There is a unique [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) $h:P\to M$ with $h(e_i)=m_i$, and on every [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md)

$$
(f\circ h)(e_i)=g(e_i).
$$

**Thus $f\circ h=g$, proving that [free modules are projective](../../../../../../../free-modules-are-projective.md).**
