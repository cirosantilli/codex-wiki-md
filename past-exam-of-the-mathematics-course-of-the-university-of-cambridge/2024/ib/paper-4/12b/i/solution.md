<h1 id="12b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}s>0$,

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L\{H(t-t_0)\}(s)
=\int_{t_0}^{\infty}e^{-st}\,dt
=\frac{e^{-st_0}}s}.
$$

This is the [Laplace transform time-shift rule](../../../../../../laplace-transform-time-shift-rule.md) in its simplest form.
