<h1 id="15a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\psi=Ae^{-Bx^2}$,

$$
\psi_t=\left(\frac{A'}A-B'x^2\right)\psi,
\qquad
\psi_{xx}=(-2B+4B^2x^2)\psi.
$$

Substitution into the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and comparison of the constant and $x^2$ coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{A'=-i\hbar AB,
\qquad
B'=-\frac{i}{2\hbar}-2i\hbar B^2}.
$$
