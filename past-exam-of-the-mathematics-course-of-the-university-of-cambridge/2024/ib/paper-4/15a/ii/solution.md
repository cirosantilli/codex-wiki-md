<h1 id="15a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $B=\xi\tan(\phi+\alpha t)$. Matching the constant and $\tan^2$ coefficients in the [Riccati equation](../../../../../../riccati-equation.md) requires

$$
\xi\alpha=-\frac{i}{2\hbar},
\qquad
\xi\alpha=-2i\hbar\xi^2.
$$

One convenient choice is therefore

$$
\boxed{\xi=\frac1{2\hbar},
\qquad \alpha=-i},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{B(t)=\frac1{2\hbar}\tan(\phi-it)}.
$$
