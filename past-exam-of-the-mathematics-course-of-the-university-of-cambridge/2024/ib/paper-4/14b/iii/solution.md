<h1 id="14b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $v(t,z)=u(t,z+at)$. Then $v_t=\kappa v_{zz}$ and $v(0,z)=u_0(z)$. Applying the heat kernel and returning to $x$ gives the [advection-diffusion heat-kernel solution](../../../../../../advection-diffusion-heat-kernel-solution.md)

$$
\boxed{
u(t,x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
K_t(x-at-y)u_0(y)\,dy}.
$$
