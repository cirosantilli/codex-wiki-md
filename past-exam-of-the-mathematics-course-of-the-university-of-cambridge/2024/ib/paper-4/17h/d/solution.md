↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Averaging the centered model gives

$$
\hat\alpha'=\bar Y
=\alpha'+\bar\epsilon.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\hat\alpha'\sim N\left(\alpha',\frac{\sigma^2}{n}\right)}.
$$

Writing $z_{0.975}=\Phi^{-1}(0.975)$, an exact 95% confidence interval is

$$
\boxed{\bar Y\pm z_{0.975}\frac\sigma{\sqrt n}}.
$$
