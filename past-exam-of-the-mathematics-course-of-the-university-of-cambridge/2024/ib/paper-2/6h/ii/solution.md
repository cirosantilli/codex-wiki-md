For observed counts $O_j$ and null expected counts $E_j=np_j$, the [Pearson chi-squared goodness-of-fit test](../../../../../../pearson-chi-squared-goodness-of-fit-test.md) uses

$$
X^2=\sum_{j=1}^k\frac{(O_j-E_j)^2}{E_j}.
$$

When the null cell probabilities are specified and the expected counts grow, its limiting null distribution is

$$
\boxed{X^2\ \xrightarrow{d}\ \chi^2_{k-1}}.
$$

Here $k=6$, so the [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is $\chi^2_5$.
