<h1 id="5d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5D](../5d.md)

Writing the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) as $(u,v)=(y,ax)$ gives

$$
\partial_xu+\partial_yv=0,
$$

so the flow is incompressible. With the convention

$$
u=\psi_y,
\qquad
v=-\psi_x,
$$

a stream [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,y)=\frac12(y^2-ax^2)}.
$$

The [streamlines](../../../../../streamline.md) are its level sets. For $a>0$, they are the hyperbolas

$$
y^2-ax^2=\text{constant},
$$

with separatrices $y=\pm\sqrt a\,x$ and a saddle at the origin. For $a<0$, they are concentric ellipses

$$
y^2+|a|x^2=\text{constant},
$$

traversed clockwise. This is the [streamline classification of a planar linear saddle or centre](../../../../../streamline-classification-of-a-planar-linear-saddle-or-centre.md).

The [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity.md) is

$$
\partial_xv-\partial_yu=a-1.
$$

Hence the flow is irrotational exactly when

$$
\boxed{a=1}.
$$

Then $(u,v)=(y,x)=\nabla\phi$, and a [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) is

$$
\boxed{\phi(x,y)=xy+\text{constant}}.
$$
