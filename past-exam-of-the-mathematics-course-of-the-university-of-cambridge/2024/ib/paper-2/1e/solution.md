<h1 id="1e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1E](../1e.md)

The [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) says that a primitive [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md)

$$
f(X)=a_nX^n+\cdots+a_0\in\mathbb Z[X]
$$

is irreducible if there is a prime $p$ such that

$$
p\nmid a_n,
\qquad
p\mid a_i\quad(0\leq i<n),
\qquad
p^2\nmid a_0.
$$

By the [Gauss lemma for polynomials](../../../../../gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md), irreducibility over $\mathbb Q$ and over $\mathbb Z$ agree for primitive [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md).
