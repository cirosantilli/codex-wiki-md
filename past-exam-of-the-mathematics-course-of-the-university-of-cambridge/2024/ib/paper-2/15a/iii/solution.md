<h1 id="15a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiating $\langle O\rangle=\langle\psi|O|\psi\rangle$ and using the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and its adjoint gives the [Ehrenfest theorem](../../../../../../ehrenfest-theorem.md)

$$
\boxed{
\frac d{dt}\langle O\rangle
=\frac1{i\hbar}\langle[O,H]\rangle
+\left\langle\frac{\partial O}{\partial t}\right\rangle}.
$$
