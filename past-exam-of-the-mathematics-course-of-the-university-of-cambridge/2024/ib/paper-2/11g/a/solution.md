<h1 id="11g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $(x,y)\sim(x+a,y+b)$ in the torus, then

$$
\widetilde\pi(x+a,y+b)=(2x+2a,y+b).
$$

This is Klein-equivalent to $(2x,y)$ by taking $(c,d)=(2a,b)$. Hence $\widetilde\pi$ descends to a well-defined continuous map

$$
\pi:T^2\to K,
\qquad
\pi([x,y])=[2x,y].
$$

The deck [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of the Klein bottle has an index-two [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) consisting of transformations with even $c$; it is generated by translations $(x,y)\mapsto(x+2,y)$ and $(x,y)\mapsto(x,y+1)$. This is exactly the image under $\widetilde\pi$ of the torus deck lattice. Therefore the [orientation double cover of the Klein bottle](../../../../../../orientation-double-cover-of-the-klein-bottle.md) has two sheets:

$$
\boxed{\pi:T^2\to K\text{ is }2:1}.
$$
