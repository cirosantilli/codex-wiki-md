<h1 id="2g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2G](../2g.md)

Use the global parametrization

$$
X(u,v)=(u,v,h(u,v)).
$$

Its [tangent vectors](../../../../../tangent-vector.md) are

$$
X_u=(1,0,h_u),
\qquad
X_v=(0,1,h_v),
$$

and

$$
X_u\times X_v=(-h_u,-h_v,1)\ne0.
$$

Thus $X$ is a regular parametrization and the graph is a smooth surface.

The [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{
I=(1+h_u^2)\,du^2+2h_uh_v\,du\,dv+(1+h_v^2)\,dv^2}.
$$

Choosing the upward orientation, the [Gauss map](../../../../../gauss-map.md) is

$$
\boxed{
N(u,v)=\frac{(-h_u,-h_v,1)}
{\sqrt{1+h_u^2+h_v^2}}}.
$$
