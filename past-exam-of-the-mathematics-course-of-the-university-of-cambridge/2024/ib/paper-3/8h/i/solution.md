<h1 id="8h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The independent identically distributed [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $(X_n)$ is itself a [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md), since the conditional law of $X_{n+1}$ is its common marginal law and does not depend on the past.

Also,

$$
S_{n+1}=S_n+X_{n+1}.
$$

Because $X_{n+1}$ is independent of the past, the conditional law of $S_{n+1}$ depends on the history only through $S_n$. Thus the partial sums $(S_n)$ are a Markov chain.
