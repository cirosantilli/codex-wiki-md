<h1 id="8h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The running minima satisfy

$$
L_{n+1}=\min\{L_n,X_{n+1}\}.
$$

Since $X_{n+1}$ is independent of the past, the conditional law of $L_{n+1}$ depends only on $L_n$. Hence $(L_n)$ is a Markov chain. This is the [running minimum of an independent sequence is Markov](../../../../../../running-minimum-of-an-independent-sequence-is-markov.md) property.
