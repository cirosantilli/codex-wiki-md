↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Smith normal form](../../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) of an integer $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ is a diagonal [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_r,0,\ldots,0),
\qquad d_i>0,
\qquad d_i\mid d_{i+1},
$$

for which

$$
UAV=D
$$

with $U\in GL_m(\mathbb Z)$ and $V\in GL_n(\mathbb Z)$. It exists and is unique.

The [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](../../../../../../structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md), specialized to $\mathbb Z$, says that every finitely generated abelian [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) has a unique invariant-factor decomposition

$$
M\cong\mathbb Z^s\oplus
\mathbb Z/d_1\mathbb Z\oplus\cdots\oplus
\mathbb Z/d_r\mathbb Z,
\qquad
1<d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_r.
$$

Equivalently, its finite part is a direct sum of cyclic [groups](../../../../../../group-split.md) of prime-power order.
