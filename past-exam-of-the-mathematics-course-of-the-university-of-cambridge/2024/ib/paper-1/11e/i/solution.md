↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A regularly parametrized curve $\gamma$ on an embedded surface $\Sigma$ is a geodesic when its covariant [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) vanishes. Equivalently, in the ambient Euclidean space,

$$
\ddot\gamma(t)\perp T_{\gamma(t)}\Sigma
$$

for an affine geodesic parameter. A [complete geodesic](../../../../../../complete-geodesic.md) is one whose maximal affine parameter interval is all of $\mathbb R$.
