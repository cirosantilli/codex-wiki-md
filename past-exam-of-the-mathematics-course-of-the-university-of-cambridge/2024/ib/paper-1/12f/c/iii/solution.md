<h1 id="12f/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At every $z_k$, the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $\tan(1/z)$ has a simple pole. The [exponential of a pole is an essential singularity](../../../../../../../exponential-of-a-pole-is-an-essential-singularity.md), so

$$
\boxed{z_k=\frac{2}{(2k+1)\pi}\quad(k\in\mathbb Z)}
$$

are essential singularities of $\exp(\tan(1/z))$.

These points accumulate at $0$. Therefore no punctured neighbourhood of zero is a domain of holomorphy for the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md): zero is a [non-isolated singularity](../../../../../../../non-isolated-singularity.md), specifically an accumulation point of essential singularities, rather than an isolated essential singularity. There are no other singularities in $\mathbb C$.
