<h1 id="9e/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A Sylow $2$-subgroup of $S_4$ has order $8$. For example,

$$
P=\langle(1234),(13)\rangle\cong D_8.
$$

It is the normalizer of $\langle(1234)\rangle$. The six $4$-cycles form three inverse pairs, and a dihedral [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) of order eight has a unique cyclic [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) of order four. Hence the three Sylow $2$-subgroups are

$$
\boxed{
N_{S_4}(\langle(1234)\rangle),\quad
N_{S_4}(\langle(1243)\rangle),\quad
N_{S_4}(\langle(1324)\rangle)}.
$$
