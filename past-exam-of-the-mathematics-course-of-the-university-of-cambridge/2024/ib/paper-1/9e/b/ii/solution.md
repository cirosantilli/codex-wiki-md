<h1 id="9e/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $gH$ is a fixed coset, then

$$
xgH=gH.
$$

Multiplying on the left by $g^{-1}$ gives

$$
g^{-1}xgH=H,
$$

so $g^{-1}xg\in H$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x\text{ is conjugate in }G\text{ to an element of }H}.
$$
