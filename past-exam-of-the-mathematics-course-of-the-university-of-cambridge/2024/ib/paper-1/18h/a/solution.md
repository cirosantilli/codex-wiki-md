<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $X_i=\theta+U_i$, where the $U_i$ are independent $\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ variables. Convolution gives the [sum of two independent uniform variables](../../../../../../sum-of-two-independent-uniform-variables.md):

$$
f_{X_1+X_2}(s)=
\begin{cases}
s-2\theta,&2\theta\leq s\leq2\theta+1,\\
2\theta+2-s,&2\theta+1\leq s\leq2\theta+2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

Its plot is a triangle with vertices

$$
\boxed{(2\theta,0),
\qquad(2\theta+1,1),
\qquad(2\theta+2,0).}
$$
