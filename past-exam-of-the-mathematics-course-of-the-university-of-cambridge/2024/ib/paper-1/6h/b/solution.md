<h1 id="6h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A sufficient statistic $T$ is minimal sufficient if it is a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of every other sufficient statistic: whenever $U$ is sufficient, there is a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $h$ such that $T=h(U)$ almost surely, up to the usual null-set qualification. Thus its level sets give the coarsest sufficient partition of the sample space. In a dominated family with positive densities, the [likelihood-ratio criterion for minimal sufficiency](../../../../../../likelihood-ratio-criterion-for-minimal-sufficiency.md) says that $T$ is minimal sufficient when

$$
\boxed{T(x)=T(y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{p_\theta(x)}{p_\theta(y)}
\text{ is independent of }\theta.}
$$
