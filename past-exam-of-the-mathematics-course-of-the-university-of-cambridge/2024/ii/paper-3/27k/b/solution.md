<h1 id="27k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [M/G/1 queue](../../../../../../m-g-1-queue.md) has Poisson arrivals of rate $\lambda$, independent identically distributed service times with a general law of mean $m$, and one server. During the first service time $S$, a mean $\lambda\mathbb ES=\lambda m$ customers arrive, and each initiates an independent descendant busy-period contribution. Thus

$$
\mathbb EB=m+\lambda m\,\mathbb EB,
$$

so for traffic intensity $\rho=\lambda m<1$,

$$
\mathbb EB=\frac{m}{1-\rho}.
$$

For $\rho\geq1$ the expected busy period is infinite.
