↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Taking a [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) element of $[O,J_3]=0$ gives

$$
\hbar(m'-m)\langle j,m|O|j',m'\rangle=0.
$$

**Thus the [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) element vanishes unless $m=m'$.**
