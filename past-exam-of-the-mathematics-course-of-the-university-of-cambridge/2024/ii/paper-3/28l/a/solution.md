↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $X/n$ is unbiased and has variance $\theta(1-\theta)/n$,

$$
R(\delta_{\rm MLE},\theta)
=\frac{\mathbb E_\theta[(X/n-\theta)^2]}{\theta(1-\theta)}
=\frac1n
$$

for $0<\theta<1$.
