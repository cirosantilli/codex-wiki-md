<h1 id="13a/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At late times the first sine mode dominates, with growth exponent

$$
\mu-\frac{D\pi^2}{L^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\mu_c(D,L)=\frac{D\pi^2}{L^2},
$$

and the population grows precisely when $\mu>\mu_c$. Faster diffusion carries organisms to the lethal ends more quickly and raises the threshold; a longer channel lowers the principal diffusion [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and therefore lowers the threshold.
