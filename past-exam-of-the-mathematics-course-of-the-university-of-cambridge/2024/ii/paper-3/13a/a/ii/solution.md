↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The diffusive flux is $j=-D C_x$. The total outward flux through both ends is therefore

$$
J_{\rm out}=D C_x(0,t)-D C_x(L,t)
=\frac{8DC_0}{L}\sum_{\substack{n\geq1\\n\ {
m odd}}}
\exp\left[\left(\mu-\frac{Dn^2\pi^2}{L^2}\right)t\right].
$$

It diverges as $t\downarrow0$: the initially uniform profile is incompatible with the suddenly imposed zero boundary values, creating an idealized infinite initial [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md).
