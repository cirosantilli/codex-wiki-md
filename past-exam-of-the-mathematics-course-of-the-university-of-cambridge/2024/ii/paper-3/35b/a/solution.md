<h1 id="35b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A grand [canonical ensemble](../../../../../../canonical-ensemble.md) describes a system exchanging both energy and particles with a reservoir at fixed $T$, volume, and [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) $\mu$. For a noninteracting fermion level $r$, occupation is $0$ or $1$, so

$$
\mathcal Z_r=1+e^{-\beta(E_r-\mu)}.
$$

Factorization over levels gives $\mathcal Z=\prod_r\mathcal Z_r$, and [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) with respect to $\beta\mu$ yields the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle n_r\rangle=\frac{1}{e^{\beta(E_r-\mu)}+1}.}
$$
