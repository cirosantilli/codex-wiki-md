<h1 id="18h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Because $\operatorname{lcm}(9,15)=45$ and suitable [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) powers of $\zeta_9$ and $\zeta_{15}$ generate a primitive forty-fifth root,

$$
L_1L_2=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{45}).
$$

The degree formula gives

$$
[L_1\cap L_2:\mathbb Q]
=\frac{\varphi(9)\varphi(15)}{\varphi(45)}
=\frac{6\cdot8}{24}=2.
$$

Both fields contain $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$, which already has degree two, so

$$
L_1\cap L_2=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3).
$$
