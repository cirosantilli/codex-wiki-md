<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The composite $L_1L_2$ is the smallest subfield of $M$ containing both $L_1$ and $L_2$. Since $L_1/K$ is separable and normal, its minimal [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) remain separable and split after base change to $L_2$. Hence $L_1L_2/L_2$ is Galois.

Restriction defines

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(L_1L_2/L_2)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(L_1/K),
\qquad \sigma\longmapsto\sigma|_{L_1}.
$$

It is a homomorphism because every element of the source fixes $L_2$ and therefore $K$. Its kernel fixes both generating fields $L_1$ and $L_2$, hence fixes their composite; the map is injective.
