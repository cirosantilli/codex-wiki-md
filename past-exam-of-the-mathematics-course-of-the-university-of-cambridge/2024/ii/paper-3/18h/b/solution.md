<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The primitive root $\zeta_n$ is a zero of the cyclotomic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $\Phi_n$, of degree $\varphi(n)$. Its minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) over $K$ divides $\Phi_n$, so

$$
[K(\zeta_n):K]\mid\varphi(n),
$$

where divisibility also follows from the embedding of the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of the [cyclotomic field](../../../../../../cyclotomic-field.md) into $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times$. Over $\mathbb Q$, Gauss's irreducibility theorem for cyclotomic [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) says that $\Phi_n$ is irreducible. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta_n):\mathbb Q]=\deg\Phi_n=\varphi(n).}
$$
