<h1 id="25j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Consider $F(A)=A^TA$ with values in the symmetric [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md). At the identity,

$$
DF_I(X)=X^T+X,
$$

which is onto the $n(n+1)/2$-dimensional space of symmetric [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md). The same rank holds at every orthogonal [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md). Thus $O(n)=F^{-1}(I)$ is a manifold, and its determinant-one component, the [special orthogonal group](../../../../../../special-orthogonal-group.md), has dimension

$$
n^2-\frac{n(n+1)}2=\frac{n(n-1)}2.
$$

The tangent equation at the identity is $X^T+X=0$, so

$$
\boxed{T_I SO(n)=\{X:X^T=-X\}.}
$$
