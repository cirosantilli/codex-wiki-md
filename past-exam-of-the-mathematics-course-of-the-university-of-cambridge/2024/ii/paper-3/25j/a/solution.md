<h1 id="25j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A subset $X\subset\mathbb R^N$ is a smooth $k$-manifold if every $x\in X$ has a neighborhood parametrized by a smooth map $\phi:U\subset\mathbb R^k\to X$ that is a homeomorphism onto its image and has [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of rank $k$. Define

$$
T_xX=\operatorname{im}D\phi_u,\qquad \phi(u)=x.
$$

If $\psi$ is another parametrization, the transition maps satisfy

$$
D\phi_u=D\psi_vD(\psi^{-1}\circ\phi)_u.
$$

The transition [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is invertible, so the two images coincide. Thus the tangent space is independent of the parametrization.
