[Watson lemma](../../../../../../watson-s-lemma.md) states that if, near zero,

$$
f(t)\sim\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nt^{n+\lambda-1},
\qquad \lambda>0,
$$

and the tail is suitably controlled, then

$$
\int_0^b e^{-xt}f(t)dt
\sim\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n\Gamma(n+\lambda)x^{-n-\lambda}
$$

as $x\to+\infty$.
