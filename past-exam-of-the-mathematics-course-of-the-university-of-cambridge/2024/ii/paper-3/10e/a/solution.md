<h1 id="10e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the Schmidt rank is one, the decomposition is $|\psi\rangle=|\alpha\rangle|\beta\rangle$ after absorbing its sole coefficient, so the state is a product. Conversely, a product state has coefficient [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $X=ab^T$, which has rank one. Therefore a bipartite pure state is entangled exactly when its Schmidt rank is at least two.
