↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Projecting the [GHZ state](../../../../../../../greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md) onto Charlie's outcome $0$ leaves the unnormalised state $|00\rangle_{AB}/\sqrt2$. After normalization Alice and Bob have $|00\rangle$, a product state.
