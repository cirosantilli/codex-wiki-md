<h1 id="39d/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $k>N/U$, then $m=i\alpha$ with $\alpha>0$ chosen so that the disturbance decays as $z\to\infty$. The [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) amplitude is then ninety degrees out of phase with $w$, since $\widehat p/\widehat w$ is purely imaginary. Hence

$$
\boxed{\langle\overline I_z\rangle_0
=\frac12\operatorname{Re}(\widehat p\widehat w^*)=0.}
$$
