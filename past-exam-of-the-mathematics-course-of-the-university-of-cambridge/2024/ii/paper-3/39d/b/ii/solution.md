<h1 id="39d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [boundary condition](../../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) gives complex amplitude $w_0=Ukh_0$. Substitution of the [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../../internal-wave.md) form

$$
w=w_0e^{i(kx+mz-\omega t)}
$$

into the internal-wave equation gives

$$
\omega^2=\frac{N^2k^2}{k^2+m^2}.
$$

Since $|\omega|=Uk$,

$$
m^2=\frac{N^2}{U^2}-k^2.
$$

For $k<N/U$, choose the sign of $m$ that gives upward energy propagation; with $\omega=-Uk$, this is $m>0$.
