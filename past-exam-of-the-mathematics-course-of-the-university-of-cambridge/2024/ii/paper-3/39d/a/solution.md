<h1 id="39d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The linearized horizontal and vertical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md), density, and incompressibility equations are

$$
\rho_0u_t=-p_x,
\qquad \rho_0w_t=-p_z-g\rho',
\qquad \rho'_t+w\rho_0'=0,
\qquad u_x+w_z=0.
$$

Differentiate the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) equations in time, take $\partial_x$ of the vertical equation minus $\partial_z$ of the horizontal one, and use incompressibility to eliminate $u,p,$ and $\rho'$. Neglecting the stated slow-background term gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2w_{tt}+N^2(z)w_{xx}=0,
\qquad
N^2(z)=-\frac g{\rho_0}\frac{d\rho_0}{dz}.}
$$
