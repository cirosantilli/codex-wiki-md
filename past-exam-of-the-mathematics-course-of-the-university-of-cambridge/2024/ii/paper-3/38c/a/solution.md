<h1 id="38c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With streamwise and normal [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md) $u,v$, zero exterior [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) gives the steady boundary-layer equations

$$
u_x+v_y=0,
\qquad uu_x+vu_y=\nu u_{yy}.
$$

At the organism,

$$
v(x,0)=0,
\qquad \rho\nu u_y(x,0)=Sx^{-1/2},
$$

and matching to the quiescent exterior requires $u(x,y)\to0$ as $y\to\infty$.
