<h1 id="15e/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Both operators map linear combinations of $|\psi_g\rangle$ and $|\psi_b\rangle$ to linear combinations of the same two [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md): $R_g$ merely changes one coefficient, while

$$
R_{|\Omega\rangle}=I-2|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|
$$

has $|\Omega\rangle\in P$. Therefore each leaves $P$ invariant.
