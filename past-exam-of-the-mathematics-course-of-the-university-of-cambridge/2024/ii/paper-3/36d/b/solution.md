↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The tensor $\sigma_{ij}$ is the flux of the $j$th component of electromagnetic [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) across a surface normal to the $i$ direction, with the sign convention used in the conservation law. Its diagonal components are normal [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) fluxes, interpreted as electromagnetic [pressures](../../../../../../pressure.md) or tensions. Its off-diagonal components are tangential [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) fluxes, interpreted as shear stresses.
