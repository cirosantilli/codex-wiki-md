<h1 id="31a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Linearization about $X(t)$ gives

$$
\dot\Phi(t)=Df(X(t))\Phi(t),\qquad \Phi(0)=I.
$$

One [Floquet multiplier](../../../../../../floquet-multiplier.md) of $\Phi(T)$ is $1$, corresponding to a phase shift along the orbit. Liouville's formula gives

$$
\det\Phi(T)=\exp\left(\int_0^T\nabla\cdot f(X(t))dt\right).
$$

In the plane this [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is the other, transverse multiplier. The periodic orbit is transversely asymptotically stable when the displayed quantity is less than one, equivalently when the divergence [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) is negative, and unstable when it is greater than one.
