↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $\nabla f(x_k)=-r_k$, exact line search minimizes $f(x_k+tr_k)$. [Differentiation](../../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
0=r_k^T(Ax_k-b)+t,r_k^TAr_k
=-r_k^Tr_k+t,r_k^TAr_k,
$$

so

$$
t_k=\frac{r_k^Tr_k}{r_k^TAr_k}.
$$
