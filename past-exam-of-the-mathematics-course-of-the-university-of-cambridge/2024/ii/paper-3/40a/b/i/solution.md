<h1 id="40a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla f(x)=Ax-b=A(x-x^*)$. Completing the square gives

$$
f(x)-f(x^*)=\frac12(x-x^*)^TA(x-x^*)>0
$$

for $x\ne x^*$, so $x^*$ is the unique global minimizer. Since $x-x^*=A^{-1}\nabla f(x)$,

$$
\boxed{f(x)-f(x^*)=\frac12\nabla f(x)^TA^{-1}\nabla f(x).}
$$
