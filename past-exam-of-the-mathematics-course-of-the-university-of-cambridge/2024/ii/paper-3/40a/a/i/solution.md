<h1 id="40a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing the error as $e_k=x_k-x^*$ gives

$$
e_{k+1}=He_k
$$

provided the fixed-point consistency condition

$$
x^*=Hx^*+v
$$

holds. Convergence for every starting [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) is therefore equivalent to $H^k\to0$, which in finite dimensions is equivalent to

$$
\rho(H)<1.
$$

These two conditions are necessary and sufficient.
