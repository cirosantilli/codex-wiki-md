<h1 id="34e/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For two solutions of the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md), their Wronskian is constant because the equation has no first-derivative term. The [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) of the Floquet [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) is the ratio of the Wronskian at $x+a$ to that at $x$, hence

$$
\boxed{\det F(E)=1.}
$$
