<h1 id="7d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The meromorphic continuations of $F$ and the [gamma function](../../../../../../gamma-function.md) have the same recurrence and the same value at $1$. Hence their residues at every nonpositive integer are the same. Their difference $f=F-\Gamma$ therefore has removable singularities there and is analytic elsewhere, so it extends to an [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md). Equivalently, $f(z+1)=zf(z)$ and $f(1)=0$ show inductively that the apparent quotient singularities are removable.
