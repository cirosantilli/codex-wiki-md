<h1 id="8e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With $H=p_jp_j/(2m)$, the stated brackets give

$$
\dot q_i=\{q_i,H\}=\frac{p_i}{m},
$$

and

$$
\dot p_i=\{p_i,H\}
=\frac em\epsilon_{ijk}p_jB_k
=e(\dot{\mathbf q}\times\mathbf B)_i.
$$

**Thus $m\ddot{\mathbf q}=e\dot{\mathbf q}\times\mathbf B$, the Lorentz-force equation with zero [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md). The magnetic interaction has been moved from the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) into the noncanonical symplectic structure.**
