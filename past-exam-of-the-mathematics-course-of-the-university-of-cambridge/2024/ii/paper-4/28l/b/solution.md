The central [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) theorem and Delta method give

$$
\sqrt n\{g(\bar X_n)-g(\mu)\}
\Rightarrow N(0,g'(\mu)^2\sigma^2).
$$

Thus one may take

$$
\boxed{\sigma_n=\frac{|g'(\mu)|\sigma}{\sqrt n}.}
$$
