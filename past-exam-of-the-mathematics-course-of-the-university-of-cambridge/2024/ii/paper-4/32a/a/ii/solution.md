<h1 id="32a/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Along a periodic orbit the [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) field is tangent and makes one full turn, so its index around the orbit is $+1$. If the enclosed region contained no fixed point, the normalized [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) field would extend continuously across the disc and its boundary map would have degree zero. This contradicts the index $+1$. Thus every planar periodic orbit encloses a fixed point.
