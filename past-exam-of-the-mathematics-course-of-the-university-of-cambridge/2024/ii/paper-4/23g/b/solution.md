<h1 id="23g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A compactly supported smooth [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) belongs to $H^s(\mathbb R^n)$ for every real $s$. Part (a) therefore puts $u$ in $H^{s+4}$ for every $s$. Taking $s$ arbitrarily large and applying the Sobolev embedding theorem to every [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) shows that $u\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$.
