<h1 id="26g/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the union bound,

$$
\mathbb P(M_n>\varepsilon\sqrt n)
\leq n\mathbb P(|X_1|>\varepsilon\sqrt n).
$$

For an $L^2$ [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md), $t^2\mathbb P(|X_1|>t)\to0$. Taking $t=\varepsilon\sqrt n$ makes the right side tend to zero, proving $M_n/\sqrt n\to0$ in probability.
