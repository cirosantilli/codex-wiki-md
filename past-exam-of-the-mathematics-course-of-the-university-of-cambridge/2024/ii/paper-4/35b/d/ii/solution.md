↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At low density,

$$
\frac{p}{k_BT}
=\frac1{v-b}\exp\left(-\frac{a}{k_BTv}\right)
=\frac1v+\frac{b-a/(k_BT)}{v^2}+O(v^{-3}).
$$

Hence the second virial coefficient is

$$
\boxed{B_2(T)=b-\frac{a}{k_BT}.}
$$
