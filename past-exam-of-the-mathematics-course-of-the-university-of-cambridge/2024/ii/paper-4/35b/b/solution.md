<h1 id="35b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Equality of [chemical potentials](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) gives $d\mu_{\rm l}=d\mu_{\rm g}$ along coexistence. Since

$$
d\mu=-s\,dT+v\,dp,
$$

one obtains

$$
(v_{\rm g}-v_{\rm l})\frac{dp}{dT}=s_{\rm g}-s_{\rm l}.
$$

Using $L=T(s_{\rm g}-s_{\rm l})$ gives the [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](../../../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dp}{dT}=\frac{L}{T(v_{\rm g}-v_{\rm l})}.}
$$
