↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At a first-order transition the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) is continuous but a first [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) such as [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) or volume jumps; there is latent heat. At a second-order transition the first [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are continuous while a second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), such as [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) or susceptibility, is discontinuous or divergent, and there is no latent heat.
