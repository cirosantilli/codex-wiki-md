↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Fit the intercept-only model with
```
glm(dis ~ 1, family = binomial, data = disease)
```
Its deviance is $73.5$. Since it has one fitted coefficient, its AIC on the same convention is

$$
\boxed{73.5+2=75.5.}
$$
