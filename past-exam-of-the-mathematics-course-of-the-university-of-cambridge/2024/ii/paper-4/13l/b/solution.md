<h1 id="13l/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Holding risk category fixed, increasing pollution by one unit multiplies the disease odds by

$$
e^{0.18243}\simeq1.20.
$$

**Thus the fitted odds increase by about twenty percent per unit of pollution.**
