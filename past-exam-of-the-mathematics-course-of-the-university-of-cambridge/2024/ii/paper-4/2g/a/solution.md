↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each degree and coefficient bound there are finitely many integer [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Hence $\mathbb Z[x]$ is countable. Every nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has finitely many roots, and every algebraic number is a root of one of these [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). A countable union of finite sets is countable.
