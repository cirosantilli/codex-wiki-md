<h1 id="9d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Before electron-positron annihilation, the electromagnetic plasma has

$$
g_{*s}=2+\frac78(2+2)=\frac{11}{2},
$$

whereas afterward only the two photon polarizations remain, so $g_{*s}=2$. [Entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) conservation in the decoupled electromagnetic sector gives

$$
\frac{11}{2}T_{\rm before}^3a^3=2T_\gamma^3a^3.
$$

Neutrinos receive none of this [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) and continue cooling as $T_\nu\propto a^{-1}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_\nu}{T_\gamma}=\left(\frac4{11}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$
