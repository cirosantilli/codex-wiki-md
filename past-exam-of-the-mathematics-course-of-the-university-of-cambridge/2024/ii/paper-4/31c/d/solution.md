<h1 id="31c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The action [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) can be evaluated exactly:

$$
I(\lambda)=\lambda\log\left(\lambda+\sqrt{\lambda^2-1}\right)
-\sqrt{\lambda^2-1}.
$$

As $\lambda\to\infty$,

$$
I(\lambda)=\lambda\log(2\lambda)-\lambda+O(\lambda^{-1}).
$$

Its leading logarithmic term is $\lambda\log\lambda$. Hence the large [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) satisfy, to leading order,

$$
\boxed{\lambda\log\lambda
=\epsilon\left(n+\frac34\right)\pi.}
$$
