A homomorphism $f:D\to D'$ is a map $f:Q\to Q'$ satisfying

$$
f(q_0)=q'_0,
\qquad f(\delta(q,a))=\delta'(f(q),a)
$$

for every state $q$ and symbol $a$, together with

$$
q\in F\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(q)\in F'.
$$
