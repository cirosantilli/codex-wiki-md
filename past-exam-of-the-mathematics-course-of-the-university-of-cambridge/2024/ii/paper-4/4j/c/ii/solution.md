**True.** In fact any fixed finite $D$ works. If $D'\leq D$, then $D'$ has at most $|Q|$ states. For a fixed finite alphabet, there are only finitely many transition tables, initial states, and accepting subsets on at most $|Q|$ labelled states, hence only finitely many isomorphism classes.
