<h1 id="33b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The unperturbed ground state is $|0,0\rangle$ with energy $\hbar\omega$. Since

$$
H_{\rm int}|0,0\rangle
=\frac{\lambda\hbar}{2\omega}|1,1\rangle,
$$

the [first-order energy correction](../../../../../../../first-order-energy-correction.md) vanishes and [first-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../../../first-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|\Omega\rangle=|0,0\rangle-\frac{\lambda}{4\omega^2}|1,1\rangle+O(\lambda^2),
\qquad
E_\Omega=\hbar\omega+O(\lambda^2).}
$$
