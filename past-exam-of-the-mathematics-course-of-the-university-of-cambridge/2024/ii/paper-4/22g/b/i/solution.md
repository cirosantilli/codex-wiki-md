↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This is the unilateral right shift. It is not compact because the orthonormal [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md) $(Te_n)=(e_{n+1})$ has no convergent subsequence. Its norm is one. For $|\lambda|>1$ the Neumann [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) gives a resolvent; for $|\lambda|<1$, $\bar\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the adjoint left shift, so $\lambda$ lies in the [spectrum](../../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of $T$. Closedness supplies the boundary. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sigma(T)=\{\lambda:|\lambda|\leq1\}.}
$$
