↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This is the unilateral left shift. It is not compact because $S e_{n+1}=e_n$. For every $|\lambda|<1$,

$$
(1,\lambda,\lambda^2,\ldots)\in\ell^2
$$

is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$. The norm bound and closedness then give

$$
\boxed{\sigma(S)=\{\lambda:|\lambda|\leq1\}.}
$$
