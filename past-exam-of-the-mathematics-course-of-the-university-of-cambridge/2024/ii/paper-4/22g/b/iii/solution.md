<h1 id="22g/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The weighted shift is compact because truncating after the first $N$ weights gives finite-rank operators converging in norm, as $1/n\to0$. Moreover

$$
\|R^m\|\leq\frac1{m!},
$$

so its spectral radius is zero. Since the [spectrum](../../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is nonempty,

$$
\boxed{\sigma(R)=\{0\}.}
$$
