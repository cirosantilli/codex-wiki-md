<h1 id="3k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) is nonnegative. Gibbs' inequality also gives

$$
H(X_1,ldots,X_n)\leq\log_2|A^n|=n\log_2 27.
$$

Divide by $n$ and pass to the assumed [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) to obtain

$$
0\leq H_E\leq\log_2 27.
$$
