<h1 id="7d/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $a=0$, $b=1$, $c=\infty$, $\alpha=\beta=0$. Comparing the $w'$ coefficient gives

$$
\alpha'=1-C,
\qquad \beta'=C-A-B,
$$

and comparison of the $w$ coefficient gives the exponents $A,B$ at infinity. Hence

$$
P\left\{
\begin{matrix}
0&1&\infty&\\
0&0&A&z\\
1-C&C-A-B&B&
\end{matrix}
\right\}.
$$
