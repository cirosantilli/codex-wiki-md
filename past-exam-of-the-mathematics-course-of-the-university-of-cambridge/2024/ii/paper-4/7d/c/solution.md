The Papperitz symbol is

$$
P\left\{
\begin{matrix}
a&b&c&\\
\alpha&\beta&\gamma&z\\
\alpha'&\beta'&\gamma'&
\end{matrix}
\right\}.
$$

It records the three regular singular points and the two characteristic exponents at each.
