<h1 id="11f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Reducing $x^2-7y^2=5$ modulo seven gives $x^2\equiv5\pmod7$, again impossible because five is not a quadratic residue modulo seven.
