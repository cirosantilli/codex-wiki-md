<h1 id="11f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Reducing $x^2-7y^2=-1$ modulo seven gives $x^2\equiv-1\equiv6\pmod7$. The quadratic residues modulo seven are $0,1,2,4$, so no solution exists.
