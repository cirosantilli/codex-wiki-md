The [unshifted QR algorithm](../../../../../../../unshifted-qr-algorithm.md) computes the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$. For a real symmetric [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) it drives $A_k$ toward a diagonal or block-diagonal [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md); the accumulated orthogonal factors simultaneously approximate its [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md).
