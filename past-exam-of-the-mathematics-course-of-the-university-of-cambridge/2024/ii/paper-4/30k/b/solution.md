↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a convex surrogate margin loss $\phi$, the empirical risk is

$$
\widehat R_\phi(h)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n
\phi(y_ih(x_i)).
$$

It replaces the discontinuous classification error by a convex penalty on signed margins.
