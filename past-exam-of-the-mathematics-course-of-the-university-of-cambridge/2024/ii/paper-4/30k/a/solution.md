<h1 id="30k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The subdifferential is

$$
\partial f(\alpha)=\{s:f(\beta)\geq f(\alpha)+s^T(\beta-\alpha)
\text{ for every }\beta\}.
$$

If $0\in\partial f(\alpha)$, this inequality says $f(\beta)\geq f(\alpha)$, so $\alpha$ minimizes $f$. Conversely, the zero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) satisfies the subgradient inequality at any global minimizer.
