<h1 id="20f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The equality of residue-field sizes says that the residue degree $f(Q/P)$ is one. Since $Q=\alpha O_L$, the principal [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) factorization of $\alpha$ has valuation one at $Q$ and zero at every other prime. Taking the relative [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) norm gives

$$
N_{L/K}(\alpha)O_K=N_{L/K}(\alpha O_L)
=N_{L/K}(Q)=P^{f(Q/P)}=P.
$$

**Thus $P=N_{L/K}(\alpha)O_K$.**
