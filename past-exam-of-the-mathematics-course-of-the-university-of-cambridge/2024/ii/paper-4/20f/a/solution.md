<h1 id="20f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $Q\mid PO_L$, then $PO_L\subseteq Q$, so $P\subseteq Q\cap O_K$. The contraction is a nonzero prime [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) of $O_K$ containing the maximal [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $P$, hence equals $P$. Conversely, if $P=Q\cap O_K$, every element of $P$ lies in $Q$, so the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) it generates satisfies $PO_L\subseteq Q$, equivalently $Q\mid PO_L$.
