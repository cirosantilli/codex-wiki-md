<h1 id="36d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Split the microscopic sources into free and bound parts and define

$$
D=\epsilon_0E+P,
\qquad
H=\frac{B}{\mu_0}-M.
$$

Substitution of $\rho=\rho_{\rm f}-\nabla\cdot P$ into [Gauss's law](../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $\nabla\cdot D=\rho_{\rm f}$. Similarly, using $J=J_{\rm f}+\partial_tP+\nabla\times M$ in the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives $\nabla\times H=J_{\rm f}+\partial_tD$. The homogeneous [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) are unchanged. Thus

$$
\nabla\cdot D=\rho_{\rm f},\qquad
\nabla\times H=J_{\rm f}+\frac{\partial D}{\partial t},
$$



$$
\nabla\cdot B=0,\qquad
\nabla\times E=-\frac{\partial B}{\partial t}.
$$
