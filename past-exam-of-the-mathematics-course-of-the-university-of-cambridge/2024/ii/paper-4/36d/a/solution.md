A dielectric is a medium whose constituent charges become polarized by an applied [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md); it may also acquire a magnetization. Spatial variation of $P$ leaves uncompensated dipole charge, giving $\rho_{\rm bd}=-\nabla\cdot P$. Time-dependent polarization transports charge and aligned microscopic current loops contribute magnetization current, giving the [bound charge and bound current](../../../../../../bound-charge-and-bound-current.md)

$$
J_{\rm bd}=\partial_tP+\nabla\times M.
$$
