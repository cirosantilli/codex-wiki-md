<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
f=(X^2-2)(X^2-3),
\qquad
g=X^4-10X^2+1.
$$

Both have splitting field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$. On the roots of $f$, the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) has two orbits of size two and embeds as

$$
\{1,(12),(34),(12)(34)\}.
$$

The roots of $g$, namely $\pm\sqrt2\pm\sqrt3$, form one orbit and give the regular Klein-four [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md)

$$
\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}.
$$

The [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are not conjugate because their orbit decompositions differ.
