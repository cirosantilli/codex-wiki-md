<h1 id="18h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Dedekind's factorization/Frobenius theorem says that for a prime not dividing the discriminant, the degrees of the distinct irreducible factors modulo $p$ give the cycle lengths of an element of the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md).

The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$ by the rational-root test followed by a comparison of possible monic quadratic factors. Its discriminant is $547^2$, a square, so its transitive Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) lies in $A_4$. Modulo five,

$$
f(X)\equiv X(X^3-X-2),
$$

and the cubic has no root in $\mathbb F_5$, giving a three-cycle. A transitive [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of $A_4$ containing a three-cycle cannot be the Klein four [group](../../../../../../group-split.md); hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb Q)\cong A_4.}
$$
