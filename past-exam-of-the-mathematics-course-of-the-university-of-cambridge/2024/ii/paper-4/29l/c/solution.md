<h1 id="29l/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $s<t$,

$$
e^{-rt}S_t=e^{-rs}S_s
\exp\left(-\frac12\sigma^2(t-s)+\sigma(W_t-W_s)\right).
$$

The increment is independent of the Brownian filtration at time $s$, and its exponential factor has mean one by the Gaussian moment-generating [function](../../../../../../function-split.md). Hence the conditional expectation of the discounted time-$t$ price is the discounted time-$s$ price.
