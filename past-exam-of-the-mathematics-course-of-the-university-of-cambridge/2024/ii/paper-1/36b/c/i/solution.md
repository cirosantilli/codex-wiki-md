<h1 id="36b/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $r=|x|$, set

$$
r=V^{1/3}(k_BT)^{1/(2n)}u.
$$

Then

$$
\int e^{-\beta U(x)}d^3x
=4\pi V(k_BT)^{3/(2n)}I_n.
$$

Part (b) gives

$$
Z_1=\frac{4}{\pi(\hbar c)^3}
V(k_BT)^{3+3/(2n)}I_n.
$$

For $N$ identical classical non-interacting particles,

$$
Z_N=\frac{Z_1^N}{N!}.
$$

Using $\log N!=N\log N-N+o(N)$,

$$
F=-Nk_BT\left[\log V+A\log(k_BT)+\log I_n+B\right]+o(N),
$$

where

$$
\boxed{
A=3+\frac{3}{2n},
\qquad
B=1+\log\!\left(\frac{4}{\pi N(\hbar c)^3}\right).}
$$

This is the [canonical partition function for an ultrarelativistic gas in a power-law trap](../../../../../../../canonical-partition-function-for-an-ultrarelativistic-gas-in-a-power-law-trap.md).
