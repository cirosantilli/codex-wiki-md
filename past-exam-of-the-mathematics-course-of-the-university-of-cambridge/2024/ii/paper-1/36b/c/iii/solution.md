↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $A=3+3/(2n)$. Since $\log Z_N$ contains $-NA\log\beta$,

$$
\boxed{
E=-\partial_\beta\log Z_N=NAk_BT,}
$$



$$
\boxed{
(\Delta E)^2=\partial_\beta^2\log Z_N
=NA(k_BT)^2,
\qquad C_V=NAk_B.}
$$

Consequently

$$
\frac{\Delta E}{E}=\frac1{\sqrt{NA}}\longrightarrow0
$$

in the thermodynamic [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md): relative energy fluctuations vanish.
