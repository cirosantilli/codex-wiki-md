↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The normalized one-particle spatial density gives the local number density

$$
\boxed{
n(x)=\frac{N}{4\pi V(k_BT)^{3/(2n)}I_n}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{|x|^{2n}}
 {V^{2n/3}k_BT}\right].}
$$

The radial probability density is proportional to

$$
r^2\exp\!\left[-\frac{r^{2n}}{V^{2n/3}k_BT}\right].
$$

Its logarithmic [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes when

$$
\frac2r-\frac{2n r^{2n-1}}{V^{2n/3}k_BT}=0,
$$

so the most likely radius is

$$
\boxed{r_{\rm mode}=V^{1/3}\left(\frac{k_BT}{n}\right)^{1/(2n)}.}
$$
