<h1 id="38b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a circular orbit, the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of

$$
V_{\rm eff}(r)=-\frac mr+\frac{h^2}{2r^2}-\frac{mh^2}{r^3}
$$

vanishes. Thus

$$
\frac m{r^2}-\frac{h^2}{r^3}+\frac{3mh^2}{r^4}=0,
\qquad
\boxed{h^2=\frac{mr^2}{r-3m}.}
$$

A real finite [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{r>3m.}
$$
