<h1 id="38b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a circular orbit, normalization and the value of $h$ give

$$
E^2=\frac{(r-2m)^2}{r(r-3m)},
\qquad
\dot t^2=\frac r{r-3m},
\qquad
\dot\phi^2=\frac{m}{r^2(r-3m)}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{
\left(\frac{d\phi}{dt}\right)^2
=\frac{\dot\phi^2}{\dot t^2}=\frac m{r^3}.}
$$

This is the relativistic form of the circular-orbit [Kepler third law](../../../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md).
