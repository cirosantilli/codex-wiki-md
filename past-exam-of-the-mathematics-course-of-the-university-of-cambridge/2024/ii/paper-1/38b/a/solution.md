<h1 id="38b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The Killing [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $\partial_t$ and $\partial_\phi$ give conserved specific energy and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md),

$$
E=\left(1-\frac{2m}{r}\right)\dot t,
\qquad
h=r^2\dot\phi.
$$

The normalization $g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu=-1$ then rearranges to

$$
\frac12\dot r^2-\frac{m}{r}+\frac{h^2}{2r^2}-\frac{mh^2}{r^3}
=\frac{E^2-1}{2},
$$

so the displayed radial energy is also constant. This is the [effective potential for timelike Schwarzschild geodesics](../../../../../../effective-potential-for-timelike-schwarzschild-geodesics.md).
