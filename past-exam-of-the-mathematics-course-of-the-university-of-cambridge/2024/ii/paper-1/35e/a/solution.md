<h1 id="35e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For real $k$, conservation of the radial [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md), equivalently constancy of the radial Wronskian, equates the incoming and outgoing fluxes and gives

$$
S_l(k)^*S_l(k)=1.
$$

For a real potential, the regular radial solution depends on $k$ through $k^2$. Comparing its asymptotic form at $k$ and $-k$ therefore gives

$$
S_l(k)S_l(-k)=1.
$$

The first identity places $S_l(k)$ on the unit circle, so

$$
S_l(k)=e^{2i\delta_l(k)}
$$

for a real phase shift. Choosing the phase continuously, the second identity gives

$$
\boxed{\delta_l(-k)=-\delta_l(k).}
$$

These are the [unitarity and reflection identities for a partial-wave S-matrix](../../../../../../unitarity-and-reflection-identities-for-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md).
