A [generalized linear model](../../../../../../generalized-linear-model.md) has three components:

- a random component in which independent responses belong to an exponential dispersion family;
- a systematic component $\eta_i=x_i^T\beta$;
- a link [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) satisfying $g(\mu_i)=\eta_i$, where $\mu_i=\mathbb E Y_i$.
