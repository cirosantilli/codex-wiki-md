<h1 id="14d/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Taking Laplace transforms and using $y(0)=0$ gives

$$
(\alpha s+1)Y(s)=F(s).
$$

Hence the transfer [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{G(s)=\frac{Y(s)}{F(s)}=\frac1{\alpha s+1}}.
$$

Its pole is at $-1/\alpha$, and its causal impulse response is

$$
g(t)=\frac1\alpha e^{-t/\alpha}H(t).
$$

It is absolutely integrable exactly when

$$
\boxed{\alpha>0}.
$$
