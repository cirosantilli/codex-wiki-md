A map is chaotic in Devaney's sense when it is topologically transitive, its periodic points are dense, and it has sensitive dependence on initial conditions. Explicitly, transitivity means that for every nonempty open $U,V$ some $n$ has $F^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$, while sensitivity means that one $\delta>0$ works so that arbitrarily close initial points eventually separate by more than $\delta$.
