↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In Glendinning's definition, a continuous interval map $F$ is chaotic when $F^m$ has a horseshoe for some $m\geq1$. Thus there are two disjoint subintervals whose images under $F^m$ each cover their union, producing full two-symbol itinerary dynamics.
