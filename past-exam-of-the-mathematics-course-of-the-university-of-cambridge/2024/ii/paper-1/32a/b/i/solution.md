<h1 id="32a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the period-three points in increasing order and take the two intervals between consecutive points. For either possible cyclic ordering, the interval-covering transition graph is, after interchanging its vertices,

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The loop at the first vertex gives a fixed point. For every $n\geq2$, the closed itinerary

$$
0^{n-1}1
$$

has least period $n$. The interval-covering periodic-orbit theorem supplies a point with that itinerary and therefore a cycle of least period $n$. Thus $F$ has periodic orbits of every positive period. This is the period-three case of the [Sharkovsky theorem](../../../../../../../sharkovskii-s-theorem.md).
