<h1 id="32a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An itinerary fixed by the eighth shift has least period dividing $8$. Those with period dividing $4$ are exactly the itineraries fixed by the fourth shift. Thus the primitive length-eight itineraries form

$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^8)-\operatorname{tr}(A^4)=47-7=40
$$

pointed words. Each cycle has eight cyclic shifts, so they give

$$
\boxed{40/8=5}
$$

distinct $8$-cycles. Therefore at least five such cycles are forced.
