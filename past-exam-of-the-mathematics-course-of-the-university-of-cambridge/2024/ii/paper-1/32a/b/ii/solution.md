↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the Fibonacci transition [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) above,

$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^n)=L_n,
$$

where $L_n$ is the $n$th Lucas number. It counts closed symbolic itineraries of length $n$. Since $7$ is prime, the itineraries fixed by the seventh shift have least period either $1$ or $7$. Hence the number of cyclic classes of primitive length-seven itineraries is

$$
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A^7)-\operatorname{tr}(A)}7
=\frac{29-1}{7}=4.
$$

Distinct primitive cyclic itineraries give distinct cycles, so $F$ has at least four distinct $7$-cycles. This is the [fibonacci transition-graph cycle count](../../../../../../../fibonacci-transition-graph-cycle-count.md).
