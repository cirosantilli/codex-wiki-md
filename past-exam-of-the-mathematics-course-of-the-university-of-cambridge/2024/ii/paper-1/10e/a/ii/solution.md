↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The Walsh-Hadamard identity

$$
H^{\otimes n}
\frac1{2^{n/2}}\sum_x(-1)^{a\cdot x}|x\rangle=|a\rangle
$$

and $H| -\rangle=|1\rangle$ give

$$
\boxed{|\Phi_2\rangle=(-1)^b|a\rangle|1\rangle}.
$$
