↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For points $z_1,ldots,z_n$, let

$$
H(z_{1:n})=\{(h(z_1),\ldots,h(z_n)):h\in H\}.
$$

The shattering coefficient and [VC dimension](../../../../../../../vc-dimension.md) are

$$
s(H,n)=\sup_{z_{1:n}}|H(z_{1:n})|,
\qquad
VC(H)=\sup\{n:s(H,n)=2^n\}.
$$

**Thus a set of $n$ points is shattered when every binary labeling occurs on it.**
