↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Condition on the sample. The set of [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md)

$$
\bigl(D_i h(X_i)\bigr)_{i=1}^n,qquad h\in H,
$$

has at most $s(H,n)$ distinct members, each with Euclidean norm at most $M\sqrt n$. The [Massart finite-class lemma](../../../../../../../massart-finite-class-lemma.md), the [Sauer-Shelah growth bound](../../../../../../../sauer-shelah-growth-bound.md), and $VC(H)\leq p$ give

$$
\widehat R(\mathcal F)
\leq M\sqrt{\frac{2\log s(H,n)}n}
\leq M\sqrt{\frac{2p\log(n+1)}n}.
$$

Taking expectations and substituting this into part (i) yields

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb E Q(\widehat h)
\leq Q(h^*)+2M\sqrt{\frac{2p\log(n+1)}n}.}
$$
