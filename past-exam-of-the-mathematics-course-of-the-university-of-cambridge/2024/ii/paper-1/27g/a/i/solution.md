↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A measure $\mu$ on $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ is $\sigma$-finite when

$$
\Omega=\bigcup_{n\geq1}E_n
\qquad\text{with}\qquad
\mu(E_n)<\infty.
$$

The $\sigma$-finite uniqueness theorem says that two measures agreeing on a generating $\pi$-system agree on the generated $\sigma$-algebra, provided the space is covered by countably many sets in that system having finite common measure. Equivalently, a $\sigma$-finite premeasure has at most one extension to the generated $\sigma$-algebra.
