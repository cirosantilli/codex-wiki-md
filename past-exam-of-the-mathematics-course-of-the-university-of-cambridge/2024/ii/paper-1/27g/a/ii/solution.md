<h1 id="27g/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Fix $x\in\mathbb R$ and define $\mu_x(B)=\lambda(B+x)$. This is a $\sigma$-finite measure on the Borel sets. For every half-open interval,

$$
\mu_x((a,b])=\lambda((a+x,b+x])=b-a=\lambda((a,b]).
$$

The half-open intervals form a generating $\pi$-system, so the $\sigma$-finite uniqueness theorem gives $\mu_x=\lambda$. Thus [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) is translation invariant.
