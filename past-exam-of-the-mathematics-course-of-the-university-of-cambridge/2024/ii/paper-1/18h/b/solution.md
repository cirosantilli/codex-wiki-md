<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $d=[L:K]$. If $\alpha=0$ the result is immediate, so suppose $\alpha=x^n\ne0$ with $x\in L$. The [field norm](../../../../../../field-norm.md) is multiplicative, sends an element of $K$ to its $d$th power, and commutes with powers. Therefore

$$
\alpha^d=N_{L/K}(\alpha)=N_{L/K}(x)^n.
$$

Choose integers $r,s$ with $rd+sn=1$. Then

$$
\alpha=(\alpha^d)^r(\alpha^n)^s
      =\bigl(N_{L/K}(x)^r\alpha^s\bigr)^n.
$$

The expression in parentheses belongs to $K$, so $\alpha$ is an $n$th power in $K$. This is [coprime-degree descent for powers](../../../../../../coprime-degree-descent-for-powers.md).
