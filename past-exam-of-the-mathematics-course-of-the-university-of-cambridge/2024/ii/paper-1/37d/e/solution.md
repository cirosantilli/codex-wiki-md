<h1 id="37d/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the static point source, take $A=0$ and $\alpha=0$. The [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) equation becomes

$$
(\nabla^2-m^2)\phi=-c^2\mu_0q\,\delta(x).
$$

Its spatial Fourier transform satisfies

$$
(|k|^2+m^2)\widehat\phi(k)=c^2\mu_0q.
$$

The stated inverse transform therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\phi(r)=\lambda\frac{e^{-mr}}r,\qquad A=0,}
$$

where $\lambda$ absorbs the transform normalization and source strength. This is the [Yukawa potential](../../../../../../yukawa-potential.md): a nonzero vector-field mass screens the interaction beyond the range $m^{-1}$. As $m\to0$, it tends to the long-range Coulomb potential $\lambda/r$. The same [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) removes the mass term that broke gauge invariance in part (b).
