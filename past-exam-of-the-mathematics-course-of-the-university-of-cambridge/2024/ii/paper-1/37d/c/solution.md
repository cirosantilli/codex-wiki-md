<h1 id="37d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the divergence of the field equation. Commuting [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) and antisymmetry of $F^{\mu\nu}$ give

$$
\partial_\nu\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0.
$$

Current conservation then yields

$$
-m^2\partial_\nu A^\nu=-\mu_0\partial_\nu J^\nu=0.
$$

For $m>0$,

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu A^\mu=0.}
$$

This is the [Lorenz constraint in Proca theory](../../../../../../lorenz-constraint-in-proca-theory.md); it follows dynamically rather than being a gauge choice.
