<h1 id="26j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a smooth [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) $N$. For $v,w\in T_p\Sigma$, the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) and [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) are

$$
I_p(v,w)=\langle v,w\rangle,
\qquad
II_p(v,w)=\langle S_pv,w\rangle,
$$

where the shape operator is $S_p=-dN_p$. The principal curvatures are the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\kappa_1,\kappa_2$ of $S_p$; the [mean curvature](../../../../../../mean-curvature.md) and [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) are

$$
H=\frac12\operatorname{tr}S_p=\frac{\kappa_1+\kappa_2}{2},
\qquad
K=\det S_p=\kappa_1\kappa_2.
$$

The surface is minimal when $H=0$ everywhere.
