<h1 id="16i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Start with the two families of uniqueness and injectivity clauses from part (i), and for every $x\in X$ add the finite clause

$$
\bigvee_{y\in A_x}p_{x,y}.
$$

This clause is a legitimate proposition because $A_x$ is finite and nonempty. It makes every $x$ have a value in $A_x$, while the earlier clauses make that value unique and make the resulting [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) injective. Conversely, every injection $g:X\to Y$ with $g(x)\in A_x$ satisfies all these clauses.
