↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The nested-model $F$-test has hypotheses

$$
H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=0,
\qquad
H_1:(\beta_1,\beta_2)\ne(0,0).
$$

Its statistic is

$$
F=\frac{(1704.9-1477.7)/2}{1477.7/170}=13.069.
$$

The reported $p$-value $5.252\times10^{-6}$ gives strong evidence against $H_0$: weight and width are jointly useful.

Neither individual coefficient is significant at $5\%$. This is consistent with the predictor correlation $0.877$: strong [multicollinearity](../../../../../../multicollinearity.md) makes the two partial effects difficult to distinguish and inflates their individual standard errors, even though their joint contribution is clear.
