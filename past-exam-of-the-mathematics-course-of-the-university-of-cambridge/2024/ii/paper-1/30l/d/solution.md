<h1 id="30l/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The same mixing argument with a fixed $q^0\in\mathcal Q$ shows that

$$
q^TZ\geq0
$$

for every $q\geq0$ in $\ker P^T$, including boundary [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md). By the [finite-state superhedging alternative](../../../../../../finite-state-superhedging-alternative.md), there is $\phi\in\mathbb R^d$ such that

$$
\boxed{Z-P\phi\geq0,}
$$

which is the asserted almost-sure inequality. No strict residual is required here.
