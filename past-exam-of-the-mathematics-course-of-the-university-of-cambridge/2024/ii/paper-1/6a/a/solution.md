<h1 id="6a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $F(n)=\alpha n-\beta n^3$, the nonnegative equilibria are

$$
\boxed{n=0,
\qquad n=K:=\sqrt{\alpha/\beta}}.
$$

Since

$$
F'(0)=\alpha>0,
\qquad F'(K)=\alpha-3\beta K^2=-2\alpha<0,
$$

the zero equilibrium is unstable and $K$ is asymptotically stable.
