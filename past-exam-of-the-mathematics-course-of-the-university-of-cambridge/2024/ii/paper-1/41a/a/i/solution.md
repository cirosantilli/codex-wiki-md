<h1 id="41a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expanding the square and using orthogonality of Fourier modes,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}|\widehat v(\theta)|^2d\theta
&=\sum_{m,j}v_m\overline{v_j}
\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}e^{-i(m-j)\theta}d\theta\\
&=\sum_m|v_m|^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Finite truncations justify the calculation directly, and passage to the $\ell^2$ [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) proves [Parseval identity](../../../../../../../parseval-identity.md) for every square-summable [sequence](../../../../../../../sequence.md).
