<h1 id="2g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The $n$th [Chebyshev polynomial](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial.md) is defined on $[-1,1]$ by

$$
T_n(x)=\cos(n\arccos x).
$$

The cosine addition formula gives

$$
T_0(x)=1,
\qquad T_1(x)=x,
\qquad T_{n+1}(x)=2xT_n(x)-T_{n-1}(x).
$$

Induction therefore shows that every $T_n$ is a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Its defining cosine formula also gives

$$
\boxed{-1\leq T_n(x)\leq1\quad(-1\leq x\leq1)}.
$$
