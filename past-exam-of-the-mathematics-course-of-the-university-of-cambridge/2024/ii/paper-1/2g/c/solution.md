<h1 id="2g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $x=\cos\theta\in[-1,1]$,

$$
T_n(-x)=\cos(n(\pi-\theta))=(-1)^n\cos(n\theta)=(-1)^nT_n(x).
$$

Both sides are [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $x$. Since they agree on an interval, the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) identity theorem extends the equality to every real $x$. Thus $T_n$ is even for even $n$ and odd for odd $n$.
