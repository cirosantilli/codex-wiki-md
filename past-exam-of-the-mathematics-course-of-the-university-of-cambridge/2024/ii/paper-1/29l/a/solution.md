<h1 id="29l/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the standard differentiability, identifiability, moment, and nonsingularity assumptions,

$$
\boxed{
\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta_0)
\xrightarrow{d}N(0,I(\theta_0)^{-1}).}
$$

Here the one-observation score is

$$
S_\theta(X)=\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\log f(X,\theta),
$$

and the [Fisher information matrix](../../../../../../fisher-information-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{I(\theta)=\mathbb E_\theta[S_\theta(X)S_\theta(X)^T]
=-\mathbb E_\theta\left[\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta\partial\theta^T}log f(X,\theta)\right].}
$$
