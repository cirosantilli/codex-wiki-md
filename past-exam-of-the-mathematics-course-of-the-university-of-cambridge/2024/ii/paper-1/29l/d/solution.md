<h1 id="29l/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Factorizing and using part (c),

$$
n(\widehat\theta^2-\theta_0^2)
=-n(\theta_0-\widehat\theta)(\widehat\theta+\theta_0)
\xrightarrow d -2\theta_0Z
$$

by the [Slutsky theorem](../../../../../../slutsky-theorem.md), since $\widehat\theta\to\theta_0$ in probability. Thus the limiting variable is a negative scaled exponential, with $Z\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1/\theta_0)$.
