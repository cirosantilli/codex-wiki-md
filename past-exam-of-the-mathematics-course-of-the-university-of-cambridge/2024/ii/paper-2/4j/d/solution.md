↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Fix $x_0\in X$. The property defining $\mathbf{Inf}_X$ depends only on the partial [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) computed, so it is an index set. It is nontrivial: an index of the nowhere-defined [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is outside it, whereas an index of the total [constant function](../../../../../../constant-function.md) $n\mapsto x_0$ has infinite domain and range $\{x_0\}\subseteq X$, and hence lies in it. The [Rice theorem](../../../../../../rice-s-theorem.md) now shows that this [infinite-domain range-restriction index set](../../../../../../infinite-domain-range-restriction-index-set.md) is not computable.
