<h1 id="30l/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The increment of the stopped process is

$$
M_{(n+1)\wedge T}-M_{n\wedge T}
=\mathbf1_{\{T>n\}}(M_{n+1}-M_n).
$$

Because $T$ is a stopping time, $\{T>n\}\in\mathcal F_n$. Taking conditional expectation therefore gives zero. The stopped variables are adapted and integrable, so the [stopped martingale in discrete time](../../../../../../stopped-martingale-in-discrete-time.md) is a martingale.
