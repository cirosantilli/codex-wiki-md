<h1 id="37b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\xi$ be the forward reaction extent. Then

$$
dN_A=-d\xi,\qquad dN_B=2d\xi,\qquad dN_C=d\xi.
$$

At fixed $T,p$,

$$
dG=\mu_A\,dN_A+\mu_B\,dN_B+\mu_C\,dN_C
=(-\mu_A+2\mu_B+\mu_C)d\xi.
$$

Minimization with respect to $\xi$ gives the [chemical-potential balance for a reaction](../../../../../../chemical-potential-balance-for-a-reaction.md)

$$
\boxed{\mu_A=2\mu_B+\mu_C}.
$$
