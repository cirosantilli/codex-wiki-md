An intensive quantity is unchanged when the size of a homogeneous system is scaled; [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) are examples. An extensive quantity scales in proportion to system size; volume and particle number are examples. Energy and [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) are also extensive, while [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) is intensive. These are [intensive and extensive thermodynamic quantities](../../../../../../intensive-and-extensive-thermodynamic-quantities.md).
