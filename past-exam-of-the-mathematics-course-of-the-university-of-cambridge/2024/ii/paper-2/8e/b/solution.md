<h1 id="8e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A coordinate $q$ is an [ignorable coordinate](../../../../../../ignorable-coordinate.md) when $L$ has no explicit dependence on it, so $\partial L/\partial q=0$. Its Euler--Lagrange equation is

$$
\frac d{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q}
=\frac{\partial L}{\partial q}=0,
$$

which proves that $p_q$ is conserved.
