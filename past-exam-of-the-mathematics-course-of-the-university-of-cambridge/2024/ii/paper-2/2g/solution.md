The [Baire category theorem](../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says that a complete metric space cannot be a countable union of nowhere-dense closed sets. Equivalently, every countable intersection of open dense sets is dense.

A point $x$ of a metric space is an [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md) when some $\varepsilon>0$ satisfies $B(x,\varepsilon)=\{x\}$.
