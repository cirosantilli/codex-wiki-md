<h1 id="26j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a positively oriented simple closed curve bounding a disc $D$ on the unit sphere, the local [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) gives

$$
\int_\alpha\kappa_g\,ds+\int_DK\,dA=2\pi.
$$

Here $K=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\int_0^L\kappa_g\,ds+A=2\pi}.
$$

At fixed length $L$, maximizing the enclosed area $A$ is therefore exactly the same as minimizing $\int_0^L\kappa_g\,ds$.
