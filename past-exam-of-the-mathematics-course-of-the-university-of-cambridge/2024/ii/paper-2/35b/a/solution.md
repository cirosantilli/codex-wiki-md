<h1 id="35b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) satisfies

$$
\rho=\rho^\dagger,\qquad \rho\succeq0,\qquad\operatorname{tr}\rho=1.
$$

An $N\times N$ Hermitian [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has $N^2$ real parameters, and the trace condition removes one, leaving

$$
\boxed{N^2-1}
$$

real free parameters.

If $A_H(t)=U(t)^\dagger A_SU(t)$, equality of [expectation values](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) requires

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_HU^\dagger A_SU)
=\operatorname{tr}(U\rho_HU^\dagger A_S).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\rho_S(t)=U(t)\rho_HU(t)^\dagger}.
$$
