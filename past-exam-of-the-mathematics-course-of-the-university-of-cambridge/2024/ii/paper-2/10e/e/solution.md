<h1 id="10e/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Applying the transform to the coset state gives amplitude

$$
\frac1{2\sqrt{12}}\omega^c
\sum_{j=0}^3\omega^{3jc}
$$

for $|c\rangle$. The geometric sum vanishes unless $\omega^{3c}=1$, which means $c\equiv0\pmod4$. For $c=0,4,8$ its magnitude is $4/(2\sqrt{12})=1/\sqrt3$. Thus the [quantum Fourier transform of a periodic coset state](../../../../../../quantum-fourier-transform-of-a-periodic-coset-state.md) gives

$$
\boxed{c\in\{0,4,8\},\qquad \mathbb P(c)=\frac13}.
$$
