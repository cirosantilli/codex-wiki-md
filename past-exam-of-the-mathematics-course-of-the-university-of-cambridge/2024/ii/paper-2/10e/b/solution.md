<h1 id="10e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The possible values are $y\in\{1,4,7\}$. Each occurs for four of the twelve equally weighted values of $x$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(y=1)=\mathbb P(y=4)=\mathbb P(y=7)=\frac13.}
$$
