<h1 id="3k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The original binary [Hamming code](../../../../../../hamming-code.md) has parameters $[7,4,3]$, so it has $2^4=16$ codewords. Its minimum distance three makes the radius-one balls about its codewords disjoint. Each ball has

$$
\binom70+\binom71=8
$$

[vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), and $16\cdot8=128=2^7$, the size of the ambient space. Equality in the [Hamming bound](../../../../../../hamming-bound.md) therefore shows that these balls partition $\mathbb F_2^7$: the code is perfect.
