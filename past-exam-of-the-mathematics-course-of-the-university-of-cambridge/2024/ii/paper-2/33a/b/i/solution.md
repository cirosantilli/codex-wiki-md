↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At $r=1$, the Jacobian at the origin is

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}
-1&1&0\\
1&-1&0\\
0&0&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $0,-2,-1$. The zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) makes the equilibrium nonhyperbolic.
