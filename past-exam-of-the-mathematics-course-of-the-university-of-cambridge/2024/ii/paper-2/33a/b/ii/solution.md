[Eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) for $0,-2,-1$ are respectively

$$
(1,1,0),\qquad(1,-1,0),\qquad(0,0,1).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{E^c=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)\},\qquad
E^s=\operatorname{span}\{(1,-1,0),(0,0,1)\},\qquad
E^u=\{0\}}.
$$
