↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

First apply $F^\dagger$, which sends $|\phi_j\rangle$ to $|j\rangle$. Then measure in the computational [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md). Because the phase has an exact $m$-bit representation, [exact quantum phase estimation](../../../../../../../exact-quantum-phase-estimation.md) returns

$$
\boxed{p_j=1}.
$$
