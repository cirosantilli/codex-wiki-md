<h1 id="15e/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The operator $F$ maps the computational orthonormal [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $\{|j\rangle\}$ to the orthonormal [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $\{|\phi_j\rangle\}$. It therefore preserves [inner products](../../../../../../../inner-product.md) and is surjective, so $F^\dagger F=FF^\dagger=I$ and $F$ is unitary.
