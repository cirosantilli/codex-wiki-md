<h1 id="15e/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The first register is

$$
\boxed{|\phi_j\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt N}
\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi ijk/N}|k\rangle.}
$$

For $j,j'$,

$$
\langle\phi_{j'}|\phi_j\rangle
=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi i(j-j')k/N}.
$$

The geometric sum is one when $j=j'$ and zero otherwise. There are $N$ such orthonormal [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md) in an $N$-dimensional space, so they form an orthonormal [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md).
