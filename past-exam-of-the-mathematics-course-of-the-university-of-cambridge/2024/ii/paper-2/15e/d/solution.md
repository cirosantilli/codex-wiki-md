<h1 id="15e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The overlap is

$$
|\langle0|-\rangle|=\frac1{\sqrt2}.
$$

The [Helstrom-Holevo bound](../../../../../../helstrom-holevo-bound.md) for two equiprobable pure states gives

$$
p_{\rm succ}^{\max}
=\frac12\left(1+\sqrt{1-|\langle0|-\rangle|^2}\right)
=\boxed{\frac12\left(1+\frac1{\sqrt2}\right)}
=\cos^2\frac\pi8.
$$

The [Breidbart basis](../../../../../../breidbart-basis.md) is the eigenbasis of the difference of the two weighted state projectors, so its decision rule is precisely the Helstrom measurement and attains this bound.
