<h1 id="13d/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For a nonzero lattice point $\omega$ and $|z|<|\omega|$,

$$
\frac1{(z-\omega)^2}-\frac1{\omega^2}
=\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}(r+1)\frac{z^r}{\omega^{r+2}}.
$$

Pairing $\omega$ with $-\omega$ cancels odd powers. There is no constant term, so $a_0=0$, and the [laurent coefficients of the Weierstrass elliptic function](../../../../../../laurent-coefficients-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) are

$$
\boxed{a_{2k}=(2k+1)
\sum_{(m,n)\ne(0,0)}
\frac1{(m\omega_1+n\omega_2)^{2k+2}},
\qquad k\geq1.}
$$
