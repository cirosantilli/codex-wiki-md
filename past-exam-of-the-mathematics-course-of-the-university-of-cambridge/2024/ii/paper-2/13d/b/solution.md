↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If an elliptic [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) has no poles, it is entire. It is bounded on the closure of a fundamental parallelogram, and periodicity transfers that bound to the whole plane. Liouville's theorem therefore makes it constant.
