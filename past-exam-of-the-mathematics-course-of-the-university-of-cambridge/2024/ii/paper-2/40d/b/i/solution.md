↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A P-wave is longitudinal. For  
$\widehat k=(\sin\theta,0,\cos\theta)$, its amplitude is

$$
\boxed{A=A_0(\sin\theta,0,\cos\theta)},
$$

parallel to the wavevector. Substitution gives $\omega=c_p\kappa$.
