<h1 id="9d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A larger $\eta$ increases the deuterium fraction at fixed [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), so the bottleneck ends at a higher [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) and earlier time. With weak decoupling unchanged, fewer free neutrons then beta-decay before nuclear reactions bind them. Since primordial helium production is neutron-limited, the [baryon-density effect on primordial helium](../../../../../../baryon-density-effect-on-primordial-helium.md) makes $Y_{\rm He}$ larger than the standard value.
