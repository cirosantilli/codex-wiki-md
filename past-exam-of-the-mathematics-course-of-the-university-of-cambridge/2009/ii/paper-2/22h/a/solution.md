<h1 id="22h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $q=p/(p-1)$. The scalar [Young inequality](../../../../../../young-s-inequality-for-products.md) $ab\leq a^p/p+b^q/q$ follows by maximizing $ab-a^p/p$ over $a\geq0$, obtaining $b^q/q$. If both norms are nonzero, apply it to $|x_j|/\|x\|_p$ and $|y_j|/\|y\|_q$, and sum over a finite initial segment. Passing to the increasing limit gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{j=1}^\infty|x_jy_j|\leq\|x\|_p\|y\|_q.}
$$

Zero norms give the trivial case. This proves [Hölder's inequality](../../../../../../holder-s-inequality.md) without assuming convergence of the product series in advance.
