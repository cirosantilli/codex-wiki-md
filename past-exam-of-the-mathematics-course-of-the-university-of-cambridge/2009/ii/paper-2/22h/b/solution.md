<h1 id="22h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply [Hölder's inequality](../../../../../../holder-s-inequality.md) to the finite sum with the second sequence $|x_j+y_j|^{p-1}$:

$$
\sum_{j=1}^N|x_j+y_j|^p
\leq(\|x\|_p+\|y\|_p)\left(\sum_{j=1}^N|x_j+y_j|^p\right)^{(p-1)/p}.
$$

Divide by the last power if the sum is nonzero. The resulting bound is independent of $N$, so passing to the limit proves both membership in $\ell^p$ and the [Minkowski inequality](../../../../../../minkowski-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{\|x+y\|_p\leq\|x\|_p+\|y\|_p.}
$$
