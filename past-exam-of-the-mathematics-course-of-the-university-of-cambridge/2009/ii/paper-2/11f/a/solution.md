The planar [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) states that every continuous map from a nonempty [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) convex subset of $\mathbb R^2$ to itself has a [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md). In particular it applies to a closed disk or a closed triangle. Convexity and compactness are essential hypotheses in this formulation.
