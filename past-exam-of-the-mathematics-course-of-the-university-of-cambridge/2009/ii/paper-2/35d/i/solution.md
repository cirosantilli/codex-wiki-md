↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Van der Waals equation](../../../../../../van-der-waals-equation.md) modifies the ideal-gas pressure in two ways. Short-range repulsion reduces the volume accessible to the particles from $V$ to $V-bN$, while attractive interactions reduce the pressure by $aN^2/V^2$. The parameters $b$ and $a$ therefore encode excluded volume and mean attraction respectively. The equation is a phenomenological approximation, not an exact microscopic equation of state.
