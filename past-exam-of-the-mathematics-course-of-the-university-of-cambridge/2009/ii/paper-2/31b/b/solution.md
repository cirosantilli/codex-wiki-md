<h1 id="31b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The exterior characteristics give $u=0$ for $x<0$ and $u=1$ for $x>t$. In the gap put $\xi=x/t$ and $u=f(\xi)$. The [Inviscid Burgers equation](../../../../../../inviscid-burgers-equation.md) becomes $(f-\xi)f'=0$. Matching the increasing states selects the [rarefaction wave](../../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md) $f(\xi)=\xi$, so

$$
\boxed{u(x,t)=\begin{cases}0,&x\leq0,\\x/t,&0<x<t,\\1,&x\geq t.\end{cases}}
$$

It is continuous and piecewise smooth at positive times and satisfies the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) condition. A discontinuous shock joining the same increasing states would be nonentropic.
