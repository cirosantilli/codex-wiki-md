<h1 id="2f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $n\ge3$, extend the restriction of the [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f$ to $[1/n,1-1/n]$ to a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f_n$ on $[0,1]$ by making it constant on each of the two remaining intervals. The [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) supplies a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P_n$ with $\|P_n-f_n\|_{\infty,[0,1]}<1/n$. Every [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $K\subset(0,1)$ is eventually contained in $[1/n,1-1/n]$, where $f_n=f$. Hence $\sup_K|P_n-f|<1/n$ eventually. This proves **uniform convergence on every compact subset**, without assuming any endpoint limits of $f$. The first two [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) can be chosen arbitrarily.
