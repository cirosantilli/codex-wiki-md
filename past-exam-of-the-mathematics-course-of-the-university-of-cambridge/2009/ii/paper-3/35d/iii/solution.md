<h1 id="35d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the classical limit the fugacity is small and $\operatorname{Li}_s(z)=z+O(z^2)$. The [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) approaches the [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md), giving

$$
N\simeq Bz\Gamma(\tfrac92)(k_BT)^{9/2},\qquad E\simeq Bz\Gamma(\tfrac{11}2)(k_BT)^{11/2}.
$$

Taking the ratio eliminates both $z$ and $B$, yielding $\boxed{E\simeq\tfrac92Nk_BT}$. The coefficient $9/2$ follows from this density of states, rather than the $3/2$ of a free three-dimensional gas.
