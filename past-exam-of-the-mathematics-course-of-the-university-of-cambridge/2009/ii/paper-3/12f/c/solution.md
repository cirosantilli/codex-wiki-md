<h1 id="12f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take $\Omega=\{|z|<1\}$, $K=\{|z|=1\}$, and $\zeta=0$. These satisfy the stated connectedness and disjointness conditions. If [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P_n$ converged uniformly to $1/z$ on $K$, convergence of the contour integrals would imply

$$
0=\lim_n\int_{|z|=1}P_n(z)\,dz=\int_{|z|=1}\frac{dz}{z}=2\pi i,
$$

a contradiction to the [Cauchy integral theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md). Thus **boundedness of this $\Omega$ permits the obstruction** excluded in part (b).
