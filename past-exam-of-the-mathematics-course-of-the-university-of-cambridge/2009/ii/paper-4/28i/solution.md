<h1 id="28i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [28I](../28i.md)

[Pontryagin maximum principle](../../../../../pontryagin-maximum-principle.md) leaves the initial [costate](../../../../../costate.md) unknown. Terminal conditions determine these unknown constants and, when terminal time is free, the time. In a maximization convention with terminal reward $\Phi$ and running cost $L$, use $H=p\cdot f-L$. A free terminal coordinate gives $p_i(T)=\partial_i\Phi$; a fixed terminal coordinate instead allows a constraint multiplier. More generally $p(T)-\nabla\Phi$ is normal to the permitted terminal manifold. The [free-terminal-time transversality condition](../../../../../free-terminal-time-transversality-condition.md) is $H(T)+\Phi_T=0$. These conditions convert the adjoint and state equations into a boundary-value problem. Reversing the [costate](../../../../../costate.md) sign gives the equivalent minimization convention.
