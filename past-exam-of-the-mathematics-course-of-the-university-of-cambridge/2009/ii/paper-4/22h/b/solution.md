A [compact operator](../../../../../../compact-operator-split.md) is a [bounded linear operator](../../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) carrying every bounded set into a relatively [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md). Equivalently, for every bounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $(x_n)$, the [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $(Tx_n)$ has a norm-convergent [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md). It is enough to require this for the [closed unit ball](../../../../../../closed-unit-ball.md), because any bounded set lies in a scalar multiple of that ball.
