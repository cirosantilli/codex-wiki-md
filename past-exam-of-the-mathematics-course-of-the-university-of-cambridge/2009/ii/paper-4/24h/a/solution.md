↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The global [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) for the induced [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_X K\,dA=2\pi\chi(X).}
$$

Here $K$ is the [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md), $dA$ the induced surface-area element, and $\chi(X)$ the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md). For a connected orientable compact surface of [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, $\chi(X)=2-2g$. For several components, sum their contributions. There is no boundary-curvature term because $X$ has no boundary.
