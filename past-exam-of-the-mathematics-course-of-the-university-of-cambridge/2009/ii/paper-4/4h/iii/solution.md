↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** A length-$d$ [linear-feedback shift register](../../../../../../linear-feedback-shift-register.md) has $2^d-1$ nonzero fills, and its first $d$ output bits recover its fill, so these fills give distinct output [sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md). If lengths satisfy $r<s$, the longer register has strictly more nonzero outputs. At least one is absent from the shorter register's output set; choosing any nonzero shorter fill then gives unequal outputs. This is the counting property of [output sets of different-length linear-feedback registers](../../../../../../output-sets-of-different-length-linear-feedback-registers.md).
