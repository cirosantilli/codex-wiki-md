**False.** Take $s_{n+1}=s_n$ and $t_{n+2}=t_{n+1}+t_n$. The only nonzero length-one fill produces constant ones. Constant ones cannot satisfy the second [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md), since $1+1=0$ in $\mathbb F_2$. Thus these [linear-feedback shift registers](../../../../../../linear-feedback-shift-register.md) have no identical outputs from nonzero fills.
