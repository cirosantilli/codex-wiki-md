<h1 id="34d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [equipartition theorem](../../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) assigns $k_BT/2$ to each of the three quadratic momentum terms, giving [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $3Nk_BT/2$. The gravitational term is linear in $z$, so it must not be counted as a quadratic degree of freedom. In the tall-box limit its distribution is proportional to $e^{-\beta mgz}$, with mean height $1/(\beta mg)$ and mean potential $k_BT$ per atom. Equivalently the generalized equipartition identity $\langle z\,\partial H/\partial z\rangle=k_BT$ follows by [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) on $[0,\infty)$, where the boundary term vanishes. **Three quadratic kinetic contributions plus one linear potential contribution give $5k_BT/2$ per atom.** At finite $L$, the integration-by-parts boundary term is precisely the correction in part (i).
