↑ **Parent:** [34D](../34d.md)

The [ergodic hypothesis](../../../../../ergodic-hypothesis.md) states that long-time averages of equilibrium observables along typical trajectories equal their ensemble averages on the accessible [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) with the same conserved quantities. It permits [statistical mechanics](../../../../../statistical-mechanics.md) to predict measured macroscopic quantities from equilibrium distributions rather than tracking a trajectory. In its microcanonical form the measure is on the constant-energy surface. It is an assumption about the relevant dynamics, not a consequence of particles being noninteracting; the calculations below use the equilibrium [canonical ensemble](../../../../../canonical-ensemble.md) at temperature $T$.
