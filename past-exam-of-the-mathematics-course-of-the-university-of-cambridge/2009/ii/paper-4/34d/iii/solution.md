<h1 id="34d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) factors between momentum and position. Normalizing the height factor gives

$$
\boxed{P(z<Z<z+dz)=\frac{\beta mg\,e^{-\beta mgz}}{1-e^{-\beta mgL}}\,dz,\qquad0<z<L.}
$$

In the tall-box limit this becomes $\boxed{\beta mg\,e^{-\beta mgz}\,dz}$ on $z\ge0$, an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with scale height $k_BT/(mg)$.
