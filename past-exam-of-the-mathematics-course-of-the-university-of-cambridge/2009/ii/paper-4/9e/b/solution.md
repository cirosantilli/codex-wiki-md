<h1 id="9e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $H=F(P,Q)$ with $P=\sum p_k^2$ and $Q=\sum q_k^2$. The [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) component $L_i=\epsilon_{ijk}q_jp_k$ has $\partial_{p_\ell}L_i=\epsilon_{ij\ell}q_j$ and $\partial_{q_\ell}L_i=\epsilon_{i\ell k}p_k$. Therefore

$$
\{H,L_i\}=2F_Q\sum_{j,\ell}\epsilon_{ij\ell}q_\ell q_j
-2F_P\sum_{\ell,k}\epsilon_{i\ell k}p_\ell p_k=\boxed{0},
$$

since a symmetric product contracts to zero with the antisymmetric [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). All three components of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) are conserved by this rotationally invariant [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md).
