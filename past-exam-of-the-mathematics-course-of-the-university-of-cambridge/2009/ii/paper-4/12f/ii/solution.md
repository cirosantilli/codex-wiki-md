<h1 id="12f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Represent the [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) by $A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3&2\\7&5\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, which has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one and [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) eight. Its distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda_\pm=4\pm\sqrt{15}$ and their product is one. A change of basis to [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) conjugates its projective action to a dilation by $\lambda_+/\lambda_-=(4+\sqrt{15})^2$ or its reciprocal. [Translation length](../../../../../../translation-length.md) is invariant under conjugacy, so part (i)'s dilation calculation gives

$$
\boxed{\ell(A)=2\log(4+\sqrt{15}).}
$$

The fixed boundary points solve $7z^2+2z-2=0$, namely $(-1\pm\sqrt{15})/7$. The [axis of a hyperbolic-space isometry](../../../../../../axis-of-a-hyperbolic-space-isometry.md) is the semicircular [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) with those endpoints in the vertical real plane.
