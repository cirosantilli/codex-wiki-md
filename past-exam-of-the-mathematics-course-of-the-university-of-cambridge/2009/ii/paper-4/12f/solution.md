<h1 id="12f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [12F](../12f.md)

A model of three-dimensional [hyperbolic space](../../../../../hyperbolic-space.md) is $\mathbb H^3=\{(z,t):z\in\mathbb C,t>0\}$ with metric $ds^2=(|dz|^2+dt^2)/t^2$, the complete [simply connected](../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) metric of curvature $-1$. The [translation length](../../../../../translation-length.md) of an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) $g$ is $\ell(g)=\inf_Pd(P,gP)$. A hyperbolic [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) has positive [translation length](../../../../../translation-length.md) attained on its invariant complete [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md), the [axis of a hyperbolic-space isometry](../../../../../axis-of-a-hyperbolic-space-isometry.md). It joins the two fixed ideal points and is translated by distance $\ell(g)$; a three-dimensional axial [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) can also rotate around it.

For the boundary map $z\mapsto kz$, the [hyperbolic extension of a Möbius transformation](../../../../../hyperbolic-extension-of-a-mobius-transformation.md) is $(z,t)\mapsto(kz,|k|t)$. Every path from $P$ to $gP$ has length at least the absolute change of $\log t$, namely $|\log|k||$, because the metric length dominates $|dt|/t$. On the vertical line $z=0$ a vertical segment attains that bound. Hence

$$
\boxed{\ell(g)=|\log|k||.}
$$

If $|k|\ne1$ the vertical line is the axis. If $|k|=1$ the map is a rotation with zero [translation length](../../../../../translation-length.md), including the identity when $k=1$; it is not a positive-length hyperbolic [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md).
