<h1 id="22h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If a [linear subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $V$ of a [normed space](../../../../../../normed-vector-space.md) contains an open ball $B(v,r)$, then $v\in V$ and subtraction gives $B(0,r)\subseteq V$. For any $x\in X$, choose a positive scalar $t$ with $\|tx\|<r$. Then $tx\in V$ and hence $x\in V$. Thus $V=X$. **Every proper linear subspace has empty interior.**
