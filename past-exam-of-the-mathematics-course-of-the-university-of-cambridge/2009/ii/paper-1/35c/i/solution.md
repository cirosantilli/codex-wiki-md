<h1 id="35c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For compactly supported variations, antisymmetry of the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) gives $\delta(-F_{ab}F^{ab}/4)=-F^{ab}\partial_a\delta A_b$. Integrate by parts in the [action](../../../../../../action.md) to obtain

$$
\delta I=\int d^4x\,[\partial_aF^{ab}-m^2A^b+\mu_0J^b],\delta A_b.
$$

Arbitrariness of the variation gives the [Proca equation](../../../../../../proca-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\partial_aF^{ab}-m^2A^b=-\mu_0J^b,}
$$

together with the unchanged identity $\partial_{[a}F_{bc]}=0$ coming from $F=dA$. The signs follow from the source and mass terms as printed.
