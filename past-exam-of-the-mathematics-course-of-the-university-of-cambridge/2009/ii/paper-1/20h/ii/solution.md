<h1 id="20h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The cubic has no rational root: its only possible integer roots are $\pm1$, and neither works. It is therefore irreducible. Let $\alpha$ be its image in $K$; it is an [algebraic integer](../../../../../../algebraic-integer.md), so $\mathbb Z[\alpha]\subseteq\mathcal O_K$. The power-basis [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) is

$$
-4\cdot2^3-27\cdot1^2=-59.
$$

The index-discriminant formula from part (i) says that the square of $[\mathcal O_K:\mathbb Z[\alpha]]$ divides 59. It must be one. Hence **$\boxed{\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\alpha]}$**, with integral basis $1,\alpha,\alpha^2$ and [field discriminant](../../../../../../field-discriminant.md) $-59$.
