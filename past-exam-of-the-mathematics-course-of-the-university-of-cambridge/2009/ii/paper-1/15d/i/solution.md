<h1 id="15d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) by $a^2$ and differentiate:

$$
\dot a^2+kc^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho a^2,\qquad
2\dot a\ddot a=\frac{8\pi G}{3}(\dot\rho a^2+2\rho a\dot a).
$$

Insert the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) to obtain

$$
2\dot a\ddot a=-\frac{8\pi G}{3}a\dot a(\rho+3P/c^2).
$$

On a nonstatic branch divide by $2a\dot a$; continuity extends the result across isolated turning points. The resulting [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\rho+3P/c^2).}
$$

An everywhere static branch needs the independent spatial [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md): the two differentiated constraints alone cannot determine the pressure when $\dot a\equiv0$. This is the qualification to deriving the acceleration equation solely by division.
