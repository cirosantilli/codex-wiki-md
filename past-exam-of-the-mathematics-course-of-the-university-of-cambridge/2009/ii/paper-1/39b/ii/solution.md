<h1 id="39b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtracting the fixed-point equation for the exact solution gives

$$
\boxed{H_\omega=I-\omega D^{-1}A.}
$$

Here $D=2I$, so the supplied sine [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$ are also [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $H_\omega$. Explicitly,

$$
\boxed{(v_k)_i=\sin\frac{ik\pi}{n+1},\qquad
\lambda_k(\omega)=1-2\omega\sin^2\frac{k\pi}{2(n+1)}
=1-\omega+\omega\cos\frac{k\pi}{n+1}.}
$$

Their distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) for nonzero $\omega$ make them an [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md); alternatively the discrete sine orthogonality gives a basis even when $\omega=0$.
