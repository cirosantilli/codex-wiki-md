<h1 id="23g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) is a quotient $r=P/Q$ of [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) with $Q\ne0$. Cancel common factors. It determines a map on the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../../riemann-sphere.md) by assigning infinity at poles and assigning at infinity the limit in the sphere. Equivalently homogenize $P,Q$ to the same degree $d=\max(\deg P,\deg Q)$ and set $f([Z:W])=[P_h(Z,W):Q_h(Z,W)]$. They have no common projective zero: finite ones would contradict coprimality, and at infinity at least one has a nonzero leading coefficient. This also proves the map is [holomorphic](../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md).

The degree of the nonconstant [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) is $d$. The degree of a [holomorphic map](../../../../../../holomorphic-map.md) of compact [Riemann surfaces](../../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md) is the number of preimages of any target point, counted with local mapping multiplicity. For target $[a:b]$, the homogeneous equation $bP_h-aQ_h=0$ is a nonzero homogeneous [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree $d$. Over $\mathbb C$ it factors into $d$ linear factors, with multiplicity, giving exactly $d$ projective zeros. Locally its zero order is precisely the map's multiplicity, since the other target-coordinate denominator is nonzero. **The two degrees are equal.**

There is a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) sending any ordered triple of distinct points to $0,1,\infty$. When all are finite one can use $T_A(z)=(z-a_1)(a_2-a_3)/[(z-a_3)(a_2-a_1)]$; the formulas for infinity are its limits. Define $T_B$ in the same way. Then $T_B^{-1}\circ T_A$ sends $A$ to $B$ and restricts to a [biholomorphism](../../../../../../biholomorphism.md) $X\setminus A\to X\setminus B$.
