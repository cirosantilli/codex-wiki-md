Here $\phi=e^{-\lambda x}$, so $\lambda=-(\log\phi)/x$ and $d\lambda/d\phi=-1/(x\phi)$. The sample [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) for $\lambda$ is $n/\lambda-T_n$, whose variance is $n/\lambda^2$. Transforming [Fisher information](../../../../../../fisher-information-matrix.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I_n(\phi)=\frac{n}{\lambda^2}\left(\frac1{x\phi}\right)^2
=\frac{n}{\phi^2(\log\phi)^2}.}
$$

For one observation the information is the same expression without $n$.
