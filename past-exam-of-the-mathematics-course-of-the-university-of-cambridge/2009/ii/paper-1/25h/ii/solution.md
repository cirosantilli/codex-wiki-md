A [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) $y$ of a [smooth map](../../../../../../smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $f:X\to Y$ is one for which the [differential](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) $df_x:T_xX\to T_yY$ is surjective at every $x\in f^{-1}(y)$. A value outside the image is regular vacuously. The condition concerns all preimages, not just one.
