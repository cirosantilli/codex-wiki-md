<h1 id="1g/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Let $r=\dim\operatorname{im}T$. Since the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and its [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) agree, the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $\dim V=r+r$. Thus **$\dim V=2r$ is even**. Every $Tv$ lies in $\ker T$, so $T^2=0$. The [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) therefore divides $X^2$. It cannot be $1$, and it cannot be $X$ since $T\ne0$; hence **the minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $X^2$**.
