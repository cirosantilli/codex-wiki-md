<h1 id="17d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The upstream flow is [supercritical flow](../../../../../../supercritical-flow.md), with $h_1<h_c$. Now $dD/dh>0$, so the depth increases over the rising hump and the velocity decreases, while the free surface rises. Since $D_{\max}<D^*$, the depth stays below $h_c$. On the descending bed it decreases back to **$h_1$ downstream**, remaining supercritical throughout.
