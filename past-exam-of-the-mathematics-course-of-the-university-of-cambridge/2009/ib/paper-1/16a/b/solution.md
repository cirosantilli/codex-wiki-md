<h1 id="16a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The charge and its proposed [image charge](../../../../../../image-charge.md) give the exterior [electric potential](../../../../../../electric-potential.md)

$$
\Phi(r,z)=\frac q{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\left(\frac1{\sqrt{r^2+(z-d)^2}}-\frac1{\sqrt{r^2+(z+d)^2}}\right).
$$

At $z=0$ the terms cancel, so $\Phi=0$ everywhere on the plane and its tangential [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanish. Thus **the image construction satisfies the earthed-conductor boundary conditions**. It has the correct point-charge singularity, is harmonic elsewhere in $z>0$, and decays at infinity; electrostatic uniqueness identifies it with the exterior solution.
