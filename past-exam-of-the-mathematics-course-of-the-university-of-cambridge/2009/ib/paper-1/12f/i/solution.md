<h1 id="12f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $(x_n,f(x_n))$ converges to $(x,y)$. [Continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) gives $f(x_n)\to f(x)$, and uniqueness of limits in a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) gives $y=f(x)$. Hence every convergent sequence in the graph has its limit in the graph, proving **$\Gamma_f$ is closed**.
