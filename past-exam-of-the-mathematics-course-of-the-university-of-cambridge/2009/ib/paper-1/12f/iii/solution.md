<h1 id="12f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take **$f(0)=0$ and $f(x)=1/x$ for $x\ne0$**. Its graph is $\{(x,y):xy=1\}\cup\{(0,0)\}$, a union of two [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md), since multiplication is continuous. But $f(1/n)=n$ does not tend to $f(0)$, so the function is not continuous.
