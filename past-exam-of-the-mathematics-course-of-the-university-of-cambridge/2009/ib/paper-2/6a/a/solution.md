<h1 id="6a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The uniform [charge density](../../../../../../charge-density.md) is $\rho_e=3Q/(4\pi R^3)$. Spherical symmetry makes the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) radial and constant in magnitude on each concentric sphere. A sphere of radius $r<R$ encloses charge $Q(r/R)^3$. [Gauss's law](../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) therefore gives $4\pi r^2E_r=Qr^3/(\varepsilon_0R^3)$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E(\mathbf r)=\frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0R^3}\mathbf r,\qquad |\mathbf r|<R.}
$$

This also defines the zero [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) at the centre by continuity.
