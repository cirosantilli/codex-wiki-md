<h1 id="20h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $i\notin B$, condition on the first step and use the [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md). The [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md) obeys

$$
h_i=\sum_jp_{ij}h_j.
$$

For $i\in B$, the [hitting time](../../../../../../first-passage-time.md) is zero, so $h_i=1$. Since $0\le h_j\le1$, $0\le(Ph)_i\le1=h_i$ there. Hence **$h\ge Ph\ge0$ everywhere**, as required. This makes $h$ a [nonnegative superharmonic function for a Markov chain](../../../../../../nonnegative-superharmonic-function-for-a-markov-chain.md), with an inequality rather than an equality on the boundary.
