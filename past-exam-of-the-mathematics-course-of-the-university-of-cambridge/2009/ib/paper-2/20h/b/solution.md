<h1 id="20h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Starting at any state already in $B$ makes the [hitting time](../../../../../../first-passage-time.md) $\tau=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{h_i=1\qquad(i\in B).}
$$

Together with part (a), this verifies both boundary and superharmonic conditions for the [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md).
