<h1 id="17a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) in the annular region is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S=\frac1{\mu_0}\mathbf E\times\mathbf B
=\frac{VI}{2\pi r^2\ln(b/a)}\mathbf e_z.}
$$

It points from the battery towards the resistor with the preceding current convention. Integrating through an annular cross-section gives

$$
\mathcal P=\int_a^bS_z\,2\pi r\,dr
=\frac{VI}{\ln(b/a)}\int_a^b\frac{dr}{r}
=\boxed{VI=V^2/R.}
$$

The energy is transported by the electromagnetic field in the space between the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) conductors; the integration recovers the power dissipated at the terminating resistor.
