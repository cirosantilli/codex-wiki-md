<h1 id="17a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose $+z$ along the current in the inner conductor. Cylindrical symmetry and [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) give $2\pi r B_\phi=\mu_0I$ for $a<r<b$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}\mathbf e_\phi,\qquad I=V/R.}
$$

The opposite current on the outer shell makes the net enclosed current zero outside both conductors; the long-cylinder approximation neglects end fields.
