<h1 id="5d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The integrand has simple [poles](../../../../../../pole.md) at $z=\pm1/2$, both inside the positively oriented unit circle. At either [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) $z_0$, the [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) is $z_0/(-8z_0)=-1/8$. Their sum is $-1/4$, so the [residue theorem](../../../../../../residue-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\frac{z\,dz}{1-4z^2}=2\pi i\left(-\frac14\right)=-\frac{\pi i}{2}.}
$$
