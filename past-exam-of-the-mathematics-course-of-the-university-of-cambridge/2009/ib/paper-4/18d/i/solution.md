<h1 id="18d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For small irrotational [surface gravity waves](../../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md), write the [velocity field](../../../../../../velocity-field.md) as $\nabla\phi$. [Incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) gives $\nabla^2\phi=0$. The rigid walls have $\phi_x=0$ at $x=0,a$, $\phi_y=0$ at $y=0,b$, and $\phi_z=0$ at $z=-h$. At $z=0$, the linear [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) and pressure condition are

$$
\eta_t=\phi_z,\qquad \phi_t+g\eta=0.
$$

Separate variables for a [rectangular standing surface-gravity mode](../../../../../../rectangular-standing-surface-gravity-mode.md):

$$
\phi=B(t)\cos\frac{m\pi x}{a}\cos\frac{n\pi y}{b}
\frac{\cosh k(z+h)}{\cosh kh},\qquad
k^2=\pi^2\left(\frac{m^2}{a^2}+\frac{n^2}{b^2}\right).
$$

The horizontal cosine factors and vertical hyperbolic cosine satisfy all rigid-wall conditions. Put $\eta=A(t)\cos(m\pi x/a)\cos(n\pi y/b)$ and $\lambda=k\tanh kh$. The free-surface conditions give $\dot A=\lambda B$ and $\dot B=-gA$, hence

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=gk\tanh kh.}
$$

Both integers are nonnegative and not both zero. The zero pair would be a spatially uniform displacement, excluded by fixed fluid volume; a spatially constant potential is only a gauge mode.
