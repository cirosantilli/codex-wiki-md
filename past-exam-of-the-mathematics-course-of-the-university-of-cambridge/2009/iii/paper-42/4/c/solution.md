<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For two independent left-handed [Weyl spinors](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) $\chi$ and $\eta$, a [Dirac mass term](../../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_D=-m\chi^\alpha\eta_\alpha-m^*\bar\chi_{\dot\alpha}\bar\eta^{\dot\alpha}.}
$$

The four-component [Dirac field](../../../../../../dirac-field.md) $\Psi_D=(\chi_\alpha,\bar\eta^{\dot\alpha})^T$ has independent particle and antiparticle degrees of freedom; after a phase redefinition making $m$ real, the same term is $-m\bar\Psi_D\Psi_D$. The two left-handed spinors can carry opposite charges under a conserved [U(1)](../../../../../../circle-group.md) symmetry, $\chi\mapsto e^{iq\omega}\chi$, $\eta\mapsto e^{-iq\omega}\eta$. A gauge-invariant [Dirac mass term](../../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) contracts conjugate [gauge group representations](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md).

For one left-handed [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md), a [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) instead has the form

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_M=-\frac12\left(m\chi^\alpha\chi_\alpha+m^*\bar\chi_{\dot\alpha}\bar\chi^{\dot\alpha}\right).}
$$

Its self-contraction need not vanish, by the [Weyl spinor bilinear exchange identity](../../../../../../weyl-spinor-bilinear-exchange-identity.md). The factor $1/2$ avoids double counting identical fields in variation. The associated four-component [Majorana fermion](../../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) $\Psi_M=(\chi_\alpha,\bar\chi^{\dot\alpha})^T$ satisfies $\Psi_M^c=\Psi_M$, so its particle is its own antiparticle. A [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) breaks continuous fermion-number phase symmetry for $\chi$, and is allowed only if its gauge indices admit an invariant symmetric contraction, as for a neutral field or a real [gauge group representation](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md). An isolated field of nonzero [U(1)](../../../../../../circle-group.md) charge cannot have it. More generally, the mass matrix of several left-handed [Weyl spinors](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) is symmetric, since $\chi_i\chi_j=\chi_j\chi_i$.
