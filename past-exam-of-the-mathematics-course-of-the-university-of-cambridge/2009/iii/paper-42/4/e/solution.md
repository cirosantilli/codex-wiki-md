<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Here [L-superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) means a left-[chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md): $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Phi=0$, and its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) is an [antichiral superfield](../../../../../../antichiral-superfield.md), $D_\alpha\Phi^*=0$. Interpret the two barred derivatives as the contracted scalar $\bar D^2=\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\bar D^{\dot\alpha}$. The key point is [chiral projection by squared supercovariant derivatives](../../../../../../chiral-projection-by-squared-supercovariant-derivatives.md). Since the two components of $\bar D$ anticommute, any product of three barred derivatives repeats a component and is zero. Thus

$$
\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\bar D^2X=0
$$

for every [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md) $X$, even when $X$ is antichiral. In particular, $\bar D^2\Phi^*$ is chiral.

The right side is chiral too. Because $\Phi$ is even, the [graded derivation](../../../../../../graded-derivation.md) rule gives $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}(\Phi^2)=(\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Phi)\Phi+\Phi(\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Phi)=0$. Constant $m$ and $\lambda$ do not contribute derivatives. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\bar D_{\dot\alpha}(\bar D^2\Phi^*)=\bar D_{\dot\alpha}(4m\Phi+4\lambda\Phi^2)=0,}
$$

so applying the chiral constraint to the equation imposes no additional incompatible condition. This is the [holomorphic closure of chiral superfields](../../../../../../holomorphic-closure-of-chiral-superfields.md); a generic nonholomorphic right side would not satisfy it.

Both sides have zero [Grassmann parity](../../../../../../grassmann-parity.md) and are [Lorentz scalars](../../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md): the two dotted derivative indices are contracted with the invariant epsilon tensor, and $\Phi$ is a scalar [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md). Moreover, each covariant derivative anticommutes with every [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md), so its squared contraction commutes with the [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md). Hence the equation transforms covariantly under [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md). It is the chiral field [equation of motion](../../../../../../equation-of-motion.md) of the [Wess–Zumino model](../../../../../../wess-zumino-model.md) with [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) $\mathcal W(\Phi)=m\Phi^2/2+\lambda\Phi^3/3$, for which $\mathcal W'=m\Phi+\lambda\Phi^2$, using the normalization in the PDF. With [mass dimensions](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $[\Phi]=1$, $[\bar D]=1/2$, $[m]=1$ and $[\lambda]=0$, each side has dimension two. No component field [equations of motion](../../../../../../equation-of-motion.md) are needed for this consistency argument.
