<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A two-component [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) is a complex spinor transforming in one of the two inequivalent [group representations](../../../../../../group-representation.md) $(1/2,0)$ and $(0,1/2)$ of the [Lorentz spinor double cover](../../../../../../lorentz-spinor-double-cover.md) $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)$. An undotted left-handed field $\chi_\alpha$ transforms by a matrix $M\in\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)$; its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $\bar\chi_{\dot\alpha}$ transforms by $M^*$. Dotted and undotted indices distinguish the two [chirality](../../../../../../chirality-physics.md) representations. Raising a dotted index uses the invariant epsilon tensor and converts the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) transformation into the equivalent inverse-adjoint form. Rotations act by the same [SU(2)](../../../../../../su-2-group.md) generators on the two chiralities, while boosts have opposite generators, so the two complex representations cannot be identified by a complex-linear [Lorentz invariant](../../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) map.

The antisymmetric [invariant tensors](../../../../../../invariant-tensor.md) $\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}$ and $\epsilon_{\dot\alpha\dot\beta}$ raise and lower spinor indices; $M^T\epsilon M=\epsilon$ proves invariance of the contracted bilinear $\chi\psi=\chi^\alpha\psi_\alpha$. The components of a fermionic [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) are odd [Grassmann variables](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md), so this bilinear is symmetric under exchange of the two spinors: the antisymmetric epsilon sign cancels the Grassmann sign. In particular, choosing $\epsilon_{12}=1$ and $\epsilon^{12}=-1$ gives $\chi\chi=2\chi_1\chi_2$, generally nonzero. This [Weyl spinor bilinear exchange identity](../../../../../../weyl-spinor-bilinear-exchange-identity.md) permits a [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) for a single suitable [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md). With mostly-minus [metric signature](../../../../../../metric-signature.md) and $\bar\sigma^\mu=(I,-\boldsymbol\sigma)$, the free massless kinetic term is $i\chi^\dagger\bar\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu\chi$, and its field [equation of motion](../../../../../../equation-of-motion.md) is $\boxed{\bar\sigma^\mu\partial_\mu\chi=0}$.
