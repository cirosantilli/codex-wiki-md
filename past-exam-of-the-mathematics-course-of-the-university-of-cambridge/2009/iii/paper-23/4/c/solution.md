<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By part (b), $i:P\to H_B$ is the [equaliser](../../../../../../equaliser.md) of $ip$ and $1_{H_B}$. Full faithfulness of the [Yoneda embedding](../../../../../../yoneda-embedding.md) writes $ip=H_b$ for a unique [endomorphism](../../../../../../endomorphism.md) $b:B\to B$. Choose an [equaliser](../../../../../../equaliser.md) $e:A\to B$ of $b$ and $1_B$ in $\mathcal C$.

For each $c$, the maps $c\to A$ are precisely the maps $u:c\to B$ with $bu=u$. Thus $H_e:H_A\to H_B$ is the pointwise [equaliser](../../../../../../equaliser.md) of $H_b$ and $1_{H_B}$, hence their [equaliser](../../../../../../equaliser.md) in the [presheaf category](../../../../../../presheaf-category.md). Both $i$ and $H_e$ have the same [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md), so their vertices are isomorphic. This proves the [representable retract criterion](../../../../../../representable-retract-criterion.md):

$$
\boxed{P\cong H_A.}
$$
