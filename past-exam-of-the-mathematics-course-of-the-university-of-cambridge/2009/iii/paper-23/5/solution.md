↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

A [poset](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) becomes a [category](../../../../../category-split.md) whose objects are its elements and whose [hom-set](../../../../../hom-set.md) $X(x,y)$ is a singleton if $x\le y$ and empty otherwise. Reflexivity supplies identity arrows and transitivity supplies composition; all possible composites are unique, so the [category](../../../../../category-split.md) axioms hold. Antisymmetry says that isomorphic objects are equal. A [functor](../../../../../functor.md) between such [categories](../../../../../category-split.md) is exactly an [order-preserving function](../../../../../order-preserving-function.md): an inequality must be sent to an inequality, and there is no further choice of arrow map.
