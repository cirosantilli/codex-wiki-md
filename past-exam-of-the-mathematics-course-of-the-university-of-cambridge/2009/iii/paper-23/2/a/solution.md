<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take any limiting cone $\ell_i:L\to D(i)$ in $\mathcal C$ of the given shape. Since $GF$ preserves such [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md), the cone $GF\ell_i:GFL\to GFD(i)$ is limiting in $\mathcal E$. It is the image under $G$ of the cone $F\ell_i$ in $\mathcal D$. Reflection by $G$ therefore says that $F\ell_i$ is limiting in $\mathcal D$. Since this works for every diagram and every limiting cone of the specified shape, **$F$ preserves [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of that shape**.
