↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Specify the expected control-group event probability at the chosen follow-up time, the intervention probability or smallest clinically important absolute reduction to detect, the primary test's [significance level](../../../../../../significance-level.md) and whether it is one- or two-sided, the desired [statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md), and the allocation ratio. [Statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md) is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of rejecting the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) under that specified alternative; its complement is the [Type II error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) probability.

For two independent binary-outcome groups, these probabilities determine the response [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) needed by the sample-size calculation. Also specify how much loss to follow-up is anticipated when converting the required evaluable sample into a recruitment target. Here the design is balanced and the stated primary [significance level](../../../../../../significance-level.md) is $0.05$. A target [statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md) such as $80\%$ or $90\%$ and the anticipated event [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) cannot be recovered from the [sample size](../../../../../../sample-size.md) alone, so they should not be invented. The recruitment inflation allows for attrition rather than changing the target effect.
