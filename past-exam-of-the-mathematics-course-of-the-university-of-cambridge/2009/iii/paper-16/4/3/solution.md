<h1 id="4/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Take the **[Möbius line bundle](../../../../../../mobius-line-bundle.md) over $S^1$**,

$$
L=([0,1]\times\mathbb R)/((1,t)\sim(0,-t)).
$$

A continuous [section of a vector bundle](../../../../../../section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) corresponds to a continuous real-valued function $s:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ with $s(1)=-s(0)$. If $s(0)=0$, it already vanishes. Otherwise its endpoint values have opposite signs, so the [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) gives $s(t_0)=0$ for some $t_0\in(0,1)$. Thus **this vector bundle has no nowhere-zero section**. Every continuous section must vanish.
