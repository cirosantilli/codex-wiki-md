<h1 id="4/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Take **$\mathbb{CP}^3$**. It is a closed [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) and is [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md): its [CW complex](../../../../../../cw-complex.md) structure has no one-cells, and attaching cells of dimension at least two to a point cannot create a [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md). Its [cohomology ring](../../../../../../cohomology-ring.md) is $\mathbb Z[x]/(x^4)$ with $|x|=2$, so $x^3\ne0$.

The theorem used is the [Lusternik–Schnirelmann critical point theorem](../../../../../../lusternik-schnirelmann-critical-point-theorem.md): every smooth real-valued function on a closed connected [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) has at least its unnormalized [Lusternik–Schnirelmann category](../../../../../../lusternik-schnirelmann-category.md) many distinct [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md), without a nondegeneracy assumption. Here category is the minimum number of open subsets covering the manifold whose inclusions are [null-homotopic](../../../../../../null-homotopic-map.md). The [cup length](../../../../../../cup-length.md) bound gives category at least four. To justify that bound, a cover by three categorical sets would make each positive-degree class vanish on its assigned set. The [long exact sequence](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence.md) of each pair lifts three copies of $x$ to relative classes, and their [relative cup product](../../../../../../relative-cup-product.md) would lie in $H^6(M,M;\mathbb Z)=0$, contradicting $x^3\ne0$. Thus **every smooth function has at least four critical points**, including functions with degenerate critical points. The critical-point theorem in this formulation is described in [Conley pairs in geometry—Lusternik–Schnirelmann theory and more](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exmath.2017.10.003).
