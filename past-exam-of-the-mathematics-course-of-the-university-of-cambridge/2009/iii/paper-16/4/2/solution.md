<h1 id="4/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Under the intended convention that the finite group is nontrivial, take **$\mathbb{CP}^2$**. It is [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) by its [CW complex](../../../../../../cw-complex.md) structure. The [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md) says that a continuous self-map of a compact triangulable space with nonzero rational [Lefschetz number](../../../../../../lefschetz-number.md) has a fixed point.

For any continuous $f:\mathbb{CP}^2\to\mathbb{CP}^2$, write $f^*x=dx$ for the integral degree-two generator, with $d\in\mathbb Z$. Preservation of the [cup product](../../../../../../cup-product.md) gives $f^*(x^2)=d^2x^2$. Only degrees zero, two and four contribute to the rational [Lefschetz number](../../../../../../lefschetz-number.md), so

$$
L(f)=1+d+d^2=\left(d+\frac12\right)^2+\frac34>0.
$$

Consequently every such map has a fixed point. In a [free group action](../../../../../../free-group-action.md), each nonidentity group element must act by a fixed-point-free homeomorphism. This is impossible here, so no nontrivial [finite group](../../../../../../finite-group.md) can act freely. This is the [fixed-point property of even-dimensional complex projective space](../../../../../../fixed-point-property-of-even-dimensional-complex-projective-space.md) for complex dimension two. **If the trivial group is included, the literal request has no example**, since the trivial group acts freely on every space.
