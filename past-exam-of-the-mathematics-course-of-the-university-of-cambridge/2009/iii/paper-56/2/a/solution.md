↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [asymptotically flat spacetime](../../../../../../asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md) is a [stationary spacetime](../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md) if it admits a complete stationary [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $K$, with $\mathcal L_Kg=0$, timelike near infinity and normalized there to a unit time translation. It need not remain timelike in an [ergoregion of a stationary spacetime](../../../../../../ergoregion-of-a-stationary-spacetime.md). It is axisymmetric if it admits an axial [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $\Phi$, whose flow is a rotational $U(1)$ action: its spacelike orbits away from the axis are closed with period $2\pi$, and it vanishes on the regular rotation axis. For a [stationary axisymmetric spacetime](../../../../../../stationary-axisymmetric-spacetime.md), the generators can be chosen to commute, $[K,\Phi]=0$. These are geometric symmetries, not merely time-independent or angle-independent coordinate labels.
