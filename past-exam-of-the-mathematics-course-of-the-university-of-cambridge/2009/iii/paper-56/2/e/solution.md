<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $0<M<a$, the ring is a [naked singularity](../../../../../../naked-singularity.md) and there is no event horizon shielding the extension's [closed timelike curves](../../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md). The [weak cosmic censorship conjecture](../../../../../../weak-cosmic-censorship-conjecture.md) predicts that such superextreme Kerr geometries are not formed by generic collapse of regular isolated initial data satisfying the appropriate energy conditions. This is a conjectural physical restriction, not a proof from the exact metric alone.

For $M>a>0$, the [closed timelike curves](../../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md) lie deep inside the ideal analytic extension, beyond the inner [Cauchy horizon](../../../../../../cauchy-horizon.md), so weak cosmic censorship does not itself exclude them. However, that inner horizon is unstable to perturbations, with [mass inflation](../../../../../../mass-inflation.md) expected in realistic collapse. The [strong cosmic censorship conjecture](../../../../../../strong-cosmic-censorship-conjecture.md) is the relevant obstruction to a regular continuation into the ideal chronology-violating region. Thus the exact Kerr extension exhibits such curves mathematically, but neither regular collapse nor the uniqueness theorem establishes that they occur in Nature.
