↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the radial null pair above, the [screen-space projector](../../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md) selects the angular tangent plane, with metric $q_{AB}=r^2\gamma_{AB}$. The ingoing covector is $U_a=-E(dv)_a$, and $\Gamma^v{}_{AB}=-r\gamma_{AB}$. Hence the [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) is

$$
\widehat B_{AB}=\nabla_BU_A=-Er\gamma_{AB}=-\frac Erq_{AB}.
$$

It is purely symmetric and proportional to the screen metric, giving

$$
\boxed{\theta=-\frac{2E}{r},\qquad\widehat\sigma_{ab}=0,\qquad\widehat\omega_{ab}=0.}
$$

For $E=1$, the [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) is $-2/r$. The area check $A\propto r^2$ and $dr/d\lambda=-E$ gives $A'/A=-2E/r$, in agreement with the [spherical null expansion in ingoing coordinates](../../../../../../../spherical-null-expansion-in-ingoing-coordinates.md). In vacuum, $d\theta/d\lambda=-2E^2/r^2=-\theta^2/2$ also satisfies the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md); the ingoing beam focuses at the singularity rather than remaining future complete.
