<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A single [rarefaction wave](../../../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md) enforces two matching equalities: equal [specific entropy](../../../../../../../specific-entropy.md) and one common [Riemann invariant](../../../../../../../riemann-invariant.md). The inequality $u_R>u_L$ does not enforce either. If the entropies differ, one smooth isentropic fan cannot connect them; even if they agree, arbitrary end velocities and pressures need not satisfy either acoustic-family invariant.

The general positive-gas [Riemann problem](../../../../../../../riemann-problem.md) has a left acoustic wave, a middle [contact discontinuity](../../../../../../../contact-discontinuity.md), and a right acoustic wave. Across the contact the two intermediate states have the same [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) $u_*$ and [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) $p_*$, but can have different [mass densities](../../../../../../../density.md) and entropies. Each acoustic wave is a [rarefaction wave](../../../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md) or a [shock wave](../../../../../../../shock-wave.md), depending on the required intermediate [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) relative to its adjacent initial state. Shocks satisfy the conservative jump conditions and increase [entropy](../../../../../../../entropy.md). A sufficiently large [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) contrast can produce a shock on one side even when the initial velocities obey $u_R>u_L$.

For sufficiently rapid separation, the two rarefactions instead exhaust the gas and create vacuum between them. Their vacuum-edge velocities are $u_L+2c_L/(\gamma-1)$ and $u_R-2c_R/(\gamma-1)$, so a finite vacuum gap occurs when

$$
\boxed{u_R-u_L>\frac{2(c_L+c_R)}{\gamma-1}.}
$$

Equality is the limiting case in which the vacuum edges meet. Thus the additional features can be two acoustic waves, a contact carrying an [entropy](../../../../../../../entropy.md) jump, shocks, or a vacuum region, rather than one fan joining the two original uniform states.
