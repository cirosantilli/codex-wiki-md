<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Decompose $\mathbf u=\mathbf u_p+u_\phi\mathbf e_\phi$ and $\mathbf B=\mathbf B_p+B_\phi\mathbf e_\phi$. In a steady [axisymmetric vector field](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md), $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$ and $\mathbf E=-\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$. The radial and vertical components of its [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md) are

$$
-\partial_zE_\phi=0,\qquad R^{-1}\partial_R(RE_\phi)=0.
$$

Therefore $RE_\phi=C$ is a constant. Regularity at the symmetry axis sets $C=0$; equivalently, one imposes no toroidal loop [electromotive force](../../../../../../../electromotive-force.md), $\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l=2\pi C=0$. Under this usual condition,

$$
0=E_\phi=-(\mathbf u_p\times\mathbf B_p)_\phi,
$$

which implies

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u_p=\alpha\mathbf B_p}
$$

where $\mathbf B_p\ne0$. This is the [regular-axis alignment of steady poloidal ideal flow](../../../../../../../regular-axis-alignment-of-steady-poloidal-ideal-flow.md). It allows nonzero toroidal components as well.

The axis or zero-loop-voltage condition is necessary for the unqualified conclusion. On an annular domain, $\mathbf B=(B_0/R)\mathbf e_R$ and $\mathbf u=U_0\mathbf e_z$ give $\mathbf E=-(U_0B_0/R)\mathbf e_\phi$ with zero curl, yet the poloidal vectors are perpendicular. This can satisfy the remaining steady equations as well: choose constant [mass density](../../../../../../../density.md) $\rho_0$, $\Phi=\pi G\rho_0R^2$ and $p=p_0-\pi G\rho_0^2R^2>0$ on the annulus. The field has zero [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md), radial [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) balances gravity, the flow advects no equilibrium quantity, and $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho_0$. It carries a nonzero loop voltage. Steady axisymmetry alone on an annulus therefore does not exclude such cross-field flow.
