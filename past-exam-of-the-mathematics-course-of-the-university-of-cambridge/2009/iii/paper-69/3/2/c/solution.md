<h1 id="3/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Combine the [B-spline interpolation operator norm](../../../../../../../b-spline-interpolation-operator-norm.md) estimate from part 1 with the inverse [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md) computed in part 2(b):

$$
\boxed{\|P_x\|_{L^\infty\to L^\infty}
\le\|A_x^{-1}\|_{\ell^\infty\to\ell^\infty}<2,}
$$

so in particular $\|P_x\|_{L^\infty}\le2$, uniformly in $n$. The [subpartition of unity for B-splines](../../../../../../../subpartition-of-unity-for-b-splines.md) is important at the outer boundary: the finite basis may sum to less than one there, but that only strengthens the estimate.
