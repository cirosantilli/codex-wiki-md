<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [powerfully embedded subgroup](../../../../../../powerfully-embedded-subgroup.md) $N$ is itself a [powerful p-group](../../../../../../powerful-p-group.md), so the finite [Frattini subgroup](../../../../../../frattini-subgroup.md) formula gives $\Phi(N)=N^p$. [Conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) by $G$ acts trivially on $N/N^p$, because $[N,G]\leq N^p$. Since $N$ is the [normal closure](../../../../../../normal-closure.md) of $X$, the image of $X$ therefore generates $N/N^p$: all conjugates have the same images as the original elements. The [Burnside basis theorem](../../../../../../burnside-basis-theorem.md) now gives **$N=\langle X\rangle$**. Equivalently, a proper generated [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) would lie in a maximal [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of $N$, which contains $N^p$ and contradicts generation of that quotient.
