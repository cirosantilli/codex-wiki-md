<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $H$ is an open [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of a [topological group](../../../../../../topological-group-split.md), each [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) $gH$ is open because translation is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). Distinct [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) partition the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md), so

$$
G\setminus H=\bigcup_{gH\ne H}gH
$$

is open. **Every open subgroup is closed.**
