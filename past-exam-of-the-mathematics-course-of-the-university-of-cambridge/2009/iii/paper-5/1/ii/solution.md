<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) of an open [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ form an open cover of the [compact space](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $G$. A finite subcover exists. Since distinct [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) are disjoint, that subcover must contain every [coset](../../../../../../coset.md). Thus **$[G:H]$ is finite**. Neither normality nor Hausdorffness is needed for this argument.
