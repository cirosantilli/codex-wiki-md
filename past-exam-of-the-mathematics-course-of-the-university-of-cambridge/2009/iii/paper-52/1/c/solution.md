<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [reachable set](../../../../../../reachable-set.md) consists of states obtainable from one specified initial state using admissible inputs and allowed durations. [Controllability](../../../../../../controllability.md) means that every target in the declared state space is reachable from every initial state there; the declared space and time convention are essential.

For a closed quantum system, let $U_f$ solve the controlled [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) with $U_f(0)=I$. The propagator and state reachable sets are

$$
\mathcal R_U=\{U_f(T):f\text{ admissible},\ T\text{ allowed}\},\qquad
\mathcal R_\rho(\rho_0)=\{U\rho_0U^\dagger:U\in\mathcal R_U\}.
$$

A pure-state version acts on normalized vectors up to global phase. [Pure-state controllability](../../../../../../pure-state-controllability.md) makes every pure target accessible; [unitary operator controllability](../../../../../../unitary-operator-controllability.md) permits every desired coherent process, with global phase treated according to the definition.

For [mixed states](../../../../../../mixed-state.md), [density operator controllability](../../../../../../density-operator-controllability.md) means transitivity on each [unitary orbit of a density operator](../../../../../../unitary-orbit-of-a-density-operator.md), not access to all [density operators](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) regardless of [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md). The [Dynamical Lie algebra](../../../../../../dynamical-lie-algebra.md) generated by $-iH_0,-iH_1,\ldots$ describes the accessible connected unitary transformations under the usual unconstrained-time control assumptions. Conserved quantities or restricted controls can shrink the [reachable set](../../../../../../reachable-set.md). Thus successful preparation of one state is weaker than implementation of an arbitrary gate on all inputs, and coherent [controllability](../../../../../../controllability.md) does not imply purification.
