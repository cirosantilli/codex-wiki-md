<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A constant [improper prior](../../../../../../improper-prior.md) would give the formal [posterior density](../../../../../../posterior-density.md) kernel $N^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{N\geq y\}}$. However,

$$
\sum_{N=y}^{\infty}\frac1N=\infty.
$$

**There is no normalized posterior distribution under this prior.** The failure is not simply that the [prior](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) is improper: some improper priors give proper posteriors. Here [posterior propriety](../../../../../../posterior-propriety.md) itself fails after this single observation, so probabilities, quantiles and a [posterior mean](../../../../../../posterior-mean.md) cannot be assigned to that kernel.
