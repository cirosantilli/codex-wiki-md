<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The assertion requires a proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md). If $I=A$, no proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) can contain it, so the literal statement without that restriction is false.

Let $I$ be proper, and consider the partially ordered collection of proper [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md) containing $I$. It is nonempty. The union of a chain is an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md), because any two of its elements belong together to some member of the chain. It is still proper: if it contained $1$, some member of the chain would contain $1$ and equal $A$. Thus every chain has an upper bound in the collection. The [Zorn lemma](../../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md) gives a maximal member, and any proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) containing it still belongs to the collection. Hence this is a [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) of $A$.

Therefore **every proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is contained in a maximal proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md)**. This algebraic argument only needs a nonzero unital commutative [ring](../../../../../../ring.md); the [Banach algebra](../../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md) assumptions become important for closedness of [maximal ideals](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) in part (c).
