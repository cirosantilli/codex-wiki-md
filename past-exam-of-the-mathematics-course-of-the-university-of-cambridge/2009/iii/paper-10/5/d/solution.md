<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every [character of an algebra](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) $\phi$, the element $a-\phi(a)1$ cannot be invertible, so $\phi(a)\in\sigma_A(a)$. Conversely, if $\lambda\in\sigma_A(a)$, the principal [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $A(a-\lambda1)$ is proper: otherwise $b(a-\lambda1)=1$ for some $b$, which in this commutative [algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md) gives an inverse. Part (b) puts that [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) inside a [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) $M$, and the corresponding [algebra character](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) from part (c) has $\phi(a)=\lambda$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sigma_A(a)=\{\phi(a):\phi\in\Phi_A\}=\widehat a(\Phi_A).}
$$

Here $\widehat a(\phi)=\phi(a)$ is the [Gelfand transform](../../../../../../gelfand-representation.md). The [spectrum equals character values in a commutative Banach algebra](../../../../../../spectrum-equals-character-values-in-a-commutative-banach-algebra.md) result does not require the [Gelfand transform](../../../../../../gelfand-representation.md) to be injective: the [algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md) need not be semisimple.
