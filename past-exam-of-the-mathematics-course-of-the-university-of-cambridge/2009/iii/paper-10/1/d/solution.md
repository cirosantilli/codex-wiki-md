<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The dominated real form of the [Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../../../../hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends the zero [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) on $\{0\}$ to a [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) $l:E\to\mathbb R$ with $l(y)\leq p(y)$ everywhere. If $x+z\in U$, [convexity](../../../../../../convex-function.md) of the ball puts the whole segment in $U$. Its increasing secant slopes give

$$
p(z)=\lim_{t\downarrow0}\frac{f(x+tz)-f(x)}t\leq f(x+z)-f(x).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x+z)\geq f(x)+l(z)\qquad(x+z\in U).}
$$

This constructs a supporting [affine minorant](../../../../../../affine-minorant.md) at $x$. Part (e) shows that it is a [continuous supporting functional for a locally bounded convex function](../../../../../../continuous-supporting-functional-for-a-locally-bounded-convex-function.md).
