↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The bound in part (b) and domination $l\leq p$ give $l(y)\leq L\|y\|$. Applying the same bound to $-y$, and using linearity, gives $-l(y)=l(-y)\leq L\|y\|$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{|l(y)|\leq L\|y\|,\qquad\|l\|\leq L.}
$$

A [bounded linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) is continuous, so $l$ belongs to the [continuous dual space](../../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) $E'$. The domination by a [sublinear functional](../../../../../../sublinear-function.md) alone was algebraic; the local [Lipschitz continuity](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) supplies the needed boundedness.
