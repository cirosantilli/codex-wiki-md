<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two [algebras](../../../../../../algebra-split.md) have the same identity, so an inverse in $A$ is also an inverse in $B$. Therefore **$G(A)\subseteq G_B(A)$**.

For strictness, take $B=C_{\mathbb C}(\mathbb T)$, where $\mathbb T$ is the unit circle, and let $A$ be the uniform closure of the [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in the coordinate function $z$. This is a closed unital subalgebra of the [Banach algebra](../../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md) $B$. The coordinate $z$ has inverse $\overline z$ in $B$. But $\overline z$ cannot belong to $A$: for every [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p$,

$$
\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} e^{it}p(e^{it})\,dt=0,
$$

and the same identity holds for every element of $A$ by [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md). It fails for $\overline z$, since $z\overline z=1$. Thus **$z\in G_B(A)\setminus G(A)$**. This [algebra](../../../../../../algebra-split.md) is the boundary realization of the [disk algebra](../../../../../../disk-algebra.md).
