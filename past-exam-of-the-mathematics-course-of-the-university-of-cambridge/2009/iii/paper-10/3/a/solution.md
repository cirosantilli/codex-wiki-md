<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Positivity of the [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) gives monotonicity. For any $g\in C(K)$, the pointwise bounds $-\|g\|_\infty1\leq g\leq\|g\|_\infty1$ therefore imply

$$
|\phi(g)|\leq\phi(1)\|g\|_\infty.
$$

Thus the [positive linear functional](../../../../../../positive-linear-functional.md) is bounded, hence continuous, and its [operator norm](../../../../../../operator-norm.md) is at most $\phi(1)$. Evaluating on the constant function $1$, which has [supremum norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) one when $K$ is nonempty, gives the reverse inequality. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|\phi\|=\phi(1).}
$$

For empty $K$ the function space and functional are zero, so the same conclusion holds. This is the [norm of a positive functional on C(K)](../../../../../../norm-of-a-positive-functional-on-c-k.md) identity.
