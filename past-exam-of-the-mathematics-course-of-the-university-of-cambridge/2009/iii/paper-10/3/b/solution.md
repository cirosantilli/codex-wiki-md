<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $c=\|\phi\|=\phi(1)\geq0$. If $c=0$, the [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) is zero and is positive. Otherwise, for a nonzero $g\geq0$ let $h=g/\|g\|_\infty$. Then $0\leq h\leq1$ and $\|1-h\|_\infty\leq1$, so boundedness gives

$$
\phi(h)=c-\phi(1-h)\geq c-|\phi(1-h)|\geq0.
$$

Positive rescaling gives $\phi(g)\geq0$. Thus **$\phi$ is a [positive linear functional](../../../../../../positive-linear-functional.md)**. This is the scalar form of the [unital contraction positivity criterion](../../../../../../unital-contraction-positivity-criterion.md).
