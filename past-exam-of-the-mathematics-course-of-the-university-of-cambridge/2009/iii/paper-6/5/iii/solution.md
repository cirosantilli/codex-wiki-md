<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Under the [Principal sl2 subalgebra](../../../../../../principal-sl2-subalgebra.md), a [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) of root $\beta$ has $h$-eigenvalue $\beta(2\rho^\vee)=2\operatorname{ht}(\beta)$, where the height is the sum of its simple-root coefficients. The six positive [roots of a root system](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of the [G2 root system](../../../../../../g2-root-system.md) have heights

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
\beta&\alpha_1&\alpha_2&\alpha_1+\alpha_2&2\alpha_1+\alpha_2&3\alpha_1+\alpha_2&3\alpha_1+2\alpha_2\\\hline
\operatorname{ht}(\beta)&1&1&2&3&4&5.
\end{array}
$$

Each positive and negative [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) has [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. The two-dimensional [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) contributes two copies of the zero [weight](../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). Therefore the [Principal q-character of the G2 adjoint representation](../../../../../../principal-q-character-of-the-g2-adjoint-representation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ch}_q\mathfrak g=q^{10}+q^8+q^6+q^4+2q^2+2+2q^{-2}+q^{-4}+q^{-6}+q^{-8}+q^{-10}.}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\operatorname{ch}_q\mathfrak g=\operatorname{ch}L_{10}+\operatorname{ch}L_2
=\frac{q^{11}-q^{-11}}{q-q^{-1}}+\frac{q^3-q^{-3}}{q-q^{-1}}.
$$

The [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](../../../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md) therefore gives the actual restricted-module decomposition $\mathfrak g\cong L_{10}\oplus L_2$, of [dimensions](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $11+3=14$. In the convention using $q^{h/2}$, the same result is $q^5+q^4+q^3+q^2+2q+2+2q^{-1}+q^{-2}+q^{-3}+q^{-4}+q^{-5}$. Both forms encode the same two [weight](../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) strings.
