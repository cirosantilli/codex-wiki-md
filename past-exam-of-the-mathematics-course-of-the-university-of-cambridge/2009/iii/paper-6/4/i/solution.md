<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), choose a [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) and positive [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) $R^+$, and put $\rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in R^+}\alpha$. For a [dominant integral weight](../../../../../../dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$, let $L(\lambda)$ be the finite-dimensional [highest-weight representation](../../../../../../highest-weight-representation.md) with [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$. The [Weyl dimension formula](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dim L(\lambda)=\prod_{\alpha\in R^+}\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}.}
$$

Here $\alpha^\vee=2\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)$ is the [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md), the pairing is between a [weight](../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) and a [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md), and $\rho$ is the [Weyl vector](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). The [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) can be any [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) invariant normalization on each simple factor: its scaling cancels from the ratios. The dominance condition means that $\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle$ is a nonnegative [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) for each [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md).
