<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $S=\partial_z\overline u>0$ and $N^2=-(g/\rho_0)\partial_z\overline\rho>0$, using the constant reference [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho_0$ of the [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md). The [mean-shear production](../../../../../../mean-shear-production-of-turbulent-kinetic-energy.md) is $\mathcal P=-\overline{u'w'}S>0$. The stable [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) destruction is $B=(g/\rho_0)\overline{\rho'w'}>0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Ri}_f=\frac{B}{\mathcal P}=\frac{(g/\rho_0)\overline{\rho'w'}}{-\overline{u'w'}\,\partial_z\overline u},\qquad \mathrm{Ri}(z)=\frac{N^2}{S^2}=-\frac{g\,\partial_z\overline\rho}{\rho_0(\partial_z\overline u)^2}.}
$$

The [Flux Richardson number](../../../../../../flux-richardson-number.md) compares stable [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) destruction with shear production; the [gradient Richardson number](../../../../../../gradient-richardson-number.md) compares restoring stratification with mean [shear rate](../../../../../../shear-rate.md). They are generally different. The stationary energy budget gives $\epsilon=\mathcal P(1-\mathrm{Ri}_f)$, so positive dissipation in this balance requires $\mathrm{Ri}_f<1$.
