<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the mean streamwise [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) be $U(y)$ and set $S=dU/dy>0$. The relevant covariance is $\overline{u'v'}<0$ on this half of the channel. The work of the [Reynolds stress](../../../../../../reynolds-stress.md) against the mean [shear rate](../../../../../../shear-rate.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P=-\overline{u'v'}\frac{dU}{dy}.}
$$

The [eddy viscosity](../../../../../../eddy-viscosity.md) relation is $-\overline{u'v'}=\nu_T S$, so

$$
\boxed{\nu_T=\frac{-\overline{u'v'}}{dU/dy},\qquad\mathcal P=\nu_T\left(\frac{dU}{dy}\right)^2.}
$$

These are the kinematic-stress conventions, with the covariance measured per unit mass; inserting a stress measured in force per area would require division by [mass density](../../../../../../density.md).
