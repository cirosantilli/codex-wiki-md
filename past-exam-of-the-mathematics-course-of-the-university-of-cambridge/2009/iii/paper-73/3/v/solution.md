<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The constant [Reynolds stress](../../../../../../reynolds-stress.md) and the logarithmic shear imply the [eddy viscosity](../../../../../../eddy-viscosity.md) $\nu_T=u_*\kappa y$. On the other hand the [K-epsilon model](../../../../../../k-epsilon-turbulence-model.md) gives $\nu_T=C_\mu k^2/\epsilon$. Insert $\epsilon=u_*^3/(\kappa y)$:

$$
u_*\kappa y=\frac{C_\mu k^2\kappa y}{u_*^3}.
$$

Canceling the positive factors yields $C_\mu k^2=u_*^4$. Since energy is nonnegative, the [log-layer solution of the k-epsilon model](../../../../../../log-layer-solution-of-the-k-epsilon-model.md) has

$$
\boxed{k=\frac{u_*^2}{\sqrt{C_\mu}},}
$$

independent of $y$ to this leading overlap-layer accuracy. In particular $k_y=0$, and the $k$ diffusion term vanishes, confirming the local production–dissipation balance in the energy equation.
