<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [law of the wall](../../../../../../law-of-the-wall.md) in the logarithmic overlap region is $U/u_*=\kappa^{-1}\log(y/y_0)+B$, with the wall-scale reference absorbed in $y_0$ and $B$. Differentiating gives the **mean [shear rate](../../../../../../shear-rate.md)**

$$
\boxed{\frac{dU}{dy}=\frac{u_*}{\kappa y}.}
$$

Here $\kappa$ is the [Von Kármán constant](../../../../../../von-karman-constant.md). This form applies outside the viscous sublayer but well below the outer channel scale; it is not valid at the wall itself or at the channel centre.
