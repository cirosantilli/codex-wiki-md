<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A [flux-limited galaxy selection](../../../../../../flux-limited-galaxy-selection.md) samples a much larger volume for bright [galaxies](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md): in the nearby Euclidean limit $d_{\max}\propto\sqrt L$, hence $V_{\max}\propto L^{3/2}$, so very numerous faint [dwarf galaxies](../../../../../../dwarf-galaxy.md) are usually detectable only nearby. With a [Schechter function](../../../../../../schechter-function.md) $\phi(L)\propto L^{\alpha_S}e^{-L/L_*}$, detected counts per logarithmic [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) interval scale as $L^{\alpha_S+5/2}e^{-L/L_*}$ and, for $\alpha_S\sim-1$ to $-1.3$, peak near $L\sim(1.2\text{--}1.5)L_*$ rather than at the numerous faint end. **The competition between increasing survey volume and the bright-end cutoff concentrates detected luminosities near $L_*$**, with the precise width depending on the selection and survey depth.
