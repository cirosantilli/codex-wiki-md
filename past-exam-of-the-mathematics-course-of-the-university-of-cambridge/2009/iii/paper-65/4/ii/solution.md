<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At small separations the extended [dark-matter haloes](../../../../../../dark-matter-halo.md) overlap, and [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](../../../../../../chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md) transfers orbital [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) to gravitational wakes while tidal interactions also redistribute orbital [energy](../../../../../../energy.md). A characteristic decay time scales as $t_{\rm df}\sim v r^2/(Gm\ln\Lambda)$, so sufficiently massive close pairs evolve rapidly towards a [galaxy merger](../../../../../../galaxy-merger.md) and spend relatively little time at small separation. **Short residence time depletes the close-pair population**; the quoted separation scale is characteristic of the population, not a universal exclusion radius.
