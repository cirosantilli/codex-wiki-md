<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [stellar population](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) has appreciable random [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md), and its declining radial density creates an outward stress gradient that supplies some support against gravity; cold gas has much less random-motion support and follows nearly the local [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md). Neglecting velocity-ellipsoid tilt, the radial [Jeans equation](../../../../../../jeans-equation.md) gives $v_c^2-\overline v_\phi^2=\sigma_R^2[-d\ln(n_*\sigma_R^2)/d\ln R-1+\sigma_\phi^2/\sigma_R^2]$, generally positive for the stellar disk. **The slower mean stellar rotation is [asymmetric drift](../../../../../../stellar-asymmetric-drift.md).**
