<h1 id="4/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Massive stars rapidly return oxygen, magnesium, neon and other [alpha elements](../../../../../../alpha-element.md) through [core-collapse supernovae](../../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md), whereas [Type Ia supernovae](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) supply a substantial delayed iron contribution. If the [galactic bulge](../../../../../../galactic-bulge.md) and [stellar halo](../../../../../../stellar-halo.md) formed much of their stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) rapidly, their stars locked in enhanced alpha-element to iron ratios before much [delayed iron enrichment](../../../../../../delayed-iron-enrichment.md), while prolonged [star formation](../../../../../../star-formation.md) in the local [galactic disk](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md) included more iron-rich gas. **The abundance contrast chiefly records different enrichment timescales**, with the [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) and gas exchange also affecting the ratios.
