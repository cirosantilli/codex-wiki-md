<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use a steady, isotropic, self-gravitating [dark matter halo](../../../../../../dark-matter-halo.md), with $V$ its constant one-dimensional [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md). The [Spherical Jeans equation](../../../../../../spherical-jeans-equation.md) and spherical [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) read

$$
V^2\frac{d\rho}{dr}=-\rho\frac{GM(r)}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho.
$$

For the scale-free outer region, put $\rho=A r^{-p}$. Its enclosed [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) scales as $r^{3-p}$, so $GM(r)/r$ is constant only if $p=2$; the [Spherical Jeans equation](../../../../../../spherical-jeans-equation.md) then gives $2V^2=GM(r)/r=4\pi GA$. Thus the [singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere.md) has

$$
\boxed{\rho(r)=\frac{V^2}{2\pi Gr^2},\qquad M(<r)=\frac{2V^2r}{G}.}
$$

Its [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md) is $v_c=\sqrt2V$. If a speed denoted $V$ were instead the [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md), the coefficient would be $V^2/(4\pi G)$, but the printed variable is a [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md).

The assumptions matter. A constant [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) and spherical shape alone do not specify a unique central [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) or orbital distribution. Even with isotropy, the [Spherical Jeans equation](../../../../../../spherical-jeans-equation.md) integrates to $\rho=\rho_0\exp[-(\Phi-\Phi_0)/V^2]$, and the self-gravitating [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](../../../../../../poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) gives

$$
\frac1{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac{d\ln\rho}{dr}\right)
=-\frac{4\pi G}{V^2}\rho.
$$

There are finite-central-density isothermal solutions as well as the singular one; the $r^{-2}$ expression is the intended outer scale-free halo approximation. A finite halo must also end or steepen at large radius, since the untruncated [singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere.md) has infinite total [mass](../../../../../../mass.md).
