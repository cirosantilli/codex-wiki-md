<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

All gas being used or expelled means $G\to0$, so $Z\to\infty$ in the formal [leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution](../../../../../../leaky-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md) and the final stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is $S_\infty=G_0/(1+\nu)$. Weight the [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md) by formed stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), using $dS/dZ=(G_0/y)e^{-Z/y_e}$:

$$
\langle Z\rangle_*=
\frac{\int_0^\infty Z\,(G_0/y)e^{-Z/y_e}\,dZ}{G_0/(1+\nu)}
=\frac{(G_0/y)y_e^2}{G_0/(1+\nu)}.
$$

Here $\int_0^\infty u e^{-u}du=1$, by [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md). **The final mass-weighted mean is**

$$
\boxed{\langle Z\rangle_*=\frac{y}{1+\nu}.}
$$

As a metal-budget check, newly produced metal [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is $yS_\infty$; stars retain $\int Z\,dS=yG_0/(1+\nu)^2$, while the [galactic outflow](../../../../../../galactic-outflow.md) carries $\nu\int Z\,dS$. Their sum is exactly $yS_\infty$. An observed luminosity-weighted [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md) need not equal this mass-weighted mean.
