<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [escape-speed scaling of wind mass loading](../../../../../../escape-speed-scaling-of-wind-mass-loading.md) makes the proposed trend dynamically plausible: the shallower [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) of a [dwarf galaxy](../../../../../../dwarf-galaxy.md) lets [stellar feedback](../../../../../../stellar-feedback.md) expel more gas per unit stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). If coupled feedback supplies specific [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) $e_*$, escape requires $\nu v_{\rm esc}^2/2\lesssim e_*$; fixed coupling suggests $\nu\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-2}$ for an [energy-driven outflow](../../../../../../energy-driven-outflow.md). Fixed specific momentum $p_*$ instead gives $\nu v_{\rm esc}\lesssim p_*$, or $\nu\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-1}$ for a [momentum-driven outflow](../../../../../../momentum-driven-outflow.md).

Since [escape velocity](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md) has $v_{\rm esc}^2\sim GM/R$, galaxies whose characteristic radius grows more slowly than their gravitating [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) have increasing binding energy per unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) with increasing [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). For $R\propto M^a$ with $a<1$, the [energy-driven outflow](../../../../../../energy-driven-outflow.md) estimate gives $\nu\propto M^{a-1}$. Equivalently, greater [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) in massive spheroids implies greater escape energy and more effective retention of metals.

For scale, one [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) of $10^{51}\,\mathrm{erg}$ per $100M_\odot$ formed supplies about $5\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{cm^2\,s^{-2}}$ before coupling losses. At ten percent coupling the energetic upper estimate $2e_*/v_{\rm esc}^2$ is about $110$ for $v_{\rm esc}=30\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, but about $0.4$ for $500\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. **Efficient loss from shallow potentials and retention in deep potentials can span the required range.** These are illustrative energetics rather than a unique prediction: cooling, entrainment, fallback and the actual [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) determine the escaping [mass-loading factor](../../../../../../mass-loading-factor.md).
