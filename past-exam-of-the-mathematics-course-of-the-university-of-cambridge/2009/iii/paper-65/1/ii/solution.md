<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A star inherits the [gas-phase metallicity](../../../../../../gas-phase-metallicity.md) at its birth. Set $y_e=y/(1+\nu)$; the preceding calculation gives $G(Z)=G_0e^{-Z/y_e}$ and $dS/dZ=G(Z)/y$. If the [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) is fixed, let $\kappa$ be the number of the counted stars per unit formed stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md); this includes any restriction to surviving long-lived tracers. The [metallicity distribution in a leaky box](../../../../../../metallicity-distribution-in-a-leaky-box.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{dN}{dZ}=\frac{\kappa G_0}{y}\exp\left[-\frac{(1+\nu)Z}{y}\right].}
$$

The total eventual formed stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is $G_0/(1+\nu)$, so $N_\infty=\kappa G_0/(1+\nu)$ and the equivalent normalized [metallicity distribution function](../../../../../../metallicity-distribution-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1{N_\infty}\frac{dN}{dZ}=\frac{1+\nu}{y}e^{-(1+\nu)Z/y}.}
$$

If $N(Z)$ means a cumulative count rather than a count per [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md) interval, [integration](../../../../../../integral.md) yields

$$
\boxed{N(<Z)=N_\infty\left[1-e^{-(1+\nu)Z/y}\right].}
$$

At a finite observation time these expressions end at the current [gas-phase metallicity](../../../../../../gas-phase-metallicity.md) $Z_f$ and the normalized distribution divides by $1-e^{-Z_f/y_e}$. A distribution per logarithmic [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md) instead multiplies $dN/dZ$ by $(\ln10)Z$; it is not the same shape as the distribution per linear [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md).
