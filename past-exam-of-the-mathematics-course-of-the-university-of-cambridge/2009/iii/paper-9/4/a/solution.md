<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Weak convergence of distributions](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-distributions.md) means $\langle T_n,\varphi\rangle\to\langle T,\varphi\rangle$ for every [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$. The [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{\langle\partial_{x_j}T,\varphi\rangle=-\langle T,\partial_{x_j}\varphi\rangle.}
$$

It is linear and continuous: differentiation preserves compact support and bounds each test-function seminorm by one of higher derivative order, so composing it with the continuous functional $T$ again gives a [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Finally, for every fixed test function,

$$
\langle\partial_{x_j}T_n,\varphi\rangle
=-\langle T_n,\partial_{x_j}\varphi\rangle
\longrightarrow-\langle T,\partial_{x_j}\varphi\rangle
=\langle\partial_{x_j}T,\varphi\rangle.
$$

Thus distributional differentiation preserves the stated convergence.
