↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a real [locally integrable function](../../../../../../locally-integrable-function.md) $f$, the distributional definition of a [subharmonic function](../../../../../../subharmonic-function.md) is $\Delta f\geq0$: for every nonnegative $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$,

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega f\,\Delta\varphi\geq0.}
$$

A [superharmonic function](../../../../../../superharmonic-function.md) has the opposite inequality, equivalently $-f$ is subharmonic. A [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) has $\Delta f=0$ distributionally, equivalently equality for every test function. These definitions concern almost-everywhere classes; the given upper semicontinuous representative supplies the pointwise version for subharmonic functions.
