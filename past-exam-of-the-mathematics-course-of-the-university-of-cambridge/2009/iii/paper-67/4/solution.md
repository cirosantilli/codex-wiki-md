<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A [planetary magnetic field](../../../../../planetary-magnetic-field.md) is evidence about electrical conductivity, fluid motion and thermal evolution, but its presence alone does not identify a present [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md). One must separate internal global fields, crustal [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md) and currents driven by the [solar wind](../../../../../solar-wind.md). The following discussion uses the observational picture available by 2009, including the early MESSENGER flybys and the Voyager, Galileo and Cassini results.

Outside a planet's internal electric currents, the internal component is approximately a potential [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field.md). Its [spherical harmonic](../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) of degree $\ell$ decreases as $r^{-(\ell+2)}$. Measurements at different positions and altitudes can therefore distinguish a deep, low-degree source from localized crustal anomalies and external magnetospheric currents. Repeated observations, radio emissions, aurorae and particle measurements supply complementary evidence for a [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md), while slow secular variation and records of past reversals test the internal field's evolution.

For [Earth](../../../../../earth.md), ground observatories and satellite measurements reveal a predominantly dipolar field of several tens of microtesla at the surface, tilted relative to the rotation axis and changing with time. Magnetic minerals in rocks record a much older field and repeated [geomagnetic reversals](../../../../../geomagnetic-reversal.md). These observations, together with seismic evidence for a liquid outer core, support an operating [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md) in conducting iron alloy. Core temperatures exceed the relevant [Curie temperatures](../../../../../curie-temperature.md), so ordinary permanent [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md) cannot explain the main field. Thermal [convection](../../../../../convection.md), cooling and [compositional convection](../../../../../compositional-convection.md) associated with inner-core growth supply buoyancy; the [Coriolis force](../../../../../coriolis-force.md) organizes the motion. The observed nonaxisymmetric components and time dependence are consistent with a dynamic source, rather than a fixed bar magnet.

Mariner 10 and the first two MESSENGER flybys established a weak but coherent intrinsic field at [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md), with roughly dipolar structure closely aligned with its rotation axis. It is much weaker than Earth's surface field, but its global character favors a deep source over an explanation solely by localized crustal patches. A [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md) in a fluid part of Mercury's large iron-rich core is the natural interpretation, supported by evidence for a molten core component. Light elements can depress the alloy's freezing temperature, and thermal or compositional buoyancy can maintain motion. Slow rotation is not enough to rule out [dynamo action](../../../../../dynamo-action.md). The limited pre-orbital measurements do not uniquely determine the detailed core structure or the mechanism responsible for the field's weakness.

For [Venus](../../../../../venus.md), spacecraft including Pioneer Venus found no appreciable internally generated global field. Nevertheless its ionosphere interacts with the [solar wind](../../../../../solar-wind.md), producing magnetic draping, a bow shock and an [induced magnetosphere](../../../../../induced-magnetosphere.md). Those external signatures are not evidence for an internal [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md). Venus is comparable to Earth in size and may possess a substantial conducting core; the absence of a detectable dynamo therefore need not mean that all of its core is solid. Insufficient core heat loss or stable stratification can prevent the required [convection](../../../../../convection.md). Its slow rotation can affect flow organization, but rotation rate by itself is not a sufficient explanation of the contrast with Earth or Mercury.

For [Mars](../../../../../mars.md), Mars Global Surveyor found strong, spatially localized crustal anomalies, especially in ancient southern terrain, rather than an appreciable present global dipole. This is [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md), indicating that an ancient field magnetized the crust while it formed and cooled. The distribution of anomalies and weakly magnetized major impact basins constrain when that ancient [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md) operated; crustal magnetization is a record of past activity, not a present global dynamo. Modern interaction with the [solar wind](../../../../../solar-wind.md) also produces an [induced magnetosphere](../../../../../induced-magnetosphere.md). Cooling and changes in core heat extraction can explain the cessation of dynamo-driving [convection](../../../../../convection.md); the observations alone do not prove complete solidification of the core.

For [Jupiter](../../../../../jupiter.md), radio emissions, its intense radiation belts and measurements by Pioneer, Voyager and Galileo demonstrate a strong global intrinsic field and an enormous [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md). The field is broadly dipolar but tilted and contains higher multipoles. It is generated in a deep conducting fluid rather than by a permanent solid magnet: the principal conducting region is [metallic hydrogen](../../../../../metallic-hydrogen.md) in the high-pressure interior. Cooling and gravitational energy release maintain [convection](../../../../../convection.md), while rapid rotation organizes the flow. Plasma supplied by Io and currents in the [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md) strongly modify the observed external field, but cannot account for the internally sourced planetary component. Jupiter's large magnetic moment also reflects its large size; a moment ratio should not be confused with the ratio of local surface field strengths.

For [Saturn](../../../../../saturn.md), Pioneer, Voyager and Cassini measurements likewise show an intrinsic field and a [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md), with a dipole strikingly close to the rotation axis. A [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md) in conducting [metallic hydrogen](../../../../../metallic-hydrogen.md) is supported by the same broad interior argument as for Jupiter. Interior cooling and differentiation can provide power, including energy associated with helium separation. The near alignment does not invalidate the [Cowling anti-dynamo theorem](../../../../../cowling-anti-dynamo-theorem.md): that theorem excludes an exactly axisymmetric isolated dynamo field, not a predominantly axisymmetric exterior with undetected nonaxisymmetric structure in its source region. A stably stratified conducting layer above the active region is one possible way to attenuate the observable nonaxisymmetric components; it is a model, not a uniquely established consequence of the observations.

For [Uranus](../../../../../uranus.md), Voyager 2 in 1986 observed an intrinsic field with a dipole tilted by about $59^\circ$ to the rotation axis and significantly displaced from the centre. Higher multipoles are substantial, so a centred dipole is an inadequate description. The field and associated [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md) indicate an active [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md), despite the planet's small intrinsic heat flux. That low luminosity constrains its thermal evolution and may indicate inhibited deep mixing; it does not demonstrate an absence of all conducting-fluid motion. A conducting, volatile-rich fluid shell is a plausible source region. The term [ice giant](../../../../../ice-giant.md) describes bulk composition, not a claim that the dynamo region is ordinary frozen ice.

For [Neptune](../../../../../neptune.md), Voyager 2 in 1989 also found a highly non-dipolar, displaced intrinsic field, with a dipole tilt near $47^\circ$. Its field, radio emissions and [magnetosphere](../../../../../magnetosphere.md) imply another active [planetary dynamo](../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md). Its larger intrinsic heat flux than Uranus supplies evidence for substantial continuing interior energy transport. Electrically conducting water-ammonia-rich mixtures at high pressure, with ionic or otherwise mobile charges, can supply the conducting region without requiring the [metallic hydrogen](../../../../../metallic-hydrogen.md) layer characteristic of [gas giants](../../../../../gas-giant.md). Numerical dynamos in convecting shells surrounding more stably stratified interiors can produce strongly nonaxisymmetric, multipolar fields resembling those of the two [ice giants](../../../../../ice-giant.md). Such models show that their unusual field geometry is compatible with [dynamo action](../../../../../dynamo-action.md), but field measurements do not uniquely identify the shell's composition or thickness.

The maintenance mechanism is governed by the [resistive induction equation](../../../../../resistive-induction-equation.md),

$$
\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B,
\qquad
\operatorname{Rm}=\frac{UL}{\eta},\qquad
\eta=\frac1{\mu_0\sigma_e}.
$$

A conducting fluid needs a sufficiently large [magnetic Reynolds number](../../../../../magnetic-reynolds-number.md) and a flow geometry that regenerates magnetic flux rather than merely amplifying one component temporarily. Three-dimensional stretching and twisting can couple poloidal and toroidal fields; strictly planar regeneration and a strictly axisymmetric magnetic field are restricted by the relevant [anti-dynamo theorems](../../../../../anti-dynamo-theorem.md). Rotation influences this regeneration through the [Coriolis force](../../../../../coriolis-force.md), but does not provide an inexhaustible power source merely by being present.

Without regeneration, the characteristic [magnetic diffusion](../../../../../magnetic-diffusion.md) time is $\tau_\eta\sim L^2/\eta$. For a terrestrial core scale of order $10^6$ metres and diffusivity of order one square metre per second, this is of order $10^4$–$10^5$ years, far shorter than geological ages. Long-lived global fields therefore require continual energy input. A schematic [magnetic energy](../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) balance is

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}\,dV
=-\int\mathbf u\cdot(\mathbf J\times\mathbf B)\,dV
-\int\frac{J^2}{\sigma_e}\,dV,
$$

when boundary energy fluxes vanish. Mechanical work against the [Lorentz force density](../../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) supplies the energy lost as [Ohmic heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md). Thermal cooling, [latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md) release, [compositional convection](../../../../../compositional-convection.md) and differentiation supply that mechanical power. Magnetic feedback eventually changes the flow and saturates the field. Strong conductivity without an adequate energy source is not sufficient for a dynamo.

**The 2009 observations support active global dynamos in Mercury, Earth, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune; Venus has no detected appreciable intrinsic dynamo field, and Mars preserves evidence of an extinct earlier dynamo in its crust.** Induced fields and surviving remanence explain why planets without a current global dynamo can still display magnetic phenomena. The diversity of field geometries and strengths constrains, rather than uniquely solves, the diversity of planetary conducting regions and their convective histories.
