<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [special linear group](../../../../../../special-linear-group.md) acts on the [twistor correspondence](../../../../../../twistor-correspondence.md) by $g\cdot(\ell,W)=(g\ell,gW)$. Since an invertible linear map preserves inclusion, this is again an incident flag. The two projections commute with that action:

$$
\boxed{\nu(g\cdot(\ell,W))=g\cdot\nu(\ell,W),\qquad\mu(g\cdot(\ell,W))=g\cdot\mu(\ell,W).}
$$

This is precisely [equivariance](../../../../../../equivariant-map.md): projecting a transformed flag gives the same result as transforming its projection. In particular $g$ sends the [twistor line](../../../../../../twistor-line.md) of a spacetime point to the line of the transformed point and sends the [alpha-plane](../../../../../../alpha-plane.md) of a twistor to that of its transformed twistor. The incidence geometry is preserved, even when a transformation takes a point out of the chosen affine Minkowski chart.
