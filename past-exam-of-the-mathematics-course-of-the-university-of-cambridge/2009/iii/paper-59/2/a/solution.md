<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the compact [twistor correspondence](../../../../../../twistor-correspondence.md), a point $p$ of [complexified compactified Minkowski space](../../../../../../complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md) is a two-dimensional subspace $W_p\subset\mathbb T$. The correspondence fiber consists of flags $(\ell,W_p)$ with $\ell$ a one-dimensional subspace of $W_p$. Its image in [projective twistor space](../../../../../../projective-twistor-space.md) is precisely $\mathbb P(W_p)$, so

$$
\boxed{\widehat p=\mathbb P(W_p)\cong\mathbb{CP}^1.}
$$

This is the [twistor line](../../../../../../twistor-line.md) of $p$. In the affine chart the [twistor incidence relation](../../../../../../twistor-incidence-relation.md) gives $W_p=\{(ip^{AA'}\pi_{A'},\pi_{A'}):\pi\in\mathbb C^2\}$. The linear map $\pi\mapsto(ip\pi,\pi)$ is injective, and projectivizing it gives the same [Complex projective space](../../../../../../complex-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{CP}^1$. The subspace argument also covers points at conformal infinity, where that graph chart fails.
