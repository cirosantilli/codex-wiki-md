<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the signature-$(2,2)$ [twistor Hermitian form](../../../../../../twistor-hermitian-form.md)

$$
h(Z,W)=\omega^A\overline\rho_A+\pi_{A'}\overline\eta^{A'},\qquad Z=(\omega,\pi),\ W=(\eta,\rho).
$$

A real Minkowski point $x$ is a Hermitian matrix $x^{AA'}$. Every vector of its incidence subspace has the form $(ix\pi,\pi)$. Substitution gives

$$
h((ix\pi,\pi),(ix\rho,\rho))=i\overline\rho\,x\pi-i\overline\rho\,x^\dagger\pi=0.
$$

If the two [null geodesics](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) meet at $x$, their [null twistors](../../../../../../null-twistor.md) $X,Y$ both belong to this totally isotropic subspace. In addition to their individual nullness, they must obey

$$
\boxed{h(X,Y)=X^\alpha\overline Y_\alpha=0.}
$$

For independent $X,Y$, this says that every point of the [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md) $\mathbb P\operatorname{span}(X,Y)$ is null: it lies entirely in [projective null twistor space](../../../../../../projective-null-twistor-space.md). It is the [twistor line](../../../../../../twistor-line.md) of their meeting point. In the finite affine setting, distinct null directions at the meeting point make their primed spinors independent; proportional primed spinors would give proportional incident twistors and the same null geodesic.
