<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the compact [alpha-plane](../../../../../../../alpha-plane.md) $A_\ell$ and [beta plane](../../../../../../../beta-plane.md) $B_H$, an intersection point must satisfy $\ell\subset W\subset H$. If $\ell\not\subset H$, that is impossible. If $\ell\subset H$, intermediate two-planes correspond to lines $W/\ell$ in the two-dimensional quotient $H/\ell$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A_\ell\cap B_H=\begin{cases}\varnothing,&\ell\not\subset H,\\\mathbb P(H/\ell)\cong\mathbb{CP}^1,&\ell\subset H.\end{cases}}
$$

The nonempty intersection is a projective [null geodesic](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md). In an affine chart its tangent must be both $u^A\pi^{A'}$ and $\lambda^A v^{A'}$; these conditions force a multiple of $\lambda^A\pi^{A'}$. Thus its affine portion is a null line, with the indicated projective compactification.
