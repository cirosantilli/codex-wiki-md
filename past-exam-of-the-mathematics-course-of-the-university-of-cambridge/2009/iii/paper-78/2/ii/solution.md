<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A plane wave returning down the guide has spatial factor $e^{-ik_0x}$ and arises from the pole at $k=k_0$, lying below the inversion contour. For $x>0$ closing below is clockwise, so the inverse transform is minus $i$ times the residue. Since $\gamma\sinh(\gamma b)\sim2bk_0(k-k_0)$, the interior residue is

$$
\operatorname{Res}_{k=k_0}\Phi_{\rm sc}^{\rm in}=\frac{iC_0L^-(k_0)}{4bk_0^2}.
$$

Hence the reflected plane-wave [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), relative to the incident [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) one, is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R=\frac{L^+(-k_0)L^-(k_0)}{4bk_0^2},\qquad
\phi_{\rm refl}=\mathcal R\,e^{-ik_0x+i\omega t}.}
$$

Equivalently, because $L^+$ carries the simple zero at $k_0$, $\mathcal R=L^+(-k_0)/(2k_0(L^+)'(k_0))$. Other guided modes, if propagating at the chosen frequency, come from the additional kernel zeros; they do not alter which residue gives the plane-mode [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Evanescent terms near the aperture are also separate. A multiplicative renormalization of the factors cancels in the product defining $\mathcal R$.
