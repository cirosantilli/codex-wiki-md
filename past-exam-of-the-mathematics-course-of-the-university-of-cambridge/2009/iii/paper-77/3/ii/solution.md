<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Warmer Atlantic water increases the reservoir of heat beneath the Arctic surface layer. However, [stable density stratification](../../../../../../stable-density-stratification.md) across the [cold halocline](../../../../../../cold-halocline.md) often prevents that heat from directly reaching the ice. The effect on [sea ice](../../../../../../sea-ice.md) depends on upward mixing and [ocean heat flux](../../../../../../ocean-heat-flux.md), with greater direct access in some Atlantic-influenced regions; a temperature increase at depth alone is insufficient to quantify basal melting.
