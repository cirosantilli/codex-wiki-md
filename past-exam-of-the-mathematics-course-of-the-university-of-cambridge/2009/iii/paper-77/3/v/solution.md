The [Arctic Oscillation](../../../../../../arctic-oscillation.md) changes pressure patterns and winds, redistributing [sea ice](../../../../../../sea-ice.md) and altering export through [Fram Strait](../../../../../../fram-strait.md). Removing thick old ice and replacing it with thinner new ice preconditions greater summer loss; winds also open leads and change ocean circulation. This is an important dynamic contributor and source of variability, rather than a universal one-to-one relation between the oscillation index and total summer ice extent.
