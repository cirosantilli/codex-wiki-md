<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Erosion or displacement of the [cold halocline](../../../../../../cold-halocline.md) weakens the barrier separating [sea ice](../../../../../../sea-ice.md) from warmer water below. It can therefore increase the upward [ocean heat flux](../../../../../../ocean-heat-flux.md) without requiring a large new warming of that deeper water. Wind-driven mixing, changes in shelf-water supply, and freshwater redistribution can alter the halocline. This mechanism couples the Atlantic heat reservoir to atmospheric and circulation changes.
