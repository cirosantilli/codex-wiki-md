<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Warmer air increases surface melt during the warm season, lengthens the melt season, and reduces the accumulated [freezing degree-days](../../../../../../freezing-degree-day.md) available for winter growth. Enhanced downward heat transfer can therefore thin the cover even without extra ocean heat. It provides a broad thermodynamic influence on [Arctic sea ice decline](../../../../../../arctic-sea-ice-decline.md), while regional winds and cloud conditions determine part of the local response.
