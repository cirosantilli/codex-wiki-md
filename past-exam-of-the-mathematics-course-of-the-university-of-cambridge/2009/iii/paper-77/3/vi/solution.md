<h1 id="3/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Long-term thinning lowers the [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md) inventory that must be supplied before a patch of [sea ice](../../../../../../sea-ice.md) disappears. Thin ice is also more mobile and vulnerable to mechanical breakup. A smaller fraction of [multi-year sea ice](../../../../../../multi-year-sea-ice.md) therefore increases susceptibility to a warm or windy summer. Thin ice grows relatively rapidly in winter because it conducts heat more readily, giving a stabilizing response that partly opposes continued thinning without cancelling sustained warming.
