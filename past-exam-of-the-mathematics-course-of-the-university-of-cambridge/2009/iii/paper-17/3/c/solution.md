<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The correspondence in part (a) respects [Lie brackets](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md). If the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is abelian, any two [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) satisfy $[X,Y]=0$ everywhere, not just at the identity. Part (b) therefore gives $d\omega(X,Y)=0$ for every such pair.

At a point $g$, the map $(dL_g)_e:T_eG\to T_gG$ is an isomorphism. Thus every [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) at $g$ is the value of a [left-invariant vector field](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md). The two-form $d\omega$ consequently vanishes on every pair of [tangent vectors](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) at every point, proving

$$
\boxed{d\omega=0.}
$$

No connectedness assumption on the [Lie group](../../../../../../lie-group.md) is needed: the argument uses its left translations and its [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md).
