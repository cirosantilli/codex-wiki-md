<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $e$ be the identity. For a [left-invariant differential form](../../../../../../left-invariant-differential-form.md) $\omega$ and a [left-invariant vector field](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) $X$, one has

$$
\omega_g(X_g)=\omega_g((dL_g)_eX_e)=(L_g^*\omega)_e(X_e)=\omega_e(X_e).
$$

The value is therefore independent of $g$, even if the [Lie group](../../../../../../lie-group.md) is disconnected. The same calculation applies to another left-invariant field $Y$.

The standard [exterior derivative of a one-form evaluated on vector fields](../../../../../../exterior-derivative-of-a-one-form-evaluated-on-vector-fields.md) is

$$
d\omega(X,Y)=X(\omega(Y))-Y(\omega(X))-\omega([X,Y]).
$$

Both differentiated functions are constant in the present situation. Thus

$$
\boxed{d\omega(X,Y)=-\omega([X,Y]).}
$$

This identity is a direct consequence of left invariance and the stated general formula for the [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md).
