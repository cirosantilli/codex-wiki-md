<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In Cartesian coordinates on Euclidean space, define

$$
\boxed{\nabla_XY=\sum_{j=1}^n X(Y^j)\,\partial_j\qquad\text{when }Y=\sum_jY^j\partial_j.}
$$

This is an [affine connection](../../../../../../affine-connection.md) by the ordinary product rule. Its [connection coefficients](../../../../../../connection-components.md) in this coordinate frame are all zero. The component formula for the [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) gives $\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y]$, so it is torsion-free. Also, since the Euclidean metric has constant coefficients,

$$
X\!\left(\sum_jY^jZ^j\right)=\sum_j X(Y^j)Z^j+\sum_jY^jX(Z^j).
$$

This is exactly metric compatibility. By uniqueness it is the Euclidean [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md). In particular constant vector fields are parallel, while the derivative of a general field is given by the boxed formula.
