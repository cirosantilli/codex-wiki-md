A connection on $TM$ is a [metric connection](../../../../../../../metric-connection.md) for $g$ if for all smooth [vector fields](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) $X,Y,Z$,

$$
\boxed{X\bigl(g(Y,Z)\bigr)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ).}
$$

Equivalently, its induced [covariant derivative](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of the metric satisfies $\nabla g=0$. This is also called a [metric-compatible connection](../../../../../../../metric-connection.md).
