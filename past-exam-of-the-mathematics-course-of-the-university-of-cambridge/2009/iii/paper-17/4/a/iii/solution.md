The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is the unique [affine connection](../../../../../../../affine-connection.md) which is both a [metric connection](../../../../../../../metric-connection.md) and a [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md). Uniqueness is encoded by the Koszul formula

$$
\begin{aligned}
2g(\nabla_XY,Z)={}&Xg(Y,Z)+Yg(X,Z)-Zg(X,Y)\\
&+g([X,Y],Z)-g([Y,Z],X)+g([Z,X],Y).
\end{aligned}
$$

It follows by adding the metric-compatibility identities with derivative fields $X,Y$, subtracting the one with derivative field $Z$, and replacing differences of [covariant derivatives](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) by [Lie brackets](../../../../../../../lie-bracket.md). Nondegeneracy of $g$ determines $\nabla_XY$ uniquely from all its pairings with $Z$. The [existence and uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../existence-and-uniqueness-of-the-levi-civita-connection.md) supplies existence; no existence proof is needed here.
