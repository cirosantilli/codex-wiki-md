<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Normalize the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) by $x=a/a_0$. [Constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) for [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) gives $\rho=\rho_0x^{-3}$. Multiplying the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) by $x^2$ and substituting the expressions from part (i),

$$
\dot x^2=\frac{8\pi G\rho_0}{3x}-\frac{kc^2}{a_0^2}=H_0^2\left(\frac{2q_0}{x}+1-2q_0\right).
$$

Take the expanding branch $\dot x>0$ and set the origin of [cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) at the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md), $x=0$. The [age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant](../../../../../../age-of-a-dust-universe-without-a-cosmological-constant.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{t_0=\frac1{H_0}\int_0^1\left(1-2q_0+\frac{2q_0}{x}\right)^{-1/2}\,dx.}
$$
