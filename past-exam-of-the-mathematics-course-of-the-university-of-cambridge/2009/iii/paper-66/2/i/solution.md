<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and zero [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives $\ddot a/a=-4\pi G\rho/3$. The [deceleration parameter](../../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) therefore satisfies the [dust relation between deceleration and density](../../../../../../dust-relation-between-deceleration-and-density.md), $q=4\pi G\rho/(3H^2)$. Evaluating today,

$$
\boxed{\rho_0=\frac{3H_0^2q_0}{4\pi G}.}
$$

Substitute this into the present [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) to obtain $H_0^2+kc^2/a_0^2=2q_0H_0^2$, or

$$
\boxed{\frac{kc^2}{a_0^2}=(2q_0-1)H_0^2.}
$$

In particular, $q_0>1/2$, $q_0=1/2$, and $q_0<1/2$ correspond to positive, zero, and negative spatial curvature.
