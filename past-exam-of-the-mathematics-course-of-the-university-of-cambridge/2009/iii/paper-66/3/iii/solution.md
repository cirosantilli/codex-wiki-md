<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A radial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) in the [FLRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) obeys $d\chi=c\,|dt|/a$. The [redshift-time relation](../../../../../../redshift-time-relation.md) $dt=-dz/[(1+z)H(z)]$, with $a_0=1$, therefore gives

$$
\Delta\chi=c\int_{z_1}^{z_2}\frac{dz}{H(z)}\simeq\boxed{\frac{c\,\Delta z}{H(\bar z)}},\qquad\bar z=\frac{z_1+z_2}{2}.
$$

This is [comoving radial distance](../../../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md). The [proper distance in cosmology](../../../../../../proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) at the clouds' approximately common epoch is instead

$$
\boxed{\Delta d_{\rm proper}\simeq\frac{c\,\Delta z}{(1+\bar z)H(\bar z)}.}
$$

The distinction is essential when quoting a cloud separation. The approximation neglects the change of $H$ across the interval and assumes the observed spacing is dominated by [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md); [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) can perturb the inferred separation. It is the redshift version of [proper absorber separation from a wavelength interval](../../../../../../proper-absorber-separation-from-a-wavelength-interval.md).
