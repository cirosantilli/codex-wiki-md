The [Lyman-alpha forest](../../../../../../lyman-alpha-forest.md) is the collection of [absorption lines](../../../../../../absorption-line.md) blueward of a distant [quasar](../../../../../../quasar.md)'s own [Lyman-alpha line](../../../../../../lyman-alpha-line.md). Foreground neutral hydrogen removes photons at rest wavelength $\lambda_\alpha\simeq1215.67$ angstroms, observed at $\lambda=(1+z)\lambda_\alpha$ because of [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md). Gas at many different foreground redshifts therefore produces many lines. The weak forest traces diffuse, mostly ionized [intergalactic medium](../../../../../../intergalactic-medium.md), rather than a population of fully neutral clouds; much stronger, self-shielded absorbers are a separate regime. [Madau's account of quasar absorption systems](https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/Madau6/Madau2.html) distinguishes these column-density regimes.
