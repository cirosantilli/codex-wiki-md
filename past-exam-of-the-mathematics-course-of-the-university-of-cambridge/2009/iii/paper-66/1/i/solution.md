<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $H=\dot a/a$ for the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md). Subtracting the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) from the second equation gives the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md),

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}\left(\rho+\frac{3p}{c^2}\right)+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}.
$$

Since $\dot H=\ddot a/a-H^2$, eliminating $H^2$ with the first equation yields

$$
\dot H-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}=-4\pi G\left(\rho+\frac p{c^2}\right).
$$

Differentiating the first equation, with constant [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) and curvature parameter, now gives

$$
\frac{8\pi G}{3}\dot\rho=2H\left(\dot H-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}\right)=-8\pi GH\left(\rho+\frac p{c^2}\right).
$$

Thus the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) is $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p/c^2)=0$. Multiplying by $a^3$ and using $d(a^3)/dt=3Ha^3$ proves

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}(\rho a^3)+\frac p{c^2}\frac{d}{dt}(a^3)=0.}
$$

This is local [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md): expansion changes the energy in a [comoving volume](../../../../../../comoving-volume.md) through pressure work.
