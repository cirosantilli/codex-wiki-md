<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w$, the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) becomes $\dot\rho+3(1+w)H\rho=0$. Consequently

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(\rho a^{3(1+w)}\bigr)=a^{3(1+w)}\bigl[\dot\rho+3(1+w)H\rho\bigr]=0,
\qquad\boxed{\rho a^{3(1+w)}=C.}
$$

The [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) includes $\rho\propto a^{-3}$ for [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and $\rho\propto a^{-4}$ for [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md).
