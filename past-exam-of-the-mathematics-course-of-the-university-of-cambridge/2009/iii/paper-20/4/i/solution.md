<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Along the smooth complex curve, there is an exact sequence of complex [vector bundles](../../../../../../vector-bundle.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow T\Sigma\longrightarrow TX|_\Sigma\longrightarrow N_{\Sigma/X}\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [Whitney sum formula for Chern classes](../../../../../../whitney-sum-formula-for-chern-classes.md) gives $c_1(TX)|_\Sigma=c_1(T\Sigma)+c_1(N_{\Sigma/X})$. The complex tangent line has first Chern number $\chi(\Sigma)=2-2g(\Sigma)$. The normal complex line is an oriented real rank-two bundle, whose [Euler number](../../../../../../euler-number-of-a-vector-bundle.md) is the [self-intersection](../../../../../../self-intersection-number.md) of the curve: a generic normal section has signed zeros equal to the intersections with a small pushed-off copy. Thus

$$
\langle c_1(TX),[\Sigma]\rangle=2-2g(\Sigma)+\Sigma\cdot\Sigma,
$$

and the [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{2g(\Sigma)-2=\Sigma\cdot\Sigma-\langle c_1(TX),[\Sigma]\rangle.}
$$

Equivalently, since $c_1(K_X)=-c_1(TX)$, it is $2g-2=\Sigma\cdot(\Sigma+K_X)$. The derivation applies to a compact smooth complex curve; its complex orientation fixes all signs.
