<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [canonical bundle](../../../../../../canonical-bundle.md) of a [K3 surface](../../../../../../k3-surface.md) is trivial, so $c_1(TX)=0$. Apply the [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) with $g=3$:

$$
\boxed{\Sigma\cdot\Sigma=2\cdot3-2=4.}
$$

This is the [Self-intersection of a smooth curve on a K3 surface](../../../../../../self-intersection-of-a-smooth-curve-on-a-k3-surface.md).
