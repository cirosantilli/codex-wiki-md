<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the boundary-orientation convention in which $-p/q$ surgery on the unknot gives $L(p,q)$. The [negative continued fraction](../../../../../../negative-continued-fraction.md) is

$$
\frac73=3-\frac1{2-1/2}=[3,2,2]^-.
$$

Attach three [two-handles](../../../../../../two-handle.md) to a [zero-handle](../../../../../../zero-handle.md) along a chain of unknots, with adjacent [linking numbers](../../../../../../linking-number.md) $+1$ and framings $-3,-2,-2$. Equivalently, take the [plumbing of oriented disk bundles](../../../../../../plumbing-of-oriented-disk-bundles.md) over three [spheres](../../../../../../sphere.md) with [Euler numbers](../../../../../../euler-number-of-a-vector-bundle.md) $-3,-2,-2$. Successive [slam-dunk moves](../../../../../../slam-dunk-move.md) change the boundary surgery coefficient to $-3-1/(-2-1/(-2))=-7/3$, proving that its oriented boundary is $L(7,3)$ in this convention.

The zero sections are a basis of second [homology](../../../../../../homology-split.md), and the [intersection form](../../../../../../intersection-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{Q=\begin{pmatrix}-3&1&0\\1&-2&1\\0&1&-2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its successive leading principal minors are $-3,5,-7$. Equivalently those of $-Q$ are $3,5,7>0$, so [Sylvester's criterion](../../../../../../sylvester-s-criterion.md) proves that $Q$ is negative definite. This explicitly constructs the required filling.

If [lens spaces](../../../../../../lens-space.md) are instead labeled by positive surgery with its boundary orientation, use the chain with framings $-2,-4$, whose coefficient is $-7/4$; its boundary is orientation-preservingly the positive-surgery $L(7,3)$, since orientation reversal changes $q=4$ to $q=3$. Its negative-definite form is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-2&1\\1&-4\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. This is only a convention change in the named boundary, and specifies a valid filling in either labeling system.
