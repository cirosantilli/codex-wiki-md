<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $h=0$ in the even sextic [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). For $u>0$, near $r=0$ the nonzero stationary branch satisfies $M^2=-r/u+O(r^2)$ for $r<0$. The minimum value is $V_{\min}=-r^2/(4u)+O(r^3)$ below the transition and zero above. The [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) is continuous, and if $r$ crosses zero linearly with temperature, the first temperature derivative of the minimized [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md) is continuous too. There is no [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md); a second derivative has a finite jump at this level.

For $u<0$, solve the two conditions $V'(M)=0$ and $V(M)=V(0)$ with $M\ne0$. Setting $q=M^2$ gives $r+uq+vq^2=0$ and $r/2+uq/4+vq^2/6=0$. Eliminating $r$ gives

$$
\boxed{M^2=-\frac{3u}{4v},\qquad r=\frac{3u^2}{16v}.}
$$

Both the disordered and ordered minima have positive curvature there, but their values coincide. Crossing this [phase coexistence](../../../../../../phase-coexistence.md) boundary changes the [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) discontinuously. The [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) jumps; for $r=r(T)$ at fixed $u,v$, $\partial_TV_{\min}=r'(T)M^2/2$ jumps as well, generically yielding a nonzero [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md). Local extrema can persist past coexistence as [metastable](../../../../../../metastability.md) states, which explains hysteresis in a dynamically delayed transition. The [spinodal points](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md), where local curvature vanishes, do not locate the equilibrium transition.
