<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $\mathbb T^n=\mathbb R^n/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^n$ with normalized [Haar measure](../../../../../../haar-measure.md) and Fourier basis $e_k(x)=e^{ik\cdot x}$, $k\in\mathbb Z^n$. For any real $s$, the [periodic Sobolev space](../../../../../../periodic-sobolev-space.md) is

$$
\boxed{H_s(\mathbb T^n)=\left\{u\in\mathcal D'(\mathbb T^n):
\|u\|_s^2=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z^n}(1+|k|^2)^s|\widehat u(k)|^2<\infty\right\}.}
$$

Weighted [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) identify it isometrically with $\ell^2$, so it is a [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) and complete. This definition includes negative orders and distributions. For nonnegative integer $s$ the norm is equivalent to the sum of squared L2 norms of weak derivatives through order $s$.
