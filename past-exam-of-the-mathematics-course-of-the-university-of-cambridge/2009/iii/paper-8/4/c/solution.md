<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $X$ be the [character space of an algebra](../../../../../../character-space-of-an-algebra.md), consisting of nonzero multiplicative complex linear functionals on $A$. We construct the required [Gelfand transform](../../../../../../gelfand-representation.md) and prove its properties.

Characters exist. A maximal proper ideal exists by the axiom of choice and is closed: if it were dense it would contain an element within distance one of the identity, hence an invertible element, contradicting properness. The quotient is a complex unital Banach division algebra, and the [Gelfand-Mazur theorem](../../../../../../gelfand-mazur-theorem.md) identifies it with $\mathbb C$, producing a character. The same argument applied to the proper ideal generated by $a-\lambda$ proves

$$
\sigma_A(a)=\{\chi(a):\chi\in X\}.
$$

Every character has norm one, since its value belongs to the spectrum and is bounded by $\|a\|$, while $\chi(1)=1$. The character equations are weak-star closed in the dual unit ball. Thus [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) makes $X$ compact Hausdorff; separability of $A$ makes it metrizable.

The algebra is inverse-closed in $B(H)$. If $a\in A$ is invertible there, $a^*a\geq cI$ for some $c>0$. For $M=\|a^*a\|$, the [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md) for $(a^*a)^{-1}=M^{-1}[I-(I-a^*a/M)]^{-1}$ converges in $A$, since the positive contraction in brackets has norm at most $1-c/M<1$. Therefore $a^{-1}=(a^*a)^{-1}a^*\in A$. This proves [spectral permanence for C-star algebras](../../../../../../spectral-permanence-for-c-star-algebras.md) in the present concrete setting. In particular a self-adjoint element has real spectrum in $A$, so characters take real values on it. Decomposing $a$ into self-adjoint real and imaginary parts gives $\chi(a^*)=\overline{\chi(a)}$.

Define $\widehat a(\chi)=\chi(a)$. This is a unital star-homomorphism to $C(X)$. Every element of the abelian star-algebra is normal, and the [C-star identity](../../../../../../c-star-identity.md) gives $\|a^{2^j}\|=\|a\|^{2^j}$. The [spectral radius formula](../../../../../../spectral-radius-formula.md) therefore gives $r_A(a)=\|a\|$. The displayed spectrum identity now yields $\|\widehat a\|_\infty=\|a\|$, proving isometry and injectivity. Its image is closed because $A$ is complete, contains the constants, is closed under conjugation, and separates distinct characters. The [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) makes that image dense in $C(X)$, hence equal to it. **$\boxed{A\cong C(X)}$ isometrically as a star-algebra.**
