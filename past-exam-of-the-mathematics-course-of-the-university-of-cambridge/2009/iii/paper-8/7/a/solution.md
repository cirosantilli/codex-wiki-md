<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix a separable infinite-dimensional complex [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $\mathcal H$ and let

$$
U_{\mathcal K}=\{u\in B(\mathcal H):u\text{ unitary},\ u-I\text{ compact}\}
$$

with its operator-norm topology. The [odd topological K-theory](../../../../../../odd-topological-k-theory.md) group of a compact metric space is defined by continuous-map [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) classes

$$
\boxed{K^1(X)=[X,U_{\mathcal K}].}
$$

[Homotopies](../../../../../../homotopy.md) use the same norm topology. Its finite-dimensional stable realization and group operation are established in the following parts, so this definition does not yet assume that [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) classes form a group.
