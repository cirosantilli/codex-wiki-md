<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$, the defining finite sum for the [Hecke operator](../../../../../../hecke-operator.md) simplifies to

$$
T_pf=p^{k-1}f(pz)+\frac1p\sum_{b=0}^{p-1}f((z+b)/p).
$$

The first summand has no $q^1$ term. In the second, summing the $p$th roots of unity selects precisely the terms whose index is divisible by $p$, and its $q^1$ coefficient is $a_p$. Comparing with $T_pf=\lambda(p)f$, using $a_1=1$, gives $\boxed{\lambda(p)=a_p}$. This also follows from the general coefficient computation in part (a).
