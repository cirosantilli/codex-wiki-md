<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the normalized [Fricke involution](../../../../../../fricke-involution.md) $W_N=f\mapsto f|_k\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\N&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, and let $D_\ell=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\ell&0\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Choose integers $m,n$ with $m\ell-Nn=1$. The matrix $\gamma=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&n\\N&m\ell\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ lies in $\Gamma_1(N)$, and $\gamma'=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\ell&n\\N&m\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ has determinant one and lies in $\Gamma_0(N)$, with $m\equiv\ell^{-1}\pmod N$. The displayed identity in the PDF reads $D_\ell\gamma^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1,\ell)\gamma'$. Taking double cosets therefore identifies the reversed correspondence with the [diamond operator](../../../../../../diamond-operator.md) $\langle\ell^{-1}\rangle$ times the original one. Conjugation by $W_N$ reverses the two diagonal entries, so

$$
\boxed{W_NT_\ell W_N^{-1}=\langle\ell^{-1}\rangle T_\ell.}
$$

The same finite-correspondence change of variables in the [Petersson inner product](../../../../../../petersson-inner-product.md) transfers a matrix to its inverse and yields $T_\ell^*=\langle\ell^{-1}\rangle T_\ell$. The [diamond operators](../../../../../../diamond-operator.md) commute with $T_\ell$, so $T_\ell T_\ell^*=T_\ell^*T_\ell$. Thus $T_\ell$ is a [normal operator](../../../../../../normal-operator.md). Multiplying $W_N$ by a nonzero scalar does not change the conjugation formula.
