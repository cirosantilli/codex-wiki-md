<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Choose time $t_c$ when the passing [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) centres are at the same height, and let $S$ reflect positions in that horizontal plane. For each instantaneous configuration, [Stokes flow](../../../../../../stokes-flow-split.md) determines particle [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md) uniquely and linearly from the fixed external [forces](../../../../../../force.md). Time reversal reverses all [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md) and the dense [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md)'s applied [force](../../../../../../force.md); reflecting in the horizontal plane reverses that [force](../../../../../../force.md) again. The neutral [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) remains force-free under both operations. Thus the reflected time-reversed motion obeys the same original forcing.

At $t_c$, reflection fixes both particle centres. Since the [spheres](../../../../../../sphere.md) have no orientational shape variables and there is no contact, uniqueness of the particle-trajectory initial-value problem makes the reflected backward motion coincide with the forward motion:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r_j(t_c+\tau)=S\mathbf r_j(t_c-\tau),\qquad j=1,2.}
$$

This [reflection symmetry of a two-sphere passing trajectory](../../../../../../reflection-symmetry-of-a-two-sphere-passing-trajectory.md) fixes the transverse coordinates and reverses the relative axial separation. Consequently, at matching large separations above and below, **both [spheres](../../../../../../sphere.md) return to their original distances from the centreline**. Their intermediate lateral excursions are undone. This uses [kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](../../../../../../kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md), geometric reflection symmetry and the noncontact result, not a dissipation-minimizing trajectory. It assumes the [spheres](../../../../../../sphere.md) pass one another; a perfectly collinear approach would not supply the side-by-side symmetry event.
