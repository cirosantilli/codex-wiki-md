**False.** Take the finite, hence countable, [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $C_6=\langle a\mid a^6=1\rangle$. Its elements $a^3$ and $a^2$ have orders two and three. A [group embedding](../../../../../../group-embedding.md) preserves the order of every element, whereas [conjugate group elements](../../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md) have the same order: $(s^{-1}xs)^r=s^{-1}x^rs$. Therefore their images cannot be conjugate in any containing [group](../../../../../../group-split.md). This gives the required obstruction. The [torsion-free group](../../../../../../torsion-free-group.md) hypothesis in Question 5 avoids this conflict of element orders.
