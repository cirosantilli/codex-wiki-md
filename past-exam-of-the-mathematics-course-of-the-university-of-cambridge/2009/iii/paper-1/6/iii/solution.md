<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** We construct a two-generated countable [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) that cannot embed in any [finitely presented group](../../../../../../finitely-presented-group.md). Choose a subset $S\subseteq\mathbb N_{>0}$ which is not a [recursively enumerable set](../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md), for example a positive-integer coding of the complement of the [halting set](../../../../../../halting-set.md). Such sets exist because there are only countably many finite programs but uncountably many subsets of the positive integers. On generators $b_i$ indexed by $i\in\mathbb Z$, form the possibly infinite-graph [Right-angled Artin group](../../../../../../right-angled-artin-group.md)

$$
B_S=\langle b_i\ (i\in\mathbb Z)\mid[b_i,b_j]=1\text{ whenever }|i-j|\in S\rangle.
$$

The shift $\sigma(b_i)=b_{i+1}$ preserves the defining relations and has inverse shift $i\mapsto i-1$, so it is an automorphism. Form the [semidirect product](../../../../../../semidirect-product.md)

$$
G_S=B_S\rtimes_\sigma\langle t\rangle,\qquad tb_it^{-1}=b_{i+1}.
$$

Its base embeds by the definition of a [semidirect product](../../../../../../semidirect-product.md). Since $b_i=t^ib_0t^{-i}$, the group is generated by just $b_0,t$ and is countable.

The [shift-invariant graph groups encode arbitrary sets](../../../../../../shift-invariant-graph-groups-encode-arbitrary-sets.md) construction has the exact test

$$
\boxed{[b_0,t^nb_0t^{-n}]=1\text{ in }G_S\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n\in S\qquad(n>0).}
$$

The forward direction for $n\in S$ is a defining edge relation. If $n\notin S$, kill all vertex generators except $b_0,b_n$. Every defining commutator then either contains a killed generator or is trivial; no relation is imposed between these two retained generators. Thus there is a retraction of $B_S$ onto the rank-two [free group](../../../../../../free-group.md) on $b_0,b_n$. Their commutator is a nonempty reduced word there, so it is nonidentity in $B_S$ and remains so in $G_S$.

Now suppose $G_S$ embedded in a [finitely presented group](../../../../../../finitely-presented-group.md) $P$. Choose fixed words $u,v$ in its finite generators representing the images of $b_0,t$. The null words of $P$ are [recursively enumerable](../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md): enumerate all finite products of conjugates of its relators and their inverses, then freely reduce. Substituting $u,v$ into $[b_0,t^nb_0t^{-n}]$ and dovetailing these enumerations for all $n>0$ would enumerate exactly $S$, contradicting its choice. These finitely many image words may be hard-coded; the argument does not require an algorithm finding an embedding. This proves the [enumerable identities of finitely generated subgroups](../../../../../../enumerable-identities-of-finitely-generated-subgroups.md) obstruction and supplies a concrete construction of the required countable counterexample.
