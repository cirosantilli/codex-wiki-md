↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md), $G_N(z)=e^{\lambda(z-1)}$, so the aggregate has a [compound Poisson distribution](../../../../../../compound-poisson-distribution.md) and

$$
\boxed{\kappa_S(t)=\lambda\bigl(M_{X_1}(t)-1\bigr).}
$$

Three differentiations immediately give

$$
\boxed{\kappa_{S,3}=\mathbb E(S-\mathbb ES)^3=\lambda m_3=\lambda\mathbb EX_1^3.}
$$

The first two [cumulants](../../../../../../cumulant.md) are $\lambda m_1$ and $\lambda m_2$. If $\lambda>0$ and claim sizes are strictly positive, $m_3>0$, so **the paper's unstandardized skewness is strictly positive**. For the degenerate count $\lambda=0$, the aggregate is zero and the third [central moment](../../../../../../central-moment.md) is zero; strict positivity presumes a positive claim intensity.
