<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), $w=0$, the [constant-equation-of-state comoving density equation](../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-comoving-density-equation.md) reduces to $\Delta''+2\Delta'/\eta-6\Delta/\eta^2=0$. Substitution of $\Delta\propto\eta^s$ gives $s^2+s-6=(s-2)(s+3)=0$. Since $a\propto\eta^2$,

$$
\boxed{\Delta=C_+a+C_-a^{-3/2}.}
$$

In the growing mode, $a^2\bar\rho\Delta$ is constant, so the Poisson constraint makes $\phi$ constant for a nonzero Fourier mode. The momentum constraint and the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) give

$$
\mathcal H\phi=-4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho v=-\frac32\mathcal H^2v,
\qquad\boxed{v=-\frac{2\phi}{3\mathcal H}=-\frac{\phi\eta}{3}\propto a^{1/2}.}
$$

This is the [growing-mode velocity potential in matter domination](../../../../../../growing-mode-velocity-potential-in-matter-domination.md), in the question's convention where the peculiar velocity is $\nabla v$.
