<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The new [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) species undergoes [thermal decoupling in cosmology](../../../../../../thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md) when its interaction rate becomes comparable to the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md). Equating the given rates gives

$$
G_A^2T_D^5=\frac{1.66\sqrt{g_*}\,T_D^2}{m_{\mathrm{Pl}}},\qquad
T_D=\left(\frac{1.66\sqrt{g_*}}{G_A^2m_{\mathrm{Pl}}}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

With $g_*\simeq100$, $G_A=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}$ and $m_{\mathrm{Pl}}\simeq10^{19}\,\mathrm{GeV}$,

$$
\boxed{T_D\simeq(1.66\times10^6)^{1/3}\,\mathrm{GeV}\simeq1.2\times10^2\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

This is an order-of-magnitude electroweak-scale estimate under the given approximation. The mass in this formula is the unreduced [Planck mass](../../../../../../planck-mass.md), not the [reduced Planck mass](../../../../../../reduced-planck-mass.md).
