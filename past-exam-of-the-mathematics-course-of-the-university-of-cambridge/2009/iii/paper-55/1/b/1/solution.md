<h1 id="1/b/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A fixed comoving volume has physical volume proportional to $a^3$. The stretched [domain walls](../../../../../../../domain-wall.md) within it have area proportional to $a^2$, and their energy per physical area is constant. The [cosmological domain wall network](../../../../../../../cosmological-domain-wall-network.md) therefore has $\rho\propto a^{-1}$, equivalently a [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md) with $w=-2/3$.

The flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) becomes $\dot a^2=Ca$ for a positive constant $C$. On the expanding branch, integration gives $a=(C/4)(t-t_B)^2$. Measuring age from the initial time $t_B=0$, $H=\dot a/a=2/t$. Thus

$$
\boxed{H_0t_0=2.}
$$
