<h1 id="1/b/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Set $a(t)=a_0(t/t_0)^2$. The remaining future [conformal time](../../../../../../../conformal-time.md) is

$$
\eta_\infty-\eta_0=\int_{t_0}^\infty\frac{dt}{a(t)}=\frac{t_0}{a_0}.
$$

A light ray sent to a comoving reflector at separation $\chi$ and immediately returned consumes $2\chi$ of conformal time. The [cosmological round-trip communication horizon](../../../../../../../cosmological-round-trip-communication-horizon.md) therefore has supremum physical distance

$$
\boxed{d_{\max}=a_0\chi_{\max}=\frac{t_0}{2}=\frac1{H_0}.}
$$

Finite-time reception requires $d_0<d_{\max}$. Indeed the total travel equation is $2d_0/a_0=(t_0/a_0)(1-t_0/t_r)$, giving $t_r=t_0/(1-2d_0/t_0)$. At the limiting distance the return time tends to infinity. This round-trip radius is half the current physical [cosmological event horizon](../../../../../../../cosmological-event-horizon.md) radius.
