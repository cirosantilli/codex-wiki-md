<h1 id="1/a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For [pressureless matter](../../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho\propto a^{-3}$. The flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) then gives $a\propto t^{2/3}$, and [conformal time](../../../../../../../conformal-time.md) $d\eta=dt/a$ gives $a\propto\eta^2$. Choose the initial conformal time to be zero. A galaxy seen at emission time $\eta_e$ has comoving radius $\chi=\eta_0-\eta_e$ and [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) $1+z=(\eta_0/\eta_e)^2$.

Two opposite comoving galaxies are separated by $2\chi$. Direct one-way communication between their worldlines before emission requires the [particle horizon](../../../../../../../particle-horizon.md) $\eta_e$ to cover this separation: $2(\eta_0-\eta_e)\le\eta_e$. Thus the threshold is $\eta_e=2\eta_0/3$, and

$$
\boxed{z_c=\frac54\quad\text{for direct communication}.}
$$

The distinction in [direct cosmological communication and a shared causal past](../../../../../../../direct-cosmological-communication-and-a-shared-causal-past.md) matters here. If causal contact means that their past light cones overlap, the condition instead is $2\chi\le2\eta_e$, giving $\eta_e=\eta_0/2$ and $\boxed{z_c=3\text{ for a shared causal past}}$. These are different operational meanings of the horizon criterion; the boundary corresponds to the limiting initial-time contact.
