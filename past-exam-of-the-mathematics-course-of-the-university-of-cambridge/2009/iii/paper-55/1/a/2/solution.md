For [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), $\rho\propto a^{-4}$ by the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md). The flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $a\propto t^{1/2}$, so $a\propto\eta$ in [conformal time](../../../../../../../conformal-time.md). Hence $1+z=\eta_0/\eta_e$.

The opposite galaxies again have separation $2(\eta_0-\eta_e)$. Direct communication requires this to be at most $\eta_e$, so

$$
\boxed{z_c=\frac12\quad\text{for direct communication}.}
$$

For overlap of their past light cones, the criterion is instead $\eta_e\ge\eta_0/2$, giving $\boxed{z_c=1\text{ for a shared causal past}}$. The two notions are precisely those distinguished in [direct cosmological communication and a shared causal past](../../../../../../../direct-cosmological-communication-and-a-shared-causal-past.md).
