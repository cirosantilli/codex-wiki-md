<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [slow-roll equations](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md) give $H^2\simeq V/(3M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2)$ and $\dot\Phi\simeq-V'/(3H)$. Substituting in the stated [slow-roll curvature power spectrum](../../../../../../slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md) yields

$$
\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}=\frac{H^4}{4\pi^2\dot\Phi^2}
\simeq\frac{9H^6}{4\pi^2V'^2}
=\frac{V^3}{12\pi^2M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^6V'^2}.
$$

With the [potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) $\epsilon_V=M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2V'^2/(2V^2)$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}\simeq\frac{V}{24\pi^2M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^4\epsilon_V}
=\frac8{3\epsilon_V}\left(\frac{V^{1/4}}{\sqrt{8\pi}\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}}\right)^4.}
$$

All quantities are evaluated at horizon exit. The two forms have the same normalization because $(\sqrt{8\pi})^4=64\pi^2$.
