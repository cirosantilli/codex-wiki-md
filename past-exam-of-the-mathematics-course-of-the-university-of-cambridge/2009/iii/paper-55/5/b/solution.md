<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [inflaton](../../../../../../inflaton.md) has quantum fluctuations while their wavelengths are shorter than the [Hubble radius](../../../../../../hubble-radius.md). Choosing the [Bunch-Davies vacuum](../../../../../../bunch-davies-vacuum.md) fixes the short-wavelength positive-frequency modes. During near-de Sitter [slow-roll inflation](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md), expansion carries each mode through $k=aH$, leaving a field fluctuation of amplitude approximately $H/(2\pi)$ on spatially flat slices.

The [inflaton clock-shift origin of curvature perturbations](../../../../../../inflaton-clock-shift-origin-of-curvature-perturbations.md) converts this field displacement into a local time shift of magnitude $|\delta\Phi/\dot\Phi|$, and hence into a [comoving curvature perturbation](../../../../../../comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) of magnitude $H|\delta\Phi/\dot\Phi|$. For the single-field adiabatic growing mode on an attractor, the [superhorizon conservation of single-field comoving curvature](../../../../../../superhorizon-conservation-of-single-field-comoving-curvature.md) makes this perturbation approximately constant at $k\ll aH$. These vacuum-generated perturbations seed the later density fluctuations.
