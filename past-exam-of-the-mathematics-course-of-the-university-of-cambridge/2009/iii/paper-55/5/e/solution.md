<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Apply the horizon-exit derivative from part (d) to the [potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) and the [second potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../second-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md). The [potential slow-roll flow equations](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-flow-equations.md) are

$$
\frac{d\epsilon_V}{d\log k}\simeq4\epsilon_V^2-2\epsilon_V\eta_V,
\qquad\frac{d\eta_V}{d\log k}\simeq2\epsilon_V\eta_V-\xi_V^2,
$$

where the [third potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../third-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) is $\xi_V^2=M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^4V'V'''/V^2$. For example, differentiating $\eta_V$ gives $-M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^4(V'/V)(V'''/V-V''V'/V^2)$, which is the second expression. Differentiating $n_s-1\simeq-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V$ now gives the [running of the scalar spectral index](../../../../../../running-of-the-scalar-spectral-index.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dn_s}{d\log k}\simeq-6(4\epsilon_V^2-2\epsilon_V\eta_V)
+2(2\epsilon_V\eta_V-\xi_V^2)
=16\epsilon_V\eta_V-24\epsilon_V^2-2\xi_V^2.}
$$

This is the leading second-order slow-roll running. The notation $\xi_V^2$ denotes a signed derivative combination and does not force its value to be nonnegative.
