<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the scalar-field [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) on the expanding branch $H>0$:

$$
2H\dot H=\frac{\dot\Phi}{3M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2}(V'+\ddot\Phi).
$$

The scalar equation of motion gives $V'+\ddot\Phi=-3H\dot\Phi$. Canceling $H$ yields

$$
\boxed{2M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2\dot H=-\dot\Phi^2.}
$$

Thus the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) cannot increase for a canonical homogeneous scalar field. The mass scale here is the [reduced Planck mass](../../../../../../reduced-planck-mass.md).
