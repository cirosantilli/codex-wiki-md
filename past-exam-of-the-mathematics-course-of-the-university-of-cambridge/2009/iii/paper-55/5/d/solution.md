<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At horizon exit $k=aH$, $d\log k/dt=H(1-\epsilon_H)$, where $\epsilon_H=-\dot H/H^2$ is the [Hubble slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md). To leading slow-roll order,

$$
\frac{d}{d\log k}\simeq\frac{\dot\Phi}{H}\frac{d}{d\Phi}
\simeq-M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2\frac{V'}V\frac{d}{d\Phi}.
$$

The [slow-roll curvature power spectrum](../../../../../../slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md) is proportional to $V^3/V'^2$. Therefore the [scalar spectral index in potential slow-roll parameters](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index-in-potential-slow-roll-parameters.md) is

$$
n_s-1\simeq-M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2\frac{V'}V\left(3\frac{V'}V-2\frac{V''}{V'}\right)
=-3M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2\left(\frac{V'}V\right)^2+2M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2\frac{V''}V.
$$

Using the [potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) and the [second potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../second-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) gives

$$
\boxed{n_s\simeq1-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V.}
$$

The neglected horizon-exit derivative correction contributes at higher slow-roll order.
