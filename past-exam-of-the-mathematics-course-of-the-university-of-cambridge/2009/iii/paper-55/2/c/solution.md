<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the linear function, $F=-1/2$ and $RF-f=\Lambda$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{V=2\Lambda,\qquad\phi=\sqrt{3/2}\log(1/2).}
$$

The factor two follows from the paper's [conformal transformation](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) $\widetilde g=g/2$. Its vacuum equation is consequently $\widetilde G_{\mu\nu}=2\Lambda\widetilde g_{\mu\nu}$. Here $f''=0$ and the scalar is fixed: the [Einstein-frame representation of metric f(R) gravity](../../../../../../einstein-frame-representation-of-metric-f-r-gravity.md) degenerates to ordinary [general relativity](../../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) with a rescaled [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), and supplies no additional propagating scalar field.
