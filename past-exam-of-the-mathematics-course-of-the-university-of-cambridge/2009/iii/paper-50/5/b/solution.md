<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [unital quantum channel](../../../../../../unital-quantum-channel.md) preserves the identity operator: $\Phi(I)=I$. For equal input and output dimensions, this is equivalent to preserving the maximally mixed [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md).

For the [amplitude damping channel](../../../../../../amplitude-damping-channel.md),

$$
\Phi(I)=A_0A_0^\dagger+A_1A_1^\dagger
=\begin{pmatrix}1+p&0\\0&1-p\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus **the channel is not unital for $p>0$; it is unital only at $p=0$, when it is the identity channel.** In particular, it biases the [maximally mixed state](../../../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md) toward the atomic ground state. The trace-preservation condition involves $\sum_jA_j^\dagger A_j$, whereas unitality involves the opposite products $\sum_jA_jA_j^\dagger$.
