<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Interpret $t$ as the largest guaranteed radius for correcting all unknown-location [Pauli operators](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) of weight at most $t$ together. Products of two such errors have weight at most $2t$, so the [Knill--Laflamme condition](../../../../../../knill-laflamme-condition.md) holds when $2t<d$. Conversely, let $F$ be a minimum-weight [Pauli operator](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) with nonscalar compression $PFP$. If $d\leq2t$, split its support into two sets of size at most $t$ and write $F=E_a^\dagger E_b$, up to an irrelevant phase. These two admissible errors violate the [Knill--Laflamme condition](../../../../../../knill-laflamme-condition.md). Thus the [correctable Pauli error radius](../../../../../../correctable-pauli-error-radius.md) is

$$
\boxed{t=\left\lfloor\frac{d-1}{2}\right\rfloor,\qquad d\in\{2t+1,2t+2\}.}
$$

In particular, $d=2t+1$ requires the additional assumption that $d$ is odd. Distance is not uniquely determined by $t$ alone. Nor does the weight of a particular correctable error characterize this radius: particular higher-weight errors can also be correctable.

For a concrete even-distance example, the [stabilizer code](../../../../../../stabilizer-code.md) generated by $X^{\otimes4}$ and $Z^{\otimes4}$ has parameters $[[4,2,2]]$. Every single-qubit nonidentity [Pauli operator](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) anticommutes with a generator, so it has zero compression. But $Z_1Z_2$ commutes with both generators and acts nonscalarly on the code: it is unitary there and its code [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is zero. Hence the distance is two and its guaranteed radius is zero. This shows why an unconditional odd-distance formula would be incorrect.

Ignoring overall phases, an error of weight $j$ has $\binom nj$ choices of support and three choices, $X,Y,Z$, at each affected [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md). The identity is the weight-zero term, so

$$
\boxed{N(t)=\sum_{j=0}^t3^j\binom nj.}
$$

For a [nondegenerate quantum code](../../../../../../nondegenerate-quantum-error-correcting-code.md), distinct correctable Pauli errors have orthogonal error-image subspaces. Each has dimension $2^k$, since a [Pauli operator](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) is unitary. All $N(t)$ subspaces lie in the physical [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) of dimension $2^n$. Counting dimensions proves the [quantum Hamming bound](../../../../../../quantum-hamming-bound.md)

$$
N(t)2^k\leq2^n,
\qquad
\boxed{N(t)\leq2^{n-k}.}
$$

Degeneracy would allow distinct errors to share an error-image subspace, so this particular counting argument would then fail.
