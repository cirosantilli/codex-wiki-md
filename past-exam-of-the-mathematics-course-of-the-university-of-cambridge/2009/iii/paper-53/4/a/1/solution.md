↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**A secure key can be produced, at one ideal secret bit per copy.** The shared [Bell state](../../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) is pure, so the preceding factorization shows that Eve's purifying system is independent of it. Measuring both qubits in the [computational basis](../../../../../../../computational-basis.md) gives matching outcomes $00$ or $11$, each with [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. Neither party announces these key outcomes. Eve's state is the same for both possibilities, so each copy supplies a shared uniform private bit.
