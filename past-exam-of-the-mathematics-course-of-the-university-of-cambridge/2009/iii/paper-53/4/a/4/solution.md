<h1 id="4/a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**No secure key can be produced.** The off-diagonal terms in the two [Bell state](../../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) projectors cancel, giving the [separable quantum state](../../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md)

$$
\rho_{AB}=\tfrac12|00\rangle\langle00|+\tfrac12|11\rangle\langle11|.
$$

A possible [purification of a density operator](../../../../../../../purification-of-a-density-operator.md) is $(|00\rangle_{AB}|0\rangle_E+|11\rangle_{AB}|1\rangle_E)/\sqrt2$. Eve measures her register in the [computational basis](../../../../../../../computational-basis.md) and learns whether Alice and Bob have $00$ or $11$. Their apparently shared random bit is therefore completely known to Eve. Conditioned on her record the Alice–Bob state is a known [product state](../../../../../../../product-state.md); public discussion and local private randomness cannot create a shared secret unknown to that record. The state has no distillable secret key in the stated purification model.
