<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The relevant security model gives Eve the purifying system and access to the public transcript. A useful preliminary fact is that [purity decouples a subsystem from its purification](../../../../../purity-decouples-a-subsystem-from-its-purification.md). If the Alice–Bob marginal is $|u\rangle\langle u|$ and $|\Omega\rangle_{ABE}$ is a [purification of a density operator](../../../../../purification-of-a-density-operator.md), then for every $v\perp u$,

$$
\|(\langle v|_{AB}\otimes I_E)|\Omega\rangle\|^2=\langle v|\rho_{AB}|v\rangle=0.
$$

Hence $|\Omega\rangle_{ABE}=|u\rangle_{AB}|e\rangle_E$: Eve is uncorrelated with their joint [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md). A [Bell pair](../../../../../bell-pair.md) then supplies a uniform shared secret bit when both parties measure in the [computational basis](../../../../../computational-basis.md). Pure [product states](../../../../../product-state.md) have no shared random correlations, while a [mixed state](../../../../../mixed-state.md) must be assessed with its purifying information included. The classifications below use the usual [quantum key distribution](../../../../../quantum-key-distribution.md) setting with an authenticated public classical channel; no full security proof is required.
