<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define $\Pi^\mu{}_{\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ and $x_P=(x^0,-\mathbf x)$. In a conventional intrinsic-phase choice, the charged [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) transforms under [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) as

$$
\boxed{W^{+\mu}(x)\longmapsto-\Pi^\mu{}_{\nu}W^{-\nu}(x_P).}
$$

Thus its time component maps to minus the conjugate time component, and its spatial components map to plus the conjugate spatial components. Charge is exchanged, the Lorentz index undergoes [parity](../../../../../../parity.md), and the overall minus sign is a consistent phase convention shared with the charged-current transformation. The analogous formula exchanges $W^-$ with $W^+$.
