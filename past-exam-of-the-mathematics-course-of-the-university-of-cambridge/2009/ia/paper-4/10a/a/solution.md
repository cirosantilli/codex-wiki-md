<h1 id="10a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $|\mathbf v|<c$, define the [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-|\mathbf v|^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. A particle of [rest mass](../../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$ has

$$
\boxed{\mathbf p=\gamma m\mathbf v,\qquad E=\gamma mc^2.}
$$

These expressions give the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) directly:

$$
E^2-c^2\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf p=\gamma^2m^2c^4\left(1-\frac{|\mathbf v|^2}{c^2}\right)=m^2c^4,
$$

so $\boxed{E^2=c^2\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf p+m^2c^4}$.

The [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{P^\mu=(E/c,\mathbf p)=mU^\mu,\qquad U^\mu=\gamma(c,\mathbf v),}
$$

where $U^\mu$ is the [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md), the proper-time derivative of $(ct,\mathbf x)$. It is a [four-vector](../../../../../../four-vector.md), so its [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) norm is invariant under [Lorentz transformations](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Choose [metric signature](../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(+,-,-,-)$; in the particle's rest frame, $P^\mu=(mc,\mathbf0)$ and $P_\mu P^\mu=m^2c^2$. In any frame the same norm is $E^2/c^2-|\mathbf p|^2$. Equating the two yields the same [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md). With the opposite metric convention both norm expressions change sign, leaving the energy relation unchanged.
