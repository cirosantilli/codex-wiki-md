<h1 id="12a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With no electric field, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B.}
$$

For nonrelativistic motion, the [work-energy theorem](../../../../../../work-energy-theorem.md) gives

$$
\frac{dK}{dt}=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac12m|\mathbf v|^2\right)=m\dot{\mathbf v}\cdot\mathbf v=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v=q(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf v=0.
$$

Consequently $\boxed{K(t)=K(0)}$. The reason is geometric: [magnetic forces do no work](../../../../../../magnetic-forces-do-no-work.md) because they are perpendicular to the instantaneous [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). They can change its direction but not its magnitude. The same energy conclusion holds relativistically, using $dE/dt=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v$ and subtracting the constant rest energy to obtain [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
