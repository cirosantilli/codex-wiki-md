<h1 id="7a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7A](../7a.md)

Let $v\ne0$ be an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of the [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $A$, with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$. Multiplying $Av=\lambda v$ by $v^\dagger$ gives

$$
\lambda=\frac{v^\dagger Av}{v^\dagger v}.
$$

The denominator is the positive real number $\|v\|^2$, while

$$
\overline{v^\dagger Av}=v^\dagger A^\dagger v=v^\dagger Av.
$$

Thus the numerator is real, and **every [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is real**. This argument does not require distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
