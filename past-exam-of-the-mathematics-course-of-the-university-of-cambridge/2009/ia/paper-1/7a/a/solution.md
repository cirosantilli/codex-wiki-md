<h1 id="7a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_i,v_j$ for distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_i,\lambda_j$. Since the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real and $A$ is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md),

$$
\lambda_jv_i^\dagger v_j=v_i^\dagger Av_j=(Av_i)^\dagger v_j=\lambda_i v_i^\dagger v_j.
$$

Hence $(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)v_i^\dagger v_j=0$, so **[eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) for distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are [orthogonal vectors](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md)**. They are therefore [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md); after normalization the given $n$ [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of $\mathbb C^n$.
