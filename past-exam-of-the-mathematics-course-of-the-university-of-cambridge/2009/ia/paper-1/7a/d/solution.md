<h1 id="7a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

There is **no such universal constant**. For a real parameter $M$, consider the real [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
B_M=\begin{pmatrix}1&M\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(t-1)^2$, so both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real and $\lambda_{\max}(B_M)=1$. But

$$
\frac{x^\dagger B_Mx}{x^\dagger x}=1+\frac M2.
$$

For any proposed finite $C$, choosing $M>2(C-1)$ violates the proposed bound. This is an explicit illustration that [nonnormal eigenvalues do not bound a quadratic quotient](../../../../../../nonnormal-eigenvalues-do-not-bound-a-quadratic-quotient.md).
