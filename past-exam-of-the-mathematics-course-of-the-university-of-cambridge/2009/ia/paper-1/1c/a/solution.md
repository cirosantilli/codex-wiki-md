<h1 id="1c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $z=x+iy$ and $\alpha=p+iq$. The [complex conjugates](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) in the PDF give

$$
z\overline\alpha+\overline z\alpha=2\operatorname{Re}(z\overline\alpha)=2(px+qy).
$$

Since $(p,q)\ne(0,0)$, **the locus is the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) through the origin perpendicular to the position [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) of $\alpha$** in the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{z=it\alpha,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$
