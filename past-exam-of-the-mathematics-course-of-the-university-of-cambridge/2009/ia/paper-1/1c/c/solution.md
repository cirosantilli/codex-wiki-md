<h1 id="1c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [branch of the complex logarithm](../../../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) must be specified to obtain a unique locus. First use the [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md). Away from the negative real axis, write $z=re^{i\theta}$ with $r>0$ and $-\pi<\theta<\pi$. Then the [complex logarithms](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) of $z$ and its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) are $\log r+i\theta$ and $\log r-i\theta$. Equating real and imaginary parts gives $\log r=\theta$. Consequently **the principal-branch locus is the open segment of a [logarithmic spiral](../../../../../../logarithmic-spiral.md)**

$$
\boxed{z=e^{(1+i)\theta},\qquad -\pi<\theta<\pi.}
$$

The origin is excluded because its [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) does not exist. If the principal value is assigned argument $\pi$ on the negative real axis, a point there equals its own [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md), so its principal [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) would have to equal $i$ times itself. That forces the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) to be zero and cannot hold there; the endpoints remain excluded.

If the printed equation instead means that some values of the multivalued [complex logarithms](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) satisfy it, write those values as $\log r+it$ and $\log r+iu$, with $e^{it}=z/r$ and $e^{iu}=\overline z/r$. Equality gives $\log r=-u=t$, hence $u=-t$, which is compatible with conjugation. Every real $t$ is allowed. Under this interpretation **the locus is the entire [logarithmic spiral](../../../../../../logarithmic-spiral.md) $z=e^{(1+i)t}$, $t\in\mathbb R$**. The PDF does not choose between these branch conventions.
