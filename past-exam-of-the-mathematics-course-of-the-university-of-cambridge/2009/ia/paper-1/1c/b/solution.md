<h1 id="1c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $z=x+iy$. Dividing by two gives

$$
\sqrt{(x-a)^2+y^2}=x+a.
$$

The left side is nonnegative, so squaring is reversible only when $x+a\ge0$. Squaring yields $y^2=4ax$.

If $a>0$, this equation implies $x\ge0$, so the sign condition holds automatically. **The locus is the [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md)**

$$
\boxed{y^2=4ax,\qquad a>0,}
$$

with vertex at the origin, [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) $(a,0)$ and [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) $x=-a$. Indeed the original equation equates distance from the [focus of a conic](../../../../../../focus-of-a-conic.md) with distance from the [directrix of a conic](../../../../../../directrix-of-a-conic.md) on its right side.

If $a<0$, the squared equation forces $x\le0$, making $x+a<0$. This violates the sign condition. Thus **the locus is empty for $a<0$**; squaring without checking that condition would introduce a spurious [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md).
