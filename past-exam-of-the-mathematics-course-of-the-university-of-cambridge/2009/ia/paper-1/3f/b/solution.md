<h1 id="3f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Separate off the integer part of the phase. Set $d_n=\sqrt{n^2+n}-n$. Rationalization gives

$$
d_n=\frac1{\sqrt{1+1/n}+1}\longrightarrow\frac12.
$$

Since squaring removes the sign in $\cos(\pi n+u)=(-1)^n\cos u$, the given [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) satisfies $b_n=\cos^2(\pi d_n)$. The [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the cosine now gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}b_n=\cos^2(\pi/2)=0.}
$$
