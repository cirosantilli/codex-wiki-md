<h1 id="3f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Rationalizing avoids cancellation between the two large terms of the [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md):

$$
a_n=\frac{n}{n+\sqrt{n^2-n}}=\frac1{1+\sqrt{1-1/n}}.
$$

By [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the square root and the reciprocal at the limiting values,

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n=\frac12.}
$$
