<h1 id="8a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A member of both [groups](../../../../../../group-split.md) satisfies $A^TA=I$ and $A^TJA=J$. Multiplication of the second identity by $A$ on the left, using $AA^T=I$, gives $JA=AJ$. A real two-by-two [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) commuting with $J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ must be diagonal, since the two off-diagonal entries change sign in this equation. Its diagonal entries are $\pm1$ by the [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) identity, and the restriction $a>0$ forces the first to be $1$. Therefore **the intersection is the two-element [group](../../../../../../group-split.md)**

$$
\boxed{\{I,J\}=\{\operatorname{diag}(1,1),\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\}.}
$$
