<h1 id="2b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the complex Euclidean [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) and the [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) identity,

$$
\|Cx\|^2=x^\dagger C^\dagger Cx=x^\dagger CC^\dagger x=\|C^\dagger x\|^2.
$$

Both sides are nonnegative. Therefore **$\|Cx\|=0$ if and only if $\|C^\dagger x\|=0$**. This proves the stronger reusable fact that [normal matrices have equal adjoint norms](../../../../../../normal-matrices-have-equal-adjoint-norms.md).
