<h1 id="2b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $A$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), so is its [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md), and

$$
B^\dagger=A(A^\dagger)^{-1},\qquad BB^\dagger=A^{-1}A^\dagger A(A^\dagger)^{-1}.
$$

If $A$ is a [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md), substitute $A^\dagger A=AA^\dagger$ into the second identity to obtain $BB^\dagger=I$, so $B$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md). Conversely, if $B$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md), multiply $BB^\dagger=I$ on the left by $A$ and on the right by $A^\dagger$. This gives $A^\dagger A=AA^\dagger$, the defining [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) identity. Thus **$B$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) exactly when $A$ is a [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md)**.
