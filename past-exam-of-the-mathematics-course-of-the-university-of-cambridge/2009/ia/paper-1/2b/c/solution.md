<h1 id="2b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The shifted [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $N=D-\lambda I$ is still a [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md), since

$$
N^\dagger N=D^\dagger D-\lambda D^\dagger-\overline\lambda D+|\lambda|^2I,
$$

and $NN^\dagger$ has the same expansion except that its first term is $DD^\dagger$, which equals $D^\dagger D$. For the given [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), $Ne=0$. Part (b), applied to $N$, therefore gives $N^\dagger e=0$. Since $N^\dagger=D^\dagger-\overline\lambda I$,

$$
\boxed{D^\dagger e=\overline\lambda e.}
$$

The corresponding [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) is the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of the original [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
