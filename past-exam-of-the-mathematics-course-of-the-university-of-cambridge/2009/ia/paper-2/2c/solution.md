<h1 id="2c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2C](../2c.md)

The [characteristic roots](../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the homogeneous [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md) are $2$ and $3$. The forcing is resonant with the $e^{3x}$ solution, so the [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md) uses the particular solution $xe^{3x}$. Thus $y=Ae^{2x}+Be^{3x}+xe^{3x}$. The two initial conditions are $A+B=0$ and $2A+3B+1=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{y=e^{2x}+(x-1)e^{3x}.}
$$
