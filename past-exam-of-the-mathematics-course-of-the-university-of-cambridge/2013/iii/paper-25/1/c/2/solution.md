↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For $0\le s<t$, linearity of the [isonormal Gaussian process](../../../../../../../isonormal-gaussian-process.md) gives

$$
W_t-W_s=X(\mathbf1_{(s,t]})\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

The squared $L^2$ norm of this indicator is $t-s$, so part (a) yields

$$
\boxed{W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s).}
$$

Also, the [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md) formula gives $\operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=s\wedge t$, the [Brownian covariance kernel](../../../../../../../brownian-covariance-kernel.md). The Gaussian statement concerns the signed increment.
