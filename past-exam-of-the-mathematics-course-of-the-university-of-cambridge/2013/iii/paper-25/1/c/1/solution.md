<h1 id="1/c/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Apply the construction of part (a) to the real [Hilbert space](../../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $H=L^2([0,\infty),du)$, and set

$$
\boxed{W_t=X(\mathbf1_{[0,t]})\quad(t\ge0).}
$$

Choose $X(0)=0$. The interval of length zero represents the zero vector of $H$, so **$W_0=0$**. All variables are defined on the single [probability space](../../../../../../../probability-space.md) already used for the [isonormal Gaussian process](../../../../../../../isonormal-gaussian-process.md).
