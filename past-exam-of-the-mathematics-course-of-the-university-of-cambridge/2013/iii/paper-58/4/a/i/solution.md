<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $n\ge2$, the [Pauli X gate](../../../../../../../pauli-x-gate.md) and [Pauli Z gate](../../../../../../../pauli-z-gate.md) act on different [qubits](../../../../../../../qubit.md) in $X_0Z_1$, so they commute. Their product is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and squares to the [identity operator](../../../../../../../identity-operator.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) have modulus one; equivalently it is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md). Therefore **its [spectral norm](../../../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) is**

$$
\boxed{\|X_0Z_1\|=1.}
$$

The $n\ge2$ qualification is necessary because the qubit labelled one does not exist for $n=1$.
