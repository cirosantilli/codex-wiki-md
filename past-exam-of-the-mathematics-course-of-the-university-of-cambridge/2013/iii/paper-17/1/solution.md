<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**The divisor determines the bundle through its local equations.** Choose an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) on which $V$ has a reduced [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) defining equation $f_i$, using $f_i=1$ on sets disjoint from $V$. Because $V$ is a [complex submanifold](../../../../../complex-submanifold.md) of [codimension of a submanifold](../../../../../codimension-of-a-submanifold.md) one, its ideal is locally generated by a coordinate; consequently $f_i/f_j$ is a nowhere-zero [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on each overlap. Inside the [sheaf of meromorphic functions on a complex manifold](../../../../../sheaf-of-meromorphic-functions-on-a-complex-manifold.md), consider the [locally free sheaf](../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md) of rank one

$$
\mathcal O_M(V)|_{U_i}=f_i^{-1}\mathcal O_{U_i}.
$$

These local descriptions agree on overlaps. With $e_i=1/f_i$, its [transition functions of a vector bundle](../../../../../transition-function-of-a-vector-bundle.md) are specified by

$$
e_i=\frac{f_j}{f_i}e_j.
$$

The ratios satisfy the [Čech cocycle condition](../../../../../cech-cocycle-condition.md), and the associated [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) is $[V]$. The section $1=f_i e_i$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) and vanishes to order one along $V$. Replacing $f_i$ by $u_i f_i$, with $u_i$ a [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) unit, replaces the frame by $u_i^{-1}e_i$ without changing the subsheaf of meromorphic functions. Refining the cover changes no local sections either. This proves **choice independence up to isomorphism** rather than merely producing a bundle for one cover.

For a [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md) of dimension $n$, the [canonical bundle](../../../../../canonical-bundle.md) is

$$
\boxed{K_M=\bigwedge\nolimits^n(T^{1,0}M)^*.}
$$

It is the [holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md) of holomorphic top-degree [differential forms](../../../../../differential-form-split.md). To compute it on [Complex projective space](../../../../../complex-projective-space.md), let $S=\mathcal O(-1)$ be the [complex tautological line bundle](../../../../../complex-tautological-line-bundle.md), so that $S^*=[H]=\mathcal O(1)$ is the [hyperplane line bundle](../../../../../hyperplane-line-bundle.md). At a line $\ell\subset\mathbb C^{n+1}$, the [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) is $\operatorname{Hom}(\ell,\mathbb C^{n+1}/\ell)$. Tensoring the tautological quotient sequence by $S^*$ gives the [Euler sequence on complex projective space](../../../../../euler-sequence-on-complex-projective-space.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O\longrightarrow
\mathcal O(1)^{\oplus(n+1)}
\longrightarrow T^{1,0}\mathbb P^n
\longrightarrow0.
$$

Taking the top [exterior power](../../../../../exterior-power.md) gives $\det T^{1,0}\mathbb P^n=\mathcal O(n+1)$; taking its [dual bundle](../../../../../dual-bundle.md) yields

$$
\boxed{K_{\mathbb P^n}\cong[H]^{\otimes(-n-1)}.}
$$

Here a negative [tensor power](../../../../../tensor-power.md) means the corresponding positive [tensor power](../../../../../tensor-power.md) of the [dual bundle](../../../../../dual-bundle.md).

The proposed sections over $U$ form a [sheaf](../../../../../sheaf-mathematics.md): compatible maps into $L$ glue uniquely, and their projections remain $\phi$. Addition and multiplication by [holomorphic functions](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) are defined in the fibres of $L$, making this a sheaf of $\mathcal O_X$-modules. If $e_i$ is a [holomorphic local frame](../../../../../holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) of $L$ on $W_i\subset Y$, every section over $U\subset\phi^{-1}(W_i)$ has the unique expression

$$
\theta(x)=a(x)e_i(\phi(x)),\qquad a\in\mathcal O_X(U).
$$

Thus the sheaf is a [locally free sheaf](../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md) of rank one. Its [transition functions of a vector bundle](../../../../../transition-function-of-a-vector-bundle.md) are those of $L$ composed with $\phi$, so the corresponding [pullback vector bundle](../../../../../pullback-vector-bundle.md) is precisely $\phi^*L$. This proves **invertibility locally and compatibility globally**.

For the local [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](../../../../../blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md), work on the projective chart $Y_i\ne0$ and write

$$
u=x_i,\qquad t_j=Y_j/Y_i\quad(j\ne i).
$$

All the incidence equations reduce to $x_j=ut_j$. Therefore $(u,(t_j)_{j\ne i})$ gives a [holomorphic coordinate](../../../../../holomorphic-coordinate.md) chart isomorphic to $\mathbb C^n$. On an overlap with $Y_\ell\ne0$,

$$
u'=ut_\ell,\qquad t'_i=1/t_\ell,\qquad
t'_j=t_j/t_\ell\quad(j\ne i,\ell),
$$

which is a [biholomorphism](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) where $t_\ell\ne0$. The incidence subset is closed in the product of two Hausdorff spaces and is covered by these charts; hence it is a [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md) of dimension $n$. In each chart the exceptional fibre is $u=0$, so it is a smooth [complex submanifold](../../../../../complex-submanifold.md), with its induced projective coordinates giving

$$
\boxed{E\cong\mathbb P^{n-1}.}
$$

Away from the origin the inverse of the projection is $x\mapsto(x,[x])$, which is [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md); the projection is therefore a [biholomorphism](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) outside $E$.

To find the [canonical bundle](../../../../../canonical-bundle.md), pull back the nowhere-zero top form on $\mathbb C^n$. In the chart above, its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) gives

$$
\pi_1^*(dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n)
=\pm u^{n-1}\,du\wedge\bigwedge_{j\ne i}dt_j.
$$

The sign depends only on the ordering of the coordinates. This is a section of $K_X$ with [divisor on a complex manifold](../../../../../divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) exactly $(n-1)E$: it has that vanishing order in every exceptional chart and no zeros elsewhere. A nonzero [meromorphic section of a holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../meromorphic-section-of-a-holomorphic-line-bundle.md) with divisor $D$ identifies its [holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md) with $[D]$, by sending the local generator $1/f_i$ of $\mathcal O(D)$ to the nowhere-zero frame obtained by dividing the section by $f_i$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{K_X\cong[E]^{\otimes(n-1)}.}
$$

This also includes $n=1$, when the local map is an isomorphism and the exponent is zero.

For a general [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md), choose a coordinate neighbourhood $U$ of $P$, with $P$ sent to $0$, and replace $U$ by the inverse image of this coordinate neighbourhood in the local [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](../../../../../blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md). Glue this space to $M\setminus\{P\}$ along $U\setminus\{P\}$ using the preceding [biholomorphism](../../../../../biholomorphism.md). The resulting charts give a [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md) $\widetilde M$ and a [holomorphic map](../../../../../holomorphic-map.md) $\phi$; the exceptional fibre is $\mathbb P^{n-1}$ and the map is a [biholomorphism](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) off that fibre. For completeness, the local projection is proper because its inverse image over a compact subset is closed in that subset times compact [Complex projective space](../../../../../complex-projective-space.md). This ensures the gluing is Hausdorff: separate points with distinct images downstairs, and points over $P$ inside the local blowup. The atlas is second countable as well.

The top [exterior power](../../../../../exterior-power.md) of the differential defines a global [holomorphic bundle map](../../../../../holomorphic-bundle-map.md) $\phi^*K_M\to K_{\widetilde M}$. Equivalently, it is a section of $K_{\widetilde M}\otimes(\phi^*K_M)^{-1}$. The same local [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) has vanishing order $n-1$ on $E$, and the differential is invertible elsewhere. Applying the [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) construction gives the [canonical bundle formula for a point blowup](../../../../../canonical-bundle-formula-for-a-point-blowup.md)

$$
\boxed{K_{\widetilde M}\cong\phi^*K_M\otimes[E]^{\otimes(n-1)}.}
$$
