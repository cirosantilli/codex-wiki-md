<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A pure spin-one-half state on the equator of the [Bloch sphere](../../../../../../bloch-sphere.md) has $\langle Z_k\rangle=0$, so $|\alpha_k|^2=|\beta_k|^2=1/2$. Its azimuthal phase does not enter the [conditional environment overlap](../../../../../../conditional-environment-overlap.md), because the interaction is diagonal in $Z_k$. Substitution in the [decoherence factor](../../../../../../decoherence-factor.md) gives

$$
\boxed{z(t)=\prod_{k=1}^N\cos(2g_kt)}.
$$

The [Zurek spin-bath model](../../../../../../zurek-spin-bath-model.md) now has a real coherence factor: positive and negative values correspond to opposite relative phases, while suppression of coherence depends on $|z|$.
