<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With every bath spin up, the two conditional bath states in the [Zurek spin-bath model](../../../../../../zurek-spin-bath-model.md) differ only by global phases. The [decoherence factor](../../../../../../decoherence-factor.md) is

$$
\boxed{z(t)=e^{2it\sum_k g_k},\qquad |z(t)|^2=1}.
$$

There is **no quantum decoherence**, even for a very large bath. The device evolves as the pure state $a e^{it\sum g_k}|0\rangle+b e^{-it\sum g_k}|1\rangle$, while the bath stays in its original product [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) up to phase. Since no device information is imprinted in distinguishable bath states, the [conditional environment overlap](../../../../../../conditional-environment-overlap.md) has unit modulus. The phase rotation must not be mistaken for decay of off-diagonal magnitude.
