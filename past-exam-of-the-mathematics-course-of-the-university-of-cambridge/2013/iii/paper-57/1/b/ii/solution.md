<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the separated [stationary state](../../../../../../../stationary-state.md), restore its time factor $e^{-iEt/\hbar}$. Away from radial nodes, the [quantum phase](../../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) is $S=k\phi-Et/\hbar$, up to a constant $0$ or $\pi$ where the real radial function has fixed sign. The [guidance equation](../../../../../../../guidance-equation.md) in [plane polar coordinates](../../../../../../../plane-polar-coordinates.md) therefore gives the [Bohmian circulation of an angular-momentum eigenstate](../../../../../../../bohmian-circulation-of-an-angular-momentum-eigenstate.md)

$$
\boxed{v_r=0,\qquad v_\phi=\frac{\hbar k}{mr},\qquad |\mathbf v|=\frac{\hbar|k|}{mr}}.
$$

The direction is $\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}$ for $k>0$ and $-\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}$ for $k<0$; the velocity is zero for $k=0$. Each admissible trajectory is a circle:

$$
r(t)=r_0,\qquad \phi(t)=\phi_0+\frac{\hbar k}{m r_0^2}t.
$$

The [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) along the trajectory is $mr_0v_\phi=\hbar k$. The origin or any zero-amplitude circle is excluded from this local formula. For the real degenerate superposition constructed above, the spatial [quantum phase](../../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) is constant on each nodal sector, so its [Bohmian mechanics](../../../../../../../de-broglie-bohm-theory.md) velocity is instead zero. Thus the circular motion is a conclusion about the separated angular-momentum [eigenstate](../../../../../../../eigenstate.md), not an arbitrary energy [eigenstate](../../../../../../../eigenstate.md).
