<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the [Weierstrass M-test](../../../../../../weierstrass-m-test.md), absolute summability of the [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) makes $\sum_n\widehat f(n)e^{int}$ uniformly convergent to a continuous periodic function $H$. Termwise integration is justified by that [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md), and [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) gives

$$
\widehat H(k)=\widehat f(k)\qquad(k\in\mathbb Z).
$$

Part (ii) then implies $H=f$. Thus **the [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) converges uniformly to the original function**, not merely to some continuous limit. In particular,

$$
\boxed{\|S_Nf-f\|_\infty
\leq\sum_{|n|>N}|\widehat f(n)|\longrightarrow0.}
$$
