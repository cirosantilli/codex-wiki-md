<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The pure gravitational action is invariant under [diffeomorphisms](../../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md). An infinitesimal diffeomorphism generated by a compactly supported [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\xi$ changes the metric by its [tensor Lie derivative](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md),

$$
\delta g_{ab}=\mathcal L_\xi g_{ab}=\nabla_a\xi_b+\nabla_b\xi_a.
$$

Using symmetry of $E^{ab}$ and the variational expression already derived,

$$
0=\delta_\xi S_g=-2\int\sqrt{-g}\,E^{ab}\nabla_a\xi_b\,d^4x
=2\int\sqrt{-g}\,(\nabla_aE^{ab})\xi_b\,d^4x.
$$

As $\xi_b$ is arbitrary, the [off-shell metric divergence identity](../../../../../../../diffeomorphism-identity-for-a-metric-action.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla^aE_{ab}=0.}
$$

This [Noether identity](../../../../../../../noether-identity.md) follows for every [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) without imposing either the gravitational field equation or the matter equations. It is a consequence of the metric action's diffeomorphism invariance, not a conclusion requiring a long component calculation.
