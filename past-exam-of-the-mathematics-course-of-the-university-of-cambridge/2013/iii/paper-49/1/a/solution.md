<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\phi_s$ be the [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md) of the smooth [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $X$, with $\phi_0=\mathrm{id}$ and $\frac{d}{ds}\phi_s(p)=X_{\phi_s(p)}$. The [flow definition of the Lie derivative of a tensor field](../../../../../../flow-definition-of-the-lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\mathcal L_XT)_p=\left.\frac{d}{ds}\right|_{s=0}(\phi_s^*T)_p.}
$$

For a [tensor field](../../../../../../tensor-field.md) of type $(r,s)$, the [mixed tensor pullback](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-mixed-tensor-by-a-diffeomorphism.md) transports the tensor at $\phi_s(p)$ back to $p$: it applies $(d\phi_s)_p^{-1}$ to every contravariant factor and $(d\phi_s)_p^*$ to every covariant factor. Thus every difference quotient belongs to the same tensor space at $p$. This definition gives another tensor field of the same type and uses no choice of [affine connection](../../../../../../affine-connection.md). The [tensor Lie derivative](../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) is defined wherever the [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md) exists, including points where $X$ vanishes.
