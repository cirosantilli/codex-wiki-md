<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write the [covector field](../../../../../../one-form.md) as $\omega=\omega_\mu dx^\mu$. From the contraction and [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) properties,

$$
(\mathcal L_X\omega)(Y)=X(\omega(Y))-\omega([X,Y]).
$$

Take $Y=\partial_\mu$. The [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is $[X,\partial_\mu]=-(\partial_\mu X^\nu)\partial_\nu$, so

$$
\boxed{(\mathcal L_X\omega)_\mu
=X^\nu\partial_\nu\omega_\mu+\omega_\nu\partial_\mu X^\nu.}
$$

Equivalently, the covariant-factor contribution comes from $\mathcal L_Xdx^\mu=dX^\mu=(\partial_\nu X^\mu)dx^\nu$. The formula holds in any [coordinate basis](../../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) and is the one-form case of the [coordinate tensor Lie derivative](../../../../../../coordinate-tensor-lie-derivative.md).
