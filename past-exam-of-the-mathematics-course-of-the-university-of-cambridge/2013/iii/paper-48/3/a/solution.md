<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the [linearized cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../linearized-cosmological-continuity-equation.md), remembering the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of $1/a$:

$$
\ddot\delta_m=\frac{H}{a}\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_m-\frac1a\nabla\cdot\dot{\mathbf v}_m.
$$

Insert the linear [cosmological Euler equation](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation.md) for the [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md), giving

$$
\ddot\delta_m=\frac{2H}{a}\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_m+\frac1{a^2}\nabla^2\Phi
=-2H\dot\delta_m+4\pi G\bar\rho_m\delta_m.
$$

Thus the [linear matter perturbation growth equation](../../../../../../linear-matter-perturbation-growth-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ddot\delta_m+2H\dot\delta_m-4\pi G\bar\rho_m\delta_m=0}.
$$

The factor $2H$ includes one expansion term from continuity and one from momentum dilution. This equation uses sub-Hubble, pressureless linear perturbations and the stated matter-only source for the [gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md).
