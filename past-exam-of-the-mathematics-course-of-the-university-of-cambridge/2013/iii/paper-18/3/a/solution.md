<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a diagram $D:\mathcal J\to\mathcal C$, a [limit-preserving functor](../../../../../../limit-preserving-functor.md) carries every limiting [cone over a diagram](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) to a limiting [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md). A [limit-reflecting functor](../../../../../../limit-reflecting-functor.md) has the converse property: a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) is limiting whenever its image is limiting. A [limit-creating functor](../../../../../../limit-creating-functor.md) uniquely lifts every specified limiting [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) over the image diagram to a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) over $D$, and the lift is limiting. These definitions concern diagrams of the stipulated shape; creation includes the lifting requirement, not merely reflection.

Let $(L,(p_j))$ be a [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of $D$. The map

$$
\mathcal C(A,L)\longrightarrow\lim_j\mathcal C(A,Dj),\qquad h\longmapsto(p_jh)_j
$$

is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md): the right side consists exactly of compatible families of arrows from $A$, and the [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md)'s [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) gives their unique factorization through $L$. The [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) is induced by the [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) projections, so it proves that [covariant representables preserve limits](../../../../../../covariant-representables-preserve-limits.md), including the empty diagram and its [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md).
