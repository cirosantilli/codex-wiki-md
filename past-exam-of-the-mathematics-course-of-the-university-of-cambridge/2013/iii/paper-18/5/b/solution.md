↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [representation of a functor](../../../../../../representation-of-a-functor.md) $(A,x)$ for $G$ says precisely that for every object $(B,y)$ of $(1\downarrow G)$ there is a unique arrow $f:A\to B$ with $Gf(x)=y$. This is exactly the [initial object](../../../../../../initial-object.md) property for $(A,x)$ in that comma [category](../../../../../../category-split.md). Conversely, an [initial object](../../../../../../initial-object.md) supplies these unique arrows, hence the representing [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md) $\mathcal C(A,B)\cong GB$.
