↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [comma category](../../../../../../comma-category.md) $(X\downarrow G)$ has objects $(A,x)$ with $A\in\mathcal C$ and a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $x:X\to GA$. A [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) $(A,x)\to(B,y)$ is an arrow $f:A\to B$ such that $Gf\,x=y$. Identities and composition come from $\mathcal C$. When $X=1$, a map $1\to GA$ selects an element of $GA$, so this is the covariant [category of elements](../../../../../../category-of-elements.md) of $G$.
