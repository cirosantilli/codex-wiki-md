<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $m=2$ and $\lambda=(2,1)$, so $\lambda+\delta=(3,1)$. A transposition in $S_3$ has one singleton cycle and one two-cycle. The [Frobenius alternant character formula](../../../../../../frobenius-alternant-character-formula.md) therefore gives

$$
\chi^{(2,1)}((12))=[x_1^3x_2]\,(x_1+x_2)(x_1^2+x_2^2)(x_1-x_2)
=[x_1^3x_2]\,(x_1^4-x_2^4)=\boxed{0}.
$$

In the coordinate [permutation representation](../../../../../../permutation-representation.md) on $\mathbb C^3$, a transposition fixes one [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) vector, so its [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $1$. This representation is the direct sum of the invariant line of constant vectors and the [standard representation of the symmetric group](../../../../../../standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md), whose vectors have coordinate sum zero. Subtracting the trivial [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) gives $\chi_V((12))=1-1=0$, as required.
