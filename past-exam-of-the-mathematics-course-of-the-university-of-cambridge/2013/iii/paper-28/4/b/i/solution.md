<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Conditional on $\lambda$, a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) has both [conditional expectation](../../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) and [conditional variance](../../../../../../../conditional-variance.md) equal to $\lambda$. For the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(1,3)$,

$$
m_0=\mathbb E\lambda=2,\qquad
a=\operatorname{Var}(\lambda)=\frac{(3-1)^2}{12}=\frac13,\qquad
v=\mathbb E\lambda=2.
$$

Thus $K=v/a=6$ and the one-year [credibility factor](../../../../../../../credibility-factor.md) is $Z_1=1/7$. With the observed count equal to one, the [Bühlmann credibility estimate](../../../../../../../buhlmann-credibility-premium.md) of the next year's conditional claim [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widehat m_1=\frac17\cdot1+\frac67\cdot2=\frac{13}{7}\approx1.85714.}
$$

The estimate gives relatively little weight to one year because the [expected process variance](../../../../../../../expected-process-variance.md) is six times the [variance of hypothetical means](../../../../../../../variance-of-hypothetical-means.md).
