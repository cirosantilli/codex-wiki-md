<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Choose a [positive system of a root system](../../../../../positive-system-of-a-root-system.md) and the corresponding [triangular decomposition of a Lie algebra](../../../../../triangular-decomposition-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $L=\mathfrak n^-\oplus H\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. A [primitive element of a Lie algebra representation](../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) of [weight](../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $\omega\in H^*$ is a nonzero vector $v$ with $hv=\omega(h)v$ for every $h\in H$ and $\mathfrak n^+v=0$. Thus it is a [highest-weight vector](../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md); the choice of positive roots is part of this definition. A [highest-weight representation](../../../../../highest-weight-representation.md) is generated by such a vector.

Let $\mathfrak b=H\oplus\mathfrak n^+$ be the corresponding [Borel subalgebra](../../../../../borel-subalgebra.md). Define its one-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) $\mathbb C_\omega$ by the scalar $\omega(h)$ on $H$ and zero on $\mathfrak n^+$. This respects the [Lie bracket](../../../../../lie-bracket.md), since $[\mathfrak b,\mathfrak b]\subseteq\mathfrak n^+$. The induced [Verma module](../../../../../verma-module.md)

$$
M(\omega)=U(L)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\mathbb C_\omega
$$

is nonzero: the [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](../../../../../poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) identifies its underlying [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) with $U(\mathfrak n^-)$, and $v=1\otimes1$ is a primitive element of weight $\omega$. Its [weight spaces](../../../../../weight-space.md) are finite dimensional, its top [weight space](../../../../../weight-space.md) is the line $\mathbb Cv$, and all other [weights](../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) are $\omega-\sum n_i\alpha_i$ with $n_i\ge0$ and at least one positive coefficient.

A proper [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) cannot contain $v$, because $v$ generates $M(\omega)$. More strongly it has no component of weight $\omega$: any finite sum of distinct [weight vectors](../../../../../weight-vector.md) can be projected onto its individual components by a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in a generic element of $H$. Therefore the sum $N$ of all proper submodules still misses the top weight line and is proper. It contains every proper submodule, so the [irreducible quotient of a Verma module](../../../../../irreducible-quotient-of-a-verma-module.md)

$$
\boxed{L(\omega)=M(\omega)/N}
$$

is irreducible and retains the nonzero primitive element $v+N$. This constructs the requested representation for every $\omega\in H^*$. It is not asserted to be finite dimensional for arbitrary $\omega$.

For the [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md), use $h=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$, $e=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $f=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, with $[h,e]=2e$, $[h,f]=-2f$, $[e,f]=h$. The [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](../../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md) gives one irreducible $V_n$ for every integer $n\ge0$. In a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $v_0,\ldots,v_n$ its action is

$$
hv_j=(n-2j)v_j,\qquad fv_j=v_{j+1},\qquad ev_j=j(n-j+1)v_{j-1},
$$

where $v_{-1}=v_{n+1}=0$. These formulas satisfy the three [Lie brackets](../../../../../lie-bracket.md). Any nonzero invariant subspace contains a [weight vector](../../../../../weight-vector.md) by [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) projection using $h$; repeated application of $e$ gives $v_0$, and applications of $f$ then give the entire [basis](../../../../../basis.md). Thus $V_n$ is irreducible.

Conversely, in any finite-dimensional irreducible module, start with an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $h$ and apply $e$ until reaching a nonzero vector $v$ with $ev=0$. This process terminates because $e$ increases the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) by two and only finitely many [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) occur. Write $hv=av$. The [sl2 highest-weight lowering formula](../../../../../sl2-highest-weight-lowering-formula.md) gives

$$
hf^jv=(a-2j)f^jv,\qquad ef^jv=j(a-j+1)f^{j-1}v.
$$

Let $f^nv\ne0$ and $f^{n+1}v=0$, which again follows from the finite set of [weights](../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). Applying $e$ to the latter identity yields $(n+1)(a-n)f^nv=0$, so $a=n$. The resulting $n+1$ distinct [weight vectors](../../../../../weight-vector.md) span an invariant subspace, hence the entire irreducible module. Therefore **$\dim V_n=n+1$ and its primitive weight is $\omega_n(h)=n$**, with $n=0,1,2,\ldots$; its weights are $n,n-2,\ldots,-n$.
