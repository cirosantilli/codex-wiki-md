<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A homogeneous static saddle minimizes $f(\rho)=-\mu\rho+g\rho^2/2$ for $\rho=|\psi|^2\ge0$. With $g>0$ and $\mu>0$,

$$
\boxed{\rho_0=\mu/g,\qquad\psi_0=\sqrt{\rho_0}e^{i\phi_0},\qquad\phi_0\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z).}
$$

The mean-field [Bose-Einstein condensate](../../../../../../bose-einstein-condensate.md) has a macroscopic coherent occupation of the uniform mode. Its continuous [particle-number phase symmetry](../../../../../../particle-number-phase-symmetry.md) is $\psi\mapsto e^{i\chi}\psi$, the [circle group](../../../../../../circle-group.md) $U(1)$ generated by particle number. Choosing one phase breaks it, and the saddle manifold is a circle. For $\mu\le0$, the constrained minimum is the vacuum $\rho_0=0$, so the condensed saddle requires the stated positive-density regime.

[Spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) is understood through a phase-selected thermodynamic description: an exact finite-volume number [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) has vanishing field expectation. The resulting [Goldstone boson](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) is a gapless phase/sound mode, while phase [gradients](../../../../../../gradient.md) carry the [superfluid velocity](../../../../../../superfluid-velocity.md) $\mathbf v=\nabla\phi/m$. This is the saddle-point conclusion requested, not a proof of true condensate order in every dimension or temperature. Long-wavelength phase fluctuations can invalidate the assumed order, as quantified below.
