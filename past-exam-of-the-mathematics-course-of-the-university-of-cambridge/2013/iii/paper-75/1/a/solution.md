<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [spin one-half](../../../../../../spin-one-half.md) states $|\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\rangle$ with $S^z=\pm1/2$, so $\sigma^i=2S^i$ and $H=-J\sum_n(\sigma_n^x\sigma_{n+1}^x+g\sigma_n^z)$. At $g=0$, every ferromagnetic bond has its lowest energy $-J$ when adjacent $x$ spins agree. On the connected periodic chain the normalized [ground states](../../../../../../ground-state.md) are

$$
\boxed{|{+x}\rangle^{\otimes N},\quad|{-x}\rangle^{\otimes N},\qquad E_0=-NJ,}
$$

where $|\pm x\rangle=(|\uparrow\rangle\pm|\downarrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Their span is the two-dimensional [ground-state subspace](../../../../../../ground-state-subspace.md); any normalized superposition is another [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md). They are exchanged by the global spin-flip [discrete symmetry](../../../../../../discrete-symmetry.md) generated by $\prod_n\sigma_n^z$.

As $g\to+\infty$, the field dominates and selects

$$
\boxed{|\uparrow\rangle^{\otimes N},\qquad E_0\sim-NJg.}
$$

The limiting polarized state is unique. At large finite $g$, exchange admixes virtual spin flips, so the product state is the limiting [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) rather than an exact finite-field [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md). The bulk expansion below gives $E_0/N=-J[g+1/(4g)+O(g^{-3})]$. The exact twofold degeneracy stated at $g=0$ should be distinguished from thermodynamic [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) in the ordered phase: a finite chain at nonzero field can have a split pair of symmetry [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md).
