<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Hellmann–Feynman theorem](../../../../../../hellmann-feynman-theorem.md) gives $\partial_gE_0=\langle\partial_gH\rangle=-2J\sum_n\langle S_n^z\rangle$. Differentiating the [Ising-chain ground-state energy](../../../../../../ising-chain-ground-state-energy.md) yields $\partial_gE_0=-J\sum_k(g-\cos k)/r_k$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_n\langle S_n^z\rangle=\frac12\sum_k\frac{g-\cos k}{r_k}=\sum_k\frac{J(g-\cos k)}{\epsilon_k}.}
$$

This is also obtained from the occupations of the [Bogoliubov quasiparticle](../../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) vacuum: $\langle c_k^\dagger c_k\rangle=[1-(g-\cos k)/r_k]/2$ and $S_n^z=1/2-n_n$. At an exact zero mode the finite-sector limiting [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) specifies the occupation. In the bulk limits, the [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) per site is zero at $g=0$, tends to $1/2$ as $g\to\infty$, and equals $1/\pi$ at $g=1$: there the integrand $(1-\cos k)/r_k=|\sin(k/2)|$.
