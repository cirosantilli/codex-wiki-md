<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the critical field,

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_k=4J|\sin(k/2)|\sim2J|k|\quad(k\to0).}
$$

The [quasiparticle](../../../../../../../quasiparticle.md) gap closes and the low-energy branch is linear, unlike a massive excitation. This is the [Ising-chain quantum critical point](../../../../../../../ising-chain-quantum-critical-point.md) of the [Transverse-field Ising model](../../../../../../../transverse-field-ising-model.md); the cusp in the nonnegative dispersion represents the two opposite propagation directions.
