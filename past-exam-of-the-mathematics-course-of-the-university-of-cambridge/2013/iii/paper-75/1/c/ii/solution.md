<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding $r_k$ at large positive $g$ gives $r_k=g-\cos k+\sin^2k/(2g)+O(g^{-2})$, so

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_k=2Jg-2J\cos k+O(J/g).}
$$

The branch is strongly gapped and relatively flat, with width approaching $4J$ against a mean energy of order $2Jg$. These are field-polarized spin-flip [quasiparticles](../../../../../../../quasiparticle.md) with a small relative exchange modulation. Averaging the expansion over [momentum](../../../../../../../momentum.md) also gives $E_0/N=-J[g+1/(4g)+O(g^{-3})]$.
