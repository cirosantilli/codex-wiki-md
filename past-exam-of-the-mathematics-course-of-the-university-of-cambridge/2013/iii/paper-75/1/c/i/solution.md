<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At zero field, $r_k=1$ and **$\epsilon_k=2J$ for every $k$**. The [Ising-chain quasiparticle dispersion](../../../../../../../ising-chain-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) is flat: the bulk quadratic excitation costs no momentum-dependent energy. In spin language these are domain-wall excitations. A periodic finite spin configuration has an [even number](../../../../../../../even-number.md) of domain walls, so the single bulk [quasiparticle](../../../../../../../quasiparticle.md) energy $2J$ does not imply an allowed isolated one-wall spin state; the first such closed-chain spin excitation costs $4J$.
