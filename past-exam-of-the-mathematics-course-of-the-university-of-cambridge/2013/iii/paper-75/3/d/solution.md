<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The low-energy localized-well basis gives the [tight-binding model](../../../../../../tight-binding.md)

$$
H_{\rm eff}=E_{\rm well}\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|-\Delta\epsilon\sum_n(|n+1\rangle\langle n|+|n\rangle\langle n+1|).
$$

A [Bloch state](../../../../../../bloch-state.md) with coefficients $e^{ipna}$ is an [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) because the two neighbouring coefficients add to $2\cos(pa)e^{ipna}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_p=E_{\rm well}-2\Delta\epsilon\cos(pa)\simeq\hbar\omega/2-2\Delta\epsilon\cos(pa).}
$$

Here $p$ is a Bloch [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md); physical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) is $\hbar p$, and writing $p$ for physical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) would require $pa/\hbar$. Spectral evolution of these [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) is exactly the Fourier integral in the preceding solution. The negative hopping lowers the symmetric $p=0$ state, and coherent [quantum tunnelling](../../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) spreads a formerly degenerate family of localized levels into a band of width $4\Delta\epsilon$. Using the printed positive Euclidean prefactor instead would give on-site energy $-\hbar\omega/2$, showing its contradiction directly.
