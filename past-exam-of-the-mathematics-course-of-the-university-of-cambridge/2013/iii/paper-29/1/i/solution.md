<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [causal time-series representation](../../../../../../causal-time-series-representation.md) uses only the present and past driving [white noise](../../../../../../white-noise.md). Thus the coefficient condition is

$$
\boxed{a_r=0\quad\text{for }r<0.}
$$

The series must have its stated convergence meaning. For centered [white noise](../../../../../../white-noise.md) of positive finite [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md), $\sum_{r\geq0}|a_r|^2<\infty$ is sufficient and necessary for [mean-square convergence](../../../../../../convergence-in-l2.md). In the usual stable-filter convention one imposes the stronger $\sum_{r\geq0}|a_r|<\infty$. A bilateral stationary [linear process](../../../../../../linear-process-time-series.md) need not be causal: terms with $r<0$ involve future driving values.
