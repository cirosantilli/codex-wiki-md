[True state dependence](../../../../../../../true-state-dependence.md), also called [true contagion](../../../../../../../true-state-dependence.md), means that a previous outcome changes the distribution of a subsequent outcome after controlling both measured [covariates](../../../../../../../covariate.md) and persistent [unobserved heterogeneity](../../../../../../../unobserved-heterogeneity.md). For example, an earlier successful week may make later success more likely through habit formation. A lagged-outcome coefficient in an inadequate [statistical model](../../../../../../../statistical-model-split.md) can also reflect omitted subject differences, so positive observed persistence alone does not distinguish [true state dependence](../../../../../../../true-state-dependence.md) from [unobserved heterogeneity](../../../../../../../unobserved-heterogeneity.md).
