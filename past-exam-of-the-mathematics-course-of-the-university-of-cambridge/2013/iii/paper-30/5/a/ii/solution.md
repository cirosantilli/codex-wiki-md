[Unobserved heterogeneity](../../../../../../../unobserved-heterogeneity.md) is persistent variation between subjects arising from unmeasured characteristics. A subject-specific [latent variable](../../../../../../../latent-variable.md) or [random intercept](../../../../../../../random-intercept.md) can represent it. Subjects with high latent success propensities tend to succeed repeatedly, so their observed outcomes can have positive [serial correlation](../../../../../../../serial-correlation.md) even when outcomes are conditionally independent given that propensity. Apparent persistence therefore need not imply [true state dependence](../../../../../../../true-state-dependence.md).
