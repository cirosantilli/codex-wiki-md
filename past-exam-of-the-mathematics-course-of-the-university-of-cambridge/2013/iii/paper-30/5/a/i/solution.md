[Observed heterogeneity](../../../../../../../observed-heterogeneity.md) is variation in outcome propensities explained by measured [covariates](../../../../../../../covariate.md), such as age or sex. In [logistic regression](../../../../../../../logistic-regression.md), subjects with different recorded predictor values may have different success [probabilities](../../../../../../../probability.md), even before allowing for their previous outcomes. This is heterogeneity visible through observed predictors.
