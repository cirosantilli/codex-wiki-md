<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Full column [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) makes $X^TX$ a [positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md), and its [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse.md) is symmetric. Thus the [hat matrix](../../../../../../hat-matrix.md) satisfies

$$
P^T=X\bigl((X^TX)^{-1}\bigr)^TX^T=P,
\qquad P^2=X(X^TX)^{-1}(X^TX)(X^TX)^{-1}X^T=P.
$$

Also $PX=X$ and the image of $P$ is contained in the [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md) of $X$. These identities show that **$P$ is the [orthogonal projection matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) onto the [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md) of $X$**.
