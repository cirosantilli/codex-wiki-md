<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Measurements from the same person share baseline strength and likely share an individual response to training. An [ordinary linear model](../../../../../../normal-linear-model.md) with independent errors treats all 600 readings as independent conditional on week; that fails to represent their within-person [correlation](../../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md). The [regression residuals](../../../../../../regression-residual.md) can remain associated even when the population mean is linear in time. A [random intercept](../../../../../../random-intercept.md) captures differences in baseline strength, and a [random slope](../../../../../../random-slope.md) can additionally describe differences in progress.
