<h1 id="1/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $m$ be a positive integer and let the [trigonometric polynomial](../../../../../../../trigonometric-polynomial.md) $t_n$ have frequencies only in $[-n,n]$. For every $j\ge n$, its [Fourier partial sum](../../../../../../../fourier-partial-sum.md) is the [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) itself: $s_j(t_n)=t_n$. Every term in the defining average of the [de la Vallée Poussin sum](../../../../../../../de-la-vallee-poussin-sum.md) therefore equals $t_n$, giving

$$
\boxed{v_{n,m}(t_n)=t_n}.
$$

This is exact reproduction of the degree-at-most-$n$ [trigonometric polynomials](../../../../../../../trigonometric-polynomial.md), irrespective of the positive averaging length $m$.
