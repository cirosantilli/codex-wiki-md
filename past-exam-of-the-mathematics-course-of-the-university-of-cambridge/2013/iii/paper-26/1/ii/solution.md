<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The equal rooted counts imply that the [self-avoiding walk generating function](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk-generating-function.md) is $Z_G(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}\sigma_nx^n$. The [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) identifies the radius of a [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) $\sum a_nx^n$ as the reciprocal of $\limsup|a_n|^{1/n}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{R_G=\mu^{-1}}.
$$

Equivalently, the [root test](../../../../../../root-test.md) gives convergence when $x\mu<1$ and divergence when $x\mu>1$. At the positive boundary $x=\mu^{-1}$, the preceding infimum formula gives $\sigma_n\mu^{-n}\geq1$, so this particular series diverges there as well.
