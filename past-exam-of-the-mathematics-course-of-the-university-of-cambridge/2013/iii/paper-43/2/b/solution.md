<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For spontaneous breaking of exact global [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), at least one [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) does not annihilate the vacuum. Its norm in the preceding expression is positive, hence

$$
\boxed{E_{\mathrm{vac}}>0\quad\text{for a spontaneously broken global supersymmetric vacuum}.}
$$

With canonical [kinetic terms](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md), this is also seen in the nonnegative [scalar potential](../scalar-potential.md), $V=\sum_i|F_i|^2+\tfrac12\sum_aD_a^2$: nonzero auxiliary expectation values signal breaking and positive energy density. The associated massless fermion is the [Goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md).

If “broken” instead means arbitrary explicit breaking by added operators, the exact [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) no longer fixes the full Hamiltonian, and the positive-norm argument does not constrain its vacuum energy. One can, for example, shift that explicitly broken Hamiltonian by a constant. The strict positivity conclusion therefore uses spontaneous breaking of an otherwise exact global theory, not a blanket assertion about all breaking terms. It also is not a statement about [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md), whose scalar potential contains additional terms.
