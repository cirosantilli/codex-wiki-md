<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Every global minimum found for nonzero $\lambda$ has $F_+=F_0=F_-=D=0$. By [energy positivity in global supersymmetry](../../../../../../energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md), its zero energy means all [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) annihilate it. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{Supersymmetry is unbroken throughout the vacuum family}.}
$$

The arbitrary neutral expectation $v$ is a supersymmetric modulus; the nonzero derivatives that would break [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) vanish even though $v$ itself need not vanish. In the exceptional $\lambda=0$ case, every [D-flatness](../../../../../../d-flatness.md) minimum likewise has zero F- and D-auxiliaries, so [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) remains unbroken even on the gauge-Higgsed branch. The [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md) permits internal gauge breaking without supersymmetry breaking. No extra constant or linear superpotential term is needed to find a zero-energy vacuum in this model.
