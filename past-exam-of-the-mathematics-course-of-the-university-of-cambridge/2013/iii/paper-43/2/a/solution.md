<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Sum the diagonal spinor entries of the [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) anticommutator. Since $\operatorname{tr}\sigma^i=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}\sigma^0=2$, the Hamiltonian is

$$
H=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}.
$$

For a normalized vacuum, [energy positivity in global supersymmetry](../../../../../../energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md) follows from

$$
\boxed{E_{\mathrm{vac}}=\frac14\sum_\alpha
\left(\|Q_\alpha|\mathrm{vac}\rangle\|^2+
\|Q_\alpha^\dagger|\mathrm{vac}\rangle\|^2\right)\geq0.}
$$

In the unbroken case, all [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) annihilate the vacuum, giving **$E_{\mathrm{vac}}=0$**. Conversely, zero energy forces each nonnegative norm to vanish, so the vacuum is supersymmetric. The algebra fixes the additive zero of energy here. For an infinite homogeneous vacuum, use a finite-volume regulator and interpret the result as its [vacuum energy](../../../../../../vacuum-energy.md) density.
