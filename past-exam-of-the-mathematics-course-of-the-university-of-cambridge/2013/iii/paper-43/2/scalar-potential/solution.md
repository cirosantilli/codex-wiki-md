<h1 id="2/scalar-potential/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar potential](../scalar-potential.md)

Take canonical charged [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) kinetic terms, gauge coupling $e>0$, and no [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md), since none is specified. Elimination of the three complex [auxiliary fields](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) gives

$$
F_+^*=-\lambda\varphi_0\varphi_-,\qquad
F_0^*=-\lambda\varphi_+\varphi_-,\qquad
F_-^*=-\lambda\varphi_+\varphi_0.
$$

The Abelian gauge [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) obeys $D=-e(|\varphi_+|^2-|\varphi_-|^2)$ in this normalization. Thus the [F-term scalar potential](../../../../../../f-term-scalar-potential.md) and the gauge [D-term](../../../../../../d-term.md) give

$$
\boxed{V=|\lambda|^2\left(|\varphi_0|^2|\varphi_-|^2
+|\varphi_+|^2|\varphi_-|^2+|\varphi_+|^2|\varphi_0|^2\right)
+\frac{e^2}{2}\left(|\varphi_+|^2-|\varphi_-|^2\right)^2.}
$$

All terms are nonnegative. The normalization of $e$ can be changed together with the vector-field normalization, but the relative charges and the zero-potential conditions cannot. A noncanonical [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) would change the inverse-metric factors in the F-term potential; adding a [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) would shift $D$ and define a different model. Neither is silently introduced here.
