<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Acting on the column $(x,1)^T$ gives the faithful [Matrix Lie group](../../../../../matrix-lie-group.md) representation

$$
\begin{pmatrix}e^\alpha&\beta\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}e^\alpha x+\beta\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Multiplication and inversion are

$$
(\alpha,\beta)(a,b)=(\alpha+a,\beta+e^\alpha b),\qquad
(\alpha,\beta)^{-1}=(-\alpha,-e^{-\alpha}\beta).
$$

Thus $G$ is the [real affine group](../../../../../orientation-preserving-affine-group-of-the-real-line.md). Differentiating the matrices at the identity gives its [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md)

$$
\mathfrak g=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&0\end{pmatrix}:a,b\in\mathbb R\right\}.
$$

With

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

the matrix [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) is **$[D,T]=T$**, and $[D,D]=[T,T]=0$.

The left [Maurer-Cartan form](../../../../../maurer-cartan-form.md) and its right analogue are

$$
\theta_L=g^{-1}dg
=\begin{pmatrix}d\alpha&e^{-\alpha}d\beta\\0&0\end{pmatrix}
=D\,d\alpha+T\,e^{-\alpha}d\beta,
$$



$$
\theta_R=dg\,g^{-1}
=\begin{pmatrix}d\alpha&d\beta-\beta\,d\alpha\\0&0\end{pmatrix}
=D\,d\alpha+T(d\beta-\beta\,d\alpha).
$$

For a constant $h$, left translation leaves $\theta_L$ unchanged, and right translation leaves $\theta_R$ unchanged. Taking their dual [vector fields](../../../../../vector-field.md) gives

$$
\boxed{D_L=\partial_\alpha,\qquad T_L=e^\alpha\partial_\beta},
\qquad
\boxed{D_R=\partial_\alpha+\beta\partial_\beta,\qquad T_R=\partial_\beta}.
$$

The first pair consists of [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md); the second consists of [right-invariant vector fields](../../../../../right-invariant-vector-field.md). These are the [invariant frames of the real affine group](../../../../../invariant-frames-of-the-real-affine-group.md). Computing their [Lie brackets of vector fields](../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) yields

$$
[D_L,T_L]=T_L,\qquad [D_R,T_R]=-T_R.
$$

All brackets of a field with itself vanish. The sign difference is necessary: evaluation at the identity identifies [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) with the matrix [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) as a homomorphism, whereas [right-invariant vector fields realize the opposite Lie algebra](../../../../../right-invariant-vector-fields-realize-the-opposite-lie-algebra.md). Equivalently, $\xi\mapsto-\xi_R$ is a homomorphism for the same matrix [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md). There is no convention in which these particular coordinate fields both have the positive structure constant while retaining the usual [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md).

The [Maurer-Cartan equation](../../../../../maurer-cartan-equation.md) gives the same check. If $\theta_L=D\ell^D+T\ell^T$, then

$$
d\ell^D=0,\qquad d\ell^T=-\ell^D\wedge\ell^T.
$$

For $\theta_R=Dr^D+Tr^T$, instead

$$
dr^D=0,\qquad dr^T=r^D\wedge r^T.
$$

Thus the left equation is $d\theta_L+\theta_L\wedge\theta_L=0$ and the right equation is $d\theta_R-\theta_R\wedge\theta_R=0$, explaining the opposite bracket signs directly.

Finally, the two given matrix curves are $\exp(\epsilon T)$ and $\exp(\epsilon D)$. Their left action on a general group element is

$$
g_1(\epsilon)g(\alpha,\beta)=g(\alpha,\beta+\epsilon),\qquad
g_2(\epsilon)g(\alpha,\beta)=g(\alpha+\epsilon,e^\epsilon\beta).
$$

Differentiating at $\epsilon=0$ gives $T_R$ and $D_R$ respectively. Hence **[Left translations on a Lie group](../../../../../left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md) are generated by [right-invariant vector fields](../../../../../right-invariant-vector-field.md)**. More generally, the velocity of $\exp(t\xi)g$ is $(dR_g)_e\xi$, which is right-invariant because left and right multiplication commute. In the other direction, the flows $g\exp(t\xi)$ generate [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) and are [right translations on a Lie group](../../../../../left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md).
