<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The geometric object underlying an $SU(2)$ [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) is a [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) on a [principal bundle](../../../../../principal-bundle.md) $P\to M$ with structure group [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md). The base $M$ is spacetime, or an oriented [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md) in the Euclidean formulation. A choice of local section identifies each fibre with [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) and produces a local [gauge potential](../../../../../gauge-field.md). Such a choice is a choice of internal frame; changing it produces a [gauge equivalence of principal connections](../../../../../gauge-equivalence-of-principal-connections.md). The bundle specifies the global gluing of these frames, while the [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) specifies how frames at neighbouring points are compared.

The [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) group consists of $2\times2$ [unitary matrices](../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) of [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) one. Its [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) consists of traceless [Skew-Hermitian](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) matrices. For definiteness use

$$
t_a=-\frac{i}{2}\sigma_a,\qquad
[t_a,t_b]=\epsilon_{abc}t_c,\qquad
-\operatorname{tr}(t_at_b)=\frac12\delta_{ab},
$$

where the $\sigma_a$ are [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md). The [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) fixes all signs below. The positive invariant inner product on this [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is $-\operatorname{tr}(XY)$. Absorb the coupling into the [gauge potential](../../../../../gauge-field.md), so the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) in the defining two-dimensional representation is $D=d+A$ with $A=A^at_a$. A convention using Hermitian [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) instead moves factors of $i$ and the coupling into $D$; the underlying [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) is the same geometric data.

A [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) can be given by a [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md)-valued [differential one-form](../../../../../one-form.md) $\mathcal A$ on $P$ satisfying

$$
\mathcal A(\xi_P)=\xi,\qquad
R_h^*\mathcal A=\operatorname{Ad}_{h^{-1}}\mathcal A.
$$

Here $\xi_P$ is the vertical vector generated by the right group action. The first identity recovers vertical motion and the second makes the construction independent of a fibre frame. Its kernel is the [horizontal distribution of a principal connection](../../../../../horizontal-distribution-of-a-principal-connection.md), complementary to the vertical tangent spaces. A curve is horizontal when $\mathcal A$ annihilates its velocity, so a [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) defines [parallel transport](../../../../../parallel-transport.md) along curves in $M$.

For a local section $s$, the [local principal connection form](../../../../../local-principal-connection-form.md) is $A=s^*\mathcal A$. Change the section to $s'=sh$, with $h:U\to SU(2)$. Differentiating $sh$ has a translated horizontal part and a vertical part from $dh$. The two defining identities for the [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) therefore give

$$
\boxed{A'=h^{-1}Ah+h^{-1}dh}.
$$

On overlapping trivializations this is exactly the compatibility law for the [local principal connection forms](../../../../../local-principal-connection-form.md). In particular, the [gauge potentials](../../../../../gauge-field.md) need not glue as ordinary globally defined [differential one-forms](../../../../../one-form.md) on $M$. On a nontrivial [principal bundle](../../../../../principal-bundle.md) there need not be any global section with respect to which one could write a single matrix-valued $A$.

An active [gauge equivalence of principal connections](../../../../../gauge-equivalence-of-principal-connections.md) is generated by a bundle automorphism covering the identity on $M$ and commuting with the right [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) action. Its local description has the same transformation formula, but its local group-valued functions must satisfy transition compatibility on overlaps. This distinguishes the structure group [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) from the full [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md) of a fixed bundle. Two descriptions related by a passive change of section encode the same [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md); two connections related by such an automorphism lie in the same physical [gauge orbit](../../../../../gauge-orbit.md). The space of [principal connections](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) on a fixed bundle is affine: the difference of two connection forms is a [differential one-form](../../../../../one-form.md) with values in the [adjoint bundle](../../../../../adjoint-bundle.md), since their inhomogeneous transformation terms cancel.

The [curvature of a principal connection](../../../../../curvature-of-a-principal-connection.md) is

$$
\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\tfrac12[\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A].
$$

It is horizontal and equivariant, hence descends to a [differential two-form](../../../../../2-form.md) on $M$ valued in the [adjoint bundle](../../../../../adjoint-bundle.md). In a local section the [gauge curvature](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) becomes

$$
F=dA+A\wedge A
=\frac12F_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu\wedge dx^\nu,\qquad
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu+[A_\mu,A_\nu].
$$

The product $A\wedge A$ combines the [exterior product](../../../../../exterior-product.md) with matrix multiplication. The [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) term is the specifically non-Abelian interaction. Substitution of the transformation of $A$, using $d(h^{-1})=-h^{-1}(dh)h^{-1}$, cancels all derivatives of $h$ and gives

$$
\boxed{F'=h^{-1}Fh}.
$$

Thus the [gauge curvature](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) transforms tensorially even though the [gauge potential](../../../../../gauge-field.md) does not. Geometrically, the [curvature of a principal connection](../../../../../curvature-of-a-principal-connection.md) measures the failure of the [horizontal distribution of a principal connection](../../../../../horizontal-distribution-of-a-principal-connection.md) to be integrable; physically it is the non-Abelian [gauge field strength](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md).

Matter fields are sections of [associated vector bundles](../../../../../associated-vector-bundle.md). A field in the defining representation of [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) is a section of $E=P\times_{SU(2)}\mathbb C^2$. Its two local components satisfy $\psi'=h^{-1}\psi$ under the section convention above. The induced [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) obeys

$$
D'\psi'=(d+A')(h^{-1}\psi)=h^{-1}(d+A)\psi.
$$

This covariance is why replacing ordinary derivatives by [gauge covariant derivatives](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) makes the [kinetic terms](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) compatible with changes of internal frame. Applying the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) twice yields $D^2\psi=F\psi$, so the [gauge curvature](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) also measures the noncommutativity of covariant differentiation. Other [group representations](../../../../../group-representation.md) give other [associated vector bundles](../../../../../associated-vector-bundle.md) and replace $A$ by its image under the differential of the representation.

For an adjoint-valued [differential form](../../../../../differential-form-split.md) $\Phi$ of degree $r$, set

$$
D_A\Phi=d\Phi+A\wedge\Phi-(-1)^r\Phi\wedge A.
$$

Expansion of $F=dA+A\wedge A$, using $d^2=0$, proves the [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](../../../../../gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{D_AF=dF+A\wedge F-F\wedge A=0}.
$$

This is an identity for every [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md), independent of any field equation. It should be distinguished from the dynamical [Yang-Mills equations](../../../../../yang-mills-equations.md).

To obtain dynamics, equip $M$ with a [Riemannian metric](../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) and an orientation, and let $*$ be its [Hodge star operator](../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md). With the [Skew-Hermitian](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) normalization above, the positive Euclidean [Yang-Mills action](../../../../../yang-mills-action.md) is

$$
S[A]=-\frac1{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}\int_M\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge *F).
$$

The [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and the [Hodge star operator](../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md) make this independent of the choice of local section. The minus sign compensates for the negative trace form on [Skew-Hermitian](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) matrices. Varying the [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) gives $\delta F=D_A\delta A$, and therefore

$$
\delta S=-\frac{2}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}
\int_M\operatorname{tr}(D_A\delta A\wedge *F).
$$

For compactly supported variations, or on a closed base, integration by parts gives the [Yang-Mills equations](../../../../../yang-mills-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{D_A*F=0}.
$$

In components these are $D_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0$. The [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](../../../../../gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) provides the complementary geometric identity $D_AF=0$. Coupling matter adds the associated gauge current to the dynamical equation; the transformation law of the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) ensures covariance of that current as well.

The [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) also supplies nonlocal observables. Along a path $\gamma$, [parallel transport](../../../../../parallel-transport.md) is determined by

$$
\frac{d\psi}{dt}+A(\dot\gamma)\psi=0,\qquad
U_\gamma=\mathcal P\exp\left(-\int_\gamma A\right).
$$

The ordering is necessary because the [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) matrices at different points need not commute. The resulting [Wilson line](../../../../../wilson-line.md) transforms as $U_\gamma'=h(\gamma(1))^{-1}U_\gamma h(\gamma(0))$. For a closed path, its [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is a [Wilson loop](../../../../../wilson-loop.md), independent of the frame at the basepoint. These observables record the [holonomy of a connection](../../../../../holonomy.md). A [flat principal connection](../../../../../flat-principal-connection.md) has trivial [holonomy of a connection](../../../../../holonomy.md) around sufficiently small contractible loops but may have nontrivial [holonomy of a connection](../../../../../holonomy.md) around noncontractible loops. Thus vanishing local [gauge curvature](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) need not eliminate global gauge information.

The global topology becomes particularly visible in four dimensions. For a closed oriented four-dimensional base, [Chern-Weil theory](../../../../../chern-weil-homomorphism.md) gives the [Second Chern number](../../../../../second-chern-number.md) of the defining [associated vector bundle](../../../../../associated-vector-bundle.md):

$$
k=c_2(E)[M]=\frac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The plus sign here uses [Skew-Hermitian](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) curvature and the mathematical second-Chern convention: expanding $\det(I+iF/(2\pi))$ with $\operatorname{tr}F=0$ gives precisely the displayed [Second Chern form](../../../../../second-chern-form.md). A convention defining a physics topological charge with a leading minus sign instead calls that charge $-k$. Neither choice changes the absolute-value action bound.

The [Second Chern number](../../../../../second-chern-number.md) is independent of the [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) on a fixed bundle. Indeed, the [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](../../../../../gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) implies

$$
\delta\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)
=2d\,\operatorname{tr}(\delta A\wedge F),
$$

whose integral vanishes on a closed base by [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md). A nonzero [Second Chern number](../../../../../second-chern-number.md) obstructs a global trivialization: for a globally defined $A$, $\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)=d\,\operatorname{tr}(A\wedge dA+\tfrac23A\wedge A\wedge A)$, so its integral on a closed base would be zero. On $S^4$, gluing two trivial bundles over four-balls uses a transition map $S^3\to SU(2)$. Since [SU(2) as the three-sphere](../../../../../su-2-as-the-three-sphere.md) identifies the target with $S^3$, such gluing maps have integer [degree of a map between oriented manifolds](../../../../../degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md), illustrating how distinct topological sectors arise.

In four Euclidean dimensions, the [Hodge star operator](../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md) squares to one on [differential two-forms](../../../../../2-form.md). Decompose $F=F_++F_-$ with $*F_\pm=\pm F_\pm$. Define the nonnegative squared norms $a_\pm=-\int\operatorname{tr}(F_\pm\wedge *F_\pm)$. Orthogonality gives

$$
S=\frac{a_++a_-}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2},\qquad
8\pi^2k=-a_++a_-,
\qquad
\boxed{S\ge\frac{8\pi^2}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}|k|}.
$$

Equality means that one component vanishes, giving the [self-dual Yang-Mills equations](../../../../../self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) or the [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations](../../../../../anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md). Such finite-action solutions are [Yang-Mills instantons](../../../../../yang-mills-instanton.md). They solve the full [Yang-Mills equations](../../../../../yang-mills-equations.md) because $D_A*F=\pm D_AF=0$. With the second-Chern convention just specified, anti-self-dual curvature has $k\ge0$ and self-dual curvature has $k\le0$. On noncompact spacetime one must impose suitable decay or boundary conditions before treating the topological integral as an integer and using the same bound without boundary terms.

**Local [gauge potentials](../../../../../gauge-field.md), matter [gauge covariant derivatives](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md), [gauge field strength](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md), [parallel transport](../../../../../parallel-transport.md) and [Second Chern number](../../../../../second-chern-number.md) are different manifestations of one [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md).** The [principal bundle](../../../../../principal-bundle.md) preserves the global information that a single local [gauge potential](../../../../../gauge-field.md) cannot capture; the [Yang-Mills action](../../../../../yang-mills-action.md) then selects its physical dynamics.
