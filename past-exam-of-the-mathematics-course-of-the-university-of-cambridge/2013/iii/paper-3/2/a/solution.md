<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Extend each element of $G$ to fix $\omega$. A [one-point extension of a permutation group](../../../../../../one-point-extension-of-a-permutation-group.md) is a transitive permutation group $G^+\leq S_{\Omega^+}$ such that

$$
\boxed{G^+_\omega=G,}
$$

with the induced action on $\Omega$ equal to the prescribed action. The [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) equality is the substantive condition: merely adjoining an element that moves $\omega$ does not suffice. If $|\Omega|=n$, the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives $|G^+|=(n+1)|G|$.
