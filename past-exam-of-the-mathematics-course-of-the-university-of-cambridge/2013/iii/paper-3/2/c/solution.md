<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $1\leq t\leq|\Omega|$, a group is [sharply t-transitive](../../../../../../sharp-t-transitivity.md) when any two ordered $t$-tuples of distinct points are related by exactly one group element. Equivalently its action on the set of such tuples is regular. In the finite case

$$
\boxed{|G|=n(n-1)\cdots(n-t+1),}
$$

and the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of an ordered $t$-tuple is trivial. The condition includes both existence and uniqueness, not just transitivity.
