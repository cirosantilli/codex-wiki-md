↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Report **the six treatment-combination means and their uncertainty**, together with useful marginal [treatment contrasts](../../../../../../treatment-contrast.md), [standard errors](../../../../../../standard-error.md) and [confidence intervals](../../../../../../confidence-interval.md). Means are needed to interpret direction and practical importance, while the fitted [interaction term](../../../../../../interaction-term.md) determines whether one factor's effects should be reported separately at levels of the other. For the pairwise marginal comparisons in part (c), ordinary model-based 95% [Student t confidence intervals](../../../../../../student-t-confidence-interval.md) use $t_{6,0.975}$ times the corresponding [standard error](../../../../../../standard-error.md); a simultaneous set of comparisons needs an appropriate adjustment.

Also describe orchard selection, actual [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md), treatment delivery, numbers of trees observed, missing data and response units. Check orchard-level residuals for unequal [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md), outliers or systematic patterns using a [residual-versus-fitted plot](../../../../../../residual-versus-fitted-plot.md) and an appropriate distributional diagnostic. Explain that twelve orchards provide independent treatment [replication](../../../../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md); 360 tree measurements provide subsampling precision. No numerical treatment means or numerical [confidence intervals](../../../../../../confidence-interval.md) can be recovered from the supplied [sums of squares in ANOVA](../../../../../../sum-of-squares-in-anova.md) alone.
