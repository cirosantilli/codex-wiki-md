<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under the usual [normal linear model](../../../../../../normal-linear-model.md) for independent orchard means with common error [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md), the treatment ratios have null [F-distributions](../../../../../../f-distribution.md) with denominator six [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). Their upper-tail [P-values](../../../../../../p-value.md) are approximately

$$
p_S=0.08754,\qquad p_P=0.17798,\qquad p_{SP}=0.47622.
$$

Use the unrounded ratios when evaluating these [P-values](../../../../../../p-value.md). Thus **none of spray, pruning or their [interaction term](../../../../../../interaction-term.md) is significant at a 5% [significance level](../../../../../../significance-level.md)**. Spray gives modest evidence if a 10% [significance level](../../../../../../significance-level.md) was chosen in advance, but this is not strong evidence.

**Failure to reject does not establish absence of treatment effects.** Only two orchards per combination and six residual [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) leave considerable uncertainty and potentially low [statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md). The table alone cannot give the direction or magnitude of individual effects: that requires treatment means. These model-based [F-tests](../../../../../../f-test.md) also rely on appropriate orchard allocation and comparable residual variation; they should not be interpreted as unconditional conclusions from the table alone.
