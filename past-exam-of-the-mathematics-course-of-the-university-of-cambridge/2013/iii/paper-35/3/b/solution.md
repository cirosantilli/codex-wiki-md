<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**First agree the scientific question and primary [response variable](../../../../../../response-variable.md).** Ask the vet about the disease's course, the proposed mechanism and duration of each treatment, the eligible population, baseline severity, and what outcome and follow-up time would represent worthwhile improvement. Is the disease transmissible, is recovery reversible, and can treating one cow affect another's outcome? These answers determine whether a [crossover design](../../../../../../crossover-design.md) is credible, whether the [experimental unit](../../../../../../experimental-unit.md) should be a cow or a whole herd, and whether individual [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md) would leave interference between groups. Agree welfare and rescue arrangements with the vet when deciding which comparisons are feasible; the statistical plan cannot determine these from an unfamiliar disease name.

**Second agree feasible allocation and adequate independent [replication](../../../../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md).** Discuss numbers of available cows and herds, variation in the chosen outcome, a scientifically meaningful treatment difference, and the desired [statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md). Use these to plan [sample size](../../../../../../sample-size.md), rather than choosing a number solely from convenience. Discuss herd, lactation stage and initial severity as potential [blocks in experimental design](../../../../../../blocks-in-experimental-design.md), then specify [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md), comparable management, concealed allocation and blinded outcome assessment where feasible. Repeated milk or health records from the same cow are [observational units](../../../../../../observational-unit.md), not extra independent [experimental units](../../../../../../experimental-unit.md); [pseudoreplication](../../../../../../pseudoreplication.md) would give misleading [standard errors](../../../../../../standard-error.md). Availability of enough independent cows or herds, together with expected variation, governs the precision actually achievable.
