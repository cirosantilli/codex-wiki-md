<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An **[orthogonal block design](../../../../../../orthogonal-block-design.md) has treatment contrasts [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to block contrasts after removing the grand mean**. This is a statement about vectors on the [experimental units](../../../../../../experimental-unit.md), with the usual [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md); the two full spaces are not orthogonal because both contain the constant vector.

For a counting criterion, let $n_{ij}$ count [experimental units](../../../../../../experimental-unit.md) receiving [treatment](../../../../../../treatment.md) $i$ in block $j$, let $r_i=\sum_jn_{ij}$, let $k_j=\sum_in_{ij}$, and let $n=\sum_ir_i$. The inner product of the centered indicators for treatment $i$ and block $j$ is $n_{ij}-r_ik_j/n$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{n_{ij}=\frac{r_i k_j}{n}\quad\text{for every }i,j.}
$$

In particular, equal-sized blocks must contain each [treatment](../../../../../../treatment.md) in the same proportion. Under an additive block-and-treatment model, adjustment for blocks then does not change the fitted [treatment contrasts](../../../../../../treatment-contrast.md).
