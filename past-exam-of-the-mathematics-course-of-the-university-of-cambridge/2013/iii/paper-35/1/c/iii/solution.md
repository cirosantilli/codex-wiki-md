<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The constant subspace has one [statistical degree of freedom](../../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). Day contrasts have $5-1=4$, and the [within-block ANOVA stratum](../../../../../../../within-block-anova-stratum.md) has $50-5=45$. Because the [orthogonal block design](../../../../../../../orthogonal-block-design.md) puts all four independent dose contrasts in that last [ANOVA stratum](../../../../../../../anova-stratum.md), the residual has $45-4=41$ [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md).

| Stratum | Source | Degrees of freedom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mean | Grand mean | 1 |
| Between days | Days | 4 |
| Within days | Dose | 4 |
| Within days | Residual | 41 |
| Uncorrected total | All observations | 50 |

**The corrected total has 49 [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md); dose is tested against the within-day residual.** The quantitative dose scale also permits the dose component to be split into linear, quadratic, cubic and quartic [orthogonal polynomial contrasts](../../../../../../../orthogonal-polynomial-contrast.md), each with one [statistical degree of freedom](../../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). This is optional and does not assume the response is linear in dose.
