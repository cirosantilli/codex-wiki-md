<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If the [biological cells](../../../../../../../biological-cell.md) are initially interchangeable, randomly allocate them to five groups of ten for the days. Within each day, independently choose a [uniform distribution on a finite set](../../../../../../../discrete-uniform-distribution.md) over assignments having two occurrences of each dose level to the ten [experimental units](../../../../../../../experimental-unit.md). One implementation is a random [permutation](../../../../../../../permutation.md) of a list containing two labels of each level; the duplicate labels give every admissible assignment the same number of underlying permutations.

Randomize the processing order within each day as well, while pairing each preparation with its immediate measurement. **Randomize dose within day, not merely the names of the days.** This [restricted randomization](../../../../../../../restricted-randomization.md) preserves the planned balance while protecting [treatment contrasts](../../../../../../../treatment-contrast.md) against systematic order effects. Conceal the dose labels from the assessor where practical.
