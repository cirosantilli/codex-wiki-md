<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use a [randomized complete block design](../../../../../../../randomized-complete-block-design.md), with day as block and each [biological cell](../../../../../../../biological-cell.md) as an [experimental unit](../../../../../../../experimental-unit.md). **Each day should contain two [biological cells](../../../../../../../biological-cell.md) at each of the five dose levels.** Each level then has ten [replicates](../../../../../../../replicate.md) overall and occurs equally often in every day: the resulting [block design](../../../../../../../block-design.md) is an [orthogonal block design](../../../../../../../orthogonal-block-design.md).

This spreads any day-to-day changes in preparation, temperature or measurement across all levels instead of [confounding](../../../../../../../confounding.md) dose with day. Treat the zero level as a control with otherwise comparable handling. Keep the treatment-to-measurement delay standardized because the [response variable](../../../../../../../response-variable.md) is a [diffusion](../../../../../../../diffusion.md) rate measured immediately after preparation.
