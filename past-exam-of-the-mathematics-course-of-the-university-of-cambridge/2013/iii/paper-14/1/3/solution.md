<h1 id="1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Call this [simultaneous antipodal quotient of two spheres](../../../../../../simultaneous-antipodal-quotient-of-two-spheres.md) $M$. The simultaneous [antipodal map](../../../../../../antipodal-map.md) acts freely on $S^2\times S^2$, so $M$ is a connected [closed manifold](../../../../../../closed-manifold.md) of dimension four. Each antipodal factor has [mapping degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) $-1$; their product preserves orientation. Hence $M$ is orientable.

The product $S^2\times S^2$ is [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md), so this double [covering space](../../../../../../covering-space.md) is the [universal cover](../../../../../../universal-cover.md). Consequently

$$
\pi_1(M)=\mathbb Z/2,\qquad H_1(M;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/2,
$$

using the [abelianization](../../../../../../abelianization.md) of the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md). The [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md) gives $\chi(M)=4/2=2$. Its rational first [Betti number](../../../../../../betti-number.md) is zero, and [Poincare duality](../../../../../../poincare-duality.md) gives $b_3=b_1=0$ and $b_0=b_4=1$. Thus $b_2=0$ as well.

The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](../../../../../../universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) now gives $H^1(M;\mathbb Z)=0$ and

$$
H^2(M;\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}(H_1(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/2.
$$

Here $\operatorname{Hom}(H_2(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)=0$ because $b_2=0$. Integral [Poincare duality](../../../../../../poincare-duality.md) further gives $H^3(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_1(M;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/2$, and $H^4(M;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z$.

Let $a,b,c$ generate these groups in degrees $2,3,4$ respectively. The only potentially nonzero positive-degree [cup product](../../../../../../cup-product.md) is $a^2$. But $2a^2=(2a)a=0$, while $H^4(M;\mathbb Z)$ has no nonzero [torsion subgroup](../../../../../../torsion-subgroup.md), so $a^2=0$. Every other positive product vanishes by dimension. Thus

$$
\boxed{H^*(M;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\{1,c\}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)\{a,b\},\quad |a|=2,\ |b|=3,\ |c|=4,}
$$

**with every product of positive-degree elements equal to zero.** The degree-three torsion is essential: it would be lost by computing only rational [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md).
