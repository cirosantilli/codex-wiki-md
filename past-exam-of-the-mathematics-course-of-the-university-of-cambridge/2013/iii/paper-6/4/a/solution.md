<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Nonnegativity and unit mass give, by the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) for the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md), $|\widehat f(\xi)|\leq\int f=1=\widehat f(0)$. The finite [second moment](../../../../../../second-moment.md) implies a finite first absolute moment, so differentiating under the Fourier [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) is justified and

$$
\boxed{\|\widehat f\|_\infty\leq1,\qquad\widehat f(0)=1,\qquad\nabla\widehat f(0)=-i\int vf(v)\,dv=0}.
$$

The exponent convention has no $2\pi$, so no such factor occurs in this [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md).
