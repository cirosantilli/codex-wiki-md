↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [damped free-transport evolution](../../../../../../damped-free-transport-evolution.md) is $S(t)h(x,v)=e^{-t}h(x-tv,v)$. Substituting it into the [Duhamel principle](../../../../../../duhamel-s-principle.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f(t,x,v)=e^{-t}f_0(x-tv,v)+\int_0^t e^{-(t-s)}g\bigl(s,x-(t-s)v,v\bigr)\,ds}.
$$

The spatial shifts all follow the same backward [characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) ending at $(x,v)$ at time $t$; the velocity is constant.
