<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For this force, $\dot V=V$, hence $V(t)=e^tv$ and $X(t)=x+(e^t-1)v$. Invert the [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md): the initial velocity for an endpoint $(x,v)$ is $e^{-t}v$, and the initial position is $x-(1-e^{-t})v$. Constancy along each [characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f(t,x,v)=f_0\bigl(x-(1-e^{-t})v,e^{-t}v\bigr)}.
$$

There is no amplitude factor here: this is scalar advection, not the conservative [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) for a compressible density.
