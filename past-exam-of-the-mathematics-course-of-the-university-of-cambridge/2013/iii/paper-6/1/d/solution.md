↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Differentiating the explicit [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md) gives the block triangular [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md)

$$
\frac{\partial(X,V)}{\partial(x,v)}=
\begin{pmatrix}I_3&(e^t-1)I_3\\0&e^tI_3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is the product of its diagonal block [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{J(t,x,v)=e^{3t}}.
$$
