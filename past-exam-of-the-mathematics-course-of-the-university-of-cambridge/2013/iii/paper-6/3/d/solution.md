<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since $\rho(M)=1$, $\Pi^2f=\rho(f)\rho(M)M=\Pi f$. Also $Lf=0$ exactly when $f=\rho(f)M$, which is exactly the range of $\Pi$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Pi^2=\Pi,\qquad\ker L=\operatorname{im}\Pi=\operatorname{span}\{M\}}.
$$

A bounded [self-adjoint](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) idempotent is an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md): if $u=\Pi u$ and $w=(I-\Pi)w$, then $\langle u,w\rangle=\langle u,\Pi w\rangle=0$. Here the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) is precisely the zero-mass subspace $\rho(f)=0$.
