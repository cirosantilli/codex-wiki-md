<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The weighted [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) satisfies $\langle f,M\rangle=\int f=\rho(f)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Pi f=\rho(f)M=Lf+f,\qquad\Pi=L+I}.
$$

The [identity operator](../../../../../../identity-operator.md) and $L$ are linear, bounded and [self-adjoint](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md), hence so is $\Pi$. Directly, $\|\Pi f\|_H=|\rho(f)|\leq\|f\|_H$; equality holds at $f=M$. Thus $\|\Pi\|=1$.
