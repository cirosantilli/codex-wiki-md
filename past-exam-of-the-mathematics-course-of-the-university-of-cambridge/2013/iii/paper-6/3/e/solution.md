<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Using $L=\Pi-I$ and the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) identity $\Pi^*\Pi=\Pi$, we have $\langle\Pi f,f\rangle=\|\Pi f\|_H^2$. The orthogonal decomposition $f=\Pi f+(I-\Pi)f$ then gives

$$
\boxed{\langle Lf,f\rangle=\|\Pi f\|_H^2-\|f\|_H^2=-\|f-\Pi f\|_H^2}.
$$

The sign is negative, as in the PDF; the converted TeX loses it at the last equality. This establishes the [exact spectral gap of normalized velocity relaxation](../../../../../../exact-spectral-gap-of-normalized-velocity-relaxation.md): on the zero-mass subspace $L=-I$, with gap one rather than the weaker half-gap estimate.
