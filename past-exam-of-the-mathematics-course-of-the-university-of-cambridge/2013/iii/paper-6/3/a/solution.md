<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [weighted Hilbert structure of velocity-reset relaxation](../../../../../../weighted-hilbert-structure-of-velocity-reset-relaxation.md), with [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) $\langle f,g\rangle=\int f\overline g/M$ and [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $\|f\|_H$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\int|f|=\int\frac{|f|}{\sqrt M}\sqrt M\leq\|f\|_H\left(\int M\right)^{1/2}=\|f\|_H.
$$

Thus $\rho(f)$ is an absolutely convergent [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) and a bounded [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md). Since $\|M\|_H^2=\int M=1$, the [normalized velocity-reset collision operator](../../../../../../normalized-velocity-reset-collision-operator.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{\|Lf\|_H=\|\rho(f)M-f\|_H\leq2\|f\|_H}.
$$

This establishes boundedness. The subsequent orthogonal decomposition improves the operator [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) to exactly one.
