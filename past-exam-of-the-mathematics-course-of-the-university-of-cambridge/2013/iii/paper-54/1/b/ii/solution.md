<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For no central accretion, $A=0$ and

$$
\boxed{\bar\nu\Sigma=B r^{-1/2},\qquad v_r=0,\qquad\mathcal G=3\pi(GM_*)^{1/2}B.}
$$

This [nonaccreting constant-torque disk](../../../../../../../nonaccreting-constant-torque-disk.md) has a finite inner stress and normally a nonzero inner [surface density](../../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md). An external inner [torque](../../../../../../../torque.md) supplies the [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) transported to an outer sink, although mass does not flow. If both this zero-mass-flux condition and a zero inner [torque](../../../../../../../torque.md) are imposed, $B=0$; there is no nontrivial positive-viscosity steady disk satisfying both.
