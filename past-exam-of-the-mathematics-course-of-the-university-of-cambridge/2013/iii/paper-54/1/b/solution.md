<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $F=\bar\nu\Sigma$. In [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md), $r^{1/2}\partial_r(r^{1/2}F)$ is constant, so

$$
\boxed{F=A+B r^{-1/2}.}
$$

The inward [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) and outward [viscous torque in an accretion disk](../../../../../../viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) are

$$
\dot M=-2\pi r\Sigma v_r=6\pi r^{1/2}\partial_r(r^{1/2}F)=3\pi A,\qquad
\mathcal G=3\pi(GM_*)^{1/2}r^{1/2}F.
$$
