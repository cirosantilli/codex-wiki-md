<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) at finite $r_{\rm in}$,

$$
\boxed{\bar\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_{\rm in}}r}\right).}
$$

With positive finite inner [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md), $\Sigma$ tends to zero there. At fixed nonzero inward mass flux, the corresponding $v_r=-\dot M/(2\pi r\Sigma)$ becomes large, signaling the breakdown of the nearly circular [thin disc](../../../../../../../thin-disk.md) approximation in the inner transition region.
