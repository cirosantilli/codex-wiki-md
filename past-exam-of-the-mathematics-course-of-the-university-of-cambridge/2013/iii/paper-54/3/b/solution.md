<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A strip $dx$ at $x>0$ passes the satellite at relative speed $Sx$, so its encounter mass flux is $Sx\Sigma(x)dx$. Each unit mass gains [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $r_0\Delta v_y$. Therefore the [one-sided impulse torque on a disk](../../../../../../one-sided-impulse-torque-on-a-disk.md) is

$$
\Gamma_{>0}=\chi\frac{r_0(GM_s)^2}{S^2}\int_H^\infty\frac{\Sigma(x)}{x^4}dx.
$$

Putting $\chi=1/2$ gives exactly the requested expression. Inner strips have the opposite signed impulse but the same positive encounter flux. The net disc [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{\Gamma=\chi\frac{r_0(GM_s)^2}{S^2}\int_H^\infty\frac{\Sigma(x)-\Sigma(-x)}{x^4}dx.}
$$

An even [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) makes the integrand vanish pointwise, so the total [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) is zero although each one-sided [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) can be nonzero. The cutoff must keep the encounters in the weak-deflection regime; an appropriate physical value is at least of order the thickness or the relevant strong-scattering radius. The infinite-sheet integral also assumes convergence or a specified outer truncation.
