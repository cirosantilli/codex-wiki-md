<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $k=|k_x|$, $c_s^2=(dP/d\Sigma)_0$ and $\kappa^2=2\Omega(2\Omega-S)$. Linearize the [barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk](../../../../../../barotropic-closure-of-a-razor-thin-disk.md) and [shearing sheet](../../../../../../shearing-sheet.md) about the given state. Write the radial and azimuthal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) perturbations as $u$ and $v$. [Axisymmetry](../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) removes advection by the background shear, giving

$$
-i\omega\Sigma_1+ik_x\Sigma_0u=0,\qquad
-i\omega u-2\Omega v=-ik_x\left(\Phi_1+\frac{c_s^2\Sigma_1}{\Sigma_0}\right),\qquad
-i\omega v+(2\Omega-S)u=0.
$$

The softened potential is $\Phi_1=-2\pi G\Sigma_1e^{-k\epsilon}/k$. Eliminating the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) components for the compressive branch gives the [softened Toomre dispersion relation](../../../../../../softened-toomre-dispersion-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\kappa^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0k e^{-k\epsilon}+c_s^2k^2.}
$$

The Coriolis/shear combination supplies the radial epicyclic restoring term, [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) supplies the short-wave term, and [self-gravity](../../../../../../self-gravity.md) lowers the squared frequency. The full three-variable determinant also has a stationary [axisymmetric geostrophic mode](../../../../../../axisymmetric-geostrophic-mode.md), with $u=0$ and azimuthal flow balancing the pressure-plus-gravity gradient. The displayed relation describes the density-wave pair; eliminating by division by $\omega$ must not silently deny that stationary mode.
