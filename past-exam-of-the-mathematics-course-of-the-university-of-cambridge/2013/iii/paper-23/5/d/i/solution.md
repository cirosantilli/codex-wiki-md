<h1 id="5/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $S$ to be the [primes](../../../../../../../prime-number.md) in the interval and $Q=\max(2,\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor)$. Every such [prime](../../../../../../../prime-number.md) exceeds $M>N\ge Q$ apart from harmless bounded small cases, so it avoids zero modulo each [prime](../../../../../../../prime-number.md) up to $Q$. The sifted-interval bound with $H=N$ and $q=1$ gives

$$
\boxed{\pi(M+N)-\pi(M)\ll N/\log N.}
$$

Since $Q^2=O(N)$ and $\log(2Q)\asymp\log N$, the implied constant is absolute. The choice of strict or inclusive endpoint in the prime-counting convention changes at most two terms, absorbed by the bound for $N\ge2$.
