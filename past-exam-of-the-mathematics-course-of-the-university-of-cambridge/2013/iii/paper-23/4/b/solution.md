<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The Dirichlet-series coefficients are

$$
a_n=\sum_{d\mid n}\chi_D(d).
$$

They are multiplicative. At a [prime power](../../../../../../prime-power.md), their values are $k+1$ if $\chi_D(p)=1$, one for even $k$ and zero for odd $k$ if $\chi_D(p)=-1$, and one if $\chi_D(p)=0$. Thus every $a_n$ is nonnegative. In particular $a_{m^2}\ge1$. This is the [nonnegative zeta-times-real-L coefficients](../../../../../../nonnegative-zeta-times-real-l-coefficients.md) identity. The same Euler expansion gives $-\zeta'/\zeta(\sigma)-L'/L(\sigma,\chi_D)\ge0$ for $\sigma>1$, with coefficients $\Lambda(n)(1+\chi_D(n))\ge0$.
