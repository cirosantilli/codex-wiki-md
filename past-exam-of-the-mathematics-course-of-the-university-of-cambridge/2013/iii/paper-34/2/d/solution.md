<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $\tau=c\sigma$ with $c>0$, the [change of variables](../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) gives

$$
p_\tau(\tau)\propto p_\sigma(\tau/c)/c
=(\tau/c)^{-1}/c=\tau^{-1}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\frac{d(c\sigma)}{c\sigma}=\frac{d\sigma}{\sigma}.}
$$

This is the [scale-invariant prior](../../../../../../scale-invariant-prior.md) as a measure. Because its integral over $(0,\infty)$ diverges, it is an [improper prior](../../../../../../improper-prior.md), not a normalized probability distribution on that range.
