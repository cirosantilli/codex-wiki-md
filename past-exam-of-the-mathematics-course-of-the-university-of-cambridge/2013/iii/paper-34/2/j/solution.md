**Yes, the pattern merits checking.** Digits two and three are visibly more frequent than the [Benford law](../../../../../../benford-law.md) predictions, whereas several large digits are scarce and nine is absent. Rough agreement for one and four does not remove this pattern.

Use the [predictive discrepancy statistic](../../../../../../predictive-discrepancy-statistic.md) comparison in part (h), together with examination of the accounts' selection and constraints. A discrepancy is evidence against this digit model, not by itself evidence sufficient to establish fabrication. No numerical test calculation is needed for this qualitative assessment.
