The [multinomial distribution](../../../../../../multinomial-distribution.md) has [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md)

$$
\boxed{p(\mathbf y_i\mid\mathbf p_i,n_i)
=\frac{n_i!}{\prod_{k=1}^K y_{ik}!}
\prod_{k=1}^Kp_{ik}^{\,y_{ik}},\qquad
y_{ik}\in\{0,1,\ldots\},\quad\sum_k y_{ik}=n_i.}
$$

It is zero outside this count simplex. The multinomial coefficient counts the individual category sequences giving the same aggregate counts; the probability vector satisfies $\sum_kp_{ik}=1$.
