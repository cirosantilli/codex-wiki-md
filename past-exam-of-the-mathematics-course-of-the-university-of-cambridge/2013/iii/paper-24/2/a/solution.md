A real [Lévy process](../../../../../../levy-process.md) is a real-valued [stochastic process](../../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md) $(X_t)_{t\geq0}$ with the following properties:

- $X_0=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md).
- It has [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md): increments over disjoint ordered time intervals are independent.
- It has [stationary increments](../../../../../../stationary-increments.md): $X_{s+t}-X_s$ has the same law as $X_t$ for $s,t\geq0$.
- It has [stochastic continuity](../../../../../../stochastic-continuity.md): $X_s\to X_t$ in probability as $s\to t$.

One convention also includes [càdlàg](../../../../../../cadlag.md) paths in the definition. Equivalently, under the intrinsic definition above one chooses the [càdlàg modification](../../../../../../cadlag-modification.md), which exists for such a [stochastic process](../../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md). Thus the usual working version of a [Lévy process](../../../../../../levy-process.md) has [right-continuous](../../../../../../right-continuous-function.md) paths with left limits. There is no assumption of finite [moments](../../../../../../moment.md) or continuous paths.
