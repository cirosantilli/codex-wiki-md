<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The second episode starts only after the first headache, at 24.8; its row is absent from every [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md) before that time. More generally, episode $k+1$ starts at the observed end of episode $k$. The left-open, right-closed interval convention assigns an event at a shared boundary only to the interval ending there. Thus the first event is counted once, and the second cannot be counted before the first. **Preserving the episode entry times and patient history enforces the ordering**; entering every recurrence as a new record starting at treatment time zero would not do so.
