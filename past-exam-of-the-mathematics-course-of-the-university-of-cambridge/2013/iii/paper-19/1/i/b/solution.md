A [strongly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../../strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md) is an uncountable [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$ that is both regular and a strong limit:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cf}(\lambda)=\lambda,\qquad
2^\mu<\lambda\text{ for every cardinal }\mu<\lambda.}
$$

Regularity excludes expressing $\lambda$ as the supremum of a shorter increasing sequence, while the strong-limit requirement concerns all smaller [power sets](../../../../../../../power-set.md). Merely being an uncountable regular limit [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) is the weaker notion of a [weakly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../../weakly-inaccessible-cardinal.md).
