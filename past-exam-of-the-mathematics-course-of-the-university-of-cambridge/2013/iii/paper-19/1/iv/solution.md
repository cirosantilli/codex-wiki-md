<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The structure $H_\kappa$ of [hereditarily small sets](../../../../../../hereditarily-small-set.md) is transitive. If $\kappa\le\omega$, all its sets are hereditarily finite, so it cannot satisfy Infinity. Therefore the assumed model has $\kappa>\omega$.

Let $\mu<\kappa$ be a [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md). The [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\mu$ belongs to $H_\kappa$, and every actual [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $\mu$ also belongs to $H_\kappa$: its [transitive closure](../../../../../../transitive-closure.md) has size at most $\max(\mu,\omega)<\kappa$. The [Power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) axiom inside $H_\kappa$ therefore produces the actual $\mathcal P(\mu)$, since all [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) relevant to the internal definition are present. This [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) itself belongs to $H_\kappa$, so

$$
2^\mu=|\mathcal P(\mu)|<\kappa.
$$

Thus $\kappa$ is a strong limit. Its regularity was assumed, and we have proved uncountability. Hence **$\kappa$ is strongly inaccessible**.
