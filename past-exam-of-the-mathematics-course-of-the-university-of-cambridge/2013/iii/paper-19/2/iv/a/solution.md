<h1 id="2/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the intended infinite [regular cardinal](../../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$, set $S_0(\delta)=0$. Suppose $0<\zeta<\kappa^+$ and the [functions](../../../../../../../function-split.md) for its predecessors have been constructed. Since $|\zeta|\le\kappa$, choose a [surjection](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md) $e_\zeta:\kappa\to\zeta$ and define

$$
\boxed{S_\zeta(\delta)=\sup_{\eta<\delta}\bigl(S_{e_\zeta(\eta)}(\delta)+1\bigr).}
$$

The supremum is below $\kappa$: it uses fewer than $\kappa$ [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) below $\kappa$, and $\kappa$ is regular. This defines a [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $\kappa\to\kappa$ at each stage of the recursion.

For any $\alpha<\zeta$, choose $\eta$ with $e_\zeta(\eta)=\alpha$. Whenever $\delta>\eta$, the displayed supremum includes $S_\alpha(\delta)+1$, so $S_\alpha(\delta)<S_\zeta(\delta)$. Therefore the recursion yields a [long chain under eventual domination](../../../../../../../long-chain-under-eventual-domination.md) of length $\kappa^+$.
