The [singular cardinals hypothesis](../../../../../../../singular-cardinals-hypothesis.md) is the assertion that for every infinite [singular cardinal](../../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$,

$$
\boxed{2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}<\kappa
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\kappa^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}=\kappa^+.}
$$

In particular, for a singular strong-limit [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) it gives $2^\kappa=\kappa^+$. This is a restriction on singular-cardinal exponentiation; it does not assert the full [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) at all [cardinals](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md).
