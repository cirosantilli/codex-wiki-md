The [Bukovský-Hechler theorem](../../../../../../../bukovsky-hechler-theorem.md) states: if $\kappa$ is singular and there are a [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\rho<\kappa$ and a [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\tau$ such that $2^\mu=\tau$ for every [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\mu$ with $\rho\le\mu<\kappa$, then

$$
\boxed{2^\kappa=\tau.}
$$

Thus an eventual plateau of the power-set [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) below a [singular cardinal](../../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md) continues at that [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md). One can see the mechanism by decomposing $\kappa$ into $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ bounded pieces: $2^\kappa=(2^{<\kappa})^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}$. On the plateau choose $\mu\ge\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$, so $\tau^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}=(2^\mu)^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}=2^\mu=\tau$.
