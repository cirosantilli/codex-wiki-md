↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonzero [limit ordinal](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) $\delta$, a [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $C\subseteq\delta$ is a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) if it is unbounded in $\delta$ and contains every one of its limit points below $\delta$. A [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $S\subseteq\delta$ is stationary if

$$
\boxed{S\cap C\ne\varnothing\quad\text{for every club }C\subseteq\delta.}
$$

This defines [stationarity in a limit ordinal](../../../../../../../stationarity-in-a-limit-ordinal.md). The regular uncountable case is the usual [stationary set](../../../../../../../stationary-set.md) setting; results such as [Fodor lemma](../../../../../../../fodor-lemma.md) require that additional hypothesis, rather than an arbitrary [limit ordinal](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md).
