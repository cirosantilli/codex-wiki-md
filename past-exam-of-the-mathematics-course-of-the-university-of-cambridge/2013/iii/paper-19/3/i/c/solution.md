↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The [club principle](../../../../../../../club-principle.md) predicts a cofinal countable ladder contained in every uncountable subset of $\omega_1$.** More precisely, $\clubsuit$ asserts that there is a sequence $\langle A_\delta:\delta\in\lim(\omega_1)\rangle$ such that each $A_\delta$ is cofinal in $\delta$ with [order type](../../../../../../../order-type.md) $\omega$, and every uncountable $X\subseteq\omega_1$ contains at least one entire $A_\delta$. It predicts a countable ladder contained in $X$, rather than predicting $X\cap\delta$ exactly. The latter is the stronger kind of guessing in the [diamond principle](../../../../../../../diamond-principle.md).
