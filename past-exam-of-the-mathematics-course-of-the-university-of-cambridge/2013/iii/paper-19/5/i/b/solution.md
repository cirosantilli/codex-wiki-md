A [nice forcing name](../../../../../../../nice-forcing-name.md) for a [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of a ground-model set $a$, often an [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md), has the form

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\{(\check x,q):x\in a,\ q\in A_x\},}
$$

where each $A_x$ is an antichain in $\mathbb Q$ and the entire construction belongs to $M$. Its value consists of the $x$ for which $H$ meets $A_x$. The antichains need not be maximal. Under the countable chain condition they are countable in $M$, which makes [nice forcing names](../../../../../../../nice-forcing-name.md) useful for counting possible [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) in extensions.
