<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Start with a ground model containing a normal splitting [Suslin tree](../../../../../../suslin-tree.md) $T$, and force with $\operatorname{Add}(\omega,\omega_2)$, the finite partial [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) from $\omega_2\times\omega$ to $2$. Such a starting model is relatively consistent with [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md): the constructible universe has $\Diamond$, and $\Diamond$ implies the existence of a Suslin tree.

This [Cohen forcing](../../../../../../cohen-forcing.md) is Knaster. In an uncountable family of finite conditions, the [delta-system lemma](../../../../../../delta-system-lemma.md) yields an uncountable family whose domains have one common root; thinning to common values on that finite root makes its members pairwise compatible. If $P$ is Knaster and $T$ is [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md), the product $P\times T$ is [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md): thin any uncountable family to pairwise compatible $P$ coordinates, then use [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) to find two compatible $T$ coordinates. Thus $P$ forces that the old $T$ is still [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) A name for an uncountable antichain would otherwise produce an uncountable antichain in this product by deciding its nodes.

Countable levels and the tree's splitting property persist, and the splitting argument from the previous part rules out a new [cofinal branch](../../../../../../cofinal-branch.md) in a [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) tree. Therefore the old $T$ is still Suslin. Meanwhile the [forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md) adds at least $\aleph_2$ distinct reals, with all [cardinals](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) preserved, so $2^{\aleph_0}\ge\aleph_2$.

We obtain **a model with a Suslin tree and $\neg\mathrm{CH}$**. Hence existence of a Suslin tree does not imply the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md). This uses [Knaster forcing preserves Suslin trees](../../../../../../knaster-forcing-preserves-suslin-trees.md), not a claim that arbitrary [CCC](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) [forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md) preserves them.
