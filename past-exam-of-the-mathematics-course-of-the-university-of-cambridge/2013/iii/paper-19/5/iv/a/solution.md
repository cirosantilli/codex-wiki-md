<h1 id="5/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For uncountable regular $\kappa$, let $\theta=\operatorname{otp}(C_\zeta)$. Since $C_\zeta$ is a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) in $\zeta$, $\operatorname{cf}(\theta)=\operatorname{cf}(\zeta)=\kappa$, so $\theta\ge\kappa$.

If $\theta>\kappa$, its [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md) ensures $\theta>\kappa+\omega$: [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) strictly between $\kappa$ and $\kappa+\omega$ are successors, and $\kappa+\omega$ has [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md) $\omega$. In the continuous increasing enumeration $c$ of $C_\zeta$, take $\gamma=c(\kappa+\omega)$. It is a limit point of $C_\zeta$ and has [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md) $\omega<\kappa$. Coherence gives $C_\gamma=C_\zeta\cap\gamma$, with [order type](../../../../../../../order-type.md) $\kappa+\omega$ and size $\kappa$. This contradicts the size clause for [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) of [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md) below $\kappa$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{otp}(C_\zeta)=\kappa\quad\text{for uncountable regular }\kappa.}
$$

**The printed hypotheses need this qualification.** At $\kappa=\omega$, choose $C_\zeta=\zeta$ for every nonzero countable [limit ordinal](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md). These [club sets](../../../../../../../club-set.md) are coherent, and the small-cofinality clause is vacuous. But at $\zeta=\omega^2$ their [order type](../../../../../../../order-type.md) is $\omega^2$, not $\omega$. The standard [square principle](../../../../../../../square-principle.md) includes an order-type bound $\operatorname{otp}(C_\zeta)\le\kappa$, which also repairs this countable case.
