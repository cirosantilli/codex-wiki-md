<h1 id="5/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Fix $\delta<\kappa^+$ with $\operatorname{cf}(\delta)>\omega$. The limit points of $C_\delta$ form a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) in $\delta$. For each such point $\gamma$, coherence gives

$$
\operatorname{otp}(C_\gamma)=\operatorname{otp}(C_\delta\cap\gamma).
$$

As $\gamma$ increases through those limit points, these order types strictly increase. Hence the [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) contains at most one member of a fixed fiber $E_\xi$. Removing an initial segment past that member leaves a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) disjoint from $E_\xi$. Therefore $E_\xi\cap\delta$ is not stationary. This proves **each $E_\xi$ is non-reflecting**, by the [nonreflection of fixed order-type fibers](../../../../../../../nonreflection-of-fixed-order-type-fibers.md).

For uncountable regular $\kappa$, part (a) gives type $\kappa$ at [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md) $\kappa$, and the size clause gives type less than $\kappa$ at smaller cofinalities. All nonzero [limit ordinals](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) below $\kappa^+$ fall into one of these cases. Thus

$$
\lim(\kappa^+)=\bigsqcup_{\xi\le\kappa}E_\xi.
$$

The index set $\kappa+1$ has cardinality $\kappa$, so this is a disjoint union of $\kappa$ non-reflecting [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md). Empty fibers can be omitted; if nonempty pieces are required, a fiber of size $\kappa^+$ can be split further, since nonreflection is inherited by [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md). Such a fiber exists because $\kappa$ sets of size at most $\kappa$ cannot cover $\kappa^+$ points.

At $\kappa=\omega$, the nonreflection definition is vacuous for every [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $\omega_1$, since no smaller [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) has uncountable [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md). The partition conclusion is therefore still true by an arbitrary countable partition, although the particular fibers from the weak printed square assumptions need not cover all [limit ordinals](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md). Under the standard order-type-bounded square definition, the displayed fiber partition works in this case too.
