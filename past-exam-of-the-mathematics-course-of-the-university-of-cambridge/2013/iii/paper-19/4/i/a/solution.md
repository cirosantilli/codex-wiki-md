A [forcing name](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) for $\mathbb P$ is a set of ordered pairs $(\sigma,p)$ where $p\in P$ and $\sigma$ is itself a [forcing name](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md). This recursive definition is made well-founded by assigning the [forcing name rank](../../../../../../../forcing-name-rank.md) $\sup\{\operatorname{nrk}(\sigma)+1:(\sigma,p)\in\tau\}$. Names over the ground model are those names belonging to $M$; conditions remain ground-model objects. A name describes which recursively interpreted elements are activated by the [generic filter](../../../../../../../generic-filter.md).
