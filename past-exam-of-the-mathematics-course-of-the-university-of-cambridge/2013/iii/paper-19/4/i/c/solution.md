A [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $E\subseteq P$ is [dense above a forcing condition](../../../../../../../dense-above-a-forcing-condition.md) $p$ if

$$
\boxed{\forall q\ge p\ \exists r\ge q\ (r\in E).}
$$

All displayed strengthenings use the PDF convention. This only requires density in the cone of extensions of $p$; it need not be dense throughout $P$, nor downward or upward closed.
