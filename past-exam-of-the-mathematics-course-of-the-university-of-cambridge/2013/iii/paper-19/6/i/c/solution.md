↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A precise form of the [generalized delta-system lemma](../../../../../../../generalized-delta-system-lemma.md) is this. Let $\mu<\theta$ be infinite [cardinals](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md), with $\theta$ regular and uncountable, and suppose $|[\alpha]^{<\mu}|<\theta$ for every $\alpha<\theta$. Every family $\mathcal A$ of $\theta$ distinct sets, each of cardinality less than $\mu$, contains a subfamily $\mathcal B$ of size $\theta$ and a set $r$ such that

$$
\boxed{x\ne y\in\mathcal B\ \Longrightarrow\ x\cap y=r.}
$$

The members form a [delta-system](../../../../../../../delta-system.md) with root $r$. A sufficient usual arithmetic hypothesis is $\alpha^\mu<\theta$ for all infinite [cardinals](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\alpha<\theta$. The finite-set case $\mu=\omega$ needs only regular uncountable $\theta$, since finite [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of each $\alpha<\theta$ form a family of size less than $\theta$. This is the form used for the finite-support collapse below.
