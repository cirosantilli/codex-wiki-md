<h1 id="6/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $p\in G$ force that $\dot D$ is a diamond sequence. For each $\alpha<\omega_1$, construct in $M$ the family

$$
E_\alpha=\{x\subseteq\alpha:\exists q\ge p\ (q\Vdash\dot D_\alpha=\check x)\}.
$$

This family is countable in $M$: choose a witnessing condition for each distinct value; different values require incompatible conditions, and the [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) is [CCC](../../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) It is not being asserted that every generic value of $\dot D_\alpha$ is a ground [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md). Only values forced equal to a ground [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) enter $E_\alpha$.

For any ground $X\subseteq\omega_1$ and ground [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) $C\subseteq\omega_1$, the [CCC](../../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) preserves $\omega_1$ and $C$ remains a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md). Since $p$ forces diamond, some strengthening $q\ge p$ forces $\dot D_\alpha=\check X\cap\check\alpha$ for an [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha\in C$, deciding the [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) witness if necessary. Thus $X\cap\alpha\in E_\alpha$. The ground set of such $\alpha$ meets every ground [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) and is stationary in $M$.

Consequently $\langle E_\alpha\rangle$ witnesses the countable-family diamond assertion in $M$. By the fully proved [countable-family diamond equivalence](../../../../../../../countable-family-diamond-equivalence.md), with $\lambda=\omega_1$ and its stationary index set, it follows that

$$
\boxed{M\models\Diamond.}
$$

Thus [CCC forcing cannot create diamond](../../../../../../../ccc-forcing-cannot-create-diamond.md).
