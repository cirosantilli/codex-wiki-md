↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** The [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) makes $B'$ compact for the [weak-star topology](../../../../../../weak-star-topology.md). Its [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) can be seen by embedding it in the product $\prod_{x\in E}[-\|x\|,\|x\|]$: the closed conditions expressing linearity identify a closed subset with $B'$, and the product is compact by the [Tychonoff theorem](../../../../../../tychonoff-s-theorem.md). The product topology is exactly [pointwise convergence](../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md) on $E$.

Part (i) supplies a compatible metric. Every compact [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) has a finite $1/n$-net for each positive integer $n$. The countable union of these finite nets is dense. Therefore

$$
\boxed{B'\text{ is weak-star separable}.}
$$

The argument concerns [topological separability](../../../../../../separable-topological-space.md), with the relative [weak-star topology](../../../../../../weak-star-topology.md) specified in the question.
