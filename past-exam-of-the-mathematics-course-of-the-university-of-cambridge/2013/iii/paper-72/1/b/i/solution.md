<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [sea ice](../../../../../../../sea-ice.md) acts as a frequency-selective filter. Over a long path, [wave attenuation in sea ice](../../../../../../../wave-attenuation-in-sea-ice.md) is generally much stronger for shorter [surface gravity waves](../../../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md), through repeated scattering, internal ice losses and water-side dissipation. Their amplitude can fall below the tiltmeter's detection level even if the source initially generated them. Thus the long-period swell survives while the shorter-period tail does not.

**The disappearance near 14 s is an attenuation and detectability limit, not a forbidden-frequency interval of the ideal elastic plate.** The [flexural-gravity wave](../../../../../../../flexural-gravity-wave.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) admits a real positive [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) for every positive [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md). In an impulsive-source picture, shorter-period [deep-water gravity waves](../../../../../../../deep-water-gravity-wave.md) also arrive later because their [group velocity](../../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is smaller, but delayed arrival alone does not explain a persistent observed cutoff.
