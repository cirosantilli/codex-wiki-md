<h1 id="11g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each $s\in S\setminus T$, maximality implies a nontrivial relation

$$
a_s s+\sum_{t\in T}b_{s,t}t=0.
$$

Here $a_s\ne0$, since otherwise this would contradict the [linear independence in a module](../../../../../../linear-independence-in-a-module.md) of $T$. Thus $a_s s\in N$. Take $r=\prod_{s\in S\setminus T}a_s$, with empty product equal to one. Because $R$ is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md), $r\ne0$. Every $s\in S\setminus T$ satisfies $rs\in N$, and this is also true for $s\in T$. Expressing any element of the [finitely generated module](../../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) as an $R$-linear combination of $S$ now gives **$rM\subseteq N$**. This is [clearing denominators relative to an independent module subset](../../../../../../clearing-denominators-relative-to-an-independent-module-subset.md); it does not require $R$ to be a field.
