<h1 id="11g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The map $\varphi:M\to N$, $x\mapsto rx$, is a [module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md), and it is injective because $r\ne0$ and $M$ is a [torsion-free module](../../../../../../torsion-free-module.md). The [linear independence in a module](../../../../../../linear-independence-in-a-module.md) of $T$ makes the map $R^{|T|}\to N$ sending the standard basis to $T$ an isomorphism. Thus $N$ is a [finite free module](../../../../../../finite-free-module.md), and **$M\cong\varphi(M)\subseteq N\cong R^{|T|}$**. This proves [embedding a finitely generated torsion-free module in a finite free module](../../../../../../embedding-a-finitely-generated-torsion-free-module-in-a-finite-free-module.md). No claim that the submodule itself is free over an arbitrary [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) is needed.
