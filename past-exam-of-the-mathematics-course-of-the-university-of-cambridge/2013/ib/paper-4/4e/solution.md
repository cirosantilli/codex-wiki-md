<h1 id="4e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4E](../4e.md)

[Rouché's theorem](../../../../../rouche-s-theorem.md) states that if $f,g$ are [holomorphic functions](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on a neighbourhood of a simple closed contour and its interior, and $|g|<|f|$ on the contour, then $f$ and $f+g$ have the same number of zeros inside, counted with [multiplicity](../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md).

On $|z|=1$, use $f=6z^2$: the other terms have absolute value at most $1+3+1=5<6$. Hence there are two zeros inside the unit circle. On $|z|=2$, use $f=3z^7$: its modulus is $384$, whereas the other terms have total modulus at most $256+24+1=281$. Hence there are seven zeros inside the larger circle. The strict inequalities also exclude zeros on either boundary. **There are $7-2=5$ roots in the annulus, counted with multiplicity.**
