<h1 id="1e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1E](../1e.md)

A [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form.md) on a finite-dimensional real [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) is a function $q(v)=B(v,v)$ for a symmetric [bilinear form](../../../../../bilinear-form.md) $B$. Conversely $B(u,v)=\tfrac12(q(u+v)-q(u)-q(v))$ recovers that [bilinear form](../../../../../bilinear-form.md). Two [quadratic forms](../../../../../quadratic-form.md) are congruent if a [linear isomorphism](../../../../../linear-isomorphism.md) $T$ satisfies $q_2(v)=q_1(Tv)$; in coordinates this is [matrix congruence](../../../../../matrix-congruence.md), $A_2=P^TA_1P$, with $P$ invertible.

[Sylvester's law of inertia](../../../../../sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md) says that a real [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form.md) admits coordinates in which

$$
q=\sum_{j=1}^{p}u_j^2-\sum_{j=p+1}^{p+q}u_j^2,
$$

and the numbers of positive, negative and zero squares, $(p,q,r)$, are independent of the coordinates. Here $p+q+r=\dim V$; $p$ and $q$ are the maximal dimensions of subspaces on which the [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form.md) is positive definite and negative definite, while $r$ is the dimension of its [radical of a bilinear form](../../../../../radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md). These numbers are the [inertia of a bilinear form](../../../../../inertia-of-a-bilinear-form.md). The [rank of a quadratic form](../../../../../rank-of-a-quadratic-form.md) is $p+q$, and the [signature of a quadratic form](../../../../../signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md), in the signed-difference convention, is $p-q$.

Completing squares gives

$$
q(x,y,z)=-2\left(x-\frac y2-3z\right)^2-\frac12y^2+19z^2.
$$

The change of coordinates is invertible, so **the inertia is $(1,2,0)$ and the signature is $-1$**. If signature is recorded as an ordered pair instead, that pair is $(1,2)$.
