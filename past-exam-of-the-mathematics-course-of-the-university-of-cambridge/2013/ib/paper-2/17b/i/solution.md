<h1 id="17b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The time-independent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives the even interior [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) $\psi=A\cos(\alpha x)$, with $\alpha^2=2m(U+E)/\hbar^2$. Outside, normalizability of a [bound state](../../../../../../bound-state.md) requires $\psi=C e^{-\beta(|x|-l)}$, with $\beta^2=-2mE/\hbar^2$.

Continuity of the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) and its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) at $x=l$ gives $C=A\cos(\alpha l)$ and $-\beta C=-\alpha A\sin(\alpha l)$. A nonzero solution cannot have $\cos(\alpha l)=0$, so division gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha\tan(\alpha l)=\beta.}
$$

At the formally allowed endpoint $E=-U$, the even interior solution is constant; [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) matching would force the exterior amplitude to vanish, leaving the zero solution. Nontrivial [bound states](../../../../../../bound-state.md) therefore have $-U<E<0$. The matching formula is the even-state condition for a [finite square well](../../../../../../finite-square-well.md).
