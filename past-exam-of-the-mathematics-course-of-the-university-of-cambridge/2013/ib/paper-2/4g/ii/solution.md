↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False without a separation assumption.** Give $X=\{0,1\}$ the [indiscrete topology](../../../../../../indiscrete-topology.md) $\{\varnothing,X\}$, and take $Y=\{0\}$. Both spaces are finite and hence [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), but $Y$ is not a [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) because $X\setminus Y=\{1\}$ is not open. In a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), a [compact subset](../../../../../../compact-space.md) would be closed; that hypothesis is absent here.
