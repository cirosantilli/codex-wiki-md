↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [convex function](../../../../../../convex-function.md) satisfies $g(tx+(1-t)y)\le tg(x)+(1-t)g(y)$ for all $x,y$ in its domain and $0\le t\le1$. If the second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) exists on an interval, this is equivalent to $g''\ge0$ there. The equivalence follows from the nondecreasing first [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), or from the supporting-line inequalities obtained by integration. Here

$$
f'(x)=\log x+1,\qquad \boxed{f''(x)=1/x>0\quad(x>0).}
$$

Thus it is a [strictly convex function](../../../../../../strictly-convex-function.md), and in particular convex.
