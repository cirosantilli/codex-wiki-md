<h1 id="9b/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [central force](../../../../../../../central-force.md) has no transverse component. Its angular equation is therefore

$$
r\ddot\theta+2\dot r\dot\theta=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac d{dt}(r^2\dot\theta)=0.
$$

Thus the specific [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is the constant

$$
\boxed{l=r^2\dot\theta.}
$$

The radial equation is

$$
\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2=-V'(r).
$$

Substitute $\dot\theta=l/r^2$:

$$
\ddot r=\frac{l^2}{r^3}-V'(r)
=-\frac d{dr}\left[V(r)+\frac{l^2}{2r^2}\right].
$$

Consequently the radial motion for $r>0$ is exactly a unit-mass one-dimensional motion in the [effective potential](../../../../../../../effective-potential.md)

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm eff}(r)=V(r)+\frac{l^2}{2r^2}.}
$$

Its conserved [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) is $\tfrac12\dot r^2+V_{\rm eff}(r)$; the second term in the [effective potential](../../../../../../../effective-potential.md) accounts for the angular [kinetic energy](../../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
