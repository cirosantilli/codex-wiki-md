<h1 id="12b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The total relativistic [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) in the original frame are

$$
E=c^2(m_1\gamma_1+m_2\gamma_2),\qquad
p=m_1\gamma_1u_1+m_2\gamma_2u_2.
$$

A [Lorentz boost](../../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) of signed [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $V$ makes the total spatial [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) $p'=\gamma_V(p-VE/c^2)$. Setting it to zero gives the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{V=\frac{c^2p}{E}
=\frac{m_1\gamma_1u_1+m_2\gamma_2u_2}
{m_1\gamma_1+m_2\gamma_2}.}
$$

It is a positive-weighted average of subluminal [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md), so $|V|<c$.

In the [rest mass of a coalescing relativistic collision](../../../../../../rest-mass-of-a-coalescing-relativistic-collision.md), [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $P_3=P_1+P_2$ provided no [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) or [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) escapes in additional radiation. For the single outgoing particle, $u_3=c^2p/E=V$, so **its [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) in the original frame is $|V|$**. Its [rest mass](../../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is the invariant norm of the total [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md):

$$
m_3^2=\frac{E^2}{c^4}-\frac{p^2}{c^2}.
$$

Expanding and using $\gamma_i^2(1-u_i^2/c^2)=1$ gives

$$
\boxed{m_3^2=m_1^2+m_2^2
+2m_1m_2\gamma_1\gamma_2
\left(1-\frac{u_1u_2}{c^2}\right).}
$$

The positive square root is taken for $m_3$. The factor multiplying $2m_1m_2$ is the relative [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md), at least one. Therefore $m_3\geq m_1+m_2$, with equality when $u_1=u_2$; otherwise the initial relative [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) becomes internal [rest energy](../../../../../../rest-energy.md).
