<h1 id="3c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $\mathbf r'=e^t(\sqrt2,-\sin t-\cos t,\cos t-\sin t)$. The squared [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) is $4e^{2t}$, so the [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1|\mathbf r'|\,dt=2(e-1).}
$$

The [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) never vanishes, so this is a [regular curve](../../../../../../regular-curve.md) throughout its parameter range.
