<h1 id="5d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $X=G$ and let $g$ act by left multiplication $L_g(x)=gx$. Each $L_g$ is a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md), with inverse $L_{g^{-1}}$. Associativity gives $L_gL_h=L_{gh}$, so $g\mapsto L_g$ is a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) into $\operatorname{Sym}(G)$. If $L_g$ is the identity [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md), evaluating it at the identity element gives $g=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}(G),\qquad g\longmapsto L_g.}
$$

This is the [Cayley theorem](../../../../../../cayley-s-theorem.md), realized by the [left regular action](../../../../../../left-regular-action.md), which is a [faithful group action](../../../../../../faithful-group-action.md).
