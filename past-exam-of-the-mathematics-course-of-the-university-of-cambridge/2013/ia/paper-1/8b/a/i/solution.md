<h1 id="8b/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Construct the [matrix of a linear map](../../../../../../../matrix-representation-of-a-linear-map.md) by expressing the images of the basis vectors in that same basis:

$$
Le_j=\sum_{i=1}^2A_{ij}e_i.
$$

Thus the $j$th column is $[Le_j]_B$. For $v=v_1e_1+v_2e_2$, linearity gives

$$
\boxed{[Lv]_B=A[v]_B.}
$$

The column $[Lv]_B$ is a coordinate vector; reconstructing the actual vector requires $Lv=(A[v]_B)_1e_1+(A[v]_B)_2e_2$. This distinguishes the [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) from its basis-dependent matrix.
