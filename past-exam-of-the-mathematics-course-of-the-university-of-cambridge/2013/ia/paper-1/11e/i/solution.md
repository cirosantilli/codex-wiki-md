[Rolle's theorem](../../../../../../rolle-theorem.md) states that, if $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on $[a,b]$, [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(a,b)$, $a<b$, and $f(a)=f(b)$, then

$$
\boxed{\text{there exists }\xi\in(a,b)\text{ with }f'(\xi)=0.}
$$

The endpoint equality and both regularity hypotheses are part of the assertion.
