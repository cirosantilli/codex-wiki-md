<h1 id="1d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use column vectors in a right-handed frame, and interpret clockwise as viewed from the positive $x$-axis towards the origin. The [rotation matrix](../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) fixes $e_x$ and sends $e_y$ towards $-e_z$. With $q=1/\sqrt2$, its images of the basis vectors give

$$
\boxed{R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&q&q\\0&-q&q\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The transverse block is the [planar rotation](../../../../../../planar-rotation.md) through $-\pi/4$. Viewing from the other end of the axis reverses the meaning of clockwise and replaces $R$ by $R^T$; the viewpoint is not specified in the paper.
