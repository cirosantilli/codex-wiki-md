<h1 id="5a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is the sum of the diagonal entries:

$$
\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i=1}^n A_{ii}.
$$

Using the definitions of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) and [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md),

$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^TB)
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n A_{ji}B_{ji}
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n B_{ji}A_{ji}
=\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(B^TA)}.
$$

This is the symmetry of the real [Frobenius inner product](../../../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md).
