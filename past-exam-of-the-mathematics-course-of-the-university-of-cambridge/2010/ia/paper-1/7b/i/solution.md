<h1 id="7b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the definition of an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), there is a nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v$ with $Av=\lambda v$. Induction using [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
A^{r+1}v=A(A^rv)=A(\lambda^rv)=\lambda^rAv=\lambda^{r+1}v,
$$

starting from $r=1$. Thus the same nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) establishes

$$
\boxed{A^rv=\lambda^rv,\qquad \lambda^r\text{ is an eigenvalue of }A^r\quad(r\geq1).}
$$
