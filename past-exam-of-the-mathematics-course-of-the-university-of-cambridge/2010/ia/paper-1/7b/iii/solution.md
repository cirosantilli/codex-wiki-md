<h1 id="7b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The scalar [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $tI$ commutes with $A$, so

$$
A^2-t^2I=(A-tI)(A+tI).
$$

Taking [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md) and using their multiplicativity gives, with the specified [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) convention,

$$
\boxed{\chi_{A^2}(t^2)=\det(A-tI)\det(A+tI)
=\chi_A(t)\chi_A(-t).}
$$
