↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $f(0)=0$, its [difference quotient](../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) is

$$
\frac{f(h)-f(0)}h
=h\operatorname{sign}(h)\left|\cos\frac\pi h\right|
=|h|\left|\cos\frac\pi h\right|\qquad(h\ne0).
$$

The [absolute value](../../../../../../absolute-value.md) of [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) is at most $1$, so this quotient lies between $0$ and $|h|$. The [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) proves

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is differentiable at }0,\qquad f'(0)=0.}
$$
