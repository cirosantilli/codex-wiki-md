<h1 id="4e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $0<a\leq b$, we have $b^n\leq a^n+b^n\leq2b^n$. Taking positive $n$th roots yields

$$
b\leq(a^n+b^n)^{1/n}\leq b\,2^{1/n}.
$$

For every $\varepsilon>0$, the [binomial theorem](../../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) gives $(1+\varepsilon)^n\geq1+n\varepsilon>2$ eventually, so $2^{1/n}\to1$. The [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{(a^n+b^n)^{1/n}\longrightarrow b.}
$$
