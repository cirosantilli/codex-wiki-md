<h1 id="4e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Pairing the first and last terms of the [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md) gives $\sum_{k=1}^n k=n(n+1)/2$. Therefore the [limit of a sequence](../../../../../../limit-of-a-sequence.md) is

$$
\frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n^2}
=\frac12+\frac1{2n}\longrightarrow\boxed{\frac12}.
$$
