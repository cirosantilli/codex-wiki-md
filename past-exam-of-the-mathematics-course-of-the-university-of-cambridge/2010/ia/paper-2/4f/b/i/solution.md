**Zero correlation means that the variables are uncorrelated:** $E[(X-EX)(Y-EY)]=0$, or equivalently $E(XY)=EX\,EY$. It does not imply [independence](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). For example, if $X$ is uniform on $[-1,1]$ and $Y=X^2$, symmetry gives $EX=E(X^3)=0$, hence zero [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md), although $Y$ is determined by $X$. Both [random variables](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) are non-constant and have finite positive [variances](../../../../../../../variance-split.md).
