**Perfect correlation means an affine relation almost surely:** there are constants $a\ne0,b$ such that $Y=aX+b$ almost surely. The sign of $a$ is the sign of the [correlation coefficient](../../../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md). More precisely, equality in the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{Y-EY}{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}Y}}
=\rho(X,Y)\frac{X-EX}{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}X}}\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$
