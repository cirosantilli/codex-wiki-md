<h1 id="4f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $U=X-EX$ and $V=Y-EY$. Their second moments are the finite positive [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of $X,Y$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) for square-integrable [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) gives

$$
|E(UV)|^2\leq E(U^2)E(V^2)=\operatorname{Var}X\,\operatorname{Var}Y.
$$

Divide by the positive product of [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) and take square roots. **The correlation bound is $\boxed{-1\leq\rho(X,Y)\leq1}$.** As usual, non-constant here means not almost surely constant, so that the denominator is nonzero.
