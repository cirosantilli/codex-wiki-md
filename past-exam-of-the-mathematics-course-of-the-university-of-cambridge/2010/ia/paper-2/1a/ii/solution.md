<h1 id="1a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $L[y]_n=y_{n+3}-3y_{n+1}+2y_n$. For the exponential trial sequence $D2^n$,

$$
L[D2^n]=(8-6+2)D2^n=4D2^n.
$$

Thus $D=1/4$ gives a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md). The difference of any two solutions of the [inhomogeneous linear recurrence](../../../../../../inhomogeneous-linear-recurrence.md) satisfies the homogeneous [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) in part (i). **Therefore**

$$
\boxed{y_n=A+Bn+C(-2)^n+\frac{2^n}{4}.}
$$
