↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [superposition principle](../../../../../../superposition-principle.md) applies because the recurrence operator is linear. Add the [particular solutions](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) from parts (ii) and (iii), then add the general homogeneous [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) solution from part (i). **This gives**

$$
\boxed{y_n=A+Bn+C(-2)^n-\frac n{18}(-2)^n+\frac{2^n}{4}.}
$$
