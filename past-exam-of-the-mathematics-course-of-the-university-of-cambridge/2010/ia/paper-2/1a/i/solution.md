<h1 id="1a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Substitution of $y_n=r^n$ gives the [characteristic equation of a linear recurrence](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-linear-recurrence.md)

$$
r^3-3r+2=(r-1)^2(r+2)=0.
$$

The [repeated characteristic root of a linear recurrence](../../../../../../repeated-characteristic-root-of-a-linear-recurrence.md) $1$ supplies the solutions $1$ and $n$, while the simple root $-2$ supplies $(-2)^n$. They are linearly independent: their values at three consecutive indices have nonzero determinant. A third-order [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) is uniquely determined by three consecutive initial values, so these three solutions span its solution space. **The general solution is**

$$
\boxed{y_n=A+Bn+C(-2)^n.}
$$
