<h1 id="3f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a fixed horse $i$, [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the race results gives [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $p_i^m$ of winning every race. The events that different horses win every race are disjoint, so

$$
\boxed{Q=\sum_{i=1}^N p_i^m.}
$$

This is a homogeneous [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree $m$. Apply part (a) to the nonnegative numbers $p_i$, whose sum is one, to obtain **$\boxed{Q\geq N^{1-m}}$**. For $m>1$ the smallest [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) occurs when every horse has winning [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/N$; for a single race $Q=1$ regardless of these [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md).
