↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [congruence classes](../../../../../../congruence-class.md) in $\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z$, define

$$
\boxed{[a]+[b]=[a+b],\qquad [a][b]=[ab].}
$$

These operations are independent of representatives. If $a'=a+Nr$ and $b'=b+Ns$, then $(a'+b')-(a+b)=N(r+s)$ and $a'b'-ab=N(rb+sa+Nrs)$. Thus changing representatives leaves both results in the same [congruence class](../../../../../../congruence-class.md). The usual integer laws descend to make this a [commutative ring](../../../../../../commutative-ring.md) with zero $[0]$ and identity $[1]$.
