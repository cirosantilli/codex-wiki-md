<h1 id="1g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [decimal expansion](../../../../../../decimal-expansion.md) gives $M=\sum_{i=0}^s a_i10^i$. In [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic.md), $10\equiv-1\pmod {11}$, and hence

$$
\boxed{M\equiv\sum_{i=0}^s(-1)^ia_i\pmod {11}.}
$$

An integer is divisible by eleven exactly when its [congruence class](../../../../../../congruence-class.md) is zero, proving both directions of the alternating-digit test.
