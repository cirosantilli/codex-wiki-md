<h1 id="14e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $\omega(k)=k^2-i\beta k$ and $E=e^{-ikx+\omega t}$. Differentiation gives $(Eu)_t=E(u_t+\omega u)$, while

$$
\bigl(E[(ik+\beta)u+u_x]\bigr)_x
=E\left[u_{xx}+\beta u_x+(k^2-i\beta k)u\right].
$$

The two sides are equal precisely by the stated [advection-diffusion equation](../../../../../../advection-diffusion-equation.md). This supplies its divergence form in the $(x,t)$ plane.
