<h1 id="2f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says that a countable intersection of open dense sets in a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) is dense. Write $E=\{e_1,e_2,\ldots\}$. Each singleton is closed in a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) and has empty interior because there are no isolated points. Therefore every $X\setminus\{e_j\}$ is open and dense. Applying the theorem also to $G$ yields

$$
G\setminus E=G\cap\bigcap_{j\geq1}(X\setminus\{e_j\}),
$$

which is **dense in $X$**. Thus every nonempty open subset meets the required difference, not merely the original dense set $G$.
