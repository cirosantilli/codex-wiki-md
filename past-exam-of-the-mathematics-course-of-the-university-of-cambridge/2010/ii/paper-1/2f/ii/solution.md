**No.** Take the [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) $X=\mathbb R$ with its usual metric, $E=\mathbb Q$, and $G=\mathbb Q\cup(1,2)$. This $G$ is uncountable and dense, whereas

$$
G\setminus E=(1,2)\setminus\mathbb Q
$$

misses, for example, the nonempty open interval $(3,4)$. Uncountability does not replace the open-dense hypothesis in the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md).
