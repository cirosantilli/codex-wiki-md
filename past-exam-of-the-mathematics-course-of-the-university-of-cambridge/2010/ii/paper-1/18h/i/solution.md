<h1 id="18h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{P(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=X_1^q-X_1.}
$$

It is a nonzero polynomial, but every element of the [finite field](../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_q$ satisfies $\alpha^q=\alpha$. Thus it vanishes on every point of $\mathbb F_q^n$. Formal nonzero polynomials and nonzero polynomial functions need not coincide over a finite field.
