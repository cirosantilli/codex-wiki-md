<h1 id="25h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract the sphere equation from the last equality to obtain $x_2^2=1/2$. Thus the equality locus is the disjoint union of two spheres

$$
x_2=\pm1/\sqrt2,\qquad x_1^2+x_3^2+x_4^2=1/2,
$$

each a smooth two-dimensional manifold. The two equality gradients are independent there: $x_2\ne0$ and at least one of $x_1,x_3,x_4$ is nonzero.

On this locus the inequality becomes $1-2x_4^2\leq a$, or

$$
|x_4|\geq\sqrt{(1-a)/2}.
$$

Since $0<a<1$, this cuts out two closed caps on each sphere. At the cap boundary, $x_1^2+x_3^2=a/2>0$, so the restricted differential of $x_4$ is nonzero. The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) for a sublevel set therefore gives a [manifold with boundary](../../../../../../manifold-with-boundary.md). Explicitly it consists of four disks, with four boundary circles, and

$$
\boxed{\dim X=2.}
$$
