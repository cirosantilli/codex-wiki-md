<h1 id="17f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A graph $G$ with $v$ vertices is a subgraph of $K_v$. A monochromatic $K_v$ therefore contains a monochromatic copy of $G$; the copy need not be induced. Thus the preceding finite [Ramsey theorem](../../../../../../../ramsey-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{R(G)\leq R(v)<\infty.}
$$
