<h1 id="37a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The steady rectilinear equation is $u''(y)=-G$, with no-slip conditions $u(0)=0$ and $u(a)=U$. Two integrations give the [Couette-Poiseuille flow](../../../../../../couette-poiseuille-flow.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u(y)=\left[\frac{Uy}{a}+\frac G2y(a-y)\right]\widehat x,\qquad v=w=0.}
$$

The first term is driven by the moving wall and the second by the specified pressure gradient.
