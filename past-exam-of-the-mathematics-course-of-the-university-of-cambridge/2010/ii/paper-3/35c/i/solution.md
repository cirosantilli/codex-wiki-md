<h1 id="35c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The one-particle [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is

$$
Z_1=\int_0^\infty KV\varepsilon^{1/2}e^{-\beta\varepsilon}d\varepsilon
=\frac{KV\sqrt\pi}{2}\beta^{-3/2}.
$$

The classical indistinguishable noninteracting gas has $Z_N=Z_1^N/N!$; for labeled particles omit $N!$, which changes none of the requested [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md). With $\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$,

$$
\boxed{E=-\partial_\beta\log Z_N=\frac32Nk_BT,\qquad
C_V=\frac32Nk_B.}
$$

The allowed equation of state gives $PV=Nk_BT$, so the [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy.md) is $E+PV=5Nk_BT/2$ and $\boxed{C_P=5Nk_B/2}$, at fixed particle number.
