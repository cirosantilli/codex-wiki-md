<h1 id="35c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\alpha_V=V^{-1}(\partial V/\partial T)_P$. The [Maxwell relations](../../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) give

$$
dS=\frac{C_P}{T}dT-V\alpha_VdP,
\qquad dV=V\alpha_VdT-V\kappa_TdP.
$$

Setting $dS=0$ gives $(\partial T/\partial P)_S=TV\alpha_V/C_P$, hence $\kappa_S=\kappa_T-TV\alpha_V^2/C_P$. Also $dS=(C_V/T)dT+(\partial P/\partial T)_VdV$, and the volume differential gives $(\partial P/\partial T)_V=\alpha_V/\kappa_T$. Evaluating along constant [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) therefore yields $C_P-C_V=TV\alpha_V^2/\kappa_T$. Eliminate $\alpha_V$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\kappa_S=\kappa_T\frac{C_V}{C_P},\qquad
\frac{C_P}{C_V}=\frac{\kappa_T}{\kappa_S}.}
$$

These [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are taken at fixed particle number, in a thermodynamically regular stable phase.
