↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $r=n/m$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\sum_v\frac1{d(v)+1}\ge\frac{n^2}{\sum_v(d(v)+1)}
=\frac{n^2}{2e(G)+n}
=\frac{n^2}{mr(r-1)+mr}=m.
$$

The proved [Caro-Wei bound](../../../../../../caro-wei-bound.md) therefore gives **an [independent set](../../../../../../independent-set-graph-theory.md) of at least $m$ vertices**.
