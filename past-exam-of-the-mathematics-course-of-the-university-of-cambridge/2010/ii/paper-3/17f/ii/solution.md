**No.** The disjoint union of $m$ copies of the [complete graph](../../../../../../complete-graph.md) $K_{n/m}$ has exactly the prescribed number of edges and has independence number $m$: an [independent set](../../../../../../independent-set-graph-theory.md) can choose at most one vertex from each component, and choosing one from every component attains $m$.
