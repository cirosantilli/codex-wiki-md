<h1 id="24h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $p\in D$, each of the given ambient rotations fixes $p$, rotates its tangent [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) and preserves the local surface. Naturality of the [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) under that rotation makes $S_p$ commute with every planar rotation. A symmetric real two-by-two operator commuting with all rotations is scalar, so every point of $D$ is an [umbilical point](../../../../../../umbilical-point.md). The trace-free part of the [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) is continuous. Density therefore makes it zero everywhere on $S$.

Locally write $S=\kappa I$. The [Codazzi equation](../../../../../../codazzi-equation.md) gives $(X\kappa)Y=(Y\kappa)X$ for tangent fields $X,Y$; choosing independent fields proves $d\kappa=0$. Thus the surface is locally a [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) if $\kappa=0$, or has constant spherical center

$$
p+\frac{N(p)}\kappa,
$$

since its differential is $I-S/\kappa=0$. A change of normal reverses both $N$ and $\kappa$, leaving this center unchanged, so these local descriptions patch across the connected surface. In the flat case the whole surface lies in one [plane](../../../../../../plane.md), where a [regular surface](../../../../../../smooth-surface.md) without boundary is open; a nonempty such set cannot also be compact and closed. Hence the flat case is excluded. In the other case $S$ lies on one [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) of radius $1/|\kappa|$. Its inclusion in that [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) is locally open, since both have dimension two, and its [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) makes the image closed. [Connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) of the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) forces the image to be the entire [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md). Therefore **$S$ is a [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md)**.
