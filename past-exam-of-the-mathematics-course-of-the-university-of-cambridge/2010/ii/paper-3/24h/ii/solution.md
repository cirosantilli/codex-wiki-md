↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) is the self-adjoint tangent map $S_p=-dN_p$, using a locally chosen [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\kappa_1,\kappa_2$ are the [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md), and their [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) give the [principal directions](../../../../../../principal-direction.md). At an [umbilical point](../../../../../../umbilical-point.md) they coincide, so $S_p=\kappa I$ and every tangent direction is principal. Reversing the normal changes both curvature signs but does not change the [principal directions](../../../../../../principal-direction.md) or umbilicity.
