<h1 id="12f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a continuous loop in $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$, choose a continuous argument lift $\theta:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ with $\gamma(t)/|\gamma(t)|=e^{i\theta(t)}$. Such a lift is constructed by subdividing the interval into pieces whose images lie in an argument chart and matching the arguments at their endpoints. The [winding number of a continuous closed path](../../../../../../winding-number-of-a-continuous-closed-path.md) is

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma;0)=\frac{\theta(1)-\theta(0)}{2\pi}\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Changing the initial lift adds one constant multiple of $2\pi$ and does not change this [integer](../../../../../../integer.md).
