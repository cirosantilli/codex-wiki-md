<h1 id="12f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A loop [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) is a continuous $H:[0,1]^2\to\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$ with $H(0,t)=\gamma_0(t)$, $H(1,t)=\gamma_1(t)$ and $H(s,0)=H(s,1)$ for every $s$. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) gives a positive lower bound $r$ for $|H|$ and uniform [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). Partition the $s$ interval finely enough that neighboring loops $\eta,\delta$ satisfy $|\eta(t)-\delta(t)|<r$. Their quotient lies in $|z-1|<1$, and has a single-valued continuous argument with equal endpoint values. Its [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md) is zero. Arguments of a product add, so $w(\eta)=w(\delta)+w(\eta/\delta)=w(\delta)$. Applying this at every step proves

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma_0;0)=w(\gamma_1;0).}
$$

This proves [homotopy invariance of winding number](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md) directly, including continuous curves without differentiability.
