<h1 id="1g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [continued fraction](../../../../../../continued-fraction.md) starts $\sqrt{29}=[5;2,\ldots]$, since $1/(\sqrt{29}-5)=(\sqrt{29}+5)/4$ has [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) part $2$. Its next [continued fraction convergent](../../../../../../continued-fraction-convergent.md) is $5+1/2=11/2$. Direct substitution gives

$$
\boxed{x=11,\qquad y=2,\qquad 11^2-29\cdot2^2=5.}
$$
