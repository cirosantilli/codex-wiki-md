<h1 id="18h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose the radicals with positive imaginary part. The [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) are

$$
\boxed{\zeta_3=\frac{-1+\sqrt{-3}}2,\qquad
\zeta_5=\frac{\sqrt5-1+\sqrt{-10-2\sqrt5}}4.}
$$

The first comes from $z^2+z+1=0$. For the second, $t=\zeta_5+\zeta_5^{-1}$ obeys $t^2+t-1=0$, so $t=(\sqrt5-1)/2$; solving $z^2-tz+1=0$ gives the displayed expression. The [Kummer extension](../../../../../../kummer-extension.md) towers are

$$
\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})=\mathbb Q(\mu_3),
\qquad
\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\subset
\mathbb Q(\sqrt5,\sqrt{-10-2\sqrt5})=\mathbb Q(\mu_5).
$$

Each step is quadratic and the base contains the second roots of unity. The second tower really has degrees $2,2$: its intermediate field is real, while the added radical is nonreal, and $\sqrt5=1+2(\zeta_5+\zeta_5^{-1})$ belongs to the cyclotomic field.
