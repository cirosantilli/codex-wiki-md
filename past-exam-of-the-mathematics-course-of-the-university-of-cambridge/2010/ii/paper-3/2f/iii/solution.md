<h1 id="2f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If the [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) converge uniformly on the full annulus to $g$, their limit is continuous and equals $f$ on the slit annulus by part (ii). That subset is dense in the full annulus, so $g=f$ everywhere. The [Cauchy integral theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) and [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on the unit circle then give

$$
\boxed{\int_{|z|=1}f(z)\,dz=\lim_j\int_{|z|=1}p_j(z)\,dz=0.}
$$
