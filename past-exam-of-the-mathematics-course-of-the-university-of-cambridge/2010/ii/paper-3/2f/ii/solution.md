<h1 id="2f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the argument in $(0,2\pi)$ on the slit annulus and let

$$
K_j=\{z:1/2\le|z|\le2,\quad1/j\le\arg z\le2\pi-1/j\}.
$$

These [compact sets](../../../../../../compact-space.md) increase to the slit annulus, and each has connected complement: the omitted angular corridor joins the inner disk to the outside of the outer disk. The [polynomial Runge theorem](../../../../../../polynomial-runge-theorem.md) says that a function holomorphic near a [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md) with connected complement can be approximated uniformly there by [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Choose $p_j$ with $\sup_{K_j}|p_j-f|<1/j$. Every compact subset $K$ of the slit annulus has positive distance from the removed positive ray, so $K\subseteq K_j$ for all sufficiently large $j$. Hence **$p_j\to f$ uniformly on every such compact subset**.
