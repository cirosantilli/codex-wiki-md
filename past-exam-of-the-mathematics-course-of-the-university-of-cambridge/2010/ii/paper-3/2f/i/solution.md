<h1 id="2f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) $f(z)=1/z$ on a neighborhood of the annulus. Every [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) zero around the unit circle by the [Cauchy integral theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md), while $\int_{|z|=1}z^{-1}\,dz=2\pi i$. [Uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on the annulus would imply convergence of these [contour integrals](../../../../../../contour-integral.md), since their difference is bounded by $2\pi$ times the uniform error. Thus **uniform [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) approximation on the full annulus need not exist**.
