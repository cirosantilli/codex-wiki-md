<h1 id="25h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the anticlockwise boundary of the region, the inward normal is the left normal, so the [signed curvature](../../../../../../signed-curvature.md) obeys the [turning tangent theorem](../../../../../../turning-tangent-theorem.md) $\int_0^L k(s)\,ds=2\pi$. Therefore some $s_0$ has $k(s_0)\leq2\pi/L$. The enclosed area satisfies $A\geq d_1d_2$, and the [planar isoperimetric inequality](../../../../../../planar-isoperimetric-inequality.md) gives $L\geq2\sqrt{\pi A}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{k(s_0)\leq\frac{2\pi}{L}\leq\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{A}}\leq\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{d_1d_2}}}.
$$

This uses the interpretation of the curve as a simple boundary traversed once; the inward normal and enclosed region in the question have that meaning.
