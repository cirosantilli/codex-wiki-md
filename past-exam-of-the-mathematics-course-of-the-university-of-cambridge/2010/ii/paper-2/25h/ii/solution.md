The [Fenchel theorem](../../../../../../fenchel-theorem.md) states that every closed regular space curve has [total curvature](../../../../../../total-curvature.md) at least $2\pi$:

$$
\boxed{\int\kappa(s)\,ds\geq2\pi}.
$$

Equality holds precisely for a convex plane curve, traversed once.
