In the [induced character](../../../../../../induced-character.md) formula above, there are no $x\in G$ with $x^{-1}gx\in H$ when the [conjugacy class](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) of $g$ misses $H$. The sum is empty, so **$(\operatorname{Ind}_H^G\psi)(g)=0$.**
