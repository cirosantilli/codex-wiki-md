<h1 id="1g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Euler's criterion gives $\chi(-1)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}=1$ in this case. There are equally many nonzero [quadratic residues](../../../../../../quadratic-residue.md) and [quadratic nonresidues](../../../../../../quadratic-nonresidue.md), so $\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\chi(n)=0$. Replacing $n$ by $p-n$ and using the [multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol](../../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-legendre-symbol.md) gives

$$
S=\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}(p-n)\chi(p-n)=p\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\chi(n)-S=-S.
$$

Consequently **$\boxed{S=0}$.**
