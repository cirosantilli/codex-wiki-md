<h1 id="39a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [39A](../39a.md)

Use the printed unnormalized inverse [discrete Fourier transform](../../../../../discrete-fourier-transform.md), with positive exponent: $x_\ell=\sum_j\omega_n^{j\ell}y_j$. Its natural domain and codomain are $\mathbb C^n$; the printed real-to-real designation is not literal, as the first example below has complex output. There is no factor $1/n$ in the convention of this question.
