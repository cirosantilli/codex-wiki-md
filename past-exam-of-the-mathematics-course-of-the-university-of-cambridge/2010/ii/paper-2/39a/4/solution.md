<h1 id="39a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For $y=(1,1,-1,-1)$, the even and odd subsequences are both $(1,-1)$ and each length-two inverse [discrete Fourier transform](../../../../../../discrete-fourier-transform.md) is $(0,2)$. Since $\omega_4=i$, the [FFT butterfly](../../../../../../fft-butterfly.md) gives

$$
\boxed{x=(0,\ 2+2i,\ 0,\ 2-2i)}.
$$

For $y=(1,-1,1,-1)$, the even transform is $(2,0)$ and the odd transform is $(-2,0)$. The same assembly gives

$$
\boxed{x=(0,\ 0,\ 4,\ 0)}.
$$
