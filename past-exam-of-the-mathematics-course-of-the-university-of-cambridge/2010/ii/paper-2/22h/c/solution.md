<h1 id="22h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The finite truncations of $x=(1,1/2,1/3,\ldots)$ lie in $c_{00}$ and converge in the [supremum norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) to $x\notin c_{00}$. Thus **$c_{00}$ is not closed.** Every element of $c_0$ is the norm limit of its finite truncations, while $c_0$ is closed and contains $c_{00}$. Hence **$\boxed{\overline{c_{00}}=c_0}$.**
