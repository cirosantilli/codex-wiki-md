<h1 id="17f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a disjoint union of $n$ single edges, each joining one vertex of $X$ to one of $Y$. Its unique [matching in a graph](../../../../../../matching-graph-theory.md) covering $X$ contains every edge, while any singleton $A\subset X$ satisfies $|\Gamma(A)|=|A|=1$. For $n\geq2$ this $A$ is nonempty and proper. This illustrates why connectedness was needed in part (ii).
