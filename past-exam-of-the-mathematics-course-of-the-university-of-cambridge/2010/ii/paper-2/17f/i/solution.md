<h1 id="17f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix any edge $xy$. After deleting $x$ and $y$, every nonempty subset $A$ of $X\setminus\{x\}$ satisfies $|\Gamma(A)\setminus\{y\}|\geq|A|$, by the strict inequality in the question. The [Hall marriage theorem](../../../../../../hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) gives a [matching in a graph](../../../../../../matching-graph-theory.md) for the remainder; adding $xy$ gives a matching covering $X$ and containing the prescribed edge. The case $|X|=1$ is immediate.
