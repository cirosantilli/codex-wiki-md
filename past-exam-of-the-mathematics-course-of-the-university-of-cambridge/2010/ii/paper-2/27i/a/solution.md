<h1 id="27i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Independence and the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) give

$$
\phi_{S_k}(\theta)=\left(\frac{k\mu}{k\mu-\theta}\right)^k,\qquad\theta<k\mu.
$$

For each fixed $\theta$, taking logarithms and using $-\log(1-x)\sim x$ yields $\phi_{S_k}(\theta)\to e^{\theta/\mu}$. **The limiting service time is the constant $S=1/\mu$.** Its moment-generating function is exactly this limit.
