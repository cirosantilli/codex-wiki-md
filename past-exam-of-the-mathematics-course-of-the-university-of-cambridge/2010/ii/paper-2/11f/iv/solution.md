<h1 id="11f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**No such map exists.** If $H$ fixed the boundary, it would satisfy the hypothesis of part (iii) for every $\varepsilon>0$. Choose $0<\varepsilon<1-|q|$. Then part (iii) would give $q\in H(B)$, contradicting the stipulated omission of $q$. Equivalently, radial projection from the omitted point would turn $H$ into a forbidden [retraction](../../../../../../retraction.md).
