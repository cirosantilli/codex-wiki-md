↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Again **$f=0$.** The [change of variables](../../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $t=x^2$ gives

$$
0=\int_0^1 f(x)x^{2n+1}\,dx=\frac12\int_0^1 f(\sqrt t)t^n\,dt\qquad(n\geq0).
$$

Apply part (i) to the [continuous function](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $h(t)=f(\sqrt t)$. It vanishes on the whole interval, so does $f$.
