<h1 id="2f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The given [moments](../../../../../../../moment.md) imply $\int_0^1 f(x)P(x)\,dx=0$ for every [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P$. Choose [polynomials](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P_k$ converging uniformly to $f$ by the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md). Then

$$
\left|\int_0^1 f^2\right|=\left|\int_0^1 f(f-P_k)\right|\leq\|f-P_k\|_\infty\int_0^1|f|\longrightarrow0.
$$

A nonnegative [continuous function](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) with zero [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) vanishes everywhere. Hence **$f=0$.**
