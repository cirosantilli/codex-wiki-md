The [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) says that for every real [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f$ on $[0,1]$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a real [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P$ with $\sup_{[0,1]}|f-P|<\varepsilon$. Equivalently, [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) are dense in $C[0,1]$ in the [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md).
