↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An irreducible nonexplosive [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) has an equilibrium probability distribution exactly when it is positive recurrent. The distribution is unique, and gives the long-run fraction of elapsed time in each state. A null recurrent chain has no equilibrium probability distribution. The finite one-state absorbing case is trivially stationary; the return-cycle descriptions concern nontrivial irreducible classes.
