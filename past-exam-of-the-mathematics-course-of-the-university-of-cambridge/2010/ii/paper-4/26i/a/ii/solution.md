↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A state is recurrent if, after departure, its return probability is one. For an irreducible nonexplosive [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md), this is equivalent to recurrence of its [jump chain](../../../../../../../jump-chain.md) and is shared by all states.
