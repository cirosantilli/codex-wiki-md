↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A state of an irreducible [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is transient if, after departure, its return probability is less than one. Equivalently its [jump chain](../../../../../../../jump-chain.md) visits that state only finitely often almost surely. This is a class property.
