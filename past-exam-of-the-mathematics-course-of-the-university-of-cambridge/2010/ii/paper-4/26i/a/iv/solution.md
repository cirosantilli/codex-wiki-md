<h1 id="26i/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A recurrent state is null recurrent if its expected elapsed return-cycle time is infinite. Recurrence itself agrees with that of the [jump chain](../../../../../../../jump-chain.md), but positive recurrence need not agree, because large states can have very short holding times.
