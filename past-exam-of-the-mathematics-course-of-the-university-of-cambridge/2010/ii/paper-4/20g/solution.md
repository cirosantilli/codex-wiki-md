<h1 id="20g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [20G](../20g.md)

A rational root of the monic cubic would be one of $\pm1,\pm3$, and substitution excludes all four. A reducible cubic over $\mathbb Q$ would have a rational root, so the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is irreducible and

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=3.}
$$

Its [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) is $-4(-1)^3-27(3)^2=-239$. The integer 239 is prime: it has no prime divisor among $2,3,5,7,11,13$, the primes not exceeding its square root.

The [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](../../../../../discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) for an integral primitive element is

$$
\operatorname{disc}(1,\alpha,\alpha^2)
=[\mathcal O_K:\mathbb Z[\alpha]]^2\operatorname{disc}(K).
$$

Both discriminants are integers, and the left side is $-239$. Its squarefreeness forces the positive index to be one. Since $\alpha$ is an [algebraic integer](../../../../../algebraic-integer.md) by its monic equation,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\alpha].}
$$
