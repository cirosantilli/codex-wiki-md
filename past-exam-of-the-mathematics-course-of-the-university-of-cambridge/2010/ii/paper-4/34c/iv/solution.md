<h1 id="34c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For occupation numbers zero or one, the energy and particle number are additive over modes. The [grand canonical partition function](../../../../../../grand-canonical-partition-function.md) factorizes:

$$
\mathcal Z=\prod_r\left(1+e^{-(\epsilon_r-\mu)/(kT)}\right).
$$

Dividing the product of Boltzmann weights by this product gives

$$
\boxed{\overline\rho_i=\prod_r
\frac{e^{-n_r(\epsilon_r-\mu)/(kT)}}
{1+e^{-(\epsilon_r-\mu)/(kT)}}.}
$$

The marginal mean occupation is therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline n_r=\frac1{e^{(\epsilon_r-\mu)/(kT)}+1}.}
$$

This is the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) of a noninteracting [Fermi gas](../../../../../../fermi-gas.md): the exclusion principle allows only zero or one fermion in each single-particle state, including its spin label.
