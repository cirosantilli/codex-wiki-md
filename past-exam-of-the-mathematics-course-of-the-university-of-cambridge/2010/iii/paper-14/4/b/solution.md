<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [canonical coframe of a surface unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-coframe-of-a-surface-unit-tangent-bundle.md) $(\alpha,\theta,\omega)$ and its dual $(X,H,V)$. In constant curvature $-1$, the decaying normal [Jacobi fields](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) have data $(j,j')=(a,-a)$, so the stable line is generated by $H-V$. The [weak stable bundle](../../../../../../weak-stable-bundle.md) is therefore

$$
E^{ws}=\operatorname{span}\{X,H-V\}.
$$

It is annihilated by the nonvanishing form $\zeta=\theta+\omega$. The structure equations now give

$$
d\theta=\alpha\wedge\omega,\qquad d\omega=\alpha\wedge\theta,
\qquad d\zeta=\alpha\wedge\zeta.
$$

Thus the auxiliary form in the [Godbillon-Vey invariant](../../../../../../godbillon-vey-invariant.md) can be taken as $\eta=\alpha$. In the natural orientation given by the oriented base and positive fibre rotation, put $\mu=\alpha\wedge\theta\wedge\omega$. Then

$$
\eta\wedge d\eta=\alpha\wedge\omega\wedge\theta=-\mu.
$$

This sign is important: in these conventions the contact three-form is the negative of the positive [Liouville volume of a surface geodesic flow](../../../../../../liouville-volume-of-a-surface-geodesic-flow.md).

Integration around each circle fibre gives $\int_{S_xM}\omega=2\pi$, while $\alpha\wedge\theta=\pi^*\Omega_a$. Hence $\int_{SM}\mu=2\pi\operatorname{Area}(M)$. The [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) and $K=-1$ give $-\operatorname{Area}(M)=2\pi\chi(M)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{gv}(E^{ws})=-2\pi\operatorname{Area}(M)=4\pi^2\chi(M)}.
$$

For genus $g\ge2$, this is $-8\pi^2(g-1)$ in the stated orientation. The opposite orientation of $SM$ reverses the sign.
