<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonsingular [smooth flow](../../../../../../smooth-flow.md) generated by $F$ on a [closed manifold](../../../../../../closed-manifold.md), the [Anosov flow](../../../../../../anosov-flow.md) condition is a continuous $D\phi_t$-invariant splitting

$$
TN=E^s\oplus\mathbb RF\oplus E^u
$$

and constants $C,\lambda>0$ such that, for all $t\ge0$,

$$
\boxed{\|D\phi_t v_s\|\le Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v_s\|,\qquad
\|D\phi_{-t}v_u\|\le Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v_u\|}.
$$

The [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) can come from any auxiliary [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md); compactness makes the resulting definitions equivalent. The centre direction is the flow direction, with $F$ nowhere zero.

One construction is a [suspension flow](../../../../../../suspension-flow.md). The [hyperbolic toral automorphism](../../../../../../hyperbolic-toral-automorphism.md) of $\mathbb T^2$ defined by

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
$$

has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_\pm=(3\pm\sqrt5)/2$, with $0<\lambda_-<1<\lambda_+$. Form the closed three-dimensional [mapping torus](../../../../../../mapping-torus.md) $(\mathbb T^2\times[0,1])/((x,1)\sim(Ax,0))$ and move in the second coordinate at unit speed. Each crossing of the section applies $A$. The two one-dimensional [eigenspaces](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) give invariant transverse bundles, and after $k$ crossings their derivatives multiply by $\lambda_-^k$ and $\lambda_+^k$. The difference between $k$ and elapsed time is bounded, so any smooth [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) gives the required uniform exponential estimates. The vertical direction supplies the centre line.

A different construction is the [geodesic flow](../../../../../../geodesic-flow.md) on the [unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../unit-tangent-bundle.md) of a closed oriented [hyperbolic surface](../../../../../../hyperbolic-surface.md). This bundle is a closed three-manifold. In curvature $-1$, a normal [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) along a unit-speed geodesic satisfies $j''-j=0$. The initial data $(j,j')=(a,-a)$ give $j(t)=ae^{-t}$, while $(a,a)$ give $j(t)=ae^t$. The linearized [geodesic flow](../../../../../../geodesic-flow.md) transports these initial data as $(j(t),j'(t))$, so they give one stable and one unstable direction with precisely those exponential rates. The remaining tangent direction is the geodesic [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md). Thus this also constructs an [Anosov flow](../../../../../../anosov-flow.md), using geometric rather than return-map hyperbolicity.
