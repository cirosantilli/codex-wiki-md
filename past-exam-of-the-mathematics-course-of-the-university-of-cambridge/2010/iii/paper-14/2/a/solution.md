<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Livsic theorem](../../../../../../livsic-theorem.md) for a topologically transitive [Anosov flow](../../../../../../anosov-flow.md) says that a Hölder function $h:N\to\mathbb R$ satisfies the periodic-orbit obstruction

$$
\int_0^T h(\phi_t x)\,dt=0\quad\text{whenever }\phi_Tx=x
$$

if and only if there is a Hölder function $u$ satisfying the flow coboundary identity

$$
\boxed{u(\phi_t x)-u(x)=\int_0^t h(\phi_sx)\,ds}.
$$

The solution is differentiable along orbits and $Fu=h$ in that sense. For a smooth flow and smooth $h$, the smooth regularity version gives $u\in C^\infty(N)$, so $Fu=h$ holds smoothly. The solution is unique up to an additive constant: the difference of two solutions is invariant and must be constant on a dense orbit and then everywhere by continuity. Necessity of the periodic obstruction follows immediately by taking $t=T$ in the displayed identity.
