<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Identify the diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) with $\mathbb C^n$ through its coordinates $(h_1,\ldots,h_n)$, and identify real roots with coordinate vectors using the invariant Euclidean [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md). A [Weyl reflection](../../../../../../weyl-reflection.md) is $s_\alpha(h)=h-2(h,\alpha)\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)$. It does not change when $\alpha$ is replaced by $-\alpha$. For the [Cn root system](../../../../../../cn-root-system.md), its explicit actions are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{root, up to sign}&\text{changed coordinates}\\\hline
2\varepsilon_i&h_i\mapsto-h_i\\
\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j&(h_i,h_j)\mapsto(h_j,h_i)\\
\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j&(h_i,h_j)\mapsto(-h_j,-h_i)
\end{array}}
$$

Every coordinate not displayed stays fixed. Thus each generating [Weyl reflection](../../../../../../weyl-reflection.md) is a signed permutation, so the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is contained in the [signed symmetric group](../../../../../../hyperoctahedral-group.md). Conversely, the first row supplies each independent sign change and the second supplies every coordinate [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md); these generate the entire [signed symmetric group](../../../../../../hyperoctahedral-group.md). This proves the [Cn Weyl group](../../../../../../cn-weyl-group.md) identification, including its size:

$$
\boxed{W\cong(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\rtimes S_n,\qquad |W|=2^nn!.}
$$
