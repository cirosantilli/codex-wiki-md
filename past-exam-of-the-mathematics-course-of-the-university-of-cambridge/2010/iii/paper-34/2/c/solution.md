<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Expected death counts are the number of users multiplied by the death risk per user, with matching time windows and definitions. Relative to the [MDMA](../../../../../../mdma.md) benchmark, the proposed use prevalence contributes a multiplier of $0.6$ and the per-user fatality risk contributes a multiplier of $0.5$. Hence

$$
\boxed{30\times0.6\times0.5=9\text{ mephedrone-only deaths}.}
$$

This assumes comparable user exposure durations and recording of single-drug deaths. Population death counts alone would not identify a per-user [risk ratio](../../../../../../risk-ratio.md) without the prevalence denominator.
