<h1 id="2/i/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

A faster expansion at fixed temperature makes the weak reaction rate fall below $H$ sooner, at higher temperature, leaving a larger [neutron-to-proton ratio](../../../../../../../neutron-to-proton-ratio.md). It also shortens the time before the [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) ends, reducing neutron decay. **The helium-4 abundance increases**, assuming nuclear rates and the temperature history of the particle bath are otherwise unmodified.
