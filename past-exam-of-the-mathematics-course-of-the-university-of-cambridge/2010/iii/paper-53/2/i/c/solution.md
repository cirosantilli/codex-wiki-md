<h1 id="2/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A thermally populated extra light [neutrino](../../../../../../../neutrino.md) species is relativistic during nucleosynthesis; its $0.001\,{\rm eV}$ mass is negligible at MeV temperatures. One neutrino-antineutrino species adds $(7/8)\times2=7/4$ to $g_*$. Thus the expansion rate increases by $\sqrt{12.5/10.75}$ and the freeze-out temperature by $(12.5/10.75)^{1/6}$. Earlier freeze-out leaves more neutrons, while faster cooling leaves less time for decay. **The helium-4 abundance increases.** This assumes the new species was populated at the standard neutrino temperature; an unpopulated species would not contribute this extra density.
