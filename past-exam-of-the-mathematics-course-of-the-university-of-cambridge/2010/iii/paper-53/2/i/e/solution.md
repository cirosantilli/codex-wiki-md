<h1 id="2/i/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The helium yield is primarily neutron-limited, so small changes in [cosmological baryon density](../../../../../../../cosmological-baryon-density.md) have only a weak effect compared with their effect on residual deuterium. Increasing the baryon-to-photon ratio makes deuterium destruction less effective relative to its formation and releases the [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) at a slightly higher temperature. Nuclear reactions also proceed faster. Fewer neutrons decay before capture, so **a small increase in baryon density slightly increases helium-4; a decrease slightly lowers it.**
