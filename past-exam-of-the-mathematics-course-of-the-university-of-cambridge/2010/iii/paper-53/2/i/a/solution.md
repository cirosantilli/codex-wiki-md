↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Reducing the [gravitational constant](../../../../../../../gravitational-constant.md) decreases $H$ at fixed temperature. The [helium sensitivity to the expansion rate](../../../../../../../helium-sensitivity-to-the-expansion-rate.md) has two effects: weak interconversion persists to a lower freeze-out temperature, leaving fewer neutrons, and the universe takes longer to reach the deuterium-burning temperature, allowing more [free-neutron decay after freeze-out](../../../../../../../free-neutron-decay-after-freeze-out.md). **Both lower the helium-4 abundance.** In particular $T_f\propto G^{1/6}$ and $t(T)\propto G^{-1/2}$.
