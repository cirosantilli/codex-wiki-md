<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [tensor normalization with unit-norm polarization tensors](../../../../../../tensor-normalization-with-unit-norm-polarization-tensors.md) identifies the canonically normalized real scalar for each helicity as $\delta\Phi_{(p)}=M_{\rm Pl}\psi_{(p)}$. The two polarization actions are independent and have precisely the massless scalar normalization, so the vacuum fluctuations derived in the introductory solution apply to each of them:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P_\psi(k)=\frac{\mathcal P_{\delta\Phi}(k)}{M_{\rm Pl}^2}
=\frac{H_k^2}{4\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^2}}.
$$

In the isotropic [Bunch-Davies vacuum](../../../../../../bunch-davies-vacuum.md), the independent helicities have no cross correlation. Translation invariance gives the wavevector delta function, and the dimensionless-spectrum convention gives

$$
\boxed{\langle\psi_{(p)}(\mathbf k)\psi_{(q)}^*(\mathbf k')\rangle
=\frac{2\pi^2}{k^3}\delta_{pq}\mathcal P_\psi(k)
\,\delta^3(\mathbf k-\mathbf k')}.
$$

This is power per polarization in the printed metric convention $h_{ij}=2E_{ij}^T$ and unit-norm polarization basis. Summing polarizations or quoting a spectrum for $h_{ij}$ changes normalization factors; neither operation belongs in the displayed per-polarization $\mathcal P_\psi$.
