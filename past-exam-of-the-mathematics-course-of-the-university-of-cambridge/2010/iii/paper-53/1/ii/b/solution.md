<h1 id="1/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $t>0$, $\dot a=nt^{n-1}>0$ and $\ddot a=n(n-1)t^{n-2}$. Consequently **inflation occurs exactly when $\boxed{n>1}$.** The case $n=1$ is coasting, and $0<n<1$ is decelerating. The [Hubble slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../../hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md) is $\epsilon_H=-\dot H/H^2=1/n$, giving the same acceleration criterion $\epsilon_H<1$.
