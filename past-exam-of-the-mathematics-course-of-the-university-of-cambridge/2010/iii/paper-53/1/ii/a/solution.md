<h1 id="1/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A physical positive scale factor here requires $t>1/C$. In that domain $\dot a=1/t>0$ but $\ddot a=-1/t^2<0$. **There is no inflationary expansion.** The range $0<t<1/C$ has a negative printed scale factor and cannot supply a positive expanding branch of this model.
