<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A matter worldline has coordinate velocity $v^i=dx^i/d\eta$. On that same worldline,

$$
\frac{d\widetilde x^i}{d\widetilde\eta}
=\frac{v^i+L^{i\prime}+v^j\partial_jL^i}
{1+T'+v^j\partial_jT}
=v^i+L^{i\prime}+O(\text{perturbation}^2).
$$

Thus **$\widetilde v^i=v^i+L^{i\prime}$.** A time-dependent spatial relabelling makes a physically unchanged worldline move relative to the new coordinate grid. This is the frame dependence of the coordinate [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md), not an added physical acceleration.
