<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Each element of the [Galois group](../../../../../../../galois-group.md) preserves the extending [absolute value on a field](../../../../../../../absolute-value-algebra.md), because composing it with that [absolute value on a field](../../../../../../../absolute-value-algebra.md) gives another extension, equal to the original by part (a). Thus all conjugates of $x$ have the same [absolute value on a field](../../../../../../../absolute-value-algebra.md). Writing $d=[K:\mathbb Q_p]$, the [field norm](../../../../../../../field-norm.md) gives

$$
|N_{K/\mathbb Q_p}(x)|_p
=\prod_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q_p)}|\sigma x|
=|x|^d.
$$

A [unit](../../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) [field norm](../../../../../../../field-norm.md) has [absolute value on a field](../../../../../../../absolute-value-algebra.md) one, forcing $|x|=1$. This means both $x$ and $x^{-1}$ are in the [valuation ring](../../../../../../../valuation-ring.md), so $x$ is a [unit](../../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md).

Equivalently, with normalized [discrete valuations](../../../../../../../discrete-valuation.md) and [residue degree](../../../../../../../residue-degree.md) $f$,

$$
\boxed{v_p(N_{K/\mathbb Q_p}x)=f\,v_K(x)=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v_K(x)=0.}
$$

This is [unit norm detects units in a local extension](../../../../../../../unit-norm-detects-units-in-a-local-extension.md).
