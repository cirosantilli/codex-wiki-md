<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is [Eisenstein](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at every [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$, so a [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) $\alpha$ generates a totally ramified cubic [field](../../../../../../field.md) $F/\mathbb Q_p$. The splitting-field [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) is a transitive [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of $S_3$, hence $C_3$ or $S_3$. Its [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) is

$$
\Delta=-p^2(4p+27).
$$

The product of the three [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) differences is a [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) of $\Delta$, transformed by the sign of the [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) permutation. Thus the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $C_3$ exactly when $\Delta$ is a square, and otherwise it is $S_3$. In the latter case the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is

$$
L=F\,\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt{\Delta}).
$$

Indeed adjoining one [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) leaves a quadratic factor, whose [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) is $\Delta/f'(\alpha)^2$, so the [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) of $\Delta$ supplies its remaining [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md).

Suppose first that $p>3$. The [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) $-(4p+27)$ reduces to $-27$, and [Hensel's lemma](../../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) makes it a square exactly when $-3$ is a square modulo $p$. [Quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac{-3}{p}\right)=
\begin{cases}1,&p\equiv1\pmod3,\\-1,&p\equiv2\pmod3.\end{cases}
$$

If $p\equiv1\pmod3$, the [field](../../../../../../field.md) generated by $\alpha$ is already the cyclic [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md), totally ramified of degree three. If $p\equiv2\pmod3$, the nonsquare [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) gives an unramified quadratic [field](../../../../../../field.md) $E=\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt{\Delta})$. Since $F$ and $E$ have coprime degrees, $[FE:\mathbb Q_p]=6$. Its [residue degree](../../../../../../residue-degree.md) is at least two and its [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) at least three; their product is six, so they are exactly two and three. Thus its [inertia group](../../../../../../inertia-group.md) is the normal $C_3$ inside $S_3$.

In both cases the [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) is three and is not divisible by $p$. The [wild inertia group](../../../../../../wild-inertia-group.md) is trivial, so all positive lower [groups](../../../../../../group-split.md) are trivial. As a different check, $f'(\alpha)=3\alpha^2+p$ has [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md) two in $F$ when $p>3$, agreeing with tame [different exponent](../../../../../../different-exponent.md) $3-1$.

For $p=3$ the [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) is $-351=-3^3\cdot13$, which is nonsquare. Hence $G=S_3$. The quadratic subfield can be written $E=\mathbb Q_3(\beta)$ with $\beta^2=-39$. It is ramified of degree two, by the [Eisenstein polynomial](../../../../../../eisenstein-polynomial.md) $T^2+39$. Together with the totally ramified cubic [field](../../../../../../field.md) this forces $e(L/\mathbb Q_3)$ to be divisible by both two and three. Since $[L:\mathbb Q_3]=6$, the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is totally ramified. In its normalized [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md),

$$
v_L(3)=6,\quad v_L(\alpha)=2,\quad
v_L(\beta)=3,\quad \pi=\beta/\alpha\text{ has }v_L(\pi)=1.
$$

All differences between distinct [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) have the same [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md), since $S_3$ permutes the [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) pairs and preserves the [absolute value on a field](../../../../../../absolute-value-algebra.md). Also

$$
f'(\alpha)=3(\alpha^2+1)
=(\alpha-\alpha_2)(\alpha-\alpha_3)
$$

has [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md) six, so each [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) difference has [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md) three. A nonidentity [three-cycle](../../../../../../three-cycle.md) fixes $\beta$, and therefore

$$
v_L(\sigma\pi-\pi)
=v_L\left(\frac{\beta(\alpha-\sigma\alpha)}
{\alpha\,\sigma\alpha}\right)
=3+3-2-2=2.
$$

The [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) fixing $\alpha$ negates $\beta$, so sends $\pi$ to $-\pi$ and has displacement [valuation](../../../../../../valuation.md) $v_L(2\pi)=1$. The other [transpositions](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) have the same value by conjugacy. The [uniformizer](../../../../../../uniformizer.md) criterion now determines every [group](../../../../../../group-split.md):

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
p&G&e&f&(G_0,G_1,G_2,\ldots)\\
p>3,\ p\equiv1\pmod3&C_3&3&1&(C_3,1,1,\ldots)\\
p>3,\ p\equiv2\pmod3&S_3&3&2&(C_3,1,1,\ldots)\\
p=3&S_3&6&1&(S_3,C_3,1,1,\ldots)
\end{array}}
$$

For the wild case the [different exponent](../../../../../../different-exponent.md) is $5+2=7$. Independently, the cubic subfield has [different exponent](../../../../../../different-exponent.md) $v_F(3(\alpha^2+1))=3$, and the quadratic extension $L/F$ is tame with exponent one. The tower formula gives $1+2\cdot3=7$, confirming the filtration. Its positive upper break is $1/2$, because $[G_0:G_1]=2$. This completes [ramification of the splitting field of X3 plus pX plus p](../../../../../../ramification-of-the-splitting-field-of-x3-plus-px-plus-p.md) for every odd [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md).
