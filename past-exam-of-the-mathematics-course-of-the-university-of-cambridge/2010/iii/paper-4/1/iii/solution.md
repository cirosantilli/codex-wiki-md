<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a subset $S$ of a [finite p-group](../../../../../../finite-p-group.md), its images span the [Frattini quotient](../../../../../../frattini-quotient.md) precisely when $\langle S\rangle\Phi(G)=G$. By the generating property proved in part (ii), this is equivalent to $\langle S\rangle=G$.

If $S$ is a [minimal generating set of a group](../../../../../../minimal-generating-set-of-a-group.md), its images must be an irredundant spanning set in the [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $G/\Phi(G)$. A linear dependence would express one image using the others; those other images would still span, so the other elements would still generate $G$, a contradiction. Thus the images form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). Conversely lifts of a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) generate $G$, and deleting a lift destroys spanning and hence generation.

This proves the [Burnside basis theorem](../../../../../../burnside-basis-theorem.md), including equality of sizes for every inclusion-minimal [generating set of a group](../../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md):

$$
\boxed{|S|=\dim_{\mathbb F_p}G/\Phi(G)
=\log_p|G:\Phi(G)|.}
$$

Inclusion-minimal [generating sets of a group](../../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md) need not have equal sizes in arbitrary finite groups; the elementary-abelian Frattini quotient is the essential p-group feature.
