<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take two [subobjects](../../../../../../subobject.md) $A,B\hookrightarrow X$ whose inverse images coincide. Their characteristic maps $a,b:X\to\Omega_{\mathcal E}$ satisfy $\bar\lambda f^*a=\bar\lambda f^*b$ by part (i). Under the [adjunction](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) $f^*\dashv f_*$, these maps transpose to $\lambda a$ and $\lambda b$, respectively, by naturality of the unit. If $\lambda$ is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md), then $a=b$ and hence $A=B$. Thus $f^*$ reflects equality of subobjects.

For parallel arrows $r,s:X\rightrightarrows Y$ with $f^*r=f^*s$, their [equalizer](../../../../../../equaliser.md) $K\hookrightarrow X$ pulls back to all of $f^*X$. Reflection of equality with the whole subobject gives $K=X$, so $r=s$. Therefore **the inverse image is faithful, and the geometric morphism is surjective**. This is the [surjectivity detected by the subobject classifier](../../../../../../surjectivity-detected-by-the-subobject-classifier.md) criterion. For completeness, faithfulness is equivalent here to conservativity: if $f^*h$ is invertible, the image inclusion of $h$ and its kernel-pair diagonal become whole subobjects; reflection makes $h$ both a regular epimorphism and monic, hence invertible in the [topos](../../../../../../elementary-topos.md).
