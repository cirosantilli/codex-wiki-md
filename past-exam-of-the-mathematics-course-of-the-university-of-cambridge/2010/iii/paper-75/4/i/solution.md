↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The defining square for the [subobject classifier](../../../../../../subobject-classifier.md) is a [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md):

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}A&\longrightarrow&1\\\downarrow&&\downarrow\top_{\mathcal E}\\X&\xrightarrow{a}&\Omega_{\mathcal E}.\end{array}
$$

The [inverse image functor of a geometric morphism](../../../../../../inverse-image-functor-of-a-geometric-morphism.md) preserves this square and the terminal object. By definition, the inverse-image truth arrow $f^*\top_{\mathcal E}$ is itself the pullback of $\top_{\mathcal F}$ along $\bar\lambda$. Composing these two pullback squares shows that $f^*A\hookrightarrow f^*X$ is the truth pullback along $\bar\lambda\circ f^*a$. Uniqueness of the characteristic map gives

$$
\boxed{\chi_{f^*A}=\bar\lambda\circ f^*a}.
$$
