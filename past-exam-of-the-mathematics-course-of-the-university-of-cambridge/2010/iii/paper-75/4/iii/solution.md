<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose $f$ is a [surjective geometric morphism](../../../../../../surjective-geometric-morphism.md), so $f^*$ is faithful. First, if a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) $m:S\hookrightarrow X$ becomes an isomorphism, then its characteristic map $\chi_m$ and the constantly true map become equal after $f^*$: by part (i), the pulled-back subobject is the whole object, and equivalently $f^*\chi_m=f^*\top_X$ because $f^*m$ is epic and these maps agree after precomposition with it. Faithfulness gives $\chi_m=\top_X$, so $m$ was already invertible.

Now let $a,b:X\to\Omega_{\mathcal E}$ satisfy $\lambda a=\lambda b$. Adjunction and part (i) identify their pulled-back subobjects $f^*A=f^*B$. Each inclusion $A\cap B\hookrightarrow A,B$ consequently becomes an isomorphism. By the preceding argument it already is an isomorphism, so $A=B$ and $a=b$. This works for every test object $X$, proving

$$
\boxed{\lambda:\Omega_{\mathcal E}\to f_*\Omega_{\mathcal F}\text{ is monic}.}
$$
