<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [regular category](../../../../../../regular-category.md), interpret existential quantification by the image of the appropriate projection. The relation $f(x)=y$ is the graph of $f$, isomorphic to $A$; its projection to $B$ is $f$ itself. Thus the subobject interpreting the existential predicate is $\operatorname{im}(f)\hookrightarrow B$. Semantic validity of the sequent means this image is the whole object.

The regular image factorization writes $f=me$ with $e$ a [regular epimorphism](../../../../../../regular-epimorphism.md) and $m$ its monic image inclusion. Its image is all of $B$ exactly when $m$ is invertible, equivalently when $f$ is itself a regular epimorphism. **This is precisely the cover condition in a regular category.**
