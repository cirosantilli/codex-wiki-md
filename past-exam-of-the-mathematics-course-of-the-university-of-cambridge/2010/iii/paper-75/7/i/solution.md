<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The equality predicate on $A$ is interpreted by the diagonal [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) $\Delta_A:A\hookrightarrow A\times A$. The antecedent equality of the two $f$-values is the [kernel pair](../../../../../../kernel-pair.md) $A\times_BA\hookrightarrow A\times A$. Semantic validity of the implication says that the kernel-pair subobject is contained in the diagonal; the reverse containment always holds. Hence the sequent is valid exactly when

$$
A\times_BA=\Delta_A.
$$

If $f$ is monic, this equality is its cancellation property expressed by the pullback. Conversely, if $fr=fs$ for $r,s:X\to A$, then $(r,s)$ factors through this kernel pair and therefore through the diagonal, giving $r=s$. Thus **the sequent holds exactly when the map is a monomorphism**. This argument uses generalized arrows from arbitrary $X$, not a claim that global elements detect maps.
