<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [subobject](../../../../../../subobject.md) $m:S\hookrightarrow X$, define the [closure operation induced by a left-exact reflector](../../../../../../closure-operation-induced-by-a-left-exact-reflector.md) by

$$
\boxed{c_X(S)=X\times_{LX}LS\hookrightarrow X},
$$

where the pullback uses $\eta_X:X\to LX$ and the monomorphism $Lm:LS\hookrightarrow LX$. Naturality of the unit factors $S\hookrightarrow X$ through $c_X(S)$, proving extensivity, and functoriality of $L$ proves monotonicity. Applying the left-exact $L$ to the defining pullback gives $L(c_X(S))\cong LS$, since $L\eta_X$ is invertible. Repeating the construction therefore changes nothing, proving idempotence.

For $h:Y\to X$, naturality of $\eta$ and preservation of pullbacks identify the closure of $h^*S$ with $Y\times_{LX}LS$, which is also $h^*c_X(S)$. Hence

$$
c_Y(h^*S)=h^*c_X(S).
$$

**The operation is thus universal: it is stable under every pullback.** The same construction preserves finite intersections because $L$ is left exact.
