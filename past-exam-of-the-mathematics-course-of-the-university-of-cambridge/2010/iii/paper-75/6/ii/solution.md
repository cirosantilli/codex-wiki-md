<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $A,B$ be fixed objects and form the ambient [exponential object](../../../../../../exponential-object.md) $B^A$. For every $X$, the exponential [adjunction](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) identifies maps $X\to B^A$ with maps $X\times A\to B$. Since $L$ is left exact and $LA=A$,

$$
L(X\times A)\cong LX\times A,
$$

and its unit corresponds to $\eta_X\times1_A$. Because $B$ is fixed, every map $X\times A\to B$ therefore extends uniquely to $LX\times A\to B$. Transposing back gives

$$
\mathcal E(LX,B^A)\cong\mathcal E(X,B^A).
$$

The fixed-object criterion from part (i) shows that $B^A$ already belongs to $\mathcal L$. The ambient evaluation and transpose universal property restrict to the full subcategory, proving **the subcategory is cartesian closed**. This constructs [exponentials in a left-exact reflective subcategory](../../../../../../exponentials-in-a-left-exact-reflective-subcategory.md), rather than merely reflecting an exponential and assuming its universal property survives.
