<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Identify the [reflective subcategory](../../../../../../reflective-subcategory.md) with its full replete image, writing $\eta_X:X\to LX$ for the reflection unit. An object $Z$ is fixed by $L$ exactly when precomposition with every $\eta_X$ gives a bijection $\mathcal E(LX,Z)\to\mathcal E(X,Z)$. One implication is the reflection [adjunction](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md); for the other, take $X=Z$ to obtain a retraction of $\eta_Z$, and uniqueness of maps into the fixed object $LZ$ makes it a two-sided inverse.

An ambient [finite limit](../../../../../../finite-limit.md) of fixed objects has this same property: its [hom-sets](../../../../../../hom-set.md) are limits of the hom-sets of the diagram, and a limit of these bijections is a bijection. Thus the ambient terminal object, products and [equalizers](../../../../../../equaliser.md) are fixed and retain their universal properties in the full subcategory. **The reflective subcategory is Cartesian, with finite limits computed in the ambient topos.**
