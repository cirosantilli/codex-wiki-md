<h1 id="6/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every characteristic map $a:X\to\Omega$ expresses its [subobject](../../../../../../subobject.md) as $a^*\top$. Universality of the [closure operation induced by a left-exact reflector](../../../../../../closure-operation-induced-by-a-left-exact-reflector.md) gives

$$
c_X(a^*\top)=a^*c_\Omega(\top).
$$

By definition, $j$ classifies $c_\Omega(\top)$, so the right-hand subobject has characteristic map $j\circ a$. The original and its closure agree exactly when their unique characteristic maps agree. Thus

$$
\boxed{a\text{ classifies a closed subobject}\Longleftrightarrow j\circ a=a.}
$$

Extensivity, idempotence, preservation of intersections and closure of the whole subobject also give the [Lawvere-Tierney topology](../../../../../../lawvere-tierney-topology.md) laws for $j$.
