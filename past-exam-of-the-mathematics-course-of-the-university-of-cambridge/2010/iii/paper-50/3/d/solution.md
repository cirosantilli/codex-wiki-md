<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both the drift and local $Z_k$ control commute with $S$, so $[H_S+f(t)Z_k,S]=0$ at every time. The propagator consequently obeys $U(t)^\dagger S U(t)=S$, as follows by differentiating this expression. Thus every reachable [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) preserves the [magnetization sectors of a spin chain](../../../../../../magnetization-sectors-of-a-spin-chain.md). If $P_q$ is a [spectral projector](../../../../../../spectral-projector.md) of $S$, then $U$ commutes with $P_q$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(P_q\rho(t))=\operatorname{Tr}(P_q\rho(0))$.

The eigenspaces are nontrivial proper subspaces: $|0\cdots0\rangle$ has eigenvalue $N$, while $|10\cdots0\rangle$ has eigenvalue $N-2$. One cannot transfer the first [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) to the second, although their [density operators](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) have the same spectrum. Nor can one implement a unitary that mixes these two sectors. Hence $\boxed{\text{the full system has neither operator, density, nor pure-state controllability}}$. Its [Dynamical Lie algebra](../../../../../../dynamical-lie-algebra.md) lies in the block-diagonal commutant of $S$. Controllability within a fixed sector, such as the [single-excitation subspace](../../../../../../single-excitation-subspace.md), is still possible and must be tested separately.
