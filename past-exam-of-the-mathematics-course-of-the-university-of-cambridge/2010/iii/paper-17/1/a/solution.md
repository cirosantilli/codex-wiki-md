<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Label the six vertices $A,B,C,D,E,F$ clockwise, starting at the upper-left vertex. The arrows identify $AB$ with $ED$, $BC$ with $FE$, and $FA$ with $DC$, preserving the indicated directions. Consequently the [quotient space](../../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) has exactly two vertices,

$$
P=\{A,C,E\},\qquad Q=\{B,D,F\}.
$$

Orient its three [topological cells](../../../../../../topological-cell.md) by $a:A\to B$, $b:B\to C$, and $c:F\to A$. The clockwise boundary of the hexagonal [topological cell](../../../../../../topological-cell.md) traverses

$$
abc^{-1}a^{-1}b^{-1}c.
$$

Every [topological cell](../../../../../../topological-cell.md) of dimension one therefore occurs once with each sign. The [cellular chain complex](../../../../../../cellular-chain-complex.md), with integral coefficients, is

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial_2=0}\mathbb Z^3
\xrightarrow{\partial_1}\mathbb Z^2\longrightarrow0,
\qquad
\partial_1=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&1&1\\1&-1&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Here the rows correspond to $P,Q$. The [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $\partial_1$ is the primitive subgroup generated by $Q-P$, and its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is freely generated by $a+b$ and $a+c$. Thus the [homology groups](../../../../../../homology-group.md) are

$$
\boxed{H_j(X;\mathbb Z)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&j=0,2,\\
\mathbb Z^2,&j=1,\\
0,&j>2.
\end{cases}}
$$

The two vertex classes are important: identifying all six vertices would give an incorrect [cellular chain complex](../../../../../../cellular-chain-complex.md). The result agrees with the [homology](../../../../../../homology-split.md) of a [torus](../../../../../../torus.md).
