<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Expanding the covariant derivative isolates the [ghost-gluon vertex](../../../../../ghost-gluon-vertex.md) interaction

$$
\mathcal L_{\bar cAc}=-g f^{abc}(\partial^\mu\bar c^a)A_\mu^b c^c.
$$

The derivative acts on the antighost. With all momenta incoming, let its momentum be $k$, the gluon's Lorentz index be $\mu$, and their color indices be $(a,b,c)$ in the order $(\bar c,A,c)$. The convention $e^{-ikx}$ replaces $\partial^\mu$ by $-ik^\mu$, and multiplication by $i$ from $e^{iS}$ gives

$$
\boxed{V_{\bar c^aA_\mu^bc^c}=-g f^{abc}k_\mu.}
$$

Momentum conservation is implicit. The [structure constants](../../../../../structure-constant.md) reflect the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), and the antighost momentum follows directly from its differentiated field. Reversing the momentum convention changes the displayed vertex sign consistently; exchanging the ghost and antighost source order also carries a [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md).
