<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $\ddot\theta=0$. Lift the path to the real line beginning at $\theta_i$. Its endpoint can be any $\theta_f+2\pi m$, where $m\in\mathbb Z$ labels the relative [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md). With $\Delta=\theta_f-\theta_i$, every classical path and its [action](../../../../../../action.md) are

$$
\boxed{\theta_m(t)=\theta_i+\frac{\Delta+2\pi m}{T}t,\qquad S_m=\frac{\Lambda}{2T}(\Delta+2\pi m)^2.}
$$

These exhaust the constant-velocity paths in the different winding sectors.
