<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [integral curve of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) $X$ through $p$ is a [smooth curve](../../../../../../smooth-curve.md) $c:I\to M$, on an interval containing zero, satisfying $c(0)=p$ and $\dot c(t)=X_{c(t)}$. The [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md) is the smoothly varying family of these [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md):

$$
\phi:D\longrightarrow M,\qquad \phi_t(p)=c_p(t),\qquad
\phi_0(p)=p,\quad \partial_t\phi_t(p)=X_{\phi_t(p)}.
$$

Here $D$ is an open neighborhood of $\{0\}\times M$; for the maximal [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md), its fibre over $p$ is the maximal existence interval of $c_p$. Local existence, uniqueness and smooth dependence for a smooth [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) give this family. Uniqueness also gives $\phi_{t+s}(p)=\phi_t(\phi_s(p))$ whenever the relevant curves and compositions are defined, and $\phi_{-t}=\phi_t^{-1}$ on their common domains.

Reparametrizing the [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\phi_{2t}(p)=2X_{\phi_{2t}(p)}.
$$

Thus **the flow of $2X$ is $\boxed{\widetilde\phi_t=\phi_{2t}}$**, with domain $\{(t,p):(2t,p)\in D\}$.
