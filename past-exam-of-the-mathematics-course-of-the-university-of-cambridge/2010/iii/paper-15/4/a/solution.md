<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A subset $Z$ of an $m$-dimensional [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) $M$ is an $r$-dimensional [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) if, for each $p\in Z$, some [coordinate chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md) $x:U\to V\subset\mathbb R^m$ containing $p$ satisfies

$$
x(U\cap Z)=V\cap(\mathbb R^r\times\{0\}^{m-r}).
$$

The induced topology on $Z$ is the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md), and the first $r$ coordinates restricted to $Z$ give its [smooth atlas](../../../../../../smooth-atlas.md). To see their [smooth transition maps](../../../../../../smooth-transition-map.md) agree, restrict the ambient smooth transitions to the coordinate planes; their inverses restrict in the same way. The inclusion then has injective [differential of a smooth map](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) and is a homeomorphism onto its image. Conversely this is the local coordinate description of a smooth [smooth embedding](../../../../../../smooth-embedding.md). An [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) need not be a closed subset of $M$.
