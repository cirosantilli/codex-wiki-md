↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [affine connection](../../../../../../../affine-connection.md) $\nabla$ is a [metric connection](../../../../../../../metric-connection.md) for the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) $g$ when

$$
\boxed{X\bigl(g(Y,Z)\bigr)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ)
\quad\text{for all vector fields }X,Y,Z.}
$$

Equivalently the induced [covariant derivative](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) satisfies $\nabla g=0$. This is [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md); it imposes no condition on the [torsion tensor](../../../../../../../torsion-tensor.md). A [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) has the additional property of being a [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md).
