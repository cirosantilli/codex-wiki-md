<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Implement the [quantum channel](../../../../../../quantum-channel.md) by a unitary dilation with a pure initial environment. The final state on $RBE$ is pure. The [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md) across either bipartition gives equal complementary [Von Neumann entropies](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md), so $S(RB)=S(E)$ and $S(B)=S(RE)$. By the [Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md),

$$
I_c(\Phi,\rho)=S(RE)-S(E)\leq S(R).
$$

The reference is untouched and purifies the input, hence $S(R)=S(\rho)$. Therefore **$\boxed{I_c(\Phi,\rho)\leq S(\rho)}$.**
