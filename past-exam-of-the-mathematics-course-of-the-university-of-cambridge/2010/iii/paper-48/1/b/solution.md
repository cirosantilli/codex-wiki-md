↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a bipartite [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$, the [Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md) is

$$
\boxed{S(AB)\leq S(A)+S(B)}.
$$

Here $S(A)=S(\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_{AB})$ and likewise for $B$, using the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md). For a tripartite density operator, the [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md) is

$$
\boxed{S(ABC)+S(B)\leq S(AB)+S(BC)}.
$$

Equivalently, [quantum conditional entropy](../../../../../../quantum-conditional-entropy.md) cannot increase upon adding a conditioning system: $S(A\mid BC)\leq S(A\mid B)$.
