↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) distinguishes phases through a macroscopic observable, such as the mean spin $m$ of a ferromagnet. Its vanishing can reflect an unbroken symmetry, while choosing one of several nonzero equilibrium values gives [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) treats it as a slowly varying field and expands the [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) in terms allowed by symmetry:

$$
\mathcal A[m]=\int d^Dx\left[\frac K2|\nabla m|^2+\frac r2m^2+\frac u4m^4+\frac v6m^6-hm\right].
$$

Here $K>0$ penalizes spatial variation, $h$ is the conjugate field, and the highest retained even coefficient must stabilize large $|m|$. For a spin-flip-symmetric system, odd powers are forbidden at $h=0$. Equilibrium in the [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md) minimizes this functional; near an ordinary continuous transition $r=r_t t$ with $t=(T-T_c)/T_c$ and $r_t>0$.
