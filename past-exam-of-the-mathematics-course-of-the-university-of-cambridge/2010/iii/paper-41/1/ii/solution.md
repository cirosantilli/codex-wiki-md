<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $u>0$, the quartic [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) at $h=0$ minimizes at $m=0$ for $r>0$ and at $m=\pm\sqrt{-r/u}$ for $r<0$. The [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) changes continuously at $r=0$, giving a [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) and a divergent response. The minimized potential is $0$ above the transition and $-r^2/(4u)$ below it, so its first temperature derivative is continuous while its second derivative jumps.

For $u<0$, retain $v>0$. A [first-order phase transition](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md) occurs when the $m=0$ minimum and a nonzero minimum have equal free energy. Combining stationarity $r+um^2+vm^4=0$ with equality of potential values gives

$$
\boxed{m^2=-\frac{3u}{4v},\qquad r=\frac{3u^2}{16v}.}
$$

The order parameter jumps at a positive $r$ before the disordered minimum loses local stability. Generic temperature dependence then also produces a jump in entropy and a [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md). Equality of global minima locates coexistence; the disappearance of a metastable minimum instead defines a [spinodal point](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md).
