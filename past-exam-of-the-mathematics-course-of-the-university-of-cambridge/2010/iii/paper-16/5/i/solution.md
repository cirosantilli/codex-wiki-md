<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $f:\mathbb P^1_k\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be the structure map, a [projective morphism](../../../../../../projective-morphism.md). Let $D$ be the union of the two rational points $[0:1]$ and $[1:0]$. The homogeneous polynomial $t_0t_1$ defines this [effective Cartier divisor](../../../../../../effective-cartier-divisor.md) and gives the exact sequence of [coherent sheaves](../../../../../../coherent-sheaf.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-2)\xrightarrow{\ t_0t_1\ }\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_D\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [twisting sheaf on projective space](../../../../../../twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) $\mathcal O(-2)$ has no nonzero [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md): the chart calculation of Question 3(iv) now requires $a(t)=t^{-2}b(t^{-1})$, again impossible for nonzero polynomials. We also have $\Gamma(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O)=k$ and $\Gamma(D,\mathcal O_D)=k\oplus k$.

The [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) under $f$ is the [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) of [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md) on the one-point [scheme](../../../../../../scheme.md). Consequently the induced sequence is

$$
0\longrightarrow0\longrightarrow k\xrightarrow{c\mapsto(c,c)}k\oplus k\longrightarrow0.
$$

Its last nonzero map has [cokernel](../../../../../../cokernel.md) isomorphic to $k$, since $(a,b)\mapsto b-a$ has the diagonal as its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). Thus **the induced sequence is not exact at $f_*\mathcal O_D$**, proving that [projective direct image need not preserve surjections](../../../../../../projective-direct-image-need-not-preserve-surjections.md).
