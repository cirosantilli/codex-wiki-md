<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An $A$-[module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) $h:M\to N$ induces, by [localization of a module](../../../../../../localization-of-a-module.md), compatible maps

$$
M_a\longrightarrow N_a,\qquad m/a^r\longmapsto h(m)/a^r
$$

on all [principal open subsets](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(a)$. These glue to an $\mathcal O_X$-linear [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) $\widetilde h:\widetilde M\to\widetilde N$.

Conversely, taking [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md) of an $\mathcal O_X$-linear [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) gives an $A$-[module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) $h:M\to N$, since $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M)=M$ and $\Gamma(X,\widetilde N)=N$. On $D(a)$, compatibility with restriction determines the image of $m/1$, and $\mathcal O_X$-linearity determines the image of $a^{-r}m$. It must be $h(m)/a^r$. Thus the [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) is uniquely recovered from $h$. Both constructions preserve addition and composition, proving the [affine module-sheaf equivalence](../../../../../../affine-module-sheaf-equivalence.md) on morphisms:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_X}(\widetilde M,\widetilde N)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,N).}
$$

No finite-generation assumption is used.
