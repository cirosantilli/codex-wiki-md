<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take the [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md) $X=\mathbb P^1_k$ and the [coherent sheaves](../../../../../../coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$ and $\mathcal G=\mathcal O_X(-1)$, where the latter is a [twisting sheaf on projective space](../../../../../../twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md). Both are [line bundles](../../../../../../line-bundle.md), so each [stalk](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) is a free rank-one $\mathcal O_{X,x}$-[module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) and therefore $\mathcal F_x\simeq\mathcal G_x$ at every point.

They are nevertheless not isomorphic. On the standard charts with coordinates $t$ and $t^{-1}$, choose frames $e_0,e_1$ of $\mathcal O(-1)$ with $e_1=t^{-1}e_0$. A [global section](../../../../../../global-section.md) would give polynomials $a(t)$ and $b(t^{-1})$ satisfying

$$
a(t)=t^{-1}b(t^{-1}).
$$

The left side has only nonnegative powers of $t$, and the right side only strictly negative powers. Thus both vanish, giving $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O(-1))=0$. For the [structure sheaf](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md), the corresponding equality is $a(t)=b(t^{-1})$, whose solutions are precisely the constants. Hence

$$
\boxed{H^0(X,\mathcal F)=k,\qquad H^0(X,\mathcal G)=0,\qquad\mathcal F\not\simeq\mathcal G.}
$$

A [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) which is an isomorphism on all [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) would be an isomorphism; this example only asserts the existence of separate [stalk](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) isomorphisms, not that they come from one [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md).
