<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The composite of two [finite type morphisms](../../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) is of [finite type](../../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md): on suitable [affine open subsets](../../../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md), take the algebra generators of the first algebra together with the images of the algebra generators of the second. To check [separatedness](../../../../../../separated-morphism.md), factor the [diagonal morphism](../../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) of the composite as

$$
X\xrightarrow{\Delta_f}X\times_YX\longrightarrow X\times_ZX.
$$

The first arrow is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md), and the second is the [base change](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) of the [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) $\Delta_g:Y\to Y\times_ZY$. Hence their composite is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md).

Finally, after any [base change](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) $Z'\to Z$, the composite is $X'\xrightarrow{f'}Y'\xrightarrow{g'}Z'$. Both arrows take closed subsets to closed subsets, so their composite does as well. This is [universal closedness](../../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md). All three conditions hold, and therefore

$$
\boxed{f,g\text{ proper}\ \Longrightarrow\ gf\text{ proper}.}
$$
