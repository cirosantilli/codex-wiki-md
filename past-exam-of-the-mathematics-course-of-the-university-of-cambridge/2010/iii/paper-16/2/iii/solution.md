<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the [graph morphism of schemes](../../../../../../graph-morphism-of-schemes.md)

$$
\Gamma_f:X\longrightarrow X\times_ZY,\qquad x\longmapsto(x,f(x)).
$$

It is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md): the square with $\Delta_g:Y\to Y\times_ZY$ and the map $X\times_ZY\to Y\times_ZY$, $(x,y)\mapsto(f(x),y)$, is a [fibre product of schemes](../../../../../../fiber-product-of-schemes.md) square. The [separatedness](../../../../../../separated-morphism.md) of $g$ makes $\Delta_g$ a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md), and [base change](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) preserves a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md).

The projection $\pi:X\times_ZY\to Y$ is the [base change](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) of the [proper morphism](../../../../../../proper-morphism.md) $gf:X\to Z$, so it is a [proper morphism](../../../../../../proper-morphism.md). The [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) $\Gamma_f$ is a [finite morphism](../../../../../../finite-morphism.md), hence a [proper morphism](../../../../../../proper-morphism.md) by part (i). Since $f=\pi\Gamma_f$, part (ii) proves

$$
\boxed{gf\text{ proper},\quad g\text{ separated}\ \Longrightarrow\ f\text{ proper}.}
$$

This argument supplies [finite type](../../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) for $f$ as well; it need not be separately assumed.
