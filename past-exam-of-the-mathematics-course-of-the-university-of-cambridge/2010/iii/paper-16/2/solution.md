A [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md) of [schemes](../../../../../scheme.md) is a [separated morphism](../../../../../separated-morphism.md) of [finite type](../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) which is a [universally closed morphism](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md): after every [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md), the resulting map of underlying [topological spaces](../../../../../topological-space.md) takes closed subsets to closed subsets.

The [valuative criterion for properness](../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-properness.md), in the Noetherian setting used here, says that a [finite type morphism](../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) $f:X\to Y$ is a [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md) if and only if the following existence and uniqueness condition holds. For every [valuation ring](../../../../../valuation-ring.md) $V$, its [fraction field](../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) $K$, and compatible [morphisms of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $a:\operatorname{Spec}K\to X$ and $b:\operatorname{Spec}V\to Y$ with $f\circ a=b|_{\operatorname{Spec}K}$, there is exactly one [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $h:\operatorname{Spec}V\to X$ such that $h|_{\operatorname{Spec}K}=a$ and $f\circ h=b$. All [valuation rings](../../../../../valuation-ring.md) are allowed; no discreteness assumption is needed in this formulation. The following arguments also use that each of the three defining conditions for a [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md) is preserved by [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md).
