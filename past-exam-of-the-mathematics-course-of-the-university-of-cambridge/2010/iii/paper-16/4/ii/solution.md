<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [affine space](../../../../../../affine-space.md) $X=\mathbb A^n_k$ is an [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md), hence its underlying [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md) is irreducible. Every nonempty open subset of an irreducible [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md) is irreducible and in particular connected. A section of the [constant sheaf](../../../../../../constant-sheaf.md) with value the [function field](../../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) $K=k(t_1,\ldots,t_n)$ is a [locally constant function](../../../../../../locally-constant-function.md), so on any such open subset it is constant. Thus its sections are $K$ on each nonempty open subset and zero on the empty set. All restriction maps are either identities on $K$ or maps onto zero.

The [constant sheaf](../../../../../../constant-sheaf.md) is therefore a [flasque sheaf](../../../../../../flasque-sheaf.md). It has its natural $\mathcal O_X$-[module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) structure because a [regular function](../../../../../../regular-function.md) on a nonempty open subset embeds in the [function field](../../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) and acts by multiplication. Part (i) now gives

$$
\boxed{H^0(X,\mathcal F)=K,\qquad H^p(X,\mathcal F)=0\quad(p>0).}
$$

This is an instance of [constant sheaves on irreducible spaces are flasque](../../../../../../constant-sheaves-on-irreducible-spaces-are-flasque.md); no restriction on $n$ is needed.
