<h1 id="5/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A [flat module](../../../../../../flat-module.md) is one for which tensoring preserves injections. This is equivalent to exactness of the tensor functor, since the [tensor product of modules](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-modules.md) is always right exact: its universal balanced maps identify tensoring a cokernel with the cokernel of the tensored map.

Suppose $M$ is [flat](../../../../../../flat-module.md). For every inclusion $N'\hookrightarrow N$, the map $N'\otimes_AM\to N\otimes_AM$ is injective. The detection property proved above therefore makes its character-dual map surjective. Under the character [Tensor-hom adjunction](../../../../../../tensor-hom-adjunction.md), this is exactly

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_A(N,M^*)\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_A(N',M^*).
$$

Every map from a submodule into $M^*$ thus extends, proving

$$
\boxed{(1)\Longrightarrow(2).}
$$
