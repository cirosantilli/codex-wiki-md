Conversely, if the [character module](../../../../../../character-module.md) $M^*$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), every restriction map in the preceding display is surjective. The character [Tensor-hom adjunction](../../../../../../tensor-hom-adjunction.md) then makes the dual of $N'\otimes_AM\to N\otimes_AM$ surjective. Since $D$ detects injections, that tensor map is injective for every inclusion $N'\hookrightarrow N$. Hence $M$ is [flat](../../../../../../flat-module.md), and

$$
\boxed{(2)\Longrightarrow(1).}
$$

Together these two implications prove the [Lambek theorem](../../../../../../lambek-theorem.md), with both the duality and the injective-cogenerator facts justified.
