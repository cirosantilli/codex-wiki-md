<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The logarithm is strictly increasing, so maximizing the [likelihood function](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) is equivalent to maximizing its logarithm. Multiplication by $-2$ reverses that ordering. Thus, whenever the maximum is attained,

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta\in\operatorname*{arg\,max}_\theta p(y\mid\theta)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\widehat\theta\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_\theta D(\theta).}
$$

Here the [Bayesian deviance](../../../../../../bayesian-deviance.md) includes the likelihood constants. Parameter-independent constants do not alter the minimizer, but a common convention must be retained when comparing criteria between models.
