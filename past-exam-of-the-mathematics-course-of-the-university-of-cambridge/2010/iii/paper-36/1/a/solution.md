↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $\pi_0=\mathbb P(H_0)$ and $\pi_1=\mathbb P(H_1)=1-\pi_0$, the [prior odds](../../../../../../prior-odds.md) on the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) are

$$
\boxed{O_{01}=\frac{\pi_0}{\pi_1}.}
$$

These odds concern which model is true before seeing the observations. They are separate from the [prior distribution](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) of the success probability within the alternative model.
