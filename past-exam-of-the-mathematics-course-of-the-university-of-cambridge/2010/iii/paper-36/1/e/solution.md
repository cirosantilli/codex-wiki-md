<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) standardized statistic for the [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) count is

$$
Z=\frac{5150-5000}{\sqrt{10000/4}}=\boxed{3}.
$$

Because the alternative is two-sided, the approximate [p-value](../../../../../../p-value.md) is $2\{1-\Phi(3)\}\approx0.0027$. A continuity correction replaces $3$ by $(149.5)/50=2.99$ and gives approximately $0.0028$, with the same conclusion. Thus **the result is statistically significant at both the 5% and 1% levels**. The [p-value](../../../../../../p-value.md) is a null tail probability, not a [posterior probability](../../../../../../posterior-probability.md) that the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) is true.
