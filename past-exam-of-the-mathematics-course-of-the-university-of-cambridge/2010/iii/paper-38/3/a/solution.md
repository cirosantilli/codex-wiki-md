<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a unit-rate [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md), $\mu=1$ and $M_X(r)=1/(1-r)$ for $r<1$. The [adjustment coefficient](../../../../../../adjustment-coefficient.md) equation is

$$
\frac r{1-r}=(1+\theta)r.
$$

Its nonzero root is

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm exp}=\frac\theta{1+\theta}.}
$$

It lies in $(0,1)$ for $\theta>0$, so the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) is finite there and the [Lundberg inequality](../../../../../../lundberg-inequality.md) applies.
