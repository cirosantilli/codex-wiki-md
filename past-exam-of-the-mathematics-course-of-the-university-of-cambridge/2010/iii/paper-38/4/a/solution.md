<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The ratio of [expected process variance](../../../../../../expected-process-variance.md) to [variance of hypothetical means](../../../../../../variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) is $K=s^2/v=(\alpha+1)/(k-2)$, and the [credibility factor](../../../../../../credibility-factor.md) is $Z=n/(n+K)$. Holding $\alpha,\lambda$ fixed, an increase in $k$ decreases $K$. Hence **the [credibility factor](../../../../../../credibility-factor.md) increases**, approaching one as $k$ tends to infinity. Extending the formula to real $k>2$ makes the sign explicit:

$$
\frac{\partial Z}{\partial k}=\frac{n(\alpha+1)}{[n(k-2)+\alpha+1]^2}>0.
$$

For the integer values in the model this establishes the same strict monotonicity. The collective claim [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) $m=\alpha/[\lambda(k-1)]$ also changes, but the conclusion concerns the relative weight of experience: conditional noise declines faster than the variation of the risk means.
