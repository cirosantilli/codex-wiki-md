<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $h=\mathbb E\theta>0$ and $w=\operatorname{Var}(\theta)>0$. Keeping $h$ fixed in the [gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md) family gives $\alpha=h^2/w$ and $\lambda=h/w$. The ratio of [expected process variance](../../../../../../expected-process-variance.md) to [variance of hypothetical means](../../../../../../variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) becomes

$$
K=\frac{h^2+w}{w(k-2)}=\frac{1+h^2/w}{k-2},\qquad
Z=\frac{n(k-2)}{n(k-2)+1+h^2/w}.
$$

As the prior [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $w$ decreases, $K$ increases, so **the [credibility factor](../../../../../../credibility-factor.md) decreases**. In the limit $w\downarrow0$, $Z\downarrow0$: the risk parameter is already essentially known from the prior, leaving little need to use the noisy [sample mean](../../../../../../sample-mean.md). The population claim [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) $h/(k-1)$ remains fixed throughout this comparison.
