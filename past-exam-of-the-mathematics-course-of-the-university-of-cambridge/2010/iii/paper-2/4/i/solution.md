<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [quiver with one loop](../../../../../../quiver-with-one-loop.md). Its [path algebra](../../../../../../path-algebra.md) has basis $1,a,a^2,\ldots$ and multiplication $a^ra^s=a^{r+s}$, so it is the [polynomial ring](../../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $k[a]$. Hence

$$
\boxed{kQ_1\cong k[a]:\ \text{generated by one algebra element, but infinite-dimensional over }k.}
$$

The algebra generators need not form a spanning vector-space set: products produce the infinitely many independent paths.
