<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If the [Strong Lagrangian property](../../../../../../strong-lagrangian-property.md) holds with multiplier $\lambda$, its infimum identity implies, for every $x\in X$,

$$
f(x)-\lambda^T(h(x)-b)\geq\phi(b).
$$

For any feasible right-hand side $u$, take the infimum over $h(x)=u$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\phi(u)\geq\phi(b)+\lambda^T(u-b).}
$$

The inequality also holds at infeasible $u$, where $\phi(u)=+\infty$, and it is equality at $u=b$. Thus $r=\phi(b)+\lambda^T(u-b)$ is the required [non-vertical supporting hyperplane of a value function](../../../../../../non-vertical-supporting-hyperplane-of-a-value-function.md). This proves the converse and completes the equivalence. Neither implication requires that $X$ be a [convex set](../../../../../../convex-set.md).
