<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two curves have different destinations, so the equality of their initial segments is not an equality of their complete laws. At the first contact with $[1,\infty)$, the initial hull separates the target $1$ from infinity. Except for the null event of hitting the fixed point $1$ itself, this happens by a contact at a real point strictly larger than $1$. The fact that a fixed nonzero real point is almost surely not visited is the [SLE does not hit a fixed nonzero boundary point](../../../../../../sle-does-not-hit-a-fixed-nonzero-boundary-point.md) property; it is consistent with the strictly positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of swallowing an entire interval at once in Question 3.

The original curve, whose target is infinity, continues in the closure of the unbounded surviving component. The curve targeted at $1$ continues in the surviving component whose [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) contains its target, which is now separated from infinity. Its domain normalization must follow that component. Ultimately one curve tends to infinity and the other to $1$. This is [target-dependent continuation of SLE6](../../../../../../target-dependent-continuation-of-sle6.md).

The precise failure of the argument is the loss of the conformal conjugacy between the same surviving components. In the transformed construction, at time $S$ the pole $-1$ is swallowed; its image is infinity. The composition $g_t^*\circ\Phi\circ g_t^{-1}$ was a [conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane](../../../../../../conformal-automorphism-of-the-upper-half-plane.md) only while both maps described corresponding surviving domains. After the [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) contact, the image of the original target-containing domain is the component containing $1$, whereas $g_t^*$ in the ordinary half-plane-capacity convention maps out the hull relative to the component containing infinity. These are no longer the same component. Equivalently, the swallowed pole collides with the growing hull, and the analytic-neighbourhood assumption at the [Loewner driving function](../../../../../../loewner-driving-function.md) cannot be continued by the preceding argument.

Hence neither the given analytic identities nor the [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) description obtained from their capacity clock licenses a continuation of the comparison beyond the [stopping time](../../../../../../stopping-time.md). The zero drift before contact does not remove this geometric obstruction. The restriction to pre-contact segments is essential, even though the curves can continue in their respective target components.
