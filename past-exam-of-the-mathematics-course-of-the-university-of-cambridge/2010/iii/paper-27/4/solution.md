<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane](../../../../../conformal-automorphism-of-the-upper-half-plane.md) with the indicated three [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) values is

$$
\boxed{\Phi(z)=\frac z{z+1}.}
$$

Its [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) matrix has determinant one, so it preserves $\mathbb H$. The inverse is $\Phi^{-1}(w)=w/(1-w)$. In particular

$$
\Phi\bigl(( -\infty,-1]\bigr)=(1,\infty]
$$

on the extended real [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md). Therefore the image trace's first contact with the latter ray corresponds to the original time $S$. The map sends the original target at infinity to the new target $1$; it does not preserve the target of the chordal curve.

We use the image [half-plane capacity](../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) as its time parameter:

$$
u(t)=\frac12\operatorname{hcap}(K_t^*)=\int_0^t\Phi_s'(\xi_s)^2\,ds,\qquad t<S.
$$

Since $\Phi_t$ is a [conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane](../../../../../conformal-automorphism-of-the-upper-half-plane.md) analytic at $\xi_t$, its real [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) there is strictly positive. Hence $u$ is continuous and strictly increasing before $S$. The original time $t$ and image time $u$ must be kept distinct throughout the comparison.
