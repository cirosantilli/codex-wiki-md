<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the thin-sheet regime, the gas [scale height](../../../../../../scale-height.md) is $h_g(R)=\sigma_g^2(R)/(2\pi G\Sigma_*(R))$. Hence radius-independent gas [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{h_g(R)}{h_g(R_0)}=\frac{\Sigma_*(R_0)}{\Sigma_*(R)}}.
$$

For an [exponential galactic disk](../../../../../../exponential-galactic-disk.md), $\Sigma_*(R)=\Sigma_{*,0}e^{-R/R_d}$, so

$$
\boxed{h_g(R)=\frac{\sigma_g^2}{2\pi G\Sigma_{*,0}}e^{R/R_d}}.
$$

The gas therefore exhibits [galactic disk flaring](../../../../../../galactic-disk-flaring.md) as stellar [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) falls outward. Stellar [galactic disks](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md), by contrast, commonly have roughly constant thickness over their main optical region; applying the same [scale height](../../../../../../scale-height.md) relation to the stars requires $\sigma_{z,*}^2(R)\propto\Sigma_*(R)$, or $\sigma_{z,*}\propto e^{-R/(2R_d)}$ for an [exponential galactic disk](../../../../../../exponential-galactic-disk.md). This comparison does not follow by imposing a radially constant stellar [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md): that extra assumption would predict stellar [galactic disk flaring](../../../../../../galactic-disk-flaring.md) too. The constancy used in the vertical calculation is constancy with height locally, not a mandatory constancy with radius.
