<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Typical present [elliptical galaxies](../../../../../../elliptical-galaxy.md) are dominated by old [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md): their brightest short-lived [stars](../../../../../../star.md) have disappeared, leaving faint long-lived [stars](../../../../../../star.md) and [stellar remnants](../../../../../../stellar-remnant.md), so luminosity per unit stellar mass is low. Ongoing [star formation](../../../../../../star-formation.md) in many [spiral galaxies](../../../../../../spiral-galaxy.md) and [irregular galaxies](../../../../../../irregular-galaxy.md) supplies luminous young [stars](../../../../../../star.md), giving **a smaller stellar mass-to-light ratio in a fixed band**; [dark matter](../../../../../../dark-matter.md), the [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) and aperture must also be specified if the quoted ratio is dynamical.
