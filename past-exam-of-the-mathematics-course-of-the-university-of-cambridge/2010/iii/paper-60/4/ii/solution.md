<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Low-mass [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md) [stars](../../../../../../star.md) live for much of the [Milky Way](../../../../../../milky-way.md)'s age, retaining representatives of many generations, while [phase mixing](../../../../../../phase-mixing.md) disperses their birth associations around their orbits. In an approximately axisymmetric [galactic disk](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md), a sufficiently complete local census can therefore sample the mixed annular [stellar population](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) at the Sun's radius despite its small volume. **This is a representative local-radius sample, not automatically an unbiased whole-disc census**: different vertical [scale heights](../../../../../../scale-height.md), selection effects and radial migration require corrections when inferring integrated population fractions.
