<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Many [open clusters](../../../../../../open-cluster.md) are weakly bound and are dispersed by [two-body relaxation](../../../../../../two-body-relaxation.md), [stellar evaporation](../../../../../../stellar-evaporation.md) and [tidal heating](../../../../../../tidal-heating.md), including encounters with [molecular clouds](../../../../../../molecular-cloud.md) in the [galactic disk](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md), so their present age histogram preferentially retains young survivors. More massive compact [globular clusters](../../../../../../globular-cluster.md) can survive much longer, while the released low-mass [stars](../../../../../../star.md) remain as old field [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md): **disruption removes clusters from the catalogue without removing their stars from the disc**.
