↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

The [neutral-hydrogen 21-centimeter line](../../../../../../neutral-hydrogen-21-centimeter-line.md) traces cold gas far beyond the bright stellar [galactic disk](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md), where a [dark matter halo](../../../../../../dark-matter-halo.md) contributes strongly to the [galaxy rotation curve](../../../../../../galaxy-rotation-curve.md). With relatively small random velocities, this gas approximately follows [circular orbits](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md), allowing its Doppler velocities to measure the [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md) after inclination and pressure corrections, whereas stellar dispersions require an orbital-anisotropy model. **Its extended radial reach and comparatively simple force tracing** make neutral gas especially useful for outer-halo constraints, although warps and noncircular motions can invalidate that simplicity.
