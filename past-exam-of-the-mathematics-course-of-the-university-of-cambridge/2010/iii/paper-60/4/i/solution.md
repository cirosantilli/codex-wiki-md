<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Older disc [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) generally have larger [velocity dispersions](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md), partly through accumulated [galactic disk heating](../../../../../../galactic-disk-heating.md), so random-motion stresses supply more of their support against gravity. The radial [Jeans equation](../../../../../../jeans-equation.md) then requires a smaller mean rotation than the [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md), producing **larger stellar asymmetric drift** than in dynamically colder young [stars](../../../../../../star.md).
