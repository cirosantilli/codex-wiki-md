<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Metal [absorption lines](../../../../../../absorption-line.md) depend on the [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature.md), [ionization](../../../../../../ionization.md) states and luminosity weights of the contributing [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md), as well as their [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md). A young [stellar population](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) dominated by hot luminous [stars](../../../../../../star.md) can have weak metal [absorption lines](../../../../../../absorption-line.md) even at substantial abundance, so **weak lines alone do not establish low metallicity**, an aspect of the [age-metallicity degeneracy](../../../../../../age-metallicity-degeneracy.md).
