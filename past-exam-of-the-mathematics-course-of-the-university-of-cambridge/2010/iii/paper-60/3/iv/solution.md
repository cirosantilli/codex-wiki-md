<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For an isolated final system with isotropic one-dimensional [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma$, the random [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $K=\tfrac12M\langle v^2\rangle=\tfrac32M\sigma^2$. The [virial theorem](../../../../../../virial-theorem.md) gives $W=-2K$ and total energy $E_f=K+W=-K$. Starting from a diffuse, almost unbound gas reservoir with $E_i\simeq0$, [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) therefore requires the [radiative energy of virialized gravitational collapse](../../../../../../radiative-energy-of-virialized-gravitational-collapse.md)

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm rad}\simeq E_i-E_f\simeq\frac32M\sigma^2\simeq1\times10^{52}\,\mathrm J}.
$$

It is the decrease in total energy, not the whole final magnitude $|W|$, that is lost; half of the binding-energy magnitude remains as [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Assuming radiation carries most of this loss, the average [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) during the nominal [galactic dynamical time](../../../../../../galactic-dynamical-time.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle L\rangle\simeq\frac{1.2\times10^{52}}{1.75\times10^{15}}
\simeq7\times10^{36}\,\mathrm W\simeq2\times10^{10}L_\odot},
$$

using $L_\odot\simeq4\times10^{26}\,\mathrm W$. This is comparable in order of magnitude to the stellar [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) of a characteristic $L_*$ [galaxy](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md) in the [Schechter function](../../../../../../schechter-function.md), often of order $10^{10}L_\odot$ with a band-dependent definition. The equality is an energy-scale comparison: ordinary present starlight is powered mainly by [stellar nucleosynthesis](../../../../../../stellar-nucleosynthesis.md), whereas this estimate concerns gravitational energy lost during formation. Appreciable initial binding energy, mass loss or external stresses alter the coefficient, and the finite-boundary [singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere.md) cannot be used as an isolated exact [virial equilibrium](../../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md) without its boundary term.
