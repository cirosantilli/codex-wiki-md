<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [G-dwarf problem](../../../../../../g-dwarf-problem.md) is the observed shortage of metal-poor, long-lived disc [stars](../../../../../../star.md) relative to a simple [closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](../../../../../../closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md). In the fully evolved comparison, the half-rate [proportional-infall model of galactic chemical evolution](../../../../../../proportional-infall-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md) lowers the low-$Z$ fraction from $1-e^{-Z/y}$ to $Z/(2y)$, and prevents the closed-box tail from extending to arbitrarily high [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md). It therefore reduces the relative weight of the earliest metal-poor generation and the very metal-rich tail.

**This particular constant-proportional-infall model is an improvement, not a complete fit.** It still forms a metal-poor population immediately from its initially pristine reservoir, with nonzero constant $dN/dZ$ all the way down to zero, and predicts a broad linear distribution followed by an abrupt upper cutoff instead of a generally smooth, concentrated observed distribution. Comparison with observed abundance histograms must first use the same coordinate: its [logarithmic metallicity distribution](../../../../../../logarithmic-metallicity-distribution.md) rises in proportion to $Z$ up to that cutoff, rather than remaining flat in logarithmic bins. [Local G-dwarf measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9510101) exhibit a prominent logarithmic-abundance peak and a paucity of low-abundance stars; translating iron abundances into total $Z$ also requires an abundance-ratio model. More realistic histories can vary the [galactic gas inflow](../../../../../../galactic-gas-inflow.md) and [star formation rate](../../../../../../star-formation-rate.md), allow recycling and delayed enrichment, and account for migration and selection of different [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md); simply tuning a constant [stellar yield](../../../../../../stellar-yield.md) does not remove all these shape deficiencies.
