<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $a=\eta_0$ and $\chi=kx-\omega t$. For a [deep-water gravity wave](../../../../../../deep-water-gravity-wave.md), $\omega^2=gk$ and the first-order [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) is $(a\omega/k)e^{kz}\sin\chi$. Therefore

$$
u_1=a\omega e^{kz}\cos\chi,\qquad
w_1=a\omega e^{kz}\sin\chi.
$$

Relative to mean parcel labels, integrate at fixed zeroth-order position to obtain the oscillatory [fluid displacement](../../../../../../lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md)

$$
\xi_1=-ae^{kz}\sin\chi,\qquad \zeta_1=ae^{kz}\cos\chi.
$$

The [Stokes drift](../../../../../../stokes-drift.md) is the period mean of the displacement correction to the first-order [velocity field](../../../../../../velocity-field.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_s&=\left\langle\xi_1u_{1x}+\zeta_1u_{1z}\right\rangle
=a^2k\omega e^{2kz}\langle\sin^2\chi+\cos^2\chi\rangle,\\
w_s&=\left\langle\xi_1w_{1x}+\zeta_1w_{1z}\right\rangle=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\boxed{u_s=\eta_0^2k\omega e^{2kz}}$, in the direction of propagation. A parcel samples faster forward motion near the top of its orbit and weaker backward motion lower down. This is the leading second-order drift of small-steepness waves, $k\eta_0\ll1$, relative to the [Eulerian mean velocity](../../../../../../eulerian-mean-flow.md).
