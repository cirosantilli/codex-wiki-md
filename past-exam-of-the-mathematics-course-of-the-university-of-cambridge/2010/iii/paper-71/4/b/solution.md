<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the positive-frequency branch with $k>0$, let $K=|\mathbf k|$, and write $\mathbf k=K(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. The [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../internal-wave.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega=Nk/K=N\cos\theta$. The [phase velocity](../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c_p=\frac{N}{K}\bigl(\cos^2\theta,\cos\theta\sin\theta\bigr),\qquad
\mathbf c_g=\frac{N}{K}\bigl(\sin^2\theta,-\cos\theta\sin\theta\bigr).}
$$

They are perpendicular: the phase advances normal to the crests while energy travels along them. For a general signed $k$, the positive frequency is $N|k|/K$ and the horizontal component of group velocity changes sign with $k$.

If $\omega>N$, there is no real wave vector satisfying this relation. For prescribed real horizontal [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md), $m^2=k^2(N^2/\omega^2-1)<0$, so a bounded boundary response is an [evanescent wave](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md), rather than a freely propagating plane wave.

For a single incompressible plane wave, [internal-wave polarization](../../../../../../internal-wave-polarization.md) gives $\mathbf u_1\cdot\mathbf k=0$. Its oscillatory displacement $\boldsymbol\xi_1$ is parallel to $\mathbf u_1$ and therefore also perpendicular to $\mathbf k$. Every spatial derivative of the plane-wave field is proportional to a component of $\mathbf k$, so

$$
\boxed{(\boldsymbol\xi_1\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u_1=0.}
$$

There is no leading [Stokes drift](../../../../../../stokes-drift.md) for that single wave. The fluid parcels oscillate along straight lines within phase planes and never sample a spatial change of the wave along their displacement. This conclusion does not prohibit cross terms between different wave vectors.
