<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Locally write the [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md) as $S=0$ with $dS\ne0$, and extend its normal as $n_a=\nabla_aS$. On the hypersurface, $n^an_a=0$, so $n^a\nabla_aS=n^2=0$: the raised normal is itself tangent. The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) and symmetry of the scalar Hessian give

$$
n^b\nabla_bn_a=n^b\nabla_an_b=\frac12\nabla_a(n^2).
$$

Although $n^2$ vanishes on the hypersurface, it need not vanish off it. Its tangential derivatives vanish, so $\nabla_a(n^2)$ must be proportional to the normal $n_a$. Hence $\nabla_nn=\kappa n$ on the hypersurface for some smooth local coefficient $\kappa$. The normal's [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) remain in the hypersurface and satisfy the nonaffine [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md). Rescale to $k=hn$, where $n(\log h)=-\kappa$ along each generator. Direct substitution gives $\nabla_kk=0$. Thus **the normal generates null geodesics within the hypersurface**, with an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) after this rescaling. This proves the [null hypersurface normal generates null geodesics](../../../../../../null-hypersurface-normal-generates-null-geodesics.md) property without incorrectly setting a transverse derivative of $n^2$ to zero.
