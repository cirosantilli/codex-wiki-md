<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the affine tangent $k^a$ to a [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md), and an auxiliary [null vector](../../../../../../null-vector.md) $\ell^a$ with $k\cdot\ell=-1$. The [screen-space projector](../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md)

$$
q_{ab}=g_{ab}+k_a\ell_b+\ell_ak_b
$$

annihilates $k$ and $\ell$ and restricts to a positive-definite two-dimensional metric in four spacetime dimensions. Neighboring transverse rays have relative displacement governed by the [optical tensor](../../../../../../optical-tensor.md)

$$
B_{ab}=q_a{}^cq_b{}^d\nabla_dk_c.
$$

Its irreducible decomposition defines the three requested optical quantities:

$$
\boxed{\theta=q^{ab}B_{ab},\qquad
\sigma_{ab}=B_{(ab)}-\frac12\theta q_{ab},\qquad
\omega_{ab}=B_{[ab]}.}
$$

The [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) $\theta$ is the fractional rate of change of transverse area, $d\log A/d\lambda$. The [null shear](../../../../../../null-shear.md) $\sigma$ changes a beam's shape at fixed area to first order; it is symmetric and trace-free. The [null twist](../../../../../../null-twist.md) $\omega$ is antisymmetric and measures local rotation of the transverse rays; it vanishes for a hypersurface-orthogonal [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md). In $D$ dimensions replace $1/2$ by $1/(D-2)$. These definitions use the trace convention for [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md), rather than the alternative screen-averaged convention.
