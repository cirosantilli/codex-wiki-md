<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [event horizon](../../../../../../event-horizon.md) is a [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md). For its spherical defining function $H(v,r)=r-r_H(v)$, the inverse radial [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) has $g^{vv}=0$, $g^{vr}=1$ and $g^{rr}=f=1-2m(v)/r$. The normal's [null condition](../../../../../../null-condition.md) is

$$
0=g^{ab}\partial_aH\partial_bH=f(v,r_H)-2\dot r_H.
$$

It follows that the [event horizon](../../../../../../event-horizon.md) follows the outgoing [radial null geodesics of the Vaidya metric](../../../../../../radial-null-geodesics-of-the-vaidya-metric.md):

$$
\boxed{2r_H\dot r_H=r_H-2m(v).}
$$

This [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) also describes other outgoing [null hypersurfaces](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md); the global [event horizon](../../../../../../event-horizon.md) is selected by its future boundary condition, not by this local equation alone.
