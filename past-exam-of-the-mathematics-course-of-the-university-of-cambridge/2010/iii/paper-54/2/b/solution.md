<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the future-directed [null vector](../../../../../../null-vector.md) $k=-\partial_r$, whose dual is $k_a=-(dv)_a$. The [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) has the [null dust](../../../../../../null-dust.md) form

$$
T_{ab}=\varepsilon k_ak_b,\qquad \varepsilon=\frac{\dot m(v)}{4\pi r^2}.
$$

For every future-directed [timelike vector](../../../../../../timelike-vector.md) $t$, $k\cdot t<0$. The energy current appearing in the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md) is

$$
J^a=-T^a{}_bt^b=\varepsilon(-k\cdot t)k^a.
$$

If $\varepsilon\geq0$, this is a future-directed [null vector](../../../../../../null-vector.md), or zero; also $T_{ab}t^at^b=\varepsilon(k\cdot t)^2\geq0$. If $\varepsilon<0$, the current is past-directed and that energy density is negative. Therefore, given the already stipulated positivity and smoothness of $m$, the necessary and sufficient additional condition is

$$
\boxed{\dot m(v)\geq0\quad\text{for every }v.}
$$
