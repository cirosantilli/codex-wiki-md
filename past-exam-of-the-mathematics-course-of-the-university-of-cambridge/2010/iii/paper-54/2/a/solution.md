<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a curve with fixed $v,\theta,\phi$, its tangent is proportional to $\partial_r$. The [Vaidya metric](../../../../../../vaidya-metric.md) has $g_{rr}=0$, so the curve is a [null curve](../../../../../../null-curve.md). Moreover, the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) with two lower $r$ indices vanish:

$$
\Gamma^a{}_{rr}=\frac12g^{ab}(2\partial_rg_{br}-\partial_bg_{rr})=0,
$$

because $g_{vr}=1$ is constant and every other $g_{br}$ is zero. Thus the [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) along this tangent gives $\nabla_{\partial_r}\partial_r=0$. The [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) is satisfied with $r$ as an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md); choosing future orientation gives the tangent $k=-\partial_r$ and an affine parameter increasing as $-r$. **These are the ingoing radial null geodesics.**
