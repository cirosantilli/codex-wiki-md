<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) has symmetric [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md), so antisymmetrization removes its connection terms. Hence the [Papapetrou electromagnetic field](../../../../../../../papapetrou-electromagnetic-field.md) is

$$
F_{ab}=\nabla_aV_b-\nabla_bV_a=2\nabla_aV_b,
$$

where the last equality uses the [Killing equation](../../../../../../../killing-equation.md). As a [differential two-form](../../../../../../../2-form.md), $F=dV^\flat$, so the identity $d^2=0$ for the [exterior derivative](../../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) proves $\nabla_{[a}F_{bc]}=0$. Contracting the preceding [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](../../../../../../../second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) gives

$$
\nabla^a\nabla_aV_b=-R_{bd}V^d,
\qquad \nabla^aF_{ab}=-2R_{bd}V^d.
$$

A [vacuum spacetime](../../../../../../../vacuum-spacetime.md) here has $R_{ab}=0$, with no [cosmological constant](../../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md). Therefore **both source-free Maxwell equations hold**:

$$
\boxed{\nabla_{[a}F_{bc]}=0,\qquad \nabla^aF_{ab}=0.}
$$

This is a test [electromagnetic field](../../../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) on the given [vacuum spacetime](../../../../../../../vacuum-spacetime.md); its own [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) is not included in that background's [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md).
