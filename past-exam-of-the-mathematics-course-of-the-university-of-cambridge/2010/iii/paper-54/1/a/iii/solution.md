<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In Cartesian coordinates $x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi$, $y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi$, $z=r\cos\theta$, the rotational [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) and its dual [differential one-form](../../../../../../../one-form.md) are

$$
V=-y\partial_x+x\partial_y,\qquad V^\flat=-y\,dx+x\,dy.
$$

Therefore the [Papapetrou electromagnetic field](../../../../../../../papapetrou-electromagnetic-field.md) is

$$
F=dV^\flat=2\,dx\wedge dy.
$$

Choose the [electromagnetic field](../../../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) convention $F_{it}=E_i$ and $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B^k$. There are no time components, and $F_{xy}=2$, so **this is a uniform magnetic field along the rotation axis, with no electric field**:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E=0,\qquad \mathbf B=2\,\mathbf e_z.}
$$

Multiplying the [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) by $B_0/2$ produces any desired uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) strength $B_0$.
