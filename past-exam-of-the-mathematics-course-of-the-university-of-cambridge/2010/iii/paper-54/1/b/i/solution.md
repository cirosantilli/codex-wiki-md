<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $r_+=\ell\sqrt M$ and $f=(r^2-r_+^2)/\ell^2$. The apparent failure of the nonrotating [BTZ black hole](../../../../../../../btz-black-hole.md) coordinates at $r=r_+$ is a [coordinate singularity](../../../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md). Define the [tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../../tortoise-coordinate.md) and ingoing time by

$$
r_* =\frac{\ell^2}{2r_+}\log\left|\frac{r-r_+}{r+r_+}\right|,
\qquad v=t+r_*.
$$

The [Ingoing BTZ coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-btz-coordinates.md) give

$$
ds^2=-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\phi^2.
$$

All coefficients are analytic at $r_+>0$, and the determinant is $-r^2$, so the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) extends nondegenerately through that radius. The [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $K=\partial_v$ agrees with $\partial_t$ in the exterior. On $r=r_+$, $K^2=-f=0$ and $K_a=(dr)_a$. It is therefore both normal and tangent to this [null hypersurface](../../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md), proving that it is a [Killing horizon](../../../../../../../killing-horizon.md).

One may include both horizon branches and their [bifurcation surface](../../../../../../../bifurcation-surface.md) explicitly. Set $\kappa=r_+/\ell^2$, $u=t-r_*$ and, initially in the exterior, $U=-e^{-\kappa u}$, $V=e^{\kappa v}$. Then

$$
UV=-\frac{r-r_+}{r+r_+},\qquad
r=r_+\frac{1-UV}{1+UV},
\qquad
ds^2=-\frac{4\ell^2\,dU\,dV}{(1+UV)^2}+r_+^2\left(\frac{1-UV}{1+UV}\right)^2d\phi^2.
$$

This [analytic extension of a spacetime](../../../../../../../analytic-extension-of-a-spacetime.md) is regular near $UV=0$, and $K=\kappa(V\partial_V-U\partial_U)$ extends analytically. The surfaces $U=0$ and $V=0$ form the **extended Killing horizon at $r=r_+$**; $K$ vanishes only at their regular bifurcation circle.
