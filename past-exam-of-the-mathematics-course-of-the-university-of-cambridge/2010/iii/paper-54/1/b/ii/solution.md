<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the exterior-normalized [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $K=\partial_t=\partial_v$ from the previous part. In [Ingoing BTZ coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-btz-coordinates.md), its only nonzero acceleration component on $r=r_+$ is

$$
K^b\nabla_bK^v=\Gamma^v{}_{vv}=\frac12f'(r_+),
\qquad K^b\nabla_bK^r=\frac12ff'\big|_{r_+}=0.
$$

The defining equation for [surface gravity](../../../../../../../surface-gravity.md), $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$, therefore gives the positive future-branch value

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\frac{f'(r_+)}2=\frac{r_+}{\ell^2}=\frac{\sqrt M}{\ell}.}
$$

This value uses exactly the prescribed [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) normalization. A constant rescaling of the [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) rescales its [surface gravity](../../../../../../../surface-gravity.md).
