<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $\alpha=1$, the phase is $-x+\sin x=-x^3/6+O(x^5)$, so the ordinary nonstationary endpoint formula fails. In the [degenerate endpoint in Laplace's method](../../../../../../../degenerate-endpoint-in-laplace-s-method.md), take $x=\lambda^{-1/3}t$ and extend the localized [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) to infinity:

$$
f(\lambda,1)\sim\lambda^{-1/3}\int_0^\infty e^{-t^3/6}\,dt=\boxed{\frac{6^{1/3}\Gamma(1/3)}{3\lambda^{1/3}}.}
$$

The last equality follows by $u=t^3/6$. It is the $\nu=0$ value of the [cubic Laplace transition integral](../../../../../../../cubic-laplace-transition-integral.md) after its normalization is rescaled to the coefficient $1/6$.
