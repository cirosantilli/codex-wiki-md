<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

We prove the forward direction of the [finite-generator criterion for a natural uniform algebra](../../../../../../finite-generator-criterion-for-a-natural-uniform-algebra.md). Suppose that $A$ is a [natural uniform algebra](../../../../../../natural-uniform-algebra.md), and let $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ have no common zero on $K$. The [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md)

$$
J=f_1A+\cdots+f_nA
$$

is all of $A$. Otherwise it is contained in a [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) $M$, whose associated [character of an algebra](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) vanishes on all the $f_j$. Since every [algebra character](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) is an [evaluation character](../../../../../../evaluation-character.md) under naturality, this would give a common zero, a contradiction. Therefore $1\in J$, meaning that

$$
\boxed{\sum_{j=1}^nf_jg_j=1\quad\hbox{for suitable }g_j\in A.}
$$

Here are the Banach-algebra details behind the [algebra character](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) associated with $M$. A proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) cannot have dense closure: if it contained an element within distance less than one of $1$, the [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md) would make that element invertible, forcing the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) to contain $1$. Thus the closure of a proper [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is proper. A [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md), obtained from [Zorn's lemma](../../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md), is therefore closed. Its quotient is a complex unital [Banach algebra](../../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md) in which every nonzero element is invertible. The [Gelfand-Mazur theorem](../../../../../../gelfand-mazur-theorem.md) identifies this quotient with $\mathbb C$, so the quotient map is the required [algebra character](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md). No closedness of the originally generated [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $J$ is needed.
