↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A [uniform algebra](../../../../../uniform-algebra.md) on a [compact Hausdorff space](../../../../../compact-hausdorff-space.md) $K$ is a closed complex subalgebra $A\subseteq C(K)$ containing the constants and separating points of $K$, with the [supremum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md). It is therefore a commutative unital [Banach algebra](../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md). Its [character space of an algebra](../../../../../character-space-of-an-algebra.md) $\Phi_A$, also called its carrier space, consists of nonzero multiplicative [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md), equipped with the [Gelfand topology](../../../../../gelfand-topology.md).

For $x\in K$, evaluation gives the [character of an algebra](../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) $\delta_x(f)=f(x)$. The map $\delta:K\to\Phi_A$ is continuous: composing it with the coordinate $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(f)$ gives the continuous function $f$ for every $f\in A$. It is injective because $A$ separates points. The [Gelfand topology](../../../../../gelfand-topology.md) is Hausdorff because distinct [algebra characters](../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) differ on some element of $A$. A continuous injection from compact $K$ into a Hausdorff space is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) onto its image: closed subsets of $K$ have compact, hence closed, images. Its image is also compact and closed. Thus

$$
\boxed{\delta:K\longrightarrow j(K)=\{\delta_x:x\in K\}\subseteq\Phi_A}
$$

is the asserted [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md). A [natural uniform algebra](../../../../../natural-uniform-algebra.md) is one for which this image is all of $\Phi_A$.
