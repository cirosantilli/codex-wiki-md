<h1 id="5/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For each $k$, the lifted [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) on $X$ is $T$-invariant and the two [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) still act freely. [Sunada's theorem](../../../../../../sunada-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Spec}(N,g_{1k})=\operatorname{Spec}(N,g_{2k}).}
$$

To rule out any other equalities, the leading diagonal [heat kernel expansion](../../../../../../heat-kernel-expansion.md) from Question 3 gives, on a closed surface,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tP})=\frac{\operatorname{area}(N)}{4\pi t}+O(1)\quad(t\downarrow0).
$$

Thus the function [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) determines area: multiply the [heat trace](../../../../../../heat-trace.md) by $4\pi t$ and take the limit. Since the chosen areas are distinct for distinct $k$, no cross-pair spectra can agree. This proves exactly the prescribed spectral coincidences.
