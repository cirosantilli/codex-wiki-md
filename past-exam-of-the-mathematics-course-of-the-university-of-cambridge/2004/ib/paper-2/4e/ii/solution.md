<h1 id="4e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

There is a small defect in the printed definition: the listed family omits $\mathbb R$ and is not a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md), since $\bigcup_{n\geq1}(-n,\infty)=\mathbb R$. We use the intended [upper-ray topology on the real line](../../../../../../upper-ray-topology-on-the-real-line.md), obtained by adjoining $\mathbb R$ to that family.

Take **$f(n)=n$**. For every real $a$,

$$
f^{-1}((a,\infty))=\{n\in\mathbb N:n>a\}
$$

has finite complement and is therefore open in the [cofinite topology](../../../../../../cofinite-topology.md). The inverse images of the empty set and the whole line are also open. Hence this nonconstant function is a [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) for the corrected [upper-ray topology on the real line](../../../../../../upper-ray-topology-on-the-real-line.md). The same inverse-image calculation verifies the condition for every ray literally listed in the question.
