<h1 id="4e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Any two nonempty [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) in the [cofinite topology](../../../../../../cofinite-topology.md) on $\mathbb N$ intersect: their complements have finite union, which cannot exhaust $\mathbb N$. Thus this is a [hyperconnected space](../../../../../../hyperconnected-space.md). Suppose a [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f$ took two distinct values $a$ and $b$. The usual real line is a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), so there are disjoint open intervals $U$ and $V$ containing $a$ and $b$. Their inverse images under the [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) are disjoint, nonempty [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md), contradicting hyperconnectedness. **Every such $f$ is constant.**
