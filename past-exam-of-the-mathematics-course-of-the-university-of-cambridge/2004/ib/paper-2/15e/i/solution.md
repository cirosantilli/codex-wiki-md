<h1 id="15e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a sequence $\epsilon$, let $C_n(\epsilon)$ be its prefix [cylinder set](../../../../../../cylinder-set.md), consisting of sequences agreeing with $\epsilon$ in the first $n$ coordinates. The specified collection consists exactly of unions of such [cylinder sets](../../../../../../cylinder-set.md).

The empty set meets the defining condition vacuously, and $X$ meets it because every [cylinder set](../../../../../../cylinder-set.md) is contained in $X$. For a union $\bigcup_\lambda Y_\lambda$, a point lies in one $Y_\lambda$ and has a prefix [cylinder set](../../../../../../cylinder-set.md) contained in that set, hence contained in the union. For a finite intersection, a point has a cylinder neighbourhood in every constituent. Taking the largest of the finitely many prefix lengths gives one [cylinder set](../../../../../../cylinder-set.md) contained in all of them. Therefore the collection contains the empty and whole sets, is closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections, and **is a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) on $X$**, the prefix topology of [Cantor space](../../../../../../cantor-space.md).
