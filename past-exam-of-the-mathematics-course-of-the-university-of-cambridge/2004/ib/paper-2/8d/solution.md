<h1 id="8d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8D](../8d.md)

The two given [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) are orthonormal. Resolve the initial state as $\psi(0)=\cos\theta\,\psi_0-\sin\theta\,\psi_1$. The [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\psi(t)=\cos\theta\,e^{-iE_0t/\hbar}\psi_0-\sin\theta\,e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}\psi_1.
$$

The second component, which is the [probability amplitude](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) for the requested final state, is $\sin\theta\cos\theta(e^{-iE_0t/\hbar}-e^{-iE_1t/\hbar})$. By the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md),

$$
\boxed{P(t)=\sin^2(2\theta)\sin^2\!\left(\frac{(E_1-E_0)t}{2\hbar}\right).}
$$

This includes $P(t)=0$ when the [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue.md) coincide or when the initial state is itself an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md).
