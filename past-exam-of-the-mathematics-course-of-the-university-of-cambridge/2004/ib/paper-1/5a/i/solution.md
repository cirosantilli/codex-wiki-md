<h1 id="5a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factor the denominator as $z^2(1+z^2)$. Near zero,

$$
\frac1{z^2(1+z^2)}=z^{-2}-1+z^2-\cdots,
$$

so zero is a [double pole](../../../../../../double-pole.md) with [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) zero. At $z=\pm i$ the [poles](../../../../../../pole.md) are simple and the denominator derivative is $2z+4z^3=-2z$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_{0}=0,\qquad\operatorname{Res}_{i}=\frac i2,\qquad\operatorname{Res}_{-i}=-\frac i2.}
$$

These are all the [poles](../../../../../../pole.md).
