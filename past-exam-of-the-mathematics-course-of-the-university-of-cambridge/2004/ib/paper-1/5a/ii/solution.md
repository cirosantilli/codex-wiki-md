<h1 id="5a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The only [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) is the simple one at $z=1$, with **$\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_{1}=e}$**. The point $z=0$ is an [essential singularity](../../../../../../essential-singularity.md), not another [pole](../../../../../../pole.md): multiplying $e^{1/z^2}$ by a nonvanishing analytic factor cannot remove its infinite principal part. For completeness its [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) can also be read from

$$
-\left(\sum_{j\geq0}z^j\right)\left(\sum_{k\geq0}\frac{z^{-2k}}{k!}\right),\qquad0<|z|<1.
$$

The coefficient of $z^{-1}$ is $-\sum_{k\geq1}1/k!=1-e$. This extra [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) belongs to the [essential singularity](../../../../../../essential-singularity.md); it does not change the list of [poles](../../../../../../pole.md).
