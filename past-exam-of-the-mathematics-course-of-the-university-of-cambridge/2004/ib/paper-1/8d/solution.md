<h1 id="8d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8D](../8d.md)

For a real potential, the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) and its [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) give

$$
\partial_t|\psi|^2=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}(\psi^*\psi_{xx}-\psi\psi^*_{xx}).
$$

The potential terms cancel. The expression on the right is a total derivative, giving

$$
\boxed{\rho=|\psi|^2,\qquad j=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}(\psi^*\psi_x-\psi\psi_x^*)=\frac\hbar m\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\psi_x),\qquad\rho_t+j_x=0.}
$$

For the [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md), $\psi_t=-i\omega\psi$ and $\psi_{xx}=-k^2\psi$. With zero potential, substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\omega(k)=\frac{\hbar k^2}{2m},\qquad\rho=1,\qquad j=\frac{\hbar k}{m}.}
$$

It is a free-particle [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) with momentum $\hbar k$, [energy](../../../../../energy.md) $\hbar\omega$ and uniform [probability](../../../../../probability.md) flux. On the whole real line it is a generalized state, not a normalized square-integrable [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function.md); wave packets or box normalization give the corresponding physical interpretation.
