<h1 id="4g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The finite [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives $F_n(x)=(1-x^{n+1})/(1-x)$, so the [pointwise limit](../../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) is $F(x)=1/(1-x)$. The error is $x^{n+1}/(1-x)$, unbounded as $x\uparrow1$ for every fixed $n$. Thus **convergence is not uniform on $(0,1)$**. It is uniform on any smaller interval bounded away from one, since the error is at most $b^{n+1}/(1-b)$ for $0<x\leq b<1$.
