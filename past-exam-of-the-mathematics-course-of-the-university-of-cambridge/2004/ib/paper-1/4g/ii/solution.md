<h1 id="4g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every fixed $x>0$, $nx^2\to\infty$, so the [pointwise limit](../../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) is zero. But $\sup_{0<x<1}e^{-nx^2}=1$ for every $n$, since $x$ can approach zero. Hence **convergence is not uniform**. For an explicit moving-point test take $x_n=n^{-1/2}$ for $n>1$, which gives $F_n(x_n)=e^{-1}$.
