<h1 id="14f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A proper finite-index subgroup of a torsion-free group cannot be a [direct summand](../../../../../../direct-summand.md) when the complement is a subgroup of that same [free abelian group](../../../../../../free-abelian-group.md). Indeed, if $L=M\oplus K$, every $k\in K$ has $18k\in M$ because the quotient has order eighteen. But also $18k\in K$, so $18k=0$ by the trivial intersection. The ambient group $\mathbb Z^3$ is torsion-free, forcing $k=0$ and hence $K=0$. This would give $M=L$, contrary to index eighteen. Thus **$M$ is not a [direct summand](../../../../../../direct-summand.md)**.
