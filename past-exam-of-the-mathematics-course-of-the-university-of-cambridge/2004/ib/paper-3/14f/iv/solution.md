<h1 id="14f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the integer [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $u,v,e_3$, direct substitution gives $w=-7u+5v+18e_3$. Therefore

$$
M=\mathbb Zu\oplus\mathbb Zv\oplus18\mathbb Ze_3,
\qquad
\boxed{L/M\cong\mathbb Z/18\mathbb Z.}
$$

The first two [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) coordinates disappear in the quotient and the third is taken modulo eighteen. Equivalently the [Smith normal form](../../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) has diagonal entries $1,1,18$.
