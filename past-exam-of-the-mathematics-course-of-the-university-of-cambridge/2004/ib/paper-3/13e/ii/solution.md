<h1 id="13e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take a size-three [Jordan block](../../../../../../jordan-block.md) at one and a scalar block at three:

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0&0\\0&1&1&0\\0&0&1&0\\0&0&0&3\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Triangularity gives $\chi_A(x)=(x-1)^3(x-3)$. On the first block, $(A-I)^3$ is zero but $(A-I)^2$ is not; on the last component the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is three. Thus both factors with exactly those powers are needed, and their product annihilates $A$. Its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is therefore also $(x-1)^3(x-3)$.
