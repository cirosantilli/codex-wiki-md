↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The form of [Rouché's theorem](../../../../../../rouche-s-theorem.md) used here is as follows. Suppose $f$ and $g$ are holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closure of a bounded Jordan domain, and $|g-f|<|f|$ on its positively oriented boundary. Then $f$ and $g$ have the same number of zeros inside, counted with multiplicity.

To prove this form, put $f_t=f+t(g-f)$ for $0\leq t\leq1$. The strict inequality prevents $f_t$ from vanishing on the boundary. The [argument principle](../../../../../../argument-principle.md) counts its interior zeros by $(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint f_t'/f_t$. This integral varies continuously with $t$: the integrand is continuous and its denominator stays uniformly away from zero on the compact boundary times $[0,1]$. Its values are integers, so the integral is constant. Its values at $t=0,1$ are the respective zero counts. This proves Rouché's theorem with the multiplicities and boundary hypotheses required below.
