<h1 id="4e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The slit plane is connected and contains $D$. If two [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on it extend the same $f$, their difference vanishes on the nonempty open disc $D$. The [identity theorem](../../../../../../identity-theorem.md) forces their difference to vanish on the entire slit plane. Thus **there is at most one analytic extension of the chosen $f$**. Choosing the opposite square root initially changes that chosen function; it does not produce two extensions of the same function.
