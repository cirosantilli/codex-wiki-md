<h1 id="17d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [17D](../17d.md)

With metric signature $(+,-,-,-)$, [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is $p^\mu=(E/c,\mathbf p)$; for a particle of [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$ it satisfies $p^\mu p_\mu=m^2c^2$. For a massive particle $p^\mu=mu^\mu$, where $u^\mu$ is its four-velocity. The total four-momentum is the sum of the individual four-momenta. If that sum is timelike, its center-of-momentum frame exists and the total energy in that frame is

$$
\boxed{W=\sqrt{(E_1+E_2)^2-c^2|\mathbf p_1+\mathbf p_2|^2}.}
$$

This follows because the spatial momentum is zero in that frame and the Minkowski norm is invariant under a [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md). A null total momentum, such as parallel photons, would not have such a rest frame.

For the photon striking an electron initially at rest, the initial invariant gives $W^2=m_e^2c^4+2m_ec^2E_\gamma$. The three final particles have total rest energy $3m_ec^2$. Their center-of-momentum energy is at least this value, with equality when all have zero relative momentum. Energy-momentum conservation therefore requires

$$
m_e^2c^4+2m_ec^2E_\gamma\geq9m_e^2c^4,
\qquad
\boxed{E_\gamma\geq4m_ec^2.}
$$

The [photon-induced triplet pair-production threshold](../../../../../photon-induced-triplet-pair-production-threshold.md) is consequently **four electron rest-energy units**. This is attainable as a kinematic threshold: at equality the center-of-momentum speed in the laboratory is $4c/5$, and boosting the three resting final particles gives each energy $5m_ec^2/3$ and momentum $4m_ec/3$. Their totals are the initial $5m_ec^2$ and $4m_ec$. Recoil of the original electron is why twice the rest energy is insufficient.
