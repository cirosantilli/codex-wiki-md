<h1 id="12f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the [quotient ring](../../../../../../quotient-ring.md), the classes of $x-1$ and $x^2+x+1$ are nonzero: neither nonzero polynomial of degree less than three belongs to the ideal generated by $x^3-1$. But their product is the zero class, since

$$
(x-1)(x^2+x+1)=x^3-1.
$$

The quotient thus contains [zero divisors](../../../../../../zero-divisor.md), so **it is not an integral domain**.
