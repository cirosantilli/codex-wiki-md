<h1 id="12f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [polynomial ring](../../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[x]$, the ideal $(x)$ is nonzero and prime: the [quotient ring](../../../../../../quotient-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[x]/(x)$ is isomorphic to the [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) $\mathbb Z$, by evaluation at zero. It is not maximal, since that quotient is not a [field](../../../../../../field.md). Equivalently $(x)\subsetneq(2,x)\subsetneq\mathbb Z[x]$. Thus **$(x)$ is a nonzero prime ideal which is not maximal**.
