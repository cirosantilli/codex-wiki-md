<h1 id="1e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Divide the [linear congruence](../../../../../../linear-congruence.md) by the common factor $3$, also dividing its modulus:

$$
237x\equiv21\pmod{300}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad79x\equiv7\pmod{100}.
$$

The [Bézout's identity](../../../../../../bezout-identity.md) from part (a) makes $19$ the inverse of $79$ modulo $100$. Multiplication by $19$ therefore gives $x\equiv133\equiv33\pmod{100}$. Thus all [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) solutions are $\boxed{x=33+100k,\ k\in\mathbb Z}$. Equivalently, the three solution classes modulo $300$ are $33,133,233$; using modulus $300$ must not discard two of these classes.
