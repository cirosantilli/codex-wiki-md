<h1 id="1e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $x=11+100t$. Reduction of the other [integer congruence](../../../../../../integer-congruence.md) modulo $79$ gives $21t\equiv-3\pmod{79}$. The [Bézout's identity](../../../../../../bezout-identity.md) $19\cdot79-15\cdot100=1$ gives $-15\cdot21\equiv1\pmod{79}$, so $t\equiv45\pmod{79}$. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{x=4511+7900k,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Indeed, $4511=8+79\cdot57=11+100\cdot45$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) guarantees one class modulo $79\cdot100$, since the two moduli are [coprime](../../../../../../coprime-integers.md).
