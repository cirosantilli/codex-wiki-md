<h1 id="7b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7B](../7b.md)

Introduce the velocity variable $v=\dot x$. The [second-order differential equation](../../../../../second-order-differential-equation.md) becomes the [autonomous planar system](../../../../../autonomous-planar-system.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot x=v,\qquad \dot v=f(x,v).}
$$

A [critical point of a dynamical system](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a point where both components of the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) vanish: here $v=0$ and $f(x,0)=0$. It is a stationary solution in the [phase plane](../../../../../phase-plane.md). The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) at each such point gives the local [linear stability of a planar equilibrium](../../../../../linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md). For the equations below, a conserved [energy](../../../../../energy.md) also determines the nonlinear [phase portrait](../../../../../phase-portrait.md); this is important because purely imaginary [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone do not generally prove that an equilibrium is a [center equilibrium](../../../../../center-equilibrium.md).
