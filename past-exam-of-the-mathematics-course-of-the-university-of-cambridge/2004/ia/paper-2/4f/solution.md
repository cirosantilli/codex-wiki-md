<h1 id="4f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4F](../4f.md)

A [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) must integrate to one. Therefore

$$
1=c\int_0^1x(1-x)\,dx=c\left(\frac12-\frac13\right),
\qquad\boxed{c=6}.
$$

The first two [moments](../../../../../moment.md) are

$$
\mathbb EX=6\int_0^1x^2(1-x)\,dx=6\left(\frac13-\frac14\right)=\frac12,
\qquad
\mathbb EX^2=6\int_0^1x^3(1-x)\,dx=6\left(\frac14-\frac15\right)=\frac3{10}.
$$

Consequently the [mean](../../../../../expected-value.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EX=\frac12,\qquad \operatorname{var}(X)=\frac3{10}-\frac14=\frac1{20}.}
$$
