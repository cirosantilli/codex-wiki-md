<h1 id="3f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) is symmetric, because multiplication of real [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) is commutative. Expanding with the [bilinearity](../../../../../../bilinearity.md) just proved gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{cov}(X+Y,X-Y)
&=\operatorname{var}(X)-\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)
 +\operatorname{cov}(Y,X)-\operatorname{var}(Y)\\
&=\operatorname{var}(X)-\operatorname{var}(Y).
\end{aligned}
$$

Identical [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) imply equal second [moments](../../../../../../moment.md) and equal [means](../../../../../../expected-value.md), hence equal [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md). Therefore **the assertion is true**, with

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cov}(X+Y,X-Y)=0.}
$$

The cancellation shows exactly why no assumption of [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) is needed.
