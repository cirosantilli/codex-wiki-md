↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) gives $\mathbb E(X+Y)=\mathbb EX+\mathbb EY$. Expand the centered product and use [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) again:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{cov}(X+Y,Z)
&=\mathbb E\big[((X-\mathbb EX)+(Y-\mathbb EY))(Z-\mathbb EZ)\big]\\
&=\operatorname{cov}(X,Z)+\operatorname{cov}(Y,Z).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus **the assertion is true whenever the covariances are defined**. Neither [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) nor identical distributions are required.
