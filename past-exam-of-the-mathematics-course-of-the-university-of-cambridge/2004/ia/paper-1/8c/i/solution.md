↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Yes.** Suppose $\alpha_1a_1+\alpha_2a_2+\alpha_3a_3=0$. Taking its [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $a_2\times a_3$ eliminates the last two terms and gives

$$
\alpha_1\,a_1\cdot(a_2\times a_3)=0.
$$

The nonzero [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) forces $\alpha_1=0$. Cyclically using $a_3\times a_1$ and $a_1\times a_2$ forces $\alpha_2=\alpha_3=0$ as well. This is the definition of [linear independence](../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Equivalently the three-column [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md).
