[Rolle's theorem](../../../../../../rolle-theorem.md) states that if a real [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $h$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, differentiable on $(a,b)$, with $a<b$ and $h(a)=h(b)$, then some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies $h'(c)=0$.
