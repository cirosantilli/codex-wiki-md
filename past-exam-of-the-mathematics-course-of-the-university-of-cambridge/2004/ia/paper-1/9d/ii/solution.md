<h1 id="9d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the two-function assertion, define

$$
H(x)=[f(b)-f(a)][g(x)-g(a)]
-[g(b)-g(a)][f(x)-f(a)].
$$

This [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) has the required continuity and differentiability and satisfies $H(a)=H(b)=0$. [Rolle's theorem](../../../../../../rolle-theorem.md) therefore gives $c$ with

$$
\boxed{[f(b)-f(a)]g'(c)=[g(b)-g(a)]f'(c).}
$$

This proves the [Cauchy mean value theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-mean-value-theorem.md) even when either endpoint increment vanishes; no division was used.
