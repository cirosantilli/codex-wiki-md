<h1 id="3c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [contraction of two Levi-Civita symbols](../../../../../../contraction-of-two-levi-civita-symbols.md) gives $[F\times(\nabla\times G)]_i=F_j\partial_iG_j-F_j\partial_jG_i$. Similarly $[G\times(\nabla\times F)]_i=G_j\partial_iF_j-G_j\partial_jF_i$. Add the two directional-derivative terms to cancel the last terms, leaving

$$
F_j\partial_iG_j+G_j\partial_iF_j=\partial_i(F_jG_j).
$$

The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) has therefore proved the [gradient of a dot product](../../../../../../gradient-of-a-dot-product.md) identity

$$
\boxed{\nabla(F\cdot G)=(F\cdot\nabla)G+(G\cdot\nabla)F+F\times(\nabla\times G)+G\times(\nabla\times F).}
$$
