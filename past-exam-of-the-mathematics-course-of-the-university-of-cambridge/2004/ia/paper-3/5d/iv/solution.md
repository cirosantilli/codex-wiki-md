↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The rotation $g$ has order six. The possible [cycle types](../../../../../../cycle-type.md) in the [alternating group](../../../../../../alternating-group.md) $A_4$ are the identity, a three-cycle, and a product of two disjoint transpositions; their orders are one, three and two. Since a [group isomorphism](../../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) preserves the [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md), **$D_{12}$ is not isomorphic to $A_4$**.
