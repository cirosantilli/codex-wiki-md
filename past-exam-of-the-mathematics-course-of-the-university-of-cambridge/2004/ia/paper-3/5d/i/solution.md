<h1 id="5d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [involutions in an even dihedral group](../../../../../../involutions-in-an-even-dihedral-group.md) are the half-turn $g^3$ and the six reflections $g^jh$. Thus the seven order-two [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are

$$
\boxed{\langle g^3\rangle,\qquad\langle g^jh\rangle\quad(j=0,1,2,3,4,5).}
$$

The half-turn is central, so **only $\langle g^3\rangle$ is normal**. Indeed $g(g^jh)g^{-1}=g^{j+2}h$, a different reflection; an order-two subgroup has only one nonidentity element, so each reflection subgroup fails the [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) condition.
