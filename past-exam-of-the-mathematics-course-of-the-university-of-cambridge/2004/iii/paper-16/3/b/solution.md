<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**This sequence does occur**, for the oriented [connected sum](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md)

$$
\boxed{M=(S^1\times S^3)\#(S^1\times S^3).}
$$

The [Künneth theorem](../../../../../../kunneth-theorem.md) gives one copy of $\mathbb Z$ in dimensions $0,1,3,4$ for each factor product and zero in dimension two. Removing a disc from each summand and gluing their boundary three-spheres, the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](../../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) makes the [homology](../../../../../../homology-split.md) in dimensions one through three the direct sum of that of the two summands. The two [orientations](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) join to one top [fundamental class](../../../../../../fundamental-class.md), so $H_0=H_4=\mathbb Z$, $H_1=H_3=\mathbb Z^2$, and $H_2=0$.
