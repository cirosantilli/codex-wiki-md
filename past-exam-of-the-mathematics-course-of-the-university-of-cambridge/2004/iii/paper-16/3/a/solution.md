<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**This sequence cannot occur.** [Poincare duality](../../../../../../poincare-duality.md) and the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](../../../../../../universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) imply

$$
H_3(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H^1(M;\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_1(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)=0.
$$

Thus a nonzero rank-two third [homology](../../../../../../homology-split.md) group is incompatible with the proposed first [homology](../../../../../../homology-split.md) group.
