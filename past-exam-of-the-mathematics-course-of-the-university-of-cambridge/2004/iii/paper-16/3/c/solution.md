**This sequence cannot occur.** The same duality and coefficient calculation gives $H_3(M)=0$ when $H_1(M)=0$. More generally the [third homology of a closed oriented four-manifold](../../../../../../third-homology-of-a-closed-oriented-four-manifold.md) is torsion-free, whereas the proposed nonzero third group is torsion. Either observation rules it out.
