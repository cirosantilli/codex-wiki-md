<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the standard integral [cohomology ring](../../../../../../cohomology-ring.md) $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2)=\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3)$, $|u|=2$. Any self-homeomorphism $h$ sends $u$ to $u$ or $-u$, and hence sends $u^2$ to $u^2$. Its [Lefschetz number](../../../../../../lefschetz-number.md) is therefore $1\pm1+1$, which is either $3$ or $1$. By the [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md), every such [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) has a fixed point.

Now $\mathbb{CP}^2$ is [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md): its CW structure has no one-cells and only two- and four-cells. If it covered a connected space with degree $d\ge2$, it would be the [universal cover](../../../../../../universal-cover.md). The usual [covering space](../../../../../../covering-space.md) hypotheses hold for the base because it is locally homeomorphic to this manifold. Its [deck transformation group](../../../../../../deck-transformation-group.md) would act transitively on every fibre and have $d$ elements. A nonidentity [deck transformation](../../../../../../deck-transformation.md) has no fixed point: uniqueness of lifts makes one fixing a point the identity everywhere on the connected cover. This contradicts the preceding calculation. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb{CP}^2\text{ is not the total space of a covering of degree at least two}.}
$$

Thus the [complex projective plane has no nontrivial covering quotient](../../../../../../complex-projective-plane-has-no-nontrivial-covering-quotient.md).
