<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) self-map, the [Lefschetz number](../../../../../../lefschetz-number.md) is $L(f)=1+(-1)^n\deg f$. At degree zero this is $1$, so the [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md) supplies $x$ with $f(x)=x$.

Let $A:S^n\to S^n$ be the [antipodal map](../../../../../../antipodal-map.md), $A(z)=-z$, of degree $(-1)^{n+1}$. The composite $Af$ also has degree zero, hence Lefschetz number one, so it has a fixed point $y$. The equation $A(f(y))=y$ is $f(y)=-y$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\exists x,y\in S^n:\ f(x)=x,\quad f(y)=-y.}
$$

The two points are distinct, since a unit vector is never its own negative.
