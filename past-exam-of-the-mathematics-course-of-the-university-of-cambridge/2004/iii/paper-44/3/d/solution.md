<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [Positron](../../../../../../positron.md) is the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md)'s [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md): it has the same [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) one-half, opposite electric charge $+e$, and fermionic statistics. A state $b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}|0\rangle$ has positive [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) $E_{\mathbf p}$ and physical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) $\mathbf p$, as follows from the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) operators

$$
[H,b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}]=E_{\mathbf p}b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger},
\qquad
[\mathbf P,b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}]
=\mathbf p\,b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}.
$$

The charge [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) gives $[Q_{\rm em},b^\dagger]=+e\,b^\dagger$.

The term $b^\dagger v(p)e^{ip\cdot x}$ in the field has negative frequency, but it creates this positive-energy [Positron](../../../../../../positron.md). Treating its coefficient as an annihilator of a physical negative-energy [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) would give an unbounded [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) interpretation. Fermionic quantization instead reorders that sector, giving positive [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) occupation energies and the opposite signed charge. The historical [Dirac sea](../../../../../../dirac-sea.md) describes a [Positron](../../../../../../positron.md) as a hole in filled negative-energy [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) levels; the field-theory interpretation uses an [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) creation operator and [normal ordering](../../../../../../normal-ordering.md), without requiring a literal infinite material sea.

Under [charge conjugation of a Dirac field](../../../../../../charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md), $\psi^C=C\bar\psi^T$, where $C^{-1}\gamma^\mu C=-(\gamma^\mu)^T$, particle and [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) modes interchange. [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md)–[Positron](../../../../../../positron.md) pair creation and annihilation change total particle number while conserving electric charge. **[Positrons](../../../../../../positron.md) are positive-energy [antiparticles](../../../../../../antiparticle.md), not physical negative-energy [Electrons](../../../../../../electron.md).**
