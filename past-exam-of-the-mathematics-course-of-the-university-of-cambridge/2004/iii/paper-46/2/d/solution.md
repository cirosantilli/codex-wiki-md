<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $\mathfrak f(u)$ for the physical free energy per site excluding the identity term, so $F/N=C+\mathfrak f(u)$. Blocking sends $u$ to $u'=R_bu$ and the identity coefficient to $C'=b^DC+c(u)$. Since the total [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is unchanged and $N'=N/b^D$,

$$
C+\mathfrak f(u)=b^{-D}[C'+\mathfrak f(u')],\qquad
\boxed{\mathfrak f(u)=b^{-D}\mathfrak f(u')+g(u),\quad g(u)=b^{-D}c(u).}
$$

The function $g$ records the field-independent energy generated by eliminated modes, including their entropy and normalization. It is the [identity-operator contribution to renormalization-group free energy](../../../../../../identity-operator-contribution-to-renormalization-group-free-energy.md); it must be tracked to recover the complete thermodynamics, even though it does not change normalized spin probabilities.

Iterating the [additive free-energy recursion under blocking](../../../../../../additive-free-energy-recursion-under-blocking.md) gives

$$
\mathfrak f(u_0)=b^{-nD}\mathfrak f(u_n)+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}b^{-jD}g(u_j).
$$

When a regular background $A(u)$ solves $A(u)=b^{-D}A(u')+g(u)$, the singular remainder $f_s=\mathfrak f-A$ satisfies the homogeneous scaling equation $f_s(u)=b^{-D}f_s(u')$. Thus **the full free energy contains an inhomogeneous contribution, while the background-subtracted singular part is homogeneous**. A finite initial identity energy $C_0$ adds a regular normalization, not a new magnetization singularity.
