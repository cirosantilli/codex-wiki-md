<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) distinguishes phases macroscopically. In an [Ising model](../../../../../../ising-model.md) it is the local coarse-grained [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md); the uniform mean is $M=V^{-1}\int\phi(x)d^Dx$. In a selected ordered phase $M\ne0$, whereas the disordered phase has $M=0$ at zero [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md). The ordered signs are related by [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md). A finite zero-field system may average both signs to zero, so [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) is defined using the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) before removing a symmetry-selecting field. For other systems the [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) can be a vector, a density difference, or a tensor rather than a single magnetization.

[Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) makes the [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) a spatial field and expands a local [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) consistent with the symmetries. For a short-range scalar system with spin inversion symmetry at $h=0$, a useful expression is

$$
\mathcal F[\phi]=\int d^Dx\left[\frac\kappa2|\nabla\phi|^2+\frac r2\phi^2+\frac u4\phi^4+\frac v6\phi^6-h\phi\right],\qquad \kappa>0.
$$

The linear source is the [field conjugate to an order parameter](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md). Odd terms at zero field are forbidden by spin inversion symmetry. The coefficients depend smoothly on control parameters away from singular fluctuation effects. With $u>0$ the quartic term suffices locally; if $u<0$, take $v>0$ for stability. The [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md) minimizes this functional; statistical field theory also integrates fluctuations about those minima.
