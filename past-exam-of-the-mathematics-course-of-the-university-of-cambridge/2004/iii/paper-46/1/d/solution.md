↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Critical exponents](../../../../../../critical-exponent.md) describe leading singular powers near a [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md). For [reduced temperature](../../../../../../reduced-temperature.md) $t=(T-T_c)/T_c$, common definitions are the spontaneous [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) $M\sim(-t)^\beta$, [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) $\chi\sim|t|^{-\gamma}$, singular heat capacity $c_s\sim|t|^{-\alpha}$, [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$, and the critical equation of state $M\sim\operatorname{sgn}(h)|h|^{1/\delta}$. At criticality the [connected correlation function](../../../../../../connected-correlation-function.md) has decay power $D-2+\eta$.

In [Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md), solve the stationary equation, evaluate its stable minimum, and differentiate with respect to the conjugate variables. For a scalar quartic theory with $r=r_0t$, $r_0>0$, the equation of state is $h=rM+uM^3$. Its ordered solution and differentiated response give $\beta=1/2$ and $\gamma=1$, calculated explicitly in the unheaded solution below. At $r=0$ the same equation gives $\delta=3$. The minimized zero-field singular potential is zero above the transition and $-r^2/(4u)$ below, giving a heat-capacity jump and $\alpha=0$. Expanding the gradient functional about a stable minimum gives inverse quadratic response $\kappa q^2+U''(M)$, so $\xi=\sqrt{\kappa/U''(M)}\sim|t|^{-1/2}$ and the Gaussian critical decay has $\eta=0$.

These are [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md). [Renormalization group](../../../../../../renormalization-group.md) methods obtain fluctuation-corrected powers from fixed-point scaling dimensions; they need not equal the Landau values below the [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md). At a marginal dimension logarithms can supplement the leading powers.
