<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the requested ordinary critical point take $u>0$, $r=r_0t$, and the quartic [Landau free energy](../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) $U(M)=rM^2/2+uM^4/4-hM$. At $h=0,t<0$, minimization gives $M^2=-r/u$. Choosing the positive [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md),

$$
M=\sqrt{\frac{r_0}{u}}(-t)^{1/2},\qquad \boxed{\beta=\frac12.}
$$

For the [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md), differentiate the equation of state at fixed temperature:

$$
1=(r+3uM^2)\frac{\partial M}{\partial h},\qquad
\chi=\begin{cases}(r_0t)^{-1},&t>0,\\ (2r_0|t|)^{-1},&t<0\text{ on a selected phase}.\end{cases}
$$

Both sides diverge as $|t|^{-1}$, with different amplitudes, so

$$
\boxed{\gamma=1.}
$$

The one-sided field limit below the transition selects a pure phase; differentiating the symmetric finite-volume mixture would instead include its macroscopic switching response.
