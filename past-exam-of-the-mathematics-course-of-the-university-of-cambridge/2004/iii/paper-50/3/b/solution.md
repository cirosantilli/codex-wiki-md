<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The same [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) gives the operator identity

$$
H=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}.
$$

For a normalized physical state $|v\rangle$ in a positive-norm [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md),

$$
\boxed{\langle H\rangle_v=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\big(\|Q_\alpha v\|^2+\|Q_\alpha^\dagger v\|^2\big)\ge0.}
$$

An unbroken supersymmetric vacuum must be annihilated by every [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) and its adjoint, hence has zero energy. Conversely, zero expectation of this positive sum forces each [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) separately to vanish. Therefore a vacuum with **strictly positive energy necessarily breaks [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md)**. For an infinite-volume field theory the corresponding statement is about [vacuum energy density](../../../../../../vacuum-energy-density.md), obtained with an appropriate finite-volume regulator. The zero of energy is fixed by the algebra; it cannot be arbitrarily shifted while preserving this identity. This is the global-supersymmetry result and does not assert positivity of the general local-supergravity scalar potential.
