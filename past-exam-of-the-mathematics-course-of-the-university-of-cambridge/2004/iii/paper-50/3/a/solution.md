<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For global four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), use

$$
\{Q_\alpha,Q_\beta^\dagger\}=2\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}P_\mu,\qquad
\{Q_\alpha,Q_\beta\}=0,\qquad [P_\mu,Q_\alpha]=0.
$$

Let $\Pi=(-1)^F$ be [fermion parity](../../../../../../fermion-parity.md). On a finite one-particle [supermultiplet](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md) at fixed positive-energy momentum, the [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) act within the multiplet and $\Pi Q_\alpha=-Q_\alpha\Pi$. Cyclicity of the finite-dimensional [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\Pi Q_\alpha Q_\alpha^\dagger)
=\operatorname{Tr}(Q_\alpha^\dagger\Pi Q_\alpha)
=-\operatorname{Tr}(\Pi Q_\alpha^\dagger Q_\alpha).
$$

Thus the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) with $\Pi$ of every displayed diagonal [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) vanishes. Summing the two components, $\sum_\alpha\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}=4E\mathbf1$, so

$$
0=4E\operatorname{Tr}\Pi=4E(N_B-N_F).
$$

Since $E>0$, this proves **$N_B=N_F$**, for both massive and massless one-particle multiplets. The restriction matters: a zero-momentum supersymmetric vacuum singlet, annihilated by every [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md), need not contain a fermionic partner. The algebra does not imply equal counts for such a trivial zero-energy representation or for an arbitrary unregulated infinite-dimensional [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md).
