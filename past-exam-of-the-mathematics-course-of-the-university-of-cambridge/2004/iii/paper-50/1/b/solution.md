<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

TeV-scale softly broken [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) addresses the [technical hierarchy problem](../../../../../../technical-hierarchy-problem.md): equal bosonic and fermionic couplings cancel quadratic ultraviolet contributions to the Higgs mass. Soft mass splittings leave corrections of order $\delta m_H^2\sim g^2M_{\mathrm{soft}}^2\log(\Lambda/M_{\mathrm{soft}})/(16\pi^2)$, so a weak-scale soft spectrum protects the weak scale against a much higher cutoff. Unbroken four-dimensional [extended supersymmetry](../../../../../../extended-supersymmetry.md) is incompatible with the observed complex [chiral gauge spectrum](../../../../../../chiral-gauge-spectrum.md): charged [hypermultiplets](../../../../../../hypermultiplet.md) contain conjugate representations, while [vector multiplets](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) have real adjoint matter. It must therefore be broken to at most $\mathcal N=1$ before reproducing low-energy [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) chirality.
