<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Positive-energy one-particle [Poincaré group](../../../../../../poincare-group.md) representations have labels **$(m,s)$** for $m>0$, and **$(0,h)$** for finite-helicity massless states; individual massive polarizations add $m_s=-s,\ldots,s$. An $\mathcal N=1$ [massive supermultiplet](../../../../../../massive-supermultiplet.md) is labelled by mass $m$ and [superspin](../../../../../../superspin.md) $j$, with constituent labels

$$
\boxed{(m,j),\ (m,j),\ (m,j+\tfrac12),\ (m,j-\tfrac12)\quad(j\ge\tfrac12);\qquad (m,0),(m,0),(m,\tfrac12)\quad(j=0).}
$$

A [massless supermultiplet](../../../../../../massless-supermultiplet.md) has constituent labels

$$
\boxed{(0,h),\ (0,h-\tfrac12).}
$$

A [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](../../../../../../cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md) additionally supplies $(0,-h),(0,-h+\tfrac12)$, with conjugated internal charges where applicable. Constituent spins/[helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) are the labels here, not counts of polarizations.
