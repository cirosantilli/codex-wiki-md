<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In a [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md), an internal [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) breaks while [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) can remain unbroken. A massless [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) absorbs a [chiral multiplet](../../../../../../chiral-multiplet.md) and becomes a massive multiplet with spins $(1,\tfrac12,\tfrac12,0)$: the [gauge boson](../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) acquires its longitudinal [particle polarization](../../../../../../particle-polarization.md) from the Goldstone mode. In the [super-Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../super-higgs-mechanism.md), local [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) itself breaks: the spin-$3/2$ [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) absorbs the spin-$1/2$ [goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md), gaining two longitudinal polarizations and becoming massive. With only global [supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../supersymmetry-breaking.md), the [goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md) instead remains a physical massless [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md).
