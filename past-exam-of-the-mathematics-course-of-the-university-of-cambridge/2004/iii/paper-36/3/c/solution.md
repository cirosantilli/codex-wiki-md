<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the same [Kakutani fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../kakutani-fixed-point-theorem.md) pair as in part (b). If any coordinate of $p^*$ vanished, the additional boundary condition would force some coordinate of every member of $z(p^*)$, including $v^*$, to be positive. This contradicts $v^*\leq0$. Hence $p_i^*>0$ for every $i$.

Now [Walras law](../../../../../../walras-s-law.md) reads $\sum_i p_i^*v_i^*=0$, a sum of nonpositive terms with strictly positive coefficients. Every term must vanish, so

$$
\boxed{p^*\in\mathbb R_{++}^L\cap P,\qquad 0\in z(p^*).}
$$

The boundary hypothesis first excludes boundary prices; strict positivity of all prices then turns nonpositive excess demand into exact market clearing.
