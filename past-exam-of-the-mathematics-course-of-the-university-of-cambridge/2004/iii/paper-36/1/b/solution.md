<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [congruence axiom](../../../../../../congruence-axiom.md) requires that an affordable alternative indirectly revealed at least as desirable as a chosen alternative is itself chosen. In the notation of part (a),

$$
\boxed{x\in x(p,w),\quad y\in B(p,w),\quad yRx\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y\in x(p,w).}
$$

Equivalently, a chosen bundle $x$ and an affordable but unchosen bundle $y$ satisfy $\neg(yRx)$. Since $xR_Dy$ already holds, this means $xPy$. The axiom therefore distinguishes a genuine [strict preference](../../../../../../strict-preference.md) for a chosen bundle from indifference between two chosen bundles; it applies to the entire [demand correspondence](../../../../../../demand-correspondence.md), including budgets with several chosen alternatives.
