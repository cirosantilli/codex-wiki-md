<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $B(p,w)=\{z\in\mathbb R^L:p\cdot z\leq w\}$ be the set allowed by the [budget constraint](../../../../../../budget-constraint.md). A [demand correspondence](../../../../../../demand-correspondence.md) is understood to choose only feasible bundles: $x(p,w)\subseteq B(p,w)$. Define the direct [revealed preference](../../../../../../revealed-preference.md) relation by

$$
yR_Dz\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\exists(p,w)\in\mathbb R_{++}^L\times\mathbb R_{++}:\ y\in x(p,w),\ z\in B(p,w).
$$

The [indirect revealed preference](../../../../../../indirect-revealed-preference.md) relation is its reflexive transitive closure. Thus $yRz$ precisely when there is a finite chain $y=y_0,y_1,\ldots,y_m=z$ whose successive comparisons satisfy $y_jR_Dy_{j+1}$. **Chains of length zero are included.** This makes $R$ a [preorder](../../../../../../preorder.md) on all of $\mathbb R^L$, including alternatives that are never chosen. The associated [strict preference](../../../../../../strict-preference.md) is

$$
yPz\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad yRz\ \text{and}\ \neg(zRy).
$$

If a convention defines indirect revelation using only positive-length chains, one must add the diagonal before the intersection assertion in part (c) can hold: every complete weak [preference relation](../../../../../../preference-relation.md) contains the diagonal.
