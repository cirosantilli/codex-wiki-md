<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**For the printed [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md), yes.** The surfaces $T=\text{constant}$, equivalently $t=\text{constant}$, are [Cauchy surfaces](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) in [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md). Every inextendible causal curve intersects each once; if its monotone time coordinate had a finite endpoint, the bound $|dx/dT|\leq1$ would give a finite spatial endpoint too, allowing continuation. Causal diamonds are closed and bounded in $(T,x)$ and hence compact. Therefore the printed [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md) is [globally hyperbolic](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md).

Initial data on a Cauchy slice determine unique causal evolution for the usual well-posed hyperbolic field equations, without boundary data at spatial infinity at finite time. The time reparametrization preserves that property.

**For the qualified AdS interpretation, no.** On the [universal cover](../../../../../../universal-cover.md), choose two central events with enough time separation for a null ray to approach a strip edge and return. Their causal diamond contains a sequence with $\rho\to\pi/2$ but no limit in the physical [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md), so it is noncompact. A constant-$t$ surface fails to meet every inextendible causal curve arriving from timelike conformal infinity. Thus even the cover is not [globally hyperbolic](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md). Field evolution needs appropriate boundary conditions, such as a reflecting condition compatible with the field theory, as well as initial data. If AdS time itself is periodically identified, [closed timelike curves](../../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md) give an additional and more immediate obstruction. These are consequences of the alternative spatial-lapse geometry, not of the printed flat [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md).
