<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a homogeneous canonical massless [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md), the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) and field equation in [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) are

$$
3\mathcal H^2=\frac12\phi_0'^2,
\qquad\phi_0''+2\mathcal H\phi_0'=0,
\qquad\mathcal H=a'/a.
$$

Put $C=\sqrt{3/2}$. On the negative-time branch, $\mathcal H=1/(2\tau)$, $\phi_0'=C/\tau$ and $\phi_0''=-C/\tau^2$. Therefore

$$
3\mathcal H^2=\frac3{4\tau^2}=\frac12\phi_0'^2,
\qquad -\frac C{\tau^2}+\frac C{\tau^2}=0.
$$

Both equations are satisfied. Since $a'<0$, this is [cosmological contraction with a free homogeneous scalar](../../../../../../cosmological-contraction-with-a-free-homogeneous-scalar.md). Its energy density and pressure are equal, so it is a [stiff fluid](../../../../../../stiff-fluid.md), and the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) tends to zero as $\tau\uparrow0$.
