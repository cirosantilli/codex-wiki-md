<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Each compact [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) obeys the noncompact equation with dispersion relation $\omega^2=\mathbf k^2+m_n^2$, where

$$
\boxed{m_n=\frac{2\pi|n|}R\quad(\hbar=c=1).}
$$

Thus momentum in the extra dimension appears as rest mass to a four-dimensional observer. There is one massless zero mode and equally massive $n,-n$ modes. For a real scalar they can be organized into sine and cosine components rather than independent complex fields.

At equal compact positions all phase factors are one, and the propagator becomes the [Kaluza-Klein tower](../../../../../../kaluza-klein-tower.md)

$$
\boxed{G_{\mathrm{ret}}(\Delta w=0)=\frac1R\left[
G_{\mathrm{ret}}^{(4,0)}+2\sum_{n=1}^\infty
G_{\mathrm{ret}}^{(4,m_n)}\right].}
$$

The factor $1/R$ follows from the normalized compact eigenfunctions. It cannot be discarded if the five-dimensional field and source normalizations are retained.
