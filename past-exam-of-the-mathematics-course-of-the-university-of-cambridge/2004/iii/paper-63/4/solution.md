<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A $5M_\odot$ star of roughly solar [stellar composition](../../../../../stellar-chemical-abundance.md) begins on the [zero-age main sequence](../../../../../zero-age-main-sequence.md) with a hot [convective core](../../../../../convective-core.md), a mostly [radiative envelope](../../../../../radiative-envelope.md), and [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) dominated by the [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md). The strong temperature dependence of the [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) concentrates the [stellar energy-generation rate](../../../../../stellar-energy-generation-rate.md) near the centre, helping produce the [convective core](../../../../../convective-core.md). Core mixing replenishes [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) within that region; it does not homogenize the entire star. The nuclear fuel supply gives a [stellar nuclear timescale](../../../../../stellar-nuclear-timescale.md) of order $10^8$ years. For a numerical comparison, Table 2 of [Ekström et al.'s solar-metallicity models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5049) gives core [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) lifetimes of about $88$ and $109$ million years for nonrotating and rotating $5M_\odot$ models, respectively.

After central [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) exhaustion, an inert [helium](../../../../../helium.md) core contracts and heats through [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md), while a [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) supplies the growing [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md). The envelope expands and cools: the track crosses the [Hertzsprung gap](../../../../../hertzsprung-gap.md) toward the [red giant](../../../../../red-giant.md) region. Its deepening [convective envelope](../../../../../convective-envelope.md) causes [first dredge-up](../../../../../first-dredge-up.md), bringing material altered by [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) to the surface. The rapid structural crossing has a thermal timescale, roughly $10^5$–$10^6$ years for a star of this [mass](../../../../../mass.md), rather than the full [stellar nuclear timescale](../../../../../stellar-nuclear-timescale.md); an estimate follows from the [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md) $GM^2/(RL)$ with the evolving [radius](../../../../../radius.md) and [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md).

The [helium](../../../../../helium.md) core reaches ignition before becoming strongly supported by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md). Thus a normal $5M_\odot$ star starts [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md) without the [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md) characteristic of lower-mass stars. The [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) produces [carbon-12](../../../../../carbon-12.md), and [carbon-12 alpha capture](../../../../../carbon-12-alpha-capture.md) produces [oxygen-16](../../../../../oxygen-16.md); these reactions build a [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md). There is a [convective core](../../../../../convective-core.md) and continued [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) activity. Core [helium](../../../../../helium.md) burning lasts order $10^7$ years: the same [model table](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5049) gives approximately $19$ and $18$ million years. The changing core and envelope structure can produce a [blue loop](../../../../../blue-loop.md) in the [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md); its extent depends on [stellar composition](../../../../../stellar-chemical-abundance.md), mixing and [stellar rotation](../../../../../stellar-rotation.md).

After central [helium](../../../../../helium.md) exhaustion, the [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md) contracts and becomes increasingly supported by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md). A [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) forms above it, beneath the [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md). The star returns to a cool, luminous [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) configuration. On this early branch, [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) activity is an important energy source; the overlying [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) can weaken during readjustment and resume later. At this [mass](../../../../../mass.md), a deepening [convective envelope](../../../../../convective-envelope.md) can cause [second dredge-up](../../../../../second-dredge-up.md). This shell-supported evolution to the first [AGB thermal pulse](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md) takes order $10^6$ years, with model-dependent duration. As the [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) becomes sufficiently thin, the [thin-shell instability](../../../../../thin-shell-instability.md) ends steady shell burning and marks the onset of [AGB thermal pulses](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md).

**[Figure 1](#4/image-schematic-evolution-of-a-five-solar-mass-star-to-the-first-agb-thermal-pulse). Schematic evolution of a five-solar-mass star to the first AGB thermal pulse**.

The original sketch shows the direction of [stellar evolution](../../../../../stellar-evolution.md), with higher [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) to the left. It illustrates a possible [blue loop](../../../../../blue-loop.md); its coordinates and loop shape are schematic, rather than a computed stellar model. The stages before the first [AGB thermal pulse](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md) are the requested track; the later remnant evolution is described below.

In the usual final evolution, [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) and an [AGB superwind](../../../../../agb-superwind.md) remove the envelope before the [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md) can ignite substantial [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md). Once shell fuel is exhausted, the exposed core contracts and heats, potentially ionizing the expelled gas as a [planetary nebula](../../../../../planetary-nebula.md), and finally becomes a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md) that loses its stored thermal [energy](../../../../../energy.md) by cooling. The relevant competition is between the [AGB envelope-loss and core-growth timescales](../../../../../agb-envelope-loss-and-core-growth-timescales.md), not just the total initial [mass](../../../../../mass.md).

Much higher late-stage [mass loss](../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md) strips the envelope sooner, shortens the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) phase, and allows less core growth, usually producing a smaller [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md). Much lower [mass loss](../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md) prolongs shell burning and permits greater core growth. In an extreme hypothetical case, retention of the envelope could let a degenerate [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md) approach the [Chandrasekhar mass](../../../../../chandrasekhar-limit.md) and undergo explosive [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md): the proposed [type 1.5 supernova](../../../../../type-1-5-supernova.md) channel. This is not an inevitable fate for a $5M_\odot$ star. [Doherty et al.'s evolutionary calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5431) emphasize the uncertainty of that channel and find that their adopted [mass loss](../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md) removes carbon-oxygen envelopes before such ignition. If [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md) instead proceeds without disruption, the possible further evolution depends on the resulting core and continued envelope retention; lowering [mass loss](../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md) alone does not establish an [electron-capture supernova](../../../../../electron-capture-supernova.md) outcome.
