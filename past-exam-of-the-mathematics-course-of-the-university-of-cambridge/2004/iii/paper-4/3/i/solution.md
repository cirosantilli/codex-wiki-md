<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Initially $G_1=G$ is [powerfully embedded](../../../../../../powerfully-embedded-subgroup.md) in itself. If $G_i$ is [powerfully embedded](../../../../../../powerfully-embedded-subgroup.md) in $G$, then $[G_i,G]\subseteq G_i^p$ and consequently $G_{i+1}=G_i^p$. The preliminary result says that $G_i^p$ is again [powerfully embedded](../../../../../../powerfully-embedded-subgroup.md) in $G$, completing the induction.

For a finite [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) $A$, the [Frattini subgroup](../../../../../../frattini-subgroup.md) is $\Phi(A)=A^p[A,A]$. Here $[G_i,G_i]\subseteq[G_i,G]\subseteq G_i^p$, so

$$
\boxed{G_{i+1}=G_i^p=\Phi(G_i),\qquad [G_i,G]\subseteq G_{i+1}.}
$$

Since the [Frattini subgroup](../../../../../../frattini-subgroup.md) of a nontrivial finite [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) is proper, this series eventually reaches $1$. Thus $P_j=G^{p^j}$, with iterated power subgroups, terminates.
