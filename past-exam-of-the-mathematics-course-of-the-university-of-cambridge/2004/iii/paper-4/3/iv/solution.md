<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply (iii) successively to the [powerful p-groups](../../../../../../powerful-p-group.md) $G_i$. Starting with $G_1=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_n\rangle$, if $G_i=\langle a_1^{p^{i-1}},\ldots,a_n^{p^{i-1}}\rangle$, then

$$
G_{i+1}=G_i^p=\langle(a_1^{p^{i-1}})^p,\ldots,(a_n^{p^{i-1}})^p\rangle.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{G_{i+1}=\langle a_1^{p^i},\ldots,a_n^{p^i}\rangle\quad(i\geq1).}
$$
