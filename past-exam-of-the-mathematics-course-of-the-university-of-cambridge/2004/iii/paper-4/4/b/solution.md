↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the faithful logarithm realization from the root Solution: $\log:G\to A$ identifies $L_G$ with the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) $\log G$. For $x,y\in G$, the convergent exponential identity in $A$ gives

$$
\operatorname{BCH}(\log x,\log y)=\log(\exp(\log x)\exp(\log y))=\log(xy).
$$

Every [Lie polynomial](../../../../../../lie-polynomial.md) and its convergent sum are preserved by this realization of $L_G$. Therefore the BCH product on the underlying set of $L_G$ has exactly the same logarithm as the original product $xy$. Injectivity of the logarithm proves equality of the two products, with the same topology, identity and inverses:

$$
\boxed{(L_G,*)=G.}
$$

This proves the reverse direction of the [uniform pro-p group and powerful Lie lattice correspondence](../../../../../../uniform-pro-p-group-and-powerful-lie-lattice-correspondence.md) directly; the forward identity alone would not imply it.
