<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [uniform pro-p group](../../../../../../uniform-pro-p-group.md) $(L,*)$, the $p^n$-th power of $X$ is exactly $p^nX$, and its unique $p^n$-th root in the appropriate layer is division by $p^n$. The intrinsic addition is therefore

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}p^{-n}\operatorname{BCH}(p^nX,p^nY)=X+Y.
$$

The linear term is exact; every non-linear term tends to zero by the convergence estimates above. The intrinsic [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) is

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}p^{-2n}\log\big(\exp(-p^nX)\exp(-p^nY)\exp(p^nX)\exp(p^nY)\big)=[X,Y],
$$

since the leading term is $p^{2n}[X,Y]$ and the terms of degree at least three vanish after rescaling. Finally the intrinsic scalar operation sends $(\lambda,X)$ to $\lambda X$. All underlying sets are identical, and all three operations agree. Thus

$$
\boxed{L_{(L,*)}=L.}
$$
