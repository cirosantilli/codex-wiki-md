<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an [open subgroup](../../../../../../open-subgroup.md) $H$ of a [profinite group](../../../../../../profinite-group.md) $G$, the open left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) form a disjoint cover of a [compact space](../../../../../../compact-space.md). A finite subcover must include every coset, so $[G:H]$ is finite. Choose representatives $t_1,\ldots,t_m$ for these [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md). The [normal core](../../../../../../core-group-theory.md) is

$$
N=\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}=\bigcap_{j=1}^m t_jHt_j^{-1}.
$$

Each conjugate is open, and the intersection is finite, so $N$ is an [open normal subgroup](../../../../../../open-normal-subgroup.md) contained in $H$. **$[G:H]<\infty$ and $H$ contains an open normal subgroup of $G$.**
