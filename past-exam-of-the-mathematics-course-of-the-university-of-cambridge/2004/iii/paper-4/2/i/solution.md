<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $H$ is an [open subgroup](../../../../../../open-subgroup.md), each left [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) $gH$ is open because translation is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). Its complement is the union of the other [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md), hence is open. **Every open subgroup is closed.** No [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) is needed.
