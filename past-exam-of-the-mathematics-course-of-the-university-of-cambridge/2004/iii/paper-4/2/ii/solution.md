<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $x,y\in\overline H$ and let $W$ be an open neighbourhood of $xy^{-1}$. Continuity of $(u,v)\mapsto uv^{-1}$ supplies neighbourhoods $U$ of $x$ and $V$ of $y$ with $UV^{-1}\subseteq W$. Choose $h\in H\cap U$ and $k\in H\cap V$. Then $hk^{-1}\in H\cap W$, so $xy^{-1}\in\overline H$. The [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) criterion using $xy^{-1}$ proves **the closure of a subgroup is a subgroup**. The neighbourhood argument avoids assuming the topology is determined by sequences.
