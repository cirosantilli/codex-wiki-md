<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The undisturbed reservoir remains at $h=H$, $u=0$, while the particle budget gives $\phi=\phi_0e^{-V_st/H}$. The edge of the [rarefaction wave](../../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md) advancing into that reservoir travels with the local left-running [characteristic speed](../../../../../../characteristic-speed.md), $-\sqrt{b_0H}\,e^{-V_st/(2H)}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x_r(t)=-\frac{2Hc_0}{V_s}\left[1-e^{-V_st/(2H)}\right]}.
$$

This is the disturbance head into the initially resting reservoir, also called the leading edge of the [rarefaction wave](../../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md); it reduces to $-c_0t$ as $V_s\to0$. The rightmost fan/shelf junction is the rarefaction tail, not this reservoir edge. Its trajectory in the full sedimenting system cannot be obtained by retaining the constant-buoyancy [Riemann invariants](../../../../../../riemann-invariant.md).

For the requested near-front approximation, hold $h_f=4H/(F+2)^2$ fixed and take the shelf's concentration initially to be $\phi_0$. The locally uniform shelf approximation gives $b_f=b_0e^{-V_st/h_f}$, so

$$
\dot x_f=F\sqrt{b_0h_f}\,e^{-V_st/(2h_f)},\qquad \boxed{x_f(t)\simeq\frac{2Fh_f\sqrt{b_0h_f}}{V_s}\left[1-e^{-V_st/(2h_f)}\right]}.
$$

This tends to $u_ft$ at small settling and predicts a finite limiting front location within the fixed-depth closure. It is not an exact solution of the spatially varying [variable-buoyancy shallow water equations](../../../../../../variable-buoyancy-shallow-water-equations.md): different material histories and resulting buoyancy gradients eventually invalidate a perfectly uniform shelf. No exact finite-time stoppage is claimed.
