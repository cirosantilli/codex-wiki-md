<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Close the unresolved head of the [gravity current](../../../../../../gravity-current.md) by

$$
\boxed{u_f=\dot x_f=F\sqrt{b_fh_f}}.
$$

Here the speed is measured relative to the resting deep ambient, and $h_f,b_f$ are the depth and [reduced gravity](../../../../../../reduced-gravity-split.md) immediately behind the head. The [Froude number](../../../../../../froude-number.md) $F$ expresses conversion of the head's [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) head into motion and depends on the ambient-depth ratio and head physics.

A front condition can be motivated by a steady control volume in the front frame: balance [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md), [momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) and the upstream/downstream [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md), with a stagnation region and the appropriate ambient return flow. Assumptions include small density contrast, approximately hydrostatic far fields, negligible imposed ambient current, specified far-field depths, and a prescription for any mixing or energy loss. Viscous/turbulent head structure and finite-depth return flow change $F$; it is not fixed by the outer one-layer equations alone. A deep-ambient ideal head often motivates a constant order-one value, but no numerical choice is needed here.

The [shallow-water approximation](../../../../../../shallow-water-approximation.md) fails locally in the head because its vertical and horizontal dimensions are comparable and vertical accelerations are appreciable. The [Froude number](../../../../../../froude-number.md) closure is a boundary condition for the outer [shallow water equations](../../../../../../shallow-water-equations.md), not a claim that they resolve the front itself.
