<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Vertical [turbulence](../../../../../../turbulence-split.md) carries particles upward as well as downward. Its mixing time $h/u'$ should be short relative to the settling time $h/V_s$; hence $u'\gg V_s$ is a useful well-mixed criterion, subject also to the eddy correlation time and particle response time. A more resolved description balances [turbulent diffusion](../../../../../../eddy-diffusion.md) against downward [settling velocity](../../../../../../settling-velocity.md). A nearly uniform interior concentration does not eliminate the downward drift.

At an absorbing bed, particles crossing the boundary settle and do not return unless [sediment resuspension](../../../../../../sediment-resuspension.md) is separately supplied. With well-mixed concentration $\phi$, the particle volume [deposition flux](../../../../../../particle-deposition-flux.md) is $V_s\phi$ per unit horizontal area. Thus stirring can preserve an almost uniform suspension while its total particle content decreases. The present model neglects [sediment resuspension](../../../../../../sediment-resuspension.md) and any significant change of bulk fluid volume due to the dilute particles.
