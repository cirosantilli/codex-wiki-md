<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [stable density stratification](../../../../../../stable-density-stratification.md), ambient density decreases with height. A rising [buoyant thermal](../../../../../../buoyant-thermal.md) encounters progressively lighter surroundings and entrains denser fluid from below, so its net [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) decreases. It reaches neutral buoyancy, overshoots through its remaining [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md), and eventually stops rising, spreads as a [buoyant intrusion](../../../../../../buoyant-intrusion.md) or excites [internal gravity waves](../../../../../../internal-wave.md). The finite rise is a consequence of this restoring effect, not just drag.

Total buoyancy has dimensions $[B_0]=L^4T^{-2}$ and [buoyancy frequency](../../../../../../buoyancy-frequency.md) has $[N]=T^{-1}$. For an ideal point release with no other dimensional source length or time, [dimensional analysis](../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{z_{\max}=C_z\left(\frac{B_0}{N^2}\right)^{1/4}}.
$$

The dimensionless coefficient depends on [entrainment coefficient](../../../../../../entrainment-coefficient.md), shape and the momentum model. A finite source radius or impulse supplies additional dimensionless parameters; dimensional analysis cannot determine the coefficient by itself.

Within precisely the spherical model, $b=\alpha z$ and the evolving thermal buoyancy satisfies $dB/dz=-N^2V=-KN^2z^3$, so $B(z)=B_0-KN^2z^4/4$. Neutrality occurs at $z_n^4=4B_0/(KN^2)$. For $P=Vu$, the momentum equation and $\dot z=P/V$ give

$$
\frac{dP^2}{dz}=2VB,\qquad P^2=\frac{KB_0z^4}{2}-\frac{K^2N^2z^8}{16}.
$$

The first nonzero-height stopping point is $z_{\max}^4=8B_0/(KN^2)$, so $z_{\max}=2^{1/4}z_n$. This illustrates both the dimensional scale and the distinction between neutral height and maximum rise; the precise coefficients rely on the specified ideal [fluid entrainment](../../../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) and impulse model.
