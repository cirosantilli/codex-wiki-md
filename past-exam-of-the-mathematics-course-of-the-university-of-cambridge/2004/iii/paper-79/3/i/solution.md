<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\omega=u+iv$, with $v>0$, and a chosen square root $k_\beta=a+ib$. Squaring gives

$$
a^2-b^2=\frac{u^2-v^2}{\beta^2}-k^2,\qquad ab=\frac{uv}{\beta^2}.
$$

One cannot have $b=0$: the second relation would force $u=0$, and then the first would require the square of a real number to be negative. Consequently

$$
\operatorname{Re}p_\beta
=\operatorname{Re}\frac{a+ib}{u+iv}
=\frac{au+bv}{|\omega|^2}
=\frac{v}{|\omega|^2}\left(b+\frac{u^2}{\beta^2b}\right).
$$

The bracket has the sign of $b$, proving the [causal vertical wavenumber branch](../../../../../../causal-vertical-wavenumber-branch.md) equivalence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}k_\beta>0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\operatorname{Re}p_\beta>0.}
$$

This selects the square root analytically in the upper [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) half-plane. At $\omega=iv$ it is $k_\beta=i\sqrt{v^2/\beta^2+k^2}$; continuation supplies the outgoing branch for other [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md). The expression $\sqrt{\beta^{-2}-p^2}$ must use this inherited branch rather than an independently chosen square root. For positive real [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) its limit is positive real for a propagating wave and positive imaginary for an evanescent wave.
