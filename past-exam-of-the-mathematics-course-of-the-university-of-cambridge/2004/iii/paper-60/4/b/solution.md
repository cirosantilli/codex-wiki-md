<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let a scalar [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) have magnitude $r>0$. If $z=-r<0$, then $x$ and $y$ have the same sign; the two lifts are **the [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) $(r,1)$ and $(-r,-1)$**. If $z=r>0$, their signs are opposite, and the lift is **the [period-two orbit](../../../../../../period-two-orbit.md) $(r,-1)\leftrightarrow(-r,1)$**. The scalar [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is

$$
m=A\delta r^{\delta-1}>0.
$$

The two-return [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) of the period-two lift is $m^2$, so in either case [dynamical stability](../../../../../../stability-theory.md) requires $m<1$. The negative [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) represents one of two mirror-related single-lobe [periodic orbits](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) in the flow, whereas the positive [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) represents a double-lobe [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md).

For $\delta>1$, the nearby [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) is $z=\mu+o(\mu)$ and its [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) tends to zero. Thus **two stable single-lobe [limit cycles](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) for $\mu<0$ glue into one stable double-lobe [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) for $\mu>0$**. Their periods diverge as the [separatrix](../../../../../../separatrix.md) is approached; $\mu=0,z=0$ is not a finite-period [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md).

For $0<\delta<1$, the small branch instead satisfies $z\sim-\operatorname{sgn}(\mu)(|\mu|/A)^{1/\delta}$ and its [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) tends to infinity. Hence **one unstable double-lobe [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) on $\mu<0$ splits into two unstable single-lobe [limit cycles](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) on $\mu>0$**. Additional finite-size stable branches depend on the surrounding map; near the resonance $\delta=1$ with $A<1$ they are resolved by the [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) in part (c).
