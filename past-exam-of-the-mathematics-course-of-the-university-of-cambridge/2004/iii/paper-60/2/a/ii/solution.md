<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $h=\mu^2+\varepsilon$. The entire unfolded diagram is obtained from $g(x)=h$, with the same [dynamical stability](../../../../../../../stability-theory.md) sign $-x(x+2)$ as before. A [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $x=0$ requires $h=0$, while a [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $x=-2$ requires $h=4/3$.

For $0<\varepsilon<4/3$, **the crossing separates into a positive stable branch and a negative unstable branch**, with no [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) near $\mu=0$. Three [equilibria](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) exist for $|\mu|<\sqrt{4/3-\varepsilon}$, and the two negative branches annihilate at the outer [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) $\mu=\pm\sqrt{4/3-\varepsilon}$. Outside that interval only the positive stable [equilibrium](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) remains. If $\varepsilon>4/3$, the diagram has just that one branch everywhere; equality is the limiting [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) contact at $\mu=0,x=-2$.

For $\varepsilon<0$, let $d=-\varepsilon>0$. **Two inner [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) occur at $\mu=\pm\sqrt d,x=0$**, and two outer [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $\mu=\pm\sqrt{4/3+d},x=-2$. For $|\mu|<\sqrt d$ there is a single stable [equilibrium](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) below $-3$; for $\sqrt d<|\mu|<\sqrt{4/3+d}$ there are three [equilibria](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) with stable–unstable–stable ordering; outside the outer [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) only the positive stable [equilibrium](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) remains. The preceding figure shows representative small unfoldings. In particular this unrestricted perturbation destroys the exact [transcritical bifurcation](../../../../../../../transcritical-bifurcation.md) crossing.
