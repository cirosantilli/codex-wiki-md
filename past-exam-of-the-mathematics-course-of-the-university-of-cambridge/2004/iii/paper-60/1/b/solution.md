<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose $f$ is sufficiently smooth and [equivariant](../../../../../../equivariant-map.md), $f(0,\mu)=0$, and the [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) on the critical space is [absolutely irreducible](../../../../../../absolutely-irreducible-real-representation.md). Its [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) is then $D_xf(0,\mu)=a(\mu)I$. Assume $a(0)=0$ and $a'(0)\ne0$. The [equivariant branching lemma](../../../../../../equivariant-branching-lemma.md) states that **each isotropy [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ with $\dim\operatorname{Fix}(H)=1$ has a local nontrivial branch of [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) with that isotropy**, apart from the common origin. Conjugate isotropy [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) give symmetry-related branches, so branch types are counted up to conjugacy.

To see the scalar mechanism, write the [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) as $\mathbb Re$ and restrict $f(se,\mu)=e\,h(s,\mu)$. [Equivariance](../../../../../../equivariant-map.md) makes the [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) invariant. Since $h(0,\mu)=0$, write $h=s\,k(s,\mu)$; $k(0,0)=0$ and $k_\mu(0,0)=a'(0)\ne0$. The [implicit function theorem](../../../../../../implicit-function-theorem.md) solves $k(s,\mu(s))=0$ near zero. For small nonzero $s$, the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is exactly that of $e$. A [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md) element reversing $e$ makes $h$ odd and $\mu(s)$ even; the generic branch is then a [pitchfork bifurcation](../../../../../../pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md). The lemma ensures existence, not [dynamical stability](../../../../../../stability-theory.md), and does not exclude further branches of smaller isotropy.
