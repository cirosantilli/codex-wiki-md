↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a vector $x$, its [isotropy group](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md)

$$
\boxed{G_x=\{g\in G:R_G(g)x=x\}.}
$$

For a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H\le G$, its [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Fix}(H)=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:R_G(h)x=x\text{ for every }h\in H\}.}
$$

It is a [linear subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md), the intersection of the [kernels](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $R_G(h)-I$. The [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) fixes a particular vector pointwise; the [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) consists of all vectors it fixes, and need not be one-dimensional.
