<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose three representative vectors, with nonzero coordinate scale suppressed. Their [stabilizer subgroups](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) and [fixed-point subspaces of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{vector}&\text{stabilizer}&\text{order}&\text{fixed space}\\\hline
(1,0,0)&\langle r_x,\kappa_y,\kappa_z\rangle\cong D_4&8&\mathbb R(1,0,0)\\
(1,1,0)&\langle\kappa_z,\kappa_xr_z\rangle\cong C_2\times C_2&4&\mathbb R(1,1,0)\\
(1,1,1)&\langle\kappa_xr_z,\kappa_yr_x\rangle\cong S_3&6&\mathbb R(1,1,1).
\end{array}
$$

Here $\kappa_xr_z$ exchanges the first two coordinates and $\kappa_yr_x$ exchanges the last two. On the first line the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is every signed permutation of the two zero coordinates; fixing it forces those coordinates to vanish. On the second line it exchanges the equal coordinates and independently reflects the zero coordinate, forcing $x=y,z=0$. On the third line all coordinate permutations fix the vector, forcing $x=y=z$.

The three [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) have different orders and hence cannot be conjugate. Their [group orbits](../../../../../../orbit-of-a-group-action.md) contain respectively six face-axis, twelve edge-axis and eight vertex-axis directions. Each [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) is one-dimensional, so the [equivariant branching lemma](../../../../../../equivariant-branching-lemma.md) guarantees **three distinct symmetry types of bifurcating [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md)**. The [axial isotropy types of the full cube symmetry group](../../../../../../axial-isotropy-types-of-the-full-cube-symmetry-group.md) are precisely these: a vector with several distinct nonzero absolute coordinate values has a separate freely variable coordinate block for each such value in the [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) of its [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md), giving dimension greater than one.
