<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Conjugating the coordinate [reflection](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) by the rotations supplies [reflections](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) in the other two coordinates. The quarter-turns, together with those [reflections](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md), also supply coordinate transpositions. Consequently the represented [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) consists of all signed permutation [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md), the full [symmetry group of a cube](../../../../../../symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) of order $2^3\cdot3!=48$.

Let $C$ be any real or complex [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) commuting with the [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md). Commutation with the three independent coordinate [reflections](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) forces all off-diagonal entries of $C$ to vanish. Commutation with the quarter-turn about the first axis equates its second and third diagonal entries, and the quarter-turn about the second axis equates its first and third entries. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{End}_\Gamma(\mathbb C^3)=\mathbb C I.}
$$

For completeness, if the complexified [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) had a proper invariant subspace, its [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) would be invariant too, since the generating [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) are unitary. The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto that subspace would be a nonscalar commuting [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), contradicting the calculation. Thus the complexification is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md), proving **[absolute irreducibility](../../../../../../absolute-irreducibility-of-a-group-representation.md)** of the real [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md).
