<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [expected return](../../../../../../expected-return.md) of the [market portfolio](../../../../../../market-portfolio.md) is the weighted mean of the asset returns:

$$
\boxed{m_M=\frac{10\cdot13+17\cdot13+24\cdot12}{38}=\frac{639}{38}.}
$$

For its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md), use $\Sigma z=d$ rather than expanding nine terms:

$$
z^T\Sigma z=z^Td=\frac{7\cdot13+14\cdot13+21\cdot12}{11}=\frac{525}{11},\qquad
\sigma_M^2=\frac{525/11}{(38/11)^2}=\frac{5775}{38^2}.
$$

Hence its return [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sigma_M=\frac{5\sqrt{231}}{38}.}
$$

Means and [standard deviations](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) here are both measured in percentage points, consistently with the given [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md).
