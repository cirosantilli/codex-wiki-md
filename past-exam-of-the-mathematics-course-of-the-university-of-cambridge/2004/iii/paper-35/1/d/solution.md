<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The risky weights of the normalized [market portfolio](../../../../../../market-portfolio.md) must sum to one. Since $\mathbf1^Tz=38/11>0$, divide each component by this sum:

$$
\boxed{w_M=\left(\frac{13}{38},\frac{13}{38},\frac{12}{38}\right)^T.}
$$

Every weight is positive in this case, although the optimization permitted [short selling](../../../../../../short-finance.md).
