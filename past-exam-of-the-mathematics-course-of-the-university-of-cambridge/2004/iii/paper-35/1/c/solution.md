<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Direct substitution into the three equations gives

$$
\frac{4\cdot13+13+12}{11}=7,\qquad
\frac{13+9\cdot13+2\cdot12}{11}=14,\qquad
\frac{13+2\cdot13+16\cdot12}{11}=21.
$$

Since the [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) is invertible, this identifies its unique solution:

$$
\boxed{z=\frac1{11}(13,13,12)^T.}
$$

The original PDF has all three components; the converted TeX's damaged binomial notation should not be used.
