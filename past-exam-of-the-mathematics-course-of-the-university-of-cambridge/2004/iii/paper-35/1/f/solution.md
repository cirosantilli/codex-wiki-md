<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The excess [expected return](../../../../../../expected-return.md) is $639/38-3=525/38$. Dividing it by the return [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) yields the [Sharpe ratio](../../../../../../sharpe-ratio.md) and upper [capital market line](../../../../../../capital-market-line.md) slope:

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac{525/38}{5\sqrt{231}/38}=\frac{105}{\sqrt{231}}=\sqrt{\frac{525}{11}}.}
$$
