<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Constant [relative risk aversion coefficient](../../../../../../relative-risk-aversion-coefficient.md) means that $\alpha=-WU''(W)/U'(W)$ does not depend on wealth, for $W>0$ and $U'(W)>0$. The defining differential equation integrates as

$$
\frac{d}{dW}\log U'(W)=-\frac{\alpha}{W},\qquad U'(W)=cW^{-\alpha},\quad c>0.
$$

A second integration therefore gives

$$
\boxed{U(W)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\frac{cW^{1-\alpha}}{1-\alpha}+d,&\alpha\ne1,\\ c\log W+d,&\alpha=1.\end{cases}}
$$

The additive constant and positive scale do not affect [expected utility](../../../../../../expected-utility.md) preferences. Since $U''(W)=-\alpha cW^{-\alpha-1}$, positive $\alpha$ gives [risk aversion](../../../../../../risk-aversion.md), zero gives [risk neutrality](../../../../../../risk-neutrality.md), and negative $\alpha$ gives [risk seeking](../../../../../../risk-seeking.md). These are respectively concave, affine and convex [utility functions](../../../../../../utility-function-split.md). The usual [CRRA utility](../../../../../../constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md) class is the positive-$\alpha$ case.
