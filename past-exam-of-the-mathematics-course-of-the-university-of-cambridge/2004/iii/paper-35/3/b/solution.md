<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the [tower property of conditional expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) and the defining [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) identity,

$$
\mathbb E[M_n]=\mathbb E\bigl[\mathbb E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]\bigr]=\mathbb E[M_{n-1}].
$$

Iteration gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[M_n]=\mathbb E[M_0]\quad(0\leq n\leq N).}
$$

The integrability in the definition ensures these expectations exist.
