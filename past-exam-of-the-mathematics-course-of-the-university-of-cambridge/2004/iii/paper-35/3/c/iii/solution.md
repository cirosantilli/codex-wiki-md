<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Assume the [bank account](../../../../../../../bank-account.md) stays positive and write $\widetilde S_n=S_n/B_n$ and $\widetilde V_n=V_n/B_n$. Dividing the time-$n$ rebalancing equality by $B_n$ gives

$$
\widetilde V_n=\phi_n\widetilde S_n+\psi_n=\phi_{n+1}\widetilde S_n+\psi_{n+1}.
$$

The discounted [bank account](../../../../../../../bank-account.md) has constant price one. This is exactly the [self-financing](../../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) equality in the discounted market. Equivalently, subtract the corresponding valuation at $n-1$ to obtain the [discounted wealth equation in discrete time](../../../../../../../discounted-wealth-equation-in-discrete-time.md)

$$
\boxed{\widetilde V_n-\widetilde V_{n-1}=\phi_n(\widetilde S_n-\widetilde S_{n-1}).}
$$

This algebra uses only positivity of $B_n$, and does not require it to be deterministic.
