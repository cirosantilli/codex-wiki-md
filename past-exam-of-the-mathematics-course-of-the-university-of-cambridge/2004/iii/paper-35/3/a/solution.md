<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A process $M_n$ is a $\mathbb P$-[martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) relative to the [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_n)$ when it is adapted, each $M_n$ is integrable, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}[M_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=M_{n-1},\qquad 1\leq n\leq N.}
$$

Here adapted means $M_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable, and integrable means $\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}|M_n|<\infty$. Thus its present value is the conditional mean of its next value given all present information.
