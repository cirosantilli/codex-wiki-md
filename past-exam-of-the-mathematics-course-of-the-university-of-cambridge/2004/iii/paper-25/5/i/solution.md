<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [existence theorem of global class field theory](../../../../../../existence-theorem-of-global-class-field-theory.md), together with reciprocity, gives an inclusion-reversing bijection between finite [abelian extensions](../../../../../../abelian-extension.md) $L/K$ inside a fixed algebraic closure and open finite-index subgroups of the [idèle class group](../../../../../../idele-class-group.md) $C_K$. The subgroup for $L$ is $N_{L/K}C_L$, and

$$
\boxed{C_K/N_{L/K}C_L\cong\operatorname{Gal}(L/K).}
$$

Equivalently, for each modulus $\mathfrak m$, subgroups between the ray-principal group $P_K(\mathfrak m)$ and the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) group $I_K(\mathfrak m)$ classify [abelian extensions](../../../../../../abelian-extension.md) whose conductor divides $\mathfrak m$. Their quotients are identified with the corresponding [Galois groups](../../../../../../galois-group.md) by the [Artin map](../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md). Changing the modulus gives the same extension when the induced congruence subgroup is the same.

The conductor criterion says that a finite place has conductor exponent zero exactly when the extension is unramified there; a real place is absent from the conductor exactly when it stays real. Thus conductor one means unramified at every finite place and no real-to-complex ramification.

Apply the existence theorem to the open subgroup of $C_K$ which is the image of

$$
U=\prod_{v<\infty}\mathcal O_v^*\times\prod_{v\mid\infty}K_v^*.
$$

The idele-to-ideal map identifies the quotient by this subgroup with the ordinary [ideal class group](../../../../../../ideal-class-group.md), which is finite. Therefore its class field $H$ exists and satisfies

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(H/K)\cong\operatorname{Cl}(K),\qquad[H:K]=h_K.}
$$

Its conductor is one, so it is everywhere unramified. Conversely any everywhere-unramified [abelian extension](../../../../../../abelian-extension.md) has conductor one; its [Artin map](../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md) kills $U$, hence factors through this quotient and corresponds to an extension contained in $H$. This proves that $H$ is exactly the [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md), the maximal abelian everywhere-unramified extension. Here the ordinary [ideal class group](../../../../../../ideal-class-group.md) is appropriate because the infinite factors include all $K_v^*$, rather than only the positive real factors used in a narrow [ray class group](../../../../../../ray-class-group.md).
