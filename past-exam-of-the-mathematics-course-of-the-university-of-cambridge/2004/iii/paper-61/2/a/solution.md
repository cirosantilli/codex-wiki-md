<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\chi=e^{-ikz-\lambda\bar z/(ik)}$. In terms of [Wirtinger derivatives](../../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md), the elliptic equation is $q_{z\bar z}=\lambda q$. If $W=A\,dz+B\,d\bar z$, its coefficients are $A=\chi q_z$ and $B=-\lambda\chi q/(ik)$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
A_{\bar z}=\chi\left(\lambda q-\frac{\lambda}{ik}q_z\right),\qquad B_z=\chi\left(\lambda q-\frac{\lambda}{ik}q_z\right).
$$

Therefore $dW=(B_z-A_{\bar z})dz\wedge d\bar z=0$, proving that **$W$ is a closed differential one-form**. The parameter restriction $k\ne0$ makes its coefficients well defined.
