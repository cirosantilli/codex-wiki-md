<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $w(k)=-i(k^3+k)$ and define the spectral flux

$$
\boxed{X(x,t,k)=q_{xx}+ikq_x-(k^2+1)q.}
$$

Writing $E=e^{-ikx+w(k)t}$, division of the proposed [divergence form](../../../../../../divergence-form.md) by $E$ gives $q_t+wq+X_x-ikX$. Direct differentiation yields

$$
X_x-ikX=q_{xxx}-q_x+i(k^3+k)q.
$$

The last term cancels $wq$, so the [divergence form](../../../../../../divergence-form.md) is exactly the normalized [partial differential equation](../../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md). This calculation fixes both the sign of the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) and the sign of the boundary flux used below.
