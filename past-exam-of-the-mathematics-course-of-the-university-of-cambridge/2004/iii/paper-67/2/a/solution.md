<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Normalize the region's reference [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) to one. [Pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) has $\rho=\rho_0a^{-3}$, so the closed [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
\dot a^2=H_0^2\left(\frac{\Omega_m}{a}-(\Omega_m-1)\right).
$$

The expanding branch can reach only $a\le a_m$, where **$a_m=\Omega_m/(\Omega_m-1)$**. Substitute $a=a_m(1-\cos\theta)/2$. Since $da/d\theta=a_m\sin\theta/2$ and $\dot a=H_0\sqrt{(\Omega_m-1)(a_m/a-1)}$, cancellation on $0<\theta<\pi$ gives

$$
\frac{dt}{d\theta}=\frac{\Omega_m}{2H_0(\Omega_m-1)^{3/2}}(1-\cos\theta).
$$

Integration from the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) establishes

$$
\boxed{a=\frac{\Omega_m}{2(\Omega_m-1)}(1-\cos\theta),\qquad
H_0t=\frac{\Omega_m}{2(\Omega_m-1)^{3/2}}(\theta-\sin\theta).}
$$

The same parametrization continues through [spherical-collapse turnaround](../../../../../../turnaround-of-spherical-collapse.md) at $\theta=\pi$ onto the contracting branch up to $\theta=2\pi$. Its maximum-expansion time is $t_m=\pi\Omega_m/[2H_0(\Omega_m-1)^{3/2}]$.
