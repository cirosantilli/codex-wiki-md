<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The outer factor is explicitly $a_{\rm bg}/a_m=\tfrac14(6\pi t/t_m)^{2/3}$, the normalized [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) of an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), with the same enclosed mass and initial bang time as the region. The correction describes a fractional reduction of the region's radius relative to this background:

$$
\frac{a_{\rm lin}}{a_{\rm bg}}=1-\epsilon(t),\qquad
\epsilon(t)=\frac1{20}\left(6\pi\frac{t}{t_m}\right)^{2/3}.
$$

For fixed mass, [density](../../../../../../density.md) varies as inverse radius cubed, so $\rho_{\rm region}/\rho_{\rm bg}=(1-\epsilon)^{-3}=1+3\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)$. The growing linear [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) is therefore

$$
\delta_{\rm lin}(t)=\frac3{20}\left(6\pi\frac{t}{t_m}\right)^{2/3},\qquad
\boxed{\delta_{\rm lin}(t_m)=\frac3{20}(6\pi)^{2/3}\simeq1.062.}
$$

The full [spherical collapse](../../../../../../spherical-collapse-model.md) reverses its expansion at $t_m$ and reaches zero radius at $t=2t_m$, corresponding to $\theta=2\pi$. In the ideal cold, pressureless model this makes the nonlinear [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) infinite. Extrapolating the linear growing mode to that same time instead gives the finite [linear spherical-collapse threshold](../../../../../../linear-spherical-collapse-threshold.md)

$$
\boxed{\delta_{\rm lin}(2t_m)=\delta_c=\frac3{20}(12\pi)^{2/3}\simeq1.686,\qquad
\delta_{\rm nonlin}(2t_m)\longrightarrow+\infty.}
$$

This is an extrapolation of a linear perturbation, not an accurate finite-radius approximation at the moment of collapse. [Virial equilibrium](../../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md) requires relaxation of the cold coherent motion and replaces the ideal singular endpoint.
