<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The regional [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) is $3H_0^2\Omega_m/(8\pi Ga^3)$, while the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) has $\rho_{\rm bg}=1/(6\pi Gt^2)$. Substituting the parametric [spherical collapse](../../../../../../spherical-collapse-model.md) from part (a) yields

$$
1+\delta_{\rm nonlin}=\frac{\rho_{\rm region}}{\rho_{\rm bg}}
=\frac92\frac{(\theta-\sin\theta)^2}{(1-\cos\theta)^3}.
$$

At [spherical-collapse turnaround](../../../../../../turnaround-of-spherical-collapse.md), $\theta=\pi$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\delta_{\rm nonlin}(t_m)=\frac{9\pi^2}{16}-1\simeq4.552.}
$$

For an isolated uniform sphere, the [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) is $U=-3GM^2/(5R)$. At [spherical-collapse turnaround](../../../../../../turnaround-of-spherical-collapse.md) the coherent [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is zero, so [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) fixes $E=U_m$. At [virial equilibrium](../../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md), the [virial theorem](../../../../../../virial-theorem.md) gives $2T_v+U_v=0$, hence $E=U_v/2$. Equating these [energies](../../../../../../energy.md) proves the [virial radius from turnaround energy](../../../../../../virial-radius-from-turnaround-energy.md):

$$
\boxed{R_v=R_m/2.}
$$

This uses the same mass and gravitational structure coefficient before and after collapse and neglects boundary pressure, mass loss and external [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) exchange.

If [virial equilibrium](../../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md) is assigned to $2t_m$, the sphere's [density](../../../../../../density.md) is eight times its turnaround [density](../../../../../../density.md), whereas the background [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) has decreased by four. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Delta_v\equiv\frac{\rho_v}{\rho_{\rm bg}(2t_m)}=18\pi^2\simeq177.65,
\qquad\delta_v=18\pi^2-1\simeq176.65.}
$$

The often quoted value $18\pi^2$ is a [density](../../../../../../density.md) ratio; the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) defined as $(\rho-\rho_{\rm bg})/\rho_{\rm bg}$ is smaller by one. This finite virialized estimate and the infinite cold-collapse contrast in part (b) describe different physical endpoints.
