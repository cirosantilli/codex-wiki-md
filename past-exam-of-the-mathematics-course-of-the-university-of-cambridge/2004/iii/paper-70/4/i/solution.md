A [tensor-product surface basis](../../../../../../tensor-product-surface-basis.md) combines two independent univariate bases:

$$
\boxed{S(u,v)=\sum_{i=0}^{m}\sum_{j=0}^{n}P_{ij}B_i(u)C_j(v).}
$$

The coefficients $P_{ij}$ are three-dimensional [control points](../../../../../../control-point.md). Fixing one parameter gives a curve in the other univariate basis; the coefficients of that curve are the corresponding weighted combinations of rows or columns of controls.

The [control net](../../../../../../control-net.md) must have a rectangular two-index topology, with row/column neighbors and quadrilateral mesh cells. “Rectangular” describes the index grid, not the geometry of the points: its faces need not be flat or rectangular in space. A surface requiring a triangular domain or arbitrary extraordinary mesh connectivity is not directly described by one such tensor-product patch.
