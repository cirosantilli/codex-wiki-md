↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [triangular Bézier patch](../../../../../../triangular-bezier-patch.md) is a non-tensor-product example. On barycentric parameters $u,v,w\geq0$ with $u+v+w=1$, define

$$
\boxed{S(u,v,w)=\sum_{i+j+k=n}\frac{n!}{i!j!k!}u^iv^jw^kP_{ijk}.}
$$

The indices satisfy a total-degree constraint, and $w=1-u-v$ couples the two independent parameters. The [control net](../../../../../../control-net.md) is triangular with $(n+1)(n+2)/2$ points, rather than a rectangular product index set. The [multinomial theorem](../../../../../../multinomial-theorem.md) gives nonnegative basis functions summing to one, but the entire basis is not a product of two independent univariate bases.
