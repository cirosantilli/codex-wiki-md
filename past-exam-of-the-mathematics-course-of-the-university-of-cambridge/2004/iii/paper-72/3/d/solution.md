<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At a fixed material point, freeze the [incremental elastic moduli](../../../../../../incremental-elastic-moduli.md) at their local values and fix a unit propagation direction $\nu$. For an arbitrary nonzero polarization $m$, the rank-one matrix $a_{i\alpha}=m_i\nu_\alpha$ annihilates the added [quadratic minor null Lagrangian](../../../../../../quadratic-minor-null-lagrangian.md), since

$$
K_{k\gamma}\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\epsilon_{ijk}m_i\nu_\alpha m_j\nu_\beta=0.
$$

The assumed positivity therefore gives

$$
m_iQ_{ij}(\nu)m_j=a_{i\alpha}H_{\alpha i\beta j}a_{j\beta}>0.
$$

Thus the [acoustic tensor](../../../../../../acoustic-tensor.md) is [positive-definite](../../../../../../positive-definite-bilinear-form.md); this is [strong ellipticity in elasticity](../../../../../../strong-ellipticity-in-elasticity.md). Major symmetry also makes it a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md). By the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), it has three real positive [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $q_1,q_2,q_3$ and an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of real polarizations $m^{(1)},m^{(2)},m^{(3)}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{c_r^2=q_r/\rho_0>0,\qquad r=1,2,3.}
$$

Each polarization gives a real [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) profile through the equation of part (a), in either sign of the propagation speed. Repeated [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) still admit three independent polarizations; the claim is not that the three speeds must be distinct. For an inhomogeneous base deformation these are local, frozen-coefficient wave modes, rather than exact plane waves throughout the entire body.
