<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In dimension four, the two positive [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) are $\alpha=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2$ and $\beta=\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2$. Using the [matrix root basis of the even orthogonal Lie algebra](../../../../../../matrix-root-basis-of-the-even-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md), define

$$
\begin{aligned}e_\alpha&=E_{12}-E_{34},&f_\alpha&=E_{21}-E_{43},&h_\alpha&=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1,-1),\\e_\beta&=E_{13}-E_{24},&f_\beta&=E_{31}-E_{42},&h_\beta&=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1).
\end{aligned}
$$

The [matrix unit](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) identity $[E_{ij},E_{kl}]=\delta_{jk}E_{il}-\delta_{li}E_{kj}$ gives $[e_\gamma,f_\gamma]=h_\gamma$, $[h_\gamma,e_\gamma]=2e_\gamma$, and $[h_\gamma,f_\gamma]=-2f_\gamma$ for $\gamma=\alpha,\beta$. Thus each triple spans an [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md).

The two triples commute. Their Cartan actions on one another vanish because $\alpha(h_\beta)=\beta(h_\alpha)=0$; their mixed root-vector brackets vanish because none of $\pm\alpha\pm\beta$, namely $\pm2\varepsilon_1$ and $\pm2\varepsilon_2$, is a root. This can equally be checked from the displayed matrix units. The six vectors are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md), occupying the four distinct [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) and two independent Cartan directions, so they exhaust the six-dimensional [so4 Lie algebra](../../../../../../so4-lie-algebra.md). The two commuting spans are consequently ideals, and

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{so}_4(\mathbb C)\cong\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C).}
$$

This gives an explicit [Chiral decomposition of the complexified so4 Lie algebra](../../../../../../chiral-decomposition-of-the-complexified-so4-lie-algebra.md), including generators for both factors.
