<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The event contribution is an ordinary density integral. Since $f=-F'$ and $H'=h$, [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E[H(X)\mathbf1_{\{T\le c\}}]
&=\int_0^c H(t)f(t)dt=-\int_0^cH(t)\,dF(t)\\
&=-H(c)F(c)+H(0)F(0)+\int_0^cF(t)h(t)dt\\
&=-H(c)F(c)+\int_0^cf(t)dt.
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[H(X)\mathbf1_{\{T\le c\}}]=1-F(c)-F(c)H(c).}
$$
