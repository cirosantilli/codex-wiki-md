<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Continuity of a nonnegative event-time distribution excludes an atom at zero, so

$$
\boxed{F(0)=1,\qquad H(0)=0.}
$$

The [integrated hazard](../../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) value follows directly from an integral over an empty interval. If a different model allowed an atom at zero, the first conclusion would require modification; that is not the continuous model used here.
