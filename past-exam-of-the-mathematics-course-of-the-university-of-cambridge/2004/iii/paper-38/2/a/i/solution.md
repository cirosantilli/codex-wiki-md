<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the paper's notation $F$ for the [survivor function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md), not for a cumulative distribution function. For a nonnegative continuous event time,

$$
\boxed{F(t)=\mathbb P(T>t),\qquad h(t)=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\frac{\mathbb P(t<T\le t+\varepsilon\mid T>t)}{\varepsilon},\qquad H(t)=\int_0^t h(s)ds.}
$$

The [hazard function](../../../../../../../hazard-function.md) is defined where survival is positive, and the last expression is the [integrated hazard](../../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md). These definitions assume the usual absolutely continuous [survival distribution](../../../../../../../survival-distribution.md) so that the displayed density and [hazard](../../../../../../../hazard-function.md) derivatives exist almost everywhere.
