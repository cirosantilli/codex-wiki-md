The [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is the negative derivative of the [survivor function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md):

$$
\boxed{f(t)=-F'(t),\qquad F(t)=\int_t^\infty f(s)ds.}
$$

Indeed the [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) in a short interval is $F(t)-F(t+\varepsilon)$, and division by its width gives the derivative relation.
