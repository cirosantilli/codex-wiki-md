<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Work with canonically normalized real components of the [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md); a complex [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) can first be written in terms of its real and imaginary components. At a [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\varphi_0$, expansion of the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) gives

$$
V(\varphi_0+\eta)=V(\varphi_0)+\frac12\eta^T M^2\eta+\cdots,\qquad M^2_{rs}=\partial_r\partial_s V(\varphi_0).
$$

The linear term vanishes by stationarity. Thus the [scalar mass matrix](../../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md) is the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) of the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md). Invariance under each [Lie algebra generator](../../../../../lie-algebra-generator.md) gives the identity $\partial_r V(\varphi)(iT_a\varphi)_r=0$. Differentiating this identity with respect to $\varphi_s$ and evaluating at the stationary point removes the term containing $\partial_rV$, leaving

$$
M^2(iT_a\varphi_0)=0.
$$

The kernel of the map $T\mapsto iT\varphi_0$ is precisely the [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) of the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) H. Consequently its restriction to a complementary space of broken [Lie algebra generators](../../../../../lie-algebra-generator.md) is injective: a linear combination that annihilates $\varphi_0$ would belong both to the stabilizer algebra and its complement, and hence would vanish. There are therefore $\dim G-\dim H$ independent [Goldstone directions in the scalar mass matrix](../../../../../goldstone-directions-in-the-scalar-mass-matrix.md), tangent to the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md). **There are $\dim G-\dim H$ symmetry-protected massless scalars.**

This [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) guarantees at least that many zero quadratic masses. The claim that these are all the massless scalars additionally requires nonzero curvature transverse to the vacuum orbit. For example, $V=\kappa(\varphi^T\varphi-v^2)^4$, with $\kappa>0$, has an ordinary spherical [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) but its entire [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) vanishes there. These [accidental massless radial modes beyond Goldstone modes](../../../../../accidental-massless-radial-modes-beyond-goldstone-modes.md) are not excluded by the hypotheses. For the indicated [orthogonal group](../../../../../orthogonal-group.md) breaking, the protected number is

$$
\boxed{\dim O(n)-\dim O(n-1)=\frac{n(n-1)-(n-1)(n-2)}2=n-1.}
$$

For the [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md), put $r=\sqrt{2\phi^\dagger\phi}=v+H$ and $z=\sqrt2\phi/r$ wherever $r\ne0$. If $z=(z_1,z_2)^T$, then

$$
U=\begin{pmatrix}z_2^*&z_1\\-z_1^*&z_2\end{pmatrix},\qquad U^\dagger U=1,\qquad\det U=1,\qquad U\binom01=z.
$$

This explicitly exhibits the three angular coordinates as an [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) transformation of a real radial field. In particular, the [polar coordinates for the electroweak Higgs doublet](../../../../../polar-coordinates-for-the-electroweak-higgs-doublet.md) can locally be chosen in the form

$$
\phi=\exp(-i\boldsymbol n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma+in_3 I)\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom0{v+H}.
$$

At the vacuum the three variations generated by $-i\sigma_1$, $-i\sigma_2$, and $i(I-\sigma_3)$ acting on $(0,1)^T$ are independent over the reals. They span the three broken directions; the remaining generator $Q=(\sigma_3+I)/2$ annihilates the vacuum. This proves that the exponential supplies local angular coordinates. An inverse local [gauge transformation](../../../../../gauge-transformation.md), applied also to the [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md), removes these three fields in [unitary gauge](../../../../../unitary-gauge.md). They supply the longitudinal polarizations of the massive [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md) and [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) through the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md). The parameterization is local about a nonzero vacuum, rather than a coordinate system through every possible zero of the [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md).

Use the metric $(+---)$ and define the charged [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md) and neutral [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md) by

$$
W_\mu^\pm=\frac{A_\mu^1\mp iA_\mu^2}{\sqrt2},\qquad Z_\mu=c_W A_\mu^3-s_W B_\mu,\qquad A_\mu^\gamma=s_W A_\mu^3+c_W B_\mu,
$$

where $s_W=\sin\theta_W$, $c_W=\cos\theta_W$, and $\tan\theta_W=g'/g$. Direct substitution into the [gauge-covariant kinetic term](../../../../../gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) gives

$$
D_\mu\phi=\binom{\displaystyle \frac{ig}{2}(v+H)W_\mu^+}{\displaystyle \frac{\partial_\mu H}{\sqrt2}+\frac{i(v+H)}{2\sqrt2}(-gA_\mu^3+g'B_\mu)},
$$

and hence

$$
(D_\mu\phi)^\dagger D^\mu\phi=\frac12(\partial H)^2+\frac{g^2(v+H)^2}{4}W_\mu^-W^{+\mu}+\frac{(v+H)^2}{8}(gA_\mu^3-g'B_\mu)^2.
$$

The [electroweak doublet gauge-boson mass matrix](../../../../../electroweak-doublet-gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) in the $(A^3,B)$ basis is

$$
M^2_{\rm neutral}=\frac{v^2}{4}\begin{pmatrix}g^2&-gg'\\-gg'&g'^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its normalized zero direction is $(s_W,c_W)$ and its massive direction is $(c_W,-s_W)$. Thus the scalar-vector terms in physical fields are

$$
\mathcal L_{H,\rm vectors}=m_W^2\left(1+\frac H v\right)^2W_\mu^-W^{+\mu}+\frac12m_Z^2\left(1+\frac H v\right)^2Z_\mu Z^\mu,
$$

with the [tree-level electroweak gauge-boson masses](../../../../../tree-level-electroweak-gauge-boson-masses.md)

$$
\boxed{m_W=\frac{gv}{2},\qquad m_Z=\frac{v\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}}2,\qquad m_\gamma=0,\qquad m_W=m_Z\cos\theta_W.}
$$

These are also charge eigenstates. The unbroken [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md) has generator $Q=T_3+Y$; on the [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md), $Y=I/2$. Since $[Q,T_\pm]=\pm T_\pm$, the combinations $W^\pm$ have charges $\pm1$, while Z, the [photon](../../../../../photon.md), and H are neutral. The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) disappears from the displayed scalar-vector terms because $gA^3-g'B=\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}Z$. **The photon is massless and has no direct tree-level coupling to the remaining radial scalar in this sector.** This does not remove its interactions with charged [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md), or exclude a scalar-to-photon interaction generated by loops.

Finally, retain the printed normalization of the [Higgs field potential](../../../../../higgs-field-potential.md):

$$
V(H)=\frac\lambda2\left(vH+\frac{H^2}{2}\right)^2=\frac{\lambda v^2}{2}H^2+\frac{\lambda v}{2}H^3+\frac\lambda8H^4.
$$

The kinetic term is $\tfrac12(\partial H)^2$, so the [Higgs radial mass for a half-normalized quartic potential](../../../../../higgs-radial-mass-for-a-half-normalized-quartic-potential.md) is **$\boxed{m_H^2=\lambda v^2}$**. The extra factor of two familiar from a potential with prefactor $\lambda$, rather than $\lambda/2$, does not apply here.
