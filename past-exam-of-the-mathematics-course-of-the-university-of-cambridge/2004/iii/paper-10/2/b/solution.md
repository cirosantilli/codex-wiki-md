<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Fix an admissible contour $\Gamma$ with interior $D$ compactly contained in $U$ and containing $\sigma(x)$. Apply the preceding spectral inclusion to $D$, so that it also contains $\sigma(x_n)$ for large $n$. Uniformly on the compact contour, the [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md) makes $R(\zeta,x_n)$ bounded, and the [resolvent identity](../../../../../../resolvent-identity.md) gives

$$
R(\zeta,x_n)-R(\zeta,x)=R(\zeta,x_n)(x_n-x)R(\zeta,x).
$$

The resolvents therefore converge uniformly on $\Gamma$. Both [holomorphic functional calculus](../../../../../../holomorphic-functional-calculus.md) values use that same contour, so

$$
\|\Theta_{x_n}(f)-\Theta_x(f)\|\leq\frac{\operatorname{length}(\Gamma)}{2\pi}\sup_\Gamma|f|\sup_\Gamma\|R(\zeta,x_n)-R(\zeta,x)\|\longrightarrow0.
$$

Hence **$\Theta_{x_n}(f)\to\Theta_x(f)$** for each fixed $f\in\mathcal O(U)$.
