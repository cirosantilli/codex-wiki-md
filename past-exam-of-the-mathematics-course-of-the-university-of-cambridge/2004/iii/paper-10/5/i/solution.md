<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The functional's stipulated positivity initially means $f(z^*z)\geq0$, without assuming continuity. For a [Hermitian element of a C-star algebra](../../../../../../hermitian-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $h$, the identity

$$
h=\tfrac14\bigl((h+1)^2-(h-1)^2\bigr)
$$

expresses it as a real linear combination of two adjoint squares. Their functional values are real, so $f(h)$ is real. Write $x=h+ik$ with $h=(x+x^*)/2$ and $k=(x-x^*)/(2i)$ Hermitian. Complex linearity gives $f(x)=f(h)+if(k)$ and $f(x^*)=f(h)-if(k)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x^*)=\overline{f(x)}.}
$$

This is the Hermitian symmetry used in the [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for positive C-star functionals](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-positive-c-star-functionals.md).
