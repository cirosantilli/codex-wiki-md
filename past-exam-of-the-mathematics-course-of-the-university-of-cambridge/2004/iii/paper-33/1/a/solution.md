<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True.** Suppose a dual [feasible solution](../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $y$ exists. For every primal feasible $x$, [weak duality](../../../../../../weak-duality.md) gives $c^Tx\leq b^Ty$, a finite [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) independent of $x$. This contradicts primal unboundedness. Therefore an unbounded primal has an infeasible dual. Here “unbounded” [arithmetic means](../../../../../../arithmetic-mean.md) a nonempty feasible set with objective values unbounded above, rather than merely an unbounded feasible region.
