↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The general estimate from part 1 and the exact inverse [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md) from part 2b imply

$$
\boxed{\|P_x\|_{L^\infty}\le\|A_x^{-1}\|_{\ell^\infty}<2,}
$$

and in particular **$\|P_x\|_{L^\infty}\le2$**, uniformly in the number of knots. This bound concerns the [support](../../../../../../../support.md)-centred sites $x_i=i+3/2$; shifting the sampling sites changes the [B-spline collocation matrix](../../../../../../../b-spline-collocation-matrix.md) and can destroy uniform stability.
