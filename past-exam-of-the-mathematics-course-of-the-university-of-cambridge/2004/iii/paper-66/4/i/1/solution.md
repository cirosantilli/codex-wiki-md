**One integral can occur in an autonomous system with a fully chaotic region and no additional continuous orbit constraints.** The [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is conserved in a time-independent [gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). If a [chaotic stellar orbit](../../../../../../../chaotic-stellar-orbit.md) is dense in a connected region of its energy hypersurface, every continuous [integral of motion](../../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md) is constant on that region. Thus it cannot supply a new independent constraint there, and energy is the only independent [isolating integral](../../../../../../../isolating-integral.md). A generic nonaxisymmetric potential can contain such regions; not every orbit in such a potential is necessarily chaotic.
