An [isolating integral](../../../../../../isolating-integral.md) is a single-valued [integral of motion](../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md) $I(\mathbf x,\mathbf v)$ whose fixed value confines an orbit to a lower-dimensional level set of [phase space](../../../../../../phase-space.md). Along the flow, $dI/dt=0$; for an autonomous [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md), this means $\{I,H\}=0$. Several [isolating integrals](../../../../../../isolating-integral.md) are independent when their gradients are linearly independent on the regular region considered. Counting them requires a specified domain: merely local orbit labels in a flow box do not establish global single-valued integrals. The following cases concern regular globally meaningful orbit constraints.
