<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an open neighborhood D of mu, its complement is closed and excludes mu. The preceding upper bound is minus infinity, so, for example, eventually $\mathbb P(X_n\notin D)\leq e^{-b_n}\to0$. Hence **the sequence concentrates in every neighborhood of mu**.

If an open E contains mu, its [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) tends to one. Its [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) divided by $b_n\to\infty$ therefore tends to zero, which equals $-\inf_EI'$. If E does not contain mu, that latter value is minus infinity and its lower bound is automatic. Thus

$$
\boxed{\liminf_n b_n^{-1}\log\mathbb P(X_n\in E)\geq-\inf_EI'.}
$$

With the closed-set upper bound this proves the [slower-speed degeneration of a large deviation principle](../../../../../../slower-speed-degeneration-of-a-large-deviation-principle.md). This degenerate rate has no strictly positive finite value, so it does not give the sequence a different [Hurstiness](../../../../../../hurstiness.md) under the stated definition.
