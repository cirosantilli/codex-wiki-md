<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $C=N/\varphi(M)$. The natural map $N_P\to C_P$ is onto. Its kernel is the image of $\varphi_P$: a fraction $n/s$ maps to zero precisely when $tn\in\varphi(M)$ for some $t\notin P$. If $tn=\varphi(m)$, then $n/s=\varphi_P(m/(ts))$. This proves the localized [cokernel](../../../../../../cokernel.md) identity directly:

$$
\operatorname{coker}(\varphi_P)\cong C_P.
$$

Thus $\varphi_P$ is a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) for every $P$ exactly when $C_P=0$ for every $P$. Part (i) makes this equivalent to $C=0$, or to $\varphi$ being a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md). Hence **surjectivity is detected at all prime [localizations](../../../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md)**, and again maximal [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md) alone would suffice.
