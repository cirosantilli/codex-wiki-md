<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Only the copies inherited through the two related parents can share recent [identity by descent](../../../../../../identity-by-descent.md); the other parental contributions come from unrelated [founders in a pedigree](../../../../../../founder-in-a-pedigree.md). The related parents are [full siblings](../../../../../../full-sibling.md). Their transmitted copies trace to the same shared grandparent with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$, and then to the same copy of that grandparent with probability $1/2$. Thus the [first-cousin identity-by-descent probabilities](../../../../../../first-cousin-identity-by-descent-probabilities.md) are

$$
\boxed{P(J=0)=\frac34,\qquad P(J=1)=\frac14,\qquad P(J=2)=0.}
$$

To compute the [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md), one random copy from each cousin selects those potentially shared contributions with probability $1/4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\phi=\frac14P(J=1)+\frac12P(J=2)=\frac1{16}.}
$$
