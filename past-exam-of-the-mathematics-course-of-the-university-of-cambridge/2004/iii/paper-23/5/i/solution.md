↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $F$ preserves [finite limits](../../../../../../finite-limit.md). Given any finite diagram in the [comma category](../../../../../../comma-category.md) $(A\downarrow F)$, first take its underlying limit $c$ in $\mathcal C$. Its structure maps $A\to Fc_j$ form a cone, and preservation gives a unique map $A\to Fc$. This object and the underlying projections satisfy the limiting [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) in the comma category. The construction also includes the empty diagram, since $F$ sends a [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md) to a singleton. Thus $(A\downarrow F)$ has finite limits for every set $A$, proving (ii).
