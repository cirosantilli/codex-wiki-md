<h1 id="5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every covariant [representable functor](../../../../../../representable-functor.md) $\mathcal C(c,-)$ preserves [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md), since a morphism out of $c$ into a limit is precisely a compatible cone. A filtered colimit of these functors is computed pointwise, and [filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets](../../../../../../filtered-colimits-commute-with-finite-limits-in-sets.md). Interchanging the finite limit and filtered colimit therefore proves that $F$ preserves finite limits, which is (i). This completes the cycle. It is also the [flat functor and finite-limit preservation](../../../../../../flat-functor-and-finite-limit-preservation.md) criterion for a finitely complete small category.
