<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $A=1$. The [category of elements](../../../../../../category-of-elements.md) $\int F=(1\downarrow F)$ has [finite limits](../../../../../../finite-limit.md). Reversing its limiting cones gives [cocones](../../../../../../cocone-under-a-diagram.md) over every finite diagram in $(\int F)^{\mathrm{op}}$; a terminal object before reversal ensures nonemptiness. Its [opposite category](../../../../../../opposite-category.md) is therefore a [filtered category](../../../../../../filtered-category.md), proving (iii).
