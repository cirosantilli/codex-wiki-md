<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The orbits of a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) $N$ are blocks for the [primitive group action](../../../../../../primitive-group-action.md). If every orbit is a singleton then $N\le G_{[\Omega]}$, giving the second alternative. Otherwise $N$ is transitive, and $G=NG_\alpha$.

Work in $G/N$. Every $g\in G$ can be written $nh$ with $n\in N$, $h\in G_\alpha$. [Conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) by $n$ has no effect on the [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) in this quotient, while $h$ normalizes $A$. Thus every conjugate of $A$ has the same [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $AN/N$. The generation hypothesis says this [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) generates all of $G/N$. But it is already an abelian [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md), so $G/N$ is abelian and $G'\le N$. This proves the [Iwasawa simplicity lemma](../../../../../../iwasawa-simplicity-lemma.md) in its possibly nonfaithful form:

$$
\boxed{G'\le N\quad\text{or}\quad N\le G_{[\Omega]}.}
$$
