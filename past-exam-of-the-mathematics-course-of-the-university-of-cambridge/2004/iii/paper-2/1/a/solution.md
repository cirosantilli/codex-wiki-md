<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Frattini subgroup](../../../../../../frattini-subgroup.md) is the intersection of the [maximal subgroups](../../../../../../maximal-subgroup.md) of $G$, with $\Phi(1)=1$. A useful equivalent property is

$$
H\Phi(G)=G\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H=G\qquad(H\le G).
$$

Indeed, a proper [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ lies in a [maximal subgroup](../../../../../../maximal-subgroup.md), which also contains $\Phi(G)$. The [Frattini subgroup](../../../../../../frattini-subgroup.md) is a [characteristic subgroup](../../../../../../characteristic-subgroup.md), since [automorphisms](../../../../../../automorphism.md) permute the [maximal subgroups](../../../../../../maximal-subgroup.md).

The [Fitting subgroup](../../../../../../fitting-subgroup.md) is the largest [nilpotent normal subgroup](../../../../../../nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md). To prove its existence, first form the [p-core](../../../../../../p-core.md) $O_p(G)$: the product of all normal [p-subgroups](../../../../../../p-subgroup.md). For two such [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md), their product is normal and has order $|AB|=|A||B|/|A\cap B|$, a power of $p$. There are only finitely many [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md), so $O_p(G)$ exists. For distinct primes $p,r$,

$$
[O_p(G),O_r(G)]\le O_p(G)\cap O_r(G)=1.
$$

Thus the product of the [p-cores](../../../../../../p-core.md) is a [nilpotent normal subgroup](../../../../../../nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md), a [direct product of groups](../../../../../../direct-product-of-groups.md) of its [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md). Conversely, a finite [nilpotent normal subgroup](../../../../../../nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md) $K$ has unique [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md), each a [characteristic subgroup](../../../../../../characteristic-subgroup.md) of $K$. Each is normal in $G$ and lies in the corresponding [p-core](../../../../../../p-core.md). Consequently **the required largest [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) exists**, and

$$
\boxed{F(G)=\prod_{p\mid |G|}O_p(G).}
$$
