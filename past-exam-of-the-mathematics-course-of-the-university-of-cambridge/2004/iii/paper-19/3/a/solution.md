<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) complete [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md) is [disconnected at infinity](../../../../../../disconnected-at-infinity.md) when the complement of some [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) set has at least two unbounded [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) components. Equivalently it has at least two ends. A [line in a Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../line-in-a-riemannian-manifold.md) is a unit-speed [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) $\ell:\mathbb R\to M$ satisfying $d(\ell(s),\ell(t))=|s-t|$ for every $s,t$.

Let $K$ be the [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) separating set, enlarged inside a fixed [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) metric ball if needed for bounds. Choose $x_j,y_j$ in two different unbounded components of $M\setminus K$, with both distances from $K$ tending to infinity. By the [Hopf-Rinow theorem](../../../../../../hopf-rinow-theorem.md), join them by unit-speed [minimizing geodesics](../../../../../../minimizing-geodesic.md). Every such segment meets $K$, since a path avoiding it cannot join its different components. Reparametrize a segment so that one crossing point is at time zero. Its parameter interval is $[-a_j,b_j]$, where $a_j,b_j\to\infty$ by the distances from the endpoints to $K$.

The [unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../unit-tangent-bundle.md) over $K$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), so a subsequence of the initial position/velocity pairs converges. Continuous dependence of the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) on its initial data, and completeness, give a limiting [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) defined on all of $\mathbb R$. For any fixed $s<t$, the approximating segments are defined on $[s,t]$ for large $j$ and satisfy $d(\gamma_j(s),\gamma_j(t))=t-s$. Continuity of the distance passes this equality to the limit. Thus **the limiting [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) is a line**, proving [a line from disconnection at infinity](../../../../../../a-line-from-disconnection-at-infinity.md).
