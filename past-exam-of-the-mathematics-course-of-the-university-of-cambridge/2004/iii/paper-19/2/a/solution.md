↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md) is complete when its [Riemannian distance](../../../../../../riemannian-distance.md) is a complete [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md), meaning that every [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) converges in the manifold. The [Hopf-Rinow theorem](../../../../../../hopf-rinow-theorem.md) says that for a [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) finite-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md), this is equivalent to [geodesic completeness](../../../../../../geodesic-completeness.md), and also to [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) of every closed bounded subset. Under these equivalent conditions any two points are joined by a [minimizing geodesic](../../../../../../minimizing-geodesic.md), and the [Riemannian exponential map](../../../../../../exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) at every point is defined on its entire [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md).

We use the usual connected, boundaryless-manifold convention for the global statements in Q2–Q5. Connectedness is essential, including for the diameter and group-growth conclusions. Without connectedness, the completeness and covering assertions apply to the target components met by the map; an unrelated incomplete component cannot be controlled by a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md) into another component.
