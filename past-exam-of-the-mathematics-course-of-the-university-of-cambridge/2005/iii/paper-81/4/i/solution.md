<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Measure $z$ from the rigid tube wall towards the air core, so $0<z<h$. To leading order in $h_0/a$ and small interface slope, the [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) entering the [Young–Laplace equation](../../../../../../young-laplace-equation.md) is

$$
\kappa=\frac1{a-h}+h_{xx}+\cdots,\qquad
p=p_{\rm air}-\sigma_0\kappa.
$$

The air [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) is spatially uniform at the order retained. Thus the linear liquid [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) perturbation associated with thickness amplitude $H$ is $p_1=-\sigma_0(a^{-2}-k^2)H$. The circumferential [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) favors growth at long [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md); the [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) opposes it at short [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md).

The [lubrication theory](../../../../../../lubrication-theory.md) momentum equation is $\mu u_{zz}=p_x$. With the wall [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) $u(0)=0$ and zero surface [shear stress](../../../../../../shear-stress.md), $u_z(h)=0$, its solution and flux are

$$
u=\frac{p_x}{2\mu}(z^2-2hz),\qquad
q=\int_0^h u\,dz=-\frac{h^3}{3\mu}p_x.
$$

Leading thin-film [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) is $h_t+q_x=0$. For a mode $e^{\beta t+ikx}$, this gives the [capillary instability of an annular liquid lining](../../../../../../capillary-instability-of-an-annular-liquid-lining.md)

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac{\sigma_0h_0^3}{3\mu}k^2(a^{-2}-k^2).}
$$

Therefore **the unstable band is $\boxed{0<k^2<a^{-2}}$**. Maximization with respect to $k^2$ gives $k_*^2=1/(2a^2)$ and

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\max}=\frac{\sigma_0h_0^3}{12\mu a^4}.}
$$

These [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) coefficients use $a$ instead of $a-h_0$ consistently at leading order in the thin-lining approximation.
