<h1 id="4/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\alpha/\lambda\ll1$, follow the unstable root near the clean-film value $L=\lambda s(1-s)$. Write $\beta=L+\Delta$ in $(\beta-L)(\beta+S)+3LS/4=0$. At first order in $S$, $L\Delta+3LS/4=0$, giving

$$
\beta=\lambda s(1-s)-\frac34\alpha s+O(\alpha^2/\lambda).
$$

This expansion is uniform near the maximizing [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md). Differentiation gives $s_*=1/2-3\alpha/(8\lambda)+O((\alpha/\lambda)^2)$. Evaluating the growth there yields

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\max}=\frac\lambda4-\frac{3\alpha}{8}+O(\alpha^2/\lambda).}
$$

The change in maximizing [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) only affects the maximum at second order. The first-order decrease therefore has exactly the requested coefficient, relative to the clean-film rate with its [surface tension](../../../../../../../surface-tension.md) replaced by $\bar\sigma$.
