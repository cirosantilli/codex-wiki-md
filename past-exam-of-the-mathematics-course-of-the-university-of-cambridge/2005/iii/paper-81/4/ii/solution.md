<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the positive undisturbed [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension.md) be $\bar\sigma=\sigma_0-A\Gamma_0>0$. An [insoluble surfactant](../../../../../../insoluble-surfactant.md) perturbation $g$ produces $\sigma_1=-Ag$. The tangential [Marangoni stress](../../../../../../marangoni-effect.md) changes the surface condition to $\mu u_z(h)=\sigma_x$. Solving the [lubrication theory](../../../../../../lubrication-theory.md) momentum equation now gives

$$
u=\frac{p_x}{2\mu}(z^2-2hz)+\frac{\sigma_x}{\mu}z,\qquad
q=-\frac{h^3}{3\mu}p_x+\frac{h^2}{2\mu}\sigma_x,\qquad
u_s=-\frac{h^2}{2\mu}p_x+\frac h\mu\sigma_x.
$$

At leading order, [conservation of insoluble surfactant on a moving interface](../../../../../../conservation-of-insoluble-surfactant-on-a-moving-interface.md) reduces to $\Gamma_t+(\Gamma u_s)_x=0$. Corrections from the changing circumference are smaller by $h_0/a$. Likewise the normal-[pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) contribution $-\sigma_1/a$ gives a coupling smaller by $h_0/a$ than the retained tangential [Marangoni stress](../../../../../../marangoni-effect.md), so it is omitted at the same order.

For thickness and concentration amplitudes $H,g$ proportional to $e^{\beta t+ikx}$, put $D=a^{-2}-k^2$ and use $p_1=-\bar\sigma DH$. The two conservation equations become

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(\beta-\frac{h_0^3\bar\sigma}{3\mu}k^2D\right)H+\frac{h_0^2A}{2\mu}k^2g&=0,\\
-\frac{\Gamma_0h_0^2\bar\sigma}{2\mu}k^2D\,H+\left(\beta+\frac{h_0A\Gamma_0}{\mu}k^2\right)g&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Set $s=k^2a^2$, $\lambda=h_0^3\bar\sigma/(3\mu a^4)$ and $\alpha=h_0A\Gamma_0/(\mu a^2)$. The uncoupled capillary growth and elastic relaxation rates are $L=\lambda s(1-s)$ and $S=\alpha s$. The off-diagonal product is $3LS/4$, so the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $(\beta-L)(\beta+S)+3LS/4$. Thus the [surfactant stabilization of an annular liquid lining](../../../../../../surfactant-stabilization-of-an-annular-liquid-lining.md) relation is

$$
\boxed{\beta^2+\beta s[\lambda(s-1)+\alpha]+\frac{\alpha\lambda}{4}s^2(s-1)=0.}
$$

This proves the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) before its lettered deductions. The physical assumption $\bar\sigma>0$ is needed for the claimed capillary instability band.
