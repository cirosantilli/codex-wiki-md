<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Steady [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) makes $\alpha u=Q$. For uniform area, $u=U=Q/\alpha_0$ is constant and the [pressure-area law of a collapsible tube](../../../../../../pressure-area-law-of-a-collapsible-tube.md) gives $p_x=p'_e(x)$. The acceleration vanishes, so momentum balance requires

$$
\boxed{p'_e(x)=-R(\alpha_0)\frac{Q}{\alpha_0}.}
$$

The external [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) must therefore decrease linearly along a positive-flow tube. Its additive constant is free; the constant transmural [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) is $\widetilde P(\alpha_0)$.
