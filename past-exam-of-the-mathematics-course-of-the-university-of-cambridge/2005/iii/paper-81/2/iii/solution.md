<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $U=Q/\alpha_0$, $R_0=R(\alpha_0)$, $c_0^2=\alpha_0\widetilde P'(\alpha_0)$ and $\nu=\omega-kU$. Let the area and [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) perturbations have amplitudes $a$ and $w$. Linearizing [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) and momentum, and using $R'_0=-nR_0/\alpha_0$, gives

$$
-i\nu a+ik\alpha_0w=0,\qquad
(R_0-i\nu)w=-ik\frac{c_0^2}{\alpha_0}a+\frac{nR_0U}{\alpha_0}a.
$$

For nonzero $k$, eliminate $w=\nu a/(k\alpha_0)$ to obtain the [friction-induced instability of a collapsible-tube flow](../../../../../../friction-induced-instability-of-a-collapsible-tube-flow.md) relation

$$
\boxed{\nu^2+iR_0\nu-k^2c_0^2-inkR_0U=0,\qquad\nu=\omega-kU.}
$$

At the stated threshold $nU=c_0$, it factors as

$$
(\nu-kc_0)(\nu+kc_0+iR_0)=0.
$$

Therefore **one mode has real $\boxed{\omega=k(U+c_0)}$**, while the other has $\omega=k(U-c_0)-iR_0$ and decays. The PDF's real-[frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) statement is valid for the neutral branch, not for both roots. The Fourier convention $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ makes positive imaginary [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) mean growth.
