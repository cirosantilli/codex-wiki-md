<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first equation is vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md): the upward [pressure gradient](../../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) balances the central [mass](../../../../../../mass.md)'s vertical gravitational force. For $|z|\ll r$, the point-mass acceleration is $-GMz/(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}\simeq-\Omega_K^2z$, where [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md) gives $\Omega_K^2=GM/r^3$. The disc's [self-gravity](../../../../../../self-gravity.md) and vertical inertia are neglected.

The second equation is local thermal balance. The divergence of the vertical [radiative flux](../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) equals [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md). In the convention of this [alpha disk](../../../../../../alpha-disk.md), $\nu=\alpha p/(\rho\Omega)$, and the dominant [Keplerian shear](../../../../../../keplerian-shear.md) has $r\,d\Omega/dr=-3\Omega/2$. Therefore

$$
q^+=\rho\nu\left(r\frac{d\Omega}{dr}\right)^2=\frac94\alpha\Omega p=\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}.
$$

The [radiative flux](../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) increases from zero at the symmetric midplane to a positive outgoing value at the upper surface. Radial heat transport, thermal [advection](../../../../../../advection.md) and rapid storage of thermal [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) are excluded from this local balance.

The third equation is [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md), equivalently

$$
F=-\frac{16\sigma T^3}{3\kappa\rho}\frac{\partial T}{\partial z}.
$$

It applies to optically thick material, with $\kappa$ the opacity per unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and $\sigma$ the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md). Here constant [electron-scattering opacity](../../../../../../electron-scattering-opacity.md) represents [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md); the formula uses a diffusive, nearly thermal radiation field. Positive upward [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) requires [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) to decrease upwards.

The final equation is the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) law, with [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) $\mu_m$ in proton-mass units. Writing $\mathcal R=k/(\mu_m m_p)$ gives $p=\mathcal R\rho T$. The [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) is [gas pressure](../../../../../../gas-pressure.md) because [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) is assumed negligible. **The four equations express vertical support, local heating balance, diffusive cooling and the gas equation of state.**
