<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For tangent vectors $X,Y$ on a slice, the [extrinsic curvature](../../../../../../extrinsic-curvature.md) is $K(X,Y)=g(\nabla_Xn,Y)$. Differentiating $g(n,Y)=0$ and using metric compatibility gives $K(X,Y)=-g(n,\nabla_XY)$. Since the connection is torsion-free,

$$
K(X,Y)-K(Y,X)=-g(n,\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX)=-g(n,[X,Y])=0.
$$

The final equality holds because tangent fields on a [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) have a tangent Lie bracket. All normal components are projected out, so **$K_{\mu\nu}$ is symmetric**. This is precisely [hypersurface orthogonality implies symmetric extrinsic curvature](../../../../../../hypersurface-orthogonality-implies-symmetric-extrinsic-curvature.md); without an integrable tangent distribution the corresponding antisymmetric vorticity need not vanish.
