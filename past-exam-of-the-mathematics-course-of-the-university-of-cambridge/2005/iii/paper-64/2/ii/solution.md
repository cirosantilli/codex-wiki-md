<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $q_{\alpha\beta}=g_{\alpha\beta}+n_\alpha n_\beta$ for the [induced metric](../../../../../../induced-metric.md). By definition of the [spatial covariant derivative](../../../../../../spatial-covariant-derivative.md),

$$
D_\mu q_{\alpha\beta}
=P_\mu{}^\rho P_\alpha{}^\sigma P_\beta{}^\lambda
\nabla_\rho(g_{\sigma\lambda}+n_\sigma n_\lambda).
$$

The spacetime [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is metric compatible, so $\nabla g=0$. The remaining terms contain either $P_\alpha{}^\sigma n_\sigma$ or $P_\beta{}^\lambda n_\lambda$, each zero because $n^\mu n_\mu=-1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{D_\mu q_{\alpha\beta}=0.}
$$

This verifies [metric compatibility of the spatial covariant derivative](../../../../../../metric-compatibility-of-the-spatial-covariant-derivative.md) directly, without assuming that the unprojected derivative of $q$ vanishes.
