<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The spatial momentum operator acts only on the plane wave:

$$
\frac\hbar i\nabla\left[a^{-1}A_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-ikt}\right]
=\hbar\mathbf k\left[a^{-1}A_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-ikt}\right].
$$

Thus **the modes have fixed canonical/comoving wavevector momentum, not fixed physical momentum**. In the usual positive-energy field-mode convention the local [physical momentum](../../../../../../physical-momentum.md) and energy are $\hbar\mathbf k/a$ and $\hbar k/a$. This agrees with conserved spatial [comoving momentum](../../../../../../comoving-momentum.md) and cosmological redshift proportional to $a^{-1}$.

The overall particle-action sign noted in (i) reverses the literal worldline canonical label relative to this usual mechanical momentum: it is $\mathbf P^c=-a\mathbf P$ for a future-directed particle with that printed action. The conserved plane-wave label therefore has the opposite sign if it is identified with that literal worldline label. This sign convention does not change the wavenumber magnitude or either conservation/redshift conclusion.
