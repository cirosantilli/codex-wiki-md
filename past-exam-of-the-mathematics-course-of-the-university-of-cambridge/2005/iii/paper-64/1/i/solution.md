<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The spatial coordinates are cyclic because the flat [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) depends only on time. Differentiating the printed affine Lagrangian gives the [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md)

$$
P_i^c=-m g_{i\nu}\dot x^\nu=-ma^2\dot x^i.
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) give $dP_i^c/d\tau=\partial L/\partial x^i=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf P^c=\text{constant}.}
$$

This is the conserved quantity associated with spatial [translation invariance](../../../../../../translation-invariance.md). The overall minus sign in the stated action makes this canonical covector opposite to the usual future-directed mechanical momentum; it does not alter the geodesic trajectories or the conservation result. The [canonical momentum sign in the affine particle action](../../../../../../canonical-momentum-sign-in-the-affine-particle-action.md) will be kept explicit below.
