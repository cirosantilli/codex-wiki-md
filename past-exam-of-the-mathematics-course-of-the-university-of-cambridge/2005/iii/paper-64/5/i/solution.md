<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\mathcal L=-\tfrac12g^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha\phi\partial_\beta\phi-V$. Vary the covariant metric while holding the scalar fixed. The supplied determinant and inverse-metric variations give

$$
\delta S=\frac12\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[g^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L+\partial^\mu\phi\partial^\nu\phi\right]\delta g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

By the definition $\delta S=\tfrac12\int\sqrt{-g}\,T^{\mu\nu}\delta g_{\mu\nu}$, lowering its indices yields the [stress-energy tensor of a canonical scalar field](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor-of-a-canonical-scalar-field.md):

$$
\boxed{T_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi-g_{\mu\nu}\left[\frac12(\partial\phi)^2+V(\phi)\right].}
$$

The sign in the determinant variation is positive for varying $g_{\mu\nu}$ and would be negative for varying the inverse metric; matching that convention is essential.
