<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\ell$ be the characteristic radius over which the [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md) changes, with [angular velocity](../../../../../../angular-velocity.md) of order $\Omega$. The winding source has magnitude $F\sim B_0\Omega$. For times too short for appreciable [magnetic diffusion](../../../../../../magnetic-diffusion.md), the zero initial [toroidal magnetic field](../../../../../../toroidal-magnetic-field.md) therefore grows as $B\sim B_0\Omega t$. In the steady balance, $F$ is opposed by $\eta B/\ell^2$, giving $B_{\mathrm{steady}}\sim B_0\Omega\ell^2/\eta$. Comparing these amplitudes gives the [diffusive establishment time of a wound toroidal field](../../../../../../diffusive-establishment-time-of-a-wound-toroidal-field.md):

$$
\boxed{t_{\mathrm{est}}\sim t_\eta=\frac{\ell^2}{\eta}=\mu_0\sigma\ell^2.}
$$

With $R_m=\Omega\ell^2/\eta$, this is $R_m$ rotation times and the steady toroidal amplitude is of order $R_mB_0$. Thus high [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) makes establishment very slow even though winding begins immediately.

More precisely, if $\mathcal L=\nabla^2-s^{-2}$ on regular [toroidal magnetic fields](../../../../../../toroidal-magnetic-field.md), the solution with zero initial value satisfies $B(t)=B_{\mathrm{steady}}-e^{\eta t\mathcal L}B_{\mathrm{steady}}$. The residual relaxes diffusively. In the unbounded domain, $\ell^2/\eta$ estimates equilibration of the rotating region; exact establishment at every radius is only an asymptotic statement, with diffusion on scale $r$ taking order $r^2/\eta$. As $\eta\to0$, the time diverges and the prescribed-flow ideal solution continues its secular winding. In a real fluid, [magnetic tension](../../../../../../magnetic-tension.md) and [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) feedback can modify the [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md) before the very large kinematic steady amplitude is attained.
