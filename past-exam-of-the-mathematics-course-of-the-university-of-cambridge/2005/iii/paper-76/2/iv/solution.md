<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [geodynamo](../../../../../../geodynamo.md) operates in the electrically conducting liquid of [Earth's outer core](../../../../../../earth-s-outer-core.md). [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) from thermal and [compositional convection](../../../../../../compositional-convection.md) supplies motion, while the [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) organizes rotating flow. Rotation together with radial stratification permits [kinetic helicity](../../../../../../hydrodynamical-helicity.md), typically with opposite signs in the two hemispheres. In mean-field language this allows an [alpha effect](../../../../../../alpha-effect.md) that converts poloidal and toroidal components into one another. A large-scale [alpha-squared dynamo](../../../../../../alpha-squared-dynamo.md) is therefore a plausible regeneration mechanism even without dominant [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md).

The growth condition compares this regeneration with the sum of molecular and turbulent [magnetic diffusion](../../../../../../magnetic-diffusion.md) on the core scale. A spherical model must impose the appropriate magnetic matching at the core boundaries and permit spatially varying, generally anisotropic alpha and [diffusivity](../../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) tensors. Rotation selects an axis, and stratification and boundaries select positions, so homogeneous [isotropic turbulence](../../../../../../isotropic-turbulence.md) is only a local idealization. Such a model can favor a predominantly axial dipole, but the preferred parity and threshold must be found from its global eigenproblem rather than deduced from the plane-wave formula alone. If mean shear is significant, alpha–Omega regeneration can also contribute.

The first-order-smoothing helicity formula is a useful guide to the sign and magnitude of alpha, not an automatically controlled numerical closure for the core. The important convective motions need not have small eddy [magnetic Reynolds number](../../../../../../magnetic-reynolds-number.md) or short enough [correlation time](../../../../../../correlation-time.md); scale separation can also be limited. Furthermore, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) modifies the flow and its correlations once the field grows. Saturation therefore requires nonlinear feedback, including [dynamo quenching](../../../../../../dynamo-quenching.md) and magnetic-helicity constraints where relevant.

The observed field varies and occasionally reverses. A constant-coefficient growing kinematic eigenmode does not itself explain these reversals or select a saturated amplitude. Spatial and temporal variations, competing modes, and nonlinear flow–field interactions must be included. **[mean-field dynamo theory](../../../../../../mean-field-dynamo.md) supplies a physical regeneration mechanism and a testable threshold, while the geodynamo requires a rotating, bounded, nonlinear realization of that mechanism.**
