<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For constant isotropic $\alpha,\beta$ and zero mean flow, the mean induction equation is

$$
\partial_t\overline{\mathbf B}=\alpha\nabla\times\overline{\mathbf B}+\eta_T\nabla^2\overline{\mathbf B},\qquad\eta_T=\eta+\beta>0.
$$

Let $\overline{\mathbf B}=\widehat{\mathbf B}e^{\sigma t+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$, with $\mathbf k\cdot\widehat{\mathbf B}=0$. On this two-dimensional transverse space, $i\mathbf k\times$ has eigenvalues $\pm k$. For example, if $\mathbf k=k\mathbf e_z$, the vectors $\mathbf e_x\pm i\mathbf e_y$ have these [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) eigenvalues. The [homogeneous alpha-squared dynamo growth criterion](../../../../../../homogeneous-alpha-squared-dynamo-growth-criterion.md) follows from

$$
\boxed{\sigma_\pm=\pm\alpha k-\eta_Tk^2,\qquad\text{growth iff some admissible }k>0\text{ satisfies }|\alpha|>\eta_Tk.}
$$

Choose the [circular polarization](../../../../../../circular-polarization.md) of the mode to match the sign of $\alpha$. This is an [alpha-squared dynamo](../../../../../../alpha-squared-dynamo.md): the [alpha effect](../../../../../../alpha-effect.md) supplies both parts of the regeneration loop without [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md).

If arbitrarily long wavelengths are permitted, every nonzero alpha has a growing band $0<k<|\alpha|/\eta_T$. Maximizing the quadratic gives

$$
k_* =\frac{|\alpha|}{2\eta_T},\qquad\sigma_{\max}=\frac{\alpha^2}{4\eta_T},
$$

provided $k_*\ell\ll1$ so the mean-field approximation is valid. A uniform field has $k=0$, zero [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) and zero growth; a constant alpha cannot amplify that uniform mode. In a [periodic domain](../../../../../../periodic-domain.md) of size $L$, the lowest nonzero wavenumber is $2\pi/L$, giving $|\alpha|L/\eta_T>2\pi$. Other boundaries and geometries give different critical constants, but the general scale criterion is $|\alpha|L/\eta_T$ sufficiently large. Nonzero [kinetic helicity](../../../../../../hydrodynamical-helicity.md) alone is therefore not a sufficient finite-domain dynamo criterion: its induced alpha must overcome diffusion on an available mean-field scale.
