<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the course convention that a [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) is finite and is a [reduced root system](../../../../../../reduced-root-system.md) satisfying the [crystallographic root system](../../../../../../crystallographic-root-system.md) condition. These hypotheses are essential for the angle restriction. For [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) $r,s$, the two [Cartan integers](../../../../../../cartan-integer.md)

$$
a=\frac{2(r,s)}{(s,s)},\qquad b=\frac{2(r,s)}{(r,r)}
$$

satisfy $ab=4\cos^2\theta$. If $r,s$ are not parallel, the strict [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and integrality give $ab\in\{0,1,2,3\}$. These values give angles $\pi/2$, $\pi/3$ or $2\pi/3$, $\pi/4$ or $3\pi/4$, and $\pi/6$ or $5\pi/6$, respectively. If the roots are parallel, reducedness implies $s=\pm r$, giving $0$ or $\pi$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta\in\{0,\pi/6,\pi/4,\pi/3,\pi/2,2\pi/3,3\pi/4,5\pi/6,\pi\}.}
$$

Here are examples accounting for every angle. In the [A1 root system](../../../../../../rank-one-root-system.md), the pairs $(r,r)$ and $(r,-r)$ give $0,\pi$. Two roots from different summands of $A_1\oplus A_1$ give $\pi/2$. The six unit vectors at angles $k\pi/3$ form an [A2 root system](../../../../../../a2-root-system.md): taking one root at angle zero and another at $\pi/3$ or $2\pi/3$ gives those two angles. In the [B2 root system](../../../../../../b2-root-system.md) $\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2,\pm e_1\pm e_2\}$, pair $e_1$ with $e_1+e_2$ or $-e_1+e_2$ to obtain $\pi/4,3\pi/4$. In a [G2 root system](../../../../../../g2-root-system.md), take short unit roots at angles $k\pi/3$ and long roots of length $\sqrt3$ at angles $\pi/6+k\pi/3$. A short root at zero paired with long roots at $\pi/6,5\pi/6$ gives the remaining angles.
