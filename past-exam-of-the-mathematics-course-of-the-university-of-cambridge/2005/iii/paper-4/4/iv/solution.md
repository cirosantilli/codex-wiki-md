<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) to an arbitrary $z$:

$$
\bigl(\operatorname{ad}x\operatorname{ad}y-\operatorname{ad}y\operatorname{ad}x\bigr)z
=[x,[y,z]]-[y,[x,z]]=[[x,y],z].
$$

Thus the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) preserves the [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md):

$$
\boxed{[\operatorname{ad}x,\operatorname{ad}y]=\operatorname{ad}[x,y].}
$$
