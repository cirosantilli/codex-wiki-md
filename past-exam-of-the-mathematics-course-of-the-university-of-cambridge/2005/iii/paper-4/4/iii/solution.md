<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Because $\theta$ is a [Lie algebra automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-lie-algebra.md), for every $y$,

$$
\theta(\operatorname{ad}x)\theta^{-1}y
=\theta[x,\theta^{-1}y]=[\theta x,y]
=(\operatorname{ad}(\theta x))y.
$$

Conjugation therefore also proves that $\operatorname{ad}(\theta x)$ is a [nilpotent operator](../../../../../../nilpotent-linear-map.md), and sends every power of $\operatorname{ad}x$ to the corresponding power of $\operatorname{ad}(\theta x)$. Applying this to the defining finite [exponential of a nilpotent Lie algebra derivation](../../../../../../exponential-of-a-nilpotent-lie-algebra-derivation.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\theta\exp(\operatorname{ad}x)\theta^{-1}=\exp(\operatorname{ad}(\theta x)).}
$$
