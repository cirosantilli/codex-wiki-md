<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) is bilinear, so $\operatorname{ad}x$ is a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md). Rearranging the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) gives

$$
[x,[y,z]]=[[x,y],z]+[y,[x,z]].
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{(\operatorname{ad}x)[y,z]=[(\operatorname{ad}x)y,z]+[y,(\operatorname{ad}x)z],}
$$

which is the defining Leibniz rule for a [derivation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). In particular $\operatorname{ad}x$ is an [inner derivation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../inner-derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
