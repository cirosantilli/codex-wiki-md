<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) gives $[L_r,L_s]\subseteq L_{r+s}$ when $r+s\ne0$. The two [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) are independent, so $r+s\ne0$, and the missing root sum means that this weight space is zero. Hence $[e_r,e_s]=0$. These adjoint root operators are nilpotent: successive brackets shift root weights by the same root, so finite [root strings](../../../../../../root-string.md) force eventual vanishing; on the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) the first bracket lies in its root space and the next is zero. By part (iv), the [nilpotent operators](../../../../../../nilpotent-linear-map.md) $A=t\operatorname{ad}e_r$ and $B=u\operatorname{ad}e_s$ commute. Every power of $A$ therefore commutes with every power of $B$, so their finite [matrix exponentials](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) commute too. Using the inverses proved in part (ii),

$$
\boxed{x_s(u)^{-1}x_r(t)^{-1}x_s(u)x_r(t)=I.}
$$
