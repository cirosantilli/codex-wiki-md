<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $v\in V_\lambda$ and $w\in W_\mu$, the [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) acts by

$$
H(v\otimes w)=Hv\otimes w+v\otimes Hw=(\lambda(H)+\mu(H))v\otimes w.
$$

Thus its [weight space](../../../../../../weight-space.md) of [weight](../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $\nu$ is

$$
(V\otimes W)_\nu=\bigoplus_{\lambda+\mu=\nu}V_\lambda\otimes W_\mu,
\qquad
\dim(V\otimes W)_\nu=\sum_{\lambda+\mu=\nu}(\dim V_\lambda)(\dim W_\mu).
$$

Substituting this into the [formal character](../../../../../../formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) and using $e(\lambda+\mu)=e(\lambda)e(\mu)$ proves

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{char}(V\otimes W)
=(\operatorname{char}V)(\operatorname{char}W).}
$$

Every sum is finite for the [Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) under consideration.
