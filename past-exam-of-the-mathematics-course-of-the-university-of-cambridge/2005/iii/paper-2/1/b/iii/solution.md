<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) labels in the preceding diagrams. For each $j$, write $\{i,l\}=\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{j\}$ with $i<l$, and define

$$
K_{j,1}=A_j-2B_{i;j},\qquad K_{j,2}=A_j-2B_{l;j}.
$$

The contraction satisfies $C(A_j)=2y_j$ and $C(B_{i;j})=y_j$, so both $K$ vectors lie in $\ker C$. They are independent and form a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of its [weight space](../../../../../../../weight-space.md) at $-L_j$.

Thus the [bases](../../../../../../../basis.md) marked on the [weight diagram](../../../../../../../weight-diagram.md) of $\Gamma_{1,2}$ are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{weight}&\text{basis in }\ker C\\ \hline
L_i-2L_j\ (i\ne j)&T_{i;jj}\\
2L_i&T_{i;jk},\quad\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\},\ j<k\\
-L_j&K_{j,1},K_{j,2}
\end{array}
$$

and the diagram of $\Gamma_{0,1}$ uses the [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $Q_j=A_j+B_{i;j}+B_{l;j}$ at $-L_j$. Each $Q_j$ is complementary to the two [kernel](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) vectors because its contraction is $4y_j\ne0$. All vectors shown are therefore genuine [bases](../../../../../../../basis.md), not merely [weight](../../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) counts. The [highest-weight vectors](../../../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) are

$$
\boxed{T_{1;33}\text{ for }\Gamma_{1,2},\qquad Q_3\text{ for }\Gamma_{0,1}.}
$$

The latter is highest because $\iota$ intertwines the [dual Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../../dual-lie-algebra-representation.md) and $y_3$ is its [highest-weight vector](../../../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md).
