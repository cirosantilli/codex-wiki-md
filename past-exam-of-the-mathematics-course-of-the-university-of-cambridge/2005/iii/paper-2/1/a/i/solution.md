<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Realize the defining [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md) [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) as $\mathbb Cx\oplus\mathbb Cy$, with $H=x\partial_x-y\partial_y$, $E=x\partial_y$, $F=y\partial_x$. A [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of the [symmetric power](../../../../../../../symmetric-power.md) is $e_i=x^{4-i}y^i$ for $0\leq i\leq4$, with $He_i=(4-2i)e_i$. Hence the [exterior square](../../../../../../../exterior-square.md) [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) $w_{ij}=e_i\wedge e_j$, $i<j$, has [weight](../../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $8-2(i+j)$.

Collecting equal [weights](../../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) gives the complete [weight-space decomposition](../../../../../../../weight-space-decomposition.md):

$$
\begin{array}{c|l|c}
\text{weight}&\text{basis}&\text{multiplicity}\\ \hline
6&w_{01}&1\\
4&w_{02}&1\\
2&w_{03},w_{12}&2\\
0&w_{04},w_{13}&2\\
-2&w_{14},w_{23}&2\\
-4&w_{24}&1\\
-6&w_{34}&1
\end{array}
$$

Every one of the ten exterior [basis vectors](../../../../../../../basis-vector.md) appears exactly once. Thus **$U=\bigoplus_{m=6,4,2,0,-2,-4,-6}U_m$**, with the displayed [bases](../../../../../../../basis.md) and [dimensions](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).
