<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $H=\operatorname{diag}(r,s,-r,-s)$, put $L_1(H)=r$, $L_2(H)=s$. Then $L_3=-L_1$, $L_4=-L_2$. The [matrix unit](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) identity

$$
[H,E_{ij}]=(L_i-L_j)(H)E_{ij}
$$

reads off the [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) directly. The complete list is

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\alpha&\mathfrak g_\alpha\\ \hline
L_1-L_2&\mathbb C(E_{12}-E_{43})\\
L_2-L_1&\mathbb C(E_{21}-E_{34})\\
L_1+L_2&\mathbb C(E_{14}+E_{23})\\
-L_1-L_2&\mathbb C(E_{32}+E_{41})\\
2L_1&\mathbb CE_{13}\\
-2L_1&\mathbb CE_{31}\\
2L_2&\mathbb CE_{24}\\
-2L_2&\mathbb CE_{42}
\end{array}
$$

Thus the [C2 root system](../../../../../../c2-root-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Phi=\{\pm2L_1,\ \pm2L_2,\ \pm(L_1+L_2),\ \pm(L_1-L_2)\}.}
$$

All eight [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) have [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. The zero-weight space is the two-dimensional [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) $\mathbb C(E_{11}-E_{33})\oplus\mathbb C(E_{22}-E_{44})$; zero itself is not a [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md).
