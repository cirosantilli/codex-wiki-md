<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the standard [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md) normalization $\alpha(H_\alpha)=2$. The symbols $H_\alpha$ are vectors in the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md), not the root-space vectors $X_\alpha$. The [root-string theorem](../../../../../../root-string-theorem.md) gives $\beta(H_\alpha)\in\mathbb Z$, so every [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) takes real values on

$$
\mathfrak h_{\mathbb R}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{H_\beta:\beta\in\Phi\}.
$$

The simple [coroots](../../../../../../coroot.md) form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of this real space.

In the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md), $\operatorname{ad}H$ is zero on $\mathfrak h$ and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) by $\beta(H)$ on the one-dimensional space $\mathfrak g_\beta$. Therefore, for $H,K\in\mathfrak h$,

$$
B(H,K)=\sum_{\beta\in\Phi}\beta(H)\beta(K).
$$

For $H,K\in\mathfrak h_{\mathbb R}$ every term is real, so the restricted form is real. Moreover,

$$
B(H,H)=\sum_{\beta\in\Phi}\beta(H)^2\geq0.
$$

If it is zero, all $\beta(H)$ vanish. Then $H$ commutes with every [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) and with $\mathfrak h$, hence is central in $\mathfrak g$. The [center of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../center-of-a-lie-algebra.md) vanishes for a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), so $H=0$. Thus the [Euclidean subspace of a Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../euclidean-subspace-of-a-cartan-subalgebra.md) has

$$
\boxed{B|_{\mathfrak h_{\mathbb R}}\text{ real and positive definite}.}
$$

Equivalently, positivity follows because the [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) span $\mathfrak h^*$.
