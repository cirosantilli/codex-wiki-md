<h1 id="5/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Evaluate the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) on the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md). For arbitrary $K\in\mathfrak h$,

$$
\mathcal B(H_\alpha)(K)=B(H_\alpha,K)
=\sum_{\beta\in\Phi}\beta(H_\alpha)\beta(K).
$$

Thus the [Killing dual of a coroot](../../../../../../killing-dual-of-a-coroot.md) is the [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) expansion

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B(H_\alpha)=\sum_{\beta\in\Phi}\beta(H_\alpha)\beta
=2\sum_{\beta\in\Phi^+}\beta(H_\alpha)\beta.}
$$

The factor two in the positive-root expression occurs because the contributions of $\beta$ and $-\beta$ are equal.

For $\mathfrak{sl}_3$, let $a=L_1-L_2$, $b=L_2-L_3$; the [positive roots](../../../../../../positive-root.md) are $a,b,a+b$. On $H_{12}$ their values are $2,-1,1$. Therefore

$$
\mathcal B(H_{12})=2[2a-b+(a+b)]=6a,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal B(H_{12})=6(L_1-L_2)=12\omega_1-6\omega_2.}
$$

This uses the unscaled [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}A\operatorname{ad}C)$. Changing that normalization would change the scalar six.
