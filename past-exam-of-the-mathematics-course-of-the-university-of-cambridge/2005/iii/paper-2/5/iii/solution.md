<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $H_{12}=E_{11}-E_{22}=[E_{12},E_{21}]$. The six [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ take values $2,-2,1,-1,-1,1$ on $H_{12}$, so the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) gives

$$
B(H_{12},H_{12})=2^2+(-2)^2+1^2+(-1)^2+(-1)^2+1^2=12.
$$

Using the preceding invariant-form identity with $\alpha=L_1-L_2$,

$$
B(H_{12},[E_{12},E_{21}])=\alpha(H_{12})B(E_{12},E_{21})=2B(E_{12},E_{21}).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{B(E_{12},E_{21})=6.}
$$

This agrees with the [Killing form of the special linear Lie algebra](../../../../../../killing-form-of-the-special-linear-lie-algebra.md), $B(A,C)=2n\operatorname{tr}(AC)$, at $n=3$.
