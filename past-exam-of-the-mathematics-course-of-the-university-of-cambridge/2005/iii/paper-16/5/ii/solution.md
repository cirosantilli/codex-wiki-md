<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $B$ and $\epsilon>0$, [inner regularity](../../../../../../inner-regular-measure.md) supplies a [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $K\subseteq B$ with $\mu(B)-\mu(K)<\epsilon/2$. Independently, [outer regularity](../../../../../../outer-regular-measure.md) supplies an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $U\supseteq B$ with $\mu(U)-\mu(B)<\epsilon/2$. Because $K\subseteq U$ and the [measure](../../../../../../measure.md) is finite,

$$
\mu(U\setminus K)=\mu(U)-\mu(K)<\epsilon.
$$

This creates an arbitrarily small measurable transition region between a compact inner approximation and an open outer approximation. The cutoff in the next step then approximates $\mathbf1_B$, rather than merely approximating the number $\mu(B)$.
