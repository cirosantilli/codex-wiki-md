<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) of the [left shift](../../../../../../left-shift.md) satisfies $x_{k+1}=x_k$ for every $k\in\mathbb Z$, so its entire two-sided [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) is constant. The constant [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) with value $i$ belongs to the [subshift of finite type](../../../../../../subshift-of-finite-type.md) precisely when $A_{ii}=1$: this is exactly the requirement that its repeated transition be allowed by the [symbolic transition matrix](../../../../../../transition-matrix-for-a-subshift.md). There is one such [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) for each diagonal entry equal to one. Thus

$$
\boxed{\#\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)=\sum_{i=1}^m A_{ii}=\operatorname{tr}A.}
$$

This also covers an empty [subshift of finite type](../../../../../../subshift-of-finite-type.md), for then no permitted constant [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) exists.
