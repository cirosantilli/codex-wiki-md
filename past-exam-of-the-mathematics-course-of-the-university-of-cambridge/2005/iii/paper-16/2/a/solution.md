<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The convention intended by this question is **existence of a dense forward orbit**, also called [point transitivity](../../../../../../point-transitivity.md). On a nonempty [compact metric space](../../../../../../compact-metric-space.md), this means that some $x\in X$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\overline{\{f^n(x):n\geq0\}}=X.}
$$

Equivalently, that forward [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) visits every nonempty [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md).

The other common definition of [topological transitivity](../../../../../../topological-transitivity.md) uses intersections of iterated [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md), as in part (b). The distinction matters on spaces with [isolated points](../../../../../../isolated-point.md): parts (b) and (c) ask precisely how the two conventions are related. We use [point transitivity](../../../../../../point-transitivity.md) for the dense-orbit convention throughout this solution. As usual for this definition, the [state space](../../../../../../state-space.md) is assumed nonempty; on an empty space the open-set condition is vacuous and there is no point with a forward [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md).
