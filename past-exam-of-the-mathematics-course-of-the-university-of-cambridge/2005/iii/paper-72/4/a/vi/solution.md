<h1 id="4/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

**More than three occur when symmetries or frequency degeneracies reduce the [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md)'s invariant closure below a generic three-dimensional [invariant torus](../../../../../../../invariant-torus.md).** For a nonradial [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) in a spherical [gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md), [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) and the three components of [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) are generically four independent [isolating integrals](../../../../../../../isolating-integral.md). Their values fix the orbital plane, [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) magnitude and radial [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md), but a nonclosed rosette still fills a two-dimensional [invariant torus](../../../../../../../invariant-torus.md). Thus more than three does not by itself imply closure. [Orbital resonances](../../../../../../../orbital-resonance.md) can further reduce the number of independent orbital phases. The count concerns independent integrals, not arbitrarily many functions of the same invariants.
