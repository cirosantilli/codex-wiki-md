<h1 id="4/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**One [isolating integral](../../../../../../../isolating-integral.md) occurs when [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is the only independent stationary invariant.** An autonomous chaotic [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) that densely explores a connected five-dimensional [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) surface is the standard case. Every continuous [integral of motion](../../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md) must then be constant on that surface, so it is locally a function of [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) rather than an additional independent integral. Irregular motion need not mean zero integrals; [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) still isolates the [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) from other [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) surfaces.
