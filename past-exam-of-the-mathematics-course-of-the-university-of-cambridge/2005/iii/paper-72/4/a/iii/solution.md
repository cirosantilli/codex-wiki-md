<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An [isolating integral](../../../../../../../isolating-integral.md) restricts the accessible [phase space](../../../../../../../phase-space.md) to a lower-dimensional regular level set. For [functionally independent](../../../../../../../functionally-independent-functions.md) [integrals of motion](../../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md) $I_1,\ldots,I_N$, the differentials $dI_j$ must be independent, and $I_j=c_j$ impose $N$ independent restrictions. In six-dimensional [phase space](../../../../../../../phase-space.md) the common regular level set has dimension **$6-N$**. Nonisolating [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) labels or multivalued constants do not further reduce the closure explored by the motion. This is the [isolating integrals and orbital closure dimension](../../../../../../../isolating-integrals-and-orbital-closure-dimension.md) distinction.
