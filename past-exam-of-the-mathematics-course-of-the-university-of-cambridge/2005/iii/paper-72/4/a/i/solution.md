A [constant of motion](../../../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) is a quantity preserved along the evolution, possibly with explicit time dependence. For $C(\mathbf x,\mathbf v,t)$ in a [gravitational potential](../../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Phi$, the preservation condition is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dC}{dt}=\partial_tC+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla_xC-\nabla\Phi\cdot\nabla_vC=0.}
$$

For free motion, $\mathbf x-\mathbf v t$ is a useful explicitly time-dependent example. Such initial-position labels should not be counted as stationary [isolating integrals](../../../../../../../isolating-integral.md) of a galactic [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md).
