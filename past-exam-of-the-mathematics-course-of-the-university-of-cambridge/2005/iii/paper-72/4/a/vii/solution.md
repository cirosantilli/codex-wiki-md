↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

**Five independent isolating [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) labels describe a regular closed, nonstationary [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) in three-dimensional space:** its [phase space](../../../../../../../phase-space.md) closure is one-dimensional. Five is the maximum at a nonzero flow vector, since every conserved differential annihilates that vector in the six-dimensional tangent space. A global [superintegrable Hamiltonian system](../../../../../../../superintegrable-hamiltonian-system.md) example is the [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md), where [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md), [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](../../../../../../../laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) have two vector/scalar relations leaving five independent invariants. A circular or radial [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) requires a separate treatment of any rank-degenerate formula for these invariants. Five integrals do not force an escaping trajectory to be periodic: unbound [Kepler orbits](../../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) also have the conserved apsidal vector. The closure criterion concerns recurrent bound motion.

Closure of one resonant [orbit](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) need not imply five smooth independent integrals on an open neighborhood of all nearby [orbits](../../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). The orbit-wise count and global superintegrability must be distinguished; the distinction matters in part (b).
