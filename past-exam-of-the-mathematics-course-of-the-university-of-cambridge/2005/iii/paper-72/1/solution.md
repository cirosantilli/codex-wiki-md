↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Both models obtain their density by integrating a [galactic distribution function](../../../../../galactic-distribution-function.md) over [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). The planar model integrates one vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), whereas the spherical model integrates all three components. In each case the resulting density must also generate its own [gravitational potential](../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) through the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](../../../../../poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md). The two printed numerical subparts are treated separately below.
