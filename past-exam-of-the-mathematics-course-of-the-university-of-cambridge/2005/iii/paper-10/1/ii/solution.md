↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** The [simplicial vertex boundaries need not increase below half volume](../../../../../../simplicial-vertex-boundaries-need-not-increase-below-half-volume.md). In $Q_3$,

$$
C(3)=\{\varnothing,\{1\},\{2\}\},\qquad
C(4)=C(3)\cup\{\{3\}\}.
$$

Both [closed graph neighbourhoods](../../../../../../closed-graph-neighbourhood.md) consist of all vertices except $\{1,2,3\}$, so both have size seven. Their external [vertex boundaries](../../../../../../external-vertex-boundary.md) therefore have sizes

$$
\boxed{|\partial C(3)|=4>3=|\partial C(4)|},
$$

although $3<4=2^{3-1}$. Adding a vertex already in the neighbourhood can reduce the external [vertex boundary](../../../../../../external-vertex-boundary.md) without enlarging the neighbourhood.
