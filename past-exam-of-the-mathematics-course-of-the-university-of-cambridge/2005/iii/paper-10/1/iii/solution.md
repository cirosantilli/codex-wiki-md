<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** [Complementary-size simplicial vertex boundaries can be asymmetric](../../../../../../complementary-size-simplicial-vertex-boundaries-can-be-asymmetric.md). Use $n=7$, $k=63$ and $l=65$, so $k+l=128$.

There are 64 sets of size at most three. The [initial segment](../../../../../../initial-segment.md) $C(63)$ contains all of them except the last triple $\{5,6,7\}$. Every four-set has another included triple, so its external [vertex boundary](../../../../../../external-vertex-boundary.md) is that missing triple together with all $\binom74=35$ four-sets: $|\partial C(63)|=36$.

The [initial segment](../../../../../../initial-segment.md) $C(65)$ contains all sets of size at most three and the first four-set $\{1,2,3,4\}$. Its external [vertex boundary](../../../../../../external-vertex-boundary.md) has the other 34 four-sets and the three five-sets containing $\{1,2,3,4\}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{|\partial C(63)|=36<37=|\partial C(65)|},
$$

contradicting the proposed inequality.
