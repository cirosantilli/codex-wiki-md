<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Always true.** Simplicial [initial segments](../../../../../../initial-segment.md) are nested: $C(k)\subseteq C(l)$ when $k\leq l$. Taking the [closed graph neighbourhood](../../../../../../closed-graph-neighbourhood.md) preserves inclusion, hence $N[C(k)]\subseteq N[C(l)]$ and the claimed size inequality follows. No extremal theorem is needed for this step.
