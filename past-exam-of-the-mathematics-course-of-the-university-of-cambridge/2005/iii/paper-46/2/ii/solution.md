<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Extend the selected $\ell_1$ to an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), writing it as $v_1$. For a [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) orthogonal to it, $a_1=0$, and therefore

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\ell^TX)=\sum_{j=2}^{p}\lambda_ja_j^2\leq\lambda_2.
$$

A unit [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_2$ attains equality. Thus **$\ell_2$ is a unit [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) for the largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) on $\ell_1^\perp$, and the maximum is $\lambda_2$**. If the largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is repeated, a second orthogonal vector in that [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) is allowed and $\lambda_2=\lambda_1$; there is no uniquely determined first pair of directions.
