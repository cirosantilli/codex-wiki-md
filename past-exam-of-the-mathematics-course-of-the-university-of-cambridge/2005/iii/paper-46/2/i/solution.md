<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_p\geq0$ be the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the symmetric [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) $V$, and choose an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_p$. If $\ell=\sum_ja_jv_j$ has unit length, then $\sum_ja_j^2=1$ and

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\ell^TX)=\ell^TV\ell=\sum_j\lambda_ja_j^2\leq\lambda_1.
$$

Equality is attained by $v_1$, and precisely by [unit vectors](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) in the top [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md). Hence **choose $\ell_1$ to be any unit [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) for the largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md); the maximum [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is $\lambda_1$**. This proves the maximizing property of the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md), rather than merely specifying a stationary point.
