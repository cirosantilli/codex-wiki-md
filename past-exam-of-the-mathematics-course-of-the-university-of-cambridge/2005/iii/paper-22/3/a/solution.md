↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [stable homotopy category](../../../../../../stable-homotopy-category.md), a [ring spectrum](../../../../../../ring-spectrum.md) is a unital associative monoid object for the [smash product of spectra](../../../../../../smash-product-of-spectra.md). Thus it consists of a [topological spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-topology.md) $E$ with multiplication and unit

$$
\mu:E\wedge E\longrightarrow E,\qquad\eta:\mathbb S\longrightarrow E,
$$

where $\mathbb S$ is the [sphere spectrum](../../../../../../sphere-spectrum.md), and the identities are

$$
\mu(\mu\wedge1)=\mu(1\wedge\mu),\qquad\mu(\eta\wedge1)=1_E=\mu(1\wedge\eta).
$$

The canonical associativity and unit identifications of the [symmetric monoidal category](../../../../../../symmetric-monoidal-category.md) are understood in these formulas. All identities are identities of maps in the [stable homotopy category](../../../../../../stable-homotopy-category.md).

A [commutative ring spectrum](../../../../../../commutative-ring-spectrum.md) also satisfies

$$
\boxed{\mu\tau=\mu,}
$$

where $\tau:E\wedge E\to E\wedge E$ interchanges the two factors. This is the ring-object convention; a coherently structured ring spectrum has additional point-set or higher-homotopy data. In particular homotopy-category commutativity alone is not an assertion of such a coherent enhancement.
