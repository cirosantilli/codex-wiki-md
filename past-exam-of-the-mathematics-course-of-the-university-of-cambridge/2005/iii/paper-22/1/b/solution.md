<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use derived [smash products of spectra](../../../../../../smash-product-of-spectra.md) and replace a [based space](../../../../../../based-space.md) by a based [CW complex](../../../../../../cw-complex.md) when necessary. The reduced [represented homology theory](../../../../../../represented-homology-theory.md) of $E$ is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde E_k(X)=\pi_k\bigl(E\wedge\Sigma^\infty X\bigr),\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Thus [represented homology theory](../../../../../../represented-homology-theory.md) is covariant in $X$: a based map induces a map of [suspension spectra](../../../../../../suspension-spectrum.md), and hence a map on the [stable homotopy groups](../../../../../../stable-homotopy-group.md) of the displayed [smash product of spectra](../../../../../../smash-product-of-spectra.md). In a well-based sequential model the same definition is

$$
\widetilde E_k(X)=\operatorname*{colim}_{j}\pi_{k+j}(E_j\wedge X),
$$

with $j$ sufficiently large and transition maps given by suspension followed by the structure map.

The reduced [represented cohomology theory](../../../../../../represented-cohomology-theory.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde E^q(X)=[\Sigma^\infty X,\Sigma^qE]_{\mathrm{st}},\qquad q\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The brackets denote maps in the [stable homotopy category](../../../../../../stable-homotopy-category.md). This is contravariant in $X$, by precomposition. For the [Omega-spectrum](../../../../../../omega-spectrum.md) $E$ it is also the group of [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) classes $[X,E_q]_*$, where for negative $q$ we take $E_q=\Omega^{-q}E_0$. More generally, for $j\ge\max(q,0)$ the [Omega-spectrum](../../../../../../omega-spectrum.md) identifications give $\widetilde E^q(X)=[\Sigma^{j-q}X,E_j]_*$; the shifts and loops supply its abelian group structure. In particular

$$
\widetilde E_k(S^0)=\pi_kE,\qquad\widetilde E^q(S^0)=\pi_{-q}E.
$$

All these definitions are reduced: the basepoint itself contributes zero.
