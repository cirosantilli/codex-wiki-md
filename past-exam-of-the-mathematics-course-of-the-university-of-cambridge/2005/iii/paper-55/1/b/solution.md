<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md) is the multiplicative $\mathbb Z_2$ quantum number

$$
\boxed{R_p=(-1)^{3(B-L)+2s},}
$$

where $B,L,s$ are [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md), [lepton number](../../../../../../lepton-number.md) and spin. Ordinary [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) particles have $R_p=+1$ and their superpartners have $R_p=-1$. Gauge invariance alone allows [MSSM](../../../../../../minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md) [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) terms $LLE^c$, $LQD^c$, $U^cD^cD^c$ and $LH_u$. Simultaneous baryon- and lepton-number violation can induce rapid [proton decay](../../../../../../proton-decay.md). Exact [R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md) forbids these renormalizable terms, since the associated [matter parity](../../../../../../matter-parity.md) is odd; it does not forbid every higher-dimensional proton-decay operator, for example $QQQL$.

Two consequences are **superpartner production in pairs from ordinary-particle initial states** and **stability of the lightest supersymmetric particle**: a single parity-odd particle cannot decay only into parity-even particles. Cascades therefore end in the [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md); if it is neutral and weakly interacting, it produces missing-energy signatures.

A continuous [R-symmetry](../../../../../../r-symmetry.md) rotates $\theta$ and the [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md). With $R(\theta)=1$, the [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) has [R-charge](../../../../../../r-charge.md) two and the fermion in a [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) has one less charge than its scalar. [R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md) is only a discrete restriction, represented by combining [matter parity](../../../../../../matter-parity.md) with $\theta\mapsto-\theta$, and need not preserve a full continuous [R-symmetry](../../../../../../r-symmetry.md). For example, a [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) violates an unbroken continuous [R-symmetry](../../../../../../r-symmetry.md) but preserves [R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md). Thus proton protection by parity and continuous R-charge selection rules are distinct requirements.
