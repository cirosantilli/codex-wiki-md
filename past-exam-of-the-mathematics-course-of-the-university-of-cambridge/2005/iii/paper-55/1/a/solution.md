<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For null [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md), $\{Q^I,\bar Q^J\}=2\delta^{IJ}\sigma\cdot p$ has rank one in its spinor indices. Positivity makes the null combinations act trivially; the remaining $N$ normalized pairs obey $\{a_I,a_J^\dagger\}=\delta_{IJ}$. Acting with distinct lowering operators on a highest-[helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) state gives the [helicity spectrum of a massless supermultiplet](../../../../../../helicity-spectrum-of-a-massless-supermultiplet.md):

$$
\lambda_k=h-\frac{k}{2},\qquad n_k=\binom Nk\quad(0\leq k\leq N),\qquad
\boxed{\sum_k n_k=2^N.}
$$

A [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](../../../../../../cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md) adds the opposite [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) if needed. Interpreting the physical spin bounds as $|\lambda|\leq s$, the width $N/2$ fits inside $[-s,s]$ only if $N\leq4s$. A literal one-sided bound on helicity without [CPT](../../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) would not give this maximum. The maximal spectra are

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
N=4:\ \lambda&1&1/2&0&-1/2&-1\\
\text{multiplicity}&1&4&6&4&1
\end{array}
$$

and

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}
N=8:\ \lambda&2&3/2&1&1/2&0&-1/2&-1&-3/2&-2\\
\text{multiplicity}&1&8&28&56&70&56&28&8&1.
\end{array}
$$

Thus **the maxima are (N=4) for spin at most one and (N=8) for spin at most two**, with 16 and 256 states. [Dimensional reduction](../../../../../../dimensional-reduction.md) of [ten-dimensional super Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../ten-dimensional-super-yang-mills-theory.md) on a six-torus gives the first: the vector yields one four-dimensional vector and six [real scalar fields](../../../../../../real-scalar-field.md), and its [Majorana-Weyl spinor](../../../../../../majorana-weyl-spinor.md) yields four [Weyl spinors](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md). Reducing [eleven-dimensional supergravity](../../../../../../eleven-dimensional-supergravity.md) on a seven-torus gives the second: metric components supply one [graviton](../../../../../../graviton.md), seven vectors and 28 scalars; the three-form supplies 21 vectors, 35 scalars and seven dualized two-form scalars; the [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) supplies eight [gravitini](../../../../../../gravitino.md) and 56 spin-half fields. These total 28 vectors and 70 scalars, with 128 [boson](../../../../../../boson.md) and 128 [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md) states.
