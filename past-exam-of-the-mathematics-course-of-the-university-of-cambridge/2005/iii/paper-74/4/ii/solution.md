<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a moderately faster [cosmic expansion](../../../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md) that still permits efficient nuclear burning, **the hydrogen mass fraction is smaller**, since cooling from [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) to helium formation takes less time, fewer free [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) decay into [protons](../../../../../../proton.md), and more surviving neutrons enter [helium-4](../../../../../../helium-4.md). If the change also affects [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md), its higher freeze-out [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) leaves a larger initial [neutron-to-proton ratio](../../../../../../neutron-to-proton-ratio.md) and reinforces the helium increase. The conclusion assumes efficient [neutron-limited helium synthesis](../../../../../../neutron-limited-helium-synthesis.md): an unspecified arbitrarily rapid expansion could instead outrun the [nuclear reactions](../../../../../../nuclear-reaction.md), so the [nuclear-burning qualification of the helium expansion-rate response](../../../../../../nuclear-burning-qualification-of-the-helium-expansion-rate-response.md) prevents an unconditional assertion.
